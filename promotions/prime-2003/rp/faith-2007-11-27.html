Faith

27 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 27/11/07 Now I know it’s a cliché, but everything happens for a reason. There was a reason for life. There is a reason for death There is a reason why Ethan Frost was able to overcome the odds and stun every single member of PRIME by defeating ‘Hall Of Famer’ Vangelus Olsig. There was of course one simple reason that Frost beat Olsig…he was better than him. It had taken a lot for the fire to be instilled in Frost’s belly again, but his time away in Canada, with his new busy Kosidlo for company had done him a significant amount of good. Hell off the back of his victory over Olsig he flew back to America and was able to defeat Jacob McKail, in doing so he thrust himself into a second shot at the Gateway Championship, a second chance at the belt when before he had been cast over. He had the Jewel In The Crown competition to thank for that. For a time he felt good, but now the realisation of everything was beginning to dawn on him. The first round of the competition had seen a lot of casualties, a lot of people who had been touted as potentially winning the entire tournament. High Flyer…gone Dusk…gone Christian Darke…gone Digital Mortality…gone Joshua Kosidlo…gone It was the last two that had effected Frost the most but in entirely different ways, the loss of Digital Mortality, especially in the first round and sent ripples throughout PRIME, the PRIME killer had been defeated and early at that. The guy that had torn through the field last year and claimed more scalps than almost any other free agent against PRIME enemies was gone. What had happened had Digital Mortality lost his edge? Surely that couldn’t be the case, Frost had spent a significant amount of time with him in UWF, his rookie federation and was a man who he had always looked up to. No the fact of the matter was PRIME was in an awfully stronger position than it was a year ago, its roster had been tweaked like a sports car being fitter with a supercharger, everything was just that little bit better, elegance, performance and practicality. The likes of Tony Gamble and Brandon Youngblood already beginning to shine like beacons in the tournament Joshua Kosidlo though was a different matter, in Frost’s eyes, he was the difference maker last time round. The two had spent a lot of time bonding and forming a nice level of friendship which had allowed Frost to relax, now though Frost had needed to fly back to Canada alone, once again the thought of his FUSE career playing in the back of his mind, but this time he had no-one to fall back on no-one to keep himself centred and to talk to and to keep his mind off things. This time around the hotel room seemed an awful lot bigger than it previously had been. He had been lying alone on his bed for a good few hours, the thoughts of the world running through his head, trying to find a way to beat Aimz, this time round for the title. He couldn’t allow himself to think like that though, not at the moment, there was a much greater obstacle in his way at the moment. Once again the powers that be had decided it would be great fun to throw Frost against a Hall Of Famer, but this time round, the calibre of opponent had risen on a sharp gradient. Hoyt Williams, the self proclaimed son of God, was a man who by all rights deserved to be a Hall Of Famer, the man was a walking monolith for a start, a demon on the stick and had more than enough ability to back up anything he decided, he also had the backing his father Duke Williams…enough said legend. When all of this was added together, not forgetting of course Hoyt’s extremely successfully run as Universal champion of PRIME left Frost with a bitter feeling in his stomach. As the minutes turned into hours, Frost realised that his faith in himself was beginning to ebb away and without faith what are you, simply a shell. Belief is the number one thing in this life that pushes people on, it drives them, people who believe in things often find that extra level of strength that can push them forwards. Frost though without someone to carry his weight, his extra burdens, he was very much a man alone who did not trust in his abilities despite the evidence that he had in front of him. ~~~ “Have you ever considered giving your life to Jesus? It was a cold afternoon, the sort of afternoon that tore through your skin to your very bones. Frost had been walking for around about an hour, choosing to take in some of the sites, but more importantly, trying to clear his mind and relax a little. A few cups of weak coffee, quickly followed by a few drinks of whisky had left Frost with a nice spring in his step and positive outlook on life. That was up until the man in front of him had sprung in front of Ethan Frost and stared at him with a grin covering his face the size of a small barn, most noticeably he was clutching a bible tightly to his chest whilst approaching him. Frost had looked for every possible escape route but before he had been able to actually act on one of those, he had been caught between a rock and a hard place there was no way out now other than turning around and running. There actually was one other route out of this. A good old confrontation. “Give me one good reason why I should.” Ok sure it was not the wittiest remark that he could think of, but it was true and in his slightly intoxicated state, it just felt right. Frost had always had a problem with people who tried to preach at him about religion, that was not to say that he was against religion, far from it he respected people that had that much faith in their lives. It was just when it was tried to be slammed down peoples throats, without allowing them to make up their own mind. “He died for your sins.” The man who had not removed the grin from his replied, he must have heard retorts like this a million times a day, but even as he spoke he had to admit that he had not seen someone stagger around quite so much as he had already seen from Frost. Nor did they actually stick around this long normally. “Please don’t make me laugh, the guy IF he existed at all was dead long before my sins even began.” There was no real malice in Frost’s voice he was simply tired, his mind had been around the houses for days now. He had not been sleeping and whenever he had been allowed a few moments peace or solitude, his dreams took over and he simply did not rest at all. He was better off awake. “No he gave himself so that you may find happiness in the eternal paradise of heaven.” “So this heaven that you talk about, have you ever seen it?” The man clutching the bible blinked wildly, he had not been asked this before, if the truth was told he didn’t normally get too far with his spiel that he practised over and over in his mind, this in all of his practise runs had never occurred. Frost in the mean time had slid his hands into his pockets to cool them from the bitter wind that was biting them. However he was feeling distinctly happier about himself and allowed himself to smile as he sensed the small man in front of him begin to squirm. “Heaven is whatever you decide it is.” “I choose life as both my heaven and my punishment.” Frost allowed himself a moment to cast his mind back to the recent months of his life, which would certainly be classified as a punishment, both physically and mentally, his mind had deteriorated to a near state of manic depression and his body had become dependant on drugs and self harm, punishment and release in ever decreasing circles which had begun to spiral out of control. In a moments weakness Frost allowed himself to look down at his body, sure it was covered now, wrapped up in numerous layers of clothing, protecting him from the hideous cold, but Frost saw through the clothes and saw every single scar that covered his body, the wounds on his legs that had not fully healed, every step he took tore the wound apart that little bit further, it was the sort of wound that would never fully heal. “What do you mean, don’t you wish to dwell in the eternal paradise beyond the pearly gates.” “Listen chief, I don’t know how to break this to you, but it’s all a load of shit. Trust me I respect you for having the belief I do, but it’s misplaced. Religion as a whole and I’m not just talking about Christianity here but all religions, are nothing more than a means of control. “How can you say that? The bible clearly states….” Before the man could even start his sentence Frost had intersected angrily. “As do the Vedas, as does the Qu’ran. All holy books in religion, tell me have you ever sat down and read the bible from cover to cover? “Well no but……” Once again, the man was intersected, he felt that he quickly wanted to run away, everything was beginning to make him wish that he had not “I have, it’s a fantastic piece of literature, but that’s as far as it goes a fantastic story which echoes every other ‘holy’ book this world has to offer. The book is a means to control, a book and an embodiment of something greater which gets you to conform to a series of rules. It was all made up by someone who wanted to rule by fear, by scaring the people into behaving for the promise of something more when we die, but that doesn’t happen, there is no proof that it does.” There was a certain sense of satisfaction as Frost ran through the extensive list of religious texts and his views on them, too often people were ignorant towards religion, but Frost actually had a significant interest in the entire subject. Just because he didn’t believe or have any faith of his own, didn’t mean that he wasn’t interested in the subject matter. “There is no proof that it doesn’t exist though, by your own lame argument you have to admit to that.” “Sure enough that makes sense I will agree to that but I don’t want to risk ruining my life on some false hope, it’s like buying a yacht on the off chance you might win the lottery on Saturday it just doesn’t make sense.” “You poor man, may god have mercy on your soul for I see there is no saving you, you will roast in the eternal fires of hell where you will be punished for your sins.” “Sins? You know nothing about my life, you come up to me in the street preaching some two dimensional bollocks and then judge me. I recommend you get out of my face right the hell now, before I do something that I really will go to hell about.” The pleasantries were now over, Frost had used up all the entertaining aspects had gone and quite frankly he was now bored. Most importantly he had been judged by someone he considered his lesser. Frost as a general rule was not an aggressive person but the mix of alcohol, anger and general sleep deprivation had gained full momentum and he was now ready to erupt at any second. “All you have to do is believe….save yourself!” They were the last words of the man who never revealed his name, as he turned on his heels and peeled off down the street, fearing for his physical well being. Frost laughed to himself for a moment before the words that the man had said began to echo through his head as he began to process them, think about exactly what was said. All you have to do is believe. Save yourself. ~~~ The only one person that was holding back Ethan Frost, was himself, he knew this to be true but he could not get away from the negativity that he surrounded himself in. He had no confidence and no self-esteem as a result, even when he was victorious, he picked negatives in his performance, dissected everything until there were no positives left just the bad. If Frost was going to be victorious he needed to find his edge, he needed to find his faith. Not that hockey crap that’s called religion though, that simply wasn’t all it was made up to be. The thing is, religion has been used as something to control the masses through fear for generations, once you suspend all belief there is no reason to worry. There is no reason to fear, simply because there is nothing to fear. People make their own destiny, it’s not predetermined and I will not be judged on the things I have done and the actions that I take. My life is my judgement because I live with my decisions every single day. Death…will be my reward and the final chapter in the book of Frost.
