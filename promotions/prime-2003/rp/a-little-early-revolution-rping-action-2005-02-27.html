A little early ReVolution Rping action.

27 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/05 ~Religion. Most people have some religious beliefs. Some do not. Different strokes for different folks, I guess. Believe it or not, Jon Kano has religion, just not an over abundance of it. He looks at religion as his crutch to support himself when he is weak. He now looks at religion for answers. He wants to know why Boda did what he did. He wants to know why Boda set him up for his sick game. He wants to know how he couldn't have seen it coming. He wants to know if he still has what it takes to wrestle like a legend. He wants to know if he can honestly wrestle his first match and not see his opponent, Shane Wethers, as Chris Sloboda.~ *The scene opens up inside of a dimly-lit Catholic church. The only light seems to come from candles lit around this holy place. It really gives the church an eerie feeling, but at the same time a very calming tone. As we approach the altar of the church, a man is seen kneeling in the front pew. The closer we get, the easier it is to see who this man is. He is one Mr. Jon Kano, PRIME's newest signee, and a very confused and angry man due to the events at Culture Shock. Kano's usually well-groomed long, blonde hair is hastily ties into a ponytail and his eyes have dark circles underneath them. His 'Kano ROCKS~!' t-shirt has a few wrinkles in it and his jeans look like they have seen better days as he reaches into his pocket and pulls out rosary bead with his right hand.* Kano: Lord forgive me. I am just a humble man wanting what a humble man wants, happiness. I came to PRIME for happiness, but now I feel the least happy I have in a long, long time. Chris Sloboda, my best friend for the last five years, has drawn the line in the sand. He played me like a fool at Culture Shock, and I know he felt it was a necessary evil. I can forgive a lot, Lord, but I cannot let this slide. He will pay the price, so help me God, I'll see that he pays the price. *Kano pauses to look up at the huge figure of Jesus Christ being crucified on the wall behind the altar.* Kano: He used his girls as tools to lure me into his trap. His girls, who may as well be my own, and he used them to play his sick game. Well I can be sick and twisted, too. I am Jon Kano, I ain't no punk. I wonder if he thought that I would just take this lying down. No, he knows me better than that. He knows that I haven't layed down for anyone my whole career, and I am not going to start now. Chris Sloboda will not have a free pass, before you, I swear, Boda will not have a free pass to reign over PRIME. *Kano's voice begins to tremble as he shakes a bit with anger.* Kano: Lord, I haven't slept well since Culture Shock. I just keep playing the scene over and over in my head... *FLASHBACK TO CULTURE SHOCK* ~Kano slides back in the ring, and stands right next to Boda, watching the two behemoths stare one another down and verbally abusing each other. Killean is helped to the back by EMTs as Black Angel starts to stir on the mat. Kano puts his hand on Boda's shoulder, as to let him know... 'I got your back man'. Boda never breaks his stare from Vampir's eyes and just nods his head up and down slowly. Nick: Come on Boda, you know you want to.... Lay that son of a bitch out, right now. Boda pulls the title in close, grasping it with both hands. The Sappora Dome is cheering for Boda to nail Vampir. The two enemies never break their eye-lock on each other. The Big Bad Boda Daddy steps back, turning to wind up the title belt. Boda swings and turns at the last second, nailing Jon Kano in the side of the head. Vampir never flinches or blinks. The fans suddenly go dead silent. The PCW Legend stumbles back against the ropes. Nick: What the........ Boda drops the title and turns and as Kano comes back in his direction, Boda thrusts both hands around his neck and jerks Kano into the air, slamming him to the mat with a thunderous sit-out choke bomb. Richard: BOUNCED CZECH!!! Boda just laid Kano out with a Bounced Czech! Nick: Why? These two are long time friends!? Boda stands back up, placing the Universal Title over his right shoulder. Vampir and him look at one another. Boda smiles, sadistically, as does Vampir from under his mask. The Sappora Dome implodes in the loudest boo of the name. Boda and Vampir first shake hands in the center of the ring. Then the two hug briefly, as Boda turns and hoists the Universal Title over his head in the air. The Japanese fans start tossing food and drinks into the ring, trying to hit the man that just shocked them all. Richard: LOOK AT THOSE TWO..... THEY JUST SHOCKED US ALL......THEY TRULY ARE THE EPITOME OF CULTURE SHOCK.~ *END FLASHBACK* *Kano's arms are now shaking as he relives his former friend's deception.* Kano: What Boda and the Dark Age now have done is sealed their collective fate. I hope and I pray, Lord, that you have mercy on them, because I WON'T. I am beyond that. So, Lord, forgive me, for I know not what I do, at least not anymore. *Kano takes big gulp, clearing his throat.* Kano: I know not what I do, and I won't be held responsible for what happens. At the next ReVolution, I make my singles debut in PRIME. I write the next chapter in my storied career. I must make an impact and make everyone recognize my skills and talent. I know I cannot focus completely on this match, for I know that the Dark Age is somewhere. They are like cancer, just when you think they are gone, they come back. Lord, I feel that you are the only one I can trust to have my back. And what I ask now is for you to make me see my opponent at ReVolution. I have a feeling that when I look across that ring, I will see Boda, and not Shane Wethers. That would be the worst thing possible for both he and I. Shane will be my opponent, but I feel that I might snap and see Boda. All of the anger and hatred I have for Boda will come pouring out of my body and my soul. I will do something to Shane that I will regret, I might end his career. Lord, I do not want to end this young man's career. I don't want to hurt him, but I do want to beat him. I know I will have some ring rust, but I can handle that. Just make me conduct myself like the legend and professional that I am. Amen. *Kano stands up, crosses himself, and begins to walk down the aisle towards the exit of the church.* Kano: Shane Wethers, the Eternal Punk, if you will. A promising young talent, someone who could excel in the years to come. It is funny. He is the eternal punk, and after what Boda and Vampir pulled on me, that is what I feel like, a punk. A no good, filthy, self-loathing punk. Someone who just gets punked out the whole time. I am no punk, I AIN'T NO ROOKIE. I have busted my ass for this industry for almost ten years now, and yet I get sh*t like this done to me. Shane, if you are a religious man, pray. I pray for you and I. I pray that I see you and not Boda. I pray that I still have the talent to do what I love and bring a crowd to its feet. I pray that we both walk out of the ring and ReVolution underneath our own power, but most of all, I pray that I can channel my anger away from you and onto those who deserve it. *As Kano passes a sink of holy water, he dips his right index finger into it and places a drop on his forehead.* Kano: Hmmm, kinda burns. Shane, for your sake, I hope I see you across the ring from me. I pray that I don't have a delusion of Boda in your place, because I might kill you. Shane, regardless of who I see, I am going to show everyone that I haven't missed a step and that I still got what it takes to take care of business inside of the ring. Believe that. You will lose the match, I am not being cocky, I am being truthful. You have never stepped into the ring with someone like me. I am walking, breathing legend, and I am not even thirty yet. I will make everyone respect my presence here in PRIME, and unfortunately for you, sir, you just happen to be the lamb led to the slaughter. Also unfortunately for you, I am not going to be in a good mood for a long time and I am probably going to very aggressive towards my opponents for awhile. But, hopefully, if all goes well, it will be quick and done for you before you even knew it had started. *Kano opens the door to the church and walks into the bright sunshine, shielding his eyes. Apparently, this church is not in a good part of town. There is trash being blown around and what appears to be a drug deal going down directly across the street.* Kano: Now I am sure some of you are shocked that I would be in such a slum, a hell hole, even some would say hell itself. I find that when I need to find answers to tough questions, it is best to come to places like this. You see, I hail from Sin City itself, Las Vegas, and I am damn proud of that. Yet, I grew up in a part of Las Vegas that wasn't bad, yet wasn't good either. I could walk two blocks over and a block down and be at a full-blown crack house. So when I come to places like this, it is like me coming home. Mr. Wethers, I know you might care to relate to this, if you can, kind sir. I know you pride yourself on being that Eternal punk persona, but Shane, sometimes you need to grow up and grow out of that. I have busted my ass day in and day out for the last ten years making myself a multi-millionaire, not because I wanted to be rich, but because I didn't want my children or my children's children to have to deal with all of this bullshit. Do you understand what I mean? I haven't gotten more than six hours of sleep in a night for ten years, most of the time I feel lucky if I get four. I think now that I have come here, I have found my answers, at least to some of my questions. I'll see you at ReVolution and show you why I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano begins to walk down the street.* ~I hope Jon really got his answers.~
