Little Blue Men (rev match RP)

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/17/05 Hoyt Williams: The world is full of nothing but lily totting liberals!! You can’t say anything anymore with out getting crucified by some special interest group so blinded by their moronic cause they can’t see the humor in it. You know if I was Jesus I’d only heal people with a good sense of humor. I’d let all the unlaughable lepers die. The Mother Mary is behind Hoyt giving him a neck message as he sits on the end of the bed with his shirt off. He is upset because he got a letter from a midget group complaining about a comment he made on one of the shows. Hoyt Williams: I tell you mental midgets is what they are. They seem incapable of reason and understanding. They get upset and dwarf out. Not cool. Jesus never lost his temper they can take a lesson from him. Mary continues to work the delicate tissue of the savior. He seems very tense and tight even with the skillful hands of Mary working his back. Hoyt Williams: One of the midgets even challenged me to a fight. Hoyt laughs out loud. Hoyt Williams: Can you imagine an unqualified little person getting into the ring with my greatness? I have fans to think about and financial supporters. We are not going to turn this federation into a pissing match because a gaggle of little people were offended. One even had the gull to call me stupid do you know what I scored on the vocabulary section of the SAT? Mother Mary: I’m sure it was astronomical. Hoyt Williams: Oh you bet your ass it was. Mother Mary: I think your spelling leaves something to be desired. Hoyt Williams: Who needs to spell when you look and speak as perfectly as me? God can’t spell very well. He’s terrible with grammar but then again he did create the world in seven days. Then again I did win the Universal Title while these midgets try to win with a scratch off lotto ticket. Little Lotto no less. But what can you do you can’t please everybody. Mother Mary: No you can’t. Hoyt Williams: Especially your mother. That woman could drive a saint batty. The used to be Virgin Mary slaps Hoyt’s back hand making him jump up for a moment. He then turns around and wrestles Mary softly on top of him. Mother Mary: Hey stud you better save your energy you have a match this week with some kid with blue hair. Hoyt Williams: Blue hair? Mother Mary: As blue as Muddy Waters. Hoyt Williams: MY GOD hair dye is a sign of Satan. A lot people don’t know that but the bible clearly tells us that age is dignified and beautiful. You think anybody would have listened to Moses if he had blue hair??? You think Noah would have gotten anybody on that ark with purple hair??? Mother Mary: Everybody thought Noah was insane and didn’t listen to him anyway. Hoyt Williams: I feel his pain but imagine if he did have purple hair. No animal would have gotten on that Ark. This blue haired kid is going to get it. You don’t think that hair dye rubs off do you? I might have a case of the flu on the horizon. Mother Mary: This coming from a guy who gets his tan from a bottle. SHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Hoyt jumps up off of Mary and quickly shuts all the curtains and the bedroom door. Hoyt Williams: There can be lip readers out there we don’t want the world to know this tan isn’t real. Plus I have good reason the sun causes cancer a tool of Satan. Mother Mary: Well fake or not I think it’s hot. You faced this kid once before he’s into terrible music. Hoyt Williams: Music of Stan which is short for Satan. How anybody could listen to this shit is beyond me. He’s too busy listening to bad music he forgets about our match and sandbags his work. Cute kid, real cute. Mother Mary: What in the name of Jesus’ are you talking about??? Hoyt grabs the remote control and turns on the Jesus approved giant plasma screen TV. He flips until he gets to the MTV which is showing reruns of the Real World. Hoyt Williams: Look at this a perfect example of American youth at play. SO brainwashed by their corporately owned devil funded music that the system is against them and the President is evil they do nothing but sit on their ass drink alcohol and bitch. Kids now in their thirty’s are still living at home because they are corrupted youth. They don’t see the need to find employment and settle down. But why should they with their MTV telling them different. Mother Mary: Preach On. Hoyt Williams: Back in my day the songs were about love and happiness. Mother Mary: When was your day you’re like 24? Hoyt Williams: IT doesn’t matter age its all about soul. The music I enjoy is about heart and the pains that go along with it. Music from the 80’s so ruled. Now its about anger and being physical and corrupt. Evil lurks at the surface of every song yet nobody blinks. It used to hide itself but the creativity of the craft is no longer needed and it makes no regrets or excuses about being what it is. Garbage and evil. Just like Blue boy and all his friends. Mother Mary: Well you’re going to have to teach him a lesson. The phone rings. Hoyt Williams: Hello? Hoyt talks into the blower like a business man would in his office. Meanwhile Mary dreams of the world and raising a child in the scary place it has become. A place where the minority take their way because they can and crap on the values and traditions of the world before that. A world that many wrongs but also many rights. Instead of learning from the past it rewrites itself not heeding any of the warnings. She knows the apocalypses is coming. Does she really want to raise a child in the world of evil?? Then again the child is the third and final coming of Christ. Only time will tell. Hoyt Williams: That was Larry at the real-estate office! We just bought the Antarctic! Imperium is officially a nation. We can now join the United Nations and have a say in global politics. Mother Mary: Please tell me we are not moving to the Artic? Hoyt Williams: Hell no! It is our nation now but SOMEBODY needs to stay here in the states to keep an eye on things. That guy is me. I’m sending Los D to run the operations in the Artic. They are Mexicans don’t Mexicans like the cold or something? Mother Mary: I think they like the heat but better then them us. Hoyt Williams: I can’t wait to tell them they are going to lead the nation while we puppet govern from here. WOW my very own continent! Mother Mary: I bet the kid with blue hair doesn’t have a continent let alone a ride to the show. Hoyt Williams: No his mother will drive him in. They have a love hate relationship like all the other punk yuppie kids. I can’t wait to call President Bush and let him know the good news. Mother Mary: You should offer him the job and let him run our country. You think he would resign here and let Cheney handle things? Hoyt Williams: That’s not a bad idea but I think I’ll let G finish his work here then have him and his father take over for Los D in a few years. Like I said I’ll let my guys run things for awhile. Get a feel of the place. Mother Mary: Do they speak English in the Artic? Hoyt Williams: If they don’t they do now! I wonder if they have cool shopping centers and stuff there. Mother Mary: It’s cold that’s all I know. Hoyt Williams: SO is Chicago and its America’s third largest city. Hey I found out today that Satan is tainting deodorant with cancer causing sinful chemicals. Mother Mary: So we are going to have to stink or die? Hoyt Williams: That’s Satan’s plan but I’ve foiled it the RISP is introducing Imperium brand deodorant. Blessed by God but PH balanced for Sinners. That way everybody can protect themselves from stink. Filthy, filthy, people are bad. Mother Mary: And rabbits. Hoyt Williams: Goes with out saying. Can you finish that message now? Mary goes back to working on Hoyt as the scene fades blue. =w=
