The Twelve Steps of Recovery: Step Three [Part 3 of 12]

22 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 22nd,2010 Intro……   There are 12 steps to recovering from addiction. These 12 steps have helped millions of alcoholics and addicts overcome the demons of addiction. I’ve addressed Step 1 and admitted I was powerless to the disease. I took on Step 2 where I came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity. Now we move onto Step 3 of the 12 steps and how it helped me overcome my addiction to drugs and alcohol.…… May 9th, 2008 One month down, hopefully a lifetime to go. Since entering the Spirit of Life center, I’ve been on a roller coaster of emotions. Imagine that, a big tough son of a bitch like me, being in the front seat of the emotion roller coaster. But I was told that it would be like this. I was told the first month is the hardest, but with hard work and determination, I would over come it….and I did just that. To think, only a month ago, I was lying, stealing and doing what ever it took to get ‘high’. I was selfish and I’ve learned that in the first month here. “Hey kids…. It’s Dad.” I finally got the chance to talk to talk to my family. They still haven’t come to visit, Stacey still needed time. But she’s finally starting to take my phone calls. I really missed the wife, but I was desperate to see the kids. Especially little man. He was only 2 months old when I went into rehab, so now, he’ll spend the next 6 to 9 months not growing up with me in his life. “Hey daddy” Carleigh shouts. “Hi dad” Kenzie says…. Which coming from a 12 year old girl, going on 22, was a pleasant surprise. “googoo” Corbin… remember, he’s only 2 months old. “Where are you Daddy?” That was the hardest question so far. Even harder than when Pastor Gary asked me what I was willing to do to become sober. Even with the years of traveling around the world, I always found time for my girls. And now, here I was, going to be away for six or so months. And it’s been over a month since I got to see them. I was twisted up emotionally inside. “Daddy’s at a place to make himself better” Stacey and I decided that we wouldn’t let the kids know why I was not going to be around, or why they couldn’t see me for a little while. The rules say no family for the first 30 days. And we were quickly approaching that milestone. But we decided the less the kids know about why daddy needed to get better, the better it was for them. “I miss you kids. Can’t wait to see you soon. Is your mom there?” “Sure…. Hold on?” This also is the first time me and Stacey have spoken in almost a month. “Yes Christopher?” She’s still not happy. You could tell it in her voice. “I just wanted to hear your voice” “Girls….say good bye to your dad” And just like that, I knew that it was going to be a struggle to her to trust me, talk to me, love me again, like it was before I become a bigger monster than any gimmick Boda ever was. I was setting out to overcome the hurt I put her through. My addiction had gotten so bad, I couldn’t tell the difference between Boda the pro wrestler, hall of famer, star athlete and Christopher, the husband, father and son. I couldn’t decide who to be, when to be that person or why. “Chris…. Let’s get to the meeting.” Rusty broke the silence. Every week, on Friday’s there was a house meeting followed by an AA meeting. Tonight was special, as I was going to be collecting my first, of many to come, sobriety chips. “Cool, let’s go.” I wasn’t really wanting to go, but it was the rules. And in here, Boda was Chris. Chris couldn’t be Boda in here. In this recovery center, I had to leave Boda where he belonged…. Deep inside me. But as I was doing this recovery thing, trying to get better, I wondered one thing…… Was being Boda the reason I was the way I was? Was being Boda a crutch to party all the time, to not being a father and husband? Was I using my celebrity status to mask some deep down issues? “Tonight, we’ll go over some housekeeping issues.” Pastor Gary starts the weekly house meeting. “The rooms are filthy. One thing I can’t stress enough is that you guys need to make your beds everyday. You need to keep your clothes in the dressers. Clean your showers. I mean come on… you all are grown men. You’re mothers and wives are not here. Take care of your spaces. After tonight’s meeting, we will do drug testing. Anyone that test positive is gone. There is a zero tolerance in this program.” Drug test? I could pass that. Show me some drugs, and I can tell you what they are. Hell, I had a PHD in drugs. “Last thing, before our AA meeting…. Tomorrow will be a bible study with Pastor Lee. Anyone who doesn’t have to work, must attend the class.” Bible study on a Saturday? Fuck. I like to sleep in on Saturdays. Wait…. Boda likes to sleep in on Saturdays….. Chris needs to be in that bible study. Chris needs all the bible he can get, in order to return his sanity and get his family back. Pastor left the classroom/dining room. Troy, one of the staff members walked in and called the meeting to order. “Hello, my name is Troy” “Hi Troy” responds the group. “Good evening and welcome to the Friday Night Spirit of Life AA meeting. I call this meeting to order. This is an open forum, non-smoking, discussion room. We welcome all newcomers and ask that you stand and introduce yourselves.” We ran AA meetings inside the recovery center the way real ones are ran. This was done to help us prepare for the lifetime of recovery ahead of ourselves. Learning how the real world worked was a huge training aide in Spirit of Life. “Okay, since we’ve got no new people, we’ll move onto this week’s sobriety birthdays. And to present the chips, Rusty.” Rusty stands and holds some coins his hands. “Here in AA we use a chip system to mark sobriety anniversaries. The first coin is this white one, which denotes 24 hours. It’s given to those that want to turn their life over to a sober way of living. Is there anyone that is celebrating 24 hours sober?” We’ve not had any new guys in the last week, so there are no takers on the 1 day coin. Rusty moves on to the next coin. “Okay, moving on…. Anyone celebrating 1month of sobriety.” I stood up, and the crowd chanted my name BODA BODA BODA BODA Just playing. I did receive around of applause. And to tell you the truth, this first thirty days clean was probably going to be the hardest. After all…. I was used to doing what I wanted. When I wanted. However I wanted. But being in recovery meant I had to play by the rules. I had to be responsible for my action and accountable to others. “Well, Champ….” Rusty and the others tagged me with this nickname, knowing that my long term goals include winning championship gold again sometime after I get over control of this disease of addiction. “You’re probably the biggest addict I’ll ever have the pleasure of giving a coin to. Congrats Chris” “Thanks Rusty” I returned to my seat. And like the gold belts I’ve worn in my career, this little gold coin, about the size of a half dollar, meant so much to me. It meant I was on the right track to getting my life back. It meant I was on course to stepping back into the squared circle on day, and maybe, just maybe, wearing a championship again. But I was still a long way away from that point. Rusty finally gave out the last of the chips for tonight. It was time to move onto the open discussion part of the meeting as Troy takes center seat. “Again, congratulations to all those that received sobriety chips tonight. For those that didn’t, keep coming back. So, are there any members that have a topic they want to talk about tonight?” “Sure…I’ve got a topic” I replied. Now, up to this point, I’ve always sat back quietly just listening in, never sharing anything. At this point, I don’t know if it was because I was a world class athlete, reduced to do rehab in a two bit crack motel, or if it was because I was scared because I was a world class athlete, reduced to doing rehab in a two bit crack motel. But finally, I was coming out of what ever shell I was in. “ I want to talk about the 3rd step of AA. ‘Made a decision to turn our will and our lives over to the care of God as we understood Him’. I want to know what you all think about what it means. I mean…. I know what the ‘turning our will and lives over to the care of God means‘…. But what about the ‘as we understood Him’ part of that sentence?” You could hear the wheels turning in the room full of addicts, cons and miscreants. One of the older residents, and by older I meant been in the center longer than I have been, gives his two cents. “Well, what the old timers meant was if you decided to worship and praise the door knob on your door, then that was your GOD as you understood Him. It simply means that everyone has a different theory on God, but the over all meaning of the 3rd step is that if you give your life and will and all that it entails to the God of your choice, then recovery will come easily. Does that help?” “Well…. Sure.” I began my response. “I get it. Back when I was at the top of my game…. Top of the world…. The Omega and The Alpha in the squared circle, everyone looked to me as THE GOD of Wrestling. And I looked at the titles I owned, the championships I won and worshiped them. They were what I set my standard of excellence upon. So, in a way, the gold I won and wore was my GOD. And I did what ever it took and gave my life and will to win all the gold belts and championships I could. So yeah… I get it. But what makes turning my will and life over to the care of GOD going to keep me sober?” Another one of the elder residents puts his thoughts out in the open. “You see Chris. As you’ll learn in the coming weeks of recovery, that for so many years, you’ve done everything in your life as how you wanted to do them. You made all decisions. You wore all emotions on your sleeve. Hell, everyone of us here have. But, it was hard wasn’t it?” I nodded my head in approval. “You made unconsciously bad decisions because you were in control of your life….and it got you where? Smack dab in the middle of a 10 month rehab stint with others just like you. But you see…. The millions before you and us that have been in AA and have over come addiction…. They’ve done it because they let GOD take control of their life and their wills.” I cleared my throat as I thought about what I was just schooled on. “Yeah I get it. I do. For years, I let the Boda character I portrayed take over the will and care of my life. I let the bad decisions affect the outcomes of my actions. And I need to change that. I need to let go and let GOD. I need to say fuck it…. God, please take over my life…. God, I hand you my will, for I make bad decisions and let the negative issues affect me negatively. I need God to control all aspects of my life. So yeah…. Why not. I never ran my life right before, so why not hire a new life manager…. The big man upstairs himself…. GOD.” I suddenly felt a weight being removed from my chest. As if suddenly everything in my life was made better. I’d felt under the pressure of that weight for so long. I think things were starting to look good for me beating this disease of addiction. PRESENT DAY….LEADING UP TO COLOSSUS LOCATION: ORLANDO FLORIDA,THE SLOBODA RESIDENCE For weeks I pulled off a masterpiece. I played with the heart strings of the fans of PRIME. I made you all want to like me, respect me and cheer for me. But I did it because I was testing what I had learning in rehab. I gave God everything. I let him guide the choices I made. I told him I wanted to be a champion again…. Scratch that….I needed to be a champion again. Devin Shakur and I came up with a plan. I left the care and will of that plan in the hands of God as I understood him. I played some dirty cards in that game…. But it all worked out as I planned. And I have to thank God for the help in making it happen. “So… you ready brother?” David said from the kitchen. He was making nachos and grabbing me an un-sweet ice tea. “What’s the score of the game?” “13 all. Bucs ball.” I replied. I’d become a pretty big Tampa Bay Bucs fan over the years. Growing up a Buffalo Bills fan left me wanting more. “Of course I’m ready. I was born to be a champion. And there’s no way in hell I am letting that douche knocker The First take this title away from me.” David’s laugh reverberates from the kitchen. “I meant, are you ready for some nachos?” “You’re damn right I’m ready. Are you ready to make your PRIME pay per view debut?” David hands me a plate of nachos and my iced tea. “Damn Skippy bro. You know I have your back.” “I know, I know. But come on… I’m not the one that will need someone to have their back at Colossus. It will be Brian Nadalny.” I smiled as I picked up a nacho covered in Mexican cheese and hot spicy salsa. “Who?” I slid the nacho in my mouth and washed it down with the iced tea. “My opponent. The First. His real name is Brian. He’s the one who’ll need to have his back watched by someone. Hell, he’d be better off standing a chance against me by turning HIS will and life over to the care of God. But then again… here’s a man, who believes his soul has been around for some 6000 fucking years. So maybe I do need your help Dave. I mean after all…. How can I, a mere man who’s overcome cancer, drug addiction beat a man whose survived 6 millenniums on this earth in some form or another.” “WOOHOO…. Another touchdown. Bucs looking good tonight” David pipes in. I was in mid rant, so I missed the brilliant pass. Bah, it’s only preseason so I don’t want to get my hopes up. “You were saying?” “I was saying that who the hell does Brian ’The First’ think he is? He may have been the first soul ever, but he’s still half the man I am. He’s won what? 1/6th of the titles I’ve worn in my career? He’s been in PRIME but a minute and gets his first shot at gold against who? Me…. And that’s why he needs to pray to God that I cut him quickly at Colossus.” “Hell Chris… If I was The First and I was facing you in a first blood match, I’d cut myself shaving just prior to coming to the ring and forfeit. It’d be easier on him.” David chuckles, knowing what the near future holds for The First. “I feel you bother. I’m not even sweating this Colossus match. I am so much better than The First. I’ve given my blood sweat and tears for this company before, and at Colossus….I’ll give them it all again… Expect the blood" THE END
