Oh dear.

Date: Vs Rezz 'Click Click Click' The newtons cradle that sat atop the desk rocked gently from side to side, although it was in theory supposed to continue forever it was never the case occasionally it needed to be started again. Whether the theory of perpetual motion was a bust, or if it was just a flimsy office toy it didn't matter. It provided the necessary distraction that was needed in a quiet office. 'Click Click Click' Steve, the man simply known as the man in the black suit had been sat there for around an hour and a half staring at the cradle as it rocked back and forth and had been taking scope of his life. To get where he had within the company he had been required to sacrifice everything, to be part of the company was to be a part of their family. Everything else took second place, your health your life and your family. Steve had thought he had been clever in getting his daughter actively involved within the company, it wasn't until a few years in when they had first threatened him, by harming her that the desperation of the situation had hit him. 'Click Click Click' It wasn't like she could just leave either, once you were in there was no real way out apart from one that drastically shortened your life expectancy. As a result he had tried to push her as far away as possible. It just seemed easier that way. If he thought of Claire as an employee rather than a daughter the things he asked of her were somehow easier, if he could forget the fact that she had feelings, that she was once his little girl everything felt that little bit better. Still there were times when he couldn't help but be a dad and he had spent the best part of this morning staring at photo albums from years gone by, rare photos of holidays when she was a child when it had been permitted by the company fond memories which he had to keep to himself, because they were more than capable than taking them away from him. 'Click Click Click' Steve stared down at the dossier in front of him and opened it carefully, pulling out a single piece of paper with details all through it, vital statistics and in the top left hand corner a photograph paper clipped squarely against the edges. He detached the picture and rose it to his eye line squinting at the image carefully before shaking his head and placing back in the document. Of all the things he had asked his daughter to do, this was the most ridiculous and dangerous thing yet. It made no sense to go down this route, yet he wouldn't dare question it. Much in the same way that Claire would not really question him, not unless it was in the spiteful way a daughter generally did. Noticing that the Newtons Cradle had stopped he extended his arm to start it again however was interrupted by a knock on the office door. “Come in” He had previously been slouching in his chair however before he motioned for entry he adjusted his position to one of power and authority, the subtle changes like sitting bolt up right and he also found that playing with a pen helped as well. The door to his office opened and in stepped Claire, the nervous almost fearful look in her eyes always cut through him to his very core every time he saw it, however he knew that if she was scared he was doing his job and that's all that mattered to the family. Regardless of his paternal instincts to go and tell her that he loved her and everything was going to be ok. “You wanted to see me?” Wearing a blue suit Claire stood in front of Steve, almost standing at attention, her hands held tightly behind her back possibly to hide a clenched fist of frustration “Certainly, sit down.” Steve smiled and gestured to his daughter Claire who he could tell was sneering inside. She sat down slowly her eyes immediately falling to the file on the desk and the gently rocking Newtons Cradle which had stopped clicking. Steve also followed Claire's eyes and new that she was all ready for business. Secretly though he wanted to know about her, about her life and what was happening within it. However as soon as that thought had entered his head he knew the answer. Nothing When you work for the company you have no life not really, not unless they become a part of the company too. If anyone got too close, it could blow everything out of the water. “So how have you been?” Steve asked a small smile on his face, it had been intended as a calm warming smile, however in the years of his position within the company any facial reactions had become forced, so when they were sincere there was no way to tell. Even Steve wasn't too sure when he was being sincere any more. “Do you really think that's appropriate talk?” It was as cutting a blow as Claire could muster. It was harder for Claire than her father, or so she thought at least. Claire had always idolised her father as a child, sure he was never around that much but when he was it was the best time in the world. He would take her on trips to the beach, pony riding lessons. She never went asking for anything and most of all she knew that her father loved her and would do anything for her. It's funny how time changes your perception on things. Now whenever she looked at him, she didn't see a father but simply a boss figure. Sure her father was in there somewhere, but wrapped up in twenty years of lies and deceit. Eventually it all ate away at you. “Can't I ask how you are now?” Steve had always known how fiery Claire was, but with every day that passed he sensed his little girl was becoming just another member of the company “You gave up that right long ago.” Another cutting line from Claire indicated to Steve that today was not going to be the day for his dream reunion. As a result he decided to prevent further heartache and continue with the reason that he had summoned her here. “Fine, if you are going to be like that. Lets continue. First of all Chris, how is he doing after the second dosage. Has there been any side effects of any sort? Is he showing any signs of resilience to the drug?” The second dosage had been experimental and the first field test had been with Chris at Ultra Violence, he had obviously been able to note that there was no major physical break down as the performance had been deemed adequate. However Claire was around him far more than anyone else so she would be the key to Chris' internal psyche. He wanted to know if there was anything that would be able to be traced back to them after his previous attempt to quit, he wanted to know more importantly if the drone aspect of the drug had worked. If he had been able to create someone who was totally susceptible to suggestion. “Nothing obvious, although one thing is bothering me. Not once since the drugs have been administered has he mentioned his family. Its as though they have been wiped from his memory. Other than that there has been no signs of anything.” Claire was struggling with the conversation, whenever she received the call that her father wanted to see her her stomach twisted in a knot. Seeing him just reminded her of the life she once had. “That's good at least and anything physical. We're not going to have another hospital episode are we?” “Not having a doctorate in Medicine I couldn't say” “What about his mind set? How is he feeling?” “Other than being disappointed at himself for losing at Ultra violence he's fine and ready and raring to go.” “Good, always reassuring to here. Now that we have fully infiltrated PRIME, the company wants to spread its wings a little within, see if we can get some others on board for our little venture and they have decided upon the next target. However you want to approach the subject is down to you however....” Steve paused and glanced around, almost fearful that he would be overheard, despite knowing that his office was relatively secure. “I say this as a father Claire. Please be careful with this one. Chris was one thing, a wide eyed college kid who we could mould into whatever we chose, this one....lets just say he's a little more unstable. Please, take care.” Steve slid the file across towards Claire. Slowly she opened the file as her eyes slid to the photo in the top left hand corner. Without uttering a word she placed the file on the table and left the office. Sitting on the edge of a bench, Meat's chest heaved up and down as he took in air, he loved this he felt better than he could ever remember and he felt strong. Claire seemed to be ok with everything as well and life was generally good. All he needed to do was get back into the swing of things and everything would be fine.
