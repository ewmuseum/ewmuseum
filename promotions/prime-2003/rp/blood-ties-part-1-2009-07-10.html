Blood Ties Part 1

10 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/07/09 Steve was feeling extremely good about himself as he took a long hard sip of his coffee, it was clay cold but it was only the dregs in the bottom of the cup anyway. After all the success of today meant that his stake within the company was finally going to be rekindled. After months of hardship things were finally coming good for him. It also meant that the pressure on his daughter would be lifted. The latest target had been acquired and the company was extremely happy with the progress that had been made. Hell there had been word that a promotion was on the cards. Impega appreciated success and rewarded it suitably. Releasing a happy sigh, Steve found himself distracted as he looked down and noticed a small spot of blood on his desk. Lowering his hand towards it he ran his finger over it and smeared it across his desk, it was still wet. Raising his hand to his nose, he became startled as a constant flow of blood was falling freely from his nostrils. “Typical, trust me to get a nosebleed before a meeting. This won't wash out of my suit.” Steve grumbled to himself as he rose to his feet and removed the plain black jacket, which he always wore. It symbolised who he was and almost felt naked without it, still he couldn't afford to get any more blood on it. As he stood though he looked down and noticed a wet patch to the front of his trousers. Lowering his hand he felt the damp patch and with fear pulled his hand back, yet more blood. A crippling pain shot through his entire body causing him to crease in two and fall backwards into his chair, his entire body being attacked by a combination of dull aching and sharp pains which combined to leave him feeling crippled. Although he couldn't notice it himself, blood was now beginning to free flow from every orifice. Letting out an agonised scream as his vision became red, the blood flowing through his eye sockets obscuring his view. Every passing second felt like an eternity of pain as he felt the life quite literally drip out of him. As he collapsed forwards on the desk his head ricocheted heavily off it causing him to slump to the side. His chest heaving up and down heavily. There was so much in his life that had not done. He had never seen the northern lights, he had never watched a sunrise at dawn but most importantly he had not sorted things out with his child. The child whom he had always truly loved. As his chest rose and fell for the last time, Steve didn't attempt to call for help, now did he cry but remained quiet and indignant and embraced the sweet release of death. With his dying breath he thought of Claire. 'I love you.' The room fell silent as the body lay sprawled across the desk, only the dull hum of the air conditioning unit overhead prevented the room from being immersed in total silence and that was the way it remained for two hours. The only thing that broke the almost silence was the knocking on the office door, it had been going on for sometime now, however for the first time the door was attempted and opened freely. A curious looking man, no more than twenty-five peered around the corner of the door, as a general rule you didn't enter Steve's office unless you were invited, but Impega worked with a hierarchy of fear so when the order came from above Steve....well the maths spoke for itself. “Sir, you are required at the 6 pm meeting.” The man's voice was filled with inflection as he struggled to control his nervousness. This was quickly replaced however by abject fear when he noticed the pool of blood which Steve was lying face down in. “Sir?” In his heart of hearts he knew he was dead, there was no need for the second sir, it was more of a reaction than anything else. The fear had consumed him totally, but could not pull his eyes away from the macabre scene that lay before him. Using every nuance within his soul he managed to cry for help. “WE NEED SOMEONE IN HERE NOW!” Earlier That Day Sat in his office Steve looked at his diary at the day ahead. A hot steaming cup of coffee sat next to him for the day ahead. His eyes were red and clearly not awake yet, running on autopilot. Regardless of a persons job, that was something that always seemed to catch up with you sooner or later, tiredness and fatigue. The one cure for it, a good hot cup of coffee. The diary ahead of him was basically empty, he had a meeting at six pm in which he would have to grovel to his superiors for more time, but that was essentially the same everyday. A knock on the door broke Steve away from his train thought as he sighed casually and called out to the other side of his office, sipping the coffee which had been tempting him for so long. “Come in.” As the door opened Steve still hadn't paid attention to whoever was now stood before him, his mind and focus almost solely on the warm brown liquid that was flowing down his neck and making the very fibres of his being tingle. “Nothing better than a good cup of coffee is there? So what can I do for.....” Steve was stopped mid-sentence as he looked up at his visitor. For a moment there was little more than silence in the room. As the two eyed each other up, taking stock of each other. Slowly but confidently Steve placed his coffee cup down on his desk and allowed a smile to form on his face. “I have to say. I didn't expect to see you today. It has been sometime after all.” Steve gestured for the person in front of him to sit down, they don't however and remain stood quite still, looking down at Steve, who in turn was still smiling quite happily. “I have the tendency to show up when I'm not expected. “So I take it you're here for a reason.” Once again, Steve gestured for his visitor to sit, this time however they relented and sat down opposite Steve, placing their hands on the table casually leaning on the rich wood. “If you give me a reason to be here, then I might just.” The visitor replied, not wanting to give any quarter or inch. “Of course. As you know we here at Impega are moving forwards leaps and bounds in the field of chemical research. Drugs so powerful that they can increase strength by almost one hundred percent, with very little in the way of damage.” “Go on....” The visitor was trying to maintain their reserve, but the intrigue in their voice was more than apparent, something interested them with the words that Steve was saying. Possibly the prospect of increased strength, and for very little effort. “Minimal side effects of course, but we struggle with field runs as I'm sure you can appreciate, however what if it was untraceable? If there was no way for anyone to know about the drug.” Steve paused for a moment at this point. There was no way to tell if the drug was untraceable, not without full tests in the field, the first batch had proved successful, but the side-effects were far more horrific than h e cared to remember. Reaching into his pocket, he pulled out a small silver container and placed it on the table opening it facing the other person in his office. “What if this little pill, could change everything?” The person leant forwards and picked up the pill and holding it between their thumb and forefinger looked at it carefully, just a simple white tablet, that's all it was no different in look to your average paracetamol. Steve had risen to his feet and was now staring out of his office window. In his mind his world was picking up, he genuinely hadn't expected to see them today, especially not at this time in the morning, now as he looked out over the city, the world suddenly became a brighter place. “For years man has wanted to better themselves, but man is lazy and is always looking for a short cut. What if we could provide that? What if we were able to take the fundamental elements of what Man wants and present it to them in an easily accessible way....” As Steve continued his speech as he surveyed the world below him. The visitor was making themselves busy as well. Making sure that Steve's gaze was averted, he snapped the pill in half and dropped it into Steve's coffee. Initially panic spread as the tablet began to float, however as Steve rambled on, the white pill began to dissolve and sank into the depths of the brown liquid. Reaching into their pocket, they then proceeded to pull out a similar white pill and placed it back where the apparent wonder drug had once been. “So what do you say?” A pause, a long silent pause as Steve returns his gaze to his visitor and sits back down at his desk and proceeds to take a large gulp of the coffee, sighing contently as he does so. The visitor smiles to themselves before standing up in front of Steve and turning towards the door. “I will have to think about it, the pill....are you sure it's harmless?” “Certainly.” “Hmmmm” “What do you mean by that?” “Nothing, just I heard otherwise. I will have to think about it. I will get back in touch with your superiors.” “Surely you can report back to me?” There was not another word spoken between the two as the door was shut silently behind them, leaving Steve somewhat perplexed sat as his desk, but mostly happy. He hadn't said no and there was definite interest in the product. Taking another large gulp of coffee Steve shut the diary on the desk as well as the silver container, which housed the pill. “Yep, today is going to be a good day.
