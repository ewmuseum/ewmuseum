Into The Abyss: Part VIII - Angel With The Broken Halo

2 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-03-09 Writhing on the four-poster bed of royal blue satin she clutched her stomach and whimpered as her guts wrenched, filling her body with a new kind of pain. Withdrawal symptoms. The very nature of those words scared her, more so now that she was experiencing them. A splitting headache shot through her skull and a foul smelling, greasy secretion perspired from every pore in her body. The drug was leaving her system and it felt like it was destroying her nerves on the way out, as her legs stiffened with cramps and her arms shook violently as she hugged herself. Tears streamed down her face and had she wore mascara the sheets would be stained through with black make-up. Catching a glimpse of herself in a full-length mirror Gloria groaned at the state of her face, reflected as an etiolated skull. Her eyes were the blackest they had been, bloodshot and ravaged by drugs. Her sunken cheeks were greyish and her lips verged on the kind of blue reserved for the dead. She buried her face into the sheets and heaved up a thick yellow bile from deep within her guts. She sobbed, gripping hold of the mattress and squeezing when the pain became too much to bear, and at times when it travelled from her torso to her groin she felt great humility at desecrating her luxurious surroundings. Violence Jack had offered a room in the east wing of the Manor reserved for those of great importance, and as a companion of Von Kelsig she was just that. The bedroom had a royal ambiance with high maroon walls and a plain white ceiling, the centrepiece of which was a golden chandelier decorated with crystals stemming from its fixing to the low-lit lights. Large oil paintings of Cthulhu captured in poses of dominance and destruction over great civilizations and the universe alike adorned the walls between lights that resembled miniature versions of the great chandelier hanging above. Before her body had succumb to drug withdrawal she had sat with Shanahan in one of the sitting rooms on a golden chair cushioned with red suede in front of a great fireplace. His features were licked by shadow and light as the flames danced and popped from the logs burning away in the pit, and he had the subtle qualities of a demon about him. He and the many others Gloria discovered living in the Manor were all clad in those black ceremonial robes, some were silent and others who bore the symbols of power gave orders and discussed matters between themselves, never taking much notice of Gloria. She knew what this place was, but she trusted Von Kelsig enough not to care. "I do not involve myself with such obsequiously ill-mannered business. How did Von Kelsig come to be there?" Jack asked, hands clasped on his lap and legs crossed as Gloria stared into the flames, basking in the warmth. "Something to do with an old debt. I remember hearing he was running a bar in town and that's when they took him..." Gloria began. "Yes...a club as I recall. Von Kelsig approached me with the desire for a loan, with promises of a share in profit. He repaid me eventually and I heard nothing more of the venture...I assume what you're telling me is the reason being?" Jack interjected. "Yeah, he was kidnapped." Gloria replied. "...Do you know who is holding him?" Jack asked, squinting his eyes at the wild-haired addict. "His name is Reuben Blanco, a big time crook. He runs his operation south of the border and wants to expand into the States; he deals in some shady business. I was his...his lackey." Her eyes welled up and in the firelight they shone like diamonds. "What is your part in all of this? Of what importance are you to Von Kelsig?" Bruce gestured to Gloria, pointing a ringed finger at her. The question brought about a flurry of butterflies in her gut and her lip quivered trying to hold back the smile as she wiped the tears from her eyes. "I...I think we need each other. I mean, we sort of found ourselves through each other. He told me I was worth saving and I-..." She paused. "I tried to help him escape." "Which brings us here..." said Bruce. "I don't know, I-...we...the two of us have a connection somehow. We rely on each other...we-..." Gloria was snapped from her fantasy by stark reality. "-...You are dependent on him as you are drugs?" Shanahan spoke softly, those words enough to kill the butterflies and bring back the sorrow. Clutching her elbow, Gloria gazed at the floor embarrassedly as he observed her. "I can offer no help with regards to his situation, but I can keep you for as long as it takes Von Kelsig to find his way. You are welcome to the guest room in the east wing, and any one of my people will help you with anything you need." He rose from his chair and left Gloria to lose herself in the firelight. Her stomach began rumbling shortly after, then it contorted and broke into spasms that left her doubling over in pain and she struggled to her room, accompanied by one of Jack’s hooded accomplices. Adamant she would not lower herself to the need of the drugs she locked herself in and began suffering...for him... ~~~~oOOo~~~~ The stench of disinfectant lingered in the air, invading his nostrils with every faint breath he took. His eyelids flickered rapidly against the light that shone brightly from above as he struggled to open his eyes. The peace and tranquillity of the semi-coma he never knew he was in to the awakening of all his senses was bridged by the rippling pain through his nerves. The thrumming aches were much duller now than the sharp cracks of excruciating pain that tore through his body, and now as he lay on the same stretcher he had woken up on after he had been shot he saw just how far into the depths he had plumbed. Barring a well-worn pair of grey boxer shorts he was naked on the gurney. He noticed the dark red stains had disappeared and realized the bandages on his right arm and stomach had been changed. A paper sheet lay draped over his lower half, on which sat a metal tray complete with surgical tools and a couple of syringes. The sight of the syringes drew his attention to his left arm and to his dismay he noticed a tiny pinprick where the needle had penetrated. He refused to believe it, but on trying to clear his head he found the cloudy haze that weighed on his mind wouldn't budge. The serenity of his sleep had been the work of that chemical hell that had been pumped into him yet again. Attracted now by the throbbing in his hands he peered down at them. They were bound by leather straps and completely bandaged as they had been after being cooked like pork sausages on a neglected barbecue. The dressings had the mark of professionalism given the tight and neat bondage to his broken hands. The good doctor Menendez was to thank for that, while Blanco provided the dope. Von Kelsig rested his head and felt the muscles in his face twitch while his mouth slackened. He drew no breath for words, uttering only silence that spoke volumes in the confines of his psyche. The great column of hope that had been building high with the help of Gloria had been toppled, broken from its foundation of dependency and reduced to rubble. His moment of glory had been stolen away and a sickly husk of a man lay in place of the raging monster that should have been. "Feeling better are we?" The voice came from behind a curtain hanging between two coat stands that stood between him and the door. A head of thin hair greased back with too much wax bobbed behind it before a figure emerged, moving to his side. Reuben. He tried to summon the strength to rise up and destroy his tormentor, but the notion passed as a twitch in the corner of his eye. "Menendez works wonders with the tools he's given. Unfortunately there's a shortage of anaesthetic so we had to make do with a little kit." Reuben remarked dryly, removing the tray from Von Kelsig's lap and dropping it. The clatter startled him. "Have you considered agreeing to my proposal yet?" He asked rhetorically as Von Kelsig gazed up at him through glazed eyes. "Of course you haven't..." He continued. "So I'm going to decide for you." Taking hold of one of the leather straps, Reuben unbuckled it and allowed it to fall to the side of the trolley. Von Kelsig thought he was hallucinating from the heroin. Blinking in disbelief he watched as Reuben made his way around the stretcher freeing his limbs from the straps before removing the four thicker bonds across his chest. Standing back with his hands out by his sides, Reuben shot a smirk at Von Kelsig. "You're free to go." The shock hit him like his first injection. "What?" Von Kelsig was stupefied at the revelation. "I said you're free to go. There will be no more injections, no more violence. You don't even have to sign anything over to me...yet. Just get out of here. I've arranged a car to take you where you need to go." Reuben added, while the mystified look on Von Kelsig's face amused him slightly. ".........What the fuck is this?" Von Kelsig demanded, slowly sitting up and keeping his hands elevated as the throbbing pain grew stronger with more blood pumping through his veins. "Nothing," Reuben replied directly. "I've come to a conclusion. My methods have failed miserably and you are a better man than I took you for. You may go." Von Kelsig swivelled on the gurney and hung his feet over the side, resting them on the cold floor as he tried to comprehend what was happening here. "Give me one good reason why I shouldn't kill you where you stand?" He said threateningly to Blanco, who chuckled at the thought. "I could put a bullet in your head before you take two steps towards me. Moreover I'm letting you go safe in the knowledge you won't go to anyone about your time here, least of all the authorities..." "What makes you so sure?" Von Kelsig butted in. "What makes you so untouchable?? Why are you releasing me?" "You have no idea where you've been held for the past several weeks Von Kelsig. You know nothing that can't be changed or erased. I on the other hand know more than enough about you to quite happily let you go off into the big bad world. There's only...one...stipulation, mind." Reuben growled. "What?" Von Kelsig enquired, curious as to where Reuben was going with this. "You're going to go back to doing what you do best, go back to your day job. Forget that pipe dream of being an entrepreneur, stick to what you know best. You'll be handing over a significant cut of your wages as a way of repaying your debt. Signing over your business would remove a large chunk of said debt, which is why I'm leaving the decision to you." Reuben concluded. "...And just what makes you think I'll agree to any of this?" Von Kelsig snarled. With that, Blanco took a step towards Von Kelsig, leaning into his victim and clasping both hands behind him. With a polite smile and an innocent look on his face he whispered something into Von Kelsig's ear that turned the giant's face from a roiled fixed look to a portrait of horror. Stepping back, Reuben cocked an eyebrow as Von Kelsig got to his feet, holding his hands to his heart and staring at the floor as though he had been dealt a life sentence. "Ok...ok you win. I'll do it. I'll sign over my business. I'll do what you ask. I'll go. You win." Tears welled in his eyes as he agreed to Reuben's terms. "I knew you'd see things my way eventually..." Reuben trailed off as he turned and made his way from the room, leaving Von Kelsig a shattered wreck. ~~~~oOOo~~~~ Another day passed before Von Kelsig could snap himself from the comprehension of what was happening. After his hands were re-bandaged to allow movement of the fingers and some semblance of grip, he left the confines of the torture chamber in which he had been imprisoned for the past several weeks and followed a man named Yousef downstairs. As they made their way along a hallway on the first floor past a series of doors, Yousef stopped him and entered one, emerging moments later with two duffel bags. Reuben had kept most of his and indeed Gloria's possessions and requested they be returned to Von Kelsig as he was led out of the building into the waiting Land Rover Discovery. When asked where he wanted to go, Von Kelsig sat up in the back seat of the 4x4 and realized all at once what was happening. There was no way Reuben was releasing him without having some kind of stranglehold on him. This was it. Von Kelsig knew Reuben had nothing on him, except for that emaciated angel lying in wait in Vermont. He was being delivered to her though he wasn't to know it, and then Blanco would make his move. It was clear now. Thinking desperately of a location where he could be set free and get to Gloria without being followed, Von Kelsig struggled to come up with any place close enough. New York was too far south and New Hampshire was no better. Still...no one knew where Wyatt Manor was unless directed those who had visited its haunting realm. "Montpelier, Vermont." He told his driver, Yousef. With that they drove off, and from the window of his office on the second floor Reuben watched as the Discovery disappeared along the street. His chubby face was stuck in a sinister smile as he rubbed his hands together and licked his lips. John Doe, the mysterious man in the fedora stood by his side. Together they gazed out of the window before finally turning to each other. "I am indebted to you, sir." Reuben said, bowing his head. "Of course you are Blanco." Doe replied. "When this all comes together I want to be informed personally so that we may continue our business...for now though I have other pressing matters to attend to..." With that John Doe parted ways with Reuben, leaving the gigantic slovenly man to brood in his office over business of his own. ~~~~oOOo~~~~ When he arrived in Montpelier he said nothing to the driver as he left the Discovery and disappeared into the first network of side streets he could find, unaware that Yousef was under the gun and ordered to shadow him. Emerging back onto a main street some way across town, Von Kelsig hailed a cab to take him to the nearest town to Brayston. Once there he made his way on foot over rolling hills and through rain and thunder and occasional sunshine soon came to Brayston. He had been regaled in the past with tales of the small town nestled at the foot of a dead forest beyond which lay the territory of the unspoken demons. Avoiding the town altogether Von Kelsig skirted its border until he came to the woods and disappeared beyond the tree line. From the town square cries had risen upon the sight of a beast crossing the fields at the edge of town and heading into the woods, and for a week after a nightly vigil was kept by the community leaders who formed a barrier around the town, expecting hell and hoping for nothing. Traversing the barren wood, his heart skipped a beat as he emerged on the other side to find the Manor sitting before him atop one last hill. He increased his pace, almost sprinting up with the help of the adrenaline that was pumping through his veins. As he hopped over the broken wrought-iron fence he noticed the front door of the Manor opening and the figure of Violence Jack emerging with arms folded and all but the smile itself adorning his face. Slowing his pace down at the realization he must have looked like a clumsy St. Bernard bounding up the path, Von Kelsig nodded respectfully to his acquaintance and walked calmly past the statues of R'yleh and Cthulhu and markings which read "Ph'nglui mglw'nafh Cthulhu R'lyeh wgah'nagl fhtagn". How apt given the Manor and its Lord he was approaching, he thought. A hundred yards or so from the door Von Kelsig noticed a dainty pale hand wrapping around the frame, and a familiar face peering out. It had changed somewhat from the last time he had seen her, her eyes were darker and her face groggy and discoloured though her hair was still as bright and wild as he remembered it. Gloria. Von Kelsig ran forward, arms outstretched as Gloria stumbled past Violence Jack and stood on the step, not quite sure what to think as the seven foot saviour came towards her. Her eyes welled up and tears streamed down her face as Von Kelsig scooped her up in his arms and embraced her skeletal frame with a smile. For a moment they lost themselves in the embrace, reunited finally after such torment. Letting the wild-haired addict down, Von Kelsig sighed with relief and turned to Jack. And with that Von Kelsig took her by the hand as Jack led them into the realm of Wyatt Manor. They had survived their hellish ordeal and at the end of it only one thing mattered to Von Kelsig. He had his angel with the broken halo.
