Wiping her good eye dry, Gloria pushed down on the mattress and forced herself to sit up, fighting the dull throbbing throughout her torso to rest against the headboard. Compared to her stacked side, jam-packed with outfits his side was sparse with only a few shirts and trousers occupying the space. It had been like that for months, and why she never attributed that to his state of mind she had no idea. There before her hung the greatest evidence that this was never going to work out. More tears welled up in her eyes as she pulled her entire wardrobe out and threw the clothes on the bed before searching for enough cases to contain her belongings. She packed as though she were an automaton, never once stopping to reconsider or ponder. ---- Sighing heavily, Von Kelsig kicked at the debris still strewn throughout his bar. He didn't plan on cleaning anything up, not when he was jetting back out to Boston later that night. He had been up since the early morning, with nothing to do but pace back and forth throughout the house from empty room to empty room. It barely felt like home any more. In fact the only reason it ever felt like home was being kicked out that night. With time to reflect he realized he was suited to life on the road after all, and this entire episode was testament to that. He pondered just selling the house altogether, or even renting it out. There wasn't much left on the mortgage to pay, somewhere in the region of the tens of thousands, but if all went well at Colossus he'd be able to pay it off in one go and get back out on the dusty trail away from all the lies and the let-downs. Grabbing his cell phone he left the bar, slamming the door and not bothering to go back and shut it properly when it jumped ajar. Walking out into the warm glow of the late morning sun he scrolled through the numbers in his phonebook. Why he chose to settle on the name he picked he couldn't tell, but to hell with it. Any voice was better than the noise echoing in his head, of Gloria groaning halfway to an orgasm. "Yeah? What do you want?" The voice growled down the line. The fans knew him as Chainz, but to Von Kelsig... "Sloan, it's me. Listen, you anywhere near Boston yet?" He replied, staring at the ground and kicking the dust. "I arrived a bit early to take in the sights. Why?" "Some pretty major shit just went down. I'm in Tennessee right now, but I'll be flying back out to tonight. First thing I'm doing when I touch down is hitting the town, you down for a session?" Von Kelsig asked, rather bluntly. Sloan pondered the notion in his head, though he was quick to answer. "Well well, I didn't think you liked me very much. What's the matter, ain't get enough at home from the missus?" Von Kelsig held his breath, immediately riled by the comment. He knew Michael had no idea of his situation and passed it off as a simple ribbing between colleagues. Scratching his hand and looking visibly agitated by it nevertheless he took another breath and blanked Sloan outright. "So, I should be in town around nine thereabouts, I'll meet you at the airport if you're game. And I like you just fine, if your work ethic translates to your personal life you're ideal drinking buddy material..." "Alright big man," Michael continued, "I was about to head out and see what Boston had to offer anyway. I guess you're as good a drinking companion as any. Give me a call when you land." He said, almost morose. Von Kelsig managed a smile, grateful for a break at a time like this. "Done. Oh, one more thing." "Yeah?" "Don't bring the wife." Von Kelsig replied, getting in a little jibe of his own while at the same time warning off any feminine distractions that could potentially dredge up and complicate his thought process. "I never do." Sloan quipped stoically. Hanging up abruptly, Von Kelsig breathed a sigh of relief and turned back to the house. Stopping at the front step he stared into the glass of the porthole window in the door and bit his lip. He couldn't see where he wanted to be past his own reflection. That ghostly image stood in the way of him walking into a family home full of love and happiness. Instead he pushed the door open and breathed in the stale smell of all the combined elements of the last twenty four hours. Alcohol and sex and the weak scent of Gloria's perfume spoiled the air as he walked back into the house and wandered into the kitchen, sitting by the breakfast bar and resting his head on folded arms. ---- Plopping a glob of gel onto his hands, Larry DeLancy ran his fingers through his streaky blonde hair, thoroughly working it into style. Eyes locked on the mirror, he occasionally glanced at his bright blue eyes and smirked a cocky smirk. Examining his stubble he pouted and judged his overall look, snapping his collar straight and winking at his reflection. "Stunning." He quipped, grabbing his cell phone from the counter and leaving the bathroom. As he strolled through his apartment he scrolled through his text messages, stopping at the last one he received from Gloria two days ago. Hiya, got house all to myself for a while I'm all alone and bored :-( wanna come over tonight? x His mind buzzed at the memory of going to the house expecting another simple night; dinner and a movie and some laughs. When Gloria finished her fourth glass of wine and leaned over he knew what he was in for and almost couldn't believe she had made the first move after always reminding him of her struggling relationship with the pro-wrestler. She had made mention of him being out on the road countless times, and after the mind-blowing sex the other night he couldn't wait for more. Gloria took it as a sign to stay put, and moved over to the bed where she held her head in her hands and prayed for release. ---- Rolling a salt shaker back and forth across the breakfast bar, Von Kelsig gazed vacantly into the knotted wood surface. His expression had drifted between a hardened sneer and a hopeless pout, settling on the latter now as he reasoned with defeat. The house had been silent for so long he had to strain to make out what the booming noise was. Glaring at the ceiling through which Gloria waited on the bed, his ears fine tuned and pinpointed the sound as it grew louder.
