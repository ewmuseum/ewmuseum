Date: 05 July 2005 “Introducing first, from Hollywood California…” “Hollywood California?” interrupted the vampire aggressively. “The days of red carpet treatment are a thing of the past, along with our residence in Hollywood California. Being the team on the party scene, socialising and frolicking with people more shallow than a midget’s paddling pool are GONE! we aren’t the same P.W.A. of Old School Wrestling or Rush-Pro Wrestling for that matter. We’ve had our fall from grace, now it’s time to pick ourselves up and dust ourselves down and reclaim our rightful place atop of the tag team food chain” The Jester rolled his emerald eyes in reply before clenching his fist once more. Like a ring announcer he rose his arm up and positioned it beneath the cheeky grin that was emitting towards an agitated giant in Nascosto Nerezza. “Like I was saying before being rudely interrupted upon by the rabid wolverine…” “What are you suggesting, eh?” Nerezza initially hissed, “I’m not that short! You’re the short one! PROVE ME WRONG!” he concluded with a grunt. If the copyright infringing of popular catchphrases wasn’t enough, the vampire drew his thumb across his throat and positioned his saliva ridden tongue over a lone tooth to give the impression a gap was present. “Anyway!” snapped the Emerald Menace. “Introducing first, from… Las Vegas Nevada, he weighs in at a trim 226 lbs and stands 5’ 11”. He is the carrier of P.W.A. and self-proclaimed King Midas of Tag, wearer of five PCW Tag Team Championships and the last reigning PCW Continental Champion. Please welcome…” The overselling self-promotion on the part of Gaz Green was almost unbearable to the extent of enduring an excruciating submission manoeuvre, something as infamous as ‘The Soprano’. NO! not that pitiful excuse of television programming but the signature move of Angelo Deville. And like its latest victim Xavier Kannon, Nascosto Nerezza tapped, well his foot at-least… impatiently. With a subtle cough Gaz interrupted enough to conclude, “Please welcome The Emerald Menace, GAAAAAZ “THE JESSSTTTTTTERRRRR” GREEEEEEEEEEENNN!!!! Now it’s your turn squire. Nothing too dramatic, it’s not like anyone is here…” With a momentary squint the Jester cast his eyes over the desolate gym that was now under P.W.A. rule. Their audience this evening were nothing more than gymnasium apparatus sitting in the dim lighting hanging overhead, which funnily enough was more than usual… people simply didn’t want to see Gaz and Nerezza. It was almost embarrassing to the point of being positively identified in a shop queue buying ‘The Best Of Chris Storm,’ which coincidentally has a three minute running time. “Eh, I’m Rezz!” Wow, what an introduction by the behemoth. Not only was it charismatic to the point of being Chris Benoit worthy but such ring announcing hasn’t been witnessed since the viewing of the last ‘Evolution’ card at Global Wrestling (GW). With his three word introduction a distant memory, Nerezza with talons held high traipsed across the squared circle after his intended target, Gaz “The Soon To Be A Dead Man” Green who’s only counter was to reply with a look of bewilderment. Fearing for his well-being Gaz cautiously backed away and into the nearest turnbuckle, “Who put that there?” was the only coherent sentence to emerge from his cherry red lips. The Jester’s teeth chattered together like an ecstasy induced clubber that’s been mesmerised by the laser lighting that usually rains down from the rafters on a Saturday night. “Hey Rezz take it easy. Remember this was only meant to be a light training session so you could help your little green compadres get in shape…” quickly blurted out the prankster as the shadowy silhouette of the vampire edged closer. “HELLO! some of us have an impending triple threat match this week. COME ON! I have Seth Master-bator and that snotty nosed kid from RUSH-Pro this week. You wouldn’t want me to beat them at half mast because you know that’ll only go to my head and you won’t hear the end of it?!” A frightening grin emerged from beneath the curtain of hair, “I won’t hear the end of it huh?” he began in an abrasive manner, “What if I remove your tongue?” the vampire asked politely. The height difference was considerable and with a bone crunching crack Nerezza tilted his head forward so that the two combatants were nose to nose, “Or maybe I’ll rip out your wind-pipe…” Luckily for Gaz the torture of having to watch the horror that is Xavier Morningstar and Shergar… I mean Xerxes making sweet love bestiality style hadn’t even crossed the monsters twisted mind. Thankfully this PCW extract did contain Xerxes and not Shergar, not only would the world have been subjected to the common Jerry Springer topic of bestiality but necrophilia too. The HORROR! I’m getting visions of Vince McMahon jotting down this very information onto a notepad and then running off to hand it to his creative team while shouting, “Triple H my love I have the perfect angle in which to put the World Heavyweight title back around your waist!” ARGH! With a howl to accompany it Gaz unleashed a right hand to his partners jaw. The vampires head rocked backwards and before he could react he was at the mercy of several more as the Jester exploded into action. One by one the punches landed and Rezz succumb to their force; groggily he stepped away from the turnbuckle, which allowed the Jester to take him via his limp arm. With a push from the ropes behind Gaz Green sent Nerezza for the ride with an Irish-whip… Like a runaway locomotive Rezz came hurtling back. And was greeted by a Harley Race inspired flying knee. The Human Horror Film had been toppled. “I’M THE JEST-ER, THE CEREBRAL ASSASSIN-ER AND SOON TO BE WORLD CHAMPION-ER! FEAR MY SLEDGEHAMMER-ER!” cried the prankster in a monotone voice. The sound emitting from his vocal chords had an aura of de ja vu; it was as though it had been heard a thousand times before. Notably on a Monday night. The Prince Of Pranks lifted his sparring partner via a handful of head hair and the two combatants stood on their vertical bases. On first inspection it appeared as though Gaz was about to bury his boot into the vampire’s midsection, the opening sequence to the dreaded ‘Pedigree’. However with a swipe of the oncoming boot Rezz countered by waving his hand in front of his face frantically, something that baffled the Emerald Menace. “You can’t see me?!” Rezz chirped. “YOU CAN’T SEE ME?!?!” “What?” replied the comic, “I can, your standing right ther-…” Before the former PCW starlet could finish his sentence Rezz quickly stole a play right out of the Ric Flair handbook of cheap shots. With his knobbly index finger the vampire poked Gaz “The Game” Green right in the eye, which sent the comic circling the ring like a wounded animal. ARGH! URGH! OUCH! GRRRRR! And BANG! Gaz hadn’t walked into a turnbuckle nor a brick wall, he’d be hard pressed to find the latter in a ring but his best chance would be call someone over in Now That Indy fed is Crap at Wrestling (NTICW). The Jester had been levelled by a shoulder block courtesy of his partner of five years. Like a gymnast Nerezza sprung back up to his feet before the fallen comic and began rubbing his shoulder as though he were the proud owner of a feather duster. And with a clenched fist Rezz dropped hitting his opponent square on the forehead… Gaz oversold in true WWE fashion while the Bakemono beckoned for him to stand. The Jester firstly crawled to his knees before squatting before his larger adversary and finally he stood erect allowing Nascosto Nerezza to lift him onto his broad shoulders in the familiar fireman’s carry. Had there been commentators there you could almost imagine what they would say in a situation like this, “Damn this hellacious, in over twenty years of broadcasting I haven’t seen nothing quite as sick… well there was this one time when I was set on fire by Kane, bless him! I mean, he’s had problems King! He killed his parents, slept with his dead girlfriend…” “PUPPIES!!!!” his counterpart would say in reply. “… Forced another girl to marry him and have his child, which she lost and then she run off with a work colleague. Ah, love him!” Suddenly the Jester wriggled free of his predicament and as though this whole episode had been scripted he fell behind his opponent. Nerezza pivoted full-circle only to be met with a kick to the stomach region and before the goliath could react, he had been doubled over, his head sandwiched between the clenched legs of Gaz Green. Without so much as a second thought towards the repercussions he may endure Gaz cinched in the next part of the sequence, the hooking of the arms, when suddenly… ~ GAZ! GAAAAAZZZZZZZZ!! GGGGGGAAAAAAAAAZZZZZZZZZZZZZ!!! ~ The Jester twitched his eyes and inspected his surroundings… he was in bed, surrounded by comfort. There was no ring and no gymnasium, however one thing was present and that was Nascosto Nerezza who stood looming over his dazed partner. The whole episode had been a dream, or nightmare depending on how you perceive these visions we have little to no control over. Some say that these ‘dreams’ are entwined in your life and that they provide a hazy perception of the future, so how could these be interpreted? Is the gimmick of the Jester going to be stolen by Vince McMahon? Is Nerezza going to join a Chain-Gang? Will ‘I’ the writer feel the wrath of those I have mocked? “Time to get up!” snarled the gothic behemoth, “You have a match to prepare for, and I have found us a nice little gymnasium that has been desolate for quite some time!” “WHAT?!?!” retorted the Lord Of Laughter in surprise. Cue the ‘Twighlight Zone’ theme tune.
