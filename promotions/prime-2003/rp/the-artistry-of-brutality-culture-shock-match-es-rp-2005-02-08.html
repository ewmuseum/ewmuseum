The Artistry of Brutality (Culture Shock Match(es) RP)

8 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/8/05 The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid is no stranger to Japan. It is the country where he first made his name, in barbaric death matches the likes of which have never been seen in America. For the first time since he left, he has returned to the archipelago nation due to Culture Shock being held in the Sapporo Dome, on the northern island of Hokkaido. There, he will compete twice. Once and for all, his grudge with Karina Wolfenden will be settled, and he will then go on to compete in a Dual Halo match, a contest that is right up the Trapezoid’s alley. Undoubtedly, a nation that has seen the Trapezoid shed blood before shall see him shed blood again. The HIT: It is so…peaceful here. His eyes peer out into the distance, looking over the expanse of a large, emerald-green lake. Lake Shikotsu is a popular tourist destination on Hokkaido, known for its mystical nature. The former PCW star stands before the lake, wearing a simple black t-shirt and jeans despite the cold conditions. The HIT: Japan is like a second home to me. When no other country would take in a young man with nothing more than a red afro and a death wish, this land welcomed me with open arms. I have competed in standard contests here, matches like any I would normally undergo in PRIME. But I have also competed in matches that no one in PRIME has ever even had nightmares about. A few feet in front of the Trapezoid is a small, stone bench. On the bench rests the Trapezoid’s duffel bag, and next to it a small photo album. Below the bench rests a cooler, presumably with refreshments for the Trapezoid to enjoy on his day trip to the scenic lake. The HIT: And that brings me to my opponent, Karina Wolfenden. The contest that awaits us does not involve beds of nails, by definition. Nor does it involve thumbtacks, or cacti, or fire. But it can if I want it to. You agreed to sign a contest with me that has no disqualifications, no countouts, and no pinfalls. For you to beat me, you must knock me out. And for me to beat you, I must make you apologize to me. Last week, I issued my own apology on ReVolution for everything that I have done to you over the past few weeks. But there will be no such apologies when we meet at Culture Shock… Rifling through the photo album, the HIT sees many pictures of himself. In some, his face can barely be seen due to the amount of blood. Others have him punctured with thumbtacks. All are grisly reminders of how the Trapezoid became a star. The HIT: You have competed in Last Man Standing matches before, if my memory serves me correctly. But you have never competed in a match like this with a man like me since our hardcore match back in Rush Pro Wrestling. And, even more unfortunately for you, you have never competed in such a match in Japan. The duffel bag contains Trapezoid’s tools of the trade. He reaches in, and pulls out a blood-stained shard of glass, carefully contained inside a glass jar. The HIT: Karina, this is a piece of the first pane of glass I ever threw an opponent through. I grabbed him by the shoulders and threw him head-first through it. Glass flew everywhere. It is a moment I will never forget, because it is that very moment that the Japanese people began to chant my name. “TRAP-E-ZOID! TRAP-E-ZOID!” It is a chant that has stayed with me my entire career. It is the reason that I put my body through absolute hell in the Land of the Rising Sun, to hear those chants! He smiles, tucking the glass jar away carefully, treating it like a precious gemstone. The HIT: As far as I know, Karina, you’ve never been involved in a match with glass or barbed wire. You don’t know what it’s like to have your body burned by flame, or punctured with nails or tacks. But I do, Karina, and that’s my advantage. Because if I decide to bring such things out, what’s a K-Wolf to do? You consider yourself an extreme athlete, after all. Snowboarding and skiing on the most dangerous slopes in the world. You’ve probably broken an arm or leg along the way, so you know what pain is. But that’s just physical pain, Karina. You don’t know the pain of being slammed on a bed of thumbtacks. When you get up, they stick into your flesh. And if you want them out, you have to pull them out yourself. Doctors set broken legs. I wonder if you’re capable of being your own doctor? Trapezoid finally takes a seat on the bench. Despite the horrible violence he intends to cause in a few short days, he is seemingly at peace. The HIT: Don’t get me wrong, Karina. I’d do this to anyone under the circumstances. Signing to face me in a match with no rules is like trying to surf during a hurricane, or snowboard during an avalanche. Once you step into the sea, or onto the mountain, you’re taking your chances. It’s taken being set on fire to keep me down for a count of THREE, Miss Wolfenden. Can you comprehend what you are going to have to do to keep me down for a count of ten? Meanwhile, I just have to make you apologize. I can do that many ways. I can simply beat on you until you finally give in. I can put you in the Extraneous Solution, and torque your neck and shoulder until you say that you’re sorry. Or I can simply brutalize you with items that you had never before thought to be associated with professional wrestling. The demented grin of the Trapezoid is the mark of a man who lost his sanity long ago. The HIT is a man who once threatened an opponent with fifty different maimings with a standard issue melon baller, and his sadistic creativity is legend in Japan. The HIT: Ever had lemons squeezed over a fresh wound? Or salt poured in them? I can assure you that it isn’t a fun experience. Perhaps you’ll get the chance to find out first hand at the Sapporo Dome. You see, I have a reputation to maintain here. The fans of Japan wouldn’t be happy to see the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid simply using his vast technical acumen. Not in a match like this, where the violence quotient is expected to be high. Were this match held in New York, or even London, I wouldn’t need to go to extremes. But Japan is Japan, and as such, I have an obligation to give the fans what they want…and what they want, Karina, is your blood. Dipping back into his grisly duffel bag, the Trapezoid comes out with a makeshift necklace. Ticket stubs are attached to the string making up the body of the necklace, and each and every stubs is half-stained red. The HIT: There is a tradition in Japan among fans of hardcore wrestling, Karina. After a contest, fans will dip their ticket stubs in the puddles of blood that form on the concrete floor, as a souvenir. You might find it a bit barbaric, but I rather like it. A small part of myself lives on with all of these people. My life force, my very essence seeps into a piece of paper that they take home with them and treasure. Are you ready to experience that, Karina? Are you ready to watch people dipping their stubs in everything that is Karina Marie Wolfenden? Solemnly, the Sultan of Sides places the stub necklace around his neck, and begins to thumb through them. The HIT: This one is from Yokohama. I lost a pint of blood that night after my opponent split my forehead open from stem to stern with a broken bottle. The one next to it is from my debut in Kawasaki Stadium, three months earlier. The blood on that particular stub is special to me, as it is the result of the first time I ever went head-first into a bed of nails. You can look at me today, Karina, and see the pink scar tissue that covers my forehead. Hardcore legends before me can today make themselves bleed if they rub their scalps the right way due to all the times they’ve been split open. I have no doubt that one day I will join them, and that it will be one of the proudest days of my life. A finger absent-mindedly runs along one of the numerous scars criss-crossing the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid’s forehead, as HIT can only wince and grin at the same time in remembrance. The HIT: You may be thinking that I am going back on my word to you. I assure you, I am not. Other men use weaponry as a means of disrespect, a means to maim an opponent. I will only use what force is necessary to be victorious. Because it is no longer mainly about the apology, despite that being necessary for me to win. It is about the victory itself. In our only meeting to date, you came out the victor. At Culture Shock, I seek to even our score, now and for all time! He traces the scar down his cheek, and then back around to his neck, feeling the scars there. These are not scars caused by combat, but rather the scars of neck surgery, the scars that nearly drove him to the brink of even more dangerous madness than he usually lives his life under. The HIT: Perhaps this is all slightly macabre for you, Karina. Perhaps you are afraid, knowing now what is in store for you here in Japan. But sleep well, Karina, for one way or another, the issue between us will be settled. Either I will beat my apology out of you, or you will knock me out, and leave me lying in front of an arena full of my fans. And if you manage to do that, Karina Wolfenden, I will take your advice…and deal with it. I will never ask you again for that apology. Not even should I win the Dual Halo match, where I could receive anything I wanted should I be victorious. No, Karina, our match will be the end of our battles. A spectacular cleansing, if you will. A purification of both of our sins through terrible violence and the spilling of blood. It’s almost poetic, don’t you think? That’s why I come to places like this to prepare for the most horrific of contests. Despite the season being winter, Lake Shikotsu is still a beautiful sight. The HIT is surrounded by trees of all types, the evergreens providing the beautiful a beautiful green color mimicked by the emerald lake. The HIT: There is a certain intrinsic beauty in brutality, wouldn’t you say? Blood can be a beautiful thing, Karina. I would know, for I have seen it cascade from many a man and woman’s body onto everything from concrete to the Earth itself. I find it most beautiful when on snow, myself. Something about the stark contrast between the red plasma and pure white snow is so…inspiring. But since I don’t have snow…I guess I shall have to settle for white canvas. Calmly, the Trapezoid removes a painter’s canvas from his duffel bag, and places it down on the bench. He goes into his pocket, and comes out with a small pocket knife. The HIT: I do this for inspiration sometimes. Medieval society saw bloodletting as a way of allowing evil spirits to flee the body. If that is true, then I want every malevolent spirit that escapes me to land on this canvas, and inspire me to new levels of brutality, new levels of violence in the squared circle! For, if I am unable to step my game up, I will not be successful against you, Karina, nor will I be successful in the Dual Halo match. With a quick slice, Seymour reopens one of the scars on his forehead. Slowly but surely, blood begins to drip from the wound, falling the three or so feet from the Trapezoid’s forehead to the canvas The HIT: To me, the Dual Halo match marks a new beginning in my career. The unpleasant affairs with Karina Wolfenden will be over by the time I step inside the two cell monolithic structure known as the Dual Halo. I see that match as an opportunity to further my art form… Trapezoid closes his eyes, desperately trying to squeeze more blood from his forehead onto the canvas. He lets out a low scream, teeth clenched tight. The HIT: Because while some men use paint for their art, and others use marble, I use blood. Other men use paintbrushes and chisels as their tools, I use barbed wire and thumbtacks! Yet…I am every bit the artist that these men are! For the first time in years, I will be before a crowd that will appreciate my unique artistry. The Japanese wrestling fan understands the beauty of the deathmatch style. They understand that any man who steps into the ring to perform the death match knows that they will leave pieces of themselves in the arena that night. He will leave epidermis. He will leave blood. He will leave hair. Those are the ground rules of a beautiful brutality that few men and women will ever know. Superstars of PRIME…you are all fortunate indeed… Finally satisfied with the pattern of blood spatters on his canvas, the Trapezoid returns to an upright standing position. Plasma still continues to flow down his face, and begins to even trail down his neck in small, tight streams. The HIT: For I shall introduce you all to the beauty of violence! And in the Dual Halo match, I shall have not one, but twenty-nine other subjects! Twenty-nine other unique individuals to use as canvases for my art, and two steel cells to use as my tools. Do you know how many different ways there are to make a man bleed with such a cell wall? I do. And I will probably come up with at least five more during the course of the contest. Such is the life of an artist…always creating, always innovating! A laugh comes from deep within the Trapezoid, the laugh of a man who has gone off the deep end long ago. With the blood seem to flow Trapezoid’s inhibitions, as the adrenaline high of feeling his own blood has sent energy coursing through his body. The HIT: Some of the individuals in the contest I know. Others I know only by reputation. Let’s start, then, with a woman who made her career by being “hardcore”. Let’s start with Amy Campbell. Aimz. A former three time Hardcore Champion elsewhere in the great universe that is professional wrestling. A “punk rock” chick. Yet another pretender who has no idea what “hardcore” means. The flowing blood slowly begins to coagulate on the Trapezoid’s head and neck, but he simply continues on, seemingly oblivious to the plasma hardening on his flesh. The HIT: You’re the type of person who wants everyone to believe that she’s tough and cool. Like the preppy girl in high school who gets her hair dyed black with red tips and starts listening to that emo crap that I have to listen to every day when I teach. And yet in the end, the only thing hardcore about you is the number of guys that you’ve put out for in your illustrious wrestling career. I mean, here we have a woman who has withstood the devastating PENIS BIZNITCH SLAP O’DOOM from Rent-A-Hero! HOLY DOUBLE PENETRATION, BATMAN! Honestly! Amy, have you ever been in a fed where you HAVEN’T slept with one of your fellow co-workers? In Old School Wrestling, you were with that nice sk8r boi, Reno Davis. What happened? Did he say “see you l8r”? Oh, that’s right, I remember, you almost got raped on pay-per-view in front of a worldwide audience. I really hate stooping to this joke, but DAMN woman, you give “hardcore” a whole new meaning! A mirthful cackle spills forth from the Trapezoid’s lips, as he seems to be truly enjoying himself. The HIT: In the end, Aimz, any chances of you winning this match are mere pipe dreams. For once I ram you face-first into the Halo, and grind your poor pretty face into hamburger meat against it, you will fall in the same position that you always do. Face down with your legs spread. And I assure you that the pain I cause you will be far greater than that of ANY bulging member. Silly rave girls, they pop a couple pills of X and they think they can take on the world. Let’s see, what other pretenders do we have in this match? Ah yes… Brandon Youngblood and Jeff Garvin pop into the Trapezoid’s mind’s eye. He smirks for a few moments, before articulating his thoughts. The HIT: We have two men from the dearly departed Squared Circle among us, Brandon Youngblood and Jeff Garvin. Tag team partners, no less, and men that I’m sure think they stand a reasonable chance of winning the Halo. Please. There’s a better chance that Aimz WON’T fuck one of the other twenty nine participants than there is of one of you two winning. I’ll admit freely that you could both wrestle circles around me. But Dual Halo is not a wrestling match. It is a fight for survival. I’ve faced down things no man could imagine, so Garvin’s friggin’ punch is supposed to frighten me? But wait! Oooh, it has a name! A BIG SCARY NAME! HELP! THE HANDS OF STONE ARE GOING TO GET ME! Load of crap! He breaks out laughing, nearly falling to the ground. His mirth has reopened the wound on his forehead, and new blood begins to flow, forming new paths down the Trapezoid’s already red-stained visage. The HIT: I mean, LOOK AT YOU TWO! The Only Diamond In Our Sport? The Tennessee Technician? And you two MORONS think you have a shot in a match with no rope breaks and no rule against closed fists? Oh wait, that’s right, Garvin has the HANDS OF STONE! OH NO! Newsflash for you, Jeff, Hands of Stone PALE in comparison to Fists Wrapped In Barbed Wire, which is what I’m bringing to the Dual Halo. And if I happen to run into you in one of those Halos, you’re going to find out just how right I am. Garvin, you of all people should understand my mission. In Memphis, wrestlers BLEED for their art. And I’m going to help you achieve the highest possible form of that art, so that when YOUR Memphis Death Certificate is published, it will read “Jeff L Garvin, 2/12/79-2/13/05. Cause of Death: THINKING HE COULD BEAT THE FUCKING HARDCORE ISOSCELES TRAPEZOID IN A MATCH WITH NO RULES WITH HIS STUPID FUCKING PUNCH!” A goofy grin appears on the Trapezoid’s face as he remembers the cooler that he brought with him, lying on the ground next to the bench. He steps over the bench, and kneels beside the cooler, blood still flowing and him still not giving a damn. The HIT: Youngblood…I’ll admit, you won our only meeting, but you didn’t pin me. You know the funny thing about diamonds? They’re the hardest substance on Earth, but if you tap them just right, hit them in the right spot, they shatter to pieces. And if you ARE as brittle as a diamond, I will be hitting you MORE than enough to find that right spot, and watch your delusions of winning Dual Halo shatter into nothingness! Man, this is hard work! I need a drink! Opening the cooler, the Trapezoid emerges with a bottle of Kirin beer and a bottle opener. He quickly rips the bottle cap off, and takes a nice, hearty swallow of the alcohol. The HIT: DAYUM! Now THERE’S the good shit! None of this watered down Budweiser crap! The Japanese know their beer! Now, where was I? To help jog his memory, the Trapezoid chugs down more of the beer. Logical, no? The HIT: Who the hell else is in this thing…oh yeah! Boda! That stupid Communist bastard from Czechlakistan or some crap like that, Ivan Stanislav’s right hand stooge! I remember you Sloboda! THE BIG BAD BODA DADDY! At least, unlike the other schmos I was talking about earlier, you ACTUALLY HAVE SOMETHING GOING FOR YOU! And that is that you’re FUCKING HUGE! No no, I’m not talking about your penis, everyone knows that the average Japanese man is better hung than Boda, I’m talking about your weight! You’re 300 plus pounds and built like a brick shithouse. Too bad that I can reduce that shithouse to just plain shit with one swing of a steel chair. I’m gonna take a lot of enjoyment in kicking yo’ ass, because once I do, I’ll have beaten the entire Red Army! I’M GONNA BEAT YOU DOWN! RIGHT INTO THE GRASS! I’M GONNA SMACK YOU ROUND! I’M GONNA KICK YO’ ASS! Premature catchphrases are so tragic in young males, don’t you think? Trapezoid takes another swig of the brew, and finding the bottle empty, simply places it down and pulls out another. By now, a combination of blood and the fact that HIT has seemingly been drinking before he arrived leads the Trapezoid’s comments to grow more and more outlandish by the second. The HIT: Then we’ve got…wait, don’t tell me, I remember this one…! Tessa Windsor! My favorite lupine’s tag team partner! Now, if Aimz is the whore of PRIME, that would make Tessa the choir girl! No wonder Hoyt Williams wants her so badly! He probably thinks she’s a twelve year old! I can’t really blame him though. Tessa, darling, WEARING SUNFLOWERS IS NOT SOMETHING ADULTS DO. Little kids wear sunflowers. Little kids who attract scary pedophiles with giant afros that look like Dustin Diamond. Don’t come complaining to me when some sicko pervert freak named Ryan comes knocking on your door asking you to wrestle with him, it won’t be MY fault! Wait a minute! That’s what this is all about, isn’t it? I got it now! I know what you want! I know what your prize will be if you win the Dual Halo match! YOU WANT…MICHAEL JACKSON! …Oh no. He is NOT going to make more pedophile jokes. Sadly, his highs, adrenaline and otherwise, won’t allow him to shut his mouth. The HIT: IN YOUR PANTS~! BECAUSE THAT’S THE CLOSEST YOU WILL EVER GET TO BEING SOMEBODY IN THIS WORLD! And by being somebody, I mean sucking the limp cock of a washed up pop star who’d rather you be a young boy. Tessa, after I utterly embarrass and humiliate you in the Dual Halo Match, all that you’ll have left is your body! You’ll have to turn yourself out to horny old men to make a living! I suggest you learn the phrase “Sucky Sucky Five Dolla”, and FAST! Hell, book a plan ticket to Thailand, even twelve year olds like you are legal there! Then you and Aimz can exchange notes! After she’s finished explaining to you the wonders of the donkey punch, you can describe a Dirty Sanchez in all of its “glory”! Obviously, a combination of blood loss and slow intoxication has begun to take over the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. What few inhibitions he has are now gone, only making his bile more vitriolic. The HIT: Now that the silly virginal twelve year old has been dealt with, I can move on to more pressing matters. For reasons still unbeknownst to the God of GCFs, namely ME, the glue factory has yet again rejected the tired, weary bones of everyone’s favorite wrestling cripple, Xavier Kannon. How do I know this? Because everyone’s favorite set of breas--err, I mean everyone’s favorite valet, Eleanor Hall came out on ReVolution last week and ENLIGHTENED all of us boobs--err, peons in the audience. And what a RIVETING segment! I could barely tit--err, SIT down! Back in the knock--LOCKER ROOM! LOCKER ROOM! Oh the fuck with it! ELLIE’S GOT A GREAT RACK! As if shamed, the Trapezoid can only continue his second bottle of Kirin on the drinking session, as the blood and alcohol on his face begin to mix. The HIT: She’s a hottie! Those cute cherry red lips…the long blonde hair…THE GIGGLES, DAMNIT, THE GIGGLES! I still hear them in my dreams from when I was in Primetime Championship Wrestling with her! Lucky fucking bastard Xavier. HE DOES NOT DESERVE SUCH A VISION OF LOVELINESS! Especially considering the fact that he’s so physically broken down now, he can’t possibly perform! How’s a cripple supposed to satisfy a woman like that! Ellie, let me take this opportunity to assure you that I am ALL MAN! And given the chance, I would GLADLY share MANY Hallmark Moments with you…in your hair…on your face…in between your breasts, AND MOST IMPORTANTLY OF ALL, IN YOUR BEAUTIFUL, BEAUTIFUL COCK SOCKET! With another mighty swallow, the second bottle of Kirin is finished. HIT grabs at his crotch, making lewd gestures presumably intended for Xavier Kannon’s wife. The HIT: Xavier…you took it upon yourself to try and ruin me back in PCW! You and your damned Front Office. Thanks to you, I LOST TO TIM WALDEN! TIM *hic* WALDEN! But now, I have the chance to ruin you. So Xavier, when we meet inside the Dual Halo, I am going to HURT YOU WHERE IT HURTS THE MOST! And of course, THAT’S RIGHT UP YOUR WIFE’S HERSHEY HIGHWAY! The anal reference makes the Trapezoid giggle like a six year old who has just heard an especially good fart joke. The HIT: But enough about the individuals in the Dual Halo match. The fact of the matter, ladies and gentlemen, and of course Aimz and Tessa…you’re going to be stepping into tons of steel with a man who is psychotic enough to do anything. A man who can take INCREDIBLE amounts of punishment! As if to prove his point, the Trapezoid picks up the second bottle of Kirin, and SLAMS it onto the top of his head. Unfortunately, the massive afro of the HIT seems to cushion the blow, and so the bottle bounces painfully off of his head with a loud *THUNK!* The HIT: Aww, DAMNIT! He drops the bottle, due to weakness from blood loss or the early stages of inebriation, either will do. Falling to one knee, the Trapezoid looks out at the lake through lidded eyes, and is able to muster a smile. HIT seems calm and tranquil, a far cry from the loudmouth he has just been for quite a while. His eyes are heavy and lidded, as he appears on the verge of unconsciousness, trying not to slur his final words. The HIT: My purity…and my vision…both shall be attained at Culture Shock. After I settle business with Karina, I will enter the Halo…and then my vision…shall be… Nothing more can be heard, before the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid slumps forward and falls to the ground, the combination of alcohol and blood loss too much for even the HIT. By the Trapezoid’s head, a small pool of blood forms., and all is silent until a pair of young men happen to come upon the fallen man. ???: Masa, come quick! There’s an unconscious man here! Masa: I’ll be right there, Kentaro! The two teenagers approach the body hesitantly. Kentaro nudges it lightly with his foot, but no movement is prompted. Suddenly, Masa recognizes the giant red afro, and thus recognizes the man. Masa: This is the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid! Kentaro: You’re right! What the heck is he doing here? Masa: He’s not moving! We need to call the paramedics! Kentaro: Alright, I will. You check him. Can you tell if he’s breathing? Masa: Yes, he is breathing. That’s good. Kentaro reaches into his pocket, coming out with a cell phone. He rapidly punches numbers in, and begins to speak to the operator on the other line. Meanwhile, Masa also reaches into his pocket, and comes out with a ticket, funnily enough, to Culture Shock. Masa: Forgive me, Mr. Trapezoid. But this is not a chance that most men have… Solemnly, Masa kneels by the side of the Trapezoid, and dips his ticket stub side down in the pool of blood, only coating half of it. By this time, Kentaro has finished his call, and he returns to his friend’s side. Kentaro: Masa, what are you doing?! The man’s unconscious! Masa: It’s not like we’re bothering him! Come on, dip the stub end of your ticket in his blood, but only halfway. Somewhat reluctantly, Kentaro does as his friend tells him. Kentaro: Why only half, Masa? Masa: Because, at Culture Shock, we can dip the other end in Karina’s blood. It’ll be the best souvenir ever! Kentaro grins at his friend, as the sound of sirens can be heard in the distance. Silently, Masa stands, and spots the blood-stained canvas of the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid. Masa: Hey Kentaro, what’s this? Soon, The HIT will be taken to a Hokkaido hospital, where his cut will be sealed and he can rest. He will be ready to compete at Culture Shock. He will settle his score with Karina Wolfenden. And then? The image on the Trapezoid’s canvas tells the story. The word PRIME, written in bloody letters, with a drip-drawn picture of the globe beneath it. As an artist, I’m sure The HIT would agree that pictures speak louder than words.
