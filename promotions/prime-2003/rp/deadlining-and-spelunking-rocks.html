Deadlining and Spelunking rocks

Date: Today Adventure Log 1 I, Adrienne Diamond, have had to put up with a lot for Vangellus Oxios over the past few years. His antics aren't dangerous or anything... it's just that he can be a handful. Van's definitely creative, but he hasn't fully-grasped how to channel it productively. Really, that thick skull isn't his only medical issue. It wasn't released to the public (but if they knew it I doubt they'd be surprised) that he's got ADHD. He can be more focused than to be just stopping one thing to do something else a minute later. But his mind isn't developed to a complete adult level yet. I'm managing a 20-something teenager. At least he has a knack for picking good people to surround himself with. Richard Hargrove is an asset to have whenever he wakes up. His research proves invaluable. Aside from 'acquiring' some of the funds for the Van World Order, he is the archivist. Everything that has ever happened to Van since his rise to celebrity, it is kept safely on three backed up 220 gig hard drives in three different locations in Canada and the U.S. In addition to this, he can, if necessary, get us any resources on opponents. Really, though, Van hasn't used any of this since RPW, and even then he just glanced at it and forgot about it right away. Even if there's an 8-year age difference, I've found the love of my life in Emi Sachiko. People probably don't see much more than a vagrant when they look at her, and maybe she does have a Samurai Jack kinda vibe going for her as Van said, but she's just so... real. Not big into pop culture, doesn't want to be rich or famous. All she cares about is getting a hot meal and a shower, really; lacking in ambition doesn't matter too much since she's taken up as Van's bodyguard. True, she hates him (or at least what he stands for), but she's willing to put up with it since I'm working for him. She's okay so long as she can wake up next to me in the morning. That's just what I need in my life. Sadly, though he was a nice guy, Darcellus Wallace has decided not to return to Van's service as a second bodyguard. Dar has a wife now, and she prefers a sedentary lifestyle. He wants to be with her, and I support him for that. Historically, he's been Van's guard longer than Emi, as the two were friends in boxing training. Big guy but nice and easy to get along with. He wasn't one to take long sentences, but whatever he did say was pretty profound. I hope he's doing well for himself now. What was I starting with? Right. I've put up with a lot for Vangellus, but this is beyond understandable. He walked into the room early this morning, flood boots and raincoat on, and said, 'We're going spelunking!' Emi and I were asleep, but far from clothed. She still didn't waste any time in tossing him out of the room (through the window, but we're on the first floor). So here we are, about to walk into 'the past' as Van says. Emi is here and dressed like the rest of us given the occasion. We're expecting a lot of water, don't want her messing up her clothes. I'm along with a flashlight and a digital recorder. I'll be giving a narrative of what we encounter, which Richard will filter and upload once we get back. Adventure Log 2 Darkness fills the cavernous ruins, a dank stench hangs with the moisture in the air as water drips off of the walls. Everything has a blue tinge to it, the stained surfaces peeling away to the white underneath. The water is knee high. I can feel things moving down there. I REALLY hope that it's just fish. Emi: Up ahead. Look. We can just make out a golden retriever. It looks hungry. Van: So long as it doesn't think we're Milkbone, I'm cool. No, it's going off now. We must've scared it off. Emi: Wild dogs aren't territorial in flood zones. It couldn't mark anything here. But it didn't attack and ran away, so that means that there aren't many visitors around here, but also that there's a food supply that it doesn't need to bother with us. You think it lives here? Emi: We wouldn't see it here otherwise. Van: Eww, this water is all dog piss? *SMACK!* Van: Hey, watch it. It's hard to see anything. The voltage on these flashlights isn't the greatest. Where did you get these, Van? Van: Hardware store. That's why. I told you to get some Mag-Lites from Sharper Image. Van: If you wanted to go 40 miles out of our way to find one, that's on you. Ugh... After coming in through the opening, there's about a 5x6 area that we can actually see with the light entering from outside. Much of it is blocked by debris and 'flat stalagmites' as Vangellus calls them. Van: You damn right. There is still a bit of a straight line formed, most likely the path of the water coming in. The mess is all along the sides. There's rock, paper-- Van: Ow! Found the scissors. And many dead plants. Is that... oh no.... Emi: Don't worry, it's not a baby. Just a doll. Oh thank goodness. Van: Hey, look at this! Wait, what is it? That looks like... Is that gold? Emi: Not real gold. Looks like it, though. This place doesn't look like it would support a lode, or even house anything worth value. Van: What about that complete DVD box-set of Jesse Jamester's best matches? Emi: My statement stands. Van: Still, every little bit of treasure helps. Put it in the bag. I don't want to touch that. Who knows how long it's been here. Van: Well if you weren't so 'I'm allergic to latex' then there would be some gloves for you. Fine, I'll get it. Hold the bag open, Emi. It's pretty big. Are you sure it's not gold? Emi: *taps* Too hard. Van: No matter... this is still pretty valuable. Emi: To who? Van: I'M taking it, aren't I? Adventure Log 3 Emi: So why are we here? And for that matter, we still don't know where 'here' is. Why did you insist on blindfolding us, Van? We're in this dark place and I hear odd noises every now and then. What's the deal? Van: It's just that... there's something here that I need to find. Emi: You're still not saying where 'here' is. Van: Southern United States. Oh god, Van, we're not in a building flooded from Katrina, are we? Tell me that's not the case. Van: No. It's been like this for a while. I tried coming in before, but I wasn't ready then. We're stepping through... okay, now it's something BIG and brushing against my leg. Emi, help. Emi: ... Catfish. Definitely the American south. How can you see? Emi: I'm used to traveling in low-light areas. Van: I used to go on fishing trips with my grandfather. I picked Belmont, North Carolina as my first temporary residence outside of the Oxios Grand Estate in Crete and Windsor, Ontario because we would every summer come down to visit family of my mother. They had a great lake for casting. You could just go out there all day and relax. A little bait and a little patience, and the fish came to you. Emi: So you're saying that you never caught anything. Van: Hey, be nice. Hahaha... Look. Guess you were right about the catfish. There's that dog, eating one. Van: Man, he's really going at that thing. Emi: Best to leave it be while it eats. Speaking of which, I'm hungry. You rushed us out without breakfast, Van. Did you pack any lunches? Van: No, but here you go. Did you just pick that bag up out of the water? Van: They're air and water tight until you open them. Emi... *SMACK!* Van: Would you PLEASE stop that? Emi: Afraid you'll get MORE brain damage? Van: You aren't a nice person. Emi: I simply have no tolerance for idiocy, which you possess in abounds. Please, let's not fight like this. I've no idea where we are and I still feel like we're being watched. Emi: We are. What? Emi: I can tell. There has been a disturbance in-- Van: The Force? *SMACK!* Van: Oww.... Emi: The water. I hear other motions, the kind that could only be human legs moving through the water. Do you think we're in any danger? Emi: I don't think so. Vangellus might be, since I'm off the clock right now. Van: Meanie! Adventure Log 4 I'm beat. We've been looking around this place for the past two hours and still nothing. There's nothing here, Van. There's nothing in the water, there's nothing in the alcoves... you're not finding anything. Emi: Save for trinkets and assorted smatterings of junk. And why am I carrying all of this? Van: Because you won't let me watch you with Adrienne. ARGH! Oh, Emi, why'd you have to do that? Now he'll be down for a while. Emi: You wanted a break. If he's not moving, you get to rest. But was it really necessary to kick him in the groin? Emi: Yes. Van: Oh god, I got the piss water in my mouth! Adventure Log 5 While Vangellus recovers, I'll go back over our haul. So far we have found a rather large piece of fool's gold, an old laminated poster dating from last year, and an utterly-ruined jacket from an underground rock band out of Bangor, Maine that Van seems to place some importance upon. I don't know why we're here, other than that he's searching for something. But what? Why couldn't he do this himself? What looks to myself and Emi to just be trash... he's taking it. There was a piece of actual jewelry in the water and he left it there. Of course he's got money from his fighting background, that combined with his giving personality means that there's not a greedy bone in his body, but still. Something like that would mean more than a poster. He's meticulously scrutizing everything that he finds, tossing back what he doesn't want like rubbish and hoarding that which he does want as though it's the most important thing in the world. Van said that he tried to come here once before, but he wasn't ready. Ready for what? I don't recall him talking about anything like this before his sabbatical last year. Was this visit during that time? It's almost like... he's looking for memories. But memories of what? How do Emi and I fit into it? I wish I knew where we were, that would help greatly in figuring out this puzzle. In the meantime, though, all I can do is rest my head back and get used to the stink of stale water. Emi: There are silverfish covering the wall, might not want to do that. Eww, gross! Adventure Log 6 What's this? Van: Looks like a photo album. Emi: And it's bone dry. Van: Belongs to that person you think is watching us? Emi: How else could it be maintained so well. There's not even any dust on it. Do you think we could take a look? I'm interested to see what would be kept in such good condition. Emi: In a place like this... whoever left it out must've wanted it to be seen, or it'd be hidden. That or they'd be rushing out to defend their property. Is it safe? Emi: I don't really care about it. If you want to look through, I'll keep watch. Thanks, Emi. Van: 'Precious Memories.' Isn't this the kind of photobook that parents get for their newborns? I think so. It even has a little stork carrying a baby here on the inside cover. How cute. Van: But these aren't baby pictures. No, they're normal adults. It's all taken in the same place, it looks like. White-washed walls. These pictures were taken for the purpose of a photo album, that's all a group-shot like these would be good for. But who are these people? Van: This one is Jake Hancock. He said that he was going to be the Sexiest Man Alive in 2005. Meanwhile he's fell off the face of the earth and there's not even a picture of him to put on milk cartons. Kinda like Chris Collins. And him? Van: Malphas. Promising but he suffered a leg injury before he could really get his start. These four girls look ridiculous. Look at those weird faces. Van: They never were too photogenic. Wait... Van, is that? Van: Yeah. Scott's girlfriend, Alyssa Sanders. And with her, Sam Alotti, Carys, and Melissa Cruz. And... is this Civil Disobedience? Van: The same. My... Van, this is the-- Van: Former office for Rush Pro Wrestling. How did it get like this? Van: They'd already made the architechtural faux pax of building into a basin. Then when Ophelia came up the coast, it got drenched. But the pipes were messed up anyway, and this place always sucked for water drainage, so it's just gathered here. Osman: You're the first... to come back. Where'd you come from? Osman: As the young lady here has said, I've been watching you since you got here. Emi: You look in good health. Osman: Yes, the catfish here are quite good. That was my dog you saw, Alexander. Van: Osman? Man, how are you? Emi: Don't hug him, his clothes are soaked. Van: It's alright. This is Osman. ..... Emi: ..... Van: From human resources. Emi: ..... ..... Van: He was the guy who showed Little Al my tapes and got me the job in RPW. He was their archivist the whole time. Man, I wondered what happened to you. Osman: I was homeless to begin with. Al needed help that he could pay for pennies so that the money could all go into hiring wrestlers and securing the arena. But I was happy to be a part of this. Van: And you just stayed here? Osman: I came to this country illegally in 1998 from Kosovo. This isn't as bad as where I was before. But I'm not important. I take it that you'd like to leave with the items you've found? Van: Yeah. It's important to me. Osman: I'm glad that it's important to someone. Take it. Adventure Log 7 A poster for 'The Kannon Effect'. A Cadaver Theory jacket, the band of Gideon Poe. And the RPW Heavyweight Title. Good haul today. And I learned something about Vangellus. Not an hour wasted.
