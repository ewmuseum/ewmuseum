You Never Can Tell

14 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-14-07 Standing on the corner across from the 3rd Church of Christ in Chicago, IL is Hoyt Williams. He watches as people enter the building wearing their Sunday best. It’s been ten years since Hoyt Williams has entered the Church where the fine Pastor Shyster thought him Religion. The thoughts of entering and being rejected cross his mind. They say you can’t go home again, of course they say a lot of things. Nuggets of wisdom tend to have a bit of truth in them. You can go home again as long as home exists but you can’t expect it to be the same. The wind blows as does Paris Hilton, people change like Lindsey Lohan, and what was once is on longer; like American values and the Packers win-loss record. Hoyt walks slowly to the front entrance of the church and pulls open the door. In the corner is Jimmy Norton the blind greeter. He taps his cane at the legs of Hoyt Williams and looks him over through his broken eyes. Jimmy Norton: Hoyt Williams! Gosh Dang good to see you boy. Hoyt could never figure out how the blind man knew he was, he was just happy he did. Lots of things work that don’t really make any sense and define logic. Like how does shit flush and where does it go, then it gets treated, then become drinking water? Water into wine is one thing but turd juice into Aquafina is another. Hoyt Williams: James! How are you pal? I have returned to save the world. Jimmy Norton: One day at a time son, one day at a time. Hoyt dips his hands in the holy water and makes the sign of the cross as he continues forward. A young gangster looking black youth approaches the PRIME hall of fame member and one hell of a guy. Youth: Yo this is a private church. Hoyt Williams: So private the Second Coming can’t enter? That seems a bit pathetic like a women champion, or eating a whole tub of ice-cream in your underwear while watching Ron Popeil selling pasta makers. Are you going to reject your savior twice? Youth: Man go fuck your self. Hoyt Williams: I don not indulge in the pleasure of sin like you do. So to self gratify myself is not something I would do. You dig? I am to pure to make myself blind like so many. I heard that’s how Jimmy Norton and Ray Charles became blind. It’s true God told me so. Listen why don’t you go get yourself some ribs while I find Pastor Shyster. Hoyt hands the kid a twenty spot. Youth: You best be checking with your quad-fin, Paster Shyster is no longer here. I don’t likes your attitude boy. Hoyt Williams: I don’t like your inability to speak proper English. You know God doesn’t allow ebonics in heaven. Youth: You would know? Hoyt Williams: I’ve been there. Youth: You’s crazy. Hoyt Williams: No, I’m Hoyt Williams. Who’s you’s? Youth: My name is Cruthers. Hoyt Williams: Cruthers I am your savior and I’m here to visit this place. Cruthers: Why don’t you visit the get the fuck out of here room. Hoyt looks around confused for a moment. Hoyt Williams: Where’s that? The black man points toward the front door. Hoyt Williams: That’s the exit not a room. You must be confused. Listen what I need to do a mass here is some hand sanitizer, a golden challis, and a stuffed spinach and sausage pizza. Crothers: I don’t think you understand so let me put it white. LEAVE. Hoyt Williams: But this is my home. Crothers: I don’t know who the hell you are and your time is past man. Hoyt Williams: I’m not here to live on the past sinner I’m here to live for the now. Now get me my pizza so we can get started. I want to do a sermon. Crothers: Man this church is out of congregation. Hoyt Williams: A church with out people is like a restaurant with out food. Crothers: That analogy sucked because we got bibles just no people. So it would be like a restaurant with out customers which is what I said. Damn Hoyt Williams you dense. Hoyt Williams: Wait a minute. God is speaking with me. Hoyt looks up to the roof. Hoyt Williams: He’s telling me we can save this church with a big JESUS BASH this Sunday. He’s telling me we need to get on the radio, tell the folk, and for you to get me a pizza. Crothers: God wants me to get you a pizza? Hoyt Williams: Umm-hummm and a taco. Crothers: You are one crazy honkey. Hoyt Williams: Yea, and I’m back. Wait God is telling me who the devil is!! CHUCK BERRY!! Crothers: You is real crazy!! Hoyt Williams: My God man I can’t believe it took me this long to see it. Don’t you get it? His only number one hit was a song about a penis. He is known for taking pictures of woman against their will in bathrooms, and he’s into scat play. Crothers: Like singing? Hoyt Williams: Like poop eating. Crothers: CHUCK BERRY IS NOT SATAN. You’re making all this up. Hoyt Williams: Am I? God told me so. Go ahead do a web search of Chuck Berry and scat. He likes freezing his lovers poop. Listen is it not clear God is telling me this on the 30th anniversary of Elvis’ death? Think about it man. A man in a suit walks in with a brief case. Man: I’m here to tell you that unless you come up with the back taxes on this building the city is going to close you down and turn this place into a club owned by some old legendary musician. The two men look at each other in shock. Hoyt Williams: Told you. Crothers: We need to get to work and save this church!! The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever. - Psalms (ch. XXXVII, v. 29)
