THE RISP PRESENTS - A HOYT WILLIAMS FILM (GAN RP)

31 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-31-07 THE RISP PRESENTS - A HOYT WILLIAMS FILM VETRASTEIN!!!!! [The camera zooms in on an old building in black and white. Spooky trees blow as rain falls like the hopes and dreams of Chicago Bears fans. Creepy organ music starts to play as a voice over is heard.] V/O Greetings my children and welcome to my Halloween tale of horror. If you are faint of heart this tale is not for you. But if you are brave and think terrorists are cowards then welcome home. This tale begins just before Halloween of 2004 in the gymnasium owned by the Mad Mad Malice Man Duke Williams. He was working hard training his son on the skills of the wrestling mat. Scary good skills. Skills so scary rumor is Bela Lugosi learned everything he knew from watching tapes of Duke Williams. Let us join them already in progress. [Duke is in the ring as his son Hoyt Williams watches on. Duke is showing a move on a life like dummy named Bodadoll.] DUKE And that’s what we call the airplane spin!! Hoss it really is one of the most misunderstood moves in the sport. If you can make a dude dizzy you can dizzy and dazzle him the rest of the match. You dig? HOYT I Think. DUKE Never think just do. [Duke downs a fifth of Wild Turkey and gobbles like a mad man. Hoyt steps up in the center of the ring with the dummy. Hoyt sticks and moves then bounces off the back rope coming forward with a close line so hard even Jenna Jamison would cringe.] DUKE BODADOLL!!!! OH NO THE HORROR!! THE HOR-RRRRRRORRRRRRR!!! [The head of the dummy known as Bodadoll rolls on the mat like a bowling ball in the gutter of America.] DUKE I can’t believe you broke my dummy now what am I going to do? HOYT Would it be gay if I gave you some head? [Hoyt tosses the head of the bodadoll to Duke who catches it, and just looks at it in sadness.] DUKE This is not the time for levity. [The camera cuts to downtown New Orleans and the exterior of a creepy voodoo type shop.] V/O With a broken doll and broken spirits Hoyt Williams struggled in PRIME for the next few months. His father depressed over the loss of the BodaDoll went on a drinking binge that became legendary involving a one eyed hooker named Hope, but all hope was seemly lost. Until one night PRIME was in the big easy New Orleans, Louisiana. The father and son duo were in the seeder part of the city looking for a church to pray in when they stumbled upon an old doll maker. Doctor Vetrastein a Jewish mad scientist who converted to the Voodoo religion back in the 1958 Chipmunk ‘Witch Doctor’ craze ran the shop. [The camera cuts to the inside of the shop where we find out heroes Duke and Hoyt Williams looking at the creepy dolls.] DUKE How much for the John Bobbit doll? VETRASTEIN It’s half off. HOYT Forget that one, how much for the Drew Carry doll I love that TV show he’s on with the improv stuff. VETRASTEIN It’s yours if the PRICE IS RIGHT! DUKE Hold the phones how much for this, this, thing? [Duke points to the small yet muscular life like doll hidden in the dark corner.] HOYT What an ugly doll, forget that thing it has no Jesus in him, I can tell. VETRASTEIN Yes the over sized youth is right this doll is not for sale. DUKE Balls! I want to buy it. 2000. VETRASTEIN It’s not a doll it has a spirit! DUKE A spirit you say? You drive a hard bargain, FINE 2500!! VETRASTEIN Its brain is not right it is my saddest creation, it is a monster. DUKE Wait a minute!! If the brain is broken we’re back to 2000 unless it has some kind of warranty. HOYT Dad the thing is a piece of junk, lets leave! Something isn’t right with this place. God just texted me and told me something evil is here. VETRASTEIN GOD? GOD?? WHAT IS A GOD!! Is it a man who creates something out of nothing? Is it a man who can give life to what is lifeless?? Is that what you call GOD? HOYT Ummmmm that and a big house in the sky. VETRASTEIN You want this doll?? You want a GOD??? Take it. DUKE Are we at 2000? VETRASTEIN Take it for nothing but do not give it to nobody as we have a legally binding contract. You are the only owners. You understand?? DUKE FREE HOT DAMN! [Duke picks up the doll and drags it out of the shop as the camera fixes on the dolls eyes which begin blinking.] HOYT Isn’t this how that movie Chucky started? DUKE I thought this was a Frankenstein parody. VETRASTEIN MONSTER I thought I would have more lines. DUKE Did that thing just talk? HOYT That’s the most Vetra has ever said in his whole career. DUKE If this thing is alive we could train it to be our own monster, to do our dirty work. HOYT I don’t like this. [A montage plays of the Vetrastein Monster getting beaten down over and over in the training center by Duke and Hoyt is shown. Finally the montage stops with the Vetrastein Monster lying beaten in the ring.] HOYT The experiment is over this thing is worthless it can’t wrestle. This Vetra monster has no talent and will never amount to anything. I’d done with it. DUKE Maybe it sucks in the ring but can cut a great promo. Give me a mic. [Hoyt tosses Duke a mic who hands it to the Vetrastein Monster who tries to eat it.] DUKE NO BAD VETRASTEIN talk into it not eat it. VETRASTEIN MONSTER GRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR I VETRA GRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR HOYT Brilliant!! We found his skill. I mean he’s no Hoyt Williams but he’s sure as hell more entertaining then Killean! He could just growl in the mic and entertain millions, boy this guy is worthless! DUKE Well I don’t know, I think we can salvage him. Let’s create a stable since every stable needs a jobber. I mean the top guy can’t lose which is you. Then hire some muscle to protect you and when things get to heavy you can throw stupid over here, to the wolves. HOYT That just might be crazy enough to work. [The camera cuts to an old man sitting in a chair it is the voice over guy talking to the camera.] V/O GUY It was crazy enough to work as Hoyt Williams created the stable Imperium. They dominated the federation of PRIME for months to come. The Vetrastein Monster lost many matches and was used as a tool of his Hoytness. Grunting and losing all the while the Vetrastein Monster got bored and wanted his independents. Sick and tired of the lack of production from the Vetrastein Monster, and satisfied with his own success Hoyt Williams did the dirty deed and gave his slave not his freedom but a new master. [The camera cuts to Hoyt Williams getting his hair clipped by his stylist Renaldo.] RENALDO OH MY you are going to look like a million dollars when I’m done with you doll. HOYT Just get it done. Say speaking of dolls you want that one over there? RENALDO Oh my he’s so cute, and dumb as a rock. Can I use him for ANYTHING? HOYT Yea you can have him and do anything you want with him he’ll be yours. RENALDO OH MY!! [The hairdresser smacks the ass of the Vetrastein Monster as he escorts it out. Hoyt just shakes his head in disgust.] V/O Hoyt Williams would go on to win the Universal Championship in PRIME. Nobody really knows what became of the Peter Vetrastein Monster. Rumor is you can catch him on the Falcon video October release ‘Halloweeny of the Hung’ in which he plays a monster who is topped by the wolfman. But nobody really knows if that was him. All was well until about three weeks ago. [Hoyt Williams is sitting backstage of a PRIME event when an old man walks up to him.] HOYT Who the hell are you? DR. VETRASTEIN I’m the man who gave you the dummy monster many years ago. HOYT OH yea, I gave him to my hair dresser. DR. VETRASTEIN YOU what? You fool!! I told you very clearly not to give the monster away. We have a legally binding contract. If you couldn’t take care of it, you were supposed to call me. HOYT Whatever. DR. VETRASTEIN The only way to solve this is one of you two have to be destroyed or the world could end. HOYT OH yea? Hey, you ever have an Arnie Palmer it’s half lemonade and half iced tea! Amazing. DR. VETRASTEIN That’s not a healthy attitude but then again it’s not a healthy world is it? HOYT WHAT? DR. VETRASTEIN YOU MUST KILL THE MONSTER!! HOYT Hey ahhh listen kid I have other things to do. I’m running for president. If you want to take the dummy monster from the hairdresser that’s fine with me. I won’t go on TV and cry like Ellen as I don’t care. DR. VETRASTEIN Destroy the monster or he will destroy you. HOYT Vetra couldn’t destroy an ice-cream cone in a heat wave. DR VETRASTEIN Do it or be destroyed. HOYT Will you people leave me the hell alone if I beat this dummy? DR VETRASTEIN Yes. HOYT Then it shall be done. Because all you people are really starting to bug me. At great American Nightmare I will destroy the dummy once and for all and be done with this disgusting piece of trash that is my past. Free and clear baby, then I can run for president and be even more important then I already am. DR VETRASTEIN GOOD LUCK you’ll need it. [Evil Laugh] HOYT ITS TIME TO SEND THIS DUMMY TO HELL ONCE AND FOR ALL!!!!! It’s the American way! THE END or is it? =w=
