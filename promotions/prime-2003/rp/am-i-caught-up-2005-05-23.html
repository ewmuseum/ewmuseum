Am I Caught Up?

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/05 I'm So Caught Up I don't know what you been told about these fools that you control now every body knows the brother calm and I covered the arm smuggle the tougue girlssay the brothers the bomb but aint no ladies in my life other then moms thats to this girl change my way and it even feels strange to say im so caught upcause I cant stand to admit cupid got good aim this man been hit 'After being faced with a critical loss many people would think that I would slip back into depression. I, myself think I would have slipped back into depression. After that loss last Sunday, I realized something, 'I'm, caught up!' Many people will ask what does that mean? What does that have to do with anything, but I understand fully. My attention has shifted from my wrestling career to her. This new girl in my life, she could just be a fling it doesn't really matter cause I have shifted my focus on her completly. That loss was my fault, and retribution is need and will be expected.' I sit here alone in a dark room all by myself weighing out my different options. Out of no where music is heard, it is the song Caught Up by Usher but it is the remix version featuring Fabolous. The chorus is heard, we then the song just ends. I'm soCaught upGot me feelin' itCaught upI don't know what it isBut it seems shes got me twisted Confrontation Time I sat there staring at my phone, wondering should I call her or should I wait for her to give me a call. Almost instantiously my phone starts to vibrate in my hand. I take a long hard stare at the number and see that it is her, she just so happened to call.'Hello?' She answered back rather quickly, 'Hello Peter, I am sorry to hear about last week. You will bounce back.' Without giving her to long to say anything else I quickly interupt, 'Last week, was last week. Now I need to focus on the future, and that starts on Wednesday with the Dark Age.' 'I'm glad you can be optomistic.' She has a slight shaking sound in her voice. I think nothing of it and continue the conversation, ' Sometimes that is all you can do. Forget about the past and look towards the future.' 'That is a great attitude, Peter. I am proud of you.' 'You might be, but I am not of myself.... that is a different story though.' Dropping such a hint will get her attention and maybe she will pry and probe to figure out what is wrong with me. 'Peter, what is wrong? It's only one match, you really shouldn't take it that serious.' Her voice was very soothing but I could tell she knew something bad was about to come from this conversation. 'It might be only one match to you and every other on looker, but to me that might have been my oppertunity, the only fifteen minutes of fame I might have ever gotten. But I am going to move on and be strong.' 'I guess I see your point, but the way you wrestled the other night, I am positive you will get another chance, another shot. As we all know you deserve it.' Her voice continued in the same demeanor but I could tell she was still trying to pry a little more then she probably should have. 'I think your right, babe. But I think...' Once I let those words out I knew something bad was about to happen, I knew she caught on and I was right she interrupted. 'This can't be good.' Her hesitation made it obvious my plan was falling in perfectly. 'No, no worries Melissa. I am not going to do anything I would regret but there is something I would like to happen this week.' I wanted to try and drag this out for a little while and see what how she would react each time. 'Ok, then continue.' Her voice sounded like she was almost ready to cry, you know that stage where you are teary eyed. 'There's nothing to be worried about.' I used this line to maybe stop the tears from flowing as I knew she was looking way to far into this then she should. 'I think this week I should be alone, I mean I need to focus on my match this week to reassure myself that I can still hang with these other wrestlers.' At that point I started holding my breath hoping she was expecting something worse and would be happy with my simple and easy news. 'DAMN YOU PETER! You had me scared. I completly understand, I am here if you need me babe.' The reaction I got from her scared me at first but gave me some gratification knowing she was thinking worse and became happy with such simple news. 'Thank You for understanding.' 'I know how much wrestling means to you, if that means I don't see you for a week, that means I wait another week before I see you simple as that.' I would have never expected it to be so simple, but I was relieved that it was. 'I will come back to Massachusetts as soon as this ReVolution is over. I just need some time to prepare and get myself on the right foot again.' 'That is fine, you know if you ever need someone to talk to my phone is always by my side. Give it a ring and we will talk at your conveinence.' 'Thanks babe, I need to get back to these notes, I will give you a call when I get a chance.' 'Alright Peter, have a great week and good luck, I will be watching.' Her voice has changed from being nervous to cool, calm, and collective. 'Thanks, have a good one.' I spoke the final words and hung up the phone. I went back into my own little reverance of what had just happened. I told her that I didn't want to see her for a week hoping I could win a match because of it. The thing is, I honestly think she might be the reason I lost. Is it Denial? Or am I blinded by the light? Hours after hanging up with her the conversation is still fresh in my mind. Had I done the right thing? Probably, was it all worth it definitly. I might not speak with the gorgeous blonde for five days but I am going to take out the Dark Age. Once I prove that I can do it, we will see that happen, then I will take my time with her. Once that happens I will learn to balance the two of them. My career and my girlfriend, something everyone always trys to juggle. I will accomplish it, when is just the question. 'Jackson Darilek, you got away with something last week. But one thing I can tell you, is if and when you face me again, you don't stand a snowball's chance in hell of getting that lucky again. I will become cellibate if needed! Your turn will come and next time I won't be caught up in the flash and lights of the PPV, or the 36DD in my face each and every night leading up to it! Any man would have done the same thing had he been in my shoes, save for you. We all know that women aren't your strong point. Darilek, I will get mine, and you, you will be forever known as the man who managed the shortest title reign in PRIME history! Now I have given my thoughts on that match, I now will focus all my thoughts on the Dark Age. The time has come and you will see a new Peter Vetra, a new and improved. A winning and successful one. This time she will not blur my vision, I will not be tainted but will be victorious.
