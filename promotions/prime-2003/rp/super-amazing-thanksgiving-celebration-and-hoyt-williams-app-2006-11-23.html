Super Amazing Thanksgiving Celebration and Hoyt Williams Appreciation Show

23 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-23-06 Announcer: Ladies and Gentleman LIVE from the Chicago Theater in down town Chicago, Illinois it’s the Super Amazing Thanksgiving Celebration and Hoyt Williams Appreciation Show! Now lets join in on our host extraordinaire; the patriarch of professional wrestling, the Turnbuckle troubadour, the President of Wrestling, Mr. Williams, the Malice Man, DUKE WILLIAMS!!! ‘Hail to the Chief’ plays as the theater crowd cheers in a dignified applause as the red curtain lifts and revels the stage. The setting is a typical upper middle class Thanksgiving Dinner table. Duke sits at the head of the table as a harem of scantly dressed blond women sit around the table. Duke is dressed in a Hugh Heffner style bath robe. Duke Williams: Anybody need some more stuffing? Girl1: Oh no you gave us enough stuffing last night. Duke Williams: Can I get some more breast meat? The girls all rip off their tops exposing their perfect racks as the band starts playing a show tune. The girls get up and start dancing around the table. Duke wastes no time jumping up on top of the dinner table as he starts singing his big opening number to the tune of Ella Fitzgerald’s ‘I got Rhythm’. Duke Williams (singing): Boobs can be bouncy with never a sigh Need a bigger cup size then what money can buy Ho’s in the kitchen sing their dayful of songs Why shouldn't we sing along? I'm horny all the day Happy with my life How do I get that way? Look at what I've got: I got women, I got turkey, I got whisky Who could ask for anything more? I've got a large penis in my pants I've got my money Who could ask for anything more? Old man Stewart I don't mind him You won't find him 'round my door I've got pussy I've got sweet asses I've got my legend Who could ask for anything more? I got women, I got turkey, I got whisky Who could ask for anything more? I've got a large penis in my pants I've got my money Who could ask for anything more? I've got rhythm, I've got rhythm ... Duke hits the big finish in a chorus line dance with the girls. Just as the song finishes the door bell rings. Duke Williams: I hope that’s Hoyt, he said he would bring the turkey. The door swings open and in enters Hoyt Williams carrying a slab or ribs. The crowd gives a loud cheer as Hoyt walks in. Duke Williams: Where is the turkey? Hoyt Williams: I had to stop at church so I couldn’t bring it with me. Duke Williams: Why not? Hoyt Williams: Because they use such FOWL language! The crowd laughs. Duke Williams: Now our thanksgiving is ruined. What are we going to do with ribs on Thanksgiving. I’m going to run to the store and see if I can find us a turkey and save our holiday. Duke exits the stage as the girls put their tops back on and circle around Hoyt. Girl: Oh great Hoyt, please tell us the biblical story of Thanksgiving. Hoyt Williams: Well the oppressors in England, the demons with bad teeth and Smiley like sense of humor; were holding the white Jesus loving man back. So God told his children to set sail, and head due west, where they would find a land of plenty. This land was created for white men and women who love each other, and most of all worship God. But you see Satan had heard of this plan that God wanted to give the Jesus loving people their own land. So he created the savage Indians. Girl: Oh no. Hoyt Williams: Oh yes. These barbaric Injuns hated the way of life you and I share. They hated our freedom, our cell phones, and Jesus. They wanted to oppress us much in the style Shawn Stewart attempts to bore us. Satan was smart, he gave these savages large numbers to overcome the Jesus loving white warriors we now call pilgrims. When we landed at Hoyt Rock the Injuns pretended to be our friends, just like the Jews do at the banks to take our money. Girl: And the blacks before they rob us? Hoyt Williams: Right Julie. So God sent down the ‘Saint of Killing Injuns’ John Wayne to warn the people of the evil Injuns intentions. The superior intelligence of the God loving people devised a plan to eat the turkey of the damned, and then give them gifts of poisoned blankets. Since poor people, old people, and Injuns get sick easy the plan worked like a charm and all the Injuns were wiped out except in Hollywood where we continued to kick the Injuns asses for years to come. Mel Gibson: Until of coarse the Jews took over Hollywood! The crowd cheers as Mel Gibson enters through the front door making a cameo. Hoyt Williams: Mel, I was just telling the girls the history of Thanksgiving. I just got to the part where we wiped out the Injuns, then threw a big party to give thanks to God for giving us germ filled blankets! Hence Thanksgiving. Mel Gibson: You see Saint John Wayne made great movies of himself destroying Injuns and Mexicans as religious odes to the battle of Turkey hill for many years. Then the Jews with the help of Satan killed John Wayne and turned Hollywood into an anti-religious, violent against other white guys, homo-friendly, drug promoting, snore fest of replayed scripts and black Honeymooners. Hoyt Williams: To da mooooon bitchhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh! Mel Gibson: Anyway I have to run I have a hot date with officer sweet tits. Hoyt Williams: Thanks for stopping in Mel. As Mel Gibson walks out in walks Michael Richard’s walks in to a wild cheer. Michael Richards: I brought the eggnog OH YEAAAAAAAAAAAAA!! Black Guy From The Crowd: Why don’t you shut your mouth, you unfunny cracker. Michael Richards: SHUT UP SHUT UP SHUT UP!! IF THIS WAS 50 YEARS AGO you would be hung upside down with a spoon on your balls. Get this niger out of here nigger nigger nigger nigger nigger nigger nigger nigger !! This is what happens when you interrupt a white man, Nigger! A parrot in the background repeats the horrible words as the crowd gasps in disbelief. Hoyt tries to calm the tall actor down reminding him it’s a family show and live. Parrot: Squawk NIGGER SQUAWK! Michael Richards: You said it friend! A knock is heard at the door Hoyt opens it up and it’s a younger actor dressed as an old man in a clan outfit. Old Man Stewart: Hello I’m Old Man Stewart you know Shawn’s daddy. I heard the N-word and knew I was on friendly grounds. I mean I’m from Kentucky and made my living getting my ass kicked by your father Duke Williams, so you know I’m a racist. Stupid as hell as well. The south will rise again and Shawn Stewart is awesome! YEE-HAW!! Hoyt Williams: We don’t support racism here. There is a fine line between what is truth, and what is over the top. Both of you guys are sinning taking things way to far. If you are a man of common intellect you understand what is poking fun at mankind and our differences; and what is hate. You are engaging in hate and that is not what God is about. He is about love and giving Hoyt Williams many titles. The crowd cheers. Old Man Stewart: Hey my son is going to fight you. I think he’s a homosexual with his ugly lady friend as his beard. Hells bells I heard he had thanksgiving dinner with the lady’s family over and a fight broke out now if that ain’t Jerry Springer south style I sure as shit don’t know what is. YEE HAW!! My boy hates the blacks too!! Did you know Shawn Stewart used to crap his pants all the way up until high school? A big stream of chewing tobacco is spit from the mouth of Old Man Stewart. Old Man Stewart: Hey MAN! What’s with all the jungle bunnies on the TV playing catch? Turn some damn Nascar on, it’s the only sport the white man still can call his own. I mean it takes a lot of skill to drive in circle for hours on end. Michael Richards: Oh yea the shines are all over the place. THIS IS WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU INTERRUPT THE WHITE MAN!! NIGGER NIGGER NIGGER NIGGER NIGGER NIGGER YOU SEE WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU INTERRUPT THE WHITE MAN? Hoyt Williams: Ok, you two over the top nut jobs, this is a family show. Now shut up and get the hell out in the name of JESUS! ****WACK**** Hoyt knocks Michael Richards out as the crowd goes wild. Old Man Stewart thinks twice about standing up for Richards and instead helps him out of the house as the crowd gives Hoyt a standing ovation. Hoyt Williams: If there’s one thing I can’t abide, it’s an ethnocentric racist! Now where was I? Oh yea talking about killing the Injuns. Girl: You just finished. Man, with no turkey this could be the worst thanksgiving ever. Girl2: Yea. This stinks. Hoyt Williams: I’ll cheer you up. How about a song? The band kicks up a stones tune as the crowd claps along. Hoyt grabs a mic and starts walking around the girls. Hoyt Williams (singing): I went down to the Boston Market To get your turkey stuffed I was standing in line with Mr. Williams And man, did he look pretty ill We decided that we would have a prayer My favorite one, about blood red I gave my sermon to Mr. Williams Yeah, and he said one word to me, and that was "dead" I said to him You can't always get what you want, no! You can't always get what you want (tell ya baby) You can't always get what you want (no) But if you try sometimes you just might find You get what you need Oh yes! Woo! You get what you need--yeah, oh baby! Oh yeah! I saw Shawn Stewart today at the REV taping He looked like a defeated man He was practiced at the art of deception Well I could tell by his blood-stained hands You can't always get what you want You can't always get what you want You can't always get what you want But if you try sometimes you just might find You just might find You get what you need You can't always get what you want (no, no baby) You can't always get what you want You can't always get what you want But if you try sometimes you just might find You just might find You get what you need, ah yes... The music fades as the girls exit and Hoyt sits down alone at the table to give his monologue. The lights dim as soft sad music begins to play. Hoyt Williams: Alone and forsaken by god and by man. I have failed to achieve in the GTT, and now I sit with no turkey on turkey day. No gobbler to gobble up because I failed to bring it. Coal, left in the dark, alone, pressured through time turns into a diamond...perhaps I am the diamond in the rough . Perhaps that diamond is the jewel in the crown of freedom to be placed on the head of king of kings sitting on his throne in Kingdom Come. For I am the son of man, and the cross I carry is the burden of all. But tonight my thoughts wonder like a ship floundering around lost at sea in a storm of mediocrity. I listen to the words of Mr. Stewart and roll my superior biblical eyes at his tripe simplicity. Shawn Stewart master of unarmed combat, I laugh at thee. If you watched my match last week, you would have witnessed the master of all combat. If you truly are the master of unarmed, I shall enter armed. Genius you can call me, but others simply say Hoyt. My despair is due to my lack of poultry; my excitement is due to the fact I’m facing an other asshole face, sinner, who thinks he’s Asain. Well Mr. Stewart you should accept what God bestowed upon you instead of training as one of the slant eyed devils. Now your sin is my pain and my goal is to transfer that pain, that sin, that evilness back to you the damned. The lights turn back up as Duke Williams enters through the front door carrying a cake. Duke Williams: No turkey but I found a sweet banana creme pie! Hoyt Williams: I don’t even think banana cream could cheer me up tonight. Only a turkey would make my frown turn upside down. The Malice Man sits down at the table. He takes a plop of stuffing and places it on his plate. Its kind of a down mood as both men appear depressed, void of turkey. Duke Williams: I could go for a joint. Hoyt Williams: What’s the appeal of pot. Duke Williams: Have you ever seen Scooby-Doo? Hoyt Williams: Of coarse. Duke Williams: You see, when your not high, Scooby-Doo isn’t funny but you still watch it. Hoyt Williams: Yea I never laugh, but its true you have to sit and watch when its on. Its always the same plot but oddly its interesting. Duke Williams: Ok, when your high on pot Scooby-Doo is funny as hell. But you can’t follow along with the plot it gets real confusing and you don’t understand why Scooby is dressed like a waiter serving pasta to ghosts. That’s the dilemma; when your sober you can follow the plot of Scooby Doo, but its not funny. When your high its the funniest show on the earth, but you don’t know what the hell is going on. Hoyt Williams: I think I need to be high to understand what the hell your talking about. Duke Williams: No, you see if you were high what I have been saying would be funny. But you wouldn’t be able to follow along. Hoyt Williams: But I’m not following along sober. Duke Williams: Then your shit out of luck. The door bell rings as both Williams’ look at each other. Duke Williams: Are you expecting anybody else? Hoyt Williams: No. You? Duke Williams: Nope. The two men to the door and open it with a big smile on their faces when they see who it is. Duke Williams: President Bush! This is a pleasant surprise. Hoyt Williams: G-dawg! What brings you here? The crowd gives a standing ovation as President Bush walks in. President Bush: Well I heard about your lack of Turkey crisis. I was on my way to Crawford when Skipper...that’s what I call the Captain of Air Force One...when Skip told me of your woes. Duke Williams: How did they know we didn’t have a turkey? President Bush: Wire Tapping of coarse. The three men laugh as does the audience. A chef enters from stage right with a silver covered patter. President Bush: Here is your Turkey. HE HE HE The platter is uncovered and a picture of Shawn Stewart sits in the center. Everybody again laughs. President Bush: You get it...he’s a turkey. HE HE HE HE!!! I make myself laugh. Here is your real bird. In walks Laura Bush to a standing ovation as she delivers a real turkey. Duke Williams: Would you stay and join us? President Bush: I’d love too!! Hoyt Williams: You guys saved Thanksgiving this is the best thanksgiving ever! Everybody sits down at the table as the band plays out and the curtains drops.
