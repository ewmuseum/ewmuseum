The Sinners And The Saints [3]

Date: May 24, 2005 The Sinners And The Saints [1]The Sinners And The Saints [2]___________________________BAM! Time is relative to the observer. The time it takes a bullet to fire from its dark nest of twisted desires to hit the intended target is lightning quick. You close your eyes and pull the trigger, and when you open them, the damage has already been done. But think of how the bullet sees it. The bullet has a life of its' own. Well, that's my theory anyway. If everything is supposed to have meaning, it must have a soul as well. The philosophical way to look at it is that the bullet's life is scaled down from our sense of time. Milliseconds must be decades, seconds must be a millennium. The bullet is perfect. It is Godlike. It dies when it hits the target. But in the travel, it must have a life. It must have feelings. It must have an understanding of what it is meant to do. It lives so others can die. It travels to kill. That must be a very taxing existence. If it has a conscious, it must feel like it is doing the wrong thing. Yet it can not do anything about it. Cosmic forces are at work and they carry it. The bullet feels out of control. It can not control the existence it leads. But we...we humans...are we any more than bullets at the fundamental level? We can not control our situations. We lie though and say we can. We say we can be anything we desire, so the lies say. But once you pull up the cloth and see through the bullshit, you see what existence is. Nothing but heartbreak and scars. Barely an existence worth living. Stay there with the bullet, and you can live a lifetime in the split second it takes to rip through muscle and sinew. In that moment, I feel like God. I am taking away and creating life almost simultaneously. I am God. The gun, metallic and cold, is merely the Shepard. The bullet is the result. It is human and violent and perfect yet is so arrogant that it simply does not drop when it sees what it must do. We can't control our fates, but we can simply stop. Yet we don't. We continue to do the things in such an arrogant manner, talking out of both sides of our mouths. The bullet...is human. It is violent and perfect. *** My name is Killean Sirrajin. I'm not an ordinary guy in ordinary town. I do not live an ordinary life. I would die. If I could. The Zippo flips to life, showing her my face. She did a good number on me, I'll say. Fucked my eye up good and bruised me all around the grill. The ventilation in the place is so poor that the sweat comes on from just sitting there. I can see the thin sheet of perspiration on her body as well. Oh, in another life, I'd rip her pants off and go to town on her and show her just what kind of man I am. But, that isn't this life. I light the cigarette dangling from my lips, sucking in as much in as I can. I blow a large collection of smoke from my nose as I flip the lighter shut. The only thing around now is the orange ember of the cigarette. I actually live in a shit hole of a rat infested gem. I live in Carcer City. They say everyone comes here to escape something. Usually, it's me. I get up and fumble with the light switch a bit. And when the light comes to, I'm reminded of the rundown apartment. Standing there in only a pair of camouflage pants with the silver handgun clasped in my hand, somehow it feels right. I reach into my pocket and I find Lucy. Lucy is a beat up quarter. Lucy is the Judge. 'Heads you live...' I trail off, flipping Lucy skyward and snatching her from the sky. 'Tails you die.' I don't enjoy this. She sits there, in all her beauty. She taunts me as she struggles in the chair, the bondage holding her in place. All I can decipher is her whimpers, the ball gag hooked in her mouth doing the job of muffling her screams. I hate it when they beg and scream and all that shit. Her eyes are red, tears falling down her face. Her mascara is a complete mess. She's a big sweat ball. This isn't what she expected. 'MMMMMMMMMMPPPPPPPPPPPHHHHHHHHHH!' she cries. I'm wondering how a dame like her arrived here, but then I remember the bat to the skull and the blurry picture comes together clear. Translucent. I feign craning my head down to look at the coin in hand, instead bringing up the gun in line with the bitch's skull. She screams through her gag. I pull the trigger. Shuuuuuuuuuuu! The bullet passes through her skull, smoke bellowing out of the silencer. I do enjoy this. I turn around and flip the light switch off. I can hear the rain pounding into the building and the sound of thunder outside. I love the rain. My name is Killean Sirrajin. I am a bounty hunter. *** The sirens blare and carry into the night sky. Another long night on the job, but they say rain is supposed to come. It's like an omen in Carcer. You wouldn't know unless you did what I do. Every time it rains in this godforsaken place, something bad happens. Bodies turn up and good cops get killed and four days down the road we are all sobbing and drinking to lives that we did not really know existed until they were extinguished. The irony in all of it was funny, yet at the same time sickening. Late nights for two years and I've seen plenty of rainy nights. But even before they gave me the shiny badge and rank of detective, the rain always brought the spectrum of death and pain. Worked five years before the promotion. Every time the weatherman said rain...I hoped to God he was full of shit. The sight of those graves and the sobbing families...it sticks with you forever. It haunts you as you lay in bed. I hate the rain. Looking up, I can see that the weatherman isn't wrong. Then, the lightning flashes and the skies echo with fright. I tremble as slight rain drops begin to fall onto my coat. I look into the sky and raise my middle finger to it. I hate the rain. Crocken screams as I put the key into the door of the car and unlock it. 'Amy...units are needed! A body turned up! Some young girl...she's real fucked from what they say.' 'Cut to ribbons?' I ask. It's almost second nature by now. 'No...but she got skullfucked by a bullet or two. They're diggin' her up now...they say that some former cop did it...Brand...' I shut the door before he finishes. He doesn't cut the victim up. Not my problem. My problem cuts people to shreds. Young girls. He rapes them and then brutally kills them. Been on the case for a year now and nothing has turned up outside twelve victims. Their faces are twisted in pain and agony as the CSI comes in an does the clean up job. And he's gotten better with time, as he has been able to cut them at the joints. From what they say, cutting at the joint, the knife does the work. First he cuts out the tongue. Then, he cuts their fingers off. Then, the eyes. Then he brings over the saw and does the rest of the job. They are barely recognizable. It is disgusting. It's the one thing I hate more than the rain. Thrush is only a throw away from here. The drive is short and sweat, the only sound being my cell phone ringing uncontrollably. I pulled my shift. Someone else can clean up this mess. Not like Vega or Emilio are there for me on one of the cut jobs we find. And I just want to sit in the tub and soak for a while. It would be nice to get laid, but I am not going to complain as long as I have a little bubble bath. Been almost a year since I had my last boyfriend and sometimes it gets tough. I pull into the lot and lock up the car. It's all a blur as I slowly make my way to the top level of my apartment and enter. It is completely dark and cold. Like a tomb. I like it that way. I throw my jacket onto the couch and begin to unbutton my shirt as I walk to the window. Looking out there, the rain is really coming down. The CSI is going to have a hard time collecting evidence for that case. I peel off my shirt and toss it behind me lackadaisically. Standing there in only a bra, you'd think I'd feel some sort of shame. But I'm just trying to relax. The knife presses hard against my throat. I can feel hot breath down my neck. I tense up. No. 'Scream and you're dead.' He unclasps my bra. No. He presses the knife harder against my throat. He giggles like a damn child. 'Yer a pretty little bitch, aren't you? Too bad the cops aren't going recognize your face.' He grabs at my breast. A tear falls from my eye. I hate the rain. *** The bullet rips through his skull. It enters peaceably enough, but explodes in silence out of the back. Blood and brain fragments shoot up in a thick discharge as he falls back, void of life. The helmet he wore...the armor, it did nothing. The protective plastic spider webbed just before the bullet connected and dropped him. Single Action Army. It lives up to the name. But two more armored SWAT team members burst on through and fire at me with shotguns. I throw myself out of the way, acting outside myself as I fire the Colt again, nailing one in the neck. He falls over, grabbing at his neck and sucking in panicked breath after panicked breath. The other one fires, and I get hit. Not directly, more like grazed. When you get hit in a fire fight, you instantly feel sick in the pit of your stomach. It’s the feeling of sudden death coming over you. I nip up to my feet, shooting him directly in the wrist, dropping the shotgun. All or nothing time. I grab into the pocket and charge. I run into him as he's roaring in pain, sticking him with the switchblade I had concealed in my pocket. I stab and pull up. I don't want him to live. This way, I cut apart his vitals and so on. I pull back and he drops, falling as nothing more than a mess of flesh to the floor. The pain finally powers into my brain as I grab at my left shoulder. Blood. And it is pouring. Maybe I was more than grazed, but who the hell knows? 'GUNFIRE! GUNFIRE!' More of them. FUCK. I lift the shotgun off the ground, and the second I see someone come through the door, I fire and pump. Again. Fire and pump. Fire and pump. Fire and pump. Click and pump. Empty. I'm dead. Dropped three more, but more are on their way and my arm is starting to really gush now. It's falling everywhere, and I then another SWAT comes down and fires. Caught in the upper thigh. I drop immediately. Dammit Brandon...get to your feet. You've been set up. She'll never be able to rest until you find her. She can't be dead. She was kidnapped. You’re going to find her. And then, we're going to run away. Oh so far away. The pit seems to grow larger in my gut. I grab at the thigh with my left hand and try to sucker the guy into a false since of security. He comes down. Can't kill me, has to take me in alive. I shoot up and stab him, catching him and buying myself seconds. The blood has soaked my left hand, and I grab at the floor and slightly pick myself up. I drag myself into the bathroom, collapsing when a gunshot can be heard soaring just above me. I fall down, falling onto the tile within the bathroom, blood all over the place. I'm gushing. I'm in trouble. They're charging. I'm as good as incarcerated. Lindsay...Lindsay...I've failed you again. I look up, and see it...the window. Right by the tub. A window. Come on Brandon. Pick yourself up. Jump out the window. Jump out the window? What will that solve? You can't find the killer if you get stuck in prison again, can you? It'll be life this time. And I can't save her if I die jumping out the window like Superman. You won't survive this if you stay here. They'll kill you. You took down too many. They'll kill you. But I'll die jumping. The risk of certain death and possible certain death are nominal. Come on Brandon, they’re coming with guns and killing and you're gonna die. I pick myself up and limp to the back of the bathroom as fast as possible. I can hear their boots stomping into the floor boards. Got to make this quick. I dive headfirst through the window. It shatters. I hear gunshots. No more searing pain. Wasn't hit. Wasn't grazed. UMPH! Nailed a pipe. My body spins. I look down. Nothing but a dumpster. I'm sorry Lindsay. Falling. No control. This is suicide. I'm so fucking sorry Lindsay. I land with a forceful thud in the dumpster. The plastic cover to it collapsed under my weight. The force jarred me into unconsciousness. I'm so fucking sorry Lindsay. *** The hardest part about killing is taking care of the body. At least I found it to be. The killing itself isn't hard if you do it enough times. And sure, that is morbid but I don't fucking care. Wolfenden said that you can't leave a trace. Make them unrecognizable and then the cops can't circle the wagons as hard. So I have to cut out her teeth. That is fairly easy, if I get the hunting knife out. I try to make a game out of it. And when I am done, I put the teeth in a box so I can make a necklace from them. I have a lot of teeth, but they sometimes are not good. Hers are very good. She too good care of them. Brushed and flossed and all that shit. I almost feel sorry. The rain makes it easier. Softens the ground and gets rid of the prints. Like I needed more help in this bog. The bog just outside town is probably the best place to do this. Disposing of bodies is easy once you get the hang of it. Nobody has found one of mine. Even with the downpour, I can't help but feel hot as I power the shovel into the ground and dig this hole. It's hard to do, but a six foot hole finally gets dug up and I can dump her body. I wrap blankets around her before I leave the apartment. Don't want blood and skin to get all over my truck. I sling her over my shoulder effortlessly. Within that moment, I throw her into the hole, and then fill it back up. Take about an hour and a half but the job gets done and I can go home, get a shower, and call my kid. I rest my back against the truck, lighting up a cigarette. Well, more like trying through the rain. I try to relax. To calm my nerves. While it is easy, I always have a sneaking suspicion that I'm going to get caught. My nerves are on fire. Adrenaline begins to flow. I've got a bad feeling. Lights. Head fucking lights. FUCK! I flick the smoke and jump into the truck and start it up and floor it. However, it dies on me. I can see them over in the distance. Cop cars. Oh shit. Nothing. Come on! They get closer. COME ON! START! I start the truck up...the engine revs. I floor it and almost bury myself by getting stuck in the mud, but I shift gears quick and get the hell out of Dodge. All I can hope for is that they don't look. All I can hope for is that the body isn't recognizable. The storm clouds roar with thunder. I almost lost it all tonight. I can't wait to see my kid. Can't wait to see my ex's face. *** His tie hung loosely as he picked up the glass of whiskey and downed it. He ran his hand over his face and then against the short stubble on his head, giving off a yawn. It had been a long day and it was time let off a little steam. No lucky breaks. Just circumstantial evidence and nothing hard enough to lean on. His tired eyes looked around, though what he saw was not registering in his brain. He felt old. Tired. Worn. Youngblood brought the past back with him out of that cell. The past catches up to you when you do not acknowledge it, or so it would seem. But where Brandon had 543 Macy, Emilio had nothing. He had no leads. All he had was a feeling of complete helplessness. Helpless in the fact that there was a disease running rampant through Carcer, rotting it to the core. He gave off a deep sigh as he sat there, eyes fixated on the glass. He was busy counting the droplets of amber that were still in the crystal cup, trying to collect himself. He was worn and tired. Tomorrow promised the same. Punch in and punch out and nothing gets solved. Emilio's eyes twitched at the thought of what was going through Carcer at this very moment. A crime lord who has the Mayor in his pocket. A serial rapist who cuts his victims apart when he’s done with them. And that was what was accepted fact. Deville gold was in a lot of pockets. He had the church. He had the department. He even had the city council. He scratched at the hairs on his lower jaw, the feeling of itchiness consuming his depressing thoughts. What he should be doing is going home. Seeing the wife and thanking the good lord that he was going to be a father. But she was going to give birth to something wonderful in the darkest of places. There was no hope. He could not protect the child. Not here. This place eats that sort of stuff up and thrives on pain and misery. Another heavy sigh. The weight of the world was on his shoulders. He wished he could simply accept what was going on around him. To just be oblivious to the things going on around him. Others must see it. They must feel it. But they just shrug it off and walk away and let someone else deal with it. No, he couldn't do that. There was the chance he could get Deville. Somehow, someway. 'I'll have what he's havin'.' Her voice was angelic. It snapped him out of his lull and forced him to take notice. Sitting beside him was a beautiful young thing with long brunette hair. She was sleek and perfect, dressed in a pair of jeans and a tight black shirt with matching black leather boots. Her face was not painted, instead it was one hundred percent natural. She would have had the envy of Helen of Troy. She sat there, chomping on a stick of gum as she looked at the various bottles of liquid courage against the wall behind the bartender. 'You know, Karina, you aren't supposed to be here.' He tried to think with his brain. But he knew her. Knew her ways all too well to think he would get off that easy. Just seeing her again got his blood pumping and his mouth wet in anticipation of the old memories that would flood back. 'Like I listen anyway...' she trailed off as the barkeep handed her a matching glass of bourbon. 'Thanks.' He could not look at her. If he did, the animal inside would threaten to rip through. His breathing was already growing more labored. 'Using people...you get off on that?' He heard a chuckle as she lifted the glass to her lips and took a quick sip. 'You know my intentions.' Emilio likened it to dominos. You knock down important pieces by tipping over lesser pieces to gain the result you desire. 'He's done ten years already. What you want to do will put him away for life.' Again, a chuckle. This brought a flood of differing emotions into Emilio. It was the arrogance, the utter disregard for human life that he could not tolerate. Even if the cause was just, he couldn't allow her to do this to him. 'You think he's going to go along with it just like that?' 'You said he would.' Caught red handed. He sighed yet again. 'And you promised promotions out of this?' 'Promotion for you...' she trailed off, taking another sip. 'Me...I think I'm gonna get closure.' Rage grimaced at the thought of what that meant. 'You know, the second Pierce finds out about this...' 'He'll applaud us.' She interrupted. Those words, they were like the advice from the left hand path. But in the world of Karina Wolfenden, there was not a right or left hand path. Just a rush and a job and a desired result. 'They have him in the Macy halfway house. What we need to do is get there as soon as possible.' He wasn't going to do this alone. 'I'm going to get Vega and we're going to make sense of this all.' 'You don't understand...do you?' She cackled. 'I have a plan already in motion. If you want to go over there, fine, but you're only going to be wasting your time.' Emilio stood up and grabbed her shoulder. She looked into his eyes. Those emerald balls of fire that shone brightly and soothed the soul of all the aches and pains. Those emeralds that were a gift from God and a cruel joke from Satan. He could not feel bad looking into them. 'You might want to get home. Got a long day tomorrow. Take your coat or you'll catch a cold.' 'In this...' Emilio smirked, trying his best not to visibly tremble before her. 'Nah. You know the rain here is hot as piss. I'm more liable to boil.' The two exchanged a pained chuckle. Partners in crime. He'd go to the ends of the Earth if she asked him to. He threw his coat over his shoulders and made his way to the door, shoving it open with a powerful push. The barkeep yelled as he was making his exit to catch his attention. 'Sir, the drinks?' He winked towards him and shot a finger in a lackadaisical fashion. 'Bill the broad.' Karina brought her glass up and gave him a death glare. 'Thanks. Like I didn't have enough trouble payin' the bills as it was.' Emilio merely laughed as he exited like Bogart, disappearing into the night. She exhaled. 'Bastard.' She downed the rest of the glass of whiskey, letting out a pleasurably sigh as it coated her insides with a pleasant burn. The ring of her cell phone was the only thing that snapped her out of her ecstasy. She dug into her pocket, flipping her phone open and answering. 'This line ain't supposed to used unless something urgent comes up.' But something urgent had come up. Namely, seven SWAT team members gunned down. Namely, a young girl turning up dead. And namely, a certain Black Dog falling out a window. For the first time, she became tense. She panicked. 'He's still alive...right?' All that planning. Closure was so close. And this fool had to go out and play stupid. He just had to kill enough SWAT team members to get him on the fast track to the gas chamber. But the end all was the suicide dive. She could not believe the carelessness her Black Dog exuded in the moment she needed him the most. The first good news of the night came up. 'He's in critical condition? Where the hell are they sending him?' Another good break. 'Memorial? Good.' She did not need to orchestrate a prison break on top of all this. She closed up the phone and put it in her pocket, letting off a deep sigh. Her goal had not changed, but the means of achieve it had. She chuckled, calling for another fill up of bourbon. She was going to need a lot to get the juices flowing. *** I hate the rain. Used and abused. I wonder why things feel so off kilter. I begin to feel like a piece of veal. Pounded to a pulp. This is different. Like, something deep inside is ruined and crushed and destroyed and violated. At first, there is an overwhelming anger, but that is replaced by an overwhelming sadness that clings to the soul. Once he stops, I roll over, a feeling of death overcoming me. It's light as air, this feeling of death. When he's done, I can't cry. I can't move. I can't nothing. Nothing and nothing and nothing and my god... He stands there, the mask covering his face. Underneath, he must be smiling. He must be satisfied. But his gaze has changed. He doesn't want me anymore. Just my body. He looks down at me blankly, the rapture still evident in his eyes. He pulls out the knife and I try and scream but nothing comes out. It catches instead. I was screaming earlier but it catches now and I do not understand why. He doesn't say anything. He doesn't need to. Speaking would destroy the aura of power he has. And he can't have that. The fuck. The sick twisted fuck. HE RAPED ME! HE RAPED ME! HE RAPED ME! Suddenly, the sadness stops and the anger takes the rightful place over my shoulder. I kick him right in his balls, dropping him to his knees. I try to stand up, but the feeling just isn't there in my legs and he gets up and slams me into the window. I can feel it spider web behind me, the glass catching with my skin and raping it as well. He's stronger than me. I can feel his breathing. Lull him into a false sense of security Campbell. I stop tensing up. His grip lessens for a moment. One moment. All or nothing. I grab him by the mask and slam his head into the window, the window breaking. He falls back, grunting in pain. He wasn't caught. Damn. I break off a piece of glass from the window and charge screaming towards him. The feeling in my finger tips is unadulterated pain but the fury inside burns beyond it. He tries to get away. I slash him hard across the back, tearing through his black suit and dropping him to his knees. He roars in pain. In a flash, he get back to his feet and pushes me down, grunting as he lays on top of me trying to grab his knife. I tried. 'FREEZE!' Salvation. He rolls to his feet and looks at the cop for one second. Crocken...thank god. The moment lasts too long. He throws the knife and it slams into his skull. All he can do is roar in pain as the knife has a tickle fight with his brain. He knows he's toast. Done. Finished. I hear the footsteps following. Reinforcements. But in a crazy moment, he takes to jumping through the window. Almost like an acrobat, he grabs onto a rail as he is jumping and his momentum carries his lower body towards the pole. And just as help arrives, he slides down. I slump forward. I sob uncontrollably. I hate the rain. *** He was there in his expensive suit and tie, dressed to kill with a pair of expensive shades to go with the wardrobe. With a cigarette hanging loosely from his lips he took in the surroundings. The sky was gray, the heat almost intolerable. He took a look down into the hole it had taken most of the morning for the crew to dig up. Dead body. Not a pretty one. She was cut to shit and burned. He removed his sunglasses and stroked his goatee as he gazed down upon Jane Doe. 'That's the work of a master craftsman. Shit makes me sick.' With his left index finger, he scratched his nose and then brought the outside of his hand across his forehead to remove the sweat that was tickling his skull. Most people saw a dead body. He saw the missing piece to the puzzle. 'You think we can get anything off her that might tell us who she is?' A young and somewhat eager young woman quickly closed in, dressed in a pair of jeans and a white t-shirt. Her hair was done up in a bun and her glasses shone beautifully even with the smoky skies. 'We can do one better Mr. Silver. Appears whoever did this didn't have enough time to sand or cut off the prints.' Sonny smirked as he looked down at the dead body, kicking a little dirt into the grave as he did. 'And let me guess, we got a connection with Deville?' She could only smirk and nod her head. 'Missy, I'd kiss yah if you promised not to press charges.' She merely rolled her eyes at the comment as she left him to his work. He couldn't keep his eyes off the body in the hole. 'Well, looks like you were a rush job. Might have rated you a bit too high on initial appearance. But, you know what? I'm glad. Because you're my one way ticket to a seat on the state legislature.' He could merely smirk as he walked away from the hole. 'Finally...the rabbit hole gets bigger.'
