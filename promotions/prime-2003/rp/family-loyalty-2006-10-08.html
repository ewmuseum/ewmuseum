Family Loyalty

8 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/10/06 “You cannot choose your family and sometimes, by God you wish you could. There are times when you would give anything for them not to be who they claim they are. All the occasions that they weren’t there for you, the times where they were self-absorbed and self-obsessed or scared to face the truth. Where were they when you needed them? Drinking out of a bottle? Wallowing in self-pity? That’s okay though because they’re parents, they have a God given right to make mistakes, right? Wrong. Families, they’re overrated. Families? Fuck ‘em.” Paul Cain on Family Loyalty “We need to talk.” His hands tightly clasped around his soaking wet hair as he ran his fingers through it slowly. The rain poured down from the black clouds hovering above him in the night sky. In the distance, a storm approached with the thunder rumbling softly like a stomach that hadn’t seen food in days. Paul walked slowly around in a circle as he watched the rain pour from the heavens above him. His black Armani suit was saturated with running water cascading off him but he couldn’t even feel it for his mind was elsewhere. Slowly Cain bowed his head and took off the black sunglasses which were there for effect. Idly, he hurled them off the edge of the rooftop that he was standing upon, caring not for the material things in life. Gradually his arms came down and folded across his chest as he stared into the night. “You brought me back…I didn’t ask to come back.” Cain turns to the left and walks towards the edge of the rooftop with his head bowed slightly. Lifting his left leg, he sets his foot down and then steps up to stand at the edge, looking down at the traffic below. His balance is impeccable as he calmly looks over at the ants that go about their business below and the cars that ferry them passing by each other. While many men would feel their hearts racing, Cain was completely still and peaceful as his pulse continued at its own regular pace. He was at home here and even though one slip could end it all, he didn’t care because he knew it wasn’t his time to die. For he had been brought back from his slumber and now, he was just a little bit pissed off. “I’ve spent years of my life slaying demons in human guises. Can you imagine how tiring it becomes fighting for every little thing you earn and knowing that there are others who would happily snatch it from you? I’ve grown weary of sifting through the grass, finding the snakes and snapping their necks to kill them off.” He spreads his arms out wide in exclamation, which would make an onlooker fear for his safety yet he remained steady in his stance. “I had reached my retirement and found my nirvana! I’d found the American Dream of the quiet house with the white picket fence and the nuclear family. My sharp tongue had blunted and my fists unclenched because I didn’t need to fight anymore. However just when I thought everything was going to be okay…There you go again, chipping away with your flapping gums and the ears that don’t listen. You know what, if you had kept quiet, if you had stayed away, this wouldn’t have happened. Now here we are, you’re on your knees and I’m back up here to a place I didn’t want to go.” His voice died down and the rain became the only sound that could be heard. Cain stood soaking up all that the weather could throw at him, feeling refreshed by its coolness. His face was expressionless as he felt his heart begin to feel that burning sensation it had experienced once before. For a long time he had believed that the fire had died but now the flames were flickering back into life. Then the words that would prove to be a trigger to his trapped rage were shot out in his direction. “But I’m your mother! Why are you being so horrible to me?!” Cain leapt back and spun around swiftly, glaring at the woman that sat on her knees. Her boyish face was stained with crocodile tears mixed with the rain from above. His nostrils flared slightly and his eyes widened with fury as he wheeled around in retaliation. “You’re my mother? I never fucking asked you to be!” He bent down and looked at her with disgust, grabbing hold of her hair with one hand and yanking her head back. “I love you!” He felt the raging fire exploding inside him as his face glowed with the redness of fury. “Love? You don’t know the meaning of the word, the only person who truly knows the concept of love in our pathetic excuse for a family…Is dead and in heaven! I’m sick and tired of your martyrdom and your so-called dedication to your only son, who you forced into believing that he owed you for your years of service! You’ve swanned around for years gaining false praise from people who don’t know any better and don’t know the harsh realities of life, life with you!” The crocodile tears didn’t stop, they just kept coming. “Why are you doing this?!” she sobbed. Cain rose to his full height and shook his head furiously before bowing back down to her level and suddenly replying quietly to her. “Because this…has been a long time coming and because you are the most poisonous thing to have entered my life! You did your very best to break my spirit, my will and my confidence so I would always be your ‘good cause’…Me, your only son...You would rather have seen me beaten down so long as I was still yours, still dependant on you…And you ask me now, why am I doing this?! I could have asked you that a long fucking time ago…Why did you continually make me feel like crap, why did you fucking leave me alone in that house for what seemed like hours, defenceless and scared?!” She was like a rabbit trapped in headlights as she looked up at him. “But…” He held his hand up to silence her swiftly. “Don’t, don’t even begin to make excuses! You are sick, you’re twisted, you’re fucked in the head…Because no matter what goodness came into my life, you fucking resented it…I find someone to love and you tell me my jealousy will drive her away…Not only that but you tried to do all you could to break her spirit, knowing that all she had was me…If she hadn’t been so determined, loyal, strong and in love, you would have fucking taken her from me as well…Then even as our baby girl was coming home, you still tried to ruin that with your self-absorbed and self-obsessed bile…A real woman would have done the right thing and the obvious thing…Praised my wife for being so amazing at taking care of your son…your granddaughter…But you couldn’t do it could you? You couldn’t do it because you are so self-absorbed that you hated the fact someone could do it better than you…And how much fucking better they have done it you pathetic bitch!” He swirled around and stood with his back to her, breathing deeply and feeling the weight of years of frustration being released. Her pitiful cries were all he could hear beyond the torrential rain that cascaded down. Yet there was no sympathy and no remorse as he stared out at the night sky beyond this rooftop that served as their own personal courthouse. She whimpered like a wounded animal yet still he didn’t flinch. Instead, he smiled to himself with satisfaction that he had finally do what he had longed to. “Why…” He turned slowly around to her and looked down in disdain at her crumpled form. “Because I’m tired of parasites leeching off me and my family, that’s why.” Cain lifted her chin with his finger and sighed heavily. “All I wanted from you was to be like my grandmother was…Proud and supportive…You…You are nothing compared to her…And you’re nothing compared to my real family…” He pushed her down onto the floor with his hand but as he did so, he heard a booming voice call out to him. “You still up here?” It was the figure of Damien Kahn whose massive frame broke through the rain and the darkness of the night. His black skin was covered by a hooded grey jacket which he refused to pull back as he approached the two. Cain glanced down at his fallen mother and then back up to the one man keeping it all together. “What you got?” The man known as D-Money to his friend smiled beyond the hood. “Just call me the snake catcher. Leave this ‘thing’, I got shit you gotta see. It’s time to go back.” Suddenly Cain’s mother clutched at his leg and he snarled down at her as he saw both hands clinging to him. With a violent pull, he snapped his leg away then pushed her back down again. “Now you’ll know what it’s like to be alone, bitch.” With that, Cain wheeled around and stormed off towards the stairs down and off the rooftop with Damien Kahn leading the way. His mother was left to fend for herself, the rain pouring down on her as she reflected on the things she had done. However he could not care less as he slammed the door to the roof shut, locking it behind him. Conversation over. Scene fades out to black.
