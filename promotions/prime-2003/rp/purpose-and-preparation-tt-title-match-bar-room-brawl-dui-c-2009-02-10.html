Purpose and Preparation [TT Title Match: <BAR ROOM BRAWL> DUI(C) v Wicked Ways. PRIME Culture Shock 2009 Special Show)

10 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 10th, 2009 The night before a PRIME event, the ring is set up so wrestlers have the opportunity to work out in the same ring they appear in during national broadcasts. Some newcomers and young stars use this time to train and hone their game. However the ring is empty now. The lights are all out in the Tacoma Dome. After all it is the night before PRIME takes over. A metal crash sounds through the locker room. The clank of another metal latching sounds off, too. It echoes throughout the locker room and seems to come from the farthest corner. What seemed seconds ago to be an empty arena now is occupied by the hottest tag team in PRIME. The resident Swede sits on the floor in a towel. Rippa: Man. Am I glads that one’s overs. Claude hates me. Scraps: Why’s that? Rippa: He always tells me to gets lost and learns to wrestles and stuff. I know he’s good…but I wishes we coulds just take it easy sometimes. The blond stands and rounds the corner, looking down the row of lockers at his redheaded teammate. He is still wearing his work-out attire of basic wrestling tights, his boots, and some elbow pads that have slid down to his wrists during his idle time. His sweat-soaked hair is held back by a black bandana. An old, ratty black bandana. Rippa walks down the aisle and sits down. Rippa: Sleepin’? Scraps: Nope. Rippa: Tired? Scraps: Just doing a mental prep for tomorrow night. Maybe you should do the same. Rippa cocks his head and looks at his partner, whose eyes are still shut. He shakes his head out of humor. Lying back, he throws his hands behind his mane of hair for support. Rippa: Not sure I’s done that ones before. Scraps: Sit back. Think about two questions. Rippa: What questions? Scraps: Why are you here? December 5th, 2008 Tom O’Connor’s locker room door slammed shut. His fist slammed into the mirror, shattering into pieces and turning his counter top of cocaine and match books into a scary scene for any unsuspecting passer-by. His fist rattled against the closet door twice as did his head. Small tears came to his eyes. But they slowly turned into tears of rage. Tom: God damnit! Just stay the fuck out of here. Come in, you’ll die. All of you. Clear? CLEAR!!? The scene outside the room was that of serenity. One woman stood outside the doorway. She was dressed in a ragged sweater and a pair of jeans. He hair was mangled and knotted and her face was disheveled. She looked like she had been starving for weeks. She pounds. Woman: Tommy! It’s Shea. Silence. Shea: Thomas O’Connor… I haven’t seen you in months. You don’t return anyone’s phone calls. The road crew says you don’t even sleep or eat anymore. You’re losing your mind, Tommy. Please. You’re losing me. Tom’s thoughts drained onto his face. His eyes looked inside his soul before they opened up to the hell he had been creating for himself inside his locker room. Creating for her outside it. Shea: Tommy, please. I miss you. It’s so hard… It wasn’t her fault he was this way. It was the road’s. It had taken a hold of him and made him a mad-man. Unaffected by emotion. Impervious to pain. Unchained by love. Tommy O’Connor loves Shea O’Connor. The end of time could never change that. Shea: ...This isn’t you, Tommy. Drugs? Booze? Whatever it is, I love you. I’m here. I love you. Unfortunately for her sake, Tom was no longer the captain of his ship. Wiping his face and starting for his bag, Tommy O’Connor officially took second in command to his alter ego. The door knob turned as the woman’s eyes grew wide. The door opened. The man that came out was not Tommy O’Connor. Scraps: Look, it’s tough. I know. It takes time. Take the house. The car. I want you to have it. Truth is, I’m movin’ on up in the world. Yep, looks like I’ve been given a shot at the big leagues. They also said they don’t need me havin’ any ‘extra baggage.’ So, good luck to ya. Don’t write. Don’t call. (brief pause) I won’t. She begins to tear. He ignores it. Shea: Tommy- She throws him a ratty black bandana. Scraps shrugs it off and slings the bag over his shoulder, walking past Shea. Walking down the corridor, the shriek of hysterical crying fills the halls. And it’s coming from a woman he might have known in the past. Rippa’s eyes open up and stare at the ceiling. They stay open for quite some time. Sweden 1993 Veljumin Rippa stands in the small house that his parents own in Helsinki, Sweden. Over his left shoulder is a giant cargo surplus bag. A look of determination is draped over his face. Veljumin: I am going to America. I love you’s both. Good byes. The older, smaller and much more Swedish parents turn to their son in awe. He holds out his chin, ready for whatever comes from his decision. His father steps forward. Father: Okay. What wills you do? Veljumin: Train. Mother: For what? Veljumin turns to his father, directly. Veljumin: I want to be a wrestler, Papas. His father blurts out in disgust. Father: Absolutely nots! Veljumin’s face turns upside down and a bit redder. He bows his head but when defeat seemed near, something came to mind. Veljumin: I will be the best in the world. I will shows you. Rippa shakes off the cobwebs and looks up to his calm and collected partner. Rippa: What’s the other question? Scraps remains silent. The big blond rolls over onto his side and looks at the seemingly asleep miscreant. Slowly, Scraps’ eyes slide open like the rising sun. He lets off a wry grin to the foreign freight train. Scraps: How does that get us those tag belts tomorrow night?
