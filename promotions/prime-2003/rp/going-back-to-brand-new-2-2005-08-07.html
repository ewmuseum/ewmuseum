Going Back To Brand New #2

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7th August 2005 In a nutshell, returning to Russia was a complete failure. As Igor and Constantino collapsed into their hotel room in Salt Lake City, their minds were still in Russia and the reception they had received from everyone there. Lying back on the bed, Igor let go of the strap of his bag and held his face in his hands. Everyone knew. Everyone was aware that The Flying Vergomovs hadn’t retired early to enjoy the American lifestyle. They had retired from the circus to join wrestling. The generations of each family that had lived for the circus were turning in their graves, and the ones still above ground made their opinions pretty clear. The trip had started well. As they arrived and stood outside the gates of the great school, that they were once welcome to visit, they had been met by Tomas Arefiev. Tomas is, well was Igor’s uncle. As the tag team stood at the gates, he pulled up and brought them to the front door. He was warm with his welcome and even embraced the two men he had once trained as nine year old circus wannabes. It turned out he didn’t know anything of their secret wrestling careers. Those that knew kept it to themselves so as not to taint the Vergomov name, the Arefiev name or the Zergolov name. Igor and Constantino couldn’t believe. Tomas couldn’t believe, but when he found out he was ready to give the two American citizens a piece of his mind. He cursed in Russian. He cursed some more. He cursed in English and then he swung for both of them, Constantino first, who he blamed for leading young Igor astray. Lucky he had got used to dodging fists in the ring. Susanna Nicodemou was the first person that made their feelings clear. Upon setting eyes on the two boys she had mothered when they left home, she slapped both, leading to Tomas finding out. Her disappointed was summed up in the force with which she struck the two men. Touching their cheeks, both men still felt a slight tingle. Not one word did she speak to the two men, but they understood. Watching Igor get slapped, Constantino was well aware of what was headed his way but he felt she deserved to hit him. And then came the important people, their families. Coming from the two biggest circus families in Russia, there is only one place that these family members retire to, The Moscow School for Circus and Performing Arts. Igor’s father, older brother, two uncles, aunt and six cousins were all employed in the grand building where the pair had earned their circus education. Constantino’s brother, sister, three uncles and a cousin worked in the same institution. So despite everyone getting it hush-hush, it was safe to say that everyone knew. Each time the pair bumped into a member of their respective families, the same look of betrayal and distain crossed their faces. Most didn’t speak to them, and those that did, didn’t have the kindest of words to say about them or their career choices. “Are you good?” was one of the inquisitive questions from a student at school. By “good,” the kid meant successful. As Constantino prepared to give a gracious answer along the lines of “no,” the children that had surrounded the prodigal sons of the circus, were hurried on by the only person to congratulate the pair on their career choice. As she approached, both men expected the same reaction as the rest of the crowd, but instead she spoke tenderly. “How rich are you?” Sophie Nicodemou, the daughter of TFV’s surrogate mother, was also the teenage love of Igor until he left to travel the world. The pair hadn’t met in years, and his heart was racing as she sat down beside the pair on the steps of the grand school. She looked amazing, or so Igor thought. The smile she wore when she brushed against him was still etched in his memory. As the three talked, it was like they were all students once again. However, both men were sceptical of Sophie’s reaction. “I expected it…” she shrugged, finally divulging her reasoning for supporting their decision. As Sophie spoke, telling the pair of how she admired how far they had got in such short time, she knew they would get tired of being the best in circus and want something else. She even offered to put them up for the weekend as they weren’t welcome at the school as long as they didn’t tell anyone. It was their little secret. The weekend was a total disaster but for one person’s blessing. Back in Utah, Igor wiped his fingers down his face, over his eyes and past his mouth, turning his head to look at Constantino who was hunched forward on his bed. “At least they know…” he smiled, breaking the silence and trying to get a smile from Constantino. “We don’t have to worry about telling anyone,” he added, not really making the situation any better. The silence quickly returned as Igor moved his head from looking at Constantino to studying the cracks in the ceiling. “What about Wednesday night?” asked Igor. Again he was attempting to break the silence, hoping to get a response from his best friend. A groan was the only reply. Arefiev turned his head from the so far 34 cracks to Constantino. “What?” he asked once again, not really sure of what the groan meant. A groan was all he got this time. Constantino Zergolov did not want to talk. He wanted to think. Sitting up, Igor was now in the corner of Zergolov’s eye as he stared blankly at the wall. He was left with two options. Either he could move his head to get rid of Igor from his view or remain in the same place so he’ll think that he’s really mad and may get the idea, but will end up making me smile. Igor sat and stared at Constantino. He wasn’t a big fan of silence. He liked emotions out in the open to be examined, to be talked about and dissected. But if that wasn’t going to happen at least they could discuss the next PRIME show. Constantino sat and stared at the wall. The silence was a good thing in his mind. It allowed Constantino time to think. He could work how he felt about the whole abandonment by his family. But he realised that wasn’t going to happen… “So what’s the plan for Wednesday?” smiled Constantino, flicking his head around to look at Igor.
