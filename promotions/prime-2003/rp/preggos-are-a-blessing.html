Date: CWMC Round 1 It's just a regular day at the HQ for Primetime Central – an abandoned military compound in the center of Lake Superior that was constructed when tensions with Canada were on the rise during the first Gulf War (let's face it, we all know those downy-bastards have their eyes on Alaska). The pines stand tall, shading the blandly opaque compound from the summer sun that shines from above. Everyone made it to work on time (even Pete, that slacker) and they go about their day peacefully, completing their jobs to the fullest (even Pete, that slacker). The Uber Judge and his accomplice, the IG Overlord, have come one step closer to finding the perfect candidate to end all this Jason Snow hysteria <see member title>. All of this, and the Chris Williams Memorial Cup is just about to get underway. In the wake of what many thought to be a drastic change in the end of an era, things are as normal as they've ever been. Because once again, the Complaints Department have to deal with yet another bitching from our returned Illustrious Face-Eater. "Sir, what would you like us to do about it?" the receptionist asks. "What the FUCK do you MEAN what would I like you to do about it?! What I'd like is for me to not have to bitch at you peckerheads in the event that YOU FUCKERS DO YOUR FUCKING JOB." Adam Dick's ferocity force the lady to lean back in her seat, her hands creeping from the keyboard on her desk to the phone to ring Security. "Sir, the bounties placed on parties' heads during the GTT tournaments, aside from those endorsed directly by PTC, are simply nominations and have no fulfillment of obligation tied to them when they are volunteered." Adam scoffs. "Bullshit, lady. Look, we both know that "Shat On" Bobino put his bitch bounty on me because I whooped his ass in the Infinite Gauntlet and he was on his period and he's had this estrogen-induced obsession with my demise. And when Jack Murphy beat me in the second round, that typical old fart pussed out and didn't even pay! You fuckers should fine his ass for being insolent! Hell, you should ban him." Such a suggestion sends chills down the receptionist's spine. Strong words like 'the B-word' aren't used so hastily around the PTC offices, unless of course you're talking about Jeremy J. (or even Hyde, for that matter). "Sir, like I said, it was a nomination of a donation and we have no authority to exercise in the event of Bobino's apparent--" "FLAKING?!" "Ahem, yes, his 'flaking.' Anyways, I think you should take solace in knowing the man's character was exposed." "Are you fucking kidding me? IF I WOULD HAVE KNOWN PTC IS FULL OF ASSHOLES, I WOULD HAVE 'NOMINATED' TO DONATE A MILLION DOLLARS TO THE HOOKERS WITH AIDS FOUNDATION IN THE NAME OF THE FIRST PERSON THAT BEAT PETER VETRA!" The spit flying out of his mouth as he shouts is enough to tip the woman over the edge, and she finally resorts to picking up the receiver of the phone. "Sir, if you don't calm down, I'm going to have to ask you to leave." "THAT'S TOTALLY NOT FAIR. This is a travesty!" "Why do you care, one way or another? It's not like he's paying you the money." she asks. "BECAUSE HE'S A BITCH AND I SHIT ON HIM, ALRIGHT?" he slams his fist on the desk. "Don't make me call Security, sir." "FINE. But this isn't over. JUSTICE, one way or another, WILL BE SERVED!" The Face-Eater stomps his feet and spins on his heels before storming out, fuming from the mouth. He pushes past the glass doors and out of Suite F on the first floor of the compound, navigating the large maze towards the outside. In his enraged state, he finds it hard not to slam his fist into every window that he passes on the doors that line the hallway. However, all of his anger from dealing with the useless Complaints Department subsides at the sight of his most prominent fetish – a visibly pregnant woman. Adam stops in his tracks, staring, his mouth agape. He can barely mouth the word, "Preggo." He quickly snaps himself into the correct frame of mind, suddenly a hunter on the prowl analyzing the situation. She looks to be five, maybe six months; almost at the peak of her bulge. And immediately, Adam finds a conversation point as he notices the woman is crying. Visibly disturbed by something in her life, she carries a tissue in one hand that constantly tends to her worn, red eyes, sniffing incessantly to keep her face dry. 'Sweet,' he figures. 'Her baby's daddy probably just bailed. RIPE FOR THE PICKIN'S!' So Facey slicks his hair back, fixes his eyebrows, then casually strolls up next to her. With a light gesture to her shoulder, he politely asks, "Is everything alright, miss?" The woman, not even twenty-years-old, turns around and attempts to feign stability. She feasibly smiles and parts a brown lock from her sight that had been soaked in a mixture of tears, sweat, and snot. Her teeth are large, glossy, and the purest white he'd ever seen. Her nice figure in addition to her pregnant stomach; all of the details were adding to a very horny Face-Eater. "Oh, everything is fine. Don't you worry." She insists before trying to continue on her path to the outside. "Come on. Everything is not fine. Or else you wouldn't be upset. What's the matter, did Jason Snow beat you too?!" She lets out somewhat of a chuckle before turning her head down, once again trying to battle an overwhelming sadness. "I can't. Really, it's just--" "You don't have to be shy. Here, I'll even introduce myself so we aren't strangers anymore. My name is Adam Dick." Facey extends his hand and once again, the woman chuckles. "April Monday." she delivers her hand. "So, let it out, April. What's bothering you?" She looks at him with a smile on her face, but the width of her eyes begin to go glossy. Suddenly, the smile transforms into a look of despair as she breaks uncontrollably into tears. "It's just that I love him so much and now he's gone and I thought we were going to get married and--." "Whoa whoa whoa, wait a second. Who's gone?" Facey asks. "My boyfriend, Brock Aardman. He wrestles under the name Brock Shepherd." For a moment, she regains control of the flood-works... but only for a moment. "And things were going so great because he was just hired at GCW and I just found out the baby's sex and he even joined this tournament--." "WAIT A SECOND.... THERE'S A TOURNAMENT?!" "Uh-huh." she nods. "The Memorial Cup. It's actually why I'm here; the first round is this weekend and Brock is nowhere to be found. I... I had to tell the officials that um..." This next part is difficult to say. "... that Brock won't be able to compete." Again, she seems inconsolable with the dramatics behind her tears. She pants heavily while shrieking loudly, causing those who pass by to look on with confusion. "....do you think that Bobino character might have joined this tournament?" "What? Who?" "NOTHING. Listen, Lady, that is so heart-breaking. I totally feel for you." "It's just... it's so hard. Losing someone you love. Not knowing anything about where they are or if they'll ever come back. It's really killing me, Adam. I don't know if I can take this pain. Everything about this is a total mystery, and I can't help but wonder if he's dead. If that's the case, I won't be able to go on. We were supposed to be happy. We were supposed to be a family. This child is ours and now he'll never grow up with a father. He'll never have that warmth and compassion that can only come from a dad; it'll be lost before it was even known. And that's what hurts me the most. I loved him, but that's selfish. He's a father now. And with him being gone, I just--." "Lady? April?" "Oh, I'm sorry for ranting. It's just... I haven't talked to anyone since he's been gone, and you were so kind to speak to me--." "April, I'm going to tell you something. I would never do this under these circumstances, but I'm a professional wrestler as well. And your story has been so-" Adam puts a hand on his heart, as if he's getting choked up. "-so heart-wrenching that I can't sit idly and do nothing about this." Her eyes perk up. "I'm going to find your boyfriend, Brock Samson!" "Shepherd." "Right, Brock Shepherd! First, I'm going to join this tournament in his name. Because I'm going to do it for him, for you, and your unborn child! I'm going to find out who or what is behind his disappearance, even if I have to travel to Vermont to find him. And God knows I hate that fucking state. And then! I'm GOING TO KICK BOBINO'S BITCH ASS." "Who are you talking about?" "Not important. What IS important, is that I have work to do. Here's my card." Facey withdraws a note-card with his cell-phone number written with a purple Sharpie. "Call me if you need anything. And I mean anything. And hopefully, the next time you see me, it'll be with the Father of your Child." Facey kisses her on the cheek and begins to storm off, back into the heart of the compound, when April calls out to him. "Thank you so much!" He spins around and takes a bow. "It's nothing, madame." Finally, the woman has a tiny portion of hope to cling to. If only she knew it won't amount to shit. As soon as Facey turns the corner, out of her sight, he withdraws his cell-phone and dials a number. "BITTY, yo wassup.....No, I don't have that hooker's prosthetic.....No, I haven't looked under the coffee table......YES, fuck, I'll look when I get home, Christ man......will you shut up about the fucking Leg? I've got some shit to tell you, man!.....You'll never guess what just happened......No, I didn't find the fucking whore's leg-LISTEN. I just ran across this six-monther, and she was all whining about losing her baby's daddy, and--......YOU DAMN RIGHT I LOVE THEM PREGGOS, anyways, so she was bitching, right? And she kept whining about how loss is devastating and all that crap, and it reminded me: I HAVEN'T GOTTEN STONED ALL FUCKING DAY! Yeah, I woke up and was so pissed off that I didn't have any weed, that I had to find some reason to bitch, and so I came to this weird place where fat people like to sit at their computers all day and I was just like 'You guys fucking suck!' and that's when I met the Preggo Bitch but her bitching reminded me that I need to score but she was rattling on and on about her lame boyfriend so I told her I'd help her find him because it was the only way that I knew I could get away from her without having to hear her bitch some more so I just gave her my number and hopefully she'll call because I haven't gotten laid since I that hooker with the prosthetic I stole.....no I don't want you to help me find her baby's daddy, you dolt, I WANT YOU TO HELP ME FIND WEED....." By this time, Facey has wondered all the way back to the Complaints Department, where the receptionist once again has her hand placed on the phone's receiver in the case of Face-Eater's getting out of hand. He merely holds up a finger, gesturing 'one minute' as he paces back and forth on his cell-phone. "DRY? WHAT THE FUCK DO YOU MEAN, DRY?!?........WELL I DON'T HAVE ANY FUCKING WEED TO SMOKE, BITTY. DO YOU KNOW WHAT THAT'S LIKE?! DO YOU KNOW WHAT IT'S LIKE TO HAVE THAT URGE TO JUST GET FUCKING WASTED OFF A FATTY DANK BLUNT, AND NOT BEING ABLE TO HAVE IT!? IT'S A COLD, BITTER EMPTINESS, BITTY, AND I PROMISED MYSELF ON THE DAY I ACCIDENTALLY PEE'D MY PANTS AND RUINED MY WEED SACK THAT I'D NEVER BE OUT OF WEED AGAIN......DON'T YOU WORRY ABOUT WHY I PEE'D MY PANTS, THAT'S FOR ME AND MY THERAPIST TO FIND OUT.....ALLS I WANTED WAS TO GET STONED, BITTY, BUT I CAN'T. NEVER WILL I BE ABLE TO HAVE THAT PRECIOUS WEED SMOKE GRACE MY LUNGS, AND IT'LL SEND ME ON A DOWNWARD SPIRAL. WITHOUT MY PRECIOUS MARY JANE, I'LL BE FORCED TO TAKE A DRUG-ADDLED ROAD TO HELL; SNORTING OXYCONTIN MIXED WITH ECSTACY OFF OF THE BACK OF A HIPPO WHILE TERRORIZING THE PLAINS OF GARY, INDIANA, SHOOTING HOOKERS WITH MY CROSSBOW.....OH YEAH, I'LL FUCKING SHOOT YOUR HOOKERS, TOO! DON'T TEST ME, BITTY." The receptionist is speechless. Then again, she's only hearing one side of the conversation. "Alright, fine, call me when you get the word." And as if his entire speech before was for nothing, Adam snaps the phone shut and slides it into his pocket. He motions to the phone, while looking at the receptionist. "Fucking drug-dealers, they never have a steady supply. Sometimes you've gotta put them in their place to let 'em know you can't be fucked with, right? Anyways, I'm here to sign up for that vacant spot in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup. Y'know, the one Brock Shepherd left open. I'd like to do what you're incapable of and TEACH THAT BITCH BOBINO A LESSON." "Okay, sir," the receptionist begins. "But this isn't the Tournament Director's Office. This is the Complaints Department." "Oh, well, I have a complaint. WHY THE FUCK CAN'T I SIGN UP HERE? Is it your fucking JOB to be difficult?" And THAT, ladies and gentlemen, is when she called Security. The End.
