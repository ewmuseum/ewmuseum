Date: Under the Gun Culture Shock THE NEW HOTNESS By Danny Ferguson FADE IN: EXT. HOLLYWOOD - DAY I think you've watched enough television to know what Hollywood in the afternoon looks like. Bright sky, famous people, everyone wearing sunglasses, blah blah blah. Today is no different, and as a rule of thumb, assume that no day is different for DANNY FERGUSON. For a man with his kind of celebrity status, it couldn't be more damn typical. On the other hand, there's a reason we didn't catch him yesterday, or the day before, or last week. We start the story here because shit starts going down here. There's something latently philosophical about that, but it's crunch time and that means everyone else is starting their stuff with some garishly overblown life lesson. Pardon me the style points if I spare you one more lecture. The shot swings through the streets of Hollywood, accompanied by what we will blandly refer to as 'hustle and bustle.' A quick swing upward takes us to an office building. You may recognize it from previous Ferguson roleplays such as the 'Precio de Opportunidad' series. It's the home office for the Superstar's power agent - and twin brother of aging porn star Peter - ALLAN NORTH. ALLAN NORTH (O.S.) It's like a goddamned fire sale out there, Danny. CUT TO: INT. PETER'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS The office is as sterile as you don't remember it. Movie posters and signed photos line the walls...as do JIM PIBB and DAMETREYUS FUQUEIAWYTAS, Los Bodyguards. They glare, unflinching, at the scene that plays out in front of us. Allan stands up behind his desk, channelling Ari Greene like we've never seen before, kicked into real action for the first time since we've met him. Danny, on the other hand, is less emphatic, and has his feet kicked up on the chair next to him. ALLAN NORTH There isn't one damn script out there. I'm calling writers directly - I'm getting fucking writers' SECRETARIES. And then I'm asking the writers' secretaries if THEY have something THEY want to option, because everything that breathes in this city has a script, and yet we can't find one! Danny lets his head hang back a little as Allan turns to look out the windows of his corner office. DANNY FERGUSON Al, I don't pay you to tell me the bad news. ALLAN NORTH When bad news is the only news, that's what you're going to hear. DANNY FERGUSON But my point is that you didn't call me here to complain, because you KNOW I've got better things to do and you KNOW that I wouldn't be pleased if the gist of your presentation is 'I got nothing.' ALLAN NORTH (gesturing out the window) There's nothing out there. DANNY FERGUSON Bullshit. There's never 'nothing.' This town hasn't been black-and white since the 40s. And it hasn't seen a 'no' since the 20s. There's something. So why don't we have it? Pause. Allan turns back from the window and looks at his client. Not breaking the glance...which is dangerously close to becoming a glare...he pulls his chair out and sits down behind the desk. ALLAN NORTH It's the schedules. DANNY FERGUSON The what? ALLAN NORTH The schedules. Shooting schedules and your schedule. DANNY FERGUSON What do the- ALLAN NORTH There's no producer or director in a 30 mile radius willing to work around this PRIME thing. It's one thing to stagger filming - it's another to miss half a week so you can fly to Hoboken or Worchester or some other miserable American town. DANNY FERGUSON Tell them it's not an issue. ALLAN NORTH It IS an issue. DANNY FERGUSON THEN I'LL FIX IT. He swings his feet down and sits forward. Of course, he doesn't take his sunglasses off, so any eye-to-eye contact is kept at a minimum. DANNY FERGUSON Christ, P, I'm an actor-slash wrestler, not a wrestler-slash actor. If we can get a gig, then I come off the road. Do Pay-Per Views and TV and that's it. Maybe even pre-tape my stuff and mail it in, so to speak. The look Allan gives him is skeptical. Skeptical is a euphemism for 'having no confidence in him whatsoever.' DANNY FERGUSON I can make it work, Allan. ALLAN NORTH Fine. I'll toss our lines out again. But like I said, casting season is over on the holiday flicks. Whatever we end up with...it might not be the catch of the day. DANNY FERGUSON Don't make a fool out of me, man. Use your discretion. Allan stares at him, unflinching. DANNY FERGUSON Fine, bring it all back. We'll sift through it once it's on the table. He stands up, and his bodyguards push away from the wall. Allan stands as well, fidgeting with his suit jacket as he does. ALLAN NORTH That's what I like to hear. We'll get you something, D. I can feel it in the air. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah, well, I'd rather you feel it in your hands, as in a script with my name on it. (Beat. He ruffles his hair.) Look, Vicki's doing some travel planning for the Japan trip. If you hear something, I need you to call me. ALLAN NORTH Will do. You're meeting with Phyllis today? DANNY FERGUSON About 15 minutes ago. ALLAN NORTH So you've still got about 45 before she shows. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah. ALLAN NORTH I'll give you a call. FADE TO: INT. H2 - AFTERNOON Danny's riding alone in the back seat of the Team Ferguson forest green H2. The bodyguards are sitting up front...and subsequently turning the thing into a drag racer by making it lean forward like that. We keep the camera on Danny, who is on the phone. And of course, despite doing very little with his hands at the moment, he's using a headset. DANNY FERGUSON ...and remember, Vicki, we need to make sure the pilot safetguards all the sharp edges in the cabin. The last thing we need is Chet Worth having a nervous breakdown because he thinks the windows and doors aren't sealed properly. (beat) Yeah. EXTRA soap. Oh, hang on. He holds up the phone that had been resting in his lap and punches a key. DANNY FERGUSON D-Ferg. (beat) What up, Facey? (beat) I can assure you that our hotel in Japan will be 100% rat free. I mean, what kind of- (beat) No, I'll bet you would, buddy. (beat) I believe you. Hey listen, can you hang on? He pushes the button again. DANNY FERGUSON Hey Vick? I've got A. Dick on the other line, a- (beat) No, not Chandler, the other one. Yeah. Anyway, that reminds me, does the pilot know about his...extracurriculars? (beat) Yeah, do me a favor and check on that. I'll stay on. At that moment, a polyphonic version of Europe's 'Final Countdown' blasts from out of nowhere. Flustered, Danny digs in his pockets before producing another cell phone. He holds it up to the ear that doesn't have the headset. DANNY FERGUSON Talk to me. He scowls and holds the second phone away from his face momentarily. DANNY FERGUSON Goddammit, Vicki, I'm on the other phone! Didn't I give you something to do? He fumbles with the first phone and pushes a button. DANNY FERGUSON Face? (beat) No, that's what I'm finding out for you right now. (beat) I don't think you need to hide it on your person - that's why we got a private plane, so we could accommodate your habit. He makes a nasty face, presumably from the response he gets from his counterpart. DANNY FERGUSON Well if you've already got 'em shoved up there, man, I don't know what to tell you. Just don't eat a lot of fiber before wheels-up. (beat) You know what? I probably don't need to be a part of this dialogue anymore, so I'm hanging up. He pushes a button on the first phone, still keeping the second at arm's length. DANNY FERGUSON Any word? Well get on it! He yanks the headset jack out of the first phone and puts it in the second. DANNY FERGUSON Sorry about that, Reg. What's the story on Lamen? As he listens in, the life of the Hollywood superstar slows down for a brief moment. We see something in his expression that he has never shown before in the short history of time we've spent with him. It's called 'concern,' and it's all but alien to the Fergmeister. DANNY FERGUSON Okay. Keep on it. I'll get with you after we touch down in Risingsunland. We'll have a good 24 hours for match prep. Later. He punches a button and pockets that phone, removing the headset jack. Before he plugs it into the first phone again, he looks up and notices something. DANNY FERGUSON Why the hell aren't we moving? JIM PIBB We're here. DAMETREYUS FUQUEIAWYTAS We've been here for the last five minutes. Danny angrily kicks the passenger door open, not waiting for one of them to get out and open it. He slides out and slams the door, and from the rustling, we can hear the bodyguards move out, too. Of course, Danny forgot one minor detail. VICKI (on the phone, faint) Hello? Danny? CUT TO: EXT. CAFE - AFTERNOON Another sweeping landscape shot pans across a nameless sunspotted and celebrity-dotted Los Angeles intersection. Dametreyus and Jim are seated at a 'delicate' table that is entirely too small for men of their stature. A table away, Danny is sitting quietly in front of a woman who could not be more of a product of Hollywood if she had a barcode stamped on her ass. Blonde, wrinkled and Jewish, she's hovering around 50 but looks more like 60 because she's trying so hard to look 30. Her name is PHYLLIS STEIN and she's the most powerful and influential publicist in Hollywood. Her mere presence commands equal parts fear and respect. Please keep that in mind as we watch her sloppily devour the pita in front of her. Danny, strangely enough, isn't interested in his own food as he watches this display. PHYLLIS STEIN (wiping her mouth) You know, Daniel, meetings like this are the reason I charge you an extra $1000 a month. DANNY FERGUSON I know, Phyllis. PHYLLIS STEIN I'm speaking, of course, about the topic of this lunch, not the lunch itself. DANNY FERGUSON I know, Phyllis. PHYLLIS STEIN Not enough people realize that: they think, you're famous, you wouldn't appreciate a free sandwich now and again. I don't get it. DANNY FERGUSON That's why I'm always here for you, Phyllis. PHYLLIS STEIN And that's why I haven't dropped you on your fucking ass already. DANNY FERGUSON I know, Phyllis. PHYLLIS STEIN Do you know the kind of work I have to do on your account daily? Does someone read those memos to you? DANNY FERGUSON I read them. PHYLLIS STEIN So you know what torture it was after you beat up that poor girl? DANNY FERGUSON She won. And I never hit her. PHYLLIS STEIN You did more on camera than Ike Turner ever did, and look what happened to him. DANNY FERGUSON I don't think... PHYLLIS STEIN I lose more years off my life from making Danny Ferguson look pristine than I do from smoking. DANNY FERGUSON I thought you quit. PHYLLIS STEIN I had to! Otherwise I'd have been dead two months ago! There's a break in the conversation. Danny contemplates his food, but opts against it. He'll eventually have it wrapped up for one of the guards to ravage later. PHYLLIS STEIN You got a movie yet or what? DANNY FERGUSON Allan's going to call me. PHYLLIS STEIN (immediately) You need to take it. DANNY FERGUSON Excuse me? PHYLLIS STEIN Whatever it is, Daniel, you need to take it. We can't go much longer without a followup project. DANNY FERGUSON Am I that desperate? The revenue for Hard Money is still rolling in. It just premiered in Eastern Europe. PHYLLIS STEIN I know, I know. The thing's a cash cow, and you'll probably be set for life. But right now you're not the 'Hard Money' guy, especially since the franchise has crashed and burned. You're the 'wrestling' guy. DANNY FERGUSON I thought this is the part where you come in. PHYLLIS STEIN Honey, I can do a lot of spinning. You give me six hours and I can have the top story on Entertainment Tonight be how Alec Baldwin's gay, and he wouldn't even know about it. But I can't fight facts. Danny doesn't respond while she takes a drink. PHYLLIS STEIN Allan's a good agent, and he can probably get you cameo's and TV guest appearances to keep you in the public eye, but that doesn't fix the fact that you'll be finishing 2006 without a leading role in theaters. DANNY FERGUSON Is it worth it, though? Taking some shit job just to be on a billboard? PHYLLIS STEIN A movie review rarely starts out with 'We hadn't heard much from So and-So since two or three years ago...' Do you know why? Out of sight, out of mind. If you disappear for a SECOND in this industry, you need to be channelling Olivier to get the spotlight back in your area. DANNY FERGUSON So I'd rather take a movie I don't want and get shat on than avoid the negative pub? PHYLLIS STEIN Sometimes you gotta shoot yourself in the foot to get attention. Besides, you did that big cancer thing a week back - that ought to make some people hesitate before they unload both barrels into you. DANNY FERGUSON Great. 'Final Countdown' bellows again. Danny checks the number on the phone and looks up at Phyllis. DANNY FERGUSON It's him. PHYLLIS STEIN Go on. Make sure he calls me to set up the press release. And DON'T get yourself killed in Japan, musclehead. Danny pushes away from the table and we CLOSE IN on his face as he holds the phone up. DANNY FERGUSON What do we got? CUT TO: EXT. HOLLYWOOD - LATER Still close on Danny's face, but from a different angle as the shot transitions over. DANNY FERGUSON No way. No fucking way. He's standing at another street corner, flanked by Dametreyus. Jim and the H2 are idling a few yards away. Next to him, Allan is pacing uncomfortably. ALLAN NORTH It's on the table. And it's an attractive offer. DANNY FERGUSON You call that attractive? ALLAN NORTH It allows for a lot of TV spot ads and promo stuff. And it shows everyone a different side of you. DANNY FERGUSON Did we consider that the side they've already seen is my best side? Allan paces some more. There's really no argument here, but apparently it's not going to be easy. The silence that ensues is uncomfortable, kind of like the way I felt when I had to sit through Napoleon Dynamite. Sorry kids, but movies need a little thing called PLOT. Don't tell me I wasn't laughing because I didn't get it. I wasn't laughing because IT WASN'T FUNNY. ALLAN NORTH It lets you act with kids. DANNY FERGUSON I hate kids. ALLAN NORTH People don't need to know that. DANNY FERGUSON Let me get this straight - you want me to play a former Navy SEAL who goes undercover as a girls' gymnastics coach so he can take down the International Olympic Committee? ALLAN NORTH It's a really progressive piece - total propaganda from a jilted parent, but they rewrote it for slapstick because the premise didn't seem believable. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah, jokes ought to help that. ALLAN NORTH Horatio Sanz is attached. DANNY FERGUSON As what? ALLAN NORTH I don't know. As Horatio Sanz. DANNY FERGUSON Jesus Christ. ALLAN NORTH They're shopping it around, Danny. I need to tell them something or it'll be pulled out from under us. DANNY FERGUSON Gee, that'd be a shame. He looks at Allan, who is obviously in no mood to have his work trashed further. DANNY FERGUSON Tell them I'm... (sigh) ...I'm interested. ALLAN NORTH I'll stall a little bit, tell them we need to finish the script. But I need an answer before you get on the plane tomorrow. DANNY FERGUSON Fine. I'm gonna...I have to talk to some people. ALLAN NORTH Ok. Allan starts walking away. Danny kicks at the ground and follows Dametreyus back to the H2. A few paces away, Allan turns. ALLAN NORTH Danny? Ferg turns. ALLAN NORTH It's not as bad as it looks. DANNY FERGUSON I've been working long enough to know that, Allan. Satisfied, the agent returns to his stride, producing a Blackberry as the camera focuses on Danny. DANNY FERGUSON (to himself) It's always much worse. FADE TO: EXT. HOLLYWOOD - NIGHT Later that same day, the crowds have come out for the big premiere. Hollywood's brightest are out on the red carpet, not so much seeing as being seen. Some of them are perfecting that glazed-over, I'm-not-really-looking-at anything-right-now expression. Fighting our way through the fake people, we settle on the fake person in question. Danny is quietly trying to push his way towards someone who we can't make out right away. All we see are the sunglasses and the bald head. Finally sick of pushing, he stops and cups his hands around his mouth. DANNY FERGUSON Hey, you fucking pussy! Everyone in the immediate area falls silent. The bald head cocks back a little bit, raising its chin as it slowly turns. Soon we, along with Danny, are face-to-face with VIN DIESEL. He removes his sunglasses slowly. VIN DIESEL I didn't think your bitch ass would show up. By now we've all been through the Hollywood cliche of pretending you're pissed at someone and then SWERVING everyone and shaking hands. So we'll fast forward through the part where these two trite motherfuckers pull that same old shit. I've sworn a lot in the last four paragraphs. Sorry. DANNY FERGUSON How's life? VIN DIESEL Gettin' on, man. You like the flick? DANNY FERGUSON I don't even know what movie this is. VIN DIESEL Real classy, D. DANNY FERGUSON I got priorities, man. VIN DIESEL Is one of them to find a new movie? Or are you going to dropkick me for asking that? DANNY FERGUSON You're awfully funny for a bitch. That's why I'm here. Have you heard about this gymnastics script? VIN DIESEL (chuckling) 'Uneven Bars?' The IOC one? Did they get you to play Steven Bars? DANNY FERGUSON I haven't said yes yet. VIN DIESEL Dude, don't say that. It implies that you're planning on saying yes. DANNY FERGUSON What kind of choice to I have? VIN DIESEL I dunno, live under a rock? Seriously, is anything out there worse than a joke like that? DANNY FERGUSON Tommy Lee Jones pulled off something like it. VIN DIESEL Do me a favor. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah? VIN DIESEL Don't compare yourself to Tommy in my presence ever again. There's a pause as photographers find them. They snap a few shots of the two stars, who pose. As they're done, we hear the following exchange. PHOTOGRAPHER #1 Who the hell's the redhead? PHOTOGRAPHER #2 Danny Ferguson. You know, the wrestler? Danny rolls his eyes and looks back at Vin. DANNY FERGUSON See? VIN DIESEL Look, dude, do what you gotta do. But remember what happened to me. I could challenged you for 'Hard Money,' but I said no because my agent said 'The Pacifier' was a can't miss. DANNY FERGUSON I know. VIN DIESEL You do something like that...it's hard to come back from. I got this mafia project coming up that ought to restore some of the Vin mystique, but goddamn if I don't hear Pacifier jokes every week. Entertainment Weekly listed me as ''The Pacifier' star Vin Diesel.' It's like, are you kidding me? Have they heard of Pitch Black? xXx? He throws up his hands, exasperated. Danny thinks about it for a little bit, then pats him on the shoulder. DANNY FERGUSON Thanks for the advice, man. I gotta pack for the trip. VIN DIESEL Don't throw your career away, bro. That's all I'm saying. And hey...kick that emo bitch in the face for me. They exchange pleasantries and dap as we pan up to the night sky. FADE TO: EXT. AIRPORT - THE NEXT DAY The sound of plane engines is typical but necessary for a scene like this. As we pan back down from the sky, we find ourselves at a private airstrip, where A-List Air, as it has been dubbed, is loading up. Chet Worth is doing some last minute folding and re-folding of his clothes...on the tarmac. Chandler is on the Sidekick. And Facey is walking like he has to go to the bathroom REALLY bad. Danny approaches the plane with his suitcase in tow. He's on the phone. DANNY FERGUSON I just don't know. SPLIT SCREEN OF: Allan North elsewhere in the streets of Hollywood, walking and talking on the Blackberry. ALLAN NORTH I don't have any more time for indecision. We need to pull the trigger, because this deal won't be on the table by the time you touch down. DANNY FERGUSON Is it in our best interest? ALLAN NORTH It's our only interest. We NEED this. Danny stops walking and looks out across the airstrip, clearly pained by having to make his own decision. DANNY FERGUSON Fine. Let's go for it. ALLAN NORTH Awesome. DANNY FERGUSON Well yeah, but I didn't think I would just leave cold, I need- ALLAN NORTH Danny, this movie doesn't require any extensive stunt work on your part - you don't need to waste your time wrestling. DANNY FERGUSON Waste? It's just that the guys need- ALLAN NORTH Well, which are you? DANNY FERGUSON Huh? ALLAN NORTH Are you an actor-slash-wrestler, or a wrestler-slash-actor? Silence from Danny. He doesn't even flinch. ALLAN NORTH Call me when you get a spare moment. I'll fill you in on contract details. DANNY FERGUSON Yeah. END SPLIT SCREEN. Danny hangs up. He breaks into a stare again, but is quickly interrupted by Facey. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Can we get this bird into international airspace already? Some of us have some baggage to eject! Reluctantly, Danny boards the plane. CUT TO: INT. STUDIO OFFICE - LATER Allan North arrives and shakes hands with the execs. He takes a seat opposite the desk where the two nameless suits stand. SUIT #1 We're really happy to have Danny on board for this. I think star power is what this film needs to really cross over. ALLAN NORTH (lying through his teeth) I was...I was thinking the same thing. SUIT #2 We've got the paperwork coming in. It's a very lucrative offer, we think. ALLAN NORTH Icing on the cake. He spies a script, face-down on the table and leans forward to look at it. As he grabs it, both suits become nervous ALLAN NORTH Is this an updated version with the new rewrites? Because I- SUIT #1 I don't think you sho- SUIT #2 We're not- Allan gets a look at the title of the script and his eyes open wide. ALLAN NORTH What's this? SUIT #2 It's another project in development. ALLAN NORTH Why wasn't I told about this? SUIT #1 We...we already locked in a star. SUIT #2 Sean Astin was very interested. ALLAN NORTH Astin? You signed Sean Astin without even CONSIDERING Danny? SUIT #2 Well, he- ALLAN NORTH Did you not think he was more suited? SUIT #1 We didn't want to typecast. ALLAN NORTH Type-...I can't believe this. One of them tries to grab the script, but Allan jumps out of his chair and flips through it, playing one-man keepaway. We still can't see the title of the script, though SUIT #2 We didn't- ALLAN NORTH I can't believe you guys didn't tell me about this. SUIT #1 (suddenly assertive) We aren't interested in paying Danny the kind of money we're paying Sean. The door to the office clicks shut and all three men look at the intern who has brought in the paperwork for the movie. Allan grabs that as well. ALLAN NORTH Great, we can use this as scrap paper. The suits both look at him as he pulls up a chair at the desk. ALLAN NORTH Gentlemen, you'd better get comfortable. (beat) We've got some negotiating to do. INT. The door to the plane bathroom is locked, but the cloud of some seeping from the seams is a dead giveaway to Facey's activities. Chandler's still on the Sidekick, and Chet is gripping the seat in front of him with a white knuckled discomfort. CHET WORTH Stop tapping. (beat) Stop tapping (beat) Stop tapping. (beat) STOP TAPPING OR I'LL RIP YOUR FINGERS OFF! Danny stops tapping. DANNY FERGUSON Calm down, big guy. No need to have a bug up your ass. CHANDLER TSONDA I could've said the same thing to you. DANNY FERGUSON What was that? CHANDLER TSONDA (not looking up) You've been a mopey bitch ever since we took off, especially when Facey was still sober and trying to talk about the team after Culture Shock. What's your deal? He doesn't answer right away, but all eyes are on him, so he relents. DANNY FERGUSON I just signed a new movie. CHANDLER TSONDA Oh yeah? You finally gonna follow through on giving me my breakout role? DANNY FERGUSON (under his breath) Not unless you want to be embarassed. CHANDLER TSONDA What did you say? DANNY FERGUSON Nothing, I... 'Final Countdown' saves him from the hemming and hawing. Danny's never picked up a phone faster in his life. This time, though, we don't get the split screen, because I'm trying to preserve dramatic effect. DANNY FERGUSON Talk to me, Allan. Yeah, it's a great time. CHET WORTH Turn your phone off. DANNY FERGUSON (into phone) Hold on. (to Chet) Your hands look awfully dirty from that seat, bud. Chet retreats to his bag to find some wet naps. Danny keeps listening in the phone. DANNY FERGUSON What do you mean you called an audible? You said we had to do this! You told me... Whatever happens next changes his mind, because his eyes open as wide as Allan's in the previous scene. He doesn't say another word for the rest of the conversation, and eventually shuts the phone, looking awfully shellshocked. They sit there in silence for a second, with Danny suddenly sporting a new mood and a bright smile. CHANDLER TSONDA Well? DANNY FERGUSON Huh? CHANDLER TSONDA Your new movie. What is it? Danny stares at his phone before looking up at him. DANNY FERGUSON It's the Terry Funk biopic. 'More Than Hardcore.' I'm going to play Terry. CHANDLER TSONDA Daaaamn, dude, that's bigtime. Danny can only manage a nod. He lets his gaze drift out the window. Japan is just barely breaking through the clouds as the plane prepares to make its descent. I guess I lied earlier. Not every day is typical to Danny Ferguson. Some of them are just out-fucking-standing.
