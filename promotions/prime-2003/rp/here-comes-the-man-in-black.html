Here Comes the Man in Black

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Galaxy Defender Sitting calmly at his kitchen table is the Universal Champion, Devin Shakur. In an antique wooden chair, he fiddles with a chisel, manipulating what appears to be a small piece of ice in his other hand. A large red blanket covers the awkwardly shaped table with a small hole cut in the fabric that Shakur’s hands work with the small piece of ice upon. His face doesn’t turn to acknowledge the camera before words start flying. Shakur: Ever since I walked into PRIME, people have said that I’m overrated. Win a belt. Lose it after the first title defense. Not a worthy champion. Carry three people to the biggest Pay-Per-View of the year. Win the match. Add another Championship. The competition was lackluster all along. Go through the unenviable process of enduring months of being second in line, roadblocks aplenty, until FINALLY clutching ‘da Universal strap’ in hand, something that only 11 other hands have grasped before. NOPE! Still the comedic relief! Caught a declining Hall of Famer and rode the wave to two preoccupied individuals and managed to catch onto the lucky rainbow for a victory. Now, Shakur equals ‘Paper Champion’. People could whoop my ass blindfolded. Record is barely above 500. Not the ideal fit to be representing such a talented roster. A violent crash of metal against ice, little shards of frozen water are sent through the air. Shakur: Now my name is being mentioned by my opponent like I’m a flash in the pan. His head shoots up, eyes like two blazing infernos. Shakur: The CURRENT UNIVERSAL CHAMPION IS BEING MENTIONED IN FUCKING PASSING LIKE HE’S NOTHING! Gripping the chisel even tighter, Shakur hammers away at the frozen square with more tenacity. Shakur: I’M JUST PART OF THE PATH FOR HER! Teeth grind. Veins bulge. Shakur: NOW, I’M ONLY A SMALL MINUTE DETAIL IN THE GRAND SCHEME OF IT ALL! Breathing intensifies. He continues to pound away at the ice with increasing vigor. Shakur: I’M JUST KEEPING THIS BELT WARM BECAUSE I DON’T BELONG IN THE ELITE! His nostrils flare. His face is flushed red with rage. Shakur: FUCK Chisel slams into block with a vengeance, the equivalent of a Rampage Jackson right hand. Shakur: YOU! With another ferocious strike, Shakur dents the ice with his weapon and presses forward. Shakur: Those off the cuff remarks you make just to get a chuckle out of the otherwise miserable day. Have any of you thought about what it does to the people you say them too? No, of course not, because their thoughts and feelings are beyond your realm of comprehension or apathy, you can’t empathize with them because that hypocrisies the act of superiority you are playing up. Let me give you a little taste of what the other side is like. Tugging at his hair, the Champ continues. Shakur: For all the bullshit that I’ve received ever since winning this Championship…What is said about me soaks into me like a sponge. When I’m standing in front of a punching bag, the voices in my head keep repeating those words over and over again: Paper champion, lucky man, living on hopes and prayers and even they doubt me. Result? My punches become harder. My kicks become crisper. My grip tightens. My focus slims down...Narrower and narrower until, like the scope of a sniper, I’ve come across my target…Guess who that is Cozen? Shakur: Ever since May 26th, I’ve been poked and prodded, laughed by at the roster because I’ve done something that a handful of active wrestlers have accomplished. I’m like an imperfect museum exhibit that all seem fit to criticize because they were born with a mouth. And it’s starting to get to me. I’m starting to roam around my cage. The bars are weakening and I’m becoming hungrier. Hungry for prey foolish enough to think they can step over the proverbial trap that is my mouth and get away unscathed. Shakur: Open my cage, Cozen. Believe it intelligent to pet the animal even though you’ve been forbidden. I’m just a stop on your road to Colossus because I have something you want. You don’t need this belt. You only want it so that you can dangle it over Troy like a carrot. But you have something that I need, desperately…Validation. If I beat you for the SECOND time, something Tyler Rayne has done only ONCE, mouths shut and eyes begin to open…Little bigger than your play date, eh? Shakur’s head tilts and he produces a devious smile, placing the small wedge of ice into the perfectly fitting hole. Shakur: You are like the Titanic, Cozen: Power, speed, skill, ingenuity, nobody’s gonna take you down. Oh, what’s that ahead, an iceberg? Pfft, it’s so small and I’m so very powerful. Yes I am. I can run right through it…Whoops, a little dent in the body. No biggie. I have enough compartments that can fill with water and still be safe! Yeppers I do…Wait, why are my alarms going off? Why is my crew panicking? Why are life rafts being boarded? Why are people jumping off my ship? Why are people kissing their loved ones goodbye…Wait, why am I tilting at an awkward angle? I’m…I’m BREAKING! HELP! I’M GOING UNDERWATER…OH MY GOD! THAT ICEBERG…IT…IT WAS FUCKING ENORMOUS! Yanking back the blanket, Shakur reveals a giant ice castle, majestically crafted through hours of painstaking work. Out
