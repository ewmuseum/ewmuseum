Phoning it in

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/26/05 'So all he could say was 'I AM THE VIOLATOR?' and 'PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!'? asked the rich contralto voice on the phone in an amused tone. 'And get this... he always said it twice!' The Trashcan Man laughed into the cell phone as he stood outside the Chicago's O' Hare airport. He voice deepened as he tried to imitate the Violator, the larger partner of that lovable tag team of Satanic Jobbers, Total Violation. 'I AM THE VIOLATOR! PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION! I AM THE VIOLATOR! PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION!' The female on the phone laughed throatily. 'You're making this up, Mike, you've GOT to be!' Trashy grinned as the crowd of travelers parted around him, continuing on into the airport or out to the street, looking harried either way. 'No, no, it's all he would ever say. Even when he was trying to ask a question, it would be 'I AM THE VIOLATOR? PREPARE FOR TOTAL VIOLATION?' 'Wow.... you've met the craziest people.' 'You said it, Suzanne. Of course, none of 'em are crazier than me.' Trashy chuckled self deprecatingly. A tone of exasperation enter's Suzanne's voice, and you can picture her tapping her foot impatiently. 'You're not crazy, Mike. You're a brilliant artist. Sure you're a bit eccentric, but creative geniuses always are.' The hardcore legend looks down at his feet, a rush of blood entering his cheeks as the unexepected and unusual praise embarrasses him. 'I'm not that good...' 'Yes you are! But I'm not going to have this conversation with you again. What I want to know is... when you get back from your business trip can we get together for coffee or something? We've been talking on the phone for a couple of weeks now, I'd like to see you.' 'Yeah, well... I told you I've been busy with work...' 'Whatever it is you do' 'Right, anyways, I'm going to be gone all weekend... I won't even get home until Tuesday night.' 'Fine! Wednesday night then, we're going out somewhere, and that's all there is to it! Pick me up at 7pm...' The alarm on the Trashcan Man's face speaks volumes as he sputters 'I... um...' 'Then it's settled! I'll see you then!' 'But...' 'Well, you better hurry, you'll miss your plane!' 'What...' The call ends as the Trashcan Man stammers into his phone. He finally closes and shakes his head. 'What have I gotten myself into' he mutters to himself as he starts to make his way through the press of the crowd into the airport and we fade to black. My deepest apologies for the immense shortness of this RP. Same excuse as usual, work's been kicking my rear. But fear not... things are about to change for the better. I'm about to have a lot more free time from work... and quite of bit of it's going into this fed. I've got plans... *rubs hands together* Oh I've got plans... Muahahahahahaahahaha...
