Convalescence And Evanescence - From KVL To KFW

26 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3-26-06 From Dictionary.com: evanescence n : the event of fading and gradually vanishing from sight; 'the evanescence of the morning mist' Karina, I’m writing you this letter because, well, I don’t know you very well. And I know you don’t know me very well. But in a few days, we’re gonna be locked in combat, blah blah, and while I don’t usually do this…I uniquely respect you amongst the PRIME roster. I regret the fact that we’ve not talked too much before. But you know, you’re busy with people you need to kick in the face, and I’m busy getting my ass kicked by Danny Ferguson and his A-List. All you probably know about me is that, and the fact that I’ve got a guitar and some cancer. And that’s not all of me. It’s a part, but it’s not the whole thing. This isn’t a plea for sympathy. On the contrary, I think I’ll take you further than you’ve had to go in a singles match in a long while. And I wouldn’t WANT you to go easy on me. That’d be lame. I don’t wrestle in PRIME to face off with 85% Karina, 60% Tchu, or 100% Psymon. I wrestle here because you’re the best. And it’s not a love letter. I know, a nice change of pace, huh. I just, like I said…want to start some kind of dialogue. Share something about each other so that when we get in the ring, you’ll know who and what you’re up against. And then, maybe, you might do the same for me. Like pen pals, but less gay. And it’s okay, I’ll start. … I love the movie ‘Highlander.’ No shit. I straight up LOVE it. And I’m not afraid to admit this. Seriously, though. Look at the opening. It’s a straight up slow-burn opening that cuts to this huge swordfight between two guys in a parking lot. Christopher Lambert knows that it’s time to cut someone’s head off. He’s cold. He’s cool. He’s the quintessential bad ass. There’s an awesome special effects sequence during the Quickening, where he takes that other immortal’s power. Augh. Just thinking about it makes me want to watch someone get struck by lightning. Oh, and then there’s the bitching Queen soundtrack. Say what you want about Freddie Mercury loving the man-juice, but that dude could sing like nothing I’ve ever heard. The movie’s got drama. It’s got character without being too campy. Likable protagonist, villain that’s both interesting and hopelessly evil, requisite love interest, creative plot… And, what do you know, it makes you believe that a man could live forever. … Plus, it’s got Sean Connery. That guy is HARSH. Anyways, I was watching it the other day, in the hospital bed. And Bekah (my half-sister), she was sitting.next to me, still half-asleep from the flight to Osaka from the States. See, she brought me all kinds of flicks, a regionless DVD player/TV thing that her new boyfriend had. But back to the flick. They’re in the church, on Hallowed Ground, and the big bad dude, the Kurrgan, is causing trouble, because that’s what he does. He’s a fucking Neanderthal. But he’s truthful. He’s hassling people in the church, showing zero respect. And we know that the final confrontation is growing near, between Connor McCleod and The Kurrgan. But the Kurrgan has one last thing to say, and it’s kind of at the dude in the pew, but from the way its shot, it also looks like he’s breaking the fourth wall and talking to me. The Kurrgan, he rears back, with that gravelly, hissing voice, and pulls back with: “It’s better to burn out…than to fade away!” And sure enough, he does. Twenty minutes later, Connor cuts his fucking head off, and blows up half of New York’s shipping district in the process. Suh-weet. (On a small aside, remember when the Highlander immortals were the only people cutting heads off in movies? Now everyone’s doing it, thanks to Tarantino. It’s still kinda cool, though.) By the time the credits are rolling, Bekah’s asleep, and I’m thinking about the movie, because honestly, there’s fuckall left to do in this hospital. Seriously. I’m going to throw out the top five things to do in a Japanese hospital for you right now. #5: Nothing.#4: Pray for a sexy nurse / sponge bath combo (note: this does not happen).#3: Read a textbook on the Japanese language.#2: Make Top Five lists.#1: Nothing. Anyways, I’m thinking…which is dangerous, I know. And I’m thinking about mortality, and what a huge disaster I’ve made of my run in PRIME so far. I mean, at CS, you at least got to beat Balthasar, and make history as the first person to win a match in PRIME by passing out. Then you made an incredible showing in the Halo, and came in third. And you’ve won it before. In case you were keeping score, this is what I won at Culture Shock: Did I give everything I possibly could to win those matches? Honestly, I dunno. I think so. But I owe it to myself to keep trying. I mean, I thought I gave 110%. And I’ve been told that you’re a real winner as long as you try. I think that that’s a lie we tell ourselves to stay happy. Actually, coming from someone who pulled off a performance called “gutsy” and “endearing” in the Halo, I can tell you that giving 110% isn’t winning. Surviving the night isn’t winning. Winning is winning. And I think that there’s the possibility (a slight one) that if I really give everything I have in the ring with you, then I might win. Sure, if I do that, there might not be enough left of me to fill a Zip-Loc bag. I might pass out from exhaustion, ruin my career, whatever. I don’t have much of a career anyways. But if people can say “Kyle Lamen kicked ass in that match-of-the-year-candidate with Wolfenden in ’06, and pulled off one of the biggest upsets in PRIME history,” that’s a different animal. That could actually be worth the effort. Survival sucks. Winning is awesome. And it’s true: It IS better to burn out than to fade away. So I’m here, rotting in this hospital bed, being fed bad natto and rice instead of doing life. Because this is what I’m told to do, to save myself. And I hate doctors. I don’t trust them. Doctors poke and prod you, and sure they’ve got their “science” and their “medicine,” but aren’t you the commander of your body? How do you know the doctors aren’t cokeheads or sadists or madmen? Because I’ve had doctors who were. At least sadists and madmen. You know what? Fuck doctors. Fuck them real, real badly. (Oh, I hope my language isn’t offending you. If so, sorry.) I’m the commander of my body, and I say if it can move, it can go. I don’t feel as bad as these Japanese doctors say I feel. PRIME’s already sticking it to me by cutting a month’s worth of shows. I mean, you’ve got ten months of wrestling left, and they pull a month out of that? I call bullshit on that. So, I did something kinda dumb. I pulled out my IV tubes. Kids at home, (you don’t have kids, right?) do not EVER do this. It hurts, real bad. Especially when you have NO IDEA what you’re doing. My arm bled quite a bit, and I get the feeling it’ll scar up. I’m afraid that someone will see it and think I’ve been shooting up again. That is, if anyone at this point gives enough of a fuck to care. So yeah, I’m wearing an ugly robe-thing. It’s not quite a yukata, and not quite a paper dress like domestic hospitals. And I while I relish for a moment in the cool breeze wafting around my package, I do understand that my sister’s in the room, so I’d better find some real clothes in a hurry. And of course, Bekah’s waking up, because I’m wheezing due to my cracked ribs, and groaning a little due to the pain and bleeding arm. I found a t-shirt and some pants, so I suit up, and start thinking about the escape plan. What do I need to take? Do I still need to pay? Why is the room all fuzzy? Bekah wakes up, and prop myself up with the bed. Naturally, I explain to her that we’re leaving, and in a hurry. Let me tell you, my sister’s great. After she hit me four or five times, she was totally supportive. She agreed to run interference, if we ran into a doctor, which is awfully cool of her, considering she can’t speak a lick of Japanese. We had to leave the TV/DVD, which was a bummer. But I think Bekah sent someone from PRIME back to get it. I hope it wasn’t Blaine. If it was, he can keep it. A receptionist tried to stop us on the way out, but it was all like “No time for love, Dr. Jones!” and I promptly ignored her. Bekah stayed behind, and spoke simple English to try and keep her occupied, while I made a break for the automatic doors. Before I know it, bam! I’m out the door of the hospital. I’m gone like a ghost. Like evanescence, man. Of course, it was, like, fifteen degrees outside, and I’m just off fluids and in a t-shirt, but I feel like a winner for about thirty seconds. Then I just feel like a moron. This “winner-for-thirty-seconds-then-moron” thing is pretty much the status quo for me now. Except sometimes the part “winner-for-thirty-seconds-then” is left out. I burned myself right out of the hospital. I performed a Kylectomy. I may be cold, alone, and tired…beaten, dying and humiliated…but it could be worse. It can always be worse. I could be Baltasar. (Oh! SNAP!) Now I’m banged up, beaten, and honestly…pretty depressed. I’m fairly certain you’re going to do what you always do, and that’s kick someone until they’re dead, then C-4 them to kill them a second, third, and fourth time. This time, that someone will be me. Maybe. And let’s be honest, if I can’t beat Ferguson mano-a-mano, what chance do I have against a REAL athlete? Even if you don’t cheat. (Don’t cheat, btw. That would be SO gauche.) But in the end, it’ll be a romp. It’s what it always is when I get to face someone I respect, and in some ways admire. I’ll do what I always do, and that’s leave buckets of sweat and a pint of blood in the ring in an attempt to be the best at what I do. I expect nothing less from you. And when we’re done with this, we’ll probably leave ugly corpses. (Of course mine will be uglier than yours.) I can’t wait for you to beat me silly. And why? Because it’s better to burn out…than to fade away! -Kyle Lamen P.S. – In the end, there can be only one. -|----> P.P.S – More Top Five Lists Top Five 80’s Songs #5 – ‘Born To Run’ Bruce Springsteen#4 – ‘Melt With You’ Modern English#3 – ‘Livin’ On A Prayer’ Bon Jovi#2 – ‘I Remember You’ Skid Row#1 – ‘Just Like Heaven’ The Cure Top Five Movies #5 – SLC Punk#4 – Battle Royale#3 – The Last Samurai#2 – Clerks (Red Bank, represent!)#1 – High Fidelity Top Five Moves I Will Take In Our Match #5 – C4 (consider skipping this one)#4 – Kick in the torso#3 – Negasonic Lupine Warhead#2 – Whirlwind Romance#1 – Kick in the face Top Five Female Wrestlers #5 – Min Takahashi#4 – Akiko#3 – Dawn McFarlane#2 – Eva Van Lorne#1 – Aimz or Ellis Nash Just kidding! #1’s you!
