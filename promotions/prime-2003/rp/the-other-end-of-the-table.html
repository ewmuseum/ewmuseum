The Other End of the Table

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 170 An evening of business was at hand for Alexander Lewis. Alexander sits on one end of a table, nearly twenty feet long and made of pure oak, while his various business associated and cohorts sit along the table discussing a variety of issues at hands. Lewis sits in this mammoth leather chair with his eyes looking around the room while his ears and mind remain focused on the meeting at hand. With a glass of scotch in one hand and his other hand rested in the nook of his chin, he nods his head at first before shaking it furiously while the veins in his neck start to harden. "No, no, no, you've got this shit all fucking backwards. You want to focus your efforts on Asia? That's fine. Tell me how we're any different then those before us. No, do me one better. Tell us how we're going to be any different then the Indian Mafia or the Salakau. We're not going to be. Sure, we'll rule with an iron fist, but your dreams and goals and mine are much different. I'm not looking to make money from a country. I'm looking to own a continent. I'm looking to change the way this world works and in the end, tear this world apart. Do you understand me?" There's a variety of grunts and noises coming from the table, but after one look into the eyes of Alexander they immediately shuffle their dissent. He sat there, lord and ruler of them all, more powerful then any had given him credit months ago. Yet, he had done the unthinkable, wrestling control of the black market away from seven different families and remaining under the radar the entire time. His dreams and goals were lofty but not unattainable. If they listened to him then they would all be better for it, and deep down inside of them they knew that. "So, what do you have in mind?" a large latino man by the name of Dominic asks him. It's almost as if Alexander had been waiting for that as he smiles proudly as he leans back in his chair. "For so many decades, I've seen men try and fail at taking over Africa. A land with so much promise and potential that has just been squandered by different men who view themselves as deities and kings wanting those niggers to be their slaves. Each and every one of them ended their lives amidst fire and death. We need to build it up, take not just a country, but all of it under our rule. Give them salvation and food, medicine and water, and watch as they bow to our feets ready to perform our every task. We install those that we need to run the country while we sit in the background and not only decide the fates of millions, but infiltrate the other countries as they bend to our will. The world will never see it coming and overnight, Africa will be the Superpower that everyone aspires to be like," he tells them all, detailing his dream in-depth as he brings them into a life of not just rich drug drealers, but a world that they ran without fear of prosecution. As the men in the room think about this new world order, the door to the room opens, and in walks one of Alexander's aides. "Sir, there's something you might need to see." Alexander looks up at the aide, annoyed with him for bothering him at this important time. "What is it? What the fuck are you bothering me with at this time? What could be so fucking important that you interrupt us?!" he yells violently to the point that aide is rather scared at this point. Alexander just glares at the aide, almost frozen in spot in his brand new black suit and his freshly cut hair. He had been on this job for less than a week and already is having second thoughts about taking it. "Look, what's your name?" The aide then clears his throat, "Johnny." Hearing his name, Alexander looks like he's entered a bad dream before looking at one of the men sitting right next to him with his mouth wide open. "Johnny? Who the fuck calls themselves Johnny anymore?" he asks the guy rhetorically before looking back at his aide. "Look, kid, your name is John. Not no fucking Johnny, you hear me?" The kid then nods his head in agreement not daring in the least bit to go against Alexander. "So, this thing you have to show me. It's a package of some sort?" The kid pauses for a moment before looking outside of the door to clarify what exactly it is that's outside of the room. As he looks back into the room he nods his head. "Something like that, sir." Alexander rolls his eyes, wondering to himself if the kid has a lick of sense in that head of his. "Look, just bring it in here and put it on the table. I'll take a look at it after the meeting is done, okay?" With that, the kid exits the room hastily as Alexander returns to his discussion with his de-facto board of directors; the men he held closest to him. As he opens his mouth though, the door to the office returns again, but Johnny isn't walking through the door. Instead, it's three men from his security detail walking through the door carrying large objects wrapped in white sheets. Before he knows it the foreign objects are on top of the wooden oak table and as he takes a closer look at it he realizes there's something red seeping out of the sheets. The men quickly jump back from the table alarmed at the quick turn of events. Alexander slowly rises from his chair as he looks over at his security detail. "What the hell is this?" he asks one of the men from his security detail, but he just waves them away as he walks closer to the objects covered in white sheets, knowing in the back of his mind what they are. With his right hand he grips the edge of the sheet and pulls it back before it reveals a face. Silvia Young. A round hole appears where there should be a flap of skin. Her eyes are large and in shock, the final images she saw before her death painful and frightening to her. Alexander's tongue rolls around in his mouth as he takes a step back and takes a deep breath in. A moment passes before he grabs the other sheet and pulls it back, but for him it's all for naught. He already knows who will be under this sheet. Warren Green. Alexander's jaw hardens into steel as he looks at the various men in the room. "Out. Now," he tells them, his voice dark and heavy, as the implications that these deaths mean hit him square in the face. As they begin to rush out of the room, unaware as to why those bodies are lying on the oak table, Alexander can only sit their frozen in time. When the last one leaves the room, in walks Malik with a stone-cold expression on his face. He looks at the scene before him with great intent and solace. Malik then looks over at Alexander with a heavy stare. "It looks like the beast has awoken and now wants to play," Alexander says in a very stoic tone. Malik nods his head before reaching inside of his jacket as his phone begins to go off. Alexander though doesn't stop talking as Malik puts the phone to his ear. "He thinks he can scare us? Put our backs into the corner? He saw what we already did to him. These two deaths mean nothing in the grand sc--." Before he can finish though Malik has hung up the phone in such a way that sends a warning sign to Alexander. He doesn't even have to say anything as Malik begins to walk to the door. "Two more," he says. "He got two more." Alexander can only take another deep breath in as Malik walks out of the room. He had a chance a few weeks back to put this problem behind them and he had squandered his chance. As he walks over to his chair and takes a seat, he has only one thought for himself. Next time he wouldn't be so lucky.
