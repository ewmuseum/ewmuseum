Jealousy (Rev 169, v. Rayne and Dawkins)

11 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 11th, 2008 August 11th, 2008; Charlotte, North Carolina It was the morning after Colossus, and those stars that competed last night were feeling quite sore. It was a night of history, a night of honor, and a night worthy of remembrance. Champions were crowned, Hall of Famers were made, and fans were satisfied. Yet, for two men in particular, last night was a night they would love to forget about. Having lost the tag team championship match to Team VIAGRA, the hot debut of the Princes of New England had quickly become lukewarm. This was the only thought that went through Connor O'Reily's mind as he slowly started to wake up. Connor, in a sleepy haze, was laying on his hotel bed with his limbs sprawled out like he just kinda flopped down on the bed and quickly went to sleep. In fact, Connor was still wearing his street clothes at the time he went to bed, which consisted solely of a pair of dirty jeans and his sneakers. Sore as he was, Connor hated returning to the waking world, especially since he had this amazing dream involving some hot lesbians kissing each other while he got to watch. So, Connor did the only thing his groggy mind could possibly do... he attempted to fall back asleep and be reunited with those lesbians. He knew little about these lesbians except that they were both blonde, dressed like witches, one of them was named Alice, and the other was named Marisa. He just knew he'd love to be in their warm embrace and then turn them back to the path of men, single-handedly. And then he was suddenly startled awake by someone smacking him in the back with their bare hand. "Get up, unless you want to stay in Charlotte for another day." the voice of a young girl said. Connor looked up and caught a glimpse of his twin sister, Miranda O'Reily, looking down on him disapprovingly. Her long hair was already neatly brushed. She already had changed into one of her favorite summer dresses, a yellow sundress with white polkadots. Her black leather purse hung by her side almost absently. "Ugh," went Connor, sounding groggy, "Come on, sis, I had a match last night and I was going to get it on with lesbian witches. Give me another hour." "Connor, it's 11 in the morning. We need to leave." Miranda said, before she paused and added, "And please, for the love of God, at least put on a shirt before we leave. You look ridiculous." Connor slowly got up, somewhat gingerly after the events of last night, and managed to maneuver himself so that he was sitting directly in front of his sister. He started to try to shake the cobwebs, "Is leaving really more important than lesbians, Miranda? Really?" "Listening to you whine about your fantasies makes me want to go lesbian." Miranda commented with a groan. "Sis, I always thought you were one, already." Connor said, which earned him a smack in the side of the head. Now that disapproving glance included Miranda putting her hands on her hips and looking down at her brother. Connor decided to look away, but in doing so, he got a quick glance around the room and realized something was missing. Something important, "Where's Simon?" "Simon?" Miranda repeated, before she looked as though she remembered something and relaxed her arms, "Simon's sulking outside the hall. He's been ready to leave longer than *I've* been." "Seriously?" Connor asked, "And why didn't *he* wake me?" "Honestly, he's not said a single word to me since last night. He's been quiet. Brooding, almost. It's kind of... disturbing, really, given how much he tends to talk. He didn't say anything since we left the arena last night, either." Miranda said. She looked away, towards the door leading out to the hallway. Connor looked to where Miranda looked, and slowly got to his feet. Connor scanned the floor and found a discarded plain gray shirt, and snatched it from the floor. He started to put it on, and after he did so, he went straight to the door. "Uh, Connor, what about packing the rest of your stuff?" Miranda asked. "You can take care of it." Connor said in passing, before he twisted the handle of the hotel room's door and exited the hotel room before he could even begin to pay attention to Miranda O'Reily's protests. Outside of the Princes' hotel room, Connor was quick to notice Simon Knox leaning against the wall right next to the doorway. His long blonde hair, normally in perfect working order, was a tangled mess. His eyes were bloodshot, though Connor didn't quite notice at first because Simon had his sunglasses on. He seemed to be wearing the same clothes he had on when Connor collapsed in bed and never woke up until now, a wrinkled gray shirt (similar to the one Connor had on) and a pair of black slacks. He was staring at a cell phone, one of those flip phones, but he wasn't talking into it nor was he dialing any numbers. Instead, he just kind of stared. "Simon." Connor said, trying to catch his attention. Simon didn't even acknowledge Connor much as he commonly didn't acknowledge people trying to insult him, so Connor tried again, "Simon!" This time, Simon turned his head, and acknowledged his in-ring tag team partner. "Yeah?" Connor took this time to notice that Simon Knox looked like he had been in a car wreck. Apart from the beating he took at the hands of Team VIAGRA, he also looked like he hadn't gotten any sleep since last night. All of this meant that he looked a lot less pretty than he often claimed he was. "Dude," Connor said, "You look like hell." "Hmph." went Simon, "It's nothing." "Did you even sleep last night? I was so exhausted that I pretty much collapsed in bed and didn't wake up `til Miranda started nagging. You sure it's nothing?" Connor asked. Simon was a strange one to him. He offered him a chance to live his dream of becoming a wrestler, and became his tag team partner. To Connor O'Reily, Simon Knox was a guy he owed. He felt like he needed to help the guy out. Simon Knox's only action for a while was to simply place his cell phone back in his pants pocket. He sighed and looked up at the ceiling, before he muttered to himself, "He did it to me again." Connor wasn't sure what he meant, "What?" "Steve." flatly responded Simon. "The midget?" "No, you moron," Simon snapped, "My brother, Steve. That bastard did it to me again." "Dude," Connor O'Reily said, scratching the back of his equally unbrushed hair, "You're not exactly making a whole lot of sense here. What's got you pissed off? Tell me." Simon Knox was a man who was commonly unfazed by anything and everything. Connor often swore that ice water ran in his partner's veins. And yet, right now, Simon Knox was the most furious he'd ever seen him. Steve turned around and punched the wall, which really kinda did more damage to himself than the wall, but he didn't seem to care too much. "Colossus was supposed to be my... no, OUR moment. We were going to win the titles! And then Steve just struts in like he fucking owns PRIME and the world and he UPSTAGES everything we worked so hard to build! Tag titles?! Poof! Gone! Up in fucking flames! All because that motherfucker got some match involving some stupid goddamn interfed championship, and thus had to show up! And he thinks he can come up to me and that motherfucker, Soren, and act all chummy with all of us?! Bullshit!" Simon shouted. He punched the wall again. His hand ached because of it. Connor saw that his tag team partner was unravelling right before his eyes. It seemed that there really were things that got him to show his emotions, just not the kinds of emotions anyone would likely want to get out of him. "Oh! And then! And then it gets even better! He goes and blurts out that I have a crush on your sister! What the hell is that? Just because he had to settle for Joey's little sister doesn't mean he has to judge ME for what I like!" "Dude," Connor said, almost as if he tended to begin most of his statements to Simon with that word, "First of all, chill out. Second of all... you mean that what Steve said about you and Miranda wasn't bullshit?" "Oh, it's bullshit." Simon said, almost as quickly as Connor said it, "Look, I worked hard to get to this position. To overcome the shadows of him and my dad. And then he goes and gets himself higher on the fucking card." Connor O'Reily was an emotional guy. Some might say he's dumb, irresponsible, kind of a jerk, and a bit annoying. However, he wasn't as insensitive as his partner was, and he was quick to notice the cause of Simon's emotional outburst, "...Are you jealous of him?" "Jealous?" shouted Simon, getting right in Connor's face at this point, "Why would I be jealous of that fucking idiot!?" Connor was not intimidated by his partner's confrontational nature, being a confrontational person himself, "Well, I'm going out on a limb here, but when people think of the Knox family and current success, they don't think of you and sure as FUCK don't think of the guy with the American flag on his head. They think of Steve Knox. They think of the only one in your family who actually won at Colossus. Which really sucks because, with me being your partner, I lost too." Simon only grumbled, and then he sighed, "I can't stand either of them. Steve or Soren. They ruined Colossus for me. Both of them." Connor sighed, "Then we'll have our own night. We'll ruin their day." Simon's response was curiously to just walk away from Connor, as if Connor didn't say anything to him. However, Simon did mutter something to himself on his way out of Connor's view, "Maybe." Simon Knox was an individual who often didn't let anything anyone ever said bother him. Unfortunately, this trait of his also worked to his disadvantage, he often never listened to anyone when he was actually upset. He was prone to sulking, he was prone to getting depressed when things didn't go according to his plan, and generally speaking, he was prone to being jealous of the successes of his brothers. To Simon Knox, the fact that his older and extremely successful brother, Steve Knox, had won the Elite Championship at Colossus was like a javelin lodged into his heart. The fact that Steve was the only one of the Knox brothers to come away with Colossus with a victory was like somebody taking that javelin and lifting him off of the ground. Most importantly, the fact that Simon and Connor failed to win the tag team championships was like his heart getting shattered like glass. Maybe next week, Simon Knox would be over it. Right now, however, Simon Knox would burn in the fires of jealousy for his brother.
