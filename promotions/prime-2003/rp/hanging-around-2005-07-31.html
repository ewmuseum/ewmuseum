Hanging around

31 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/31/05 'HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT! HOLY SHIT!' 'Yeah, holy shit indeed,' a hoarse voice proclaims as Mr Smiley Face reclines in a black leather chair, staring at the tv screen, which is showing the last few moments of the Intense championship match from Colossus II. He presses a button on the remote control in his black gloved hand, rewinding the video to see Vangelus Olsig and himself trading blows at the top of a ladder. 'I've really gotta learn to stay away from ladders, I NEVER win these damn things. Last time I was on a ladder with anyone, DarkStar completely ignored the laws of physics and gave me a Pressure Drop from the top. I still have no idea how he did that. Lousy matchwriting is what it is...' The Smilin' Dude coughs into his fist at that blatant breaking of kayfabe before continuing in his hoarse voice. 'I mean, DarkStar was an amazing athlete...' His mask jerks back in remembered pain as he watches himself being Sacrificed from the top of the ladder through the barbed wire wrapped table below... his leather trench being torn up from being caught on the cruel barbs. 'Olsig called that one the Sacrifice... an appropriate term for me, I'd say. Ever since I was created, I was a sacrifice...' Taking a sip of his Country Time lemonade through a Krazy Straw reaching under his mask, he leans back and reminsces about his early days... (Yeah, that's right, it's a flashback. Be thankful I didn't go OLD SCHOOL MSF and pull out Danny Bonaduce video taping him with an old black and white video camera while he cut really bad promos.) 'Dr. Lovecraft?' queries a nervous lab coated attendant. His voice shakes slightly as he tries to get his superiors attention. 'S-s-sir?' He slowly extends a trembling hand towards the man behind the desk, who is apparently oblivious to his presence as he rifles through a folder, muttering to himself. Just before he touches, Dr Lovecraft's cold dead eyes snap up and stares into the attendant's. 'What have I said about physical contact in the workplace, William?' asks Dr Lovecraft in his light, melodious voice. 'Uh... I... I...' the young man, barely out of medical school stutters as he takes an unconcious step away from the administrator and lead pychiatrist of the privately owned Evanston Clinic For The Mentally Disturbed. 'It's unnecessary, isn't it?' Waving away young William's stammered apology with his long, slender hands, Dr Lovecraft gazes back down at his file. 'What do you want?' 'Well sir... it's Michael... from the Special Services wing...' 'Yes, yes, a most frustrating case of pyromania and what appears to be mulitple personality disorder. Our patron is very interested in him...' 'Yes sir... he's been... he's acted up again.' Dr Lovecraft looks up at the attendant, arching a delicate eyebrown behind the round lens of his spectacles. 'Acted up, you say?' He gaunt features wrinkle into a slight frown. 'Yes sir... apparently he somehow set a trashcan on fire and attacked some of the personnel in another escape attempt. Two guards are laid up with burns and bruises, and we think he might've cracked the skull of one of the orderlies...' 'Yes, yes, but did he escape?' 'N-no Doctor... he's been sedated and is in the padded cell, straightjacketed and manacled.' 'Good, good...' Dr Lovecraft steeples his long fingers together in front of him as he ponders. 'Michael, Michael... what ARE we going to do with you... I told you what would happen if you attempted to escape again.' He shakes his head, chuckling almost fondly, like a parent amused at a child getting into light mischief, not a young man trying to fight his way out of captivity. 'Very well, apparently stronger measures need to be taken... William, prepare the electroshock treatment chamber, and let them know we'll be increasing the output dramatically, so maintenance had better prepare the device accordingly.' 'Increasing the output sir... are you sure that's a good idea?' Faltering at Dr Lovecraft's gaze, he stammers a bit but continues. 'Already you've used record setting voltage on him... anymore and won't you cook his brain?' 'William... when I want your opinion... well, I can't conceive of any moment past, present or future that I would want your opinion, so follow my orders or be terminated from employment. Do I make myself clear?' 'Y-yes Dr Lovecraft,' replies the attendant who scampers off in fear. 'Good help is so hard to find these days... I think I might have a new subject for testing out some of my new therapeutic techniques.' Dr Lovecraft rises to his impressive six and a half foot height, his sparse frame covered by a tight white lab coat over a white shite, black tie, and black slacks. His shoes are polished to a mirror finish, and his raven black hair is slicked back from his widow's peak with not a hair out of place. Touching the tip of his long narrow beak of a nose with the tip of a finger, he nods to himself. 'Yes, young William is unfortunately showing too many alternative ideas for his own good.' He presses a button on his desk. 'Mr Winter?' he says into the air. 'Yes, Dr Lovecraft?' replies a gruff voice from a speaker. 'Young William should be detained before he returns home tonight. We'll be placing him in Project Awaken.' 'Very good Dr Lovecraft.' We flash a little later to see a lean youth with shaggy brown hair, no older than fifteen, strapped down to a table. His only rainment are white cotton sweatpants and the leather manacles holding his wrists down to his side. Several attendants of various races and genders swarm around in lab coats, adjusting this, checking that, doing pretty much what you'd see in this sort of situation. The boy is pleading for mercy, his begging going ignored by the employees of the Evanston Clinic. Suddenly his eyes widen with fear as he starts to jerk his head side to side, muscles straining up against his skin as he tries to break free of the straps holding him to the table. 'Now Michael, you've been very naughty.' Dr Lovecraft enters the room, clipboard in hand as he gazes down at his patient. 'Yes indeed, let's see, two guards with second degree burns, one of them with a broken wrist, the other with a broken clavicle... And that poor orderly...' The doctor tsks. 'Looks like he's hemorrhaging in his brain from the strike to the skull yo delivered. Quite impressive, my boy. But still, I'm very disappointed in you. Resorting to violence... AND you went back to using fire again... You remember what I told you would happen if you did such a thing?' Young Michael starts to cry out in panic as orderlies force his mouth open and insert a red rubber mouthpiece. 'That's right, Michael. You're going to be punished again... worse than before. You've been a bad boy, and you have to be disciplined.' Michael sobs in defeat through his gag as an attendant attatches some electrodes to his head. 'Now remember... this is going to hurt you a lot more than it's going to hurt me... Engage!' A loud humming is heard, followed by a thud as every muscle in Michael's body spasms at once, his body arching off the table before slamming back down again, his scream muffled by the mouthpiece. 'Again' commands Dr Lovecraft, and he's obliged as Michael once again jerks around to the voltage slamming into his skull. He collapses, breathing in pain through the rubber gag in his mouth. 'That should drive some of the rebelliousness out of you... wait... what are you doing?' Dr Lovecraft leans over and stares into the tear streaked face of Michael. 'You're.... laughing?' And he's proven correct as the youth's sobs are slowly changing to laughter, still muffled by the rubber mouthpiece. The boy's eyes widen in hysteria as he continues to laugh, his entire body shaking in his incessant mirth. 'Stop that this instant! Hit him again!' Dr Lovecraft pushes an orderly out of the way when he doesn't move fast enough for him and throws the switch himself. Michael's body jerks up, straining at his bonds, his face locked in a maniacal grin as he screams his laughter. Lovecraft hits him twice more with the juice to no avail as Michael continues to cackle madly. 'That's enough... sedate him and put him in the padded cell for observation... I want reports every half hour on his vitals.' The orderlies scamper to obey his wishes, and Michael is put back into a straightjacket, laughing wildly as the gag is removed and he's led off. 'Dr Lovecraft... what's he doing now?' 'Without further observation, a diagnosis is impossible at this juncture, but my gut instinct is that all our attempts at locking down the second personality has done the exact opposite. I think we've splintered his mind into a third personality. I hope I'm wrong. Mr Kriegman's going to be furious...' (Ok, flashback's over. See, that wasn't too bad. It could've been worse, remember, the PCW Archives are linked on the PTC forums, I could easily go hunt down Mr Smiley Face's first match and do a point of view from that, that seems to be really popular lately. But don't worry, I won't do that to you guys... yet.) Mr Smiley Face comes back to himself to see Olsig's hand being raised on the screen. Of course, no one's coming to help the Original Prankster hanging from the noose behind Olsig, but isn't that typical? He presses another button on the remote freezing the screen as it focuses on his eyes bulging out in the holes of his mask. Smiley rubs his throat and winces. 'If they'd have left me hanging there longer with a little vaseline, I could've been the lead singer for INXS.' (Sorry for this interjection, but I feel like explaining this joke, because, you know, some of these guys aren't very old and might not get the joke. You see, the lead singer for 80's Austrailian rock band INXS died why engaging in autoerotic asphyxiation... What do you mean, you don't know what that is? It's when you strangle yourself when indulging in... you know.... Beating the bishop? Choking the chicken? Saluting your flag pole? Spanking the monkey? Punishing the Pope? Flogging the dolphin? Burping the worm? Pee Weeing the Herman? Consulting Dr Jerkoff? Churning your butter? Bopping the Baloney? Cleaning your rifle? Waking Wee Willie Winkie? Test firing the meat missile? I can do this all day, you know... I think you got the idea now. Back to the RP) (Wait, further interjection. I mean I can come up with more slang for masturbation all day. Not jerk off all day. I'm not Bobby Stormbringer for feck's sake.) 'Sacrifice... I've always been the one that's taken over when Trash has had too much... and now I'm stuck here. What am I going to do now?' You can burn! 'Trash, is that you... WHOA!' Smiley feels himself being dragged down, whirling in a vortex as the mental barriers holding him in the dominant position suddenly crumble. He looks around at the barren wasteland that has been the Mindscape lately. 'Finally, the barriers are gone... Trash, where are you?' Smiley calls out, his voice clear as a bell as here in the Mindscape, his vocal cords weren't damaged by a prolonged hanging. 'Here I am, traitor.' The Smilin' Dude whirls around, seeing the Trashcan Man standing behind him. He snarls 'You thought you could take over and give me to Kriegman?' Mr Smiley Face tilts his head in confusion and holds his black gloved hands out defensively. 'No, no Trash, you've got it wrong. Didn't Ed come down and talk to you?' 'I tried Smiley, but he wouldn't listen.' The flickering form of the Ghost of Ed appears, shaking his head sadly. 'I just can't get through to him.' 'I know he's got you poisoned agaisnt me Ed. But this bastard has been helping your murderer all along!' The Trashcan Man turns to the flickering ghost and pleads. 'Stay out of the way Ed, I'll explain everything later. Trust me...' 'No Trash, you're wrong this time. Smiley's on our side, he's always been...' 'Listen to Ed, Trashy. You know deep down that I'm your friend. Who took over when Lovecraft would hurt you? Who took over when Dagoth was gunning for our friends? Who took over when Kriegman had pushed you too far into attacking Tempest...' 'DON'T YOU DARE SAY HER NAME!' screams the Trashcan Man as winds rise around him. His eyes flicker with a rising flame as fire starts to lick from his fingertips. 'Calm down Trashy... listen to me...' 'You worked for Kreigman all this time... you betrayed us to him...' 'I chased his overcliched ass with a chainsaw, Trash!' 'You're just trying to confuse me... NO MORE!' With that scream of defiance, the Trashcan Man extends his hand a tongue of flame uncurls from his orange gloved palm and flashes towards Mr Smiley Face, who yelps and dives out of the way. 'No reason to get hot under the collar, Trashy. C'mon now, we'll sit down, have a slice of pizza and talk this out...' 'No more talking, Lachesis. Now you burn!' The flames in his eyes writhe as he extends both arms and sends a bigger blast of flame at the Smilin' Dude, who once again dives out of the way. 'Trashy, you don't want to do this... the Mindscape is my plaything, you know I'm stronger here than you...' 'Because Kriegman made you that way Lachesis...' Another bolt of flame, and this time Mr Smiley Face responds by clenching a fist and a chunk of the earth rises up into a small wall, the flame splashing against the rock and leaving a scorch mark. 'Don't make me hurt you, Clotho.' begs the Smilin' Dude, as both men are now calling each other by the names assigned to them by Alan Kriegman so many years ago in the Evanston Clinic. 'Don't you see? This is what Atropos wants! He wants us to weaken each other so he can take over!' 'YOU LIE!' His eyes burning constantly with fire now, the Trashcan Man raises his hands to the sky, and in response two pillars of flame erupt from the earth on either side of him. He points them at Mr Smiley Face, who's no longer clad in his customary black trench coat. Instead he has a bright yellow trenchcoat and a fireman's helmet. In his hands is a firehose, that he turns on and blasts the incoming jets of flame with his high pressure water. A cloud of steam fills the area, causing the Trashcan Man to spin around, trying to locate his other personality. 'Surf's up, Big Kahuna!' The Trashcan Man turns and his flaming eyes widen as he's suddenly engulfed in the shadow of the giant wave looming over his head. At the crest of the wave is Mr Smiley Face, balancing on a surfboard clad in only shorts and his bright yellow cardboard smiley face mask. 'Cowabunga' he cries as the wave crashes down onto the Trashcan Man, burying him under thousands of gallons of water. Mr Smiley Face waves his hand and is back in his customary black trenchcoat. 'That's brisk, baby!' he laughs as he looks down at the motionless, drenched Trashcan Man. 'Ok, Clotho, I think that cooled you off for the time being, let's get you up on your feet and talk this out.' He bends over Trashy's form who flips over, eyes still ablaze and snarling with hate as he reaches up with a flaming fist and grabs the Smilin' Dude around the throat. 'Traitor... you will burn!' Trashy snarls in Smiley's face as the flesh around his throat blackens and burns, sending smoke into the air as Mr Smiley Face writhes in pain, cackling madly all the while. 'Trash, don't do it man. Don't be like Kriegman!' Trashy spins to see the ghost of Ed's flickering form beside him. 'This scum works for Kriegman, Ed. He's probably part of the reason Kriegman was able to force me to help kill you. He deserves to die!' His other hand also catches on fire as he slams it into Smiley's gut over and over again, the flesh on Smiley's throat flaking away into ash. 'This isn't you, Trash. This is the dark one's influence...' 'This IS the dark one, Ed. And it's time I finished him once and for all, then I'll finally be free!' Trashy flings the heavily burned Mr Smiley Face to the ground and raises his fist. Smiley looks up at the snarling visage of the Trashcan Man and sees his fate. Reaching out, he suddenly feels... something's amiss. 'Atropos,' the Smilin' Dude whispers. 'He's trying to escape...' With the last of his strength, Mr Smiley Face reaches out and folds the Mindscape around him, dispersing his essence into the barriers holding the dark one in his tower just as the fires blasts where he was lying. When the smoke clears, all that's left is a half melted smiley face button that the Trashcan Man sneers down at. 'Finally, Kriegman's hold is broken. I'm free of him forever.' With that, the Trashcan Man ascends, taking over the body again, leaving the ghost of Ed in the Mindscape. Ed bends down and picks up the smiley face button. 'This... can't be good.' Back in the real world, a hand reaches up and pulls off the smiley mask. The Trashcan Man looks at it for a long moment, turning the mask over in his gloved hands. 'Smiley...' With a shake of head, he flings the mask across the room and rubs at his throat. 'Goddamn my neck hurts... what happened while I was trapped?' He's startled at the hoarseness of his voice, but not as concerned as he is when he feels the scabbed over sores around his neck. Glancing at the TV provides him with his answer, as he sees the frozen picture of Mr Smiley Face hanging from a noose. Frowning he rewinds the tape long enough to see Vangelus Olsig receiving the Intense title. 'Couldn't even defend my belt while I was gone? I dunno if I'm scheduled for a match or not, but I do know that me and Mr 'I'm going to attack Trash from behind to get a title shot' Olsig are going to have a little conversation.' He cracks his knuckles as he rewinds the tape to the beginning. 'Might as well see what I've missed.' He hits play and starts to watch Colossus II as we fade to black.
