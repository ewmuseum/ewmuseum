Behind the curtain (RP for Dual Halo)

27 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/27/07 A plastic bag tumbles across a quiet street, a cold winter breeze sending on it's journey. It wanders aimlessly, letting the night wind carry it wherever it will. It falters at a curb, it's travels momentarily impeded by the supposedly insurmountable concrete barrier. Appearances can be deceiving however, as a gust from a passing car drives it up and over the curb and on into the yard. Moonlight sparkles on the frost covered grass as the modern age tumbleweed continues it's rustling trek to the end of it's destination, a tall oak tree. Standing in the shadow of this tree stands a dark figure, clutching a brightly wrapped package in his gloved hands. His breath mists in the cold air, but he seems to be oblivious to the chill in his black t-shirt and jeans. His face is a picture of misery as he gazes at the brightly painted cottage in front of him. Bright light shines from the window, taunting him with a warmth, coziness, and cheerfullness that he knows he can never have. Not any more. Jumpin' Jack Flash had seen to that over a year ago when he first came to be. What was once his home was now eternally denied him. The warm, caring embrace of the woman inside would never be his again. He would never lay in bed beside her, his hand on the swell of her pregancy, her small, delicate hands covering his burned scared mitt without the slightest bit of hesitancy or disgust. Whispered conversations about the glorious future their son would face, the world famous artist, the brilliant doctor that cured cancer, the diplomat that would rise to be president and bring about a peace the world had never seen, these would never be shared again. Nor would the quiet moments of loving, interspersed with gasps, moans, and sweet words. Instead, if he were to look into her warm brown eyes, he would see only hurt, betrayal, and shame. When Jack arose he had said some hurtful, horrible things that no eight month pregnant woman should ever have to hear. An expecting mother should be cherished for the beauty and wonder that she is, not cast off as being unattractive, unappealing, unwanted, or unloved. But that mattered not to Jack. He had awakened ready to live life to what he considered the fullest, and having a fat, gassy, hormonal woman had no place in the life he had envisioned. And there was no way in HELL he was going to sit around after she squirted out a pissy, shit covered yowling moodbreaker of a parasite. And so he told her so. Even more eloquently than above. There was no coming back from such a speech. Jack had crossed a line... and bought a plane ticket and began a world tour of sex, booze, sex, drugs, sex, partying, sex, and even more sex. The brightly painted blue door was forever closed to Michael Kriegman in all his personas. Since that was the case... what was the Trashcan Man doing here? Simply put, it was his son's first birthday. And the Trashcan Man had bought a present for his boy. A big plush Teddy bear, something for his son to clutch at night as he slept... Suddenly he turns towards us, a look of disgust on his face. "OK, that's enough of that shit. Time the fuck out." The suburban nightscape fades out to a horizonless grey expanse. The Trashcan Man cups his orange gloved hands to his face and shouts out. "GET DOWN HERE! WE'VE GOT TO TALK!!" He's greeted by a high pitched cackling as Mr Smiley Face, clad in his standard square yellow cardboard mask, black gloves and black trenchcoat with an upside down smiley face button on the chest and the words "KICK YOURSELF" in bright yellow on the back fades into view. "Going to Norma Rae up in this bitch, Trash?" enquires the Smilin' Dude. The hardcore legends nods as he turns, looking for something in the empty space. "Got that right Smiley. This just isn't working." "'Bout time someone said something. Didn't think it was my place, being the new guy," pipes in Jumpin' Jack Flash, clad as always in his skintight yellow, orange, and red jumpsuit. "I was expecting a huge storyline, complete with a title run when I showed up. But what do I get? Tasered by that flat chested Karina Wolfenden and I had to... " He sighs. "I had to give up to the friggin' Soprano. How hard is it to roll forward and escape that piece of crap, I mean really?" "At least you didn't rip off a scene from a Tarantino movie," chimes in a dark figure who is somehow completely cloaked in shadows, even though there's no apparent light source around, much less something that could shade someone from the nonexistant light source. "I've been hinted at for over four years... and I'll probaby end up jobbing to Wolfenden in my first match. What kind of juggernaut of evil can I be if I can't even win a debut match?" All four personalities glance at each other and nod as one. They turn and in stereo, yell up at the grey sky... "MIKE, YOU FAT FUCK! GET DOWN HERE! RIGHT FUCKING NOW!" A rip appears and a portly figure strides in. Did I say portly? I meant fat. Yeah, he's a porker. He's got shaggy dark blonde hair and a beard that's in need of a good trimming. He takes off his glasses and wipes the lenses on his black hooded sweatshirt before replacing them on the oversized melon he calls a head. Pulling out a Malboro Light (a poor attempt at quitting smoking had him switch from Reds a couple of years ago), he lights it with a black zippo and takes a drag, gazing around at his creations. He sighs out a puff of smoke and shakes his head as he rubs the dark circles under his eyes. "Alright guys, I'm tired, I'm hungry, I haven't gotten laid in two months, and I'm stressed the fuck out. What do you want?" The personalities of Michel Kriegman all gaze at each other, then Jack, Smiley, and Lazarus motion to the Trashcan Man to take the floor. "Listen Mikey, we've got to talk about the... fuck it, the piss poor writing you've been subjecting us to the past couple of months." With a motion of his chubby hand, the handler known as [name withheld] manifests a folding chair and spins it around, sitting ass backwards. "Guys, I know it's been rough. Work's kicking me in the ass, and since my company's getting sold on Wednesday, I don't even know if I'm going to have a job next week. I probably shouldn't have signed up for GTT6, and I probably shouldn't have let Mat talk me into coming back when I did." The shadowy figure shakes his head. "That's fine, Mike, we understand that... but you've been plotting my debut for years. What happened to the long, drawn out emergence? You had that great image of Trash getting beat down, followed by Smiley taking over and getting the continued beat down. Remember the image you had in your head? The taser blast to the face, catching Smiley's mask on fire? Having both of them speak at the same time as half the mask was singed away, saying something to the effect of "You fools, you have no idea what you've unleashed?" That was a great idea, and my coming would've been huge. Instead I've got people bitching that trying to catch Karina on fire is too far... although how that's too far when Chainz is running around talking about child rape and whatnot is fine and dandy, I'll never know." "And what the hell is up with making me bisexual?!" interjected Jumpin' Jack Flash. "I dig the outfit, the entrance needs a little work but it's passable, and I love the movelist... but having me wake up in bed with a girl AND a guy in my first RP? What's that?" Mike sighs as he rests his forehead on the cool metal back of his chair. "I was hoping a little shock value would let me get a win over Jeff and Dave. Hell, I live with a gay couple, I was curious to see how an openly bisexual but not flaming character would get over. I didn't know Ember/Jay Phoenix was gay until after I wrote that." He raises his head and takes another drag. "But I was planning on making you bi for quite some time Jack. Don't look so shocked. Just wish I had more time to write it better." The Trashcan Man takes a step forward and squats in front of his creator. "Mike, we know you've got a lot on your plate right now. But I've got to ask... what's with all this emo shit? I've cried more in the last two months than..." "Tammy Faye Baker when she found out about Jim!" Mr Smiley Face chimes in. "It seems to be the way efedding works now guys. Everyone's got kids, or a wife, or a family, or some shit like that. I want you guys to be successful, I want the respect of my peers, I want you guys to be known like you were before." "Dammit Mike... don't you get it? I... WE never had to get over with this new style crap. All this garbage about emotions and love and growth... it was never our thing! God damn it, I'm the mother fuckin' Trashcan Man. I'm the hardcore legend! I set shit on fire, I go on wacky adventures. We didn't get where we are by talking about our feelings, we got where we were by occasionally telling reality to sit down and shut the fuck up. We were witty, we were funny, we were dark, we were all over the place... and dammit, that's why we were loved." Mike nods as he rises to his feet. "You know, maybe you're right. This obviously isn't working for us... maybe it's time to go back to what brought us to the dance." Lazarus sighs. "It's back to the cage for me, isn't it?" Mike shrugs as he replies. "Probably Laz. Face it, I'm not ready to write for you yet. Only reason I brought you in so early is because Mat wanted to feud with someone dark, and I never liked writing Trash as a heel. So yeah, I'll probably stick you back in your box for a little bit." Jack raises his hand. "What about me?" "No box for you... but you're still bisexual." "Dammit!" "Smiley, good chance there'll be some pranking coming up soon, get ready." Mike's demand is met by high pitched cackling. "Mike... um... about me... what are we going to do with this kid and ex girlfriend/wife/fiancee, whatever the hell she is?" worriedly asks the Trashcan Man. Scratching his beard, the handler thinks for a moment, then grins. "Fuck it... we'll no sell the whole goddamn thing." "What, like DC continuity?" asks Mr Smiley Face. "Yeah, except we'll be more obvious about it. Suzanne and little Eddie... " He snaps his fingers. "Poof, they're gone. That storyline sucked, I'm done with it. Alright guys, back inside my head where you belong, after this PPV, we're going back to basics. All four personalities cheer and fade out as the scene changes to a bedroom. Shelves of fantasy paperbacks and gaming books line the wall behind Mike as he types away at his keyboard. Taking off his headphones, he finishes up his RP with this... "I'll probably not win Dual Halo... and I doubt I'll have time to write something good enough to beat Mat at CS... but after this I am going to be coming back old school. You guys might love it, you might hate it... but I'm going to write like I used to... for my amusement and my amusment alone. Hope you enjoy the ride." Fade out.
