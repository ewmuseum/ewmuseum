It's Not Over Yet.

21 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 21/07/05 She felt that the water would wash away the hurt and the pain. From the moment she left the hospital the first thing she thought of was having a hot shower to cleanse her face from the smell of his whiskey fuelled breath. Still the nightmares continued and even now she was convinced she could still feel the glass on her body. Slowly she opened the door to her bathroom and turned to the left to look into the mirror. Claudia Lopez was a dark shadow of her old self, her hair was ragged and unwashed, her skin pale and lifeless like a corpse. As she watched herself in the mirror, she moved her left hand down to her stomach which was covered by the large white man’s shirt she was wearing to hide her form. Then it struck her again like a dagger to the heart, the reason for her lifelessness was the death of the life that had been growing within her. All that he had left of her was an empty shell of a woman. If she had had the energy she would have cried a thousand more tears but she had nothing left. All that she could do now was try desperately to remove him from her mind and body. She turned slowly around to look at the shower whose curtain was pulled back invitingly. Slowly, Claudia edged towards the shower and faced it as she began to unzip her jeans and pull them gingerly down. The bruising on her legs had gone from her white ghostly legs but in her mind it was still chillingly there as if it had only just happened. She knew that this nightmare would not leave her but she had to do all she could to try and fight against it. He had already taken her child and taken a part of her, she would do all she could to stop him taking the whole of her. As she discarded her clothes and stepped inside the shower, she wondered whether it was already too late. Claudia turned the shower and the hot water cascaded down onto her head in an aggressive initial burst, causing her to flinch as it reminded her of the whiskey flying into her eyes. The water drove it’s path down her neck and flowed its way down her chest and stomach, to her legs and feet. She leant her open hands on the tiles of the shower with her head bowed and eyes closed, allowing the water to wash over her. Claudia was desperately trying to relax but she was tempted to look beyond the shower curtain as she had become convinced that he was everywhere, watching her every movement. In one way, she wished he was, but she wished it was the “Serial Thrilla” Paul Cain, not this sick and twisted loner that she had faced in the bar that night. The problem with wishes is, that sometimes they come true. Fade out to fade in. Whilst the prey attempts to wash the invisible scars off her body, the predator recommences his hunt. He however is nowhere to be seen as he is hidden behind the lens of a camcorder which is recording every little detail. He is stood outside the door of her apartment filming it up and down before zooming in on the doors handle. Setting the camera down on the floor of the corridor, he walks over to the lock and begins trying a set of keys on it until he hears the satisfying creak of the door as it opens. He steps back and turns around as he crouches down to pick up the camera. His face appears in clear view with his hair this time combed and slicked back. He smiles to the camera as he collects it. Cain: So busy chasing lost causes that she forgot her lost keys. He then disappears from view again as the camera is put on his shoulder and he walks into her apartment. Panning around to the left, the door to her bedroom is ajar where a single bed can be seen with a bedside table next to it on the right. Cain [OSV]: It must be so cold at night when you are all alone. Moving towards the door, his arm can be seen coming into view as he opens the door further and enters the room. Cain makes his way to a chest of drawers to the left of the bed and he opens the top drawer revealing her underwear. He picks up a handful of thongs and bras then scatters them across the room. Cain [OSV]: Bitch never did a damn thing for me but fortunately I’ve got a good imagination though. The camera then tilts up to see a picture of both Paul and Claudia in happier times surrounded by a silver frame. Cain’s hand reaches out and takes a hold of it for a moment before dropping it onto the floor, cracking the frame down the middle. Cain [OSV]: Sentimentality is a waste of time as the past is dead to all of us. It’s such a shame that she, like all other women, is held back by her uncontrollable emotions. His feet can be heard trampling over the glass of the shattered picture as he walks out of the bedroom. He stops for a moment and listens to the shower and the water that is falling loudly from it. Behind the camera can be heard the deep breathing of a predatory Cain as he takes small steps towards the open bathroom door. Cain [OSV]: Poor Claudia, she does not need to wash away any sins because I already succeeded in cleansing her the moment I killed that creature that lived inside her. He enters the bathroom and turns to face the shower as he films her behind the curtain. The outline of her body can be clearly made out and she has her back to him as she washes her arms with a washing cloth. The sound of the running water is loud enough in her ears to have blocked out his approach. She thinks that maybe his hatred for her has driven him away from her yet she is about to find out that she still has one purpose to serve. His hand reaches out from behind the camera and grabs hold of the shower curtain, tearing it off the rail and throwing it down onto the floor. She spins around, covering her chest with her arms and crossing her legs together. Her scream pierces the room and her body is visibly shaking with terror. She hurriedly steps backwards towards the tiled wall, slipping slightly in the water on the shower floor as she tries to seek some kind of protection. Cain [OSV]: Hello darling, good to see you’re up and about again. The camera view turns to the right and then Cain takes the recorder off his shoulder and sets it down on the toilet, facing the shower. With a demonic smile, Paul winks at the camera and then glances at Claudia who is huddled up against the shower wall like a terrified child. Returning to his full height, Cain walks over to the shower and steps into it throwing his shirt off onto the floor. He leans on the tiled wall with both arms outstretched to block her escape and brushes back her soaked hair so that he can breathe into her ear. Cain: Do not think for a moment that I came here to apologise for the justice I exacted upon you. I did that creature a favour by terminating its existence before it could be born, for it did not deserve to feel the pain and suffering that is human life. It also did not deserve a lifetime of misery with you as its mother. So trust me Claudia when I tell you that I have no regrets and I have proved that I stand for everything right and just in this world. She cannot bear to look at him as he smiles proudly and mockingly at her. His hand then grabs at her throat and twists her head around forcibly so she cannot avoid his gaze. Cain: Do you see this? He points at the pentagram carving in his chest with his forefinger whilst not taking his eyes off her for a moment. Cain: This is your fault Claudia. You kept going on and on and on about this wonderful ‘ordinary’ life that we could have! You told me that it would be so magical, you, me and this [sarcastic tone] wonderful bundle of joy that just HAPPENED to come along as if by magic! Because of you, I was made to take my eye off the situation around me, you lowered my defences and that allowed them to take my life from me! YOU DID THIS! YOU DID IT! His emotions snap uncontrollably as he yells at her with no remorse or respite. She closes her eyes in an attempt to block him out but there is nothing she can do to ignore it. His vile breath is all over her again and she can feel the grip around her neck tightening like a boa constrictor. Her mind is in turmoil and all she can think about is why the man that she cherished has turned into such a disgusting person full of bitterness and poison. Cain: You are JUST as guilty as the rest of them Claudia! Sure you might not have been in the building that night but you had been in my ear, night after night, day after day with your pathetic romantic plans! You were just as responsible for killing the man I was with your hopes and dreams! They weren’t the things I wanted and needed but you couldn’t stop could you, you had to keep pushing and pushing until something happened! Well it did happen, you got my attention, you got me thinking and then it let THEM get ME! So believe me, what happened to you in the bar that night, you fucking well deserved bitch! You deserved it for attempting to change me and playing your own part in murdering the man that I was! Everything that’s coming back to you is exactly the punishment you should be receiving, just like THEY will at Colossus and beyond! Fuck your happy clappy bullshit Claudia, take your skipping through a field as one happy family and go fuck yourself with it because that shit just isn’t me and thanks to you, never will be! He turns his head away from her and closes his eyes, taking deep breaths to attempt to calm himself. After a few moments to regain his composure, Paul concentrates on her once again however this time with a quieter and calmer tone. Cain: I realised however that there is one thing left for you to do before I discard you into the same gutter that I found you in. In order to redeem yourself for the sins you have committed, you will stand before the watching eyes as an example. For I want you to show Peter Vetra that he and I share the same world together, that he cannot escape the truth. When he looks in the mirror, I want him to realise that if he looks deeply enough, he will find me, because we come from the same place. I want you to show them all, what happens when people push me. You owe me that much…DARLING. Claudia squirms under Paul’s vice like grip but he simply tightens it harder around her throat. Then, he pulls her forward before hurling her against the wall like a rag doll. Turning to the camera, he screams at it as the water crashes down on Claudia, mixing itself with tears she thought she no longer had. Cain: DOES THAT BRING BACK MEMORIES PETER, DOES IT?! DOES THAT MAKE YOU REMEMBER WHAT IT WAS LIKE STRIKING HER DOWN?! THIS IS OUR WORLD PETER! THIS IS OUR WORLD AND YOU CAN’T ESCAPE IT! MY PUNISHMENT WAS DEATH AT THE HANDS OF VAMPIR, YOURS IS YET TO COME MY FRIEND! COME WITH ME, COME WITH ME ON A JOURNEY! YOU TURNED YOUR BACK TO ME AND YOU COMMITTED A CRIME SOCIETY WON’T FORGIVE YOU FOR! YOU STRUCK A WOMAN! I WILL PUNISH YOU FOR YOUR CRIMES! I WILL…PUNISH YOU! His delusional and maniacal rant comes to an end as he turns to look down once more at the quivering wreck that is Claudia Lopez. He picks her up by the hair and drags her to her feet before throwing her out of the shower to the floor. Tightly gripping her by the hair he thrusts her face into the camera and squeezes his face in the shot too. Cain: Jackson Darilek and Tyler Lopez, you both understand what I can do. However for your benefit and that of John Edwards too, I want you all to take a good look into her eyes. By the time I am finished with her, she will never forget me and I will be forever etched in her mind until the day she dies. Nobody will be able to help her and nothing will stop the pain. You know something gentlemen, there was a point in time, deep down inside, deep deep down inside, where I used to feel something for this thing. Now remember this one important question…If I can commit this terror campaign on this creature, what would I do to people that I hate? There is an uneasy pause as Cain uses his forefinger and thumb to attempt to force either side of Claudia’s mouth into a smile. Cain: Smile for the camera darling, you’re being watched. He stands up to his full height again and drags her up to her feet yet again like he is dragging an animal around. Picking up his camera, he puts it to his own face and smiles. Cain: Sins are costly gentlemen and it’s time to pay. Then, without any warning, there is a click and the camera shuts off, leaving the terror campaign to go on, with Claudia truly all alone. Fade out to fade in one more time. The black Mitsubishi Warrior jeep waits outside the apartment block as Paul Cain flies out of the doors to the building, dragging Claudia Lopez with him. To show some kind of humanity, he at least allowed her to get dressed again and she wears the same white shirt and pants that she had worn on her return from hospital. She had worn nothing else as she could not think of anything other than the loss of her love and her child. Now all she could think about what where was he taking her and when was this going to end. He opened the passenger door with his captive forlornly following, her legs trailing underneath her with depressed lethargy. Grabbing her by the shoulders he bundled her into the seat and slammed the door. She screamed out as it crashed against her leg before she could get properly into the vehicle. Cain: Shut the fuck up and quit your bitching. You owe me one last journey darling and you have no choice, so sit there quietly and do as I tell you, then I might just let you live your life in peace with your punishment served. The smile on Cain’s face seems stuck to him now as he walks around the front of the car and to the drivers side. He leans against the open door with his back to it and his arm resting on the roof. Cain: I’m about to give you all something to think about. With that he slides into the jeep and the door crashes shut. In a puff of tyre smoke they roar away into the night and the horrors continue.
