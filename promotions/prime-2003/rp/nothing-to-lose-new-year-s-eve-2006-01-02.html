Nothing To Lose (New Year's Eve)

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-2-06 Sooner or later, we all learn that time has wrecked us. Let me be completely clear here. It has fucking wrecked us. You know, like those pictures you see of people getting hit in the face with hammers, and a tiny caption underneath that says 'owned.' For me, it would be the picture of me looking at a CAT scan in October. Tumor. OWNED. One year to live, with or without surgery.* * * * * Now it's New Year's Eve, and I'm watching this clock. It's blinking 11:53 in these little blue digital lights. Seven minutes until the last year of my life. 2006. I've thought about life and death before. I mean, I had that thing in years ago...with that paramilitary group, and the chip implantation... Wow. I haven't thought about that in a while. But yeah, the only real interesting things here are that clock and countdown, the girl in the other room, and this blue formica countertop. Oh, and the beer. Most of these things are self-explanatory. But let me give you my thoughts on the countertop for a minute. Can you imagine that with all a countertop's been through, that it might havea reasonable sense of wisdom to it? I mean, you always hear expressions like 'If these walls could talk...' but I'd be more interested in hearing what an apartment countertop has to say. With all the things they've seen, they could be like flat polished Buddhas. The only true tragedy is that they are born without mouths or telepathic projection faculties. Pity. So yeah, you know why the countertop (philosophy), the clock (I'm dying), and the beer (yum!) are interesting to a guy like me...but about the girl. She's young, blonde, got a great body, and gainfully employed. Terrific qualities that've made lesser men fall in love, but they're not what's doing it for me. ...well, I mean they ARE. But it's not it. Sarah's like absolutely no one else I've ever met. Let me clarify, Sarah's one of the most amazing women I've ever met. She's beautiful, yeah, but not so much where she centers herself around her looks. She's organized, and she curses like a sailor. Some guys look for a sense of humor, but I don't need a woman with that. I need a straight man, a Dickie Smothers. Except with a better rack. ...MUCH better. And I don't need a woman who cooks, I'll tell you that much. When you're raised in a one-parent, low-income house with another sister like me, these 'home-cooked' meals like everyone else seems to love don't really do it for you. 'Home-cooked' is EZ MAC or Shake and Bake. 'Home-cooked' is paper plates, paper napkins, and diet root beer, but only the ACME brand. Sarah doesn't cook, but she can tell me the difference between a '97 California Cabernet and a '04 Malborough Sauvignon Blanc, when we're out at dinner on the road. This is sexy, and I like it. But yeah, make no mistake...we only eat together because she's contractually obligated to as my personal assistant. 'Oh fuck, we're in Des Moines for a show and the only decent restaraunt that isn't fifty miles out is Merlin's on Ninth.' Some people might say that the reason I'm interested in Sarah is because she's not interested in me. I think she's got a thing for her boss/my manager, Kensuke. I think, wait...I know, that these people are stupid. I mean, you think I like putting myself in painful situations? Yeah, I like to take risks sometimes. It's the way I am in the ring, and it's the way I am with my life. I'm impulsive, but I'm not a masochist, I assure you. And if I could choose the person to fall in love with, it'd be a hot Asian groupie. Or a hot Brazilian groupie. Or anyone else, anyone but her. The beer's starting to get warm. Hell. Yeah, we've talked about the tumor thing. Right after I got the results back. I think she was upset about it, because she yelled at me, and I think it's her way of coping. That, and not really speaking to me since I've been off the road. I mean, it's not like I've had anything else to do than take care of my concussion and make the rounds with the family, so I actually managed to handle my own stuff for a while, while she kinda disappeared. Coping, I guess. Well, her form of coping sucks hard. You never know exactly how you feel about a person until their somewhere else. My first roommate at the wrestling academy was this kid named Tim. Tim was kind of a douchebag, and smelled like week-old cabbage. I think it had something to do with his diet. Anyways, the guy barely speaks word one to me while we're living in this shitty dorm above the gym, and one day he just disappears. And I tell you, when he left, it was weird. I never cared much about the guy, but when he was up and gone, well...it felt pretty damn lonely in that room. So I'd gone out on a limb and called Sarah, making small talk about going back to PRIME (which she thinks is stupid), and I snap at her. Yeah, I'm an asshole. I'm upset, and she does something I don't expect, and offered me an invite to an old sorority sister's New Year's Eve Party. And I'm here now because, well, in the intervening time between when I figured out I'm probably going to die in a year, and now, I've realized that I'm at least a little in love with her. Meanwhile, she ran into some kid she knew in high school, and I haven't seen her most of the night. He's got a gym body, wears shitty prep clothing and keeps his hand just above her ass. I hate him more than I've ever hated anyone else in my life. His name is Chad, and if I weren't such a nice guy, I'd rip out his trachea. ...maybe if I have a few more beers, I'll stop being so nice. All this violence. Oh yeah, I've got the first match back against JJ and Damien Cruz. Fuck those guys. No, seriously. I request a match against a real competitor, one of the guys I'd faced before, and they give me Jamester and Cruz? Fuck that. Come on, I decided to come back to PRIME so I can achieve something before I die. Grab ahold of one last shot at glory. So they're going to face me against JAMESTER AND CRUZ, two guys who, put together, have done NOTHING memorable in their stays in PRIME. I've watched while I was gone, and I've yet to see them stand on their own. Do I have a right to be pissed? Probably not. I fucked up Super Series, and lost the two real important matches in a row before Ferguson fucked me up. To think, I was worried about my arm back then...I knew I'd have to earn my way back. Fine. Let them put me up against Damien Cruz...I can outpace him, I know it. And Jamester, well...his best days are behind him. He's never looked like he did when he won the Global Title. I know I can beat them. And if I don't, then I just may be fucked. But, I have to try. And when you get down to it, I'm just missing an opportunity if I fuck this up. In the grand scheme of things, I've got nothing to lose. And that's why, in about thirty seconds, I'm going to walk into the other room, and kiss a woman who may not even like me. Because the worst case scenario is that it will break my heart, and I will die. And by the end of the year, I am almost assured that this will happen anyways. The beer is now warm, and tastes like shit. This is weak. No more messing around. The clock winks at me, and maybe the formica says something, but I'm not even listening. I'm walking. She's in the other room, next to some other people, standing by the television. She's too cool for one of the stupid party hats. I kinda love that about her. It's one of those things where I could say she looks like a goddess, but if you looked at her, you might think she's pretty, but not like I do. It's weird, and I can't explain it. But for a moment, the wind catches in my throat, as I make contact with her eyes. She smiles at me, but there's an arm moving behind her. God, she's standing with that former classmate of hers, and that dickhead looks like an Abercrombie ad. I don't know him, but I hate him. The countdown starts. I'm vaguely reminded of the Europe song 'The Final Countdown', which may in fact be the worst song I've ever heard. Between that and Chad, I've got the strangest feeling of desire and hatred in my gut. It's weird, it feels like I did when I had that match with Hoyt, right before the bell sounded. 'Ding' I say to myself, and start walking towards her. Around us, there're people saying six...five...four...three, but it's like it's just one shallow corridor connecting me to her. She doesn't move, and I don't stop, don't change a thing until I'm just a half-step away from her and That Asshole. Two... I let my elbow catch the douchebag in the sweater on his shoulder when I make my move, just to give him a little something else to remember me by. I mean, it's not like everyone here doesn't know I know my way around a fight, and after the shit he's put me through tonight, I honestly feel like I've earned it. One... She furrows her brow a little, trying to take measure of what I'm doing, and she parts her lips as a 'Wha-?' escapes. And really, that's all the moment I need. People are yelling something (I forget what, something about a thing and a year) as I press my lips against hers, and while I'll probably be slapped, probably have my heart broken, and probably (in the end) be buried in a pine box in 2007...I can't say I didn't try. Nothing to lose. * * * * * 'Willing / Waiting / Never knew this was inside of me / I'm dreaming / Facing / My future now whatever it may be / I'm on the brink and wide awake / An open mind, an unknown fate / I've still got time, so I won't fight destiny...' - Inspection 12 'Nothing To Lose'
