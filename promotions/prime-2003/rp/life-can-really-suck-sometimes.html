Life Can Really Suck Sometimes

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Listed... Monday, May 19, 2008 Standing behind the island table in his kitchen, Killean was mixing a few drinks for his guests. Vegas sat across from him on the right and across on the left sat his lawyer in a gray suit. James Vanderhoos had stuck by Killean through thick and thin behind the scenes on the drug and assault charges. However, James was going to have to stave off the public eye on a public scandal. “Ok so really, what choices do we have here?” “Well…” Vanderhoos was thinking, trying to come up with ideas on the fly. “You could just go out and admit it. You may lose some popularity but might be looked upon better in the end for coming clean.” “Clean about what exactly? It’d be a lie. I don’t need to come clean, I AM clean!” “Sure about that?” Both men look over to Vegas, sitting with his hands folded. He puts them up in a defensive position. “Look, all I’m saying Kill is that you’ve failed two of their tests. One is easy to debate but two straight? Two more detailed tests too. “ “I told you before. You think I’d lie to everyone? I’ve maintained that I’ve been clean all along… I just don’t know how to prove it anymore.” “Well that brings me to option two.” James started, winning the attention back from the men around him. “I happen to know a few guys involved in sports like MMA and stuff. They owe me a few favors. But it’s still gonna cost a bundle.” “What, you want me to go out a get my ass kicked to show I’m not juiced? What are you getting at?” “They do a lot of testing on their athletes. They go through a couple different companies to get it done, some of the most reputed in North America. We could get third party testing done, but it would cost a lot.” Vanderhoos finished and took a large gulp of his drink. Killean did the same and nodded his head. “Then that’s what I do. The money means nothing. Whatever it takes.” “It’s just gonna take a week or so to arrange it and then probably a couple weeks for full results. But I’m telling you now, if you test positive, your career will probably be finished.” James finished his drink and he looked right at Killean. “Oh yeah, like I’ve got that much left to lose.” The sarcastic tone coming from The Supreme Machine only served to summarize the feel in the room. He walked out from behind the Island table and towards his family room window. He looked into the partly cloudy sky, wondering just when the sun would shine down on him and his luck would go back to being good. |~o~| Dinner Time “Oh great, you too now!” Sirrajin leaned back in his chair, moving away from the table he shared with Jenny. She just leaned forward, looking her significant other in the eyes. “I believe whatever you tell me, I’ve no reason to think otherwise. But do you really think these other tests will come out any different?” She asked, sipping her water. Killean returned to a normal seated position, trying to put the words together in his head before verbally repeating them. “What reason do I have to believe they’d be positive? I am not lying to anyone, especially you!” Silence. Even at night, the thoughts rolling through Killean’s mind prevented him from enjoying this phenomenon. But, with the sudden halt of speaking between the two, for a split second he heard nothing at all. They both put a few forkfuls of food into their mouths and had another sip of their drink each before the verbal discussion continued under a different subject. “Kill, I meant to ask you this a little while ago, but I didn’t really wanna open that can of worms.” She paused, wondering if it was the right time then. She just didn’t want to wait any longer. “I was watching CineMax on the fifth of May. I know it may be none of my business but why was seeing Aimz such a big deal?” It was just what he didn’t need. He almost joked on his drink, which would have been a waste of good alcohol. He coughed for a moment, taking his napkin up to his mouth. In the lungs or in the stomach, he didn’t waste the booze even though he was choking because of it. He was a warrior. “His-AHEM-History between me and Amy is just that… history. I was just confronting her on what happened and why I was left in the dark. That’s all.” “Ok, I can accept that.” Jenny nodded, taking another bit of food into her mouth, immediately washing it down with her wine. She signalled the waitress for another refill. “Except for the fact that I found her number on your call display. Did you call her or did she call you?” “So you looked at my phone. Great… and what happened to the mutual trust we were supposed to have?” “Nothing, you just left the phone on the table beside me as you stepped out.” “And you had to look?” “Look, I know she didn’t call you, so just say it.” Killean paused after Jenny finished what she was saying. He looked at the table for a moment as if what he wanted to say was sitting on his plate right in front of him. “No one called anyone ok? I almost did, but I didn’t. I figured it would be up to her to grow the balls to admit to me what went wrong. I just wanted to close that chapter of my life since it’s been open for over two years now with no ending. Is that what you wanted to hear? Cause that’s all I got!” His straight forward demeanour won the attention of everyone else sitting in the restaurant. They all glanced over and Jenny was quick to try and hide her face. Killean slumped back in the chair and gave a look to the other people as if to say, “You’re fuckin’ food is on your plates, concentrate on it”. He didn’t want to raise his voice, but it was becoming common place recently for him. Confronted by Amy, Logic and Cantrell within a span of two days was almost too much to bear. Now he had this on his plate along with the information about the second drug test. He couldn’t have worse luck if he was a GCW employee. Oooooo snap! Anyways… “So if it was all in your past, why would you need to call her? What… are you friends now? Did she want you to come out for a beer or a booty call?” “Jenny I…” “You nothing Killean. Look how much digging it took for me to get this information from you. You always told me if I wanted to know anything, all I had to do was ask. So I’m asking you now, is there anything going on between you and Amy?” Sirrajin paused again which was a mistake that he just didn’t need to make at the time. “I see.” Jenny said bluntly, reaching for her purse. “Nothing is going on Jenny… at least not on my end.” Killean lied… and Jenny kind of knew it, but she was guessing a little. “Tell ya what, when you feel the need to be honest with me, I’ll be at home. Oh…” She got up and threw her coat over her arm. “You’ll be walking too.” She was quick enough to swipe the car keys off the table. She then turned on her heel, her reddish hair swinging around with her movements as she disappeared out of the restaurant. Killean meanwhile just sat there watching her leave like a goon. Well, at least to the people around him. He knew that it wouldn’t do any good to give any type of chase. Jenny usually liked to have some time to herself upon disagreements to basically let go of her hold on the matter and work things out. So Killean would give her that. As he finished up the rest of his meal, he knew he’d be walking home… he knew he’d be going home. What he didn’t know though is when. |~o~| A little while Later… The door flew open; bringing what was left of the sunlight into the back room. It quickly closed though, leaving only the light from the windows and the hydro illuminating the office. “Dude, to what do I owe the pleasure?” Vegas said, getting up from behind his desk. The music from the live band could be heard inside the club and the crowd was certainly not silent, showing that business was good. “Can you leave me to work out a little man?” “Sure, but weren’t you supposed to be out with Jenny toni… ahhh…” He stopped upon seeing the glance from Killean. The Supreme Machine didn’t need to say anything. “Ahhhhh shut up Vegas and go out into the club.” Vegas said, quickly making his way for the door and opening it, causing the ruckus of the live band to invade at a much louder volume. Once the door shut, Killean opened the gym bag he had sitting in the corner. He quickly changed into his shorts, minus a shirt and made his way over to the Bowflex. Sure it had one of the cheesiest infomercials on TV, but Killean thought it was a good substitute when he didn’t have access to his weights. That, or when Jenny was sitting in the loft, waiting for him to come home so they could find many different positions… erm ways to say they were sorry. As he sat down, he silently wondered for the fiftieth time since dinner what Amy was thinking. The night he showed up in SCCW, she ended up winning the Universal Title over there. In all the time he associated with her, she had never won a major title like that. Mind you, he had never seen her on the rag as much as she was now in Sin City. But even though they had a near meeting of the fists, he still saw something of a softness hiding deep in her towards him. She was married now and he was in a relationship AND had a daughter, but something seemed odd about their meeting. He was thinking purely about the fact that Amy left PRIME. That was ok, but when she left, she left him too without a single word. She never called at all. Not to mention, she didn’t answer her phone. He didn’t keep up with the charade for long though. It didn’t take him long to forget about it. But May 5th, she showed up outside the arena and it felt like it was late 2005 again. But he knew the past was the past and Amy had moved on. He moved on too but he just wanted to know what the deal was. It would kinda close a chapter, instead of leaving a cliff hanger. He wasn’t about to call her however. She looked like the stupid one in the whole situation so it was up to her to fix it. Just a couple minutes later, after a few sets, Killean just didn’t feel like working out anymore. He figured that Jenny had about half an hour already so it was about time to head home. Vegas came back in for a moment, the band taking a short break from the music and the line up at the bar becoming a little too long. “Sorry to bug ya man, just need my apron to help behind the bar.” “Don’t worry Veg. I’ve lost my interest to work out. I’m just gonna go see Jenny.” He slouched on the bench, putting his shirt back on. “Killer, it was about Amy wasn’t it?” Vegas asked, tying the apron around his waist. “How’d you know that?” “Look dude…” Vegas started, putting a hand on his shoulder. “Jenny asked me earlier today about it. I said that part in your life was done and finished. It was an entire lifetime ago for you. I said as little as I could but I knew she would be coming to you. I barely knew Amy at all man, but I knew enough to know that was just a fling. Jenny’s a keeper.” He patted Killean on the shoulder and then made his way back towards the club. “Thanks man.” Killean said, catching the attention of his friend who nodded before disappearing through the door once more. Killean changed back out of his shorts and put his small gym bag inside his desk. Pulling the cell phone from his pants pocket, he quickly dialled home. The crowd was screaming in the club now as the bell rang for the Happy Hour rush. One dollar shots and beers out the wazoo. Killean smiled at the success of his idea as Jenny picked up on the other end of the phone. “Baby, it’s me. I’m comin’ home. Let’s talk.” After saying his goodbyes, he ended the call, flashing a quick smile. He would quickly clean up this mess with Jenny and perhaps talk with Amy to get the answers he wants and nothing more. Following those hopeful events, it would be off to Chicago to initiate the first phase of his plan to salvage what he felt was left of his tarnished career. Open your mouth about something you don’t know the details on, you get hurt… unless your name is Jenny. Kinda leaves Logic in the crosshairs doesn’t it?
