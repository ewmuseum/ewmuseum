Premature

19 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5.19.2008 There are simple pleasures that come with success in the wrestling industry. Fame, fortune and power are three of the biggest and most apparent. Once an individual or individuals begin working for a big-time company they become more visible and gain fame. With that visibility comes the big contracts and endorsements that give them fortune. And then comes power. What more power can you have than after winning a major championship? You get to choose your opponents, show up when you want and act how you like. Right? Maybe not. Fade… “You have got to be kidding me.” Tyler blurted out. He stared towards the letter in his hands and read it to himself again. The usual shit-eating grin that occupied the lower half of his visage was absent at the moment. As he finished reading the document the second time it hadn’t gotten any more funny than the first time he read it. “What is it?” Aaron shouted from another room. “Just listen to this. It’s a letter from PRIME.” Tyler said. Dear Mr. Aaron Andrews & Mr. Tyler Chance, You are cordially invited to attend UltraViolence on Sunday, May 25th 2008. The event will be taking place in Chicago, Illinois at historic Wrigley Field. Your invitation includes the opportunity to defend the PRIME Tag Team Championships against TEAM V.I.A.G.R.A and Delta Upsilon Iota in a Tables, Ladders and Chairs Match. Attendance to this event is not mandatory but strongly recommended as failure to appear and defend the tag team titles will result in an immediate stripping and dismissal from the company. We look forward to seeing you on the 25th! C.P. Cantrell, Executive Producer “Are you serious? Is this their weird attempt at a joke?” Aaron said as he walked into the room. He snatched the letter from the clutches of his partner and looked it over himself. It looked official with the PRIME logo at the top and a penned signature at the bottom. Aaron gave it another look as Tyler turned around and put his hands on his head. “I know. I don’t know what to do. I’ve never been in the presence of strippers before.” He said. Aaron begin slowly smiling as the comment registered in his brain. He slowly turned to look at his partner who turned and locked eyes. Aaron finally broke into laughter directed towards his partner. “What the hell are you talking about?” Aaron was able to blurt out through his laughter. Tyler flashed a look as if to say “what do you think” before walking over and pointing to a certain portion of the letter. “Right there it says stripping. Idiot.” He said. Aaron shooed his hand away from the letter and folded it back up. He cleared his throat as he stopped his laughter and placed the document back into its envelope. “Tyler, sometimes I wonder how you graduated. And not from high school. From kindergarten.” Aaron said. Tyler gave him an annoyed look and moved away, taking a seat in a nearby chair. He looked back to his partner and came back with the best thing he could. It wasn’t very good. “Well then you interpret the letter moron.” Aaron sighed and walked to a seat across from Tyler before speaking. “Okay let me break it down for you. The higher ups in PRIME are unhappy that we haven’t been coming to shows. So in return they’ve booked us in a TLC match that we have to defend our titles in. And if we don’t show up to do it they’ll take the titles and fire us.” He explained. Tyler narrowed his eyes and looked to the ground. “But we’re the best there is when it comes to tag teams. We’ve defeated the best teams in ROH, PRIME and plenty of other places. Why would they want to fire us?” Tyler said. Aaron nodded in agreement to all that Tyler said. “They don’t like the way we conduct ourselves as champions. I think it’s in our best interest to go to this show and defend those titles. Are you ready to kick some ass? Are you ready to put those old men and those poor excuses for alcoholics to shame?” He said with forcefulness in his voice. Tyler nodded and watched as Aaron stood up. “Are you ready to prove to all the naysayers that complain that we’ve only defended the titles against two jobber Mexicans that we are the real deal?” Aaron yelled. Tyler stood up and yelled back. “I’m ready!” He shouted. “I can’t hear you.” Aaron replied. “I’m ready to beat the living hell out of all four of them! I’m ready to prove that we are the most dominant team to ever step foot into a PRIME ring! I’m ready to show why they named it UltraViolence!” Tyler shouted. The two begin exchanging pushes to hype each other up and begin to breathe heavily. After thirty seconds of this, Aaron stops and looks around. “Well, I think that psyche up may have been a little premature. The show isn’t for another six days. Crap. Well in six days when we are getting ready to step through the curtain I’m not doing this again. So think back to it and get psyched because I am not walking out of Wrigley Field without those belts. You got that?” Aaron says. Tyler nods and extends his hand. The two finish the talk up with a handshake and part ways.
