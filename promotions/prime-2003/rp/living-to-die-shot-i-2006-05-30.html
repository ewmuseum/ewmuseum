Living to Die: Shot I

30 May 2006

Date: 5-30-06 --- Death is not the endDeath can never be the end. — January 1, 1967Costa Alegre, Mexico Warm, emerald eyes gazed deeply into the inside of the wooden crib, watching as two precious angels engaged in a miasma of slumber. Two restful souls with no care in the world, other than living life to it’s fullest...the concept of it all made their appointed God Father, Vangelus Olsig, bitter in the fact that he could not partake of their trouble-free world, but as he peered into the gentle faces of them both, he couldn’t help but cast aside bitterness in exchange for love. He loved them as his own, even though he was merely their God Father. Their real father, Ignacio Chavez, was a dear close friend of Vangelus Olsig’s, so much to the point where he’d appointed Vangelus God Parent of both children. It was then that Vangelus grew a bond with the pair of youth that even the most influential of men could not break. “Their as precious as their father, eh?” Vangelus yanked himself around to peer into the eyes of Ignacio himself, crafting a broad smile on his face at the sight of his closest friend preparing for his first inaugural Ao’ Nuevo speech by buttoning his maroon designer’s shirt. Vangelus had traveled from Bogota, all the way to Costa Alegre, Mexico just to stand alongside Ignacio, and also to serve as a Honorable Detective under Mexico’s famed security department. What he didn’t expect upon doing so, though, was the experience of a lifetime. “I see you’re all prepared for the big event.” Vangelus stated in response to his close friend’s prior greeting. The smile on his face died down as he headed over to the jacket rack, snatching down his brown leather jacket, bracing himself to make his exit in preparation for the day’s celebration. “As ready as I’ll ever be. Today’s a big day in both my life and also in the existence of Costa Alegre...got to make it big.” Stated Chavez as he snagged his matching sports jacket off of the rack, slipping it on and adjusting it so that it fit him to the tee, thus pitting him in the image of a worthy Czar. “True enough. These people seem to adore you and your family, which only means you’re destined for great things. Just don’t forget to remember us little people on your way to the top.” Vangelus responded, obvious grin on his face as he eyeballed his ally. “Little People? Psshhh...if it wasn’t for people like you, this place would be hell on earth. With the way the sun beats upon this place, it nearly feels that way...” Ignacio cried out as he smiled, patting his friend on the back before continuing on in order to apply his suede shoes. “Yeah...being a detective gets difficult at times, especially in a foreign country such as Mexico. In the States, our criminals simply shoot and run. Here, though...you all craft up unique ways of producing violence. Our last case involved some idiot killing a banker, chopping him into thirty pieces, and placing each body part into different regions of the country.” Vangelus stated in an awkward manner, half believing his own words which he knew were true. “Yeah, well if you think solving the murders is hard...try ruling over them. There are days in my life in which I simply refuse to get out of bed...but then there are days such as this in which I know everything will work out perfectly.” Ignacio stated after applying his shoes. “And with two angels as children, creating a crime-free environment is what you should want more than all.” “Indeed. Now if you’ll excuse me, I have a certain important speech I must attend to. You understand, don’t you?” Ignacio stated, poking his elbow into the side of Vangelus in a joking manner. “Most definitely, Chief. Knock them out...” And with that, Chief Czar Ignacio Chavez made his way out of the five-star home and unto the celebration-filled streets of Costa Alegre to address his adoring public, leaving behind Detective Olsig...the top in a Security Corp Agency, lined up by some of the nation’s greatest detectives. His past work had placed him in such an elite position... But his greatest challenge was only yet to come. --- Death is the road.Life is the traveller.The Soul is the Guide — January 1, 1967Costa Alegre, Mexico Assembled among the gathered masses were citizens of various nationalities, all united under one cause...honoring the Mexican celebration of Ao’ Nuevo (New Years Day). Flags of the Green, Red, and Black influence flamboyantly danced throughout the humid atmosphere, producing a sense of celebration accompanied by triumphant waves of balloons and floating pinatas. Alcohol, no doubt, served as an impairment to most in attendance, resulting in mere chaos amongst specific regions of the gathered masses. Timid fires even served to erupt in small areas, causing a small yet noticeable uproar of civilians. All of this came to a halt, however, at the thunderous sound of Chief Carlos Gurreri, the head of National Security. All whom consisted of even the most minimum of knowledge knew that their was a line in front of Chief Gurreri that no man should ever cross. Chaos of all varieties happened to be amongst that line, thus causing it to cease as he approached the microphone. “Citizens of Costa Alegre, Mexico...I bring you greetings from the head of National Security.” Began Gurreri, drawing a small beckon of applause that ceased to exist as the wave of his hand. His deep toned voice cut through the scattered chatters as he spoke out once more. “Thank you. Today marks the celebration of Ao’ Nuevo...otherwise recognized as day one of the year 1967. A mere sixteen hours ago, we saw the conclusion of the year 1966...a year which saw many triumphs, as well as many tragedies. A year that saw the Tragic death of our beloved Chief Czar, Sir Walter Gonzalez...but yet also saw the rise of our new beloved Chief Czar, The Great Ignacio Chavez.” He stated with great enthusiasm evident in his tone...The mere mention of Chavez influencing a massive round of cheers amongst the gathered crowd. “Thank You. The year 1966 is behind us, however, and now we strive to make 1967 a memorial one. Here to ignite this memorial year is none other than the Chief Czar himself, and with him comes a special announcement that is sure to push Costa Alegre unto the right track heading into this tedious year. So without further due, I present to you all at this time...the GREAT IGNACIO CHAAAAAAVEZ!!!!” Deafening cheers flared in the form of chants, parading music, and a large assembly of floating balloons to invade the illuminated sky. The vicious Mexican sun diced through the massive Mexican Flag, constructing a radiant glow that displayed the significance of this event in the most broad of sense. All of this celebration multiplied itself numerously as The Great Ignacio Chavez approach the podium. As the applause grew, so did Chavez’s attempt to tame it with settling words and hand motions. Finally it decreased to a state where Chavez could clearly begin his speech. “Thank you all very much. Today we are here to celebrate Ao’ Nuevo, and what better way to celebrate such a memorial occasion than by introducing a document in which I and the Canadian Authoritive Government have officially passed. A document which will change the landscape of Costa Alegre, Mexico while also boasting Canada as a Power Nation. Get set, citizens of Costa Alegre...for now I introduce to you, the Treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo!” Rounds of cheers followed as one of the authoritive assistants stepped up unto the platform in order to transfer the document unto the hands of Ignacio Chavez. “This document not only extends the boundaries of Costa Alegre, but also grants equal protection under the Mexican Government for EVERY citizen of the country, which means a vast increase in income, school funding, and best of all...freedom for all!” The previous wave of cheers ensued once more at the announcement of freedom within the extended boundaries of Costa Alegre, Mexico. “Great Chavez! Great Chavez! Great Chavez! Great Chavez!” sounded the cheers as confetti and balloons flooded the stage upon which Ignacio Chavez stood, waving his arms highly for all the masses to see. The National Theme of Costa Alegre fell upon every soul in attendance as Chavez reached down, retrieving his two baby children who’d scrambled upon stage, holding each child in one hand, displaying them both as his own personal achievements to the world below. The sound of numerous flashbulbs snapping towards stage served as enough to make even the most ignorant of men aware of the significance in which this event, and the Chavez family held over the Mexican population. Joining Chavez and his two children on stage was Marissa Chavez, the wife of Ignacio and a woman whom most declared as the most beautiful in the land. And as the first family stood positioned on the stage, the gathered masses reached the conclusion of the Mexican National Anthem, expressing the words with deep pride... For you the olive garlands! For them a memory of glory! For you a laurel of victory! For them a tomb of honor! “BLAM!!” Gunshots ringing throughout the atmosphere was all it took to send a wave of panic and fear throughout the gathered crowd, ending any and all forms of celebration instantly. What drew screams of terror, however, was not the sounds of the gunshots...but instead the sight on Ignacio Chavez lying on stage in a pool of blood that flowed from the back of his head...with both of children to each side of him, lying in their own pool of blood... Dead. Replacing cheers of celebration were screams of terror. Officials and Police fleeted to the scene, half attempting to save any amount of life left in the beloved first family, half trying to capture the culprit whom had committed such a blasphemous act. Needless to say, they came up empty handed on both ends. All that was left was the duty of taming the rapid wave of rioting citizens whom sought to fleet the scene before they were left to suffer the same fate in which their beloved leader had suffered. News cameras zoomed in on the fleeting faces of the terrorized civilians, displaying expressions of fear, anger, and bewilderment, all balled up into one big ball of Panic. And as the widowed Marissa Chavez wept over her families slain bodies... Ao’ Nuevo ‘67 marked the beggining of the end... For a nation destined to fall. --- Our mind thinks of death.Our heart thinks of life — January 17, 1967Costa Alegre, Mexico It’s the biggest question in the last century...who did it? Reporters and Detectives are no closer today in revealing the man who killed the most beloved Chief Czar in the history of Mexico than they were seventeen days ago when the incident first occurred. Many have speculated by analyzing possible motives behind such terrorism, but nothing seems relevantly close. Whoever did it, however, just created quite possibly the largest uproar in the countries history since the death of President Benito Juárez. The nation mourns the loss of the First Family, and also for the widowed wife of The Great Ignacio Chavez...but even in mourning, the real sorrow comes from the fact that this could very well be the final blow in the existence of Costa Alegre, Mexico. The Television transformed into a completely black screen as Chief Carlos Gurreri stared blankly, watching as Prime Minister Santa Dolores paced back and forth before him. The two stood alone in the cold, empty Conference Room as the 17th day of January progressed into the 18th with the stroke of midnight. Prime Minister Dolores was in no form a detective under the Mexican Security Corp, but he was as smart a man as one would ever know, and a dear friend to Chief Gurreri. His love for The Great Chavez boasted itself greatly through his desperate attempts to help bring to justice the man behind such a heinous assassination, even in the wee hours of the night. For what seemed like the hundredth time tonight, Minister Dolores sat and flipped through the transcript of possible suspects once more, no closer to narrowing down the hundreds of possible suspects than he had been the first time he flipped through. The silence floating throughout the room was deafening. The only thing capable of being heard was the repeated sound of the gun shots ringing throughout the atmosphere, followed briefly by the screams of terror. Chief Gurreri took the initiative of flipping the television back on, this time firing up a tape of the shooting that was acquired through several news sources that had taped the event and caught the assassination. Each time, he played the shooting in slow motion, watching in anger as the silver bullet caught Ignacio in the back of the head before catching his two children as well. “Wait...play that again.” Demanded Minister Dolores, cutting through the silence as he pushed himself out of his seat and walked closer to the high-definition television. Without muttering a word, Chief Gurreri did as commanded, rewinding the painful occurrence and playing it once more in slow motion. “You see that?” Minister Dolores stated as he pointed directly towards the bullet on the screen which had been paused right before striking Ignacio. “That bullet came from the direction in which the Balcony sits.” Minister Dolores stated. “Yeah...and your point?” Gurreri asked as he pushed himself out of his seat in order to garner a closer look as well. “Who were the only two people sitting in the Balcony?” Minister Dolores stated, speaking up once more thus not granting Gurreri any time at all to answer the proposed question. “Elder Gonzalez...and Vangelus Olsig.” Gurreri froze as he heard the mention of Olsig’s name, tracing through his mind for any valid point of evidence which could possibly disprove the notion that Vangelus Olsig was the culprit behind it all. In addition to being a close friend of Chavez, Vangelus was an even closer friend to Gurreri. So much so, that it was Gurreri whom had recruited Vangelus into the Elite Security Corp in the first place. The last thing he wanted to see was Vangelus Olsig accused... “Think about it, Gurreri.” Began Minister Dolores as he circled around the frozen Chief Gurreri. “Elder Gonzalez is a eight-seven year old man in a wheelchair for crying out loud. He can barely lift a finger, better yet a .22 magnum! It pains me to say this, as I’m sure it pains you to hear this...but I think we’ve just revealed our culprit.” He stated, stopping directly behind Gurreri and glancing at the back of the timid Chief’s head. “It can’t be, Minister. Chavez was damn near a brother to Vangelus, and his children were Vangelus God Children. And If anyone knows Vangelus Olsig, I do...and I know that Vangelus is a far more intelligent man than that. Surely he wouldn’t commit such a fraud if he knew it would be THAT easy to figure out.” Gurreri stated as he turned around to face Minister Dolores face to face. “Who’s to say that he KNEW the camera angle would be so perfect as to where we could tell where the bullet came from? What you must realize, Gurreri, is that theirs a loophole in everything. No matter how much you cover something up, theres always a hole that can and will be exposed, thus allowing others to solve the issue. If there wasn’t, there would be no need for Detectives nor Lawyers. We just found our hole.” “I’m sorry, Minister Dolores...but I refuse to believe that Vangelus Olsig...my dear friend Vangelus Olsig is behind all of this...” “And Neither will I...” Both men jerked around towards the door, spotting none other than the devil himself standing their. Vangelus stood in the doorway with a look of utter rage built in his face as he stepped inside of the conference room, brushing past Chief Gurreri and positioning himself face to face with Prime Minister Dolores. “How dare you accuse me of killing my own brother, Dolores. That’s right...he was a brother to me, and for you to sit here and accuse me of his assassination disgusts me. You’re supposed to be a Prime Minister, yet you go against one of the most holy of demands in the bible...you judge someone. If it wasn’t for the oath I swore when I was accepted into this Elite Corp, I’d rip you apart right HERE!” “Calm Down, Vangelus” Gurreri stated, stepping in and restraining the bubbling Vangelus Olsig with both hands. “Now it’s late, and this is a very emotional situation. But remember why we are doing this, guys. No matter how emotional it becomes, though, we must focus on the culprit at home. I, quite frankly, believe you Vangelus...” Gurreri stated... “Yeah, but thanks to this hypocrite...you’ll probably be the only man in this city who does. I’m out of here...” And with that, Vangelus freed himself of Gurreri’s grasp, ferociously storming towards the door while being sure to grant Minister Dolores a middle finger on his way there. “You see that? The kid is a degenerate.” Minister Dolores stated once more. “Look, Dolores...we don’t need any more accusations tonight, okay? Tell you what, though...Tommorrow...bright and early, me and you both will be at St. Peter’s Cathedral. I’m not a very religious man, but I’m willing to do anything to avenge the death of my dear...no...my BEST friend, Ignacio Chavez. Tommorrow, we’re going to pray over his slain body.” Gurreri stated, grabbing his coat amongst doing so as he prepared himself to call it a night. “I am never one to turn down a willing prophet, Chief Gurreri...I shall see you there.” With that, They both made their exits. Tempers were flaring, and Emotions were high... But the worse was still yet to come. --- Our soul thinks of Immortality — January 18, 1967Costa Alegre, Mexico The Catholic Organ music sounded throughout the massive, yet empty Cathedral as both Gurreri and Minister Dolores stepped through the doorway, headed towards the illuminated alter in an attempt to pray over the slain body of their dear friend. “Ugh...before we go through with this, I need to take a tinkle. How do I find the bathroom in this place?” Gurreri questioned, expressing a look that suggested he’d been holding it for a while. “Down the hall to your right.” Dolores responded, and without a moment’s notice, Gurreri disappeared into the abyss, leaving Dolores to approach the alter alone. Upon reaching the Alter, he removed his symbolic hat and placed it to the side, taking the time to kneel upon the alter and fold his hands in a convincing manner. With great sympathy in his eyes, he glared towards the sky and began to cry out to his father... Thou who art in heaven. I come now seeking your presence as we desperately seek peace amongst the chaos in which the death of Czar Chavez has caused. His deep mediation made him totally oblivious to the slow opening of the door positioned in the choir stand of the cathedral. Tempers have Flared, Emotions have risen...things that are not of you have occurred. I come, first to ask for forgiveness of sins that I, or any of my fellow brethren, have endured. For all have sinned and fallen short of your glory, and therefore we ask forgiveness in your name. Through the opening created by the doorway, a man stepped through, slowly creeping down each step in the choir stand until he reached the leveled bottom. Now that we are forgiven, I ask that you bring this culprit into light. Show us the light in which we must follow, in order that we stay on your rightful path and not stray into that of our own. The click of the bullet inserting the gun sounded, but not audible enough for Dolores to hear it. This my father, I pray... The gun raised... Amen. “BLAM!!!!” Dolores crashed to the ground in a pool of his own blood as the rapid stomps of the culprit exiting through the door sounded. “What’s going on in-...OH MY GOD!!” Cried Gurreri as he sprinted down the long stretch until he reached the lifeless body of Minister Dolores, holding the bloody body in his arms. Through the silence that invaded the Cathedral, however, Gurreri continued to hear repeated voices... If it wasn’t for the oath I swore when I was accepted into this Elite Corp, I’d rip you apart right HERE! The threat in which Vangelus Olsig had stated. In a rushing manner, Gurreri dropped the lifeless body back unto the bloody carpet, reaching into his back pocket and retrieving an old modeled walkie-talkie. With it, he dialed his assistant in order to issue a demand. “Alex...this is Chief Gurreri. Get the officials here, NOW...I’m going to tell you something, and you have to promise me you wont panic. Minister Dolores has just been killed in the St. Peter’s Cathedral. Now don’t panic, and simply get me some officials over here pronto.” He shouted into the device as tears began to build up in his eyes. “Oh and one more thing...” He struggled to say it... “...Warrant the arrest of Mister Vangelus Olsig.” To Be Continued The Following has taken place in a fictional setting, dating over 46 years back.
