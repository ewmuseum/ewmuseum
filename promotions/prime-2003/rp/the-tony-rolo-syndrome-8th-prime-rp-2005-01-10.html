The Tony Rolo Syndrome. [8th PRIME RP]

10 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01-10-05 “Last time I face Tony he had just lost the Global Championship. It was in front of my hometown fans. We put out a great match, one that saw each one of us give the other one our best. But to this day, almost 2 months later, I still don’t understand what happened after the match. The man that I thought of as a friend attacked me for no reason. The same man that was grateful to me for saving his ass the night I debuted last May. And he’s yet to come to me, and apologize for it one bit. But I’m not mad at him. I just don’t understand why he did it. I don’t understand his motives behind it. Hell, he disappeared for almost 2 months right after too. Was he scared? Was he hiding from something? Maybe he’ll tell me one day, maybe he won’t. But I do know one thing……” We are shown the inside of an office. There is a high back leather chair in the middle of the room, with the back to us. As the camera pans around the chair, a leather couch comes into view. And laying on the couch, none other than PCW Hall of Famer Chris ‘Boda’ Sloboda. He lays there, looking up at the ceiling. A shot to the left with the camera, reveals the occupant of the high back leather chair…. A slender, middle aged man, dressed in a suit and tie. In his right hand, a clipboard, accessorized by a pen in the other hand. “With all that I’ve been through the last 3 months, from the kidnapping of my girls, to Vampir crucifying me…. The last thing I needed was Tony Rolo going super psycho and attacking me like he did. So…. Doctor… what do you think?” Chris rolls his eyes from the ceiling tile to look at the man in the leather chair across from him. Jotting down some notes on the clipboard, the doctor sits up, straightening his posture. He looks over the top of his glasses at the man laying on the couch. “Well, Chris….let’s pretend for a minute that there really is a Tony Rolo….and we can analyze that he is your inner self, wanting to get out…..” “What the hell are you talking about?” Chris stares at the doctor. “Tony Rolo is real. He’s a wrestler, like myself. And he attacked me for no reason two months ago, before disappearing for some time. I’m here, not to have myself analyzed, but to have you tell me why you think Tony attacked me. I want you to analyze that.” Chris relaxes his gaze, returning his eyes to the spot on the ceiling tile he’s stared at for hours now. The doctor writes some more notes on his clipboard. “Okay, well, if Tony Rolo is indeed real, then this Vampir you speak off, he most certainly represents some deep regret in your life, trying to resurface. And from the look of the scars on your chests, you’ve not taken this figment of your past to well, and have taken to self mutilation to make up for what ever you’ve done wrong in the past. “ Okay, that’s it. Chris sits up, and rips open his shirt. “LISTEN MAN….DO YOU HONESTLY THINK I DID THIS TO MYSELF.” The full effects of the scars now visible. The doctor cringes. “I’m telling you this once more… Tony Rolo is a real. He’s a superstar in our business. He’s generally a nice guy, who for some reason flipped out, and went slap happy on me. Vampir is a real being. He’s got fangs, and has a cult following. They are not figments of my imagination, making up for a bad childhood, or for my uncle molesting me, or my mom not breastfeeding me or any of that shit. I’m ONE HUNDRED PERCENT SANE. I came here, for you to tell me WHY ROLO ATTACKED ME LIKE THAT. I want to know what’s eating him inside. I don’t need any therapy for me. I’m fine.” Chris sits back down on the couch, a snarl piercing his lips. “So, your uncle molested you? Tell me about that.” Seething, Boda looks over at the doctor. “NO…. I WAS NOT MOLESTED. I WAS JUST TRYING TO MAKE A POINT. And that is Vampir crucified me, made my life a living hell. He’s as real as you and me. Tony Rolo bangs fat chicks. He loves stinky meat flaps, and he’s as real as you and me.” Boda rubs his hand through his blonde hair. The doctor takes down some more notes in his clipboard. He twirls the pen around his fingers a few times. Stopping briefly, he puts the pen in his mouth, gnawing on the end a bit. As he stares at the PRIME star laying on the couch. “Okay, okay…. So, you say these men are real. You think this Tony fellow has something wrong mentally, that caused him to attack you for no reason. Okay. Well, you claim Tony bangs fat chicks right?” “Well…. I never said he did. I was just stating what my buddy Emilio told the world last month. And well, Tony does come across as the type that would chase a fatty for a ride.” “Then, what if Tony was banging a fat chick, and she wanted to play carnival. And in the midst of sitting on Tony’s face, while he was guessing her weight, he passed out from lack of oxygen. Well, I can see that the damage from his brain not getting that precious oxygen would cause him to go ‘One Flew Over The Cuckoo’s Nest’ and attack you.” The doctor marks in his clipboard some more. “Interesting.” Chris smiles and nods. “I never thought about that. After all, without properoxygenation, the brain would not function properly. Hell, maybe he’s so addicted to fat chicks,that over time, he’s finally felt the effects of the brain being deprived of oxygen. Hell, that beats the hell out of my original theory.” “What was that?” Chris sits up, looking at the doctor. “I just figured something crawled up his ass, and attacked his brain. But shit…. This makes more sense. I think we’ll call it The Tony Rolo Syndrome. Love a fat chick…your brain goes crazy from oxygen deprivation. Dammit doctor… after I beat Rolo this week, I’m going to have to help him fight his habit. Got a phone book?” The doctor stands and walks to his desk. He takes a yellow pages directory and hands it to Chris. “Here you go.” “I’m going to start helping him right now. Let’s see if there is a Fat Chicks Anonymous listing under help groups. Man, I never knew Tony had a problem…. Poor guy.” And so we have it. After an almost pointless visit to a psychiatrist, Boda has figured out why Rolo uncharacteristically attacked him two months ago. Will our PCW Hall of Famer be able to help the PRIME Hall of Famer to overcome his addiction? What kind of effect will this discovery have on the Lumberjack match this week on ReVolution? Only time will tell…. And if any of you see Tony with a fat chick…. Stop him… it’s hazardous to his health.
