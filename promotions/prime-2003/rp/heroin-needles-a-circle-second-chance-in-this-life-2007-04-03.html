Heroin Needles A Circle (Second Chance In This Life)

3 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/07 I remember it always coming to me in a flash. It sticks though, lingering in the air...giving me the full scent as the situation plays itself out before me again. The world surrounding me is a blur of red. I literally jump out of my seat in a fit of rage while she rises on unsure footing. I lunge out, grabbing her by the shoulder, using my strength in a pinch. She trembles in my grasp, her eyes already shrink wrapped in tears. She's scared. Scared of what is going to happen and what I'm going to do. I do not need the background because it's entrenched into my head to the point of redundancy. What matters are the actions, because they will define the kind of man I am until my very last breath. She whimpers something out through ruby lips, but I don't hear it. I'm fixated. My drunken rage is growing. I always feel those stabbing glances from all around. It's like a scene from a movie. But there isn't no saviour here. Nobody is going to thwart the big diamond bull. All I can hear are the heavy pants of my own breath. All I can feel is the pounding rush of blood ripping through my insides as my blood pressure rises to uncontrollable levels. She uses both of her dainty little hands, clawing at the skin of my forearm to get me to break my grip. She pierces flesh, and all of a sudden I let go, wringing my left arm out as little cuts from manicured fingernails trickle with blood. She backs away slowly, her face looking genuinely apologetic. If only for an instant. I close the gap, and the eyes upon me burn. Burn my clothes off. Turn me into a primal animal without a stitch of humanity. She turns, trying to run. Running in stilettos was never the best idea in the world, and she'd actually had practice doing it. Hell, she always went to rip those damn things off whenever she was being chased around a ring. I don't think there was time for that, though. Melissa was always very aware of her surroundings and adapted to them. But I wasn't allowing her to, and her usual practiced bravado eroded into full out frantic panic. It takes less than a second, but I'm on her, turning her around with a hand. I don't remember exactly which because this is the part where I lose a lot of my awareness. She turns around. I throw a punch. Like I was going through a piece of wood, my fist tears into her jaw. Her legs go limp, her body swaying with the momentum of the blow. She falls downward, in slow motion. The Earth trembles when she hits the carpet of the fine five star restaurant we had, just moments before, been enjoying. And my world is enveloped in red. Red with anger. Red flames burning out my retinas in madness. The dark crimson trickling out of those ruby lips. There never is a second chance from this. *** Even back in those days, I was a whiskey first kind of guy. But I had acquired a taste for scotch sometime after I got back on my feet. Those Alcoholic's Anonymous meetings were a distant memory of a fallen man who did not even know where to start to pick up the pieces of a shattered life. Had it really been so long since Melissa ate that punch and took the ambulance ride out of my life? Or was it the other way around? Did I take the damn cop car to central booking on the way out of hers'? A lot had changed since then, I openly admit. But it acts in a cycle. And my cycle of redemption found me at the doors of PRIME once again, the site of my greatest career failure. I'd let politics and addiction uproot me from the pinnacle position of a promotion I had helped forge. I remember, all the time, I would sit there and rattle off the amount of time passed since I had been released. It was therapy for me, a chance to shoot my mouth off. But I'd overcome that...and now with news stations and industry insiders beating into the ground "sixteen months"...I was seriously just sick of hearing about it. That was the past. Something in the rear view mirror of my life. All I had was the today and tomorrow, looking at the neon brilliance of Los Angeles from a studio apartment overlooking the Staples Center. I no sold the past. And in my position, anybody would. *** I thumb through the menu, but my eyes aren't fixated on it. Nor is my attention towards it. I'm busy looking at my wife, embroiled in a conversation that seemingly refuses to stop. "Look...Brandon...maybe this is the new lease on life you've been looking for. You haven't been looking all that great...the drinking--" she pauses for a moment, sipping on some Hennessey from an ornate glass. I know what she's about to say, the current hypocrisy alluding her. "--it's gotten out of control. Maybe if you want to continue this...this wrestling...this wrestling thing...I think PTC should be out of sight and out of your mind." The PTC development was a new twist to the same old song and dance. To my knowledge, that bridge was not completely burned down to the point of crossing over. I try my best to keep my composure, looking towards her half cocked and ready to strike. "I didn't think PTC was outta the equation." Her neck strains as she gives me a stern look. "We're out in public Brandon. The least you could do is keep the Texan inside." It was funny. She was once again giving lectures in public about my conduct. I dressed in a suit and tie by my own damn accord. What the hell else did she want? As if reading my inner monologue of disdain, she snaps off yet another bullet of class directed right towards my brain. "You are more eloquent in the ring with a microphone in your hand than you are in the public domain. You always talked about being high class...maybe you should act like it." I savored the bullet like she savored the liquor that professional wrestling had paid for. I no sell it, smirking towards her. "I didn't think PTC was outta--" I catch myself, then correct. "--I did not think PTC was completely out of the equation. We still have yet to hear from the feelers at this point. Something could break." She sighs. When she sighs like that, she usually crosses her legs and gives me an ear full to chew on. "Brandon...how many times do you have to be told that they aren't interested? You're black balled Brandon...nobody wants to touch you because doing so is going to probably break some unspoken agreement between those organizations." "Yeah. I know...I'm still shocked that they ain't called yet. I mean...you think those Atlantic Wrestling guys are going to play hardball forever? I mean...shit...they're gonna make me take some deal where I'm making as much as a curtain jerker and then push my ass to the moon. You know how it goes." She stares at me like a retard. I've gone back to the dark side, back to speaking in comfort rather than with a broomstick up my ass. I've also become disillusioned with reality, looking at the positive rather than the overwhelming negative. "Brandon...they are not playing hard ball. They are playing no ball. None." And that stings my pride. *** Scotch has been a saving grace more than I care to admit, honestly. Whiskey goes down like water does now. I needed something new to swish in my mouth, to bring back a sort of frantic inner energy beyond simply taking shot after fucking shot. It got me through a lot of rough patches the last couple months. Lots of cold, lonely nights were made easier thanks to the bite of a little bit of alcohol. It's very funny how you can get to that point. Hell...PRIME 5-Star Champion...the present and future of that company. Women literally throwing themselves at my feet, wanting to see if my claims of superior stamina were true. Now? I was stuck by myself, forced to live in a world that was fixated completely on wrestling and none of the spoils for success. I mean...I succeeded where everyone said I would fail. But it was always about proving everyone wrong now, joyless victory after joyless victory. That was a mental block. Like Mariah Carey, I was rising through the charts like a phoenix now. Brandon Youngblood was once again turning into a must have property in professional wrestling. Bookings, money...it was all coming my way. I said fuck you to the bookings, instead making my money in the California independents and endorsement deals. I got a Xyience energy drink deal, and also am making some coin off of students from my BloodWorks dojo getting bookings. Wrestling's most hated individual was swinging some power once again, even at the most basic levels. Which is only half the reason I'm in San Diego still, after the Revolution stopped being televised. I had some guys working a local show for Cal All Pro, which was this little group that was part of a much bigger collective of California based promotions. The same promotions I made my mark upon years before. Now I was coming to them to oversee how my talent was getting run and if there was someone I could poach to with the promise of more dates. And oversee I did. I stopped working the insane slate of bookings months before, and simply focused my energy on getting myself into the best possible shape I could be. Sounded better than whoring myself out to establish a buzz and getting hooked back on pain pills, then coke for kicks. I didn't need to establish a buzz or get my name out there anymore. I was in. But PRIME wanted to know something. They'd extended the olive branch out to me, but thought I wasn't the only one deserving of a second chance. I did not know at first who they meant. And then they dropped my ex's name out there... Melissa went back to her old last name after we got the divorce taken care of. What stung her the most was the pre-nup that she promised to sign when we started our relationship. She said something like "I trust you not to hurt me...babe." She followed through her with her word, but I didn't.. She'd fallen back on her teaching degree, back teaching high school freshmen how to read properly just like she was when I met her, this time in San Diego. I knew that she was trying to get back into the game, to capitalize somehow on the lost chunk of her life and live better than she was right now. But she was just like me, forced to work her way through the shit like a bottom feeder who had fallen through the cracks. She was a referee, calling matches for Cal All Pro, playing the ditsy, biased official towards the heel. She is at the level most girls in this business never make it past...working in front of two hundred paid and getting groped by hormonally challenged twenty-two year olds who think they are the next big thing. She never HAD to do that before, mind you. She skipped paying her dues and went right to feature manager material because I could pull strings. She just wanted to get involved in something where she got to travel and enjoy the company of her lover boy. Except it turned twisted, and it became more about finding a way to cash in what I had made and live in a nice town, acting like philanthropists. Like those fuckers from that shitty book, The Great Gatsby. Lounging around all day, drinking and looking at expensive art and shit. That wasn't me. And now...that wasn't her, proving the point by getting down on all fours and counting the pin fall to some drink of water from Albuquerque. She got up, her referee shirt tied in a knot around her midsection to show off her toned stomach, and raised the hand of the kid before planting a kiss on his cheek. The fan's hooted and hollered, asking for their turn. And as she goes to the back for a break, I rise from my back row seat and walk towards the curtained off area, my pass firmly in hand. *** "No ball? Missy...I'm the damn 5-Star Champ. Nobody beat me for it. I could go on their damn television show and up the credibility they have just by throwin' it in the trash." She stares at me again, the broomstick dislodged out of my ass once again. Whoops. "Brandon...nobody wants you. You're done. Finished. AWC is more interested in Amy Campbell. She has more marketability than you...plus Harber wants to get more females under his umbrella. You're damaged goods to them." The words ooze out of her ruby lips before taking another sip of Hennessey. I can feel the anger begin to truly build up. "Well fuck 'em. You know...just fuck 'em. I'll make some other promotion, y'know!" People are starting to look at us, and I can see Melissa's face flush with embarrassment. "Brandon...please don't make a scene. Please." But I'm beyond that point. "No. I dunno what crawled up Global's ass. They say one thing then they do another like spineless fucks. I dunno what AWC is thinking. And UWF and those pricks won't even return my phone calls. Only company that has shown even a bit of interest is GCW and even then...someone is stopping that from going through. I'm the fuckin' MAN in PTC. A made man. And these pieces of shit think they can piss on ME? Well fuck 'em. Fuck 'em all." If she could crawl into her white dress, she would without hesitation. Her skin is bright red, almost as if the dimmed lights surrounding us burned her like tanning bed left on too long. "Brandon...wrestling isn't the answer. You should go back to school...take the money you saved and go back to school. Hell...work in stocks like you wanted when you were growing up." "I ain't doin' that. What I'm gonna do is simple. I'm going to keep plugging away. Someone will break." "Nobody is going to want a damn dead man Brandon!" She's getting stern now, matching my fire. All else in the world centers on us now, the spectators now simply witness to the two of us bickering back and forth. "Nobody is going to want a damn heroin addict--" My eyes shoot open and I interrupt. "What?" *** "So...I heard." She says to my back, not really sure of the footing her words have. "Heard you're back in PRIME. Even heard you were taking on some of those AWCers you'd wanted to test yourself against for a shot at the 5-Star Championship." She exhales. "I'm happy for you." I smirk, but she can't see it. "Thanks...I think." *** "Heroin addict? The fuck you get that from?" I go accusatory. I had gone to extremes to shelter her from the truth of my addiction. But heroin? HEROIN? No...no...never that far. Wait. Oh shit. Oh fucking shit. *** "Look...I never knew about the thing with Al. I never knew how bad he was. I didn't know what you tried to do." She had her head down, in shame. The dynamic was hilarious, what with the spouse in shame and the abuser just standing there.. "It's in the past. It's all in the past...finally." I turn towards her, and she is just there with her hands on her knees, not even able to look me eye to eye. "We were both out of control back then...weren't we? Like little kids, trying to figure out our way in a bad world." "Yeah." I say. "I guess so." *** "That needle. THE HEROIN NEEDLE! The one you took with you in your baggage to King of Kings! How do you think PRIME found out about your addiction Brandon? You'd been paying off doctors after binges. I gave it to Pham and he told me it was strike three for you. You think I'm stupid Brandon?! Do you?!" She's fuming towards me, and now I find myself on the defense. But I see through the words. I don't ask about the allegations. "You got me fired? You're the one who ruined my fuckin' life?" The words escape my lips, and the red starts to work wonders in my head. *** She talked to me about diet pills some time after the divorce, before contact really broke off when me and Eva VanLorne were getting serious. She, of course, was playing sympathetic the entire time, for coming down on me when she was just as dirty. I came clean to her after the divorce about the pain medication...about the cocaine. But the coke was early into the relationship, back when I was seeking cheap thrills with cheap hookers in cheap hotels. That and ring rats, for some kicks. But the heroin needle...that was something a lot more sinister. And even though I broke her jaw...even though I was the one who flew off the handle...it's why she comes at me all apologetic and mournful. "Look...PRIME couldn't contact you. You changed numbers?" She nods. "Yeah. Sorry about that." She begins untying the knot around her ribs, letting the unbuttoned flaps of her referee shirt fall down onto her stomach for cover. "I figured when they gave you the call that they would be thinking of getting the two of us together. Funny...GCW didn't even give me a damn call." I chuckle at that. "GCW didn't care about my past. They thought bringing you in was going to lead to distractions...especially if we were put together. Caldera thought it was too much a bad PR move." She smirks. "I don't even know if I would have wanted to be in that company anyway. I wish Harvard was still around...he was a caring man. Didn't want me out there looking the part of centerfold...wanted me to be comfortable in my own skin. It's a shame good men like him die the way they do." A shame good men like him die while bad men like me live. "Look...there's still a few weeks left in the semester. I kind of like teaching...it's soothing." I cock and eyebrow, looking over her attire. I might not be the most intelligent individual out there, but the fact that she was going this far to get back into the business told me a little something. "Which...I mean...that's why you're in that get up...why you've been doing this act for the past eight months." A sigh. "Gives me a chance to unwind. Getting back on the road...that's a huge life altering decision. And it's one I don't know if I can make right now with a clear conscious." *** Her words have purpose now, her frustration mounting. "Ruined your life Brandon? YOU WERE ALREADY DOING THAT! I'm not a damn saint...but HEROIN?!" "It's not mine gawd dammit..." I seethe. I was turned into a running joke over something that wasn't even MINE! Our meal ticket was gone and she didn't even have the BALLS to tell me when it happened?! "Who's the FUCK was it then Brandon? Did the tooth fairy give it to you? The fuck Brandon?! I'm not stupid!" "I'M NOT ABLE TO GET A JOB BECAUSE OF YOU! ALL THAT WORK?! GONE! FINISHED! Maybe I was doin' something good with my life for a change...HUH?! HUH?! Fuckin' Al was killin' himself with that toxic shit so I snapped him out of it. HE'S IN REHAB MISSY! FUCKING REHAB! I TOOK THAT GODDAMN NEEDLE OFF HIM TO SAVE HIS LIFE! And you fuckin' GET ME FIRED OVER IT!" "Calm down Brandon" The information clouds her judgment. She's lost her footing. "Al's in Japan. Al's IN JAPAN BRANDON! He's working a tour over there. NEKO CASE IS ON FUCKING BILLS OVER THERE!" "So you don't believe me now?! YOU FUCKIN' THINK I'M LYIN'?! FUCK YOU!" "You SELF RIGHTEOUS SON OF A BITCH! I'M GLAD! I'M GLAD! Now you can make something of yourself...you goddamn good for nothing drug addled PIECE OF SHIT!" And that's when I rise. And the world is enveloped in red. *** Alastair Nagatsuka...my running mate. He was in Los Angeles, not going over for his scheduled tour. He was too busy getting high. And that's how I found him, after a frantic phone call. I found him in his apartment, piss soaking his jeans. He was way out of control. He's clean now...found his way in life. I found him in a bad way in his apartment and took his heroin, intending to flush it down the toilet at my hotel. Melissa must have found it and turned it over. We lost our jobs because of it. We lost our lives because of it. We lost our marriage over it. It's too bad...because with a little communication...it could have all ended up so different. We never apologized to one another, but we knew. If we had it to do over again, we would change it all. But there isn't time to worry about the past. I let it die, and push forward to tomorrow. Work my life back up rather than punishing myself for the past. Focus on the chances bestowed upon me. It's weird. For a while, I truly hated her. And now? Now that my world was back in alignment, I see her in a different light. "Second chances Melissa. Don't get them often in this life." I look at her, trying to be as tender as I can be. "I know Brandon...I know."
