The Final Countdown (Vs. Deville)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Justin Williamson hadn’t even showered after costing himself and Angelo Deville the PRIME World Tag Team Championships. He had simply returned to his locker room, grabbed his bag, and left Madison Square Garden. With his gear in tow, he began the long walk to his car, wanting nothing more than to get the Hell out of dodge. His plan, however, was ruined by the woman that approached him, an incredulous look on her face and her hands on her hips. “What do you think you’re doing?!” screamed Eleanor Kannon-Hall, the manager of Angelo Deville. “Do you have any idea what you’ve just DONE?!” The former Deville’s Disciple looked at the wife of retired former PCW Champion Xavier Kannon, and responded calmly. “Yes, I do Eleanor. Why the fuck else do you think I’m getting out of here? I know exactly what I’ve just done,” he said, staring off into the distance. “I’ve fucked over a guy who’s used to fucking over others.” “And when he wakes up,” Eleanor finished, for him, “he’ll be mad. Beyond mad. As in, he’ll shoot you in the face mad.” “Again,’ he replied, “that’s why I’m getting out of here. This wasn’t a spur of the moment decision, Eleanor. I’ve thought this through. Come with me. I have a bunch of things that I need to do before next week.” Eleanor wanted to ask him questions, but Justin was already power-walking towards his car. Mrs. Kannon-Hall had no choice but to follow him to satiate her curiosity. “What’s next week,” she asked, already dreading the answer that she knew was to come. “I’ve got Angelo,” came the cool response. “And I’m going to beat him. If I do nothing else in this industry for the rest of my life…I’m going to defeat Angelo Deville, in the middle of the ring.” Eleanor shook her head at him in her usual way. “You might NOT do anything else for the rest of your life even if you succeed.” The look on her face was startlingly frank, not at all the usual playful expression Ellie~! was known to wear. Williamson, in his mind, was already going over his list of things to accomplish before his date with the Deville in the Air Canada Centre of Toronto. Thus, his response was short and to the point. “I know, Eleanor. I know. Now get in the fucking car.” She got in the fucking car. Again, she had no choice. No sooner had she fastened her seatbelt than Justin punched the gas and took off like a bat out of Hell. Eleanor silently thanked herself for having gotten her seatbelt on before Williamson’s mad dash. Now, at least, she had him boxed in, and could ask questions at her leisure. “Why did you do it,” was the next of her litany of questions, and really, it was a fair one. Williamson didn’t even bother thinking about it before answering. “Because, he was going to stab me in the fucking back anyway. That’s what he DOES, Eleanor. You weren’t safe either,” Justin spat, bitterly. “That fuck thrives on fucking with people. Making them feel comfortable and then taking advantage of them. Leaving me in the cell in Mexico was a warning. I was in there for THIRTY-SIX HOURS before the riot ended and I was let out. A DAY AND A HALF of my life was spent in a dirty prison cell. Because of him. So, fuck him getting to fuck me over. Let’s see how he enjoys it!” “He has CONNECTIONS, you know!” Eleanor screamed. “He can kill you whenever he wants! He can do it himself or hire someone else to, and make it look like an accident!” “So, I’m supposed to be afraid of him? That’s what he wants! I’ve been all over the world, Eleanor. I’ve seen things that Deville couldn’t handle, been in matches even HE’D throw up at. I don’t care if he can kill me…the fact is he can’t do it in the ring next week at ReVolution. There, he can’t use his connections or his weapons…he’s got to face me ONE ON ONE.” His devil may care attitude was matched in his driving, as he pushed the car to 80 miles an hour, whipping around street corners in the early New York City morning, driving like a man possessed. “Where are you even GOING,” she demanded of him, still unwilling to believe that all of this had happened. “I’m going to say my peace, Eleanor. To get my affairs in order.” “What does THAT mean?” Williamson’s voice and face were deadly serious as he answered. “I’m going to sew everything up. Take care of all of my loose ends. So, if something DOES happen, I go to the grave with an open conscience.” She was thunderstruck as she stared at him, trying to figure this out, trying to understand and failing miserably. “You can still get out of this,” she told him, thinking frantically. “Fly away! Leave the country!” “No, Eleanor,” he replied. “He’ll find me. No…I’m going to make my last stand in Canada, in the ring, man to man. I’ll give him my best shot, to do something few men have ever done: defeat the Crimson Angel. If he wants to kill me AFTER that, all he’ll be doing is admitting for the world to see AGAIN that I’m the better man.” Eleanor shivered as she realized that he had no other recourse. Rather than give into Fate, Williamson had resolved to fight until the bitter end, to scratch and claw against a force rumored to be many times more powerful than he was. “You’re noble,” she managed, meaning it. He, on the other hand, did not have such a high opinion of his deeds. “Not really. I got myself into this mess when I agreed to team with him. You could say all of this is my fault. I wanted power and success so much, I would do anything. I sold my soul to the Deville…and now I’m going to try and get it back.” Justin said no more, as he finally began to slow the car down, driving at a more legal speed. He turned through several neighborhoods, seemingly with a destination in mind, as Eleanor looked on in wonderment. It was insane, really. Contending with the Deville was a losing proposition. It always had been. It always would be. And yet Williamson was intent to fight, even if it didn’t change anything. In some ways it was inspiring, in other ways foolish. Angelo was so difficult to read. Often times, she had no idea what he was thinking. She knew that he was manipulative and dangerous, and knew of his various “connections”, but the true evil that he could perpetrate was known only by rumor. “What have I gotten myself into,” she wondered out loud, as Justin pulled the vehicle into the parking lot of a park. He stopped the car, and turned it off, before making his announcement to Eleanor. “We’re here,” he said, quietly, but that helped her very little. Where was here? Williamson exited his vehicle, and silently strode around the back to pop the trunk. Inside was a video camera, which he removed. Returning to his passenger’s side front door, he opened it for Eleanor, before handing her the camera. “Film me,” were his only two words, as he walked to a nearby bench and took a seat. It took Eleanor a few minutes to set it up, but she managed just fine, signaling to Williamson to begin. “Go,” she said, still wondering what the heck he had planned. Nothing, however, could have prepared her for his first words. “I, Justin Williamson, being of sound mind and body, do hereby dictate my last will and testament…” His WHAT?! Eleanor’s eyes went wide. Williamson was seemingly convinced of his own demise, of his lack of immortality. “…As such, due to a lack of living family, I do hereby bequeath all of my assets and property to Eleanor Kannon-Hall…” She couldn’t have heard that part right. Could she have? “You…why?” was all she managed, coherent thought seemingly behind her at this point. “Eleanor,” Justin began, somewhat sadly, “I have no family to give it to. You and Kannon have been trying to make ends meet since RPW. Take it. It’s not as much as I’d like for it to be, but it should help out…” “You’re not going to die,” she choked. “He couldn’t possibly kill you…” “So you’ll get the money when I die at the age of 40, then. Face it, Eleanor, with my former style, I’m not long for this world anyway. At least this way, my death can do some good. Besides, I’d love to see the look on Xavier’s face when he realizes I’M his benefactor.” They both chuckled nervously at that, before Williamson continued. “With that out of the way, I’d like to address my former tag team partner. Angelo Deville…you think you have everything figured out. You like to control men, use them as chess pieces. You’ve probably seen this coming for weeks, months. I don’t care, Angelo. Next week is the time when I do the one thing I have left. You see, I’ve fought in five continents. I’ve competed in every match you can think of. I have two goals left. One of them’s not happening. I will NEVER be a World Champion. But at least one of my goals is reachable. One of them is attainable…” He paused to smirk into the camera. “Making Angelo Deville SUBMIT. Angie, I don’t want a pinfall. I want to put you in Justinian’s Code, and wrench you, and torture you. I want you to yield to me, to give up, to surrender. To prove that I am the better man. After that…if I die…I go to the grave as the only man ever to die one up on Angelo Deville. Turn the camera off, Eleanor. I’m ready now.” And she did.
