Foreshadowing (RP for PPV)

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/05 Confidence. Not something the Trashcan Man has been overburdened with lately. Face it, he's been Hoyt Williams' bitch. The Crown of Thorns match was Trash's idea. He thought that the idea of a barbed wire crown was unorthodox enough to throw off the zealot, but he was more than up to the challenge. He beat Trash, and he beat him badly. Writing that quarter million dollar check and sending to RISP had hurt. Signing it as Michael Kriegman made it even worse. Luckily Hoyt hadn't demanded a public payment. That would've been humilating. Even more than the loss itself. More humiliation followed however. Trashy had brought out Bob and laid down some payback on the Pontiff of PRIME. And then who had appeared and one upped him easily? Token Fucking Weed. Of course the Trashcan Man couldn't have that. So he did what he always did. Lowered his head and charged. And somehow he ended up on his back, Token's pot smelling breath hot in his face as he took shot after shot from him. And then Token spat in his face. Humiliating indeed. PRIME hasn't been the triumphant comeback that the hardcore legend had envisioned. Sure he made an impact his first night, dropping off the Dual Halo cage onto Hoyt's car. Of course, he missed his target. The HIT dodged out of the way. And the week after, the HIT pinned him. AFTER Trash had jammed a trashcan on his head. Not quite the confidence booster. Trash could try to ease his pain with the thought that out of the four losses he's endured in PRIME, he was only involved in the decision for two of them. Scant balm for the gaping hole in his pride. So now he's in a Last Man Standing match with the two men who have one upped Trash at every turn. The marks out there have claimed that this match is his to win, that there's no way that he could lose this. The insiders however think this is just a stepping stone for Hoyt to chase after the Universal title. That this match is a moot point, Hoyt's got it in the bag. He's destined for great things, and Trashy's not on his A game anymore. That his time is probably over. And they might be right. Sure he puts on a face for the crowd and his coworkers. He smiles at the interviews. He grins at the cameras. He poses for pictures with the fans. But deep inside... He's scared. What if he doesn't have it anymore? What if he keeps losing match after match after match, fading into obscurity. What if he starts drinking again? Late at night, he sometimes wishes that Kriegman was still in control. Not for long, and he always hates himself for those quiet nocturnal thoughts, but they're there. If he was still working for his 'Master', he wouldn't have to worry about anything. Just doing what he was told. 'My life for you.' Four words... that can bring such relief. On his own, the Trashcan Man's life has lacked direction. He's got his metal sculptures, but that's just a hobby. It doesn't fill the void in him. Life under Alan Kriegman's control... it was filled with pain and suffering, but it was simpler. Easier. He's falling deeper and deeper into despair. It's getting harder and harder for him to crawl out. He needs something. He needs to silence that quiet voice that speaks to him late at night. He needs confidence in himself. He needs to win. He looks down at his hand, watching it slowly blister as he holds his palm over his battered silver Zippo. He's engaging in his ritual, using the fire that he loves so dearly to try to burn away the doubt and the fear. Fire's always purified him. It's his best friend, his lover, it holds him in it's warm embrace. He's been embracing it a lot lately. Evidence of this can be seen all over both his hands in the form of fresh burns. There's been a lot of doubt. A lot of fear. Trash, knock it off. I need to talk to you. 'Not now Smiley.' He mutters under his breath. 'I need this, I've got to get ready.' Excuse the pun, but we've got bigger fish to fry. This is about HIM. Get down here, I need to talk to you face to face. The hardcore legend sighs as he flicks shut his Zippo, reveling in the pain in his hand for a moment before easing himself onto the floor, sitting tailor fashioned. 'Is this absolutely necessary, Smiley?' Now, you know I enjoy the smell of our burning flesh as much as the next guy, Trash, but yeah, this is necessary. I think HE'S up to something. Trashy nods and closes his eyes, breathing deeply as he descends... ­­­­­­­­­­­­­­_______________________________________________________ The Mindscape. That's the name that the Trashcan Man had come up with to describe the mental construct he and Mr Smiley Face had accidently devised as they explored their relationship. After the chip in their head had been completely deactivated, the barriers between the personalities had started to weaken. Before, Trash and Smiley had very rarely spoken to each other, they couldn't hear each other's thoughts. The dominant personality would have to let the passive one partly take over so they could communicate. But not any more. As they talked more and more, they had discovered that instead of letting the passive one come up... the dominate one could submerge himself partially. It was good for face to face discussions, especially with the third one trying to break free. The Mindscape was never the same, always changing according to the whims of the submerged personality. This time it was one of Smiley's visions, an old amusement park. An empty roller coaster ran through it's track, up the hills, down the dips, around the curves. A carousel spun endlessly, the painted horses going up and down to the cheerful music. The abandoned midway was full of games of chance, filled with toys and stuffed animals, lights flashing to try to catch the eye. 'Throw a ball, knock over all the bottles, and win a prize!' Mr Smiley Face stands there, ball in his hand, his square yellow cardboard smiley face mask there as always, although he looks a bit different. Leaner, his shoulders not quite as broad as they are in real life. His black trench flows around him, the upside down smiley face button on his chest making a small clicking noise as it bounces against one of the metal buttons lining his coat. 'Whaddya say Trash? Care to test your skill?' 'Not right now Smiley' replies the Trashcan Man, who also looks different here. His form is more squat and brutish, his brow is a bit more pronounced, his nose a little bumpier, the scars on his forehead more extensive. Because in the Mindscape, the personalities show themselves as they think they are, and Trash has never thought of himself in the kindest of lights. 'What's going on? You said He's trying to escape.' The Smilin' Dude shrugs and cackles madly for a few moments before tossing the ball behind him, knocking over the pyramid of bottles on the shelf behind him. Smiley's never lacked for confidence. 'Things just don't feel right, Trash. Something's happening down here, and I'm not sure what it is.' Smiley turns, showing the bright yellow letters reading 'KICK YOURSELF' on the back of his trench. He points a black gloved hand off in the distance. 'He's been quiet lately, I think He's up to something.' Trash follows Smiley's finger, gazing out into the distance to an unnasuming squat stone tower, not more than 30 feet high. It has no windows and no doors that can be seen, and other than standing out in the amusment park, it shows no sign of the dark presence it contains, other than a feeling of unease that comes over the observers. 'I don't get it, Smiley. The prison looks the same as always.' He closes his eyes and concentrates. 'It feels the same as well, slightly weaker but it's still holding.' The mask and the smiley face button both wink. 'Looks are deceiving Trash. Feel around some more. It's not the tower itself, it's something all around us. Like's something's... I don't know... peeling?' Trashy frowns and continues to conentrate, casting his thought over the mental construct. 'Hmmm... you're right. It's as if... it's swelling? And flaking away... kind of familiar...' Smiley claps a hand on the hardcore legend's shoulder. 'Familiar? What do you mean?' Trashy's eyes suddenly open wide. 'Oh no... this... it can't be...' 'What is it, Trash? You forget to turn the stove off or something?' 'I remember where I've felt this before... it's no wonder you don't recognize it... You remember anything before you came to be?' Mr Smiley Face shakes his head. 'Can't say that I do... I just sort of, I dunno, woke up. Can't recall anything before that.' Trashy sinks down, squatting on his heels as he reaches down and touches the ground, shaking his head. 'Yeah, it's happening again.' 'What's happening Trash? You figured out your dick's for something other that pissing?' 'I last felt something like this before you showed up, Smiley.' 'You mean...' 'Yeah... it's gonna get more crowded. We're splintering again, there's another one of us forming.' 'Is this one on our side? Or is he one of His?' 'I have no idea, Smiley. I have no idea.' In the darkness of the tower, something smiles and steeples it's fingers together. 'Hmmm... this could be very interesting.' It chuckles throatily as we fade to black.
