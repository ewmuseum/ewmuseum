Date: Who cares Scars We all have them Some wear them like badges of honor Others cloak them in fear of castigation Regardless, they stigmatize the soul and always remain pertinent They yearn for attention Wishing to tell their story Behind Devin Shakur’s surface Lies a community of pain and remembrance The inhabitants contribute to his daily ride on the roller coaster of insanity He is locked in constant combat with them. Some days he wins and some days he loses. But he knows one thing He can’t live without them Otherwise he wouldn’t of gotten to where he is today… ================================================== December 24th, 2006 Location: Damon’s Bar and Grill, Raleigh, North Carolina Waiters and waitresses are usually a jolly group of individuals around Christmas time, at least the ones in this establishment are. The day has been long and arduous with a great amount of business, and all were looking forward to quitting time. Soon, they would either be out getting in last minute Christmas shopping, or going home to spend the holidays with their loved ones. One hour left in their shift and everybody is shitting themselves in fear. At the premium table in the establishment sit a threesome that garner such respect. They stand out from the norm, all wearing 1000 dollar suits that make them look straight out of a mafia movie. The only difference is that two of the three are women. One of the women has raven hair that many get lost in due to its illuminating radiance. She looks to be the kind of woman that has the ability to take charge of a room from the moment that her feet enter. Her cold green eyes can make any man or woman in her presence cower in horror. She doesn’t take shit from anybody, anywhere, anytime. Meet Shannon Austin The other woman at the table seems to be the complete antithesis of the aforementioned Shannon. She is the epitome of the gimmick ‘girl next door’. If you were to meet her, you would not be able to deduce that she has eleven known kills under her belt, and that she enjoys torturing her victims for hours upon hours. To her, the process of killing someone in this manner is just as gratifying as sex. Meet Valerie Swanson The man of the group seems to scream out douchebag with every step that he takes. The arrogance with which he walks disgusts everybody else around him. He knows that he is better than you, and doesn’t give a fuck about your opinion. He does not mind injecting himself into any conversation, because his words have made him rich. This is the guy that drives with the convertible top down all of the time when driving so women can look at his pretty face. Later in his bedroom, they get the same view before he shoves their head down to give him a blowjob. Meet Nathan Murray Together they are the Braintrust, Devin Shakur’s supposed worst nightmare, and owners of this restaurant. The last time that we saw these individuals together, they were giving Devin the ultimatum of die or kill Tony Gamble. Now they are meeting here to enjoy the food, but more importantly to discuss business. "Do you get the feeling," Valerie blurts out "That our operation is going too smoothly?" Nathan cocks his head to the side. "What do you mean, Val?" "It’s just…When have you ever known Devin Shakur, millionaire businessman, to ever listen to someone other than himself for this long?" Shannon interrupts, "What are you implying, that he does not value his life?" Valerie shakes her head. "You are missing the point. He’s had death threats against him from protest groups, he’s been blackmailed, and he’s been held at gunpoint several times. The man does not fear anybody. Why are we all of a sudden capable of shutting him down?" "Because we are fucking brilliant at what we do, and he knows that." Nathan chimes in, giving himself a pat on the back. "None of those people had access to his personal life like we do. It’s like we have the key to his heart, and we can open and close it at our own free will." "You were just as nervous as ever right after we met him." "Babe, time heals all worries. It’s not like the boy is going to go around behind our backs. I mean, who is he going to go call? We are the top organization in the state. Hell, we’re the best when it comes to efficiency in the whole fucking world. The pigs think they know about how we roll, but they don’t have any idea." Shannon folds her hands across the table. "He does make a good point though, Val. You do need to get out of this honeymoon of worry. I mean what if the boss were to walk in here now and hear you?" "Yeah, that is a big what if ain‘t it?" A voice from behind the triumvirate says, commanding their attention. His face looks like it made one plastic surgeon a boatload of money, the same goes for any company that provides black hair dye. The attempt to salvage the image of his youth is failing miserably. Soon, he will become a horrendous wreck of a man. Meet Anderson Wilkerson, the boss of the Braintrust. The boss sits down next to Valerie. "Why is it that this case has had two thirds of you in fear? None of you are supposed to be scared, especially not with this prick as the mercenary. There’s no way in hell that he could go rogue on us, and if he did then I wouldn’t hesitate to send someone out to kill him. Devin Shakur doesn’t want to fuck with me. He knows damn well what I‘m capable of." Shannon and Nathan nod in agreement, and after a second Valerie concedes to peer pressure to give her nod. They all knew that Anderson was a hell of an orator. Moving the conversation along, he gives a courtesy nod to his trio. "So now that we have this out of the way, I suppose it is time that I gave you three the next wave of instructions. Devin is going to be moving very shortly to get into affiliation with that wrestling group, and I want us to have points at all of their upcoming events. That means all of you are going to be doing some field work for this one. I want this one to be over with before April, and should that not be the case-" Nathan interrupts, "We know, we know" Nathan puts the side of his right pointer finger on his throat and slowly slides it across to the other side. "At least you are paying attention, that’s good. This place, PRIME, looks to be a hot commodity so I’m lucky that I went ahead and got the tickets now. The location for their next events are as followed:" Anderson says, before reaching into his pocket and presenting a folded up piece of paper. A pair of reading glasses emerge from his left pocket, and he starts to read off the names. "January 10th, Atlanta, Georgia, the Georgia Dome." "January 18th, Brazil, Marcena Stadium" "January 24th, Johannesburg, Africa, Ellis Park Stadium" "February 1st, Barcelona, Catalonia, Palau Sant Jordi" "February 8th, Calcutta, India, Yuba Bharati Krirangan Stadium Nathan can’t stand it any longer. The jet lag is going to be a pain in the ass, and he might only get to plow three women in each place. "Alright, we get the damn point. Give me the paper and we’ll look at." Anderson flicks the paper at his protégé, folds his hands across the table, and clears his throat. "Devin has his own private jet as we all know. There is no chance he will be flying commercial, or on the company‘s plane if they have one. I want you to be there before he is. I want you to push every contact that you have in each place. Make sure that you get his hotel room number, but more importantly get the room right next to it. I don’t care if you have to pull the current residents out of there, you will all be on Devin Shakur like white on motherfucking rice. Are we clear?" "Crystal" The three subordinates say simultaneously. "Acquire access to his phones as soon as you can. The first moment that he leaves the room, get in there and bug everything. Go through his bags, make sure that he doesn’t have any weapons or the like. One of you has to follow him wherever he goes. I don’t want him being able to have any peace of mind, because he certainly doesn’t deserve it. By chance if he meets with someone at any destination, take them out. Make it look like suicide, you guys are better in that department than I am. Anderson lets out a loud set of coughs. He knows that, at some point, smoking a pack a day is going to be the death of him. "Goddamn lungs. As I was saying, you need to make sure that you don’t leave any stone unturned. Above all else, make sure that you don’t leave any kind of trail. I know better than anybody else that Devin has a mind that can be more paranoid than a witness before testifying against the mob in court. If he sees any of you three once, I really don’t have a problem with it so long as you aren’t speaking. I’m sure that your voices are engrained into his hippocampus. He won’t forget them for as long as he lives." Anderson pauses to take a sip of water from the table. He wipes his mouth with the green napkin on the table before pointing a finger in the direction of Valerie. "So long as we have a firm grasp on the situation, and so long as we keep ourselves in a controlling aspect, Devin is not going to go above and beyond us to try and escape. He will want to do the deed so that he can get back to his life of success. We want this done so we can alleviate one of our last problems. I don’t want this compromised in any way whatsoever." "And if Devin sees us more than once" Nathan chimes in, having been quiet for way too long in his own humble opinion. "He would assume that we are playing from a desperation perspective. We have to stay back and give him the little tidbits like what we did with the information on Gambillini." Anderson nods, "Never let them see you sweat, or it’s over. Never. If Devin sees that, I will make sure to kill all three of you before the storm manifests itself. You get me?" They all nod in unison. Anderson grabs the glass from the table and raises it up to his lips, taking a huge chug of the water before slamming it back down. "The idealistic way that I would like to see this come out goes like this: Devin plays it cool, scouts Gambillini for a while, gets inside of his head, figures out where he is going to go, and then when he doesn’t expect it, knock him off. I want Anthony Gambillini dead at the Culture Shock Pay-Per-View. That’s the bottom line." Anderson takes one more slam of his water. "Now let’s get the fuck out of here. I need a cigarette." The four members of the Braintrust all stand up, slide their chairs in, and exit from the restaurant without sticking around to even order their food. One booth on the far right side seems to be paying particular attention to the foursome as they make their departure. The three men all look to be of Italian descent, and appear to have a certain panache about them that is characteristic of individuals who are in the mafia. Funny thing about that… They are More specifically, Joey DeAngelis’s mafia. They do not wait a second longer than they have to, all rising up to their feet with one throwing a couple of bills down on the table for a tip. Another one of the men has a cellular phone raised up to his right ear. "Boss? Yeah, Shakur wasn’t fooling. This Braintrust definitely exists." "Follow them for a couple more hours, just to make sure that they don’t do anything ridiculous." "You got it" The man puts the cellular phone back into his pocket, and the hired guns make their timely exit… Unfortunately for them, the entire scene is being watched by yet another party. A woman from the opaquely lit back of the restaurant comes walking out into the main dining section. She walks with a precision that suggests she is not there for the food, or to take in the eloquent paintings all over the wall. With an extra kick to her step, she makes her way over to the table in which the Braintrust were sitting at. Never breaking stride, her right hand reaches under the table and a second later reappears with a microscopic black listening device. The woman pockets the device and makes her way outside the restaurant, where she takes out an already open cellular phone from her other pocket. The woman puts the phone up to her mouth. "You got all of that didn’t you?" "Every last word. Good work Rach, we’ll call you again when we need you." The voice of Christian Daniels says. "Feels good to be back in the game doesn‘t it?" Rachel inquires Christian chuckles lightly, "Getting my people to bug their phones and their restaurant is just foreplay. The fun hasn’t even begun yet." ==================================================== December 26th, 2006 Location: Mama Gamble’s house Time: 11 PM Because Devin tipped off Joey DeAngelis about the Braintrust, Joey returned with an offer that allows for the chance to get up close and personal with Tony Gamble. This will give Devin the opportunity to see Gamble at the apex of vulnerability. If the mission is completed, Gamble’s mental psyche will be nothing short of obliterated. He will become even more paranoid than ever, and not be able to trust anyone. The thought of who could penetrate such a sacred place in his heart will be paramount. The only catch? Breaking into Mama Gamble’s house. Joey knew that Devin was the prime candidate for this break in, he had committed burglaries from the age of 15 through 18. Most kids that age do not have the ability to go through with such endeavors while living on the street. Devin had the skill and the motivation to thrive in the field because he knew that if he was not able to complete his mission, there would be no food or any kind of nutrition for the next couple of days. The thought of death was enough motivation to bring him back into the game. Devin spent the past couple of days surveying the house in which Gamble was spending the holidays at. He took febrile notes for the entire time: When people came into the house, when they left, the tendencies inside of the house, and when they went to sleep. Half a notebook was littered with idiosyncrasies. He decided that the best time to strike would be when both mother and son were asleep, which happens to be now. With an arrogance not seen in a while, Devin emerges from his hiding place in the Gambillini‘s backyard. This provided him great cover since the trees are very dense, and most of the neighborhood is out of town. With nobody to worry about watching him from either the right or left side of the home, Devin has a free pass to the back door. Devin makes his way up the stairs and to the sliding glass door. If his notes are correct, there is a good chance that this door will be open. Mama Gamble apparently has a bit more faith in the fact that robberies usually do not occur around this place anymore. Devin grasps the door with both hands and gently pushes backwards…Bingo. Upon entering the house, Devin scans the ground floor for any bedrooms. The only rooms that he finds are a homely kitchen, bathroom, and the living room straight ahead. Keeping his focus on the living room, a stairwell attracts our burglar’s attention. With verve in his step, Devin makes his way toward the stairs, swings around, and makes it to the top without making a peep. Standing at the top of the stairs a quagmire presents itself, there is one room to Devin’s right and one room to his left. If he picks the wrong one, Gamble could have time to react and that would be most unpleasant for the revamped burglar. Breaking code, our boy takes a minute to stand there and think about this. After mulling, Devin points over to the right and enters the room. Upon stepping inside, he can see that the right choice was made. Tony Gamble is off sleeping in la-la land. Devin walks on the front of his feet, trying to make sure to put as little pressure on the floor as possible. The wooden floors could involuntarily creek on cue if they so desired, and Devin would not be able to gain any satisfaction out of this mission. Ten paces into the room, Devin stops and looks down at Tony Gamble. He doesn’t suspect a thing. A wry smirk comes across Devin’s face. This was one of the many satisfactions he got as a kid, being able to invade someone else’s privacy without them able to do anything about it. Now, all he has to do is find something that would make this worth his while. He has already been on the scene for a good four minutes, and that is way too long without making legitimate progress. With silence like the wind, Devin tiptoes over to where Gamble’s dresser is and scans for valuables. A few seconds later, a quiet sigh is emitted when Devin doesn’t find anything of worth. Taking a huge risk, Devin shoots his head over to Gamble to see if he’s still dreaming about winning the Universal Championship before opening one of the dresser drawers. In the first drawer, Devin finds a bunch of useless items. Swearing to himself, he shuts the drawer, crouches down to the second drawer, and opens it. Nothing of note, just socks and extra clothing. Grimacing in annoyance, Devin shuts the drawer, and makes his way down to the final drawer of the three. Devin opens it up and is about to move elsewhere, but something catches his eye, a little brown box in the back left corner. He crouch walks over to a better position, grabs the box, and quietly opens it up. The one thing that he finds makes the head cock to the side. A lone photograph. Devin holds it up to the light of the window to get a better glimpse at what the photo contains. Once a full view is seen, Devin gives a sadistic smirk before rising up to a standing position. This would definitely suffice in terms of a strategic win. Reaching into his pocket, Devin pulls out a marker and begins to write something on the back of the 8 by 10. "Too easy," Devin whispers to himself, "Too easy." After a couple of seconds Devin is finished with his writing. Devin strolls over to where Gamble is sleeping and stares down at him. It would be so easy right now to take your life Anthony, so easy that it’s not worth it. You are that pathetic. If I have to, I will publically lynch you to spare my life from the Reaper. I still have a purpose on this planet Anthony, you sure as hell don’t. Devin places the photograph down on Gamble’s sternum. C’est la vie With the mission completed, Devin turns and exits from Tony’s bedroom, now picking up the pace to get out of there. A quick twelve steps down, swing around to the living area, through the kitchen, and out the sliding glass door. Just like that he’s gone. Almost as if on cue, Mama Gamble comes walking down the hallway toward Tony’s room. A light knock on the door brings Gamble out of REM, his body slowly awakening itself while rising up to a sitting position. "What’s wrong Mom?" Gamble asks, rubbing his eyes. "I thought I heard a noise coming from the room. Everything alright?" Mama Gamble inquires, while looking over her shoulder to make sure that nobody is waiting to ambush her. Gamble tries to calm his mother‘s nerves, "Maybe it was the dogs from next door. They have been acting up the past couple of days for whatever reason." Mama Gamble sighs, "Maybe you are right. You need anything while I’m up?" "I’m good right now, thanks Mom. "Good night, son." "Night Mom" Mama Gamble walks back down the hall into her bedroom. Gamble rests on his elbows for a couple more seconds, ensuring that Mama gets to her room alright. Once the door closes, Gamble is about to lay back down when something irks, a photograph is on his sternum. Gamble raises the photograph up. The photograph is a picture of a young Tony Gamble and his late father sitting on the front stoop of their first apartment, one of Gamble’s most prized possessions. On instinct, Gamble flips the picture over to find the inscription that Devin wrote. "Cherish what you love while you can still love. Soon you’ll be smiling for me…" Signed Death Shooting out of bed quickly, Gamble balls both of his fists up and prepares for a fight. This has to be another one of DeAngelis’s people. Why in the hell did he make himself so obvious? The only logical place that he would stay during the holidays would be here, how could he be so stupid? They had gotten the jump on him this time. Gamble looks down at the photograph one more time and gives a dejected sigh. "Goddammit" Gamble places the photograph on the nightstand and wipes the sweat from his brow with his left arm. A quick second later, the light in the room is on, and Gamble gives another reassuring sweep of the room with his eyes. He won’t be getting back to sleep this evening. A pair of eyes are out there somewhere, staring at him, watching his every move, and waiting for that one little mistake so that another statistic can be added to the record books. Gamble’s eyes are transfixed by the darkness outside of the window. Somebody was going to die before this was all said and done, and it damn sure wasn’t going to be him "This…is…war" ==================================================== I hate to be the one to break this to you Dusk… Judgment Day isn’t a choice… It’s an inevitability Yours is coming, but you want to fight it off. You think you can. The Intense Championship doesn’t mean anything to you. It’s just leather and gold to you. To me it’s a representation of my life It’s my redemption to all that doubted me It’s my injection of ecstasy. I beg of you Dusk: Kick me, punch me, maim me, massacre me, and give me my daily prescription of hell Go ahead and scar me for life. I’ve already got a name for the ones you place upon my being… The scars of freedom Why you ask? If victory screams my name, I get this… You…Just get a belt. Keep thinking you can knock me out for the three count…Keep thinking it… Because that’s as far as your dream will go.
