The Code (Part Three: Chuugi)

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-05-05 Bushido For the uninitiated, a code of ethics that prevailed among the warrior class in feudal Japan...the code of the samurai. This is the story of Kyle Lamen, the story of a man who unintentionally lives by this code. Not because he read it in a book, or saw it in a movie. But because inherently, it is his way. The Six-String Samurai. The seven virtues of Bushido: Jin (Benevolence)Rei (Respect)Chuugi (Loyalty)Gi (Rectitude)Yu (Courage)Meiyo (Honor)Makoto (Honesty) * * * * * THE CODE: CHUUGI 09.04.05. New Fucking Jersey. Kyle Lamen has a rather idiosyncratic style of wrestling. Lots of power grapplers or technicians play things through a very deliberate pace. For power guys, they're usually naturally slow.y The pound through high-impact moves. And technicians usually work at their own pace, preferring to think through maneuvers before they perform them. On a contrast, there's brawlers and high-flyers. Brawlers and high-flyers tend to go for short bursts of speed, and burn out quickly. Spot-spot-spot-rest...or blow-blow-blow-rest. [Aimz joke deleted.] What separates Kyle Lamen from the rest of the PRIME roster is his pacing of a match. He's a technical high-flyer, a style combining his size and natural proclivity for acrobatics with a solid mat and technical foundation laid by his mentor, “Jackhammer” Jason Core. But instead of the furious series of spots, with breaks between, or the smooth deliberate pace of the larger or more technically inclined wrestlers, Kyle goes at 110% speed... ...and he does it the entire match. Granted, oftentimes he doesn't get to show it off. Over the span of his PRIME career so far, he's been booked in exactly two one-on-one matchups. One of those was a four-person melee, and the other was against our Universal Champion, Hoyt Williams. Kyle was not a factor in the pinfall of one, and he won the other. So far, strategy is good. Of the PRIME roster, the person that comes the closest to Kyle's style is actually the #1 Contender, Brandon Youngblood. His aggression fuels him in much the same way Kyle's natural energy reserves fuel HIM. A match between the two would be a spectacle. But while Kyle's natural energy reserves work wonders for his style, it'd take a pretty intense training program to keep him going, right? And therein is our point. See, while you were getting all of that long exposition, Kyle Lamen was running. He had been for a while before that, and he will be after you're done catching all of this. Kyle runs, and when he runs, he RUNS. We're not talking about your grandmother, and how she jogs, kids. Kyle Lamen, to start his workout, runs HARD for an hour and ten minutes, framed by ten minutes of warmup, and twenty of cooldown. Right now, we see the shirtless, chiseled torso of Kyle Lamen, pushing himself to his limits on a treadmill inside the old Core Wrestling Academy gym in Red Bank. His blue hair no longer spiked, but limp with sweat, and the gel that kept it up seeping in mother-of-pearl lines around his hairline. Kyle doesn't notice much, he's got his iPod on. The iPod is a critical part of Kyle's workout, for two reasons. 1: Kyle, as has been documented before, gets bored easily. Don't call it ADD, because as long as he's doing something that interests him, he can be very focused. But if there's nothing to occupy his mind it wanders like a drunk hobo. So the constant stream of awesome music keeps him going. 2: Usually, for most of Kyle's run, he sings. ”Television...television...television...*huff*...television!” When you're busy like Kyle, you do your best to multitask...and this is the ultimate in multitasking. He gets singing practice, he works on his oxygen intake, and keeps himself occupied. Amazingly enough, despite all the bookings, the travel, and everything else that goes on in Kyle's life, wrestling, and non-wrestling related...he's done this every single day, without fail, for the last two years. Not bad, huh? Almost makes one forget about the last couple of STUNNING DISAPPOINTMENTS, like losing his first PPV match in PRIME, and not being able to get a Universal Title match to follow up on his pinfall over Hoyt Williams. He should probably hack it out with the company for a little while longer, right? Put in his dues, and the company will reward him, right? Every day, Kyle comes to compete. Every day, Kyle runs. And then he goes into the muscle-building part of his workout, and does it hard and fast. His body, in general, repays him. It doesn't fail when it needs him (with the exception of his maybe-psychologically-damaged right arm). PRIME will come through when Kyle needs it. He will be repayed. * * * * * 04.19.05. Japan. The members of 'American Ninja', one of the most successful up-and-coming bands in the greater Osaka area, sat around the kotatsu (small floor table) in Naota Mizuno's slummy fifth-floor apartment. The homeowner, Naota-san, was a man with both impeccable goatee and appalling fashion sense. As the bass guitar player of the band, he wore some sort of bathrobe and a cowboy hat onstage for each performance. While this is bad enough, he also wore these out in public. All the time. A stolen peek into his closet showed precious little else. And we're not talking about kimonos here. We're talking terry-cloth, with the loopy belt. Ikko sucked pensively on a cigarette. To his right, Keiji Kimura. Keiji is wearing a pink polo shirt with the collar popped open, and is a clean-shaven, tanned Japanese man. Keiji fiddles with the pop-top on the top of a can of Asahi beer, and stares absently into space. Continuing to the right, is a white guy with hair dyed platinum blonde. This is Nick Bradstreet, and he twirls his drumsticks absently in his hands, Slightly overweight, and not-particularly-good-looking, he sneers in the direction of the person to HIS right... ...Ikko Nara. Ikko also sports a goatee, but his is dyed the same color as his hair, streaked with a violent violet. He wears thick-rimmed emo glasses, but his entire body is tensed and throbbing beneath his Ramones t-shirt, as if he's barely able to keep from jumping up and expending energy in some matter. Finally, we come to his right, where Kyle Lamen sits, hair dyed an insane, powerful scarlet. Instead of his usual half-grin or full smile, he looks uneasy. He quickly throws back a big hit from a can of Asahi, finishing it off, before throwing it over his shoulder. He opens his mouth to speak “We made a commitment to this band together. I made a commitment to you guys. But now, I've got to go back to America.” The people around the table all kind of dumbly nodded, except Ikko, who looked a bit outraged. Kyle just continued. “There's so much left to finish there, y'know. I'm going to go back to wrestling. There're things about the sport that still haunt me, and things that still call to me. I've got to make another go at it, before I let it go forever. And I'm running out of money soon. And there's the issue of my family...” Ikko, the purple-streaked Japanese man, leaps to his feet and points an accusing finger at Kyle. “Oai! We made deal! Bond forever! You can not walk out!” Kyle shook his head, but both Nick and Keiji nodded slightly in agreement. Kyle's face was a mild frown, but he reached down below the table. The other members of the band looked about in apprehension, and then Kyle pulled forth a manila folder, and threw it on the table. As it hit the table, it flew open, and revealed five Northwest Airlines plane tickets. All four other members of the band looked at the tickets in sheer shock. “There we go, then. I've got arrangements set up in the states. We take the band to New Jersey, where we can actually get more gigs and stuff anyways. I've got the restaurant about to open up in an attempt to keep some of my money from growing legs and walking away, and I'll pay for your housing and travel at first, until you all start drawing paychecks. The band stays together.” Instead of looking dumbfounded at the tickets, they're now looking dumbfounded at Kyle. “This is the last of my spare petty cash. Either the band goes to Jersey, or I start working as a gaijin for a federation here, but either way, I'm keeping the band together. I made a promise, and I'll keep that promise. American Ninja's too good to let go of just yet. I mean, we're not GOOD-good...but we will be, right?” Around the table, we see the faces of the different band members. Naota, cool as always, just nods and takes another puff from his cigarette. Keiji smiles his crooked smirk, and touches his perfect flowing hair. Nick smiles with true affection. And Ikko, still standing, pumps his fist into the air, and exclaims. “Fuckin' right!” Kyle nods, feeling a little better already. “Fuckin' right.” * * * * * 09.05.05. New Fucking Jersey. In contrast to the prior day, Kyle Lamen in knocking on the door to a cruddy house, on the backstreets of River Plaza, New Jersey. A Japanese-American man answers, Naota Mizuno. Naota wears a bright purple bathrobe. “Yo, Kyle. Ready?” Kyle nods. “Yep. But I've got a flight later. We've gotta get going.” Kyle enters the house, which is a riot of music, beer, and hot girl posters. Naota blithely steps over a huge pile of pizza boxes and empty Ho-Ho wrappers. He barely looks over his shoulder to talk to Kyle, as they make their way through the foyer. “'Nother tag match? 'Gainst Civil D and Ferguson?” Kyle nods, and smiles. “Yep.” They make their way through the door to the basement, and head down. Naota speaks again. “Gonna target Masters?” Kyle nods, and smiles. “Nope.” This causes Naota to stop, briefly, and look at Kyle with his eyebrows raised in surprise. “Why not?” Kyle shrugs. “Targeting Ferguson. The other two are a unit. They'll watch each other. But chances are, they don't care about Danny...and I'm damn sure Danny doesn't care about them. I don't wanna lose again, you know? I can take Seth one-on-one, without “softening him up” this week. I'll just let the Forsaken do their job, and I'll do mine.” Naota nods. “You always were the smart one.” The walk a little further, and set up are the other three members of American Ninja. Kyle takes up his spare guitar, an Ibanez, and starts tuning it up as he slings it over his shoulder. “I'm smart because I'm always surrounded by good people, y'know?” Naota nods. “Fuckin' right.” Kyle and his partners get set, and the drummer, Nick clacks his sticks together in a quick beat. ONE-TWO-THREE-FOUR!
