Date: June 23, 2007 “So…how hard do you want it?” For those who missed introductions the last time, well…shame on you. His name is Tyler Rayne, and the confidently mischievous grin upon his face has been known to flutter more than a heart or two. This particular smile, however, is the first in nearly two weeks. Since the airing of ReVolution 130, which marked Rayne’s in-ring debut with PRIME. An…embarrassing endeavor to say the least. Now, dressed in a simple pair of black Nike windbreakers, Tyler Rayne stands in a ring for the first time since that disappointing evening. The deity of your choosing has blessed our hero with dashing good looks, a rugged handsomeness that belies the hardships of the life he has chosen. Hardships outlined by the various scars cut into the unbelievably chiseled form of his torso. If not for the marred memories of otherwise forgotten violences, some may claim that his physique could be considered a modern work of art. Though, with art these days, one may not find such a statement quite the compliment intended. “You know me, Ty. I like it rough.” Her smile is one of the few expressions naughty enough to nearly match his own patented grin. She is Katterina Wylde. The innuendo above is a fairly simplistic way of cataloguing the history of the female Wylde. Her dark hair, pulled into a rather tight-looking ponytail, is a stark contrast to the creamy white skin she has no problems revealing. The basic black sports bra she dons, containing an ample, if not perhaps envious bosom, is probably made up of more material than the teensy-weensy lycra shorts she has somehow managed to paint upon her fantastically crafted ass. In this raven-haired temptress, Tyler has found something of a kindred spirit, and for nearly seven years, the two have shared adventures lurid enough to give Michael Sloan pause. Through a dozen federations long past dead, former lovers and scorned enemies bent alike on revenge, ninja warriors, supposedly immortal vampires, government conspiracies, alternate demon dimensions and a thousand empty tequila bottles, Katterina Wylde has always stood firm as the single most reliable entity in the bizarre and torrid world of Tyler Rayne. In the most juvenile of terms? They’re BFF. Yeah, I hate me for actually saying that, too. “Your funeral, kid.” He shrugs, but the smile never leaves his face. This is going to be fun. Tyler’s arm stretches up, fingers plying at an invisible rope. Line from Carl Weathers. “Ding…Ding.” Tyler drops into something of a boxer’s stance, putting his speed to good use with a little fancy footwork and mock punching. Katterina rolls her eyes, taking the moment to stretch a few of the more utilized ligaments before getting this exhibition under way. The ring is a custom job; the canvas bounds with a little more spring than usual, the ropes twisted just a hair tighter than average. Minor adjustments that tend to favor Tyler Rayne’s particular style of wrestling. The two meet at mid-ring, circling over the large white skull splattered upon the otherwise black canvas. Tyler, still sporting that faux boxing pose, throws out a few quick jabs, more to test Katterina’s dexterity than to strike any real blows. The Dark Angel, as she’s known from time to time, doesn’t even bother putting up a defense. For the moment she’s contented with a simple bob-and-weave maneuver, occasionally leaning an inch or two more in either direction to dodge the half-hearted jabs. This continues for a minute or more, the smile slowly fading from Tyler’s face as it grows on Katterina’s. Then, once a pace has been set, Tyler lashes out with a sudden kick that takes Katterina by surprise. Her reflexes are quick enough to bat the kick away, but the attack has done an admirable job of throwing her off game, leaving Tyler the opportunity to cinch a side headlock. “Almost too easy, kid.” “Easy this, old man.” Katterina, displaying a strength her lithe frame certainly does not impose, lifts our hero into the air, dropping him down on his neck with a simple back suplex. She springs to the ropes, hopping over Tyler’s prone body for the opposite side. Tyler gets to his feet in time to leap frog over Katterina’s returning figure. She stops thereafter, patiently waiting for Tyler to hit the mat again. He lands deftly, almost immediately feeling the tug of her hands at his shoulders, and then much more unfortunately, feeling the point of her knees in the small of his back. He mumbles something about just where she can stick that apple, rolling to face up at the spinning leg drop descending upon him. Katterina flattens him with a quick standing senton, then proceeds to bound off the far ropes, rolling out of a somersault to smash him with something of a modified frog splash. With no referee available, Katterina proceeds to lay down her own count, which ends at two. Wasting no time, she rolls off of the cover, standing to a vertical and pulling Tyler up along with her. His hands spread upward, knocking away her grip on his hair, and retaliates with a few quick punches. Katterina blocks a fourth, a boot doubling Tyler over. A double-underhook butterfly suplex puts him close to the turnbuckle, and a split-legged moonsault puts him in another predicament. Another pin. Another kickout. This time Katterina’s a bit slower to get up. A bit winded after the offense. Tyler grabs her by the ankles and pulls. A simple but effective takedown. Katterina sits up quickly, but Tyler is already pushing himself off the second turnbuckle, catching his dear friend with a leg lariat. Of sorts. He’s up and back down with a flipping leg drop. Two legs in nearly as many seconds, and he’s back in the “match.” Tyler goes for a surfboard, more to catch his breath than anything else, but notices Katterina’s foot is wiggling beneath the rope and quickly releases the hold. He steps back to the middle of the ring, allowing his friend a moment to get up and catch her own breath. “Jesus, Ty, you really are out of ring shape.” He shrugs. “It’s startin’ to come back.” She nods, rubbing her throat. “I noticed. How long’s it been?” “Year and a half. Maybe two. I stopped counting. Just gets…depressing after awhile.” “I think that’s just called getting old.” “Keep talkin’, kid. Keep talkin’.” They meet with a collar-and-elbow tie-up. It lasts all of about one second before Tyler kicks Katterina in the shin. She takes a step back, well, more of a hop, the throbbing shin putting her off balance enough for Tyler to take advantage with a short clothesline. She’s right back up, but he’s ready and waiting. A knee to the gut followed by a snap suplex. If you couldn’t tell, that’s an emphasis on the snap. Tyler takes a run toward the nearest set of ropes, bounding off the middle for a springboard elbow drop. With Katterina sufficiently stunned in the middle of the ring, Tyler runs for the side ropes, springing off with his own version of the Lionsault. He hits squarely, knocking what little air may have been left from the vixen, before rolling to his feet and leaping backwards, landing deftly on the middle rope before slamming down again with a springboard Shooting Star Press. Tyler’s first cover ends at two and seven-eights. He drags Katterina to her feet and whips her into the far turnbuckle. Tyler charges in, Katterina preparing to raise the boot. Fortunately, our hero expected that, and Katterina is not nearly as prepared for the baseball slide that takes her out at the ankle. She lands in something of the splits, Tyler having slid out to the apron, now leaping over the top rope to jack her face to the mat with a vaulting leg drop. The force is so much that Katterina’s head snaps back up, where she is unfortunate enough to meet Tyler’s boots in the form of a front dropkick. He leaps over her now prone body, feet landing on adjacent ropes of the turnbuckle. A bit of spring from the middle rope sends him up, gravity bringing him back down knees first on Katterina’s chest. Another cover ends pointlessly, as her leg is once again outside the bounds of the ropes. A small hint of frustration creases his face, but Tyler once again backs off to the middle of the ring. “Rough enough for ya, yet?” The smile has returned. Katterina furrows her brow and pulls herself to her feet. She answers in the form of a charge. It’s obvious and blatant and far too easy to counter. A drop-toe hold once again has the beauty eating canvas, though the follow-up elbow drop finds nothing but empty mat. Both come to their feet at the same time, immediately launching a barrage of punches and kicks that are simultaneously blocked or ignored by the other. The furious assault continues until Katterina finally breaks Tyler’s defense with a forearm to the jaw. He staggers, taking a back elbow and then a spinning back elbow to either side of his head. A spinning heel kick puts him to the mat, and a series of boots to the ribs send him rolling out of the ring. Tyler turns in time to admire the beauty of the flying body press that drives both friends down to the rather unprotected gym floor. Katterina is up and in the ring first, Tyler following about two seconds behind. She uses this advantage to attempt a baseball slide, which Tyler bats away. He puts the toe of his boot into the back of her right knee, then follows up by slamming that same knee down across the edge of the apron. Katterina groans in pain, immediately pulling her legs back into the ring. She gets to her feet and limps a step or two before Tyler catches her in the back of the knees once again with a springboard dropkick. She falls damn near on top of him, right into the waiting arms that lock up a full-nelson. Tyler then bridges upward, sliding Katterina’s head and shoulders down to the mat for a Dragon-esque pin. She fights her way out of a presumed two count, getting to her feet in time to catch an attempted superkick. She pushes the foot aside, Tyler spinning with the momentum for a Dragon Whip. Of course, she saw that one coming too, and ducks underneath. Tyler gets to his feet and ducks a clothesline, then attempts one of his own that also whiffs. They turn to face, give each other a respective wink, then sprint for opposite ropes. Again a pair of clotheslines are ducked, again the two friends spring off respective ropes, and this time they meet center ring in a crash of cross-body blocks. Naturally, they get to their feet at the same time. Tyler catches her with a forearm, then a knee to the gut sets up for another suplex. Katterina puts a few punches to his abdomen, breaking up the hold and swinging around back to wrap him up for the German. Tyler lands, miraculously, on his feet, now positioning himself behind Katterina for another full-nelson lock. She slides out before he can lock it in, rolling back on her shoulders and putting her feet beneath his arms. Now a little push on his ankles rolls him nicely into a pinning predicament for all of two seconds. He powers out, quickly turning to put his shoulders into her legs, pushing her down onto her shoulders for a pin of his own. A one count, at best. She kicks out, puts him down with a quick leg sweep, immediately going for the pin. He pushes her off before a count even begins. Katterina scurries back, moving in for the side headlock. From there Tyler uses his weight advantage to roll her over, another pin that she just as easily rolls out of. Again taking advantage of his superior weight and strength, Tyler stands, lifting Katterina for a receipt back suplex that she very gracefully rolls out of. Two palms to his back send Tyler chest first into the ropes, and he bounds back to a forearm in the kidney. Tyler drops to one knee, attempting to catch his breath. The Shining Wizard to the back of his head does nothing to hasten the process. Katterina counts herself a very tight two and nine point nine-eight-three-seven seconds. Katterina begins to pull Tyler back to his feet, but a quick shoulder to the gut stops her and puts her in prime position for the Fireman’s Carry. She finds herself on his shoulders for only a second before dropping head first to the black mat in a modified piledriver. Count of two and some randomly assigned fraction that is very close to three. Up again and near exhaustion, Katterina’s final burst of offense puts a boot to his gut. She syncs him up for the double-underhook piledriver, a move that Tyler himself used for many, many years, planting him firmly to the mat for yet another close kick-out. Frustration evident, Katterina puts a boot or two to Tyler’s still stunned body before climbing up to the top turnbuckle. Her 450 Splash brings her spinning straight down atop his folded knees. Katterina, in great pain, rolls toward the center of the ring, clutching at her abdomen. Tyler, looking all kinds of disoriented, pulls himself to his feet with the ropes, struggling to take a seat on the top turnbuckle. Patiently he waits for Katterina to rise, her own disorientation making circles in the ring. When one of those circles faces her away from Tyler, he pounces, placing both knees firmly into the back of her head and driving his dear friend face first to the mat. He rolls out of the maneuver with a somersault, continuing his momentum straight out of the ring, not even bothering with the pin. A slow but steadily increasing applause brings his attention to the entrance of his personal gym, where a beautiful young woman has, apparently, been looking on. Tyler snatches a towel from the floor, wiping away sweat as he marches to meet the onlooker. The butler, a pleasantly dressed and balding man, greets his master with a short bow and slight English accent. “You have a visitor, sir.” Tyler narrows his eyes, looking from his servant to the guest, one Angelica Brooks. “Yes, Alfred. I can see that. Thank you, though.” “Of course, sir. Will there be anything else?” “Dinner, if at all possible. For me and my two guests.” “No. I can’t. I mean, I’m not really—” “Of course you can. You’re already here. Annoying as that is to me. Might as well have a bite before you go back to…whatever it is that you do.” Her mouth opens to continue protest, but the stern look set upon his face leaves no room for argument. With a resigned smile, she nods, putting her hands in the air as if in surrender. “Fine. Fine. I’ll eat.” “Shiny. Alfred.” “We will begin at once, sir.” With another slight bow, the thin little servant takes his leave. Tyler turns his attention to another doorway, marching toward the bright light and tiled floor beyond. Angelica, as expected, takes stride to keep pace. “That was, um…impressive. Maybe you can wrestle after all.” “Cute, kid. Keep that kind of sweet talk up and you’ll be in my pants in no time.” He doesn’t have to look back to see the smile on her face. “And who said I even wanted in your pants?” At the doorway, he pauses, Angelica’s keen eye noting the all too evident signs of a shower room. The smile on his face does nothing to assuage the sudden flutter in her stomach. Nor does the wink. “Yeah, but, ya never said ya didn’t, either.” With that, he actually hands her his pants, now proceeding into the shower with nothing but a thong to cover him. Generally she doesn’t care for men in thongs, but… “So, how the hell did you find out where I live?” She certainly didn’t expect him to be bellowing from the shower. Hell, she didn’t expect him to be taking a shower right here in front of her. Actually, to be quite honest, not a single moment of this impromptu visit has gone as expected. “Huh? Oh. Well, I…um, I kind of…I dunno. Maybe, sorta, possibly might have snuck into Blaine Blair’s office and snooped around ‘til I found your address.” “Bit stalkerish. Wasn’t there someone there you could’ve, I dunno, asked?” “I guess. I mean, I just kinda saw that he was out, and you know, it just seemed kind of…uh, journalist…y. Like in the movies.” “Riiight. So what is it you came to—” SMACK!! The rest of his question is lost in the resounding echo of a hand leaving its red imprint upon her left ass cheek. Angelica squeals, turning to grab at the now throbbing portion of her lower anatomy, while also taking a moment to look over the dark-haired young wonder smiling mischievously in her direction. As she’s entering, Tyler is taking his exit, a small towel wrapped around his nethers, and another, larger towel in hand to sop the water from his hair. Usually means the conversation’s over. Which means it’s time to take you on the tour of Rayne Manor.” He turns back to Angelica, a little lift of the eyebrows easing some of the tension. They don’t wanna do it.” Katterina stands, joining the two as they exit the private gym for the main wings of the mansion. “He has to pay the guy an extra hundred bucks a week just to keep the mustache.” And with that, we say fin.
