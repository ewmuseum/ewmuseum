Date: When The Fuck It Matter? To Whom It May Concern A Garbage Bag Production If you're reading this, and I'm not quite sure who would be, it means I ain't around anymore, and that I haven't been for possibly quite some time now. The truth is, I always knew that something like this would happen, and, of course, that's safe to say now that it happend. If it hadn't happened, this all wouldn't be here, and nobody would be able to prove anything one way or another what I thought, and then again, by some virtue or other, nobody ain't have nothing not to be able to prove anymore. I'm not very sure where I've gone and stumbled off to, but I imagine it can't be too far off from where I was. I just remember that there was this shit I had to do, and I did it all the time, and it drove me fucking crazy. I don't remember much what it was -- something about financial exchanges for following simple instructions -- but it just chiseled away at me like an ice sculpturist working his fucking magic or other. For a while, now, I knew I wouldn't be able to handle this shit much longer. It was bearing down on me, but I ain't one to tie a rope or take a dose I can't handle. I can handle any dose you throw at me, so it doesn't make much of a fuck either way. Present coherence aside, I've always known it'd only get worse, until even the most confident of scribes tried to decode my logic and threw in their towels. Every stinking one of the thinning lot of those bastards. I've just been losing it, thinking in the realms of metacosms that I'd be okay if I just processed combinations of everything known fast to make things unknown to combine and recombine into some compliant state, but I was ever too drunk to think fast, and ever too stoned to even think slow. I had hit the rocks, and some say I wasn't thinking at all, but there it was, like a blipping lantern towards shore from a wayward ship of lost souls, as lousy as that may sound, having been told by canonical fake pirates. Fuck them. Fuck them all. At least that's what I'd have said to 'em. So where was I? In the middle of some cyclone no doubt. Or was it a cyclops? Ah, shit. Maybe I'm not going crazy. Maybe I'm just going fucking drunk. Did I mention what this was about? I think I did in code. This loss of grips on reality? This aversion to accommodating elsewhere from where I deem comfortable? I tell you. There's not many fucking places anymore for a man to feel comfortable. At least I got this pile of clothes to sleep on, and this bottle of whiskey ain't exactly empty, but the other ones are, and I could beat an intruder unconscious with one of them or crack it on a steel edge and cut some motherfucker up. Shit, I used to mean something. I think. Now, if there's one poor sap, one sorry ass motherfucker, who can derive something out of this, I feel sorry for that fuck. There was something I could've said, and maybe it would've helped y'all out in some way or another, but I can't remember what it was, or if maybe I did say it. No idea how this shit began, or how this shit ends, but just that it's shit, you know? And I'm stuck deep in it, so there's shit all around, in every direction. Vile, putrid shit. It's getting late. Time to kill off another disappointment and forget about it. I'll scratch by tomorrow some. Today wasn't so bad. It was just average in a whole string of them that will be just as average as average sinks as average averages as average averages, settles on the bottom like some scum that don't neither float or sink but just settles in between. Some common typo corrected. Defeated after a well-scouted and hard-lost battle. Plenty of ways to make a plum look like a peach, huh? -Garbage Bag Johnny
