Apathetic.

Date: Who cares? :-) The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid couldn’t remember ever being quite this disappointed. Sure, Karina Wolfenden had apologized to him, and sure, he’d done okay in the Dual Halo, but he had gone into Culture Shock expecting better of himself. Now, he was back home, in an apartment in Salt Lake City, recovering for the coming week of action. As he graded geometry quizzes, he knew that he was in for little reprieve. For he would have to face two fellow lovers of hardcore. Two men who dared call themselves Legends… ------------ The HIT: You know, these quizzes really don’t get any better as the year goes on. He frowned, looking down at a quiz paper that already had copious amounts of red ink on it. Another incorrect use of the SAS Postulate, he surmised, marking a small ‘x’ over a portion of a proof. At times like this, he seemed anything but the hardcore warrior he was. If not for the afro and scars, he would be little different from any other teacher. The HIT: Ah well. Geometry’s not an easy subject. Neither is wrestling, for that matter. My kids at least manage to get 70s and 80s. Wrestling matches are, unfortunately, pass/fail. History tended to not remember much about the losers of the world, and yet the winners were glorified in terms beyond wildest comprehension. In his own type of match, he had lost to Karina Wolfenden, who had then managed to go on and win the Dual Halo after Trapezoid had thrown his best shot at her an hour or two earlier. The HIT: Of all the people in the world to win the damn thing, it HAD TO be Karina. Had to. Pretty much summed up my night in Japan. Karina Marie Wolfenden beat me in front of my fans. In my match. And then, she went on easy as pie and won the Dual Halo. Forgive me if I’m not jumping up and down with joy…but that’s over and done with. Trapezoid stood up from his chair, body showing all the signs of soreness and pain that one would expect after fighting Karina Wolfenden and entering the Dual Halo wrapped in barbed wire. He walked through the small apartment, leaving his living room and entering the kitchen. The HIT: Trashcan Man and Razor Blade this week…cripes, they don’t let up here, do they? Although, I probably should have seen it coming… Opening a refrigerator, the HIT pulls out a small box of apple juice. The childhood drink is one of his favorites, despite his alcohol indulgence before Culture Shock. Driving the straw through the top of the box, the math teacher sucks contently. The HIT: PRIME hires two new hardcore guys, oh what to do what to do…hey, let’s throw him in with the OTHER hardcore guy! Despite his best efforts to avoid it, the HIT rolled his eyes. The HIT: Maybe I’m being a bit cynical…or maybe it’s just that I’ve got more to offer to wrestling than being hardcore. Don’t get me wrong, I love hardcore wrestling. But at the same time, I’ve got more moves in my arsenal than most men will ever know. I can fly, I can use suplexes. I’m not just about barbed wire like they are… He sighed. Trashcan Man and Razor Blade were both successful grapplers, both competitors who had gotten far in their careers. Farther than he had, that was for sure… The HIT: Trashcan Man’s a guy I’ve heard a lot about for a long time. Primetime Championship Wrestling’s hardcore icon. Believe it or not, I’ve never been on the other side of the ring from him until the Dual Halo at Culture Shock. He’s insane. Of course, that was a description that could be used for The HIT as well quite easily… The HIT: Jumping off of a steel cage onto a man lying on a car is not a rational act. The Trashcan Man is not a rational human being. But I am. That’s the problem with all of this, really. I’m rational. I know what I do is stupid, but I keep doing it. Does that make me more rational than TCM, who is by all accounts an utter loon? I don’t know. I don’t know what the hell I am anymore… He ran a hand through his afro. Suddenly, he didn’t want to go to Revolution. He didn’t want to grade math quizzes. He didn’t want to do anything but sleep. And despite a certain degree of horror at that thought, he went to bed.
