Living To Die: Shot II

Date: 06.06.06 --- The sacrifice of love we give, Takes less and yet gives more; An everlasting hand of love, The heart an open door. — Costa Alegre, MexicoFebruary 2, 1967 “Two illustrious murders with each containing evidence pointing directly to you. Please tell me something, anything valid enough to kill this assumption.' Stated Gurreri as he paced across the small room with his hands behind his back and his eyes firmly affixed on Vangelus olsig. 'I am not a killer. That should be the only validation you need.' Stated Olsig as his long hair began to sprout, covering his face and portraying him in the image of a true convict. He held his hands folded in front of him as he continued to peer down at the concrete floor below. 'Look, Vangelus...we've been allies for as long as we've known each other. At one time, you were like a brother to me. I c-...' 'One time? What's that supposed to mean?' Vangelus questioned, lifting his head for the first time in order to send a sharp glance towards Gurreri. Gurreri stopped in mid-pace as he struggled for a quick response to the proposed question. 'Well...you know, we just don't communicate as much as we used to.' He stated, adding a crooked smile to accompany his flagrant lie. The only thing he drew in response from Vangelus Olsig, however, was a shake of the head in pure pity. 'Bullshit. You think I did it, Gurreri. You think I'm the mastermind behind both murders just because a couple of people pointed out some things that may point evidence my way. I thought we were brothers in this industry, and in being brothers, I also assumed that we could trust each other. I told you I didn't do it, but I see you're too full of shit to even trust my word anymore.' shouted Vangelus as steam began to build up in his eyes, forcing him to push himself out of his seat and get into the face of his allied opposer. 'You're taking this too far. I ne-...' 'Me taking this too far? I'm not trying to convict one my dearest friends of murder!' Screamed Olsig as Gurreri turned his back towards Olsig, simply taking in the accusations before exploding. 'I'M DOING MY DAMN JOB! You don't know how difficult it is to hold a position of my stature!' Gurreri screamed out, shaking profusely as he pointed his index finger into the face of Vangelus Olsig. The rage that began to build became evident through his violent shaking and transforming of shades into that of a reddish glow. 'I don't know how difficult it is? I was right there with you when we were forced to retreat to the Canadian border just to protect the life of the Canadian Prime Minister. I was right there by your side when Guy Collier III had a gun pointed to your head, ready to end your life at any given moment. I held your hand during the march for freedom from the corrupt Costa Alegre government, so don't you dare tell me that I have no clue what it's like, because I know all too well.' With little to no thought towards the action, Gurreri charged forward, spearing Vangelus Olsig to the ground below and wrestling with his former best friend before mounting him in a position as to where he held Vangelus gripped by his shirt, screaming directly into his face. 'GET OFF MY FUCKING BACK!' With that, Vangelus sent a punch hurling towards Gurreri, connecting with the soldier and forcing him off of Vangelus. Olsig responded by spearing Gurreri into the opposite wall, and pinning him against the wall with his hands gripped unto his shirt. 'Calm yourself, Carlos! I know you're upset, but you will NOT take your anger out on me, understood?' Shouted Vangelus as he maintained his grip on Gurreri’s shirt, still managing to pin the man against the wall. His eyes began to boil over as a moment of silence came to pass between the two. After assuming that Gurreri had calmed down, Vangelus loosed him, allowing him to slump to the ground below, burying his face in the palms of his hands. 'Listen...I'm just trying to do a good job as head of National Security. Is that so wrong?' stated Gurreri as his voice began to crack, possibly showing signs of emotional breakdown. 'No...not at all. But neglecting your duties as a friend for that position is.' Stated Vangelus Olsig as he snatched up his jacket off of the rack and headed towards the door. He gripped the knob, but before exiting, he turned once more to leave Gurreri with a few choice words. 'When this whole thing is over, and I'm proven innocent...I won't seek revenge. Your conscious eating you alive, and the confidence in knowing that you lost a Damn good friend will serve as revenge enough.' And with that, he turned his back in order to make his exit. 'I'll be in my cell.' --- The willingness to give of self; To lay down your own life; To touch another persons heart, In loving sacrifice. — Costa Alegre, MexicoFebruary 4, 1967 5 4 3 2 1 The conclusion of the countdown signaled the ignition of such an inaugural meeting of the minds. United States president, Lyndon Johnson, sat steadfast in his plush leather seat, directly across from Mexican President, Gustavo Diaz Ordaz, as the two gathered to discuss the transfer of Mexican lands that had become part of U.S. when the Rio Grande changed course during flooding. Separating the two was famed News Journalist, Richard L. Davis. For such an illustrious gathering of foreign politicians, it was only fitting for it to be broadcasted by the best in the land...and Richard Davis was undoubtedly that and more. His silk, custom made suit boasted the riches that his mastery of journalism had earned him, and his firm intelligence boasted the purpose of said riches. With a smile that influenced all of the ladies, Davis broke the mold. “Hello citizens across the globe...my name is Richard L. Davis, and I’ve been granted the privilege of hosting this illustrious gathering between Mexican president, Gustavo Diaz Ordaz, and U.S. president, Lyndon B. Johnson. It is a privilege to have both of you here, this evening.” Davis spoke, greeting each politician with a head nod that signaled his appreciation. “It is indeed a pleasure to be here.” Stated President Johnson, taking the liberty to greet the motion of appreciation with expressed words of his own. “As you all may know, we are gathered here this evening to publicly discuss the transfer of lands acquired by the US during the major flood that changed the course of the Rio Grande River. If there is no issue, I’d like to open the floor with discussion from President Ordaz.” Davis concluded, stretching his arms towards President Ordaz signaling for him to begin. “Well first, let me take the time to thank Mr. Lyndon Johnson for accompanying us here today in discussion of this landmark event. It is indeed an honor.” Ordaz began, taking a moment to motion appreciation towards president Johnson through a nod of approval. “Our proposal on this issue is to build a man-made canal around the Rio Grande, thus preventing it from changing courses ever again. However, at this time, we currently lack the proper fundings and spacing for such a construction. This is where the United States government comes in.” Ordaz proposed, shifting his eyes towards that of Lyndon Johnson, awaiting an answer. “I personally believe that a man made canal would be the ideal thing to prevent such an incident from occurring once more, but-...” “HEADS UP!” ”BLAM!” In an instant’s moment, a random stand-by leaped into the chair of President Lyndon Johnson, pushing him violently out of the chair and unto the ground. With little time to spare, the stand-by sought to dodge the bullet that had just been fired, but in doing so he still managed to catch it in the neck. A large gasp overtook the room as several officials rushed in to the aid of the man whom had just sacrificed his on life for that of President Johnson’s, who remained lying motionless on the hardwood floor, heart pounding from the instant event that had just taken place. Panic spread throughout the room like a virus as security in attendance rushed to the door from which the gunshot had been fired, seeking to capture the culprit of such an attack. Large, rapid stomps could be heard echoing throughout the hallway as the two security guards desperately chased after the culprit, taking the time to withdraw their pistols while running. Desperation made for a difficult shot, but the security guard fired anyhow, missing terribly as the culprit turned the corner. The black Ski-Mask covering his head kept the identity of the culprit hidden, even from behind. The gunshots continued to fire, however, as the culprit approached the exit door of the conference room with the guards hot on his tail. Without so much as a moment’s hesitation, He burst through the door and unto the back alley of said conference room. The swift closing of the door slamming in the faces of both guards bought the culprit just enough time to make a sudden turn, leaving both guards clueless as to his location after finally reaching the outside. “Where’d he go!?” shouted on the guards as he stopped and placed his hands on his knees in a desperate attempt to catch his breath. “I’m not sure! Lets check this way!” Shouted the second, much more lean officer as they both ran to the right side of the alley, leading back out unto the main road, leaving behind the culprit who stood smiling behind a dumpster on the left side of the alley, joyful that he’d been saved from capture...even if only slightly. The adrenaline of such a rundown forced the culprit to remove his ski-mask in order to gain a healthy dose of air, hoping that it would rejuvenate his drenchful and fatigued spirit. Slowly, he limped to corner of the alley, taking the time to slowly turn the corner with gun positioned tightly in hand...when all of the sudden... “OOMPH!” He hit the concrete grounding with impact as he bumped into a running kid who was paying little to no attention as to where he was going. The sudden and impactful collision forced the culprit to no only drop the gun...but his mask as well. And as the frightened kid lifted himself from off the ground... He stared directly into the face of the Serial Killer. And in knowing that his identity had been revealed, the killer dove for his mask, completely forgetting the gun he’d dropped as well. Shaken up, he positioned the mask back unto his face as he prepared himself to take off once more before turning back to pick up his gun... But upon doing so, he found it pointed directly towards him. With no words, the Kid pulled the trigger... But all he got was a click. The culprit smiled at the sight of the gun holding no bullets, thus preventing his immediate death. Without a moment’s thought, he charged forward, spearing the kid to the ground as he placed his hand directly in the kid’s face, preventing from moving and muffling his screams. With the alternate free hand, the culprit reached into his back pocket, retrieving his camera phone as he quickly hurried to take a picture of the kid. After completing the task, he slipped the phone back into his pocket and sent a punch hurling into the jaw of the frightened teen, who looked to be of the 17 year old persuasion. “Now you listen here, punk. I may not have the tools necessary to kill you right now, but I do have your picture. If you tell anyone of my identity...and I mean ANYONE...I’ll personally see to it that your entire family ends up in a grave. You hear me? Your entire fucking family!!” He shouted directly in the face of the teen. “Now Scram” came the demand as the kid quickly pushed himself to his feet and took off, leaving the culprit behind...desperately hoping that his identity would not be revealed. Today’s murderous adventure hadn’t gone so well. But there would be plenty more opportunities to make up for it. --- The chance that God has given you, To reach another soul; Forever changed by kindness, A life your love made whole. For life is but a circle, — Costa Alegre, MexicoFebruary 21, 1967 17 days since the incident in the meeting between President Lyndon Johnson and President Ordaz of Mexico that ended in the death of random stand-by, Roger Galley, citizens are still in uproar, demanding the identity and immediate conviction of this serial killer who is on the loose. This killer has been properly labeled “Killer Seventeen” because all of his murders have occurred 17 days apart from the last. Needless to say, tonight is the 17th day since his last murder, which leaves many to speculate who his next target may be. We here at Ozarks News will be sure to bring you the latest coverage if anything indeed goes down. The sharp sound of the alarm clock rang throughout the small bedroom as Alex Marconi, the only citizen in the world to know the identity of “Killer Seventeen”, awoke rapidly. Ever since the incident, he hadn’t been able to sleep, eat, nor leave the house...and the effects it had taken became evident from the stub that formed across his chin and the laziness in his eyes. Slowly, yet surely, he pushed himself out of the bed and limped over to the bathroom to begin his morning rituals of teeth brushing, face washing, shaving. His parent’s had taken a vacation, leaving him home alone to tend to himself. Little did they know that he would only be tending to his broken spirits that had come as a result of the incident that occurred 17 days ago. What bothered him the most, however, was the fact that today was the 17th day since the last murder, meaning that Killer seventeen was destined to strike again...and the only person even remotely capable of preventing it was Alex himself. But the cost for such a feat was far too great. He loved his family far too much to even entertain the thought of risking their lives for some stranger’s life whom he knew nothing of. He lived the perfect life with the perfect family who shared loved that was hard to come by in today’s day and age. No way was he giving that up...No way. Knock, Knock, Knock The knock on the door forced Alex to retreat from his morning rituals as he headed towards the door, opening it with little concern as to who may have been on the other side. “He-...who are you?” Alex stated as he peered into the eyes of a stranger whom he’d never witnessed before in his life. In contrast, the man was decked out in complete rags...looking as if he’d just walked out of a dumpster. His facial hair, clothes, and smell resembled that of your everyday bum. “My name is Jeffery...and I know your secret.” He stated with a smile that boasted his less than favorable, yellow teeth. Without invite, he stepped inside of the household and took the liberty of closing the door behind himself. “Okay...so we’ve established your name and the fact that you know whatever secret you’re referring to. Now tell me why are you in my house?” Alex stated as grabbed hold of the doorknob and gripped it tightly in fear of his own life. “I am here to gather information, Alex.” He stated, with his back turned to Alex and the bewildered look on Alex’s face. “Information if the form of...” “Tell me who Killer Seventeen is.” Jeffery stated as he yanked himself around and stared directly into the eyes of Alex...with a .22 magnum firmly gripped in his hand. Alex gasped as he spotted the gun, backing up as Jeffery stalked him with it. He continued to mobile himself backwards until he found himself tripping over the arm of his lazy boy chair and falling into the seat of the chair, leaving him practically trapped and at the mercy of the bum with a gun who stood in front of him. “H-how...did you know?” Alex struggled to say as his heart began to race, accompanying the sweat that rapidly raced down his face. “Who were you running from that night when you bumped into Killer Seventeen? If I remember it correctly, you were wondering in that alley when all of the sudden, you bumped into me. I scared you so badly, that you took off...leading to you bumping into the Killer. Little did you know that I followed you there and heard everything you two discussed...but I didn’t hear nor see the identity of Killer Seventeen. Now, you’re going to inform me of that identity.” He stated, raising the gun once more as he toyed with the gun, taunting Alex. “I c-can’t, He said he’d kill my family if I did.” Alex responded, holding his hands up in defense. “Well it looks like you have an ultimatum. You either tell me, or I’ll kill you.” Stated the bum, showing his lack of intelligence for anything more persuasive. “Why are you doing this to me, guy!?” Alex cried out as tears began to from inside of his young pupils. “Look at this fancy house you live. All of my life, I’ve lived on the streets, searching each night for a trashcan I could eat out of. This information alone would make me a self-made millionaire. I’m tired of living on the streets, and with this information...I won’t have to anymore. I’ve already informed the press, and even they know of your secret, but only I will reveal it, because you will tell me. Now do so...now.” He stated, taking the gun pointing the barrel into the chest of the teenager. And in a sudden act of desperation, Alex took hold of Jeffery’s arm, twisting it violently thus forcing him to drop the gun. With his right leg, he pushed Jeffery down to the ground before leaping over and snatching up the gun. He grabbed hold of the guns barrel, taking the time to smack the bum in the face with handle before mounting him and pistol whipping him with the gun until the bum was a bloody mess. Jeffery struggled for life ass blood drenched his face. He pushed himself to all fours, desperately, only to receive a huge kick to the face courtesy of Alex. With the gun in hand, Alex took a deep breath before reflecting back on things that had been stated... I’ve already informed the press, and even they know of your secret If you tell anyone...and I mean ANYONE...of my identity, I’ll kill your entire family. Even with Jeffery out of the equation, Alex still had an ultimatum to decide. The press knew of his secret, and would be hot on his tails to learn of it. But if he were to allow them to do so, his cherished family would be slaughtered like sheep. He knew that there was only one sure way of keeping such a secret and ensuring the life of his family... So he raised the barrel of the gun and pressed it towards his head... “I love my family.” BLAM!! --- Each life part of the chain; Each link is joined by sacrifice, That causes man to change. To turn and reach a hand of love, To touch and other's life; Will cause the circle to be whole, In loving sacrifice. — Costa Alegre, MexicoFebruary 22, 1967 “I’ve got to apologize once more, Van.” The velvet sky above casts it’s darkness above all those below as Vangelus Olsig sat in the passenger’s seat of Gurreri’s Oldsmobile with the head of National Security himself taking the liberty of driving the vehicle. “Like I said man...don’t worry about it.” stated Vangelus Olsig as he took another sip of his Country club’s soda. “I just don’t get it, though. The Seventeen Killer is supposed to strike every 17 days, and yesterday was the 17th day...yet no murder.” Gurreri stated as he continued along the roadway. “Actually he did commit a murder yesterday.” Stated Vangelus Olsig. “What are you talking about?” “Yesterday, Alex Marconi killed himself because of a threat that Killer Seventeen delivered unto him. In doing so, he took with him the identity of Killer Seventeen, and another 17 day death. Face it, Gurreri...this guy is smarter than we think.” Vangelus stated, taking another sip. “You know what...you’re right. And in 17 days, we’ll see yet another murder.” “Indeed we will...which leaves one question...” And as the vehicle disappeared into the darkness, that very question lingered through the night sky... “Who will the next victim be?” To Be Continued The following has taken place in a fictional setting.
