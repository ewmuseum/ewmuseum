Mid Life Crisis - 'Playing Foolish with a Bull'

14 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 14/6/07 13th of June, 2007 It had been well over two months since the entire project had began, but finally, Dave Price would be seeing the beginning of the fruition. It had taken him a long time to finalise the details, cross the t’s and dot the i’s, but finally, eventually, he had grabbed the one person that would help to galvanise the entire operation. Up until now, Dave had worried himself that the proceedings just wouldn’t go as well as they would but now, finally, they could rest easy. They had their guy to play off of Jack and bring them both to the top of the mountain again. It wasn’t that Dave hadn’t been confident of the entire plan from the start. His power and talent were never in question to himself, he was able to combat whatever situation arrived on his doorstep. No, it wasn’t his abilities that would be a hindrance to the exercise. Instead, it was opinions of the two other intangibles up to this point that were holding him back. Barney for one thing was a buffoon. A pathetic, old buffoon who didn’t have his mind in the right kind of decade, let alone year. To him it was still the era of the Hulk Hogans and the territory runs where they’d roll into town, do a couple of shows, drink the stills dry and then make away after pillaging the populace of all the entertainment value that it had. Barney was lost in this sense of grandeur that the business just didn’t have. He revelled in it whereas Dave just couldn’t bear the boy’s club kind of atmosphere that the progressively older and more chagrined characters of the industry entertained. They had their injokes and the pranks and tricks that they’d pull on each other just for the hell of it. They messed with society just because they thought they were above it. It ground on Dave’s mind that these men were just like the stupid high school jocks he had to run from and ignore as much as possible. Just because you were different from the crowd didn’t mean that you had to be persecuted because of it. And Jack was one of the worst that he’d encountered. Not so much because of his active participation but more because of his latent lack of caring towards the morality of the situations that often happened. It was like his moral compass just didn’t connect with the blatant tomfoolery that was going on. Sadly though, they were both integral to the plan. For now anyway. Barney would be the first to go however. His usefulness was running thin along the ground and after Overkill, his actual purpose would be sketchy at best. It wouldn’t be a problem to get him out of the equation so that he could take all the credit instead. It would be easy to frame Barney; he was so stupid that he’d fall right into whatever was put before him, Dave was sure of this. Still, it bothered him that he would have to add another part to the mix. It also really bothered him that the person he had to add was so volatile, especially when mixed with Murphy. They would not get along, no matter what Dave had in mind and that could be a problem. However, it was a necessary evil. Hopefully, the man would get his usual self-importance on track and would inflate his ego beyond the point where he thought he wouldn’t need Dave or Jack. That would be for the best and it meant Dave didn’t have to get him out of the equation himself. In the end, things were coming along just nicely, even if Dave said so himself. It wouldn’t be more than a couple of months and he would be out of this godforsaken place, with a cushy - and high paid - job in a television production company. He would be sure of it. *** 14th of June, Will Rogers World Airport, Oklahoma City How and sweaty would probably best describe the flight from the east coast over to Oklahoma. Hot and sweaty. Despite the plus of having some legroom by the emergency exit, (they trusted me to be capable enough to open the door) I was still stuck by a woman who was taking care of her child. Not only that, legroom doesn’t mean squat when you can’t actually fit in the chair properly. I guess this aircraft wasn’t counting on guys being over six foot. Still, I couldn’t complain for the most part, I was just glad to be on ground again. I didn’t particularly like flying in general so for the entire flight to go smoothly and just like it should, well that was certainly a good sign for the ensuing day. It was something about being cooped up that didn’t work well with my psyche. It didn’t matter what environment it was, (so maybe it was just claustrophobia) but being on a plane really didn’t help it. Knowing that not only was I closed in, the only escape from such a place was in the air until it landed. Doesn’t exactly help somebody who’s verging on a phobia. But…it was done and gone. I didn’t have to worry about it till the next time. I picked up my luggage pretty quickly and I slid into the general hubbub of people. Thankfully the majority of the people who use airports don’t have the slightest idea who I am and therefore being stopped was highly unlikely. Also, most of the rest of the PRIME roster weren’t expected till tomorrow or Saturday at the latest so the rabid fans wouldn’t be pitching till sometime later today. I was safe from being bombarded. As I began to queue up for customs, I felt the brush of a slight, yet arrogantly pushing man sneak in front of me. ‘Hey, what are…’ I stopped though because the man turned around and flashed a smile. It was Dave, clinging onto his precious briefcase and looking perfectly trimmed in his business suit. The grin from him was sickening and I rolled my eyes in irritance. ‘Good to see you Jack. Pleasant trip?’ ‘It was fine.’ ‘Shame you didn’t get my message, I did have first class tickets for yourself and Barney if you’d wanted.’ I shrugged and inside I hoped that he was lying. ‘Regardless, are you looking forward to today?’ Ah, it’s not like Dave to play around with his supposed toys, not when he has enough time to do that in his business etiquette anyway. He was referring to of course some last minute arrangements between himself, Barney and, well…me. Basically he had the proposal of the entire course of action running up to Colossus and that he just wanted to go over the fine print to make sure that we were both aware of what we would be taking part in. ‘Sure, I guess. Where are we meeting anyway?’ ‘At the hotel, apparently Barney has been lounging around there for the past couple of days with the rest of the bookers.’ I’m not surprised in the slightest. Barney had been taking advantage of every comp that he could get, whether good or not, and this was no exception. The entire booking committee was being taken care of because this was the last real think tank before Colossus. It was here that the main foundations for Colossus would take place. As such, every booker had to be there…which made it curious. ‘Why aren’t you there already then Dave?’ He faltered for just a moment, perhaps like he’d been found out at something but I couldn’t quite pinpoint it. It only lasted for that moment though and he quickly regained his composure. ‘I was finishing off the final details of course.’ ‘Of course.’ Price was next for the customs. ‘Well, I shall see you at the hotel Jack. You’ll know when it’s time. I’ll call you.’ And with that he was through the customs and out into Oklahoma. Well, that didn’t seem foreboding at all. I didn’t like that kind of sense. *** Perhaps he liked messing with Jack’s head too much, but for Dave, encountering and giving him little snippets would be sure to set the temperature rising in his mind. Perfect for Dave because when Jack got in any way riled up, he couldn’t think clearly and it was far easier to manipulate him into what was going on. He’d need to do that for everything to go smoothly. He wasn’t worried. It would. *** We all sat in a cold, air-conditioned room. Barney, David and I sat at a table, separate and as far away as possible. We argued over and over for well over two hours as Dave revealed to us the true plans. I wasn’t happy to say the least and Barney also seemed to be swaying to my side. Of course, Dave had realised this and had a kicker for both of us. For Barney he had managed to bargain for a permanent spot as a booker for him. Provided of course that this angle went smoothly and the way it should. In other words, if it bombed, Barney was gone. He quietened down a lot more after that had been dropped. For me, he threw an awful lot of things at me, reminding me of all the potential rewards for this angle if it went the way it should. I wasn’t having any of it and it did start to irk him. Of course, then he dropped who I would be dealing with. And I really didn’t want to do it then. But…I couldn’t tell him no. Because this was all far too important for my career. Barney looked at me hopefully throughout, hoping that I would join him and that this would be right. Dave too seemed to be dependent on my involvement and for once I felt like I was in charge. I told him that certain things would have to be done my way and he said that wouldn’t be a problem. I wanted the interaction with this man as limited as possible. ‘So…at Overkill…I have to wrestle him?’ ‘Yes.’ ‘And that’s it Dave. I don’t want any more involvement with him than I have to.’ ‘That’s fine. They just wanted a catalyst for his return and you seemed to fit the bill. It also helps us out. You don’t need to even speak with him again after this.’ ‘You better not be lying.’ And that was that. Signed, sealed and delivered. I hope to God this goes the way it should.
