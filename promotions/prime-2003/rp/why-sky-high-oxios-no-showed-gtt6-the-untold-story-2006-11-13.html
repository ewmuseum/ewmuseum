Why Sky High Oxios no-showed GTT6: The Untold Story

13 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/13/06 Okay, it was about time that I said why I, Vangellus Oxios, did not show up for the first round of the Eeyore Bracket at GTT6. I've kept quiet because I was too ashamed to talk about what actually happened that night. Don't think for a second that I was dodging that Olsig joker... no, it's worse. So the night before I was totally gonna fly out to the first round, I was partying over at the mansion. Oh yeah, I got a mansion; don't think anybody said differently. So I was partying... Scott was there with Aly because they don't have kids tying them down to the house just yet. And then about 200 of my close, personal strangers were also there using my bathrooms, eating my food, and making a mess of my place. I was just hyped that I was gonna kick that punkass's ass. "Damn Olsig, trying to steal my thunder, my name... I'll get him," I said, with my friend and former partner Scott there, as well as my manager Adrienne Diamond. And Yaphet Kotto was there. And he smiled. Scott said, "Yeah, but he beat us both in ladder matches." To which I shot back, "Yeah, but there won't be any ladders in the first round. That'll lead to his downfall." And Jason Snow proved as much in the first portion of the JitC, but that's another topic. Aly had had a little too much to drink. She wasn't going crazy or anything, but she was dancing on top of my counter. She had to be drunk, I specifically asked her not to dance up there. Scott kept an eye out for people who'd try to do anything with her... but when you're married to a 6'6" 350lb. behemoth, that kinda kills the libido or at least steers it toward other people. Scott's slight homophobia aside, we're good friends because he knows that he can leave me alone with Alyssa for like 3 days and nothing'll happen (other than I might braid her hair). Adrienne shook her head, sipping her pina colada like it was too hot or something. "I still think this is a bad idea. You've got a match tomorrow, you should be asleep until your flight. Last thing you need is to go into the GTT with a hangover." I was like, "But that's how Doctor Curiosity won the damn thing!" That might not have been true, but hey. "Besides, I can beat Olsig in a straight-up wrestling match. He's the only guy with a finisher he never uses because he has to have weapons involved. Really, I don't even think he knows how to do it right anymore." Scott saw that one of the guys was getting a little rowdy and trying to touch Alyssa, and went to deal with the problem. "No murder in my house!" I called at him over the sounds of Faster Kill Pussycat by Brittany Murphy and Oakenfold. "I'm not covered for that on my insurance." He grunted, and I hoped he'd take that seriously because I've already had like 3 guys get shot in my house. Advice for all the budding celebrities out there: NEVER invite 50 Cent and Game to your barbeque. They might not do anything, but their entourages might get ignorant and start shootin' niggas. And Yaphet Kotto was there. And he smiled. That's when I heard glass break apart. "Scott, what'd I just say!" I looked in the direction, seeing Joe Rogan making a scene being his usual drunk and wasted self. He was walking around with a lampshade over his head and talking a big game about how I didn't mean nothin' and stuff. So Adrienne had to go over and talk him down. He was all "I'm just speakin' my mind, nazi skank." But dropped it there, leaving everyone stunned that he could speak so coherently while he was wasted. I was standing there in the distance, watching Rogan make an ass out of himself, and Scott making some kid wet his pants that he tried to touch Alyssa, thinking about how I needed security for these parties. "Oh HELL no!" Richard cried out, as the surge protector that allowed him to play 3 screens of porn upstairs got ripped out of the wall. He rushed down the grand staircase to plug it back in. Believe me, I have never seen a fat guy go that fast. Rich jammed it back into the wall and told "all you inconsiderate kids" to stay away from the outlets and watch where they were stepping. Somebody asked, "What smells like fish?" Pwned. I went over to Richard and told him to go upstairs and not bother the guests who can get their own piece if they want to. I dunno why he goes for porn so much... I could just get him a mail-order bride if he wanted. But he's got standards or whatever. "Oh HELL no!" I said as I heard that Joe Rogan was pissing on the pool table. I spun on my feet and had to stop before I started going because Adrienne was standing right behind me. And Yaphet Kotto was there. And he smiled. "You've got to deal with him," she reminded me. I mocked her and repeated, getting a smack on the back of the head. Least it didn't hurt. Though the pinch on the arm was not necessary at all. As Rogan was finishing up and pulled his pants back up, I told him that he had to leave. "You think you're the boss of me, bitch? I'll kick your ass!" Again, I could make out every word that he said perfectly... wasn't his usual drunk. But I had to get this taken care of or he'd just keep trashing my house. And besides, I'm the Whole Freaking SHO, I can't back down from a challenge even if it's from a guy who looks like he'd be washing cars for a living if not for Fear Factor. So we go outside, out back where there's more space to move around and not as much expensive stuff to break. Some people formed a circle, not that I needed the audience. But people form a circle around me whatever I do and wherever I go. I like how symmetrical they always get it... these are accomplished circle-makers that follow me. So Rogan downs his umpteeth beer and comes at me. I put up my hands, figure it'll be easy. I lost count of how many times he kicked me in the face before I hit the ground. Somewhere between 16 and HOLY SHIZNITTLEBAM! I didn't just pass out, I got knocked the f*** out. And why? Because nobody advised me that Rogan's like a World Champion Drunken Tae Kwon Do master! And dude... people just left me there on the lawn, all night, continued the party without me. I woke up in the morning with a black eye, a sore chin. Not only that, but I was missing my pants. And my ass was sore. And Yaphet Kotto was there. And he smiled. So yeah... that's why I wasn't at GTT6. But I'll be there for Hoyt Williams... I'd hafta shoot myself if I no-showed that.
