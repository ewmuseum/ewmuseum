Living in Fear

2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-09-05 There was a time when you used to believe that what you wanted more than anything was to be a superstar in the world of wrestling. You used to dream about top rope elbow drops, dropkicks, and holding titles made of ten or more pounds of gold high over your head, pride beaming out for all the world to see through your giant smile. That dream used to drive you and make you believe there was always something to get up for the next morning. Then you got a little older, went through a maturing and growing up process and realized that you were heading down the wrong path. For all the athletic ability you do own, and there is enough, you realized long ago that wrestling in the ring wasn't really your calling in life. Sure you joined the Primetime Central Cruiserweight Tournament to try and prove something to the world, or so you said, but really all that was was a chance to prove to yourself once and for all that you really didn't have that needed desire and drive to be the best in you any longer. You've resigned yourself to the role of manager for Los Diablos cause you truly believe it is the post that you were born to take charge of. You still love the wrestling business, even the cold and heartless parts of it don't hurt your desire to be part of it, but you no longer care about holding up those titles yourself. Now it is all about your boys holding up titles, gaining victories, and forcing those who won't respect them on their own to learn to have to respect them the hard way. Being the man behind the talent, beyond the spotlight, and without all the glitz and glamour is just fine with you. Now if only someone else would get that hint and let you be. If only they would quit placing you into situations that you don't desire to be in any longer. Seven or eight years ago a spot in the main event of a card would have had your blood pumping so hard you'd swear that your heart would explode before the match ever arrived. The pure thrill of fighting for honor in front of a national audience, in the main event of the evening, would have driven you to insane lengths of trainning. That isn't the case anymore. Instead of trainning for the shot of a lifetime you're sitting around feeling sorry for yourself and wishing that the whole world wasn't against you. Sometimes you even allow yourself to wonder if this is all really worth it. The first time you got that feeling was a while back when you knew you were stepping into the ring with that demonic freak of nature Hessian. You knew then that this whole 'wrestling thing' wasn't what you wanted to do yourself. Manage your team to victories, titles, and celebrations is what you wanted now and right now those things seem a million miles away. 'I know how you are feeling hermano but don't stress out about this. You are going to be out there with Senor Williams and he will do the majority of the dirty work. After all he isn't called God's Champion for nothing.' Baltasar says to you as you sit in the booth across from him, picking at your eggs with your fork. You don't even bother to look up as you continue to swirl the food around on the plate, slowly. 'Easy for you to say. You're a world class athlete and have the love for fighting running through your blood naturally. I used to have that same mind set but I lost it long ago back when I realised that managing was my calling and not actually stepping through the ropes and competing.' You reply somberly. You aren't afraid to admit the fact that your scared. Scared of what might happen to you in that ring now that you've become 'soft' and worthless when it comes to fighting. 'Don't give me that crap. You lasted longer than a ton of other people in the Dual Halo match earlier this year. Hell even lasted longer than Angelus and myself. You gave Hessian a run for his money as long as you possibly could. I don't think you need to pretend that you don't have it in you at all to win matches essa. You just need to motivate yourself the way you motivate Angelus and I.' Baltasar says to you before loading a heaping fork full of sausage and eggs into his own mouth. You take a deep breath and let it out slowly before only slightly raising your gaze to look toward him. 'My boy don't be so ignorant. We both know that I survived in that Halo in the only way I possibly could have, the cowards way. I ran, hid under the ring, and even was screaming like a girl at a horror movie in order to try and avoid any actual fighting. Same thing with Hessian. I ran like a tool and hid under the ring again and attacked him with a steel pipe. I wasn't getting by at all in those matches on talent or even luck. I simply got by on being a coward and cheating as much as I could. That doesn't make a person a great wrestler that is what makes a person a fantastic manager, and I am the best in the world at that.' You smile weakly but only for a brief moment. You know every word is true and you can tell by the look in his eyes that Baltasar knows every word of it is true. While your big man has a lot of wit, talent, and bravado there is one thing he doesn't have much of and that is a poker face. 'I'm only trying to help you ease your mind about this match up holmes. You don't have anything to worry about. Hoyt is going to take care of the match for you and together you guys are going to once again make Imperium look like the unstoppable force it should become. You just have to keep your eyes open and make sure you are helping to give Hoyt every advantage possible and nothing more than that is required of you.' Baltasar says to you in an another vain attempt to cheer you up and make you quit thinking about the inevitable pain that you know your are going to go through this week. Killean is not coward and there for you know he isn't afraid of Hoyt. But Killean is also no fool and will gladly take the chance to gain the victory far more easily by pounding your little luchadore ass into the mat. Killean is the Universal Champion but more importantly he is a unstable and extremely dangerous and violent man. Who in their right minds would want to step into the ring with a man like him? With a mystery partner no less so there is no telling what sort of foul plan that the champ has in store for Hoyt and yourself as he plans on making his final statement before putting the gold on the line against God's Champion just a week later. 'That is what a manager does Baltasar. That is what I do best. But that isn't going to be possible from the corner of the ring knowing that I am going to have to be part of the war in the combat zone. Then to top that all off he has a mystery partner that we are going to have to face and deal with. Do you realize what type of sick individual that Killean is likely to come up with in order to make his point heard? There is no telling what kind of sick and twisted person could walk out from behind that curtain and stand by his side but you can rest assured that no matter whom he bring to the match I am in for a very long and painful evening. If I wasn't to scared to do so I'd throw up right now over this whole ordeal.' You tell him as you set the fork down on the table and take a sip of your water. The uneasy feeling is beginning to plague your stomach once more and the unfortunate part of it all is that this is just Saturday. Your mental anguish still have four more days to come up with ways that Killean is going to tie you into knots and attempt to cripple you as a message to Williams and any other who dares to screw with him. 'Nothing like that is going to happen to you hermano. I'm going to be at ReVolution as well and I can assure you that Killean will not be seriously injuring you or he'll have to answer to a wrath far greater than that of God. God is loving and forgiving. I don't have to worry about such restraints.' Baltasar says to you in as matter-a-fact a tone as you've ever heard in your life. You know he means it and that he'd die to help you out of any jam. Still you know that you shouldn't be forcing others to risk things in order to save you from situations that you used to love to be in. Why that passion is completely gone you simply don't know how to explain but you certainly wish you could get it back. Sunday, July 10th You tried getting some sleep to make you forget your fear but it wasn't meant to be. Sleep, beer, movies, games...none of them seem to work to ease your troubled mind. You know what is waiting for you in three days and you simply can't get it off your mind. In reality the fact that you aren't losing site of that focus is probably going to be the one thing that saves you from being crippled in that ring against a lunatic on a mad dog kick. The only place you'd rather be at less than in the ring with Killean and his mystery attack weapon this week is the chair you are sitting in right now, in the living room of a man you'd rather not have anything to do with anymore, having to discuss the near future. 'You know what you need to do is grow a set Morales. You sure don't seem like the same cocky, arrogant, smart ass that I met a few months ago.' Cross says to you with his piercing blue eyes staring you down. It is almost as if he is analyzing you very being, the soul that you don't let many others in the world actually know. This man, this 'business partner', has given you more than one reason to cringe since you met him. He was supposedly your saving grace for the first round of the CW2 tournament and now you are going to be his. 'That was all smoke and mirrors when you first met me Cross. The truth is I had very little actual confidence of winning that tournament but I knew that all those men were around my same size and skill level. None of them were going to be lifting me ten feet in the air and planting me into the arena floor via a quick stop through the ring canvas. I've got a real problem with Killean being on the opposite side of the ring from me. He is nuts, a rabid dog looking for a kill, and I'm just the type of small animal the man could sink his teeth into and make an example of.' You say with a groan as you rest your head back against the black leather of his designer couch. No doubt this man has never had to fear many things in his life. Partially cause he is a pretty damn big man himself, were not talking Los Diablos big but big enough, and his family was rich enough that any real problems would be met with a serious lawsuit. Besides Jacob Cross is a man who is always in things for himself and no one else so it isn't exactly like you really expect him to actually care how you feel about this situation. 'That is the difference between guys like you and I Senor Morales. You look at this as a horrible chance to get injured, maimed, or worse. I look at this and see it as a chance for you to let out the inner animal in you and prove to the world that you are not some weak, sissy, manager who people can push around without expecting retribution for it. You need to show Killean that you have no intention of being his punching bag. I mean it is either that or potentially die, so you can take your pick.' He says with a grin as if the thought of watching you die in the ring actually amuses him. No doubt if you already had him working matches for PRIME he probably would find your death to be very amusing but without that promise of work firmly in his had yet you're pretty sure that was nothing more than his way of saying to suck it up. You just sit there and stare at the ceiling for a while, not sure if you have anything else you want to say to this man. You are going to have to deal with him enough in the near future and right now all you want to do is just crawl in a hole and die. There is no time for that, however, as Cross is ready to talk business with you. Why is it that so many men are impatient like Jacob Cross? Is it just American men's nature to be in a hurry to do everything? 'I know that your ready to speak about how we are going to take care of the business we have coming up but I'm really not in the mood to talk about business right now. I have to worry about getting into the ring with Killean this week and I've got more things to worry about with that match than I things to worry about reguarding you and our deal.' You respond to him with a bit of a tone. You are a bit angry with him, as you have been since you met him, but at the same time you know he is going to help your team out in the future. He is going to be an asset to your group and to the success for Los Diablos and The Imperium during their PRIME careers. 'As much as I like seeing you squirm Enrique I'm going to cut you a break here and let us get down to the important business following the huge Pay-Per-View your boys have coming up. No sense in distracting you before that cause it would be pretty nice to have the boys walking around with their belts still when we all have to start working together. After all I am champion in life so I need only to surround myself with champions if at all possible. Don't want that stick of loser rubbing off on me, ya know what I mean?' He asks with his usual dick head grin and flashy arrogant attitude that he always seems to be displaying. You simply sigh and get to your feet before walking toward the door. 'As usual it was such a wonderful experience having to talk to you. Try and learn some sort of humility before the Pay-Per-View comes and goes, otherwise this is going to be one partnership that isn't fun in the least for anyone involved.' You say to him as you walk out the door. You just shake your head as you walk down the hall toward the elevators that will take you to the freedom that is being away from Jacob Cross and his Penthouse suite. As you step into the elevator and push the first floor button you finally realize for the first time in several days that you'd better get your head out of the shit hole and into defending itself. Come ReVolution 70 it is either going to be fight like a caged animal or get seriously injured. You may not like the odds of facing hte Universal champion and walking out with no serious injuries but you damn sure don't like the fact that the odds will be far worse if you don't stop balling up like a little child and instead prepare to fight for you life. That is what this week amounts to for you and the continuing of your managing career. A fight for your honor, pride, respect, and in all honesty...your life.
