Date: Friday, February 11 Almost late. I must’ve been eager to please, ‘cause that headrush was worse than a bad round of coke. I took my seatbelt off when I took to the corner. The parking lot's empty and my stomach didn't feel too great. Besides, I was almost to the restaurant and needed to hop out quickly, because I knew I was expected and being waited on. That little strap wasn't a necessity until I heard the creak, thud and shattering of a thousand glass shards. Seconds before, I’d heard the steady roar of a revving engine, but it didn’t register as anything more than the same thing you always hear when cars are passing. Next thing I knew, my head had come open. My back contorted itself in two and the dead crunch of my ribs failing me would've been enough to make me faint, if I wasn't verging unconsciousness already. It was like falling into a pool of boiling water. After that, there was nothing. Chapter 1: Pre-Game20 minutes before...''C'mon! Up! You don't get paid to take a three-count!'' Amy stayed prone on the canvas. They'd been training non-stop today, and her head was swimming. She could've gotten up, but this wasn't a real match and she needed a moment to center her vision again - she shouldn't have, but she did. The sudden boot to the gut her teacher delivered didn't help. ''Do not leave yourself open to attack in here. Treat it just like a match, or do you think they’ll go easy ‘cause you’ve got nice tits?'' Another boot, and this one had some enthusiasm behind it. He was trying to raise the redhead's ire so he'd see a defense worthy of her name. ''FuARRRRRRRGH!'' was Amy's jumbled cry as she swept her leg across the mat in a surprise attack that took down the one and only Dave Gibson. The elbow she sent to the side of his head sealed the deal, and Aimz stood up to demonstrate that time hadn't done a thing to her frame of twenty two years. She was young and not quite as hopeless as she'd let on, but the fog of withdrawal was still clouding her head. Throughout the hours they'd been training, her eyes were glassy and red... what was unusual was that the redness and mental block had nothing to do with being stoned, but everything to do with not being stoned. An exceedingly difficult month showed on her normally fair features that were now wrought with the creases and lines of an increasing exhaustion. No amount of coke, no ecstasy, no painkiller could do anything but sicken her now. She'd let herself down that night in the church when she self-medicated, coincidentally just prior to Jeph finally admitting his feelings. The morning after that, she'd woken up to a fierce lecture and a headache that tortured her tenfold. She had all the reason in the world to stay sober; hell, she was even winning matches. The Red Raver smiled at the thought of the week before and her main event victory. She'd out-performed a handfull of PRIME stars there, and what's to say she couldn't do it again? With that thought and abounding confidence in mind, she slid under the ropes and tossed her sweat-soaked tanktop to the floor, reaching in her Warped Tour duffle bag for a Bleeding Through t-shirt. Amy was hasty and wriggled her way into the stretched garment before Gibson had the chance to roll over and sneak a peek. While he groaned and nursed his twisted knee, the result of Amy Campbell's vengeful nature, the girl most simply called Aimz was stuffing her cell phone and wallet back into her ripped jean pockets. In the meantime, Gibson stood and leaned against the turnbuckles in the nearest corner, rubbing his own neck with his right hand and grimacing. as he peered over at the unusually stoic redhead. He wanted so badly to pass on more advice, but really didn't have any. For once in her life, Amy seemed to have things in order for herself. She had priorities, and this new federation that had signed her was number one on the list. The smile he'd seen when she stepped in the ring earlier in the day indicated some newfound glory - it was as if she enjoyed wrestling again. ''Holding up all right?'' Amy asked with a raised eyebrow. ''Good enough, I guess,'' Gibson said. ''I know how to protect myself in the ring, and my students don't have the skills to do any serious damage. Any cowboys who have ideas about showing me up get set straight pretty quickly.'' ''But enough about me. I'm a step slow and haven't competed in a real match in more than a year. You won't be so lucky - or afford to let your guard down - against your actual opponents. Especially when there are thirty of them.'' ''I know... plus, I had to take the cheap way out.'' ''Hey, don't knock it!'' That familiar wolfish smile flashed across Gibson's angular face. ''Anybody can learn to mat wrestle or go high-risk, but cheating? That's the last true art inside the wrestling ring. Plus Amy, you've GOT to be that way in this kind of match. ''My advice? Take your shots when and how you can and forget about honor.'' Amy nodded as she pulled her bag up over one shoulder and pressed the button on her car's remote starter, ''Honor was never a part of my life until now, and I’m a little nervous to let it go in there… but I’ve gotta do what I’ve gotta do, eh?'' Dave cocked his head slightly. He really hadn’t seen this side of her. In all her years wrestling around – though never with – him, she’d been hotheaded and sloppy, but incredibly effective in her speed and quick strikes. She was incredibly intelligent, and it showed in the delivery of every word, but in the ring? That was another story entirely. Bright as she was, Amy always let her heart get far ahead of her brain but now… well… the two seemed to be working hand-in-hand. He shook the thoughts away long enough to give some sort of response, noting her rather hurried departure. ''And what’s with leaving so soon? It's only been three hours...'' ''Got a text from Jeph,'' she answered, pulling her hair back into a loose ponytail that left fiery strands to fall across her cheekbones, ''I owe him lunch today and forgot, so I'm gonna deal with that and watch the first Halo tape over again.'' ''Scouting, eh? You know…'' his eyebrow raised, ''I've never seen you so focused.'' ''Well I have to be if I'm gonna win, right?'' Amy nodded in reply to her own question, ''Might not be purely by talent or anything god-given, but you know the old saying about what you put your mind to...'' ''Well, you've definitely done that,'' Gibson nodded. ''Same time tomorrow, then?'' ''Same time tomorrow.'' Amy grinned and cast him a half-hearted wave in reply, hurrying herself out the door of the warehouse gymnasium with the game she lived in mind. Twenty nine times the competition was coming up. Twenty-nine individuals all out to take twenty-nine others out. The odds were against everybody...but she planned on pulling them her way. 'K-Wolf, HIT, Hoyt, Nova...think about it - they've got matches before Halo. That wears down some key players and doubles my lu--' Her own thoughts were deafened when a car pulled through the intersection in front of the gym, its performance muffler tearing through her eardrums as she hopped into her own ride. ''Fucking idiot kids.'' Bleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeep! In her pocket, Amy's phone was reminding her that anger and wrestling weren't the only things occupying the earth. It also let her know that she was late already for a dinner date. ''Fucking idiot ME!'' Disregarding her seatbelt, the little redhead took off out of the parking lot. She followed the path of the same car that had zipped by and nearly deafened her before. According to her watch, she was at least three minutes late for dinner. Jeph wouldn’t be pleased but, moreover, this just wasted time. Amy hated to waste time… This hurry was sickness, depression, withdrawal, and exhaustion… even disdain over her timing. All of that and more figured into a horrid cocktail of chemical imbalance. She felt a rush of blood to the head coming to the first stop sign, and reached half-heartedly for her seatbelt. Chapter Two: Too Much is Not EnoughThat very same morning…Packing everything up from the old apartment, Jephrey Howe had found any and all drug paraphanelia short of a syringe. That was expected, however, since The Pack Rat – sometimes known as his new girlfriend – had a tendency to keep even the most eroded and destructive of memories around. Those tools wouldn’t see the light of day in their new home, and neither would Amy’s propensity for using them… but that was an entirely different subject. At that moment, he had to concentrate on not falling back to sleep - especially without waking Amy up. At least one of them had to be productive in the run of a day and, if it happened to be one of those days between ReVolutions, it was almost guaranteed that the productive party would not be Amy Campbell. The light filtered in just barely through sheet they’d used as a makeshift curtain or peep-show blocker for the neighbors they hadn’t bothered greeting yet, and Amy was absolutely dead to the world underneath the covers. She’d taken refuge in the glorious little kingdom of darkness, rest and warmth that laid on the underside of each fluffy blanket, on top of every pillow that surrounded her head as she was spread across the mattress, supported only by the hardwood floor. It was perfectly serene under there and inside her dream – a calm that was interrupted all too soon by a kiss on the side of her head and a gentle hand shaking her shoulder. ''Hey,'' he said, ''Open your eyes for a second.'' While Amy was stirring and sliding her way back to reality, Jeph’s head was cocked to one side, leaving him to peer at the slips of bright red hair protruding from under the sheets. He spoke softly to the point of barely mouthing the question, ''are we awake?'' When the napping redhead’s ears received the words, Jeph’s breath was drawn far back into his chest. What struck anybody who saw Aimz open her eyes for the first time in a lifetime, a morning or even after one of her little fits of nervous blinking were the intense colors. One eye was a mahogany brown, while the other remained an oceanic blue that nearly faded to a grey or white in any amount of light, and they were no less striking this morning… the one that opened, at least. Her eyelid cracked open to examine this strange movement – the intruder daring to wake her up – and the icy blue eyeball exposed itself just long enough to notice Jeph, roll sleepily and shut tightly once more as its owner groaned a simple ''Hmph…no,'' in reply to her partner’s query. That reaction was far kinder than the slap in the head he’d expected, at least, but Amy wanted to make herself useful… just not at that moment, or at all before noon. In the long run, she wanted to have productive days – it just never ended up happening. Today, though, she had actual training to do… an old friend was in town and he was not going to wait, that much Jeph knew. ''Gibson’s probably waiting for you already,'' he grumbled, ''C’mon…we can’t be in bed all day.'' Amy rolled over and softly sighed, discarding the sheets and grinning below eyes that fluttered open only when her devious words were complete, ''That’s what last night was for.'' Jeph blinked, ''You…'' With his stutter came Amy’s awakening. She sat up on the mattress and eventually slid off to find clean clothes. Thanks to most of their possessions being in transit, she had to lean into a large cardboard box to sift through a bundled mess of her sweaters – citing pajama pants and a sports bra as inappropriate wrestling wear – and finally found a little black t-shirt that’d do the trick. It was caught at the bottom, most likely on packing tape. One slip lead to another and, thanks to Jeph pulling a foot out from under her, Aimz fell headfirst into the dilapidated box. He expected a slightly more aggressive reaction than the casual laughter he received when her head poked out from under the mountain of clothes that her tumble had upset. In her own true form, she continued their mini-conversation nonchalantly while crawling out of said mess. ''I what? I suck, right?'' ''No, you…'' came Howe’s correction, ''…You’re something else, Amelia Campbell.'' Amy smiled and bit at her bottom lip. She wasn’t used to being looked at like he was looking at her… that loving stare, it wasn’t normal. He truly admired her, and it scared her half to death despite that she felt the same way – just without admitting so. Whenever that topic had come up – which had been rare prior to the evening of firsts they’d had the previous, drunken night – she’d tapdanced around it hurridly, an overwhelming fear splashed across her features in sweat whenever commitment or the ‘love’ word came to mind. The most she could muster up was a bashful smile when it came to finding the words for a reply – and even that was stifled when she deliberately shifted her attention to stretching her lithe form. A yawn, fit of blinking and cracked neck later, she kissed Jeph on the cheek quickly and sprinted for the door, curses abound, after a glance at the clock on the radio. She promised a meeting for lunch to repay him unnecessarily for the unusually romantic and fine dining he’d treated her to the night before, and disappeared out the door. Bound by a schedule she’d made for herself leading up to Culture Shock and the only match she’d ever been so determined to make an impact in, Aimz – thanks in no small part to a timely and newfound inspiration in living – wasn’t missing a beat… …She was, on the other hand, missing pants. Back to Thought: The EpilogueFor the first time in her life, Amy Campbell had made a decision entirely of her own free will – without anybody telling her what was right or wrong. In her eyes, it was easy to see that her heart was torn in two between love and the fight that kept her heart beating. She wasn’t aware that love had actually prevailed, even though she chose the fight. I knew much better. Ames had committed herself to two things in the span of a month – myself, and wrestling. I was her lifelong friend, wrestling was her lifelong career… but she’d only stepped up and taken note of us in the light of love recently. I knew she loved me, and I knew now that she loved wrestling… fighting… winning. That’s what she wanted – to win and prove everybody wrong; To show that she could truly be as big as people claimed she once was, and to show she was only getting stronger in her talent. She’d been training hard, struggling within both wrestling and how to deal within a relationship that actually meant something. Tonight was only another lesson in her rapid education. Amy had decided that she wanted to win – she wanted to be the best, and wanted for others to see how valuable and skilled she truly was. What amazed me was that wanting all of those things… well, it meant that she saw herself as something – a respectable wrestler, a person great at what they do. For the first time in the lifetime I’d known her, she seemed to respect – dare I say, love - herself and what she’d chosen to do with her life. In choosing to leave me in that time of need to wrestle, she’d chosen love. She loved me, but she’d also just realized another true love; Wrestling. Instead of brushing it aside like she would have a month ago, she dove headfirst into a challenge and loved every damn moment of it. Aimz never abandoned her friends, but she always fought to the end for what she wanted.
