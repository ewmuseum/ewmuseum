The Price of Betrayal

4 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 4th, 2007 Tuesday, May 1st, 2007 The darkness shrouds Craig as he sits there, looking out the glass window; his thoughts are as lost as the rain that is pouring outside. He listens to the rain as if it were singing a song to him and his red eyes are just staring straight ahead as if there was a movie playing in the rain. Secretly, there was. Never had he felt so happy yet lost at the same time. Part of him was hoping that looking at the rain would give him the answers that he continually keeps searching for. Unfortunately, it looked like the rain wouldn’t be able those answers to him. Yet, he continues to look out at the rain, feeling a calm serene sweeping over him. Behind him, as faint as the rain crashing into a lake, he can hear footsteps. To the normal person, they wouldn’t hear a thing. However, Craig isn’t what you would classify as normal. "Can’t sleep?" Craig asks before she can even form the words in her mind, much less her mouth. She freezes behind him momentarily, wondering to herself how he could do something like that. He had done time and time again since she had come back in his life, and yet she continued to feel weird after he did it. Craig’s body turns slightly towards her almost as if he is inviting her to continue walking towards him. Once again, it freaks her out, but she puts it behind her as she finally sits next to him. The two just look out at the rain, both sharing a moment together that they usually had little time for. "So, what has you up this late, Sarah?" Craig asks his daughter calmly, never once removing his eyes from the backyard. She sits there next to him, feeling like a stranger not only to him but to the house she had lived in for the past three weeks. It had been an adjustment period for both of them trying to live with each other again. For Craig, he had to still focus on PTC and PRIME making sure he fulfilled his contractual obligations. He had been on a whirlwind tour for the past two months, one that drained him even more than the international tour he had embarked upon when he first started with PRIME. He had responsibilities, responsibilities that no longer were required of him ever since he lost at the PTC Unification Match. Yet, things hadn’t slowed down for him thus far. Instead, he was tossed into another title match, this time against his nemesis Devin Shakur. It was a match he was eagerly awaiting. "The rain," she says and he smiles slightly at hearing this. He knows she tells the truth, but it still interests him how the rain could be so inviting to him when the rain was so distracting towards her. He chalked it up to the fact that they were still learning each other even though they obviously had a connection that was rarely seen in fathers and daughters. Craig was still coming to terms to the fact that he had a seventeen year old daughter, something neither of them could explain. He just wrote it down on the list of things, the rather long list, he had to figure out still. None of that fazed him though as his heart skipped a beat each time he saw her. He loved his daughter more than life itself it seemed and he would do anything for her. Sarah had been having a hard time adjusting to this new life. She had given up hope that she would ever see her father again and had lost herself to a world of drugs and temptations. It was a life she was having a hard time rebelling against. Yet, she knew deep in her heart that she had to for her father. He had gone so far to get her back that she didn’t want to screw it up for either of them. The two of them sat in silence like they had many times. Over the past few weeks, Craig had started to tell her what he remembered, trying to lay it out all out for her to understand. However, it was a story that had taken many weeks to tell. One that he still wasn’t finished telling to be quite honest. He knew that eventually he would have to tell her how it all ended, but it was a part of the story he had pushed into the depths of the mind. It was something he didn’t want to relive until he had to. Yet, she had to know because soon it was going to hit him in the worse way possible sooner or later. He just hoped it would be later rather than soon. "Dad," she finally said, speaking in the soft tone she normally used when she was going to broach a topic that she knew he wasn’t going to be a fan. Craig already knew what it was going to be as he turned his red eyes onto her. "Yeah?" he responds, giving her a chance to ask him the question that he was hoping to prolong. "Can you finish the story?" she asks and he just simply nods his head before sighing. With each step, he was getting closer and closer to the ending that could potentially push her away from him. He was certain of it, but knew that he had to. She had stuck with him this far, but nothing could prepare her for the ending of this story. His eyes turn back towards the rain and once again, he’s lost in his thoughts. Thoughts that he is pulling out from the dark crevices of his mind. ---- December 2006 "Enough," he said and before Craig even looked up, he knew it was Master Thibaut. Craig’s eyes averted back towards the staircase, and his assumptions were correct as the Master stood there looking down upon both of them. Melody and Craig both looked up at him as if they were almost sorry for the disturbance they had created. Without speaking another word, the Master had started to walk down the stairs. The anger in his eyes was evident. Evident enough that everyone left the room immediately, leaving just Craig, Melody, and Master Thibaut. Whatever it was that he had to say, it was going to be bad. "Such foolishness behavior, acting like children," he began to say as he walked up to the two adults, and it dawned on them that he was completely right. Here they were letting their emotions get the better of them instead of being smart about it all. Melody knew in her heart that she could no longer blame Craig for things he didn’t truly do. It was her anger that was setting off his anger and she knew how dangerous that could be. Craig needed to be calm to truly find himself, and she wasn’t helping at all. "I’m sorry, Master," she whispered before lowering her head before him. Without a moment’s hesitation, Master Thibaut took his right hand and softly tapped her chin, telling her to lift her head and not to feel the shame that she was feeling. For the first time in a while, Craig felt the anger he had for Melody escape and he was happy with that. "You act in emotions, Melody. It is how you are. You care about those that step foot into here and to have them attacked in such a way put you in a place that any normal person would’ve felt. Don’t feel ashamed about it. Instead, learn how to harness that anger into energy instead of lashing out at our guest. Trust me Melody, he’s confused as it is," he said in a soft tone that caressed Melody. She simply nodded her head as the two turn towards to Craig. "Did you accomplish what you needed to?" the Master asked of Craig and Craig could only nod his head. Part of him was still sickened by what he had done even if it was necessary. Yet, he pushed it out of his mind, hoping to move forward and stop living in the past. "Good," the Master spoke once again after letting the silence clear the air. He looks at him and suddenly his eyes turn cold; coldness that Craig had yet seen from the Master. Then, his body language turns cold as well and he wonders what exactly is going on here. He looks at Melody who looks as lost as Craig does. This worries Craig even more as he waits for the Master to speak his mind, but as the seconds tick by he says nothing. Yet, it’s clear that something is bothering Master Thibaut. "What is it, Master?" Melody finally asks him and he immediately responds with an answer. "There is someone in the temple, someone who doesn’t belong," Master Thibaut tells the two of them and this obviously chills Melody. Craig is surprised by this, the mere fact that Melody didn’t realize someone was inside of the temple that didn’t belong. Then, he wondered as to why this person still remained in the temple. "Who is it?" Craig asks him, hoping it’s a clear answer. Yet, the look in the Master’s eyes told him it was obviously not that clear to even him to this point. "I wish I knew. I’ve only seen him a few times. At first, I thought he might be one of Melody’s students as sometimes she has student that not even I am aware of, but I have yet to see him in one of her classes. I don’t see him often enough, but when I do it’s at the oddest times. Even now I don’t know where he is at yet," he says in response to Craig’s questions. Instantly, Craig’s eyes dart across the room in front of him, hoping he might catch a glimpse of something he hadn’t seen before. However, there was nothing there. "Why is here, do you know?" Melody asks, her eyes dashing across the room as well hoping that she would see something. The fact that there is someone in the temple that she wasn’t aware of irks her as well though she is much better at shrouding her feelings. However, to Craig, it was obvious as day. Her personal body language changed when she wasn’t in her environment. It was something that Craig had learned quickly due to the fact that every time he was around her she would get into a defensive mood set as if he was trying to take something away from her. "I don’t know, but I suspect a reason," he begins to say and then he points a finger at Craig, which quickly confuses him. "I think that reason is you, Craig," he finishes his sentence before putting his finger down. Craig looks at the Master, then at Melody, before finally going back to the Master hoping for a suitable explanation. There wasn’t the slightest reason as to why someone would be following Craig, especially now that he had finished off Silsbee. Yet, part of him knew there was still so much to learn about his past that anything was indeed possible. "Why do you think that, Master?" Melody asks him before Craig has the chance to. His eyes dart in anger towards her, but he quickly focuses upon Master Thibaut once again. "It’s the vibe this man gives off. As if he’s waiting for you, Craig. I tell you to warn you, not to chastise you. Nor shall you Melody," he speaks once again, his eyes averting towards Melody who is foaming at the mouth. Not that she needed another reason to hate Craig, but she instantly has another one. "You let him remain here, threatening us all. Why, Master, why?!" she speaks, this time raising her voice slightly as she is obviously irritated by this string of events. However, Master Thibaut refuses to even look at her as his eyes refuse to move from Craig. Instead, he answers her in a way that shuts her up immediately. "Because, he needs our help. Because I’ve decided to give him my help. That is all you need to know Melody. For now, that is all you need to know," he finishes saying before returning his attention to Craig. "Now, you need to be careful, but I can only imagine that you are exhausted from your journey. So, I shall let you rest until your energy is back. Then, we can continue with our training," he tells him and Craig only nods. His defenses were at an all time high as he turned away from Melody and the Master. The news didn’t settle well with him as he walked up the stairs and towards his room for a few hours of sleep. He was tired of being hunted time and time again. This time, he would be the hunter. ---- Tuesday, May 1st, 2007 The grandfather clock in the corner of the room rang loudly, signaling that another hour had gone in the telling of his tale. Craig’s eyes looked to the right, wondering what time it was, and saw that it was already four in the morning. As he looked out once again he noticed that the rain had ceased during the telling of his story to Sarah. He looked over at his daughter and saw her staring right back at him, hanging on to every one of his words. Two hours had passed, but her interest had not waned one iota. It amazed him slightly except for the fact that she her interested had no shifted at all over the past few weeks that he had been telling her his stories. "It’s time for me to get some sleep," he says to her and immediately sees the disappointment in her eyes. She could tell that she was getting closer and closer to the end of the story, but she had little idea of how much there was left to tell. Not in terms of actual length, but in terms of emotions that were still left to be explored. He wanted to finish telling her the rest of the story, but not tonight. He needed her still, needed her to complete him. He wasn’t ready to give up on that quite yet. Instead, he would stop. He would stop just to know he had another day left with her still. "We’re getting there, I promise you," he tells her, trying to quell her disappointment. She smiles at him before leaning in and kissing him on the cheek. It still made him happy when she did little things like this as he was still coming to terms with the fact he had a daughter, much less that she was right there next to him. It was reasons like that he feared he would lose her. Slowly, he pulls himself up and starts walking towards the stairs that would lead him to his room. However, as his left foot hits the first stair, she stops him with a question. "Did you find the person who was looking for you, dad?" she asked him, knowing that he would only tease her. He slowly turns towards her and looks her in the eyes. "Yeah, I did," he tells her, hoping to finish this portion of the story for the evening. "And?" she responds, obviously not satisfied with the answer. "He’s no longer looking for me," he tells her before turning his back towards her and heading up the stairs. With each step, he can only feel his heart get heavier. It was slowly eating away at him, but this his way of coming to terms with what he had done. That dark chapter of his life had lied dormant for such a long time. This was his time to finally come to terms with it, come to grip with it, and put it to rest once and for all. ---- Saturday, May 5th, 2007 The airplane finally came to a rest on the tarmac after the two hour flight and as it did, Brady’s eyes shot wide open. He had finally arrived in St. Louis, knowing it was time for him to do what he had longed to do. Weeks had passed since he was told what he would have to. He craved to do that exact thing. Kill Dusk. Finally, he’s told he’s allowed to exit off the plane and he does exactly that. He had been told that everything he needed would be here in St. Louis, waiting for him. He would be given a set of instructions that he was to follow explicitly. He grinned at the very thought of this, knowing this was his chance of redemption. This was his chance to change everything he had done wrong up to this point and make it right. He had lost his wife and his child not to mention his entire life. As he walked towards the exit of the plane, the stewardess, a middle aged woman who had obviously been doing this for a number of years, smiled at him before telling him the same thing she told countless others throughout the years. "Enjoy your stay in St. Louis." His eyes landed upon her and a sad smile crept across his face, startling her slightly even though she refused to show it. "Trust me, I will." Then, he walks off the plane, knowing that in a few short hours everything was going to change for him. Most importantly, his luck. ---- Saturday, May 5th, 2007 After hours and hours of interviews, he had reached the point of exhaustion. Craig opened the door to his hotel room and could rather easily just pass out in the bed before him. Tomorrow evening would be an evening he would never forget, he was certain of it. The animosity between him and Shakur had grown to a fervor now, and was easily a match people were anticipating with ever breath. After leaving the arena, he had been told that they would go on right before the main event, a spot that was one of the most coveted spots in any card. They would have their chance to show what they were made of. Craig was definitely ready for this, especially after the debacle at the Unification Match. As he lies down upon the bed, he could finally feel his muscles relax, something that he hadn’t felt in a while. His workouts had intensified in the days leading up to the UltraViolence PPV. He had started to push himself to the extremes and he knew it. Yet, he could care less. This was his chance. Craig was going to show Shakur what kind of fool he was. It was something that excited Craig as he would get a chance to unleash his anger upon Shakur. With sleep starting to wave over Craig, he heard a knock on his door. He tried to ignore, hoping that whoever it was would just go away. Yet, the knocking persisted to the point that Craig had no choice but to answer. Slowly, he pulls himself up and walks over to the door wondering who could really be bothering him at this time of night. As he opens the door though, he’s shocked and surprised to see who is standing behind the door. Sarah. "What’re you doing here?" he asks her, literally shocked that she is here. In the days before, he had asked her if she wanted to come, but he continually declined stating that it wasn’t really her thing. She claimed that she had grown out of it after seeing him wrestle when he was younger. Now though, she was there in St. Louis with a bag across her shoulder. He opened the door even more, letting her in, but still wondering why she had made the trek up here. "I decided I should come and see you wrestle again. See if you’ve still got it," she tells him and he just laughs as he shuts the door. He walks over to her and the two hug once she drops the bag onto the ground. "See if I still got it, eh?" he asks her as they break the hug and look at each other. She just smiles as she lightly punches him in the arm. "Yeah. There was a time when you won world championships, but something tells me that you’ve gotten a little soft," she replies jokingly. Craig just shakes his head at his daughter, who he had come to know as quite the sarcastic one. Instead of responding with his own wit, he just let it roll off of him and decided to move on with the conversation. Unfortunately, he didn’t have a bed for her to sleep on as he wasn’t expecting her to show up. "You want me to get you your own room? I’m sure they’ve got a spare somewhere," he asks her, but she just simply shakes her head. "I’m fine for now. A rough plane ride, but I’ll be fine for now," she says, hoping she doesn’t have to say anything else. It had been a few days since she had heard the last part of the story, and she was thirsty for more. Just by looking at her, Craig knew this and sat down on the bed, motioning for her to do the same thing. His red eyes were dull as he looked at the wall in front of him, reaching deep into his mind once again for the story he was determined to defeat. Yet, he didn’t know if it would eventually defeat him. ---- December 2006 "Because, you told me the secrets you housed in your head, were far too important for you to even get them back if you ever lost them without making sure you were the same person you had been before," the Master explained to Craig and Craig just couldn’t talk anymore. The reality was starting to hit him. He knew what Jocelyn had done to him and he wanted death. Yet, he couldn’t bring death to him. So, he would bring death to her. The scenes continued to play over and over again in his head, almost as if it was unable to stop doing so. It was like a video was being played before him with it being looped for him to get the full torment from it. Slowly it was starting to eat away at him as he sat in the middle of the dining room trying to come to terms with it. It had been a few hours since everything had played itself out, but he knew he had all the facts now. Jocelyn didn’t love him. Instead, she was using him for his own benefits; the only problem was that he didn’t know what those benefits were. Part of him didn’t even care. He just wanted revenge. He craved the revenge. He was lost in his thoughts, letting the images in his head take over instead of trying to fight them off. The images were fueling his anger and he refused to let them go. He couldn’t tell himself, he couldn’t let himself forget what had been done to him just in case his mind tried to make him forget once again. Instead of letting that happen, he forced himself to relive the moments until it was imprinted into his mind. He needed to make sure it was there because of that fear of losing it again. With him being so lost in his thoughts though, he was blissfully unaware of wooden bamboo stick heading straight for the back of his head. CRACK! It had been like someone had slammed his head against the hood of a moving car. Instantly, his eyes closed up as he felt the pain coarse throughout his entire body. His body slumped forward and he could feel the blood pouring out of the back of his head. Consciousness was starting to get away from him no matter how hard he fought to keep it. As his body collapsed within itself, he felt a swift kick to the ribs, one so swift that he lost all the air in his body as he fell onto his side. The attack didn’t stop though as he felt another kick, this time to his face, come out of nowhere. The impact sent him rolling a few feet away from his attacker. Yet, any hope of recovery from this was ill wrought. Instead, his foe was on top of him with a few fists to Craig’s unprotected jaw. After the third or fourth shot, he felt like his jaw was going to explode. His energy was being drained out of him with each shot and there was nothing he could do to stop it. He just felt the pain and that was it. It felt like his body had given up on him. With each passing second, the attacker’s ferocity increased as if Craig had murdered him several times over. Without a moment’s hesitation, the attacker ripped Craig off the floor and slammed him into the wall hard enough for a cracking sound to be heard. It was then that Craig was certain that two of his ribs had cracked. The pain was intense, but he gritted through it even though he couldn’t even see his attacker due to the pain being so intense. Yet, he refused to give up on himself as he threw a haymaker hoping to connect with something. Instead, he just connected with air before he felt a knee colliding into his stomach. The pain just increased with each shot and he was certain that the pain would kill him before long. As another kick collided with his ribs, furthering damaging the two cracked ribs, it was like something had exploded in Craig’s head. The pain had turned into anger and instead of feeling weak, he began to feel strong. Never had he taken such a beating and never had felt the mixture of pain and elation he was starting to feel. Slowly, he could feel his body taking over and his eyes fluttering open. The person continued to assault him, but his body was starting to store the pain in an area where Craig could no longer feel that pain. Instead it felt like a gnat attacking a herd of lions. Pointless. As his red eyes opened and looked at the world around him, he could feel the fire ignite in them as slammed his elbow into the person’s stomach. Immediately, the person was done on the ground, shocked by the out of nowhere elbow from Craig. Without waiting a single moment, Craig pulled himself back up to his feet and looked down at his attacker. He was partly surprised that he had never seen the man, but also irritated as to why this man had taken such a violent aggression towards him. Yet, Craig was in no mood for asking questions. Just for fighting. He slammed his foot into the chest of the man several times, hearing the pained breaths the man was taking. Craig took pleasure in it as he grabbed the man up and slammed his forearm into his face, making sure he broke as many bones as he could along the way. With his focus upon inflicting damage upon this man, he was blissfully unaware of the dozens of people watching him. They stood there in awe as Craig slammed his knee first into the man’s stomach before breaking the man’s nose against his knee. The man fell backwards, blood spurting out of his nose as if it was a fire hydrant. Yet, Craig didn’t care. He wanted answers and he wanted them now. "Who are you?" Craig yelled at him, not even making it sound like a question. "S-shawn," he managed to mutter through the pain. "Who sent you?" Craig yelled once again, shaking the rafters with the reverberations from his voice. Yet, Shawn laid there silent, blood pouring out of him. That was all Craig needed to have answered for him. He knew exactly who had sent Shawn. Jocelyn. She was smart enough to know that Craig would eventually get his memories back and wanted to make sure that when Craig did exactly that, he wouldn’t be able to extract his revenge against her. Yet, he was a man possessed. He walked off to the side and saw a weapon, a sword, fit for his taking. He yanked at it and held it up high in the air. Slowly, he walked over to Shawn, still unaware of the people watching him with awe. Without a moment’s notice, he jerks the sword up high in the air and brings it down with a swiftness that you could feel the air from the sword several feet away. The end of the sword pierces right through Shawn’s throat, the sword, or as much as possible, sits in his throat as Craig walks away still not caring about what he’s done. Instead, his focus is solely upon Jocelyn, knowing it was time for the end game. Yet, he knew it would be his end game that won in the end. ---- Saturday, May 5th, 2007 The words finished rolling off his tongue, another two hours having passed, and he felt spent. He looked away from Sarah, knowing he had told her of killing people before. Yet, he knew the last part of the story was coming soon and it scared him to the very bone. He knew that everything that had happened beforehand led up to that one moment months ago, but it still sickened him. Everything that had transpired he had been required to live through over and over again until he was sick of himself. Yet, Sarah refused to let him drop the story. Craig hoped he could though, but he could see it in her eyes that she wouldn’t let him and it pained him. "I need you to finish the story. Not just for me, but for you," she told him and he could only simply nod his head. "I promise you Sarah, when I finish this, you will not see me in the same light you see me now," he says, knowing he speaks the truth. She tells herself that’s not true, but those words shock her to the very core. She knew it was painful for him to tell her this story, but didn’t realize until this very minute why he had such a hard time telling the story. Not so much because it was painful for him, but because he knew it would be painful for her. It killed her that he thought like that, and wanted to be able to see him in the same light. Yet, he had put the fear in her, and part of her didn’t want to hear the story. But, she knew she had to. She needed to. "I will always love you," she says and he just nods his head. In his heart, he knows it will ruin everything between them, and as he takes a breath in he attempts to steal himself. It’s futile though. Instead of reaching into his mind for the last piece, he reaches into his heart where’s buried the story as much as he could. Yet, he knew this wouldn’t stay buried forever. ---- December 2006 "Craig…," was all she could manage to say, the very sight of him making her almost drop to her knees. He walked towards her, closing the door behind him wanting privacy with his wife. She just looked on, her bottom lip quivering as he tried to find the words to tell her how he felt. "How…," he began, but couldn’t find the words to finish saying what he had to say. It was impossible for him. As he looked at her right then in this moment of time, he was at a loss for words. The betrayal he had felt was immense, a betrayal he had never felt before. The anger seared his body, but even then he was still at a loss for words. She looked at him, fearing what he would say to her, what he would do to her. Yet, he just stood there, lost for the words that he so desperately needed. "Say something. Please," she finally whispers, breaking the minutes of silence. He just looks at her with disbelief in his eyes. "How could you? How could you lie to me?" he asks her, needing a moment to gather himself. "Because, I love you. Because I had loved you for so long and I had made so many mistakes that I couldn’t fix them all. That’s how," she spoke softly, knowing that wouldn’t satisfy Craig. It didn’t as he felt the anger burn inside of him with a passion. "I trusted you. I lost everything and I trusted you when I was told I shouldn’t. Instead, I ignored it and believed in you because you were there for me, because your loyalty never wavered in those first few days. Instead of pushing me away, you stayed there by my side refusing to leave me even when it looked like it was going to be over before it started. Then, you were there time and time again, and I fell in love with you! I LOVED YOU!" he yells at her, beside himself with anger and tears. "I know--," she begins, but he cuts her off. "You knew I was getting my memories back, and yet you never once tried to stop me. Why?" he asked her, lost as to why she had pushed him to do a thing that she knew would only end badly for her. "Because I needed a question answer," she said truthfully. The answer shocked Craig as he looked at her. "And what was that?" he wondered, hoping she could shine some light on it. "An answer I know you don’t have or else you wouldn’t ask me that," she said before she walked over towards him. Without a moment’s notice her lips met his, sending a shiver done his spine. Not one of love, but one of coldness. She continued to kiss him, knowing fully that it would be her last one. There was no survival after this. All the times she had escaped death, she wouldn’t escape it this time. As her lips moved around his, she felt his teeth clamp down on her bottom lip. It wasn’t a playful bite, but it was one that drew blood. The pain was intense. Then, she couldn’t breathe. Craig had wrapped his hands around her throat so tightly that she felt the air cease at that exact moment. Her arms started to tingle as she could feel her body giving up on her. She fell on the bed behind her with Craig still on top of her as he could feel the life being sucked out of her. As she looked back up at him though, she was startled by one thing. The last thing that she would think about. His eyes weren’t in his usual blaze. Instead, they were silent. Empty. Dead. Snap. Her neck broke. Then, there was nothing. The life in her body was gone. Jocelyn was dead. Craig could only look on, and then the tears came. Over and over again. ---- Sunday, May 6th, 2007 Sarah could only look at her father with disbelief. Even though she knew that he was in the right for doing so, this even startled her. She moved slightly away from him and then saw the tears moving down his cheek just as they had done months ago. It was in this instance that she knew she truly loved him. Even though he had done something like this, killing someone with his bare hands when the person couldn’t honestly fight back, she knew that he had to do it. He had buried it so far down, that even with it resurfacing now, it literally was killing him. He just sat there, the tears streaming down his face and she kissed him on the cheek. He had feared losing her, but he hadn’t. She trusted him. She knew he had done the only thing he could have. Jocelyn had once killed Sarah. For some reason Sarah was back and the love from her father was protecting her now. Craig had done what any father would do in his position. Seek revenge upon the person who had taken the most valuable thing in his life. In Craig’s case, it was his daughter. There was no love that could replace that. There was no possible thing Craig could do to lose his daughter’s love. He could care less about a title. He had already gained the most important thing in the world. His daughter’s love. Fin.
