Lines in the Sand

1 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1 FADE IN... [Troy Douglas, PRIME's Intense Champion, just days away from his first defense of that aforementioned title, stands in front of a red, white and blue "SHOOTING STARS AND ARMBARS" banner backstage at the legendary Mid-South Coliseum. Douglas, wearing his traditional ensemble of a black Colossus V t-shirt and khaki shorts -- plus the Intense Title held proudly over his right shoulder -- looks at the camera as if he's thinking about smiling, but opts against it as he narrows his gaze onto the lens.] Troy Douglas: Trust me, Jason, this is a VERY big deal. Now, before we get this whole song-and-dance routine started, we can make this one little thing clear, Natas. Very plainly, very simply, very matter-of-factly, you are NOT the man I want inside that ring this week at ReVolution. I've made no bones about the fact that there is one man, and one man alone, that I am absolutely DYING to get inside that squared circle with. Especially because it seems that's the only way I'm ever going to get my hands on that particular individual. I want to get the man who called me a fraud inside the ring and I want to beat him to the point that he regrets even thinking that he had some sort of claim to the Intense Title. [Almost on instinct, as he mentions his championship he adjusts the belt on his shoulder, seemingly trying to make sure it's secure and isn't going anywhere.] Troy Douglas: Of course, now that I think of it, maybe going all HULK SMASH on that locker room door wasn't the best idea. Even moreso seeing as it got a restraining order slapped on my ass, so if I even look at Kaiser Vashaun the wrong way before I'm allowed to step in the ring with him, C.P. Cantrell's going to strip me of the title, maybe put me in the stocks or have me drawn and quartered. But, I've got a little more self-control then I may have let on the last few weeks, and if I've got to wait to get inside the ring with Kaiser Vashaun, then that's exactly what I'm going to do. After all, good things always do come to those who wait, right? However... [At this, Troy relaxes for a moment, adjusting the title belt once again and easing into a slight smile.] Troy Douglas: That doesn't mean I won't waste the chance to take out a little of the ol' pent-up aggression on a surrogate this week. And Jason, as much as you might want to think I'm overlooking you or I don't think you're a worthy challenger, believe me when I say that none of that bullshit could be any further from the truth. Because when it comes to this Intense Title -- MY Intense Title -- there ain't no such thing as a night off. There is no such thing as an unworthy contender, Jason. Which is why for this week, with your name lined up across from mine on the program, there is no Kaiser Vashaun. There's just Jason Natas, the man who thinks he's going to take my Intense Title from around my waist. And that, Natas, that makes you Public Enemy Number Fucking One in my book. Got it? Because if I'm going to prove that I deserve this belt, I better damn well come out to that ring and unequivocably silence ever wannabe and pretender that thinks he can lay a claim to this belt. Jason, it's just damn unfortunate for you that you have to be the first on that list. [Now fully rolling, the Intense Champion cracks his knuckles before easing his hands back to a comfortable position.] Troy Douglas: You see, Jason, every time I step out in that ring, with MY title on the line, I've got a bit of a modus operandi. When this title is at stake, I draw a line in the sand. And nobody, Jason ... NOBODY ... is going to cross that line. Not Dusk. Not Kaiser Vashaun. Not Jason Natas. Not ANYONE. As far as you're concerned, this belt might as well be inside goddamn Fort Knox, protected by alarms and lasers and motion detectors and trained hulking guards with Uzis. Oh, and a biometric access system. That shit's just kinda cool. [A slight smirk crosses Troy's face at his random bit of absurdism, but he quickly regains a serious demeanor.] Troy Douglas: Understand this with crystal fucking clarity, Jason. I'll say it once, and because I know you've got that whole "attention problem" thing going on, I'll let it out nice and slow. I will do ANYTHING to protect that line in the sand, Jason. Anything. You say I've got to throw five punches for every one of yours? I'll throw ten. You say I've got to hope you don't back up? Son, I'm BEGGING you to get back up, again and again, just so I can't put you back down until you can't take anymore. I know you're tough, Jason. I've been in that ring with you enough times in the last few months to know you're every bit the cold, uncaring badass you like to say you are. But, what I also know is that for absolutely everything you can try and throw at me, I can provide the answer. For every problem, I will be the solution. For every disease, I will be the cure. And for every shitstorm that Jason Natas throws at me, I will be a goddamn force of nature. There's no lengths to which I won't go to preserve this title, Natas. None at all, and if you think you're special just because you're you, then you've got another fucking thing coming, son. When I beat Dusk for this title, he thought I was going to be his night off. His trip through the bottom of the lineup, if you may. He'd beat me, then move on to bigger and better things. I've never made that mistake before, Jason. And trust me, I never will. Every night I get in that ring, every time I'm going to put this title on the line, I'm treating it like the biggest match in the world, no matter who I'm facing. From multi-time Universal Champs to guys on their first nights in, they're all the same to me. They're all aiming to take away what is mine. They're all trying to cross that line in the sand, and Jason, I will KILL to hold that line. I don't want to go that far with you. But, don't think you're going to "wipe me off the fuckin' map", as you so eloquently put. I've been around a long, long time, and no matter how tough you think you are, it's going to take a better man than you to erase me. You get your shot in Memphis, Jason. Make the best of it, because I've got plans to send you right back to the end of the line. Or, better yet, the end of the road. ...FADE TO THE PRIME LOGO
