Frank Miller’s 3

19 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/08/08. 3 A retelling of the battle of Charlotte, 2008 A.D. A pulse of lightning detonated above the Bank of America Stadium, strobing the lights backstage and inflicting a dose of static upon the banks of monitors that surrounded him. Following in its wake, a rumble of thunder churned within the clouds’ swirling belly, before it belched out across the twilight sky, threatening to bring the crumbling heavens crashing down upon North Carolina. Nose tingling with ozone where the blown circuits scorched the air, he swelled his body with a fortifying breath, an animal kingdom act of exerting dominance. He secured his armour, pads encased elbows, tape mummified balled fists, boots laced so tightly they imprint on his shins. PRAISE HUBBARD, was slashed into the tape with red and black ink. The thin cover of black curtain did little to stifle the blood-thirsty howls of the 70,000 enemy at his gates, threats of death and prophesies of maiming slithering through the loose weave of the fabric. He puffed the breath from his nostrils with contempt, the static bristling in the air almost igniting his nasal exhaust into dragon’s flames. His fingers entwined, knuckles cracking in the key of war. On the eve of battle, he retreated into himself… passion, determination, ferocity, desire, all pulled in by the gravity of his focus, before collapsing under their own weight to explode, driving him out through the curtain. A disorienting blizzard of golden confetti engulfed him. He embraced it with arms wide open. Through the golden storm, they fired their first shots, a volley of camera flashes that exploded every scrap of shimmering confetti like a dazzling grenade. Through battened down lids, pin-pricks of light stabbed at his eyes, the darkness he slammed down across his vision amplifying the vitriolic din that filled the bowl around him. YOU’RE GONNA DIE! they grimly forecast. Just as their barbed words, raining down from all sides, threatened to overcome him, his battle cry drove their baying ranks back with its ear-shredding screech. Buoyed by the rousing chorus that flooded past him, he descended into the valley, rank after rank of enemy at either flank, penned behind un-scalable barricades. Barrages of verbal arrows bounced off of him, his ears heavily shielded with chainmail tightly woven of disdain. Stout in his beliefs, he was deaf to the cries denouncing his faith as preposterous. It was there, at the foot of the valley, that he came eye to eye with his foe… the Cyclops. Its brows collapsed under a fierce scowl, shadowing the jagged scar tissue etched into its gouged eye socket. A living tattoo brimmed within his shoulder, a shadowed horse with a lightning bolt of an eye. The Cyclops’ ghastly followers continued to foretell of his demise, alone against a raging, vengeful foe. Come, it hissed, serpent’s tongue fizzing in the heavy air. Come meet your fate. As the enemy legions closed around him, his battle cry faltered, opening up the air for the taunts of the Cyclops’ zealots. DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! DIE! Just because their fanatical words filled the skies didn’t mean that they filled his ears, nor his heart. Hubbard be with me, he uttered, steadying himself. With that declaration of faith pulsing from his heart and throughout his body, he charged, letting loose a roar that crumbled the earth before his feet. Playing hop-scotch upon the fractured ground, he was drawn deeper towards the gravity of the Cyclops scarred, gouged socket. Overcoming the 70,000 dissenting voices weighing him down, he leapt at his foe, arming his hands by balling them into tight fists. A row of tape-encased knuckles ravaged the cheek of the Cyclops, swatting its head to the side and spraying the earth with jaundiced-yellow, festering saliva. Its one eye grew wide at the insolence, shots of blood flashing the red warning sign. As his world closed in around them, only himself and the Cyclops existing, the snickering and sneering of the 70,000 tumbled back out of his ears. Another fist tried to cave in the Cyclops’ skull, unleashing a sharp grunt of thunder above. Before a third blow could land, the Cyclops’ hand thrust out and clasped his throat. Lungs starved of air and head throbbing, his feet were torn from the earth. END HIM! he heard the snivelling followers urge as the outside world came crashing back in… and he went crashing down. Spiked on the back of his head by the Cyclops, the dark, swollen clouds looming above appeared to seep down across his vision. A foot crunched down into his stomach, crumbling the ground beneath him and sending a sweet acidic taste up his throat In the distance, mountains were heard collapsing as the tremors from the Cyclops’ blows resonated through the earth’s crust. Another pummelling blow ground him into the earth, dust clouds coagulating together with the plume of blood expelled from his mouth. Bones broken, but spirit not, he cracked a sticky, crimson smile that perplexed the Cyclops, who remedied the confusion by slamming a fist through his bloodied grin. Yet it remained. Again, the Cyclops’ fist ploughed through the defiant display of crimson-smeared enamel. Yet it remained. Its knuckles flayed to the bone, the clubbing fist of the Cyclops recoiled to deliver the killer blow, through his grinning teeth, through the back of his skull and through the earth’s crust below. The ground beneath him, his bruised and swollen flesh, his teeth, his hair, the Cyclops’ fist, all stained red. Then, just as the Executioner was about to drop his death-blow, the air behind him was infected with the same red hue. Her body engulfed in raving, raging scarlet hellfire, she entwined herself around the Cyclops. The sickening stench of scorched flesh and burnt hair hung in the dusty air, air that was shredded with an agonised wail from the Cyclops. Tighter his fire-consumed saviour constricted, the searing of flesh threatening to slice. Stretching towards the heavens all around them, the Cyclops’ helpless followers could only pour their scorn down upon the scene, their unfaltering faith unable to accept their eyes’ interpretation. Some convinced themselves it was trickery, an illusion. Witchcraft. His broken body held together by frayed muscle fibres, he planted a firm foot in the blood soaked, battle ravaged soil. The sole of his boot taking solid roots in the earth, he lifted his weary frame from the shallow grave he’d been pummelled in to. Another pained roar emanated from deep within the Cyclops, his mouth barely able to contain the eruption. The ominous, swollen clouds above succumbed to the Cyclops’ slashing cry, releasing their rain in one almighty deluge. With the Cyclops’ feverish restraints extinguished by the downpour, he burst free of his shackles, flinging her into the fractured ground. A bolt of lightning hammered the earth beside him, sending rocks and dirt flying in every direction. The elements at the behest of Cyclops, another blinding flash heralded a second bolt. As he leapt, the scorched air around the bombardment broiled his propelling leg, leaving him crashing down, face-first in the rubble. Watching the torrential downpour fill up the rocky bowl around his face, the Cyclops let out a guttural, belittling laugh. The rain ceased, and steam rose up from the hot rocks at the Cyclops’ feet. Gritty brown rainwater filled his lungs. The Cyclops’ foot ground into the back of his head. It was over. Or so he thought. A fierce beam of vivid white light tore a swath through the low, gloomy clouds. As they evaporated, the full glory of the brilliant white was unleashed, channelling its radiant focus into the Cyclops’ eye. The grim waste ground of the battlefield was engulfed in the fierce platinum glow, the Cyclops and his 70,000 heckling zealots brought to their knees. Senses were dazzled, synapses overloaded. A thick, brown soup was expelled from his lungs as his hand slapped down around a rock. Her ravenous flame reignited, the red binds again tied the Cyclops, while beside her, luminous white toes touched down upon the soil. Within the platinum aura, greenery flourished up through the cracks, muddied water was filtered crystal clear and the flesh of his leg, grotesque from burns, regenerated under fresh skin. Revitalised, he was lifted by the incandescent air itself. It’s over, he decreed. The blinded, bound Cyclops at his feet, and its 70,000 followers silenced, he brought the final blow down upon it. Rock crunched through bone, and as the Cyclops collapsed onto the glistening earth, the blood gushing from its head coupled with the breath venting from his followers, leaving all lifeless and empty. With fresh blood staining his hands, he stood side by side by side with the red and the white. 70,001 defeated by the brave, glorious 3. The historical accuracy of the above events is still open to debate…
