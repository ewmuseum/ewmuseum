The (Future) Champs Are Here!

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 1st, 2005 Seated on the couch in their living room, Clyde and James stared aimlessly at the TV as Walkins continuously tapped a button on the remote control, cycling through endless infomercials and advertisements for yeast infection creams, before coming to a channel that captured his attention. “Are you a weak person routinely faced with abnormally difficult challenges? If so, God might hate you. But we have good news, because there’s a new reality series coming to FOX that will play to your strengths. Introducing When Life Attacks! We take those harsh everyday challenges and pit you against them in a series of increasingly complicated tasks. If you can outwit, outlast, and outkill the competition, you’ll win our grand prize. If not, well…at least you amused the folks at home.” Cut to three men and one woman huddled together inside a labyrinth of some sort. Two of the men are the typically buff contestants you’d see on reality shows, with the third being a seventy-year-old war vet. The woman may as well not have a head at all, as she’s just blatant eye candy. “Ok,” one of the buff guys says. “If we’re gonna get out of this maze, we have to work together. Now, the announcer said that there’s a Minotaur to the west, and a Centaur to the east, so if we stay right here in the middle we should be pretty safe. Centaurs and Minotaurs are natural enemies, so once they catch a whiff of each other they’ll engage in an epic battle and we’ll be able to slip out the south end.” “Like, you’re the first guy I’ve met that doesn’t want to slip in the south end for once,” the leggy blond pipes up. And at that exact moment, an arrow pierces the head of the silent buff guy, as a war cry can be heard in the distance. “It has begun. The ‘Taurs are engaging in battle,” the lone buff guy whispers in growing panic, as the body of his friend crumples to the ground. “And they have even worse aim than the legends foretold. We’re right in the line of fire!” “Like, I once went to see this movie called Line Of Fire with my friends. And, like, we were sitting in our seats when, like, this bitch in the front row starts talking on her cell phone. And I’m like, hello, you know? Like, whatever.” Yeah, annoying. “I say we use her and the dead guy as human shields, and get the hell out of here,” the vet says, motioning to the only other guy left alive. “If there’s enough left to salvage after we’re out, you and me can take turns with her body.” The buff guy takes a second to look over the blond, then promptly agrees. As he makes a move to grab the dead guy’s body, the withered old bastard clubs him in the back of the head with his army boot, before slinging both men’s bodies over one shoulder. “Dumb bastard. Gus eats tonight!” Ambling off, the old man now known simply as Gus leaves the blond alone for a few short moments, then comes walking back in. Silent for a second, he scratches his head with his free hand, opens his mouth to say something, but then just brains her with the boot as well, slinging her over his free shoulder and disappearing back out of the camera’s view. “Yeah…we’re just not even trying anymore…” Back to The Forsaken. “Hmm,” Clyde mused for a moment. “Looks promising. I loves me the reality TV.” His head slowly turned to look at James, a creepy circus-esque grin spreading across his face. “I wish I could watch it all the time.” Farwell hung his head in quiet disappointment. “I wish I’d stop waking up.” Ohhh! It’s Clyde! And James!And all their crazy games!Clyde does crap, James gets mad,Hits Clyde in the head with a baseball bat! Their crazy misadventures,Always seem to be the norm!And when a week goes by without excitement,We’ll just make another clip show! When everyone else is all the same,And even watching Simpsons starts to feel gay,You need a show that isn’t lame!James and Clyde, Clyde…and…James! Looking to have been standing around for several hours, Clyde and James held position near the front of the line for some Comic-Con rip-off, waiting for the general public to be allowed inside the building. “I wish someone would’ve told us it isn’t first-come, first-serve, so we didn’t wait in line for three days…” James muttered to himself, pissed. As people were slowly being let in, Clyde pushed his way through a group of handicapped children looking to get some autographs, stealing a Star Wars hat off some drooling wheelchair-bound monkey in the process. Wanting to keep an eye on his own retard, James followed as close behind Clyde as possible, finally finding him just inside the doors. “You can’t just run off like that. You could get molested again, or worse,” Farwell scolded, looking around the room for any NHL stars. Seeing none, he could breathe easy for a few minutes. “But look at all the sweet stuff I got already!” Clyde shoved out armfuls of memorabilia and other such merchandise with wide eyes. “Where’d you get all that?” Farwell asked, then remembered the ‘tard incident and realized the answer before Walkins could respond. “Goddamn it, Clyde. They have cameras here. Now stay close, because this is a big place and I don’t want you to get suckered into buying crap. Look around, everyone’s trying to sell you something,” James motioned to all the vendors in the building. “Even Jesus.” Cut to Jesus standing beside a table with various bottles of all sizes on it. On the bottles, as well as the sign above the table, are the words ‘Christ’s Magical Healing Cream.’ “What the hell is this?!” Jesus yells at the guy standing beside him. “I told you to put ‘Christ’s Miracle Healing Cream’ on the label! I’m Jesus. I perform miracles, not magicals, dammit!” “But, if you’re Jesus,” his partner meekly chimes in, “can’t you just change the label yourself?” “If I were a wizard, perhaps.” Jesus’ eyes narrow. “You dick.” And then he swipes the bottles clear off the table with one arm, while his partner begins to openly weep. Back to The Forsaken. Again. “You worry too much,” Clyde attempted to reassure James by waving him off, but only succeeded in spilling half his ill-gotten loot. “It’s not like I’m gonna run off and buy the first thing I see with your Mastercard.” “I have a Mastercard?” James asked, genuinely puzzled. But before he could get an answer, Clyde had already taken off in the opposite direction, spotting a bin marked ‘Free Pokémon Cards’. “My prayers have been answered!” he screamed, turning back to James. “Quick, lend me your arms!” The next several minutes consisted of frenzied grabbing and stuffing. Which would sound gay in an Ellis Easton promo, and justifiably so. But not here. Not now. Not ever. … They’re not gay, dammit. “Yours is the biggest I’ve ever seen!” Clyde cried in excitement. And I feel compelled to reiterate the fact that they’re not gay. But they’re not making it easy for me to defend them, either… Pointing to Farwell’s pile of Pokémon cards, Clyde gleefully giggled in excitement at the prospect of having the Most. Cards. Ever. Around them, people just walked by, wondering what a fifty-year-old man was doing luring some hapless twelve-year-old lad into his predatory grasp with the promise of outdated worthless cards, as Clyde jumped into James’ arms. James immediately pushed free of Clyde’s completely heterosexual embrace, but the damage was done. Security was upon them within a few quick seconds. “Is this child your son?” The first of three security guards eyed Farwell suspiciously. Moving his hand towards the gun holstered to his belt, the guard made a move to step between James and Clyde. “No, he’s not my son,” James replied, irritated. “And he’s not my grandson, either. In case you were gonna ask…” “What’s your relation to the boy?” the second guard asked, backing up the first as the third checked out a group of teenage girls walking by. James was getting pretty annoyed, while Clyde remained quiet, shielded by the body of the first security guard. “He’s hardly a boy. He’s forty-four, for Christ’s sake!” “How old are you, son?” the first guard asked, turning to face Clyde as the second guard moved up behind James. Meanwhile, the third started checking out some teenage boys walking by. “I’m this many years old,” Clyde responded, holding up two hands with all ten fingers spread out. Closing them each into a fist, Walkins opened and closed them three more times, only holding up four fingers on one hand the last time. “Well, his story checks out,” the first guard called over to the other two. “Sorry to be a bother.” Waving off his teammates, he nodded to James before disappearing into wrestling promo obscurity forever. “Thank God I’m not Muslim, or those guards would’ve been on us like stink on…well, I guess stink on a Muslim,” James smiled smugly at his racist quip, knowing he wouldn’t be the one to take the heat for it if somebody gets offended. “MUSLIM?!” some old woman screamed in panic, before hitting the dirt. “Get down! We’re all gonna die!” And then everyone else followed suit. Even Clyde. Until only James was standing. Security did visit him again. And what a visit it was. After two long and very uncomfortable hours, Farwell limped bull-legged out of the security office. Clyde’s excited grin did nothing to lift his mood. “What do you want to do most of all in the world?” Clyde asked, growing more and more enthusiastic by the second. James didn’t share his enthusiasm. “I just want to lay down somewhere and die…” Clyde was taken aback for a moment, but his smile quickly returned. “What’s the second thing you want to do most of all in the world?” “Forget this day ever happened,” came the only response. “Well, I’ve got the next best thing! I entered the both of us in a lightsaber contest!” Clyde said louder than necessary, holding up their entry numbers. “I’m number one, and you’re number two. We’re like Pee and Poo!” “Why do I have to be Poo?” James whined, then realized he didn’t even want to be involved in some stupid American Gladiators rip-off with the Star Wars logo on it. “How about you just be both, and I’ll go wait for you in the car.” “But, pleeaase,” Clyde begged, breaking out the puppy dog eyes. “We’re up next, and all you have to do is take a fall. Then I can win the Chocolate Wookie.” “The Chocolate Whatie?” Farwell couldn’t believe two such words could ever exist side by side, but there they were. “It’s the Star Wars CW Lightsaber Battle™! CW stands for Chocolate Wookie. And Clyde Walkins. It’s a sign from God! I must have it…” Clyde’s tone suddenly became very serious. “I need its powers.” “What powers?” The Innovator of Old School asked. But before he could get an answer, Clyde had already stapled the number two sign to his chest. “Jesus-fucking God!” James cried out in pain. “What did you do that for?!” “Because we’re up next, and if we don’t show up I’ll get disqualified. If you really don’t want to do this, just take a dive and we both go home happy.” Clyde smiled to cement the point that he really wanted the Chocolate Wookie. “I’m not doing the job for you in any damn thing, Clyde. Besides, once I win I’ll just give you the damn Chocolate Wookie. It’s not like I want it. Hell,” James said, “you’re lucky I’m even thinking about doing this in the first place.” “Fine then,” Clyde’s eyes narrowed. “I have ways of making you lose…” James just watched as Clyde walked up to the stage, then sighed to himself. “How the hell do I get myself into these things?” The thought stayed in his mind all the way from the moment he started limping to the battle area, to the moment he was suited up and ready to do battle with Clyde, American Gladiators jousting-style. The battle was fierce and long, but in the end the victor was the more deserving man. Actually, I lied. Five seconds into the match, Clyde reached into the waistline of his pants and pulled a small knife on James, holding his foam padded “lightsaber” jousting stick just below his stomach while thrusting the weapon towards his tag team partner. The knife was only visible to James and Clyde, but while the “lightsaber” blocked the view of everyone watching as to what was really happening, it made it look like Clyde was jerking off at James. In fact, the announcer even made reference to what it looked like as James yelled out, “I don’t need this shit,” dropped his jousting stick, and walked away. The following words shall forever be etched into the minds of the fans watching what they thought would be wholesome family fun: “The first match in the opening round of the Star Wars CW Lightsaber Battle™ should prove to be as wild as a barrel full of half-naked teenagers. Monkeys. A barrel full of half-naked monkeys. Oh God, I’m gonna lose my job for this…” And after that guy was replaced by a sixty-year-old woman, and the first few matches came and went, the epic battle between Clyde and James was set to take place. “Now this fight is between a father and son named…Jamesy McDickhead? That can’t be right. Is it Dykehead, or Deekhead? Deekhead, I think. Anyway, McDeekhead will be battling his son, Clyde The Conqueror, in the last match of our first round.” And after both men were prepared to do battle, and James had yelled that he wasn’t Clyde’s father, and the match had officially started, Clyde pulled the weapon that triggered this reaction: “The young boy appears to be reaching into his pants for someth-…oh dear. I…I think he’s…oh God, he is. He’s interfering with himself! Right in front of everyone! And his grandfather’s had enough! Jamesy McDeekhead has just forfeited the match in the wake of what shall forever be known as the darkest day in lightsaber competition history.” Three rounds later, and Clyde was in the finals. Facing off against the best lightsaber battler known to man, Pyro from American Gladiators dressed up like Boba Fett, Clyde had planned on using the tactic that brought him to the game. He was going to shank Boba Fett. Little did he know, however, that a certain NHL legend with the initials MM was in the crowd, and had snuck in a discreet fondling as Clyde passed by him, jarring the weapon loose. It fell on the floor a few feet away and was soon collected by the only NHL star to appear on OSW’s End Game, who promptly tucked it into his coat after a quick sniff. Clyde knew he was screwed when the blows started making him see red. And it wasn’t from anger, either. “My eyes have the stigmata!” Clyde screamed out as another shot hit him right in the middle of the forehead. It was only at that point that James noticed former American Gladiator Nitro was helping Pyro by jabbing Clyde in the back of the legs whenever he came near. Rushing up to inform the ref, James accidentally clubbed him in the back of the head when he tripped on the edge of the stage, sending the ref face first to the floor. Seizing the opportunity, Clyde ripped the foam padding off his ‘saber, revealing the metal pole within. While Pyro played up to the fans, Clyde turned and smacked Nitro upside the head, finishing the American Gladiator off once and for all. Waiting as his newfound nemesis turned back around, Clyde stayed on his knees and drove the metal pole straight up into the unusually sturdy package of Pyro. As the second American Gladiator fell to his knees, a bloody-eyed Clyde stood and raised the pole into the air. “There can be only one.” And with a mighty swing, and a loud clunk, Clyde felled the man known as Pyro. Half-assedly placing the protective foam padding back on the lightsaber, Clyde ran over to help James in assisting the referee to his feet, pointing to the unconscious body of Pyro. Looking at the grinning idiot in front of him, then the downed Gladiator, the dazed official had no choice but to award the match to Clyde The Conqueror. After receiving his twelve-inch Chocolate Wookie in a golden wrapper, Clyde raised the prize high into the air, amidst cheers from the crowd. Cheers for a cheater, you ask? No. Cheers for a child who did what it took to even the odds between him and more experienced adults. Cheers for a mentally deficient boy who overcame adversity and proved to the world that a plucky spirit can be all that you need to achieve glory. Cheers for a kid who made us believe in ourselves. … Ok, so they were a bunch of redneck wrestling fans and Star Wars geeks who wanted a cheap thrill. And Clyde gave it to them. At that moment, he was their God. Five minutes later, a free viewing of Batman Begins was their God. As for Clyde, well, a day later an official from the Children’s Make-A-Wish Foundation called him about being their spokesman-slash-pity machine. The whole ‘everyone’s dream can come true’ kind of thing. From what happened at the Star Wars CW Lightsaber Battle™, they obviously figured he was handicapped in some way, which would make for great publicity. But did Clyde accept? Well, that’s a story for another time. Like…next week. Stay tuned, bitches. Foreskin out.
