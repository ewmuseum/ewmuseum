Date: Who cares April 10th, 2007 Location: 35,000 feet in the air, en route to Utah When Devin Shakur closed his eyes, the only thing that he could see was the shape of a leather belt. The flashback was so familiar to him: In his father’s bedroom, the blue sheets on the bed, the wooden nightstand over on the left of the bed, the giant phone on the right side that hung on the wall, and straight ahead the window in which he could look across the street and see the faces of happy people… A people he would never become part of. The only sound that he could hear was the belt being whipped into his back. The hand of his father holds the weapon with an iron vice. No remorse, no regret, no sorrow, no mercy. There is no way to convince Anthony Shakur that doing this to his three year old son is wrong. Because Anthony is guarding the keys to the gate of heaven with his life Devin will never go there if he has anything to say about it. Anthony, once a devout Puritan, has come to the point of refusing to be under someone else’s jurisdiction, even one that falls under the demographic of supernatural. Anthony took the fact that Devin would awaken him from his blissful sleep, everyday, as a sign. In order to get what he wanted, Anthony could no longer relinquish power to someone up above. To deepen the wounds to the degree that he wanted, he had to seize the power within his own two hands. Anthony would have to become God. He would have to control life and death. He would have to declare the beating of his son to be a good thing, and anybody who questioned him to be evil. Devin never understood why he could see those little lines on his back days after a beating. He could never understand why the stinging sensation would be there in the morning. He never understood the miracle of red drops coming from inside of his skin out onto the surface. He never understood why his father’s strikes got harder and harder after seeing tears running the front of his son’s face. Devin never knew what it would take to make his father stop. On this day, he tried to move away from the next lash. This move surprised his father. The look of shock on his face could tell the entire story. This was not something Devin was supposed to learn…Ever. The flashback ends with a lash being sent right between his young eyes. =================================================== December 18th, 2006 Location: Raleigh, North Carolina Devin had rehearsed this part many times in his head. The call would not take more than ten minutes out of his day, and yet still his hands were shaking. The previous three times he was unable to push the Talk button on his cellular phone. Calling a mobster to tell him that you were going to be the guy that killed his enemy, when in reality you were working for two other groups at the same time, shouldn’t be this hard right? Devin once again took the phone in his hand, gave it a glaring look, as if to try and exorcise the fear out of his body, and started to press the numbers one more time. The procedure took no longer than two seconds, and before his mind could tell him to stop, the Talk button had been pressed. “FUCK!” Devin looked up into the mirror to see a reflection of his own image. Needless to say, he did not like what was staring back at him: A man scared out of his mind. A man scared of someone else. A man scared of death. “Ha, I’m fucking death proof.” This brought another one of those macabre grins upon his face. “Hello?” A heavy New York accent inquired, breaking into Devin’s ego stroking paradise. Devin quickly brought himself back into character. “Is this Michael DeAngelis’s office?” “Nope, this is Roselli’s Italian restaurant. No Michael DeAngelis here.” “Are you positive?” “This restaurant has been here for generations, son. I’d know about a Michael DeAngelis if he ever walked in here.” “So I’ve been given a wrong number?” “I’m afraid so.” “Funny, because my people don’t give me wrong numbers” Devin’s eyebrows went up immediately after he spoke. Did that just come out of his mouth? “Well son, I’m afraid they did this time. Now beat i-“ “What if I said the name Anthony Gambillini, would that mean something to you?” There was a pause on the other end of the line. Devin hoped that he did not scare away whoever was on the phone. Christian had never gotten something wrong before… “…Depends on what’s behind the name.” The thick accent replied. Bingo “What if I said I would put him out of commission? Would that get me any closer to Michael DeAngelis?” “It could…What’s your name, kid?” “Devin Shakur. I’m a representative of a name that Mr. DeAngelis might be familiar with, Christian Daniels.” “Hold on a second” The man was sitting in one of the back offices of the restaurant in his reclining chair. He got up and walked down one of the halls and pushed open a door to find a man that had all the looks of a Soprano character sitting there with his hands folded across the desk. Michael DeAngelis, a man who could once strike fear in the hearts of people around the world, was reduced to a shamble of a man holding onto the dreams of the past, hoping for a miracle to enter his life. “What is it Mario?” DeAngelis said, looking rather bothered that someone could just walk into his office without having the common courtesy to knock. Mario swallowed hard and tugged at his collar. “There’s someone on the phone for you, boss, says that he works on behalf of Christian Daniels.” A smirk that took the clock back to the old days was etched upon Michael DeAngelis’s face. “Patch him through” Mario walked down to his office and pressed a button to transfer through the call over to his boss. The smirk still remained on DeAngelis’s face. The same smirk was etched on Shakur’s face a good six hundred miles away. They both had the same thought running through their head. He has no idea what he’s gotten himself into. Michael picked up the phone and got back into business mode. “Mr. Shakur, I’ve been expecting your call.” “Have you now?” “Or rather I have been awaiting a call from your representative. I’m in no way happy with the lack of time that has transpired between our first exchange in July and now.” “Well they have to make sure that the right person comes along for the job. You wouldn’t want any of this traced back to you now would you?” DeAngelis had to smirk at the verve that flew from the mouth of Devin. It brought him back to a time when he was as bold as Devin, with the thought that nothing in the world could touch him. “No, Mr. Shakur that is the last thing we want at such a critical stage in the project. Let’s skip the bullshit. Tell me why you make the perfect candidate for this job?” This part was verbatim from Devin’s head, precise and to the point. “I’m good at what I do. You need someone who can finish the job because your own people can’t catch him long enough to put an end to his life, right? I will have no problem hunting him down away from the cameras and finishing him. I could even make it…look like an accident.” This was what DeAngelis had wanted back when he first heard about his father handing the legacy down to Gambillini instead of himself. “An accident you say…I like that. Let’s go with that.” “How do you want it? Bullet accidentally in the chamber, trips over something and falls over the balcony, poison, get somebody to befriend him and eventually put the screws to him?” That last part was a trap that Shakur was hoping DeAngelis would bite on. “I’ve got the inside person in there already. The shmuck doesn’t suspect a thing?” “Who is it? Perhaps I can work off of them?” “Violet Rayne” That was one answer he did not expect to hear. He thought that Violet was working undercover as a CIA agent under a different name. “What’s her objective?” “To give me his whereabouts, which I can give to you and allow you to finish the job.” “That seems like a good plan.” “We can’t just do it like that though, the last team that tried that ending up failing miserably. When she gives me the coordinates then I’ll give them to you. I want you to wait for the right opportunity, so you will have to follow him everywhere that he goes. This is not going to be a one night operation.” “That shouldn’t be a problem. I can even follow him around in that wrestling company that he travels with.” “How are you going to do that?” “Well let’s just say that I already took the liberty of signing up for there and soon I will be on their official roster.” Michael DeAngelis could not contain the laughter this time. He was now convinced that this was karma’s way of repaying him back for all of the bad things that had happened to him recently. He now knew that the wait was well worth it. “Mr. Shakur, you’ve got yourself a deal, and just so you know…Money will not be a problem. You get the job done and you can have whatever your heart desires.” “It will be a pleasure to do business with you Mr. DeAngelis.” “The same with you Devin, the same with you, can you start in a couple of days by chance?” “With a private jet at my disposal, the only thing that I need is the when and the where.” “I’ll call you with the information then.” “I’ll be waiting for it.” The two hung up on their respective ends and smiles were all around. Michael DeAngelis because he thought it was too good to be true, without knowing the ulterior part of that cliché. Devin Shakur because he knew that this would be easy… And the other person listening in on the call who knew it would be easy, who knew it was too good to be true, and who would use that fact to his advantage when taking out DeAngelis at a date of his choosing… Christian Daniels “Boy done good…Boy done good” ================================================== December 21st, 2006 Location: Columbus, Ohio Tony Gamble was riding a tidal wave of momentum. He had just come off a victory over Angelo Deville, a feat that nobody would have expected out of him at such a young age in his PRIME career. He now went from being on the cusp of Main Event superstar to full blown Pay-Per-View headliner set on a crash course with Nova. The media circus that would happen is the perfect cover. Today was just a small example. Fans flocked to him while he was sitting in the auto dealership for this public appearance. Little kids looked up to him as the next big star, and some of the mothers of said children looked at him as the next fuck that they could get in their bed. Violet Rayne was not with him, and it was probably for good reason. She was the one who had tipped DeAngelis off about the whereabouts of Gamble, and in turn DeAngelis got into contact with Shakur. Now Devin sat across the street at Starbucks, faux drinking a cup of ten dollar trash that someone gave a fancy name to. It was exhilarating looking into the eyes of the man you would soon put six feet into the ground. No real words could provide an accurate depiction. With every passing fan that came through, with every hand that he shook, with every pair of excited eyes that Gamble looked into, Devin Shakur was typing away in his mental computer. He could tell that there was a small sense of paranoia about Gamble. He was only doing this because Chet Worth was demanding it of him as the new superstar that would soon grace the covers of many magazines. Gamble had the fear that someone would get him at a public event when he least expected it. He would be the wrestling world’s Robert Kennedy, struck down on the verge of greater aspirations. Unfortunately there would be no Sirhan Sirhan, no woman in a polka dot dress, and no secret tapes of government officials telling witnesses that they did not see what they saw. It is hard to be a man that soon everybody is going to hate, right Tony? On the surface you will relish in the fact that you are what causes so many men to seethe. Yet behind the charade of cameras, the nights that you used to cherish in a bed behind a locked door will be spent crouched in a corner. Whenever your vehicle stops outside of a place that you will soon enter, those beady eyes will wonder if two people are going to Paul Castellano your ass by shooting you up while you are still inside the car. I will make you focus on every other person Tony Until you are so scared that your shadow causes you to cower in fright… And that is when I strike. Then when they want to find out who did it, the possibilities will be endless. Nobody will ever think that it is the quiet one…Because nobody ever does…Until it is too late. The flash of a picture being taken by a mother of her son and Gamble is the last thing Shakur’s eyes see before he stands up and exits out the front door of the Starbucks. ================================================== April 10th, 2007 Location: 35,000 feet in the air, en route to Utah Devin awoke because the analgesics were starting to wear off. The pain that his hand was in could not be described, not to mention the fact that he had to change a good portion of his lifestyle after his sweet Sunny put two bullets into his ribs. For the first time in a long time, she was officially on the backburner along with Jonathan Winters, Pierce Lavelle, Dusk, and Tony Gamble. The one person that was making his way into Devin’s thoughts went by the name of Asa Fountain. The Champion that Devin had broken his hand just to get a shot at. This would have to be a moment that Devin could not screw up. The attack was definitely going to come quickly on the two injured areas. The hand would not be hurt as much as the ribs due to the protection of a spica cast. The ribs were helpless. Devin knows that he is going to suffer, but suffering only strengthens the resolve. When we step into that ring Asa… I’m not going to show remorse. I’m not going to show regret. I’m not going to show sorrow. I’m not going to show mercy It has taken me this long to get here because I have been under the jurisdiction of a boss, be it Blaine Blair or Chet Worth. Yet in our match, there are no rules and there are no regulations. I am sick of letting other people put my fate into their hands. I am sick of having to relive the moments in which I have been oppressed. Nobody will whip me anymore, Asa. You will be the child that was me…And I will become my father. I will light up your skin and make the scars come days later. I will make the stinging sensation be there in the morning. I will open the crowd’s eyes to the miracle of cutting epidermis to make blood appear. I will make you helpless before my onslaught. Finally, I will take that gold piece of justification off of your shoulder. At Revolution 124, Asa… I’m playing God.
