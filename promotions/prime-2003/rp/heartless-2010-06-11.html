Heartless.

11 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 11, 2010 The Universal Title. I’ve been trying to secure it since my first day in this company. But I always fell short. Look at Olsig…he’s good…but he’ll never be great. Emilio Rage. Hate to say it, but he’s like a brother. Both of us began PRIME, competing weekly in the Alias League. He beat me…and left. But I stayed, and managed to snag the Intense Championship. Made it my own. At ReVolution 94, he returned…for one night only…to rid me of that honor. But he failed. I beat him. Beginning, Middle…and now the climax. The Universal Championship. The Main Event. A pay-per-view glorifying violence…my forte. I have to win. The stars are aligned. The script is perfect. First, the Dual Halo…now, the Universal Championship. This victory single-handedly could set me above the Hoyt Williams…above the Novas….above the Sonny Silvers…above the Lindsay Troys…above the Igantius Lisieuxs… My greatest foe stands between me and my greatest triumph. And backing him, a newcomer…hardly fit to participate in such a prestigious battle. Stevie said ‘You can’t say you shall and not fight through hell…’ Winning is impeccable. In one night, this Hall of Famer SHALL become a full-blown LEGEND. And I’ll gladly fight through hell to achieve such. --- Airport. Ultraviolence is fast approaching and with it, one of the biggest opportunities of his career. The chance to solidify his name as one of the greatest ever to compete inside of the squared circle. With determination brewing in his eyes, he yanks up his baggage and prepares to head for the plane. "Mr. Olsig…" comes an authoritative voice from Olsig’s right. "if you would, come with us, sir." "Sorry, buddy, but I have a plane to c-…" "Mr. Olsig, this is not a request." The officer insists, his voice a bit more stern than before. "Please come with us…now." "Your way with words, charming as it may be, doesn't negate the fact that I really have to catch this plane. So, if you'll excu-…" "Mr. Olsig, this is a matter of federal exigency, so we're not going to reiterate…please come with us…now." Olsig’s face spells confusion. "Federal exigency? To what degree?" "We were only given orders to bring you in, sir." "And this $300 plane ticket? I guess I'll just have to charge it to the game, huh?" "I'm sure Mrs. Ramos will be happy to reimburse you for all expenses." That name hit Vangelus like a ton of bricks. He inclines his ear, as if they deceive him. "I'm sorry, who?" "Mrs. Ramos…Mr. Olsig, we really have to g-…" "Ramos? As in Glenda Ramos? Chief Executive of the Colombian National Police?" "Precisely. I'm sure she'll be happy to answer all of your questions…now if you would, follow us." --- "No, Stephens, I know exactly what I'm doing. If we don't extinguish this thing now, it may…no, scratch that…it will spiral much too far out of our control." The office of Glenda Ramos, Chief Executive of the Bogotá Police Force. She’s an older woman, but one look at her and you’ll know that she exudes power and esteem. Her eyes are cutting, her shape is firm…her demeanor along demands the utmost respect. Looks didn’t get her here…ambition did. Olsig walks in, still rattled by the request of his presence and still accompanied by the officer from the airport. "Right. I understand. Listen, I'm going to have to call you back…a very important 'client' just walked in. Yeah, him. Bye, Stephens." She hangs up and greets Olsig with a very telling smile. "Vangelus Olsig…how honored I am to see you." She extends her hand, they greet. "No need to be, Mrs. Ramos." "Non-Sense. Don't think I haven't been keeping up with you, sir. Why, you've gone over to the states and made a name for yourself. Being inducted into the PRIME Hall of Fame is one thing, but winning the Triple Halo?" "Dual Halo." "Yeah, that too! No doubt, I speak for the entire city of Bogotá when I say that we are proud to call Vangelus Olsig our own." "I'm grateful." She smiles and nods…even more telling than before. With an outstretched arm, she motions for Olsig to have a seat before taking a seat of her own behind her mammoth desk. "Tell me, how's life over in the states?" "It's fine. Can't complain." "And your career? How's it treating you?" "It's fine…still no complaints." "And the w-…" "It's fine. Mrs. Ramos, please pardon my haste, but I really am curious as to why you've brought me here. I hesitate to believe that it was simply for personal adulation of my recent achievements which you seem…oddly…invested in." An expression of both artificial warmth and slight offense quickly develops on her face. "Why, with that tone, I'd think you were bothered by my request." "Well, I di-…" "But if you're dying to know, I guess we can skip the complimentary small talk. There is a reason for your being here…one of grave importance. Vangelus Olsig…I need a favor." Olsig tilts his head slightly. "A favor? What could an aging, semi-retired and often emo-labeled professional wrestler such as myself do for you…the chief executive of the Bogotá police force?" "Well, Vangelus I-…." She pauses and glares at the officer who posed at full attention near the door. "Excuse me Officer Berez…would you mind giving us the room?" He nods and makes a speedy exit. "Now…it's funny that you should mention your occupation, Vangelus, because from what I understand, you weren't always an aging, semi-retired, far-too-emotionally-driven, overly-tattooed, borderline-depressant, ki-…." "Ummm…" "Sorry…heh." She says, chuckling in an embarrassed manner. She clears her throat. "But as I was saying, you weren't always this person. You embarked on that childhood dream of becoming a professional wrestler right here in Colombia…but only after you wrote the final chapter of your previous occupation." Olsig looks at her, obviously keen to the direction this conversation is headed and non-too-delighted by such. "I know what you're referring to, and I sincerely hope that is not what you've brought me here for, because if it is, I regret to inform you….I don't do that anymore." "Oh come on, Vangelus. You were one of the sharpest, most strategic, and downright ballsiest consultants to ever grace this bureau. There are criminals still sitting behind bars because you, with your deducing skills and keen observation, helped put them there. That's not a learned trait…that's an embedded one, which tells me that you've still got it. People don't lose abilities like that. They just don't." Olsig leans in closer as to make his point perfectly clear. "Maybe I do, maybe I don't, it doesn't matter anyway because I…DON'T…DO…THAT…ANYMORE. End of discussion." She draws a blank, as if her options have been exhausted. "And from the stone look on your face, dare I say end of meeting." Olsig forcefully pushes himself out of his seat and prepares to exit. "Vangelus, wait. At least let me explain to you the situation. Maybe it'll soften your heart." He looks at her and sighs. "Doubtful…but try me." He responds, easing back down into his seat. "Hugo Moreno. He wants my job. Join the club, right? I mean, there are dozens of people out there who want my job. A quick survey of the hell I walk through on a daily basis, and you can't help but ask why…but they do. This guy, however, is a bit more ruthless than the others. While they want to take me down, he really wants to take me down, and for no other reason than to be my successor. What makes him scarier than the rest is that he's managed to rally both supporters and the press behind him, further strengthening his agenda." Olsig looks on, neither moved nor completely turned off. "However…recently I've heard chatter that Mr. Moreno is hiding a certain secret that, if exposed, would cripple his campaign. It'd sweep the rug right from underneath his feet." "And you want me to find out what that secret is so you can keep a job that you yourself has deemed to be hell." "Only in the most flattering terms. It's certainly no pleasant endeavor, but someone…the right one…has to do it. This police force has been feeding off of my breast milk for too long…if I weaned it now, it'd crumble." "…We are speaking hypothetically here, right?" "Vangelus, this department has bore witness to much triumph with yours truly at the helm. This Moreno fellow wants to come in with a life full of secrets and scandals and rid me as well as this bureau of everything we've worked thus far to achieve. He's built a false image that many have bought into, but he has several compressed secrets that all deem him unfit for this office. The public and the media can't see that, however…in order to win this battle, we must show it to them, stuff it down there throat, so they can't deny the fact that underneath the prince charming guise lies a ravenous beast." "You mean you must stuff it down there throat. Look, I sympathize with your dilemma, Mrs. Ramos…I'd even string a few sad melodies on a violin if I had one…but for the last time, I don't do that anymore. That part of my life is long gone." Once again, he pushes himself out of his seat. "And speaking of long gone, I believe I shall be as well. Truly an honor meeting you, Mrs. Ramos. Good luck with the case." And with that, the Bogotá native progresses towards the door. "Vangelus, I wouldn't walk out of that door if I were you." He stops and turns around, as if amused by the pseudo-threat. "And why not? Is Moreno, the ravenous beast, standing on the other side?" She pushes herself out of her seat as well, emerging from behind her desk. "No, but a beast far more ravenous is. Look, Vangelus, I tried to play it easy with you, but since you insist on playing hardball, let me show you just how far my sleeves can roll up." He glares at her, both confused and amused. "We have outstanding proof that only a few years ago, while still employed with your little wrestling company, you became involved in some heavy drug usage. Illegal drugs. Very illegal drugs, half of which were obtained and utilized right here on our soil. But because of your position and influence over the former occupant of my position, the evidence was suppressed. But it still exists, Vangelus." The hint of amusement on his face has long ceased. Now he’s simply enraged. "Now you can walk out of that door if you want to, but I can guarantee you won't be walking back to PRIME to fight in ANY main event for ANY championship, because we'll have you BURIED underneath the jailhouse. How will PRIME feel when they're forced to invoke their "Card Subject to Change" clause because their main star is behind bars?" Like a vulture, she begins to pace around him, tilting her head…exuding that power. "Not so witty anymore, huh? Look at you…I wouldn't hesitate to guess that you're still doing drugs." She leans in closer to him as if inspecting him. "…you are still doing them, aren't you? That's why you were fidgeting on your little show a couple of weeks ago, making a complete fool of yourself before passing out in front of a national audience. You're still on them. PRIME has to have a strict Drug Policy in place, so tell me…how do you get by? Who do you pay off? What scheme do you use so that it's undetectable? Maybe you're off them. Maybe the fidgeting that night really was due to nerves." She's trying to get into his head. Can’t say it’s a complete failure. "But the fact remains…I have you by the balls. So are you going to help me?" With a startling "Gotcha-by-the-balls" gesture, she smiles…knowing that the cards have changed. Now it’s no longer she that needs him, but he that needs her. ---- Inside a house. Actually, more like a mansion. The Oilers?" "Oh yes! I just know they're destined to win the Big Bowl this year!!" Before any additional bewilderment can etch across the face of Olsig, a buzzer rings. "Chives…bring him in."
