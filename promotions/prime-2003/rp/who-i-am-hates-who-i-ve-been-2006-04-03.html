Who I Am Hates Who I've Been

3 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/04/06 “You know that feeling you get when you wake up, like everything is the same? I have to say that I completely and utterly hate that feeling. I suppose it only really comes with the mundane regiment of school, work or even family, you all probably know exactly what I mean. People live through their lives following the same patterns and routines forever in a day, yet some never seem to complain, which is something of a miracle. I assume they do it out of love for their families, to put food on their table and clothes on their backs. The feeling I speak of today is one that I haven’t ever experienced, but I guess, in some ways, I should be thankful to PRIME for that. I mean, when is any given day as a professional wrestler boring or the same? It’s a different city – sometimes different country – a week, which means a different hotel room, different means of travel, different faces, different voices, different fans, different weather and different opponents. One week I could be wrestling Peter Vetra in Philadelphia, another week I could be up against the entire roster in Osaka, Japan. That right there is my life with this company and that’s why I do this. Other guys have families, friends and homes to fly back to after every Wednesday night ReVolution; I guess you could say I live more of a “nomadic” existence, or perhaps a “wrestling” existence. Yeah it gets me down a lot, I’m not going to lie to you; it’s sometimes not very easy to deal with. My Mom is exiled in the United Kingdom, my Dad is dead and you probably won’t be surprised to hear that I was an only child - but I suppose it’s easy to complain, and in my case probably quite galling to read. “How dare he!?” you might splutter - as though being a world class wrestler in a world-famous promotion makes up for years of antisocial living, injuries and constant confusion. But you know, the funny thing about it is that if it ever came down to it, if there really is a creator and if he ever gave me a choice between wrestling and normality I couldn’t say which I would choose… and I think that’s because of you. You, the wrestling public. The men, women and children who inspire me to go the extra mile. The PRIMEates who chant my name and cheer my every move, you are the reason I do this. It’s no longer about the titles and the money – hell; I’m writing this from a Motel Se7en – it’s no longer about being considered “the best”, it’s about giving thanks and giving payback to the everyday folk who made me who I am today and put me where I am today, I won’t ever forget you, I won’t sell you out again. I dedicate this all to you.” - Ignatius Lisieux in a special one-off column for SLAM! Adam looks up towards the elder statesmen of the trio with a raised eyebrow and a curled lip, he blows his silver hair from his eyes before firing a burning question towards him, “ John… are you… are you crying?” Cue ultra defensive Mr. Incredible: “Heck no! I haven’t cried since June twenty-seventh, nineteen-eighty-four!” Adam can’t help the loud laughter that scuttles mischievously between his lips as Mr. Incredible fires an icy glare in his direction… “That’s her birthday, right?” pipes up Ignatius, suddenly interested in the conversation, raising his fluffy head from the brick-like bed of his motel room he asks: “You haven’t cried since the day she was born?” “Heck no, what do I need to be crying for!? I’ve seen things that’ll make your toes curl and your hair turn white…” and then he looks at the two crazy-haired superstars, “…Naturally white, I mean.” Adam grins again, “Okay there John-boy, but s’okay to cry now and again y’know?” Noble considers the “Number One Son” with a bemused expression, “The day I cry over some melodramatic wrestling article is the day you can slap my butt and call me Shirley… which’ll be never, before you get any twisted ideas ‘bout it!” With the conversation turning away from Johnny Noble’s daughter, Ignatius’ interest wanes once more as his head drops back down to his itchy beige pillow with a dull thud, tiredness and jet-lag engulfing the fabric of his being. “Will you get a load of this sleep-monkey over here?” points Noble accusingly with a frustrated expression on his face, “We get on the plane home and he sleeps… We get back from Japan and what does he do? He sleeps… He wakes up and we head to my hometown, he sleeps… then we get here and he checks into a dingy little motel… and he sleeps! Always sleeping!” Adam slowly drags himself to a vertical base and stretches his impressive biceps out with a gawping yawn, “T’be fair to the bloke… it is only five-past-nine.” Noble shakes his head defiantly, “You were up when I got here…” “Aye, I was… but then I never actually went to bed,” smiles Adam with a wink. “So that’s what the smell is… You mean to tell me you stayed out all night!? What kind of an example is that!?” Adam looks confused for a moment as he scratches his back, “Whaddya mean? An example to who?” Noble slaps Adam playfully around the back of the head, “Who the heck do you think, smarty pants? Iggy over here has got his first main event match for over a year in two days time, he needs to be prepared… I don’t want him letting the side down in my hometown.” The “Number One Son” grins cockily and pats reassuringly on Noble’s back, “The day Iggy gets worried about Boda, Olsig or Cain is the day you can spank my ass and call me Shirley!” “Pfft!” spouts Noble, almost spitting feathers at Adam’s reaction, “It’s a good thing Ig doesn’t share that opinion of himself! We’d be in a whole heap of trouble otherwise!” And then things turn a wee bit sour as Adam releases his arm from the resting place it had found on Noble’s shoulder and delivers a home truth to the veteran superstar, “Oh he does alright, he’s just too scared that if he shows people that side to his character that they’ll stop rooting for him again… and we’d hate for Iggy to be unloved, wouldn’t we?” Not waiting for a response to the stinging parting shot, the “Number One Son” turns on his heels and heads for the door without so much as another word, leaving Noble to stare sullenly at the floor before taking a perch on the corner of Iggy’s bed. Lisieux, awake through the whole ordeal but choosing not to interject finally raises his ruffled head and regards Mr. Incredible with a sympathetic expression, “Don’t worry about him, Johnny… I guess Adam ain’t used to sharing me with anyone, he’s pretty possessive.” “You don’t think your match on ReVolution is an easy win, do you Ig?” asks Noble tentatively, completely bypassing Ignatius’ words of support over Adam due to his overwhelming fear of the Lisieux ego resurfacing… Lisieux pauses for a moment, the pendulum in his mind swinging between the “right” answer and the “honest” answer… “No, ‘course I don’t.” “Stop right there. There’s always a damn catch!” Later that Day Arriving back at the motel during nightfall and feeling even worse than he had upon leaving it, the fatigued figure of Ignatius Lisieux slouched against his room door as he searched desperately for the key to it. The persistent jangling continued as a few other motel guests walked on by, a couple tipping Lisieux a knowing, respectful nod, until Adam swings the door open violently and speaks in a drunken slur, “Ah! Yer back! Where’ve you been man, I’ve already busted open the JD! Get yersel’ in here!” Doing his utmost to avoid disappointing his old friend, Lisieux hides a sigh deep inside of himself and fakes a smile before cautiously stepping into his trashed room. “How much have you had already?” he asks the Scotsman as he observes the scene, fearing the worst with the forthcoming answer… Adam pauses for a moment, the cogs in his brain almost visible as they turn and turn, searching for an answer… “About three quarters of this bottle!” he exclaims proudly, holding up an almost-empty two-litre bottle of Jack for Lisieux to observe. “What’s this in aid of, man? I promised Johnny I’d play it safe until after Wednesday…” pleads Ignatius, doing his very best to remain as diplomatic as possible, but seriously tempted by the offer of free booze. “To hell with Johnny, man, let’s just hit the tiles… have a proper old school night in!” comes the retort as Adam kneels down, wobbling, onto his single bed. “I can’t remember the last time we got wasted just for the fuck of it!” Lisieux shakes his head as he places his sports bag down by the door and closed it over gently; kneeling beside his bedside table he flicks on his lamp before beckoning Adam to hand him the bottle… “What the hell, one drink won’t hurt me… In fact, I need it after that session Johnny put me through…” “I talk to absolutely no one, couldn't keep to myself enough.And the things bottled inside have finally begun to create so much pressure that I’ll soon blow up. I heard the reverberating footstepssinking up to the beating of my heartand I was positive that unless I got myself together,I would watch me fall apart. And I can’t let that happen again‘cause then you’ll see my heartin the saddest state it’s ever been. This is no place to try and live my life.” “That’s the way!” beams the Scotsman, heartily passing the black-labelled bottle to his compadre. “What’s on your mind, anyway?” he asks as he slumps back into the headboard of his bed with a satisfied sigh. “Never a clever question to ask me, bro, you should know that by now” grins Ignatius as he takes a hard swig of whisky and forces it on down into the bowels of his stomach, “It’s just really strange, y’know? After the Halo, the landscape has changed yet again…” Lisieux took the silent nodding from his Scottish amigo to be an invitation to continue, “When I came back it was all about revenge on Ozric and Olsig and the Dark Age guys, I was full of anger towards a whole lot of different people… but once the Ozric match came and went things just sorta went away, I guess I found myself without any real reason to be back here.” “Ah…” burps Adam proudly, “…so that’s wha’ this obsession wi’ the fans is all about then, eh?” analyses the Scotsman, his normally Americanised Glaswegian accent finding its native roots with the alcohol gurgling around in his system. “I guess it must be…” considers the French-Canadian before taking another swig of the ailing supply of whisky, “…you think that’s why being cheered has become so important to me? The revenge stuff is over with, despite Nelson’s best intentions at stirring up a hornet’s nest this week with it, I’m not involved in any of the title pictures, I don’t really know anyone these days… I guess the fans are the only people I can identify with.” The drunken pseudo-psychologist burps again as he pulls the bed sheets up over him to make himself comfortable, “You’ve changed a hell o’ a lot since I knew you man…” begins Adam again, his words slowing with the combined effects of drunkenness and tiredness, “The guy I knew was the best and knew he was the best… now you’re letting piss-ants like Hoyt and Karina get inside of your head, where’s tha’ self-belief gone to?” Ignatius is silent for a moment as he stares blankly against the cream walls of the cramped motel room, allowing Adam’s words to resonate and reverberate around inside of his mind as he desperately searches for an answer to his friends’ question… “I can feel it sneaking back into me, the old Lisieux I mean, the confidence is coming with every week longer I last in this place… Beating Ozric, beating Vetra, winning the tag match… longest time lasted in the Halo, eliminating Gamble, Ito and Nelson. I’m not so worried about my limitations anymore, it’s starting to become about what I can achieve again… rather than just being about making sure I survive, y’know?” Adam didn’t know… Adam was asleep, snoring gently as he lay on his back, silver wisps of hair fluttering against his twitching eyelashes. Lisieux looks over at him when a reply isn’t forthcoming and his face drops when he sees the snoozing Scotsman, “’Night man…” he whispers, then he was left alone with only his thoughts to confide in, starting with wondering if he was *quite* as exciting as he liked to think… “He fell asleep again!? God-dammit, I must be PRIME’s equivalent of Matt Hardy in the old speaking department, that’s two nights in a row… but maybe it was just the booze, after all, the guy hadn’t slept at all last night… I wonder what she’s doing right now… I wonder if she’s thinking about me – Noble! I wonder if he’s still awake, maybe I could give him a call… nah, I’m sure he goes to bed at ten-sharp every night… Damn, Karina was looking fine-as-hell on the last ReV, how the heck can she expect me to want to hit her!? Maybe hit on her… Argh, I cannot think along those lines anymore, it’s not allowed – Johnny would kill me! I wonder what kind of shape Boda is in after being away for so long, that’s something I definitely need to take advantage of… Mmm, pie, why haven’t I eaten today? I need to find Olsig before our match this week, he needs to know that it’s not personal anymore, people can change – I believe that now… Cain’s gonna be dangerous, especially with Gamble backing him up after what happened in the Halo… Oh no… oh I don’t wanna move, I’m too comfortable… go away… no… bladder… quit it… ARGH!” Bursting from the bed like a greyhound from the traps, Lisieux makes a beeline for his less-than-impressive en suite: it is basically a toilet in a cupboard. Forgetting the fact he arrived back at the motel needing to “go” really badly, he had let Adam distract him from his bladder movements. “Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh………” comes the familiar soundbite as the sound of some urine-on-water action takes control of the air while Adam sleeps in a merry stupor. Lisieux’s shoulders hunch down as pleasure sweeps over his body, that kind of unexplainable masculine pleasure that just might be better than sex – or pooping. While he’s draining the old one-eyed trouser snake, he can’t help but feel the overwhelming – and completely platonic – urge to telephone Mr. Incredible to take a weight off his mind. Once his business was over with, and without washing his hands (!), it was straight for the cellphone he’d owned since two-thousand-and-four and Noble’s home number was on speed-dial… Ring-ring, etc. and then the answer came… but not from the person he was expecting to hear: “Stop right there. That's exactly where I lost it.See that line. Well I never should have crossed it.Stop right there. Well I never should have said that it's the very moment that I wish that I could take back. Stop right there. That's exactly where I lost it.See that line. Well I never should have crossed it.Stop right there. He didn’t even realise who it was, he was so transfixed on needing to speak to Noble, “Hey there, is Johnny around?” Excitement filled the girl’s voice at hearing the French-Canadian accent on the other end of the line, “Ignatius?” “Hayley!?” beamed Lisieux, excitement becoming the order of the day for him, too, since he hadn’t seen her since Culture Shock. “Ignatius!” exclaimed Hayley again, “How’s it going!?” Lisieux smiled on the other end of the line, “It’s going okay, thanks. Sorry to ring so late, hope I didn’t wake you up…” “Nah it’s fine, I was watching TV, I think my Dad is already asleep though – you’re lucky I answered quick, he hates being woken up!”, Lisieux could imagine her grinning after saying that. “Give me a sec-… Adam’s asleep, I’m just gonna go for a walk so I don’t wake him…” replied Ignatius, while sneaking towards the door. “Where are you?” asked Hayley inquisitively, more than a hint of the devil in her tone. Ignatius smiled again as he carefully closed the door over and sucked in a healthy slice of spring Tacoma air, “Not too far from you, actually… I’m at the Motel Six in your town.” “No way! Dad never told me you were here, too! How come you aren’t staying with us?” she demands to know immediately. Lisieux laughed, recalling the man-to-man conversation that Noble had subjected him to after the “kiss” he had shared with his daughter, “After last time I don’t think your Dad was too comfortable with me staying there, I felt like I let him down a little with what happ-” Then the tone of the conversation changes: “I cannot believe him! I hate how he treats me like such a kid sometimes - I’m twenty-one in less than three months!” The MVP is quick to intervene in any potential diplomatic breakdowns between father and daughter, “Whoa, whoa, he’s just looking out for you, that’s kinda his job.” “Don’t you defend him, either, you don’t know what he’s like…” shoots back Hayley with venom spiking her tone. Lisieux’s eyes widen a little in shock at the miniature outburst aimed down the barrel of his ear but he gathers his composure before speaking again, “I’ve missed you a little bit, you know? It’s been nearly a month since I saw you.” Did you see that topic-change? The guy is a relationship wizard! Her defences neutralised by the charm onslaught, Hayley softens immediately, “How do you think I feel!? It’s okay for you, though, you’re off travelling the world being lauded like a king and I’m stuck in college…” “You make wrestling sound so romantic, try living between cheap and nasty motel chains for months on end and then we’ll see how you feel about it!” laughs Ignatius, not noticing the proprietor of his current dwelling hovering on the level below giving him the evil eye while sweeping the walkways with a worn old broom. “I guess… so… what are you wearin’?” asks Hayley with a sly giggle. The immediate reaction is Lisieux grinning to himself devilishly, but then the superface inside (maybe the voice of Noble himself) takes over and he sighs to himself, “Come on Hayley, I can’t do this right now, I promised your Dad…” “You wanna know what I’m wearin’?” she persists with another unbearably rude giggle… “No… no… I don’t” protests Lisieux meekly. “I’m wearing some teeny tiny little pink hotpants with little bunnies on them… then I’ve got a tight, white tank-top with nothing underneath…” Hayley speaks slowly and sensually, causing Ignatius’ resistance to weaken dramatically. But he remains steadfast: desperate to kill the mood before a third “member” enters the equation, Lisieux makes a wisecrack, “You sound like you’re wearing wrestling attire over there, Daddy been dressing you again?” “Oh come on! Stop ruining this!” argues the twenty-year-old non-virgin with a high-pitched squeal, “I miss you Ignatius, I haven’t seen you for weeks…” she continues, her voice becoming the trademark little whimper of the spoiled girl who isn’t getting what she wants, unfortunately it’s not a rare sound to Iggy’s ears. “I know, I know, me too… you’ve just got to be a little patient with me” explains Ignatius, rubbing the back of his neck in an agitated fashion, “I’ve got too much respect for your Dad to ruin this by taking things too fast.” Hayley giggles cheekily again, “I’d say it’s a little too late for that after last month!” then she continues (thankfully) in a more serious fashion, “I understand though… but it doesn’t make it any easier. Hey, at least I have your cell number now!” Lisieux rolls his eyes, unbelieving of his rookie mistake, but he quickly comes to accept this is far more than his usual game at stake here, “That’s fine by me - I still don’t have yours, though…” “I know!” laughs the mischievous Hayley, “I bet that’s a turn up for the books! See you later… lover boy!” “W-wait!” pleads Lisieux, realising he has just been completely outfoxed by a female of the species for the first time since high school, “Hayley? Hello?” But she was gone. A page right out of the hard-to-get manual, a page right out of the player’s manual… Hayley Jane Noble had just schooled Ignatius Lisieux in the art of playing games with the opposite sex, and you know what? He absolutely loved it. If there’s one thing that the French-Canadian enjoyed, it was the chase, especially when he thought there was a chance that he could fall for this girl. Staring at the phone with a disbelieving smirk on his face Lisieux then looks down towards the owner of the motel with a friendly wave, but is completely ignored, much to his surprise. He shrugs his shoulders with an offended expression now etched onto his kisser and folds his phone up before placing it into the back pocket of his faded denim jeans. Looking up at the Washington night sky he finds his mind aflutter with thoughts of young love, until images of Boda, Paul Cain and Vangelus Olsig imprint themselves onto his mind’s eye, causing him to hastily reach for the “play” button on his Creative Zen to take his mind off things (take that, iPod marketing campaign!) Playing the upbeat introduction to Relient K’s “Who I Am Hates Who I’ve Been” was the decision his little piece of technology made for him, and what a grandly appropriate and coincidental (!) decision it turned out to be… “Who I am hates who I've beenand who I am won’t take the second chance you gave me.Who I am hates who I’ve been‘cause who I’ve been only ever made me... So sorry for the person I became.So sorry that it took so long for me to change.I’m ready to try and never become that way again‘cause who I am hates who I’ve been.Who I am hates who I’ve been.”
