Although it’s not raining outside, lightning flashes in the doorway, illuminating a rather illustrious figure (+30 allit.). The Illustrious Face-Eater. “Doesn’t feel too good, does it, Fucknuts?” Facey’s voice seems to reverberate off the walls, an enraged, scratchy whisper. “Ohhh…owww,” Nova groans, pulling himself up onto a knee, “actually d00d, you overshot and managed to kick me directly in the asshole, which differs on the pain and nausea scales somewhat from what I paid those Testigram people to-” “DIE, YOU FIEND!” With something that he probably thinks resembles a war cry, Facey dives onto Nova. Nova yelps and shoves Facey back, pressing his legs against Facey’s stomach and shoving him backwards hard. The Face slams back against the room’s standard-issue bureau, his legs fly up and his back smashes the mirror hanging above on the wall. Par the course, Facey dutifully pulls the slow-motion fall forward as shard sprinkle around him, artsily reflecting different parts of his body (my apologies for the mental picture). Nova stares at his arch-nemesis’ downed form for a moment, almost like making sure an animal is dead before carting it off. He climbs to his feet and tip-toes over to the A-Lister, poking him in the side with his toe. “Sanka, ya dead, mon?” Nova says in his best Jamaican accent. “Nah, mon,” Facey mumbles into the carpet before turning over quickly and dead-legging the Rising Star, sending him down to his knees. The former follows by popping up and slapping Nova hard across the face twice. “Taste my palm, bitch! Taste it and then try to understand why you can’t be makin’ my grits so goddamn watery!” “I’m…I’m sorry, baby!” Nova weeps instinctively before stopping and shaking himself out of the trance, “HEY! Fuck you!” He grabs Facey around the neck and slams him back against the now-battered bureau a second time. Awesomely, a vase of flowers didn’t get knocked over in first contact falls now, crashing on Facey’s head and leaving a lone sunflower perched atop the Tag Champion’s masked dome-piece. Nova lets out a hearty laugh, but this is his mistake as Facey sticks his fist in it. “Taste my fist, bitch! Taste my fist, and then try…,” Facey begins triumphantly, but Nova cuts him off. “There's an old saying in Tennessee — I know it's in Texas, probably in Tennessee — that says, fool me once, shame on — shame on you. Fool me — you can't get fooled again!” Facey pauses a moment to let the topicality of Nova’s retort enjoy its moment in the sun, and then fires back. “There’s a saying in the A-List, too! It says, ‘Why listen to the cocksmooch with the shitty bandanna if you can just drop the lamp on him that you picked up a second ago?’” Nova recognizes the peril, and the next couple of seconds pass in slow-motion. The Rising Star shoots his foot out and catches Facey in the shiz-n00ts. I included the “iz” to illustrate the intensity of this particular nut-shot. Nova’s foot catches the Face under the shaft, capturing only the balls and crushing them against the top dresser drawer of the bureau. Please try as hard as you can to understand how terribly unfortunate this must be for the Illustrious Face-Eater. Ladies, if any of you are reading this (and why would you be, on a Friday night? Giiiirl, go out and shake them thang-thangs!), imagine the worst punch to the box ever, times a hundred trillion. Unfortunately, a bean-grinder on this order of magnitude causes Facey to relinquish control of every muscle in his body (including his sphincter), and that means the vase obeys gravity and shatters atop Nova’s noggin. Facey hits the floor, and Nova’s head slumps to the side. After a moment, the only sounds in the room are Facey’s soft, shocked whimpers and Nova’s snoring. “Ahhhh…ahhhh…ahhhh-ahhh, God…ahhh, God…” “Zzzzzzzz…mmmm, boobies…zzzzzz…” Ten minutes later… Nova comes to, grabbing his head and moaning. For the first time in a long time, it isn’t because of a hangover. The Rising Star brushes bits of vase off his face and leans over. Facey is still on the ground, cupping his nuts and groaning, though his panting has decreased ten-fold. They stare at one another for a moment, contemplating the resumption of their struggle. Oddly (or not, depending on how you look at it), Facey breaks the standoff. “Is…is that a Lord of the Rings collectible of the One Ring?” “Yeah,” Nova groans, hand still over the swollen lump on his forehead. “Do you wear it out?” Facey asks, voice strangely childlike in its curiosity. “Sometimes,” Nova replies, “but I don’t want its value to depreciate too much.” “I didn’t know you were into SkyMall,” Facey says, genuinely surprised. “Now that I have that sweet new endorsement deal for Munchies™, I don’t have time anymore to go to the store like regular chumps,” Nova explains, “I do all my shopping through SkyMall now. Peep the ninja sword.” “From Batman Begins?!” Facey screeches as his head swivels around, but he quickly regains his composure. “Eh, no big deal. I have Excalibur at home, and that’s way cooler.” “Cooler than the DaVinci Code cryptex sitting on my nightstand?” Nova counter, and Facey wails. “DAMN YOU! My roll-up piano shits on your cryptex!” the Dick declares. “OHHH! OH-HO-HO!” Nova clutches in his chest in agony before breaking into extended guffawing. “So you say, you goddamn asshat, but will it trump…THIS?!” Nova reaches behind his back and whips out a scooter, almost like that guy from the Dominoes commercial, except that a completely p!mpD-out motorized scooter weighs a lot more than one of those Razr guys, so Nova just weakly yanks it a few inches off the ground and swings it violently in Facey’s direction. It hits him underneath the shin. “OWWW, MOTHERFUCKER!!” Facey cries as he hops up and down on his good leg, gripping his wounded extremity…and no, complete lack of attention doesn’t put your dick in the “wounded” category. He quickly sobers up. “Dude, a fucking scooter?! Nasty!” The Eater of Faces wrenches the scooter from Nova’s grasp and immediately begins motoring around the room, leaving adorable little puffs of smoke and echoes of “putt-putt-putt-putt” in his wake. Nova shrugs and goes to the slammer. When he emerges from the methanous dungeon, he sees Facey eyeing his Potato Cannon. “Nice gun you got here.” Nova lights a cigarette and cocks an eyebrow. “Oh, that’s no gun, friend. What you used on the show a few weeks back…that was a potato gun. What you see before you, Piss Ant, is a Potato Cannon.” Facey snorts. “Oh? And what makes it a ‘Potato Cannon?” Nova exhales smoke in Facey’s direction and shrugs. “Dunno…could be that it will SHOOT A SPUD UP TO FOUR HUNDRED OR FIVE HUNDRED YARDS.” Facey’s jaw drops. “NO. WAY.” He gives the Cannon a second look before noticing a tube on the countertop. He begins laughing. “What’s this, faggot? Your lube?” “Hey, be careful!” Nova shouts. “Dude, that’s PVC glue from me building my gun! It’s like the stickiest shit…” “You…you gettin’ ‘er good and shiny before you beat off to cock porn, you knob-slobbering son of a bitch?!” Facey manages to gasp between fits of laughter. Nova ignores the verbal musings and rushes forward, grabbing at the tube. He and Facey now share control of its fate. “You idiot! Stop fucking around or something ridiculous is going to happen!” “Are Elton John and Alexis Baldwin,” Facey continues, now struggling to breathe from the OD off maniacal cackling, “waiting in the bathroom…hahaha…waiting in the bathroom for you to come pump their asses full of…hehe…full…hehe…full of man mayonnaise?! NOVA HAS GAY WANG LUBE!! ATTENTION WORLD!!” “STOP YOU FUCKING…oh, my God,” Nova groans as an enormous glob of glue splats out of the end onto Nova and Facey’s hands, intertwined around the tube. Almost instantly, to Nova’s horror and Facey’s oblivious cackling, the glue hardens, sealing the Rising Star’s skin to his enemy’s. Facey stops laughing and stares down at his hands, and then up at Nova, who’s red in the face and fuming. “DO YOU SEE WHAT YOU’VE DONE?!” the Rising Star shouts so venomously that his voice cracks. “WE’RE IN A REALLY FUCKING TIGHT SPOT NOW BECAUSE OF YOU, AND…and I really wanted to be in bed early for my World Cup pre-gaming tomorrow.” Facey blinks. “Can we smoke a joint? And by that, I mean smoke me out, faggot.” The veins on Nova’s head bulge out, but the color in his face returns as he takes a deep breath and closes his eyes for a moment. When he opens them, they’re narrow slits. “Who the fuck would roll it?” The weight of this situation now falls upon the Dick in its entirety, and he stares daggers into the Rising Star. “WHAT THE FUCK DID YOU DO?!” Suddenly, Nova drops down on his knees in front of the Face-Eater, and the latter barks out a hearty guffaw. “I knew you were gay! That whole sordid ‘ex-wife’ thing was such a cover!” But Nova only uses his glued hand configuration to grab the lighter out of the chest pocket on his Hawaiian shirt. He grabs a cigarette out with his mouth as well. He yanks the four-handed modern art towards his mouth and lights his cigarette before standing up. He grins and blows a plume into Facey’s mug. A-Dizzle coughs and tries to turn his head away. Nova gives him a wink. “Now, we’re gonna find a way out of this, and you’re gonna fucking cooperate, or I’ll head-butt you into next fucking month. Dig?” He blows another plume into the Illustrious Face-Eater’s face, and Facey hacks loudly. “Alright, goddammit! Alright! But I just want you to know that I took a picture with my camera phone when you went down on your knees like a Brooklyn hooker, and that shit is gonna be ALL over the internet.” “Fair enough,” Nova concedes, “as long as we’re being open here, I sold your poetry diaries on eBay last month, and they’re getting a strong reaction from the, um…from the ‘alternative’ community.” Facey lets out an anguished wail, and Nova drags him toward the door.
