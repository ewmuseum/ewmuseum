To Be The Man, You've Gotta Sell Out To The Man

28 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-28-07 “Oh, don’t be so sour! Look, this gimmick is genius! Trust me, you’ll be over like Gangbusters!” –Blaine Blair to Sonny Silver on Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME. Politics. Favoritism. Golden children. It’s all I’m surrounded by lately and quite frankly, I’m sick of it. This plane ride’s got me resorting to some of this introspective mess in order to guide my mind away from the madness of intermittent sobs from the toddler in front of me to the guttural snoring of the fatass sleeping in the rear. I can’t help but think of the PRIME Universal Championship stowed in the overhead while I kick back my seat. That title was supposed to be the magnum opus of a career forged from infamy. It was supposed to be my everything; my way to shut every last doubter up and to prove I’m not some overrated choke artist that likes to kick things with incredible force. Instead, all it is just reminds me of the time when I had to sell out to get ahead in PRIME… “Ratings, my friend! This gimmick is mass appeal. You can tell your little WWE jokes, make people laugh and rise up the card faster than just going around kicking things. You don’t have to swear a lot like you’ve done in the past, or nothing! Let loose out there with it!” Yeeeeaaaah, Blaine. You want me to let loose and not censor me, but you DON’T want me to cuss? Do I LOOK like Nitz Donnelly? Do I LOOK like some giraffe you lock up in a feeding pen for the amusement of the masses? I get saddlebagged with a stupid character while others are free to do what they want. Lindsay Troy never got hindered. Hell, if Blaine wasn’t such a poof, I’d have figured Troy and he having coitus. Danny Ferguson, DESPITE being a roid-head and me not touching a single drug or painkiller in my life, gets cheered while I get booed? Then, Chandler Tsonda has the NERVE to come up to me and accuse me of some shit when all he’s done is shill his merchandise and scream “CHOPPY CHOPPY YOUR PEE-PEE!” at me and the crowd eat him up despite the fact he’d rather earn a fucking paycheck than give his heart and soul to a sport I do. And what’s worse, all I get in return for being the flagship of the biggest sports entertainment conglomerate is screwed time and time again with management siding overrated pieces of shit like Tsonda, Troy, Fergie, Tchu, Killean, et al to get this title off me. Bitching about title shots and holding their breaths until their cheeks turn blue. They’re celebrated by the masses and cheered for breaking the rules, but when *I* do it… “Who the HELL do you think you are?” – Head Bitch in Charge of Talent Relations “You walking around that title makes me sick! Troy, Tchu, Killean, they all carried it with some class!” – Other bitch in charge. Yeah, that’s awesome. You let TROY hold onto that belt for nearly three months without defending it, a feat duplicated only by Hoyt Williams and yet, you’ve already had me booked against Tchu the second he sent you an e-mail cashing in a ticket he RELINQUISHED. … Yeah. Sorry, Sonny. Don’t mean to rant. I shouldn’t let any of this get to me. I should be strategically thinking about how I’m gonna deal with any combination of these guys coming at me, not… not ENVYING them. Think about how Danny and Lindsay will come at me with friendship and teamwork like Hallmark-bred idiots with feelings pouring out of them. I HOPE I face those idiots. I’ve beaten them both a couple times before, what’s one more gonna hurt? I’ve gotta consider how much Devin Shakur’s wanted this shot and partnership be damned, how he’ll kick my head into oblivion if I discount him. Hoyt can never be refuted as he’s wanted this title shot for two years now and at a moment’s notice, he could overpower me and rob me of the gold. Tsonda as the referee? Yeah, no way THAT’S a screwjob. And complaining about it? Yeah, been there, tried that. “Sonny, get over it. The rules are rules and they’ve been set in motion. Good luck with your match.” – Take a guess. Starts with “buh,” ends with “lane.” Hmm. Maybe I should just up and fucking quit with the title. Go back to school, work on my other hobby of cars and comprehend the viscosity of oil fluid. Nah, they’d want that. Wait! I’ll sell out again! Get my Peppermint-tinted chapstick ready and kiss some frigid Tyler ass. Or… Or I could change up the game completely! Be like Angelo Deville, eat a thesaurus and stare deep recesses of my soul! I’ll castrate myself and do an action vehicle opposite Pauly Shore like Danny… GOT IT! I’ll leave my balls right where they are and be exactly like Lindsay! Yeah, sit around and do nothing, yet get a paycheck and be too much of a pussy to cash in my rematch for two months! I’ll get in touch with my hermaphroditical side; shake hands, cocktease Tyler Rayne and Wade Elliott simultaneously! Genius! I’ll spread my message to the folks in PRIME Wrestling Academy: “Kids, you wanna rise to greatness here? Simple. Go out… do a movie, walk around aimlessly for a paycheck instead of actually WRESTLING, shill your merchandise on company airtime, explore the cavernous regions of your boss’s asshole with your cranium and you’ll be holding titles in no time! Oh, and don’t cuss. But do so with proper parental supervision.” I… I just can’t believe this. I want to be happy that I’ve shut people up time and time again. Dusk, Troy, Elliott, Ferguson, Codemaster, Chet Worth, Karina have all fallen before me. I’ve cemented my spot in the HoF next year. I’m the goddamn champion! But… Why do I still feel so dirty?
