Delphinus

12 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 12th, 2007 Today was a day that Coral Avalon probably could have done without. He had shown up to wrestle this evening, but had simply walked out of the arena dejected and annoyed that he practically did nothing more than stand on the apron and watch the Codemaster and his bestest friend in the whole wide world, Mr. Silver, the Chairman of PRIME, beat up some jobbers. After the match, and after the Codemaster and Scott Falk had somehow gotten away from Jack Murphy, he gave Coral the indication that he wanted him and the rest of the Rogues to show up in the parking lot after the show. So, here Coral was. He stood out in the parking lot with his arms crossed. He had donned a black "PRIME" T-shirt, a pair of khakis, and some tennis shoes. His scowl indicated that he just wanted to get back to the hotel and hopefully not be bothered by anyone whose name wasn't "Annabelle Summers". Speaking of our fair lady Summers, she was standing next to Coral. She wore a purple sweater and a long black skirt that reached her ankles, along with a pair of flat-heeled black dress shoes. Her hair was in its usual long pigtails. She held her violin case, as they usually did, to her side while clinging to Coral's right arm with her left arm. While standing around in the parking lot, Coral half-expected to see Slash McCoy beating Burnie McCoy with a billy club, but he didn't, and he was somewhat disappointed with this since he remembered not liking Burnie very much. Robert and Scott Falk stood some distance away. Robert, in his usual muscle shirt and pants, just stood idly while Scott Falk, in whatever the hell I might consider his average outfit, just paced in front of, to the side, and all around Robert Falk. All the while, he was yelling and cursing about Jack Murphy trying to kill him and Codemaster. The three members of Mega Job, a distance away from both the Falks and our darling young couple, were going through a massive script rewrite. Actually, they were just ripping apart the old script while Steve jumped upon the little bits, and just discussing all of the inane things that they'll probably catch Ferguson doing. Although, Beef WAS gushing like a school girl about finally meeting Danny Ferguson. Alexandria and Codemaster, however, were nowhere to be seen. Coral was wondering about this. He had been a little suspicious of the two of them ever since they came to his temporary home in Japan. However, he knew that Alexandria didn't seem too interested in anything resembling a relationship, and that calmed his nerves just a little bit. Then they got rattled again when he saw a bus pull into the parking lot of the building. Coral had to rub his eyes, and make sure that he wasn't merely dreaming the sight that he saw. The massive abomination that parked in front of him was, indeed, a bus. More particularly, it was one of those private buses that rockstars ride in. It was large, and Coral was surprised that it could fit in the parking lot. However, Coral's surprise more gravitated towards the bus's paint job. The top half of the bus as painted black, but the bottom half had been paint in a blue color, with a line of white separating the blue and the black. However, Coral knew at once who the owner of the bus was when he saw the logo on the back end of the bus that read in every clear letters: BLUE ROGUES. "Dammit, Allen..." Coral muttered to himself. Speaking of the Codemaster (Allen being his real name and all), he hopped out of the monstrosity not too long after it had been parked. He sported a white T-shirt with a 1-Up Mushroom in the center and an arrow pointing at his crotch, and a pair of jeans. The bald-headed leader of the Blue Rogues smiled a glorious smile as he approached the converging members of the Blue Rogues. "Brothers. I don't think I need to tell you what I got out of Blair." the Codemaster said. Coral didn't have a sarcastic remark. He didn't even have a remark in general. He was stunned. Surprised. Shocked. Flabbergasted, even. He just stared at the Codemaster, and then at the bus. Meanwhile, Annabelle had a similar surprised expression, but she was able to ask the golden question, "Y-you... you got a bus?" "Bingo! The little girl gets a 1-up." the Codemaster said, laughing and clapping like he's Damon Gant. Annabelle looks away, and mutters to herself, "I... I'm not a little girl..." Coral finally got over the shock long enough to ask some important questions, "How? Why? Huh?" ...Okay, Coral, you need to work on elaborating your questions a little better. I mean, seriously, those are too monosyllabic. You should've gone for "How did you get the bus?" or "Why did you get a bus?" or "In what way were you able to afford getting a bus?" Nevertheless, the Codemaster answered the first of the two permissible questions, "I had a nice, long chat with Blaine Blair. He was so nervous that he made Florina look self-confident. As soon as I brought up our difficulties in transporting nine intricately linked people and how we'd need a bus, he was on it like Riku on Sora." Then he turned back towards the bus and smiled the smile that a thousand Princes of Persia couldn't hope to smile, "So this is our Delphinus. It shall be that which carries us to our destinations. Also, it's built to accommodate eight people and a midget." The three members of Mega Job headed right inside the bus. Alexandria Malone headed out just a few moments later. She wore a white tanktop and black leather pants, and I don't mind telling you that Alexandria looks damn good in leather pants. Anyway, she walked over to where the Codemaster was, and was downright perky about her mood, "I love this bus! It's got, like, everything! Ever!" Coral looked at Alexandria, then at the bus, then at the Codemaster himself, "How much did you have to pay, exactly, for this seven ton bucket of bolts?" "Nothin'. I just convinced Blaine that it was for the good of this here Shinra company." the Codemaster said. Coral looked at Codemaster like he didn't believe him. Alexandria saw this expression on Coral's face and jerked a thumb at the Codemaster, "He threatened to read a list of all 493 Pokemon on national television." "That's quite the threat." Coral said, sarcastically. "Oh, it's just a ratings killer," the Codemaster said with a smile, "Kinda like 'putting Brandon Youngblood on television' and 'letting Easton Hall talk about *anything*'." "I'm sure." Coral said, with a sigh, before he decided to ask, "So, he let you just get a damn bus?" "Yuuuup." Codemaster said, "I got it before Tyler got hired to be the general managing commissioner slash interview lady slash annoying bitch, too, so I didn't have to talk to her at all." "And he agreed to this with just a simple threat of reading Pokemon names on national television instead of... y'know... firing you?" Coral asked. "Hey hey, I don't tell you how to execute a figure four headlock Sharpshooting Haas of Pain, you shouldn't have to tell me how I do my job around here." the Codemaster said with a shrug. The Codemaster turned his back to Avalon and admired his bus from afar, his hands on his hips and his smile ever-lasting. Finally, he walked over to join the Falk Brothers and talk to them about the bus. Coral sighs and turns to Alexandria, "Why does he never tell me these things until it's too late?" "`Cause you usually make a scowly face if he tries telling you things before he does them, so he totally avoids it by just not talking to you about it, now." Alexandria explained, moving her hands around erratically in an attempt to convey her message through hand movements. "I do not do that." Coral said, with a grumble. "Coral... I've seen you do it, too..." Annabelle said. "Yeah!" Alexandria said, before she mimicked Coral's scowl, "Like this." Coral narrowed his eyes at Alexandria, "I do *not* look like that when I scowl." Annabelle looked over at Alexandria. "No... he looks more like this..." she said, before she mimicked his scowl as well. Coral did a double take upon seeing Annabelle trying, before he chuckled. "You are too adorable to even try it." he told her. He put his arm over Annabelle's shoulder, and looked at Alexandria, who's still trying to mimic Coral's scowl, "And you should stop that." Now Alexandria got all... "pouty"-faced, "Well, okay then." She shrugged and then skipped off, and yes, she really was *skipping* off, to go talk to Alan and the Falks. This left our young couple by themselves. "It seems a bit excessive." Annabelle observed. The two of them approached the bus. As they did so, Beef and Janito suddenly raced out of the bus, before being followed by an angry Steve who was shouting "DEATH" at them. "Yeah. I dunno how he managed to talk his way into this. Maybe it's just the Lex Express repainted or something." Coral suggested. He climbed into the bus with Annabelle following him, and looked around. They were surprised to find that the bus seemed brand new, and had all of the basic necessities for travel by rock stars. It had a kitchen, a bathroom, and even a place where at least eight people could sleep. The only person the bus probably couldn't fully accommodate was Robert, but it was likely, given the size of the driver's seat, that Robert was meant to be the one driving the bus in the first place. The two entered the bedding portion of the bus, and sat on one of the eight beds. They were arranged in bunks, with two on top of each other. Annabelle set her violin case on the floor, clutched Coral's left arm, and leaned on his shoulder, "This is better than those cramped rental cars, though." "I guess. I hope he doesn't expect us to live in here, though." Coral said. "I... I wouldn't doubt it." Annabelle said. Coral reached over with his free arm and brushed Annabelle's right pigtail out of the way so he can get a better look at her face, "Well, that'd be awkward for us, wouldn't it?" "But... you like travel." Annabelle responded, "I think this is better for you." Coral shrugged. Maybe she was right, but he still couldn't shake the feeling that he was being intentionally left out of the loop on the matters of the Blue Rogues. He didn't know about what Codemaster had intended with their six man tag earlier in the night, nor did he know about this bus thing until just now. Something was bugging him about this partnership. Something was bugging him that the Codemaster wasn't telling him *everything* about this PRIME thing. Something was bugging him that he might have made a mistake in coming here. However, he couldn't put his finger on his feelings. The only thing he could do was put his arm around Annabelle and sigh, "I'm not so sure it is, myself." Annabelle turned her head to Coral, questioningly. However, Coral didn't elaborate. He simply looked ahead of himself, deep in thought, until Annabelle gave him a little peck on the cheek. "Quit worrying about everything. You'll end up like me." Annabelle, a girl who was noted for being crybabyish, said. Coral smirked, and took Annabelle into his arms so that he was close enough to kiss her with impunity. Somewhere after their kiss, Coral gave her his word. "I'll try."
