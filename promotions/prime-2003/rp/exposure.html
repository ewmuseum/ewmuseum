Date: 6/3/2008 " Wilson ! Is your ass in your head?" "I think you mean if my head is up my ass, Sergey." "Who cares! I just now it's your ass and your head together! Asses and heads belong nowhere near each other on the same person!" I hate it when my editor yells at me. Not because it actually scares me or anything but it's just hard not to laugh at the poor guy. Really, it's the communications industry. A firm grasp of English should be mandatory. But not Sergey, who hails from... someplace I can't find on a map. I don't want hurt the guy's feeling by cracking up at his mangled attempts to seem threatening. At least this time it's over the phone so he can't see me rolling my eyes. "And where is your head and your ass right now, Wilson ?" "At the psychologist's, Sergey." "What? You waste my time and FX's money so you can go bitch that you were pot-trained too soon?" "That's potty-trained, Sergey. And it's not my shrink. It's Emily Swanson's." Emily Swanson is a young up-and-comer. Commercials while in grade school, half hour comedy on Disney while a teenager, pop album with mediocre sales and even more mediocre songs, and now getting into films. First it was a squeal to Can't Hardly Wait called Still Can't Hardly Wait. Then Creeps, a horror flick that was better than it had any right to be. Now Emily's in the running to play Princess Allura in the Voltron movie. Personally, I'd go with Megan Fox but the studio didn't ask me. She kept herself pretty clean except for one small crack... the fiancé. Holden "the Gunslinger" Salinger. Salinger comes from one of those big empty parts of the country where men are men and pigs are fuck buddies. After being an amateur wrestling star at some university that offers degrees in beer bong construction and date rape technique, he decided to get into mixed martial arts. I hear that stuff is fake and all the outcomes are predetermined, but who cares... it's entertainment. Salinger made it more entertaining with his cocky attitude and tendency to get in unsanctioned fights in bars. Last year, the brass at MTV decided to pair up this bad boy with the girl next door, Emily Swanson, to be presenters at the VMAs. In typical Hollywood fairy tale fashion, opposites attracted. Six months later, they were engaged. Beauty ad tamed the beast. Or maybe not. First, Salinger failed a drug test. Not to do anything half-assed, he tested positive for both steroids and cocaine. That got him a one year suspension from the cage. Then came the drunken driving arrest. Kinda hard to bullshit your way out when you drive into the lobby of the Luxor . Casinos tend not to leap out in front of cars. "The Gunslinger" didn't let that get in the way of his bar room brawling. But what really put Holden Salinger in contention for Asshole of the Year is the rumor that he was using Emily as an unwilling sparring partner. A truly sad story... one that guys like me love. Sergey wants to really get the scoop on this, which is probably why he’s pissed I'm leaning on my car across the street from a Beverly Hills building of swanky medical offices instead on being on the trail of good ol' Holden. "Why aren't you in Holden Salinger's ass, Wilson ?" "You mean on his... nevermind. Sergey, Holden Salinger does dumb shit every day. We need a new angle for The Real Dirt. Let's get how it's effecting Emily Swanson. That poor girl must have the whole weight of the world on her shoulders." "Who the fuck do you work for... People? We don't care about getting warm and furry!" "Fine, Sergey. I don't like showing my cards before the hand's over, but check this. Emily's family are Christian Scientists." "What the hell? Like John Travolta?" "That's Scientology, Sergey. Christian Scientists are the ones that get press for picking Jesus over a doctor. I'm thinking Ma and Pa Swanson wouldn't be thrilled if their little girl chose a shrink for her problem instead of a prayer group. Then again, she's engaged to the Anti-Christ. But that leads to the second red flag. This psychologist, Dr. Karen Gilbert, also worked for Salinger's MMA bosses. They hired her to give him some help in anger management. Seems kinda hinky that in this town of hand-holders and yes-men that both sides of this equation have the same source for head-examination." "Uhhh... what?" "Just trust me, Sergey." "Fine, Wilson . But if you screw this up, it will be your ass in a hammock." "Sling, Sergey." "Just get me a fucking story." The next sound is Sergey slamming the phone down. What a charmer. I put my phone in my jacket and look over at the Healing Annex. It's a New Age-y name for an office building based on patronizing the rich. Besides shrinks there's massage therapists, plastic surgeons, nutritionists... and if some millionaire is tired of drinking expensive French spring water, there's places that'll blast it up his ass and into his colon. You know... what sick fuck invented the enema anyways? Someone had to be the first person to shove a nozzle up his poop chute and let it ride. But nevermind that. The same Mercedes that I've seen coming here every Tuesday and Thursday the last few weeks is pulling into the parking. I know Emily Swanson's in it. Time to get to work. I haul ass across the street and to the Healing Annex. As I enter a couple guys in coveralls are leaving. I give them a manly smile and nod. "How's it going, guys?" "Good. How 'bout you?" "Just fine." The reason I'm being cool with these lugs is they just happen to be my ticket to this story. See, I can't just follow Emily Swanson to her appointment and listen in at the door. That gets me hauled away pretty quick by the Healing Annex's security. No, I have to beat her to Doc Gilbert's office. The way I do that go past the guard in the lobby and go to the second floor. Then I get off and go to the stairs. I climb the flights of steps, even past the seventh floor where Karen Gilbert's practice is. Damn, good things I've been doing cardio for PRIME. I keep going another four flights until there's nowhere else to go.... except the roof. Once I'm there, I see just what I've been expecting. The scaffold for the window washers. See, the Healing Annex gets its windows cleaned every Tuesday. And by the luck that protects children, small animals and Keith Richards, Emily's appointment just happens to fall on the same time the washer break for lunch. Previous recon revealed that Doc Gilbert will gab with her patient in the reception area for a little bit before going back to the closed doors of her office for the session. That gives me just enough time to hop on this contraption and lower to where I need to be. Not right smack dab in front the window to Gilbert's office. I go a bit lower so I'm just underneath and can't be seen. Then... I wait. Check my voicemail. Text my assistant to push back my appointment with my wrestling sparring partner. This cross-promotion with PRIME deal really puts some sand in my Vaseline, but if it helps get my stories out there then I’m all for it. Speaking of which, time to see if I got one here or if I get to be the instigator for another of Sergey’s pistol-whippings of the English language. I press the button to raise the scaffold and the winch on the roof pulls me up pretty damn quietly. Stopping the scaffold as soon I can get a decent view. I pull out my digital camera and take a peek inside the office. Now, I've been in the celeb news biz for a while now, so not much surprises me. Gotta admit I was taken aback seeing Emily Swanson laying back on the proverbial couch dressed in one of those cute floral print summer dresses that button up the front. For one thing, I didn't think shrinks actually had couches, kind of like how I've never seen a skeleton in any doctor's office I've been in. For another, that pretty farm girl dress is completely unbuttoned and the good Dr. Karen Gilbert has has her face buried in in the groin next door. Gilbert's not bad on the eyes herself, she got a MILF/hot teacher vibe going on. And even though she's not a medical doctor, her finger's doing a pretty good impression of a rectal thermometer right now. This, of course, is money. The whole Emily Swanson being muff-dived by her therapist. Hell, Holden Salinger's therapist. I try not to grin as I snap picture after picture. So great. A tough, cocky son of a bitch like Salinger not being man enough for his woman, so she runs into the arms, and mouth, of her own gender. Serves him right. He's just like those self-righteous bastards in wrestling. The ones that kicked my ass and tried to ruin me for breaking their "code." Fuck them. I came back. And I'll tear them down just like I'm gonna be tearing down Holden Salinger and his switch-hitting sweetheart. I don't know if it's the thought of a major scoop or the hardcore girl-on-girl action right in front of me, but I'm getting seriously turned on here. Hmmm... my camera work works just fine with one hand, so I unzip my pants and whip it out. Emily Swanson is really getting into her unlicensed physical and I'm working both my pieces of equipment as fast as I can. Just hope no one walks underneath me... or that they'll at least have a hat on. I love my job.
