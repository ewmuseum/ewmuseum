Some Things Never Change And Some Are Psychotic

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26, 2005 A recently painted black Camaro pulls up into the driveway of a two-floor white house in Vancouver, Canada. The engine revs to a stop producing the only noise within the vicinity of an otherwise calm neighborhood. The car comes to a stop and Juan Santos walks out with his baggage accompanied by Alex Reynolds. Ahead of them, the two-floor house is known as the TITan mansion, but is not the original one. The original TITan Mansion is located in NY, but the term TITan Mansion is used to refer to any house they buy in any city. Rather than spend time at a hotel, all members of the TITans have always bought a house near the cities where the next business showing was to be at. Over the years the TITans have purchased houses in over 50 cities in North America. “We haven’t changed anything since you left,” Alex said, opening the door. “Well almost anything.” “Yeah, it’s not a big problem.” Juan replied, walking in with his luggage. “Besides, you said Stephanie died. Alex cleared his throat as he walked in closing the door behind him. “Yeah…” The inside of the TITan Mansion (Vancouver Edition) is relatively humble and modest. In fact, all of the TITan Mansions lack anything in regards to elegant decorative. This particular mansion was blanketed with cream-colored carpets, white curtains, and beige walls. It was merely just a resting point for them while they finished their duties. For Juan Santos, those duties would be competing in the next Revolution in a Bar Room Brawl with PRIME’s own Hollywood star, Danny Ferguson. The “Irish” was going to be a formidable opponent, and one that Juan Santos hoped wouldn’t have to challenge until later on. Regardless, the match served to Juan as a sort of prelude to a possible JITC semi-finals match. “So, what changes did you make around here?” Juan asked, surveying the house. Alex forced a laugh. “You see, the thing is we didn’t change anything. It’s just that there are some things that haven’t changed.” “Like what?” Just then, Juan is tackled more viciously than an extra wearing Tag body spray at a supermarket by wild, sexually deprived women. In fact, he is probably tackled more viciously than Joe Thiesman, minus the unnatural bending of your bones. The culprit of the vicious attack appears to be a brunette, in her late twenties, has long curly black hair, and is dressed like she tried to get dressed. Her skirt is hiked up revealing that she doesn’t believe in the magic of underwear and she is kissing Juan all over his face not letting him catch his breathe. Kiss. “Oh, my God, I’ve missed you so much,” Stephanie said, breathing hard. Juan struggles to get free of Stephanie. “What…” Kiss. “I never stopped thinking about you,” Stephanie said, continuing to kiss him. “Who?” Kiss. “I knew you were going to come back one day,” Stephanie replied. “Get off…” Kiss. “Everyone told me I was crazy but I knew you were coming back,” Stephanie said, continuing to kiss him. “Help…” Kiss. “I can’t tell you how much I’ve missed you,” Stephanie said. “Alex, what the fuck are you doing?” Juan exclaimed. Kiss. “I new you were gone a long time but it’s okay,” Stephanie said, smiling. “I took the liberty of planning our wedding.” Hearing the word “wedding”, or anything involving commitment, debt, debauchery, or signing your life away, is a surefire way to unleash the adrenaline in any man. Upon hearing the word “wedding”, Juan begins to pump up like Popeye on Super Spinach and throws Stephanie on him. Stephanie is then restrained by Alex Reynolds. “So, about those changes,” Alex said, forcing himself to remain calm, “we had to lie about Stephanie being dead. “Really?” Juan asked, in a sarcastic tone. “Wait until you see this,” Stephanie said, smiling and pulling Juan by the arm. She was quite strong, and rightfully saw being a former Woman’s champion. “What is it?” Juan asked, feigning interest. He looked back at Alex Reynolds and gave him a glare as if to tell him to do something. Stephanie led them down a corridor to a series of bedrooms. She stopped in front of a red-colored door. On the door were three different plaques. The middle one, which read: Syko Arsonist, appeared to be made a long time ago, as it did not retain the luster the other ones did. The other two plaques were nailed above and below the first one reading: The Love Nest of Syko Arsonist and Stephanie Ali-Reynolds-Arsonist. “We need to change the middle plaque now with your new identity,” Alex whispered, pointing out the fact that Juan was no longer Syko.” “We need to change all the fucking plaques,” Juan whispered loudly, pointing out the fact that his name isn’t important as his health with Stephanie. Stephanie opens up the door and introduces it like a game show hostess. Juan walks in a few steps and just looks around with his jaw literally gaping. “Dear Lord…” “Do you like it,” Stephanie asked, giggling. “I decorated it myself.” Juan blinks, hoping that he somehow has supernatural powers. He doesn’t. He gazes upon the room again. It’s not a room. It’s a shrine. The floor is a carpeted dark-red color, and is the only sane thing. The walls are covered inch by inch, millimeter by millimeter, of photos of Juan Santos, Stephanie Ali-Reynolds-Arsonist, and some photos of them together. Actually, those photos appear to be custom-made with cut out edges revealing serial-killeresque tendencies. The main attraction is located straight ahead in what appears to be a small shrine inside a shrine. Inside the trophy stand is memorabilia of all of Juan’s previous wrestling engagements. “Do you like it,” Stephanie asked. “I think I may have gone a bit overboard.” Juan smiled and turned around. “No, it’s fine. It’s um…unique.” Juan quickly walked out of the room dragging Alex with him. “Why…why?” “It was the only way we could stop her from killing us,” Alex replied. “She even set fire to the cars the other day. Hey want to see the training room?” “What?” “The training room,” Stephanie replied, with a big mischievous grin. “I know you are going to love the training room. Juan, Stephanie, and Alex arrived at the bottom of the stairs towards the basement area. Initially, the basement was used as a storage room for beer. Then, the TITans had the bight idea to just construct another house next to theirs: The Beer Place. “There’s nothing here,” Juan said, seeing that the room was filled with nothing but scattered mattresses. “How the hell are we supposed to train here?” Stephanie rolled her eyes. “You think people really need to train with weights, and treadmills, and Stairmasters, and all that junk? This shit is better. You build stamina, strength and technique.” “Somehow, I don’t think this works…” “Then let me show you how it works!” The image fades to black – much to the sanity of the viewers –, but not the audible. Through the black screen you can ear sounds of thumping, bumping, and things that go bump in the night. But, they don’t appear to be pleasant sounds as it appears to be more of a struggle, and one of them is losing, badly. This is the way the TITans have always trained. They call it the only way to train: sex. Who wants to go run and break a sweat doing exercises? You’ll burn fat, get faster, and build stamina -all of which Juan needs now after he’s experienced his first loss in PRIME. Technically he wasn’t pinned, but to Juan a loss is a loss. There was much more he could have done for his team. He never went into the match, hoping that his team could bail him out, but he did go into the match hoping that he could bail out his team. And after years of training like everyone else in the world maybe it was time to go back to the old ways; it was time to go back to TITism – their newly founded religion. The black screen of death slowly begins to focus clearly (and drown out the grotesque audible) into the new scene. We’re now looking at the large (and empty) room of the TITan mansion. Alex Reynolds is seated next to his wife, Sarah, and the other member of the group, Mike Black, is seated across them. Sarah Reynolds is a former small time television star, appearing in a few soap opera episodes. She turned to wrestling after meeting and falling in love with her husband, Alex Reynolds, and even partook in a short stint as a wrestler herself. She’s the most attractive of the TITan women, and also the most timid. Mike was a relatively new member, joining the team, after Juan Santos had already announced his initial retirement. “I can’t believe Syko Arsonist came back, “Sarah said, cuddling with Alex on the couch. “Actually it’s Juan Santos now,” Alex said, correcting her of Juan’s new identity. Mike let out a sigh of relief. “Finally got a judge to change that stupid name?” Sarah rolled her eyes and Alex chuckled a bit. “Actually, Juan Santos was his real name. Apparently his mother showed up, and now he’s like a new man.” “I thought he was conceived by Divine means,” Mike said, “and hear I am thinking that all wrestlers are orphans.” “That’s so stupid,” Sarah replied, annoyed. “That’s like saying Stephanie’s child is the messiah because she can’t remember who the father is.” “Hey, that’s what she says and we’re sticking to it,” Alex said. “But it’s wrong,” Sarah yelled, pulling away from her husband. Mike took out a picture of a church and flashed it in front of Sarah Reynolds. “That’s not what the donations to the Church of TITism, or the tax free benefits we get each year, say!” “Why do we still have to play the religion card?” Sarah asked, annoyed. “We already got what we wanted.” Alex turned to her and caressed her face. “Read my lips: No Taxes.” “Whatever.”“Besides, can we help it if we have believers in TITism?” Mike asked. “Yes!” Sarah yelled. “You can tell them the truth. Alex and Mike look at each other and then break out laughing, much to the disappointment of Sarah. “Something really stinks around here,” Alex said, sniffing the air. “You think?” Sarah replied, still angry with him. “No, I’m serious.” Alex said. Mike grimaced. “He’s right. It smells like fish.” “Stephanie, is that you?” Alex asked turning around. “What the fuck are you looking at?” Stephanie replied, smacking Alex. Sarah grinned noticing how Stephanie’s hair was a mess and her skirt was wrinkled. “I can’t believe you two are getting back together.” “I know,” Stephanie replied giggling. Sarah and Stephanie begin to talk in a gibberish language that has confused mankind throughout history. Alex and Mike look at each other and then towards the girls, trying to decipher what they are talking about. But they cannot make out any words through all the screaming, yelling, giggling, and high-pitched screams of delight. Whatever they are talking about, they sure seem happy about it. “We need to get a woman-language translator,” Mike said. Alex nodded. “It might be a job for HIM.” “Maybe we should watch What Women Want, again?” “Maybe we should kill you in your face!” Alex replied. Just then Juan Santos walks in with his pants wrinkled, and his once ironed shirt in shambles. His hair isn’t totally a mess as he had just cut it previous to his PRIME debut. “Wow, it’s good to have you back!” Sarah said, smiling. “And I cannot believe you and Stephanie are getting back together!” Juan, Alex, and Mike all reply in unison. “What?” “That’s right,” Stephanie agreed. “We already got the wedding date planned.” ”But…” Juan stuttered. “Hey, what happened to your hair?” Sarah asked. “Oh well I decided it was time for a new look,” Juan said. “So, I cut it right before my PRIME debut.” “Oh,” Sarah said, “no wonder I couldn’t find your picture on the PRIME website.” Mike Black stood up and patted Juan on the shoulder. “Congratulations man. Congratulations on the wedding.” “I’m not getting married!” “Yes, we are!” Stephanie screamed. Alex shook his head. “Always getting beat by a girl.” “Excuse me?” Juan asked. “Well, didn’t you just lose to a girl last week?” Juan laughed. “Um, no I wasn’t pinned.” “But Karina Wolfenden is a girl, right?” “Yes.” “She was on the other team, right?” “Yes.” “They won, right?” “Yes.” “You lost, right?” “…yes.” “Exactly,” Alex said, “you lost to a girl.” “But I wasn’t pinned!” Juan exclaimed. Stephanie flashed him a grin. “I can change that if you want.” Juan shuddered. “No thanks.” “Hey, if you are having problems with women in PRIME, you can always watch some Peter North tapes,” Mike said, chiming in with his wisdom. “Who the hell is Peter North?” Juan asked. “He’s a man who’s had his fair share of handicapped matches…with women.” Mike explained. “Wait, didn’t he have a brother,” Sarah asked, tapping her chin with her pinky. “What was his name? Alan? Allen? Allan?” “Who cares?” Mike asked. “He isn’t important. And whoever he’s with isn’t important either.” Juan sighed. “So, this Peter North guy always comes out on top?” Alex and Mike nodded. “Sometimes even on the bottom. But he always wins.” Juan walked over to wear he had left his luggage and carried it to his room. “Hey, you all have tickets to see me at this week’s Revolution in PRIME.” “Um, yeah we’ll all be there.” “We wouldn’t miss it for the world!” “We’re behind you all the way!” “I love you!” As Juan walks into his room, locking the door, the rest of the TITans begin to bicker among them. “Do we really have to go see him wrestle?” Mike asked. “Yes we do!” Sarah exclaimed. “He’s our friend and we agreed to go.” “Women!” Alex said, “Women and their morals.” “I don’t know about the rest of you,” Stephanie said, stretching. “But I’m going to see my future husband in action. And then maybe reward him.” Mike and Alex both shudder at the gruesome image and watch Stephanie leave for her room. Inside his room, Juan Santos is busy unpacking his suitcases. However, he cannot get his next match out of his mind. Danny Ferguson is going to be one hell of a talent. That 1-2 record he carries with him is misleading, considering the amount of caliber he possesses. But Juan Santos is merely worried, not fascinated with Danny. He knows that he can beat Danny Ferguson. This is going to be a warm up for the second round of the JITC. And if he should meet Danny Ferguson in that tournament as well, then he’ll simply have to make it 2-0 against the “Irish” and his team of extras. Juan continues to go about his business unaware that the real problem isn’t in a PRIME ring, but in the very house he now lives in… In her room, directly above Juan’s, Stephanie Ali-Reynolds-Arsonist (soon to be Santos) is surveying the room with a bit of vileness and anger she did not show before. Her room is pretty much decorated in the same kind of obsessive shrine fashion that she decorated Juan’s room with. The difference here is her. She walks to her stand where she has pictures of Juan Santos and even a ragged doll with Juan’s picture on it. She caresses the pictures and slowly drags a large, pointy pair of scissors across them. “How could you leave me?” she asked, dragging the scissors across the desk. “We were meant to be. We were in love. I told you I loved you. Everyone thinks I’m crazy…” she laughed. “I’m not fucking crazy. I’m in love. I love you, Juan. But then you had to go fucking leave and fall in love with that girl, Emilia. But she died…” Stephanie said, giggling. “She died. How sad. But it’s okay Juan…I forgive you. I forgive you for leaving me…” Stephanie said, this time grabbing the pictures in her hand. “We are meant to be. Now, you will never ever fucking leave!” Stephanie yelled, slamming the scissors down hard on the desk, protruding through the wood. “We are going to be so happy together…” TO BE CONTINUED…
