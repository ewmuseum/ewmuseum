Date: Special Edition A story is told everyday in this world. These stories are told from the many different points of view from the different personalities. No one story is the same as the next. They all have their own unique qualities that make them appealing. The differences are endless, but the difference that is going to make this story unique is the fact that it is being told by the one individual that knows the most about it. This isn’t some story you’ll find in the tabloids about a celebrity. No, this is a story told by the person living the life. It’s being told by the person going through the everyday trials and tribulations that another party cannot understand no matter how hard they try. Looking at the current state of today’s magazines and newspapers, some of the stories almost make you physically ill. All it really is is just some big money making roller coaster that so many people are willing to take a ride on. It could be a story about Britney Spears putting her baby in danger yet again or it could be a more serious story about an individual putting their life on the line over seas who is fighting to protect our rights and freedoms. But really, is the story the real deal unless it comes from the person themselves? No, it never is. Speculation is what drives today’s media. Honestly, it’s enough to make any legitimate story teller want to throw his pen and paper in the garbage and give up. Nobody wants to hear the real deal unless it is juicier than the original fake story that is posted on newspapers and web sites around the world. Last week was the perfect example. No matter how insignificant the matter was to most people, it was yet another very personal matter to myself and the other person involved. Let me explain… To be honest, the date really doesn’t matter anymore. Sometime last week, May Twenty-something let’s call it, an incident took place in a hotel in Charlotte, North Carolina. It was meant to be more than just your normal get together from the beginning. There would be no drinks shared, no fond memories recalled and no shaking of the hands. From the get go, it was a visit that had to do with family members talking to people that were not well liked for what ever reason there may be. Getting to the point, I was assaulted in my hotel room by one Matthew Ward. There is no sugar coating the story, there is no purple monkey dishwasher talk that may come with the story being told from one commoner to the next. This information is straight from the mouth of the person who experienced the event in question. Now most people may say, “Big deal, we see stuff like this every day in the world of professional wrestling. How many drinks did you guys have after the acting was finished?” I’m sorry to say that unfortunately, no alcohol was consumed in a group setting following the sequence of events that happened in Charlotte. You would not have seen Matt Ward and me sharing the wonderful experience of a cold beverage in any drinking establishment in the world that evening. Fact of the matter is this is not all about wrestling any more ladies and gentlemen. This whole long tale is not about the money, about the publicity or about the fame. This is one hundred percent personal. The story can never be told the same way unless it comes from the mouths of the individuals it’s about. Then again, there are exceptions to that rule. Some celebrities will tell a bullshit story just to save face. This is not about saving face. This is about the clashing of personal lives to the point where families are affected by the wake in the water so to speak. This isn’t some Nicole Richie, Paris Hilton “I don’t like you” story either. If they truly didn’t like each other as much as they say they don’t, then they wouldn’t be working their piece of crap TV show no matter how much money they were being offered to look like the best of friends. Honestly, if I was to be made to look like a friend to a person like Matt Ward at this moment in time, I would walk out of the wrestling business until someone woke up and realized that our heat is legit. Hollywood plays no part in this little episode I like to call life. As much as anyone tries to tell some lame brain story about me and my goings on, I will be there all the time to correct them and put them in their place. Once again, last week was the perfect example. Reading the paper a day or two after the incident was quite humorous. First, professional wrestling is not the kind of sport that will hold down front page stories in the major newspapers around the country. Just the other day, I am sure Barry Bonds was on the front page of plenty of newspapers in America when he passed Babe Ruth on the all time home run list. Baseball is the kind of sport that doesn’t have to wish for front page stories. But how often do you see professional wrestling as a front page story? The only time I have seen professional wrestling involved in a front page story is when a wrestler is involved in any illegal activity such as illegal substances or when someone in the wrestling community passes away. Pretty awful way to get a front page story huh? Not that those events shouldn’t be important in the news world, but come on. What about the accomplishments of all the professional wrestlers these days? Even though professional wrestling is thought out in advance to a point (My GOD who’da thunk it?!), the physical conditioning of the athletes involved is almost unparalleled. For example, I’m not all that bad at swinging a bat. I’m not talking about at another person either. I grew up playing a little baseball, but I am in no way saying that I could hit home runs like a Barry Bonds or a Willie Mays. But I would like to think that in regards to overall conditioning, I am at the very least their equal and perhaps their superior. I could not see Barry Bonds coming to a wrestling ring and doing what my colleagues and I do night in and night out. Of course, that is pure speculation once again. Mr. Bonds could very well be in better physical shape than I know of and if he thinks he could jump into a wrestling ring and do what we do, he would be more than welcome to try. By the way, just for the record, whether Bonds is on any illegal substances or not is irrelevant. In my opinion, he is a god amongst mere mortals when it comes to his ability to hit a baseball. Just wanted to make that clear before I see any headlines that read, “Professional Wrestler calls out Bonds on steroid abuse”. And for my fans who wonder what is on the play list for their Universal Champion… On the ol’ jukebox right now… Chevelle “The Clincher” But everyone in their right mind should see that the tabloids make all their money these days off the crazy stories that are so stupid that you just have to read them. What is it about needing to read something that is just so out there? I mean these papers look more incompetent every day yet they are read more than reliable sources. But after getting off topic, last week was something that I would like to forget. Not because Matthew came to my room and did what he did. It’s because of the media explosion that followed. Not once was I approached to shed light on the situation. Instead, reporters were looking for information from bellhops at the hotel and desk clerks. They even went so far as to try and interview Charity about the situation. First off, she wasn’t even there! Second, she had no idea the visit even took place because I never told her. Instead I have an angry woman coming to me asking me what Matt did to me. She was upset that I didn’t tell her about all of it. Now when I read any story in the paper about me that I know is not true, I shrug and pretend that it was never published in the first place. But when the tabloids start messing with my personal life in that way, that’s when I say “Fuck y’all” and fight back with my own words that could only be the truth. That could also be considered something everyone should know about me from this point on. Outside the ring when I am not slamming a chair, or in my case a DeMarini baseball bat, over the head of Joe Wrestler I will tell it as it is. I have no reason to lie to anyone if they want to know the truth. They need to come to me first however. Otherwise if you mess around and attempt to deliver some story that stretches the truth, I may not even honor your question with any type of rational answer. Instead I may just tell “Mike the Reporter Guy” that I enjoyed his wife the other night and to quote me in the sports page. There is nothing more that anyone needs to know about the incident in the hotel last week. Matthew came in, pushed me around a little and told me not to speak with his wife. Now before the media explodes with adultery scandals and all that jazz, I will make it clear here and now. Most everyone in the wrestling world should know that Mary Ward witnessed Matt getting his ass handed to him backstage at a PRIME event by me. That was the entire situation in a nut shell and I only went to apologize to her because she was never supposed to bear witness to such an event. Matt thought that was unacceptable and his actions followed. As Matt stood in my hotel room, I reminded him of some harsh realities that he should be aware of. Harsh realities that really don’t need to be mentioned here and now as they are not relevant to the point I am trying to get across. It is no secret that Matthew Ward and I have our differences. Our battle goes far beyond the squared circle. This has nothing to do with suplexes and pile drivers. This is life. Imagine one person not liking his neighbor because of some incident the involved the neighbor cutting his lawn one millimeter shorter making his own look less cared for. Now take that scenario and make it worth something that anyone would give a damn about. Matt Ward is not friends with Killean Sirrajin. Nor am I friends with Matt Ward. But our anger towards one another can be controlled. On the ol’ jukebox right now… Coheed and Cambria “The Final Cut” Just for everyone’s file on Killean Sirrajin, I do not get any of my ideas from the lyrics of music. What I listen to is my taste in music. So go blow it out your ass if you don’t like it. But this song specifically reminds me of ReVolution this coming week. Why you ask? Well I happen to have a teammate this week that enters the arena to just that song I mentioned above. Adam Dick is exactly what his last name says… he’s a dick. Now open your notebooks because it’s time for Sirrajin lesson one. Now anyone who even claims to know anything about me should know that I have never been one to willingly participate in the tag team scene. I feel that I do better on my own. But I don’t have an option this week. Adam Dick has talent, no doubt about that. The man just lost the Internet Championship and he can only go higher from there. Many have thought that he held himself down by holding that title for so long. The same can be said for my other partner. Vangelus Olsig used to be known as Dani Furher. He really broke out around this time last year. But who did he break out against? Come on trivia buffs, you know the answer. That’s right. Olsig took me to the limit in my hometown in a chairs match. Some may even argue that Olsig had me beat. Now I’m not going to sit here and tell everyone that they are full of shit because I would not be telling it like it is. To be honest, all it would have taken was another big move or two from Infamous Olsig to gain himself his first Universal Title reign. He made his impact and has not looked back since. However I can argue the fact that the Intense Championship is holding Vangelus down from accomplishing bigger things then he has ever dreamed of. In reality, I could sit here all day and tell stories about every individual I am going to be in the ring with at ReVolution 95, but then I would have to send this piece off to get published because it would turn into a novel. Oh the stories I have about everyone involved in this match. Well, all except for Adam Dick, but I thought I already made it perfectly clear. He’s a dick, end of story. But in saying that Vangelus was one move away from glory, the same could be said for the only person in this match who can claim she has balls in the ring but not in her pants. Karina Marie Wolfenden has gotten it right. She has killed many men, shattering their dreams all in the short three seconds it takes to pin their shoulders to a mat. She has gotten away with it. Not many can claim that they have stopped the Negasonic Lu… wait a second. What the hell does Negasonic mean anyways? Look in the dictionary, I dare you. Locate the word Negasonic. You’ll be looking all day. Sorry, that’s the end of that rant. But not many have stopped Miss Wolfenden in the squared circle. Not too many can claim that they have grounded the K-Wolf much less pin her or make her submit. I however, have done just that. In doing so, there is only one thing that everyone needs to know. This is the key to beating Karina Wolfenden in the ring. You need to prove you have more balls than she does. Simple enough right? Wrong. When I was in the ring with her no more than a year or so ago at Ultra Violence 2005, it would have taken another big move or two to seal the deal on her first title defense. She took me to my limit, just like Vangelus Olsig. The main difference is that Karina does not give up… EVER. She slows down and needs to rest, but if the match was to be decided in a King Kong Bundy-esque five count fashion, I’d suffice to say no one in PRIME can beat Karina Wolfenden. Think I respect her abilities much? But damnit girlie… stop being such a drain on society in the backstage area. You’d swear the world put you into a gun fight with nothing but a stick to fight with. Not everyone has dealt you the worst cards at the poker table. It’s all about poker faces. When the world hands you a bunch of lemons, make some fuckin’ lemonade! It’s not that bad really. Yeesh, these rants are getting longer. I promise I’ll keep my composure long enough to not sound repetitive. Now some of you may be wondering, why is it that I have been looking out for the welfare of Ignatius Lisieux? Well there are two simple reasons… First, I already explained that Tchu and I are bound to meet sometime in the future. Why should he need to be considered number one contender? When I want to beat him, I’ll beat him. Second, Ignatius was and has been a good friend of mine. Sure we may not have the same views on everything, but he’s always been there. Except for when he left and went mad… but that’s… yeah… When I was hurt way back in mid 2004, he called and asked how I was. He called and wanted to know when I would be back. But he cared about my health and well being. You can’t fake that kinda sentiment. When I did come back, I was sort of in a funk. I mean come on… I lost the Intense Title and then I lost to Tyler Lopez. I mean most people’s careers would have been over at that point, but Ignatius told me to keep fighting. He said things would eventually go my way. I then won my first match since returning. The rest as they say is history. Even if Ignatius is a friend, he is also a professional wrestler. Wrestlers can’t be trusted! It’s a proven fact that wrestlers actually do change their mind more than their underwear. For Karina’s sake, I hope she is the exception to the rule otherwise I’d like to keep my distance thank you very much. I won’t say much more on the topic. But Ignatius is as trust worthy as Matt at this point. I don’t trust anyone. I could go on but really… do I have to? I think my point has been proven quite well. The press and the different media dickheads over exaggerate stories to benefit their bank roll. I would rather have the truth out there. No more of this Tom Cruise freak show business. Everyone knows the fucker has a screw loose. Same with Ellen, you’re a damn dyke already, admit it… Oh, she did? Hey, I just pulled a C-Rad. Inside joke, not many of you would understand. But you know who you are pal. Either way, the truth is out there. But this time, it won’t take Mulder and Scully to find it. Come and ask me for the truth about Killean Sirrajin. It would make the most sense. However if you wanna know the truth about some other knuckle fucker like… Face-Eater, your outta luck because to him, left is green and up is white. He can’t tell the difference between a wrist lock and a wrist watch. Cut him a break though, it’s not his fault. Blame it on… eh screw it, it’s his fault. But don’t tell him that though, he might try to Titan St. James my ass and have his head squashed like a pimple in the process. But I hope you have all enjoyed this endless and perhaps mindless rant I have prepared. It only took a few beers and a good lay to put me on edge enough to tell you all the truth. Some of you attention whores in the back should have taken notes. And as for Adam Dick… Do I really need to repeat myself?
