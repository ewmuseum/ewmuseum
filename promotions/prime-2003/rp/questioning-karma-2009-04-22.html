Questioning Karma

22 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22/04/2009 “You have twenty six new messages, to listen to your messages press one. To save your messages press two, to delete your messages press three.....messages deleted” The shrill automated voice of Chris' answering machine faded once again into non-existence as he sighed and tossed the telephone to the side of his bed, causing it to bounce off and land on the rich wood panelled floor. The latest side effect was the constant headaches. From dawn until dusk they plagued him now. The second he opened his eyes he was greeted with a pain as though someone was drilling into his eye socket. He tried to make it through the day, to continue as normal but the slightest thing would set him off. The crying of a child in the street, a loud obnoxious co-worker who decided to talk at twenty decibels louder than everyone else. Even the dull hum of the air conditioning ripped through his skull causing him agony. As the pain seeped through he felt nothing but drained, he had not been to the gym in days. Even though his ever bulging muscles were crying out to be used he simply did not have the energy in him to get up and go. Instead choosing to simply lie in a darkened room and wish the pain away. As silly as it sounded he didn't want to take any drugs, which given his circumstances was understandable. He refused to go see the doctor for fear of them finding something out so instead he suffered in silence, acknowledging people when he had to when really it felt as though a marching band was going to town on his temples. A rush of nausea flooded Chris as he lurched forwards and vomited over the floor, narrowly avoiding the mobile phone. Clutching his stomach Meat breathed heavily, the veins on his neck bulging as his face turned crimson and panting heavily. He wasn't sure if this was side effects of the drugs, or side effects to the side effects. All he knew was that like clock work he was throwing up after meals now. His body was simply not keeping anything down. Taking in deep intakes of breath he was tempted to try and take stock of his life, but over the last few weeks he had given up on that. His mind was beginning to fight back, slowly but surely he could no longer blame anyone else for the problems in his life, because they had all been caused by him. He couldn't run away from it any more he had to face up to the truth. Stretching down he reached for his phone and raised it with a weak shaky hand in front of his face, clicking the buttons frantically. The phone was raised to his ear as her stared into the space ahead of him, his stomach churning as it attempted to bring up the remnants of his last meal. This had to end now. He couldn't keep running away any more. He had to take charge of his life. “We need to talk....now!” ~@~@~@~@ It had been a long lonely flight, but a flight that for all purposes, she had never wanted to end. She knew that as soon as she arrived at her destination, her mission was to destroy another woman's life. To split up a family unit. It went against every moral fibre in her being as she came from a broken home herself, she knew the effect it had on her not having a father figure. Hell you just had to look at how she treated men to understand that one. However her job was not as clear cut as right and wrong. Occasionally it ventured into the venue of life and death, more often than not hers. Yet as she sat in her car, looking at the quaint little home that was the Williams homestead, she could not bare to get out of the hire car, the car she had been sat in now for over an hour, just staring at the house. Ok the information that she was going to relay was true. She and Meat had been intimate on more than one occasion, for the beginning that was the main corner stone of their relationship. She needed to build the trust for the second part of the plan that was now more than into fruition. The youthfully optimistic Chris Williams was now well and truly little more than a slab of Meat, who entered the ring took the beatings and dished them out. The look on his eyes when he stepped into the ring was almost totally glazed over these days. As though he could not wait for it to end. Truth be told, she didn't even know who Chris was any more. He certainly wasn't the man with whom she shared a bed. He wasn't the man who, against all better judgement she had fallen in love with for a brief time. Maybe she would be doing him a favour, maybe it would send Kelly into a rage and snap him out of it. Claire shook her head in dismissal of the curious notion that had slipped to the forefront of her brain. Of course it wouldn't snap him out of it. Nothing would, except being taken off the drugs which she was administering on a regular basis, they made him strong but the long term side effects were beginning to show. He barely spent any time with anyone now, instead locking himself away where possible. Sure when the cameras were on he could put on a semi-cohesive showing of Meat and his manager, but as soon as they switched off he became the recluse again. The loss had not helped him either, after being beaten by Natas he had walked back to the communal dressing room, mumbling to himself repeatedly. She hadn't caught much of the in cohesive nonsense, but one thing seemed to break through, to be repeated more than others. “I didn't feel a thing. I should be in pain. Why didn't it hurt?” And she had no answer for him, nothing that would alleviate his pain or worry, not a single answer would be suitable, nothing except the outrageous truth would give any sort of indication as to why this was happening. She glanced at the clock once more and sighed, she couldn't procrastinate any longer for fear of the neighbour hood watch calling the cops, that was the last thing she needed trying to explain it all to the police. No it was time to do that which she had put off for so long. Closing the door of the car behind her she began the long walk up the path towards the door. The house was quaint, it had that rustic lived in feel about it that she imagined many a happy family to have been a part of. It was a house the screamed history but a history of happiness. The house radiated love and togetherness, now it was her job to shatter that. A few simple words is all it would take, a few simple words would end this image of happiness in this home and would instead turn it into one of despair and sadness. As Claire extended her hand to the doorbell she noticed that her hand was shaking. Claire was disgusted with herself at this notable sign of weakness, but this did not sit well with her in the slightest. If she didn't seem convincing then she may not believe her, more importantly why was she shaking? It wasn't as though she truly cared for him, not any more at least in the same way as before. Yet with everything that had happened between them, she could not help but feel guilty something which a career bitch never got used to feeling. She didn't have much more time to think it over as the door in front of her was pulled open, startling her somewhat. On the other side of the door was Kelly Williams, Chris' wife. She was pretty there was no doubt about that, but something seemed off about her. Her clothes looked dirty, her face drawn and pale, in total she looked as though she hadn't slept in about three days. Claire had never really been told much about Kelly, Chris rarely spoke about her and the company had only given her a very basic dossier. With all this she had made her own interpretation of how she would look, her demeanour, features even her figure. For some reason in her mind she had built her up as this stunning blonde supermodel, yet the woman in front of her was just a simple woman, you would find better in any mall across the country. “Miss Williams, may I come in?” Kelly looked at the woman in front of her through blood shot eyes, as though she had been sobbing constantly for days. As Kelly took in Claire and all that she was about she felt a pang of jealousy, prettier, younger and clearly more well off than her. An immediate bitter twang tore through Kelly. “Who are you and what do you want?” The words were cold and blunt, which caused Claire to take a cautionary step backwards, she had not wanted to come here in the first place, let alone do what she was about to do. Yet there was something about this situation that felt wrong, something wasn't right. She felt that honesty maybe the best policy. Not total honesty anyway. “Sorry, how rude of me. My name is Claire Saunders. I am an acquaintance of Chris, well his manager anyway. I have heard an awful lot about you.” Claire extended her hand towards Kelly's. However it was not greeted in return. It was instead greeted by a flood of tears and her hand being swatted away with a spit of disgust. “His manager? So maybe you can explain exactly why he's not been in contact with me?” Kelly strode forward and grabbed Claire shoulders shaking her violently. No one would normally come within two feet of Claire, let alone try to shake the life out of them. Claire however did nothing and was feeling notably worried about the situation. It wasn't fear as she was more than capable of handling herself. It was just that from all that Chris had spoken to her about Kelly, not once had he described her like this, as far as she could make out in the past there was not a violent bone in her but now. “I.....” she had barely allowed the first utterance of the words to be formed before she was cut off. “Perhaps you can tell me why he's been ignoring my calls and voice mails.” Kelly's bloodshot eyes were now flowing tears as she began to pound away at Claire's chest, causing her to step back slightly. There was no real weight behind the blows, just pain and frustration that had been trying to escape for some time. “Well he has been very busy, his new job and …...” It was the truth, but sometimes the truth is a fruitless argument. This time instead of being cut off, she stopped herself. When a woman is this angry and this upset, there are no words that are able to break through, the wave of emotion just needs to ride its cause before rational thinking can begin again. “To hell with his new job. I need him!” “Kelly....are you ok.” It was the most sincere thing that Claire had said in months, through all the lies, cheating and worst of all drugging of her client. Yet as Kelly stood in front of her, she could not help but feel sorry for her. “No....come in.” As Claire followed Kelly into the home an immediate wave of emptiness struck her like leprosy. She followed Kelly into a living room that had not been touched by any cleaning implement in days. Kelly's steps slow and weighted as though every step was an effort. After being pointed to a dirty sofa, Claire obligingly sat down in what she deemed as the cleanest part, trying to not make it too notable at has concern of the filth around her. Whilst she allowed her eyes to scour the room, she never let them leave Kelly for more than a few seconds. She was clearly a woman on the edge who was capable of doing anything. “What's happened Kelly?” Kelly was now sat opposite Claire, rocking back and forth. Every time her lips parted, nothing but a short burst of air came out. This continued for a while until Claire noticed Kelly's eyes dart sideways towards an open door to a bedroom. Rising slowly to her feet she approached the room tentatively, looking over her shoulder at Kelly who simply nodded at her, a sign that she was ok with it. As Claire approached, she could not help but notice how immaculate the room was, a direct contrast to the almost sty like conditions she was currently standing in. As she took in the room and its furniture, a horrible image was beginning to paint itself in her head. In the corner of the room was a freshly made bed, a small brown teddy bear sat on the pillows at the head of it. An open wooden chest full of toys, a wooden train and other numerous cuddly toys poking out from the top, no doubt filled with a mountain of similar belongings underneath. A faint smell of roses wafted across her nostrils as the spring breeze blew through the window and caught the scent of the yellow roses on a small desk to the side of the bed. Also on the desk was a small picture frame with Chris and Kelly holding their child and smiling happily. A smile that she had not seen on Meat's face in as long as she'd known him. Turning around she looked at Kelly once more, her chest became tight and struggled to breath as she forced out the question once again. A question that in her heart of hearts she knew the answer to, but prayed and hoped for something else. “What's happened Kelly?” As before, there was nothing to begin with, a low sobbing barely audible from Kelly who's face was buried in her hands. Claire remained silent as she made he way back to her seat, urging Kelly in her head to admit to the truth, begging her to answer if only for herself. “...he's dead.” The words were little more than a whimper, yet they held more weight behind them than any sledgehammer ever could. As Claire heard the words that Kelly had managed to sob out she rose to her feet, walked slowly and calmly over to Kelly and held her in her arms. She was physically shaking, the pent up emotion all being released as she sobbed continually in her arms. With every tear that landed upon her skin, the realisation came to Claire that she would not be able to complete her mission. She was a human being and would not put this woman through any more pain than required. Her mission was to break up their family. Nature had done that for her, she had nothing to do here. There was no point in furthering her despair, it would just be a wanton act of cruelty. She continued to hold Kelly in her arms in silence. This was not a time for words, it was a time for being there for someone in need. It didn't matter that she didn't really know her, she had shared her deepest emotions with her and at this moment in time. Claire was closer to Kelly than Chris had ever been. They remained in silence for a long time and as Claire's eyes fell upon the abandoned room once more, the room that had been kept so clean and pristine in place a tear fell from her eye. ~@~@~@~@~@ “Exactly that Stephen. I quit. I can't cope with this any more. My body is shot to hell. I am throwing up after every meal and sunlight makes me want to tear my brain out it hurts so much. But do you want to know the real crux of it Stephen? The real deal breaker for me? It's my family, I don't know if you have ever been in love or if you even have a family, but I was doing all of this for them. They are my world. My wife and my child. I want to be with them, I don't want to be a stranger to them.” With renewed strength and vigour Meat rose to his feet, for the first time in hours. He felt strength surging through him, but not the kind that had left him feeling numb in the ring. But passion and love, he was feeling again he had broken through the other side and had found his anchor within himself. “My son, is the most beautiful child I've ever seen. I don't want to come home and for him to not know who I am. Since me and Kelly found out she was pregnant all those years ago, all I ever dreamed of was being a dad. Being there through all of his troubles and strife's to be a guiding light to him and to smother him with all the unconditional love a father can give and then some. You see he's my world, my blood and my son. I can't leave him. It's been tearing me apart being away from him, it only took me poisoning myself to realise that I don't need money to be happy. I need my family.” The fire in Meat's eyes was now burning like a Phoenix rising from the ashes, he suddenly felt as though he could take on the entire world. “So take this for what you will Stephen, I quit. I am not going to be your puppet any more. Find someone else stupid enough to poison themselves, because in the end we were never any closer to taking them down. They are too good, how many tests did you do and find nothing? Well I tell you I am done. I don't want you to contact me again, I don't want your money. Hell if it costs me my job in PRIME so be it. I don't deserve it anyway, we both know they wouldn't have touched me knowing I was drugged up. No. Karma will be my judge here and will deem my punishment fit enough”
