A Day In The Life

28 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 28, 2009 "Who do you think will show up next?" A teenage boy asks, holding a sign that reads TSONDA FOR WORLD CHAMP! He almost has to scream so that his friend can hear him. Both of them are pushed up against the battered steel barricade, just inches away from their favorite wrestling stars. The often used security measure smells like... well, have you ever had a penny stuck up your nose? Like that, but after all these years it was still effective. So much so that PRIME takes it on the road with them for everything from autograph signings to catering lines. "I don't know, we haven't seen Bryan Dawkins yet have we?" "Dawkins. That'd be epic!" Tonight, that faithful security measure was held together by thick metal chains and master locks under an azure blue sky, now infused with tints of orange, purple, and red. Security guards dawned in yellow numbered sweatshirts surrounded the weathered barriers outside of the Crowne Plaza Hotel in Cleveland. It was arrival night for PRIME's biggest and best, and the city's wrestling fans turned out in droves. Neon colored signs could be seen in every direction. Everyone proclaimed to be someone's biggest fan, just like in every other city. It was almost like the red carpet at the Oscars. Of course, without the carpet, the wardrobe, or half the media it was more like the Razzies... but to the nearly one hundred fans who showed up to go star gazing it was better than any red carpet they could ever go to. Admittedly the talent often got a kick out of it themselves. While some could care less about the attention, others ate it up. Who could blame them? Most people grow up dreaming to be on the receiving end of moments like these. "Oh, here comes the next one!" A ginger kid yelled from the front row, slack jawed in awe as the next rental car pulled up. This one was a silver Honda Accord, slowing as it approached the valet station. The aspiring photographers in the gathering got their cameras ready while others made one last push to try and force their way to the front. This one was going to be the one they were waiting for. This next person arriving was going to be their favorite. The one they'd been waiting their entire lives to meet. As the valet driver approached the car, the crowd hushed in anticipation. The driver's side door opened slowly as a set of keys were tossed into the air. Catching them with a smile, the valet asked the driver a question before being waved away. Up on their tippy-toes, they could almost see who it was. So close yet so far away. It was Tony Gamble! No wait, it wasn't. Bryan Dawkins! Brandon Pierce? Troy Douglas? Speculation ran rampant as the huge figure emerged from the driver's side, almost as if he was emerging from a clown car. With his back to the crowd, a leather jacket hung over his shoulder. Hanging on his every movement, it didn't take long for the fans to let out a collective sigh as he turned around and the fans of PRIME suddenly found themselves face to face with one-half of the former PRIME Tag Team Champions... "The Iron Wolf" Kazys Jankauskas. "I was hoping it was Von Kelsig." A voice could be heard as Kaz walked around the front of the car, obviously making his way to the passenger side of the vehicle while the valet attendant waded his way through way too much luggage in the trunk. "I'm just glad it wasn't Chainz." That comment was accompanied by a shiver from a middle-aged woman, keeping a not-so-close eye on her two young boys trying to funnel their way to the front of the crowd. The Iron Wolf took a quick gaze over the crowd before turning his back to them. Grasping the silver passenger door handle, he opened up the passenger side door to the sounds of flickering flashbulbs. It was the most beautiful sound she'd ever heard in her life. "Ladies, gentlemen, children, and the elderly... this is not for the feint of heart. If you suffer from any pre-existing medical conditions that may be triggered by uncontrollable fits of excitement, please leave the premises. People of Ohio, finally your starlet has arrived!" Almost jumping out of the passenger side of the Honda Accord, the other half of the Wolves Of Slaughter posed with her arms in the air as the flashes reflected off of her big Jackie-O style sunglasses. Wearing a pair of designer jeans and a lined custom black leather jacket, Elise Ares smiled and posed for the cameras as her tag team partner just shook his head in embarrassment. "Please, please one picture request at a time. I can only be one place at a time." The crowd couldn't hear her as she stopped her poses and just greeted her 'adoring' pubic with a smile. While there was a huge ovation and everyone was clamoring for her attention in her mind, in reality the noise level wasn't all that much higher than it was before she arrived. Reality stopped her before, so why should that stop her? Plus in her defense, at least 75% of the crowd knew who she was. At least that was a significant improvement over Waco last show. With a big fake smile and continuous waving to the crowd, Elise's gestures became a little too much even for Kaz who stood next to her with an obvious grimace of frustration. As they drew closer to the crowds, the poster boards started waving more and more. It also because increasingly clear how much The Swaggeriffic One cared about those legions of 'adoring fans.' "You want an autograph?" She asked one fan, holding out a program and a black sharpie marker. He couldn't be any older than 12 years old. This was going to be his first PRIME show and he couldn't be more excited. "Yes miss." He asked politely due to the urging of his mother standing next to him, wondering how exactly she got roped into attending such an event with her son. She looked horribly out of place, finding herself making eye contact with one of PRIME's worst role models. "That'll be fifteen dollars." Elise said with a smirk. "Fifteen bucks?!" The mother quickly interrupted anything her son might have to say, "Everyone else has been signing for free! He's just ten years old!" "Well you have to realize ma'am, I'm not everyone else." The arrogant Ares had no issues showing as much, "I'm Elise Ares. I'm the greatest female wrestler in the history of PRIME." "Then why have I never heard of you before?" She chirped in, "I've heard my son talk about some girl named Lindsay Troy, but never an Elise Ares." The Swaggeriffic One measured up the little fan as Kaz tried to distance himself away from the situation as much as possible. He certainly isn't getting himself involved in this one. "I'd guess your son either has terrible taste in talent or is a special needs child and isn't capable of making decisions on his own." Elise looked on as his mother made a horrified face, "As a parent you should've done a better job teaching him to have good taste." Swinging for the fences, the little boy's mother winds up to hit The Swaggeriffic One with the bitchslap to end all bitchslaps... but quickly the men in yellow shirts jump in to pull her back. Elise looks down at the program and then back at the kid. "I take it you don't have any money?" Elise questions the ten year old boy. He doesn't answer, obviously more concerned about what is being done with his mother next to him. "Sorry kid, I don't do handouts. I'm not running a charity here." Tossing the magazine back over the security barricade, the audience looks horrified as Kazys hangs his head in shame. He just wants to hide, or at least get out of the public. How does she do this week after week? She seems like she doesn't know any better but how can a girl who is so smart be so... I don't think a word in the English language could possibly do it justice. "Kaz!" Elise walked away from the people, now throwing stuff at her out of hatred "I think we should go inside now, these people don't have any money." It was the most beautiful sentence he'd ever heard in his entire life.
