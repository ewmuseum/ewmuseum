Breathing life into the Balloon of depression

3 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/05 In an otherwise ordinary room, sit’s a rather extraordinary person, alone. Slouched in a large wooden chair, his shoulders hang lazily in front of his massive chest and his head is rested comfortably in a bed supplied by the palms of his hands. Audibly, the room is hiding nothing but it’s own silence, visually, the room reeks of eclectic normalcy, it’s the kind of room a Mormon who has otherwise given up would consider paradise (no offense). Yet, this individual remains, with Zen-like patience, waiting, waiting for something, anything at this point. The door behind the figure slowly opens, and the soft creaking isn’t enough to break his trance, but what follows is most definitely sufficient. “What the hell are you moping on aboot” The figure in the chair quickly raises his head from his hands and spins around to find himself staring at a twelve year old boy standing in the doorway with a sack thrown over his shoulder. Abrasive would be an understatement if you were going to try to describe him, yet he smiles broadly as the large figure in the chair grows as he stands to greet his visitor. “Dusty,” Azala Zameer greets his little friend with a big smile, “last Azala hear that Dusty was stuck in Canada. Azala tried to make phone call to get Dusty free, but Azala not really sure how to use phone all that good.” “Don’t worry aboot it big guy, I played my cards right and got the flock out of the shit hole of a country.” “Why Dusty keep saying that?” Zameer inquires “Saying what?” “Aboot?” “Canada does weird things to people big guy, really weird things.” Dusty trails off as he takes in the serene scene he just busted in one. Patiently looking around, until a quizzical façade attacks his visage. “What is this?” Dusty asks as he continues to inspect the room, “This room looks like an emo kid’s wet dream.” Taking a moment to look around himself, Zameer’s face turns downward. “Azala not know. Things have not been good for Azala lately and Azala can’t even remember what he had for lunch today.” “I bet it was Pizza Hut!” Dusty blurts out pointing to a red stain on Zameer’s shirt that can unmistakenly only be pizza sauce. Zameer shakes his head again then looks back to the floor, “Azala love Pizza Hut, but Azala not know for sure. Azala think he can taste pizza still, but……” “Hey, don’t sweat it big guy. On the way back into town I stopped at one of those internet cafes and looked up on your wrestling. You get a shot at the tag team championships this week on ReVolution!” “Azala not even know about that anymore.” Continuing to stare at his own huge feet, Zameer falls silent after his last statement. Up until this point, it’s been The Big Snack Daddy breathing life-wind into Dusty’s sails, but a little reciprocation is long overdue. “What are you talking about!” Dusty throws out raising his voice a little bit. “I’ve read the stories, I’ve seen the papers, hell, I’ve even was talking to this dude on my trip back to the states and he was going on and on about how his kids love you and how you’re the best thing to happen to professional wrestling, well, ever!” “Ever?” “Ever!” responds Dusty even more enthusiastic than the first time. Zameer’s posture takes in a little life as Dusty continues on. “He was going on about how he used to love watching wrestling, back in the days of Dr. Hardcore, D-Spayre, The Rock-n-Roll Killer Jeff Miller, and a whole bunch of other names. Apparently the wrestling gene was passed to his children who now love watching wrestling, but with how professional wrestling has gone downhill the last five to seven years, he wasn’t sure about letting his kids watch. He was talking about he’d rather have his kids learn about sex education at school, he’d rather have his kids learn about drugs at school, he’d rather have them learn how to speak properly rather than running around cussing and swearing, imitating these wrestlers they watch on TV.” “This man lets his children watch Playboy TV?” “I don’t know, he had a mullet and wore a “Skid Row” T-shirt, I’m sure he has a satellite on his trailer home,” Dusty admits. “Back on track though. He told me that the only reason he allows his children to watch PRIME is because of you! You brought back the “Do the Right Thing” to a profession where guys and girls are constantly doing the wrong thing. He told me, that if his kids could grow up and be like you, then he’d be a success as a father.” “He told you that?” “Yeah, he told me a bunch of other things too, but that’s all I really remember. He kept rubbing his nose and he wouldn’t shut up for two seconds.” “But Azala not thing that Gibbo wants to be partner anymore.” “Why the hell would you think that?” Dusty retorts. “You and Gibbo took PRIME by storm! You two accomplished something that most other teams only dream about! Gibbo partially owes his success in PRIME so far to you, and you to him! Why the hell would he not want to be your partner?” “Azala not know. Azala not see Gibbo much at all for past two weeks, since Gibbo started problems with Killean and Aimz.” “Ya know, I read about that too,” Dusty admits, “and that’s bull shit! Gibbo can’t throw away everything you have because he’s got some problems with other wrestlers!” “Azala just don’t know anything right now. Azala has been turned against in the past, betrayed, and broken, but Azala never been abandoned before. Betrayed, broken and beaten made Azala mad, full of rage! But abandoned just make Azala, sad.” “Would you listen to yourself!” blurts out Dusty snapping Zameer attention away from whatever thought he was trying to process. “You don’t even know what Gibbo’s going through! Nobody does except for him, Killean, and Aimz! “Azala just wanted Gibbo to be Azala’s friend” “You are friends! You’re better than friends! You guys are gonna be the Tag Team Champions of the World!” “The World?” replies Zameer “The whole MOTHER, FUCKIN, WORLD!” screams Dusty at the top of his lungs. “Now pick up this phone, call Gibson, and get your shit in order, we’ve got to leave if we’re ever going to make it to…………where the hell is Rev this week?” A mystery voice breaks the sudden bizarre silence, “Denver Colorado” “Man I hate the Mountains” Dusty states as plainly as he’s stated anything thus far, “Never the less, get your stuff, it’s a long haul from………….. where the hell are we?” Same mystery voice, “Milwaukee, Wisconsin” “And how in the hell did we end up in Milwaukee?” Dusty asks to nobody in particular as he begins rounding up Zameer’s stuff. “Azala saw giant hot dogs running around baseball field and got hungry, so Azala flew to Wisconsin.” “See! You’re remember things all ready!” Dusty runs around with a flare of excitement that Zameer has lacked the last few weeks and actually starts to infect Zameer as well. Zameer: Azala think things be better this week too! “Why the one line of script after a whole novel style rp?” questions Dusty. “Azala need practice to get ready to do Segs again now! “Whooooooooooooooo~!”
