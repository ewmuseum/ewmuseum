Why me? (Rp for Morales vs Hessian)

17 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04-17-05 Why? What in the hell have I done to these gringos? Am I to believe that this isn't yet another racist move on the part of the PRIME owners? Another move to yet again do their best to destroy any member of Los Diablos that they can? Sure they are packaging it like they've done me a favor. Making it seem like they are helping me out by giving me what they would like to refer to as a 'warm up' match to get me ready for the Cruiserweight Tournament. To those gringos who subscribed to this theory I have this to say... BULLSHIT! This isn't a warm up match for Enrique Morales. Oh no it is something on the exact opposite end of the spectrum to a warm up match for me. It is more along the lines of a damn massacre. An attempt to end my career, not only here in PRIME, but wrestling in general...rather than to help me with it. How else could you possibly view a travesty of this nature? Is there any other way to describe the clearly cruel and unusual punishment that these racist owners of PRIME have bestowed upon me? With my first round match up in the Cruiserweight Tournament coming up this couldn't have possibly come at a worse time for me. Hessian. By God all mighty they have given me Hessian. Sent me out to be the sacrificial lamb in the face of an angry Satanic demon. He is their answer for destroying and ending another complete nut job, Vampir, and what do they do? They stick me against him as a way of 'getting back' at me for, God knows what, while giving Hessian a nice, easy, warm up for his arrival in PRIME. Why me? He is the answer to destroying one of the biggest maniacs in all of professional wrestling and now I, Enrique Morales, am suppose to step into the ring with him and attempt to beat him? This is insane! Damn it I don't deserve this type of respect from this company. I've done so much for them already. I have gotten them exposure by joining the Cruiserweight Tournament, by getting Baltasar booked in the dual main event of the up coming Core Wrestling show, in Atlantic City, against Munson and Seymour Almasy. I brought their tag team division the premier tag team in all of wrestling, when I gave them Los Diablos. So how do they repay me? A fucking one way ticket, straight to hell, at the hands of quite possibly the most unstable person to ever set foot in this business. Damn you Hin See. Damn you and your little boy too. Damn you both straight to hell for this. 'Mr. Morales how do you feel about the chance to face off against Hessian?' 'Mr. Morales are you afraid of the Satanic Demon?' 'Mr. Morales will Baltasar and Angelus come to the ring and save you when things get out of hand?' The questions kept coming at a fever pace. It seemed that every single wrestling reporter, for every single wrestling magazine was standing between him and the doors to the hotel where his tag team of Los Diablos and himself were staying, preparing for ReVolution 61. 'Please, gringo morons, give me a bit of space! One question at a time you damned fools!' Morales began to yell, very irrated like, toward the crowd of reporters in front of him. Fire burned through his eyes, but the truth be told, the fire was simply hiding the fear that was hiding behind his eyes. The fear that the reality of his up coming match still hadn't even fully set in for him. Sure he was already scared...but he wouldn't know how scared he would be until he actually stepped foot through those curtains and saw the face of the Satanic Demon, in person, for the first time. 'Mr. Morales could you tell us how you feel about Hin See and Tuan Pham giving you a match against Hessian, as your first official match up for the PRIME company?' The question came forth from the mouth of a large reporter in a sports coat. The guy, who had to weight around the three hundred fifty mark, was already sweating a great deal from the hot sun that was beating down on him from the Orlando skyline. A skyline that was a perfect blue, to most of the world, but seemed as dark and black as could be to Enrique at this point. 'How do I feel? How the hell do you think I should feel essa? I am being booked to face a legit monster! A man, no scratch that notion gringo, a fricken lunatic who is going to try and kill me to prove a point. To say that I am not pleased with this decision is a bit of an understatment I would think!' Morales excitedly replied, flapping his arms off to his sides in a wild manner in an attempt to further display his displeasure with this whole spectacle. 'Mr. Morales do you feel that you have any shot at all against Hessian without the help of your friends, and clients, Baltasar and Angelus?' A sly smirk came across the face of the young, brash, and clearly cocky African American writer as he asked the question. Clearly he was confident that he would get Morales to say that his plan would be to have Baltasar and Angelus take care of this problem for him. 'My brother,' Morales began with an annoyed look on his face, and quickly getting the attention of the none to amused writer, 'I feel that I have a chance in just about any match on earth. But let us face the facts here for a moment, essa, when we speak in referance to this match. This match isn't one made of earth, now is it my friends? No...this match is clearly one that can only be made straight from the depths and evils of hell. Only sinners like Hin See and Pham could possibly come up with something like this.' 'Are you saying Mr. Morales that you are afraid to step into the ring with Hessian?' As the question rolled off the lips of the tiny, white, female reporter with long, dark, curly hair Morales just looked back at the women with complete and utter shock. Not like he was offened that she would think him a coward, but almost perplexed that she could even ask a question of such a stupid nature. 'For the love of all that is good and holy in my home country of Mexico, are you mentally challenged woman? Look at the genetic freak that I am going to step into the ring with! Who, in their right frame of mind, would possibly step into the ring with a mad man like that and not be scared?' Morales screamed with the fear seemingly rushing forth from his body. The little man had never displayed this level of fear, caution, or anguish in all his years in the business. This might have even been the first time that anyone had seen him with real fear about an upcoming match, 'Hell I wasn't even this scared sneaking my way into the damned Halo match!' 'Do you have any plans for how you intend to approach your match with Hessian this week?' The question was surprising, only by the fact that it actually wasn't expected. Usually guys were asked how they planned to win matches, not how they planned to approach them. A smile came over the lips of Morales. 'Yes I indeed do. It's called the don't get killed plan. I have every intent of walking out of that ring in one piece, if at all possible, and that is my plan for ReVolution this week.' The reply from Morales brought a few chuckles from the crowd. It even lightened his spirits, just a tad, which had been dangerously low since the announcement of his match. 'Is it possible we could see the hiding under the ring trick once again, in order to survive?' The middle age man smiled as he asked the question, in an almost joking manner, but you could almost sense that he really believed that it maybe the only way Morales would walk out of the match in one piece. 'Essa, if I thought for one minute that it would work, you better believe my Spanish ass would look out for itself and use that trick again,' Morales paused for just a moment as the people in attendance all got a little laugh out of the response, 'but unfortunately I don't see that being a real option this time. I'm sure Hessian is no fool and will know all about that trick long before he brings his sick and twisted ass to the ring this week.' 'Mr. Morales do you believe that this match could be in response to your part in the stealing of the gate money on the last edition of ReVolution?' This question did, clearly, offend Enrique. The almost hurt look that crossed his face would have made even the most staunch Los Diablos hater, look at him with pitty. This quickly brought to an end the almost pleasant nature the process had started to take, causing Morales to crash back into the reality that things indeed were still very real for him at this moment. 'STEALING! What the hell are you driving at gringo? The Imperium didn't steal a damn thing on ReVolution. We simply relocated it, in order to make sure that it was given to the proper charities and groups. That isn't stealing, you racist prick, that is called giving back in the glorly of the Lord, who has given us so much.' Morales finished just as his darkly tanned face turned a bright shade or read. The anger could be seen, clearly, right behind the fear that still danced in his eyeballs. 'Well sir, with all due respect, you did attack some unarmed cashiers and such with baseball bats. That doesn't look much like a relocation of funds...it appeared to be more like gang warfare than anything else.' 'You, sir, are a foul mouthed, lying, Mexican hating gringo! You only compared that to a gang attack cause three of the members of The Imperium are of Spanish backgrounds. Are you implying, you pompus ass, that all Mexicans must be gang members?' By this time Morales was really heated. The pressure of having to face Hessian, the tournament, and the tag team title match that his team of Los Diablos had coming up were clearly causing him to crack. Finally he snapped. 'NEVER MIND! Don't even try to answer that. I have enough problems on my shoulders right now with needing to prepare my team for their title shot, and equally as important, trying to figure out just how the hell I'm going to keep from getting killed at ReVolution this week. If this is the only type of questions you people have for me then I am going to get on with my business. Adios Amigos.' Quickly Morales snaked his way through the throng of people before him, all of whom were still trying to get a quick answer or quote to add to their reports, and into the lobby of the hotel. He quickly made his way down through the lobby and to the elevators at the back in order to get to his room. As he punched the up button for the elevators and waited he began to sweat even more and a look of sickness covered his face. He felt light headed and almost as if he would puke.. the thoughts of facing Hessian now flooding his thoughts once more. 'Dear Lord...I'm going to die.'
