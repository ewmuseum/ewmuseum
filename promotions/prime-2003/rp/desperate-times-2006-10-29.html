Desperate Times

29 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/29/06 In a Pittsburgh area hotel, there could be no joy for Kenjiro ITO. A bottle of champagne remained on ice, unopened. The phone book remained opened next to it; on the end table, the number of a local call-girl establishment had been circled. That number, of course, remained uncalled. They sat there as reminders, preparations for a celebration that would now never occur. Reclined in the bed, alone, was one Kenjiro ITO. The Bringer of the Ice Age wore a pair of powder blue boxers, lying on top of the covers. As physical as his match at the Great American Nightmare was, he showed few signs of it. After all, Adam hadn’t pounded him into submission. Hadn’t dropped him on his head. All he’d done was choke ITO the fuck out…which was enough to retain his title. Enough to keep Kenjiro ITO title-less in PRIME. Enough to keep the life of the party in his room. “Fucking Sonny won his match,” ITO groused, obvious disdain in his voice. “It’s a damn good thing I unplugged the phone from the wall, or the bitch would have called to gloat by now. The Chinese bitch and Tony FUCKING Gamble won titles tonight, and I lose to *ADAM*? Fuck…I need a beer. Strike that, I need a 12-pack.” With a quick click, the room’s television whirred to life, and ITO flipped through all manner of garbage that was on at 2 AM. SportsCenter and re-runs of sitcoms lived and died in between clicks, until ITO settled on an infomercial. “Oh God, not this guilt-trip shit again…” Representatives of Feed the Children stood surrounded by impoverished children, pleading for money to help them. It seemed so easy. Just $7 a month provided fifty pounds of food for a hungry child. Even Kenjiro ITO, noted cynic and hard-ass, was touched. After all, while dead baby jokes were very easy to come up with, starving children weren’t quite so easy a target. “Fucking ridiculous,” ITO said, flipping the channel to try and rid his mind of the images. “This country’s the wealthiest nation on Earth, and these people have to beg the scum of the Earth who watch TV at two in the morning to help starving kids? You’d think the government would just write a big check, and bam! No more hunger. Sometimes, the United States gives me a fucking headache. It’s like those guys who wear red that I steal change from around the holidays. The Sal-something Army. Whatever. This place is rich as shit and we have to have old people beg for money. This shit never happens in Japan!” Rather than bother finding another television channel, he simply clicked the TV off. Kenjiro was pissed, and for more than just an infomercial and a loss to a man he called the “Scottish Sheepfucker”. “Hessian,” was the only word out of ITO’s mouth, as he rolled out of bed. That was all he really needed to say to know the variety of shit that he was in. Kenjiro was a confident man by nature. There weren’t a lot of people he doubted his ability to beat. But a 7’2”, 315 pound man he likely couldn’t hit with the IIA sent ITO’s doubts whirling throughout a mind that admittedly wasn’t in the most confident of moods. Silvio Fiore had beaten him. So too had Tony Gamble. Three matches with Adam had ended in two draws and a loss. Fuck, ever since the Dual Halo, he’d won one singles match… “Against Kyle Lamen,” ITO spat, finishing his own thought. “A guy who was dying. Great. The best I can do is beat a terminally ill man. What’s next, Kenjiro Ito vs. Colon Cancer Patient? Yeah, I can build a really good rep by hitting the IIA on some guy with heart disease. My career PRIME record is comparable to that of Peter Vetra. God, when did I start sucking this hard? I’m putting Killean Sirrajin’s free time in the slammer to shame!” The joke would have made him laugh most of the time, but he couldn’t say he was especially interested in his own material right now. Not with Hessian looming on the horizon, threatening to hand him yet another defeat. “Jewel in the Crown’s my only fuckin’ chance to make something of myself. Hell, I actually LISTENED to that pissant Chet Worth and kept quiet on 109 because I wanted this chance so damned badly. This is everything for me. If I lose…what the fuck’s left? Teaming with El Rayo Azul in opening matches?” ITO made his way through the room into the bathroom, taking a good look at himself in the mirror. The blonde dye in his hair and goatee was half sweated out, naturally black stubble clinging to his jaw line. In short, he looked like Hell, which pretty accurately mirrored his emotional state. “I was molded in Hokkaido to be one of the best. I came here to America to prove those stuffy fucks who run wrestling in Japan that I could succeed without two years being a glorified jobber to shit like Boston crabs. And now, I’m in the biggest promotion this country’s got to offer…and I can’t buy a fucking win. I CAN’T EVEN BEAT A SCOTTISH GUY!” His hand shot out, sweeping his toiletries from the sink counter. A rather expensive bottle of cologne shattered and broke, but the Osakan didn’t even notice. The only thing he could think about was his reflection in the mirror. In the mirror, he no longer saw himself. Instead, he saw one of the children from the infomercial. Hungry, pleading eyes stared back at him, and the words of the presenters echoed in his mind. “Just $7 a month,” he heard, again and again. That might work to stave off the starvation of a hungry child, but Kenjiro knew that he had no such quick fix. The specter stalking him was not an invisible killer like hunger, but rather the very easily visible visage of one of wrestling’s most feared men. Hessian was a mercenary. Like hunger, he didn’t care who he killed. ITO blinked, and shook his head hard. When he glanced up at the mirror again, his face had returned. Though his countenance remained grave, the experience had made him realize something. Something that reminded him just WHY he found the Feed the Children commercial so disconcerting. Something that gave him hope. “Those kids are helpless,” ITO whispered. “Dependent on charity to save themselves. They sit and wait and hope that someone will help them. That someone will save them. Fuck that shit.” Though his back was against the wall with a lethal psychopathic monster breathing down his neck in practically his last chance to make something of himself, there was still something in Kenjiro ITO that made him believe that he would tap-dance on Hessian’s broken dreams of Jewel in the Crown glory. “I’m Kenjiro ITO,” he stated, drawing himself up to his full height, and gazing into his own, now-confident expression. “I save myself.”
