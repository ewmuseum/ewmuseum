Breathing and Beeping

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1-23-06 Apathetic. That is the word that describes my mood right now. I've got a match coming up against Nova, and two people I don't even have the time to bother to remember, and yet I couldn't care less. This match doesn't bring me any closer to where I want to be in PRIME. Why should I, or anyone else for that matter, care if Baltasar gets a victory next to his name in the history books in a match where four of those involved don't even matter? Sure if this was a one on one match up this would be something to look forward to. A match to put a stamp on one's career with. This isn't one on one though. This isn't Nova vs Baltasar. This isn't the main event of a ReVolution or Pay-Per-View where the draw is seeing if the hero of the fans can slay the raging monster who is pissing everyone off. No this is a match. A six man tag where four others, who don't even matter at this point, are also involved. Anyone of of those four could win the match. Anyone of them could lose it. This is just some thrown together match to open ReVolution with. So why exactly am I meant to care? People are going to say that I should be taking every shot I get to prove I'm a big time player in PRIME. Taking every single match up I'm placed in as another shot to push my name that much further. That is a fine thing for people like the fans and bottom feeders ,who will never be anything more but just that, to say. But for someone who has been around long enough to know a little bit about how this business works it isn't a statement that I'll hang my former mask on. Winning a six man with two new arena light inspectors as my partners isn't going to be something that gets my name deeper into the record books. Does PRIME management really believe that I should go out there and give on hundred percent to win a match with Jake Keeton as my partner? I mean seriously. This is more of a joke than the time someone tried to sell people on the idea that Black Angel and Boda were legit champions. It simply won't float. Jake Keeton is a nothing. He has always been a nothing and he will always be a nothing. Is winning the Primetime Championship Cruiserweight Championship a couple of times suppose to impress someone? I hold a worthless title like that right up there with winning the Power Surge Wrestling World Heavyweight Championship. Those belts are mere paper weights for the pencil pushers of real companies desks. This is nothing for me to be excited about. Whoopie! I get to carry Jake Keeton to a victory in his debut match. Yay for Baltasar! PRIME has really done me a great favor this time!!! WOOOOOOOOO. Pardon me if I sound less than happy or optomistic about such a wonderful 'gift' by PRIME. Of course it gets worse. Yes I know that is hard to believe. Worse than Jake Keeton seems almost impossible. But believe me folks there is worse. Much worse. It just so happens that much worse is the other partner I have. El Vice. Now here is a Spanish man that absolutely represents everything that is wrong with our culture. He is just the kind of guy that allows foolish, racist, idiots in America to make their little comments about Spanish people, and especially about Mexico. The Spanish Elvis. Even I, a truly proud Spanish man, knows that is the most idiotic thing I've ever heard. Elvis was the King of Rock. Leave him the fuck alone. So as you can see I have less than great things to be really excited about heading into my first match with Nova. This should be one of those moments people are watering at the mouth for. It is the type of match people should be paying their money to come to the show and see. Instead they are paying to see us open ReVolution, with some has beens and people who have yet to be, and the main event is being occupied by people that no one could possibly be willing to honestly say they are a fan of. Such is the life of a wrestling superstar. Specially the life of one who makes his home in PRIME. They say the cream always rises to the top...that isn't always the case here. Sometimes all you need, hell a lot of the time, is some silly ass attitude, a funny line or two, and a couple of morons to follow you around in hopes of becoming something 'greater' while rubbing elbows with you. All this coming at me after one of the greatest, and yet still not complete, nights of my professional career. A mere week after taking it to anyone who was foolish enough to get in my way, laugh at me, or speak out of turn got exactly what they had coming. Everything was absolutely in line for the most perfect of evenings for me. Ending all this silly speculation that I needed people. Putting to rest all these theories that I owed Enrique or The Imperium for everything I've accomplished. Finally having the chance to prove that the only support I'd ever needed was that of Angelus. My buddy. My best friend. Hell the man is like a brother to me. Possibly it is more approperiate to now say was. Yet even the man that I respect more than anyone else on this planet, the man that I've put my body, career, and life in his hands during matches was able to find a way to be as useless as common sense to a person working personally for Tyler Nelson. Angelus couldn't get the job done. He couldn't even manage to keep his shoulders off the mat long enough for me to once again carry his ass to a victory. Once more he was able to screw up a big time win and moment for me by being a complete waste of God given breath. Well he won't be wasting anyone's time like that again. He certainly won't ever be wasting mine in that manner again. Angelus is gone. Out of PRIME forever. Hell if he ever even walks again it's going to be a damned miracle. Angelus is the lamb led to the slaughter for everyone in PRIME to take notice. What happened to him can just as easily happen to anyone who decides they wish to make me their personal joke, stepping stone, or punch line to their mocking jokes. Don't you see? I have nothing to lose. I have nothing to keep me sane anymore. Even those who I actually do love and care about in this business are not safe from my actions. Keep that in mind this week Nova, and his little partners as well. If I am willing to destroy my best friend, a man who was pretty much my brother, to the point of him more than likely never walking again...then you can only imagine what I'll be willing to do to those who I hold no personal attachment too. See this match is nothing to me. Winning or losing it means zero in my book. It's just another meaningless match. Something to go through the motions of. But the silver lining is that since I'm not tied down to caring if I win or not. Since losing isn't even important in matches like this then it means I'm truly free. Free to do as I please. To destroy what I feel needs to be destroyed. To remove anyone who I feel shouldn't be in my path at any given moment. Tread lightly dear Nova. What Youngblood gave you would be a walk in the park...compared to not walking at all. ********** Breathing and beeping. The only two sounds being made in the dark room marked 324, in the intensive care unit of Presbyterian Medical Center in Philadelphia were just that. Breathing and beeping. The slow, very quiet, peaceful sound of Angelus, former Los Diablos member and PRIME superstar, as he rested in a deep sleep. The long painful days ahead only a distant thought in the back of his sleeping mind. The beeping was slow and at a certain rythm as the machines hooked to him recorded his every vital sign. The first week or more of his long process would be cruical. No one was even considering the idea of walking again. That was far, far, far into the future...if it ever even happened again. No, right now, the only thing that was important was making sure nothing went wrong after the surgery. Making sure that nothing became out of line or dangerous to his life. Walking might never happen again but that certainly didn't mean he had to die either. He continued to breath as peaceful as possible. 'I'm so sorry hermano.' The words came forth from the darkness in a very weak and tired state. The shadow of Baltasar, The Red Devil, or the man who put Angelus in this state (however you prefer to look at him) stood a few feet from the bed looking down at the beaten and shattered body of his once best friend. Only the outline of his body could be made with just glimpses of the rest of him dancing in and out of the moonlight that strained to come in to the room from behind the curtain. 'This is something I will never be able to make up for or undo.' Baltasar said quietly as he took a couple of steps closer to Angelus. After a moment Baltasar reached his right hand out and touched the back of Angelus's left as he lay in the bed sleeping. Everyone who knew them had long ago left. Off to the hotels to get a good nights rest before the long day tomorrow, and the many more after that. If any of them had been there then it wouldn't have been possible for Baltasar to show up here. In all honesty there was no way anyone could blame them either. Angelus only laid, confined to this bed and Halo, cause of Baltasar. 'My anger and destructive nature has finally got the best of me hermano. That anger you begged me to take care of long ago. Finally it has snapped me. Taken me to a place I never thought I'd go. By my own hands you lie in this bed now waiting for the news that you'll more than likely never walk again. All I can think about is for what?' The question trailed off out into the silence of the dark room. What had it all been for? To prove that he wouldn't take losing lying down anymore? To prove that he deserved some sort of respect that more than likely he'd never earned? What exactly was it about wrestling, and wrestling for PRIME in particular, that made destroying the rest of his best friend's life seem like a good idea? To be the champion someday? 'I will never be able to tell you just how sorry I am Angelus. My pride and stupidity will never allow me to admit the wrong I've placed upon you...to anything other than your sleeping form and the darkness of my own thoughts. I'll never be able to show my remorse to the world, to you personally, or any of your family and our friends. The anger that fuels me is too strong for me to over come I'm afraid. I'm a lost man in the sea of a million lost men and women. Content in the knowledge that I'll never be able to escape the hold.' In the moonlight, that happens to sneak it's way the the slits in the sliding curtains, a tear drop rolls down the face of The Red Devil. His hand shakes as he finally moves it away from Angelus and back into the dark with him. Almost symbolic in a way. As if his hand represented the last little bit of him that was still within the light, finally being pulled into the darkness with the rest. 'I've made my bed, and unfortunately yours, with my ways dear Angelus. It's something I'll now have to live with forever. I wish I hadn't allowed the darkness to consume me so much that it took your life as well. You may never walk again dear friend but you're still a far bigger and better man than I shall ever be. My fate is probably sealed to a wheelchair. My fate is now sealed to eternal damnation. I've gotten what I deserved for my actions. A date with The Devil for all eternity. ' Baltasar pauses as he slowly backs his way further into the shadows, across the room toward the exit of the room, before finishing just as he reaches the doorway. 'I can only hope that one day you get what you deserve as well. Hopefully you'll not pay for my actions against you forever.' He opens the door and turns for one last look before walking away for good. 'May the second chance you deserve be in your future.' The door closes. The room returns to near silence. Only the breathing and the beeping may once again be heard.
