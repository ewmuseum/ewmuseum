Blood On My Hands...Finger-Lickin' Good! (ReV 113, w/ Fuck You vs. Troy-Kill-Tchu

29 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/29/06 All is dark and quiet in Nova’s TBA hotel room (that’s where ReV 112 went down, right?). Moonlight fights through the drawn curtains and tiny mellow beams dance triumphantly on the opposing wall. A muffled voice can be heard outside, getting louder as its owner drunkenly – no – wastedly – no – SHMAWASTEDLY – approaches. “SHO ME DAWAYDA GO ‘OME! ‘CUZ I’M TIREDENAIWANNAGODABED! HAD ME ADRINKABOUDA ‘OWWERAGO, ‘N IT WENT STRAIGHTOOMAH...” THUD! With a dull…um…see above…the door to the Rising Star’s temp abode rattles. Apparently unlocking the door and turning the knob to gain entry is really gathering steam with the kids these days, but Nova attempts to play it Old School, saloon-style. The THUD! you heard was the door winning, BTW. After so much jiggling of keys, shameful swearing, and general buffoonery that if I wrote it out it’d make the Dual Halo look like one of Pete’s two-liners, the door swings open and Nova lurches inside, his body leaned forward and his fate consigned to whatever gravity has in store for him. And tonight, gravity is down. Without pause, Nova sails past the doorway and face-plants happily onto the mattress of his queen-sized bed. QUICK ASIDE: Do you know how they came up with “Queen-Size” mattresses? Government scientists measured exactly, down to thousandths of an inch, how much mattress it would take for Lindsay Troy and her hair to sleep comfortably. Back to Nova. Drool makes its way in a single brave rivulet down through his beard towards the bedspread. There’s definitely a reason Nova is so betrunken this evening… …and that reason’s name is Jason Snow. The whole [STABLE NAME REDACTED] gang… …wait…this is my RP, right? I don’t have to do that? FUCKING SWEET! The whole FUCK YOU gang went out for a couple of rounds after ReV 112 to celebrate a few truths that had been gloriously upheld over the course of the evening: A) In the words of Outkast, they were still, collectively, “the baddest mother-funkers on the planet.” B) Despite the Worth’s ricockulously stupid decision to fuck Nova out of a match that the Rising Star had in the bag before he woke up that morning and smoked his first joint, at the end of the night it was still FUCK YOU slapping fives and loving life. So drink they did, the four horsemen, but Nova, despite his love for the sauce, cut himself off after a few beers because he’s tight-fisted with money. Deville wasn’t having it, pronouncing to the barkeep (and everyone else in the place for loud he said it) that he would be picking up Nova’s tab. From then on it got disgusting. Eventually Deville faded back from the steady stream of liquors and carbonated grains to sit in his swivel-stool, arms folded across his chest and a smirk/smile smeared contentedly across his face underneath glassy eyes. Even Rollins eventually topped out, stumbling blatantly away from his barstool in search of a jukebox that he could ambush and either rock out to or destroy. He had no preference. But Snow…boy howdy. He hung in there, wasted as he was, and when the dust cleared and the final drink was ordered, the Original Villain looked over to his comrade-in-drunk to find Nova passed out in his chair, shot glass still gripped in his hand and mouth hanging wide open, his tongue flapped out like a desert wilderness challenge survivor witnessing rain for the first time in days. Deville got two fanboys standing nearby to carry Nova out to his rental car, and paid them each with hairs plucked from the Most Dangerous Pubes in Wrestling. Up to speed? Understand? Sweet. Just before losing consciousness for the rest of the evening, Nova rolls over onto his back, hands running up the sides of his face into his blonde mane…and he begins laughing. Hard. He had been fucked. He had returned fire. He had ended the night on his feet, not on the cement floor like a goddamned sucker. And that felt good. Shit, that felt fucking amazing. “Zzzzzzzzzzzzz…” Aaaaand he’s out like a light, having never realized that the answering machine next to his bed was blinking furiously. That’s when the phone rings. And rings. And rings. “Hrmph…” Nova groans incoherently, rolling over on his side and sheltering his head with a pillow. Ring ring ring…CLICK! “Chris? It’s Ariel. I’ve been trying to contact you all night. The front office people at PRIME said this was where you were staying, but I don’t…oh, well. If you haven’t checked the other messages, which I guess you haven’t or you would’ve probably called, I was just…Lulu is sick. *sigh* I just wanted someone to talk to about it. She never gets sick like this. I’m driving myself crazy trying to figure out what to do. Please call me, Chris. Please. Goodbye.” CLICK! “Zzzzzzzzzzzzzz…” Nova doesn’t even stir. ------*~*~*------ A MESSAGE FOR TCHU: I’VE GOT BLOOD ON MY HANDS! I’VE GOT BLOOD ON MY HANDS! Hey, everyone, quick…change it to ESPN2. Tchu is on, and he’s playing his favorite sport…Talkmadshitaboutpeoplewhenyouknowyoudon’thavetobackitup Ball. “And that’s why I will see you tonight during your match. I will cost you that match. And then I will kick your stoner ass.” That is what you said, right? On the show? I can’t really remember for all the feathers that were flying around…you know…from you and Killean attempting to ruffle your feathers up and appear more intimidating somehow. You’re a couple of peacocks, Tchu. A regular rafter of Turkeys. Yes, a group of turkeys is called a rafter. Thank me later. But in the African Bush that is PRIME, my boys, we’re the lions. We’re the pride. So you didn’t do what you said you would, opting instead to swing a chair wildly at Deville and hit just about everything but. You’d think you were fuckin’ allergic to tagging someone with that thing. But I know why you didn’t cost me that match, Tchubert, and it’s because for all the chairshots, piledrivers, powerbombs, und so weiter that you take week-in and week-out, you still have more marbles rolling around upstairs than the guy in charge of the fucking company, amazingly. You let Fat Boy waddle down and take the L, because you knew, Tchu… …you knew that costing me that match would be the worst mistake of your life. Now Chet knows, too. AND KNOWLEDGE IS POWER! So here’s a little Back-It-Up 101, Pupil Ward. Watch and learn: I will get the Universal Title shot for King of Kings. And then I will take the Universal Title from you. And for that, my friends won’t be necessary. There. Done. Now we just wait for it to come true. What I don’t understand, though, is how you can get so high and mighty about the company I keep. Gimme a goddamn flippin’ break. You’re buddies with Killean WHY? Because he’s a warm body to put in our path that will help disperse the effect of the crushing blow we plan on sending straight to your dome-piece, priority mail. Admit it. You’re looking for anyone who will even out the numbers game, which is hilarious, because we over at Camp Fuck You didn’t even think about it in terms of numbers. We thought, “Hey, who do we need to totally rule this shit?” Angelo Deville. Check. Rich “The Renegade” Rollins. Check. Jason Snow. Check. The next Universal Champion (moi). Check. And that’s all. Or is it? >=) I guess you’ll have to wait and see. A lot of waiting going on here, I know, but the best things in life generally come that way. The best things in my life, anyhoo. The worst things in yours, I guess. Don’t pretend you know something I don’t, Ward. I knew waaaaaaaay in advance what was going to happen to Kannon. Kannons. Both of the bastards. Nothing personal against ‘em, honestly. Never said two words to either of them. Hell, roll the ReV 111 tape. Let’s clear up any misconceptions. … Doug, I said roll the tape. Nova: So, Doc…what's the plan for the evening? Not that I couldn't watch the zeppelin all night, but surely there's something else on the agenda. It's early. Love you, Doug. ;) The Deville chuckles. Angelo Deville: Yeah, there is . . . I mean, you know me. We've some bad intentions tonight. But . . . Well . . . Setting the gun in his lap, Deville leans forward and looks at Nova. Angelo Deville: I'm not sure it's the sort of villainy you wanna get involved with. Brutality will build a shrine tonight, an altar, if you will, and . . . It's gonna be scandalous, man. Popping the top off his Corona with the edge of a lighter, Nova spares a glance up at the zeppelin ("FUCK YOU: Like Russian roulette, only with six bullets.") before looking at his comrade. Nova: If it's gonna be as bad as you say, you'll need me, no? Hell, everyone already expects me to have metamorphosed into an axe-wielding baby rapist anyway, maybe I should just…oh, fuck, nevermind. There's gotta be some capacity to which I can at least protect you. Angelo Deville: That's the thing. It isn't going to be bad for us. Shit, I could handle it all by myself if I wanted, and-- Well, let me just break it down for you . . . THE CAMERAS CUT TO SOME OTHER THING THAT ISN’T NEARLY AS ENTERTAINING, BUT WAIT!! WHAT’S THIS?! THEY’RE STILL TALKING?! Angelo Deville: …let me break it down for you. This crazy fat bitch, sister of a girl I used to mess around with, she’s been stalking me, man. Even at shows and stuff. She’s started trailing Rollins and Snow, too. She wants the Cadillac. Nova: I’m telling you, dude. I can help protect you guys. Angelo Deville: It could get really ugly, man. Are you sure you want to be around if she finds us? She’ll inhale at least one of us before we can even land a punch, and even then, no guarantees. Nova: I’m on the mother-fucker. Now, the other business about tonight…you have my flamethrower, right? Angelo Deville: >=) Nova: You’re the man. Angelo Deville: o/ So you see, a little planned misconception there on our part. Apologies to all. Now give them back, I didn’t mean that. You know, you’re wrong about something else, Tchu. Standing at ringside trying to torch anyone who goes near the ass beating your buddies are giving to an innocent rookie isn’t pathetic at all. It’s freakin’ BAD-ASS. FUCK YOU.
