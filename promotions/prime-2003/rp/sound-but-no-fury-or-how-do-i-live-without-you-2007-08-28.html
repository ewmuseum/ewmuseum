Sound, but no Fury; Or: How Do I Live Without You?

28 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/28/07 SOUND AND THE FURY There’s no I in Island…wait…am I mixing clichés again? Ian Nackedy is alone and sad. All of the surrounding light is removed, save for a lone spotlight on one half of the hip new tag team Sound and the Fury, who despite being hip and new don’t win a lot of matches. Ian Nackedy looks up at the spotlight and winces into it, letting loose what might be a tear down his cheek. Such tenderness and vulnerability is foreign to our hero. It’s kind of strange to see him in this predicament. After all the laughter, all the comedy, ALL THE GODDAMNED FUNNY, it’s alarming to see Ian Nackedy cry for his injured friend. “Uh, God…I’m only crying because the light’s too bright. Can you cut that out?” Oh. Sorry. The light fades a bit, and Ian Nackedy looks down to his feet, his mood not lightened any. He sighs heavily, thinking about what happened at Rev 137 last week and how it may impact the wrest of his restling career [intentional sic]. By the way, if you don’t know what happened at Rev 137, you should totally check it out and see why Ian is so upset. What? You don’t want to check it out? You want me to TELL you? Seriously? Wow, aren’t you a demanding audience. Fine, fine…I’ll tell you, secret style like. Last Revolution…[font color=white]GILDENSTERN WAS SET ON FIRE AND IS NOW IN THE HOSPITAL[/font] …what do you mean it didn’t work? I used an HTML code and everyth…oh. Whoops. Damn forum codes! So much for secret style. Heheh. Anyway, Ian knows that Gil will eventually be okay, but for the moment he feels alone, as if the one thing he cared about most in life was suddenly wrested away from him. Wait…I’m actually narrating actions and inner monologues? Oh my God, this is affecting ME! Come on, Ian, DO SOMETHING! “I can’t,” says Ian, forlorn. “There’s no one to interact with in a comedic manner.” Well, what about Nate POTCROTCHDIRTMONKEY? Or maybe Bobby “The Dilapidated Asshat” Ottilio, the guy who was trying to kill you? Did they leave too? “Nah, they’re over there,” Ian says, dismissively tossing his head in the direction of a particularly large crate. Looking closer, we see that the crate is rocking slightly and that there are chains on it. Many, many padlocked chains. Scrawled on the crate in hurried, messy handwriting is UNNECESSARY SUPPORTING CHARACTERS. Muffled cursing springs forth from the crate every few seconds. …that’s got to break some laws, I think. “Eh,” says Ian, “you know supporting characters aren’t real people. Can we get back to my sadness?” Um, sure. “I’m so sad,” says Ian, melancholy dripping out of every orifice (ew). “I’m so very sad and alone. Gildenstern is burned, and Ian is bein…uh…being alone. Yeah.” Ian has reached the end of his rope of elaboration, and so there is a few seconds of pause. However, it is broken by a sadder, higher-pitched voice. “At least your friend will come back.” Ian perks up his ears at this voice, then follows the source of that relatively depressing interjection to find a small child. The little boy does not look up as Ian approaches. “What are you talking about, little boy?” Ian asks tentatively. “It’s…it’s just my parents. They…” and he begins sobbing silently, making Ian look awkwardly uncomfortable. “I’m…I’m sorry, kid.” Ian tries to put his arm around the young man, but he gets a slap by his tiny hand. Looking confused, Ian looks down at the kid, who finally looks up to Ian in annoyance. “Back off, you’re ruining it,” is all he says before silently sobbing again. Before Ian can react, a young woman of about 18 comes up to the small boy. “Aww, honey, are you okay?” Ian backs off a few steps, looking up in wonder. “I wonder what that was all about?” Beats me. After a second, the lady leaves, and the young boy snickers, putting a piece of paper in his pocket. Ian, confused, goes up to the kid again. “What’d you get there?” Smiling, the kid watches the girl go. “Her number. Man, this is too easy!” Ian pauses uncomfortably, hating to play the straight man. But, feeling he must, he asks, “You just got that girl’s number? So your parents aren’t dead?” “Nah, my mom dropped me off at this internet café so I could write about my wrestling characters.” The young boy points behind him at the building they are standing near, which is incidentally an internet café. “But I practice picking up older girls while I’m here, because I figure when I’m older I’ll be playing too much poker to concentrate on it.” “Write about wrestling characters? What’s your name, strange boy?” The young child points to himself. “I’m 5 Year Old Integer Chris. I enjoy writing about wrestling, playing poker, and hitting on cute kindergartners. This one time a girl told me she would kiss me and give me cooties. Know what I told her?” Ian looks up to me, angry at being the facilitator to a child’s jokes. “What did you tell her?” “I don’t mind cooties. Give ‘em to me.” And he puckers up, then giggles. “I AM ALSO DESTINED TO BE ALL THAT IS MATCH WRITING.” His eyes flare wildly at this, and Ian looks at him in fear. “Riiiight. Tell me, kid, how long before your mom picks you up?” Cut. Ian is now in PRIME’s studios, ready to shoot a menacing promo. He is wearing camouflage and has a big gun. “Mike Wade, you guys went too far. And I’m going to make you pay.” He holds the gun up in the air and his eyes become slits. Seriously. SLITS. “You set my tag team partner on fire. And I am now determined to hurt you beyond any pain you’ve ever felt before. It’s time to get my revenge, and it won’t be pretty.” He cocks the gun, and everyone around him screams. We are in a studio, after all. Ian pays no mind to the reaction. “I’ve read up about you. You were a big deal in AWC both by yourself and with Face-Eater. I’ve only been a tag team wrestler. Don’t do much in the way of singles matches. And that would seem to favor you, now wouldn’t it? “Well, not this week. Because my gimmick this week gives me the edge.” He snarls, which with his eyes as slits makes him the scariest looking person of all time. “This week, Mike Wade, I am the one man who can get the job done all by himself. The one man who needs no army. This week… “I am Rambo. “And John McClane. “And Steve Austin from The Great Escape. “And you?” He points. “You’re the German bad guys. “And whoever else those other guys beat up. “You’re the enemy. And I will win.” Ian throws the gun down and walks off, the camera following him as he reaches a reception desk. 5 Year Old Integer Chris is crying as an attractive woman pats his head. “It’s okay, young man, your mom is in a better place.” “5 Year Old Integer Chris, I’ve found you! Don’t bother pretty women like that,” says Ian, looking at the lady and smiling. “Hi. I’m Ian Nackedy.” Fade.
