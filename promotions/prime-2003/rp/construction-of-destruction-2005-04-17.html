Construction Of Destruction

17 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-04-05 Pus-ridden gauze. I could always remember that scent, no matter how much time passed. My mind would conjure up the pungent aroma and it would tear through my nostrils as though there in my very presence. As I stood upon the monolithic Jumbotron in Old School Wrestling with my mentor in my arms I knew, or at least I thought I knew that that was the end. He had already suffered in the events leading to his demise, and as we stood there 40ft closer to heaven than the rest of the world it all became clear to me; that it was the destiny of Vampir Nosferatu to die by the hand of his creation. The dagger felt like an extension of my being as it sliced through the thick hide of the now-dishevelled creature so many feared, tearing arteries and flesh and releasing that stink into my nostrils, until the blade stuck fast in his vertebrae. The greatest delight came in knowing only I would witness it all, whilst the world could only view from afar. Cracking the blade up I smiled as the glazed blank stare in Vampir’s eyes turned to one of unadulterated fear; he knew it was all over. Like the “Fall from Grace” he preached on so many occasions he parted from me, down…down…down… Back to Hell. -------------------Detroit, MIMarch 6th, 20057:21pm------------------- “I have an interesting proposition for you Mr. Kelsig.” He said. That was the spark that set it all off, beforehand I didn’t pay any mind to what felt like just another recruitment call. “That’s Von Kelsig, Mr…” I ushered a short breath allowing him time to re-introduce himself to me. My mind was weary and tired from the weeks of planning and preparation into my first business venture out-with the squared circle. After the loss of the Global Title in PTC and the closure of Rush Pro Wrestling I was free…or rather left to roam as I pleased and find my footing in the world once more. So many opportunities were available to me, and being a man of honour and incredible wrath I put into motion a plot to reclaim that honour, of my good name…and unleash that wrath on one who had wronged me horribly twice in my life. James Carlisle. “Hin See, Mr. Von Kelsig. I am a co-owner with PRIME, you may have heard of us within the Primetime Central community?” Came the hasty reply. It was obvious from the tone of his voice that he wanted something, yet it lacked the slimy confidence that the usual recruitment agent oozed. Carlisle reeked of it, believing he was the proverbial King Shit in all senses. The story had been told already but there were those who still raised an eyebrow at his mention. A private loan firm based in my hometown of Detroit was his base of operations, and the landmark that represented my father’s spiral into financial ruin. A sinful appreciation of gambling led him on the path to disarray and the deaths of every family member I held dear to my once rich and pure heart. “Certainly, I believe your roster harbours a few of my old RPW comrades? How’s business doing with them on board? Anyone jealous yet?” I teased him a little, wondering if he realized the controversy that surrounded the RPW alumni, and indeed that of Old School Entertainment and Primetime Championship Wrestling. The backing of such controversy allowed my run with the Global Title to be a memorable one, albeit lasting a single month. I constantly had to remind myself I had a rematch for that belt. The Global Title meant more to me nowadays than when I held it, for now I could truly appreciate its symbolic nature in my life, the second chapter of that fateful little tale involving Carlisle. If it wasn’t for his meddling, I came to realize I wouldn’t be here today. Hadn’t he murdered my family in cold blood and left me for dead I wouldn’t have encountered my mentor. It was fate that everything be taken from me at the whim of a manipulative, greedy, twisted creature…and ironically given back to me by a creature of the very same nature. The Soul Stalker watched everything unfold like it was written to be so, and in the years that followed broke me down and rebuilt me in his image, his creation the Hessian. For me to reach the same feats as Vampir must have been a crying shame for the first ever Global Champion. He certainly would have liked to bear witness to the drama surrounding my conquest of PTC’s greatest prize, at how Carlisle returned to reclaim a debt that died with my father, putting my life on the line against a measly fifteen grand bet. Luckily for both of us I destroyed the competition, lucky for him because he had the chance to make a little money before our next encounter at the close of the last year… “Heh, certainly not Mr. Von Kelsig…may I call you Angelus?” Hin See asked, the trembling in his voice worsening slightly. “Yes you may.” I interjected quickly, allowing him to continue as I produced the keys for the building, spinning them around my index finger. I noticed the chain contained thirteen keys, strange considering the property only required the use of twelve respectively at any given time. “Ok Angelus, well the Rush Pro wrestlers that signed here are loving every minute of it, and that’s really why I called you today…with regards to your current state of employment within the business.” He came closer to the point, although I could tell from the way he presented himself there was something other than a contract signing on his mind…certainly because I would be speaking to an intern of some description at the moment weren’t there something else on his mind. “It came to my attention that you had parted ways with Global Wrestling, and I understand it may have been for any number of reasons but I am curious as to whether any other companies have picked you up yet? Or if indeed you are interested in wrestling at the moment?” He asked, clearing his throat in anticipation. I pondered the thought a moment, I had taken my leave of GW on good terms with the staff, it being the first company I had ever quit in all my years of wrestling. Surely no one had recommended me? “Well that depends…what have you got to offer me?” I chuckled in retort, as did he. My time in GW was unfulfilling, really the one joy I did get out of my entire association with the company came on the cusp of my debut. With so much time to think between RPW closing and GW calling I afforded myself an opportunity to rectify one of the biggest mistakes I had made…allowing Carlisle to walk the streets a free man while his guilty conscience lay suffocating beneath a heap of ill-gotten gains. As droplets of rainwater trickled down the cold, unforgiving steel of my faceplate I watched Carlisle find his way home after a night on the town, almost hoping with every shadowy nook and cranny he passed on the street that he would glance in and look for me. To him I was an already forgotten client, a freak wrestler to be taken for a ride. Little did he know of my true nature, and little did he know as he cracked open his last bottle of Brandy with a friend-soon-to-be-witness that something wicked crept along the roof of his studio flat, hidden by the shadows of the night and the frantic lashings of rain against the building. Toasting to the petty acquisition of the day, the knock at the balcony door came in the form of my right gauntlet. Lightly tapping the double-glazing so as not to lose my balance I brandished the rope, holding the noose over the edge above the door. I waited patiently as the violent winds howled through the city like a thousand mourning spirits, and seeing the silhouette at the curtain I sneered and grit my teeth. A sonata of screams, gurgles and sharp crunches accompanied the rising sun over the Detroit skyline. Police had already infiltrated the household to find Carlisle contorted in rigor mortis with a look of horror plastered upon his face. His friend awoke that day with the broken needles of five heroin-filled syringes in his arm and a rope and baseball bat in both hands, each with his fingerprints upon them. The fuzz called 187 on the case and in the months that followed I heard nothing of Carlisle. I couldn’t care less if he was ground up and used for dog food, I had exacted my revenge. He had his now I got mine. One annoyance put to rest…and now the time had come for me to confront the true bane of my existence. I looked up at the pair of 22E-sized boots resting cross-legged on the mahogany desk in front of me. A sleek telephone and fax machine combo situated to their right and the only remaining PC that worked in the building on their right. The main office was relatively tidy even after the building had been abandoned a few months prior to my purchase of it. After a leg injury sustained while training in GW I discovered to my surprise that the late James Carlisle’s office building was being put to auction after failing to sell on the market thanks to its ill repute of the past. Wasting no time I made a stake at the claim and won it by a margin of $25,000. Whether it was out of spite for the deceased or the chance at doing something other than wrestling I took it and immediately made plans to transform it into the Slaughter House I had in mind…with the help of a like-minded associate. “Vampir Nosferatu.” Tired though I may have been, the name brought me to full attention. Having been in the process of yawning it must have seemed to Hin See like a roar of outrage. I gripped the phone tightly, the plastic covering crunching from the pressure. My eyes darted back and forth trying to make sense of what I had just been told, was it some kind of recruitment tact…or something else? “What do you mean, Vampir Nosferatu?” I asked, listening intently to the hurried breaths of Hin See, obviously emotional after uttering that name himself. “I’m saying Angelus that he is in PRIME, and doing a good job of ruining us. I’ll be honest I would like to recruit you to the company, but it would be under one circumstance…” I could hear a cloth rubbing against skin, absorbing perspiration as Hin See tried to seal the deal. “I heard the story about you and him and what happened in OSW. I need Hessian to come to PRIME; I need you to do what you did to Vampir to put a stop to him and this damned Dark Age of his! Nothing seems to work against him, and the next best thing I can think of is…you.” He continued. “Wait a moment…are you telling me that THE Vampir Nosferatu is in PRIME? That’s impossible! He can’t be alive…and if he is he shouldn’t be able to move anything below his eyelids!” I felt myself become frantic at the thought of the Soul Stalker once again roaming the earth. “I’m sceptical myself after hearing what you did to him…but all I know is there is a Vampir Nosferatu in PRIME and he’s getting out of hand. Angelus I’m offering you a contract to PRIME plus the opportunity, I guess you could call it, to put a stop to Vamp-…” Before Hin See could finish I interrupted him, and on any other occasion it would have been rude. This time however Hin See was glad I cut him off. “I’m in. I accept.” We discussed the final preparations, and I hung up. From my slouched position in the leather recliner I got to my feet and glanced around the room again. Just a few moments ago they held a trivial purpose, a simple office to do with as I pleased in preparation for the grand opening of the Slaughter House. Now as I saw over all I owned, I realized the new meaning it held, the new purpose it served. Along with my associate and investor, namely one Bruce Shanahan, I would turn these three storeys into my own personal Hell. All manner of depravity and disgust would flood through the doors and take up residence under my watchful eye as I indulged in the vilest of activities. To prepare myself for Vampir I would have to reach a mental plateau, a place where I could look down upon Vampir and all he had accomplished and laugh, knowing he could not scale up to my level. By the time I finish, Vampir Nosferatu will shudder at the very mention of my name… -------------------Detroit, MIApril 18th, 20055:12pm------------------- Wiping the sweat from his brow, Hessian shifted the last table inside. As he carried it from the loading bay into the storeroom a voice called out from ahead, sidestepping as Hessian powered through, lowering the table to the ground and straightening up, a loud series of cracks emanating from his vertebrae. Tipping his cap and whistling, the smaller, skinnier of the two deliverymen clutched the straps of his stain-covered Royal blue overalls and gazed up at the behemoth before them. “You’se a strong ol’ bugger ain’tcha!” He chirped. Hessian could only glance down at him and chuckle, while the stockier of the two men sighed, mashing a piece of chewing gum around his mouth in a slack-jawed manner. “Well, I try.” Hessian replied, mockingly dusting off his biceps. “That’s the last of it then?” He asked, nodding to the table and running a hand across the smooth sheen of the blackened solid oak. An intricate Gothic detail covered the entire surface, faintly visible yet enough so as to maintain the high quality appearance. “Yessir, that’s everythin’!” The younger one exclaimed. “Ok, well if that’s that and the paperwork is signed I guess that’s a job well done…here’s something extra for your trouble.” Hessian replied, digging into his pants pocket and producing a couple of crisp ten-dollar bills. Accepting them graciously, the two deliverymen departed. As the hulking beige delivery van trundled into the alleyway and manoeuvred its way back onto the street Hessian wandered back into the loading bay, stopping by the open entrance and jamming a finger into the large red Close button. Slowly but surely the metal doors unfolded from the roller above and juddered to the ground. With a loud slam signifying its closure, Hessian made his way back up the concrete ramp and into the storeroom. Met by the table, he clamped down on its sides with his massive paws and hoisted it into the air, pacing towards the staircase leading to the first floor of the building. “Uhh…this can’t be good for the back…” He uttered under his breath, rather ironically from a wrestler’s point of view. Taking five or six steps in one go before stopping for a breath, Hessian glanced behind him at the prospective fall, and shaking his head continued upstairs. Had any of the employees to James Carlisle walked upstairs, a trick of the brain would be the first excuse for the change in scenery. What used to be a drab turquoise lobby and receptionist’s office had been done away with in place of a lounge area and cloakroom. Where cold, plastic chairs and false fern plants once sat there were now ten, including the one he was carrying, of the fine oak tables nestled comfortably in the centres of ten groups of sofas and recliners, crafted from fine black leather and invitingly comfortable to the eye. The room had a personality now, even with the burgundy carpeting and especially the small bar in the corner next to a flight of stairs leading to the second floor. Surrounded by a dozen refurbished vintage black Victorian bar stools topped off with burgundy suede to match the floor. “Not bad. Not bad at all…” Hessian whispered to himself, hoisting the chair over to the one remaining circle of furniture lacking a table, and placed it carefully in the centre. Glancing up at the dark walls patterned with red, blue and green he could make out the embossed crests designed into the wallpaper and highlighted by the lamplights. Only the windows high up the walls had to be changed with frosted glass to give a more claustrophobic yet friendly atmosphere. The punters would stroll in and immediately feel welcome exactly as Hessian imagined, dropping off any extra baggage in the cloakroom before meeting with friends and having a tipple from the bar before heading upstairs to what was quickly being built into a large dance hall. Thankfully Carlisle had thought ahead when he first bought the building and placed toilets on all floors, if only he could see the place now. Put simply, Hessian was building his own club from the ground up, or at least the inside out. A few wrestlers had chosen this path as a separate means of income, and Hessian saw no reason why he could not follow down the same track. Taking a deep breath and looking around the finished lounge, he grinned and turned to head back down to the storeroom when a ringing erupted from a telephone hidden in the bar. Casually strolling across the room, Hessian reached under the bar and produced a blue cordless phone. “Hello?” A simple yet effective opening. “Hello, Mr. Von Kelsig?” Came a squeaky voice on the other end, belonging to Blaine Blair. “Yeah, speaking.” Some might say that were a retort to such a question posed by a caller required, that was it. Hessian applied it to the conversation anyway. “Ah, hello there. My name is Blaine Blair, I’m calling with regards to your first match at PRIME’s ReVolution 61 show this coming Wednesday.” He spurted the words out as though they were written on a piece of paper, and Hessian already knowing Blair was an intern guessed just that. “Ok great, I did ask since I’d be busy at the close of the show if someone could call about it, thank you. What’s the match?” Hessian replied politely. He had no need to be all doom and disaster on the phone to a simple intern, why be an asshole? “Well Mr. Von Kelsig it’ll be yourself facing off against Morales, the manager for Los Diablos in the opening match of the night. We here at PRIME look forward to seeing you compete, see you at ReVolution 61, Hessian!” There, quick and painless. Hessian was new so rather than beat around the bush Blaine cut to the chase, knowing the new ones had to be disciplined the most if they were looking for a free ride to the top, unless they were a seasoned veteran like the Titanic Satanic Insaniac. A couple of goodbyes later and Hessian hung up, heading off back downstairs to the storeroom to do a quick check before locking up. Glancing around the rather bland store, Hessian kicked an empty box at his feet into a small clutter at the corner shelves that extended around two of the four walls. Dust blew into the air, the force of the kick strong enough to bring it up to Hessian’s head height. Walking through it, he shut the door leading into the loading bay over and locked it, looking as he did so at that thirteenth key that had no apparent use. Shrugging his shoulders he spun on his heels and began walking back to the stairs when an inadvertent deep inhalation caused the dust to engulf his face. Coughing and spluttering, Hessian collected a wad of saliva in his mouth and spat the dust particles out, clearing it from his eyes and wiping his faceplate down. Opening and closing his eyes rapidly to clear his vision, Hessian looked at the foot of the stairs, and noticed all of a sudden a notch in the ground. Cocking his head he walked over to it, kneeling down and rubbing at it to ensure it wasn’t just a skid mark from his boots. As he rubbed away pieces of latex peeled away from the notch, and becoming more confused Hessian picked it all out until what lay before him took the form…of a handle and keyhole. His eyes wide with surprise, Hessian glanced around the notch and felt his way along the floor, even more surprised to find a minute line in the floor forming a large square around the handle. “…It can’t be…” He muttered, producing the chain of keys in his hand and picking out the mysterious thirteenth key. Rubbing at the metal with his finger, Hessian raised an eyebrow, the only one not hidden by his faceplate, and placed it into the keyhole. With a slow turn the locking mechanism could be heard turning, and with a look of excitement spreading across his face Hessian grabbed the handle and pulled up, bringing with him a large door! “A basement?! This wasn’t in the blueprints…” He was obviously excited now. Looking around on the shelves of the storeroom, Hessian locked his eyes on a torch resting near the top, and grabbing at it turned it to the dark hole in the ground at the foot of the stairs, pausing for a moment and hoping…and with a flick of the on/off switch grinned as light poured into the basement. “Goddamn, I got myself a basement…and I’m more excited about that than facing some Luchadore in my first match…damn, can’t argue with my priorities.” He chuckled as light shone onto a set of concrete steps descending into the abyss. Taking his first tentative steps Hessian peeked down at the interior, unsure of what lay beyond. Wallowing further into the thick black with every step, his breathing became heavier until his head disappeared and he was totally submerged in nothingness, with only a beam of light to guide him. From the storeroom the silence seemed to linger below and for a moment everything was nothing, until a booming voice resounded from the depths… “Holy shit…I don’t fucking believe it!” To be continued.
