Like An Emo

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Featuring Tony Gamble as the Performance Reviewer Devin Shakur has done some pretty stupid things in his life. But agreeing to a performance evaluation hosted by Tony Gamble has got to be one of the stupidest. Devin Shakur walks into The Grin’s office. Both are looking fly as awww snap son. The Grin has on a fake mustache and hops into his office chair. Tony Gamble: Mr. Shakur, thank you for…Excuse me a second. The Grin hops out of his chair and places two phone books on the seat. He hops back up and raises the seat so he can be seen by The Man in Black. Tony Gamble: Thank you for coming to your performance review. Devin Shakur: No problem. Tony Gamble: So you are in charge around here, is that fair to say? Devin Shakur: Absolutely. I’m The Emo. Tony Gamble: Ok so take us through a day in the life of The Emo. Devin Shakur: Well the first thing I do is… Talk to corporate (Like an emo) Devin Shakur menacingly stands over Lisa Tyler, threatening her with bodily harm if she fails to give into his demands. Approve memos (Like an emo) The Man in Black struts around threatening to have Bryan Dawkins fired at ReVolution 198. Lead a workshop (Like an emo) Shakur is shown at the head of a class with a razor blade next to a dummy wrist. He points the razor blade vertical and cuts up with an approving nod. Next, he points the blade horizontally and cuts across, shaking his head No. Remember birthdays (Like an emo) Shakur hands a gift wrapped package to Sonny Silver at the PRIME Retirement Home. Sonny is delighted that anybody would remember his birthday (aside from Mega Job who showed up earlier with toasters). Sonny opens the present and finds a cordless microphone. He’s ready to fight. Direct workflow (Like an emo) Shakur offers Hessian 200 large to take Bryan Dawkins out at ReVolution 198. My own bathroom (Like an emo) Shakur shoves past Elise Ares and goes into the womens bathroom. What, they have a place for him to put on his eyeliner. Micro manage (Like an emo) Devin Shakur gives the Oompa Loompas directions on how to find Gamble's locker room. Get it? Micro manage...They are short...Yeah, fuck you. Promote synergy (Like an emo) Shakur introduces Chainz to a few of the new interns. Worst. Hazing. Ever. Let’s just say they are never seen again. Hit on Lindsay (Like an emo) Shakur walks up to Lindsay Troy wearing a Tyler Rayne mask. Get rejected (Like an emo) Sun Tzu points her pair of guns at her hubby’s head and threatens to blow his brains out. Swallow sadness (Like an emo) If Devin thought that was bad, once The Queen leaves, Sunny brings out a sword and points it at his head. Not the one on his shoulders. Hit some double axes (Like an emo) Shakur leaps from the top rope and lands a double ax handle across the back of The Murder Show. Call a sex line (Like an emo) Shakur comes back after cutting a promo and finds a note underneath his door for a sex line. Bored, he picks up the card and walks into his locker room prepared to call the number. Tony Gamble is giggling in the next room when his company phone rings. Cry deeply (Like an emo) Shakur screams into the phone about how his girlfriend is an overprotective dictator. Gamble chuckles when he hears the word dictator and summons Dam into the room. Demand a refund (Like an emo) Once Shakur hears Dam’s voice on the other end, he slams the phone down and storms down the hall in disgust. Gamble and Dam laugh and split the 20 bucks they made from the call. Eat a pineapple (Like an emo) Devin Shakur lets out a loud grunt while a confident Bryan Dawkins nods his head in approval. The Man in Black doesn’t lose bets often and hates having to pay up. He takes a huge bite out of the pineapple while Dawkins films it for YouTube. Harassment lawsuit (Like an emo) Devin Shakur, Chainz and Tyler Rayne all chase Lisa Tyler down the hall with wet towels. No promotion (Like an emo) The Man in Black wonders what he could have done wrong when he is clearly the best candidate in the business for a promotion. Lisa Tyler throws a cup of water in his face and orders him out of the room. Dam chimes in with a "Sorry, boss", leaving Devin to look up and threaten to punch the one man security guard out. Dam smiles and does the "Call me" hand motion. Roll a joint (Like an emo) Nova teaches Devin Shakur the fine art of rolling bombastically bitchin big booty blunts. Shit on Lisa’s desk (Like an emo) Devin Shakur goes 1999 and backs a manure truck into Lisa’s office while cutting a promo on camera. The crowd pops like mad. Buy a blade (Like an emo) Shakur puts a quarter into the designated slot and watches as his very own razor blade (inside a small pod) rolls down the ramp and into the Exit area. Two feet away, a five year old kid and his mother watch a giant ball of candy do the same thing. In my mouth (Like an emo) Shakur places the blade in his mouth and threatens to end it all in the most brutal of ways. Right before the cut, he has a change of heart. Devin Shakur: Awww f**k man I can’t f**king do it shit…OH COME ON YOU CAN’T BLEEP THAT FUC- Pussy out (Like an emo) Tyler Rayne snaps his fingers and the six women lined up in a row are no longer wearing anything. At all. Shakur is stuck hanging out with Gamble and Dam back in the locker room. Smash Lisa’s desk (Like an emo) Shakur rushes in with an axe and obliterates the desk before walking away. Jump out the window (Like an emo) Shakur jumps from the first floor of PRIME Headquarters. Slash a dude’s wrist (Like an emo) Shakur cuts a homeless man in the streets who identifies himself as Jimmy Carlos Johan Santana Rob Thomas Smooth Bonafide. A shriek emits into the night air. Jimmy Bonafide: GUNZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Score some coke (Like an emo) Shakur purchases a can of Coke from the vending machine. Crash the clown car (Like an emo) The Grin asked to borrow Shakur’s car to head back to the hotel and The Black Plague agreed, leaving him to drive Gamble’s car. He leaves the car in a haze, grasping at his legs. No leg room whatsoever in that piece of shit. He also couldn’t listen to Dashboard Confessional. Slash my own wrist (Like an emo) Shakur winds up and performs his first ever act of emo on camera by cutting his wrist. He no sells it like a former Universal Champion should. Eat some Mega Job waffles (Like an emo) Shakur eats El Janito’s stack of waffles at The Waffle House. He cries into Beef’s shoulder. Steve clotheslines Beef and El Janito before asking the waitress for a Kid's Box. Chop my balls off (Like an emo) Shakur rushes back into PRIME Headquarters and steals Tyler Rayne’s sword. He chops his balls off. Black out in the sewer (Like an emo) With no balls and having slashed his wrist, Shakur stumbles down the alley and collapses. Meet a giant fish (Like an emo) By sheer coincidence, Matt Mills is waiting for someone to play him in a friendly game of poker and invites Shakur to play for a while until the shark arrives. Little does he know… Donk him on the river (Like an emo) Shakur rivers a Royal flush to beat Matt Mills out of all his money. Turn into a jet (Like an emo) With almost no blood in his head, Shakur believes he can fly. He believes he can touch the sky. Bomb the Polish (Like an emo) Shakur throws a stink bomb into Tom Walczak’s house and runs away before he can react. Crash into the requiem (Like an emo) Shakur bolts back into the building and runs down a corridor at full speed. Hessian rounds the corner and Shakur crashes head to chest into him. Now I’m dead (Like an emo) Shakur is out like a light. Gamble walks by and boosts all his dough. Back to Gamble and Shakur in the performance evaluation room. The Grin acts professional and is quite confused at everything Shakur just admitted to. Tony Gamble: Uh huh, so that’s an average day for you then? Devin Shakur: No doubt. Tony Gamble: You chop your balls off and die? Devin Shakur: Hell yeah. Tony Gamble: And I think at one point you said something about cutting your wrists? Devin Shakur: Nope. Tony Gamble: Actually, I’m pretty sure you did. Devin Shakur: Nah, that ain’t me. The Grin pushes off his phone books and stands up. Tony Gamble: Ok, well this has been eye opening for me. Devin Shakur: I’m the emo. Tony Gamble: Yeah, I know, you said that about 400 times. Devin Shakur: I’m the emo. Tony Gamble: Yeah, yeah I got it. The Grin walks out of the office, leaving Shakur feeling very uncomfortable. Devin Shakur: I’m the emo! Tony Gamble: Yeah, great, I heard you, bye. The Grin slams the door on Shakur, who stares into the camera. Devin Shakur: LIKE AN EMO!
