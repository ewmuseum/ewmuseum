Untitled Brandon Youngblood Roleplay (Dual Halo 07)

Date: like...a while ago It was a mask he wore for so very long. Exposed before the world, he was relegated to opinions that he could not separate himself from the lies of which he perpetuated. The mask of his own hatred fueled deceit and self inflation had been resolutely branded into the flesh of his face. He rode the current of it towards his immediate demise, unable to control the wheel as he crashed steadfast into the brick wall that was the world of professional wrestling. This was his end. *** Like many things in life, it was through a chain of events. A plow of dominoes that had held his life up on a shaky foundation that toppled down and buried him in a sea of wreckage. Wreckage of which the pompous prick Brandon Youngblood was completely unaware of until he was left to drown. November 7th, 2005. It hung around in his mind like an elephant in the room that he could never shoo away. The day in which the very company he had helped foster abruptly cut ties and left him stranded for dead, as if he was Killean Sirrajin's dealer. "PRIME is the way it is because I made it that way." Smoke bellowed out of his flared nostrils and began to hang in the air around his shaved skull. It danced between the imperfections of his skin, made so by the battles he had waged all over the world. A scar healed over still leaves a scar. Hatred still filled his heart. It was not as if he was unaware of the culture shock that PRIME went through in the sixteen months subsequent his departure. Countless faces appeared out of nothing only to fade without leaving an indelible imprint. It was hard for Brandon to see himself as a forefather for the professional wrestling organization known only as PRIME. And even now, it was difficult to see the impression he made upon it now nearly two years since he first stepped into their hallowed halls. Along the way, the past had been swept under a rug to be forgotten for the now. His finger prints stained the parchment of which their lives played out upon, but they went onward as children without knowledge of what had been and what was to be. It reached beyond the culture. PRIME had become an entertainment conglomerate, touring world wide and selling out every place they went. Merchandise flying off the shelves (actually being sold, not touted by an embellishing wrestling hype machine when in reality business was doing horribly), buy rates spiking, a two day extravaganza...a celebration of professional wrestling. The roster had swelled to an insane size, almost to the size of their closest competitors in Stamford, Connecticut. Even with the industry in a huge downswing, PRIME was experiencing an unheard of boom in the modern wrestling culture. Those building blocks, be it the under the radar signing of Matthew Ward, the unknown and disfigured Anthony Gambillini, or even signing Hollywood Golden Boy Danny Ferguson...were brought in under a swelling boom from the first true spike in business the company had in the mid to late 2005. It was Brandon Youngblood who was headlining those shows, putting the 'asses in the seats' as he took on Hall of Famers like Killean Sirrajin, Karina Wolfenden, and Hoyt Williams as well as to be Hall of Famers like Nova and Angelo Deville. Youngblood was a fairly inexpensive signing himself, but succeed quickly at main event pay level. Without Brandon playing a crucial role as THE sensationalist heel that everyone in the company hated, it was very possible that the world that PRIME stands upon now might not have ever been forged. It went beyond Youngblood's own self impression of his value to the company, as when the surge was picking up speed it was Brandon who was running over everyone to take the crown as the Universal Champion. Before Ward...before Caesar...before even the fluke Clyde Walkins...it was Brandon who was headlining shows to and fro. The Universal Championship was not a question of if, but when. PRIME's world, with the AWC refugees, Fuck You overachievers, and home team supporters, was built upon the back of a man that they had turned into the living embodiment of a joke. *** Maybe it was a large scale joke to Chet Worth to bring in the old battered PRIME outcast for the single biggest match in professional wrestling history. Hell, the beating he would surely take from some young guy like Gabriel Afeaki could take that kid's stock up a few points almost instantly. Maybe Chainz would throw him around and eat him to up his body count. Or maybe it was recognition on Chet's part all too late that the entity he and PRIME had fought tooth and nail against was only stomped to ashes by him. Certainly nobody in PRIME had taken Deville to the absolute limit as often as Brandon had in their running feud. And most certainly nobody had ever done to Nova what Brandon had done. Which was another thought going through the tired mind of the self proclaimed Greatest Wrestler Alive. Most wrestlers gearing up for a match with the implications of the Dual Halo had would be jumping around on nerves going electric inside in anticipation but Brandon was stoic. His shoulders felt as if a huge weight was burdened upon them, the achy joints of wrestling's persona non grata.
