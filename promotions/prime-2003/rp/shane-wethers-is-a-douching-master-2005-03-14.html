Shane Wethers is a douching master

14 March 2005

Date: 3/14/2005 KNOCK-KNOCK Taun Pham looks up from his desk and sees his secretary opening the door to his office. Secretary: Mr. Pham, Mr. Rolo is here to see you. She steps aside allowing room for The Specialist to squeeze by into Pham’s office. Pham rises to his feet and extends his hand to Rolo, which is shaken in a strong but respectful way by the former 2x Global Champ. Taun Pham: Tony… Nodding towards the PRIME official… Tony Rolo: Pham. Taun Pham: It’s good to see you. I haven’t had a chance to speak to you since I woke from my coma. It’s nice to see that not everything has changed around PRIME in the past nine months. Tony Rolo: Yeah, right. Taun Pham: A lot has changed in nine months… who would have thought we’d have all these new faces. I’ve been watching tapes trying to catch up on everything that happened, but I’ve been so busy I’ve only seen a few so far. I did hear that you won the Global Title for a second time at Colossus against Lisieux. Congratulations on that. Tony Rolo: Uh, thanks I guess. Taun Pham: I’m not even sure what happened to Lisieux… or Black Angel, Ian English and others for that matter. Tony Rolo: Well, Ignatius Lisieux got in a horrible skiing accident and died. Taun Pham: He’s dead!? Tony Rolo: As a doornail. Taun Pham: Oh my God, that’s horrible. Tony Rolo: Yeah, tragic. And Ian English came out of the closet. Taun Pham: What? Tony Rolo: He’s gay. Taun Pham: Gay? Tony Rolo: Gay. Taun Pham: How do you know? Tony Rolo: He was caught fornicating with JTF. Taun Pham: What about Troy? Tony Rolo: He became a Buddhist monk. Taun Pham: Okay, Ignatius Lisieux dying and Ian English being a homosexual seem possible, but Joey Troy becoming a Buddhist monk is an absolute lie. Tony Rolo: I swear to God… he showed up at my door one day asking me to refer to him as Obi-Wan from now on and saying that Buddha is his master. Taun Pham: Wow… strange. Tony Rolo: Yeah… well, I think Joey damaged most of his brain cells with his water bong. He’s one nut short of a sack, if you know what I mean. Taun shakes his head in awe. Taun Pham: So anyway, Tony, why was it that you called to meet with me? You didn’t seem to willing to divulge any information over the phone. Rolo begins to slowly look around Pham’s office before leaning in towards his desk and speaking at a low level. Tony Rolo: You don’t think your office would be bugged, do you? Taun Pham: Bugged? No. Why would it be bugged? Tony Rolo: I don’t know. I think there’s something fishy going on in PRIME. Taun Pham: What do you mean? Tony Rolo: I don’t know. Taun Pham: Tony, I’m confused. Tony Rolo: Alright, here’s the story. ReVolution 45… it’s me versus Boda in the main event. After the match, Vampir attacked Boda… and for some reason, so did I. I beat him unconscious with a chair. Now, in retrospect I’m glad as shit that I did because Boda is a fuckface… but, I didn’t want to do it. I couldn’t control myself. And in all actuality, I don’t remember doing it. I’ve only watched the footage on video. I went on to attack Bernie Roberts and came close to knocking the shit out of Nick Stuart and Richard Parker with a steel chair. That’s when I dropped to both knees, screaming “what did they do to me?” Taun Pham: Who is “we?” Tony Rolo: I don’t know. I don’t remember saying it. I can’t explain it. Taun Pham: That’s very odd. But… Tony Rolo: There’s more… that’s only the tip of the iceberg. Taun looks on, puzzled. Tony Rolo: After ReVolution 45, I disappeared. There’s a few seconds of silence as they just stare at each other. Taun Pham: Well where’d you go? Tony Rolo: How the fuck do I know? I woke up half a week later in an empty field 40 miles south of Chicago. Taun Pham: And nobody knows what happened to you? Tony Rolo: Nobody. Taun Pham: Nobody had a motive? Tony Rolo: A lot of people had a motive, that’s the problem. Now, after all that I started having a reoccurring nightmare. I see people in white smocks standing above me. My sight is blurry, so I can’t really make out what is going on. I hear my own voice screaming for them to stop… only I don’t know if it’s only in my head or not. Taun Pham: And you don’t know who those people are? Tony Rolo: Well, I do think I recognize one of them. Taun Pham: Who is it? Tony Rolo: Shane Wethers. Taun Pham: Shane Wethers… really? Tony Rolo: Yeah, I think. Taun Pham: But why? Why would he be involved in any of what happened to you? Tony Rolo: I don’t know. Like I said, the dream is blurry. I didn’t notice him at first. But then shortly after he returned to PRIME, I ran into him in the back during ReVolution one Wednesday. I sorta blacked out for a second and has a flashback to the dream and saw his face. Taun Pham: Are you sure it was him? Tony Rolo: Yeah, pretty sure. He acted really weird about my flashback. I didn’t say anything to him about it. He seemed really curious about it… but he didn’t want to say anything that might give himself away. I didn’t want to say anything until I could find out more. I figured I’d play into anything he had planned just to see where things went… but then nothing else happened. Like he backed off or something. Or maybe I’m completely on the wrong track… I don’t fucking know. Taun Pham: So that explains why you flipped out when you saw Wethers inside the Halo. Tony Rolo: Yeah. Taun Pham: Does anybody else know about this? Tony Rolo: Just your father. I went to him about a month and a half ago and told him everything I just told you. Taun Pham: What’d he say? Tony Rolo: He told me that I’m crazy and I should go see a shrink… so I told him to shove it up his ass. I shouldn’t have even gone to him… I still don’t trust him. Taun Pham: You don’t trust him because of his prior relationship with Toshiaki Motoki? Tony Rolo: No, I don’t trust him cause he double knots his shoelaces. Of course it’s because of Motoki. Taun Pham: Have you ever heard of forgive and forget? Tony Rolo: Yeah, you usually hear that from the same guy that says “bend over, I got something to show you.” Taun Pham: I don’t think you’re giving my father a fair chance. Tony Rolo: No, I gave him a chance. I went for him for help and he turned me away. My wife left me because of this shit, Pham. I’m not fucking around with this. I’m here to ask for your help. Your father’s refusal makes me very suspicious that he might know something. If I find out he knows something… God help him if he’s holding something from me. Taun Pham: Now Tony, don’t jump to conclusions. I’m sure if my father knew anything he would help you. Tony Rolo: Do you know how serious this is? It’s turned my whole world upside down. It ruined my marriage… I can’t see my own son because my wife took out a restraining order. I need help. I don’t know who I can trust. But I know I can trust you… you were in a coma for the entire thing and you always were a man of your word. Will you help me? Please. Taun Pham: But… Tony Rolo: Please. I’ve never asked you for anything before. I always took care of things myself, in the ring. But this battle is beyond the ring. I don’t even know who I’m battling. All I’m asking is that if you see or notice anything strange, tell me about it. That’s all I ask. Can you please do that for me? Taun Pham: Tony, you’ve been in PRIME since day one. You’ve always given us your all. That’s the least I can do for you. I’ll help you with anything I can. Tony stands up and looks Pham firmly in the eye and extends his hand. Tony Rolo: Thank you. After another handshake, Tony turns to leave the room. Standing in the doorway, he turns back to Pham. Tony Rolo: Oh, another thing about all this that is really strange… I think I had that dream for the first time the week before Colossus. I just didn’t think much of it cause I had been having weird dreams about Ian Eng… just some weird dreams at the time. And with that Rolo leaves, closing the door behind himself. Taun Pham stands there looking very concerned by Tony’s final comment. ***** Tony Rolo: First of all, when did Los Diablos and Vetra drink the fucking Kool-Aid. Hoyt Williams thinks he’s Jim Jones. This isn’t a stable, this is a cult… a cult of cunts. It’s a cunt cult, if you will… a bunch of fucking pussies. And does anybody else find it ironic that a team named Los Diablos, or the devils, if you will, is joining forced with a guy who claims to be the son of God? That’s almost as ironic as Shane Wethers getting laid. Imperium, last week you guys made an example out of me… but more importantly you guys made a mistake. The four of you will pay the price… maybe not this week, but you will. And who knows… maybe you will pay the price this week. We’ll see how your luck goes. All I know for sure is, I’m not gonna forget the end of ReVolution 57. Tony Rolo: As for my match this week in the Wetherlution Invitational… big fucking deal. Who do I have to face? Let’s start with Shane Wethers. First of all, Shane… I know we’ve gone over this time and time again, but you do realize you’re a fucking douche-bag, right? Lame is the name of your game. First of all, you think you’re funny. You’re not. Second of all, you think you’re some big fan favorite. You’re not. The only thing you are is a little piss-ant stepping stone for everybody else in PRIME. You go around calling ReVolution Wetherlution… do you know how fucking gay that is? Nobody cares about you. You are a NOBODY Shane. You’re a loser… always were and always will be. It’s been proven time and time again. Winners that don’t quit have heart and are heroes. Losers that don’t quit have no brains and are fucking morons. You, Shane, are a fucking moron. If I have to hear about your dumbass brother Silo, or Silus, or Shamus, or whatever the hell his name is, I’m gonna pull your tongue out of your head and strangle you with it. Seriously, this world would be a much more intelligent place if you stuck your head in the oven and baked it. Oh, and one more thing… Wethermania. You can have that. From now on I don’t want anybody in PRIME to ever make a reference to Rolomania. I don’t want anything that would ever make me comparable to this fuck-stick. Tony Rolo: How about Nova? Oh yeah I hear you guys out there saying “Nova was the 5-Star Champ… a belt that Ian English and Joey Troy have held.” Come on, remember who he beat for it? JTF… the guy who was more worried about being a male model. Do I even need to tell the story about how JTF’s father was Asian and his mother was American, but he ended up with his mother’s last name? His father was such a chach that he took his wife’s last name… what a pussy he was and what a pussy his son was. So Nova beat a pussy for the title… big fucking title. And who did he lose that title to? Who was that, you say? Hoyt Williams, was it? Now shit, if I remember correctly, there was a guy that looked just like Hoyt Williams that I beat during the main event of ReVolution last week. Nova, I’m not gonna sit here and completely degrade you, though. You got some talent. You’re just too green still. You’re not on my level and at ReVolution I’m gonna show you that I’m not a bullshitter. Tony Rolo: Opponent numero tres… Tyler Lopez. Has anybody ever met a Mexican guy named Tyler? That’d be like a Chinese guy named Todd. Tyler… what a queer ass name. Almost as gay as Nova. And honestly, I didn’t even know he was still in the fed anymore. I didn’t know they allowed people to stay at the bottom of the cards for so long without handing them a pink slip. Now, he is proof that Mexico is about 15 years behind the rest of the world considering he still has a mullet. I haven’t seen a mullet that greasy… well, since I saw Hoyt’s mom’s crotch on that Girl’s Gone Wild video. Hey Lopez, Oscar Hernandez hasn’t shown up to work in quite a while, do you think you could come trim my shrubs? You’d be better off doing that Wednesday because if you show up at ReVolution you’re gonna get beat worse than a piñata on Cinco de Mayo. Tony Rolo: And last is Jon Kano. The big PCW legend himself. You know what I think of PCW legends? I don’t. I don’t think we’ll be seeing the letters PCW in the middle of that ring at ReVolution 58. Although, Kano, the only way you’d see anything at ReVolution is if it were painted on the ceiling because you’re gonna be spending the night on your back. I’ve watched you since you walked through those doors in PRIME and you haven’t shown me shit. If you represent what PCW once was, I’m glad it got flushed for good. You haven’t impressed me one bit. In fact, it’s been so pathetic that if you do somehow pull out a win, I’ll actually promise to blow a goat. Wednesday, this PRIME legend looks forward to stepping in the ring and going toe-to-toe with all 6 foot 10, 315 pounds of your PCW legend ass. We’ll find out what legends are made of. Tony Rolo: As for what’s inside the bag at the top of the ladder, I’ll pull it down to win the match… but I want no part of Wethers gay ass chains, whips, and gag balls. You should have made the match again the guys in the Dark Age if you were looking to be sodomized by the victor. What I will do is the same thing I said I’d do last week… continue my uphill battle to the top of PRIME once again and show everybody that this old man is still the man to beat around here. In PRIME legends are legends for a reason. We come to fight. Bring, it bitches.
