Distractions

29 April 2008

Date: 4/29/08 The sun shines high in the midday sky, brightening the beautiful cerulean and occasional wispy white cloud that floats by. Another warm and sunny spring afternoon. The people are out in force. Nothing like a little good weather to bring the citizens together. The park is bustling with activity. People walking dogs. Chasing toddlers. A few old men playing chess at a picnic table. A gaggle of fraternity brothers playing Ultimate Frisbee. And, of course, the joggers. One jogger in particular seems to be gaining quite a bit of attention. Tyler Rayne, sweat glistening over his bare torso, offers a smile and approving nod to an energetic little red-haired sorority girl he’s caught staring as he passes. Though he doubted she’d mind, it was probably best that, in a very rare case indeed, his eyes were hidden behind a pair of Oakley O ROKR Pros. Probably the single most expensive piece of…anything Tyler Rayne had ever worn on his person. “Tink, look, man. I told you. I’m sorry. You wanna hear me say it? Fine. I dropped the ball last week. Now can you please stop riding my ass about it.” The obvious joke remains unsaid. Sheesh. PRIME’s 5-Star Champion wearing designer sunglasses and passing on a chance to make the lame homosexual joke on Chandler Tsonda? Are we sure he hasn’t been replaced by a Skrull, too? Oh, and if you were wondering, the O ROKR Pros come equipped with Bluetooth. Thus the ability of our great and glorious hero to speak to the Jewel in the Crown without having to fumble with his cell phone while jogging and checking out the smokin’ hotties playing in the sun. “Yes, I realize the thing with Cozen looked weird. But the queen of the pod people isn’t exactly on the sweet side of normal herself.” Another smile creases his lips as a beautiful blonde bounces by. “No, I don’t know what Troy thinks about the whole thing. I haven’t even talked to her since I saw her in Ceep’s office.” Sigh. “Yes, I do think she’s still mad about the whole Ferguson thing. Regardless of the fact he was a little twat.” Tyler turns his head to check out another woman who’s taken to jogging up at his side. He flashes her the smile, which incites a wink in return. She traces the tip of her tongue around ruby red lips before speeding up. Instead of matching pace, the Golden Boy prefers to lag behind and enjoy the newfound scenery. “Look, as entertaining as this whole retrospective on the many mistakes of Tyler Rayne has been, you think it’s possible we could, I dunno, try and focus on next week? While I realize a workout might cut into your manicure time, some of us are actually out here training to get back on the winning team.” The brunette with the fantastic ass turns to face Tyler, now jogging backward as she puts a curled hand near her mouth and bulges the side of her cheek with her tongue. That, for those who don’t know, is the international sign for ‘I want to suck your cock.’ Tyler smiles and puts his thumb to his ear, pinky stretched down to his mouth. International sign for ‘Call me.’ “What? Sorry, Tink. Got distracted.” The woman begins flashing numbers at Tyler with his finger. He watches intently, the smile never wavering. After she finishes, the woman shrugs. As a reassurance, the Underground Pimp flashes the numbers back to her flawlessly. “I’ve been jogging for the past…two hours? Something like that. Damn near listened to the whole ‘Empire’ soundtrack.” If a wide and pearly smile is ever indication of satisfaction, the woman would appear to be more than pleased with Tyler’s recitation. Before leaving, she makes a ring with her thumb and forefinger, driving the index finger of her other hand through the hole. Repeatedly. The international sign for ‘I want you to fuck me.’ Or possibly ‘I want you to finger bang me.’ Or even the rare ‘Please stick a finger in my asshole.’ Really, this one’s always a toss up. “I’m about five minutes from the dojo. Got a little awareness training planned.” The brunette, now having turned her back (or more importantly, her ass) on Tyler and sped up once again, trails away to the left around the next bend in the path. Tyler smiles, turning his head to watch as he follows the path down to the right. * * * * I wiped the sweat off my forehead with the turquoise towel at hand. Blackberry via Bluetooth was in full gear as I sunned on the balcony of Chateau de Chandler. San Diego was lifting up her skirt for me, showing a cloudless blue sky. And although business was calling my name, technological had blessed me similarly. To talk shop with Rayne and even out one’s bronzing of the chest was a beautiful thing. “You keep apologizing and my win-loss record keeps looking the same, bucko.” I was venting in a non-productive way, burdening Rayne with last week’s loss. On some level, the only person in PRIME I could reasonably call a friend understood my plight. He had watched his golden goddess go down in blue flames against Cozen, as well. “That Keebler she-wolf nearly decapitated me from behind. Your best bud Cozy is seriously headfucked” Even so, I had my own fault in all this. Each week had turned into a carousel of events, from dealing with Gamble to Cantrell tossing me around the card. And there was the looming monolith, the mythmaking giant of a Pay-Per-View less than ten weeks away. Colossus is like the beach; it doesn’t matter what the weather’s like, come that first sunny week in May, it’s all anybody can think about. “Well, Ty, have you asked your gee eff what she has to say about the situation?” Rayne had his flaws, don’t get me wrong. She was an undeniable number one, but that would be for another conversation, another time. “Maybe the old lady is a wee bit menstrual about the whole…Dual Halo thing.” What we both knew I meant was the “Danny Ferguson” thing, but I was still wrapping my mind around the facts. Danny was gone. Forever. And not like a wrestling forever that lasts until the pot runs dry. Dude was financially secure enough to live off the end of his PRIME contract (and DVD sales from that sickening, crushing fall in the Halo). If I ever wanted to get revenge, I wouldn’t be able to seek the solace of the ring. I’d pay with years of my life, spent in California’s spiffy, privatized prison system. And we all know what happens to beautiful people in prison. “Speaking of twats, ya got a mirror handy?” To be sure, there was a different dynamic to this “friendship” with Rayne. The same bickering and pissing contests that I’d shared with He Whose Head Works No Longer and Facey now had people cheering (albeit in smallish packs) for me. It’s a weird world out there . “HEY! I’m chiding you, dweeb! Wait…are you checking out girls? HEY JERKWAD!” I couldn’t fault the man for ruthless pursuit of box, but inattention to Tsonda was unacceptable. He mumbled a half-apology, more for his own nature than for any actual remorse. “What are you even doing? Oh ewww…you’re not mid-coitus, are you? Because we’re cool and all, but the whole phone voyeur shit is not gonna work.” On some level, I appreciated Rayne because his wit was nearly as sharp as mine. Nearly. “No thank you very much,” I opined. “I’m for The Fountain or Friday Night Lights. Ya know, some abstract prog-rock mellow shit.” I didn’t tell Rayne that I’d popped a Tylox to deal with the most racking pain I’d suffered since Culture Shock. Maybe Cozen knew a little something when she put me in the Piano Wire. Maybe she’s just a psycho bitch. “I’ve got my own pre-ReV setup right here.” I declared, admiring the glorious setting. A tall glass of lemon-tinged water was my wingman, fending off the sweet onset of San Diego summer. “Hold on, Ty, I’ve got someone on the other line.” I tapped a button on the BlackBerry for the other line. “Chandler!” The uncommonly frenzied voice of Dr. DiPendo jumped across the line. “Doc, what’s the happenstance, man?” I needed to verbally brush off the fact that I’d just stopped seeing my own shrink no less than a month ago. He had probably spent some weary nights between ReVolutions, wondering whether some hotel maid would find me in a bathtub full of O negative. “Generally, Chandler, it’s polite in cordial doctor-patient relationships to let one’s psychiatrist know when one is planning to run for the hills and give no clue as what he might be doing.” He didn’t have my problem of pride, but his own: compassion. One of my weepy stories had locked DiPendo to my fate. “I’m sorry, Doc,” I sputtered, but was cut off before I got much else out. “Chandler, I’m not going to patronize you. You’re a grown man.” There was clearly another clause coming, one to underscore just how patronizing he was about to be. “But I can’t help feel like a ward of your mental health. And if you just go cavorting off…” He sighed loudly, a noise that made me uncomfortable to have the wireless handset on. “I think you and I should chat.” “Doc, I think I’ve got everything squared aw-” “It doesn’t have to be in my office. We can grab coffee. Something.” He was a man tied up in knots, unsure of whether to take my new aloofness as a cry for help or actual independence. “Yeah, sure. When I’m back in Diego after work next week, I’ll call your office.” Work? “Okay, take care, Chandler.” “You too, Doc.” With my mind still fully occupied, I clicked back over to the Frenemy Direct Line. * * * * Tyler Rayne pushes the door open, stepping cautiously into a spacious room. The hardwood floors glow with the twisting illumination of dancing torch flames, the only source of light for the empty… THWACK!! “Fu…ck.” The 5-Star Champion drops to a knee, struggling to catch his breath. The whistling of the wood staff as it cuts the air is a good indication of the speed, and thus devastation, with which it descends toward his shoulder. Tyler rolls away, cringing at the sound of the staff as it cracks against the floor. “What? Oh. Nothing. Just a few broken ribs.” Tyler pushes himself to his feet, watching as the lithe but deceptively deadly figure of Milena Takahashi steps from the shadows, twirling a twelve inch wooden bar in her hand. “No, I’m not serious. They’re probably just bruised.” Yes, he’s still on the phone. This, kids, is what we call multi-tasking. Milena lunges forward with the staff, Tyler able to easily sidestep. “I don’t even know who we’re supposed to be fighting, Tink.” Tyler rolls forward, lashing out with a right hook that misses Milena by centimeters. He falls into the momentum, letting himself roll forward again, this time bringing his foot around so that the sole of his boot smacks right against the assassin’s forehead. “You’ve got to be fuckin’ kidding me.” He makes a move to grab the staff, but Milena is quick little minx, and all he manages to take is a knee to the gut, followed by another vicious strike of the staff to his back. He falls face first to the floor, immediately rolling away so as to not take the far too obvious downward strike that follows. “Simply Beautiful and Logic?” The Golden Boy spins on his back, throwing out a leg that trips up Milena and drops her to the floor as well. Tyler nips up, attempting to land on Milena’s face as he does so, but she rolls underneath. As soon as his feet touch the floor, he’s in the air again, a front flip that keeps him from getting tripped up by Milena’s attempt at a sweep. “I mean, I know we fell a few rungs down the ladder. I didn’t realize we’d fallen off the gods damned thing.” He jumps back, narrowly dodging another swipe of that club. Again. And again. Another lunge from Milena. Again he sidesteps, this time immediately pushing off and stepping into her. A shoulder to the gut takes her next few breaths. He spins under her outstretched arm, coming up behind and hooking her for a Reverse DDT, dropping her spine right down onto his knee. She grunts, releasing the weapon and trotting away. “Fuckin’ big men. I hate big men.” Tyler spins around, dropping to a knee and raising the baton in both hands to block a strike coming from what was behind. Katterina Wylde smiles at him for just a second before putting a boot straight to his chest and knocking him about six inches back. “Logic. He’s another one of those giant sumnabitches. Probably bald, too. Stupid fuckin’ nipple headed bastards.” She knows him too well. The Dark Angel comes in swinging wildly, though not without a semblance of planned attack. He manages to stave off most of the salvo with a fantastic defense. The whole thing has turned into a sword fight. Or, considering the source…a lightsaber battle. “I am not unnecessarily bitter toward big, steroid jacked ass bags. And no, this doesn’t have anything to do with Polish beating me like five times when I came in.” The battle rages with Tyler still on the defensive. He’s yet to take a blow, but Katterina is doing a fine job of backing him across the room. And it hasn’t gone unnoticed that Milena is beginning to snuff the flames as the two pass them. “Can we just drop the whole Polish thing? Seriously. I’m 5-Star Champion and he’s not even here, so can we please move on?” CRACK!! “It’s nothing. I told you I’m training, didn’t I?” The two sticks cross high in the air, both Katterina and Tyler pushing for an advantage. Momentary stalemate. “Um…let’s just call it reflex training.” Tyler takes the advantage with an unexpected kick to the shin. With Katterina momentarily distracted by the childish tactic, Tyler cracks her on both shoulders. She winces and backs away, joining Milena at what is now the last burning torch. All three of them smile in the glow of dancing flame. “Look, Tink, I’ma have to call you back later. Things are about to get…intense.” The flame goes out, casting the entire room in complete darkness. “Yeah, sure thing, bro. See ya then.” He can’t see shit. The soft scratch of his sunglasses sliding across the hardwood is the only sound in the room. Beyond, of course, his own voice. Damn if he doesn’t love the sound of that. “Alright, ladies. ‘Nough distractions. Let’s see if you can give me a real workout now.” * * * * “Well, I do know who we’re facing, Mr. Too Big For His Britches To Check The Card.” I was over the whole “go in with nothing but gusto” approach from Rayne. Who was I to argue with whatever tactics had won the Dual Halo? Whereas months ago, I’d bested him at DDT, the thought of actually testing myself against him in the ring was foreign. He was getting better; more aggressive and more in control. I played, in my mind, with the notion of who would become Universal Champion first. “Simply Beautiful and Logic.” I flipped over onto my stomach. “I kid you not. Cantrell’s taking the kiddies out of the sandbox and throwing ‘em right into the lion’s den.” There was no love lost between Ceep and I; our past attempt at cooperation had been a nuclear holocaust of a disaster. He had wanted final cut on the show; I didn’t want something on TV that made me look like a prick. Suffice to say, our unnamed project had been aborted before it had legs. “Undercut by Dust and now this. Rolo Junior and Too-Tall The Mathlete” I looked up at the sky; dark grey hordes amassed on the horizon, threatening my lovely day. “Maybe you should watch some Chan TV, bucko. I’ve taken down Hoyt and TSJ, twin towers of PRIME.” Rayne ignored, going off on an exploratory tangent. It sounded like he was practicing for a gnarly, ripshit pre-match promo. Naturally, I tried to break his concentration. “Logic? Pfft. He’s cake. Maybe you’ve just got a problem with big, steroid jacked ass bags.” I had, instinctively, looked up tapes on the guy. They were pretty few and far between. A couple fWo dark matches, some polished ring work in two-bit spots. Another new face among a dwindling number of old ones. “A little Tombo Walczak on your mind, pardner?” We each had a singles win against the other to boast about, but I wouldn’t let Rayne forget his rookie mistakes “Hey, what the hell are you doing? No sex on the phone applies to use of toys and dolls as well, you sicko.” The dark tendrils of clouds now threatened to choke the breath out of my perfect blue sky. Calmly, black plumes were sliding across the heavens. Sunning any longer was useless. “Gross, I’m gonna throw up the Panera I had for lunch.” Whatever Rayne was doing at least had the sound of unholy sex. Luckily, he explained that he had to go to sodomy practice or something. A female giggle in the background confirmed my suspicions “Alright then, I’ll get at you when I get into the Twin Cities.” It was good, though, to have someone to shoot the shit with before matches. Someone who, worst comes to worst, my peoples come first. The threatened rain clouds now descended. Minute drops pelted my bare back. If San Diego was going to be like this, I thought, it was time to get a head start on making it to ReVolution.
