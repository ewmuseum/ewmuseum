Date: 8105 'Today, the Screen Acting Dinosaurs' Guild filed suit against Barney the Lovable Dyno for defamation of character. SADG President Tyrone Rex issued this statement to cameras from the breakfast table of his pristine white Malibu mansion.' Rex: Barney is only 6 feet tall. He teaches the kids about fairness and tolerance and all that crap. Plays in the playground, gives them a good moral fiber to begin life with. Totally antithetical to the natural instincts and duties of a dinosaur that are for the most part left only to those of us who are sustained through expensive special effects from Mr. Spielberg. Dinosaurs don't help people, we eat people! 'Mr. Rex said that while enjoying toast with a jam made of freshly-squeezed people. The real losers -- Tyrone Rex's pet dingos, which were forced to watch through a outside the patio from the outside of the house as Rex ate a whole platter of piping hot babies without offering the dingos a single one.' Such was on the television at the time. It was late into the night, and Colossus II had just passed. Teh Pwn hadn't won, in fact, they didn't even live up to their name. They didn't Pwn, they got pwned. Man, how disheartening. That's like The Ream Team getting sexually assaulted by Sweet Illusion. Vangellus was looking up at the motion detector's beeping green light, in keeping with the movements of Gabriel Brown. Gabe packed his bags, fully expecting to get on the road early in the morning. 'Securitas?' Oxios questioned the device. You read that right. 'They trying to sound Spanish?' Brown answered since the machine clearly wouldn't. 'Not Spanish. It's an attempt to invoke a sense of Latin.' 'That's what I said, Spanish.' Gabe shook his head. 'They're not the same.' And his wrestling tights rolled neatly, placed into an airtight bag, and into the Samsonite bag. Vangellus asks, 'What's the difference?' 'About 3000 years,' Gabriel answered the question in classic in-a-nutshell mode. Ignoring the fact that he just got served, Oxios clapped his hands together. 'Well, we didn't get Fallen Halo, but things'll change next time around. We've got a single booking against The Forsaken, right? You've beat them before, and I vaguely recall facing them in RPW so I must have won. It'll be a piece of pie.' But then... 'No.' And with that, the dreams of championship success... okay, scratch that. Let's be honest, this is Vangellus Oxios that we're talking about. He's getting a belt, no matter what. So to start over *rewind* 'No.' And with that, the championship aspirations of Teh Pwn were dashed upon the concrete like so many misbehaving children. Hey, everything is bigger in Texas. Especially capitol punishment (take THAT, retards! *pelvic thrust*). 'What do you mean no?' came from the Mouth of the Mid-South (gave himself the name retroactively as a shout-out to RPW... did I just say shout-out? God, no one says that anymore -- man, I'm disastrously unhip), Vangellus Oxios. Oxios had the ice pack over his neck, sitting on his bed at the Country Inn Suites. He and his partner went cheap, went Dutch, and went for the double-beds. Neither of them were going to be getting any women tonight, anyway. Not that they couldn't, but damned if either of them were in the mood right now. 'That's it.' Unlike Oxios, Gabriel Brown had already changed out of his ring attire. He would forego any pain-dulling agents. Whatever he suffered, he just gritted and bore it. Being habitually slow, Oxios repeated, 'No?' 'No,' Mr. Brown sighed. 'The word that ends all discussion because it signals that there is no alternative, there is no debate... No means no.' Their ring pants had alternating colors, but otherwise were the same. That was supposed to be just one of the things about them that signalled that they were the real deal. A true tag team. And the chemistry... at least the chemistry between the jokester and straightman. Each had their role to play and did it well. Gabriel continued. 'Our team is done.' The man in the Hello Kitty pajamas (that would be Gabe... didn't you know? Hello Kitty is Christ-erific!) plopped down onto the previously undisturbed floral-patterned bed comforter. He hated his positioning, being closest to the air conditioning unit that blasted 50 degrees on high fan. Both of them wanted to be as far as possible from it, but Oxios won the 'Connect 4' playoffs (don't ask) to get the further bed. 'I'm just not feeling it.' Oxios felt hurt. 'What is it? Am I not sexy enough for you?' A pillow flung into his face was his answer. 'Seriously,' Gabe sat up, looking at his former RPW cohort. 'This whole team... it's just not right.' Using the word 'seriously' is not a great way to start a conversation with a man who has not a single serious bone in his body. Brown continued. 'Do you remember why I joined PRIME with you?' Vangellus's stare was blank and quizzical at once. Gabriel blinked twice, the outward extent of his building frustration. 'Did you ever care to ask?' Oxios shook his head. Brown said, 'Okay, you know the other place I'm working? New Frontier? Yeah, well, it's fine and all. But I'm doing it myself there. And that's okay with me. But there was this nagging feeling I had, like maybe I hadn't done all I wanted to do with tag team wrestling. Can't even say why, since that's what I've done most of my time in the past few years of wrestling. So I got you as a partner and tried it out.' 'Me because I'm the greatest partner EVAH!' Oxios thrust his arms into the air triumphantly. Plainly, Gabe denied it. 'Really, I picked someone like you because I cold easily lose you.' 'What?' Shocked, Vangellus ran his hands seductively down the sides of his body while saying, 'How could you think you could so easily walk out on all of this?' Another pillow. 'Van! Look, this whole Teh Pwn thing. It's over. It's not you, I had no expectations of you. It's just that I expected too much of the whole situation. I'm done with tag team wrestling. For now, for the foreseeable future.' Oxios paused for a moment, then with a sly grin he returned, 'You miss Rampart.' 'RAMPAGE,' Brown corrected. Oxios shrugged. 'Whatever.' Gabriel stood up, walking over to the window and looking out. The night sky was overcast, and below two kids were making out in the parking lot, outside of the car. 'I'll admit, it was good teaming with him, but I was still carrying him. No one would have hired a guy like him without a spoiler to take the edge off and keep him from killing guys in the ring. Why else would I, even as that ridiculous Invincible Gabe character, get the title shots and he barely get singles matches?' Turning back to Oxios, Brown was as candid as ever. 'You couldn't hold a candle to him. I attribute it to Skylar Green's infinite patience that your team with him lasted as long as it did in RPW. You don't just need to be carried; you're a complete novelty. Your shelf-life in any federation is a few months, at best. You've got no depth, you refuse to improve in the ring... you don't have any longevity in this sport. So yes, you're easy to dump, as a partner.' And that was the end of the conversation, as Gabriel left the room. He began to say something else, but just stopped. No sense in it. Kind of a conversation killer, what he said. Oxios wasn't hurt. In fact, he was motivated. Needed to be carried? Far from the truth. 'Eclipse Champion in RPW, baby!' Didn't come easy, but he did it. One would wonder why he shouted that aloud in an empty room, though. 'You think I'm useless? Well then, see how you like it when I continue with this team. WITH the guy you think needed you so badly.' Scott Matthews? Needed Gabe? We'll see about that. It's like George Carlin said when approached about a Bill and Ted sequel. {Well, f*ck, if you can get Keanu Reeves, I can get Alex Winter!}
