Bringing The Band Back Together (Part 1, the Codemaster)

19 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 19th, 2007 Allen Brown. Many once knew him as "the Codemaster". As a hammer-swinging, fireball-flinging fiend, the Codemaster hopped on the Goombas of the wrestling world, crushing their exoskeletons with the force of his heat alone. He made many attempts to work with the fans Wrestling organization, and he has had touch-and-go success every time he came to the organization. Since the fWo's collapse back in late 2005, the Codemaster had become a very infrequent in-ring competitor. Choosing instead to manage his "minions", the Falk Brothers, the Codemaster has had limited success in the independents. The Codemaster knew that he wasn't going to succeed if he played second fiddle to the Falks, however. However, he had few options left. The fWo ship had sunk long ago, and with it, any shot he might have had of using his entourage of minions to catapult himself into the main event scene. All-Star Championship Wrestling also sunk, and with it, his most recent meal ticket, Andrew Sharp. That was why the Codemaster was biting the bullet. He was taking a chance at trying to impress a talent scout from an organization called PRIME with his charisma. That's why... now was the time on Sprockets where he danced. "Mr. Brown... why are you dancing?" the talent scout asked. He was one of those guys who looked fresh out of college, wore a clip-on tie, and literally had no business asking the Codemaster what he was or was not doing. He held a clipboard, which presumedly held a contract to the organization he worked for. The two men were standing alone in the parking lot of the convention center that the Codemaster just got through wrestling at. There, he had wrestled one of his old Blue Rogue partners, Scott Falk, to a twenty-five minute match that ended when the Codemaster used his usual manners of trickery and deceit to come away with the victory. The man, clearly black and therefore possessing mad skills when it comes to the art of busting a groove, was still in his blue and white singlet from his match. "Doin' my best Agent Spin, you hear?" the Codemaster said, as he was indeed doing an Elite Beat Agents dance. He finished his dance by pointing right into the scout's face and shouting, "Agents are go!" The talent scout adjusted his tie. It was going to be a *long* day. The scout cleared his throat as the Codemaster relaxed his stance, "Mr. Brown. You had a good showing tonight. Would you be interested in a..." The Codemaster put his hand in front of the scout's face, as if he wanted him to shush. The scout stared at the Codemaster's hand for a few seconds as it lingered there. The scout was confused, but he soon managed to squeak out the rest of his sentence, "...tryout?" "Brother, you're ruining my good vibe. Didn't I just get through trying out with you?" the Codemaster asked. "But... but..." the scout said, trying to object like he was Phoenix Wright, but was coming off more like Winston Payne. "Look, you got a busy job. Save the both of us some time, skip all this 'tryout' crap that'll just feel like the first three hours of Kingdom Hearts II, and just give me something to sign." the Codemaster said. "But... that's... that's now how it works..." the scout said. The Codemaster sighed, came over, and put his arm around the scout's shoulders. He looked down at him, as the scout wasn't very tall even compared to the only-of-average-height Codemaster. "What's your name?" the Codemaster asked in a faux friendly tone, as he was really just irritated on the inside. "L-Larry..." he said. "Mr. Butz," the Codemaster said without skipping a beat, "Do you know what they say about people like you? They say 'if something smells, it's probably the Butz'." "That... that has nothing to do with..." Larry said, in a pathetically attempted try at objecting to the Codemaster's quick-witted speech. "Ah, but it has everything to do with the situation, my dear Mr. Butz," the Codemaster said with a wry smile on his face, "See, I ain't the only one you're gonna sign to your little contract. I got myself a bunch of sideshow freaks who'll bring me to the top of your rinky-dink promotion... uh... PRIME, right? Yeah. Why anyone would call their promotion that is beyond me. Makes me think of prime ribs, actually. And let me tell you, just so we get some confusion out of the way... Yoshis do not translate to good prime ribs. So, whenever you go to Japan, I wouldn't suggest ordering the Yoshi ribs. Hoo, doggie, that's worse than the gag reflex I went through when I found out that the winner of the Man Festival got man raped. That makes me wary of any wrestling rings being stacked on top of one another, you know? Anyway, you get my point in all of this, don't you?" "No!" Larry objected. The Codemaster overruled him, "It means I'm gonna take this here contract from your hands," he said, as he did just that, "I'm gonna make a couple of copies... oh, about seven or eight, I think... and then I'm gonna hunt down the people that run your little company and convince them to let me and my posse join. Oh, and by the way, said posse has nothing to do with some band of street thugs. Just `cause I'm black doesn't mean we gotta play the race card, y'know? Anyway, you, of course, don't have a problem with that... right, Mr. Butz?" "Y--" Larry attempted to counter, but let's face it, the guy was probably the worst possible person to have brought a contract to a guy like the Codemaster. "Great. So, I'm gonna go on my mission from Mantorok now. I got places to go, people to see... you understand, don't you, Mr. Butz? Good. Catch you on the Hyrule side." the Codemaster said, before he walked away with the clipboard in hand. Larry just stood there, stunned from what just transpired. Finally, he sheepishly said to himself, "My... my name's not 'Mr. Butz'..."
