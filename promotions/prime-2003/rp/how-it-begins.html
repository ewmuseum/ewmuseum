How It Begins

Date: Here and There St Augustine's New York City 11:14 a.m. April 19, 2008 Tyler Rayne steps out of the alcove of St. Augustine’s on 124th, pulling a cigarette from the pack already in hand. The Camels disappear within the lining of his jacket, replaced by everyone’s favorite novelty lighter. Flame is just about to touch paper when the door to St. Augustine’s bursts open. The man stumbling out the door unintentionally shoulder checks Rayne as he pushes for the nearby alley. Tyler grunts, noting with annoyance the proximity of that flame to his perma-stubble. He checks over his shoulder, watching for just a second to make sure that door doesn’t come exploding again before he lights the cigarette. PRIME’s 5-Star Champion takes a sweet drag of inevitable cancer before putting boot to pavement and following the sporadic trail of the man who bumped him. The man, dressed in the blues of New York’s finest, steadies himself with a hand against the brick wall of the church. His body heaves as he wretches. Tyler waits quietly, watching the man’s attempts to vomit. "First time on a scene?" The officer holds up a finger, begging for patience as the scene from St. Augustine’s sanctuary finally succeeds at bringing up breakfast. Tyler turns away, taking note of the few curious bystanders behind the yellow line. Labored breathing is Tyler’s indication that, at least for the moment, the rookie has recovered. "Don’t sweat it, kid. Lotta people do that on their first time." "Ah…fuck you, pal. I been workin’ the force for twenty god damn yea’s…" Tyler’s brow furrows in curiosity, eyes narrowing to absorb finer details of the upset policeman as he takes another satisfying pull from the cigarette. The cop pulls off his cap, revealing a short crop of salt and pepper, wiping sweat from his brow with the same forearm that just wiped his mouth. Tyler doesn’t bother to mention this. Crow’s feet spider out from the corner of the man’s eyes, pulling tightness into skin that otherwise hangs heavy, not so much from age, but from the intense wear and tear of years with the NYPD. "It’s not the age. It’s the mileage." "Huh?" The cop shakes his head, obviously confused. Tyler waves the quote away, pulling the pack of cigarettes from his jacket again and offering one up to the officer. "Quit ‘bout twelve yea’s back. Wife’d kill me she knew I stahted up again." Tyler just shrugs, satisfied to suck down his own cancer. "What’s your name, kid?" "Paulie. Wha’s it to ya?" Another shrug. Tyler snubs the embers of ash with his boot, rolling his eyes at the stereotypical New York attitude. "Yer one a da Feds, right?" "Somethin’ like that." "So what is this, then? Some kinda fuckin’ serial killa thing or somethin’?" "You got awfully chatty all a sudden." "Twenty yea’s on da force, and not once I evah seen somethin’ like wha’s in ‘ere. Now here you is, some kinda special fuckin’ forces or some shit. Me? I figah ya got some answers. Likes…how da hell a person can go and do that to someone else." "Who told you that?" "Tol’ me what?" Tyler smirks. "It was a person." "Da fuck you mean?" Tyler’s smile widens, though it still lacks any real mirth. Before he can explain further, however, another figure joins the mix. Both men look up to acknowledge the addition, Tyler’s smile filling with the emotion it previously lacked when he recognizes the loose, dark hair and hardened features of Alexis Grant. She approaches, the badge attached to her jacket emblazoned with all kinds of special Federal status. "Officer, would you mind leaving us alone for a bit?" "Sure thing, lady." Paulie the curious policeman replaces his cap, giving a final nod to both Alexis and Tyler before leaving. Rayne watches him go, testing out the sturdiness of a nearby trash can before he hops up on the lid for a seat. Two more cigarettes are removed from the pack, one of which he passes to Alex. "You alright?" She gives the slightest shake of her head, not necessarily as an answer itself, but more to emphasize the point that she just doesn’t have one. "I get a call at six in the morning to come down here and check out this scene. Which means I’ve spent the last five hours absorbing the details of seven eviscerated twenty-somethings in a church sanctuary. I’m not Catholic, but shit like that will certainly send you lookin’ for God." "Or wonderin’ if He exists at all." "Seven people, Ty. Crucified and gutted. Got their intestines spread up into the rafters and crossed like some kind of fuckin’ psycho spider web. Eyes gouged out and replaced with silver dollars. And those are the details I can manage to talk about without wanting to cry. So, I think, just for the sake of clarification… it’ll be a long fuckin’ time before I’m ever alright." He nods, blowing out smoke through his nostrils. "You check the date on those coins?" "Didn’t think to, no. You really think it’s him?" "Bet my ass on it." A thoughtful silence falls between them. Alex is the first to speak her thoughts aloud. "So why now?" "That’s the million dollar question, kid. He must know I took the job in Japan. That I’m lookin’ for him. It just…doesn’t make sense. Disappears for a couple years just to come back and start with the crazy only a month after I’ve taken his bounty. He’s fuckin’ with me." Another pause. "You don’t think this was the first one, do you?" Tyler shakes his head. "Wouldn’t make sense if it was. I’ll check the exact dates when I get back. Start lookin’ for anything suspicious that pops up seven days after I get back. Or maybe seven from when I took the job. There’s gotta be…something." "I could do that, you know. It’s kind of, like…my job." "True. But I need you to do another favor for me. Something I can’t do." "Wow. Something Tyler Rayne can’t do. This is quite the shock." "Hardy har har. Look, there’s this girl come in…" "Cozen?" He smiles. "You’ve been watching?" "When I can spare the time. Saw you win that big match a few weeks back." "Right. That. So…the girl. I need you to find out what you can." "Won’t be easy." "Never is. Can you do it?" "I can try. What are you thinking?" "I don’t rightly know. Girl’s a shapeshifter. Damn good one, too. Twice now she’s had everyone fooled ‘bout just who exactly she was. Definitely had some training. No one gets that good on their own. Try maybe looking into some missing persons. Thus far they’ve been female. Young. Pretty. Don’t have enough to exactly count it as MO, but it’s a start." "I’ll see what I can do. You gonna tell Katt about…this?" Alex waves her arm toward the church entrance. "He won’t have to." From the opposite end of the alley she comes. Out of the shadows. Stiletto heels to the knee and slinky black dress that ends just above. "Good God damn, kid." She shrugs but gives an appreciative smile. "Well, if you plan on having lunch in New York, suppose you’d better look the part." "Fair enough." "Any idea who they were?" Alexis sighs. "No positives. We assume they were drug runners of some sort. One of the local blues thinks he recognizes one of them, but with all the… well, it’s just hard to tell much of anything right now. We’ll know more when the lab work comes in." The answer drags a smaller smile across the Dark Angel’s face. Something begins working under those smooth features. A kind of mischief Alexis can’t quite place. But Tyler recognizes that gleam in her eye, and immediately makes moves to subvert the conflict before it comes. "Car’s around the block, Katt. Let’s roll." She sighs, turning to blast Tyler with an annoyed glare. Without an argument, though, she proceeds to march in the direction he had nodded. Tyler pushes himself from his makeshift seat and steps up beside Alexis. "Thanks, kid. I’ll give you a call next week." "No problem. Is she gonna be, uh…" "I’ll handle it." He offers a reassuring smile before kissing her on the forehead. "You’ve got enough to worry about." Alexis Grant offers a thankful nod and smile, her hand slipping off his shoulder and down his arm as he jogs off to catch up with Katterina. "You know I’m right." "Katt, please." "They weren’t innocent people, Ty." "No one’s innocent, kid. Don’t really feel like arguing the point." He unlocks the door to his Mustang and holds it open for her, letting her slide in before he slips on over to his own side of the car. "I’m just saying, if it was him—" "It was him. I drove here from the Manor this morning." He taps the clear plastic screen over the odometer before starting up the car. Exactly 77.7 miles since he last reset the counter. "It was him." ---- Miazami's New York City 12:47 p.m. April 19, 2008 "That’s the third time Brooks has called you. Today." Katt sighs, tossing his phone back down onto the table. He looks up, expressionless, chewing through a mouthful of sushi and enjoying the pleasant breeze blowing through downtown. "What’s up with you two?" He shrugs. "They fired Faith." "And?" "And she got worried ‘bout her job. Some big shot new producer running around, tryin’ to toss some weight. Told her if she didn’t think a little more carefully about her personal relationships, she’d be following Faith out the door." "So she told you. And you’re being a jerk about it." He sighs, turning an aggravated gaze over to her. She just raises an eyebrow and shrugs, daring him to prove her wrong. Instead, he just laughs. "You’re such a fuckin’ bitch." "So you’ll call her back?" "Yes. I’ll call her back." "Good. Hear from Kairi lately?" "Just the other day. Drake’s doing well. Trial starts tomorrow. Isn’t Melina still over there?" "She’s keeping an eye on things. Pretty little minx, but not so great with the detailed explanations." "Point." "So…" "So?" "So…what’s up with you and Troy?" He laughs. "Nothing." "Nothing my ass." "It’s nothing." "Bullshit." Another laugh. He smiles, shoving another mouthful of sushi into his mouth. ---- Casa di Troy: Northeast Cambridge, Mass 2:23 p.m. April 11, 2008 The sun beats bright in the sky, shining down on a rich Boston suburb to create one of the most picturesque and beautiful scenes he could have the pleasure to enjoy. It was just warm enough for Tyler Rayne to forsake his favored leather jacket for a simple black t-shirt and remain quite comfortable as he proceeded to mash and mix a menagerie of foreign plants into a small bowl. He sits on the steps of a very expensive looking house, listening to the insistent bark of some very angry dogs from within the home. He can hear her footsteps bounding across the hardwood floor. Her shouted command for the dogs to silence. The jingle of a lock and the slightest creak of the hinge of the door. "Well that didn’t take long." Tyler flashes his infamous smile, hoping it would help the former Universal Champion forget her exasperated sigh. Lindsay Troy offers a weak smile in return, stepping out of the foyer to address her visitor, eyes squinting in the sun. "What are you doing here?" The breeze blows her long hair forward, managing for the briefest of moments to hide the harshness of a ghastly burn near her right eye. He holds up the bowl and offers another appeasing smile. "Thought maybe you could use some more." "Right. So you just happened to show up at my house…which, how did you find my house again?" He shrugs. "Had to call in a few favors with the locals. But, well…you know. I know people." "No. I don’t know. And I don’t want to know, either. You have to stop doing this." "Doing what?" "Showing up." "What? You don’t wanna see me?" Lindsay pinches the bridge of her nose in frustration. "I don’t want you lurking on my steps like some kind of stalker. People can see you." Tyler stands. For the first time, when he looks at her, he’s not smiling. "So it’s okay for you to be seeing me, so long as no one else does. That it?" She sighs. "My neighbors are straight out of Bravo's 'Real Housewives...' series: snooty, busy-bodied gossipers and any day now I expect the TV trucks to pull up and start rolling footage for next season. So just get inside. Please." The smallest of smirks crosses his face as she pulls open the door for him to step inside. "And we're not dating," came the reminder as his foot crossed the threshold. The house was beautiful. Bright. Lots of windows. Lots of sunshine. Though his eyes dart about the house, recording and cataloguing the personal touches, his vector never strays from a straight shot to the kitchen, where he pulls a chair from the bar and nods his head toward the seat. "Sit." She sighs again, but follows the command without question. Tyler scoops an ample supply of that herbal remedy goo from the bowl, waiting for the Queen to find a comfortable position before he begins rubbing the salve into the burn below her eye. "That was one hell of a fight, kid." She says nothing; only snorts in disgust. "Look at me." He grabs her chin, not forcefully, but with just enough pressure to get her attention. To make her eyes meet his. "That was amazing. It’s been a long time since I’ve seen someone put up that kind of fight. You may have lost, but you did worse to the girl than you know. You scarred the bitch. Marked the invisible man. Every day that girl wakes up and looks in the mirror, she’s going to see your face. Not just because she’s wearing it. Because that scar, til the day she dies, will be a reminder. She’s not untouchable. She’s not a ghost or a phantom. She’s a person. Real and whole. You gave her somethin’ to remember that. Every day. Every time she sees a reflection. She took your belt. Big fuckin’ deal. You’ll get it back. Next time you two meet, she’ll be looking across the ring, not at a victim, but at an equal. You’re just as lethal. Just as deadly. She fucked up. Now she knows. And next time…she’ll be afraid of what you’ll do for the encore." "She's not afraid." Her fist instinctively clenches in defense and her voice drops low. "She's the goddamn fatted calf parading around with my looks, my hair, my eyes and now my belt. It's a big fucking deal that I lost, Tyler. Maybe your rah rah cheerleader act can't grasp it, but I've never lost to another woman before. Not in twelve years. I'm more than just flips and twists and I demanded that match because it was time I went back to what helped get me to where I am. Where I was. Where I am now is sitting in my kitchen after taking another fireball to the face and trying to grasp the reality that I lost to someone who isn't anybody at all." He sighs, rising up and stalking toward the fridge in hopes of finding some beer. "She’s somebody now, kid. You made her somebody. You can bitch and whine about losing that match all you want. Losing sucks. Losing that title probably sucks worse." Beer is good. Beer is refreshing. Beer is soothing. "But whether that girl knows it or not…she’s fucked. You tell me what a girl who hides in shadows is gonna do when she can’t get away from the spotlight. You tell me what a girl with no name is gonna do when, everywhere she goes, she’ll be known as the champ. As the woman who finally beat Lindsay Troy. Stings like a bitch. And you can be cold and callous as you want, but don’t think I haven’t lost my share of personal battles out there. Thing is, a week or two from now, Cozen’s going to have an identity crisis. First time in her life…the girl’s going to have a real identity. You don’t think that’s gonna fuck with girl’s head? She’s jacked. Just doesn’t know it yet." He smiles and passes Lindsay a bottle. "And that’s not even counting what I’m gonna do to her." "I don’t need you fighting my battles, Rayne." "Nope. That’s what you’ve got Wade for." Her eyebrows smash together, hazel eyes narrowing. "I don't need him fighting them either and don't you dare throw him back in my face." He rolls his eyes but puts both hands up in submission. "I’m not. Honest truth, I’m over Wade. Country can do what he wants. More power to ‘im. I got my own plans for the Skrull. No need to worry ‘bout that. But it isn’t what you think. Won’t be what anybody’s expecting. She may be clever. She may be ready. But I promise you…she’s not ready for this. All this talk about our little doppelganger, however, has got me a little worked up. Any ideas on how to relieve some pent up frustrations?" The smile. She can’t help but return one of her own, hand reaching out to snatch him by the shirt and pull him closer. "I’ve got an idea." "Yeah?" "Mmm." So close it hurts. Her lips are just about to touch his when her head moves to the side, sliding past his cheek. Breath heavy on his skin. Sultry voice in his ear. "Ciro and Isis need to go for a walk." He laughs. Can’t help it. She pulls away, a wry smile spreading her lips as well. "You’re such a fuckin’ pain in my ass." This time when he grabs her, it’s a little more forceful. A little more to the point. This time, their lips do meet. She doesn’t fight, doesn’t try to pull away. When he finally does, he’s still smiling. "Where’s the leash?"
