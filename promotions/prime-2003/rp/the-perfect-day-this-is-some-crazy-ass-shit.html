Date: I'm not high...I Promise Leading up to the Dual Halo, Devin Shakur has been a nervous wreck. Last year, he finished in 19th place as a relative unknown and was eliminated by Jonathan Winters. With a year of PRIME experience under his belt, Shakur has been thrown into the Top 10 by many wrestling experts and columnists, with some calling for him to take the whole enchilada. The thought plagues his sleep, crinkling sheets and tossing pillows to the ground. Shakur is allowing the pressure of such a big event to mold him like Play-doh. His confidence has gone from an all time high since Revolution 152, to less sturdy than one block on the base of a twenty row high Jenga game with only a week to go before the big match. After debating the pros and cons, Shakur concluded that in order to alleviate the pressure and promote a lucid thought process for Culture Shock, he needs to construct the most TOTALLY perfect day before the media circus. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Location: Los Angeles, California Through the front window of a suburban home, sunlight mingles with a decorated flower rug and parquet flooring at the base of a thirteen step staircase.
