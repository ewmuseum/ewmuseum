Head in the Game

1 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/1/07 The world was a swirl of emotions around him. He sat there, stoically, staring straight ahead as people patted him on the shoulder and uttered words he never heard. He just sat there, staring straight ahead. Nothing changed, but inside, his guts churned and his mind raced. He never did really learn to deal with grief, he just always put it away as to not think about it. It wasn't expected, and it certainly wasn't fair. He wasn't supposed to have had such a wonderful woman that had lived through the insantiy of dealing with the Poison Tree only to be here in the hospital now. It was staggering. They had been driving to get some dinner in one of the few places that he could sit comfortably and relax in a private area. He had been looking forward to ordering the Alaskan King Crab and she absolutely adored the Atlantic Cod plate. He had taken an easy left in his Escalade with a green arrow when the world had exploded. What was it? It was basically a Savannah yuppie that was yapping away on his cell phone and not paying a bit of attention to what he was doing, so he rolled right through a red light while he was digging a bag out from behind his seat. Four broken ribs and a broken elbow had proved it, instead of him trying to claim his light was green and he was paying absolutely attention. Titan wasn't hurt. The impact had smashed his left elbow through the driver door window and left glass scattered all over the road. The airbag had gone off and hit him in the chest. But it was Stephanie he had been worried about. As soon as the vehicle had stopped skidding from where the Porsche SUV had slammed into them, he had yanked the console open and pulled out the folding knife he kept there. With a flick of the wrist, he had snapped the blade out and stabbed the passenger airbag. Stephanie was tiny, just a shade above five feet tall, and the airbag was covering her face. It wasn't to say that he didn't know what to do. He didn't get her out of the car, he didn't move her at all, but he did get out of the car himself and went around to check on her. Her side of the vehicle had taken the impact full-on. There was a huge crushed in section right on her door, and he could see through the empty window where she had been hit. Her upper arm had a noticable break to it and her face was bruised and already swelling from the impact from the airbag. He did nothing but talk to her, calling her name, and she slowly turned to look at him, answering only in mumbles and blinking as if trying to focus. Now she lay in the hospital bed, asleep from painkillers she most definitely needed. One broken arm, a sprained wrist, fractured femur, broken ankle, concussion and severely bruised face. He sat there, looking at her fiery red hair and delicate little hands and could only think how much he wanted to choke the life out of the other driver. He had come close. Not at the scene, because the man couldn't get out, but on the arrival at the hospital. Titan had squeezed his massive frame into the ambulance to hold her tiny hand in his massive paws and talk to her while the paramedics kept her immobile and assessed her injuries. When the stretcher had gone into the emergency room, he had stood outside, feeling helpless. He was seven feet tall, he weighed four hundred pounds and he could do nothing. And that was when he'd nearly committed a felony. "You moron!" had come the shout as the man came out of one of the examination rooms. "What in the hell did you think you were doing? I ought to sue you within one inch of your..." He had trailed off when he got close to Titan and realized how big he was. Titan rounded on him with a growl and eyes of cold blue stone. His big hands curled into fists the size of country hams and he started towards the man. He stood probably just a shade under six feet and probably could bully around people in his office. His stark white shirt was dribbled with blood, and the stains on his face indicated his nose had started bleeding from the airbag. But his skin paled visibly as Titan started towards him. "You... little..." Titan started, and bit it off. "What did I think I was doing? I was turning through a green light." Another step, which made the other man scurry back four steps to match. "You don't seem to understand, do you?" Another step from him, another four for the other man. "My girlfriend is in there with I don't know how many injuries and you..." Another step, another four. "You come out here yelling at me?" "Don't... don't you touch me!" he shouted. "I'll... I'll..." "I didn't live through years serving in Israel and five instances of shrapnel being removed and three gunshot wounds for some pathetic excuse for a man to ruin my life." Titan took a long, hard breath. "Don't... ever... talk to me... again." Now, here he sat. How could he even hope to stand up in the match with Tsonda? How could he even hope to make a decent showing? Stephanie was laid up in the hospital... He sat there, staring at her sleep, before he reached into his pocket, took out a small box and slid the ring from inside on her finger. He stood as a nurse came in. "Tell her I'll be back in two days. And that I'd like an answer."
