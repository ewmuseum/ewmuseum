Spotlight.

25 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 25, 2010 The marker smells strongly of strawberry, but that’s the point. Scented markers add spice and invite the senses. So, with that in mind, eight-year-old Dante gleefully furnishes the squiggly drawn heart with the red marker, taking in its scent and smiling. He’s in the process of creating the perfect card, sure to steal his beautiful mother’s heart and entice affection of the most endearing degree. Scruffy barks. “Calm down, Scruffy” the boy stated, playfully patting the warm muzzle of the chocolate-spotted Beagle. With his tongue peeking out of his mouth, eight-year-old continues to diligently hammer away at the card. Perfection was his goal. It can’t be dull or boring, no – it needs excitement. It needs complexity. It needs glitz and it needs glamour. He yanks out a blue marker. Blueberry scent. Scruffy barks. “One minute, Scruffy. I’m almost done.” Scruffy whimpers. With the precision of a well-trained assassin, Dante outlines the heart with the dark-shaded blue. Once he connects the ends, he replaces the top on the marker, leans back, and takes a deep breath. Relieved. Satisfied. Scruffy barks. “Alright, boy, let’s make the delivery!” With joy, he jumps out of his seat, homemade card in hand, and scurries down the hall. Scruffy, equally as joyful, charges right behind him. --- Sweat pours profusely down the champion's face, partially blinding him. But he perseveres, pounding away at the heavy duty punching bag with great intensity. "Alright, Vangelus, that's enough for today." Chives the Agent says. But Olsig continues to pound away at the bag, completely deaf to his Agent's instructions. "Vangelus…I said that's enough." Hears nothing but the violent thumping of his heart. It inspires him. Shift "Tonight, Vangelus Olsig steps inside of the ring with Killean Sirrajin in a chair match. It's his very first shot at the coveted Universal Championship!" Shift "Vangelus…stop!" Olsig continues to pound away. Violent thumping. Inspiration. Shift "Sirrain covers Olsig! One…Two…THREE!!! It's over! The champion has successfully defended against the challenger in one of the most tightly contested Universal Title matches we've ever seen!" Shift "Vangelus, you're going to kill yourself…" Shift "Six men enter into Tropical Turmoil, all vying for a shot at the Universal Championship! The top six contenders in PRIME today…Karina Wolfenden, Angelo Deville, Brandon Youngblood, Aimz, Ellis Easton, and Vangelus Olsig!" Shift He continues to pound away. No longer inspired…but infuriated. Shift "That's it! Vangelus Olsig is the first to be eliminated!" Shift Pissed. "Vangelus! Stop!" Shift "Tchu defeats Vangelus Olsig and walks away with the Universal Championship strapped around his waist! What a contest!" "But it seems as if Olsig just fell a tad bit short…again." Shift Enraged. Shift "Tonight, Nova, Tchu, and Vangelus Olsig step inside of the squared circle to fight for the prized Universal Championship!" Shift "Tchu drapes his arm over Olsig! ONE…TWO…THREE!!! It's over! Tchu retains the Universal Championship!" Shift Chives darts towards Olsig, yanking him away from the bag as Olsig struggles just to stand. "Vangelus, snap out of it! You're going to kill yourself!" Deep, heavy breathing. He’s about to pass out. But whatever it takes. --- ”So I told her, if you want me, you’re gonna have to grow an extra leg, honey!” Studio laughter. She sits motionless in the recliner. It’s been a long day. All she wants to do is sit back and drown herself in sitcoms – anything to offer her an escape, even if only briefly, from the mess that has become her life. Solitude. Relaxation. Escape. She embraces it. Cherishes it. Scruffy barks. “Mommy! Mommy! I’ve got something for you.” Her eyes clinch. Not the sound she wanted to hear right now. “What is it dear…” she responds, not even pretending to be the least bit enthused. Before Dante can get to her, Scruffy gets there first. The sight of the dog causes her face to light up. He leaps into her lap. She embraces him warmly. “Scruffy! I wub you, boy! Mommy wubs you!” Scruffy receives the embrace, reacting joyfully. Dante stands back and smiles at the embrace. He proceeds. “Here ya go, Mommy. I made it especially for you.” As Scruffy leaps out of her lap, Dante hands her the card. The gloom look returns. She looks at it unexcitedly. Almost annoyed. “What is it, Dante?” “It’s…a card. I made it…for you.” She rolls her eyes. “Thanks.” “Do you like it?” “Yeah, it’s wonderful.” Silence. “Is there anything else you need, Dante?” “Ummm…not re-…” “Dante, why do you do that?” “Do what? “Act so…weird. I keep telling you that’s not cute. Stop it, okay?” “I wasn’t tryin-…” “You know what? Just run along. Go play with Scruffy..or something.” He detects the apathy. It doesn’t anger him. He’s used to it. But it still hurts. “You’re welcome.” With Scruffy in tow, he runs along…as told. And she resumes soaking in mundane solitude. -- A big, intimidating door. Dante dare not step through it without a valid reason. A valid reason = he’s dying. In other words, Dante dare not step through it at all. But today he’s feeling unusually risky. Or maybe usually melancholy. He knocks. No answer. But someone is on the inside talking. Dante slowly opens the door, the creaking sound piercing his ears – almost like a soundtrack to an inevitable doom. “No, Brock, listen…yeah, that’s what I’m trying to tell you. No, just shut-up and hear me out.” Dante just stands there and gazes. His presence is unnoticed. Scruffy barks. The man turns around and lays eyes on the pup who charges into his arms. “Scruffy! How are you boy?” He takes a break from the intense conversation to embrace the dog. “Love me some Scruffy! Yes I do!” He playfully wiggles the dog before placing him down. He lifts his eyes to see Dante standing there as well. “Dante…is there something you need, son?” “I just wanted to talk.” “Talk about wh-…No, Brock, hold on….talk about what, Dante?” “Well…nothing really. I just wanted to talk to y-…” “Look, this is going to have to wait, okay? I’m in handling some very important business.” “But, I ju-…” “No buts, Dante!” his voice grows fiercer. It always scares Dante when this happens. “You know better to come I here without permission!” “Sorry…” “Now leave. We can talk la-…” Before the sentence is complete, Dante is halfway down the hall, sprinting. Scruffy barks. --- Dante sits at the edge of his family’s pool, waddling his feet in the water. Distraught. Rejected. He should be used to this feeling by now, but even at the tender age of eight, he realizes this isn’t something you just become ‘used’ to. It hurts. Scruffy barks. The pup runs up near Dante and begins to tickle the boy with his nose. Dante doesn’t laugh. He realizes that he lives in a paradox world. He, the child, who both desires and deserves affection, receives nothing but rejection from the very ones who nurtured him. But Scruffy, the dog, receives that affection…that attention that Dante so desperately desires. All he wants is a little attention. A little time to talk. A sincere thank you. Attention. He looks at Scruffy in disgust. The pup continues to bark playfully, running around Dante in circles, beseeching the boy in animal lingo to join in the fun. But Dante is only disgusted by the dog’s presence, and the paradox that he represents. Scruffy jumps into Dante’s lap. Just having the dog in his lap causes Dante to writhe with anger. He snatches the dog and forcefully tosses him into the water. Scruffy barks. And struggles. And barks. And skirmishes. And barks. And wails. Whimpers. Drowning. Dante watches…unmoved. Unaffected. Cold. Relishing in the fact that he’s just eradicated his world of the breathing symbol of his life’s paradox. --- “Mr. Olsig? You were saying?” “Oh, I’m sorry…just kind of had a moment.” Olsig readjusts himself In his seat. “Well, this championship means a lot to me. I’ve fought for six long years to receive this sort of…attention…and, umm…” He coughs. “I want to make it clear to the world, but especially to Mr. Strife, that I have no problem eradicating ANYONE or ANYTHING that attempts to snatch this from me.” “Strong words.” the interview states. “It’s a mindset, Jason. Every since I got into this company, I’ve played second fiddle to DOGS!” “…dogs?” “Yes, dogs. Little creatures bent on stealing my spotlight…taking away the attention that I so rightfully deserve. I DESERVE. A human is greater than a DOG, Jason. I DESERVED the attention.” “…” “But they wanted to take it from me. Dogs…BARKING! Dogs named Nova, named Tchu, named Deville, named Sirrajin – they’re ultimate goal was to take it from me and keep it for themselves. All I wanted was a little attention, Jason. A little time to talk…a sincere thank you.” “…right.” “This belt has finally granted me the attention that I’ve longed for. All of the sudden, my name is in lights. All of the sudden, by place in the Hall of Fame is merited. Suddenly, I’m ranking in the top ten over at PTC. My soul has long hungered for this attention, and now my soul has been fed.” “Uh huh.” “Which is why I consider it the ultimate crime for Castor Strife, a little newbie that pops up out of nowhere, to try and take away my shine. Steal my spotlight. Divert this attention that I’ve garnered…this attention that I cherish.” “And you’ll do anything to prevent that.” “Anything, Jason. I’ll drown the dog if he attempts to take what is mine again.” Jason Wilder, journalist for the Pro Wrestling Chronicles, appears confused. “So the bottom line…” “Castor Strife will DIE if he attempts to steal my spotlight.” “Scattered, but strong words from the mouth of the Universal Champion.” --- Tears. Weeping. Dante stands inside of the home, peering through the window as his family and neighbors mourn over the breathless corpse of Scruffy the dog. He feels no remorse…only anger. Even in death, Scruffy manages to steal Dante’s spotlight. Even in the death, Scruffy manages to garner the attention that Dante longs for. Shift Swelled eyes. Intimidation. Olsig stands inside of his home, clutching his Universal championship, peering at a promotional poster. Vangelus Olsig vs. Castor Strife. There’s fear…but there’s also anger. Anger towards the fact that some white-washed rookie stands on the grandest stage of them all…attempting to steal his spotlight. Anger towards the fact that some white-washed rookie has somehow managed to skyrocket to the top, driven by one objective: snatch away the attention that Olsig has longed fought for. The attention that he’d always come just a ‘tad’ it short of securing. The attention that would cause people to stand up and deem him worthy. Not lucky, not privileged – but worthy. Shift Dante stands, peering. No remorse. Only anger. And a fully-conscious resolve to eliminate anything and everybody that ever tries to steal his spotlight again. Rob him of his due attention. At 16, that resolve serves as the fuel that helps him break into the wrestling industry. A few years later, that resolve serves as the fuel that helps him break into a rising company called PRIME. In 2010, that resolve serves as the catalyst for him running through 29 of that company’s best, heralding him as the official Dual Halo winner. A month later, that resolve serves as the catalyst for him defeating Castor Strife to secure the coveted Universal Championship. On a summer night in August… It’ll be that same resolve that will cause him to triumph over yet another would-be thief of his due spotlight. His due triumph. In 2008, he gave up on this dream. But in 2010, he’s living it. Colossus. The biggest stage of all. Universal Championship. The grandest prize of all. And Vangelus Dante Olsig… Standing in the spotlight of it all.
