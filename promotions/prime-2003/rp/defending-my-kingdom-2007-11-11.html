Defending My Kingdom

11 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th Nov 2007 “So that basically covers it is there any questions at all?” The man who the voice belonged to was Smitty T Duluth, CEO of FUSE with an overactive tone of excitement ringing in his voice. Sat in front of him were Joshua Kosidlo and Ethan Frost. Kosidlo looking on intently at Smitty, whereas in comparison Ethan was sat arms folded across his chest looking less than impressed with the situation. It had all stemmed from one of Smiitty’s latest schemes to get some more notoriety FUSE, he sought to do this by sending two of his own wrestlers to compete in PRIME’s Jewel In The Crown Tournament, in Smitty’s head it must have seemed like a good idea, beating the competition on their own doorstep, sending a powerful message that FUSE was going to be gunning for PRIME’s title very soon. However his choice in representatives was questionable, well one of them was at least. Joshua Kosidlo was arguably one of the best new signings to FUSE in recent times, he kept his head down and ground out results and was quickly rising through the ranks, making a name for himself. Then of course there was Ethan Frost, the man who would dub himself the misfit of FUSE. “So let me get this straight Smitty, you want to send me and Josh, off to Canada to take part in another companies tournament, whilst at the same time expecting us to meet our obligations here. Have you really thought this through? We’re going to be exhausted and what for? To simply try and get one over on the other guys, I’ll be honest Smitty, I don’t see the point in this, I don’t see the point in this at all!” Ethan Frost had listened to what Smitty had to say, h would always give people that common courtesy, but after processing this information, he simply didn’t agree with it. “Oh come on Ethan, it will be fun, a chance to take a few PRIME scalps along the way, what’s the matter? Scared of a little healthy competition?” Kosidlo joking jabbed Frost in the shoulder, which was not received well in the slightest, Frost notably tensed up, the rage within him building slowly. “I’m not scared of anything, I just think it’s a waste of time. Anyway think about it Joshua, he’s not exactly pulling in the big guns here? He’s not sending off Rhine or Sage. What makes you think we’re even first choice, we’re just the two guys that are stupid enough to agree to this.” Frost retorted giving his own jab to Kosidlo slightly harder than would have been deemed, ‘friendly’ but not hard enough to give any sort of damage. “Josh, could you leave me and Ethan alone for a moment please?” Smitty spoke quietly, his tone notably changed from the excitement that he was exuding earlier, his idea having been shot down by a man who was looking at him with nothing but contempt. I Kosidlo rose from the table and shook Smitty’s hand, there was a knowing nod between the two which Frost simply sneered at, soon it was just Frost and Smitty staring at each other across a desk. The two had never really crossed paths before but Smitty had begun to become disillusioned with everything about Frost. His attitude his mentality even his in ring performances were becoming lack lustre. The only thing with Frost though was that it was hard to broach the subject. He had been going through a significant amount of stress as of late, he was combating through his drug addiction, which for all intensive purposes had now subsided, this had been replaced however by his self-harming, another way to release the pain. So it left Smitty with a conundrum of how to approach Frost, he had given him the common courtesy up until now of trying to give him some time to readjust himself, to get back into the swing of thing, but two months on there had been no change, no positive change at least. Frost had become complacent in his role, he was not pushing himself forwards as much as he was, the hunger had died and it was becoming more and more apparent that Frost was willing to rest on his laurels and collect a pay cheque week in week out. “I know you aren’t happy about this Ethan, but let’s be honest with each other here. You are slipping, you aren’t reaching the levels we both know you can reach. I thought a change of scene might be good for you, a chance for a quick change to reignite the fire. Ethan I remember the man I hired in the beginning of the year, you were bright eyed, ambitious the world was at your feet, but then you had your set backs, you broke you arm, you had your….incident, but that’s behind you now and I’m doing what I feel best for you.” Smitty had decided to play the good cop approach with Frost, although he had been growing in testicular fortitude in the past few months Smitty was as a whole not a confrontational man he liked to talk his way around problems. “Sure I get that Smitty I do, but you’ve said it yourself, I have the world at my feet here in FUSE a few bad results means nothing, I’ll bounce back I always do. I don’t need this tournament to push me on any further, Smitty, if I wanted to be in PRIME, I would be.” The seconds the words came out of Frost’s mouth he regretted it, he knew that it would be misinterpreted and that he would sound like an ungrateful child, it wasn’t what he meant though but before he could even explain himself Smitty had jumped in, his own levels of anger beginning to rise, his self imposed mantra of non confrontation was beginning to be tested. “Do you genuinely believe that Frost? Do you really think you are PRIME material, because I’ll be honest with you here, you walk in with that mindset, that you are better than them. You won’t get past round one. You’ve been put against Vangelus Olsig in the first round, a hall of famer. They say it was a random draw but if you believe that then you’ll believe anything. PRIME want to prove to the world that they are better than us, they see us as a threat, that’s why they’re throwing their big guns at us straight away, can’t you see that? If you can get past Olsig, you have taken that step forwards to being the king of Prime, the jewel in the crown, that’s what it’s all about.” It was through Smitty’s tone, his almost pleading like gestures that Frost was finally beginning to get a bigger picture of all this. This was so much more than just Frost vs. Olsig, this was about PRIME vs. FUSE. It went without saying that in the wrestling world PRIME was considered as the place to be, a number a superstars had passed through their doors and even more had decided to take up residency, but over the last year FUSE had been building momentum. It had started slowly but soon the signings began to filter in and FUSE was positioning itself so that very soon it would be able to strike and take over. The roster was possibly even more powerhouse than PRIME’s now, the likes of Jason O’Neil, Seymour Almasy and Clinton Sage all gracing the roster, all FUSE was lacking now was the x factor, that little something to give them an edge and that extra publicity to thrust them into the position of primary wrestling company in the world. This was Smitty’s dream, this was his drive. He wanted FUSE to be world famous, he wanted the company to be second to none and knew that if it was truly going to happen they would have to branch out. They couldn’t sit content in their own little bubble anymore. “I hear what you’re saying, believe me I do. The only thing is that I don’t want it. Why the hell would I want to be king of PRIME? I’ve told you. If I wanted to be in PRIME I would be. I am FUSE through and through, I have beaten Clinton Sage, a two times Universal Champion as well as Primetime Central United champion, arguably the greatest in the sport at the moment. I have beaten him, you think I’m happy watching him parade around like the cock of the walk? That should be me Smitty! Why the hell would I want to be king of PRIME, when I need to reclaim my kingdom in FUSE? FUSE should be mine, but it’s not and I wake up every single day hating myself for that.” The pain and regret in Frost’s voice was echoed in his eyes, as he allowed himself to briefly skip back to that day he’d pinned Sage, to the day he was touted as the next world champion, before it all slipped away from him and left him in a rut. “Then do this for yourself, you think that success in PRIME would go unnoticed? If you can go to the lengths that I know you can. If you can push your self forwards as much as I’ve seen you do in the past. Most importantly of all though, if you can re find that love you have for the business and win over an alien crowd then you will be taking the steps to claiming your kingdom in FUSE. Nothing would make me happier than to see you with the credit you deserve, to have the fans cheer you again. The truth is that unless you do something spectacular then you aren’t going to win them over again, you said it yourself, fans are fickle, but give them something amazing and they will love you again. If you ask me beating a PRIME Hall Of Famer is pretty amazing. Don’t you think?” Smitty paused for a moment, trying to gauge some sort of reaction from Ethan, who had fallen silent, his mind clearly working a mile a minute trying to assess everything in his head, just trying to make some sort of sense from it all. “I can’t force you to do anything that you don’t want to do Frost, I have learnt that by now, but give it a thought. The flight leaves in ten hours, all you need to do is show up. The crown is yours for the taking Ethan. You just need to find yourself, once you’ve done that, then there is no-one that will be able to stop you. Not in PRIME and not in FUSE, but you need to take that first step.” Smitty placed an understanding hand on Frost’s shoulder and let it linger for a moment, part of Smitty wanted to slap Frost and tell him to snap out of it, but depression didn’t work like that, it would have made him retreat into his shell even more and any progress that had been made would have been lost. Smitty had now left the room, leaving Ethan Frost alone with his thoughts, his mind racing at a million miles a minute, it was an opportunity of a life time that was for sure, but it was his old friend self-doubt that was taking over his persona, to rule his own kingdom of FUSE, he had to dethrone a king of PRIME. It was something that Ethan Frost doubted he could do. “Alright Smitty, you win, but I’m doing this for FUSE. I’m doing this for my kingdom.” It was then there was something happened to Frost that he had not felt in a long time. A twinge of excitement. A glimmer of hope in his eye. The smallest hint of pride and passion had returned. But would it be enough?
