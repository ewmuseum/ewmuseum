Life is just a leap of faith. Spread your arms, hold your breath, and always trust your Cape. "The Cape"

17 November 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/17/09 I’m having a shitty day. The kind of day that just won’t end and despite wishful thinking it only gets worse. It started early this morning when I woke up late and missed a radio interview to promote Prime. Now I’m going to have to hear a lecture on responsibility from a jag off in a suit that’s never had to stand in the center of fifty thousand people and make every one of them hate him. Although after talking with this guy I think he may be able to pull it off. Interviews come and go but breakfast is as sacred as a biblical lamb. For me nothing says good morning like a big bowl of diabetes birthing frosted mini wheat’s. I got my lucky blue bowl out with the picture of the Hamburglar on the side. Why McDonalds killed off the Hamburglar is half as good of a question as why they sold a bowl with his picture on it. I filled the bowl with my morning mistress and prepared to fill her with the satin white sheets of milk. Only my God damn wife used the last of the milk and didn’t tell me. So there I was stuck with the burden of a bowl of dried wheat. I felt like an ass without a hole and the terrible need to go. On my morning jog I was following a young girl with a perfect ass when I tripped on a pothole and skid my knee. I heard her laugh as her half moon faded into the sun rise of the freshly born day. While on the ground a strange bug bit my hand as I was getting up. It stung, like the realization that all I’ll ever be is the caricature of what you think of me. A refreshing realization of an actuality I can’t fully understand. Perhaps I should paint a better picture of myself, choose my words my wisely, and dress to impress. “Fuck it Williams it was never your style”, I mumbled to my own bumbling self as I got up to face the rest of the terrible day ahead. When I returned home my mailbox was full of junk mail the real symbol of modern America. Out of touch, over sold, and wastefully corporate! What a joke. I threw out all the mail right in the dumpster without even flipping through it, why bother on such a boner of a day. I went inside to watch my favorite show Jury Duty only to find it’s been replaced by some show about doctors talking about how to take a better crap every morning. Gee the crap they put on TV these days. With nothing on the boob tube to capture my imagination like real boobs I decided to check out the internet. I checked my mail box only to find it loaded with spam and more junk mail. What can a savior do in high tech age of high tech garbage? I rubbed one out only to find I was jerking it to a tranny site. Yikes, that’s going to be a long conversation with my father who unlike your old man knows ALL and see’s ALL. You think you’re parents are hard to deal with trying having a deity for a daddy. After repenting to God and family while eating a whole tin of Altoids to get the taste out of my mouth I decided to take a nap. Maybe it was depression, maybe I was tired, and maybe it was really the most exciting thing I could think of to do. I laid down. I closed my eyes. I started to drift off to tranquility when out of nowhere a jackhammer started beating the street like Chris Brown on Rihanna, or Bobby Brown on Whitney Houston, or anybody versus the Cleveland Browns. I threw the pillow over my face and wished for silence, or death; both found through heavy drug use. I decided the only thing that could cheer me up was a cold drink of Nesquick Chocolate milk from my local 7-11. I walked to the store still sore from my morning fall when my cell phone rang. It was my grandmother who told me she sent me a Thanksgiving card that should have arrived in the mail today along with some rare pictures of my grandfather. Fuck. I threw out all the mail without looking through it. I turned around headed back for home, only to arrive to watch the efficiency of the city’s public works system as the garbage truck drove away with my family’s history and my chance for Ed McMahons ghost to bring me a million dollars. The idea of rummaging through other peoples rubbish didn’t appeal to me even if it meant losing those photos forever. Sometimes you have to weigh cost versus reward and this was one of those times. That bummed me out worse than a veteran with mental problems and a drinking addiction so I decided to return on my mission for Chocolate milk. I hummed a sad George Jones song but I couldn’t remember the damn tune only adding to my frustrations and general pity. I walked into 7-11 with a classy look only to be followed by the untrusting Indian fellow like I was a thief in the night. I guess trust and acceptance is a two way street not everybody is ready to go down regardless the color of the President, or the cut of cloth on our back. When I got to the cooler I found more disappointment as the only Nesquick milk was strawberry milk. Gross. I asked the Indian about the prospect of a product being stored in the back only to be again let down with a recitation about company policy and procedure. What’s this world coming to? Just then a man walked in wearing a turban with a gun who instructed us all to, “stick it up.” After the mix up earlier with the tranny porn I was sure of one thing, it would be some time before I could get it up so I refused. This caused a commotion and the man hinted at the notion of filling me full of lead. I informed him that lead poisoning or colica pictonium was one of the leading causes of death for children under the age of six throughout the 80’s. As a child of the 80’s I survived this great danger and his threat of lead was not even remotely worrisome. Hell if I could witness the fall of the Berlin wall this camel jockey’s got nothing on me or my overtly racist commentary. I hit the ground with one of the greatest pains I have ever felt in my life. I started itching really bad with a burning sensation shooting throughout my entire body. I started to convulse as the robber ran away in a panic shouting something about Allah, crazy American’s, and 7-11. I tried to catch his height based on the measuring tape by the door but I was shaking like Jerry Lee Lewis playing the piano in rhythm with Satan. “Well I said come over baby we got chicken in the barn oooh... huh.. Come over baby babe we got the bull by the horn-a We ain't fakin' Whole lot of shakin' goin' on” -“The Killer” Jerry Lee Lewis Next thing I knew a Jewish doctor with a large nose was looking into my eyes with a bright light. How long I had been out and how I got to the hospital was as big of a mystery as Sara Palin’s consistent media attention. I asked for some chocolate milk but my mouth was swollen shut and I couldn’t get anything out but some random gibberish. Which most people would describe my writing as. Everybody is a fucking writer or critic these days. A terrible pain over came my body again as I started to shake like Michael J. Fox testifying to congress. Again I vanished from consciousness -- I wish I had a story to tell you. Something colorful yet dreary representing death and the unconsciousness state of existence. A masterpiece defining hibernation from everything that matters, and everything that doesn’t matter, which is most of life but unfortunately, I don’t. I simply went down and woke up again with no remembrance of the time in between. I was laid back in a hospital bed with IV’s and tubes monitoring my so called existence. The doctor came in to tell me that I was no hero and was not shot. Instead some weird bug bite my hand infecting my system with venom that Fraggle Rocked my nervous system sending me into some weird kind of shock that could only be explained by that doctor on that FOX TV show I never watch but always mean to because I like the few times I’ve seen it, ‘House’. The good news is in a few days I will be fine. The bad news is I have days of horrible pain and uncontrollable seizures to look forward to. It’s good to have a purpose and a job in these hard times in the land of plenty. I wish I had more time but this day happened on short notice and I can only count on tomorrow not remembering much of today. Such is life all memories until our hair turns gray, we fade away, and die. Wish me luck boys, I can use it. =w=
