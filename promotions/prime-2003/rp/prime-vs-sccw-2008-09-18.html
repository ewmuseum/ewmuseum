Date: 9/18/08 Prime Vs SCCW The scene opens up in the basement of the Boss Man house, other known as the dungeon. We see the basement is a little filthy with trash on the floor, and empty pizza boxes. A couch in the middle of the room and a flat screen TV. Next to the couch is a black lazy boy. We see Bonafide and G. sitting down on the couch with stacks of money layed out on the wooden table in front of them. All of a sudden footsteps are heard coming down the steps and incomes the boss man. Boss man was very heavy set man with a bald head. A thich mustache and goatee that connects and he wears black sunglasses, so you cannot look into his cold eyes. Some say he looked like suge knight. He sits down on the lazy boy that next to the couch, and looks at all the money that was layed out in front of them. He looks at G. and Bonafide and begins to speak. “I am very proud of you guy. You guys didn’t fold under pressure and came through when you guy’s needed t.” He says, while he gives G. and Bonafide there cut. “Thanks man.” G. say’s with a smile on his face. “Yeah, well good work guys. And you Bonafide I remember you telling me that you wanted out. Do you still want out of this game?” Boss man asks. G. and Boss man looks at Bonafide, waiting for him to answer the question. “You know what, I think I am done. I can’t do this anymore, I lost my cousin to this game and I don’t plan on losing my life for this game. I have a beautiful family and two kids that love me very much, and I can’t disappoint them.” “Well okay, your wish is my command. Go back home and be a family man, and take care of what you need to take care of back home.” Boss man says. “So just like that I’m out?” “Yes, you were one of my best man’s. You know that if you ever want to come back your are more than welcome.” Boss man tell’s Bonafide. “Thank you.” Jimmy say’s. “But I have one question for you now, how do you plan on making money?” “Well I plan on focusing more on my wrestling career and my music.” Boss man looks at G. and they begin to laugh at what Bonafide just said. “Really, why is it that every Hustler wants’ too be a rapper, and every rapper wants to be a hustler? What is this damn world coming too?” “Beat’s me. I just now I want out of the game.” “Well as I said you are free to go, and if your music thing don’t work out you can always come back home and get money with us. The big boys!” Boss man say’s with a smile on his face. “Yeah, man us going to miss you.” G. say’s to Bonafide. “So tell me guy’s what went wrong, when you guys went to get my money?” Boss man asks. [Flashback] “So let’s get down to business.” G. say’s “Yes, about that. I don’t have it all in full but all I have on me is $30.000.” “What do you mean you don’t have it all, you owe the boss man $60.000 I don’t think he is going to be happy if I go back to him missing his other $30.000.” “We can probably, work something out guys.” “It’s not us; you have to work it out with its boss man.” “Maybe you guys can hold him off for me, just a little while?” “No, you just don’t get it. If we don’t get that money, its then our ass on the line, and I’m sure my friend here doesn’t want that to happen.” He says as he points to the silent Poster Boy sitting down motionless. “So what’s going to happen?” The bald man asks. “So what’s going to happen, what’s going to happen is.” Before he can finish his sentence the bald man pulls out a desert eagle and puts it in G’s face. “Hey! What’s the gun for?” Bonafide asks “We got no beef.” “Shut up, you are in no position to talk.” The stocky man says. “You just stay put and don’ move quiet man..” He tells Bonafide. “You! Get your ass up from m couch.” He yells out at G while he points the gun at his forehead. “So you were going to kill me?” He say’s while h pulls up G’s shirt revealing the glock 9. He takes the glock 9, from G and begins to speak. Now get your ass on your knees.” The bald stocky man tells G. “What, I aint getting down on my fucking knees. Fuck you!” G. screams out. The man just looks at G and swings the pistol, caching G on the side of his head. Knocking him down on the floor. G is now staring down the barrel of a glock 9 holding his head, trying to stop the blood from the cut over his eye. Due to the devasting blow from the pistol. “You see I told you, to get on your knees. Now you are bleeding all over my carpet.” “Hey aint no need for all that.” Jimmy tells the man. “Shut the fuck up you mute.” The bald stocky man yells out. “Just keep the money and let us walk out of, here with our lives.” Bonafide explains to the man. “Just be quiet and let me think for a minute.” [/Flashback] “To make a long story short, someone had knocked on the door. I guess it was one of his boys, But when he turned around to open the door, I quickly ran up on him with the glass case and cracked him with it, right over his head. Then we seen an opportunity took all his money and left.” Bonafide say’s quickly cutting of G. and making the story a lot shorter. “You see what, I mean. I hate 2 see you leave us, but handle what you need to handle and we will always be here for you. We started this all together with your cousin and AR. Rest in peace to them.” Boss man explains. “Well let’s celebrate one last time.” He says. Boss man get’s up from the couch and walks up to a little cooler that he has in the corner of the room, He opens it up and pulls out 3 40’s for G. Bonafide and himself. He sits back down on his recliner gives each man there beer, they open the beers and cheers to each other for a well job done and Bonafide last job. Boss man looks at his watch and notices the time, he looks at G and Bonafide and speaks. “Hey you guys don’t have anywhere to go now?” “No, we don’t.” “Nah, I don’t have anything to do.” Bonafide says. “Good so join me and watch the football game.” Boss man says. “Sure.” They both say. “Who wants’ to bet on the game?” Boss man say’s -Scene ends.
