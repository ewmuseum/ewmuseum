The Press Conference

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today This is not all Chet Worth hoped it would be. Winning the Dual Halo was a double sided sword. The normally reserved, quiet Worth was now thrust into the spotlight, and had been forced to do a lot more media and publicity for PRIME. At first the questions were fine. Being in Japan, the members of the media were respectful of him, and treated him as if he were an athlete. Things changed when he returned home. Paparazzi followed his every move. Fans called his hotel room. Reporters called him at all hours of the day. People wanted to throw money at him, simply so he would say their product's name on Revolution's premiere episode on FX. At a press conference for Revolution, Worth was scheduled to meet with members of the media alone. Usually, Motoki, Nelson or someone within PRIME was at his side. This time, he was alone. PRIME PR Guy: At this time, we'll have ten minutes for questions and answers with Dual Halo Champion Chet Worth. Worth will be taking on Damien Cruz on Revolution's premiere on FX this week, and has revealed that he will tell the world his wish for the “Golden Ticket”, so to speak. Reporter 1: Chet, Bill Diamond from Pro Wrestling Monthly. First off, congrats on the Dual Halo win. Chet Worth: Thank you. Diamond: How do you wrestle with OCD? Normally, people with OCD are afraid to touch other people. Are you germophobic? CW: Well, it's difficult, honestly. As you've seen, if things don't go the way I want them to, I tend to lose my head quickly. I like doing things in certain ways, and if I can't do that, I usually lose the match. It's hard to bear. On one hand, I want to give my opponent the best match I can. But, on the other hand, this isn't the WWE. We're not sitting backstage for hours going over matches. Sure, we talk about it a bit, but, for the most part, it's every man for himself out there. But, no, I'm not germophobic. If I were, I'd be in the wrong business. Diamond: What about your personal life, Chet? Is there a Mrs. Worth? CW: My personal life has no bearing on my wrestling career. Diamond: Why? CW: Next question. Diamond: Come on. It's fair to ask. These people just want to be your fans, Chet. CW: [slightly agitated] Next question. Simon Uchtect: Heya, Chet. Simon Uchtect, National Busybody here. Is there any truth to the rumor that last night you were partying with Danny Ferguson in Malibu? CW: No. I haven't talked to Ferguson since before the Halo. I'm pretty sure that our personal lives aren't going to cross for a while. SU: Why's that? CW: Would you talk to someone you paid a bunch of money to make sure you win a match, only to have him go on and win it instead? Simon Uchtect: We have pictures we're ready to release of you and Hilary Duff -- CW: Feel free to do so. But, they're not me. Guaranteed. I was in bed last night at 9:47PM. Call the Hotel. SU: What about the rumors.... CW: They're just that. Rumors. Seriously. Leave it alone, Simon. I'm not going to answer any personal questions. This is about wrestling, FX, and PRIME. Ted Childress: Chet, Ted Childress here from Fox News. Are you a republican? CW: Next question. Ted Childress: Well, it's important for people to know where you stand politically. Every other celebrity does it. CW: I'm a wrestler, not a celebrity. Next question. Ted Childress: Are you a communist? CW: Doubtful. Ted Childress: Nazi? CW: Can we have him removed? Ted Childress: Any truth to the rumor you helped Hitler and Osama bin Laden plan 9/11? CW: ... Ted Childress: I have pictures. Just give me a few hours to get them to you. CW: Riiiight. Next? Jeanie Moos: Chet, Jeanie Moos, CNN. Can you tell me what shape your underwear is in when you ball it up and throw it on the floor? CW: Excuse me? JM: I do human interest pieces, and this is what I'm doing for this week's show. CW: Do you get paid for that? JM: Sure do. CW: Someone's paying you way too much money. Next? Frank Fitzwater: Chet, Frank Fitzwater with The Weekly Wrestler. How do you see your match with Cruz playing out on Revolution this week? CW: Finally. A real question. You know, Cruz is a tremendous talent, and he poses a serious threat to me. I don't do well against speedy wrestlers, and he's probably as quick as Karina Wolfenden. We all know I lost to her a month or so ago, and it's still in the back of my mind what I did wrong. I'm going to have to take his legs out from under him, and keep him on the mat. Otherwise, I'm probably going to lose. FF: Any idea what you're asking PRIME management for? CW: I do. I've already confirmed it with Mister Motoki, and I'll announce that on Revolution this week. FF: Any hints? CW: It's not a title match. No one wants me as a champ. Trust me. FF: What do you make of the rumors that you and some other members of PRIME have formed a bit of a clique and have basically forced management to bow to your wills? CW: What? I've never heard anything of that. FF: It's all over the internet. CW: Right. And everything from the internet's true right? FF: Well, people seem to think that your winning the Dual Halo has a lot to do with your friend Peter Maxwell's friendship with people like Xavier Kannon and Angelo Deville, and less to do with any real skill you possess. CW: What? I've been wrestling for ten years. I've got plenty of... FF: Well, the rumors persist... CW: Let them. They won't get in the way of the job I have to do. FF: Which is? CW: [very agitated] Are you stupid? FF: It's just a question... CW: Well, it's a stupid fucking question, Frank. Stupid. Fucking. Question. And fuck you for asking it. FF: ...well. CW: Yeah. Speechless, huh? I'm going to save you all a bunch of time here. Get out your fucking pens and paper, and write this down: I am a wrestler. This is what I do. I go out to the ring, week in, week out, and I kick people's asses. I try my best to wrestle them to the ground. I try my best to entertain the fans that want to take two hours of their time off, and watch our show. What I do when I'm not on camera is my time. It's not Chet Worth, it's Chester Holecko, walking the streets, taking care of my disability, and doing what I can to make life better for those around me. I'm nothing more than a character that you see on television once a week, and that's all I'm ever going to be. If you want to talk to me about Chet Worth, feel free to do so. Chet Worth will tell you what ever is on his mind. But, once you cross over into things that have nothing to do with Chet Worth, I'm not going to answer it. People don't need to know about my private life. FF: That's not fair. CW: Then deal with it. And with that, and four minutes to go in the interview, Chet Worth simply stood, took a look around the room and walked out the door, flashbulbs flashing as he walked out in a huff. The Internet was abuzz with deluded tales of what happened in the press conference, each one painting Worth in a worse light than the last. But, it was true. Just because he had a bit of fame now doesn't mean he had to compromise himself and others. Yes, I ran out of time. Sorry, Cruz.
