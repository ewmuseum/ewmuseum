Uninvited

26 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/03/08 I You can scour the world for your own little corner of paradise. You can take mere bricks and mortar, and using the alchemy of domestic bliss, sculpt them into your dream home. You can awake to a chorus of songbirds, even on Christmas day, and you can be soothed to sleep by the caress of the ocean along the beech. But unless you can control the borders, frankly, you’re fucked. They’ll let anyone in. “Um,” Ellie alerts. “I think there’s some handicapped guy collecting for charity… he’s walking all funny.” Checking the footage from the security cameras, an approaching, presumably uninvited guests busts out what can only be described as an ‘arm-flapping power-walk’. “I think he has rickets,” she adds. II “I’ll get this,” a wary Xavier states, striding past his wife as the doorbell chimes. And chimes again, impatiently. And again. “Xavie-” the man at the door goes to greet, before… HISSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS! Claws digging into the floorboards, Eleanor’s fluffy white cat gnashes its teeth, emitting a seething hissing. His pupils a narrow slash of black, ‘Mr Jones’ starts to turn into a snowy-white puffball. Stood in the doorway, Son-MR SILVER, CHAIRMAN ON PRIME-ny Silver looks as if he’s used to such a reaction. “Why are you here?” Kannon asks with a mix of mistrust and… more mistrust. We should have got the electrified fencing is what he thinks. “Why am I here? Why ammmmm I heeeere?” Silver mulls over, taking on an Agent Smith tone. “Well, that’s some fucking gratitude, huh? I wanted to welcome you back to PRIME so badly that I PARACHUTED down from the PRIME Corporate Jet! Every time we tried to set her down somewhere solid, locals kept getting in the way or fleeing for their lives… and trust me, they’re a bitch to get cleaned out of the engines. Can you believe you have to fill in TWO separate forms if that happens? So much for saving the fucking rainforests. Sting should write a fucking hippie song about that injusti-” “Someone needs to, like, impose a word limit on his ass,” Ellie calls from the hallway, trying to pry Mr Jones out of the floorboards. “If you let him in, will he shut up?” “Probably not…” Kannon pre-empts Sonny’s reply, regretfully. “Oh well,” Ellie chirps, dismissively. “If we let him in, we can claim breaking-and-entering if anything, like, you know… happens to him. It’s win-win… win. Win.” “I like how she thin-” “Don’t like anything about her,” sternly warns Kannon, pushing the door shut behind Silver. III “Let’s hear it,” Kannon invites. Sat on the most uncomfortable chair that Eleanor could find, Sonny Silver faces the council of Kannon - Xavier, Eleanor and Isabelle. Thankfully, for his face, the feline member of the clan has not been invited. “I come before you, a humble man,” Sonny begins, hamming it up so strongly that you may as well stick an apple in his mouth. “I may not ha-” He is broken from his amateur-dramatic routine by a trio of icy stares, almost frosting up his greying hair. “Okay, not buying that than,” he mutters. “There’s more chance of me buying Uggs,” Ellie informs him, although it’s not much help to Sonny. “And it’s not 2006 anymore.” “She means ‘no’,” Isabelle translates. “I knew that,” Silver snaps, sounding wounded. “I’ll skip to the point, since your wife so kindly put me on a word limit. You’ve just come back to the wrestling world where the fans still treat you as a hero. Hell, you were all ‘Snow and me are buddies’ and then Deville was all ‘he’s behind you!’ and you were all… well, you weren’t anything, because Snow kicked your face in with a Superkick and you got beaten shitless. I have it on my YouTube favourites. Anyway, my point is, even after Deville made you what he’d have called in the slammer ‘his biyatch’, the fans still worshipped you like a returning hero.” “You really have a way with words,” Isabelle remarks. “Not a good way in any sense, but a way.” Leaning forward in his chair, Silver looks right into Kannon’s curious… and rather peeved… eyes. “But every true hero needs a villain, a nemesis,” Sonny declares. “And I can provide him for you… in a way that we both benefit.” “I will deny all knowledge of every having said these two words to you in any scenario, but…” Kannon can’t help himself from uttering. “Go on.” “In one corner, you have the returning hero, the scrapper, Mr Neversayturnmylifesupportoff, the King of Wrestling… Xavier Kannon,” Silver’s voice almost shakes the walls in announcing, putting Vince Howard to shame. “In the other, the muscle-bound Future of Sports Entertainment… my very own Captain Justice. Imagine it… your return feud being against an unstoppable mass of righteous muscle, my new protégé’s first rivalry being against a man who has almost ascended to wrestling Godhood? And, this is the clincher… you’ll win.” “Wow,” Ellie sarcastically exclaims. “You really have this management thing nailed down, don’t you?!” “I nail things on levels you can’t even comprehend,” Silver smirks. “Captain Justice will be a true hero, and every true hero needs their setbacks. You’ll triumph in your first feud, he’ll suffer a loss that will only temper the steel within him… and orchestrating it all, the two greatest minds in PRIME… the left side of my brain, and the right side of my brain… and you.” “There’s a part of me that should really be disgusted at every word you just said,” Kannon spits Silver’s way, before breaking into a chuckle. “Notice I said ‘should’. Count me in.” “Buuuuuuut there’s an extra price…” Silver chips back in with, looking across to Isabelle. “You have to make him a costume that doesn’t make him look like he belongs in a Daft Punk video.” “Can I give him Wolverine claws?” takes Iz all of a millisecond to fire back. “Better not…” Silver declines, sucking air in through his teeth. “He’s a big guy… and if it chafes anything like the current one, he’ll do a Lorena Bobbitt on himself before you two get to face off.” IV As Silver heads back down the winding path, the trio of Kannon’s can’t help but watch his theatrical arm-flapping power-walk from the window. “He’s going to screw us,” Kannon nonchalantly suggests. “Isn’t he?” “Somewhere uncomfortable,” Ellie concurs. “Sideways,” is then contributed by Isabelle. “I’m giving the costume Wolverine claws.”
