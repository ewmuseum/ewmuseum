One Last Happy Moment

4 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 4th, 2007 One Last Happy Moment Sunday, December 3rd A peaceful, almost serene morning. For two weeks, that's what Craig's been able to look forward to as he lies in his bed, propped up on his elbow while watching the woman he's crazy about sleep. For the first time in a long while, he can finally feel like he's living a normal life. It's almost foreign to him and he feels like it's almost too good to be true. Yet, in this moment in time, he loves every second of it. As he lies there, Nicole begins to open her eyes, and looks up at Craig with a smile on her face. Nicole: You're watching me sleep? Craig: Yeah. Nicole: I hear that in some cultures they consider that stalking. Craig: Do they? Nicole: I think I heard that on like Animal Planet one time. Craig: Animal Planet? Nicole: Or maybe Law and Order. Craig: Alrighty then. I'm going to have to kick you out of my bed now. Nicole: Because I don't remember where it came from? Craig: Nope. For the Law and Order. Nicole: Are you serious? You're joking! Craig: Was it old school Law and Order, or new school? Nicole: What's new school? Craig: No Orbach. Nicole: Oh... Craig: Yeah, get out. Nicole: You're so mean! Would you kick someone this beautiful out of your bed? Craig: Look at you thinking you're the most beautiful woman to ever be in my bed. Nicole: Oh, you're going to pay for that. Craig: Whatever. Craig slowly leans in for a kiss. Her teeth run softly over his bottom lip and send a tingle up his spine as he gazes deeply into her eyes. There's a feeling of content there, one that Nicole finds comfort in. After a moment, Craig rolls out of the bed, and puts on a pair of shorts before walking into the bathroom. He looks at himself in the mirror and just shakes his head. Nicole: What have you got planned today? Craig: Buying every season of Law and Order with Jerry Orbach in it. Nicole: No, seriously. Craig: Got some errands to run. You know, the fun stuff. Nicole: Because that sounds like so much fun. Craig: Look, not everyone can be the manager of a restaurant that's only open for like six hours. Nicole: Seven. Craig: Whatever. Nicole: I'm going to need you to know the hours of your restaurant. Craig: I'll get right on that. Nicole: Thanks. Nicole gets out of bed too and grabs her robe that's on the floor next to the bed. She quickly puts it on and walks into the bathroom. As she does, Craig looks at her in the mirror and she just pauses there, happy with just him looking at her that way. Craig: So, what do you have planned? Nicole: Amazing sex with this guy I'm crazy about. Craig: Cool, what's his name? Nicole: Raul. Craig turns around and looks at her with a cocked eye, obviously not expecting that response. She just cracks a smile at him as he begins to get a little jealous even though she is obviously joking. Nicole: What? I like them Latino. Craig: You're Latino. Nicole: Am I? I had forgotten. Craig: Well, you're like half. Nicole: I know, it doesn't show very well, well, except in the ass. Damn sun never getting through the clouds here. Craig: Clouds? Nicole: Smog, clouds, same thing. Craig: Sure, I'm sure nobody will agree with you. Nicole: Someone might. Craig: Paris Hilton doesn't count. Nicole: Now if you're just going to exclude people, then that's just not fair. Craig: Well, okay, if you insist. Craig walks out of the bathroom and opens a drawer where he pulls out a black sweater that he quickly pulls over his head. The fabric clings to his chest as he tries to stretch it out a little bit, but to no avail. He rolls his eyes in the mirror before him. Nicole: Did I tell you who stopped into the restaurant the other day? Craig: Denzel? Nicole: No--, wait, seriously? Denzel? That's who you come up with it? Craig: I just have to ask him why he only gets Oscars when he plays a bad guy. Nicole: This man is one of the best actors in our time and you want to ask him that? Out of anything you can ask him, you ask him a racial question? Craig: Don't be jealous that I stole your question. Nicole: I'll try. Craig: Admitting it is the first step. Craig opens up another drawer and pulls out a pair of jeans and slips them on while watching Nicole as she stands in the threshold of the door. Nicole: So, moving on. Guess who I saw? Craig: Who? Nicole: That basketball player that you talk about so much. Craig: Which one? Nicole: You know the tall one. Craig: The tall one? Nicole: You know, he's kind of tall, has muscles. Craig: Was he black? Nicole: Yeah! Craig: And between 5'9 and 7'4? Nicole: I'm pretty sure. Craig: You know that I just described every player in the NBA, right? Nicole: Yeah, I started catching on when you started mentioning the height differences. Craig: Yeah, I believe you. Nicole: Fine then, don't believe me. But, you know who I'm talking about? Craig: Can you give me a team? Nicole: Probably not. Craig: Then no, I don't know who you're talking about. Nicole: Then you suck. Craig: Because the most you can remember is that he plays basketball? Nicole: You've gotta learn and keep up with me. Craig: Oh, I'll make sure to do that. Nicole: Good. She walks out of the bathroom, looking rather seductive as she wraps her arms around her neck. She kisses him softly on the lips and returns the favor. Nicole: Don't forget about dinner tonight. Craig: I wouldn't forget for the world. Nicole: Dave is bringing the food around 8 I guess. Craig: Sounds good to me. Nicole: And you're going to make sure the house is empty? Craig: The house has like twelve bedrooms. For all I know, Elvis and Michael are sharing a room down the hall. Nicole: I thought I heard someone snorting cocaine while singing really high. Craig: Oh, that was Dave. Last Thursday, right? Nicole: Yeah! Craig: Yeah, that was Dave. Nicole: Well... that was odd. Craig: I thought so too. She begins to walk away, but stops in her tracks and looks back at Craig. Nicole: You think Katie is here? Craig: Pretty sure she is. Nicole: Damnit. Craig: Don't worry; I'll drag her out of here so she won't see you. Nicole: Thanks. Craig: No problem. See you later on tonight. Nicole: Bye. Craig walks out of the room and down a flight of stairs. Inside of the robust kitchen sits Katie, carefully picking apart a bagel while reading a magazine. As Craig steps inside of the kitchen, Katie looks up at him and smiles brightly. Katie: Morning. Craig: Hey. Katie: Did you know that Brad Pitt is going to rebuild New Orleans? Craig: With his bare hands? Katie: Something like that. Craig: Because, that'd be kind of cool. Not only is the guy in Fight Club, but he can also rebuild an entire city. That kind of stuff makes you immortal. Katie: I'm sure it doesn't. Craig: Yeah, probably not. Katie: So, what're you up to this morning? Craig: I have to go down to the doctor and get a quick physical. Katie: Sounds rather exhilarating. Craig: Please, try not to sound so excited. Katie: I plan on doing my best. Craig: Good. You feel like riding out? Katie: With you? Craig: That was the idea. She looks at him, almost pleading with her eyes not to make her go. Katie quickly takes another bite of her bagel, refusing to move her eyes at all. Katie: Do I have to? Craig: I let you live here for free, right? Katie: Yeah. Craig: Then that's your answer. Katie: Fine. Let's go. Craig: That was fast. Katie: I really would like to not sit all day at the doctor's office. Craig: Young and the Restless doesn't start until 12:30. Katie: Yeah, but what about the pregame?! Craig: Are you serious? Katie: ...no? Craig: Okay, good. Katie begrudgingly takes another bite of her bagel before grabbing the paper towel and throwing it into the trash. She wipes the crumbs into the sink and begins to walk towards the front door, not a single pep in her step. Craig follows behind her and opens the door before stepping outside. Katie closes the door behind him and begins to walk towards the car, but Craig doesn't move. Something doesn't feel right to him as he looks around, certain that it's in plain view. Katie: Everything okay? Craig: No. Katie: What is it? Craig: I'm not sure. Katie: Okay, that makes sense. A few more moments pass by before Craig moves towards the car, still certain that someone is watching him, but there's no one in sight. Craig starts the car and pulls out of the driveway, still scanning the area for something out of place. It's a couple of minutes before Craig resumes his conversation with Katie. Craig: So, how's life? Katie: Seriously? Craig: What? Katie: You want to get in the car and ask me how life is? Craig: Is there something wrong with life? Katie: You never ask me how life is. Craig: I don't? Katie: No. Craig: Well, I'm trying to start a new tradition then. Katie: Really? Craig: No, not really. I just never realized that I never said that to you before. Katie: That's sad. Craig: No, what's sad is that you actually remember what I ask you? Katie: Well, you know-- so, physical? For what? Craig: Look at you trying to change the subject. Katie: And now you're trying to change it! Craig: Sneaky. The physical is standard in my line of work. I have to get one every now and then just to make sure that I'm still fit and not ready to melt. Katie: That melting thing would be nice. Craig: You want me to melt? Katie: No, I prefer you not to. Craig: Well, that's sweet. Katie: Thanks. Craig: So seriously, life? Katie: Nothing interesting. You know me, I lead a boring life. Craig: You're telling me. Katie: Look here Mister Superstar, not all of us can fly around on jets and compete in tens of thousands of people on a weekly basis. Craig: You consider that interesting? Katie: It's something to do. Craig: You need something to do, is that what's wrong? Katie: I guess. You know, I just don't feel like I have a purpose anymore. Craig: It's not easy, being like the rest of us. I understand that. Katie: Yeah, so I try to find a place in the world, and I guess I'm fitting in alright and everything, but I still can't find my place. How am I supposed to know what to do in life? I don't have a college degree or anything like that. Craig: Do you want one? Katie: I don't know, I guess. Craig: What would you want to study? Biology? Katie: I really like Psychology. Craig: Really? Katie: Yeah, I just like figuring people out. I've been trying to figure you out. Craig: That's never a good idea. Katie: I know. Craig: What did you learn about me? Katie: Well, you're very private and secretive. Craig: This sounds like the beginning of that unauthorized biography that came out a few months ago. Katie: Quiet, let me finish. You're very sarcastic and that keeps you from having to be honest with people. You make jokes so people won't see the cracks that lie underneath the surface. Deep down, you're torn and I don't know why. You feel like you haven't paid for your sins and you keep trying to right them. Most importantly, you hide so much that you don't even know what's real anymore. He remains silent as he continues to drive before pulling into a parking lot. He slowly pulls into a parking space and leaves the car running. Craig looks out the window, silence filling the car, as Katie looks at him and feels some kind of regret. Katie: Sorry. Craig: Don't be. Katie: I didn't mean to-- Craig: No, don't apologize. You're right; I hide myself from those around me. It's how I am. You've seen what I'm capable more than anybody, what I've done. You know it because you were part of it. I'm scared of those secrets coming out, especially now when I finally have my world and life together. Katie: I understand. Craig: Yeah. Katie: Look, I'll stay here with the car and wait for you. Craig: Yeah. Craig gets out of the car and walks towards the doctor's office. A few moments after he gets out of the car though, a phone begins ringing inside of his car. Katie sits there, looking at the cell phone, and quickly realizes that it's Craig phone. She looks up to see if he's still around, but he's already gone. She looks at the caller ID screen and instantly realizes the name on the screen. Without hesitation, she flips the phone open, and puts it to her ear. Katie: Hey Dave! Dave: Katie? Katie: Yeah, I answered Craig's cell phone while he's in the doctor's office. Dave: Oh, word. How you been? Katie: I'm alright, you know, as well as I can be while sitting in the car. Dave: Yeah, sounds like a world of fun. Katie: You know it. How about you? Dave: Business has been booming at the restaurant so I'm normally asleep late and wake up early. One fucked up schedule if you ask me, but I wouldn't rather be doing anything else. Katie: Well, that's good. Dave: Yeah, it is. Katie: Did you want me to take a message? Dave: Yeah, tell Craig that I should have the food at his place around 8 tonight. Katie: Food? Dave: Yeah. I guess for some kind of dinner? Katie: Dinner? I don't know anything about any dinner. Dave: Well, that's just odd. Katie: Most definitely. Dave: I wonder who he's entertaining. Katie: Who knows? Dave: Hey... I've got an idea. Katie: On whom it is? Dave: Nah. Why don't we crash it? Katie: Crash it? Dave: Yeah, you and me. When I come to bring the food, we can just stay and eat with him and whoever else he's got over. Katie: Isn't that kind of rude? Dave: Yeah, but that makes life that much more interesting. Katie: Well, I'm not doing anything else, and I can't imagine Craig having dinner with anyone else. Dave: Point. Katie: You said you're bringing the food around 8? Dave: Yep. Katie: Then I'll see you then. Dave: It's a date. Katie pauses at hearing that word and expects to feel weird about hearing it put that way, but she isn't. Instead, she almost welcomes the idea. Katie: Yeah, it is. Dave: See you then. Katie slowly hangs up the phone as she sits back in the chair and thinks about the evening ahead of her. Who had Craig been planning dinner with? And why did Dave call it a date? Inside of the doctor's office though, things aren't sounding that cheery for Craig. Doctor: So, tell me more about these headaches? Craig: Just mind-numbing for the most part. They come completely out of nowhere and just leave without warning. Doctor: How long have they been going on for? Craig: Three months? Doctor: And you're just now seeing a doctor? Craig: I'm on the road a lot for work, you know? I just thought it was exhaustion or some injury, but they're getting worse. Doctor: How bad? Craig: I lost all control of my body and smashed my head against a wooden bench. Doctor: That explains the stitches on your forehead. Craig: Yeah. Doctor: What happened afterwards? Craig: Couldn't move? Doctor: From nausea? The pain? Craig: I felt numb. Paralyzed almost. Doctor: Paralyzed? In the arms? Legs? Craig: Everywhere. I tried to speak, but I couldn't move my lips. Doctor: That's rather odd. Craig: That's what I thought. Doctor: Have you experienced anything like that before? Craig: No. Doctor: How about since? Craig: No. Doctor: Have you been throwing up? Heat flashes? Anything else out of the ordinary? Craig: Just feeling exhausted. Sometimes my joints hurt a little more than usual, but I just chalk that up to the constant strain I put on my body. Doctor: Of course. Craig: But, this is just getting really bad now, I think. Doctor: I'd agree. Look, let's take some blood, and run some basic test. Start ruling some things out and see if anything abnormal shows up. From there, we can run some more tests, but it could just be a mixture of stress and exhaustion. Craig: I hope so. Doctor: Yeah, I understand. Go ahead and stick out your arm. I'm going to get a tube and take some blood. Craig: Yeah. The doctor stands up and walks over to one of the cabinets. He quickly pulls out a syringe and a small tube for his blood. He walks back to Craig and sits down before cleaning the spot, and finding a good vein to draw the blood from. As Craig feels the prick of the needle against his skin, he just wishes that he could finally get rid of the headaches. ~~~~~~ Later That Evening A quiet evening. It's all Craig wants as he steps out of his bedroom after hearing the doorbell ring. As he walks down the hall and down the stairs, he has this weird feeling that he won't get what he wants tonight. He opens the door and sees Dave standing outside with bags of food. Dave: Hola. Craig: I don't remember you being Spanish. Dave: I'm also not a delivery man. Craig: Touché. Dave: I've got everything you asked though. Lasagna, bread, salad. Desert is in this other bag and I've got some drinks for you tonight. Craig: Thanks Dave for going through all of this for me. Dave: Oh, it's no problem. Craig: Here, let me help you with all of that. Craig decides to help Dave and grabs two of the bags and Dave follows him inside where they put the bags of food onto the counter. Dave starts unloading some of it as Craig takes a good look at all of the food and just smiles. He glances quickly at the clock and notices that it's getting close to the time that Nicole should be here. Anticipation and anxiety starts to overtake him as he needs to get Dave out of there. Dave: Well, I think that's everything. Craig: Thanks so much Dave, again. Let me walk you out. "Wow, look at all of that food!" Craig looks up the stairs and sees Katie standing there, with a slight grin on his face. Craig: I thought you were out for the night. Katie: Yeah, I lied. Craig takes a look at Dave and his eyes narrow rather quickly. Craig: You told her. Dave: On accident. I thought the dinner was for you two since Nicole had an engagement tonight. Katie: And I know nothing of a dinner, so I figured why not stick around, and find out who you're having dinner for. Craig: Oh, you guys are making too much out of this. The doorbell rings and Craig knows who's standing outside of that door. As he pulls it open, he sees Nicole standing there with a smile on her face, but it quickly vanishes when she sees Katie walking down the stairs and Dave still standing there. Nicole: Okay... Katie: You're having dinner with Nicole? Craig: Yes. Craig quickly moves out of the way as Nicole walks in a little flabbergasted by all of this. Nicole: It was a work meeting, for us to discuss the restaurant. We do this monthly actually. Katie: Oh. Dave: Oh. Craig: Yeah. He looks at Nicole and just smiles at her for covering so fast. Nicole: Look, we've got more than enough food for all four of us. Why don't we sit down and enjoy it? We can talk about the restaurant and everything. That sound good? Katie: Yeah, that sounds wonderful. Dave: Well, I don't want to intrude. Craig: No, it's all good Dave. Come and sit down. Dave: Well, I am the assistant manager after all. Craig: Good point. They begin to move towards the dining room and Craig follows behind them. Yet, as he reaches the dining room, the doorbell rings again and Craig stops dead in his tracks. Craig: Hold on, let me get that. Katie: Were you expecting someone else? Craig: Not really. Craig turns around and walks back up to the door. He opens it and sees a face he didn't expect. Craig: Daniel... Craig looks down and sees a gun pointed at him. Craig takes a few steps backwards as he's not prepared for this in the least bit. Daniel: Craig... The look in Daniel's eyes is cold. His hair is dirty and the facial hair on his face has clearly not been shaved in weeks. Daniel: I hope you don't mind if I come in, do you? Craig: What're you doing here? Daniel: I saw food. Katie: Craig, who is it? Katie, wondering what Craig's doing, steps out from the dining room and lets out a loud gasp when she sees Daniel there. Katie: What are you doing here?! Daniel doesn't waste a second as he points the gun at Katie. Daniel: Into the dining room. How about we join them, Craig? Katie quickly walks back into the dining room, scared. Craig follows here, all happiness taken away from him. As he walks back into the dining room, Nicole and Dave look up to see Daniel following him with a gun in his hand. Nicole quickly tightens up as she begins to walk forward frightened. Daniel: Stop moving. Nicole: Who are you? Daniel: The name's Daniel. Dave: What the hell are you doing here? Daniel: Now that's a funny story. Daniel then points the gun right in Craig's face. Daniel: Isn't it? A story that Craig had never intended upon telling. He looks at Nicole and can feel everything crashing down around him. Possibly for the last time. To Be Concluded
