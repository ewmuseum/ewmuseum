Smokin' Joe (ReV 99, vs. Stanislav/Sandusky)

29 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/29/06 Senator Joseph Lieberman’s eyes open slowly, squinting at the daylight peering through the window and illuminating the bedspread. His stomach turns. “Unnngh…” He feels something he hasn’t experienced in many moons…the onset of a hangover. This befuddles the Democratic Dynamo because he can’t remember having had so much as a glass of wine the night before. His chest feels heavy, as well…like he smoked a pack of cigarettes. But Joe Lieberman doesn’t smoke. Cigarettes give you cancer, and the companies that sell them are murderers. Pondering these peculiarities, Joe rolls over to look at his wife. It’s always so enjoyable when he can stare into Hadassah’s face and almost will her to wake up, and he is the first thing she… ...Joe’s pleasant train of thought plummets off the metaphorical cliff-side as his eyes struggle to take in the massive form of the large black woman lying next to him. The large black woman who is staring into his face, almost as though she could will him to wake up, and she is the first thing he sees. Joe’s lips tremble for a moment, his eyes gone wide in shock, as the sound wells up in his throat. “AAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH-AHHHHHHHHHH-AHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!! AHHHHHHH!!! AHHHHHH!!!” The woman is unfazed by this, and reacts with surprising quickness in jamming a swollen, sausage-like finger into Joe’s mouth, silencing him. Joe’s eyes follow the finger incredulously, as though he can’t believe it’s protruding from his important mouth. It tastes like ass. “Shush, Novie,” the woman purrs, her voice shades of James Earl Jones, “[detail withheld]” She gives a laugh and rolls over gleefully in the bed, like a hippopotamus frolicking in the Congo River. To avoid certain death by way of suffocation/flattening, Liebsy leaps from the bed and backs up against the wall. Looking around, he notices that this is not his New Haven bedroom. Posters of Tupac Shakur and Dr. Dre cover the walls. The Cheech & Chong wall clock showcases Cheech with a doobie in his hand. All of a sudden, Cheech’s arm clicks upward, and the clock blares “It’s like, noon, man!” “Where am I?!” Joe shouts, his legs shaking. “You’re in my lair, sugar,” the woman replies, [detail withheld] “No, no, no!” Joe denies, “no, I went to sleep at my home in New Haven, with my wife! Where am I now?!” “Nova, I knew you had copious amounts to drink last night, baby, but this is crazy,” the woman (for lack of a better word) says, amused, “[detail withheld]” “Stop saying that!” Lieberman demands, “I don’t know what’s going on, but [detail withheld]” [detail withheld] “Oh my God!!” Joe groans, his voice heavy with nausea, “oh my God, oh my God, oh my God, oh my God…” [detail withheld] Joe doesn’t give that last comment time to sink in, bolting as quickly as his surprisingly muscular legs will carry him for the door. “You better put something on before you go downstairs, baby,” the woman coos, “[detail withheld]” Joe grabs a random pair of jeans off the floor and jerks them on before slamming the door behind him and jogging quickly down the stairs. However, his jogging soon slows before he screeches to a halt, standing half-naked in front of a group of men standing around a television. They’re giving him a standing ovation. “Hahaha! Congrats, Nova!” one of them shouts above the din, “[detail withheld]” Joe slumps against the stairs, sitting down on one of them and putting his head in his hands. Part of this must be the inherent fear he feels being surrounding by black people. “Relax, Nov,” another says, “[detail withheld]” “[detail withheld],” another pipes up, his voice shaking. “[detail withheld],” another states in a tone that brags of the amount of alcohol it must have taken to perform such a feat. The group collectively shoots him disdainful looks. Even Lieberman. “Alright, superstar,” one of them says, holding a PS2 controller out to Joe, “pick up them sticks. Let’s see if you walk the walk, ‘cuz I’ve heard your ass talk the talk about a thousand fuckin’ times.” “What…what game are you playing?” Joe asks, and then gasps as he sees the screen pop up. “GRAND THEFT AUTO: SAN ANDREAS?! NOO!!!” Out of instinct, Lieb-nasty dives in front of the television, blocking it to the best of his ability. “Stop! STOP!! Violent media of any kind, be it music, television, or video games, will corrupt absolutely and send this country spiraling down into chaos because people can’t separate right from wrong, and only know it as it’s dictated to them through what they hear and see! VIDEO GAMES ARE THE ANTI-CHRIST!!” As he finishes this, his eyes turn blood-red for a moment and a small burst of flame shoots out of his mouth…but no one notices. “Sit yo’ ass down, white boy,” one of the men with a controller says, and another grabs Joe and yanks him into a chair, “I thought you loved GTA.” “Mo, you’re a fuckin’ couch potato,” another man says, “playin’ those games all the damn time. Your brain is prolly all about to fizzle out and shit.” “Nah,” Mo replies, “that would be ‘cuz a’ all this funky-ass kill we been smoking.” As if on cue, one of the men hands Joe a blunt. “Here, Nova…you chill out on this for a minute.” “No, thanks,” Joe says uncomfortably. All heads turn and stare at him. The silence is painful. “Wait, wait, wait,” Mo says, “in all the time I known you, you ain’t never passed on a blunt. What the fuck? You sick or somethin’?” “No, no, I just…,” Joe begins shakily, “I just…” Everyone continues to stare at him, and Joe’s shoulders slump in defeat. He grabs the blunt, and takes a rip. One d00b-tastic smoke session later… “It’s like I was saying, man,” Joe says slowly to the rest of the men, their faces obscured by the thick cloud of ganj smoke that hangs swirling in the air, “the LIHEAP-that’s Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program-really would lower cost of energy for people in Connecticut, man, and that’s, like, really important to helping combat, like, this growing…” “Shut the fuck up,” one of the men moans, “shit, Nova, you’re starting to sound like a goddamn politician.” Joe snaps out of his rant and turns to look out the window. His hilariously squinted eyes (we’re talking the color of blood as it's about to coagulate here) widen in shock. It’s pretty dark outside. “Shit!! What time is it?!” “Chill, man, where’s the fire?” one of the men playing PS2 says. “I gotta go!” Joe insists. “Alright, Nova, alright,” Mo says, “Smokey can take you wherever.” “Yeah, I gotcha,” Smokey pipes up, and pulls himself off the couch, “to the Smokemobile, bitches!” Everyone laughs at his expense. “Man,” Mo says between chuckles, “that piece of shit you drive gotta be the ugliest car ever made, Smokey.” “At least I got a car,” Smokey retorts, offended, “not sittin’ around waitin’ for peeps like me to drive your worthless ass to the crack house.” Mo stands up. “The fuck did you say, nigga?” “Oooookay, well, it’s been a real treat, guys!” Joe intervenes, “Smokey, shall we?” Joe pulls Smokey towards the door, and they finally exit. They make their way down the driveway to a rusty, dented-in automobile, the make and model of which has long been in the annals of time. Which sounds like “anals.” As they travel along the road, Smokey fucks with the stereo until Dr. Dre blasts over the system, which is definitely worth way more than the metal cage surrounding it. “Keep their headz ringin’ (ring ding dong, ring-gading ding ding dong), keep their headz ringin’…” “SAY, SMOKEY,” Joe shouts over the music in an attempt to make conversation that we all know was doomed ten years before it ever started, “DO YOU KNOW WHAT EDGAR ALLAN POE USED AS A TERM FOR THE RINGING OF BELLS? HE CALLED IT 'TINTINNABULATION.' NEAT, HUH?” Smokey turns and stares at Joe for a moment blankly, and Lieberman’s grin vanishes like Howard Dean’s election hopes (oooooh). “Man, I hate this shit!” Smokey curses. “What?” Joe asks earnestly. “We gotta fuckin’ drive past the zoo,” Smokey explains, “and the fuckin’ monkeys throw their shit through the cages at my ride!” “Oh, certainly it’s not that ba-AAHHH!!!!” Joe shrieks as a massive pile of monkey shit splatters against his window. “YOU MOTHER-FUCKERS!!” poor Smokey screams, pulling a pistol out from under his seat. “Smokey!! Smokey I really don’t think…OH GOD DON’T KILL ME!!” Joe screams, ducking as Smokey fires off several shots through the passenger side window (what was the passenger-side window) at the monkey cages in the darkness. “FUCK YOU!!” Smokey continues, “I FUCKIN’ DOUBLE-DOG DARE YOU HAIRY BASTARDS TO COME OUT WHERE I CAN SEE YOU!! I’LL MAKE YOU EXTINCT!!” Joe feels a warm, soothing sensation and assumes he’s relaxing under pressure. In reality, he’s pissing himself. Ha. Smokey takes a few breaths and puts the gun away. “Aight, I’m better now.” “Fantastic,” Joe moans, but then his eyes light up. “Wait! Wait, that’s the state capitol building! Yes! YES!! Smokey, let me out here!” “Nova, you crazy?” Smokey asks, cocking an eyebrow, “you wanna go, lookin’ like that, to the state capitol building?” “Yes, yes, dammit yes!!” Joe says excitedly, “here! Here! Let me out!” “Alright, damn, let me stop,” Smokey mutters before pulling up to the edge of the curb. “Ha, HA!!” Joe cackles as he leaps out of the car, “you’ll be handsomely rewarded for this, Smokey! You’re a good American!!” “Get you and yo’ piss jeans outta my car,” Smokey replies, gratitude dripping off his words. But Lieberman doesn’t hear him…he’s already yards away, running at full-bore towards the capitol building. Here’s the image that the guards standing outside see coming closer and closer to them: a well-built man with no shirt on, jeans soaked in piss, no shoes, long blonde hair and a wild beard, sprinting directly at them. Commence flipping out. “Stop where you are!!” one of the guards shouts, holding up his rifle. “No, no, no, young man!” Joe shouts, still coming closer at a pretty wicked pace, “it’s me! U.S. Senator Joe Lieberman! Thank God for you lads! I was captured somehow by a gang of hooligans who called me Nova, [detail withheld] forced to smoke illegal drugs, and then I witness a shoot-out between a man named Smokey with a gun and a group of monkeys with poop! Oh, thank God I’m here and safe!” “SIR, STOP WHERE YOU ARE, RIGHT FUCKING NOW!!” the other guard yells, raising his weapon. “Wait…what?” Joe says as he slows down, “wait, there must be some mistake. I’m Joe Lieb-” “EMPTY YOUR POCKETS AND PUT YOUR HANDS ON YOUR HEAD!!” the other guard shouts as the sounds of police sirens can be heard in the distance getting closer. Joe reaches his hands into his front and back pockets, and then down into the cargo pocket on the side of the left pant leg…where he feels cold steel. “Oh, God,” Joe whispers with a gulp, thinking back to the house. “You better put something on before you go downstairs, baby,” the woman coos, “[detail withheld]” Joe grabs a random pair of jeans off the floor and jerks them on before slamming the door behind him and jogging quickly down the stairs. “Relax, Nov,” another says, “[detail withheld]” Hand shaking, Joe removes the pistol from his pocket. “HE’S GOT A GUN!!” one of the guards shouts as police cars roll up. SWAT Team members pile out of vans, rifles locking into place. “Wait! Wait just a fucking second!!” Joe screams, the tensions of the day finally coming to a head for the Connecticut senator. “I’M JOE FUCKING LIEBERMAN, A SENATOR FROM CONNECTICUT IN THE UNITED STATES SENATE!! I DON’T DESERVE THIS! I DON’T DESERVE ANY OF THIS!!” It’s Thanksgiving for the news networks. “We interrupt our story to bring you breaking news…” “We’re going live to the Massachusetts state capitol building, where a man…” “A man is apparently wielding a pistol outside the entrance to the building…police and law enforcement presence is mounting by the minute…” “We interrupt the Simpsons to bring you…oh, just kidding, we wouldn’t interrupt the Simpsons…” “…tense and dangerous situation in Boston tonight as a crazed unkempt man claiming to be Senator Joe Lieberman stands surrounded by police at the entrance to…” “I’M JOE FUCKING LIEBERMAN!” Joe wails, waving the pistol in the air, “AND I’VE HAD ENOUGH OF THIS! PLEASE, SOMEONE LISTEN TO ME!!” Inside a police van, someone important is watching the screen. “He’s too dangerous. He’s gonna kill someone. Tell Chop One to take their shot.” “Chop One, you’re clear to take your shot,” a man radios. High above the scene, a man crouched at the edge of a helicopter puts his finger to his ear before nodding. He steadies the sniper rifle on his shoulder and puts his sights on the back of Joe’s head. He says a quick prayer, his ritual, and pulls the trigger. ------*~*~*------ Joe Lieberman shoots upright in bed, gripping the back of his head as sweat drips down the sides of his face. He looks around. Nothing out of place. It’s his bedroom in New Haven. He falls back onto the pillow, sobbing. “Oh, Thank God. Thank you, God…” Suddenly, the door to the bathroom opens, bathing the room in light…and an obscenely rancid stench. A gargantuan shadow blocks out the light, and a tree trunk of an arm extends, [detail withheld] “[detail withheld]” “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!”
