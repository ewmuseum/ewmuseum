The World's Biggest Underdog

30 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 30th, 2008 "Alright brother, I’m fixin’ to deliver some news. What do you want first, the good ‘er the bad?" Throwing his gargantuan frame backwards into his favorite black leather lounger, Hank Cobb immediately presses the creaking and splintered wooden frame to its very physical limit. Somehow, however, the cagey old recliner weathers the three-hundred and fifty pound storm, more than making up for what it may lack in trendiness by offering comfort and resourcefulness unmatched. Wearing khaki shorts and a light blue polo, Hank Cobb has also donned the requisite brown leather Rainbow flip-flops and Croakies that not only shod his feet and hold his sunglasses just so, but also proudly proclaim and affirm his southern heritage. "You’re such a little girl sometimes, Cobb. And I don’t mean that in a good way like that ex-gymnast Amber I introduced you to at last week’s tailgate. Because a spinner you are not, my friend." With white Nike athletic shorts, black sandals and a black t-shirt with "Miami Still Sucks" across the front and "But Now Florida Swallows" across the back, Korver makes up for what both he and his wardrobe may lack in decorum with an altogether copious amount of honesty. "And I swear to you right here and now, with Hoyt as my witness, if your good news has anything to do with either ‘A,’ Carrie Underwood, or ‘B’ Kelli Pickler, and there’s not another option ‘C,’ where all of the above are presently and perhaps even willingly naked and somewhere upstairs in my room right now, I’m really not interested in your news at all." "And I just wish you’d let your guard down and shoot straight with me sometimes, Korv. Just seems to me like you’re holdin’ an awful lot inside, afraid of rufflin’ feathers or oversteppin’ your boundaries. You can’t just stay such a quiet young man forever." "I’ll work on it, that I promise you." "Well I really do appreciate that, brother, but let’s get right back to the issue at hand. Good or bad?" "Bad like another outbreak down south on the eve of spring break or bad like you wake up after yet another blackout Friday night only to find an extra large leopard print thong and a broken condom. "Well I guess I got good news and good news for ya then, Korv, cause ain’t none of my news near that bad." "Then fire when ready, big guy, because I got a solid buzz going already and it’s only twenty after two. Whether you waste my time or not, I’m still going to have myself a pretty nice, little Tuesday afternoon." "They didn’t book Delta Upsilon Iota on this week’s ReVolution card." "That’s the bad news? Are you fucking kidding me? An extra week of rest and relaxation with some adult beverages and bad decisions mixed in? Remind me to send Cantrell a fruit basket…no pun intended. From now on, that’s the kind of news you deliver me, nothing but the good stuff, my man. Now I can’t even begin to imagine what the good news is if that was really the bad. You just blew my mind, big fella, I hope you know that." "And I may just be fixin’ to do it again." "Scarface 2?" "Not a chance." "Young Guns 3?" "I like where ‘yer heads at, but unfortunately no." "Godfather IV?" "Only if they can get you as Fredo." "Rocky V?" "Korv, they already made Rocky V." "Don’t you ever speak of that again!!!" "With Tommy ‘Machine’ Gunn in the back alley?" "That movie never happened, god dammit!!! Do you hear me?!? It NEVER happened!!!" "Alllllright then. Balboa over Drago, roll credits. I got it, Korv." "Thank you, Henry." "Don’t mention it. Seriously though, if you agree not to mention any of…that, whatever it was…again, I’ll stop invitin’ my boys over for NASCAR." "Agreed. But for the record, it’s not your boys that I have the problem with." "So then what in the blue hell gets your feathers so ruffled each and every time?" "Ok, I lied. It is your boys. I tried to think of another reason but I ran out of time. I just can’t figure out why deodorant and full sets of teeth have to be so inversely proportional to longitude. Seriously, do you guys wear so much camo so that no one will be able to locate where the smell and ignorance is coming from?" "Ah, come on, Korv. They’re just good ‘ol boys watchin’ racin,’ what’s so wrong with that?" "Nothing, so long as left turns continue to scintillate and enthuse for hours on end." "The history of racing in the south is long and distinguished." "So is my Johnson, but you don’t see me inviting dozens of people over every weekend just to…ok, bad example." "Well seems like we’re just fixin’ to go round and round all afternoon, not getting’ anywhere." "So you’re finally admitting how boring NASCAR is? I’m proud of you, Hank." "No! I’m not admitting anything except for how big of a pain in the ass you are." "So that’s your big news?" "No, cause that ain’t really news at all, is it? What is news, however, is that come tomorrow, yours truly will be making his singles debut here in PRIME." "Seriously? That’s fucking great news, man. Not only do I get the night off, but I still get to watch my main man Mr. Cobb tear it up in the ring. So who’s the unlucky schmuck about to walk headlong into a little Southern Justice, courtesy of Hank the Tank?" "That would be…Tyler Rayne." "…" "Thanks for the vote of confidence, jackass. All the shit you’ve talked over the years and you can’t come up with anything to support my cause." "Look, I’m not saying you’re going to lose, big guy. Lord knows that nobody believes in you more than me. I was with you back in Global in the second round of Master of the Mat, when you stood toe-to-toe with Hall of Famer, and resident mean motherfucker, Salaok and took his ass out in the middle of the ring." "Yeah, I enjoyed that one myself." "And I was with you in on our first night here in PRIME, when you almost broke Tom Walczak in half a few seconds after entering the Dual Halo. And…if I remember correctly, also took it to Tyler Rayne, alongside your trusted partner of course, on that very same night." "Well when you put it that way." "But…he is Tyler ‘Motherfucking’ Rayne." "And ‘yer point?" "My point is this: do I believe you can beat him? Abso-fucking-lutely. But it’s going to take everything you’ve got. Blood, sweat, hell, even cry a few alligator tears if it’ll throw him off his game. Because if you don’t, you’re going to lose." "And if I do?" "Then we’ll have ourselves one hell of a night, and PRIME will have itself one hell of an upset."
