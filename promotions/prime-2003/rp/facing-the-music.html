Facing the Music

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 162 A wise man once said that when we are no longer afraid is when we truly begin to live. With a year left in his life, he would have to overcome the adversity rather quickly. A soft light flickers in the background. Two white couches sit in the light's shadow, the couch to the left is where Craig's sitting, his legs planted firmly on the ground, and his elbows resting awkwardly on his knees. Across the way are Katie and Nicole, both flabbergasted and taken aback by the news they just heard. "What'd you just say?" Katie's voice trembles from the reality of the situation. Only moments ago had Craig returned to the house and it seemed like everything was going to be just fine. However, it had turned out to be just the opposite. Sitting next to her, Nicole has yet to say a word as her bottom lip quivers while her eyes are on the verge of tears. "I guess I should start from the beginning." Craig takes a deep breath in and feels the air filling his lungs rapidly. He slowly lets it out while he closes his eyes and allows his thoughts to settle down. His left hand shakes slightly, but he manages to calm it down for a brief moment hoping to steel his nerves. "Around seven months ago, I started to experience these really bad headaches. Earth splitting headaches. I would compete in matches and be completely drained of all energy or will to live. I'd get back into the locker room and collapse from the agony my body was going through. I would be talking to one of you two and have to go to the bathroom as I felt my skull popping and the world around me begin to spin. I hadn't felt this bad in all of my years. I'd suffered from torn ligaments in my knees and bullet wounds, and nothing had made me want to give up then those headaches and that everlasting exhaustion." He licks his lips and can feel how dry his mouth is. His hands grab the edge of the couch and steady him as he gets up before walking over to the kitchen. "Eventually, I figured that I should probably start getting this all checked out. Probably right around the time that I passed out in front of Lindsay Troy. Lord knows that I'm not interested in having something like that happen again. So, I went to this doctor that I had been referred to, and he did some tests. Days turned into weeks before he knew what was wrong with me." Craig grabs the cup out of the drawer he had pulled out and puts the glass underneath the water faucet. "And that was MS?" He turns slightly to look at Katie and nods his head. "Exactly. It wasn't the easiest thing for me to hear. Mainly because I really don't think I truly understood what it meant." Craig then turns off the faucet before walking to a counter in the kitchen where he puts his drink down and continues to speak. "After I did some research and talked to the doctor a few more times, I finally realized what it was that I have. A disease that basically attacks and kills my brain. Not the most appealing death I'd dreamt of, but it is what it is, you know?" Katie looks over to Nicole who is still seemingly frozen. She then looks back at Nicole before sighing. "Is there any kind of cure for it? A way for you to beat it?" Craig bites his bottom lip, afraid of the reaction he's about to get. "No, there's no way to beat it or cure it. It's just there, eating away at you until there's nothing left for it to eat. Slowly and eventually I'll lose bodily functions and I won't be able to walk on my own. From there, I'll be essentially paralyzed until everything inside of me is basically dead." Katie shakes her head, almost as if she doesn't want to believe his answer. "There has to be something that we can do, Craig. Something to slow it down? There's something there that we can do!" He taps his fingers on the ceramic stone that makes up his counter as the light in the background flickers. Craig finds his eyes planted firmly on Nicole who still hasn't mentioned anything. He could only imagine what it is that she's thinking or going through, but he was certain that anything he came up with wouldn't be anywhere close to where she was. "The doctor offered me some solutions, some ways to slow everything down--" "Like what?" Katie interjects, hopeful that something can slow this whole process down. Craig can only shake his head. "He recommended that I give up wrestling, change my entire life--" "Whoa. There we go! That should solve the issue right there! The physicality of it all and the stunts that you pull off in that ring is definitely..." Her voice trails off though as she looks into Craig's red eyes and sees the answer staring her right in the face. "The doctor told you that when you first found out, didn't he? And you of course, turned it down. Couldn't leave that behind, now could you? Didn't think for a second that those who care the most about you would want you around in life?! Didn't even have the decency to tell us in the first place that you had it, so of course you wouldn't give it up. Typical you." "Katie...," "No, Craig. I can be there for you because I want to be there for you. I can stand there and support you and help you through those difficult periods, sit there by your side when you feel the headaches, do whatever needs to be done when you feel your entire body is on fire, and carry you when you can't walk any further because your brain is attacking you. But, not when you won't do the things to help you. Not when you deliberately spit in its face and go the complete opposite direction. Not when you're doing more harm than good." An awkward silence fills the room. Craig takes a sip of his water as he looks at Katie who's enraged now. "I don't know what to tell you, Katie." "Of course you don't. You went against what the doctor told you and you're paying for it now, aren't ya? A year left to live? Because you couldn't just walk away, now could you? You have to keep living this ridiculous dream of being the best ever and you will literally die from it?! Have you thought about the people in the ring with you? If you're a year away from death, how long before you lose control of your body? When do you draw the line, Craig?! When?! When someone is seriously injured? Knocked unconscious? Or dies because you're inability to think of anyone else beside yourself?! She walks over to him, each step angrier then the last, before she looks Craig right in the eyes. "I can't believe you, Craig. I really can't. I thought you were better than that. I thought you had put so much behind you and come so far. You left that old life behind you finally and have been living the good life. But, I guess you can't really change, now can you? I promise you Craig, if you don't quit right now, and start trying to live your life for the people around you, for your future kids, then I won't even be able to shed a tear at your own funeral. You'll get what you deserve Craig." For a moment, she lingers there as the two leave so many unsaid words hanging there. Katie turns around and heads right for the stairs. As she reaches that first step and grabs the handrail, she stops and turns back to look at Craig. "You were going down the right path, Craig. You were repenting, and now, I don't know what you're doing." Craig opens his mouth to say something, but nothing comes out. He's left there stunned as Katie turns back around and walks up the stairs. The seconds pass by like hours as he just stands there wishing that the entire thing could've gone better. He was trying to open up, but with the fear staring him in the face and the rejection right on the cliff of giving up, it was starting to seem fruitless for Craig. "Nicole..." He looks over at her and hopes that she'll finally say something, but she just continues to sit there silent and stewing. Craig licks his lips before he puts his glass down and walks over to her. Her beauty is evident as he sits down next to her and he puts his hand on her leg. So far he hadn't really addressed the fact that she was pregnant with their child, something he never thought of happening. One of the reasons he'd turned her away when she came back was the fact that he had MS and he didn't want her getting too attached when thing would go so terribly wrong for her. Now, he would have to stare that reality in the face and walk down that road with Nicole right there beside him whether he liked it or not. "Nicole, please say something." Her eyes slowly move to meet his and he can see the pain in her eyes. "I understand..." She finally speaks, but stops herself as she tries to regain her composure. Another moment passes before she works back up that strength again, the same strength that is holding back her tears. "I understand that this has always been your life, Craig, I get that. You've been in that ring for as long as you can remember and you don't want to have it taken away. I understand and I know the heartache you have to be feeling each and every day as you wrestle for that emotion. But..." She then grabs his hand and places it on her stomach. "We have a kid coming, Craig. A kid that you and I created. I came back because I finally had to face that reality that I'm going to have this kid and that you're the father. And as the father, I wanted you around and still do. I'm willing to look pass your history and your faults, and help you walk the line to redemption. But, I won't bring a kid into this world and introduce him to a father that won't be here in a few months time. I refuse to do it and I thought that knowing you're about to be a father again would've made you see that. Yet, even with that reality staring you in the face, you continue to put your life in danger. You don't reveal your true self to the people who matter the most. You don't let us in." Slowly, Nicole raises Craig's hand to her lips and kisses it gently. "You didn't tell me your past and let me find out about it from some complete stranger, and I still don't understand it at all. I need you to understand that and whether we like it or not, we're each other's future. This kid is not something I'm ready for, but I'm facing up to those responsibilities. You need to do the same thing. You have responsibilities to this kid and I expect you to fulfill your end." As she lets go of Craig's hand she stands up and looks down at him. "It's time to grow up and become a man, Craig. It really is." She then walks over to the stairs and walks up them, unable to look back at Craig. Nicole just leaves Craig sitting there as he feels himself being taken in by the darkness. The same darkness that had been sitting there on the edge of his consciousness for so long that he had been able to keep at bay. He wants to let it just engulf him, but it's like Nicole had told him. It's time to grow up. To. Be. Continued.
