Year of the Starchild (Colossus IV, vs. Danny Ferguson)

2 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/02/07 The Conference Room, PRIME Headquarters, Somewhere Important-yet-Interesting, USA, August 1st, 2007 “I…he’ll be here soon. Really.” H.R. Greadly, associate of the PRIME contractual department, growls under his breath and rolls his eyes before putting on his best “I can deal with this…for you” face in front of the investors’ board gathered around the large, black round table in an otherwise solidly white room. “You know how these guys get. They think they’re Rock Stars.” Down the hall, in the men’s restroom, Nova stands in front of the lavatory, hands clutching the lip of the marble sink in a white-knuckle grip. He raises his eyes to the mirror, his eyes partially dilating as patches of light brown and pink swirl in the high portions of his cheeks not covered by the deep brown of his beard. Prolonged experience with psychedelics will do that, turn mirrors, the sky, any image that isn’t grounded in concrete physical reality, into one’s own personal Funhouse. But it isn’t the slight changes in the pallor of his face that the Risen Star is focused on…but rather, his face itself, and the changes wrought on it by the last year and everything that had happened. Suddenly his eyes widen as shoots of long blonde hair explode from his scalp like yellow Play-Doh pushed through a shape cutter. His beard expands out from his face slightly, and his skin tone darkens as the bags under his eyes fade away. Nova puts a hand to the mirror, inhaling sharply. Like paint across primed wooden slats, streaks of blue wrap around his forehead, connecting above the divide in his eyebrows to form his old signature bandanna. “Chris? You okay in there?” Ariel’s voice floats in from outside the door. Nova takes another deep breath, splashing water from the sink up into his face. “Yeah, babe…just a second.” He pushes open the bathroom door and Ariel turns, shoulder-length brown hair whirling away from her back as she flashes a warm, encouraging smile. “Everything okay? You ready?” “Yeah,” Nova says with far less confidence than the nod that accompanies it belies. Ariel puts her arm around him as they walk together down the hallway towards the Conference Room, resting her head against the side of his arm. “You’re gonna be great, honey. Just relax, and let them see who you really are.” “God, don’t tell me that,” Nova replies, faking a cringe, “you do want me to be able to provide for our daughter, don’t you?” “Awww, Chris,” Ariel replies, rubbing his arm, “that’s why your wife is a badass lawyer.” “Touche,” he agrees. They stop outside the double doors and Nova turns, cupping a hand around her cheek and giving her a nod before letting go and pushing open one of the doors, letting it swing close behind him as he steps into the room. He looks out across the seemingly vast black table at the men in suits seated in front of him. One of them makes their way around the side of the table and approaches Nova, arm outstretched. “Mr. Vega, I’m H.R. Greadly, glad to finally meet you in person.” “Uh, nice to mee-“ Nova begins, but Greadly doesn’t bat an eye. “We saw the promotional tapes you submitted,” the Contract King goes on, “really great stuff, I gotta say. Admittedly, most of the leagues in your resume…” Without warning the man’s face explodes outwards, not in the “Oh, DAMN! SHOTGUN TO THE DOMEPIECE~!” kind of way, but like his face actually elongates, eyes running out sideways as the color is stretched. His body juts jaggedly in different directions, and then a hole, leading to a white void, expands out from his midsection, bubbling and skewing every image that falls under its advancing blackish front. It consumes everything. Cigarette burn. A kid sits at a desk, curly blonde hair tousled from sleep, and stares at the screen of his mother’s laptop. He shakes his head and chuckles before laying his fingers on the keyboard. Huh. So that’s how it ends.
