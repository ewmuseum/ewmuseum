The Wrestler's Wife

11 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/11 (OOC: To make a long story short, it's been a pretty shitty week for me. Putting this piece of what was supposed to be a full RP up in an effort not to totally no-show. Apologies to Harv.)) Of the 9000 seats in the Heart of Texas Coliseum, the most important to Benjamin Johnson were three mid-tier seats in Section 202. While Benjamin himself prepared behind the curtain for the contest he would have later in the evening, the three inhabitants of those seats were making their way towards them. The mother of Johnson's two children was leading the way -- in theory, only, considering her two children were clutching giant bags of popcorn with one hand, and clutching at her clothing with the other in an effort to make her hurry. "C'mon, mom," Jacob Johnson exhorted, "the show's gonna start soon!" Rebecca was shyer than her brother, but she tugged a bit harder at the waistband of her mother's pants, prompting the woman to sigh. "We'll be there soon enough," she insisted. "There's no need to hurry." "But the show's gonna staaaaart soon," Jacob whined, letting go of his mother to dart his way up the stairs, in search of their row. "I don't want to miss any of it!" Jacob was, in all honesty, the only wrestling fan in the house. Rebecca didn't *hate* wrestling, per se, but under most circumstances, she only really cared about it because of her dad. As for their mother, Erica? She hated professional wrestling, with most fibers of her being. Professional wrestling was what kept her husband away from her for so much time. Never mind that she'd only had to put up with the worst of it for a month or so. She also had to deal with her friends and family that constantly reminded her that she was dating a Neanderthal. Except, of course, her Benjy was anything but one. By god, though, he was one of them rasslers, so he had to be an idiot! She wasn't so much afraid of what wrestling had done so far as what it *could* do. She'd read the stories. She knew that the life of a wrestler's wife wasn't an easy one. It consisted of staying at home and taking care of the children while her husband travelled the world, drinking with the boys, and quite possibly screwing any of a number of perfectly willing women along the way. Erica knew all the stories, but she knew deep down that her husband was different. They had been married for years, he'd gone out for drinks with his friends, but never once had he drank to excess. Never once had he so much as looked at one of the rats who threw themselves at the wrestlers with abandon. But then, why was she so worried? The answer was simple. In PRIME, everything was bigger. The money was bigger; his first paycheck had dwarfed anything Ben had ever made in his life. So, too, were the expectations. The fame. The quality of ring rat was also higher. So was the quality of alcohol. And as she settled into her seat to watch her husband compete in the most important match of his life, all she could really do was hope that, win or lose, he'd be the same old Benjy.
