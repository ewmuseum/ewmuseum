Celluloid Heroes (Culture Shock '07, Universal Title Defense)

7 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/07/07 Suggested listening for Celluloid Heroes – “Some Weird Sin” by Iggy Pop Faith slips -- and laughs, and rallies -- Blushes, if any see -- Plucks at a twig of Evidence -- And asks a Vane, the way -- Much Gesture, from the Pulpit -- Strong Hallelujahs roll -- Narcotics cannot still the Tooth That nibbles at the soul – - Emily Dickenson, “This World is not Conclusion” An ovular shadow the size of a softball grows larger and larger on a carpet the color of visceral crimson. In slow-motion, an ornate vase decorated in floral patterns descends, tumbling end over end, before shattering on this fluffy Red Sea. The impact sends out a cloud of white porcelain dust in all directions across the carpet, and conversation in the room is momentarily silenced. “I…I’m, err…” Nova takes a step back from the table, physically disoriented, and holds out an arm in hopes of stabilizing himself against, well, anything. Sweat beads on the pallor of his face, and his breaths come too quickly for much oxygen to reach his lungs. “Meestah Noba…” The Risen Star’s eyes widen as the faces of the men dressed in business suits and women in cheongsam (“long dresses”) seated around the table begin to blur and distort, alternating between indistinguishable head-shapes and ultra-focused, nightmarishly warped gazes that seem to pierce Nova’s soul. “Tha vassss…” The ‘words’ drip out of Nova’s mouth like molasses. His tongue feels too big for, like, this room. He had wanted a party. He wasn’t sure about much of anything going into Culture Shock, except that Guangzhou is reportedly a bitchin’ ass spot to throw down on if you’re ever in the neighborhood. Nova had wanted to go out, get down, and not worry about Fuck You, PRIME, or… …or… …okay, so that’s basically his life, but still, he didn’t want to worry about it! He wanted a party. Now he just wants to be able to talk. “Meee-uhhh…Noooo-errr…” A woman approaches him, her calls of “Mister Nova, Mister Nova” melting into vowel-ish nonsense. Nova fights the panic rising in his stomach. Everything seems to be fading, but he distinguishes the woman as the one whom he met much earlier in the evening at Focus, one of Guangzhou’s most famous clubs. After some drinks (and the disclosure of the nature of Nova’s business in Guangzhou), he had followed her to a “private party” as she described it, which turned out to be a small gang of Guangzhou’s social and financial elite doing the Macarena. Did I say the Macarena? I meant mescaline. Nova sways before falling backwards into an upright potted bamboo palm. His head rocks the wall behind the plant hard and he rolls out of its folds onto the carpet, unsure if the laughter he hears is coming from the doped-out suits or his head. The woman who approached him moments before is gone, the hues of her fuzzy silhouette having blended into the tornado of color that now surrounds the Risen Star’s field of vision. In the tug-of-war between his sanity and the drug, the former is coming up feebly short. Shaking uncontrollably, Chris Vega rolls over onto his side and closes his eyes. BLAST OFF! Sitting on the cockpit of his own mind, Nova is thrown back against his captain’s chair as the Millennium Psycho-Falcon shoots into hyperspace. The relative darkness of blacks and greys streak past the Risen Star, and despite what Nova perceives to be nothingness around him, he can feel wind, or something to that effect, hitting his face and body. Am I dead? A tear rolls down his cheek, prompted not by feelings of sadness, but rather, awe. Could I have OD’ed? Are a bunch of mescaline-snorting Chinamen standing around my body right now, wondering whether to call the police or sell me off to an organ harvester like that dude Dick Face I’ve heard rumors about? YOU AREN’T DEAD. The words crack across the void like a thunderclap. The echo is lost as a loud roaring sound like an oncoming train fills this place. The grey and black streams of color swirl clockwise away from the Risen Star down into a vortex, at the bottom of which appears a bright white light. It expands out rapidly, engulfing Nova after a moment in its brilliance. He winces and shuts his eyes. When he opens them, he is floating in a white void. He gasps as his eyes adjust to behold an enormous cloaked figure suspended in front of him, whose face is obscured by a long hood. The reaper-like tatters of robe rippling soundlessly at the figure’s feet do nothing to encourage the Risen Star. “Who are you? Mescalito?!” MY IDENTITY IS IRREVELANT. KNOW THAT I HAVE POWER. “Um…good power?” POWER. “Ah.” THE POWER TO TRY TO CHANGE ANYTHING ABOUT YOUR LIFE THAT YOU REGRET. Nova’s eyes instinctively widen as the spectre grabs his attention. Anything? They narrow again as Nova tries to keep his cool despite nothing at all anywhere making sense. Information would be nice. “What if there is nothing I want to change?” YOU ARE A TORTURED SOUL. No arguing with that one. The last few months of Nova’s life have been the best of his life… …in terms of his career. Personally, the house that was his family, one that he had been so close to rebuilding, had burnt to the ground, leaving only columns of ash, most of which had been blown over long ago. He had lost a shit-load of weight despite drinking like a dehydrated rugby team, and the combination of his infamous head-shaving and the bags of sleepless nights under his eyes had transformed his appearance from happy, giggly stoner man to Holocaust victim. However, Nova isn’t getting the impression from this being that he’s looking for a sob-fest. So the Universal Champion raises his head and assumes the Poker position. “I’m not so tortured.” YOU WOULDN’T BE DOING MESCALINE WITH A GROUP OF FOREIGN STRANGERS IN A CITY YOU DON’T KNOW IF YOU WEREN’T. “Well, actually…” YOU DON’T DESIRE THIS. SO BE IT. The being fades, and suddenly the sound of Nova’s baby girl crying pierces his head in repeated, almost rhythmic squeals. “WAIT!!” As though the figure had never vanished, it appears again before the word makes it all the way out of Nova’s mouth. YOU DO DESIRE THIS. “Yes.” I WILL GIVE THIS POWER TO YOU. KNOW THAT IT WILL CONSUME YOU. “Right, good, whatever.” Nova is only half paying attention at this point, the echoes of Lulu’s cries still bouncing around in his head. How the fuck did that happen? Like she was here for a moment almo- The thought is interrupted as streaks of yellow and blue energy crackle outwards from the being and strike the Risen Star in his chest, sending him into convulsions as the threads course up and down his body. It’s hot!! Holy shit, it’s HOT!!! “AAAGGGGHHH!!!” Nova screams, “IT’S FUCKING BURNING ME!! HEY!! HEY MAN! HEY MAN, IT’S BURNING ME ALLIIII-ive?” Just like that, it’s over. Panting, Nova looks down at his body and hands. Tendrils of steam rise off of him, but he’s otherwise untouched. DONE. “Thank you,” Nova says, still out of breath, “Okay, so what now?” MAY I HAZARD A GUESS WHAT IT IS YOU WISH TO CHANGE? “What, you don’t know already?” Nova asks, cocking an eyebrow as he fumbles for the cigarette that isn’t there. I’M NOT GOD. I SAID I HAD POWER. “Alright, shoot.” The being offers no guess, only booming laughter as it begins spinning in circles, slowly at first and then faster, faster, faster as the white void is somehow pulled towards him, ripping away to reveal only black. Suddenly, Nova isn’t suspended anymore, but is free-falling down into the darkness. Down. Down. Down. He lands in a heap on cold, moist earth. His surroundings are illuminated by the moonlight to be a graveyard. He gasps (were he allergic to them, he’d in one of these plots himself by now) as the light falls on his daughter’s tombstone, which reads: SAMARA LUCINDA VEGA (March 23rd 2004 – December 14th 2006) BELOVED DAUGHTER LIGHT OF OUR LIVES Nova feels a pang – no, butcher knife – of guilt stab his chest. Even after the waste of a father he’d turned out to be, Ariel still put “LIGHT OF OUR LIVES” on the tombstone. He feels another presence and turns to see the figure floating above him off to the side. Nova offers a mirthless grin. “Good fucking guess.” He turns back to the grave and stares into the stone’s inscription once more. WELL. Nova shoots a glance at the spectre. “Well what?” DO YOU NEED ME TO HOLD YOUR HAND? RESURRECT HER. Everything stops. The Risen Star whips his head around to the grave, his eyes wild as his mind begins to wrap itself around the being’s words. “You mean…I…I can…” I TOLD YOU. ANYTHING. “How?! How do I do it?!” JUST CONCENTRATE. THEN HOLD OUT YOUR ARMS…AND DO IT. “I…I can actually…oh, my God…,” Nova mumbles to himself as he holds his shaky arms out towards his daughter’s resting place, “concentrate…concentrate…concentrate…” He does indeed concentrate, on images of his daughter that he remembers (they’re diminishing with embarrassing rapidity), and steadies himself. Nothing. “I don’t think this is…” Without warning the same yellow and blue beams of energy explode from his fists, snaking through the air in an instant and blast the earth in front of the grave, sending up a cloud of dirt at first and then chunks of protected wood as the coffin underneath shatters. Through the debris Nova can make out a tiny, crumpled body rising in the air. “AAAHHH!!” he shrieks as he looks down at his arms. Parts of his skin have peeled back to reveal reddish streaks of tender dermis. Staring in horror at his hands, he can see they’ve been burned, though no pain accompanies either injury. “What happened?!” Nova cries, looking up to the spectre for answers. I SAID IT WOULD CONSUME YOU. Nova turns back to the open grave and feels bile rising in his throat. On the slab in front of the tombstone lies the decaying corpse of his daughter, twisted into an unnatural position that only dead bones will allow. Several small insects scurry away from her body into the newly-expanded grave plot. “No…,” Nova says softly, tears forming under his eyes as he sees the ruins of his only child, “no, I can save you from this, Lulu…I can make this all better…I CAN FIX IT!!” The Risen Star thrusts his arms forward, and beams of energy erupt from his hands again and dart over to the corpse, rattling it on the stone. “I CAN FIX IT!!” Nova screams again as his hands begin to char and more skin flakes away from his arms and into the air. He steps closer. Lulu’s body spasms on the gravestone as blue and yellow streaks criss-cross over her chest. With no adjectives in the English to describe Nova’s reaction, parts of the decayed matter clinging to the remnants of her ribcage begin to move towards one another magnetically, forming rudimentary patches of flesh on her body. “DADDY CAN FIX IT, SWEETHEART!!” Nova cries maniacally. A chunk of one of his cheeks breaks away, and his body begins to smolder. The flesh is reforming across Lulu’s body as her bones begin to realign. Nova leans his head back involuntarily as his short blonde hair bursts into flames, but he doesn’t need his vision to hear the blood-curdling squeal of life being bestowed for a second time on his offspring. “YES! YES!!!” Nova screams as the flesh continues to peel away from his body, at some places exposing bone. He holds the flow of energy. Lulu thrashes now on the gravestone, releasing inhuman screeches from lungs that are in the process of coagulating from the soft tissues inside her chest. Her decrepit arms and legs flail around as the bolts continue to swirl over her body. SHE’S ALIVE!! Nova rejoices in his madness as the tips of his ears turn to ash and blow away in the wind. I’M DOING IT! A crude membrane now covers Lulu’s body as she continues to thrash and shriek. She appears almost human… …she’s beautiful… …and then the beams cease, as quickly as they came. Nova stares down at his gnarled, smoking fingers in horror. “NO! NO!!” He thrusts his arms out again, and a few meager bolts crackle over to Lulu’s writhing form, causing her only momentary, useless jerks. She squeals again, softer and weaker than before, and her movements begin to slow. “FUCK!!! NO!!” Nova shouts, turning back to the still-looming presence of the spectre, “WHAT HAPPENED!?” He tries again in desperation to summon the energy from before, to no avail. Lulu gurgles softly, her arms and legs barely kicking in the fight for life. She is caught in limbo, between the dead and the living. “NO!!! NO, PLEASE!! What happened?!! You said I could change any…” I SAID YOU COULD TRY. The Risen Star stares back at Lulu in anguish as her dirty, dilapidated limbs kick out several more times…and then slump to the gravestone, lifeless. She’s dead…again. “LULU!!” Nova howls, the crushing loss returning to him, “PLEASE!! GIVE ME ANOTHER CHANCE!! GIVE ME ANOTHER CHANCE!!” NO. THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES. “What?!” The moonlight shines on the figure, who throws the hood back to reveal the face of Chet Worth, more evil than ever the pudgy bastard had looked before. I SAID THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES!! HAHAHAHAHA!! THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES, AH-HAHAHAHA!! In utter disbelief Nova feels himself fall backwards onto wet dirt. Despite the intense, macabre expression on the Worth’s face, Nova can only stare on at the corpse of his daughter as sobs rack his chest. “Please…please, I need to…” THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES!! The terrifying laughter continues, and Nova can only look, mind numbed, as the face of Chet Worth morphs into his ex-wife Ariel. THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES, HAHAHAHA!! Yanking himself to his feet, unable to cope on any level of his psyche with the scene before him, Nova begins sprinting down the cemetery, past rows and rows of tombstones…other people’s loved ones. THERE ARE NO SECOND CHANCES!! HAHAHAHAHA!! He loses his step and falls forward, but there is no ground to embrace him… …only darkness. Down. Down. Down. ------*~*~*------ His vision is a blur, but an image is coming into focus slowly…black, then pale, then a host of other colors as shapes form in front of him. The image that pieces itself together is the last he saw before he lost his grip on reality…the girl from the club leaning over him, her face full of worry. “Meestah Noba? Meestah Noba, you okay?” After a moment of collecting his nerves, the Risen Star looks around at the penthouse loft. Then he pushes up with a grunt and pulls himself to a seated position. “Yeah, Hao…I’m…I’m okay.” I’m okay. I’m okay. I’m okay. “You should leave, Meestah Noba,” Hao warns, “someone else get scared, carl police.” Looking around, Nova notices that no one else is in the room anymore besides the two of them. He gets up and moves towards the entrance to the apartment. He stops after a few steps and turns back to the girl. “Thanks.” She offers an encouraging grin, and Nova gives her a nod before turning and bolting out the door, to the soundtrack of Chinese police sirens. I wish my life was a non-stop Hollywood movie show, A fantasy world of celluloid villains and heroes, Because celluloid heroes never feel any pain, And celluloid heroes never really die. - The Kinks, “Celluloid Heroes,” Everybody’s in Showbiz
