Over

Date: May 2, 2010 ONE | Eggs Over Easy Arrogance wafted in the air so thick you could almost smell it. Everything from the elaborate construction to the romanticized "touch of class" screamed of a place that was oozing with swagger. No matter how hard she tried, however, she just couldn't fall in love with the place. In fact, each visit seemed to expand her disdain for the place exponentially. Yet out of habit, she kept coming back to the same seat. Looking at the same beautiful view of Miami Beach. Sitting in the same wrought-iron deck chair. Whether it was out of habit or out of familiarity, she tapped her fingers on the circular hard wood table. Dressed with candles and the finest of tablecloths, you couldn't even see the luxury underneath the local paper sprawled across it like a tactical World War II map. With forehead rested on her hand, the disappointment quickly consumed her otherwise annoyed demeanor as she read the classified add posted square in the middle of the page: "Sexy, intelligent, well-spoken, successful, and wealthy female searching for chauffer, bodyguard, and personal assistant combo. Must be good looking, able to self-motivate, and have much experience. Ability to follow sometime complex orders and fluent English is a must. Must not have hesitations about vulgar displays of violence. Leave message if interested." To her it sounded like a dream job (other than being herself, of course), but the offers just weren't coming in. Perhaps the target audience was just too ignorant to realize what an amazing opportunity this was, or maybe they were intimidated by her accomplishments. Regardless of reason, a sign escaped the lips of The Swaggeriffic One as she folded up the newspaper in front of her. Once in a manageable clump, she took the newspaper -- the very same one that she demanded not-so-politely from the waiting staff just a few minutes ago, and tossed it over the deck rail and watched the wind sail it in pieces over the picture book landscape the surrounded her. "Miss, we don't do that here." The stuffy voice of a much older man broke her blank stare. With a blink she turned around and looked back at the waiter, who proceeded to lay her plate down on the table next to her cell phone. Still without as much as a ring. No texts. No e-mails. No voice messages. "Do what now?" She replied, confused. "Littering." He responded, laying out a white cloth napkin and a knife and fork next to her plate. "I think you're confused." The Swaggeriffic One glared back at him, "You don't tell me what to do, I tell you what to do." "It's against the law." He stated, adjusting his jacket as he went to turn around, "Enjoy your... eggs over easy." "I asked for sunny side up." "It's the same thing, miss." "I don't like your attitude, Jeeves" Elise responded, clearly combative as she poked at her egg with a silver fork, "and your eggs are way over-cooked. Bring me new eggs." "Miss, could you lower your voice? You're making a scene." "How DARE you talk to me that way!" She stood up before sticking an egg with a fork and lifting it into the waiter's face, "Then expect me to eat this... this..." Flinging the egg at the waiter, everyone around her stopped and watched the scene unfold. As the runny, yellow goo rushed down the front of the elderly man's tuxedo jacket, Elise Ares sneered. "I'm a champion. I'm IMPORTANT. You don't talk to me that way servant." "Miss, I'm going to have to ask you to leave." The man spoke almost to the second to when security arrived at the table. Two much bigger man grabbed the arms of the Havana Harlot, causing her to attempt to jerk her way free to no avail. "Get your hands off me!" Elise demanded, "Kaz, where in the hell are y..." As she was being drug away against her will, she was almost dumbfounded by her own lapse of memory. There was no Kazys Jankauskas. He was fired in the middle of the PRIME ring. Twice even. Despite her struggling she had no choice but to accept the inevitable and leave the restaurant under someone else's power. It was embarrassing. It was insulting. A searing reminder of the exact reason she hired Kaz in the first place, this was the very reason an asset like herself needed a bodyguard. And an assistant. And an accountant. Or just a friend. If things had just turned out differently, she'd still be sitting there waiting on her eggs to return with Jeeves tied into a pretzel. But she wasn't, because the outcome of their tag team was inevitable from the start -- just like any relationship Elise Ares had ever involved herself in. They say that a zebra can't change it's stripes no matter how hard she may try, and did she ever try. Yet somehow, as completely expected as it was, it still hit both of them like a truck. TWO | Head Over Heels "Dammit!" The scream of The Havana Harlot echoed through the hallways of the Holiday Inn. "You cannot be serious!" With a grunt she shook the chrome handle violently as Kazys Jankauskas dropped his bag on the floor behind her and slid the keycard. The second the light turned green and the handle turned, she lowered her shoulder and plowed into the door. Picking up her cargo bag, she hurled it across the room as Kaz just remained silent, trying to ignore the temper tantrum of his tag team partner. "You..." She looked back at The Iron Wolf as he simply dropped his bag on top of the nicely made bed, a stark contrast to the ravaged sleeping space of The Swaggeriffic One, "You've lost it haven't you?" Again, Kaz didn't speak a word. She walked right into the face of The Iron Wolf, who went about his business without even as much as eye contact. "You couldn't last in that ring for more than two minutes?!" Elise screamed, "Are you even trying anymore?! Do you want me to get killed out there?! Why in the FUCK am I paying you to be my personal bodyguard if you're not even competent enough to stay in the ring for more than TWO FUCKING MINUTES to have my back? WHY?! PLEASE TELL ME WHY YOU'RE NOT A WASTE OF MY GODDAMN MONEY!" Not a single word escaped the lips of Kazys Jankauskas. Nothing more than a sigh as he unzipped his bag and pulled out a tiny baggie full of shower supplies. Her rant fell upon deaf ears as he obviously would rather spend his time counting how many bars of soap he had left than hear another word come out of Elise's mouth. "Hey, hey! HEY!" Elise screamed before winding up and landing a hard shot right across the cheek bone of The Iron Wolf. "Listen to me when I'm talking to you, you son of a bitch." The slap hardly seemed to phase him as he stood perfectly still, other than dropping his Ziploc baggie down onto the bed next to his personal belongings. Looking up at Elise Ares, the glance she saw looking back at her was that of a killer. An honest to God killer. A man capable of ripping out the hearts of innocent children and feeding them to his dogs. "You left me alone out there to be pinned by some homeless guy who smelled like garbage." Elise responded, "You can look at me like that all night if you want. You're the one who failed us." Showing his teeth with a snarl, Kazys Jankauskas was a time bomb waiting to erupt. "There's some intensity." Elise threw her arms up in frustration, "FINALLY, you give a damn about somethi..." Before she could even finish her sentence his hand struck her throat. She didn't have time to think or react before she was jerked across the bed and slammed into the wall. Impact occurred with a wince across her face. The shockwaves knocked the picture off the wall and shattered against the carpeted floor. Her eyes grew wide as she looked back at her tag team partner. He was going to kill her. "Do it." Elise chocked out the words, "Show me you still got what it takes so I don't fire your ass." His grip squeezed tighter around her vocal chords ending her words. Lips moved but only silence made it out. She squeezed his massive arm, trying to release some of the pressure as it became harder and harder to breathe. Her heels beat against the hotel room wall and she scrunched her nose. A snarl of her own crossed her lips as she tried her damnedest to free herself. It was useless as she managed to squeeze out one last phrase. "What's taking you so lo..." But before she could even finish, her words were stopped. It wasn't by a lack of oxygen or a lack of vocal support however, but by the lips of The Iron Wolf. Her heart skipped a beat as he let go, letting the oxygen rush back into her brain as she fell helpless onto the floor with an umpf. Sitting up, she looked up at Kazys with a deer-in-headlights expression accented with extended blinks as she smacked her lips together trying to place what had just happened. Using the back of her hand she wiped her mouth and continued to stare in uncomfortable silence. "..." Elise's mouth opened but nothing came out. For the first time in her entire life, she had absolutely nothing to say. Kaz simply shook his head and closed his eyes before turning his back to PRIME's 5*Star Champion. Picking his plastic baggie back up off of the floor, he made sure everything he needed was inside for a very long and hot shower. He almost didn't want to turn back around and look into the blank, shocked expression of his tag team partner. He had to through, she was between him and the shower. Turning around, he kept his head low to avoid making eye contact when suddenly he felt a burden on his shoulders. The body weight of Elise shocked him, forcing him to stagger back and fall into the bed with her on top of him. Before he could even react she shoved her tongue in between his lips, jerking up at his tight black t-shirt before ripping it off of him and throwing it across the room. He pulled her back down on top of him, running his huge hands up the back of her shirt as she sucked on his bottom lip. A sense of freedom rushed over her body as the strap to her bra came undone and he forced her lips away for a second to pull her own baby-tee off over her head. Her heart pounded out of her chest as she sat on top of him with her chest exposed, feeling the sense of adrenaline and excitement that she'd wanted to feel for months. A smirk crossed her face as she grabbed his wrists, guiding his hands up to her chest. Feeling his touch she closed her eyes and let the night take place as it was intended to do for all this time, no matter how hard she tried to fight it. It had never felt so amazing. THREE | Long Over Due The sound of chirping birds once again ushered in the new day. Light began to enter her unfocused world as she slowly opened her eyes. Brightness just added to her pounding headache as she ran her hands through her hair with a slight grimace. "Owww..." She whispered to herself out-loud, hoping not to awake anyone. Pushing the covers aside she found herself naked as a jay bird, as she'd heard some redneck at a wrestling event say once. Her entire body was screaming out in pain. Sitting up was even a chore as she felt like she'd just been hit by a truck. Looking down at herself she looked like it too. Bruises and scratches covered her body. Her stomach, her hips, her arms, her thighs... evidence of the night was everywhere she looked. Everything was knocked off the walls, laying just a few feet away from where they originally were. The alarm clock, the notepad, the books, even the television were on the floor... leaving every flat surface of the hotel room empty and bare. With a grunt she used the nightstand as a crutch to reach her feet, biting her bottom lip as she reached a vertical base. It felt like walking for the first time as she toes curled into the carpet, not getting very far before stumbling upon a pair of blue panties. Bending over, every vertebrae in her back cracked in unison as she navigated her feet into the holes and pulled the panties up around her waist. Grabbing her baby tee off the lamp on the floor, she slid it back on too -- trying to make herself decent in case house keeping decided to show up unexpectedly. Turning around she looked back down at the bed that she left, where Kazys Jankauskas still slept not-so-silently. His snores sounded like a bear as she watched him sleep. It was just then when reality began to set in from what happened the night before. This wasn't like the other guys. She could feel the butterflies turning in her stomach as she watched his chest go up and down. A smile a mile wide forced its way across her lips. It took every fiber of her being to not crawl right back into bed with him and kiss him one more time. "What in the fuck is wrong with me..." She mouthed to herself as she became overwhelmed by emotions. This wasn't right. This had never happened before. This was supposed to never happen. "I have to get out of here." With those words she turned around with such velocity that she almost knocked herself over. Thoughts began to race through her mind about what was going on with her feelings. Panic mode set in as she frantically searched the room for everything she could find without waking The Iron Wolf sleeping in the bed next to her. Socks. Shoes. Bra. Wrestling Gear. She had to have it all and she had to have it now. With a anxiety filled sense of desperation, she violently shoved everything she could find that even remotely looked like it belonged to her into her suitcase. She didn't have time to put on a bra or her socks and shoes, she had to leave now. Sliding her feet into a pair of lounge flip flops she packed everything down into the suitcase with one last shove before zipping it shut as quietly as she could. Slinging her bag over her shoulder and jerking the suitcase up off of the bed, Elise grabbed her keycard and tossed it into the trash next to Kaz's boxer briefs and forced her injured body to move quickly towards the door. Opening it with her wrist due to her full hands, she managed to pull the door open with her foot and creep out silently without waking the monster in the bed. Getting completely in the hallway, she paused for a moment as the door shut behind her. "You can't run away from this forever." That was the phrase that repeated itself inside of her head over and over again as she couldn't quite force herself to walk away. Her hands began to shake as she looked down at her battered body. Reaching back towards the door she stopped herself just inches away with a sigh. Closing her eyes she took a step back away from the door, turned around, and left. Hopefully leaving her emotions locked away inside. FOUR | Getting Over You Opening the door, she found her modest Miami Beach apartment completely ransacked. It looked like a hurricane had struck as she dropped her bags on the floor just inside the doorway and shut it quickly behind her. The apartment was just as she left it... "Dammit." She said to herself as she walked over to the couch and replaced the cushions, picking them up off the floor one-by-one and forcing them back into their respective places so she could relax, "You'd think Tasheka could have at least cleaned up the place a little." Tasheka was an older black lady who lived a few doors down. Elise had left her in-charge of dropping by twice a day to check on CATastrophe for the attractive salary of $20 a day. The Swaggeriffic One had even gone through the trouble of setting up two hidden webcams in her bedroom and in the living room hoping to catch a day where Tasheka didn't show up. Thus voiding their verbal contract, and relieving her of any obligations to pay the poor woman. Unfortunately, Tasheka was a woman of her word. That stupid bitch. Jumping up in the air, she landed in a laid back position on her couch before brushing the various trash and make-up supplies off of her coffee table to find the remote control. There it was in black and silver beaming glory under the dusty ceiling fan. Reaching out she pressed power, hoping for some background noise to help her relax. Without warning, however, an unexpected voice startled her... "Meow." "Oh... it's you." Elise stated, staring down the butterball-like pussy cat wandering into the room. Its different colored eyes and snaggle-tooth appearance made the cat so ugly it was cute. Its fur was grey and white, obviously a combination of different types of cats. Almost like it was some mutant cat hybrid. You may call him... CATastrophe. "Meow." "I knew you'd ask about him." Elise answered, pulling her cell phone out of her pocket. It rumbled once again, alerting her that she had a tenth new voice mail to accompany the nineteen missed calls from Kazys. "I'm afraid we're not going to see him anymore." "Meow." CATastrophe replied, concerned. "It's complicated." Elise responded, patting her lap for the cat to come up and join her. For the first time in its life, it followed a command and did just that -- landing on her lap like someone had just dropped a bowling ball on her poor aching legs. "Maybe some day, you'll understand." "Meow." CATastrophe agreed... probably. "I don't know what I'm going to do." Elise looked into the discolored eyes of a ferocious feline beast. "No one showed up for an interview." "Meow." "You're crazy, he hates me. He wouldn'..." "Meow." "You can't be seri..." "Meow." "Really? You think I should?" "Meow." "Hmmm..." Elise expressed interest as she ignored the voice mail and opened up her contacts list. Flipping all the way through, she skipped every single contact on the list until she got to the very bottom one. She looked at the name for a few moments as CATastrophe made himself comfortable and purring on her lap. With a slight hesitation, Elise struck the call button and put the phone up to her ear. The ringing was nerve-racking as she waited for an answer. After every tone she contemplated hanging up the phone, but decided to give it a shot. Hell, why not? Everything else had failed... "Hello?" She heard on the other line, taking a breath before speaking. "Hey, this is Elise." She stated before... CLICK. "That bastard hung up on me!" She screamed, looking down at CATastrophe before redialing. She hoped that he wouldn't hit the ignore button recognizing the number, if she could just get a word in edgewise... "What?" The voice answered through the receiver. "Hey listen, I know you don't like me but I think I have a pretty sweet deal." "I'm not interested." "Trust me, I could make it well worth your while." "I doubt it, but this should at least be entertaining. What do you want?" "Well, I have a job opening..."
