It All Gets Better In Time? (REV. 174 vs Dawkins)

4 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4th, 2008 She was beautiful even with the gun in her hand. The shining steel of the pistol in the afternoon only reflected her magnificence for her son to behold. Her state was pathetic and drugged up, this frail Goddess, who was placing her only son in what was a sobering position for anyone. Blonde, pigment challenged hair covered his eyes from the reality of it all. On this beautiful, summer day, pinned up in a moderate apartment Rhett Locke would come to realize that he was all alone on this planet. Rhett Locke was only seven years of age and was in a phase of hero worship for his mother, who despite not having a job or a chance to succeed did everything she could for him (to an extent). Her slender, pale legs were being grasped at by the only thing left that loved her. He cried so hard that he was choking. Though prone to panic attacks, he'd never experienced the kindof heartbroken, sick, frenzied trepadation. A knot, so tight in his stomach that his organs could hardly operate. Adrenaline pumping so harshly through his veins that he trembled like a Pentecostal during a rapture. "Get away from me, son. Please. I have to do this." Rachel Locke's voice was foreboding, holding a Hemmingway quality to it all. As though it was her against the world. And perhaps in her acidic state, she really felt that way. Her words were not as loud as her sons pleas for her to stop acting crazy, "No one cares about me anyway. Not my mother, not my father. Not even the father of my son, the only man that I love. What would it matter if I just pulled the trigger right now?" "I care about you! I love you," he cried. Her chuckle was condescending and hardly caring, a far cry from the way she might normally act. "Of course you do, dear. Now stand aside." She placed the barrel of the pistol to her temple and closed her eyes. Rhett did everything he could to rescue her. The only thing that seemed to work for the moment was biting her on the leg. "AAAAAAAHH!" She screamed, Rhett biting so hard it threw off her acid-laced train of thought. Later in life he would never forget how her eyes looked when she turned around: possessed. Her face, normally pale was flushed with anger. The weak angel struck her son with the back of her hand so hard a loose tooth came out of his mouth. "You little bastard." Rhett's eyes widened. His mother, the only person he'd ever known to count on in his entire, albeit short life, had just cursed him in such a way that no one had done before. He felt his heart tear apart when she said that, realizing this was only the beginning of a very long and hurtful spiel. "...After everything I've done for you, you bite me in the leg? I took care of you when you were sick, feed you every day and you bite me in the leg? You're the one who's HEAD should have a gun put against it. Maybe I should have listened to Chris." He was crying so hard that he was losing his breath. How could she treat him like this? And Chris? He had heard this name once or twice. "Chris, by the way is your deadbeat, piece of shit, black father! Chris, a man who wouldn't HAVE you, because you're albino and 'shit aint like that'. Whatever the FUCK. THAT. MEANS." She kicked over a plastic trashcan. Garbage spilled onto the linolium. "Please," Rhett implored. "I love you. Please. Stop it." "PLEASE, STOP IT!!!" She mocked him in a crude, obnoxious way while simultaneously pointing the gun down at him. This would be the first of a few times in his life where he feared for his life. For him, this would be a defining moment of his childhood. "If I hadn't got pregnant with you, Chris and I would still be together. I wouldn't have to FUCK men every night to keep you fed. You turned me into a slut!" She accused of her child. He was, literally, on the verge of choking to death on tears. When Rhett thinks back to this particular afternoon, what occured next was always what he thought to be the pivotal moment of the entire day. The hair still raises on his arms and his eyes still water, thinking about it today. On this afternoon, in an apartment as dark as the events unfolding in them, a question was answered. "Would you rather me kill you or kill myself?" She asked in a sick calm, an accepting one. He would never understand why it couldn't be one or the other. He was gasping for breath so hard he could not give her an answer. Though he didn't have one anyway. To ask a child that question is proposterous and unfair and no one could truly expect him to answer that. Not even her. And without a further word she-- BANG! ...without a further word she was dead. Brain matter soiled the surface of the floor. Blood filled an area around her neck and what was left of her cranium. Rhett did not react immediately, but his gasps for air in this anxious, crying state became more extreme. Locke outstretched his arms, falling to his knees and rushing towards her. His arms extended, he tried to hug her though she was too heavy to lift. There was no one alive for him now... he didn't know what to do. The Police Officers that arrived on the scene responded to a report of shots fired in a residential apartment complex. They described the situation in Aparment J2 as "a nightmare" on the evening news: An albino child laying on the corpse of his mother who commited suicide, whimpering. ----------------------------- "This... this can be it Bryan. This can be the last of any sort of problem between us. All you have to do is pick yourself up once I leave, get in your car, haul ass to your pretty little knocked-up slut and never make contact with me again." The Albino lifts the bat over his head, he appears posessed. "Let this be the alarm sounding, Dawkins. Our time together, while painfully fun, is up." Crack went the bat. On the way to the next Revolution, in that rinky-dink truck of his, he thought very little in regards to the beating he had given Bryan Dawkins. It was of little consequence to him at that time. No one would rebound that quick from a beating that bad. Bryan Dawkins may be a father-to-be but he was no Clark Kent. Shit, Superman wouldn't get up from a beating that bad. He had stopped just short of beating his face in. No reason to go to jail. On this night, Rhett only thought of his mother. The brain matter on the floor was an image that had burned deep into his skull. When Rhett Locke would grow old and get Alzheimers, forgetting all of his past accomplishments and becoming more and more mentally indisposed, nurses would wonder why he did not sleep well.
