Jonathan Rhine Will Hate It When A Plan Comes Together

17 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/06/08 I - For Every Action A haunting classical score seems conspicuous by its absence as a pair of PRIME technicians dive in slow-motion ‘Platoon style’, springs, circuit boards and shards of plastic casing exploding around them. The skull-splitting resonance of machine gun fire is deputised by the smashing of glass screens on hard floor tiles, and the blood-curdling screams of grotesquely wounded soldiers would sound akin to a feeble whimper under the booming roar of one extremely peeved Gold Patron Meritorious. “WHERE IS HE?“ explodes a guttural outcry from Kannon’s diaphragm. “WHERE IS THAT SMARMY LITTER FUCKER?“ With emerald eyes about 6 adjectives up from ‘wild’, a once exquisite suit having nearly been torn from his frame in frustration and his dyed red hair frizzing up in the humid arena, the entrance of Jonathan Rhine into his life seems to have transformed Kannon from smug and slick to rattled and dishevelled in a matter of mere minutes. And, just because it’s been 5 seconds since the last time, a monitor is hurled into the nearest solid wall… only for it to slip from Kannon’s sweaty hand and find itself thumping into the abdomen of an unfortunate production crew member. As the victim collapses to his knees, wheezing cringingly as his lungs threaten to collapse in the wake of their forcibly expelled capacity, Kannon pins every trembling crew member to the spot with a wide-eyed, judging glare. “WHICH ONE OF YOU TRAITOROUS SIMPLETONS CUED UP HIS MUSIC?!“ with snaps of the neck that one could easily mistake for time-capture, Kannon’s eye rests upon every suspect within 5 seconds. “I WANT TO KNOW WHICH ONE OF YOU WAS AN ACCOMPLICE TO THAT TRAVESTY!“ They’re given one solitary second to confess themselves or incriminate another. With no answer forthcoming, Kannon heaves a sound-mixer off a table and hurls it over his shoulder, the wires still attached dragging down what would pass as a whole wall of equipment down in its wake, crushing the table it once rested upon. “WRONG ANSWER!“ the Gold Patron Meritorious decrees. Huddled down close to the debris-strewn floor, Eleanor scurries in and amongst the cluster-bombs of electronics to round up the celebrity Scientologists who had bore witness to Kannon’s moment of embarrassment. “Um, you’d better, like, run for your lives,” she warns, bobbing her head down just as a lethally jagged dagger of glass whizzes through the air above her. “When he gets cranky, he can go a little… um… totally overboard?” Crouching down for cover behind the winded technician, Ellie ushers Beck, Nancy and Laura towards the nearest exit… only to pause, face betraying that nagging feeling that she might have forgotten someone. “That’s, like, all of you, right?” she queries as her human bomb-shelter adds a broken metatarsal to his cracked ribs. Looking over her shoulder to where Jason Dohring’s hand flops limply out from under a scrapheap of electronic parts, Beck decides that no-man-left-behind is overrated, and nods in reply. “DO YOU PEOPLE EVEN KNOW WHAT YOU’VE done?!“ a infuriated and distraught Kannon continues to vent, the raw and vicious tone to his voice finally cracking. “Tonight was meant to be the most glorious moment of my life… a night I truly became who I was destined to be… and he…” Dropping to his knees, the Gold Patron Meritorious’ voice retreats back down his throat and the technicians look on both bemused and relieved as a tear runs down a defeated, spent Kannon’s cheek. “He… he ruined it,” trembles off his tongue as someone yanks a tremolo bar deep down Kannon’s throat to stretch the words into a high pitch whine. “Why would he do that? Why can’t everyone be as generous and giving as me? I… I…” A little creeped out, the wounded and flat-out baffled PRIME employees back away slowly, worried this is the fizzle before the final detonation. “I’m… I’m…” is uttered, barely a whisper. “I’m… him… I’m going to ruin him.” There, stood amidst a war zone of smashed equipment and walking wounded, Kannon’s head seems to sway right and left like a compass, seemingly able to locate the departed Rhine as its magnetic north. Staring through the walls of the Fieldhouse, Kannon seemingly knows he’s piercing holes in the back of the New Life’s head as his eyes narrow. “You don’t know what you’ve done…” he whispers with more than a hint of the maniacal. “You don’t know what you’ve done…” Having successfully evacuated three quarters of the celebrity Scientologists without harm, Ellie returns to the scene, her pursed lips mulling over the scene by sliding back and forth. “Um, sweetie,” she hushes just enough so that he can’t really hear her. “I’m, like, going to Nova’s for a Casablanca... or twelve. Just, like, totally don’t wound anyone else, ‘kay? Promise? Good. Byezzies…” II - Storm In A Pee-Cup “You realise this is highly illegal, unethical and… well, down right cold, right?” When you set out to bring a fellow professional athlete’s world down around them, you don’t just go for the instant direct hit and try and knock them flat. You have to plant as many little charges around their foundations as you can, set to go off shortly after one another, until they crumble under their own weight. And to do that? Well, you need yourself a good demolitions crew. “That was the general appeal,” an emotionless Kannon dryly answers in ‘Agent Smith’ drawl. The man whose question he answered? Rush Mason. “Yeah, who am I kidding,” Kannon’s manager cracks his faux-concern. “Can’t say I’ll be losing much sleep.” Now, let’s rewind just a few minutes, shall we? Loosening his tie and breaking the pressure-seal of his top button, Rush Mason slouches into the mindset of an early Friday finish. He drops his cufflinks into his breast pocket, orchestrates the chimes of Windows shutting down with his index fingers, and finishes off the last of his once-iced water in one long sip, before tossing the plastic cup over his shoulder and into the trashcan. Only one thing can happen when a man fully gives himself to the idea of total and utter relaxation. Only one thing will happen when a man fully gives himself to the idea of total and utter relaxation. “MASON!“ overwhelms inches of solid wood and invades his office. “TELL THE GUARD DOG WE DON‘T NEED AN APPOINTMENT!“ Deflating at the sound of the Gold Patron Meritorious’ voice, Rush looks out through floor-to-ceiling windows and down eight stories at the bustling street below. If it was six, he’d have taken his chances jumping. Before Mason can even buzz reception, the Kannon breeze into his office, leaving a frazzled receptionist in their wake. Xavier doesn’t look much better himself. His body is hyperkinetic, imagine a cat fed caffeine tablets for a prank… imagine it, seriously. The humidity is playing havoc with his hair, leaving it looking like it’s been styled along the lines of a particularly haphazard Queen Amidala. As for Ellie? Well, you would. Enough said. “There’s a pair of experienced hands at the nearest Turkish baths with my name on,” deputises for a greeting from Rush. “So this better be quick, involve you writing a cheque, or preferably both.” Mason does everything he can to portray the role of ’man about to leave office after 70 hour week’. He slips into his jacket, slams his briefcase shut and slaps the locks on as conspicuously as he can, before juggles his keys about in his pocket. “I really hope that’s, like, totally your keys your juggling,” Ellie remarks, helpfully. Not even perching on the edge of his desk or resting against a wall, there is nothing stationary or anchored about Rush Mason. So, Kannon will take the hint, right? Righ-Nooooooooo! “You saw what happened on ReV, right?” Kannon spews, the words turning his stomach. “Yeah,” is the toneless reply from Mason as he decides to begins to draw the blinds. “YouTubed it.” “You totally saw the Panda sneezing too, right?” Ellie chirps in with, as if by watching one video, he would have naturally seen the other billions. As the last blind drops, Rush flicks the air-con off and does everything other than just walk straight out the room. He’s obviously being too subtle. “Then you know what we have to do,” urges the vengeful Gold Patron Meritorious. “Fine,” exhales Rush. “On a scale of one to ten, let me know how you want this to go down… then add that number to my percentage.” There are several benefits to having Rush Mason as a manager. First, he’s 6’5 in dress-shoes and looks like he could open his briefcase by tearing the side open with his bare hands. Second, he doesn’t get people to show him the money, he gets them to show the money, the bonuses, the share of global marketing rights, the stock options, the merchandising revenues… basically, if someone could make money from his client even breathing, there’d be a payment related to a percentage of lung capacity. Third, when you’re accepted as a client, you can pretty much execute the President in his office and he’ll have you seen by 100 people at a signing the very second the trigger is pulled. “Fifteen,” Kannon snarls in answer. Mason pauses for a second… just one, before weighing up the proposal and deciding that until Rhine knocks on his door for representation, he’s got no issue. “Done,” accepts in gruff fashion as Rush Mason strides to one of his cabinets, slides the top drawer open, and reaches up inside, retrieving an envelope. “I’ve a ‘friend’ who works as an independent quality control supervisor for testing labs, deals with pretty much every wrestling promotion that wants to cut the juice out. Beauty of it, his job is to show up anywhere without notice.” Clutching the envelope tightly between his thumb and index finger, Mason displays it to his scheming client. “You get one call to this number,” Mason relays the terms of the deal. “48 hour notice.” Kannon snatches the envelope away like it were a golden ticket, almost breathing in the corrupt power it gives him over Rhine. “You realise this is highly illegal, unethical and… well, down right cold, right?” III - Guilty Until A Judge Orders Me To Put ‘Allegedly’ In Front Of Guilty This is an office with a view. With the temperatures lifting themselves for summer, Torres Wilson reclines under cover of a street cafes umbrella, watching the blessing of global warming bring about a skin-show he’d normally have to make sure he had a wallet full of singles to orchestrate. “Would you like anything else, Sir,” asks the waitress, her accent and attire packing a subtle Gallic hint. “Hmmm,” Torres ponders, licking his top lip. “I could seriously go for a cupcake.” Keeping an eye out for a passer-by worthy of his attention, wrestling’s Ultimate Insider returns to feverishly tapping away at his keyboard, quenching the insatiable appetites of the wrestling fans longing to know about Caesar Vega’s suspension. He neither confirms nor denies that the man known to millions as Nova is a Skrull. “Gossip Girl,” greets him with all the warmth of a liquid-nitrogen popsicle. “Mind if I take a seat.” Having progressed through his dishevelled phase, a shaved and slickened Xavier Kannon drops down opposite Torres Wilson, more than a lingering hint of resentment for Wilson’s premature ‘outing’ of him at UltraViolence in the air. “Sorry, Big K,” Torres dismisses the Gold Patron Meritorious without even looking up from his keyboard. “Inside The Tights is booked up for the next two months, oh, and you already used Gossip Girl once, at least think of a few and rotate.” “Oh, I’m not here to book a TV slot,” Kannon reveals, an all-too-memorable smirk growing across his lips. “This is a more, how do I put this, personal project… and if you feel inclined to join me, I have enough money ready to be wired to your account to pay for your site’s bandwidth until the sun goes supernova.” And with that, Torres Wilson’s interest is perked, although coupled with some mild concern. “That’s not going to be too soon, right?” he probes with a mocking belief that Kannon would somehow know. “I just paid next month’s rent already.” “I’ll take that as you being in,” Kannon reads into Wilson‘s quip. Torres closes his laptop, which roughly translates to serious business discussion time. But, before that can begin, Ellie skips out to their table, tongue peeling the ledge of frosting away from a pink cupcake. “I both bitterly hate you yet feel a need to applaud you in equal measure,” Wilson sighs with a shake of the head. “Now, do tell.” As Ellie perches on his knee and continues toying with her cupcake, a glimmer of mischief crosses Kannon’s eyes. “I want to ruin Jonathan Rhine.” “Like, so ruin him,” Ellie chimes in with, taking great delight in every word. Drumming his fingers on the table, Torres bounces his gaze back and forth between the pair, even the blind able to read the vengeful looks on their faces. “Now, the vibe I’m getting is that this will be a ‘gloves off’ kind of deal,” probes he of the Dirty Sheet. “If so, then ruin him you will.” With a quick cocking of the eyebrows, Torres swings his laptop around and flips it back open, double-clicking a desktop link to open a portfolio. “We have your basic minor indiscretion rumours, DUIs, soft drugs and the li-” “I’ve got the drugs angle covered,” Kannon discloses with assuredness dripping off the tongue. “Multiple attack angles,” beams Torres, nodding in the kind of appreciation that only a man in his line of work can. “Nice. I’m guessing we can skip over the more ‘intermediate’ stuff too, because I feel I’ve got myself a couple here who’re wanting something off the top shelf.” The air around them almost blackens with sleaze, dirt tarnishing every word. “And, in this day and age, there are only two words that anyone cares about on the internet…” “Sneezing Panda,” Ellie holds out hope for. “…Sex Tape,” the seedy new PRIMEate flicks off his tongue. “So we leak that there’s a Jonata-” “I’m gonna have to stop you there, Red,” Torres slams on the breaks, holding a pair of pausing hands up to the Gold Patron Meritorious. “You might be highly ranked amateurs, but you’re employing the services of a pro here. We don’t say there’s a sex tape, we say that Rhine says there isn’t a sex tape.” “Um,” Ellie stalls as she visibly tries to trace the path. “Won’t he, like, then say that he so totally didn’t say there wasn’t a sex tape?” “Exactly!” exclaims Torres, jabbing his pen down onto the table. “Double negative, and thus, there is one… and the New Life will pretty much kiss goodbye to his Home Life. Oh, that was good.” “Wow…” gushes Ellie, in awe. “This is, like, your job?” Torres answers with a smug ‘I’m the king, behbee’ nod. “That is so totally fetch.” The smugness of Wilson’s face softens out a little. “Sorry, still never going to make it happen.” “Damnzzies,” Ellie curses. Reaching over to grab the wheel, Kannon sharply steers the conversation back on course. “So we’re good to go, then?” “Oh, we’re good to go,” confirms Torres, resting back in his chair and rubbing his hands together in anticipation. “By the time this is through, we’ll have dug enough dirt to bury him in the hole that’s left.” Sensing they’re on the same very, very low wavelength, Kannon lets out a sinister, nasal chuckle. “That’s the plan.” IV - Talk Is Not Cheap His name is William S. Powell. He’s good at talking. VERY good at talking. “Was prohibition really that wrong?” his platinum tongue delivers, every microsecond of added emphasis pitch perfect to the ears of his audience. “Look at our country today, ladies and gentlemen. Every effort this nation has ever made to nuclear disarmament will never save as many lives as banning the production and sale of alcohol.” His booming, authoritative tone fills the room to every corner, shaking the cobwebs loose. Their ears saturated by his words, the audience have no choice but to think every word he says is gospel… there isn’t one iota of doubt in even one syllable. “So I leave you with one final thought,” Powell concludes, eyes sweeping the room to connect with each and every one of them. “If I were to tell you tomorrow that I’d invented a new drug that would lower people’s inhibitions or trigger violent rage, that I’d found distributors on every main street in this country and that everyone in this nation from Priests to crack addicts would be sitting in their homes consuming it… would you ask for a glass, or head to the nearest port by midnight? I bid you good day.” He’d pretty much likened every prim and proper audience member to drug addicts, yet he still leaves to a standing ovation. After a few polite, near-motionless waves to a few key attendees, Powell exits from the stage through a passageway of curtains, emerging the other side with a hip-flask clamped between his lips. “That was quite the show,” compliments him from the shadows. “You should see me at an Abortion forum,” Powell entices, dropping the flask back into his pocket. “I got the two sides mixed up once and still got a standing ovation.” The ex-wrestler once dubbed ‘The Master Debater’ (or by the fans, the cruder, less witty ‘Complete Wanker’) recognises the voice of Xavier Kannon without even needing to take a peek for confirmation. “I heard you’d found a new use for that mouth of yours,” remarks the Gold Patron Meritorious as he emerges from the shadows. “It’s all about jumping on the bandwagon before everyone else,” Powell coolly reveals, no desire to keep it a secret. “Anti-tobacco is old news, nobody will touch firearms until Heston has been in the ground a few more months, so you move onto the next thing people enjoy. If they enjoy it enough, I’ll convince them it’ll kill them within a year. Quick hint… ignore anything you hear about caffeine in the next few months. If I drop Starbuck’s US turnover by 5%, I can pretty much retire.” Piling leftover literature into his briefcase, the former half of Politically Incorrect doesn’t seem the least bit surprised by the sudden appearance of an ex-colleague he hasn’t seen for nearly 5 years. “I’m in, by the way,” explains why. “You are?” a curious Kannon bounces back, eyes narrowing in curiosity. “Well, let’s see,” begins Powell’s path to the conclusion. “You’re back in the business…and not just ‘back’, but BACK. Only you just got what the kids today would call ‘owned’, so right now, knowing you, you’re pretty much planning to get revenge by destroying everything around them, then everything about them, and then, if there’s any amusement left in it, you’ll pick them off. Stables mean too many knives to watch from over your shoulder, so I’d hazard a guess that you’re enlisting a few ’associates’ to help. Smart money is you’ve had Mason on side since you even thought about returning, probably someone new you haven‘t screwed over too, and then you‘re figuring once you start all this, you‘re going to need some pretty convincing public statements… and that brings you to me, rounding off the team.” “And here I am,” grins Kannon, offering a nod of approval. “How far we taking it,” Powell casually asks, tossing one of his bags for Kannon to carry. “Stombringer,” sums it up in one word. “Ouch,” is the instant reaction. “Complete and utter career implosion accelerated by mental breakdown… Rhine must have really gotten under your skin.” “He wrecked the most glorious moment of my life,” Kannon seethes, tossing the bag back into Powell’s chest. “Now I make sure he feels that everyday of his life.”
