Lord of the Manor, King of the Treetops

13 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov 13th, 2007 There was a loud bang as the door slammed behind Jack Harmen on his makeshift bedroom. His television rocked and swayed from the vibrations left behind by earthquake Kate Young, formerly Young-Harmen. "I have half a mind to change the last name of our son to Young you know!" The Neighborhood Lunatic's mouth dropped, arms extended in a pleading position. "You can't do that! Then his name would be an Arabian Battle Cry. A-LOC-AH-YUNG!" Flyer shrieked, tongue protruding from his lips. "I mean, you ever give George Bush the power to wield detention camps and our son's the first to go. Do you really want that on your conscience Kate?" Kate crossed her arms, narrowing her eyes in fury, never departing their lock with Flyer. She began to tap the soul of her shoe against the hardwood floor. "If you're trying to stare through me, you'd be best to just," Flyer ducked to the side, "move, ever so slightly." Flyer twisted over his shoulder to look behind himself. "I'm sorry, was I blocking view of a clock?" "Jack?" Kate breathed heavily; her cheeks flushed red from depleted anger. "You promised me." She rolled her eyes. "And I swear, it seems every time I trust your promise that you wind up turning me into a fool. And I've never been comfortable feeling like the fool in a relationship with a man who once threw a bowling ball up into a ceiling fan because he didn't understand physics!" "You try predicting the effects of gravity without an experiment!" Flyer shouted in defense. Kate continued, unnerved, "Who believed that his flu induced best friend turned into a bunch of gerbils and POGS!" Flyer gasped. "You can't tell me magic isn't real! It's SCIENCE turned IGNORANT!" Kate stamped her foot, "Who believed SO much that his father was living his life vicariously through you because you won the World Title for ONLY five minutes." Kate's jaw now dropped. "That you kicked me in the FACE!" "Hey! That never aired!" Flyer shouted. "You're totally breaking kayfabe!" Kate sighed, falling to the couch in Flyer's room defeated. The silence hung there like metaphorical damp dew that hadn’t been cleaned in months. Kate however, would never let such mold grow in this house naturally, she thought as she swayed her legs slightly over the couches edge. An aroma then flew up her nostrils, and she contorted her eyes in confusion for moment. She mouthed to Flyer, "You're smoking?" before lowering her gaze into the corner of Jack's room. "Uh," Flyer stuttered, leaning down slightly and pulling at his collar to smell his corduroy jacket, "Not anymore." "Oh," Kate said, staring into the distance. She twirled a hair pin in her fingers as she did. She had no reason to have this hair pin, but seeing it twirl through her elegant fingers gave her a sense of calm. So much so that she didn’t notice the Lunatic falling beside her on the couch, and place his arm onto her shoulder. She certainly felt it, flinching slightly, enough to force Flyer to pull his hand completely away. “So,” Flyer muttered, “how’s this gonna work?” “I don’t know,” Kate said, shoulders slouched, “I mean, I just know we can’t be fighting for power over the control of our children’s lives…” She rose to her feet. ”And we certainly can’t be trying to control one another’s...” She paced, “We have to learn how to get along again. How to function, even without the,” She lowered her head slightly, attempting to speak before raising her head to the Lunatic’s eye line, “the attraction.” “Oh great, first the Writer’s Guild of America strikes and now you don’t find me attractive anymore.” Flyer rolled his eyes. “This week’s SUCKED! I swear to God that I’m gonna kill that gypsy.” “Jack, if I can’t trust someone, I can’t love them… certainly can’t live with them.” Kate sighed, again. She was very close to contorting her face forever in such a sigh if she were to continue. “And you promised you’d stay in the North East. That,” she began to speak in a deeper voice to mock him, “Oh Kate! Don’t worry! No brand Wrestling will be lucky to get to Maine!” Kate’s eyes bulged. “But then you go and join PRIME?! AND BEHIND MY BACK none the less!” Flyer raised his hand to quell her rage. “First off, I didn’t join Prime, I joined their Tournament. It’s called the Jewel in the Crown tournament.” Flyer’s eyes sparkled, as if an idea had taken him over. “AND! Second off, I thought that if I won this Jewel in the Crown tournament, I could take the jewel and win back your affections.” “What would you do with the crown?” “Obviously I would keep it and pretend I was king until I was bored with it.” Flyer contorted his eyes in thought. “And then I’d let Tony have a turn, but force him to do so in the midst of downtown Philadelphia or New York. And then, if Tony somehow survives without having blood protruding from all his orifices, AND brings back the crown… I just give it to Allocca as an early Christmas gift, or belated. Depending on if Tony Davis is beaten up, robbed, and then he extracts vengeance on his persecutors and reclaims what is rightfully his, but then immediately shall become my sons because it’s actually mine.” Flyer pulled out a note pad. “This would make an awesome movie for someone like Jackie Chan, I swear.” Kate rolled her eyes, and stormed out of the room quite quickly. This left the Lunatic pondering alone in a metaphorical cloud of dust. “Uhhh… did Jackie Chan kick you in the face too?” Jack shouted out after her. ** Later that evening, the Harmen family sat down to dinner. At the head of one end of the table resided Tony Davis, the true matriarch and idea man behind this wonderful living arrangement. He was a large man, the current tag team partner of the Lunatic in no brand wrestling. They had wrestled around the world together, and had become as close as brothers before Tony married into the family. Tammy Davis sat by his side, holding her husband’s hand. The ring felt strangely cold in the autumn air to Tammy, and that struck a nerve with her. Not only had the ring been sending little chills up her arm, she’d been on edge ever since Kate and Jack had entered the same house with one another. Tony wasn’t her first man, and their arguments reminded her of her first failure at married life. She nervously shook her foot underneath the dining room table, hoping not to repeat both hers and their mistakes. On the other side of Tammy rested the one and half year old Veronica Harmen. She sat coyly on the edge of her high chair, staring intently toward her father. Since Jack returned home, Veronica hadn’t taken her eyes off of him whenever they remained in the same room. She had moppish brown hair that had barely grown to her earlobes, as she sloppily fed herself with her spoon. Next to her was Allocca Harmen, Jack & Kate’s first child of six years old. He stared at his food, pushing jello around, to the point where it fell off his plate and stained his Transformer’s t-shirt. “It’s okay,” High Flyer said, standing from his end of the dining room table, the other head of the table. He reached to his side and pulled his napkin, before dabbing it onto Allocca’s shirt to clean it. “No worries.” “No worries?” Kate said, sitting next to Tammy with her arms crossed. “You’ve got to tell them something.” Allocca stared up with his glossy familiar eyes, “Daddy, what’s going on?” “Oh,” Jack shrugged his shoulders. “I’m just going to be on the television twice a week now, y’know. At least for the next month or so. Or perhaps less even. I dunno. Daddy’s potential boss people don’t tell daddy anything to keep him on a short leash.” Veronica Harmen immediately began to cry. She may not have known a lot of words, but she understood the tone of Jack’s voice to understand he was leaving. “You got another job?” Davis said, ears perked up. He chomped on a piece of steak as he awaited the reply fervently. Tony had only gotten employed with no brand Wrestling through the hands of Jack, and surely he wished not to be left behind. “No,” Flyer said, running his hands through his now short white hair. “I just… I joined a tournament, alright? You get a small paycheck, I fight this guy named Tsunami and I get on with my life after he beats my ass, okay?” Flyer rolled his eyes, “It’s not a job, it’s a gig. It’s a favor. There’s a lot of people I owe favors to in this business to be where I am today guys, to have a place like this to live in!” Flyer said, thrusting out his arms to point to their various fancy decorations and high ceilings. “We get residuals. The High Flyer and Team VIAGRA name gets national exposure, and everyone wins.” “Except your children,” Kate snipped, not bothering to look up from their plate. Flyer dropped his shoulders, eyes closed. “Man. I’m just gonna go,” He sighed. “Tony? Wanna road trip to Montreal?” “DO I!?!” Tony exclaimed excited. He looked to Kate, who stared with a furious anger back at him. Tammy sat, arms crossed, shaking her head. “Do I?” Tony asked, less forcefully this time. “Uhmm… I don’t know if I do.” Tony said, this time to Jack. “Well,” Flyer kissed Allocca on the forehead, rubbing his raggish hair. After, he kissed Veronica on her forehead. She however, clinched on for a hug that she wouldn’t let go. “If you want to go Tony, call me in an hour. Honey!” Flyer tried to push Veronica off slightly, “Daddy has to go.” Crying, Veronica slowly loosened her grip. Jack reached into the corner of the dining room, and grabbed a dufflebag full of his wrestling equipment. Kate charged after Flyer as he went to leave. “You exit that house and you and me are DONE!” Flyer’s eyes narrowed. “Didn’t you say that three years ago?” His eyes twitched. “Aren’t we done NOW?!” And with that, he slammed the door behind him. At the dining room table, an awkward silence fell. Allocca continued to play with his food as Veronica let out some quiet sobs. Kate and Tammy both sat with their arms crossed. Tony stared toward the door, and then back to his plate, and then back toward the door. After, he quickly grabbed Tammy’s attention. “Can I please go wi-AIIEEEEE!” Davis said, screaming at that moment and holding onto his foot. He yelled back at Tammy, “Why are you wearing heels to an interior dinner! No one can even see em!”
