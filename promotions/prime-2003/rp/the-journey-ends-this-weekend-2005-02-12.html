The Journey Ends This Weekend

12 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-12-05 “Athena…. Listen honey, I’m on my way to the airport. I’ve got a few days to fly home before having to be in Pittsburgh for the show before Culture Shock.” Boda walks through the parking garage of the Raleigh Sports Arena, talking on his cell phone as he nods to the man standing in front of a Cadillac Escalade stretch limo. The man opens the door for the #1 contender to The Universal Championship. “Okay, meet me at Orlando International in a few hours, okay? Good. Love ya…..” Boda hangs the phone up and slides into the limo. As the door shuts, his cell phone rings. Looking at the caller ID, it reads ‘PRIVATE CALLER’. “Hello?” “Well, Well…..” a deep and dark voice answers on the other end. “Seems your career is on fire as of late. A title shot in two weeks time. Well, let’s just see how ‘hot’ you can get……….” The phone call ends. But before Boda can hang up, he looks out the window at his right, and jumps back as a car crashes into the door, coming to a dead stop. Boda is quick to turn to exit the opposite door when he dives forward in an attempt to avoid another car that crashes into the driver’s side rear door. “WHAT THE FUCK” Boda screams. He looks out the tinted windows that aren’t blocked by two cars, and sees Tiamat and Dani Furher getting out of the driver’s seats of each car. “Those mother fuckers.” Boda turns to look through the privacy glass, at the driver. But as he does, all he catches is a glimpse of the chauffer being yanked out of the driver’s seat. Then Boda sees a figure step onto the hood of the Cadillac Escalade limo. “Son of a bitch…..” Boda curses to himself as Vampir steps onto the hood. Boda tries in vain to escape, but the glass won’t give as he kicks at it. Suddenly the limo is engulfed in flames, as Vampir looks through the windshield, watching Boda struggle to escape………….. A light comes on as Chris Sloboda sits up quickly in his bed. Sweat pouring from his head. He grabs the back of his neck, as if to get his bearings. Another light come on, as Athena is awoken also. “Had that nightmare again, hun?” Athena turns over to look at her man. She places her head against his chest. The bandages of his recent run in with Vampir cover his arms and left pectoral area. Chris slides over, sitting on the edge of the bed. He looks at the alarm clock on the table. 3:30 am Chris picks up a glass from the end table. Taking a sip of water, he nods his head to Athena’s question. “Yeah. It’s the same fucking nightmare I’ve had every night since Vampir tried to end my life in that limo. All because my girls outlasted his curse, all because I get to wrestle for the Universal Title. Vampir can’t stand my success…. And he tried to take me out once and for all……” Athena slides over next to Chris. “Babe…. I hate to say it… but maybe you shouldn’t go to Japan.” Athena knew it wasn’t the right thing to say, but she has a valid reason. After all, Vampir has made Chris’ life a living hell ever since his debut in PRIME. And now, he came really close to ending Chris’ life. Had it not been for the quick response of the local fire department, Athena could have lost not only her lover, but also the father of her unborn child. So indeed, she has anxieties and doubts about Chris going to Culture Shock, and not only facing two men for The Universal Championship…. But least she forget the heinous Dual Halo match, that can indeed end a career… or two. “I’m going to go take some pain medication. I’ll be back in a bit. Go back to sleep, and don’t worry about me….. I’ll be fine at Culture Shock.” Chris stands up, taking the glass of water with him as he leaves the bedroom. Athena lays down, turning off her light and falls back to sleep. Chris walks quietly through the house, following the glow of the light he leaves on in the kitchen. The big man stops in the living room, and stares out the bay windows that overlook Lake Windermere. Chris seems lost in thought as he gazes upon the reflection of the moon. Maybe she’s right….. Maybe I should stay home, pass up this shot to become The Universal Champion. Maybe I should let Vampir have his way, and finally prove that he’s the better man…… No, no… what am I thinking? I’ve fought long and hard. He’s tried to take my kids away from me, and I persevered, and over came that. He tried to take me out of this business, but the wounds in my wrists healed. And dammit, if two fucking holes in my wrist can heal and allow me to wrestle the match of my career at ReVolution 50, and allow me to become the #1 contender for The Universal Championship, well dammit man……. But what if Athena is right. What if I do go to Japan, what if I don’t beat Black Angel or Killean for the belt. What if Vampir finally ends not only my career, but my life, at Culture Shock….. And shit… what about The Dual Halo….. He’ll have every fucking opportunity to finish me off there. Chris shakes his head and rubs his eyes. He continues his trek to the kitchen, in search of his pain medication. Obviously, the stress of the upcoming pay per view is getting to him, not only physically… well, the physical stress has a lot to do with the firebomb he survived almost two weeks ago, but definitely mentally. Vampir has always been a master of mind games, and going into the biggest PPV of his career, Boda is definitely wrapped up in what can only be described as a supreme mind game. The big man slumbers into the kitchen, flicking on the main light. As the room brightens up, Chris shuts his eyes, the intensity of light awaking the dark is to much for the Hall of Famer. After a few moments, Chris is able to open his eyes. It is now, that we fully see the effects of the stress on PRIME’s biggest hero. The bloodshot eyes, the drawn out look, the bags under his eyes, the unshaven look. Yes, this isn’t the Boda we see week in and week out. This is a man that’s under pressure. Chris opens the refrigerator, and reaches in to pull out a gallon of milk. He shut the door, and steps to the side, where the counter meets the fridge. Reaching up, Chris takes a glass from the cupboard, and fills it with the ice cold by-product of cows and pasteurization. Putting the gallon back, or more so what’s left of it, Chris reaches up on top of the fridge, pulling down a prescription bottle. The big man looks at the label. Hydrocodone , take two for pain every 4-6 hours as needed “Well, at least I’ll be able to sleep for a few hours, till the effects wear off.” Chris mumbles to himself as he takes two out of the container. Placing the bottle back up out of reach of his kids and his dog, Chris steps back over to the counter where his glass of cold milk awakes. As the big man drinks, we see a calendar on the side of the fridge, with some dates marked on it. February 9th…. 10 am flight to Japan February 13th …… I win The Universal Title, and for the hell of it…. I’m going to win The Dual Halo too…. And why not, after the shitty year I had last year, I deserve this. Okay, so those last notes run across the calendar, from the 13th till the 19th. But hey, you try to write all that in a little 1 inch by 1 inch box on a calendar. Believe me, it isn’t so easy. Of course, like most people do, the days of the month that have passed are X’d off, and according to the X’s on this calendar , the big man has a 10 am flight… today. After finishing the glass of milk, Chris places the glass in the sink, then picks up his cell phone from the counter, unplugging it from it’s charger. A few pushes of some buttons and the little communication miracle comes to life. Chris looks at the phone, then at the time on the microwave….. 4:00 am “He’ll be up, hell, it’s only 2 am in Vegas and I know he’ll be up partying.” Chris talks to himself as he dials his cell phone. WE can hear the gentle tone of the cell phone dialing. Chris waits, taking a seat on a dinner table chair. “Hello? You know what time it is?” the voice on the other answers, not as awake as Chris would have thought so. “Hey man, it’s me.” Chris speaks softly into his phone. “Dude, why you asleep?” “I got a morning flight to Japan. What about you? Don’t you have a flight to catch in the morning?” the unknown voice asks. “Yeah, 10 am. But man, I can’t sleep.” Chris slides off the chair and paces around the kitchen cooking island. “Man, we’ve been friends along time….and you know I never back down from anything….but I got to tell you…. Man….I’m kind of having second thoughts about attending Culture Shock.” “Why man….. I don’t get it…. So you were in a limo that was set on fire. Chris, I’ve known you long enough, to know that something like that, doesn’t scare you off to easy.” the voice on the other end tries to comfort Boda. “Chris, come on, don’t let what’s happened to you the past 5 months or so keep you down man, that’s so not like you.” “I know man, but come on….” Chris slumbers across the kitchen and into the living room again. “I’ve got the biggest match of my career upcoming. And Vampir isn’t happy that he’s not involved. He’s tried to kill me for fucks sake. Of course I’m having some inhibitions about going. Besides, you know Athena is worried sick about me. I’m far from one hundred percent. And not only do I have to beat two men to become The Universal Champion…. Man, then I’ve got to tangle it up with 29 other PRIME stars. She’s worried that I won’t walk out of Culture Shock…. And man…. I can’t help but feel that way too.” The big man sits on the couch in the living room, taking the remote control from the end table and flicking on the 72 inch wide screen that sits opposite the couch. As the room comes to life from the brightness of the overly large screen, Chris settles into a comfortable spot on the couch. “Chris, I’ve seen you beat 29 other men to become the PCW World Champion. I’ve seen you take on Meanstreak, Ivan Stanislav, Duke Williams and the like. And you’ve never backed down, tucked you tail or ran like a bitch. You’ve taken a hurricarana from a 20 foot stage to the hard outfield of Safeco Field. You got up and finished the match, and won too. Hell, you got suplexed from the top of a steel cage back into the ring.” The voice on the other end reminds Chris of his past matches and accomplishments. “And dude, just because you’ve got a few small burns on your chest and arms, don’t let it stop you from fulfilling your quest. Besides, you know I have your back this week. I’ll make sure it goes down without any problems from The Dark Age.” Chris stretches and yawns, the effects of the pain killers setting in. “All right man, you’re right. I’ve got nothing to worry about this weekend. This is The Year….. [yawn]…. Of …..[yawn]….Boda. And this weekend I’ll……… [double yawn]…… show everyone why it’s just that. Hey bud….man, I’m about to fall out…. These burns and the pain killers are draining me. I’ll see you in Hokkaido on Sunday.” “All right Chris, take care, have a good flight. And remember…. I’ve got your back.” Chris hangs up his cell phone, laying it on the table next to the couch. The big man stands and walks back to his bedroom. Seeing Athena lay there, asleep, brings a smile to the #1 contender’s face. Chris watches Athena as she sleeps somberly. She took a chance with me last year. She gave up everything to stand by my side. She knew Kasper wasn’t going to take her places, and she figured I was better for her. So what does it mean if I follow her advice, and stay home, forgoing my shot this weekend. I know she’s concerned, but when I win the title, that’ll elevate our relationship to the next level. As Chris continues to think about Athena and her plight to keep him safe, Boda slips into his king size bed, careful to try and not wake his woman. As soon as his head hit’s the pillows, his eyes slowly come to close. Fuck it….. My mind is made up. I don’t care if Vampir has an evil plan to finish me off. He’ll have to take a fucking number, and get in line with the other 28 stars that want to win The Dual Halo. I’m going to go to Japan, I’m going to win the Universal title, not only for myself, not only for my fans, but for Mackenzie and Carleigh, for all that overgrown bat put them through. And then I’m going to flaunt the win in Vampir’s face during The Dual Halo. And just like that, the big man’s thoughts, his world suddenly emerged in darkness, as if The Dark Age is there, in his room, watching, waiting, for the right moment to strike. ……………………………….......................................... For the first time in the past two weeks, Chris sleeps, with no nightmares, no visions of the past 6 months of hell. In fact, he sleeps so peacefully, it’s as if he, himself, has found peace. Shrouded in darkness, Boda dreams of what is to come. He envisions his match against both Killean and Black Angel in a few nights. His subconscious flirts with visions of him holding the Universal Title over his head, for the fans to see and to rejoice the coming forth of a true champion for PRIME. One by one, his mind plays off the list of those he’ll face in The Dual Halo. VampirPeter VetraBig PoppaXavier KannonThe sHIT One by one, as the names roll through his head, a smile crosses his face. Like a kid the night before Christmas dreaming of sugar plum fairies, Boda dreams of eliminating the 29 other PRIME stars, thus cementing his name in PRIME history. Suddenly, the darkness turns to light, as Boda’s slumber is interrupted by some sort of shaking. Chris rolls his eyes around, trying to get his bearings. But soon his lids shut again as the shaking has subsided. And once again, the big man’s mind opens up like damn releasing water. Peace. Finally, my mind is at rest, no nightmares, no visions of The Dark Ages. And finally, this weekend, I can extract revenge. In just a few short days, I’ll shock The Dark Ages. I’m going to win the Universal Title, something Vampir hates. And then, when The Enlight……… Once again, Chris’ eyes open as his REM thoughts are interrupted by shaking. The big man rolls his eyes once again, trying to see what was happening around him. But the PCW legend can’t keep his eyes open much longer, and once again falls back to a state of calm. First it was Aimz, beaten down in her locker room, like a 2 dollar hooker who didn’t pay her pimp. Then the attacker followed suit, taking it to Nova soon after. And because I wasn’t at ReVolution last week, my little buddy got his mouth washed out with an urinal cake. I’m the biggest hero in PRIME, yet I was unable to help these three when they needed it. After Culture Shock, I’m going to personally out the attacker, and show his identity to the world. That is my first goal, to allow Aimz, Nova and Rage to find out who attacked them, and maybe help them extract a little revenge. What the hell? For the third time, Boda is awoken by some sort of shaking. This time, Chris sits up and looks around. He’s on an airplane, which is evident by the long, cylinder like room he’s in, and the rows of seats around him. Chris looks around, but doesn’t see anything out of the ordinary. All the passengers are either asleep, or keeping to themselves. The stewardesses are busy at both ends, preparing drinks and the next meal. “Oh well, must have been turbulence.” Chris figures the only logical reason for him being shaken from his sleep three times. The big man sinks back in his seat, to fall asleep again. As he does his head rolls to the right, and he spies the source of his being awoken so many times. “Hi, my name is Tommy….. And you snore…. Loud.“ Yes, indeed, the big man has been awoken by an adolescent, of at least 12 years of age. The kid stares at Boda, the big man just looks at him, popping a smile. “Hey kid, how you doing?” Chris tries to be his cordial self. “Good…. I know who you are. You’re famous.” Tommy smiles. “I’ve seen a lot of your matches over the last 2 years. My dad told me that you used to be a great basketball player, but I didn’t believe him. I thought it was all part of a gimmick. Is it true? Huh?” “Well Tommy…. You should believe him. It’s true, I used to be a baller, but I love wrestling a lot more. So, why are you one a plane heading for Japan? And where are your parents?” Boda decides he might as well stay up, and talk to the kid, who seemingly is alone on this flight. And since Athena stayed in Orlando, it only makes sense for Chris to talk to someone, and why not this sweet, innocent looking preteen. “Well, you see…. I was on Yahoo chat, and I was talking to some Japanese girl, and fell in love with her. I ran away, and we are going to elope and live together, forever, in Japan.” Chris can’t believe what he just heard. Here’s a red headed 12 year old fan, telling him that he’s running away to Japan to get married, to some girl he met online. “Kid, are you serious?” Chris asks. Maybe, just maybe, it was the pain killers clogging his hearing. “No, I was kidding. My dad is in Japan, he’s a member of the United States Army, and has been stationed there for a few years. I’m flying over to stay with him for a few weeks. And guess what….we’re going to Culture Shock, and I’m going to watch you beat Black ‘FUCK FACE’ Angel to become the Universal Champion.” Tommy smiles. Chris can’t believe what he just heard. “You just yelled out fuck face…. Are you okay?” “Sure.” Tommy says. “I’m good. I’ll be even better when you climb into the Dual Halo match and eliminate a ton of guys. Heck, I want to see you eliminate Vampir ‘SHITFUCKER’ Nosferatu, Dani ‘ASS PIRATE’ Furher and First ‘SPERM BURPER’ Blood…….” Chris quickly covers the kids mouth, as other passengers nearby stare in their direction. “Kid…. I hate those guys too, but it’s not nice to call them those nasty names.” Chris shakes his head in disbelief of what he’s hearing. A tap to his left shoulder, Chris turns to see a stewardess standing in the aisle. “Sir, I’m sorry, is little Tommy bothering you?” The pretty blonde asks. “No, he’s cool, but he cusses worse than JTF when he lost his 5 Star Title.” Chris makes a personal reference, apparently one the stewardess doesn’t understand. “Excuse me? I don’t get it. But any how, young Tommy here has Tourette Syndrome.” “Oh… so he’s not cursing on purpose, is he?” Chris smiles. “Okay, miss…. I’ll be okay with him. I’ll watch over him for the rest of the flight.” Chris watches as the stewardess turns and walks away. The legend turns his attention back to Tommy. “So… you poor poor kid… got some uncontrollable disease, that causes you tooo……” Chris watches as the stewardess disappears to the rear of the plane. “Okay, listen, you’re a fan, so lets say we play a little game of word association…. Tourette Syndrome version. I’ll mention someone from the Dual Halo match, and you tell me your thoughts on him or her. Okay?” “Sure, sounds like fun.” Tommy sinks back in his seat, copying the big man. “Let’s play.” “Fine, here we go…..”Chris clears his throat. “Tony Rolo” “Legendary ‘HAMSTER FUCKER‘ wrestler” Chris laughs, then continues. “Aimz” “Great ‘SLUTY BITCH’ female wrestler” Another chuckle from the big man. “Hoyt Williams” “Son of ‘TOUCHES BOYS PENISES’ God.” Chris almost falls out of his seat. “Okay, okay… those were good, but what do you think of Karina Wolfenden?” “Tall, powerful ‘DIRTY SKANK’ woman.” “Dammit, this is fun.” Chris laughs harder. “Emilio Rage” “The Ragester ‘EATS URINAL CAKES’ owns.” “Hey, he’s a friend, but dammit, that was funny.” Chris looks around to see if anyone is starring or making a complaint to a stewardess. “Okay, one more, because kid… I can’t take to much more, you’re making my burns hurt from laughter. Tyler Lopez.” “Who?” Tommy smiles. “Ladies and gentlemen…. Please buckle your seatbelts. We will be approaching Hokkaido Airport now. Thank you for flying Air Sushi, serving Japan none stop since 2000.” A voice, hopefully that of the pilot, comes over the loud speakers, interrupting Boda’s little name game. “Tommy…. How long I been asleep?” Chris asks his little flight mate. “Well, we left Orlando about 18 hours ago…. So I’d say you been SLEEPING LIKE A LAZY FUCKER for at least 16 hours.” Tommy buckles up. “Who you calling a lazy….. Wait, that’s right, you can’t help it. Sorry kid.” Chris pats Tommy on the head. “Actually, I really meant it. You’ve been sleeping for a long time, and every time I tried to wake you up to talk to you, you ignored me and went back to sleep.” “Well, kid…. Let me tell you something…. I’ve got burns on my chest and my arms, from Vampir’s ruthless attacks and….. Wait…. Why the hell am I justifying myself to a kid? Tommy, thanks for the laughs. Have fun in Japan with your dad, and remember….. Cheer for me when I do the impossible this weekend, and win not only The Universal Title, but the Dual Halo also.” The scene pans out to a shot of the plan landing at the airport in Japan. So there we have it, the end of a journey of one man, reaching for the biggest prize in the land. It’s been a long strange trip to get to Culture Shock. From his debut last May against Hoyt Williams, to passing out at The Great American Nightmare and not tapping out to Vampir’s finisher, to watching as his little girls overcome the curse Vampir saddled them with. And now, in a few days, justice will be served on a cold platter. One man will win The Universal Championship, one man will win The Dual Halo. For Chris Sloboda, he’s betting it all on him being that one man. And so should you.
