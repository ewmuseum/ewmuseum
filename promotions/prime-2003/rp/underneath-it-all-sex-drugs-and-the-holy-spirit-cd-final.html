Date: Today ''I didn't mean to kick you in the mouth...'' ''If Jesus existed,'' Jeph mumbled over a swollen lip, ''He would've struck you down for that.'' Amy sighed and swerved around on the first-row pew of the large chapel they sat in, ''If Jesus existed, he'd give you cancer for putting out a cigarette against the crucifix, so don't give me that crap.'' While Jeph cringed, nursing his headache, a sly expression came over Amy and she slid two Vicodin tablets from her pocket and pressed them to penetrate her dry lips before his eyes opened. She swallowed, and the second subtle phase of feeding was through with. They'd searched the entire church, and the only window with a latch wouldn't have fit either of them by a longshot - not to mention that they couldn't reach it in the basement of the cathedral...oh, and it was locked - so the chances of their escape being that evening were growing increasingly slim. They'd finally accepted defeat, it seemed, and took to relaxing in the stiff wooden pews, Jeph nursing his bleeding lip with a sleeve and watching Amy casually flipping through a Bible as if it was the latest edition of Cosmo. ''What a bore,'' she sighed and closed the book with an idea, ''Hey...'' Jeph stirred. He'd halfway fallen asleep, staring up at the ceiling with all possible thoughts in mind...''Zuh?'' ''Let's make use of this time. We can figure out the things we've always been too scared to ask one another.'' ''What?'' he jolted, ''Uh, there's nothing you don't know about me.'' ''Bullshit.'' Amy took the first turn without any alarm, ''Are you gay?'' ...If nothing else, she was blunt. Blunt, and causing her friend to scramble. ''I...what the hell, Amy? I've had girlfriends!'' Jeph was a tall, generally attractive guy. At 6'2', with tattoo sleeves down his arms and collared to his neck, he was a sight to see. His hair was cut unevenly, leaving a bang that hung nearly to his chin on one side with a stud on each edge of his lower lip, tucked in among a beard of stubble he kept constantly groomed. He wasn't hard on the eyes, and could've had almost any girl - or guy, for that matter - but he'd been biding his time in hopes of one in particular. Unfortunately, she made herself incredibly unavailable by getting caught up in all the wrong things...and men. On top of that, his friendship with his love was something almost holy. And she wasn't interested anyway, right? She also wasn't waiting for his answer. ''Well? You've only ever had, like, three girls. That's an easy cover, y'know.'' ''I don't like men.'' ''You don't have to like them to like sleeping with them,'' she teased, ''Just look at me!'' Jeph trailed off slightly. He WAS looking at her, almost constantly in fact... ''I...yeah...I know that. But sexually or, uh, whatever...I'm only open to girls - especially after seeing guys fuck you over all the time. Guess I figure I might as well be the only nice guy on the planet, then maybe....some girl would...uh, be thankful for having me around, I guess. Let's just lay off, okay?'' ''Jeez, fine.'' He shifted nervously and decided to exact revenge, ''How long have you gone without a boyfriend? Like, three days?'' ''I haven't dated anybody since spring of last year, thanks.'' ''That's not counting people you've slept w---'' Amy's brow furrowed and the eyes that Hoyt Williams claimed made her the daughter of the devil shot Jeph a nearly satanic stare as she cut him off, ''Everybody makes mistakes, okay?'' ''You make a whole lot more than anybody else, it seems.'' This was growing heated. Amy had ventured a bit too close to getting Jeph admitting his feelings, and he was already testy thanks to their inescapable situation. When he fired back, it was entirely in defense of his hidden emotions...she didn't know that, though, and this looked horribly blatant. The little redhead stood up swiftly, ''Are you seriously saying this? What the hell is wrong with you?'' ''Just...damnit, Amy,'' he sighed, ''I'm sorry, okay? Just sick of seeing you get screwed around because you're constantly looking for some mythical love in all the wrong places when it's right in front of you and always has been - through pain-killers, cocaine, E, EVERYTHING - and you've always been too busy looking afar to see what's right in front of you.'' Amy blinked, having been suddenly slapped off of her own defensive stance, ''What?'' Jeph sighed once more. This time, he was very visibly nervous - shaking, even. ''Damnit,'' he began, ''Are you that clueless?'' ''I...'' Amy was interrupted - not to mention surprised - when Jeph leaned in to kiss her gently. The surprise in both of them rose tenfold when she began to kiss back just as tenderly, not pausing for a breath until... 'Ouch!' Jeph yelped, suddenly withdrawing and rubbing his noticeably bloody lip with cheeks growing red of embarassment, ''Err...bad time for that move, huh?'' ''Um, yeah,'' Amy grinned meekly. She was bashful for the very first time in her life, but it couldn't have lasted long. A dizziness came about her, a fraction of a second before darkness took over... The morning after... It was one of those days when you couldn't get out of bed... quite literally. Initially, Amy spent her first attempt trying to pry her eyelids open and followed it shortly by writhing underneath the sheets. ''Welcome to earth,'' came Jeph's familliar voice from his seat in front of her computer, ''Sobered up yet?'' He'd turned around to look her in the eye from across the room, his eyes speaking everything. He knew she'd drugged up at that church...that much was obvious, since she'd passed out. Thankfully, the morning janitor they encountered had been nice enough to accept that she was simply sleeping long enough for Jeph to drag her to the car and into bed. Judging by his expression, he wasn't feeling so generous anymore and she was about to get an earfull... Instead of answering to the would-be barrage of 'Why'd you give in?' questions, she simply fell back to sleep and avoided confronting any of the issues the last night had presented. That's the Campbell way of coping, and nobody was going to change it.
