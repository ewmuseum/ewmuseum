Where did I go wrong? (Break Up Series Part I)

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27 The sky is dismal the clouds are dark, showing the chance of rain. The darkened skies are broken by the red and blue flashing lights coming off the top of several squad cars. Their spotlights shining in surrounding yards trying to find someone or something. Just a few hundred yards up the road sits a battered and torn woman, leaning against a tree where she had just been 'assaulted' moments earlier. One of the cop cars stop alongside and see the fallen blonde bombshell, the officer tries to consult her and take care of any other problems, while the others look for the other person involved. The police office consulting the girl hears over the two way radio on his uniform that the other person has been apprehended down the road near the scene of the 'crime'. Now are standing in front of a well built three stall garage, a window is missing from the first stall with it's glass spread all over the ground. There I stood with my arms spread against the wall, both parents standing behind me, my mother in tears as Patrolman O'Rouke searches me and takes my switch blade knife from my left pocket along with all my other personal items including my identification. There he stood looking over my licensce drilling me about my past record and what happened tonight. I stood there legs spread feeling ashamed, why did this happen? 'Mr. Vetra you have the right to remain silent, anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law. You are allowed a lawyer if you can't afford a lawyer the state will appoint you one....' I sat there in tears along with my parents who were standing behind me. I listened as he read me my rights and then he slapped the cuffs on my hands. He then turned me around and made me face the wall not allowing me any contact with an other human being, torture is one thing, but this is mental torture, but this was just the beginning. I was then put into the back of the cop car, sat there alone in silence looking out the window trying to figure what was going on in my life at that moment. Drinking that evening might have been a bad mistake, hell going to the party probably was the wrong move. I knew I didn't want to go for a reason. While I sat in the back of the cop car I was able to replay most of the evening in my head, some parts were missing but I tried to piece it altogether so I could try and make some sense of what had happened. Saturday June 11th 2005 12:00 am (About) A simple night was expected, not too many problems but something I knew I did not want to attend but was being forced to, a party for my friend. She only turns twenty-one once I guess. The way there Melissa and I were normal, well as normal as we had been in the last couple of monthes of being boyfriend and girlfriend. The party itself was rather boring people I didn't know and some people I just didn't feel like hanging out with, I sat there drinking down my beers watching the time slowly drift by. The night was rather pointless but Melissa and the rest were off having fun. The time finally started getting close to midnight and I was rather tired after my long day of working out and I approached Melissa as she was chatting with her new found friends. I ask her to leave and she said she was not ready just yet so I let it go and we agreed on after the next drink it would be time to go. During that time I went and spoke with my 'best friend.' After telling him repeatly that I was tired and such he understood and understood why I was tired. After telling me numerous times that he was my best friend I approached Melissa again hoping this time she was ready to leave. This time I was expecting ok fine we can leave but that was the least of what was about to happen. I sit here trying not to remember all the details but I remember a fight ensued in front of this crowd of people some of which I know some of which I do not know. To be honest I really didn't care I was drunk, tired and just wanted to go home. After getting in a huge fight in public we finally get into the car where an even larger fight ensues. The whole way home we argue, this is nothing new. Whenever either of get really drunk this type of thing happens, do I expect it? No, do I want it to happen? No. But the fact of the matter it seems to be a trend. Another thing that seems to be apart of this trend is whenever we get in a fight or whenever she gets depressed she threatens suicide, something to me which is serious as she is not taking her own life but something from everyone she has ever touched or spoken with. That was something I was always worried about when got into a fight, but that was not he only thing I had to worry about her doing something she had only told me about, that is cutting herself. So I thought I was doing the right thing here and not allowing her to leave my presence after this fight after the fear she would not see the morning. I made the wrong moves though, I went about it the wrong way. So we get out of the truck, both of us angry at each other. She tries to leave I would not allow her as I was afraid she would do something she would regret. She pushes me trying to make me move, I push her back. This is where I made my fatal move, I touched a female. She gets a little more pushy with me, I decide not to push her back but set my fist through the closest window shattering it and leaving pieces of glass in my knuckles, my palm and blooding dripping all over my clothing and surrounding areas. I thought I made a smart move by not hitting the girl, I was so wrong. She finally gets away, there she goes running down the street I made another fatal or stupid mistake, I chased her. After running a few hundred feet I catch up to the fleeing girl, I corner her into a tree slowly I walk towards her, with each one of my steps she back peddles another. No more then five steps she hits the tree, not from me pushing her but me back peddling her into the tree not allowing her to get away. This is where I hear the words I never wanted to hear, 'He has a knife in his pocket.' Now why would I be afraid of those words? It is simple that meant she was on the phone with the cops. I ran I did not know what else to do, I ran into the night trying toget away from the situation as fast as I could. I guess I couldn't out run the problems that had just happened. Back to present time; June 11, 2005 somewhere near 1:00 am 'Mr. Vetra, you know what you are being charged with?' A rather large burly man asked me as I sat cuffed to a wooden bench. The name of his badge says Medeiros, he seems to be playing the bad cop in this situation. I am confused worried and unsure of what is going to happen to me, but I find a way to answer the question, 'Yes sir, Domestic Assault and Battery.' 'That is correct Mr. Vetra, we are going to finger print you and such now I am going to uncuff you but if you try anything I will be forced to take physical steps to stopping you.' 'As you say officer.' I just put my head down in shame, I have disgraced my family, I have disgraced my own name, worst of all I am in jail over a silly lovers quarel with the girl I thought I was in love with. It must have just been an illusion.
