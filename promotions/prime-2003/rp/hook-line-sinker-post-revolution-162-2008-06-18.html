Hook Line & Sinker (Post-ReVolution 162)

18 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/18/08 “C’mon, get up…” Darkness. “Get UP, I say! Up! UP!!” The black is peeled away with the eyelid effect as a blurry image of Allen Brown, better known as Codemaster, becomes apparent. He’s leaning over Nova, softly booting him in the side. “GET UP, FOOL!! By the Hyrulian Heavens, if the Ahrimans in Final Fantasy IV slept this soundly I would’ve STROLLED lackadaisically through the entire Ymir Forest!” The Risen Star rolls over, bits of carpet and wood stuck against his face and in his beard. He squints, holding a hand over his eyes to shield his throbbing brains from the harsh 60-watt glow overhead. “Ungghhh…dude…what the hell…” “Sentiments shared by the wider population, my booze-besotted friend,” Codemaster replies, extending a hand to help Nova up. “But time is short, and you need to make like a Goron and roll yourself out of here NOW.” Nova looks around the VIP suite, scratching the back of his head. “I don’t…remember being so…destructive…” “Lucky for you it’s already been immortalized,” Codemaster says dryly, slapping a copy of the latest dirt sheet into Nova’s midsection. “HOLY SHIT!” Nova gasps, staring down at the INDY-SCRIBABLE DEBAUCHERY! headline and the shot of him passed out amidst the ruins of a broken table, “I…I…HOLY SHIT, DUDE.” “Holy shit, dude,” Codemaster echoes, nodding his head, “it gets better.” “It does?” Nova asks hopefully. “Apparently you’ve been fired,” Codemaster replies. “How is that better?!” Nova cries out. “That’s just a turn of phrase,” Codemaster says with a shrug, “but ENOUGH! You’re wasting time, and the dark legions of the Press Horde are upon us! You must FLY, Celestial One! FLY THIS CHAOTIC COUP BEFORE IT’S TOO LATE!!” “With what?!” Nova replies, wide-eyed panic written all over his grubby face. “You can use my scooter,” Codemaster offers helpfully, producing a tiny key. “I…I’m a fucking Hall of Famer, Allen, I’m not leaving the arena on a scooter,” Nova says, putting his hands on his hips. “First of all, I just woke you up out of a pile of GARBAGE, so let’s not go into issues of pride,” Codemaster replies with brutal clarity, “and second, YOU’RE NOT LISTENING TO ME. YOU HAVE TO LEAVE NOW.” A rumbling in the distance turns both their heads. Codemaster looks back at Nova, his face stern. “NOW, RISEN STAR!!!” Nova grabs the key from him and runs for the double-door entrance. “NOOOOO!!” Codemaster screams. “THE SIDE DOOR, MAN!! ARE YOU STILL DRUNK?!!” “YES!!” Nova shouts back, swerving and darting through the side exit. There is another loud BOOM~! then another, like the footsteps of a Tyrannosaurus Rex approaching. The front double doors rattle. Alone at last, the Hyrulian Hellraiser hefts his legendary Megaton Hammer in his hands, a battle-ready snarl curling his lip. The lights overhead gleam off his visor. “Mana shield up.” The doors rattle again, dust rustling in the air. A dull roar approaches, like a freight train. “Elemental buffs activated. Casting Armor. Casting Image. Casting Wall.” BOOM~!! The doors buckle, and Brown’s knuckles turn white as he grips the Hammer and raises it over his head. “Casting Bloodlust.” BOOM~!!! The door bursts open, and a hail of camera flashes flood the room as reporters hurry inside, looking around. “Nova? Are you there? Do you have a comm-OHMYGOD!!!” “COME MEET YOUR DOOM AT THE HANDS OF THE CODEMASTER!!” The Breaker of Kingdom Hearts plunges Hammer-first into the crowd of unsuspecting media folk, cutting a wide swath with his mighty swings and kicking at anything that LIVES. Several blocks away… Putt-putt-putt-putt-putt-putt-putt-putt… Slowly Nova turns Codemaster’s scooter into a parking space outside the Rolling Foal, one of downtown Indianapolis’…ummm…greasier joints. Immediately he hears peals of raucous laughter from a nearby group of bikers. Looking down over the front of the windshield, Nova notices a large glittering Triforce mounted under the headlight. Peeking around the back, he sees the license plate: MUSHRM1 Another time, the Risen Star might have conceded his apparent ridiculousness. But it’s been a rough night, and a 500-yard sprint out of the arena did NOTHING for his formidable hangover. “SOMEBODY GOT A FUCKING PROBLEM?!” Nova roars, all but lunging off the scooter towards the nearest tattooed neck. Turns out nobody does. At 6’3” and hovering around 250 lbs. Nova is EASILY the most intimidating hippie they’ve ever seen. “Tryin’ to save the environment…dicks,” Nova mutters, shouldering past them as he makes his way inside the bar. Inside, Nova immediately lights a cigarette. It’s THAT kind of bar air, where it’s honestly healthier to be smoking. The Risen Star makes his way through the crowd up to the bar, and scoots his way onto a stool. “Jameson. Double, please,” he says without looking up. When the bartender doesn’t immediately respond, Nova looks up to see him eyeing the already-infamous tabloid shot hanging out of a basket by the cash register. Somehow these things have circulated everywhere, despite all this happening about an hour and a half ago. “Ummm…,” the bartender says, eyes darting between an impatient Nova and his likeness on the dirt sheet’s cover, “don’t you…think maybe you’ve had enough for one night already?” “Ummm…,” Nova sneers, “don’t you think YOU maybe should do your fucking JOB and get me a drink?” It’s at this most flattering moment that a sultry voice grabs the sides of Nova’s head and turns it around, coming almost nose-to-nose with the young Asian woman from the party. “Scratch that,” she says at the bartender, her eyes stuck on Nova, “make it two double-shots of Patron, and if you have some orange wedges back there, could you sprinkle a little cinnamon on them for us?” “That’s class,” Nova grins, “so no body shots, I guess?” “If I were gonna hand out body shots to strangers,” she replies, lips curling into a smile, “I would have stuck around your party. (Extending her hand) My name’s Sakura.” “No-err, Chris,” Nova says, shaking his head at his own slip, “sorry, it’s just been ages since I introduced myself to anyone who wasn’t five seconds away from trying to club my skull in with their forearms.” Sakura watches him run a hand through greasy, unkempt hair before he puts his cigarette out. “You’ve…had a rough night, haven’t you? Are you okay? Do you need…” She reaches into her purse and removes a money-clip. She begins to pluck bills out of it, and Nova laughs in spite of himself. “No, no, I’m…I mean, I’m not laughing AT you,” Nova says, waving her money away, “it’s just…yeah, I’ve had a fucked up night, kind of my own fault…” “Kind of?” Sakura repeats, cocking an eyebrow. The Risen Star nods, his face getting serious. “You sound like you’ve read the article…” Sakura’ eyes go genuinely wide with shock. “Oh, I um…” “You see the name underneath it?” Nova asks, “Torres Wilson?” Sakura nods, offering the bartender a brief but warm smile as he sets down the tequila shots in front of her and shoots Nova a dirty look. “Well, next time I see that little Courtney Cock-sucker, I’m gonna rip off his bottom jaw and scrub out his fuckin’ brain with it,” Nova growls, before mentally face-palming himself, “I mean…shit, I’m sorry…” “Hey, I brought it up,” Sakura says with a shrug, handing Nova a shot, “To better thoughts.” “I’m open for any suggestions,” Nova replies with a grin, and they take the shots, Nova giving himself a little head-shake afterwards. “Whew…” “So…,” Sakura says, crossing one leg over the other, “before I so RUDELY interrupted…you were going to say?” Nova has to think for a moment. “Oh…no, it’s just…I appreciate you offering to help me, but despite what the images of me sleeping in garbage might convey, I’m actually set up pretty well here for the next day or two. They’ve got me in a suite at the Omni.” Sakura nods. “That IS impressive. I’d…” She leans in a little. “I’d like to see that…Chris.” Nova takes a moment to soak this in, before nodding and leaning in himself. “I think we need another drink first.” The next morning… Nova rolls over in bed to the classic “Nobody there” body imprint. Slowly, he pushes himself up, turning over and resting his back against the headboard (which has SERIOUSLY depreciated in value over the last twelve hours). He looks around, and no trace of Sakura is present in his spacious quarters. No sounds from the shower, no clothes strewn about (even his are neatly folded on a chair)…no nothing. But something else missing…are any feelings of loss, regret, or rejection on the part of the Risen Star. She had treated him right, like a human being instead of a voodoo doll, with a kind of feminine tenderness instead of violent curiosity, that he hadn’t experienced in months and months of non-stop competition. The guys who forget a woman’s touch, who know only the feeling of muscle-on-mat and the endless butting heads of a lock-up, the dull thud of bones becoming brittle, brutalized…those are the guys that wind up killing themselves, either accidentally or intentionally. Nova rolls over to shuffle a joint out of his cigarette pack. He feels rejuvenated, and he might not know her last name, but hopefully whatever it was she was looking for, she left as contented as he feels now. Life is good. Fuck Torres Wilson. Fuck CP Cantrell. “I’m back.” The Risen Star lights the joint, sucking deep and blowing streams of thick smoke out of his nose, leaning his head back against the wall. He blows a smoke ring, and then another smaller one that sails through the first. He closes his eyes and smiles wide. “I’m fuckin’ BACK.”
