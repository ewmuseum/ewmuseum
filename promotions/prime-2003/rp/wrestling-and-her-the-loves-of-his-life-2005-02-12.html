Wrestling and her - The Loves of His Life

12 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 12, 2005 After a long week of sitting and thinking about what happened back at ReVolution 55, Killean Sirrajin was perturbed. He knew that finally, the Dark Age had made a profound impact on his career in PRIME. Recently, things had been said and threats had been thrown out, but nothing really came of it. Guys like Boda, Emilio Rage and Paul Cain had felt the repeated assault of these shadow dwellers like a whip cracking against bare skin. They had been beaten and yet they came back to get even time and again. Paul Cain looks to be lost in an unknown world with now former Dark Age member Shadow. But Emilio Rage and Boda were both fighting for their careers and to free PRIME of the grip of this devastating force. Killean had been left with his thoughts and had not cracked amidst the constant pressure this was putting on him. What was he to do to retaliate? He had met with Tchu to discuss the current feeling and emotions that were turning his mind into mush. Tchu had suggested that he come out with it all, but not in the verbal fashion. He recommended that Killean begin to keep a journal of all his feelings, void from any verbal filters that may be added. Killean had first denied that, stressing that he tells it like it is. But Tchu knew that even though Killean was the kind of guy to do that, there are always things being held back, too personal to speak of. Some of which Sirrajin himself didn’t want to be discovered. He attempted to make his tough exterior impenetrable and to this day no one has been able to break through the security and peer into the soul of the Supreme Machine. Tchu was asking him to break down this force field himself and begin to release his mind into words through a pen and paper. Killean had been a pretty decent writer but it had been a long time since he had last written anything besides his signature on a contract or check. But if he was to feel better at all, he knew it would be wise to follow the advice of Tchu and begin to put his thoughts on paper. After all, who was going to read this? It would only materialize to help him, nothing else. So what the hell? Sirrajin knew this was ground breaking. For the first time, a delve into the mind of The Supreme Machine was to become a reality, even though no one else would experience it. ---------------------------------------------- Journal Entry #1Wednesday February 9, 2005 On this day, I am to be releasing all of my feelings onto this pad of paper. A mere pad with lines, turning into the only confines left for the thoughts that run through my head. I remember her smile, the way she lit up a room with nothing but her presence. Her silky smooth brown hair was cascading down around her shoulders, hugging the curves of her upper body. Her blue eyes, like lakes of warm water that you could only imagine touching. They reached deep within me, perhaps because I let her be the only person who could. How could I resist? She melted my heart. I was ready to spend the rest of my days with her, making her happy. The days I spent thinking about her and the nights I spent holding her in my arms, never wanting to let her go. It was like magic to me and I never wanted it to end. I can still hear her wonderful angelic voice… “I love you Killean.” She would say while I held her tightly, sharing a moment and a tender kiss. To this day, I repeat those words through my head, wishing that I had her with me now to repeat those words for real. I get frustrated going through the memories like I would a house during spring cleaning, looking for something that makes me happy. But what would make me happy is having her back with me again. I can wish, I can cry and I can beg, but I know I have the memories. Sure she is always with me, but is that enough? How can I last not having her with me? I remember the last words she ever said to me when she left to head to the hospital for a routine check on our child. These words still make me weep for her even now. “You will be such a terrific father.” She almost shed a tear when she said that as she drove off in the vehicle with our unborn daughter. I can only imagine the pain and suffering she went through amidst the pile of broken glass and crudely bent metal. I never got the chance to say goodbye and I never got the chance to be the terrific father I was supposed to be. It was all taken from me in a heartbeat. Why did it happen to me? WHY?! I had visited her grave the other day. The flowers I had placed there a week earlier needed to be watered, so I took the watering can and walked to the tap across the yard. I saw the graves of hundreds of people and I began to think. All these people meant something to someone at sometime. My Kira was taken from me, just as some of these people were snatched up probably just as quick. Why do people suffer like this? Regardless of faith, background and feelings, we all suffer the same. As I walked back with the water, I saw her face. The soft skin that was always warm to the touch, I needed to feel it again. I became obsessed and I wept at the grave of my beloved. Just another of the many times I craved her presence. How I could strangle that creepy detective. I want to choke him until his eyes pop out of their sockets. How could he accuse me of plotting my wife’s murder? It was like a gun shot through the heart, causing it to burst on impact. My mind ran through all the demonic things I could do to this little man, making him suffer only portions of what I have. But I must not let him crack me. That is what they want. They want me to curl up into a ball and whine like a child who had just skinned his knee. They want me to stop being defensive but that will not happen. I will fight to the death if I must, trying to protect the love that I have for my wife. ----------------------------------------------- He needed to take a break. Rising from his seat he walked to the kitchen to get something to drink. As he opened the fridge and reached for one of his cold beers, the phone rang. After the second ring, Sirrajin grunted, not really wanting to talk to anyone right now. But he picked up the phone anyways. “This is Agent Bosharme Mr. Sirrajin.” How ironic…after writing about how much he would love to brutally torture him, this slime had to call. What else could make Sirrajin seethe in anger as much? “I would appreciate it if you were to visit us here at the station before the week is out. We have some information to share. You have a good day Mr. Sirrajin.” That was that. He called to ask Killean to come to the station. More questions to ask and more lies to tell no doubt. How could he even take this? He put the phone back on the hook after using his own creative words to say “Goodbye” to Agent Bosharme. After taking a large swig of the beer, he carried it with him as he made his way back to his office to continue writing. He had to admit, this was lifting some of the weight off his shoulders. He was actually feeling a little better. Maybe this will work after all. ------------------------------------------------- What was it that made PRIME what it was? The thoughts were running through my head like sharks circling a fresh meal. PRIME is a gathering of many different people from different places if you will. These people are here for the same reason. Sure some come and say they are there for revenge, others say it is for a title. But the fact of the matter is that PRIME is our livelihood. Losing matches does not make anyone much money. The winners circle is the place to be if you hope to go home and feed yourself and your family if you have one. But sometimes, the livelihood of it all gets pushed aside for typical human emotions. There is greed in all sports today, including the sport of professional wrestling. Money and power are the two most craved possessions. The power could be holding a title, showing that you are the dominant force. Another could be a position of authority. But come Sunday, the Dual Halo comes forth with a reward for the winner that is the epitome of greed and power, anything. The winner can choose anything that they want. This could be a changing force in the industry. Who knows what people will choose? The biggest fear for PRIME is the victory of the Dark Age or more specifically, Vampir Nosferatu. Vampir is the main bad apple in PRIME and for someone like him to win would be nothing short of a disaster. Vampir may be a sick individual, but that only means he is smarter than we all think. The conniving and thought blended mind of that human being would conjure up a plot that would surely spell the end of PRIME. How I would absolutely love to inflict the pain and torture that they have inflicted on everyone. Emilio Rage and Boda would gladly enjoy any infliction of bodily harm that would be coming to the Dark Age and Vampir. How I would love to tie him down with some chains and torture him ten fold compared to what he has done. His body would quiver in sheer agony and even though I am not a sick creature like he, I would surely enjoy it thoroughly. PRIME needs to be rid of this identity before it is too late. But even then, there are the lesser evils in PRIME. They will not do the things that Vampir would do, but things need to come to a head at some point. The Dual Halo will be nothing short of hell for all 30 participants involved. Only one will walk out with whatever they want, but even still, there are a few people who will be hard pressed to even participate at all. The Universal title is my one true goal yet only one person has had the privilege of holding that very prize. Even though Black Angel was very deserving of the title that he won, he has brought shame to the game and the title has lost some valuable meaning. Black Angel has been absent so to speak, when he does show up, hardly any effort is put forth. When I was face to face with him in the ring, he took me far however for someone as talented as he, it was hardly the most difficult match I have had. Hin See saw it fit to include me in the festivities so I guess I should be grateful. But I guess it would even be a little unfair to Boda if I was to walk out with the gold around my waist. After all he did win the shot and I was in the match that he won. How fast the tables can turn. Life can be like that. One minute you are flying high, the next minute you will fall face first into the dirt that contaminates your being. ------------------------------------------------- Journal Entry #2Friday February 11, 2005 I went to her grave again today. Seeing the grass hugging the sides of the tombstone reminded me of the times that I held her close to me. I used to run my hand through her beautiful hair and she would make these weak, maybe apologetic sounds. They were music to my ears as we shared many good times together. I remember sitting out on the deck from the bedroom watching the sun set. Sipping on a glass of wine, we would hold each other and spend the night together. We also spent quite a bit of our time on the beach. I would throw her in the water and she would come back and pinch my arm for it. I was completely ready to sacrifice everything and spend the rest of my life with Kira. She knows I’m still in love with her to this day. To be seeing the numbers engraved on the tombstone makes me wish I had that day back just once. Things would change and I would have the love of my life back. I took my wedding ring off today. Not many people noticed but I always wore it except for when I was in the ring. The shiny gold band had kept me company in the worst of times. When I used to sit thinking about her I would hold my hand close to my chest trying to make the pain go away. Without Matt to help me, I would not be as far in the process as I am right now. I am forever in his debt for everything he has helped me accomplish. Who was to think that my arch nemesis in the ring at one point would be my saving grace? It was odd to think of at first but I am glad that I went with it all. I will keep my ring in the jewelry box that Kira left. Many of her pieces of jewelry are in that box. Her beautiful diamond earrings are one of the most notable. I gave her those earrings a week before I proposed to her. I remember it well too. We were sitting on the beach on our favorite rock and the sun had set. The moon was reflecting off the waves coming in off the ocean and her eyes were twinkling. They were so beautiful I almost melted into them. I had given her them as a gift and she absolutely loved them. The tender kiss I received next was my gift. I just wanted to see her happy and I knew she was. Everything was going the right way for us. Those were the days I will cherish the most. I will forever keep my wedding band in this box and it will serve as a stone in the step to my complete recovery from the emotional stress. I will love her forever, but as I right this with tears falling from my eyes, I will move on and become strong for her, for that is the way that she would want it to be. ------------------------------------------------- Leaving the journal to sit for another day, Killean went to the kitchen to get a beer again. The whole journal thing was really helping him cope and deal with the stresses in his life. How he hoped for the days that he would be able to think about Kira and all the happy things that they did instead of shedding a tear for all the depressing things. One day it will all be over and he will have moved on. He just hoped that moving on would involve more gold around his waist this Sunday doing what he loves to do and he knew that deep down, she would be watching over him and saying those four words that forever linger around his soul… “I love you Killean.”
