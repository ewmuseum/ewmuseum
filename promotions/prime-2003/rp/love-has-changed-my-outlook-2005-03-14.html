Love has changed my outlook...

14 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3.14.05 Darkness is something very hard to overcome. Only a few things in life can overcome it, a new ray of sun light and maybe even artificial light. Even out of those two things you only want to see one of them, real sun light. Depression is something that makes you feel like you are always in the dark; you are always left out of the loop. For Peter Vetra he might have just been diagnosed with depression last week but it looks like he is getting his first ray of light. Or so he thinks. After this weekend’s wonderful weather New England has been having many of the people of Massachusetts are crying to have spring come in. Many people are quoted saying it is March already the snow should be done. But nonetheless people need to continue on their daily lives. The people of the streets are all bundled up, with their scarf’s and hoods to jackets up, anything to try and shield the cold air. An average sized man wearing a long leather jacket the look resembles one of a bomber jacket but a little longer. The man looks like a man on a mission. He reaches a store front and opens the door to let himself in. Once he walks in many people recognize him at the local coffee shop. Peter walks over to a corner table and takes a seat, as he is waiting for the waitress to come over he pulls a book out of one of his jacket pockets. Try and remember what the doctor’s said. They said it would be good for you to try and find a female friend/acquaintance. Do they really think that I like being all alone? What do they know anyways? The waitress approaches Peter Vetra with a cup of something in her hand and a smile that would attract the most taken men. “Good Day Peter, I see your travels have brought you back home again. Where have you been this time? Also just so you know I brought your tea over just the way you like it. The girl gives a wink of the eye trying to show a little interest in Peter Vetra. “Why thank you, Melissa, I have had a great couple of weeks on the road, but as you know there is nothing like home. Even though I am only here for another day or so before I leave for California.” Peter who has taken the cup from the waitress notices her slight attraction towards him and gives a little signal back seeing if maybe she would catch on. She quickly does, but is not to strong on the offensive as she never wants to make the first move like many women. “Well Peter, I hope you have a good trip and I hope I get a chance to see you either before you leave or when you return.” The young blonde waitress gives another wink and does some sort of spin as she turns and walks back towards the register. Peter thinks nothing of it and continues to drink his tea and reads his book… Although his mind is really not with the book it is more entwined with the encounter he just had. I have known her for years, many of times before we have exchanged the glares we just did, many of times we have even conversed but this time I think I need to take it to a new level. This time I need to step up to the plate and bring my A game or maybe I should just forget all about it. My doctor did say I need to start being happier and one to be happier is by finding someone who makes me happy. Maybe she is the one? Who knows? But I think I need to find out. Peter’s thoughts are interrupted by the ringing of his cellular phone. He places the book down, not that he was actually reading it more was it a object for display. Reaching in another coat pocket he pulls out his cell phone. Glancing quickly at the name that appears on the caller I.D. he hits the accept button.“Hello?” Peter answers with uncertainty as he is unsure of the number coming through. “Hola esse.” A heavily accented man is on the other line, seeming to have a strong background in the Spanish dialect maybe even from Mexico or a similar Spanish speaking country. “Ahh…” Peter Vetra realizes that the man who he hardly understands is his newest alliance in Morales. “Good Day or evening Morales, which ever it may be where ever you are. What is it I can do for you?” Peter is still wondering why Morales is calling him is eager to listen to his new found friend. “Well Peter, I don’t think I need to stress the importance of this week to you, we as a team are looking for a win to start this off right. And there is no better win then a win at the hands of a fallen Dark Age member.” Morales is eager to speak with Peter and try and encourage the usually down man who is not acting like himself and actually seems happy. “Morales, no one knows the importance of this match more then I do. This is a strive for me as a singles wrestlers but more importantly it is the first time we as a stable are in the squared circle with our mortal enemies and soon to be fallen foes, the Dark Age.” Peter who seems to have a new found confidence in his words and his actions is feeling happy about this new challenge he is about to face. I don’t want to get over confident now, I need to keep my composure and do my best against Dani Furher. I have faced him once before and I doubt he remembers as he has gone on and done more important things, but the fact of the matter is I never forget a loss and I suffered one at the hands of him. Peter Vetra shifts in his seat so he is more comfortable. “Peter, I am sure you are ready for this match but I am in dire need of stressing it to you, I have done so now I will see you later.” Morales is quick to hang up the phone so quick that Peter is unable to say his good bye. For a good career you need a fundamental start, well this week Imperium is resting their weight on my shoulders hoping I can bring home a great start with a win against Dani Furher. Is it his time? I would say so but even more so I would say it is my time! I have given my all to this federation for a long time now, last week was a building block showing I deserved my number one contender ship. This week I am going to show why I should be the champion. Dani Furher has no chance in the ring against a man of my stature, a man who is physically smaller then him but mentally stronger. Dani count your blessings the time has come for a reality check and I have come to deliver it! After finishing his tea Peter gets up from the chair he has fallen comfortable in for the last half hour or so. He places his book back in the pocket he had originally retrieved it from. Grabbing the cup of tea and the saucer it came on he walks over to the counter to pay the young lady known as, “Melissa.” “Peter, I see you are all done. This time it is on the house.” The beautiful young lady continues to throw things at Peter. Hoping that one of these times he will catch one of the signals she has thrown at him. “Well that is very nice of you, and for such a kind gesture I would like to give you something in return.” Peter reaches in his pocket grabs a pen and a piece of paper. He quickly scribbles down his phone number and slides it across to the attendant. “What is this Peter?” The young blonde is playing dumb hoping to be able to pull more out of him then just getting his phone number. “Well Melissa, that is my phone number, to repay you I would like to take you out to diner.” “To repay me?” Melissa is quick to question. “Well you could say that or you could just say I am trying to get a date out of it. What do you say? You and I for dinner when I return from California, you have my number now.” Peter is very persistent trying to get her to give him an answer. “Well Peter, you have a date, I will be calling you within the next couple of days, until then have a great day.” The blonde smiles as she continues to work happier then ever she just landed a date. Peter turns slowly heading out the door trying to remember that he should not get too excited as things always seem to start out good for him then turn sour. He is then quick to take away the happy thought with the thoughts of Dani Furher his next victim.
