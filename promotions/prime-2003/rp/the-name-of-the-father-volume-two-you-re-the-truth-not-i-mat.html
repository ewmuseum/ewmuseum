The Name Of The Father - Volume Two - You're The Truth, Not I (Match RP - v Walkins )

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Blah You're The Truth, Not I.Written: 9th January, 2006. 'Nothing picks us up when we are down,Nothing picks us up when we are down. Complement the atmosphere,Fill the ground with all our tears,Dry them up to make it clear,We do no wrong.' - Thirteen Senses. I 'What the fuck was that?' the driver demanded to know as the sound of glass smashing slashed through his ears. Craning his neck back to see around the seat, he was met with the sight of a bucking Karina Wolfenden defying the constricting wishes of his 3 colleagues. With shards of glass sticking out from her sneakers, she contorted her foot back through the smashed window, then slammed her foot down into the unfortunate thigh of one of the men. Pin-stripe suit trousers offered little protection against the sharp, jagged edges of the glass... and the pain from the unclean tearing of skin only intensified as the K-Wolf dragged her laces along his thigh. 'GAHHHHH!!!' was released as a shriek, causing the deafened driver to slam his eyes shut as a reflex. Having to harshly fling the car left to make his turning, the driver teased the peddles to keep the rear of the vehicle behind him, where it should be. The four entwined behind him in the back of the vehicle were all flung to the right, leaving the unfortunate man with the slashed thigh to go elbow-first through the remaining glass. 'AHHHFUCK!' he roared, mouth forcing the words out through a screwed up, agonised face. With blood soaking into his clothing, and shredded skin in need of stitches, the hefty man tried to make his retreat from battle, crawling through into the front of the vehicle, only for a sudden acceleration to cannonball him back into his far skinnier colleague. As a winded hmph was released, the third man tried to ensnare the squirming Wolfenden's arms in a full-nelson type hold, only for the back of her skull to flatten his nose into his face. 'Will you lot fucking keep her still!' the driver demanded, trying to keep his mind, and all four wheels, on the road. Not even pausing to ask who, what, where or why, Karina Wolfenden hadn't kept a single muscle still since she was seized. It was hardly an ambiguous situation, where a short explanation would leave her letting out an all-knowing ahhh. Outside of the car, in the alley, she'd have stood little chance. Stubbornness and a sharp temper were no substitute for wily street fighting skills. But, they'd dragged the K-Wolf into a confined space, and they were paying for it with blood and easily-breakable body parts. Twisting herself down into the seat, Wolfenden spun her legs up, before pumping both feet up into one of the aggressor’s faces as he tried to loom violently over her. His clenched fists unfurled to grab his cheek and jaw, before a second thrust from Kari's powerful thighs smashed the back of his head into the window. Hearing another window shatter, the driver, understandably paranoid about driving at high speed through the city with 2 broken windows and a struggling young woman in the back, again leant around. 'Didn't you bring ties?' he demanded to know, before quickly shooting a look back to the road ahead. 'If she smashes anything else, I'm going to have to go the back street way.' 'She threw them out the window!' a voice answered him from the back, followed by the kind of wince that followed an elbow ploughing into a ribcage. Another pained, primal scream was released as Karina again pumped her feet out, scraping the sides of the victim's head through the broken glass. Losing patience, the larger of the trio gave up trying to stem the blood flow from his leg, and swung a right at Karina, blindsiding her with a hard row of knuckles to the jaw. Not quite knocking her clean out, the shot managed to subdue her for a few seconds, while the skinner of the men grabbed her arms. 'Keep her still or I'm dumping all of you out!' went the driver's final demand as he only just managed to avoid a streetlight on his high-speed dash through the city. With a snarl having taken up home on his blood-smeared face, the bulky man with the shredded leg and elbow managed to contort his body to reach down into a bag tucked under the passenger seat. Trying to unscrew the top of a bottle, the swerving of the car left the contents splashing out as he did so, immediately spreading a hazy, chemical stench throughout the vehicle. 'What NOW?' the fuming driver harshly questioned as the heavy man used his controlling bulk to drag Wolfenden towards him. Despite her flailing limbs, Karina couldn't avoid a blood-soaked cloth tainted with chloroform from being pushed down over her face. Furious fists split into gouging fingers and thumbs as she desperately tried to break free once more, before her racing metabolism sucked all the fumes in. 'Hey, go easy with that stuff,' the driver cautioned as the heady fumes began to effect more than Wolfenden. As he turned back to see why his eyes were beginning to sting, and his brain felt like cotton wool, a desperate foot flung itself back, catching the oblivious driver plush on the chin. Flung back, the driver bounced off the dashboard, knocking the stick on his way, before the seatbelt pulled him firmly back into his seat. Two thick arms wrapped themselves over Karina's face, pinning the cloth there as her motions became increasing docile and desperate. But just as her final, defiant limb went limp, the car mounted the sidewalk, and the flank of the bumper bounced off a fire hydrant. 'Jesus Christ!' the skinny man in the back exclaimed, while his two colleagues suddenly snapped their attention back to the outside world. 'Remember the orders, we can't let he-' With the dazed driver's foot still planted on the accelerator, the car pinballed back across the street, before a shop front acted as its crash barrier... events culminating in a crash that could easily justify 3 tildes and a generous handful of exclamation mark. Maybe even a @ to replace the 'a‘. The vehicle ploughed through the display units in the window, setting off a deafening alarm that was almost loud enough to cut through the dreamy sedation of the chloroform. 'Ugh-Cuh...' was expelled with a spluttering cough as a hand slapped down in one of the few glass-free patches around the car, before the wrist propping it up collapsed under the effort. II It was like waking up under duress from a blaring alarm clock, only silence was there to greet her. Bursting up from her chemical induced slumber, Karina Wolfenden's brain had to play rapid catch-up, and took a slither of a split second to run a recap of the last... she didn't know how long. The chemical's effect wearing off had prompted her to awaken, rather than a natural requirement fulfilled, leaving her eyes dry under heavy eyelids, and her whole body feeling sluggish. Far from its natural, effervescent state. 'Where...' she asked of herself, flummoxed, while the rubbing of her irritable eyes failing to help them focus. She remembered the footsteps, the struggle, the helplessness, the car, the deep-programmed, animalistic instinct to fight at all costs, the stench of chloroform, the world hiding away from her, and a distant, echoing smashing sound. Sitting up, Karina felt as if her brain had tumbled through the back of her skull in doing so, and was hanging from the most sensitive of nerves to drag itself back up. 'Well, doesn't that just hurt like the fucking Dickens...' Kari puttered, massaging her temples, not even realising that sensation had yet to creep back into her extremities. The recollection went on a loop, snapshots flashing through the parts of her memory that had recovered from the shorting-out. Rolling over, Wolfenden quickly hissed in pain as her shoulder ground against the wooden floor, and the lack of a soft, supportive surface below her began to flick nerve endings back on. Like a new-born foal, she tried to stand before her body was ready, and legs promptly turned to jelly beneath her, before a numb collision with the wood grounded her once more. For someone so used to being in total control of her body and its actions, it was paramount to torture for the K-Wolf. Like being locked out of home, multiplied by approximately 7 million. Trying not to panic, Kari reached out for something solid to support her, the world around her being revealed as her eyes followed the probing fingers on their mission. 'Home?' she mumbled as eyebrows almost turned inside-out in curiosity. The one familiar memory of a postcard left on her kitchen counter set of a chain reaction, a domino effect of familiarity that spread throughout her apartment. Shedding mental light upon her surroundings, Karina's vision spread like the sunrise, bringing the world back to life in front of her. Probing hands pressed onto the kitchen counter, supporting the cautiously rising body attached to them. 'How did...' eyes narrowed back from their open bewilderment to quiz. As the loop continued to play in her head, memories opened up subconscious mental blocks. As she saw her foot smash through the car's window, she felt where her right foot's skin had been punctured by shards smuggling inside her sneaker. The sight of her fist lashing out at another's face brought a dull sting to her knuckles, while the sight of the chloroform rag descending upon her triggered smell sensations, almost causing the K-Wolf to vomit. 'Him...' Karina was then left exclaiming, almost as soundless as it was breathless. Each time the mental loop went around, one face was sharper, jumping out from the frantic blur. The whirr was interrupted by a click every time he flashed up. The face, something about it. The nose? The cheekbones? The hair line? The... looking back over his shoulder. 'I know him,' gained in confidence each time she repeated the mantra. 'I know him. I know him. I know him.' Saying nothing other than those accusing words, Karina released the counter, the intense desire to do so allowing her to stand un-assisted. The face had made the rest of her memories redundant, numbed the pain in her foot, cooled the burn of her knuckles, and eased the urge to vomit. 'I know him. I know him. I know him.' It nagged her. Who? had become Where? Aimlessly, she wandered her apartment, hoping something would connect the dots. She wasn't imagining it, and it wasn't just the stress of the last day getting to her. She should have been back at the hospital with Max, she should have been apologising to Angelo for being her, and she should have at least been questioning why 4 strangers dragged her into a car. All this before questioning how she got back to her apartment, in one piece, rather then 10 awaiting disposal in the nearest river. But every recent event was forced into a bottleneck, amalgamating, forced all into the one, solitary question of importance. 'Where have I seen him?' Desperation had Karina competing turns in her stride, fishing around for something to bite at her probing eyes' hook. Every photo almost melted under the intense stare of the K-Wolf, until her eyeballs were almost wrenched out of their sockets, such was the attraction to one particular photo. Caring little for the discarded frames swept to the floor to clear a path, the K-Wolf snatched the photo, then dropped to her knees. It was him. At her father's 50th birthday, flanked by his family, with champagne glass held aloft, stood Lawrence Wolfenden. With his smile almost negating the need for a flash, he dominated the scene in such a way that everyone else in it became peripheral... pushed to the sides of the photo, and the back of the mind. But there, in the background, looking back from his chair to see what was going on... 'It's you.' ...was the driver. III Broken glass and split wood blanketed the floor of Karina Wolfenden's apartment. Every photo within the 4 walls, whether framed, pinned to her wall, or tucked away in the back of a drawer had been dredged up to the surface. Old bank statements, take-away menus, and red reminder bills littered the floorboards, while old negatives cut shimmering black, divisive lines through the mess. No face was ignored as she looked through each one in turn, squinting until her eyes hurt if a face was blurred. Showing off an almost autistic, or at the least anal, skill for order, the photos had found themselves into piles. If there were 2 pictures at the same party, she'd study them both, just in case she could match the clothing of a disguised face to a body-less head in the background of another. It had gone beyond being a mere task, and had shot straight into her bloodstream as a fascination, a sudden addiction. All the people that Lawrence Wolfenden surrounded himself with, all the people that she managed to politely ignore at every party she was forced to attend... who were they? They were in her house, they kissed her mother on the cheek, they left their coats on her bed. And she couldn't put a single guess of a name to a single face. Until... 'No...' she tried to deny what was in front of her by turning the gasp into a statement. 'It's not.' There was her father, Lawrence, again encased up to the Adam's apple in a suit, this time going to the shiny dark grey. Beside him was a beaming Max, hand on his father's shoulder, and a tall drink in hand. Either side of the father and son, strangers stood with bowl-glasses of after dinner liquor, cigars held up to lips. They were nameless, almost faceless, close to being lost under the photos shimmering surface. But in the background, one guest had no trouble standing out. 'Angelo,' spat forth the accusation. Feverishly, she tore through the piles, angry eyes almost ripping through the photos such was her ferocity. There he was again, poised in the background, casual body language blending him in with his peers, but an icy blue gaze staring into the heart of the camera's shutter mechanism. A photo from 2 years ago, and there he was, same slicked-back hair, same eyes looking right out at her. From 4 years ago, attention turning from a conversation to the photographer. 6 years ago, features still ripening, but unmistakably of the Deville. Her 18th birthday, there he was. Max's graduation, there he was. Her parent's silver wedding anniversary, there he was. 'How?' she pondered in defeat, dropping down seated from being perched on eager knees. He had always been there. Through Wolfenden ran a strain of Deville... but was it mutually beneficial, or a leeching virus? IV It somehow managed to stand prouder than all the surrounding houses, grandeur expressed in every angle of brickwork, every carving of window frame, and every sculpting of steps up to the looming door. It was a home that kept one of Angelo Deville's feet in a bygone age, one where things were solved the old-fashioned way, and a man with the right connections drafted his own laws. Inside was enough technology to make the most up-to-date of tech-geeks weak at his nobly knees, but every wire was concealed behind a lavish decor, every control panel hidden from blighted view by thick, sturdy oil paintings So many times she'd knocked at the door, walked hand in hand, or been carried over the threshold that lay beyond the Excalibur test-of-strength that was the door knocker... but this time, she was uninvited, and thoroughly expected to be repelled. That was the main reason Karina Wolfenden was perched on the ledge that ran around the upper floor, rather than strolling up the driveway. Encased almost completely in black, blending in against the shadows cast by the swelled-up moon. Taking a deep breathe, Kari readied herself, then swung up onto the roof. Crouching with toes perched on the last of the tiles, and heel balancing on the gutter, Wolfenden then fluidly slithered down a little, hooking her feet under the facia. Crossing arms across her chest, the K-Wolf dropped back, hanging up-side down in front of one of the upstairs windows. 'Sometimes I really wish I knew someone who did this for a living...' she lamented, heart thumping against the inside of her chest, fearing her feet might slip. With the top section of the left window open barely a couple of inches, Wolfenden took care in prodding the handle up far enough to un-trap it from the knob. With her entranceway loosened, Kari wedged it fully open, before reaching up inside her sleeve, and retrieving her make-shift tool, which glamorously consisted of a small wire loop at the end of a slender, extendable baton. Safe to say, she wasn't a professional. With an unsteady hand that betrayed the kind of nerves anyone breaking into Angelo Deville's home would be wise to endure, Kari hooked the latches in turn, and freed them, leaving the right hand side window to sigh open a few centimetres. Then, just as the intense beams of a passing bus washed along the side of the house as it turned, Wolfenden slipped in, closing the way-out behind her. 'It actually worked,' she breathlessly exclaimed in shock, cynicism there in lavish amounts. 'Something must be wrong.' Creeping footsteps took her across the bedroom to the door, where the thick oak acted to insulate the room against all noise from the landing outside. Deciding it was a little too late to be frozen in caution, Wolfenden eased the knob around, letting the door break its snug seal from within the frame. 'Look, your medical insurance may go through what books I let the government see, but it doesn’t mean I'm going to hold your hand and take you to the hospital like I'm your fucking mother.' The rasping, dismissive tone, forced out through snarling features could only have belonged to Angelo Deville. Down the landing he stormed, feet taking his apparent agitation out on the floorboards, before he brutally stomped each of the stairs on his way down. Slipping out of concealment, Karina traced his steps, then paused at the top of the staircase. His conversation echoed up towards her, before the pulses of noise grew closer as he turned back on himself. By the time the Deville's shadow was cast across the foot of the steps, Wolfenden was halfway down them. 'How do you know my father?' she dramatically bombarded down onto Angelo as the surprise left his bottom jaw dropping down, before its safety mechanism kicked in and stopped it after just a centimetre or so. 'I don't kn-' he went to fob the persistent Wolfenden off with, holding both hands up to her in dismissal as much as hesitation. 'Not this time,' she warned, cutting his words off in serrated fashion. 'No more denials, no more turning it around so I'm left feeling like a prize fucking toolbox. So unless you're about to start speaking the truth for once, I'd advice getting something in the way of your face, before I put my knee right through it.' Chuckling, Deville rubbed his chin, as if he was waiting for her to crack and let the joke free. 'Or you could leave, then find someone to prescribe you lots, and lots of medicat-' Before he could finish the defensive verbal deflection, Karina launched herself from the stairs, flying with both fists outstretched. Before Deville could dodge or get any defences up, the K-Wolf's knuckles ploughed into his face, sending him toppling back, with her landing both knees onto him. Mounting Deville, she raised her fists up, then paused to make her demands known. 'How do you know him?' she repeated, following the second or so pause that followed it by smashing both fists down into Angelo's face once again. Despite his precarious position, Angelo simply wiped the blood from his lip, face not forming a single wrinkle as he tried to remain the voice of reason. 'I think we'd be better to talk about this whe-' As Karina reared back to rain another pair of blows down onto the Crimson Angel, he seized the moment that he knew would be coming around, and bucked Wolfenden off of him, before hopping back to his feet in sprightly fashion. As Wolfenden lashed out at the floor in frustration, Deville released a sleeve from it's cufflink, before wiping his mouth with the white fabric. 'Now,' he decreed, well and truly having tired of Wolfenden's persistent presence. 'Take whatever the problem is today OUT of my house, because the next time you so much as roll an eye at me, I'm hitting back. And we both know what happened last time we let it all go. Well, I remember, I don't know what was left of your memory when you finally came t-' Before he could finish, the palm of Karina Wolfenden swatted his cheek, letting loose a sound more akin to a thunder-clap than a bitchslap. 'To quote that guy from Mortal Kombat,' she beamed. 'FIG-' Holding true to his word, Deville's right hand whipped across his body, smacking against Kari's cheek with a backhand slap. And it was on. Making Smith v Neo look like a couple of drunken tramps trying to see straight enough to punch for a Bumfights video, the duo unleashed all they had at one another. Violence was the only thing left to keep the truth contained, and it did it's job dutifully, prompting fists, knees, feet, elbows, and heads to fly liberally. Blurring hand speed tested the steel of jaws, while the points of knees began to marble flesh a deep, queasy brown. Using the eager Wolfenden's momentum against her, Deville flung her into a bookcase, leaving her draped over a shelf as book after book tumbled down around her. As his foot followed her in, Deville had to watch as, in the blink of an eye, Kari was no longer there, leaving his foot to smash through the wood. Seeing Deville's soft-spot open, Wolfenden had no qualms about unloading a harsh kick up into the groin, before following up with a trio of knees that left the Crimson Angel sounding as if he was about to cough up the Deville family jewels. 'That the truth I hear?' Kari taunted, landing a right cross to the pained Deville's jaw. 'Or just another load of useless bal-' Deville is like a bitch of a beat 'em up. You can't just pause halfway through. With a brutal elbow that swiflty forgot all the fond moments they once shared, Deville smashed down above Kari's eye, splitting the brow and leaving her stumbling back... thankfully just out of range of the fist that followed it. Back and forth the almost-stylised violence continued, half resembling a bar brawl, half an elaborate dance... or mating ritual. 'Is it really worth all this?' Karina pressed, gripping the off-balance Deville around his neck in a clinch. 'This better than the truth?' He could punch her in the face, knee her in the ribs, and kick her in the spine... but he couldn't tell Karina Wolfenden that it was all for her own good. An endless tirade of knees threatened to crumble the sharp features of Deville's face, before desperation broke him loose with an uppercut that snapped Kari's head back sharply. Concentrating more on just staying upright that sending a counter-punch Angelo's way, Kari was then sent flying back as the Crimson Angel pumped his foot out brutally into her ribs. Winded, Kari thumped against the wall, before Angelo's fist flew un-hindered, flicking Wolfenden's off-switch as her head snapped around. As Karina slid down the wall and unfurled across the debris-strewn floor, Deville's adrenaline subsided, and he collapsed to his knees, the jolt amplifying every throb. Beside Karina, his body slowly shut down, going into repair mode as his diaphragm struggled to limply push out just a few words. 'You... win...'
