Date: 7/9/07 Blog of AWESOMENESS Entry 1 "Oh Shit." Hello there, internet readers, my name is Eli VanNess, sultan of slick, king of kick ass, duke of ‘doin it’, but I digress. It seems you have stumbled upon my blog, possibly one of the sickest, most awesomest pieces of literature on the webernets (that’s cool lingo for the internet). Now, I confess, I don’t hold myself to the highest grammatical standards, but hey, when you’re this awesomely good looking, grammar takes a backseat, but once again I digress. This little "blog" of mines is called "theUPstarts - BLOG OF AWESOMENESS" and will be run by myself and my partner in crime, Felix Young. We’re two brash, fun-loving bro’s who have recently been given a lot of time off from school (actually we’re off forever, but I’ll get into that sooner or later). We’re both twenty one, sexy, and single. I favor the more jock-ish look, with my perfectly spiked hair and my intricately manicured burns (sideburns, for you n00bs), I am a picture of perfection. A "snapshot of sexy", if you will. Felix on the other hand favors the "black soul/my heart bleeds for you" look. You know, the one that scares little children but attracts sexy girls looking for that "sensitive" man? Bullshit, if you ask me, but whatever. Once again, I digress. We were both students and the University of Loyola, a nice place if you ask me. Plenty of cold brew and hot chicks, both equally crisp and refreshing. We had a class, here and there, but I mean who can be bothered with that book shit? That’s what high school was for. I think. Anyway, we were both friends from high school because we were both on the wrestling team. I know, I know, two buddies on the wrestling team, they must be gay. Well we aren’t, fuck you. Anyway, we both applied and were accepted (I sent and 8x12, Felix just sent a few head shots) and decided to go there. We got on campus, scoped shit out and decided that collegiate athletics weren’t for us. We did decide, however, that Greek life was the life for us and pledged to the most infamous frat on campus – Delta Phi Kappa. They had quite a reputation on campus and we figured that the best should join the best and we applied. I’m not going to go into details of our pledging or initiation (because they do some weird, fucked up shit in college. You don’t want to know, trust me.) but I will say that we got in and were accepted as brothers. Our first three years passed in a blur of beer, babes, and bong hits. I would consider it the three most productive years of my life. For our senior year I was elected frat president and appointed Felix as my right hand man (no homo). The previous President had made quite a few poor decisions (that fucker, may he rot in hell) and we were in debt with the house we were living in and had a few fines levied against us for minor school rule infractions (Felix had forgotten that pants, although optional at parties, were not optional for english class). Felix and I knew we had to get the money together somehow, so we ran through our list of options. Chippendales wasn’t hiring, and other than stripping, we weren’t qualified to do much else. Then, on one fateful night, it happened. Loyola’s near bye liquor stores are notorious for carding everyone and anyone, and underclassmen have a rough time scoring some brewski’s if they don’t have proper identification. One night, myself and Felix were looking for some crisp refreshment and happened to be by one of these said liquor stores. As we approached the door a shadow spun from around the corner and blocked our path. It was a squat freshman named John Ashton. He didn’t have many friends and I wouldn’t consider him a real looker, so I couldn’t figure out what he was doing in an alley next to a liquor store. "Hey dudes, hook a brother up with a few brews, man. I’ll slip you a few extra bills." He had said, salivating at the thought of some rocky mountain refreshment. I paid it no mind and snagged the wad of cash from his hand, pocketing and extra twenty and producing his beer. This minor occurrence had no impact on me, but Felix had some thoughts "brewing" – no pun intended, nerds. He suggested starting a ‘racket’ so to speak, providing crisp, refreshing beer to underage and overpaying freshman. Felix suggested making business cards reading "Eli and Felix – Your Frosty Friends" along with our address and phone number. To this I replied with a hasty: "Fuck yeah." We used the last of our cash to print up the cards and dished them out at freshman orientation. Within days the calls, along with the cash, came pouring in. I knew what we were doing was wrong: corrupting youth, overcharging them, taking their money, but fuck it, we needed the cash and they needed the refreshments. We continued on like this for the first couple months of school, whoring out beer to kids, until one night. When we got our shit tossed. We received a phone call from Christopher C. Daniels, who’s grandfather was Jack Daniels. Yes, THE fucking Jack Daniels. This kid was fucking loaded from head to ass with cash and he was throwing a party. Why he couldn’t swindle his own alcohol, given his family connections is fucking beyond me, but I wasn’t even thinking of that by then. We picked up the brew, swung by his dorm, and dropped it off. He, like everyone else we had supplied beer to, politely asked us to stay and enjoy a few with him and his friends. "Fuck yes, we’ll drink your beer." We had said. The party was standard, decent babes, borderline gay guys. The lot of us were just sitting, maxin and relaxin, when out of nowhere Christopher stormed back into the room. "Fuck get the fuck out. Everyone get the fuck out of here." Personally, I thought he was tripping on some fucking shrooms or smoked some bad herb, but he was balls out serious and wanted us out. We realized why when smoke started billowing out of the bathroom. The idiot had dropped his cigarette on the decorative rug he had in his bathroom. First of all, who has decorative rugs in their bathroom, second, if you can’t keep the joint between your lips when your taking a piss, you don’t deserve the herb, simple as that. The shit had caught fire and it was spreading mad fast. We flew the fuck out of there, along with everyone else and went home, not thinking anything of it. The fire department put that shit out, but not until it had destroyed Chris’ dorm and the three neighboring ones. Thankfully, I guess, no one was killed, but little did I know mine and Felix’s asses were about to be baked. And I’m not talking weed, homies. Two weeks passed and we hadn’t heard from Chris or anyone else at the party. We figured shit had been taken care of and that the whole fire thing would eventually just blow over. We were wrong. We got a phone call about two and half weeks later from the Dean of the school herself, Lynda Maxwell, summoning us to her PERSONAL office. We figured it could be for one of two reasons. She was hot for us, and was feeling a little frisky, or we were in trouble. It was definitely the latter. We showed up, got chewed out. She said that Chris and given up information about our whole scheme and had gotten his friends to come forward. That rich motherfucker tossed the blame on us so his fortune-inheriting ass wouldn’t get chapped. We didn’t argue and just threw her the middle finger and said we didn’t do shit. She replied that our status with the college would be mailed to us in a week after she had made her decision. This was about a month ago. Myself and Felix were relaxing in our room, enjoying a tightly packed bowl, when our freshman bitch brought the mail in. There was a letter from the dean, for us, and she figured we should read it. We sat there nervous for a few seconds, but I mean fuck it who cares what happens, they can’t kick us out of school for buying some beer. We were wrong. The letter contained our dismissal from college, we would not graduate and would not receive and sort of diploma. This was no big deal, because to tell you the truth, I have no fucking idea what I was even majoring in. No sweat we said, but continued to read on. We had been fined $100,000. That’s right, you’re reading that number right. ONE HUNDRED FUCKING GRAND. Where the fuck were we supposed to get that sort of fucking money? On top of that, the school had called both our parents and needless to say, they weren’t very proud. They demanded that we pay them back for the first three years of college that they had waited their money on. That brought the total of monies we were responsible for paying to $340,000. THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY FUCKING THOUSAND FUCKING DOLLARDS. "You’ve gotta be shitting me." But nope, they shit-us-not. We had to get an apartment because we were no longer allowed to live on campus and jobs because that 340,000 wasn’t going to grow on a fucking tree. We labored for a few weeks, me as a bouncer at a sleazy stripclub and Felix as an extra in some indie flick about vampires. The money wasn’t exactly pouring in, but one night Felix came home with some great news. "Dude, I was shooting a scene today and my buddy Manuel stopped by." He had said, barely able to contain his fucking excitement. "By shooting a scene you mean milling around in the background while the people making the real money are acting, right?" Zing. "Fuck you man. Me and Manuel got to talking and he told me that recently he’s been working with PRIME." "That wrestling promotion?" "Yeah man, they’re the biggest shit out there right now. He said he could definitely get us a job there." "AWESOME! Wait a fucking second. What exactly is Manuel’s role in the company? Talent scout, trainer, writer?" "Oh no, he’s the janitor." Felix had replied coolly, as if this didn’t pose a problem. "So alright let me get this straight. The janitor, of PRIME, is going to get you and I a job with the company? DOING WHAT, CLEANING THE TOILETS? WIPING THE WRESTLER’S ASSES? WHAT THE FUCK KIND OF JOB CAN HE GET US?" Well turns out one of the higher ups in the company owed Manuel a favor. He got us in with a talent scout, made us fuck around in the ring and inked us to fat contracts. I use the term fat loosely. We were set to make a decent amount per show, and they gave us a $500 signing bonus. I’m told it would have been bigger if we actually had some professional experience, or as the scout put it "knew what the fuck we were doing" the bonus would have been bigger. Whatever, it is what it is. Which brings us to now. I’m askin’ all you fans (that are now fans, after reading this intense, heart warming ‘bend but don’t break’ story) to come out and show us some love at the show. We’re set to square off against some duo who thinks that that are actually "crime fighting super heroes". CRIME FIGHTING SUPER ZEROS IS MORE LIKE IT. Burn. Pure sick burn. When they step out into that ring with us, it won’t even be a contest. I know they don’t give points in wrestling for looking good, but hell, it doesn’t hurt. Me and Felix have been workin on some super awesome, super cool, secret moves that will make these "super heroes" look like "super WEENIES". The pwnage is off the hook right now! Anyway subjects (I’ll call all you readers subjects, because I think ‘peon’ is too harsh a word), I have an appointment with a tanning bed and then a haircut because I wanna look sexy when I make my television debut. Hopefully Jessica Alba or Beal or Simpson (hell one of the fucking super sexy Jessicas) will be watching and I can score myself a date. Catch theUPstarts on REVOLUTION. Or else I’ll rip your head off. With Love, Loyal Subjects, Eli "So Sly" VanNess P.S. How does that "So Sly" sound? I know it sounds good, I mean, I came up with it, but I’m wondering what my loyal subjects think. Also, check back because after our first match we’ll be posting a poll in which you, the loyal subject, gets to vote who is hotter: Me or Felix. Think hard, for this decision could impact the rest of our lives!
