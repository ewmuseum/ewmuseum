Adam Dick is his name and the corruption of American youth is his game.

17 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-17-07 Stock red, white, and blue balloons decorate an office building giving it that bullshit patriotic feel. It is the campaign headquarters of the illustrious soul saver Hoyt Williams. A team of volunteers and supporters work diligently at their computers, phones, and Starbucks Pumpkin Spice Latte’s. Dan: We must win our first campaign stop against this….. The doors swing open and smoking a Sherlock Holmes style pipe is none other then Hoyt Williams. He sucks on the pipe but nothing comes out as tobacco is a byproduct of Satan. Hoyt Williams: Adam Dick is his name and the corruption of American youth is his game. I true peddlephile peddling intoxicants to our youth. This man is evil. Dan: What do you plan to do? Hoyt Williams: What any upstanding servant of the constitution would do. Bomb him till he waves a white flag and then impose our Judeo-Christian faith, our capitalistic approach, and our Democratic system upon him. Research what do we know? The girl in the research department pulls up the PRIME website and clicks on Adam Dicks bio. Gilda: He enjoys juvenile humor. Hoyt arches his eyebrow in a curious nature. Gilda: His ring style is unorthodox. Hoyt Williams: What is that leaf on his mask? Gilda: I’m not sure. The straight lace man in a suit saunters over looking at the mask then shakes his head in disgust. Dan: My brother turned into an evil hippie after touching that deadly plant. Friends we are not dealing with a normal man as this plant has changed him. The devil snatched his short term memory while convincing him that his hand was amazing and Taxi is an outstanding television show. He has been poisoned by sweet jane, refer, pot, sweet leaf, the deadly marijuana! Classic music plays as everybody in the staff looks in the camera in shock. Hoyt Williams: Tell me more about this mar..a…juana?? Dan: First it makes you happy and think stupid stuff is funny. Then it works to destroy your body by making you want to eat everything and anything. Hoyt Williams: My god man the body is our temple to the lord. Dan: This does everything to tear it down. Hoyt Williams: I think I have heard of this drug you know late night television infomercials? Julie: Yea I hate those they take away from the good programming. Hoyt Williams: Instead of wholesome reruns of genius television like I Love Lucy, or the Honeymooners you get garbage being sold to you. But who is watching this junk and more so who is buying pasta makers, the carrot juice engines, and so forth? The POT HEADS!! If I wasn’t past the rp deadline and had more time to finish this thing I would drive down to the TV network and stop the evils that be. Julie: There is always next week. Hoyt Williams: Good point my well busted assistant. Till then there is only one thing I can do and that’s take down the icon of this deadly plant. Julie: Willie Nelson? Hoyt Williams: Worse! Dan: John Stewart! Hoyt Williams: Not that bad. Julie: Tommy Chong? Hoyt Williams: Much more relevant in today’s day and age. Julie and Dan: YOU MEAN???? Hoyt Williams: I WILL DESTORY ADAM DICK in the first stop of our long campaign. I may be less of a heel then Nixon but I’m just as cut throat. Dick better get ready to be Crucified and Patriotized in the NAME OF HOYT!! So it is spoken so it is truth and the truth will go marching on!!! Dan: AMEN!!!! Julie: God Bless America!! This Role-Play has been approved by Hoyt Williams Hoyt Williams 2008 American Made unlike Obama
