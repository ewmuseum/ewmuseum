The Confession: Part II - Pulling Together

28 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28-10-10 “He wudn't bullshittin' Dev, he's got 'em both here.” Daniels was careful to whisper into his cell phone. Standing at the front door of the house he glanced both upstairs and down the hallway at the door leading to the basement ensuring no one could hear the call to Shakur. The air was thick and humid; the odour emanating from the flaking walls and mouldy carpeting. With its boarded up windows and unmown lawn the dilapidated property served its purpose as a temporary safehouse, and with ticker tape declaring it structurally unsafe hanging off the wrought iron fence out front it was the perfect hideout. A clever ploy on the part of the giant, Christian had thought. “Ya gotta see fer yerself.....” An ominous creak from the floorboards above put the Biker on alert. “Look the viking's right, we don't want this shit gettin' out there're else we'll have 'em fuckin' pryin' inta our business too. That can't happen...” Another creak emanating from the ceiling prompted the Biker to end the conversation quickly as a groan fraught with agony resonated above. Pocketing his cell he retreated back to the basement, treading quietly over the floor and listening to the voices coming from the chamber ahead. “Why do you need to keep him here? Why can't we do this at the hospital?” “You know why Tracy,” Von Kelsig drawled sympathetically, “until we get to the bottom of it we can't let Michael go. The only reason he's here and not at the hospital is because we know him. By rights I should've handed in the evidence and left it for the proper authorities to deal with...but we don't want that do we?” “I guess so, but why here? Why not our place? Why does it have to be this...this dump?” “People talk.” The large frame of Daniels blocked the exit. Strolling in he closed the door behind him and leaned against it, folding his arms and staring a hole through Tracy. “If anybody sees us cartin' yer man around an' it turns out he's done somethin' wrong it ain't gon' look good'n us now is it?” Tracy shrugged her shoulders, gazing blankly at the floor before welling up and holding a hand over her face. Growling at Von Kelsig, Daniels motioned for the giant to say something. “He's right. This is a serious situation, and if you had nothing to do with it the only logical step is to ask Michael if he did.” Von Kelsig produced the tattered note he had previously shown Sloan and examined it again. “Why would my husband have that? What if you left it amongst his things? Surely your stuff must get mixed up sometimes in the locker room? You did spend a whole year working together. Isn't that a possibility? Why would he have something that belonged to your girlfriend?” She didn't have to elaborate on that. The implication showed in the stern look on Von Kelsig's face. Tracy worried that Michael may have been cheating on her, but the giant knew the only infidelity had involved a greasy rat bastard from Memphis. “It's not what you think it is...he never went anywhere near her, and I damn sure didn't lose it amongst his things. I didn't even know Gloria had this in the first place...” He had done well to hide his emotions through it all, but the words written on that paper hung heavily on his heart, and he had to purse his lips to stop them trembling. Gloria had made her decision when she went behind his back with another man; Von Kelsig had loved and lost and he could live with that. The revelation written on this note however, changed everything. Had he known now what he had known then he knew he would have done anything to keep his family in his life, even if it meant leaving his career behind to start fresh. “So why did he have it?” The giant sighed. How was he going to convince her that her husband had murdered his ex when he had a hard enough time accepting it himself? It was the only explanation he could think of, it had to be true. All the evidence, no matter how far-fetched, had pointed to that one explanation. It had all started with a conversation he had shared with Sloan before last year's Colossus, during a heavy session on the sauce at some dive in Boston. As soon as the drink hit him he remembered spilling his heart out to Sloan about his failing relationship with Gloria. He couldn't remember much of it, just a few sentences and that sinister look in Michael's eye... “Fucken bitch. I hope sh gess wuss comin ta hurr. Bitch.” “I’m sure she will. Things like this have a way of working themselves out." Sloan patted Von Kelsig on the back, a smile spreading across his face. Slowly the smile twisted into a sadistic grin. As quickly as it grew it disappeared when Von Kelsig reared his head and locked eyes with Michael. "Cheersh mayn. Yer alrite." He grinned. "Hey," Sloan returned the grin and winked, "what are friends for?" He didn't think anything of it at the time, in fact the only reason he remembered it at all was because it had been his attempt to befriend the man who would put him through hell for the next year. A notion came to him just then. “Tracy, do you remember the Colossus show last year in Boston?” “Not really...” “Well...do you remember Michael saying or doing anything out of turn? Anything strange?” “I've never seen him do anything strange. He's never around long enough to do anything strange. Whenever we travel to shows or whatever he's out by himself. Half the time I don't know where he is...” Von Kelsig cocked an eyebrow, looking over at Daniels who must have been thinking the same thing, judging by his narrowed gaze. “Did he up'n vanish at Colossus last year?” “I don't...” “Think about it,” Von Kelsig pressed. “Boston. Biggest show of two-thousand-nine. You must remember Boston? Was he there with you? He wasn't booked until the second night so he had a whole week after arriving in town to spend time with you. Did he do that or did he go out by himself? You must remember something Tracy...” The petite blonde ran her knuckles under her eyes, wiping away the runny mascara stains. Exhaling a short sharp breath she calmed herself and gazed all about the room, trying to think. Daniels rolled his eyes and groaned, shifting his weight from one foot to the other. “Goddamnit I ain't gon' waste my fuckin' time here much long-...” “Yes,” Tracy finally pipped up, shooting Daniels a mean look before turning to Von Kelsig. “He did leave me. I remember that much. I don't know where he went but I remember being left in the hotel alone...he left me and...I don't know, I think I met up with some friends or something. I'm sure he definitely went off on his own though. I'm sure...” Von Kelsig glanced up at the Biker then shot Tracy a smile. “Good. If you're sure then-...” “I'm sure.” “...Then that's all I need to know.” The giant rose up from his seat next to the bed and made for the door. “Wait! Do I need to stay here?” Daniels opened the door for Von Kelsig and followed him into the basement, turning and giving Tracy one last look. “It won't be fer long. Just bear with us.” With that he closed the door, leaving Michael's wife alone on the bed, confused and upset. It wasn't long before she started sobbing again, as the two men made their way back up to see Sloan. ---- “Why'd ya ask her 'bout last year's Colossus? Fuck's'at gotta do wit' anything?” “If nobody can place Sloan on Night One, on the same night Gloria was murdered, then it makes it all the more plausible that he had something to do with it.” Daniels growled in retort as they made their way up the last few steps to Sloan's room. “Even if he weren't around it'd be a fuckin' coincidence. How's he gonna travel thousands'a miles just to kill a chick then ride all the way back'n time fer the show? Too many fuckin' holes in yer theory.” “Only one way to find out.” When they entered the dank room, they found Michael staring up at the ceiling with those serpentine eyes of his. As they approached him he clenched his jaw and groaned as another ache throbbed through his torso. Whatever comfort the morphine had provided was waning now, and it showed in the slightest movement he made. “How you feeling? Not going to pass out on me again are you?” Michael's eye twitched, but he never averted his gaze from the light above. “You better be fixin' to talk Sloan. I ain't gone through all this shit fer one'a yer fuckin' games.” “He'll talk.” “Whaw-huh,” Michael croaked, much to their surprise. “The fuck you just say?” Daniels asked, trying to decipher the raspy breath. “Whaw-huh...” “Water?” Von Kelsig chuckled, “you'll get a drink when I get some answers.” Michael tried to clear his throat, then coughed hard and let out a long wail as his insides burned. “Whaaaaw-huh.” “Tell me what I want to know and I'll give you-...” With a grunt, Sloan closed his eyes. “Hey,” Von Kelsig growled, nudging Michael in the side. Expecting him to yelp out the giant was angered to find no reaction from the broken Monster. Daniels took a turn, digging a finger deep into the bandages. After appearing to be in so much discomfort it unnerved both men to see Sloan lie so peacefully while they poked and prodded him like some kind of sick science experiment. Determined to get a response, Von Kelsig slapped his hand firmly down on Michael's chest. A shiver ran up his spine, and Christian too could not help but feel queasy as Michael lay perfectly still...his only reaction coming in the form of that forsaken smile which had fuelled the giant's rage at Colossus VII. He raised a fist high into the air. Eyes wide in fury he threw it down, and gasped as Christian Daniels reached out and snatched it from its trajectory. Glaring at the Biker, Von Kelsig pulled his hand away, seething. “Let's go.” Von Kelsig opened his mouth to protest. “Now.” The giant stomped out of the room behind the Biker, slamming the door shut and clenching his fists and throwing them up in frustration as Daniels held a hand up. “I'm done with his fucking mind games, Christian!” “Fuck 'im. He fucked up right there. Ain't nobody with nothin' to hide I knew lay there and smiled like the fuckin' Cheshire cat. Might be there's somethin' to this fuckin' theory'a yours. Time I finish with 'im he'll be singin' like a goddamn canary.” “Then let's get started,” the giant growled. “Nuh uh,” Daniels wagged a finger. “Ya said yerself I know how to deal wit' shit like this. Where's the evidence?” Von Kelsig produced the note and unfolded it, reluctantly handing it to Daniels and biting his lip. The Biker read it over and shook his head, quickly handing it back with a scowl. He could tell it upset the giant. “Ya spoke to the doc to confirm that?” The giant gazed at the floor, a slight nod verifying Daniels' query. “Yeah. Had a blood test and everything......I'll kill him Christian. If I find out that he did it, that he robbed me of my family...I'll kill him with my bare hands.” A violent man by nature and a connoisseur of pain and anguish for many years, Daniels was conditioned for punishment. The suffering he caused to his enemies came automatically, and Von Kelsig had witnessed that first hand. The giant's thirst for revenge on such an emotional level was rare for him, and the Biker couldn't shake the small morsel of pity he felt for the man. “There's more gratification in a lifetime'a sufferin' over an instant kill.” “Right now I couldn't care either way.” They were silent for a moment before Daniels broke the still. “Go to the store. Pick up some limes'n some salt, bottle'a Tequila'n a bottle'a Cointreau.” “What? This ain't no time for a fucking margharita...” The giant's naivete ired the Biker. “Just get the shit'n get back here A.S.A.F.P.” Cowering back in humility, the giant made it halfway down the stairs before turning back to see Daniels fumble around in his pocket and produce a Zippo. Then from inside his jacket he removed a knife of Japanese craft, its nine inch blade folded back into the handle. One flick of the wrist would quite literally open up a world of hurt for whomever crossed its steel. “What are you going to do with them?” Daniels smiled a sinister smile, licking his lips. “I'ma get the party started.” ---- Von Kelsig had to be cautious of his departure from the hideout. Though the street was filled with similarly empty properties and was just a stone's throw from the highway it was still in full view of the rest of town. He managed to ride out in his car without notice, and headed out of town towards the next county for the things Daniels had asked for. Throughout the drive the image of the Biker wielding that weapon stuck in his mind. It put things into perspective for him. He wasn't a cold blooded killer, and while he had dipped his toe in the grimy underworld that Daniels inhabited, still knowing what was going on at the house unnerved him. An old wound had been re-opened, and only the thought of becoming Universal Champion kept him from succumbing to a breakdown. His entire career had been leading to this upcoming title match against Castor V. Strife. His focus and determination had never been stronger, and with that the sting of this revelation concerning Sloan and Gloria didn't hurt so much. Were he back in the bad place of several months ago with nothing going for him he knew he probably wouldn't be breathing right now. The evidence pointed straight to Michael, to the man who had stolen from him the chance to become something other than a champion of the squared circle. He had no idea at the time that Gloria was with child, but that piece of paper he held onto so closely, his only connection to the past and a future that will never be, was the only proof that there was something else in this world for him other than cuts and bruises and pinfalls and submissions. He had struggled his whole career with the realization that that was all he was cut out for, and now thanks to Sloan it was all he was destined to be. ---- He bought the things Daniels had asked for without incident, and made the journey back to the safehouse in silence. He imagined walking through the door to find bits and pieces of Sloan strewn around the room, and the Biker pulling information from him with such ease. Michael was a sick fuck with an entire graveyard locked away in the back of his closet, but even he wouldn't be able to withstand Christian's interrogative prowess.........would he? Oncoming traffic forced him to drive right by the street on his return. He didn't want to risk anyone seeing any kind of activity in that suburban slum, even something as trivial as a car pulling into its unlit pavement. He drove on until the road was clear and did a U-turn, quickly manoeuvring off the main road before any more headlights showed up. As soon as he killed the engine he could hear cries emanating from the first floor bedroom. Quickening his pace he ran through the front door, racing up the stairs with supplies in hand and bursting through the door unsure of what exactly to expect. What he saw didn't shock him nearly as much as what he heard. “He won't fuckin' talk! Motherfucker! SPILL IT YA CUNT!” Daniels raised the hand clutching the knife, now stained with a blackish glaze, and swiped it across Michael's body. Dropping the bag on the floor, Von Kelsig moved around to get a better view and immediately staggered backwards in horror. Michael's body was drenched in claret, flowing freely from what looked like a thousand lacerations, deep and shallow, long and short. The Biker had carved him up like a butcher, and Von Kelsig could see the flesh twitching at various points. Michael's bandages, which Daniels had removed and deposited in a pile on the floor, were saturated with blood, and small pools were forming from drips running off the table. “What the fuck have you done?! Did you kill him??” “Like fuck. He's alive, he just won't FUCKIN' TALK!” Sloan's face had been sliced up worse than all the cuts on his body combined. No wonder he couldn't talk. His eyes were closed despite the lids being torn, his expression slack. He certainly looked dead...except for one thing that both infuriated and horrified the giant. That tell-tale smile remained etched on his face. His lips were slashed and bulging from repeated shots from the Biker, but the twisted corners formed that distinct grimace that had greeted the giant in the face of annihilation. He had grinned through the entire ordeal, with each cut smiling wider as if deriving pleasure from the horror. It had practically driven Daniels insane with rage. “Gimme the fuckin' juice!” The bag was in his hand in an instant. Everything was laid out next to Michael's body, and the knife was cleaned off. The thick odour of copper filled the giant's nostrils, and he had to take a step back as Daniels poured out a handful of salt, mixing it with lime juice and smearing the knife in it. “I'ma wipe that smile off his fuckin' face right quick.” “He's not human, man...” “Aw he's human a'ight. Watch this.” Before Daniels could pull Sloan's pants down, Von Kelsig backed away to the door. The Biker's large frame blocked his view of Michael's body as he hunched over with the knife in hand... ...the night air rang out with a shrill porcine scream as Michael finally stopped smiling.
