The New York Times “An Open Letter to America”-- From the Mind, Desk, and Registered Trademark of the World’s Most Marketable Tag Team Champion, Vangellus V-Izzle Oxios First of all, SHAME ON YOU! Since when did the collective intelligence of this great country (though not as great as Canada, but hey, we like to let you guys think that you’re super-cool and stuff) sink so low that Clyde Walkins seems to possess better faculties for logic and reason? I read the transcript of the 48 Hours show last week, and definitely saw The View (screw you, Elizabeth Hasselbeck is teh secksay). Scott is being so dangerous an individual? Recklessly endangering fellow wrestlers, if not actively attempting to injure them? Right. 48 Hours sounds about right in the title; it took them about 1 hour to dream up some kind of fictitious story about Scott, and the next 47 hours trying to find footage that went along with their skewed opinions. The world of professional journalism is in trouble when it makes FOX News actually look fair and balanced. Stone Phillips was off that night, and good for him because it seems that every wrong story on primetime news forces someone to resign. Well, whoever the hell that intern was who narrated for this story, get him the hell off of the network’s payroll. Listen up, kiddies; got some knowledge to drop on you people. WRONG: “Scott Matthews has an uncontrollable rage complex that makes him a danger to everyone around him.” FACT: Scott Matthews gets angry, just like any other person. He has his buttons, but techniques used to agitate him would work on anybody. Generally, it is understood within the wrestling world that if you don’t mention his family, and don’t hit him in the head, he’s no more dangerous than anyone else in the ring. The occasional botched move aside. WRONG: “Matthews made it a habit of hurting people that he got in the ring with.” FACT: Early on in professional wrestling, when he was learning, he had to learn how to tone down his strength. Yes, he’s a big boy, but he played college football and was accustomed to dealing with people, big or small, who could take the hits. There was an acclimation period, most definitely, but that’s always the case. There are a lot of instances of wrestlers coming in from other sports or combat disciplines and routinely bruising their opponents. WRONG: “It was on everyday wrestlers that were simply trying to carve their niche that this beast practiced a craft of pain and injury. FACT: Unintentionally injuring people hardly makes him evil. And whatever happened to assumption of risk? I mean, hell, why is Scott injuring somebody different than anyone else who injures somebody in the ring? What makes Mr. Matthews here get censured on national television, while Hessian was legitimately (with a smile on his face, mind you) injuring people left and right for like 3 years and nobody said anything? Hold on, a second, that’s the phone. Oh, America? Hypocrisy is on the line, he’s asking for a week off. WRONG: “Scott Matthews is an irresponsible influence on the children, who see him on television and will try to emulate him.” FACT: People will point to Lionel Tate as an example of wrestling gone wrong. Lionel Tate killed a little playmate with the Stone Cold Stunner. The prosecution tried to put wrestling itself on trial, but that fell through. It always does. Why? Because people with normal-level intelligence know not to do that. Everyone of you who are drawing parallels to Lionel Tate, and worried that kids are going to do like that by copying Rampage, stop it. NOW. How many of you retards seem to forget that Tate is legitimately, legally, medically retarded? Is that the case with the children of America? Are they all so stupid that they’re going to watch PRIME on Playboy TV (which most homes don’t even have on the cable box, so hey, your fears are blown right out of the water like a Battleship) at night, then go right out the next morning and get into one big country-wide battle royale? If so, isn’t that your fault, for raising such idiotic children? In fact, you people seem to be missing something very important. Scott Matthews is 6’6” and well over 300 pounds of muscle. How many kids in America fit that billing? Come on, people, let’s be reasonable. The United States is the fattest country in the free world. Your children, honestly speaking, aren’t in good enough shape to do what Scott does. And besides, wasn’t everyone all worried that the Power Rangers, way back when, would influence children to go all kung-fu on each other? And then they’d go to school and they’d all be kicking the air? Understand this about wrestling: it’s just as much a matter of good guys and bad guys as anything else. And guess what -- no kid wants to play the bad guy. Remember back when you were all kids playing cowboys and Indians? Everybody wanted to be the cowboys. Why? Because the cowboys were the “good guys”, and were supposed to win. So it’s the same deal. IF you’re worried about kids trying to act like wrestlers at home, what you’ve really got to be concerned about are jackasses like this. http://newentropylords.com/dumbass_kids.wmv And you’ll note that none of Scott’s moves involve what you just saw. WRONG: “Scott Matthews was an assassin that was sent to injure quaterbacks in high school football games?” FACT: Assassins don’t injure, they kill. For money. So tone down your language, people. But come on. That was Scott’s position as defensive lineman, to make sure that the ball didn’t get into play by plowing through the offensive line and take down the quarterback. He was doing what he was supposed to do. If you fault him for that, then you’ve got to fault every lineman in football, because they are all taught the same way. Take down the quarterback. People didn’t try to stop him because he was big and could head butt steel. People didn’t try to stop him because he was carrying out his duties as part of the team. People, at least those who supported his team, wanted him to do what he was doing. It’s about the win. And anybody who gets onto the football field and plays full-contact for more than three minutes has to be operating on the understanding that they can be injured, and in the case of the quarterback, that there’s a nice little bulls-eye on their chest at all times. Here’s a question: whatever happened to assumption of risk? WRONG: “James Combs, known under the ring name of Jimmy del Marco, was one of the victims of Matthews’ vicious attacks.” FACT: There is no record in any wrestling federation of there ever being a wrestler by the name of Jimmy Del Marco. There is a James Combs who worked for Ashfield Wrestling Conference out of Virginia, but he was a referee, and 50 years old. With but a little research, it can be proven that not only has Scott never wrestled a match against anyone named Jimmy Del Marco or James Combs, but these two have never even worked, in any capacity, for the same federation. But don’t let a little thing like the truth get in the way of your smear campaign. Geez, you’d think Scott was running for president the way that people are coming out against him. Oh, and a note to 48 Hours -- next time you want to have someone on the program, make sure you do a background check on them. It takes like five minutes. What do you people do all day, play foosball? WRONG: “No one is safe from his violence. Opponents, women, even his own tag team partner...” FACT: What’s Scott supposed to do, stand there and let his opponents win? You hit them until they stay down. And guess what, he stops there. That “frightening football tackle” that he puts people out with? Doesn’t kill anybody, doesn’t break any bones. All it does, aside from the pain of getting run over by a guy his size, is knock the wind out of the target so that they don’t have the strength to continue. After the three count, it’s over, he walks away, and the poor victim (who apparently had no idea that they were wrestling against someone so ferocious) gets up and leaves under their own power. Not that the last part matters to you. I won’t say that it’s a figurative low blow to show footage of Karina Wolfenden taking the RAGE. That’s just complete ignorance on your part for doing what should not be done: underestimating the toughness of K-Wolf. Hell, I’ve called her boring, and an ass, but as for her athleticism there’s a toughness and resiliency that cannot be denied, even by the blind. She’s taken far worse in more brutal fights with people like Magnum Wolf and Angelo Deville, and she keeps getting up. Hell, Karina has defeated Scott in singles competition. She’s far from defenseless. And really, if you had done that five minutes worth of research on her, you’d know that she’d be incredibly pissed if anyone went easy on her in the ring. So Scott going all out on her is really a respect thing between athletes. But the non-athletic community would know nothing about that. It’s intersex for a reason, people! Not every woman collapses into the fetal position when struck in the face. Some bring the fight to the man, no matter how big. Those women, you’ll find in wrestling. You do a great disservice by treating them like weak, defenseless victims when that is exactly the kind of image that these women attempt to dispel. K-Wolf footage… DENIED! And lastly, yes, Scott has taken me down with the RAGE. But, once again, there’s the R-word that comes into it. He did that to me when we were not partners. That was back in Rush Pro Wrestling, last year in the summer, when I was a singles wrestler (except for the brief team with Skylar Green in which I faced off against Scott back when he partnered regularly with Gabriel Brown). How can he be faulted for hitting me with a move when we weren’t partners at the time? Is he expected, looking back, to have said, “Wait a minute… a year from now, he’ll be my partner. Maybe just do a shoulder block…” WRONG: “Matthews attacked a religious retreat.” FACT: If a certified cult counts as a religious retreat, then the Branch Davidian was just a church picnic that was waiting for Billy Graham to swing by. It’s called the internet people! www.google.com. Why don’t you know this already? WRONG: “Scott has instigated bar fights in multiple states.” FACT: Scott Matthews does drink alcohol. But guess what? So do the other guys in those bars. And after they’ve downed like 20 Smirnoff Ices, those fools get their judgment impaired and think that they can actually take Scott in a fight. Has it ever occurred to you that Scott, being so big, KNOWS that he’ll win a fight with your average schlub in a bar, and thus doesn’t waste his time trying to start fights? But of course, if someone starts with him, it’s not like he won’t defend himself. What’s common bar etiquette nowadays? The guy who wins is the one at fault. Sometimes big-city logic isn’t that great. You can’t say that the people who start something with Scott aren’t at fault anymore than you could try to say that since he’s 350 pounds he should just let guys wail on him all they like. I’ve seen bar fights, and yes, even I have been in them, and those drunken yahoos will break chairs, pool sticks, and bottles on you if they feel like they need to. Scott is supposed to let himself be attacked like that so that the children of the world can have one less badass to emulate (which is good; one down, countless to go) _____________ So, in conclusion… DAMN, you people are dense. And that’s the problem. That’s MY job! I don’t like this role-reversal thing here. Can’t we all just go back to the way things were? Scott Matthews is not public enemy number one. He is not out to injure everyone he runs across. He wrestles to support his family, and makes far more money when he wins than when he loses. The man is a nice guy if you don’t go to lengths to piss him off. He’s got a fiance whom he has not once struck or to whom he’s not even as raised his voice. His mother and sister; they’re fine. They come and go in the house whenever they please, and don’t fear him at all. And his opponents; they know how to wrestle him. Scott is human. Maybe a stronger human than most, but still just human. He’s not a monster, and he’s not unstoppable. Don’t try to oversell him. We’ve got Nick Stuart for that. Sincerely, Vangellus Oxios
