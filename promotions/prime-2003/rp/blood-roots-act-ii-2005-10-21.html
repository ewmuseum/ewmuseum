Blood Roots: Act II

21 October 2005

Date: 10.21.05 Blood Roots: Act II Slumber. It had become a necessary part of my life lately. So many twists and turns in this saga that I called life had turned sleep into my most treasured moment. For it was through those congested eyelids and forgiving abyss that I found comfort and recess from the hell that the world threw at me. It was through that brief moment of oblivion where I found peace of mind. For that, I cherished it, and welcomed it anytime it presented itself, never clinging the urge to fight it off, for it was too precious of a moment to pass up, and the only rest period, which kept me on the brink of sanity. But on this night, I could not sleep. Trials and Tribulations had plagued me the past couple of days and sleep, even though sought and granted, had become just as painful as wake. Every time I clinched my eyelids closed, I saw his fiery scarlet eyes staring back at me with that look that only he could give. Every time I peered into his drastic pupils, I felt regret…regret for selling my soul to the spun of Satan himself. Regret that I gave up everything just for peace in my life…a peace that never came. Because through knowing that I placed my heart solely in the palms of his hands, I found nothing more than sleepless nights, heartache, and fraught. Suicide, at this point, was tempting, but I held my ground knowing that death would only summon me unto the Devil’s hell…an even worse abyss than presently so. I threw the sheets off of my body and glanced over at the alarm clock, which read Three O’clock a.m., informing me that the night was nearly over and I hadn’t got even five minutes worth of rest. Two more days remained until I stepped aboard to take that long plane ride to defend my belt at King of Kings, but that was the least of my worries right about now. I knew I could get it done in the ring…the Intense Championship around my waste and past accolades were testament to that. I wasn’t involved with any backstage politics, for quite frankly I could care less. The only thing that appealed to me was a signed paycheck at the end of the day, something to brighten up an otherwise bleak existence on God’s green and blue earth. I sat up in my bed, realizing that sleep was near impossible to come upon right about now. I took my fingers and ran them though my hair as I stood to my feet and stumbled to my nearby closet. I opened the closet door and retrieved a plaid shirt in which I slipped on, wrinkles and all. It was three o’clock in the morning…half of the country was asleep, so it was highly doubtful that my appearance would be significant. It was walking time. Sleepless nights always caused me to retreat to the outside area and take long walks to reflect over my purpose for living and maybe brainstorm on how to go from slum to king…again. I pushed opened the door, not even bothering to lock it as I stepped unto the cold pavement. All of my valuables were in Bogotá, so I wasn’t too worried of anything noteworthy being stolen. They say the freaks come out at night, but I could never comprehend exactly why. The eerie feeling that this environment gave you was chilling. The silence was a pain to bear, and the roaming car that drove by every ten minutes only added to the loneness feel. Even with its faults, though, it held prestige in being the only place in which I could browse with a clear head. I’m a constantly thinking person…sometimes that hurts me, other times it helps me. You would think that someone who was constantly thinking around the clock could easily come up with a solution to his troubles, and in most cases I could… But not this time… Not with Him as that existing Problem… I continued to saunter down the sidewalk, looking around and observing a neighborhood I knew nothing of…and from the looks of things I wanted not to know anything of. This place was a complete dump, two more days in this hole seemed nearly impossible, but I had learned how to prevail against the impossible. I could hear the roar of another car turning the corner, and as it passed me I glanced over and observed it. The person inside peeked at me with a wondering look on his face, but I shrugged it off…it was a look I was more than familiar with. As I glanced at the car though, it also caused me to glance at the opposite side of the street where I spotted a woman leaning against a telephone post. The loneliness that her face, illuminated by the street headlights, expressed made me think of my own pain as petty. The look on her face showed sorrow, despair, trouble in the heart. If anyone would know, I would… It’s a look I’ve expressed on many occasions…. Feeling that my presence would probably do the girl some good, I walked across the street. It was the middle of the morning, so I wasn’t too worried about being hit by a car although a side of me did wonder if this woman was some sort of killer waiting for some poor prey to bite on her lonely look. I was no stranger to danger, though, so fear barely came if ever. I had conquered some things that many would deem impossible…it was the only way I learned to survive. I reached the pavement and slowly made my way over to the girl who wore a hood over her head with her hands buried deep into her pockets. I figured she would notice me and maybe speak, but she never did. I attempted to take a couple of deep breaths to get her attention, but she never bit. Finally, the silence became too much for me to bear and I spoke up, hoping that she would maybe recognize my face from television and see me as no threat. My short tenor in the squared circle, though, hadn’t resulted in my name being very broad and she looked like the type that didn’t watch wrestling anyway, so it was nothing more than a mere hope it seemed. 'Ugh...Hello.' I began, trying not to come off as too agressive or abrupt. She didn't even bothered to look my way or even move for that matter, but I continued on anyway. 'Ar yo-...Are you okay? You look sort of l-...' 'I'm Fine.' she interrupted in a less than welcoming manner. I was a bit taken back by the sudden snap, but I held my cool knowing that it was not worth getting into an altercation this late at night that would only lead to the media finding out and having it spread all over the internet five hours later. I heard her take a deep breath as she prepared her apology as I stood there in awe. '...I-I'm sorry...its just I'm not too keen on strangers...or any sort of company for that matter. If you don't mind I-...' As she stated the latter, she began to turn around to look me in the eye. Once she was fully around, she immediately recognized my face and stopped mid-sentence as a half smile, half moment of shock expression crossed her face. '...Wow...you're Vangelus Olsig.' She stated, extending her arm for a hand shake. I smiled and extended my arm and shook her's firmly. I wasn't too fond on the whole fame and glory thing, but it was niced to be recognized by random browsers on the street. I mean, I wasn't a Karina Wolfenden, or Eli Fliar, or even a Boda for that matter, but for what I was, it pleasured my heart to know someone cared enough to...you know...care. 'Yes, I am Vangelus Olsig. Pleased to meet you Mrs...' 'Gordon. Veronica Gordon.' 'Nice to meet you Veronica. So tell me...what has you out this late at night in such dreerie weather?' 'Well....' She began as she slowly took her arms and slowly pulled down the hood from over her face, revealing a woman of pure beauty. Her skin was as tan as the color could get, her eyes consisting of a crystal blue contrast which seemd to grab me by the neck and yank me in. Her face was free of wrinkles, bruises...it was if the God's had taken special motives in crafting such a beautiful woman. Her body wasn't too shabby either. 'I'm not the most social person in the world. as a matter of fact, alot of the times I am alone, so I spend my time out here...observing.' She stated in a soft voice. I almost missed the message as I glanced over her beauty, but I quickly regained myself and caught on. Her story tore my heart in pieces...how could such a beautiful woman be trapped in such a lonley existence. The pity expression on her face did little to comfort my sorrow for this woman. It was the type of feeling that made you want to do something...anything nice to help that person out. 'How could such a beautiful woman like you be so lonely?' I asked, attempting to comfort her and respond appropriately at the same time. I could see her blushing a bit, but as she looked back at my face and realized who I was, the blush turned into another pitiful expression of sorrow. '...You're just saying that to be nice.' She stated, deeply believing in her small fragile heart that it was the truth, but what she didn't know was that everything I spoke of was pure genuine. She was as beautiful a woman I had seen since being in this hick town in the heart of Ohio, and ranked up there with some of the most beautiful I've seen...ever. There was something about her, though, that just drew me in with little to no effort. Something about her that made me feel like a part of her soul. Something about her...that attracted me to her. 'Actually...I'm serious. You're a beautiful woman and you deserve to be treated like a queen. Don't you believe you deserve that?' I stated, taking my palms and grabbing hers as I held them gently, grazing my thumb over her soft tan skin. She did nothing but smile, seemingly tainted by the fact that she didn't believe anything that came out of my mouth to be true. 'No...I've already recieved what I deserve.' She stated, drowning the smile once again. It began to seem as if my efforts to comfort this woman, though even for my very own pleasure, were falling helplessly in vain. 'No, you deserve better. You deserve someone who can hold you and bring forth a sense of comfort that you've never experienced before. You deserve someone who can make you feel like you're on top of the world each morning you wake up to thinking of him. You deserve someone...like me.' I stated, leaving her to glance at me with a bewildered look on her face. 'What are you trying to say?' She asked. 'Let me take you to dinner tonight...' I replied ,using my sense that since it was around three O'clock in the morning, Tonight would be the proper term. '...I-I don-....' 'Please. It'd be my pleasure.' I replied. Suddenly she released my hands and turned around to stare into the abyss for a moment. I could see her thinking hard on the situation, so I crept up closer to her to convince her slightly to side with my decision. Finally, she turned around and faced me... '...Okay. Just this once.' 'Great...I'll pick you up around seven. I'm going to show you the best night you've ever seen in your life.' I stated, smiling towards her and wrapping my arms around her in a hug as she returned the hug barely but surely. I never believed in love at first sight... But now I was victim of it... The air in this place consisted of a very elegant scent. It was the most fancy restaurant in the city and I had to give it credit...it was definetly a step up from some of the other dumps that I'd seen in this city. The waiter, decked out in a tuxedo, escorted us to our table where we both took our seats ready to enjoy a nice night of romance. For the first time in what seemed like ever, I wore a smile on my face. There was reason, now, to smile. I had before me a very beautiful woman, and any man that has been with a woman of such proportion knows how she can turned a bad day, or even year for that matter, into a good one with one swift action. This was the case here...a beautiful woman whom had rocked my otherwise troubled world. I forgot about the Intense Title defense...forgot about the troubles that plagued my life...forgot about my recent losses and suffering, because in front of me I had something greater them the all. And it was a positive... 'Nice Place' She stated, taking a few moments to glance around the building. I did the same. Obviously we were both seaching for something inside of ourselves to break the ice, and judging by the silence left unpure by the gentle sound of a violin, our attempts were falling in vain. Finally, I spoke up. 'I'm glad you decided to join me tonight, Vanessa. Believe it or not, you have done some incredible things in my life from the short period of time I've met you. Ever since we met, I haven't been able to take my mind off of you.' I stated, unable to maintain the smile that broadened across my face. 'Wow...Noone's ever said anything remotely similar to that to me before.' She replied. I reached over and placed her palms in my palms once again. Her hands were soft and moist, drawing me in like a magnet. I kissed the her hand gently and brought myself to look at her face once more which was also decorated with a broad smile. 'Yeah...well you deserve it. I just want to know, though... have you e-...' Suddenly I was interrupted as I heard stomps coming towards my way. I glanced over to my side to spot a man making his way over to my presence. Before I could even react, he was there with a sharpie and paper in hand. He extended the paper my way, obviously wanting a signature which I wasn't too fond on giving at this time. All I wanted was a nice night with a beautfiful woman whom I was deeply attracted to, but fame would not give me such. It always comes at the wrong times, doesn't it. 'Y-You're Vangelus Olsig!' The man begin as I just shook my head, bringing forth a half smile. 'Please Mr. Olsig...would you sign my napkin?' As much as I wanted to get up and send the guy tumbling down to the ground in a heap, I held my cool and calmly snagged the napkin and the sharpie, attaching my John Hancock to te napkin without saying a word and hadning the napkie back over to the man. 'Oh my Gosh...the kids will love this!' He stated, and with that he zoomed from my existence. I sighed, looking over Vanessa who preteneded to be playing with her nails in an obvious attempt at not making herself noticeable of the sitaution at hand. '...Sorry about that.' I stated. 'Its okay.' She replied. There was sudden silence between us again, something that I really wasn't too fond on having. 'So...what were you going to say before...you know...' She stated, trying hard not to play my attention as something that was bothering her, but I could tell by the sudden shift in her voice that it bothered her a bit and made her uncomfortable. It was times like these that made me reconsider the wrestling business, but that wasn't really relevant right now. What was relevant was making the woman before me happy and thats exactly what I sought out to do. 'Oh yeah...I was going to ask you...if you believed in love at first sight.' I stated. The expression on her face gave me the impression that she could feel me coming on to her. 'Well...I...' 'MISTER OLSIG! MISTER OLSIG! PLEASE CAN I GET A PICTURE!' came a booming voice which startled the both of us. I looked behind me to see a man with a camera on the verge of gracing my presence. I took another deep sigh, taking a moment to close my eyes and tell myself not to snap on these people. 'I'm Mark Seawright with the Ohio Advertisement...please can I get a quick picture.' The man stated, not even giving me time to respond nor crack a smile before he snapped the camera. Thankfully, the waitress walked over and informed the man that no cameras were allowed in the facility and that it seemed as if her was bothering the guest, so he escorted him out of our presence. They say good help is hard to find, but they sure are cherishable when you do find them. I looked into her eyes which gave a less than appauled look. As a matter of fact, the excitement she expressed when she first got here...she looked as if she wanted to get out of this date and get out quickly .I was bound to make it up to her, even if it took killing the next person to interrupt. 'Look, baby...I've truly, truly, truly sorry for all of these interruptions. I mean, I want this night to be special, but th-...' 'Don't worry about it, Vangelus. Lets just go ahead and decide what we're going to order.' she stated. '...Right' I replied. We both picked up our menus simultaneously, browsing through the various different options in order to decide on just which one we wanted. 'Were have you been!!!' I head a voice boom. I prayed to God that it wasn't another interupption, but the following pound of a fist on our table signaled that my prayers had gone unanswered. The anger boiling inside of me could not be expressed in mere words. I looked up and there before me was George Caspano, my designated trainer for my Intense Title defense against Paul Cain and he was outraged. 'I spent 5 hours in the gym waiting on you and you never show up? Whats the deal with that Olsig!?' He shouted. I wanted to remain mannerful, though, so I replied in a such form. 'Look...we'll talk about it later. I'm trying to enjoy dinner right now...' 'No, we'll talk about this NOW! You have a HUGE title defense comig up and you've got to be victorious or I don't get paid ' 'Look, George...I said we'll talk about this later. I'm in the middle of something right now.' 'Is this the reason you stood me up, Olsig? Huh? Just so you could eat at a poor excuse for a five star restaurant with some two dollar skank!??' He shouted, which, of course, put me over the edge. I quickly looked over to Veronica who was already stormed halfway out of the door. I jetted to my feet and stood toe to toe with George as I looked him soley in the eye. 'How DARE you insult her like that. You're fired, George...get out of my sight. I never want to see you again.' I stated, and with that I stromed off leaving George with a vacant look on his face. I quickly rushed to the outside to catch Veronica before she got away. As I turned a street corner, I finally saw her and ran behind her to grab her by her shoulders. She shurgs them off, but I place my hands on her shoulders once again. Finally, she turned around and stared me eye to eye. 'Look, Vangelus. It's been fun...but obviously fame won't allow us to be together. I'll be alright...trust me.' She stated. 'B-But, Ver-...' Before I could reply, she was already storming down the street. I buried my face into the palms of my hands and fell to my knee's... Killed by Love... Again. The scenario was different, but the situation was just the same. I was on my knees in the middle of my bedroom, burying my face in the palm of my hands trying to wash away all of the sowrrow that graced my heart. Tonight proved to me that I was not meant to have happiness in my life, therefore I simply gave up on it. If sleeping was hard last night, it was going to be virtually impossible tonight. Love, it had killed me before, but everytime I had fallen right into its trap. Happiness... Long Lost.... Grrr Suddenly, I shot my face up to stare into the abyss in which I had heard the growl come from. I looked all around me, but I saw nothing. I figured it as nothing more than a stigment of my imagination and returned back to burying my face, but then the growl presented itself again. This time I stood to my feet and surveyed my surroundings carefully. '...Who's there' I said, backing up towards the dresser in which a mirror stood. 'You said you wanted triumph.' Came the deepest, sadistic voice I'd ever heard in my life. It sent chills down my spine...not for the mere fact of its cold vibration, or its deep toned nature...but because I knew it was him. '...I'M NOT PLAYING YOUR FUCKING GAME ANYMORE!' I yelled out. 'You'll play for as long as I won't you to play, Vangelus.' came the reply 'No...NO! Its over. Threaten my life all you want, throw all the curses you want...but I'm tired of living for you. Leave me alone, Nosferatu.' 'Ah, but it's not that simply my child. You sold your soul to me in exchange for your life...theres no redeeming that. Look at me...' He stated. Suddenly, I turned around and peered into the mirro where I saw that oh so familiar face of his accompanied by those dark eyes. 'The terror in my eyes means that I am on a mission...that mission is going to be reached through you. Look at the counter.' He demanded as I followed and look at the counter where a carving knife laid with dried blood covering it. 'Oh m-...How'd this get here.' I stated. 'That's the knife we used to carve boda's chest in....thats the knife we used to end the career and times of Richard Larkin, but theres on man who proved himself as our greatest adversary. Even though we threw almost everything we had at him, he still fought back. His name is Paul Cain, and he's your opponent for this week. Finish the job that should have been finished a long time ago...end the man that brought torture to the Dark Age. Your designated weapon is laid before you...the match stipulation is perfect. End him in my honor, Vangelus...' He stated. I held the knife in my hands, glancing at it as it brought back all sorts of memories. Black Angel, Iganitus Lisieux, Emilio Rage, Boda, Shadow...they all fell victim. It was a time during my life in which I could not live with myself and wanted everyone else to feel my pain. A time in my life, so hideous that I fear ever going back again... '...Forget it, Nosferatu. It's over...OVER!!!' I yelled, tossing the knife at the mirror and shattering it to tons of pieces. I fell to my knees as the shatters of glass fell in place around me. Through my reflection, I found myself burying my face in my palms once again...knowing that I had just given that man...that demon....every reason to end me for good. Just like the Glass of shatter... So had become my life. Paul Cain. I am the Intense Champion. I defend this honor with pride. I accomplish my accolades with pride. You have acknowledged me as a threat... As have I... But now is not the time for ideal threats. Our history is filled to the core with violence. Betrayal. Suffering. Anguish. But if we can just take one moment to place that aside... Because for the first time in my life...I need you. I need you to end me. I need you to end the myth of Vangelus Olsig. I can't live this way anymore. I can no longer fight it. I wanted salvation from this madness... You can bring me that. End me, Paul... Or you may regret it. I may turn into something uncontrollable. I may reach a point in my life where I become a second spawn of Vampir himself. I can't do it Paul...I can't. End me while I'm still tameable. Forget ideal threats of blood and defeat. I need you to End me, Paul. End Me... Please.
