Punk'd

12 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12th July 2005 Ducking under the lariat which the Civil Disobedience hurls his way, The Jester flings himself into the ropes, only for Trevor Hawke to casually hook his foot. Feeling it just in time to stop himself falling flat on his face, Green grabs the ropes, the turns to kick at the Civil D member on the outside. With Gaz distracted, Masters again scores a brutal roaring elbow to the back of the Jester’s neck, almost turning his legs to jelly. Dropping forward onto the ropes, Gaz tries to cover up as an unrelenting barrage of forearms follow the elbow up, before Seth sets the Prankster up for a Side Russian Legsweep. As Gaz tries to fight free, Seth shifts his weight forward, dropping Green throat-first into the top rope, before flinging back with the Russian Legsweep. The Triple Threat bout flashed before the Jester’s emerald eyes like a near death experience as he stepped out from the confines of his bedroom. The laminate flooring was cold to the touch of his bare feet whilst the sun penetrated through the panes of glassed windows that allowed this resident to study his beloved Las Vegas Nevada from afar. Like a scientist pressed to his microscope, Gaz would often sit high above the smog infested streets below, watching with interest. “The back of my neck is sore as hell!” hissed The Emerald Menace aggressively as he draped one hand over the cause of his aggravation like an icepack, “Now I know what that Anne Boleyn must’ve felt…” “Not quite!” came the interruption. The Jester slowly pivoted full circle towards the source of the intrusion, and by doing so allowed a stream of sunlight to filter over his 5’ 11” frame and onto his unexpected guest, “ARGH!” he whelped. His screams would be more accustomed to that of gunshot victims, not those blasted by the UV rays of the sun. “SUNLIGHT! What are you trying to do, kill me?” cried the vampire as though he had been scolded by boiling water. Instinctively the P.W.A. behemoth threw his long flowing leather jacket up and over his exposed face creating a ultra-violet shield. Cautiously the comic ventured over to the victim, “You could do with a tan!” light-heartedly joked the Jester when suddenly streams of smoke began filtering through the various exit points of the leather jacket barrier. The Prankster’s cheeky grin had been tossed upside down from the events unfolding; his cheeriness was to be replaced as a look of horror washed over his face. “Rezz?!?!” he whimpered with panic and concern. The cocooned Nascosto Nerezza remained motionless, boxed in his black trench coat with clouds of smoke for company. The scene looked like something from the original ‘Gremlins’ movie, however cocooned wasn’t the peroxide blonde Stripe but something equally ugly… I mean terrifying, PRIME’s Nascosto Ireul Nerezza. “Rezz?!?!?!” again murmured the Jester. Pressing his angelic face closer, Gaz could smell the leather, and then it happened… AAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHH!!!!!!!! Like a lion emerging from the undergrowth the vampire peeled back his protective casing of leather and sprung forward snarling and chopping his saliva ridden incisors whilst Gaz defensively stumbled backwards from the oncoming threat. The former PCW starlets teeth chattered in response, only to be drown out by the maniacal laughter of his colossal partner who stood distinguishing a live cigarette in the palm of his pale complexioned hand. “You’re not the only one who can mastermind a prank or two!” he grunted as Gaz stood opposite trying to recompose. With deep inhalations the Emerald Menace regulated his breathing from a spluttering asthma sufferer to that of a decorated athlete, something Green had been referred to in the past. He was decorated alright, in his own spittle. “YOU… YOU… YOU BASTA-…” Again the vampire injected himself mid-sentence, “Now, now Mr. Green! there’s no need to get all blasphemous. Take it on the chin Gaz, you fell victim to a prank by your nearest and dearest, you have nothing to feel embarrassed about. Embarrassed would be if you were Punk’d by some eye candy toy-boy with a television crew…” “Where are they?” asked a paranoid Gaz Green. Frantically the Jester rummaged around his penthouse apartment, work surfaces cleared, doors yanked open and furniture tossed over, still he chirped, “Where are they?” with an assertive mannerism. “WHERE ARE THEY?!?!?!” he cried, still in a state of paranoia. Being a P.W.A. role play the shock factor would be lost if we opted to have Ashton Kutcher casually stroll in with a defeatist attitude, so we’ve decided to use him sparingly as PRIME don’t pay nearly enough to have BIG walk on parts. With the gift of the gab he’s sweet talked his way into television and movie roles, hell he’s even charmed his way into Demi Moore’s knickers, thus P.W.A. will put a halt to this surge and give him three minutes airtime, which coincidentally is the same as ‘The Best Of Chris Storm’. Which can be found in bargain bins across the nation, rumours has it that the DVD makes a good beer mat whilst the decorated case, if left over a fireplace wards off small children. “Where are they?” asked a paranoid Gaz Green. “Where’s who?” questioned the Bakemono with a tilt of his eyebrows. “You don’t honestly think my Oscar winning performance was concocted by a bunch of students, especially that Ashton Kutcher. Now I’d love to do a Demi and nibble his neck but I’ve seen more meat on a vegetarian’s toothpick!” he concluded, and with an open palm he slapped his partner on the back with a friendly pat. “AAARRRRRRRRGGGGGHH!!!!” winced the LAST EVER reigning PCW Continental Champion. I like to emphasise ‘Last Ever’ since I never officially lost the title even though it was put around the waist of another after me, but that doesn’t count since the last incarnation of PCW was, well, crap! The Prince Of Pranks arced his back as a dose of pain shot up his spinal cord, the after-effects of competing in a triple threat with talent on the merit of Gabriel Brown and Civil Disobediences’ Seth Masters… Ignoring the ref’s count, Gabe releases, then again pummels Gaz with a string of hard forearms to the back of the neck. With the resolve beaten out of him, Gaz is then flung overhead with a German Suplex, the landing angle so steep that Gabe can’t get his bridge. Grabbing the back of his head, Gaz instinctively staggers up, only for Brown to lift him for a Backdrop Suplex, then spin and flip him down into a Blue Thunder Gutbuster. As Green doubles over, Gabe locks in a double underhook, then heaves Gaz up to drive him into the canvas with a Butterfly Powerbomb. Gabe then pushes Gaz’s legs forward pinning him. “Still reeling from last weeks ReVolution huh?” “You could say that squirARGH!” blurted Gaz “The Jester” Green, howling like a banshee as he did so. The pain was evident as the comics face contorted with every murmur within his fragile body. What doesn’t kill you makes you strong, or so they say, well Gaz felt like a bullet to the side of the head would be more gratifying as another pain shot up his bruised back. The vampire could only smirk. “You know what your trouble was last week, you went in there all guns blazing. We’ve been away from the business awhile, so instead of battling away in the hope you can dig out a victory, try using you head…” with that said, Rezz placed both hands over his partners head, his talons sweeping through the green forest Barnet. “ARGH!!!!” yelped Gaz. But as Gaz goes to grapevine the leg, Seth squirms free, then adjusts his footing to trip Gaz, seamlessly collapsing them into an STF. The crowd hurl boos at the ring as Seth secures his leg grapevine, then wrenches back on Gaz’s neck. The muscles straining are plain to see as Seth tries to break the Jester in two, smirking as he sees the ropes out of reach. With all eyes on the submission attempt, and on Green’s wavering hand, Gabe rolls out under the bottom ropes, and drags himself to the top. Before the growing cheers can alert Masters, Gabe hurls himself off into a 450 Splash, crashing his rotating body down onto the straining duo. The extra torque applied just as Gabe hits leaves Gaz close to passing out, and he barely manages to roll to the ropes, then drops to the mats outside. “Oh, sorry squire!” said Nerezza drawing his hands back carefully, “The only reason I came over was to sneak in a little gym time since we are booked but seeing as you have the mobility of Steven Hawkings today I think we better do a God…” “WHAT?! You mean, make some ridiculous wrestling promotion and embarrass our locker-room by having them compete in submarines and light sabre duels…” intrusively injected Gaz, thankfully he was cut off by the more level headed of the two in Nascosto Nerezza. “Wrong God, I was thinking of the geezer from the Bible. The one that has dumb asses building boats and writing grocery lists on stone tablets, well he had a day of rest and I think we should follow. So on the seventh day Rezz said let P.W.A. have the day off and let it be known as the Sabbath, thus the Sabbath was born… just it wasn’t black nor did it have a stuttering mess for a lead vocalist!” The Human Horror Film was right, as usual. Their bodies weren’t use to the physicality of the wrestling business, having been away from the industry for more than a year there would need to be readjustment. The Conniving Collective needed conditioning if they are to compete with the best of PRIME, but at-least the last two weeks have offered glimpses of promise and with ‘Colossus II’ looming, now is the time to come good. It wasn’t the guillotine of his throat, or the German Suplex to his back, nor was it 212 lbs. of Gabriel Brown plummeting down on his head that hurt the most… Stunned, Seth springs up out of instinct, only for Gabe to grab a front facelock, then step through to fling himself back into a Legsweep DDT, smashing Masters’ forehead into the canvas. Just enough left in the tank to roll Seth over, Gabe drapes an arm over him. One! Two Three! It was the Jester’s pride that hurt the most. In recent weeks it had appeared as though he was dropping the ball, two straight defeats with the only excuse, “Well I haven’t been pinned,” at his disposal, and now faces the hat-trick as Pranks With Attitude square off against three other teams. Having crippled Chris Storm and offloaded The Solution from the OSW talent roster, the pranksters have been brought back down to earth with a BANG! Both Gaz and Rezz slouch into the leather reclined seats occupying the living quarters. The vampire with remote at his disposal switches on the neighbouring television set and the screen flickered into life, a kaleidoscope of colours. Staring at the duo was various snippets of wrestling footage that have stood the test of time, some key points of the WrestleOnline (WO) era. Scientologist Xavier Kannon, a ditzy Eleanor Kannon-Hall, Dukester The Goblin, The LUNAtic James Edwards, Jason O’Neil in all his various guises, a pencil pushing Tim Walden, WAIT, has there ever been any other version? “Even some of the greats have had to repackage themselves!” proclaimed the vampire, his eyes fixated upon the screen. “Yeah, that’s right. Hmm, I never thought of that…” The behemoth’s words struck a nerve with Gaz Green, without realising it he may be onto something. With an impending Four Corners Match just around the corner, let’s hope so as P.W.A. go after their first PRIME victory.
