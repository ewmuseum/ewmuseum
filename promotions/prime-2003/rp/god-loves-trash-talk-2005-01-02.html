God Loves Trash Talk

2 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/05 The holiest man in the world and your Universal Champion Hoyt Williams sits on the couch with his fiancé head resting in his lap. She is lying on her back looking into his eyes while he strokes her gently. She smiles softly dreaming of marital bliss. Hoyt Williams: The owner of PRIME must have something against me forcing to work this week and open the show. The Mother Mary: At least you’ll be done quickly. Hoyt Williams: Always the optimist aren’t you? The worse part of it not only am I opening the show in Memphis aka pig town I’m facing a black guy. The Mother Mary: Tommy Collins is black? Hoyt Williams: As the bottom of my shoes. Do these people not understand that not only am I PRIME’S champion but also God’s son. I can’t be bothered facing rookies I mean he doesn’t even have a win yet and he’s facing me? The Mother Mary: He also doesn’t have a loss. Hoyt Williams: Neither does Mr. Bean but that doesn’t make him in the same league as me. I have walked on water, turned fish into steaks, and cured Tom Cruise of homosexuality. Not to mention prayed Boda out of wrestling, scared Hessian off, made Vampir see the light, and knocked off Killean to become the Universal Champion. Now the powers that be want to make me teach some ham and egg eating rookie the ropes? Who am I Kano? The Mother Mary: No you rock much harder. Hoyt Williams: Damn right I do. The Mother Mary: You want some tea? Hoyt Williams: Earl Grey please. The mother of Hoyt’s son gets up and heads to the kitchen to make some tea. Hoyt sits on the couch shaking his head staring at the profile of Collins. Hoyt Williams: Hey babe, it says this guys middle name is Lamar. LAMAR!! If that doesn’t reek of Assault and Battery and ten years in the pen I don’t know what does. This guy thinks he’s an enigma I just think he’s a waste of a roster place. I guess PRIME does need somebody for Kano to defeat. The Mother Mary: What he rubbed Kano’s feet? “Your Personal Jesus” doesn’t correct Mary who is in the other room listening over a steaming kettle of water. Hoyt just stares harder and harder at the stupid picture of the less then attractive Collins. Hoyt Williams: Speaking of black folks did you arrange for the boycott of the Kentucky Fried Chicken down the street? The Mother Mary: Yea, we should have about thirty demonstrators there to help shut down that hide out of Satan. Hoyt Williams: The bible clearly says that our bibles are our temples to the lord yet these walking dead still go to KFC? There is nothing on that menu that is good for your body. Plus they lace their poultry with cocaine to hook the black folk into coming back perpetuating an army of obese, addicted, soldiers of Satan. Have you ever seen a picture of Coronal Sanders? That’s clearly Satan. Who else would wear a white suit with think black glasses and a creepy beard. How dumb are people. The former Virgin Mary walks into the room carrying two mugs full of hot steaming tea. She hands Hoyt his drink and sits on the other end of the couch to watch a little TV. Hoyt Williams: Does this Collins putz think this is amateur hour at the Show Time At The Apollo dance theater? Because if he does his ass is about to be chased off the stage by a clown with a broom because I’m not having it. The Mother Mary: Since when did you start watching the Apollo? Hoyt Williams: ahhh it was a research project on the behaviors of African Americans in a theater setting. Anyways what matter is that I show the world why I’m God’s hand selected champion and it’s not because I’m his son. God isn’t about nepotism he’s about picking the best man for the job, and that man just happens to be his son, who just happens to have a prefect body with a perfect lady, and the perfect in ring skill, and that just happens to be me. Jesus was a fucking hippie but his baby brother is a man and a warrior and Leon Collins is about to learn that. The Mother Mary: You mean Lamar? Hoyt Williams: All those names sound the same to me the champion. Hey look it says this guy has held some gold in some half ass wrestling organization. Don’t these bastards understand this is PRIME and not the minors? I’m not some wrestler claiming to be God’s kid, or an angel, or God himself but rather I’m the real deal. So Mote It Be. The Mother Mary: Since day one people have doubted you but time and time again you have proven them wrong. You have risen to become the most dominate wrestler in the business today. Don’t forget they doubted Jesus too. Hoyt Williams: But unlike him I’m not going to let them send me off to boarding school. Maybe I can offer this kid fifty bucks and a watermelon to stay the hell away from me. Not because I’m scared of him but rather because my time isn’t worth being wasted. After this match this kid is going to have a story to tell all his friends. Hey I stepped in the ring with GOD’S SON and thee UNIVERSAL CHAMPION and got my ass kicked. It was the greatest day of my poor ghetto beaten life. I’m NOT Dr. Phil it’s NOT my job to give rookies stories to tell their friends. The Mother Mary: That’s assuming a sinner like him even has friends. Hoyt Williams: Chapter Three begins a new revelation in wrestling the book of Hoyt. From this day forth all past history will be forgotten as the greatest in the history of the business christens a new era. We will move forward cleaning up the air waves and making PRIME a temple of the lord. A program of worship in our culture battle of good and evil. The secularists will not win not with me leading the charge. Imperium now rules the wrestling world and nobody not Nova, Killean, or Collins is going to stop. The faces are now looking at the face of God himself and it’s simply stunning. The Mother Mary: Well spoken champ. Hoyt Williams: So it is written so it is truth. =w=
