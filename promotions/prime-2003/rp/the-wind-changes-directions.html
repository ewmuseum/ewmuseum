The Wind Changes Directions

undated
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReV 151 Her head turns ever so slightly, the long brown hair moves with sheer grace, and a pair of soft blue eyes pierces his heart. Craig can only stand there, feeling the weakness in his knees, as a wave of uneasiness washes over him with sanguine hope. He gazes at her with, sadness lingering in those piercing red eyes. Craig's hands move unconsciously to his pants pockets while he wonders what she's doing back her in his house. Emotions hit him like a freight truck; sadness, happiness, anger. She'd walked out on him, not given him a chance to explain himself, and now here she was. "Katie..." His voice wavers ever so slightly, trembling from the shock. A radiance comes over her face. She licks her lips ever so peacefully as it's evident she's been enjoying herself the past few weeks. "Hey. Long time not see." "I just...," he scratches his head, trying to regain his confidence, "I just called you." The words just stumble out of his mouth, almost falling over each other, while he pulls out his phone and shows it to her, hoping to validate his statement. Katie just simply nods her head, almost wishing she hadn't come back; partly because Craig was acting like a dork now. "I noticed. I just wanted to surprise you, that's why I didn't answer." Craig nods his head, the uncomfortable silence filling the room by the second. "So, what're you doing here?" "I missed you. Not a day went by that I didn't think about you." "Then, you should've never have left." "Are you being serious? Could you honestly, truly, be that dense?" A purse, black and leather, drops from her right hand and onto the couch as she strides over to him, anger taking over her mind and filling her face. "You lied to me. Kept things a secret. I never did that you! Never!" "I know I kept things from you, but that's no reason to walk out on me like you did!" "You've got to be kidding me. You told me that you couldn't have a relationship! You told me that and then you go behind my back to be with Nicole! You lied straight to my face!" "I know what I told you Katie--" "Then, why?! WHY?! That's what I don't get." "It's complicated." "Then uncomplicate it!" "I-- I just can't." "You can't?! You can't explain it to me?! I poured my heart out to you, Craig! You told me that you couldn't be with me, not anyone, and you go out with Nicole! Do you understand what that did to me? To hear it from Daniel?! I LOVED YOU!" Silence. It fills the room ever so perfectly as Craig just stands there stunned. A look comes over Katie's face as it's obvious that she didn't mean for that to come out. Craig just looks at her as her cheeks begin to blush from the outburst. Her eyes glance down as she twirls her fingers, trying to figure a way out of this. "You loved me?" "Yeah. Still do." "I'm such an idiot." "Well, if we're going for the obvious statement award, then you win." "I should've been honest with you." "I wish you would've been. You could've told me about Nic--" "No, not about that. I mean, why I couldn't be with you." "There's another reason?" Craig nods his head as he takes a step back, wishing that he could've kept this from her. "My daughters’ death. Every time I was with you, I just thought about her. I couldn't stop. When I'm with you, I equate her death to you and it drives me crazy. Purely insane. I can't handle it. It's just too much." Katie can only stare at him, baffled and surprised at the revelation coming from Craig's mouth. Now, Craig can't look at Katie though as he closes his eyes and wishes that he was someplace else. "You do?" "Yeah..." "I never knew..." "I know you didn't. It's not your fault, I know that too. But, you came in during that time and I put you and her together, and it's just too much. I miss her so much that it nearly kills me! I wanted to give us a chance and I couldn't because her face filled my mind each and every time." "I can't imagine..." She can't finish the statement though. Katie just stands there, shocked by everything. Part of her wants to run, get out of this house, but she doesn't. Her mind knows that this is where she's supposed to be. Here, with him, in his life. "We've got a kind of crazy life." "No kidding." "So, where do we go from here?" "Move forward." Katie nods her head as she begins walking up to Craig. Deep in her stomach, Katie can tell that Craig's hurting. The thought about his daughter must be nearly killing him at this point. Her feet carry her the rest of the way as she wraps her arms around Craig and he just holds her. "I'm sorry, Craig." "I'm sorry for not being honest with you." "It's okay." They stand there in silence for a moment. "You know that she's going to come back, right?" "God, I hope so. I really do." They break their embrace. "I know she will. Nicole will remember why she fell for you and come back. You just have to be patient." "Some days it's so hard." "It's supposed to be. She found out--everything-- in such a short time. Your past, it's always going to be there, haunting her now." "That's what scares me." "It should, but she's strong. She'll come back." "Yeah..." Then, Craig looks up at Katie with a certain look on his face. She can see the look in her eyes and knows that something's wrong. "What is it?" "Nothing." "What?" "Nothing, look, I'm going to go lay down. I’m just tired all of a sudden. Sorry. Excuse me." "Alright." Then he walks away. Yet, it isn't exhaustion that's got him on the verge of tears. It isn't anger or sadness tearing at him that nearly causes him to pass out in front of her. It's the pain in his head that sends him crashing to the ground the moment he closes the door to his room. It's the headache that has him on the verge of unconsciousness. ****~~**** KNOCK! Riiiiing! KNOCK! Riiiiing! KNOCK! Riiiiing! Slowly, his eyes begin to flutter open. He tries his best to ignore the pain between his eyes that nearly blinds him, and looks around the room. Craig hasn't moved an inch since collapsing on the floor. How long have I been out he thinks to himself as his wishes desperately turn to trying to make the world stop spinning around and around. His nearly numb fingers finally find his phone and he places it to his ear. "'ello?" The knocking though refuses to cease. "Craig, are you okay?!" Craig closes his eyes as he tries his best to block out all of the noise assaulting him at the same time. "May I speak to Mr. Maloof?" His arm suddenly jerks away as he looks at the phone and searches his mind for who's calling him. He glares at the screen and sees the phone number displayed prominently. [detail withheld] "Mr. Maloof?" KNOCK! The frustration grows to be too much. "HOLD ON!" Then, there's nothing. He breathes a sigh of relief as he puts his head back down on the wooden floor and closes his eyes. "Thank you." He breathes the words effortlessly as he can feel the pain in his head only getting worse. After looking at the phone again, Craig figures out who it is that's calling him and quickly puts the phone back to his ear. "Yes, this is him." "Have I caught you at a bad time, sir?" "Nah, I'm good now. How can I help you?" "My name is Samantha and I'm calling from Doctor Green's office. I just wanted to call and inform you that Doctor Green would like to know if you have any free time today to come in and see him?" "Yeah, around what time?" "He says that it needs to be as early as possible." "Okay. Is everything alright?" "The final test has come back sir." His mind freezes as he wonders what it's revealed and whether or not it will help stop the migraines from happening incessantly. The silence fills the air before he takes a deep breath. "I'll be there as soon as possible." "Thank you, sir. I will let Doctor Green know that." Craig nods his head, not remembering that the woman on the other end can't even see him. He flips his cell phone closed before putting it back on the ground and then wipes his face with both of his hands. A million thoughts rush through his mind as he tries to figure out what the news will be. His stomach begins to churn as he goes over the different possibilities. Anxiety starts to set in as he sits up and remembers that Katie is standing on the other side of the door, waiting for him. As he begins to stand up, he can feel the pain in his head return, but he pushes it away for now. At this particular moment, she needs to know that he's alright and not to start worrying about him. He walks over to the door, each step sending a wave of nausea through his body, before opening the door and smiling at Katie. "Hey." She looks at him, completely unconvinced. "I was knocking on your door for five minutes. I'm pretty sure you didn't become a heavy sleeper all of a sudden." He shrugs his shoulders. "I didn't hear you. What do you want me to say?" "Who was that calling you?" "My... psychologist." She looks at him for a second with a cocked eyebrow. "Okay, there's about fifty questions that come with that revelation. Why's your psychologist calling you this early in the morning?" Now it's his turn to look at her confused. "Morning?" "Yes, morning." His mind begins to scramble, wondering how long he'd been out. He turns his torso slightly and looks desperately for a clock of some sort. Unfortunately he can't find any as he scratches the top of his head, trying to recall some kind of memory. The last thing Craig can remember though is him passing out on the floor. Had he been out for that long? Part of that question worries him some before looking back at Katie. "What time is it?" "Almost eight. Why do you look like that?" He holds up his hand quickly, shocking Katie slightly. "Sorry, just give me a second. I'm trying to get my bearings back." She nods her head as she takes a step back. "Alright, I'll be downstairs. I made you some breakfast. Wake up a little bit more and we can talk." The thought of breakfast brightens his mood, but also worries him. "Since when do you cook?" "It's one of the things I learned during my mini-vacation." "Oh, joy." The tone from him is rather sarcastic and she just smirks at him as he turns around and walks back into his bedroom before shutting the door. As he tries to open his eyes all of the way, he can tell that there's something seriously wrong with him as he's certain that he blacked out yesterday. Craig steps into his bathroom and flips on a switch before walking up to the bathroom mirror. The look on his face tells him nothing, but he can some faint marks on his face and feels the haziness inside of his mind. Without even thinking about it, he turns on one of the faucets and splashes his face with water, hoping to come out of his dream. "Come on, Craig, snap out of it." The words seemingly come from another person as he shakes his head before turning off the running water and walking out of his bedroom. Draught sets in his mind as he begins to worry more and more about what the doctor has to urgently tell him. Craig desperately tries to push the thought from his mind, but is unable to. He opens the door to his bedroom and begins walking down it, each step in the long, narrow, stark hallway feeling like a hundred years pass in between them. Somehow, he finally walks down the wooden steps before making his way into the castle-like kitchen. On top of the bar style counter rests a paper plate with some eggs and toast. He smiles at the thought of waking up to a home cooked meal, his first in quite some time. The last time he’d had a meal like this was when Nicole cooked for him. The thoughts of Nicole flood his mind, but he quickly puts up a wall to block them so he can remain focused on Katie. Craig walks over to the plate and sits down before noticing Katie in the kitchen, making some more eggs. "Now, this is honestly a scary sight." She turns towards him and sticks out her tongue at him while her pink t-shirt slightly sticks to her chest from the sweat of working in the hot kitchen. He just shakes his head before rolling his eyes. "Dig in." Cautiously, he picks up the fork sitting next to the paper plate and sticks into the scrambled eggs. They look rather moist and tasty, so he takes a deep breath, bracing himself for possible death or his esophagus closing up. He sticks the few pieces of egg into his mouth and begins to taste the cooking. Surprisingly, and it shows on his face, it tastes rather good. "See, you should just trust me." Craig smiles as he looks at her and chews on the seasoned eggs before swallowing. He begins to relax now as he picks up the darkened and buttered toast and takes a bite of it. Katie walks over to him and just shakes her head. "You're just too funny." "Me? You're the one cooking now. That's a treat in itself." "You have no idea." "Can you cook anything else?" "You'll have to wait and see." He nods his head as she's won this small battle. She looks at him before taking a bite of his toast and then walks back over to the stove to finishing making her breakfast. "So, you're seeing a psychologist?" "Yeah." "That must be rather interesting." "It is." "Breaking through any barriers?" "I've only seen her twice. Today will make a third time." "Did you threaten to kill her or anything like that?" He laughs at this, only because it's the truth. "Yeah, you could say something like that." "I know you far too well." "You do." "Why is she calling you to make an appointment then?" "Oh, the time we'd set for today didn't work for her and she wanted to know if I could come in early. That's all." Katie turns around takes a look at Craig. She studies him for a second, wondering to herself if he's lying at all. Before she can finish though, Craig's phone starts ringing. As he stares at the caller ID screen for a second, he knows this number immediately. "Oh, no, I'm not talking to Lisa this early in the morning." "Tyler? She called the house earlier." Craig looks at her, slightly alarmed. "She did?" "Yeah, she needed you to give her a call as soon as you can." Craig takes another bite of his eggs, trying to figure out what could be so important for Lisa to be calling him at home and on his cell phone. He thinks about this for a second before shaking his head and taking the last bite of the eggs on the plate. Craig licks his lips, wishing there was more to be had, but then the fear sets back in and it nearly paralyzes him as he places his cell phone down on the counter. Katie takes a look at him and can just tell something's not right. "Craig..." Her voice wakes him up as he looks at her and smiles. "Sorry, still a little tired. I'm going to go take a shower and get dressed so I can get this appointment over with, and then we can hang out some more today. Maybe do some shopping?" She nods her head at this suggestion. Then, a smile appears on her face. "That sounds like a good idea." "Okay, good." He then gets up, leaving his cell phone there, and walks upstairs with the weight of the world on his shoulders. Katie watches him walk up the stairs before walking to the counter and grabbing the phone. She flips it open and begins playing with it for a second before finding the phone number that had called him while she knocked on his door. She looks at the phone number before picking up her own cell phone and dialing it into her phone. Katie looks at the screen for a second before hitting the talk button and placing it to her ear. It rings a few times before a woman picks up. "Doctor Green's office." She pauses for a moment, before moving forward. "Hi, my name is Katie, and I was wondering if Doctor Green had an appointment open today." "He does have an opening today, but I'll need some general information first. What kind of ailment are you suffering from?" "Ailment?" "Yes. What kind of headaches are you having? How serious is the pain? Any other symptoms?" Katie is rather shocked by this as she can't find the words to say anything. Moments pass while she tries to digest all of this information. "Hello?" "I'm... sorry. I think--sorry-- I think I have the wrong number." Then, without even waiting for the response from the other end, she hangs up her phone and puts it on the counter. Her eyes move over to the staircase and only one thing is on her mind. What is he hiding from her? ****~~**** "I'm sorry for keeping you waiting." Craig turns in his chair, having lost himself in some random daydream, and smiles at the middle-aged man dressed in tan slacks and a white lab coat. He quickly gets up out of his chair and shakes his hand. "It's okay sir, I just hope everything's alright, Doc." "Please, call me Warren." "Okay, Warren. What do you have for me?" Warren moves to his desk and takes a seat in a rather plush leather chair before pulling out some files out of his desk marked with confidential on them. He flips open one of the manila folders and begins to look at a few documents. "As you have asked, I've kept this extremely confidential, not letting out who the patient is and looking at the results myself. No one on my staff knows why you're coming in here, and when these results came back to me, I decided that I would talk to you myself on this because I know how important it is to you." "I appreciate that very much, Warren." "Now, when you came to me in November, we talked about the different possible situations that it could be. You told me about how you get frequent headaches and migraines so I prescribed you with a few drugs hoping to help remedy that problem, but from what you told me at that time, that didn't work at all." Craig shakes his head as he recalls the evening of ReVolution when he was in the middle of a discussion with Lindsay Troy and collapsed. The bottle of pills previously mentioned had rolled out of his right hand and she protected him by hiding them. "So, we started running some more basic tests, which of course were difficult because of your heavy travel schedule. But, your MRI and CT scan showed no abnormalities. I was concerned about some swelling, but after your second MRI it disappeared and I just imagined that it might've been a brutal bump that you took during one of your recent matches. However, fatigue was starting to set in and I knew that whatever it was, was only getting worse by this point." "Correct, doc, and it's not getting any better to tell you the truth. I passed out yesterday from a migraine sometime in the afternoon and didn't wake up until this morning when your office called me." Warren looks at Craig and purses his lips before looking back at the paperwork. He then settles into his chair and takes a look at Craig curiously. "You have a tingly sensation in your arms and legs every now and then?" Craig nods his head. "Sudden or prolonged pains in your joints?" "Yep." "Well, okay. Interesting. So, we took some blood and that came back inconclusive. I did a biopsy and that came back good. That told me that you didn't have cancer, which is always a good thing. So, I decided to do a procedure that I don't tend to like to do which is the lumbar puncture. Normally, because it never gives out a good reading. Alone, it won't always reveal everything, but along with other tests, it can normally give you a good conclusion as to what it is. Those results came back to me this morning and confirmed a fear that I've had, one that I haven't shared with you quite yet." "Alright, Warren. What's that fear?" "Craig, you have..." ****~~**** "So, what did he say you have?" Craig realizes that the words are stuck in his throat while he looks down at the ground. The words had replayed in his mind over and over again, never sounding quite right, but nonetheless, true. As he looks back up and stares at Silvia, his psychologist, he tries to find the right words to say it. "Craig?" "You know, when he told me..." "Just tell me what it is Craig." She moves to the edge of her chair, knowing that whatever it is, it's serious. Craig looks at her, sitting there in her black, knee-length skirt, a lacey white blouse that's probably far too revealing for their meeting here today, and a pair of pink heels that are just odd with the combination that she's wearing. He licks his lips before tilting his head back, wishing that this was all just one bad dream. Yet, he realizes that it's not, and settling his head on his shoulders, it moves from side to side as he just tries to come to terms with it. It's no use. "Craig." His eyes open and he looks at her again. "So the doctor told me that I have..." He goes silent again, the words stuck there. "Craig?" Craig clears his throat. "I have multiple sclerosis."
