The Dungeon!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: The Super Bowl onwards Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): Welcome, TO THE DUNGEON! (Shots of crippling torture are seen. People being caught up in wooden racks, chained to ceilings. There's an abrupt record skip sound heard, and the picture freezes as if it's an old VHS tape.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): NO! Not that dungeon! (The picture picked up speed, as shots of professional wrestling trainees are shown gliding around the ring. One combination of amateurs sees a tilt-a-whirl side slam. And then another shot shows two trainers, showcasing a teamwork maneuver to a group at large.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): Come to the High Flyer Dungeon, where you will learn all you need to enter the business of Professional Wrestling! You'll learn strategy. (Cut to a classroom, where High Flyer stood in the front of the room, dressed in a business suit and wearing thick black frame glasses.) High Flyer: Now children. Before we move on from this day, we must know that wrestling is fake! (There's a bunch of quiet whispers in the crowd. A student, large, 6'2 240 raises his hand.) Student: Really? (Flyer crossed his arms, and turned stern.) High Flyer: Get the fuck out of here. (The Student was taken aback. Flyer stared him down.) High Flyer: No. I'm serious. Get your refund and move it. (Flyer paced, looking around the room.) High Flyer: That goes for anyone else here who believe that crazyiness. Let me tell you, you're gonna get hurt. (Flyer leaned into the student's eye line.) High Flyer: And I may be the one to hurt you. (Flyer raised a back palm and the student quickly grabbed his things and scurried from the room.) High Flyer: Anyone else want the taste of my hand?!? Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): Really Jack? *sighs* Learn moves and conditioning from our special guest trainers! (Cut to Dusk, standing over a student who's drenched in sweat. Dusk looked angry, arms crossed over his chest.) Dusk: How many bottles of water do you drink a day? Student 2: Uh... 3? Dusk: 3. (Dusk shook his head in disbelief.) Dusk: To be a professional wrestler, you have to be constantly peeing like a race horse. I want to see you with 8 water bottles a day and at least 10 bathroom trips. Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): And learn character from one of the best in the business! (Cut to a classroom. Students are all filed in, watching intently. At the head of the classroom resides Tony Davis, completely naked, staring out of the window at an apple tree. The students are obviously one confused. One tentatively raises his hand. He receives no response.) Student 3: Teacher? TEACHER! (Tony is startled slightly, and quickly jerked his head around the classroom.) Tony Davis: Oh God. This dream is a nightmare! (Tony runs out of the room, dongle jingling in the breeze.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather(V.O.): On second thought... maybe you shouldn't come here. (A logo for "High Flyer's Dungeon" faded on screen. Music continues to play in the background, with "1-800-Dun-geon" on screen. A disclaimer appears below, saying "Not to be confused with 1-888-Dun-geon." Slow fade out.)
