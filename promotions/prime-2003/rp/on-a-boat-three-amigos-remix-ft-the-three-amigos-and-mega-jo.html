"On a Boat (Three Amigos Remix)" - ft. The Three Amigos and Mega Job

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Don't read this. “SILENCE.” The oh-so-familiar voice of Mega Job’s third member (oh boy does that sound wrong) shouts orders to none other than, well…Mega Job, who are conversing back and forth whilst securing a video camera. Beef: Didn’t we just do something like this a couple weeks ago? El Janito: Probably. Seems like this is what happens when writer’s block takes over. Beef nods and they continue fooling around with the camera, only now quite a bit more silently. *BANG* No, that wasn’t a gun. The door to the room swings open, slamming into the wall (someone should really install doorstops in this place), causing Beef to flinch, sending an elbow right into Janito’s face. El Janito: DAMMIT! Steve: SILENCE. In said doorway stands the Three Amigos of PRIME: Bryan Dawkins BRUHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! Tyler Rayne RAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH! And Chandler Tsonda WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! (For the record, Janito and Beef were the two cheering.) Janito hits the ‘record’ button on the video camera. Dawkins stands in the center of the trio, holding a coupon. Steve: ACTION. Bryan Dawkins: Whoa. Free boat ride for three. Janito and Beef look over in confusion. Placing a closed fist up to his chin in thought, Dawkins elaborates. Bryan Dawkins: Now who should I take? Tsonda and Rayne, who are now standing in front of Dawkins, are joined by Janito and Beef, who are on their knees, hands clasped. Beef: TAKE MEEEEEEEEE! Janito sends a backhanded slap in Beef’s direction, clocking his cohort in the side of the head. Beef collapses to the concrete floor, knocked out cold, as Janito stands up and walks back over to the camera. Meanwhile, Steve sits in the director’s chair, facepalming. Bryan Dawkins: Chandler… Chandler Tsonda: Yesssssssssssssssss… Tsonda pumps his fist and smiles that million dollar set of teeth at the camera. Bryan Dawkins: Annnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnd… Before Dawkins can reveal his second boat companion, a nearly-unconscious Beef latches onto his leg, begging for mercy. Beef: PIIIIIIIIIIIICK…MEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE… Dawkins shrugs Beef off of his leg with little effort, sending him crumpled into a ball on the floor. Bryan Dawkins: …Rayne. Rayne, suddenly donning a pair of sunglasses hanging down by his chin, white top hat, and leopard print thong (what?) nods. Tyler Rayne: Cool. Suddenly, terribly loud rap music blares throughout the small room, and the Tres Amigos hop aboard a terribly shoddy looking boat replica. Two of the three (Dawkins and Tsonda) are now wearing tuxedos, Tsonda’s white and Dawkins’ orange with aqua pinstripes, and Rayne is still in his top hat, sunglasses, and thong. Tyler Rayne: SHAWWTAY! Bryan Dawkins: AWW SHIT, GET’CHA PINEAPPLES READY IT’S ABOUT TO GO DOWN! EVERY BRUH IN THE PLACE HIT THE FUCKIN’ DECK! BUT STAY ON YOUR… Tyler Rayne: MOTHAFUCKIN’. Bryan Dawkins: TOES! WE RUNNIN’ THIS, LET’S GO! All the while, El Janito is running around the set like a chicken with his head cut off, attempting to get various dynamic shots of the performance. Too bad he’s only got one camera, and one useless partner, who’s still passed out on the set floor in front of the prop boat. Dawkins & Tsonda: I’M ON A BOAT. Rayne: I’M ON A BOAAAAT! Dawkins & Tsonda: I’M ON A BOAT! EVERYBODY LOOK AT ME CUZ I’M SAILIN’ ON A BOAT. Rayne: SAILIN’ ON A BOAAAAAT! Steve: CUT. Magically, the music cuts, Rayne’s artificial member deflates, and Janito falls from the side of the boat into a kiddie pool filled with water. El Janito: WHAT THE HELL?! Steve: SILENCE. Dawkins hands a pineapple to Steve, who then lobs it at Janito. The juicy fruit cracks Janito in the head, knocking him out. Without a camera crew, the music video has essentially lost all of its purpose. There’s no one to shoo the video. They have a director who shouts one-word directions at the cast and crew. And they have a cast of guys who have no business being anywhere near a rap video. Or any music video, for that matter. Bryan Dawkins: So uh…what do we do now, bruhs? Tsonda and Rayne look at each other and shrug. Then, the Amigos glance at their director. Steve turns to Rayne, Dawkins, and Tsonda. He only needs one word. Steve: BOURBON. Within minutes, the four are pounding down Bourbon. Dawkins is the only one drinking it mixed, with pineapple of course, while the rest are going shot for shot. Shockingly, Steve is handling his liquor well, and is well on his way to drinking the Three Amigos under the table. After downing his tenth shot of the affair, Steve snaps his fingers, and the audio track for “On a Boat (Three Amigos Remix)” once again blares inside the small room. The four begin rocking out as only these four could. *KNOCK KNOCK* The door swings open. Devin Shakur: What. The. Shit. Dawkins peers over at the Man in Black and tips his drink toward him, nodding simultaneously. Bryan Dawkins: WE’RE ON A BOAT, BRUH! A MOTHERFUCKIN’ BOAT! I GOT MY SWIM TRUNKS AND MY FLIPPIE FLOPPIES. WHAT YOU GOT, BRUH?! Devin Shakur: More wins than you'll ever have. At this point, the other two thirds of Mega Job have somewhat recovered, and have found a way to construct a mini-boat out of a nearby cardboard box. The two are now sailing the deep blue sea of the kiddie pool that Janito had fallen into just moments ago. Beef & Janito: WE’RE ON A BOAT TOO! Devin Shakur: You are fired. Both of you. Beef and Janito continue floating around the kiddie pool as Shakur storms out of the room, slamming the door violently behind him. The Three Amigos plus Steve are still slamming their Bourbon (and pineapple juice, in one case), all the while jamming to their remix of “On a Boat.” It’s a good day to be an Amigo. * * * “I FUUUUUUUCKED A MERRRRRRMAIIIIIIIID!” T-Pain’s voice startles Dawkins, who is sitting at his computer, staring at the screen blankly. He glances down at his glass of pineapple juice, and picks it up, examining it carefully. He cautiously takes a sip of the sweet juice, and tastes no bourbon. “Damn, bruh…”
