Captain Justice, Special Issue #1: Tim Teegan

undated

Date: All over May 12th, 2008 "Explain yourself!" "Come on, Soren, you know it isn’t true!" "EXPLAIN YOURSELF! How could you do this? YOU of all people?!" "Soren, I don’t know what he’s talking about! He’s a son of a…" The last thing he remembered? Fist meeting temple, flesh hitting pavement, and before long, eyes sinking into black. No more replies but the murky escape of hot breaths shooting into the chilly night sky. It was the exact moment his professional wrestling career ceased to exist. ~ ~ ~ March 28th, 2008 Silver Lining Academy "Ow, ow, ow!" A pair of Redwood-sized legs coiled themselves around the scrawny chest of Tim Teegan. A sense of dread was skittering through his stomach as he tried with all the might that a meager one-hundred fifty-seven pound body could muster. His opponent – Michael Malloy, a much bigger athlete with that "look" to him – kept him pinned to the mat with a Body Scissor. Through a bleary haze, he could make out that old Sonny Silver "shittalk" he was legendary for "Damn it, Teegan!" The derisive shouting crawled into his ear, wrapping around his brain. "Go for the leg, you midget! You’re slipping!" Being in this situation was nothing new to our fair Teegan. For years, he’d been dubbed "The Human Shock Absorber" for his ability to absorb copious amounts of pain. Taking such punishment and begging for more to be handed out was his personal little "FUCK YOU!" to the world, extending that middle finger to every big man that thought they could bully him into submission. It’s what made him a proud standout for three years among the sea of grime that had washed in and out of Silver’s gym. DAMN IT, TIM, JUST PUSH! GET TO THE ROPES! In desperation, Tim launched himself to Malloy’s opposite side. Ignoring the pungent around of Malloy’s MASSIVE BO problem and the feeling of sweat making his skin stick to the grungy canvas, Teegan reached a hand for the ropes, but his more adept opposition cradled him back to the original position. Feelings of failure started to set in and panicky thoughts raced through his head faster than he could make sense of them. Any plan of attack he could think up this tricky quickly pushed into the recesses of his brain as he threw caution to the wind and errantly reached for the ropes with his feet. "Come on, Teeg!" Malloy’s voice boomed proudly, making fellow wrestling hopefuls on the outside cease and desist their own training regimens to take notice of their clinic. "Just give it up, Frodo. No way your scrawny ass is busting out of this!" Teegan had weathered through worse storms of browbeating, from bigger and badder tormentors. Hello. SONNY, anyone? He peppered Malloy’s abdomen with a series of elbows, while trying to wriggle free once more. Unbelievably, Malloy’s grip somehow tightened to the point where Tim’s face had turned several shades of an ugly red-purple catastrophe. Like a Boa Constrictor ensnaring its prey prior to chow time, Tim’s oppressor boisterously howled. "TAP!" He screamed the word until it had lost all meaning. By this point, all eyes in the Silver Lining Gym were solely focused upon the practicum the two men were putting on. Random chants from the lookers-on were blasted in alternating beats to the throbbing in Tim’s temples. Giving up? Not an option, not even a notion he entertained. In this world that catered to men twice, even three times his size, Teegan needed an X-factor. He needed that special something to set him apart from all the other wrestlers out there. The prototype for the "lovable loser" in Mick Foley gave him that X in the form of never allowing the word "quit" to be educed from your lips. In his mind, he didn’t hear the sound or feel the pains of Malloy handing out punishment like candy. Instead, all that was there was the notion that he had to escape, lest this training clinic continue for ridiculous amounts of time without a decisive winner; each passing second favoring his resistance that much more. Squirming like a worm on a hook ready to be submerged into the deep, Tim had one last course of action for Mister Malloy in this match, surged yet again, pinning Malloy to the canvas. Teegan bit down on his elbow pad not unlike a rabid dog, getting through the worst part of the pressure and silently prayed this would be enough. "ONE! TWO! THREE!" The sound of that referee striking the mat for a third time was music to the ears of Teegan as both combatants collapsed on the canvas. Malloy rose to one knee, reeking of the pungent aromas of sweat and defeat. In a show of solidarity, Mike helped his opponent of the last twenty or so minutes to his feet, even extending the olive branch of a handshake. "Fucking-A, Tim!" Sonny nodded, extending a salute of approval to his most tenured student as a round of applause from the students subsequently trailed. Smiles and high-fives of the "way to go, sport!" variety were exchanged between Teegan, Malloy, and the wrestling faithful "All right, enough!" His whistling reverberated throughout the gym, drawing all focus back towards the teacher. "For all you newbie punk bitches that have walked into this gym today thinking that just because what greets you in the mirror is eye-appealing or that the extra slab of muscle is going to win you a match, I really hope you got a good look at those two!" Both Tim and Mike cleared the way for their ring general to enter the fray and raise the hands of his two star pupils. A small smirk crossed the face of Tim Teegan as he gazed out to the other students. Hee hee. Suck it down, kids. THIS is why I’m Sonny’s favorite student!" "You embody heart, discipline, you talk smack in the face of adversity, and you don’t give up even when it seems like the House is conspiring against you to take all your fucking chips so you can’t go out and bang that hooker you’ve been eyeing half the night!" Sparse laughter filled the gymnasium, but quickly dissipated as Sonny’s stern look told them he was going somewhere poignant. "But most importantly, you work. You work your ass off, you get somewhere, and those federations are gonna be knocking down your door, ready to throw title belts and main events your way. Now, all you sweaty bastards wash up, go get changed, then meet back in thirty so we can work on stretches. And I don’t mean that pussy Richard Simmons shit." Dismissing his class for the moment, Sonny crawled through the ropes and headed to his office. The smile on Tim’s face at that moment was equivalent to that of receiving a blowjob from [your choice of hot actress here]. Nothing was going to erase this moment he’d worked so hard for. Sonny, in his eyes, had seen him in the light he wanted to be seen… the perfect student. "Ah, Soren!" The sounds of adulation in Sonny’s voice pierced Tim’s bubble. From inside his office, the former Universal Champion including roll out the metaphorical red carpet as Soren Knox entered his office, shaking his hand. "How’s my favorite student? You’re not shooting the shit with these bums, are you?" That bubble? Reduced to the sounds of shattering glass as Sonny slammed the door behind he and Soren, probably discussing their new business arrangement for PRIME. Lucky bastard. Tim could feel his pride crash through the floor. Why does HE get the big fucking PRIME contract? Soren’s nice and all, but doesn’t have my guts OR my heart! Screw this, I need a shower. Halting in his tracks, Teegan fell to a knee, feeling like a creature was about to burst through his chest cavity at a moment’s notice, suddenly remembering Mike Malloy had spent the last fifteen minutes turning his ass into Swiss cheese. He picked himself off the mat and surveyed the now-empty gymnasium, ready to relax. Limping towards the locker room, his mind began racing as slowly as he was. Just another day in a long line of many. Okay. Bandages first. THEN shower. ~ ~ ~ APRIL 3rd, 2008 Evening Time First to come, last to leave. Tim Teegan, if anything, was a setter of many precedents. He hoped that by not only being the first attendee of Sonny’s training seminars and being the last busy little beaver to leave for the day, it would set a great example for all those wanting to get into his good graces. Throwing on his last clean pair of Levis and tying up his Doc Martins, Teegan slammed his locker shut and prepared himself for facing the horrors of the Seattle Metro Transporation System. He only had about ten or so minutes to race down the block to catch his bus and that new episode of "How I Met Your Mother" wasn’t going to watch itself on TiVo. His shoes fell hard against the ground as he tossed his duffel bag behind him to make a hasty exit. Nestled up in his Seahawks jacket, he raced out of the locker room in a half-sprint, hopping across several stretches of tile floor on his way outside. "You want me to WHAT?!" The exclamation was enough to impel his feverish pace, having to prevent himself in a split second, close to being the first of Sonny’s students to have his face conjoined with a steel door. He wasn’t some weird eavesdropper that got his rocks off regarding anybody’s personal business, but a question mark appeared in his head. What was Sonny getting so worked up about? He poked one eye through a slit in the 3/4-closed blinds. In his line of sight, Sonny leaned against the door, arms folded and looking out the window into the insanity of Seattle Rush Hour traffic off I-5. Across the table, a woman Tim couldn’t quite recognize. Some Kaley Cuoco-looking girl with flowing strawberry-blonde hair extending to her lower back. As she sat at the desk opposite Sonny, her body language read something to the effect of "get to the fucking point" as Silver continued to scream belligerently about something. Kneeling against the door jamb, and trying to make out movements in between the office’s blinds, Tim keeps an ear open to the goings-on. "Look, Iz, I gave you the fucking money," he hissed. "I don’t know how much more you guys want from me, but I assure you, it’s going down…" "Yeah, about that…" the woman named Iz trailed off, carefully searching for her next words. "Twenty-five thousand more. I’m not going to repeat myself again." The phrase uttered from the woman had absolutely no faltering behind it. She wanted serious coin from Sonny. "Bullshit," he fires back. "This isn’t some negotiation, this a plan to get a kid over. Pure and simple" "You’re right. It’s not a negotiation." Tim can see through one of the blinds Sonny’s face curl downwards into an annoyed frown. "Look, whatever you’ve got going on with Christian, Xavier, whatever the fuck he’s calling himself nowadays, not my problem. My problem is you bunch of pricks coming to me for money. You were compensated fully for this entire idea and the Captain will be taking a dive at UltraViolence." Tim cocked an eyebrow as he mouthed the words "take a dive?" like a self-query. What the fuck was going on around here? He can tell as Iz kicked her feet up on his desk that she’d officially run out of patience. It was obvious by his own posture Sonny was trying to remain in control and not fidget. But Tim had trained with Sonny long enough to know when he’d been rattled. "Either you pay up or we go right to Cap J., Soren Knox, whatever YOU call him, we blow the whistle on this charade and you lose your little pet project. He finds out you’re basically living through him vicariously like a parent, you’re planning on using him to resurrect your career so you don’t look like a putz, then you lose face with him AND with your students." Her stable blue eyes greet his shaken browns before the sheen tells Tim that his trainer was officially against the wall. Opening a drawer, he spied Sonny opening up a checkbook, letting it hit his desk with a thud before gritting his teeth. "You get this now, but I don’t want to see your 8 Simple Rules-looking ass on my property again until all this is over," he declares as he scribbles a figure on the check. When he finishes, Isabelle puts a hand on the check, but Sonny withdraws his own, offering up the money with a proviso of his own. "If Kannon does so much as BREATHE the wrong way during this whole scenario, I have Captain Justice make what Deville and his hyenas did to him look like a day at the spa." "Something about idle threats and them getting you nowhere," Izzy nonchalantly says she pries the check from Sonny’s grip. "And something about pleasure doing business goes here. Ciao." "Fuck you," he sneered as Iz stood up to make her exit, practically beaming at the sight of the large number on her newly-acquired check. On the outside, a confused Tim tried to process all this as his fingers pressed against the glass, looking for any more sorts of clues. "Soren… no." Part of him wanted to barge in there and rip them both limb from limb from this apparent charade at the expense of Soren Knox’s young career. Another part wanted to just bite his lip and watch Soren fall from grace. The real reason that all the attention he was getting was merely a by-product of Sonny’s own obsession with this business. No. He didn’t deserve it. Something had to be done. He just had to avoid the pitfall of being caught before he could do anything about it first. "Shit!" Tim whispered to no one as he dove back into the locker room, not even glancing back to see the last staredown between Sonny and his apparent blackmailer as she exited the doors. Making sure he was alone, he felt his heart sink deep within his chest as he huffed and puffed. Soren Knox… much as he didn’t like Sonny’s preferential treatment of that guy, he didn’t deserve any of this. What the fuck was going on? And he couldn’t forget the real Millon-Dollar Question: What did Sonny get Soren into? ~ ~ ~ APRIL 20th, 2008 EVENING TIME Gravel spinning under tires, both student and teacher found themselves barreling through Seattle back streets. Tim had the unfortunate goddamn luck to miss the bus to get to his weekend session. In all that time he’d poured into his training and all those shows that Silver Lining Wrestling had put out, he’d never been late to a single one. (Un)lucky for him, the all-too-familiar Chevy Avalanche that Sonny Silver loved to show off had come rolling around the bend, offering him a ride to keep that attendance streak alive. Now finding himself in an incredibly awkward position, Tim couldn’t help but peek in the back seat of the massive truck, trying to find that elusive elephant that wouldn’t go away. For three weeks, he'd wanted to say something to Soren about what he heard, but he'd never found a good opportunity. Business meetings pulled him away or they just missed each other. But he'd find the time. "Something on your mind?" The cold face of the former Universal Champion appeared to not look at him, but rather through him. Tim tried to read his expressionless mentor, searching every last wrinkle in his forehead and graying hair on either side of his scalp, but this wasn’t Sonny’s first poker game. Tim on the other hand? Hated that shit. Especially the internet version that robbed him of all his free time. "Nah," Tim drifted off, eyeing each sign and contemplating whether or not he’d have to secretly write a "HELP ME!" message with his breath on the passenger window. "Couldn’t sleep. What you been up to lately?" "Training. Deals. Wrestling. SOS." His eyes caroused the truck’s neat, immaculate interior. Nary a speck of dust could be spotted with the naked eye, demonstrating just how much Sonny loved his mode of transportation. The eyes continued until they fell upon two Starbucks cups in either cup holder, then wandered back to the wintry brown orbs of Silver. "You can have one if you want," Silver told him, his gaze locked on the road. "Uh…" Tim wasn’t sure whether or not it would be right to accept the peace offering or not. With a wave of the hand, Silver dismissed his hesitation. "I got it for Rex who was supposed to be in, but dipshit called me at the very last minute to cancel. Something about a doctor’s appointment, I think. Better drink up before it gets cold and drank by me." After an uncomfortable sip of what turned out to be Peppermint Mocha latte, an even more uncomfortable laugh escaped from his lips. Time to make with more small talk. "I… uh… how can all this traffic be around here? You ever wonder how the hell it starts forming? I mean, it’s gotta start with some douchebag who can’t figure out "gas, break, honk." Torture, eh?" "Torture?" Sonny echoed with the cock of an eyebrow. "You haven’t experienced torture until you’ve had to put over green pieces of shit on WWE Heat, then you get into a fistfight with them backstage, blowing your entire shot at the big time because management’s too stupid to understand the words "Strong Style." Okaaaay, awwwwkward. Tim came to a complete halt, not sure how else this entire situation could be any worse. He tried to swallow, but despite the fact he’d been downing a hot coffee, his throat was completely dry. "So you like spying?" His face became ghostly white. "Wha-wha…" He stammered. "Spying," Sonny repeated. "See, my dear boy, the thing about my line of work? I’ve got a lot of idiots that like to wander around my office. I’ve had money stolen from me before by a bunch of dickfaces that I took under my wing, only to be repaid with a broken window and an empty vault." He extended a finger as he rounded the corner. "Never again, Tim. Never again." "If this is about Soren…" he couldn’t help but feel his voice squeak like an adolescent as Sonny’s porcelain face continued to disconcert him. "Security cameras don’t lie, Tim. I caught you skulking around my office during my important business meeting with one of my many benefactors. I thought you were a smart kid, Mr. Teegan. Always admired your guts in my class. But you had to walk into something that didn’t involve you." Something in Tim wanted to bolt out of this car right now, but going 65 up I-5 would not grant him a result that would give him enough time to warn Soren Knox of what was going on. Street pizza couldn’t talk. Something in Tim wanted to just pray that he could find a way out of this that wouldn’t result in this closet maniac hurting him. "You call somebody shaking you down for money a benefactor?" Oh, yeah, Tim, dig yourself deeper. "See, that’s what I’m talking about, that thing that doesn’t involve you. Sorry, Tim. I liked you. Really did. But…" Reaching for something behind the seat, Tim braced himself for whatever the hell was coming his way. Club, mace… gun? Manila folder tossed in his lap? "That, my dear boy, is a new contract for another federation I have a couple contacts with, Global Championship Wrestling." Raising an eyebrow to his captor, Tim opened the folder and tossed his way through the documents quickly. "I know of them. What does this have to do with me?" "Well, it’s like this. That, my favorite pupil, is one of those contracts much like the one I signed Soren to. It’s not quite the same dollar figure that he’s making, but it’s much more than the pocket change you’re getting with me See, you’re in what we in the business call "a position of power." You haphazardly walked into a bad situation, but I’m doing right by trying to get you out of it so nothing bad happens to anyone. Couldn’t get you to PRIME, but GCW was scouting new talent. Sign that contract, I’ll fax it to Steven Caldera first thing tomorrow morning and you’ll be on a plane to their headquarters tomorrow." No way this was happening. But… he had to at least tempt the thought. Tim was not an idiot, so he spent a few minutes thumbing through the contract, making sure every last ounce of bile being spewed from Silver’s mouth had an iota of truth to it. Satisfied with what he saw, Teegan dropped it in his lap and rubbed his palms to his face nervously. "That’s all I gotta do? Sign?" "Basically. There’s no muss, no fuss, nobody has to know anything and we’re all good." "I… I can’t. I don’t owe anything to Soren, but I can’t just sell him up the river. I promise I won’t say anything, but…" he chose his next words VERY cautiously. "I can’t take a bribe." He shut his eyelids tightly, ready to brace himself for whatever punishment was coming his way. "Okay." That did it. All this? Too fucking weird. Tim pinched himself quietly to make sure he hadn’t ended up in some very fucked-up version of the Twilight Zone. "Okay?" "Okay," Sonny reiterated coolly. "I appreciate your candor, sir. You’ve always been a man of your word and I do take it. Consider the subject dropped." Tim shot a half-hearted smile Sonny’s way and to his shock, it was returned with a smile of unexpected warmth. "Now, let’s put this shit behind us," Silver said as he turned off the 165 exit towards Seneca Street, nearly there to his class. "Trust me when I say Soren will be fine and his PRIME career is going to be a very lucrative one as long as he continues on the path he’s going. You both have bright futures in this business and… you know, I’ll tell you what. I’ve got a lot going on, so I’ll ask you a favor.. Want to take over the class for a few and give them some drills?" "Uh… yeah. We can do that." "Good." Silver extended a hand with the coffee, to which Tim reciprocated with a nervous smile. "Drink, dude. You look like you need one. Take the edge off." Glancing out the window, Tim started to look on the sun disappearing behind the concrete skyline. Brushing this whole episode out of his head, he took in the last of the impressive landscape as the truck continued to rumble towards its destination. "Good coffee, eh?" Sonny chuckled as he sipped from his heated brew. "Yeah…" The Underdog trailed off as the last roar of the engine signified the end of their trip. At least the most awkward car ride of the year was over. ~ ~ ~ May 12th, 2008 Battalion Hall "Sonny, sorry I’m late!" Huffing and puffing from the long run down the boulevard, Tim came to a dead stop in front of the doors to the show. Silver Lining Wrestling Presents: Into The Fire, one of its marquee events was scheduled to go down soon and Tim couldn’t help but feel ridiculous in his mentor’s eyes for dropping the ball. As he hastily threw the doors away, he ran into a large barreled chest, falling to the floor. "OW!" Stirring to make sure all his mental faculties were in check, Tim glanced upwards to see the two forms of Soren Knox and Sonny Silver. He brushed the dirt off his jacket and extended a hand. "Sorry, guys! Didn’t see you coming." Expecting a hearty handshake as Sonny did with every employee of his to show good graces, what he found in kindness’s stead was sheer apathy. Perplexed, Tim read the looks on both their faces. "Guys, what’s up? You look like somebody died." "You have some explaining to do, Tim," Sonny crossly threw yet another folder at his chest, nearly knocking the runt back a few steps. Scrambling through the folder’s contents, something on page two made Tim’s eyes bolt wide open. "Random drug screenings I did last week," Silver explained. "Do them every so often since you know how much I don’t like that shit being peddled in ANY of my projects. In fact, it’s in each of the contracts you sign. One strike, you’re out. Tell me, Teeg. What do you see in your results?" "No…" The red POS(+) symbol next to HGH taunted him the entire time he spent reading the page. "Come on… look at me!" Tim screamed. "This is bullshit! I’m one-fifty, soaking wet! You think I would’ve touched this shit?" He read Soren’s expression, which demonstrated that of pure melancholy. Then to Sonny’s, which expressed only the highest levels of fury. "It makes you a prime candidate, Teeg," Sonny prodded. "Do you KNOW how many people before you came along, Tim? They were dreamers. They wanted to push their bodies well beyond their means and they’d do ANYTHING for the spotlight. And most of these people? Smaller than yourself. They’d pump their bodies with testosterone, somatropin, stanozolol, whatever the fuck they could get their hands on." Like a father scolding a child, Sonny took a palm and placed it on his shoulder, making sure to clench with every ounce of power to make Tim wince. "I thought you were different, Timothy. You were an honest man who came to me, nearly broke and homeless, yet I gave you an opportunity to rise. From day one, you were an example-setter. First to come, last to leave, the one that never gave up in his abilities, but I saw the way you looked at Soren when he came into my office that day. It was a world you wanted to be a part of. And that drove you nuts. You hated Soren, so you did what you would’ve done to try and gain and advantage!" "NO!" "YEAH, YOU DID! YOU BROUGHT THAT SHIT INTO MY SCHOOL, KNOWING HOW MUCH THIS DRUG USE BUSINESS IS SUCH A HOT-BUTTON ISSUE FOR GUYS LIKE US, AND SULLIED MY NAME AND MY BRAND JUST SO YOU COULD GET WHAT YOU WANT!" Fighting back sobs, Tim did everything short of groveling. "I swear!" he shouted to the heavens and anyone who would hear him. "I have no idea where this shit came from! You know me!" "Explain yourself." Tim perked up at Soren’s sudden reaction, Sonny caught off-guard by these turn of events. "What’d you say?" "Explain yourself!" "Come on, Soren, you know it isn’t true!" "EXPLAIN YOURSELF! How could you do this? YOU of all people?!" "Soren, I don’t know what he’s talking about! He’s a son of a…" The last thing he remembered? Fist meeting temple, flesh hitting pavement, and before long, eyes sinking into black. No more replies but the murky escape of hot breaths shooting into the chilly night sky. It was the exact moment his professional wrestling career ceased to exist. "SOREN, STOP!" A voice cried out to Soren, halting him from making another rash tactic. Muscling in between Soren and Tim, he put both hands up to prevent any more chaos from ensuing. "Soren, he’s gone from this gym and from my shows," he reasoned with his charge. "He’ll have a long time to think about spreading any more drugs amongst my students. Besides… gotta save all that fire for UltraViolence next week." Conceding, Soren nodded. "Fine." With a pat on Knox’s chest, Sonny headed back into the building, not even bothering to bat an eye towards the prone form of his now ex-student. Tim did whatever he could to try and regain his footing, but it was all for naught as he slumped over the sidewalk, struggling to make sense of anything. "You fucked up," Soren lamented as he turned on his massive heel to reenter the gymnasium. "You fucked up bad, Tim." He heard the sounds of the gym’s doors locking, emphatically closing this chapter of his life forever. As he could feel his mouth fill with the metallic taste of blood ooze through his teeth, Tim remained slumped over the edge of a fire hydrant, watching the occasional car whiz by him and into the night. He wanted so badly to have what Soren had. The life, the glamour, the money, and the fame. Now? Tim Teegan was a merely a beaten man left to his own devices as he sat on that cold concrete, sobbing quietly as he watched a paper coffee cup roll by.
