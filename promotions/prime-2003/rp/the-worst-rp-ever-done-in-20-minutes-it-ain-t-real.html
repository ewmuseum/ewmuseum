The Worst RP Ever Done in 20 Minutes (IT AIN'T REAL)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Huh To put it cordially, Devin Shakur isn't the kind of guy that is going to be found grinding in a nightclub to the latest Kanye West song. He doesn't do media appearances for the simple fact that he absolutely despises any forum that repeatedly asks the same question over and over again. "What is it like to work with Tyler Rayne?" Fuck Tyler Rayne. That miserable little shit is on the decline. Like every other great sensation, he's had his time under the sun and now he's being burned. Soon he will be scorched with absolutely nothing to protect him. Devin Shakur walks over to his office chair and sits down in front of a computer screen. In order to acquire news about his favorite sport teams and local news, since Channel 14 is a continuous loop and is a crock of shit, Shakur uses Al Gore's greatest invention (with global warming being a close second) the Interwebs. Shakur puts on his headphones, listening to some smooth Mindi Abair jazz from her latest album "Stars". The smooth blend of a saxophone and a soothing voice will put even the angriest and one of the most macabre wrestlers on the roster at ease. However, The Man in Black makes one critical mistake. He goes to YouTube and looks underneath the tab 'Featured Videos'. One video intrigues him to the point of clicking on the link, 'I'm On A Boat' PRIME remix. His eyes grow big. The CD player (yes, some people still have those and use them frequently) goes flying across the room, almost shattering a tall glass lamp. Tyler Rayne and Bryan Dawkins are standing in suits rapping like the true imbeciles they are. Chandler Tsonda is standing there doing his best T-Pain impression. Shakur is on the verge of dry heaving when the latest member of his entourage walks into the room. Union Jack's former butler, Jeeves. Jeeves: What seems to be the quagmire, sir? Devin Shakur: Rayne. Dawkins. Video. Sickening. Gonna vomit. Tyler Rayne shouts with pride, "I'M ON A BOAT MOTHERFUCKER" Jeeves: This is the most appalling sight I have seen in my days, sir. Dawkins shows off a pair of flip flops and calls them his "flippy floppies, bruh" Devin Shakur: Alright, the hell with this. Jeeves, go and get my industrial sized razor blades. I'm going to need to do some ultra mega super fantastic ZOMG wrist cutting to lose enough blood and forget about this. Jeeves: Very well sir, would you like me to request the presence of Lady Tzu to arrive in her voluptuous paramedic outfit afterward to rescue you from certain death? Shakur ponders the matter for a second and shakes his head back and forth. Devin Shakur: No, Jeeves, I think I really need to kill myself this time. Jeeves: As you wish, sir. Shakur hits Alt F4 as fast as his fingers can smash into the keyboard keys, shutting off the computer after a matter of clicks. Unfortunately for Shakur, the carnage isn't over. "OH SHIT, GET YOUR TOWELS READY!" One voice from around the corner shouts. "IT'S ABOUT TO GO DOWN" The other replies. Shakur facepalms and slowly turns his head to see Beef and El Janito. Beef: EVERYBODY IN THE PLACE HIT THE FUCKING DECK! El Janito: BUT STAY ON YOUR MOTHERFUCKING TOES! They are paddling a canoe across Devin Shakur's living room floor, going at a top speed of two hundred feet per hour. Considering how weak Mega Job is in terms of physical strength, this is pretty impressive. Devin Shakur: What in the holy mothershit are you two idiots doing? Beef: I'M ON A BOAT! El Janito: WE'RE ON A MOTHERFUCKING BOAT! Shakur once again facepalms, wondering where the blue hell Jeeves is at. He usually doesn't take this long to get the blades. "I'M ON A BOAT MOTHERFUCKER, TAKE A LOOK AT ME!" Shakur grunts and looks back over, expecting to see the worst thing possible. And he does. Tony Gamble is being pulled by Jeeves in a bathtub. Devin Shakur: HOW THE HELL DID YOU GET MY BATHTUB OUT? Tony Gamble: Straight flowing on a boat on the deep blue sea! Busting five knots, wind whipping out my coat! You can't stop me motherfucker cause I'm on a boat! Gamble flashes his special rubber duckie and aluminum foil grill. The trio and their transportation eventually make their way to the front door and out into the cold February morning. Devin Shakur: Hope those motherfuckers freeze. Tony Gamble: Nah dawg, we got a plan now. Devin Shakur: I'm scared to ask. Tony Gamble: Well we stole the surround speakers you have also and now we're going to go bump The Benny Hill theme song while trying to pick up chicks in OUR MOTHERFUCKING BOATS! Devin Shakur: It's called Yakety Sax, not the Benny Hill theme. Tony Gamble: You want to hop in? There's room for one more. Devin Shakur: … Tony Gamble: I got an extra rubber duckie. Devin Shakur: I hope you are wearing clothes. Tony Gamble: Absolutely not. Most downloaded man on the Internet for a reason. Devin Shakur: Get the hell out. Tony Gamble: Your loss. Gamble shuts the door and Shakur goes to kill himself.
