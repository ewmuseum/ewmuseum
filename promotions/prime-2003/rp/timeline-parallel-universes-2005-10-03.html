Date: October 3, 2005 What’s wrong? Why are you there cuddling in the corner? Did you expect me to feel sorry for you? Did you expect something in return? Don’t cry to me that the world is giving you a bowl of gruel and not the five course meal you ordered. This is America, bitch, you get what you put in. Don’t come to me with that America sucks talk because they took away the only thing you were good at. You weren’t even that good of a fighter. You needed two chances just to get to the second round of the JITC. I only needed one. Already I am better than you. Speak any English? Make that twice. You grew up in an orphanage because you didn’t know your parents? Big deal-so did I. But now I’m here on top of it all. You? You’re still trying to relive your own perceived glory days. What glory days? The days of being an incompetent Polish? The days of your parents leaving because they were too embarrassed of having someone like you to be their son? Get over it. The world isn’t fair and you’re not getting any breaks. That five course meal you ordered has been gobbled up by the strongest and smartest. No one is going to wait for you. No one is going to drag you by the arm and tell you to color within the lines. No one is going to give a damn about you. It’s about fucking time you realized that. It’s about fucking time you realized you’re on your own. You want something to fight for? Try fighting for your own damn self and not some Polish whore named Janina. There’s probably a dime a dozen girls like her who charge 150 an hour. She doesn’t care about you. You’re not special. You’re just like everyone else. # Juan Santos seldom experienced love and when he did it was great. The feelings – all the emotions and caring shared by two people were immense. Juan only experienced it once, however. As many flings as he had in his lifetime – and there were many of them – he only really loved one person: Emilia. When her vibrant candlelight was extinguished, so was his. Over time love just became another four letter word like “fuck” and “shit”. It didn’t matter who expressed love to him. They weren’t worth it. But of all the would-be Mrs. Santos, the only one to remain determined was a young brunette by the name of Stephanie Ali-Reynolds, a hopeful Mrs. Santos. But to understand their relationship, one must go back to where it all started: The beginning. # September 1998 Stephanie Ali-Reynolds never talks about her family. She never talks about anything in her life, period. It was that fact coupled with her unusually bitchy attitude that has some people believe she is the Devil incarnate. How else can you explain a woman who has the beauty to trap any man, yet drives them away instead? It has more to do than just her attitude, her health, and her manner of being. She’s driving people away because she wants to be alone. In the fall of 1998, Stephanie found work as a valet for a then-unknown wrestling federation and an even more unknown wrestler named Alex Reynolds. Alex had joined the ranks of a pretty prestigious stable through connections and was set to make a good name for himself. Stephanie was just eye candy for the crowd, hardly ever given any more responsibility than just standing there looking pretty. Rumors ran wild during her tenure there. Some said she had moonlighted as a porn star before. Others say her contract was iron-clad. Whatever the reasons, only the second rumor was verified to be true, as Alex Reynolds found out one night. Alex was in his locker room preparing for one of his early matches. Stephanie walked in on him tying his boots and in one of their rare conversations asked him if she could wait this one out. Stephanie had hardly spoken in a month of employment – a fact that shocked Alex. Alex brushed it off and instead insisted that she put her chromosomes to a better use. “Alex, I want to sit this one out.” Stephanie said, looking more slouched than ever. Alex nodded. “Yeah, I think I need you here instead.” “What for?” Alex whipped out a long list from his pants. He grinned. “I need you to sweep the floor, mop it, and then wax it. Go to my house and clean it all from top to bottom, and make sure you get the mold in the bathroom. Do laundry. Go to the dry cleaners and pick up my shirt. Call my parents and tell them I’ll be out of town. Take some messages for me. And last but certainly not least, go fix the car, order some beer….no a lot of beer, and make dinner. Oh, and wear something sexy when you do it.” Stephanie was left speechless. She couldn’t think of anything to say. “Oh, and make sure you’re barefoot in the kitchen. You need to be head down and ass-“ SMACK! Stephanie ripped her hand across his face. “Fuck you, you stupid son of a bitch!” She stormed out. Alex touched his faced and smirked. Somehow, her outburst seemed out of place. But the truth was…that was the real Stephanie Ali-Reynolds. # October 1998 Juan Santos had been recently hired to an organization under the moniker of Syko Arsonist. He, like so many others, remained introverted and kept to himself. This was just another job for him and he had yet to experience the wealth of the business. He had been found wrestling in underground leagues. Unlike most wrestlers with such backgrounds where they almost never lose and overcome all the odds as if God molded them, Juan was a mediocre wrestler. It wasn’t his losing record, his talent, but it was his drive and motivation that inspired onlookers. He had potential. He just had to meet he opportunity. That opportunity came in the fall of 1998. The first few matches were irrelevant to him having split the first couple of matches. When October neared its end, Juan met a woman who would change the rest of his career. In turn, he would change hers. Her named was Stephanie Ali. Juan had heard that she had married some schmuck who thought he was Muhammad Ali for his money. She managed to divorce him on adultery and neglect and was well off. Juan also knew that she was much like him – introverted and collective. In one of the nightly shows, Juan Santos went back to the locker room having won his match. On the way there he bumped and spilled coffee all over Stephanie Ali. “Watch where the hell you’re going,” Stephanie yelled, wiping he coffee from his blouse. “I’m sorry but who the hell drinks coffee here at night?” Juan replied, looking for some napkins. At that point Stephanie still had not looked up to see Juan. She did now. She was stuttering for a bit. She had never seen him before. Now there he was, standing before her. He was wearing his black – almost gothic like – wrestling attire and sporting his trademark long red hair. “You’re right,” Stephanie replied, changing her tune. “It’s stupid to be drinking coffee at this time.” Stephanie laughed and Juan Santos forced a chuckle. He dismissed himself and went about his way. Stephanie sighed and looked back at him. Maybe there was something more to him than he was letting on. # June 1999 By this time Juan Santos was rapidly becoming a household name under the pseudonym of Syko Arsonist. He was a fan favorite – especially among the minorities – and was at this time a two-time Intercontinental Champion. He still had yet to experience world championship gold and it was in the early summer days of 1999 that he set out on that goal. He joined a prestigious and powerful stable called the Horsemen of Apocalypse. Dark, end of the world nick names aside, the stable was far from any resemblance to a gothic, Four Horsemen, or Flock-like stable. The talent was at diverse as it was unique there, but the only one who mattered was a former World and tag team champion himself, Alex Reynolds. Alex was the one who offered him a spot, having seen him perform in the business and quite admirably. Over time, the stable was completely modified and changed to the TITANS. A brief lawsuit with some dork resulted in the stable changing the spelling of their name to TITans, much to the delight of teenagers everywhere. The group was no longer serious, and Juan was no longer the wrestling expert persona he carried. It was now time to have some fun. # August 1999 Stephanie, too, was at the top of her game – and at the top of many things if you get my drift. She had become a two time women’s champion and maintained a hate-love relationship with Alex’s girlfriend Sarah. However, there was more to that relationship and more to Stephanie’s success. She had now become a Jezebel – the kind that frightens even the sturdiest of men. It was almost as if she had permanent PMS. Her wits were far superior to most and she proved it a month earlier by getting Alex Reynolds to marry her while his girlfriend was out visiting her parents. That was mistake number one. Mistake number two was getting caught having sex with Stephanie on video. Alex walked out of the bathroom and headed for the bedroom drying his hair along the way. He walked in and left the door open. Inside, he saw Stephanie working feverishly with the VCR to edit out the sex tape of them. “Why are you erasing it?” Alex asked, drying his hair at the door. Stephanie flashed him a confused look. “Are you kidding me? This is fucking disgusting I don’t want anyone to see me.” “I dunno…it’s kinda artful.” Stephanie sighed. “Who the hell put that camera in there anyway?” Alex shrugged. “That’s what I would like to know.” Alex turned around, stunned to see Sarah back so soon. “Uh, hi honey, what are you doing back so soon,” Alex asked, forcing a laugh. Sarah stormed into the room with a wrath of a dozen scorn women. She went straight for Stephanie and the two engaged in a catfight of extreme limitations. Somewhere, someone was hoping they’d get naked. Their loud commotions and Sarah’s screaming gave alert to everyone in the mansion. Everyone rushed to the room, led by Juan Santos. “Aren’t you going to stop them,” Juan asked. “Are you kidding me,” Alex replied. “This is fucking great. Juan sighed and everyone left. The fight was eventually broken when Sarah choked Stephanie Ali-Reynolds into passing out. That was the beginning of another relationship. Sarah and Stephanie began to have intimate encounters themselves –an act known to everyone – and the TITans cashed in. They videotaped their encounters and sold them by the millions. Roommates Gone Wild. # May 2000 Juan Santos’ relationship with Stephanie grew but their paths did not meet. For Juan, it was just another notch. For Stephanie it was much more. She wanted Juan for the economic value, but later on found herself falling in love. She had never experienced love before. She was always so conniving, so calculating, so isolated from others. She had suckered men into marrying her for money, but she had not been able to do the same with Juan Santos. Maybe it was that challenge, or maybe it was the fact that Juan was genuinely different from the others, but whatever it was, Stephanie was in love. That was not to change her other ways though,. She was still seen as a disgusting jezebel and was known to sleep around. Juan didn’t care, because he wasn’t in love. In fact, he was against it. He wanted no part of Stephanie, not even her ravaged honey pot, and was more concerned with his business. He was a four time intercontinental champion and approaching even more success. But none of that was set to prepare him for what was to come. Stephanie called Juan and told them they had some important news to talk about. Juan walked into Stephanie’s bedroom where she was sitting on the edge looking both happy and sad. “We need to talk about something,” Stephanie began. Juan nodded. “Look, if this is about your clothes being sold, I swear I had nothing to do with it.” “No, it’s not about-what the fuck?” Stephanie screamed, and ran straight to her closet. “Where the fuck is all my clothes?” “I-I don’t know anything,” Juan said. Stephanie sighed. “Look, that’s not important. The thing is you know you and I have been spending some time together.” Juan shuddered. “I try not to think of those moments.” “Oh, you know you like it.” “No.” “Anyways!” Stephanie exclaimed. “The thing is we need to be a little more responsible from now on.” “I am in favor of abstinence when it comes to you.” “This isn’t a fucking joke,” Stephanie yelled, throwing her shoe at Juan. “I’m pregnant.” Juan Santos ran. # June 2001 Two months into her pregnancy and everyone denied being the father of Stephanie’s child. June was also the month of one of the federations biggest PPVs. There, Stephanie vowed to proof to the world who the father was. She had everyone in the TITan stable take a paternity test. Through out the show, one by one, they all went to the office of Dr. Doogie Howser and claim their results. Mike Black. Negative. Mayhem. Negative. Although they did find some strange DNA in his specimen belonging to a monkey. Alex Reynolds. Negative. It was now Juan’s turn. Stephanie waited with him eagerly outside of the office. Juan was nervous. If the others weren’t the father, then he surely was. “This is so excited.” Juan had that deer in the headlights look. “I know you are happy,” Stephanie said, clinging to his arm. “I always knew, and hoped, it would be you.” “HOW LONG IS THAT FUCKING DOCTOR GOING TO TAKE!” Juan blurted out. Just then Dr. Howser stepped out side. “Why are you guys waiting out here for? I’ve been waiting inside forever.” “Oh.” “Well, the results are back.” Dr. Howser said, reading the papers on his clipboard. Stephanie was grinning from ear to ear. “We are going to be a big happy family. I love this man so much.” In the meantime, Juan kept praying the Our Father prayer in Spanish. Dr. Howser cleared his throat. “Juan is not the father. In fact, they all popped negative. I’m sorry.” Stephanie cried. Juan celebrated. Stephanie looked down at the bump in her belly. Who was the father? “Could this be the Messiah?” And so TITism was born. # August 2001Stephanie named her son Jesus firmly believing that it was the Messiah. Everyone agreed with her except for Sarah Reynolds who refused to use a belief of a billion people against them to some money. The others did, and they received hefty sums for TITism. Making her religion aside, Stephanie was feeling a bit out of place. She wanted more. She wanted Juan Santos. That was too much to ask for. She neglected to realize that Juan Santos was at his breaking point. Politics in the business coupled with his love, Emilia, turned Juan Santos inside out. There was too much for him to bear there and in August announced his retirement from the federation. He left the business. He left the TITans. He left Stephanie. # Present Juan Santos came back. Stephanie wants to marry him. End of story. Not quite. Juan Santos sat at the head of the table staring down at the meal before him. No, it wasn’t steak, chicken, pizza, lobster, or anything you might fancy. In fact, it was not food at all. The entire table was lined up with beer and in front of Juan was a tall glass. “Why am I doing this?” “Training,” Mike answered. He was standing on one side while Alex stood on the other side of Juan Santos. “Of all the matches to lose, you lose a Bar Room Brawl?” Alex asked. “Good God, Juan, we got an entire warehouse of beer next door.” “I didn’t lose the match because I couldn’t drink!” Juan replied. “I lost because I got distracted.” “Excuses.” Alex Reynolds remarked. “Now here, let’s start with some Sam Adams.” Juan shook his head. This was no way to train. What the hell happened to watching tapes, working out, and working on your wrestling moves? “I think the night went good for me,” Mike replied, grinning as he took a piece of paper from his pocket. “I got this hot Latina’s phone number. “I did better,” Alex said. “I got two numbers.” “Aren’t you married?” “Sarah doesn’t mind.” “Look, this is stupid,” Juan replied, “how is this going to help me train? I’m not even fighting Danny this week. I’m fighting some Polish dude.” “Then here,” Alex said, slamming down a bottle of Zyweic on the table. “Have some Polish beer.” “How the hell is this going to help me?” Juan asked. “You drink this bitter, disgusting, poorly brewed beer, and you can conquer any non-English speaking, Janina-humping, orphanage idea-stealing, second chance JITC winner, Shrek-like Polish ogre.” Juan shook his head. Stephanie walked into the diner. “What’s going on here?” “Training.” Stephanie grabbed a tall glass and sat across from Juan Santos. “I’ll help you train.” “What? This isn’t fucking training.” “I’m not talking about that,” Stephanie replied. “I got a deal for you.” “What?” “Marry me.” “No.” “Then out drink me, and you don’t have to marry me.” “I’m not going to do that anyway!” Juan replied. “But whatever. Let’s start with that Polish beer.” Juan and Stephanie both drank the Zyweic brand and they both spat it out. “Goddamn fucking Polish men can’t make a decent beer.” “I think I’m sterile now.” # I hope you’ve had enough time to think of your consequences. You chose to come to America. You chose to wrestle. You chose to join PRIME. There is no use now crying over spilled milk. That milk was spilled by you. You dropped the ball. Not the Americans. Not the people who hate you. Not that former heavyweight boxer you claimed to beat. You made this bed and now you have to sleep in it. Congratulations are in order for needing two tries to get done with what I got done in one: move on to the second round of the JITC. That’s as far as you’ll go. You see the world hate whiners. They hate whiny foreigners even more. That fairytale ending you had in mind? It’s been squashed, flattened, grinding, and kicked to the curb. This is reality, Tom. Time for you to join us. This is why people like you are left in their rooms crying over what ifs. People like me go and fuck other people’s wives. The world wasn’t meant to be handed to you. It was meant to be taken. That’s why I’m going to the third round, and you’re going nowhere but an ESL class.
