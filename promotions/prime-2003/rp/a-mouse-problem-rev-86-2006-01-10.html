A Mouse Problem (ReV 86)

10 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tues, Jan 10, 2006 A MOUSE PROBLEM INT. JOHNNY NOBLE’S HOUSE - DAY JOHNNY NOBLE and his son, JETHRO (mildly retarded, ala Forrest Gump) have just finished their morning prayers and Bible reading and are now sitting in front of the fireplace talking about things that make life great. JETHROFireplaces! JOHNNYJesus! JETHROHoyt! JOHNNYUhm… Johnny scratches the back of his head uncomfortably. JOHNNYI think that’s sacrilege, bud, how about… Sons! JETHRODads! The two hug whilst SAPPY MUSIC PLAYS in the background, indicating that we ought to weep. You know - if we were chicks or whatever. So anyway… Noble & Son are hugging like a couple of fairies from the Brady Bunch when the TELEPHONE RINGS abruptly JETHROPhones! JOHNNYYeah I - I guess. The evolution of technology is a good thing. Although Bell would never have invented that blasted thing if he knew telemarketers would target the dinner hour to pho-- JETHROYou gonna get the phone? JOHNNYRight. Johnny stands and walks to the phone. He stands in perfect posture as he answers the phone. JOHNNYHello? DONALDGood day. My name is Donald Blasco. I am a lawyer from the Disney/Pixar alliance. This call is to follow up on a notice we sent pursuant to seeing you appear on an edition of the popular PRIME program, “ReVolution” last week. We noticed that you were and continue to be introduced as “Mr. Incredible” Johnny Noble. Our correspondence addresses that. Did you receive our letter? JOHNNYOh. A letter? Interesting… no. Or if I did I threw it out. DONALDThrew it out? JOHNNYI probably thought it was some Disneyland brochure. DONALDIn a legal size envelope? JOHNNYI guess. DONALDAddressed from the office of “Disney/Pixar”? JOHNNYYeah. DONALDTo the best of your recollection, sir, do you know the name of Disneyland to have been changed to Disney/Pixarland?! JOHNNY… DONALDWell!? Do ya!? Punk! Johnny didn’t have time for this. He had a busy day ahead of him. JOHNNYLook, Mr-- Blaster? I don’t have time for this. I have a busy day ahead of me! I come from America, land of free speech, and I can call myself whatever I want. DONALD(Angrily)My NAME is Donald Blasc-- Johnny, not interested, hangs up the phone. JETHROWho was that, dad? JOHNNYNo one important son. JETHROWhat’re we doin’ today, dad? Johnny takes a knee beside his boy, the gleam in his eye paling the sun; the radiance of his smile a beacon of hope unto the world as a whole. JOHNNYFirst we’ll have a healthy, well-balanced breakfast. Then we’ll go read to the blind kids at the children’s hospice. After that we’ll take in a lunch of lentils and greens. Then we’ll hand out Bibles on Skid Row. Subsequently, we shall finish off our day with a prayer group at the prison. Jethro tears up a little JETHRONo coaching wheelchair basketball today? Johnny tries to keep a stiff upper lip, but this simple question has clearly hit him where he lives. He takes a deep breath and gives a purposed response. JOHNNY(choking up)No, son. Dammit, we just don’t have time. We. Don’t. Have. Time. If ONLY there were just a couple more hours in the day… Johnny pounds his fist on the arm of the chair and his son pats him on the back. JETHROIt’s alright dad. God sees your heart. JOHNNYThanks, son. Dramatic pause. ... Still. ... Almost done. ... Okay, good. JOHNNYHey! Let’s have some apples and Wheaties! Johnny stands and turns, walking to the kitchen. JETHROWith orange juice? Cue shock & awe music. Johnny whips his head around and stares a hole through his son. JETHRO (CONT’D)No O.J.? JOHNNYSon, the Citric Acid in the juice will curdle the milk in your stomach and make you terribly sick. Then we won’t be able to hand out those Bibles. We’ll be depriving those whores, pimps and crack-heads the opportunity to meet Jesus. What if we don’t have another chance? D’you want to be responsible for their eternal damnation? Noble’s adopted son almost cries. JETHRONo. JOHNNYAnd what about those prisoners? JETHRO(With repentance)NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! I’m sorry, dad. Johnny pats his sons head. JOHNNYIt’s okay, son. We all have moments of supreme selfishness, where we don’t think of others first. Jesus will forgive you. Jethro sniffs and wipes his nose with his forearm. JOHNNYNo time for that! C’mon! Granny Smith’s waiting for us! JETHRO(Quickly forgetting his sadness)Apples! JOHNNYHuh? JETHROI’m thankful for apples. Johnny laughs a little. A polite laugh, but not because he thought it was actually funny - cute maybe, but not funny. JOHNNYMe too, son. But we’re done that game now. JETHROOh-- okay. Breakfast is eaten; a healthy time is had by all. The duo put their shoes on and are just about to leave the house. Johnny unlocks and swings open the door to see DONALD BLASCO with a silk suit, a G-A-Y Ned Flanders moustache and a bad combover. He’s holding an attaché case. Noble (both of ‘em) gives the man a twice over. JOHNNYWhat can I do for you, sir? DONALDHello, my name is Donald Blasc-- JOHNNYBlaster? You again? Didn’t I already hang up on you? DONALDYes, bu-- JOHNNYLook, I don’t want to go to Disney/Pixarland. I don’t want to make any celebrity appearances. I’m not interested. I have a full day ahead of me with my boy. DONALDMr. Noble, this is a second letter of notice, I advise you to open it. JETHROBut, dad! Our hookers will be disappointed. A look of shock overcomes Donald. Johnny smiles awkwardly as his face goes flush. JOHNNYIt’s just that we-- DONALDI don’t need to know, sir. JOHNNYYeah, but we’re just ha-- DONALDReally, I’d rather not. Johnny snatches the man by the collar and yanks him into the air, pulling him close. JOHNNY(Angrily)We’re just handing out Bibles. They’ve started to expect us. Johnny releases the man. JOHNNYI suppose you’ve never tried to change your world for the better. DONALDI’m a lawyer, that’s not my job. Johnny opens the letter. INSERT: LETTER FROM DISNEY/PIXAR Mr Jonathan Noble,We have seen you on PRIME television introducing yourself as “Mr. Incredible” Johnny Noble. As I’m sure you’re aware the character of Mr. Incredible was made popular by the Disney/PIXAR alliance and is also copyrighted as such. You’ll need to cease and desist immediately in order to avoid further legal ramifications and civil penalties. You have two business days to comply.Thank-you,Disney/PIXAR BACK TO SCENE JOHNNYAnd when was this sent? DONALDThursday. JOHNNYRight. You guys work fast. JETHRODisney! JOHNNYWhat? JETHROI’m thankful for Disney. Johnny looks back over his shoulder at his son with an annoyed twinge in his eyes. JOHNNY(Irritated)No, son. Disney’s suing us. And that game is over now. Stop it! Donald reaches back into his briefcase and pulls out a second, folded, piece of paper and hands it to Noble, who rips it open and starts reading. DONALDIt’s a subpoena. You’ll need to be at the courthouse today at 3 P.M. or you’ll be in contempt of court and will face heavy fines. Noble shakes his head and looks back up. JOHNNYYou know what… the hell?? Where’d he go? Donald Blasco is gone and in his place is a dissipating poof of smoke. Johnny Noble steps through the poof and looks up and down the street - there was no silk anywhere. Not that he cared. He was too busy to listen to some jackass from Disney/Pixarland - he had to get down to the Children’s Hospice. INT. CHILDREN’S HOSPICE - BLIND KIDS WING - DAY NURSE KELLY walks into the room with a huge smile dressed in white. JOHNNY and JETHRO are standing behind her int eh doorway. The KIDS (MIKEY, JOSH, KENNY, WILL, JANNAH, BLANE, ANNICA, JILL & JOEY) hear the DOOR CREAK as she walks in and several sit up in their beds. The wing is long and contains several beds. It’s one, very large room. KENNYNurse Kelly? NURSE KELLYHey kids. It’s Tuesday, do you know what that means? Johnny and Kelly smile at each other. MIKEYAre Johnny and Jethro here? Johnny can’t keep it in any longer. JOHNNYYou know it! The kids rush out of their beds as fast as blind kids can run to Johnny and Jethro, who are standing in the doorway still. There is much hugging and embracing and things are good. JOHNNYSo, what story would you kids like to hear this morning? The kids are yelling out ideas frantically. WILLCurious George! JANNAHCinderella! JOEYThe Sword And The Stone! Noble tries to bring order to the room by making the suggestion he intends to follow through on. JOHNNYHow about ‘The Paper Bag Princess’? The kids reacts in chaos again. THE GIRLSYeah!The boys grumble and moan about the story being for girls. JOHNNYBut it’s got dragons! The boys are now on-board. JOHNNYOkay, now, everyone back in your beds and I’ll read you the story. The kids run again to get back into their beds and Johnny pulls up a chair in the center of the room with Jethro beside him. He opens the first page and begins reading. PULL AWAY, DISSOLVING TO:HALLWAY - DAY - LATERJOHNNY and JETHRO walk out of the Blind Kids Wing and close the door behind them. Both are beaming with the self-satisfaction that can only come from a job well done. The walk down the hallway a bit, not needing to talk, but smiling like goons. NURSE KELLYYou boys have a good day, alright? JETHROThank-you Nurse Kelly! Johnny and Jethro stop in their tracks as the open the door to the stairway (more healthy than the elevator) and see DONALD BLASCO standing there, smiling. You know, the kind of smile that makes you wanna punch a guy in the face until he stops, but you kinda hope he doesn’t stop too soon… a wee bit of disfigurement is good him! DONALDMr. Noble. My watch says that you have three hours in which to be at the courthouse. Have a wonderful lunch. Johnny reaches out and snatches Donald by his tie and yanks him in close. JOHNNYListen here, Blaster, I don’t want to break you’re your neck, but I will. So help me God-- JETHRODon’t do it, dad! Turn the other cheek! DONALDSpeaking of which, if you’d like to SAVE your cheeks, you’d better show up at the courthouse. Donald pushes Johnny off of him, with his massive quail arm. DONALD (CONT’D)And let go of me before I toss in an assault charge, sir. JOHNNYDon’t you have anything else to do with your time? Why are you following me around like a lost little puppy? DONALDFine! Have it your way! With a bright flash of light Donald is gone again and Johnny Noble finds himself standing in front of a piece of paper that simple says “15:00”. DISSOLVE TO: INSERT: CLOCK TICKING TO 1:00 P.M.DISSOLVE TO: INT. DELI - AFTERNOON The deli is nicely decorated. It’s downtown and classy, but not overly high-priced. Upper-middle of the road - they have tablecloths! JOHNNY NOBLE and JETHRO are paying the WAITRESS for their meal. WAITRESSAlright, y’all have a great day now! The waitress waves goodbye to Johnny and Jethro who walk out the door. JOHNNYNo, thank you! EXT. DELI - AFTERNOON The streets are crowded and busy as usual. JOHNNY and JETHRO are walking back to their fuel-efficient Smart car when they heard a LOUD CRASH in the intersection. Johnny and Jethro stop in their tracks and run to the intersection to see what they can do to help. JOHNNYRun faster, Jeth, those people could be dying! Pick up those knees, soldier! Johnny charges up towards the wreckage of the car accident and tries to open the door to a blue Lexus. It won’t budge Johnny starts pulling on the door, trying to rip it from its very hinges, but it doesn’t seem to want to budge. Jethro tries to help by enlisting the aid of those around the scene. JETHROJohnny! Johnny! Johnny! Before long the crowd is joining behind the young man and cheering on Mr. Noble. CROWDJohnny! Johnny! Johnny! Johnny! Johnny, lets go of the door for a second then lunges at it again and rips it clean off the car and tosses it to the side. He pulls out a limp and listless DONALD BLASCO.Cue suspense music.Johnny drops him on the ground and watches his bad Ned Flanders moustache twitching, then he opens his eyes and looks at Johnny. DONALDTwo hours left. JOHNNYFine! Dammit, Blaster, I’ll go! But I promise you that if even one of those pimps is shot tonight and burns in hell because I couldn’t get him the saving message of our Lord and Saviour-- DONALDSo you’ll go? JOHNNYDon’t interrupt me, that’s pretty rude. Didn’t your mother ever teach you proper manners? While Johnny is rambling Donald falls limp and dies. JOHNNYWell, that was uncalled for! JETHRODad, I think he’s dead. JOHNNY(Standing up)He’s just faking it cause he doesn’t want to talk to me. Johnny starts jabbing at Donald with his foot, poking and prodding with no response. JOHNNYC’mon, Donald! Get up you slacker! He starts kicking harder, in the face, Donald’s head flails about and scraps and bruises start appearing, but there’s still no response. MAN (O.S.)Hey! He’s beating that guy up! Johnny stops and looks around, his eyes darting left and right. He nudges Jethro and they both make a break for it. EXT. BEHIND DELI - AFTERNOON JOHNNY and JETHRO duck into the alley that leads to the deli’s parking lot as the masses run past them and down the street, chasing no one. JETHROSo, are you going to court? JOHNNYWell… Cue suspense music. ANNOUNCER (V.O.)Will Johnny Noble lose the rights to his name? Is Donald actually dead? Will Noble beat Baltasar? How does this RP apply to that match!?
