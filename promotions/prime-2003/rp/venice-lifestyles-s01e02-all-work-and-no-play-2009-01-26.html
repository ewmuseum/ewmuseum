Venice Lifestyles - S01E02 - All Work and No Play

26 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 26, 2009 Monday, January 26, 2009 – Early Afternoon Given that Nitz Donnelly had been in Venice Beach for one day shy of two weeks would lead most people to believe that he had not dug himself out of the hole of personal poverty he was in when his plane landed. Not everyone in these shallow economic times could arrive anywhere and succeed fast enough to get themselves back on track in a fortnight. In saying that, a couple questions arise. Why then was Nitz sitting pretty on the beach, a cold beer in one hand and a Maxim Magazine in the other, enjoying the sunshine and scenery with his newest friend Vance? The answer is Leticia. Yes, Leticia had been putting Nitz up, getting him groceries and other amenities along with letting him live in a beach house she had a hand in owning with her father. Question number two was a tad more complicated however. “Shouldn't you be out job hunting?” Vance's deep voice was certainly enough to distract Donnelly from his reading. Venice's newest resident rested the magazine on his knee. “You know as well as I do there's nothing out there.” “Of course I do. I'm just bustin' your chops. Wrestling not something you wanna do again?” An uncomfortable look appeared on Nitz's face at the mention of wrestling. Sure the paychecks were really good and sure the fame was something he started to become accustomed to. But ever since he left PRIME due to injury and was released, hit stomach began to do back flips whenever it was mentioned again. “Honestly man, I've been telling myself I'll go back when I'm ready. Whether it be to PRIME, GCW, SCCW or any of the big names out there. Fact is, I'm just not ready.” “I hear ya Nick. I told you I was in FUSE, I know how it feels.” Nitz nods and is about to go back to his magazine when he spots Leticia walking up the beach towards them. He also spots about a dozen other bikini clad women and with no offence to his friend, he was wishing one of them was approaching them instead. “Sunning without me now?” “What can I say Letty my dear, soon as Vance took me down here, I've been hooked. We don't have shit like this at home.” As soon as he mentioned home, his eye twitched. It was more of a natural reaction, as he wondered just what his family was doing and if they were wondering exactly where he was. The very slight action went unnoticed by his friends. “Heads up.” Leticia tossed something up in the air and Donnelly hat to drop his magazine to catch it. “A phone?” “A Blackberry. Not to mention there's a new suit hanging on your door.” “You know damn well I don't wear suits.” “Sure, but when you go see my Dad, you gotta dress nice. Last I checked, you had a few pairs of jeans, some shorts and a pair of board shorts for swimming. I don't think he would appreciate that kind of dress.” Leticia smiled and sat between Nitz and Vance on a blanket. “Would have been nice to know this a little ahead of time.” “I just talked to him an hour ago. I happened to mention your name and he said he might have some work for you.” “Slit my wrists right now if I have to wash dishes in the restaurant or bring someone room service. Death would seem more welcoming.” “Actually, you'd be working with Vance in some capacity.” Her words caused Vance to raise an eyebrow. As Nitz was still looking away, she raised a hand, basically promising to explain once Nitz was otherwise occupied. “Guess it wouldn't hurt. But I'm not wearing a tie.” “His number is in the phone. Call him to see if he's free.” “Love your Dad and all, but I swear if he calls me kiddo like the night after I got here...” He sees the number listed in the phone book of his new phone. As soon as he dials it and puts the phone to his ear, Vance tugs on the side of her shirt a little. She leans over to him, acting somewhat inconspicuous. “You sure that's wise?” Vance quietly spoke. Leticia cleared some hair from her face. “Don't worry. It'll be just security for now. Maybe later you'll have him doing more. I already talked to my Dad. The talk is irrelevant. He's in if he wants to be.” “Hope you know what you're talking about.” Vance goes quiet as soon as Nitz looks to disconnect the phone call. The rather secretive talk would sound suspicious to anyone if they heard it. Clearly, Nitz was left out in the cold about certain details. “I'll be seeing him in an hour.” “Good.” Leticia waved off Donnelly from handing her the phone back. “Phone's yours. Go get ready and I'll go up there with you. Need to check on a few things anyways.” As the words escape her mouth, a young lady approached Nitz. She was quite attractive and rather well endowed at both her front and rear bumpers. She kneeled down on the other side of him and was about to fulfil the wish of Donnelly and many other male patrons in the sand. “Could you rub some sun tan lotion on me? I'll burn so fast in this sun.” The redheaded beauty handed him a bottle of suntan lotion. He grinned and glanced back at both Vance and Leticia who both shook their heads. Vance did however crack a slight smile. “You bet darlin'! Can never be too safe with skin as beautiful as yours.” “Awwww thank you!” The well built woman said as she dropped the straps on her black bikini top. She turned her back to Nitz and he turned and glanced back at Leticia again. “Gimme about 10 Letty.” He smiled. Vance smiled. The redhead even smiled. Leticia just groaned. |>~o~<| So there he was, sitting in front of the secretaries desk on the third floor of the hotel condo. The middle of the building was hollow, a few tall palm trees growing in the middle. The hotel seemed to be built around the trees with glass on every floor towards the middle. One side of the circle was also glass, making the building almost like a horseshoe type but not oval in shape. Donnelly looked up at the secretary and she smiled a bit at him before answering a ringing phone. He sighed and fixed his collar on his white shirt. Not for a single second did he regret his decision to leave the tie at home. He hadn't worn a tie since his grandmothers funeral and he was only a kid then so his mother always put out his clothes for him. He was out for dinner the second night he was in town with Leticia and her father and that meeting was purely personal. He had only seen him one other time last week when he decided to visit the gym to see Leticia. He was on an exercise bike as they both went into her office, most likely to discuss the establishments finances. Nitz had known her father from the moment he got to know Leticia when they were just children. He had always loved spending time at her place back then cause her Dad always had the brand name soda, the good potato chips and the really good food for dinners. Plus he always took the kids to see movies, to the arcade and even to the amusement park. Nitz's brother and sister were always somewhat jealous, even though they were both invited to join them every now and then. But it wasn't just the material things that he loved about being there. Jose Mendoza had always treated Nicholas like he was his own son. In return the Donnelly family, though not as well off as Mr. Mendoza, treated Leticia like one of their own as well. Even though they didn't always have the money to completely fill the fridge, freezer or pantry, they were always happy to set out an extra place setting at the dinner table or to leave a spot open on the couch for the broadcast premiere of a made for TV movie or the rental of a VHS tape. So what Nitz Donnelly saw in front of him while sitting before the large wooden doors leading into Mr. Mendoza's office was just a bit intimidating. What he felt was that Mr. Mendoza, with all his success and the power he seemed to have, was now larger than life. Perhaps even larger than his life he thought. “Sir, Mr. Mendoza will see you now.” Nitz took a deep breath and nodded as he got up from his chair and opened the large door. The beautiful office was lit up with a view of the ocean. The sun shined in and almost blinded Nitz for a second as he covered his eye. As he got closer to the desk, the sun disappeared behind the pillar in the corner of the room. Jose Mendoza was sitting in his desk, reading over some papers and sipping a golden liquid that resembled scotch. Nitz took a sat in the chair across the desk from Mr. Mendoza and sat for a moment, intertwining his fingers and trying to figure out why Leticia's father hadn't yet addressed him. He didn't sound like a happy person on the phone either. Finally, he acknowledges the young man's presence. “You happy to be here, making me feel pressured into helping you out after all I did for you all those years ago Nicholas?” OK, Donnelly was officially dumbfounded. He even felt like a heel. He knew Leticia may have been a tad bit more shifty in earning Nitz this little talk with her father but he could have never guessed that Mr. Mendoza would be this resentful of their ideas. The obviously successful businessman got up from his chair and buttoned the front of his suit. “You just think these big ass doors are just open to anyone who walks in here, looking for a job, money, a free stay in a penthouse or even a beach house?” Ouch. Jose Mendoza shook his head and made his way around the desk to sit in front of Donnelly on the corner. “My daughter spends all kinda of money and now I'm supposed to give out another salary in these tough economic times? Get up and face me kid.” Immediately, Nitz was wishing for the extra “do” on the end of kid. He suddenly missed that term as he stood, a tad nervous of where this was going. “Now I've made you think haven't I? Feelin' just a bit bad about the terrible position you've put me in?” “Y-yeah.” “Yeah? You mean yes!” “Y- yes.” The normally uncensored Donnelly was now tight lipped and uncharacteristically buttoned up around the man he admired as a kid. He wouldn't dare toss out insults. “Why you stuttering kid? Yes what?” “Yes... sir.” “Good...” He paused and a stern look was visible on his face. “Leticia will be happy her little prank worked.” He smiled and reached out his arms and stepped to the young man, wrapped his arms around him and patted him on the back. Nitz's eyes opened wide and in an act of second nature hugged the man he considered a second father. He still wasn't entirely sure about what happened. Jose Mendoza broke the hug and grabbed Nicholas by the shoulders as he laughed. “Damn Nicky, you're gullible!” He smacked his shoulder a little and walked towards the little bar he had in the corner of the room. Donnelly exhaled in relief. “Leticia put you up to that?” “You shoulda seen the look on your face kiddo.” There was the extra “do” he craved just a short moment ago. “I had a bet with Leticia about whether you'd fall for this little charade. Turned me into a losing man by clamming up the way you did.” He poured a couple more glasses of scotch as he spoke. Nitz was wiping the sweat from his forehead. “I'll get her later for this.” he sharply exhaled again, still working the tense situation out of his system. “Oh I know you will. But come here and have a drink first son.” Mr. Mendoza held forward the now half full glass of golden liquid. Nitz approached slowly and took the glass, relieved the little prank was over. He sipped on the liquid but couldn't stop there as he took a larger gulp. The warmth of the wonderful beverage soothing his dry throat. Jose Mendoza laughed after taking a sip of his own, carrying the drink over to his desk and placing the new glass down to the empty one he was sipping from moments earlier. “You're family Nicky. The son I never had. You'd actually believe for a second that I'd let you flounder and falter out here in Venice? This is the land of sun, surf and sultry women son. Oh and scotch.” He raised the glass and took a deep gulp. “I intend to give you a nice amount of time to enjoy all that. But you'll be enjoying it with cash in your pocket too. THAT'S how I treat family of mine.” “I know sir, it's just...” “SIR? Oh come on kiddo, you know better than that. What'd you call me when you were a kid?” “Papa Jose.” “That's right. Just Papa as well. It's been many years kiddo, but nothing's changed.” From the beginning, Nitz called Mr. Mendoza Papa Jose. Dad was reserved for his own father and the only person on the planet allowed to say Daddy in addressing Jose Mendoza was Leticia. Plus, it would sound a bit feminine and odd coming from Nitz. “So where's that outspoken crude personality I saw on TV a little over a year ago?” “I wouldn't dare, not here... not with you.” “Don't let all this that's around you deceive you Nicky. I'm not untouchable. I'd certainly like to be called out on something if I was wrong. And it'd show balls to do it much like you did on TV.” “Some of that's an act. I mean I'm really like that, but I'd like to think I could be a little more stern and abrasive than calling someone an ass raping, cock smoking son of a bitch all the time.” Nitz took another sip of his drink and the vulgar humour won a bright smile from Papa Jose. “True, but it's funny as hell.” He paused as he nodded his head with a smile. “K kiddo, here's the deal. During every executive meeting we have here, we need security. Some of these people are millionaires and some of them are near billionaires. I'm not afraid to admit that plenty of them could buy and sell me if they wanted to, but they respect me, just as I respect them. Based on that mutual respect, we demand the top in security just in case. That's why I don't have some silly group in here that has the simple requirement of a vest and a flashlight. These guys expect the best and I know Vance is the best. How about you?” “I'd always do what I could for you, you know that.” “I do. Three hundred bucks for every three hours of work per meeting. If a meeting goes long, perhaps four hours, you'll get an extra hundred-fifty. The job is basically to secure the main conference room, and keep an eye through the glass on the front door for any suspicious activity. Also, you and Vance walk a couple execs back to their rooms where their own personal security takes over or walk them to their limos. That's it.” Nitz immediately raised his eyebrows at the mention of the payment. He was stunned at the money being thrown his way and felt a smart ass comment was in order. “Really? Who am I supposed to kill?” “If someone has the brass to mess with anyone in my building, I'd hope you and Vance could get a hold of them well before they caused the people they were after any grief. What you do when you get a hold of them is your business. But it'd be expected that you'd contain them and call the police.” Clearly there was no joking to be had. Or so it was originally thought. “As for who you kill, maybe Graham Hightower. He owns an insurance firm in California. He can be a dick.” “If he's a dick, I'm sure he's got people who want him dead. We'll let them get by on the promise of not hurting anyone but him.” “Hahahaha you're learning already son.” He walked around the desk, patted Nitz on the shoulder and grabbed his hand in a gentle shake. “You may not be in Boston anymore Nicky, but you're still with family here. Your family doing well?” “From what I know.” “I hear ya kiddo, change in scenery and all, you just need a break. I'm not asking any questions about why you're here, but we're sure happy you are. Venice treating you well?” Immediately, Nitz thought about the redhead on the beach that he had a date with scheduled for Friday. Her name was Michelle and she was from Arkansas. He silently wondered if she knew how to grease a trailer hitch. “Not much pulls me away from Beantown, but Venice is one of the few places. It's great here.” “You betcha Nicky. Give it a little more time and a few more bikini's and you might never leave.” Donnelly couldn't do anything but nod in agreement. Everything that had currently happened in Venice seemed to be driving a nails into his feet, keeping him from leaving the Golden State anytime soon. |>~o~<| “I really owe you one for that little prank you pulled on my with your Dad.” “Hahaha, yeah. Bet my Dad dinner that you'd fold in from of him like an accordion. So enjoy that lobster cause it's on him.” Leticia laughed, taking a sip of her wine. “I hope Vance isn't being held up.” “Yeah, Big Van's missing out on some top notch seafood here.” Nitz wasn't sure exactly what to say. It had been two weeks since he showed up in Venice and this was only the second time that he had a quiet, private time with Leticia. The first being the discussion about Vance and how Leticia “saved him”. But he was anxious to catch up with his former friend. Anyone looking on with a blind eyes would assume that they were a couple judging by how happy they were together. But even though Donnelly could appreciate that she grew up to be a beautiful woman, his feelings towards her extended to a really strong friendship and even being her brother. “So... what've you been doing here since you guys moved?” “Well I'm not going into detail about much of being a kid, so I'll start when I was 19.” She paused, taking another sip of her wine and motioning for more to top up the glass. Nitz obliged her. “I had the idea for the club, presented it to my Dad who was happy to help me by financing it. It was just paid off at the end of last year but he still owns a piece of it and runs it with me. Had a boyfriend since I was 18 and was set to get married after he proposed in late 2006. But by mid 2007, he cheated on me, tried to steal some of my money to finance his bitches singing career and I dumped him on his face... literally. I managed to shove him out of a moving car. Last I heard he had to spend the last of the money he took from me to finance his third nose job after he lost half of it on the free way. I'm sure he's his trailer park's darling right now.” Nitz thought about everything that was just mentioned to him. It was a cold, yet content tone that she spoke with. He never knew Leticia could be so calculating and so brutal as to remove a guy from a moving vehicle, but it's like they say... hell hath no fury like a woman scorned. “I'm sure glad I wasn't that guy.” “Yep, and his name was Ralph.” “OK, and his parents hated him too!” Both of them shared a laugh. Leticia smiled at her friend and Nitz smiled back. It was like they were never apart for all those years. Both broke eye contact to focus on their meals again. Almost as a final act of finishing off a great day for Nitz, Leticia's cell phone buzzed just as she dipped a piece of lobster into her butter sauce and put it in her mouth. She wiped her hands as Donnelly took a final sip of his wine before needing to pour himself more. Leticia read the message quick and returned the phone to her side, but not before deleting the message. “Vance isn't coming. Had to drive a guy to the airport who was staying at the hotel.” “Gotcha.” Nitz seemed somewhat happy Vance wouldn't be making it. It was no offence to the big guy whom he'd grown rather fond of in the short time he knew him, but he just had that urge to play catch up with her. “So what about you Nicky? What happened out in Boston?” “You know me, I keep things short and sweet. Trained for wrestling in Mexico and Japan, joined up with PRIME. Brother acted like a dick head in how he left for Seattle. Dad was a drunk for awhile. Ummmmm, Mom beat cancer, I got hurt in PRIME and it took months to get my mobility back. Dave has his own chowder bar and got dumped by someone I cared about. How's that?” “Oh... wow.” Leticia knew who dumped Nitz but she wouldn't dare mention the name in front of him. “That's pretty detailed in just a few sentences. Makes me sound like I was talking too much.” “Never Letty, I enjoy listening to you.” “You know Nicky, I know that you've heard this a lot since you came here, but My Dad and I especially are really glad you're here.” Leticia placed a hand on Nitz's and he smiled at her warmth. “It's been so many years and I know you love Boston, but we'd be thrilled if you stayed here with us.” “As many times as I have heard it, it doesn't get old coming from you guys.” Both of them smiled and held hands briefly. The waiter brought by the dessert menu, prompting them to get done with their dinner before the night grows even later. Meanwhile... Raymes opened the trunk to retrieve a tire iron. It was almost 70 degrees outside still and yet, he was wearing gloves. The car was not running, nor was it stopped on the side of the road. A quick glance at the tires revealed that all four tires were still inflated. But unlike his neighbourhood of Venice Beach, he was certainly somewhere else. The building in front of him was run down, broken windows and peeling paint. Spray paint littered the outside of the building as well and the view of the back lane showed a couple torn down vehicles with nothing left but the shell and frame. Upon grabbing hold of the tire iron, he closed the trunk and used the same free hand and gathered his cell phone from his pocket. Upon a bunch of keystrokes, it was obvious he was sending a message to Leticia about meeting for dinner. ”Running too late. Not gonna make it. Terms of a contract need to be clarified.”
