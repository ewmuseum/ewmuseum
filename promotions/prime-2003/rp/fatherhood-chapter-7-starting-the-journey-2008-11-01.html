Fatherhood - Chapter 7 - Starting the Journey

1 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 1, 2008 SATURDAY NOVEMBER 1, 2008 HILO MEDICAL CENTER ROOM 3233 2:30 PM For the past few days, the Pacific Ocean had contributed to over 72 hours of rainfall on the city of Hilo, Hawaii, making the normally popular tourist magnet a ghost town. The beaches, normally crowded nearly every day in the afternoon hours, were barren. The restaurants that normally held a 45 minute wait before you were seated for your meal were now holding a 5 minute wait, if that. The local shops, filled with fun souvenirs and vacation keepsakes, were desolate. Aside from the local inhabitants, Hilo was, under all intensive purposes, dead…except for Room 3233 of the Hilo Medical Center, which was bustling with activity. Why? It had been exactly one month since Nicholas Chandler Dawkins had been born, and the day that he would be removed from his incubator. Family and friends of the Sivec and Dawkins families waited in 3233 for Nicholas to make his arrival. The nurses had taken him away nearly an hour ago to remove him from the device that kept him healthy and gave him strength over the past month, and were running some final tests to ensure that this was, in fact, the ideal time to expose him to the real world. Mike and Pam Sivec stood close to Bryan, hand in hand, their eyes glued to the door, in anticipation of their (grand)son’s arrival. Their grip on Bryan’s hand tightened with each nurse who passed the door, and there was a sense of panic with each passerby. As the moments passed, nervous glances were passed throughout the room, and slowly, family members and friends alike were mumbling to each other, no doubt wondering if something had gone wrong during the tests. Everyone in the room knew that these families had endured enough pain and suffering for a lifetime, and that there was a great possibility that another tragedy could very well push them to their emotional limits. Pam and Mike shot nervous glances to each other, and kept their grip on Bryan’s hand. He knew that he had to be the strong one from the day that Nikkie passed away, even if knowing that he is fully responsible for taking care of another life by his lonesome is the most terrifying thing Bryan’s ever had to face. He had to be the emotional support for them, whether he was ready to undertake that role or not. He had to be ready. Bryan gave both of them a quick smile and took a deep breath. BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ BZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ Dawkins jumped, somewhat shocked from the random vibrations of his cell phone. Knowing all too well that he couldn’t answer the phone in the room (due to being reprimanded by various nurses on numerous occasions), Bryan excused himself and made his way out the door and into the hallway. Taking a few steps down the hall, Bryan finally got around to removing his phone from his pocket , only to find that he had missed the call. Dawkins flipped the phone open, reading the message that the small screen displayed: MISSED CALL CHANDLER TSONDA 3:05 PM - NOVEMBER 1, 2008 "Shit, my bad, bruh…" Just as Dawkins pressed the "Send" button on his phone to return Tsonda’s call, he was interrupted by a voice he knew all too well. "Apparently you haven’t learned your lesson. No cell phone usage allowed." Dawkins, ashamed of being caught for what must have been the tenth time, turned around to face the music. However, he turned around to see Marnee, Uncle Chan’s "sweetheart", standing in front of him, with a bundled up Nicholas Chandler Dawkins in her arms. Marnee gave Bryan the opportunity of a lifetime. "You wanna take him in, or would ya like me to do the honors?" The Flyin’ Hawaiian’s eyes lit up, and a tear started to form in his left eye. "…" It wasn’t like Bryan didn’t know the answer. There laid Nicholas Chandler Dawkins, the son that Bryan hadn’t even been able to hold yet, and he had the opportunity to welcome his son into the family by carrying him into a room filled with his family and friends. Needless to say, Bryan was speechless. "I uh…I mean…you know…" Marnee continued to stand there with a confused expression painted across her face. "So is that a yes or a no?" Bryan didn’t even hear the question. He was too busy looking at his son. The amazing thing was the fact that Nicholas was making eye contact with his father. Those little emerald eyes were locked onto Bryan’s, and it caused that tear from his left eye trickled down his face, and onto his cheek. After wiping the moisture from his face, he simply extended his arms out to receive his son. "Alright Nicholas, here’s your daddy." And with that, Marnee handed Nicholas over to his father, causing Bryan’s eyes to well up tenfold. Marnee took the time to pull out a tissue to dry the tears from Dawkins’ face before giving him a gentle shove in the general direction of his room. "Alright Nick, time to go meet your family…" * * * After hours and hours (which really seemed like days and days) of visitors, Pam, Mike, and Bryan were left alone with Nicholas. Surprisingly, the one-month-old was still awake, and was receiving his goodbyes from his grandparents. For the first time, Pam and Mike kissed their grandson on the forehead, bringing them both to tears of joy. It was an amazing day, and the real start of Nicholas’ life story. The grandparents exchanged hugs with Bryan and then went on their way, for there was shopping to do; cribs to buy, diapers to stock up on, toys to assemble, and a room to decorate. They took one last glance at their grandchild and would-have-been son-in-law and went on their way. Bryan was left alone with his son, without a barrier between them for the first time. For almost an hour, the two sat there, speechless (one because he is physically unable to form words, obviously), communicating simply with their actions. Bryan would offer his index finger as an object for Nicholas to latch on to, and the baby Dawkins would grab hold of it with one hand and tighten his grip as much as his little body could. Bryan simply smiled, and finally broke the silence. "You’re a strong little bruh, aren’t ya, Nick?" Little Nicholas, seemingly unable to understand the language of his father, continued to test his grip on his father’s finger. Occasionally he would kick his tiny legs up into the air, flailing them aimlessly, but eventually he would calm back down and resume his control over his father’s digit. "Your mom would’ve been proud." More tears formed in the eyes of the most proud man in Hawaii. "Ya know, your mom and I, we were gonna get married. I was gonna give her this…" Reaching into his pocket with his free hand, Bryan pulled out a small box, covered with a velvet black material. Flipping open the top, he revealed a ring with three gigantic diamonds in it. One, the biggest, was the typical glassy clear diamond you would normally see on a wedding ring. The other two, the smaller diamonds, were blue and emerald, respectively. "The blue one is for me, because she always talked about how she loved my deep blue eyes. The green one was for her, but I guess now that you’re around, it would be for you too, ‘cuz you and your mom…you guys have the most amazing green eyes, little bruh. Those are gonna get ya a lot of looks from the ladies when you get older…I’m tellin’ ya. And the white one…" The tears began to flow yet again from the Hawaiian’s eyes. "…that one was gonna be to show that no matter what kinda storm we’d have to go through, things would always clear up. Things wouldn’t always be great, but no matter what, she would always be able to look at this ring and see that they’ll eventually be crystal clear, just like this diamond." It might have just been that Bryan was seeing things. Or maybe it was the fact that his vision was blurred from the tears that had formed in his eyes. Whatever it was, Bryan could have sworn that when he glanced down at Nicholas, the infant was staring intently at his father, almost like he was listening to every word that Bryan was saying. For that brief moment, Bryan really felt that father-son connection that so many people talk about. Some people may say that it’s all a myth, but Bryan learned from first-hand experience that it’s very possible to connect with your son. "I love her, little bruh. No matter what happens, I’ll always love your mom. And I’m gonna be tellin’ you that ‘til the day I die and get to see her again. Until then, I’m gonna be here for ya. I know there’s a good possibility you’ll never remember this conversation when ya get older, but on the off-chance that ya do, I want ya to listen." Dawkins set the ring on a small table next to him and gave his index finger back to Nicholas to play with. "Don’t ever be afraid to come to me, Nick. As long as I’m around, I’m gonna be here for ya. Every cent I make is gonna go to make sure that ya have a good life. It’s gonna be rough for these first couple months, takin’ care of ya and everything, but ya gotta give me a break, little bruh. I’m new at this, ya know? I’m pretty sure I’ll get the hang of it sooner or later, but we gotta give it time, Nick." Again, for a moment, Nick stopped playing with his father’s finger and stared into Bryan’s eyes. "And even if I mess this up, at least we’ve got Uncle Chan, right? I mean, bruh’s been steerin’ your dad in the right direction since he got this new job with PRIME, ya know? Chan’s gotta know somethin’ about bein’ a dad, right?" Wait. Chandler Tsonda. Being a dad? C’mon now, that’s like saying Tyler Rayne is going to be in a successful long-term relationship some day. Anyway, I digress. "Okay, maybe not, but still. Nick, what I’m sayin’ is that I’m gonna need time to adjust to this whole ‘being a father’ thing, so if ya promise to be patient with me, I won’t get mad the first few times ya decide to use permanent markers on the wall to make your first childhood masterpiece. Sound like a deal to you?" Nicholas nonchalantly continued to test his grip on his father’s pointer finger. "Alright, little bruh, sounds great to me, too." "Sounds like Nicholas has a pretty good father, even if he’s new at this whole thing." Marnee. "Haha, yeah right…" Bryan smiled. "…so uh, how long ya been there?" Marnee strolled into the room and pulled up a seat beside Bryan. She then pointed at the open ring box sitting on the table beside him. "Oh…" Bryan’s smile faded, and she could see his eyes begin to water yet again. "It must be hard losing someone you love, but then gaining a brand new part in your life. I understand that you can’t just replace one with the other, so it couldn’t have been easy." "It’s terrifying, ya know? The only reason I looked strong through her whole pregnancy period was because I didn’t want her getting’ all freaked out, ya know? I had to be strong, even if I had no idea what the hell I was doin’. Hell, I still don’t know what the hell I’m doin’." Marnee listened carefully. Bryan needed someone to talk to. Someone who could talk back. "But you’re doing all you can, Bryan. I’ve seen it. Every day that you haven’t had to be somewhere for your job, you’ve been here with Nicholas. I haven’t seen a new father as dedicated as you are since I’ve started to work here. You’re here every hour of every day that you possibly can be, and that’s all you can do. Like you said earlier, there are gonna be bumps in the road, but part of being a great parent is learning how to deal with those bumps and moving on from them. And judging by how you dealt with losing the person who meant most to you, I’m pretty sure you’ll be able to handle anything thrown at you." "Thanks…" Bryan turned his attention back to Nicholas, who was still clamped onto his finger, like it was Christmas morning and Bryan’s finger was his favorite new present. Marnee smiled and gently gave Bryan a soft punch to the arm. "Anyway, I gotta get back to work. Don’t hesitate to stop me if you need something." And with that, she was gone, and Bryan and Nick were left by their lonesome. "Well, little bruh, let’s get ya good and healthy so we can get ya out on the road with daddy…"
