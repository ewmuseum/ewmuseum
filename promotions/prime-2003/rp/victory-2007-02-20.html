Date: 2/20/07 Match preparation is always a big part of a wrestler's life. Your tacticians will spend hours watching tapes of their upcoming opponent's matches, looking for anything they can use as an advantage against their opponent. Does he drop his right shoulder just for a moment before he shoots in towards a leg? Does he always follow an Irish whip into a corner with a splash? Does he favor that surgically repaired right knee just a little bit? They rely on their knowledge to get the victory. Your big power wrestlers will hit the gym hard, hammering away at their muscles, lifting weights with utter abandon, trying to get every ounce of performance they can squeeze out of their bodies.. They'll punch heavy body bags, they'll take protein supplements, they'll do exercises that sound like positions in a gay porno like the squat thrust or the clean and jerk. Their bodies are their temples, and they pray to the fitness gods to get the victory. Your high flyers will eat spend hours contorting themselves into awkward positions, trying to stretch their ligaments in ways nature had never intended. High impact cardio workouts, lots of time on exercise bikes, treadmills, swimming laps, all to try to extend their wind a little more so they can keep their high speed offense from faltering in lengthy matches. They'll run ropes, they'll jump on trampolines, they'll walk balance beams. Their speed and flexibility are their roads to victory. And then there's Michael Kriegman. An unorthodox man will undoubtably have unorthodox preperation methods. This is definately the case with primary persona, the Trashcan Man. Before he had lost all his money, before he had lost his fame, back when he was on top of his game and on top of any federation he was in, he had the Danger Yard. The Danger Yard was an old scrapheap the Trashcan Man had turned into what he referred to as a "hardcore dojo". An eccentric mishmash of traps and obstacles, programmed to trigger in a random order everytime. He'd have to duck flames from rows of acetylene torches as he'd disable hubcap flinging mannequins with a lead pipe. He'd be flat on his back after knocking over an old tackling dummy and barely roll out of the way, avoiding a car being dropped from a magnetic crane high above. Barbed wire wrapped scarecrows would rake at his flesh as he'd use them as a shield from the nail guns firing slivers of iron at him. He'd come out of his training sessions bruised, bloody, and burned. Toughness has always been his path to victory. Match preperation takes a completely different form for Mr Smiley Face. Instead of physically getting ready for a match, he chooses instead to get himself in the right mindset. And over the years this has taken many forms. It could be as simple as sitting around reading Archie comics and watching Monty Python sketches. It could be as convoluted as kidnapping Danny Bonaduce and stealing the Partridge Family bus. He's forced a demon trapped in a little girls body to watch The Sound Of Music over and over again for days on end. He's visited some of the roughest streets in all the world... Sesame Street. Comedy and chaos are his partners on the way to victory. This week will be the biggest match Michael Kriegman's had since his return to PRIME. He's coming off a very tough loss against one of PRIME's newest rising stars in Garbage Bag Johnny. He's going to be facing two men who have to be counted amongst the greatest wrestlers in the world today, Jason Snow and Angelo Deville. And they were allies. With these overwhelming odds, match preperation would be key. So how does one prepare for an undertaking such as this? If it were the Trashcan Man, would be slamming his head into a brick wall, trying to toughen himself up, hoping to outlast the brutal beating both men would be delivering? If it were Mr Smiley Face, would he dress up as the ghost of Steve Irwin, stingray implanted in his chest as he ran around the streets of Melbourne trying to convince the native Australians that Jesus Christ himself had brought back their national hero to life? These questions will not be answered today, no sir. You see, another persona would be in this match. One untested in the ring, thrown to the lion's den with an unarmed, hogtied Christian's chance of survival. Jumpin' Jack Flash. Offended by his romantic advances, the current 5 Star champion Sun Tzu had stacked the deck against this most flamboyant of Michael Kriegman's personalities. How would he prepare for this... how COULD he prepare?
