Kill Me in the Morning [2]

17 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Aug. 17, 2005 Kill Me in the Morning [2] The air in this place was thick, tumbling my insides for a place to reside. Nonetheless, I continued to sit there, watching as the bar tender served up drinks to all of the local customers. My focus was not on those present around me though, but instead on those who were not present around me. I came to this bar tonight in hopes that I would find a very special woman. A woman who I had been watching for quite some time, embracing the day in which I would be able to call her my own. Several different sources led me here, as I was told that she spent a lot of her free time in places such as this. This was my first time being in such a place...of course being a deviant such as I doesn't sit too well with the general public and doesn't draw any real positive attention towards me. But tonight I retired the face paint, revealing my scarred and battered face to mankind, in hopes that it could bring this one woman to become my own. She was a real woman, the one you didn’t mind taking home to your mother. I watched her beautiful body sway back in forth everyday with the utmost elegance, punishing each and every male species standing before her with guilt that she was not in the comfort of their arms. She was highly favored in the beauty department, and even more favored in the skill department. She packed the perfect package for any woman. Brains, Beauty, and skill...what else could you ask for? Of course I knew she would never want to be involved with a man who walked around, threatening people with different forms of violence…especially one with a reputation resembling mine. A woman of her stature could easily get any man she wanted with the snap of her finger and use that man anyway she pleased...why would she want me? Despite obviously realizing that she would want nothing to do with my presence, even if I begged, I continued to sit at that bar, hoping that she would come in and treat me to her warm and luscious goodies. I smiled as I thought of her goodies and just how good they must have been. Dreams...that’s all they were...Dreams of her providing me with the satisfaction and joy that had been absent in my life for far too long...Dreams of her pleasing me in each and every way that she possibly could until I pleaded for her to stop...Dreams of her tickling my fancy and making me one of her own. They were dreams for now, but soon I looked to make them so much more. I had been her for 10 minutes already and no sign of her yet. Suddenly, 10 minutes turned into 20 minutes, and 20 minutes turned into 40 minutes, and 40 minutes turned into an hour, and an hour turned into 2 hours...After that second hour is when I realized that she wasn't going to show. It felt as if my heart had been shot with an unforgiving .22 as I watched the few remaining people left in the bar exit. The bartender scanned over me as I took one last glance over the bar, making sure that I hadn't overlooked her. Leaving anytime soon? He looked at me with a hint of annoyance in his eye, probably waiting for me to leave like everyone else so that he could close up shop. I took a deep sigh as well as my final glance before snatching up the 8th glass of scotch that I had ordered some 30 minutes ago and downing it. I grabbed my coat, slipped it on, and also the roses I had bought for her and made my way out of the bar, with my head hanging low like it always did. Roses, Money, Boxes of Candy...I bought all of it prematurely, looking to draw her into a romantic dinner that would lead to something even more satisfying...but just as my luck had it, she didn't show up. Down on my luck, I walked home through the rain, not bothering to shield myself from the wet splashes. I thought that around this time tonight I would have her wrapped up and cuddled in my arms...but it wasn't meant to be so. Reaching my low-budgeted apartment, I opened the door and flicked on the light, exposing my dirty trash field room. I took another deep sigh and placed the roses on the nearby dresser before walking over to my dress up mirror. There it was...the one thing that I could count on no matter what. The one thing that would bring me joy and satisfaction no matter what trials Life brought my way. The one thing that I could always count on to be there during any storm, during any weather. My best friend....Heroin. I took it and placed the cold needle on my upper left shoulder, pressing down so that it injected itself into my skin. I smiled as the fluid trickled through my veins but then suddenly yanked the needle out, throwing it through the mirror that stood on the dresser, shattering the mirror into tons of pieces. I looked at myself through the shattered glass and began to shake my head, no. I wasn't going to go through that again...not this way. I had been emitted into the emergency room one time for an overdose and I nearly lost my life...I couldn't do it again. I had to live...I couldn't die until I finally had the touch of her skin. I couldn't risk my life until I had felt the warm embrace of her soft gentle hands. I couldn't end my life...without her. I slowly walked over to a table that sat in the center of my living room. On the table sat two lit candles and two empty plates. That is where I was going to enjoy a fancy, class-filled dinner with her, but it wasn't meant to be. I grabbed the roses off of the dresser and placed them on the table. I then grabbed a picture of her off of the dresser and placed it on the table. I browsed over to the other chair and sat down across from the picture, looking straight into her beautiful eyes, imagining that it was reality and not mere fiction. Why didn’t you come to me? No man will ever love you like I do… Slowly I grabbed the roses once again and readjusted them so that they sat right in front of her picture. My mind wandered, hoping that one day I would not need a picture to see her true beauty...hoping that one day her actual face would replace that picture. But as of right now it was only a mere hope. Nonetheless, I couldn't wait until the moment in which we would embrace each other and realize that fate had brought us together for a purpose, and that purpose shall I fulfill…with her. Dawn Blasky... My Love. I stood over her lifeless body, attacking myself with rapid thoughts of what I had just done. Blood seeped from her forehead, staining the marble flooring and flowing over until it formed a liquid border around my shoes. The woman who I waited for in every bar until I found her was the woman to whom I had just placed a silver bullet, hours ago. The same woman who I sought to marry was the same woman whoms blood I now held on the palm of my hands. That same woman whom I loved, I now ended…All for what? Hatred… Mistrust… Fear… The all so familiar themes seem to be the only logic behind my motives. A Text Message from Mafia Dunn, Sal Francois, led me to believe that she would make an attempt at killing me, but I struck first…maybe even prematurely, because as I stood over her gasping soul, I wondered all of the possibilities that could have prevented such a tragedy. The fact that she may not have gone through with the plan; maybe she rehashed the same love that I held for her all of these years. Or maybe it wasn’t even intended for me? Maybe she was carrying out Francois’s dirty work on some other poor soul. No matter what conclusion I came to, none of them changed the fact that she was dead… And the blood was on my hands. One man knew this all too well. ”What have you done, Sir Olsig?” I jerked around, staring deep into his unrelenting eyes. This was the man who had caused me so much pain. He obviously had this thing planned all along and I played perfectly in the confines of his little plan until I landed this label of a murderer. This man held a mysterious key to my past, and he used it to make me vulnerable. I looked to end his streak right now as I lifted up the same glock in which I ended Dawn with, placing it before his chest with my finger yearning to pull that trigger. ”Don’t do it, Sir Olsig. Think of all the heartache it would cause. First, Dawn here…and then a man as valuable to this place as I am. What would your people think? Even more relevant…what would your father think?” It was as if my ears began to bleed as I heard those words. My father, Czar Olsig, the man whose throne I had taken over. The man who I swore a blood oath to…on his grave, I swore that I would do anything and everything in my power to protect this city of Bogotá, Columbia that he helped build. I swore to use my inheritance of his throne for justice, and not that of evil such as that which stood before me. I broke that promise. I only feared the consequences. ”How do you know these things, Sal… He smiled as he saw my grip on the handle of the gun loosen. Once again, I was sinking into his carefully scouted plan, with no visible way out. This man held my history in the palm of his hands, and it was a more lethal weapon than any other man crafted arsenal on this planet. How he did it, I would probably never know. Why he was doing it…that reason was all too obvious. ”Its simple, Sir Olsig. Your father, Czar, ended the original Francois Regime ran by my father when I was but a boy and you were nothing more than a mere thought in the back of your mother’s head. I learned a lot about your father, and as I watched him bring down my father’s empire, I swore to one day exact revenge. Seems as if I am a little more secure with my promises then you are, Sir. I watched you grow up from a crazed teen into the sovereign that you are now. I know you better than you know yourself.” The smile on his face broaden as I took the time to think about the past. I could not remember ever coming in contact with this man or anyone even close to him. His words forced me to slowly lower the gun in which I would end him with. His words were so…true. People fear the truth because it takes them into a place inside of themselves that they dread. He was taking me to that place and beyond, with no provided escape route except to kill him and thus further shame my fathers name. I couldn’t do it. ”What do you want from me, Sal?” “It’s simple. You have laid waste to your father’s name. You have done the one thing that no man of your position should never do. You have placed this city in danger with the United Nations by doing what you have done, Sir. It is now time that you step down and allow someone more worthy of the throne to take over. Step down, Vangelus…if you truly care for this country, if you truly care for these peoples….if you truly care for your father….Step down.” I looked at him, the fury in my eyes building up at rapid pace. His words were like brisk stabs into both my heart and my conscious. I was being faced with the theory that I should step down as the sovereign of this city in which, for two years, I had long protected. I dropped the gun down to the ground, knowing that I could not give in to the temptation, no matter how great it was. He was stating pure facts, and I would have to face those facts like a honorable man. ”Do it, Vangelus…or I’ll reveal to the entire city this heinous act in which you have committed. You’ll tarnish your father’s legacy if you don’t act upon this, Sir Olsig. It’s fairly simple…Step down.” I looked at him, speechless for the mere fact that there was nothing I could hide behind from the truth in which he spoke of. ”…Get out.” I snarled, turning his broaden smile into a more shameful look. His words weighed heavy on my heart, but he did not know that. Obviously, his main thought was that he would have to go back to the drawing board. He mumbled his final words before exiting… ”Do the right thing, Olsig. If not for this city…for your father.” I simply pointed to the door, signaling for him to make his way out. I had more relevant things to worry about, like how I was going to deal with this body without revealing the fact that I was the culprit behind its death. Sal’s words were like a knife that cut through my heart… Simply because of one thing they held… Truth. The pound of the gravel sent a shockwave up my spine. This place was more devious than I had previously expected. The cold steel handcuffs that tamed my hands were like iron shackles, preventing much movement as I stood before the judge with two officers standing by each of my sides. Sal had turned me in, and now I stood before the judge awaiting my final verdict. ”Case #3832, the city of Bogotá, Columbia versus Sir Vangelus Olsig. Mr. Olsig, how do you plead?” The gathered masses of Bogotá citizens all grew dead silent, awaiting my plea. I took a deep sigh, knowing that this would be one of the hardest things that I would ever have to endure. Nonetheless, I spoke up, stating my plea. ”Guilty, your honor.” The room gasped, following that by immediate murmurs amongst several different parties in the room. The judge banged his gavel, ordering decorum in the room. Three bangs and he finally reached that as my head only lowered in shame. This was what I had become…a man standing before a court judge in fear that his throne would be stripped from him. I remembered Sal’s words and knew there was only one thing I could do. ”How will you complete your status as Sovereign, Sir Olsig?” “I retire from the throne, your honor.” This sent even more elevated murmurs throughout the room. Reporters, shocked by the news and random citizens, some shocked, some ecstatic, some very angry. I felt like Jesus of Nazareth standing before the Israelites, awaiting his fate. I would not be battered with a whip, nor nailed to a cross…but for the rest of my life I would be persecuted for what transpired on this day. My wrestling career would suffer, people would look at me in a different light than before, the Olsig reputation would tarnish by the blood that stained my hands. ”Sir Olsig, are there any recommendations of someone to overtake your throne and be voted on by the people of this city?” I took a deep breath, knowing what the following statement would cause me… ”Yes your honor…Sal Francois.” This time the murmurs turned into loud jabs. This city was very evident of Sal’s faults and to have him lead them was something that they dreaded. I, however, saw no other way for it to go about. ”Order in the Court! Order!” The people drew silent as the Judge made his verdict. ”In Case #3832, the defendant has pleaded guilty and offered to retire from the throne of Sovereign. Sir Olsig, I must say that this is a sad day for the city of Bogotá. You are the culprit of a very serious crime and you have realized that you cannot, under any circumstance, continue under the light. We thank you for your service towards us, but in the same light we find you guilty for the murder of Dawn Blasky. Case Dismissed.” That final pound echoed through my torn heart. It was over. Two years of shaping this city, crumbled under one bullet. Two years of fighting justice and protecting these streets, crumbled under one turned bullet. Two long years… Gone. The needle injected inside of my arm. My conscious was pleading and screaming in the back of my head for me to turn away from this life that I once lived, but as I stood face to face with myself in the mirror…I saw a man who had been broken under the people who he longed protected. A broken soul, catered by the mere fact that no matter what happened, he still had his best friend… Heroin. I smiled as the cold fluid trickled through my veins. No other feeling could compare to this one. The fluid turned me from a typical good guy into a man who had reached such a desperation point that this was his only way out. Nothing else in the world mattered to me right now, for this drug was my only friend. A 6-Pack Turmoil match set for PRIME’s next Pay per view, in which I looked to regain a shot at the Universal Championship by fending off Angelo Deville, Nova, Brandon Youngblood, Ellis Easton, and Aimz…but right now it held no relevance. Right now they were nothing more than distant thoughts. Ferguson… He meant nothing. Nobody, Nothing could tear me away from my current state. The cold fluid took a rush down my arm, making me smile from the enjoyment I was receiving. This was more than an addiction, it had become something I depended on daily. It had become something that I could go no day without, even though the temptation to stop presented itself. I had become a slave of this demon drug… It had become a part of me… And I, a part of it. “How Dare you.” I jumped in fright, immediately yanking the needle out of my arm through pain and tossing it to the ground as I jerked around to look for the person who had spoke those deep toned words that made my body chill. No One. The drug had finally taken its toll on me, and now I had began to hear voices. I laughed, passing it off as nothing more than a side effect. I picked up the needle, and set to place it within my arm once again, but then I heard the all so familiar voice once more. ”You have failed me.” I dropped the needle to the ground once again, this time pushing myself up to my feet even though I felt a bit drowsy from the effects of the drug. I yelled out into the absolute nothing… ”W-Who are you?” ”Vangelus…you know who I am.” That voice…it was so familiar, but I just couldn’t make out whom it belonged to. It was Sal Francois…it wasn’t Roman Esposito…it wasn’t anyone that I knew of recently. It belonged to a man, far gone. ”You have tarnished my throne, and thus tarnished my legacy, Vangelus. You swore a solemn oath to me, Vangelus and you failed me. You shall forever be a victim of my wrath.” “…F-Father?” He appeared right there in front of me. It seem so…surreal. “You have become weak. You are no longer a part of me. Cast yourself, because in giving up our throne to Sal Francois, and basically selling your soul…you have portrayed me as no importance to you.” “STOP IT! ITS NOT TRUE!” “I took Dawn away from you to teach you a lesson. A lesson that would make you grow in the ministry…but you failed, and in doing so you have failed me. Cast yourself, Vangelus…for I know not who you are.” “H-How could you? How could you take her away from me!? Fuck you and your legacy Czar!” “Cast yourself…” “You said you would always be there for Me!” The vision of my father slowly faded as he continued to repeat those words. I ran forward trying to stop him…but he was far gone. “…But you’re not.” I crumbled to my knees, burying my face in the palm of my hands. My life was crashing down all around me…my father had abandoned me…my people had abandoned me. Life’s meaning slowly decreased with every hour. Tears filled with the pain of this world. Nothing was left to live for… Nothing. TBC
