The Brute and The Swift: Part One

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1998 The Brute He stood there, a devilish smirk on his face. The boys around him weren’t smiling. Their faces were littered with looks of fear. One by one they each reached into their pockets and came out with a few crumpled one dollar bills. Gently and slowly they raised their hands and set the little money they had in his hand. After each of the bills was placed in his hand they all scattered, leaving him to count it. “I’m such a dick.” He said with a smirk. At the same time he straightened the bills, counting as he did. His eyes were focused on the task at hand, only slightly moving side to side when somebody passed by. He was missing one of his “customers” today and wasn’t about to let him miss a day. “Thirteen bucks? Damn it’s a slow day.” He said to himself. The five foot ten inch frame held by the boy carried his one hundred seventy-five pounds swiftly as he shoved the money into his pocket and began to walk from his position. School was over for the day and it was time to move his trade outside. It’s where his insurance policies of punches and full nelsons became “legal.” A man in business had to have a way to ensure payment. “He’d better not skip out on me. If he does he’s going to have hell coming to him Monday.” He said to nobody in particular. As he walked, smaller boys scattered from his path, afraid they’d become his next client. He pushed his way outside his eyes began scanning the landscape for that one elusive boy. He stepped off of the school’s front steps and began bumping into and past kids trying to make their way home for the weekend ahead of them. “Hey, douche bag! Where the hell do you think you’re going?” He shouted out to a smaller boy ducking out from behind a couple of people. The brute took chase, shoving people out of his way and making haste towards the grassy field ahead of him. This is where his target was walking swiftly with a book bag tossed over one of his shoulders. This boy was much smaller and much faster at five foot six and one hundred sixty pounds. “Tyler, you little son of a bitch. Don’t you walk away from me.” The bigger man yelled. Tyler as we now know him began to slow his pace, realizing he wasn’t going to escape. He sighed and dropped the bag from his shoulder before turning around to face his foe. It took the bigger man a few seconds to reach him, but when he did it was evident he wasn’t happy. “What’s wrong, Aaron? Did you miss the English assignment or something?” Tyler asked. This only frustrated Aaron more. It began to build up visibly on his face. He wanted to lunge forward at Tyler, but refrained for some reason. Instead he stood there and eyed Tyler’s movements. He stared at the smirk that sat on his face and wished it would disappear. “I’ve never had to kick your ass before. Why make me start today?” Aaron said on the defensive. Tyler stood in place for a moment, placing his hands on his hips. Aaron watched him intently, wondering why his little frame wasn’t on the ground, taking hit after hit. For the first time in a long time, Aaron felt awkward as he pushed around one of his “inferiors.” “I felt fat today and actually ate lunch today. I don’t have any money left.” Tyler said smiling. Aaron began shaking his head slowly, not believing one thing Tyler said. Aaron changed his hakes to nods as he placed his own hands on his hips and looked to the ground, hawking a loogie to the green grass below. “Bullshit.” Aaron said as he looked up. Tyler simply shrugged his shoulders and smiled again. Normally Aaron was able to smell the fear of his customer’s, but Tyler left him confused. For once, the aura escaping into the air wasn’t that of fear, it was one of confidence. He didn’t believe it, telling himself that Tyler was just a good actor. “Sorry, Aaron. Can’t steal from me today.” Tyler said as he reached for his bag. Aaron was finally fed up. He looked at his fists, which had become clenched with anger. He knew what it was time to do, but hesitated once more. His gaze moved from his balls of fury to his prey. He hadn’t stood up from grabbing his bag. The Swift Tyler looked at his bag for an extra second. He knew the first swing was going to come then if it ever was. He took a deep breath and stood up, bag in hand. Just as he had suspected, as he turned he saw Aaron lunge forward. But it was too late, it landed. “Dammit!” Tyler exclaimed. The bag fell from his right hand as he reached for his jaw. Tyler felt the irritation on his face from where the punch landed. It was warm and tender. Before he could completely regain his senses, he saw another right hand flying towards him. He couldn’t cover up, it landed on his nose. “Bitch.” Aaron muttered. The words flowed into Tyler’s ears as he fell to the ground. He didn’t even have to reach up to his new wound to know it was bleeding. The faint smell of iron filled his nostrils. He looked up to his attacker, his vision blurred by the water flowing from his eyes. But through the liquid he saw Aaron’s frame moving towards him slowly. “That’s enough, man. I got it.” Tyler said through the pain. Tyler saw his foe cock his head sideways in disbelief. He began to crawl on his hands and knees, trying to move away from the inevitable third blow. As he did he could feel the throbbing begin in his face. Tyler was sure another blow to the face would knock him out, he had to do something. “I’ll say when it’s enough.” Aaron snorted. Before Tyler could make a move, a kick landed to his kidney. “That was a kick, compliments of Aaron Andrews.” Aaron spouted. Tyler saw his chance. The arrogant prick kicking his ass stood back to admire the blood pouring from Tyler’s nose. Tyler pushed himself to his feet and readied himself for another blow. When he turned to face Aaron on spaghetti legs, he saw it coming and ducked. He knew he had to think quick and turned to one thing he loved. As Aaron turned back to face his prey, Tyler kicked his right foot into the air, connecting with the nose and jaw of the bigger man, knocking him down. “Superkick, compliment of Shawn Michaels.” Tyler said. Quickly he grabbed his bag and tossed it over his shoulder, wiping some of the blood onto his shirt. He looked at the fallen bully for a second, spotting a trickle of blood fall from his nose. Tyler smirked a bit before turning and running off, happy with his work. “This isn’t over, Tyler!” Aaron yelled, turning over to a knee.
