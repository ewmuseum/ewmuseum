A Bandersnatch Not Involving Code Veronica

13 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 13th, 2007 There was this diner not far from Interstate 55, a sort of a country diner where the average person could be assured that these people probably killed their own livestock to serve as their meat. It was also the sort of place that you could be assured that 85% of its patrons wore camoflauge and were likely packing some serious, deer-killing heat. We're gonna go out on a limb and call it "Katie's". It was this diner that a certain bus carrying nine passengers pulled up into the parking lot. This wasn't your typical bus, for it was blue and had the name "BLUE ROGUES" written on both sides. That's right. The Blue Rogues... were taking over. Their evil plot was to take up different spaces in the restaurant. That's right, they were sitting at different points in order to discuss their own evil plots. What gave them an advantage in their grand takeover plot of some dumb country diner in some bumblefuck town in Missouri was the severe lack of people inside Katie's at the time. In fact, it was regulated to some crazy old guy calling himself "The Bandersnatch" who wore camoflague and spoke of the golden days when a man didn't need a gun to take down a deer, but instead dressed half-naked and wrestled the deer to the ground in order to place such painful submission holds on it that it literally wanted to die. We will, thankfully, not be talking about The Bandersnatch. Instead, the attention shall focus on the Blue Rogues, who have split into three units placed throughout the restaurant. The first unit consisted of Coral Avalon and Annabelle Summers. Coral's hair kinda stood up in places, he wore a wrinkled gray shirt and jeans, and he looked like he just wanted to curl up and die. Coral's disshelved appearance could lead one to believe that he had been asleep in the bus prior to the stop. Annabelle, by contrast, was far more groomed for a girl who wore her hair in long pigtails, and wore a short-sleeved white shirt and a flowy purple skirt. Everyone's favorite couple (hai Mat) simply sat together in something resembling bliss. Two years together, and they still hadn't had much of a problem. The second unit consisted of Alexandria Malone and, much to Alex's annoyance, the three members of Mega Job. Alexandria was wearing a long-sleeved black shirt, only the sleeves were seethrough, and a pair of jeans that would've helped give attention to her butt were she not seated. Janito was in his usual snazzy suit, but that's mainly because it is literally the only outfit he has now, and it has endured some wrinkles... much to Beef's annoyance. Likewise, Beef was in the only outfit HE had, which was his director's outfit that consists of a beret, a white shirt, and baggy black pants. Steve, however, could afford several outfits and he was in a white tanktop and black pants. To tell the truth, Alex would've sat with Coral and Annabelle, but she knew that she'd just be a third wheel. Likewise, she would've sat with the third unit, but the Codemaster explicitly told her that he needed to talk business with the Falk Brothers. She got stuck with Mega Job primarilly because Beef and Janito both had a crush on her, and Steve just hangs out with them to keep them in check. The third unit consisted of the Codemaster and the brothers Falk. The Codemaster, in his common white T-shirt with a super mushroom in the center and an arrow pointed towards his crotch and black jeans, seemed like he was formulating a plot. Robert was in a blue and black T-shirt and a pair of jeans. Scott, meanwhile, wore an old black T-shirt from the ACW days that read "BADDEST MOTHERFALKER ON THE PLANET" on the front, and a pair of khakis. The Codemaster had asked both Falks to join him, away from the other Rogues, to "discuss business". However, with the Codemaster, "business" didn't always mean finances. All three units were situated far enough apart that they were outside of earshot from one another unless yelling began. "Though I will come to regret it later on in life," the Codemaster said to the two Falk Brothers, "I have come to let Scott Falk into my web of lies and entanglement." "Speak English, shitstick." Scott snapped. "He's got a crush on Annabelle." Robert said, fairly simply. "Oh." Scott said. He thought about it for a few seconds, before he realized something, "Wait. You mean Pigtail Girl? What the fuck?" "I know," Robert said, before grunted, "It's madness." "Madness?" the Codemaster asked, pulling a sly grin. He then stood up with his palms on the table, and proclaimed into the heavens. "THIS... IS..." *.* "...Caketown!" Beef said with a smile. "Um, Caketown?" Alexandria asked. "Yeah. That is TOTALLY the town I'd have my honeymoon in if, in fact, the planets align and I somehow find a girl who's interested in a guy who calls his penis the 'Mean Beef Machine' and generally bullies Mexicans." Beef said. "I've... never heard of a Caketown before?" Alexandria said. "Well, clearly, it is a town that is literally made out of perpetually fresh cake. No matter how much you eat, more appears! It's a wonderous wonderland of wonderment and wonder." Beef said, with a wide smile on his face. "Beef, I've been meaning to tell you this for a while now, but Caketown doesn't really exist like that." El Janito said with a low tone. "What?" Beef asked, as if the bubble that contained his dream just popped and sent his dream hurling into the abyss at speeds in excess of Mach 3. "Well, if Caketown was made of cake, surely it would receive daily assaults from nearby armies of ants, consuming everything in their wake. Also, I would imagine that everyone would be working as a baker of some type, or else the cake wouldn't stay fresh all the time. What do they do with the cake that goes stale?" Janito asked. "I... I don't know..." "ETHIOPIA." Steve suggested. "No, I don't think so." Janito said, "If that were the case, we wouldn't have to hear anything about hungry Ethiopians." Alexandria wanted desperately to just get away from these freaks. "You know, if common sense were food, you guys would be..." *.* "...starved." Annabelle said, sulking. "I wish they'd just hurry up and get our food already." Coral mumbled to himself. "I know..." Annabelle said. In truth, our young brunette hadn't eaten since they had left, and being that she was one of those annoying types that didn't gain weight from eating a lot, the fact that she was hungry was really unsurprising. Coral looked at Annabelle, and sighed, "You think I got in over my head?" Annabelle perked up, and tried to figure out what Coral was getting at before she asked, "What do you mean?" "With PRIME. I think I got in way over my head." Coral said, taking a glance at the Codemaster, "For starters, I never know what that guy's thinking. And then there's the fact that a guy got shot at our pay-per-view. I mean, since when do people get shot at pay-per-views? You know, other than IWO. And fWo. ...Or basically any promotion where you have to check your reality and sanity at the door." "Ummm..." Annabelle tried to say, slightly confused. "I've lost you, didn't I?" Coral asked of his girlfriend. "Just a bit... Are you sure it wasn't just a blank?" Annabelle asked. "You know, he fell over. Blood seemed a bit bright, though. Maybe it was a ketchup packet. Or, you know, one of those paint blood bullets. You know, you shoot a guy and then there's a blast of red paint." Coral said. He simulated the sort of effect this would've had by clinching his fist and then expanding all five fingers, completed with a "whoosh" sound. Annabelle blinked at her boyfriend, "That seems kinda..." * . * "Ridiculous!" Scott Falk exclaimed, pointing at Codemaster, "You're ridiculuos!" Scott then pointed at Robert Falk, "You're ridiculous, too! What the fuck? I thought you had god damn common sense, Allen! But this... this is just... fucked up beyond all recognition!" The Codemaster took a panicked glance towards the area that Coral and Annabelle were sitting, before he put his hand in Scott's face, "Shut up, Scott." "Fine." "But yes, I am as serious as a Level 50 Frank West in a mall full of zombies." the Codemaster said, "Annabelle's the one for me." Scott glanced at Annabelle, who was smiling and laughing at something Coral had said, before he turned to see Alexandria sitting there with Mega Job (and growing angry at that sight), before he next turned to Codemaster, "I fucking thought Alexandria was the one for you, you fucker." "Alexandria?" the Codemaster asked, before he got this bemused smirk on his face, "Sure, she's hotter than the Golden Sun, but why would I seek out that which you rejoined the Blue Rogues for?" "Er..." Scott started to say, he even had a finger held up as if he really has a retort, but he didn't have one, so he muttered, "Good point." "Besides," the Codemaster said, "That girl got Coral, the dullest person I ever met, into crazy things. The DS. Fighting games that ain't Soul Calibur. One Piece in its original, Japanese form. Alexandria doesn't even know what any of that stuff is." "Better that way," Robert said, with a cross of his massive arms, "That type of stuff has a tendency to rot your brain." "Ain't that the fuckin' truth." Scott said, as both he and his brother took a glare at their leader, the man who called himself the "Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom". "Shut up, or I will perform a Link-like Fire Rod Drive By on you freaks." the Codemaster said. Robert looked down at Scott and Scott looked up at Robert, before both men turned slowly towards the Codemaster. "That's better. Now then, I wish to discuss my plan, so listen carefully, and I'll..." *.* "...freaking kill you." Alexandria said, as she held Beef's arm in a bone-breaking hold with a glance of death directed at him. "OW!" Beef shouted, not even caring about the stares (even from the people outside of earshot, like the other two units of the Blue Rogues, and even The Bandersnatch), "What did *I* do!?" "Your hand was this close from touching my breasts." Alexandria said, with a glare, "Only I get to touch those." "But they seem like unplucked melons, waiting to be harvested by those such as IOW!" Beef said, before Alexandria twisted Beef's arm in such a way that she could easily break it, "OKAY! OKAY! I WON'T TRY ANY MORE!" Alexandria let go. "...not today, anyway..." Beef whimpered to himself. Alexandria slapped him in the back of the head. "OW!" "Man, the physical abuse budget in this roleplay is extraordinarilly high today." El Janito observed. "INDEED." Steve said. "Roleplay?" Alexandria said. Janito and Steve looked at one another, before Janito panicked. "AHHHH! I BROKE THE FOURTH WALL AGAIN! EMERGENCY! EMERGENCY! WOMEN AND JANITOS FIRST! WE'RE ALL DOOMED! NOBODY IS..." *.* "...safe? You want me to be... safe?" Annabelle asked of Coral Avalon. Coral scratched his head, "Well, I've been thinking about this a lot lately. We've kinda been travelling together for two years and we never really had a permanent residence." "No... not unless you count that building that Mr. Heimdal owns..." Annabelle said. "Right," Coral said, "But... I was thinking, what with the shooting and all that, that... we should probably settle down. Like we did in Japan, only more permanently." "Ummm... but I wouldn't be able to see you very often if I settled down, since you still travel to wrestle." Annabelle said, sulking, "I don't think I'd like that very much." Coral blinked, and took one of Annabelle's hands in his own (meanwhile, on the other side of the diner, an observant Codemaster could be seen wincing), "You don't get tired of the travel?" "W-well... it's hard, sure." Annabelle said, before she smiled ever-so-slightly, "But, not to get all cliche on you, but as long as you're with me, I feel I could probably endure anything." "Even the cliches?" Coral asked. "Especially the cliches." Annabelle responded. They smiled to one another. * . * "Look at them," Scott said, glaring at Coral and Annabelle, "They're all smiling and happy and shit. Makes me kinda sick. You SURE you want to jump into that?" "Sure as Sylvarant, buddy." the Codemaster said wirh a smirk, "If I'm there when Coral's not, then she'll become attached to me as well. And when the time is right, when she's attached to the both of us... I'll find a way to make Coral look bad. When that happens... bam. She'll be on me like white on a Gardevoir." The Codemaster continued smirking, while the Falks just looked at each other like they both thought the man was completely loco. "You know, she's white." Scott said. "That don't matter." the Codemaster replied. "What's going to happen to your partnership with Coral when your insane plan actually works?" Robert asked. "I imagine it'll be dissolved." the Codemaster said, quite bluntly. The Falks, yet again, look at each other in puzzlement. "So, you don't care if the team is dissolved? At all?" Robert asked. The Codemaster laughed a laugh that could be considered evil laughter. "Coral's just a means to an end, gentlemen. Part of a bigger plan of mine. I needed him `cause PRIME wouldn't have taken me just by my lonesome. And if I'm gonna be forced into lettin' him close to my inner circle... that being you guys... then I want somethin' from him. Simple as that." the Codemaster said. Robert Falk reflected that the only person who knew the "real" Allen Brown was a woman by the name of Leigh Landers. Robert, himself, had known Allen for years, but had never seen such a malicious side to him before. The look on his brother's face suggested that he, too, had never seen this side of Allen before. The Codemaster looked at the brothers with this dark look on his face, "You guys are with me, right?" Scott didn't want to argue, "S-sure boss... whatever you say." If Scott was in... Robert was as well, "Yeah..." The Codemaster's mood suddenly lightened up, "Yeah. That's what I thought." He clapped his hands together and shouted towards the people running "Katie's". "WHERE IN THE NAME OF KRATOS' UNBRIDLED FURY IS MY DAMN CHICKEN!?" the Codemaster shouted.
