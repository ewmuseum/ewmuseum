The Brute and The Swift: Part Four

undated

Date: 1998 - Present day Aaron walked down the street, a plain black hoodie covering his torso. The crisp May morning air nipped at his nose as he moved towards his destination. It wasn’t normal for Aaron to be up this early on a Saturday unless he had detention; and he hadn’t done anything to warrant that during the past week. He had laid low, not taking part in his usual business transactions. “I can’t believe I’m doing this.” Aaron muttered to himself. He continued to move his frame along the sidewalk at a brisk pace as the thoughts of his actions raced through his mind. All along he knew he had been a grade A dick, but he never thought he’d regret it. Now he found himself walking toward a house he never thought he’d set foot in. He didn’t know if he would, but he knew he needed to. “Here goes nothing.” He muttered aloud. His movement had stopped in front of a modest sized baby blue house. He followed the sidewalk that led to the porch with his eyes; his gaze went to the stairs and then to the front door. He studied the porch for a moment as he fumbled with his pride. He made numerous attempts to walk up the stairs with his feet like he had done with his eyes. But each time one foot stepped forward, it snapped backwards. Again his eyes gazed upon the furniture that occupied the quaint entrance in front of him. To the right was a two seat glider that had just been brought back out from winter storage. To the left sat a couple of packages left by the UPS man earlier that morning. “Maybe he’s not home.” Aaron said to himself. He wanted to find a reason to not go through with this; yet at the same time he still wanted to say what needed to be said. He shuffled his feet in place for a few more seconds before sighing and shaking his head. In one swift movement he walked forward and up the five steps onto the porch. He extended his right hand and rang the doorbell. Aaron knew one thing for sure; he didn’t want one of his parents to answer the door. Aaron waited for a few seconds in the warming air with no answer. “Dammit.” He said. Aaron threw his hands into the air and looked to his feet as he waited an extra second longer. He sighed and reached up to ring the doorbell again. He heard it ring inside the home as he looked to his feet again. Finally, feeling confident nobody was home he turned to walk back down the steps. “Aaron?” A voice asked from behind. Aaron turned around to see Tyler standing at the front door. This is what he had wanted, but wasn’t exactly sure what to say. He slowly walked back to the door as Tyler stepped out and shut the entrance behind him. “Hey Tyler; I need to talk to you.” Aaron said. Tyler looked a little suspicious as he looked at the guy who had beaten him up numerous times before. He had reason to be weary. Tyler was sure this was a low profile payback from his attack just six days prior. Images of Tyler on Aaron’s back with his arms wrapped around the throat raced as Aaron stepped closer. Finally the two were only two feet apart, bringing a slight flinch from Tyler. Aaron chuckled and held up his hands to say “chill out.” “Are you here to kick my ass?” Tyler asked as he let the tension escape his body. Aaron shook his head side to side putting Tyler at ease even more. Now he stood there more confused than ever. If he wasn’t there you lay a beat down, then why? Tyler walked to his left and took a seat on the glider. Aaron followed him over and leaned on the railing across from him. “I’m not here to beat you up. I’m here to apologize and thank you.” Aaron said. Tyler’s eyes went wide and his forehead folded in further confusion. Aaron cracked a small smile and looked away for a moment. “You’re serious?” Tyler asked. Aaron turned back and nodded. “Completely. What you’ve done to me over the past week or so has shown me how much of a dick I really am.” Aaron stated. Tyler couldn’t help but nod in agreement. “And I’m sorry for beating you up all those times. You gave me what I really needed; a taste of my own medicine.” Aaron stated. He reached to his nose and slightly pushed it, wincing in pain as he did. Tyler hadn’t succeeded in breaking it with the kick to the face, but had moved it aside a little for sure. A moment of uneasy quiet came over the boys as they continued to sit. “You understand I wouldn’t ever pick a fight with you, right Aaron? I was just defending myself.” Tyler asked. Aaron nodded in agreement after the comment. The two looked to each other for a moment. Aaron locked eyes on Tyler’s shirt. Simple white text on black fabric that read “Austin 3:16”. Aaron smiled and pointed to it. “WWF right? Is that where you learned all that stuff you did to me?” Aaron asked. Tyler looked down at his shirt and laughed aloud. He nodded as he looked back up before covering his face in embarrassment. Only his other wrestling obsessed friends knew he liked the “sport.” “Yeah, you probably think I’m a big dork, don’t you?” He said. Aaron laughed as he stood up straight and looked to his feet. The air had warmed up since he began his trek to Tyler’s house, rendering his hoodie un-needed. “No, I don’t think you’re a dork. In fact, I was surprised when you locked the katahajime on me.” Aaron said as he began to take off his hoodie. Tyler looked up, stunned at the knowledge that Aaron had shown. How did he know what that was called? His answer was soon visible as Aaron dropped the black hoodie to the side and revealed his own t-shirt. It had a picture of Peter Senerchia, better known as Taz, in his orange singlet. Tyler smiled at the sight. “An ECW fan? That’s awesome man.” Tyler said. The tension that Tyler had felt was completely gone now; the two men who just two weeks before had nothing in common, held the same primary interest. “I’ve been watching wrestling all my life. The superkick, the STO and the katahajime all just astounded me. I’ve never known anyone as into it as I am. I couldn’t let my dickish ways get in the way of it now.” Aaron said. Tyler nodded and stood. “I’ve got last week’s Over the Edge pay per view on tape. You want to come in and watch it?” Tyler offered. Aaron nodded and grabbed his hoodie before following his newfound friend through the front door. Fade… “And that’s how we became a tag team.” Aaron said. Tyler and Aaron sat at a small table inside a bar with a few unrecognized people around. One could only assume they were fellow wrestlers. The two both had numerous bottles near their place at the setting. “The best fucking tag team in the world.” Tyler stuttered. He extended his hand out and Aaron slapped in high five fashion. The two laughed for a moment before finishing their Miller Genuine Drafts. Aaron belched before looking to his partner and laying an arm on his shoulder. “So, we’ve got this bitch Lindsey Tyler and some douche bag named after a time of day this week.” Aaron said. Tyler giggled. “How do you know Lindsey’s a bitch? We’ve never even talked to her. She’s kind of hot dude.” Tyler responded. Aaron was turned around, calling for more beer. When he turned back he looked at Tyler. “Well, she’s a woman so she must be a bitch. Haven’t I taught you anything?” Aaron asked. The two shared another laugh as a waitress appeared with their drinks. She had heard their comment and didn’t seem too pleased. At least now she had a reason to walk away immediately before having to endure another round of lame pick up lines. “I don’t think you’re going to get her in bed tonight, Aaron. She seems pissed.” Tyler said. Aaron turned and watched the young woman walk away. His focus was on her tight jeans, distinguishing what could only be called the perfect ass. “She’s playing hard to get.” Aaron said, interrupted with another belch. Tyler nodded in agreement. “So, you think we can take them?” Tyler asked as he took a sip of his beer. Aaron swallowed the alcohol he had in his mouth and scoffed. “Of course. What’s going to be hard about beating a woman and the synonym for as the son goes down?” Aaron replied. Tyler belched his own massive sound before standing and finishing off his beer. “Man, let’s get out of here. This place sucks. You’re never picking the bar again.” Tyler said. Aaron nodded and followed suit. His face broke into a smile as they walked away. “We got wasted didn’t we?” He said with a laugh. Tyler agreed as they pushed towards the door and into the frigid Pennsylvania air.
