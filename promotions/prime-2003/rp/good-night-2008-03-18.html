Good Night

18 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 18th, 2008 “I don't wanna say goodbye to you…so I'll just say good night to you” - Kanye West’s Good Night (Mos Def) "So...," his voice trails away, quivering from the mere sight of it all, and unequivocally shaken by the entire experience. The roses remain in his hand, full and alive, even as inches of snow lay at his feet. Slowly, he kneels down, stretching out his arm until the flowers rest oddly against the tombstone. Tears flood his swollen eyes, the pain constant throughout his entire body, partly from the wear and tear of the business he's in, and the rest from the heartache he feels as he stands before the tombstone. "I couldn't do it," Craig speaks this time, a little more weight in his voice, but much more intensity in his mind. Days have passed since what many were claiming as a remarkable weekend for the storied veteran. Critics and fans alike were speaking about his Intense Title run and his admirable and gutsy performance in the Dual Halo. Sure, he had picked up a victory on Night One, but on Night Two he had fallen short of his goal. Fifth place wasn't first in his mind, regardless of how others perceived everything. “And for that... I owe you an apology. You gave me so much, you taught me how to succeed in that ring, and I've once again just come up short. I haven't been able to live up to the lofty dreams you had for me, and it's with that that I continue to disrespect and sully your name. I'm so sorry, Frank, so very sorry.” The remorse in his heart is unquantifiable. So much rage is pouring out of him, coming so close, yet still so far away from his ultimate dream, his goals and desires. As he stands back up, the snow creeping into his boots, he grimaces as he can feel the torn muscles and ligaments around his shoulder. “My stupid shoulder, it's been unbearable the past few days. I talked to the doctor and he told me that I need to have surgery on it. Yet, when I talk to him about the surgery I can see the fear in his eyes. The fear that I'm going to die on that table. This disease, it's becoming worse and worse. I can't even tell you the hours after the Halo. It's all a blur to me. The doctor had to take me to my hotel room and pump me full of drugs just so I didn't crash and die. Is that what my life has become? A never-ending cycle of competition and near death? “ His right hand massages his shoulder, hoping to calm it down, but it just roars back with such ferocity. Craig closes his eyes, fighting with every fiber left in his body to block out the pain. It takes a while, a skill that Craig learned young in his life, but eventually he's able to soothe it so he can focus once again. “For so long, I was ridiculed for not coming to your funeral. If they could see me now, I think they would understand why it's so difficult looking at you. I'd hoped to visit you and be sharing my success story with you, but instead, you get to about another failure. It's what my life is all about, isn't it Frank? Failure after failure? I know others wouldn't say that, but you and I know the truth, don't we Frank? Failure. So, I guess now, the real question is how do you turn a failure into success? Is that even remotely possible? I guess it's not, but it's what I do Frank. I do the improbable and the impossible. It's what I do. You taught me that, remember Frank? You sat me down on your lap, my forehead soaked with sweat, and a musty smell coming from you that I'll never forget, no matter how hard I try. You told me that sometimes, you'll have to do the impossible, and I never understood how that was possible. I guess now... now... I'll have to learn how to do that. “ His head bounces for a second, his eyes closed, as he tries to pull himself together. So close. So far. So far, no cigar. A constant struggle, a constant reminder, a constant feeling. Finally, he opens his eyes and looks at the tombstone, the tombstone that's been haunting his dreams for the past fifteen years. Staring at it now, he feels a sense of pride and strength that felt like left him so long ago. “On top of everything else, I miss her, Frank. I miss Nicole. She just… she just fit, ya know? It’s weird I guess. True love for me has always been at my fingertips, but I’ve never been able to fully grasp it. Have I lost it this time? I hope not, Frank. I really hope not.” Craig looks around him, oblivious to the world around him, and yet, rattled at the very same time. With the way that he holds his body it’s obvious that this isn’t where he wants to be. He glares at the tombstone, his mind on so many things at the same time. “They say a funeral is a chance to say goodbye. I'm not ready to say goodbye Frank. So... I guess this is good night.” He rolls his tongue around in his mouth for a second, a chill rushing through his body as the wind whips up once again around him. Craig places his right palm onto the tombstone and nods his head once again. Good night. End. Any lyrics found in this piece can be attributed to Kanye West's Good Night.
