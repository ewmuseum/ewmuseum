Absolute Power

29 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29/01/2007 Last week, Silvio Degrassey was a relatively happy man. Sure, both his wife and his mistress had both threatened to cut his balls off and feed them to him during two different domestic arguments, but that was to be only to be expected. It was a weekly routine he underwent with both of them and although he would never admit it, he secretly enjoyed it. He liked the passion, the anger and more importantly, the make-up sex. Then, three days ago he’d followed orders received from his squad leader, Rune Wallasey, and was abruptly conscripted into a task force assigned to capture a rogue associate of their employer, Cornelius Stakiri. Now, hanging upside down over the edge of a thirty-storey building, held up only by his ankles, he wished he never accepted the task in the first place. His eyes, bloodshot and wild, darted upward toward the perpetrator of his current misery. Stakiri’s rogue agent and laboratory pet, Jonathan Winters. “N-no man, don’t.” Winters was leant over the edge of the building, Degrassey’s ankles in each hand. Degrassey dared not look into his cold, dead, eyes a second time. Winters sneered at him remorselessly, his merciful please unheeded, and loosened his grip around his right ankle. Degrassey let out a weak yelp and Winters nostrils filled with the sickening stench of urine. The odour overpowered him and Winters was forced to look away. “You know why I’m here,” Winters told him. He’d been at this now for ten minutes already and these were his first words; up until that point, words had been irrelevant. “So tell me.” “I-I-can’t!” Degrassey whimpered, fighting the urge to look down. “He’ll kill me!” “Perhaps.” Winters allowed his right ankle to slip out of his hand completely and Degrassey began to sob, desperately. “But what do you think I’ll do to you, if you don’t tell me?” “Ohgodohgodohgodohgodohgod...” Degrassey muttered, his mind now firmly fixed on the possibility of becoming street pizza. Winters grinned. He had him. “Decide.” “N-no, don’t,” Degrassey feebly pleaded. “I’ll talk.” Winters retook Degrassey’s ankle and held it firmly. “Go on.” Degrassey glared upwards at Winters, his face engraved with confusion. “W-wh-what? No! Drag me up first!” “No,” Winters told him. He lowered Degrassey lower, pointedly. “Tell me where Rune Wallasey is. Now.” “M-Ma-Man-Manhatton,” he spluttered. “Stakiri’s safe house on thirty-fifth street. Now let me up, will ya?” Winters hauled the weighty man’s limp body up and over the two foot protective wall that diligently guarded the building top and dropped Degrassey on it’s edge. Upon doing so, Winters grabbed Degrassey by the hair and yanked his head backward. “D-Do-Don’t kill me, please! I had to do it!” Winters leaned closer. “I ever see you again, you die,” he whispered. He drove Degrassey’s head forward with force, smashing his face into the side of sturdy brick wall without regret. Winters then stood, rearranged his knee-long, black business coat and then rewound his expensive dark grey scarf for a comfortable fit. Now the real fun could begin. *** Sat in complete darkness, Winters couldn’t help but conjure the picture of Degrassey’s fear-ridden face and joyfully reminisced about how good it made him feel to have that amount of power over another. Soon, he will experience that same sensation and he had to admit, it excited him. I wonder whether this is how it feels to have the world at your feet? He idly considered, impatiently checking his watch for the second time in as many minutes. Is this how it feels to have absolute power? Is this how it feels to be Angelo Deville? Winters grinned and chuckled to himself. He wondered how long it would take for his mind to churn up that name again. He’d received an e-mail from PRIME’s administration team that morning to confirm booking for this weeks edition of Revolution and it’d been playing on his mind ever since. Angelo-fucking-Deville. The man who’d managed to effectively put Winters career on standby, eliminating him from PRIME’s coveted Jewel In the Crown tournament last year. The incident had played upon Winters ever since and he was man enough to admit, the wounds were still fresh. Not this time, you fucker! He silently vowed. This time will be different. This time I won’t be preoccupied... The lock of the apartment door clicked open and the clink of keys, made Winters sit up in his seat expectantly. This is it, he thought. Game time... *** Rune Wallasey shot a pleasant grin toward the pretty blonde fitness instructor from two doors over and was almost surprised the smile was reciprocated. Stop thinking with your dick, Wallasey! He quickly checked himself, going about his business. You’re supposed to be laying low ya dumb shit! With one hand firmly supporting a brown paper grocery bag, Wallasey fished inside the pocket of his pants with his free hand, promptly extracting his door keys. The key slid into the lock smoothly and with one revolution, the door was open. He entered the apartment and frowned. I left a light on, didn’t I? He stood there for the longest time. Fuck! This is it! They’ve come for me! I-- He paused, vaguely recollecting flicking the lights off upon leaving about half an hour ago. He shook his head and flicked the switch again. “You stupid fuck, Wallasey!” He muttered to himself, resting his grocery’s upon the kitchen counter. “You’re going to give yourself a heart attack!” “...And we wouldn’t want that, would we?” Wallasey’s heart stopped and his pulse raised. Oh Fuck, oh fuck, oh fuck, OH FUCK! He didn’t even have to turn around to know who it was. The voice told him that much and he knew Jonathan Winters motive was perfectly transparent. He rushed toward his kitchen draw and aggressively yanked it open. “If you’re looking for your piece, you’re not going to find it in your draw.” Fuck! Wallasey turned to face Winters. He was sat upon the worn, reclining couch just over in the next room, legs crossed and wide grin firmly spread across his face. “I know why you’re here, Winters,” Wallasey told him, unable to hide the fear in his voice. “And I know what you intend to do, but you have to know: everything is not how it seems!” Winters brow furrowed. “Explain.” “She told us you’d gone rogue. She said that somebody had gotten to you and messed with your head,” He feebly pleased. “She said you’d been turned. We didn’t realise we’d been lied to, until later. That bitch used us and we had no reason to doubt--” “Who?” Winters abruptly cut short. Wallasey allowed a long, nervous sigh. “Aeryn.” Winters eyes widened and anger flared up from deep within. He rose from the armchair and drew closer. Wallasey flinched and glanced toward the door. Can I make it in time? Can I--? “Enough lies!” Winters warned, lurching forward at phenomenal speed, clamping his right hand down firmly upon his throat. “Tell me who really sent you, Wallasey!” “It was Aeryn,” he choked and spluttered. “It was Aeryn, okay?” Winters swallowed hard. Aeryn was the only woman who he’d ever trusted, the only woman he’d ever loved. She can’t be involved in this, he silently questioned. Can she? “Fuck!”
