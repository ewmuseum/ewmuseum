Date: ReV 174 Jesus Chris he had to piss. Tyler Rayne’s eyes felt abnormally heavy. Weighted by the unseen force of sleep and dreams. It was an effort to raise the lids and open the whites to a dull red glow. He blinks in the night, unconsciously scratching at the back of his head. He needed a shower. The bright red letters of the digital clock gave off far more light than seemed necessary. Maybe he was just hung-over. A soft moan from beside him tore bloodshot eyes from the alarm clock. Lindsay Troy, sleeping (almost) quietly, snuggled up tight against the hotel room pillow. Her skin still glistened with a sheen of sweat from the night’s earlier workout. Her hair draped in damp clumps upon the pillow. Why was everything so goram red? The bathroom was cold, despite the layer of fog that covered the mirror. A very long mirror, at that. He didn’t remember the mirror being so long. Or taking a shower, for that matter. He knew Lindsay hadn’t taken one… “How much did I have to drink?” Quite a bit it would seem. He stood at the toilet for long enough to realize… “This is a long fucking piss.” …before the stream finally subsided. He stumbled groggily to the sink and stuck his hands under the faucet. The water was warm. Steam rose from the sink, adding to the thickness of the mist upon the mirror. Such a long mirror. Tyler’s eyes drifted down from the mirror, following the rising steam to its source. The water collecting within the basin was a familiar tinge of pink. He squinted and looked at his blood-soaked hands. “The fuck…” He proceeded to wash them thoroughly (just to make Jules proud) before drying them on the soft white hotel towel (see, Vincent, no stains). The bathroom had seemed so much bigger than before, but now that he was leaving… it was only a step and a half from the sink to the door. His toes flexed against the touch of carpet, balling in response to a long forgotten joke. He pulled the sheet back from the bed, pausing to admire the long frame of a woman now exposed. Her back was marred with scars he’d never have expected. The black ink of tattoos quietly juxtaposed with the forever-pinked reminders of thumbtacks and barbed wire. More alike than either had anticipated. Or would have likely been willing to admit. He slid into the bed beside her. Could not resist the urge to touch her. Feel her. His finger traced down the curve of her arm. His lips pressed lightly against her shoulder blade. She stirred and rolled to face him, a smile stretching across pink lips. Without a word she pressed those lips to his own. So it begins. He rolled the Queen onto her back, pressing tighter against her. Against her lips. Against her thighs. His hands wandered across her body. He had to feel her. She had to feel him. Her lips parted in a shocked but silent cry. He pressed against her. Into her. He closed his eyes and fell into her sweet embrace. The faintest hint of perfume upon her shoulders. The bittersweet taste of sweat and desire on her neck. Their lips found purchase again. One against the other. Him against her. He pulls away, looking for more leverage. She moans beneath him, red hair bouncing across her face. “I’ve wanted you for so fucking long.” Hands pressed firmly against his chest begin to curl. Red fingernails digging ever slightly into flesh. Angelica Brooks is surprisingly flexible. She slides her legs from beneath him. Carefully, so as not to disturb the rhythm. They find new purchase around his waist. Her thighs pressed firmly against him. The young little interviewess shoves him away, though her thighs (among other bits of anatomy) cling tightly to him. He falls to his back and she swings along with, now taking the dominant position atop him. Angelica Brooks doing what she’s wanted to do since the first time she laid eyes on him. Every fucking inch of him. His hands slide smoothly up her thighs. Above the waist. Moving higher still. He cannot help himself. Cannot contain his desire. He has to taste her. He sits up. His hands glide up her back. She’s so small. So thin. He can feel her spine beneath his fingers. The broken, jagged fragments of her spine. Pieces of bone that jut from the skin at odd angles. Neither of them stops. Neither of them wants to. She tastes like fresh picked strawberries. He cannot pull his lips away. Hands move across her wet back. Warmth beneath his fingertips. Not the cool, slick sheen of sweat. A thick, warm substance that oozes between his fingers. Her own fingers dig into his back. Tearing at the flesh. Poking at his spine. She throws herself back and then forward in ecstasy. Her head lowers toward his shoulder. Lips nibble at his ear. “You couldn’t protect me.” Blood. His hands soaked in blood. “You can’t protect anyone.” Fingers through his hair. Digging. Holding. Tugging. She wrenches his face away from her body. Shoves him back down to the bed. Her tongue glides seductively across black lipstick. Her nails dig deep into his shoulders. Katterina Wylde, pressing dominantly down upon him. “You’re such a fucking bitch.” She smacks him. He tastes blood at the corner of his lip. His retaliation is equally as strong. She loses her balance. Falls to the bed. She tries to crawl away but he pounces upon her. She rolls him over. He rolls her. She turns to crawl away and he presses a forearm to her back. Face down in the thick comforter of the hotel bed. He kisses her between the shoulder blades. Her neck cranes as black lips reach around to meet his. He tastes blood. Both hers and his own. The night continues. She speaks to him between pleasured moans and labored breaths. “You did this to them, you know? The Japanese girl. Brooks. Lin. All of them. You brought this on them. Sooner or later, you’ll do the same to me. And to Troy. All of us will be gone. You’ll be left alone. Just poor ol’ Tyler Rayne. Not a fuck in the world…” He responds in the only way he knows how. Harder. Better. Faster. Stronger. The wall-mounted television blinks to life. A bizarre scene displayed before them. Bryan Dawkins and Chandler Tsonda both dressed in cheap suits. They’re sitting at a news desk, or something that passes for once. Each has a microphone extended in their direction. Dawkins is sipping coconut juice through a straw. “I’ll tell you something right now, bruh… that’s how you take it to the hole.” “I may have taught you how to wrestle, but this guy right here… he sure know how to score.” “So do I, bruh. How else could I make you the godfather of my baby?” “Touche. Now I will grant you one favor, on this, the day of my daughter’s wedding.” Devin Shakur walks in front of the camera, almost completely blocking the commentary behind him. “Not cool, bruh. Not cool.” The Commie Emo offers Tyler Rayne his own bit of commentary. A middle finger. “Looks like Emo wants to be next in line, sugar bear.” ---- The 5-Star Champion sprang to life in startled surprise. So much spring (and surprise), in fact, that he bounds right up off the cot he’d been sleeping on. A dull ache reawakens in his thigh. His legs are stiff when he tries to stand. “Uh… you all right?” Angelica Brooks is still laid up in a hospital bed. Her spine is in tact. She is not nude. She is not on top of him. “Just a… Jesus. Weird fucking dream.” “Oo. Wanna tell me about it?” “Um…no.” “Aww. You’re no fun.” “Trust me, kid… you don’t wanna know. I’m gonna grab a smoke. Cool?” She nods. He quickly scurries to the door, never once looking her in the eyes. His hand presses against the cool metal handle. Almost free… “Ty?” “Yeah, kid?” “…Thanks. For everything.” A moment passes between them in silence. His mind fumbles for an appropriate response and fails to find one. His legs find movement again. His feet find the floor. The door shuts behind him and Angelica Brooks is left alone in her hospital bed, staring longingly at a man who’s not there.
