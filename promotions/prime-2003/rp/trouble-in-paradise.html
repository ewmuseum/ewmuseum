Trouble in Paradise

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReV 171 Christian Novak was angry. Wait. Christian Novak was pissed THE fuck off! What else is new? Not only had the engine of his Mustang blown hours before; but, the memories had come back to haunt him. Fire. He could still feel the warmth of the flames; watching helplessly as they overwhelmed his home and the people he loved. He could still hear the cries of his parents, wife, daughter, and his two younger sisters as they perished right before his eyes. The sickening crime scene of coroners carrying out body bag after body bag topped off his thoughts and wounded him the most. Though there was clearly nothing he could do, he still felt like it was his entire fault. The feeling of ‘what-if’ rested heavily upon his shoulders and weighed him down like an anchor at the bottom of the ocean. He had lost it all. Snapping back to reality, he felt someone nudging him. “Ay you,” the bus driver grumbled underneath his voice as he continued to poke away. “Get up! This is the last stop!” Christian opened his eyes and found himself being looked down upon by some old man dressed in a blue short sleeve shirt tucked into matching dress pants. A stupid little hat rested atop his head and covered his gray hair. “Come on, son. I got to get going. Now get up and get,” the driver insisted as he pulled out his pocket watch. Christian glared at him like a caged tiger staring out a rabbit on the other side of the bars. He helped himself up by way of two seats and outstretched his arms. Growing impatient, the driver tapped his foot and crossed both arms, quietly urging Christian to get a move on. “Listen er… Orville is it,” Christian asked pulling the senior’s name tag from his shirt. “Do you enjoy being a bus driver or would you rather be a pilot?” Offended, Orville bellowed through pale ashy lips, “I beg your pardon?” He placed his hands on his hips and thrust his challenging face toward Christian. A humored Christian chuckled and in a split second he grabbed Orville by his shirt collar and lifted him inches from the ground. “I asked because if you ever… and I do mean EVER catch that tone of voice with me again, you’re going to go flying out that window.” Christian’s unibrow formed a V-shape. “I ought to knock that damn hat off your head for even challenging me!” He returned Orville to the Earth’s surface and chuckled once more. “Orville… pfft. What type of fucking name is Orville?” Without another word Christian brushed past Orville, the smile still planted on his lips and one hand in his suit pocket. Orville adjusted his collar nervously still taken over by the anger of Christian. He tried to follow him toward the front of the bus but was caught off guard with a swiping palm across the head. Christian had knocked his hat off. “Sorry gramps,” Christian uttered with remarkable composure. “I couldn’t resist.” Christian pressed on leaving Orville in a pool of shame. Orville grabbed his hat from the base of the bus and crumpled it with two hands as he watched in embarrassment as Novak exited down the stairs. When Christian reached the concrete, Orville quickly released the lever to shut and secure the door. He knocked on the window capturing Christian’s attention and greeted him with a bony middle finger. Before Christian could react the bus lurched forward leaving behind a thick cloud of exhaust. “Homo.” Christian couldn’t help but laugh. It wasn’t his first altercation with the elderly but it was definitely the funniest one to date. As the bus scurried upon the road, Christian was flabbergasted to notice Kale Ryder leaning against a silver Mercedes Benz and waving directly at him. Taking his life in his hands, Kale darted across the street, dodging in and out among the vehicles with the curses of drivers in his ear. Out of breath Kale said, “Damn boy. I didn’t know you were catching the bus today. I’ve been waiting for you for like forever.” Slightly embarrassed and all the way pissed Christian scratched the back of his head with five grubby fingers. “Yeah, my car fucked up.” “I could have always come and gotten you bro,” Kale humbly retorted. “You know that. That’s what friends are for.” A white smile crept across Kale’s face as he brushed his golden locks from his blue eyes. Christian cleared his throat and loosened his red tie. “Why do you always do that?” “Do what,” Kale inquired still smiling. Not really understanding exactly what was going on, he bent over to catch his breath. When he lifted himself back up he noticed Christian’s face full of stone. He immediately knew something was wrong. “What I do,” Kale asked once more growing impatient and uneasy. “Nothing. Just forget about it.” Kale shrugged, “fine have it your way.” He softened down his eyebrows with a spit drenched thumb and swayed his hair in the wind. “Faggot,” Christian muttered under his breath heading towards a building with the words ‘Riley’s Agency’ up the street. Kale followed him in behind trying not to trip out of his penny loafers. “So you never told me. What happened to you at the hospital?” Obviously pinching a nerve Christian replied. “They did that shit on purpose.” “Did what on purpose?” A dumbfounded look formed over Kale’s face. “I’m not understanding this whole thing.” “You wouldn’t,” Christian barked back. “No one would. I just know that somehow those people controlled me in there. You know… they induced my rage. They wouldn’t let me out. There was fire and everything. It felt like I was going to burn alive in there.” Remembering the story of Christian’s past Kale immediately understood. He tapped his partner on the back to ease his thoughts. “Look, I understand if you don’t want to talk about it,” Kale told him. “I’m just worried about your condition.” “Don’t.” “I’m just say—“ Christian interrupted, “let’s just concentrate on this PRIME shit. We’re off to a good start and I don’t want anything standing in our way of taking this division over.” Kale laughed. “Yeah, we did make light work of those frat boys didn’t we?” He lifted his palm to Christian’s face awaiting a high five. Christian’s eyebrow narrowed. “We? Huh… WE didn’t do anything. You danced like you always do and I took over. I got us that win.” “I beg to differ my dear friend,” Kale countered holding the door open motioning for Christian to enter first. “I did what I do best and put on another spectacular match. They don’t call me the ‘King of Quality’ for nothing.” Christian roared with laughter, “no one calls you that!” “Yeah, yeah,” Kale replied laughter consuming him as well. Trying to change the subject he said, “Hey, I wonder what Riley wants to see us for this time. Hopefully it’s some new endorsements and cool shit like that.” Christian shrugged. “Who knows? But it better damn-well be good and quick. My patience is reaching its peak with Riley and my head feels like it’s going to explode.” “Relax, relax. Let’s at least see what he has to say first.” Kale reached into his pocket and pulled from it a piece of Hubba Bubba bubble gum throwing it into his mouth. “Yeah you’re right.” The two Awakening members headed towards the reception desk not knowing exactly what they were getting themselves into. Christian Novak was still pissed and more-so now than ever, tired.
