Wake up from your slumber PRIME...

Date: Today Matthew Ward… What does this name mean to me? It used to mean that I had a friend. I had someone to turn to in my time of need. This was a person that I entrusted my entire life in. I told Matt information that I haven’t shared with the best of friends, yet I thought he was a person in that group. We got along well and Mary was a sweetheart. She always used to ask me how I was doing. Every time I went to their house, she always offered me a drink or something to eat right away. She meant well and I thought Matt did too. Christmas was another time that we shared together. I truly felt like I once again had a family. ReVolution 76 was a turning point in my life once again. This is where I lost a friend, an ally and a family. All it took was a single violent act and that relationship was like a glass vase striking a concrete floor, shattered well beyond repair. Thinking back to what I shared with this man, it makes me sick to my stomach that he knows such personal things about me. He knows how I live, how I spend my spare time and what time I like to go to bed at night. He knows what medications I take, what I eat on a regular basis and what I drink as well. He knows me better than I probably know myself. Yet he carries on that I am his focus of destruction. He carries on about the past and the disturbing incidents that took place in the Underground. While I must admit that my actions were quite violent, they were carried out for a reason. Like it or not, I was paid to take out the Inhuman Being. That’s right Matt; I was a hit man, a machine hired for one soul purpose. My goal was to hurt Matt, to make him feel the pain that Sephiroth had felt. If I was to capture the Underground’s top title, the Immortality Championship, then so be it. Yet, our death match never happened. The owner of the Underground, Troy Mason, saw fit to close the fed, just before the PPV on the beach in San Diego. Could it be that Matt was to be spared? That he was being protected from total annihilation? ------------------------------------------------------------/ Sunday, October 2, 2005 Nothing could be more comfortable than sitting in front of your TV on a Sunday afternoon watching NFL Football with a nice large sub sandwich and a… erm… coke. Damn blood thinners, I hate the fact that I can’t enjoy a cold beer. Small price to pay I guess. Although getting slammed in the head with a steel chair really didn’t work wonders for my thin blood either. I felt like I was bleeding for hours. The few stitches it took to close the cut were not pleasant either. But football was consoling me. Not to mention that I was currently watching The Philadelphia Eagles losing to the Kansas City Chiefs. That brought a whole new joy to me, but now it was halftime. Twenty-four to thirteen was not bad, but it could be a lot worse. My loft was a mess. I didn’t really feel like cleaning up at the moment. I strolled over to the large window that overlooked Bannatyne Avenue in downtown Winnipeg. ReVolution was in only a few short days. I had seen her earlier this week and no doubt I would be seeing her again. Amy Campbell was really all I had left. I had lost Matt and damnit I was not going to lose Amy too. We spent a couple days together before I set off for home. Having to fly really gave me more time overall, but I didn’t enjoy it as much. I always loved driving all over Canada and the United States to get to PRIME events. But it has been recommended that I do not sit for long periods of time in a vehicle due to my medical condition. Either way, it gave me more time to spend with Amy. We had enjoyed a couple of days and we learned a lot about each other, besides how good we were in bed. The previous week she had been a wreck. She was worried about the PTC event. But this week, she was quite a bit more relaxed. He liked that in her. She could be such a bitch if she wanted to be, but when she was in a good mood she was the sweetest and most caring person he had ever known. So besides her sticking to my body like glue, she instead talked to me about her life. She was able to hold an extensive conversation for once. Finally it seemed like everything with her was going well. I heard a noise. I looked behind me and noticed that halftime was still on but the noise wasn’t coming from there. I made my way over to the kitchen to see my cell phone dancing around on the counter from the vibration. Amy was due to call me but when I picked up the phone; it wasn’t a phone call at all. Instead it was a text message and it definitely wasn’t from Amy Campbell. “New text message from Matt Ward.” The letters seemed to burn their image into my mind. I know that for the next few days until ReVolution, that phrase would constantly be in my mind. I had tried to call him after ReVolution 76 but instead I got Mary. We didn’t really say much. “Big match this Wednesday, Killean. Where's your mind at?' Simple words were his forte. He’d say anything just to distract me. Too bad for him, I have already put my head into the match this Wednesday. Rampage, a rather disturbing individual. A big guy from Kentucky. It wasn’t often that I was caught in the ring with a specimen bigger than myself. He actually reminds me a lot of a former PRIME superstar. Kincade was the Intense Champion when I entered PRIME. It had been over two years since I had been in the wrestling business. A rather tall man, maybe about six feet nine inches in height. He was a little taller than Rampage and weighed perhaps a bit more as well. But he had the same attitude, almost to a tee. Both men seem to be under the impression that they are bigger than everyone else. Maybe in size, but size doesn’t always matter. I cut Kincade down to size in the Syndicate Structure and I could do the same to Rampage in a singles match in the JITC. But there was a problem. It was only a singles match, disqualification was a factor. I wasn’t in the frame of mind to fight my way around a DQ. All the events that have taken place involving Tchu will most definitely put me in a bad mood when I enter the ring on Wednesday. Rampage is just in the wrong place at the wrong time. Like Kincade, Rampage will fall to me, just as many other competitors in my nineteen wins. He will not be considered in my twelfth loss. But it was time to try and face the music. I held onto the phone and dialed slowly. Matt’s home number showed up on the screen and when I pressed send, the text scrolled, “Calling Matt Ward”. The phone seemed to ring forever. Matt must have been out on errands. Just as I was about to give up I heard the click of someone picking up the phone. I took a deep breath just as I heard… “Hi Killean.” Mary’s voice. I exhaled. “Hi Mary, how are you?” She seemed to hesitate, but then her words came out crisp and clear. “I’m doing fine, how about you?” “I’m not bad.” I lied. I didn’t want to but as far as I knew, Mary was a quite clueless to the fact that Matt was back in wrestling. I couldn’t really discuss my problems involving Matt with her. “Is Matt around?” “I can go check.” I heard her put down the phone. I bet that Matt wasn’t there but I decided to wait. I was sure that Mary couldn’t remember where Matt really was and I was certain that he would just give her some plain old excuse when he did arrive home. “Killean?” I suddenly heard her soft voice speak into the phone. I was taken back that she would return so soon. “Yes Mary, I’m here.” I waited for her response eagerly, expecting to hear that she had no idea where Matt was. “Matt left me a note. He had this crazy idea that we come up with a system to keep track of our personal affairs. It was explained on the fridge and for some reason I don’t remember making those arrangements. But he is out right now doing up the final paperwork for his office. I don’t know what that means really, but he has to be back soon to give Georgie a kiss goodnight.” His daughter, I knew the name when I heard it. Boy was she a sweetheart. I had been her unofficial god parent since I had met her. Matt really cared about her. But perhaps I was wrong about Matt and Mary. Maybe he really did care. He came up with a system to keep in touch with Mary so she wouldn’t forget where he was. I was certain that he never mentioned the wrestling in his notes, but I was sure he had an excuse. In that situation, I can’t blame him for keeping his return to the ring a secret. She never wanted him to do it again, but the mental anguish he had to go through when Mary’s accident took place must have been too hard to bear. He had to return, I see it now. I can also see why he hasn’t been himself. “Ok Mary, just let him know I got his message and that I know…” “Wait wait I need to write this down.” She was writing down the message. But she also didn’t know that Matt and I were on rather bad terms to put it lightly. I didn’t want to cause the Ward family anymore stress. That’s not my right. “Tell Matt that I appreciate his concern, but I am fine and I will see him this week.” I sort of lied and sort of didn’t. She was still under the impression that Matt was still a psychologist and he was helping me. But it wasn’t my duty to tell Mary about Matt’s return to the world of professional wrestling. Matt will one day have to inform her. “Ok Killean. Take care of yourself.” She was still sweet and this just made the problems that much harder to accept. “You too Mary. Good night.” “Good night.” I pressed the end button on my cell phone. The Ward family of Dayton, Ohio certainly had some large obstacles ahead of them. Mary would have her short term memory return and Matt would have to either quit wrestling once again or he would have to admit the truth. The baby was also a concern. I knew that I may have been wrong about Matt not caring, but what his family needs right now is him. He needs to be there for Mary and for Georgie, the little sweetheart. Matt wants to clock me in the head with a chair? Well I will do the entire Ward family a favor and return Matt home one way or another. His family needs him and I will do anything to help him see it. I rested the phone back on the granite counter. I walked back to the TV to see the football game come back from halftime. But I had to admit to myself that I was done watching this game. Matt was wondering where my mind was at before the match this week. I hadn’t even studied a tape of Rampage. I couldn’t possibly be silly enough to go into the match this Wednesday and not watch a tape of Rampage. That was not my style. Maybe Matt was right. I had to focus. Funny, this was my rival giving me advice. It was too ironic to even think about. I slipped a tape of last week’s ReVolution into the machine and watched the Triple Threat Tag Team Title match. One thing was immediately obvious. Scott Matthews was green in the ring. But he also had it in his head that Rage was his only way out. That gigantic gore that he executes looks like it hurts like hell. So my main goal is to avoid it at all costs. The problem with a move like that was that it had countless counters. There really wasn’t much else to study on Scott Matthews. His inexperience in the ring would no doubt lead to his demise this Wednesday. I didn’t come back to PRIME to lose. So whether it is Scott Matthews, Danny Ferguson, Jesse Jamester, Karina Wolfenden or even Tchu himself, I plan on winning the Jewel in the Crown tournament. I came here to win, I returned to PRIME to give the fans what they wanted. I would get my shot once again. Killean Sirrajin is back and everyone will take notice this Wednesday. I KNOW WHO I AM! PRIME will have to wake up from their long sleep and remember…
