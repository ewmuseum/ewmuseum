KING: Masked Riches

3 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2006 Foreword I, Your Inevitable Executioner I, Your Chronic Crimson Advocate I, Your Anticipated Hand of Death Triumph countered Sovereignty, breeding the cold dreadful sweat creeping down my face. Humidity mounted, crafting a surreal atmosphere that only played key to the scenario at hand. With one swift motion, I swung my arm in a downward motion, cringing at the echo of steel upon steel as I slammed the sledgehammer into the metal scrap below. Desperate times call for desperate measures is what I've been told, none more true than my current situation. The realm of reality had been shaped, offering me a scarce root of happiness...one in which I'd long desired. I yielded the sledge once more, crushing the steel below upon impact as a sound of thunder roared through the velvet sky as if on cue. The tears of angels above descended in the form of rain, creating a profound melody fitting for the mood of the night. I took the back of my palm, taking a moment to wipe the sweat drenching my face. Afterwhich, I dropped the sledge unto the ground, kneeling for a moment to regain a suitable level of fatigue before continuing on with the day's assignments. Disarray in my life, all caused by betrayal of my best friend, and failure to succeed inside of the ring. I tried to kill myself, but I failed at that as well...So now I’ve decided to instead kill my outer shell and breed a new age in my life. Ashes to Ashes. Dust...to Dust. I retrieved the sledge once more, resuming in progress of my mission, but not as to fade away from my thoughts. No, there were four other individuals on my mind, those names being Ignatius Lisieux, Paul Cain, and Chris Sloboda. Though these three men, I’d re-enter a hell that I thought I left behind me long ago. Now as the rise and fall of the Dark Age reaches it’s one year anniversary, I repeat the same rituals. Last time it was Dani Furher whom I killed...this year, it would be the shell that replaced him...Vangelus Olsig. Yielding the sledge above once more, I crushed the scrap metal yet again wielding a dimensional figure in which the smile crafted on my face signaled I was pleased with. Thunder crackled once more with such demanding authority that the wooden table in which I labored on shivered softly. Nonetheless, I lifted the piece of scrap metal which I'd shaped and placed it upon an automatic saw, taking a moment to slip on my safety goggles in pre-caution before activating the power switch. Golden sparks danced through the atmosphere, possibly in tune with the low-toned hum of the operating saw. The force of the saw challenged my fatigue, forcing me to garnish a measurable amount of strength in order to tame it. As I resumed my mission, thoughts of the situation that I was prepared to step into continued to invade my mind. Thoughts of certainty ran rapid...was I certain I wanted to kill the shell of Vangelus Olsig? But as the constant thoughts of failure erupted, they provided me a new sense of certainty. I turned off the power saw, lifting my creation into the air. lightening struck, illuminating the sharp edge of the sword which I'd hand crafted myself. Lifting the Sword in the air, the Thunder crackled... As I declared... Death unto Olsig... And Rise of... KING It started off just as any day would. The winding sky swerved gently into a crimson approach, dying down with the sun and breathing night once more. With the retirement of day also came my release from the hell hole known as work...atleast for the day. Tommorrow would present the same challenges as this day did, challenges of battling disgruntled co-workers and tyrant-like bosses. If this was, however, any other day...I’d be on a fast-track home, bracing myself for as much of a relaxing night as I could possibly achieve. This day, however, extended far beyond the realm of regularity. One sharp turn on Sandlewood road was met with a stiff punch to my jaw, sending me spiraling to the concrete grounding below in a shock. From that point on, it was nothing more outside of unrecognized kicks and brutal battering. I tried desperately to lift my eyes, in an effort to identify my attackers, but said attempt met defeat in an horrific manner, brushing aside as I felt two massive hands clinch my arms and drag me ferociously into a dark alley. By the now, the sky had welcomed darkness, and with darkness came my slow demise as I felt a sharp boot plant itself right in the center of my neck, creating a miasma of tortured coughs and nearly causing me to choke on my own blood as I spit it up. The crimson substance drifted from my lips and stained the cold concrete below as the kicks and battering continued with no signs of letting up...not anytime soon atleast. A cold fist landing square in my jaw was enough to daze me for mere moments, numbing me to any pain that occurred through that brief moment. As I awakened once more to the pain, I felt myself being lifted before being slammed with authority against a brutal brick wall. I could smell my own blood swimming through the atmosphere as a stiff boot buried itself right into my abdomen as I stood pinned against the wall. Following the boot as a cold back hand slap that send blood shooting out of my nose and mouth simultaneously, landing right on the black shirt of one of the attackers pinning me against the wall. A nauseous, mild substance circulated through my nostrils, acting as if vomit as on the verge of erupting through them. Dizziness mixed in with a sudden shock of drowsiness...or maybe that was just unconsciousness on the verge. Either or, it tortured my frayed and battered body. One punch to the bridge of my nose sent everything spiraling ...I think I just passed out. The guard led me to a conference room in which I was scheduled to meet my personal Councilor, Doctor Theodore Mayfield. He was scheduled to give me advice as to how to carry out my life now that I’d been emitted, but in my mind he was only wasting his time. I knew what my future withheld, and that was a beautiful life with the one woman I would never allow myself to be without, Ms. Anicka Swan. As soon as my powdered boot touched the outside surface of this Asylum, my mission would focus directly on finding her and garnishing that life of paradise that was bound to happen. Yeah...that’s what my future withheld. Suddenly, a beam of light illuminated the room, presenting me with my first true sense of vision ever since regaining conscious inside of this black hell. Through the light stepped a man dressed in all black, muscles basically ripping through the muscle shirt that squeezed his torso. Every form of ripple displayed themselves clearly, deeming the man one of brute strength. I had a feeling in the pit of my gut that such a strong man as he served a purpose...and more times than not, that purpose was to protect and serve an even smaller man. But what’s the only reason strong men bow down to smaller men? Because that smaller man has power. The man I was destined to stand toe to toe with in a matter of moments had power...I could smell it. Without so much as an utter of words, the man reached down yanked me up out of the seating position, the white straight jacket rendering me helpless to defend myself against his aggressive and demanding attitude. A nudge of my elbow into his abdomen did little to please him and only resulted him smacking me across the back of my head in an effort to “Tame” my crude behavior, so to speak. We guided me down a narrow strip of a hallway. I glanced at each wall as we resumed down, observing the various photos. Photos of men in suits, most with cigars in their mouths and pistols in their hands. It was obvious from that point that I was dealing with some sort of Mafia Lord...I don’t care how big a Godfather fan you are, no one has that many mafia pictures without partaking in the industry themselves. My assumptions were proven correct as The big black man basically tossed me through the doorway, as two other guards standing near the door way immediately grabbed me and forced me into a cold wooden chair. Both stood to each side of me, monitoring my every move with muscles that boasted steroids. In front of me was a solid wooden oak desk, and a man sitting in a chair. Of course, like all the Mafia Lords, he had his chair turned backwards so that his face was not visible, but it wouldn’t stay that way for long as I finally spoke up, obviously not in the mood for any Mob Games. “Where am I and who are you...” The chair slowly rotated until it reached a point where the man sitting in it faced me eye...to mask. Covering his face was a white and black mask, one side white and the other black. Out of the mask weaved moderately long and stringy black hair, slicked in the form of a ponytail. The masked assassin held a mechanical device against his throat, radiating his voice as he spoke aloud. “Vangelus...you are here for your own protection.” He began, his voice sounding in the most annoying, screeching crackle that one could imagine. It was as if I was standing face to face with a high-pitched clown who’s voice not only annoyed, but made me tingle in the process. “You see, Vangelus...you are what we consider, a liability. In your founding country of Bogota, Columbia, you are considered among the Most Wanted. To the Dominican society, you are a terrorist, not because of your actions, but because of your name. Your father’s seaman birthed you, and ultimately placed you under his throne, and I’m sure you know by now that your father was far less than a righteous tyrant.” “Could we please abandon the subject of my father...I doubt you know him in a honest sense, therefore you have no right to judge him, his name, or me.” I shouted out, obviously unaware of just how dangerous the man under the mask was...or maybe it was just that at this point I didn’t care anymore. If he’d killed me, I’d be grateful...seeing as I, for some reason, couldn’t seem to complete that mission myself. “There you are wrong, for I know your father very well. As a matter of fact, your father worked under my wing before he betrayed me and led me astray, leading a crusade of his own.” The crackle voice sounded once more. The screech of his voice disallowed me to tell if he was being serious, or putting on a cocky flamboyant act. “You’re insane. My father worked for two people...Ralphus Washington, and I-...” “Ishmael Muhammed V. That is my name, Vangelus...and that insight is to not leave this room, understood?” Ishmael Muhammed V. I’d heard so much about this deviant Lord whom caused my father so much agony and pain that he was forced to rally up a cult and fight against him. Ishmael was the hand that fed my father, bringing him up from his early stages of life and flowering him into a mature and sadistic minded being. He always told me a strong mind was the key to survival in the streets...and in Bogota, Columbia the streets were the only relatives you had to push you along this battlefield known as life. From what I’d learn, though, Ishmael was assassinated in a riot...hidden identity wouldn’t be a first though. The mask began to make more sense. “And what If it is to leave this room? What If I ‘accidently’ let it slip to the fuzz. Are your roid-buddies going to chop me up and spread my body across the continent? Been there done that, Ishy...” “Nah, Actually I was going to chop off your wanker and send it to your beloved girlfriend in Tracer City before chopping HER up and spreading HER body across the continent. Killing an Olsig is too easy...you douches were born to die.” I must admit...he was smart. Very creative too...Just the sound of him axing my wanker made me cringe in pain. I started having second thoughts about mouthing off to a man like him. 'Now as I was saying...you are a target, and in my hand lies your safety. Your father corrupted the Dominican population with biological warfare, spreading thousands of diseases among their peoplee and killing other's with anthrax and things of that nature. When your father died...' 'He's not dead.' My interruption served little in pleasing the masked villian. The crinkles on the small exposed part of his face displayed his discontent with being interrupted. He ran a hand through his slick greased hair, as he took a deep breath and continued on, pardoning the interruption the best of his abilities. 'Vangelus...contrary to your belief, your father is dead.' 'Or maybe he faked his death like...ugh...lets say...you.' I felt a smack in the back of my head, forcing me to glance at the guard guilty of completing the action. The narrowing of his eyes told me that neither he nor his boss was pleased with my snotty behavior. 'Watch your tone. Your father is dead and you are heir to his throne. In their eyes you're no better than Czar was, and the only way to rid a problem is to rid THE problem. My friend...the problem is you, but I am the solution.' 'Oh really? And just how did you suddenly become my solution.' 'Your father only threatened the Dominican population with Biological warfare...I have enough power to launch an all out Nuclear assault. They won't mess with me, nor those associated with me, because they know what's good for them and their country. You are now filling the void under my wing that your father left. Life outside of that void equals death, but if you serve me faithfully, your days shall be numbered and filled with the riches I've inherited.' Thoughts of grandeur and emancipation raced through my head, bringing me to a revelation of freedom and triumph over the distraught life that I'd embarked upon. Through a blood oath to this one man, I could go from chronic depression to mass riches. The sound of it made my mouth water, but something on the inside told me that it just wasn't that easy. 'Riches, eh? Riddle me this, Ishmael...if my father proposed such a threat to the Dominican Population, and if you seemingly pose an even greater threat...why isn't the paparazzi covering this like mad?' 'It's a trait I've been taught in my early stages. Start small and build your way up. Only a third of the population realizes the threat that I hold against them. And besides...I'm dead, remember?' A small chuckle erupted from underneath as the guard on the right nudged an elbow in my shoulder, once more instructing me to cut down on the cocky behavior and become more attentive. They were the last subjects on my mind, though. I'd seen worse on the Sopranos. 'I hear you. Though, I wonder...what have I done to deserve your almighty mercy...' 'You are the heir of Czar. Theres no denying that Czar was a powerful man, therefore his son is destined to only be greater, correct?' 'Pssh...not me. I don't do Organized Crime Governments...' 'I know, I know. Wrasslin' is your forte. But wrestling won't get you anywhere, my friend, and I've seen you in the ring...I now know why you have a part-time job under the federal agency, speaking of which...you've got to drop that.' I shook my head in shame as the masked Ishmael poured orders unto my lap. 'Oh really? And why is that so, sir?' 'Put the pieces together, Vangelus. Federal Agency...Crime Government...they don't exactly mix. Don't worry...my name is Ishmael Muhammed, and all of my associates are taken care of. Under my wing you have nothing to fear and nothing to worry about. It's the life you never had, but yet the one you've always desired.' He was right, and the swirving feeling in the pit of my stomach told me he was right. For so long I'd fought for a chance to be amongst the stars, living a life of grandeur and riches, but it seemed that no matter how hard I fought, something always kept me down. I'd set a record for holding the Intense Championship, but something constantly held me down. I'd beaten some past greats in Hoyt Williams and Ozric Mortimer as well as some future greats in Danny Ferguson and Rampage...but something still held me back. Riches is what I longed for...maybe this was truly the only way of achieving it. 'But first, you must prove your allegiance.' I glanced at him once more as he reached under his desk and pulled out a black and white photo of a beautiful woman, placing it on the oak desk and sliding it my way. 'Her name is Tabitha Seagar. She is the son of Vincent Seagar III, a man that's been hot on my tail for five years. None of my cronies seems smart enough to get the job done, but I know you Vangelus...you're brighter than the rest. You will get the job done. Seagar is a very cunning individual, but his life revolves around his princess daughter. Kill her and you'll cut off his support...but most importantly, you'll gain the favor of Ishmael Muhammed IV.' PRIME's debut on FX saw me pitted in a Dark Age Reunion main event...and sadly enough, I'd found myself in yet another Dark Age-Esque environment, serving under the wing of a fraud. This time, Ishmael Muhammed replaced Vampir Nosferatu, and the actions required of me were far more authentic. The guards yanked me up by my arms, lifting me out of the chair as Muhammed spoke one simple word that sent me along my blood path... 'Go.' Patience. A demanding virtue that spawned success in the form of dominance. Those that wait shall mount up on wings as eagles, boasting success in every form. Patience had brought me to this Herald...one I'd sought ever since laying my eyes on her. Smiles disrupted my unpleasant facial expression, revealing a sinister sensation of satisfaction. Her screams muffed in the palms of my cold, sweaty hands as I covered her mouth tightly, preventing any form of attention being drawn unto the situation at hand. Beauty was in the eye of this beholder, and of that beauty lied a basket of glory, seized by a tyrant with uncertain intentions. I wish she'd calm down, for my intentions were not to hurt her, but neither were they to aid her in any manner. Behind those hazel eyes, behind that soft pale skin, behind that silky and luscious hair resided a woman who's purpose to rid herself of me was on the verge of avail, but in a much more triumphant manner. Triumphant for Tabitha? Of course not. Ishmael wants to market me as some sort of disoriented and sadistic Tyrant...Ishmael gets what he wants. Poor Tabitha, caught in the crossfires of a blood feud...fed to an animal whom's quinch craved her blood. The events that led us to this destination...triumphant. Narrowly, She dodged a ramming black Lincoln Cadillac that swerved massively off of the road and unto the sidewalk, nearly tearing our dear Tabi a new vertebrae. Shaken and disturbed, I took advantage of her frayed soul, creeping through the velvet night sky and swooping her up into the shadows of the alley. The definition of this place portrayed the mood of the night in a near perfect sense. a Maroon descent rang through the air, reeking of blood and gore. The stench alone made it perfectly obvious that someone, somewhere, had been murdered in this very spot no less than hours ago. Tabitha kicked and squimished, attempting a method of escape, thus forcing me to slam her frail body up against the blood stained brick wall, afterwhich tossing her body completely into the unstable, rusty fence. Retrieving the black painted hand cuffs from the inside of my trench coat, I carefully snapped them around both of her wrist, locking her up to the chained fence, thus restricting any escape. She looked so beautiful right their, swinging freely trying to find an escape thats blink to her existence. It brought a smile to my painted face....yes, her anguish brought a painted smile. Shut Up...This won't hurt. I stated, blocking out her muffled screams and proceeding with wielding out a small pocket knife, cutting the tip of my index finger slightly in assurance of it's sharpness. I proceeded to grasp the face of Tabitha, tightly as to prevent movement. Slowly, yet surely, I placed the sharp end of the knife unto the crinkled, glossing lip of Tabi, carving it open slightly thus drawing blood. Slowly I smiled as tears mixed with her makeup, creating a black streak down her cheek. I placed the knife aside, cutting the lip open slightly, yet big enough to draw the right amount of blood needed to dip my feathered pin in. As the blood covered the tip of the pin, I released her, setting my attentions towards the wrinkled napkin that I'd stolen from the convenience store. I placed the napkin up against the brick wall, using it as a surface to write. In blood stained letters, I wrote the note instructed of me by Ishmael... Your blood is my blood...Your pain is my pain...Your anguish is my anguish...Your loss is my gain... Love Ish... Why Are you Doing this!? She quiered through muffled tones. She knew more than anyone why I was doing this, though. Ishmael made me do it. He granted me this allegiance, blatantly disconcerning my obsession with glory. I never desired this rank...I never even wanted to be here, but slowly he lured me...first offering me death which I still regret declining, afterwards granting me life under his wing. He placed his corrupt on my weak shoulders, and expects me to carry it. Look at the mess I've gotten myself into because of it. I'm a target in the America's eyes...I'm seen as an advocate of Satan himself...and now I have a man who's promised me death if I disobeyed his will. I welcome it though...I plead and pray that he delivers on his promise. I mean surely death couldn't be as bad as this. SHUT UP! I screamed out, slapping her in the face, producing a stream of blood. I yanked back her hair, placing my lips on hers and slipping my tongue down her throat, basking as it tickled her rippled lungs and caressed her dancing tongue. She tried to fight it off, but their was nothing she could so. I had her right where I wanted her...right where I needed her...and because of that she was helpless. Years stacked upon years had brought me to this moment as I exited out of her mouth, pulling back and sending a boot to her stomach before catching her with a knee to her cheek. With Tabi just about out and done, I knelt down and looked into her eyes, whispering something to her before she slipped out into unconsciousness. I'm Sorry . . . I wiped a tear from my eye...I couldn't believe what I'd just done. Guilt ran through my heart as I watched her helpless body slump into a state of nothing. Triumph conquered grief, though...for the objective in which I'd set out for had been achieved. But as the glare through my eyes comprehended the stained crimson blood on my hands, I then truly realized what I'd just done...and I'd then truly realized who'd I become. And in realizing this, I proceeded to snatch up the blood-cursed note, tearing it into shreds before uncuffing the lady mistress and placing her head against a moist cardboard box. Slowly, I retrieve a wet napkin from the moist street, dipping it in a puddle of water before dabbing it over the scars scattered across the beautiful face of the woman I'd just harrsassed. Moments passed as her crystal blue eyes peeled open, immediately planting themselves on my image. She quickly jumped in fear, but I tightened my grasp, hugging her close to my chest in order to show her that I was no longer a threat...no longer hear to harm her, but instead to help her. “Don’t worry...I’m going to help you.” I stated as I continued to rock back and forth, aiding her frail soul. “I know who you are...” She stated, pausing everything. Suddenly I stopped and placed the wet napkin down and pushing myself to my feet, helping her up as she attempted to do the same. All I wanted, now, were answers as to how exactly she knew who I was. “Ho-...” “Ishmael sent you.” “How did you kn-...” “He’s the only opposer left who wants my father dead, and their five year long feud has brought out the worst in them both. He’s convinced that nobody knows of his existence, but I indeed do. Don’t worry...I haven’t told anyone, so the rat won’t go against your card.” As her soft and warm breath brushed up against my cheeks, I began to realize that she was truly a princess. I knew just from one glance at her picture that she held all the beauty features fit for a princess, but deeper than that, she held the affection of one as well. How could she hold such affection after what I’d just committed upon her? “I’m supposed to be upset over how you’ve just treated me...but I can’t be. Ishmael’s never attack out of desire...but instead out of demand. I cannot hold you accountable for the seed he has planted in your mind. But tell me...why have you spared me life? You had me in the perfect position for death, so why didn’t you follow through?” Her words were deep, and so was her question. I slowly walked over the steel cage and leaned my forehead up against it, embracing total shock over what I’d just done. Her soft words made it difficult to comprehend what had pushed me to do such a thing to such an innocent being. “I don’t want to be this way. I don’t want to be evil...but no matter what I do, I can never escape it. I am my father’s son, and through holding that acclaim, I must also withhold it’s legacy. Unfortunately, that legacy is corrupt with evil...evil that I’ve seemingly inherited against my will.” She took a deep breath, lowering her head as she felt the emotion that I’d just displayed through words. She limped beside me and placed her arm around my shoulder, slowly pulling my head into her bosom in an affectious manner. “I understand. We are two of the same kind. As Daughter of the evil Tyrant, Vincent Seagar, I am expected to be some sort of jezebel as well. But you must do what I’ve done...rise among the ashes of your past and realize that you are more than a heir. You are king.” You are King... You are King... You are King... Her words echoed throughout my head, presenting me with a revolting thought that I’d long searched for, but never could find. Now I’d find it...now I must embrace it. “As king, you must garner your crown...by any means necessary. King...take your throne.” She stated, smiling as she took her arm from around my shoulder and turned around wiping the dry blood from her cheek. “You are right...you are indeed right.” Slowly I reached into the inside of my trench coat and retrieved a .22 pistol, turning around and aiming it directly to the back of her head. “Thank you, Princess Seagar.” BANG! It hurt me to do so, but I did it. She told me by any means necessary, claim my throne as king. Those means of necessity lied in her death. As the blood flowed from the back of her head, I turned around to embrace my exit... SMACK! I hit the cold concrete with authority as a result of the hard right hand. A momentary daze faded as I regain my vision and glared through the white eyes of Ishmael Muhammed IV. “If you ever hesitate to do my will again, I’ll end you for good....King.” And he walked off, leaving me stranded in a world I thought I knew, but found out I truly knew nothing of. And when the trumpets arise... He will then be declared... KING
