Brave New World, Part 2

October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 2008 Three stories beneath them, Tokyo was alive, a glittering mass of light and sound so vibrant you didn't need to understand a single word of Japanese to immediately get swept up into it. T hat was the last thing on the mind of either Dexter Carroll or Toby Jenkins at this moment. The two young American transplants were both too sore to do anything but remain as stationary as possible, ice their aching limbs and flip through Japanese television channels. Game show. Game show. Seizure cartoon. American game show. Toby was perched in the half-broken recliner near the kitchen of the small apartment the two shared above the school. Dexter was sprawled on the couch, nearly asleep, his face buried between a cushion and his right arm. On the coffee table between them was what remained of their dinner, yet another meal from the take-away joint just down the street. They'd moved into the apartment a few weeks after training began, mainly because neither could afford to live own his own and neither - particularly linguistically-challenged Dexter - wanted to live alone in the middle of Tokyo. That the school kept a few apartments above the training facility for students was just an added bonus. Well, sort of. While it was convenient not to have to venture out into the vast city before the grueling training regimen each day, the building's elevator had been busted for what seemed like decades, and though it was just a two-story walk to their apartment, the stairs were nearly endless and damn near vertical. What they gained in convenience in the morning, they paid back in pain at night. At this particular moment, both of them were paying out the ass. "You still awake, Dex?" "Mmmpprghhh." Taking that as an extremely mild "yes", Toby reached to the floor beside him and slowly picked up a discarded throw pillow, his joints creaking with every inch his hand creeped towards the ground. Then, slowly and painfully, he lobbed the pillow across the room. It wasn't a hard shot, but it was direct, clipping his friend and roommate across the crown of his head. Dexter did a quick double take as the impact snapped him out of his almost-vegetative state. "Huh? Whozits huh whuh?" After much tired pointing, he found the object of his search. He tried to sit up and grimace at Toby, but he was too sore, ending up half-propped against one arm of the couch with his face in a strange half-snarl he could barely maintain. Giving up, he sagged back against the couch and tried to get what sleep he could before the hellish training began again the next morning. And hellish it was. Though neither was surprised by the intensity of their training after three-plus months, it never let up, and it never got easier. In fact, as soon as Koji Matsumoto saw one of his students look like he was getting comfortable with the regime, he stepped up the torture to a previously unthinkable degree. Today might have been the worst of all. Running what seemed like an ultramarathon inside the cramped, sweaty, poorly ventilated gym before the now-routine endless sets of squats, sit-ups, push-ups, leg raises and whatever other bizarre contortionist positions he could bend his class into. Then came the ring drills. Rope-running, bumps of every shape and size done over and over again until their torsos were covered with unnatural colored welts. It was until then that the actual "teaching" began, although most days, training consisted of little more than Koji and his two deputies twisting the students into human pretzels, tenderizing their flesh with chops and dropping them at any manner of dangerous angles all under the auspices of getting them "ready" for when they reached the supposed big time. Now, with his right shoulder dangling at his side like a lead weight, was one of the times Toby would've been perfectly happy back in his bed in San Diego rather than on a 25-year old chair in the middle of Tokyo. BRRRRRRRRRRRRRINNNGGGGG!!! The phone was ringing. Toby arched his neck to check the caller ID, and noticed the name Carroll scrolling across the top. "Dex, phone." No response. BRRRRRRRRRRRRRINNNGGGGG!!! "Dexter ... phone for you." BRRRRRRRRRRRRRINNNGGGGG!!! "Ah, screw it." BRRRRRRRRRRRRRINNNGGGGG!!! Too tired to get up and answer the phone himself, Toby let the call go to voice mail, sending the familiar sound of Dexter's answering machine message lilting across the air. "Hey, this is Dexter (AND TOBY!). Not in right now, so leave a message at the beep. Arigato." A brief pause, before a woman's voice kicked in, shaky and uneven. "Dexter, I'm not sure where you are right now, but you need to pick up the phone. There was ... an accident, and David ..." "Mrs. Carroll? It's Toby. Dexter's kinda out of it right now, but I'll get him up." "Please." Just when you thought the torture couldn't get worse, huh?
