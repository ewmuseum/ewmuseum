But in recent weeks, the one question I keep getting is... what's the deal? And it's a pretty simple answer. In the days since Dusk's departing of PRIME, I've no longer had to deal with keeping him "in line." Dusk was always the hotshot, the renegade, the ineffectual loner who would go off and do his own thing, getting himself in trouble. Now that Dusk is gone, I don't want to say I'm happy, because I'm not. But I can be the man I am now. I don't have to worry about what Dusk is doing, I only have to worry about what I'm doing. I've been feeling very angsty toward PRIME, and anyone who's been getting cheered for lately. I feel being rewarded with a new contract is something I'm deserving of, and to be completely ignored by those in charge? It frustrates me beyond levels that I could have even imagined just three months ago. Why does my boy Dusk get his pink slip, retire from the organization, and all of sudden, we're cannon fodder? Whatever. PRIME didn't care about Dusk, and they didn't care about Viagra. They just laughed at our antics and our preplanned stupidity and our, ultimately, lack of care.
