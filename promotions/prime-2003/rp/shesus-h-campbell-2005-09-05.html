Date: September 5, 05 I'm really close tonightAnd I feel like I'm moving inside herLying in the darkI think that I'm beginning to know herLet it go Food For ThoughtSeptember 2nd, 2005Once you go about accomplishing one of your biggest goals, life gets pretty sweet. I mean, I had to accept the trade off of decimating Wolfenden, but being completely unprepared and unfit for the main event match. Every second between them was absolutely glorious, but it only let the swelling and tenderness grow around my bones and joints. By the time the scratched intro riff of my song played, I could hardly move to jump and pump through the stinging stream of pyro for the second time in a single night. Beating Karina was my personal glory, but most of the fans and critics were looking to the main event as the biggest match of my life... if I won it. Unfortunately, the feeling of hearing the third slap of the ref's hand on the mat in that first match was tainted by the knowledge that just about everybody in the arena was thinking ahead about an hour. They knew what I knew. I was a bloody idiot to ruin my shot at the world title. Now, if you're me in that situation, you could give a shit less. You just beat Karina Wolfenden! If you're one of those pansy ass online smarks, though, you're sitting on AOL Instant Messenger blasting me to bits because you know just as well as I do that I'm not going to be able to go the distance later. Some of the dumber fans are going to wonder why you're hobbling out in the main event so realistically because wrestling's fake, right? No matter what smoke and mirrors anybody convinces you of... if you're Amy Campbell, every ounce of your energy gets poured into the now - into reigning supreme over whatever stands in your way at that very second. It's shitty luck, but 'the now' for you was one of the first matches of the night. To be able to live with yourself, you needed to win that. Now, you're left with nothing. You'll give it your all and you know you'll last until the end because you just DO NOT accept laying down, but the main event is little more than a reason to be aching more tomorrow. To you, it doesn't matter... you just beat Karina Wolfenden! To them, you just blew your chance at truly proving that you can not only tangle with, but dominate the big boys of the industry. So, if you're me, you're feeling pretty weird right now. My mom bitched me out yesterday on the phone, claiming that I ignored the fact that I had a career for the sake of winning some personal vendetta. I'm waking up, groggy as all hell, knowing that I'm totally alone in the apartment because my sister's taken off on a road trip to see The Rolling Stones tomorrow night - a show that I can't attend thanks to the schedule of the career I so gloriously 'ruined' last week. I forgot to take out the Heather Nova CD I was nursing a bottle of wine to last night and 'Winterblue''s depressing, simplistic little western melody is dancing around the edges of my ears. Waking up, hungover and alone to a love song is quite possibly an indication of the fall of mankind. It's one of the select few worst feelings in the world. Next to getting slapped by your boyfriend for breaking it off minutes after sleeping with him, of course. Since I enjoy drawing away from my own idiocy, I stuff a pillow over my face in a sad attempt to fall back asleep without needing to get up and turn the stereo off. The phone rings to remind me that I'm not alone in the world. Joyous. ''Mmhm?'' If one of my parents didn't just spontaneously combust, I'm gonna be pissed for picking this up. ''Amy,'' it's Da--- wait -- who... ''...the fuck is this?'' ''Whoa now, girlie. It's Jerry! Your agent!'' ''Since when did I have an agent?'' ''The dawn of time. You just never get job offers. Remember, I'm the guy PRIME called when they wanted you?'' Wait! The short, chub-- no, that's your lawyer... ''Come on,'' he continues, ''Jerry! I work for Jenna Jameson when she does music videos, remember?'' Oh! ''The tall, kinda hot guy with the tattoos and the suit?'' ''...There ya go! Look, are you feeling alright? I can call b--'' ''I'm fine. Why're you calling so early?'' He laughs, which seems pretty fucking rude to do after you've called someone at like 8 AM. Well, at least it seemed rude until... ''Uh, it's 4 in the afternoon. You sure you're okay?'' What? I never said I was good at telling time by the sun - which is KILLING my vision, by the way. ''I told you, fine. Now, what can I do for you?'' ''Cameron Crowe called and asked me to congratulate you on your win. That, and, uh... see, he's reshooting some scenes for an upcoming project, and there was talk of a part you might be interested in...'' People really shouldn't prank call me, especially when I'm barely awake. I hung up on the sorry son of a bitch, but sleep doesn't seem quite right - though it's still inviting - after I've sorted out enough brain process to speak English. Actually, a beer's in order to settle my stomach enough to take in a bagel or toast. Luckily, I don't need a fridge for that... I just drape my arm over the box next to me and pull one out. I'm French, we like it hot. Opening it by pressing it into my forearm, I'm reminded of a welt I really shouldn't have. Now, was that from hitting the turnbuckle during Dead Aim, or is it shaped like a nose for a reason? I can't remember busting anybody's face, but it's not a far-fetched concept. Probably a casualty of the main event that I barely remember. That whole ordeal was nothing more than a blur and the counting of seconds. Every time the count came, it was the same. At one, I was trying to gather the energy in amongst my shoulderblades. Two never failed to seem like forever; I'd pry one side of my body away from whichever bastard happened to be on top of me. Three never came. I wouldn't let it. What followed my kicking out was some of the worst pain I'd ever been in. People were twisting me every way imaginable. I mean, you don't see this shit in a porn! Every time I found a new way that my arm could bend, I nearly threw up. Things were getting bad out there. My eyes were open long enough to see Nova fuck up in a spectacular way... at least I wouldn't have to worry about him for the rest of the match. Deville and Youngblood were doing their thing and I recalled getting minorly pissed off at how viciously the referee seemed to be guarding Angelo's best interests. Ridiculous, really. Brandon was putting it to everybody, and that gave me a tiny chance to breathe... until, of course, Deville had me twisted up like a pretzel by capitalizing on an opportunity I was too weak NOT to leave opened. While I was trying to bear the relative degree of agony, I could only think of how sweet hearing the three was not too long ago. My conscious self and my body had become two separate entities five minutes before, which is why I was still thinking of that triumph while my hand was waving like mad to put an end to the hurt and save my body. It's not like I was going to be able to stand up and really compete in the rest of the match. Even I have to be realistic sometimes, so I tapped. I hoped people had their VCRs recording, because me tapping out is even rarer than me beating Karina. So, to most of the world, I'm a moron... but I.BEAT.WOLFENDEN. After my spectacular failure a few months ago, kicking the snot out of her was just what my career needed - whether others saw it or not. It gave me the passion and drive to continue on for something other than cash. The constant fight she put up rekindled my love of the competition I recieved as a wrestler. Tapping out at the moment that I did gave me the ability to walk onto the plane in Hawaii under my own power and, despite being achey as all fuck, I'm gonna walk into the arena for ReVolution with ease. I can't say the same for Karina Wolfenden, Angelo Deville or Nova. They pushed it, but they LOST it. They're feeling what I've been feeling for years. They dropped the ball and their bodies suffered for no good god damned reason. At least I won that night. At least I was walking to win AGAIN. I'm no quitter. I tapped out as a means to an end. Normally, I'd chance everything and keep going until my knee was hanging by a stringy tendon just for the possibility that I MIGHT win the match. I might get a shot at taking the gold off of our joke champion. But, for the first time in the history of... well... ever, I thought things through. Perfectly rested, I could beat any Hoyt or Youngblood. If I still had legs, I could tear Kano limb from limb for fun in the match I knew I had coming in a week. If I got myself put on the shelf for a month, though, it'd set me back. I'd get tossed straight back to the lower card just because I went too far, as usual. After accomplishing the one thing I'd wanted to do in the past few months and living to tell about it, I was NOT going to challenge my luck or risk not being able to continue my little streak of domination, so I tapped. It wasn't worth the risks I was taking to get a shot that I'll end up earning in a month or two. For the first time in my life, I chose to wait... and it was quite possibly the smartest thing I could've done. I took Karina's notch in my belt and ran with it. After all, what good is beating your only equal if you don't have the physical ability to prove yourself again the week after, or a month later? I'll take my win with a grin and tell anyone who doesn't think I'll earn a world shot again to go fuck themselves with ease. A title's always there, but Wolfenden's smug little smile wasn't going to go away unless I beat it off of her face. I think I just realized that my talk with Youngblood rubbed off. Gibson's help was seeping into my head. Their rationalization was actually coming through in me. After so many months, I pinned Karina in PRIME. I lasted to the very end of a match with five of the best. I beat the odds in a single night. Who else could say they'd done that? Certainly not Lucky Hoyt Williams, or Jon ''I Rock...ed in 2001'' Kano. Fuck the smarks and the internet - I just let everybody know that it's MY time to shine. I proved that I can contend, and I didn't have to die to do it. You think you know, but... In The Words of ShesusSeptember 4th, 2005''You have no idea...'' Amy was staring a bell hop straight in the eye, ''How angry I'm going to be if you don't let me know where you're taking this bag.'' She'd caught the young man brandishing a bag, ready to do his job and take it to its destination - to a man whose unmistakable signature was on the handle. A man that Aimz, in true fashion, was a fan of. Someone who'd gained a lot of her consideration lately. Someone she refused to walk on by. The bell hop simply blinked at her. ''I, uhh... it...'' Scared, star-struck kids weren't what she wanted to deal with. Runarounds weren't what she wanted to deal with, either. ''Look, you know I'm not a fangirl and why would I want to hurt him? Besides, you're not aiding in anything if I just so happen to follow you to his room.'' ''Uhhh...'' The tiny redhead reached into her pocket, sighing and shaking her head in front of the 6-foot-something attendant as she produced a Canadian fifty dollar bill. He stared at it for another moment before taking it from her hand for examination and then snapping into sudden proper posture with a smile. Rather suddenly, he seemed to be inclined to form words. ''It's been a pleasure doing business with you, ma'am... follow me!'' Money talks, and Amy walks. Her footfalls were cautious - she didn't want him to be alarmed when the bell hop arrived sounding like he had two separate footsteps. The bellboy knocked on the door and dropped the bag, leaving Amy alongside it as he hurried away from the scene - not wanting to invoke the wrath of more than one pro wrestler a day. He left her to wait on the doormat with no signs of life inside. Minutes that seemed like lifetimes passed before she got up the courage to knock herself. ''Who's there?'' came the voice from inside. His voice. She needed an excuse, and quick so, true to herself, Aimz put aside her dignity and muttered ''Housekeeping?'' in a meek, masked tone. The door opened, and it was time. ''Okay...'' she prepared a speech for when his eyes fell upon her, ''So I know I lied about being housekeeping...'' He saw her standing there. Now or nothing. ''Yeah, pretty desperate move coming from the person who couldn't wait to shalack me and send me away.'' ...Yeah, so Amy had paid that kid fifty bucks to take her up a flight of stairs to Killean. Don't act surprised, or like it's any of your business. In response to his harsh comment, Amy brushed a hand across her still fainty bruise cheekbone, ''You don't hit like a girl, so why act like one? I didn't expect you to appreciate or want me here, I just saw your bag and found out that you were travelling with us for some paperwork andandand...'' She paused a moment while Killean stewed, then sighed and tossed her hands to her sides. ''...And I don't know what else to say, Kill. I mean, I could apologize...'' No matter what men think they know about women, they themselves are the TRUE masters of mixed signals. Killean demonstrated that prowess by looking coldly into her eye, picking up his back and walking back into the room without a word. The mixed part of it was that he'd left the door open, and not by accident. Making some semblance of a noise, Amy goaded him to turn around. She still hadn't entered, but he spoke firmly... ''An apology would mean that you're sorry for all of this happening. Not to mention the fact that you showed up here. Sorry if I'm sounding just a little confused, but the signals I'm getting are pretty screwed.'' Then it was Amy's turn to make a move. What she did was far from in her favor; She walked in and closed the door behind her, a good three feet closer to Sirrajin as she leaned against the entry wall and closed her eyes in frustration, ''I don't know what I want, but I know I don't want this to... I don't know. To not be there, I guess.'' ''Amy...'' Killean put the bag by the sofa and walked to within five feet of the steamy redhead. He was definitely still attracted to her. The best thing to do would be to tell the truth, to be the man. ''I cared about you and I still do. But pardon my french, you did the moonwalk all over my fuckin' feelings. To be honest, I don't know what to think. You know how I feel about you.'' He knew it was all up to Amy by that time. She controlled the direction of their sinking ship, and it needed to be repaired before it ended up like the Titanic... only with a worse love story. She took a few steps forward to make sure their eyes met - so he'd know she was standing up on the same page - then turned. She controlled the direction, and hers was toward the door. She knew how he felt, yet she was still walking away. She spoke so softly, ''I'm in room ten thirty eight when you know what to think.'' With those words, she passed with the silence of the moments before a hurricane. Secretly, she wanted to ask so many questions; Why he was there, why people had told her that he was there to sign his release... Why didn't he try at all when she left him? She was so used to people like Jeph, who'd never give up. She hadn't expected so little from Killean. She...
