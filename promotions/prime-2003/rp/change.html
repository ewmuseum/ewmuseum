Date: Rev 162 There's big things going on in Angelo Frazetti's life. Big things, indeed. He just opened up All-Star Wrestling, and the first show is scheduled to go down by the end of the month. But, every new beginning starts with some other beginning's end. He will no longer be Simply Beautiful's trainer - he just hasn't got the time to take care of his promotion and his star pupil's career at the same time. SB took the news well enough. He's so locked in now that it didn't really even faze him. His hard work has finally paid off with a huge win over TEAM V.I.A.G.R.A. with partner Tony Rolo. Things are on the upswing now for him too, and he wishes Angelo the best. So, now left with only his will to get himself motivated, it's him against the world yet again. (line) BAM The image of Troy Douglas hitting his End of the Road piledriver in the TEAM Invitational Tournament flashes across SB's TV screen. SB: Son of a bitch. How did my neck not break again? The one, two, three that followed was academic, and SB was once again beaten by Troy Douglas. The best he's ever managed against Douglas is a measly draw...but that's all about to change, and SB knows it. SB: I gotta stop lettin' this bastard overpower me. This time, it's strictly stick 'n' move...like Angelo always told me to do against bigger guys. SB nods to himself after mentioning Frazetti; he misses him already. He can break film down well enough, but there's always that one extra thing that Angelo could spot...that one thing that always seemed to give SB a better chance to win. Sighing, hecracks open a fresh Heineken Light and kicks his feet up on the table. He sits silently for a few moments...his eyes close, his brow furrows, and he lets out a soft moan. He lifts up his shirt to reveal his heavily taped ribs. He hadn't taped against Team V.I.A.G.R.A. because Rayne treated the wrapping like a target the whole match. He should really go to the hospital, but he doesn't want to miss any time NOW. ANY time but now - he's on the cusp of something big, he can feel it. He knows he's being stubborn, but he has this unnatural fear that any time spent away from the business will end up with him being passed up by it. Dan Ryan broke his fucking neck, and he missed three weeks. A doctor friend of his advised him to retire, but all he did was miss two shows in total. Stubborn? You don't know what it means until you've met Andrew Rossi. Rossi presses on the injury, as if he were able to force the pain away, but it's searing. He doesn't want to get dependent on any painkillers, considering the laundry list of pro wrestlers who've thrown away their lives for their addictions, but it hurts bad enough right now to warrant popping a few pills. His hand fumbles around the table looking for the small Ziploc bag he keeps them in under the mess of magazines, trash, and other assorted junk on his hotel room desk, and after knocking off a soda bottle he picks it up. He opens it up cautiously to make sure he doesn't lose any, and drops two of the yellow pills into his beer and takes a big, healthy slug of it. SB: That oughta do it. "BLIND ME! ERASE WHAT WAS!" SB: Who the fuck could be calling me now? The International Sex Symbol flips open his powder blue Razr phone and holds receiver up to ear. Zakk Wylde's thrasher cuts short. Bummer. SB: It's SB - you're on the air. Voice: Andrew? SB's eyes light up - could it be? *Click* SB: Hello?!? HELLO?!!? Rossi tries to check the received calls list, but the number was private. But God damn him if that didn't sound like Becky. ==================================================== What can you really say about days like this? It's raining, the Yankees have off, and it's too damn early for a fantasy fooball draft, but all the magazines are out. That's bullshit, man. I wanna just fuckin' draft already, I'm pumped! It's just one of those days to sit back and tie up any loose ends while you're not busy on account of the weather. But not for SB. He's just on his way back from the Cincy-area Gold's gym now, yellow poncho handy. He looks like the Unabomber's attractive cousin. Check that. Step-cousin. His headphones are on, pumping the Red Hot Chili Peppers' "Soul to Squeeze" into his ears. He'd just as soon be on his way, but a man lying on a park bench stops and calls out to him! Man: Mister! Mister! Spare change? SB just keeps on walking, oblivious. It takes a lot to break his focus when he's out walking. Man: HEY! MISTER! SB's head whips around and he plucks his iPod headphones out of his ears. He turns to face the man, fists up and ready for a scrap. SB: Whatta you want, man? You want my wallet you're gonna have to take it! Man: I...I just wanted some spare change. SB: Oh, shit. I'm sorry man. How much ya need? Man: Oh, whatever you can do without. I don't mean to press... SB: Take this. SB hands the man a crisp new hundred dollar bill. The man looks up to him, embarrassed. Man: Sir...I couldn''t possibly... SB: Please - money only causes problems for me anyway. Maybe I can help solve yours. Man: (laughs) Uhh...could you set me up with a house? That'd solve a lot. SB laughs with him. SB: I ain't that generous. Say, you mind asking me how you ended up like this? I mean, you haven't been out on the street your whole life, have ya? Man: Oh, no. Things were different. It's been about - oh, six months since I lost my job. SB: So sorry to hear that, Man: And my wife. SB: (grimaces) Sorry. Man: And my kids, my house, and all of my money. SB: Jesus. Here, take this, chief. It's all I've got on me that's valuable. You like music? Man: Hmm...got any Sinatra? SB: You bet your ass. Man: Thank you. SB passes off the iPod too. He's got another at home, it's all good. SB: How'd you lose your job. Man: They just up and fired me one day. I guess I outlived my usefulness? SB: It's a cold world out there, I know. Man: They all say I worked too hard. Never took any breaks. I guess I just got burnt out, and before long I stopped producing like I used to...everyone around me just passed me up. I could sell sand to an Egpytian in my heyday, but near the end I couldn't sell a damn thing. (laughs) I had enough sicks days saved up to take a two month vacation before I retired. I was only five years away...(shakes his head) eh, you don't wanna hear my sob story anymore. You have a good day. SB: Right. You too, sir. SB flips his hood up over his head and walks away from the man in the park...the man who just scared the living daylights out of him with a few kind words of unintentional advice. ==================================================== "Do I work to hard?" SB has the phone pressed to his ear. His mother is on the other end of the line. He always calls her for advice. His father...well, he's not so good at it. Mrs. Rossi: Well, Andrew, some time off wouldn't kill you. Have you gone to the hospital like your father told you? SB: Yeah, I'm fine. He doesn't like lying to her, but he's not sure if he can stomach getting chewed out by her again. Plus, he's a little loopy from the painkillers. Mrs. Rossi: Ok, so why don't you take a nice, long sabbatical. Other wrestlers do it all the time and when they get back the fans still love them! SB: Right, but I'm not that popular in PRIME yet, ma. I don't wanna risk it...I just, I dunno. I just wonder sometimes, you know how I am. I don't wanna get screwed over. Mrs. Rossi: It looks to me like the only person who can screw you up is yourself. That's how it's always been, sweetheart. SB: I mean, I just don't wanna end up like fuckin' Chris Benoit and snappin' one day! Mrs. Rossi: Jesus H CHRIST, Andrew! Don't say things like that, do you want me to have a stroke? SB: Shit, I'm sorry ma. Mrs. Rossi: Andrew...baby...take a vacation, will you? SB: I can't. I...just gotta fight through this. My ribs are fine. I'm fine. I gotta go, I need to call Angelo. Andrew hangs up before his mother can answer. He punches his fist together a few times as he leans over the table, and then slaps his palms down on it. SB: I can fuckin' do this. FTB
