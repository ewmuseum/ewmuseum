The Way Things Were And Won't Be

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/2/06 The pain is unimaginable. You can’t even fathom the depth to which the word pain is applicable until you’ve worked match against the man known as Penance. We’re at the Pro Wrestling Circuit’s weekly show, PWC TV. The year: 2003. I’m bleeding from the forehead and my partner Jimmy Nguyen is worse for the wear, as well. It’s only my second week working here but I’m just minding my business in a tag team clusterfuck and out of nowhere comes this guy on a leash. Leading him is ‘the Prophet,’ Jacob Solomon, his keeper. The guy doesn’t speak; he just steps into the ring and dishes out liberal portions of ass-kickery. “Gwinn, what the fuck was that,” I wheeze at him. He’s still too out of breath to answer me. Our unit, 2GVC, has just been decimated. We were supposed to be winning the Alliance Titles, but we both ended up tossed out of the ring, with Penance as the only one left standing, the lone holder of both Alliance Titles. But right now I don’t care about wrestling or Penance or even Gwinn, I just can’t imagine a person going through this type of pain every week. After all, this is only my second week as a professional wrestler. But I know that if this type of stuff is going to become commonplace, I’ll pack my bags and head straight back to Milan. “Dude…I…I don’t know. I thought Starr told you we were winning that match.” I don’t even know the term screwjob yet, but I know that Ron Starr pulled the wool over all four of our collective eyes. We were coasting because we were supposed to win; he knew that Penance would come in and squash us, much to the delight of those ignorant blue-collar New Jersey fucks. This is the legendary Phantom Booker? I spit up a little blood and look back at Gwinn. “I think I need to go to the hospital.” I start a coughing fit as I stop talking, but Gwinn climbs to his feet and hits my back. Hard. “Haha, you’re fine. You’re just not used to it yet.” My curiosity is piqued, so I raise an eyebrow at my 2nd Generation Viet Cong comrade. “Used to what? That beatdown so bad that my ancestors in Vietnam are still shaking?” He nods and laughs. I climb to my feet as well, almost losing my balance. It was pretty excellent, though, that thrill of being out in the ring with every eye focused on you. I don’t quite know how it compares to being on the runway, but it’s up there. So now we’re chilling backstage for a moment before we’re off to the hotel. Gwinn says we’ve gotta go celebrate our first night as a team by getting black out drunk and going to a strip club. I look at the entrance and I see a flash of red. I’m taken aback and within seconds there’s nothing to look at, but this girl…woman…whatever is remarkable to look at. “Oh, that’s Wolfenden. Apparently, she’s just some rich bitch who paid her way onto the roster. I think Starr even booked her against Magnum Wolf to see if she can really cut it. I’ll go a hundred that she leaves one of the first five shows crying.” I can’t yet see the irony of Jimmy Nguyen questioning the make-up of another wrestler because I don’t know that he’ll become a headcase, skipping shows and showing up drunk to the ones he does make it to. “Yeah.” It’s not a sexual attraction, but rather a morbid fascination. Clearly, this girl is a hundred pounds soaking wet and has absolutely no chance of succeeding in a wrestling ring and yet…if Starr knows that and she knows that and everyone else in the fed knows that, what trump card does she have? Out in the parking lot, there seems to be some sort of stir amongst the fans who couldn’t scalp tickets to the show. “Dude! Check out this sick bike! Apparently, it’s that red-haired chick’s!” No matter, it appears to be time for drinks, strippers, and my shitty hotel. PWC isn’t glamorous, but it’s a step towards the wrestling career that I want. * * * * * The bottle in the center of the table was staring me in the face. Mockingly, it sat there just waiting for me to break. In a melodramatic movie, this shot would be filmed in an entirely dark room with a sole light hanging down in the darkness, the bottle looming on the edge of shadows. But this is real life so I’m just sitting in the kitchen. The bottle is sitting on top of a Blender Magazine. No melodramatics. “So…what am I supposed to do?” I speak into the phone softly, deeply suspicious that the feds will bust in at any moment. This is not MetaTrim; this is genuine ball-shriveling juice. I never thought it would come to this, but I’ve been sent home from arguably two of the biggest matches of my career with nothing to show for it. Pulse Title? In the hands of Illustrious Face-Eater, who’s quickly becoming the Dr. Claw to my Inspector Gadget. Great American Nightmare? Let’s not talk about it. As Paddy’s becoming the darling of GTT5, I’m just…stagnant. Oh Sebs, don’t worry. This isn’t another RP about how ReV matches are starting to mean a lot to me because I’m progressing further into wrestlerhood. This one’s probably gonna be about inner conflict and at the end, I learn a valuable lesson about myself and my fellow man and…wait a second…just read it, you Limey sneak! /Parks “But how traceable is it? I mean, I’m sure I could pull this off for a couple weeks while the Twinkies mourn, but…” Trailing off, I’m wondering about the possible ramifications of being caught. The evil Tsonda immediately butts in, demanding that I wonder about the possible ramifications of beating Karina Wolfenden in her triumphant return. She’s still a huge win in a PRIME ring. I know I hate her and I hate women in general and when the lights come on, I’ll explain to you just how much of a rug-munching, bra- refusing bulldyke she is. But…she’s the last Dual Halo winner, she’s a former Uni Champ, and she fucked Deville. Apparently benevolent Tsonda is out of the office at the moment. I’m nervous about the drugs. I’m nervous about being caught and being one of “those guys,” one of those guys who they’ll say “I had no idea he was on the juice, man” after the heart attack. I want to win desperately against Wolfenden, but I have an infinite desire to live. What to do, what to do. “This stuff is total underground, black-market shit, right?” The guy on the other end is one of those assets that gets passed around ring workers like a cheap Aimz. We don’t do names. Hell, we barely do faces. Just know that he’s quite cordial with several other members of the PRIME roster. He replies that is definitely illegal paraphernalia. I twist my mouth into a half-scowl. What’s the point? Evil Tsonda shrieks that the point is to succeed, to not be a doormat for guys like Face-Eater and Paddy. I see his point. “I got it. Thanks.” And the phone goes click. No more creepy guy on the other end, just a confused Chandler sitting in front of the bottle. And fuck if I know what I’m supposed to do here. I’m the bad guy and I suppose I could spin this into more heat from the fans, if it ever came out. But idiotic is neither heel nor face. Against Wolfenden, the placebo effect would probably have more impact on the match then juice started less then a week prior to the match. Evil Tsonda wonders what happens after that. He doesn’t want to play it by ear; he wants to get stronger, more athletic. He says this will lead to wins, big ones, starting with Wolfenden. Benevolent Tsonda isn’t coming back to argue with him. I can only look at the bottle, afraid of both action and inaction. In the words of Frank the Tank, I’ll do one. * * * * *I walk into the PRIME offices, vaguely aware of Blaine Blair and Hin See. The cowboy hat tilts back and forth lazily over See’s brow, shielding his eyes that search for and find everything. Even in my state of awe, I can sense those acute eyes sizing me up. I wanna make a stupid Asian joke to escape that perpetually acute gaze; I say nothing and my agent Gil Lavenstein starts to make shitty small talk. “Mr. See, it’s nice to finally see you. I know that we’ve conducted much of the negotiating with Mr. Blair, but I just want you to know that it’s a pleasure to be working in coordination with your project over at PRIME.” That’s probably bullshit and from the slight change in Hin See’s face, I can tell that the old man knows so too. I can read him just a smidgen. “Gil, it’s not a project. It’s a wrestling federation.” What an epic sonofabitch. He may be a face, but god damn the old man can ruin your day. Gil’s got a great poker face, but everyone at the table knows he just got his shit owned. Blair quickly tries to keep things civil by proceeding with the negotiations. “So we have all that we need from your side except for Chandler’s signature,” he declares. “And then you’re a member of the PRIME roster…no, the PRIME family.” “We hope that you get along with the other members of the family, just not too well that you’re not gonna kick the hell out of them on TV,” Hin See opines. He smiles modestly and then puts his head back down, content to let the intern think he’s in control. “As you know, that family includes some superstars you’ve worked with, like Hoyt Williams and Karina Wolfenden. Others like Magnum Wolf and Angelo Deville have also I stop in my tracks. This isn’t really gonna be the selling point, is it? Blaine Blair doesn’t consider me stupid enough to believe that anyone in PWC was perfectly assimilated into this massive star-powered machine that is known as PRIME. Hell, they can even afford five capital letters. And don’t forget that it doesn’t matter what “K-Wolf” or “Your Personal Jesus” did when they got here. They’ve been fawned over by marks everywhere. And oh yeah, I fucking hate them. It looks like it’s time for me to speak up. “Listen, Hoyt, Ka…okay everyone listed above can go rot in hell for all I care. I don’t want to be another PWC guy here anymore. I’m sick of being identified solely by my place in a federation that wanted to place those two no-talent hacks ahead of me on the card.” Hin See snickers beneath his hat. I know who he is and I don’t really want to get fired before I’m hired, but I might just want a fight. “Mr. Tsonda,” he softly chuckles, “Hoyt Williams is our Universal Champion and Karina Wolfenden is the reigning Dual Halo champion. It seems you don’t pay enough attention to PRIME to know that or to know that they’re two of the best ring workers to ever step foot in PRIME.” Well…I’m in a rare state of speechlessness. What can you say to a guy who’s so freaking powerful that he can have a dumbass nickname like the Asian Cowboy and not get Brokeback Mountain jokes 24/7 (hey fuck you if you call me out on this “anachronism”)? I sign the contract without another word. There’s work to do to show Hin See that I can put my money where my mouth is. * * * * * And I can’t do it. There’s more then ample reason to cheat. Hell, I cheat in the ring at every chance I get. Why not do it out of the ring too? But I can’t do it. I look at the bottle one more time and get up from the mundane kitchen setting. Don’t accuse me of having a conscience, but at every turn there’s another roadblock. Besides, evil Tsonda conveniently forgets the pledge I made to show up Hin See a couple months ago. Well, it’s 2006 and maybe it’s time for me to make good on my pledge. And not to sound like a good guy or anything, but a little part of me is mad that Hin See won’t get to mark out Wednesday night. Aww, don’t look at me like that. All you assholes better have fun digesting that big portion of fat cock you’re currently feasting on. There, that better? Does that stop all the warm cozy feelings that were just conjured up? Let’s keep it that way.
