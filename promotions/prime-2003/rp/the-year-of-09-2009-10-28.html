The Year of '09

28 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/28/09 CHAPTER 1: I hope they have KFC in heaven. “That attaboy,” I yelled at a cartoon caricature of Coronel Sanders. It’s another rainy night in Chicago but one thing is for sure the KFC on Harlem is packed. At least he’ll be remembered for as long as humans eat fried chicken, and by the current rate of consumption and enjoyment I would guess that may be forever. Great authors eventually are forgotten. Musicians who capture the heart and soul also erode in time. Every generation has its artistic icons that trample over the past in a consistent cycle of recreating what’s already been done by God and the Romans a long time ago. Even old wrestlers are long forgotten as you’d be hard pressed to find anybody under thirty who can pick Gorgeous George out of a lineup. Even the guys I grew up idolizing are either forgotten or a parody of their once splendor. I think America like an old wrestler is also becoming a parody of its once splendor. I like that line. Being recognized has always been a passion of mine. When I’m in the ring I want all eyes on me, when I’m on the big screen I want to captivate the viewer, and when I’m in bed I want to mesmerize my lover. It’s an ego, but it’s a healthy one and let’s face it things in good health have been hard to find lately as a killer swine flu has invaded my one nation under God. It’s true I need to shake off the ring rust and get ready to recapture my fallen star in PRIME by winning the Jewel in The Crown tournament. But the world needs me more than ever, and to be honest, I love the feel of being in demand. God sent me a panicked S.O.S. in the form of an ass crack of a bent over Plummer fixing my leaking sink drain yesterday. A talking ass crack is sobering symbolism for a savior high on gin and tonic and I didn’t really appreciate it. As a matter of fact just thinking about it makes me want to grab my sterling Burt and Ernie Flask. I took a drink. “Hey,” yelled a cop, “you can’t drink dat on da streets.” “Drink what,” I questioned. “You a wise ass?” “No, but you’d drink too if you saw a talking ass.” “I feel like I’m looking at one.” That made me mad. That indifferent kind of mad that has swept the amber waves of grain where its more on the surface then anything more, like when Derrion Albert was beaten savagely to death by gang members as fellow peers watched on. Another student videotaped it all for the top story at ten. What could I do? What should I do? I took another drink. The bastard charged at me as I just laughed like a horror movie psychotic becoming sexually aroused by the horror he was committing. A sick twisted laugh you might expect out a degenerate like Chainz but not from a Savior like me. He hit me with club right to the stomach knocking the laugh out of my throat and as quick as lightening I had cuff on and I found myself in the back of Chicago Police car no longer finding humor in the situation. An hour later and forty pounds of bureaucracy produced paperwork I find myself behind the cold steel bars of Chicago’s finest holding cell. Normally I wouldn’t give a shit but the talking ass crack sent by GOD himself told me I had less then forty-eight hours to eliminate the Swine Flu or not only would the Jewel in the Crown tournament would be cancelled, but so would the good in humanity. I needed the money and couldn’t afford for the tournament to be canceled so I used my only phone call to ring my childhood best friend Larry Gorka. In recent years Larry had become a distant friend of mine and a notoriously dangerous biker. CHAPTER 2: Holy Moses! The authorities let me out in Larry’s custody which I wasn’t sure was such a good thing as he looked coked up and crazed. He was wearing his black biker boots, blue jeans, and a well worn McCain for President t-shit. Not because he was a Republican but rather because he was a racist. I reached for my flask but it was gone no doubt now the property of Officer Asshole. “Me bailing you out,” Larry taunted, “my how the mighty have fallen.” “I didn’t fall Larry I simply stumbled and you’re not bailing me out you’re giving me a ride to Gary, Indiana to meet up with an Angel named Biff.” “Are you high?” “High on Jesus,” I proclaimed. God told me I had to get to Gary in order to meet up with this angel, who could point me in a direction on how I, a mere Savior, could save humanity yet again. Larry was dangerous but a hell of a fast driver and at this point speed was needed over safety. Speed was needed, and some Beano as I farted right into the now cruising bright yellow Mustang. “Jesus fucking Christ you stink boxing me,” Larry blasphemed. “Easy, you’re talking about my family.” “It smells like you’re family is dead and deep up in your ass.” “Excuse me. There I said it. Let’s not forget Jesus died for ALL our sins including the occasional dirty joke told by my ass.” “Dirty joke my ass,” Steve responded as he handed me a little blue pill, “take one of these.” I popped it in my mouth and swallowed hard. I’ve never needed water to take pills. To me it’s just a fucked up need to get the task over with. Most of my life I’ve learned just to swallow whatever is thrown at me, except for penis because I know that is what you are thinking sinner. Shallow the hard times, the good times, swallow religion, swallow information, and swallow as many pills as needed to just feel numb. It’s a feeling a lot like spirituality only with a much rougher landing. My world started to wobble. “Give me what say you gave me,” I mumbled, “this isn’t beano!” The same fucking laugh I laid upon the over masculine, under sexed, cop just hours ago Larry was now lying upon me. I watched Chicago pass through the windows as I saw the bloated head of the mayor weeping over the loss of the Olympics. I passed by the burial of Derrion Albert as non influential black faces cried over his coffin. I even saw Mrs. O'Leary's cow the one that started the great Chicago fire and jumped over the moon. Chicago was in the distance and Scary Gary Indiana was on the horizon as was Halloween. Scary times indeed. “A nigger and a monkey are hanging out on a tree,” Larry started up, “what do you call the monkey?” “Come on now.” “Branch manager!” Larry again laughed in the sickness of his own joke as he lit a cigarette while doing a hundred down the Bishop Ford. The interesting thing about Larry is he really cared about people. If a black person was hurt or being treated poorly he would defend and help them. He was a freedom fighter who somehow, somewhere in his life found comfort in separation. To him from a distance he found humor in a very primitive kind of way. We fear what we are not, and what we don’t understand. It’s the easiest and safest thing to do as it forces the mind to approach with caution. Say what you will, but organized religion isn’t dying because of black or brown skin but rather because of old white men. Maybe it’s time we start to reprogram how we look at the world. Just don’t start by looking at me, Hoss. “Where are we meeting this Angel,” Larry questioned.” “The cross roads.” “You Jesus freaks are all very predictable.” “I don’t set the rules I just play by them” Larry went on for the next hour telling me about every woman he is currently fucking. Weird, kinky, degenerate sex that would either repulse me of make the Hoyt of my pants raise from the dead. About four of the stories involved him fucking married women while their husbands slept upstairs. Out of sight, out of mind I reckon. As the son of God that wasn’t my thing, but as a creature of the earth I love my women. At the end of it all he told me how all these girls wanted him but he couldn’t get the one girl he wanted. I asked him who that was and he shocked me when he told me his recently divorced ex-wife. C'est la vie so mote it be. We arrived in Gary at the crossroads which turned out to be a bar and not an actual set of roads just as my high was thankfully wearing off. I kissed the ground like a pope off a plane as I wasn’t sure I would ever feel the living earth again after that car ride. The place was a rundown shack of a building with rotting wood as its exterior. But the sign read ‘open’ and God usually steers most people in the right direction when they are willing to listen. The inside wasn’t much different than the outside as the floors and walls were made of old rotting wood. The place had a smell of….rotting wood and urine. A place the health department either didn’t know existed or was too scared to enter. Gary, Indiana was a ghost town long forgotten after Michel Jackson and the factories faded away. Just violent criminals, immobile poverty, and shanty houses remain. A sad portrait of real America you never would see Norman Rockwell illustrate. The kind of place roaches searched for motels to kill themselves in. “Whatcha having,” asked the Bartender. The guy behind the bar was old enough to remember the first coming of Jesus Christ. “You look familiar,” the bartender said, “do I know you?” “Maybe my brother. I’m here looking for somebody, have you seen anybody different around here?” “Just you two and the mailman Elroy, but he be gone now. No loitering you drink or go.” “A bottle of whiskey,” piped in Larry, “your cheapest bottle.” The thought of getting into the Mustang with a drunken outlaw suddenly supplied my head with horrible images of terrible traffic accidents show to scare the youth into responsibility and compliance. Larry was like a wild dog so it was in my best inertest not to stop him from his vice even though I knew it was a mistake. I tested a stool to make sure it wasn’t going to collapse before sitting down on it. “What ya boys doing in town,” asked the bartender. “Looking to kill the Swine Flu before the dirty Swine kills us all,” I snapped back as I sipped my whiskey. People never understand war as it goes against every cultural teaching we are programmed with. It’s incomprehensible to most people to understand, that sometimes words can’t stop the basic animalistic core of survival. Killing and death is as much a part of our planet at every level of life, and it is just part of the machine we must maintain. Killing a child on a playground for no reason is not war. It’s horror. The holocaust was not war. It was evil and beyond comprehension. War is something that has a justifiable end that can only be achieved by beating the bastards before they beat you in their equally justifiable reasoning. The deaf man sometimes needs to hear the blind man’s song. The swine flu was something evil as it attacked children first. God warned me that it was about to escalate and he felt as if the devil had something to do with it. The President declared a national emergency out of political gain for his bumbling health care plan. But he wasn’t doing enough. At least not in my partisan glare. Truth is I had very little to go by and I’m starting to get frustrated that this angel isn’t here yet. “I’m telling ya boy, you sure do look familiar,” the bartender repeated. “Uncle Moses,” I said, “I thought that was you. How have you been?” The bartender was indeed a reincarnation of Moses and my uncle. This must be some heavy shit for God to send down the big dog. Something was up and I was about to find out. Larry was lost in his whiskey which was a good place for him at the moment as it was all about to get really weird and the only way to deal with it if you’re not a God is to have a belly full of booze or a head full of acid. Moses got right into it. “Here’s what we know. The flu was natural but the devil is using it to turn the entire world into zombies. You see he increased its strength and he will continue to do so unless we can stop him. This naturally produces fear, and the stronger it gets the stronger that fear will become. The flu really isn’t that dangerous but he will use the media to paint the picture he wants you to hear. When people panic they do foolish things and that is what he is counting on. Satan is behind the vaccination shots.” “Why,” I wondered. “When you volunteer to accept a shot Satan has every right to plant his evil into you. The shots are what is dangerous and if mass hysteria hits as it’s suspected to in the next twenty four hours, the world is quickly going to become an evil place.” “You don’t say.” “Oh I do. So the only hope is to somehow find the factory where Satan is manufacturing this evil and stop him. Maybe even spread through the churches that this is nothing more than an evil ploy perhaps we can call the news stations.” “Getting it out to the masses won’t work as we’ll just be written off as Christian scientists and crazy. Anytime religion goes up against science we lose because humanity is at the point they don’t’ trust God or think they are better than him.” The world in the last fifty years has moved quite aggressively away from religion. The very tide the bound groups into societies and later nations is quickly coming undone. If religion goes the way of good country music and well written television something will need to fill in as a new bonding agent to keep progress possible. My guess is technology would become the religion of the future. A Neil Postman type worship of massive corporations who will supply free media and entertainment to their followers keeping them subservient and connected and the bosses in power. “Where do we start,” I asked? “All God was able to come up with was the number nine.” “Nine? Nine is the number that comes at the end of journey symbolizing a full life, the final digit. Nine hours Jesus spent on the cross before leaving humanity. It symbolizes the power of the holy trinity and is three (the most perfect number) three times (3x3=9) representing law, order, structure, and progress. Let’s see what else is Nine? Oh. Jesus visited the apostles nine times after his resurrection. But what does all this mean? Is the year of ’09 really the end?” “New Hampshire,” Larry slurred. “What,” I shot back? “The ninth state admitted to the union was New Hampshire.” In the obscurity of the situation my drunken friend wasted in the sight of Moses and the Second Coming of God at a bar in Indiana just struck gold. It actually made sense as a lot of weird things had been coming out of east. With no other leads it was where we needed to be and so the journey begins. Chapter 3: NINE I parted ways with Larry back in Illinois and headed solo to New Hampshire on PRIME’s dime as a ‘promotional’ trip. More to come.
