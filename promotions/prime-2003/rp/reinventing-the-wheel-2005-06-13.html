Reinventing The Wheel

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-13-05 Bridges were marvelous things, when one really thought about them. Before they came into existence, crossing large gaps was nearly impossible. One would have to find a way around the river, or the cliff. Still, they make some people nervous. What would happen if the bridge collapsed, or an explosion took a chunk of it out? What would happen if you fell over the edge, hundreds of feet to the canyon bottom or icy-cold water? Most rational people would never find out. But for some irrational people, it would be their final discovery. These men and women are called the suicides. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ “This place is a fucking hellhole,” Justin Williamson spat, as he looked around him at Middle of Nowhere, Manitoba, Canada. As if that wasn’t desolate enough, it was the middle of the night in the middle of nowhere, meaning that the current population was, well, Justin Williamson. The only light illuminating the road came from Williamson’s rented Civic, headlights illuminating a small suspension bridge crossing above some God-forsaken body of water than Justin didn’t know the name of. Reflecting off the metal beams of the bridge, the headlights showed Williamson where he was going to be going very soon indeed. Unceremoniously, Williamson pulled the Civic off the road. He heard a loud pop as a rock punctured one of the rental car’s tires, but the former Deville’s Disciple really didn’t give two shits. He threw the car in park, and reached over into the passenger’s seat, groping blindly for his only accompaniment on this evening. It didn’t take long for him to find it, either. A thick length of rope was in Williamson’s hands, a length that he coiled around his arm for easier carrying. With this task finished, he opened the door to the vehicle, exited, and slammed it shut with authority. “FUCK!” came a scream from deep within him, as he realized that he had locked the keys to the Civic inside. “Oh well,” he whispered, solemnly, eyes closing. “Not like it matters.” To Williamson, nothing else mattered, either. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ This is how it’s going to end for me. In fucking Manitoba, coldest goddamned place on Earth in the winter, fuck Antarctica. Ah well, last time I’ll ever feel cold before simply BEING cold. I don’t know how I came to this conclusion. Really, it doesn’t matter though, does it? I’m about to off myself. Do the stupidest fucking thing in the world that I promised myself I’d never do. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid would have rather chopped his own testicles off with a rusty butter knife than kill himself. He would have screamed in pain as he castrated himself, but he would be alive. And then, he would go put his balls in the mouth of whoever was stupid enough to piss him off. But I’m not him. I’m Justin Williamson, a 25 year old man who can’t take any more. I dropped the hardcore shit to win, and I’ve proven quite thoroughly I can’t win without it. Really, it’s my own fault. In fucking over Angelo Deville, I fucked myself. I HANDED the one thing that let me believe everything was okay, the PRIME World Tag Team Titles, over to Los Diablos. And with those belts gone, I had to do shit on my own again. And I failed, miserably. Just like I failed against Karina. She almost got the job done in Rush Pro Wrestling. Snapped my neck almost in two. But I came back. For some idiotic reason, I got the idea I could come back and wrestle as well as I could before. I really am an idiot, aren’t I? I’m not 21 anymore. Sure, I’m only 25 now…but in four years I took more punishment than most human beings will take in a lifetime. Second degree burns. Hundreds upon hundreds of stitches. And a broken fucking neck. That’s not even counting the minor shit. Like the fact that it hurts to walk. Like the fact that my face is so scarred that no woman could ever love me. Wrestling takes from you, it does. It takes your health. It takes your body. And it takes your soul. Hmmm, maybe I should write that down before I go hang myself off of this fucking bridge. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Both of Williamson’s eyes shot open, focused directly on their target. This bridge fit every criterion Justin wanted for his final moment. It was high above the river that it spanned, probably thirty or so feet. It was made of metal, so there would be something to tie the other end of the rope to. It was too dark out to make much else of the bridge. He didn’t even know its name. All that he really knew was that he had wanted to find a bridge to hang himself from, and this one would do just fine. After all, he’d already been searching for a few hours. “Really hope this’ll work,” Justin said, eyeing the metal beams carefully. “Considering the complete lack of everything in Manitoba, if this bridge isn’t suitable I won’t find another one for a day and a half.” Grinning ever-so-slightly at his own dry wit, Williamson walked onto the road, giving one last look at the rental car that he was leaving in the bushes. Once someone found him, the authorities would be called in, and the tale of what happened would be crystal clear. Nervous legs carried him quickly to the bridge itself as he pondered the chain of events. He decided that he would be cut down from the bridge, or more likely pulled back up and his body would be searched. Justin had left all of his ID in his car, so such a search would be fruitless. As the Mounties canvassed the area, however, they would find the car. Then the calls would go out. To PRIME, telling them that their challenger in this week’s 5-Star Title Match would be unable to make it. From there, the news would be leaked to various wrestling websites, who would post the news. From there it would trickle down to message boards, where Justin Williamson would once again be relevant. The fans would discuss all of his accolades. They would discuss his hardcore wars, his reign as IWO North American Champion. His four UWF THC Championship runs. Hopefully, they would forget the fact that now, he was a sad sack of a man with fused vertebrae and a lack of meaningful victories. And after a brief period of mourning, everyone would move on. Nova would get a new opponent for his match. The net would find new topics of discussion. And Justin Williamson would be forgotten. Just how he’d planned it. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ I’ll be free then. Free from my own doubts and worries. Free from having to come to grips with the fact that I’m nothing now. Sure, I can still wrestle. I can give people a good point. But if you can’t win a fight, why bother fighting anyway? Each match I lose makes my pain more acute. It makes me more stressed, more angry, more nervous--more everything. In turn, that makes me lose more. Whether or not the company knows it, PRIME is taunting me now. Taunting me by giving me a shot at Nova and the 5-Star Title. A shot at something I want, but can’t win. Nova’s the PTC Extreme Champion. That makes him the second-best competitor in all of PTC. There’s only one person better than him. Karina Wolfenden. The PTC Global Champion. Fucking figures, doesn’t it? In years past, I would have devoured Nova. I would have joined PTC’s Extreme Division, fought my way to the top with lead pipes, flames, and barbed wire. Left bodies broken behind me in tables and panes of glass. And then I would have fought Nova in the most violent concoction my mind could envision, and then I would take his title from his bleeding hands, and raise it high for all to see. I would be the King of Hardcore, as trite and overused as that honorific is now. But now? Nova would destroy me in a hardcore environment, as I refuse to use weaponry. And he would destroy me if I were to meet him in the ring at ReVolution because I am a man who cannot win. This wasn’t supposed to happen. Dropping the moniker of the Trapezoid and the senseless violence was supposed to make things better, not ruin them even more! I guess in the end, what I have learned is that the world is governed by senseless violence. And in the end, it is the only thing that can set me through. I chose the wrong path. Were I the Trapezoid now, this would not be happening to me. I would not be climbing onto the very edge of the bridge, ready to step off into the void with a noose around my deck. But I’m not. So I am. ++++++++++++++++++++++++ Justin gulped as he looked down. Thirty feet was quite a long distance in the obscurity of darkness. Really though, it was only double the distance of many of the falls he had suffered off of cages. But under the circumstances, it may as well have been thirty-thousand. Williamson stood with his back pressed against the railing, as he began to uncoil the rope from around his arm. From here, his task was simple. Tie a slipknot to create the noose, and slip the noose around his neck, before attaching the other end to a bridge beam. Nice and easy. With shaking hands he held the rope, trying to get his knot done. “Damned nerves,” he croaked, fingers trembling as he slid the rope between them, trying to make sure his knot would be secure and ensure his proper hanging. The rope slipped from sweaty palms, and bounced off of his foot. Before Williamson quite knew what was happening, he was minus one length of rope. Not to mention being minus some of his nerve. He took a deep breath in, and blew it out. The loss of rope was unexpected, but he could still kill himself. All he had to do was take one step off of the bridge, and his body would be carried down into the river, never to be seen again. So easy, in theory, yet so hard in practice. A foot nervously rose off of the bridge, extending out over the precipice. All that remained was for him to lower it enough, or for a gust of wind to off-balance him, and his goal would be achieved. “Come on,” he told himself, voice growing in volume. “Let’s get this fucking OVER WITH!” But he couldn’t. Try as he might to fling himself off of the bridge, he couldn’t. +++++++++++++++++++++ Dear God, what the hell am I DOING?! I’m supposed to throw myself off of this bridge in Manitoba. The pain’s supposed to go away. But you know what? I think I’ve decided on a last minute rewrite to this script. The more I think about it…the more I realize there are so many people I let win by stepping off of this bridge. I let Angelo Deville have the satisfaction of believing him defeating me inspired me to end my life. I let Karina Wolfenden having the satisfaction of knowing that no matter how hard I tried, I couldn’t defeat her. I let Nova have the satisfaction of not facing Justin Williamson on ReVolution. But most importantly., I let the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid have the satisfaction of being right. I can allow none of these. Angelo, Karina, Nova, and the HIT will not defeat Justin Williamson. I must defeat each of the first three in order to defeat the damnable Trapezoid. Nova happens to be the first of them I can do something about. ReVolution 66, in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. A chance to yank my career away from the precipice. A chance to become a champion again. No longer am I at the end of my rope. I don’t even HAVE a rope. The Deville/Williamson alliance may be no more, but my Manifest Destiny remains. And it’s telling me that the 5-Star Title is coming home with me. ++++++++++++++++++++++ Moments after his epiphany, Justin Williamson was power-walking back to his Civic. His head shook from side to side, unable to believe the mistake he nearly made. “Note to self,” he muttered, “never be emo again. Who the fuck am I, Aimz? Karina Wolfenden? No. I’m Justin Williamson, the next 5-Star Champion of PRIME.” Closer and closer to the car he came. It was about then that he realized that the keys were still inside the car. The locked car. “MOTHER-FUCKER!” Rage tore through his body for a few moments, before he calmed enough to reach into his pocket, pulling out a cellular telephone, beginning to dial for aid. He had survived himself. Surely, he could survive a few more hours outside.
