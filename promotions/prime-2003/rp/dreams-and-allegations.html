Date: ReV 140 He swallowed the last of his beer, clinking the empty bottle against the wood finish of the bar. A nod to the bartender before Tyler Rayne pushed himself from the barstool, adjusting the fit of his favorite brown leather jacket over a white t-shirt. His boots fell heavy against the hardwood floor, both hands rising to push through the swinging saloon doors. Tufts of frozen breath fogged from his lips, the tiniest of snowflakes falling from the sky alongside a generous offering of lotus blossoms. He failed to notice that, despite the snow, it wasn’t really that cold at all. The fog rolling from his mouth was now the byproduct of a half-smoked cigar he held in place with two fingers. Tyler paused, only a block or so from the bar, senses flaring with the tingle of foreboding. His eyes scanned the rooftops and alleys. He stared at shadows, waiting for them to move. Then it happened. From every angle imaginable, black-clad ninjas began to fall from the sky, armed with all the sharpened weapons of their trade. Dozens, maybe upwards of a hundred ninjas began to surround Tyler Rayne, ensnaring him within a pulsing circle of imminent death. He smiled that patented rogue’s grin, taking a last puff from his cigar before flicking it at the closest edges of the circle. His head tilted to either side, neck rolled around the ring of his shoulder. Sufficiently loosened, Tyler fell into an unconventional fighting stance, the smile growing even wider than before. SNIKT!! Three adamantium claws slid from between the knuckles of both hands. “Who’s first?” The circle collapsed in an instant, every ninja attacking at once. The first one took three claws through the throat. The second lost half his face. After that, it all kind of went red. Limbs flew here and there. Ninjas were eviscerated and sliced and hacked and decapitated. That’s not to say that the assassins did not get their share of shots in. Tyler’s jacket and shirt had been ripped away, leaving him a bloody, shirtless mess, but nothing seemed capable of stopping his attack. Ninja after ninja, Tyler Rayne sliced his way down the street, leaving a trail of blood and corpses in his wake. But the ninjas refused to relent. An explosion threw Tyler from his feet. He landed with a thud on the concrete, the bodies and pieces of ninjas raining down around him. With a grunt he pushed to his feet, turning to see what had caused the explosion. “The fuck…” Standing in the middle of a crowd of ninjas, blasting away with a plasma beam fitted to the arm of her metallic bounty hunting space suit, was none other than Samus Aran herself. It was an odd scene, the bane of Metroid existence blasting away at the minions of Elektra. (Or the Skrulls, not exactly sure who’s running The Hand nowadays, but that whole mess is far too complicated and confusing to get into here). A particularly bright blast from the plasma launcher illuminates the helmet behind which Samus has always hidden, and in that brief flash, Tyler is given the unbelievable privilege of glancing the face beneath. “Uni?” Yes. That’s right. The face beneath the Metroid busting armor belonged to none other than PRIME’s own Universal Champion, Lindsay Troy. She continued to blast away at the ninjas, disintegrating some and freezing others, (depending upon the particular power-up she chose to use). Tyler, now recovered from his momentary stupor, jumps in to join her, slashing and slicing wildly at the ninjas with his adamantium claws. Behind him, the blood begins to rise, creating a sort river of crimson that floats over the battle, flowing past them and into the darkness beyond. As the river takes form and begins to flow more steadily, it hardens, taking on more colors as it transforms into a beautiful rainbow. The rainbow leads up over the battle and down to a stage, similar to a set from Woodstock, though surrounded by dozens of screaming little emo kids. On stage, Shawn Colvin rockin’ out an acoustic guitar while the kids break out the lighters for ‘Sunny Came Home.’ A portion of the rainbow splashes open, two red dolphins leaping from the colors to rise above the scene of battle. Upon the red dolphins are a pair of riders holding hands as their aquatic steeds lead them over the danger zone. Devin Shakur and Sun Tzu pause just above the two combatants, staring down at the clawed Tyler Rayne and Lindsay Troy in her bad ass spacesuit. “…” “…” “Oh! I love this song!” And thus the dolphins leapt away, riding the tide of the rainbow down toward the concert below. With them, the ninjas vanished also, leaving Tyler Rayne, who was now wearing a black tuxedo, standing alone with Lindsay Troy, (oddly enough, sporting her ring gear). In his hand were a dozen roses. He handed the flowers to Troy, a sudden roar of approval from the concert crowd turning his attention. Something warm flowed over his finger, and Tyler turned back to find he was now holding the handle of a knife, and not the bouquet of flowers. Lindsay Troy had turned her back to him, now much shorter and with darker hair. Not like Lindsay Troy at all. The knife left his hand as the woman turned, the wound still seeping a thick, dark blood. She turned to him. A pair of gray, lifeless eyes stared dully into his awestruck face. “Lin.” He gasps, sitting up in bed without even realizing where he’s at. Sweat runs in rivulets down the sides of his face, dripping off his cheeks to splash like little raindrops against his thigh. Tyler Rayne runs a hand through his drenched hair and takes a series of deep breaths to compose himself. “Fuck.” His head falls, landing in his palm, the elbow propped against a raised knee. He sits there for a long while, collecting his thoughts, trying to shake the image of her lifeless, accusing eyes. Sensation begins to return to his body, a chill shaking him as the perspiration cools in the air of an open window. He raises his head, looking around the darkened room to speak to no one. “I need a god damn drink.” He pushes the sweat-soaked sheets away, groaning as he stands. Every inch of him hurts. Tyler stretches, his naked body glistening in the moonlight streaming through the window, wincing at the sharp spike of pain that courses through his arms. With just the slightest of limps, Tyler Rayne leaves the bedroom and proceeds down a short hall to the kitchen. He takes a wide, shallow glass from a cabinet, ripping open a packet of Alka-Seltzer and tosses one of the tablets into the bottom of the jar. He opens the fridge, popping the top on a can of ginger ale, and turns to find Katterina Wylde staring at him from the other side of the counter. “ ‘Nother dream?” He nods, eyes scanning the counter for a bottle he can’t seem to find. She smiles, raising a hand to showcase the tequila bottle clutched within. He takes the outstretched bottle with little hesitation. “Thanks.” A generous pouring of vodka over the tablet, followed by the addition of some ginger ale. Tyler places his hand over the glass and lifts it from the counter just a bit before slamming it back down, shaking the contents into a volatile reaction of fizz and bubbling gasses. The entire concoction goes down in one gulp, and he’s already busying himself with mixing a second before Katterina has a chance to speak. “That bad, huh?” He answers her by drinking a third. “I’m not one to talk, Ty, but maybe you should back off. You look exhausted enough as is.” How could he not be? He was traveling coast-to-coast with PRIME, working his way up the ranks in the gods knew how many triple-threat and tag matches, not to mention the random public appearance or two and frequent meetings with the marketing department, (thus far, the company was at a stalemate to produce merchandise that both Rayne and parents would approve). The business itself was exhausting enough without constantly getting into bar brawls and ninja fights every other week. Then there was all the extra travel he’d clocked in; trips to Japan and Hawaii and a dozen other places in between. The fact of the matter was, the last month or so had been the most physically taxing series of weeks he could recall. “How’s the kid?” Drake Roberts, an eyewitness to a triple homicide in Tokyo, had been kidnapped after his return to the States, and only recently rescued by our unlikeliest of heroes. Currently, he was locked up in the second bedroom of the small house the three currently occupied. Katt shrugs, taking back the bottle of tequila and pouring a shot of her own. “It’s three in the morning. I figure he’s fuckin’ asleep.” “We got any tea?” She shakes her head. “Piss.” With an aggravated grunt, Tyler marches out of the kitchen and toward that second bedroom. He pulls the key from a hook on the wall and unlocks the door, flipping the switch to illuminate Drake’s room. The younger man groans, rolling over to stare through sleep heavy eyes at the door. “Who the…are you naked? Is this a dream?” “Yes. No. Get up.” Without another word, Tyler leaves the doorway, stalking around the couch and disappearing down the hall. A minute later, the sound of running water can be heard from the bathroom. Katterina pushes herself from the bar, carrying a mug back to the living room, where Drake is lowering himself down to the couch. She hands him the mug and he takes a sip, almost immediately spitting the liquid back out. “The fuck!” “Not a tequila fan, I see.” She takes the mug back. “Not when I just…don’t you people have coffee or something?” “We have alcohol and herbal tea. But we’re out of tea.” “Great.” He rubs his eyes and forehead, runs a hand through his hair. “So what’s with that guy?” “Tyler?” “Yeah. Sure. He’s, um…weird.” “He saved your ass, kid. I’d be a little more appreciative, I were you.” “Guy rescued me from crazy Japanese people so he could bring me back to…wherever the fuck we are and lock me up in a bedroom while you two do the deed all day.” “We don’t—” “I fuckin’ heard you.” “You heard me. We don’t…Tyler and I aren’t…” “Does he know you’re cheating on him?” “I…first of all, it was a vibrator you little cunt. And I only did it out here to give you somethin’ to spank to. I wanted you to hear me. Second of all we don’t…he’s not my type, okay? Like it even fuckin’ matters to you.” “You’re right, it doesn’t matter. All I want is to get the hell out of here and go home.” “Not happenin’, kid.” The two of them turn to see that Tyler has re-entered the room, pulling a white t-shirt over his head to compliment his jeans and boots. “I’m about tired of your—” “Your parents are dead, kid. Murdered. You watched your girlfriend die, and if it hadn’t been for me, you’d probably be halfway to meeting them yourself. Only person you got left is me, so don’t start pushin’ my fuckin’ buttons. When I get word from my man in Tokyo—” “I’m not going back there.” “The fuck you aren’t. You’re a witness, and I’m taking you in to testify.” “I’m not going back. The cops are…one of those guys was a cop.” “I know. What I need from you is the rest of the story.” “Are you going to let me go?” “I just told you what I’m going to do. But I didn’t go through all this trouble just to turn around and hand you over to some dirty cops. Who the fuck you think paid those Yaks to kidnap you?” Drake sighs. “Yeah. Right. Okay. Summer and I…we were lookin’ for this nightclub I’ve heard of. ‘Sposed to be a really happenin’ place, but we got lost. I convinced her to cut through this parking garage, and we stumbled right into this argument. I know some Japanese. Watch a lot of anime. So these two guys, they’re arguing about girls. I thought maybe they were drunk at first, but we stopped to listen and I heard something about slaves. I didn’t understand all of it, but they were definitely arguing about a shipment of girls. Summer tripped over this pipe, and they heard us. One of ‘em pulls a gun, and the other one flashes his badge. He shoots Summer, the cop shoots him, and he shoots the cop back. I…I couldn’t go to the police. I didn’t…I had to leave her there. I...” He lowers his head so the two can’t see his tears. “The Yakuza occasionally deal in a kind of slave trade. Asian girls, usually Chinese or Vietnamese. They buy them or kidnap them or whatever, then sell them back to wealthy white businessmen with loose morals. There’s a lot of money to be made in it, but most Yakuza prefer to keep to more honest illegal business dealings. I didn’t know, I mean, I’ve never heard about any Departments being involved as well. This is…look, Drake, we’ll go back and make it right. I’m waiting on a call. Guy I know we can trust. We’ll deal with him and him alone. We go back, you give a statement, and he’ll have cause to open this up and see how far it goes. Then we’ll get you set up somewhere safe. I’ll pay for it myself if I have to. Deal?” Drake nods, but doesn’t raise his head to speak. Tyler turns to Katterina. “I’m gonna pop out, get some tea. You be okay watchin’ him?” “I’m sure I’ll be fine. Grab another battle of Jose while you’re out.” “Yeah. Be back in an hour.”
