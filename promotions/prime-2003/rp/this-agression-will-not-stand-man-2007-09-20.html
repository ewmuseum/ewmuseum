This Agression Will Not Stand, man.

20 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-20-2007 September 20th 1:34AM Atlantic City, NJ Big Bear slowly struts out of Sue’s Pancake Shack, patting his tummy and smiling brightly. His mustache still resembles the sweet, salty aroma of hot butter and syrup. His head dons a welt the size of a tennis ball via Jason Snow’s last ditch effort to save his 5-Star Title. Big Bear walks through the doorway and turns back to make sure nobody gets a face full of wood and glass. Woman: Plea- please stop! My baby, somebody help my baby! Big Bear’s head turns, probably shifting a few discs in his neck, and starts looking around for the cries of a female in distress. He charges down to his left, glancing back and forth between opposite rows of cars; checking for anything. Big Bear: Crap! Pretty winded from the match that-won’t-be-named earlier, Big Bear lowers his head and charges like a steamroller down the way he came. This time though, he trips. Woman: Please help me! That man just stole my baby! Big Bear: Stole your baby!? Woman: YES!! My baby! Getting up and not wasting anymore time with questions, Monsoon continues his charge down the parking lot. Big Bear: YO CHUMP! The man turns, holding onto what looks to be a small child. His snarling grin doesn’t stop the Big Bear as the two lock eyes. Big Bear runs two fingers over each region of BAD ASS MUSTACHE, raising one brow and shrugging. Big Bear: Let me guess… you’re the baby stealer? With that, a rock is kicked up from the ground, into Big Bear’s hand, and then tosses it with perfect form as if Leonidas of Sparta were coaching him in his ear. The rock ricochets off of the man’s nose, right into his eye. The baby falls, but not before Big Bear makes the Sportscenter Top 10 with a diving grab. Baby: BUSS DAT CHUMP! A look of confusion spreads like Country Crocker over Monsoon’s face. He shrugs it off, setting the baby down behind the car. Big Bear looks up but finds that the coward is gone. However, he hears a familiar sound: footsteps. CUE CHASE SCENE Tearing around the corner, Big Bear spots his target sprinting with Hell behind him, constantly turning back to keep tabs on his massive vigilant. Meanwhile, Big Bear’s inner monologue rolls on… Inner Bear: That has to be Jason Snow. Big Bear: Dude, that’s not Jason Snow. He’s face now. The thief turns around, still only spotting one large man. Baby Thief: WHAT? Big Bear: Oh, I’m just talkin’ to myself. Keep running. Inner Bear: A “face” who beat you with some bush league, cockamamie Snake Eyes shenanigans! Not to mention he stole that gold from the real face! Rounding the corner, Big Bear slows up with short strides. He slams his hands on his hips, catching his breath and looking up and down the dimly lit street. Big Bear: Lost him. (shaking his head) It doesn't matter hos he won it, Snow won that match and belt. Get over it, chump. Almost on cue, a loud thunder clap rumbles seemingly right above Monsoon’s head. The sound also cues up a roaring rain storm that engulfs PRIME’s former 5-Star Contender with a rain storm that would roll through Seattle, which is ironically where this road to the 5-star strap started. Inner Bear: Perfect. What’s that!? Snapping around, the shadow of the man runs across the street and the chase begins again. This time Big Bear somehow navigates his way through pure darkness, passing by empty oil drums set afire for the homeless and night walkers of the alleyways. The footsteps remain constant, as does Big Bear on the trail; close enough to hear the man splashing through the rain soaked asphalt and puddles. Some neon lights shine up ahead. Inner Bear: See, we’re going underground! That has to be Snow. Big Bear: My money’s on Gamble, or Silver. Those are the only two who would- A swooping beam comes toward Big Bear’s head as he rolls under it. Standing up, Munson stares backward as if he was surprised at his own reaction. Suddenly, the alley is quite. Not a tick or tock from anything nearby, complete silence. Then a steady click sounds off. Then another. Click… click… click. Big Bear: Sounds like footsteps… Big Bear spins to find his target coming back for him and catching him off guard. “DING! DING! DING!” Darkness, total darkness. Inner Bear: Get it yet? Big Bear’s eyes open, looking around his room and realizing where he is. He rubs his head, shirtless and furry; he rolls out of bed and walks to the door. He opens his eyelids, still half asleep. The door opens… Big Bear: POL-PUN! Tom’s face, huge and way out of proportion, glares in at Big Bear as the room and everything in it begins to fly toward the vortex that is the doorway. Munson wraps his mitts around the top of the door frame and Walczak’s head spins around, laughing along with many little heads floating around and bobbing in then out, then in again toward the burly mammoth. The James Dean lamp flies off of the desk. Inner Bear: Not the lamp! Big Bear: James Dean! Noooo- Letting go, Big Bear’s eyes slim down and his brow furrows; diving toward the giant Walczak head and into the spinny, vortex-like madness that begins to turn and twist Munson around to the point he blacks out. Big Bear: -ooooo. SLAP. Big Bear’s eyes open, finding a pair of waitresses at Sue’s Pancake Shack standing over him. His face is wet and turning beet red, from the slap. He rubs his eyes, wiping the water form his face as well. They smirk, looking at each other. Waitress #1: See hun, just sleepin’. Here ya go, fella, some more coffee. Big Bear sits up and waves it off, grabbing the check and slapping down some cash while he still tries to get his bearings straight. He stands up, nodding and smiling at the women, and makes his exit to the parking lot. Pounding his fist into his own palm, Munson looks up at the moon. Inner Bear: Walczak… Big Bear: Walczak. With a long gaze up into the bright twilight, Big Bear’s mind and body seem to grip the meaning of his syrup and Bisquick inspired dream. His lip curls and then the scene fades to black.
