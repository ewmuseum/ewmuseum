Finding Out About One's Past...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Various Date: April 24, 1974 Location: A Small Farm House Outside of Gdansk, Poland A fragile looking woman sits alone at a table in the kitchen of a rundown farm house. She holds a handkerchief as she wipes tears from her eyes. Her breathing keeps breaking up, as she continues to cry in the dimly lit room. The front door of a house swings open, and a large man walks into the house. His face and clothes are covered in mud, as rain is seen coming down behind him. As he takes off his dirt covered jacket, he hears the woman crying in the background. "Jancia?" He speaks but gets no response. The man makes his way to kitchen where he sees the woman sitting with her face covered by her hands. He pulls up a chair and sits down next to her putting an arm across her shoulder, trying to console her. "Jancia, what’s the matter? What happened?" The man brings her in closer to him, almost engulfing her in his massive frame. She wipes the tears from her eyes one more time before looking up at the man. "Marek…I’m pregnant." "Jancia, that’s great. We are finally going to have a child." "No it’s not great Marek. It’s not." Marek is taken aback by the comment. He slowly removes his arm from his wife, and grabs her hands in his. "Why? We had always wanted a child, and now we finally have one. We have tried for years and years, and now we have what we want." "I do not want to bring a child into this world. There are so many things going on in our life, and this is not a place that a child deserves to be. The communists take more and more from us each day." "You know that we are trying to raise interest down at the shipyards. We are trying to fight against the communists." "That is the other problem. Who knows what will happen if they find out, they will take you away, lock you up, or worse." The woman buries her head into her husband’s chest as she once again starts to cry uncontrollably. He tries to calm her down, not saying anything else, because although he would like to think otherwise he knows that his wife is right. October 14, 1974 Location: A Small Farm House Outside of Gdansk, Poland The dimly lit kitchen has barely changed in the months that we had last seen it, but now the kitchen table is filled with men who seem to be in a heated discussion, with some of the men even slamming their fists down on the table. "We have to act now Marek. If we don’t act than things will just get worse." "Janek, we do not have the support to act now. If we do anything, than all that we have accomplished up to now will be lost." "Marek is right we must hold off any action." A woman comes into the room holding a tray filled with bottles of beer. The woman is none other than Jancia, who by now is nearly eight months pregnant. She places the tray of beer on the table, and gets a loving kiss from her husband. "What are you boys discussing today?" "We have a plan to bring our resistance into the public eye, but your husband, feels that doing so would not be a good idea." "I think that he is right, for what it’s worth." "Of course you do, but Jancia with everyday that passes we are in trouble of being found out." "Again Janek, if we go bring our plight to the forefront than we will be found out anyway. We do not even have a hundred members in our resistance yet, and our support is only now slowly starting to build." Marek grabs the beers and starts to pass them around the table. Jancia leaves the room allowing the men to get back to their discussion. "Janek, give it a couple of months. The shipyard is now buzzing with the information of our resistance. By January we will have enough members to finally be able to come into the public eye." Janek takes a swig from the bottle shaking his head in agreement, but his eyes tell another story. The men continue to discuss the situation hoping to bring their cause to the to the forefront soon enough. Date: December 15, 1974 A Small Farm House Outside of Gdansk, Poland Jancia is frantically running around the home picking up clothes and various other objects and putting them into an open suitcase. As she makes her way to the front of the house the door bursts open Marek running through it. His shirt is covered in blood though it does not appear to be his own. Jancia screams when she looks at him, but he grabs her by the shoulders shaking her. "Where is Tomek." "My sister just came by to pick him up. Marek what is happening you told me nothing on the phone." "We were found out. The Soviet troops burst in on us before while we were hiding out in the factory. They ambushed us, and started shooting. People died Jancia it is not safe for us they are looking for me. Hurry up, there is very little time left." Marek runs over to the kitchen table grabbing the suitcase and his wife before once again running out the front door. His wife pulls her hand away stopping right outside of the front door. "I am not going." "This is no time to joke around Jancia. There are troops heading this way right now, and there is nothing left for us here." "We cannot leave Tomek. He is a couple of days old, and there is no way that I am going to leave him." "THERE IS NOTHING MORE WE CAN DO!!! He is better off with your sister. We have to go now." He grabs the hand of his wife who unwillingly follows him. A car is waiting for them in the distance, but Marek sees troops coming in the distance. He speeds up making his way towards the car as nearly a dozen jeeps make their way towards him. Marek picks up his wife tossing her over his shoulder, and now going for a full sprint towards the cars. He makes his way to the car opening the door to the car. "BANG…BANG…BANG…" Shots ring out and Marek falls to the ground. His wife stands beside him not able to speak or move as she looks at her husband, blood slowly beginning to seep out of the side of his skull. "JANCIA!!! GET IN, WE CAN’T SAVE HIM!!!" Ignoring the man inside the car, Jancia falls to her knees shaking her husband, as if she was trying to wake him, her face still expressionless. "BANG…BANG…BANG…" Another set of shots ring out through the farmland, and seconds later Jancia joins her husband in a heap, next to the car. Knowing that there is nothing more to be done, the driver takes off getting out of the way of the oncoming troops. Date: December 15, 1974 Location: Jancia’s Sister’s House, Gdynia, Poland A woman sits on her couch rocking a baby back and forth in her hands. The baby can’t stop crying, almost as if he senses that something is not right, and no matter what the lady does, she cannot calm the boy down. "…Ring…Ring…Ring…" The phone next to the woman rings, starting her, and causing the baby to grow into an even louder crying. She picks up the phone on the third ring, all the while maintaining a firm grip on the child. "Hello?" "Yes…hi, is this…Marta Kopera?" "It is, may I ask who is speaking?" "My name is Janek…I am a friend of your sister and her husband." "Oh, hello, is there something wrong?" There is a long pause on the other end of the line, and Marta seems to know what is coming next as her eyes start to water. "I don’t know how to say this, but something happened, and the Soviet Troops…they gunned down both of them." "Thank….thank you…for letting me know." The man on the other line says nothing, but a click is heard on the other end of the line, signaling that he is no longer there. "Krzysztof…Krzysztof…" The words whimper out of the mouth of Marta, breaking up as they try to escalate in volume. A man slowly comes into the room seeing his wife tearing up. He is eating and apple, and does not seem to be overly worried about his wife’s hysterics. "What happened?" Biting into his apple nonchalantly the man still does not seem to feel that anything truly bad has happened. "Marek…..and Jancia…..are dead." At those words the lady breaks out into full out sobbing. The husband did not expect those words to come out of his wife’s mouth, and he drops his apple going over to console her. She still holds the child in her arms, but he has stopped crying not even knowing that his parents are no longer with him. Date: December 21, 1974 Location: Swiete Jadwigi Sierocinec A nun sits behind her desk writing some paperwork. She hears a knock on the other side of the door, and quickly finishes off the last bit pushing it to the side. "Come in." A man walks through the door, holding the door for his wife. Both of them are dressed in dark colors, and the woman is holding a child wrapped up in blankets. "Mr. and Mrs. Kopera, I was expecting you a little bit later." "Yes, we did not expect to catch the early train, I hope that it is not a problem?" "No problem at all." The man pulls a chair out for his wife to sit in. She carefully holds onto the child as she slowly settles into the seat. "So, first things first, I understand that the child is not yours." "No, it is my sister’s boy, but unfortunately a couple of days ago she and her husband passed away." After explaining what had happened to the nun Marta brings a handkerchief up to her eyes trying to hold in the tears. "I am sad to hear that, so you feel that you are not in the right means to take care of the child." "It is not that my wife and I feel that we are unable to take care of the child. His parents were taken away from him in very unfortunate terms, and we do not feel that him being left with us is the best thing for him. A stable and neutral environment is best, and we may not be able to provide that." "I see, well we will be more than happy to take care of the child, but we will just need to fill out some paperwork." "We understand, we just ask for one thing." "What is that?" "No matter what the circumstances, please do not tell the child who his parents were, or how they passed away." "I don’t see that as being a problem." Krzysztof nods appreciating the understanding nun, and they get to the paperwork. The man’s wife stares at the child knowing that in only a few short minutes she will have to separate herself forever. Date: August 12, 2007 Location: Brighton Beach, New York "….Mr. Walczak, you do not know who I am, but you will be very interested in the information that I have to offer you. I know that you had grown up in an orphanage, and I also know that you have no idea who your parents were. If you would like to find out more meet me outside of Vladamir’s Bakery in Brighton Beach. Be there at 3:00 in the afternoon on Sunday." Walczak had listened to this message over and over again for the past week, at least three times a day. His entire life had been sent out of whack since that message first hit his ears. Sleep had become almost non existent, and the loss at the Pay-Per-Vie had seemed only a minor detail compared to the possibility of finding out more about his parents. He had gotten to the bakery nearly an hour in advance hoping to run into this mystery man, but an hour had passed and no sign of the man. "Mr. Walczak?" Walczak turns around to see a short older man standing in front of him. The man is wearing a beige suit with a white button up shirt underneath. The shirt is only buttoned half way up, his chest hair sticking out, but the feature that immediately gets Walczak’s attention is the extravagant amount of jewelry the man is wearing. All ten fingers are covered in rings, and at least a half dozen gold necklaces are around the man’s neck. Walczak does not particularly care for the man at first sight, but if he knows anything about his parents he is a friend to him. "Yeah, it’s me, who are you?" "That is not any of your concern right now, just follow me to a place where we can speak…in private." Not liking the fact that the man is being so short in his introduction, but he knows that he holds all the power in his hands. The man looks around, making sure that no one sees him, and leads Walczak to a door next to the bakery. They head up the staircase which is somewhat dark, and does not seem to be the ideal meeting place. Once they reach the top of the stairs the man leads Walczak into a room. The room is nearly empty except for a desk. The windows are closed, and it is nearly impossible to breathe in the room. "Take a seat." "No offense, but I just want to find out more shit about my parents. I am not here to make friends, or make myself comfortable." "Be patient, all in due time, but first you will have to help me out…"
