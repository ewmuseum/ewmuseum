Trust

22 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/22/07 Trust. Titan stood on his porch, looking out at the reams of spanish moss that hung from the trees around his house and tapped his finger on the porch railing. That one word had been bouncing in his skull for weeks now. Chandler Tsonda had decided to tell the world he had turned over a new leaf and that he was a different man than the one that Titan had dealt with in the past. The big man still didn't buy the gig. From what he had been able to tell, Tsonda had been a self-involved, egomanical, egotistical jerk for quite a while. At least, that was the case from the tapes Titan had watched and the research he'd done. Was he just tired of being that way? The big man doubted it. But what was there to gain? He could be aggravating his friends or the A-List could be concoting a big scheme. And in the midst of all the suspicion, Titan was supposed to trust Tsonda and he would have a straight up match at the Great American Nightmare. Not only that, but he was supposed to trust the little pipsqueak as his tag partner this coming show. It wasn't exactly a relationship built on trust. There wasn't really animosity, but there was distrust and distaste, on both ends. To look at it any other way would be foolish. It wasn't like old IDF buddies that he trusted with his life every day. In fact, it was more like the only relationship that had held over from those days... With that thought, he turned on his heel to his left to see Profile 21 sitting there in one of the porch chairs, his feet flat on the wooden slats and his hands resting lightly on the arms of the chairs. "How long have you been there?" Titan asked. "Just a moment," 21 responded. "You're keeping your edge. It's good to see that." "Why is that?" Titan asked. "Wh-, no, I don't care. Explain to me what you're doing here." 21 just looked up at him, his gray eyes flat and expressionless. "I was in the area." "No," Titan responded, leaning back on the rail. He stood back up when it creaked and shifted to lean back against one of the support posts instead. "No, you weren't. You came here expressly. In fact, you've been coming here expressly. There hasn't been a hint of your activity in the area except when you show up here. And you took time off from your work in order to keep tabs on Yelemench." Titan smirked as a realization dawned on him. He pointed a finger at the smaller man. "In fact, you even warned me that he was coming. Tell me what that's about." 21 turned his gaze back out to the landscape in front of him. A falling sun gave the arching trees a red cast and drew the shadows underneath them long on the rich soil. "I'd hate to see my best pupil taken down by some drug dealing religious fanatic." Titan rubbed his jaw. It was almost plausible... "No," he said again, his smirk reasserting itself. "No, that's not it, else you would have shown up when I was in Israel more. You... you, little man, consider me to be the closest thing you have to a friend." 21's eyes fell back on him, slightly sharp. It was one of the few times Titan had seem expression out of the man, which meant he had hit pretty close to the mark. "Tell yourself that if you want. I talk to you because you're one of the more intelligent people I can talk to. You think I can just randomly talk to someone? Even I like social interaction, introverted as I am." "Are you under the impression I'd turn you in?" Titan asked. "What could I say? 'Yeah, there's a guy I know, but only by a code name from when I was in the IDF and he trained me about interrogation and reading people and I have suspicions he might be an assassin.' Who, exactly, would I take that story to, and who would I fool with it?" "You might find John Voight as some government official and he'd be willing to listen," 21 countered. Titan couldn't help but laugh. "I never thought I'd hear you make a joke. I've put a lot of trust into you in the past, but largely because I didn't think I had much of a choice. Now, it's just me trying to live as normal a life as I can in the circumstances, and you're still here. I could try and shut you out, or I could sit down and you and I could consider this one of the strangest friendships that ever existed." "You said 'try and shut me out,'" 21 remarked. "Yes, I did," Titan replied, taking a careful seat in one of the porch chairs. Even being ginger in his seating, it still creaked under his bulk. "I know exactly how good you are." "Exactly?" "Close enough." "So," 21 said, "what were you standing out here thinking about?" Titan couldn't help but smile. Being right was kind of nice. "Ironically enough, I was sitting out here thinking about trust." "In conjunction with whom? I doubt you'd be standing out here trying to logically quantify the value and virtues of trust." "Chandler Tsonda." "That does bear some thought." And with that conversation, Titan felt like he had finally broken through to his old instructor. He wasn't quite as pathetic as JD from Scrubs is with Dr. Cox, but really, finding a friend you didn't expect really makes you want to write a mushy end to an RP.
