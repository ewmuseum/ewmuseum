Preparing to change the battle lines

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-06-05 :::: It would appear that the landscape in PRIME is an ever changing one. Every time you turn around you see yet another 'big' name come walking down the aisle to wrestle for the PRIME federation. At this point and time, it would appear, that PRIME is the place to be in the eyes of anyone who wants to be 'known' in this sport. So it's with that idea in mind that my team, Los Diablos, steps into the ring in tag action for the first time since they were screwed out of the PRIME tag team championships by the K-Wolf. For the first time in over a month they step into the ring to do what they do better than anyone else, and that is work as a great tag team unit. But it's not like the last month they have been sitting back on their asses and doing nothing. Just a couple of short weeks ago they were kicking the hell out of people and taking down names in the Duel Halo. Proving, once more, that they are the most dominate tag team unit in the sport today. Then last week my good friend, a man almost like a blood brother to me...Baltasar stepped between the ropes to face his first singles action in the United States. It was his PRIME singles debut, a match he looked forward to and also naturally worried some what about since he is, after all, a tag team specialist, and he went out there and did himself and Angelus and myself very proudly. But it wasn't Baltasar that the PRIME federation was worried about making sure looked good. It wasn't my good friend that Hin See was worried about promoting or letting the world know was wrestling his first singles match for the company. Hin See didn't care about one of his hottest young prospects, as usual, cause he had to promote the 'big' name that he had lured into PRIME with a big money deal. It was more important to him to run around telling the world all about how this was Angelo Deville's PRIME singles debut. Angelo Deville...the man is a talent and there is no way around that. As usual, however, when it comes to this company, or American wrestling promotions and wrestlers in general, they had to make sure they went out and did everything they could to screw the foriegn wrestler. First off we get blasted on all the time for bending the rules, even having Angelus and myself removed from ringside, and yet it was perfectly fine to allow Ellie~ to stay are ringside and do exactly what everyone proclaims is so wrong for Los Diablos doing. She cheated. Don't mistake that fact. Don't try to over look it. There is only one reason that she was allowed to stick around, continuing to cheat the whole way, just like she has done her entire career. Oh yes, I am no fool, I did my homework. I know how much Mrs. Kannon-Hall likes to stick her nose into the matches and 'cheat', as they would say, to make sure he guy wins. The fans were out there cheering their heads off for her doing it. Hin See and his official had no problem allowing her to cheat to ensure a victory. Just like with K-Wolf and the now, apparently, departed Tessa Windsor...Hin See has chosen to turn his head the other way and be alright with it all. Cheating is a bad and sorry thing to do, when Los Diablos does it, but as long as it's the eye candy out there cheating it's all fine and dandy. Well let me tell you something, gringo's, it's not fine and dandy. The blatant disreguard for being fair about the carrying out of the rules has gone on long enough. Two times the officials have allowed Los Diablos to get screwed out of a win and did nothing. Two times now they have allowed it and pretended like it never happened. But you can bet your last damned American dollar that if Los Diablos had won those two matches with direct cheating, such as happened to us, we would have either be disqualified or Hin See would have run down to the ring and made the decision be over turned. You see...no way is he going to allow his 'American' money tickets to get done the way they do others. Everyone of them bitches, cries, pisses, and moans about us cheating but they go out and do it themselves with absolutely no remorse. If we get cheated out of matches, that is fine, as it's going to happen. That is how this business goes, and frankly, if your not going to do every thing to win a match...well then you just don't care enough. But what sickens me...what really just pisses my mexican ass off to no end...is the blatant racist reasoning behind the way rules are enforced on Los Diablos...and the way they are enforced for the big time, money making, American names. Well NO LONGER! The days when people like Angelo Deville and his unfaithful pet manager, K-Wolf, or anyone else gets to screw with Los Diablos and the head is turned the other way...are over. There comes a time when people realise that they must take greater measures to win a war. That time is now. :::: 'Things will start looking up, as of this week, boys.' Our scene opens to the inside of a lowrider car, that from the wide angle we are looking at it from, is purple on the outside with the name Los Diablos painted on the body of the car in a bright gold lettering. In the car sits the members of Los Diablos, with Angelus driving while Enrique lays down across the back seat and Baltasar stares out the passenger side window. All of them are in different types of workout clothing as they drive along the highway, at this point, heading toward the airport and their plan flight out for the upcoming ReVolution. 'Señor Morales I don't see how things could be looking up all that much. Once again Hin See has booked us to face his next set of 'legends' that he would like to see make a name for themselves and his company at our expense. I don't see us getting anything but railroaded as usua,' Angelus replies with a snarl as he focusses on driving down the road. 'I see your point,dear Angelus, but you must trust in old Enrique. I have made some plans, contacted some associates, and made some arrangements to make sure that Hin See stops trying to screw us over...in order to make sure his meal tickets come off smelling like roses,' a satisfied grin forms across Enriques tanned faces as he laces his fingers behind his head and rolls onto his back to stare up at the roof of the purple symbolism of the Spanish culture. 'What are these plans you have made Señor Morales,' Baltasar asks without allowing himself to look away from the window to the outside world. 'Well, my boy, your going to know those plans soon enough. Best not to spoil the surprise for you this early,' Morales replied to the larger of the two Mexican Devils with a sly cock of his head. 'Too bad you didn't have that plan in place last week...when the bigot officials of PRIME were busy screwing me out of my singles debut and my shot to prove I am so much more than a tag team wrestler,' Baltasar said with a shake of his head. 'Baltasar, my friend, you have got to quit kicking yourself over that B.S. from last week. Everyone with half an ounce of a brain left in their skull knows that the gringo, Deville, wasn't good enough to beat you. He proved that fact last week by having to be saved by his manager,' Angelus remarked as he glanced out the window to his left to see the car that had pulled along side them. 'Besides that, essa, you're going to get another shot at Deville. I'll make certain you get another shot at him in the future,' the voice of a weary Morales chimed in from the backseat where he was about to fall asleep. 'Hopefully in the near future. Deville and I have much to settle...not just in the ring,' the monster member of Los Diablos replied with a cold, heartless, manner to his tone. Both Enrique and Angelus looked toward their friend with a confused look. The statement even caused Morales to sit up in the backseat and pay more attention to his good friend. 'What the hell are you talking about,' Angelus asked. 'It's nothing important...well nothing important to you guys. It doesn't involve either of you,' came the reply of The Red Devil. 'My boy anything that is important to you is also important to us,' replied the quick tongued Morales. 'Let me just say that Angelo and myself have a past. Well, not directly, with one another but in an indirect manner. Something from Señor Deville's past, and his less than celebrated other career I might add, effected me in a personal manner,' the Red Devil replied, still not looking away from the window. 'Homes your really confusing the hell out of me. What could he possibly have done to ever cross paths with you in anyway,' asked Angelus as he glanced over at his good friend. In the passenger seat Baltasar shifted his weight to get more comfortable. Just as he did, Angelus pulled into a parking spot at the airport and killed the engine on the car. The three set in silence for a moment before Baltasar sighed, opened his door, stuck his right leg out before pausing and speaking. 'He killed something important to me.' Angelus and Enrique both just stare at one another as Baltasar gets out of the car and walks toward the terminal.
