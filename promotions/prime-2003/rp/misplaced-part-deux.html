Date: earlier that wasn't late MISPLACED (Cont'd)By The A-List FADE IN. EXT. JUNGLE - MORNING EXTREME CLOSE-UP on someone's freakishly Inhuman Eye. The pupil dilates in the sunlight that surrounds them, and after a moment of hesitation, the eye blinks. ANGLE ON TCHU jumps to his feet, immediately looking towards BRANDON YOUNGBLOOD, still trapped in the cage. He has been ranting for about TWENTY SEVEN PAGES on MSWord by now. Tchu yawns loudly, and surveys the rest of the camp which is largely unactive. Tchu steps quietly down the beech, careful not to wake anyone on this early morning. As he readies his pack for a jungle expedition, he doesn't hear the footsteps approaching him from behind. FACEY (O.S.) (cocking the hammer of a gun) I. WANT. MY. DRUGS. Tchu cautiously turns around to see the ILLUSTRIOUS FACE EATER, bags under his eyes and looking sickly. His hand is shaking, holding a black 9mm steady to Tchu's head. TCHU Now hold on, Facey. The Island will decide- FACEY THE FUCKING ISLAND? (fires a shot, barely missing Tchu's head) THE FUCKING ISLAND STOLE MY POT! What's your secret, Tchu? Why are you so happy to be here, huh? YOU KEEPING MY SHIT TO YOURSELF?! TCHU I'm just a survivor; like you, Face. The Island brought us here for a specific reason- FACEY (fires another shot) FUCK YOU, TCHU! I think you're crazy. Tell me, why did the Island bring YOU here, huh? Whoosh, mother-fuckers. Flashback to- INT. PRIME LOCKER ROOM - NIGHT. TCHU is looking desperate for sleep in his locker room, although he must get ready for the night. He puts on his wrestling elbow pads slowly, until he is interrupted by Universal Champion, CLYDE WALKINS. CLYDE Hey dip-shit. Ready for our match? TCHU Uhhhh uhhhhh can we please do it next week? I think I strained my- CLYDE No more running, Tchu! I know you've been ph34r1ng me since the day you got here, and now I'm going to TEAR YOU TO SHREDS! AGAIN! And there isn't a damn thing you can do about it. Tchu begins to cry in fear, until Blaine Blair steps in. BLAINE Hey guys, just to let you know, your match has been put-off until Culture Shock. Nelson wants to see it in Tokyo. CLYDE Dammit! TCHU PRAISE THE LORD! Whoosh back to... EXT. BEACH FRONT - MORNING Facey is looking extremely displeased. FACEY You're saying the Island protected you from Clyde Walkins? You really are a pussy, you kn- Before Facey can finish his statement, he is TACKLED by KYLE LAMEN and KARINA WOLFENDEN. They struggle to wrench the gun out of his hand, and Tchu gets to his feet to watch the struggle. FACEY (CONT'D) I wasn't going to shoot him-well, in the face-I just need my fucking drugs! LAMEN Dude, where the hell'd you get a gun?! FACEY I dunno, I stole it! He pushes himself away from the two Heroes, getting his distance and holding the gun up so not to frighten anyone. Everyone had woken up from the gun shots, and thought Facey was going on a crazy murderous rampage. FACEY (CONT'D) I mean, I'm supposed to bone a pregnant bitch out here (lord knows I have a thing for Preggos) but there isn't even a single fucking one! Then I'm supposed to kill the infiltrator guy, but he's already captured! What reason do I have to kill him now? LAMEN What 'infiltrator guy'? Who are you talking about. FACEY That douchebag who got fired for being a Madonna. I fucking hate Madonna. What's his name? Brandoid Youngboys? That guy over the- (Facey's gun goes off as he points at Youngblood, purely 'accidental' of course) ANGLE ON Brandon Youngblood, BLEEDING from a gunshot wound to the chest. YOUNGBLOOD Fuck this hurts! K-WOLF You fucking shot him. FACEY It was an accident- K-WOLF No, it's totally cool. Good job. FACEY Oh, in that case- Facey fires ten more rounds into Brandon's chest, killing him instantly. LAMEN What the hell was that for!?! FACEY Oh come on, he was a cold blooded murderer and was going to kill us all-you know it! LAMEN He was harmless! FACEY You say tomato- LAMEN That doesn't even make sense! Lamen storms off, angrily, but rips the gun from Facey's hands. He begins to walk down the beach before Karina stops him. K-WOLF Kyle! Kyle wait. LAMEN What is it? K-WOLF I think you should lie down and get some rest. LAMEN You think he should have killed Brandon, don't you? Karina shrugs. LAMEN (CONT'D) Everyone wants me to be a leader until I make a decision no one likes. K-WOLF Well, there are ways to get on people's good sides. Maybe you should, you know... NOT be a bitch? LAMEN Real nice, Karina. The only thing I'm worried about is that pipe-- Both are interrupted by the obnoxiously Southern DANNY FERGUSON, who walks up with his shirt off reading Watership Down. DANNY You the one jackin' Facey's stash, Hero? K-WOLF Danny, just go into the jungle and die- DANNY Easy, Skittles, I'm just tryin' to keep the peace, 'round here. We don't want everyone dead before we get rescued. LAMEN Rescued? Dude, it's been like a week. No one's going to find us. DANNY `S'why Duke and his boy have been working on a way to get us off. They got themselves a boat and I made sure I'm one of the few passengers allowed on. K-WOLF They made a boat? With, like, bamboo and stuff? DANNY I dunno, the man has that special boy of his. Wouldn't be surprised if they worked up some black magic. LAMEN You go work on your boat-I've got a pipe to deal with. Lamen storms off, following Tchu in the jungle. Danny and Karina both look at each other for a moment, and decide in unison that desperation will not apply here before walking their separate ways. EXT. DEEP JUNGLE - DAY. Kyle Lamen and Tchu are pretty deep in the thick of the jungle and have been walking for quite some time. They don't notice the pretty Asian footsteps approaching them from behind. TSONDA Wait for me! The duo turn around to see CHANDLER TSONDA, dressed to thrill, approaching them with a fiery step. LAMEN Chandler, what are you doing here? TSONDA Well, I figured being lazy would get me little screen time. Thank God Baltasar died, though, I didn't want to do that crazy love storyline anyways. DISGUSTING. BALTASAR (O.S.) I'M NOT DEAD YOU BASTARDS, THIS MONSTER HAS JUST LEFT ME TO DIE WITH HALF OF MY INSIDES IN IT'S FUCKING STOMACH AND NOW THAT YOU CAN SAVE ME YOU HAVE STILL CHOSEN TO DO NOTHING! WHY MUST I BE CURSED LIKE THIS?! I AM SLOWLY DYING BUT YOU ALL KEEP IGNORING ME. PLEASE, SAVE MY LIFE NOW OR PUT ME OUT OF MY MISERY!!! TSONDA So now I'm hunting. The ladies say I'm great with my hands and I figure they could be put to good use here. LAMEN We're not fondling the boar I hope you know. BALTASAR (O.S.) PLEASE HELP ME, I HAVE NOTHING LEFT TO LIVE FOR. I CAN FEEL THE MAGGOTS SLOWLY EATING AWAY AT MY VITAL ORGANS-I AM PAINFULLY WASTING AWAY. JUST PUT A BULLET IN MY BRAIN AND KILL ME PLEASE IF YOU HAVE ANY MERCY. TSONDA I know we're not fondling the boar, imbecile. Although I don't what you're doing here; I thought hunting meant being physically capable and not a Vegetable-In Training. LAMEN Right, as if you're more capable- TCHU Excuse me gentlemen, now's not the time for petty bickering. We need to focus on the task at hand. LAMEN Quiet Tchu, you haven't even told us why Paddy's dead yet. That has to be eventful in some way, we can't just pass it off as a one-off comment. TCHU He was a sacrifice that the Island demanded. Everyone has to give something to the Island. LAMEN Explain how he 'sacrificed' himself. WHOOOSH, more flashbacks. FADE TO: EXT. JUNGLE - MID DAY. This time it's on the Island, however. ANGLE ON TCHU and PADDY O'SHEA are squirming through the vast jungle, trying their damndest to catch the wild boar that runs just feet in front of them. PADDY Eye'm tarred, mate. Can we rest fer just o'moment? TCHU We have to press on, Paddy. We don't want the boar to escape or else we lose our dinner- The men are interrupted by the pig squealing incessantly, running towards then past them. The noise is overwhelmed by the sound of a loud 'hum', followed by the clanking of gears and trees falling over. Yes, TREES FALLING OVER. And it's coming right for them. PADDY S'pose we should be running? TCHU The Island will decide when its time for us to run- PADDY Fer Christ's sake JUST RUN! Paddy shoves Tchu to break into STRIDE, just as CG RAMPAGE!! busts into view, eating trees and endangered species in one foul swoop. CG RAMPAGE!! ROOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAARRRRR (Drool) ROOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAARRRRR Tchu's sense of courage that he had gained from being on the Island suddenly fades, and he shrinks into a little ball and cries in fear of the giant monster. Paddy turns back to save him, dragging him by his arm. PADDY Git up mate, we gatta git out of AHHH!!!!!!!!!! In his last act of strength, Paddy SHOVES Tchu to safety just as CG Rampage!! leans down to eat him. Tchu shoves a stick of DYNAMITE in Paddy's hand as he's being wisked away. TCHU Quick! Use the Dynamite I just found; it MUST be a plot device. Paddy throws the dynamite down the monster's throat. The monster takes Paddy a few steps away from Tchu until the blast goes off, sending both into a bloody oblivion. The scene closes in on Tchu's eye as we... FADE TO: EXT. PIPE ENTRANCE - DAY. CLOSE UP On Tchu's eye, a tear falling from it. ANGLE ON Lamen and Tsonda had just finishing hearing Tchu's story as they arrive to the hatch. TSONDA Wow, how noble of you. LAMEN Shut up, Chandler. He just lost a close friend- TCHU Actually I've never seen the guy before in my life. He was quite annoying. LAMEN Well that's...heartless. This still doesn't explain how you found the pipe. Were you exploring? Did the Island 'tell you' where to find it? TCHU Actually I just used the warp code. You know the one from Super Mario 3? LAMEN That doesn't make sen- TSONDA YOU USED THE FLUTE? I fucking LOVE that game. TCHU You're kind of supposed to. Aren't you Japanese? TSONDA I'm Italian, you ignorant spoon. TCHU Huh. So is Mario. While Lamen feels SUICIDAL due to the idiocy, the scene must... FADE TO: EXT. BEACHFRONT - DAY. FOCUS ON A part in the jungle is made as LISA TYLER runs frantically in the wide open, screaming. LISA TYLER Oh my God that was so horrible! I was held captive for weeks without being fed. But luckily you don't have to search for me any longer as I've escaped the dreaded capture of the ADDITIONALS! DUKE WILLIAMS, DANNY FERGUSON, THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER and KARINA WOLFENDEN all crowd the hysterical Lisa in an effort to console her. DUKE Quiet down there lass we need my boy to get some sleep so he can conjure me up a hooker in the morning unless you're willing to fit the bill and I don't think you are. K-WOLF Now's hardly the time, Duke. Can't you see she's a wreck? DANNY I honestly didn't know she was missing until just now. K-WOLF Hah, neither did I. Strange how that works. DUKE I didn't really care to know, and I still don't. So frankly this whole conversation is a waste of my time. Duke walks off and Danny follows suit, leaving Lisa stunned. LISA TYLER You mean to tell me that NO ONE noticed I was kidnapped? FACEY ARE YOU THE SAME MOTHER FUCKER THAT STOLE MY PUT YOU FUGGIN BITCH?! Karina shoves Facey away from the girl as she fights tears before consoling her herself. K-WOLF Tell me what happened? Who are 'the Additionals'. LISA TYLER The Additionals are those who couldn't fit in the regular cast so they're disgruntled, and their purpose is to KILL US ALL! They PROMISED that they'd BE HERE, TONIGHT at NINE, to KILL EVERY SURVIVOR! K-WOLF Everyone? But there's over 40 of us, it'll be like a wolf in a hen house picking off it's prey. The looming danger immediately casts everyone into a panic. Lisa begins crying some more while others begin looting their fellow cast aways. DAMIEN CRUZ What are we going to do?! ERIC EVANS We're all going to die! Their cries and over-reacting is interrupted by... ANGLE ON KYLE LAMEN, CHANDLER TSONDA, and TCHU stepping out of the jungle and onto the beach. LAMEN No one is going to die! His words call for SILENCE to fall upon the entire camp, all ears eager to listen. LAMEN (CONT'D) Tchu and I have discovered a pipe that could lead to a place of shelter and safety. Now we don't know who these Additionals are, but they don't sound too friendly. And judging by how everything else is going on the Island, we probably shouldn't stick around. TOKASHI MOTOKI Excuse me, but how do we know that whatever is down this pipe you found is safe? LAMEN Do we have a translator here? Does anyone speak Lebonese? TOKASHI MOTOKI I AM NOT FROM LEBONON AND I AM SPEAKING ENGLISH! No one answers Lamen's qualms, but a few people made cracks about how isolated Motoki and Taun Pham must be feeling. LAMEN Alright well if no one has any problems, lets begin packing our things and we'll set out for the pipe before nightfall. Everyone turns to gathering all of their stuff for the treck, while the scene is forced to... CUT TO: INT. APPARTMENT - DAY NOVA is chillaxin like a mother fucker, smoking a blunt while sitting in front of the TV on this nice ass lazy boy. The door behind him SLAMS open, and in walks KILLEAN SIRRAJIN. KILLEAN Hey man, I got our Lotto tickets. Let's just watch the picks before we leave. NOVA But we have to make our flight! I don't want to miss out on Culture Shock because you want to add a few zeros to your bank account. KILLEAN Ah, I never win. These numbers are bad luck, I just like to see if it'll ever turn around. NOVA Okay, that's weird. Just sit down and we'll watch the draw-but then we have to go. Killean agrees by sitting down. NOVA (CONT'D) 1, 2, 30, 32, 41, 53. Why these numbers? KILLEAN They just came to me- TELEVISION '...And tonight's winning numbers are: 4, 8, 15, 16, 23 with tonight's LUCKY number 42!' Just as Killean eats a Cheese Nip, he begins choking from excitement. With little breath, he mutters the words 'I won.' KILLEAN (Out of breath) I WON. NOVA Are you okay man, you're wheezing? KILLEAN I WON. NOVA Bullshit! Right 'UNLUCKY NUMBERS' my ass, you just fucking won the lottery. Killean falls over, dead from loss of breath. Nova picks up the ticket and looks at it. NOVA (CONT'D) Bastard, call me when you can stop gloating with your over-reacting loss of consciousness. I'm going to the airport. Pfft, like YOU have unlucky numbers. MY numbers are the unlucky ones! As Nova walks out of the appartment, the shot... FADE TO: EXT. BEACHFRONT - DAY. NOVA is walking down the beach, following a group of survivors who follow Tchu and Kyle Lamen through the jungle. Nova is carrying mysteriously large bags, being that Nova doesn't pack things normally (like most hippies) FACEY (O.S.) Give. Me. My. Drugs. Nova turns around to see the Illustrious Face-Eater standing in front of him, despaired and shit to hell. He looks like he hasn't sleep since the dawn of time, nor eaten in even longer. NOVA What drugs? FACEY YOU THINK I DON'T KNOW IT'S YOU SMOKING MY SHIT?! Facey tackles Nova, and almost instantly, it's BATTLE OF THE POT HEADS! Nova punches Facey, Facey punches Nova. It's a back and forth exchange. Kyle Lamen runs with Tchu from the front of the line to break up the chaos. LAMEN STOP IT GUYS, COME ON WE HAVE TO FOCUS ON MOVING. With Lamen securing Facey and Tchu pulling back Nova, no-one notices the pillar of black smoke that towers near them. Faint whispers surround them in the jungle. Until they are all cut-off by the sound of 'Wake Me Up Before You Go (Go)' by Wham! LAMEN (CONT'D) Do you guys here that? TCHU Shhhhhhh! It is too late. From the depths of the jungle emerges the horror so horrible. The terror so terrifying. The unspeakable horror that can obly be described as 'The Additionals.' CLYDE WALKINS BRING ME PEONS TO EAT, SLAVE-BOY. JAMES FARWELL You keep downtalking me like that, we'll see how fat you stay. Yes, the dreaded FORSAKEN are here. And they're hear to kill everyone. FADE TO BLACK. THE END. FOR NOW.
