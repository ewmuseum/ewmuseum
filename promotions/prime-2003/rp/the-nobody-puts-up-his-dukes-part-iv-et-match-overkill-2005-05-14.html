The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part IV (ET Match, Overkill)

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/05 Character Name: NovaFederation: PRIME WHAP! WKKP Fox 41 Anchor-in-Chief Brock Wolfson brings down a paw, slapping a stack of papers out of one of the station intern’s hands. They scatter across the floor. “Oh…oh, my,” Brock feigns, “so sorry, my dear. I’m afraid I’m not quite as coordinated as I used to be.” “Tha-that’s alright, Mr. Wolfson,” the intern timidly replied before bending over to gather them back up. Brock creaks his around to her backside. “Mmm, mmm, mmm…” He winces, almost painfully, out of want. “I could just eat right through those nylons and mun-” “Brock, we’re on in two! One!” Brock quickly spins around on his stool and stares intently into camera two. “Blake Wolfson reporting for WKKP Fox 41, San Francisco. Our top story tonight…” He turns to camera one. “In a bold and daring move, Nova, the PRIME up-and-comer slated to vie against Global Wrestling’s Shadow for the PTC Extreme Title at PRIME’s Overkill PPV, has declared that he is suing Primetime Central for supposed breach of contract. Apparently unhappy over information vital to the Extreme Title match that was disclosed to him only after the match had been signed, Nova had this to say:” Nova glares into the camera. “I mean, ya know…ya know, first there was the Extreme Title Match, getting through the Royale and that was big shit, pretty stressful. Then I find out if I win, PRIME makes history with belt-holders in a record five months, and that freaks me out a little bit…” Nova shuffles a Camel out of his pack and lights it, exhaling through the nose. “…but then I find out the bitch is a vampire? What…the fuck?” “Nova picketed outside the Cow Palace today, where inside a PTC road crew made way to film the event, protesting what he feels is…a bum rap.” “This is a bum rap!” Nova shouts into a megaphone as he stands atop a Ford Explorer surrounded by a small by local contingent of followers. “It’s embarrassing to the staff at Primeti-no, more than that, it’s embarrassing to me, that they’ve refused to admit their error here.” “And what exactly would the error be on the part of PTC, you ask?” “I had absolutely no warning ahead of time that I would wrestling the undead,” Nova protests, as his followers cheer, “a vampire? A vampire?! Is that even covered in my insurance? I don’t think Mail Handlers covers vampiric assault, and I know I’m not paying for protection from turning.” “…yeah, and I said ‘Well, dear, the day you wear nipple tassels, I’ll stuff one-dollar bills in your underwear! Bwu-ah-ha-ha-ha…wait, is this thing…oh, fuck.” “You know what?” Nova trumpets to his audience, “I’m boycotting the match…I won’t be there!” Cheers! “Wait, no, she won’t be there!” Nova shouts quickly, and his followers cheer again for his new decision. “Yes, we’ll all scare her off and she won’t be there for the match!” They all laugh heartily. “Yes!” Nova cries, “we’ll run her…oh, my God…wait.” Face in the Crowd #1: That’s just a format change, Nova. It happens sometimes. Nova: Not that…something else… The cheers and applause cease for a moment as Nova stares off intelligently into space from atop the sport utility vehicle. Nova: Gentlemen…what are we thinking? We can’t run Shadow out of town! The Crowd: We can’t? Nova appears dumb-founded. Nova: My God, no…fifty cc’s of newsflash, stat! We aren’t Barbarians here…are we? Are we a bunch of Huns? Any Huns in the hizzouse? No one speaks up. Nova: I didn’t think so. Now, we can’t run her out of town. She’s a witch. We have to KILL HER~! She’s a Beast! She’s too dangerous to live!” Nova reaches behind him and pulls out a silver mirror exploding with green energies. In it’s face is a video screen of Shadow’s movements…at the moment, she’s washing her jock strap. Nova: The Beast will make off with your children! The crowd gasps in fear. Nova: She’ll come after them in the night! A woman faints. Nova: We’re not safe until heat up rusty meat thermometers and impale her on them! I say we kill the Beast! The Crowd: Yeah! Kill her! Man in Crowd #1: We’re not safe until she’s dead! Woman in Crowd #1: She’ll drink blood until she’s full! Man in Crowd #2: I used to think this vampire shit was all a bunch of bull! Man in Crowd #3: She’ll destroy us if we let her so we must destroy her first… Nova: So it’s time…to find some salt and garlic, time…to…kill…the…Beast! Nova leaps off the Explorer and lands on the ground in the midst of the crowd, the mirror lighting his face up with an eerie glow. Nova: In the dark, over there, though it’s very to make out, I see eyes surveying everything we say! Must be still, or be killed, ‘cuz it’s vampires we’re hunting and if we’re not careful we will be their prey! Who could know, that Sha-dow, was a vampire? Now it looks like I better get a priest……when my jugular’s slat and I’m dead on the mat, only one thing will I plea…KILL THE BEAST! Man in Crowd #1: Shadow dies tonight…by my stake! Man in Crowd #2: Huzzah, bitches! Nova jumps back up onto the hood of the Explorer and someone tosses him a lit torch. The mirror displays an image of Shadow shaving the curve of her neck under her chin. Nova and his mob roar with anger in the firelight. Nova and the Mob: Grab your blades, garden spades,What-ever you get your hands on,‘cuz we’re gonna lynch that Global wynch tonight!Cock your guns-yes, we said ‘cock’-because the time is drawing closer,And you better bet there’s gonna be a fight!Shadow’s goose, it be cooked, and she knows it,By our stakes her heartbeat shall be ceased!Grab your gun, song is done, trust me it...will be fun!There's no where that she can run... …KILL THE BEAST!* The Nobody Puts Up His Dukes, Part IV“No, Honey…Honey, scaffolds…um, scaffolds aren’t really any more dangerous than…than…*cough*…so, so I like that blouse on you. That’s a good blouse on you.” I don’t even know how to begin this. Imagine a game of tennis, where one of the players lays down on the court and the other has the opportunity to spike the ball anywhere they want and they stall for a moment in sheer joy at the sensory overload of opportunities… …that’s what I’m holl’in at right now. I’m sorry…I’ve just gotta get this in quotes… Telling a woman who had lived through the Dark Ages that she was a femi-Nazi—how much was there that this cocky little ass had missed in his high school level history classes taught by the football coach? Now I’m just gonna count to ten and let that one sort of soak into the crowd for a minute… One…Two…Three…Four…Five…Six…Seven…Eight…Nine…Ten. Good’n saturated? Me too. I mean, you can imagine my surprise, right? Flip on the ol’ TV set and expect, Hell, who knows, maybe a few jabs, couple of baiters to see if I would come out and bite, I dunno… “Nova, I’d have a ten-and-two record, too, if I wrestled the mid-card every week.” “Nova, why do you laud your accomplishments so much? Afraid they’ll be gone next week?” Anything like this would have done fine. What do I get? “Nova, I’m a vampire.” And here I thought the incisors were some weird fellatio thing! Man was I off! Well, my admittedly clever jokes aside, I’m not intimidated by your White Wolf LARP, so save your breath. You aren’t gonna rip me to pieces or anything in the ring and you fuckin’ know it. That’s what ALWAYS gets me about the lunatics claiming to be vampires…why do we kick your asses? Shouldn’t you be invincible and all that jazz? And running with that logic, if you were invincible, I wouldn’t be making a list of all the cruel things I plan on doing to you in the ring, would I? No, I wouldn’t, because that wouldn’t make any sense. This list, in that case, would NOT include: 1) Grabbing you around the waist and German-suplexing you into next Wednesday afternoon.2) Punching you in the tit.3) Headbutting that nose of yours that kept threatening to jab out my eye in the interview we did.4) Punching you in the tit.5) Stomping on your foot until you embarrass yourself by acting foolishly.6) Kicking you in your box.7) Maybe getting to rip a criminally rank fart at one of those inopportune times where your face is really close to my ass…like a diagonal pin!8) Punching you in the tit. Bottom line, if you’re a vampire, then I’m Humphrey mother-fucking Bogart. First losses are humbling experiences, you know. Take my word for it. Are you ready? The House, Detroit, MI… “You have everything?” Ariel grills carefully. “Yeah, I think so,” Nova replies, looking down at his bag and backpack, “pretty standard trip, really…why do you ask?” “Who are you facing this week? It’s special, right?” his wife continues, “I knew, but forgot, I guess…” “Shadow,” Nova answers non-chalantly, sliding on the backpack and hoisting up the duffel bag, “from Global Wrestling.” “Aren’t you worried she’s done scaffold matches in the past?” Ariel asks. “I don’t think it’ll be an iss…,” Nova pauses, gritting his teeth. She had caught him. He hadn’t told her about the stipulation of the match…scaffolding twenty to twenty-five feet in the air, and someone was taking the elevator down. He didn’t want to worry her, and he knew she’d be pissed if he admitted to picking the match type himself. But that was all fucked now. He had slipped up. But how had she… “What the fuck is this?” Ariel asks, holding up an open Wrestling SOURCE! magazine to a page where Nova’s visage is printed across that from the admittedly sexy, leather-sporting Extreme Champion. In between them and in the background of the double spread is a scaffold graphic, and the article title reads: “Nova makes the challenge! Overkills sees a scaffold!” Nova feels his blood beginning to boil as he and his wife settle back into the same argument they’d had three or four good times since he had joined PRIME…since he had come back after the leg injury, really. “I don’t believe this,” Ariel says softly, so softly it’s almost a whisper…but way too pissed off for a regular whisper, “so now you’re looking for ways to hurt yourself? Do…do we mean that little to you?” “Don’t be so damn dramatic,” Nova snaps back, not in the mood to listen to the same tirade he’d memorized painfully months before. “It just isn’t enough for you, is it!” Ariel yells, her arms going up, “It isn’t enough to overstay your body’s welcome in this idiot ‘profession’ of yours, is it?! Now you need to be the group daredevil, is that it? Huh?” Nova says nothing, staring icily at his wife. She never belittles his profession, even in their most intense arguments, and her words still hang on the air like the thick humidity of a Bible Belt ghost town. She can tell she’s crossed way over a line, and Nova can tell she really wants to snatch the words back out of the air. But, regardless, she doesn’t offer an apology and Nova doesn’t give her time to make one. Silently, he turns and walks out of the house. |-~-~-~-~-~-~*~*~*~-~-~-~-~-~-| May 15, 2005 – Cow Palace, San Francisco, CA, OVERKILL “BLLEEEEEECCCCHHH!!” Nova’s back shudders as he leans over a garbage can. He lifts his head up, wiping hair out of his eyes. He hears Lacuna Coil’s “Heaven’s A Lie” blasting through the arena, and above that even, he can hear, with increasing volume… “NO-VA! NO-VA! NO-VA! NO-VA!” Nova wipes his hand against the scruff of his mouth and stands up. He walks towards the steps leading up to the entrance ramp. As his begins climbing the steps, a voice stops him. “Nova!” He turns back to see a random road go-for looking up at him with admiration. “Win it for PRIME!” Nova stares at the kid for a moment, a kindly look spreading over his face… …before scowling and flipping the kid off as he walks up onto the stage. Fuck that. No way he’s dedicating this one to PRIME. This one’s for himself. Nova steps out into the blinding lights and the crowd erupts. *Inspired by “The Mob Song” from Disney’s Beauty and the Beast
