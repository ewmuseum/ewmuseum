Finding the will...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday “Victory has a hundred fathers but defeat is an orphan.” -Galeazzo Ciano Jackson Darilek sits alone in the solemn silence of his dimly lit bedroom. Several layers of white athletic tape adhere a soft cotton cast mat cast to his torso as a result of the injuries he suffered at Culture Shock. Two bruised ribs on one side, and a minor crack on the other. Nothing new, and nowhere near the worst of his injuries. No amount of physical torment, pain, or punishment could even begin to surmount the anguish imposed on the young man’s ego. If anything were immediately made apparent from watching him in action he’d had broken bones before. Cuts, bruises, all of these things were of no consequence at all in Darilek’s mind so long as they served their purpose... victory. No amount of misery was maligned for long in the bright, shimmering, warm, soothing light of triumph. Winning invigorated so much so that even the threat of losing life and limb was without gravity. It was like a drug. But now that feeling was gone. And it had been gone for quite some time now. So like any addict, Jackson was going through withdrawals. Bad dreams kept him up at night, tossing and turning in bed wrestling against himself within the sheets as nightmares of continued failure haunted him. Often causing him to awaken abruptly in a cold sweat. During his waking hours, his normally boisterous and jubilant disposition was replaced with one of malcontent and soul-numbing depression. Once considered overly talkative, Jackson was now borderline antisocial. Only speaking to others when need be, and even then only in curt three to four word bursts. Sometimes when you’re winning too much you start to feel like you should never lose again. Every week Darilek walked down the ramp expecting to win, but seemingly each week he was met only with defeat. Slowly moving his hands upward over his face and rests them atop his head with their palms near his hairline, as though they were attempting to prevent an explosion, he sighs. Then, sliding his hands even further back, bringing them down over his ears en route to the sides of his neck he utters to himself “It wasn’t supposed to be like this...” Grandiose delusions of fame, acclaim, and multiple title reigns thrust his ego through the roof when he first heard PRIME was interested in him. Expecting to instantly take on all comers, flying past those on top and etching his name into PRIME history. How quickly that tower of dreams fell... In an albeit impressive showing, Jackson lost his first match as a result of a rookie mistake. Jackson had made minimal effort to prepare himself for the match, and ended up paying the price as Brandon Youngblood was able to walk away with the spoils. Then came Shane Wethers... In a shocking and all too easy appearing match, Jackson put the seasoned vet away in short order... only to have the match ruled a fluke. Confident that he could do it again just as easily, Darilek thought only one thing when the possibility of a rematch was laid on the table... Too easy. In Jackson’s mind, Wethers stood no chance. If anything, the match was considered more of a personal appearance and a chance for fans to catch a glimpse of the nineteen year old phenomenon as he quickly seized victory for himself. But on that night this was not to be the case. Wethers saw an opening and turned the one percent chance he had of winning into the spark to ignite an engulfing blaze. But it was just that, chance. Chance can allow you to accomplish a seemingly unattainable goal every once in a great while, but consistent achievement happens only if you love what you are doing. And on any given night in any given arena, after a win, it was more than evident during his indy career that Jackson loved what he was doing. It was all he knew how to do. Win. His achievement was nothing if not consistent, he was perpetually on the rise. Jackson Darilek was blowing up like spontaneous human combustion on a weekly basis in front of hundreds of fans, and expected the rise to continue on in PRIME. He even began to refer to himself as “The new face of PRIME”. A few weeks ago it was a handsome face smiling mischievously with a middle finger held high overhead in everyone’s general direction. Now it was melancholy, unshaven, and staring at a middle finger every time it passed by a mirror. Darilek had chalked up his loss to Wethers as chance, but after his dismal performance in the Dual Halo match which he’d thought to be nothing more than an interpretive dance preceding the raising of his hand in triumph... Jackson full on hated himself. With doubts about his future swirling, self loathing beset Jackson like a pack of hounds on a bloody fox. With each pass by a mirror Darilek couldn’t help but scowl and sneer at the reflection he saw. A loser. He’d grown to hate himself so much in such a short time that just looking into his own countenance was too much for him. With each glance he could taste his own bile welling up on the back of his tongue. This was the taste of defeat. You can learn a line from a win, and an entire book from defeat. Yet all Jackson had seemingly learned to do was detest himself for it. He was usually described as unbearable in victory and unbeatable in defeat because he always came back even more ferocious than before, but now it would seem that his will had been beaten out of him. He hadn’t taken any calls or accepted any visitors in a number of days, opting to sit in the frigid silence of his room. His phones rang off their hooks and knocks at the door went without acknowledgment. Darilek had become a sovereign state, completely cut off from the outside world and it’s rules and values. Even those “rules and values” that went by the name Dalia... his girlfriend. After several days of sullen sulking, a very melancholy Jackson Darilek heard a rattling noise coming from the other side of the door to his room. The same door which had been locked for days. At first he turned a suspicious eye toward where the noise was coming from, but chose to remain seated. Deeming himself utterly worthless had taken away his will to fight for himself, or his possessions. He’d decided instead to put himself at the mercy of whoever was to pass through that door. After a few more rough jerks, all became silent once again as whatever was on the other side of the door seemed to have stopped. Slowly, the cold steel door knob began to turn, making only the slightest of noises as the bolt slips from it’s cylinder. With the door ajar, the intruder surreptitiously ducks into the chilly room and with purposeful steps makes it’s way toward Jackson’s location. As the unknown visitor’s footsteps grow closer, Darilek finally decides to inspect as he wraps his boxer clad body in a thin sheet and stands to his feet. Just as he prepares to investigate, suddenly the lights turn on, almost blinding him in the process as he’d been in the dark for days now. When he finally regains his sight he finds himself standing across the room from none other than Dalia, who appears to be a bit fed up. Placing a hand on her hip, she taps her foot repeatedly on the floor with an expectant scowl directed at her dejected boyfriend. He hadn’t accepted or returned any of her calls in days, and she was sure he’d been home on a few of the occasions she’d attempted to stop by. It would seem she’d finally had enough and decided to take matters into her own hands. With a shrug, Jackson turned and shuffled back towards the bed, this time gingerly maneuvering underneath it’s sheets as if to shield himself from anything from the outside. “How’d you get in here?” comes from underneath the covers in a weak and slightly muffled voice. This causes Dalia to weave her neck a bit while cocking an eyebrow at Jackson, even though he can’t see it. “How did I get in here?” Dalia repeats as she walks toward his bed, angling herself toward the side he’s turned toward. “You’re not the only one who knows how to pick a lock! Good thing too, cause I’m sick of you acting like this!” She exclaims, her tone growing more exasperated by the second as she rips the comforter away from Jackson while continuing “Look at you! You look like who done it and why, this isn’t you...” Dalia’s words fall on deaf ears as Deacon just glances at her with glazed eyes before turning to the other side. “Get up!” she pleads with him, then thinking quickly, she decides to change her strategy. Taking a seat on the edge of the bed she leans back a bit and rests her elbow on Darilek as she continues. “I know what it is, you’re afraid.” This finally manages to elicit some sort of response from Jackson as he rolls over to face her and looks up into her eyes with wild eyed bewilderment. A knowing smile creeps across her face as she stands back up to her feet and continues. “That’s right, you heard me... S-C-A-R-E-D, SCARED! You know you can’t beat those guts so you just wanna wallow in bed all day your match with him comes and goes while you’re asleep. Well things aren’t always that easy, sometimes you actually have to try! Now get up!” Jackson glared at her a bit in disbelief as she stared at him waiting for him to rise from the bed. Slowly but surely, Darilek climbed out of bed and onto his feet. Standing before her in nothing more than a pair of boxers while exhibiting the poorest of posture, Jackson Darilek was truly a sight to see, and a far cry from the him that was usually seen on television. After sizing him up, Dalia began to rattle off instructions. “Now first, we’re gonna get you shaved and in the shower. Then you’re going to the gym and having a nice long work out while I go to my spinning class.” Jackson’s shoulders slumped even more at the idea of such physical exertion, over the course of the week he’d grown quite accustomed to sloth. Dalia took note of his ever deteriorating posture and quickly nipped it right in the bud after flipping her fiery red hair over her shoulder. “And stand up straight! Ya know, the worst thing you can do when you’re feeling bad is straighten up and hold your head high. Wanna know why?” Jackson lackadaisically shrugged at her inquisition before replying “Why?” in a tone that conveyed just how little he actually cared. Dalia quickly shot back “Because then you might actually start to feel better, now go get cleaned up!” With a scoff, Darilek collapsed back against the bed mattress and covered himself once more with sheets and blankets. Both of Dalia’s eyes grew wide as her boyfriend continued to sulk. Then, reaching into her bag for it’s last remaining trick, she finally struck gold. “Fine, you wanna play hardball? We’ll play hardball! Until you go out and win that match... YOU’RE CUT OFF!” These words sparked a fire inside of Darilek, who immediately burst from underneath the covers and sprang to his feet before they even had a chance to hit the ground. Staring at his girlfriend with bulging eyes, Jackson leapt from the bed and raced immediately into the bathroom. Seconds later water could be heard spraying from the shower head as Dalia smiled to herself while taking a seat on the edge of the bed to revel in her own genius. “Damn I’m good.”
