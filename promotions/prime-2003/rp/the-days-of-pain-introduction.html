The Days of Pain: Introduction

Date: Here... Now... I’ve been back from the visit to the doctors for a while now. “A split personality” he called it. I had changed right there in the office in front of him, showing off my terrible side, my harmful side. It’s the side that seems to not have a care for anyone or anything... a drastic difference from the original me. Seems like my body is getting tired of being a good person and has decided to take on a mind of its own. It’s hard to say what will become of me. What will happen to me when it involves the people I know and love? Cameron and Trina Donnelly. I’d been around them so much growing up you could swear they were like my second parents. The most warm and caring individuals I have ever known. But amidst all this, I had to catch myself the other day, stop that other side of me from saying something I would never forget and never forgive myself for. Why did I have these feelings for these wonderful people? It’s not me... it’s the other me. Chad Donnelly used to be the epitome of the perfect guy. He always used to sacrifice his time and his effort to make you happy. But that dreadful day came when he abandoned everyone. His attitude mixed with his brothers like a thick viscosity oil and water. Then, what he did to that poor girl was heartbreaking. The emotions she must have felt, the tears in her eyes... I wish I could just give him numerous shots to the face, to make him feel only half as bad physically as he made that poor girl feel inside. Wait, what was I saying? Is this me talking or... you? Nitz Donnelly, the youngest brother. He is what you’d call a loudmouth, speak it like it is, the world is what you see of it kinda guy. Somewhat charming in his own special way. Even though the man had been involved with and enjoyed coitus with women of all different types, there was another side to him. When he wants to be nice, he certainly can be but on the flipside, he can be the dirtiest of the pricks as well. He was almost like his own type of psychitzo, although not entirely abnormal. He was like this since day one and has not changed in all the years I have known him. What’s this feeling eating at me? What is going through my mind... our mind? Hailey was a piece of work... always sticking close to her brother for protection, she was a calculating, sour bitch. No... NO! That’s not her at all. Stop doing this to me... I don’t feel any of that towards the person who has been like a sister to me for so many years. She was there for me in my time of need, my turmoil. She would be there again too, sucking the very life force from me and showing that she was a dominant person in every way, her sometimes unfeminine guttural voice demanding and demeaning... AGAIN! Stop it... please. It’s beginning to take control again. The more I fight it, the more it fights back. The medications are not doing enough for me and I need to wait a few days before upping the dose. I’m stuck like this, sharing a body with my evil twin, the one who is bound to get me in trouble, to help shine a new light on me that no one would expect nor is it something that I would want to be known for. I feel my cheeks cave and my lips purse together, thinking if only for a moment that I was losing the fight to this monster inside of me. I fear that soon, I’ll be a completely different person, watching the goings on of the other me from a distance. I’d be like the fly on the wall, except I would be forced to endure sometimes harsh acts. Who am I? Where am I going? Not to mention... what kind of hell am I falling into? My life will be like a zoo and no longer would I blend in like the smaller creatures and animals. Instead, I’d be widely known and hard to miss, like when the giraffe tends to hide behind a tree but never quite becoming successful. It’s hard to hide or disguise such a being. But that’s not even the biggest fear I have. Along with being forced to watch my own dismemberment from my physical being, I’d watch this being take over my mind as well, eventually poisoning me until I simply ceased to exist, melting away into the memories of everyone I knew who would then despise me. But worse than all that, I’m powerless to prevent it. No matter what I do myself, I’ll just continue to fade further and further into obscurity. Who am I? I can’t even tell you anymore...
