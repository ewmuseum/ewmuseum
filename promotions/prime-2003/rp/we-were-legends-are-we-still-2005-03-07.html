We Were Legends, Are We Still?

7 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/2005 ~What makes a legend? Is it the glamour, the fans, the performance, or the combination of all of those things? Jon Kano and The Trashcan Man are both legends, there is no doubt. Many argue who is the bigger legend, but who really cares? They both bled and sweated for this industry. They busted their respective asses night in and night out to please the fans and to make PCW into the powerhouse it once was and and are trying their best to make PRIME as big and better that PCW could have ever aspired to be. On this week's edition of ReVolution, all hell will break loose as these two join forces in a tag team tandem. The fans will love it, but can these two living legends co-exist and defeat the young team of Los Diablos, or will the young team take out the old legends?.~ *The scene opens up outside on a clear, starry night with the moon shining brightly upon everything below. As the scene pans from the sky it focuses in on a jet black 2005 Lincoln Navigator sitting upon a cliff in Godknowswhere, USA. Sitting 'Indian-style' on the hood is Jon Kano, wearing a 'A Legend Never Dies' t-shirt, baggy blue jeans, and Doc Martins. Kano has a serious look on his face as he stares off into the abyss of the night and onto the city below.* Kano: You know, as I look out there upon the city, I see people walking, going about their lives. Where are they going? No one knows. Do they know I am up here? Do they care? I could go on with a million questions. I am doing my best to answer all questions regarding myself one ReVolution at a time. Last week, I laced up my boots for the first time in almost a year. People asked how much ring rust I would have. People asked if I still had my touch. People asked if I still had what it took to get the job done in the ring. Well one match later and a mark in the win column tells me that yes, I still got my touch. I can still do business inside of that ring. And I can do it like none other. *Kano stands up on the hood of his luxury sport utility vehicle.* Kano: I mean, my God, who else in this whole damn industry is almost seven feet tall and goes to the top turnbuckle and flies like a lucha libre wrestler? Damn sure not Boda. That brings us to Los Diablos, this week, my challenge. They seem to be convinced that they can't catch a break here in PRIME because they are Mexican. I am so sick of hearing shit like this. The one thing that I have learned in the business, above all else, is that you make your own way, for the most part. I just hope they don't think that TCM and myself are just going to lay down for them this week. Hell, I don't care if what they say is true that they can't catch a break, because it doesn't matter to me if you are black, white, brown, yellow, red, green, or turqoise. I am an equal opportunity ass kicker. Everyone, regardless of who they are or where they come from, will succumb to the Kanoplex. That isn't being cocky, that is the truth. *Kano jumps down off of his hood and onto the ground below.* Kano: What a history of success my team has. Think about between TCM and myself, we have held the biggest titles this industry has to offer. We are both Hall of Famers. We are two of the very best to ever grace a wrestling ring, that isn't hype, that is the truth. What makes Trashy and myself so damn good? It is really quite simple, we are focused. You see while Los Diablos are riding around in their low rider and dwelling on the past and how much the deck is stacked against them, I am here focused on the match. It has been awhile since I have been this focused. I see this match as another stepping stone in my quest to get Boda in the ring and make him pay for his crimes and sins. *As Kano speaks, he begins to pace around his Navigator.* Kano: I do wish Los Diablos the best of luck, don't get me wrong. I have never been one to hold down up and coming talent, but they ain't gonna make a name for themselves on TCM's and my account. I got this feeling that this tag team is going to go to school at ReVolution and TCM and myself will be the instructors. And we will show them why we are legends, and why we are hall of famers. *Kano leans up against the driver's side door, with his massive back resting against the window.* Kano: I guess Los Diablos are just sitting back studying tapes of TCM and myself in action and reading scouting reports on us, hoping to gain an edge. At least, I hope they are doing that. They most probably are still bitching about the match against Angelo Deville last week. I learned long ago that you must cut your losses and learn from them. Make your losses motivate you to not make the same mistakes twice. Plus you know, those scouting reports and tapes can't be all that reliable. I mean remember I am the one who has only wrestled about thirty matches in the last two years. Look at TCM, for Christ's sakes, what could you learn from watching his tapes? The fact that he loves to set things on fire? The fact that he can take an extreme amount of punishment? The fact that he loves to make people scream in agony? Jesus, no wonder I like him so much. Los Diablos will get a first rate education in the artform of professional wrestling from two men that have doctorates in different disciplines of the art. I hope they are ready for that. And I hope they are ready for us to show them what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano gets into his SUV and starts the engine.* ~Is anyone beginning to understand what being a legend is about?~
