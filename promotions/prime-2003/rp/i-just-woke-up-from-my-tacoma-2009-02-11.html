I just woke up from my Tacoma...

11 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/09 They couldn’t do better than the Tacoma, Washington Holiday Inn for old Duke Williams? I arrived last night and after taking my screw driver to the lock on wet bar; I met a midget – I fine American of small stature and quick wit. Me and my wee friend got good and tight as we roamed the hallways banging on doors seeing if anybody had a bathrobe I could borrow while singing old Ray Charles songs mixed with an eclectic sampling of old negro spirituals. The night was filled with angry faces of traveling salesmen, innocent girls doing very un-innocent yet very natural things with married men, and agitated hotel workers trying to stifle my free speech in their Mecca of rooms filled with TV guides, phone books, and holy bibles in the true trinity of Americana. The last thing I remember about last night was the little man giving me a pill that made me forget the world and sleep. I woke up in a dingy hotel room bathtub wearing a strange bathrobe with ducks on them reeking of booze, hash, and general sin. Not a good way to start the day for a professional athlete, one half of the tag team champions, and one hell of a guy, let alone a journalist of the people. Snoring on the bed was the middle aged midget I met last night while roaming the lobby. He looked like somebody that I have seen before, but I couldn’t place him. It really was that kind of morning and it really was that kind of night. I couldn’t take any chances with the little monster, he could be a spy for Wicked Ways the neo Nazi duo that are hell bent on stealing my gold. My spies called to tell me that two members of the cult know as Wicked Ways, a well known terrorist organization filled with sexual deviants, were hanging around the arena as the ring was being set up last night. My guess is they are planting bombs or other terrorist actions that are intended to force fear into the hearts and minds of the brotherhood of DUI a noble and righteous fraternity of heroes, saints, patriots, and Duke Williams. Shit, I heard a noise just outside my door, I thought they are coming, who else could it have been? Think fast Williams and remember we got Christ on our side. I took the last swig of booze from one of the half dead soldiers that littered the floor. I swung open the door hard, fast, and ready to fight as I damn near knocked the cleaning lady out in the process, no doubt a spy for the Wicked Ways. I proceeded to question her on her intentions but she played coy like she didn’t speak English, I didn’t have time to continue my little interrogation, so I grabbed a broom, an extra strength Hoover hotel vacuum cleaner, a pile of fresh towel, small soaps, and a few garbage bags. I went back into my room placing the ‘do not disturb’ sign on the door knob before slamming the door in her face. It was time to get down to brass tacks, an old school way of doing things, unlike the kids today who pussy foot around like a bunch of pussies my generation took charge. I let all the acid I had left melt on my tongue and numb my mind as I tied up the midget with duct tape that I took from the janitor’s closet sometime last night. My fellow travelers always remember to grab all the small things you can while staying at a hotel and stuff them in your pocket, because you never know what you’re going to need and when you’re going to need it. The only thing I could get the little bugger to stick to was the Hoover extra strength vacuum cleaner, a trick I learned in Nam -- a great biker bar in Montana. I turned on the powerful machine and began sucking up all the sour cream and onion Ruffles that were rained down upon my room late last night in a kinky wrestling match with a Thai hooker named Pong. The good, good, good vibrations must have awoken the little feller from his Ambient induced slumber as he began flopping around and yelling. Thankfully the sound from the strength of the vacuums motor drowned out the pleas from the midget keeping us from alerting the authorities. I turned off the machine and began poking him with the other end of the broom demanding answers of who he was, a question most of ask our own selves time and time again in our life journey. Never an easy question to answer, but I wasn’t in the mood for foolery or philosophy I wanted to know who hired him and what his intentions were. Duke Williams: Who are you? Who let you in here? Are you a member of the communist party or Wicked Ways? Did you let these fucking birds into my room? Midget: I’m Matt Roloff I sell hotel stools for little people, let me go and I won’t call the police. I jabbed harder… Duke Williams: Who the hell would want to buy midget poop? Does it have some kind of intoxicating priorities? If it does you better start shitting little man! Roloff: No I sell tiny ladders to help little people get to the sink and have better accessibility. Sounded like bullshit to me but I had enough so I wheeled the little man tapped to the vacuum cleaner out into the hall where I left him for Wicked Ways to recover. Now after successfully foiling their plot I’m mere hours from getting into the ring to take them out. Time has come fellow travelers’ to defend what is mine in an epic showdown of good and evil where I will return these hell minions and domestic terrorists whiskey bent and hell bound. More to come …
