How to Say Lackluster in 1000 Words

28 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/28/2005 On ReVolution, I’m fighting a clown...and Ozric Mortimer. There’s only one tiny problem. I have an irrational fear of clowns. I don’t fear clowns like Nova, though. Nova is a joke. I fear clowns like Ozric Mortimer- clowns that do the whole painted face, suspenders, and big shoes deal. I remember how it all started. It was 1985. Michael J. Fox and Christopher Lloyd delighted audiences with the first “Back to the Future.” Atari was the hottest gaming system on the market. Reaganomics was in full swing. Wham! featuring George Michael was captivating music fans. Some of you might even remember General Nimeiri being overthrown in a military coup...particularly if you were living in Sudan at the time. But you get the idea. Anyways, here’s what happened. The scene switches back to 1985. Nova and I are sitting on a green couch. We look exactly the same as we do now except Nova has a mullet, and I have a flat top. I’m dressed completely in a windbreaker, and Nova is wearing an orange headband with a white t-shirt underneath a Members Only jacket and black sweatpants with rainbow colored stripes on the side. Ironically, even though Nova is wearing a Members Only jacket, we’re listening to Phil Collins’ “No Jacket Required” on my brand new cassette player that I got for my 10th birthday a few months back. BIG POPPA: “So, did you hear that Guatemala drafted a new constitution?” NOVA: “No way, dude! That’s pretty radical.” BIG POPPA: “Yeah, I guess.” There’s an awkward pause in the short conversation. Neither of us really know much about the Guatemalan constitution. I don’t even know why I brought it up. NOVA: “So...um...” BIG POPPA: “Yeah...uh...” Lightbulb! BIG POPPA: “You wanna see my Rubic’s Cube?” NOVA: “Sure, dude.” I take out the colorful cube and set it down in front of Nova. Nova immediately begins to pick it up and work on it as I stand up and turn the TV on. I surf through all the VHF channels before switching to UHF and settling for an episode of Who’s The Boss? God, I love Tony Danza. He’s not so much funny as he is wholesome. Like fiber. BIG POPPA: “Man, that damn Cube is so frustrating. I’ve worked on it for days, and I just can’t figure it...” I look over at Nova, and he’s already finished the Rubic’s Cube. God, I hate this guy. He’s always trying to outdo me at everything...at my own things. Get your own damn Rubic’s Cube, or go play Dungeons and Dragons or something. Why can’t you just be good at that? Why do you always have steal my spotlight? You stupid sonofa... BIG POPPA: “Wow! How’d you do that?” SUPERNOVA: “Easy. I peeled all the stickers off and put them back on in different places.” BIG POPPA: “I get it. The answer lies beyond the instructions. You have to think outside the box.” SUPERNOVA: “Huh? Think outside the box?” BIG POPPA: “Oh, never mind. That’s just an annoying corporate catchphrase that will become popular in about ten years. It’s like the word paradigm.” SUPERNOVA: “Pair of dimes? Don’t mind if I do.” Nova takes out two dime bags full of weed as a rim shot plays off in the background. The studio audience (or laugh track) starts chuckling, and we go to commercial break. After all, it’s very important that every ten minutes, corporate America reminds the ovulating population that such and such brand tampons caters towards women with such and such shaped vaginas. It’s also important to keep in mind that fat people should go to Subway, black people should go to McDonalds, and lumberjacks should go to Hardee’s. Anyway, we’re back, and Grandpoppa Poppa has entered the room. He’s holding three tickets. GRANDPOPPA POPPA: “The circus is in town, boys. You’re in for a real treat!” Nova and I start cheering wildly. We both start break dancing, as was the style back in the 80's. Little did we know at the time, however, that the circus was ridiculously gay. If I was walking down the street nowadays hooting like a chimpanzee about how the circus was coming, I’d surely be pelted with rocks and then urinated upon. We load up into the car, my grandpa then drove a Cutlass Supreme. That was a sweet ride back in the day. Pair that car with my grandpa’s thick 80's style mustache, and Grandpoppa Poppa was an unstoppable explosion of sexual desire. The ladies loved him, and he loved giving it up the ass. NOVA: “I call shotgun!” Dammit! That little fucker is one upping me again. I’m a patient fucking man, but twenty years of Nova shoving his goodness down my throat is enough! I could’ve called shotgun first, but I was a good host and a good friend. I figured I’d get my turn to ride in the front seat when his grandpa took us to the circus, but guess what! Nova’s grandpa never took us to the circus! He never even took us to the dog tracks! Fuck Nova for having such a shitty grandpa. Parkinson’s disease my ass. Soon enough, we’re all in the Supreme. Nova’s in the front seat with my grandpa driving. I’m in the back seat. Grandpa puts the keys in the ignition and turns them, starting the car. As he’s backing out of our driveway, he knocks down some trash cans before peeling out into the road and taking off. He never was a good driver. His drivers licence was actually revoked after he drove through the display window of a Chinese restaurant. So we’re on the road when suddenly, a police car sneaks up behind us and turns its lights on. GRANDPOPPA POPPA: “Oh shit.” Grandpoppa Poppa tries to escape the cop with some nifty moves as the police officer demands that the car pull over through a megaphone. Unfortunately, my grandfather’s weak chicken legs can’t fully compress the gas pedal, and he realizes that he’s running in vain. He pulls to the side of the road, and the officer approaches the vehicle. Nova and I sit completely still. Nova probably has a gram and a piece on him, and I have an embarrassing stick on tattoo on my ass. OFFICER: “Sir, do you know why I pulled you over?” GRANDPOPPA POPPA: “No.” OFFICER: “Your right turn signal has been on for the last seven minutes. I’m just going to issue you a warning, but...” The officer starts sniffing the air. OFFICER: “Sir, have you been drinking?” GRANDPOPPA POPPA: “No! It must be the boy!” Grandpoppa Poppa points to me, and I shrug. The officer makes my grandfather get out and take a breathalyzer test, as Nova and I watch through the windows. My grandpa can’t keep his balance to walk a straight line, but he just has joint problems. He can’t recite the alphabet backwards, but he never bothered to learn it forwards anyway. These tests are biased, but the officer starts to search the car. He opens the front door and pops the trunk before walking around to examine it. I notice that Grandpoppa Poppa has gone pale. OFFICER: “Just as I suspected! There’s a dead hooker in there!” We never made it to the circus that day, and I never got to see the elephants or the flying trapeze. I guess that never really explained my irrational fear of clowns either. Come to think of it, all it did was remind me how much I hate Nova. I do the guy a favor, and he goes running back to his wife. Nova always wins! Nova always gets the girl! Everyone loves Nova. Why? Why? Why? Why? WHY!? What the fuck is so great about Nova? So what if he’s handsome and he has a lot of heart and the kids think he’s cool because he smokes weed a lot. So what if he eliminated me from the Dual Halo? Does that mean he’s better than me? No! It means that he’s not afraid to hit me with a cheap shot. I can’t think of one time I hit Nova when his back was turned. Not one single time. I defy anybody to prove otherwise because no proof exists because it didn’t happen! Who does Nova think he is? Who the fuck does Nova think he is? He thinks he can go around wearing his headband like a jackass just because he can eliminate some people from Dual Halo behind their backs? At ReVolution, I’m fighting a clown...and Ozric Mortimer. But I don’t care about the match one way or another. I don’t care about winning or losing. I don’t care about tag teaming with Dani Furher. Win or lose, I’m intent on opening Nova’s eyes. I want him to see that it was us who wronged Razor. Because now I know how Razor felt. I know how Razor felt when I abandoned him and branched off because I thought I could be better than he ever was. And for a while, it appeared that I was succeeding. And then Nova did the same thing to me. But Nova couldn’t escape. Nova couldn’t run too far. I don’t give a shit about PRIME. The only reason I came here is because Nova came here. And if he leaves, I leave, and I follow him wherever he goes. And I follow that no good son of a bitch until I finally catch him where I want him. Until he finally sees what it is he’s doing. The arrogant bastard couldn’t wait to one up me. He’s always trying to one up everybody. I’ve got to stop this raging, lunatic egomaniac before his ego feeds more and grows to self destructive levels. Nova. Let me save you.
