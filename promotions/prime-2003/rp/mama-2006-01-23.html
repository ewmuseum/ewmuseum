Mama

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/23/06 How many days had it been? Jason had lost count. He had been sleeping in the chair beside Eric’s bed for so many consecutive days that they had all started to run together. When he finally did decide to leave he went back to his hotel room. Jason needed to collect his thoughts and figure out what was going to happen. His best friend was hospitalized and it was all one man’s fault. That bastard had attacked Eric for no reason, an unprovoked attack. “Wrong place, wrong time” didn’t cut it here. Even worse, Jam had smashed his favorite bass in the process of defending Eric. There were at least a dozen others on the tour bus, but that one was his favorite. “What are you complaining about? Eric is in the damn hospital and you’re worried about a music instrument? Hold on….why am I talking to myself?” The Hairy Beast of Londonderry said to himself as he sat on the bed in the small hotel room. There was another bed next to him which was where Eric would have been if he wasn’t in the hospital. Jason ran a hand through his hair, well, attempted to. His hair was far too thick and curly for that. After fighting to get his hand out he sighed heavily and fell back onto the bed. Though the big man would never admit it, he was worried about his upcoming match. It was only his third match in the company and he wasn’t with his usual partner, it was a different type of match as well. Instead of two-on-two it was now three-on-three and Eric was not on his team. As his thoughts began to wonder, he was cut off by the sound of a cellphone ringing. “What? Who’d call me here?” The Five Stringer sat up from the bed and walked across the room to his bag, going through it for a moment before producing a dark blue flip-top phone. He flipped it open and answered. “Hello?” “Mi hijo.” The voice was smooth and feminine. He was surprised by the voice on the other end. Of all the people to call him, this was the last one he expected. How often did pro wrestlers or rock stars talk about their mothers? Yes, it was Jason’s dear old mother on the phone. The very woman whose genes were responsibility for the THUNDER! He possessed. “Mama. Como es usted?” “Bien, hijo.” As had been revealed when Seek and Destroy played Spanish string instruments, Jason could speak rather fluent Spanish. It should have been obvious considering his complexion and accent. Not overly Latino, but he had darker skin and a very light Spanish accent. “Como es Eric?” She wanted to know how Eric was. Somehow, through the thoughts of seeing his band mate lying in a hospital bed for the past four days and getting revenge on Baltasar, hearing from his dear ole mother was able to calm him. He still spoke Spanish back to her. Though Jason was half American and very capable of English, as was Mom, the two always spoke Spanish to one another. “Él está consiguiendo major” “Bien, bien.” The Muther Plucker began to pace across the room. He forced the images of Eric after the attack and then all wrapped up like a mummy from his mind. Though a smile did come to his face when envisioned Baltasar a bloody mess, begging for mercy and for the pain of the tremendous beating courtesy of the THUNDER! to stop. He chuckled, but quickly hid it. “Mama, tengo que ir.” After their goodbyes Jason flipped the phone shut and nonchalantly strolled back over to his bag. He placed the phone back in it and then removed a sleek black checkbook from the bag. Smiling to himself he set it on the small table and then turned away. As he walked across the room he began to strip, carelessly dropping the disrobed articles onto the blue carpet. He hadn’t showered in a few days because of the stress of worrying about Eric and his upcoming match. He knew what would calm him and give him some peace until then. “Five Strings of Rage, Steel, and Pain.” His deep voice said as he entered the bathroom, now completely in the buff. He stopped a minute to look at himself in the mirror; his face and neck were covered in stubbly, coarse black hairs. He needed to shave badly. He ran a hand through the thickness and smiled proudly, he could grow a beard that would make Zakk Wylde proud. “Zakk would drink a beer to this bad boy if he could see it. Maybe I’ll just trim up and keep it.” He once again ran his fingers as far as they’d go through his mane and noticed a few soap chunks from the other day still in there. Those had to come out. Not that he really minded his hair capturing things, the ladies didn’t dig it, and when he was unkempt and around Eric, “The Six String Sex Symbol” didn’t get much play either. It mattered to Eric, so it mattered to him. The sound of water running was heard and steam began to fill the room. Jason liked his showers like his women and his food: HOT. The heat was the only known thing that could subdue the THUNDER! long enough to be groomed. A wince was heard as Jam stepped into the shower and felt the hot water splash against his skin. He grabbed a bottle of shampoo and opened it up, squirting the liquid into his massive mane and began to rub it through. Some of the massive locks began to unravel and fall down, revealing that Jason’s hair was a lot longer when it wasn’t puffed out. After the shower he threw on a pair of faded jeans and a black tee with the words “VAN HALEN” scribbled across the front in bright yellow. One quick look in the mirror was done to make sure the THUNDER! had returned after the assault of hot, clean water. Sure enough, it had dried and was once again big and out, but still falling enough to prove it was indeed long locks and not an afro. He began to speak to himself, using the same serious tone he had used previously. There was a time for Rock and a time to be real, and Jason realized that. That alone proved he was one of the most rocking guys around, he knew when to live it up and when not to. “This isn’t over. We’ll get Baltasar back, bro.” With those words, he jerked open the door to his room and walked out into the parking lot. He wasted a perfectly good instrument last week and it needed to be replaced to keep the amps cranked and the pain dealt.
