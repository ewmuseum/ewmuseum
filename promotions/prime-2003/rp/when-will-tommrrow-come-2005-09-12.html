When will tommrrow come?

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/12/05 So many mixed emotions… Each breath is vastly different then the one that came before it… I am now seeing that each waking moment is different then the previous… I guess I am finally learning to cope with it… I have achieved success on a couple of occasions… But it has always been overlooked by my constant failure… I feel depressed but at the same time happiness… Can I explain it? No but the time has come for me to maybe figure it out… And it all starts with Killean Sirrajin and me taking home the Jewel in the Crown tournament… Chapter I: Those Words meant so much…but what does she mean? It might have only taken place a couple of weeks back, but the fact of the matter is it is still running constantly in my head. She called me, I didn’t call her, all she had to say was “I am pregnant” Those three words are going to change my life completely. I went from utter happiness when I was with her, to her putting me in jail, then I was faced with the reality I would never have to face her again. Then that fucker Paul Cain brought her into my career, he put his nose in a place it should have never been. And now, she wants me to have a baby with her, I have to spend at least 18 more years with this women, but yet I feel compelled to spend the rest of my life with her. Alone I sat in my chair at home; this place had become very familiar, the surroundings never changing except the time of day, actual day, and the poison of drink I keep in my right hand. Tonight I am drinking Jack on the rocks; he has become my friend as of late along with Jose, Jameson, amongst numerous others. Do I have a problem? It is a possibility but the truth of the matter is my relationship problems out number any sort of drinking problems I may have. “What am I too believe? I have Paul Cain convincing her I am useless and a piece of shit, but then I have her coming back to me and saying she is having my child. Then a couple of weeks ago at Tropical Turmoil she came to my rescue to stop that mad man Paul Cain. So many mixed signals I am not sure where to draw the line.” I take a sip of my Jack on the rocks and look down at it after I take a sip, I am sadden as I noticed the glass is now empty. As much as I might be sadden but it is not enough for me to get up and get another drink. Now I am sitting here with my glass empty left to ponder my thoughts. “There really is only one way for me to find out how she is feeling. But do I dare call her?” I place my glass down on the table right beside my chair and pick up my phone; I look at it and am tempted to dial her number. Instead I just look at the time it reads 10:55 pm; another night wasted drinking and reminiscing about the past. I put the phone down and notice I am still out of liquor. It’s weird how when you get a little tipsy you always hope your drink will magically get refilled, but it never happens. I had to get up to get more, but was it worth it? Of course it was, I needed something to keep my mind a float. “My good ole buddy Jack, one of the few that keep me a float in life, I mean it might take me away from reality but sometimes that is all someone needs. An escape from their horrible lives, I mean take me for example. The last months have consisted of me going to jail, breaking up with a girl I loved, losing match after match in a career I thought I was finally turning around, and then finally finding out I might be the father of a baby from a mother I no longer get along with. Things have definitely changed and the alcohol soothed the never ending pain.” I start to get up going for another drink but I can’t find the energy. I get half way up and then I fall right back into the chair, I decide it is not worth it between my drunken state to me being so tired I decide to rest my eyes. I close them starting to get a moment of rest. The moments started turning into hours…. The hours just kept passing… Until… My phone started to vibrate on the table next to me, I was in a daze not really sure what was waking me up or why I was still sitting in this chair instead of laying in my bed, where I belonged. Those questions were quick to leave my head as I was more worried about my phone. I picked it up and noticed I had one new text message. I was excited so excited I exited out to see the time and found it was just pushing 2:00 am, I had been out for almost four hours. I rubbed my right palm over my face to try and wake me up just enough to make it to my bedroom. It took a moment or two but I was able to muster up enough strength to get out of the chair I shut the lights off and grab my cell phone and made my way to the bedroom. I am walking in the darkness but my cell phone illuminates the room enough for me to see where I am going. I start to open my most recent text message. All it says is… “I don’t know what to do.” I look at it shake my head and quickly shut my phone, if she isn’t sure how the hell am I? I slam the phone down on my night stand and lay down in my bed sticking the pillow over my face trying to suffocate myself, it only takes moments before I am fast asleep again, not thinking about what is going on, I am in a nice peaceful drunken sleep, I will worry about it tomorrow. Chapter II: Tomorrow has arrived I lay there fully clothed, the sun shining in through my blinds, I don’t want to get up. Last night when me and my friend Jack hung out he hit me hard, and I am surely going to be feeling him today. I tossed and turned for another few seconds as I did not want to have to get up but the sun shining in made it almost impossible to fall back asleep. “Why do I keep doing this?” I questioned myself, I was unsure of myself and my abilities and drinking seemed to be the only way to make it feel better but the fact of the matter is the next day I always regretted drinking the night before. I always had to spend more hours in the gym then I wanted to, I always had to work harder since I spent the nights wasting away, hurting myself way more then I am helping myself, well that of course is in the physical aspect. The drinking made the mental and emotional aspect so much easier. “What fucking time is it?” I kept tossing and turning for a few more minutes until I turned right into the sunlight and I started to rub my eyes. I looked at the clock which is right next to me and the time states that it is 10:59 am. I had not even been asleep long enough since I was last interrupted at 2:00 am when she decided to confuse me even more. I was not really sure what she meant by her statement but I knew that the state I was in I should not act on my feelings. If I had only done those weeks before we probably would have never been in this situation, I probably would have never gone to jail and she would be lying right next to me. But the world is full of probables and if foresight was present in this world, it would be a much better place. I dug my grave and now I need to find a way out of it. I remembered about the text message I had received from her and I decided I needed to read it again to try and figure out what I was going to say in response. I flip my phone open and notice it had died while on my night stand. “Damn it!” I was easily angered that my phone had died, I lived by my phone and when it was dead I had no knowledge about whether or not people would be trying to get a hold of me or not. I shook my head angry at myself for not plugging it in before I went to bed but when I shook my head I realize why I didn’t plug it in, I was drunk and shaking my head hurt like a bastard. Once the phone was plugged in I was able to turn it on and that is when it blew up. I was in fluxed with a bunch of messages. Slowly I started reading each one, all from her. The first one was a simple question, “Why aren’t you answering me.” There was a simple answer to that, which is because I had yet to get the text message. She continued on with a message almost 5 minutes on the dot after the second one. This one asked if I still cared about her, it also went into how she felt and how she really did miss me and that the problems we had been through we can fix and make better. “How the fuck can we make this better? You dumb bitch, you put me in jail!” The angry side of me was starting to show a little more but I continued reading the text messages. I got to the third or fourth missed one, she was easily upset at this point and I was unsure about what I wanted to say to her. I knew one thing though; I was not going to express my feelings over text messages. I want to do it in person. “I guess I need to address this problem.” I put the phone down and let it charge, I walked out into the living room where I notice the chair was still reclined and in the position I left last night. The empty handle of Jack Daniels was on my kitchen counter. I look at it with a puzzled look on my face. “Did I drink all of that last night?” I think about it for a second, “Well I did just buy it yesterday; I guess that can explain why I feel like ass.” I walk over to the refrigerator I pull out a gallon of milk. After looking at the date and realizing it is ok to drink I take a sip or two. I place my elbows on the counter and rest my hung over head on top of them. Not sure what I should do, I sit there for a minute or two trying to decide. “I guess I should call her.” I sit there stressing for a minute or two, I know I shouldn’t try and contact her, but I need to. I need to speak with her. I walk to the bedroom pick up my phone and press the number one; holding it for a moment this then starts the speed dial and the name Melissa comes up on my phone. I look down reevaluating the situation wondering if I am doing the right thing. There is no chance to turn back now, I hear her voice, and it soothes me. “Hello Peter, I have been hoping you would call.” “Good morning Melissa, sorry I was asleep last night and my phone died.” “ It is ok, but we need to talk.” “I agree, but can we do it in person?” “I’d prefer that.” “Meet me in an hour at Bickfords.” “Ok.” We both hung up the phone knowing we would see each other soon but still having a reasonable doubt that the other might stand us up. I hang my head in shame, how would my dead mother feel about this? Talking to a girl who incarcerated me? She would not be happy but I am sure she would be understanding and supportive as this is what I want to do, or would she? I guess I will never know but I hang my head down thinking about what I had just done. “Only time will tell what will happen, now.” I shake a little bit before stabilizing myself, I guess the only way to get any better is a shower, I do smell. I start to walk towards the bathroom, the many of different outcomes running through my head, I hope this shower will calm me down. I guess we will see…
