Just not yet...

11 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th July 2005 ReVolution 70 is the last PRIME wrestling show before Colossus II. It’s the last chance to get things right. It’s the last in-ring time to try things. ReVolution 70 will be the last few hours before the Halo’s fall for seven tag teams. “I’m feeling rested…” Igor Arefiev whispered as Constantino Zergolov and he took their seats in the cinema. ReVolution 69 was a week off for the tag team. They spent an extra few days at home in California and then arrived in New York early for ReVolution 70. The added relaxation time allowed the tag team to finally get out and see some of the city. PRIME has been going well. The contracts are final; no ideal 4 competition can screw it up now. The tag division is reportedly “the most pwnful thing in wrestling since… ever!” Igor had been spending some time on the net and was reading this in numerous places. It assumed it was a good thing, as he wasn’t really “home with the downies” lingo, but he felt it leant itself too much appreciation for Teh Pwn. Anyway, they feel privileged to be apart of something that many consider to be amazing. Zergolov nestled into his seat as Igor sat to his left. Arefiev was dressed in blue jeans and a white tee while Zergolov was wearing khaki tee quarter length trousers and a navy tee-shirt. Neither man had popcorn nor drinks, must be a Russian thing as they sat and looked around. “Are you afraid of the Falling Halo match?” whispered Arefiev as he leaned in. Both men were initially looking ahead but Igor turned to gauge the reaction of his tag team partner. Zergolov nodded. He was well aware of Igor watching him and didn’t feel the question deserved an audible answer. Without saying it, he would never really hit him that he was scared of the match. Scared of the opponents, no… Scared of the cage that would surround the, yes… Queue the history lesson. Don’t worry I’m not going to take you back to the time in Russia, that Zergolov was trying to protect his father and ended up with nuclear waste in his eyes meaning he was blind but had enhanced his other senses to the point that he could picture his surroundings in his mind by the way he reflected or didn’t reflect the sound waves around. And don’t worry I’m not going to take you back to the time, when Igor while trying to “woo” a girl that he liked he brought her on a tour of a research laboratory. She was the intelligent sort. I’m not going to tell you that he was bitten by a radioactive spider and rather than that goodie two shoes Peter Parker, who was actually a clone, Igor stayed in wrestling. Nope, that isn’t the history of the Flying Vergomovs either although those two ideas are just MARVELlous, or maybe not? Zergolov nodded again in reply to Igor’s second question of the evening. “Do you think we can win the Tag Team Titles?” That question brought with it a more assured answer. He had to believe. They had to believe. But first… “What about Wednesday?” No; not the film beginning… yet. Igor’s third question... Zergolov nodded a third time, even though the question did not lead into a yes/no answer. He was thinking. His eyes were locked on the screen in front of him, playing out a move in his mind, not the time when he was the Daredevil or the time Igor became the money-hungry Spiderman, all the times his body had suffered because of his career decisions. Every bump he had suffered as one of the world’s greatest circus performers echoed in his eyes. Colossus II would be like nothing The Flying Vergomovs would face and sadly, even though they knew they had a match on Wednesday; their attention was focused on the remaining Halo and the match involving the whole tag team division. “Zergolov… Zergolov…” Igor’s voice creep into the thoughts of Zergolov as he pictured every big fall he had ever made and realised he would have to make all of those falls and jumps again just to weigh one match. A career worth of trapeze falls for some tag team titles… “Is it worth it?” whispered Constantino. Igor responded with a confused look. The tag team were both looking at each other. “What? Wednesday?” “No” snapped Zergolov, his voice rising over the recognised tone for the cinema. A few of the other patrons in the cinema cast their eyes towards Zergolov before turning away quickly. The film hadn’t started. He hadn’t interrupted anything. “Colossus… Are the tag team titles worth putting our bodies on the line for?” Igor nodded. 18 months as a wrestler and the pair had nothing to show for it. They were not immortalised yet, but to win at CII would reach that immortality for the pair. “Stop worrying about the Falling Halo…” coupled Igor with his nod. It was true. Since Wednesday, Constantino was far more preoccupied with the Falling Halo match than his colleague. Since Wednesday, Constantino had paid little, if any attention to the four corners tag match that they were scheduled to compete in on ReVolution. Granted, the three teams that were TFV opponents on Wednesday would feature in the Colossus match-up but Zergolov was only considering them in the capacity that they will locked inside the Halo with them. Igor was more concerned with Wednesday. Arefiev admittedly sees the Wednesday match as a chance for two things to happen. The Flying Vergomovs get one over on the Killer Pirate of the Outer Caribbean and they can soften up the three teams in time for the Falling Halo match to follow ten days later. But they are a team. Two men with different objectives won’t beat the three teams on Wednesday. Maybe the victory is what’s required. Maybe the damage is. The two Russians sat in the quiet cinema theatre. Both men had their minds on two separate matches. They can’t win. They don’t expect too… Just not yet…
