Team VIAGRA presents : the "Nothing to Say" Song

26 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/08 (Cigarette embers burn. Smoke fills the air, uncharacteristically, considering smoking is banned inside of most establishments. Very little light seeps out from hanging candles, perched on their shelves. A bartender quickly make drinks for a few of the local patrons, none too concerned with the stage resting front and center. On the platform, rests Tony Davis, holding an acoustic guitar. High Flyer is by his side, clutching the microphone in his palm. CUE: Light airy ballad music, strummed along rather badly by Flyer's partner in crime.) High Flyer: *singing* There's just nothing to say. Tony Davis: *Singing* Not a thing in the world! (Tony Davis, slurring his words, almost falls off the stage.) High Flyer: You okay there? *singing* So I wrote this song to be bold. But still to be told. There's not a thing I can sing or say... (Tony Davis hiccups.) Tony Davis: Dude. I forgot my line. And my line after that. Just the majority of my lines, if you could feed me them, that'd be great. (Flyer glares, and then smacks the mic stand down. It lands, clattering on the floor. Flyer storms off stage, and walks right to the bar. He grabs a drink, and slips the bartender a ten. Davis, confused, shrugs his shoulders.) Tony Davis: C'mon! We've got a whole set to do! There's the bridge where we talk about how DUI should get a DUI and arrive someplace DOA! (Mary-Lynn Mayweather walks up, and lays her hand on Davis' shoulder, rubbing in concentric circles to calm him down.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Don't worry Tony. You'll get screen time when we don't have a word limit. Tony Davis: You're close. See, you broke the fourth wall but you didn't add any dimension. It's totally flat. It fizzles like uncarbonated soda. I - E. Not at all. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Are you guys actually going to prepare to fight PRIME's latest addition to their rosters, DUI? (Davis and Mary-Lynn make their way over to Flyer, who overhears her question.) High Flyer: Well, we're in a bar. I'd say that's as much prep work as you need for a DUI, y'know what I'm sayin'!? (Flyer raises his hand, and Davis reciprocates the high five.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Hardly if ever. High Flyer: Don't worry Attorney, We've got this in the bag. (Flyer leans into Davis and whispers into his ear.) High Flyer: We still have that bag, don't we? (Davis' eyes look to the side, and don't return to gaze Flyer.) Tony Davis: .... Shhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhiiiiiiit. (Fade out.)
