Death Comes For Me?

12 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/12/2005 Cold. Bone chilling coldness. Like being locked in a meat freezer. Or worse…. Like death itself. That’s what I feel right now. This is the sensation my body is experiencing at this moment. But it’s not the only sensation. My body also feels like it’s weightless. As if it’s floating, slowly, downward. As I look upwards, the light fades till I don’t see anything but darkness. Pitch black darkness. Where am I? I’m so confused right now. And that’s not like me at all. Last thing I remember was beating Hessian in the ring, and then talking to Hin See about the security force he hired to keep me away from Kill……. Killean……. Killean……… KILLEAN Now I remember. I was preparing to go after him, to bury him alive, to show him what was going to happen to him at Overkill when I take his Universal Title away from him. And return it to where it belongs. Around my waist. And then it hit me, hard. With that baseball bat he carries. Using it like The Los Angeles Police Department used nightsticks to beat Rodney King. Over and over again. He beat me senseless. And then the champion used chains to bind my arms and legs. Restricting me from being able to fight back. Like the lion in The Wizard of Oz!!! COWARDLY I also remember getting in a couple of shots against Killean, inside his car though. He hadn’t secured my arms enough to keep me from breaking free. I attempted to escape, but once again, he used that bat against me. And just as quickly as I had broken free, Killean secured me again. And then, as if I was a piece of trash, Killean tossed me into the river. I don’t know what Killean did after that, but I know I couldn’t see him anymore. SPLASH I remember the sound of my body hitting the river’s majestic water. I remember the water rushing over me, as the chains and locks acted like an anchor. Slowly it seemed, I was being pulled downward. Into the darkness. Into the coldness. Away from the light. Towards certain death. I do know where I am. That answer is no longer unclear. I am falling to the river bed of my burial plot. I am heading towards my final resting place. The spot Killean chose to end my career at. Instead of facing me at Overkill. COWARDLY……. VERY COWARDLY And very slowly, I am losing my ability to breathe. My life is very slowly being withdrawn from me. It’s not suppose to end this way. Something I slept with until I started to grow bigger than the rest of my schoolmates. My mom took me and David to the Farm, something that became routine until I became a teenager. Of course, that‘s the year Dad never came back. And I hated him for years. And so here I am. At the bottom of a river. Why am I still alive? Why hasn’t my life ended. Hell, I shot up from six foot one the year before, to six foot eleven. I had made the Varsity basketball team ever since eight grade. I was on pace to break the school record for career points this coming junior season. “Chris…. Man…. Want to hit this joint?” Mark was there. Hell, Mark was every where I was.
