Lost in Translation (Culture Shock 2007, singles match vs. Sonny Silver for the Intense Title

7 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 7th, 2007 His head jarred, thumping against the tiny window on his right, waking him instantly. His eyes popped open, blinking a few times as his mind focused from dream to reality. He looked over his shoulder to see the rest of his fellow first-class plane-goers, mostly of Chinese descent. He set his head back against the headrest, his round afro flattening against the surface. He dragged his slender black fingers down his face and along his bushy beard, another attempt to adjust to the world of the conscious. "Good nap?" He turned his head to the woman sitting next to him, a young Chinese woman with defined features, long dark hair, and clad in a tight fitting gray suit that only complimented her pristine figure. To keep it simple: she was a Chinese bomb-shell. "Heh, yeah. Damn good nap," he replied, flashing a white smile and sitting up from his reclined position. He was looking pretty snappy himself, sporting a tan suit jacket, unbuttoned to show a simple black t-shirt tucked into tan pants held up with a black leather belt. "First trip to China?" she asked, turning a page in the book she was reading. "You got it," he replied. "May I ask what brings you this way?" "Business trip. May I ask who’s askin’, suga?" The question made her grin. She closed her book and slid it into a brown purse, then extended a hand. "Alicia. And you are…?" "Name’s Asa," he said, gripping her dainty palm. "It’s a pleasure." "Likewise." "Now, slap me if I’m bein’ rude, but you’re lookin’ real Asian, and your name and your voice say otherwise." She laughed a little. "I wasn’t born in China. I grew up in America to Chinese parents." "Comin’ home to visit the motha-land?" "I travel back and forth frequently." "I dig, I dig." She smiled again with perfect teeth. She reached across him, pointing out the tiny window. "It seems our trip is almost over." Asa peered out the window, the coastline of China breaking through the clouds. It was a massive piece of earth, intimidating to an outsider. "You know, suga," said The Fro, eyes still drawn to the massive continent. "From what I hear ‘bout China, a tall, black man with big hair is gonna stick out like a real sore-thumb, an’ I don’t exactly know where I’m goin’ or what the hell I’m doin’." He turned his gaze back to her brown orbs. "How ‘bout you enlighten me over a hot cup o’ coffee?" She smiled once more and placed her hand on his shoulder, leaning in close. "I’d love to." * * * * * * * "什么您要不要?" The Sultan of Soul turned to Alicia, raising an eyebrow from across the small, round table. "I’ma need a li’l help." She laughed a ridiculously cute laugh. Asa chuckled along. "He’s asking you what you would like." "Well, in that case could you tell ‘im I’d like a black coffee?" Alicia turned to the small, Chinese waiter. "他会想要无奶咖啡, 并且我会要一些绿茶" He bowed to the both of them and disappeared to the back. Asa watched the waiter leave, then turned back to Alicia, his face a tumult of confusion. "I’d betta keep you around, girl. Else I’ma be all kinds of lost." She giggled. "I guess you’ll have to." The waiter returned shortly, a black coffee for The Ayatollah of Funk n’ Soula, served in a small, handle-less tea cup, and a pot of green-tea for the beautiful Alicia. Asa cooled the steaming beverage with his breath before taking a sip. "No offense, suga," said Asa, setting the cup on the table, his face askew with distaste. "But American brew is lot more pleasin’ to the taste buds." "If you can call it ‘American brew,’ replied Alicia, sipping her tea. Asa paused a moment. "Good point." She grinned. Asa’s gaze panned around the tiny coffee house, or tea house, whatever they’re called in China. He surveyed the patrons, sipping tea and chatting in what to Asa was an incomprehensible language. He shook his head, trying to focus on the gorgeous lady across the table. "So tell me ‘bout this place, ‘Gwang-zow.’ That how you pronounce it?" "Wahn-zo," she corrected. "It’s the third biggest city in China with about twelve-million people." "Damn." "Heh. It’s the manufacturing hub of China. It brings in a lot of money." "Anywhere to get funky?" "Excuse me?" "I mean, is there any place where a real funky African brotha and one foxy Chinese lady can get their groove on?" "Are you asking me if there’s a place where we can dance?" "If you wanna sound like a damn robot," Asa replied with a wink, sipping his coffee. She sighed in an amused, accepting fashion. "Yes, I know of a few places where you and I can ‘get funky.’" "That’s it, girl! Now you’re groovin’ with me!" "Just wait until we step on the dance floor." * * * * * * * "Can I ask you a question?" The two strolled down the street. Being China, it was very busy , but the evening was upon them, and things had thinned out to a degree, enough so they could comfortably walk side by side. "Of course," she replied. "What’s a young, beautiful lookin’ woman like yourself doin’ spendin’ the day with an’ old, funky lookin’ brotha whose head’s stuck in the seventies like me?" She looked away, a small smile on her face. "Well, you’re…different." Asa looked around at the rest of the population, filled to the brim with the faces of Chinese men and women. "Don’t know what you’re talkin’ ‘bout." "Ha. And as for your age…I got bored playing with boys a long time ago, and found I was more interested in spending time with men." "Even men with big ol’ afros?" "Ha. Even those kinds." The two turned into an alley, stopping halfway down, away from the prying eyes of China, if that’s at all possible. Alicia leaned against the wall, hands behind her, eyeing Asa with that "come and get me" look, biting her lip. Asa stepped close, looking down at the breath-taking Asian beauty. "You got your own place?" he asked with a knowing smile. She nodded. "We goin’ there?" She shook her head. "We ain’t? Then where we goin’, suga?" A dull "thump" came from behind The Fro. He let out a quick groan as his eyes widened and rolled back into his head. He fell to the ground with a hard ‘thud’, out cold. "Someplace quiet." * * * * * * * His head jolted, immediately recognizing the splitting headache at the back of his skull as he came-to. He rolled his eyes around, trying to remember the past endeavor. He lifted his head, cringing at the pain. He looked around. He was in the basement of…someplace. It was large and completely empty, save for puddles of accumulated moisture in the corners and four square pillars holding up the cement roof. He was tied to a small metal chair, his suit jacket missing. He could feel a caked film on the back of his neck, probably dried blood. He let out a heavy sigh. "Well ain’t this a bitch…" "I’m afraid you’re the bitch here, asshole." From behind one of the pillars stepped the Chinese bomb-shell herself, Alicia. She walked up to Asa, hands on her hips, still clad in her business suit. "Hey, good to see ya, suga. What’s good?" She smacked him across the face, rocking his brain. "Call me that again and I’ll tear your fucking tongue out," she spat. "I wanted to vomit every time you used one of your idiotic, dated terms of endearment." "Whatever makes ya groovy, baby," he replied, spitting out blood. She smacked him again. "The same goes for ‘baby.’" He shut up. She stepped to his side and kneeled, wrapping a hand around his jaw and lifting it so he could look straight ahead. "Now, let me introduce you to the only person with the right to call me ‘baby.’" From behind the adjacent pillar stepped a young Chinese man, about mid twenties. He was dressed in a black suit and had a gelled faux-hawk on his head. A black nine-millimeter sat tucked in his belt. He stepped in front of The Sultan of Soul, arms crossed, lips drawn up in a sneer. Alicia rose and took place next to him, running her hands along his shoulders. "I’d like you to meet Lee." Asa looked him up and down, raising and eyebrow. He turned to Alicia. "I thought you said you got bored with ’boys.’" Lee smashed Asa in the jaw with a heavy back-hand. Asa was pretty sure it left knuckle prints. "You watch mouth, stupid funky nigger!" Asa smiled, not without pain, at the man’s awful English. He again looked to Alicia. "Musta found this one ‘round the block." She stared at him angrily. Lee spat on him, the loogie landing on his pant-leg. "You start talking, or I start hurting!" "Talk about what, jive turkey? This some real bullshit!" "Don’t play dumb, you fucking head-case," piped Alicia. "Girl, I don’t know what the fuck you’re talkin’ about." Another open hand across the face. "You black mother-fuckers have been the biggest pains in the ass, you know that?" she continued. "Now, before my boyfriend here fuck-starts your head, why don’t you tell us where you’re located?" Asa stared at her, completely and utterly lost. "Located? I’m from New Orleans, bitch! I told you I’m here on business!" Alicia’s boyfriend quickly pulled out his gun, gripped the barrel, and smashed The Most Happenin’ Cat in Wrestling to the side of the dome. Lights out. * * * * * * * He awoke to a bucket of water on his face. He shook his head back and forth, spitting the cold liquid from his mouth. His head-ache was outrageous, pulsing from the back of his skull and his temple. His head lolled about until his eyes came into focus. Standing in front of him was not Alicia, nor her boyfriend. This time it was a very large Chinese man, sporting a thin goatee and short hair, wearing a simple black suit. He waited for Asa to come into some kind of consciousness before speaking. "Where your gang?" This one’s English was a little better. A relief. Asa coughed a few times before answering the question. "Brotha, what gang?" A heavy slap across the face. "Where your gang?" he repeated. "Please, my man…*cough* I’m a wrestler! I wrestle for PRIME Wrestlin’, ya heard of it?" Another slap, this time colliding with the right cheek. "Don’t watch wrestling. Where your gang? Last time I ask." "I’m beggin’ ya, cat daddy! I don’t know what’s goin’ on! I…*cough* *cough* I don’t know anythin’ about no gang! You gotta believe me!" With a hard fist to the jaw, Asa found sleep once more. * * * * * * * He coughed his lungs out, spitting water all over the floor as two large Chinese men lifted his head out of the bucket, holding him by his feet. Water streamed down his face and strained through his afro, which now closely resembled a sopping wet brillow pad. "Alright, that’s enough. Put him back and leave us." The two men set The Fro back in the small metal chair, binding his hands to the back of the seat with a pair of hand-cuffs. Asa’s head hung low, hacking up gobs of water at his feet. Alicia took a moment to ponder the pathetic figure that was Asa ‘Fro’ Fountain. She shook her head back and forth, an act of pure sarcastic pity. She sauntered up to the gasping Fromaniac, kneeling down to address him face to face. "Listen," she cooed. "This can be over in a moment. Let me lay it down for you…" He lifted his head, spitting out the last of the water from his lungs. She looked into his deep brown eyes, that same look she had given him days ago in the tea-house. She ran her small hand up his thigh. "We know you’re a contact from the States. We know you have information on the Black Tiger gang…" Her fingers came dangerously close to The Sultan of Soul’s packaged goods in a terribly teasing fashion, but withdrew, running down his inner thigh. "…All you have to do is tell us where they’re located, and we’ll let you go. And who knows…" She brought her lips to his ear. "…maybe I’ll take you to my place after all…" Asa coughed and huffed a few more times, his face not that of excited hope, but that of furious anger. "Bitch, you must think I’m some kind of damn idiot if you expect me to believe that jive." She slapped him viciously, a fingernail catching his cheek, drawing blood. "You are an idiot. An idiot and a fucking fool." She leaned in close, eyes narrowed. Asa returned the hateful gaze. "You have one more day, asshole. One more day before we put a bullet through that moronic bush you call a hair-do." She turned and walked off, hips swaying. Asa breathed heavily, blinking water out of his eyes. "Love you too, suga." * * * * * * * The view of Guangzhou was quite a site to behold. Asa stared out across the massive city, looking down at the tiny Chinese population pushing their way through the crowded streets. As colossal as the city was, it had a depressing feel to it, everything gray, including the sky above them. Wind blew across his face, stinging his wounds as he was pushed to the edge of the roof, a good twenty stories high. The barrel of a pistol pushed through his afro, resting against the back of his skull. He knew it wasn’t the only gun trained for his head. Lee’s whole gang was right behind him. "This your last chance," came the voice from behind, no doubt belonging Lee. "You tell us location of Black Tigers, or I put bullet through back of head!" Asa’s hands were up at his sides. He closed his eyes and took a deep breath. "Look, my brotha…" "I not brother!" screamed Lee, pushing the gun harder against The Fro’s noggin. Asa stopped, trying not to make things worse. "Look," he continued. "I don’t know what’s goin’ on. My name is Asa Joseph Fountain. I’m from New Orleans, Louisiana. I’m a wrestler for PRIME wrestlin’. I don’t know who the hell the ‘Black Tigers’ are. I don’t know anythin’! You gotta believe me, cat daddy!" "Just blow his brains out," said a voice that could only belong to Alicia. Lee grinned. "Good idea. Too bad, dumb nigger! Make piece with black God!" Lee cocked back the hammer. Asa closed his eyes and grit his teeth, ready to meet his maker. Click. Click click. Click. Lee stopped. His eyes narrowed at the sounds of a multitude of loading fire-arms. He spun away from The Fro. Asa waited for a bullet to kill the last of the funk this world had to offer, but it never came. Slowly, he turned to see what was going on. His heart nearly jumped out of his head to see a large group of black men on the opposite end of the roof, clad in your general gangsta rap attire; flat brimmed ball caps, big puffy jackets, baggy pants, etc. Each one had a "I’ma bust a cap in yo’ ass!" kind of look, not to mention a big gun pointed at Lee and his gang. At the forefront stood a smaller brother, clad in a Philadelphia 76ers jacket, flat Philly ball cap, corn-rows, a thin goatee, and one big fucking automatic weapon. Lee’s eyes were wide with fright and anger. "You black mother fuc…" "Why don’t you shut yo’ god-damn mouf an’ leave our brotha alone, Wang Chung," said what apparently was the leader of the now present Black Tigers. Lee’s gang was caught off-guard, guns at their sides, afraid to lift and aim at the group of angry African-Americans. "You bitches wanted to know where we was at? Well, here we is," continued the leader, pulling the magazine pin of his weapon back, clicking into "waste your ass-mode." "How the hell did they find us!?" screamed Alicia, frozen with fear, but with balled fists at her side. "We always know when a brotha comes into Guangzhou," answered the small black man. "You think we was gonna leave a nigga hangin’ like dat?" In unison, the entire Black Tiger gang lifted their weapons and took aim. "I don’t think so." Lee’s face boiled red with rage. "This not what supposed to happen! I kill all stupid niggers!" "Aww, shit!," yelled Asa, diving to the ground. Lee lifted his pistol to fire, but the roar of twenty or so machine guns interrupted. Lee and his gang twitched as their bodies were riddled with bullets, red clouds of spray drifting into the air. They fell more or less as one, a few bodies rolling off the now blood-stained roof. In a moment it was over, only the sounds of the busy city of Guangzhou filled the air. Asa lay with his hands covering his head. He waited, then slowly stood to his feet. He looked at the crew of angry black men, guns now lowered. He turned his gaze to the pile of dead Chinese gangsters, Lee’s face frozen in rage. Asa shook his head, running a hand through his afro. "Well ain’t that some shit…" The small leader of the Black Tigers walked up to Asa, hand extended. "You a-ightt, nigga?" he asked. "Yeah…helluva lot better now," replied Asa, clasping then knuckle knocking the leader’s fist. "Name’s T-Billz," he turned and pointed to the rest of the gang with his gun. "We the Black Tigers. We heard you was comin’ this way, and heard dat bitch snatched you up real good. We woulda saved yo’ ass sooner, but we had to find out where you was first." "That’s cool, my man, that’s cool. I’m just glad you came at all. I should be lyin’ on the street down there with a big ol’ hole in my face, know what I’m sayin’? "Yeah, I know watcha sayin’. Looks like we show’d up just in time." "For real." The two then engaged in a very long, complicated negro handshake. Upon finishing with a knuckle knock and finger wiggle, a whimper came from the ground. They looked down to see Alicia, curled up with hands over her head, eyes shut tight, gritting her teeth.. T-Billz snorted. "Huh. We missed a bitch." He pointed his gun down to the prone Alicia, but was halted by Asa with an extended hand. "Hold up, brotha," T-Billz obliged and backed away. Asa knelt down, gently grabbing Alicia by the shoulders and helping her to her feet. She twitched with fear, but slowly opened her eyes when she realized she wasn’t getting shot. Her eyes fell upon Asa’s. "You alright, suga?" She nodded. "Too damn bad!" Asa clobbered her in the jaw with a heavy blow, knocking her out cold and sending her back to the concrete roof. The Black Tiger gang chuckled as Asa shook the sting front his hand. "What’s good, bitch? What’s really good?!" he yelled down at the unconscious Alicia. T-Billz laughed and draped an arm around The Ayatollah of Funk n’ Soula, helping him hobble over to the group. The rest of the Black Tigers gave Asa high fives, knuckle knocks, and claps on the shoulder. Asa cringed in pain, but his white smile was intact. "How’d ya’ll brothas know I was comin’ this way?" asked The Fro. "You shittin’ us, nigga?" said one of the gangstas. "We be watchin’ PRIME fa real!’ "No doubt!" piped another. "We knew you n’ tha PRIME crew was havin’ that pay-per-view here in Guandzhou," explained T-Billz. "We figured we’d keep n’ eye on a brotha. We niggas stick out ‘round here." "Good to know ya’ll got a brothas back," said Asa. "So, what, your little war with this jive turkey’s crew over?" asked Asa, nodding toward Lee’s lifeless body. "Naw," replied T-Billz. "That bitch is small-fish. There a lot more where he came from." "I dig, I dig. You gonna pop a cap in that bitch’s ass?" asked Asa, pointing to Alicia. T-Billz scratched his chin with the barrel of his gun. "Hmmm….now that I think about it, naw. I got plans for that trip-ass biatch." A larger member of the gang walk up to Alicia’s tiny, out-cold body and hoisted her onto his shoulder. "She gon’ help us find the rest of these motha fuckas." Asa nodded, contempt in his eyes as he glared at her unconscious face. He turned back to T-Billz. "So, think ya’ll cat daddies can give this funky brotha a ride to his hotel?" T-Billz laughed, clapping Asa on the shoulder. "Nigga you ain’t goin’ to no hotel! You comin’ to stay wit us! You gotta rest up, eat some soul-food, n’ drink some forties, ya feel me?" Asa smiled, patting T-Billz on the back. "I feel ya, brotha. Can’t do enough to repay ya." "You can start by gettin’ us tickets to Culture Shock. We wanna see you whup Sonny Silver’s cracka ass, fa sho!" "I gotcha covered, my man," said Asa as he and the gang started to leave, filing through a small door and down a flight of gritty stairs. "But you gotta answer me one question," "Watchoo wanna know?" asked T-Billz. "What are ya’ll niggas doin’ in China? Why ain’t’cha back home in Philly, or wherever you’re from?" "Nigga, you know how much dough there is ta make in China?" answered T-Billz, as if it were obvious. "N’ besides, we all got a thing for them yellow-skinned hoes." Asa turned to look at Alicia’s motionless frame, draped over the big man’s shoulder, then turned back to T-Billz, smiling wide. "No offense, my man, but when it comes to the yellow-skinned hoes, I think this brotha's lost his appetite."
