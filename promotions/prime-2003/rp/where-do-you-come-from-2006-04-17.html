Where Do You Come From?

17 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/17/06 Years ago, a young man joined the Israeli Defense Force. He was tall, strong and handsome, caught the eye of the girls around him and was instilled with a sense of duty that his parents had instilled in him from the time he was old enough to understand their words. Living where he did, everyone that was a citizen served in the IDF. He was not about to shirk from this duty. Basic training hadn't phased him; he enjoyed keeping in shape, being strong, being as well-conditioned as he could make himself. Boot camp had pushed him a little, but not enough to truly bother him. Drill sergeants would have him do extra push-ups just because he was the biggest in the group, and he did them without complaint. He strove to keep pace with the fastest runners. And now, he'd been selected for a special assignment. While the rest of his battalion went off to infantry training, he did not. So there he sat in a small, dark room, with one lightbulb overhead in a chair that was entirely too small for him. But he sat there, anyway, hands on knees and eyes straight ahead, looking at the door ahead of him. He'd tried to observe everything in the room: it was made of concrete blocks, ten across from wall to wall and twelve high. The door was made out of solid steel and obviously locked from the outside. There were no apparent windows or mirrors, there were two vents into the room. There were no apparent cameras, though the big youth knew that didn't mean they weren't there. Click. The door opened and in the doorway stood a man in unremarkable gray fatigues. He looked at the big youth in the middle of the room. He stepped in and pushed the door shut behind him with little apparent effort. The big youth regarded him carefully. He was slender, tall and lean. He seemed to have had every bit of unnecessary body weight boiled off of him. His eyes were dark and unreadable and his face was set into a mask of neutrality. He was unlike anyone the kid had ever met; his body language betrayed nothing, his face was totally in control. All that the recruit knew was that this man was quite possibly one of the most intense and possibly dangerous men he'd ever met. “St. James, Nimrod,” the man said in a flat monotone. “But you've been called Titan for a long time. Nimrod is an honorable name, the name of a man that was a great hunter and warrior. Thanks to the cartoon character of Bugs Bunny, it has picked up a connotation of an insult. But, your nickname is appropriate and acceptable. Therefore, you will be Titan.” The man walked into the room, taking measured steps. Titan could feel his eyes probing, examining. It was like being under a microscope. It also made Titan feel remarkably like a bug on a pin; no movement, nowhere to go but just sit there and be examined. “You obviously can't be part of the infantry, so you've been assigned to me.” He looked at him, pausing. His heels clicked together, his hands behind the small of his back. “Of course, you don't know what this will entail, do you? You're being trained to be part of the most strict military police unit in the world. No other military has the need for such a powerful military police, no other military has the need for such discipline as we have. The IDF does not fail, because failure means loss of everything we hold dear. Mothers, fathers, sisters, brothers, homelands, all lost if the IDF falls.” Titan regarded the man coolly. Was this some sort of indoctrination? Was it some sort of induction? He knew what he fought for, he knew why the IDF existed. But who was this man? What did he want? “Now,” the other man began, “drop to the floor and begin doing push-ups.” Titan responded immediately, dropping to the floor and beginning his repetitive task. He did not go quickly, because the man had not specified how many to do. Instead, he moved at a smooth, even pace, up and down, up and down. “I,” the man continued on in his neutral monotone, “am called Profile 21.” Titan blinked Profile 21? It was a designation he'd never heard before, no profile designations in his mind from boot camp or any other time. “I am not part of the military proper. I'm actually someone that's more of a paramilitary, ready to serve this nation in ways that many find appalling, but are assured by its constitution.” Titan knew what that meant: assassination was allowed under their constitution. This man was nothing to regard lightly. “I am not going to instruct you on becoming what I am,” he said, his boots clicking on the concrete floor as he circled the large man. “You are too big, too bulky and totally unable to blend into your surroundings because of it.” The big man was not sure whether this was a blessing or a curse. Still, he continued on, lifting his weight, dropping, in smooth motions. The boots stopped right in front of him. “I have to admit, I'm not entirely sure why they assigned you to me. I know why they told me they did, but their motives...” he stopped abruptly, sounding for the first time like he was not simply reciting a litany. “Suffice to say, I have my doubts about you.” Titan didn't look up, but his eyes suddenly lit up. The man had doubts about him? Granted, what he said was true enough; he couldn't blend in well, he was conspicuous to a fault just because he stood out in any setting. But having doubts about him? The boot heels parted as the man took a step back. Titan could just see the shining toes of polished black at the top of his vision, then had to blink. It was hot in the room, but it hadn't seemed that way before. He blinked furiously, trying to bat the sweat out of his eyes. Still, he continued, up and down, up and down. “You did fine in boot camp, but we have a cadre of cadets pass through basic training every year. Some shake out well, others do not. You, I think, will not. You rely on your size and strength to do too much, you won't think near as much as you should. A hot temper will get you in trouble and you will assume that your physical might will get you out of it.” The boots stepped closer again. “In fact, I know very well you won't do well. Your parents are not native Israelis, they're Americans. You, my massive recruit, are soft.” Titan wanted to hop up, get in the man's face. He wanted to prove he was not what he was accused of. But he had been given an order, and he was going to see it through. He continued his push-ups, though his arms began to shake at the effort. “Look at you,” he said, “weak. You don't have the blood of survival in you, it's the blood of conquering sick natives, cheating them of territory and erecting a fast food restaurant.” Titan's blood could almost boil. It seemed hotter in the room as well. That's when the boots picked up, and landed right on his shoulders. Titan's arms shook under the weight, but he took a moment, then went on to the next push-up. “How are other people supposed to depend on you when you can't carry the weight of just one more?” came the mockingly neutral statement. “Like I said, kid, you're soft.” That word rang in Titan's ears and he set his jaw and forced himself to continue, now with the near two hundred pounds on his back. He knew this was a test and he wasn't going to fail. He... wasn't... going... to fail... The man kept his balance remarkably well. While he stood on Titan's back, his feet made small shifts and changes in order to keep him in place. Titan's mind kept going despite the extreme physical exertion. This man was like one of those types that people wrote about in spy novels: he was intelligent, strong, balanced and so on. Titan's arms shook with exertion, but he kept going as best he could. The boots mercifully stepped off after what seemed like forever. Titan wanted to collapse to the floor, but he kept his arms straight, then lowered himself again. Then back up. His arms screamed in burning exertion. That's when a boot took him directly in the rib cage. It was followed up by another, then a stomp on his left shoulder. Titan hissed at the addition to his exertion. He forced his arms to straighten to get the boot off. Another kick took him in the shoulder. “Weak. Weak. Weak. You'll never be what they want you to be.” The attacks just kept coming. Booted feet hit Titan all over, but the big man doggedly kept trying to do more push-ups. His arms quivered as he pushed himself up.... “Attention!” came the barked order. Titan dropped his knees down and rose to his feet as quickly as possible, snapping his heels together and pulling his aching arms close to his sides. The man that called himself Profile 21 looked at him. “Have a seat, St. James.” Titan blinked, but complied with the order. He set his forearms across his thighs and kept his back straight. “At ease, recruit,” came the next statement. Titan kept his back straight, refusing to bend. The man broke into a smile. “You're tough kid. You're real tough. You knew what I was doing was a test, but you passed the real test. After this whole thing, I give you an at-ease and you don't take it. Son, you're a certain kind of tough, a certain kind of duty-driven. That's what I needed to know. Know I need you to be honest with me.” Titan nodded slowly. “What I'm going to teach you to do is interrogation, finding deserters and defending positions. It's not all that I know how to do, but it's what the higher-ups want out of you. This isn't going to be pretty. You'll be breaking men's spirits, you'll be the villain for the boy that ran home when he couldn't take the service. But you're needed. They need someone that can operate as a one-man recovery unit or get information on his own. You're needed, and I can make you into it. Or you can go back to the infantry and be a big target.” Titan looked at him. Someone was finally being frank about what was expected, no needed of him. Titan sat there, looking at him, rolling this over in his head. Profile 21 stood there, looking back at him. His face was impassive, his body betrayed nothing. Titan's decision wouldn't bother him in the slightest, whichever way it went. “I'll do it,” came Titan's reply.
