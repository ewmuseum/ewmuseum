Team VIAGRA, the PILOT(FX Pitch, '09 Season: Working Pilot title, "Penis Envy")

September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September, 2008 EXT. THE HARMEN FAMILY HOUSEHOLD -- NIGHT Bright lights beam from the inside. Loud shouts are heard. It appears that Tony and Tammy Davis are currently in the midst of an argument. TONY DAVIS I don't care how hot our kid would look. No! TAMMY You told me you'd do anything for this family! TONY DAVIS Yeah! With my OWN penis! Not Matthew Mcconaughey's. TAMMY Well, maybe you need your penis checked... TONY DAVIS You know, you have more crazy in common with your brother than you think. Tony Davis steps out onto the front porch, and slams the door behind him. He takes a few fuming steps toward the driveway, where Flyer's '87 Chrysler Lebaron sits idling. Dusk sits shotgun, and Mary-Lynn is huddled over the back passenger, hugging the seat cushion in front of her. HIGH FLYER Are you embarrassed or pissed? TONY DAVIS (ignoring question) Why am I here again? DUSK Why IS he here? Dusk crosses his arms and does not return a single glance from the Degenerate. HIGH FLYER Hop in. The Vegas train is LEAVING STATION FORTY TWO! All aboard the crazy train! TOOT TOOT! There's a bit of a silence. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER (in disbelief) ... did you just go toot toot? Tony Davis sighs, and then rushes around the backside of the car. He leaps up and tries to swing himself through the passenger side window, but his jean gets caught on a small metal petrusion, which catches him in an awkward position and causes him to smash his head against the top of the car. Granted, it's a top up convertible, but it also ripped a straight line up the seam of his pants leg. He slips in through the window gap and flops into place, disgruntled. There's a soft CRUNCH sound as he lands. HIGH FLYER ... oh shit. You just sat on your fortune. Tony reaches below him and pulls out a crumpled and destroyed Chinese fortune cookie. Which a shift into gear and a bit of burning rubber, Flyer peals the car in reverse out of the driveway with reckless abandon. He shifts into 1st without coming to a completely stop, and tosses his fist skyward. HIGH FLYER (screaming) TOOT TOOT! Dusk can't look at Jack as he does this, and can only stare at him confusedly out of the corner of his eyes. With a quick lurch, the car speeds off into the distance. A card flies onto the screen. It reads, "INSERT TITLES" EXT. LAS VEGAS HOTEL -- POST MIDNIGHT The group of four exit their parked car and begin to walk side by side. With all the blinking lights, it hardly feels like night time. HIGH FLYER Shooting blanks eh? Good thing you aren't in the civil war. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER Does anyone else find it ironic that half of Viagra doesn't work anymore? TONY DAVIS Would you stop talking about my penis as if it's dead! (turning to Dusk) I'm sure you have something witty and sardonic to reply to me with. Go ahead. Chop me down. Make me feel the crotch kick of your personal crotch related insults. Dig deep. Choose your best ball joke Craig. GO NUTS. They reach their destination, the Bellagio Grand hotel and casino. Tony swings open the glass doors and enters, as Dusk grabs the door and holds it open for his compatriots. They enter before him. INT. BELLAGIO CASINO -- CONTINUOUS Immediately, an onslaught of your senses occurs. Lights, bells, whistles, a maze like structure of a vacuum of personal fortune. They enter the chaos as if it's no big deal. DUSK I don't, HATE you Tony. And if you've got a real problem I'm not gonna make it worse. But shit man, you annoy me when you're not in despair. Wait, is that a bad thing to say? Is it a bad sign that I hate someone when they're happy, but love them when they're miserable? HIGH FLYER At least you're honest about it. It's like Bush and black people, except he's Harvey Dent'ed. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER What? HIGH FLYER Two faced. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER Oh. (to Tony) Just, forget about it. Y'know, let's have fun! Win some money! ... Then you can buy yourself a new penis. Flyer doubles over laughing. Mary-Lynn had to stop herself as she said it, and bursts at the moment Jack does. Dusk snickers slightly, but tries to hide it. He turns, faking a cough, as he catches the eye of a cute brunette Blackjack dealer. He winks, and she laughs before cracking a smile. A montage, flurry of shots. Mary-Lynn pulls down a slot lever. Dusk flips a poker chip in the air. High Flyer raises his hands at a roulette wheel. Tony Davis waits for the bathroom. Mary-Lynn is dragged away, kicking and screaming by security. Dusk spits in Daniel Negreanu’s face. And finally, we land on Tony Davis, sitting at a blackjack table, playing with the same cute dealer that Dusk caught glimpse of earlier. TONY DAVIS Come on. You guys have got to stop raping me. Rape is still illegal in Vegas, right? Even if it stays in Vegas? INT. LAS VEGAS CASINO BAR – LATER High Flyer and Mary-Lynn Mayweather sit next to each other, sipping on their drinks. Behind them, Dusk kicks a slot machine. He ROARS, as if he’s going to destroy it, but then storms off. HIGH FLYER So, I’m sorry this is less celebratory than initially intended. I figured you’d have had a good shot against Savant. Still. Flyer raises his glass, and the clink in a toast. HIGH FLYER To personal victory. To achievement. To the future. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER It’s just… it’s disappointing, exhilarating. It… It felt right and wrong all at once, y’know? Flyer smiles. HIGH FLYER I lost my first match. Mary-Lynn reacts. Flyer smiles and raises his hand to calm her question. HIGH FLYER Not just here in PRIME against Tsonda. I lost my first match in Mexico back in 1993. El Hijo del Santo, guy was a legend. Then I came to America, I lost my first match against Edward Glide. I never got to beat that guy. And my first IWO Match? I lost to some sucker named Dr. Foog. I STILL don’t even know how to pronounce his name! And in fWo? I lost to Great Dragon… so I know how it is to go out into unknown territory and fail. I mean, it’s like gambling. See all around here? You know you’re not going to win EVERY time you pull the lever, every time you get some hole cards, every time you split eights… I mean, what’s the point if you’d always win? The thing is, you’ve got to win more often than you lose. You’ll experience both, but you gotta push past the bad, revel in the good. Otherwise this world’ll kill ya ‘for you know it. And with that, Flyer downs the last of his drink, and spins on his barstool. He spots a busty female and wraps his arm around her waist, walking in stride. HIGH FLYER Just look pretty until we get to the end of the row. It’s for the cameras. They walk, stride in stride. The woman gives Flyer a glare and looks weirded out. As they reach the edge of the aisle, Flyer retracts his arm and the two go their separate ways. Flyer looks back at the camera as he walks, and noticing it still filming. He fumes, races up, and starts studying it, looking for the off switch. He sighs, frustrated. HIGH FLYER This’ll teach you to take away my illusion of sexiness! Quickly, he drops the camera and punt kicks it, causing the entire picture to go to static. FADE
