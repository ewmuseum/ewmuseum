Buddy Comedy, Part II

Date: Space-time continuums need not apply! SUNDAYDanny's still in front of the TV. The same report is playing on E!, because you know that a channel like that doesn't have enough material to run original shit 12 hours a day. A slight stubble has appeared on Danny's face, and his hair is still uncut. He's got a phone to his ear, and you can guess who he's talking to. ‘ARE YOU FUCKING WATCHING FUCKING E!?!’ Danny holds the phone away from his ear for a moment, waiting for Facey to finish. ‘Of cour-’ ‘ARE YOU FUCKING WATCHING E!—ASSHOLE?!’ He says, obviously unfinished. ‘Of fucking course I’m watching E! I’m not calling you for the fun of it you heat-thieving bastard. It is true, you know? You ARE a heat-stealing son of a bitch. But to THINK that you would sell out your partner, your ‘friend,’ makes me disgusted to even be associated with the name Illustrious Fa—’ ‘THAT FAKE NEGRO PRICK COMPARED MY TO JONATHAN LIPNICKI!’ A ritual, Danny again removes the phone from his ear. ‘I’m not going to fucking stand for it, Dan the Man. I’m suing his ass for sexual defamation. There is no fucking way my head is as big as that ugly shit.’ Of course, Danny’s anger is peaking. He means, the nerve of this fucking asshole, calling him after taking the opportunity that he provided, leaving him in the dust. That’s not the A-List way. Well, it is. But it’s still rude. ‘I’m going to destroy the Stuart Little franchise because of this, do you hear me D-Man? You’ve got to help me. What’s your lawyer’s name? Isn’t that slander or some shit?’ The Good Christian Soul within Danny is nonexistent, so he doesn’t have any hesitation in telling the Face to fuck off. ‘Adam; fuck off.’ ‘What?!? Where’s the love?! Is that how you’re gunnnnnna treat me, Danerino? After everything I’ve done for you?’ Instantly, it’s as if Danny was possessed by Satan. ‘Everything... you’ve done for me?’ With so many problems pertaining to the situation, Danny didn’t know where to begin. It all sort of came out at once. ‘I take you under MY wing, give you gigs at MY projects, let you hang with ME and Chandler; and you can call me and cut ME off? Ignore MY concerns? You spineless son of a bitch, you don’t even care about wrestling together! Then you steal all of MY thunder from Hollywood, an opportunity I gave you, and don’t even help with MY projects? And what’s with the fucking nicknames?!?’ Danny takes a breather, allowing Facey to chirp in a few answers. ‘Actually, Fergonomics, that’s funny you should mention that, because that’s one of the things Ollie and I talked about the other day; the use of nicknames in Hollywood. So amusing, wouldn’t you agree?’ ‘Shut up. Ollie who? What are you talking about?’ It should have clicked to Danny right away. Sadly, realization will take its time to sink. ‘Well that’s why I called, Danmark. Yesterday, when you dropped me off I met this guy named Ollie Something who directs flicks. He’s some old Vietnam vet who has a hard-on for making movies about the damn war I guess.’ ‘ohmygod.’ Danny whispers. ‘And he said he needed help on his next project, some western about-’ ‘SON OF THE MORNING STAR?! Is the movie called Son of the Morning Star?’ ‘Actually, now that you mention it-’ ‘YOU MET OLIVER. FUCKING. STONE?!’ Danny interrupts again. He had been in the business for over a decade, and he couldn't even get an invite to the same party as Oliver Stone. He was three steps away from sitting outside the guy's house like a fanboy. How the hell could Facey meet Oliver Stone after being in LA for 1/2432569859ths of the time that he was? ‘Ollie’s first name is Oliver? What a fucking faggot! I’m going to give him so much shit for that-’ ‘Facey, you have to listen to me.’ Somewhere, in Danny’s mind, there was a solution in all of this. ‘I need you to talk to him for me. We need a director for ‘More Than Just Hardcore’ really bad.’ Oliver Stone is a great director, one Danny needs, and Facey knows him. There are no other possible scenarios in the world when he could land Oliver Stone. Oh boy, maybe Facey wasn’t really selling his fellow A-Lister out. Maybe he really was a reliable guy. Maybe Danny should stop question the how's of his sudden success and start doing what he does best - milking the situation for his own personal benefit. ‘Eh, sorry Dizzan. See, Ollie was all tied up with some stupid project about westerns, so I told him. ‘Dude, Ollie, Westerns are gay.’ He made a Brokeback Mountain joke but I slapped him because those are played out, and I think he realized what he was getting in to. So I showed him this comic book I’m reading, the Walking Dead-’ Danny can’t believe what he’s hearing. ‘The Walking Dead?’ he asks. ‘Yeah, it’s this totally cool zombie comic book. Anyways, I told him that epics are boring people these days and if he wants to cash in, a sick ass zombie flick is the way to go. So we talked to the writer of the book and got the rights, and now we’re making a zombie flick.’ ‘Wait wait wait wait wait. You’re telling me that in a single day; you became a major player in Hollywood, met Oliver Stone, convinced him from making what could be a classic Western film, instead told him to make a campy zombie movie, and now you’re in business with him?’ ‘Yeah, and we want you to star in it.’ It’s not exactly the news he expected, but it could work. ‘Well, I guess I could be involved when the Funk Biopic is done.’ ‘No can do, DFerg. We’re making up for wasted time on Son of the Morning Star and casting right away. Shooting starts in a month.’ ‘Well it’d be physically impossible for me to work with you guys, Adam, you know I’m under contract-’ ‘WELL SNOOZE YA LOOZE ASSHOLE, DON’T SAY I NEVER GAVE YOU NOTHING!’ And the Face hung up. Good thing, too. Danny needs to be alone. MONDAY Out on the streets of LA, the daily living of the inhabitants is disrupted, as usual, by some stupid Hollywood thing. The left side of a stretch along Rodeo Drive is blocked off to everyone but the unpaid extras who came here from Cedar Rapids and Sioux Falls so they could work the night shift at In 'N Out and hope some big executive notices their pretty face when they ask for a value meal, but every idiot in this town knows that the assistants are the ones who get the food. Cameras are everywhere, and a makeshift makeup room to the right of the 'set' is dolling up some other nobodies in some pretty gross zombie getups. Two director's chairs are sitting slighty behind the cameras. The names on them make sure we know what's going on here. O. Stone I.F. Eater As we take in the scene, letting ourselves tear up slightly at the magic of Hollywood, we are once again privy to the enemy of words like ‘magic’ and ‘serenity,’ Adam Dick. ‘...UGUGGGGGGUUUUUUHHHHHHHH! Just like that, really pronounce the Gs.’ Facey is standing outside a shop being used for the shoot. To his left is a man in half-finished zombie gear, and in front of him stands one of the doe-eyed waitresses we discussed earlier. Facey is coaching the performance of his zombie actor...while at the same time copping a mean feel on this girl's rack. ‘Really squeeze when you slam her against the wall. You're not pulling them off, but you want to leave a mark. How's this treatin' you, sugartits?’ She's too frightened to manage a response, but luckily the situation is interrupted when the trademark green H2 rolls into the parking lot - and into Facey's peripheral vision. He gives her a parting grope before wiping his hands on the zombie and storming towards the SUV. A security guard tries to hail him, but he's already screaming. ‘This is a CLOSED MOTHERFUCKING SET, Pablo! You know what that means? El closed! Like the fucking border gate you had to climb over to get here!’ He shoves the guard aside and stands, hands-on-hips in an ‘Oh No you didn't!’ pose, in front of the Hummer, waiting for Danny Ferguson to emerge. When Danny sets a foot down on the pavement, Facey kicks the side door, smacking it into his partner's face. ‘OW!’ he shouts, jumping out and grabbing his head. ‘What the hell was that?’ ‘This is a closed set, Daniel-san, what are you doing at my party?’ ‘Well I was supposed to be doing make-up interviews across town, but whaddya know, no one came because everyone's out here to watch you guys. How the hell are you filming already? ‘We bumped up the production schedule,’ Facey snorts. ‘Turns out most of the time movies take up is lazy, promotion-based nonsense. I could film a four-hour epic in eight hours if I was really prepared.’ Danny's bodyguards pile out of the car, followed closely by an E! crew. This does not please any Eaters in the house. ‘WHAT? WHO LET THESE DOUCHEBAGS IN?’ ‘You did see them just climb out of my car, right?’ ‘WELL SINCE WE'RE ASKING QUESTIONS, WHY DON'T YOU TELL ME WHAT 'RHETORICAL' MEANS, COCK-ASS!’ ‘Relax,’ Danny says. ‘They were here to spend a day with me, and now that my promo stuff has been cancelled, they need to get footage somehow. So they're tailing me. They're not here to sneak on your stupid zombie movie.’ Adam prepares to unload on his fellow 5-Star champion for his use of the word 'stupid,' but he's interrupted by a voice from back on set. There, a gray-haired man who looks equally intense and stoned gestures to him. ‘Facey,’ Oliver Stone says, ‘we're ready for this shot.’ ‘Hang on, Ollie Oxenfree, I'm just wrapping up with some uninvited guests.’ As he walks off, we hear Stone's voice again. ‘Is that Sean Astin?’ Danny lunges to go after him, but Pibb and Fuqueiawytas hold him back, avoiding what would surely be some atrocious pub. Left alone to his own devices and his own camera crew, Danny stews for a second, then calms down enough for his self-serving killer instinct to kick in. ‘Fine,’ he growls. ‘Dick hijacked my event last week, and now I'm going to hijack his.’ A few minutes later, the E! crew is set up just beyond the set, virtually unnoticeable. Danny is perched on a chair next to Epiphanny, the bleached bimbo in a sleeveless polo who poses as an interviewer. ‘Now, Danny,’ she says, ‘you say you were originally supposed to star in the new StoneFace production, The Walking Dead?’ ‘That's right, Epiphany, but I turned the role down.’ ‘Why would someone turn down a role in what could become the biggest film of the year, featuring two of Hollywood's most talked-about power players?’ ‘I simply didn't believe in it. I'm not just an action hero, Phanny. I want to be taken seriously as an actor.’ ‘Are you saying you couldn't be taken seriously in a movie like The Walking Dead?’ ‘Your words, not mine. I'm sure there are plenty of people out there who take big, bloody, comic-book zombie movies seriously.’ If this was war, Danny had just pulled the pin and lofted a smoke grenade, sky-hook-style, overhead into Face-Eater territory. His snide smile sealed the deal. ‘Besides,’ he continued, ‘I already have a project in the works. 'More Than Just Hardcore' is going to surprise a lot of people, and we're all really happy with how it's shaping up.’ ‘Even though you don't have a director?’ ‘This project is so big that a director will just be the icing on the cake. Besides, we ought to have a decision made on that by the end of this week.’ The interview wraps and the crew begins to pack it in. Danny and Phanny keep chatting, though, with the cameras off. ‘I'm impressed, Danny,’ she says. ‘To turn down a project like this...especially to let someone like Bruce Campbell take over the starring role.’ ‘WHAT?’ Danny spouts. ‘Campbell's in this? What the fuck!’ He looks around the small crowd of people and eventually spots the former Ash standing at the craft services table. ‘ET TU, BRUCE?’ Brisco County shrugs at him and then takes another bite of his turkey sandwich. ‘I had an hour to kill,’ he replies between mouthfuls. Ferg curses before turning back to his company. She's shaking her head in that way people do when they marvel at something. ‘You know, part of me wants to condemn this project for what it will do to Stone's career and for the far-reaching economic impact that the expedited production schedule will have on the smaller and more impressive film houses...but I'll be damned if that Face-Eater friend of yours can't charm the heck out of anyone. On set, Facey's ears perk up, bringing him out of his chair. ‘WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU SAYING ABOUT ME, BITCH? I FUCKING HEARD MY NAME! DON'T YOU EVER SAY MY NAME IN PUBLIC AGAIN! I WILL RIP OUT YOUR VOICEBOX AND THEN SHIT IN THE HOLE WHERE IT USED TO BE SO THAT YOU WILL BE TALKING SHIT FOR THE REST OF YOUR MISERABLE BOTTLE-TANNED LIFE!’ Studio personnel detain him as he tries to get at the E! girl. Danny, feeling his work is done, high-tails it for the H2, ready to leave the E! people behind if worse comes to worse. The damage had been done and the spotlight was at least partially back on him. Meanwhile, Facey, still fuming and cursing, is dragged in the opposite direction by an ever growing crowd. Oliver Stone can only watch the scene play out, wondering what might have been. ‘Cut,’ he says. TUESDAY It started out as a day much like any other day. Danny receieved a phone call from Facey... ‘Yeah?’ Of course Danny hadn’t shaved since the E! crew decided to stick around the base of operations for ‘the Walking Dead’ simply because he hasn’t felt the need. ‘What the hell is all of that noise?’ He didn’t even watch E!, in bitter contempt of their disregard towards the arrangements they’d made. Instead, he watched Jetex all morning for a marathon of the old X-Men cartoon. ‘Is that the sound of someone banging a monkey’s head against a rock by his tail? Adam... what’s ‘so fucked’?’ It was in the middle of the Apocalypse series of episodes, so it must have been a good reason to pull Danny away from such awesome mutant mayhem. He even ditched a reading of the script revisions to watch cartoons, and in turn, to help Facey out. ‘Help? Why do you need my help?’ Danny found the answer to his question as soon as he arrived on-set. Hearing all of the calamity in the background amidst his conversation with the Face-Eater, perhaps there was a riot going on? Seeing it with his eyes, he never would have thought such a ludicrous statement to be true. But here it is; a small-scale riot. He almost wished he hadn’t sent Pibb and Fuqueiawytas early. Okay, fuck saying ‘almost’. He’s texting the shit out of them as we speak. ‘WE DEMAND RESPECT!!!’ ‘OUR CALLS WILL NOT BE IGNORED!!!!’ The sound of over-sensitive, thought provoking protest calls filled the large mob. The scene almost prevented Danny from making out what he could definitely recognize the day before as a film production. Now it’s just chaos. ‘Oh fuck me...’ he mumbles to himself. From the sea of bodies, a path is cleared by three rather large security-looking folks in black tuxedos. Menacing and huge, everyone clears their path because while mob mentality may reign supreme, no one wants to get their fucking ass kicked by a big dude. ‘Mr. Ferguson—right this way.’ One of them says to Danny. They guide him through the people, pass a set of barriers to keep the crazies at bay and into one of the large studios on the lot, where assistants and grips and gaffers and all these other necessary douche bags are running around frantically. Nothing makes sense to Danny at all; all he sees are angry people and a production in dissolution. Something happened, and it’s probably the Face-Eater’s fault. ‘This is so fucked.’ Danny hears Oliver Stone’s voice travel smoothly across the room. Damn, those acoustics are fucking awesome! But that’s not what Danny notices. Instead, he notices Oliver slapping his forehead while Adam Dick, the culprit of all this mayhem, turns to an approaching assistant. ‘Here’s your Passion Fruit Tea Lemonade from Starbucks, sir!’ He bows as he delivers it to Facey, never looking him in the eye. Getting a hearty drink from his straw, Facey gives himself a moment to examine the taste before exclaiming, ‘No, Ollie, THIS is so fucked! You call this a fucking lemonade?! I told you, SIX PUMPS OF VALENCIA AND THREE PUMPS OF RASPBERRY.’ He slaps the cup into the assistant’s face, sending plastic shards and ice and lemonade exploding all over him in a humiliating mess. Facey smiles at the deed, then kicks the boy away. ‘BE GONE, PEON.’ Danny, disgusted at the sight (merely because he hadn’t thought of doing it first), approaches the two quickly in hopes to finish the favor ASAP. ‘FERGULAAAATOOOOORRR—MOUNT UP!’ Facey calls out on sight. ‘Quick, I don’t have all day.’ ‘Alright alright, I’ll get right to it. Have you ever heard of a Screen Actor’s Guild?’ ‘Of course.’ ‘HOW COME I’M THE ONLY MOTHER FUCKER ON THE PLANET WHO’S NEVER HEARD OF A SCREEN ACTOR’S GUILD!?! Are you guys trying to hide it from me as apart of some sick joke to make me look stupid or something?’ Danny’s lost, already, as to be expected in a conversation with the Face-Eater. ‘Whoa whoa whoa.’ He says. ‘Slow down. What did you do? Why does the Screen Actor’s Guild have to do with-’ Then it clicks. ‘Oh, that little protest deal going on outside. Bummer.’ ‘Yeah, apparently they’re all up in arms because I made the extras sign contracts where they agree to be paid in promo material for ‘Nip/Tuck’ and ‘the Shield.’ And I thought people would like still-shots of Doctor Doom and the Thing half naked, but I guess no one’s comfortable enough with their sexuality in this day and age.’ ‘YOU IDIOT! Oliver, I am so sorry for my friend’s-’ ‘He can’t hear you.’ Facey interrupts. Danny leans in to get a good look at Oliver Stone, who’s eyes are dilated to the point that no color can be seen. He looks ‘out of it,’ so to speak, and Danny turns back to Facey for an answer. ‘What’d you do to him?’ ‘You know that whole nickname thing? He kind of took it a little too far. He kept saying things like ‘imbecile, incompetent,’ and ‘black-listed,’ and I didn’t really get it so I was all ‘dude if you’re going to keep fishing for compliments because of your articulation I’m going to murder you.’ He started talking about lawsuits or whatever so I crushed up these pills called Oxycotin and put them in his drink and now he can’t stop saying ‘This is so fucked,’ while staring into space.’ ‘Oh my God,’ Danny waves a hand in front of Oliver Stone’s eyes to no reaction. ‘How many did you give him?’ ‘At first? Only three. Hahah it’s actually kind of funny to mess with him.’ Casually the Face guides his arm around Oliver’s shoulders and shakes him a bit. ‘If you want a hilarious voicemail message for when your friend’s call: now’s the time.’ ‘Now’s hardly the time!’ ‘This is so fucked.’ Oliver says. ‘Dude. No. Its cool. He told me he’s taken it before anyways.’ ‘That would explain ‘Alexander.’’ Danny muses, until he pulls away with a refocused agenda. ‘I still don’t see why I’m here.’ ‘Money. I need yours. Now. Please.’ SMILE =D ‘Ahem. Since you asked me so politely...’ Such a blunt question requires a bit of time to regroup oneself. ‘How much are you asking? I’m probably on the same contract in PRIME as X3 but unlike The Ninja Who Speaks Ebonics, I know the meaning of the term ‘networking.’ And please, tell me why I should invest in this already sinking project?’ Hehehe, Facey begins to giggle. Nervously. It’s a different shade than what we’re used to seeing, but easily visible due to his flushed face and scratching of the arms. ‘Well there’s the matter of the actors and their bullshit ‘wages’ and then some stupid fines by the MPAA and then compensation or some fucking I don’t know the specifics, man. I just know it’s money and I don’t fucking have it! I lied about my entire background to get in—I don’t own a fucking production company! I didn’t even get the rights to this comic book!’ Danny slaps his forehead. ‘How much do you need?’ ‘This is so fucked.’ ‘I’d say a cool 25 mil would set us on course.’ SMILE =D ‘You’ve got to be fucking kidding me.’ Danny scoffs, begins walking. ‘Dude, come on. I’ve got no where else to go.’ Danny doesn’t stay for rebuttals. He’s already hot on his heels and towards the studio exit, but Facey calls out to him in desperation. ‘Help us, Ferguwan Kenobi! You’re our only-FUCK! I PROMISED MYSELF I’D NEVER REFERENCE STAR WARS FUCK ME!’ As Facey begins smacking himself in the head repeatedly, Danny’s out the door and back into the chaos. For a second, he doesn’t even second guess his decision against funding the film. It’s a ridiculous investment in fact. What he doesn’t realize at first is that he just turned his back on an A-Lister. Oh! The hypocrisy!
