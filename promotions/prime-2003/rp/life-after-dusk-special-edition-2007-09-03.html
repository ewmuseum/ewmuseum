Life After Dusk: Special Edition

3 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/03/07 Voice: Good Morning, PRIME! Then, the camera fades in as you see the bright, smiling face of PRIME Superstar, Dusk. You can’t help but to flinch back from this shocking display coming from Dusk as he’s walking down a corridor. To see a smile on his face is surreal as he’s not the kind of person to smile very often. Yet, here he is, bright as day with a smile on his face. Kind of shocking, isn’t it? Well, let’s move forward, and let Dusk do the talking and not the narration. Dusk: Shocked to see me? I imagine so. As you might’ve heard earlier this summer, I’ve signed on to do a reality series with NBC titled Life After Dusk. Cute, eh? Basically, PRIME set up this deal with NBC so to get my name out there a little more especially as I travel to some other federations and wrestle over there. Of course, we’re not going to mention them here today as that just wouldn’t be fun. Yet, in the weeks up until the premiere, Monday, September 24th at 10PM Eastern Time after the Season Two Premiere of Heroes, cheap plug yes I know. Back on track now, in the weeks leading up to the premiere, ESEN TV has had the exclusive rights to show some pre-premiere stuff to kind of entice people to watch it. There’s just one problem with that. I’m a PRIME guy through and through, and was able to get it so that you guys could get an exclusive piece to yourself. Well, isn’t that great? Just what you need as you eat your Lucky Charms in the morning, the day starting off with Dusk. Actually, that is pretty fucking great. It’s better than 99% of the shit that normally airs in the morning, and seeing as how you watch the PRIME channel this could be rather entertaining. Dusk has a habit of normally fighting somebody or just embarrassing them so in the end you should win out as Dusk does what he basically feels like it. Pencil it into your calendar because you know it’s going to be exciting when the show premieres in a few weeks time. Moving on. Dusk: A few weeks back, the press and the networks has this thing called the TCA’s. Don’t ask me what they stand for though because I couldn’t personally tell you. Now, what happens is that you have the cast of existing and new shows come out to answer questions from the press to build up hype for the show and give the critics and press a reason to talk about your show. In the end you hope it’s all positive. Now, I was supposed to show up at this event for a press conference type of affair, but I was over in England competing so I couldn’t make it. My agent set it up and, well, he’s an idiot. You’ll probably see him in a few minutes, his name is Joshua Warner. Try to be nice to him, because, well, I treat him like shit. Then, Dusk turns down another corridor and standing down the hall is a man with a huge smile on his face and pretty boy looks. We’re going to assume that’s Joshua because well, you could see why Dusk would give the guy some shit if he looks like that. The guy starts walking towards Dusk and the two shake hands. Dusk then turns back towards the camera and smiles. Dusk: See why I give the guy shit? He then smiles before turning back towards Joshua who is looking him over. Dusk: Joshua, stop. The audience is going to think that you’re gay and that doesn’t go over well in that demographic. Joshua: Demographic? Since when do you start using words like that? Dusk: Look, I don’t have time to be explaining all of this shit to you. I’m a business man— Joshua: You are? Dusk: Look, I’m going to beat your ass right here and now if you interrupt me again. Now, like I was saying, I’m a business man, or so the people who watch this show are going to think of me as. Plus, demographics are important, aren’t they? Joshua: Majorly. Dusk: Yeah, my demographic doesn’t like you using words like that especially since it isn’t a real word. Joshua: Sorry. Dusk: It’s okay. Are we ready to start? Joshua: Yeah, most of the press has arrived. E! Online, TVGuide, Entertainment Weekly, New York Times, Los Angeles Times, Access Hollywood— Dusk: Look, I really don’t need you quote every single press agency here. Let’s just get this show under way. Joshua: Sounds good to me. NBC Co-Chairman Ben Silverman is here to kind of guide you through the ropes on stage. So let him take front and center stage— Yet, Joshua never gets to finish his statement as Dusk walks off. He goes into another room where he’s met by a room full of press and up on stage is man that must be Ben Silverman. Ben looks a little shocked to see Dusk just walking into the room like this, but quickly welcomes him on stage. Ben: Ladies and gentlemen, the star of Life After Dusk, none other than Dusk himself! Dusk walks onto the stage and shakes hands with Ben as he smiles out at the crowd. He poses for a second as the cameras start flashing like crazy in the room. In the back of his mind, Dusk knows that one of the writers out there is making mention of his black sweater and tan pants. As the cameras stop flashing, he takes his place on one of the stools as he looks out at the crowd, ready to do this thing. Ben: First, before we start I’d like to say that we’re proud to have Dusk with us today. To get him to one of these things is extremely difficult due to the schedule that he’s on as he travels around the world and his training regiment as he prepares for his upcoming Universal Title shot at Dice, Dollars, and Tragedy. Dusk: Thanks, Ben. I must say, it’s a pleasure to do this. It took me a minute to warm to this idea as this is completely out of my realm. Yet, as I started to have the camera follow me around I found it easier to do and actually a little bit more. I think this show will show the people who I am away from the camera and what it’s like to be a professional wrestler this day. So many people have this misconception that it’s fake, but that’s a different aspect of wrestling. What we do is a sport and is very athletic, and yet it’s also very marketable. We spend many hours not just training, but having to work with our managers and agents to help increase our image all over the world. It’s very draining, but is no different then the athletes in the NFL, NBA, or MLB or actors and actresses all over the world. Ben: I think that was very well put. Life After Dusk premieres in a few short weeks after the season premiere of one of America’s favorite television shows, Heroes. I came into this show after my predecessor, Kevin Reilly— Dusk: That’s Ben’s way of saying if it sucks then to blame Kevin. The entire crowd laughs at this and so does Ben. Ben: Not exactly. I’m very excited about the aspects of this show and feel that Dusk is a very interesting person behind the scenes and away from the ring. But, enough talking from me as this is very much questions oriented. Please remember that Dusk does have to be somewhere else in an hour so we’re going to be sticking to our schedule very strictly. So, let’s open the floor. Immediately, a hand flies into the air and Ben points at the man. Man: Yes, my name is Brian Sullivan and I’m from The Futon Critic. My first question has to do with your name actually. Mr. Silverman has been using the name Dusk, and I wonder if it’s a rule on the show to use your ring name, or your birth name Craig Maloof. Ben: Of course, that’s a very good question. We have no rules against that and I’ve been using it here to kind of remind people the name of the show. However, Craig has said that he plans on allowing his name aired as he knows that people aren’t crazy enough to think that his real name is Dusk. Dusk: I’ve tried changing it on a number of occasions, but those damn judges just don’t listen. The crowd laughs once again at this. Dusk: But, yeah, I think you’re going to see me transform more into Craig as the show goes on. My initial reaction is to be Dusk on camera and eventually I’ll get out of that habit as I become more of myself in front of the camera. It’s a hard transition to make. Then, another hand goes up in the air. Woman: Yes, Korbi Ghosh from E. My question has to do with the length of your contract with NBC. Is your show only going to be on for thirteen weeks, and is there a chance of more weeks being added on like most scripted shows? Dusk: Actually, the contract that I have with NBC is structured much in the same light as your standard television contract. They’ve given me an initial contract for 13 weeks, but have an option for a full season slate of 22 episodes, and then of course more options for a second season if it should come to that. Anything past the first season though has to be an agreement between me and NBC, which is unique from other shows as the network controls the show’s fate at that point. However, with the way my schedule is I have to find if it’s feasible for me to continue doing the show. Good question, thanks. Another woman’s hand shoots up and Dusk calls upon her. Woman: Yes, Kristin Veitch from E as well. I have a couple of questions if you don’t mind. Dusk: That’s fine, go ahead. Woman: First, how are you shooting this show? Your life changes so much from week to week, and normal reality series normally shoot weeks in advance. Is that the same thing here with your show? Dusk: No, it’s not. That’s one of the things that was first asked of me, but I felt it would be better if the show was more real time ala Big Brother. So, they’ll be doing a week for each episode and then editing it together as we go. I’ve got a great team of editors and producers on the show that is committed to making this work. The past few weeks, including right now, have been to get the team prepared for this. In the next week, you’ll see me preparing for DDT and everything involved in that. That’s the kind of reality that we hope to bring with you as you know what I’m going through as I go through it. Kristin: Now, what do you think we can expect to see from you? Entourage is a fictional show— Dusk: I love Entourage so you don’t have to explain it to me. Kristin: Oh, okay, sorry. But, can we expect to see actors and actresses to show up on the show randomly? Dusk: If I encounter them in my everyday life, sure. I don’t hang out with many actors or actresses, but I’m always at functions so that is always a possibility. Kristin: Is there anyone that you would like to see on the show? Dusk: Yeah, I’d love it if Kristin Bell showed up. Kristin: Maybe a cameo on Heroes, then? Dusk: I think that would be great promotional work if you ask me. Ben? Dusk then looks over at Ben who is nodding his head with a smile on his face. Ben: I like the sounds of that actually. I might have to get on the phone with Tim Kring on that one. Dusk: Make it happen. I think I’ve got the perfect character— Ben: Let me guess, big sex scene with Kristin Bell, Hayden Panettiere, and Ali Larter. The crowd laughs again and Dusk does the same as he nods his head. Dusk: I love this guy. He knows me rather well! Kristin: Final question so we can keep the show moving. Do you have any aspirations to become an actor? Dusk: Honestly, no. I’m not Danny Ferguson, as I’m a better actor, and have no aspirations to become a D-level actor like my co-worker and upcoming opponent at DDT. However, I can’t say no to it I think if it’s something that I really click with. I can’t wrestle forever and that might be an option for me. Thanks. Kristin: No, thank you. Another hand flies up into the air. Dusk: Yeah? The man stands up and smiles. Man: My name is Michael Ausiello, and I’m from TV Guide. Dusk: Go ahead. Man: Thanks. This is for you and Ben actually, are you two afraid of the impact that Dusk’s recent arrest will have? Dusk: To be frank and instead of covering it up, I wasn’t charged with anything you’ll notice. I don’t have any charges pending or anything of that matter. Ben: Yeah, Michael, we immediately had a meeting with Dusk who explained the situation to us. There are no charges pending upon him and from our discussions we don’t see any coming at this time. Michael: Fair enough. Ben, as you said this is a project from your predecessor. What were your initial feelings on this project when you came in? Ben: To be honest, I was hesitant. I didn’t know what to expect, but as I sat down with Dusk and I talked to him, I could see that he is very personable. The people like him and he is a great person to get to know. He won me over much like I’m certain he’ll win everybody else over who watches the show. The questions continue for an hour and Dusk answers them all greatly. As the panel begins to wrap up, Ben Silverman stands up for the crowd. Ben: Now, I have a huge announcement to make. We’ve decided to add another aspect to the show that should really liven it up. Dusk: We have? Dusk looks around lost as to what’s going on. In the back of the room Joshua stands there, trying to calm his client down. Ben: We’re bringing on a host who will be with Dusk to bring you his impressions of the situation as well. Let me introduce to you, Shawn Hart! From behind a curtain comes a man that waves out at the crowd and smiles. Dusk looks at Ben and then at the guy with apprehension to this announcement. Shawn: Thanks Ben, it’s great to be here. I’m very excited about th— Yet, he never gets to finish his sentence as Dusk decks him in the face and the guy goes down hard. Dusk then looks at Ben and shakes his head. Dusk: Never do that to me again. Dusk then walks off stage and exits the doors as everybody watches him. His agent runs out behind him and looks at Dusk like he’s crazy. Joshua: CRAIG! Dusk: You knew about this? Joshua: Just found out about it. Dusk: Take care of it and take care of that guy. I’m not having some guy stalk me for 22 weeks. Joshua: Got it. Then, a phone begins to ring. Dusk then pulls out his cell phone and sees that it isn’t him. Joshua then pulls out his phone and answers it. Joshua: Joshua here, how can I help you? He listens to the person on the other line and then puts the phone up to his jacket. Joshua: It’s for you. Dusk: Who is it? Joshua then looks at the camera and then back at Dusk before he whispers something in his air. Dusk: Oh, really? Yeah, let me talk to him. Joshua then hands the phone off to Dusk, and Dusk looks back at the camera. Dusk: Well, that’s been your sneak peek PRIME. Hopefully I’ll be able to get you another one soon. But, for now, I’ve got to take this phone call. It’s been a blast and I hope you watch my show. I’ll see you guys soon. Dusk then puts the phone to his ear and begins to walk away. Dusk: Hey, K— Yet, you never get to hear who it is as the camera fades to black.
