Batoru Rowaiaru! (King Of Kings, JiTC Semi-Finals/Finals(?))

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/12/06 ---- Real-time Student List Update Student No. – 19 Student Name – Antonio ‘Digital’ Vargas Cause of death – Sharpened toothbrush (randomly assigned weapon of Student #15) to jugular vein Killer (if applicable) – Student #15, Adam Students remaining – 4 ---- Hour 68: 4 Contestants Remain Adam (Student #15) tilts his wrist to check the time and sighs; the face was covered in a dark layer of Digi’s rapidly congealing blood. He spits on it and rubs with his thumb, clearing enough of his ex-classmate’s sticky remains to be able to read the digital display: 20:04 “Shit… Four hours left.” He pulls out the student list from his (once white, now mainly deep red) shirt pocket and scores Digi’s name off the list. He gives the list a quick count; there were six students remaining at the last six-hourly announcement and he’d just killed one. There may have been other deaths – it could be down to just two now – but it was safer to assume there were as many as possible still alive and after his ass. As he folds the list up again and tucks it back in his shirt pocket he thinks to himself; “One more ‘exchange student’ left.” He was suspicious as to the reason for the inclusion of these extra students in the competition, but with the very real possibility of death within the next few hours he wasn’t in a position to think long on the subject. In three hours and fifty-six minutes the belts on any students remaining alive would explode – unless there was only one survivor left by that time. Yep, by midnight the world’s pay-per-view audience would know the winner of this year’s Battle Royale tournament – and the special prize awaiting them for their victory. Rumour had it that the winner would be waited on hand-and-foot for the rest of their life; the cost of doing so a mere fraction of the worldwide profits from the competition’s licensing, PPV sales and ‘Highlight Reel: Student #XX’ DVDs. Others insisted the winner was just shot and disposed of as soon as the final camera had stopped filming their victory celebrations. Adam didn’t care as long as he survived – though he was intrigued by something he’d read in the notes he’d found scribbled on a pad of paper (some poor sap’s randomly assigned “weapon”) in a viscera-drenched cabin he’d stumbled across the day before. It had hinted that whoever had written it had hacked into the island’s communication system and had overheard something the competition’s operational staff had said about the winner of this Battle Royale meeting the winner of last year’s for a PPV (of course) extravaganza one-on-one showdown. By the looks of things, though, his hacking was discovered and was none too well received – explaining why Adam had found the body in two halves, completely unidentifiable after the radio-controlled explosive belt’s blast had torn him apart at the waist. Aside from the notepad (unreadable for a few pages because of the contestant’s innards soaking through the paper) there had been little to write home about in the cabin – the putrid stench of slowly rotting flesh notwithstanding. The half-full bottle of water and the loaf of stale bread in the unidentified student’s standard-issue backpack had come in handy, though. Adam guessed the death had been an early one – explaining the extent of the decomposition and the lack of any stolen weapons in the student’s possession. Approaching a small stream Adam breaks from auto-pilot and grinds to a halt, alarm bells ringing in the back of his head. A quick consultation of the supplied map in the moonlight confirmed his suspicion – the stream was the boundary between this zone and the next; the land on the opposite bank was declared a danger zone during tonight’s 18:00 announcement and had clocked over at 19:00 according to his scrawled note on grid zone D15. Ducking back into the woods he had just left – just in case anyone passed by – Adam retrieves his supplied torch and consults the map again. He could follow the stream down to the change-over to zone E16 then hop it there and continue along toward the central starting bunker, but it was risky – there weren’t any clear-cut landmarks on his map to show where E15 ended and E16 started. He didn’t want to get this far only to blow himself up by jumping into a danger zone because he couldn’t read a map properly. He chuckled, thinking to himself; “I’d rather not appear on the blooper reel, thank you very much…” Nope, it appeared he’d have to head upstream and cross by the waterfall he’d passed by during the first day’s exploration – a longer route to the bunker where the class had woken up almost three days prior, deigned to be the point where it would all end. From his sketchy knowledge of the previous years’ Battle Royales (he cursed himself for not having paid more attention before) the island was closed off as danger zones from the coasts inward. This left the final battle to rage around the bunker where it had all started at Hour 0. “Some sick fuck’s sense of irony, no doubt.” He tries to stop himself thinking back to the start; the 32 students waking up, drugged, in the bunker – sitting there incredulously as it was explained that their class was the ‘lucky’ one picked from a nationwide random lottery to participate in this year’s Battle Royale competition before they were sent out onto the island, one student every minute, collecting a pack on the way out the bunker with their randomly-assigned weapon, bread and water enough to survive on for three days and various other useful items – torch, map, student list etc. “No…” Shaking his head, Adam clears his mind of the thoughts – not letting himself dwell on them. He had to get to the bunker, the island would start closing down the with danger zones in fifteen minute intervals shortly – he had to move fast to get down to the final four zone area before the rapid lockdown began in earnest and he was trapped somewhere, just waiting to be torn in half by the explosive belt locked round his waist. The belts… he’d seen the remains of the unidentifiable student whose belt had detonated (he had a suspicion it was one of the exchange students who’d joined the game; presumably added to make up numbers – theirs was a small class, he mused). The sight was certainly not something he envied happening to himself. Word had it that in the early Japanese games the belt was a collar fixed around the neck, but after mass suicides from students thinking the quick death of a broken neck was better than playing the game it was rethought into a much more painful variant. According to the instructional video played to the class at the beginning of the game the explosive force of the belts varied from student to student – you could have a “humane” one with a charge powerful enough to kill you instantly as it ripped you apart at the waist or you could be unlucky enough to land one of the belts that would explode only enough to spill your guts but leave you alive to suffer the agony of the slow death that followed. Being honest, Adam really wouldn’t rather find out what kind of belt his was – he just wants to finish off the few competitors remaining and get the fuck off the island already. He was a lucky one in that he’d been able to find shelter safe enough to grab a few hours’ sleep – around twelve hours across the three days, in two to three hour slots – but he worried about meeting a fully-rested ‘camper’. He shuddered involuntarily as he thought of Tony Gamble, the twisted ‘class clown’ of sorts – he was the kind of sadistic fuck that might have set himself up a sniper’s nest near the bunker and picked off students as they passed by, stealing their weapons, food and packs to build himself a veritable fort stockpiled high with guns, knifes, crossbows… He doesn’t need to consult the list any more to know who was left alive as of the last announcement; Tony Gamble (Student #18), Angelo Deville (Student #6), one of the exchange students (Student #7) and Jonathan Winters (Student #30). Anywhere between one and all of them could still be alive and he knows he has to watch every step of the way for any sign of them. And talking of signs of possible opponents: something soft felt underfoot causes Adam to slowly step back and duck down – a hand. A dismembered human hand. Keeping as low a profile as possible Adam moves into the undergrowth nearby and gingerly pulls his silenced Walther PPK from his waistband (he’d taken it from the exchange student he’d killed earlier (Student #2), but it was his now). He tries to stay as silent as possible as he moves through the brush to look for more body parts. It doesn’t take long before he comes across the rest of Jonathan Winters’ mutilated remains – a quick look at the face shows it not to be Gamble’s work: though it’s covered in bruises, cuts and slashes the cheeks are pretty much intact. From the six-hourly announcements Adam had learned that Gamble had been awarded a special prize for winning a real-time online poll for best kill in a six-hour period; his tendency to slit his victim from ear to ear (before or after the kill) was much appreciated by the live viewing audience, apparently. Given the very obvious bad blood between Gamble and Winters in class normally, there’s no way ‘The Grin’ (as he liked to call himself) would have passed up on leaving his mark on his nemesis if he had even the slightest chance. Still keeping as hidden as possible, Adam looks about for any signs of the killer, but whoever was responsible – Deville or the exchange student, by process of elimination – was probably long gone by now. Adam puts his gun away again as he keeps his profile low in the bushes and is about to score Winters’ name off his student list – not that is was really necessary now, but it reminded him of the stakes of the game – when he spots the handle of a Bowie knife lying under Winters’ lifeless body poking out a little. “Hold on… a hand lying out by the stream plus a weapon left by the body plus a nice, open moonlit clearing.” As the equation comes together in Adam’s head he drops to the ground – just in time to avoid the hatchet that buries itself in the tree immediately behind where his head was not two seconds before. Scrambling to his feet, Adam catches a brief glimpse of the blade of the hatchet embedded in the tree; more specifically, he sees what’s been carved on it: >=) “Deville. Shit.” Taking to his heels, Adam runs – simply runs. He grabs the single flash-bang grenade he has left out his pocket (acquired from Killean’s backpack, Christ knows who got them originally), nearly dropping in the process, and blindly throws it into the bushes he’d just fled from. He doesn’t hear any moans from Deville after the muted crack of the device going off, but he isn’t looking for that – he just wants Deville to lose sight of him for the two seconds he needs… Lying on his back, soaking wet, Adam trains the gun on the wall of water he’d just leapt through – he had found the cavernous cubby hole hidden behind the sheet of water when he’d first come across the waterfall, exploring on the first day. He just hopes and prays that Angelo Deville – possibly the meanest, sickest sonuvabitch in the whole class year – hadn’t seen him make the dive through. He lies, ready and waiting for Deville to cross the stream looking for him so he could pull the trigger and blast a fucking hole in his head. Two minutes. Five Minutes. Ten minutes. Adam lays still, his clothes and hair slowly drying, but his gun kept steadily trained on the secret entrance to his hideaway. Only when his belt bleeped the passing into the next hour did he dare move. Hour 69: 4 Contestants Remain Slowly Adam breathes out: a long, deep exhalation, but not one of relief; no, the pressure was still on and he had to move fast – this whole area would be closed off as a danger zone soon and he’d wasted a long time waiting for Deville to provide him an opportunity. Sneaking a peek out the waterfall by breaking the flow with his hand Adam scouts his surroundings – no sign of any life, but then again… Deville could be stood in the stream directly above just waiting for him (hell, it was just as likely Gamble or the exchange student could be doing it). With a deep breath Adam takes a run and dives out, landing on his back on the grass – gun trained at the top of the waterfall. “Thank fuck for that…” Nothing there. But a sound screeching nearby nearly causes him to nervously fire off a muted shot anyway. The screeching is just the Battle Royale announcement speakers straining back into life for another ‘uplifting update’: “Hey, hey kids! I know I’m not due back on for another three hours to announce the winner, but I figured you four needed a bit of a pep-talk to get the rest of the killing done – you naughty boys didn’t add even a single notch to the tally in that last hour; despite the best intentions of some of you, I must add!” The terminally cheery DJ’s voice crackling over the loudspeakers installed all over the island was irritating on one level, just plain creepy on another and terrifying in a way on yet another. But it was good for information on the status of the game – Adam’s suspicions were right; the lack of any screams or gunfire (bar his own) for the past while on the island meant that it was still ‘twenty-eight down, four to go’. “Time to get cracking, boys – the island lock-down is in full swing now, get your asses to the starting bunker to finish this thing up so we can crown one of you the King of this Battle Royale so you can claim your special winner’s prize! One contestant – you know who you are – has just exited a newly-classified danger zone and will have to get his skates on to clear the zone he’s in now before it gets reclassified!” Adam thanked the announcement for one thing, at least – the loud, piercing squawk echoing all over the island covered the sounds of his panicked run to clear zone D16. “By the sounds of things, I’m the only one still on the outskirts.” “That’s all from me for now, guys, start that killing!” “Fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck!” Adam could see the break from D16 into D17 ahead of him, marked by a fallen tree, and he’d make it easily before the danger zone classification kicked in. He was more worried about making the run through D17 – solid woodland – into D18 within the fifteen allocated minutes. Even with the time he was shaving off the D16/17 transition it was going to be tight. “Just keep running…” As he leaps the tree he looks down to his blood-smeared watch; 21:10 “Five minutes, saved – c’mon! Use it! Keep going!” It’s tough, but he knows he can make it – it’s just running, he can run. Being tired from it then needing to leap straight into killing again… that he’s certainly not looking forward to. But if it means he has even the slightest chance of surviving, he has to take it. Ignoring the burn in his legs, Adam pushes on harder, faster. His mind, left with little else to do with every conscious thought focussed on making it to D18 on time, starts to run a little mental defrag – combing through all the details, thoughts and memories of the past three days and attempting to make some semblance of order of it all. He thinks of the first time he’d killed a classmate; his mind brining back the image of Killean leaping at him – arms raised high overhead with a fire axe in hand, screaming manically as he leapt toward Adam. All it had taken was a simple sidestep, knowing that there was a 40ft sheer drop immediately behind him, and leaving his leg stuck out… but it proved tough to think that he’d actually killed the big guy by tripping him over the edge when he landed. But that was the point, wasn’t it? Kill or be killed. If he was giving it any level of deliberate thought Adam would probably have to stop and compose himself again – he’d been pretty cut up when it had first happened. He hated to say it, but the subsequent kills had been much easier – not physically, he’d actually had to put up quite a fight with them – but in terms of being able to strike the final blows. If anything, the Battle Royale had taught him to have an iron constitution – the cool, calm and collected way he’d surveyed the maimed and dismembered corpse of Jonathan Winters would have shocked him if he’d actually had time to stop and thin- “There!” The bridge that crossed the ravine that cut through most of the island! That was the crossing point into D18. But… no. Shit, please… no! Under the bright moonlight he catches the merest glint of light reflecting from somewhere in the middle of the bridge. He slows down and walks the last few steps to the rickety wooden structure, his worst suspicions confirmed with a quick check by the light of the near-full moon – incredibly fine fibres criss-cross the bridge from the wooden planks of the base to the splintered handrails either side. A quick look under the planks revealed the explosives taped to it – ready to blow him up if he’d run straight into it to head across. “Man… how did someone end up with C4 – tripwires and everything – and all I got was a fucking toothbrush as my ‘weapon’?” He didn’t even think about how inane a thought that was to have, the little time left to safely cross the ravine weighing more heavily on his thoughts. 21:29 No time for a deep, steadying breath. No time for a last wish, hope or prayer. No time to think about anything other than just getting a good enough run to clear the ravine in one jump. No time to spare – he jumps, grabs and hangs tight. “I’ve come this far, I’m not gonna be beaten by some sneaky bastard’s trickery.” He claws himself up, pulling at clumps of grass and earth to get enough of a grip to get back on solid ground again. A wicked grin crosses his face as he rubs his hands to knock the dirt from them. One rummage through his backpack later and he retrieves one of the four or five bottles of water he’s picked up from various backpacks along the way. With a sly grin he lobs it at the tripwires on the bridge, turning his back and running as soon as he releases it – the distance he manages to get between himself and the bridge before the C4 detonates still not enough to stop him from being thrown to the ground from the sheer force of the resultant explosion. “Hopefully there won’t be an announcement to spoil that…” He grins again as he gets to a vertical base, spitting out some of the dirt that had ended up in his mouth. Deciding to take a breather, (relatively) safe now that at least one other person is assuming he’s dead, Adam catches his breath and fills himself a little on the warm water and stale bread left in his backpack – it’s sustenance at least. Something catches in his throat though – a nauseating smell that makes him wretch and cough out the bread he was chewing. Tossing the remains of the loaf aside Adam gets to his feet and follows the pungent stench to its source – just ten feet from where he was just sitting lie four body halves. Covering his nose and mouth with his hand, Adam checks his map – another step or two forward and he’d be in the danger zone that Sun Tzu (Student #2) and Kenjiro Ito (Student #29) had been locked in battle in when it’d tripped at 11:00 today and blown the two apart. “Christ…” He shakes his head, wondering if their deaths had been instant or drawn-out from a low-level explosive belt. He imagines how crazy the fight must’ve been to be so caught u- Instinctively he ducks and hikes his shoulder up, catching the charging Tony Gamble in the gut. Before Gamble has a chance to catch his breath he’s flung over Adam’s back toward the danger zone where pieces of Sun Tzu and Kenjiro Ito litter the ground. The look of sheer panic on Tony Gamble’s face as he flew through the air upside-down would later be picked as one of the game’s funniest images in an online poll, giving it the distinction of being one of only five exclusive downloadable wallpapers available from the official Battle Royale website. But, for now, laughter had no place in the game – Adam looks on as Gamble smacks into a tree, just on the boundary of the danger zone. “Damn! So close. Wait… did I just think that about killing the guy I sit next to in Maths?!” There’s no time to mull it over though, as Gamble is back on his feet almost immediately and charging at Adam again – his eyes wild and bloodshot. Wanting to reason with him, but knowing there’d be no point, Adam ducks aside and drives a knee into Gamble’s stomach. ---- Real-time Student List Update Student No. – 7 Student Name – Exchange Student #3 Cause of death – Hatchet to head (randomly assigned weapon of Student #11) Killer (if applicable) – Student #6, Angelo Deville Students remaining – 3 ---- As Gamble doubles over Adam grabs him around the neck and holds him tight in a headlock. He’s about to try and talk to ‘The Grin’ now he has him held immobile, but the absurd levels of adrenaline coursing through Gamble’s veins means the headlock is nothing but child’s play to break free from. Adam’s easily thrown aside into a tree, hitting his coccyx hard on the root he drops down on. He opens his mouth in a silent groan, but doesn’t have time to sit and dwell on it as Gamble lunges toward him with a screwdriver – driving it straight through his left shoulder. “Fuuuuuuuuuuck!” In an act of pure rage Adam kicks out, hitting Gamble in the knee dead-on – the resultant crack of bone breaking as the knee bends back in a way it’s really not meant to simultaneously disgusting and delighting the viewers watching the fight from one of the island’s 32,000 concealed and not-so-concealed cameras. The crazy look in Gamble’s eyes gives way to reality again as he’s snapped from his homicidal trance. He stumbles back, blinking rapidly, his mouth trembling open and closed like a guppy fish. He tries to put weight on his mangled leg with a step back but just collapses, falling flat on his ass. “Oh Christ, no…” Adam clambers awkwardly to his feet as an electronic beeping starts to emanate from Gamble’s belt, slowly but steadily building in both frequency and volume. Adam looks from the flashing green and red LED display on the belt to Gamble’s face, ‘The Grin’ looks up from his belt to meet Adam’s eyes a second later – realisation setting in as he breaks from the shock of his broken kneecap. He opens his mouth to speak but is cut short by the detonation of the belt tearing his belly apart. Adam looks on at the whole thing, eyes full of pity, unable to do anything but stand and watch. Gamble’s innards coat the trees all around, spattering over most of what Adam is wearing as well. Wiping a speck of blood from under his right eye like a tear Adam retrieves the silenced handgun from his waistband, aiming it directly between the eyes of the still-gasping Gamble. ---- Real-time Student List Update Student No. – 18 Student Name – Tony ‘The Grin’ Gamble Cause of death – Single gunshot wound, silenced Walther PPK (randomly assigned weapon of Student #9), to forehead after danger zone belt explosion Killer (if applicable) – Student #15, Adam Students remaining – 2 ---- Adam replaces the gun in his waistband, hoping that Deville and/or the other possibly remaining student still don’t know he has it – Deville couldn’t have seen it when he’d found Winters’ body, could he? He lets his shirt drape over to cover it before he sits back down against the tree to build up the strength and nerve to yank the screwdriver out his shoulder without screaming and giving himself away. With a branch he’s picked up from the ground nearby gripped tightly between his teeth Adam takes hold of the handle and pulls hard, carving deep tooth marks in the wood as he does so. He tosses the screwdriver aside, spits the stick out his mouth and gingerly raises his arm, grimacing with the pain, but he’s relieved to see his fingers are still able to grip and curl. “I can’t deal with this any more… I just want out already.” After cleaning the wound as best he can with the limited resources available, Adam gets to his feet and steps out the woodland, covered in a mess of gore with his left arm hanging limply by his side, only to meet – face-to-face for the first time in the competition – the one… the only… the inimitable… Angelo Deville. Deville starts a slow clap as Adam drags himself out the bushes, his ever-present patronising smile fixed firmly in its usual place. Deville: Bravo, bravo… As continues his belittling applause, Angelo Deville gears up for the speech he has planned so meticulously in his head; “Congratulate the pathetic underdog-done-good on lasting this long – especially in avoiding having me listed as his cause of death thus far. Commend him on getting past the magnificent bridge trap I set up especially for him and lull him into thinking this will be a long, flowing speech designed to subdue him. Try to convince him that this ‘meaningless’ killing is pointless and we should just shake on a truce and wait ’til the end to blow up together; “a final fuck you to the system” I’ll call it. Of course, he won’t believe a word of that bullshit, but it’ll be enough for him to think that I’m trying to talk my way out of death at his hands because I know he’s better armed than me. As soon as I see a spark of that recognition, the second he thinks he has the upper hand over me – as if it were ever even possible to get one up on the Deville… then I dart in and slash him gut to gullet before he even has a chance to so much as move a muscle. My adoring audience will lap it up.” And as he so rightly predicts, the world is indeed watching, with bated breath, for another arrogant and mendacious spiel from the Deville (one of their favourite things so far in the competition, independent online polls suggested). Viewers from coast-to-coast and in 63 countries overseas can barely contain their excitement in awaiting something eloquent and persuasive, yet treacherous and deceitful, to trickle forth from the poison lips of a certain Angelo Deville. They just know he has something up his sleeve to dupe and cheat his dim-witted foe enough to clear a path out of whatever shit has befallen him, leaving it free for him to rise above it all in a blaze of glory. Sadly for the world’s viewing audience, Adam knows the tricks behind Deville’s charms inside-out from years of seeing him work them on unsuspecting classmates; everything from ‘borrowing’ lunch money to somehow getting a copy of tomorrow’s English homework to a conniving his way into a spot on the football team he deserved less than twenty other guys. ---- Real-time Student List Update Student No. – 6 Student Name – Angello Deville Cause of death – Single gunshot wound, silenced Walther PPK (randomly assigned weapon of Student #9), through right eyesocket Killer (if applicable) – Student #15, Adam Students remaining – 1 ---- Deville’s body falters slightly as the bullet from the gun he knew nothing about tears through his skull – he hadn’t even managed to finish his third condescending clap or start on the wondrously planned sermon he had deemed Adam worthy to be the recipient of. He meets Adam’s detached and unfeeling gaze with his one remaining eye and manages to force out two hoarsely whispered words before he collapses, dead. Deville: Fuck you… Adam drops his gun and falls to his knees, assuming the position (as best he can with one near-dead arm) – one of the few things he did remember hearing about from previous years’ competitions. As predicted a SWAT team exits the bunker nearby and flood over to him, surrounding him with shields then performing a thorough search for any remaining weapons on his person before the TV crews are allowed out for the first exclusive footage of him, this year’s one and only winner – The King of Battle Royale.
