Shattered

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/05 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' The screams of anguish go unanswered as the Trashcan Man rails against the stormstruck sky. Lightning flashes in utter silence, illuminated the grey clouds and the blasted landscape beneath it. The Mindscape hasn't been exactly a cheery place as of late. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' He has been screaming nonstop for three days. If this were the real world, his voice would've gone hoarse, as it did so many times in the Evanston Clinic. There he would scream and scream until his throat was rough as sandpaper before he'd finally pass out. Or until he got on the guards' nerves enough that they would shoot him with a tranquilizer gun. But here there was no release in sleep. Here he was always awake, and his fear ran rampant. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' His despondency had grown after he suffered the pinfall from Hoyt Williams two weeks ago. Once again he had found some solace in the bottom of a liquor bottle, casing himself into a sweet oblivion that he searched for fruitlessly now. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' Last week he had some modicum of success. He had defeated Token Weed in an impromptu match, and he had done it quite handily. It was bittersweet though, as it had almost seemed too easy. As if he were just going through the motions. Olsig had attacked him from behind after the match, and instead of shrugging it off and looking for revenge, he just collapsed and let the medical team haul him off. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' He was a champion, but he didn't feel like it. Alan Kriegman's letter had cut him to the bone, and his already anemic confidence had all bled out of him. He tried to tough it up and call out the biggest threat to his title, Nick Brocken. But even that gesture of pride was cut short by Eve. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' Now he's scheduled to face Ellis Easton. And Easton was HOT right now, tearing through the competition like tissue paper. Easton's everything that Trashy is not. He's a pretty boy, young, talented, handsome, men were jealous of him, women wanted to be with him. And the Trashcan Man... was the Trashcan Man. Worse record in the fed this side of Jon Kano and Token Weed. Ugly, unloved, no real friends. Was it any surprise that he went on a bender? The bender to end all benders. He didn't remember much about the few days before he ended up down here. Flashes of ordering shots in bars, throwing up in bathrooms, waking up in alleys all flew through his head on gossamer wings, the memories bursting like soap bubbles when he tried to concentrate on them. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' All he remembered is a terrible wrenching sensation, as if something had pulled him in an ever tightening spiral before he found himself somewhat lucid in the Mindscape. He had looked for Mr Smiley Face, but couldn't find him. And scarier yet, he didn't have access to his senses. Before, whoever was not dominant in the body could still see, hear, and feel what was going around them. Trashyvision is what Smiley had always called it. He could concentrate and it was like watching what was going on like a dream, fuzzy, ephemereal, but it was contact with the outside world. But not now. All was severed, and he was trapped here. And if there's one thing the Trashcan Man fears more than anything else, it's being trapped. 'LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT! LET ME OUT!' Confused, Mikey? The incessant screaming faltered to a halt as the Trashcan Man heard a voice, a sibilant whisper that emanated all around him. A voice he hadn't heard in a long time. A voice he feared. 'You... you did this somehow, didn't you?' A throaty chuckle is his only answer. 'Please... I need out. I...' Mikey, I didn't do this to you. I have always been your friend, haven't I? 'No... you... you're the evil...' he sobbed as he collapsed to his knees. Evil, Mikey? Evil is label given to people like us by those who can't understand us. Those who hate us because we're different. The Trashcan Man slams the heels of his hands against his head, trying desperately to drive the voice away. They've always hated us, Mikey. Hated us because we're not like them. They call me evil. They call you a freak. Now that was a word that unlocked doors in the Trashcan Man's psyche. If there ever was a word that brought on his full unbridled rage, it was freak. 'I'M NOT A FREAK! I'M NOT A...' The voice interrupts him gently. I know that, Mikey. You're not a freak. It hurts when people call you that, doesn't it? It hurts me almost as much when you call me evil. I'm not evil, I'm just different. Just like you. And besides... We're the same. 'No... we're not... I'm... I'm me...' Yes, Mikey. You are you. And I am you too. I've always been here with you, haven't I? A quiet sob answers the voice. But the laughing idiot that calls himself Mr Smiley Face... he didn't arrive until... A long pause, then the Trashcan Man looks up, tears falling down his cheeks. 'Evanston...' he whispers. That's right, Mikey. You were always bright. The smiling one isn't part of us. He was put here by our tormentors to drive us apart. 'But... he's one of us... he helps me when I'm hurt...' He was never one of us, Mikey. He's a disease. If he was our friend, he'd be here, wouldn't he? Where is he now? 'I... I...' He's just been biding his time, Mikey. He's been marshalling his strenght, keeping us apart until he could make his move. And now... well, I certainly don't see him anywhere, do you? 'No...' Matter of fact, I don't see him anywhere. I don't see anything now, do you? The sobbing stops as a grim expression of resignation slowly crosses the Trashcan Man's brutish features. 'No, no I don't. He did this, didn't he?' So very bright, Mikey. You were always the best part of us, my old friend. He's taken over, just like our 'father' had commanded him to do. 'You mean... this is all Kreigman's doing?' Who more than anyone else would do this horrible thing to us, Mikey? If ever there was a man who could be called evil, it's Alan Kreigman. And do you know what Kreigman promised the smiling one if he succeeded? The one thing you've wanted more than anything else... 'Tempest...' That's right, Mikey. He's going to take our life, he's going to take our career, and he's going to take our love. And there's nothing we can do about it. Flames erupts around the Mindscape as the Trashcan Man's will leaps out, the very rock igniting in his wrath. 'He'll make a mistake. He'll come back to gloat. And when he does...' Fire springs from the Trashcan Man's eyes as he stares up at the sky. 'He will burn.'
