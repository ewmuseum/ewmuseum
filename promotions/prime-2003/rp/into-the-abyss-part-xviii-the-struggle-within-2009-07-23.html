Into The Abyss: Part XVIII - The Struggle Within

23 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-07-09 Under the baking heat of the summer Florida sun the waves brought sparkling crystal upon their crest, lashing the shoreline and the white sands hugging the coast for miles in both directions. Black specks dotted the beach sporadically; families, lovers, kids and even a few water-friendly dogs all enjoying the fine weather and a good time. At the far end of the beach where the sand gave way to a rocky outcrop at the foot of a cliff peppered all over with gulls, there stood perched a massive silhouette surveying the land. Von Kelsig. Studying the specks as they buzzed along his attention was suddenly drawn to someone else crouching below him in a small cove at the water's edge. Standing firm atop the rocks like the Argonath over the River Anduin his stiff poise slackened as his gaze fell upon the beauty of his Gloria teasing the wet sand between her toes as she rolled her blue halter top over her head and letting it fall to the ground. Her skin rippled as a cool breeze rolled over her and she shivered excitedly. Glancing up at Von Kelsig she slipped out of her jeans and tossed them to the side, revealing a petite bikini. She motioned for him to join her, and taking one last look at the surrounding area he made his way down to the shore, carefully treading the loose rocks to get to her side, causing a minuscule landslide of dirt and grit as he skidded onto the beach and dug his heels in, sinking a few inches into the beach as he plodded towards Gloria and scooped her up in his arms. "You're going in!" He yelled, charging towards the water as Gloria wrapped her arms around his neck and screamed into his beard. Stopping short of the frothy waves he planted a kiss on her head and spun her back to the sand, placing her dainty frame back on terra firma and laughing heartily. For his efforts he received a slap across the chest. "Don't do that!" cried Gloria, kicking sand at Von Kelsig's heels. The giant stopped dead and stood tall and proud once more, puffing his chest out. "Oh, get over yourself." Gloria muttered, furrowing her brow as Von Kelsig stared down at her menacingly... ...Before bursting to life with an almighty roar and chasing Gloria across the beach, kicking through the waves and reaching out for her. Like a little kid she sprinted off, half screaming and half dying with laughter as Von Kelsig kept pace with her and hammed up the monster act. He chased her further into the surf and after a couple hundred yards lunged forward and scooped her up once again, this time charging full steam ahead into the waves and jumping into the air before the two of them disappeared under the tumult, emerging moments later and splashing each other wildly. Trying her hardest to out-splash him was a futile effort against the monstrous tidal waves Von Kelsig produced by thrusting his arms back and forth. Finally they stopped, and as the water rippled back to a gentle ebb and flow Von Kelsig saw Gloria in a different light. Putting her weight onto one foot she stood half submerged in the surf, placing a hand on her hip and posing like a swimsuit model as the sun beamed down on them. Her bright green eyes reflected the sparkle of the sunlight against the ocean, and her glistening tan gave her a Mediterranean flavour that Von Kelsig found all too tempting. Stood before him was the beautiful, sumptuous, trusting and loyal companion he never thought he could have, and yet he had her all to himself. Gone was the image of the ghostly white, wily-haired junkie squatting in the abandoned offices of a drug lord's safehouse, replaced now by the image of perfection. She regarded him in an equal light. Stood proud in the waves he resembled Poseidon with his rugged beard and bulging muscles, commanding the sea around him. He was her epitome of strength and power. Von Kelsig's heart pounded as he ran his gaze over her curves, and wading through the water he picked her up in his arms and embraced her. They shared a long, loving kiss in the ocean before he carried her back to land and they strolled down the beach hand in hand, taking in the natural coastal beauty together. "This is nice." said Gloria, hugging his arm and staring up at him as though he were some kind of idol. "It is, isn't it?" He replied, taking a breath of fresh air and absorbing the atmosphere. A smile grew on his face and he tightened the embrace with her. His head swirled and swooned and the feelings grew into butterflies fluttering around his gut, something he rarely felt until he met her. He couldn't have imagined that walking by the sea with someone could make him feel so content and warm inside. She was special, to be able to give this joy without the need for slams, bombs or drivers against the rapturous cheers of thousands in attendance and make him happy. They ambled aimlessly like flotsam across the beach, keeping close to the cove where Gloria had stashed her clothes. No one ventured near them and they stayed far from the crowds, happy to indulge in one another's company without the nuisance of prying eyes disrupting the peace. Later that day as the sun began looming closer to the horizon Gloria lay on a beach towel, watching the sea and playing with her hair while resting against Von Kelsig's stomach while he in turned lay against a makeshift pillow of sand he had scooped up for posture. He ran his fingers over her arms, through her hair and across her breasts and when he tickled her cheek she took his hand and placed a kiss on each knuckle. "I love being with you." She cooed, nestling her face into the palm of his hand. He didn't say anything, watching instead as the glorious sun melted into the horizon on a glimmering sea of orange, red, yellow and purple. Before it disappeared completely he nudged Gloria and pointed to the sunset. "Watch this." He whispered. Gloria watched as the tip of the sun dipped into the ocean, and in the middle of the vibrant warm colours came an emerald flash. It only lasted a fraction of a second on the exact moment the sun fully set, but for that nanosecond Gloria was astounded by the rich beryl wink on the horizon, heralding the end of another day. "Wow, that's beautiful." She exclaimed, glancing up at him. "What was it?" Von Kelsig thought for a moment, a wry grin threatening to betray his stoic calm. "It's the inspiration behind the twinkle in your eye." Gloria paused, sitting up and turning to face her lover. He knew it was sappy and intended it to be, but the look in her eye was of lust and arousal. Surprised by this, he held his tongue and waited for her to make a move. She pounced, shoving both hands into his chest and playfully knocking him back onto the sand. As Von Kelsig lay there waiting for her to climb on she started giggling and got on all fours, looking him up and down before getting to her feet. "You're a total cheesemonger, you know that?" She laughed, shrieking as Von Kelsig let out a chuckle before roaring to his feet and chasing her back up the beach as night began to fall on the idyllic coast. ... That evening after an intimate candlelit dinner in a classic, classy restaurant they retired to their villa for the evening. While Gloria collapsed and sank into the soft cotton of their four poster bed, Von Kelsig fetched champagne and strawberries from the kitchen. Distracted from the star spangled night sky by the sound of clinking flutes, Gloria turned to see Von Kelsig saunter into the room with the treats in hand. Admittedly he wasn't Casanova, and a fuzzy recollection of Pretty Woman on a flight to Vegas was no basis for a romanticist's advances but a bottle of Krug Grande Cuvée and strawberries was as good a start as any. Gloria didn't object, and they spent the evening in each others arms feeding each other and enjoying the champagne. As midnight came and went and the chirping of crickets filled the silent night air so to did the orgiastic groans of a couple in love. By the time the screaming and screeching bed springs fell silent a red hue bled across the sky signalling another dawn, and as they lay in bed naked and beaded in sweat Von Kelsig stared at his lover and couldn't remember the last time he felt such happiness. "I love you Gloria..." He whispered. Suddenly his world became dark and cloudy, as Gloria opened her weary eyes and smiled. The sound that came from her wasn't the alluring tone of her voice but a distorted crackle of sorts, though to her she sounded perfectly normal. "I love you too." She said, snuggling into him and closing her eyes again. What? Von Kelsig cocked his head, though he didn't move. Gloria was slowly being consumed by the dark, though he was sure he wasn't falling asleep. A low hum rang in his ears even though the night was silent. Gloria, do you feel that? What's happening? The more he protested the more still he appeared to be. He tried to shake Gloria awake, and while he could feel himself grasping her something felt wrong. Further and further into an imploding abyss she fell until only the darkness remained. ---- "This is nice." said Gloria, popping another piece of duck into the hoi sin and savouring the sweet taste. "It is, isn't it." Larry replied, finishing off a rib and licking his lips. They sat in the glow of candlelight in a corner booth of the Wild Blossom restaurant. The soft notes of traditional Cantonese song flitted through the air over the quiet ramblings made up of conversations at each of the tables. Gloria had taken up her boss's offer of dinner and joined him on this night while Angelus sat in some dark hole consoling his thoughts. He couldn't have been any further from her thoughts as she sat across from Larry DeLancy, a red dress and diamond earrings complimenting her soft attractive features. "I wish I'd made more of an effort for you." Larry said, bringing attention his his own garb, a freshly pressed pair of pants and a fine silk shirt. "What do you mean? Everything's great." Gloria replied between mouthfuls. "Oh." Larry feigned modesty. "Alright, as long as you're enjoying yourself." He added with a smile. "I'm having a great time, this beats spending another night alone in that house..." "So tell me a bit more about yourself. What attracted you to the salon?" Larry asked, cutting in on the end of Gloria's sentence. Taken aback, she glanced to the left before meeting his gaze again. "Well...I just kind of leaned towards that career I guess. When I lived in Detroit I did a little hairdressing from home for neighbours and friends, I've never had to want for anything so when I decided to work I picked something I knew I could do that didn't need a lot of smarts." She said. "Aw, don't kid yourself you're very intelligent." Larry added, heaping flattery on her. "You say you've never wanted for anything?" "Yeah..." Gloria paused. "I've always had someone there to take care of me..." Before the silence had a chance to consume the atmosphere and churn out tension, Larry took a sip of red wine and caressed Gloria's hand. Her eyes were drawn to his hand running over hers, all she felt was a tickling sensation in her stomach. "Well I tell you what, after we're finished I'll take care of the bill and then we're heading off." Larry grinned. "Oh?" uttered Gloria, raising an eyebrow. "You like stand-up?" He asked, waiting with bated breath for her reply. "Yeah sure. I mean, I've never been to see any stand-ups but I like watching comedians on the television." Gloria said, and inside Larry DeLancy was dancing happily with the Devil. "I'm an acquaintance of the manager over at the Grande, and there's a great act on tonight. Some touring comedy group from Chicago, they got a great review in the paper...if you fancy going?" Again, a celebration with his inner demon hinged on Gloria's answer. He was not disappointed. "Sure I'll go, sounds like fun." She replied with a smile. Larry's heart jumped at the sight of her happy gorgeous face and he slapped his palm against the table, biting his tongue. "Settled. You enjoy the rest of your meal and I'll go call Marv about getting us seats." Larry beamed, jumping up from his seat and dashing outside with his cell phone already pressed to his ear. Laughing under her breath, Gloria returned to her meal. Occasionally she glanced up at Larry through the restaurant window and grinned as he waved back and shot her the thumbs up. It had been a while since she had been treated, and seeing Larry's enthusiasm to please made her feel warm inside, made her feel wanted. ---- "Uhn..." Von Kelsig's head snapped back up from the cool beechwood, a coaster stuck to his forehead by beads of sweat and beer. As it unstuck and dropped to the floor he wiped his eyes and face and stared up at a neon clock hanging above a rack of spirits and liqueurs. In fifteen minutes it would be Tuesday, July 21st. He wasn't in Florida with Gloria, he was half asleep at a bar somewhere in Hartford. There was no blistering sunshine, no clear blue skies or white sandy beaches or anything resembling a summer's day. The closest thing to summer in here was the grainy figures playing beach volleyball on the little television up in the corner of the bar. That and the little umbrella in a cocktail belonging to a rough looking trick sitting in a booth behind him with a shifty looking shyster in a leather waistcoat. Von Kelsig dipped his middle finger in a puddle of spilled beer and flicked it back and forth, sighing heavily and tipping his own tankard back, gulping down the tepid beer. As he swallowed his taste buds writhed at the warm bitter flowing down his neck and he pushed the beer aside. "For another?" Asked the barkeep, a chubby blading man in an apron with red cheeks and pursed lips. "Yeah, throw in a couple shots of Whiskey." He replied, tapping the steel tankard with his finger again. The bartender nodded and fetched the drinks while Von Kelsig spun on his seat and observed the inner sanctum of the public house. When he first entered a few hours earlier the place was a few people heavier than it was now, with only the tart and her man in the corner and a quiet little gent in the far corner of the pub struggling to keep his eyes open while his heavy set wife barked at him for some reason or another. "Goddamn dive..." Von Kelsig muttered under his breath, turning back to the bar and sliding his arm across the puddle of beer. Groaning, he wiped it off on a bar towel and reached for his wallet as the bartender brought his drinks over. "That'll be seven straight, big guy." Von Kelsig forked over a ten and downed his first shot of Glenfiddich, the sharp taste spiking his throat from tongue to stomach. Resting his head in his hands he rubbed his eyes and thought about the dream. The visions of him frolicking with Gloria on the beach and spending a quiet night in together was a far cry from the state of affairs today. It had been over a year since Von Kelsig and Gloria had been to Florida, and the last time they had been away together. As it was he hadn't seen her since their argument before he picked up her engagement ring a couple of weeks before, and as people do he went through the argument in his head and imagined what Gloria must have been feeling afterwards and even now without him there. Their love was strong from the beginning, but something in the way Gloria handled his uncertainty on a chosen path in life irked him. No matter how hard he tried it just wasn't possible to imagine a life outside of the ring, through fear of repercussions in the real world and feeling vacant and useless doing anything that didn't end in a three count. He knew she wanted him all to herself and every second he was away from their home was another second she lost faith in him. The house in Tennessee was the turning point, he thought. Before then Gloria had been happy with the state of their relationship, and waved him off as lovingly as she welcomed him back. They had travelled together and been separated and things were great. Now that a fixture had been introduced in their lives Gloria had twigged that things were getting serious and wanted him in a different light. No longer was this about two lovers having a good time together, she was looking at him as a potential father and/or husband. By buying the diamond ring Von Kelsig knew in his heart he wanted that too but against a life on the road and wrestling week in and week out a home life just didn't feel right. The ring was meant to anchor Gloria to the fact that he wouldn't always be there, though he would love her as strongly as though he were by her side. His hesitation to ask her the big question only served to fuel her recent doubts, and now as he sat there alone at another bar in another town before another show he too was beginning to have his doubts. His memory of that day at the seaside was the happiest he had ever been with her. Many other times they had shared laughs and enjoyed themselves but that day was different. The perfect atmosphere, the time alone and the intimacy of the trip all contributed to that one day being the happiest time of his relationship so far. It was a template for what life could be should he choose to step away from the ring and commit himself to his woman. Every day spent talking and cuddling, learning and listening and finding out at last that something in the real world could bring him peace. But then... HESSIAN! HESSIAN! HESSIAN! HESSIAN! HESSIAN! The roar of the crowd. It was as a siren song, pulling him away from Gloria and back through the curtain. Walking down that ramp and hearing his name being chanted, being cheered or booed, sent chills up his spine. Walking up the steel steps and through the ropes to immortality every single week made him feel like a God. Looking out into a sea of thousands, each and every one fixated on him was a massive thrill, as it was to watch him at work. To know that he was idolized by millions and that every action meant something to someone in that audience gave him a sense of purpose. He had influenced many generations as Hessian, and no matter where he went, providing there were likely to be fans around, he was praised as a superstar. Hessian was infallible, he was the Murder Show. Angelus Von Kelsig? Who was he? The freak who lost his parents to criminals as a boy and spent a life skulking around the states in the shadow of the mighty Hessian. The loner who couldn't make it as an average joe in the street. Wrestling was his life, and he was doomed to live it out as a hero in the ring and a nobody out of it. Only the shining ray of light that was Gloria could change all that...and as he sat in the dank dark of the pub he didn't know which path to take. A creeping ache crawled up his side into his shoulder, and necking the other Whiskey he groaned as the muscle throbbed before calming to a dull pulse that eventually faded away. Sure, this life was comfortable. He knew better than most how to do the job, handle the bumps and deliver the threats. He could find his way from Miami to Anchorage with no hassle and still find time in the week to stop by Memphis and see Gloria and get some training in before the week's match. The pain? That came with the territory, but in a world where that was the biggest concern he was in his element. But to trade it all in and worry about all the challenges of life unnerved him. There would be no more baying mobs, no ardent fans. Only Gloria. One woman, one person whom he had to serve and be served by. As amazing as she was he couldn't help hearing the chants of thousands of people over her sweet little voice. His stomach lurched as he downed his beer and he pushed the mug away, spinning on his seat and staggering to the toilet. Barging into a cubicle he dropped to his knees and as his gut rolled once more he sprayed the bowl with spew. His head pounded as he threw up, nauseous at the thought of having to choose between this and a life with Gloria. Gloria Carmichael Von Kelsig. It had a ring to it... Spitting the last of the sour vomit from his mouth, Von Kelsig ambled over to the sink and washed the small chunks out of his beard, staring into baggy bloodshot eyes glaring back at him in the dirty mirror. Last week he barrelled through half the roster in one night and came within a hair of victory. Here he stood, dishevelled and tired, sick and confused. "This or Gloria?" He spat at the glass, marring his reflection. He stood there for a while, looking at the grotesque misshaped form staring back at him behind the murky glass. This or Gloria... ---- "Hahaha, I had a great time!" Gloria yelled as the cab pulled into the driveway. Together, her and Larry spilled out of the back seats and as he asked the driver to wait she stumbled towards the front door, tanking the dregs from a bottle of champagne. As she slurped down bubble and air she hiccuped and discarded the bottle in the rockery along the front of the house. She wobbled to the side and retrieved the door key from her bag, fiddling with it in the lock while Larry staggered towards her. "Told you it'd be good, didn't I?" Larry exclaimed, placing a hand on the small of her back as she finally conquered the infernal locking mechanism of the front door. "Yeah, ye did. I really enjoyed muhself tonight Lar, thanks f'r takin' me out." Gloria slurred, and she leaned in to give Larry a peck on the cheek. In her mind it was a simple gesture, a friendly notion to thank him for spending time with her. There was no other intention than that. In Larry's mind, a beautiful drunk woman was leaning in to him, and as soon as her face was close enough he turned and met her lips with his own. The warm summer's eve became a chilled limbo in that instant as Gloria's eyes shot open upon feelign Larry's lips on hers. He initiated the kiss and executed it to perfection, and to his surprise felt the puckering of Gloria's lips back against hers. His loins were stirring as Gloria placed her hands on his chest...and pushed him away. "Larry!" She yelled. "What? What is it?" Larry groaned. "Ohh...shit..." he muttered, holding his head and suddenly realizing what he had done. "You kissed me! What the hell..." She stumbled back and knocked against the door, catching her footing before it swung wide open. "Oh, God I'm sorry Gloria. I didn't mean to, it's the drink...fuck I'm sorry Gloria, sorry..." Larry face-palmed himself and staggered back to the waiting cab. "Wait, Larry!" Gloria called after him, but he was already in the back seat, door closed. The cab drove off, the red beams of the tail lights casting an eerie glow over the shrubs and bushes as it rounded the corner and sped off along the main road. Gloria gazed at the drive in shock, mouth agape and mind blank. Taking one last longing look at where the cab had disappeared into the night Gloria turned and walked into the house, closing the door behind her and backing against it she shut it tightly. "Jesus..." She whispered. "What the hell just happened." Adjusting her dress she got up and made a beeline for the bedroom, a sullen look splashed across her face. Throwing off her clothes she clambered into bed as her emotions oozed through the numbing effect of the alcohol, and she sobbed into her pillow before glancing at Von Kelsig's side and throwing herself into his pillow, shaking ever so slightly and bleeding tears onto the sheets as she lay wrapped up in the duvet. "What have I done..."
