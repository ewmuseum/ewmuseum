Sometimes The Past Can Sneak Up On Ya'

25 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/25/05 Where could I possibly begin... There is so much to tell... Everywhere in life their are people that stand out amongst others. This seems to go with wrestling as well, seems like in every federation their are those few that just hold the federation in tact. Those that stand out like a man with a painted face. However, we've already got one of those here, so I guess a better analogy could'a been used. Perhaps a 38 D as your Sex Ed. teacher? Yeah, that'll stand out alright. However... In this case, we're dealing with a splinter in your finger. Oh how it annoys you digging in deeper each time you try to pull it out. Or better yet, a bullet that grazes your skin... Well, ... not A bullet... but THE bullet. For I am that bullet! I am the man that has just stepped through the doors and will leave my mark in this federation. You will all bare witness to the Legend of the past that has come back to reclaim his spot of the Legend of the present. You think I'm like every other joe blow... You think that this is all talk... You have no idea what your getting into.. Soon... Very soon... And it starts with two young punks that think they know what hardcore means. *The scene opens up inside a hotel room. Well, to be more specific, the hotel room's bathroom. And our superstar? Yup, he's here...early morning it is, Razor Blade sits on the toilet...reading a newspaper spread out...* Razor Blade: Damned mexican food! *fart* *Razor grabs the lysol spray and sprays around trying to weaken the foul odor* Razor Blade: I knew I should'a just ate a burger! Voice: Hey Raz, you ok in there? Razor Blade: Yeah Bob, be out in a minute. *Bob, the man outside the bathroom. Bob is a slender man who probably weighs a good buck twenty five soaking wet. Bob has been Razor's manager for the longest, and a damn fine one at that. Bob may not be the smartest, but he sure as hell knows what he is doing.* Bob: Alright, take your time heh. Voice: Man.. he's been in there forever...you sure he doesn't need any help? *This voice heard is none other than Bald Wall. Bald Wall.... the name pretty much says it all. It's weird.... and so is he. Bdubbya seems to be the biggest goofball around and usually confused for a retard. However, most of the time he just says the wrong things at the wrong times, or is in the wrong spot at the wrong time. Never-the-less he jus----Eh who are we kidding, he's a retard!* Bob: Bdubbya if you wanna go in there, fine. But I on the other hand wouldn't go in there without a gas mask and some pest control! Bald Wall: Pest control? Bob: As in the poison spray. Friggin smells like dead rats from out here already. *It's about this time when Razor swings open the door and looks at his two friends, if you could call them that, and exclaims..* Razor Blade: Awwww breathe in... breathe OUT! The Razor Blade Brand baby! *Razor swings the door back and forth to add to insult whiffing it around towards the two* Bob: Aww that's just fuckin disgusting! Bald Wall: Heyyyy that kinda smells like cooked carrots! I love cooked carrots! Bob: Screw that! Raz you can take Bugs Bunny over here and get the hell out! Razor Blade: Yeah , yeah whatever. *The three of them walk out in the room and sit down.* Bald Wall: So Raz, how do you feel about PRIME. Razor Blade: I feel the same way I've always felt about it. Bald Wall: Ok, cool. Razor Blade: Your weird. Bob: Ya know Raz, this is the week you make your in-ring debut... and your facing what seems to be two of the most hardcore men in PRIME, The HIT, and Trash Can Man. Razor Blade: As far as those two are concerned, I couldnt really give a rats ass about them. I dont give a flying fuck what they have accomplished, they aint shit. Hardcore isn't something you have done or even endured for that matter, hardcore is something you have lived through! It has to first flow through your body as pure as virgin mary herself. Bob: Yeah. Razor Blade: And those two yuppies are about as pure as oil in water. Bald Wall: Raz, I want to go swimming! Razor Blade: You truly are the king of random thoughts Bdubbya, and actually, if you want to go swimming thats fine by me because I plan on going to 24 hour fitness and you can go in the swimming pool while I work out. Bob: Work out? Are you serious? This doesn't sound like you? Razor Blade: It doesn't, but there is some fine young ass there. Bob: True, very true. Heh. *Razor and Bob out the hotel door talking* Bob: Just remember Raz, use your head in the ring and you will come out on top. Razor Blade: Ofcourse, I always do. These guys run around acting like they are somebody but I was the one busting my ass when they were still in highschool. Bob: So I guess your really back. Razor Blade: Yeah and this time it will be no different, you know why? Bob: Why? Razor Blade: Cause I aint going to give them a chance to take my spotlight away from me. Forty five years old for christ sakes, I dont got much spotlight left to be sharing it with anyone! Bob: Hey atleast your not having to wear those adult diapers yet haha. Razor Blade: Shit, after today's vist of dropping the kids off at the pool they barely didnt make it in time. Bald Wall: Can we go yet? Razor Blade: No! Bob: You know some guy called for you earlier Raz. Razor Blade: BP? Bob: Nah man.. some dude.. he claimed to be your brother. Razor Blade: Your shittin' me! Bob: No, really... I've known you for ages now... I didnt even know you had a brother. Do you? Razor Blade: I used to. Bob: Wait... so it's true.. man, what happened? Razor Blade: Ok Bald Wall, we can go to the gym now. Bald Wall: YES!! I cant wait to try on my new speedo! ......::::::::::...... My Brother? What the hell does he mean my brother!? I haven't seen my brother in years, and it seems those years weren't nearly long enough! I was told he was serving life, I barely walked out with my life in tact that day and I definitely dont need to be seeing him again. I remember that day... I'll never forget it... It's o' so clear to me... I was thirty-three years old and feeling good that day cause I just caught news from the boss that I was next in line for the world title shot at the next ppv. My brother Jason who is younger than me was sitting next to me in my black corvette. Jason never was good at much, only because he never put his mind to anything. Jason envied the things I had, hell, he probably even envied my life in general. I dont really know what was going through Jason's mind when it happened... but never the less, this was one thing I wish I could redo. It was a hot day in Houston Texas and the sun was glaring down on the black vette. 'Hey, why dont you take the top off Raz?' Jason said as he looked over my way. 'Why dont I just turn the AC on!?' I spoke back sarcastically. As I reached an oncoming stop light Jason was relentless 'No , come on Raz... just put the top down and pull over right there.' He exclaimed. 'Fine fine..I dont understand you.. but whatever.' I responded. I pulled my vette over and took the hard top off, while I was doing this Jason had walked right into the Popeye's chicken restraunt that I had pulled in front of... it wasn't much longer when Jason came running out... It was all really sudden but I looked up and a man came running out with a huge knife...when all I remember seeing was the man hitting the concrete and a loud blasting noise... I looked over at my younger brother and a gun was in his hand... a nine millimeter at that. Here I was a man with money and a future ahead of me and I was too blind to see the troubles my own brother was going through... How could I ever let it get to this.... Why couldn't he just ask me for money if he needed it... Jason jumped in my vette and screamed 'Go.. Go , Mash the gas!' I dont know what came over me... I guess it was just the shock of the whole situation... I was stunnned... I coudlnt move... I could see and hear and that was about it... I remember seeing Jason jump out and run off...about two mintues went by and cops were swarming... I looked over and there was the most horrific sight I could ever imagine seeing.... The man with the knife... he was a worker there at Popeye's... he was fine... He was sitting on a bench that was outside the establishment... when Jason shot the gun at the man, the man had tripped and fell to the ground... the bullet didnt strike the man... the bullet struck a six year old girl right in the face.... I couldn't bare to look after I caught a glimpse ... her face looked like spaghetti ... it was the most horrifying thing I had ever seen... and to think.. this all happen at the hands of someone related to me...my own flesh and blood. I tried to put my hands to my face to cry...but I couldnt... I then realized I wasnt in my black corvette... I was in a black and white squad car and my hands were handcuffed. The door swung open and there stood a tall slender white male cop. 'Look what you fuckin did! If you think your friend is going to get away your crazy! We're going to find him and kill him!' he yelled with anger in his voice. I couldn't say nothing... I was speechless.. I didnt care about whether or not they believed me that I had no part in this... I just cared about that little girl.... A few days later I was let out of jail after alot of questioning. They knew I was innocent, I never left the crime scene and I was more than willing to help them on bringing my brother in. However, I didnt end up having to do so since they caught him in a stolen car a week later... he was on drugs to top it off. I didnt even know Jason did drugs! I really dropped the ball with my brother... Mom and Dad told me to look after him and here I was an egotistical selfish prick that cared so much about myself that my younger brother wound up with a murder case and Grand Theft Auto... sentenced to prison for 30 years to life! He was lucky to get that, I paid thousands on his attorneys just out of guilt for not being there when he most likely needed me. I had put all this behind me and went on with my life, and for what? To only be revisited by these painful memories upon his release!? twelve years... twelve fuckin years and he's already out... Is that what a six year old girl's life is worth? Her life was pure and innocent, and his was struck by guilt. Twelve years was the price he paid for her death... he will once again breathe fresh air while she will never make it into middle school, never graduate from high school, never have a boyfriend, never go to college and never get married. Her life was taken, and for what... green pieces of paper!? The world is evil ... And here I am apart of the evil. *The scene opens up inside a black cadillac sedan deville. Bob is driving while Razor is sitting in the passenger seat talking away on his cell phone which everyone can hear the conversation due the hands free kit that plugs into the car speakers. Oh, and Bald Wall, he's in the back.* Razor Blade: I dont understand Jim, I pay you to be my agent... Jim: Yeah well, the keyword there was 'pay' and that you dont, hell, you owe me now for the past three months! Razor Blade: I'm employeed again now Jim.. I want the information on my brother and I want it within the hour, make it happen! *The conversation ends as Razor presses the 'end call' button on the cell that is connected to the dash.* Bob: Still no answer eh? Razor Blade: Damn Bob, I dont understand this bullshit... Bald Wall: I DO! Bob: You dont know jack shit. Bald Wall: Yeah I do... Raz wants information! Razor Blade: Oh god. Bald Wall: AM I RIGHT!? AM I RIGHT!? Bob: Seriously Raz, I dont understand why he still tags along. Razor Blade: Tax write off. Bob: Haha that's a good one. Razor Blade: No, really, I'm serious. Bob: Oh...how so? Razor Blade: Well he may only be forest gump slow ... but I claimed him retarded, so taking care of the mentally ill is a big tax write off. Bald Wall: FOREST GUMP! I LOVE FORST GUMP! Bob: Stupid is as stupid does. Bald Wall: I like when he talks about all the shrimp! Bob: That was Bubba. *At this time the cell phone rings and Razor presses the button to answer the call* Razor Blade: Yeah? Voice: Hey Raz this is Jim. Razor Blade: Tell me you got the 411. Jim: I do, I do. Bald Wall: Shrimp Soup... *Heard faintly in the background* Razor Blade: Speak. Jim: Well, he was sentenced to thirty years like you said. Thirty to life infact. Bald Wall: Shrimp Cocktails...*Heard faintly in the background* Razor Blade: Tell me something I dont know! Bob: Really. Jim: Well he has just gotten out on good behavior, hence the twelve years instead of thirty. Bald Wall: Shrimp gumbo... *Heard faintly in the background* Razor Blade: Good behavior!? Eighteen years off the sentence for fuckin good behavior!? Jim: Well not excactly.. twelve years off for good behavior. Bald Wall: Fried Shrimp... *Heard faintly in the background* Razor Blade: What about the other six? Jim: He knocked those off for serving double duty. Bob: Double duty? Bald Wall: Popcorn shrimp...*Heard faintly in the background* Jim: Yeah, it's wear you work a day and it takes a day off your sentence. Razor Blade: So he knocked off six years... Jim: That's the idea.. Bald Wall: Shrimp po-boy Razor Blade: Bdubbya! Shut up back there! Bald Wall: Ok. Bob: That was easy. Razor Blade: Alright Jim... thanks for the info. *With that Razor hangs up the phone.* Bob: Well now we alteast know why he is out early. Razor Blade: Yeah, I guess. He must have had some attorneys re working his case as well though. Bob: Where would he get the funding for that? Razor Blade: I have no idea, and im tired of thinknig about him for now... time to get more focused on my match this week. Bob: True that, your going up against some talent. Razor Blade: Meh, sorta... Bob: Seriously Raz, dont brush off the HIT and Trash Can Man. Razor Blade: Pfft, trust me, they're going to get brushed off alright. Bald Wall: I'm glad Nova is in PRIME, I love Nova! Razor Blade: Shut up! He's a lying punk! He acts like he was there for me, keyword acts. Nova just doesnt understand.. somethings never change. Bob: Yeah but just remember he is a tuff cookie. Razor Blade: I dont give a damn what he is... he will get what's coming to him eventually. Bob: But first the HIT and TCM. Razor Blade: Ofcourse. Bob: Well just be prepared, this is a big match early on and it's time to give a good first impression. Razor Blade: I made my impression the day I walked in here and this match will be no different.
