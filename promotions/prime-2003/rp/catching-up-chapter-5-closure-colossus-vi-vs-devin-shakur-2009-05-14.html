Catching Up - Chapter 5 - Closure (Colossus VI vs. Devin Shakur)

14 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 14, 2009 THURSDAY MAY 14, 2009 Sarah Dawkins stood before her son in the midst of a situation that neither of them could have foreseen over a year ago. Bryan sat in a holding cell after cursing out a judge in custody court, who happened to be the judge ruling over the case in which his mother was on the other side of the law. It was a long and complicated road that no one could have planned for, and the outlook didn’t seem too bright for either party. On one side of the coin, you had Bryan, a single father with nobody to help him care for his first born son, aside from his family and in-laws. Was he fit to be a father? In some ways, yes. In others, not so much. The young Hawaiian had all of the intentions in the world to take his son onto the road with him to live the long, grueling PRIME road schedule, stopping in various cities throughout the week with very little time off. The good intentions were there, but as far as ideal situations go, that would not be one of them. On the other side of the coin, you had the Dawkins and Sivec families. The Sivecs supported Bryan entirely through the entire matter until Nicholas was set to leave the hospital. Bryan had nothing prepared to set Nicholas up with as far as in-home care, and was planning on taking the prematurely-born less-than-year old baby out on the road with him for 48 weeks per year. That was simply enough to change their minds and side with Bryan’s parents, who were absolutely against the idea of Bryan taking his son on the road with him. Kenneth and Sarah Dawkins were against the idea at day one, and felt that way until this very second. With Sarah, it was clear that Nicholas’ best wishes were always her intention, but with Kenneth, mixed signals and ulterior motives were always to be expected. Kenneth and Bryan’s family rivalry had been well documented, and the current situation only stoked an already white-hot fire. “I just couldn’t take it anymore, sweetie. I couldn’t watch this happen.” Bryan peered up at his mother. Her makeup was running, her face sporting a pale expression of guilt and regret. She looked like hell. “I couldn’t watch a judge who doesn’t even know Nicholas determine his fate. I couldn’t let him go ahead and make a decision that we all could have just sat down and discussed. Sure, it wouldn’t have been an easy discussion to have, but at least at the end of the day, Nicholas would be getting the best situation presented to him.” Sarah sniffled and pulled a tissue from her handbag. Patting her face dry and inhaling one long breath of musty air, she again fixated her look on her distraught son. “Look what it’s done to you, Bryan. You quit work, you don’t even look like yourself anymore, and when I look at you, I can even seen through those jet black sunglasses of yours to see your cold, lifeless eyes. I can’t believe how much this has changed you. Bryan, this is tearing our family apart, and I don’t like it. I don’t like it at all.” Bryan could do nothing but sit and stare at his mother. She was right; his eyes were cold and empty, his face covered by an overgrown beard, and his pearly white smile gone in favor of a straight face which showed absolutely no emotion whatsoever. “You can’t even say a word to your mother. God dammit, Bryan, snap out of it. I’m tired of this. The trial’s done with.” Bryan’s eyes perked up to attention and he semi-corrected his awfully poor posture, whilst finally opening his mouth for the first time since he cursed out Judge Mayhan. “It’s…done?” Bryan’s expression was full of uncertainty as he peered up at his mother, clearly distressed. Sarah was again patting her face dry of the tears that seemed to be flowing freely from her eyes. “Yes, done.” Bryan adjusted his sunglasses slightly and was almost sitting erect again. The news wasn’t quite what he was hoping for, but certainly what he was expecting. It was a no-win case, and he was well aware of it. “So he’s all yours now? Nicholas, I mean.” Sarah sniffled once more and placed the used tissue in her bag. Without saying a word, she sat down by her son and placed her arm around him, putting him in a warm embrace that he hadn’t felt since this whole ordeal began months ago. A few moments passed, and with each tick of the clock, Dawkins’ heart rate began to rise slightly, perspiration beginning to ooze from the pores of his skin, and that overwhelming cocktail of fear and nervousness began to set in. At this point, Dawkins had all but given up. Apparently Sarah had given up as well. The only possible reason that she would be down here would be to apologize. The past few months were hell for everyone involved, and it was finally coming to an end. The light at the end of the tunnel was approaching, although for Bryan, it appeared that the light was tainted in a way that couldn’t be replaced. His son was going to no longer legally be his, and there wasn’t a thing in the world that he could do about it. He sat in his mother’s arms for what seemed like an eternity. It was such a simple act; the same warming embrace that millions of mother-son duos had enjoyed since man began walking the earth, and it was the same warming embrace that millions of other mother-son duos will enjoy until the very day that the earth ceases to exist. But this wasn’t quite like all of the other embraces. It wasn’t so much happy as it was relieving. The stress and fear was gone. For Bryan, heartbreak would set in and the realization that his son was gone would be with him for the rest of his life, but he would live to see another day. For his parents, a new addition to the house was coming. Nicholas Chandler Dawkins was now a permanent part of their family, and the practices of caring for an infant that they hadn’t gone through for years were now going to be resurrected and put into action yet again. A tear from Sarah’s cheek slowly rolled down and eventually dripped from her jawline and fell onto Bryan’s hand. After months of uncertainty, Bryan was now about to receive the news that would change the rest of his life. “He’s yours, Bryan.” One tear crept out from under those matte black sunglasses onto Bryan’s cheek before quickly disappearing into the forest of facial hair disguising the mug of the Flyin’ Hawaiian. “I knew it…” His voice trailed off toward the end of the final word, and it took a moment for his mother’s words to set in. Another tear crept from under the glasses and disappeared yet again into his beard. Sarah reiterated. “Bryan…Nicholas is yours.” Taking off his sunglasses for the first time all day, tears began to flow freely from the young Hawaiian’s bloodshot eyes, filling the dark circles that hung below his eyes and making his beard a wet mess. He simply couldn’t believe the news that his mother brought to him. “He’s…mine?” It had to be too good to be true. Tears continued to gush from Dawkins’ eyes and a few tears began to make their way down the sculpted face of his mother. For the first time in months, the tears flowing from the eyes of a member of the Dawkins family were tears of joy. The tables and circumstances had completely reversed themselves and the nervousness and fear that Bryan was feeling not even a moment ago was replaced with the overwhelming feeling of pure happiness. For the first time since Nicholas was brought into the world, Bryan was ecstatic about life, because for once in this situation, he came out on the winning end. However, the question was simple… …how? “But mom, what happened? A couple hours ago, it was an open-and-shut case against me.” His mother smiled at him the way only a mother can smile and reassure her son. “Like I said before, I couldn’t take it anymore, so I decided to take a stand.” Bryan was still teary-eyed, but fully attentive. “I couldn’t sit there and let the court take hold of the decision that we should be making as a family. I stood up to your father and the court and decided to drop the charges on my behalf. After a short recess, I talked to Pam and Mike, who also decided to drop their end of the charges, which left only your father to win over. Unfortunately, you know how your father is with this whole situation.” Just merely hearing about his father sent chills up Bryan’s spine and instantly caused the hair all over his body to stand on end. “Fortunately for us, since three-fourths of us dropped the charges, he couldn’t go on himself because of a lack of a reasonable case against you. So essentially, because the three of us dropped our charges, we forced your father into doing the same exact thing.” Her son’s arms reached out and brought her in for a tight hug and kiss on the cheek. Bryan couldn’t begin to thank his mother for what she had done, because most importantly, she got her son the freedom to raise his own child himself. But more noteworthy than that was the fact that she had the discipline and courage to stand up to her husband in the way which she did, without his blessing. Bryan admired his mother more than ever now, and was forever grateful for all that she had done for him today. “But Bryan, honey, you’re not getting off scotch-free with this. There are gonna be guidelines.” Bryan nodded and wiped his eyes of the tears. “Sure mom, anything.” “Well, we can’t have you hauling Nicholas around the country for ninety percent of the year. That’s simply not healthy for him, and it’s going to cause way too much stress for both you and us. It’s just not the ideal situation for him, and I truly believe that it wouldn’t end well for anyone. Now I know that you feel like you could handle supporting yourself as well as taking care of Nicholas, and I’m fully confident that you could do that if you so chose to, but in the best interest of Nicholas, I think that we should at least wait a year or two until we decide to make any decisions like that.” Sarah looked at her son to attempt to read his expression, but to no avail. His face was shrouded by his hands, which were wiping away the tears from earlier. “Honey, we all love you, myself and your father, Pam and Mike, and most importantly, Nicholas. He loves you to death, and it’s blatantly apparent that he hasn’t been doing well because you haven’t been around. Your son needs you, Bryan. He needs to see his father and know that everything’s gonna be alright. And believe it or not, we need you, Bryan.” A hint of a smile crept onto Bryan’s face upon hearing the last sentence. It wasn’t what he had planned, but with some tweaking here and there, it was appearing as though everything was going to work out for the better. “Now I need to go find your father, and catch up with Pam and Mike to see if we can figure out how to get you outta here. We gotta get you over to the hospital to see Nicholas and get him home to his crib. And my, my, we need to get you in bed too. You look like hell, sweetie. We’ll be right back to get you outta here.” And with another warm hug and quick kiss on the forehead, Sarah was gone to bail her son out of the holding cell, leaving him to think about the unbelievable chain of events that had happened over the course of the day. Bryan Dawkins’ life was now beginning to reshape itself. He could be his own man again. He could return to work. And most importantly, he can begin to finish what he started back in October… …being a good father.
