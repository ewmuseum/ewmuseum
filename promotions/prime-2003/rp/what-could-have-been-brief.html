What Could Have Been (brief)

Date: Today Seattle was many things. It was a city with a vibrant sports scene. It was a city with a really cool Space Needle. It was a city that Mr. Silver once hailed from before he realized the American mainland sucked. But, most importantly for tonight, it was a city where it fucking rained a lot. This meant that rather than being out looking for bitches, Kenjiro Ito was stuck in his hotel room, flipping through channels on the TV. “Kenjiro fucking Ito, there’s nothing on TV! Damned hotels and their five channels.” Stretched out on the hotel room’s king sized bed, ITO didn’t much care about Revolution. He didn’t much care about anything really, for that matter. A loss to Tony Gamble and a draw with Adam had Kenjiro in something of a funk. “Fucking Hell. I was the most prolific champion in the wrestling world last year. I defended the damned Ambassador belt 14 times! Now I can’t even beat two midcarders clean? What the fuck?” ITO sighed, continuing to flip through the channels. A movie here was too “faggy”, a show here too “homogay”, until finally, he stopped on the NLCS game between the Mets and the Cardinals. “This’ll do,” the Bringer of the Ice Age mused, watching the game play out before him. Had anyone else been in the room with him, his interest would have been extremely obvious. The reason for this might surprise you. “I coulda been doing this,” ITO said. For once, though, his statement wasn’t usual hyperbole. “University third base star. And then it all went down from there…” When reminiscing, it could be noted that Kenjiro Ito didn’t seem to be his usual, jerkish self. That was likely because he wasn’t a jerk. Not then, anyway. No, believe it or not, once upon a time Kenjiro Ito was a normal, well adjusted kid. And then, of course, it all got fucked up, but that’s a story for another day. “Until I’m here. PRIME. Fucking Jolt. Fucking IOWi. Fucking every place I was set to be the next big fuckin’ thing fuckin’ collapsing out from under me…” As much as he didn’t like to admit it, Sonny Silver had saved his ass. Saved him probably from falling out of the sport and being a jobless bum. Saved him from a life of mediocrity among the rubes he hated so much. Saved him from having to get a…*shudder* …REAL JOB. Can you see Kenjiro working at a movie theater? Fuck no. “Damn it all though, I’m the best athlete in this company and no one gives a fuck,” he muttered, still watching the game. Both teams had at various points employed Japanese players. Had he become good enough, he could have made it to America with his bat one day. Could have made it to the top. Just like he did in Jolt Wrestling…only to be there for thirty minutes before the fed shut down. Kenjiro Ito was a frustrated man…but a man on a mission. A man with a definite goal, a definite objective. “Don’t want to be no midcarder,” ITO said. “Want to be back in the main event one day. Get my ass back to the big payday. Sonny’s got five gajillion dollars, I sure as fuck don’t. I need the cash. The booze. The whores. All of it.” The lifestyle was what preoccupied Kenjiro Ito. Always had been. Probably, to an extent, always would be. The titles and the honors were something he took pride in sure, but they were means to an end. Wins meant more money, and a better life. Losses meant less money, and a worse life. Really, it was that simple.
