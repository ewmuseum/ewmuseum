Nightmares and Daydreams - The Important Things in Life

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Inside Monday, May 11, 2009 Vegas' lounge room had taken on a different shape these days. He had transformed it into more of a games room. But it wasn't your typical North American male games room. There was no foosball table to be seen, no dartboard taking up space on an already crowded wall or no crazy indoor basketball net that you may find at places like Chuck E. Cheese's. No, all this room consisted of was the standard pool table and let's face it... what warm blooded male would even consider calling an area of his home a game room without a stunning, green felted, hard wood beauty? A bar adorned the corner and much like the previous assessment of said pool table, a bar is a must have in any room classified as a fun room. But the most obvious change is the addition of a flat screen TV, a large and powerful sound system that could rock the walls to the ground and a large storage unit for these appliances plus all of the movies and other DVD videos. Oh yeah... there was also an Xbox 360 Elite system resting on one of the shelves. “Quick man quick, you're losing out on the goddamn points!” “I'm doing the best I can! I've never played this shit before!” Yes, the staple of most game players homes had to be Rock Band. Vegas' home was no exception. Killean was banging away on the drums and apparently according to his friend, he was NOT doing a bang up job so to speak. “There... green for overdriv... AHHH you missed it!” “Damn man, gimme a break here!” It wasn't long before Vegas was pushing his friend aside to take control of the drum kit. He began to pound away with expertise, hitting all the required notes and scoring the extra points. The stars increased and the crowd began to sing along now, instead of booing the band out of the building. Killean meanwhile just shrugged and took a long swig of beer. The pounding of the drums eventually ceased and the screaming crowd from the game replaced it as Dave stood up from his couch behind the drums. “Thank you Winnipeg! And Goodnight!” He tossed the drum sticks aside and looked over at his best friend, a smile hanging from his lips. “Man you spend way too much time on this stuff. It's just a game.” “Only the best game ever built! Seriously, what game can say it actually taught you how to play drums?” “You can't play drums... you don't even have a real drum kit.” “Oh it's comin'. Now that the bills have come and gone and the money is not much of a problem, I've cleaned up a perfect little spot right over there. It's the fun things in life I care about because without fun... what's the point?” Vegas smiled again, sitting behind the drum kit for Rock Band one more time to start another song. “Yeah, the fun things, giving Hanna the right to smack you upside the head every time a drum stick hits a real drum after you buy it.” “Nah, besides man, after my health issues, I've realized that fun is the key to a healthy life. I exercise, I lift weights, I eat... decent. Granted I could do with a few less chicken wings here and there but a guy's gotta live damnit! “Ha, I agree with you there.” Killean replied, it having been a well known fact for quite sometime that one of Killean's weaknesses was good chicken wings. “Heck, let's do lunch man. You go get the wings and I'll fire up the grill.” “Hey wait a sec.” Vegas paused his game and got up, stopping Killean before he could make his way out on to the deck to flare up the barbecue. “What's up?” “Man while we certainly don't mind you staying here, what are you gonna do about Jenny? It's been over three weeks since the fire and you haven't talked to her at all.” “She's...” Killean paused, the subject clearly taking a toll on him already. “She's with Hilary and Cadence. They took her in. She's fine.” “Fine? Dude it's been over three weeks like I said. Heck your new place will be ready this week. You gonna move in without her?” “Look Dave, I don't need the fifth degree about all this. The fuckin' loft burned down and everything was burned with it, including me emotionally. So let's just leave it alone and let me think about it ok?” It was all Killean had done since the fire. He thought, day and night, about Jenny, the fire, his relationship, his career and his daughter. It all still meant something to him, but Jenny was in a world of her own recently. The makeshift split up had affected her, bringing her back into a sense of reality. Her medications were lowered and they were working, but he had only talked to her once, saying hi on the phone and asking for Cadence. The funny thing was, he asked his step sister how Jenny was doing instead of asking Jenny herself. But really what was a man to do? His home was destroyed, his cherished memories ruined and his daughter favorite play things charred and broken. The loft wouldn't be ready for months and it was then that Killean decided to buy a home just up the street from Dave and Hanna. It would be ready this week. Jenny knew nothing about it. “Dude, fine. Go start the grill, I have some wings chillin' in the fridge in some marinade. I'll be right out.” Vegas left for the kitchen and Killean left for the grill. It had been exactly like this since Killean stayed with them. Vegas and Killean doing something and Desiree spending time with her Aunt Hanna. Things looked to be peachy, but certainly Killean had more on his mind than chicken wings. |~o~| Random Thoughts It's been a while since I booted up the computer and just started writing. The last thing I wrote about was my utter hatred of reality television. But really, is what I'm doing week in and week out on ReVolution not a type of reality TV? Was I not participating in the same grand scheme that I hated to see on cable? It's ugly nature polluting TV screen week in and week out for all to see. No, we had gone far beyond the early days of reality TV into a world of demented torture. I don't wanna watch someone get married in front of a camera because it pays for their wedding. I don't wanna see wives changing places and taking care of each others families. I think I saw a porno like that once. But at the same time, not every one wants to see me or the other stars in PRIME doing what we do all the time either. I would however have to agree that while wrestling to some might hold similarities to today's reality flock, there are glaring differences, differences that only the retired stars of yesterday could make proper sense of for wrestling these days is a mix of reality and your run of the mill daytime soap opera. Eat your heart out Young and the Restless. Having said that, the recent hatred between myself and Wade Elliott is pure reality at it's finest. We don't just hate each other for the pure sport of it. No, our hatred runs deeper into our minds and our bodies, lashing out at the very fabric of our being. Hence why we could only take one road... down the very disastrous path that is known to the PRIME Faithful as Ultra Violence. I've been in four matches at Ultra Violence. Two for the Universal Title, one for the 5-Star title and one for the pure sport of it all and every time I came out on top. I wonder what makes Wade think he could best me this time? |~o~| “Dude ok really, you need some lessons with this shit or something. I mean ya aren't bad at guitar but the drums just aren't your thing.” Vegas paused the song in the middle of a rather messy drum solo by his friend. He shook his head and picked up a chicken wing from his plate. “I'm not one to take this stuff seriously... but if you cut me off like that again this drum stick will quickly find it's way down your throat via your nostril.” “Big threats from a big man. Guy can't even hit the huge drum pad right, nevermind something as small as my nostril.” Sick burn. “Nah, if I had to hit something any smaller it would be inside your pant... hey Hanna!” Killean quickly stopped himself as Hanna opened the door and brought Desiree inside from a day out on the town. The little girl was dressed in a new summer outfit with a cute hat that had flowers on it. Desiree jumped into her father's arms just as soon as he got up from his seat. “She was real good today.” Hanna said, hugging her man as she talked. “Did you have fun with Aunt Hanna?” “Sure did! Like my hat?” “We love it!” Vegas said, throwing his arms out for Desiree to jump into after her Dad's. “It's cute, almost as much as you.” “Thanks Uncle D.” “Uncle D?” “Yeah, Aunt Hanna taught that to me.” “When you're older kid I'll have to teach you a good name for Aunt Hanna.” Vegas said, putting Desiree back down and smiling. Killean just smirked and Hanna sarcastically stuck her tongue out at him. “What's that name?” “Heh ummm Dizzy, let's go and try on your other new clothes ok?” Hanna said, taking Desiree by the hand and leading her to the door. She glanced back at Vegas with open eyes that certainly had to question just what he was thinking. “Alright man, I'm finishing this drum line and then I need to step out. Need to see a guy about a home theater set up for the new den.” Killean sat behind the drums and readied himself but before he could restart the game, Vegas spoke. “So did you think about what I said earlier?” It was right before he stuck a chicken wing in his mouth. “About?” “Jenny...” “I told you befor-” Killean tried to hit the start button on the game but Vegas pulled the drum controller out of the Xbox. “Nuh uh, you ain't getting' off that easy man. This is something you need to address and soon.” “I know you wanted to finish this song for me anyways, so here.” He handed the drum sticks to Vegas who took them. “As for Jenny, I'll deal with everything soon just like you said.” Sirrajin made his way towards the door and all Dave could do was watch him leave. He had to wonder just what Killean was thinking because at this point, Killean hadn't talked about it, he hadn't even mentioned it in passing. Truly, Dave wondered really if he would be seeing Jenny at all anymore. |~o~| More Randomness... Could it be that Elliott sees something that I don't? Perhaps the Bad Dog knew something that had evaded me. Cause I could swear that Ultra Violence was my event. I beat Karina Wolfenden for the Universal Title here. I beat Clyde Walkins for that same honor. Maybe it's time to use Wade's own words against him. You don't beat a dog in his own fuckin' back yard. It could be that Wade sees something that Brandon Youngblood didn't prior to Ultra Violence in 2007. I mean the cases are similar. Brandon came in running his mouth... check. All he wanted to do was start a fight... check. He pranced and danced around, claiming to be the guy who could put me on my shoulders for a three count or make me submit... check. Frankly, a lot of guys have done that and the list of people who have is really not that long. Logic thought he could do it last year. This new comers shows up, claiming to be completely against me, claiming to prove that my fake drug tests really said that I was on something. It really was a sick situation and I ended it. I beat Logic, shut his mouth and has he been heard from much since? Nope. What about Brandon Youngblood? Here's a guy who claimed to be the future of this company and he disappeared faster than a bottle of whiskey at Elliott's. In reality, Wade doesn't sound much different than any of these guys. He has a southern drawl sure and maybe he's not sober all the time but let's face facts, this is almost a carbon copy of years past featuring a not so different opponent. So much like years past, the conclusion will be the same. The carbon copy doesn't lie. |~o~| Killean was waiting in the aisle of Winnipeg's local Best Buy outlet, watching the people walk by. It was amazing what some people would do to buy the home electronics that they wanted. It all came down to people living above their means. One guy he saw had tried four different credit cards before finding one that worked and all he was buying was a Blu-Ray Disc player. Killean however was not walking out spending any less than Three Thousand Dollars. Heck, the TV he was picking up was over Two Thousand Dollars alone. A beautiful Sharp Aquos 52” LCD TV with a refresh rate of 120 hertz. Sure that may be a stretch for some people to understand, but that's what he was going for. The clerk came out of the back room with a cart full of equipment. A home theater system, Blu-Ray disc player, full entertainment unit and a stack of DVD's to boot. He approached Killean with the cart. “Alright sir. From what I'm reading here on the order, we have someone coming to your home to install and set up the equipment. Is that right?” Killean originally ordered home installation, but he wondered if Vegas could help him install it. It would certainly be a good day project, maybe more. “Nah, cancel the install. I'll be doing it myself.” He said, thinking that a self install would allow him to develop the perfect set up that he liked. “Ok sir. Will this be on credit?” He asked. Steve was his name, tall but not thin, yet he looked small next to Killean. “Cash.” “Excuse me?” “All cash.” Steve looked shocked. He just assumed based on most customers ordering such an extensive setup that credit would be used. Certainly most people would not have the money to buy such equipment. With the surprised look still etched on his face, he rolled the cart towards the front register. They passed by the iPods, cell phones, movies and video games. The last one stopped Killean. He thought about what happened earlier at Dave's. He knew Dave was right about having fun and enjoying yourself in life. Things can't always be so serious after all. “Hey hold up there man.” Steve stopped, observing where Killean was standing. He was looking at the Xbox hardware. “Sir?” “Go ahead and load up one of these Xbox Elite's and that Rock Band kit over there.” Steve went to go and grab the items requested. Killean smiled a little. “I'll show Dave.” |~o~| Final Random Thoughts Ok so let add everything up. It's 2009, Ultra Violence is the event that I'm undefeated at and Wade much like everyone else who came before him is claiming that he will be the one to put me down. It all spells a recipe for disaster if you ask me. Much like plenty of the reality shows on TV that are supposed to be “blockbuster hits” and “critically acclaimed”, Wade's critical acclaim went down the shitter once he turned his back on me, PRIME and it's fans. I've done that once before and to be honest, I'm lucky I was accepted back into the fold and once again respected by the fans. It's become common knowledge that beating me at Ultra Violence is akin to completely shutting down mafia operations. It's damn near impossible and the world knows it. Try to take away part of the mafia and in most cases, they will find something else and come out just as well of if not better than before. I am much the same way. I've been beaten before, I've lost titles and I've lost matches. I'm not unbeatable. But the last major event that I participated in was The Great American Nightmare where I was in the roulette. PRIME's best all in one match and I was beaten by only the Universal Champion. Everyone else fell before me, but I lost in the end. So now, it's time for me to come back stronger than I was before and what better place than Ultra Violence. Do the math Wade. Don't just sit there and run your mouth and say that you're going to put me down. It's common knowledge that the universe balances itself out. So now, it's time to balance it and the only way that can happen is to not see you come out on top. It's my time. Ultra Violence is my event. Nothing else matters. |~o~| “Dude, I'm glad you asked me to come and do this. It's much better doing this yourself so you get the exact set up you want that's for sure.” Vegas said, mounting the second of the rear speakers on the wall. Killean meanwhile was hooking up the last of the video components to the TV and the surround sound system. The Xbox even had it's own shelf in the unit, with the entire Rock Band kit set to the side. Boxes surrounded the outer area of the room and new furniture was wrapped in plastic. Killean was grateful that the insurance money came through so fast. He didn't have to empty one of his accounts to pay for a home full of furniture. Hanna walked into the sunken den which was quickly being turned into a theater room. She sighed loudly, winning the attention of the two guys in the room. “Dizzy is asleep for now. But I don't think she'll be down long.” There was a pause in the room, before someone spoke up. “Knowing her, she'll be down for at least an hour.” Everyone froze for a second. The voice was not male and Hanna certainly wasn't talking to herself. They all recognized the voice, but had to turn and make sure. There she was, standing in the opposite doorway, her red hair touching her shoulders. Jenny actually looked good, much better than three weeks earlier. She looked rested and healthy. Hopefully looks told the whole story. “What the... how'd you...” “I called her earlier.” Killean replied without letting Vegas finish what he was saying. He got a look of surprise from his best friend and his wife. Killean immediately dropped what he was doing and moved to Jenny. Vegas and Hanna slowly approached behind him, but they gave him room. “She didn't know anything about this place, so I had to tell her where we were.” Jenny pursed her lips, ready to ask the question everyone wanted to know. “So we talked earlier. Kill, are we ok?” The room fell silent. Killean looked at his feet for a moment and then at the opposite wall behind Jenny. He once again recalled what Dave said earlier about having fun and it kind of made Killean realize that if he wanted to make life fun, he wouldn't be able to do it without Jenny. It was a fact and he nodded his approval. “We're good.” He smiled, and Jenny followed. A large hug was shared between the two with a kiss soon after and their friends were not far behind. “Great to see you again girl.” Vegas said before hugging Jenny. “Yeah, we missed you these past few weeks.” Killean and Dave patted each other on the shoulders while Jenny and Hanna hugged. Vegas mouthed the words, 'Ya big lug.' as the friends finished their embrace. Sirrajin then stood with his arm around Jenny and presented the options for the evening. “Alright, how about we order some dinner. Then I'm gonna show you Dave what playing Rock Band is all about.” “Yeah? We'll see about that dude. Hook 'er up and let's rock.” “We got Rock Band now?” Jenny asked excitedly. “Sure do.” “Great, I've been playing for the past few weeks with Hil and Cadence. I got the drums!” Jenny exclaimed, grabbing the drum sticks off the set in the corner. Killean and Dave both voiced their thoughts. “Hey!” It was all about fun, like Dave said. Ultra Violence would be the same way but Killean would take control of things then. For now, Jenny was in charge with a smile radiating from her already bright face.
