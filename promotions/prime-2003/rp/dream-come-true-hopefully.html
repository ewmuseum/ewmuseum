Dream Come True... Hopefully

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today Here I stand... Atop a turnbuckle in front of hundreds of thousands of screaming fans. Absorbing the crowd’s reaction, and gathering my wits, I look down on my most dangerous of foes... Peter Vetra. The same Peter Vetra who’s shadow was cast over me when he eliminated me from the Dual Halo. The same Peter Vetra who stole at shot at the Alia title right from under my nose. The same Peter Vetra who, although unbeknownst to him, I hate with a furious passion. At every turn he has been the thorn in my side, the shackles on my feet, and the albatross around my neck. Pretty impressive for someone I’ve never even spoken to outside of the incoherent gibberish I scream moments before landing on top of someone. Yet still, I can’t stand him. In my most chaotic of losses, it always seems to be the face of Peter Vetra that I see. Every time we’ve met, though none of them have been in direct one on one competition, he’s always come out on top. Luck, I say. He’s always in the right place at the right time. Take our Alias title match for instance, he waiting for me to clear out the competition, then he swooped in for the in when the match was as good as mine. One on one, he would’ve never stood a chance. I should be on top of the Alias Title heap, and as I launch myslef skyward, I’ll show everyone in this arena just that. With Peter Vetra laying lifelessly below me sprawled out across the canvas, I catapult myself into the air performing a the first of the two back flips I’ll do before slamming into my opponent. I could probably win without pulling this move out of my bag of tricks, and it’d probably be smart not to as the risk it involves has cost me many a match. I doubt most others would bother to go for it, but it’s more or less my become my identity here in PRIME... and probably the only reason I’ve still got a job. If nothing else, everyone knows I can do a double shooting star press. Besides, where there’s risk there’s fun, and like I always say... What good is winning if you can’t have fun doing it? My ribs are jarred within my chest as I come crashing down across Vetra so hard I bounce back up before slamming back down into his midsection again. Laying in a vertiginous heap, making the cover is practically all I can do, with anything involving movement becoming a lost cause as exhaustion takes hold. Three seconds later the crowd is going nuts as my name is chanted over and over, with the voices of the thousands in attendance gradually going down and meshing into a collection of only a few voices that seem much closer than those of the fans. “Jackson! Jackson!” I hear as my body shakes uncontrollably. Suddenly I open my eyes to find four of my closest friends standing around my bed leaning over me while Dalia has opted to sit indian style on my chest... as she continues to shake me. “Alright, alright, I’m up!” I mumble, pulling Dalia close to me as I roll to my side and cover my head with a pillow. I don’t know why I even bother, that never works, so as usual, the harassment persists. “How come you didn’t tell us you were doing a show in town?” “Why didn’t you tell us your match was here?” “Can we have tickets!?” “You’ve GOTTA get us backstage!” Underneath my pillow I roll my eyes, somehow they found out what I’ve been trying to keep quiet all week. I can’t have them come and see me, I’ve finally getting some chances in singles competition and don’t need any extra distractions. Besides, it’s not as if I’m exactly the shit in PRIME, it’d kill me for them to find out that half the people backstage only know me as “the crazy flip guy”. I’ve gotta get outta this. I’ll tell them no, they’ll persist, and then I’ll lie. I’ll tell them the show’s sold out. Or maybe that I haven’t been signed long enough to have ticket privileges... like theme park employees. Regardless of what lie I come up with to keep them away, I know one thing’s for sure... I’ve gotta get outta this bed if I expect to have any sort of peace. As I stumble my way into a pair of flip flops and stretch a question of real importance pops into my head, one actually worth waking up for... “I wonder if we’ve got any food.”
