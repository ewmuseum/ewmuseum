Pegasus Chronicles – Dust to dust

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 26, 2005 Falling from place, a dark haze in the form of speckles. Burning then dying out. As it hits the ground, its blown away in an instant. Inhale. “… ahhhhhh.” Sigh. Exhale. “phew.” Slowly burning away, a distorted ring of fire, before the gray fog exhales escapes a pursed set of lips. Then the ash drops, crumbling and blowing away with the lightweight wind that’s present. Inhaling and then exhaling before a satisfying sigh is heard, the smoke freely floats off into the atmosphere, disappearing just as quick as it was smoked. ”Ahhh, relaxing.” Finally the bronze butte is dropped. It hits the ground and is then smashed in, letting it’s inside be exposed like road kill. ”Cancer sticks… heh.” The fumes escape his lips for the last time as his face lets out another relaxing sigh and is fulfilled with a giant smile. ”We all die sometime, who cares if I push for it.” His voice hoarse, not showing any care for his health, or concern of the death he speaks of. However, in front of a church people feel more confident. Strolling through the front door and walking down the middle aisle towards the front, where an iron casket is set. Bowing his head, he looks down at the ground, as the few people on both sides of the aisle sit in somber. Sometimes… life escapes us, and we lose the control we thought was in our possession. Walking up to the casket, he peers down at the lifeless pale body. The face of the man showing a soothed appearance, however his body stiffer than a porn stars erection. Looking down at the man, his eyes show no fear, a peerless white gel, with the green embers surrounding a black marble. ”Bless thee my brother, in lord as in heaven, has thee by thee name, and watch over him until thee is passed upon the white carpet of your kingdom. Bless thee my brother, blood, and fellow warrior…” Whispered is the dialogue as he bends over the chest of the diseased. Removing a cross necklace upon his neck, and placing it in the folded hands of this warrior. Returning to his normal poise, Jesse stares down at his face and gets once last glimpse of an old friend, before turning around and walking up the aisle, and out the front doors of the church. It never strikes you that death is inevitable, or at least to most it doesn’t. We forget, we’re not immortal, we’re not unbeatable, our bodies do take tolls, and we will eventually descend from our peaks and hit the ground like the shooting stars we are. The wind meets his face with some intensity, as does the sprinkles of rain that follow, and then CRACK! Thunder pursues. Like an angels wings being mercilessly slaughtered, the heavens weep to the antics sending all there emotions hailing down in this uproar of a storm. Yet beyond that, we have to understand something. While we have life, we need to utilize it. Potential is not to be wasted, and desire is meant to be fulfilled. Because every shooting star has at minimum, one wish, that shall come true. Walking down the steps he raises his hand, and a taxi gradually pulls up to him. Opening the door, he steps in, and says some street number, then looks up at the church, staring at cross upon it’s front gates, and then making a visual of the path he walked down. Upon the outer wall is iron gates, and there on the far left closest to the street near a street light, sits a crow perching over with something in it’s mouth. What looks to be an olive, is actually an eyeball. Jesse stares at it, only before the taxi passes, and then he lose focus and just stares off at the road in a mindless trance. Start of a War. Click. Click. Click. Click… Click. ”Yesterday, local citizen Sean Mitchell Leon, age 27, better known to the wrestling industry as Sean Lere along with his tag team partner Mike Lere, was buried. The professional wrester had a short-term career in the ring that ended with a torn ACL and two fragmented discs in his spinal column. As of August 24, 2005, Sean was pronounced dead due to unknown reasons at this time. Professionals are looking into the case hoping to find the result, but at this time nothing is known. When more information is provided, it’ll be given. That is all, back to you..” Click off. The remote is dropped on the table with little care for it’s future condition. Shaking his head as he rubs his goatee, Jesse is disgusted and pondering over the announcement. Sean Lere was his old friend, apart of a stable with himself and his other friend Gilbert Alfy, or Geno as the called him in the ring. Throughout his career Jesse had trained him to be a follower of the warrior ways, as Sean had shown potential in an earlier organization. However due to his injury that ended his career, he was purely a knowledgeable man of Jesse’s training. ”Something seems wrong with that news report, like… perhaps its… ahhh… WRONG!?” Swiping the table with his hand he chucks the remote at the wall, and breaking the remote in the process. Shaking his head as his hand trembles, his body suddenly stops moving as if on pause in a video game. Brushing the hair out of his eyes, Jesse looks back up at the television and rubs his goatee again. ”I lose not only a friend, but a man who I taught everything I knew. He was as close to me as family, and now I know he’s six feet under. That’s not something I want to deal with… damn Mike, what the hell is going on?” Was he crazy? Who was Mike? Speaking to what seemed to be nobody, Jesse buried his head into his arms on the table. Suddenly,”Jess man, I know how you feel. Trust me, it doesn’t do any better to weep over it.” From the side of the table, leaning back on his chair, sits the Mike that was referred to in Jesse’s dialogue. His face a tan brown, as if bronze but with a bit of Hispanic heritage showing throw its tint. He has short auburn brown gel-spiked hair, with hazel pearls for eyes. ”But he was your half-brother, how can you be so eased at a time like this?” said Jesse as he rose from his arms, and stared at Mike with questions circulating from his eyes. ”You can’t live on the past Jesse, remember, you taught us that. Life is the same outside the ring as it is inside. You don’t look back at what has happened, but instead look forward to stop what is to happen, and change it to benefit you,” replies Mike in a calm voice. The nemesis warrior’s eyes close as he sighs and exhales. Had this warrior forgot his own teaching? Had he slipped on something he himself once took to heart? Could it be? Well even so, it happened to everyone, and in this state, it was common for someone to forget a teaching they had because of the overwhelming of feelings. ”Yeah I apologize, this is just… you know how my life is. Damn it Mike, every person close to me is either dead or so far away I never see or talk to them. It’s all just caught up to me over time.” Staring back down at the table, Jesse begins to smile, not in a twisted freak show like way, but smile as in a grin of remembrance. ”Remember that time, when Sean, you, and I all dumped paint all over that jack asses limo at the hotel we were staying at, since he treated his woman like shit. Awwh was he pissed, Woke up the next morning, saw his limo and nearly freaked. Pink, yellow, and cyan go well together when painting cars, don’t they?” Mike couldn’t stop but grinning,”What can I say, we had a knack for causing a ruckus.” Standing up Mike laughed as he walked to the window and peered out it to the clouds. ”You know Mike, he may have passed on, but it sure the hell feels like he’s still here with us. I mean, its just this intuition or some-“ Ppppssssslllll. Jesse turned around in a jiff, looking over at the broken window, and Mike who is standing in front of it. ”What the hell happened Mike? What’d you do?” Stumbling backwards, Mike drops to the floor clutching his chest. Dropping from his chair without even touching his feet on the floor does Jesse. Gripping the hands of Mike he looks at him as he stares up gasping for air, stunned, but also shaking somewhat. ”What’s wrong Mike?! What happened!?” Unable to speak, his hands let loose of his chest allowing blood to spill over the area of his chest onto the floor as his lips attempt to form words. Jesse places his hands back on his chest as tears form in his eyes. Grabbing the phone he dials 911, getting someone, he gives information as he falls to the floor next to Mike and tells him he’ll get through it… Double or nothing. Silence is almost touched in an angelic way, as the door is opened and two boots pace there way to a still motion, in which the body of those boots sits down. There Jesse looks down upon the body of Mike, lying in a hospital bed on oxygen support and tubes sticking out of his arms every which way. Surprisingly, he made it. The gunshot as it was later determined, shot him in the chest, and hit a lung, collapsing it. In the process it messed up quite a few muscles in the chest area that support the lung from being damaged. Ahhh, how does this come about… when are people suddenly allowed to run around with weapons in there possession. Fucking society… give me a gun and I’d be on America’s Most Wanted for killing all these pansies. Ehhh. Looking over to the small counter, he places his coffee on it, and then notices a small card and some flowers. His eyes in question as to which they are from, his curiosity gets the better of him and he finally picks it up. Opening the card he begins to read: Dear Michael, I hope in this time of needYou find a comfort of much likingFor your life will soon take a new pathIn a direction similar to your brotherFeel free to carry onFor now is the beginningAnd when you are all deadIt shall be I who stands victorious Warriors are merely menMistaken by prideSo show some nowAs I give you one last good byeBefore you die Signed Sincerely Yours,M.A.K. At the bottom is a smiley face with X’s for the eyes and its tongue hanging out. Grumbling at the paper, Jesse looks at Mike with sorrow for what he may have brought upon him. ”I’m sorry man… really, I never meant for this.” As his words slip from his lips, so does the life in Mike. The machines around him begin to go haywire with beeps and emergency noises, sending Jesse into immediate reaction. He runs out the door and screams for help as he comes back and grabs Mike by the hand and hits the buzzard in which a patient can reach a nurse by. Talking to him, he says a prayer in whisper. ”Lord by thee name, as on earth as in heaven, save thee warrior, and spare another, for he deserves better. For life, for death, for all that is holy, bless me this wish and take something far less…” His voice shortens out as he repeats I under his breathe, just as a nurse comes rushing in. She pushes him aside and begins to check everything, as Jesse bows his head continues to pray in silence.
