(not so) Smooth Criminals

15 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 15th, 2009 FADE IN. INT. BANK – DAYTIME Parting through the glass revolving doors, the Lunatic’s bright green hair draws attention from numerous patrons. Mary-Lynn Mayweather is by his side, holding a manila envelope in her hand. Flyer stops near the entrance, taking in the bustling scenery. HIGH FLYER : I hate banks. Flyer sneers, wiping his nose and continues on. Mayweather quickly scurries behind him. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: It's the fifteenth. You've got active investments. And since you won't let me talk to a machine... Flyer looks over his shoulder back at the petite redhead, just as a young elderly couple wander in behind them. HIGH FLYER: I've told you, the Robot Apocalypse will someday use my bank routing number to destroy John Connor. I can't let that happen. A flash of light. Three sparks from the tip of a 9 millimeter gun. Followed by panic, terror. Everyone stops looking at Flyer's green hair, and they fall to the ground. ROBBER #1: EVERYBODY DOWN! BEND YOURSELF DOWN! Flyer however, remains standing, perplexed. Mary-Lynn tugs at his jacket, dragging him to the floor.. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: (Whispers)Get down Jack. Three robbers stand in the middle of a bank waving around pistols. Each of them are adorned in black ski masks, black leotards. Flyer scoffs from his position. HIGH FLYER: (Whispers) I see those three guys dressed like that walkin' down a busy street, I'm thinkin' robbers. I'm sorry. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: (whispers-quickly) Oh God-I-Don't-Want-To-Die... HIGH FLYER: I'm pretty sure we saw them when we went past Starbucks. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: (whispers-quickly) -PleaseIdon'twanttodie. HIGH FLYER: I woulda said something but I figured they'd be robbing that Starbucks. One of the robbers, the scrawniest of the three, rushes over and points a shotgun into the Lunatic's face. Flyer tosses his hands in the air quickly, and stares down the barrel of the gun. ROBBER #2: Nobody make a sound. Flyer continues to stare, holding his hands in the air. The robber gets incensed. ROBBER #2: ARE WE CLEAR?! Flyer stands there, and quickly glances at Mayweather. ROBBER #2: Well!? HIGH FLYER: But. The Robber points his shotgun further in flyer's face, nudging his nose. ROBBER #2: BUT WHAT! NO SOUND! Are we clear?!? Flyer pauses, mouth agape. The Robber places the shotgun's nozzle around the Lunatic's lips. HIGH FLYER: (mumbles through a shotgun) I don't know if you want me to reply or not. Quickly, the robber slams the butt of his gun into the Lunatic's face. He falls into Mayweather's freaking arms. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Oh God! Jack. The robber shouts and points the shotgun at Mayweather. ROBBER #2: Don't get any ideas Red, or you'll be seeing it. Meanwhile, the main robber has hoped over the main counter, and is angerly threatening a clerk to open up the back safe. Our third robber has the other side of the room covered, currently disarming a security guard and taking his tazer. Mayweather strokes Flyer's hair, a small wound trickling blood into his eyes. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: We're gonna be okay Jack. Hold on. (rushed)Pleasedon'tkillusibleedtooeasily! HIGH FLYER: HEY PIMPLES! The second robber sways the shotgun from Mary-Lynn to Jack. He pumps it, effectively making all prior threats to them meaningless. HIGH FLYER: (calmly)You keep that shotgun pointed my direction friend. And you won't lose your legs, yeah? ROBBER #2: Oh? Like this? The second robber points the shotgun down to Flyer's injured left knee. Suddenly, a BURST. The glass revolving doors SHATTER. Our second Robber is stunned from the blast, just long enough for Flyer to swiftly kick him in his testicles. The Lunatic quickly swept his leg, kicking the shotgun away. The FBI bursts in, guns pointed vests worn. They quickly take down the third robber, and a second squad wanders over to the fallen second robber, guns drawn. Quickly, Flyer grabs Mayweather by her arm and drags her out of the bank. Out of that hellhole, and into the awaiting arms of the local police, ready to get their sworn statements.
