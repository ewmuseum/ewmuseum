Date: Between ReV 202 and CVI Journal Entry: August 14, 2009 I'm retiring from professional wrestling. Even weeks after I made the announcement it just doesn't sound right or real. It may hit me when I get to Fenway Park, maybe when I unpack or even pack my bag or maybe when I'm pinning Tyler's shoulders to the mat. But I can say with confidence that the reality of the situation hasn't hit me yet. Before, when I retired, I thought it was what I wanted. I wanted to be home with my little girl and my family. Everything went well for the first little while. I was happy to be home all the time and I spent more time at the club helping to turn it into a huge success with Dave. But as they all say, that bug can strike again. A little insect that crawls around on your shoulder that no matter how many times you try to get it off or scratch the itch, nothing seems to make it completely disappear. It sits there and it seems to talk to you. It whispered in my ear that it wasn't my time to step away from the lime light. I still had more to do. In the end, that little bug ended up being right. So I came back. I'd like to think that my time is PRIME is considered a success and that I didn't make my comeback a failure. I beat some big names after coming back and I also lost some matches I didn't think I could lose. But if Matt Ward and Karina Wolfenden were any type of example in creating huge winning streaks in the start of their PRIME careers, they taught us all a huge lesson. Everyone, no matter how good or how dominate they are, loses a match. But in retirement, it's given me the ability to see things from a whole new light. I haven't been the central focus on the shows leading up to Colossus but people are looking forward to seeing me hand Tyler Nelson his still beating heart before he hits the mat. Not being the constant center of attention has helped me understand new things. Sure I'm retiring and it'll be extremely important to me to say goodbye to this world again. But my career just doesn't have a meaning to yours truly. No, other people see my career as a beacon of light. They've supported me through thick and through thin and have been there every step of the way. If it weren't for the fans, we wouldn't have a career or a life in this business to speak of. But I think it's time you understood the life and story of another fan. Someone that I have formed a special bond with in the weeks leading up to my final big dance in front of millions of people. His name is Christopher. He's eight years old. |~o~| July 29, 2009 The flashing of a TV illuminated the room. A wheelchair was positioned at the dining room table and there was a small bowl of cut up fruit sitting in front of it. The clock over the table was drowning out everything in the room with it's constant ticking but that could probably be because the TV was muted. A small hand reached over for a piece of fruit in the bowl and slowly removed it, bringing it back in front of the chair out of view. A couple people could be heard in the adjacent kitchen talking when a small voice suddenly spoke up from the wheelchair. "Mom! It's time!" A woman ran from the kitchen into the dining area and smiled at her son. She looked youthful, possibly in her mid thirties at the oldest and grabbed the wheelchair from behind. Two small hands reach over to the bowl of fruit and remove it from the table. The chair starts to move. "You just can't miss the first few minutes can you Chris?" She said, rolling him through the maze of furniture to a final spot in front of the couch. She reached over for the remote and changed the channel on the TV to the FX Network. A second later, the intro for PRIME's ReVolution flashed on the screen. "You know it mom." The boy was quite tiny actually. He just didn't have small hands, but his entire body was a bit smaller than a normal sized child his age. He had light brown hair and a great big set of brown puppy dog eyes that would melt any humans heart. Another female set foot into the area, much younger than Christoper's mother. "I sent off the email mom. Hopefully they get it." She paused, spotting her little brother parked in front of the TV with his eyes glued to the screen. He took another piece of fruit and put it in his mouth, a cute little grin appearing on his face. "It's amazing how he's glued to this thing once his show comes on." "You're telling me." The mother replied. "Let's hope they do respond to that email. It would be a dream for him." They both look over to the wheelchair smiling. The intro finishes showing pictures of all the main superstars. The boy reaches over and grabs his replica title belt off the side of the couch. He holds it in his lap tightly as the show begins. |~o~| July 31, 2009 "Hun, I'm home." Killean closed the door to the house and placed his bag on the old style chair by the front door. He slipped off his shoes and made his way for the spiral staircase but he was cut off by a speeding Jenny on her way down to meet him. "SHHHHHH! I finally got Dizzy down for her nap. You wake her, I kill you." She smiled when she spoke the last few words. They embrace and share a kiss, something not uncommon when Killean arrived back at home after a PRIME event. "You're late." "I know I know. Last couple weeks with PRIME you know. Had to make the appearances and please the fans." "Speaking of pleasing people, you got another fax from the head office." Jenny said, moving over to the dining room table in the next room and grabbing a piece of paper from it. "You might wanna read this one Kill." "If it's another contract, I don't wanna even look at it." Killean replied, removing his dressy type shirt and unzipping his bag to retrieve a fresh tank top. He pulled it over his muscular frame as he walked to where Jenny was standing. "They don't seem to get the hint. I'm retiring. But every time they want to send me a contract, to see if they can coax me into staying." He put his arm around his bride to be and sighed. Jenny shook her head and smiled a little and Sirrajin immediately took notice. "That's not what this one is about." Jenny said with a slight tear in her eye. Killean grabbed the letter and pulled out a chair. He proceeded to sit, Jenny hovering behind him placing her hands on his broad shoulders. Killean took his time reading, a small grin had appeared on his face as well after a few moments. "This is amazing. I've never received something like this before. They don't forward anything to me unless it's really moving and that's hardly ever." "I know." Jenny said as she wiped the single tear from her cheek. "I just had to read it when it came through." Killean sat and stared at the white piece of paper for a moment. Numerous different ideas ran through his mind. He smiled at all of them. "Hun, where's the expense account book for the club?" "Upstairs. Why?" "Good. Leave it there. Call Dave and tell him I'll be in tomorrow but that we're leaving for Boston a few days early. This little guy and his family are gonna experience Colossus in a light they never dreamed of." He got up from the table and quickly made his way for the kitchen, leaving the ltter on the table. For once, he was motivated to leave his home, something that hadn't been the case much in the recent past. He had found a new motivation, a dream that he could share. It was a chance to help someone less fortunate than himself and for anyone who knew Killean Sirrajin over the years, how could he resist? |~o~| August 7, 2009 A weeks worth of plans were in place and Killean inhaled deeply as he approached the front door to the home. He has spent the better part of three days actually being in Boston, trying to prepare everything he wanted to. Everything else he was able to accomplish over the phone to actually avoid leaving home two full weeks before Colossus actually took place. The Supreme Machine raised his hand, knocking a couple times on the hardwood door. His arms retreated behind his back where he rests his hands. His new Supreme Machine t-shirt was bright and wrinkle free and his sunglasses were on, creating and completing his trademark look. The door opened and a young lady peeked out. She glanced quickly at Killean and greeted him but quickly turned her head around back into the house as she spoke. "Chris, I'll be right there. Hang on a second." She turned her head back to Killean standing on their front porch. Before she could recognize who it was his deep voice greeted her in return. "Actually, Christopher is just the guy I wanna see." He smiled. Her teenage eyes locked with his and based on the amount of PRIME television her little brother watched, she recognized him immediately by association. She yelped a little bit and swung open the door and leaped into his arms giving him a tight hug. This action caught him a little by surprise but her words were quick to relax him again. "OH MY GOD! You have no idea what this would mean to my little brother!" She let go and stood back on the ground as she fanned her face to get a hold of herself. Another woman came through the doorway and her jaw dropped straight to the porch floor as soon as she saw who was standing there with them. "Ms. Edwards I presume?" Much like her teenage daughter, she shrieked but didn't leap into his arms. She covered her mouth and just stared at the mountain of a man on their front porch. He smiled lightly again. "Oh my... yes! I am and the hyperventilating teenager who greeted you is Kate. I can't believe this!" "Well PRIME got your email and forwarded it off directly to me. I got it as soon as I arrived home and made the arrangements to arrive here much earlier than Colossus to meet with your wonderful family. So where's Christopher?" He tried to look around the pair of ladies in front of him but Ms. Edwards, otherwise known as Diane, grabbed his hand and invited him in. "We really weren't expecting company today so you'll have to ignore the mess." She said as she pulled him inside. Kate closed the door behind them and he followed obediently through the hall and towards the living room. The TV was lighting up the walls and Killean spotted a wheelchair parked not too far from it. Ironically, he recognized the content playing on the screen as the stage of Colossus III. He was in the ring against Tchu, possibly one of the greatest matches of all time in the wrestling world. His mind briefly flashed back to those times but he was quickly pulled back into reality as his wrist was jerked into the other direction. He stumbled a little into the kitchen as he realized the hand that pulled him away was the one of 17 year old Kate. "Gee, what a grip you got. Ever thought of wrestling there my dear?" He said as he rubbed his wrist, playing like she actually hurt him a little. "I'm in Jiu Jitsu actually." "We just didn't want him seeing that you actually came just yet. It's a surprise to him that we even sent the email and he would go a little bonkers if he saw you right now which wouldn't be a good thing for him." Diane said, grabbing a couple glasses from the cabinet beside her. "Drink?" "Uh sure, water would be great thanks." "I have to ask you Mr. Ummmm..." "Please, Killean is just fine." "Ok, Killean. So what made you take notice of our email? I mean you must receive hundreds of them all the time." Diane said, setting the water down on the table and inviting Killean to sit with her. Kate grabbed a soda from the fridge and sat down too. "It's really quite simple Ms. Edwards..." "Diane." She cut him off. He smiled and shook her hand as she introduced herself by name. "Diane... PRIME gets quite a few requests via email but they happen to know that I'm a very charitable individual. They knew I couldn't resist helping a family in need experience a once in a lifetime opportunity, especially with the way you spoke of Christopher's unwavering support of our programming." "I wrote the email Killean." Kate spoke up, seemingly proud of being the one to get their attention with her writing. "You have no idea. When PRIME is on TV, the world stops for him. He loves to watch all of your shows. He even watches the reruns and as you saw, the old DVD's too. He's got so many collectibles now too. He's your biggest fan." The last line made Killean smile brightly this time. He took a sip of his water and opened his mouth to ask the much more difficult question. "That's amazing. I can't wait to meet him. But I have to ask, what was it he has again? I don't know the exact meaning of it all." "He has childhood multiple sclerosis. He was diagnosed two years ago and it really took it's toll on the outset." Diane said, shaking her head a little. "Basically it's left him unable to walk much at all so he's unfortunately confined to a wheelchair plenty of the time." "Well even though it was my decision to come here, PRIME asked me to act on their behalf too. We have plenty of surprises for all of you and most of all for Christopher. But first I must give you this." He unfolds a piece of paper he just took out of his pocket and handed it to Diane. "What's this?" She asked. "It's blank." "It's a check. On top of the donations I'm making to the multiple sclerosis foundations around the country, I'm also giving this to you to cover medical expenses for him for the next while. Write in the amount, as long as it covers everything for the next long while." In tears upon hearing the explanation for the check, Diane gets up from her chair and wraps her arms around the now standing PRIME Choice. "My lord, you have no idea Killean what this means. It's been hard getting by without much insurance." "I took that upon myself too. Starting in a month's time, an insurance policy has been set up in my name, all made possible by the MS Society and the info they will provide to us upon approval by you of course." Diane's hand covers her mouth again and her daughter is right there beside her hugging her close. "I don't really know what to even say." She replied. "You don't need to say anything Ms. Edwards. It's just the tip of the iceberg that I've arranged for the days leading up to Colossus. We've got quite a few fun filled days ahead of us coming up." He smiled. |~o~| Journal Entry: August 14, 2009 Continued I was always a giving man. It just seemed to be part of my nature. Sure I have a gruff exterior on television and in person, but behind it all I'm really just a teddy bear of a man. Dave and I give to Thanksgiving and Christmas charities every year to make sure people less fortunate than us have food on their tables for the holidays. We buy gifts for those people as well and we donate to numerous charities in our companies name. Christopher's will be just another one added to that list. Soon after talking to the Diane and Kate, it was my pleasure to meet Christopher. He couldn't believe his eyes and I couldn't either. He looked at me for the first time with those great big brown eyes and they were so full of joy and braveness. What he was fighting through was something a man just couldn't fathom. Normally kids do not suffer the effects of MS at such an early age. But as I have learned, few do and Christopher is what it really means to be a fighter in my opinion. He was a delight to talk to, holding his replica title belt from PRIME in his arms as he almost couldn't contain himself. For someone going through what he had been, it was truly special to see him just being a kid at heart. I have a healthy daughter and upon meeting Christopher I'm extremely grateful that I have learned that I sometimes take for granted the little things in life. His life is a constant fight, a step in the right direction can sometimes lead to two in the wrong direction but he always seems to manage to get himself back on track. Back when my home burned, I was quick to jettison Jenny from my life and from my mind. But it was all material things that burned. My collections of awards and titles from PRIME meant a lot to me, but after the events that I just experienced, I quickly remembered what means the most to me about my career under the spotlights. It's how I touched the lives of others that really mattered the most. In the years to come, people won't remember all the titles I obtained or all the matches I won. They won't care about the big pyro explosions or the bright lights. No, what will matter the most is how we all treat the fans of our product. If it wasn't for them, no one in this business would be successful. Much like the tours to the middle east and supporting people here in our end of the world, people are what matter the most. It's the people who support us who deserve our undying gratitude. Tomorrow and Sunday will be my final bow to the world of PRIME. I'm so happy to have worked with this company for the better part of five years now. Taking the time off before Colossus IV really gave me a chance to recharge and I was back in less than a year. I've been through my own trials and tribulations in my career and I have shared them all with numerous people in hopes that they don't make the same mistakes that I do. I have nearly lost my life on a few different occasions and don't want to see anyone else experience the same troubles. I was a lucky man to survive the ordeals I've been through and now it's time for me to settle down, to play back the memories in my mind of a career that I hope many will remember for the person behind all the glitz and the glamor. People need to know what really is behind the shade of these sunglasses I wear. I'm much like they all are deep down. I'm a loving and caring person and family man first and foremost. In waving goodbye to the world I have known for this past half a decade, I will have my family with me in the front row at Fenway Park. On top of that, I will have my new friends Diane, Kate and especially Christopher. It will be their first ever live PRIME event and I assured them it would not be their last. Unfortunately they couldn't afford to attend any events in the past with the stockpile of medical bills incurred. But all that changed when I received their letter from PRIME. Jenny and I were wed the day after I met Christopher in Boston. My family was there with us to share in that special moment. Many of my friends from PRIME were there as well, making the sacrifice to come witness our special day. Christopher and his family attended the service and the after part as well and it was really my pleasure to have them there to support us. An eight year old boy has made a lasting impression on me, one that I can hopefully take with me into retirement, my home life and into the years ahead of me. During one of the final events leading up to the jam packed Colossus weekend, I brought everyone out with me to the group fan events in and around the Fenway area. During the time I was signing autographs and introducing people to my new friend Christopher, Jenny leaned over to me and hugged me tightly, telling me that she wasn't sure when she left home why she was feeling a little under the weather. She recently found out why. She patted her stomach and my new wife let me know that soon, my beloved daughter Desiree would have a little brother or sister of her own. I couldn't have asked for a better way to leave this world I've been such a big part of. It was always a goal of mine to perform in front of thousands of people and now I get to wave goodbye in front of millions watching the world over. Win or lose my big retirement match with Tyler Nelson, how could anyone wish for anything more? I've never been more proud to be Killean Sirrajin.
