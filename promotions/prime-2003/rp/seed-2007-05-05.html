5 May 2007

Date: May 5th, 2007 Annabelle Summers found it difficult to sleep in moving vehicles. This, of course, made her trips to Japan into fun-filled doses of insomnia trips, from which she often has to be helped or carried out by her boyfriend. Some people who didn't know her often thought she was drunk, rather than tired, upon seeing such a scene. She often suffered no end of embarassment when having to explain why she's so tired from long trips, moreso than most people who just experience jet lag. It also wasn't making this late night rush to St. Louis any easier for her. The Delphinus, which was the name that the Codemaster gave for his "pimpin' like CJ's new ride", was custom built to ensure that the Blue Rogues would travel from locale to locale quickly, safely, and with the feeling that they were rock stars. In truth, it was a prop that PRIME ordered for another stable entirely that the Codemaster got his hands on and had repainted, but he wasn't about to actually tell anyone that he wouldn't leave Blaine alone until he broke down and let him have the bus that was about to get sent to a scrapyard. It was also the place where Annabelle was trying, and failing, to sleep at about one in the morning. They were halfway through the state of Kansas, and it looked fairly likely that they wouldn't reach it for another couple of hours. Annabelle, wide awake, laid in her small bed, looking up at the ceiling. Knowing she couldn't sleep, Annabelle simply laid there while the other members of the Blue Rogues, with the exception of Robert Falk, slept idly. Coral was sleeping in the bed above Annabelle, with the beds being too small to accomodate the two of them. The Codemaster slept at the bed by Anna's feet, with Scott Falk draped on the bed above him, his left arm and leg hanging loosely off of the bed and making it look like Scott was in danger of falling. Alexandria slept in the bottom bunk of the side to the right of where Annabelle laid, and she had mounted her newly won "Undisputed Amy Dumas Memorial Jizzbang Championship" on the foot of the bed like a mirror. Steve slept on the top bunk. Don't ask how he got there, I don't know either. The bottom bunk of the bed that was above Alexandria's head actually belonged to Robert Falk, but he wasn't actually there. As for the top bunk... Beef and Janito were sleeping while hugging one another, as there was only one other bed and neither were willing to risk a beatdown from a seven-foot-tall monster even if it meant not looking gay while they slept. Eventually, Annabelle got fed up and slid off of her bed, sitting up and landing on her bare feet. Brushing her waist-length hair out of her face and smoothing out the sleeveless brown dress she wore, Annabelle carefully walked towards the front of the bus. Behind the driver's seat, there existed a small table with a chair that wrapped around the corner that formed from the presence of the driver's seat and the wall of the bus. Across from this was a rack that contained clothing, with the majority of it belonging to Annabelle herself, and a television set that was currently turned off. At this point, as she approached the chair, she saw the man who was driving the bus, Robert Falk. Robert took an idle glance towards Annabelle, then resumed his gaze towards the road, "Couldn't sleep?" "No... I never can on these trips." Annabelle said, with a sigh. She sat with her back facing Robert's back, and placed her hands in her lap, before taking a glance back at Robert, concerned about the larger member of the Blue Rogues being the one taking this late night drive, "Are you sure you're okay, Mr. Falk?" Robert shrugged, "It's nothing." There was a pause, before Annabelle quietly notes, "You know, you don't... really... talk much." "My brother's an idiot, so I try to avoid making people think that I am as well." Robert explained, simply. He noted the mile marker that the bus just passed, and reasoned that he could get to St. Louis in a little over two hours if he stayed at his current speed. "He does seem... out of control." Annabelle observed, her voice maintaining its usual quiet as she pulled her long hair back so that she may place it in a ponytail. Robert took a glance back towards Annabelle, though all he could see were her left leg and part of her shoulder. He turned his attention back towards the road, "My brother's mouth is the sole reason why we never, before Allen brought us aboard, got a chance to leave the independents." "Huh?" Annabelle asked, as she finished tying a pretty bow from one of her white ribbon into her hair in order to complete her ponytail. She turned a glance in the direction she hoped Robert was, because if he wasn't, she'd have to wonder who was driving the bus, "What do you mean?" The much larger man sighed, looked up at the ceiling of the bus, and brought his gaze back towards the road, "Scott and I were trained by Daniel Phillips, who was known in this business of ours for a foul mouth, a bad temper, and this tendency to just insult anyone who even remotely annoys him. Scott tends to take after anyone with a strong personality, so when he met Daniel... he kinda adopted some of his more... 'colorful'... characteristics." Annabelle blinked. "You mean..." "Yeah. He really wasn't like that before." Robert said, with a sigh. He turned to Annabelle's direction for a second, before he turned right back to that road that never freaking changed because it was boring old Kansas, land of boring corn and even more boring roads. "So, why can't you sleep?" Robert asked of Annabelle. "I'm... I'm always afraid of sleeping in a moving vehicle..." she said, timidly. Robert Falk felt a disturbance in the Force. "Afraid? Did... something happen to you?" he asked. Annabelle realized at that moment that she probably said more than she meant to say, but knew she probably shouldn't hide it from a guy who always stayed quiet. "I... fell asleep in the back of my... parents' car." Annabelle said. She was quiet at first, and for a while, the only sound heard would be the engine of the bus as it steadilly continued down its night path. Finally, Annabelle made it through her next few words, "The night they died." Robert was his typically silent self, but this silence proved uncomfortable for the two of them. "I-I don't even remember... anything. One minute, I'm... I'm in the backseat of my car... the next, the crash happened and I got knocked out..." Annabelle said, with a whimper. "You were... in a car accident that you don't even remember?" Robert asked. "Y...yes." Annabelle said, "I, um... I woke up in a hospital two days later. They... um... they had to support my spinal cord with metal, and... well, let's... let's just say that that's why I set off metal detectors at airports even if I don't have anything... metallic... on me..." Robert heard a sniffle, which meant that this line of conversation had caused her to cry. Though he knew that Annabelle was a bit of a crybaby, it was the first time that he was the one who initated it. After he heard her sniffling quiet down a bit, Robert heard her continue. "After that... I never slept in a car or a plane or any form of transportation, ever again." Annabelle said, with a sniffle and a sigh. Robert paused, before thinking of the first thing that came off the top of his head, "All this time, I just thought you didn't sleep because of motion sickness." Annabelle, despite crying over recollecting what she knew of her parents' deaths, managed a giggle. She stood up and gave a quick bow to Robert, "Thank you... I feel a little better now." "Take it easy. You wouldn't want to worry Coral." Robert suggested. Annabelle nodded and returned to her bed. Though she felt something was a bit off when she returned to her bed, she thought nothing of it and soon tried, though still failed, to sleep. ... The Codemaster slowly and quiely walked over to Robert Falk and sat in the spot Annabelle had sat in a moment ago. Robert didn't even have to guess who was behind him now, "Allen, I'm not your psychiatrist. What do you want?" "Man, you're harsh," the Codemaster quietly said. He then took a glance back to the bedding area, where Annabelle had gone to sleep to see if she'd notice him, but since she didn't, the Codemaster simply turned back to Robert, "So, what you think?" "About?" "About Annabelle." the Codemaster said, in an almost prodding sort of manner. "She's sweet, but cries a lot." Robert said, simply. He took a quick glance back at where Codemaster sat, and quietly added, "What's your interest?" The Codemaster smirked a knowing smirk, "I think I like her." Robert Falk was normally the calm, rational type. He was also normally a good driver with a bus. As his brain processed this information, he very nearly missed a car in front of him that was abruptly braking, but quickly caught on and quickly moved to the thankfully free lane before he could hit the car. Once back in the original lane, Robert snapped his attention towards the Codemaster, "What?" "Hey, man. Eyes on the road." the Codemaster said, simply, the smirk having faded from his face due to Robert's somewhat reckless driving. Robert grumbled, and complied. Robert didn't want to talk so loud that anyone in the back could hear them. So, he whispered to the Codemaster, "You like Annabelle? The girl who is nearly impossible to separate from Coral? THAT Annabelle?" "Yup." the Codemaster said, with a newfound smirk, "She's cute, she's smart, and she's got that vulnerable side of her and could use a darin' black knight to rescue her from time to time." Now his smirk's become a full blown, wide, toothy smile, "She's perfect." Robert thought that the Codemaster was losing his damn mind. He knew, himself, that Annabelle and Coral were a perfect match for one another. The idea of Codemaster and Annabelle together seemed ludicrous to anyone else but the Codemaster, himself. "Personally, I think you're crazy." Robert said. He sighed, but knew that he was also a curious individual, and he had to ask, "And how, exactly, are you gonna win her over?" "With my indisputable charm and good looks." the Codemaster said. Robert hated his curious side. "Naw, man," the Codemaster said, "If I had a plan, I ain't gonna babble it off to ya. That'd be a mistake. By the time you recognize it, she'd already be mine." "What makes you think I won't just tell Coral?" Robert asked. The Codemaster laughed, and started to walk away. However, while still out of earshot from everyone else, the Codemaster stopped and turned back towards Robert. He spoke lowly and almost with a sinister tone. "Because you owe me."
