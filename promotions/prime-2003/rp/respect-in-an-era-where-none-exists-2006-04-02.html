Respect in an era where none exists

2 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 2, 2006 ### Disclaimer: A lot of the earlier bits are mainly for background and shows Neil and Gideon off camera as well. By separating things into televised and non televised I’m going to be using the camera as a 3rd person to be introduced to the scenes when relevant. Neil Whittaker and Neil Fracas act similar but are different in many ways while Gideon will consistently be a ruthless asshole. ### It is several weeks ago. The Alliance has been planning their return to pro wrestling and the day of their return was getting closer. They haven’t been very active in a long time. The last time the Alliance were in the ring as a team was October 6, 2003. Neil hasn’t been in the ring since February 9, 2004. Even Gideon hasn’t been wrestling since NEW’s Catastrophe PPV September 5, 2004. Needless to say there has been a lot of changes since those days so long ago. Wrestling has been dying and their love of wrestling has been on life support for years. Yet more friends passed away. The most recent passing was Eddie Guerrero and to see the bastardization of his life turned into a cheap pop for sports entertainment infuriates both Gideon and Neil greatly. To say they are unimpressed is putting it lightly. There are some places you just don’t go and that is one of those places. Neil had to cripple a man in the ring because he used the passing of his friend and mentor Rick Rood (Rude) for cheap heat. Neil was never punished for ending the career of Tru Soldier. It was understood that a line had been crossed and there had to be consequences. When you help bury a man who was a friend to you. When you see his family there. When you look at dirt being piled on his casket. When you know that in the end it will be all for nothing. There is not going to be a best of Rick Rude DVD. There is not going to be more then a passing ring of the bell. The man helped paved the way for the wrestlers of today. Now he is forgotten like the wrestling he represented. Things like that make people like Neil and Gideon very angry. It can cause many sleepless nights like tonight……… Neil wakes suddenly. His heart racing and he looks around the room. He is home and Stephanie is sound asleep beside him. He starts calming down as he realizes that it is all a dream. He is looking up at the ceiling and he is thinking. Why in god’s name am I going back to wrestling? He has everything he could possibly want. Stephanie is one of the hottest women in the world and they love each other. They have fought together in the ring and outside the ring in many a bar. They could out drink damn near anyone. They have a son and he is going to be turning 4 soon. Stephanie wakes up and notices that Neil is staring at the ceiling. Stephanie: What’s wrong? Neil: Too many things going through my mind. Sorry if I woke you. Stephanie: I wasn’t sleeping myself. What’s bothering you Neil? Neil: I don’t know……… Stephanie: You are wondering why you are going back aren’t you? Neil: …………… Stephanie: Everything will be fine Neil. I’ll be taking care of Ricky. Bobby and Tony are here if I need help. You, Gideon, and Liz will be no more then a phone call away. I know you aren’t worried about Ricky. What’s wrong? Neil looks into her eyes but can’t find the words. She is looking up at him and understands what is wrong but she can’t tell Neil why he is so bothered by going back to wrestling. She is waiting for him to say something but there is only an awkward silence. Stephanie: You feel it’s wrong that the 3 of us aren’t going to be kicking ass side by side. Neil: …………… Stephanie: And more then that you are worried your rage is going to cause you to permanently cripple someone. Neil: It just doesn’t feel right. I feel like a total asshole because I’m letting myself get buried in all the backstabbing and bullshit when I should be here with you taking care of Ricky. It wasn’t all that long ago that I was at another friend’s funeral. I haven’t watched wrestling in years because I’m disgusted by this sports entertainment crap. Stephanie: And you know the first one of these sports entertainers you face you are going to want to break him in half with a Fracasplex. Neil: Yup……… Stephanie: Don’t worry so much. From what Gideon told me Motoki and Tyler won’t even care. Neil: And that bothers me too. As much as I hate these bastards for ruining Wrestling I think about what I’d do to their families. Stephanie: If they get hurt in the ring it is NOT your fault Neil. You are a pro in the ring. If they can’t survive a move they should be working at McDonalds not being in the ring. Have you ever thought of what these guys can do when they start botching moves? Neil: Yeah. That pisses me off. No one is safe with these idiots in the ring. It’s like giving a goddamn monkey the keys to a Truck and hoping for the best. Stephanie: You are thinking too much. You will be fine. (Chuckling) I know how to cheer you up……… Stephanie starts kissing Neil’s neck and throws a leg over his hips and lies on his chest. She kisses his forehead as they are both smiling. Her chest is pressed against Neil’s and she slowly slides down his huge frame. She stops and they look into each other’s eyes as a throaty gasp comes from Stephanie as she continues sliding his body until they are finally hip to hip. They are giggling as they share a passionate kiss. The bed creaks from the strain of their movements as they both enjoy a good night’s rest. Gideon couldn’t sleep that night either. The first rays of sunlight enter the window of Gideon’s mansion in Osaka, Japan. He’s drinking a glass of orange juice and reading the paper. The contracts that Motoki sent him for himself and Neil are on the table in a folder. He has looked them over several times and everything is in order. There is nothing out of the ordinary and even a few loopholes left in as a “courtesy” for possible “favors” in the future. The mansion is pretty much empty. Neil isn’t due in Japan for a few days before the PPV. Catherine decided to visit Liz and her grand daughter while he is in Japan. Gideon reads over the contracts once again. He must have read them a dozen times and even had his legal staff go over them for 3 days. His copy of the contracts has the loopholes highlighted. Time to make a few phone calls. Gideon grabs his cell phone from his jacket pocket and dials a number. Gideon: Travis how goes the overhaul of www.NeilFracas.com and www.GideonFontaine.com ? Travis: It has gone great so far the Interviews have been uploaded to the downloads section. Your new theme music Step Up by Drowning Pool has been cross linked to the Drowning Pool website and the webmaster has been cross referencing it as well to the Gideon Fontaine homepage. Gideon: Are the forums up and running now? Travis: Yes there has been quite a stir lately about you being in Japan. Are you still using the SN “PureWrestlingG”? Gideon: Yes I have a few others when I feel like stirring the pot so to speak. Travis: There has been a problem getting some of the match footage for the moves archives. Gideon: No big deal there. Anyone that wants any match footage just has to google it and use any number of file sharing programs. Travis: Good point. Any new moves that you are planning to add to the archives? Gideon: Neil has 3. The Snap Dragon Suplex, The Snap Dragon Fracasplex and Welcome to New York. I added a few myself. The Greetings from Osaka, The Best Kick Ever, and Welcome to the Big Leagues. Travis: I thought you were pulling my leg with those last two. Gideon: (Snickering) Truthfully I am. The person I’m facing would have to be pretty damn stupid and slow to get nailed by either one. Still might as well list them on the site and have people thinking they MAY come. Greetings from L.A. is actually serious as well. The Just another body on our highlight reel move is pretty much fluff. Travis: Anything else you want done? Gideon: Talk to the graphics guys about some alternate banners. I may be going with the Osaka Assassin name after my debut. Travis: We will get 12 done and send you the prototypes via E-Mail. Gideon: Good work Travis. I’ve got a lot more work to do let me know if there is anything that needs to be done. Travis: Yup. After Gideon hangs up the phone he stands up. He walks towards the front door deciding what to do. He decides to head to the Shooto Dojo in uptown Osaka and see what is going on. It’s great to be home again. Mabye he will get to do a little sparring and sharpen up his takedowns and submissions. More then likely he will just say hi to old friends and enjoy his free time before he gets mired down in the pro wrestling cesspool again. Now it is the present. The Alliance has made their presence felt.The Alliance has debuted.The Alliance has put all of PRIME on notice.The Alliance now has to prove themselves again. Neil and Gideon are at Gideon’s office at Fontaine Enterprises in L.A. plotting strategy for their upcoming match as well as plans on how to cut their promo this week. Some people just go to the ring and talk a little crap about their opponent and call it a promo. Others like Gideon wield the spoken word like a surgeon’s scalpel and cut to the very core of their opponents. Then there is Neil. He wields the spoken word with absolutely no subtlety much like a barbarian wields his axe. They have looked over the tapes of Seek and Destroy. Now comes the most important thing. How are they going to humiliate Seek and Destroy both in the ring and before the match even begins. Neil: I don’t like these punks. They are the worst type of sports entertainers. They hunt in a pack because they don’t have the skills or testicles to go one on one with an opponent. Looking at the tapes I’d have to say that Jason Bass is the weak link of the team. Gideon: Agreed. So standard isolation and take him out of the equation very quick and dbl team Eric Evans? Neil: Doesn’t really matter. Evans isn’t much better. They have as much a chance of becoming a wrestler as Carrot Top has of getting an Oscar. As long as we aren’t sloppy shouldn’t be that hard to either over power them or outwrestle them Gideon: You remember when I did the guitar bit in Core? Neil: Still trying to forget that one Gideon. That shit was awful. Gideon: I was thinking that should be our focus for a promo this week. Use a guitar and basically show them how easy it is to do what they do. Well more accurately what they USED to do before they got into the legal problems that keep them from performing. Neil: What did the idiots do? Gideon: Basically behaved like complete jackasses all through their career. Though the lawsuit by Metallica amused me a lot. Neil: Metallica sue someone. Go figure. Can’t figure out why the reason Devane liked St Anger. Gideon: I think anytime you have to have a concert in a prison to get a large crowd that should be a sign that you might have lost something over the years. If they put as much effort into their music as they have suing people they wouldn’t have went from stars to self absorbed shit heads living in the past. Neil: We going to try and seriously hurt these idiots? Gideon: If the opportunity presents itself accidents happen. Neil: I was having some problems with that until Steph said if they can’t survive a move they should be working at McDonalds not being in the ring. Gideon starts laughing his head off. Neil: I know that laugh. Spill it. Gideon: That just gave me a hell of an idea. I’ll explain on the way to the studio. I have to get Amanda to make the arrangements while we cut a promo. This will be classic Neil. The Alliance leaves the office and makes their way to the studios where they are going to do a promo for the upcoming Revolution show. Gideon starts making arrangements on the way and as soon as Neil finds out what Gideon thought up he starts laughing his head off. It is a short ride to the studios and the camera men are there waiting. Neil grabs the guitar that has been prepared as they go into studio 5. As the cameras set up Gideon and Neil sit on the couch and wait for the signal to start. Neil tests the guitar strings for pitch. They are given a countdown 5… 4… 3… 2… 1………… Gideon: Welcome PRIME fans to your slice of reality and your bastion of truth in the sea of lies we call Sports Entertainment. We are here to set the record straight and shed some lights on a couple of pretenders and posers in both the sport of Wrestling and the world of Entertainment. They are of course Seek and Destroy. They are two men that are half assed in both wrestling and entertainment. Neil: We understand you guys are musicians. We’d have baked you a cake or got you a card congratulating you but we don’t give a damn. Isn’t like playing a guitar is any big thing. I did it in University to bang chicks. Neil starts playing a few chords from one of their songs “Event Horizontal Mambo”. Gideon is snickering a tad at Neil’s choice of one of Seek and Destroys songs. Gideon: You see Neil is able to play the guitar and has played it for several years. It isn’t something he regularly does because lets face it most people eventually grow up and start learning about responsibility. Of course there are people with talent that can make a living out of music. Then there are people like yourselves who merely see it as an easy way to get cash and fame. Neil: I’m a bit rusty and I missed a few of the chords. Know what I don’t care. Here is the guitar Gideon. Neil passes the guitar to Gideon and he smiles as he starts playing Amazing Grace. It is amazingly well done considering Gideon only learned it for a segment over 2 years ago. Neil: How much does it annoy you two idiots that you aren’t anything special? Nothing you two punks have done or will ever do will amount to anything because you two losers are always a couple punks that look for the easy way out of everything you can and try and slide by. Gideon: It became a tad personal to us when you decided to come into our backyard. Naturally we have to go to your backyard. I play the guitar and Neil plays the guitar. To call us musicians is as ludicrous as anyone calling you wrestlers. Anyone with a little skill can fake being able to do something like you two do every time you step in the ring. Neil: Your biggest claim to fame is attacking Balthasar. Gideon: It took an electric cattle prod. It took chains. It took handcuffs. It took Angelus. It took 'Electric' Eric Evans. It took 'Jammin' Jason Bass. It took 'Bangin' Buddy Samson. It took 'Keys' aka Jeremy Hunglo. My god you guys are good. You fought a Herculean battle against the forces of evil. My god the intestinal fortitude required to stand against such incredible insurmountable danger and still prevail. Neil: And you wonder why you punks don’t get any respect. Playing a guitar doesn’t mean shit in wrestling and attacking someone 5 on 1 with weapons isn’t anything more then a punk ass bitch move. If you want respect you have to do something to earn it. Gideon: Sure you can break kayfabe all you want and try and be edgy and controversial. Rappers do it all the time and quite frankly they do it a lot better. You say you aren’t wrestlers and we are very aware of that. Everything you do in PRIME proves that. Unfortunately for you that is a very real problem. Let me explain what we are and what we do in the ring. We are men that can fight for real. Neil is an ex bounty hunter and has a No holds barred record of 76 wins, 3 losses, 33 wins by knockout. My skills in the Martial Arts world are well known. My WKBA record is 43 wins, 1 loss, 39 wins by knockout and a record of 94 wins, 0 losses, 81 wins by knockout in No Holds barred fights. We are two of the most driven and ruthless men in wrestling today. We are on a crusade to rid professional wrestling of Sports Entertainers. Neil: I get an opportunity I’m going to seriously hurt you. Ain’t making any pretty speeches to sell T-Shirts. I get a hold of one of you and I’ll either try and break you in half with a Fracasplex or I’ll try and break your bones with joint locks. Gideon: I’ll be working on your limbs. There are over 100 joint locks that can permanently cripple a man in less then 2 seconds. One quick twist of the arm and an elbow can be ripped out of its socket or an ankle snapped. If I get the opportunity I may try and end things with the Greetings from Osaka. Have you ever thought about what it would be like to spend the rest of your life paralyzed unable to clean yourself relying on a person to keep you alive in a life that is worse then death will ever be? Neil: Every match you idiots have in the ring someone can get hurt. Gideon: We are the guardians of the gates. We are not going to coddle you like children like you have been coddled throughout your life. If an……… how did we put it Neil? Neil: Accident. Gideon: Ah yes. Great word. If an “Accident” happens in the ring just think of the buy rates for PRIME. We will be booked as the most dangerous tag team in professional wrestling. I am sure that we will be fined by both Tyler and Motoki because of our reckless actions and they will be totally appalled by our recklessness. That is until there is need of our version of wrestling Karma to visit someone they no longer want in PRIME. Neil: Before we cut this off we want you thinking about this match. Will we use power. Will we make you look bad wrestling. Will we try and injure you with submissions. This one’s my favorite by the way. Will we just simply beat the living F$%k out of you two punks and make you bleed. Gideon: Since this is our first match I think we may go that extra effort and try and do all 4. After all we do enjoy putting on a show. With those words the cameramen give Neil and Gideon the signal and the cameras stop filming. Neil and Gideon are smiling. The cameramen are getting things put away and preparing the tape to be shipped to PRIME. Gideon checks his text messages on the cell phone and sees that everything is a go. He stops the cameramen from packing everything and updates them that they have some bonus footage to go into the promo. They are about to take a small road trip to a local McDonalds that has been closed to the public. They are getting set up to film and Gideon puts on a McDonalds hat. He takes off his Armani jacket and puts on a nametag there are 3 of them taped together saying “Jasonito Claudio Miguel Raul Santiago Curtis”. Jason Bass would have been a hell of a lot shorter but nowhere near as fun. Gideon looks at the predetermined customers waiting and smirks. Neil goes behind the counter. The camera crew is giving them a countdown. 5… 4… 3… 2… 1………… Gideon: To show we aren’t totally heartless we will give Jason and Eric some advice on a new career choice more suited to their skills and intelligence. A lady comes up to the counter and asks for a coffee. Gideon gets a coffee. He puts it in 4 cups and dumps a scoop of ice cubes in it. She looks at Gideon in confusion. Gideon: Maam coffee is hot. Since you look somewhat stupid I have put ice cubes in it and have even put it in 4 cups. I must request that you sign a waiver verifying that I have told you that coffee is hot just in case you are not smart enough to know that. I have taken the precautions to make sure the coffee is not hot just in case you are that stupid as not to grasp the coffee is hot statement. And before I forget do you want fries with that. She walks off in a huff while Gideon smiles. The next man avoids Gideon’s register. Neil ignores him and gets a large coke. As he starts drinking it he notices the guy is still standing in front of him. Neil: I’m assuming you want something. Let’s make it easy and I’ll just tell you now I don’t care. Neil goes to the back of the kitchen. The man steps up to Gideon’s register and asks for a Quarter Pounder. Gideon: Neil can you get me a Quarter Pounder please? Neil: You ain’t going to believe this Gideon. Step back here a minute. Gideon: Excuse me. Gideon goes to the back of the kitchen. He comes from the back and shudders as Neil shakes his head. Gideon: May I recommend a salad? It appears that there are certain…… problems that make me consider that you do not want a Quarter Pounder or anything beef related. The man looks at Gideon and then walks off in a mixture of confusion and disbelief. Gideon: I have to admit working at McDonalds seems a bit more difficult then I thought but I am fairly certain that no one in their right mind will allow you to actually handle food. With a cash register that does all your counting for you even basic math skills are not required. We have to teach you both a few words to recognize then you will be prepared for yet another profession to half ass and eventually fail at. Neil: In case it hasn’t sunk in yet we haven’t even got enough respect to have contempt for you. Gideon: Remember the magic words “Do you want fries with that” and be all that you deserve to be. That gentlemen is the end of your 15 minutes in 5… 4… 3… 2… 1… The camera cuts on 1 and the Cameramen start putting their gear away. The Alliance has cut their first promo in nearly 2 years. Now all that is left is to send it into PRIME before their match on Revolution 91.
