You ever hear that Ancient Chinese Proverb about the 'Man Who Wear Vagina Plug On Head'?

Date: a damn good one One man chuckles to another, wasting away their time on the graveyard shift with petty observation. He remarks something about how strange it is that he can see his breath, disregarding that it is, in fact, November already. Posted right outside the double doors leading into a set of barracks, two guards of the People's Liberation Army of China stand looking rather bored. Of course they're fucking bored: they're infantry privates whose duty for the next three hours consists of standing around and sticking thumbs up ones' ass. '您聽見了什麼約翰Kerry 認為?' One guard says to the other while overlooking the dark open area in front of the building they guard. '他說 '如果您做一個工作成績聰明, 您將做很好。如果不是, 您去伊拉克'.' While his partner is alert--stiff from the ball of his feet to the tip of his spine--the other guard leans content against the wall with a cigarette. '如此?' ''如此?' 您意味什麼, 如此? John Kerry 認為軍人是為人沒有遠期!' Says the other man, slightly worked over how calm the other remains. '他正確。所以我連接了。' The man defends his position by simply smoking his cigarette. '他觸犯了整個武力!' The man attempting to argue sighs. '您知道美国人不应该甚而是在伊拉克，无论如何。' Cigarette smoker coughs. '什么。我生气在什么Rush Limbaugh 认为 Michael J Fox。' Before the smoker can even flick the end of his cigarette, he and his partner are taken completely off-guard as an unknown figure drops behind them from the Barracks' rooftop. 'DIE, COCK BITCH BRAIN FUCKS!' screams the assailant as he plummets to the ground. Before the smoker or his partner can process what is happening, much less that an intruder has made their presence known, the Assailant donned in skin-tight black fatigues wraps his hands around both men's necks and twists them to the point of breakage. The guards fall, lifeless, never to become entangled in petty argument over trivial political* events. *dept. of redundancy dept. The man does a few ninja poses over the bodies, karate chopping the air a few times. He then pulls his face-mask down a bit so he can speak clearly. 'So that was good, Facey? I did alright?' Chet Worth asks, looking up at no one in particular. 'Yeah, except you were supposed to say 'Cock Brain Bitch Fucks,' but who's keeping score? By the way, you need to kill three more people to pass the test.' The Illustrious Face-Eater answers from no-where in particular. That's what not-existing can do for you. 'I don't think I can kill three more people, dude. I've got, like, a conscience and stuff.' 'YOU'LL KILL WHOEVER THE HELL I POSSESS YOU TO KILL IF YOU WANT YOUR GODDAMN DOUBLE DECKERS AND NACHOS SUPREME W/O SOUR CREAM!' The mere thought of Taco Bell makes Chet's stomach cringe. 'Alright, Fine, no Taco Bell, but dude, I'm telling you! It's not that big a deal! Remember? YOU're not even killing anyone! I am! You're, like, a vessel or some hippy shit like that.' 'WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT, I'M 'NOT KILLING ANYONE'? I JUST BROKE TWO PEOPLE'S NECKS! I WATCHED MY HANDS TWIST SOMEONE'S FUCKING HEAD OFF! HOW AM I NOT KILLING ANYONE?! Plus, I'm starting to wonder how legit this whole 'possession' business really is. Especially when it seems like you have to talk me into everything...' A pound of tightly saran-wrapped brick, dirt, MEXICAN weed flies across the open area and hits Chet Worth directly in the nutsack. He keels over. 'IS THAT CONVINCING ENOUGH FOR YOU, CHET?! LET'S GET ONE THING STRAIGHT, I DO THE VOICE RAISING AROUND HERE! I'M THE ONE THAT MAKES THE ORDERS! YOU DO AS I TELL YOU AND YOU DON'T HAVE A FUCKING CHOICE EITHER WAY ABOUT IT, GOT THAT?! NOW.... MAY I PLEASE HAVE A UNIVERSAL TITLE SHOT?!?' Just then, a bunch of Chinese Guards come to inspect the commotion. Immediately, they begin opening fire with their machine guns on the CEO of PRIME. '杀害NINJA!' 'Fuck! Hurry, Chet, inside Sun Tzu's heavily guarded Fortress of Fornication!' Chet seems hesitant, staring at the surplus of chronic on the ground with somewhat lusty eyes. 'But what about the weed?' 'Forget the weed! It's bum-schwag! Hoyt Williams' abundance in back-hair is more valuable than that shit.' Chet leaves the bag and runs inside, bullets whizzing by with every inch he moves. He slams the door shut, locks it, and collapses on the other side. Catching his breath with the few minutes he has, the Worth is breathing too hard to take in his surroundings. 'HOLY SHIT!' Facey screams. 'IT'S A SEX-GODDESS' PALACE!' Chet stands up and soaks it in; they're standing in very bare and cold looking Army Barracks with nothing out of the ordinary about them. 'There's nothing sexually god-like about this at all!' 'Use your imagination, Chet! After all, this is the place where Sun Tzu sleeps! Now look for her panty drawer. Chandler Tsonda once told me 'you've never lived unless you've smelt a China-woman's panties' so if you're going to bring me back to life, you got to find those panties!' Loud bangs from the door outside are heard as various people try to get through. Chet quickly halts their efforts by shoving a few cots and chests in front of the doorway, obstructing it from opening. He then goes to opening various people's personal lockers, searching through their belongings in hopes of coming across a pair of Sun Tzu's panties. However, all the clothing seems to be military-fashioned and none of it looking like it belongs to a woman, so Chet gets frustrated. 'Dude, I don't think there's--' 'DUDE DUDE DUDE! MY TOTALLY OMNISCIENT GHOST ASS JUST GOT A PREMONITION ABOUT SOME FREAKY SHIT---GO TO THE BACK.' Questioning himself and the voice that guides him, Chet reluctantly heads to the back of the Barracks where there is a sectioned-off, private quarters. The door is locked, but Chet is fat so he can break stuff easily and this door is no exception. He busts in, and immediately is taken back by the room. It's lavish. It's extravagant. It's very snazzy, to say the least. The golden decor scheme, the elaborate carvings on all the wood furniture, and the gigantically huge bed that sits in the center. Neither of them realize that they are in the private quarters of one General Chang of the People's Liberation Army, but if they did know I doubt they'd care either way. 'This is it?' Chet asks. 'Totally. PANTIES! NOW!' The Worth hurriedly rushed through the drawers, the impending army forces outside still trying to break their way in, and he withdraws a rather large piece of fabric. In the dresser, he holds up a size 50w pair of panties, stretching as wide as his wingspan. 'Um, Facey? Are you sure this is Sun Tzu's room?' But he receives no answer. 'Facey?' 'Dude, Nightstand, What, The, Fuck, Is, THAT?!?!?' Chet looks, and what he sees is a large, what-looks-like one of those rubber cap-poppers that you turn inside out, and then set them on a flat surface and they pop up. You know what I'm talking about, you pay, like, five tickets at Peter Piper for this cheap ass toy. Well, this object looks like one of those. Only it's the size of Chet's head. 'Is that what I think it is?' Chet asks. 'IT IS, CHET! IT'S A MAGICAL HAT! NOW STEAL IT, WEAR IT!' 'I don't think that's a hat--.' 'GOD DAMMIT IF YOU DON'T PUT THAT MOTHER FUCKING HAT ON YOUR HEAD I WILL SHOVE YOU IN AMY CAMPBELL'S VAGINA--DO YOU WANT THAT?!?' Chet yelps like a girl and puts the 'hat' on his head. As he dances around haphazardly, the doors leading to the barracks swing open, and in walks General Chang being flanked by fifteen guards armed to the teeth. '什么是意思--? AMERICANS?!?' Chet immediately turns around, realizing how much trouble he's in. 'WHAT DO YOU THINK YOU ARE DOING WEARING MY WIFE'S DIAPHRAGM ON YOUR HEAD LIKE A SILLY HAT?!?' Chet rips the 'hat' off and gives it a glance-over, then back to the China folk, then back to the bed where he took it from. Upon closer inspection, he realizes that the Bed is no 'bed' at all, but a massively fat Chinese woman who is resting on her stomach. Apparently, this is General Chang's wife. And Chet Worth is wearing her vagina-plug like a hat. 'Listen, I can totally explain!' 'KIRR DE AMERICANS!' The Chinese people start screaming in unison, like it's their crazy war-cry, until they see three pounds of dirt weed fly from out of no-where, landing in front of them. 'CHET, GET BACK!' Facey orders him. All of the sudden, a bottle rocket flies and collides with the weed, exploding, and instantly sending the room into a smoke filled haze. 'NOW, HURRY! WE MUST ESCAPE!' The guards begin opening fire on Chet, but the smoke is too thick to get a steady aim. Not to mention, they're hand/eye coordination is probably a bit off, now. One soldier screams, '我无法呼吸!' Another yells '我突然想要听Raggae 音乐!' Chet manages to slide out of the barracks, undetected, and back into the cloak of the night for his speedy escape. While flying the jetpack back overseas towards the next event PRIME held, Chet bitches at Facey about 'Sun Tzu not living in China.' 'Well, it's not like the ten-billion people that look like her wouldn't be suitable.' Good point, Facey. Good point.
