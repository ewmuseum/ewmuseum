Into The Abyss: Part V - Creeping Death

9 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-02-09 His heart was breaking as she glanced up at him and for a moment, as a twinkle glinted from somewhere in the back of those grey dilated pupils, he almost considered keeping his mouth shut and giving up. His choice was clear, and the thought of festering away like an open wound in this hell hole overpowered his desire for Gloria. There was only one thing he could think of that would give him a chance of freedom and unfortunately the solution was kneeling in front of him, hand feeding him a bacon, lettuce and tomato sandwich. His taste buds popped with flavours he hadn't experienced in so long. This simple meal that anyone else would take for granted felt like Mana from heaven and he savoured every last crumb as saliva flooded his mouth and his heart beat that little bit faster every time Gloria raised her hand to his mouth for him to take a drink from a bottle of mineral water. At first the sandwich soured his mouth with a distinct taste like what he imagined heroin to taste like, and he thought that perhaps the amount of drugs he had been intravenously force-fed was to blame for the marred sense of taste. Little did he know it was creeping death infesting his body and taking over, filling his every physical and mental notion with a little friendly nudge that said, "Hey...you look like you could use a little pick-me-up." and pretty soon he would be powerless to stop it. The more he ate the more he thought about Gloria, about his decision. Gradually with every chew he wished the forthcoming swallow would choke him dead as a soft smile grew on her lips, covered in little flaps of broken skin where she had been nervously biting as the itch grew with every minute that she wasn't high. He had studied her like a book and as disgusting as anyone else would find her something made his heart flutter when she walked into the room. Perhaps it was because she was the only female he had been around in months, or perhaps it was simply the product of narcotic lust. When Reuben had told her of his intention to wear his captive down until he was nothing more than a husk of a man, she remembered the look Von Kelsig had given her when she first met him. Her instructions were get in, bandage him up, then get the fuck out. A little brown bag awaited her completion of the task and she carried it out as precisely as Reuben's threat of harm against her had been, thinking nothing of him at the time. It was only later when she was strung out that her mind wandered back to the room where the mysterious giant lay captive. Her addiction was much deeper than his and was very much a part of her, but an urge that wasn't driven by her craving drew her to those dark eyes of his. Something about him had struck her as intriguing, he was much bigger than any of Reuben's previous "clients", and there was a gentle sorrow in his eyes when he didn't furrow his brow and grimace like an angry Silverback. She knew by his unwillingness to succumb readily to the dope that he was much stronger in mind as well as body than those unfortunate victims before him. He was new and different and it was this unusual break in her routine of shooting up and carrying out menial tasks for Blanco that had given her cause to reflect on him. They weren't falling for each other and the sexual desire that arose every so often was down to the creeping death that slithered through their veins. The sickness that sank its hooks into every nook and cranny of their being blinded them with urges and desires. These would ultimately turn from sex and love into an appetence for more and more heroin, leaving them with a desperate hunger that could never be quelled. That's how it worked and nothing could prevent it, they would be as the living dead...but then there was that impulse that was gently throbbing way down deep in their souls. It was hope. A tiny ember glowing down in the dark recesses of their mind, feeding off of their basic virtuousity to repel the onslaught of addiction. They didn't know it and it was different for both of them but it was hope that quietly burned away in the background. They needed each other in different ways, and as powerful as the poison chemicals in their systems were, something good and natural wanted to make them fight it. He finished the sandwich and took another slurp of water, working his tongue around his teeth to dislodge any remaining crumbs. Gloria capped the water and placed both the plate and the bottle by his feet then rose to hers, ready to take her leave again before he spoke out. "Wait..." He implored softly, trying not to sound too desperate. "I want to talk to you..." He felt his palms becoming clammy as she cocked her head and took a step back towards him. "I-...Reuben doesn't want me hanging around you... " She began. "Then why are you here?" Von Kelsig asked softly. "He told me he was keeping you here for a while longer, and he left about a half hour ago so I thought you might want something to eat." She scratched her arm and bit her lip again as Von Kelsig looked her over. "Thank you." He muttered, staring at the ground. "I need help Gloria. I'm not supposed to be here, I don't want to be here and I don't want to end up like-..." He paused suddenly, looking her in the eyes he felt a slight shame at what he was about to say. "Like what?" Gloria interjected. "Like a fuckin' junkie, like me?" The way she shuffled on her feet suggested she had taken more offence than Von Kelsig had thought and dropping his head he closed his eyes tight in the hope she wouldn't storm off. "No, I just don't want to suffer like you any more. I want my life back, and that fat bastard had no right to take it away from me!" He wanted to shout, but for fear of being heard he forced the air out of his lungs in a raspy whisper. Gloria hung her head and dropped to her knees at his feet, sliding down to the floor and sitting with her back against his leg. Wisps of hair tickled his fingertips and though they had been badly burned a light sensation passed through them. Picking at her own nails, Gloria sighed and produced a pack of cigarettes from the pocket of her leather jacket, lighting up as Von Kelsig took in the smell of tobacco. Something else filled his lungs with a new pleasant aroma, and taking another sniff he realized her hair was scented of lavender. The damp stuffy room had a stale smell to it with a hint of pork, of burned flesh, and even the cigarette smoke lingering around his face on its own had a refreshing effect on him. "I had a life too, before Reuben took it all away. I was married and had a nice place out in the suburbs. My husband was executive director in a company that manufactured guardrails and barriers. I don't know how the hell he got involved with Reuben, but one night his men came and took us both away from our home. I ended up in this room just like you......I had no idea what had happened to my husband." She didn't flinch at the mention of her former life, merely staring into space as Von Kelsig watched her. "Anyway, Reuben pumped me full of skag and forced me to sign over the deed to our house and pay him the insurance money I got after it turned out my husband's body had been found in a burned out wreck in St. Aubin Park. After that he made me work for him, and I had to do whatever he asked so I could get my fix." She sighed and took a long drag of her cigarette. "How long ago was that? Von Kelsig asked. "Over a year ago." Gloria replied, glancing up at him. He tensed up and clenched his fists. With all his strength he pulled his arms up, digging the soft flesh of his forearms into the cable ties that bound him to the chair. He wasn't trying to break free, rather using the pain as an outlet for the frustration that was building in him. It wouldn't have bothered him so much if she had gotten into trouble herself but for Reuben to punish her, an innocent girl in place of her husband made him angry. They were both innocent, both made to pay for someone else's mistakes, and it made him furious. The skin began to break on his arms as he forced the cable ties deeper into the skin. "Don't get worked up about it. It doesn't do you any good, just accept it and give in like I did. Like everyone does." Gloria reached up and clasped her fingers around Von Kelsig's fist, pulling gently on it as he drew blood. He stopped at the feeling of her hand on his, and slowly rested his arms. "Didn't you ever try to get away from here?" The pain pulsed through the cracks in his skin as they seeped blood and he took a deep breath as he asked, his palms becoming increasingly sweaty as Gloria stroked her thumb over his fingers. "No point. I was never involved in this kind of world, I was just a simple girl with a simple life. Reuben took my I.D., my credit cards...if I left I'd have nothing because he would find me anyway." She replied, stubbing out the cigarette and flicking it away into a dark corner of the room. "But you could have told someone and gotten help. Hell, you could have gone to the police!" Von Kelsig said, louder and more stern this time. "You don't know much about guys like Reuben do you, honey?" Gloria looked up at him again, drawing her hand away from his. "This isn't like his headquarters, he can disappear in under half an hour with no way to trace him. It's happened before, and I saw what happened to the guy that ratted him out. Plus, Reuben has contacts in the police department so he gets advanced warning of anything going down. This is just where he does business when he's in the area." "So you know where we are? Who hangs around this place? How to get out, if you wanted to?" Von Kelsig asked carefully, a straight look on his face as Gloria judged his eyes, trying to figure him out. "Yeah, I guess. But there's no way in hell I'm leaving...no way..." She shook her head and rose to her feet, patting her skinny jeans down and scratching her arm again. "Gloria..." He spoke with authority now as she started to twitch, an uncertain frightened look in her eyes. The creeping death was telling her to evacuate and find solace in one of her little brown bags. "What if I could help you?" He sat forward and held a stare now, burning a hole through her darting eyes as she turned to the door. Creeping death was telling her to get out before Reuben came back, before he hurt her again. The hunger was becoming strong again. "What if I could get us both out of here?" The proposition stopped her dead in her tracks. Her heart was racing like that of a deer in the headlights. Creeping death was offering a way out. She tried to calm herself down taking a long slow breath while Von Kelsig shifted in his seat, a few beads of sweat appearing on his forehead as he waited for an answer or a reaction. Gloria ran a hand through her wiry peroxide blonde hair and bit her lip again, turning from Von Kelsig to the door and after a moment turning back again. Her mind was conflicted, as the little ember of hope struggled against a wave of poison trying to extinguish it. She glanced at her hands and noticed they were shaking now, a sign that her addiction beckoned her to the stinging kiss of a needle. Shortly her stomach would twist and turn and she would have to inject to stop the pain. The thought of more pain made her want to turn away and retreat to the warm embrace of the drugs, but that would mean turning Von Kelsig down. The glimmer of hope that filled him like an aura attracted her and as strong as the ache for heroin was, it couldn't fully dispel the desire to reach out to this giant, to this saviour. To say she was between a rock and a hard place was an understatement as the severity of her decision weighed heavily upon her. "Why me? Why not save yourself?" Gloria whispered softly. "Because..." Von Kelsig began, his tone as soft as hers. "I believe yours is a life worth saving, Gloria...". She didn't know if it was his smooth baritone voice or the very words he had uttered, but she trembled as he spoke. The drugs has numbed her to her troubled past but the thought that someone cared enough to want to save her made her well up. Rubbing her eyes she looked upon Von Kelsig once more and saw that his face was straight and stern. The determination in his eyes coupled with the intensity emanating from his hulking frame put him in a new light, he was no longer weakened by his torture or weeping as pain flooded his body. He was a caged animal, and had the pent-up aggression of a monster on a leash. All she had to do was break his shackles. "I...I want to-..." "GLORIA!!" The door burst open suddenly and in stormed Reuben. She jumped at the sudden intrusion and spun on her heels, while Von Kelsig quickly adopted a slouch and a pathetic look as his captor entered the room and drew back a gloved hand, slapping Gloria on the side of the head and sending her tumbling to the floor at Von Kelsig's feet. She yelled out and held a hand to her throbbing temple as Reuben stepped forward and placed a massive black shoe on her head and kicked her to the floor. "What are you doing in here?!" He demanded in a raspy high voice. "I was giving him something to eat!" She yelled, holding her head in both hands and waiting for another strike. "GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE YOU WHORE!" Reuben roared, slamming a foot into her stomach and driving the wind out of her. Coughing hoarsely between uncontrollable sobs she struggled to her feet and raced out of the room as he turned and swung out again, barely missing her head with a closed fist. Turning back to Von Kelsig, Reuben sneered at his prisoner and drew back a fist, faking a punch at which Von Kelsig flinched. "Yeah I thought so..." He muttered, removing a pair of black leather gloves from his hands and tucking them into his overcoat. Running a hand through the oily slicked-back hair he fixed the few loose strands that had fallen out of place during his outburst against Gloria and licked his lips. Seeing the paper plate and bottle by his feet, Reuben kicked them away and wrapped a thick hand around Von Kelsig's neck, five equally chubby fingers squeezing him by the throat. "Excuse her, she's a little dense sometimes. You'll be thankful she had the gall to feed you then, hm?" He paused for a moment and studied the worried expression on Von Kelsig's face before moving his grip from around his throat to his face, pulling at his cheeks and holding a rather musky smelling palm over his nose and mouth. "No answer as per fucking usual." He threw Von Kelsig's head back and stepped away. "Are you going to inject me again?" The words came from his mouth and he almost didn't believe it. Von Kelsig hadn't meant to utter them, not in that capacity in any case. Had he meant to his thoughts would have been focused on fooling Reuben into thinking he was afraid of taking another hit and that thought hadn't even entered his mind. Something had stirred inside him and forced the question from his lips. Reuben's eyes lit up as he heard Von Kelsig speak the words he longed to hear, in one form or another. "Ohh, you want to know if you're getting a fix today?" He asked, with a sinister grin upon his chubby face. "Sad to say there's none 'til next week, friend..." Von Kelsig never answered, his stomach dropped and his heart skipped a beat as he realized he had asked for a hit and had been bothered by the denial. What happened next shocked him. "...Although I do keep a small stash in times of need." Reuben continued, producing a syringe from his pocket and removing the needle cap. "Why, is it getting too much for you? Can't take it any more?" Von Kelsig shook his head and looked away, momentarily glancing back at Reuben before closing his eyes and hanging his head. Reuben knew by his body language that he was putting on a front. He knew Von Kelsig wanted him to think he still detested the injections, but the casual manner of his declination told him that he was becoming more eager for a hit. His plan was working. As Reuben leaned forward and pierced the skin with the needle Von Kelsig winced not from the sting, but the fear that he was no longer infuriated by the process. The high would be preceded by the same old sick feeling and he would strain as he choked back the vomit, but this time it wasn't due to his fear of the heroin or his anger at being injected...it was just a side-effect of the smack. He had to convince Gloria to help him and escape from this prison and from the creeping death. Time was running out, and he felt desperate... ...He felt hopeless... ...He felt...Euphoric...
