Date: 28.03.2007 Looking Ahead: Culture Shock. JonathanWinters.com. Posted by admin LAST WEEK at 20:32pm. Yes folks, it’s that time of year again. For arguably the pinnacle of PRIME wrestling. Culture Shock, the date on the PRIME calendar everyone has marked out ready. A showcase for wrestlers and their fans alike. And yes, your *sarcasm* hero */sarcasm* will be there. On night one, he’s booked against fellow master of the dreaded Superkick Dusk in a one on one battle for the greatest prize of all…Pride! (That and bragging rights to the, um, deadliest move out there!). Then comes day two. The dreaded Dual Halo match itself. The construction crafted bone and blood. The event that shepherds utter and total chaos out of the purest order. Enough hype for you yet? Make damn sure you tune in! Review: Culture Shock. JonathanWinters.com. Posted by admin YESTERDAY at 20:32pm. Culture Shock has been and gone and if you missed it, where the hell were you? You missed one helluva show. But for Jonathan Winters? Well, for Winters it didn’t go so well. He lost in his Superkick vs. Superkick battle against Dusk and therefore the lost the right to use The Indifference Maker as his signature manoeuvre. Hoping to get an exclusive for JonathanWinters.com, I e-mail our *sarcasm* Lord and Master */sarcasm* in an attempt to uncover precisely what move he’ll be using from now on (Perhaps The Decision Maker or even Down to the Numbers???), but predictably I have yet to obtain a response and the odds of me getting one are equal to the likelihood of Charity Manale taking a vow to give up sucking cock. Anyway, onto the big one itself. The Dual Halo. Carnage, Chaos and glory. Did Winters avenge is poor showing on day one? Sort of. He lasted a respectable time and eliminated Dusk too boot. So, that’s at least something to smile about, right? Right? Yeah, right. Winters smile? Pffffftttttt… Where Were You? JonathanWinters.com. Posted by winters TODAY at 20:32pm. So, where were you?
