This is AWFUL

29 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 29th My name was Titan St. James. That was until the accident. Now, when I become angry, I lose control. And I shrink. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- All I wanted to do was get to the airport to fly out for ReVolution 99, but the traffic was so bad... “HRARGH!” The beast claws out and marches into the airport terminal, travel bag dangling from the left fist. There was a copious amount of empty space around him as people cleared away. “Oh GOD, he smells like [name withheld]! RUN!” The beast stomps through the terminal, up to the ticket counter. There, standing behind the counter, was the poor high school kid with the dubious distinction of looking at this massive, beastly, six-foot-eight, [name withheld]-smelling creature. The creature regards him with eyes almost looking ready to pop out of its head and reaches thick fingers into its bag for the ticket. “I GO HERE!” “Uh, uh, uh,” stuttered this couch potato forced out by his parents to get this crappy job. He could almost hear the tintinnabulation of his funeral bells. But he didn't even know what tintinnabulations meant. Kinda like [name withheld]. “Y'know, you don't have to check with me, you can just go straight to boarding your plane.” “HRARGH!” It shouts and grabs the travel bag and marches towards the metal detector. The security guards, rotund and [name withheld]-like in their polyester blazers, look up. “Oh man, that's big.” “I just made a Token Weed in my pants...” The massive great looks at them. The one that has fizzled a bomb into his drawers points nervously at the x-ray machine. The big creature slings its bag not ON the belt, but heaves it so that it flies THROUGH the machine and lands on the other side. The beast turns to the stinky man, double-dog-daring him to say something. With a big sniff, he glares at the man. “YOU SMELL LIKE [name withheld]!” The security guard could feel the crap in his pants (in case you haven't gotten it BY NOW [name withheld]) coagulate into a squishy butt cushion. And he had just taken his break, which means he had to stand there for the next three hours. The monster marched through the metal detector. It went off. He hit the metal detector. It quit going off. He glared at the other, daring the fat hippopotamus of a man to say something. The man waved him on by. “GOOD MAN!” And so, with the confidence of a bear in a daycare center. His massive, shuddering steps took him towards the gate for his flight. Where the plane was sitting, but was not boarding yet. “HRARGH!” So, with the big monster hungry, he turned and lumbered away. He meandered like an out-of-control steamroller with a Drunk [name withheld] at the wheel. That's when he heard the loud, annoying voice. “Get your schnitzel! Get your schnitzel! Get your schnitzel! Get your schnitzel! Get your schnitzel!” The beast walked up to the vendor, glaring at him out of overly intense eyes. “You wanna schnitzel? You got the guts? You walk the walk for a schnitzel, Green Giant?” “FOUR, NO SAURKRAUT!” The vendor was literally knocked down by the wind of the creature's breath, which smelled like [name withheld]'s breath, only without that smell of dog turd. The man immediately handed them over to watch the massive beast stuff them all in his mouth, wrappers included and then chewed up and swallowed a bottle of water. It's like watching [name withheld] eat when he remembers his manners. “Flight 813684468512654681651651651325535186 now boarding!” The monster turned and began to run towards the gate. People standing in line were knocked aside in waving fists of unrestrained rage. A handicapped kid had his wheelchair knocked over and wedged around his bodycast in the turmoil. “TICKET!” it shouts. The man behind the podium doesn't even look up, just tears the ticket and waves him on through. So the beast rumbles by and onto the plane. He looks around in first class, finds his seat and then opens his overhead compartment. He shoves his bag there, which doesn't fit, but he punches it frantically [detail withheld] He sank into his seat. “You look like you've got anger issues,” said a calm voice next to him. Chuck Norris. Calm began to sink in. His skin went from green to the tan it normally was. He grew from the six foot eight inch size back to his normal seven foot one. His clothes fit again instead of being all baggy. [detail withheld] “Hello, Mr. Norris, I'm Titan St. James.”
