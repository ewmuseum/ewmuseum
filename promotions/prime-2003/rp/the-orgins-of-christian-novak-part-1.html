The Orgins of Christian Novak Part 1

undated

Date: ReV 170 “Anger is a gift.” Malcolm X His head throbbed like a thousand hammers banging their way out of his skull. Agonizing pain shot from his eyes to his brain, blinding him momentarily as he dropped to his knees. Beads of sweat trickled from his face and dribbled into the cracks of the wooden floor. Gritting his teeth together, he clutched his fists and crawled through the darkness his eyes had created. “Got… to… calm… down,” he stumbled over his words. “But I can’t bear… the… pain.” It was a feeling Christian Novak was all too familiar with; one in which he could usually control. Suffering from intermittent explosive disorder from a young age, only a few things could cause the onset of Christian’s rage to ignite like a wildfire. A couple shots of alcohol usually being the fuel to its inferno. Once that fire had been ignited however, it was hard to extinguish. “I’ve got to find a way out of here,” Christian grimaced with each movement. But there was no way out. Christian edged closer to a table which sat in the middle of the room. Upon arrival and without much hesitation he clawed into it trying to regain his balance. Deep nail marks and drops of sweat being left within its wooden design from the impact of each scratch. In failure, green bottles toppled over onto its surface resulting from the behemoth’s massive strength. Fighting through the pain, he tried once more and this time successfully and slowly rose himself to his feet. As if taken over by some type of satanic force Christian screamed from the bottom of his lungs. “HELP ME,” he shrieked in intense ferocity. “I CAN’T BEAR THE PAIN ANY LONGER!” He repeated in the form of single words. Peering through his eyelids; eyes almost shut completely, Christian swatted one of the bottles away with the back of his hand and it shattered against a wall. He cleared the table with his other causing the remaining bottles to crash and shatter against the floor. Effortlessly, he lifted the table inches off the ground and heaved it across the room. It went crashing through a one-way mirror forcing the observant doctors on the other side to duck instinctively. It was all a test. “That’s it,” one yelled out. “Get in there! Push the lithium! Quick before it’s too late!” Three doctors all wearing white lab coats charged into the other room like football players and lunged onto Christian. They tried to restrain him but his natural reaction was to fight back. He punched one in the face smashing his bifocals instantly and sent the other two in opposite directions with no regard as to where they landed. Three more doctors charged, this time taking Christian down to the ground. Realizing Christian’s restraint, an older doctor from the observing room snatched a syringe from a desk drawer, filled probably with some sort of paralytic or tranquillizer. He flicked on the light with a push of a button and joined his team in the other room. “Hold down his arms you idiot,” the older doctor instructed wiping sweat from his gray eyebrow. “Keep him still!” “Get off of me,” Christian growled continuing to resist. “I swear, if you mother fuckers don’t unhand me you’re going to be pulling your heads out of each other’s asses!” “Is that so?” A restraining doctor asked sarcastically. Christian laughed angrily following the commenting doctor’s voice with a swift turning neck. “Listen you little cocksucker,” he told him. “Don’t get cute with me princess! Wait until I’m free… Then let’s see how much shit you’re going to talk with my FIST IN YOUR MOUTH!” In blind rage, Christian spat at the young doctor. The spitball spiraled through the air; luckily for the young doctor it missed him by just a few inches. The resident swallowed a hard lump in his throat and tightened up his grip. He wanted to make sure that there was no way Christian could escape before the meds had been pushed. Two other doctors wheeled over a gurney. They all were needed to lift Christian onto its surface. “Wait,” Christian cried out. “What are you doing to me? HELP! Somebody please!” But no one could help him. The determined older Attending Physician held the syringe in his teeth and hovered over the gurney fastening the restraints around Christian’s wrists. “He’s altered,” the attending exclaimed. “Tighten the hard restraints around his ankles,” he demanded the others. Christian tried to fight back but suddenly felt motionless. The syringe pierced into his skin and ended all the resisting. It also ended the ear-splitting pain he had once felt in his head. “Fuck…” Christian exclaimed in relief. It felt like a huge weight had been lifted from his shoulders. A tear rolled down his numb face as his eyes forced themselves open giving him one last glance of the chaos around him. Drifting off to sleep he could see the doctors wiping their foreheads in relief and struggling to catch their breaths after fighting the near three hundred pound man. A few of them exchanged high fives feeling as if it was a job well done. Christian faded. ~~~~~ …A few hours later… The EKG machine beeped in a smooth rhythm softly at Christian Novak’s side. Connected to wires and machines of all sorts, the familiar attending physician stood over Christian recording numbers on to his medical record chart. To the side of his bed sat Kale Ryder who was blowing his bubble gum like always. “Is he going to be alright,” Kale asked the physician. “Just fine.” “Is he sleeping?” “Sound,” the physician replied irritated by the dim-witted questions of Novak’s partner. “We’ll wake him in a couple of hours. He blacked out after the attack.” Kale nodded and squirmed in his seat, trying to ease the cramps out of his stiffening muscles. Looking down at his watch Kale said, “Ay doc. You think I can get a fresh stick of gum?” The physician reached into the top of his white coat pocket and removed an empty Wrigley’s gum wrapper. He shrugged his shoulder. “I’m sorry but I’m all out.” “No worries,” Kale countered. “Besides I’m a hubba bubba kind of guy anyway. I’ve just been chewing this one for close to two days now that’s all.” “Hubba bubba?” The doctor asked confused. “…two days?” He scratched the gray hairs in the scalp of his head in confusion. “Never mind.” Kale didn’t feel up to explaining his love for gum. I mean this guy REALLY LOVED gum. It was the type of love Linus had for his blanket. More like an addiction. He felt incomplete without it and found it hard to cope when it wasn’t in his procession. Kale blew a bubble nearly the size of the doctor’s head. “Try the vending machine down the hall.” Kale placed his hand in the air and motioned for the doctor to stop. “I’m good. I’m just going to wait here until Christian wakes up.” “Very well then,” the doctor declared. “Will you please have the nurse get me when he does?” “Sure. What’s your name again?” The doctor pulled his yellow nametag from his blue scrub toward Kale and said, “Dr. Howard Carter.” Kale nodded and gave him two thumbs up. The doctor nodded as well and exited the room. Kale sat back in the hard orange chair and twiddled his fingers back in forth. “…is he gone yet?” A muffled voice came from across the room. Kale jumped in shock. “Gee wilikers, Christian,” he exclaimed in shock. “You gave me quite the scare… I thought you were sleep. Hold up lemme get that doctor fellow back in here.” Kale rose from his feet and advanced toward the door. Christian stopped him dead in his tracks. “Damnit Kale you idiot wait!” Quite embarrassed Kale turned around toward Christian. He walked over toward the bed and kneeled down next to his partner. “Look,” Christian demanded Kale. “Something fishy is going on here. I can’t quite put my finger on it yet but I don’t wanna wait around here and find out.” “What are you saying Christian?” “Get me out of here you moron!” “So you don’t want me to get the doctor?” “No!” Christian shook in the gurney trying to release himself from the restraints. “Just get these damn restraints off of me!” “Wait,” Kale tried to persuade. “How do you know? The doctor told me you blacked out in there?” “Yeah, after he tranquilized me you jackass! And what do you mean how do I know? I have photographic memory, remember?” Christian was right. He had an eidetic memory and could paint the picture of an entire scene just by seeing it for thirty seconds. A gift had resulted from all the rage and anger he had experienced throughout his life after all. Kale undid one of the wrist restraints followed by the other. “Are you sure you’re going to be able to control yourself?” Kale asked. “You were brought here for help. I don’t want to be on the receiving end of that temper of yours again.” “Just get me out of here! I’ll explain everything later!” Kale finally undid the ankle restraints. Christian grabbed his bag of belongings from underneath the hospital bed. He stumbled to his feet before falling flat on his face; the result of the medicine. Kale grabbed the much bigger Christian by his arm and helped him up to his feet. Christian wrapped his arm around Kale’s body for balance as the two limped their way out of the hospital room. ~~~~~~ A few minutes later Dr. Howard Carter reentered the room back first. He turned around holding a tray of medicine in his hand as the door closed softly behind him. “Chris….tian?” Howard Carter looked around the room and slapped himself across the face. “Damnit, he’s escaped.” He dropped the tray with the meds to the floor; all the contents of the bottles scattered across the ground as Dr. Howard Carter rushed out of the room and down the hall.
