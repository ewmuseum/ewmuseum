Facing Facts and Fast Points

6 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 6th, 2005 The room spun. How much did she drink? She rolled over, her hand laying idle at the side of the mattress. It brushed across the handle of a suitcase, and not far from that was the sensation of someone suddenly snatching the object away. Amy was awake. ''Wha...'' she mumbled, opening her eyes to find Jeph dragging a large luggage bag into the hall, ''Wait...what's this?'' He spoke plainly, looking to the ground while shuffling the last of his bags into the hall... ''You're not supposed to be awake.'' ''What's going on?'' ''I packed when you passed out.'' Amy's head was foggy and the spinning sensation hadn't stopped, not even when she closed her eyes to will it all away. ''Jeph,'' she said, this time with a curt tone, ''What're you doing?'' ''Leaving,'' was the only answer, ''I'll miss your friendship, Amy.'' He was serious. This was serious. She needed to play dumb. ''Excuse me?'' Here it was... ''It's been coming to this since Culture Shock. You're the most self-destructive, absolutely volatile human being I know. You might be charming, but when you're not talking or thinking about yourself, you're busy enough talking and thinking about Karina fucking Wolfenden. I'm a man, not some fuck-toy you push between thoughts of snorting coke and fighting some chick for fear that she might be better than you. Face the facts. I'm gone.'' The tiny redhead shrugged. She had this coming. ''Bye, then.'' With only an expression shocked by her indifference, he was gone. Jeph was gone and Amy, though she'd pretended to the contrary, had expected it. Much sooner, actually. She groaned and rolled back over. He'd speak to her again...someday. Her eyes were locked on the tequila bottle she hadn't quite emptied; The new conquest for this morning. With a quick swig and swallow Aimz shook her head, patted down the tussled sheets and went back into a peaceful sleep. With the morning's work and inevitable parting out of the way, Amy could sleep without a thought outside of PRIME. It felt cold to let go so easily, but that was only another pin pricking her back - One of many that stung far, far worse. Now, she could sleep knowing that she really and truly was entirely alone in the world, without a single adoring entity. With nothing but the fight left in her.
