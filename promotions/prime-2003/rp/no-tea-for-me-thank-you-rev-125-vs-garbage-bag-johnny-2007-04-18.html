No Tea for Me, Thank You (ReV 125, vs. Garbage Bag Johnny)

18 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/18/07 (FADE IN: Universal Champion and [name withheld]’s Wrestler of the Month for March of 2007 (What? Let me shill! I just read that shit yesterday!) Nova sits on the floor of his hotel room Indian-style, one gloved hand wrapped around the neck of the water bong in front of him and the other bringing a lighter’s flame down to the slide.) Oh, I forgot. He’s wearing some form of containment suit. Smoke trails out of the vent around the neck of his suit, its source being the cigarette hanging out of the Risen Star’s mouth. He flips the grid up and takes the cigarette out of his mouth, setting it in an ashtray nearby, and then rips the bong. Leaning back, the grid flopped down into place, smoke shoots out of its holes as Nova wheezes out a painful cough. His head isn’t visible through the dome’s glass. After a moment, it clears out a little and tears are visible welling up in the veteran smoker’s eyes. NOVA: Whew…*cough*…goodness… He takes a few more deep breaths as the redness in his face gradually subsides. He then turns to the camera. NOVA:I know what some of you are thinking. "Hey, WTF man? Chris only uses this script-style crap when he writes Nova’s FW RPs. Why you fuckin’ with my head like that?" And let me be the first to tell you, I don’t know. I don’t. And when I see him next, I’m gonna have some choice words for his ass, so don’t you worry about it. It won’t happen again. Now, onto more important matters… He pops his neck, careful to avoid bashing his head on the sides of the glass dome, and locks his bloodshot eyes on the camera lens. NOVA: Things are pretty stressful for me right now. There’s a mob building backstage – pitchforks, torches, and all, I swear to God – who wanna lynch me, I’m staring down the barrel of another title defense…and now there’s Denver, Colorado, ReVolution 125, and Garbage Bag Johnny. AWC Legend. Sweet deal. He’s a fighter. Nothing wrong with that…buuuut…it makes me uneasy that as of late, I’m not sure a single one of his opponents has escaped the ring, victory or no, without the Dirtiest Dude in PRIME’s nuts slapping their face at least once. I mean, damn! Last week GBJ must have tea-bagged Dusk, like, four times during that tag match! And Tony Gamble? I don’t even need to postulate about the number of times he’s had to wash out nut-mouth with some Listerine…but I will. Two bottles this month means a lot of nut-mouth. The Risen Star grabs his forgotten cigarette out of the ashtray and puffs on it. NOVA: I mean, I’m no stranger to the unorthodox artform that is the tea-bag. Shit, I practically invented the Pendulum, the 101st Airborne, and the Sleeping Beauty, ALL of which are used today in pro-level tea-bagging tournament play. So I know a deadly ‘Bagger when I see one… He stares intently into the camera, looking both ways before narrowing his eyes and speaking in hushed tones of awe and caution. NOVA: …and Garbage Bag Johnny might be the deadliest ‘Bagger of them all. The champ lets this line sink in for a moment, taking a dramatic drag off his cigarette. He also nods slowly, as if to re-confirm his own words. After an overly-long pause, he snaps out of it. NOVA: See, when it happened to Dusk or Gamble or whoever else’s chin Johnny’s balls have graced *cough*Charity*cough*, it was funny. I mean, tea-baggings generally are. But now it’s me in his hairy, unwashed sights, and I feel like that reverend, Martin Niemoller, who said that shit about how he didn’t speak up for anybody and then when the Nazis came for him, there was no one left to speak for him. I’ve enjoyed the tea-baggings thus far, but now Garbage Bag Johnny’s balls come for me, and there’s no one to help me. He picks up a baggie and examines how much weed is left before setting it back down. NOVA:So I thought, "Wow, man, this thing doesn’t bode well for me…what should I do?" And it really didn’t take me that long to come up with an answer: full-body protection against the menace. A containment suit. I’ve always kind of wanted one, anyway, to see what happens when you box it out. Now I’m fuckin’ set. Not only will I have a protective barrier between myself and GBJ’s balls, but now he can’t spit sandwich crumbs on me, which is just gross, and I also prevent myself from contracting any viruses or diseases from his general filth. I heard he’s really a leper, but I also heard from the same source that he’s got Chlamydia, so he’s probably off his rocker. I mean I’m sorry, but there’s no way I can see Johnny ever getting laid ever. Not in the Red Light District of Amsterdam. Nova takes one more draw from his cigarette before putting it out. NOVA: So on Wednesday, you’ll see me, and my fucking pwnerfully hyper-badass containment suit open the show in dramatic fashion by kicking the Dirtiest Dude in…shit, the fucking WORLD, I would I imagine…all over the ring and setting yet another record for this company…becoming the first person to emerge from a Garbage Bag Johnny match in PRIME without the taste of stale ballsac in his mouth. Word! The scene fades as Nova leans over and begins packing the bong slide again.
