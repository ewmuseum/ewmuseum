Blues Fest

12 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-05 Hoyt being the modest and mindful professional that he is wouldn’t bring up how many times he’s destroyed the Trashcan Man. Or how bad Kano sucks and he certainly wouldn’t bring up the fact that Boda can’t even beat his meat anymore. That’s not his style as Hoyt isn’t about talk but rather action. Action is also the best word to describe the busy day Hoyt has been blessed with. It’s now three o’clock in the afternoon “Your Savior” has already worked out, wrote hate mail to Howard Dean, ran three miles along the shore line of Lake Machine, and is now searching for some seating at Chicago’s annual blues fest in Grant Park. Hoyt Williams: Look at all these black people. Hoyt is accompanied by his long time girlfriend Mary with her cherry, his slightly obnoxious RISP brother Wayne, and local Scottish transplant turned radio star Ross. Mary: Ewe it smells like an oil leak. Wayne: They use that in their hair. Ross: What did you people expect it’s a blues fest. Hoyt Williams: Hey you don’t have to tell me I’m all about the blues. I mean come on Kenny Wayne Shepherd, Blues Traveler, and Elvis is as down home blues as it gets. I just never knew that the blacks also dig the blues. Ross: Those guys are far from true blues musicians like B.B. King, Buddy Guy, Muddy Waters, or Jimmy Reed. Hoyt Williams: Never head of them people. I mean after all what do black people have to sing sad about? We pay these peoples welfare and they never even work. We gave them a free boat ride to God’s country how many years ago and never asked a cent from them. Seems like they should be the one’s singing the happy songs. I bet that’s what that rap music is all about thanking the great white race. Ross makes a face like he disagrees but knows its better to just leave well enough alone. Mary is standing in line to buy some BBQ ribs and watermelon from the busy street vender. Ross: So you have a four way match on that wrestling show this week? Hoyt Williams: Yea, as you know God wants me to win the world title and this is one of my stops on the “Hoyt To Greatness 2005 tour”. It’s not really a big deal the match is full of what we call in the wrestling industry, jobbers. Ross: Jobbers? That’s interesting jargon. Hoyt Williams: I know I tried to talk the board of directors to change it from jobbers to Kano’s but I lost by one vote. Wayne: Now that makes sense if you say he that guy’s a Kano. I know he’s a guy who makes a living sucking and losing making his opponents look good. Hoyt Williams: Screw the board, from this day forward a professional loser shall be know as a Kano. Mary: Boy these natives sure enjoy their watermelon. Hoyt Williams: That’s an interesting fact you see back in the nineteen hundreds African American’s fell in love with Watermelon and ate it all day. Chicken and Watermelon is what these people lived off of while the rest of the world worked to feed them. They are a blessed people with a very soft history. The foursome of biblical brotherhood finally get their food orders in and the delicacy immediately delivered up. They are shown sucking on slices of watermelon as they try as neatly as possible to eat their giant BBQ chicken leg while walking in the hot down town summer sun. Hoyt Williams: I need to take a Furher but I don’t see a spot around. Mary: Your going to take a Furher in a place this crowded crawling with the devils tanned army? Hoyt Williams: When I man has a go, a man has to go, savior or sinner we all crap the same. Wayne: Yea but those public stalls are full of disgust and stink. I mean they got more diseases then Aimz. Hoyt Williams: There is no way that’s possible. Ross: The blokes got a point man. Lets foot it back to my flat where you can Furher in comfort. Mary: Can’t you just hold it or send it back up the river? If Moses can part the red sea’s certainly you can reverse the flow of colon discharge. Ross: He’s a Savior not a miracle worker. Hoyt squeezes his buns in an effort to make Furher disappear in the bowels of well his bowels. After a few nasty faces it appears that a miracle has been indeed worked as the urgency to crap vanishes like Trashcan Man’s legacy in the sport after losing on two consecutive pay-per-view’s to the great Hoyt Williams. Wayne: How many times are those idiots booking PRIME going to shove you in a match with the Trashcan Man. Hoyt Williams: As many times as they want to until I take control. Wayne: WHAT? Say again. You hope Mary is on birth control? Mary: What? That’s nobodies business besides I’m a virgin. A loud guitar solo plays with perfect pitch and range as the legendary Buddy Guy takes the stage. The crowd goes nuts as he begins singing a sad song of woe in a traditional blues melody. Hoyt places his hands over his ears as Ross begins praying for the lord to make the noise stop. Hoyt Williams: What is that horrific racket? Ross: That’s the blues. Hoyt Williams: Oh God THIS IS the blues? I thought the blues was that music with bubbles and hula skirts. Ross: Your thinking Do Ho and Hawaiian music. Hoyt Williams: WOW black people have sure messed up the blues! Let’s get out of here. Wayne: Say again? What happened to your shoes? Getting a cab in the city of Chicago is very simple and requires very little effort. All you have to do is place your arm out towards the road with your pointer and middle finger extended towards the first yellow chariot you see. They will then cross four lanes of traffic, cause seven accidents, and kill two pedestrians in order to answer your hail. The only time this magic chain of events fails is if you on Addison after a Cubs game, Grant Park after a Bears game, if it’s raining or if a festival is going on. With the blues festival in full gear the great foursome of spiritual togetherness struggle. Ross: You ever wonder why so many dot heads are doctors? Hoyt Williams: Because the devil uses his brown servants to slowly kill us with medical bills and impossible to understand diagnosis? Ross: Nope, it’s because they can’t drive cabs. The two friends and one lover of Hoyt laugh as they lighten up the mood of the hellish wait for a portable yellow, curry smelling, potpourri of culture. Mary: I can’t wait to see you put an end to Boda and his reign of reek. Ross: No question Kano is a Kano but Boda just needs to retire. Wayne: Boda put the B in Bad. I think your only worry should be the Trashcan Man. Hoyt Williams: I’ve beaten him more times then Ike on Tiny. The guy was a legend all these guys were legends too bad when you DIE God can careless about what you did on earth unless you’re his kid. Like me. PCW IS DEAD as is the careers of these poor saps who really need to understand that fact. I never graced PCW with my superior skills as I was too good for their circuit. I would have embarrassed half of their roster. But now here I am in PRIME stuck facing these guys like some BAD acid flashback. Which is strange since I’ve never done any drugs. Well it’s time to show these fat, balding, zit faced old school fans that a new generation of legend has emerged and we simply don’t have room for the old ones. The Eskimo’s do it right they take their elderly and place them in an igloo on a broken ice berg and send them off to sea. That’s what I’m going to do to these decrepit shells of one time success. Their swan song ends now as I put them out of their misery in many ways relieving ours of having to watch these dinosaurs try to hang with God’s like me. This match is nothing but a bus depot on my journey to the unification of the two top title of PRIME. Make no mistakes about it I am PRIME’s SAVIOR and YOUR PERSONAL JESUS. Ross: So it is written so it is gospel. Wayne: Hey what about if Furher wins. Hoyt Williams: I though that was behind us. I guess if he wins I’ll need to change my pants. Wayne: No I mean Olswig or what ever the hell his name is. Hoyt Williams: Oh that’s right Killean is taking on Furher in the battle of horrible names. The only damning thing is that the Universal Title is on the line. Isn’t that a crying shame watching a champion embarrass that tile by allowing such nobodies as Oswald match up with him. Trust me once I unify the title there will be no title defenses with unworthy opponents. I’m not that nice of a guy. Mary: Isn’t Furher a Kano? Hoyt Williams: I sure as heck thought so but Killean need a squash match to make him look good in front of his hometown. It’s kind of like Mike Tyson boxing that no named boxer in an attempt to restart his career. Everybody knows the other guy doesn’t have a chance he’s just a Kano doing the job. Wayne: He beat Tyson last night. Hoyt Williams: Well I’ll be damned. I guess the guy who beat Tyson now has more wins then both Kano and Furher and moves out of the Kano category to a TCM. Oh Well. Mary: Hey look a cab! The cab pulls over to the side of the road causing only two accidents in the process. The clan gets in to the strong stench of curry and a delightful Indian cab driver Sudhir Sunipipi. The cab drives off leaving us with a cover version of John Lee Hookers “Boom Boom” =w=
