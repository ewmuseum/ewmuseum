RoloVolution

17 June 2008

Date: 6/17/2008 “What the fuck!?” Tony Rolo doesn’t sound too happy. In fact, he sounds down right pissed off. “Lisa, this is fucking bullshit and you know it,” Rolo exclaims irritably. “Last week I was supposed to be in the main event, then Cantrell changed that on me. So I take that shit match against that shit tag-team that are poor excuses for champions that he scheduled for me, and I whip their asses. Then I ask Cantrell for a better spot on the card this week and he has you call me to say I’m not needed. I mean, I’m not trying to pull the ‘I’m a Hall of Famer’ card out on you, but I am a Hall of Famer, you know. Does Cantrell have any fucking idea what the hell he is doing running PRIME?” The Specialist stands there on his cell phone listening to Lisa Tyler, PRIME’s Vice President of Talent Relations, on the other end. “Uh huh… yeah… uh huh. Yes, I understand that… well can I come down there and meet with him?” Rolo throws his arms up in disbelief. “You just said you just came out of his office.” Tony’s obviously growing more and more frustrated with this conversation. “This is horseshit… tell Cantrell next time he has something to tell me that he call me himself, and not to have his whore call for him… yeah, I said WHORE… you know, a woman who sucks an inordinate amount of dick.” He pauses as Lisa is likely responding to his verbal assault on her now. “Call human resources… see if I give a shit. I’m sure GCW would love to have a PRIME Hall of Famer on their show next week?... No, that isn’t a threat… it’s a fact, bitch.” Tony waits for her response again. “Yeah, you can tell him I’ll still be there this week. He can count on that, jizz-breath!” Rolo whips his cell phone off the wall and it bounces off with the battery flying off in the opposite direction. “That fucking cocksucker motherfucker!” _________________________________________________________ A few hours later… “I swear to God, Joe, I’m fucking fed up with C.P. Cantrell. The guy is fucking clueless on how to run this fucking company,” Rolo says while looking next to him at Joey Troy in his car. With a mouthful of Arby Sauce dripping from his face onto his shirt, Joey mutters, “Whuffs the big fookin’ deal?” “What do you mean, what’s the big fucking deal?” Tony doesn’t seem to be agreeing with his former partner’s laid back approach to the situation, and he makes it known as he continues, “Joe, he changed the damn card last week… taking me out of the main event, putting me in the middle of the card into obscurity… seriously, all I can say is, what the fuck?” “First of all, Tone, you’re rambling and not making any sense. Second of all, if you’re so pissed off about it, why don’t you do something about it? This way I don’t have to keep listening to you bitching and moaning like a 15 year-old who can’t find his father’s porno stash.” “You know, you’re right. Fuck Cantrell… fuck him right in the ass.” Joey stops chewing on his sandwich and turns towards Rolo, speaking with a mouthful of beef. “I oo ope yer speaking me-ah-froiquy.” “What the fuck did you just say?,” Rolo states. Joey swallows it all in one big gulp and repeats himself, “I said, I do hope you’re speaking metaphorically.” “No, I meant that I was really going to fuck C.P. Cantrell in his ass,” the former champ declares in a very sarcastic tone. “Yes, I was speaking metaphorically!” “How are you gonna go about the anal rapage, then?,” Troy offers up. “You know, I’ve been relatively quiet since returning at Culture Shock. I’ve minded my own business… I’ve played nice. But not anymore. All of that is over and done with. I play by my rules and nobody else’s now. I used to wreak havoc around PRIME… cause chaos. That’s why Motoki and Hin See were always hell bent on getting rid of me. But I got soft in my old age. And where did that get me? Fucking nowhere, that’s where. Well enough with that shit. Fuck C.P. Cantrell! The old Tony Rolo is back… and our Executive Producer is gonna regret it.” Joey interrupts his best friend’s little tirade, “Yeah, I get it… but how do you plan on going about this?” “ROLOVOLUTION!!! That’s how.” Joey looks at him inquisitively, “That’s the term for the evolution of gay people, right?”
