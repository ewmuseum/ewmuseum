Revelations

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/08/05 “What are you doing here?” Melissa talked to herself under her breath as she lay on the couch looking up at the ceiling with confused eyes. This place was not familiar to her yet she had found herself lured to it by the safety it provided. As she looked around the spacious lounge, everything was alien to her but strangely reassuring. The furniture did not have Peter’s touch on it, the room did not smell of his cheap aftershave and the floors did not carry the sound of his shoes. She knew that she was safe here and his presence could not haunt her whilst she remained in this place. This feeling of reassurance was one that she had not felt for some time and it was a wonderful feeling. She no longer had to fight or protect herself from his ways; instead she could lie here and stare into the ceiling for as long as she chose. “This is madness.” Like any story though, there are always two sides involved. Whilst the smell of the leather from the furniture and big windows from which light poured in, provided her with safety, she knew nothing of the man she was staying with. Paul Cain had proved himself to be some kind of guardian angel, protecting her interests and challenging Peter at every opportunity. However, deep down inside she wondered about Paul’s motives and whether he was truly as genuine as he pretended to be. They had spoken long into the night about the places that they had been both mentally and physically, yet when the mention of Peter came along, Paul’s eyes would flicker with a look of intent. As she glanced across at the leather chair he had sat in that night, she could still picture that expression and it didn’t feel right. “You’re just being paranoid.” She began to wonder whether she was being influenced by Peter even now. She was wary of the hand that was feeding her yet that person was not obliged to be doing this good deed. Perhaps this situation with Peter was clouding her judgement and that her suspicions were unfounded. Paul had trusted her to stay in his house alone whilst he conducted his business, whatever that was. She knew nothing of this but his sombre demeanour as he left the apartment was proof enough that something was weighing heavily on his mind. There were stories to tell in his mind, although she wondered whether her imagination was now being affected by her own experiences. Slowly she hauled herself up off the couch dropping the blanket that had covered her onto the floor. Her body was fully covered by a baggy white T-shirt and pants, just two of the new items she had hastily purchased after being given the chance to stay in his home. It had not been her intention to become so heavily involved with him but he had offered her something that she badly needed at this time, security. She walked to the curtains of the lounge window and threw them open to glance at the brilliant blue sky of the early morning. This was one of those days where she actually felt calm and relaxed as she tried to put the recent events out of her mind for a time. That was until the phone rang. She turned to the table where the telephone sat as the shrill ringing pierced through the room. Although she was not Paul’s secretary, she considered whether to answer it or not. After a moment, she moved away from the window and rested her hand on the receiver. Her curiosity was getting the better of her as she continued to wonder about the personality that was Paul Cain. Eventually she gave in to temptation and lifted the receiver off the hook and calmly answered it. “Hello?” There was an initial silence but breathing could be heard at the other end. “Who is this?” The voice that replied to Melissa was a female one and spoke with genuine confusion but suspicion at the same time. “I’m Melissa, I’m a friend of Paul’s. He’s not here right now; can I help you at all, or take a message?” Her politeness was one born out of innocence and she was clearly unprepared for the response from the other end. “What are you doing there?” She paused and wondered what this was all about. The woman sounded disgruntled as if Melissa may have been treading on territory that she shouldn’t have been on. The voice sounded slightly familiar but she couldn’t recall where she had heard it from. “I’m staying here for a little while; Paul is putting me up while I get my life together.” Melissa chose to be honest as she had nothing that she needed to hide. The reply from the woman however was unsettling and reminded her of her caution over what history and secrets Paul was hiding behind his charms. “You…Are in danger.” The chill shot up Melissa’s spine and her stomach tightened as the words sank into her mind. Something was not right here. * The metal door of the prison cell slammed shut with a haunting crash. Paul Cain looked over his shoulder as the sound reverberated around the room and then slowly returned his attention to in front of him. He was dressed in a black open shirt and black jean pants with his hair hanging down over his eyes. His hands were jammed into his pockets and he paced slowly up and down the width of the cell. There was an eerie silence and Cain also noticed the cold chill in the air as he walked. “I wondered whether you would come.” Cain stopped in his tracks and turned his head to look at the man that was sat at the opposite end of the table. He was wearing an orange prison uniform and a white T-shirt underneath. His frame was huge and his skin was as black as night, he had to be closer to seven feet tall than not with muscles bulging under his uniform. The mammoth man was sitting forward in his seat with his hands clasped together. His eyes shone brightly and he stared intently at Paul who returned his gaze with a stone cold expression. “What do you want Damien, I don’t hear from you for months and NOW you summon me here like a lapdog?” When addressing this big man, Paul was stern and impatient. His mind was on other things, in particular the mental and physical battle with Peter Vetra. He did not have time for matters which he felt were trivial and inconsequential to the more important matter at hand. Damien leans back in his seat with his arms folded, his head cocked to one side observing his opposite number. He studied Cain carefully before responding. “You’re a different man these days, ain’t ya Paul? You look…Tense.” Cain continued to pace up and down the cell width but his speed increased as his patience level decreased. He was not comfortable around this man but he felt he had no choice but to go. They had been through a lot together, none of it was comfortable and none of it was safe. They had been to places that were dark and violent; they were once very similar characters. However now, Damien sat and looked at a man who seemed so very different to the person he once knew. “I’ve got a lot on my mind.” As he replied with a short and sharp retort, Damien smiled to himself as he bent his fingers back, cracking the bones as he examined Paul. “Whatever happened to the man that had so much to say for himself?” Cain walked over to the right hand wall and leaned on it with his arms folded. “I ran out of words.” Damien turned his head and glared coldly at Paul with a look that could kill. “Quit with the bullshit attitude Paul, we know each other better than that.” Cain said absolutely nothing in response and simply stared ahead at the opposite wall, slipping his hand under his shirt and touching it to the Pentagram scar. He trailed his fingers along it’s outline and then bowed his head as if transfixed by it. “I’ve been watching you Paul. I’ve seen your obsession over that ‘thing’ on your chest and how your personality was killed by these occult motherfuckers. You need to get your head back in the game son, you’ve lost it, you’re loco and you’re losing control.” For a moment there was silence but then Paul turned back to Damien and took a long series of steps to the table and leant down on it. “You don’t even know me anymore so you can’t even begin to judge my life!” A confident and calm smile crossed Damien’s face again as he did not even flinch in the presence of a temperamental Cain. “I know enough my friend. Take my advice Paul, walk away. The Serial Thrilla is not dead, you’ve just allowed these people to get inside your head and screw with it. Go out there and look in the mirror, see what you really are Paul. This isn’t you, you’re the cocky, confident, cool bastard that used to control the National Wrestling Council. You’ve allowed these people to get inside your mind and take you to places that you ain’t got any need to be going to. That scar means nothing now, it’s just an unfortunate thing that happened. You’re better than this Paul, walk away from it all and be who you REALLY are.” Cain stands seething as he glares furiously at Damien, his eyes wide and bulging with anger. He wondered who gave this man the right to come back into his life and pass judgement. “You don’t know what it was like, they destroyed me and took my life away.” Damien scoffed and shook his head. “The only thing they took from you was your sanity. Get a grip on yourself, go back home, sit down and think very long and hard about where you are going and what you’re doing. The Serial Thrilla side of you never died on that night, they only did what you allowed them to. Finish your business with Vetra and walk away from it. Leave him to suffer his own fate, don’t get dragged into his world even if you think you share the same place. As for Melissa, leave her be too, you don’t need her and she doesn’t need you. Go back to your past Paul and remember where you came from. This ‘thing’ that stands before me is not Paul Cain, you might look like him…” He then sniffs the air for a moment and looks disgusted. “Cheap aftershave, you smell like him…But I hear your words and you sure don’t sound like him. You disappoint me.” Cain looks up at the ceilng and sighs under his breath. “You don’t understand.” Damien holds his hand up and stops him there. “Don’t give me cheap excuses Paul. Make your choice and make the right one. Finish your business and…Walk…Away.” There is an icy cold silence and Paul stands to his full height and turns to walk away as he heads towards the door. Under his breath he mutters to himself. “You don’t own me.” Damien smiles and looks at his nails idily. “My time in here is running out Cain and I’ll soon be free. If you’ve not made the right choice by then, I’ll make sure you do.” The scene fades out to black as Cain slams the door shut behind him, leaving a calm and relaxed Damien Kahn sitting smiling with his feet up.
