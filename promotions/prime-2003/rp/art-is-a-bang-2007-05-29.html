Art Is A Bang

29 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 29th, 2007 Coral Avalon never really wanted to come back to New Orleans. To be honest, even though it's immoral of him to think this way and even though he's aware of this, Coral kinda wished that Hurricane Katrina had destroyed more of the western side of the city. The reason was because of a certain mansion on the city's west side, a mansion that he long since dreaded to visit or even acknowledge the existence of. Coral regarded the people who lived in this mansion as "selfish freaks of nature who make him look utterly bland in comparison", and has tried for a long time to keep Annabelle from wanting to visit them. Unfortunately for Coral, with this actually being the first time Annabelle had been in Coral's hometown, she was quite interested in knowing why Coral wanted to avoid these people as desperately as she did. Coral was the sort of person who couldn't exactly say no to the girl he loved, although it should be noted that very few people actually say "no" to her to begin with. So, here Coral was, standing in front of the gate just in front of the mansion. Coral did not, however, dress like he was going to a mansion. His long black hair was barely combed, he wore a wrinkled white T-shirt that looked to haven't seen a washer in at least two days, and a pair of jeans. He wore his usual pair of sneakers, which were in dire need of replacement in the first place. By contrast, Annabelle looked well-dressed and well-groomed. She wore a flowery and flowy pink dress and black dress shoes, and had her hair placed neatly into their usual waist-length pigtails. The two were like day and night, yet they were as closely tied to one another as a sunrise or a sunset. The gates opened. Coral and Annabelle walked down the paved path that led to the mansion. While Coral walked slowly, as if trying to delay this meeting further, Annabelle seemed to look forward to it. When Annabelle noticed that Coral was lingering behind her, she turned around and smiled sweetly at him before taking one of his hands in her own and continuing to walk forward. Eventually, the two reached the front of the mansion, where they were greeted by a butler. This butler, who was taller than Annabelle, but shorter than Coral, was an older gentleman. He had gray hair, wore a monocle, wore a tuxedo, and generally seemed to just be your typical butler. In fact, his name is Alfred Rosefeld. Yes, his first name is Alfred. I'm as shocked as you. "Good day, Master Coral." Rosefeld said, "I trust that your visit will be met well this time." "She dragged me here. I didn't come willingly." Coral insisted, as he was indeed being dragged along by his insistant girlfriend. Annabelle looked agt him for a moment, but then smiled at Rosefeld. "Hello, madam." he said to her. "It's nice to meet you. I'm Annabelle." Annabelle said, before she did a slight bow to Rosefeld. Rosefeld didn't get too much of a chance to introduce himself, however, as the front doors of the mansion flew open and out stormed a young girl. This girl was about as short as Annabelle was (5'5"), but seemed a little older (she's actually 23 now). She looked like a goth, though she wore her hair out of her face using a head band. She wore a long-sleeved robe-like black shirt and a knee-length black skirt with knee-high black boots (not exactly something a normal person would wear in New Orleans during the month of May). She stormed right over to Coral and proceeded to slap him right in the face. Coral, turned away from the girl who just slapped him, could only say, "Ow." Annabelle was confused on this action, but didn't dare step in just yet. "You have a lot of nerve to show your face back here, Coral Avalon." the girl said. "Why does everyone think that I came back here willingly? Seriously. The less I come to this place, hell, this town... the better I'll probably feel about life." Coral said. He still hadn't turned to face the girl, but he was looking at her with his left eye. "Hmph. Then I assume she dragged you here." the girl said. She turned to Annabelle, looking her over. Annabelle, filled with the manners of a girl of a much higher class than she actually is, bowed before the girl in front of her, "Hello. I'm Annabelle Summers." The girl blinked, but casually said to Annabelle, "Uh... I'm Lily," she then gestured at Coral, "I'm this bonehead's little sister." "Oh, I see." Annabelle said. She smiled, but she felt so awkward talking to Lily, "I'm... um..." "You're the girlfriend?" Lily asked. "Y-yes..." Lily looked at Annabelle, then at Coral, then back at Annabelle, and then to Coral again, "At least you got one with manners this time. That last one you brought here deserved a punch in the mouth." "I was afraid of even coming here simply because I thought you'd 'punch Annabelle in the mouth'." Coral said, "air quoting" his last five words. Upon this comment, Lily reared back and slapped Coral in the face again. Coral, turned away from Lily, could only say, "Ow." LIly turned back to Annabelle, "So, what do you see in this idiot, anyway? He's a wrestler, he travels a lot, he's not very intelligent," Lily said, the last of these comments got a 'Hey!' from Coral that was quickly ignored, "He's an idiot with money, he probably doesn't make in a week's work what dad makes in an hour, he's rude, he's sarcastic, he's broody, and he's self-absorbed with his own problems." Annabelle looked at Coral, noticing that Lily's words made him quite uncomfortable, before she turned back to Lily, "I admit that he has flaws, but... I guess you can say that I see a lot more good in him." "Hrmmm... so you're one of those romantic types, aren't you?" Lily asked, "You know, the ones that cry at the end of Steel Magnolias and pine over boyfriends?" "I... guess so." Annabelle said, with a sigh, "Then again, I play violin, so that should be expected from me." Lily perked up, "The violin?" Annabelle seemed surprised, "Um... yes." "A sign of someone with a decent education. Unlike SOME people." Lily said, directing a glare at Coral who merely groaned at the stare, "There might be hope for you yet, Annabelle. Tell me, do you have any other talents?" "Well... I'm not that well-educated... I kinda always liked the arts and music. Especially music." Annabelle said. "Can you draw?" Lily asked. "Yes, I can." Annabelle said. Lily smiled, and turned to Coral, "You actually found a girlfriend who doesn't look like a harlot and shares my interests? I approve." "Yes, because whenever I find someone I love, I'm always seeking your high standards." Coral said, sarcastically. Coral's relationship with his little sister, Lily Avalon, although strained, was really his best relationship within this part of the Avalon family. It's hard to tell what kind of relationship he has with his older brother, Elvis Avalon, since Elvis was a mute. He got along fine with his father, but the one person he really, truly despised the most and the person that nearly kept him from chasing the dream that he currented lived today... was his mother. Fortunately, his mother and his father were away on business, and Elvis was off to one of his anger management courses, which left Lily and the butler in charge of the home. Coral was very thankful for this information as he was told by Rosefeld, on his way inside the mansion. * . * Lily invited Coral and Annabelle to her room, on the second floor of the west wing of the Avalon family mansion. Lily decided to make her way there first, to "set up the stage" for the two of them. As the two of them slowly walked down the long corridors, Annabelle turned to Coral, "So, um... do you not get along with anyone from your family?" "I only get along with Emmy." Coral said. Emelia "Emmy" Avalon was the oldest of the four Avalon siblings that Coral was involved in. Emmy was Jonathan's (Coral's dad) daughter from a previous marriage, which explained why she had red hair, instead of the black hair found in the other three siblings. Emmy was a genius biologist who used to work on government grant, but currently teaches college-level biology in Ohio. Emmy, much like Coral, was ostracized from the family for leaving "without permission". "Oh." Annabelle said, "But... Lily seems nice." "She's nice to *you*." Coral said. "True... but I like to think that the way she treats you is the way she shows she likes you." Annabelle said. "Ever the optimist." Coral said, sarcastically. "W-well... it's kind of easy for me to relate to her... I'm good at the arts but pretty poor at everything else. She seems to be the same." Annabelle said. "It's not that she's the same, Annabelle. It's that she has no interest in anything else but the arts, particularly drawing. She's one of those uninterested genius types. Far smarter than me, or you, or pretty much anyone in this family." Coral said. Annabelle sighed, "Oh. I... I see." The two of them reached the end of the corridor and entered Lily Avalon's room. The second most glaring thing about Lily Avalon's room is that it's very simple in structure. She kept only a bed, a dresser, an artist's table, and a television set inside it... not especially the most exciting things to find in a young girl's room... normally. The most glaring thing about Lily Avalon's room was the mural. Starting from the door and wrapping all around the room, Lily Avalon had taken an age old tradition in a child's artistic evolution, drawing on walls, and taken it to the next level. Lily's mural was the one important feature in her otherwise bland and uninteresting room, though Coral notices something. "You changed it." Coral said, a certain degree of surprise in his tone of voice. "I was bored." Lily said, simply, twirlling part of her hair in her finger in boredom as she said it. She was still dressed the same as she was when she met Coral and Annabelle at the front of the home, only now she had followed Annabelle's lead and put her hair in pigtails as well, much to Annabelle's surprise and delight. Annabelle was fascinated by the mural immediately, taking closer looks at bits and pieces of it that interested her. Coral, looking around, looked at his little sister, "Does it still tell a story? The images seem... happier than last time." "Yes, bonehead, it still tells a story. What'd be the point of a mural if it didn't?" Lily rhetorically asked. Annabelle saw one image that struck her as odd. It struck her as odd because it seemed out of place given Lily's personality. She turned to Lily, "Miss Lily... why is there a wrestling ring in your mural?" Lily blinked, before she realized what Annabelle was pointing at, "Oh. That." Now Coral was curious, "Is this a mural about me?" Lily ignored Coral, but only for a second to talk to Annabelle, "See what I mean about him being self-absorbed?" Lily then turned to Coral, "Yes, dumbass, it's about you." Coral seemed... hurt, "You didn't have to throw in the 'dumbass' part... just a 'yes' would've worked." Lily crossed her arms, looked away, and gave him a "hmph!". She then walked over to Annabelle, still admiring the artwork, and spoke to her, "I'll tell you, but only you, the story." "Just... just me?" Annabelle whispered. "I don't want your idiot boyfriend to hear it from me." Lily whispered. "O-okay..." Annabelle asked. Lily nodded, and then turned to Coral and said very bluntly, "Leave." "Huh?" Coral asked. "Begone. Depart. Ascond yourself from my room. Get out." Lily said. All while she's doing this, she pushed Coral until he was out of the room, and then she shut the door on him, locking it behind him. Lily placed her back on the door and looked to Annabelle, who was still admiring the mural. Lily approached Annabelle, tapped her shoulder, and then pointed at the door, "It actually starts there." "Oh." Annabelle said. "This man... it's not Coral... it's... um... Boral. Yeah. Boral..." Lily said, unconvincingly, "...he grew up as a fan of wrestling. So much so that he abandoned a lot of things that could've benefitted his life to pursue his stupid dream. He gave up his family, he gave up his wealth... he gave up pretty much everything just to get hit on the head a lot. He didn't care about the family fortune, all he cared about was fulfilling a life's dream. So, he gets there, and he finds nothing but heartache and sorrow. He finds that the dream he wanted to live in was just that, a dream. The people he drags into his web, he's forced to keep them away from it in an effort to avoid getting them hurt in the process. His family... they no longer want anything to do with him at all, they just pretend as if he never existed. And so, he goes through life, fighting his battles, growing more and more disillusioned with his life..." ... "And then?" Annabelle asked, very concerned for the outcome of this story. However, Lily sighed. "And then... I ran out of wall." Lily lamented. ... "Oh." Annabelle said. There was another awkward pause. "Um... the door?" Annabelle asked, noticing that the door was not painted over. "I didn't know how to end the story." Lily Avalon said, with a sulk and a sigh, "But I think I might have an idea now, if I were to include you in it." "Me?" Annabelle asked. "That girl that Coral was with the last time I saw him, Allsion... they didn't match. I think you two stand a better chance." Lily said, "You don't see his flaws as crippling to your relationship, you see his good parts along with his bad and you admire him for being human, rather than being perfect." "Um, yes... that's... that's about right." Annabelle said. "You'd be a light in his life, someone who guides him through his trials. He's clearly more confident with himself now than he was when last I saw him, and I think you had a lot to do with it. You're someone who gives him strength." Lily said. Annabelle thought about it, but smiled. "I didn't think you'd be so profound." "Well, it's not like I do anything other than draw and think of things like this." Lily said. She then walked over to the door, and turned back to Annabelle, "Take care of my idiot brother, though. Unlike me, he's too dumb to be left unattended to for very long." Annabelle smiled. "I'll try." Lily opened the door to find that Coral had been trying to listen in on the conversation. Lily sighed, tried to surpress her anger, found that she couldn't, and then promptly slapped Coral in the face again. Coral, turned away from Lily, could only say, "Ow."
