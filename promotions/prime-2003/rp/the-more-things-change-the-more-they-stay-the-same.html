The More Things Change...The More They Stay the Same

Date: Read it January 9th, 1996 Christian told me on that night I would become a man. I hopped off the back of his motorcycle and the two of us stood in the driveway staring up at a light brown house. Superficially it looked like just another house, but that was if all the history was stripped away. This house had taken on urban legend status ever since the mid 80s when a deli store owner was beaten to his grave inside for failing to comply with the orders of the owner. For the last decade the underground of North Carolina WAS this house. The bikers inside were of a breed that the 1920s popularized, so frightening that even some of the police were working for them. They formed the group known as Death. The front door opened and I was allowed to enter. The temperature inside of the house was freezing, much like that outside. Christian gave a courtesy nod to the large tattooed man sitting on the couch before continuing past the living room. I did my best not to make eye contact with anything that would cause me to gawk. However, that hope was extinguished when my left eye caught a large bookshelf where a random collection of items were on display. On a couple of them I saw remnants of dry blood, and the dinner I had earlier almost hit my throat. Christian tapped me on the chest and gave me a look that moved my feet even though I wanted to know more about the items. Christian then slammed his knuckles twice against a blue door. After a couple of seconds we both stepped into a small interrogation room. Two fellow bikers greeted Christian with a salute while I scanned the room with my eyes. This had to be the room where people met their untimely demise. The walls were covered with a shade of red that made me consider this to be a prison. A victim gets to see all the blood, figure out the stories, and then experience their own drops of history. A quiet muffle turned my attention to the two bikers, more specifically to what was between them. I saw a balding man in his 40s with crimson running down the front of his face, struggling to escape from the chair that he was tied down to. One of the other members of Death laid a fist into his temple, silencing his potential liberation. An aura of evil overtook Christian’s voice when he spoke the next words. "Devin, this is Mark Sexton…Say Hi, Mark." Mark muffled something underneath the bandana that none of us could make out. Christian smacked him hard across the face for his inaudible tone. "He is the man that you will kill tonight." The entire room went silent. Smiles crept across the three members of Death. Mark’s eyes became as big as saucers. My entire body froze. ------------------------------ July 29th, 2007 Tonight will be a testament to my manhood, and a necessary step if I want to continue living on this Earth. I received a tip from Christian that the Braintrust, a group that wished for me to kill Tony Gamble, was displeased that my mission had yet to be complete. I was kidnapped seven plus months ago and conformed to their demands to kill Gamble in exchange for my own life. Christian also told me that they were going to send Nathan Murray, a core member of their group, to kill me. Apparently they had found someone who could get the job done faster and my services were no longer required. So I have to get to Nathan first. For thirty long minutes I have waited here in the parking lot across from the strip club, listening to whatever song comes on 96 Rock. I am rather impatient, since I know damn well what the repercussions are if someone spots me. The headlines would be horrendous. I check over my shoulder to make sure that no cars have pulled in behind me. Nope. I’m solid there. My right hand grasps a pair of binoculars in the passenger seat and I look for people on the rooftop. I bet you there is someone there waiting on me to make a move so they can put me down…Alright, not there, he’s probably just wait- Nathan! There he is! That piece of shit! Come here! I flip the hood of my jacket over my head and don my sunglasses. It is now or never. My right foot slams down on the accelerator, with all of the activity that goes on in the area Nathan does not even think to look in my direction. I fly across the parking lot, past the narrow street, and slam on the breaks mere feet from him. This time his senses perk up when my car prevents him from getting to his own. With lightning fast feet I jump out of the car and run up to him. Once the pipe is raised over my head, he knows what is coming. That doesn’t mean he can stop the blow from smacking him right between the eyes, dropping him like a sack of bricks. I can hear a loud scream about forty feet ahead of me. SHUT UP YOU STUPID BITCH! SHIT! SHIT! SHIT! MOVE IT! MOVE IT SOLDIER! I scoop Nathan up into my arms, power walk over to the back seat, release my right hand, open the door, and throw him in. After what feels like an eternity with that macabre scream decimating my ear drum, I back the car up and punch it away from the strip club. HURRY! HURRY! HURRY! BEFORE SHE GETS THE LICENSE PLATE! SCREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH ---------------------------------- January 9th, 1996 What are you supposed to say, at seventeen years of age, when someone just asked you to end another man’s life? I had slashed my father to bits in a moment of rage that was fifteen years in the making, but this guy? "You…You want me to kill him?" I said with fear flying out of my voice like it was on sale. "No, Devin I don’t WANT you to kill him…You HAVE to kill him." Christian replied, still dwelling in that domineering role, a side he had never shown around me before at home. "Why?" I decided to put my hands in my pocket to avoid any of the Death gang seeing my fingers trembling. "Well," Christian said, now walking around Mark in a circle while Mark’s eyes were following every step, "You remember robbing the Mason’s place, the huge mansion up on Lane? Hell, who wouldn’t, you cleaned that place out in twenty minutes. Anyway, at the time you were cutting through the yards to get back to the getaway car, Mark over here decided that it would the absolute PERFECT time to get a midnight snack. I guess that’s what you get when your wife can’t cook for shit and you have to pretend to enjoy the food. So he’s up in his kitchen with the light turned on, slapping some mayo onto his sammich, when all of a sudden out of the corner of his eye he spots someone with two pillow cases bolting across his neighbor’s yard. His mind takes a second to figure out what he just saw, and once he does, he begins to take notes about what you look like. Mark then makes the stupidest mistake of his life by deciding to go outside the house to get a description of the car. Unfortunately for Sherlock over here, since the Mason joint was your biggest score ever, I decided to have a couple people watching our asses at crucial points…The getaway being one of those. So my people knock him out, drag him to the casa, and here we are some twenty hours later." The three bikers in the room looked over at Mark and shook their heads while tsk’ing him. Through the bandana in his mouth, Mark attempted to express his thoughts. A chuckle from Christian emerged in response. "Oh look, I think he wants to say something. What wisdom are you going to enlighten us now? No, wait, lemme guess, you are going to say that you never meant for this to happen, you are sorry, you won’t do it again, blah, blah, blah, blah. Ah well I need the humor." Christian yanked the bandana out of Mark’s mouth. "Please, don’t this to me! I won’t tell anybody! I’ve got a wife and three kids!" Mark shouted, pleading for his life as he knew the seconds were ticking away. Christian rolled his eyes into the back of his head before he delivered a hard elbow on the top of Mark’s head, knocking him back into a state of semi-unconsciousness. "Why in the hell do people think that argument is going to work? Jesus Christ on a goddamn pogo stick, do you know who you are dealing with here? I don’t give a fuck if you’ve got ten wives and forty kids! Do you know how many people have come into this room and walked out alive? One and he was a fucking fed who owes me HUGE! You think that a state worker like you is going to have enough pull to make us put you on a leashed garrote?" I watched as Christian backed up away from Mark and stood with his confidants on the other side of the room. If there was a chainsaw nearby, I don't think that the tension in the room could have been pierced a minute bit. Then, without warning, all three of them burst out into hysterical laughter. Mark looked over at them, probably envisioning a pack of bloodthirsty hyenas. The walls were getting pounded viciously, an indication that they all legitimately found this to be hilarious. "State worker…And he…HAHA! He thinks he’s going to live…Oh dude, help me I’m about to die here." Christian said, wiping the tears from underneath his eyes. "HIYOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO" One of the nameless bikers shouted in delight. Christian somehow found the ability to continue speaking after delivering the most tasteless joke I had ever heard. "Devin, Devin, give me a rimshot on the table for that one…HAHAHAHA!" Doing as I was told, I walked over to the table, smacked it twice, paused, and then hit it a third time. Christian leaned up against the wall and was still chucking every now and then for about the next thirty seconds. "Ah man, you seriously should have gone into stand up with the routine you are putting out right now…" A loud SMACK then echoed throughout the room. "Unfortunately for you, Mark, laughter is not the best medicine in this scenario. You crossed a line with us and we’re not about to let you slide. You have sinned and now we are going to give you fair repentance. Devin, make it happen." Christian extended the gun to me. Would that be the moment that would change the rest of my life as I knew it? No, I had to say something for morality’s sake. "Is this really the road that we have to travel down? I mean the guy said that he wouldn’t tell anybody and we’ve already beaten him enough." The two nameless bikers shot a glare in my direction, but Christian held his hand up to back them up a couple of steps. He put the gun back behind his back. I deduced that he had foreseen that the moment would manifest itself. A huge sigh followed before Christian went into another one of his speeches. "There are times, Devin, when we all have to do things that don’t make sense when we do them. You might not even be able to understand this for the next couple of years, or hell you might never be able to understand it. Just know this one piece of information…If you get caught, it’s all over for you. Nobody will ever look at you the same. You won’t get the same opportunities. Society is going to deem you a reject, an outcast, an untouchable, you get where I’m going with this? There will always be a glass ceiling over your head, one that you can’t ever break. Say you are rehabilitated all you want, that you’ve changed, but once they ask you the question "Hey, weren’t you in prison for such and such?" then immediately the put on their goggles and evaluate you based off of that. If you let Marky Mark over here go, then that is what you’ll become. Do you want that?" Submissively I shook my head no, that I don’t want to become that. Christian gave a nod of his head and handed me the 22 caliber pistol. That time I clutched it into my hands. I did my best not to make eye contact with Mark, but I could tell by the noises he was trying to fight his way out now. His last shot of redemption had just faded away into dust particles. I also heard a couple of muffled laughs from Christian, pretty much sealing Mark’s fate. I looked down at the gun and started to wonder how such a small device could create such widespread chaos once a small little trigger was pulled. A bullet was just barely bigger than an eraser and yet it came out of there so fast. I continued to be amazed by the power of the weapon while the other activities in the room continued. "You got any last words there boy?" Christian said while buttoning up Mark’s shirt and making sure that all of the wrinkles were out of it. He even went so far as to adjust the man’s glasses to make them look perfect. "YOU ARE NEVER GOING TO GET AWAY WITH THIS!" Mark shouted at the top of his lungs, hoping that someone outside could hear him and get help. Christian shoved the bandana back into his mouth. "Of course we will. You really think that your wife and kids are going to believe that you got yourself knee deep with a biker gang? Heh, now even they aren’t THAT stupid. Alright, come on Devin, let’s do this." I took a hold of the gun and pointed it at Mark’s head. ---------------------------------- July 29th, 2007 Did I lock the door? Did I lock it? I walk over and grasp the handle, pulling the door forward to make sure that it doesn't come unlocked. Good. Good. No problem there. What about the window? Oh hell, if I didn't lock that then someone might hear me. I flip open the curtain and check the lock on the window. Good, very secure, very secure. My eyes scan outside to make sure that nobody is there, giving the house a suspicious look. To the right...I see nothing, to the left...Also nothing. What about that guy across the street, I know that son of a bitch tries to get a look at Sun Tzu while we are having sex. I push open the glass door and step outside. I cover my forehead with my hands to ensure the sun doesn't block my view. Ok, that son of a bitch is probably watching someone tanning topless from his other window. Wait...What if someone up the street is watching me...Alright come out slowly. Come on...Move the head over the fence...Not too much...Nobody. Alright, let's do this. While walking back into the house, I scan behind the backyard to ensure that nobody is eyeballing me from that direction....We're good there also. I shut the door behind me and focus on my subject, Nathan Murray, who is tied up in a chair with a bandana lodged inside of his mouth. Look at him. A man who was once so powerful and mighty has been reduced to a blubbering mess. You once held all the cards on me Nathan, BUT NOW I'VE GOT THE GODDAMN ACE UP MY SLEEVE! My feet start to make a circle around Nathan, his eyes are tracking my every move while trying to think of a way out of this...But I'm not going to let him. There will be no deal made now. I stop in front of him and remove the bandana from his mouth. He seems really eager to talk so I’ll give the little shit a couple words. "You don’t want to do this" Nathan says, now trying to get into his best car salesman pitch. "Yeah, you are right." I respond, once again circling him with my arms behind my back "Because when someone knocks another person out with a pipe, kidnaps them, brings them to a location that they have never been before, and proceeds to tie them up…THOSE AREN’T THE WARNING SIGNS OF DANGER!" …Someone had to hear that. I’m going to leave this pathetic piece of trash to cry for a minute to check out the window. My head swings out from behind the curtain, I open the door and leap up to get my head over the fence to check for intrigued civilians…Negative on that front. Good cover work Suddenly another avenue creeps into my mind…Motherfuckers. Oh I bet you the fucking CIA has a satellite on me. They are working with the Braintrust and when I least expect it…Fucking kabow, they’ll drop me like a bad habit. I’m not going to let them… Alright, calm the fuck down you idiot, you need to get this done. If you don’t then you will end up missing a grand opportunity to have one on the opposition. Nathan Murray is going to be the prime example of what happens when you mess with the wrong man. I dash back into the house, shut the door, and smack Nathan hard in the face to calm his nerves. "Stop trying to pull this innocence bullshit with me you prick!" I shout, having had it with all of their mind games. What in the hell do they expect someone to do who has their life hanging over their head? "I know who you are and you know who I am. You are one of those rapists who kidnapped me and wanted me to do the dirty work for your company. However, if I cannot complete the job in a specific amount of time, you guys get worried and decide to kill me. What the hell? Do you think I’m going to get cold feet and walk out? I’ve escalated this slowly, hell I even took the calculated risk of meeting him outside of the arenas. I went right up to him and his slut and told them exactly what I think of both of them. You should have been there, oh man it was spectacular. The fear in his eyes, the fear in her eyes, it looks almost kinda like…" I stop to look down at my victim: The sweat, the fear, the angst, the regret, and the horror. "You" I give a sick smile toward Nathan before dropping down to the floor Indian style and staring at him. I look over the restraints one more time to make sure that he doesn’t have the possibility of escaping. "Here’s a little riddle for you Nathan…Four people are inside of a restaurant discussing a high priority matter. Now, these people are the crème de la crème, at least in their own subjective eyes, so they believe that nobody is going to snitch on them. Hell, they own the restaurant so why the hell should someone, right? Well they get to talking about a very public man, where he works, his upcoming flight schedule, and then they say that they are going to keep very close tabs on him. I believe the words like white on motherfucking rice were used. All four of them leave condescendingly without checking to see who all is in the restaurant…This sound familiar to you Nathan?" Nathan painfully shuts his eyes and is forced to remember the conversation since he was a part of it. I just sit there and stare at him like I’m in awe, waiting for his reaction. He gives a poignant "Ah, ah, ah, hiyooooooo, congratulations you got me, you want me to bring in Ed McMahon also?" "I’m not exactly a hard person to find Nathan. People know where I live, they know who I am, and they can approach me in the street because to them I’m just the funny communist guy with the emo haircut, who people call a fag…" I now allow my inner desire of evil to roam free in my tone while standing up. "But they don’t know this side of me Nathan. They don’t get to see what I’m really capable of once all the doors are shut. You all underestimated me and for that you are going to have to pay. I’m not going to be your fucking lap dog any longer." Nathan lets out a loud sigh and stares into my eyes. I can see that he’s still trying to negotiate his release by giving me a look of defeat, perhaps he’s trying to get my guard to drop just for a second. I better check those restraints again to see if he’s got anything loose. "Alright, just tell me this…You can even consider this my death wish…How? You don’t have the time to follow me around long enough to get my routine. Who is helping you with this?" I stop in the midst of checking to give him a glare of perplexity. I know that he did not just ask me to give up my sources…Did he…Really… "AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!" I cannot contain myself any longer. I drop down to my knees and eventually start rolling around on the floor, my howling probably reaching across state lines. "HAHAHAHAHA! Oh God…AHAHAHAHAHA! Dude, you kill me, seriously. AHAHAHA…You are making my throat hurt over here." I stand up, but I can’t say for how long. My head is about to start hurting that shit was so funny. I lean up against the wall with my back to Nathan, hitting it in intervals of two seconds while still trying to condense the hilarity of the situation… Suddenly my eyes become really big and the rage starts to flow through my veins. This fucker just asked me to give up my assistance. Does he think I have no integrity? I swing around and slap the taste out of his mouth, his face becoming dark red after the shot. "DO YOU THINK I’M THAT FUCKING STUPID!?" I shout to him while he is trying to shake the cobwebs loose. "Where in the hell is your head at right now? You know what, don’t answer that, I don’t want you to…De…I can’t believe you! Even in your last moments on this planet you are trying to fucking be the manipulator instead of the one being manipulated. I’m not going to allow you to have that kind of freedom…In fact…FUCK YOU! I’M NOT GOING TO LET YOU LIVE ANOTHER SECOND FOR ASKING SUCH STUPID SHIT!" Doors…Windows…Someone could have heard that! You don’t know unless you check again! One final time I decide to pop open the door and check outside. Being this paranoid and having OCD probably doesn’t help my life out, but it sure makes me feel a hell of a lot better. One check over the fence…We’re good. Run around to the backyard…Nobody’s there. I walk back into the house and give Nathan a smirk like that of the man who taught me that it was alright to do what I had to do, just so long as I didn’t get caught. Here we go… "You got any last words there…boy?" I ask, making sure that Nathan looks picture perfect in his moment before death. A moment of docile tranquility seems to come over Nathan. "Just know this one thing Shakur…Even if you kill me and the others at my level…You’ll never know who is behind all of this." I take a hold of the gun and point it at Nathan’s head. ---------------------------------- It is like I’m living déjà vu. I cannot deduce which scene holds a difference because they are both one in the same at this point. I watch as he comes to the realization that this is the end of the road. The gun cocks back. I can see his life flashing before my eyes. His birth His first steps that he is watching on film while his mother smiles with glee. The school days flying by with the culmination of throwing his cap in the air at college graduation Purchasing his first home Marrying his lovely wife Having his first child, the smile on his face is touching. The second and third are even more special. His first legitimate job working for a big organization His first taste of power His first time fucking the sweetness that is greed at my expense And then I can see my own eyes in his, they are cold, remorseless, and final. Him focusing on that picture in his bedroom where his wife and kids are as close as they can be to him BOOM! The head slumps The life flat lines
