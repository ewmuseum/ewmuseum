Nightmares and Daydreams - Intermission

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November Monday, November 17, 2008 “Seriously hun, just by watching this market update I'm losing money.” With a few investments in his own name and others under his companies, Killean had to watch the market updates. He was more inclined to do so over Vegas, who hated the markets with all of his existence. He actually never believed in expanding into such a gamble but Killean had played the stock game many times in the past with some great success. He talked Vegas into certain investments, not without a complaint from his partner. As Killean worked his way down the list based on the ticker, the phone rang. He was a little too immersed in his work and Jenny knew it. “I got it Kill!” She yelled from the other room. Killean was shaking his head, noticing the downturn in most of his investments and blamed it purely on the turn in the economy. He was happy though because besides losing money in stocks, he had money put away in rock solid savings plans, so losing some in the markets was not the end of the world. Just as he finished the last of his checks, he heard something hit the hardwood floor. Not paying much attention, he half-heartedly asked if everything was ok. When he didn't get a response after a few seconds, he dropped his pen and paper on the coffee table and made his way towards the bedroom. As soon as he got near the door, he could see the cordless phone on the floor, the battery cover off of it and the battery across the room. Jenny sitting on the edge of the bed, shaking her head with a look of disbelief etched on her face. “Hey, you ok? Looks as if you've seen a ghost.” Killean observed as he sat on the bed beside Jenny, putting an arm around her. But Jenny jumped from the bed and began to tear at the front of her business suit in an unorthodox manner. She unbuttoned it and ripped it from her frame, leaving only her blouse to cover her top. She tossed the jacket across the room and then turned to pound her fist against the wall. “Am I ok?! No, I'm not ok!” exclaimed the redhead. “Ok, I'm not a mind reader here. Talk to me.” “I've been laid off! Apparently, twenty-five people from my department have been let go and as good as I am at my work, I don't have the qualification like half of them!” “Hey, it's ok. We ca...” “No Killean! We can't!” Jenny seemed to over react a bit as she dropped herself back on the bed. “Come on Kill, I love working and I would go mad if I didn't work. They said perhaps in the future a few people might be asked back but I shouldn't hold my breath! I'm not a house maker, you know that. Meanwhile there are no jobs out there in my field and with the others also looking, I'm fucked!” Jenny didn't blatantly swear often and when she did, she was dead serious. Killean knew this, but he certainly didn't see this reaction coming from her. “Look, I've got a wrestleback match this week at ReV, but I can stay here if you want me to. We could come up with something together for you.” In the end, nothing mattered to Killean more than family and friends. If he was willing to stay home and not show up for a PRIME show, certainly friend or family problems had something to do with it. But Jenny really wasn't all there right now, going a little overboard way too early. Heck, the phone hit the floor only a moment or two earlier. “No Kill, I couldn't possibly ask you to stay home for this. I may be out of work, but both of us don't need to worry about this. I do.” She argued, even though Killean really didn't want to listen. He just saw the break down ten seconds earlier, yet she wanted him to think she could handle it. He couldn't be fooled, but he thought it better to let her figure things out on her own. “Alright, but if you need me, you know I'll be there.” He finished his statement and gave her a quick hug around the shoulders. Getting up for the bed, he headed for the door. “You're... not leaving now are you?” She said, almost sounding needy. “Nah, but I do have to go to the club and settle up some bills with Dave. I don't fly out until tomorrow remember? Anyways, Viv will be here in a couple minutes so just relax. She'll look after Dizzy” He turned to go down the hall to see Desiree in her room. “I'll be back this afternoon” The only thing he didn't like about coming back in the afternoon, he really didn't know what he'd find after the morning events. |~o~| Killean opened the door to the back of the club. The hinges creaked, usually notifying everyone inside someone had arrived. While he expected to be greeted with Dave's deep voice, he spoke first anyways. “Hey man, check this out... someone wants to give the business a loan for more renovations. Like we need any more.” Killean's head rose from the letter and noticed that the room was devoid of life. Instead, he saw two broken windows, a desk in pieces on the floor and a chair lodged into the drywall. That was just the serious damages. The countless documents and paperwork on the floor would take a week to clean and organize. “Perhaps we might need it after all...” Sirrajin observed the room, noticing there was no forced entry at the door and nothing specific had been thrown through the windows. In fact, upon closer inspection, it seemed as if the damage came from the inside originally. “What the hell happened here?! Dave! Mac! Where you at?” He dropped the envelopes and papers from the mail on his seemingly untouched desk as he headed for the club. He pushed the door open to see Mac behind the bar, wiping up some fluids from the counter while Terri, one of the waitresses, was sweeping up some glass only a few feet away. “Mac, what the fuck happened here?!” Killean's voice displayed anger, but he really didn't mean it that way. Instead he was somewhat shocked and worried after not seeing hide nor hair of Vegas. “He went nuts boss, tossed a filing cabinet out the window.” “Who did?” “Dave.” Terri replied, walking in front of the two men to dump the glass into the trash. “Ok, so why would he do such a thing? I'm not a mind reader here.” “To be honest boss, I dunno. He mentioned something about money going missing and problems at home and took off out the back door. He tossed this bottle across the bar and almost hit me with it.” “Well thank christ you're ok... both of you. But his car is still in the lot.” “Would you have chased after him? Did you SEE what he did to his desk? I wasn't about to try. What he did after he left I don't know.” “Ok granted. He probably went home. Call someone to get the office put back together. Most of those contractors we used for the renos before. They know me and I'll settle up with them later for payment. Tell them I'll add a bit extra for some urgent attendance.” “You bet.” “And I'll get the plumber on the phone too.” Terri said, flipping open her own cell phone. “Terri, I've told you before, you don't need to use your personal phone for busi... wait... what's wrong with the plumbing?” “Didn't you see the john boss?” Mac questioned, rinsing out the bar cloth he was using to clean the mess. “No and I don't want to. Reach me on my cell if you need me.” “You got it boss.” Killean left the main room of the club and carefully worked his way around the debris in the office. Vegas didn't live far away so it was certainly the first place he was going to look. |~o~| Killean walked up the steps in front of the large condo and rang the doorbell. Fully expecting an enraged Vegas to answer the door based on what he did to the office, he was disappointed yet relieved that Hanna answered the door instead. “Killean, thank god. Is Dave with you?” “Huh? I was hoping he was here. He left the club in ruins.” “I tried to talk him out of it, but he went crazy on the phone.” Hanna explained as she leaned against the door frame, clearly looking disturbed regarding the unknown status of her husband. “He said something about us going broke and losing everything. I couldn't understand much of it so I tried to reach you at home.” “Must've just missed me then. But where the hell could he be?” Killean thought for a moment, clearly not coming up with an answer of his own. Hanna however, suddenly came up with the solution. “He's not answering his phone and whenever he wants time alone, he usually goes for a walk down by the riverfront.” “There's snow on the ground. What would he go there for?” “Snow never stopped him before Killean.” “Alright, grab your purse. I'll meet you in the car.” Hanna nodded her head and shut the door to grab her things. Killean made his way down the steps and approached his car. He paused, looking at the tall buildings downtown, trying to get a grip on everything that had happened in such a short period of time. First Jenny and now Dave. It was a morning of mishaps indeed. |~o~| The Hemi Charger growled as it crawled to a stop just on the edge of the river walk down by the new riverfront housing development. The gravel crackled under the tires as the light snow fell from the cloudy sky. The car came to a full stop and Killean placed it in park, shutting off the car as both he and Hanna observed Vegas sitting on a ledge by the water, draining the last of a bottle of vodka before tossing it into the river. He then produced a whole new bottle from beside him, something unseen to the two of them in the vehicle. “Hanna, you'd better stay here. If there's anything I've learned to do in the years I've known him, it's how to deal with him when he's drunk.” Killean said, opening the car door and slowly stepping outside. “Alright, but be careful. He doesn't seem like he knows what he's doing.” Killean shut the door and made slow steps over to where Dave stood, apparently talking to himself between swigs from the big bottle. He didn't get too close at first, instead playing it safe and trying to be humorous. “Smooth man. Public drunkenness and littering... don't you remember when I had to bail you out in San Diego for being drunk in public? Cost me a pretty penny!” He smiled, but Vegas didn't turn to face him. Instead he expelled an inebriated laugh. “HAH! Good then, cause ya know, we're fuckin' broooooke now. You'd have to foot it, cause we're mutha fuckin' broooooooke duder.” Vegas certainly didn't hesitate to lift the bottle to his lips once again after the diarrhea of the mouth stopped. He took a good couple gulps before offering the bottle to the man who was practically his brother. Blood or not, Killean and Vegas had been there for each other every time something went down and nothing was going to change. Killean turned down the offer of the bottle though. “What the heck you talkin' about Dave? What's all this about money and being broke now? We have a lot of it put away, some in the business.” “Grrryeah we di-did. Used to. Had it. You know, now we don't!” Vegas attempted to say in the middle of a drunken stupor. He took the time to toss the half full bottle of vodka to the ground, smashing it and showering the cement in alcohol. “All the investments and all the damn money in the company is gone... missing... kaaaaaaa-PUT! Hahahaha!” Dave laughed heartily and slipped off his stoop and stumbled along the pavement. Killean dropped down quick to help keep him steady, signaling to Hanna to get the car door open. “Easy there pal. Gotta sober you up so I can figure out what the hell you're talking about.” He lead Dave to the car, opening his cell afterwards to call up Mac at the club to put a large pot of coffee on. |~o~| Two Hours Later... “You heard me Kill. The investments have disappeared!” Vegas said, practically yelling after taking a large sip of coffee. He had done a fine job sobering up but now he was mad again. Not a good exchange. “I got a call today, after checking into things, more than half the investments have plain as day disappeared and another portion has sunk so low it's irrelevant. Also, I got a second call about some charitable donations we made and apparently the charities didn't get the money. It's gone. I tied up all my cash in it all and now, we're dead as day fuckin' broke!” “How?!” Killean started. “How in the hell could this happen? Things were going good. Can't we get someone involved?” “No can do man. Spoke to some advisors and since a good chunk of the funds were tied up in American ends, the powers that be here have reason to believe this is a case for the Yanks. Their hands are tied and so are ours! It's bullshit!” “But what does this all mean for the club?” Mac said, worried that the loss of Killean and Vegas' money would mean the ultimate loss for him... his job. “I don't wanna go back to another garbage job. I love this place.” “Don't worry Rich. The club is staying where it is. I'll just have to sink some of my own money into the place until we find out just what the hell happened.” Killean said, shaking his head. Financial problems were a headache, hence his savings plans for multiple years. He didn't waste, he just always wanted to be comfortable getting what he needed day to day without issues. “Kill man, there's something else. I was counting on some of that money coming to maturity soon. I tossed a little too much into things and now I have nothing. No money until our salaries are paid through the business and I have the final payments on my cars and condo to make. If I don't make them...” Dave sighed, not wanting to imagine losing what he worked so hard for. The crunch in the financial sector of the economy everywhere meant problems for all. “One that money came to maturity, it was gonna replace our savings, pay everything off and have more than enough left over to take Hanna away for our anniversary. I had everything planned and now it's disappearing.” Speaking of Vegas' wife, Hanna walked in from the club area and wiped her hands with a clean cloth. “There, the club itself is clean and ready for opening tonight and I'm glad to see you guys got off your behinds for a few minutes to clean up the papers and toss that broken desk back in the lane for garbage pickup.” She stuck her tongue out at Killean and smiled, sitting beside her husband. “Dare I ask Davey hun? How bad is it?” “It's pretty b-” “Things will be fine Hanna.” Killean interrupted. Vegas thought for a moment that Killean was trying to give Hanna a false sense of security and nodded his head sarcastically while mouthing the words 'Oh Yeah, just fine'. “Lots of money has disappeared... illegally. Something will have to be done and things may be a little tight around the club for a while. Until then, I'll just have to jack some money into the club myself. It'll all come back to me over time, but at least it'll keep everything a float.” “Dave, what about the house? The cars? Weren't we gonna get some of that money back to pay the final bills?” “I gotcha guys.” This time, Sirrajin spoke before Dave even had a chance to reply. “All the plans you had and all the arrangements, consider it done by me. I'm not about to let my friends fall into problems. You're practically family to me, so the condo, the cars and the other plans will be taken care of.” “Dude come on, that's gonna cost you a fortune! I can't have you doing that.” “No way am I letting this go down like it is. I'm gonna pay it all and it looks like I might just have to stick around here to help out a bit more. We have papers to organize and forms to fill out. We need to get something done about this.” He flipped open his phone and dialed his home to talk to Jenny but unfortunately all the phone did was ring, eventually going to the voice mail. “Yeah it's me. I'm not going to the show this week, problems in paradise at the club that I'll explain more when I come home. You must be busy with Desiree so I'll see you in a bit.” Killean closed the flip on his phone and returned it to the belt clip. “Alright Mac, what'd the guys say about this office?” Vegas said as he leaned back in his chair, finally looking somewhat relaxed. “They'll be here this afternoon and will work this evening. Should be good as new in a day or two.” “Good.” Killean replied. “Once this office is fixed up, we'll get started on these papers. I gotta head by home and I'll be back tonight.” “Later boss.” Mac said before making his way back into the club. Once the door closed, Killean got up from his chair and started for the back door, but was cut off by Dave and Hanna standing in front of him. “Hey man, you know I don't ever want to do this right?” “Don't mention it.” “No, I'm gonna mention it. You're like my brother man and if it wasn't for you, I really don't know where we'd be. You've saved us before and you're doing it again.” Dave said, sounding more sincere than he's ever sounded. Hanna however had a problem spitting out the words. “We have tried so hard only for something like this to happen. Really we wouldn't be here now if it wasn't for you.” She said, tears falling from her eyes as Killean leaned over to give her a hug. “Guys, it's what family does for family and as far as I'm concerned, you're not only best friends, but family too.” He broke the hug from Hanna and shook Dave's hand, giving him a quick half hug as well. “Things will be tight for a while, but we'll get by this and move on together. I'll be here, count on it.” Dave nodded and Hanna did too. Killean excused himself and opened the back door of the club, stepping out into the cool winter air. Vegas and Hanna hugged each other as the door shut, returning the warmth to the room. |~o~| Jenny What am I going to do? I can work, I can prove my worth but more qualified people are out there. Killean's going to leave me I know it. He'll think I can't pull my own weight, that I'm a screw up. I don't want that. I don't want to be another Stacey or Charity. I want to be me, but I just don't feel like me since getting that call. I'm having chills as I sit here on the bed writing this. I bought this journal a year ago and this is my first time cracking the cover. My hair is a tangled mess from destructive brushing, knotting ends of it up instead of removing them. I feel empty inside, like these last few years of work have meant nothing in the end. Why did I do it all? What does it mean? I feel like putting my foot through the wall, creating a hole in the drywall much like the hole left in my having my career ripped out from under me. I know I'm not the only one, but it was just a few months earlier the company said layoffs would be a last resort. Now here I am. Killean said he would stay home for me, but that would just give him more time to make me feel bad, to tell me that I need to work to have a meaningful life. He said at one time I could quit and stay home if I wanted, but he really didn't mean that. Stacey was a mooch and so was Charity and I don't want to be them. I heard enough about them when I asked Killean. I shouldn't have ever asked. I've got to be honest with myself. I'm a failure. Even now as I sit her, the phone was ringing and I really didn't even feel like picking it up. But I can't let Killean know I'm feeling this way. Luckily Desiree is a self-sufficient kid for the most part. She's been getting her own fruit cups or good snacks. Meals will be easy enough I hope and she usually takes a nap on her own in the afternoon without being told to. She's a good kid, hardly deserving of a failure like me looking after her. But I've got to hide my feelings. I've got to act happy. But I've got to see the doctor. I'm already feeling depressed and it's only going to get worse. Then Killean would surely get rid of me. I feel useless... helpless... powerless to guide my life.
