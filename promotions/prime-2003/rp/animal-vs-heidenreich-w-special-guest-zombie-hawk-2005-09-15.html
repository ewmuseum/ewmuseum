Animal vs. Heidenreich w/Special Guest Zombie Hawk

15 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 15th, 2005 WHAAAAAT A RUUUUUSH! The sound of the Legion of Doom theme resonates throughout the packed arena, prompting the arrival of Road Warrior Animal, much to the delight of the crowd. JR: BYGAWD THIS IS SURE TO BE A SLOBBERKNOCKER HERE TONIGHT BYGAWD!!! King: Puppies! Raising his arms into the air, Animal slowly walks toward the ring, taking his time…for he knows what lies ahead. Garcia: The following match is set for one fall, with a thirty-second time limit! Introducing first, already in the ring…ROAD WARRIOR ANIMAL!!! HEIDEN…HEIDENREICH….HEIDEN….HEIDENREICH! Garcia: And his opponent…ROAD WARRIOR HEIDENREICH!!! Pumping his fist as he marches down the ramp, the man who was originally supposed to be a cryogenically frozen Nazi from the 40s, who just so happens to wrestle in the future, wears the spiked shoulder pads with pride as he nears the ring. However, just as he’s about to reach the apron, he’s attacked from behind by a pale-skinned creature, who immediately tears the shoulder pads from Heidenreich’s shoulders as the big man escapes into the safety of the ring. Coach: Heidenreich and Animal will have to watch their backs in that ring, because Road Warrior Hawk is the special guest zombie for tonight! Looking at the shoulder pads as if vaguely remembering them, Hawk tries to wear them once again, but only manages to get them wrapped around his neck as they hang lopsided off him. A few fans make the mistake of laughing at the ‘silliness’ before them. They’re promptly devoured. JR: BYGAWD THIS SHOULD BE ONE HELL OF A SLOBBERKNOCKER BYGAWD!!! Inside the ring, the bell is rung and the timer begins counting down. Immediately, Heidenreich hits a hurricanrana on Animal, sending him across the ring. But Animal rolls with the momentum, jumps onto the middle rope, and comes back with a flying crossbody block on Heidenreich. Everyone watches in amazement. Hawk eats a kid. Coach: What an amazing athletic display by both men, and we’re not used to seeing moves from those two. What a treat this is! JR: BYGAWD BUY MY BARBECUE SAUCE BYGAWD!!! Rolling back with Animal’s crossbody, Heidenreich pops to his feet and promptly lands the swinging sidewalk slam, only for “No Chance” to start playing through the speakers. Vince: Stop the match! Stop the damn match! This isn’t what I wanted, dammit! I’m Vince McMahon, dammit! You don’t cross the boss, and I’m the boss…dammit! King: Puppies! JR: BYGAWD VINCE MCMAHON BYGAWD!!! Vince: This is awful, dammit! What do you two think you’re doing, putting on a match worthy of airtime? Dammit, I’m Vince McMahon, dammit! I didn’t get rich off giving the fans what they want, dammit! In the ring, both Heidenreich and Animal look ashamed of themselves for doing more than a suplex and maybe a hip toss. Vince: Don’t give me that ‘I only did steroids for eight years, please don’t fire me, Vince…ULTIMATE WARRIOR needs this job’ look, dammit! You two…YOU’RE FIRED!!! Everyone looks at the two in the ring, hanging their heads, then back to Vince. Vince: …Dammit! Just then, Road Warrior Hawk corpses his way into the ring, attacking Heidenreich from behind. After disposing of the Nazi General, Hawk turns his decaying rage of his former partner. Animal: Please, man…don’t do this. I have a family…I needed to come back and work, please…Vince made me do i-ahhhh!!! Grabbing him by the neck, Hawk begins munching down on his former partner. But in Animal’s dying moments, he’s able to end the curse of Hawk, breaking the zombie’s neck as all three men lie in the middle of the ring. Meanwhile, Vince has already made his way to ringside and is just now entering the ring. JR: BYGAWD VINCE MCMAHON IS URINATING ON THREE SUPERSTARS BYGAWD!!! King: Penis! Coach: That’s it, I quit. Throwing his headset down in disgust, Jonathon Coachman stands and starts walking away, while Vince continues to literally take a piss on all three men, rather than just figuratively as usual. Sitting up in bed, James Farwell breathes heavily as he slowly reaches beside himself and grabs the TV remote. Turning it on, he switches to the channel for RAW, his breath coming quicker as his heart races in his chest. This channel has been locked out. Farwell: Thank God…
