Writers Block = Spiritual Constipation

12 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/12/05 You can hear the ice melt in the dark diet soda that sits on Hoyt’s desk. The glass sweats almost as much as Hoyt does as condensation builds on its sides. Last night the air condition went out and the Greek repairman won’t be able to fix it until tomorrow. Hoyt does his best to stay cool sitting in his underwear sitting at his computer trying to come up with some new material. Writes block or as Hoyt calls it “spiritual constipation” is not a good thing when you make your living speaking. Especially when you’re on a quest to become the best your business has to offer. The last few weeks Hoyt realizes his lines have been repetitive and sub par. But it’s not easy to come up with new bits and jokes when the heat doesn’t work and you have spiritual constipation. However despite these less then perfect working conditions Hoyt knows he has to produce today. Mary lies on the bed with the green-eyed cat reading the latest in Biblical Wigs Weekly. Well more so Mary reading as the cat licks her own asshole in an almost political statement. Her dedication to the job makes it almost seem commendable. Hoyt Williams: You think the cat is trying to tell me something? Mary: Huh? Hoyt Williams: The cat. She’s licking her asshole and it’s distracting me. Mary: Are you jealous? Hoyt Williams: No, I’ve tasted your cooking and it’s close enough. Mary shakes her head at the insult turning her attention back to the dogma doo of the week. The cat is repulsed by the fact that her asshole could even be compared to the garbage that Mary cooks so she jets out of the room with out looking back. Hoyt turns his attention to a more pressing matter which is the blank screen in front of him. Hoyt Williams: Have I mentioned that Killean is boring? Mary: A few hundred times. Hoyt Williams: Oh. Well we’re going to be in New York perhaps I can call them all sinners. Mary: Sinners that’s very creative dear. Maybe you could make them do jumping jack too. Hoyt Williams: May the good lord bless your sinister soul. Mary: Well what do you want me to say honey, you’re out materiel. You can only call Aimz a whore, Killean boring, Furher a name changer, Deville a crook, and Nova a hippie so many times. Hoyt Williams: I never called Nova a hippie. Mary: Well he is. Hoyt Williams: I KNOW HE IS I’M JUST SAYING I NEVER CALLED HIM THAT. Jesus Christ. Mary: He never ran out of materiel. Hoyt Williams: Fluck no, when the going got tough he went to boarding school and died. Plus he had a writing staff of twelve apostles I got you and the mean eyed cat. Hoyt sits up in his chair and places his fingers over the keyboard with his wrist limp like they teach you in school. He was always a studious kid even if his grades never reflected it. He was one of those guys who could do no work yet pass every test with flying colors. That and when the going got though the bullshit started flowing. Too bad writing new material wasn’t as easy as a test. Mary: What about if you did something amazing this week that didn’t take a lot of talk. Hoyt Williams: Like what? Mary: I don’t know like walk on water or something like that. Hoyt Williams: We’re going to be in New York these people are not impressed by walking on water. They want something that would blow their mind. Something big city not some small Middle Eastern parlor trick. Jesus had it easy he wasn’t an American. Mary: Well you better figure something out you’re two weeks away from the match you can’t be constipated for. Hoyt Williams: You don’t think that’s on top of my worry list?? You don’t think that’s probably the cause of this spiritual constipation?? You don’t think I spend every waking hour worried about blowing my big break and destroying my career. For God’s sake I’m taking on the Legend Of Sleepy Canada watching the microwave pop popcorn is more interesting. If I lose to this putz my career is OVER. Mary: Don’t be suck a drama queen. Right on queue the cat jumps back on the bed taking her throne next to Mary. Hoyt ponders the statement but comes to the conclusion that he is right and that he must win Tuesday night and then again at the Pay-Per-View. Both pressure and heat are not good laxatives for the stuffed up mind of a writer. Hoyt Williams: Did I ever tell you about the time I met the guy who invented the light up yo-yo? Mary: THE WHAT? Hoyt Williams: You know, those yo-yos that light up when they go down. They tend to be red on one side and blue on the other. Very popular at carnivals and fairs. Mary: I hate yo-yos. Hoyt Williams: Probably because you never had the one that lights up. They add a whole new perspective and excitement to the yo-yoing community. Mary: There is a yo-yo community? Hoyt Williams: Oh yea don’t you remember the Smoother brothers? Yo-yo’s used to be a national phenomena to bad simple like that doesn’t still rule the world. Mary: I hate yo-yo’s even the light up ones. Hoyt Williams: Well anyways I know the guy who invented them. Mary: What’s your point? Hoyt Williams: My point? What do you mean what is my point??? I thought I would share that with you. Although Mary wasn’t a fan of the yo-yo the cat did seem to be listening to Hoyt talk about the stringed toy. Most cats enjoy toys that involve tuna or strings. But her fascination with the yo-yo argument, which yo-yoed between the two humans, didn’t last, as her asshole once again became her focus. Mary: It was a very interesting story. Let me get this straight you have no material going into New York facing the same guy one week before you face him in the main event of PRIME’S biggest show of the year and you’re telling me stories about some guy who invented the light up yo-yo? Are you kidding me? Get your ass in gear or look for a new profession and by the look of that blank screen and knowing your crappy spelling we can surly cross writer off the list. Hoyt Williams: Thank you for your support. Mary: Type. Hoyt again stares at the blank screen this time attempting to start. He cracks his knuckles and begins to hunt and peck out a few lines. He smiles as he really starts to get into it. The keyboard almost gives off a musical serenade. Hoyt Williams: I GOT IT. New York is full of foreigners like Killean. New York smells like urine just like the Mexicans. Who eat Mexican food just like the blacks. The blacks steal things just like the sinners who all smoke pot just like Nova. Mary: Ok? But you’re not facing Nova. Hoyt Williams: These idiots are too stupid to understand half my material they tend not even notice who I’m messing with. Oh and I heard Hessian quit so I can take credit for running his ass out PRIME. Mary: Damn right you did. Hoyt Williams: Damn right I did. Then I’ll make some outrageous claims about screwing Killean’s dead wife up in heaven. Hell maybe I even will. Mary: Maybe you won’t. You’re my man even in heaven. Hoyt Williams: Ok but I can claim to bang her. Then we can go to her gravesite dig it up and steal her wedding ring. Since Kill sold my belt it only seems fair. Mary: Betty White is coming to stay with us this week. Hoyt Williams: BETTY WHITE? From the Golden Girls? That same Betty White? Why?? Mary: She’s a big fan and donated a lot of money to the RISP so I invited her. Hoyt Williams: WAIT? You bitch at me for telling you a story about the guy who invented the light up yo-yo yet you invite Betty White to stay with us the week I’m going to fight for the Universal Title? Are you NUTS? Mary: She’s a big supporter. Hoyt Williams: Well her ass is coming with us to get that damn ring. Mary: You’re going to take Betty White grave robbing with you? Hoyt Williams: Damn right I have things t get done. She’s not bringing her colored husband Barry White is she? Mary: No, he just has the same name as her they are not married. Hoyt Williams: Thank God. Mary: I told you she’s a member of the RISP. Hoyt Williams: Right. Leave me be while I finish typing up the speech to give during my segment this week. The cat closes her eyes which seems fitting as this glimpse into the life of Hoyt Williams comes to an end. One more match and one more segment until the biggest match of his career. What has been said and what will be done shall be talked about. Who wins and walks away with the title will always be remembered. It’s on and only God and Seb’s knows how it will turn out but the Turmoil match should be a good indication. =w=
