Exes and Ohs

2 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 2, 2008 “That’s it, man. Game over, man. Game over. What the fuck are we gonna do now? What’re we gonna do?” A thin smile cracks the otherwise contemplative expression of Tyler Rayne. A chink in his stony armor just wide enough to slip the lip of a beer bottle into. He absently pulls down a tug of the thick lager, almost oblivious to the sweet drain of the viscous alcohol. A shake of his head pulls determined brown eyes from Bill Paxton’s most iconic film role. He places the bottle back down on the coffee table, fingers sliding almost automatically from the glass neck to a stack of photos. He sifts through the various images of PRIME’s former Universal Champion with a grunt of disappointment. “Nothing.” “Nothing.” The echo comes from a plush leather chair to his left. Alexis Grant, hair pulled back in a tight bun, leans forward to take one of the pictures herself. Heavy, tired eyes graze across the image of Cozen, absorbing the details. Details not her own. A face that belongs to another woman entirely. A weary, frustrated sigh passes through the pursed lips of an FBI agent, flapping the edges of the picture. “What about the Autumn girl?” Alexis shakes her head. “Dead end.” The agent sighs. “She’s a fucking ghost, Ty. I can’t…nobody could find shit on this girl. Not the Fed. Not the Agency. No one. She just…doesn’t exist.” He nods in response. A slow, thoughtful gesture. His eyes do not drop from the capture in his hand. Do not falter from the flat, expressionless gaze of a non-existent entity staring back blankly from the picture. Another grunt of frustration. Short shake of the head and his eyes are back on Bill Paxton and Michael Biehn. A handful of seconds pass between Rayne and the downtrodden Marines stranded on LV-426. With a reluctant shrug he turns, casting a reassuring smile in his friend’s direction. She accepts with the corresponding thankful grin. “Forget it, Alex. We knew it was a long shot.” “I know…” Her gaze falls from him, casting back down to one of the pictures on the table. She plays at checking the status of her cuticles. “I just…hate losing.” “So do I.” A derisive laugh. “Not like you’ve been doing much of it lately. Put a hell of a beating on that Wade guy, from what I saw.” Now it’s his turn to stare absently into space. “Yeah. Suppose I did.” Another tug on the bottle. His eyes are unfocused. Spacey. Lost on something beyond the passing cars and Detroit streets outside the window. “Ah. Here. Maybe you’ll have better luck with this.” His hands sift through the photos again, shifting images of Cozen to the wayside in search of…yes. There. A manila envelope hidden beneath the photographs. He snatches the envelope in hand and passes it over to Alexis, who opens it immediately. More photos. “Who are they?” “Andreas and Siena van der Wal.” “Hmm. Related?” “Supposedly. …I think, anyway. What… can’t you see the family resemblance?” Here he offers that infamous smile. She tries not to melt under its allure. “Got anything on ‘em?” He shrugs. “Not much. They show up a few weeks back. Seem to be her handlers. Don’t strike me as the big boss types, though.” “Someone pulling their strings.” “Be my guess. These two are some shady fuckers. They’ve got that…edge. See it in their eyes. But they’re nothing more than glorified babysitters. Me? I want to know why someone sends their Number Twos out to keep an eye on little River Tam.” “They want to use her?” “Most likely. I need to know who they’re working for so I can find out why.” “Why not? You’ve seen how good she is first hand. Seems like a good enough reason to me.” “She’s also a pro wrestler. That brings a lot of attention. Girl does what Cozen does should be looking to draw as little attention as possible. So should someone wanting to hire her. There’s got to be something more to it than just the skills. Something… I dunno. She’s just too high profile. It doesn’t make sense to hire someone with that much pub.” “Doesn’t make sense to you.” He nods. “Anything new from Se7yn?” “Nah. Few lowlifes we might be able to pin on him. Similar scenarios, but nothing with his usual…flash. If it was him, he’s trying to stay low pro.” “He’s up to something. I know he is. Fuckin’ …” He stands, finishing the last of his beer in one, long swallow. “Cozen. Se7yn. This fuckin’ Scorpion bitch. Feels like everyone’s just a step or two ahead. Like they’re all taunting me. Fuckin’ with my head. It’s… gorram frustrating.” Now it’s her turn to offer a reassuring smile. “You’ll figure it out, Ty. You always do.” “Just afraid it won’t be soon enough.” The soft thump of footsteps brings their attention to the foyer. A handsome man approaches, stringy dark hair reaching down below his ears. He is clean shaven with bright, vibrant eyes and familiar features. His hands are covered in leather gauntlets. A well-sculpted body exposed beneath his fishnet top. “You still dress like a fuckin’ fairy, Nate.” “And you still act like one, Rayne. Got a visitor.” “Me?” “Yeah. Some chick. Hot.” Alexis rolls her eyes. “God knows why you keep getting visitors at my house, but—” “Come on now, Nate. Don’t be upset that me and Katt are the only ones bringing girls home nowadays.” The man lifts his right hand, flashing a middle finger proudly at Tyler Rayne. “Fuck.” The left hand. The left finger. “You.” “Hoyt knows someone needs to.” “Now now, boys…” Her voice is silky smooth. The kind of sensual that brings men to their knees. A time or two in the past, it’s done exactly that to Tyler. He stands, a dumbfounded look on his face as she enters the room. Short, dark hair that falls in waves around her shoulders. Skin tight jeans disappear beneath a pair of black riding boots that end just below the knee. She saunters toward him with supreme confidence. Her hands reach out from the short denim jacket covering a black halter, fingers rubbing across the thin layer of stubble on his cheek. Normally he’d be looking down at the opening in her halter, admiring an attractive, but not overflowing, amount of cleavage. With her, though, it’s damn near impossible to tear away from her eyes. Dark. Brooding. Absolutely drowning with passion. Deep red lips split into a seductive smile. “…hate to see you fighting over me.” He swallows hard. “Michelle.” Her hands tighten around his face in response. The boots add a few inches. She pulls him down the rest. Pulls him into her embrace. Thrusts her lips onto his. A shocked gasp opens his mouth. She wastes little time in slipping the tongue. For a second, he is astonished. Embarrassed. Almost abused. Then he remembers who he is. Tyler Rayne. So he does what he does best. He matches her greeting with one of his own. Responds in kind. Gives the woman what she wants. Alexis takes a few steps back, sidling up next to the handsome hunk of Gothic in the foyer. “Think they know each other?” The kiss is full of lust and passion and the familiarity of former lovers. It is hot and steamy and almost a little to intense to be watching. Alexis and Nathan both shift with slight discomfort before Michelle finally pulls herself away. “Been a long time, Ty.” CCCCCCRRRRRRAAAAAAACCCCCCCCCCCKKKKKKKKKKKKK!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The slap comes quick and violent. As passionate and intense as the kiss just a second before. His head snaps away, hand immediately rising to rub at the reddened and tender skin. The two onlookers exchange a mischievous grin. “Yep. Definitely know each other.” “Don’t you two have something better to do?” Nathan offers a devious smile to the 5-Star Chamipon. “Nope.” Tyler shakes his head. “So…long time, Shell.” “Eight months. You never returned my calls.” “Yeah. That. So…that’s what the uh…” He points from her to his cheek and back again. “Yeah. That.” “Okay. Deserved. How’d you find me?” She laughs condescendingly. “Please, Ty. PRIME rolls into Detroit, and you don’t think I’ll know to look at Katt’s house for you. Didn’t realize she let her brother live here, too, though.” “Hey. This is my house, sweetheart. She lives with me. And how’d you know she—” Michelle smiles. “You have the same chin.” Tyler holds up both hands between the two, stopping the exchange before bringing his attention back to the woman. And the soreness in his jaw. “Okay. So…point. But why with the visit?” She shrugs. “You’re a complete asshole, Tyler Rayne.” An agreeing nod from the onlookers. “But somehow I can’t seem to let you go. Why is that, do you think?” He shrugs. “I’m a good fuck?” Alexis nods, a smile spreading across her face. Nathan Wylde offers her a sidelong glance, which slowly brings the nod to a stop. “There is that. Look, I can’t stay long. The girls are waiting on me. Need to rehearse. We’ve got a show tonight at The Covenant.” Tyler stiffens, his hand involuntarily balling to a fist. Michelle drags a card from her pocket. “My new number. And our hotel. Call me up if you’re not busy. Our tours happen to coincide for the next month or so.” “Do they now?” She offers him a coy smile. “Quite a coincidence, huh?” “Indeed.” “Call me.” She takes a step forward, bringing their bodies just centimeters apart. She leans up. Forward. Breasts pressing against his arm. Breath a whisper in his ear. “I’ve missed you.” She drops to the floor, offering one last seductive smile before striding out the way she came. Tyler stares blankly after her, his dumbfounded gaze falling to the card in his hand. Nathan waits until the front door echoes back to them before speaking. “So that was Michelle Chase?” “Yeah.” “Fuckin’ A.” “Yeah.” “Katt’s, uh… Katt’s not gonna like this.” “Like what?” “The, um, Michelle… thing. Sis has got quite the hard-on for you and that other girl.” “What other girl?” “That Troy girl.” “Fuck Troy.” “That’s what she’s hoping.” “Look, me and Troy are…” “Are?” “It doesn’t fucking matter. If it’s going to piss her off that much, then Katt just doesn’t need to know.” Vested in the exchange, no one happened to notice the Dark Angel slipping in the back door. Sliding into the foyer in the middle of the discussion. “I don’t need to know what, exactly?” Well…shit.
