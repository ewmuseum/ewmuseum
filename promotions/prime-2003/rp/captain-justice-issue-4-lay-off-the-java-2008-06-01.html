Captain Justice, Issue #4: Lay off the Java

1 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-1-08 The time was creeping up on 3am. The place, some 24-hour diner called the Dunk Tank out in the middle of one of Detroit’s seedier neighborhoods. With few bodies scattered across the diner, all was quiet around Dead Time. What the Dunk Tank could not offer in flashy gimmicks or witty commercials like Denny’s and IHOP, it desperately tried to make up for with what employees described as "nostalgia." Various works of famous artists -- more appropriately, badly-created knock-offs -- littered the walls, notably one of the corners rocking one of the best Norman Rockwells ever imitated created. In a corner to himself, Soren Knox was quickly learning that the price of fame was a costly one as moments like this where one could declare "Time out!!" were becoming rarities. Paperwork, public appearances, TV shows, and steel chair shots were beginning to take their toll on his brain, so when the opportunity presented itself to be free of Sonny Silver’s never-ending business regimen, if just for a moment, the respite was cherished. Beyond being a world-class competitor contending on one of professional wrestling’s grandest stages, Soren considered himself a connoisseur of all things java. He loved him some fresh brew in the morning. Not any of those overpriced all-style-no-substance Starbucks drinks, but a nice little cup of Joe from some little Mom-and-Pop shop or some Folgers from the grocery store; the GOOD stuff. So when the elderly waitress presented the Dunk Tank’s finest offering in its signature "Coffee Black Attack" Soren was ready to declare what many internet nerds and WOW fans referred to as an epic fail. "Compliments of the house, stranger," the raspy voice muttered as she fought back an attack of Smoker’s Cough. The shit-colored concoction sloshing around within the unwashed mug nearly made his stomach turn. A second glance revealed the Dunk Tank’s most famous drink barely qualified as something even remotely comparable to fecal matter. Soren offered up a weak smile, but the reciprocation wasn’t much more appealing than the contents of his mug. The hue of her curled grin matched that of a five-pound tub of margarine. Thankfully, she turned away and went about her business, leaving Soren to his own devices in a particularly dim section of the diner. Fighting back the urge to toss the contents of his mug over his shoulder, Knox lamented his choice of locale as he inspected his surroundings more closely. The dying fluorescent light above his head taunted him with cries of "FIX ME!" during each blink. He took a whiff of air and quietly surmised that this place had seen as many cans of air freshener as it had patrons – very little in number. Ducking in here wasn’t the wisest move Soren had ever made, but it was almost the perfect haven from all things PRIME-related. The key word in the aforementioned sentence being "almost" as another non-regular to the Dunk Tank wasn’t fooled by Knox’s civilian attire or choice of unnatural atmosphere. "What’s the matter?" A voice taunted in his ear. "The big PRIME superstar finally letting the world of glitz and glamour get to him?" Soren shifted his neck to the source of the ridicule. His eyes bulged from the sockets as one of the last people he was expecting to see was here in the flesh, staring back at him underneath a tattered denim jacket and Mariners ball cap. "Teegan?" Knox cried in shock as the disgraced former student of Sonny Silver positioned himself in the booth opposite him, brandishing his own cup of Joe. "What the hell are you doing HERE?" "Hey, keep it down," Tim raised a hand, inspecting the diner to make sure no other eyes were fixated on them. "If I wanted to really come here and ruffle some feathers, I’d have gone all postal worker on you." "I don’t know," Soren jabbed. "Following me across the country and stalking me in a diner pretty much qualifies as," Overused air quotes. "’Going all postal worker on me.’ You want another shiner coming your way or you gonna tell me what the fuck you’re doing here, following me around like this was Misery?" "Listen, Rockhead," Teegan snapped back. "You can’t afford getting into trouble so soon into your tenure and I bet the last thing that PRIME wants to hear about is how some muscle-bound idiot went crazy and punched out a fellow patron. Us folk are already in a lot of hot water as is and I don’t even OWN any copies of the Bible." "You mean my kind. I don’t go around like a little lapdog looking for scraps at the master’s table, then go shoot up drugs when nobody’s looking because I’m too much of a selfish prick to bust their ASS off for an opportuni-" "Stop!" Teegan’s voice became an unkind whisper to hint that people were staring. "Look, I didn’t come here to play Dirty Dozens with you. I have a reason for being here." "Fine," Soren conceded before repeating his previous inquiry. "Now, how did you find me and why the fuck did you follow me here?" Tim said nothing, merely pulling a scrap of paper from his jeans pocket. It was a flyer he printed off the internet for the upcoming ReVolution 161 in Detroit where PRIME’s very own Captain Justice would headline the show against fellow PRIME gladiators in the two-time Universal Champion Lindsay Troy and the 2006 Jewel in the Crown winner Tony "The Grin" Gamble. "Wasn’t that hard, but I spent the last of my fucking money just to get a ticket out here," Tim explained. "Trailed you around town. You and that idiot, Silver, were very hard to keep up with, but let it be said I am tenacious, if nothing else." "You’re a stalker, now?" "No," Tim bluntly stated. "I’m here to warn you about what’s gonna happen if you allow Sonny Silver to keep latching onto you like a leech." "First off, you confuse tenacity for insanity," Soren spat venom at him, landing perfectly on Tim’s cheek. "You’ve got a lot of nerve following me here after that shit you pulled, throwing out this warning bullshit. Insulting the guy that took you under his wing, making Silver Lining Wrestling and the Gym look like a laughingstock and trying to deny it when you were CAUGHT red-handed. Sonny RAISED us to steer clear of any of this drug-peddling nonsense that’s made guys like us have to work THAT much harder in a world where we’re scrutinized as it is. You were just mad you got caught with the smoking gun and now you’re here to take it out on me and Sonny, peddling some sob stor-" "The hell I am!" Tim harshly spat back in another hushed tone, countering Soren’s poison with that of his own. "I busted my ass for THREE years in that school! I was one of Sonny’s top students when you were still pushing crap at Puget Sound Pipe. Man, for somebody with all these muscles, it's a shame the only weak one you have is the one between your ears." "Press be damned. Give me one good reason why I shouldn’t reach over this table and beat your scrawny ass right here, right now?" "Because you’re stupid, that’s why. You were sweet-talked into this little PRIME contract and thrown into this first HUGE match against Xavier Kannon, not knowing that Sonny Silver is poison to whatever he touches. You and I are both more than well-aware of the reputation he has going on when it comes to playing in the big leagues. You’ve heard the stories. How he abused his own family to get the Universal Title. How he was hated by PRIME, how they only keep him around for ratings and nothing more. How he screwed over EVERYBODY that’s worked with him, even that Troy woman you’re off to face next week." "Gee, Tim," Soren drowned the restaurant in a thundering deluge of sarcasm. "Oh, those God-awful rumors! Surely, that’s only half the story. He’s got the bodies of all those people he hates in his basement and he has a big creature Joss Whedon cooked up severing the heads. He showed me himself." He let out a dry scoff. "Besides, if he’s the King of Shitheads you're painting him out to be, why’d you spend so much time burying your head up his ass?" "Naivety." Knox sat there, not uttering a thing as he choked down some of his brew. He allowed that one word to be sucked into his brain before rising to Tim’s bait. "You think I’m naïve?" Soren asked him as his eyes locked with those of a gruff-looking gentlemen seated on a stool across the room who’d been watching the two chatterboxes go at it. He shook his head in disgust at the pair while Teegan paid him no mind. Soren continued. "You think poor old Soren’s being taken for a ride, that Sonny’s gonna siphon all his money out of his contract from under him, that right? Well, got news for you, Teeg: I DID spend some time in business college before I decided that wasn’t where my heart was at. I DID, however learn enough to know I understand every last little dot of the "I" and cross of the "T" on that contract and there’s nowhere that says he’s taking money from me. In fact, he only asked for five percent. He doesn’t need my money." "You’re correct. He doesn’t need your money. All he needs is a doey-eyed kid with a little glimmer of potential to mold into his own image. He needs somebody to avenge everything wrong he did with his career. He’s a parent living vicariously through his son and he’s using you to fulfill whatever sick, obsessed fantasy is floating around in that deathtrap he calls his business mind and you don’t know what ends he’ll go to in order to keep his new breadwinner on his side." Tim nearly jumped out of his skin when Soren’s response to his tirade was met with boisterous laughter. "That was great, Tim. Probably worked that whole spiel up on the plane ride over, huh?" Soren sat up, relieving his wallet of a couple bills by tossing them on the table. He extended a finger in Teegan’s direction. "Storytime was great and all, but this bitterness act isn’t very becoming. If you want, I’ll fork over some cash so you can get home, but I don’t ever want to see your ass around me, Silver, PRIME, or anything associated with my family. Peace." "You’re a puppet, Knox!" Soren stopped in his tracks as he could feel the cold dig blast him smack-dab in the back of the head. His hands wrapped tightly around his wallet, ready to tear the leather in his steel grip. Instantly, his head snapped back to Tim still seated at the booth, curling his lips into a downtrodden frown. "I tried doing this the nice way, but it’s not getting us anywhere," he admitted. "I hate the fact that you’re walking around with fame I busted my ass for. It burns me every time I see Captain Justice on TV. That should be ME getting top billing, facing off with people in the league of Tony Gamble or Lindsay Troy… I hate your fame, but it’s not worth this… not worth you getting screwed… Soren, you need to know the truth." With a hard swallow and a dry throat, Tim looked Soren in the face, man-to-man. "Sonny… that no-good fucker set me up. I don’t know how, yet, but he did. I overheard him talking to some woman named Isabelle and she was blackmailing him for cash. Besides... How long have you known me and how long have you seen the work I’ve done for this gym? First to go, last to leave, all times I ran drills, got along with everybody, this shit happens and suddenly, I’m the black sheep. Nobody wants anything to do with me because I’m sure, somewhere, that asshole blacklisted me. No feds are hiring me because I don’t ‘fit the bill’ apparently, even though they’ve got TWELVE scrawny kids like me running around doing loop-the-loops. It’s bullshit. He bent me over the table and fucked me hard and if you don’t watch your ass, he’ll get you, too." The colorful colloquialism aside, Tim felt he made his point. He wanted to stop there and pray it’d be enough, but now that he had Soren’s attention for just this moment, he was going to make the absolute most of it. "UltraViolence… you beat Xavier Kannon. Biggest win of your career right?" Soren, deciding that he would carry on with a degree of tact just a little longer, remained seated. "You better choose your next words carefully." "Did you even PAY attention to the Kannons during that match, because everyone else sure did. Sonny was messing with you more than once during that match." "I know." "Excuse me?" "I know what was going on," Soren restated. "I was a little pissed that Sonny didn’t tell me what was going on, but he laid it all to me afterwards. Isabelle... Xavier Kannon's sister... they were discussing business. She's a costume designer and Sonny paid her good cash for my UltraViolence costume." "And the match itself? How do you explain Sonny constantly fucking with you?" "He said the Kannons were blackmailing him with some shit involving the likes of something involving his daughter that wasn't true... huh... far be it from Xavier to pull some shit like that, but I heard of his PCW days. The guy practically handwrote the book on backstabbing. His cheating ass could sure write some stories." Tim shook his head, disappointed as he kept a firm hold on his cup. "That’s exactly my fucking point. Stories. That’s all that comes out of Sonny's mouth, Soren. I can't believe you're buying his shit... Soren, you need to fucking snap out of it. He’ll keep spoon-feeding you this bullshit until your eyes turn brown. He'll keep his sleight of hand over you and you’ll just keep being brainwas-" "Stop." Teegan finally looked upward and saw Soren stand to his full height, demonstrating the tell-tale signs of a man possessed: the clenched fists, grinding teeth, the beet-red face. He was ready to escalate this private conversation into a full-scale World War III. "You’re fucking pathetic!" Soren bellowed as he garnered the attention of the entire diner with his outburst. He picked up his jacket and slung it over his person, tugging at the collar fiercely. "I’m not some kind of simpleton who doesn’t know what’s going on. Nobody’s getting duped and the fact that you got busted is weighing on your head, making you fabricate this crap. You’re just trying to bring me down with you when quite frankly, it’s the saddest thing you’ve ever done. Don't you dare put me in your fucking class. Besides, here’s my question… you have any proof that Sonny’s been doing these things? Something? Anything?" Tim bit his inner cheek and lowered his head against the table, giving Soren his answer. "I didn’t think so." Soren dropped a trio of hundreds on the table in front of his ex-fellow student, emptying such hot breath from his nostrils, it singed Tim just to be near it. "Take it, you scrub. Buy yourself some decent clothes, take a bus back home, choke on a shitty breakfast, do whatever the hell you want with it. If you come near me, Sonny, of anything having to do with PRIME at large, I’ll see to it your ass gets arrested, then you’ll REALLY need to worry about getting bent over." Without another word, Soren spun on his heel and exited the diner, leaving Tim to hang his head He didn't even bother watching his former friend push open both doors and observe his subsequent departure into the night. All he could do was sit there and contemplate how this one last shot at redemption devolved into him sitting here, alone in some squalid hellhole. No one to rely on anymore, no fake Sonny Silver playing nice by picking him up off the street like when he was lost at bus stop, offering him some REAL coff… Oh, shit. The coffee. A fist grinding through the wood of the table ensured that all eyes in the Dunk Tank remained drawn to him, but Tim didn't register a thing beyond his next acidic assertion. "That FUCKER."
