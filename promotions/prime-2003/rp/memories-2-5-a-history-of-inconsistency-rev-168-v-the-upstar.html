Memories [2.5]: A History Of Inconsistency (Rev 168 v. the Upstarts)

Date: Time remains ever perplexed. The trouble with Mega Job wasn't so much that they were a trio of troublemakers designed to cause as much trouble as possible. Rather, the trouble was that the entire lot of them were pathological liars when it came to their previous history. In fact, a lot of their history was mired in lies, inconsistencies, and outright fabrications. If we were to believe the stories spun by Mega Job themsleves, as well as the rumors and half-truths perpetuated by the wrestlers they often worked with, then their history would look a lot like this. * . * The History Of Beef Beef the Slightly Annoyed was born Prince Beef of the Barbarino Family of the Soverign Kingdom of Booshyaba. The future king of a great country, Beef was known for his valor in combat and his skill in not only his swordmanship, but also with the ladies. He was arranged to be married to the lovely Libby McCheese, daughter of Duke McCheese, a glorious magistrate from far away. He lived life without a care in the world, until one day, a terrible dragon came roaring in from the south. Known as the terrible Lindzbeast, it ravaged the lands, the people, and even Prince Beef's lovely castle. As a final insult, the Lindzbeast kidnapped Lady Libby and dared Beef to follow her to her dark, twisted castle, known only as the Castle of Horrible Abominations. There, the mighty Prince Beef did do battle with the Lindzbeast, and defeated her. But just as he was about to rescue his fair maiden, the evil Lindzbeast played a cruel trick on Prince Beef. She stripped the Prince of his power and sent him spiralling through a portal into an alternate universe. There, he would be powerless and pathetic, forced to live out his remaining years as but a mere jobber and loser. And thus, the story of Beef the Slightly Annoyed began. * . * The History of El Janito El Janito the Mexican Stereotype was born Janito Garza in Tijuana. The son of a well-known drug runner in the area, Janito became a capo in his mafia army and helped the horde large amounts of cocaine across the border. He was said to be the go-to guy for getting out of sticky situations, once shooting members of a rival gang using stylized gunplay he saw in a movie once. He lived life without a care in the world, until one day, an Interpol spy was found to be working in the Garza family. Their latest deal was going to get them caught. Realizing that the family was going to be busted, Janito attempted to stage a daring rescue of his father, but failed. He managed to escape, flee the authorities, and stay out of sight for a few years. Having lost everything, the man who became El Janito decided that the best way to remain out of sight and yet maintain some sort of living was to simply become a professional wrestling jobber. So far, it's worked out pretty well. And thus, the story of El Janito the Mexican Stereotype began. * . * The History of Steve Steve the Rambling Communist was born out of the forehead of Chuck Norris. After a gestation period of exactly two days, Steve burst out of Chuck's forehead, which Chuck immediately healed and recovered from. By this point, Steve was already fully grown to his natural height of three feet and nine inches tall and could already speak intelligently, albeit, with only one word at a time. For a time, Steve travelled with Chuck Norris, learning all he needed to know to succeed in life. After having a falling out with his "father", Steve and Chuck Norris engaged in a violent, pan-dimensional karate fight that ended in a draw. After determining each other as equals, the two of them went their separate ways as true masters of the universe. Somehow, along the way, Steve came across a couple of pathetic losers and decided to train them in the ways of universe mastery so that one day, they would be like him and Chuck. So far, it hasn't gone according to his design. * . * Needless to say, all of these stories were bizarre, unrealistic, or simply untrue. And maybe that's the way it *should* be.
