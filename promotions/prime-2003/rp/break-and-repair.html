Break and Repair

Date: ReVolution 193 David\'s dark brown eyes shifted from side to side. His sisters had trapped him like hunters would do to a bear that they intended to kill. Andrea removed the jacket she wore and slung it across the back of the kitchen chair closest to her. Across from David sat Samantha whose eyes were fixated upon Andrea as she rolled her sleeves up. In the corner stood Sarah with her arms folded across her chest as she looked at David with a hint of indifference. Slowly, she gazed across the room and sighed. \"I\'m going to bed. You three have fun,\" she informed the trio and David looked over at his younger sister. \"Oh, yeah, thanks. I\'m sure we\'re going to have plenty of fun now that you bought the Wicked Witch of the West home for her flogging exercises,\" David responded dryly. A cute smile appeared on her face and with a raise of her eyebrows, Sarah felt vindicated while she walked out of the room. David felt the tension in the room as his head fell forward onto the kitchen table. This is what he had feared more than anything. \"Alright,\" David began as his voice was muffled from being pressed into the table. \"Who wants to go first in the \'let\'s talk about how horrible a person David is\' massacre?\" His question was met with silence as David lifted his head and glanced around the room. \"Come on Andrea, I know you\'ve been letting it build up for the past 21 years of your life.\" \"David,\" Andrea started as she sat down at the table. \"No one is here to tell you how bad of a person you are. Yet, we all need to talk about what has been done and the future. Because, the fact of the matter is--,\" David interjected as he raised his hand. \"Um, teacher. Sorry, but--,\" but before he had the chance to finish, David noticed Samantha had burned a hole through his skin with her eyes. David swallowed hard as he looked at Samantha and he saw that she was in no mood to mess around on this evening. \"Andrea was talking, David,\" she said with a short curt response. David nodded his head as he opened the floor back to Andrea. \"Like I was saying. The fact of the matter is that Dad is dead, David, and you\'re now the man of the house. However, you took Mom away from us. For months. You weren\'t answering our phone calls and wouldn\'t allow anyone to see Mom either. Don\'t speak like you love us, David, when you do something like that. For Heaven\'s mercy, don\'t pretend that you\'re doing us a favor by keeping Mom away from us because it\'s frankly one of the most cruel things you could do to any of us when we\'re struggling over here,\" Andrea railed on David as he just took it like a slave took a beating with a whip. David didn\'t fault her as most of her points were actually point on. That\'s what typically happened when you were a member of Mensa by the time you were 10 and had received a degree from Harvard Law in International Business before your 20th birthday. \"The only thing I guess I can really say,\" David started before his voice gave out on him. He grabbed the bottle of whiskey, but before he managed to pour the liquid into his glass, Samantha snatched it out of his grasp. \"Hey!\" \"Finish what you were going to say,\" Samantha told David and the tempered male just balled his fist up as if he was an infant. \"Alright, fine. I\'m sorry,\" David said in a sarcastic tone before he took the bottle back from Samantha and proceeded to pour the contents into his glass. Andrea just rolled her eyes as her left hand came upon her forehead and began massaging it at a slow pace. \"Yeah, sure you are. Just like you were sorry when you were sixteen years old and broke Derek Long\'s jaw during the High School Championship game. You\'re not sorry for things, David, because you don\'t feel them. At the end of the day, you feel right and vindicated for what you did,\" Andrea retorted and David found himself with no defense. The glass remained in his hand for a good minute before he felt the glass against his lips and the cold liquid escaped down his throat. \"That\'s what Mom wanted. She needed a chance to get away and clear her mind,\" David informed them, but Samantha shook her head. \"I\'ve heard that line for months now, David. Is that what Mom wanted or what you convinced her to do? Because, I\'ve seen the way you have her wrapped around your finger--,” but she didn’t get the chance to finish her statement as another voice entered the picture. At first, it was a simple clearing of the throat, and as everyone looked around they found the source. “Enough,” the matriarch of the Noble family stood in the threshold of the kitchen door and wore an evening robe with a sad look etched into her face. “I will not let this farce continue any longer. I won’t stand by and watch as the two of you dress down David for doing what he felt was right. Especially you, Andrea,” she continued as she walked into the kitchen and kissed Andrea on the top of the head as the 21-year-old closed her eyes in solace. “Hey, Mom,” Andrea responded with a warm tone to her voice. Rebecca moved around the table and wrapped her arm around Samantha. “Andrea, I haven’t seen you in quite some time, and we’re not going to count the funeral. So you’ll have to forgive me for stopping you two before you had the chance to riddle my only son with proverbial holes because I would like to spend time with my big-time lawyer of a daughter instead of having her belittle someone. Does that sound good?” “Yeah,” Andrea began. “It does sound good.” She came off very hesitant in her voice as her hands fumbled around on the table aimlessly and carelessly. Rebecca then glanced over at David. “Look, why don’t you get some rest? I heard you on the phone earlier and I know you have to work out. No sense in wasting your energy in a fight that’s not going to be solved in one night, right?” David simply nodded his head. He stood from his chair and began to walk towards the set of stairs that led to the upper half of the house when Andrea stopped him in his tracks. “David,” she started and he turned to look at his beautiful sister. “I love you.” David paused for the briefest of moments. “I love you too, Andrea. Good to have you around,” he told her and Andrea smiled. David then looked around at the room. “Good night everyone.” With those words, he disappeared from sight as he knew the gossip in the kitchen would go on for hours. At the end of the talk though, he knew that Andrea’s mind would hone back onto him with the intent to finish their interrupted fight. This time though, he would be ready for her. *** Morning in the Noble household tended to be a little more on the quiet side. Sarah stood in the crook of the kitchen between the sink and the oven with her eyes focused on the sun as it rose in the distance. Sleep filled her eyes while her frazzled hair sat uncomfortably on the top of her head in a messy bun. The smell of coffee filled the household ever so subtly, but she knew it was too early for anyone to actually care about waking up, even if the aroma smelled appetizing. She turned around to find David as he walked into the house and towards the kitchen. Drenched in sweat, David almost didn’t see his sister as he walked right by her. Sarah’s eyes turned into surprise from the complete disregard that David had shown her. “Hello?” she asked sarcastically and David spun around with a shock look on his face. “What the hell are you doing awake so early?” he asked her and Sarah shook her head as she pulled the coffee pot out and grabbed her mug. “Trying to wake up. Though, it looks like some of us decided to run in the New York Marathon this morning,” Sarah told him as she refereed to David’s hectic look. David looked at himself before he shrugged his shoulders and opened up one of the cabinet doors. As he pulled down a bottle of Vodka, his eyes returned to his younger sister who poured herself a cup of the coffee she had made. “Gotta stay in shape no matter what. People, they’re expecting big things out of me right now and I’ve got to perform.” David said through shallow breaths as he grabbed a glass from another cabinet and began to pour the Vodka into the glass. “Is Vodka part of your steady regiment?” she asked him and he looked at her with this uneasy look on his face. “What the hell are you talking about?” David asked her bluntly and Sarah’s faced showed an exasperated look. “Seriously? It’s like 7:30 in the morning and you’re pouring yourself a glass of Vodka just after you ran through a Chinese Sweat Shop. I don’t know what your trainer is telling you, but water is definitely the choice of drink after you do your Egyptian Pyramid Pulling exercises,” she said with this sarcastic attitude to her tone. It was something that David didn’t like one bit as he put the cap back onto his Vodka bottle and placed it back in the cabinet. He then turned and walked to the refrigerator before he pulled the door wide open. “Haven’t you ever heard of Orange Juice and Vodka?” he quizzed her and Sarah offered a short laugh. “Haven’t you ever heard of your liver dancing all over you face as it sweats out alcohol until you die?” she retorted and this time it was David who laughed at the absurdity of his sister. “Don’t you worry, I don’t have a problem. Just need something to keep my going,” he informed and Sarah rolled her eyes in return as she walked to the kitchen table with her coffee. On the table were a few pieces of toast on a plate which she eagerly picked one up and placed into her mouth. David followed behind her as he took a sip of his mixed drink and sat down across from his sister. “So, what the hell are you doing up so early?” “I volunteer,” she responded in this short tone. David’s eyes rose as he looked at Sarah with a multitude of responses in his arsenal. “Alright, Miss Attitude, since when did you start volunteering?” He asked her in a tone that fitted Sarah’s attitude this morning. She took a bite of her toast and waited for a moment as she finished it. “Hm, let’s see,” she began as her finger began to tap away at her chin. “It would have to be when I grew a set of boobs, my vagina started to bleed, and every guy in a ten mile radius of me wanted to stick it inside of me.” David’s eyes grew wide as he nearly choked on the piece of toast in his mouth. As he spat it out onto the ground, his gaze returned to his sister and noticed she hadn’t even missed a beat during her tirade against him. “Was all of that really necessary?” David asked her and she once again rolled her eyes at him. “Whatever, it’s not like you cared anyways,” Sarah mumbled under her breath though it was still loud enough for David to hear. David’s mood turned somber as he finished off his drink and his eyes looked inside the empty glass for a few moments. “I do care,” he said softly and Sarah shook her head at him and the anger had began to get to her. “Don’t even give me that crap, David. If you cared, you would’ve been around here. Instead, while you were gone, I grew up if you haven’t noticed,” she began to tear into him. David glanced up at his sister and didn’t show an ounce of emotion on his face. “You know, you come back here and expect everyone to just worship the ground you walk on. It doesn’t work that way. Everyone might’ve fawned all over you when you came back a few months ago, but I still didn’t forget how you acted like a jerk.” As David heard those words come out of her mouth, he had reached his breaking point. “Whoa, whoa, look here. I’m sorry that I found your little boyfriend at the time removing your shirt and trying to nail a homerun. Didn’t realize it was you at first and even when I did, your shirt still shouldn’t have been coming off! That little bitch deserved to get punch for thinking he could do anything to my sister,” he went off on her, his finger pointed straight at her face. “You lost that privilege of defending me when you walked out of here. I don’t need you defending me. I can defend myself,” she responded to him and he nodded his head. “I know,” he said softly which shocked even Sarah who felt the pain he was going through. “That’s the only thing I could think of the whole entire time I was gone. That you would grow up and not need me anymore. I loved being there to protect you and now… now I’ve lost that.” Sarah’s eyes began to well up with tears, but she pushed them back as she scooted to the chair next to David. She placed her hand on his shoulder and sat there for a moment. “Come on, I was just saying it out of anger. I don’t mean that. I want you around and you’re right, that guy was a jerk. I was going to kick his ass, but you beat me to the punch,” she confided in him and David showed her a smile. “Well, that’s good to know,” he told her and the two laughed together. “That doesn’t mean you’re off the hook though,” she told him and David nodded his head. “But, at the end of the day, I still want my brother around. These guys, they’re gutsy.” David’s eye cocked as he sat straight up. “Point them out to me and I’ll take care of them,” he told her and she held her hands up in defense. “Nah, I got them. Don’t you worry,” she told him as she jested. David looked at his empty glass and wished he had some more. “Want me to pour you some?” he asked her and Sarah shook her head. He stood up and walked over to the cabinet. “So, any of them actually get, you know, lucky?” David then pulled out the Vodka once again and Sarah paused. “They wish,” she told him and the two laughed again as the sun continued to rise in the distance, and brought hope to David that today would be the start of a new life. A new chance.
