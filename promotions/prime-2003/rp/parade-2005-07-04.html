Date: 7/4/05 This is a fucking nightmare. I’ve stepped outside my apartment building and into a red, white, and blue hell. Why do holidays have to have suck DIASTEROUS color schemes? Like Christmas with all it’s bright red and green shit. Or Valentine’s Day, when the world pukes pink. Or even worse, Halloween and Thanksgiving. Two… months… of… fucking… ORANGE! You’d think at least the queers could have a tastefully decorated celebration. But no, they trot out all those damn rainbows on Pride Day. Maybe I should get my dad to push for Bill Blass’s birthday to be a national holiday. Now THAT would be something to have a parade about. I would soooo much rather see dozens of hardbody model types in eggshell white double-breasted suits and lime cocktail dresses than the mobs of idiots dressed like George Washington and some fucking Uncle Sam on stilts. As I stand awed by the wave of nationalistic tackiness, I’m approached by a woman who can’t decide if she wants to project “hottie” or “soccer mom.” It’s Leah Barnette, reporter for “New York Minute,” a local cable show that specializes in “human interest stories.” You know, the type of material that says “if you actually find this shit interesting, then your life soooooo sucks. She got a crew with her and she’s coming right at me, flashing a smile that paid for a new set of clubs for her dentist. “Look who we got here enjoying the parade! Local celebrity Ellis Easton!” Local celebrity? You’re the local celebrity, bitch. I’m a star. I imagine choking Leah Barnette to death by shoving that microphone down her throat. The sexual imagery of it amuses me enough so I smile, which the cunt interprets as me being happy to see her. She gets right up next to me, something she soooo would not do if she had any idea what I was thinking. “So great to see you take the time out to show your patriotic spirit, Ellis!” “Oh… yeah. I love… Memorial Day, Leah.” “I believe you mean Independence Day, Ellis!” “Like… with Will Smith?” To her credit, Leah’s face keeps its mask of sincere glee. Maybe she’s on Ecstasy. Maybe she’ll share. She gives me a playful swat on the arm. “Oh! You’re such a big silly. Ellis!” Or maybe she’s just retarded. “I sure am, Leah.” Leah then does her best to emote depth. It doesn’t work. “Now, Ellis. I’m sure on a day like today, your thoughts, like the thoughts of many New Yorkers, are on the troops.” “They sure are, Leah.” Actually, my thoughts are on my pores. I was planning on getting and avocado and cucumber facial today along with a deep tissue massage at a new day spa that Debra Messing gushed about it in “People.” I mean, I made reservations, but how the hell am I going to get there with all this shit in the streets. God, they better not be closed for the holiday. I would sooooo be pissed. Who cares about the fucking Declaration of Independence? It’s already dried and wrinkled, I don’t want my skin getting that way. “So would you like to share them?” Oh, you know I would. I am soooo fucking SICK of hearing about the troops. How brave they are. How they’re defending our freedom. My sweet, toned ass. You know who most those guys are? They’re guys who couldn’t get into college. No talent, no skills, no future. Or they knocked up their high school sweetheart and Wal-Mart doesn’t pay enough to feed three. So what does Johnny Mullet do? He joins the Army. Not out of some red-blooded American patriotism running through his vein. Because of the paycheck. The Army will give him benefits. The Army will give him tuition money. So for the cost of braces and Freshman English 101, Johnny Mullet will shoot whoever the Army tells him to shoot. And to think people call ME a whore because I use my totally awesome bod to make money. Whatever. At least when a photographer barks orders at me, no one dies. Plus I can tell him to fuck himself and storm off to go grab a mango smoothie with being thrown in the brig. The troops. Stupid bastards. And because their loser asses got shipped off to get shot at in a desert thousands of miles away, I’m just supposed to think they’re heroes? “I think the troops are heroes, Leah. I mean, people see me in magazines and on TV and act like I’m something special. But they’re the ones that are special. Sure, I have a face that fashion agencies fought tooth and claw over, a physique like a Greek god but not one of the ones that like boys, more money than I know what to do with and I do a lot, a sense of style that’s evolved into an art form in itself, and I can make one phone call and three hot, young, up-and-coming model-slash-actresses will accompany me for dinner and they all put out on the first date… uh… what were we talking about again?” “The troops.” “Oh yeah. The troops. Love those guys.” I give a Sammy Sosa-style chest thump while looking into the camera. “Now I understand that your father is in the government, Ellis.” “Uh, yeah. He’s the senator from… ummmm… some state that starts with a vowel. Uganda? “I think Uganda is in Africa, Ellis.” “Oh… yeah. Africa. Live 8 and all that. Rock ‘n roll.” “From what I hear, the word is a lot of Congressmen are beginning to question the war in Iraq. Does your father speak to you about it?” “Well… not really, Leah. We tend to avoid our individual shop talk with each other. Y’know, he doesn’t bore me with stories about what Dick Cheney said about Howard Dean on Fox News and I don’t tell him what happened when Kate Moss took that ex-Libertines dude to the Viper Room…with a certain Johnny Depp in attendance.” “Ooooo… what happened?” “Not on the record, babe.” Leah giggles and gives me another weak swat. “Party pooper! Well, I hope you have a Happy Fourth of July, Ellis! Are you planning on watching some fireworks?” I smile at Leah like she’s Lindsay Lohan after her fourth peach schnapps. “Maybe I could SHOW you some fireworks, Leah!” In the worst job of acting this side of Aimz faking an orgasm for Lars Ulrich, Leah pretends to be shocked and swats me yet again. “Ellis! You’re so bad!” “Wouldn’t you like to know HOW bad I can be?” I give her a sly wink, like I mean it. Please. Like I would fuck a frigid bitch like this. Frostbite on my cock? No thanks. Before Leah can say anything, she sees her producer giving her the sign the wrap it up. “Wooo! It’s getting hot on this Fourth of July! I’m Leah Barnette and I’ve been talking to Ellis Easton! Thank you and Happy Independence Day, New York!” The camera stops rolling and Leah turns to say something to me. Too bad I’ve already started walking away. Hey, if there’s nothing in it for me, I’m gone. I take out my phone and try to remember the number to the spa. I soooo need that massage after all this shit. Hmmmm… don’t I have a match this week? Oh yeah, the Canadian Cunt and Oscar the Grouch. Rerun. And some asshole named Gillian or something. He usually doesn’t wrestle until after I do, so I’m already out the door and looking to score while he’s still hanging out in the locker room with oiled-up men in tights. What a loser. Damn, I can’t remember the number. I decide to just head back to my apartment. I work my way through the crowd of sweaty parade gawkers until I reach the door to my building and get inside. I fucking hate the Fourth of July.
