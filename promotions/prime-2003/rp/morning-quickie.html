Date: Today She was wriggling under the sheets, avoiding the Florida sun leaking into the window from warming her skin. The plan had failed once again, and life was topsy turvy. She and Killean had gone for drinks, and then... ...She'd fallen asleep in his room. Not what you expected, was it? Get your mind outta the gutter. They keep talking their shit. Talking and talking and talking until my gut instinct is just to kick them in the lips. Standing in the arenas gets difficult when you've gotta wonder what the wrestlers are thinking or saying about you constantly. The crew members are my only real friends, outside of Dave and maybe Killean. They're normal people without diva attitudes or perception that I'm not as tough as them. Those guys treat me like I'm fairly normal, which is exactly what I need to calm me down when my head's going sideways thanks to a wise crack from some steroid-riddled jackass or bearded bitch sucking God's cock. The prettyboys are the worst, too. They don't think they're tough so much as they think they're prettier than me, and probably want to share makeup tips. They manage to be some of the most irritating trash on the planet. I mean, aside from Ellie - who I've always considered to be little more than comic relief - we don't really have chicks around here... until you see Ellis Easton and Danny Ferguson. I've actually spent a lot of drunken time contemplating whether or not it's possible that Ferguson really is the chick from the Black Eyed Peas. The mannerisms are a little more than coincidental, if you know what I mean. Of course you don't, you're just a piece of paper. Fuck you, little buddy! Sometimes I sit around and question whether or not I'll live another year. Well, that isn't true... I USED to sit around and question whether or not I'd live another year. Now, I sit around and wonder when I'll next be tempted by some shit I won't want to touch. I'll never try to kill myself or get back on that, but it gets bad at times. One of the only people I tolerate in this federation was in bed with me a moment ago... talking, unlike anything we'd ever done before. It's odd how you can sleep with someone and know nothing about them. As her journal would show, everything's been unexpected recently. From Killean forgiving her to Karina not killing her for being kind, to Dave actually accepting his mother's death and moving on as a friend. The question posed, however, is the following... In a world where everything's grown so backwards, how - and when - do you find the drive you once had to complete challenges? To win? How sideways can your gameplan get? My brain's taken a vacation, which is why it's so easy for me to train under these circumstances. It's Florida; Hot, humid and a nightmare for a Canadian like me, especially when I've gotta go beyond the call of duty to get myself back into working condition after suffering through injuries during my last three matches. Wouldn't want me to fall apart, now, would you? Amy Campbell
