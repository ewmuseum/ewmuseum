Team VIAGRA Presents : The Trial of the Lunatic (pt1)

20 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th, 2008(also, 2006) (FADE IN: Guess what! IT'S SETTING TIME! [FOLEY: The ticking sounds from 24 accompany...] (Friday, May 19th, 2006) –Hereto known as the Past. 8:58:43 AM 8:58:44 AM 8:58:45 AM 8:58:46 AM BANG! No, that's not the sound of a gun being fired. Good guess though. Most of a weak writer’s repertoire matches the simplistic array of death, kill, or sex in order to find an initial audience. It's lazy writing though, which is why we don't succumb to trying to promote our Google hit rate. [Editor’s Note: Boobs.] Oh, that banging sound! Quite right. Good of you to ask. We were almost sidetracked completely. My apologies. But before I get sidetracked by apologizing for getting sidetracked, I want to tell you that that loud bang was the sound of a man in a robe, sitting behind a podium, slamming a wooden mallet onto an oak surface. Everyone stood up as he entered the room and everyone took their seat when he sat in kind. A great deal of respect was shown by everyone, but especially by one man. Jack Harmen. Known to the professional wrestling world as High Flyer, the Snow man, or the Neighborhood Lunatic, nervously and anxiously sat in the defendant’s chair at his own trial. The trial of the Lunatic. For the sake of paper work, the subject is known as Jack Harmen, 29-492-3049(That's not his real social security number. It's just a random string of numbers. Don't try to use this to obtain credit. You will fail and be promptly arrested by Batman, or if Batman is busy, one of the lesser DC superheroes, like Aquaman. How’d you like to get served by Aquaman? Yeah, pretty humiliating to be beaten by a fish on land, eh? I really hope I don’t get fired for all this sidetracking.) Jack would have been tapping his fingers on his defendant’s table, had he not been locked in a straightjacket. His ever changing hair color had returned to an original dusty black, and it was oddly combed in such a fashion that presented Jack as a professional. Of course, the sweating and uneasy twitching completely undermined his attempts to look calm and collected. Who could be calm while on trial for a capital crime? Especially when the trial had just begun, and his defense attorney hadn’t made her dramatic entrance. And he can't stop his leg from shaking. Sitting directly behind him, over the makeshift benches wall, was his tag team partner and brother-in-law, Tony Davis. You see, Tony's got a few marbles loose. A few screws thrown in the air, as Tony would say. Essentially, he's just as nutty as the Lunatic, but much more passive. Like, currently, Tony Davis waved a large foam finger, which read "VIAGRA #1" on it. He was dressed like a Rastafarian and had the hair to boot. The courtroom was a hushed silence. High Flyer looked over his shoulder at Tony, and then returned forward. He breathed heavily, and then twisted back to Davis, his mouth agape. But again, he spun back to the judge’s seat, paler than he had looked seconds before. Jack lowered his head in silence, and then rolled a crick out of his neck. As he rolled, he caught eyes with Tony again. TONY DAVIS: What! (Tony shouted this at the top of his lungs.) HIGH FLYER: Dude. Inside voice. TONY DAVIS: This isn't a library. JUDGE: SILENCE in my court room. I will not have you make a mockery of the legal process. HIGH FLYER: It’ll do that on its own self, no? JUDGE : I already don’t like you. HIGH FLYER: Doesn’t that mean mistrial? The judge frowned in response, easily not amused. JUDGE: Where is the defense’s defense? Bursting through the classic wooden double doors was a disheveled mess of a woman. Head to toe, dressed in the color red, she quickly hurried through the bustling and murmuring crowd. She looked up and smiled sheepishly, waving to the crowd. Woman: Sorry. Sorry. She quickly passed through the swinging gate and swept herself into her seat. Jack Harmen looked at her as if she was insane. She could only reply with a shrug of her shoulders. JUDGE: I ask this because I must, but also because I am concerned. Is the defense ready Miss…? Woman: Mayweather. Mary-Lynn Mayweather your Honor. Mary-Lynn stood tall as she addressed the judge, and placed her arms on her hips to keep her posture sturdy. It was a trick she used during long mock trials at her University. It was surprisingly effective in the real world, just as much as the fake. She quickly wiped that thought out of her mind. She was professional. The fate of her client rested in the balance. The fate of her young career. The fate of her friend. Jack Harmen, however, would not be persuaded to stop yanking at Mary-Lynn’s suit jacket, trying to get her attention. Mary-Lynn turned to the judge and raised her finger slightly. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Pardon me your honor. I’d like a quick word with my client. JUDGE: I would like to remind the defense that this is a trial for a capital crime. The judge nodded in her direction, and Mary-Lynn quickly jerked her head to Jack. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: What! HIGH FLYER: How come you’re late to my trial? I wasn’t late to pick you up at the airport! I swear, the next time you ask me for a favor, your dog’s dead. ** FLASHBACK – 7:15 AM Yeah, I know it’s unseasonal to have a flashback inside of a flashback, but y’know, I buck the trends yo. Or I steal them from Lost. Regardless, just an hour ago, Mary-Lynn Mayweather, prim and proper, quickly drove down the 405 freeway. She recklessly applies the last bit of makeup to her person, when the car in her right lane shifts. Its bumper low, it acts as a jack under the front of Mary-Lynn’s car. Her car promptly flips off its wheels, driving over top of her spinning accidental prone driver. Her car flies airborne. ** Return to the Present. Mary-Lynn Mayweather sighed, and blows the bangs out of her face. This reveals a deep gash over her left eye as she smiles. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Just an unrelated incident. Now, do you trust me Jack? ** FADE IN: Present day, Night of Ultra-Violence The buzz of Ultra Violence has swept Chicago in frenzy, and none have felt it worse than the ever anxious Team VIAGRA. They arrived hours before the event was even close to beginning, uncharacteristic for such a veteran duo. Then again, this was the first time they would be in tag team action on a PRIME pay per view, and you always remember your first time. They only wished Mary-Lynn could enjoy it. All week she appeared somber, stroking her air cast and staring out into space. She might have well been looking into a rain soaked window, staring out to the New York City skyline. High Flyer was dressing himself, quite early in anticipation. He’d put on his tights already and was taping his knuckles white. Tony Davis, meanwhile, continued his habitual hobby pursuit of becoming as smart as Phoenix Wright. He never had a taste for the law, finding it unseemly, scary, and potentially hazardous to one’s health. But in video game form, where it couldn’t harm him, Tony was quite confident with his ability to survive. Mary-Lynn’s head jerked up. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: You know, it’s true Fly. She reached out to Jack’s arm with her good hand, but Flyer quickly retracted his arm. She looked away, sweeping her foot across her body as if she was kicking an imaginary rock. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: They didn’t mean it. High Flyer finished taping his knuckles, and tosses the tape into the corner. Not overly violently, but without anyone to fill this empty area, the sound echoed around their locker room rather loudly. HIGH FLYER: Don’t call me Fly. You wanna go be ‘part of DUI, go be their Sunny. We’ll just Legion of Doom ourselves. Tony Davis’ head perked up. TONY DAVIS: There’s no way I’m fighting Superman. Flyer looked at Davis out of the corner of his eyes, not even giving him the satisfaction of a simple head turn. He sighed, and turned toward the locker room door before being stopped by… MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER(yelling): I won’t help you cheat! This causes Jack to stop in his tracks. Turning on a beat, he immediately walks up to Mary and stares her in the face. HIGH FLYER: I don’t think you realize… through all that coddling you thought was training, how much of a God damn war the ring can be. I mean, fuck, you wanna know how many bones I’ve broken? *thinks* You’ll actually have to call my doctor to find out, but nevermind, that just proves my point! MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: That logical conclusion can only be formulated if I trust the premise of you not remembering the amount of bones you broke. HIGH FLYER: Smart girl. You’re gonna need that. It’s 23. You wanna know how many times I’ve wrestled WITH broken bones? *thinks* Now, actually, I don’t know that one. But fuck it Mary. You’ve got a job to do. Shape up, ship out, and do as you’re told. You’re boss isn’t just Cantrell, it’s ME! Mary-Lynn shrunk down at this moment, turning slightly red. HIGH FLYER: And to argue with your logic, it’s not cheating when the bosses throw out the rulebook. *claps hands* Now, let’s fill up our snow cone wheelie cart we stole from that fruit vendor full of dangerous sharp objects and a bunch of heavy shit. I wanna rival the hammerverse in terms of weaponry. Defeat the vast armies of Napoleon if I must. Because for all you know, that’s exactly the ace up the sleeve of Risk and Reward. Flyer turned to leave. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER(yelling): I won’t hurt Hank! Flyer stopped, and looked over his shoulder quickly. HIGH FLYER: Sure, you go ahead and nurse that crush. Watch out who you hurt with it. Flyer slammed the locker room door shut as he left, leaving Mary-Lynn with her shoulders slumped. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER (softly): … don’t you trust me? *** (Friday, May 19th, 2006) – the Past Returning to the moment we left off, Jack and Mary-Lynn are caught in a deep gaze. Mary-Lynn smiles sheepishly, 19 year old prodigy, barely passed the bar, defending her first client. And Jack Harmen looked away, breathing heavily, concerned this would be one of the last few days of his life in the public eye. He turned to Tony over his shoulders, who waved his #1 VIAGRA finger and threw up a thumbs up in response. Jack twisted to Mary, and raised his eyebrows. As Jack nodded sheepishly, to the judge Mary Lynn spun. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: We’re ready your honor. JUDGE: Then, we shall begin. Our task here today is ever the difficult one. To call into question the sanity of a crazy person is no light feet. To determine the motivations and the events that occur through a perspective as skewed as this man’s, is an arduous task we must take on as the ever prevailing force of the law. The judge stopped, and cleared his throat. He stared toward the defense. JUDGE: Mister Jack Harmen… on the charge of murder in the first degree, how do you plead? (Slow dramatic zoom in on Mary-Lynn and Jack Harmen.) JACK HARMEN: … Not guilty. FADE OUT.
