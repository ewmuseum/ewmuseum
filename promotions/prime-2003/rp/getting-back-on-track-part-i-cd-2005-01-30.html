Getting Back on Track (Part I- CD)

Date: 30/01/2005 -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Prelude : Many things can be discovered from a mans passed, such as his true self, his pride and the messages deep in his soul. As for Shane Wethers, he lived a traumatic life full of up’s and downs. Living in a crime family is never easy and growing up as an innocent criminal is difficult do. But how can one man live a clean life when previous ancestors have torn his reputation to pieces? Since his troubled upbringing, he found something that could solve the distress of everyday life, that something is his flourishing wrestling career. Shane has been the holder of many infamous titles worldwide and now has switched to PRIME. In todays epic we look deep inside Shane’s mind and see if a star can really wipe out such a troubled passed. Shadwell in eastern London was a crime filled place with mixed ethnic races from Asian, Taliban and British. There were no shortage of crimes and leaving the house at night was always a worry. A small thatched house on the side of the terrace held the Wethers family. Now my family was mixed of Italian, American and British blood. My Father was Paul Wethers… a hard working British father who had an Italian Father, in fact it was my Grandfather that introduced him my Mother Leila Santini, as she was known from her maiden name. Apparently, when they were introduced they hit it off straight away. Now try to imagine this, a British family with an Italian flair to it, this meant my mother had the temper of a possessed taxman. Money was the thing that my family wanted more than anything, but make no bones about it, they loved their family. My Eldest brother, Silus was a typical example of them when he was younger, he would be the only one that held the family together. Especially when my parents argued and it was like the World War all over again. My Sister Anna was another story, she always stayed out late and we hardly ever saw her. She just told my father that she was going to visit friends… little did he know that she was seeing Rechez, who only happened to work for the Columbians. Now no matter who you are, French, German, Swahili or even Italian, you just don’t date my sister unless your part of my father’s crime family…at least that’s what my father believes. Needless to say, the news was finally revealed and Anna left our household to live with Rechez. My Father totally freaked, he was like King Kong making a comeback whilst my mom tried to calm him down, all whilst I’d sit there quietly wondering how I was born into a family that resembles an Italian version of the Osborne’s. Silus was sick to his stomach about the whole news and plotted to kill the Columbians. I remember that fateful day as it was yesterday, I was only 10 and my brother was 14. Anna was 17 and believed she was old enough to make her own decisions. Silus had always been a whiz when it came to fixing up bombs, Mother once banned him and threatened to make him leave home if he didn’t stop. Despite the warning, Silus began to make them with out my Mother’s knowledge. On the Friday night of August 1983, Silus left the house and told my Mother that him and me were going to the park. We left the house and for a reason that I didn’t know at the time, Silus sneaked out two crates. “Silus, what are these for?” I asked but he’d just smirk and reply “nothing.. just some junk. Listen, I need you to help me take them to the dump them”I nodded and looked confused.“Where?”“Follow me, there’s a special place I dump them” Silus replied as he continued to walk on. We caught the next bus and it took fifteen minutes to get to this mystery destination. When we saw the area I was a little concerned, all of a sudden it resembled the place where I grew up in Leeds, where even at the age of seven, I was in fights involving knives and sometimes-even guns. I was brought up in a crime family, so guns and knives were just like toys to me. We approached a large block of apartments and we walked up a stairway to a small apartment house. “Silus?” I asked“Shhh!!!” he said sharply, this is where I began to worry. He placed down one of the crates in front of the door and took the other one off me. He placed that a meter away from it. I watched on confused and unaware of what exactly was about to happen. He grabbed me by sleeve and ran back down the stairway. “Silus???” I said, looking at him with a bewildered look.“Shut up… Just run damn it!” After he said that I just trusted my instinct, we were halfway up the street when I turned back in terror to see a large explosion that wiped out half of the apartments. Silus watched on with a sick grin and shouted… “There you go you @ssholes! You don’t mess with my family and get away with it!” As if things couldn’t get any worse, a bunch of Columbians crawled from the remaining flats. From then on I knew what had happened and felt like I was in the middle of a nightmare. Things DID get worse as we turned to see another gang of Columbians were stood behind us, looking at us with death in there eyes. Silus dragged me by the sleeve again as we both began to run. We sprinted down the street and through a few backyards of houses in the neighbourhood. The adrenaline was flowing through my veins like I had never experienced before, then the moment that would scar me for the rest of my life took place. A large black car cornered off Silus, three men wearing black walked over to him and took out their guns. All Silus could do is take out the small pistol he had stolen from my father and attempt to fight for hi life, but that wasn’t going to help him as the angry group fired away at him. I stepped back and tried not to look as the firing stopped and Silus fell helpless to the ground, dropping the gun across the floor. ::Shane suddenly sits up in a panicking and distressed manor. It now becomes clear that he is sat inside a small office with glass windows and a marble wall. Shane is sat upwards on a long black chair whilst a male shrink sits beside him and scribbles onto a note pad.:: Shrink: Are you ok Mr Wethers? ::The shrink hands Shane a handkerchief and he wipes his brow:: Shane: I think so. ::Shane stands up and reaches into the inside pocket of his black leather coat place over a chair in front of him. He pulls out a white packet of aspirin and takes a glass full of water off the table. He swallows a tablet with a drink of water:: Shrink: So what happened then? Shane: Well… My Silus’ body was supposedly found in a river a mile from where he was shot and Anna was killed in the fire attack. It was a problem that ended up in more problems. ::Shane walks over to the window over looking the city outside, tall skyscrapers can be seen:: Shrink: So your brother returned last year when he attempted to kill you? ::Shane takes a packet of ‘Benson and Hedges’ cigarettes out of his back pocket. He opens them and takes out a cigarette, putting it to his lips. Shane: That’s right… seems the son of a bitch faked his own death. He ends the two problems in one go. He killed Rechez and faked his own death, that way he could convince the Columbians that he was dead and he could also escape the clutches of his family. He was the Gallina (coward) of our family and to think my father once believed he would run the family business one day. He failed in his attempts to frame me and it was himself who ended up in pokey (prison). ::The shrink watches him through the reflection in the window:: Shrink: So you find smoking a way of escaping your problems, right? ::Shane ponders for a moment and takes the cigarette from his mouth and puts it back in the packet:: Shrink: How were you’re parents killed? ::Shane stares out the window again and bites his bottom lip:: Shane: A getaway gone wrong. My father led the Capodecina’s. A Capodecina is a ranking member who heads a crew of ten. Three Capodecina’s were caught but my Father wouldn’t give up, my father was caught cold (killed) escaping from his getaway. My Uncle Frankie was killed along with him, he was the bread and butter (insane). Shrink: And your mother? ::Shane looks to the floor:: Shane: died in the clubhouse (jail) twelve years after she was caught. Overdosed, after she lost my father she felt like a brass cupcake (worthless).So I spent the rest of the time living with my grandparents. Shrink: Mafia causes? ::Shane shakes his head:: Shane: No, they did in the last five years. The doctor said it was natural causes. Though I can’t help feeling that there were someone behind it all. ::The shrink jots some more notes down on the pad in front of him before looking up at Shane:: Shrink: Well that was a long time ago, so what’s made the problem so difficult now? ::Shane falls silent for a moment, staring out of the window:: Shrink: I know that your brother has come back into your life unexpectedly but what else could have reminded you of such a traumatic time? Is there something your not telling me? ::Shane walks over and pushes a piece of paper on the desk, in front of the shrink’s nose:: Shrink: Ah...the Eternal Wrestling days? but i dont get it...whats the problem here? ::Shane looks down on the shrink and points again to a place on the paper:: Shrink: Hmmm….. ::the shrink pauses and looks up at Shane with a concerned look, Shane nods his head with a troubled look upon his face:: Shrink: So you're telling me that this is whats been choking you up got so long? ::Shane looks away with an angered look on his face:: Shrinnk: You need to let this go Shane, you've got rematch this week. Take care of business here with Jackson first.. then we'll look into getting rid of this little issue you have from your EW days (The scene ends as the cameras fade)
