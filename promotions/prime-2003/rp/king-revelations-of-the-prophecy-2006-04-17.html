KING: Revelations of the Prophecy

17 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 17, 2006 Previously Beating and taken against his will, Vangelus Olsig stood toe to toe with the most influential and powerful figure in the Crime-filled area known as Tracer City whom was supposedly killed back in 1993. This man went by the name of Ishmael Muhammed, but operates under a mask and codename as to hide his identity. Now he’s reluctantly informed Vangelus Olsig that Vangelus is indeed a wanted man amongst the Columbian Government, all because of his father’s participation in corrupting various portions of the country. Ishmael says he wants to help Vangelus, by protecting him under his wing, but Ishmael wants something in return...Olsig’s services. His first task was to rid Ishmael’s most despised rival by killing off his daughter, in which Vangelus reluctantly did under secrecy. Now His services required him to partake in various other activities...ones that could have Vangelus either in jail or dead. The Tragedy of Olsig declared another chapter. The winds cut through the cult atmosphere, howling simultaneously alongside the roar of thunder in the velvet sky above. The tears of angels fell in the form of rain, dampening the soil below and crafting the habitats of puddles and swamps. This particular area was populated by trees moreso than people, and really resembled more of a forest than that of a neighborhood. Only one house inhabited the whole near-by area and while fairly large, it only housed around two-three people, maid included. The shadows of the trees blended with the darkness of the night to create an atmosphere barely capable of being seen. Boots splashing through puddles of rain located on the ground below echoed throughout the forest accompanied by the deep, hard breaths of a man who’d seemingly been running for a distant amount of time. The minimum amount of illumination in such an area did little to aid in identifying the man as he sprinted through the forest full speed, brushing through various branches and igniting a continuous ruffling sound all throughout his path. Slow chants of ’Help, Help Me....’ could be heard escaping his mouth in anti-dramatic whispers, obviously taming them enough as to not draw any attention from his attacker. At this point he knew not where his attacker was located, nor where he was located for that matter...all he desired was escape. That desire, however, was short lived. In one swift motion, this man fell face first into the puddle of mud, courtesy of a rope that caught his leg and discontinued his balance. The muddy water masking his face made it difficult to see, so he simply relied on instinct as he desperately pulled on the rope, attempting every in and out in order to untie the thick strand of yarn that was tangled unto his leg. He’d loosened it a bit until a shadow engulfed his entire body from above. A large gasp overcame him as he lied helplessly in the presence of his attacker. He stared fearfully down the cold barrel of a magnum .22, watching as previous event flashed before his very eyes. Desperation set in and enabled him to yank his foot out of the tangled rope and continue once more in a full sprint, ducking in motion as the sounds of gun fire echoed through such an abandoned area, pushing three bullet with the third one chipping the man on the shoulder, producing a passage for blood. A deep level of fatigue began to set in as the man engaged in a sharp turn, immediately hiding himself behind a tall tree in hopes that it’s massive build alongside it’s accompanying darkness would provide him with enough protection to atleast aid his scorned shoulder blade. Wasting little time, he immediately ripped off his shirt and pressed it upon the large gush of blood oozing out of the opening present upon his shoulder. It made him very weak as his stomach drew up at the sight of such a gash on his own body. He’d only felt the pain after witnessing the wound, but that could have been attributed to the fact that while sprinting through the woods, pain was the last factor on his mind. He heard the slow pound of boots through muddy puddles as he knelt there, no doubt configuring it to be his attacker as he forcefully pushed himself back up to his feet and once again resumed in a full sprint, this one much slower than the previous in coherence with his wound. The sprint turned into a jog, though, before turning into a full out limp. Each step he took grew more slower than the last before he’d finally collapsed with his palm holding the gash on his shoulder blade. He grunted in pain as he heard the footsteps of his attacker close in nearer. Desire to maintain his existence forced him to continue on in the fight, though, as he began to crawl with his elbows and knee’s, producing a pain that almost made living not worth it. Suddenly, though, he felt a sharp boot buried deep into his kidney, forcing him face first into a puddle of mud and riddled helpless against the man whom he’d been running over an hour from. He clinched his eyes tightly, praying to the God above to spare him life...atleast for a few more weeks, but his attacker completely disregarded the act, reaching down and yanking the man up by the back of his shirt. With brute force, he slammed the man back first against a tree and swung the handle-end of the .22 magnum, smacking the man in the face with so much force that blood began to trickle. Afterwards, the attacker simply let go of his prey, allowing the man to slump down to the muddy grounding of mother earth below. The victim struggled to maintain consciousness as he slowly felt it slipping away, but at this point, immunity to his pain may have been exactly what he needed. “Tell me what you know.” The deep-toned voice demanded, sending a chill up the spine of his victim as he shivered in unrelentless fear. The victim began to shiver even more ferociously than before, portraying his fear in a broad sense as he held his hands up as if begging his attacker to spare his life. As he witnessed the cold magnum barrel being raised between his eyes, though, he realized that begging was of little affect. This one he’d have to fight his way through. “I-I Can’t, it-...” “Tell me or I’ll re-populate this forest with every damn limb in your body.” The attacker threatened, taking the barrel of the magnum and pressing it up against the forehead of his prey, motioning his finger on the trigger signaling that he was set to pull at any time if his requested information was not granted. “Please...h-have a heart, man. I have a wife and four kids...I can’t leave her to raise all four of them alone. Ishmael told me that If I were to utter one word to anyone he’d kill them all. I can’t live without them man, can’t you see that!” The man stated, tears forming in mass amounts inside of his eyes. “That all sounds very touching, but I’m not doing this for me. I’m doing this so I can rid that joker and get out of this hell-hole of a city for good, so spill the Damn beans.” The attacker stated once more as his patience with his prey grew weary. The man shook ferociously as the dreadful tears mixed in with the cold sweat, painting the portrait of a man shaken by fear. The fear only mounted further as the attacker pressed the barrel of the magnum so hard against the skull of his prey that it hurt. “Now.” Firm, thunderous claps erupted amongst the gathered masses of people in attendance in the small media office of Mayor Daniel Cornelius of Tracer City. The abundance of reporters jotted down their finale notes as the camera men surveyed the Mayor as he pushed himself from behind the podium, taking a moment to grant waves to several of the citizens in attendance. They were short lived, though, as he was desperate to get out from in front of the cameras and bare the repercussions that were undoubtedly set to follow the announcement he’d just made. Two of his assistants led him down a narrow hallway with his private office as the destination. For the first time ever in Mayor Cornelius’s reign, there was complete silence amongst the three as they brushed down the hallway. Realizing the foreignty in such a situation, one of the assistants took it upon himself to break the mold of silence. “So How di-...” “Don’t ask” came the immediate and direct response from Mayor Cornelius. He already knew what the question would be...exactly why and how did he come about such a situation as he did. There were only four weeks remaining in his term before a man by the name of Richard Brockens was set to take over, so making major decisions in this period referred to as the “Lame Duck” period was almost unheard of. That’s what Cornelius wanted though...or moreso the man who was running Cornelius. They countered their destination as Cornelius gave his men the nod, signaling for them to go about their everyday manner as he entered his office. One turn of the brass doorknob granted him admittance as he stepped in, immediately setting his eyes on the masked man sitting atop his desk, clapping slowly and firmly, drawing a less than pleasant smirk from the face of Mayor Cornelius. 'Bravo, Mayor Cornelius...I must say that your 'taking order' skills are greatly increasing.' The masked man stated as Cornelius simply shook his head in shame and made his way completely inside of the room, slamming the door behind him. Without so much as a notion to his guest, he reached his coat hanger and immediately disposed of his business jacket, kicking off his shoes and untying his tie as he spoke out for the first time, directing his guest. 'You know what you made me do out there was not right, Ishmael. Boasting scandalous lies and passing them off as excuses to shut down Forcelli's line of Casinos, all so that he could no longer financially support his candidate, thus pushing whoever it is you have replacing me to the forefront.' He stated, without so much as a glance towards the man who’d been secretly operating his campaign since day one. He made his way over to his leather chair and sat in it, turning his back towards Ishmael and peering deeply outside of a massive window unto the crime-busy city he operated. 'Aw come on, Cornelius. Those weren't lies...he really did order the death of three men, and I have proof, Cornelius, because they were MY men...that's why he, like every other Crime Lord who wants to knock me off of my throne at the top of Tracer City, must suffer my wrath. You did a good thing out there Cornelius...pat yourself on the back.' Ishmael stated with a smile on his face, pushing himself off of his desk and snatching up a sheet of paper off of Cornelius’s desk. 'Whatever' 'Oh, by the way...I took the luxury of signing this Turf Restriction Bill for you.' Ishmael stated, taking it upon himself to extend the paper unto Cornelius who turned around to glance over it. The expression of disbelief on Cornelius’s face told the story. 'And what did you go and do that for? I had people willing to pay millions for those acres of turf!' Cornelius shouted, slamming the document unto his oak desk below, pushing himself out of his chair as he leaned forward against the oak desk peering into the eyes of the man who was slowly tearing him down with anger. 'And you know who those people were, Cornelius? Indians. And you know what Indians do? They take advantage of US, Cornelius. And you know why? Huh...do you?' Ishmael queried, leaning forward on the other side of the oak desk and returning the same fiery glance. 'No, but I suppose you're going to inform me...' 'They do it because some Goof thought it was a good idea to sign a Treaty in the midst of the French and Indian War period that granted them immunity to the laws and boundaries of these United States. That's why they have a string of Casinos running from Tallahassee, Florida all the way to the Damn city of St. Paul, Minnesota even though it's clearly illegal in a majority of the states that separate the two. But you listen to me, Cornelius, and you listen good...they will NOT corrupt Tracer City...not while Ishmael Muhammed V is still alive and kicking.' 'Heh, funny thing is I'm the only person who even knows that Ishmael Muhammed is alive and kicking.' Cornelius stated, smiling mildly as he pushed himself away from the desk and turned back around to glance at the view of his city. 'Don't be so sure of that. I know high people in low places, Cornelius. Make sure to always remember that.' 'Right. So you're telling me that your signing of the Turf Restriction Bill is all in favor of aiding Tracer City? Nothing to do with Chief Chatumseh being head of the purchase...the same man who turned you in back in 1987?' Cornelius queried once more, taking a moment to glance at his nails in an attempt to mentally break down Ishmael Muhammed. 'Look, I know Chatumseh like the back of my hand. His betrayal of me in '87 came as a result of lust on his behalf. He was my Judas, and I know the only reason he wants Tracer City is to garner revenge by dethroning me. He's doing so because he knows that we wont be able to do anything about it.' 'He was offering 7 million dollars for turf, Ishmael. And plus, shouldn't your little ‘Mob Family’ have been enough to back you up?' Cornelius asked as Ishmael made his way over to Cornelius, yanking him around so that the two faced each other nose to nose. Ishmael pointed his index finger directly in the face of Mayor Cornelius as he continued on his verbal tirade. 'You've got to understand, something, Cornelius. These people are fickle...they sway in the direction of the turn-coat. When they see something that looks good, they immediately jump on it. Anything that glitters, they automatically assume it’s gold. Their loyalty is worth about as much as your mother's thong.' He stated, withdrawing his finger from Cornelius’s face and turning his back to the Mayor of Tracer City. 'Still, Ishmael...what you're doing isn't right. We are in the Lame Duck period of my Mayor term for Pete's sake...I'm not supposed to be signing Turf Bills and shutting down empires. All I'm supposed to do is hold the office steady until this new guy you have comes and takes over. Your talk of Lust is a pure example of the pot calling the kettle black, because just as Lust turned Chatumseh against you, your Lust has ruined me. My Damn wife walked out, the Damn children followed, and I'm barely managing the Damn bills! I thought I had a career in politics, but obviously that isn't happening anymore.' Cornelius stated as he leaned his back against the wall and slumped down unto the floor below, burying his face in the palms of his hands. 'Listen, Cornelius. You are not ruined...in fact, you are only progressing. John Adams stayed up until midnight passing bill's during his Lame Duck period, and he was the Damn president for crying out loud! Thomas Jefferson did it too...and you know what? Those two are still regarded to this day as two of the best presidents in our nation's history. Remember the Deceleration of Independence? Yeah...those guys constructed it. What you are doing makes you a man with backbone, and that's rare in politics my friend.' Ishmael countered with, walking over to his comrade and lifting him up as he pointed his finger in his face once more. “You are the future of this country man. Men like you will lead this country sooner than later, and I’m going to make sure of it. And if theirs one thing you can count on, that’s Ishmael Muhammed’s word.” He stated, patting his open palm on the facial cheeks of Cornelius in a sure sign of assurance. Cornelius returned the favor, though, by simply pushing Muhammed’s arm down and pointing his own finger into the face of Ishmael. “You know what? I’m sick of this whole operation. I’m sick of having to bear all of your secrets...I’m sick of being some figurehead while you make all the decisions and then pass them along for me to express, I’m sick of it all Ishmael.” Cornelius stated, shaking ferociously in anger as a blush of redness overcame his complexion. “That’s fine...you can be sick all you want. I mean, you only have four weeks left in office as it is before my main man, Richard Brockens, takes over and I run him the same way I ran you. But you take note of this, Cornelius...don’t you EVER get so sick to the point where you spill the beans that Ishmael Muhammed is still alive and that he’s secretly operating the Tracer Legislation. That would kill me and my empire...and for it I’d kill you. Do you understand that, Cornelius?” Ishmael stated with even more anger, taking a moment to wrap his massive palms around the collar of Cornelius, lifting him up and pinning him up against the wall. “I said, do you hear me...” He mumbled. Not even able to mutter so much as a word, Cornelius simply nodded in agreement. “Good. Now brush yourself off, we’ve got a w-...” Suddenly Ishmael stopped mid-sentence as he rolled his eyes up, sniffing while releasing Cornelius. “What’s that smell?” Ishmael asked, before turning around and spotting a janitor sneaking out of the closet of the Private Office, attempting to make his way out of the room without being seen. “Hey you! Stop!” Ishmael shouted as he immediately ran over and snagged the Janitor up against the wall. He glanced at his name tag before wrapping his palms around the neck of the Janitor, threatening to strangle life out of the man. “Christopher Washington, eh? How much did you hear, Christopher...” Ishmael asked. “I-I he-heard nothing...” “Come on, leave him along Ishmael...he’s a good guy.” Cornelius stated, making his way over the scene and stretching out his arm in an attempt to calm the Crime Lord, but Ishmael simply turned his head and shot a fiery glare towards him, which was enough to make him back off of the situation. “Don’t you dare lie to me...TELL ME HOW MUCH YOU HEARD!” Ishmael shouted, squeezing Christopher’s neck tightly, but keeping it loose enough so that he was able to speak. “I-I Heard it a-all...” He stated, drawing a less than pleasant expression from Ishmael Muhammed. “That information that you now have would ruin me if you were to spill the beans to any living being. Listen Christopher...I am going to kill you right here and right now, but if you somehow manage to survive and you indeed spill the beans, I will slaughter every member of your family, understood?” Ishmael asked, tightening his grip so that Christopher could no longer speak, but instead only nod. Ishmael turned his head towards Cornelius while still holding Christopher up. “Give me that handgun out of drawer C, Cornelius.” Ishmael demanded. “Come on, it isn’t wor-...” “NOW!” ”SMACK! During the distraction, Christopher managed to sneak his hand in his tool belt, retrieving a flashlight that he sent smacking dead into the bridge of Ishmael’s nose, forcing him to release the hold as the Janitor took of full speed down the hall way. Ishmael fell to one knee, holding his bleeding nose. “That little Runt...I WILL KILL BUTCHER YOUR FAMILY IF YOU SPILL THE BEANS, WASHINGTON! BUTCHER THEM!!” Ishmael shouted as he sat on the flooring holding his bleeding nose. Suddenly he began to crawl over to the desk of the private room, retrieving his cell phone and hitting one speed dial button. “What are you doing?” Cornelius pondered. “I’m going to rid that clown before he rids me.” Ishmael responded. The high rings of the cell phone could be heard three times before a voice finally sounded, answering it. “Vangelus...I have a job for you...” “Why are you doing this to me?” Christopher asked of his attacker, Vangelus Olsig. The barrel being pressed up against his forehead made his heart race. All he could think about was his family, and the punishment that would be inflicted upon them if he’d been forced to release such information. “You want to know why? I am a wanted man of the Columbian Government for the simple fact that I am Czar Olsig’s son. My father, Czar, corrupted that country and I’ve become the heir of his bad reputation, which has made me a target. My only saving grace is through Ishmael, or so he says...I personally think he’s a liar, but I can’t even bother to find out because one step out from under his wing will get me killed. He sent me to kill you off, but I don’t want that...I want the information. This information would ruin him and thus set me as well as others free. Now complete the justice and accept life, Christopher.” Vangelus stated, pressing the barrel harder. “Alright, Alright...just s-spare me, s-sir. Ish-Ishmael stated that he has been plotting a scam to kill off Mayor Richard Brockens and assume the Ma-Mayor position under his codename, R-Rufio Faneli.” The prey managed to force out, obviously having had rehearsed such a lie prior to this encounter. His eyes clinched one more as the cold sweat raced rapidly down his cheeks, only serving to add to the already morbid atmosphere. “I know Ishmael more than you ever would, and I know that he’d never run for mayor, because he as well as everyone knows that not one single being in Tracer City would accept a man as corrupt as him. They may not know that Ishmael is under that mask he wears, but they know Rufio Faneli, his so called Code Name, and they know that him as Faneli is no better than him as Ishmael. Now I suggest you give me the truth.” Vangelus stated, smacking Christopher in the cheek once more with the gun for good measure before placing it up against his forehead once more. “Either way my life is done with, so screw this and Screw...YOU!” He shouted, sending his foot straight forward while shouting the latter word and landing a boot right into the grapefruits of Vangelus Olsig. The shot made Vangelus immediately cringe, dropping the gun which Christopher fought through the pain to dive for and retrieve. Knowing how much pain crawling would cause, he decided to roll through the muddy soil until he was a good distance away from Vangelus. That’s when he placed the barrel against his own temple. And pulled the trigger. “NO!” Vangelus shouted as he scrambled over to the scene only to find the deceased body of Christopher Washington, the man who’d taken with him to the grave the very information that would have terminated the sovereignty of one Ishmael Muhammed V. Vangelus grabbed a chunk full of his hair, pulling it tightly as he witnessed the same failure in which he’d become so familiar with. Clap...Clap...Clap...Clap...Clap Vangelus glanced to both his right and left as the claps grew closer and closer until Vangelus was able to identify the men performing the act. Their were Six of them...Ishmael’s Six Disciples. They were Ishmael’s Six most trusted men and they were now assembled in a forest, praising Vangelus Olsig as he knelt before the deceased body of one Christopher Washington. “What’s the meaning of this?” Vangelus asked, as Arick, the supposed head of the group, stepped forward and embraced Vangelus. “You, Sir Vangelus, are an Immortal. We Exalt you for your courageous acts” Arick stated, extending his arm as Vangelus reluctantly shook it. “What are you talking about?” Vangelus wondered, as he continued to reluctantly shake Arick’s hand. “In one fatal swoop, you rid us of the powerful Crime Lord, Caesar, by killing his daughter...” “I didn’t ki-...” “And now, you have done what no other man was seemingly able to do. Christopher’s information made him the most powerful man in Tracer City...simply because he had the key to the destruction of the former most powerful man, Ishmael Muhammed. Killing him off is something we couldn’t have done, but you single handedly did it.” “I didn’t kill him...he killed himself.” Vangelus stated, ripping his hand from the grip of Arick. “But you inspired it! Stop being so humble and accept your position as an Immortal...as a King. Why shouldn’t your name be written amongst the same lines as Ishmael Muhammed and all of the other powerful figures that have encountered Tracer City?” Arick stated as the other five disciples nodded and cheered in agreement. “So, now you’re praising me? What about Ishmael?” “Forget him...you are a true warrior. He is nothing more than a figurehead.” Arick stated. He was certainly right about them being Fickle Vangelus thought as he stood amongst the men who’d accepted him under their wing as his leader. He couldn’t Reject.... Nor could he Accept. So what exactly could he do...when he didn’t know what to do? Vangelus stood steadfast, peering deeply into the engraved image and letters upon Christopher Washington’s tombstone. No one knew exactly how he died besides Vangelus and those whom he’d informed, and feeling a portion of guilt, he figured it only right that he pay his respects. Things weren’t exactly as they seemed, though. “I hear that my guys want you as their leader now...” Stated Ishmael, startling Vangelus as he stared deep into the engravement. He immediately turned around and faced the man, realizing that the situation wouldn’t be very pretty. “I guess so...I, in no way heed to it, though.” Vangelus stated, pleading his case. “Hehe...Congratulations. If they want you as their main man, go ahead. I’ll follow along...Maybe I could learn a thing or two from you as well.” Ishmael stated with a huge smile on his face, completely throwing Vangelus off guard. “You mean, you don’t object?” Vangelus stated in bewilderment. “Of course not. Give them what they want...I’d even enjoy working under the wing of Vangelus Olsig myself.” He stated before placing his hands unto the shoulders of Vangelus. “You’re a good man, Vangelus...you’re a warrior...a King. You deserve a King’s riches.” Ishmael stated. With that, he smiled once more and nodded before turning away and slowly walking off. “What do you think you’re doing? You’re really going to let him get away with this?” Stated Richard Brockens softly as he ran up to Ishmael and pondered as he continued to walk along. “Of course not, Richard...you know me better than that.” Ishmael stated, continuing on with that broad smile planted upon his face. “And how do you plan on stopping it.” Richard queried. That’s when Ishmael stopped mid-stride and turn to face him...smile completely washed away, replaced by a ruthless expression.... “By showing him what Crime Life is really like.” Stated Ishmael, smiling once more and walking away leaving Richard Brockens standing there in utter bafflement. Intensity. For nearly a year it has defined me. Intensity. For nearly a year it has ruined me. Now I face Intensity once more in the form of Kyle Lamen. He’s a man I’ve come to know, hate, but also respect. But Intensity won’t let me spare him. The beating I will Lash upon him will not be for my good... But for his. For it will teach him the corruption of life. It will teach him that not all things are as they seem. It will teach him fear...and how to face it. Wednesday Night I step into the main event once more. To unleash upon you, Kyle, a lesson in Intensity. You can thank me later.
