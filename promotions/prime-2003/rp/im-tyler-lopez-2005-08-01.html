I’m Tyler Lopez

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/01/2005 '[I'm paranoid] only because everyone's out to get me.' - Fox Mulder Fade in to a cooler than normal New Orleans, Louisiana summer morning. A light mist overcomes the shot as we open into an empty park, Armstrong Park. Nothing to special, benches, leaves, and a man-made pond. This pond is a special one, it’s the type of pond that people toss pennies in and make dreams, and hope upon hope that they come true. Today will be no different, as sitting on the edge of the pond, wearing a black blazer jacket, black slacks, , is none other than your very own, The Scientific Apparatus, he is possibly the hardest working man in PRIME, Tyler Lopez. Lopez stares into the pond, as it stares back at him, as he speaks in a trance like tone. I hope... Lopez's voice drifts off. I hope, that my opponents this week have a lot more ammunition than Genie in a Bottle stories, because if not, then all their whining and complaining will be for not. Lopez pauses. How pathetic are all of you? Have any of you taken a deep hard look at your selves and asked that question? You’re so delusional, so ‘by the numbers’ it’s ridiculous. You think that because you made it into PRIME because you hold a PRIME contract that you are a big thing. Nothing any of you say, or nothing that any of you do has any hint or originality or charisma, all of you are carbon copies of carbon copies, and the best you can do is bring up old and outdated stuff. This business isn’t about old and outdated stuff, this business is about the here and the now, this business is about whose on top of their game, now . Lopez pauses. I’m the ‘Apparatus’, the end all and be all of this storied organization, you name it, I’ll do it. Unlike most of you, I don’t sink to lows like listing off every single accomplishment I’ve done, to validate myself, believe it or not, I never have. I don’t need to do that, because I don’t need validation, no, no I don’t. Lopez pauses for a moment, not sure if he just contradicted himself or made a good point, but he smirks nevertheless. You see I’m the Man, The Myth The Apparatus, which plain and simply means all of you are beneath me whether you like it or not. And why shouldn’t you like it? I’m a man you can be proud of, a man the people can be proud of, a hard working man, I earned my reputation fair and square, and no one, not any of you can say I didn’t earn it in the middle of the ring, because damnit I did. Lopez’s tone is very erratic, he states the point and almost seems to be doing so to convince himself. His eyes dart back and forth, nervously, unsure. You’re all jealous, you’re all against me. I hear the whispers, I hear you plotting, to take it all away from me, to stop me from ever winning any championships in PRIME. I’ve worked months, weeks, days and damnit even hours for. You all are against me, you all want what I have, legendary status, money, fame, prestige. Lopez breathing is unevenly paced, his words nervous, his tone, not the normal cocky tone, but one of paranoia, and some uncertainty. I’m not old, I’m not past my prime, you ask me to wrestle a sixty minute Iron Man Match, and I’d do it, I wouldn’t break a sweat, I’m the Apparatus, I’m Tyler Lopez. But I’m no fool, I have to be prepared, I have to be ready. This PRIME is filled with un-honest, back-stabbing people, people who won’t work for anything, people who want everything handed to them, people who get by off name value alone. Lopez pauses, and looks at himself in the reflection of the pond and sneers at the reflection. You people make me sick! People like Peter Vetra and John Edwards you’re the epitome of what’s wrong with this place, and starting this week, I’m going to one by one start eliminating those problems. But I can’t do it alone, not because I don’t have the skill, no, no, that’s not it. It’s because, you’re all against me, you all want to take my pride away from me, and I can’t allow that to happen. I don’t need any titles to validate me, I’d be a superstar, a legend without it, but I need it so you won’t forget me, so you won’t forget that I’m a legend that I’m a superstar, my legacy needs to live, you’re all trying to take my legacy, and I can’t allow that, I won’t allow that. Uncomfortable silence. So I need an entourage, all the great ones have entourages, so I too shall have one. I don’t need to hide behind a mask, because everything is out in the open. This time, the only animosity is that in which you’ll all feel, when I have my revenge. Because like I said before, I shall finish this game. Because I’m the Apparatus, I’m Tyler Lopez...And soon, very soon you’ll all learn how this game is to be played. Lopez tosses a penny goes into the pond, as the shallow water ripples and distorts Lopez’s face in the water. Spreading it and dispersing it throughout the pool. As the water slowly returns back to it’s natural state of serenity, another face appears in it behind Lopez’s shoulder. A lovely yet devilish face, ravenous hair, all wrapped in a scarf and a long black jacket. The face of business manager and the mastermind behind who is Tyler Lopez, the woman known simply, as Passion. Passion places her hand on Lopez’s shoulder, and rubs his face with her other hand... Passion: No more worries, your insurance policy is here, and as of now, PRIME will never, ever, be the same again. Lopez’s face for the first time is reassured, as he smirks at Passion and stares back into the pond. Passion: Lets get out of here, we have a small army to assemble. Lopez glares at Passion as he stares into the pond. Tyler Lopez: [Nodding his head] Time to finish the game. The water ripples from the sound waves of Lopez’s voice, and his face again becomes distorted from the ripples. But this time, the reflection doesn’t come back, his face just remains distorted as he stares into the water waiting for it to return, but it never does. Lopez stands as he and Passion slowly walk away. The camera slowly fades but not before catching a bit of conversation between Passion and Lopez. Passion: What’d you wish for Tyler? Lopez pauses and just glares back at Passion. Tyler Lopez: Nothing… Lopez looks at Passion and then nervously chuckles. Tyler Lopez: Nothing at all... I’m The Apparatus, nervous chuckle] I’m Tyler Lopez. The words “One cannot be betrayed if one has no people. “ fade onto the screen. And then we fade to black.
