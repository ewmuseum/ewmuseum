...And Found

2 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Nov 2, 2007 “Fucking ridiculous.” The voice is smooth and familiar, comforting despite the hint of malice that underlines every syllable spoken. Its melodic, steady rhythms seep into his ear like the consistent trickle of blood that flows out, drying in the stale air to create an uncomfortable collection of crusted crimson. He coughs, a raspy sort of sound that tears at the raw lining of his throat. His head bobs forward, muscles far too weak to hold steady, ejecting the tiniest drop of saliva from the corner of his cracked and broken lips. His awareness is itself a torture. That one drop would have been like sweet relief to the painfully dried mess of muscles in his throat. If only he could wet his tongue with more than blood. “We used to be stronger than this, you know. Much stronger. A man to be feared. A man without fear himself. Now we are weak. Now we are pathetic.” All he tastes is the bitter sting of blood and iron. All he hears is the soft mockery of this taunting voice. All he sees is endless dark. “You are pathetic. You are weak. You have forgotten me. Forgotten us. What we used to be. What we could be again. If only you’d embrace me once again.” “Shu…shut.” His voice cracks with every letter. The pain of stretching blood-dried chords with every sound is near unbearable. It hurts so bad he wants to cry. Wants to die. But he can do nothing more than listen. “Shut…up.” The voice lets loose with a shrill laugh, colder than the crisp touch of winter on his bare, beaten skin. Colder than the sweet embrace of death that refuses to come. “Tough man now, are you? What are you gonna do? Can’t get up out of that chair. Shit, kid, you haven’t left that seat in weeks. When’s the last time you felt your legs, huh? You even know if you still have legs? Or did they cut those off with all those little strips of your flesh they’ve been feeding you to keep you alive?” He tries to open his eyes, but does not have the strength to even flutter an eyelid. His head hangs defeated, lulling on the broken swivel of his neck. He tries not to think of the pain or the cold, familiar leer of that voice, but he can barely remember anything else. He thinks of ways to die, ways he may be able to end his own suffering, and wonders with more than a smattering of hope if today will be the day they finally end his life. While his senses have been dulled to the point of near non-existence, there are still a handful of recognizable stimuli that jack his heart rate a few paces per second. One of them is the quiet, muffled clink of metal on metal. His captor working the key into the lock. This is always followed by a moment of apprehensive silence, before the creak of rusted hinges announces the entrance of his tormentors. There’s the recognizable click of a light switch followed by the buzz and hum of powerful overhead lights attempting to amp themselves to full volume. Despite his inability to open his eyes, the magnitude of the lights still pierces through the skin of his eyelids, brightening the dark spots of his mind and sending sharp spikes of unpleasantness into the back of his brain. Always too bright. The distinctive and deliberate echo of boots upon cement that usually follows this series of sounds is replaced today by the hurried clack of rushed heels. Heels… Who would wear… “You’ve got to be fuckin’ kidding me.” “Ty! Jesus Christ, Ty! Oh my God, we’ve been looking…fuck me. Jesus, Ty. What’d they do to you?” Her voice is cracked and strained through barely contained emotion. Her breath comes in quick, irregular exhalations. He cannot see her, can barely feel the graze of her fingers as they work at the ropes binding his arms behind the chair. He more senses her presence. It empowers him. Gives him hope he’d long thought lost. “You’re such a fuckin’ pussy. Gotta have a girl come save your ass. Remember back in the day, when we used to be the one saving the damsels? Getting our dick sucked? You’ll be lucky if this dyke doesn’t shove her dick straight up your ass while you’re still tied up.” “K…Katt?” “Yeah, Ty. It’s me. Hold on a sec, okay? Just take it easy.” “Who…who’s here?” “There’s nobody here. It’s just me. I swear to Christ, I get my hands on these fuckin’ bastards…” “Some.. someone was…was … talking…talking to me.” He hasn’t spoken this much in weeks. It hurts more than he thought he was capable of feeling anymore. But her presence has bolstered him against the pain. Rejuvenated his hope. In the immortal words of Gloria Gaynor, “I will survive.” The bonds are loosened and his body immediately falls forward. She stops him with a palm to the chest, readjusting herself to pull him into a supportive embrace. “Here, drink this.” Water. Oh sweet merciful lord, she has water. It’s almost impossible to even drink, the pain is so unbearable. But with a neck that won’t support his head, there’s much less drinking and a lot more drowning, which is still effective enough at moistening the desert dry recesses of his throat. Katterina Wylde carefully lowers his body to the cold concrete floor and stands, pulling a sleek cellular phone from the confines of her denim pocket. “Alex, it’s me. I found him. Exactly where Pierce said he would be. He’s…he’s in bad shape, Al. I don’t know if he’ll… I’m…just get out here as soon as you get this. We need to get him—Hey! Yeah, he’s right here. … It’s not lookin’ good, Alex. … No, you have to get out here now. … Yeah, I’ll stay with him. Okay. … See you soon.” “Well, isn’t that just fuckin’ quaint? Hell of a way for a girl to act when you’ve never even stuck her? And Pierce? You’re getting help from Pierce Lavelle? My, how far the mighty have fallen.” It is a concentrated effort to summon enough energy to open the eye that isn’t swollen shut. He’s not seen the light of the real world in weeks, and it’s a burning, stinging welcome back to reality. His vision is blurred, but even through the fog he recognizes the leering, sinister face looking down upon him. The rotting flesh and broken bone of a human skull pulled straight from the depths of Hell itself. An evil visage attached to the scarred and beaten body of a professional wrestler. The very face he saw in the mirror every day for damn near ten fucking years. “Help’s on the way, Ty. Just hold on a few more minutes. It’s all going to be okay now. We found you. We’ll get you fixed up, and then we’ll kill every last mother fucker that was even remotely involved with what they’ve done to you.” “You’re god damn right we will, bitch.” His mind, weak from fatigue and malnutrition, cannot make sense of any of it. He only knows he is safe, despite the looming sense of foreboding tickling the back of his brain. He trusts her. He believes her. And for the moment, he’s assured that he will be okay. With that thought, the first assuring thought to cross his mind in more than a month, Tyler Rayne allows himself to slip lazily back into unconsciousness. “That’s right. Get your rest. You’re gonna need it. You and me, kid, we’re gonna paint this town red. God damn are we gonna paint this bitch red.”
