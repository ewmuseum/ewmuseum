Date: Whenever 192 was The long recognized old school blue and black PRIME logo flashes three times. An abridged version of “Sound of Madness” blasts through a computer screen while various clips from ReVolution and Pay-Per-Views of the past fly by in rapid succession. PRIME PRESENTS... A WEB EXCLUSIVE... SEGMENTS FROM THE VAULT Matt Mills, outside the usual realm of being the subordinate and frightened interviewer, sits in a leather chair with relaxed hands folded across his lap. The cameraman gives him his cue to start talking. Matt Mills: Good evening fans and welcome to this web exclusive material brought to you by Prometheus. I am your host Matt Mills. The interviewer adjusts his reading glasses and tie. Matt Mills: Devin Shakur and Tyler Rayne have been embroiled in what can only be described as an insanely crazy OUCH war of attrition. Their rage has spanned every building PRIME has inhabited since the new year. In the past few weeks, the madness has gone above and beyond PRIME into psychologically unhealthy levels of sickness. Both men have fancied kidnapping the other. On ReVolution 191, Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels hatched a plot to torture Rayne for exposing The Man in Black at his most vulnerable. Mills shutters, the memories of Shakur without a shirt resurfacing. It was a horrific sight for everybody. Matt Mills: Hessian thwarted the plan, hired by Rayne accomplice, and PRIME Hall of Famer, Lindsay Troy. In one of the last encounters of 191, Shakur found Rayne in the parking lot. Well, more specifically, Shakur got a loaded baseball bat over the back of his head and stuffed into his own trunk. Rayne subsequently drove off with Christian Daniels in pursuit, just a second behind the action. Matt Mills: Due to budget cuts within the company, most of the affiliates carrying FX were not privileged to see all of ReVolution 192. Lisa Tyler and the Prometheus board of directors would like to sincerely apologize for these happenings. We have started this feature on PRIME dot org for the benefit of those markets. Matt Mills: In this short session, we will see what happened after Tyler Rayne told Christian Daniels the phrase, “The king never knows anything” just as Hour 1 of ReVolution came to a close. The Biker wasn\'t too happy and continued storming through the arena in search of his younger brother. Where he went and what he found intrigued the PRIME audience immensely. Let\'s take a look. ReVolution 192 Minutes 90:04 to 93:39 “TROY! YOU STUPID BITCH! I\'M GONNA HANG YOU ON THE BACK OF MY BIKE FOR THIS BULLSHIT!” Christian Daniels smiles at the same ratio human eyes see Halley\'s Comet, perhaps even a few years later. When he rolls into a building, everybody takes notice because 99 percent of time The Biker has the biggest build and baddest attitude around. His fists are enveloped in chains and trigger arm ready to fire should someone look at him incorrectly. Seldom does he lose a fight in any venue and when he does, payback is inevitable. Even though he\'s getting the silver medal for size and weight in the Allen Fieldhouse, his intentions are second to none. He\'s a hunter unable to catch his prey, military issue boots pounding every door and crevice of the building. Rounding a corridor already thoroughly examined, The Biker has missed something in an essential locker room. He could swear that no inch of Lindsay Troy\'s haven wasn\'t toppled and destroyed. Christian had an inkling neither Rayne or Troy were in the building, but knew that a Hall of Famer like Troy could hide in some pretty odd spaces if she desired. “I\'D START WRITING YOUR WITTY FUCKING EPITAPH NOW!” Snorting like a bull, Christian rounds the last corner and slams his shoulder into the locker room, coiled to knock Troy six years back with one arm breaking punch. But he only sees the handiwork created earlier, every drawer and leather cushion on the floor, assorted items scattered throughout the room like the results a haphazard robbery. Angry brown eyes shoot over to the door, unable to rule out a possibility of ambush. It doesn\'t matter who or how many show up, Christian will run through them all and leave aching bodies behind. He moves to the bathroom, shoving open the door and scanning for any signs of The Queen. Nothing. Back behind the couch. Up to the ceiling. Rayne\'s seen that trick utilized by one Pierce Lavelle many ReVolutions ago. He doesn\'t find anything. The Biker leans up against the wall and pounds his fist against the concrete bricks until blood trails from the knuckles. His weight shifts forward and mind starts working, talking out loud. Christian Daniels: Fuckin\' bitch has a bigger stake in this shit. She ain\'t doin\' this \'cause I\'m running roughshod over\'t locker room. That\'sa load\'ve bull. Why\'s she actin so damn stupid, taintin\' her career by becomin\' involved with this shit and Rayne? Ain\'t fuckin\' companionship\'n security, that\'s fer sure. The Biker mulls some more and comes to a realization. Troy isn\'t the kind of person to play mind games where she hides. She\'s fearless, an intrepid woman who isn\'t fooled easily. She\'s someone with tenure around the backstage area and somewhat of a leader to the younger and elder roster members alike. This situation is too suspect. While stuck in the thought process, Christian continues to scan the room and check the door for signs of impending entrapment. In the quest for answers, his eyes come across an envelope tucked underneath a stack of items. An envelope that yearns to be found, sticking out too far. One that wasn\'t there earlier. Christian reaches forward, sliding the useless materials away and ripping open the envelope. Inside is one picture that infuriates Christian down to his core, riling his blood and sending him from the room with a thunderous boot that separates door from hinges. Studying the picture with head down, Christian sees his brother stuck in an identical position, only forced down by agony and torture. Standing next to him with an extended middle finger is The Underground Pimp, brandishing the same baseball bat from ReVolution 191. The photographer remains anonymous, but it would take an idiot not to decipher that mystery. Sticking his hand out to avoid hitting a wall, Christian looks at the background and little information given in the picture. Nobody would stick the picture in that locker room after the fact if they didn\'t want it found. Thus, someone, the obvious culprits, want Christian to find Devin and perhaps walk into their perfectly setup scheme. When Christian stares at an emblem in the bottom right corner, he pockets the picture and picks up speed, heading straight for the parking lot. He\'s got an idea of where Devin is. Christian Daniels: I\'MA KILL THOSE MOTHERFUCKERS! GET\'THA FUCK OUTTA MY WAY! PRIME Vaults Matt Mills remains in the same seated position. Matt Mills: And just moments later, before Jason Snow and Killean Sirrajin\'s Main Event, Christian found Devin and got a surprise he never saw coming. ReVolution 192 Minutes: 103:56 to 106:57 For the better part of four months, Devin Shakur played a plethora of mind games with Tyler Rayne, taking away every element of control he held in their rivalry. The effects of his broken ankle lingered, but Rayne was difficult to deal with on any level. One couldn\'t be passive, otherwise they would find themselves steamrolled and left for dead. Shakur elected to punch Rayne in the mouth over and over again, defeating him at his own game and becoming a made man in the process courtesy of Lisa Tyler and the Prometheus board. The end result leaves him here, shirtless and beaten unconscious, hands over head and dangling from the ceiling. His body ravaged by fresh scars from the massive beating Rayne inflicted over the long week. If PRIME fans didn\'t have his smug image embedded in their heads, they would not be able to recognize the swollen cheeks, involuntarily shut eyes, and open wounds with a dab of salt on the ends. If one poked Tyler Rayne enough, their career and life would regress. Anybody who needs verification on that could inquire with Wade Elliott. No longer does Devin\'s body struggle against the ropes. No energy is left in his starved being. The faintest kick nor twitch doesn\'t register on the radar. He\'s clean unconscious with no whereabouts The only question is does Tyler Rayne want to finish the job or leave an enlightening impression on Devin? An angry seven foot biker slams his front wheel through a wooden door and swings around two sharp turns. The building is worn by age, on the verge of collapse, and only enhanced by a few sitting accommodations surely added by Rayne and presumably Troy. Christian follows the clues, roaring his bike engine, and rams through the locked door. Despite all the commotion, Devin doesn\'t move an acknowledging inch, fixed in his position. The Biker shifts his eyes back and forth, looking for some kind of setup. The rope is there but no string or wires are around the room. In fact, not much is in the room other than Devin hung from the ceiling. No trap doors or closets. Not even a bathroom. Only a television and stool. The stool is conveniently placed for someone to reach up and free Devin from his predicament. Christian parks his motorcycle and steps onto the stool, pulling a blade from his pocket and placing a hand around Devin\'s waist just below the rope fastened to his sternum. With three cuts, the bloody rope tears and Devin drops into his brother\'s straining grip. Christian quickly jumps off the stool and seats Devin, placing his knee against the bottom bar of the stool and continuing to liberate Devin from the ropes. One swift cut brings new breath into Devin\'s lungs. Christian moves around, keeping Devin\'s chin level. The Biker kneels and works through the stubborn leg rope, needing five seconds of work to eat through the material. Sticking out his free arm so Devin doesn\'t collapse, Christian places Devin on his back and pulls a water bottle from his back coat pocket. He lifts Devin\'s head up and puts some water into his mouth. Devin instinctively turns his head and tries to resist. Coughing up blood, The Man in Black doesn\'t fully recognize where he\'s at, his senses beaten to the point of elimination. His arm lifts up and attempts to throw a weak punch. Christian doesn\'t bother blocking, the hand dying out before reaching his chest. Christian Daniels: Dev, DEV! Stop fighting, Rayne ain\'t fuckin here. It\'s me, kid. Coughing more blood onto the creaky wooden tiles, Devin grunts and rolls away from Christian\'s hand on his shoulder. He mutters, still ingesting the air into his lungs. Devin Shakur: Fuck...you...Tyler. Christian reaches into his pocket and pulls out a cellular phone, punching one number and placing the device up to his ear. Christian Daniels: Yeah, it\'s me, we\'re in some worn down piece of shit joint round the corner from the fuckin\' Allen Fieldhouse. I got Dev, ain\'t no sign of Ty or Bitch. Bring the car and someone to get my bike...Yeah, get here quick. He\'s barely hangin\' on. Christian pockets the phone and hovers over Devin, who shuts his eyes to shun himself from the room light. Devin Shakur: You...are...dead...Ty...I\'m gonna fuck...ing...kill...you. Christian pours another few ounces of water into Devin and waits impatiently on the car and backup. “I swear to Christ, I\'m never doing that shit again. Stubborn fuck.” Devin swings upward in presumed defense and Christian looks around, instantly recognizing the voice that uttered those words. The visual is cued up and a standing image of Tyler Rayne is seen... Stepping from his parked car in the Allen Fieldhouse. Lindsay Troy steps from the passenger seat and the two casually stroll into the arena. Devin Shakur: You are dead...Ty. Christian burns a hole through the television, ready to tear apart the confidence and swagger staring back at the camera into the PRIME crowd and his fifteen inch television. Devin would heal, he\'s been through worse. Christian would go forward, unsure of whether or not he can deal out worse. PRIME\'s fearless duo just signed their death warrants. PRIME Vault Matt Mills: Folks, that is all the time we have for the moment. Join us next time when footage from 193 involving these same four participants is shown. End
