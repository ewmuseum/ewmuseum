Date: May 11, 2009 Public Transportation. Riding the bus is never something anyone really wants to do, but if you don't have a car what choice do you have but to suck it up and pay the fare. Where else can you sit down and let someone drive you across town for a dollar? Unfortunately, as easy as that sounds, how comfortable can you feel when every face looks like it wants to pull you out of your seat and beat the living hell out of you. For the most part, Rosa Dominguez Veracruz avoids any and all eye contact. Still, that doesn't stop the African American man standing near the exit doors from checking her out. With his hoodie pulled down low over his eyes, no one can tell that he has not stopped looking at her since she got on. The fact that she is attractive gives him reason to be in awe – smooth caramel brown skin, hazel eyes, and frizzy, golden brown locks that bounced when she walked to her seat – but his intentions are far from honorable. His heart races as he reaches through the hole cut out in his pocket. He made the hole specifically for times like these, when he felt the urge to touch himself in public. Hunched over, the bottom of his hoodie reaches near mid-thigh. With his hand through the hole and under his jeans, he can't help but be excited that he didn't wear any briefs. “Excuse me.” Startled by the voice of an older, heavier-set, woman with a metal grocery cart trying to get past him, and off the bus, he presses his back against the partition. The face she makes as she brushes past him, not to mention the gasp of air that escapes her lungs, make his eyes go wide. He was caught with his hand on the cookie jar, and pulling his hand out would only cause him to pitch a tent beneath his hoodie. He couldn't take that chance. Especially not with a pregnant wife sitting at home waiting for him. She is due in a few weeks, a baby boy the doctor had told him. How would he explain getting arrested for jerking off in a public place, to a woman he promised to honor and cherish for the rest of his life. She would be devastated if she ever found out about his secret fantasy, and he does all he can to hide it from her. Keep your mouth shut, you old bag. He isn't sure why the older woman did not say anything, but a wave of relief washes over him as his eyes shift back to the young woman sitting a few yards away. Her skirt barely reached mid-thigh, the majority of her leg exposed for him to imagine running his tongue across. That isn't the only thing he can imagine running his tongue across, and watching the young girl use her index finger to tuck a stray strand of hair behind her ear gives him a clear view to her profile. There is no way she can be more than fifteen years old – just the way he likes them. The sound of someone pressing the bell in order to signal their stop, reigns him in from his thoughts. He watches as she stands up, her angelic smile lighting up her face as she turns to walk in his direction. He couldn't control himself any longer, and the warm, sticky fluid in his palm is something he wants to share with her. The fruity scent of her shampoo grabs a hold of his senses and drags him out the door behind her. He lets go of himself, wiping what he can onto the inside of his pocket as he follows closely behind her. Am I really going to do this? His heart played a techno beat in his chest, and he could taste the nervousness in the back of his throat. He had looked at pictures, he had masturbated to insane thoughts of deflowering a young girl just a few minutes ago, but actually following through with it was a whole new ball park he had yet to explore. Did he have the stones to do it, to take what he wanted by force. She smells so sweet... like strawberries. It feels like a swarm of bees is living inside his stomach, and he does everything he can to keep from vomiting all over her back. It is then that he notices that she is starting to walk faster. Did she see me? She rounds a corner, her walk resembling more of a sprint. He isn't that far behind, so when he rounds the corner and finds her near running toward the other end of the alleyway – his mind tells him to forget the whole thing and disappear. It is the voices coming from the other shoulder that convince him that the closed fence at the end of the alleyway have her trapped with nowhere to go. She wants you to follow her. She wants you to fuck her. She wants you. And so he follows her down the alley, quickening his own pace. She just now realized that there was no escape from this alley. He can see her banging against a door, searching for a way out of what he knows she wants. She's playing hard to get. She wants me to... The door opens and the girl slips inside. He starts to run, like a lion eager to pounce on an antelope. It is the large African American man peaking his head out from the open doorway, that causes him to stumble over his own feet trying to come to a stop. “Can I help you with something?” He can feel the raw skin from his fall on his elbows and palms, as he tries to push himself up off of the ground. “Hey, buddy, you deaf or something?” There is no way he is going to be able to weasel his way out of this one. This man was no child... He was no old woman. “There a reason you were following Rosa?” He needs to answer the man before he gets aggressive. Maybe he can talk his way out of this, after all. “I just wanted to talk to her.” “She's a little young for you, ain't she?” “Well, yeah. I – Well, you see.” He stands up, his mind calculating the time it would take him to reach the main street. Can he outrun the man standing a few feet from him? “No, I don't see.” The pale yellow shirt has the words 'Coach Dawkins' where the left breast pocket should be. “Why don't you come inside and explain it to me.” The man standing in front of him appears to be physically fit. Trying to outrun him would be stupid and a waste of time. “In – Inside?” Dawkins nods, “Yeah, you never know the type of people you can run into back here.” It is then that the man starts crying. Tears stream down his face as he collapses in Dawkins' arms. “I'm so sorry.” “You didn't do anything,” Dawkins replies. “I would have,” the man tells him between sobs. “I couldn't control it anymore.” “Temptation,” Dawkins says with a shake of his head. “It gets the best of us.” “Are you going to call the police?” “Now that depends.” Dawkins backs up a bit, then turns around and walks through the door. “You coming inside or not?” The man steps toward the door and stops, he glances from the empty doorway to the end of the alley for a brief moment.
