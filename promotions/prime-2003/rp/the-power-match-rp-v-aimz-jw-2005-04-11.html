The Power (Match RP - v Aimz/JW)

11 April 2005

Date: 11/04/05 The Power.Written: 11th April, 2005. 'But I can't help feeling like I'm due for a miracle,I'm waiting for a sign.I'll stare straight into the sun,And I won't close my eyes.' - Thrice. I - Do You Call My Name? They had every right to stand there in silence, save the occasional mutter about choking or being a disappointment. Just three weeks before, their chants, rasping their throats and leaving them sore for days, had helped carry Karina Wolfenden to victory over Boda. When the bell rang, when the exhausted Karina was handed the belt, soaked in sweat, and with muscles scorched, the crowd were just as ready to drop. No matter what was said about her by those on the internet, members of other promotions, or even her fellow PRIME-ates, the fans had stuck behind Karina Wolfenden. UltraViolence was meant to be her chance to pay back that faith. An opportunity to show she hadn't made a leap too far in capturing the Universal Title. A battle to prove that her record wasn't a fluke. And at the end of the night, Killean Sirrajin had pinned her lifeless body to the blood and sweat soaked canvas for the three count. The fact that the man who beat her was just as beloved by the PRIME fans didn't matter to Karina, as her own pride had taken such a blow that it couldn't differentiate. She'd begun to buy into her own hype, even devised a move to bring her the elusive 11th straight victory, and to make sure that the 'Global Curse' didn't go Universal. But still they chanted, 'KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA!' When Karina Wolfenden's head battled against the gravitational urge to hang low, they were there to grab a handful of lilac hair and yank it right back up. Karina left the Fleet Centre with short, slapping steps. The seemingly endless amounts of energy that gave her stride its bounce had been depleted by Killean, who absorbed everything she could throw at him, only to use it back against her twice as forcefully. It was like playing tennis against a wall, all you could do was marvel at your own impressive shot for a split second, before the realisation that it would come right back hit. Each time her foot rebounded off his body, Karina felt her breaths having to deepen, while every leap of her body turned up the burn in her muscles. Cracked, and with no insulation, her fight escaped, dissipating into the stuffy arena air, before Killean dealt the decisive blow with his Supreme Justice. Still feeling the warmth of the shower she'd stepped out of just 10 minutes ago, the chill hit Karina as soon as she heaved the double doors open. With the building's seal ruptured, the wild night flung itself inside, swirling wind almost plucking Karina out, only the ballast of the K-Wolf's bags keeping her grounded. She hadn't seen another wrestler since she sought refuge in her changing room, and only lingering event staff appeared to be hanging around. No doubt her fellow PRIME-ates were in the midst of rowdy victory celebrations, or licking their wounds back at their hotel room. So the last thing she expected to see was a huddled mass of PRIME fans, combating the cold in gloves and scarves, waiting outside to give her a hero’s send-off. There were no looks of pity, no heads shaken in veiled disappointment, just a pure desire to let Karina know that they were still proud of her. 'Ka-ri-na,' it began to swell with, smiles forming in the crowd as clenched fists ploughed through the foggy air. 'Ka-Ri-Na!' With the weight of her bags uneven, Karina gritted her teeth through the pain in her side, while steps tried their best to look normal length, opposed to a burnt-out stumble. They'd chanted the same thing when she was in costume, leaping from the ropes and spinning through the air... and they reprised the chant as she left, dressed down in jeans and a jumper, hair falling limp over her plain-featured face. 'KA-RI-NA!' it rose in volume to, brining a little vibrancy back to Karina's eyes, while the corners of her mouth began to raise into an appreciative smile. Barely able to raise an arm without toppling off balance and collapsing under her baggage at their feet, she pulled a grazed hand up into a faint wave nonetheless. 'Thank you.' She'd wished she had the energy to stay and talk, but it was the best she could do to remain standing, aching body craving the backseat of the ride she'd called in. At least she felt she owed them a little more than a simple thanks and a tired wave. But the spark they'd provided would help replenish her fully, letting her come back brighter at ReVolution, knowing she had a lot left to prove to herself... as well as the PRIME crowds. 'KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA!' echoed behind her, words spreading warmth through the bitter night. As a familiar face opened the car door for her, Karina knew she only had a few more paces to keep up appearances for, now wanting to repay their kudos and respect with a show of weakness. The bags were peeled from her shoulders, letting Karina collapse onto the back seat, heavy eyes jolted shut almost immediately. With her head nesting into the hood of her jumper, Karina let out a stifled yawn, holding it back as best she could for the sake of her aching side. As the driver's door clicked shut, and the seatbelt locked in, Karina's snoring was already filling the car. Thrown headlong into slumber by sheer exhaustion, all that could penetrate Karina's off-limits dream world were the empowered words of her fans. 'KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA! KA-RI-NA!' They'd treated her equal in both victory and defeat, shown that their adoration wasn't just a fair-weather friend to the K-Wolf. As she numbed her aches and pains with sleep, their words would stay with her. II - Magnificent Monotony. 'K-Wolf Saves New York!' had become a regular newspaper headline, and after the first few months, public adoration had forced even the harshest of editors to praise the young woman. For many years, apathy had dulled her gifts, and it was only when the sense of purpose grew from nurtured seeds of trust that her telekinetic talents blossomed. From petty muggers to new-age terrorists, from bank robbers to fellow 'Powers', she was there to save the day. Even when the stress threatened to cram the young woman back into her shell, the supportive words of those who adored her kept the fires of enthusiasm stoked. There were days where all she wanted to do was take a break, Snowboard in the French Alps or negate her powers for a Base Jump from one of Malaysia's majestic towers... but duty tied her to the city. 'Word has broken that there have been sightings of the Rave downtown,' the newscaster then interrupted the K-Wolf's MTV lounging with. A grainy piece of footage from a high-above weather helicopter did its best to zoom in on the K-Wolf's arch-nemesis, the woman she'd never been able to defeat, but the heroine’s emerald eyes were already darting away from the screen. Reality-orchestrating powers allowed the K-Wolf to tie her hair back hands-free, working a braid into the middle to show off the finesse of her control. Black velvet and lilac costume clung to her flat, toned body - allowing the padding enclosed to form her desired shape - while a gesture of the hand caused the balcony doors to separate like those of an aircraft hanger. 'Here we go again,' the dutiful heroine muttered, hovering off the floor as if it were the most natural of tasks. Locked in a far more graceful take on zero gravity, the K-Wolf willed her body above the air molecules beneath, then began to rotate around her x-axis, letting the faintest trace of gravity back into her world to accelerate her spin. Then, with enough kinetic energy built up, twisting the elastic band, a click of her fingers cut it loose, and accelerated her lithe body out into the midday cityscape. There was an easier life out there... but none that could mimic the soul-satisfying warmth of a simple thank you, gifted upon her by a protected citizen. Without fear she followed the sirens, and the sense of panic that screamed through her thoughts, knowing that she was about to come face to face with the undefeated. A true test of whether or not the adulation of many could overcome the destructive power of one redhead. III - The Source. 'Where's your K-Wolf now?' the Rave bellowed, licks of flame being expelled from her rasping tongue. All around the fiery redhead, panic began to overcome the normally level-headed populous. Fears for the safety of loved ones promoted selfishness, and barging shoulders began a domino effect, leaving the weak to tumble onto the cracking sidewalk, covering up against pounding feet. 'Do you really think she was ever a match for me?' was added by the fiery female, a heat-haze causing the air around her to ripple. Her footsteps left concrete sizzling, the scant moisture fizzing on the surfaces where her boots had peeled off, leaving a trace of rubber sole. Her outfit, black fishnet and vivid red, hung scantily, yet covered everywhere that modesty required. A mane of braided, scarlet streaked pink hair swung behind her, skipping off the ash-encrusted ground, somehow protected against being singed. One too many times she'd been thwarted by the K-Wolf - if not defeated - and there was no longer any financial gain to her criminal actions. Now it was all about the kudos, all about who the next day's headlines would declare the victor. Her fiery temper, realised in her powers, made the Rave a spontaneous adversary, and one to be rightfully feared. 'Where are you?' the Rave then lowered her voice with, words barely even producing a release of steam. 'Where. Are. YOU?' The Rave held her hands to her sides, pushing down towards the sidewalk and causing it to sway and crumble. The voids between slaps were filled with a molten orange, bubbling, and sending plumes of choking smoke out to further hinder the directionless, panicking masses. Her powers were of the soul, and at that moment in time, they were angry. To those watching above, too scared to make the decision about whether to flee, the gaps between the slaps spelt her question - Where Are You - like an orange, digital display. 'Where's the K-Wolf?' a panicking victim cried, hobbling on a twisted ankle as he tried to withstand the jolts and bumps of the crowd. 'Maybe she needs a louder alarm call,' the mischievous Rave sneered, raising herself into the air on a blanket of warm air. Even the red and orange of her fingernails seemed to flicker with flame as her eyes closed, crimson lips pursed tightly together. The Rave's body was then ravaged by a rippling shudder as toasted eyelids shot open. Heat-haze began to orbit her body like a cyclone, flashes of yellow, orange, and red almost creating a flaming cocoon around the Rave's intent body. Eyeballs caught alight, the blaze so ferocious that it threatened to gut the sockets, swelling the pupil to blanket the entire eyeball black. Screaming innocents began to flee from the building opposite as a few tiles were dislodged from the reception's entrance, and a length of guttering crashed to the floor, narrowly missing the escapees. Window panes began to crack, before the melting glass instantly repaired them, until frames warped and sent the remains plummeting to the ground. The red-brick exterior took on an orange hue, before the Rave flung her head back and launched her arms aloft, sending a white-hot fireball up through the buildings spine, blowing the roof off. As dust and rubble formed a suffocating rain, screams for family and friends went out, distant police sirens offering little hope. 'See?' her words sounded in flame, 'even the K-Wolf knows that she's no match for me!' With the air around her incinerating any debris before it even had a chance to strike her, the Rave floated back down onto the dynamic pavement, riding the slabs like a surfboard on a wave of lava. Smug satisfaction added true warmth to her fierce heat as she basked in the triumphant glow. 'Ehem,' then ruptured the shocked silence around the Rave, and attention turned hopefully to the K-Wolf. 'There she is!' the crowd began to hum. 'She's here! We're safe! Thank the Lord!' The rivals felt no need for banter. The fuming lack of conversation letting the bystanders know that they'd better swiftly turn into runawayers. Against a backdrop of pounding feet and screeching tyres, the K-Wolf flew in a high orbit above the Rave, conscious of any pockets of heated air suddenly forming to throw her off. Straightening her body, the K-Wolf drifted up higher, before flipping back to plummet down towards the Rave in what reporters had dubbed the Negasonic Lupine Warhead - a double-fisted suicide torpedo dive. Having done her homework, the Rave merely scoffed, gesturing nonchalantly to the manhole covers that littered the street, causing a plume of lava to send them flying. As white-hot metal slapped down against asphalt, the K-Wolf desperately tried to contort her speeding body around the columns of lava, which seemed to snake after her, before diving back down through the holes. With snakes of molten fury criss-crossing in front of her, the K-Wolf was forced to retreat back, leaving the mischievous redhead to snort ash. The K-Wolf then held her arms to the sides, spreading her fingers and toes out to increase her surface area. Shimmering green eyes rolled up into her skull as she pulsed her thoughts out like radar, exerting her control over every molecule they touched. Omega-class telekinesis let her thoughts feel the bitter, singed wood of the torched building, and the pitter-patter of the rats fleeing the sewers. It was virtual reality, like nobody had the vision to even... well, envision. She became the environment around the Rave, from every atom of brick-dust to every molecule or air. The warped window frames of the building began to rattle, offering up a threshold that with quickly overcome to pluck them from the debris. Before the eyes of the adoring public, the metal re-forged itself into a cage, entwining liquid strands like a rope was being woven. 'Wow,' they gasped from what was deemed a safe distance, wanting to get a first-hand look at their hero and savoir before the police cordons went up. Seeing that Karina's mind had encapsulated all matter around her, the Rave's spiteful side brought a sinister smirk across her lips. Battling artificial gravity to raise her crossed arms, she then ripped them apart, sending a blast of intense heat up towards the focussed K-Wolf, superheating the air around her. Like grabbing the handle of a hot pan, and not being able to withdraw quick enough, the K-Wolf felt the probing fingers of her mind blister. The intense heat flash-fried her brain, with no psychic cold-tap on any mental-plane to soothe them. A wail of agony went out as the K-Wolf plummeted to earth, the mental ropes that held her up singed and frayed. The gasps of the public sucked warmed air into dusty lungs. 'Nooooooooo!' some wailed as the K-Wolf impacted against the concrete, a blast crater burrowing itself out all around her, leaving the heroine’s body curled up around debris. 'You never could beat me, could you?' the Rave scoffed, forming a barrier of flame to keep the police units at bay. 'No matter how hard you try, no matter how many people cheer you on, you'll always make that mistake that PROVES I'm better. Because in life, that's all that really matters. Being better than the people standing in your way.' 'Is she dead?' a child cried, hugging her mother's shivering leg. 'Don't let her be dead! Someone has to stop the bad people...' The Rave licked her lips with a sizzle as she looked down at the prone K-Wolf, a gusty cough expelling a mixture of phlegm, blood, and ash. Slapping gloved hands on the jagged rock threatening to impale her, the K-Wolf crawled from the blast crater, while the Rave stalker her like a Matador, ready with the final, brightly coloured spike. Focussing her pyrotechnic powers into a lance of pure psychic flame, the Rave willed all her strength into the weapon, its tip threatening to tear even the air surrounding it. 'Any last, uneventful, and admittedly meaningless words?' the Rave queereed, spinning the lance between fingers. All the K-Wolf could do was let out a breathless groan as her attuned powers picked up on the rapid heartbeats of all those watching, the shivering going up their spine being felt down her own, throbbing column. Every pained sniffle, every desperate gasp, every defeatist whimper. She was their hero, and she wasn't about to betray their faith. 'No?' the Rave continued to mock, tossing the scorching javelin between her hands. 'Well, other than the 'uhk' you're about to expel.' Her love of the show taking hold, the Rave began to spin the lance above her head, only for the K-Wolf to lock swirling emerald eyes on it. 'Huh?' the Rave exclaimed, her wrist no longer able to twist. Squeezing every last bit of focus through lacerated skin and fractured bone, the K-Wolf expanded the lance with her thoughts, stretching it like elastic around the Rave, before finally pulling her thoughts away. 'Noooooo!' the frenzied cry of her rival went as the disintegrating heat was sucked into the vacuum encapsulating her, the incineration playing out in slow motion for the more strong-stomached of onlookers to watch. 'Is she?' the crowd began to ask as the last of the Rave's red and pink hairs were burnt to a black crisp. Uncooperative muscle slowed the K-Wolf's rise, her body spent of all energies the universe possessed, but the buoyant adulation of the public acted as a shoulder to lean on. Her lilac mane was frayed and singed, while her face was smeared in black ash... and the less said about her slashed and torn costume the better. But she'd won, fulfilled the hopes of her adoring public, and kept them safe for one more day. 'We love you,' the cheers began. 'Thank you! You rule!' 'All in a day's work,' the K-Wolf panted, appreciating the cheesiness of the line. 'All in a day's work...' IV - Comfort In Sound. 'All in a day's work...' the weary, dreamy Karina mumbled as she skirted close to the edge of slumber. 'All in a day's work...' As sketchy vision battled through the dark, Wolfenden lifted her aching head up off the back seat, hair all kinds of wild and tangled. Rubbing her eyes, Karina wished she hadn't tried to sit up so quickly as a strained sensation locked her sides down, while an itchy rawness cut across her neck from where she lay across the frayed edge of the seatbelt. 'You awake back there?' her ride asked, his stuffy Vauxhall a fair way away from the more luxurious Taxi service Angelo Deville had tried to force upon her. 'Define awake,' her aching words answered, each breath feeling like Killean was right there beside her, dishing out even more punishment. 'I think I'm breathing, if that's anything...' 'You'll be fine,' he then assured his friend, having known the K-Wolf long enough to know that the next morning never seemed to follow on from the night before as far as she was concerned. 'We're only 15 away, so won't be long now.' 'Yeah, I'll be fine,' Kari sighed with a proud smile as her body slid down deep into the seat, knowing that the same chants of support would still be there to meet her at ReVolution 61, whether Aimz was there to orchestrate them otherwise, or not.
