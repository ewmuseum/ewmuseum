Date: totally in the central time zone THE THREE ENEMIGOS By Teh List FADE IN: EXT. WIDE OPEN PLAINS - EVENING The setting sun casts an orange glow and long shadows across the floor of the Wild Western flatland. Amidst the empty husk of a town that we find ourselves stranded in, nothing seems to breathe. No people. No life. A shop sign swings heavily in the breeze, slapping just slightly against the beam from which it hangs, giving us that rustic ghost town sound to go along with the image. The faded brown of the wood sinks into the burnt orange of the sky and ground, putting everything into a kind of clay colored fog, devoid of anything that might remotely resemble comfortable. Three burly, hulking shadows step into the scene, preceding their sources by a good twenty feet. They're either men on horseback, or the most fucked up camels you've ever seen. The mammoth shadows pause at the mouth of this yawning skeleton of a village, considering for a moment that maybe they should just move on, that this place isn't what they were told it was. When there's nothing to see except sand and a horse's ass - and the animal he was riding on - it's not difficult to make a wrong turn. Maybe this was not the right place. HORSEMAN UNO Maybe this isn't the right place. See? HORSEMAN DOS I checked the map. This has to be it. HORSEMAN UNO If this is it, then I'm going the fuck home. The shadow on the far left pulls up a little and his steed begins to double back in preparation for one of the Top 20 hastiest retreats this sector of the West has ever seen. HORSEMAN TRES We came all the way out here and you're going to just turn around? HORSEMAN UNO Of course not. Nobody writes a scene where the guy just turns around and heads home. I'm just waiting for one of you limp-dicks to give me a reason to stay. HORSEMAN TRES Okay, but first you're going to have to tell me why 'limp-dicks' is an insult here. Am I supposed to be aroused by either of you, or the ponies you're riding on? HORSEMAN DOS Dude, if I was a horse, I'd totally Barbaro this little bitch right here. He slaps the hind quarters of his own ride, and she bucks up onto her hind legs, needing to be calmed down before this scene continues. HORSEMAN DOS Take it easy, babe. I said 'if.' (to his comrades) She's fiesty. HORSEMAN UNO Okay, I think I AM leaving after all. Before he can turn himself around, a fourth voice, somewhat disembodied as he doesn't have a shadow to go along with it, calls out from beyond the town limits. STRANGER Are you the ones? The shadows don't reply. Or rather, the sources of the shadows don't reply. Shadows can't talk, silly. STRANGER The boys from Hollywood? Are you them? With that, we swing around to get a good look at our horsemen, who were certainly done a lot of justice by a setting sun. They are not nearly as large and intimidating as their projections. As a matter of fact, they're about as far from intimidating as you can get. As you may have guessed, they are our heros, or the closest facsimile. They are The A-List. All three in ponchos. All three in sombreros. Facey somehow wearing both a cape and a mask in addition to the other stuff. All three of them sweating balls. HORSEMAN UNO (AKA CHANDLER TSONDA) I guess this is the place. HORSEMAN TRES (AKA DANNY FERGUSON) Told you. HORSEMAN DOS (AKA THE ILLUSTRIOUS F.E.) Barkeep! Fix me some of your finest marijuana! STRANGER My finest what now? THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE EATER Don't hold out on me, old man! Hiya! With that, Facey's horse takes off, chasing the old man down the street and firing a gun - we don't know where he got it from - in the air, Yosemite-Sam-style. Chandler and Danny remain behind. CHANDLER TSONDA And you say these people just e mailed you? DANNY FERGUSON Yeah. You'd be amazed at how strong wireless connections are these days. CHANDLER TSONDA And they thought you were an action hero? DANNY FERGUSON And that I could save their village. CHANDLER TSONDA And now we're going to rip them all off and burn it to the ground? DANNY FERGUSON More or less. And with that, the joke gets tired and the deadline has long since passed. There was probably more material here to prove that the A-List is the best trio in all of PRIME, but Noble's bitch-ass doesn't even have a partner, so we're just going to beat up on him anyway. The List ain't got nothing to prove. We pan up into the orange sky as gunshots echo across the nameless frontier. FADE TO BLACK.
