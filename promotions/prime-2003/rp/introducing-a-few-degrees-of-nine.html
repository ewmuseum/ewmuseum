Introducing A Few Degrees Of Nine

Date: Several different ones "None the less, the following became universally known: first, that he maintains that the soul is immortal; second, that it changes into other kinds of living things; third, that events recur in certain cycles and that nothing is ever absolutely new; and fourth, that all living things should be regarded as akin. Pythagoras seems to have been the first to bring these beliefs into Greece." - Porphyrius, Life of Pythagoras 19. --------------------------------------------- This was the first time Owen Farhat ever had a knife held to his throat. Even at the age of thirteen he was obsessive, compulsive and this was highly unusual. The sweat that seeped from his pores escaped at an alarmed, frantic pace. His heart was a stampede that did not relent, thumping harder than anything he'd felt in his life. "I love you," she said to him placing a kiss on each cheek. Her naturally pouty lips were soft and always sent shivers down his back. However, it appeared to the bright, young mind that she was utterly insane. So, after careful deliberation for all of about five minutes, he had decided to break her heart and end the relationship. This did not fester nicely in the belly of the 19 year old, demented blonde. "I know you do," he replied. Not in the tense of holier-than-thou, or better than her that he normally spoke in. Rather, it was said in a way that showed something far worse to any woman: Pity. Or charity. She pressed the dull edge of the knife to his throat harder. A 180 degree turn would cut his jugular and end his life.Of course, he observed, a multiple of 9. He was not surprised. "Do you think I'm some sort of whore? A fucking concubine?" Owen replied teary-eyed, "No." "So I just fucked your brains out for no reason, then? Huh? None of that meant anything to you, you bastard?" "...I'm just thirteen." The blonde's cold eyes narrowed. There was something as sharp as the knife she gripped so tight about the words that escaped her aforementioned lips in the next moment. "Not for long." The knife finally turned 180 degrees with a swift flick of the wrist. Owen's hands covered the wound of his neck. Before his eyes closed he saw his parents step into the house. He heard their screams. He looked at the calendar: March 5, 1999 03+05+1999=2007 2+7=9 Something in that comforted him as his eyes closed, despite the loud sirens blaring just outside. --------------------------------------------- "So, you're telling me what, exactly? You changed your name to Nine and that you're really into Pythagoras?" It was dark outside. The house they sat in still held the tinge of the 1950s on its inside from when it was built. Strange, pre-retro tile lining the surfaces where there wasn't ugly, shaggier than shag carpet. The two men sat on a couch while the t.v. blared unimportant news of how some fire burned down some apartments in some area of Metro Atlanta. While the dumbest of the unfortunates were broadcast sharing their side of what happened, the owner of the house passed Nine what remained of a joint. The grown man with a scar still on his neck (hidden by the scruff of post-pubescent life) flashed a grin at his hispanic friend. "It's more than that. I'm not just really into Pythagoras. I'm a numerologist. A Pythagorean at that." Nine had changed quite a bit, not just on his driver's license. He had grown out his hair and a full, thick beard. The kind of man you might find following the likes of Jesus Christ, eating locusts in the desert. "I see, a Pythagorean. Tell me, do you drive a hybrid and enjoy the scent of your own shit?" Nine passed the joint during his rebuttal, "No. But I am a mathematician and a black belt in judo. So I should probably start, eh Carlos?" Carlos wasn't a gang banger and had no desire to do anything of the sort. He was just a simple man. The type of guy that worked, came home and didn't bother anyone. It just so happened he enjoyed smoking marijuana as well. This wasn't a problem in Nine's eyes, who indulged freely himself at times. "A black belt in judo? What the fuck for?" Carlos attatched a clip to the end of what had become a roach of its former self. "Seriously?" Carlos grinned himself, "Yeah man. Seriously." "Well, I got tired of attempts on my life." This was an answer said in such a surprising, non-chalant way, that Carlos regretted taking a gulp of his beer because he spit it out in shock. "ATTEMPTS ON YOUR LIFE?" --------------------------------------------- ...third, that events recur in certain cycles and that nothing is ever absolutely new..." - Porphyrius, Life of Pythagoras 19. It's not often that someone in this world experiences the panic and horror of someone taking your life from you. Unfortunately on February 2nd, 2003 for Owen Farhat, the second attempt on his life was made. Jordan Tatum, the type of redneck you'd only find in a town like Homerville, Georgia, had been very forward in getting Owen to help him cheat on his first semester math final. The kid was college bound off of his football prowess. However with his high school transcript he'd more than likely be playing for Georgia Southern than the University of Georgia, his dream school. Although if things didn't change academically, even Southern would have a hard time granting him a scholarship. So, Owen agreed. Deep down, he had not forgotten the hell Jordan Tatum had put him through. Beating him up just because he was smaller, making every school day a mortal Hell. Jordan had been a senior in high school now for his 2nd straight year. Farhat wasn't a sophomore anymore and had grown into a man himself. Feeling confident in his ability at the time to defend himself, Owen came up with a plan that would not only fuck over Mr. Tatum but show him that he would always be beneath the likes of Farhat. It was ridiculous that Homerville allowed Tatum to be in a strong mathematics class anyway. So, he memorized his calculus final questions and put down the wrong answers for Jordan to copy. Then, just three minutes before class was over, erased his own answers and put the correct ones in their place. Two months later, this was a decision that he regretted dearly. He was flogged nearly to death by five guys in varsity highschool jackets. They were to make an example of him, but the drunken teens had taken it too far to turn back. Having realized much later that he could report this to the police, Tatum decided his future was more important than Owen's life. So they drug him to a swampier area of the town. They hit him when he whimpered. Knives were in each of their hands. He couldn't stop sobbing. Pretty soon Owen found himself in murkey waters on his knees. Surrounded by kids with knives. "...I'm just thirteen..." Jordan snapped, "Shut the fuck up you stupid fuck. You're 17." He had a bag over his head. "Are you sure you want to do this?" One of his cronies asked. Jordan screamed, "SHUT THE FUCK UP! I'M THINKING!" "Well... fuck you dude. Fuck you. I'm leaving." "Yeah man... yeah, me too." "Fine," Jordan answered. "All of you just fucking leave then. Traitors. Turncoats! Fucking yankee loving bastards anyway..." Pretty soon they were all filing out, one by one in their Ford and Chevy trucks. Shortly afterwards, Jordan had made up his mind. He traced the scar across Owen's neck left by a knife with his very own. --------------------------------------------- "So... Yeah," Nine answered, hitting the joint. "Attempts." Carlos sat in a kind of weird silence. The man wasn't the type of person who believed in fate or destiny and definately not numerology. He also wasn't the type of person to tell things about his past. Nine knew Carlos as his present self and was perfectly fine with who he was. This was just a strange coincedence, Carlos thought, but that doesn't change the fact that were he to lift up his shirt he'd reveal knife wounds of his own. Soon, the television caught his eye. "Check it out, the peach is dropping." Indeed. From the 60 second mark the large, televised peach sunk in Underground Atlanta, to mark the New Year. Couples were gathered and ready to celebrate. Hell, thousands were. And even the two men in the dingy house, high and with way more in common than either knew, counted down from 10. "HAPPY NEW YEEEEEEEEEAR!" They hung out for another couple of hours, eating burritos and watching kung-fu movies. When it was all said and done and Nine was ready to leave, they embraced. Tomorrow, he'd be leaving to train for professional wrestling and it would be a while before they saw each other. "Carlos, man, I love you." "Don't be gay. See you later my friend. Have a good '07." Nine grinned on his way out, "2007. A good year." "Friends are as companions on a journey, who ought to aid each other to persevere in the road to a happier life." - Pythagoras
