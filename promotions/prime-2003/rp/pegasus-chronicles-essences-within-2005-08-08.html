Pegasus Chronicles – Essences Within

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 8th, 2005 Prologue. A whisper heard on a stormy night, is a secret told to a loved one in fright. That voice that drops in the dark, knowing nobody is there, but their hair standing at end from fear. It’s a moment of stress combined with the need of protection. Mixing together, it brings out the true personalities of any human being. In wrestling, a simple slap can change the progression of a wrestler’s attitude. That’s why some men are bound to succeed, and others develop into the local whipping boy. Determination is a push of the will, and those who can summon it, go far in the wrestling business. Yet now, a man with determination pouring from his fingertips is in desire of more. He must dig deep, to overcome this drawback of an injury in his shoulder. Overcome 25 men last week in the battle royal, Jesse proved he could push the limits on his arm, but Karina Wolfenden made sure he wouldn’t push any further when she laid him out, and pushed him under the bottom rope. This week, Jesse faces Peter Vetra. Known as one of the best highflying technicians in PRIME, he proves to be a threat if he can knock Jesse down and expose his injured shoulder. Time will tell, as this Warrior goes into training, and digs into his past roots…Story Continues… Cringing sites of blood tainted walls. A dungeon like atmosphere surrounds the Nemesis Warrior, as he stands in the middle of a dim lighted room. His body glistening from water and his hair let down past his shoulders, no longer in braids as it was on ReVolution the week before. Shrugging his shoulders, Jesse looks to be in preparation to start a training regiment, yet there is no weights, no jump rope, not even bars to hang from to do pull ups. Merely a ten by ten room, with a wooden door, and old stonewalls surround him. The floor is replaced with a thick layer of sand, answering the question as to why Jesse has no shoes on. Ahhhh… the history of this place, the memories I have of my training, and the nightmares of the brutality that was unleashed here. So much happened here, it’s like a movie going through my mind at fast forward. Sure did help me though.. Taking a seat in the sand, he reaches down and scoops up a handful of sand, letting it slip through his fingers. Leaning back he gazes at the ceiling where a dinky light bulb is placed to illuminate the room. His face emotionless, unlike ever before, as the blue in his eyes begins to twinkle in the light. Ah… the men who became warriors in this room is a reason alone for me still being alive today. Had I not been taken off the streets and guided under the wings of Trigger, I shall say my life today would be much different. Spreading his legs out, he extends his arms out touching the tips of his toes, and then holds the position for a good half-minute. Releasing, he inhales and then exhales, letting fresh air cycle his windpipes. If theirs one place I can find peace, here is that place. No distractions, no responsibilities, and no worries. Here… I find solemn serenity. Placing his left arm out, he reaches for his right toe, and does this for a half-minute, then does vice-versa with the other leg. Letting go, he places both legs together and reaches forward grabbing his toes, this time holding the position for a minute. Releasing, he exhales and inhales, showing a routine in his breathing. Taking time to gather your thoughts is important. Had I done this last week, I may have proved myself worthier in the Battle Royal… yet lasting twenty-five people is pretty impressive for a debut show. This week will prove to be even more impressive as Vetra and I go at it. I have some new tricks in my bag for him. Rolling his neck to the right five rotations, he reverses it and goes left, before stopping. Placing his right foot into his left thigh, he reaches forward with both hands grabbing the bottom of his left foot, and stretching his torso and legs all at once. … It’s a shame though, how people perceive me. Since I left Global Wrestling, my fan base has steadily dropped, as they feel I backstabbed the company, and left them in a vulnerable situation. Truly though, I made a business move. Global is a sinking ship, while PRIME is a shooting star. In Global, my stock was raising by the pennies, while here their rising with fat zeros at the end of my paycheck. I know I always vowed to love this sport for just that, the sport, but theirs a time when a man has to step up and start doing it for the reason it keeps him alive, and that’s the money. Reversing the stretch to his right leg, Jesse continues doing his routine, and then when done with a stretch, would exhale then inhale taking in fresh air. Theirs no shame in being here for the money, not when you are good enough to prove your worth it. I know theirs many men in this business who do it strictly for the money, yet can’t back up a dime of the money their given in that ring. I on the other hand… (Breathes out) can do all that and more, running circles around anyone who feels they have the nerve to challenge me. Inhaling, his face expresses a deep concentration as he places his hands to the sand and then pushes himself to his feet, where he begins squatting and leaning to the side, stretching the legs a little more. Theirs a chance I may not always win, but that doesn’t stop me from proving I want it more than the other person. Anyways, when did wins count for anything? A man can try to all extents, pumping his sweat from every pore, and still come up short to a man who just happens to have a trick up his sleeve, or a weapon at his disposal. It’s just not everybody’s night, and those who lose must deal with it by picking up the pieces and assembling their engine better the second time around. Spreading his legs to the outside, he reaches down and drags his fingers in the sand like a rake, then stops and plants his palms flat as he applies pressure to his groin muscles. His hair slides down from his shoulders in front of his face, yet shows no distraction as he has his eyes closed during this stretch. At ReVolution, it’s not about winning for me… it’s about finishing off a veteran whose time has passed. For now is the time of a new anarchy, a time that is granted to a younger generation of the PRIME-ates. To me, it’s my time… to shine. Vetra is merely a rest stop I must pass on this highway to my goal. Suddenly, Jesse takes a fall and lands on his back. The landing seemed to hurt, but with sand it’s unknown whether or not it did. Yet, in an instance, we find out, as he nips up landing on his feet and standing in a fighting position. In one night… I’m going to make allies, enemies, and cause controversy that will surely put me in the view of many fans eyes as genuine evil. Whether or not they like Peter Vetra, is seriously an unaware and unneeded knowledge that floats in my mind. Yet, at the end of the night, he’ll be left to collect his own pieces and crawl back to his hotel to lick his wounds. Gripping the door handle, the knob is turned, and the door opens ever so slightly. A foot steps through, and then an arm appears. Shuffling to a side step, Jesse prepares himself for whatever is to come through, before the door swings open completely…
