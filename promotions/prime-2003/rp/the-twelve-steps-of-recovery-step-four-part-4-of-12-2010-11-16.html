Date: 11/16/2010 Intro…… There are 12 steps to recovering from addiction. These 12 steps have helped millions of alcoholics and addicts overcome the demons of addiction. I’ve addressed Step 1 and admitted I was powerless to the disease. I took on Step 2 where I came to believe that a Power greater than ourselves could restore us to sanity. In Step 3 I 'made a decision to turn my life and my will over to God as I understood him. Now we move onto Step 4 of the 12 steps and how it helped me overcome my addiction to drugs and alcohol.…… ****PRESENT DAY**** “Bro.....Bro... are you all right?” I suddenly snapped out of the trance I was in. David looked so far away from me, yet he was right next to me. “Chris..... you there?” It's been a rough couple of months since winning the Five Star Championship. Being Five Star meant I was better than everyone else. And I've not been afraid to let everyone know that. The First, Muse, the fans of PRIME. Lisa Tyler. You've all been witness to the greatness of Boda as The Greatest Five Star Champion ever. I've walked the walk and talked the talk, but for some reason, for some unknown, minuscule reason, Nitz Donnelly was getting to me. He outsmarted me and took possession of the title. He hadn't beaten me for it, but he stole it like a shoplifter at WalMart. He was always a step ahead of me, until last week. I finally got back on my game, and beat Nitz to the punch. “Yeah, Dave. I'm cool.” I replied. “I was just thinking about what happened over the last few weeks. It really bothers me that I let Donnelly get to me like that. I'm better that he is, yet he was able to outmaneuver, out think me at every turn.” David tried to be reassuring. “It's over now. You got the belt back. And you'll be walking down the aisle with the gold. And dammit.... you know you'll be walking out with it too.” And then I was left to think..... I know I am confident and could beat anyone in PRIME at the drop of a hat. But for some reason, I had reservations about the match against Donnelly. My mind slipped back to a time when I was rebuilding my confidence, learning about the mistakes I've made, the people I've heard before. I closed my eyes, and I was suddenly back in the recovery center, where I spent almost a year of my life, learning to become a better father, husband and person. ****Place: The Spirit of Life Recovery Center**** ****Date: Sometime near the end of May, 2008**** "The year was 2006. I was in a downward spiral. I was flat on the mat, primed to be pinned for a three count. My wife finally convinced me to see the doctor. And what followed next was something I would spend the next four years of my life overcoming. When I heard the words 'cancer' from the doctor, I broke down. I was distraught. Hell... I was fucking scared. I cried. I looked my wife and kids in the eyes and cried. I felt vulnerable for the first time in my life. I'd wrestled Ivan, Meanstreak Jason Snow and Xavier Kannon. But I was scared of wrestling the cancer. And that is where I believe I turned to alcohol and drugs. Sure, it was thyroid cancer. Sure it was beatable. But looking back, something in my mind made my recollections for common sense go into hibernation. I fell into despair. I fell into a cycle of using and abusing. I ignored my children's attention. I ignored my wife's sexual needs. I bedded with hookers and sluts. I was no longer a world class athlete.... I had become a world class asshole and crack head. The surgery to remove the tumor in my throat and muscle around it was a success. When I woke from the hospital the next morning, I saw the resulting scar. Stacey assured me it didn't look bad, but in my mind, my depressed and scared mind, I looked like a monster. I let that thought eat at me while in the hospital for the next few days. I was standoffish. I was irritable and didn't want anyone to visit me. On the day I was released, I was told that in two days, I had to start treatment. This meant that Stacey and the girls had to leave the home for 2 weeks, so I could be treated with radiation and not be a threat to small kids, animals and women, That didn't mean I wasn't going to be a threat to myself. And threat I was. I went on a binge. A major blow out of a binge. At this point, I wanted to die. I had 60 stitches in my neck, was wearing two bags to drain the blood and fluid leaking from the surgery site. I had the energy of a 100 year old dead man. And I hated it. I couldn't wrestle. The family was not around.......Sorry...I need to take a break" I was choking up. Wait, that's a lie... I was in fucking tears. Rusty seemed to understand. "Listen Chris...." Rusty put a reassuring hand on my shoulder. "Step 4 is a tough one. It's the principal that 'We made a searching and fearless moral inventory of ourselves. You're searching deep inside yourself. You're doing some soul searching. Take as much time as you need. Matter of fact, let's get some lunch and we'll get back to this in a bit." Rusty headed off to the dinner hall, to grab some lunch. I stepped out onto the back deck of our Room #7. It was a beautiful day. There was a different smell in the air today. Almost like there was a freshness in the air. Which, compared to the usual smell of the mushroom farm fertilizer about a mile away, was very welcomed. There were a couple of boats and water skiers in the near distance on Lake Ola. It brought back memories of a better time in my life...... "Daddy.... can we go out on the boat?" Kenzie stuck her head in our bedroom. I rolled over and kissed Stacey on the forehead. "What time is it?" was her reply? "7 in the morning. What can I say, she's happy I'm home for a week" "Well daddy? Can we?" Carleigh chimes in now to. Sometimes, I think these two made plans at night to do what they could to wake me and their mom up too early when I am home. Stacey accepted it.... it was part of being married to a professional wrestler. The girls were born into it. Hell.... they've been a part of it. But Stacey had to raise them when I was on the road some 300 days out of the year. So when I was home, it was Daddy and his girls time. The thoughts of a happier time started to depress me when I looked around the large backyard and was snapped back to the reality of being a victim of the disease I was trying to defeat. A few of the guys in the program where playing horseshoes in the far end of the yard. I slowly walked towards the chow hall, wishing I was back on that boat with the girls. I was beginning to believe in this program. Part of me still wanted to get fucking high, but every week it was becoming a smaller and smaller piece. I was starting to learn to cope with life, learning to face life on life's terms. It was a slow process, but somehow it was working. "Hey champ" I stop in my tracks, as Jason, one of the newer guys in the program stepped out of his room and stepped into the same stride as me. "What you doing?" "Hey man.... heading to get some lunch? What bout you?" "Gonna work out.... want to join me?" Jason was a muscle head. And a heroin addict. He was a good kid. He also had a young son and daughter. And his wife..... what a hot piece of ass. "Maybe later tonight. I'm working my steps with Rusty today." I really wanted to hit the iron again. It's really been a long time, hell, almost a year since the last time I threw up some metal and grunted like a gorilla. "That's cool. Hey, I really wanted to ask you.... " Jason was a shy guy though, and really didn't like to ask for help. "would you mind helping me with my step work?" Helping others was part of the steps to recovery. It was giving back to others. When I entered recovery, I wanted nothing to do with anyone. I wanted to do my time, get better and get back with the wife and kids. But something changed the last month. I was opening up more, laughing more. Could I be becoming a better person? Was I starting to learn to live my life drug free? Perhaps...... "What's for lunch?" I elbowed Rusty. He was busy talking to one of the newer guys that just arrived last night. You could see the dejection in the young man's face. I decided to introduce myself, but was cut off by the new guy. "Holy Shit..... You're Boda...." I was somewhat taken back by this kid knowing me. Maybe I was off my game a little today. "Yes I am... how are you?" I put my hand out to shake his. "Boda... you have no idea what this means to me. I've been a fan of yours for so long. I was there during the PCW2 days, the Red Army run, the Vampir Nosferatu feud. I've seen you win and lose championships..... Hell, I stalked Karina Wolfenden after she cheated and beat you for the Universal Title at ReVolution 58." I shot a smile at the kid. You could tell how excited he was to meet one of his pro athlete heroes. "Relax kid. What's your name?" "Spanky" "Of course it is. Well, Spanky.... welcome to Spirit of Life Recovery. Grab a bite to eat, and take a seat with me and Rusty. You'll do just fine here." In reality..... 10 out of every 100 men that came through this program to beat their addiction, made it. The other 90 didn't. Spanky was one of the 90. Three weeks after getting into the program, he was shot to death in the front parking lot, after trying to rip off his dealer. That's a cold hard fact about addiction.... you either beat it, or it beats you. End of story. So there I was, sitting at a table with Rusty and Spanky. For the first time in months, I was smiling. I was actually on the right path. For the first time since becoming a resident at The Spirit of Life, I was enjoying the very bland and dull turkey and swiss sandwich that Big Momma made for us today. ****PRESENT DAY**** “Chris, phone call for you.” David calls out to me. My train of thought, my recollections of my time spent at Spirit of Life is interrupted by someone trying to reach out and touch me. I pull myself off my white leather couch and meander over to my home office. Calling out from the office, I let my brother know I have the phone. “Hello........sure.......how's it going? Well, I'm all set for what will go down as a historic night..... what bout you?..........Sure.......Yes...... I know we had a deal...... I chuckled. You were doing great. Let's just roll on.” I lay down on my bottom bunk, my feet hanging over the end. Laying my head on the plastic pillows we are given when we arrive, I almost want to go to sleep. But I press on, knowing that if I want to beat this addiction and get back to my own life, I had to move forward. “The day of my first treatment, I tried to kill myself. I was so depressed, I spent a grand on some crack and meth. After the doctor released me, I went home and locked myself in my house. It was so empty, so cold. I started partying by myself, and I didn't' stop. Hours later, I was so out of it, I couldn't think straight. I could barely walk. The darkness inside my living room crept into my head like an evil monster. Suddenly I was startled by the door bell. Part of me said fuck that door bell.... the rest of me knew I had to answer the door. John I stood up, and braced myself against the wall. Chris.... wake up.......' I was out for 30 minutes according to John. He said I didn't even say hello, I was down for the count. He somehow managed to get me into the bathtub and cover me with cold water and ice. I tried to overdose and end my life. I was pathetic, depressed and had no desire to be alive. I thought 'how could I be doing this to them'?. I refused to go to the hospital. But I didn't refuse to change my ways. I continued my ways. Even after the cancer treatment was over. I couldn't stop using. I couldn't make it through the day with out numbing my soul and my spirit. I pushed the ones that loved me further and further away. I started sleeping around on Stacey. She didn't deserve it. She deserved my loving embrace, but I didn't give a fuck. If it had a pussy, I put my dick in it. I even spent many a nights in this same room, when it was a pay by the hour motel for fucking the local sluts. The things I did, they didn't represent the true me. I was disgracing my family, my profession, and most importantly, myself. But I couldn't stop. I wanted to, but I didn't. I wasn't being a champion of life, let alone the wrestling industry. I didn't care who I hurt, as long as I was hurting myself. Dammit.... that's about it man..... I really can't go on anymore.” “Very good stuff man. You've got a lot of issues inside you that you've been able to get out today.” Rusty reassures. “That's pretty much what Step Four covers. You made a searching and fearless moral inventory of yourself. You did that. How's it feel?” I won't lie. I couldn't. It didn't feel good that's for damn sure. “It feels like shit. I never realized that I was such a piece of shit, a pile of monkey feces. This addiction took me places I never thought I'd visit. And Rusty..... I don't want to be there anymore.” “Keep up the good work, and you won't have to be Chris.” ****PRESENT DAY**** “DAVID......” I yelled from my home office, for David. POOOOOOOFFFFFFFFFFFWWWWWAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH David lets go and steps back. “Now how do you feel?” “You know what? It wasn't me being worried about the match at all, it was a fart I was feeling. I've taken Five Star to a new level..... and come Rev 234...... Nitz Donnelly will be wishing I was still in rehab, and not using him as another stepping stone to the top of PRIME.
