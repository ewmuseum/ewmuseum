The Search for Answers...and the Perfect Coconut

20 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/20/08 After walking for what had seemed like hours, Polunu had finally found the perfect spot. In front of him stood a cluster of coconut palm trees with dozens and dozens of coconuts affixed at the top, ready to be harvested by Polunu, who was staring in awe at the trees. Beside the coconut sculptor stood his good friend, Bryan Dawkins, who didn’t seem to be as enthused by the trees as Polunu. Squinting up towards the tops of the trees, Dawkins questioned his longtime friend as to the purpose of their trip. "So, uh…why am I here again?" Polunu pulled his gaze from the coconuts and focused on Dawkins, giving him a nasty look. "You’ve OBVIOUSLY never been coconut picking." Dawkins rolled his eyes and let out a sigh. "No, Polunu, I’ve unfortunately never been coconut picking before. But you still haven’t answered my question, bruh. Why am I here? This seems like a one-man job to me. You climb up the tree, cut the coconuts down, and let them land in the sand. Collect the coconuts, take them back to your place, and you proceed to carve faces into them. I’m STILL not seeing where I factor into the equation." Dawkins peered to his side after a few moments of silence, only to find he was alone. After returning his gaze to the trees, he finds that Polunu is almost halfway up the trunk of the first palm and shimmying his way further towards his destination at the top. "So...I just stand here, then, bruh?" After trying to catch his breath for a few moments following reaching the top of the trees, Polunu pulled out a small machete and swung it at a cluster of coconuts, setting a handful of them free. "Polunu! Where the hell did you get one of those, bruh? I didn’t even see that thing on the way over!" Looking confused and awkward, Polunu gazed down at Dawkins. "What are you talking about, bruh?" After realizing the context that his earlier statement could have been taken in, Dawkins decided to rephrase. "The knife. The machete. The blade. Whatever the hell you just cut those coconuts down with. That thing’s ridiculous." Looking a little less confused, Polunu looked at his blade and smiled. His grandfather had given the knife to him when he was in his teenage years and Polunu vowed to never let it out of the family. It was a family keepsake, and Polunu had used it on every coconut harvest he partook on since he became a coconut artist. "Ah, the blade? Yeah, she’s a beaut’, isn’t she? Got her from my pap when I was younger." "How old were ya, bruh?" "Oh, maybe nine or ten? Why you ask, bruh?" Dawkins’ eyes nearly popped out of his head after hearing Polunu received the blade when he was hardly a decade old. Bryan barely trusted him with it now, so it’s understandable why he would be concerned that he got the knife at such an early age. "No reason…" Bryan’s voice trailed off as Polunu shrugged and took a swing at the next cluster of coconuts. Just as quickly, six more coconuts came raining down onto the sandy beach next to Dawkins, just missing him and causing him to jump. The Flyin’ Hawaiian slowly inched backwards a couple feet and peered back up at his childhood friend, who was already gazing down at him. "Yeah Bry, it’d probably be a good idea if you would back up a little bit. The last thing you need is one of these things to crack you in the head. Somethin’ tells me that you’re gonna be taking enough punishment this weekend anyway. That Jason Natas seems like he’s got your number, bruh…" "Don’t be worryin’ about my situation with that behemoth." Dawkins sneered at gazed at one of the coconuts which had cracked open upon impact with another coconut. "I’ve got my ways of dealing with him. You just make sure you’re not takin’ me off my game by gettin’ me whacked with one of these things." Dawkins pointed out the cracked coconut to Polunu. "Like you said, I don’t need to be in any sort of compromised condition when I take him on. I’m not even thinkin’ about the match. It’s nothin’ to me, bruh." Polunu smiled and hacked another group down. This group landed a mere foot away from Bryan, who was obviously not at attention, because he didn’t even flinch this time. "Ah, now that’s the Bryan Dawkins I know, bruh! Not worryin’ about his competition and just rollin’ with the punches! And from what I saw, you’ll be takin’ on plenty of those with Natas. Dude was a streetfighter for how long, bruh. Gotta watch what you’re doin’ around him if you wanna walk out of Wrigley Field with your head still attached to your shoulders." "Shut up, Polunu. You act like I need ya to tell me what the guy does. I’ve been dealin’ with him for the last two months, bruh. All I’m gonna do is take him out of his element and make sure he can’t keep up with me, ya know, bruh?" "Yeah, I hear ya." With that, Polunu took another swing at the top of the tree, dislodging another couple coconuts. "Yeah, I’ll be fine this Sunday, bruh. Ya just gotta trust me—" CRACK! "WATCH OU…out."
