Seeking Leverage

18 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/18/05 It’s been a good couple of weeks for Tyler Nelson. The man who once ruled the wrestling world as CEO of Primetime Championship Wrestling was back in action….sort of. After a nearly two year hiatus from wrestling, courtesy of the United States Department of Justice, Tyler Nelson got back in the game. Making an astonishing return to the sport, he used one of his front companies to lure the CEO of PRIME, Hin See, into turning to him for money. Certainly a win-win situation for The Greediest Player in the Game. If PRIME flourishes then Tyler gets handsome dividends paid to him in the form of interest on the money loaned. And we all know how Tyler Nelson feels about money. But if PRIME should falter….if it should fail and flounder under the pressure of competition, then it would fall into the lap of the man who for years worked so hard to destroy its predecessors. The dream of Sebastian PRIME would belong to his hated rival to do with what he wished. If there was one thing that could conflict the emotions of Tyler Nelson and force him to choose between it and money…..it would be POWER. The trap is set. The venomous snake has injected its victim with deadly poison. Now he just waited for it to die so that he could devour it whole. With the juicy rabbit now running off to slowly die, the deadly viper could turn its attention to another issue…..a favor. A favor for the man who helped him wiggle his way into the bidding to be PRIME’s so-called savior. The rabbit would keep. It was a favor that Tyler took pleasure in. It was simple. It was fun. And by God it felt good! All he had to do was lay out some Mexican with a broken back. Easy enough. He’d dealt with Mexicans before. He ran the White Mexican out of PCW. He beat Frisco for the PCW World Title in that famous four-way steel cage match. He fired several Mexicans from his grounds keeping crew just the other day. Tyler Nelson knew how to deal with Mexicans. Show them who the boss is. And that’s what he did. With the crack of a steel briefcase over the back of Emilio Rage’s head, Tyler showed the Nicaraguan Nightmare what bad dreams were all about. Unfortunately, Rage didn’t get the message that was so clearly sent. Therefore the message may have to be broken down into smaller pieces, much like Rage’s broken back, and delivered one part at a time. We swoop in with an overhead view of some unknown countryside, moving through farmland and small towns as we follow a set of train tracks the whole way. It’s a rather beautiful late fall landscape with different shades of earthy browns, yellows and oranges filling the view. Moving with the steel rails of the train tracks we come across several freight trains moving in the opposite direction of our travel, with all of the engines painted a money green color and the initials “TN” emblazoned in gold on the sides. Finally we come upon the rear of a passenger train, with the same color scheme and initialing as the other trains we’ve seen, moving along at a nice 70 mph pace. “I tell you what, he’s lucky that those security guards pulled me off of him or I might be on the way back to the slammer right now on murder charges.” The unmistakable voice of Tyler Nelson brings us inside the last passenger car of the train. The CEO special, or “Greed Express” as he likes to call it, has all the comforts of home. Lavishly decorated in a black and white motif, the plush snow white carpet runs the entire 80 feet of the car and connects to the black marble walls. There is a long, oval shaped table in the center of the car with mahogany legs and a black marble top, surrounded by 10 comfortable looking black leather chairs. At the far end of the mobile executive board room is a large, 55 inch plasma screen that hangs on the wall. Also present inside the car is a wet bar, tended by a bartender in a white shirt and black bow tie. Seated on one side of the oval table are Mrs. Beasley and Mr. Jordan, the two associates of Tyler Nelson that facilitated the deal with PRIME. Both are dressed fashionably in business attire and have paperwork scattered on the table in front of them. They seem to be buried in their work, shuffling back and forth between the pages strewn out in front of them. Stalking around the table, holding a cell phone to his ear with one hand and a glass of Dom in the other, is your friend and mine, Tyler Nelson. His white dress shirt is tucked neatly into a pair of hand made grey trousers, with the sleeves rolled up to his elbows. His deep red tie is loosened around his neck. “I just don’t get why people continually try to test me,” continues Tyler into the phone. “Don’t they realize what I’m capable of by now?” He sips from the flute of champagne as he listens to the voice on the other end of the call for a moment. “Well, this joker is gonna find out real quick that I’m the wrong greedy fucker to fuck with,” Tyler declares. “Anyway, do some checking on this Rage jack-off and find something on him for me. A greasy Mexican like him has to have some skeleton in the closet that I can exploit.” A short pause before Tyler concludes the conversation. “Hey, tell the girls that next time they throw a party like the one last month to let me know ahead of time. It’s a pain in the ass to have to pay people off after the fact for something like that. If I get to the cops before hand, they never even show up. Just makes it easier to clean up, if you know what I mean.” And with that he punches the “End” button with his thumb and tosses the phone onto the table. “How is the President today, sir?” asks Mrs. Beasley. Tyler sighs and takes another sip of the bubbly. “Same old shit with that guy. He wants me to throw some more money at the mercenaries we have over in Iraq so they can take over more oil wells.” The CEO of Greed, Inc. shakes a finger and his head at the same time. “I told him he screwed up with all that democratic garbage over there. All he had to say was that those savages weren’t capable of policing themselves, which they aren't, and just made it a U.S. commonwealth. Who’s gonna tell him he can’t do it? The Chinese? The French? The Russians? They’re all a bunch of pansies.” “Heh, you can say that again,” chimes in Mr. Jordan. Mrs. Beasley quickly shoots a glare at her nephew. Nelson stops in his tracks and looks at the young assistant, who now realizes that he’s better seen and not heard. “Don’t you have some work to do, kid?” questions Tyler. “Er…um…yes, sir!” Jamison Jordan quickly buries his nose back in his paperwork as Mrs. Beasley offers an apology. “Sorry about that, Mr. Nelson. I’m still working with him on his somewhat errant comments.” “Meh…I’ve got bigger things on my mind, but if he keeps it up he’ll be looking for employment elsewhere.” Mrs. Beasley nods her head submissively. “Now what do we have on that Latino leach that soiled my suit the other night? I had to throw the damn thing away due to the grease stains he left on it with his grubby hands. And I liked that suit, too.” Mrs. Beasley shuffles through some of the papers in front of her, then grabs some of the ones sitting in front of her nephew. “We don’t have much at this point. Most of our people are waiting on information from their contacts. Something will turn up, I’m sure of it.” “I’m sure of it, too.” The confidence oozes on Tyler’s voice. “If we can’t find something, then we’ll make something up, but either way I’m going to ruin that son of a bitch for thinking he could lay his hands on me. I’m Tyler Nelson for God’s sake! No one does that to me!” It’s somewhat of a misnomer, however, because there have been plenty of people who have indeed done that very thing to him. One way or another, however, Tyler got them back and on most occasions it was several levels worse. The mind is a terrible thing to waste, and while Tyler Nelson has been called many things in his life….stupid is rarely one of them. The ultimate strategist, Nelson prefers to have the upper hand at all times. Leverage can be a powerful ally in battle, and he will use that leverage to break and destroy whatever or whoever is up against him. What will his leverage be against Emilio Rage? Tyler glances down at his watch as he feels the train starting to slow, then walks over to the bar. At the snap of the CEO’s fingers, the bartender reaches under the bar and produces a telephone receiver. “Dial up the engineer,” he demands. The bartender does as he’s told, and momentarily Tyler is connected with the engine at the head of the train. “What the hell are we stopping for? Maintenance of way? Jesus Christ! Get them off the track already or run ‘em down! MY TRAIN DOES NOT GET STOPPED FOR ANYTHING!!” Tyler slams the receiver down on the bar then waves with his hand for the bartender to hang it up. “Mrs. Beasley, make a note to fire the dispatcher, corridor manager, and director of this territory for letting those heathens out in front of me,” snarls Tyler. Mrs. Beasley jots something down on a notepad. “It will be taken care of, sir.” As the train slows even further, Tyler looks out the window and sees the maintenance gang working on his railroad. As his train passes them by, something catches eye. “Would you look at that? Extraordinary!” We take a look out the window and see that there is a fight going on amongst the gang. Several of the workers are attacking a single worker, but they aren’t having much luck. The lone man, standing about 6’3” and weighing roughly 400 lbs is taking three men to task and fending them off rather easily. The bald man with a red, pointed goatee throws his attackers around like rag dolls in between spits of chaw. Nelson is fascinated as he looks on, pressing his finger on the glass of the window. “I think I want to have a little talk with him.”
