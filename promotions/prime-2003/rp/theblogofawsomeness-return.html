Date: 168 TheBLOGofAWESOMENESS RETURNS~! Mood: Bouncy Currently Listening To: “Fight For Your Right” The Beastie Boys 7/22/08 Eli VanNess What’s up serfs? Eli here, and you know what that means; THE BLOG OF AWESOMENESS~!~!~!~!. Yes, yes, the infamous and most famous of all the interweb blogs has returned to the tubes to give you more of what you want – insight into the daily lives of your favorite tag team. A quick side note: why does the tilde-exclamation point make a phrase more intense than just an exclamation point? What the fuck s this tilde and who made it so damn influential all of the sudden. But alas, I digress from my point. So the last week since Revolution has been pretty chill. Aside from our loss, a few good things came out of the show. We met some guys called D.U.I., which although I had never heard of them, they seemed like some pretty solid bros. Long story short, we’ve joined forces. Pretty much all that means is that we’ll have a couple extra heads to kick some money for brews. They also were holding some banging buds, so we’ll probably combine forces when it comes to flipping that. LITTLE KNOWN PRIME FACTOID: Many wrestlers like weed. This week we’re facing off against the team of Mega Job. I’m not exactly sure how to pronounce it because they’re very *cough*ethnic*wheeze*. I don’t know if it’s pronounced normally as in Job or Spanishly with a soft “j” as in Yob. I guess I’ll call them Mega Yob, as to not offend them. I’m in touch with my darker skinned brothers…or esse’s. Anyway, we’re just hanging out and having fun. To be honest, we haven’t won a match yet, and we’re not exactly feeling the pressure to win. I mean shit, once we start winning we’re going have to keep winning. Then we get all bent out of shape, have to quit smoking, drinking. Winning will be no fun. So we’re just enjoying the bliss of no expectations. Picked up that new Lil' Wayne CD off of iTunes today. Shit is fire. I repeat, SHIT IS FIRE! That bangin tune "A Milli", I'm not even sure if Motzart could have completed a feat of such musical brilliance. A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI A MILLI I'm actually considering getting some tattoos like Young Wayne himself. I'm still shaky on the face tattoos, but all the kids are getting them these days. Now, it’s on to some fan mail To: The Upstarts (PRIMES hottest tag team) From: Cindy Sue (sexy Fan) Hey boys, I was just curious as to what your typical day is like on the road when you’re not wresting. Can you tell me? P.S. Wanna trade pics? Well Cindy Sue, I know that many of our fans are eager to make their way inside the hollow empty space we call our heads, so I’d be more than happy to share with you a normal day in our life. 1:00 PM – Wake Up 1:03 PM – Piss 1:05 PM – Roll 1:07 PM – Smoke 1:30 PM – Food 2:00 PM – Nap 3:00 PM – Wake Up 3:03 PM – Piss 3:05 PM – Roll 3:07 PM – Smoke 3:30 PM – Food 4:00 PM – Beer 4:00 PM – Beer and Video Games 5:03 PM – Piss (while still playing videogames) 5:05 PM – Roll 5:07 PM – Smoke 12:00 AM – Clubbin 3:00 AM – Laid. Wash. Rinse. Repeat. Baby, it’s that simple. Some people say we’re living the dream, but I like to say we’re living the dream. It doesn’t have to make sense, so don’t worry about it. Also, any planned activity in the schedule becomes null and void if some young lady interrupts and nookie calls. Eli – out. P.S. Trade pics? You start. I’ll have Felix hold the camera so you can see some action shots.
