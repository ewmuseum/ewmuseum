"That…That's a quote from the script…" "Mr. Brocken, when you looked behind the paint of this man, Vangelus Olsig…what did you see?" "I saw a man who lost everything…whose only hope hung on the possibility that one day, justice would be served to whatever or whoever killed him on the inside." "That's the exact answer I was looking for. Your honor…Ladies and Gentlemen of the Court…you heard it straight from the mouth of the man closest to Olsig during these grueling eight months of despair. Olsig was dead on the inside…and Mrs. Vanessa Miller has the blood of this death all over her hands." Olsig turns back to Brocken. "Thank you, Mr. Brocken. You are excused." Brocken’s expression seems to have shifted from bewilderment to intrigue. "Umm, nah…I think I want to stick around and see how this whole…thing…plays out." "Very well…please take a seat in the audience." Brocken goes to sit down as the Judge reads from the script. "Wow. Okay….we haven't heard much from the defense. Is there anything you would like to say?" Olsig walks over to the table where Vanessa sits, now representing the defense side of things. "Yes, your honor. The Court has brought in witnesses and testimonies that, instead of proving that Mrs. Vanessa Miller is indeed the murderer of the man formerly known as Vangelus Olsig, only prove that Mr. Olsig was nothing more than a weak and faint individual who fell to the wrath of this thing called love and simply could not handle it. If Mr. Olsig did indeed die on the inside, it wasn't because of Mrs. Miller's actions…it's because he fell down and STAYED down. When life knocks you down, you get back up…you don't stay down, swimming in depression with a 'woe-is-me' attitude. Mr. Olsig is a weak man who was killed on the inside by his own inability to bounce back from a painful loss. It happens to everyone…suck it up. Olsig, however, preferred to spend eight months sulking in it, and the result? A man who is now only a shell of his former self. If Olsig wants his killer, he should look in the mirror. That is all." Vanessa looks at Olsig confused. The Judge speaks. "Is there anything else the Court would like to say before we…finally…end this thing?" "Yes, there is…" Olsig says, pushing himself from his seat. He makes his way to the podium where the Judge sits. *WHACK!!* The slap delivered from Vangelus to the Judge echoed throughout the room, followed by a round of ‘ooh’s and ‘aah’s. "Last time. Stick…to…the…SCRIPT!" His point made clear, Olsig walks back to the opposite side of the court floor. "Now, the defense raises a somewhat valid point, but to say that Mrs. Miller is totally innocent here is pure lunacy. What she did was betrayal in its purest form. She took the knife and gutted it into Mr. Olsig's heart, and for that she should be held accountable. Mr. Olsig has been invited to participate in this years Dual Halo, yet he has been unable to formerly accept as of this date because he could not push forward without bringing the very woman who murdered his character to justice! He couldn't step between those curtains knowing that her betrayal had not been dealt with…knowing that she was still walking around with a mask of innocence! What Mrs. Miller did to this man was of the utmost destruction, and for that she deserves her due punishment." Fighting the sting of the resounding slap, the Judge reads from the script. "Thank you. At this time, the….jury…will convene?" Olsig stands up. "I'm guessing you're going to be the jury as well?" "You should play the numbers tonight…" Olsig prepares to exit into a room in order to convene with…himself. Before departing, though, he turns to the audience to make one thing clear. "Nobody moves." And with that, he exits. About five minutes pass by, and he’s back. "The Jury has reached it's verdict, your honor." The look on Vanessa’s face tells the story. She knows it’s over for her. It’s inevitable. She’s not totally sure what her fate will be, but she has no doubt it will be brutal. Tears well up. "In the case of The Court vs Mrs. Vanessa Miller, the jury declares Mrs. Miller…innocent…on all accounts." Shock penetrates the entire court room as mumbling ensues. He ruled against himself? "Truth is…I'd long killed myself on the inside way before I met you, Vanessa. In fact, that's the reason I clinged to you so tightly…because I felt that I finally had someone that could rescue me from my loneliness. You don't know true pain until you find yourself in a position where you're all alone. The phone doesn't ring anymore…friends don't come around anymore…all you desire is intimacy with someone…anyone." He begins pacing around the court floor. "So, when you turned against me, Vanessa, I retreated back to the only place I felt safe…that place of loneliness. That place of solitude. A place where man couldn't hurt me anymore….where the evils of this world couldn't pull on me anymore. The only place I could ever really call home." He runs his palms through his hair. "All I have is myself, and honestly, I'm on the verge of giving up even on myself. So, I'm going back to PRIME…I'm going to step through those curtains once again…and I'm going to fight with everything that's within me to win that Dual Halo…so that I'll have some legit reason to maintain hope in myself. Some reason to not give up on me. Because if even I don’t believe in myself, I have no reason to live. None." Olsig looks out into the audience. Their faces still ring with disorientation. He takes a deep breath. "I’m…I’m sorry. I shouldn't have gotten you all involved. Call the police…contact authorities…whatever you want to do. I don't care…I just want some sign of hope that there's a brighter day ahead. I'm going to England to fight at Culture Shock. To fight for that sign of hope. I have to win….my life depends on it." And as silence rests over the room, Olsig begins his exit out of the courtroom, unsure of what lies ahead of him. But before he escapes through those doors, he hears a noise. An applause. It starts with Brocken. Continues with the Henchmen. Proceeds with the Youngblood look-a-like. And before he knows it, he has a whole courtroom applauding for him. Cheering him on. He smiled.
