How Did I Get Here? (ReV 63 Re-post)

Date: Before the deadly theewe.net crash of '05 “You may ask yourself – Well, how did I get here?” - Talking Heads, “Once In A Lifetime,” Remain In Light V-V-VRRRROOOOOMMMM!!! V-R-R-R-ROOOOOM!! The roar of an engine slices through the still of a spring evening in suburban Detroit. The sound stands out for more reasons than one. It’s extraordinarily loud, sure, but it’s more than that. It sounds…expensive…foreign. People around this area of Detroit, they buy Camrys. They buy used Corollas. The better-off soccer moms buy Caravans… …they don’t buy 1999 Nissan GT-R R34 Concept cars. From his rocking chair on the front porch, Nova watches the unlicensed speed machine come to a smooth, growling stop where the curb meets his lawn. An elderly Asian man shakily climbs out of the driver’s side door, his cane aiding him as he begins the trek up directly through the front yard to the porch. Nova knows it’s Hin See. He doesn’t bother putting the joint out. “Hin? Brother Hin, is that you?” He jokingly feigns unbridled excitement. “It is! To what do I owe the honor?” Hin See sniffs the air and stares at the glowing embers jutting out from Nova’s mouth as well as the smoke cloud hovering above his head. “Hmm…is it just me or did a skunk keel over nearby?” Nova grins, the setting sun now casting a beautiful glaze over both of them. “You gonna tattle on me?” Hin See returns the grin. “Maybe not this time…but there IS a reason I came here other than to watch you engage in gleeful insubordination of your contract.” Nova coughs loudly as he exhales a plume of smoke into the humid Midwestern sky. “Do…*cough*…*cough*…do tell…” “How’s your leg?” Hin See asks. Nova looks down at his thigh, pulling up the leg of the sweaty pair of swim-trunks he wore to mow the lawn, revealing a long, deep scar running the length of the top of his knee to his hip. “It was the strangest thing…it was such agony…pain so bad I almost wanted to die – I mean really almost wanted to die – and then, poof! Gone…like a really bad dream…” “That doesn’t reassure me,” Hin See replies, his voice low, “could it be your old injury?” “I don’t know,” Nova replies, “but that’s not for you to…” “Oh, the hell it isn’t,” Hin See snaps quickly, “especially after…” Hin See pauses. “Especially after what?” Nova asks. Hin See sighs, removing his cowboy hat for a moment to scratch his bald head. “Nova…there’s been…well, there’s been a…a modification in the schedule that you and I discussed.” Nova shoots an eyebrow up. “Modification? What kind of modification? I’ve still got a slot for Overkill, don’t I?” “Yes, of course,” Hin See replies, “that was never the issue…but, I’m afraid…I’m afraid you’ve been booked for ReVolution.” “WHAT?!” Nova shouts before looking around and lowering his voice to an angry hiss, “what?” “Don’t look at me like I’ve betrayed you or something,” Hin See says irritably. “Well, I’m trying not to,” Nova begins, “but for some reason, I distinctly remember a certain telephone conversation whereupon I was assured that I would not be booked for ReVolution…” “I was backed into a corner,” Hin See replies defensively. “You were backed into a corner?” Nova repeats, a tone of desperation rising in his voice as he points to the interior of his home, “she is gonna flip her SHIT when she hears about this! Can’t we figure something else out?” “I wouldn’t have acquiesced in the first place,” Hin See replies, “but Taun…” “Oh, how did I know you were gonna…,” Nova begins, but Hin See interrupts him. “Shut up and listen,” Hin See snaps, “Taun was talking about vacating the 5-Star Title. A booked match was my only recourse to save YOUR ass. I could have given the nod and you’d be a former two-time 5-Star Champion.” “Vacating the…,” Nova spits, so angrily that he can’t finish the sentence, “What is it about your son? Have I done something to warrant this kind of bullshit?” “You have to understand, Nova,” Hin See says calmly, “Taun is having a difficult time right now. The company he came back to was not the company he left. Things are more…complicated now.” “Amen to that, brother,” Nova says as he lights a cigarette. “Taun just needs some time to adjust to a different kind of PRIME,” Hin See continues, “he’s worried about stagnant title divisions, and he over-reacted.” “You think so?” Nova replies sarcastically. Hin See shoots him an icy glare and Nova throws his hands up. “Okay, okay…so what are we gonna do? Ariel’s gonna crucify me if I tell her I have to go.” “Of all the things I have to worry about,” Hin See replies with a grin, “that, Mr. Nova, is not one of them.” Hin See nods respectfully and turns to leave. “Fuckin’ great,” Nova replies, exhaling smoke, “just fuckin’ great…” Hin See makes it out about halfway across the lawn before turning back. “Oh, I almost forgot…I managed to get Vetra for you.” Nova’s eyes narrow at the mention of the man who had bestowed upon him migraine after migraine in the previous week. “Huh…you don’t say?” “I do,” Hin See replies before turning and walking back to his vehicle, “I take it I’ll see you Wednesday?” Nova sighs before putting his cigarette out in the ashtray next to him. “Of course you will.” -----------*~*~*----------- Nova thought Ariel had taken the news very well. All things considered, she had been reasonable. After all, Nova told her he would be able to stay home. He had thought it was a certainty, so he expressed it as one. Ariel had every right to feel angry when he explained that circumstances had changed and he would have to report to Lewiston, Maine, for ReVolution 63. Sure, she had raised her voice a few times. Nova didn’t hold that against her…a perfectly natural response to feelings of abandonment and hurt. Maybe his ears were still ringing a bit, but hey, that could also be the hum of the television screen. Okay, so some might say that Ariel’s subsequent cooking boycott was over the line…Nova didn’t think so. Heck, his wife worked damn hard…if she wanted a night off from the hot stove, Nova was more than happy to fend for himself. He could eat lasagna another time. It wasn’t every night that he got to just sit down and enjoy a hearty, home-made peanut butter and jelly sandwich. Sure, Ariel might be with-holding sex…but was that really so unnatural? Nova didn’t think so…in fact, he found it perfectly normal. They weren’t twenty years old anymore, especially after having a daughter, and if Ariel was tired or something, Nova was happy to let her rest. Besides, Nova could always rub one out in the shower by staring at the little hanging breast exam reminder/tutorial…something about the curvature of the areolas in those sketches that just screamed “modern art.” And really, when it boiled down to it, Nova didn’t mind sleeping on the couch. -----------*~*~*----------- Nova lounges on the couch, watching the tape of ReVolution 61… …or more accurately, staring at it. He has the show paused on one of the last frames before the credits rolled. In the still, Token Weed and Trashcan Man are flailing and thrashing at one another as security attempts to break them apart… …and if you really focus, you can see someone on their knees in the lower right-hand corner of the shot, holding something shiny in their hands. It’s Nova, of course, cradling the belt that he wanted so badly to recapture after the disappointment at Culture Shock. Nova stares at the still image of the paused show, and can’t help but expound on it as symbolic of something bigger than an odd camera angle. This image…this tells the story of my career thus far in PRIME… …I win the 5-Star Title, and the last shot people remember is TCM and Token Weed ripping each other to pieces. The quiet revolution. With little to no fanfare, I’ve made history. I won the 5-Star Title in my second match, setting a PRIME record in the process. I made it down to the final six in the Dual Halo. I’ve mentioned the others’ names before, but needless to say, the other five were admirable company. I won the 5-Star Title again, becoming the only person to do so in the history of the company, and got Wrestler of the Night for it. I somehow worked my way up to #1 Contender status for the Intense Title. I beat some of the best in PTC in a fourteen-man Battle Royal to earn a shot at the PTC Extreme Title. I quietly amassed arguably the second-best win/loss record in PRIME on 9-2, topped only by Ms. Wolfenden’s 11-1 streak of domination, and on Wednesday, I plan on making it 10-2. The point isn’t to laud my accomplishments, as much fun as that is. The point is, I’m destined for something here in PRIME, and whether the spotlight’s on me or not, I’m gonna chase that something down and beat the shit out of it until I’m satisfied. How did I get here? I’ll tell you how…pinfalls. Ladder matches. The “old-fashioned way,” if you will… …and I know you will. That’s how I got here… …and “here” is only looking better and better.
