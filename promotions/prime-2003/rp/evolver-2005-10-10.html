Date: October 10th, 2005 ''I dont like your tragic sighsas if your god has passed you bywell hey, fool... that's your deception.'''Ice', Sarah McLachlan ONESome people never had to make the choice between friend, foe or a friendly 'Fuck you' from those who once supported them. They lived their comfortable lives in comfortable jobs where the only decision they got faced with was the difference between number two pencils or those fancy-pants fours. This month's progress report versus last month's. Black ink, blue? Toner or laser copying? Some people see all sorts of numbers and statistics. Toner to them is a stack of weights, or an amped up version of your morning jog. Copying gets them spit on, and they have to deal with the ages old 'Black or blue' questions, too... 'What color is my eye gonna be tomorrow?' They deal in black, blue, brown, green, yellow, purple and a plethora of shades in between. In reality, these people are kind of like your average person. Most are, anyway. Take Joe Schmoe and add some muscle, you've got yourself a pro wrestler at the bottom of the card in any competition. Some people, however, were different. They were above and beyond even the pros. Those people see red. Karina Wolfenden, much to her dismay, regularly sees it. The Red Raver. Born with blazing hair, mismatched eyes and a lazy sense of fashion, the normally laid-back woman could quickly transform into a flaming ball of anger. Her nickname caught on because of the color of her hair matched with her usually drugged-out, fun-loving demeanor and lifestyle, but most knew her as Amy Campbell, or Aimz. Others saw her as a fist entering their vision too quickly. Most loved her. Those who got close usually loathed her. She was anything and everything that tended to be wrong with youth. Fancy-free and amused by all sorts of twisted concepts from piercings to tattoos to Julia Stiles movies. Her 'rags to riches to rags to riches, then back to rags and finally riches once more' story was compelling, edgy and sweet at the same time. She was everything, but also nothing... and that's not in an overly philisophical sense, either. Amy Campbell's your normal punkrockwhatever chic girl, but manages to stand alone in any and all crowds. That's mainly, of course, because she's used to performing - 'Releasing stress', as she referred to it - in front of them. The only thing she was in front of on that chill October morning, on the other hand, was the face of her usually dramatic love life. She paced from side to side at the foot of the bed, not especially anxious - but hardly calm. The pale light from the autumn morning came into the window, casting a light shadow and immeasurable grace on all things that it touched, including Amy Campbell's nearly bare midrift... which was what had her audience for that moment captivated. Ignorant of that fact, she had continued her speech until a name's mention found it interrupted. ''I'm determined to make this week a good one,'' she said, matter-of-fact and unrelenting of the concept in the cool light, ''Juan Santos will get pinned. Mark Blackwolf will make sure Karina doesn't let Paul Cain steal this chance, and Tyler O'Neill w--'' ''Who cares about that guy?'' The interruption's source would make no bones about his jilted tongue, but he would be easily found out. Amy moved to lean gently, one knee on the soft mattress as she dug both hands into the comforter that covered the unusually outspoken and jealous man. She was tactile with her words, ''Whose bed are you in?'' An eyebrow raised at the big man, known in the public as Killean Sirrajin, but held to Amy Campbell as a rock - the unwaivering support she needed. Or, so she'd say, the usually unwaivering support she needed, ''What's wrong with you? He's a nice guy. Bit quiet, but barely anyone else is. It's refreshing.'' ''I'm hoping you don't find him too 'refreshing', that's all.'' His shifted tone elicited a less than sugary response from the redhead at the edge of the bed... ''Don't get jealous like that. I'm not your wife.'' Little did she know, the counter was already prepared. ''And you're not my whore, which is why I'd rather keep this as an exclusi--'' ''You realized we technically broke up more than a month ago, don't you?'' ''Yes, and you technically continued sleeping with me. And coming to me for advice, and...'' Amy's face lit up in an abnormal response to defeat. ''Touche.'' Her features continued drifting toward a smile as she climbed up the sheets of the bed and came to rest lazily beside Sirrajin's overmuscled arm, gently resting her head on his bicep and speaking just as softly, ''But this is still a budding thing. Public or not, I'm not in a position to be giving you up anytime soon.'' ''And I love how you refuse to refer to it as anything but a 'thing','' he smirked bitterly, ''But that's an argument for later times, I guess. I should enjoy the now. You know, barely getting laid and having to hear all of your problems.'' He winked, but she wasn't so convinced of his humorous observation, and a quick jab to the side cemented that fact. Still, things were right between them... as right as they could be. Both had been married but unlike Killean's, Amy's union had been young and bitter and entirely unsuccessful, so she was still very much jaded when it came to relationships... or would-be relationships, as it were. That went without saying, and Killean knew he'd have to learn to deal with that fact if he hoped to have any sort of future with the feisty little redhead - something he did occasionally ache for. Once they'd taken sex out of the equation, their relationship had progressed far more than the months before had allowed it to. Now, with the communication they had... he could see a future beyond the hotel rooms and stolen moments. They just had to go about it gingerly and with care not to scare either party away. ...And that was only ONE of the awkward situations in Amelia Campbell's existance. TWOSlurring. ''I'm on TV 'n if I hit you first, yer gonna press charges.'' But not too stupid. Amy Campbell would not throw the first punch in a fight with civillians. She wasn't even feeling that violent, having spent the better part of the evening drinking beer brewed in her hometown while she sang along to Danko Jones songs in her head. The pulsing hip hop over the dancefloor was driving her halfway insane as she realized exactly how identical most mainstream songs were. It was the same beat over and over and over again, and everybody else was too drunk to care. Unfortunately for her mood, Aimz had found herself accosted by a small group of people who were hardly wrestling fans. Rather specifically, they saw it as a redneck sport that she was only eyecandy in. The long and short of it was that those people DIDN'T watch wrestling. If they'd ever given it a chance, they might have known not to approach the seemingly small and unoffending redhead. But they were close-minded and generally not aware when their safety began waning. It all went downhill from the moment that one of the young men sat down beside Amy in the booth she'd occupied mostly alone, but occasionally with an old friend walking up to see how things were going. He'd tipped her glass over with his elbow - deliberately, of course - and struck another nerve with the words that had followed. His friend had come up to her other side, boxing her in at the little table until she shoved one straight out of his seat to make room for her to escape the red vinyl and glass tabletop. From there, they'd both dared her to hit them - citing that a wrestler can't really punch, anyway. ''Pfft. Please,'' said one, entirely aware of the growing crowd and his need for a penis extension, ''If you hit me first, I'm not gonna feel it - let alone press charges.'' He proceeded to lean over to one side, intentionally making eyes at Amy, '' 'Specially not against someone with an ass like that.'' A prospective fight always sobered Aimz up, especially when it was with a growing bundle of wasted air. By the time she could even reply, she noticed that the bar had grown bare of her friends and instead housed the inner circle of jerkoffs she was faced with. It was a friend's ex-boyfriend, who she knew only by reputation, and his little posse. She'd have called them miscreants, but 'losers' would suffice. Needless to say, she wasn't about to respond to the cat calls and stood, waiting for him to say something even mildly worthy of her hearing. ''C'mon, girly,'' the makeshift gang leader goaded, ''There might be mirrors and some smoke in here, but I don't see any turnbuckles or ropes, so you're gonna have to fight this one out the right way. Do you even know how to fake punching me, or are you really what I hear you are - just some tits for the rednecks with the Playboy channel. And speakin' of that, can't the company afford to buy you bitches implants? I mean, Jesus... am I really expected to get off on those nubs?'' Amy had been trying to keep her cool. She really, really was... but it was going too far, and she was letting this jackass say too much. ''Let's do it this way,'' she snarled, ''I'm not gonna touch you, and you're not gonna mention any other part of my body that you haven't seen bare. Clear?'' He inched forward, completely unaware that all of his talk was about to garner a painful - and legally standing - reaction, ''Not really. And don't talk like you wouldn't show me in a s--'' Within the blink of an eye, the young man was on the floor - groaning and grasping at his face. Blood trickled from between the fingers of his hand that was covering his nose, and his friends all either dove to help him or backed away with wide eyes. Amy Campbell, on the other hand, was smiling. ''I waited for you to run your mouth and come forward to the point that it was harassment,'' grinned the redhead, ''That way, me hitting you cleanly once - no matter who did what first - is entirely legal.'' There was a lot of shouting and trash-talk being tossed her way, but the people in the club spread out when she walked for the door. She'd hit the biggest man in there - and not lightly. It wasn't worth a bar brawl, but it had garnered her a little bit of space when she decided to make her exit in the wake of breaking someone's nose. Still grinning, she looked back toward the still-downed man and shouted loud enough for him to hear, ''And you were right, I really can't fake a punch!'' Walking out into the cold rain of her hometown wasn't especially pleasant, but what she'd done made the redhead carry a smile. Normally, that would be when you see the hero drive off in their slick Corvette and go home to listen to their world touring metal band and get laid by their model wife. Normally, the story would be cool and edgy, but not nearly so interesting. She'd gotten the one-up on 'the haters', but Amy Campbell was: 1) Still drunk.2) Calling a cab back to her loft. And, finally... 3) Not interested in models, wives or sticking ANYTHING in a vagina that wasn't hers. It was still a pretty triumphant moment, though. At least it had been, before she reached into her back pocket and noticed that her wad of bills was gone and had left her with one option: To recall and rekindle the ancient art of getting a free cab ride. Thankfully for Aimz, that art involved less begging - and a LOT less oral sex - when the person attempting to get said ride was even the smallest of celebrities. Again she was fortunate, as she was one of the biggest celebrities in her born corner of Canada. Next to an NHL upstart by the name of Crosby, of course. Hockey was religion in Eastern Canada, while wrestling was an amusing distraction between games and in the warmer seasons... so it was expected that she might have taken a backseat to a homegrown Gretsky. Regardless of where she fit on the ladder of local celebrity, an eager cabbie had quickly recognized her face and swagger, and didn't hesitate to pull up beside her. Instead of questioning whether or not he thought she was a prostitute for once, she confidently slid into the back seat of the car and announced her destination - not forgetting to mention that she didn't have any ready cash, but she could run up to her apartment and bring some back down when they got there. Given that the bargain didn't involve her inviting him into the apartment, the driver declined and handed the little redhead his Yellow Taxi business card. You know, for future reference... with his cell number written down, and an extra glance cast to her back when they arrived and she left the vehicle. Her brain was running on overdrive, one too many beer having had an adverse effect on her psyche that caused her to stumble up the stairway to her home, opening the door only to find that Killean had stuck around with a smile. He was taking advantage of her satellite TV, and she could have cared less - him 'using' her or lounging around like a leech didn't occur to her, especially considering the amount of money the slick businessman had invested. His future was secure, and he didn't need her. Maybe that's what was so appealing. She smiled and cleared her throat, dropping her soaking wet coat straight to the floor and settling down wordlessly next to Sirrajin, who only spoke after the strong scent of ale wafted to him. ''I thought you'd gone to see friends?'' ''Yeah,'' she chuckled, ''And my friends bought me beers to celebrate the fact that we've all got jobs and good lives.'' Amy adjusted in her seat and looked Killean in the eye, noting the genuine concern he had. Given her prior substance abuse, he had REASON to worry... but she was determined to calm him. ''Don't worry... I was drinking to good times.'' He'd noticed that she winced when he touched her hand fondly, and that had been where the concern came from - not just the fact that she'd been drinking. ''You normally bust up your hand when you drink?'' ''Come on, Kill. Relax. Enjoy the... Trading Spaces?'' She giggled when her eyes caught the TV. Killean hadn't even noticed or cared what he'd been watching, as he also held a copy of the Sports pages beside him, ''I was reading anyway. Now, who'd you hit?'' ''He deserved it!'' ''I didn't ask for your excuse, Ames. Who'd you hit?'' The little redhead finally relented, pawing at the tattered fabric of her jeans with a meek expression, ''I dunno, some guy a friend used to date. They were talking drunken shit and brought my tits into it.'' ''Alright, he DID deserve it.'' Killean laughed himself, but put a protective arm around Amy. They were still in an awkward vaccuum between friendship, a relationship or whatever else they could have been, and it wasn't treating either of them especially fairly... but regardless, she'd asked him to stay over her long Thanksgiving weekend, and he did so without protest. They weren't dating, but they weren't not dating... but they'd also already been down that road once today, so he wouldn't voice his concern. Instead, he was determined to keep her safe and worry-free over her mini-vacation. Of course, given who she was, that plan would fail miserably. Amy loved to create problems for herself. At the very least, though, she wasn't chasing after someone who didn't want to be found. She wasn't hanging onto the past like Karina Wolfenden had come to do, and instead looked to the future. To the next week, the next month, the next event and the next match. To Juan Santos and to her plans to insure she found another proper fight with the K-Wolf at the end of the JiTC rainbow. Of course, that counted as creating problems... but they were worth it, and they were problems to be dealt with tomorrow - not yesterday. Moreover, she hadn't become a weakling over a random guy with delusions of grandeur and a penchant for the overly dramatic. In these ways, she was better than Karina Wolfenden. In the ring, she'd proven to be equal - if not better. Now all she had to do was be better than the rest of the federation. THREE''I don't pretend to be unbreakable, I am unbreakable.'' The redhead stared back at her with all of the excited rage she wanted to aim at the television-loving world. Those mismatched eyes were familliar; Who was looking back at Amy Campbell? ''Fuck, I can't even convince myself.'' Aimz was. She found herself staring down at the last three knuckles on her right hand. They were stained brown and green with blood that had escaped just underneath the surface of her skin when it collided with a hard surface - namely a wall - in a fit of her frustration over physical fitness, or a lack thereof. In weeks past, she'd trained as hard as she possibly could, but the past couple of weeks had found her growing lazy for a moment in some sort of depression. It wasn't a pretty thought, given her so-called relationship with Killean, but this Tyler O'Neill kid she met had rekindled a bit of her motivation to train. His body was tight, and his punches looked hard. He was actually ridiculously imposing, and apparently being given a shot at a space on the PRIME roster. That meant the company was bringing forth more and more competition, and things only seemed to be getting better. Plainly put, people who don't brush up on their fight skills don't win tournament matches, and they certainly don't win titles. Aimz hadn't won a title or a tournament in PRIME, yet she managed to be one of the top names there. The key word was 'name', of course, because she'd built a reputation on being anything but average - unpredictable right down to the quality of her work in the ring. You couldn't tell when Amy Campbell was on her A-game until the bell rang, and even then it was usually questionable. Her record was less than stellar, and she'd done just enough to keep up. The difference between someone like her and, say, Brandon Youngblood was that she had the personality of an entertainer... whether she wanted to or not. That unpredictability was what helped fill seats and ratings columns week after week, and PRIME loved it. She had no diva attitude and generally got along with anybody who wasn't a wrestler. In fact, she probably respected management more than Karina Wolfenden, which wasn't difficult - but hardly expected if you were to look at the little stereotypical rebel with her tattoos, pierced nose and messy hair. On top - almost - would describe Aimz. What was disappointing to her was the fact that she was 'almost' a lot of things, and one wasn't respected. The only people who truly respected the redhead were those who'd felt how hard she could punch or kick. Her closest friends hardly had any respect for her, and her family certainly didn't. Of course, respect was something she'd been parading about for years... all the way back to fWo. She'd always ALMOST had it, but could never rest assured that she'd really shown people how good she could be. Not until she beat Karina in a PRIME ring, and now that she'd accomplished that... she still didn't have that feeling of satisfaction. A little confidence, maybe - but no real, concrete stability. She sometimes wondered if beating just Karina was enough. There was an influx of new talent into the federation, and with every newcomer who lasted more than a week or two came another hurdle to jump; Not just for her, but for all of the company's upper tier. They had to continue proving that they deserved where they were. Some even questioned whether or not they deserved it. Amy knew she did, it was just a matter of proving it... and beating the K-Wolf again was only part of the battle. To truly feel comfortable in her seat, she realized that she'd have to take on the rest of the world, too. No, not in the punk rock rebellion sense - She simply needed to prove herself, as usual, by winning. Winning not only against her main rivals, but winning over a whole slew of new and old men and women who called themselves superstars. It was an uphill battle - a constant one - and she hadn't truly felt that until she accomplished her first big goal in the job. All that was left was to keep winning, to not let the off weeks phase her and to shoulder on with the confidence she had leaving her last match with the K-Wolf. She had to bottle that feeling and bring it to every fight she entered whether it was Juan Santos, Brandon Youngblood or Jesus himself. That was the key to earning the respect, let alone the self-respect that she needed to feel comfortable or right where she was within PRIME and the wrestling world. She'd take it one week at a time... and hopefully go a few days without pissing off Dave Gibson. Sure, that meant giving up on one of her favorite hobbies - Unconsciously testing the limits of everyone around her - but it'd come in handy when she really needed a pal to smack some tight-clad jackass from behind with a chair, because god only knew that nobody else would attempt such a thing for her. Well, except Killean... and hopefully Tyler O'Neill, but that was beside the point. Relationships were trivial. Rivalries were too time-consuming, at least until her next one-off with Karina. Fighting hard was essential, as if it hadn't been before. The hardest PRIMEate to lay out was getting motivated by the minute. You could only expect to see gallons of blood spilled week in and week out, just the same as it was any time she was determined to win a match. If there was one thing respectable about the girl, it's that she just wouldn't die. That's why they loved her so much. There had been a transformation between Culture Shock and Campbell. She was still dynamic and ever-growing, always improving her craft. That's why Amy Campbell needed to hold her head high, avoid dwelling on the bad days and keep on winning. She also needed to quit with the extensive mental dialogue.
