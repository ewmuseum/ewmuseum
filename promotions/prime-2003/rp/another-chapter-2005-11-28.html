Another Chapter

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/28/05 It’s Lopez nd Passion's temporary apartment, their sanctum sanctorum, the place where they can go to escape from the insanity that is sometimes their lives…barring those instances when the insanity sees fit to come to them. It’s a rather spacious apartment, probably costing an absolute fortune in rent every month. Tyler Lopez is seated on an armchair that matches the sofa in color, his legs tucked up underneath him, in a display of somewhat surprising grace and flexibility given his sheer size. He’s dressed extremely casually, wearing only a plain white T-shirt and a pair of blue jeans. A mug of some liquid or other sits on the table in front of him, the steam visibly rising off of the top. Lopez’s hands tubmle restlessly one over the other. His face looks slightly drawn, with an ashen pallor to it. The faint beginnings of dark circles are visible underneath his eyes. He reaches out to the table in front of him, grabbing the mug in his hand, seemingly oblivious to the temperature. He takes a deep drink from the mug, as if drawing life from the substance contained within. He then sets it carefully back down on the table. Lopez: The merest whisper of the name name of a match can cause wrestlers to soil themselves in fear. To shrivel in terror at the thought of participating in one of the mosr violent matches in history. Lopez: What we as a country have endured over time…has been beyond imagining. Beyond conception. I’m aware that this is most likely the point where I’m supposed to mouth some platitudes about the greatness of America, about the strength of my patriotic sentiment, about how the United States has been bloodied but not bowed. And I agree with that. All of it. Deep in my heart, I do believe that we will become a better nation. But I have seen a five-year-old child who lives across the hall from me ask his mother, with tears in his eyes, whether he’ll ever be able to go home. I have seen one of my professors from the Point get on CNN and talk about the mishaps that occured after Katrina. I have spent the last few days making sure that those I love are safe and letting them know that my wife and I are fine. I have watched the news coverage, heard the terrified screams, endured the relentless, unceasing stream of misery and heartache brought to us…and continue to grow very, very weary. I say this, not to try and make myself appear to be some sort of beleaguered martyr, to trumpet myself as some sort of brave soldier persevering in the face of adversity. I say this, to tell everyone in PRIME that I have had far more important things on my mind these past few days than a third-rate bullshit artist with an overdeveloped vocabulary and a penchant for black. Especially one whose crowning accomplishment in terms of verbal badinage is: you’re an idiot. Having an overdeveloped vocabulary does not make you a paragon of intellectual superiority. Knowledge of Nietzche does not make you a sophisticate. It makes you literate…although I wonder just how much attention you might have actually paid to the Nietzche you read. For, if you had, you would have put us all out of you misery and ended your life already…instead of just pretending to do it. Another sip. Lopez: Despite what you may believe or have been told by the front office or…well, who knows how your mind works, it seems to be a dark, scary little place where none dare tread…I have never held myself up as some sort of paragon of virtue and moral rectitude. I have done plenty of things that I’m not proud of. I am intimately familiar with my dark side. I have seen my shadow side, and it holds no fear for me. But yeah…I guess I am the “good guy” here. I make the effort at leading a decent life. I succeed some times, fail at others. So be it. But I continue to make the effort. So, you all can just stay up on your ivory pedestal, high above the plebeians, self-satisfied and smug…and you wither away just a little more each day, gradually getting to the point where a casual observer would start to think they could tell your age by counting your rings. Never mind that playing Lucifer would probably require more intellectual effort than you’ve been able to muster as of late. After all, at least Lucifer had the balls to try and take God on. I wonder, if you had been in his position...could you have been bothered? Lopez picks up the mug again, taking one last final sip. He then places it back on the table. Lopez: So, gentleman…I will see you soon. I will see you, so that we may write yet another chapter in the by now mammoth tome that is “PRIME.'
