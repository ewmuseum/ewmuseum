Okay, being perfectly honest, this isn't just last minute publication, it's last minute all around.

undated

Date: Now. A black backdrop. You know, one of those times where they're too cheap to invest in set design, so they just hang some blankets on the back wall? Yeah, it's like that. There a stool and a microphone set up, with the microphone there so that someone can talk into it. The stool, I assume, is for sitting. I mean, it's definitely the sitting kind of stool. Not that other kind of stool that I complain to my doctor has too much blood in it. But I've said too much already. At this point, for the purposes of clarification, allow me to tell you that, yes, this is being filmed. It's one of those times where the point of the promo (bet you guys haven't heard that term in a while) is to make it so that people can see. Everyone goes home after the show and we can't get our fill of them until the next week. It gets a bit lonely. Sometimes the wrestling fans are home at night, cold and lonely, and that's when they need to turn on a tape of their favorite wrestler's midweek promo and wank themselves into a comfortable dreamland of sunshine and smiles. I don't mean that it should be like the whole Chet King schtick of only doing these, and for that matter taking wrestlers' personali information upon himself. That was just silly. Who'd want to pay attention to Mark Makonie's private life? But anyway, once in a while it's all good. Vangellus Oxios walks in. Only it's not him. No, he couldn't be bothered to appear in his own promo. Instead, it's baxy (my own combination of badass and sexy, patent-pending) Hollywood actor James Franco. Half-burnt cigarette dangling from his lips, his expression one of half-confusion/half-apathy, he takes his seat on the stool. Franco has to be careful not to wrinkle his gray suit. It's a nice-looking suit. The cigarette comes in between the index and middle fingers of his right hand, pulled off to the side to dash the ashes away. After another couple of moments of portraying himself as uber-baxy (seriously, anyone else use this word and you will be stabbed to death in the face with a spoon), he tosses the cigarette away and speaks. I'm James Franco. You might remember me from the Spider-Man movie. Or something else, people tend to forget that I had a movie career before that. But that's not the point. I'm here to explain what's wrong this week. You see. They had a great promo idea planned, and they were going to put it out for the world to see. And by 'they', I mean Vangellus Oxios. But once he realized that this was an 8-man tag, he got complacent. I think his exact words were Forsaken is alright, but Face-Eater and Collins? What the hell? It's a tossaway booking, I don't have to do jack! Although it involved more cursing, and some pelvic thrusts that I am not at liberty to repeat. Needless to say, he decided to save that great idea for a booking he actually cares about. Thank you for your time. I'm James Franco. *End*
