Nightmares and Daydreams - Part 2 - A Private Moment Published (PRIME vs. SCCW)

undated

Date: Sunday and Monday Monday, September 15, 2008 The computer keyboards were finally put to rest for the day, the quietness almost deafening in a building run like a fun house almost 10 hours of the day. Papers were all over the floor and garbage was over flowing in their refuse bins, showing the lack of a cleaning crew so early in the evening. The sun was just starting to set beyond the horizon, but one hardcore worker was still busy tapping away on his keyboard trying to get his story finished for the next publishing. He stopped typing for a moment to rest his eyes as he rubbed his temples, clearly ready to end his stressful day. Once he was close to dozing off at his desk his phone rang, spooking him back to life. “US Weekly, this is Francis.” “Francis, it's Collette. I saw your light on, what are you still doing here?” His boss responded. “Working to finish my short story for the publishing tomorrow afternoon.” “I thought you were taking the week off for this paper.” “Something came up and I had to do something.” “What possibly could be so important that you aren't at home with your wife and sons?” Collette replied, trying to give him the hint to call it a night. But Francis was having none of it. “It's an exclusive. We'll be the only ones to cover it across the country and it's about someone not even living in the US.” “Unless Bin Laden's been caught, go home.” “This is pretty good I think. It's about a wrestler named Killean Sirrajin. I wanted to do this cause I know wrestling was in the news a lot recently cause of steroids and this guy was framed to be on them.” “That won't sell papers anymore.” Collette sounded stern and wasn't buying into what her writer was saying, but she never heard the half of it. “It's not that... you just gotta see the picture. It was professionally taken and well, this guy claimed he would never do this again. Plus there's a rumor of a his finances being messed with.” |~o~| Sunday, September 14, 2008 It was Sunday, not an overly special day in the Sirrajin home. They certainly weren't church goers. Sure it was the second week of football's regular season, but Killean was just not that into the NFL this year for some reason. Regardless, the Patriots and the Jets were playing early in the first quarter on the big screen TV. The Supreme Machine was not watching though, instead he was talking on the phone, taking advantage of the tail end of Desiree's nap. “Seriously, it's happening when she gets home from the store.” Killean said as he paced a groove in the carpet. He was clearly nervous about something. “You crazy son of a bitch. Is that what was in the package you had delivered to the club?” Vegas asked on the other end of the line as he relaxed in his easy chair back home. “You bet, you dunno how long it took me to work up the guts to do this after claiming it would never happen again. Plus I had to be discreet. I had someone else pick it out and size it. I don't want this becoming any type of news. I've been a fan of having my private life private if you get me.” “Sure I get you, but wow man, that's huge. Why are you telling me now though?” “I had to tell someone. This has been a terrible secret to keep. The only guy who knew was Tim cause he's the one who went to get the work done on it. He barely even knows Jenny so I know he would keep quiet about it. Hell, Desiree is like a little voice recorder now. I was scared she would repeat something to Jenny. I used to be able to tell her anything before she could talk without it getting out. Now I had to fight to keep this quiet.” “My lips are sealed dude. Not like I'd call her on her cell phone anyway before she got home.” “Too late!” Killean said. He moved to the door and put his ear against it, recognizing the repeated clap on heels on a floor. Jenny was approaching the room. “I gotta go man. She's here... wish me luck!” “Luck won't save you from a lifetime of hell after you talk to her now. HAHAHA!” Vegas joked. “Very funny.” Killean responded in a tone dripping of sarcasm as he turned off the cordless. Keys began to jingle outside the door and within a second, the deadbolt unlocked and the door opened. Jenny was carrying a couple bags of groceries into the loft. “Little help maybe?” She said half seriously. Killean obliged right away, taking all the bags into the kitchen, putting them on the island before quickly making his way out to the main room again to join her. “Oh Kill, the milk will spoil if it's left out much longer.” “Who cares about the milk. I need to talk to you.” “But I...” Jenny barely had a chance to take her shoes off before Killean took her hand and led her over to the sofa. He politely asked Jenny to sit and after seeing the serious look in his eyes, she developed a bit of worry as to what the problem might be. Killean stood there, just a bit of nervousness causing his hands to shake gently. This was it. He had thought about how to do it for a couple weeks and still he was gonna have to fly by the seat of his pants. It wasn't the optimum choice for him, instead it was his only choice. He had no clue what he was doing. “Look Jenny, we've been seeing each other now for quite sometime. I had left PRIME for the first time when we started dating. I could not have asked for a better person to be a motherly figure to Desiree. Even after the whole fiasco with Charity, you agreed to take it slow and I appreciated it. Geez, I don't even know how to say this, but with things going on recently, I realized a few things. I've like how it's been for us, but I think it's time for a change.” Here it was, the day Jenny was fearing since Killean rejoined PRIME. She wasn't sure from day one if Killean would be able to sustain a relationship while he was on the road wrestling. They discussed it quite extensively before the decision was made for him to go back and they made that decision together. But there they were, Killean looking for a change and her with tears in her eyes. It was going to be hard to adjust. She closed her eyes immediately, trying to hide her hurt. She didn't want to live without him, but it didn't look like she had a choice. “Will you...” Killean said reaching into his pocket and getting down onto one knee without her noticing. She loved Desiree as a daughter. She had been watching her for so long and didn't want to let the little girl go. What about Desiree? How would she take it? The tears were freely flowing, making damp trails along her smooth cheeks. What would she do now? “...Marry me Jenny?” Killean finished, opening the ring box he removed from his pocket to display a very beautiful white and yellow gold two carat engagement ring to her. Her eyes were still closed, trying to regain her composure before facing him again. She took a deep breath and as she opened her eyes, she finally realized that what he had just said was completely different than what she thought was happening. She came face to face with a smiling Killean and a rock that had enough of a surface to skate on. She started to shake, the tears flowing even more freely but for a different reason. Her mouth dropped open, her jaw quaking slightly. Lights were flashing outside, but they both paid them no mind as ambulances and emergency vehicles were quite prevalent in the downtown area of Winnipeg. She resisted the urge to jump out of her seat. “OhmygodyesIwill!!!!!” Her words were a jumbled up mess it seemed, but it was quite obvious what her answer was as Killean slipped the ring onto her finger. She jumped out of her seat at that point and wrapped her arms around him tightly, clearly crying of pure happiness now as she looked at the ring on her finger again. She couldn't believe what had just happened. Killean had said to her before that marriage was most likely not an option anymore and that he was happy living as they were. She had agreed with it, having once been married before herself. Needless to say, they both had heartbreaking experiences. “Ok, you might wanna loosen up a bit. I can't breathe.” Killean tried to choke out. Jenny giggled and released her tight grip. Sirrajin took a deep breath. “You have no idea what I've gone through to make this happen Jenny. I've been a nervous wreck since I got that ring.” Jenny smiled, not wanting to look a gift horse in the mouth so to speak. She would enquire later as to why he seemed to change his mind about the whole wedding idea. “Better get your best clothes on though, we have a reservation for dinner at 6. Vegas is coming over to watch Desiree.” Killean said, leading her to the hallway so they could get changed for their celebration. It was indeed a happy day in the Sirrajin home. With the money in from the overseas accounts, a new era was starting with absolutely no troubles to bog them down otherwise. Or so Killean thought. |~o~| A camera sat next to the window, her meticulous mind wanting the shot to be absolutely perfect. Her pocketbook was $100 lighter, but this view was the best to get the shot. Out of her large bag popped the head of her little dog again. Trixy took a second to scratch her ear before barking at Jordan. “Hey, I know we're in a weird place, but take a look for yourself. We got a perfect view.” She paused for a second to observe the camera again, making sure the power was on, setting the picture quality to high and adjusting for the bit of sun peaking over a nearby building. “A hundred bucks was little to pay to get these pictures. They'll be worth a fortune to someone. He has kept pretty quiet about this whole thing. Good thing the walls are somewhat thin between our lofts.” The dog barked again, hopping out of the bag to take a quick drink of water from the small Tupperware bowl Jordan had placed there. “Ooooooo, here they come!” Jordan said looking directly into the camera as Killean and Jenny came into view. “Look at her, she's starting to cry already. What a touching moment.” She rolled her eyes at her own comment, readying her finger on the button to snap a few once the ring came into view. The flash would go off twice before she realized her mistake. Hoping that the happy couple wouldn't notice, she flipped the switch off and took about a dozen more pictures of Killean putting the ring on Jenny's finger and her explosion off the couch to wrap her arms around him. “She so doesn't deserve what she is getting from him. That silly bitch! But these pictures will make a hot topic in the tabloids. I'm sure someone will pay enough money for them. Heck, it might even make the cover of something. Right Trixy? The dog wasn't paying much attention to Jordan, instead eyeing a potted plant on the floor in the corner. She readied her claws and jumped at the defenseless plant. Jordan turned only in time to see Trixy taking a bite out of one of the leaves, her back paws buried in the still damp mud from an earlier watering.
