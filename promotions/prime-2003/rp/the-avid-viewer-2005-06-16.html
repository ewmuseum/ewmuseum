The Avid Viewer

16 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/06/05 My nose stings as the acidic beer refluxes through it. Clinging to nasal hairs, the pops burn as I take gasping coughs, caring little for the glass landing with a padded umph on Eleanor's favourite rug. Wheezing breaths finally get enough air into my lungs to sneeze, while a veil of unintentional tears cloud the television screen. 'You're fired,' went the Deville's barbed, venomous words, lashing harsh flails of inflection across my wife's back. As I rub my eyes, I grate grains of salt onto the irritable surface, leaving the abrasive sting to draw a wince. With a soured face, I try and push my lids far apart, padding t-shirt collar against eyeball. 'That's right...' he went on, words jabbing me through the burning blindness. 'There's one thing you can bring that I want...' I didn't need to see the maniacal, yet creepily serene grin across Angelo's face to know what would follow. Like the Sword of Damocles, it had been hanging from a fine, fraying hair since he first returned to my - our - lives. 'Your beloved husband,' hissed through my hearing as vision returned. Not too long ago, I was the man stalking my territory between the ropes, spewing forth verbal territorial pissings to warn others off. I relished the talking. A 15 minute in-ring promo meant 15 minutes that I wasn’t leaping from the top turnbuckle into the crowd, or taking bump after bump to the outside, off the stage, from the set, onto chairs, through anything I thought would get a reaction. My vocal range far surpassed by wrestling repertoire, and it was far easier to get out of bed the next morning after a segment than a match. That was then. Now, I'm trying to swallow the pocket of beer defying my attempts to swallow it, while a sheen of perspiration breaks through across my forehead. 'Xavier Kannon.' It's like a mocking nickname. An insult. It's not my name... it's just what they call me. 'Christian?' then came Isabelle's concerned words as her footsteps bunny-hopped down the stairs. She usually watched PRIME to see her sister-in-law on TV, as well as taking the chance to be overly-critical of the designs she'd gifted Deville, Vetra, and Brown, among others. I composed myself with a gargled cough as the door was pushed open. For a brief, blissful second, my thoughts crossed over onto oiling the hinge. 'Are you okay?' she queried, overly protective of me, sweet in a way, but devastating to my pride. Or what remained of it. 'Oh, I forgot it was Re-' 'One. Million. Dollars.' the Deville broadcast on every frequency across every channel. 'Holy shit...' Isabelle blurted out, reading Deville's eyes as they came to life of the flat screen, while Eleanor's despairing look couldn't make even the smallest chip in his toughened, emotional armour. Isabelle matched the Crimson Angel's stare, her uppity, protective side forcing its way through. Slumping forward, my elbows ground into my thighs as I watched the golden, frothing beer soak into the voluminous rug. 'Iz,' I sighed, my eyes resisting looking up at the screen, polar opposites repelling. 'When Ellie gets back, we need to talk.'
