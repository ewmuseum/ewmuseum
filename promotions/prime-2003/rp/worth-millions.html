Date: Someday As a bouncer, there's always some sort of perk that falls your way. Sometimes, it's free drinks, free food, and very occasionally, you'll get the scraps of some smelly, drunk skank that just broke up with some guy, couldn't find a man to take home for a bit of the Rebound Hoedown, and ends up on your couch, passed out after the thirty seconds of glory you've subjected her to. And sometimes, it leads to another career. Look at that mountain of muscle, the God of all Bouncers, Vin Diesel. Just the mere sight of him one night caused a major motion picture actress to go into convulsions, and demanding that he star in her movie, knowing that someday, Vin Diesel would become the Crown Prince of Hollywood. It was just a matter of time. Chet Worth, however is not Vin Deisel. Hell, Chet Worth isn't even Freddy Gasoline. If anything, he'd be Jimmy Methane; smelly, gassy, and produced by cow intestines. The only thing the two had in common was the fact that at one time, they both worked the same night at two different clubs thousands of miles away. Of course, that didn't stop Chet Worth from telling everyone who bothered to turn an ear toward him that he used to know Vin before the fame and fortune turned him against his friends. And really, it was a great story, filled with fictitious explosions, falsified police chases, and a finale of a blood pact between the two to be brothers until the end. One thing Chet Worth does have over Vin Diesel is anonymity. If Vin punches a guy in the face, it's major network news for at least a week, explaining how the guy had it coming. Chet Worth punches a guy in the face, and it's another court case and disappointment. Such is the life of a failure. And then came the night that changed the life of Chet Worth forever. It began like any other night. Painted up hussies with their breasts falling out of their tops, wearing skirts that just barely hid their second hairstyles, standing in line, waiting as Chet Worth let through only the hottest women in line. At Majorica, the standing rule was that unless she was at least an 8, she could stand outside, and figure out what to do to make herself hotter the next week. That is, unless she felt like showing a bouncer exactly what she'd do to get in. Yes, I mean that. The uglier the girl, the uglier the bouncer. A guy like Randy Peete could expect to grab himself a 7.8, and she could get in, provided she kept her teeth where the should be, and took a shot in the mouth. Chet Worth? Anything below a 4 was all his. But, when you close your eyes, it all looks the same. It was just before one of these rendezvous when Chet Worth had to act fast. He was one of the only bouncers on duty that night, and Randy was already in the back with a 7.3. Somewhat hot, but, her face had a pretty noticeable scar on the side of her cheek. Not in the Tina Fey sort of look, but the Bride of Frankenstein 'holy Jesus, that's a fresh scar' kind of way. Standing in front of Worth was Peter Maxwell, a washed up formerly famous wrestler and fellow Cleveland resident. Maxwell had fallen out of quite a few companies by completely overestimating his own worth. Since he'd once been in the same ring as Angelo Deville, he figured that would send his nightly appearance fee up ten-fold. Some promoters were more than willing to pay it, simply so that they could put the name 'Angelo Deville' on the bill, without actually having to hire Angelo Deville. By then though, Maxwell was a drunken mess, a shell of his former potential, trying to ride strictly on star power, rather than real talent, of which he always lacked. Hell, the only reason he even had a job was because he looked reasonably similar to Jason O'Neil. On each of Maxwell's arms, were solid fives. Not hot, not ugly, but rather homely kinds of chicks that you'd probably take home to mom. 'Sup?' Maxwell said, already slurring, drunk on Vodka Tonics, his drink of choice. 'Good evening, Mister Maxwell,' Chet Worth said with a smile. It pained him to smile. That was the one downside of the job. He always had to look like he enjoyed being there. 'I need VIP. Have suite.' 'Sorry, Mister Maxwell. VIP is closed tonight. Big party coming inside.' Maxwell was incensed. His eyes turned bloodshot and red, nearly instantly, and by the look in his eyes, he was about to cause a scene. 'What the fuck do you mean 'VIP is closed'? You do know who I am, don't you?' Oh, snap. Maxwell went there. Chet Worth was unfazed. 'Yes. I do. And you're not getting into VIP tonight. Especially with your selection of women there. Why don't you head down to Club 33?' Remember when Bugs Bunny got pissed off, and his eyes turned into Mushroom Clouds? So does Chet Worth. Apparently, so did Peter Maxwell, because he reenacted that very scene. Maxwell let go of the ladies, and put a finger to the large chest of Worth. Worth smiled, and set down his clipboard. What happened next was to be expected. Maxwell, known for his nearly glass chin swung at Worth, missed, and Worth connected with a shot the the jaw. Like a ton of bricks, Peter Maxwell hit the ground, and didn't move for a few minutes. Chet straightened his tie, picked up the clipboard, and motioned for a few of the hotter women to step forward. 'Ladies, you lookin' to get in tonight?' he said. They nodded along with Chet. 'Fantastic. To prove you're not totally worthless, show me your tits.' Within a nanosecond, each girl had their respective tops around their necks, their breasts exposed to Worth. Chet looked on, a serious look in his face as he studied each tit, as if he were looking for Waldo. He pointed at each as he spoke. 'Great tits, go inside. Little saggy, but go for it. National Geographic, hit the bricks. Perfect tits, go on in.' The three girls shrugged and let their friend stand alone outside as they walked inside the club. Her eyes teared up, as Chet Worth showed his heart. 'Honey, come here.' he said, holding his arm out. He pulled her to the side, and smiled. 'You really want to get in?' The girl nodded, and looked up at Worth with apprehension in her eyes. 'Good. Well, we'll go into the breezeway over there. You do what you think you need to to get in, and if it's what I'm thinking of, you'll get in. Sound good?' The girl stuttered a minute, and looked up at the Marquee. This was Majorica. The creme de la creme of clubs. She would have swallowed a watermelon whole to get in. She nodded, and followed Chet. Since I'm not about to go into what happened, let's focus on Maxwell, shall we? He stood, rubbing his jaw, looking around for his female companions. All in all, this was just another night for the former star. He was used to getting punched out, and the fact that some fat schmuck at a shitty club he didn't really even want to go to just knocked the shit out of him didn't bother him a bit. He'd been knocked out by better punches. 'Dude just got lucky. Yeah. That's it. Just a lucky shot,' Maxwell thought, still rubbing his jaw. Chet Worth came back a few minutes later, the girl from earlier on his arm. 'Congrats, he said to her. Not only did you blow your chance to get in ... literally, but, you're now Worthless. Hit the road.' As the girl ran away, crying, Maxwell walked up to Worth, a glint in his eye. 'Hey, pal. Ever think about wrestling?' Worth scoffed. 'Does this look like some generic promo, where I kick someone's ass who happens to be a well known wrestler, and then he offers to train me? Get the fuck out of here.' 'No. I wasn't even going to ask you that,' Maxwell lied. 'I was going to say if you ever considered it, don't. Fat ass.'
