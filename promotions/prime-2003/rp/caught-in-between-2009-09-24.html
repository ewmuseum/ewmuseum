Caught In Between

24 September 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 24, 2009 "He's late..." Elise Ares said under her breath, looking out from the familiar boardwalk terrace. "They're always late." The plate in front of her which once contained her lunch now sat empty, a waitress hadn't been by in nearly fifteen minutes. Probably better off, in case you haven't realized it by now Elise isn't quite the most cordial customer in the world. The breeze off the ocean blew her chocolate brown hair around as she checked her cell phone again. Thirty minutes late. Looking up from her wallpaper, which was a picture of herself and Kazys Jankauskas holding the PRIME Tag Team Championships, she began to see a large man being walked over to her table. Quickly she took the empty plate off of the table and sat it under, much like she'd done with Kazys the first time she'd met him. She quickly ran her hands through her hair, hoping any little imperfection she could fix would better her chances. The man approaching was very tall, well over six feet tall. He was bald and very tanned. Wearing a pair of expensive Oakley sunglasses, Elise judged he was an experienced bodyguard. That means he'd probably ask for more money. Between PRIME and Daimon though, she had money to burn. The extra expenses shouldn't put a noticeable dent in her seemingly endless funds. He wore a black business suit, looking much more expensive than the one the waiter seating him was wearing next to him. When he got to the table, Elise didn't rise from her seat. There was no need. He was there to work for her, why should she bother getting up to greet him? "You're Elise, correct?" The man asked, his massive frame dwarfing her. "I am." She smiled in response, "Take a seat and we can discuss the job opening." "I'm sorry for my tardiness." The explained as he took a seat, "There was an accident on the way here. Traffic was awful." "Right, whatever." Elise looked back at the man, surprised by the lack of a moral filter between her brain in her mouth. He'd have to get used to that. As she continued to stare at him, she couldn't help but think about how much he looked like Vin Diesel. Vin Diesel hasn't been in a lot of movies lately. Sure, he was in that new Fast And The Furious movie... but what was he doing between that and The Pacifier? She couldn't remember any movies off hand. He had such a bright future too, once upon a time everyone was saying he was going to be the next Stallone... "Ms. Ares." The waiter interrupted her thought process. Her eyes narrowed and shot him a glare. Why was he still standing there? "Would you like to see a menu?" "No. I'm fine." She replied. Elise then motioned her hand towards the man across from her, "You need a menu?" "No, I'm here for business. I've already ate." He replied. "Good." Elise smirked back at the waiter, "You can leave us be now Jeeves. Thank you." The waiter rolled his eyes at the PRIME Tag Team Champion before slowly turning around and walking away. Sighing, The Swaggeriffic One looked back at her lunch companion and extended her hand. "My name is Elise, and you are..." "Dominic Martinez." He grabbed Elise's hand to shake, easily being able to crush her hand if he so desired. He could probably lift her up by her head. "But you can call me Dom." "I have to ask," She couldn't contain her thoughts any longer, "You're not Vin Diesel are you?" The man looked back with surprise. "No... I just told you my name was Dominic." "Damn." She responded, "So why are you here?" Dom stared blankly back at Elise, giving a look that's akin to are you serious? "You placed an ad in the paper for protection. I believe my services as a professional bodyguard to some of the biggest stars visiting Miami is more than a reasonable resume. I've protected such names as Mariah Carey, Tara Reid, and Scarlett Johansson. Plus honestly, I'm a big fan." "Who isn't?" Elise responded with a smile, "You know me from jumping that shark don't you?" "No, from PRIME." The man responded, "You're in that tag team with Kazys Jankauskas. Wolves Of Slaughter, isn't it? You guys are pretty talented." "I've done more than that you know," Elise interrupted, "I've jumped a shark. I've won more Miss Hawaiian Tropic competitions than I can even remember at this point. I'm worth millions of dollars, Dom. I'm kind of a big deal. It's not all wrestling guys on television you know. Think of the other avenues when you look at me. Acting, modeling... I'm one hell of a singer." "I'm sure, you have a beautiful voice." "I know." Elise Ares nodded her head in agreement, "I do. You don't even know the half of it." "But I was under the impression that this job was already filled by Kaz?" Dom asked, worrying about job security. "When you're in a line of work like me, job security is a big concern." "Kaz and I don't really talk much anymore." Elise responded, "We had a falling out over a... personal conflict. I'm looking for a replacement." "But I just saw you two on television a few days ago." Dom responded, "You seemed fine." "Kaz and I are still tag team champions, I see him every week. He's still my tag team partner even though he's no longer under my employment as a bodyguard. Honestly, I don't know if I could tell you the last time I spoke to him while not on television. He's sort've a bore, honestly." Elise explained, "As far as the social aspect, he doesn't bring much to the table. I need some protection that's going to keep me entertained. I'm a woman on the go. I have big aspirations and do big things. One time I jumped a shark." "I think you mentioned that." Dom jumped in. "You're damn right I did." Elise Ares explained, "Who else has that on their resume?" "I know I don't." "Exactly." Elise stated, "And I'm going to keep saying it until I think you're sufficiently impressed." "What happened between you two?" Dom asked, "It's strictly an employment question." "Well your employment question isn't any of your business Dominic." Elise came back with the claws, "It was a personal matter and doesn't require your input." "I need to know what you expect from me as an employee." "I expect you to do as you're told." Elise replies, "Which you're already doing a terrible job of. As a matter of fact, I don't think this interview needs to go on any longer. You're obviously not able to handle the pressures of protecting an important woman like myself." "What a waste of my time..." Dom says as he simply gets up, and walks away. He leaves a full glass of water laying on the table as he makes his way out to his car at a brisk pace. Sitting at the table alone Elise Ares goes back to looking out over the ocean, running her finger along the top of her water glass. Why is everything so difficult with Kazys gone? She got by fine without him before he took the job and she should be fine now without him. With the right weather conditions, Miami was almost like paradise. It was the type of day that the millions of tourists would write home about, but Elise Ares was no tourist. As a matter of fact, the Swaggeriffic One was lacking her signature swagger on her way out of the restaurant. Her stature was better described as stomping. Her heels clicked hard down on the concrete as she approached the white corvette parked illegally in the handicap spot down in the front row. Snarling she grabbed onto the handle of the passenger side and ripped the door open. Thousands of dollars of engineered finesse deserved much better, but Elise Ares wasn't in the mood for pleasantries. Sometimes even her needs and wants to have top of the line items took a back seat to her needs and wants for attention. "No luck, I assume." A man's voice spoke over the muffled radio as she slammed the door shut behind her. She lowered her sunglasses and just glared back at him while she latched her seatbelt. He simply closes his eyes and shook his head, almost as if sharing his disgust as he shifted the car into reverse. "It's hard to find good help these days." The man added. "No fucking kidding." Elise finally answered, looking ahead, "Daimon, the longer I'm around this city the more incompetent I think everyone around me is." "What did this one say?" Intrigued, Daimon Vytautas couldn't help but ask. He hadn't seen her this pissed off since her fight with Kazys at the hotel. "He wanted personal details about Kazys." Elise responded, "Why he isn't my bodyguard anymore, why we don't talk. He claimed he was worried about 'job security' but he's just like those dirty, pathetic internet wrestling community reporters. It's none of their fucking business how I live my life. They should just pay their money, sit in their seats, and just enjoy the show I bust my ass to put out for them. How I live my life isn't any of their concern." "The weak are nosey by nature." Daimon added, "They're insecure." As they came to a stop light, silence filled the vehicle. Elise was doing something that most PRIMEates probably don't think she's capable of doing; processing deep thought. Her arm rested on the top of the passenger side door with the window down, letting her hair flap in the breeze as the light turned green. While around Elise Ares, silence doesn't usually last long. "Why can't they just be more like Kaz was?" Elise said out loud, "He did his own thing and had his independence. He does his job well. He's just so damn touchy." "He's not really any different than they are, is he?" She was answered immediately, "His own curiosity about your lifestyle is what led to your fight in the first place." "It was a bit different though." Elise explained, "He was just looking out for me. He was wrong, but he seemed to care." "I'm just glad you're too smart to let Kazys influence your decisions." Daimon took a subtle jab at his former comrade, "He's just like a child. Always out to get me. Always trying to make anyone look like the bad guy except for himself." "Why does he dislike you so much?" Elise couldn't help but ask, perhaps she was a hypocrite for prodding... but such animosity doesn't last the test of time without some real umpf behind it. Something bad must've went down. The story has at least the potential to be mildly entertaining. "Where to start... where to start." Daimon spoke, temporarily taking his eyes off of the road. "We grew up together. He was like my brother. Lived down the hall and went to school together. Spent a lot of time together, so we bonded. We had a lot in common you see... we both had military aspirations and studied military vehicles. We'd always say that we'd man one together someday." "Then what happened?" Elise stated, perhaps not as interested as she played on. The excitement for joining the military was completely lost on her. "Kazys was a great solider, in that he had no problems taking orders. Guys like Kazys need a strong figure to latch onto. For him, it just happened to be me." Daimon smirked, he seemed like he almost loved talking about his greatness as much as Elise did. Feeling territorial on greatness, Elise resisted the urge to punch him as he went on, "I was the star athlete, I was a better student, I was far more popular. I had many girlfriends while Kazys... struggled. You see Kazys was known as 'Daimon's best friend' and it put a chip on his shoulder. He was jealous. Jealousy tends to bring out the worst in people. He's spent his entire life trying to be like me, but he's not."' "I can relate to that..." Elise added, just for the sake of alerting Daimon she hadn't fallen asleep. "I got accepted into a Soviet military school at twelve. I was ahead of the curve. Kazys couldn't make the cut though and was left in the slums of Vilnius. He was stuck going to pathetic excuses for schools around there run by the state." Daimon stopped at the light and looked over at the beautiful PRIME Tag Team Champion in his passenger seat, "He'd talk bad about me while I wasn't around. Trash me as a bad person because he was bitter. Kazys... has trouble accepting that some people are just better than he is." "I see." Elise answers as Daimon focuses back on the street. "He's jealous of you too, you know." He continued, "You're very beautiful, you're talented... obviously the heart and soul of Wolves Of Slaughter. You're a star and he's just there. If you ditch Kazys you'd still be successful and he'd be out of a job. He should be thanking you for all you've done for him, but instead he acts the way he does. Talks to you like you're a child. You deserve better than that, don't you think?" "I do." She answers, "I brought him with me to PRIME because I felt bad for him. I mean, he was new to Miami and didn't have a job. He's a big guy, he has potential. He could be great in PRIME. Maybe not as great as I am... but who is?" "Is exactly, being on your plane of existence is too big of shoes for anyone to fill." Daimon expressed his feelings, padding Elise's already over-inflated ego, "One of these days he's going to cost you those tag titles. Then you'll be able to cut him loose. Go off on your own without him holding you down. But try to be gentle with him." "Why is that?" Elise asked, interested on where he was going with all this. "It's pretty obvious he's in love with you." Daimon expressed, "It's why he's so pissed off you're dating me." "Who isn't?" Elise answered with a smile before looking out of the window. She's more than accepting of any and all forms of flattering, but the arrogance coming off of Daimon Vytautas was almost nauseating to her. There was only room for one ego that large inside of the vehicle and that belonged to her. It was worth tolerating though, the paychecks were huge and he bought her a fucking 'Vette. Who could argue with those kind of results? But it wasn't the Corvette or the money she thought about the rest of the way home. It was why Kazys was the way that he was. Why he reacted the way he did. Why he basically brushed her off and then one day out of the blue suddenly started giving a damn about her. It was baffling to her... even someone as smart as The Wolf In Sheep's Clothing couldn't read the guy. Deep down inside it drove her insane. She always prided herself on being read whoever she wanted and playing them for everything they're worth, but it didn't work on Kaz. It never worked on Kaz. Why? ---- Chapter Two: Partners In Name Only ---- Two Days Later... "Up... or down?" This is a daily question in the life of Elise Ares. Sure her hair looks absolutely fantastic no matter how it's worn, but keeping people guessing is what makes her a connoisseur of style. Being a trend-setter in the world of fashion is a tough job, but someone has to do it. Might as well be her. "Up... we're going with up tonight." Elise nods at herself approvingly in the mirror, already dressed in her ring attire. A black leather almost sports bra style top with blue violet tribal designs. For the rest of the outfit she's wearing a shiny blue-violet bikini bottom and black leather chaps with blue violet tribal designs matching the top. Yes, perhaps this outfit isn't fashion savvy, but it's ring attire. She has to show off the goods you know. Suddenly, something breaks her concentration on herself... which is much harder than you would believe it to be. Behind her Kazys Jankauskas walks into the locker room, which is shared by just the two of them being PRIME Tag Team Champions. It's one of the many perks of having a pretty title to show off. Kaz just glares over at his partner, hesitating for just a second before tossing his gear bag onto the ground and taking a seat on the bench. Not a single word was spoken. Unfortunately (or maybe fortunately if you're Kazys and don't have to listen to Elise run her mouth) this has become normal for the Tag Team Champions. While their on-air personas seem to be spot on, behind the scenes was quite a different story. Their dynamic was non-existent. The old school mainstays of tag teams spending every minute together and knowing each other so well that they don't even have to verbally communicate couldn't be farther from what the Wolves of Slaughter has become. Distant. Isolated. Cold. Kazys Jankauskas never thought he'd see the day where he missed Elise's annoying little quirks. The way she pretended not to pay attention even though she'd heard every word. The way she'd dance around in a display of confidant ecstasy. Those days were long gone. As much as he'd restrained every fiber of his being to merely tolerate the Swaggeriffic One's existence... he now kind of missed wanting to punch her square in the nose. Now there was no emotion at all between the two. Complete and total apathy. It was working for now, but Kazys knew it wouldn't work for long... and perhaps Elise thought the same. A part of Kaz thought maybe even Elise was planning on everything to fall apart, giving her every opportunity of cashing in that 5-Star Title shot and ditching him the first chance she gets. A month ago, if it wasn't for the paycheck he would've jumped at the opportunity to get rid of his child-like partner in crime. Now he wasn't getting paid anymore, and couldn't believe he missed it. "How was your week?" Kazys finally broke the silence, pulling out a roll of medical tape to start taping his fists for the night ahead. "Fine." Elise answered, refusing to even make eye contact with Kaz. "Was with Daimon all week." The very mentioning of his name made his rip off the tape prematurely in anger. "What'd you do?" "Chill." "Did you go anywhere?" "Nope." "Doesn't sound very exciting." "It was." Kazys paused for a moment, realizing the game she was playing. Avoiding conversation at all costs. "Well, are you at least happy?" That question made Elise Ares pause in her attempt to put up her hair. "What's up with all the questions?" "I'm your tag team partner, instead of sitting her in silence like the other one doesn't exist... we could at least discuss strategy or something for the future." Kaz answered defensively, "We're certainly not going to hold onto these belts giving each other the silent treatment are we?" "It's worked good so far." Elise answered before going back to fixing her hair. "If it's not broke why fix it?" Maybe talking to her wasn't the best option, it was just making him infinitely frustrated. "You never answered my question." Kazys proclaimed, leaving Elise to drop what she was doing and letting her hair fall back down onto her shoulders. She turns around, facing Kaz's back as she finally answers him. "I enjoy the money, if that's what you're getting at." Elise cuts the crap, "It's better than being poor." "Sometime there are worse things than being poor you know." Kazys answered, "I was poor most of my life and I think I'm doing just fine for myself. It's better than being used." "Uhhh... you didn't just say that." Elise postured, even though Kaz wasn't even looking at her, "I don't think you understand how this whole me getting rich and obtaining a brand new Corvette before dumping the bastard when he gets all clingy thing works, but it certainly doesn't involve anyone using me. He's not using me Kaz, I'm using HIM." "If you insist." Kaz uses this opportunity to press her buttons, "Since you know the guy so well." "Oh boy..." Elise retaliates, "This is turning into story time, isn't it?" "This is the way Daimon is and the way he's been his entire life." Kaz finally turns around with one fist taped to look into the eyes of his ravishing tag team partner, "Once a back-stabber, always a back-stabber. The guy's a snake." "Why, because he got into a better school than you?" Elise explains, "I've already heard this sob story." "No, because he and his entire family were traitors." Kaz rebutted, "When I was a little boy my family was active in the democratic resistance, always talking about it. How great things would be once our country was free, and since I was young and influenced by my parents I couldn't imagine how anyone could think any different. When you were a kid your parents were always right." "Speak for yourself." Elise answered. "I am." Kaz continued, "I talked about it all the time with Daimon. He was my best friend. I heard new things everyday and I was eager to share. Little did I understand that his parents were snakes. They lived up the hall, and were wanna be social-climbers. Looking at any chance they could to get ahead in society the easy way." "I can relate to that." Elise smirked, "I like their style." "Real life is a little different than professional wrestling, Elise." Kaz grunted frustrated, "After we get done screwing over people here in PRIME we go home and sleep in our own beds and we normally don't have to worry about our consequences until the next week, and we get to face them head on. Daimon was more than happy to tell everything to his father. My uncle and oldest cousin were arrested. They were interrogated and tortured in prison for weeks. I didn't sleep for weeks at a time because I could've been next. Having to live out of sacks and old luggage because at any second we might have to leave the country because Daimon's family wanted him to go to a nice military school." "But you made it out alright." "Yeah, but I had to watch as he was rewarded for holding my family hostage and blackmailing them for everything they were worth." Kaz said, "It sits with you. Here he is now snaking his way back into my life. Anything to better himself. To him you're just the next step in whatever his master plan is in making it big. Who knows... maybe he's trying to secure a PRIME contract himself. You're being used Elise." "Jealousy isn't a good color on you, Kaz." Elise responded. "Just show up in the ring and kick some ass, and leave whatever petty little problem you have with me and Daimon at the door... because honestly I don't give a damn about your thoughts and opinions on my personal life." She was too stubborn for her own good. In her mind she always knew the answers and nobody dare questions her to be wrong. Perhaps it was arrogance, or maybe a defense mechanism... but Kazys' approach certainly wasn't the way to break though to her. It became blatantly obvious when she marched by him and right out the door. Angered, Kazys picked the medical tape up from next to him and threw it across the room as hard as he could before slamming his fist down on the bench. It didn't break in what turns out to be an appropriate metaphor. Some people are just too stubborn to be pounded into submission by the truth. "One day," he muttered under his breath, "You'll know what it's like to be where I was..." ---- Chapter Three: Twist Of Fate ---- The Next Week... A day earlier he'd suggested they travel together this time, for old time's sake and for whatever reason she agreed. Maybe she was feeling particularly lonely that day, or perhaps it was the full moon. Yet for whatever reason, the time has come and not as much as a single ring comes his way. While it is incredibly possible for Elise to forget to charge her cell phone (something she does on a regular basis while on the road), he didn't feel this was the case this time. He has spent a lot of time reading people and situations, and something just didn't sit right with this one. One more call. He'd try one more time and he was done. Instinctively Kazys picks up the gun and takes it apart and throws it to opposite sides of the apartment. Just as he's about to thank Elise for saving his life he's shocked to watch her slam the floor lamp down on his back. WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! WHACK! Again and again, the blows from the floor lamp begin to make blood seep through his blue dress shirt. Breathing heavily, her eyes are wide open and she stares down at him before just dropping the floor lamp at her feet. He isn't dead and he isn't unconscious, he just lays there... shocked and in terrible pain. Kazys' mouth is wide open, gaping at the violence he just saw erupt from the tiny frame of his tag team partner. Her teeth are still snarling as she looks down at him. At this moment, she truly was the wolf in sheep's clothing. Picking her purse up off the table, she pulls out her wallet and tosses three ATM cards down in front of Daimon and then a set of car keys belonging to the Corvette parked out in front of the complex. "I'm not your fucking toy to be played. Nobody play me..." Elise snarls and pauses, looking up at the shocked Kazys Jankauskas standing across from her, "...or him." Kazys doesn't know what to make of the situation as Elise slings a cargo bag over her shoulder. "Clean yourself up and get the hell out of my apartment. I never want to see your face again." Those were her last words before she stepped over Daimon, now getting to his hands and knees suffering a mild concussion, two lacerations, and a few cracked ribs. That's what the hospital treated him for anyway, his own arrogance wouldn't allow him to tell the doctor's what had really happened that day. He claimed he was in an accident, nobody could make him confess any other way. Kazys silently and reluctantly followed Elise out of the apartment and towards his Chrysler 300. If she could harness that rage and violence in the ring, maybe the Wolves of Slaughter would be as unstoppable as everyone claimed they could be. A new side of Elise Ares was brought to the attention of Kazys Jankauskas that day. A side that he never really wanted to see again, but a side that let him knew Elise Ares was much more than a pretty face with a so-so work ethic. Deep down inside she was a fighter, just like her father. Maybe she was destined for the fame and fortune that she always claimed she was entitled to. She was born for this business.
