Champ Chamberlain Rp #1

Date: 06-23-08 I – Stupid Tony, What Ever His Name Is! Wednesday, June 18th, 2008 U.S. Bank Arena Cincinnati, Ohio Just moments ago Champ Chamberlain displayed the smile, enthusiasm, and down right giddiness of a young man who hadn’t just simply won a debut wrestling match in the federation of PRIME but of a young man who’d just won the Universal Title. He might just have been happier, when talking with Matt Mills, about his win over that masked Mexican wrestler than Devin Shakur was with his win over Cozen and Lindsay Troy. That, as noted, as moments ago and now his mood is one of depression and anger. While all those around him, probably fifteen or twenty people scattered about the spacious locker room, are all dancing, drinking Champagne, and laughing Champ simply sits on the wooden bench, in front of the lockers, and broods. With his elbows on his thighs, and his chin resting in his hands, Champ simply stares straight ahead at the wall while mumbling quietly to himself. ‘Stupid jerk Tony Romano, who does he think he is anyway? Who is he to mock me and tell me what I can and can’t do in this company? It’s not like the guy is in the Hall of Fame for being a great wrestler or something.’ Champ lets out a deep sigh as he leans back against the wood of the locker and folds his arms over his chest continuing to brood until someone from the party notices him sitting there and walks over to him and sits down next to him. ‘Dude, Champ, what are you doing? This is your party bro, you should be up there having a good time.’ Champ to his left and gives the guy a once over before his facial features are contorted into a look of confusion. ‘Do I know you?’ Champ asks while looking away from the guy and back at the wall he’d been starting at for a good ten or fifteen minutes. ‘Man it’s Allen. The guy who works at Starbucks that you came into this afternoon. You invited me here, remember?’ Allen says as he smiles over at Champ hoping he’ll remember that the PRIME wrestler invited him to the building and not think he’s just some weirdo whose stalked him. ‘I did? Whatever. Great. I invited all these other people too and I probably don’t actually know any of them either. So what makes you think you’re more than any of these other JAGS here that you can just come over and talk to me like we’re buddies?’ ‘I don’t know Champ. You just looked like you needed someone to talk to. You seemed sort of glum for a guy who just won his first big match in such a prestigious company.’ Allen responds while patting Champ on the shoulder. ‘I am glum.’ Champ responds with a deep sigh. ‘Why so glum?’ Allen asks with a genuine, heart felt, interest. At least as much genuine, heart felt, interest that a person can muster up for someone they met all of about five hours ago. ‘Can we stop saying glum?’ Champ responds with a roll of his eyes as he gently bangs the back of his head against his locker. ‘Sure thing.’ Allen responds while leaning back against the wall at the end of the wood bench. ‘It’s just this was suppose to be different, you know? Tonight was supposed to be perfect, special. Instead that jerk off Tony Rodando messed it all up.’ Champ said while swinging a right into the air like he was punching an imaginary Tony face. ‘I think his name is actually Tony Rolo, dude. I’m pretty sure he’s a well known around here.’ Allen responds much to the displeasure of Champ who just glares at him. ‘It matters.’ Champ says through clenched teeth before turning his gaze back to the wall and continuing to vent to the Starbucks Coffee employee who he just met five hours before. For Champ this is really nothing new. A man with his kind of attitude and need to always be at the center of everything it’s very hard to keep any friends much less really good ones that you can talk to and confide in. Living the dream of one day becoming so popular, so needed, so wanted by millions and millions around the world certainly had it’s price. ‘The problem is that just like my parents Tony has swooped in, acted like a jack off, and ruined my moment. My special evening and achievement can’t be celebrated by the most important person in this company, ME. And it’s all because Tony Ricky Racardo Reali decides he has to be super cool and steal my thunder. It’s not like he was the boss or something tonight.’ ‘Champ I don’t want to seem rude, or anything, but do you ever pay attention? The dude was the guy running the show tonight. He even told you that.’ Allen responds in a mystified manner. He honestly can’t believe how little Champ pays attention. ‘Whatever. If he wasn’t talking about something that was great for me, and my career, then I couldn’t be bothered to listen. When he started into his long rant about ages and seeing horoscopes for years now and all that jazz I couldn’t help but tune him out. I’d have been put to sleep right before my match others Mickey. What was I suppose to do?’ Champ exclaims with a seemingly interested look on his face. ‘Actually it’s Allen.’ ‘It matters.’ Champ responds with a now equally disinterested look on his face. ‘You can go tell all those people that you probably know better than I do that the party is canceled. I’m not in much of a partying mood.’ ‘You sure that’s what you want? I bet all these people were thrilled to be invited here by you.’ Allen says as he slowly gets to his feet with the clear look of disappointment in his eyes that his partying at a PRIME show is ending prematurely. ‘Huh? Oh, yeah, whatever.’ Champ responds, clearly not at all interested in the small talk any longer. Defeated Allen slowly turns around mumbling something that is just barely audible as well. ‘Stupid Tony Rolo. Now he ruined my night.’ Allen says as he starts to walk off toward the others to break the bad news to them. ‘Testify my brother!’ Champ responds, mostly to himself, as Allen has already walked off into the small crowd of unknown people to let them know that Champ is sending them on their way. With thoughts of Tony Rolo running through his mind Champ leans his head back up against the wood, closes his eyes, and begins to think back to another time and place. ‘He’s just like my parents.’ Champ mumbles to himself as he fades away into a memory. II – I HATE YOU May 21st, 1998 Home of the Chamberlains New York City, New York Jacob couldn’t wait to get home. He’d been waiting all semester to bring this report card home to his parents. The report card he’d worked so hard to achieve. Five A’s and one B. The best report card that anyone in his family had ever received during their school years. He was sure his parents would be so proud of him for all the hard work he’d put in. He could hardly contain his excitement as he sat on the bus, in the front seat as usual, with his report card, or in this case, being a high school kid, it was really nothing more than a slip of paper with his grades on it, clutched tightly in his right hand. This would be his crowning moment of life, to this point. He’d see the smiles on their faces beaming so brightly that he’d probably have to call the neighbors and let them know that everything was all right in the home, that the bright light they saw was just the glow from his parents proud face. Finally after twenty-five long, excruciating minutes, the bus slowed to a stop in front of the apartment building that he and his family lived in. Immediately Champ jumped out of his seat and headed for the door, moving so quickly that the driver barely had enough time to get it open before he reached it. A few moments later he was bounding up the steps and flying up the stairs to the families fifth floor apartment. There was no time to wait for an elevator today, Jacob thought. ‘This news is just too big.’ Jacob said to himself, with a smile, as he hurried up the steps to what he believed would become a huge family party in his honor. Two minutes later and Champ burst through his front door, waving his grades in front of him like a mad man, and smiling from ear to ear. ‘MOM! DAD! LOOK AT THIS! LOOK AT THESE GRADES!’ Champ exclaimed as he rushed into the living room where his family, all of them in fact, were already sitting. As he handed his mother the sheet of paper he didn’t even bother to notice that they were all sitting there smiling already. His mother, father, older brother Dan, who was a Senior and a good football player, and his two younger sisters Diana and Deanna. He waited with baited breath for his mother to say something as she handed the sheet of paper to his father. His father, Dan Sr., looked the page over as Jacob stood there, ready to explode, awaiting their approval. He’d worked so hard to achieve these grades and he knew that right now is when they’d pay off. ‘That’s nice son, real nice. Dan Jr. tell us more about your scholarship to Rutgers.’ Dan Sr. said as he casually tossed the grades onto the coffee table in front of him. Immediately Jacob could feel his head spinning. Nice? Real Nice? That’s it? After all that work, all that studying, all the preparation put into bringing home the best grades, ever, and all he gets is a nice? Jacob turned his gaze toward his older brother, the athletic yet incredibly stupid, jock of the family. Once again, despite doing nothing that even resembled trying in a classroom, Dan Jr. had managed to trump him again. Had managed to belittle his accomplishments just because he could bench press a house or run over a fat defensive lineman as if he was a darned locomotive on steroids. Probably was on steroids, the giant jerk off. As Dan Jr. continued once more to brag about his football scholarship to play for the Scarlet Knights Jacob could feel his face burning red hot with rage. All his life he’d had to live in the shadow of his brother. Everything he ever tried was always trumped by something Dan Jr. did. Jacob could win a spelling B, Dan Jr. would win MVP on the Baseball team. Jacob could get a perfect attendance award, Dan Jr. would trump it with setting a school record for most touchdowns runs for a junior high student. It never mattered. The stupid jock would always trump him. He’d always take his spotlight, his moment. ‘No, not this time.’ Jacob said quietly to himself. No one even seemed to bother to notice that he was still even in the same room with them. He could feel his body starting to tremble, his anger building up and starting to take him over. Finally, after years of frustration, years of being over looked, years of being crapped on cause what he did was never as good as Dan Jr., the final straw broke. ‘NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!’ Jacob screamed, much to the alarm of his parents and sisters. Dan Jr. looked at Jacob just in time to see the first right smash into his face. Quickly, with unbridled aggression, and years of pent up anger, fueling him on Jacob began to pummel his older brother. All around him his sisters and mother were screaming for him to stop. His father had gotten up from his seat on the couch and was attempting to put a stop to attack but nothing seemed to click with Jacob. He couldn’t hear them screaming. He couldn’t feel his father attempting to pull him away. Nothing was getting a single moment of his attention away from the beating he was laying on Dan Jr. ‘YOU SELFISH PRICK! YOU NO GOOD USELESS JOCK! I HATE YOU! I HATE YOU!’ Jacob continued to scream the phrases, over and over, even after his father was finally pulling him off of Dan, and Jacob was landing a couple of kicks along the way to being drug away from his brother, he continued to scream at him. ‘I HATE YOU! I HATE YOU! YOU ALWAYS HAVE TO TAKE EVERYTHING FROM ME! I HATE YOU!’ Finally after what seemed like ten, fifteen, maybe twenty minutes, but in reality had been all of maybe a minute, tops, Jacob was thrown to the side by his father. The old mans face filled with rage as he stared down at Jacob. Even his rage toward his younger son didn’t keep his attention on Jacob long before he was turning his attention back to Dan Jr. who was lying on the floor, a bloody mess, in the arms of his mother. His face was badly beaten, swollen, and bloody. With all the rest of the family paying all their attention to Dan Jr., while Deana called 911, Jacob stood up and stared blankly at the scene before him. He hadn’t been in control of his actions for that brief minute or so, at least that is what the therapists and such would tell him for months afterwards, but he knew. He’d known the whole time what he was doing he just didn’t care to stop. He’d known that he was beating his brother senseless, in the course of the beating he’d badly damaged his brother’s right eye. An injury that would be life long and would take the Rutgers Scholarship away from Dan Jr. but Jacob didn’t care. He was tired of being over looked. He was tired of being forgotten because Dan was some stud athlete. It was in that moment, as he stood there staring at the frantic scene before him, that Jacob realized he had something inside of him that he could use. Something that would always get him the attention he’d been denied all these years, an ability that was buried deep inside him, beneath all the desires to read books, study, and be a good student. Jacob was a natural born fighter. An athlete, in a different manner, though he didn’t know then that it was something he could apply to athletics, nor did he even care to. All he knew at that moment, standing there, staring at his bleeding brother, was that he had something inside of him that could give him what he craved. And he liked it. III – You Made Me This Way, Deal With It Saturday, June 21st, 2008 Kennedy Airport New York City, New York ‘You know you can’t go around doing things like that stunt you pulled at Revolution a couple weeks ago. You realize you could get fired for that?’ The agitated voice of Champ Chamberlains agent is music to the ears of Champ. He couldn’t care less if his agent is agitated with his actions. He’s an agent that’s his job to deal with things like this, am I right? That’s the way Champ thinks about it anyway. Besides there is added bonus to messing with his agent. ‘Look, Danny boy, it’s your job to deal with the backlash of these sorts of things. What do you think you’re getting paid to do? Nitpick me like mom?’ Champ asks as he slings his carry on bag over his right shoulder and slams the trunk of his car closed. ‘Yeah it’s my job to keep you employed but a little help from you on that front isn’t too much to ask. You make things so much more difficult than they have to be.’ Dan Jr. responds as he grabs the handle of his rolling suitcase and follows his ‘baby’ brother toward the airport entrance. ‘Oh come on now. It’s not that bad. It’s not like I stabbed someone’s eye out, or something like that. I wore a sock to the ring. Big deal. Besides I’ve got more important things to think about now than whether C.P. is angry about me coming out onto his show with nothing but a sock on. I’ve got to decide what to do about Tony Bolamba.’ The name, albeit screwed up as usual, comes through gritted teeth for Champ. ‘Two things that are wrong with that, one I think we’d be better served worrying about your opponent this week, and two his name is Tony Rolo. PRIME Hall of Fame member, the first one in fact, you’d think you’d bother to learn these important fact.’ Dan Jr. pleads to what he know will be deaf ears. ‘It matters.’ Scoffs Champ. ‘I can’t let the travesty of his mocking of me stand Dan. I just can’t. As for my match this week I’m starting to wonder what it is I pay you for other than the nitpicking and keeping me out of trouble. I asked you to get me in the ring with people like Xavier Kannon, Rayne, or Chandler Tostada but you keep getting me booked with these second rate wrestlers who jerk the curtain.’ Champ replies as he picks up his pace toward the airport entrance. ‘That’s because you’re looked at as a guy who jerks the curtain right now Champ because you haven’t done anything to make PRIME executives think otherwise. The only thing you’ve accomplished so far is to piss off a PRIME Hall of Fame member, one whose not always the most mentally stable I might add, and to wear a tube sock out to the ring bringing down the ire and screaming of your boss. But of course that sort of stuff doesn’t come down on you it comes down on me. I have to take the heat for all your little stunts and it’s not easy Champ, it’s not easy at all.’ Dan Jr. says, well more like whines, in Champ’s general direction. The Man in Demand suddenly stops and turns on his heel, smiling into his brother/agents face. He lifts his right hand and points a finger right into the face of his brother. ‘It’s not easy, huh? Well tough. You just remember one thing Dan, and you remember this well. You’ve got no one to blame but yourself for things being hard on you in life. No one but yourself, you understand me? You made me this way so now you’ve got to live with it.’ Champ, still smiling, quickly turns on his heel and begins his fast pace toward the doors. Lugging his suitcase, at a slower rate, behind Dan Jr. can do nothing but shake his head and let out a little smirk of his own. ‘How could I ever forget, you remind me every day.’
