Time to Come Home

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A couple of weeks ago ‘It’s been four years man. Why are you so worried about this?’ Jacob can’t help but chuckle a little as you take sip of your water. Jacob Cross has known Joseph, been best friends with him in fact, for more than twelve years now and his no pressure, don’t worry about things, have fun attitude still catches him off guard. ‘I don’t know why it’s bugging me so much. I guess it’s because the magnitude of this decision is just started to set in over the last couple days. It’s been four years since I was in PRIME and yet it feels like it wasn’t that long ago at all.’ Jacob says as he nervously rings his hands together. ‘I know it’s been four years, I just said that. It’s why you shouldn’t care. I’m sure most of the fans, and certainly all the wrestlers, have forgotten all about you. I doubt they remember one match you had, let alone the fact that you were such a jerk.’ Joseph smiles as Cross simply rolls his eyes at the good-natured ribbing. ‘Thanks. You’re doing wonders for the confidence, buddy.’ Jacob wads up a napkin and tosses it across the table at Joseph. ‘Well you’re sitting around here lately crying like a baby about nothing. It was four years ago like you said, heck it’s been nearly three and a half years since you were in a ring at all, and you’ve changed so much as a person since then. There is no way that most of those people are still going to hold things you did four years ago against you.’ The matter of fact nature in his voice can only make Jacob sigh and shrug his shoulders a bit. Jacob leans back in the booth and looks up at the ceiling of the small town diner. It’s been there for as long as he can remember, since he was a young kid, and it’s always been one of his favorite places to come and get a plate of cheap, but great tasting, French fries and share a conversation with family of friends. Usually coming to the diner made any tough decision, or situation, easier to digest or handle. Today it just didn’t seem to be working the way it normally would. ‘I don’t know. It’s really not so much that I’m worried about what the fans think at first. I wouldn’t blame them for hating me to start with. I was quite the asshole when I used to wrestle. I guess that goes the same for any of the wrestlers in PRIME who might have still been around back then. That stuff will work itself out over time when they’re all able to see my attitude, dedication, and ambitions for the business have all changed.’ His fingers begin to tap against the table as he continues to look up at the ceiling, almost looking right through it, as if God might come down and just give him the answer to all his questions and worries. ‘What is it that’s bugging you so much then? If you’re not worried about what they’ll all think then what’s there to worry about?’ His arms cross over his chest as he looks directly into the eyes of Jacob. ‘It’s a bunch of things I guess. I mean I’ve got a wife and two kids who I’ll be leaving behind, while I’m on the road all the time. My daughter is thirteen now, ya know, and she’s in all the sports. My son is in all kinds of sports and cub scouts and everything. So I’ll be missing all that. Which is bad enough…’ he trails off as he takes another deep breath. Several seconds of complete silence goes by as Jacob thinks about how he’d like to word the rest of his thoughts. ‘Well? You’re killing me here man. I’ve only got an hour for lunch.’ He smiles as he flicks some water at Cross. ‘Perk up a bit, pal.’ ‘Don’t give me that hour for lunch stuff. We both know you don’t do anything anyway and you can, and do, take pretty much all the time you want off for things. I bet having a career like that must be really tough.’ The sarcasm in his voice is so thick that it almost feels like it floated through the air and smacked his friend right in the face. ‘Please. Like your job is all that difficult. You travel from city to city, being cheered on by millions of fans, making millions of dollars. Yeah that’s tough. Real tough.’ His mocking tone returns the favor. ‘It’s not hard if you’re good at it and don’t embarrass yourself. Then it’s one of the easiest, albeit physically demanding, jobs in the world.’ As the words roll out of his mouth a look comes over the face of Joseph almost as if he just had a light bulb go on in his head. ‘That’s what this is about? You’re worried you won’t be good enough anymore? You’re afraid that you’re going to get your butt handed to you all over the place in PRIME? Correct me if I’m wrong but didn’t you used to be pretty good at this whole wrestling thing?’ Once more Jacob shrugs at question. It’s amazing how he always seems to think a shrug will give the answer to someone question but more and more the last couple of years he’d found himself shrugging at almost everything. ‘Yeah I guess I was. Depends on what you think of as successful. I won a lot of matches but it was different back then. I was different back then.’ He adjusts himself in the seat as he looks down at the table and begins to tap on it again. ‘I didn’t care what it took to win back then. I’d screw anyone over in anyway possible to get a win and get to the top. I’m not like that anymore. I finally grew up and realized I didn’t want my children looking back on my career and thinking that my actions back then are the acceptable way of doing things. You can couple that with the fact that I’m about to turn thirty years old and I haven’t been in a wrestling ring in nearly four years. There’s a lot of reason to question myself.’ ‘I don’t believe this,’ Joseph starts and it’s clear that he’s not kidding about anymore. The conversation has now turned to dead serious for him. ‘You’ve in the best shape you’ve ever been in your life. You’ve got the good Lord above on your side, a Born again Christian, and you’ve got a wonderful support system of family and friends and you’re worried about things like being almost thirty? You might be almost thirty but you need to grow up, bro. You were born to be a wrestler and if the last four years, out of the ring, trying to get by doing other things didn’t prove that to you then you need your head examined.’ ‘You’re probably right. I’m sure you’re right. I’m sure it’s just nerves cause it’s been so long so I’m trying to think of every excuse I can in case I go out there and fall flat on my face.’ He reaches across the table and pats Joseph on the shoulder with a smile on his face, still sort of unsure of himself. ‘I appreciate the fact that you’ll shoot things straight with me. I don’t know what I’d do without you and Dawn to tell me how stupid I can be about things.’ ‘Yeah you couldn’t figure out how to get dressed, or function on a daily basis, if not for the fact that your wife and I are around to tell you what to do and how to think.’ He smiles as he gets up from the table to head back to his job. ‘I’ve got to get back to work but seriously man you don’t need to stress yourself out so much about this. Just go out there and do your best, give it you’re all, and show people the new, dedicated, you and you’ll be fine. You’ve got that shot to right some wrongs, to redeem yourself, that you’ve been privately hoping for four years to receive. It’s your chance to do that. Win, lose, draw, become a superstar again, a regular Joe worker, whatever comes, you’ll be able to say you went back and did it all on your own terms. You can’t ask for much more than that.’ He sticks his right hand out and Cross does the same in return. ‘Thanks, Joe. Your support means a lot. Tell the wife and kids I said hi and when I’m back in town again I’ll bring them some souvenirs from the road.’ Cross watches Joseph leave and smiles at the thought of their conversation. The feeling of dread wasn’t quite as strong but it was still there and would stay there till after Revolution. *********** It’s been a long four years since I last stepped foot in a ring for a PRIME event and I can say I’m honestly thrilled at the prospect of finally doing so once more. I’ve sat around for all those years wondering just what might have been had I not been such a pain the backside back then. How much different would my life, my wrestling career, heck everything around me, be if I had been more of a dedicated worker. What would I have accomplished in the biggest, and most competitive company in the wrestling business, if I’d just put in the time. If I’d only given the effort required to be one of the best. What could I have had I not decided to simply take the easy routes all the time. If I hadn’t figured that it would be better to screw everyone over for my own personal gain. There are lots of things about my life that have changed. There are lots of things about those changes that have made me not only a new man but also a whole new wrestler. This time around the people of PRIME are going to get the real Jacob Cross. You’re not going to get the packaged character that was brought into the wrestling scene with nothing but the goal in mind of getting over. I’m not that spoiled rich kid who screwed people over all through life simply because I thought it was fun. I’m the kid from an average, middle of the road, family who first got into wrestling because I loved it and because I had a dream of making it big despite the doubts of many, including my father. The new beginning starts with Troy Douglas on Revolution 204 and I can’t say that I’m expecting to win. It’s been so long since I’ve been in a ring, and Douglas is one of the better talents in PRIME right now, so winning in my return would be icing on the cake. What I really care about is going out there, getting my feet wet, and proving that I at least belong in the ring with the people who make PRIME the best federation on the planet. There is plenty of ring rust to be knocked off over the coming months but I do believe I’m finally in the right place in my life, spiritually, mentally, physically, to give PRIME the best Jacob Cross there is. The time has finally come for the real Jacob Cross to come home.
