Who you all are

4 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/04/05 -July 1st, 2005- You've always wanted to be a champion. From the first time you stepped into a building and began training to become a great professional wrestler the only thing you could think about was the day you'd finally wear one of those gold belts from a big time company. It is what has driven you, molded you, and obsessed you for years. Now you've got one of those belts and you are not willing to let it go. 'Zameer is going to be the real threat of this match with Team Zambo. He is tough, powerful, and pretty quick for such a large man.' You say to your manager Enrique as he counts your sit-ups. You've been working your ass off for this match up. The first defense of your titles since you took them from the death grip that Deville had on them. 'I wouldn't count out that Gibson gringo. He is a great mat wrestler and that can be a weakness for the two of you boys.' Enrique says to you as he continues to count your reps. Never once do you even so much as slow down on your sit ups, your work out, anything at all as you two speak about the people who are coming for your belts. 'Gibson seems a bit reckless lately though. Like he is distracted by something. It is possible essa that he won't come into this match completely focused. Zameer on the other hand is pissed off us in the worst way.' You reply as you finish sit up two hundred. You pause for a minute to take a breath before you begin your push-ups. The time for conditioning and being ready for the challenge of matches has never been greater. A defense of your title two weeks out of the Pay-Per-View, where you know that your bound to defend them against all the new blood in the company once more. The next stretch of matches is important, possibly more so than any other stretch of matches you’ll ever have in your career. 'After all I broke a damn chair over his big head. Who could blame him for being angry? I know I'd be going after the gringo, who did that to me, if I were in his position.' You continue as you begin to start pump out the push-ups. You manager Enrique simply shrugs his shoulders as he counts your every movement from the ground back up. 'I suppose you feel that you are going to have something to prove to Zameer then? You do realize Baltasar that every match isn't about proving yourself to one person. You are one half of the tag team champions of PRIME and you are there to prove to everyone in PRIME that Angelus and yourself are the best. You aren't here to simply prove to just anyone who angers you that you're better than him or her. I don't want to see this turn into another thing like you've got with Deville. We have enough troubles without that happening.' Enrique replies as he crosses his arms over his chest and continues to count. Is he right? Are you turning this thing into more of a personal thing with Zameer than making it about keeping the tag team gold that you strived so hard to get? Is it possible that you could even hate a man like Zameer? After all Zameer is only having fun in his career and really doesn’t mean to offend or harm anyone. It isn't like facing off with Deville where you need nothing more than your hatred to drive you. 'This isn't like with Deville. I don't hate Zameer. He might hate Los Diablos, or maybe just myself now, but I have nothing anywhere like the feelings I have for Deville. Zameer is just a giant, talented, obstacle in the way of me keeping my gold.' You reply as you continue to pump out your push-ups. Each one getting a little harder, a little more strained, and the sweat pours off you faster. Each push up representing that of the next challenge that you'll have to face on your road to being the best damn tag team that PRIME, and the world for that matter, has ever known. “As long as you keep things in that type of perspective Baltasar then you guys should be just fine. I want you to realize something else as well. Whether you walk out with the belts or not this week is not what is important to me. If you guys go in and lay your hearts and souls on the mat for the win, like you have every single time you've stepped into the ring thus far, then I'll be proud of you both. You know that right?' Enrique asks as he watches you push out the last two of your push-ups. Two hundred of those as well and now your body parts are aching, tired, and ready to quit. But you know you're not going to let it quit. You are not going to allow it to stop before you are satisfied that you've pushed it to the limits you believe it should be at. This is just the beginning and you've got far to far yet to go for your body to dictate to you what is going to happen. You roll over onto your back and just smile up at your manager. 'You'll always be proud hermano and you can take that statement to the bank.' You reply as you get up, grab a towel, and wipe yourself down before tossing the towel into corner and grabbing the jump rope you've placed on the bench near where you do your warm up exercises. 'We've got other things to consider as well Baltasar. The Pay-Per-View is on the horizon and I've got a feeling that you'll be seeing all those men in the ring that you fought against and along side just this last week. Angelus and yourself are really going to have to make a go of it on your own then, not just like you did this last week when the two of you refused the help of your partners. All those guys are going to be gunning for you too.' Enrique says as he sits down on the bench with a stopwatch to time your jump rope session. You listen to his words and inside you smile. You know they will be against you but then again how is that different than it always has been? As your heart begins to pound harder and faster you are happy and content with the thought that everyone is gunning for you. If they want to beat you then you know you've got what they want. The titles are there for the taking for anyone who can topple you but it isn't just about the titles. No, every single one of those teams is looking to make a statement and earn their respect here in PRIME. The titles are simply the prize for being able to do that, but the real respect and honor is earned by besting you. You've proven yourself to be the measuring stick of the companies envied tag team division cause you were there to help build it. You were the guy, along with your great tag team partner, which PRIME built this legendary division on. Together the two of you have become the benchmark for what everyone else wants to be. You smile as you continue to jump the rope before turning your head slightly toward your manager. 'I've considered it, thought about it, and know all about it. I wouldn't want things any other way at this point.' You respond as you continue to jump the rope and work toward the next level of the mountain. You are on the top level of where people who win titles get but now you've got to work to go to the next level...where Legendary champions get. -July 2nd, 2005- A few days have passed since you went into battle with your best friend. Baltasar has been in your life for more years than you can possibly remember now. You've fought along side one another now in some of the biggest battles of your career. Walked out of a cage after a war that he led you into. You've bled, hurt, injured yourself, and bruised your ego and pride all in the name of making sure that you never let him down. Still the question has continued to remain on your mind for months now. Would he be better off if he simply dumped you by the way side and moved along on his own? 'It is do or die time for me these next few weeks Enrique. If we aren't able to retain the belts this week and then we walk into the Pay-Per-View and lose as well then I don't know if I can continue to ask Baltasar to tag along with me.' You say to the man who has managed you for several years now. He has seen your alliance with Baltasar through all the great times and every single one of the dark times. It isn't uncommon for you to feel down and less than a great partner when you think about the times you've let your buddy down in the ring. 'Now isn't the time to doubt yourself my good friend. Baltasar needs you at one hundred percent over the next few weeks more than he ever has. If you start doubting yourself now then you are only going to let yourself down by doing so. Baltasar is never going to look at you and point a finger and I know I've had to tell you this a million times now.' Enrique replies with a deep sigh. You know that deep down he feels bad for you but also you realize that he must get tired of hearing you doubt yourself. After all you are one half of the tag team champions of the world and there for you have proven yourself to be one half of the best tag team in the world. The problem is that in your mind you are without a doubt the weaker half and the man who has been your shining light for years carried you to that greatness. Living up to the great man that Baltasar has been has always been your goal. You love being a wrestler and proving yourself, bringing honor to Mexico, and showing the world that there is a place in this sport for Mexicans who don't fly around the ring like lunatics. But in the middle of that all there is only one thing you’ve wanted to prove more than anything else is that you are on his level and you are his equal in the ring. The level that he has set for you to achieve. Every time you finally feel like you've made that level you look up and realize that he has moved up three more on you as he continue to smile down on you and drag you into the history books along with him. Baltasar has never complained to you about it. In fact there is a good chance that he really enjoys it and finds it be the best thing that could happen to him in his life. He never had anyone depend on him, look up to him, or care for him the way you have since you guys became buddies. In a way taking you by the hand and dragging you into history is like a game for Baltasar. Like maybe it is why he was put on this earth and it can be the legacy that he leaves behind. In your mind you've allowed yourself to go along with that ride so far. But in your heart and soul you know that you need to step it up and help him out. It is time for you to help do the dragging into history, so that he doesn't get burnt out, causing him to end his time in this business long before he really should. 'One day Baltasar is going to be a great Universal Champion for this company. If PRIME doesn't allow him that move then he will be a great champion for some company. I've just got to prove that I was worth bringing along this whole time before he gets to that point. When that happens I'll be more than happy to retire and watch him grow even further and further. After all I never wanted to be anything but a tag team wrestler, and I only wanted that cause my best friend needed a partner. When he finally decides that he is ready to move on with his career then I am happily going to retire and call it a day. I never loved this sport the way he does anyway.' You tell Enrique as he pats you on the back. He knows that you don't have the passion or the love of the sport the way that Baltasar does. You do it cause, despite the way you get down on yourself, you know you're pretty good at it and it makes you a living. For your partner the money and all that doesn't mean anything. He'd wrestle for free if it meant he could beat men up and show that he is the toughest and best the world has ever seen in the sport. For Baltasar his passion is all about this sport and being the best at it, while yours is simply not letting him down until the time that he will move on and you can retire to your home country and open up a shop of some sort. You smile as you think about the fact that one-day you'll be able to kick back and never worry about another thing in your life again. You smile cause you know you'll have Baltasar to thank for that, like you always have with anything good in your life. 'Does Baltasar know how you feel about this business and sport essa? Have you ever sat down and told him how you feel?' Enrique asks with the slightest hint of sadness. He has always loved you both and he loves managing the two of you. He has always known that one day your part in the business would end but he has never really allowed himself to get ready for it. Deep down he hopes that some how, some way, you'd eventually find the passion for it all that Baltasar has and that this team would never end. But on the surface he also wishes that you'd just tell Baltasar and get the inevitable ending over with. So the sadness can pass as soon as possible and everyone can move on and just be happy again. 'No I haven't essa and I won't. Baltasar will let me know when he is ready. Until then I will not burden him with such things. I don’t want him to move on before he feels he is ready and I don’t want him to feel like he has to stay and tag with me, in order to keep me happy. I don't hate this life, after all, so I can hang around in this sport as long as he feels he is happy being a tag team specialist. When he is ready to be the superstar and champion that we all know he can and will be, then I will step aside and cheer from the side lines as I've always known I would.' You reply with a grin and a pat on the back of your friend and manager. You can't help but feel nothing but pride as you know you finally have two people in your life who truly care about you, after years of having no one. This is why you continue to fight, continue to train, to strive and to try and get better. The smiles on their faces are worth more than all the gold title belts in the world combined. 'Don't sweat this hermano. I'm ready to take up my place and go to war with Baltasar just like I always have been. I'm ready to keep seeing those smiles on the face of you two once more.' You respond as the two of you hug and then walk out of the hotel room in search of training of your own. Baltasar is off doing his run, as he always does, and now it is time for you to hit the weights and such. The time to prove yourself is fast approaching once more and you have no more time for this silly notion that you're holding anyone back. -July 3rd, 2005- 'You owe me Morales. We had a deal and I lived up to my end. Now it is time for you to live up to your end.' You hear the man say as you simply stand there in shock. Who does this guy think he is coming in here right now and trying to ruin what you've worked hard to help build. A man you know very little about but some how believes you owe him something. 'I don't see where you have that idea Mr. Cross. I didn't win the Cruiserweight Tournament, so I have no idea where you get the idea that you actually lived up to anything.' You say with an angry look on your face. You can't stand the thought of this guy trying to bully you into something that he hasn't actually earned. You give him a look like he is simply ignorant as he begins to smirk at you and then starts to chuckle. What in the world could this man possibly find funny about this situation? He wants to force his way into PRIME through your help, yet he never did anything to earn your sticking your neck out for him in such a manner. Now the fool is standing there and actually chucking at you like you’re the one the joke is on. “Senor Morales you are quite a humorous individual. Did you actually think that our deal was to lead your less than talented ass to the finals of the CW2 tournament? Call your lawyers right now and ask them what the deal really read. I just had to lead you to victory. A single victory is all that was required.” He tells you with a wide sadistic grin. The color in your face goes out and you start to feel lightheaded. This man has pulled one over on you and now you realize you have no choice but to live up to the end of the deal that you made. “You are like the great American car dealers of this country, Cross. A real prick that does anything he can to get his way.' You say to him with anger, not quite sure what you can say. He has you right where he wants you. He is going to be getting back into PRIME one way or another and it is now your job to figure out how that is going to happen. 'Coming from a man who is suppose to be the brains behind the PRIME tag team champions I'll take that as an insult. It wasn't exactly hard to out smart you Morales, as you were simply all to eager to sign on with me if I could help you in any way. Now I am simply looking for what is owed me in return. Does that make me opportunistic? Yes. Could I possibly be a dirty car salesman? No. That is giving me far too little credit when you compare me to such things.' He responds with a cocky grin and another chuckle. You would love nothing more than to beat the hell out of him right now. Just stomp him into the ground and forget you ever met him. For an instant, as you bawl up your fist, you even think about a sucker punch. As he continues to grin at you though you realize it would do no good. The man is a world class athlete and you are simply no match for him. If Baltasar was here all things would be taken care of but alas this man is far smarter than you have ever given him credit for. 'Fine. Let me see what I can come up with. I'm not sure what I'm going to do but I'll think of something that will work for everyone involved.' You respond and then follow that up with a deep sigh. You know what he wants and you know that you’re going to go through hell trying to convince your boys to go along with it. They tried to warn you when they first met this man that he was only in things for himself but you were greedy. You wouldn't listen to them and now they, along with you, are going to have to pay for this mistake. 'Morales I'm sure you'll do just fine. Just remember that your boys better watch how they handle this or you'll be in breech of a legal contract and there will be consequences for all involved if they don't tow the line as well.' He responds to you. He knows that Los Diablos isn't going to like this, especially Baltasar, and he is making sure you know that he took the proper precautions to avoid their trying to screw up his plans. 'They will follow what I say cause they trust me. It won't be easy and I can promise you that Baltasar is going to be looking for your head if you screw something up for them. So you better keep that in mind.' You say in an attempt to at least appear like you might still have a little bit of control even though you know you have absolutely no control any longer. Cross-knows this as well as he simply smirks once more as he shrugs his shoulders and starts to walk out of your office. “I’m sure he will do just that, Senor Morales. That is what is going to make us such a threat to everyone in PRIME. While he is pissed off about me he'll be taking it out on opponents and we'll be winning matches and moving further up the PRIME mountain. Give him my thanks when you next speak to him.' He says just as he walks out of the office and your life...but only for a moment. -July 4th, 2005- As you walk out of into the bright sunlight you simply can't help but smile. The plan you put into motion many months ago is finally coming to the end. You've put yourself into position to finally get back into the company, in the business, that you always loved being in. The time is no fast approaching when you will finally step foot back into a company that you once worked for and were once considered an up and coming star in. It has been two years since you were last seen in PRIME, last seen beating someone in a wrestling ring, last remembered for being a star on the rise in this industry. Since then you've only been able to sit at home, training, and running your family business as you waited for the perfect opportunity to present itself. You brush some dust, hair, or whatever off your perfect black shirt as you walk toward your car with the thoughts of suplexing someone in front of a live audience fills your mind. The rush you received by defeating people who weren't on your level. The way you lived for humiliating and embarrassing people is beginning to once more fill your mind and make your heart beat a little faster. The lights, the fans, the thrill of victories, and the love of the business all come rushing back to you so hard and fast that you can almost feel your body being taken over by the moment. You become a little light headed now yourself with the excitement that is encompassing you at this moment. You stop and sit down on a bench near the parking area to take a break and to simply smile at what you looking forward to experiencing again. As you sit there a small kid walks by and stops to look at you. 'Are you a wrestler too?' The little boy asks as he stares at you. You simply nod as you look down into his freckled face. The sun shining off his blond hair and a brighter smile on his face. He seems really thrilled to actually talk to a wrestler. 'Are you a wrestling fan young man?' You ask already knowing the answer to the question. He quickly nods his head up and down in excitement as he stares into your piercing blue eyes. 'Are you going to work with Mr. Morales too?' The little boy asks and you simply chuckle. Morales has already become famous enough that the residents of the surrounding area know him and know what he does in the business. You reach down and pat the child on the head as you stand up to continue the walk toward your car. 'You could say that my boy. I'll be part of Morales career for a long while now so I guess you could say we’d be working together for a while as well.' You tell the boy as you walk off and leave the kid to run off and tell his friends that he actually spoke to a wrestler that would be working with Morales. You take in a deep breath, hold it for a moment, and then slowly let it out as you reach your car and unlock it. Your time is here and soon enough that time is going to bring your fame to the streets and all the children will be excited about whom is working with you and who knows you. 'PRIME will never be allowed to forget who I am again.' You say to yourself as you get into the car and prepare to drive away. One final glance at the building that houses your redemption and your ticket back into the world that was stolen from you and you finally pull out and take off. Your thoughts now relaxed and you now feeling that you shall once again rise to the top of the mountain, just like you were always destined to do.
