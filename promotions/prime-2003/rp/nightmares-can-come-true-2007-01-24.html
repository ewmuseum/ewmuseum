Nightmares Can Come True

24 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24/01/2007 Some people adore the night, others loathe what it can bring. The very demons we hold in our sub-conscious can almost come to life without the protection of daylight shining down on us. It doesn’t matter who you are, you could be the young girl with an abusive family or the strongest man with seemingly no trials or tribulations, either way they are both at risk from fear. Paul Cain is a man with plenty of trials and plenty of fears and as he sits in his car, the darkness of the eleven pm night hits him just as hard as anyone else. He doesn’t even know why he is here, sat outside the place he used to call home. His wife will be getting ready for bed now and his daughter will be sleeping soundly in her crib, cuddly toys squashed up around her. He misses them both, more than he imagined possible, yet he still stands by the courage of his convictions. Paul Cain used to be terrified of losing his family but that’s happened now and he is dealing with it. One day he will get them back and show them he was right to have faced the man that tried to destroy his family. He will succeed, that he doesn’t doubt because now there is a new fear born out of realisation that the love rival was nothing compared to the evil that had now entered their life. Who will protect his wife and child now? As he got out of the car, he knew exactly what she would say to him. It would be the usual rehashing of “I told you, I don’t want to see you until you’ve changed” or “What the hell do you want?”. Take the basic notion, dress it up with some fancy words and then put them in the mouth of a woman scorned. She certainly wouldn’t appreciate this late night visit but then, she didn’t appreciate much he did these days so why break the habit now? However this time, it wasn’t about her this time, it was about him and the sickening feeling of anxiety that churned around his stomach. In his mind, he felt something was wrong somewhere but he couldn’t be certain of what or where it came from. The one thing he did know was that he had to see his daughter and his wife, to be sure that they were safe. The door might be slammed shut in his face but hey, part of the old Paul Cain still lived in him, it would take more than one act of hostility to hurt his feelings. He had to protect his wife and child; he would stop at nothing to ensure that happened. With a sharp intake of breath, Cain banged on the door of the place he used to call home. He could feel his heart beating that much faster, not through anxiety over confronting his wife again but because of his fear for the unknown. He could not ignore the voice in his head that drove him here and the same voice which told him those most precious were not safe. Throughout the journey, Paul had recited to himself over and over again that he was imagining things and that everything was going to be okay. He had played out the scene in his mind of his wife glaring at him, swearing at him, calling him crazy and then slamming the door shut. That he would settle for though, especially as despite his third and most frantic rapping against the door, there had been no response. Silence is not always golden. Silence can be the sound of tragedy. “Teresa! Teresa!” He could feel his heart pounding quickly as he kicked his foot hard at the door, calling out her name. There was no response and even his little baby girl, Amber, could not be heard crying. Now that voice in his head was screaming to him, deafening him with its prophecy of doom. As the words “You should never have left them” reverberated inside his mind, Cain launched his foot again into the door with enough force to finally send it flying open. He took a large step into the hallway and looked around skittishly. There was no sign of a disturbance and all the usual belongings were left in their usual places, generating a perhaps false sense of security. Paul could feel his heart trying to almost fight its way into this throat with all the pounding it was making. His shouts were desperate and panicked as the waves of terror swept over him. “Teresa! Teresa, answer me!” His judgement was clouded but his instincts remained razor sharp. Glancing to the stairs, Paul turned and sprinted up them, trusting his deeper senses that guided him there. Grabbing the banister rail at the top, he swung himself around to the left, calling his wife’s name as he moved yet still being greeted by that same cold silence. As he reached their bedroom, he suddenly froze on the spot. The door was slightly ajar which was unusual for Teresa, she was a creature of habit and would always shut it tightly before she slept. Apparently it made her feel safer, although Paul could never understand what difference it would serve if someone was that desperate to break in. Now though, he could see a sliver of light pushing through the gap between the door and it’s frame. This didn’t feel right, in fact, it felt all wrong. “Teresa…?” Paul Cain was not intimidated by mere mortals. He had never for one moment felt fear in the squared circle. Even as Vampir Nosferatu formed the Pentagram in his chest out of blood, Paul had not known torment and anguish like this. He could take any act of violence dealt out to himself, he could take and give back the mind games that people played, he backed down from nothing. However this wasn’t about him and as he nervously called out for his wife one more time, he felt something he had never truly felt before. Real, unadulterated, animalistic, terror. His hand was shaking as he slowly leaned on the door and pushed it open gingerly. As he took his first look into the room, the moonlight illuminated all his fears being realised. He slipped backwards through the shock and grabbed at the door frame with both hands to steady himself. It was at that moment that his stomach heaved and he threw himself forward, terror induced vomit launching from his mouth onto the carpet. Tears welled up in his eyes as he moved his head gradually up to gaze numbly around this place that was once full of love, now full of horrors. Blood. Chains. Writing on the wall. The room where Paul and his wife Teresa had made love on so many occasions, had been turned into a torture chamber. Multiple lengths of cold metal chains were hung up on the ceiling, draping down to hover just above the floor. He moved one aside, their coldness felt like deaths touch, as he began to edge into the bedroom. What had once been a brown carpet was stained with the crimson tide of blood and their white bearskin rug was soaked through. He put his hand onto their king size bed as he moved slowly around it, his eyes locked on what was his wife, Teresa. Her hands had been clamped to the headboard by two smaller chains and her legs forced wide apart by the same vile method. The satin dressing robe that she always wore was left open; exposing the majority of her ample breasts yet this view was anything but sexually stimulating. Her eyes were closed and her head hanging limply to the right, lost in a world of unconsciousness…at best. Cain’s eyes moved down to her groin where he saw a white thong stained with drying blood at the front. She had been raped. And left to die. Any sense of reasoning and sanity had been left at the front door as Cain lunged for the chains and tugged at them desperately. Tears were falling from his eyes as he shook and pulled at the restraints that held his wife captive. As the strength drained from his body, he sank to the floor and sobbed with his head in his hands. Every display of fear had been shown, the sickness, the tears and the loss of control. This grotesque display of torture and torment had broken Paul Cain into pieces as he watched his wife, trapped in her metal prison, blood unceremoniously covering her body. Paul’s breathing was shallow until it suddenly dawned on him, there was another. His daughter, Amber. Rising quickly to his feet, he wheeled around and wildly threw his arms around, shoving the hanging chains away as he rushed to her crib. Where ordinarily it would stand upright, it had been thrown on its side, just like any other piece of furniture in this room. Wooden pieces from one side were snapped and strewn across the newly crimson carpet. It was there that he spotted it, the soft and cuddly symbol of innocence amongst a room full of horror, the stuffed Piglet toy that his daughter treasured. He kneeled down and shakily held it in both hands. As he did so, he noticed a hole had been cut out of the bottom of it and a sticky substance covered its rim. He trailed one finger along the edge of the hole and as he pulled it away, the liquid came with it. His own wife had not been the only object to feel the animalistic sexual force of a faceless predator. With a scream of rage and anguish, Paul threw the toy across the room and it was then that he saw her. Sweet, innocent Amber. He rushed over to her to find her covered only in a blanket. She stared in shock at her father, eyes wide open and her little body shaking in fear. Sweeping her up in his arms, he held her close over his shoulder as he sobbed in both relief and despair. Amber was unusually silent and only her rapid breathing and beating heart could be heard in his ear. She was less than a year old but had seen the ultimate evil that mankind could exact. As Paul rose to his feet, his daughter wrapped tightly in his arms, he saw for the first time what had been scrawled on the far wall in human blood. He mouthed the words as his stomach performed somersaults once again. “You Failed.” Paul began to turn in a circular motion as he took in once more the horror chamber that had been created in his own bedroom. The sickening chains, the blood soaked carpet and his wife, mauled at the hands of a psychopath. He repeatedly kissed the head of his daughter and then lowered down so he could look at her. His eyes scanned her quickly but there was no bloodshed here or sign of injury. Despite the turmoil in his mind, he breathed a shallow sigh of relief as his baby girl was safe in his arms. Nobody would hurt her, nobody would get to her, she was going to be okay, yes she was going to be alright. He stroked her cheek with his finger in the hopes of distracting her and raising a smile. As he did so, her mouth opened and as he desperately hoped for a smile, his hopes would be shattered and his sanity tested. From her mouth came words that he had never heard before from his precious little baby girl. “He’s going to fuck me like an animal , Daddy!” Time freezes. The mind fails. The heart stops. Restarts. Pounding. Now scream. And…. ************************************************************************************ “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!” Paul Cain’s eyes flash open and he sits bolt upright in the single bed. Sweat is pouring from his body, its coldness clinging to him. The rapid slamming of his heart in his chest is almost audible from outside his body. He can feel his fists closed around the covers of the bed, his knuckles almost whitening with the tightness of the grip. The fear was surrounding him and almost choking him as he fought with his own lungs for air. All he could hear was the ticking of his alarm clock which sounded almost deafening in his ears. Breathe Paul, breathe. It was just a nightmare. An ugly, disgusting nightmare. It’s not real. It’ll never be real. It’ll never become real. It may become real. It will become real. He’s out there. He’s waiting. He’s coming for them. Chainz. ************************************************************************************ “Dusk! Dusk!” Paul Cain’s voice booms out around the vast warehouse which has seen many statements of intent made by this PRIME superstar. In the middle of this open space is the battered old wrestling ring where Cain has both trained and sat in solitude to gather his physical and mental strength. Now he walks a wide circle in his black hooded training top and matching pants with his arms spread out wide. He tilts his head back and yells out to the roof of the warehouse. “Are you listening to me Dusk?! Can you hear me?!” Cue a complete change of view, Cain is now sitting down in the corner of the ring, leaning his head back against the lowest turnbuckle pad. His face is obscured by the side of his hood and in his right hand, a pair of red dice. He toys with them between his forefinger and thumb for a few moments before he speaks, his voice a firm but calm tone. “I warned you once Dusk, you never heeded my warning. I told you to hide your ignorance but you couldn’t do it. Your quest to find new admirers here in PRIME, who will masturbate furiously and ejaculate over a picture of you, has blinded you. This isn’t GLOBAL and you know it, your fan boys don’t live here and the people that will worship you daily are no more. Now you crave that adoration, that need to exist, the responsibility of being a hero to many, you would do anything to have that again. Even force a man, whose very family is at threat from a psychotic, twisted necrophilia loving paedophile, to fight for your cause, a cause that quite frankly, he never gave two shits for.” Change of view number two, Paul Cain is leaning on the top rope, looking down to the floor. “We both had choices, Dusk. Mine was to fight for something I didn’t believe in, or fight for something that I have always believed in, my daughter. Your choice? To do what’s right or to walk away? You chose the latter and now I’m faced with a liar who claims to be a family man but clearly doesn’t understand the value of your own flesh and blood. YOUR child, YOUR creation, YOUR loved one who is threatened by someone who wants to harm her. What would YOU do then? Help a self serving bastard who you care nothing for, or be there for the one you love? You couldn’t answer me then, and you answered me that night by expecting me to be at your side. Remember, you turned your back on me first and that I won’t forget.” Change of view number three, Paul Cain is now standing on the top turnbuckle looking up at the rooftop once more. “Fuck Garbage Bag Johnny. Fuck The Little Dick Eater and fuck you with your over-hyped fan boy filled career. I never wanted to cross paths with you, I never wanted to be a part of the Dusk Welcoming Committee and I sure as hell didn’t want to be a spin doctor for the Dusk For PRIME Main Eventer Campaign! This is real life that I’m talking about, my daughter and the man that wants to hurt her in order to fuck up my world. You don’t come anywhere near to my radar, Dusk and you never will. So I’m sorry that I’m not falling at your feet, I’m sorry that I’m not throwing my daughter to the wolf in order to serve you but that’s life, get fucking used to it!” Change of view number four, Paul Cain is standing over the camera, looking down at it. “You made this choice Dusk and now you will be the one to serve me, not the other way around. Because it’s time to show the hunter just how hunted he can become and I’m going to give you the pleasure of being my example to set. We could have been friends Dusk, but you blew it. We could have been so good together, but you screwed it up. We could have helped each other, but you don’t know how to help anyone but yourself. We could have been the cure to PRIME’s cancerous tumours but you would rather become one yourself. You made your choice Dusk, now I make mine.” Paul lifts the camera up with his hand and holds it tightly. “And I’ll do it all for love…” Cain shoves the camera down as the scene fades to black.
