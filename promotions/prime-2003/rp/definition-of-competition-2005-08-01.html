Definition of Competition

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/1/2005 ~Competition. Competition fosters creativity and greatness. I guess it was competition that made Jon Kano into the world-class wrestler, respected businessman, and all-around amazing human being that we have all come to know today. Who else in the wrestling industry today can honestly say that they came from nothing and before they were thirty years old were walking, breathing, bonafide legends. Not a whole hell of a lot of people. Who else in the wrestling industry today can honestly say that they wrestle not for the money, but for the sheer love of the wrestling industry? Nobody. Kano is the only one. Kano has risen so far above the rest that he no longer has competition. Kano has often said that wrestling is a drug. A drug that he is hopelessly addicted to and can't kick. Nor does he want to. Ten years from now, maybe twenty, people are going to look back on PRIME, and they will say, 'I miss Jon Kano. He was better than all the rest, and he was far ahead of his time. He wasn't understood while he was a wrestler, but now everyone sees that he wasn't some flash in the pan superstar, he was a REAL LEGEND.' Kano has recently been on a streak of bad luck, he hasn't been able to quite grasp his former glory. He feels that wrestling may not be worth it anymore. But in very recent weeks, Kano has discovered that to find his old self, he had to consort with an unsavory person for advice. As much as Kano has a distain for this person from his past, he still respects what that person has done for the industry and for Kano himself. This has led to Kano finding his former self and his former glory. He goes out night after night and puts over the 'stars of tomorrow.' He has never asked, 'Whats in it for me?' Well now he must ask, 'Whats in it for me?' ReVolution this week is the dawning of a new era. Nobody entertains like Jon Kano. This era will be a return to the old school for Kano. No more catering to what people would like to hear. In this era of Kano, it will be for one person, and one person only, Jon Kano. He has long been held down by his own fear of not living up to his legendary status he forged in PCW, this will be no longer. Times, they are a-changing. This new era will be a grand era, and an era like none other. Competition will not be an issue. The issue will be if you can keep up with the definition of competition known as Jon Kano.~ *The scene opens up in a remote wooded area. It is darker than three midnights, with the only light coming from a raging fire in a cleared area of the forest. A man dressed in Army fatigues from the head down, and in black combat boots. This man is tall, about six feet, six and weighs about two hundred and fifty pounds. He has short blond hair and resembles Jon Kano remarkably in the face.* Man: Jon, I come to you tonight to tell you that I am very proud of you. You took our dream, and made it come true. I only regret that I am not there in body to see it, but Jon, I am there in spirit. I live through you. I am there when you wrestle. I am there when you sleep, making sure that you sleep soundly. I wake you in the mornings. I was there when you cried into Kristin's arms at my funeral. I was putting my arms around you, only you couldn't feel my hug, no matter how much I wanted to comfort you in your time of grief. I love you more than any other person on that earth. I know you think about me everyday, and trust me, Jon, I think about you. I think about when we were kids and how we used to play together. I think about how we watched wrestling every week, and never missed it. I remember how I set all the records in high school in baseball, and how you shattered them later. I remember how much I wanted to stay with you, but the Lord called me, and it was just my time. My last thoughts were of you. I thought about the pain you would go through, and I prayed to God himself that I could just hold on to say goodbye to you. Well, Jon, as you know, God answers all prayers, but sometimes the answer is no. He does everything for a reason, and my death has made you into a better person, and to financially secure our whole family. I know that is not much of an explanation, but Jon, you have become a king among men, a prince among thieves, a saint out of a sinner. You constantly do the best not for yourself, but to entertain people who paid money to see you, and for the overall good of whatever promotion you are working for. Jon, you have made me VERY PROUD of you. I love you, Jon. Everything is going to be alright. You take care of your beautiful wife and son. Bye, Jon. *The scene changes abruptly to a sleeping Jon Kano. Kano dressed in KANO ROCKS~! pajamas suddenly wakes up with a start and sits straight up from underneath his silk bed sheets and reaches out with his right arm.* Kano: KEN!!!!!!!!!! *Kano calms down and realizes that he was just dreaming about his deceased brother. He reaches to the table next to his bed, flicks on a light, opens the drawer, pulls out a cigarette, and lights it.* Kano (exhaling smoke as he speaks): Son of a b*tch, I hate when I do that. Ken is dead, why can't I just stop dreaming about it. God damnit anyway. If Ken is trying to get to me somehow, why can't he be like a real spirit and haunt me. Go figure, he was lazy anyway. He probably figures it would be too much work to haunt me. *Kano chuckles to himself.* Kano: He sure could be lazy at times. Christ, I miss him. His timing is pretty damn good. *Kano pauses to take a deep breath of the cancerous smoke that will probably kill him and exhales slowly.* Kano: I hate to say it, but I think the unmentionable one whom I will refer to as Mr. R was right. It wasn't backstage politics that gave me the tools to become a legend. It wasn't the old guard of PCW that made me a legend. It was my motivation that gave me my gift to become a legend. It was Ken who motivated me to become a wrestler in the first place. Ken is the fuel to my fire that burns for this business and this industry. A fire that will never go out. A fire that cannot be extinguished. That is why I do this amazingly dumb thing I do known as professional wrestling. I don't need the money, that is for damn sure. I am filthy rich and I would wrestle for free. Yet I go out on the road 250 or 300 days out of the year away from my great house, Fortune 500 company, and beautiful wife and infant son to bleed, sweat, and practically kill myself all because of the fire that burns inside of me. This fire I can't spend, hell I can't even see it. I guess I am not as smart as some people may think I am. *Kano pauses just long enough to take the final drag off of his cigarette and put it out in the ashtray on the table next to him.* Kano: And now, here we are in the new chapter of PRIME, the first ReVolution of Chapter in PRIME history. And to usher in this new era, we are having a huge battle royal as the main event of sorts for ReVolution. The prize for the winner is yet to be determined, so somebody is going to get screwed over. But just imagine thirty-six of us kicking the hell out of each other, and the sad thing is it won't make a difference. I want to know who is going to toss my seven foot tall, three hundred pound plus legendary ass over the top rope and to the floor. There might be three members of the active PRIME roster in this match who can match up against me and have a chance at doing that. Regardless, in this new chapter, this new era of PRIME will forever be remember as mine. Nobody will dominate like Jon Kano. I will set the definition of competition. I will set the bar so high that no one will be able to compare. This all begins at ReVolution 71, the dawning of the Kano era in PRIME. This is where I show everyone why I am exactly what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *The scene fades to black as Kano begins to roll out of bed, obviously fully awake now after giving himself a little pep talk.* ~Is Kano really back or is he just showing a glimmer of his former glory? Only time will tell, but if he can follow through on the majority of the things he has just said, God have mercy on PRIME, because Kano sure as hell won't.~
