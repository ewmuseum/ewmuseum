Date: Mmmm hippo I finally did it…I finally did it Those four words kept repeating on a continuous loop in his mind ever since his hand was raised by Bernie Roberts at the conclusion of UltraViolence. Devin Shakur was still soaking in the congratulatory messages from fellow superstars, staff members, and celebrities until 2:30 in the morning. Most of them were forced, as Shakur is not exactly the most embraceable individual on the PRIME roster, nor the most congenial, but nonetheless respect was mandatory. He had just defied the odds and gone from the third wheel in the Universal Title match to the top dog with one haphazard firing of his leg that found Cozen’s temple. Regardless of luck, Lindsay Troy’s assist by knocking Cozen down at 9, Shakur managed to fight his own body and rise in the allocated time to acquire the biggest victory of his wrestling career. No longer could people call him a choker, or someone who could never close the deal. He had fifteen pounds of gold and two middle fingers to shove back in their face. Not to mention a plethora of pain littering his entire body. Before midnight, bruising began to set in and Shakur knew the anguish would only intensify. Troy and her doppelganger left no portion of his anatomy unscathed in their relentless assault for supremacy. Thankfully, no bones were broken, but The Man in Black would be in dire need of a hot tub or a bottle of vodka, whichever was easier to acquire. With a grimace on his face, Shakur rises from a recliner to give his final guests, Chuck Liddell and girlfriend Erin Wilson, a fist pound and handshake respectively. Once the two escort themselves from the makeshift locker room, Shakur grabs a rolling suitcase along with a heavy gym bag beside the recliner, and slowly walks down the corridor in search of his limousine. Shakur receives "Good nights" from the late night staffers who are desperately trying to rearrange Wrigley Field to accommodate the returning Chicago Cubs. Turning the corner, Shakur elbows open the ‘Exit’ door and finds only one limousine waiting in the parking lot. For him and him only The Champ gives a quick smirk until he picks up his right leg and feels a stinging amount of pain ride up the entire limb.
