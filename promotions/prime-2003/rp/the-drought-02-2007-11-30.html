The Drought (.02)

30 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/30/07 Cage Steele had come too far to stop now. He stood defiant, the wide chest of his six and a half foot frame heaving from the recent exertion. His ice blue eyes stared forward, cold and hard and exuding every heated fiber of vengeance and retribution that had set him upon this path. His hair, matted with blood and sweat, clumped in odd patches upon his head. His left eye was swollen. Blood trickled from his nose and the corner of his mouth. But he stood, while the unconscious bodies of more than a dozen suited Mafiosos circled the concrete around him. Now, finally, there was nothing between him and his brother’s killer. After a long, bloody trail, Cage Steele had come to the end of his journey. He gave up his badge, his wife, even his own closely held morals to come this far. All the sacrifices seemed worth it, though, to be standing in this moment, staring into those eyes, and knowing, one way or the other, it would all be over soon. “This ends tonight.” His calm voice, tainted with the slightest of English accents, is more terrifying than any angered roar or violent outburst. Cage Steele is no longer the man he used to be. No longer the emotional, play-by-the-rules detective he’d been so proud to be just a few short months ago. Grief and hatred had twisted him into something unrecognizable. Something horrible and violent and absolutely unforgiving. Tonight, yes tonight, Cage Steele would finally have his revenge. His nemesis, mob kingpin and all-around bad guy, Tony Spiccoli smiles at the man who’s come to take his life. “The only thing ending tonight is you, Steele. I’ll kill you, just like I killed your little brother. And then, then there will be nothing left to stop me from taking complete control of this city.” ************* Tyler Rayne rolls his eyes, attempting not to laugh derisively as he watches his long time friend and former partner, Duke Valentine, film the final fight scene of his latest straight to DVD release, Caged 2: Unchecked Aggression. With complete abhorrence he watches the ghastly spectacle, a poorly polished battle that looked like it had been choreographed on the back of a napkin after one too many lines of blow. The man portraying Tony Spiccoli had all the grace of a three-legged elephant after an all-night bender, and it wasn’t more than about thirty seconds in that he blew his first spot. Duke, to his credit, remained professional as the director and Tony, (Tyler could neither remember nor cared to remember the actor’s real name) discussed a different approach for the scene. “We’re wasting time.” Tyler sighs, crossing his arms over the black t-shirt stretched tight across his chiseled frame. Though he continues to watch the debacle unfolding before him, he sees, from the corner of his left eye, his own masked persona pacing impatiently about the floor. “There are people out there who tried to kill you. Kill us. They’re laughing at you right now. Laughing at you being a pussy. Laughing at you being a shell of the man I used to be. And what are you doing? You’re watching some washed up former wrestler attempt to carve a meager living by making movies that would make Jean-Claude Van Damme cringe with disgust.” “I’m not talking to you right now.” A woman standing nearby turns to give Tyler a quizzical look, but as he does not acknowledge her presence, she quickly returns to her duties. His eyes glare firmly forward, as painful as it may be to watch Tony trip over his own left foot, blowing yet another take. This time, Tyler cannot keep himself from scoffing, which draws a look from more than just one woman. “I can’t believe you. I cannot FUCKING believe you. What, did that big bitch Troy put your balls in a jar on her nightstand? You lose your fuckin’ nerve, son.” “Stop.” Even the director turns with a questioning stare, but his attention is almost immediately drawn back to the scene, where Tony has seemingly injured his fist when he accidentally followed through and legitimately punched Duke in the jaw. Duke is, not surprisingly, less jarred by the event than his co-star. Unfortunately, Tyler doesn’t get a chance to watch the pathetic affair, as his masked and completely imaginary alter-ego steps right in front of him. Those cold, dead eyes blaze with a hate and fire Duke only wishes he could fake. “Stop? STOP? Why don’t you stop, huh? Stop being a FUCKING PUSSY! Take your dick back from that Amazon whore, sew that shit back together, and get to fuckin’ steppin’. I can’t even understand you, man. You know, what? Hey. I’ve got an idea. Maybe we should go kill her, huh? Maybe we shove some cool chrome up that bitch’s dried cooch and give ‘er somethin’ to squeal about. Yeah. How ‘bout that? That’s what you want, anyway, right? Make the ice queen squirm. Fuck knows that bitch is so frigid she could use a good, long somethin’ shoved up her—“ “ENOUGH!!” He does it without thinking. Without realizing where he is. Without stopping to consider the consequences. It’s a reaction. Something he barely thought to control. But as the last echoes of the outburst reverberate through the studio, as all eyes turn shocked and offended in his direction, Tyler Rayne realizes he’s fucked up. Somethin’ fierce. The director is glaring needles now, but it still takes a moment for the shock of the situation to pass before he begins a very determined march in Tyler’s direction. Duke is only too quick to follow, but both of them are pushed aside by Tony, who marches quite defiantly and haughtily up to Rayne. “Don’t think I haven’t heard you laughing back here, you little maggot. Who the fuck do you think you are, anyway? Some coffee boy or something I imagine. Watched too many movies yourself. Think this is so god damn easy, do you? Think you can do better?” Tyler stands very still. Very quiet. He’s trying not to explode all over this jackass. Trying not to take out all his frustrations on some over-inflated ego who can’t even manage a fight scene in a C-movie. Duke places a calming hand on his co-star’s shoulder. “Rich, look, mate—“ The hand is quickly, and very harshly, shoved away. “Fuck off, Valentine. If the little gaffer boy here thinks he can do better, I’m simply inviting him to try. C’mon, then. What’s a matter? Cat got yer tongue? Okay to laugh on the sidelines, but someone comes and gets in your face, and you’re just nothin’ but a big…fuckin’…pussy.” The last three words are each accentuated with a forefinger jab to Tyler’s chest. “Kill this cunt drip.” Tyler has to bite his bottom lip to keep from speaking. His hands are drawn into fists so tight his recently regrown fingernails are close to drawing blood. Yet he manages, with more effort than imaginable, to remain still. After a handful of seconds in this stand-off, Rich scoffs, dropping a disgusted and superior look over Rayne. “Fuckin’ thought so.” The actor shakes his head with contempt before turning to reclaim his spot on stage. Duke shoots Tyler a quick “what the fuck” look before following, and slowly, the rest of the crew begins to return to their duties as well. All except for the director, who sidles up very close to Tyler so that only the PRIMEate is able to hear what he says. “Another outburst like that and I’ll have you arrested and prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law. You’ll be taking a tour of Los Angeles in the backseat of a police car.” “Un-be-fuckin-lievable.” Tyler’s hand snaps forward, snatching the director by the collar of his pretentious silk shirt and dragging him within a centimeter of that rough, unshaven exterior. Tyler does not look at the director as he speaks, but rather focuses entirely on the actor now known as Rich. “You just keep those cameras rollin’, dick lick. This is gonna make for one hell of a YouTube video.” The director begins to open his mouth in protest, but one very icy, very hostile look from Rayne brings all that to an abrupt halt. Instead, the director finds himself cowering, as much as he can while still in that grip, before subsequently being shoved to the side. Crew members quickly and enthusiastically move away as Tyler stalks toward the set. Duke’s the first to see him coming, and the look of horror on his handsome face is enough to turn Rich’s attention. The actor smiles derisively, that is of course, until Tyler pulls the t-shirt from his body, revealing a menagerie of scars and burns that draw gasps and hushed whispers from the crew, and effectively wipes anything resembling a smile from Rich’s face. Duke rushes forward, attempting to keep pace at Tyler’s side as he stalks onto the stage. “What the fuck are you doing, man?” “Giving him what he asked for.” “Look, you and I both know you can kill this git with one hand. There’s no need to—“ The same cold, menacing glare that stopped the director brings even this, a close and long-time friend, to a screeching stop. Knowing the futility, and stupidity, of arguing further, Duke Valentine simply raises his hands in submission, backing away to watch the massacre soon to commence. Taking Rayne’s suggestion, the director motions for everyone to start filming again, as Rich seems to have composed himself. That confident, superior grin back on his face, Rich steps forward and falls into the same fighting stance he had previously been using against Duke. “Your last chance to back out, punk. It’ll be real hard to fetch my coffee after I break both your legs, eh?” Tyler, much to the surprise of Duke Valentine and anyone else who knows him well, does not respond. He simply glares, not even bothering to drop down into a defensive pose, and waits for the actor to strike. Rich comes faster than expected, though not fast enough to actually land a punch, and Tyler realizes very quickly that the actor has, shockingly, had some sort of formal training in hand-to-hand. Tyler, however, simply backs away, sidesteps, and occasionally leans away from the relentless and hastily thrown punches coming at him. Three months of training in some two-bit McDojo is hardly enough to best someone of Tyler’s unparalleled fighting experience. Tyler sidesteps the next punch, reaching out to grab Rich by the wrist, and does quick work of twisting the arm behind the actor’s back. His other hand comes to the back of the actor’s head, and with as much disappointment as he can muster into a single gesture, Tyler shoves the actor away. Rich stumbles a handful of steps forward, damn near tumbling over his own awkwardness. With a roar of frustration, Rich charges forward, attempting a spear or shoulder block or something equally as ridiculous. This isn’t a movie, and Tyler Rayne all too easily steps away from the charge. Rich halts himself almost immediately, face contorted in rage and embarrassment when he turns once again to unleash his assault. Tyler just as easily dodges these attacks as last, though now the actor attempts to throw some sloppy kicks into his repertoire, forcing Tyler to actually use his hands in his defense. He swats one leg away, then the other, and just as quickly lunges forward with a headbutt that smashes the actor’s nose and knocks out two teeth. The explosion of blood is almost instantaneous. A female member of the crew gasps almost as loudly as Rich screams out in pain. This, as they say, is only the beginning. Tyler’s fist flashes forward, smashing against the actor’s throat with the same devastating force as the headbutt. The actor’s body involuntarily attempts to spit blood and gasp for air at the same time, producing something of a dry heave effect. Tyler steps around the actor’s back, hooking his defenseless victim in a full-nelson. From there, Tyler’s left knee rises repeatedly and quite viciously into Rich’s kidney, continuing a reenactment of that dry-heave motion and eliciting more than a few startled gasps from the otherwise funeral silent audience. After more than two dozen of these intentionally paralyzing blows, Tyler pushes his victim away with all the contempt the actor had shown him only moments earlier. Rich stumbles face first into a large stack of palettes, a reprieve for about half a second before Tyler hits him with a flying kick to the back, and immediately thereafter, a knee to the side of the head. Tyler, of course, lands on his feet without much effort, glaring down at the actor with that same unforgiving stare. There is absolute silence on the set, with the exception of Rich’s hacking attempts to cough up blood through a ruptured esophagus. Duke Valentine is the first to move, and he quickly rushes to Tyler, attempting to pull him away. “I think you proved your point, mate. Let ‘im be.” Tyler shrugs the bigger man off, taking two quick and agile steps forward. He places a boot against the stack of palettes and pushes off, launching himself into a back flip over the fallen actor. Tyler pulls his legs up, reaching back to grab his own ankles, heels touching his ass, effectively turning himself into a human missile. A missile that crashes knees first onto Rich’s chest, forcing an eruption of crimson to spew from his otherwise useless mouth. Tyler rises slowly, pointedly, turning to face the crowd of aghast on-lookers. The woman who first looked at him is crying into the arms of a fellow crew member. No one else speaks or moves. Few of them bother to even close the gaping “oh” formed by their mouths. He sweeps that cold, uncaring glare across the crew as a whole before stalking across the floor, bending to snatch his shirt from the floor as he goes. Just as before, the crowd parts very quickly, giving Tyler Rayne a wide berth as he storms off the set. He does not turn to address any one person, does not stop to ensure that one of them has recovered from shock long enough to heed his suggestion. He simply speaks loudly and clearly, assuming that one of them will have the wits to listen. “I’d suggest you call a medic soon. If they get here within the next five, they might be able to keep him from drowning in his own blood. And if he does live, someone tell him to keep his fuckin’ mouth shut next time.”
