Date: Today Chapter One - Poppy Tea ''...Adrian.'' Amy looked up from her cup of tea, regarding a tall man in a hoodie from behind thick-framed glasses. ''Amy!'' he smiled with a sarcastic enthusiasm, ''I see you still tote the emo glasses to see. Hope they don't cramp your style too much.'' The redhead took another sip of her tea. This was going to be a long encounter. Adrian was her ex-boyfriend and not at all someone she could claim to be a friend to. Whenever they saw one another, a fight was inevitable - he knew how to pluck at her very last nerve, and tonight was absolutely no different once he spotted her drink. ''Doesn't smell like booze,'' he observed, sitting down across from her at the tiny dining table, ''Tea's an awful funny drink for ya, Amers.'' Amy simply rolled her eyes. Nothing - not even Adrian - would interrupt this pleasant little trip. ''So I hear Jeph had an accide---'' Adrian paused abruptly, noticing some of Amy's breakfast leftover on the table in the form of a fine powder next to a card of paper. He ran his finger across the little spot of dust with a wry smile. ''That's sorta funny, this almost looks like cocaine!'' he chuckled with a tongue still wet from dripping sarcasm, ''But I'm sure it's just sugar spilled from your cereal bowl, right?'' Amy was still pleasantly seated as Adrian looked over the table, then glanced into the empty sink and completed his jab, ''But...not a bowl in sight? Doesn't that seem odd to you? I mean, I'm sure your family wo--'' ''Your money's under my sneaker by the door,'' Amy interrupted with a mutter, ''Now drop the pills and fuck off.'' ''Fine, but cocaine and poppy tea aren't a good combination, little one. Far be it for me to look out for your safety and all, but I know you're trying to speedball without injecting...it's not gonna work, 'cause you'll just need to redose the coke twice in an hour to keep with the opium buzz and that'd just make you an addi...oh, I forgot who I was talkin' to for a second!'' ''Drop.pills. Fuck.off.'' Adrian sighed and tossed a small prescription bottle onto the table in front of the flustered redhead, ''But what's to say I'm not gonna start talkin' 'bout this, huh? I mean, you're getting pretty big again...could be some press in it for me.'' It was an absolutely empty threat and Amy knew that, but answered to spite him. ''Because you've got a son you don't deserve to have, and he'd be gone if I ever tipped the cops off to your little enterprise.'' ''Guess what, Amy? You've got one too. At least I support mine...you let your parents bear your burden. And what e---oh, you're MARRIED! Does Jepha know that you haven't divorced that heroic roidfreak?'' The redhead's rage was only growing, but she wasn't capable of lashing out as she would've wanted to. Her body was far too relaxed to show Adrian the pain he deserved. Instead, she took the high road with a response that came from between clenched teeth, ''Jeph knows everything, we just don't waste our time talking about things that're taking care of themselves.'' ''Nothing takes care of itself with you around, Ames. Your existance is complicated beyond belief, take our relationship for exa---'' ''Get out.'' Amy stood up and Adrian felt the tension rise with her form. He snarled right back to her, ''Or what? You're too high, and fuckwad's not even outta hospital.'' ''I've had enough of you, Adriano. I don't have time for this shit, and you know me - when I've had enough, I've had enough,'' she repeated as her fingertips grazed the handle of a steak knife that had been laid on the table. It was a hollow gesture but a threatening situation no less, ''So leave before our 'relationship' ends all over again.'' Adrian's hands raised in the air as he backed towards the door. He only paused to bend and take a small wad of money from underneath one of Campbell's sneakers as he stepped out with ''Still such a fuckin' sketch.'' and no more. Chapter Two - Sharks And Danger2 weeks later. Week of March 14th. The soft fleece cover was comforting beyond belief, but not quite enough to keep Amy in bed all day. She'd just had a rare night's sleep, but her brain in waking up operated a mile a minute. There was so much that had gone on, and she'd been coming back down off of drugs since Jeph came home from the hospital, but not enough had ever been said. They always spoke of the obvious problems and never what would lay beneath. She assumed that's why she really should have been seeing a therapist, as opposed to talking to her boyfriend. While she rubbed the sleep from her eyes and munched lightly at a cinnamon raison bagel - her diner ritual having been one of many habits recently broken - the little redhead watched the rise and fall of Jeph's chest as he slept in one of the straightest, most uncomfortable positions she could imagine. His back would soon heal enough to spare him that annoyance, but that knowledge made it no more comfortable for Amy to gaze upon. Watching his deep sleep made her contemplate a number of things - one being subconscious reception... maybe she could tell him the things she didn't understand the Amy way, instead of trying to explain her skewered thoughts while he was awake. What if people could process spoken word in their sleep? It was worth a shot. ''I'm sure this is all gonna sound really ridiculous and, uh... I don't exactly expect anyone to believe all this. For the last little while I've been kinda keeping correspondance with my sanity, and uhh, I really get the feeling that I'm starting to lose interest in the letter writing process,'' she began from her sudden perch beside their bed, ''See...the pieces of the letters, they just get progressively shorter and shorter until it's not even a reply.'' His chest rose again, only to cave into a burst of air. It was a peaceful, happy sight...but Amy wasn't smiling. ''I...'' stuttered the redhead, ''The whole thing makes me really nervous. I just wish I could show you what a huge problem this is. I kind of have this...I dunno...this really terrible habit of asking myself all these stupid questions, right?'' It's not like he could respond. ''Like what if...what if there's a spot on my body that I can touch to keep my heart from beating? Or, like, what if...what if theres this stray bullet out there that's gonna come through the window and take me out? What if there's this huge, unstoppable comet that's just gonna like, destroy the earth and all of us? Like what... what if that's happening right now? How can I avoid all these questions if I don't have that sense of myself? Who's gonna behere? Why... who'll be there in the hospital? Who's gonna tell me that all this is bullshit... it's a dream? This is why I can't ever sleep, Je---'' Blue eyes suddenly fluttered open across the room. ''Hmph...'' groaned the waking punker, gazing over at his girlfriend in her frenzied and surprised state. He hoped he hadn't interrupted anything. ''Shit! Err...uhh...morning.'' Amy tried putting on her warmest smile and Jeph's sleepy eyes glanced around the room in confusion, ''Who were you talkin' to?'' ''Oh, that? Just the phone. Sorry I was so loud...that must'a been what woke you up.'' ''Ah,'' he replied, stretching out as best he could, ''It's pretty early...who called?'' Amy Campbell was a bad liar, she just hoped Jeph wouldn't take notice this time. Direct communication could be such a chore. ''Who...oh, uhh...I called them. Gibson, actually. Dave. Yeah. Just felt like chatting with someone...'' Paranoia. Withdrawl. Confusion. That was all Aimz. She didn't even realize that he actually had been awake to hear her words, and he wasn't out to embarass the normally withdrawn girl. ''Ah, Gibbo.'' Jeph grinned at his perfectly flawed redhead and rubbed the sleep from the edges of his eyes as she curled back up next to him, on top of the blankets this time. She was determined to be awake for the day, and fought her body's instinct to sleep the moment it calmed under Jeph's gaze, but that was one match she'd never win... Chapter Three - And All Things Will EndA week later... ''...And I'm out of that.'' Amy Campbell was finishing her scrawled signature on a rather hefty looking stack of stapled papers. She stood in a lawyer's office and smiled, because she wasn't the accused this time. The name beside hers on the paper read 'Miles Massey - Divorce Lawyer'. That was really all she needed to see to get her in a signing frenzy. ''You are indeed, ma'am,'' the aging law man smiled warmly and stuck his hand out as a formality, one she was oddly couth in taking and shaking, ''And you're sure you don't want to try for a percentage of the estate, or joint custody of...'' he made a glance at the sheets of paper, ''Sean and Ja--'' ''They're not even mine, and I don't need to take the guy's kids or money. I didn't fit into that life or family, that's all.'' ''And that's well and good, Miss Campbell, but...'' ''But this is all I need. You'll be getting your check from my dad, don't worry. You've been a lot of help, Miles...thanks for that.'' She shook his hand again and was guided back to the lobby of his office, reflecting on three very wasted years of her life... ...Kids, a family that was a family before she came along, a man who was worlds and lightyears apart from who she truly was... Amy had a headache again. Luckily, the cure met her just outside of the lobby. She was walking out into a rainstorm that had started sometime during the hours she'd been in that cursed office, wearing a soon to be sopping a pair of dress pants - her half-hearted attempt to keep the lawyer from running the other way - and a black wife-beater that matched her slacks only in color. Her smile grew wide when she saw Jeph waiting outside, ready with his car, but it dimmed in a confused twist when she noticed a taser in his hand. He tossed it at her playfully and Amy caught it, still confused as she scurried up to kiss him on the cheek and follow his escape into the car. ''Weird,'' she chuckled, regarding the light weapon with a raised brow, ''You didn't strike me as the kinky type...'' Jeph laughed along as he turned the coupe's heater up all the way. That rain was far too chilling and, despite her best efforts, Amy was visibly shivering. 'Funny, but it's for you,'' he smirked and started to pull away from the curb, ''Maria from the PRIME offices called, said she was sent your next booking...'' Once more, Amy cocked her eyebrow at the little yellow and black stun gun. ''...And I'm facing a team of riot control cops?'' ''No, Boston 'T'ea Party match.'' ''Hot liquid and facial burns! IT'S LIKE A DREAM COME TRUE!'' Jeph was laughing all over again at her somewhat psychotically violent - albeit sarcastic - glee but he soon corrected her, ''Four-way thingy, standard rules except that you can legally use any weapon that begins with a--'' ZZZZZZZAP! Suddenly, the air was filled with male curses and the giddy giggling of an all-too amused redhead. ''NEW RULE,'' Jeph scoffed, ''No weapon-testing on the one with the penis.'' ZAP! More giggles from Amy, followed by her retort of ''Fine, I'll tell you when I see him.'' Howe had his eyes set firmly on the road from there on in, and they drove in silence for nearly a minute until Amy couldn't take another moment of boredom... ''...C'mon, are you actually mad 'cause I zapped you? It's your fault for giving me the thing!'' Jeph was playing angry, fully aware that he had his girlfriend convinced it was genuine. ''Jepha?'' Amy plead, ''Come ooooooon. Fine, just explain the match to me? No taser this time, promise.'' ''It's a Boston 'T' Party dealie...you can use anything that starts with a T, like tape or taser, legally.'' ''Now, was that so difficult?'' Jeph sighed, ''...With you holding a taser? Yes. I shouldn't have given that thing to you so early, but Gibson thought it'd get the creative juices flowing.'' He couldn't help but notice that Amy was distracted by staring out the window at her rainy hometown, carefully noting and mouthing anything she saw that incorporated the letter T. This was going to be a silly game for her, and he knew it. The problem with that was that, after being focused on her career to the point of beating Karina Wolfenden cleanly in fWo, Aimz was slipping backwards. She'd become so focused that she was distracted far too much by small details - like the current dilemma of finding creative weapons - and concentrating less on simply winning. That had demonstrated itself against Ito weeks earlier, and distraction already threatened to rear its ugly head in this match...which she'd only become aware of not two minutes before. He decided to try focusing her again, but the comment came off haphazardly. ''We're going to Canadian Tire, by the way. Gibbo's already there...I figured this is the only way I could ever actually help you prepare for a match,'' he chuckled, ''Listing shit that you could use to win.'' Ah, shopping for weaponry at your local hardware store...every girl's most romantic fantasty! ...............Well, if you were Amy Campbell. ''And while we're on the subject of tea parties...'' Jeph was going to catch her while she was distracted by street signs and violent fantasies involving tambourines...tanks...tasers...tacks....... ''...My parents are gonna be here two days after your Pay-Per-View, they're coming for dinner to meet'cha.'' Ta-----what? Jeph's parents? Amy wasn't only preparing for a match, but for her only meeting with Mrs. Howe since...well...since she was ten years younger and FAR less tattooed... ''...Yes, speaking of 'T' - the term 'Tight Bitch' just randomly popped to mind.'' Opium tea when Jeph wasn't around, clean Boston T Parties when he was. She was recovering from addictions but now, well...the thought of his mother, even her memories from being 12 years old, drove Amy to crave a stiff drink. Perhaps a punch if the stiff old bi-- ''Ames, stop contemplating maiming my mother.'' ...Damnit. [detail withheld] and busy this week, apologies if this came up short.
