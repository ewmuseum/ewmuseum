A Letter To Sieze An Imaginative Lamentation.

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-28-05 Remember. Everything you see here is pure imagination because that's Ozric's ordinary provision. He'd never want it any other way, no matter how many times he may say he does, because the realistic downside has been repeated so many times. Never turn away from his theories because they're only going to become more idiosyncratic as time goes by. Afterall, this a madmen assembling a revolution. What do you expect? The world never... Trample the daises and burn the mothership, afterall this is your plantation between two abandoned bananna islands that are close enough to swim to. Steal a handful of air from our dear sky and feed it to an animal you've become obsessed with. Follow your own shadow - as if it's a friendly snake inviting you to brunch inside his reptillion den - into the bowels of a forbidden forest to study the fruits of the unknown. Dedicate the remainder of your life to counting stars in the curiosity of human possibilities. Throw your obligatory calenders and to-do notepads away and take a ride down a road you've never been on. Look over the peaceful rememdy hidden from the over-anxious and peer into windows as an introduction to saving this planet from further deterioration. Believe it or not, we decide what our outcome is going to be in this act of civilization. Why bother mutilating our daily lives with diversity and cultivation when there's this massive pendulum ticking the epoch of everyone? There's a certain freedom embedded into us and we've taken advantage of it well, but, I've come to find the true brutality of it all. Where all good things come to a screeching end in front of your face. I've walked through the murkiness that defines the end and it put me at a puzzling predicament. My toes were barely gripping to the canvas underneath them, my body at the edge of a red mountain with two options floating in front of me like two silver clouds. I could either jump and sacrifice myself in hopes that the enemy within would be rid or step away from the passionate drop and gather my dicrepit independence for a minute to reconsider. In hindsight, death is only a transition, but a transition that I never expected to see so soon. My chest felt inexorably heavy and a rush of heat sent a shockwave throughout my body - familiar effects of an anxiety attack, but much more painfui - compelling me to collapse to my knees. Hunched over as air automatically escaped me, I took unlimited measures in fighting it. From the corner of my eye the moon smiled at me and started to count down from specific start numbers in an act to calm me. I expected something weird in my fight against the end, but nothing to this extent. The winds - handled by the hands of rhe Gods - started to pick up in girth until it reach manic speeds that had me gripping to crevices in the rock. I crawled backwards until something solid as the sun landed on both of my legs. I turned my head and noticed it was a pile of medical books crushing me. I pulled my legs out and envisioned the familiar face of a special psychiatrist by the name of Dr. Klingman through the firey atmosphere. The moon, atomizing from the acidic bearings, was no longer wholesome and colorful and peaceful, but moreso wasteful and coliginous and phantasmal. This was the story of my life; an event of which I'd never forget and would have never been able to predict earlier. Alas, deeply in the realm of the looking glass, there's always good to each scenario. It may have to be found or created out of your own hands, but its always a possibility that never inexcusably storms out. This was the start of something special, wasn't it? This where the decision of whether I wanted to live came into play, right? The sky was clearing up and the fire died down to a glaring ember in the middle of everything. The ground was shaking violently and with each tremor came a tremendous bolt of lightning that'd hit in random sections of the ground. Fearlessly, I rolled away from a couple of near strikes and started to scream at the top of my lungs: I'm no longer your puppet! You can't be inside of me any longer because I've become my own man! I've discovered an imagination bigger than the sky and I'll fight you for everything you're worth! I opened myself to the enemy and shot my arms into the air, nearly dislocating my shoulders. I could feel a sense of clarity rise in my system as the bolts of lighting grew much more aggressive. There was a boldness surfacing around me as I closed my eyes and repeated the same speech. It'll never be to my knowledge whether this was the right way to take things on but motivation was on my side and I took the option of finishing this monstrosity off or going down with the ship. Like a jagged screwdriver, lightning impaled my midsection and lifted me from the ground. There wasn't a pinch of pain, and if it wasn't for the uncanny weightlessness, I'd have felt rather comfortable. I smiled as the bolt continued to pierce into one section and pinball its way through various others. I'm as thick as the ocean, bold as the color black an tranquil as the rainforest. I no longer have an entrance way for you to safely come in. This is Nicholas Brocken taking the equity that is life by the horns and rocking the boat a notch. Better yet, this is Ozric Mortimer exposing the corruption of conception's transitions. A dull roar, like a pack of spirited wolves, sounds off around me in a ploy to scare me. It's been such a long time suffering, a long time waiting to understand why its been just that. I've opened my eyes to a certain light, a light that has guided me into the accurate direction lately and I never realized. A dozen explosions blast off in front of me, like a barrel of firecrackers, shooting oddball shrapnel toward me, cutting up my arms and legs. There is nothing left for me inside this time glass. I've got business to attend to on the outside and I intend on doing it in a fashion that I never knew I could. Once I'm given my bus ticket out of Dodge, I plan on capturing the dream that got away from me. The ambition of an entertainer at heart must be completed to truly ignite the flame of my story. I'm going back into a wrestling ring to regain my dignity and obtain the public eye one more time. This isn't about victories or breaking bones. This is about becoming something more than what I am today. To level up in this life that has turned a moldy green color in such a sudden fashion. I've got plans to be something, so the kid dressed in all blue said to me as I nearly bled to death on the side of an unknown street, plans to light a fire underneath the rumps of the irresponsive. OzricDid you hear me? I've got plans! The EndWhat plans would a recluse reject drug-addict have? OzricPlans to become more than you'll ever be! The End Please Nicholas, I am the firm definition of 'everything', you know that. Ozric You're living in the shadows of those you've taken advantage of. What will happen when everyone wakes up? The EndNobody wakes up, not even you. OzricWhat do you call what I'm doing now? The End A transition. The emergence of The End was revolutional. Where distinct colors of red blossomed into a solid machine with arms and legs and yellow eyes. Pristine, engulfed in perfection, the machine stared down at me. I could feel my knees buckling, shrinking lower and lower to the ground in fright. I could never explain his presence in words and I'd never try to, but he was built for war and proud to express it. I turned my head and glanced at that glorious drop for a second time before shooting a glare into the eye of The End. I shoved my foot into the gas pedal and scurried to the back of one of its steel feet and reached the edge of the mountain again. OzricWhat other choice do I have? The EndYou know your options. My intentions were never to kill you, persay, but to put you in the position of considering doing the deed yourself. It didn't take long to break you, didn't it Nicholas? OzricYou never broke me, Slavon. The machine twitched and immediately crumbled to the ground, forming into an actual humanoid, wearing a black and white suit with a yellow rose hanging out of his coat pocket. The EndI'm the end of all things, Nicholas. You will call me by my appropriate title. OzricI'm the prime example of reinvention, Slavon, and I'm known by the rest of the world as Ozric. You will call me by my appropriate title and fuck off afterwards. The EndAh, but that's where things get a bit sticky, Nicholas. I'm your divine spirit, boy, a trail of sin in your system that has made its resting spot for eternity. I could hear the turbulent rumbles of machines, my body shaking from the tremors, eyelids flickering and covering my eyes from the reality of it all. Slavon, my mentally-constructed arch nemesis was made of fire and guilt and used them both as weapons against my weakening resistence. I used everything within me to push on in a sprint, charging into the fierce enemy with one shoulder into his stomach. I felt the arduous burn melt my shoulders. The abhorrent stench of incandescent flesh was nauseating enough to form a distraction, like cateracts, in front of my eyes as I felt a heavy hand whap against me, sending my chassis into the air, smacking land nearly two hundred feet from Slavon. The EndYour demise is now! In this moment time stopped for me. Everything glazed over and Slavon was in mid-dart towards me. The world seemed apologetic to me; so peaceful and consoling. It was almost as if it was publicly regretting everything it had exposed to me. The true beauty of it all. The beauty of it all. The winding of time catching up to me was surreal and in a mind-boggling fast motion. Slavon shot past me like a speeding bullet as I dived to my right and closed my eyes. I heard a howl and saw him transform back into the steel machine, but before he could make even the slightest attack, his structure is helplessly thrown over the edge of the mountain. The EndI will leave you for a short period of time, Ozric. This has never ever ended, has it? I'll see you on the other side, dear friend. Remarkably, the red sky flushed out before my eyes and regained its deadly blue color. The finishing touches of death's grip put a damper on my reaction time, but the exploit that I had rid of what stings helped me through the puzzle. There I stood, in the middle of everything, staggering to a balance to remain a durable spirit, because that all we're ever going to be, right? I'm not the bonified reflection of perfection and neither are those in the ring with me. This may just be a dream. This may just be a transition, I'll never know. What I do know, on the other hand, is that this is the start of something wonderful. The start of something mechanically beautiful. I'm coming back with more motivation than I've ever had. I didn't nearly burn someone to death and disapear for absolutely no reason at all. I didn't orchestrate something so devastatingly perfect for absolutely no reason. I did it all to break this cycle of repetition. I needed to either create a revolution of my own and be the revolution through it all. It looks like I've killed two birds with one mighty stone. In the end, I will have developed a groundbreaking masterpiece into PRIME that'll force my name in lights. I will reach an objective that nobody ever thought I'd come close to. Ignatius.. Rampage.. Psymon.. Lopez.. scattered bodies that remain aestetic through the hearts and souls of everloving crowds around the world. The enchantment of topless, muscular men dancing inside a ring in hopes they can tire their opponent out. Talentless washouts that cling like a feline to things that are realistically unreachable. It's always the same.Carbon-copies. The drama is never needed. For there's a revolution calling, a revolution calling towards all of you. A revolution that has more purpose than all of you combined. A revolution built by the machines I've mentally allied myself with. The Inner VoiceWake up [i]Ozric[/i], do not fear, for your madness is here. Wake up Ozric, do not [i]fear[/i], for your madness is here. Wake up Ozric, do not fear, for your [i]madness[/i] is here. I woke inside a barricade of blankets, within the comfort of my bed. Crows screamed at eachother from the outside, squirrels softened their nests in the peaks of trees by gathering mouthfuls of leaves. Traffic had turned into a stretch of sludge by the time I had opened my eyes and the sunlight warmed my skin from the cold night. For once in my life I felt very alive and cognizant. Afterall, this is how my story starts. This is my own time to describe everything to those who've always wanted to know the deepest layers. You see, I've stood on the edge of something much too deep for much too long. I've always wanted to take the dive from fatally high points because I thought of myself as fearless. Suddenly, it's come to my attention that life has its obstacles that bring you back to a state of fright, no matter how immune you may be to it. Whether it be a dream or a sequence inside a plump thriller novel, fear rolls into every life at the most random times and can even be for no reason at all. To bring this all to a whole, I've written this letter to let you know, dearest Jack Klingman, that your service is well needed, if only to fulfill my desire for a friend. I've confronted my father and came at peace with my mother. I've walked miles and miles down highways, brutally wounded, but happy about the outcome. I've slipped in and out of sanity and have seen some of the most amazing things, because of our friendship. To keep this machine, to keep Slavon, from coming after me again, I must make one request for your assistance. To sieze the painful pictures inside of my head and scenarios stirring like a bowl of steaming stew in my head, to recreate the beauty of it all. The beauty of it all. After the dust cleared, I envionsed myself sitting on the edge of the mountain, staring at Slavon's mangled body at the bottom. I could sense that he was still alive, but ignored it. I stood back up and turned myself around to face my bedroom door. I opened it and there was a state of peace once again. The living room, the bathroom, the kitchen and the various decorations lying everywhere. This was where I was supposed to be, but would it ever last? The beauty of it all? Never. Just a transition in this lifelong journey, I suppose. ...felt so right to me...
