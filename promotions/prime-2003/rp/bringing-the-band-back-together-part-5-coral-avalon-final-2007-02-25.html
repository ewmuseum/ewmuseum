Bringing The Band Back Together (Part 5, Coral Avalon) (Final)

25 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 25th, 2007 Coral Avalon. Since his debut in Action! Wrestling back in 2002, he had been thought of as an incredible and ever-evolving talent. His willingness and ability to literally reshape his wrestling style on a whim had given rise to the notion that Coral was not an ordinary wrestler. He started his career as "the Kleptomaniac", one who took the finishers of other, more well-known stars and made them his own. He was severely bad with submission wrestling and relied primarilly on high-flying and adapting to his opponent's wrestling styles simply by enduring them over the course of a match. By the end of 2005, Coral had shed his Kleptomaniac roots and had become one of the best submission-oriented wrestlers in North America. Coral was known for being a name brought up as one of the "Best Wrestlers To Have Never Won A World Championship", even if he had come close on many occasions. Coral had not been seen on American television in a year, now. He suffered injuries during his last run in ACW that required time off to heal. Many of those injuries came from a brutal match known to some as "Gimmick Hell", the brainchild of Lowell Dot Com that happened to get all three wrestlers involved injured. Good job, Lowell. You fucking douchebag. After healing from his injuries, Coral chose to return to Japan, where he was also a big star, and continue wrestling there. Part of his motivation for returning to Japan was because his girlfriend of two and a half years, Annabelle Summers, happened to love the country. The couple currently took up temporary residence in a Tokyo house that belonged to Coral Avalon's close friend, Marcus Dox, known to most people as "Max Danger". Max bought the home so that he had some place to go whenever he decided he wanted to be in Japan, which happened to be frequent. Currently, however, Max was nowhere to be seen. Probably because that Sunkist-addicted bastard was probably plowing his hot wife in his gigantic mansion in Maine. ... Not gonna get mad. Not gonna lose my temper. The living room of the Dox home, for simplicity's sake, had just a sofa in it at the moment. Why there wasn't anything else could be attributed to the laziness of the person trying to describe it. But that's a shoot comment, brother. Coral was sitting on the sofa, fiddling with a Nintendo DS (the older model, Coral isn't that cool yet, kids). His long black hair essentially a tangled mess from a match he had wrestled earlier in the day, Coral was dressed in a simple white tanktop with a long-sleeved plaid shirt worn over it (but unbuttoned), and a pair of jeans. He was frowning because he was getting his ass handed to him in some Japanese game that Annabelle insisted he try out. It's the whole "not being able to read the language" thing. It's really frustrating for him. Yet, he didn't want Annabelle to look over his shoulder and help him because he figured that would be cheating. Speaking of Annabelle, she was in the kitchen. In the interest of saving oneself time and energy, we will describe the kitchen as looking like a kitchen. Shock and awe, I know. If you really want to know what a kitchen looked like, then here's what you do. Get out of your chair, walk over to your kitchen, and stare at it for an extended period of time. About as long as it would take me to find your house and kick you in the babymaker. That's what a kitchen is. Only, you know, without the kick in the babymaker. Annabelle's long black hair had been tied into a pair of long pigtails on both sides of her head. She wore a simple blue T-shirt and a short brown skirt that reached her lower thighs. Her feet were bare, because Annabelle was one of those people who almost never wore shoes when they were indoors (or inside a wrestling arena). Annabelle's story started to coincide with Coral's almost three years ago when they met by chance at a restaurant in Cleveland. Annabelle, who played violin in the restaurant, was both a captivating beauty and a skilled violin player, and caught Coral's eye enough for him to chat with her. As it turned out, though, Annabelle slowly developed a crush on Coral when he kept coming back, and admitted her feelings even at a time when Coral was already dating a blonde, busty referee named Patricia Grayson. However, when Coral also developed feelings, he foolishly mentioned loving both girls to Grayson, causing Trish to storm out of his life and leaving him with Annabelle. Fortunately, the two of them were a perfect match for one another, and they've been travelling and living together ever since. Fortunately for Coral, Annabelle didn't mind Coral's near-nomadic lifestyle, given that he hasn't technically had a permanent residence to call his own since he turned eighteen. Now you know the life and times of Annabelle Summers. Store it in the back of your mind, because I don't think I'll be typing that spiel again. Jerks. Apart from Annabelle's excellent abilities with a violin, she happened to also be good at cooking, which was why she really was the type of person who spent a lot of time in kitchen cooking food for her significant other. Sometimes, female empowerment just lies quiet in some girls, which is probably fine for Coral since it happened to have empowered his previous two girlfriends by more than he could've generally handled in the first place. Anyway, Annabelle was busy making with the food, when she heard a knock on the door. This surprised her a bit, and she soon poked her head out of the kitchen, to see that Coral was still fiddling with his Nintendo DS, but was attempting to get up. She decided to ask him, "Coral, did... did you decide to have guests and not tell me?" Coral closed the DS, letting the game of "Jump Ultimate Stars" that had been kicking his ass pass him by for a brief moment, and looked over to Annabelle, "I was going to ask you the same thing, actually." Annabelle stepped out of the kitchen, and being closer to the front door, went and opened it. When she did so, she was immediately grabbed and hugged by an unknown, but kinda strong black-haired woman. Annabelle was very confused by this, and so was her hugger when said person realized that the person she was hugging was, indeed, quite unfamiliar. Without letting go, the girl asked, "You're totally not Coral, are you?" "Not..." Annabelle said, though she really rather liked oxygen and wished this woman would give it to her, "Not since I last checked..." The girl let go and got a better look at Annabelle, before she scratched her head and gave a goofy grin that honestly mirrored the sort of grin her brother would have made, "Ahhh, sorry, sorry. I get carried away. I'm Alexandria." Coral had not recognized the young girl with the shoulder-length black hair who wore a blue turtleneck sweater, black pants, and sneakers until she mentioned her name. At that point, it clicked in his head. "Alexandria Malone?" Coral asked, dumbfounded. He got this inquisitive glance from Annabelle at first, before Coral said this next statement, "I hardly recognized you. What with you being, y'know, not fourteen." Somewhere on the inside, Annabelle breathed a sigh of relief. She's the rational type. A great rarity in the female specimen, you see. "Ahaha," Alexandria laughed, with a hand behind her head, "Allen said the same thing. Apparently, everyone's all in shock that I grew up and stuff." You know that feeling in your gut that you get when you remember something unpleasant? It's kind of a twisty feeling, like your intestines get tied up into knots. You just want to pound on your stomach with your fists until you look like Tarzan, really. Once it got through your intestines, it would creep up your spine in that chilling sort of way until it reached your brain, at which point, alarms would go off and tiny little versions of yourself would be screaming inside. If you must know, they'd be screaming, "SHE CANNOT TAKE THE PRESSURE, CAPTAIN! WE GOTTA ABANDON SHIP!" Coral had to ask, "Allen?" As if on cue, as if he was waiting until Coral actually said his name, Allen Brown (henceforth known in this little tale as "the Codemaster") stepped in front of the door. He stood there wearing a black leather jacket, a white T-shirt with the Triforce symbol on it, a pair of jeans, and a pair of sneakers. By the way, if you think I should actually start naming name brands to describe a person's footwear... kill yourself. The Codemaster, with his hands in his pockets, had only one thing to say, "Yo." Annabelle recognized the Codemaster in that "oh, hey, I kinda know you" sort of way, as Coral walked over to join his girlfriend in talking to the Codemaster and Alexandria Malone. "What're you two doing here?" Coral asked of the two. "Well, actually, Locke, I was plannin' on coming here alone, but the princess insisted she come," the Codemaster said, as he gestured to Alexandria, "Apparently, she couldn't resist an overseas trip to visit old friends." In the corner of his eye, the Codemaster could see Alexandria just sticking her tongue out at him. He elected to ignore his childish partner, however. Coral crossed his arms and stared straight down the barrel of the Power Blaster, however, "One does not simply come to Japan on a whim to see old friends, Allen. They usually come with an intention in mind." The Codemaster chuckled, "Ya got me. I got an offer for you." "Last time you gave me an offer, it was mainly to get me to help you stick it to a promotion that was practically on its last legs." Coral said, "So, what? Are you saying there's another promotion you want to stick it to and you need my help or something?" "Nothing like that." the Codemaster said. He reached into the inside pocket of his jacket and pulled out a folded piece of paper. He handed it to Coral, who unfolded it and looked it. Meanwhile, Annabelle stood right next to him and tilted her head to get a better vantage point. Coral blinked. "PRIME?" he asked, "I've heard that name before. It's been a while since I stayed in the States long enough to keep up with the scene over there, though." The Codemaster nodded, "Yeah. I hoodwinked this guy named Larry Butz into giving a contract to me, and then I kinda... sorta... might have made about seven copies to give to our old running crew." "Isn't that illegal? Like, forgery and stuff?" Coral Avalon asked, genuinely concerned that the Codemaster's trickery and deceit might have had a fatal flaw. "On the contrary, I spoke to some of the people in charge. They'd be interested in having us, but it's gotta be the full unit." the Codemaster said, before he pointed a finger at Coral Avalon, "And it ain't a full party if you ain't in it, Locke." The man formerly known as the Kleptomaniac thought about the words before he realized something, "Wait. You implied there were eight contracts, but I only remember there being *seven* Rogues. Does that mean..." "I'm the eighth." Alexandria said, flatly, interrupting Coral's train of thought. "Oh, you've got to be kidding me..." Coral said, with a shake of his head, before he looked back at the two of them, "Whose bright idea was that?" "Mine." Alexandria said, with a cross of her arms and a glare at Coral. Coral, feeling the absolute ice of Alexandria's glare, decided that the subject needed to be changed. In a hurry. "So, let me get this straight. You're coming here to essentially offer me a job in the States at PRIME, putting me once again in a team that is essentially against everything that relates to my principles, and you're doing all of this with Alexandria in tow?" Coral asked, with all of his usual calm intact. The Codemaster put his hand on his chin and really, really thought about it. For maybe all of about two seconds. "Yup." the Codemaster said. "I think you might have lost your mind, Allen." Coral said. He handed the contract to Annabelle, who merely took it so she could give it a better look herself. The Codemaster, meanwhile, merely shrugged his shoulders. "Maybe. I did once think that I could beat the end of Parasite Eve by running directly at the thing that would instantly kill you if you touched it." the Codemaster said. This got weird stares from everyone else in the room, including Alexandria, promptly the Codemaster to add, "What? Don't tell me none of you ever tried it at least once." "None of us live in your crazy fantasy land where we all play your video games, Allen." Coral Avalon said. Annabelle glanced over at the table Coral had sat against earlier and saw something that bugged her, "Coral, you should just turn the power off on the DS if you're not going to use it." This, also, got stares from everyone, this time directly at Annabelle. As Annabelle was far less comfortable with herself than the Codemaster was of himself, it prompted her to simply hide behind Coral even if he was also staring at her. Finally, after that little awkward moment, Coral turned back to the Codemaster, "Let me just confer with my associate, and by associate, I mean girlfriend, for a second here." He grabbed Annabelle by the hand and pulled her aside to the entrance of the kitchen, and whispered to her, "What do you think?" "Is... is there a problem with going with him?" Annabelle asked, her hands held close to her heart, "Why don't you just say yes?" "I dunno, the constant travel did kinda strain Joey's marriage a bit. I'm worried about what it could do to you." Coral said, with a shrug. "The travel doesn't bother me. I grew up like that." Annabelle said, before she smiled nicely, "You should do what you want. I'll follow close behind, as usual." "Are you sure? I mean, we'd probably uproot right away since I have nothing keeping me here at the moment." Coral asked. "Yes." she responded. They look like they're about to kiss, but the Codemaster pretends to clear his throat to end the lovey-dovey stuff at about this point, and force Coral to snap his attention towards the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom, "We're in." The Codemaster smiled the smile that ended the universe. Or not. But he did smile. His plan could go into motion. "Just give us a bit to clean up and pack our things. We'll probably leave right after we do that." Coral added, a few moments later. His plan could go into motion *IN AN HOUR*.
