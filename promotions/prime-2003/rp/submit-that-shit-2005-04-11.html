Date: 4/11/2005 Sometime in March of 2003… Tony Rolo sits across the desk from his former boss during his tenure in $oldout Wrestling, Sebastian Starr. “Tony, I’m starting a new promotion,” Starr says confidently. He continues, “And I’d like to offer you a contract.” The Specialist leans back in his chair, crosses his legs, and rubs the side of his face, scratching the stubble of his five-o’clock shadow. He’s obviously mulling this idea. “A contract, huh.” “A good contract,” Starr fires back with. “I have a partner this time around. Actually, two partners. A father and a son… from Japan.” A puzzled Rolo blurts out, “Japs? You better watch out, I hear they’re still upset about Hiroshima.” Defending his newfound partners, Sebastian counters Rolo, “The father has made quite a fortune for himself in business… now they’d like to make wrestling their next venture. Apparently a successful PCW tour of Japan sparked their interest in the industry.” Rolo inquires with interest, “So who else have you recruited so far?” “You’re the first, Tony. I’m not going to lie… a new promotion run by Sebastian Starr isn’t going to attract the biggest names right from the get go. While I’m respected, I do not have the name recognition of ever being at the head of a PCW, fWo, or an OSW. But I have good financial backing now where we can attract big names as long as we have good competition and can make a name for ourselves. As long as a good product can be produced from the beginning, I see PRIME being extremely successful.” Looking disappointed, the past IcWa superstar mutters, “PRIME? That’s what you’re calling it?” “Yes, Tony… PRIME, number one by definition. That’s our slogan. Kinda catchy.” Starr seems impressed enough with himself. Rolo shrugs his shoulders, “It’s alright, I guess. I’ve heard worse.” Before continuing, Rolo pauses thinking to himself… “So what are you trying to do with this fed? What are you trying to accomplish?” “My goal?” Sebs retorts. “It’s right there in the slogan. To be number one.” Quickly Rolo comes back with, “And you’re coming to me first. I’m the guy that’s going to bring you there? I’m the man for the job?” “Don’t you think you’re the man for the job, Tone?” That was all Tony Rolo had to hear from Sebastian Starr. This was a challenge for him now. Starr respected Rolo based on their working relationship in $oldout Wrestling and from Rolo’s reputation garnered through IcWa. He knew this was a man on the level of PCW and fWo competition… a man that would eat, drink, and sleep PRIME… one that would bleed for the company with pride. This was his man for the job. Sebastian Starr felt he could put PRIME on Tony Rolo’s shoulders in the respect of the talent level, and let him run with it long enough for the company to gain the respect of those around the industry. And Tony Rolo was ready for this… he was ready to pick up the ball and run a marathon.
