Olsig vs Romani

3 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3rd, 2007 Olsig vs. Romani</color> How'd it get to this point? I'm holding a gun to my Wife's head? This isn't me. --- JUNE 27, 2007 "What's on tap tonight? Caviar with the President's cabinet?" Lightly, I tugged on my red and black tie, placing the finishing touches on my usual business attire. It was like this every morning. I would get dress for work, and she would nag me about any and every thing. To say I was sick of it would prove to be a fatal understatement. "Sherri, I can do without your sarcasm tonight. I'm not enjoying the most pleasant days of my life to begin with. This job has me stressed out." I stated with my back turned to her. I could see her rolling her eyes through the mirror's reflection. "Oh really? Well tell me, which job are we speaking of. The Advisors Firm...which actually pays...or the wrestling gig...the one which only serves to stroke your desire for fame and notoriety." "Sherri, I-..." "Oh wait, that'll never come either because you're too busy trying to hide behind your animal farm mask! The damn neighbors don't even know who you are, so how do you expect the 'world' to?" I shifted around to face her with a less than pleased glare present in my emerald eyes. "What I do inside of the ring is not about fame nor is it about notoriety. It's about tradition and honor. It's what I do to release the built up anger that comes in the form of conversations such as this. It's what I do to remember the pleasant years I shared with my father. And thankfully for you, Sherri, it's about the only thing that's kept me from slapping you silly all across this house like your ex-husband did." Pushing herself violently off of the bed from which she resided, I found her unforgiving and quiet familiar finger placed directly between my eyes, jerking with every point of emphasis. "Don't you dare go there, Francois. And where do you get off threatening me? Even if you wanted to slap me silly across this house, you'd be too 'Holy' to do so. What you lack in balls, you more than make for in mouth...because that's the only proper attribute you possess...A MOUTH! One that your ass can't even properly support." "That's fine if you feel that way, Sherri. As a matter of fact, I hope those feelings lead you right out of that door because for too long I've turned the cheek to your premature bickering. From here on out, that's out the window. You want to challenge my authority, well be prepared to face the consequences that come along with it." "Oh what are you going to do? Read the Bible to me?" "Sadly, demons are immune to the Psalms, so that wouldn't do any good." A look that expressed sorrow mixed in with regret formed across her face. It's effect was minimum. Sherri had no heart and therefore no emotions. "...that was uncalled for Francois." "What you are doing to me is uncalled for, Sherri. I work two jobs in order to satisfy your desire to sit at home all day and be waited on hand and foot. The least I could get is an offer to iron my shirt once in a while. But no, instead I'm fed this bullshit. You're right...alot of things around here are uncalled for." In silence, she took a seat at the edge of the bed once more. I could feel my words ruffling throughout her mind, seeking that one strand of care that maybe...just maybe...survived the three years of our spiteful marriage. "Can I...iron your shirt?" I looked at her disgusted. She had to be joking. "Yes Sherri, you can iron out the wrinkles It's formed between tying my tie and lacing my shoes." "Now you see why I never offer to help." "Be real, Sherri." She rolled her eyes before pushing herself to a vertical base once more, presenting me with that 'whatever' look that I'd become all too accustomed to. "I'm off to the Firm." "That's funny, because I could've sworn the Firm didn't begin operations until at least two hours from now." "Yes, but you're forgetting one thing, Sherri. It's my firm. I can open my firm whenever I want to." "You said something earlier about being real?" That smirk of arrogance crossed her face. She knew something. "I'm glad to see your hearing hasn't been affected as much by your constant nagging as mine has." "Let's be real, then, Francois. You're not going to the Firm, are you?" "No, I'm going to lounge around at Mom and Pops." "If you could stop being hilarious long enough, I'd like to ask you...honestly...where you are going." A deep sigh escaped me as I placed my hands in my pocket. I always did that when I found myself on the verge of telling a lie. "I'm visiting Daniel Pritchor." "...concerning?" "Business." "That 'business' wouldn't happen to have anything to do with his family's investment in the Firm would it?" I flashed her a glance that properly displayed my surprise in knowing that she knew something that, quiet frankly, she had no business knowing. "...Who told you about that?" "Francois, I'm your wife. If it involves you, then by rule of thumb it involves me." "Well take the rule of my middle finger as telling you to stay out of this." "Francois, The Pritchor's are seeking to invest millions of dollars into the firm. MILLIONS. Have you become so holy that you can't see a grand opportunity when it basically slaps you in the face." With my face rumpled in a hasty expression, I took a few steps towards her so that my words could be heard very clearly. "Sherri, you know as well as I do that the Pritchors deal with nothing but blood money. From Grandfather Pritchor all the way down to his grandchildern's grandchildren. They're illegal dealings span decades. My Uncle wouldn't accept their blood money and neither will I." "And what are you basing this off of? Speculation? Because from what I understand, the Pritchors' cash flow is all too legal and comes in the form of expensive and luxurious suits. Daniel even sent you one for your birthday." "I'd really hope that you weren't as naive as you're making yourself seem, Sherri. It's called a cover up." "Maybe so. But going to his home, THREATENING him, and provoking him to back out of this deal is not only a felony, but also grounds for termination from your firm. Your uncle put emphasis on that upon his death. If you want him to back off, you do it legally. Take it up with the advisors at the Firm. Otherwise, you're a criminal...and with as many Judges as Daniel has in his pocket, prison wouldn't seem at all farfetched." Suddenly, my expression shifted from disgruntled to concerned. "If I didn't know any better, I'd say you were codoning this. Besides...who said I was going to threaten him or provoke him to back off of his deal? As you stated, he sent me a very expensive suit for my birthday. I'd say that justifies us as friends. And friends do chat, don't they?" Her answer came in the form of silence. Yet, in the midst of that silence, she found time to reach underneath the mattress for something that I couldn't quite identify. As a matter of fact, at the time I didn't care to identify it. "With your current amount of friends, I wouldn't expect you to answer that." Uncharacteristically, she slowly swayed over to me, tugging on the wings of my coat as if she was attempting to pull me in for a kiss. I knew better and apparently she did as well. The kiss never happened. "All I'm saying is...be careful." "Is that concern? I think it's about time for me to leave." As I turned to walk away, I felt her pat me on the shoulder as if she was waving me off. "I'll...have dinner ready when you get back." I turned around...somewhat amused. "Sherri, do you even know how to turn on the oven." "The ashes from your thousand-dollar loafers should answer that question." "Goodbye Sherri." And with that, I exited... Leaving a smiling devil behind. --- JUNE 28, 2007 This place was a mess. Ever since my Uncle passed, leaving the firm in my control, the place has been on a fast decline. Shareholders were dropping all over the place, resignment notices were stockpiled on my desk. It was a mess, simply put. But somewhere in it all, I maintained hope. Hope that one day it'd all pay off. Every company had it's slumps, right? That's all this was. And even if it failed, I still had PRIME. "Sir...the board has requested an immediate meeting of urgent proportions." stated my assistant, Nancy, who was quite the attractive one if I may say so myself. "Must be about our recent acquisition of the O'Reilly Center. Tell them I'll be right out." "Certainly, Sir." The door gently closed as I took a deep breath, adjusted my tie, and began to make my way over to the conference room for yet another meeting in what seemed to be a dozen of them. Taking a seat at the head of the round table, I presented my advisors with a smile before initiating the start of the confrontation. "Alright, fellas. Let's get started." "Certainly, Mr. Francois." stated a client by the name of Wayne Crumpler. Jumping ahead of myself, I hopped on the O'Reilly Center issue. "Now, what I was thinking was that we could use the O'Reilly Center as a sub-agency of sorts. You know...for our up and coming advisors. With the support of the g-..." "Uhm...Sir. I hate to interrupt you but, that is not what this meeting is about." "I'm sorry...what is it then?" "It's about the Daniel Pritchor offer." Simultaneously, I rolled my eyes and took a deep sigh. This was the last thing I wanted to hear about. The very last. "You've got be kidding me. I thought I nipped this thing in the bud!?" "Yes, sir, but we're afraid it's turned a bit more...drastic...for lack of a better term, of course." "Forget it. I'm dismissing this entire ordeal immediately." "Sir...I'm also afraid that you do not have that authority." "Excuse me?" Suddenly, a woman by the name of Sherrell Duncan inserted herself into the equation. "If I may, sir. With the recent increase in your travel schedule with the wrestling company you work for, we find that the combination of stress, fatal blows to the skull, and inconvenience have all played a factor in your making of some very....questionable...decisions. The fact that a man of Daniel Pritchor's stature has requested involvement in our firm and offered to make millions seems almost too good to be true. It was, however, very true. But you ruined it because of personal issues with Pritchor. Those same personal issues cost all of us some much needed cash flow. It's not secret that this firm hasn't been quite the success since the passing of your Uncle." "And you think that Daniel Pritchor's investment would improve upon that?" "Considering the amount of money he's offering...yes, yes I do. We all do." "That's all well and good, but the answer is still no." From here, Crumpler took over the conversation once again. "We figured that'd be your answer...which is why we all exercised the majority rule clause and-..." "Voted me out." "Well..." "No need to answer, because you all failed to read that clause properly. The clause calls for grounds of majority rule, which you don't have. You're smart enough to know that my simple refusal to do business with a client I deem unfit is not considered 'grounds' for anything." "Yes sir, we know that...but there's another cause you may need to consider." Suddenly, Crumpler place a very small, miniature recorder on top of the table, pressing a key that caused it to begin...and the contents, for me atleast, were not pretty. Daniel. Francois? What are you do-... I don't have the time nor the patience for petty talk, Daniel. Well, sir....you ARE in MY house. If it isn't a problem with you, may I ask why? Keep...your money...out of my firm. Excuse me. Don't act like you don't know what I'm talking about. I assumed your firm was quite interested in doing business with me. They might've been...but they don't know that all of your earnings have come as a result of smuggling, killing, and cheating the government. That's a daring thing to say, Francois....especially without any form of evidence. I don't need evidence. I know it's true, you know it's true, and because we both know it's true...you're going to keep your money out of my firm. Am I right? I hate to break your heart, but I-... *click* Am...I...right? Didn't know you were going to pull a gun out on me. But I'm hoping you can guess what I'm going to do with it if I'm not satisfied with your answer. Fine. If you want to miss out on my millions, that's fine by me. You're only hurting yourself. That's where you're wrong. I'm helping myself AND my firm from being ripped off by the likes of you. I've backed down...you may leave now. Gladly...it stinks in here. The recording ended. Silence. "Where'd you get that." I demanded "A...reliable source." "Sherri..." I mumbled under my breath. With fury in my eyes, i ferociously pushed myself away from the table, grabbed my suitcase, and stormed out of the firm. "Sir? Sir! Wait!" But I wasn't stopping. She was about to pay for what she'd done to me. --- JUNE 28, 2007 I burst into the home... "Oh, hey honey...I didn't expect to see you so s-..." "SHUT UP! How DARE you go behind my back and pull a stunt like this?" "Francois, what the hell are you talking about?" "YOU KNOW DAMN WELL WHAT I'M TALKING ABOUT?" A confused glare crossed her face. She was a brilliant actor. "The recording, Sherri. I'm not going to even ask how you got it on me...I figured that out on the way home when recollecting how you tugged my coat for the first time in years. I simply want to know why." "Look around us, Francois. What do we have? NOTHING! Why is that? Because you're jeopardizing your job at the firm for this stupid wrestling gig! And now the perfect opportunity for us to gain an increase in our finances comes along and you decide not to bite? It's my duty as your wife to correct your mistakes and I did just that!" "I did what I did for a reason, Sherri. You should've trusted my judgment instead of going behind my back and presenting my firm with a scandal that could very well reserve my spot in prison." "Sacrifices, Francois. That's what it's all about." "You know what Sherri...you're right. It's all about sacrafices. In fact, I should make on right now." I was a pretty good actor myself. With a planted smile on my face, I headed over to a nearby drawer, snatching it open and yanking out it's content. "Francois, what are you doi-..." I held the gun to her face. "SHUT UP!" "Oh my G-..." "I've had just about enough of you as I'm going to take, Sherri." Suddenly, a conversation that occurred between my agent and I struck me in the midst of my holding the gun in my hand, ready to blow her brains out. Shift "Vangelus Olsig is a killer. He's broken down so many people mentally to the point where they ended up killing themselves. Birds of a feather, Machaiveli. It's only a matter of time before you start acting just like him." Shift "Francois...please...put the gun down." Shift "It's sinner vs saint, and it goes without saying that sinner always wins. Why? Because it's much easier to be a bad guy than it is to be a good guy in these cruel and evil times we live in. You'll bite sooner or later, Machaiveli, and that whole good guy gimmick will be but a faded memory." Shift "Francois, I'm so sorry...please just don't." Shift "There aren't too many genuine good guys left in the world, Machaiveli. I'd hate to see you...one of the last few good ones remaining...corrupted by someone like Vangelus." Shift I clicked the safety lock. Shift "If you want to hold on to any innocence you have left, you'll make up your mind now that any dealings with Vangelus Olsig are to be left as strictly business. It simply cannot extend beyond that. Not if you want to remain a saint, of course." Shift My hand nervously pressed the trigger lightly. Shift "It's Romani vs Olsig." Shift "Please..." Shift "Good vs Evil." Shift "PLEASE!" Shift "Sinner vs Saint" Shift "NOOOO!" Shift "Please...don't let him win." Shift *Crack!* The gun hit the floor. How'd it get to this point? I'm holding a gun to my Wife's head? This isn't me. This is...him. Marinating on that thought, I turned my back towards my weeping wife and slowly dragged myself out of my house. Eerily, I could see his face in the gray clouds. It really was me vs him. ...And I was losing. Shift "Please...don't let him win."
