Team VIAGRA Presents : How Not To Travel In Time [pt1 - ACT 1]

12 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/08[written] 2/02/02[occured] INT. HIGH FLYER’S BASEMENT – EVENING A large metallic object obstructs view through the small window just below street level. It would have been mistaken for the heater, had it not been located directly next to the actual heater. Mary-Lynn stands in the corner of the room, in her attorney’s garb. She chews on the edge of a pen. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: As your attorney, I’ve got to ask you. What the hell is this? High Flyer walks into view, coming from a sub-basement room. He dangles a cigarette from his lips. HIGH FLYER: Time machine. Hey. I actually DID buy Huey Lewis and the News as a kid. You were right. I’ll get you five dollars later. Or do you just want an LP? Like… say… Huey Lewis and the News? EH? It’s pretty freaking awesome if you can trust a ten year old version of me. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: I can’t trust this version of you, how could I trust you at ten. HIGH FLYER: I was much more innocent. Tony Davis bursts from the sub-basement. He holds an Atari system in his hand. TONY DAVIS: GOLD MINE! *thinks* I’ve never played Atari stoned… MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: So, you have a time machine? HIGH FLYER: Of course. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Excuse me for my complete and utter shock of disbelief… but how do YOU have a time machine. HIGH FLYER: I’m quite the business man. You’d learn this about me if you’d have been listening, like, ever. Mary-Lynn shrugs, biting the tip of her pen clean off. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: I’m sorry. You just talk so damn much. High Flyer glares at her, and Mayweather holds up her hands to stop him. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: I speak as your attorney, nothing else! Suddenly, there’s a brisk amount of smoke filling the room. Tony Davis is finally curious about something, and goes to investigate. He sees the bright blue lights flashing, when the door clicks open. INT. HIGH FLYER’S TIME MACHINE – CONTINUOUS High Flyer, Tony Davis, and Mary-Lynn Mayweather all peer into the time machine. EDDIE SCOTT POSER, the supposed king of Poland, lies curled up in a fetal position on the time machine’s floor. He is currently wearing boxers with hearts, a large sparkling robe, and a burger king crown on top of his head. He is also sucking on his thumb. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: So… you’ve got a squatter, eh? Tony Davis walks over and kicks Poser in the stomach. He stirs, shifting his body before falling back asleep. Pow! Tony Davis kicks him again. Poser is knocked backward by the blow, directly into the awkwardly and unfortunately placed trigger switch for the time machine. Quickly, anyone or anything with any part of their body inside of this time machine is sucked inside it’s quarters. The machine restructures itself, giving them the illusion of space. Panels become full computers with HD LCD screens. If Jack Harmen’s basement had all the ancient technology you could imagine, this space held almost all you could imagine throughout space and time. And it was all bought with a single foot of snow from an equally insane individual who ran a porn shop. Rob Kestler, considered a member of Team VIAGRA until his eventual betrayal. How they cursed his name as they fell back through time and space. High Flyer regains his balance and begins to expertly navigate this complicated computer system. It’s almost like Wash and his switches. TONY DAVIS: Where are we going! MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: You’re not serious! [to Poser] Is he serious? EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I’m sorry, what are you people doing in my home? Flyer slams a button down on his console. The computer paneling splits apart, revealing an alcove. Flyer grabs a pistol and trains it directly at the groggy Poser’s skull. HIGH FLYER: Consider this eviction. Flyer turns back to his paneling. Davis scoffs. TONY DAVIS: Dude, gay! You should have at least shot him! HIGH FLYER: Really? We could run through it again. Or I can figure out where in the FUCK we're going. Mary takes a seat on a metallic bench. She holds her head, and begins to rock back and forth. David takes a seat next to her. MARY-LYNN MAYWEATHER: Is… is this like, gas? Are we all drugged right now? TONY DAVIS: Nope. Full on time travel through the space continuum. You know Back to the Future.[she nods] It’s nothing like that. [Flyer glares at Davis] What! I’m trying to help! Flyer walks over, holding a rubber band ball. He places it onto a table that rises up from the floor. HIGH FLYER: Time and space are like a large rubber band ball. Each rubber band is considered an alternate dimension. We live at the very center, so normally, connections to the other planes of existence is almost impossible. The whole universe is based on a loop, and with the right mechanics, the right fuel source, and the right chemical reaction, we can bend and travel through time, but traveling through space. Understand? Mary-Lynn certainly listened to every word the Lunatic spoke, but she understood none of it. Tony Davis, had already decided to hook up an Atari to one of the HD LCD screens. This was considerably more difficult than he had imagined. Flyer smacks Poser in the side of the face. Poser winces. HIGH FLYER: And I’ve told you a hundred times not to live in the time machine! We have a guest room. CRASH! Flyer and Poser are lurched forward. Flyer flies in the air, smacking back first against the side of the time machine’s wall. Poser’s face is shot forward, and it collides with the edge of the table, before he falls down on the ground, motionless. Seatbelts had shot out automatically, securing Tony and Mary-Lynn to their bench. That didn’t stop a skylight from falling onto Tony Davis’ head. Davis blocked it with his forearm as best he could, but it cut him up badly, and still knocked him unconscious. Alone, the door to the time machine opens. A whaft of smoke reveals a jam packed arena. INT. ARENA – SOMETIME IN THE PAST OR FUTURE Mary-Lynn Mayweather steps out from the time machine’s entrance. She is supremely overdressed. A show held in the deep south, the fans of the crowd seem quite rowdy. She scans the arena, and sees a banner for NWA the National Wrestling Alliance. Harley Race appears to be wrestling. She grabs a fallen booklet hyping today’s show… APRIL 23rd, 1980 Mary-Lynn drops her jaw in shock. She races back to the time machine, which has found itself lodged into the stage’s platform. She steps back into this large room that reminded her of the camping scene in the fourth Harry Potter movie for its extra space, and hurls directly into a potted plant located near its entrance. She stands up quickly, wiping the excess vomit from her chin. Her eyes roll back into her head. THUD. … … … … LATER Tony Davis’ eyes shoot open. He quickly jumps to his feet, and then his eyes dart skyward, to his already bandaged head wound. Davis scoffs, swinging his head low to the floor. Lying in his gaze is a small mechanical robot with a large red cross on its back. It quickly and efficiently stitched Eddie Scott Poser’s gash, at a speed much greater than a human. A second medical robot transfers blood through a portable IV into Eddie’s arm. Davis wobbles to his feet, and walks to Flyer’s side. He nudges him awake. TONY DAVIS: Jack. Up and atom Fly-boy, you’re the only one who can get us home. High Flyer shakes his head, trying to stir the cobwebs. He clammers to his feet, having been on his neck upside down in the corner. Cracking his neck, Jack winces. He notices Mary-Lynn, and rushes to her side. HIGH FLYER: Mary-Lynn! She’s okay. She’s okay. He double checks her pulse. That’s when he notices the flyer in his hands. He takes the flyer out of her hand and stares at it for a second, keeping it away from Tony. Flyer’s jaw now drops. TONY DAVIS: What is it. Past? Future? Alternate reality ruled by carrots? I can take it! Tell it to me straight, then let’s blow this theoretical popsicle stand! Tony Davis begins to shiver as Flyer hesitates. HIGH FLYER: I-It’s….I-It… Flyer’s head jerks up. He stares at Tony for a few moments, and Tony shakes his head in protest. HIGH FLYER: We can’t leave yet. TONY DAVIS: No. No way dude. You heard what that old geriatric dude used to say when he would appear out of nowhere! I mean, that guy was in Karate Kid and Gremlins… I’m pretty sure he knew what he was talking about. We shouldn’t fux with the past Fly. We have to go home! Without warning, Flyer clocked Davis in his face with a metal wrench, sending him back down to the cold metallic floor. The first mechanical robot rolled over to his side, and seemingly shot a look of anger at the Lunatic. Flyer searched the room… Mary-Lynn, Poser, and Tony were all knocked unconscious. And so, Flyer quickly exited the room. END OF ACT 1 NARRATOR: Next week, on act 2 of part 1 of Team VIAGRA's How Not To Travel In Time! INT. TIME MACHINE – APRIL 23RD, 1980 Poser and the mechanical medical robot are playing chess. The medical robot’s eyes squint in its form of confusion to Eddie, who looks none-too-happy. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: I swear if I owned a hand gun I’d make you repair yourself. INT. LOCKER ROOM -- LATER High Flyer is currently lacing up attire. He seems to be dressed as an evil mustached frenchman. Tony Davis is there, and Flyer seems to be in the middle of a tirade. HIGH FLYER: You know how many parallel universes are named after me? ELEVEN![holds head in hands] I’ve created my own string theory. [fade out.]
