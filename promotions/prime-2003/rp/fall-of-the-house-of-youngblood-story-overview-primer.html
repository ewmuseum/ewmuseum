Date: updated after every rp Alright, trying something out for the first time. A primer if you will for the story, in case someone wants to skim through older parts or understand some background to the characters involved. The story is happening in media res, and for many a lot of the information is new, so I believe it is important to give something quick and easy to have access to. Will be updating this after every part. CAST OF CHARACTERS Lindsay Youngblood: Narrator of the story, she is the third of the four Youngblood children, and also the youngest sister. Former art student in New York City, she decided on a whim to move back around her oldest brother and attend UCLA. She switched majors, to English. Has multiple piercings, struggles with her emotions, and does not understand herself well mentally. Resembles more of her mother's physical traits. Suffered a bad break up, which might have caused her to shift her life in a new direction. Brandon Youngblood: PRIME wrestler, is the second oldest of the Youngblood children and is the oldest son. Looks like his father and is considered to be on the outs with the entire family save Lindsay. He is the black sheep. He essentially is the father figure of the family financially, paying for Lindsay's schooling as well as for Thomas. He has paid off the loans for the Youngblood estate in Bexar County, Texas. Aurora Andrews: Lindsay's friend. At this point, not much else is known. Candace Youngblood: Oldest of the Youngblood children, is the oldest sister. Considered to be the second in command, since she owns power of attorney over Abigail. Thomas Youngblood: Youngest of the Youngblood children, is the youngest son. Is slipping down a path in which there might not be a coming back from. Benjamin Youngblood: The patriarch of the Youngblood family, has been deceased since August of 2005. Overdosed on pain medication and slipped into a coma. Had a brain tumor, and the only person he informed was Brandon. Tried to make amends with his son before death, and did so. Abigail decided to pull the plug. Abigail Youngblood: The matriarch of the Youngblood family, has had trouble coping with the loss of her husband to the point where she might be considered mentally unfit to live on her own. Staunchly religious to the point of fundamentalism. Unknown what exactly in her condition is an act and what is real.
