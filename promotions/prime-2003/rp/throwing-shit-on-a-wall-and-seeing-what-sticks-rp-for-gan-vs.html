You know, one of those promotional pieces to let them know that they’re in five levels of deep shit when they cross my path. I mean, yes. I have defeated opponents many times over. Yes, I have conquered the odds. Yes, Ted Turner may be in the “wrasslin’” business but I’m in the Sports Entertainment business. Ted, should you happen to come across my memoirs one day, know that you can kiss the finest, most toned part of my ass for thinking you could compete with PRIME… also know that next week when you, me and John Rocker meet for Golf, I’d be more than happy to share my thoughts on how certain types of people shouldn’t be allowed on the golf course. Oi. I’m just going off-base again. Let me reiterate my mantra… life is hectic. In fact, lemme share some of my highlights of the week. Maybe it’ll give you people an inkling as to what my life is like… crazy. Pure crazy! First of all, there was that awful, awful tour in that Asian corner of the world that I think Sun Tzu calls home. -------------------------------------------------------- Sonny Silver, dressed in a sharp-looking business suit and Kenjiro ITO, clad in powder-blue dress shirt and a spiffy brown Armani suit are taking in the surroundings; a bustling bazaar filled with sights that amaze the mind and tease the senses. Many people from all walks of life (to be honest, loads of Asians with the sparse man/woman/child of the white/black/Hispanic/whatever kind of person other than Asian) go about their day. Some shopping, others heading home. Some taking in the sights, some probably looking for some ginseng herbs because his dick doesn’t work. Don’t let anybody tell you that Chinese herbs don’t work. Last time I checked, there WERE two billion Asians or something. Silver and ITO exchange glances before Sonny finally opens up. Silver: Well… you must feel right at home. ITO: How many times are you going to say that? Silver: As many as I fucking want, that’s how. We came here to do a promotional piece for my match at The Great American Nightmare and by God, that’s what I’m gonna do just as soon as we finish this tour. It’s busy all the way on this side of the world and people here have a different way to go about things. While we in America treat the great custom of wrestling as a big flash and flare show full of dick-measuring contests, people here look upon it as one of the greatest and gratifying pastimes they’ve ever seen. Contests about pride, honor, tradition, and all that other babyfacey shit. …Plus, scientific fact: Asians – you aside, ITO, at least you claim -- have small penises, hence why they don’t have dick-measuring contests here. It’d be like whipping out a penny to see whose penny is worth more. The Bad Will Ambassador shot Sonny a slightly annoyed look. ITO: You're married, what would you know about your own penis anymore? Silver: ITO, you get one of those per trip. That was it. ITO: Pssh, whatever… besides, it’s not like we’re actually IN the Orient… this is Chinatown. You dragged us out to Seattle, remem… He finds his mouth quickly being covered by Sonny as he nervously looks into the camera. Silver: Shut up! This is close enough! You think I’d actually GO to Japan? Last I heard, they were trapped on an island doomed to sink into the ocean while fighting earthquakes, SARS, and gigantic moth creatures. Taking a moment to recollect himself, he takes his hand off the annoyed ITO’s mouth and brushes his suit off. ITO: All China had to do was agree to the Greater East Asian Co-Prosperity Sphere. But nooo, they had to fight us, and then get all pissed when we went in there and raped them. Literally. That reminds me of Sun Tzu. I'd really love to rape her Nanjing... Silver: Yes, she really is quite delightful. ITO: I concur. Silver: Now… to the arena! The two assholes walk a couple hundred feet before coming to a dead stop at some random restaurant, Wong’s. A quizzical look crosses the face of ITO as he turns to Sonny. ITO: We’re performing here? Where’s the parking at? Where’s my limo? What the shit? Silver: Well, here’s the thing… By perform, I mean “we’re gonna go in here and kick the shit out of some Chinese people.” I figure it’s okay because we’re filming a promo, nobody’s gonna stop us. ITO: I agree with that logic. Let us beat some Crouching Tiger ass. The two march brazenly into the restaurant before the scene fades out. --------------------------------------------------------------------- Man, that was one of the higher points of the week… I nearly drowned somebody in their bowl of soup and people were none the wiser. I mean, since this is an RP, I can’t legally be held responsible for my actions since people think I’m merely filming a show. I love nerd logic! But to my point… It’s hard out there for a pimp like me. Well, it is until I go home every couple days to spend some time with the old ball and chain. A REALLY hot ball and chain while I’m at it. Then I just think all those other pimps need to quit their bitching. But when I can’t come home because I’ve got loads of work to do, I ALWAYS do my daily phone call to make sure that everything’s going great. Eme and Gabrielle are both very dear to me. Gabrielle because she’s the product of two really hot people and Eme because she gives good head… oh, and love, marriage and all that other BS. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Now, the scene fades to Sonny sitting on a United Airlines flight heading to the show for the Great American Nightmare. The weather’s absolutely perfect outside and the newly risen sun is just starting to shine miles beneath them. Sonny’s got himself some half-eaten blueberry muffins on the tray in front of him and a bottle of Apple Juice nearly finished. While in level flight, he runs his Debit card across the phone in the plane so he may use it. It rings several times until a noise is heard on the other end, that of his wife, Eme. Eme: Hello? A smile finds its way across the face of the PRIME star when he finally hears the voice of his beloved. Silver: Hey, baby, how’s it going? Eme: Good! Me and Gabrielle just back from a trip through the park with Moana. Moana was his sister-in-law, her sister for those who keep score of such things. Eme: You’re on your way to Pittsburgh, right? Silver: Yup. I got about three hours or so before we land. Myself and the other guy you don’t like – ITO – we… uh… had to stop over in Seattle on some business. Lots of stuff. We performed at a show. Eme: Oh? How’d that go? Lot of screaming and crazy combat waged between men in tights and the prototypical run-in against unsuspecting victims? Grinning sheepishly, he tugged at the collar of his shirt. Silver: …Yeah, something like that. I figured I’d call and check in for a couple minutes before I have to get to work on some papers. Eme: Papers? Silver: Yeah, paperwork. I had to talk with my agent recently and it seems to be that time of year where I have to renew my health insurance and discuss what’s being covered, how much money I’m worth if I’m dead, blah blah blah killmenow, you know. Eme: Ah, yes. The fWo had you do that, too, right? Silver paused to clear his throat, then continued. Silver: Unfortunately. When I land, I immediately gotta find a gym, then get a tan, then head to the arena so we can go film one of those mandatory “I’m gonna beat your ass” segments. Well, speaking of, I gotta let you go for a little bit, but I’ll call you back when I’m done filling this crap out, all right? Eme: Sure. Have a good flight and PLEASE – if you love me – refrain from making an ass out of yourself on the plane like the last time when you yelled at the flight attendant for giving you flat soda. Silver: Pssh. The bitch asked for it… A strident sigh could be heard on the other end of Silver’s phone. Silver: I mean, love you! Eme: I love you, too. Silver: Bye. He hung up the phone, then stood up for a moment to procure his insurance papers from his bag. After sitting back down, he took notice of a beautiful young blonde lady with a set of eyes as blue as the sky right outside. The gears began to turn in the head of the would-be leader of PRIME as he gathered up the courage to speak to her. He cleared his throat and smiled at the young lady. Silver: Hey. Woman: Hello. She went back to reading her book for a moment before Sonny finally gathered up all his strength to say… Silver: Miss, I’m a wrestler. Would you like to be part of an angle where I make sex with you multiple times on TV and you have my kid while becoming part of a complicated love triangle involving a lesbian? --------------------------------------------------------------------- She said no, by the way… and slapped me. And said I was crazy. Bitch. She could’ve bypassed my PRIME Diva Search idea right off the bat and everything. Ah, well, what’s an owner to do? Look at that, everybody. I go, stick to my work, make appearances wherever I deem worthy, I make a little time for my family and do it all again everyday. Later on that day when we landed, I immediately met with my agent, gave him the paperwork, and failed to pay attention to a good three-quarters of whatever the Hell he was saying. Life this, you’re worth this much, God my life is boring, dah dah dah. Then the gym. That’s a given. No way do I touch that steroid shit. I learned my lesson with that long ago. Then to the arena. I walked in, treated my lowly subjects with a nod if I felt they deserved it and I totally blew them off the next. That part’s always fun. It’ll be showtime in a few hours, so I gotta quit talking about all this and go film a promo. So I’ll leave my opponents with this for now… ---------------------------------------------------------------------------Now, the scene cut to a generic PRIME backstage set-up with the logo high above the backdrop. Standing in front of said backdrop is none other than Sonny with a microphone in his hands. Silver: Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls… my opponents are all gay. They engage in all-male orgies backstage to keep themselves fit for combat and I won’t have any of that shit. Guys, prepare to die. The end.
