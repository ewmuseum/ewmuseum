Advocating.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today No-rope barbed wire deathmatches in Tokyo. Street fights in Glasgow. Hardcore matches in New York City. Justin Williamson had endured them all. But now, Los Diablos had forced his hand. The man once known for wrestling’s highest pain tolerance and innovative legal maiming methods had sworn off all hardcore competition. The Mexicans cared nothing for such lofty ideals, declaring their title match with Manifest Destiny to take place in a Mexican prison, a filthy, disgusting hovel. The losers would be locked into cells, like common criminals. Williamson figured that Deville had seen the inside of a cell or two in his time. As for himself, however…there was no desire to get acquainted with Moctezuma’s Revenge in a dingy stone building.. ------------------------- “Damn them,” spat the Deville’s Disciple, standing outside of literal Hell on Earth. In the middle of a desert sat the prison that Los Diablos had selected for the contest. Whatever insulting things he could say about the team, they knew how to pick terrible places for battle. “I don’t believe this. I’m not a petty hardcore thug anymore, I’m a technican. A grappler. I’m above fighting in a prison like a rapist or thief…” Even as the words came out, however, experienced eyes surveyed the building. The fence surrounding the facility was laced and strung with barbed wire and its deadlier cousin, razor wire. The building itself was made out of solid rock, rock that would not give when the human skull was bashed against it… He sighed. Years of thinking like the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid were not so easily erased. Despite the sand-covered fine Italian shoes he wore, and the suit that adorned his body even in the oppressive heat, the Trapezoid remained. It wasn’t like he could just forget everything he knew. The knowledge of how to be the Trapezoid remained, and it always would. He had innovated things few other humans could even remotely imagine; things that were best sympathized with by serial killers. “What choice do I really have, though,” he asked himself, head shaking. “I’m going to be fighting in a prison. It’s not like an arm drag or a front facelock is going to help me when two dirty Mexicans will be coming at me with every weapon in the book.” Williamson stared at the damned prison, wincing at every single observation he made. Memories flowed back to him of things he had done to others and had done to him with every single object he spotted. One step closer he moved to the facility. Really, the prison was no different than any of the other ridiculous structures he had fought in. Barbed wire cages, malls, and all manner of other demented things. But on all of those occasions he had eagerly signed his name on the dotted line, most likely as the man proposing the wonder of stupidity that was being agreed to. Now, he had no choice. It was accept the match or be fired, be stripped of the championship belt that he loved so. Los Diablos had taken advantage of him. They had made him do something that he had less than zero desire to do. They wanted to fight like animals, such was their hatred for the Deville. To listen to their rhetoric, Deville was the main objective. Deville was the target. Justin Williamson was just an afterthought. Just like he had been to Karina Wolfenden and Aimz a few weeks ago. “Motherfucker,” he whispered, to himself, kicking some sand towards the prison. Really, his transformation from Archbishop of Angles to Deville’s Disciple hadn’t earned him the respect he’d sought. Karina overlooked him. Amy…a fucking crack whore, of all things, overlooked him. Los Diablos did the same. The old fuel was coming back. Anger had always fueled the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid when he was at his best. It inspired him to graphic violence and horrific results. “No respect. I may as well still be a giant joke. How the hell did this happen? The only thing I’ve got going for me now is a mobster who’ll turn on me at a moment’s notice and with no reason, and he’s my TAG PARTNER!” Rage-filled words echoed through the desert sands. The transformation was beginning.Justin Williamson was a technical masterpiece. The Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid was an innovative spot machine to end all spot machines. The two were separate, but did they have to be? Obviously, ‘no’ was the answer. Especially considering the fact that Los Diablos would do whatever it took to win. In the wrestling ring, Williamson could be as technical as he wanted. But in a prison, waistlocks were setups to anal rape. This was his chance to defend himself, to make a statement. For Justin Williamson to exist and thrive, the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid had to come out within him, if only for one night. In a Mexican prison, rules of engagement didn’t apply. There was no gentile combat. One fought for survival, to subsist, to remain intact and whole. Justin Williamson’s hopes and dreams, just as those of Los Diablos, would soon be locked inside a jail. Only one hope would be freed. The other would be left to rot.
