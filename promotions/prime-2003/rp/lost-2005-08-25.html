Lost

25 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-25-05 The seething black blood of pain and torment fluctuated through his veins. Darkness, frustration and hate; all thoughts and emotions churned within the thick skull of his head like butter preventing him from accessing the awkward memories of the night before. Black tears of blood slowly trickled down his swollen cheeks as he lay amidst in darkness. The thick and humid air caused sweat to seep through the thickness of his pores sending him into distress. The pace of his heart sped and his breathing became uncontrollable. His fingers began to tremble on the bed in which he lay and something wrapped around his neck made it hard to swallow. Panic gripped his heart like a vice as fear lingered throughout his motionless body. What was there to be afraid of? His mouth became parch like a desert in Arizona. He felt like the walls had begun to close in on him; each illusion caused him to breathe heavier. He couldn’t take the pressure. Like salvation, a blast of radiant light crept into the sky. The birds began to chirp happily as the sun shone brilliantly illuminating the darkened room with light. Its rays pierced his eyes blinding him briefly. Adjusting to the light he slowly opened, then closed, and reopened them once again. The last few droplets of tears fell from his long black eye lashes as he blinked in haste. His breathing had now returned to normal as well as the pace of his heart. Pain had finally reached his arms which caused him to look over his shoulder; here he noticed them handcuffed to a bedpost. Confusion finally arose. “What the…?” He lifted his head slightly and scanned the bottom of the bed where his legs had been cuffed apart as well. He felt like his savior Jesus tied to a cross only without the nails in his hand. A bright pink dog collar which matched the boxer briefs over the torso of his half exposed body struck his attention. “This can’t be happening.” His face amended beetroot and his heart began to thump once again this time only harder as if it were trying to break free. “Hello?” He cried breaking the silence of the night. “Is anyone here, there or anywhere?” Nothing. Chris Collins’ limp body became drenched in silence once again. “I have to get out of here,” he said to himself. “How?” His eyes widened and like surveillance light began to scan the room for any possible object of escape. The beaming rays of the suns continued to reflect through the window blinding him again with its light. Through the corner of his eyes with the use of his peripheral vision he noticed another bed the same size of the one in which he slept. This bed however was different. It was empty and the covers and sheets were still neatly in place. It looked like it hasn’t been touched in days. To the right of the bed and in the center of the room a nightstand stood so woodenly. There was a lamp and a bible in the middle of the stand but way out of arms reach. These surroundings caused him to realize that he was trapped in the silence of a hotel room. From across the room a beam of green light dazzled above the doorknob. Someone was coming. The doorknob slowly twisted and as if automatic the wide doors jolted open like an elevator door. Something glared at him through the shadows. Anxiety began to boil sending a dense temperature throughout his blood. He lie there motionless quietly trying to hide his appearance for whatever was to come. The shadowed figure took a step forward shielding a few blinding rays from the sun. The figure was small and had voluptuous curves of a woman. “Is anyone in here?” Its voice came concluding the hypothesis of a woman. Chris didn’t know what to do. Should he just lie there, as if the room, were empty, or was this his only chance of survival? The woman moved in closer. Sweat poured from his pores like never before as the floor creaked with each step. The woman reached for the lamp on the nightstand setting the room aglow with the pull of its beaded string. “AAHHHH!” She yelled as soon as her eyes met with Chris’. Her scream startled him a bit but it was nothing too drastic. “Shhh,” he demanded. “Quiet.” She took a few steps backward and in shock her breathing began to rage. “Who did this to you?” She asked before taking a seat on the bed behind her. “I don’t know.” Chris replied. “But can you please help me get out of here?” She swallowed hard. “I don’t know. What do you want me to do?” She panicked. Chris grunted utterly similar to that of a hog in disgust. He slowly shook his head. “I don’t care. Do something. Get my hands and legs out of these cuffs, I’ll think of the rest.” “Right,” she nodded. She removed a Barbie pin from the bun in her hair and dove over to the opposing bed bouncing on top with a sickening thud. “Careful.” Chris motioned in pain. “Sorry.” She wiggled the pin in the holes of the handcuffs and after a couple of minutes his arms were broke free. He felt like a slave free at last. He reached around his wrists as the shackles fell to the ground. The woman went to work on the cuffs at his legs next popping those just as fast as the ones around his arms. “Thank you.” Chris graciously said. He lifted up and covered the bulge in his briefs with his two hands. “Can you please hand me my pants.” He said pointing towards a chair in the middle of the room. “Sure,” the woman smiled. Chris blushed. She tossed the pants on to the bed like a heavy sack of potatoes and turned her back being the lady that she was and allowed him to change. He pulled the pants over his legs and finally above his torso. The cold metal buckle of his belt caused him to quench and squirm a little. He rose off the bed and stretched like he hadn’t in three days. “Feels good.” He told her. She giggled. “What are you doing here anyway?” He asked. “Well,” she paused. “The people next door complained about a rough night and a lot of noise coming from here last night. It’s not my job to intrude but I was just curious and had to check it out to make sure everything was okay.” “Well, I’m glad you did.” He told her. He reached into his pocket and this caused his eyes to widen once again. “There’s only one problem.” “What?” She asked. “My wallet. It’s missing.” The woman laughed. “Your wallet? You’re lucky you’re still alive.” She told him. “Yeah. I know that.” He told her. “But all my money is inside, and if I want to get to PRIME’s Tropical Turmoil for my Storm the Beach Match I’m going to need some money.” “Oh. I knew you looked familiar!” He placed his hand over her lip cuing her to be quiet. “There’s no time for that. Help me find it.” To be continued.
