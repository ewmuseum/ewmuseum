Scars of a Battered Champion

6 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 6, 2006 Happiness is only a wrist-cut away I A torturous kaleidoscope of events made up the entirety of my vision. The sound of my crackling ribs as I soared from the top of a twenty foot ladder, crashing through a cold wooden table and dismantling the body of one Ozric Mortimer. The unforgiving sounds of glass gushing in and out of my forehead in the forms of stabs, courtesy of a deranged and adrenaline-driven Karina Wolfeden. The roar of the crowd that accompanied my bloody sight as I struggle to hold on to consciousness while lying in shambles of broken table with my bloody arm draped across the chest of Emilio Rage. The fear that traced my heart as my bloody soul fought for balance on top of a twenty foot cage, ultimately losing the battle thus sending me hurling unto the frail bodies of both Chandler Tsonda and Kyle Lamen. The sharp stinch of blood rushing through my nose as Tchu contiously planted his fist, cracking my nose further open with each and every shot. The slow, torturing effects of Rampage carving open my forehead with the remains of a broken glass plate, smiling as my very blood stained his massive paws. As I sat at the poorly conditioned table, taking in the stench of sweat and grease, the pain which circulated my body reminded me of these very events. One year since defeating the Hardcore Legend, Trashcan Man, had brought forth the most trialing times of my life. My body had taken scars and wounds that no human body was constructed to endure. Heading into the 100th edition of Revolution, I was set to face my biggest challenge to date... And I was a wreck. Tabu Diner. Bogota, Columbia. Celebration of my klan’s anniversary had commenced, resulting in my being here at this broken down diner, awaiting the arrival of my boyhood friend, Tarvis Beacon. I was a wreck and so was this Diner, so it seemed only fitting that the two of we made one. Sweat stains pinched through the already small white tee-shirts of the cook, thus turning me off from any food in which they were serving. I held my aching ribs in pain, glancing up into the eyes of Tarvis whom had just made his way in. “An apple a day keeps the doctor away...” He took a drag from the cigarette which remained planted in his mouth and took a seat across from me. Tarvis was the definition of a broken down man. His wife had left him, both kids killed in a drive by, and those very killings had led him to a life of investigation, serving as a detective...that only served to bring forth numerous death threats, however. His rag clothes and cigarette stinch would have never hinted his involvement with the corrupt government of Columbia...then again, it may have. “I need more than an apple. I’m flirting with death, chief...” I reached for my ribs once more as I felt the humid cigarette smoke press against my face, welcoming a stinch that did not help my painful situation any more. “I envy you, Dante. Famed with all the riches in the world, and crying over such a petty wound. The states have turned you into a softy, I’m afraid.” He replied. He was the only man who’d ever call me by my middle name. It was his own personal way of being “Distinguished”. “Petty wound? I’m on my death bed trying to defend this championship. Know how long I’ve had it? A fucking year. You know how many defenses I’ve had to endure that year? You’re the detective...go ahead and figure it out.” Came my immediate reaction. The emotion that poured through told the story. “What happened to the Dante who fought with knives as a kid? “Oh he’s still here. He’s just moved up from “playing” with knives to surviving by “using” knives.” A small chuckle escape my breath, pinching my nearly broken ribs as it came through. “Yeah, yeah. Let me tell you about my worries, though. I’m one, count it, one merit away from stepping up the ranks and becoming a full fledged Security Adviser.” He stated. He’d only took a drag from his cigarette after announcing the good news, but I could tell he was excited...even through the lack of emotion. That was just Tarvis’ way. Emotions made him weak in his eyes. “Ah? I hear they get paid the big bucks.” Came the not-so-concerned reply. “Well, the guy whom I’ll be replacing is living in a three story house that man should only dream of possessing. I guess you could call it big bucks.” He stated once more. “For a man on the brink of such a goldmine, you don’t look very excited. I know you’re an emotionless hack, but come on.” “Not Excited? I’ve spent my entire life working in the slums for this corrupt government. I’ll kill in order to achieve this opportunity. Emotion is for the bewildered.” I shook my head as a moment of silence lingered between the two of us. Silence didn’t seem to affect Tarvis much. He’d been used to it for quite some time. For me, however? Silence forced me to direct all of my attention towards a cracked rib that was on the verge of collapsing. I loathed silence. “Argh...” “You alright, Dante?” “Do I look alright?” He rolled his eyes in response to the smart aleck comment while reaching deep into his black coat pocket and retrieving a small plastic ziplock bag, tossing it unto the middle of the table. Inside the bag was an even smaller ziplocked bag of yellow substance and a needle. “In that plastic bag is the answer to your problem.” “Drugs? Seriously?” “They’ve kept me in-tact..that’s for sure. Let’s be real here...isn’t like you don’t know how to use them.” “I’m trying to wash away that phase of my life.” “Egh, well think about it like this. Head into whatever this match is you’re gunning for with excruciating pain, or sell your pride for a moment, get rid of the pain, and do what you’ve got to do. Not a hard decision, I’d say.” He pushed himself out of the booth and took one last drag before disposing of the cigarette and preparing himself to make his exit. Before doing so, however...he held out his hand. “You expecting something?” I asked. “Drugs don’t come cheap these days” “Never said I was accepting..” “But you will.” “How do you know?” “I know you...” That’s why he’d been proclaimed as one of the best detectives around, because of his ability to break down any one person mentally. I reached deep into my pocket and retrieve the cash, assorting it and withdrawing the standard amount before placing it in his hands. He exited leaving me with two simple words... “Have Fun.” Peace comes through Death...only. II I felt a sick comfort as the needle injected itself into my naked arm. The fluid inside trickled through my veins, washing away every sober thought and thus replacing it with the influence of activity. Death, Sex, Drugs, it all ran rapid through my forsaken mind as I took in the warm embrace that the drug induced needle provided me with. It tickled my fancy, satisfying my craving pleasure. I loved every bit of it. The guilt of sin weighed down my already burdened concious, but it’s affect was minimum, because no amount of regret nor safety concern would be able to deliver me from this pleasure. It was the type of feeling that you read about in books, but never thought imaginable in reality. Who’d ever think that a mortal man could give birth to something so intoxicating, so manipulating, so cunning. The danger that this drug posses, I knew what it was, but this feeling...the fluid taking over my barren body, it felt too good to resist. The feeling drew me to one conclusion. I'm an addict. Finally I placed the needle down as I allowed the aftermath of the drug take its toll. In this poor life that I suffered, I had nothing much else to live for. When pleasure such as this came, I held on to it. I wanted to retain the Intense Championship...it was just as addictive as this drug, but to obtain it I would have to take my body through countless more beatings. All of them even more ungracious than the last. The type of pleasure that this drug provided for me required no blood loss at all. Though I knew of its faults, I ignored them...for pretty soon I had known this drug..this addicting demon that came in the form of a needle and liquid...as my only friend. The toxant drew me into a state where the future meant nothing to me. It introduced me to such an influenced state of mind that I cared not about winning the Intense Championship, I didn't care about tearing limb from limb the helpless soul of Adam Dick, nor did I care about showing that punk Fiore the consequences of playing mind games with me. I just wanted more. My eyelids grew heavy as the effects began to wear off...not because it was a cheap or short lived toxant, but because it began to drag me into a numb sleep. But through my numb state, I could see images of the past. Images that drove me to this point. I thought I was dead, with my life flashing before my very eyes. Throughout that saga, images of Piedmont Junior High School began to intake my soul. Yes, images of that faithful day...that day in which I went from normal, mortal, average kid into psycho, craving, lunatic. That day which has brought forth so much hurt and pain in my life. That day...that March 11th day. It was as clear as day, me as a 14 year old eighth grader, making an attempt at studying my lesson all the while the bullies in the back toyed with my mind. I hated to be toyed with. They tossed spit balls that smacked me in the back of my head. They made jokes about me, treating me as some cheap form of entertainment. Momma always told me that words could never hurt me, but that still did not heal the pain that I was made to suffer. A ten year span of rage began to build up inside of me. For ten long agonizing years I had let bullies treat me like I was some kind of toy. I was tired of it and set to do something about it. I thought about it for a second though and decided that instead of releasing the fury inside of me, I would instead take it like a man and do the right thing. My naive mind didn't know any better. So I raised my hand high in the air as Mr. Thomas acknowledged me. I told him of my troubles and what was his response? He told me that Daniel, the bully who had been torturing me for so long, could do no wrong. To him, Daniel was just another smart, average kid who knew not the meaning of wrong doing. What really got to my feeble mind, though, was the fact that Mr. Thomas ordered me to detention after school for interrupting his class with such an outlandish remark. I knew something had to be done. The detention came and so did a new and more brutal version of 14 year old Vangelus Olsig. I walked into the room, where no one but Mr. Thomas was present, and I slowly made my way to the desk of Theodore Thomas...my English teacher. What is the problem Vangelus? He asked me...but I responded by yelling out something along the lines of 'Stop acting stupid...you know what the problem is.' Finally years worth of torment had began to prosper. “Please go sit down Mr. Olsig” is what Mr. Thomas ordered of me, but I wasn't going anywhere. As far as I could tell, War had been declared. I reached down into my pocket and retrieved a loaded .22 pistol pointing it at the head of Mr. Thomas. “Where did you get that from” he pondered...but I yelled at him demanding that he get down on his knees and shut up. Emotionally I began to to poor out the years of pain that the bullies in which he thought were such sweet hearts had caused me. I began to tell him of my desire to pull the trigger and send years of torturing out of my life on one silver bullet. But I restrained, slowly depositing the gun. Mr. Thomas then attempted to take a stab at me, leaping up to try and pry the gun from my grasp...that was it. No teacher dare put his hands on me. As we tugged for the gun, I had enough positioning to snap the trigger sending a bullet through the throat of Mr. Thomas. I found joy in watching him scramble across the floor as blood leaked from the big gash in his neck. A sick joy...but joy nonetheless. My eyes slowly peeled open...I thought I was in hell now. The drug had taken me and battered me in a manner that no mortal man could. I had become a victim to the madness of satan's intoxicating tool. As my eyes pilled open, my sight remained blurry, but through the blur I could still make out my surrounding. A yellow light shone in my face as I could barely see two figures standing over me...metal objects in hand. I could hear them speaking... 'Looks like this one got hit heavily.' 'Are you sure this was done purposely?' 'I'm not sure...could have been gang related.' 'True. For anyone to get stoned this heavily is brutal.' 'Think he'll make it?' 'Nope.' I knew exactly where I was. I was in the emergency room with my life on the line. This drug could have very well been my cradle to the grave. I never thought I would end this way...and I wouldn't, because I was going to fight to stay alive. I made a promise to myself that I would not leave my legacy unmarked, and it’s a vow I seek to keep. Adam Dick seeks to tarnish my legacy..and nothing was going to stop me from handing him the consequences of his actions on a silver ...intense, platter. As I felt the sharp needle inject into my skin as a result of the doctor whom was treating me..the only thing that ran through my mind was death. What if I died right here and right now. The shere thought of dying was not what bothered me the most, but to die without leaving my mark in the business that I created. To die and leave PRIME forever..leave my Intense division forever, releasing it to a bunch of wild sheep who knew nothing of the sport...only of the paycheck they recieved daily. To die and leave my title in the hands of a walking gimmick. That is what bothered me the most. That is what kept me fighting for my life when I should have just accepted death for what it was. For that...I lived on. You don't have any friend's in life. Trust Me. III I felt a small chill as my eyes peeled open once more. I wasn’t inside of the emergency room this time...no, this location was largely distinct. I was outside...in a graveyard? How’d I get here? I attempted to lift my head from off the ground, but as I did so I felt my neck nearly snapped. It brought forth a revelation, because afterwards I felt my ribs hurting even more so than before, and as I struggled to place my arms over them...they cringed as well. The blood that rushed down my forehead, entering my pupil is what made me realize that someone had taken advantage of my broken state, checked me out of the emergency room, and beaten me to a pulp. I could taste death as it lingered throughout my body. My blood stained the ground below, creating a puddle which made the hair on the back of my head stand on edge at it’s very sight. Footsteps. They got louder as I could hear someone approaching. I rolled my head and looked up. The figure stood above my motionless body as I struggled to peer through the blood covering my eyes in order to identify the person...but just as I attempted to make out the blurry figure, I sunk into an abyss of unconsciousness once more. The figure stood over my lifeless body. Detective Tarvis Beacon. ...Bloody crowbar in hand. He reached into his back pocket and retrieved a communication radio, pressing one button as he sounded through the microphone. “Detective Beacon reporting in. I have a stranded comrade down and he’s struggling to survive. Culprit unknown. Send help immediately!” He reached down and held my head in his arms as if he’d cared. And yet he smiled. He meant it when he said he’d kill to achieve that last merit. He was now a millionaire.
