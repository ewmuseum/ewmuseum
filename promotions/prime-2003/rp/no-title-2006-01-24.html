Date: 1/24/2006 'Chris... you've got to help me!' With deep concern Chris Collins became aghast jumping up from his satin sheets with his Palm One cell phone still glued to his ear. The miniature fan blowing from above his head made the situation even worse. He was still covered in sweat and more aggravated from being awaken from his sleep then ever. It must've been another blistering night in the town he had grown to love, Los Angeles, California. 'Huh?' He mumbled. 'Who is this?' 'It's me.' The voice replied deeply. 'Randy.' Chris' eyes bulged from their sockets. Yeah, the name rang a bell but he just couldn't put his finger on it. He threw his black covers to the floor and switched his Treo to his right ear. Repositioning his body, he flicked the lamp on over to his left. 'Randy... not trying to sound rude anything but who's Randy?' He had to ask. Unexpectedly a chuckle came from across the other line. Just how much trouble was this Randy really in? Growing even more confused Chris leapt out of the bed. His toes cooled from the wooden boards in his floor. He walked over to the window and placed his left forearm smack dab in the middle. 'Don't tell me you forgot about me already brother.' The man known as Randy continued to chuckle. 'Remember Athens, Ohio? Ohio University? Pre-Medicine? Basketball? Is any of it ringing a bell?' 'Oh...' Chris recalled. 'Randy Evans right?' 'Yeah.' Randy exclaimed. 'I can't believe you forgot about me already. I mean it's only been two years since you dropped out of college.' He paused. 'Besides. How can you forget the man who dished out assist after assist to make you look good on the court?' Chris looked down at his watch noticing that it was only seven o'clock in the morning. Which meant that it was ten o'clock in Cleveland. He rested his head across his forearm and let out a yawn. 'I didn't forget about you Randy.' He tried to insist. 'You just slipped my mind.' 'How on earth did I do that?' Chris shook his head in irritation. He wanted to tell this Randy to leave him the hell alone but he just didn't have the heart. Besides, he asked for help right? What did he want. 'Look Randy.' Chris tried to grab his attention. 'I don't really have the time. I'm sort of busy right now. What can I help you with?' 'Oh... oh...' Randy muttered feeling a bit ashamed. 'Sorry I almost forgot. I was making a scrapbook about my first years in college and was just wondering if you had any ideas?' Chris removed his head from the window and walked back over to the bed. Like a sack of weightless potatoes he let himself go falling face first into the comfort of his abode. 'No I don't have any damn ideas. Quit calling me!' He held the red button on his phone ending the call and terminating the power of his Treo 700. He threw it on the floor with no care of what happened to it. Next, he rolled back under the sheets grabbing the black covers from the floor and replacing them above his half-naked body. It was hard for him to fall back to sleep. He placed his hand on his forehead and wiped a few drops of sweat from it's surface. 'Damn moron.' He closed his eyes and when he did thoughts began to pour within his skull. Kobe Bryant... 81 Points. Another Reason why he loved his new hometown. Adam Dick better known as the Illustrious Face Eater. The man he hated. The man EVERYONE hated. Tchu. The man he didn't know but because of his big impact respected. The Innovator of Offense. The King of the Impossible Himself. Togetherness. PRIME was bound to be on their edge of their seats.
