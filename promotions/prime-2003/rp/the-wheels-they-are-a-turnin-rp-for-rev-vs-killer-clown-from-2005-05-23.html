The Wheels, they are a turnin! (rp for rev vs. Killer Clown from Outer Space)

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23 “Do you find it bizarre at all?” Dusty asks as they walk away from the Pizza Hut they’ve just indulged themselves at. “Bizarre?” replies Zameer quizzically. “Azala not sure what you mean.” The night air has become rather crisp in the two hours time they were inside Pizza Hut. Not at all odd for this time of the year in Chicago, yet a little surprising, as it’s been quite warm as of late. Dusty pauses for a second, taking a deep breath, then stares intently at his shoelaces as they proceed to Dusty’s dwelling. “It’s just that, in our world, here and now, we’ve only known each other for about twelve hours, but in reality, over three weeks has passed. I mean, you’ve already fought in four matches, winning your contract with PRIME. Dusty trails off and pauses again, this time staring up to Zameer, who could honestly be wondering about anything at this point, most likely though he’s wondering exactly what was on that last slice of pizza that made it taste like dessert. “I mean, it just seems a little weird to me, that’s all” “Azala don’t think it weird at all” “How can you not think it’s weird big man?” Dusty questions. “Somebody, somewhere, is going to notice what’s going on and call you out. I mean, you’re leaving an open door for other people to make fun of you cuz you don’t link all your stuff into one giant role-play, you cut it off and continue each week! Are you even paying attention to me?!” Zameer’s snaps out of his unconscious-like state, well, I guess partial conscious, unconscious state would better explain it as he’s most certainly aware of what’s going on around him, just not completely in tune with anything specific, as per usual. “What do Dusty mean?” “Listen man, you’ve got a big match this week, THIS WEEK! Meaning time shall move forward, yet here we are stuck in the same story from four weeks ago! You need to move forward so you can prepare for your match against that Killer Clown guy! I mean, Christ, he’s a fucking clown! Do you even……” “A clo……. A clown?” Zameer nervously responds “A Christ, you’re not going to tell me you are afraid of clowns now are you? Well, are you? Hello!” Dusty attempts to prompt Zameer out his latest coma like state of thinking but to no avail as everything slowly goes black. “Azala not sure” *************************************************************************** “Jensen!” A thunderous voice booms into a cubicle, bringing the sleeping, skinny man behind the desk, dressed in a business suit, to life. “Wha? Who? Why?” Jensen mumbles as he slowly gathers his wits. “Get yo ass up yo, we’z already late as a muthafucka!” The ebonics laden voice catapults from a rather overly dressed young black man. Loose fit Sean Jean jeans and a black FuBu t-shirt wrapped carefully around an enormous spinning platinum chain upon his neck so that it’s always visible. The glare from the spinner is what first catches Jensen’s attention. “What in gods name is going on?” Jensen inquires rather sleepily still. “Emergency meeting, we’re already ten minutes late bro! Hurry yo ass up!” The two men scurry frantically through the maze that would be their office. Dodging in and out of cubicles and around corners, narrowly missing a woman who appeared to be almost floating along with a stack of papers piled higher than her head. “Excuse us” Jensen yells back as they continue to the large meeting room. Upon entering the room, it actually looks more like a chamber. Something from the future perhaps, yet it could be a little retro at a second glance. The color scheme is painfully gray, but business is buzzing all around the translucent room as the two men join a committee of others, already seated. Starting at the far north end of the table and working clockwise there were exactly 10 men sitting around a pristine white oval shaped table. First there was Mr. Wambozee, he was easily the most intelligent of the ten, and rumor had it that he had amazed the owners so greatly upon his written application, that he was hired via mail courier, without them even seeing the guy, sadly enough though, he was a mute. He really didn’t vocalize much of what he thought, well, because he couldn’t, and by the time he had it written out, brilliantly might I add, in words, the committee was already moved on to the next topic. Working clockwise was Jeffery McDoogle, a white man, dressed the most professionally of all surrounding the table. His specialty was apparently business management, but his intense ADHD limited his work-rate immensely. Albeit brilliant, his ideas would pass so quickly that he very seldom ever had time enough to fill anyone in until the next idea would pop into his head. Next to McDoogle was a portly fellow named Liam, an Englishman would be jest in calling him cheeky as his rosy cheeks and button nose almost resemble someone rather famous who would don a red suit every twelfth month, had he been wearing a red suit that is. However, he wore what appeared to be a leisure suit, lime green with a dark green ruffled vest underneath and a pair of rose-tinted sunglasses. He was truly the Hunter S. Thompson of the group, and never really had anything relevant to talk about. He was however the bosses nephew so everyone just kind of let it slide. To Liam’s immediate left, set back in the shadows was a rather mysterious figure, a man of few words, yet one could tell he commanded complete respect at all times. Smoke swelling around his head in the shadows his eyes pierce through the haze bright yellow as he ashes his cigar on the floor. His name is simply Mr. E, horribly uninventive I agree, yet in hopes of keeping this all simple, it works well. Next to Mr. E sits a set of twins, Rory and Cory, not really sure of a last name there, but they always dressed exactly alike, which is a terribly painful thing twins do to irritate the rest of the world, and also spoke at the exact same time. Some would call it telepathy between two linked beings, I just call it plain old clever plotting, as a matter of fact, I caught them one time practicing their lines, but that’s a story for another time. Moving around the table from Rory and Cory was where DeAndre, the young black fellow whom entered with Mr. Jensen, and Mr. Jensen sat next to one another. DeAndre was always yelling something about oppression and never being heard in a public forum like this, I’m not exactly sure what he was talking about, I wasn’t really listening that closely, but Jensen, lately Jensen has been the mastermind! Jensen suffers from a sleep disorder known as sleep apnea as well as narcolepsy. The two combine to form quite a formidable team of inspiration, but only when he’s sleeping. Our technology crew has come up with a machine to monitor his brain activity while sleeping, and our last three huge idea’s stemmed from his dreaming. It’s really quite intense. Anyhow, moving on, next to Jensen was me, I’m really a nobody, but to fit the quota, I have been included. Merely a student intern I once blurted out “jelly doughnut” in a meeting and the rest was history. It may have been due to the massive amount of drinking that day, or the fact that Mr. Wambozee just couldn’t disagree in time, but the team took the jelly doughnut idea and made it a masterpiece. And finally, to my right, was oversized third grade kid, wearing a dashiki and flip flops. It seemed weird at first that he was here, but after a good amount of time and much deliberating, he’s become the key to our ultimate success, for he is the mold. “Lets bring this to order, and quickly please,” Liam states, the overwhelming importance of this meeting vibrating with every word he manages to work out. “It’s come to my immediate attention that we have encountered something new, something none of us has thought about to this point.” Pausing for a moment to rerun what he just had said in his head to make sure it makes sense, Liam is interrupted. “Well, what in gods name brings us all together, and over lunch hour no less” questions Jeffery McDoogle, in a most professional type manor. “Work is all good and dandy, but we do need to eat.” “Clowns” Rory and Cory simply state together “Clowns?” the seven men at the table repeat, who can, Rory and Cory counting as one, most of them a little confused. “So yeah, I had this idea…..” “Jeffery, can we please try to stay on task today,” Jensen urges, “I, unlike others, have a 2:30 pm meeting up in the cerebral cortex that I just can’t afford to miss.” “Fine” McDoogle retorts, “What were we talking about?” Wambozee scribbles tirelessly on his notepad but to no avail, “clowns, we’re talking about clowns ya broken headed Bee-atch!” insults who else but DeAndre. “Hey, I resent that!” “Shut up” Mr. E replies in his monotone voice from amidst his shadow. “I say we just kill em, we grab em by the throat, rip out their larynx, and shower the innocent in the blood of the righteous!” “Christ, the clowns? Question Rory and Cory. “Everyone” Mr. E responds again, even more sinister this time. “Christ Mr. E, that’s your damn solution to everything.” Jensen blurts out what everyone else was thinking. “Well, who’s the guy that steps up whenever we get in trouble?” Mr. E begins. “When the rest of you run and hide, when the shit hits the fan, who steps up? Every time shit gets sticky around here and you all run and hide!.” “Um, I was just out of the office last time it happened, I wasn’t hiding.” McDoogle tries to retaliate “Right, sure you were, you are the biggest pussy of the bunch, and the lot of you aren’t much to look at either.” Mr. E is really letting the group have it, but nobody can really correct him for when times get rough, it’s always his deep, brooding, evil background that pull the group through it. His actual background isn’t all that well known, but he’s pulled the system out of more than just one situation, and everyone, quietly, in their own way, respects that. Everyone save for Wambozee that is, who just never seems to get his ideas to the forefront quickly enough to be acted upon. “The problem here,” Liam starts, “is that we don’t have any schematics to deal with this, we’ve never even thought of this as a possibility, at least not this late. “Schematics, flamatics,” McDoogle replies snidely drawing everyone’s attention over to where he has just opened a bag of Cool Ranch Doritos. The other men at the table, myself included, all lean forward waiting for another witty retort from McDoogle, but he chooses to just stick to his snacking for now. The pause in action allows Wabozee an opportunity to scribble something onto a piece of notebook paper, then slam it down on the table, getting everyone’s attention. As all nine men not involved with the actual writing of the piece crane their necks to see what he has written, one word is visible. SCARED ~ Whoever could have thought that one word could bring so much commotion to a room of professionals. Everyone began speaking all at the same time, everyone save for Wambozee, Mr. E, and Jensen who had fallen asleep about three minutes ago. The soft chatter turned into a match of one-upsmanship, each man trying to be heard over his colleague, and soon the room turned into a boisterous shouting match. Now this is where I come in. “Hey! Everyone just shut the hell up!” I shouted whilst pounding on the table. For some reason, everyone stopped what they were yelling about and looked to me. I immediately regretted my decision to speak up, but nevertheless, I had to move forward with what I had to say. “Just think of it, being scared of clowns is the last thing anyone would expect from us, we’ll be able to play a weak hand, so to speak, and catch that big card we need on the river to win in the end. Nobody will ever see it coming!” The other nine men looked on as if I was going to continue, unfortunately, I hadn’t thought that far ahead, the idea just popped into my head and I ran with it. “That’s proposterous!” shouted Liam. “Do you have any idea how long it takes to reprogram the system once it’s been set in motion! W merely program this system, then it takes over and matures, it learns from the base program we lay into the mainframe! Starting a debacle like this could possible blow up in our face! “Or, we could simply just be big and brash, the tough guy routine ya know,” adds Cory and Rory together. “Yeah, that’s real fuckin original ya crackaz!” “Listen, hey! Listen!” Interjects McDoogle. “Wait, what were we talking about?” “Christ.” Reply Cory and Rory in unison “I think this can actually work, it’ll be a lot of, ummm, well, work I guess, but I think it’s the only idea we’ve got.” Suggests Jensen who had awoken early enough to get the gist of what was going on. “Don’t forget about the tough guy routine!” interrupts Rory and Cory again. “Mutha fucka! That’s the most unoriginal shit I’ve heard of! Everyone does that shit, all the fuckin time nigga, and that ain’t what we’z about!” Again through the arguing Wambozee manages to write something on his pad of paper and slams it down on the table, and all nine men once again crane their necks to see. “HEAR HIM OUT!” Since he was the unspoken leader, all the other men turned their attention back to me. Sheepishly I glanced up again. Eighteen eyes staring back at me was quite unnerving, yet I had to hold my ground, and I knew that. “Listen, it’s easy, it’s our only play cuz it’s the play that nobody expects to see. I mean, who would think that a system of this size would be scared of anything, especially clowns” “Well, lets not forget the heights issue, I mean, he is scared of heights” Not even knowing specifically where that last comment came from I have no choice but to defend my idea here. “Anyone can be scared of heights, it’s one of the biggest fears on the planet aside from dying or speaking in public! This is totally uncharted territory, we have to do this, it’s our only chance to win this week! How can you guys not see this?” A moment of brief silence was followed by, “Well, ok, all in favor of being afraid of the clowns, say aye.” Going around the table, Wambozee holds up a sign that reads “Aye”, then to McDoogle, another “Aye”, around to Liam, yet another “Aye”. Mr. E remained back in his fortress of shadow and smoke, welling from his cigar around his head and up to the ceiling, his eyes could be seen shifting back and forth as if he was shaking his head no, “Nay”. “Ok, so that’s three ‘Ayes’ to one ‘Nay’ so far.” Continuing on to Rory and Cory, both saying together, “Nay” (figures, it wasn’t their idea), then to DeAndre, “Hell yeahz Nigga”, then to Jensen, Jensen…. “Jensen!” “Wha, what? Oh, yeah, I’m in” “We all know where I stand.” I state as plainly as possible. “And with the vote now six to three, it doesn’t matter what he says.” Motioning over to the portly third grade kid sitting in silence playing a PSP. “So it’s a go then, we’re all in agreement…..” “You have all lost your way!” Mr. E powerfully exclaims. “Have you all completely forgot where we come from? Have you completely abandoned what we used to be!” Growing ever more intense Mr. E stands from the chair that could no longer hold his frustration and raises his right hand high above his head. Bringing it down with full force, on the table, he snaps the table in half sending shards of wood flying throughout the conference room and everyone else at the table ducking for cover. “You all make me Sick!” After the dust settles, the other 9 men start peeking out from where they had all hid, Mr. E had already left the room to their relief. “I guess that’s that then, we’re scared of clowns.” Liam says still with a little nervous stutter in his voice. “We hate when he does that.” Rory and Cory admit as the picture turns to black and begins to fade slowly back in. *************************************************************************** “Azala? Hey! Azala!” Dusty has been trying now for some time to snap the Big Snack Daddy out of his trance, and finally some life slips back into Zameer’s eyes. “Man, you were really out of it there, where the hell did you go?” Azala looks around quickly making sure he is fully comfortable with his surroundings before responding, “Azala not sure, Azala think he black out.” “I mention clowns and you just started trippin.” “Clowns?” Azala ponders……………………. “Azala A-scared of clowns” “Ah Christ!” Dusty exclaims. “I need to fix this now! Come on, let’s go!” Zameer follows Dusty down the street, trailing just a mere two steps behind him, “But where are we going?” “Where are we going? Where do you think we’re going!” Dusty blurts out, walking ever more briskly, we have to find a 4th grade birthday party!” “OK” Zameer responds, “Will they have cake?” “Cake is the last of your worries right now big guy,” Dusty shakes his head at his new friend of four weeks, erm, 12 hours, then looks back to the Big Snack Daddy. “You aren’t afraid of Pirates too, are you?” **************************************************************************** The scene flashes back to the destroyed meeting room where the nine men are working to find all of their items that had been strewn across the floor when that one word reverberates through the room. All nine men look up at the same time and exclaim, “PIRATES!” Well, all save for Wambozee, who just holds a sign up reading, “PIRATES?” as the scene fades to black.
