Eulogy of the Deadline

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Hugging deadline like biker shorts Today, wrestling has lost a great man. Buddy Kingfisher, one of the all time perfectly average wrestlers, has passed away. Yes, Buddy Kingfisher, holder of the Core something-or-other championship. I can’t be bothered to remember what it was. This is CORE we’re dealing with, after all. Yes, yes, I know, it’s not proper to speak ill of the dead…BUT I’M KENJIRO FUCKING ITO! What did you expect? Tact? Grace? A heartfelt tribute to the career of a crybaby who’s best known for getting annihilated by geriatric overweight Russians? That’s not my bag. Besides, I know damn well no one’s giving me a nice goodbye, so why should I bother to do it for anyone else? And yes, I know, making Core jokes is like shooting fish in a barrel. Not fair. Unsporting. And that is why, in a rare magnanimous gesture, I will stop making jokes about Core Wrestling. Besides, Kingfisher’s an easy enough target as it is. The man’s spent his career facing lesser-knowns and never-wases. Building up a record in the process. Winning titles. All the while knowing that once he came to the big time, he couldn’t handle it. Maybe that’s why he killed himself. I don’t know. Or maybe someone else shot him because he sucked. I have no clue. I’m not the fucking coroner. I’d have more to say, but I’m a dirty, dirty procrastinator, and the deadline’s coming. Go ahead, bitch at me. I double dog dare you.
