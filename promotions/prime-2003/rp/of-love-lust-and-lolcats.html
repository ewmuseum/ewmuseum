Usually, these places were packed with people lined up to take a piss -- this early in the night, it was just one guy with bleached hair who gave Erick a thumb's up. He didn't know how to react to that. He didn't know how to react to any of this, because the woman -- she'd said her name was Siena or Sierra or something -- she wasn't giving him a lot of time to think with the big head. They'd barely been introduced before they were dancing, and, really, who can hear anything out on those dance floors with the deafening volume they play the music at and the fact that you can barely see your dance partner. He'd smiled some, laughed a little. She ground against him like she was trying to start a fire, and then she'd risen up to her tiptoes and shout-whispered in his ear that they should go somewhere quieter. Which is how they'd ended up in the hallway, her fitted against him, her hands under his jacket, unbuttoning his shirt. Her lips were everywhere -- she was like some kind of freaky machine, cast as an auburn-haired Asian girl (the hair color wasn't natural, obviously, but, really, quibbling over details like that would just seem rude). Girls didn't do this with him; they didn't just threaten to break him against the wall. She pulled her lips away from his neck. He could hear the sound of her lips pulling into a grin as she slid one hand between them. "Do you live close, Erick?" she asked, breath warm and slightly moist against his earlobe. "I... yeah. My roommate and I are splitting rent on a place right up the street." "And will he mind if we're a little bit... rambunctious?" "She's a she, actually."
