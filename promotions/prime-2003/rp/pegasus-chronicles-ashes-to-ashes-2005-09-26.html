Pegasus Chronicles – Ashes to Ashes

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 26th, 2005 “Try to fathom this… Your being watched, every second, of every day. Your life is slowly diminishing, as the people you care for, are dropping like flies around you. Your true love, is also your job, and it’s the only way to escape the hassle of ending up dead. Your not safe, even in your own house, and your family, is your biggest regret. Your world is spinning out of control, and theirs no stopping until you hit the ground. Is this a way to live? Is this freedom? Or is this just life getting it’s retribution on a guilty man? …Till death do us part, some will say, they are bound by a chain of love… but what does love really have to do with it? Does love stop us from making dumb mistakes? Or does it cause us to make larger ones? It’s a matter of the mind, a formality if you wish. What one mistakes for love, passion, hate, and desire, can all be thought of as an obsession. So what does that mean for me? Well… maybe I am obsessed with wrestling. But then again, maybe that’s just because that’s all I have left…” A deep sigh interrupts the stillness. White exhales spill into the air as the nemesis warrior breathes. On this very night, his body is standing on a last nerve, as his body hair stands on end with the chilled air. “I can’t believe this has happened…” mutters Jesse as he talks to himself, a typical matter done when the beast has been unsettled for a while. “I just don’t understand it, who has something against me? And what are they looking for?” Scratching his goatee, his face shows serious concentration, dwelling on pass memories to remind himself, yet nothing comes to mind. “Argh, this pisses me off. Ah, damn, I’m out of cigarettes,” grumbles Jesse, now frustrated even more so because of his lack of nicotine. Standing up on the edge of the porch, he stops as his eye is caught on something, a shooting star. “Well… might as well make a wish on something, my lucks been in the shitter lately,” he sighs as he looks up at the shooting star closes his eyes, lip speaking his wish to himself. “Please, make that come true,” and as he finishes speaking he opens his eyes, and he walks into the house, closing the door behind him, shutting out the chilly air. *** *** *** A dying motor echoes from a black dodge truck. Fog rises from the ground, as two feet plant themselves down moments later. The surroundings depict that of a local neighborhood parking lot, and the skies display the proof of morning dew being what was thought of as fog. A man stands leaning against a brick wall right in front of the truck. As the foots shatter the silence of a new day, the man upon the wall lifts a bag in his left hand to his mouth, and for a few seconds keeps it there, before bringing it back down to his side. “Jess...” mutters the man, before he belches quietly. Nodding, the nemesis warrior shows no kind heart as he walks up to the man. Not even extending a hand, he just leans against the wall next to him, waiting for what must be information. “So what do you know?” hastily says Jesse getting to the point. “I know nothing if your going to be disrespectful,” mutters the man, as he exposes his face more as he lifts the hair out of it. A roughed southern type face, with a partly shaven beard, but mostly outgrown from what’s noticeable. His eyes sink in his face, bringing an almost majestic look to him, but in a stoned sort of way. His lips chapped, as his body looks thinner than that of a parking meter. “I don’t feel like dragging on what is really not a conversation. So instead of killing me with bullshit, give me the facts, and I’ll be on my way,” Jesse replies frustrated. Sighing, “You just don’t get it do you Jess? This isn’t something I can just tell you, but in honesty I must show you… You’ll never grasp the truth to this until you see the men whom I have behind me, and what they are willing to do for you.” The man looks past Jesse, at the road, noticing the dew slowly fading up into the air. “Look Jess, you know as well as I do, that reinforcements are your best option, if not you’re only. You may think your some kind of lone star prodigy who can take on the world, but no warrior has ever lasted against an up rise alone, without coming out with a part of them missing.” Gazing down Jesse’s eyes seem content, but his fists show a not so-kinder emotion. The words may be true, but Jesse doesn’t want to here them. To him it’s all or nothing, one last try, or the end of the road, and this guy is just leading his mind into a parallel perplexity that deserves not to be brought into the conscious of mind. “Care to tell me why these reinforcements are needed? What do they possess that I do not? What have you taught them that I don’t already know? What are they but pawns upon this battlefield, in which the only king left standing is the sole survivor of the game of life? Huh, tell me that!” bellows Jesse now letting his anger out in a tone. “You’re not grasping the concept. It’s not the fact that there more skillful than you, or that they may die, but that they are there, proving their alliance to us, and showing me what they can do…” mutters the man in an almost whisper. Tilting his head up, Jesse’s eyes strain as they peer at the man, almost in a craze. “So you want me to take these men under my wing, fight with them, and mold them into warriors, or let them die under my guidance due to them not having the knowledge I do? Either I’ve lost the idea, or your playing slaughter the lambs, and these ‘pawns’ your speaking of are waiting for the butcher… Damn it, what has this lifestyle come to. It seems more like a government-based religion, than a stature to test the ones who are wanting to learn. You might as well line them up and shoot them, it’d save them some pain,” states Jesse now disgusted with his conversation with the man. “As you wish, but realize this… when you need your reinforcements, when you ask for help, this hand will always be here, and I’ll gladly… gladly pull you back up to your feet, Jess,” says the man with a smile crossing his face as he finishes his sentence. “You know it’ll be the turning point of hell and heaven before I reach for your hand Kaine, damn well know that,” laughs Jesse as he trots off to his truck door, slamming it as he jumps in, and pulls out of the parking lot, letting his tires screech upon the pavement. *** *** *** “Errrr… Beep. Paging Doctor Romero. Paging Doctor Romero.” Appearing before the front desk in a shiny long sleeve maroon colored shirt, black jeans, and clean-shaven, is Jesse. His eyes wander amongst the patients in the waiting room. A few of them showing illness in their faces, others merely waiting to hear the news of whether there loved ones are fine or not. “Excuse me miss, I’m here to visit Mick Lere. Room 210,” says Jesse. The woman behind the desk glances up, and then to her computer, typing in the name and room number. “Ohm, give me a moment sir. Our computers are acting up at the moment,” she replies in a low voice. “No problem,” sighs Jesse trying to act patient. ’It’s one thing that hospitals are slow, but another to have problems with technology when that is what there dependent on. Eh… at least I’m not one of those poor saps who has to sit in the waiting room.’ In his left hand is a card, most likely a get-well card from what the cover displays. Looking over the desk Jesse glances at a clock on the near wall, 10:23 PM. His eyes make there way back to the lady, whom is still trying to find the information. ’I don’t get why things are so abstruse for these hospitals. They are trained to do their job and do it to there best abilities, yet they are unable to satisfy the requirements of a patient in a mere minute. Psssh. That’s one of the more noticeable reasons to the economy’s downfall. Everything is done half-assed now adays.’ “Sir, the patient you are looking for is checked out. He checked out around eight o’clock today,” states the lady now as she stands up from the computer and grabs a bottle of water on the opposite side of the desk. “That can’t be right, he was signed in by me. I’m the only one able to sign him out,” exclaims Jesse with an aggravated tone. Now leaning on the desk, he looks at her directly as she goes to explain, but he cuts her off, “Check again Miss… Please!” Without hesitating his orders, she sits back down and begins to type, but Jesse not wanting a repeated answer begins to direct her with her processing of the name, “It’s spelled M-I-C-K-space-L-E-R-E. Room 210, Miss.” She shakes her head again before opening her lips to talk, but Jesse blows her off and walks away. Strutting down the hallway through a door that has a sign above it labeled “Patients” he turns and hurries down the hallway of the 200’s. 201. 203. 205. 207. 209… “Bingo, 210.” Walking in he sees an empty bed, and the Television turned off. His eyes do a double take as he checks for anything out of the ordinary. On the far left near the bed, sits a Teddy Bear and an envelope that is placed between the bear’s hands in a holding fashion. Picking it up he tosses the bear onto the bed after tearing the envelope from it’s hands. Ripping the seam, he takes out the contents of the envelope to discover a letter that has been printed from a computer inside. ’To the one whom this may concern most, I have taken Mr. Lere from this bed, in hopes to strengthen him in his contingency of life. By my means and the power of modern medicine in which I have in possession, I will take care of Mr. Lere until my requests are met. At the end of your services, I will free Mr. Lere into your care. My requests are simple. Do what I say, and everything will go as painless as possible. First things first, go to the phone booth on Longview Street, 123rd avenue. I will call at promptly 9:10 PM tomorrow night. If you unable to do this then you will be warned, and trust me when I say warned, I don’t mean a slap on the hands by any means. Until then, take care. Sincerely, Anonymous Falling back onto the bed, the nemesis warrior is taken back. His eyes begin to mist, but he quickly shakes his head and breathes through his nose, forcing the sobs back in him. ’… no, I will not weep on this tragedy. Strength, it’s all I have left if I wish to defeat this…’ Sighing, he shakes his head in a defeated way, and then stands up. ’A warrior never walks from a battle of revenge, but takes vengeance on the soul in which has been taken that is closest. God forbid my ways, god forbid my choice, but god pity the soul of the one who takes these actions as games and attempts to play you in your ways of life. Pity them… please.’
