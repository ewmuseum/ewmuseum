Broken Memories

3 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3, 2007 Diego Delgado flipped unthinking through a set of pictures, each face he passed a tribute to a moment still held close to his heart, each one withheld from him with only the glossy replica remaining to remind him he had actually spent some time outside this god damned cell. What a waste. He could not help but think that somewhere along the line he should have managed to take more than a half dozen pictures. He halted momentarily on the last picture before placing them back inside their plastic bag and into their hiding place underneath the sweat soaked sheet substituting for a mattress. He let out a dramatic sigh, letting himself briefly indulge in the lost elements of his life. The Latin Kings, the only family he had ever had, they would ride again someday. He was sure of it, or at least as sure as something with his disposition ever was. And that’s pretty much all he ever had. Diego paused again, the painful realization that his previous life held no value creeping through his body like a virus. He was about to remind himself that was exactly how he wanted it when the bed above Diego’s head groaned slightly, signaling another incoming question from its all too inquisitive occupant. The intruder peaked his head over the edge, coming to a halt less than six inches from Delgado’s face. “You looking at those pictures again?” “How many times I gotta tell you?” Diego tried waving him away like a unwanted stench. “Don’t try that backyard psycho bullshit on me.” Even for an unrelenting, probing know-it-all, it was impossible for Diego to hate his cell mate, but that didn’t mean he took the effort to learn his name. For the months they had both been confined to the eight by six concrete square, Diego had survived the conversational onslaught by referring to him as whatever came to his mind at that particular moment. “I’m just saying,” he spoke with a knowing smile plastered on his face. “That you’re definitely suppressing something in your past.” “That your professional opinion?” “Like, I don’t know, maybe you want to find out more about your mother?” Diego sat up fast, nearly cracking his forehead across the metal support bar. He had never told anyone about his family, only in his dreams did the visions and memories from his family arrive to him. “Huh? What are you talking about?” “Easy, D. Sometimes you just talk in your sleep, say some weird ass shit.” Before he was able to divulge anymore night time secrets from the depths of Diego’s past, the guard’s voice echoed down the corridor. “OPEN ON 14!” Yeah, I watch Prisonbreak too. The metal door swung outward, the frame suddenly filled by the hulking silhouettes of the correctional officers finest. “Delgado, you got company.” ### “I don’ know who’s shit you been fuckin’ with,” Palmer spoke between sips of coffee. “But the Feds have been sniffin’ around this here facility lately. And they catchin’ the stink right off you.” Diego was now seated in what he could barely believe was a smaller room, this one shrewdly thrown together for this impromptu meeting. He didn’t like Palmer, the racist bastard that he was, but since he ran the prison, Delgado didn’t have a fucking choice. “I haven’t-“ “Save it, I don’ care what y’all gon say. I reckon your kind gon lie about this and that, but what I can’t stand is having them big city hot shots around here on account of your useless ass.” Delgado had previous altercations with Federal agents before, they tend to look down upon terrorizing large cities in gangs now-a-days, especially if your of foreign descent, but this felt different. He should be off their radar by now. Suddenly the door shot open, and Palmers smirk vanished. “I’m special agent Mattson,” the large man spoke directly to Palmer. “And this is special agent Collard.” The smaller woman slipped in the door behind her partner. “And if you’d excuse us, we’d like to have a quick word with your prisoner here.” ### His head was spinning as he exited the make shift conference room, his newly gained assets thrown inside a large cardboard box. Back to PRIME? Simple enough. Back to PRIME with a hidden agenda? A little more difficult. None the less, he couldn’t contain his smile as he arrived back as his cell a few minutes later, the keys to his future still tucked safely away in the cardboard box. He was greeted by the wide eyed stare of his soon be ex-cellie. “What happened? What’s with the box? Where you going?” Diego put a hand up in front of his face, a feeble attempt at stopping the machine gun like questioning. “Man, calm down.” He slowly turned, peered slightly over his shoulder and when the guard had cleared the vicinity spoke in a low whisper. “I need your help.” He peered around as well, completely confused as to who or what they were trying to avoid, and whispered back. “With what? What the fuck is going on?” “There letting me out-“ “WHAT!?” Not a whisper, a yell in fact. “Keep it down,” Diego whispered harshly, barely avoiding smacking him across the face. “It’s some top secret project that the Feds need help with, but I don’t know everything yet.” “And they asked YOU for help?” “I guess I got a way in, I used to work there.” “But they know you never finish anything, right?” Diego smiled. “Not yet, it never, uhh, came up.” “So what do you need me for?” Diego glanced around one last time, and leaned a little closer. “I’m going to need your help, sooner or later.” He looked around the cell, holding his hands out at his sides. “Well, you know where to find me.”
