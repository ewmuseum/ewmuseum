Interrogation

15 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/1/2007 Reboot Jonathan Winters’ eyes begrudgingly flickered open and, for all the good it did him, his perplexed conscious thought snapped back into life. Ugh...what the fuck? He’d been placed in a chair, his hands sufficiently bound with a durable plastic restraint and his only company was the darkness that surrounded him. What the hell happened? He thought, feeling the uncertainty of panic grab hold of him. What’s going on here? Wait...his mind raced and his memories began to return at frustratingly slow pace. How long have I been here, like this? What about Brazil? What about PRIME? Have I missed the plane? Fuck... With a heightened sense of urgency and the majority of his faculties now fully at his disposal, he made a concentrated effort to unleash himself from the uncomfortable plastic manacles. But then, mid-struggle, the door of the room creaked open, prompting a reassuring array of light to flood the room. Winters squinted and was just barely able to make out a small silhouette figure, standing in the doorway. “Well, Mr. Winters,” the crackled voice began, “it appears that you’re already awake.” His eyes adapting now, Winters noted the elderly man glare his extracted pocket watch and raise an speculative eyebrow. “Impressive.” Winters glared at the man. I recognise him from somewhere, don‘t I? Then, as he deftly put a name to the face, the rest of his memories came flooding back to him, like one giant tidal wave of realisation. “Fuck!” Inevitability Winters hit the small red disconnect button of his mobile phone and simply stood there, dumfounded, for what later seemed like an eternity. Well there‘s a hell of a thing, he thought, slumping down into the comforting embrace of his brown leather sofa. It was his manager, Charity Manale, informing him of arguably the most important match he’d ever been involved in. A main event match. In Brazil. Against Nova. For the Universal Title. Do I even have a passport? It hadn’t been much of an issue before, he’d never intended to leave the country. He flicked open his mobile phone for the second time in as many minutes and punched in Aeryn’s number. He figured she’d probably want to hear the good news first hand and perhaps could even help him out on the passport situation. The noise came before he could even hit the last digit of her number. His heart stopped. What the fuck? At first he questioned what his brain told him, but when the noise repeated itself, he could no longer deny it. A gunshot, just outside of his apartment door. He extracted himself from his couch and swiftly stalked over toward the door. He took a deep breath and then opened it slightly, peering through the crack. Two men, wearing all black and carrying hand cannons. Nice. They’d just blasted a hole right through the head of the elderly African-American woman who lived at the end of the corridor and now they headed his way. A hit team, Winters needlessly surmised, shutting the door behind him and heading toward the window. So, they’d finally found him. About fucking time, Winters grimly concluded. Fucking amateurs. His illustrious employer, Cornelius Stakiri, had been under attack recently, within his boardroom, but mostly outside it. The landscape of organised crime hadn’t changed much in the last decade or so and Stakiri liked it that way. And why wouldn’t he? Cornelius Stakiri owned all of the New York territory and ruled it with an iron first. Apparently, someone didn’t like it and felt a changed was necessary. Thus far, there’d been a number of skirmishes here and there, but it was only a matter of time before it all turned into a full-out territory war. And ever since Stakiri’s right hand man, Miguel made a play for it all, a few weeks back, Winters’ employer had become somewhat paranoid. Hell, Stakiri even had Winters followed last week. Winters had become a big player in Stakiri’s outfit over the past few months and now the enemy had finally realised it. Now they’d come to nullify the problem, before he became a rather sizeable thorn in their side. He opened the window and climbed through it, onto the fire escape, just in time to see the black unmarked cars screech to a halt just outside his apartment building. Apparently, they had backup. Glancing backward, over his shoulder to make sure he wasn’t being followed, he set foot upon the concrete roof. However, upon doing so, Winters heard heavy breathing and whirled around on the sport, to be confronted by at least ten, combat ready individuals, wearing black military-styled body armour and opaque head gear. Fuck! He silently cursed, angry with himself. It was a trap and in his everlasting arrogance, he didn’t even see it coming. The unmarked cars were simply to roust him from his apartment, and undoubtedly provide much needed back up for this strike team, and the roof was the only real place he could go. Or was it? His eyes shifted to the building just across from him and noted its fire escape in close proximity. The jump was lengthy to say the least, but he could make it. What other fucking choice do I have? He turned, ready for the necessary run up, but before his legs could establish momentum the strike force were upon him. The first of them wrapped himself around his right arm, putting his entire bodyweight behind it, desperately trying to drag Winters to the ground. It didn’t work. Winters struck his attacker on the top of his protective helmet, with a fierce axe handle blow and he simply withered to the floor like a paralysed snake. Out of the corner of his eye, he noted the second attacker priming himself for some kind of half-assed flying tackle. As ever, Winters timing was perfect. Taking a step backward as the masked fool pounced, he avoided the moron with metres to spare and the attacker propelling himself to the concrete floor with a rather audible thud. The obvious leader of the strike force, stood at the back of the group, watching the battle from a distance. When the second man fell, he shook his head with disgust and pushed his way to the front of the crowd. “Jonathan Winters,” the individual said, announcing his presence. Winters turned to face the man and as he did, the leader of the strike force raised the visor of his helmet, unafraid. The stone etched face of numerous bad experiences stared right at him. It was Rune fucking Wallasey. One of Cornelius Stakiri’s most trusted men. He grinned a toothy grin and revelled at the shocked expression deeply engraved into Winters face. “What the fuck?” Winters questioned. Then the realisation sunk in and it all made sense to him now. “Fucking Stakiri...” It wasn’t the enemy that’d come after him, it was Stakiri. His paranoia had obviously reached optimum levels and presumably he thought Winters had been compromised by the enemy. What a fucking idiot! “You’re lucky the old man wants you alive, Winters,” Rune told him, “or you would’ve been dead already.” Winters glared at the two unconscious men at his feet and shook his head. “Doubtful.” Rune nodded an agreeable response. “Guess I can’t argue with results,” he began, replacing his visor in one smooth motion. “But neither will Stakiri, when I take you in.” Winters dropped the first with an open palm strike against the smooth surface of the helmet, sending the poor fool sprawling backward over the prone body of his first victim. The second was dispatched with a rather vicious yakuza kick to the chest, which almost knocked that poor bastard over the side of the building. However, the aggressive movement forced Winters out of position and allowed two more of the attackers the window of opportunity to strike. They leapt upon him and pinned him to the ground, despite his struggling. Rune didn’t allow Winters any time to retaliate, quickly unsheathing a rather bizarre electric cattle prod device from his belt. Rune marvelled at it for a second and allowed a few seconds for Winters to see what was coming. “This little baby has enough voltage to take down a horse. I told Stakiri it would be overkill for a little punk like you, but he insisted.” The cattle prod loomed closer. Winters struggling became more desperate, but couldn’t hope to prevent the electric poke of the device connect with his gut. Everything faded to black. Questions Winters only knew him as Talbot, the name Stakiri had once assigned him when Winters asked the question, but he never really expected it to be his real name. Stakiri only ever used this sick fuck to crack his most difficult opposition, but understandably, Winters chose not to bask in such an accolade; his ego was already big enough. The elderly man glared at Winters speculatively, with his one remaining eye, keeping a safe distance and leaning heavily upon his chromed metal cane. Winters noted that Talbot’s black suit seemed to be pointlessly stringent about his entire body and he suspected that the sick bastard wouldn’t even need a cane, if he wore that actually fit him. He mentioned the same thing to Aeryn once, in passing, but she told him he did purposefully, in order to buy penance for his sins. At the time Winters didn’t accept the explanation, but now, confronted with the old vulture, it started to make sense. Talbot shot him a shark-like grin. “Well, Mr. Winters, it seems that we finally meet. In each of my many business dealings with Mr. Stakiri, he’s always spoken very highly of you,” he told him. “I must admit to being very impressed.” Winters simply glared at him. What does this guy want to do? Torture me, or suck my dick? “Mr. Stakiri also told me that you’re a man of few words, so let try me to reciprocate in this the best I can,” Talbot said. “I’ve been sent here to siphon every last shred of information out of you regarding Mr. Stakiri’s mysterious opposition and your role in all of this. Be smart, Mr. Winters. Cooperate and make this easier on both of us.” Yeah? Really? Fuck you! He silently responded, desperately trying to prise himself free from his plastic shackles. Talbot sighed, noting Winters struggle for freedom. “Now, now, Mr. Winters,” he warned, “we can’t have that.” With this, Two heavily built individuals wearing expensive black suits stormed the room and stood at either side of him, clamping meaty hands upon each shoulder. “So,” Talbot began anew, his shark-like grin increasing, “will you cooperate with me?” Winters fiery glare made even Talbot squirm on the spot, but quickly the torturer righted himself. Winters grinned. “Now listen to my counter offer,” Winters began, staring at each of the three in turn. “End this farce now, and I won’t staple your testicles to your forehead.” Talbot chuckled in response and his two goons joined in. That’s right, fucko, laugh it off. Don’t take me seriously. See where that fucking gets you. “A stubborn one,” the torturer said, “my favourite.” Talbot retrieved a chair from just beyond the doorway and clunked it down upon the bare metal floor, just a few metres away from Winters. He sat opposite him and rested cane upon his lap. “From what information I have been provided for this case, I have managed to extract only two things in this life you care about.” Winters remained silent as, from within his jacket, Talbot produced an old PRIME publicity photograph of Winters in his full wrestling gear. “First, your superlative wrestling career,” he told him, his grin more vicious than ever. “I’m not a big fan of the sport myself, too much showboating these days for my liking. But then, it doesn’t really matter whether I like it or not, does it?” Presumably from the same pocket in which the photograph emerged, he then extracted a sizeable blade and presented it for Winters to see. “I know you have a match in Brazil this week and I know it’s for their most coveted title. So the question is, how much do you really want that title shot, Mr. Winters?” Fuck! ***** Several nasty incisions later, Talbot wiped the bloodied blade clean with a previously pristine white handkerchief. Winters hadn’t said a word and wouldn’t, even if he knew anything that be of any valuable to the butcher. He did it out of principle and, of course, the overwhelming desire to piss Stakiri off beyond belief. The pain was nothing new. Winters had felt a thousand times worse from what of his life he could remember and so a few random cuts were of no concern to him. He figured Talbot had realised as much. “Well, Mr. Winters,” the elderly man began, “it seems you care very little about your career in the squared circle.” Talbot sheathed the blade. “It seems, a less gentlemanly method is necessary. I told you I know the only two things you care about. The first was yout wrestling career and that failed. The second, I’m sorry to say, is Aeryn.” Winters raised his head to establish eye contact with his tormentor, but remained ever silent despite it all. Talbot produced a toothy grin. He had Winters by the balls and he knew it. “You should know Mr. Winters I’m not one to make idle threats. Allow me to demonstrate” Talbot told him, gesturing toward the wall on his right. The wall became transparent almost immediately and presumably in next room over, Aeryn sat in much the same position as he. Winters heart sank and upon seeing this, Talbot’s smile widened. Winters lowered his head in shame at the fact he could be gotten at in such an easy manner and muttered something inaudible under his breath. Talbot raised a speculative eyebrow and leaned in closer. “What did you say?” “I said ‘big mistake,’” Winters told him, a half-grin perched on his face. Quickly he propelled his head forward at a considerable speed, planting it squarely upon Talbot’s nose, which effectively exploded, sending blood spraying in all directions like a sprinkler system. The thugs at either side of him reacted too slow to prevent the attack, but fully intended to make him pay for it nevertheless. The one to his right landed a particular powerful blow to the side of his head, sending him sprawling to the floor with enough velocity to break the chair. Now free, Winters shook off the blow and quickly got to his feet, hands still tied to his back. Before the other thug could stop him, Winters charged at the one who’d hit him, shoulder first, crashing him into the wall with a sick thud of broken bones. By this time, the other thug had composed himself enough to attempt another sizeable punch, but Winters proved quicker, propelling himself up into the air and landing both feet upon the much larger man’s chest, sending him crashing into the wall with an equally sickening thug. Winters got to his feet again and muttered, “Big fucking mistake.” He then went about making sure the three of them would remember him for the rest of their pathetically short lives. Defiance With Talbot’s battered and bruised body casually slung over his shoulder, Winters marched to Stakiri’s office with purpose. The office staff gawked at him as he went by, whereas the janitor simply shook his head at the trail of blood that followed him. Winters kicked the office door in and found Stakiri in the middle of a very important board meeting. The other board members quickly rose to their feet, startled, but Stakiri simply sighed and leaned back in his chair. “A bit of a dramatic entrance, don’t you think?” Stakiri said, desperately trying to mask his disappointment and possibly fear behind a weak smile. Winter hurled the body of Talbot onto Stakiri’s desk, almost smashing it’s delicate French glass structure. The other members of the board muttered a few obscenities under their breaths as the full damaged done to the body truly became apparent. Winters made eye contact with Stakiri, to show his employer just how much shit he was in. “Next time you want information from me, Stakiri, ask me. Next time you fuck me about, I‘ll gut you like a fucking fish.” A wave of relief visibly washed over Stakiri’s face and Winters simply turned to leave. “Where the hell are you going?” Winters shot him a fiery glare over his shoulder. “To find Rune. He and I have a little...business.” “Fuck,” Stakiri exclaimed, massaging his forehead forcefully. “I liked him too.” Winters continued and, as he reached the elevator, just beyond Stakiri’s office, at the end of the hall, he heard a soft yelp emanate from the office, which was promptly followed by, “What the fuck’s that stapled to his forehead?” Winters couldn’t contain his grin.
