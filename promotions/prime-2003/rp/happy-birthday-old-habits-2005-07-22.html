Date: July 22 Intense. Insane. Iconic. As a person, Aimz is unlike the majority of her peers. She appeals more to the Hollywood style of celebrity than even 'Hollywood' Hulk Hogan. She attends more MTV Awards than The Rock, and probably wouldn't look to shabby in a Tarantino film. If you approach her, you're more likely to get a balking opinion of the latest White Stripes album than any insight into the stereotyped world of professional wrestling but, for whatever reason, you just can't stop listening. At barely five feet five inches tall, the notoriously violent and habit-riddled redhead lets her voice propell her, weighing in at a hefty Canadian tonne when it comes to anything from punk rock to politics. A little familliar? Of course. But instead of acting out your usual Angelina Jolie or Avril Lavigne, this young lady's fists are possibly the only thing more potent than her outlook on the world. She entered wrestling via the wide world of street fighting, enticed by one of New York's underground not-so-prettyboys when she met Kellen Kinkade while attending art school there. But don't worry, she was already well on her way with drug and alcohol abuse before meeting Kinkade - far be it for Amelia Campbell to be influenced by a man. From a less-than-successful run on the underground fight circuit, she became known as a little girl with a propensity for pain. Truer than her unbreakable spirit is Amy's body - tattooed and scarred, but able to take punishment that even the hardest drill sergent would cringe at. She's fared several hardcore titles, even worldwide gold in her earlier days, and remains able to compete at any level she wishes. Her performance depends on the day, but her resilliance is a constant. At 22 years old, you wouldn't expect the woman to have a divorce, adopted son and so many trysts under her belt, but Amy Campbell's personal life has always proven to be as interesting as the twists and turns she provides in the wrestling ring. In fact, not even this article - or an entire magazine, for that matter - could rightfully serve as a summary of her tumultous existance... - Excerpt from Rolling Stone, July 2005. CHAPTER One: It's awake, not a funeral.post-ReVolution...It was that euphoric awakening, occuring amidst the alarm sounding - ringing out a new day from a tiny clock in a massive hotel made to intimidate masses and seduce corporate greed with its high-speed wireless connectivity. The fact that she didn't want to think about their end at all said far more than she wanted it to. Something was very, very wrong, and Amy's thoughts instantly shot to her family. ''Is everyone okay?!'' ''Everyone... they're fine,'' Paula sniffled, ''I just... Amy...'' ''Let's get these bags inside, then we'll talk.'' Within a minute of shuffling the bags in through the door, Amy and her second-oldest sister were on her couch - Paula having started to gain composure, but it was obvious that there wasn't any smiles in her future. She had a big chip on her shoulder, it seemed... ''I hate asking this when you've just moved in,'' she began, ''But I, uhh... I need somewhere to stay for a while.'' Instead of her mother or father, Amy instantly zeroed in on Paula's husband - the normally nice guy, an only child with some hard partying habits but a seemingly endless love of her sister. ''If Seann touched you, I'll kill hi--'' ''No. No! We're just having some rough times. He needs a little... well, needs a little help with his selfishness and everything you already know about. You know I normally wouldn't just pop in and ask this, but mom's stressed out enough dealing with John.'' She'd referenced their youngest of five brothers, John - a 16 year old with the worst of attitudes and a propensity for experimentation. For parents rounding sixty, that was a practical no-fly zone, but he remained at home with them. Amy knew what he was like to deal with, and it made her almost regret her teenaged rebellions. ''You can stay here for as long as you need,'' she blindly offered, ''Just... I dunno. Are you okay?'' ''I'm alive, that's all I need right now.'' ''You sound like me.'' ''And you look like death warmed over,'' Paula snapped, and the 29 year-old's maternal instincts kicked in, ''Are you eating properly?'' ''Define 'proper'. I have no appetite half of the time. It's like that, getting off of the dr-- dealing with myself.'' The redhead corrected herself instantly, not wanting to bring such a heavy topic into the already mud-thick situation, ''You know what I mean.'' ''I watch you out there... like, on TV and shit,'' her sister noted, ''And you haven't looked quite right. You never tell me what's up on the phone, but you always hear my problems... as long as I can stay here, we're gonna ignore my issues and focus on you. 'Kay?'' ''Uhh...'' Amy was taken back by the sudden focus and caring. She'd heard all of the problems between her older sister and Seann, her husband, but Paula never heard about AMY'S issues. Still, the redhead was hesitant to share. ''Well... for today, I guess.'' ''And I'm sorry this is so sudden. Normally, you know I wouldn't a--'' ''Don't worry about it...'' I can't live with ANYBODY, much less my fucking older sister! ''...It won't be a problem,'' she lied, and hoped it wasn't so obvious. Amy had a big heart, but no patience... this would be a real test. Fast forward an hour. Paula had moved in quickly, setting up a small army of blankets and pillows on the couch Amy had brought into the new apartment not even a day before, and no sooner did she get comfortable that she had Amy wrung into a conversation about wrestling, of all things. ''So you're gonna kick this Easton guy's ass? Seann already has the show ordered! I don't give a fuck if he wants me there or not, I'm gonna go home to watch my little sister. I mean, you rip that guy a new asshole like every damn day!'' Paula laughed, ''He's got it comin' to him, just don't fuck up like...'' ''Like I usually do?'' ''I didn't say th---'' ''You were about to.'' ''Don't be tough like this, Ames.'' she snapped, ''I was just gonna say... y'know, you haven't had your head in it lately. Even mom can tell, and she watches as little as she can. After that last big match, you kinda had your ass handed to you by that tall bitch. And I'm saying this with love, as your sister...'' Hearing the reference to you-know-who, Amy stood up and nearly flipped herself over the coffee table, ''Can we NOT mention her?! FOR TWO SECONDS!'' ''Fine, but can we talk about this week and you not getting yourself killed?'' ''How about you stop looking out for me, let's concern ourselves with the fact that... I dunno... you're GETTING DIVORCED?! For some reason, I find that a little bigger a problem than prettyboy Ellis Easton.'' Yeah, let's deal with some other problems...
