An Old Friend: Part One

2 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.02.08 “I can’t believe you dragged me here.” Tyler said as he and Aaron walked along a sidewalk. The air was dark and crisp on a Pennsylvania evening. The former PRIME Tag Team Champions walked along the downtown area of their hometown making their way towards a former hot spot of theirs. “Oh come on. We haven’t been here since you turned twenty-one. It’ll be fun.” Aaron replied. The two of them turned a corner and approached a dimly lit plate glass window with neon signs lighting the pavement. They stopped momentarily a few steps from the door as Aaron reached up and straightened his collar. “Yeah and it was the worst night of my life.” Tyler said with a laugh. Aaron joined his laughter for a moment and then looked up to the sign on the building. “Fools.” The sign was filled with bright colors and an overly exaggerated clown’s face. Aaron sighed and reached into his pocket and pulled out a small amount of cash and counted it to himself. “You think a hundred bucks is enough to get obliterated on?” He asked. “With how you hold your alcohol you could put eighty of that back in the bank.” Tyler replied. Aaron punched him in the shoulder and turned to walk towards the door. Tyler followed, rubbing his shoulder. Aaron stepped inside and walked past the burly man standing at the door and right up to the counter. He looked around as he waited for the bartender to come up. The watering hole had a lot of old time firefighter gear hung on the walls. One of the walls was filled with helmet shields from all of the departments in the area. Just as Aaron was focusing in on them he felt a tap on the shoulder. “Dammit Tyler. Gimme a minute to get a drink.” He said as he swatted the hand away. The hand came back down and grabbed his shoulder. Aaron turned around abruptly and started to say something but was stopped short as he came face to chest with the burly bouncer. He offered a small smirk but didn’t get one in return. “Can I see some I.D. please?” the bouncer asked. Aaron narrowed his brow and looked up to the man. “Some I.D?” He replied. The bouncer nodded and folded his arms. Aaron’s eyes focused on the biceps in front of him. They were a minimum of twelve inches around. “I am a former PRIME Tag Team Champion and feel insulted. Sir.” Aaron stuttered. It was the bouncer’s turn to narrow his own brow and look down upon Aaron. “I don’t know what the hell a PRIME is and don’t really give a damn. Show me some I.D. or else you’re going to be back on that sidewalk. And it won’t be on your feet.” He barked. Aaron’s hand slowly moved to his back pocket and removed his wallet. The bouncer snatched it from his hands and opened it. He looked over the driver’s license carefully and then handed it back. “You check out. But don’t be throwing that attitude around here anymore.” Aaron just nodded and turned back to the bar where the bartender was finally waiting. Aaron ordered up a Sam Adams and a shot of tequila to drown away his bad start of the night. As he paid for his drinks Tyler walked up beside him. “Hey man. Isn’t that Megan Weston?” Tyler said as he pointed across the room. Aaron turned and his eyes fell onto a beautiful brunette sitting at a table with a few other girls. He nodded as he thought back to their days in high school. He had always thought she was attractive but never made a move while in school. He really wanted to turn a bad night into a good one and started to walk over. “She’s looking good. Better than she did in school. I think I’m going to go talk to her.” Tyler said. Tyler’s words brought Aaron to a stop. He spun around and looked toward his partner. “Dude. You know I had a thing for her in high school.” Aaron objected. “So did I.” Tyler retorted. The two came together and began a heated yet childish exchange of “No I did” that lasted nearly thirty seconds. They finally stopped as Aaron laid his hand upon Tyler’s shoulder. “All right then. We’ll let her decide. You go talk to her and then I will. We’ll see just who gets more out of it.” Aaron proposed. Tyler nodded in agreement and pointed towards the bar where Aaron’s drinks were waiting. Aaron smiled and retreated grabbing his shot and downing it. He looked across the room and watched as Megan’s face lit up as Tyler approached. She stood and gave him a big hug. As the conversation went from a reunion to a little bit too friendly Aaron turned unable to watch. He took a big swig out of his beer and ordered another. By the time it arrived he was finished with his first. By the time he was three deep, Tyler returned waving a piece of paper. “The fuck is that?” Aaron asked. “Her number. Let’s see what you’ve got.” Tyler said with a smile. Tyler pushed past his partner and ordered up a beer of his own and watched over his shoulder as Aaron approached Megan. Quickly enough the bartender returned with Tyler’s drink and he took a swig. When he turned he couldn’t believe what he saw. Megan was walking towards him holding her small purse and waving to her friends. Just a few feet behind her was Aaron. He had the biggest shit-eating grin on his face one could imagine. He waved to Tyler as he passed and headed toward the door. Before he could exit, Tyler grabbed his arm and pulled him back. “You know she’s something special man. You do anything to hurt her…” Tyler trailed off. “And you’ll do what?” Aaron asked. Tyler stood in silence for a moment with no other words. Aaron smiled again and walked away and out the door to rendezvous with his old friend. Tyler snatched his beer from the counter and downed what was left before ordering another.
