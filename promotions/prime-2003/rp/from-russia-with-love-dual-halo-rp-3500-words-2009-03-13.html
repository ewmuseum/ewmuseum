From Russia with Love (Dual Halo RP/<3500 Words)

13 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, March 13th, 2009 Friday, March 13th, 2009 Olympic Stadium, Moscow, Russia Culture Shock, Night One Colby Korver sits back in his steel folding chair and slowly brings a Camel Turkish Silver up to his lips. With a flick of his right thumb, he brings the dormant black lighter to life, sparking the cigarette end and establishing a bright cylindrical glow. Inhaling deeply, Korver pauses for just a moment before letting the excess smoke escape through his nostrils. Tomorrow will mark the one year anniversary of Delta Upsilon Iota’s arrival here in PRIME. Last year, Colby Korver and Hank Cobb made their respective debuts at Culture Shock, Night Two, in the Dual Halo match. This year, they will once again both take part in PRIME’s most famous match, stepping into the ring with thirty-eight other of the best the wrestling world has to offer. This year’s Night One, however, will be very, very different. Last year, Cobb and Korver used the first half of the Culture Shock experience to experience life backstage, meeting and greeting both active roster and PRIME crew alike. This year, however, they are scheduled for their very first pay per view tag team title defense, as they will team with Duke Williams to take on all three members of Team VIAGRA in a six person tag match for the PRIME Tag Team Titles. It took the frat boys months and the addition of one grizzled old hoss to reach championship gold. But it will only take three seconds to lose it. And with that very moment of judgment almost at hand, we find Colby Korver awaiting his partner’s response to the question at hand. "So what’s it gonna be, big guy? Like that sand bagging son of a bitch Marc Anthony, I need to know." Across the locker room, bowing the bench upon which he sits like a tiger would a toothpick, Hank Cobb leans forward and deposits a mouthful of imported Skoal into his brand new and soon to be exported CSKA (Central Sports Army Club) Moscow dip cup. "That’s easy. I’d kill Ruby. I’d marry Mary-Lynn Mayweather. And I’d screw Madison." Wearing a pair of worn camouflage shorts and a vintage The Highwaymen brown tee shirt, Hank Cobb leans back against the wooden locker frame and presents his partner with his own trio of possibilities. "Ok, this is a good one. Lisa Tyler, Lindsay Troy…and Dixie Normas." Korver cocks his head in deep consideration, before releasing his personal assessment. "I’d have to marry Lisa Tyler, strictly for the early retirement package. Then, I think I’d have to kill Ms. Normas based solely on ridiculousness of name." "So you’re fixin’ to throw it in the Queen of the Ring? I ain’t so sure your boy Rayne’s gonna be too happy about that." Korver brushes some cigarette ash from his black sweat pants before responding in turn. "Might not be, but if I can keep your dirty little Mr. Cozen secret then you can surely keep mine vaulted up in that fat ass of yours as well." "I ‘reckon so." "So, give it to me straight, is the shirt too much or what? Because personally, I think they’re gonna love it." Korver turns and reaches down into the duffel bag at his side, producing a bright red tee shirt with a large white sickle and hammer in the middle and "Party’s Over" written across it in black. "Brother, I think that’s one hell of a fashion statement. I only wish I had thought of it myself." "So big guy, you ready for this or what?" "Son, if you think an albino Mr. T and a Nintendo fan boy are going to take this title from me, you must be in violation of every one of Miss Tyler’s testing policies." "I’m not asking if you’re ready to kick ass tonight. I’m asking if you’re reading for everything that comes with it. We’re champions now, Hank. It took us almost an entire year, but we reached the top when you and Duke took those titles at King of Kings. Now I finally have a chance to stand with you and help keep them. And I can tell you beyond a shadow of a doubt." Korver leans forward in his chair, dropping his usual sophomoric antics and looking his partner square in the eyes. "I am ready." "You sure you didn’t give up too damn much to get here, boss?" "You let me worry about that. You just worry about keeping that pretty little belt you got there." "We got there, Korv. We got there."
