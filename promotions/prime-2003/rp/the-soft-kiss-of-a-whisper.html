The Soft Kiss Of A Whisper…

undated

Date: Two Days Before Joining PRIME The Soft Kiss Of A Whisper… Token Weed slowly opened the door to his apartment, his soft black leather couch sat silently before him. It seemed as if his eyes were trained on making it to the couch; as he slammed the door, he slowly paced over to it and laid down. Making it a point to throw the bag over the couch. go It came slowly, like it was an intergalactic transmission from across the universe, managing to make it’s way to our planet to remind us that there are other possibilities in life that aren’t our own. The whisper slowly entered his fragile little mind, making a point to pay no mind that he didn’t want it there. The brain seemed to almost smile as the whisper slowly went inside… Token slowly looked around the room, it was happening again to him and he knew it, he knew exactly what all this meant. Flashbacks, drug induced flashbacks. He usually only had these after being tempted by them, but this one seemed stronger than usual. It brought him back to a time in his life he wanted to forget… This simple little sound, sounded so much like a sound he had long forgotten. go It tickled the hair follicles of his nasal cavity as it slowly slid it’s way up, past the gunk and the grime, finally reaching just behind his eyes. Slowly it made it’s way to his brain and gave a soft kiss, sending a sensation up his spine. Token shook his head to the side, he didn’t want these anymore. He didn’t want this life of constantly dealing with these flashbacks… It’d taken him long enough to kick the habit, but to deal with the constant torment the illegal substances gave him… It could kill him. He sighed, he knew it was only going to get worse. He could feel the pain coursing through his veins already, as they craved the ever so warm thrust of a needle. They pulsated with every beat of his heart, seemingly screaming at Sean Williams to feed them. Silently Sean refused, he knew in his heart that he didn’t want that life anymore… He’d never want that life again. He could feel the beads of sweet slowly begin to form across his forehead. His muscles began to contort and contract… He sighed he knew what would come next. His eyes began to water as he felt his thighs cramp up, the cooling sensation coming over him was obvious as he screamed out in pain. The withdrawal would always be there, and he knew it always would. But for once the devil inside of him smiled as he reached over and picked up the phone, dialing quickly he made the call. He knew what he was doing, but it was like an out of body experience. He seemed to watch as his ghostly white hand picked up the receiver, the cold plastic seemed to have a calming effect on him as he slowly dialed the number. His veins had seemed to stop the pulsation, they seemed to know it would all be over soon enough. He stared at the coffee table, and watched as his body overpowered his mind as it stared at it. He wanted to get up, to leave this place but alas he couldn’t. His mind seemed weak, like he was at a total and complete stand still with everything around him. The proverbial hamster on the wheel stopped running… He stared at the table for what seemed like a few seconds, but when he heard the knock at the door he knew it had been for at least fifteen minutes. He stepped up, once again not controlling his body. Approaching the door he opened it, the conversation was brief and the exchange of a small wad of money for the drugs he so desired seemed reasonable enough as he slammed the door shut. He through himself down on the couch, the cramps in his thighs continuing to pain him as he fumbled with the plastic camp that was on the end of the syringe. Finally pulling it off he held it in one hand, while in the other he wrapped a black belt around his bicep… He stared at it, knowing that this was it. He smacked the inside of his elbow and once the vein appeared, he jammed the needle down into the vein following it up with the jamming down of the plunger injecting the heroin into his body. He tossed the empty syringe down onto the table, as the drug started working almost instantly… The warm sensation that flushed over him was almost like complete and total ecstasy. His arms and legs both grew heavy as he smiled. He shut his eyes for a moment. Shame it felt like an eternity. --------------------- He thought he died. It was like a glimpse into hell as he felt the darkness engulf him. The slow red glow that began in nothingness smiled at him, almost a welcoming smile. He could see the ugly inside of it, the blackness that emanated from the red glow. Slowly the one thing he didn’t want to see seemed to appear out of the mist. Go Token glared at him. “So it was you who was calling me?” Token asked, as a wind seemingly kicked up in the land of nothing sending his blonde and blue locks flailing in the wind. My Knight it’s been a long time. The darkness said to Token as he looked confused. This seemed like déjà vu to him but he had a feeling he was terribly wrong. “What are you talking about?” Token asked as he continued to look around in the darkness. He knew something was odd about this situation but he couldn’t tell what. “You Forget so soon my knight?” The term knight confused him even more as he now could feel the voice echoing. “Why do you call me your knight? I know nothing of this,” he stated as he glared into the darkness, slowly he could see large brown stone walls appearing. “So you do forget… I’ll have to fix that.” Token’s mouth dropped open in pain as a blinding white light seemingly entered his mouth, he began to shake… He could feel the light seemingly pulsing through his veins as finally he seemingly awoke. ------------------------------- It seemed like it was all a dream… He glanced at the clock noticing the letters read something a bit odd. 6 pm, and he had a feeling like he had to be somewhere… But where? PRIME, Airplane, Six Thirty The Black knight nodded. He instantly began to pack his bags, every single thing he could find… That would be of use. He reached into the closet and grabbed two pairs of blue jeans, six t-shirts, a bullet proof vest, three knives and six guns. Yes kids, six guns. Token Weed headed to the door… and he knew from that moment onwards his life was never going to be the same again.
