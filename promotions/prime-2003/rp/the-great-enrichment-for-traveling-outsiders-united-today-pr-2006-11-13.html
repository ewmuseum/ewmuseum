The Great Enrichment for Traveling Outsiders United Today Program

13 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/13/06 “The test of courage comes when we are in the minority. The test of tolerance comes when we are in the majority.” – Ralph W. Sockman “Tolerance is the virtue of man without convictions.” –G.K. Chesterson “In the practice of tolerance, one’s enemy is his best teacher.” –Dalai Llama ---- The American flag was waving proudly at full mast on a lovely morning out in the middle of a randomly selected park. In the background, the pooled laughter and cheering of kids fully enjoying their youth were heard. The sun’s perpetual rays of light bombarded the fresh dew still remaining on the grass, creating a bright scene to be witnessed by passers-by. A form could be made out in the middle of the aforementioned scenery, most noticeable by a black business suit, sunglasses, and a million-dollar smile whose glimmering shine rivaled that of the yellow orb above. He takes one look over his shoulder to the families off in the distance, then back to the camera, hands remaining in the pocket of his coat. Soon, the visage of one Sonny Silver was recognizable. “Greetings and salutations, folks, and thank you for joining me today. Allow me to pose you a question, dear viewer. What do you think when you hear the word ‘American’? What comes to your mind? Patriotism. Opportunity. The name for a citizen currently taking residence within these United States of America.” He walks closer to the flagpole as the camera pans above, giving the viewer a visage of the very representation of the country, stars, stripes, and all. “I’ll tell you what it stands for. Unity. Freedom. Tolerance for your fellow man.” Sonny nods to the playground. “These children behind me come from all walks of life; Caucasian, African-American, Asian, Spanish, and so forth. But there’s one thing that unites them all: America. Behind me is the picture perfect scene of that oh-so-delightful sense of unity. People coming together as one under the banner of the good ol’ USA.” Once more, our gaze remains fixated on Sonny as he walks to a strategically placed chart propped up on a board in front of the playground. He flips to a picture of the first page and smiles, the pride of America filling his heart. “Ladies and gentleman, this is what it’s all about here: our proud American colors. We have your whites… representing those of the native Caucasian class blending beautifully with the reds – those being all the other ethnic colors of the rainbow – and topped off with the blue… the ocean around our country or something. But there are some people who are more than willing to take everything that we hold sacred and spit upon it: “I want you people to take a good, long, hard look at this man. His atrocious behavior is very fitting of the word ‘man.’ He is a racist. A bigot. A homophobe. A monster. This isn’t character assassination, either. This is just fact. Shawn Stewart is a racist living right in the middle of some squalid prejudice-filled hole in the middle of Kentucky.” He flips the page once more on the chart book. “On his off-hours when he isn’t busy practicing his martial artistry upon the likes of poor defenseless foreigners, he’s going back to his home coming up with new and exciting ways to besmirch the good name of today’s immigrants that seek solace in this great country. He’s sitting here coming up with all kinds of jokes that do nothing more than make me sick…” At this point, Sonny finds himself burying his face in his hands, looking like he’s fighting back tears to say what he’s about to say. “What’s the only positive think about homosexuals? HIV. Why are there no Mexicans in Star Trek? Because there’s no lawns in outer space. And the most heinous of them all… What’s the difference between a Jew and a canoe? Eventually, the canoe’s still going to tip.” But through all the pain and anguish, a ray of hope washes away the former feelings and strengthens the resolve of the Chairman of PRIME. “Well, I’ll tell you something, Mr. Racist Bigot With Your Moronic And Juvenile Jokes. I’ve come up with the solution to you and your little KKK members spreading that stupid propaganda. I’m going to show you a little initiative dedicated to helping rebuild everything that people like you destroy with your filth and lies.” One more time, he flips the page to unveil his greatest idea to the world. “Ladies and gentlemen, I give to you the Great Enrichment for Traveling Outsiders United Today program. For years, the people who immigrate to this country have been shunned, kicked to the curb, and generally treated like they’re the lowest common denominator. Well, I’m here to tell you that on this day… TODAY… this is going to stop. Immigrants of this country, unite today and fight back against the tyrannical oppression that guys like Shawn Stewart threaten you with every single day of your lives!” From off-camera, The Chairman of PRIME is handed a megaphone. He turns that sucker all the way up and turns his head to yell at the children and their parents in the background. “LADIES AND GENTLEMAN, I, MR. SILVER, CHAIRMAN OF PRIME, DECLARE IT MORNING AGAIN IN THESE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA! LITTLE PABLO AND SHANIQUA CAN LIVE LIFE JUST LIKE THE REST OF US! YOU TELL PABLO THAT IF HE WANTS TO CLEAN MY TOILETS AND TRIM THE GARDEN WHILE I’M AT WORK, THAT’S HIS CHOICE TO DO SO AND NO RACIST WILL TAKE THAT AWAY FROM HIM! AND YOU TELL SHANIQUA THAT SHE CAN MAKE ALL THE MAURY POVICH APPERANCES THAT SHE WANTS IF THAT’S WHAT IT’S GOING TO TAKE TO FIND OUT WHO HER BABY’S DADDY IS!” The camera pans over to a young married Asian couple who usually frequent this park, filling out their daily routine by keeping themselves fit via a good jog. Sonny turns to them as well. “YOU TWO! SUPPORT THE GREAT ENRICHMENT FOR TRAVELING OUTSIDERS UNITED TODAY! YOU ASK JOHNNY BIGOT WHO THE HELL ELSE IS GOING TO BE SUPPLYING THE WORLD WITH AN ENDLESS SUPPLY OF FORTUNE COOKIES AND CHOW MEIN!” They take off running faster when they see this strange man yelling in their direction. He puts down the megaphone and turns back to the camera, proud of the work he’s accomplished in the last two minutes. “See? Those two fine upstanding citizens are off spreading the good word of my program. People who are deemed ‘inferior’ just because of the color of their skin, what kind of clothing the wear, how much money they covet and how many matzo balls they eat is just too much, folks.” He gestures back to the dubious title of his new program and nods accordingly. “Shawn Stewart is a man that goes around and believes he can make other races seem inferior. His moveset is comprised of signature techniques he claims to have gotten from beating up on you… the minority. Help me help you. Lend me the strength of the mighty Swiss people that lift weights. Give me the speed of the African-American who wrongly runs from police every day. Give me the smarts of an Asian and a Jewish person combined to outfight, outspeed, and outsmart the oppression that Shawn Stewart brings to you each and every week. Thank you. I’m Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME. Good night.”
