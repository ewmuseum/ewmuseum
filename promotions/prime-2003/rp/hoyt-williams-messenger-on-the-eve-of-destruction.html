Hoyt Williams: messenger on the eve of destruction.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th, Twenty alt Eight "I must write to you in enigmas, so that if my letter be intercepted by land or sea, he who shall read it may in no degree comprehend it. All things surround their King; they are, on account of Him, and He alone is the cause of good things, Second for the Seconds and Third for the Thirds." --Plato Man is a hammer in the tool belt of God. Does that shock you that we are nothing more then tools? Why? It shouldn’t. Think about a hammer swung wildly, it no doubt will destroy its environment and possibly the person swinging it. Now apply thought, reason, and direction, and the hammer becomes a tool of progress. I was conceived to help God re-focus the masses and get us back on the track of evolution. I have failed. Mans unregulated force has destroyed the environment, created criminals of the innocent, and protected the evil. For many years man was a hammer well directed by the words of God progressing toward the master plan. Now we are sheep with out a Sheppard, a wrestler with out a promoter, and souls with out salvation. Ultimately mans own selfishness rose up against the will of God. The bible spelled it all out and warned of a horrible and destructive future to those whom chose to ignore the words of the creator. Look around and you see people abusing their bodies, selfishly taking advantage of their brethren, and even homosexual escapades being allowed by the government. Don’t blame Hoyt Williams any longer, for I am not your savior but rather the messenger on the eve of destruction. I came and warned you through thought and humor in the tone of today’s words. You did not hear me nor did you respect me, and now hell will follow. Like a child that refuses to take a shower thumbing their nose at authority just for the sake of doing so all the while it’s not the authority that suffers but rather the child in his its stench, rash, and filth. Do you not see the end in your daily news papers? The terrorism in the name of a God?? The tornados, the earthquakes, the climate change, the cost of living, the shortage of food???? Are you really that blind or that ignorant that you refuse to heed my warnings? You laughed at Jesus too and in the end you hung him out to dry. Faded, jaded wrestling star is what you say, but you know I’m better then that. You know I could take you at any point through wit, wisdom, or writing. I choice not to because why save those who don’t seek salvation. You go to school to learn and read and write then they hand you a piece of paper and you stop seeking the light. You start on a path of self reliance and selfishness. Your comfort and your wealth and your happiness out weigh the importance of God, the love of country, your obligation to duty. They jeered me but you are the true villain. You are the true joke. You are the ignorant I am the informed. Now my days of PRIME are behind me and my path to tomorrow is closed. The world as viewed from the heavens has a giant going out of business sign on it. PRIME will continue on, and soon I will be nothing more then a name on a list of forgotten fallen faces. I loved you, and I’m sorry to go. Earth will be around for another generation, then it to will be like my memories and just fade away. A failed unfinished project on a shelf in the celestial garage of God. I spoke to him this morning and he has no plans to destroy you. He has no plans to bring about Armageddon as you’re not worth it. God is now given up on earth, and you all are left to rot. Good luck and God speed, HOYT WILLIAMS Pontiff of PRIME Savoir of Man God’s Champion (Hoyt posts the message on the PRIME message board before shutting down his computer. One more match then he will ascend to heaven and retire from the hell that is earth. He turns off the monitor and grabs a coat as its a bit chilly outside. He starts walking down the street kicking an old beer can down his Wrigleyville neighborhood sidewalk in Chicago.) “The onward march of the human race requires that the heights around it should blaze with noble and enduring lessons of courage. Deeds of daring dazzle history, and form one class of the guiding lights of man. They are the stars and coruscations from that great sea of electricity, the Force inherent in the people. To strive, to brave all risks, to perish, to persevere, to be true to one's self, to grapple body to body with destiny, to surprise defeat by the little terror it inspires, now to confront unrighteous power, now to defy intoxicated triumph--these are the examples that the nations need and the light that electrifies them.” --Albert Pike So this is the end, my only friend thee end. I remember when my mother was murdered she was nothing more then an innocent church secretary whom walked down the wrong street on the wrong night. Nobody will ever hug you like your mother did. You will understand these words once you are with out a mother. Your wife loves you but hugs can lead to sex and the impurity in that tarnishes the love of the hug. In marriage love is saved for sex diminishing the impact of Love. Your friends, your father, your grandparents can hug you but there is always a bit of an uncomfortable feeling to them. Are we hugging too long? Your children will be the closest but at some point they will become uncomfortable with the hug as they look to spread their wings. You can understand that as you get older you pushed away your mother just as I did mine. Push hard enough and well…. (A few tears fall from Hoyt’s steel grey eyes) The lesson of the story of the passing of my mother is one of love. Find it, seek it, grasp it, and respect it. Find it in God, find it family, and find it in country. Don’t down play anyone of those three words, as they are the universal pull that binds civilization, and separates us from the untamed and evil to the enlightened and wise. Love leads to pain, love leads to agony, and love leads to emptiness. Like any drug some question if the going up is worth the going down. Let me tell you my brethren, it is. Seek it in the final moments of existence and every moment up to that point. Life is to short with pending doom hanging over your head to be angry or unpleasant. (Hoyt smiles as a he watches a kid chasing after an ice cream truck.) My war with Killian was about faith and the faithless. A man who had lost love and was driven to an evil place void of light. Drawn by rage and anger the mind is easy to corrupt. He was the lesson of the fallacy of the unforgiving. He blamed God for the destruction of his wife and the ending of their love. He did not forgive and a man unforgiving is shackled by the chains of rage. If you can’t move on you hold on and that again stifles progress. When you are stagnant for too long your faith like any unpolished machine will start to rust and rot. Killian was a rotten man but not a lost soul. He needed a boost in the back to move beyond his broken down faith. He needed to move his anger and rage from God to another object. An object he could grow to forgive and move on. I of course was his angel sent to redirect his focus. He went on to refocus, reclaim his glory, and find salvation in faith. I saved many souls along the way from Rolo and Troy and their sexual feelings for each other to that other guy who really annoyed me. Look at Rolo now! Clearly no longer attached at the butt plug to Joey his partner. I am a miracle worker and magical magistrate riding on the magic carpet of mystical ministry. Olsig remember him? Or is he still around??? I don’t know he was nothing more then a nat on the natural skin that is me. I flicked him away as a lesson that God can and will reject. He doesn’t have to save nor does he have to destroy. Like human man God has free will and can decide to ignore. The child was a lost cause with no talent to take advantage of. If I would have spent my missionary mission on him those in real need who can help God progress toward the agenda would be shut out. Like Youngblood you remember him? Brandon Youngblood was the devil himself and the representation of evil. Nothing short of total destruction was the only option. To use your vernacular and educational level let me put it like this. Remember Roddy Piper in, “They Live”? You know he had those glasses that let him see the evil was living amongst us walking as us?? WELL I GOT THOSE DAMN GLASSES AND HE WAS ONE OF THE BAD GUYS!!! He was the ringleader in a gang of evil dwellers infesting the wrestling world reaching out to the masses. So I had to wobble in like John Goodman in Arachaphobia and battle him and his sneaky crawly friends with a big can of bad ass biblical bug spray. Oh yea, and I did so kicking ass left and right. HA HA HA HA (Hoyt laughs for a moment) Youngblood represented EGO, ENTITLMENT, and STUBBORNESS. The three destructive tools of Satan. If Youngblood had achieved the Universal title his level of entitlement would have been achieved boasting the ego and making him even more stubborn. He would have perpetrated that pungent shit amongst the people in putrid misery. But I being your humble savior, living legend, hall of fame inductee, Universal Champion, Gods Champion, 5 Star Champion, PTC Extreme Champion, pontiff of Prime, king of kings, HOYT WILLIAMS I could not allow that happen. There were also faces like Trashcan Man where in order to end his misery he had to meet his match. He was an example of redemption. He was a man who did not hate God, resent God, nor feel indifferent to God. He was a man who feared God. He needed to face God and feel his wrath. In a genius bit of reverse psychology that I’m know for back home in the heavens, I made it appear as I Hoyt Williams (not my real name as most of you have figure out by now) feared the Trashcan guy and not the other way around. I fed into his EGO and allowed him to let his guard down. Then when stripped of all security and defense I presented him with the face and voice of God. He was stunned to see his greatest fear up close and personal. Judged like a contender on American idol the three faces of God talked him down. God gave him some advice and sent him on his way ONE TWO THREE in the ring. After that he was a healed man and no longer set fires. Somewhere along the way I lost my voice and my power. I no longer was able to affect the unaffected and save those who sought saving. I couldn’t win a match or speak the gospel to the masses. For the times they are a-changin as the Jewish feller once sang. I have reported to God and he has decided to pull the plug on the plagued planet and move on. So mote it be. So one more match and one more soul to save. After the match I will come home and shoot myself in the head, wait three days, then rise to the heavens. You would think god would have an elevator or something but nope, that’s how it goes. Crucifix is the last match of Hoyt Williams SEE I do have a sense of humor in my symbolism. (Hoyt laughs almost to the point of tears.) Crucifix, the executioner who’s face is masked in the tradition of Rome. What a perfect fucking ending isn’t it? Lectori Salutem, A bene placito a capite ad calcem a Deo et Rege a cruce salus!! Aut disce aut discede avarus animus nullo satiatur lucro. Ora et labora, PRIME locus poenitentiae iunctis viribus pactum serva~! Ite, misse est! P.S. Inter nos, Isto pensitaris? The Creator =w= Afl Obesa cantavit
