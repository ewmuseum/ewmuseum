Date: ??? The scene opens in a NYC bar, we see Jimmy Bonafide sitting down at the bar table on a stool ordering himself one last drink before he goes home and calls it a night. The bartender finally comes back with his drink, and Bonafide passes him a tip… “Thank you sir.” “Yeah, no problem man.” “Hey, aren’t you that guy that saved that little girl from getting hurt?” “Yeah, that’s me.” “Hey, man that took a lot of balls what you did?” “I did what any human would do, who had kids. She needed help, and I was there too help her.” “Yeah, man. I totally understand. Is it true that you were awarded with a plaque of bravery while you was at the hospital, healing up?” “Yes, I received a plaque for my bravery, from the NYC police dept. I was honered.” While, Bonafide continues too talk to the bartender the door open to the bar, and in comes in D.U.I they walk up to the bar and sit on each side of The PosterBoy and begin to talk to him. “Hey, man…didn’t expect to find you here.” Colby say’s too Bonafide.. “Yeah, just having a couple of drinks before I turn in and call it a night. How about you guy’s, how was your night?” “Couldn’t be better, we just finish leaving the strip club.” “Yeah, and then we decided too stop by our favorite spot.” Hank Cobb say. “Sounds like an exciting day. So you guy’s ready for our match against Natas/Mega Job?” “Oh yeah, we are ready!” They both say together. “We showed Natas, not too mess with us.” Cobb screams out. “We sure did, but now he will be out for his revenge. Know we need to be on our A game and make sure we do not make any mistakes at all.” “Yeah, don’t worry man. We got this under control.” “Im not worried, it’s just a warning.” “Let’s have one more drink, before we call it a night and head back home.” “Yeah!” “Hey, bartender let me get 3 tequila shots. For me and my friends.” The bartender get’s them there shots and place each cup in front of them. They look at each other and on the count of 3 they drink there shot….They pay the bartender, give each other handshakes and exit the bar and go there separate ways. -Fin.
