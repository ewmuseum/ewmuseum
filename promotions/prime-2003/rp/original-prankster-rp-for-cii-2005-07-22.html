Original Prankster (RP for CII)

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/22/05 'This is going to be great!' The sound of metal scraping on concrete can be heard as a manhold cover is pushed up. A black gloved hand reaches out, grasping the edge and slides the steel disc to the side. Another hand reaches up and grabs the edge of the gaping hole in the street, joined by it's mate. A slight groan can be heard as Mr Smiley Face, bright yellow cardboard smiley face mask intact pulls himself out from beneath the street. 'This'll teach him to interject himself into MY match!' declared the Smilin' Dude, raising to one knee and reaching beneath his customary black trenchcoat to pull out something dark, plastic, and covered with buttons. Cackling mightily, like he does, he straightens up and glances across the street at the target of his prank... The Detroit Holiday Inn Express. 'Hey-lo, Smiley. What's got you crawling out of the sewers like Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth looking for his dignity?' The high pitched giggling stops as the Original Prankster turns to see... wait for it... The flickering, pale, almost transluscent form of the ghost of Ed. The dead hardcore legend is sporting his customary lopsided grin as he gazes at Mr Smiley Face. 'Ed? This is an unexpected surprise.' The ghost of the hardcore legend chuckles. 'I bet, Smiley.' 'I mean... you're just a construct I created to talk to Trash when Krieggy was in control. It was the only way I could speak to him through the mental...' Mr Smiley Face trails off as the import of what he just stated dawns on him. He starts cackling again as he continues. 'You can get through the barriers! But... how... I thought you were dormant after Tempest helped free Trashy.' 'Hey, I'm the mental construct with 'it', baby.' Ed smiles as he walks over, placing a hand on the Cheerful Warrior's shoulder. 'Besides, how'd you make me?' 'Well, I took Trash's memories of you and splinted off some of my own... you've formed up into a new personality?! You're the one we've felt manifesting?!' The ghost shakes his head. 'No, that one's still forming. I'm just a splinter of you, really. I'm like the subconscious of the subconscious. But, on the bright side, the barriers don't keep me contained as much, as I'm not a full personality.' Mr Smiley Face giggles in delight. 'So you know what's been going on since I've been stuck out here in dominant!' At Ed's affirmative nod, he starts dancing a jig of sheer joy, throwing back his head and howling his amusement to the sky. 'Ed, ya gotta tell me, what the hell is going on in our mind?' The spectre sighs. 'It ain't pretty, boyo, I assure you. The dark one's got Trashy convinced that it's your fault he's trapped down there. And that you're working for our very best friend...' 'Kriegman? Oh Trash, you've always been our heart, and as intelligent as we are, you can be so STUPID sometimes.' Ed nods. 'Yeah, the dickhead's got Trash ready to fry you as soon as the barrier drops. Thing is... I'm not sure what brought the barrier up in the first place. Although I'm thinkin'...' 'That it's Lachesis. That fucker always was a sneaky, manipulative bastard. He's going to be impossible to keep down there if Trashy's turned against me. Ed, you think you go back and talk to him, try to make him see the error of his ways? Do a little preachin' from beyond the grave again?' 'I can give it a shot. Got nothing better to do than listen to the new guy trying to figure out how to break free...' The Smilin' Dude's eyes narrow from beneath his mask. 'The new guy... what do you know about him? Is he one of us, or does he belong to Lachesis?' The flickering form pauses as he rubs his goatee. 'Well... you know how you said that Trash is the heart, rigiht?' At Mr Smiley Face's nod, he continues on with 'And Lachesis, or the dark one, is the dark side of the mind, right? And you, well, you're the willpower.' 'I'm the WILLPOWER?!' He laughs again in delight. 'Since when am I the willpower?' 'You're the one that keeps the dark one locked up. You're the one that keeps Trash from hurting himself too bad when he's emotionally tore up. Hell, the body's an alcoholic and addicted to tobacco, and I don't think you've had a drink or a smoke since you came out.' 'Well, there was that blueberry dacquiri I ordered, but I just wanted the nifty umbrella...' 'So that makes you the willpower.' 'OK, I'll buy that for a dollar. What about the new guy?' 'He's your ego.' Dead silence. 'Our... what?' 'Ego. Your confidence. Your self esteem.' 'Since when do we have any sort of confidence? Have you SEEN Trash lately? He's turned into such a pussy... oh fuck me gently with a chainsaw.' Ed chuckles. 'You got it. New guy's got it all.' 'We are fucked worse than a whorehouse in Amish country.' 'Anyways... to my original question... what on earth are you doing playing around a Holiday Inn Express?' The Smilin' Dude chuckles, you know, like he does. 'Well, I checked the guest registration and there's a Daniel Pierce staying here. Another name for Daniel Pierce is a Eve worshipping match inserting himself into prick by the name of First Blood. So, I figured I'd give him a little taste of what it's like to be on my bad side.' Ed adjusts his flickering flannel shirt for a moment. 'You do realize that... ' He flicks some imaginary dust off his shoulder before looking back up at Mr Smiley Face, 'there's likely more than one guy named Daniel Pierce in Detroit, right? Do you know if this is the right guy or not?' The chuckles erupt into a full blown gale of laughter for a good minute or so before Mr Smiley Face catches his breath. 'Does it matter?' With that, he stabs the button on his remote and puts a finger to his lips. 'Shh now, or you won't hear all the fun.' Both men stand there, one a flickering form only seeable by the other, a crazed man in a square yellow mask rubber banded around his head. They stare in complete silence at the Holiday Inn Express, a haven for the weary traveler that promises relatively clean sheets, a not TOO noisy air conditioner, and vending machines to fill yourself up on while you try to sleep in a strange bed. Suddenly, several loud screams can be heard, as well as what sounds to be several waterfalls. Mr Smiley Face falls to the ground, rolling back and forth, kicking his heels on the street in a paroxysm of laughter. 'What... what just happened?' queries the ghost of the hardcore legend. Smiley barely gets himself under control, answering through bursts of hiccuping laughter. 'I set up... the pipes... the toilets... are all now... blasting water... all the way to the ceiling!' 'Pierce's toilet?' 'ALL OF THEM!' Again, the Smilin' Dude is overtaken by a fit of laughter as he rolls around on the street. Ed's ghost looks down at him fondly. 'I'm so glad I never actually took YOU on when I kept fuckin' with Trash. Hey, isn't there another guy? Olsig or something?' 'Oh yeah, him... Well, I figured I never buy anything for myself with all the money we've earned, so I did some hacking into the PRIME computers and found out where his home address is. Every pizza place in town has been delivering a large anchovy and pineapple pizza to his house every hour on the hour for the past week. I've paid with a credit card, so if he doesn't answer the door and take it, they just leave it on his front porch!' Ed just stares at the Smilin' Dude for a moment. 'Brilliant' he whispers before shaking his head. 'Anyways, I've gotta get back and see what I can do for damage control. Good luck with your match.' 'Thanks Ed. Take care of Trash for us. We're going to need him soon'. The ghost of Ed flickers and fades away. Smiley rises to his feet and dusts himself off as a fat guy runs out of the hotel, pants around his knee screaming. 'Ewwww, I'm covered in my own shit!' Smiley stops for a moment, then collapses back to the street in laughter as we fade to black....
