Date: July 21st, 2008 to August 8th, 2008 Manifesto (of Love) July 21st, 2008 by: High Flyer My therapist told me that I should write down whatever comes to mind, keep it under lock in key inside a tapestry of blank white pages and rage. Filled with the thoughts of a crazy person, this may arguably read as a manifesto, rather than a journal. I mean, the whole concept of a journal, so very gay. I called her gay for suggesting such a gay idea, but she wasn't a lesbian. She wasn't even bi-curious. I'm completely disappointed. Such a shame. But... why not let the world see your pain? And so, it begins. Let's just say that sex is on the brain. You try not thinking about it when she crosses those daintily legs of hers. Shuffle. Align. Reset. Unrequited love is hell on earth... Unrequited lust is just an amusement park of sorrow. Every week, I head to the ol' burnt down fWo headquarters, just to get my brain picked apart by a med school drop out with a figure rating 9 on the rictor scale. Court ordered. Apparently I need to see a psychiatrist to work. You know it's illegal for me to work in 14 states and 3 foreign countries if I don't take an hour a week to lie down on a couch somewhere and spew out my feelings of loneliness, sorrow, and hedonism. So, how did I get here, to this point? How am I without my wife, chasing a girl I can never have from a promotion that's nothing more than an echo in the ether, given everything and nothing at the same time... Let's just say the ol' cliché... It started with a girl... ** at or around November 14th, 1991 Flowing brunette hair, curling in the wind. Bouncable. Perky. Rose cheeks like a clown as she puffed away on her flute, an instrument she always hated. Years later, she would tell me that when she graduated, she tied her flute to an exploding bottle rocket and said goodbye in a Valhalla styled tribute. If I wasn't already in love with her, I would have fallen right then and there. We'd met in seventh grade. I kicked a rock into her car to get her attention. Which is probably why her parents never really liked me. All the way up until our junior year of High School(oh great, I used "our," I'm gonna hear it for that. Stupid Jack. Write in Pencil. PENCIL), we were really good friends. Inseparable really. It was inevitable for us to take our relationship to the next level, even though, in difference to the cocky persona I've portrayed on television for years(I Mean, seriously, how can you be a wrestler and not be cocky? Answer, Eugene.), I'm really a fucking pussy. It took me quite a bit of nerve and some "confidence sauce" to walk up to the wavy black haired girl of my dreams. Kate and I would spend the next two years together. We'd go to our prom, we'd flirt with the idea of marriage, even at such a young age. I may not have been fully matured by that point, but maybe that's why our relationship worked back then. We weren't who we eventually became... Enough What-If-a-sizing, I do know one thing. We were happy back then. ** Revolution 168 July 23rd, 2008 by: High Flyer "I wasn't even supposed to BE HERE!" - Dante, Clerks.(1993) So, if any of you paid attention, you may have noticed something on this week’s show that made you tilt your head to its side and go... "the fuck?" The Princes/VIAGRA segment was supposed to go quite early in the show. We had pre-taped that footage on Saturday and had arranged with Cantrell to have it play early. We wanted to disappoint the fans by having VIAGRA say they wouldn't be at the show early, just to completely null that and have them appear out of the crowd in a frenzy. Which would have worked better, had Flyer and Dusk not talked fifteen minutes earlier backstage. But hey, who am I to complain. I'm simply pointing out what many numerous internet chat sites have already stated. That it was off putting and jarring. I just want to assure everyone it was not my fault. Even though no one will read this. Shit. Maybe I should buy some advertising. ** Revolution 168 in Haiku Fashion July 23rd, 2008 by: Tony Davis Rocket vegetable. Neil Diamond's golf cart. Don't pull the finger. ** Ghosts (of Love) July 25th, 2008 by: High Flyer I see her sometimes. In the corner of an arena. In my rear view window while I'm driving. Hell, I even saw her dismantling the wing as I rode the skies in a seven forty seven. And I know it's not her because she isn't screaming at me. I defined myself for years about what Kate thought. When I went to Mexico without her to start my career, I was in the lowest place imaginable for four years. When Kate left me in the fWo, I fell to pieces. I broke down, I made huge mistakes. Poison Ivy is blind because I failed to keep my marriage. Deacon very well could have died just because I don't know how to be a husband or a father. And now, I won't ever have the chance to find that out. Do you know how that makes a man feel? Normally, I'd regress into my sport, put myself and my body in harms way just to feel that rush of adrenaline course through my veins, just to keep myself alive! But now... even wrestling's thrill has lost its sparkle, and I sit here without my smile, a part of both worlds and yet not belonging to either. Man. See, this is why I don't like writing down your thoughts. Your thoughts make you sound like a whimsical philosphifying douche-bag. ** July 27th, 2008 by: High Flyer Lemme talk about the sport for a little bit. As a professional wrestler for fifteen years, I can tell you that I've seen, well, not everything. I've still yet to see a Carpenter rise from the dead, or aliens who know Muay Thai destroying the Canadian Parliament, but I've been witness to a great many of wrestling's misdeeds. In May of 1999, I was wrestling for a promotion called the Internet Wrestling Organization. Fairly big. I would think it begat PCW which begat PRIME, but I digress in my self delusions... So, we're getting ready to start our pay per view that month, entitled May Mayhem. Which, if you don't know, houses a huge battle royal-esq match that gives the winner a championship opportunity at Beach Party. Now, I'm waiting backstage to head out there and put on a show when I hear about the tragic death of Owen Hart. Now, mind you, this is the same night, it's all occurring in real time. We're all dealing with the news of a fellow comrade perishing to their death, and then not ten minutes later, we're heading out to wrestle a royal rumble styled match... on a SCAFFOLD?! You're going to go through with a match stipulation which required 39 wrestlers to essentially mimic the very events that caused Owen Hart's death. Jesus. I'm sorry. I've never gotten that off my chest. Fucking retards, I swear. They're all over in this business. PRIME's got quite a few of them. It makes things more colorful for the audience, but at the expense of the performer's sanity. ** Doctor’s Visit July 29th, 2008 By: Tony Davis So… apparently you can’t get aids from a toilet seat. Hmmm. ** Why Can’t We Be Friends? July 30th, 2008 by: High Flyer So, Craig still won't ride with us. I ask him, what are you, some kind of solitary lone wolf like Rambo? He simply nods. Man, that guy needs an extra strength dose of Zoloft, three tequila shots, six beers, and clonopin to open up. Not that I'm any better, quippin' to him all the time because, really, the whole thing is hilarious from my viewpoint. I took everything with a complete lack of self-awareness for years. Looking in from the outside really changes your perspective, on what's important, on how to even go about choosing strategy. You've got to be free flowing. I like to think of the Joker. Not a schemer, but a messer of schemes. It's easier to create a single point of chaos in someone else's grand manipulated plan than it is to craft an execute said manipulated plan. I know from experience. My plan to win the fWo world title blew up in my face, and well, Ivy's... I still haven't been able to ever explain that. But in this new world we live in called PRIME, this friendship with Dusk... I haven't had to explain it. And that's what friendship is. ** 7 Days August 2nd, 2008 By: High Flyer Colossus is only a week away. And I don't mean the X-men action figure, coming to a store near you. No, Colossus V, a pretty big show. I remember the first couple wrestlemania's being eh, and then four through six were like barn busters. I've gone through my share of big time parties, but never here in PRIME. It's great, because every place did things differently. For the fWo's Cyberslam, we had a fan festival, which was truly a lot of fun. Some of these kids worship you, others have such deep respect, they hang onto every word you say. Which certainly scared me at the time. Which is why I hardly cursed inside the walls of the fWo. Why I hardly shouted Cuntington McTwatershits or something like that. I mean, we were run by fucking Disney. We had money, but PG-13 money, y'know? In IWO, I got to be a part of Ice Age three years in a row. Each time was a completely new experience. The first year, I was green, fresh, hungry. I had just gotten a foothold on my character, and had been selling snow for the past two months. I defended my United States title against Da Bronx Bro and a good friend(and phenomenal athlete/worker) Sam Potright. The next year I participated in the North American title match, proclaiming the NA belt to be my unborn child Noah Alexander. Which, again, was great to play, had a lot of elements tied to the history of the IWO. (Inanimate objects taking on human characteristics was quite common, see : Pen, an inanimate spatula.) The next year, I helped spear head an organization of a cruiserweight battle royal, grabbing talent from all over the world. In addition, each year, the IWO held its award show around this time. Think the Slammy's, if you can remember the slammy award winning Owen Hart, you can remember even a hint of slammy pride. As for PRIME, I'm not entirely sure what's in store. But it will be an experience like no other. Each place has its own culture, it's own... rules if you will. And for Colossus, I can only imagine that each rule is pushed to its very breaking point. ** Excitement, a new fragrance from Calvin Klein August 3rd, 2008 By: Mary-Lynn Mayweather Wow. Can you believe it? I certainly can’t. My first big show, comin’ up. Granted, I’m not wrestling, but I’m an integral part to a title match. Not many people can say they have the qualities of one Johnny Polo, but this fiery red head Mayfly can! Colossus… doesn’t it sound huge? Sounds like some Greco-roman Olympic trilliad, set to cut a swath over the recessed American populace. Charging sixty bucks a head for nose bleeds seems a bit much when people can’t even afford gas anymore. Then again, when has going to a wrestling show been cheaper than filling up your tank? Well, it’s the here and now baby! Enjoy it while it lasts. I’d like to say that I love Miranda’s shoes. They’re absolutely one of the best pairs of shoes I’ve seen. The I-Phone of shoes. I would make love to her shoes, but only if I took them out to dinner first. Simon Knox and Connor O’Reilly are going to be quite the challenge for Viagra. Tony and Jack have been working out on a stiff regime these past few days to prepare themselves. Simon and Connor grew up in tSC country, a place renowned for its stiff and qualified wrestling competitors, so I have no doubt that Viagra/Princes could very well steal the show this Colossus, even with all the headliners above us. Good luck goes to Craig, of course. And Tyler. Really, I wish they just lit up that arena. Put on a show. For me? I dunno. But for the fans, because they deserve it, and this whole arc has built up wonderfully to this final epic showdown between the two.
