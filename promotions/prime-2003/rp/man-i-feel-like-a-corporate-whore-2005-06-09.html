“Man I feel like a corporate whore…”

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09 June 2005 Jason Jameson grumbles as he with hands on his hips. He looks annoyed. The blonde 6’ 4 wrestler available for independent bookings is wearing black tracksuit bottoms and a white “TFV” t-shirt. His attention is directed to the outside of the practice ring he is in. Running around the outside is Igor Arefiev, giggling followed closely by his tag team Constanino Zergolov brandishing a chair above his head. Jameson shakes his head. The Flying Vergomovs run around the ring twice, giggling all the way. Jameson decides he had had enough and walks over, reaching over the top rope and plucking the chair from Zergolov’s hands. The tag team let out a sigh of disappointment coupled with the disappointment of the two watching spectators. “Get in the ring…” commands Jameson swinging the chair wildly, “We aren’t finished…” The Flying Vergomovs look at each other and then proceed to roll into the ring. They stand to their feet and look at their new trainer, the former NTICW and PCW star. “I think we are going to leave the hardcore lesson for a few weeks…” decides Jameson as he throws the chair to the mat. Another sigh of disappointment seeps through the red and blue mask’s of the respective Vergomovs’. “I’m sorry… But you guys just don’t train hard enough so you get no toys…” shrugs Jason. “So what are we going to do?” questions Arefiev as he leans up against the ropes. “Nothing!” demands a new voice to the situation. The deep, rough voice bellows throughout the sports hall that the PRIME superstars had been training in. The three men turn their attention towards the hall doors, where the voice came from. “You guys have some work to do!” commands the dark shadow that fills out the door. The man sets into the poorly lit sports hall. He is wearing a black suit, white shirt and red tie. His hair is slicked black and flattened down. See him in a bar and you would describe him as sleaze personified but instead the government know him as former PCW2 and NTICW superstar, the retired Scotty Killer. I suppose now would be a good time to explain the new faces on board the TFV bandwagon. It began the morning following Revolution 65 and that defeat by Vetra and Los Diablos against Hessian and The Flying Vergomovs. Igor and Constantino both crawled out of bed in pain, but one of them had a plan in his mind. “I have a plan in mind…” stated Arefiev over bagels. The Flying Vergomovs had gone out for breakfast. Why cook when you can afford to eat all the time? Exactly. “Uh-oh…” Zergolov’s immediate response to a statement like that from his best friend was always met with some concern. No matter what the idea could involve, Zergolov was always concerned. The cogs in the brain of Igor were not meant to turn. “We need a team…” “We ARE a team stupid… We’re a tag team!” Zergolov knew that Arefiev couldn’t come up with an idea on his own. “No… We need a backstage team… We need people looking after us… We need a full-time trainer!” Zergolov munches on his bacon filled bagel and is therefore unable to speak as Igor continues. “We need some to run our money… We need someone to get us interview bookings and public appearances… We need someone to get us promotion deals!” Zergolov swallows quickly and stares back at Igor. “Why? We will have to pay them wages… We are no where near the best wage in PRIME… We can’t afford a personal assistant, that’s why we had to let her go,” protests a very adamant Zergolov. “But we need them…” Zergolov shook his head. As he looked at his tag team partner he knew that this wasn’t a suggestion. As Igor’s eyes peered back at him, Constantino knew that Igor ad already hired the team that he thought The Flying Vergomovs required to succeed. “Who have you signed up?” conceded Zergolov. Igor began to grow a smile across his face as he looked to his left and right at the small café. For the first time since the pair had arrived Zergolov noticed two burly men sitting at the table directly behind Igor. Two men that did not look normal, two men that looked like wrestlers. “Let me introduce,” beamed Arefiev, “former NTICW and PCW star, our trainer, Jason Jameson…” The man on Igor’s left stood up from his table. He had short, spiked blonde hair with a goatee of matching colour. He was easily 6’ 4, which compared to The Flying Vergomovs is huge. He was wearing a black suit. “And our financial advisor, former PCW2 and NTICW star, Scotty Killer!” The second man soon followed his partner’s path of standing up. He was slightly taller about 6’ 6 or 6’ 7 but wearing a matching black suit. He was older than Jameson with slick black hair. “Hello,” greeted Jameson. “Good morning,” nodded Scotty Killer. And there you go. The Flying Vergomovs have their entourage sorted out. Jason Jameson, the independent wrestler will be looking after their training regimes, their diets and every step they take. Retired Scotty Killer will be booking the interviews, sorting out the sponsorship deals and getting The Flying Vergomovs every extra dime possible. “It’s fine…” Zergolov would reassure himself as the two men followed TFV’s every move, “Los Diablos have Moraless… Killer Pirates have got von Doom… Team Zambo have… eh, their cake! Every team have an entourage…” No matter how many times Zergolov had gave himself that speech into the mirror, he never felt good about Scotty and Jason’s involvement. A second bankruptcy was not on Zergolov’s immediate wish list. Yes, a second one. Remember LOW, Lights Out Wrestling? The Flying Vergomovs were the ultimate playboys during their few months with that promotion and they racked up colossal debts. Their only rescue was the contract settlement that TFV received which just about covered the failed Russian tag team’s money problems. Constantino did not want a repeat performance. “Relax” Igor would say. “Relax; we will get them on the PRIME pay list. We can have them as our managers. It’s fine. We are part of the up-and-coming tag team division. The PRIME staff won’t say no to us.” Igor liked to abuse any position he found himself in. But it seemed to Zergolov, he had things worked out. Anyway, back to the interrupted training session. “You guys have some work to do!” commands Scotty Killer as Jameson and The Flying Vergomovs stand in the ring. “Scotty! Get the fuck out of here! I have these guys until four! Then you do your promotion and interview crap.” To say there was tension between Jameson and Scotty Killer was a slight under statement. The pair had never fought r even crossed paths in the same federation, however ‘old-man’ Killer had managed a better win-loss record in NTICW and retired just before Jason Jameson stepped into the federation. “Sorry Jason, but we have a big meeting with a film studio!” “Are they making a film of our lives?” Igor sprang to life jumping onto the bottom rope and leaning across the top rope. He didn’t enjoy the promotion stuff. Neither did Zergolov. Both men felt like corporate whores but they knew they had to get ahead in this game before their bodies pulled them back. But then again given the chance to make a film of your life? Hell, even ‘STU!’ would do work for that one. Scotty Killer shook his head in a disappointing fashion. And the passion was gone, Arefiev returned to the ring mat and shrugged at Zergolov. The tag team look at each other through their masks, knowing this will be another boring meeting where Scotty will talk, agree to let TFV do something for money and then suddenly The Flying Vergomovs will be working a commercial in Japan. Joy! “Fine!” agrees Jameson. He is cut from the same mould as The Flying Vergomovs. He never enjoyed the promotion and sponsorship deals. Maybe that was why he was always shown the door rather quickly when things started to turn sour for a promotion. The Flying Vergomovs’ begin to remove themselves from the ring. Unlike their performances in PRIME, The Flying Vergomovs are very simple about their exit. Igor hops between the second and third rope and then drops to the sports hall floor. Constantino drops to the mat and rolls out of the ring and onto the mat. Both men remove their masks…. and begin to towel down their faces before sliding their masks back down to cover their face. Twenty minutes later, Scotty Killer, Igor Arefiev and Constantino Zergolov are sitting in their car on the way to the meeting with ‘Troublemaker’ studios. The studio name rang up alarms in Zergolov’s head. He had seen a film recently that came from those studios, hadn’t he? Zergolov shrugs as he sits in the backseat. “What is the meeting about?” pestered Igor. He stared eagerly at Scotty who was driving. Scotty shrugged. “I don’t know. I think they want you to promote a film…” “What the…? You took us out of a training session for a meeting that you know nothing about!” snapped Zergolov from the backseat. “I got a phone call this morning. They told me they had work for you two. It was yes or no, then and there. I said yes as a fav…” Scotty’s voice trails off as his nearly finishes that sentence. “A favour…” Zergolov completes the sentence for him. Scotty nods. Zergolov shakes hi head in disgust. He eyeballs Igor using the pull down mirror that Igor is looking in. Igor shrugs. Zergolov sighs. “What is wrong with you?” Skip forward twenty minutes and The Flying Vergomovs are standing in the toilets. “Our ‘financial advisor,’” Zergolov does the “” hand movement thing, “has just organised a meeting as a favour!” “So?” questions Igor, shrugging and clearly seeing no problem for it. “We could end up doing porn promotion work knowing the past of this guy and the favours he would owe people!” “Con…” “What?” “ReVolution is aired on Playboy TV… Porn promotion would improve links between the network and the company… And whatever we can do that is good for the company is good for us…” And it’s sold. We have one new believer. Namely Constantino Zergolov. “Sin City?!” One hour later, the meeting is over and the film promotion has been indicated. “What have two circus performers got in common with the characters in Sin City? How can we promote it?” Okay, so Zergolov isn’t a complete believer in Scotty Killer just yet. “They explained it,” enlightens Arefiev. His tag team partner’s constant rejection of the idea is getting on his tits. “We can pretend to be characters from the film for the whole broadcast…” Zergolov just accepts the idea, he can’t fight it. The Flying Vergomovs or at least one half has agreed with it with open arms. “But it was released months ago. Why bother to promote it now that everyone has seen it?” “UK release, Constantino, the UK release,” clarifies Scotty Killer. “We have to think on the global level for The Flying Vergomovs. Playboy TV reaches fans the world over, and we need to get TFV touching fans the world over.” Disgruntled, Constantino gets into the back of the TFV red Mustang and folds his arms, pissed off. He looks at the three boxes now joining him in the backseat. One reads “TFV t-shirts”, the next “TFV badges”, and the final one “TFV posters.” This new financial advisor has global plans for The Flying Vergomovs. “Man I feel like a corporate whore…”
