Always Being Put Down....

Date: Various Date: January 14, 1990 Location: Docks, Gdansk, Poland The docks are surrounded by clouds on a cold January day. A couple of snow flakes make their way down from the sky, and steam comes from the mouths of the dock workers as they wait for their assignments. “Walczak…Walczak…” A man makes his way around the docks looking for Tom Walczak. Finally, he walks down the last dock, and sees Walczak coming down from a ramp that leads into a massive ship on the side of the dock. “Walczak, get down here.” Tom Walczak makes his way down towards the man, and as he gets closer we see that he is wearing only a t-shirt to compliment his jeans. Steam comes off both the arms and head of Walczak, as his warm body mixes with the cold winter air. The man checks over his clipboard, as Walczak stands in front of him. “I was just looking over my clipboard here, and I realized that you were the only person assigned to load this ship.” “Yeah I was.” “Would you mind explaining why this is?” “I talked to Krzyszek last night after my shift, and he said that if I get here early enough I could get this load for myself, and he said that if I finish loading up the ship I would get the five hundred that went with it.” “I don’t think I’m going to allow that Tom.” “Janek, you know that I need this. I need all the money I can get to move out of that orphanage. I am the oldest kid in there and the only time I ever get out is to come and work here. I need this.” “I really do feel sorry that you have such a bad situation, but I can’t risk it.” “I will get it done, with time to spare, I promise. You know I work faster than anyone else on this dock, don’t hold the fact that I am only fourteen against me.” “Trust me Tom the fact that you’re fourteen has nothing to do with this. If this load doesn’t get loaded on time it’s not you that they are going to complain to. I am the one who has to hear everyone’s shit when things don’t get done. This is the biggest shipment we have today, and I am not gonna risk losing it. I am gonna send some more guys over here to help you out, and that’s the last I wanna hear of this.” “But Janek, I gotta get more…” “I’m sorry, but no, and for Christ’s sake put a jacket on you’ll get pneumonia, and then you won’t be able to make any money.” Janek walks away from Walczak not saying another word, and seconds later three men make their way towards Walczak. Walczak shakes his head disappointed, and gets back to loading the ship. This would be an ongoing them for Walczak throughout his life, people getting things that Walczak feels belong to him. Date: March 15, 1991 Location: Polska Restauracja Tom Walczak sits alone at a table in the middle of the restaurant. Now, fifteen years old he begins to fill out his body, and is slowly turning into the mammoth man that he is present day. He is dressed rather nicely, wearing a yellow golf shirt, tan khakis, and a worn in pair of loafers. It is not the most high end clothing available, but for Walczak it is the best he could do. He twiddles his thumbs nervously as though he waits for someone else to make an appearance. Then his attention towards the front door of the restaurant. A beautiful young girl enters the restaurant. She has long blonde hair down to her shoulders, and is wearing a tight white t-shirt, and a blue denim skirt. Walczak’s face brightens as he lays his eyes on the girl, but then his face quickly sours, as two other people enter behind the girl. The first, a boy, who although is probably near Walczak’s age, is much smaller. He is also dressed much better than Walczak, he wears a black button down shirt, and black dress pants to go with newly shined black dress shoes. Walczak can only think of one word when he looks at the boy, douchebag. A girl follows at the end of the line, she is also very good looking, wearing a nice pink polo shirt, and tight blue jeans, but has a look of disinterest on her face, showing that she has no joy from being in the restaurant. “Tomek…Tomek…How are you?” The girl runs over to Walczak giving him a big hug, making a smile appear again on his face. They let go and Walczak turns his attention to the other two members of the party. “Magda, who are your two friends.” “Oh, this is Piotrus, and this is my friend Krystyna, I hope you don’t mind, but they wanted to tag along.” “No problem at all.” Walczak leads the group over to their table, and they all sit down. Walczak is disappointed that Magda has chosen the farthest seat from Walczak directly across from him. “So, Tom, that’s your name right?” “Yeah, that’s me.” “Where do you go to school?” “I don’t really go to school, I am schooled in the orphanage, but I usually spend most of my time working on the docks. That’s where I met Magda.” “Oh, so you’re an orphan. That must be horrible.” Walczak looks at Krystyna, who seems to have taken pity on him, and starts to blush. Walczak never liked the fact that he lived in an orphanage, and was always hesitant to share it with people for this reason. He hated when people took pity on him. Magda nudges Krystyna, and Krystyna takes her eyes off of Walczak. “It’s not that bad, I mean there are very few kids my age, but other than that it’s alright. I don’t really consider it my home.” Walczak looks down at the table no longer wanting to make contact with the others at the table. The conversation seems to go dead with only the three others talking about school projects or other classmates, but Walczak stays out of it. “If you guys don’t mind I am going to excuse myself. I have to run to the men’s room.” Walczak gets up from the table and makes his way towards the restrooms. He opens the door, but he really has no need to be in the bathroom. He just stands above the sink looking into the mirror. Why does this have to happen to me. I was just looking to have a good time, for once in my life I thought I deserved it. I have been stuck in that orphanage for fifteen years, and it is horrible, Krystyna was right it is horrible. I guess I have to get back there and face them. They’ll think that something is up. Walczak washes his face in the sink before making his way back out to the restaurant. He sees the three friends sitting at the table talking among each other. More conversation than they had when Walczak was at the table. Slowly he makes his way back towards them. “Magda what are you doing here. You know that that boy is not like us.” “Yeah, I mean I thought I was average size for my age, but that kid makes me look like a midget. Are you even sure he is fifteen?” “Plus, I don’t know if you heard him, but he lives in an orphanage. There is no way a boy from an orphanage is good enough for you.” ‘Stop it guys. Tom is a really nice boy, and I really do like him.” “I don’t know how that kid is a freak. We could probably leave before he gets back, you wouldn’t see him for a while, and it would save you a lot of wasted time.” “Cough…Cough…” Walczak coughs letting them know that he has arrived back to the table. They are immediately cut off from their conversation, and they turn their faces away from Walczak, almost in shame. They are now the ones who faces are blushed. “Sorry that I took so long. I hope I wasn’t missed too much.” “No, um…The waiter came and we just ordered you a water. We didn’t know what you wanted.” “Water is fine.” Silence once again fills the table. With Walczak resuming the twiddling of his thumbs. Every time Walczak looks up, or attempts to open his mouth the three others turn away. “You know what, I actually have to be getting back to the orphanage. I am out a little later than usual, and the sisters get kind of pissed off if I come home late. I’m sure I’ll see you around.” Walczak gets up from the table and makes his way towards the door. He is about to push the door open, and then… “Tomek, wait.” “What do you want?” “Just come back, those two don’t know what they are talking about. I really do like you there is no reason to leave.” “Your friends are right. I am a freak, and there is no reason that you should be out with me. You’ll just get into trouble, I always have a way of finding that.” “Tomek, don’t go.” “It’s alright, I know I’m just a…freak.” Walczak turns away and makes his way out of the door. He once again has something taken away from him that he felt was his. Date: May 21, 1997 Location: Greenpoint, New York Walczak stands outside a Greenpoint Brownstone. He is now twenty two years old, and is dressed in his normal attire. He wears a tight white undershirt, and ripped up blue jeans to go with his Timberland boots. There is something else that Walczak is wearing. Something that is rarely seen on Walczak. A smile. Walczak seems to be genuinely happy as he looks up at the building. “Tom?” Walczak turns around to see who had just called his name. He sees a man that is much smaller than himself. The man carries a briefcase, and is dressed in a nice business suit. Walczak’s smile broadens as he sees the man. “John, it’s so good to see you here. I was just hoping that I could get another look around the place, before the closing. This could be the happiest day of my life.” “You didn’t get my message.” ‘Message, what Message?” The smile disappears from Walczak’s face. What was once a happy feeling is no longer there. Walczak knows that something is about to go wrong. It always does. “Well, I called the restaurant a couple of days ago. It seems that the owners of the home have decided to go with another buyer. Earlier this week they made an offer that was substantially larger than yours.” “I don’t care how much larger it was than mine. I had a deal that was ready to go. I mean the reason I’m here is to sign close on the contract this house is supposed to be mine.” “Mr. Walczak, just calm down a little bit. I am not the one who decided to accept the other offer. That would be Mr. and Mrs. Stencel. I really do feel sorry that you had to find out this way, but unless you could more than double your offer there is no way that you can get this house.” Walczak knows that there is no way that he can gather this much money in such a short time. It had taken almost six years for Walczak to gather this, and he had barely had enough to make the offer. “Who did they sell it to anyway?” “I wanted to avoid any confrontation, but it seems that the new owners seem to have arrived.” Walczak turns around to see a brand new Rolls Royce pulling into a spot a couple of feet away from him. Out of the car step two older gentlemen. Both wear similar outfits sunglasses, even though the sky is overcast, and leather jackets, even though the weather is quite humid. Walczak can’t even look at the two men without laughing. They slowly make there way over to Walczak, walking much slower than any normal human beings would. “Hi, the name is Marco, and this is my buddy Antonio.” The man sticks his hand out towards Walczak, but Walczak does not reciprocate. He just stares through the man almost growling at him. “John, I didn’t think that you were going to need muscle for this meeting. HA HA HA.” Marco laughs at his own joke, and Antonio snickers, but Walczak and John do not look the least bit amused. “Lighten up there. What are you doing here then.” “I was the one who had made the original offer on this house. You see I was under the impression that this house was gonna be mine until today.” “Yeah sorry about that. Me and Antonio were just driving around the neighborhood saw the sale pending sign, and knew that we had to make an offer that would make the owners think twice.” “I didn’t want the owners to think twice. I worked for over six years sleeping in the back of a restaurant to save up for this house. I’m not some nouveau-riche prick who just buys up any property that he sees for sale.” “NOUVEAU-RICHE! I have never been so insulted. I come from a long line of rich ancestors. Some of them dating back to King Phillip II of Spain. I have come from money, and there is no way that I wish to be lumped in with riffraff such as the nouveau-riche.” “Take it easy. I didn’t know that it would hit such a nerve, but there is no way that you deserve this house more than I do.” “Well, I could possibly rent it out to you. I mean I am a man of charity, and you could obviously use it.” Walczak clenches his fists in anger, ready to strike the man. The man looks over at Antonio, and smirks. Walczak knows the man has no intention to rent the house to Walczak he is just trying to play on the big man’s nerves. “Yeah I have a policy of not renting from faggots. You see I guess it’s more religion than anything, but I don’t feel comfortable going anywhere where some guy has plunged another guys ass. I do however know that the walls are sound proof, so you won’t have to worry about the neighbors thinking down on you.” Walczak walks away from the rest of the group disappointed yet again, but then there is a tap on his shoulder. As Walczak turns around he is met with a stiff fist to the jaw. The shot does little to move Walczak, and Marco immediately realizes the attack was a mistake. “Did you think that through before at all before you did it? I mean did you think I would just take a hit from you and not do anything because you are related to king dickhead?” “KING PHILLIP!!!! THE THIRD!!!!” Those would be the last words that Marco uttered, as Walczak picked him up by the neck throwing him into a nearby pile of garbage. Antonio who until this point had remained still, and silent runs towards Walczak, but is met with a forearm that sends the man to the ground. Walczak kicks Antonio in the abdomen before he once again makes his way towards Marco. He delivers an even more devastating kick that causes him to gasp for air. “YOU AREN’T BETTER THAN ME.” Walczak spits on the beaten body of Marco, and then finally makes his exit. Date: July 11, 2007 Location: Staples Center Tom Walczak stands inside the office of Lisa Tyler, and he doesn’t look that pleased. It may have something to do with the company he is in. Tyler Rayne and Big Bear Munson Munsoon, two men who Walczak has had a number of run ins with recently. Than there is a man whom he doesn’t know much about, Tony Gamble. Tyler Rayne is making a number of dumbass comments, and Big Bear shows that he may not have a fully developed brain, while Gamble goes on and on about being the best ever, but Walczak just wants to get out of the meeting as quick as possible. “The 5-Star Challenge has been cancelled.” These are the first words that truly register with Walczak. Once again something he had worked towards had been taken away. “Tony Gamble will now be taking part in the new 5-Star Challenge.” This next comment only angers Walczak more. How does this punk get a bye towards his chance to be the 5-Star Champion. He is not the one who has been in the ring over the past couple of weeks risking life and limb to get a shot. “Gamble and Walczak will take on Rayne and Munsoon in a tag match the winner of which will square off against each other for a shot at the title.” Walczak looks over at Gamble and his ever present grin, as it seems to almost mock Walczak. He will now have to co-exist with this freeloader. Walczak barely pays attention to the rest of the meeting, and makes his way out of Tyler’s office after the meeting is adjourned. Shit like this just seems to happen me non-stop. I have been put down time and time again in my life, and there is no way I am going to allow it to happen again. I am done just letting people walk all over me. The 5-Star title should be mine already, and I am going to make sure that this time what is mine will remain mine.
