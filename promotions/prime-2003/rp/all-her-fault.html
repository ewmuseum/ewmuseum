Date: Feb 11th and 12th Wednesday, February 11th After ReVolution: OverKill “Lift your head so I can glue ya shut.” “Right.” Kaiser Vashaun shifted his weight in the steel folding chair, then looked up at the doctor backstage at OverKill. “Bad?” “Nah, several cuts. But none of ‘em are too grizzly.” Kaiser took deep breaths, the sweat and blood on his body starting to evaporate, cooling him in his seat. His head throbbed, in-part from walking around with blood blurring his vision, in-part from the cuts, and then partly because of a kick that had unhinged his jaw. Bitch. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Wednesday, February 11th After ReVolution: OverKill Several Minutes Later... “You done?” “Hold on… just finishing” The doctor ran his thumb across Kaiser’s brow, cutting off the last trickle of blood before it rolled into the champ’s eye. “Alright, hit the showers. You smell like a goddamned ferret.” “Thanks.” The legs of the chair made an intestine churning screech as The Next in Line pushed himself up and started to stumble towards the shower. Lindsay Troy. She was charming, so much so that had it not been for the fact that she stood between himself and immortality, and the fact that his current headache was courtesy of her public payback…he might actually find her tolerable. “Hey, Champ… Troy really kicked your head off tonight.” Or not. “Yeah.” Kaiser did his best to ignore the crew guy. Stepping over to the bench, the champ looked down at his belongings. From a burlap sack, a hockey mask glared up at him, and he shook his head. Grabbing at a piece of his wrist tape, Kaiser pulled, unraveling the now pink wrap, and tossing it into the trash. Rummaging through his suitcase, Kaiser checked his watch to see that it was well past midnite. The Next in Line began to unwrap his other wrist, but stopped halfway through and stared at the cuts and tears… the frayed edges… the stain of blood, his own and others… the physical pain… the emotional exhaustion… the jealous rage. And he thought about having to do it all over again next week. With a brief laugh, Kaiser spoke to no one in-particular. “Good thing I’m seeing a shrink.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Thursday, February 12th 8:43A.M. ‘DING’ The sound of the elevator arriving on the ground floor snapped Kaiser out of his brief slumber, and nearly brought him out swinging. Peeling himself off of the back wall of the cramped quarters, Kaiser grabbed the handle of his suitcase and stepped out into the hotel lobby. He was running on about five hours of sleep… five less than he needed The schedule of a professional wrestler was starting to catch up with. Add in bi-monthly trips to Ohio on his occasional days off to visit with Dr. Ward, and the lack of sleep was starting to kick his ass. Kaiser rolled up to the front desk and rang the bell, despite the fact that a young lady was standing right there. “Um, sir. The bell is usually intended for alerting staff when no one is currently at the front desk.” “Sorry. That one was for me. Needed the ringing in my ears to keep me awake.” “Are you checking out?” “Yeah, Room 521.” Kaiser pulled the room key from his pocket and slid it across the desk, as the woman punched a few keys on the computer. He had one hour till his flight left Tacoma. Tonight was a house show, another on Friday and Saturday. Home on Sunday, then to Ohio on Monday for a therapy session. “Ok, Mr. Vashaun, you’re all paid, so you’re good to go.” “Thanks.” Pulling his suitcase behind him, Kaiser headed across the parking lot towards his rental. He hoped traffic wouldn’t be bad, it was at least half-hour on the open interstate to the airport. If he hit rush hour traffic, Delta Flight #4131 might be short one passenger. As he loaded his bags into the trunk of the car, Kaiser’s cell phone buzzed in his pocket. The battered champion pulled the phone from his pocket and flipped it open. “This is Kaiser.” “Hello, Mr. Vashaun. This is Nancy from Dr. Ward’s office.” “Hey, Nancy. How are you.” “Good. How are you?” “Little banged up.” “I would imagine. Mr. Vashaun, I’m calling to inform you that Dr. Ward said he would need to cancel your scheduled appointment for this coming Monday.” “What? Why’s that?” “He said to tell you ‘184’.” 184. He understood. “Ok. Thanks, Nancy.” Kaiser flipped his phone closed and slammed shut the trunk of his car. Guess he would be able to catch up on that sleep afterall. Bitch.
