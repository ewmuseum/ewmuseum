Genesis

15 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/5/07 Berlin 1970 “Just one more push “ The sounds of pain and anguish filled the delivery room, a woman, drenched in sweat lies on a pure white bed. The only other people in the room, a single doctor assisting with the delivery and two men in suits stand in the corner of the room, observing the situation. One of them holding a clipboard, looking down at it frequently, flipping through the numerous pages as though to ensure something is correct. “You know the situation, you will not see the child. You will not hold the child. To you, this child does not exist. Do you understand?” The man clutching the clipboard utters the words with a certainty and malice which would be described as un-nerving. The other man remains silent his eyes not moving away from the doctor who was attending to the birth. From the bed the woman looks down at the doctor, who doesn’t return the gaze as though scared to face her. Another shrill cry of pain and exhaustion fills the room, her panting breath as she tries to fight through the pain. “Do you understand?” A much harsher edge to the tone initiates a response from the woman who with a look of turmoil and torment simply nods, before letting out another cry of pain, the room in soon filled with the sound of a baby crying. Immediately the man who had not spoken a word strides forwards and takes the child off of the doctor who was trying to clean the baby, having cut the umbilical cord. The gentleman holds the child in front of him and peers at it over the top of his spectacles, inspecting the child as it squirms and cries it’s lungs out, clearly scared, fear and fright taking over natural instinct if you will. “He’s Pure.” The man returns the child to the doctor, who had still not uttered a word as he begins to clean the child. On the bed the woman is fighting every instinct in her body, her child was being taken away from her she wanted to protect the child, to love the child and hold it, but she knew these men and she knew the agreement that she had made. ”Are you sure Otto, we can’t make any mistakes.” The man with the clipboard removes the papers and hands them to the woman who reluctantly takes them, no compassion in any aspect of the man, not the slightest hint of sympathy is shown as he turns his back to the woman “I’m always sure, Johann. Don’t ever question my work again.” Otto responds sharply, shooting a venomous glance at Johann. In the corner of the room, the doctor is noticeably on edge, he had not looked comfortable from the first instance of being in the room, but now though it was different, there was a tang of fear in the air. “Doctor Schultz.” Johann stated abruptly, causing Dr Schultz to stand up straight, his face pales quickly and to the keenest of eyes, his hands are noticeably shaking. “Ye…yes.” Stuttered Dr Schultz as he attempted to back away, despite the fact he was already firmly against a wall. “Is the baby healthy?” The look in Johann’s eyes indicated that regardless of what the answer was in reality, if the correct answer wasn’t given now it would seriously affect his way of life, breathing for a start. “Yes, but I really must insist that the child remains here for at least a week for observation.” The moral fibre of his very being had broken through, he was a doctor first and foremost. He needed to ensure the child’s health, it was who he was. “Dr Schultz, the child is pure, the child is strong he will be fine I can assure you. We have every facility the child could possibly need to ensure his well being, so please spare me your opinion, all I asked was is the baby healthy? You answered yes is that correct?” With every syllable Johann stepped forwards with an ever growing menace. “Yes….but” Once again Dr Schultz tried to interject with his professional opinion, but his mind stopped him this time as he shot a glance towards Otto, who had remained quite still and quite silent since Johann had snapped at him yet at the same time he seemed to control the room, there was a look in Otto’s eyes that Dr Schultz didn’t want to question. “Good, we will take him now. Otto!” On Johann’s command Otto moves forward and in a manner unbecoming of his character, he gently cradles the child in his arms, rocking him gently until the crying subsides and he calms down before leaving the room, leaving just Dr Schultz, Johann and the woman who had just given birth, who is still noticeably shaken, both from the trauma of her giving birth and seeing her child taken away. “Both of you. Today you were part of a new Germany, a brave new world which you will be a part of, you have started the rejuvenation, with your efforts today, Germany will be great again, and Germany will be pure!” Johann nods to the two of them as he turns sharply on his heels and makes his way to the exit. “Kurt….”the woman from the hospital bed whispers, barely audibly partly through fear and partly through exhaustion. “Pardon?” “The child….call him Kurt, please, it was my fathers name.” Tears are welling up her eyes now. Johann stops in the entrance to the room, clutching the door tightly as for the first time Johann shows a sign of humanity. “Your father was a good man. A noble man, it would be an honour.” Johann nods at the woman who allows a slight smile onto her face as she finally collapses backwards, her body finally giving up on her. Breathing heavily she allows herself to relax and rest. Johann smiles in what can only be described as a soft way and closes the door behind him to the operating room. On the other side of the door Otto is tending to the child, placing it in a crib whilst covering the child in a blanket. Two soldiers, clutching rifles are flanking the entrance to the operating room and salute as Johann walks through before maintaining their alert status. “She wants the child to be named Kurt.” Johann muttered quietly whilst avoiding eye contact with Otto. Otto on the other hand had shot a glare at Johann and quickly strode across the room before slapping Johann firmly around the face causing it to ricochet firmly as his cheek began to redden immediately. “You are weak Johann! A name is a form of attachment, the child can have no connections. Leave now, you know what must be done!” Otto immediately points to the exit. The rage building up inside him. “Otto, please she is an innocent!” It was Johann’s turn to look scared, Otto clearly had some form of control over him, to the point that despite the protestations he was still heading towards the exit, his head hung low and pale. “No Johann, she will be a casualty of war, for the greater good.” Immediately Johann turns to the two soldiers who are guarding the operating room. “Kill them.” The soldiers nod silently in acknowledgement and enter the operating room closing the door behind them, there is the sound of commotion, a muffled scream and two gunshots quickly followed by an eerie silence. Otto proceeds to walk over to the cot when the child is lying quite silently, having not been disturbed or effected by the events that have occurred in his fledgling life. He looks at the child silently before uttering a single word from under his breath as a twisted smile forms on his face. “Kurt…” ** ** ** Little Rock 2007 “Ok so I just need your passport please Mr Panzer…” The pretty airline assistant asked with a nice spring in her step, however as she looked up at Kurt Panzer, she quickly went quiet. Kurt Panzer, for all intensive purposes was a beast, standing six feet and nine inches tall and weighing in close to three hundred pounds of pure muscle. Having a man of that size and stature staring a hole in you did nothing for your confidence no matter how chipper you were feeling. There was an uneasy silence as Panzer continued to stare at the woman, whose smile was beginning to fluctuate slightly, hesitation and nerves were kicking in as Panzer’s piercing blue eyes bore into her very soul as the sun reflected off of his natural blond hair. “You’ll have to excuse my brother, he doesn’t deal with demands well.” The voice was clearly German in origin, but had more than a slight American twang to it, a hybrid you may say. The man sidled around Kurt Panzer and leaned casually on the counter, causing the woman to step back almost agog. The two men looked identical, the same height and the same build. The only difference between the two men was his jet black hair, clearly from a bottle that occupied the head of the Americanised German. “My name is Klaus Steinheil and here is my passport and here is my oafish brother’s, I can only offer my apologies my dear.” Some may have taken Klaus’ words to be smarmy, but there was a certain charm about them, especially with his accent which was almost enchanting. “Oh no need to apologise at all Mr Steinheil, although I do have to ask, if the two of you are brothers why do you have different surnames?” It was a simple enough question, yet it provoked a strange reaction between the two brothers who shared a concerned glance. “It’s a long story my dear, now if we could just proceed. We need to get some sleep before we go to work. It has been a long flight, I’m sure our new bosses wouldn’t appreciate us being late on our first night. “No of course not, well everything is in order here, thank you.” Klaus nods at the woman and beckons for Kurt to follow him, who hesitates for a moment and stares at the woman for another few moments before slumping off after Klaus, the two walk in silence for around five minutes until they reach a quiet part of the airport it’s then that Kurt begins the conversation. “They will suspect.” ”They will suspect nothing, because it is not in their interest to know. We will do what we do, we work quietly until it is time to proceed.” “You aren’t my brother though Klaus, they call us brothers.” “I know Kurt, but just make sure you remember who I am. I am Klaus Steinheil! I am your blood! And I’m better than you in everyway!” ** ** ** Berlin 1975 “Are you sure this is the best course of action Otto? Kurt is showing positive testing in eighty percent of his trials?” Johann was sat at a desk his head in his hands, in five years he seems to have aged by fifty, his skin has become wrinkled and dry and his hair now grey and in bad condition. Opposite him sat Otto clutching a small phial of blood with a label on it which simply read ‘Panzer K’ “Eighty percent is not good enough, even now he is showing signs of resistance, our methods have been compromised somewhere along the line.” Otto continues to clutch the phial in his had, Johann is unable to take his eyes away from phial, for the past week Otto had carried the phial around with him everywhere he went, with no explanation as to why. “Our methods are correct Otto! It is human nature to grow and develop naturally, you can’t control every facet of a child’s development they need to learn something’s for themselves, through their surroundings through experiences. Face facts Otto, this entire scheme has been a failure, you can’t create the perfect human being.” Johann had pulled himself to his feet and was now pacing up and down in front of the table, his hands running through his hair almost tugging at it. He had found himself at his wits end in the past few months as tensions developed in the working relationship between Otto and himself. “That is where you are wrong Johann. How much do you know about ‘Project Genesis’??” Otto smiles to himself as pulls himself up slowly to his feet. “Very little, from what I know it was an experimental project during the war in which we tried to create the perfect soldier, the funding was cut and the project closed down due to it being too radical.” Johann is now suitably confused, he hadn’t heard anything mentioned of Project Genesis in close to thirty years, it seemed peculiar that it was being bought up now, yet there were alarm bells ringing in his head. He didn’t like where this conversation was heading. “Project Genesis was never closed Johann, it was kept going strictly confidential. Between nineteen forty two and today there were two hundred and seventy two failed attempts.” “How do you mean failed attempts?” Johann was now suitably concerned, he knew the type of organisation that he worked for, a failed attempt could mean literally anything it didn’t warrant thinking about. “That doesn’t concern you. Johann do you agree that Kurt is the perfect specimen? A total Arian male? “Of course, that’s why he was chosen in the first place.” “So what if I was to tell that with Kurt’s blood, pure blood. We were finally able to create that perfect soldier, the one who would be different from the rest, the next stage of evolution.” Otto’s voice was now booming with pride and passion, so much so that he needed to steady himself on a chair. The silence between the two seemed to last forever, there was a tension between the two men that had been present ever since they had been assigned to work together, their radically differing ideas had often led to conflicts, but there had never been silence like this before, someone would always have to have the final word. “We’ve named him Klaus Steinheil.” “Steinheil? That’s your name! Five years ago you killed a woman for requesting a name and now you give this child your own name!” “Johann, I am old and tired I finally succeeded in my life’s work, this is my legacy! Klaus will lead us to the Promised Land. “I hope you are right, because you’ve crossed the line Otto! You aren’t god this is too much! “Trust me Johann, Klaus will be better than anything we could have ever imagined, he will make Kurt look normal!” ** ** ** Little Rock 2007 “Now say it!” Klaus had his had rapped firmly around Kurt’s throat his blue eyes piercing into Kurt’s, despite the equal size and strength Kurt was not retaliating and was simply stood allowing Klaus to do so, he remained silent however refusing to back down, this was his way of fighting back. Klaus continued to apply pressure around Kurt’s throat, noticeably so that pressure marks were beginning to form on the throat. “You are better than me.” Kurt eventually relents and utters the words through a strained grimace through his teeth. As soon as it was said Klaus removed his hand from Kurt’s throat and begins to walk away “Don’t ever forget that Kurt.”
