Captain Justice, Issue #8: Shoulders

Date: Here July 23, Phillips Arena Atlanta, GA, 10:26 PM Not even a quarter of a century old yet and I have the biggest opportunity of a lifetime standing before me. Me, of all people, a guy with a minute four months to his PRIME tenure and already, I’m stepping foot in the ring with not just anyone: THE most decorated Hall of Famer to grace its halls, Killean Sirrajin. Part of me has a sick fanboy appreciation for the gravity of the situation, but I’ve wanted to test my mettle against a man like Killean. A definitive measuring stick for every last noob walking into PRIME with aspirations and hopes aplenty. When my dad first introduced me to his world of pageantry and showmanship, I knew its splendor was something I’d want to be a part of. Well, that’s what I thought initially before Earl passed away. After he died, my interested waned and I let it pass me by as I latched onto a life of normalcy. A home, a job, and a steady paycheck fresh out of high school was all I needed. Then a better class of woman than what I’d grown accustomed to inspired me to do great things; told me I should get back to what I loved because she knew I’d regret it otherwise. I went back to professional wrestling, slamming my back and icing it down every weekend for two years. Even then, I didn’t see any of this coming; Moving from show to show across the world, giving 100% to legions of fans, all the time, ever time. …But here I stand, feeling more nauseous than shit as I exit one of the back entrances of the Phillips Arena. The murderous piranha in the guise of wrestling fans migrate with each step I take, rattling the chain-link fence offensively as I hear towards the parking lot. Gobs of hateful bile spew from the mouths of the young and old alike, teens, small children, intoxicated adults and above all else, the die-hard Killean Sirrajin fans. Despite my innards feeling like they’re being slashed away by Wolverine, I muster the best poker face I can and ignore their cries. Droves of the aforementioned Killean fans cry that their hero’s going to come back and make what I did to him on ReV 168 look like a day at the spa. Some drunken fans use more colorful language involving my mother and a horse, the impressionable minds of the young tell me I suck. One gentleman even comes up with the witty pun of "Flaming Flaggot." Snapping my head in their direction, I proclaim to the bleating sheep that I’m a hero who did what was right by ridding PRIME of that dirty Snowback menace, trying to keep total character up, and tell them they should be worshipping the ground I walk on. I carefully punctuate the syllables of "HE-RO" once more… I hurriedly swallow the vomit bubbling in the back of my throat. I can barely finish those words without the enormous pangs of disgrace getting to me. Heroes don’t hang innocent men. ~ ~ ~ August 7st, 2008 Embassy Suites, Charlotte, NC, 1:28 AM Sometimes, when life’s heavy burdens were beginning to crash around you, the appropriate response is to just get the fuck away from it all. While Soren and his trusty rental car traversed the vast open highway, he’d seen his face in the rear-view and noticed bags under his eyes that were beginning to make him look like Steve Buscemi. The rigorous schedules were taking their toll on him, coupled with his impending guilt of the situation, so the best thing to do, he decided, was to get sleep. He’d checked into the hotel, albeit very last minute. He’d thrown his bags across the room. He’d left his travelling companion in Sonny Silver elsewhere, tending to business relating to his school. All was well… Except for that pesky little issue of the creeping insomnia. What had started as one sleepless night the day after Soren Knox nearly took the life of one of his fellow wrestlers had grown with each setting of the sun. The steroid scandal? Yeah, it would tarnish a man’s reputation and yeah, it would’ve worn on the conscience of a normal person, but that was what Sonny described as a "hook." Killean was at the top of the PRIME food chain, a man who could walk into any wrestling organization and be given the world because of the respect he commanded in the industry. And here was this hotshot rookie kid who was going to have to find ways to "work the system" if he wanted to get ahead and fast. "Working the system" was quickly transforming into "leave your morals at the door" and the thought didn’t settle well at all, especially with Soren’s stomach as the Silver protégé casually tossed the sheets to the floor in a huff. With the hour gently creeping into two AM and insomia gearing up for the post-match victory party over tranquil dreams, Soren walked to the balcony, bare feet pattering against the floor. Clad only in a t-shirt and boxer briefs, the big man felt a slight summer breeze collide with him as he stepped ouside, feeling that very first taste of Charlotte night air. Taking in the unchanging landscape of the downtown area, he was one among the many of night-owls currently partaking in everything flashy downtown nightlife had to offer, albeit his situation wasn’t of his own accord. "Lucky bastards…" The voice that escaped lacked his usual calm edge and was replaced by one of resentment. In this post-midnight hour, with Colossus looming directly overhead like a black raincloud ready to drench the citizens below, the only place Soren’s mind would take him was bed. He couldn’t bring himself to hasten towards any million-dollar matches, so it was back to the bed for another attempt to get back to the Land of Nod. Shifting the pillow over his eyes, Soren adjusted into the California King-sized bed and drifted, taking this rare moment of peace and trying to run with it. … … … "YOU’VE GOT NO CHANCE…. NO CHANCE IN HELL! "WHA?!" Soren jolted like the building was being set ablaze with him inside. Panting, he quickly snapped his head to the side where his cell phone rested. "PICK IT UP!" "AH!" Nearly jumping again, Soren came dangerously close to tumbling out of his bed and putting a hole in the floor, crushing the inhabitants below. Looking up, plain as day, was that ugly, matted mess of brown-black hair dangling from his scalp. That hideous lumberjack beard, accompanied by the swanky suit. "Answer the phone, Soren." The voice of Mr. Silver boomed. "Sonny, what the fuck is going on?" He swiveling his head to make sure he wasn’t the victim of some horrible hazing ritual PRIME wrestlers were prone to receiving in their early months. Still unsure how he got in his room, let alone how he found him, he looked up at his mentor. "I… I thought you were doing business at the school?" "Hey, hey, hey, hey, hey!" Mr. Silver scolded in a voice designed to impart lessons to children. He pointed a finger at the phone. "No. THAT’S Sonny. Me? What do you want to call me? Hmm… well, lemme break it down for you, Soren… I comprise the completely unorganized part of your brain that’s driven by mere primordial impulses." "…What?" "I’m the Id, nigga." "Little too late to try and… Fruedalize me." Soren snorted as he rolled over, opting not to indulge his apparent dalliance with insanity. "I’m going back to bed." Try as he might, Silver quickly appeared on his other side, making him jump back a foot in the bed. Soren clinched his teeth together and fumed as his antagonist continued to trek about the room, the infamous shit-eating grin stretching the very limits of his wrinkled face. "Get with the program, junior. I’m what you want deep inside and what you want? That’s to be famous, kiddo!" "No. You’re not. You’re just a dream, I’m getting sick of this shit, so I’m gonna roll back to this side, now hit the bricks." As if he were a complete professional at being rid of unwanted company, Soren quickly tossed the phone into the bag, ending the perpetual buzzing across the oak of the nightstand. He buried his eyes with his pillow, attempting to get comfortable in his unruly surroundings. Unbeknownst to him, the deep vein in Silver’s forehead started to pulsate. "HEY! FUCKWAD! You’ll do DAMN good to respect me! Do you understand?!" An uneasy five minutes passed by before Soren peered an eye from underneath the pillow, effectively ending his bluff of taking a siesta. He threw the pillow back, Silver not budging an inch, vein still throbbing profusely. "Look," Soren snarled. "I’m getting sick of these games. You need to leave, now. I need sleep, I’ve got THE match of my career and I’ve got enough on my plate as is." "You’re working on NOTHING!" Silver bellowed, shaking his head disapprovingly. "You’re working on taking a goddamn nap, which you should’ve done HOURS ago. Instead, you’re sitting here, puking every night like a goddamn anorexic and not sleeping because you’re too busy worrying about Killean instead of worrying about Killean AT COLOSSUS." A flash appeared in the eyes of the oldest Knox brother as he finally came face-to-face with his dogged shadow. "You think you know what’s best for me?" "YES! You wanna outshine your brothers who are ALSO competing at Colossus. You’ve got Steve coming from out of NOWHERE on the Sports Entertainment scale tearing things up in this tournament. You’ve got Simon and his friend, Connor, in the tag ranks, but they’re poised for a shot at gold while you choked on your first one and you’re gonna let Jiminy fucking Cricket get to you?" He pointed to the bag, containing the vibrating phone buzzing aloud like a Happy Meal toy. "That’s most likely Sonny right now, telling you that he’s got a lead on how Killean’s doing after we took his throat out on 168. Knowing him, he’ll show up to Colossus, and he’s had a little time to recover, but if you follow his lead, he’ll be slim pickings for you, Captain." "SOREN" He corrected. "I’m not donning the mask right now and this isn’t about me, Captain Justice, or any of this shit on me. You were right; this is about another man’s LIFE. I could’ve given the world just one more reason to hate wrestlers by almost choking another man to death on national television!" "Killean’s life?" Silver spat back. "Please. Killean Sirrajin is a man who got where he got because he, too, played the field when it best suited him. One of his two big title wins came from aligning with the enemy! Some of his career has been spent soaking in adoration, but when it came time to get an out with Tyler Nelson, he took it! You’re just taking his game and going pro!" "He didn’t try to strangle Clyde Walk…" "SHUT UP!" the shrill voice of Silver cut him off. The voice had somehow managed to become more menacing, the atmosphere chilling Soren to the bone. "Do you not watch the same ReVolution broadcast I do? That guy, Tyler Rayne? Not too long before you two were beating the hell out of each other, he was off nearly committing VEHICULAR ASSAULT and getting cheered for it. Lindsay Troy? She was off taking pictures of your mentor’s wife and kid during their separation just to get an edge over him and people still eat her shit up. There ARE no more good people, Soren, and you’d be wise to learn that." Unnerved by Silver’s words, Soren rose to his full height. Even towering over Mr. Silver, it didn’t instill any more confidence in him. "I’ve seen it come and go a thousand times!" Silver continued, twirling a finger next to his head as the international sign of somebody going coo-coo for Cocoa Puffs. "Rookies with lots of promise fight with their emotions because all they see and hear about is the fame, fortune, and the glory. Not a lot of them get to peer into the seedy underbelly. And right now, Soren, you’re neck-deep. You think if you really stop at this point, Killean’s gonna forgive you for what you did?" "WE did," Soren corrected once again. "No, YOU did." Silver interjected. "You had the chain, you did the dirty work. A week, two weeks, months, years from now, they’re gonna remember you as the guy that hung Killean Sirrajin out and if you keep going the way you’re going now, ironically, YOU’RE the one who’s going to choke." Reaching into his gym bag, Mr. Silver pulled out the buzzing phone and sneered. "I’ve said it to you once… destiny’s calling, boy. Just accept the charges." Having weathered the worst of the verbal storm, Soren nodded as he looked at the buzzing phone. Wrinkles emerged from Mr. Silver’s face as he smiled, silently egging Soren on to answer the phone cupped in his hands like some sort of Holy Grail. As Soren started to reach for it, a pair of hands belonging to neither man prevented him from doing so. The voice that belonged to those hands? All too familiar to Soren. "Son, if ya touch that phone, you’ll be regrettin’ it the rest of your life." Standing as healthy as the days he was while in his prime, Earl Knox stood proud, that fancy tailored brown suit he loved to wear still on his back. An awkward silence hung in the air before Soren finally found the gumption to speak. "Dad?" "This is stupid, Soren," he spoke proudly in that southern drawl as he hung the phone in front of his eldest son’s face. "Did I teach ya anything? Ya want to get somewhere in this business, you don’t listen to nobody but what’s in your heart, son. Deep down, ya know what’s right. Ya nearly robbed a little girl of her dad by listening to this Silver’s horseshit and if ya take whatever stupid insider tip he’s got going, it’s gonna eat at ya for the rest of your days. The fact you’re sitting here fighting with yourself is proof enough that you know this is wrong. Call off the match." "Good God, it’s like he’s reading shit straight out of a Disney flick!" The verbal jab practically blasted Earl Knox in the back of his head as Silver now stood behind both Knoxes, laughing to the brink of exhaustion. Earl burned a hole through Mr. Silver’s body as he poked a snarky head in between father and son. "Stop…" Soren belted, but to no avail as both wrestling legends began to size each other up. He tried to utter one syllable, but as is the case with any member of the Silver clan, when they spoke, nobody else got to. "Pfft, THIS is Earl "The Pearl" Knox? I’ve taken bigger shits than this guy." "Sonny, don’t…" "Sure would explain that funny walk of yours." "Dad, please…" "What’s YOUR finisher? The Jizzing Pearl Necklace? You actually make other GUYS submit with a move called that?" "Hey!" "I’ve submitted many people in my time, boy. And if World Wrestlin’ Entertainment didn’t exist, ya wouldn’t even HAVE a character right now!" "I’m a better father to Soren than you’ve EVER been!" "I AM Soren’s fat…" "SHUT IT! BOTH OF YOU!" The scream bounced off every single wall and launched out into the world, alarming passers-by in the street below. Misters Silver and Knox looked on in shock at Soren. Biting his lip, he clutched his chest, his frame subjugated to uncontrollable shaking. "Soren!" Earl cried as he ran to his son. Feeling like his stomach was being hacked away from the inside, he collapsed to a knee and felt the world spin around him. "He’s mine!" He could hear Silver’s looming tones scream in every direction. "He doesn’t love you anymore! I’m taking him places, not you!" Through all the noise, colors blurred together in a horrible mess until all he could see was black. ~ ~ ~ The ear-piercing screeches of the buzzing alarm clock shook Soren from the depths of unconsciousness. He glanced around his backdrop with great unfamiliarity before rolling a muscular arm over to shut off the source of his irritation. The Normison he took to battle the insomnia was doing a fucking NUMBER on his head, but it was worth it to partake of a good night’s sleep. "Fucking-A…" He steadied his hand and wrapped his fingertips around his Blackberry, in a hurry to stop the ringing. "Hello?" "Soren, you awake?" The voice of Sonny was on the other line. "Yeah." "Listen… I got confirmation. Killean’s at the arena and he's not looking so good. We need to move and we need to move fast if we're gonna take him out tonight. Any objections?" "…No." "Good. Then get cracking, I’ll meet you in an hour." Click. Free from restless nights, it was onward. To PRIME. To Killean Sirrajin. To Colossus. Soren muttered under breaths in tune with the throbbing of his temples. "I hate nightmares..."
