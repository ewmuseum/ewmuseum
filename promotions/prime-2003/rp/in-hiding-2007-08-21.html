In Hiding

21 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/21/07 Name: Yartesk Yelemench Last known criminal associations: -Cult of the Poison Tree Last known whereabouts: -Eastern Kentucky, Appalachian mountain chain Notes: -PhD from Calcutta -Contacts with IRA -Notable personal wealth -Connection to leader of Poison Tree as that of desired mentor/son profile ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Titan sat back, staring at the screen, his eyes grainy. He had moved everyone to safe houses, locations the FBI recommended. Stephanie hadn't wanted to leave him, but his highly visible stature in the public eye meant he was constantly moved and guarded when on the move. He was escorted to the airport, he had air marshalls on his flights, and agents escorting him to the arenas. The suggestion had been to basically cut from the PRIME roster, but Titan had managed to make his point: doing so would embolden the only missing member of the Cult into hitting targets. His parents' house had been swept, with nothing turning up there. Stephanie's apartment see4med to be safe as well, but his home had turned up a surveillence post. However, there were no actual items left, but the markings of it. Possibly a tarp set up with a telescope? He sighed and rubbed his forehead. This was not the kind of thing he wanted to deal with. Things had been going very well. His time with PRIME was picking up. He and Stephanie were getting along so well. His parents were happy where they were. And some pillhead cultist had to go and ruin the whole thing. A soft click brought his arm up, his fifty caliber Desert Eagle levelling. His blue eyes were cold and even, ready to end the entire affair right here and now. But the gun levelled at a familiar man. “So, 21, aside from apparently having a death wish, you can't warn someone before his car blows up?” Titan growled. He lowered the pistol and turned his chair to face the visitor. “Given that you never open your vehicle manually, not since the advent of those wonderful reomte openers,” the smaller man said, unconcerned. “Yelemench doesn't know how to build bombs that propel shrapnel, simply explosions.” He turned and took a seat in the small, dark room as the window clicked shut behind him. The heavy curtains fell closed. “I don't suppose I should bother asking how you know that, or how you knew this location,” Titan said, motioning to the only other chair in the room. “I know who Yelemench learned from, and this location isn't exactly a secret to me.” “And yet you answer anyway,” Titan sighed as he sat down. “How are my parents and Stephanie?” “They are actually in another state and quite secure. Yelemench could not get close.” “There's that, at least.” “What do you have planned now, Titan?” 21 asked, sitting perfectly upright, feet planted, arms on the chair arms. He was completely unencumbered and very much in control. Titan could guess that the fatigues the smaller man wore hid at least one firearm. “Probably to continue as normal. I can't exactly go hunt him down myself, but I can infuriate him by not changing my behavior.” “So you're attempting to anger him into making a mistake?” “That's the idea,” Titan replied. “You disagree?” “Hardly,” 21 said, slightly declining his head. “Yelemench looked to the Cult's leader as a mentor, and he's deserpate to have the man's approval.” “So I figured,” Titan said, pointing at the computer screen. “Who do you think put that there?” “I did.” “I know.” Titan sighed. “I hate you sometimes, you know that?” “I, in fact, do know that. So what is going on for you this week?” “Why exactly do you care?” 21 shook his head slightly. “What else am I going to do with my evenings?” “You mean you don't stalk other people and their families?” Titan responded. “Only when I'm working.” Titan nodded. “It's part of the build to this Roulette affair. I'm facing Troy and Ferguson and teaming with Gamble.” “You had a run-in with your opponents before?” Titan sighed. “Troy and I were on decent terms, Ferguson was one of the tag team I took on myself. It's not exactly like I'm going in without decent backup.” “But you don't know him that well,” 21 responded. “Pretty much,” the big man said. “I've been checking the routes from here to the airport,” 21 said, taking a packet of paper from one of ten pockets on his black attire. He extended it to Titan, who took it hestitantly. “Those are the safest routes, with points of highest potential danger marked.” Titan snapped the papers open, finding maps lined with each possible route and nodded. He looked up to see 21 at the window. “And Titan,” he said quietly, “keep your eyes open.”
