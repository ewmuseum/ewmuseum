Into The Abyss: Part XIII - Taking Out The Trash

22 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 22-04-09 11th January, 2007 They had barely spoken since stepping foot into the clinic, and the disbelief had struck Von Kelsig like a stroke. Ushered through the welcome tour and into their room for the duration of their stay had been a blur in the blink of an eye to him while Gloria skulked along at his back. Black tears streamed down her face as mascara ran over her puffy eyes, and she struggled to stifle her whining. Von Kelsig moped along grumbling under a veil of defeat as John and Frances, facility supervisor and chief medical consultant respectively, organized individual screenings for them. His story was not dissimilar to that of the wild-haired pixie, though the time-frame disturbed Frances. The mask of futility struggled to hide a truth he did not want to be revealed, for the sake of a brighter future. "So, Mr. Kelsig?" asked Frances. "It's Von Kelsig. Just call me Angelus." He croaked, crossing his legs and folding his arms. "Alright Angelus, how long were you using for?" Her first question came on the immediacy of his body language, he had put his guard up before she had even opened her mouth. He sat in silence for a few minutes. Frances observed the behaviour as dismay in having to delve back into the past and face his demons. Far from it, this was the moment where he would sink or swim. It was one thing walking into a rehabilitation clinic, quite another to admit to being a lowlife user but that was the choice he would have to make lest he reveal his true past. His answer would determine his fate from here on out. "I..." He bit his tongue, holding on to that last precious second before everything changed for good. "Take your time." Frances uttered, a sweet smile gracing her aged face. "...I don't know exactly, since November?" He threw a hand up and glanced away, huffing in frustration. He remembered wrestling for PRIME at the start of November, and dropping a match to his opponent to allow them to advance in some tournament, whilst he stepped away from the ring in a bid to lead a normal life outside the business. His kidnapping at the hands of Reuben Blanco's thugs on the 8th of November two days after that very match shattered that dream. "Really? Well might I say I am very impressed that you had the strength to find us so soon! The good Lord must be watching over you, Angelus!" Frances smiled, though it quickly mellowed with her next question, asked in part due to her fascination with his height. "How did you get involved in drugs?" Von Kelsig took a moment to reflect. His imprisonment and subsequent torture had felt like a lifetime, and yet it lasted roughly one month, every single day of which the heroin was forced upon him in large doses by a rotten criminal in Blanco. "It was kind of forced on me." He paced himself, using the vulnerability expected of him to take the time to think. "My life's been going kind of downhill recently. I suffered some personal losses, quit my job...usual story. I couldn't really deal with it all happening at once and went down a stupid route trying to block it all out with drink. Then drink wasn't enough..." "I understand." Frances cut in. "You say forced on you?" "I was surrounded by a lot of bad people, it was only a matter of time given my state of mind before something gave out..." He glanced up at her, pleased with his ability to cover his tracks and cater to her enquiries simultaneously. "And was there any other substance abuse other than alcohol and heroin?" She leaned forward, pen in hand poised over a clipboard of notes. "I wouldn't say alcohol was part of the problem. I used it to numb myself when I was feeling down but I never drank because I felt the need to drink. But no, other than heroin there was nothing else. Nothing else could do the job..." He bit his lip and cracked his knuckles, imagining Reuben echoing that sentiment before plunging the needle in again. Frances probed him further, taking in his medical history and comparing his case with Gloria's before deciding on a course of action. After the interview the decision was made to move them into the same room on the basis of their intrinsic relationship. The vehemence with which Gloria rambled about their hardships and intertwining destinies was typical of a standard inpatient, though it was obvious they were trying to distance themselves from the drug rather than allow themselves to fall into the downward spiral that had claimed so many others before. ---- So almost a month had passed since Von Kelsig had felt the cold sting of the needle piercing his skin, followed by the sensation of tiny hooks clawing through his veins and scoring euphoria into his flesh in their prickly wake. In the time that passed all he could muster from memory was the nip of the penetrating needle. "All done." The nurse pressed a cotton bud over the needle and gently pulled it from the vein. It didn't help the recovery process much to have to take a blood test and feel that old familiar sting, but he endured regardless. As he held the bud down on the tiny hole in the crook of his elbow, the nurse tapped the side of the syringe for no other reason than force of habit, regarding the mercurial viscosity of the blood. "I'll send this off for tests and we should hopefully have results in a couple days, Angelus. OK?" He nodded politely and lay back down on his bed as the nurse packed up her kit and the syringe and ambled off out of the room. Wiping away a coagulated speck on his arm Von Kelsig placed the cotton ball on his bedside cabinet and rolled over, clutching at his pillow. In the sanctity of their room the silence was drowned out by the most subtle gestures. Von Kelsig's pursed lips and muscles taut with indignation screamed volumes, evident in the glazed sheen in Gloria's eyes. Her petted lip and the way she tilted her head and exposed her neckline to him defined her vulnerability. "Hi..." She lay on the bed next to him curled up in the foetal position, head resting on clasped hands. Her face was devoid of the blotchy black mascara and light kiss of rouge on her cheeks, without which she might as well be naked. He glared at her, holding his tongue while she furrowed her brow and bit her lip. "Hi." He growled, clenching his fist against his chest and squeezing his thumb until the bone clicked. "You OK?" She asked, producing a tissue and dabbing her eyes. "Just dandy." "I'm sorry. I..." "Don't apologize." Von Kelsig interrupted. "I don't want to hear it. We're here now so don't waste your breath..." She buried her face into the pillow, smothering her wails. Von Kelsig sighed and stared at the floor at the sight of her sorrow, his resentment dissipating as guilt and heartache overcame him. "I didn't mean to snap at you like that Gloria...I'm sorry. I just...I didn't want this, I feel weak giving in and admitting defeat. I don't know why, I just hate the idea of being so vulnerable in front of people...especially in a situation like this." She snorted, turning to him and staring at him with big wet eyes before clearing her nose with the tissue and discarding it on the floor. "We couldn't have gone on much longer without help, Angelus..." She started, holding back the tears. "You're not superhuman. We're here because it's been nearly a month since your last hit and you're still suffering withdrawal symptoms. I know you're not chasing the dragon but it's affecting your health and that's why I called this place and booked us in......" She paused from her mini rant and took a breath, glancing away momentarily before meeting his gaze once more. "...Because I care about you." His heart leapt every time she conveyed her emotions, and even in his bitter state it warmed his soul. Now he felt truly guilty, and after a momentary silence sat up and moved across to her bed. She inched back giving him space and as soon as he sat down she threw her arms around him and nuzzled her face into his stomach. Enveloping her in his great arms, Von Kelsig kissed her on the head and stroked her hair. "I'm sorry. I'll give this place a shot, for you." They lost track of time, lying there in each other's arms. To overcome adversity would be a struggle, but he had Gloria now and Judging by her morals she would never leave his side. Maybe being human wasn't the worst thing in the world after all. ---- 12th January, 2007 When he woke the next day she was tucked under the duvet watching him. As their eyes met a soft smile grew on both their lips. "Mornin' gorgeous." Von Kelsig managed through a yawn. "Hey sleepy." She replied, quite alert for first thing in the morning. No sooner had he rubbed the sleep from his eyes than a knock came at the door and the same nurse that had taken his blood the day before poked her head in. "Are we awake?" She called. "Yup." "Yes ma'am." She pushed the door open with her ample rear, lifting a silver tray from a large trolley parked in the corridor and carrying it into the room. Von Kelsig reached up behind his headboard and tugged on the cord to open the blinds allowing sunlight to flood into the room. "Angelus Von Kelsig; lofexidine and buprenorphine. Gloria Carmichael; valium and buprenorphine." She waited for them to sit up in their beds before handing them their respective cups and waiting for them to down the pills before handing them a cup of water each. Gloria downed half a glass and stuck her tongue out to a satisfactory nod from the nurse, while Von Kelsig knocked it back like a shot before glancing at Gloria and opening his mouth wide for the nurse. "Alright folks, breakfast is in half an hour. Next dose is due at twelve." With no spare time for pleasantries the nurse left them once again, closing the door gently behind her on the way out. Von Kelsig listened to the sound of the trolley wheels creaking along the floor and stopping outside the next room. "Let's see what life's like at Vista Bay then..." He said, kicking off his sheets. As Gloria got out of bed he jumped to his feet, stretching and groaning as the sun cast a huge silhouette against the door in front of him of a hulking shadow with its arms raised in the air in a V. Later that morning they found themselves in the common room after a simple breakfast of cereal and toast. Von Kelsig found, rather to his dismay, that beyond their rooms and the common room the way was locked to inpatients, while "Play" by Moby caressed the airwaves from the PA system. Those on a 10 day detox program were allowed out with escort after a week or so, as he learned after sitting down to read the Tahoe Daily Tribune. "I wouldn't read that if I were you." Came the voice from over his shoulder. "Huh?" Von Kelsig tilted his head back to see a slim figure sleeking past him, spinning on his heels and dropping onto the sofa adjacent to him. "Excuse me?" He asked. "The literature in here is all lies. I've read every article in every crap publishing and pamphlet and it's all geared towards one thing." The stranger swayed a little before easing into his seat and crossing his legs, ducking his head and focusing on his twitching hands. "Uh, you've lost me pal." Von Kelsig chuckled, glancing across the room to see Gloria hunched in front of a computer, one of several in the room. "Look, you're new here so I don't expect you to understand. Just trust me OK? Everyone else knows the score that's why you're the only one in this room reading the paper." A spindly arm shot up, and the stranger raked at his long black hair, scraping it over his face and shifting on the sofa, pushing himself into the corner as though trying to dissolve into the leather. Von Kelsig quickly scanned the room. Sure enough of the occupants in the room not one was reading any form of literature, preferring rather to watch television or surf the internet. Cocking an eyebrow, he regarded the stranger as he struck something on his jeans and wiped his hand off on his striped shirt. "So what you in here for?" He tried to steer the conversation away from paranoid rambling. "That's not important, we're all in here for something aren't we? If we weren't we'd be out there for nothing and if there's nothing out there then what's in here but the reason we stayed away in the first place. You need to ask yourself if you were out there because you didn't want to end up in a place like this or if you're in here because what's out there is a lie?" The stranger rambled, his eyes rolling in his lolling head. "Look buddy this is a rehab center, not some nut house." Von Kelsig began. "Is it? You sure?" The stranger whispered, staring at the grain in the wood of the coffee table in front of him. "Pretty confident on that one. Look..." He tossed the paper on the table and turned to the paranoid man, crossing his legs and resting on the armrest of his chair. "My name's Angelus, what's yours?" The stranger ran his tongue over his teeth, deep in thought. Von Kelsig waited for a moment before sighing, shaking his head and turning away. "Whatever man. I don't have time for-..." "Call me Ishmael." "...Seriously?" Von Kelsig turned back, settling back into his chair. "I wouldn't lie, Angelus." Ishmael began. "And if I did or do then someone else has lied first. Which brings me to why we're here..." "I know why I'm here, Ishmael." "Of course you do Angelus, of course you do. No one's disputing that, but what about other people?" Ishmael asked, to which Von Kelsig narrowed his eyes. "What on earth are you talking about?" "Why would someone else want you here? There's nothing out there remember, so if there's something to be had in here who wants it and who's got it?" Ishmael shot a mad smile, revealing teeth tinged with yellow grime behind flaky lips. Von Kelsig drew no breath as Ishmael studied the giant's white shirt, almost able to make out the throbbing pulse beating below the skin and pounding the fabric. Maybe something had clicked, maybe he was given the wrong meds but something made sense to Von Kelsig in that instant. "Vegas is Vegas y'know? Plus it's a hot-bed anyway..." Gloria's words bounced around in his head like a ricocheting bullet mashing through his brain. A million thoughts ran through his head at once, and he barely noticed when Ishmael pounced out of his seat and skittered behind his chair. "I'll leave you with that thought...if you want me you know where to find me, have a whale of a time." He whispered in Von Kelsig's ear before disappearing through the double doors leading to the bedrooms. He drew no breath in the moment that the stark realization hit him like a high voltage shock. Regardless of whether the strange drug-addled vagrant was right or raving, he had struck upon an idea that Von Kelsig had never once contemplated. In his rancorous state he had shuffled into the clinic and admitted himself quite readily without question. Now the pieces were starting to come together. It had been Gloria who initially suggested Nevada, confident that Reuben would not operate out of the city or the surrounding area. Yet she blamed the hustle and bustle of society's underbelly in Vegas for his lack of action there. What if Blanco had operatives in Vegas? What if Gloria knew more than she was letting on? She had brought them here and he had followed her blindly without question, and now he was surrounded by strangers who could by all means be watching him, waiting to make their move. Was this even a rehabilitation center? Why were half the so-called "patients" dressed like a washed-up Steve Buscemi? He hadn't asked enough questions and now as he gently shifted on his seat and stared into the distance he wondered if something was going down. A quick glance to Gloria confirmed she was engrossed with whatever web page she was looking at. A burly Hispanic guard, faded black tattoos bleeding across his arms and a puffy goatee flapping with every chew of his gum sat watching a wall of monitors behind a secure window. His post was immediately beyond the locked double doors of the common room that led to the reception area and other wards of the clinic. Von Kelsig gazed at him, unaware he was being watched from a camera in the corner of the room by the guard. When he turned around and nodded at Von Kelsig with a smile it sent a chill down the giant's spine. Why was he nodding? I'm trapped. Had the ranting, bug-eyed vagabond unwittingly clued him to a conspiracy, or was he part of the plan? Beads of sweat formed on his forehead as he tried his hardest to look casual and uncaring, which everyone knows is the hardest façade to court, if you're guilty or hiding something that is. He tapped his thighs softly with his hands and sucked air between his teeth as he inhaled. The understanding that he may have been led into a trap subsided to confusion as to why Gloria, the woman who had sparked a little humanity in his life would simply act as a cohort to Blanco for some unknown reason. But it wasn't plausible, was it? He had to ask her. No! That would be foolish. He played the scenario over in his head several times. All they had been through, the conduct of the staff and patients, the entire set-up. It was too much for one man to orchestrate. Gloria wasn't capable of that, she wanted to be clean and she wanted him as he wanted her. Von Kelsig inched forward and rose to his feet, his heart beating harder as he approached Gloria. She saw movement from the corner of her eye and turned away from the monitor to greet him with a soft smile, extending a hand and latching onto his fingers, pulling him closer. "Hey look at this, I've been checking out places we can go after we're clean; with apartments for rent, or houses if you prefer. I've even been looking at job vacancies and scouting some independent wrestling circuits around the area. Sit down and-..." "Gloria." Von Kelsig cut in, gripping her hand and stroking his thumb along her dainty fingers. "Yeah? What is it Angelus, you don't look too hot. Is there something wrong with the pills you took?" She enquired, pushing her chair back and wincing as it scraped along her floor, grinding her nerves. "No, not that. Gloria is there any possibility that Reuben could find us here? I mean, when you said he wouldn't operate near Vegas were you sure on that or is there chance he could somehow catch up to us?" He kept his plea to a whisper as a couple of fellow inpatients logged off the computer next to Gloria and moved to the sofa where Ishmael had sat moments before. "No, Angelus...no I told you I was sure. We're safe out here, the further away we are from places like Detroit the better! Reuben won't venture out to places like this where he knows he can't make a profit somehow. Why, what's the matter honey?" Gloria cocked her head, rising from her seat and hugging Von Kelsig. "I...it's just..." The lump of tense fear was hard to swallow. "If you had any inclination something might happen you'd tell me wouldn't you?" "What?! Honey what's gotten you so agitated?? Is it your medicine? Look, I'll go get the nurse and she'll help. Wait here-..." "No! Gloria no it's not that, it's just...ah damn it." Von Kelsig sat her down at the computer and leaned against the wall beside her, the hairs on his arms bristling against the cool lemon yellow wallpaper. "What's wrong, Angelus?" She managed a look of stern concern and he drew a deep breath. "I don't know. I'm just paranoid." He was defeated, again. "It just feels like there's this spectre hanging over us after all the shit we've been through. I just want to know we're safe to get through this and safe to carry on with our lives. I want a life together with you and the thought of Blanco being around every corner or watching our every move is freaking me out a little..." Her heart started to beat quickly now, and she looked him up and down. "Look, we're safe. Especially here, clinics like this are always government funded or involved somehow, Reuben would be insane to make a move here. When I said he wouldn't tread near here I meant it. I'm not stupid Angelus, I know what I'm talking about and I spent enough time with Reuben to know his greed and lust for power will keep him tied to places like Detroit and such..." She paused for breath. "Maybe you should go lie down for a little while honey, before midday. Hm?" "Maybe, yeah I guess I could stand to catch a couple hours extra kip. OK. OK. Yeah, I'll get some sleep...sorry if I put you on edge, Gloria..." He bent down and kissed her on the lips, and shuffled off while Gloria watched him, turning back to the computer and wiping away a tear from her eye as Von Kelsig pushed through the double doors back to his room. ---- He was one door from his room when a loud clunking noise alerted him to activity in the room adjacent to his. Stopping, he leant into the door and pressed his ear against the hardwood, listening closely as quick breaths and grunts emanated from beyond the door. Glancing at the door, he shook his head and swatted at any notion. In the moment he stepped away from the door he heard a familiar voice. "One and one is three...it has to be. If two and two is five, it has to be!" Suddenly the door burst open and a flustered face poked out streaked with sweat, black hair plastered across the rivulets. Von Kelsig recognized Ishmael immediately, and before he could utter one sentence the strange inpatient had skittered behind him and pushed him into his room, slamming the door shut and darting for the window, trying in desperation to prise it open. "Those don't open past a couple inches, Ish." Von Kelsig motioned to the stiff window. "Keep us from hanging ourselves or escaping, y'know?" As the blinds shook with Ishmael's frenzied struggle, the sunlight flashed across Von Kelsig's eyes, blinding him momentarily to the graffiti scrawled on the walls in black marker. A vast equation was scrawled over the window pane and the beige wallpaper surrounding it. Long arrows swerved through vital sums to key points of the window, and upon seeing the markings on the wall Von Kelsig gasped and took a step back, watching Ishmael carefully. "What in tarnation you doing, boy? You owe me some goddamn answers!!" "They know. Angelus they know everything, I don't have much time. You see that?" He spun on his heels and pointed at a small surveillance camera tucked in the corner of the room staring down at them. "They've been watching me the whole time I haven't been here and now I'm done for. I tried to find a way, God did I try!" "Tried to find a way to what, exactly?" Von Kelsig queried, bracing himself on the linoleum for whatever was about to happen. "If the sausage is a crow left of the murder then you'll kill for a bite to eat. Remember that Angelus, you'll need to know that. Urrgh!!" Ishmael clawed at his tongue. "They gave me the good stuff, I can still taste it! Angelus it's all a lie! There's no way out but through their damn lies! I just-...if I can...only one remainder...I..." Ishmael seemed to die on the spot. The colour flushed from his skin and a zombified look filled his eyes, the tips of his fingers twitching while he stood stone dead in front of the window. Von Kelsig took a step back, taking care to move slowly while the only sign of movement on Ishmael was a sliver of sweat trickling down the hair matted to his face. Suddenly a succession of whooping coughs from the silence and startled the seven footer. "WHOA! What in the hell was that...?" Ishmael muttered after a massive shudder nearly rocked him off his feet. "Ishmael...you alright there brother?" Von Kelsig extended an open hand, waving his fingers across Ishmael's line of sight. "Wha-...who's Ishmael? Who are you?" "...What? It's me, Angelus...are you OK Ishmael?" Von Kelsig took a step forward, jumping as the sound of the double doors bursting open echoed out in the hall and the sound of dull footsteps thudding against the floor became louder. "Sorry buddy I never heard of Ishmael in my life..." He replied, pulling the hair from his face with his pinky and blinking rapidly to clear his vision. "Who the hell are you now?" Von Kelsig demanded. "What in the name of Christ are you doing here?" The man formerly known as Ishmael stumbled backwards and shook his head, clearing the popping sparks that fizzed around him. Staring at Von Kelsig he opened his mouth wide and slapped his ears with both hands to save them from popping. "Uh, what are you doing in my room, man?" He asked in a stoic tone. Before Von Kelsig could answer, the bedroom door burst open and the Hispanic guard barrelled in, followed by a slimmer, taller man with a buzz cut and a protruding bottom lip. Together they pushed Von Kelsig out the way and tackled the groggy stranger to the floor, pinning his arms behind his back and hauling him back to his feet. He never fought back, seemingly still preoccupied adverse after-effects. "Wait, where are you taking him?!" Von Kelsig pleaded, grabbing the Hispanic man by the shoulder, to which he stopped short of the door and obliged Von Kelsig with an answer, pushing words through short breath. "This is the son of a bitch that's been checking hisself into clinics around the state and getting doped up on shit he can't get on the street." He struggled to hold the 'son of a bitch' up before starting to walk away. "Who is he?!" Von Kelsig roared. "Don't nobody know his name, man. He just one dirty dude, and now he going to jail! Ain't that right home boy?" The guard taunted him in his face and dragged him along the corridor. They disappeared into the common room and upon his exit into the reception the minor disturbance excited muffled whispers and chuckles from the sane inpatients. "So he's a goddamn junkie?! I don't fucking believe it..." Von Kelsig smacked his forehead with his massive paw and fell against the wall, staring at the floor in disbelief. His anxiety and distress, the entire panic had been the result of a mind-bending hallucination courtesy of a dishevelled delusional drifter. Von Kelsig stared up at the camera, sliding down the wall and slumping to the floor. He let out a relieved chuckle and held his head in his hands, shaking ever so slightly. "Oh, if I ever meet a freak like that again, it'll be too soon..."
