Date: Read Twenty years. It’s closer than I’ve ever felt it. And it may very well be my last chance to retrieve it. I want nothing more than to climb that ladder at the Great American Nightmare and hold onto the title that’s awaited me for twenty years. I thought back in ’04 when I held the World Title of The Squared Circle in my grip that my hunger for self-affirming glory would be sated. My stomach still burbles and flails about wildly to this day, waiting for the perfect course to whet its appetite. I grow weary of attempting to have that void filled with other meaningless morsels. People from all walks of PRIME life have sought this gold, but not the way I do. Holding the title means that every victory that Troy’s been lucky enough to stumble onto against me would mean NOTHING. More importantly, it means that every bad choice, every corner cut, every last body stepped on and every life ruined in the name of my success was not in vain. The Great American Nightmare isn’t just a place for me to make my whims come to pass. Devin Shakur has finally stepped into the upper echelon of PRIME and has rubbed elbows with and thrown elbows against the greats with unmatched ferocity. Danny Ferguson is in his line of sight now and I would not wish to be in Mr. Ferguson’s shoes when nearly 30 years of pent-up aggression are unleashed upon him in countless waves. Tony Gamble is tired of being an afterthought. You see his size and you assume he’s a pushover. You throw all the “midgetOztinyshortshit” jokes you want, but while you’re sitting there poking fun at his size, look at some of the names he’s taken down in our sport. Angelo Deville. Jonathon Winters. Illustrious Face-Eater. Jason Snow… TWICE. Only a small handful can only claim that feat just once. And he’s going to recapture the glory that made him Jewel in the Crown last year when he goes after and finishes the job me and Shakur started on The Big Dog. Coming up on ReVolution 143, a shit-ton of great talent will rally against one another in one final bid to bring success their way. Sought-after momentum is the name of the game and the three of us will be DAMNED if we let those motherfuckers stunt our rise this close to the grand show. So we’ve set out on a tireless, life-altering voyage of self-discovery. We’ll laugh, we’ll cry, we’ll say some things we don’t mean, say some we DO mean in an attempt to show you just how dire a win for our camp is and how grave we take the situation. Our last sojourn before the Great American Nightmare is going to spell victory for our team. And want to know what you can do about it? Two things, as a roided-up monster me and Lindsay Troy know very well had to say. This time Silver slams down on the gas and Shakur is left running after the car with all of his speed. Gamble and Silver cannot contain themselves anymore and are pounding the steering wheel and dashboard respectively in laughter. I’M NOT PLAYING!” Shakur gets lost in the shuffle with the van gaining momentum. * * * * * * * “Damn, we need someone to finish the song.” Silver says, looking over at Gamble and smacking him in the back of the head. “We could have told him what to say. Gamble chuckles nervously, “Oh, hey Devin…You'll never guess what just happened.” Shakur is breathing heavily and stares up at Silver who runs a hand through his short hair and looks out the window. “Long time, no see, how goes it, sir?” Shakur grasps a hold of Silver’s head and slams it into the steering wheel, which makes him yell out.
