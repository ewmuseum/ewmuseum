Can You Say Masterpiece?

29 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/29/06 Okay, so I’m on the telephone with my good friend Daniel, bless his heart, and he tells me about a quaint little excursion that he’s planned for us. Now, you know me; I just LURRRRVE the outdoors. You find me a tree brimming with sap and boy oh boy, that’s all I need in life. Just to see nature flowing ever onward, to see life progressing is so enriching. Forget material goods, that suits me just fine, brother. Putting that aside, Daniel Ferguson (a wonderful little gentleman, but a little shy, I must say) is telling me and I just burst out, unable to contain myself. (Quite hard to start a paragraph with a “q” word, it is.) “Really Daniel, that is a marvelous idea!” Why, I don’t know how I could ever disagree with such a remarkably well-mannered and soft-spoken boy. Sometimes, I do wish he would speak his mind; it’s hardly ever that I know exactly what he’s thinking, but I can read my buddy like a book. Then again, when is he not smiling, that dapper pal o’ mine? So he drives over in his Prius and we load the mountain bikes in the back and gosh dangit if we don’t start singing the new Allison Krauss song! Well, we’re both a bit bashful of this, so things are a bit quiet while we tie the bikes down. I suppose you could call us both introverts, to some extent. I guess we just both appreciate the value of a good silence and always measure our words to speak in the most cautious and inoffensive way. The last thing we want to do is make others feel bad, like today when the FedEx guy brought my latest shipment from Omaha Steaks a full three hours late; do you know what I did? That’s right: I took him by the arm and asked him if he wanted some nice Blueberry Essence from my expansive collection of teas. I know that his job must be taxing and I just wanted him to know that I think what he does is vital; if the little people weren’t helping us all the time, we’d never make it to the highest of heights. Usually, we go for lovely rides on those awesome dual bikes (dare I call them bi-bikes…hyuk hyuk), but today we’re riding regular ol’ mountain bikes. My cell phone rings but I don’t answer. Sometimes you just have to get away from the clunky nature of technology and be liberated; besides, my cell phone is an old piece of junk. I use it for emergencies only. If I have to call someone, why can’t it wait until I get home? Then I got to wondering if our neighborhood Face-Eater (oh, it’s just a silly name, don’t worry; he’s quite the mild, meek character as well) might’ve been trying to find me. I race inside my shoddy two-room apartment (who needs luxury, huh?) to find that I indeed have a message. Very excited, I check the machine to hear the following exchange: “What? No, Timothy, I can’t help you with…oh, wait…Chandler? Hello, this is the Illustrious Face-Eater! I just feel so silly saying that name. Don’t you think I might shorten it? Oh, I’m on a tangent, silly me. My little Cub Scouts are gonna be going a little long today and I’d hate for ol’ Scout Leader Facey to disappoint them! Too bad today’s lesson was all about botany and herbs. I’ve got absolutely no idea which weed is which! Oh, I must sound like a Negative Nancy when I talk like that. Anyway, I’ll meet you and Daniel at the national park. And don’t worry; I’ve got all the money to pay you fellows back with. See you soon. I love you!” You know what? I don’t give a darn about Pete’s silly rules; I’m skipping right to ‘y’. I’m even interrupting the narrative, switching up the flow, reverting to the real world for one paragraph. Ain’t shit you can do about it. Nyah. Zany as it sounds, good listener, it’s not strange for us to often tell each other how much we share a platonic love. After all, my bosom buddies go everywhere with me. We go to the monster truck rallies , the NASCAR races, and community service at the old folks' home together. Well, Face-Eater doesn’t really like going there; being around all the medications and drugs makes him very uncomfortable. “Amigo, I don’t want to rush you, but I think maybe perhaps we should think about getting going at some point fairly soon, Chandler.” Daniel is so good about being wary of stepping on others’ toes. “But surely, Daniel. Race you to the car, friend?!” We both jog to the car, hoping to let the other win. Daniel defers to me, though, and lets me drive. Cruising for a short while gets us to the park and it’s a beautiful day out and we unload the mountain bikes and take a picturesque ride, rarely broaching the beautiful silence. We find ourselves upon a clearing where I pull out the surprise: picnic goodies! After several minutes of chatting very civilly about the possibility of gay marriage becoming legal, Face-Eater joins us! And I’m not one to focus on appearance, but my if he doesn’t look dashing in a simple khaki and sweater vest ensemble. Defying the ants to come find us (ha, just kidding…keep those little buggers away…haha, buggers!), we picnic to our hearts’ delight. I’ve got a surprisingly ravenous appetite, allowing myself more than my usual share of slightly-sugared ginger slices. No red meat since I was very young. Animal cruelty is just down right wrong. Eventually, it’s time to clean up the picnic, but our lovely little peace and quiet is ruined by a big ol’ mean mister bear. However, I’m the hero type, exactly the type to step in front of a bear for my two best friends, so I do the honorable thing. Fortunately, the bear isn’t rabid. Get this, though: his teeth are quite large and they tear through my puny neck quickly. How’s that for a death? It’s better that I can die in the arms of my two best friends though. And at least I know that I gave my life so that others could have a better world. Actually, you know what? Just pretend this RP was better. Kidding, kidding. This was the best thing you’ve ever read. No joke.
