18 August 2005

Date: 8/18/05 The scene fades in as a leather chair positioned in front of a roaring fire swivels around to reveal Michael Gough, still in a red terry cloth robe and slippers, still with a pipe in his mouth. The only addition is a Santa hat perched festively on his head. He takes a puff from the pipe and smiles at the camera. 'Good evening, and welcome back. You might be wandering why I'm wearing a Santa when it is far from Christmas time, indeed. Well, for this week's edition of the [name withheld] Series, I must take you back to Christmas of 2004. ReVolution 50, subtitled 'Christmas Comes Early,' had been a smash hit. History was made...Vampir Nosferatu defeated Tony Rolo in the Face A Legend Match, Brandon Youngblood released a promotional video in anticipation of his arrival to the company, Hoyt Williams returned from a leave of absence, Bastion Cossack defeated Tessa Windsor in a Sleigh Match, The HIT defeated Dani Furher in a Parking Lot Brawl, Black Angel retained the Universal Title against First Blood, Boda won a shot at the title of his choosing...and, most pertinent to what we are about to see, Nova won the 5-Star Title for the first time, in only his second match.' Gough puffs on the pipe, and a cat leaps from off-screen into his lap. He pets it lovingly. 'It was an interesting time for the Rising Star and new 5-Star Champion, an exciting time...especially in the lapse of two weeks between ReVolution 50 and 51. We go back now, to that time...' The screen shimmers. '...to Christmas time...to the third installment of the [name withheld] Series. Enjoy.' =Part I= “Is it appetite for destructionSlap a murder rap on this production, I touch somethin trust nuttinIron Lung/Twisted MetalI see em duckin my dart gun, bustin, from every angleWorldwide total carnage, the sickest flowthat be code named Agent Orange, killin you slowIt's only right you pay homageto those that's bout to blow like that shit up your nose, solid” - Method Man, “Reunited,” Wu-Tang Forever Nova uppercuts Ozric Mortimer, sending the behemoth crashing down to the mat twenty feet below. “Wasted effort by Mortimer, and…,” Nick begins, but stops mid-sentence as the focus shifts to Tyler Lopez, who flails for a moment before losing his balance altogether, falling backwards off the ladder and hitting the mat with a vicious THUD! The fans are on their feet... “WAIT A MINUTE!!!” Nick screams from his announce position. “FREEDOM! FREEDOM!” Richard cries as the fans turn their attention to Joshua Taro Freedom, who pushes the EMTs away and bolts back into the ring a moment too late as Nova unhooks the 5-Star Title and the bell rings. “No! NOOOO!” Richard screams, echoing the sentiments of the former 5-Star Champion. “And it's OVER!” Nick shouts, “It's OVER, it's OVER! And we have a NEW champion...NOVA is the new 5-Star Title Champion!!!” Nova slumps over the ladder, staring at the face-plate of the 5-Star Title in disbelief as JTF falls to his knees, his head in his hands. “This TLC match has left its mark on these four superstars...,” Nick comments, observing the carnage surrounding the ring. “Give me a break Nick!” Richard spits indignantly, “this match tipped the scales against Joshua Taro Freedom right from the start! He had to defend his title against three guys with no prior warning whatsoever! It wasn't long ago that he and Mike Wade had that shocking fall from the PRIME-A-Tron rigging at The Great American Nightmare and now this!” “Well Nova might have had to hand back the Tag Team Titles, but he's got his hands on some official gold this week and Motoki won't be taking this belt off of him!” Nick says before the camera pans back to Nova, who has climbed down off the ladder. The ref raises his arm, and Nova can only smile as the fans roar their approval. He takes the title, climbs out of the ring, and begins walking up the ramp. He stops at the entrance and turns around, lifting the 5-Star Title overhead as the crowd pops one more time. He pulls the curtain back and steps into the backstage area… …and stops cold as employees are lined up, clapping and shouting. “Whoa,” Nova whispers, overwhelmed. Immediately a group of men rush forward, hoisting Nova up onto their shoulders. “NO-VA! NO-VA! NO-VA!” “This bub’s for you, Nova!” a woman shouts, handing a bottle of champagne up to Nova as he is paraded through the backstage area. “Quality champipple! Sweet!” Nova says, overjoyed at the reaction his victory is receiving. “Alright, Nova! YEAH-HE-AH!” a man dressed, oddly enough, in a collared shirt and sweater vest, shouts as Nova passes by. “WHO’S THE CHAMP?! WOO-HOO!” Nova cries triumphantly, reaching down and slapping Oddly Out-of-Place and Overdressed Guy a high-five as he floats past. The celebratory troupe carries Nova into a room already packed with people and set him down in the middle of it.“Congratulations, Nova!” two busty blondes say in unison.They each undo the top two buttons of their tight pink tube-tops and expose their cleavage. One of them grabs Nova’s hand that’s gripping the bottle of champagne, undoes the cork, and lifts his arm up, twisting his hand around and pouring the champagne all over both of them. “SA-WEET!” Nova says, staring at the bub-soaked beauties before turning his attention to the rest of the room.Everyone stops what they’re doing to stare at the new 5-Star Champion. “Wow,” Nova says, awe-struck, “thanks so much for this. I really didn’t think me winning the 5-Star Title would cause this kind of reaction.” Everyone in the room erupts into laughter. That’s kinda weird, Nova thinks to himself, but shakes it off, “So…I mean, yeah…thanks a whole lot.”Another round of uproarious guffaws from everyone in the room... “Well,” Nova says with a chuckle, “you guys sure are a lively bunch. Been dipping into the punch bowl a bit?”People are leaning on each other, laughing so hard that tears are pouring down their cheeks. “Jesus, I feel like Dean Martin,” Nova says under his breath, “Umm…so…why did the chicken cross the road?” Everyone in the room continues to howl with laughter. “Because you’re a faggot!” Nova shouts with a grin, pointing at the nearest man he can find. No reaction as everyone in the room continues laughing. In the background, someone falls over, rolling around on the floor, holding their sides as screams of laughter continue to bounce off the walls. “You guys are laughing at anything I say,” Nova says to no one in particular. More laughing confirms this hypothesis. “I love big-breasted bitches that let me pour champagne all over their TAT-TAYS!” Nova shouts before adding a few pelvic thrusts to seal the package. All of a sudden, the room grows quiet. “Too much?” Nova asks quietly. The crowd in the room parts in half, revealing a plush king-sized bed. A woman bearing remarkable resemblance to 1982 Playboy Playmate of the Year Shannon Tweed sits on the plush bed in a skimpy red negligee. “Take me, Champ,” she moans as she begins to peel off her underwear. “SPICY!” Nova shouts, pumping his fists in the air. Nova wakes up. His head shoots up from his pillow, looking around for a moment at the surroundings of his shitty hotel room. His hair, slick with sweat, hangs out limply in all directions, his beard crusted with a bit of drool from sleep. “No,” he groans softly, “no…please, God, no. Don’t rob me of this opportunity!” Nova pounds his face back into his pillow. “Sleep! Must sleep!” he commands himself. “Don’t do this to me! Sleep! Sleep…sleep…” Slowly, his body relaxes. The crowd in the room begins to part in half. “Ha, ha!” Nova laughs victoriously, “That’s right! Where are ya? Nova’s back, baby!” The crowd separates completely, revealing a king-sized bed. Angela Lansbury sits atop the plush mattress, garbed in a revealing black negligee. “Take me, Champ,” Lansbury moans, licking her chapped and wrinkled lips. “AAAAHHH!” Nova shrieks. “NOT SPICY! NOT SPICY!” Showing agility that betrays her age, Angela leaps from the bed, tackling Nova to the floor. She leans down, her sweater-puppets drooping so tremendously that they rest on Nova’s stomach. Her breath is stale and uninviting. “I’ve got all the experience you need,” she hisses. “OH, MERCIFUL GOD!” Nova screams. “WAKE UP! I MUST WAKE UP! WAKE UP!” “WAKE UP!” Nova shouts, sitting upright in bed.He looks around…no mystery-solving, scantily-clad Jessica Fletchers in sight. “Oh, thank Christ,” Nova says, breathing a heavy sigh of relief. He rolls over and grabs his bowl and lighter off the nightstand. He sparks it, and immediately pulls it away. “Ugh,” Nova gags, sticking his tongue out in disgust, “cashed.” He stands up, scratching his ass and comparing in his head the dream celebration with the real one… Nova walks toward the parking lot. An employee he passes begins laughing. “Stole JTF’s belt, huh?” the employee asks, “good job. That prick deserves it.” Nova frowns at him before opening the door to the parking garage. “HEY! HEY, WAIT!” He runs over to a tow-truck in the process of hoisting a Nissan Altima onto its trailer. “Hey!” Nova yells at the tower, “That’s my rent-a-car!” “Do I look like I give a shit?” the tower replies angrily, “you’re parking in Arena Staff parking. I’m towing it.” The tow-truck begins to lurch forward before stopping abruptly. “Oh, I almost forgot,” the tower says, tossing a piece of paper out the window of the cab, “you got a ticket, too.” Nova stares down at the crumpled yellow slip of paper. “FUCK!” The phone in the hotel room begins to ring as Nova pulls on a pair of jeans. He leans forward and tries to hobble over to it. “WHOOOA!” Nova loses his balance, falling forward and snatching the phone receiver off of its receptacle. “OOF!” Nova hits the uniformly-decorated carpet chin-first, phone in hand. “Ugh…hello?” As Nova pulls the phone to his ear, the cord grows taut…too taut in fact, as the rest of the phone pulls off the top of the table and lands on Nova’s outstretched hand with a CLANG! “OWWW!” Nova shrieks, “Ohhh…ohhh….” “Nova? Nova, are you there?” comes a voice on the other end of the phone. “H-hello?” Nova groans, his hand throbbing in pain. “Nova! Hey! This is Matt Mills,” Matt explains. “What the hell do you want?” Nova replies crankily, trying to nestle the phone in his shoulder as he rubs his injured hand with his fully-operational one. “Hey, listen,” Matt says, “Motoki needs you to fly out to Kansas City for a Glad-Lock commercial shoot.” “Glad-Lock?!” Nova repeats incredulously. “Matt, you’re not serious. Talk to me here. Kansas City?” “Your flight leaves in four hours,” Matt continues, ignoring Nova’s pleas. “Why me?” Nova asks. “Well, we couldn’t find Angel,” Matt explains, “and Shadow didn’t want to do it. You’re the 5-Star Champion, and that means you’re next on the…” “Shadow ‘didn’t want to do it’?!” Nova shouts angrily, “what kind of bullshit is that?” “Well, I mean, come on, Nova…,” Matt says weakly, “you know about everything with the Dark Age, and especially after Lisieux…I mean…” “Oh, gimme a fuckin’ break, Matt!” Nova interjects. “Motoki needs to grow a nut or two and tell those idiots to put away their twenty-sided die and GET SOME FUCKING LIVES.” “Regardless, you’ve got the job, so get ready for your flight,” Matt says plainly. “And if I refuse this most shitty of assignments?” Nova asks. “Motoki told me to inform you that he would book you in a Handicap 5-Star Title match on ReVolution 51 against Tony Rolo and Boda if you didn’t comply with his wishes,” Matt answers. “WHAT?!” Nova cries in outrage. Nova is still cursing Motoki when he steps off the plane in Kansas City. Looking around, he sees a man dressed in a nice suit with a chauffeur’s hat and dark sunglasses on. He holds a sign that reads “Mr. Nova.” “Alright!” Nova says, cheering up a bit, “well, SOMETHING is going right. At least I get chauffeured transportation. Lemme guess…Lexus? Limousine?” Nova waves to the man and follows him outside the airport. Nova lights a cigarette and looks around. He sees a few people walking through a terminal toward the parking lot, a four-wheeler, two old women waiting on a bench for their rides, scattered pages from a newspaper blowing in the wind… Strange…he must have forgotten to pull up. How amateur! The man walks over to the four-wheeler and climbs on. He takes a key out of his pocket and starts the engine. Oh, FUCK, no. “HEY! HEY!” Nova shouts over the revving of the four-wheeler’s engine, “where is the car?!” The man stares at him blankly as the engine roars. “THE CAR!” Nova screams, “WHERE IS THE CAR?!” The man nods and gestures his hand to the “backseat,” as it were, of the four-wheeler. Let’s make this quick. Looking around, Nova speedily hops onto the back of the four-wheeler and gestures for the man to go. Forty minutes later… The four-wheeler stops outside of a collection of shabby buildings. Nova dislodges himself from the vehicle and steps, bow-legged, away from it. “Thank you!” he squeaks to the driver before limping inside the first building he sees. TO BE CONTINUED… =Part II= Rose slams the door behind her as she storms into Johnny “The Snake” Rodriguez’s office. Johnny looks up from some seemingly important paperwork to stare her in the face. Under his heated and intimidating glare, she steadies her resolves and speaks, loudly and firmly, as if to convince herself as much as him that she isn’t afraid. “I want out, Johnny,” she says quickly, “I’m done, see? I’m goin’ out west to staht ovah.” “So I hoid,” Johnny says, showing no physical reaction to this unheard-of intrusion into his place of work. “Yuse…yuse already hoid?” Rose stammers, unprepared for this kind of reaction. “Yeah, I hoid,” Johnny repeats calmly, “now listen here, you stupid broad. Yuse turn ya’ pretty little behind around and march outta this office now, and maybe, just MAYBE, I’ll fahget you evah had the gall to act like yuse was big enuff ta look me in da face.” For a moment, it looks as though Rose is going to stand down…but she pipes up again, her eyes full of fire. “NO, JOHNNY!” she cries defiantly, “I’m leavin’! And yuse ain’t gaht nuthin’ yuse can do abowt it! Good-bye, Johnny!” Rose turns around and walks out the door slamming it behind her. Johnny leaps up from his desk. “YOU’RE A STRIPPAH!” he screams at the door, “YOU’VE ALWAYS BEEN A STRIPPAH, AND YOU’LL ALWAYS BE A STRIP…strip…stripp-ah…ah…ah…AH-CHOO!” “CUT!!!” A fat bearded man with a head-set walks into Johnny’s office from the side, where a wall should be. “I’m sorry, Lou,” “Johnny” says, holding a finger under his nose, “I tried to hold it…” “Forget it, Tom,” Lou says, “Okay, people, let’s set it up again and get ready to roll tape…AND CAN SOMEONE GET THIS MAN A FUCKING KLEENEX?!” Nova stands at the entrance to the studio, staring around at the different sectioned-off areas reserved mostly for shitty Made for TV movies and crappy commercial shoots…crappy commercial shoots like the one he is here to shoot for. Goddamn Motoki. I can’t believe he’s forcing this on me. I’m gonna piss in that mother-fucker’s coffee the very first chance I get. “Mr. Novak! Mr. Novak!” Nova winces and turns at the sound of his wrestling moniker being butchered. It wouldn’t be the first time he’s been involved in a name mix-up. A fogged-up Dodge Caravan rocks back and forth as “All-Star” by Smashmouth, at that time #1 on the Pop Charts, plays over the speakers inside. “Oh! Oh! Ungh!” the girl moans, “Yeah! Yeah!” “That’s right,” Nova groans, “tell Nova how you want it!” “Nova?” the girl asks as she puts a hand against his chest stopping him, “I thought you said you were Brett Michaels from Poison.” “Ummm…,” Nova says before looking quickly for his nearest exit. “Mr. Novak!” a pudgy, balding man with glasses says again as he waddles up to the 5-Star Champion. “It’s just ‘Nova,’” Nova says, irritated, “no ‘Mr.’, no ‘k’ on the end…Nova.” “Right,” the man says, obviously paying little attention to this minor detail, “well, my name is Reggie…no ‘Mr.’ and no ‘k’ on the end, either! Ha, ha!” Nova stares at him blankly. “Ha, ha…ahem…you’re here for the Glad-Lock shoot, right?” Reggie asks. “Unfortunately,” Nova grumbles. “Right this way, then,” Reggie says, his nasal whistle of a voice already beginning to annoy the snot out of Nova. He follows Reggie inside towards one of the mini-studio shooting areas. Nova sees a large, cushiony black chair with a golden star painted on the back. ”Now that’s what I’m talking about!” Nova says as a grin spreads over his face, “first impressive thing I’ve seen since I’ve been here!” “Oh, that?” Reggie says, following Nova’s gaze over to the chair, “that’s for the kid who does the Old Navy holiday commercials.” Nova looks over at the adjacent studio, where a child dressed to the nines in tacky cold-weather gear looks into the camera positioned a few feet away from him. “And furry mittens, too!” the kid gushes, “cute” practically oozing out of his pores into the air like steam. Nova feels his blood begin to boil…he despises those commercials, he despises that damn kid, and thinks that the director of the Old Navy ads should be convicted of crimes against humanity and shoot…forthwith! “Yeah, that’s his chair,” Reggie says, “yours is over there.” Nova turns his gaze to a dilapidated dusty white plastic chair that looks like a toddler would have trouble squeezing into. “Great,” he mumbles. He walks over to his illustrious post and attempts to sit down with as much dignity and grace as he can muster on a day that so far has seen nothing but indignation for the new 5-Star Champion. “AHHH!” Nova squeaks as he tries to squeeze into the chair, “this is like reverse child-birth!” An astute observation, indeed, as Nova can feel his hip bones being forced inward. Finally, he pops into the chair. “Ahhh,” Nova breathes a sigh of relief, enjoying at last the comfort of a good… CRACK! POP! Without warning, the chair collapses, bits of plastic skipping across the cold concrete floor of the studio warehouse. “Nova! It’s time to begin shooting!” Reggie yells from a distance as he walks over. “Nova?” “Yes?” Nova replies from amidst the mound of broken chair parts, attempting to act as though absolutely nothing was out of the ordinary, “we’re shooting now?” “Uhh…yeah, we are,” Reggie says, staring down at Nova, “are you okay?” “Who, me?” Nova asks innocently, slapping a plastic chair leg against his palm, “ha, ha! Yeah, I’m fine…just…just, uh…yeah.” Nova stands up, brushing himself off. “Lead the way.” Reggie leads Nova over to a table set up under overhead lights, with three large black cameras positioned in front of it. “Here we are,” Reggie says, “okay, now the idea for the shoot is that you’re gonna hold a Glad-Lock bag full of food…” “What kind of food?” Nova interrupts. “Um, I dunno, we have all kinds,” Reggie replies with a shrug, “we figured we’d just shoot it a few times with different bags of food and then pick the one that looks the best. Anyway, you’ll hold up the bag, then turn it upside down and look into the camera and say, ‘Glad-Lock…in a world full of responsibilities, it’s just one less thing to worry about.’” Nova mouths the words, trying them out. “Sound good?” the man asks. “Set ‘er up, and let’s do this thing,” Nova replies. “Okay, get ready to roll tape!” the man yells. Nova walks up to the table. Aides rush up, quickly combing his hair and dusting his cheeks. One hands him a large Glad-Lock bag filled with spaghetti. “Okay,” the director calls from his chair, “and…ROLL TAPE!” “Glad-Lock,” Nova says with a grin, looking into the camera as he holds the bag up and quickly turns it upside down, “in a world full of responsi…AWW! AWWW!” The bag bursts at the bottom, and spaghetti splatters across the table and onto Nova’s clothes. ”CUT!” “For fuck’s sake…,” Nova groans as he pulls a chunk of tomato out of his hair. “I don’t know what happened!” Reggie says, waddling up to Nova as aides hurry to clean up the table and wipe Nova off, “must’ve been a faulty bag!” “YOU DON’T SAY, REGGIE!” Nova replies scathingly. “Here, try this one,” an aide says, handing Nova a big bag of creamed corn. “Okay, let’s try this again…” the director grunts. “GLAD-LOCK TAKE TWO!” a man yells from the sidelines. “Annnd…action!” the director says authoritatively. “Glad-Lock,” Nova says with a big smile as he turns the bag upside down in front of the camera, “in a world full of responsibilities, it’s just one less…” SPLASH! Nova stares angrily at Reggie as creamed corn drips off his nose. “CUT!” The aides rush in to clean him up and hand him another bag. “GLAD-LOCK, TAKE THREE!” ”In a world full of responsibilities, it’s just…” SPLAT! “FUCK, REGGIE!” Nova screams as broccoli and cheese soup soaks into his underwear, “I thought that this was supposed to be one less thing to worry about in a world full of responsibilities! How can I say it, if I don’t believe it?” The takes wear on. “GLAD-LOCK, TAKE SIX!” “In a world full of…” KER-SPLAT! “GLAD-LOCK, TAKE ELEVEN!” “In a world full of responsibilities, it’s one less….” SPLOT!! “GLAD-LOCK, TAKE TWENTY-TWO!” “In a world full of horrible tragedies, someone kill me plea-“ KA-SPLASH! After thirty-one takes, Nova slowly walks out of the building, various cream sauces and casseroles having crusted over his skin and solidified his hair into dread-locks that would put Shadow to shame. “Thanks for coming!” Reggie yells as Nova walks out to a taxi waiting for him, “Glad-Lock hopes to continue a profitable exchange with PRIME!” Nova turns around. “Reggie,” he begins, “if I wasn’t so tired, I would probably try to strangle you right now.” “Hmm,” Reggie says, a dumb grin still plastered across his face, “probably good that you’re tired then, huh?” Nova stares at him for a moment. “Probably.” That night, in his hotel room, Nova steps out of the bathroom, steam following him after a much-needed shower. He sits down on the bed, and turns on the television. He sees Bob Cratchett celebrating Christmas with his family in A Christmas Carol. “God bless us, everyone!” Tiny Tim says gleefully. Nova quickly changes the channel. He sees Jimmy Stewart, surrounded by friends and family, in It’s A Wonderful Life. “Hark, the herald, angels sing, glory to the new-born king!” they all sing in unison. Nova shakes his head and changes the channel again. It’s Miracle on 34th Street, the scene where the girl runs inside the house and the mother and love interest follow. ”There IS a swing, there IS a swing!” the girl cries from the backyard. Cue making out of the mom and love interest…and they’re all one big happy family. Family… Nova reaches over and grabs the phone, nervously. He punches in a few numbers and waits… ”Hi, you’ve reached Chris and Ariel…and Lulu! We’re not home right now, but if you’ll leave a message, we’ll get back to you as soon as we…” Nova hangs up. “Ah, this is stupid. What am I doing?” He looks back over at the phone…and picks it up again. He dials the numbers. ”Hi, you’ve reached Chris and Ariel…and Lulu! We’re not home right now, but if you’ll leave a message, we’ll get back to you as soon as we can! Thanks!” BEEP “Uh…,” Nova stammers, “um…Ariel. Ariel, it’s Chris. I was…I was just thinking about you. I want to talk to you, Ariel. I want to hear my daughter’s voice. Please. If you’re there, please pick up.” Nova waits a moment…nothing. “Well…I…I just felt like calling,” he continues, “M-merry Christmas, Ariel. Merry Christmas, Lulu, my baby girl.” Nova hangs up the phone and wipes a tear out of the corner of his eye as he lights a cigarette. I couldn’t believe it as I stared down at the 5-Star Title. The whole thing happened so fast. One minute Motoki’s goons are pounding the shit out of me…the next, I’m trying to work the pain out of my ribs in the locker room when someone tells me I’ve been drawn into the 5-Star Title match. The next, I’m walking down to the ring, desperately trying to come up with some kind of strategy for tables, and ladders, and chairs… …a strategy that immediately fell to shit as we all got swept up in the pandemonium. All that mattered was the belt, and the four of us scrambled towards it, like rats headed for the dry spot on a sinking ship. Strategy? After a while, my strategy devolved into this: A) Get up when you fall down.B) See A. That victory was everything I needed and more. I mean, hell, I know it’s only one match, but that loss to Wolfenden sort of shook me up. Got me thinking. Thinking about if I could still be where I thought I once was. The thing I’ve always prided myself on was that I knew, KNEW deep down that I could handle anyone. There was no one I had seen that I truly thought I could not beat, if I gave it my all. As stupid as it sounds, the debut loss made me start to question that. Maybe I had lost something. Or maybe I had just been King of the Mid-Carders. Maybe the competition in PTC was too much for me. But the win…jesus, the title…was like a salve on the wound inside my head. I’m still the guy who pinned a feared PRIME Hall of Famer to win the title I had stared at for so long. I’m still the guy who busted open the record for longest AW World Title reign… …and I’m still breaking records…and I’m gonna break more of them. Merry Christmas, PRIME. It’s gonna be one hell of a New Year.
