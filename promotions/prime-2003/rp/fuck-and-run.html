Date: ReV 169 “You pretentious mother fucker you...” Tyler Rayne cracks a smile in spite of the words just uttered. His fingers dance across the keypad of his phone, texting a furious reply to whatever message warranted this most recent vocalization. Katterina Wylde pushes herself from the black hood of her Charger and walks around the shiny grill to where her bestest of best friends is leaning against the wheel well. “Tsonda?” “Yeah. Little fairy fuck wins the Universal Championship and all of a sudden thinks he’s hot shit.” “All of a sudden?” “Point. Still. Gotta keep the kid in his place.” He shrugs, flashing Katt that infamous smile before shoving the phone down in his pocket. Her eyes wander up from his face to the Laund-0-Rama across the street. The windows are dark. Business closed for the evening. The Dark Angel leans back against the car. A more comfortable position from which to watch the laundromat. “You sure you want to do this?” Tyler doesn’t look up to answer the question. He’s far too busy pulling a pack of cigarettes from his jacket pocket to worry about eye contact. “Of course. Seph and I go way back. Old friends.” “She tried to kill you, Ty.” “She tried to shoot me. There’s a difference.” “Fine. She tried to shoot you.” “And she missed.” “So?” “So… no harm, no foul. Besides, she apologized for it.” “Because you were going to shoot her!” “I was not.” “Oh, bullshit! You had a gun to her cooch, Ty!” “I… nah, I wouldn’t have… did...?” The flame of his lighter puts an eerie orange glow to dancing across his stubble. He pulls a drag from the cigarette to help him concentrate. It works. A smile lights his face as a laugh escapes along with the cigarette smoke. “Yeah. I guess I did, didn’t I?” “Yeah.” “Think she’ll still be pissed about that?” “Yes, Ty. I do. This is a very bad idea.” “Oh, come on. Jesus, Katt, if I went around holding grudges against everyone who’s tried to kill me, I wouldn’t have any friends left, now would I?” His eyes narrow accusingly at her for all of half a second before the smile returns. Tyler pushes himself from the car and begins marching across the street. Katterina follows his lead. A stream of barely audible curses follows Katterina. The duo steps into the alley just at the side of the Laund-O-Rama. It’s a tight corridor of brick walls that leads them to the heavily reinforced steal door at the back of the building. Tyler puts his fist to the door and pounds three times. “Bad idea…” “Oh, will you hush it already?” Scuttling can be heard from the other side of the door. The tinks and tangs of metal. A piece of steel slides away from the door, creating a rectangular peephole through which a pair of steely blue eyes peer at the two in the alley. “What’chu want?” “We’re here for the game.” “Dunno wha’cher talkin’ ‘bout.” “The poker game, asshole. One in the back. What? You expect me to believe they’ve got you back here guarding the dry cleaning?” “Um… uh… dunno nothin’ ‘bout no—” “Look, I’ll make this easier. Just walk back there and tell Seph she has a visitor.” “Who?” “Persephone. Ya know, the cute little brunette runnin’ that poker game I was just talkin’ about.” “I dunno wha’cher talkin’ ‘bout, mister. Gonna havta ask ya ta leave, though. Laundry’s closed.” “Just tell her Rayne’s here, jackass. I guarantee she’ll want to see me.” The guard responds with a grunt and shuts the peephole. “I told you.” “Goram it, Katt. Have a little faith, will ya?” She rolls her eyes, crossing her arms over the creamy skin of that cleavage she’s always managing to show off. A soft tone emanates from Tyler’s pocket. The sound is muffled through the layer of denim, but it certainly rings distinctly like the Mario 1-Up ding. Tyler digs into his pocket, pulling the phone out and snapping it open to read the latest text. “You two certainly seem to be getting along well…” “Hmm? Yeah. Suppose so. Jealous?” “Hardly. I’m much prettier than he is.” “I dunno. Tink’s got this supposedly revolutionary exfoliation technique that… makes me sound like a fairy just mentioning it.” “Little bit.” “Thanks for coming, by the way. You know, just in case.” “Don’t mention. Couldn’t pass up the chance to watch you get shot again.” “She didn’t shoot me.” “She tried.” “And she missed. Why we gotta keep bringin’ this up? Can’t we all just—” The door opens with a heavy clang. “See. Just a little faith.” “Yeah, yeah.” He laughs as he steps through the door, Katterina right behind. Tyler strides confidently into the hidden recesses of the Laund-O-Rama. Katterina keeps a keen eye on the doorman, who’s keeping those steely blue eyes of his on her ass. The two meander through a short succession of corners and hallways before popping somewhat suddenly into the back room. It’s a dark and dingy little hole. Like an unkempt basement. Three Japanese businessmen sit around a table, cards in hand. Their attention isn’t on the game, though. It shifts between the two unexpected guests and the Desert Eagle gripped tightly in the hand of the short brunette at the head of the poker table. “Tyler Rayne. Never thought you’d be stupid enough to show your face ‘round here again.” “Oh come on now, Seph. Is that any way to treat a friend?” “We’re not friends, Rayne. Fact, if memory recalls… didn’t I try to kill you?” “Shoot me.” “Shoot you.” “Yes. But… you missed.” Her grip tightens around the Desert Eagle. “Suppose I’ll have to rectify that this time around.” “Didn’t come here for any trouble, Seph.” “You’re nothing but trouble, Rayne.” “She makes a good point.” “Thanks, Katt. Seph, look… now you and I got some history, but—” “You fucked me in the parking lot of a 7-11 and then left me there without any clothes.” “Yeah. There’s that. But—” “Gimme one good reason why I shouldn’t kill you where you stand.” “Besides the fact you can’t shoot worth shit?” The room isn’t terribly large by any means. It’s dark and Persephone, beautiful as she may be, weighs all of about a hundred pounds. Soaking wet. So when her finger jacks back on the trigger of that Desert Eagle the recoil is enough to nearly rip her shoulder out of socket. The Japanese men scatter. They’re probably screaming, but it’s hard to tell over the ringing echo of the Eagle’s mighty roar. The .50 caliber bullet intended for Rayne’s chest tears out a good chunk of ceiling instead. Tyler and Katterina are both across the room before Persephone can recover. It’s the Dark Angel that reaches Seph first, dropping the little entrepreneur with a vicious right hook. Tyler’s boot hits Persephone’s chest at about the same time her head hits the concrete floor. Dazed but not out, the trigger happy vixen finds herself staring into the barrel of her very own gun. “Now then…” “Look, Rayne, I…” “Shut the cock trap, Seph. I’m not gonna kill you.” Relief washes over the woman’s face and she even smiles up at him. “But if you piss me off, I will shoot you in the fuckin’ cunt. Saavy?” The smile fades as does the relief. Persephone nods grudgingly. “Good. You still got contacts out east?” Another nod. “There’s a Fed. Aidan Danvers. Kid went and got himself killed. I need to know who he talked to before that happened. I need you to find out for me.” “And why would I do that?” “Because you like your clit in one piece, Seph. Also… you come through… there’s a payment on the other end. That’s if the intel you get me is good. You’ll get a package in the mail tomorrow with all the necessary info. It’ll be enough to get the job done. You start diggin’ any deeper and I’ll let Katt come back to tear your fuckin’ clit off. With her teeth.” “Ooo. Sexy.” “You’re both crazy.” “You knew that ‘fore you tried to shoot me, Seph. We crystal on this?” “Yeah. We’re crystal.” “Shiny. Sorry ‘bout the game. Looks like you had a peach of a hand.” He smiles. It’s that fucking smile. The one that got her pants off in the first place. It’s enough to make her want to tear her hair out. Or his clothes off. She can’t decide. Persephone watches from the floor as the two quietly make their exit. She wipes at a stream of blood running down her nose and hates herself for wanting to go after him. After him for things other than revenge, anyway. “You are an asshole, Tyler Rayne.” News? No. But sometimes a good point is worth reiterating.
