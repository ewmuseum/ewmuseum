The Good Shepherd

27 October 2006

Date: 10-27-06 The Good ShepherdArson City Stories I believe in Arson City. In order to truly understand Arson City, you must come to accept two things. First and foremost, Vincent Senegar III is the undisputed sovereign of the city. He is King Herod and the citizens of Arson are his Jews. For 30 years he's basically ruled Arson City. He's run the government, his millions buy the judges, the jurors, sometimes even the attorneys. It made be a vile statement to make, but he is practically a god among men in Arson City. That's the first thing you must understand. The second thing is the fact that Arson city is a total dump. The citizens are feeble-minded, the government is corrupt, and violence is present on every street corner. And to think, at one time it was brewing with so much potential. That is until Vincent Senegar entered the picture and completely took over. Now the place is a ruin. Yet still I believe in Arson City... Even if it refuses to believe in me. ---- Alfred Senegar. Twenty-Five years old on this day, and his birthday celebration couldn't possibly be filled with any more grandeur. He was a made man, not because of his profession...he didn't even work. Instead, he was a made man because of a secret he possessed. A secret concerning his father, Vincent Senegar III, notorious Arson City crime lord. Alfred was set to testify in court against his father, revealing all of his father's shady robbings, killings, and government corruptions. Needless to say, they didn't share the greatest of relationships...that is until Vincent began to spoil his son with riches and money in an effort to keep him quiet. It worked, and now Alfred was enjoying the good life. But on this graceful evening, something evil was brewing. While on the inside a celebration was occurring, on the outside a master plan was being drawn up. At 10:24 PM, that master plan would be complete. It was now 10:23:45 The figure on the outside cocked his .22 handgun. 10:23:50 He pointed the figure through the opening of the facility window. 10:23:53 The gathered masses concluded with the 'Happy Birthday' song. 10:23:56 He placed his finger on the trigger. 10:23:59 He pulled the trigger. 10:24:00 Alfred Senegar fell to his death. ---- His blood-stained knuckles find themselves crashing through the solid steel bag once more. Sweat dripping profusely from his hair establishing a mild burn within his eyes, one in which he refused to take time to address. Through the pain, he fought...nailing the steel bag with every amount of force that he could muster. He'd been going at this thing for five hours, but the price of victory was too great for him to stop now. Two years he'd worked for this opportunity. The first time, he failed...a fact that he'd come to accept. The second time, he had it stripped away at the hands of a dastardly soul. This third time, however, would be the charm...he assured himself of it, and it was that very assurance that drove him to land one final shot unto the steel bag before his body collapsed unto the concrete floor. While planted against the cold, concrete floor, he finds the clicking of boots sounding through his ear. The sound grew closer as Olsig immediately yanked himself to sitting position in an effort to directly address the person who was making their presence felt inside of the gym. When he looked up, Olsig found himself in an awe of surprise. 'You resemble your father in every way, Vangelus.' Vincent Senegar III. A day after his son's brutal death, one would expect him to still be in a state of mourning. Instead, however, he stood before Vangelus , decked in his designer suit and costly shoes. Right hand in his pocket, he greeted Olsig with as much of a smile as his crooked face would allow. Senegar was not a man accustomed to smiling, therefore it didn't come as simple for him as it would for most. 'May I help you?' Came Olsig's replied as he pushed himself back unto a vertical base, paying little attention to Senegar's presence as he sauntered over to the refreshment table in order to snag a cold cup of Gatorade. 'More than even you could imagine. I take it you've heard of my son, Alfred's, tragic death, correct?' He placed the Gatorade atop the table in order to answer the question directed towards him. 'Indeed. My condolences.' 'I am much obliged. But at this point, I could use a little more than your condolences. Vangelus, I held my son dear to my heart, much like your father held you dear to his heart. If there was one thing me and Czar had in common, despite all of our opposing differences, was the fact that we both loved our children. Why, I remember holding little Alfred's hand in mind as we strolled across the park, his eyes filled with so much joy...as if the world was his own oyster. But now...now he's gone. I-..I want...' Suddenly, Vincent paused, taking the time to regather himself before his outburst of rage. 'I wanted revenge dammit!' For the first time since his arrival, Vangelus turned his attention towards his opposing counterpart who'd grown so weary that a small tear found itself trickling down his cheek...a sight that no man had ever seen prior. Senegar quickly brushed the tear away, crafting a look of anguish upon his aging face. 'Y-...You alright?' Olsig asked as he peered into the emotional eyes of the notorious crime lord. 'Fine. It's just...the boy meant so much to me and to have him snatched out of my hands like that pisses me off to no end. For years, before you were born even, I've ruled Arson City with an iron fist. For years, I've been untouchable. My money have practically made me the governor of this city for over thirty years...but for the first time, I feel as if my empire is crumbling before my eyes.' A pause came once more as Senegar ran a hand through his hair...a sure sign of depression accompanied by desperation. An adjust to the tie, and he continued on. 'I'm climbing the ranks of age, Vangelus...I don't have much time left on this earth, but the time left that I do have, I want to spend it gracefully. Alfred's death has restored n aura of peace within me. It has let me know that this politic shit ain't getting me nowhere. I've never been there for my kids and Alfred's death has made me guilty of that. In my waning years, I want to give them what I should have been giving them thirty years ago...a father.' 'And what's stopping you.' came the countered response from Vangelus. 'Vangelus...I'm going to die. Not by natural causes, not by health complications...I'm going to be murdered. Somebody has it out for me. Somebody wants to end Vincent Senegar III.' 'What makes you think that?' 'This...' Reaching deeply into the pocket of his designer slacks, Seneger retrieved a wrinkled letter made of plain wide rule paper and stained with a bad case of handwriting. He unfolded the letter, handing it to Vangelus who took the letter and immediately began to scan over the contents. The letter was short, but served the obvious objective of informing Senegar that his son Alfred's death was only the first step in a long line of murders that would ultimately accumulate with Vincent's. Someone had a hard on for Senegar and his family...as hard as that was to believe. 'I know you've got your wrestling career, Vangelus, and I've heard about your big title shot...but I desperately need you right now. I don't need Vangelus Olsig the wrestler...I need the guy prior to that. I need Vangelus Olsig the assassin. I need the Vangelus Olsig who solved any and every crime brought before him. I need the Vangelus Olsig who was exiled out of Bogotá, Columbia because of the threat he imposed. As a matter of fact, fuck that...I need you to reach down and pull out your father's aggression...that aggression which led him to kill my wife...that aggression that made me fear him. I need you to become Czar Olsig in order to save me from my fate, Vangelus.' 'I-I do-...' In one swift motion, Vincent fell to his knees before the feat of Olsig, glancing up at the son of his greatest rival all the while folding his hands in a form that suggested desperate begging. One look at him, and anybody could easily see that desperate begging was indeed the case. 'Please, Vangelus. You're the only one that can possibly help me. You're the only one I can depend on to get the job done. Any amount of cash, you name it...I'll pay you any amount. Just please, do this for me.' A moment of serenity occurred. The silence seemed more defeaning than anything else, matter of factly. With tears crawling down the cheeks of the kneeled Vincent Senegar, Vangelus thought it over. The anticipation of an answer served as a constantly stabbing knife in the heart of Senegar, but the end result, he hoped, would be well worth it. 'So let me get this straight...you engaged in a long, enduring war with my father, a war which accumulated in his death might I add, and you also made life here in Arson City absolute hell for me and the rest of the citizens...but now...now you want me to help you?' Vangelus stated. '...Please?' Olsig lowered himself a bit so that he could look Senegar dead in the eye while giving him his answer. 'Fuck...You.' And he walked off. A look of shock, anger, and confusion molded upon the face of Senegar who, shamefully, pushed himself back to his feet, brushing off his slacks as he addressed Vangelus one final time. 'Very well...I hope your conscious eats a hole through your heart!” Vangelus slammed the gym door shut. “...Bastard.' ---- Alexis Senegar. It had been another hard day in the office, and all she desired at this point was an opportunity at relaxation...and a nice cold glass of water. Keys dangling, she unlocked the door to her three-story home, funded by her father Vincent, of course. She stepped in, removing her jacket at the door and placing her briefcase there as well. Illuminating the home with light, she took a deep sigh and progressed unto the kitchen where she looked to quinch her thirst. Upon snagging a glass out of the cabinet, she heard footsteps coming from behind. Figuring it was her butler, Johnny, she gave him her usual greeting. 'Good Evening, Johnny.' She stated. But she didn't get a response. 'Good Evening, Johnny' She repeated, a bit louder this time. Still no response. She turned around, eager to know why Johnny wasn't responding. Instead, she found the .22 magnum pointed to her head. She screamed... And then she hit the floor. Alexis Senegar... Dead at 10:24:00. ---- The mood was somber. With two guards at the door, Vincent Senegar sat in serenity within his office, mourning the loss of his precious daughter. He did not shed a tear...he couldn't. Not in front of other people, atleast. All he could do was reminisce...reminisce on the good times they shared, because the bad times would only make him break down in tears. A father's love for his children cannot be described in mere words. Even a father as dastardly as Vincent. Yet suddenly, during the midst of this somber mood, the door flew wide open, prompting the guards to jump on the person entering. Upon realizing that it was none other than Vangelus Olsig, however, they ceased, knowing that if anyone had passage to the boss, it was the very man who's help he needed the most. Without so much as a glance at the guards, Vangelus stormed the desk of Senegar, looking him dead in the eye. 'Let's get one thing straight from the start...I'm not doing this for you. I'm doing this for Arson City and the people that inhabit it.' stated Vangelus with great fury present in his voice as well as in his eyes. 'So your conscious did eat a hole through your heart like I'd hoped, eh?' Came Senegar's immediate response. A small hint of joy brewed within his voice, for he knew that he now had the help he so desired. 'Your demeanor surely doesn't resemble that of a man whose just lost two children in the span of three days.' 'Is that your way of questioning my love for my children? Because if so, I'd suggest you cease immediately. I have thirty henchmen in this very building who could and would take your head off at the snap of my finger. That's how serious I am when it comes to my children.' 'You're in the wrong position to be making threats, don't you think? Remember, you came to ME for help, not the other way around. I don't have to be here...I could be training for the biggest match of my life, yet out of the goodness of my heart I've placed that aside in order to assist you in your time of need. So don't push it.' Shouted Vincent. The narrow look in his eyes and the rasp in his tone assured all of those within the room that he was a man who desperately loved his children and one who was willing to go to hell and beyond to defend them. Olsig sighed deeply at the way things were flowing between the two so early in their encounter. Vincent took a moment to regather himself before continuing on. 'Okay, alright...maybe we got off on a bad foot. Let's start over. Mister Olsig, I am delighted that you have decided to help me in this quest of mine.' 'Let's get on with it, Senegar.' 'Very well. As you may or may not know, reporters are labeling this 'killer' as the '10:24 Assassin' in response to both killings occurring at approximately 10:24 P.M.' 'Is that all the information you've managed to gather?' 'I am in mourning, Vangelus. Spare me a little mercy, aye?' Olsig rolled his eyes. 'Now, I've narrowed this thing down to two people. Number One...Craig Boss.' 'Craig Boss?' Olsig queried. 'Imported mob boss. He's from Carcer City...basically ruled that place with an iron fist. He's made his intentions of coming over here and doing the same quite clear.' 'And what better way to establish yourself than to kill the very man who's ruled Arson for over thirty years.' 'Precisely. And then theres this guy named Thomas. Real Scrawny guy, but he's got brain.' 'What's he have against you?' 'Couple of months ago one of my henchmen killed his favorite cousin. The whole thing blew up in court and we ended up counter-suing him, stating that he was behind the death of one of my henchmen, who was never killed by the way. Paid the judge a good amount of sum, got the court to rule in our favor, and then bragged about it afterwards. Him sending guys to destroy me wouldn't be all that surprising right about now.' Olsig took a moment to think over the situation to himself. He focused his attention upon Senegar once more. 'No offense, but your arguments seem very transparent. I mean, the way you put it, any random mob boss or victim of one of your attacks could be behind this thing? After all, what does a mob boss want? Ultimate control. How do you gain that? Kill of the guy who has it. In other words, that means every mob boss in this city is a suspect, in addition to your victims. I think we're going to need something a bit more concrete. Anyone else you can think of?' 'Well I-...' 'I know exactly who it is.' Suddenly, both men shifted their view to the open door way. Their stood Arnold Pointdexter, the head assistant to Vincent Senegar III. He was your typical corporate chronie...decked out in the orange bow tie, green suspenders, and penny loafers that died with disco. Briefcase in hand, he slowly entered the room with a grim look upon his freckled face. 'Mind telling us?' Olsig replied in a sarcastic manner. Out of nowhere, Pointdexter dug deep into his pockets, yanking out a small compact mirror in which he graciously handed to Vangelus Olsig. 'That guy.' 'Me?' Olsig states in a appalled manner. 'Him?' says Senegar in an equally appalled manner. 'Yes...Olsig. Let's be real here, who out of all the people in Arson has the biggest vendetta against the Senegar family? Olsig. His father's death fell at the hands of the Senegar family, for crying out loud...why wouldn't he want to return the favor?' 'You're insane.' Olsig shouted, slowly building up the courage to slap the bifocals off of Arnold's puny face. 'Am I? Why are you even here Olsig? Just yesterday you dismissed Vincent, here, when he came to you for help...that is until you realized that the farther away he was from you, the easier it'd be for him to figure out that you're the killer. So you come marching in here, acting as if you're doing this city some favor when in reality you're simply trying to get close enough so that you're not suspected of anything.' 'That's the most inane argument I've ever heard in my life.' Countered Olsig. 'Oh yeah? then explain this .22 handgun that I found in your car outside. The same .22 magnum used to kill Alexis.' 'It's evidence. And w-...' 'Evidence? Since when does the almighty grappler, Vangelus Olsig, retreat to a crime scene in order to gather 'evidence'?' 'ENOUGH!' shouted Senegar as he pushed himself out of his recliner chair. 'Pointdexter, you may excuse yourself. I can assure you that the matter is being handled with the utmost care. Me and Vangelus need to talk.' 'But, Sir I-...' 'Please, Arnold.' Quite upset with the sudden dismissal and possible 'cool' points lost with his boss, Arnold dropped his head and made his exit, taking the time to glance once more at Vangelus before departing. 'Don't believe a word that creep sa-...' 'I know, Vangelus. I fully suspect that you're telling the truth. Right now, however, I'm fearing for my-...my-...COUGH COUGH, COUGH COUGH.' 'You alright?' Olsig asks. Suddenly, Senegar clutches his heart as he descends into a fit of coughing rage. His face begins to change shades as he falls to one knee. His guards immediately rush to his aid, guiding him to his feet all the while quickly carrying him out of the room in order to gather help. 'Should I call one of his family members?' Olsig asks one of the guards. 'It's useless. Besides his sister, Bella, his family is vacationing overseas.' he responded. And with that, they hauled the elderly crime boss out of the office. Meanwhile, Olsig lingered on the last words of the guard. The rest of his family, besides his sister Bella, were vacationing overseas. That was just the information he needed. He now was ready to catch the 10:24 Assassin. ---- The night was cold and dreary, especially for Bella Senegar. She had witnessed the murders of her niece and nephew, and besides being struck with grief over their death, she found herself fearing for her own life. She made her way upstairs carefully, surveying her surroundings with every step in order to make sure that no one was there. Suddenly, she heard a noise coming from upstairs. She shivered with fear and ran back down to the living room floor, but yet she still heard the noise. She looked over and realized that the noise had been a cricket all along. She fell unto her couch, burying her face deep into her palms. She was paranoid. She looked at the clock...it was 10:23. She sat on the edge of the couch in absolute fear, literally counting down the second until 10:24. On the outside, however, another figure was making preparations. That figure was Vangelus Olsig who had positioned himself outside the house of Bella Senegar in an attempt to capture the 10:24 assassin. She was the only member of Senegar's family left in the country, so it only seemed logical that she would be the next victim. 10:24 struck. Nothing. On the inside, Bella gave a deep sigh of relief. On the outside, Vangelus did the same thing. 10:25 struck. Still nothing. Figuring that the killer had ceased for this evening, Vangelus packed up his things and began to make his departure. 10:26 struck. A scream followed by a loud gunshot. Bella Senegar fell to the floor in death. The 10:24 Assassin had flipped the script. Vangelus ran to the doorway of the house, but found himself on the ground after running into a figure who was exiting the same doorway. The figure was decked out in all black and was desperately attempting to get back to his feet in order to make the getaway. Vangelus Olsig was too quick for him, however, and immediately tackled him to the ground just as quickly as he'd gotten to his feet. He punched the figure once before yanking off the black ski mask, revealing the identity of the 10:24 Assassin. '...VINCENT!?!' ---- I believe in Arson City. Before me is a great journey, one in which I've fought too hard for. Yet, I find myself in a nasty predicament. I've been sidetracked by the poison that rids Arson City, but yet my eye is still focused on the prize. The Universal Championship. The Holy Grail. It's what I've worked for...what I've longed for. Theres no way I could let it slip through my hands now. But two gladiators stand in my path. Two gladiators with whom I've familiarized myself with greatly. Two gladiators who desire the same great prize in which I do. They, however, don't have an Arson City to side track them. They don't have a Vincent Senegar to keep them off of that in which they should truly be focusing. I, on the other hand, do. But in retrospect, I have a determination. A determination to succeed. Failure at this point is deadly. I cannot fail. No weapon formed against me shall prosper. I have a city to save... But no weapon. I have a crime boss to prosecute... But no weapon. I have a championship to obtain... But no weapon. Now is my chance. I may never receive another after this. I want this so badly that it hurts. But yet I comfort myself in knowing that my passion for this win extends much deeper than that of my opponents, and is that very passion that will lead me to victory. Nova will always be remembered as an enigma... Tchu will always be remembered as a dominating force... But I, Vangelus Olsig, will be remembered as the man that conquered them both. I believe in Arson City... Even when it, and the rest of the world, refuses to believe in me.
