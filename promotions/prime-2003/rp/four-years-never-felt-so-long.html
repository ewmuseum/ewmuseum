Four Years Never Felt So Long

Date: Today friend•shipPronunciation: 'fren(d)-'shipFunction: noun Webster’s defines the word friendship as the state of being friends. It’s also defined as the quality or state of being friendly. Now while that may not be too in-depth, you can never really understand the word until you experience what is considered a true friendship. In this case, I have a friend. Yeah, I still consider Matt Ward my friend. As it turns out, more than half the time, we are friendly towards one another. The unique feel of our friendship is that while we may be at each others throats sometimes, we are adults and can sit down and have a talk like nothing was ever wrong in the first place. Really I don’t know what it is. It’s very hard to explain. I can’t explain it to Charity and as everyone has seen, she is quite confused as to how Matt and I can just sit and talk like old pals getting together to watch a football game and have a beer. Not to mention, males bond in a way that can seem disturbing. They swear at each other, like they are mad and in the next breath, they share a laugh about it all and their anger is non-existent. It goes against most of human nature, yet it all seems natural. Explain that one… cause I can’t. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Thursday, August 10, 2006 12:30 PM “So what are you saying? This is getting to be too much for you?” The hum of the crowd in the diner makes it somewhat of a comfortable location to be when discussing personal issues. Couples regularly attend these kinds of places in fear of losing control of their emotions in private. In this scenario, the man was just having problems understanding the point the woman was trying to get across. “Kill, this all started out as something that all friends go through. You and Matt were great friends and then you had a falling out. But now, the personal shots are being fired and I’ll be honest, I’m not comfortable with what happened at 103.” Charity looked perturbed. Just over one week before, she encountered the currently estranged wife of Matt Ward. Mary always made it clear that she would never support Matt with his wrestling career, yet there she was, in the ring and in person last Wednesday. That alone could confuse anyone. But apparently, it was all her well thought out plan. Show up, have Matt worry about her safety and then fire it right back at him. Even Charity had to admit that Mary was a woman who knows what she wants and she will do what it takes to get it. Matt’s got himself in a dilly of a pickle. “We talked about this before Charity. Remember the long discussions? We wanted to get into his head like no one else has. Why do you think he has the kind of record he does? He thinks no one can get into his head.” Killean sounded confused with the sudden change of heart by Charity. First she was the one who was all ‘DEATH TO TCHU! YAY!’ Turns out she didn’t want it to go this far. “That was why I looked and felt so uncomfortable last Wednesday. I looked over to you and all you had was a big smile on your face. But I couldn’t have that smile. To me, this has gone too far.” “Charity, I need you to stick with me through Sunday. It’s that simple. Once I beat Matt on Wednesday, his spirit will be crushed. His record since he joined PRIME won’t matter. No one will remember him for only losing twice in his first eighteen matches. Sunday, when he loses, he will be the guy remembered for dropping the ball at the big show… the center stage. After Sunday, Matt’s finished and that’s that.” Charity still looked distracted. She listened to what Killean said. He was right. They had come this far and they needed to see it through to the end. But she couldn’t get her mind set straight to that. Every time the thought crossed her mind, she couldn’t help but see that it’s going too far. Mary came to ReVolution and no one thought that would happen. “I’ll try Kill. That’s all I can do.” Killean nodded in response to Charity trying to keep her wits about herself. “That’s all I ask.” Killean wiped his mouth and drank the rest of his milk. Yeah, milk does a body good. “Now it’s time to go meet Dave for a quick training session.” “Dave?” “Vegas. You know that.” “Actually this is the first time you have referred to him by his first name.” Killean waves the waitress over for the check. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ So where is it that I stand now? Do I want to send Matt home in a pine box? No. Do I want to send him home as a cripple? Believe it or not, I don’t. I want to send him home so he can once again be a father but he wouldn’t be able to do that if he was hurt. I want to punish Matt. I want to make him see that PRIME is MY stomping grounds. I’m a hall of fame wrestler here. I am a two time Universal Champion, a two time Intense Champion and I have been an official member of the PRIME roster for over two years now. I have been to this point before. I have been to the Colossus main event. Sure I lost last time out, but this match will be one on one. This is the biggest match of both of our careers. People all around the world will be tuning in to watch. People all around the world will be glued to their TV’s watching each move executed, each counter performed and each punch thrown. This is not Matt’s time. This is not the year of Tchu. This is the year of Supremacy. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Thursday, August 10, 2006 1:45 PM Killean reached into the trunk for his gym bag. After he pulled it out, he closes the trunk with a slam and makes his way to the door. The sun was beating down, heating up anything in its path. His sunglasses were reflecting the bright light. He opened the door, ready to step into his last practice before leaving Winnipeg to head to Florida. He was ready. That is, until he stares into the long faces of Vegas and his delivery guys. “Should I even ask?” “Kill, you should sit down.” “Great.” Killean tosses his gym bag on Vegas’ desk. Vegas stands up as Killean sits down behind his own desk. This was Killean’s life outside of wrestling. He ran a club and a delivery service in Winnipeg with Vegas. The delivery service was run out of the back dirty room of the bar. But the club was not your regular, everyday drinking club. It was a strip club. “Kill, man we have a big problem.” Vegas started to explain. Apparently there was a delivery guy named Spinks and he joined the team about three months back. Killean had met him once when he was first hired and he seemed like a real down to earth kid. That was what was needed in the business because sometimes, depending on how busy things got, these guys would be working twelve hour days. If they played as a part of the team, they would be taken care of. Killean and Vegas paid their delivery guys very well, perhaps better than even some of the guys who worked for the real big businesses. Fact was, half of their business was hand off jobs from the big boys of courier and delivery. “Are you telling me…” Killean had a look of anger on his face but he also was feeling pain in his heart for the kid who seemed so straight forward and just wanted to work and make the money he needed. “… That Spinks has been dealing drugs in the area he’s been working?” “Pretty much boss.” One of the drivers spoke up. His name was Gregory. He was one of the most reliable drivers that Killean and Vegas had. That is, except for Spinks. Spinks never dropped a job, he never made a mistake and he never complained. He did his work and went home with a smile on his face. Gregory was much the same way. Killean meanwhile stood up behind his desk and motioned for Gregory to come over to him. When the driver got there, Killean grabbed him by the shoulders, looked right into his eyes and almost struck the fear of god into the poor kid. “Tell me Gregory, tell me exactly how you know this. And I better not hear that you are playing me.” Killean was stern, brutal and upset. He had thought that he would see Spinks one day become the manager of the club. He was just determined to make it. Killean let Gregory sit in a chair in front of the desk. Killean sat back down behind his desk, his hands clasped together, waiting to hear the complete explanation. “Well, a couple weeks back, we had noticed that Spinks was working mostly in the North End area. So Vegas decided that since he knew the area so well, he would make him the go to guy to get things done around there. Everything went well until this morning.” Vegas came to stand beside Killean. He could see that Killean was distracted. He had the biggest match of his life coming up in only a few short days. Now he was being told that his chosen guy in his business at home was dealing illegal drugs along his routes. He put his hand on Killean’s shoulder and could feel how tense he was. “I went outside when Spinks came back from a couple deliveries. It was around eleven o’clock and I was getting ready to go on a couple runs before lunch. When I went to go say hi to him, I noticed that he had been trying to clean up a spill of some sorts. He didn’t think I saw what he was doing, but he had a small gym bag and I saw a bag of weed in there and I also saw some coke. I just walked away with a smile like I saw nothing and as soon as I came back for lunch, I told Vegas. We sat down in the club and had lunch and now we have been waiting for you to get here.” Gregory caught his breath. It was a long yet detailed enough explanation. Killean nodded and looked up to Vegas. “Find out where he is now.” He then got to his feet and went around his desk to see Gregory. Killean shook hands with the driver. “Thank you for bringing this to our attention. You did the right thing kid.” Gregory nodded in response. “I’m lookin’ at the board and I’m seeing that we aren’t that busy today. Take the rest of the day off. You’ve worked for five hours today. Go home with another five in extra pay.” Gregory smiled and thanked Killean. As he took off out the door, Killean turned to the remaining four drivers standing off to the side. “You guys, I’m sorry that you had to see all this happen. I sincerely hope that you learn something from this because unfortunately, Spinks will have to be punished, if not fired. We need to guys to clear this board today so I got a little extra incentive for you. This company will not be turning a profit for the rest of the day. Every penny that is being paid to us for these deliveries, is in your pocket. If one delivery earns this business two hundred dollars, you get two hundred for delivering it. So get your asses going and earn some big bucks today.” All four drivers had big gleaming smiles on their face. They ran to the chart and picked their shipments. Killean made his way back to where Vegas was sitting and he heard one of the drivers running out the door yelling, “Two fifty baby. WOO!” “Did you find him?” “He’s on his way to his first delivery out of three. Once he finishes the third, he SAID he is making a stop to see a friend quickly. I Okayed it because that could be our chance to catch him in the act.” “Good. Sad as it is, he needs to be taught a lesson.” Thirty Minutes Later… The twenty-one inch rims turned slowly through the back lane in the North End of Winnipeg. The Dodge Ram SRT10 came to a halt about fifty feet behind the Mazda RX7 that belonged to Killean’s driver Spinks. Vegas was behind the wheel of the truck as both he and Killean looked on as Spinks went back to his trunk and pulled out the gym bag in question that Gregory claimed contained drugs. Spinks closed the trunk and made his way behind a fence with the bag. Vegas slowly began to pull ahead until he came to a stop behind the car of Spinks. “What do you suppose we do?” Vegas asked. “Take one end of this truck club, I’ll take the other. We need to make sure he is doing what we think he is doing.” Vegas shut off the truck and took his end of the truck club. Both he and Killean made their way around the truck and then around the fence where they saw Spinks standing with one guy and the bag held out in front of him. Spinks had his back to them, so once they circled the fence, the other guy could see the two large men approaching. Vegas, a six foot six inch tall black man walked along side the six foot four inch Killean. The guy dropped what he was holding and turned to disappear from the scene. Spinks yelled out at him in confusion, and then was too slow to turn around when Vegas smacked him in the shoulder with part of the truck club. The kid crumpled to the concrete, revealing exactly what Killean and Vegas feared he was doing. Spinks rolled away from his bag to look at his assailants. When he turned and saw Vegas and Killean standing with a weapon in their hands, his eyes went wide as saucers. Meanwhile, Killean bent down to take a look at what exactly was in the gym bag. Numerous small baggies of marijuana were inside as well as various other drug paraphernalia. In the side pocket, Killean found a few tightly wrapped packages of cocaine. “Guys… please, let me explain.” Spinks said as he held his shoulder. “There is no explanation…” Killean said as he tossed the bag across the driveway. It hit a wall and some of the packages of drugs broke open and spilled out on the concrete. There was fire in his eyes and there was anger in the way he moved. He was perhaps even more upset than all of that because he felt betrayed. He closed in to where Spinks was on the ground and picked him up by his neck. He then tossed him back against the fence and cracked one of the fence boards. “Did you really think you could do this behind our back?! Did you really think you could put yourself and OUR business at risk by trying to come up with some kinda stupid pay day?!” Spinks cringed as Killean landed one closed fist right into his left cheek. He slouched against the broken fence board and would have fallen to the ground had Killean not had a death grip on the front of his shirt. Vegas then moved in and confronted the younger driver. “What could you have possibly been thinking? Honestly we should just leave you here and let the cops pick you up.” “That’s exactly what we will do…” Vegas and Spinks both looked over to Killean. Killean tucked away the part of the truck club that he had and gave nothing but a disgusted look to Spinks. “You will be left here to face the police. All these drugs and the contents of that entire gym bag will be left with you here for them to pick up.” Killean wound up and kicked the kid right in the side of the head. He fell further back and through the fence board, landing on his side… unconscious. Killean then marched over to where the drugs were sitting on the ground. He picked up the bag and some of the contents spilled out some more. He then tosses the bag on top of the kid and wiped his hands of the situation. Vegas and Killean made their way back to the truck. After one final look back at the unconscious driver, Killean pulled out his cell phone and dialed the police to come and take care of the kid. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Four years… For four years I have been involved one way or another with Matthew Ward. We could have been at each others throats or we could have been sitting down and sharing a drink and laughing together. At one time, we even sat across from each other in his office and I opened up my life to him. He was writing it all down on a notepad, taking a journal of my entire life. I’ll be the first to admit that Matt helped me become the secure and stable individual that I am today. I could never put a price tag on the help that he gave me. But to be honest, he should have stayed in his leather office chair and buried his face in the notepad while trying to help someone else. What Matt doesn’t see is that he had a hand in creating the monster that is today’s Universal Champion. Matt had a small hand in where I am today, personally and professionally. The only thing is, come Colossus, the creation that Matt had a hand in creating will be all over him. I’ll be relentless until he can no longer continue. Until his shoulders are counted down for three or until his hand hits the mat for a tap out, I won’t stop. Never did Matt think he would step into the ring again before he joined. Now, he will be wishing he never did. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Back at the club, Killean and Vegas were sitting at the bar having a drink and some nachos. Killean walked behind the bar and got himself a refill of his beer. Vegas broke the silence that had been going on for probably about ten minutes. “Was it really a good thing to hand him over to the cops like that?” “I think so. He may have been one of our best drivers. However he was willing to put our business at risk by pulling these deals on company time. He was a liability.” Killean sat down at the bar again and took a sip of his beer. Vegas put a few nachos in his mouth and both guys nodded, knowing that overall, they were on the same page about the situation. “She’s got talent, I’ll say that.” Vegas was looking over at one of the dancers swinging around a pole. She flashes a smile to him as Killean looks as well. He nods. “Don’t I have talent?” Charity came into their area and put her arms around Vegas from behind, giving him a hug. She then moved over to Killean and kissed him on the cheek. Killean put her arm around her midsection and pulled her close. “Want some nachos babe?” “Sure, but I’ll take a sip of your beer first.” Girls drinking beer, it was not uncommon but it was not all that common either. Killean liked that fact that Charity shared the same taste in drinks as he did. Vegas reached over and grabbed Charity by the belt of her pants and dragged her playfully to him. “Kill you’d better keep a tighter grip on this girl. Otherwise she will be one of my star dancers.” Charity laughed. “I’ll keep that in mind if I ever want another job… Dave…” Vegas let go of her and his eyes were wide. Killean smiled. “It is your name isn’t it?” “I hate that name you know that.” Charity danced around to come beside Killean again. She took a nacho and ate it, and then wiped her hands. “We gotta be at the air port in two hours hun” She took another sip of his beer to wash the nachos down before Killean took it from her and downed the entire glass. “Vegas man, we gotta jump.” Killean stood up beside his friend and shared a handshake and a hug. “Look, sorry about the training and stuff.” “No worries, the business was more important. I’ve had plenty of training and then all day Saturday as well. I’ll be cool.” Charity gave Vegas another hug and then both she and Killean made their way for the door. “Good luck Sunday!” Killean waved as they both left the club. Vegas went back to his beer as the song stopped. He motioned over to the girl who was dancing and he accompanied her to the back room. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tick tock… Tick tock… Tick Tock… The timer is counting down. Killean Sirrajin goes one on one with Tchu. “The Inhuman Being” up against “The Supreme Machine”. Years in the making. Sunday is not that far away. The days could not go any slower than they are now. I know that you have been thinking about it too Matt. Head turned forward, looking at nothing in particular. Your mind goes blank and then you see my name. Where ever you turn you hear the name, “Killean”. No matter what you do, you think about Colossus Three. It’s constantly on your mind. Wrestling is your love and your passion. You will do anything to win this match. I know that. Think no less of me. This is the biggest match of our careers. Finally the people get to see Killean and Tchu tie it up one on one. Will it be enough for us to only tie it up once? I doubt it. But my goal has always been the same. I want to send you home crushed enough that you will never be able to show your face here again. Remember that Matt. Do you have the heart to stop me? Tick tock… Tick tock… Tick tock… The waiting is over.
