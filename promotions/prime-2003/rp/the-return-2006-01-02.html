The Return

2 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/02/06 *Note: Credit Song lyrics to Theory of a Deadman*'The country's gone to hellBut believe me I didn't do itYou left me all aloneAnd now they call me stupid' Have you ever known what it feels like to be on top of the world and then have it snatched away from you in an instant? Have you ever been living out your life long dream one moment only to see it stripped away from you the very next? If you have then you know exactly what it is like to be in my position. You'll have some idea what it is like to be what I used to be. I was a champion. I was feared. I had everything for one moment. For one moment I was right there at the top of the mountain, completely on the fast track to every dream I'd ever had. I was ready to take my place in wrestling history. I was ready to secure my spot as one of the greatest tag team wrestlers of all time. To leave my name imprinted in all the history books of not only PRIME but of wrestling period. Now what am I? I'm nothing but a forgotten wrestler. No one remembers me anymore. No one cares that I was once part of the most dominant tag team in PRIME's history. No one could possibly tell you that I was one of the founding members of The Imperium. On second thought at this point in time that might not be the most horrible thing for people to forget. Which brings me to my biggest problem right now. My friend Hoyt Williams. The guy who was suppose to helping to look out for my best intrests. The same man who sent my ass to fricken cold ass Antartica. A 'special' and important assignment huh? Try again Hoyt. That was nothing more than crap. I'm not sure why you sent us there and frankly I don't care. But this whole load of crap about how I have to prove myself again...frankly it doesn't compute essa. I look around the landscape that is now The Imperium and I don't understand what I have to prove. You've got a bunch of guys around you now who haven't done jack shit in this industry essa. You've got people like Vetra who could never and will never be able to hold my jock strap. Prove myself? You'll see just what I'm all about in the near future Mr. Universal Champion. 'Didn't think it would take this longto see the things that you've been doingDidn't think I could wait this longto make the point that you've been proving' For months I sat in that cabin. Sat there holding down the fort for the good of the cause. Doing what I was told was needed to make sure that The Imperium could live on and make the world a better place in the future. I sat there with nothing to do every day but work out and think. Think and work out. Work my ass off until I gained even more muscle to my impressive body but more importantly gained something even more suitable to my style of wrestling.Hatred. Anger. Fury. All these things you gave to me Hoyt. You strengthened me in every single one of these areas. You gave me reason to focus more and more on the three things that make me more and more dangerous every time I unlesh them. As I was working out and thinking about my place in the wrestling world, The Imperium, and the eyes of my peers I began to grow more and more angry. Maybe your assignment had the best intentions for the future of our group in mind. Maybe you were thinking way into the future when we were out of water. When the whole world was in a giant crisis. 'Just when you think you've found a friendThat's when they call you stupidJust when you think that life is dullThat's when they call you pathetic' Then again maybe you didn't. Either way essa you'd better learn something real fast. I'm not the same Mexican that you could order around before. I'm not the lap dog to do your bidding. I still want to consider you a friend Hoyt. I still want to do whatever is needed to make The Imperium feared, respected, and the most powerful force in the wrestling world. I'm just not willing to do it any longer while having to take a backseat to anyone....not even the second 'son of God'. In everyone elses eyes I've become a joke. A rib for everyone to take advantage of. I guess I have to blame myself for part of that essa. I guess I can't put all the blame on Hoyt, on Enrique, on the world. If I hadn't stupidly let others decide things for me maybe I wouldn't be in this mess. If I had made a few more smart decisions on my own, for my own career, and not for what is best for others then maybe I'd be staring into the eyes of fellow wrestlers who were a little more worried about my return instead of seeing me as giant walking joke. 'Didn't think it would take this longto see the things that you've been doingDidn't think I could wait this longto make the point that you've been provingDidn't think it would take this longto see the things that you've been doingDidn't think I could wait this longto make the point that you've been proving' 'I wish we all could make itI wish we all could make itI wish we all could make itI wish we all could try, try, try' 'Didn't think it would take this longto see the things that you've been doingDidn't think I could wait this longto make the point that you've been provingDidn't think it would take this longto see the things that you've been doingDidn't think I could wait this longto make the point that you've been provingThe point that you've been provingThe point that you've been proving' Things could have been so different. Things could have been so much better. Things SHOULD have been much better. Right now I should be celebrating my second, or should things have hit a snag, third PRIME World Tag Team Title reign by this point. I should be looking to move onto my singles career with a clear mind of being one of the best in PRIME.Instead I'm walking into my singles career with the monkey of having to prove myself once more essa. Once more I've got to show people that The Red Devil isn't just some other wrestler on the card. Once more I've got to prove myself worthy of people fearing me. My honor and pride will once again be on the line. The sad thing is that it is probably going to start off badly. I'm probably going to be staring up the mountain, preparing for that long climb, after a loss. Here I am returning, after months of working my ass off while freezing in that God foresaken place, to start my new career. To prove myself a threat in the singles ranks of PRIME. Yet what do I find when I return? I'm in a tag team match. Worse than that I'm in a tag match with a partner I've never heard of and facing four people I've already beaten. Four people who've already fallen at my hands in tag team matches. I have nothing left to prove by beating these men in tag matches essa. I have nothing left to prove as a tag team wrestler. I'm not just some tag team monkey who needs a partner every time I go out there. I've been there and done that essa. I've already carried a partner to enough wins and a title. I've already proven myself the most dominate tag team wrestler to ever grace PRIME. Now I return and I'm just expected to do it again? Now I'm expected to just take another partner along and make him a star as well? Forget it essa. It ain't going to happen. Maybe I'll win this week if I feel like it. Maybe I won't. Maybe I'll help this Worth guy out and get him a win in his PRIME debut. Maybe I'll just get myself pinned and cause him a debut loss. Maybe I won't even bother going out to the ring this week. I'm back in PRIME to prove a point to all the gringos here. That point isn't to be proven by winning meaningless tag matches with no name partners who mean less than nothing to me. My point is only going to be proven by taking certain matters into my own hands. This week at ReVolution I'm going to start proving my point. My win/loss record might wind up with another notch in the loss column but that won't be what the gringos are going to remember. It won't be what people are talking about afterwards. The point I prove will be. My point.
