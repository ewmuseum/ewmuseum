The Code (Part Five: Gi)

19 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-19-05 Bushido For the uninitiated, a code of ethics that prevailed among the warrior class in feudal Japan...the code of the samurai. This is the story of Kyle Lamen, the story of a man who unintentionally lives by this code. Not because he read it in a book, or saw it in a movie. But because inherently, it is his way. The Six-String Samurai. The seven virtues of Bushido: Jin (Benevolence)Rei (Respect)Chuugi (Loyalty)Makoto (Honesty)Gi (Rectitude)Yu (Courage)Meiyo (Honor) * * * * * THE CODE: GI 09.18.05. New Fucking Jersey. Right now, Kyle Lamen was doing something that you (the PRIME audience) has not seen him do since his appearance in the federation: Kyle was laying on his back for three seconds or more. OH! SNAP! Some members of the PRIME roster were prone to this act more than others, like former PRIMEate Jon Kano, and [Aimz joke deleted]. But I digress, Kyle was relaxing, laying back on his bed (not too big, a queen) and it's navy blue sheets, pillow under his head, and absently plucking at the strings of his trusty guitar, Beck. The afternoon light was slowly filtering through the Venetian blinds on the window, pale and straw colored, and one could see the dust floating gently in the air in that light. Kyle's iPod was plugged into his ears, and he was listening to 'Get Set For Sound' by The Stereo, Occasionally, whispers would exit his mouth. Despite taking in the silence alone, sometimes you just can't help but to sing along, even if it's not on purpose. 'Spent my summers just listening...with no reason to turn it down, I would listen...' He pricked the guitar strings with increasing intensity...completely ignoring a light, female voice from the next room over. 'Yo, Kyle! I've brought the papers! Today's the big day...' Kyle either ignored her, didn't hear, or a little of both. Since he wasn't exactly aware that someone else was in his apartment, I'd figure it was more the latter of the two options, rather than the former. 'Kyle! Get out here!' Again, Kyle did virtually nothing different, just plucking away...the light female voice taking on a steelier tone bothered him not at all. 'Kyle! CBGB's!' Well, Lamen's eyes flashed open. 'Be right out!!' He pushed Beck off of it's position on his chest and stomach and over onto the bed beside him, and jerked the earphones of the iPod out of his ears. Bounding off the bed and to his feet, he jogged out the door to his room, and into the main drag of his loft. Despite moving back in nearly four months ago, it still was terribly bare. Sure, there was a couch, and a flat-screen TV, and a desk with a computer setup, but remarkably little else. With all the time that he spent on the road, Kyle just wasn't all that much for home decor. Maybe if he ever got a week off from PRIME...but we digress (again). 'They approve my petition to buy the place?' Sarah McIntyre, Kyle's lovely personal assistant, was standing holding a stack of white paper in her left hand. 'Kyle, I already told you that you don't have enough money to buy CBGB's. You should help in the fundraising process, but that's really about all you can do right now. See if you can get someone like Danny Ferguson to get the cash together...I'm sure HE'D have enough.' Kyle's eyes narrowed a little at the mention of Fergo. 'Ugh, must we mention people like that in my home? I thought some things were sacred.' Sarah just shrugged it off, dusting her knuckles on the shoulder of her taupe peasant blouse. Kyle was suddenly struck by the fact that he liked this style of peasant blouse...you know, the kind that doesn't make you look like a peasant, but instead, a little busty. '...' Sarah noticed Kyle's gaze lingering a little too long at her chest, and she crossed her arms in front of her irritably. 'Kyle, stop it. I came by to drop off your mail for the week, and...' Kyle immediately noticed something odd, and his eyebrow raised to question it. 'Uh, Sarah...why are you dropping off my mail. I live here, so my mail should be here, right?' Sarah could do naught but roll her eyes. 'Yeah, whatever. I've started having it all forwarded to my place, so I cango over it, throw out all of the solicitations, and actually make sure you get what you need, as opposed to it just sitting around and overflowing your mailbox. Seriously, man...how often do you check your mail?' Kyle simply shrugs, and wipes his right hand on his black track pants. 'Um, you've got me. Every once in a while.' Sarah, as she is prone to do, sighs. 'No, the actual reason is because we've got another bill from the Lancaster family.' Kyle, usually flippant and irreverent, typically happy-go-lucky, almost childish, lets his face harden into a stern one. His mouth is not his trademark grin, it's a straight, tight line. 'Oh. Let's do this, then.' Sarah hands him the handful of papers, and with uncharacteristic urgency, Kyle walks over to his dining room table (which still has gashes in it from where he dumped a mess of half-ruined steel shards onto it) and motions Sarah to join him. Sarah walks over tentatively. 'You know, Kyle...as much as I get frustrated by the way you are...so cheerful, so hyper...it really worries me when I see you like this. What's the deal with the Lancaster thing anyways? All I know is that this woman, Mae Lancaster sends letters and bills to you, you get mad like this, and you pay them, immediately. What's going on?' Kyle slumps into the chair at the table, and doesn't even look up to address Sarah when he speaks. 'It's none of your business.' Sarah simply walks over to Kyle, and smacks him upside the head. 'It's your finances, and you PAY ME to make that my business. After that debacle with the stupid bus...' Kyle winces at the thought of when he bought the tour bus with straight cash a month ago... '...I'm not letting you send a DIME out through your checking account until you explain this to me.' Kyle, face still a stone mask, looks away from Sarah, at a blank white wall. 'Fine. But if I tell you, you have to promise not to quit working for me.' Sarah was completely aghast, taken aback. The color drained out from her youthful face. 'Um, what the fuck, Kyle? Is it drug money? Are you paying off a hitman or something?' Kyle can't grin, even though he wants to. 'Nope. Sorta like that, though.' * * * * * 07.19.02. New Fucking Jersey. The inside of the Core Wrestling Academy was pretty crowded, the walls lines with flyers, posters, and the like. The big PPV poster for CCW's Gold And Glory 2002 was up there, a few PCW PPV posters (March Madness, Spring Fling...with Boda running through a field of flowers), and just a few older one-color flyers with names like Jason Core and Razor Blade on them, to name a few. There was workout equipment, gear bags, and mats strewn about the arena, and even here and there, limp forms of some of the trainees. Headmaster Jason Core was on a cell phone in the corner, hurling curse words at his agent over the termination of his contract with PCW following the scandal that had been his negotiations to go to OSW...and in the main ring were two men, surrounded by a horde of students. The first man was a very young black man, head shaved and wearing black trunks, with a rather impressive physique. He smiled big and smiled often, and despite being mouthy, was one of the top two students in Jason Core's academy. Lucas Lancaster was a serious success story, graduating from his high school in Memphis and instead of being involved in gangs or violence, instead of doing nothing and working manual labor, or going to college, he risked it all to move to Red Bank and train with the three head trainers of the CWA. Headmaster Jason Core (who was now forced to split time with others due to his contract with PCW), and his two associate trainers. The always dependable, albeit not-tremendously skilled Kensuke Suzahara had recently won the CCW Heavyweight Title, and was touring with that company, so the other main trainer was in the ring with Lucas Lancaster. Eyes bloodshot and weak-looking, Kyle Lamen glared at Lucas Lancaster from across the ring, where he had himself propped up in the corner. His t-shirt was dirty, and his boots were unlaced. He had recently been fired from Five Star Wrestling for a combination of not reporting an injury, and getting into a screaming fit with management, and was now stuck here, working part-time for Jason, and loathing himself and drinking full-time. He opened his mouth to speak to Lancaster, who was grinning widely. 'Say it again.' Lancaster smiled, and only backpedaled a little. Tough kid. 'I just said, you look a little wiped, bro. I'm not surprised FSW kicked you to the-' The first punch from Lamen caught Lucas across the jaw, but the second one missed entirely, as Lucas Lancaster, who had been a varsity wrestler at Jackson High, took Kyle Lamen's legs out from under him. He quickly mounted Kyle, and threw a few punches, and Kyle simply covered his face with one hand, and weakly tapped with his right. 'Aright, aright. You've got me, now let up!' Lancaster rolled off of Kyle, and shot him a look of disgust. Kyle slowly got up to his feet, trying to stretch out the right arm that he'd tapped with...the same one with the 'mysterious' nerve damage that he'd been fired from FSW for. The nerve damage they insisted was psychological. 'You win, and as per school rules, I teach you a new hold. But you're bumping it first, alright?' Lucas, still mad as hell, nodded. 'Better be a good one, Lamen.' Kyle brushed him off with his left hand. 'Power move, since it's all you've got going for you. That and some brawling. This one's called a Michinoku Driver. You familiar?' Lucas Lancaster simply nodded. 'Okay, lemme scoop you.' Lancaster tentatively walked towards Kyle, almost afraid that Kyle would take a liberty with him. But Lamen had been like this for nearly a month now, and still failed to do anything that serious to any of the students, especially under his father-figure's watchful eye. Jason Core was never far from the action in his own academy. 'Let's do this.' Kyle pulled Lucas up with his his right arm for a scoop slam, talking as he did so... 'Regular scoop slam setup, and-' Time slowed down, as Kyle lifted Lucas up, his right arm went completely dead, and Lucas Lancaster, a two-hundred forty-pound can't-miss prospect... ...fell cleanly on his head, and everyone in the arena heard it crack. Kyle Lamen, completely stunned, let a rivulet of sweat trickle from his brow...before running at top speed into the locker room. * * * * * 09.18.05. New Fucking Jersey. Sarah, stunned and silent, sat at the table with Kyle now. 'I've paid every single one of his hospital bills. He broke his neck, but regained feeling in his legs real soon after that. Lucas's never spoken to me after that day, I do all of his bills through his mom. We used to talk, but now...not so much.' Sarah, still stunned, looked at the letter that Kyle passed over to her. Kyle, tired, continued. 'She says that this is the last one. Lucas has made a full recovery, finally has his AA, and is gonna go to Memphis and get a degree in business. That's it. I'm all done, finally. I've paid out, according to what Kensuke told me, approximately two hundred thousand in medical bills. The family was kinda poor, and they didn't have much insurance...' Sarah, still unable to speak just walked over to Kyle and hugged him. Kyle was thankful for the hug, and started to like the peasant blouse even more. Sarah'd never hugged him before, and he noticed that her hair smelled slightly of cinammon. He liked it, unsurprisingly. When Sarah broke the hug, Kyle was disappointed. 'My arm could go out on me again at any time...I'll probably have a few weeks notice, there're warning signals...but I know that at any point, I could lose feeling in it again, this time permanently. Or worse yet, I'll seriously injure someone again. I may be a wrestler, but I dunno if I could live with that a-' Sarah cut him off, seeing as how upset he was getting, the lines in his face growing deeper and breeding like cockroaches. 'Hey. Let it go for now. You're almost past this, right? You've got that big match you asked for, and you're not the type to dwell on the past, I know you. Let's go out, get some ice cream, and forget all about it for now, okay? Paul Cain isn't to be taken lightly, you know...and you've got a big run up to King Of Kings, don't you?' Kyle nodded, but couldn't bring the words to his mouth other than... 'Thanks, Sarah.' The two young adults stood up, and in relative silence, gathered their personal things. Sarah got her purse, Kyle grabbed his wallet, and the two of them left out the door. Kyle Lamen reached back to the door, and reflected very briefly on his life since the accident. It's behind me now. Like Sarah said. I let my arm ruin the best thing I had going ever before, but it's gone now. I've got a good run going in PRIME, and Paul Cain this week. The past is the past...can't dwell on it. What would I do without her? Kyle reached back for the handle of his door, to close it, and in so doing, close the chapter on that part of his life. It seemed like so long ago, and he was a different person now. Kyle felt good. That is...until he touched the door handle, and felt a too familiar shiver of pain slice down from his right shoulder, to his right wrist, and his right hand went numb and dead. TO BE CONTINUED
