To Protect and Serve One of Two (Stephen)

18 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Feb 18th His day begins like any others who live in the city, the blurry eyed transition from silencing the screeching alarm clock, to the hot shower, to the even hotter coffee, the frantic search of gathering up ones tools and possessions needed for ones job, and then inevitably the helter-skelter commune to work be it on a bus or car crawling slowly down the freeway amidst a sea of half awake people, flashing middle fingers and a symphony of honks and beeps. He can feel the migraine setting in already. It’s somewhere in between these familiar but often unpleasant morning rituals that he undergoes the transformation, he can’t pinpoint exactly when it happens even though he’s been trying to for years, but somewhere between point a and point b he goes from blurry eyed commuter to an officer of the law, all he knows is that when he pulls up to the station and see’s those words carved into the stone “Serve and Protect” it makes him smile. And it’s a smile he rarely loses throughout the day. Stephen Devereaux loves his job, you can see it in his walk, in his smile and in his actions, those who know him (And that’s mostly everyone on the force) will tell you he’s his mothers son, a kind soft hearted young man of unwavering determination and confidence, with iron composure that didn’t crack even when he laid his mother to rest far too soon on life’s unpredictable journey. After that the force became his family, opened up it’s arms and excepted him warmly, gave him the encouragement he needed and the confidence it took to join it’s ranks, though the job was physically demanding Stephen was no stranger to athleticism, he’d been the captain of his wrestling team, Straight A Student, the kind of human being you could get behind, maybe even the kind of guy you’d like to see run for President someday, you might not vote for him but hey politics are a different animal entirely. From the minute he walks through the door to the moment he sits down at his desk he’s embraced by his family, not always in the physical sense of the word but that’s not to say he doesn’t get a hug now and then, co-workers, friends, partners each offer their morning salutations to him, he smiles and returns each one, this is one of the parts of the job he enjoys the most. “Stephen...Captain wants to see you in his office!” He’s only been sitting a moment going through his mail, bills, credit card offers, junk mail, something about Penile Enlargement...he swears he’s going to pepper spray the person who let his address slip to “that” company, it could be any one of his smartass co-workers, he smiles and tosses the mail down jumping up he makes his way towards the Captains office his step light his smile unwavering. “Stephen...Good morning” The Captains face is Grim a stark contrast of his words, for a moment Stephens face wavers “Good morning sir...” For a moment his words are just as hollow “Is there...something wrong sir?” “I wanted to talk to you about the Angel case...” his superior sighs and then quickly adds “And no there’s nothing wrong...at least not yet” Stephen is already getting that sinking feeling in his stomach as he pulls up a chair and sits down, the Captain takes his silence as permission to continue. “It’s your brother Dominic” “What about him?” The smile wavers again in danger of collapsing “Stephen we’ve learned he’s taken the Angel case via an independent contract” Devereaux speaks without hesitation “He’s a Private Detective sir...it’s how he works” “I know Stephen, look I know you’ve only known him for a little while, I know you two have a great deal of catching up to do, I’m not trying to attack you Brother here but I look at his past records...” the captain gestures to a rather thick looking manilla folder “And I worry...specifically I worry about the future of this case” The smile wavers again... “Sir he graduated from the Military academy with superb marks, he was also a valued member of the SWAT, they don’t come much tougher then Dominic” The Captains sighs heavily, he knows he needs to deliver his point quickly and there’s only one way to do it. “Stephen he killed a man in the line of duty...” Direct hit, shields down, the smile falls. “He can be a loose cannon Stephen that’s all I’m saying, I know the Angel case is yours but I want you to make sure you know where Angel is at all times alright?” Stephen nods putting the smile back on with effort. “Who knows maybe I can work with him and we can close this case together” “If you can then do it” the Captain replies “Just don’t cross any lines Stephen...Please” Stephen stands up with a nod, he’s anxious to be out of the office all of the sudden, on the surface he’s calm if not a little annoyed with this talk, but inside he’s got that sinking feeling and it’s quickly becoming a stomach churning knot of anxiety, he needs to find out where Angel is! Dom moves like a shark who smells blood with these things. Stephen leaves the office trying not to rush, as he makes his way down the hall to his car he’s already punching in a number on his cell phone. He only hopes he can get to Angel before his brother does.
