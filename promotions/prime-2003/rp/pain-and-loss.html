Date: Colossus V July 17, 2008 Birmingham, Alabama 00:23 a.m. The back of the eyelids are strangely impressionable little filaments. It was as though entire scenes were imprinted upon the flesh. Recorded reminders of the most recent surroundings and events. Silently Tyler Rayne stared at the hazed photograph impressed upon the back of the lids. His head rested somewhat uncomfortably against the cold stone walls. He breathed evenly, softly. Hands clasped between his legs, the PRIME 5-Star Champion was a picture of serenity as he sat near motionless upon the bench. He listened, and though most would never know from looking at him… he observed. The muffled scratch of rubber scraping sand across the stone floor. The various paces of breath coming from the other six men sharing his cell. The shifting of shadows that indicated movement beyond the singular scene left scorched upon his eyelid. He listened and he observed and he used these tiny little cues to fill in the missing pieces of his scene. To change the photographic image to moving film. With closed eyes and steady breath, he played the scene upon the back of his eyelid as if he were actually watching. He ‘watched’ the two sharing his bench slide slyly and cautiously from his vicinity. Felt more than heard the approach of the large balding man with the Swastika tattoos on both hands. The sudden change in atmosphere as the five other men drew in breath. Anticipation hung in the air. He could almost hear the pitied musings bouncing throughout their heads. How unfortunate it was that he’d never even see it coming. To a point, they were right. He hadn’t seen shit. “Somethin’ on your mind, stranger.” Then again, perhaps one does not need eyes to see. Another collective intake of breath. This more from shock than anticipation. He feels the demeanor of the cell shift once more. Things were getting interesting. Business was about to pick up. “You Tyler Rayne?” He need not open his eyes to open his mouth. “That’s the rumor.” “Thought so.
