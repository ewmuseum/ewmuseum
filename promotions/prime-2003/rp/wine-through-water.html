Date: Several different ones "A human being is part of the whole, called by us "Universe," a part limited in time and space. He experiences himself, his thoughts and feelings as something seperated from the rest - a kind of optical delusion of the consciousness..." ------------------------------- Her red hair was curly and unkempt. The little girl was surrounded by a void of bleak darkness. Blue eyes, encompassed by freckles, held an innocence that only a young child's could bare. A finger, pale and shaking with fear, pointed at him. She cried for his help but nothing escaped through her grit teeth and wide open lips. Blood streamed from her eyelids. Owen darted upward from his bed, covered in cold sweat and panting. He'd dreamed of her before, the red haired child. She had haunted him for the thirteen years of the life he'd lived. Though he never knew her personally, he knew her. His hand found itself trolling through his messy, brown hair. "Fuck..." The blonde in bed beside him stirred, draping an arm around him. "What's wrong, Owen?" He wouldn't answer. His breath was cold and was visible in his room, a price that he paid for his parent's lack of employment. She pulled him closer as he laid his head back down on the pillow. "Answer me, Owen." Maybe he was just frustrated with his recurring dream. Maybe he was just being thirteen. He didn't know why he said the things he did. Even today, he could not tell you. "Do you ever think that you're fucking crazy?" She giggled and kissed the back of his neck, "Why would I think that?" Owen answered quickly, "Because you are. You're nineteen... and you're dating a thirteen year old. You're sick in the head, you know that?" She paused, carefully considering what she said. "Sick in the head for you." Which made it all the more embarassing . It was in this moment; in this incredibly awkward, uncomfortable moment that Owen realized just how trapped he was and how badly he needed salvation. ------------------------------- WE CANNOT LEARN WITHOUT PAIN! The phrase was painted on a board that was nailed above the door, sideways and unprofessional. Nine could not decide if it was a mantra or a warning. Red paint, having dried only after the bottoms of the letters had dripped to the bottom of the board, appeared foreboding and unwelcoming. The door was solid metal and painted an ugly teal. Though it appeared to be the only metallic piece of the entire structure. It creaked only after Nine had grabbed the handle and started to open it. "So what? You do karate?" Nine looked around. Seemed like a gym enough, he supposed anyway. Though sans all of the workout equipment and mats on the ground, it was really just a wrestling ring in the dead center of a building. He sat the bag over his shoulder down on a bench. "Hey buddy, you see me talkin' to you over here? Guy with the martial arts outfit!" Nine looked down. Martial arts outfit. "Yes. Hi." "Do you do karate?" His questioneer was an older gentleman, in his mid fourties. His bald spot was certainly the shiniest part of his body, Nine noted. "No. Judo." The older man responded, "That's great. The name's Mike. We respect the martial arts here at The Dojo." The Dojo. Nine recalled the name now. It seemed simple and humble enough at the time. Suddenly it appeared silly and over-complimentary. The place was a dump. The workout equipment even seemed shoddy and worn. Cables to TurboGyms and what have you missing and out of place. "My name is Nine. I signed up for professional wrestling class sometime ago to begin today," Nine looked around. No other students. "Could you tell me where the other students are?" He had already made up his mind this wasn't the place for him. Though he allowed Mike's spiel about how Nine was actually his first student and how Nine shouldn't leave just because of looks as some others had because he has a mouth to feed. He sounded like a used car salesman. "I used to sale cars for a living, and--" Well that explained that. Nine was doubting Mike even had a mouth to feed, thinking of what the best way to let this guy off easy was when he saw her. She couldn't have been older than eight, he thought. Her clothes were thrifty and she seemed cold, rubbing her own folded arms with her hands. Her red hair was curly and almost orange. Freckles were dotted on her cheeks. Though she wasn't crying blood, Nine recognized her right away. She held a coffee mug of water with both hands and shuffled around the room aimlessly. ...A finger, pale and shaking with fear, pointed at him... "I'll stay," Nine replied. His own brown eyes couldn't help but drift to the girl's blue ones. In his heart of hearts, or what Nine would call his heart of hearts, he knew there was a reason more divine for his selection of this place. There always was. This time, though, it felt different. He knew she needed his help. He fucking knew it. ------------------------------- Upon realizing that he was trapped and needed out of his relationship with the 19 year old blonde, the young Owen Farhat was short of breath and anxious. His fingers tapped the matress in what was a vicious, tense rhythm. The restless-legged Owen breathed, counted to three and said, "I don't want to be with you anymore, Lilly." Lilly replied, "What do you mean?" Though she knew all to well what he meant. Denial was not only a river in Egypt, but a fountain of hope for the 19 year old, soon-to-be attempted murderer. "I mean. Well, what I mean is, is that I think that we should not be together. That I don't want to be your boyfriend." Lilly respected Owen for his courage. She even admired him for it. However, her feeble mind and childish tendencies, along with her black, murky heart would cause her to commit what she would later describe as, "a crime of passion." She stood from the bed and began gathering her things. "But thank you though," Owen said. "For everything. The Pythagorean books, the numerology texts. Everything, seriously." Lilly nodded her head, holding back tears. Sliding her sandals on, she picked up her purse and walked out of the room. Owen rolled over onto his side. As Owen laid there, wondering if he had indeed done the right thing, his question was answered quickly as He heard the pace of footsteps and rolled to face the door. Lilly ran into the room with a vase in her hand. "Fuck you, Owen Farhat." The vase crashed to pieces over Owen's head. ------------------------------ "...This delusion is a kind of prison for us, restricting us to our personal desires and to affection for a few persons nearest to us. Our task must be to free ourselves from this prison by widening our circle of compassion to embrace all living creatures and the whole of nature in its beauty. Nobody is able to achieve this completely, but the striving for such achievement is in itself a part of the liberation and foundation for inner security." -Albert Einstein ------------------------------ She had watched him from afar for three months now. He'd been training with her Uncle Mike for sometime. Normally, she would never trust anyone associated with her uncle. But he was different. He was a figure that occupied her dreams. The night before, her nightmare wasn't real. Though there were several nights in which they were. She knew that last nights wasn't, because he had saved her once again. Multiple colors flew around her bedroom. Her uncle was there. When was he not? She tried to escape his clutches but he overpowered her. He always did. Once again she would fall victim, flinging and thrashing tirelessly as Mike tore her clothes away. The door swung open and he came to her rescue, as though he were destined to. She screamed a number, "NINE!" Her Uncle had been working with him one on one for sometime. She never ventured close because Mike was always there. He was having lunch in his office though, and the man she desperately desired to meet was sitting on a workout bench, out in the open space of the gym. "Hi," she said. Accompanied with a smile. His brown eyes fell on her blue ones and she waved. His smile was warm, even with the scruffy beard around it. "Hello." Though he didn't seem like much of a talker right away, she had to get to the bottom of who he was. Even at the age of 6, she was inquisitive and a CSI fan. "What's your name?" "My name is Nine." She gulped. What was that? "Your name is Nine?" "Yes. What's yours?" It was unreal. No, surreal. Her dream tied together like a perfect knot. She had not screamed a number at all. It was his name. "My name is Carrot." And there it was. She hated her name. She couldn't stand it. Why had her mother and father named her that? A middle name she might could even understand. But her first name? And she had RED HAIR? Nine smiled, "Carrot? Really? Wow. That is an amazing name. So unique!" This brought a smile to the young girl's face, though it was wiped away when they made eye contact once more. Something about the look on Nine's face gave away his investigation. "Thank you." "You're welcome," Nine replied. He pulled a banana from his bag and began peeling it. "Wanna piece of my banana, Carrot?" He giggled. So did she, "Yes." Enough small talk. Carrot knew he was here for something other than wrestling. He was already decent and outworked her Uncle in the ring during training. Nine was an obvious superior to Mike. "So," Nine began in a way that surprised Carrot. "Why do you stay with your Uncle here in the boonies?" Tears welled up in Carrot's eyes quickly. He almost regretted saying anything, though she got a hold of herself enough to continue the conversation. "My parents died shortly after I was born. My Uncle has been watching me ever since..." Nine nodded his head, "I see. So do you like it here?" Her emotions nearly overwhelmed her, crippling her voice box to where she could only croak her next sentence. "Mr. Nine, do you ever see me in your dreams?" And what a sentence it was. ------------------------------ The sirens blared most unlike the kind told of in Homer's Odyssey. As paramedics sent tubes into Owen Farhat's arms and monitored his breathing, one female EMT in particular sat there and pat his head in an attempt to comfort him. They were driving very quickly to the hospital. "She missed his jugular," declared one of them "Thank God. Did they take her into custody?" "Of course," he replied. "She broke a vase over his head, tied him to a chair and cut his throat. Why wouldn't she be taken in?" Nothing was said. What had happened in the Farhat household that night was an absolute tragedy, the only saving grace being he would later check out of Piedmont Hospital. "It's strange," said the Paramedic who was taking the time to try and comfort the victim. "The cut on his neck, it looks like a '9'." ------------------------------ "Mr. Nine, do you ever see me in your dreams?" Nine was quiet for the briefest of moments, sitting stunned. How did she know? "Yes I... have." Carrot replied sobbing hushedly, "Because I see you in mine. You save me." Nine was whispering, "From what?" "From Mike," Carrot answered. Her red hair was tangled and knotted and she was running her hand through it nervously. Nine asked, "What is your full name, Carrot?" "Carrot Michelle Collins." He took a moment before answering, "You know, your number is one." "One? Is your number Nine?" "Yes to both questions. And you know what that means? The fact that your number is one means you have a forceful, willful nature. If you want something bad enough, you can get it Carrot. You even came over here and initiated contact. I'm glad that your natural instincts correlate with your number." She wondered why she was hearing all of this. Not that it wasn't interesting, just... why was he telling her this now?" "Is he spitting out that numerology mumbo-jumbo again?" Mike. Her uncle. The biggest demon in her life. Nine had noticed him approaching and changed the subject, she just knew it. Nine winked at her. "Not mumbo jumbo Mike." "Sure, whatever." Mike chuckled and rubbed Carrot's head. Carrot cowered away from his endearing gesture rather noticeably. "Awkward kid," Mike said in 'jest'. "Anyway, I thought the idea of a virgin giving birth was strange. But you tell me that numbers are the source and reason for everything? Hardy har-fuckin'-har." "Whatever man," Nine replied. "You ready to start?" ------------------------------ "Above the cloud with the shadow is the star with its light..."- Pythagoras ------------------------------ 9 Hours Later Carrot was panicking in the passenger seat of the old 96 teal Pontiac Grand Am, which he was driving rather quickly. Nine didn't notice right away, driving about 10 miles over the speed limit. "Could you... slow... down..." She had not been in a car but three times before in her life. The story, as Nine found out, was as tragic as it was appaling; it was as disgusting as it was pathetic. A baby named Carrot was left in the hands of her only surviving family, Mike Collins. She had confessed while they were pulling away from the gym that she could not remember a time she was not imprisoned by the awful man. He had sexually abused her for most of her very short life. ------------------------------ That night, a few hours before, on the very day they had spoke for the first time, Nine had stayed awake. At around 1 am, he could hear muffled screaming when he decided to stroll through the gym on a bit of a patrol. Without haste or true understanding of what he was doing, Nine kicked in the wooden door to Carrot's room. As soon as the door creaked Mike had thrown the girl to the floor in fear. She was in her underwear. "Put your clothes on, Carrot." Nine said. Carrot cried out in embarassment and agony. She wasn't moving quickly, so Nine pointed at Mike. "You stay right there, you bastard. You stay right-fucking-there." Mike stepped towards Nine, "Or what?" Nine didn't waste time punching Mike in the face. Mike's nose was as crooked as his perverse thoughts and streaming blood as badly as Carrot did from her eyes in Nine's dreams. Carrot was in her clothes. Mike screamed, "You fuck! You broke my fuckin' nose! Oh my god, you motherFUCKER!" "Step outside for a minute, Carrot." She did so. Nine closed the door behind her. "You're going to stay here Mike. You're going to stay here until the Police arrive and you're going to go to jail." "Fuck you, this is a misunderstanding." "No. No it isn't. You're fucked, Mike. Fucked." Mike screamed, "No I'm not you bastard! I'm not going to jail!" Mike tried to run towards the door. Nine's foot was all it took to make the sick, twisted asshole fall to the ground. Nine found himself strangling Mike in the judo hold, Jigoku Jime, until he'd passed out completely. ------------------------------ "Yes, I'll slow down. Sorry. I forgot you aren't used to riding." They drove for a while. It may have been a good fifteen minutes of just silence before they reached the police station. On the way, they didn't talk about much. She heard The Beatles on the radio for the first time and loved it, so they talked about them for most of the ride. Then Carrot asked a question that took a moment for Nine to answer. "So are you gonna be my dad?" He didn't expect the question. But he knew the answer and so did she. Nine may not have been ready for what he had decided, but that didn't matter. They were connected through fate, even stranger, through dreams. Though the supernatural isn't something that actually happens in the reality of most, in theirs it certainly did. He'd known Carrot long before he met her. "Yeah... Yeah, I'll be your dad." ------------------------------ "I've dreamt in my life dreams that have stayed with me ever after, and changed my ideas; they've gone through me, like wine through water, and altered the color of my mind." -Emily Bronte
