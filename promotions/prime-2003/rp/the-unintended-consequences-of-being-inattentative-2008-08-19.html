The Unintended Consequences of Being Inattentative

19 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/19/08 (Tony Davis casually ate an ice cream cone while standing on a busy suburban street. Well, he was less eating said ice cream than he was holding it, staring out into the street as if he were a pet dog, just waiting for the right car to chase. After moments, Tony's ice cream falls off of his cone, and lands with a sloppy thud onto Tony's shoe. Tony looks around quickly. Left. Right. And then brings his boot up to his face and lightly licks the non-leafified portion of his milky ceamy goodness.) FREEZE FRAME (Tossed onto the dining room table of the Harmen family was a newspaper, bearing this image of Tony Davis. Tony sits behind said paper, holding his hands in his head. High Flyer is there, chewing on a toothpick.) High Flyer: What, were you trying to eat your own foot? Tony Davis: My foot tasted delicious for a few moments there. High Flyer: So no regrets? This is like that time you got hit by that bus? (CUT TO: An airport structure. Busses whiz and whirl past. One stops on the edge of the street. Tony Davis comes rushing across, and slams side first into the stopped bus. SMACK. He freezes in a Wild-E-Coyote position, before tipping backwards and smacking hard, back first on the cold asphalt. RETURN.) Tony Davis: Well, my parents didn't see that. High Flyer: You have parents? I thought you were magically congealed in some sort of test tube. (Tony gives him a look.) High Flyer: I mean, fuck what your parents think. My parents are dead. You know how much fucked up shit of mine they've seen being dead? ALL OF IT. Or none of it, if Christain mythology is a myth. Tony Davis: I feel like when I was waiting for the delivery package. (CUT TO : Morning, outside the Harmen estate. A DHL truck quietly pulls up and parks itself. Stuck in a tree, high above, looking down, was the Degenerate, Tony Davis. The DHL truck driver lumbers out of his vehicle. He grabs the package and walks over to the front door, ringing the bell. Tony Davis, meanwhile, mutters on as he watches.) Tony Davis: Okay. Set it down. Set it down. Back away slowly. (The DHL driver rings the bell again. Nothing.) Tony Davis: Just. What are you doing? Hmmm... Should I get down? I should get down. (Tony then begins to try to climb out of the tree, but tumbles, and his foot gets stuck between two branches. As he hands upside down above his house, the DHL employee gives his boss a call, and then promptly leaves. A moment later, the tree branch breaks off, and Tony Davis lands with a thud. RETURN TO PRESENT.) Tony Davis: I really should have been waiting for the driver. High Flyer: We can just go around burning every copy of this newspaper. I mean, it's only the New York Post. It's not like more than a hundred people read it. Tony Davis: You're right. I just... it's just bringing up a lot old memories. I realize... I'm not acting like the person I want to be. Like, last week... (CUT TO: Empty streets. Tony Davis stands in the middle of a busy intersection with car horns honking like crazy. He, inattentively, is playing Nintendo DS. Suddenly, a stroller RUSHES past him, and Tony Davis doesn't even look up. There's a crash, and a ROAR, and then what appears to be a chopping sound, along with blood splattering all over the Degenerate. Cars begin to swerve, people scream, fleeing away, all as Tony doesn't notice Godzilla rushing through, baby guts hanging from his lips. RETURN.) Tony Davis: I missed out on meeting Big Foot man! And now Big Foot is dead! I mean. How can I go on living knowing I'll never get to see Big Foot tear about New York City? High Flyer: We could always make a movie about that. (Fade to black.) Narrator: Coming... next summer. (Fade in. A few city buildings stand tall. People are gathered in the streets for a celebration. The mayor waves. A boy loses his mother in the chaos of celebration, and a construction worker finishes fixing the electricity on this same city block.) Narrator: In a world... (A woman screams at her husband in their dining room as their kid silently eats his peas. An elderly lady is robbed at gunpoint by three hoodlums.) Narrator: Where no one is safe. (A man is HURLED through two buildings before being impaled on a swordfish.) Narrator: Only one "man" can save us all. (Fade to black, music builds to crescendo, the camera quickly rushes up in a sweeping fashion, as we pass Big Foot. He's dressed like Superman, cape flowing in the air.) Narrator: And he's no man at all! (Cut to various images of Big Foot hurling the same hoodlums that robbed the elderly woman from before. Cut to Big Foot, in the UN, given a heated speech about Global Warming and how much he likes trees. Cut to Big Foot slamming his fist through the Mayor's desk to the shock of all those around. And finally, cut to one final shot of Big Foot, charging toward someone, ready to strike. That someone? Godzilla. Yeah. You know you wanna see it.) Narrator: Coming July 2009, unless Harry Potter causes us to push it back further... Big Foot versus.... EVERYONE! (A shot of a baron lake. A single boat resides in the center of the ocean. A father and son duo are fishing.) Father: I... I like the quiet. (Quickly, the Loch Ness Monster emerges, and devours them whole. "Summer 09" flashes one last time, and we fade completely to black.)
