Date: the land that time forgot The Magic of Cinema THE MAGIC OF CINEMA By The A-List FADE IN: INT. RESTAURANT - DAY Danny checked the pleat on his pants and shook his leg a little. If we were in his head, maybe there was some sort of explanation that didn't make him look like another slightly crazy Hollywood actor. Except this isn't a first-person perspective, it's third-person. So he just looks crazy. The doors to the VIP room at the restaurant were pushed open in anticipation of his arrival, and as a path was cleared through the 'common' area of the eatery, he practiced his 'actively bored' look for the slew of promotional photos that he would be taking in just a few hours. Finally, Allan North, his agent, hailed him, and the procession of bodyguards and secondaries lead him into the room. DANNY Do we have a director? ANONYMOUS STAFFER Hello, Danny. DANNY Hey there, whatsyourname. Do we have a director? Allan stopped the staffer before answered logically and cost himself a job. ALLAN NORTH The powers-that-be are making their way down the short list. DANNY (beat) Hmm...that's interesting...because I would have assumed that I constituted the quote-unquote 'powers-that-be.' What does the MOTHERFUCKING STAR OF THE MOVIE have to do to get in that loop? ALLAN NORTH It's a strategy thing, Dan- DANNY And it's working wonders, let me tell you. ALLAN NORTH ...if we keep you informed, you run a higher risk of dropping something to the media at today's press event. Danny drops into a chair and kicks his feet up on a table. Nonplussed is an adjective that comes to mind, but neither you nor I know what 'nonplussed' really means. DANNY Can someone at least tell me who's NOT on the list? The staffers exchange a look. Allan finally nods to them, and one clears his throat. ANONYMOUS STAFFER Well...Spike Lee was ready to sign...until he found out that 'Terry Funk' wasn't a black musical act from the 70s. ANONYMOUS STAFFER #2 And Spike Jonze was in until he found out we already had a script, and that it didn't contain interpretive dance. DANNY (interupting) Why do we need all these braniacs? People won't come see this to think. All we need is a douche with a name who can turn the pages of a book and yell 'cut' every few minutes. ANONYMOUS STAFFER We agree, and we figured you'd say that, so we called Todd Phillips... DANNY Who? ALLAN NORTH The guy who did Old School. Impressed, and giving that 'I'm impressed' face, Danny pulls his feet down and sits forward, suddenly animated. And I mean dynamic, not hand-drawn. DANNY Well that's something, huh? A different direction, I know, but maybe he could do drama and surprise a fe- ANONYMOUS STAFFER He wanted to replace you with Will Ferrell and bring in Owen Wilson as Dory. DANNY Fuck him. ANONYMOUS STAFFER Right. DANNY I mean, honestly. Who does he think he is? ALLAN NORTH We did call Owen about that, though. DANNY What'd he say? ALLAN NORTH Let's just move on. ANONYMOUS STAFFER Anyway, those were the most recent 'No's. Danny curses to himself and looks at his watch to check the date. It had been two weeks since his attachment to the project, and no advancements had been made since then. For his own publicity purposes, it was better for the star to sign on and launch right into filming. In fact, at that moment, his 'Berry went off again - another call from publicist extraordinaire Phillis Stein. He let it go over to voicemail, knowing full well she would't leave one...save for a string of harsh profanities. DANNY So what's the message? Do I drop any of those names to the media? ANONYMOUS STAFFER #2 If we drop a name we'll get embarassed. Stick with the Statuz Quo. DANNY What the hell is that? ALLAN NORTH It's kinda like sticking to the usual, but it's a little more annoying and impossible to ignore. Before Danny can respond, eyes and ears are drawn to another conversation across the room. It's between another staffer and...I dunno, someone else, on the other end of the staffer's phone. ANONYMOUS STAFFER #3 What do you mean? (beat) Well, where did it go? (beat) No, we didn't... (he notices all eyes on him) I'll call you back. He slides the phone back into its holster and looks around at everyone. ANONYMOUS STAFFER #3 Did any of you...by chance...make a large withdrawal from the film's PR budget? The dead faces are a clear no. ANONYMOUS STAFFER #3 Because I just got a call from the site of today's event...you know, the one we scheduled to make up for the last one, when it turned into the Coming Out Party for Danny's friend? Ferguson looks at the floor to avoid eye contact and the ensuing guilt. ANONYMOUS STAFFER #3 We're overdrawn. We can't pay because the account is practically empty. CUT TO: EXT. SUNSET BOULEVARD - NOT A HALF HOUR LATER Not a half hour later, Team Ferguson and Team More Than Just Hardcore - renamed Team MTJH for length reasons - are on the scene, where all of the promotional materials for the planned press event are still packed up in the trucks. Allan North and the studio personnel are doing most of the negotiating, but Danny, letting frustration get the better of him, storms in looking for answers. DANNY What did you do with our money? The flabbergasted venue attendant (i reed books) tries to relieve himself of the initial period of starstruck-ed-ness. VENUE ATTENDANT I'm sorry, Mr. Ferguson, but payment didn't go through on the reservation. Another studio rep - an intern, likely - wanders over. ANONYMOUS STAFFER I just got off the phone with the bank. This guy's right, the account is dry. Danny turns his attention from the attendant and grabs the intern. DANNY Fine, what the hell did YOU do with our money? ANONYMOUS STAFFER Nothing, sir. DANNY Then where is it? ANONYMOUS STAFFER Well, it seems a rather large withdrawal was made in the form of a check made to...'A. Dick.' I assume that's some sort of fake name - honestly, if these hackers and thieves are so good at what they do, you think they could come up with more clever names to go by. The other da- Danny slowly releases the kid. DANNY It's not a fake name. ALLAN NORTH (overhearing all of this and wandering over) Excuse me? DANNY It's not fake. Let me guess, the check was for $25 million? ANONYMOUS STAFFER ...actually, yes. ALLAN NORTH (growing frustrated) Danny, did you- DANNY Of course not. But I know who did. CUT TO: EXT. FERGUSON ESTATE - EVENING Some nameless California punk band rocks the walls of Danny's mansion. Normally this is the kind of place where you'd expect a ton of cars to be parked out front on a weekend evening, but that's not the case. In fact, there's not a single car out there, at least not until Danny's green H2 pulls into the drive. Danny and his bodyguards pour out and head straight for the front door. Luckily (/sarcasm) it's already half open to welcome them in. JUMP CUT TO: INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - CONTINUOUS Clothes are strewn everywhere across the dais of the mansion, although you're less inclined to notice that as you are the mysterious stickiness on the floor. The music is even louder than before. In the middle of it all is the big screen television that you've become accustomed to seeing from previous episodes of this show. As always, it's set on E!: Entertainment Television. This time, however, the sound is muted, so we only can read the captions. CAPTIONS ...since his shocking betrayal of beloved director Oliver Stone, the Face-Eater has disappeared from the public eye... THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER I DIDN'T GO ANYWHERE, YOU STUPID WHORE! I'M RIGHT FUCKING HERE! Adam Dick appears out of nowhere on the couch in front of the TV, wearing only his mask, cape, socks and boxer shorts. He grabs his junk a couple of times to emphasize his...uh...whatever that was. Annoyed at everything in general at this point, Danny doesn't bother being sensitive to his tag partner's situation. DANNY Facey. The Internet Champion whirls around, prompting a tense standoff with the bodyguards. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Danimal...my life is RUINED! DANNY Couldn't have happened to a nicer guy. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER These Hollywood parasites have destroyed my career! I used to BE SOMEBODY in this town! Now look at me! LOOK AT ME! DANNY You look the same. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Yeah, but now I have the stink of failure all over me! DANNY It's hard to pick up over the pot smell. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Don't worry, you'll work up a tolerance. But we're talking about ME here, Fergie! I'M the one in peril here! He looks at the group for sympathy but finds none. With a sigh, he flips back on the couch. DANNY Did your 'peril' include a $25 million withdrawal from my movie's account? Pause. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER What's the name of your movie again? DANNY You took my money! Facey sits up instantly. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER You said I could take it! DANNY I did not! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Did to! DANNY Please tell me what part of 'No, you can't have the money' constitutes a Yes to you? THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER I thought you were being droll! DANNY You don't even know what droll means! Silence. Well, silence aside from the thumping generic punk music and the wall-shaking that results from it. Letting his shoulders sag a little, Danny breathes deeply before letting the next words go. DANNY Get out. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER (thinking that they are still arguing) No, YOU get out. DANNY I'm serious. Get out of my house. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Pfft. And go where? DANNY I don't care. You came here claiming to work on our team, and ever since you've been nothing but a selfish prick, using me and my rep to pull yourself up, and then stepping on me on your way up. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER So in other words...I started living in Hollywood. DANNY Get out of my house or I will throw you out. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Please. You and what army? With that, the bodyguards each grab an arm and a handful of cape. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Eep! CUT TO: EXT. FERGUSON ESTATE - CONTINUOUS Facey flies out the door and does a DJ Jazzy Jeff faceplant on the sidewalk. The loud punk music dies, as if someone just ripped the stereo out of the wall. Standing at the door, Danny gives his fallen teammate one final look...then slams the door. FADE OUT. FADE IN: INT. HOLLYWOOD OFFICE - THE NEXT DAY Danny, Allan and the bodyguards are walking somewhat briskly through a 'contemporary' hallway. Allan is particularly excited, and we join him mid-thought. ALLAN NORTH The call came yesterday. DANNY Was he even on the list? ALLAN NORTH No. That's the thing - we were told he was booked solid, then his guy calls out of the blue to gauge our interest. DANNY And we're interested? ALLAN NORTH I know LA has a lot of bullshit meetings, Danny, but at this point we wouldn't be standing outside the guy's door if we weren't desperately interested. They arrive on the scene and see a few familiar faces. Vernon Wallace, production head at Epimetheus Films, their studio, stands to shake hands. Scott Williams, co-author of 'More Than Just Hardcore,' is also introduced. VERNON WALLACE (hushed) This is all very exciting, Danny. If this works out, it would be huge for the film. DANNY So I hear, Mr. Wallace. Let's just hope he's willing to treat our baby with respect. How did we miss on him the first time? VERNON WALLACE Quite frankly, Dan, we didn't think we could affor- They're interrupted by a chirpy but not-worth-describing personal assistant. ASSISTANT Gentlemen? Mr. Mann will see you now. CUT TO: INT. MICHAEL MANN'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS The delegation from 'More Than Just Hardcore' pours into the room, where famed director Michael Mann is already seated at his desk. He makes sure to shake hands. MICHAEL MANN Guys...I hear you need a director. Everyone nods...walking on thin ice, really anxious to seal this possible deal. MICHAEL MANN Well...I want your film. VERNON WALLACE But Michael, don't you already have a film? Your schedule seems booked solid. MICHAEL MANN I don't want A film, Vernon. I want YOUR film. The level of unspoken excitement in the room is akin to a teenager who's about to see his first boobs. Still, no one has the balls to speak. Well, no one except Danny. DANNY Why? All eyes turn to him. Most are menacing. MICHAEL MANN Why? DANNY Yeah. We've got a lot of people looking at this thing, Mike. Sell me on you. Why should you direct our movie? ...and suddenly the girl's mother calls and she buttons the bra back up. Luckily, Mann takes the question in stride. MICHAEL MANN That's easy. Money. You'll have to excuse everyone else if they're a little taken aback. Noting the provacative statement, he elaborates. MICHAEL MANN I was told that you guys have come under a recent budget crisis. (pause for their nods) I need a low budget project to flex with. Miami Vice is killing me - we're overspending and I'm taking the heat. I need something small that confines me, that gives me a challenge. With a workable script, a great topic and a cooperative cast, More Than Just Hardcore is #1 on my radar right now. The excitement is a little more visceral and spoken now. Lots of hand-shaking and back-clapping. Excuse us if we don't include that part. CUT TO: EXT. HOLLYWOOD - LATER Danny and Allan burst out the doors onto the streets of LA. Neither could be happier right now, because More Than Just Hardcore booked a director. Michael Mann - he of Ali, Collateral, Heat and countless others - would be in charge of turning Danny into Terry Funk. And finally, E! would have a story to report that didn't involve their cash woes. As they approach the curb, they turn to continue conversation while waiting for the H2 to come around. ALLAN NORTH Do you want to break the news to Phyillis, or can I? DANNY I'd better do it. That way I can pretend that I knew this was coming, and that's why I never told her anything. ALLAN NORTH Fine, fine. I'll get with the studio people on that release. Meanwhile, you've got a flight to catch in six hours. Back to the moonlighting gig. DANNY PRIME beckons. I'm thinking about working a few Funk moves into the match this week. ALLAN NORTH I'm sure Michael will love that. Just don't get your face scrambled. We want any on-camera broken noses to be applied by la... (drifting) ...tex... Allan's eyes have shifted upward and are focused on something in the distance. Danny cocks his head to the side. DANNY You getting enough sugar there, cowboy? ALLAN NORTH There's someone...standing...on the Hollywood sign. DANNY A jumper? Again? What, is it Friday already? No big deal. One less audition tape to sift through. ALLAN NORTH Sure...except...I think it's your tag team partner... Danny slowly spins and looks off in the distance. Sure enough, a man is standing in the middle of the W on the famed Hollywood sign. And sure enough, he's wearing a mask, cape, socks, and boxers. DANNY Well, shit. FADE TO: EXT. HOLLYWOOD SIGN - LATER The best emergency vehicles that Tinseltown money can buy are lined up as close as possible to the W, with lights flashing and bodies racing between them. The Illustrious Face-Eater is still perched in the middle, teetering occasionally to give everyone a scare. E! is set up nearby, broadcasting an over-the-shoulder with the potential suicide scene. Away from it all, staring off at the scene, Danny keeps the BlackBerry close to his ear. DANNY ...and I didn't see him since. I mean, until now. CHANDLER TSONDA (via phone) Right, right. And...uh...he didn't say anything? DANNY Are you even listening to me? CHANDLER TSONDA (via phone) Dude, I'm multitasking. I'm at the gym. DANNY You're talking on the phone at the gym? CHANDLER TSONDA (via phone) There's this great new technology, Dan-o. It's called a 'hands-free' headset. I think the name is self explanatory, but the gist of it is- DANNY Alright, alright. I'm sorry to bother you with such a trivial concern when you're so busy. CHANDLER TSONDA (via phone) Who were we talking about again? (beat) Listen, D-Ferg, I'm on the Kyle Lamen training program. Gotta be the picture of health since he's, you know, not. I'll throw a text your way when I'm done tanning, cool? DANNY Yeah, fine, whatever. He ends the conversation and slides the phone/microcomputer back into its holster. He turns to Allan North, who is sporting the same kind of confused look he had when he first caught eye of Facey's location. DANNY Can you believe that guy? ALLAN NORTH Who? DANNY Chandler! He doesn't even care that Facey might kill himself! (beat) I mean, does he not realize what kind of hit my public image would take?! I can't afford a suicide! ALLAN NORTH I know, it's awful. Michael Mann won't even be the top story on Entertainment Tonight. DANNY We've gotta do something. If this goes to live coverage, we're sunk. He starts heading towards the scene. Allan does a double take before giving chase. ALLAN NORTH What are you going to do? DANNY End this. CUT TO: EXT. CLOSER TO THE SIGN - CONTINUOUS The emergency workers part as Danny stomps onto the scene. He looks right up at his partner and wastes no time. DANNY Get the hell down! Facey responds, but the...uh, hours of homelessness have taken their toll on his body and voice. He sounds strained and husky. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER FUCK YOU, SUPERSTAR! You've got a brand new movie! My life is practially over! DANNY You're being ridiculous! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Speak for yourself! I have no Hollywood career! Now what do I have to live for? DANNY Uh, maybe everything that you had BEFORE this week started! Pause. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER I'm gonna jump! Everyone screams. Danny tries to quiet them. DANNY So you know I'm right! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Huh? DANNY You didn't respond to me, so you know I'm right! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Fine, action hero! You tell me when it's ok for me to jump off this giant W and end the life that you helped ruin! DANNY If you give the media one more quote like that to print, I'll come up there and throw you off myself! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Yeah, good luck climbing when you can't even lift your arms over your head, Needle-Ass! With a quick 'that's it!' Danny grabs the W and starts scaling it. It wobbles with the pressure, and Facey follows suit. The whole crowd gasps as the structure teeters. ALLAN NORTH Danny, get down from there! The structure wobbles once again, and Facey stumbles back. With one more screech, he falls backwards off the W, and the crowd screams in terror. Well, they scream until they see what looks like a cardboard cutout of Facey falling from the W, backlit by a convenient spotlight. Facey's scream slowly dies off, followed by a fake thud. Danny drops off the W and dusts himself off. He's as confused as anyone else. DANNY What the hell was that? VOICE (O.S.) Aaaaand...CUT! Suddenly all the emergency workers stop looking concerned and begin exchanging high-fives. A small group of them begin moving towards a craft services table that seemed to appear out of nowhere. DANNY Allan, what the hell is going on? THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Cinema, my good man! Facey strolls out from behind the W, a director's beret over his mask. DANNY What did- THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Long story. Turns out my numbers guy did the calculating wrong on that $25 mil. I only needed, like $15,000. But I had all these SAG extras on retainer, so I figured, what the hell, let's teach Danny a life lesson that he never picked up from after school specials. DANNY A lesson? And what lesson was that? THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Jesus, I don't know, quit asking so many questions. Something about not throwing out your tag partner or worrying about money or something. Whatever. DANNY This... (waving his hand) ...was all staged? THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Pretty cool, huh? And it only cost me a small fraction of my new fortune. DANNY That's my money! THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER From what I hear, you're doing better without it. Reality sinks in, and Danny gives up. He turns to slink away, back home for a nap and an Advil before he had to be at the airport. Facey chases after him. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER Aw, come on! Did you not get the Legion of Doom reference? Is this not going to make us stronger? DANNY Leave me alone. THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE-EATER It didn't kill us! DANNY Not yet. The two continue arguing as they disappear over a hill. The sun is setting in LA, giving our scene a neat little silhouette. In this peaceful scene, one extra, a guy in a fireman's outfit, walks up to another, dressed as a concerned citizen. EXTRA Hey, did you get your paycheck yet? We're just trying to be a better tag team. Our name is The A- List *cue twangy down-home jingle* FADE TO BLACK.
