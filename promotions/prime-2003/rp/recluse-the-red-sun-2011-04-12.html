Recluse & The Red Sun

12 April 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-12-2011 Beijing Today... Mike Munson thought this might feel slightly better than Hell on Earth but what did he expect after all? He stole the belt, outright, from a man who had been broken before his very eyes by a man he hates with all of his soul. Devin Shakur was getting the last laugh right now, sitting wherever pompous assholes sit, and yucking it up at the fact that the only reason Big Bear has the 5*Star Title is because he allowed it. This was the punishment for committing the crime of wanting that strap so bad, so much, for so long. The names of his idols, his heroes, and even his allies run through his mind and he continues to question how they could do the things they did for that belt. He hasn’t left the hotel since he got to Beijing, he didn’t plan on it either, and there was little on his mind than getting himself out of this rut caused by the Emo. The sunlight reflects from across his suite twenty-two stories up. Mike hisses at first, unsure what it is, then recognizes the golden glow and quasi-circular shape. His eyes drift to the floor but snag a glimpse of the nearly empty bottle of Jack Daniels before they land on the plush red carpet under his feet. His left hand unscrews the cap while his right snags a glass, and goes through the whole inserting of ice cubes into the glass routine. The last thing he looks at is the dark brown whiskey fills the glass halfway before his mind drifts back to a time when he didn’t use a glass, or ice, or drinks to kill his emotions.
