Ensemble - Episode 3

Date: July 17th, Way Past Deadline Mike Munson stands in the middle of the ring, blood pouring from an open wound just under his left eye. Like two pearls in the Red Sea, his eyes are filled with pain masked by accomplishment. His arms raise high above his head, along with his newly attained GWA Alaska Championship. I did it, thinks the Big Bear to himself. The two-hundred and fifty-one west coasters from Portland were jumping up and down, shaking the George Hoover High gymnasium as Big Bear basked in his newfound glory. Hoping to use this as his launching pad, Munson waved for a microphone. “Whoa, whoa whoa.” From the back, a voice booms over the makeshift PA system. Confused, Big Bear snaps his neck, now facing down the aisle to whoever was interrupting the great moment in his 22 years on earth. “Please don’t tell me that you think this is over?” Out from behind the curtain emerges Clinton Bisk, the New Orleans native who founded the Global Wrestling Alliance. Flanking him were the GWA Tag Team Champions, the Prophets; Sin and Saint. It also happens Sin was next in line for an Alaska Title shot and was already the World Champion. Big Bear lowered his brow, muffled and gazing right into the back of Bisk’s squirrelly, little head. “See, Big Boob; there’s two things you always seem to forget.” Sin began as Bisk passed the mic off to his prized pounder. “The first being that these fans don’t want to hear anything that you have to say. It’s always chump this, homochump that-” ‘HO-MO-CHUMP! HO-MO-CHUMP!’ Sin gazed around through his long, black hair. Meanwhile, a grin spreads across Munson’s face as his arms fold over his bearlike chest. Big Bear was now staring a hole into Sin’s ugly mug. “Oh yeah, like that wasn’t coming. Really unpredictable, kids. I’m not here to talk to these saps, however. I am here to serve notice, though. The GWA Triple Crown is mine, Mikey Munson. It’s time to take a bow and head for the hills, while you have a chance. You and the rest of these idiots.” “You just park your two-minute trained ass right there and listen to a story,” barks out the Big Bear, calling for a roar from the crowd as blood still rushes from his face. “I just put in a Sixty Minute Iron Man match for this belt. I walked from Seattle to Spokane to make sure that I would get this shot. It’s not my fault that the Puppetmaster up there can’t follow your conspiracy plans close enough to keep the Bear out. It’s nobodies fault, because I’m here now, Sin. Here for good.” “Oh, right,” says Bisk, once again holding the microphone and rubbing the back of his head. “It’s been brought to my attention that the contract you signed had a special stipulation. Since you beat Ryan rock tonight in Portland, that means you’ll be defending against Sin. In Portland. Right now.” The boos rain in as Big Bear’s eyes narrow and he serves notice himself, on Bisk’s head. The three chuckle at the end of the aisle, beginning to make their way down to the ring. Big Bear drops the mic, preparing himself for whatever comes next, but then the trio stops in the tracks suddenly. “In an ‘I Quit’ match. Bust that, Chumpy.” Bisk gives Munson a shooting, sharp grin of arrogance and then Sin steps forward, removing his shirt to reveal his six-foot eight-inch, three-hundred plus pound frame. His arms were like marble columns, chiseled and seemingly created as masterpieces. Without hesitation, Big Bear shot backward and ran across the ring in a matter of nanoseconds – diving over the top rope and cross body-clocking the new menace of his night, Sin. The dings of the bell sounds as the fans begin to cheer and chant for the Bear Creek Brawler’s second match of the night. Bisk and Saint staggered back out of the action, except for when Saint charged the Bear. Forgetting about the second, Big Bear was sent into the guard rail as Sin got some help to his feet from his partner. ‘Ohhhh!!’ The Prophets were taken down by a vicious double clothesline from Munson’s trunklike arms. He laid some boots to Saint and then lifted Sin to his feet. With a Gorilla Press, Sin went flying into the crowd and onto the cold, hard wooden floor of the gymnasium. The fans around began to push Sin before he began tossing them out of his path to the guard rail. Out of nowhere, though; Sin stopped moving and slumped forward, revealing a small man holding a chair. The Dodge. Big Bear’s now former, now unemployed tag team partner, Derrick Dodge had just lambasted Sin over the head with a chair. Wearing a CHUMP BUSTER t-shirt and raising the chair in the air before attacking, Dodge hammered down on half of the GWA Tag Champs with no mercy; much like Sin and Saint treated Dodge at the Denver, CO show where he was injured and dismantled, only to be fired for misconduct by Bisk. Saint was getting the same type of treatment as Big Bear suplexed him onto the ring steps. The crowd was losing it and so was Bisk, who had begun pacing back and forth at the front of the entrance. He pulled his hair out with each chair shot or Chump Busting maneuver by Munson. The volume in the arena grew as Bisk continued to freak out. Stopping in his place, he noticed that Big Bear was standing at the opposite end of the aisle, pointing at him. “Your up.” With the fear filling his every vein, Bisk took off into the back. Big Bear gave chase. The Dodge did not; he was busy murdering the man who killed his career. Saint was out cold, slumped against the stairs and lifeless. As Bisk rounded the corner, he spilled garbage everywhere and threw people onto the ground to impede Mad Munson’s path. Sprinting down the corridor he did this until he made his way to the parking lot. ‘GET-THAT-CHUMP! GET-THAT-CHUMP!’ Inside the arena, the Dodge had entered the ring and started the ‘Chump Busting Chant’ his ex-partner needed. The giant sized projector screen portrayed the chase that was going on backstage: Munson finally made it into view and was running through the manmade obstacle course as he heard the scratching of a car that wouldn’t turn over. Bisk, who was sitting in his BMW M3, could not get it to start no matter how hard he wrenched the key over. He slammed his hands against the steering wheel, although his audio was muted by the windows. Then, the lights went out. Bisk gazed out the window to see a lot of chesthair and muscle as Big Bear removes his shirt. He quickly wrapped it around his hand and delivered a 5-knuckle-crucnh to Bisk’s face and window, knocking both out of sight. Munson opened the door and dragged Bisk out, picking him up by his sad excuse for a power tie. “Biskchump,” Big Bear warned. “You’re busted.” Spinning around, he threw Bisk into the back door of a van. Bouncing off, Bisk held his bleeding head, and walked right into it. ‘CHUMP-BUS-TER! CHUMP-BUS-TER!’ The arena rocked, inside and out, from the total demolition of the bad guys of GWA. Big Bear rests his hands on his hips, standing over the barely conscious ‘Only Owner, Ever’ Clinton Bisk. He spit on his cheap suit then looked into the starry night, taking in a deep breath and closing his eyes. He opened them. 3:35 AM Mike Munson rubbed his eyes as he glanced at the clock on the dash of the cab. He stretched, looking out into the darkness that was the highway surroundings. Just another dream. “You win?” Cabby says, tilting his head backward a bit so he can hear. He also smirked, glancing back at Munson finally. “Sounded like you won, anyways…” Big Bear let out a grin, still three-quarters asleep, and nodded to himself. “I always win, Cabby.” “I’d say you better start, then.” His dark skinned friend said, interjecting his bit of motivation. “Hah,” chuckled Big Bear. “Is that supposed to be my inspiration or something?” “No,” said Cabby nonchalantly. “That is.” As the cab passed along the highway, a giant light sat ahead. Big Bear rolled down his window as he peered out the front windshield to see a candlelight vigil in the median of the California Interstate Highway. “Bust that chump! Bust that chump! Bust that chump!” chanted the mass of people, all holding candles and signs for Big Bear. One read, ‘THE ONLY 5-STAR MATERIAL.’ Another; ‘BUSTIN CHUMPS SINCE GWA.’ The last one Mike saw was his favorite, a ‘Minichuuuump… BUST!’ sign. That sonofabitch, Gamble. He’s the cause of this. Ever since Night 1, he’s been screwing me like I’m some Asian whore. Not three. Nobody beats me three fucking times, let alone somebody who is shorter than Paris Hilton. Say goodnight, Gamble. I’m gonna rip you a new- “You been close, Big Bear. Damn close. But, as my Grandpaps used ta say; close only counts in horseshoes and hand grenades. Ya dig? (silence) What I’m tryin’ to say…” Cabby turns around, pausing to make sure this won’t anger his passenger. “Is you better start doin’… whatever it is you do, Mista Big Bear. Othawise, these people won’t be too happy, are they honkey?” Big Bear sat back in his seat, contemplating the advice. He thought to himself, eyes open and staring at the moon. Bustin’ chumps. That’s what I do Cabby. That’s what I do.
