Sleepless Nights

undated

Date: Various Date: 10/14/1990 2:40 AM Location: New York Alleyway behind a bar The moonlight outlines a number of dumpsters, and trees, but leaves the rest of the alley covered in darkness. Various sounds fill the alleyway including wind rustling the leaves, and voices and music coming from the bars that still remain open. No one roams the alleyway until a backdoor opens and two men are thrown out of the bar, one of them going directly to the floor while the other falls into a dumpster. “Don’t fucking come back here if you are gonna start shit.” The door quickly shuts taking the light out of the alleyway. The man on the ground gets up dusting himself off, while his friend slowly pulls away from the dumpster. “Man, that is some weak shit.” “Yeah those guys were fucked up bumping into shit everywhere and we get thrown out.” “Fucking New York. This place is a fucking shithole.” The two men make their way down the alley kicking rocks and grabbing at tree branches. They move from side to side unable to walk a straight line, and then suddenly one of them trips over something and falls to the floor. “HAHAHAHA! Are you that fucked up?” “No man, I tripped over something.” The man who is standing helps his friend up and the two of them look down to see what caused the fall. A rather large lump lies in the middle of the ground covered entirely by a dark blanket. “What the fuck is that?” “I don’t know kick it man.” One of them kicks the lump, and the lump lets out a low groan and moves around, but does not get up. The men jump back realizing something is alive under that blanket. “DUDE WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT?” The man again kicks the lump this time much harder. This causes a louder groan to come from underneath the blanket. There seems to be a person under the blanket as it rolls into a fetal position and starts to roll on the ground in pain. After the blanket stops moving one of the men pull the blanket off to reveal a boy underneath. “It’s a fucking kid!” “What should we do?” “Get the fucker up.” The boy has a ripped up t-shirt, and jeans and his eyes are open wide as he has no idea what is going on. The two men repeatedly kick at the boy until he scoots back into a wall. “Co wy robicie?” “What the fuck did he say? What did you say?” The two men stare at the boy, but the boy does not respond. The boy finally rises to his feet, and easily rises above the two men. The boy’s face is hairless, and he has unkempt brown hair, but his body is that of a man’s. Through the hair we see that this is the face of a young Tom Walczak. The men stare at the boy not knowing what to do next, until one of the men winds up and pushes Walczak into the wall. Walczak does nothing in retaliation, just staring confused at the two men. “What is up with this kid?” “I have no fucking clue. What is your deal boy?” “Ja nie muwie po Angielsku. No speak English.” The two men erupt into laughter and stumble around trying to catch their balance. They lean on each other trying to catch their breath while emitting their last fits of laughter. “We’re dealing with a fucking immigrant boy.” “This kid has no fucking clue what is going on.” One of the men gently slaps Walczak’s face, and this sends of a trigger in Walczak’s head. He forcefully pushes the man sending him crashing into a tree. The man’s friend retaliates trying to push Walczak, but Walczak manages to stand his ground. “What the fuck was that man? You just fucking pushed my boy.” “Ty mie obudzilesz. Masz problem, CZY TY MASZ PROBLEM?” As Walczak raises his voice the man backs up right into his friend. The two men look at each other, not looking nearly as confident as they had before. “Maybe we should just fucking leave. This kid is fucking crazy, and he can’t even speak fucking English.” “Na man, fuck that. We’ll attack him from two sides. This kid is gonna fucking learn his first lesson in America. Don’t fuck with people.” The two men rush at Walczak from opposite sides. Walczak quickly ducks a hook from the first man and throws a knee to the second from the clinch sending him to the ground. He bull-rushes the second man drilling him into the tree yet again. He punches the man in the sternum knocking the wind out of him. Walczak has little time to recover as the first man latches onto his back trying to choke Walczak. Walczak flails his arms wildly trying to throw the man from his back. He finally grabs a hold of the man’s collar and tosses him of his back, but as he turns around he is met with a lead pipe to the stomach sending him to the ground in an instant. “Hurry up lets get the fuck out of here.” “Yeah let’s go. Fucking pussy.” One of the men spits on Walczak as they run out of the alleyway. The ruckus must have been heard inside because the same door opens up and a man comes outside to see what is going on. He sees Walczak lying in the middle of the alleyway and rushes over to him. “Are you alright?” Walczak lets out a moan, but remains unresponsive. The man rushes back inside and calls an ambulance. Date: 10/15/1990 9:15 AM Location: Sacred Heart Hospital, Queens, New York Walczak sits on a hospital bed keeled over holding his right hand over his stomach. He is in the room by himself, but has been waiting in the bed for over an hour. This is after he had spent three hours in the waiting room. He is irritated and tired, but his mind is focused on the pain. Finally, a doctor walks into the room. He has on his white overcoat, and thick black glasses. He stares at his clipboard trying to see exactly what problem Walczak has. “Mr. Walczak, I see that you have a problem with your sternum, is that correct?” Walczak cannot understand the man, and the words mean nothing to him. “Mr. Walczak do you understand me?” “No speak English.” “I see.” The doctor once again looks into his clipboard. He takes a deep breath not knowing what to do next. “Okay, well let’s just have a look under your shirt see if anything is visibly wrong.” Walczak moves his arm out of the way, and the doctor pulls his shirt up a little bit. A large welt the size of a basketball takes up most of Walczak’s stomach. The doctor takes a step back visibly shaken by what he has just seen. “That you are even walking amazes me Mr. Walczak. We are going to send you over to the X-Ray lab and see how many ribs are broken, and than we are going to run some tests to see if there is any internal damage.” Walczak gives the doctor a dumbfounded stare. He pulls his shirt back down slowly grimacing in pain. “Right, you don’t speak English. Just follow me and we will take care of everything.” Walczak gets up and slowly hobbles after the doctor leaving the room. As the leave the room the doctor pawns off Walczak on a nurse who takes him down the hall. The doctor turns around and is met by a second nurse. “Why did you leave that patient Dr. Rambis?” “He didn’t speak fucking English, couldn’t understand a word. He is gonna be hell to deal with, and I’d bet a month’s pay the bastard doesn’t have insurance.” “We have more and more of them coming into the country every day. What nationality was he?” “I don’t know, but his last name sounds like he was a fucking Polak or something.” By this time Walczak is half way down the hall, but he hears the word Polak, and instantly knows that the doctor wants nothing to do with him. He thought that this country would be different, that he may have been accepted, but anywhere that Walczak goes he is not accepted. Date: 6/2/2007 10:40 AM Location: Walczak’s House Walczak is lying on his couch staring blankly at the ceiling. He takes a gulp from his beer, and tosses the empty beer can into a pile beside the couch. The television is on in the background playing Poland’s latest European Cup Qualifying Match versus Azerbaijan. “And Poland wins yet again with a final score of 3-1. They have been running away with group A in this qualifying round running up their tally to 19 points. Poland will be in action again on Wednesday looking to firmly cement their place in Euro Cup 2008.” Walczak sits up on the couch and grabs another beer from his nearly finished thirty. “POLSKA GURA!” Walczak raises his hands into the air, and falls back into the couch. For the past few nights Walczak had been binge drinking, unable to sleep as he reminisced about the past terrors he had faced in his life. “You know what Eubeniusz, Poland will never get respect.” Looking around the apartment no one, but Walczak is inside, but he sits on the couch waiting for a response. “No matter what happens or how much we will accomplish they will always laugh. Isn’t that right Eubeniusz?” Walczak takes a gulp of his beer, and again awaits a response from the empty room. Walczak finishes up his beer and getting up from the couch he stumbles on his way to the kitchen. He opens up a cabinet and pulls out a plastic, red, Solo Cup. He stuffs the Solo Cup with a paper towel, and starts to pack a tin of Skoal on his way back to the couch. Walczak sits on the couch focusing intently on the television as he stuffs a large dip into his mouth. “Now we send it down to our field reporter, Janusz Michalek.” “Thank you Thommy, I am joined by Polish striker Eubeniusz Smolarek, who played a great game setting up two of Poland’s goals.” We now see that Walczak was not talking to anyone in the room, but instead to the television, to his favorite player, and fellow Pole Eubeniusz Smolarek. “It was great game, and I am happy we won.” “Do you think that this will advance Poland in the World Rankings?” “Uh, It no matter if we advance in World Rankings. We will show what we can do in Euro Cup.” “Thank you Eubeniusz, and once again congratulations on your victory.” Eubeniusz runs off the pitch and makes his way to the lockers. Walczak watches his countryman, but he doesn’t seem to approve as he shakes his head. “Eubeniusz, you have to understand no matter what happens you cannot change the opinion of people across the world. You can win the Euro Cup, but people will still laugh when you tell them you’re from Poland.” Walczak turns off the TV, and lies back on the couch. Eubeniusz you will eventually see. You cannot live your life trying to please everybody. Focus on Poland, fuck the world. Fuck…the…world… Walczak nods off immediately emitting loud snores. These are the first moments of sleep that Walczak has had in over four days, and he is visibly happy as he sleeps with an awkward smile with tobacco spilling from his mouth. Date: 11/30/2005 11:25 PM Location: 248th Street, Queens, New York Walczak sits on his couch, thinking about his encounter with Mr. James Rogers just minutes ago. What was that guy thinking coming into my home like that. He does not come to welcome me to this country, or even to try and sell me some worthless American goods, no he comes to accuse me of something that I am not guilty of. Why was I so gullible to think that I alone could change these Americans? Hundreds of years of brainwashing people to think that all which is not theirs is wrong, they are helpless. THUD…THUD…THUD Walczak is shaken by the thunderous knocks at his door. He swiftly rises from his couch and makes his way towards the door. “Who is it, and what the fuck do you want?” “Walczak this is the NYPD SWAT team, and we would appreciate that you come out peacefully.” “Why? Why the fuck would I come outside, only to be humiliated? I don’t think that I will be coming outside at all.” “Walczak this is your last warning. If you do not come outside we will be forced to use physical means to come and get you.” “I’m gonna go make some dinner, so come inside if you want anything.” Walczak turns from the door, and heads towards the kitchen. When all of a sudden, CRACK! The front window shatters and gas fills the room. It is followed shortly by the cracking of windows on the side, and more gas empties from canisters. “WHAT THE FUCK!!! YOU GUY’S ARE FUCKING CRAZY!!!” Walczak rubs his eyes furiously, and the skin on his body starts to turn bright red. The gas slowly starts to die down, and go away, as the door is cracked down by the SWAT forces. A number of men all wearing gas masks barge into Walczak’s home. The first pair of men try to grab Walczak as he continues to rub his eyes. As the first officer grabs Walczak’s arm he knocks him down viciously with his free one. Feeling the second man is near him he knees him in the gut and throws him to the side. More forces charge towards Walczak, but seeing what he has done to their compatriot they hesitate in progressing any further. One of the officers pulls out a taser and shoots at Walczak from a distance. “AHHHHHHH!!! WHAT THE HELL IS HAPPENING!!!!!” The taser shot stiffens Walczak, but does not send the behemoth to the ground. Taken aback by the resilience the giant the men do not know what else to do. A man from the back of the pack comes to the front with a rather large gun held in his hands. “Move away, let’s try this.” The officer shoots a non-lethal bag at Walczak that hits him squarely in the kidney. Walczak keels over, and land on one knee grabbing at his gut. A second shot fires from the gun and hits Walczak in the head this time knocking the big man unconscious. “Alright men, move in.” Six officers gang tackle Walczak handcuffing both his arms and his legs. Once he is securely tied up the officers grab him and slowly bring him outside. He is loaded into the back of a SWAT truck and the doors are closed. The officers who stayed behind take off their masks, and try to regain their energy. “Man, that guy is one tough son-of-a-bitch.” “Yeah, fucking roided up Polak.” “Why were we getting him anyway?” “He came into this country illegally man. The guy has no respect for this country.” “Man, we went through all of this just to get some guy out of this country. That sounds a little bit rough doesn’t it?” “Are you kidding me? He got what he deserved. Our parents worked hard to give us a life, and this guy just comes in trying to take that away from us.” “Alright man, whatever you say. I gotta get going, but I’ll talk to you tomorrow Dombrowski.” Date: 6/2/2007 11:32 AM Location: Walczak’s House Walczak wakes up fervently, breaking into a sweat, and breathing heavily. He was not even able to get an hour of sleep. He walks over to the kitchen sink, still breathing heavily. Walczak washes his face and tries to gather his bearings. “Damn, I just can’t get any sleep.” Walczak spits out his tobacco into the sink, and grabs a paper towel to dry his face. These Americans have ruined me. They are in my head, and I am unable to do anything to get them out. I want nothing more than to rid myself of the people and culture of America. They have done nothing but humiliate me every time that I enter this country. I want to show them what they did was wrong, and I will show them that Poland is not a country to be messed with. POLSKA GURA….
