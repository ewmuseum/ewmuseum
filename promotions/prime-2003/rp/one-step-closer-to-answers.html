One Step Closer to Answers

Date: ReVolution 175 “When you have come to the edge of all light that you know and are about to drop off into the darkness of the unknown, Faith is knowing one of two things will happen: There will be something solid to stand on or you will be taught to fly” Faith. It’s all Craig had at this point. It’s all you can have when you’ve had so much ripped away from you, torn from your very essence, before you reach that point of no going back. So much of the pieces in his life had been analyzed over and over again that his mind had become numb thinking about it all. Righting wrongs couldn’t be any more difficult then what he had encountered. His back was up against the wall and he could feel every fiber of his being ready to lash out at any and everything that crossed his path. Somehow though, Craig’s mind had taken over and decided to go the logical route before a war broke out that he couldn’t begin to put back inside of a box. Faith had taken him this far. Eventually, though, he’d have to let go of that faith and end this the way he should’ve ended it a long time ago. As he stands inside of a large bookshop, a place one wouldn't typically find himself in, a man walks out from the back with this cautious look on his face as he holds a picture in his hands; one of Craig surrounded by a woman only known as Kristen and a man that's unknown to Craig. Yet, answers were needed, and he had come to the one person he thought could help him. Judging by the look on his face though, he didn't have the answers Craig was looking for at this particular moment. Craig takes a long look at him before taking a deep breath in and slowly letting it all out. "Nate, how long do you think this is going to take?" Craig asks him and the older man gives him this crooked look for a moment with one eye on Craig and the other on the picture. Craig sticks both of his hands into his pants pockets and begins to walk up to Nate. Nate then puts the picture down by his side as he takes a long look at Craig. "Not quite sure on how long it's going to take. I don't recognize the other two, just you. I'll have to call someone and see if they have any information, see if they can help you at all," Nate responds in this wispy, high-pitched voice as he pulls his glasses off of his face before rubbing the bridge of his nose. Tension was obvious in his face and shoulders, and Craig could only wonder what was wrong with his old friend. He places his hand on Nate's shoulder and gives him this look to just calm down, but it's not use as Nate just is in his own world. Craig then takes a step back to give Nate his space. "Thanks man. I just need this... bad. I don't know what else to do at this point, you know? I need answers; I need to know which path I need to go down to solve the things in this world. I've done so much bad... I need to make sure I do something right for once. You're the only one who can help put me down that path," Craig responds, knowing that he's only putting more pressure on Nate's shoulders. He wouldn't put that pressure though unless he thought Nate could handle it. He looks over Nate though and notices that it's just not stress, but that he just doesn't look well physically at all. Something was on Nate's mind and it was something that Craig wasn't going to get out of him anytime soon. "Well, I hope I can help then, Craig. I'll call my guy now," he says, his voice cracking as he does so. He then turns away from Craig before putting his glasses back on his face. Craig then looks at the room around him and just shakes his head. He wasn't quite reading kind of guy. Something just always appealed him more about hitting things with his fists. "Thanks. I guess I'll just stand here, and you know, look at books... and stuff," Craig says jokingly, hoping to calm Nate down just a little bit. "Ha, have fun," Nate responds with much tension in his voice as he disappears behind a curtain. As Craig begins to look around at the books, hoping to find something to keep him occupied for an hour or so while Nate did his research, Nate walked towards the phone out of Craig's range. Nate looks around nervously as he picks up the phone and dials a few digits on the phone before putting the receiver to his phone. The sweat is starting to build up on Nate's forehead as he starts licking his lips furiously. "Hey, he's here. What? No, I don't know if there's anyone else with him. He just came in here with a picture of him with two other people. I'm not sure how long he's going to stay though and honestly, he can tell shit like that. So, get down here now," he says in the span of forty-five seconds. As quick as the phone call begins, it ends, and he hangs the receiver up. As he turns around though and begins to walk through the curtain again, he runs smack into a brick wall. As he straightens himself up, he notices that it's Craig standing there, and not looking quite too happy. Nate's eyes get rather large as he takes a step backwards as Craig just glares at him. "What the hell did you just do?" Craig asks him threateningly, his anger starting to grow. All Nate can do though is swallow a large gulp of saliva. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "We've got operations running at full speed in Paris, Baghdad, Moscow, Tokyo, and Beijing now. In another fifteen days, we'll be looking at everything going in San Paolo, Buenos Aires, Madrid, London, and Johannesburg. Oh, and of course, we've got North America more then covered," a voice booms confidently. That voice belongs to one Malik Randolph, the close confidant and right hand man of Alexander Lewis. Dressed in a rather expensive suit with a handful of men standing behind him, and a handful of men behind them he looks cocky and confident all rolled into one. The violence had quieted down on one front and things had improved to the point that there were no worries as they continued to take over globally. As he looks over the different men, coming from different background, and economic standings, he can only smile at his position in everything. "Any questions?" he asks them as they all look around and nod their heads, pleased with the progress that Alexander's corporation had made in such a short time. For Alexander, his dreams were coming true and without the pressure he thought would come from them. Then, one of the men raises his hand and looks at Malik with great interest. "Mr. Randolph, I don't think I quite understand one thing. How do you think all six of us will be able to co-exist? No offense, but we work in a business were backstabbing is quite... well, how should I put it, common. What steps have you taken to ensure that it won't get out of hand like it has many times before hand?" the man asks in a foreign accent. As those words leave his mouth, Malik already has the answer for him as he takes a few steps backwards with a large smile on his face. "I'm glad you asked that question," he begins to tell them. The man who had asked the question has this peculiar look on his face, getting the sense that something wrong is growing around him. "You see, we've decided to address those issues right here and now, because you see, my boss, Mr. Lewis, is very much aware of those things. You see, he doesn't enjoy taking large risks, and well, quite frankly, you six are just problems in the equation he has in mind." Then, he takes a few steps backwards, as the six men all look around rather lost as to what he means by that. CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! CLICK! Six bullets. Six men fall into the dirt face first without even the notion that their deaths were coming. Malik looks around at the dead bodies before looking to his right and then to his left to see several agents moving in to remove the bodies from his view. As they do just that, Malik walks up to the men most trusted by these leaders and looks them each in the eye. Fear is written on their faces, unaware of what was to come on this very day. Yet, that fear will wash away within seconds of hearing Malik soothe their worries. "Don't worry; we're not going to kill you as well. No, instead, you six will become the new leaders of your different organizations and businesses. But, there's one stipulation. You walk out of here knowing that you report to me, and to Mr. Lewis. We run your businesses now, and if you have a slight issue with any of that then now would be the time to speak up. Otherwise, if you leave here today with the silliest notion that you're in charge, it won't just be you who suffers their fate," he motions to the bodies being dragged away. "It will be your families as well. Understand?" The question though is rhetorical as Malik smiles before he turns on his heel and begins to walk away. Just another day. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Craig's hand lashes out as fast as possible as he wraps it around Nate's throat and lifts his puny body off of the ground! His red eyes are flashing hot as he looks Nate in the eyes and gives off this sense that Nate better answer every one of his questions. "What... have you done, NATE?!" he asks him while his voice rises in anger with each syllable leaving his lips. Nate looks like he's about to piss himself when all hell begins to break loose. SMASH! The sound of glass shards falling to the ground echoes throughout the room as Craig quickly lets go of Nate and turns his head towards the front room where the noise came from. Nate can only look around frantically as he wishes he hadn't gone down this route. This was the wrong route for him to go down. "Ohmygoodness... Craig, the money out on your head right now...," he mutters randomly as he looks around. Craig's eyes snap back towards Nate as he wonders to himself when a bounty had been put on his life. Yet, he doesn't get much time to think about this as the door behind him is kicked in and three men rush into the room with one intent on their mind. As Craig turns his body, readying himself for a fight, another three men rush through the curtain, surrounding Craig in a position that doesn't make him feel rather comfortable. Craig looks around at his situation and notices that things are pretty bad. "Alright," Craig says to the six men intent on either killing him or taking him to their boss, Alexander. "We can do this the difficult way, or, well, I think there's only really the difficult way." As the last word exits his lips, Craig takes a step backwards and slams his elbow into the sternum of one of the men who rushed in from the front room! Then, he manages to narrowly duck a kick from the man standing right next to him, causing Craig to duck down to his knees and takes the man down with a simple leg sweep! As Craig leaps back up to his feet, he's tackled from behind by two men from the other trio and sends him flying into the front of the bookshop and into a bookshelf! Books start flying down as Craig reaches behind him and grabs the neck of one of them before Craig slams his boot into the man's stomach. As the man crumples down to the ground, Craig turns around only to be met with a fist across his jaw! The blow is enough to send Craig backwards into the shelf and knocks it down with Craig going down with it! As he lands onto the shelf, a boot comes flying straight at his face and he manages to grab it just at the last possible second before slamming his foot into the man's chest! The guy stumbles backwards into the wall as Craig gets up to his feet and goes for a jab to the guy's face only for him to duck out of the way and Craig's fist slamming into the wall, putting a dent in it. Craig then takes a step backwards and narrowly dodges a fist coming at him. Behind him though, one of the other men puts him into a full nelson while another man slams his knee into Craig's stomach and then slamming Craig's face into his knee! Craig drops down to one knee before being punted in the ribs by another guy! Slowly, Craig starts to get back up to his feet, and as he does so one man rushes at him with a knife! Craig is quick enough though to grab the wrist of the man and break it to cause him to drop the knife. With little effort, Craig picks it up before slamming the end of it into the guy's back and pushes him into the nearby wall! As he turns around though he has to duck quickly to miss another knife being slashed across his face! Craig goes to knee the man in his stomach, but is met with a knife to his left shoulder blade. "ARRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRGH! Craig bellows loudly enough to wake up a few dogs down the street as the blood begins to pour out of his open wound! He reaches back to pull the knife out when another knife cuts into him, this time into his right shoulder blade. The pain momentarily blinds him as he finds himself onto all fours with the distinct feeling of blood staining his back. As he digs down deep though, he can feel another round of strength and adrenaline entering his bloodstream as he flips his body sideways and kicks one of the men in the stomach before he's met with another kick to the face that sends him back down to the ground. The remaining three men though circle around Craig, waiting to finish him off as he fights up to his knees in a world of pain. "So, this is how it ends for me? Then come end it," Craig mutters to them, knowing that the end is closer than even he thought. As one of the men moves towards Craig though, a gunshot rings through the air and the bullet rips through the man's throat, dropping him on the ground! Another gunshot goes and the bullet tears apart the brains of another man! With the element of surprise at Craig's use, he gets up before slamming his elbows into the final guy's chest and sends him into a wall. As the man drops to the ground, sitting up, Craig takes a step back before slamming his boot into the guy's throat, and crushing every bone in there, leaving him to suffocate. With the fight over, for the moment, Craig looks around to find where the bullets came from only to find a man standing in the back doorway with this menacing look on his face. Craig reaches behind him and manages to pull one of the knives out of his back as he glares at the man. It takes a few seconds before Craig pauses and notices the man staring back at him; the man in the picture with Kristen and him. "You...," Craig whispers without much else to say. The man then walks up to Craig and helps him pull the other knife from his back. "I think it's time we talk," the man says. "There's a lot of explaining to do." It's the last thing Craig hears though as he passes out before the man, the loss of blood getting to him.
