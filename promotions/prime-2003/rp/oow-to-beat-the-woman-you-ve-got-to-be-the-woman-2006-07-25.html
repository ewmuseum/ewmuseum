OOW! To Beat the Woman... You've got to BE the Woman!

25 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25/07/06 The Briefing (1202 Hours - Saturday) In case you haven’t noticed recently, PRIME’s longest-serving current roster member Adam has been acting a little – how can I say this politely? – …irregular? Not as if being a bisexual Scottish wrestler with long silver hair and two children is anywhere CLOSE to being regular in the first place, but lately… well lately he’s been pretty much deluded. Something inexplicable has occurred that has made the “Number One Son” believe he’s some sort of undercover agent for the CIA and perhaps even more inexplicably than that… his best friend, Ignatius Lisieux, has barely batted an eyelid about it. Oh no, Mr. Lisieux himself is partial to the old “cup of crazy” now and again and so in his eyes having a fully-grown man playing Commando with the world isn’t anything to get particularly flustered over… in fact it’s something that can be taken advantage of – especially when Adam seems to think Ignatius is his Chief Commanding Officer, or something like that… So here Ignatius is, fresh out of hospital and with not a lot to do other than try and get himself match-fit for ReVolution One-oh-Two and something interesting strikes him… just like Dastardly Dick… our protagonist has a cunning plan. “Dude, this is seriously the best idea I’ve had since introducing a little moisturiser into the sex life of Palmela Handerson and Little Richard…” The “Dude” wasn’t in reference to anyone present other than the man in the mirror, Iggy was actually alone in the bathroom of Adam’s luxurious (NB: formerly luxurious) apartment checking himself out in full military apparel. “Why!?” I hear you cry from your chair (which, if you sniff it, probably smells a whole lot like ass… yes, even yours, Lindz)… but not so hasty young one, much more to explain there is. Because of Adam’s insistence on saluting Lisieux every time he entered the room, the French-Canadian had conjured-up an ideal plan of action for their contest with Lindsay Troy and Joey Melton this week… If Adam wanted a commander, then Lisieux would give him a commander and so, with beret intact, he dragged himself away from the mirror and made his way to the living room – where he fully expected to find his crazy Scottish buddy exactly where he had left him. “Corporal Weir… Stand to attention.” Without a moment of hesitation, Adam was onto his feet with a salute in place as Ignatius strolled formally into the room. I have to tell you about the scene, seriously, it’s golden… Adam’s sitting at a single military-style high school desk with a bunch of crumpled white paper and a solitary pen, the lights in the room are dimmed and there’s a projector set-up on the couch which has a projected slide of the PRIME logo aimed onto the wall of the apartment. “Sir, yes, Sir!” He held the salute firmly in place and stood as still as humanly possible, wearing a white vest and khaki shorts, topped-off with chrome dog-tags and suede boots. “At ease, soldier…” Adam quickly relaxed and immediately returned to his seated position, eager and hunched forward, awaiting the “top secret” briefing. “Now, Corporal Weir… I have brought you to this location today to discuss our top secret battle plan: codename “Pre-Op”… this plan will be enforced in approximately sixty hours’ time against our enemy: the mysterious “Business & Pleasure” regiment of the PRIME forces…” The Scotsman scribbled away furiously at his pad and Lisieux couldn’t help but smirk… he was having a harder time than he had imagined trying to keep a straight face about the whole ordeal. “If you would turn you attention towards the slideshow, our briefing shall begin…” Ignatius grabbed his “pointer” from its resting position and immediately slapped it against the white wall as an image of Business and Pleasure clicked onto it. “These two are the targets… Joey Melton here… and Lindsay Troy here… as you may have noticed: she is a female.” Adam nodded, taking the entire thing completely seriously. “Now, you know as well as I do Corporal that you must become “one” with the enemy in order to understand it’s tactics and therefore defeat it… agreed?” “Sir, yes Sir!” He understood alright, as shown by the velocity of his yelled response. “Seeing how both teams consist of two members, we must strive for equilibrium of the sexes… I won’t beat around the bush here - I’m looking for a volunteer to become a female for the week…” Again without hesitation, Adam’s hand was flung into the air. “Excellent Corporal, excellent… I already have our plan of action in place, we must achieve complete submersion into the female psyche. It’s not going to be easy, but I have full faith in you to triumph.” “I will not let you down, Sir!” Ignatius Lisieux smiled: he loved every minute of this. “…Firstly, we must achieve physical parity with Lindsay Troy… we have a rendezvous scheduled with a special-ops team at the local mall at precisely thirteen-hundred hours… quickly Corporal, man the vehicle.” “Sir, yes Sir!” And with that he was gone… up and out from his desk faster than a flash of lightning, headed for the exit of the apartment with car keys in hand. Ignatius merely smiled - he knew exactly what was on the cards for his running buddy, all of the effort he’d put into things so far looked like being worthwhile. His boredom had been crushed, Adam was going to be humiliated and they might even beat Business and Pleasure… he followed Adam from the apartment with a definite spring in his step. “Physical Parity” (1305 Hours - Saturday) Adam was laid-out on his back, his modesty only just protected by a skimpy white towel as Ignatius and a friendly-looking female looked down on him with smiling eyes. They were inside of a well-known beauty store in the local mall and the “Corporal” was being prepared for the first stage of “physical parity”: waxing. “Are you sure you’re ready for this, soldier?” “Sir, yes Sir! Ignatius grinned again and looked-up towards the Asian attendant… “May I take this one, please?” “Of course Sir, go ahead…” *SCHLIP* “UNGHHnnnnnhhhhhhhhhhhh…” Adam gritted his teeth valiantly, desperately not trying to scream as his “Commander” proudly waved a clump of chest-hair in his face… “Very good, Corporal… very good - I shall let this young lady take it from here, I need some food - I’ll just be over the way getting a burger…” Truth-be-told, he just didn’t want to see anymore. Before this, Ignatius had booked him in for a manicure, a pedicure, eyebrow shaping, a crew-cut and a spray-tan… all in the name of “physical parity”. What a GREAT friend, eh? He figured that in around an hour the Scotsman would look like a hermaphrodite Olympic swimmer… or a chemo-case. As Ignatius strolled off towards Fuddruckers – still fully attired – Adam was left to contend with what he soon came to perceive as an Asian interrogation session… which brings us to… “Sir, SIR!? You need to come… right away!” “What the hell’s happened!?” Ignatius’ freshly-prepared burger hung just millimetres from his lips as the receptionist from over the way called towards him… “It’s your friend... he’s trapped Mao Ling in the waxing room!” The burger dropped down to the plate as Iggy’s face sunk. Karma. “Oh Jesus… why didn’t I see this one coming!? We’d better hurry…” Back in the waxing room, Adam (who had successfully completed the chest, back and leg parts of the waxing already) was kneeled over Mao Ling with wide eyes, breathing heavily… “HOW DO I ESCAPE THIS PLACE!? There’ll be damn Charlie everywhere!” “PLEASE… let me go, please!” “You cowardly snake… how can you do that to a man’s private area!? What kind of sick torture methods are you Vietcong bastards employing in this hell-hole!?” Mao Ling was visibly shaken… she had a crazed six-four Scotsman kneeling down on her, basically naked, and all she had been doing was her job… seems the old sack-wax didn’t sit particularly well with the “Number One Son”… “CORPORAL WEIR, AT EASE! IMMEDIATELY!” Ignatius, looking exceptionally flustered, burst through the door at a rapid pace. “Sarge, you came back for me! …What are we gonna do with this one, Sarge?” Caught between a rock and a hard place in regards to fantasy and reality, Ignatius waited a moment before deciding which course of action to take. He looked down at Mao Ling appeasingly and gently lifted Adam off her… “We must leave her be, Corporal… (I’m sorry about this!) …Now, get dressed and come with me, we must move onto stage two of the plan immediately (Really, I am… we’ll settle this later on, I promise!)” “Stage two?” inquired Adam… “Camouflage…” Blending In (1356 Hours - Saturday) “Where to now, Sir?” Despite the trail of destruction that had been left at the beauty store, Ignatius Lisieux and Adam walked casually through the mall as though nothing at all had happened… there were gossiping bodies all around them, but they paid them no heed. Ignatius just wanted to get out of that place as soon as possible, but as much as his head told him to “quit”, he was determined to see the charade through. “Corporal… stage one is (almost) complete… we must now achieve camouflage via disguise…” “Disguise, Sir?” Lisieux smiled a devious smile… “Yes, Corporal… we must find the most flowery dress in this entire mall, and you must wear it… and then it’s make-over time.” The French-Canadian sniggered a little at the prospect of the massive Scotsman in a flowered dress wearing lipstick, but Adam remained oblivious. He would do exactly what he was told. “Hello there, welcome to Tracy’s… buying something for the wives?” Ignatius stumbled over the greeting a little bit and almost began to blush before replying… “…Erm, no … we’re here to get something for Adam…” She might as well have just said “rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrright” and rolled her eyes, it would have given the same effect that the look of pure surprise and disgust did. “Would you be so kind as to help us out?” Immediately pouncing on the glare, Lisieux decided it best to punish the teenage girl as thoroughly as possible for her distaste at two men buying female clothes for one another. The French-Canadian was a stickler for human rights… and he knew how Adam was, anyway. “We’re looking to get some nice summer apparel… maybe a few flowered dresses, a couple of pairs of slip-on sandals… some accessories?” Ignatius creepily put his arm around the girl and prompted her to lead them in the required direction, Adam followed close behind, constantly scanning the area for a foreign threat (a turban, basically.) “Ah, now this would look simply fabulous… and oh! I LOVE this one, too!” In his best “camp” voice, Ignatius minced around that store like a man possessed. Adam must have tried on around a dozen outfits, but only one would suffice… the crew-cut Scotsman paraded awkwardly in front of a mirror in a pink flowered dress, complete with yellow slip-on sandals… “Oh my, don’t you just look wonderful!? …Doesn’t he look fabulous, dear?” “Errr… yeah… you look stunning.” The sarcasm was evident enough for Ignatius to deliver a fabled death-stare at the back of the young girls’ head… “Will that be all?” she asked Adam in a “relieved it could be over soon” sort of a way. “Ask the Commander…” “…the Commander?” “That’s me!” piped up Ignatius, “I’m the Commander and he’s my Corporal…” CUE: (“Rrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrrriiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiigggghhhhhhhhhhhhhhht”) Or that’s what she thought again, at least. “Well I’ll bag these up for you and put them behind the counter, okay? Thanks for shopping at Tracy’s!” And then she basically… no she literally… RAN from the scene. Ignatius patted Adam on the back with another smile… “You’re doing great, soldier… it’s time to get some war paints… err, leave that one on please…” Adam shrugged his shoulders as Ignatius led him towards the counter, a man dressed in a military costume and a man dressed as a woman… perhaps for this week alone the duo could be called “Freak-Show”. War Paint (1447 Hours - Saturday) “Sir… I don’t feel comfortable, Sir!” “Just relax, will you? I’d say the dress is the worst of it over with and you’ve been handling that alright…” “Sir, is the blonde wig really necessary, Sir?” “Of course it’s necessary! How many women do you see with crew cuts?” “Sir, not many, Sir!” “Sir?” “Yes, Corporal?” “…Is the blusher necessary, Sir?” “Yes, Corporal.” “How about the lipstick, Sir?” “Definitely, Corporal.” “…the eyeliner, Sir?” “Definitely. No more questions.” Because Ignatius was about to pee his pants with laughter, he simply couldn’t take any more. He was already almost doubled-over and ready to go as he looked down on perhaps the greatest sight he had ever seen… Adam, at six-four and nearly three-hundred pounds was utterly hairless from the neck downwards - he was wearing a pink flowered dress, yellow sandals and a blonde wig and was carrying a delightfully petit handbag with him and right at that exact moment… he was struggling to apply some lipstick to his already-covered face. JC Penny had never seen anything like it. By now, it being around four in the afternoon, a so-called “five o’clock shadow” was creeping onto the scene with a vengeance, much to the amusement of the French-Canadian. The transformation process was almost complete but the Scotsman still looked like a four-dollar transsexual hooker – rather than like the admittedly gorgeous Lindsay Troy. “Well…” began Ignatius… ”I’d say our job is almost done, here… Let me take a look at you.” Adam placed the lipstick back down on the shelf; straightened up the curly blonde wig and turned back to face Lisieux head-on… to say the French-Canadian was amused would be an understatement. “Sir, what’s so funny Sir?” “…N-Nothing Corporal… I’m just remembering a joke from yesterday…” Obviously, he wasn’t. He was laughing because Adam looked RIDICULOUS. “One last thing…” “Sir, yes Sir?” “…the voice… needs to be a little more “falsetto” I think…” “Like this, Sir?” chimed Adam. Ignatius immediately grinned in amusement, “Exactly Corporal… exactly!” Man in Drag (1532 Hours - Saturday) You have never truly understood the meaning of the word “embarrassment” until you have walked through a crowded shopping mall dressed as a military commander with a man wearing woman’s clothing by your side. However, the French-Canadian figured it would be a relatively small price to pay in the grand scheme of things. After all, it was Adam who would have no body hair for at least three weeks… “Corporal, your work so far has been seamless… your transformation into Lindsay Troy is a complete success.” “Sir, thank you, Sir!” “We are well on our way to understanding the enemy, Corporal, but there is still a lot of work ahead…” “Yes, Sir?” “Yes indeed… to achieve physical parity is not enough. In order to truly understand our enemy you need to live as a woman throughout this week and until the end of Thursday evening…” “Sir, wouldn’t that mean wrestling dressed as a woman, Sir!?” “Yes, indeed Corporal… yes indeed.” *GULP* “Affirmative, Sir.” “In particular, Corporal… to understand Lindsay Troy we must immerse you in a male-dominated environment and see how you fair… and to best imitate the subject you must act overly-cocky, decidedly brash and confident to the borders of nausea… understood?” “Of course, Sir!” “Excellent… now we must return to base so that you might prepare me my dinner and do the household chores.” “Affirmative” Ignatius grinned yet again as he swung open the door to Adam’s Range Rover, absolutely delighted at how everything was panning out. At this rate he wouldn’t be cooking or cleaning up after himself for a whole week and he would have Adam wrestling on the next ReVolution dressed as a woman. An idea from the Vince Russo school of thought had never been so ingenious on so many different levels. Now if he could just come up with a name for “her”… Prep Talk (0615 Hours - Tuesday) A shrill buzzing alerts our under-cover hero to the start of a new day, he gently rolls from bed and takes plum-crushingly-painful knees-together steps through to the bathroom, where he silently curses his chauvinist room-mate and commanding officer for yet another toilet seat left up. After righting the situation, and giving a quick disinfecting clean round the seat for good measure, he takes a seat and relieves himself in a most ladylike fashion. The morning’s make-up routine follows on from a good scrub in the shower (using only the finest creamy-soft soap, of course), although already a master of disguise and infiltration this is no mean feat for your favourite CIA operative – mascara’s a different ballpark to a few smears of boot polish, but combine it with lipstick, blusher, rouge, eyeliner and nail varnish and you’re playing a totally different game. Nonetheless, failure is not an option and this man is no mere mortal – with everything dried, set and smudge-free it’s onto clothing; the delicate, pastel-coloured flower-pattern dress chosen on Saturday should go down well in the light, breezy summer air today. After the stockings are on come the shoes and, though a tight fit, they match the dress perfectly, complimenting the day’s handbag in a most delightful way. “And, just in case, a lady always needs protection…” Indeed a lady does, and so this lady equips himself suitably – the dress is hiked up and a garter tied round the top of the stocking on his right leg, the little piece of protection (should it be needed) was now safe and readily at hand. There’s another shrill buzzing, this time just audible through the wall from Ignatius Lisieux’s adjoining room. One thump later and it’s off, another thump and the French-Canadian has rolled out of bed and begun the stumble through to the bathroom. With a glance up at his shaven headed tag partner and travel buddy struggling to get his ‘breasts’ sitting right Ignatius just shakes his head and continues through to the shower, just missing the slightly-too-late salute. With his chest now level it’s time for the CIA’s finest to get his wig sitting right in front of the hotel room mirror. With a recently re-shaved scalp it’s not too difficult, but it takes time to get everything sitting flawlessly. Later, Ignatius walks out from the shower, hair gelled and generally smelling good, and carries on back through to his room leaving the ex-Scot to decide which necklace goes best with his nail varnish. Under a minute later he strolls back through, fully dressed and ready to go. “Are you not ready yet, ‘Emily’?” Though he had tongue firmly in cheek it doesn’t stop the evil look he’s shot for his troubles, even if it is coupled with another salute. He raises his hands on the defensive accordingly. “Easy… and you can stand down.” “Which one, Sir?” He holds out the two necklaces, one in each hand, the decision obviously too much for him. Ignatius looks briefly from one to the other then simply points at the one dangling from Adam’s right hand. “Really? See, I thought it might look a bit gaudy, Sir.” “Well… go for the other one then.” “But this one does sit better with the neckline on my dress, Sir.” Ignatius simply sighs. Finally the pair leave the hotel, ‘Emily’ insisting on having his arm taken as he was lead down the stairs – much to the stupefaction and amusement of other hotel guests. Dolled up to the nines and holding the arm of an obviously mortified punky escort Emily really was quite a sight. (And he went for the first necklace in the end.) Leaving his ‘partner’ to fine-tune the last of his make-up Ignatius requests a cab from the grinning reception staff – the day’s first task; morning tea in town. Morning Manoeuvres (1003 Hours - Tuesday) The horseless carriage had arrived for his ladyship and he’d been promptly packed off without a companion, Ignatius mumbling something about thinking it better for him to get into the Emily character by himself than to get in the way. Poppycock! An unaccompanied lady venturing into the dizzying heights of the city, why, what if someone were to think of him as a loose woman or a moral-lacking hussy like that awful Troy female? It was just not going to do, and the spineless coward that had so heartlessly abandoned a woman in need would get an earful for it later (with appropriate salutations of course). After huffing and pouting all the way into the town centre, the lady was forced to put up with even more indignant conditions, what with being made to pay for a cab – no gentleman to step in and handle it instead. An earful indeed Commander Lisieux would be getting. But our man is never one to quit, and so he soldiers on in the face of mounting adversity – or at least takes a seat in the outside area of the café on the corner. Sitting in the sun, relaxing, enjoying the cool breeze blowing by and trying not to appear too flattered by the many heads turning, yeah… this is the life. Forget being a regular Joe and having to attract waiters’ attention; the staff here were falling over themselves to serve him – they all just wanted a look at the vision beauty that had waltzed into their midst. Needless to say, Adam was fair mastering this Emily role – even if his worldview was slightly off kilter from everyone else’s; as far as the staff were concerned they just wanted a real, up-close look at the gruff-voiced, fifties-obsessed, cross-dressing freak that had awkwardly toddled in. Enjoying his tea, a nice crumpet and the continued ‘admiration’ of his fellow patrons and passers-by allowed the usually sharp-as-a-hawk attention of the agent-extraordinaire to slip, just momentarily. Add that to the time-warp in our protagonist’s brain that sadly didn’t figure for the massive increase in street-crime in the half-century he was out and things were bound to go wrong. With possibly the least feminine shriek possible Emily leaps to his feet, shocked and dismayed at the cad who had just snatched his purse from the table top and was now making a getaway at rapid pace. After a brief pleading look to some of the supposed “gentlemen” clientele also in the café, and a sudden flashing reminder of the “What Would Overconfident Troy Do?” message his superior officer had issued, it appeared that the lady was going to have to come to her own rescue. “Oh, fuck you all!” And with that less-than-effeminate (although still achingly falsetto) outburst the shoes are kicked off, the low fence hiked over and a new 400m Men’s sprint-in-a-summer-frock record set. The assailant was spotted, targeted, tackled and neutralised – with one knee across the downed villain’s neck and the other pinning down his right arm it was time for this lady to prove she could take care of herself. In one swift motion Emily’s hand was up under his dress and back out again, his ‘protection’ grasped firmly in his right hand. “You picked the wrong girl to fuck with, punk.” A look of disgust crosses the impromptu vigilante’s face as he looks down at his would-be mugger starting to cry. One roll of the eyes later and the knife is resheathed; Emily gets back on his feet and offers his assailant a hand up. Standing face-to-face with him was, well… a challenge: the kid was only about 5’5 and was lucky if he was a day over 14, much neck craning was in order for direct eye contact. Slight guilt for the knife incident started to creep into the conscience of our ladyboy hero. “Umm, sorry about the knife…” The kid has moved from all-out bawling to merely sniffing dejectedly now. Reaching into his reclaimed bag Emily takes a 20 from his purse and hands it over. “Now piss off and don’t spend it on crack.” He winks and does the clasp on his bag up as the kid runs off; turning round a few yards away to shout back; “Thanks, lady!” Emily froze on the spot, his disillusionment from the café earlier washing away – the kid had actually though he was a woman. In a daze, he wanders back to the café, absent-minded and ignorant of the looks he’s attracting from passers-by. After prising himself back into his shoes at the café he took a moment to stop and think things through: if he could pass as a real woman to the kid in their brief meeting, could he get away with it in a more prolonged encounter? …Only one way to find out. Radio Communication (1154 Hours - Tuesday) Ignatius Lisieux’s phone started ringing as he flipped through the CDs in the Vinyl Frontier record store a few blocks from the hotel the two were staying in. He takes it out his back pocket and flips it open. “Hello?... Ada-, uh Emily. Yes, greetings Corporal… I’m just browsing some important documents.” He moves round the corner to the next rack of the ‘punk’ section. “Definitely – go for it… Tonight? No current re-con plans, why?… You want me to take you out for dinner?!” He attracts the attention of a few fellow patrons with this outburst, so resumes his perusal as quietly as possible, continuing to talk into the phone in a harsh whisper. “I thought you… No, listen… I know this exercise is designed to make us more of a team, but…” Gloomily his shoulders drop as he’s reminded of his idea of the ‘team dynamic’ that they need to master. “Fine… Right, okay – see you at eight, sorry – twenty-hundred hours… You’re dismissed.” He flicks the phone closed and exhales, long and slow. He was gonna treat himself to a lot of CDs to make up for the ‘fun’ this evening was going to be. ”Practical Makes Perfect” (1309 Hours - Tuesday) Back in the café Emily flip his phone closed, pops it in his bag and sets off to purchase a more ‘independent women’ outfit for Thursday night’s B&P encounter – the current garb being a bit too loose for grappling. After a few minutes walk in the city centre he picks a shop, takes a few deeps breaths and walks in. He’s immediately greeted by a smiling, if slightly apprehensive, sales assistant – Claire, according to the nametag. “Afternoon, ma’am – can I help?” “Yep; I’m looking for something sporty to keep me strapped down and with a little, umm… room, in the…” He waves his hands vaguely over the crotch area of his dress (the brashness failing him slightly here), eliciting a slightly worried look from the poor girl serving him. “I prefer not to feel too… restricted in my physical activity.” “Certainly, would you like a look at our plus-sized collection?” Twenty minutes later, a few outfits tried and tested and bingo – that’s ringwear for Thursday sorted. A couple more “ma’am” and “miss” comments had ‘the Agent formerly known as Adam’ feeling rather chuffed and a bit more confident in his ‘new skin’. He’d fooled a woman; that had to be the holy grail of cross-dressing. With a spring in his step and a grin on his face Emily left the shop and had a brainwave for tonight’s engagement… An Excellent Choice (2003 Hours - Tuesday) “Reservation in the name of Lisieux, for eight o’clock, there’s a, uhh… a ‘lady’ to follow.” A rather well scrubbed-up Ignatius is lead to a table by the waiter, who presents the wine list before melting away into the general restaurant hubbub. Taking a stab at the nicest sounding name and hoping for the best was Lisieux’s usual tactic for wine, but just as he was about to call a waiter across to take an order the list is snatched form his hand and a familiar but increasingly unrecognisable face sits down opposite him in a gown that would look stunning on a slightly more petite frame, but still manages to come off looking surprisingly good on the ‘plus-sized’ model wearing it. Ignatius looks across the table, stunned, at his frighteningly well-disguised tag partner. “Adam?!” “I don’t know who you’re talking about, Sir.” The ‘Sir’ was added rather quietly at the end, discretion being needed in public places for such ‘hush-hush’ assignments as this one. Ignatius just looks on speechless as a gloved hand is raised to catch a waiter’s attention. One duly appears, notepad in hand. “We’ll have the Chateau Duhart-Milon Rothschild 1981 Pauillac, please.” “Excellent choice, Madame.” And so away he scuttles, leaving Emily to give questioning glance to her date for the evening. “Are you going to sit slack-jawed all night, or are you going to give the lady some conversation, Sir?” Slowly Ignatius resumes his composure, still shocked that his CIA-fixated partner has managed to convincingly pull of his womanly allures. “Of course, how silly of me… umm…” “Can I be so bold as to suggest we drop military formalism for the course of the evening, so as not to draw unwanted attention and to blend more readily in, Sir?” “Quick thinking, you can stand down.” Another waiter walks past and his attention is grabbed with another quick waved of a gloved hand. “We’re ready to order; for starters could we have the German meats mixed platter, please, then for mains the gentleman will have the Veal Forestiere in the mushroom sauce with artichoke hearts and I shall have the Calves Liver with lemon thyme, thank you.” “Of course, it’ll be with you as soon as possible.” With a smile of thanks Emily dismissed the waiter and returned the pressing matter at hand. “Now… tactics…”
