One if by Two, you mean, Me and You.

June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/??/2005 One if by Two, you mean Me and You, improper sentence structure. You and I, right? The commotion caused in ones mind from confusion can properly be summed up with three simple words. No Fucking Clue. When one has no fucking clue, it’s hard for them to really produce anything specific, or positive, reflectively speaking, in their life. When one can’t remember what they’ve done not but 10 minutes before hand, or even recall what they had for lunch that day, it tends to interfere with life in general, on the grander scale. Think of not remembering your name, or waking up in the morning having no idea where you are, or even worse, who you are sleeping next too. Not that college memory of waking up next to some random girl, but really having no idea on a daily basis, when you wake up, even who the hell you are until you look in the mirror. And even then not all is explained. For the face staring back at you sometimes seems more foreign than others, and on occasion, you have absolutely no idea at all the mystery behind the visage staring back at you through the cloudy mirror after your shower. Frightened~ I think any man of normal sense would be most frightened at this thought of absent mindedness, the realization that when you wake up tomorrow, you could find yourself walking blindly into traffic due to the mere fact that you have, at this point, just plainly given up. Frustration~ Shards of glass scatter at your feet as you devour another mirror with your fist. It consumes you like a virus, spreading throughout your usually patient demeanor, turning you into an animal of mistrust and misunderstanding. Mistrust in the sense that you no longer trust yourself, your life, your very existence. It’s moments of frustration and complete and utter misunderstanding that lead to …. Self Destruction~ As you deteriorate slowly in front of your own being, you lie in shame, but aren’t sure why. It’s a feeling that engulfs your every decision, shame. It constantly makes you second guess everything you are doing, will do, have done, Shame. You slowly become less than nothing, but not to the people around you, they don’t see it, only to yourself, as it punctures the very thing that makes you whole as a person, Shame. Until the day that you stand over broken shards of glass, looking at the hundreds of tiny reflection of the pieces of you, and consider your options, or lack there of, Shame. And at this time in space, you are presented with an option. Let shame consumer you, and lift a measly shard of glass and end it all, or try as hard as you can to take a step forward. Realization~ That aforementioned step forward begins with realization. A short lived moment of clarity that allows you the briefest glimpse into your past, a moment that give you a reason to fight, instead of a reason to hide, like so many others.. Instead of allowing yourself to follow the same path of so many before you, you stand and fight for that next step, and as soon as you commit to that fight, you begin taking steps, albeit baby steps, out of your own personal hell, and up to something better. Steps that will eventually be met along the way by………… Trials & Tribulations Falling down into a desperate hole, or sliding along that downward spiral is much easier that simply walking out of it. You’ve spend time digging that hole, many would be proud of such a hole as this, yet you’ve chosen otherwise, you’ve chosen not to be proud of the hole that has seemingly dug itself underneath you. You’ve chosen to make a difference. Yet, the road before you seems just as cloudy as the road you’ve walked up to this point. Never being in a situation similar to this, you have no idea if what you are doing is correct, or if it is all in vein. Concussion~ The word that rings over and over in between you ears, concussion. The chair shot taken two weeks ago will have a lasting effect as long as you continue to submit your body, especially your head, to continual stress each and every week. The Doctors, what do they really know? Just because a man has on a white coat with a stethoscope around his neck, for some reason he is to be worshipped like a god, taken at his word that HE knows what’s best for you! Why is it that you cannot remember simple things? This is a question asked only to prove to you that you need to listen, but it doesn’t supply an answer, nothing is an answer in this book of constant, never ending questioning, which brings me to ………… Set Back~ Suddenly you wake up, standing in front of an arena in Anaheim, California, with no real idea what you are doing there, aside from that fact that you know you have a job there. You struggle mightily with the loss of vision from an hour ago, let alone a week ago. Long term information tends to stick in your head like gum to your shoe. You know you work for PRIME, you know you are a wrestler, and you know you have a purpose here, but short term memories from the past two weeks flightily soar in and out, leaving you with only three words that keep resurfacing. Diablos Chair-shot REVENGE~! And so it goes, with nothing else standing between you and your destiny, not that you will remember it, you take that next step forward, the next step to something different, something that you hopefully will remember. And then, out of the side of your eyes you see it……………….. Dunkin Doughnuts And suddenly you remember something remarkable, something so important it will change the course of you very situation……….. You remember that, that….. You’re fucking starving! And you’re life, for yet another moment, is put on hold.
