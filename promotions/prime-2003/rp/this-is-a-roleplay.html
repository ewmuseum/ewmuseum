Date: When it was done. Yesh yesh, ya'll. It's dat hotness, The V.O. Izzle, Vangizzy Oximus. The hotness is ready once again for another tag team contest. This time, my partner and I are teaming up with Team Zambo, Civil Disobedience, and... dammit, I know this one. Flying Vergomovs! That's it! Anyway, we're up on that shizzizzle while P.W.A., Los Diablos, Covenant, and those Killer Pirates are on our nutsack. How is this fair? Well, to put it another way: how is this a challenge? P.W.A. -- Nerezza's claim to fame is what, temporarily putting Hessian on the shelf? As though that meant anything when he would turn right around and proceed to win absolutely NO titles in RPW? He was a multiple time PCW tag champion, but when Playaz, Inc. is like your only competition, it's going to be rather hard not to win those things a lot of the time. Nerezza was like, what, 6'1' the whole time? It was like, 'Yo, I'm small, right? Not totally small. Still around the size of Scott Steiner when he wasn't juiced like the biggest freaking orange in existence. And then, when he got angry, he'd develop green skin, grow much larger, and run rampant as Nashulksto Nerezza. Gaz, meanwhile, would go on to break up the team, and being the more charismatic one, went farther than his more vicious partner ever could. Ha! The Covenant -- Weren't they like ready to skip out on the whole Cthulu thing and go back to being regular wrestlers? I distinctly remember Gabriel talking about wanting to form a pure-wrestling tag team with the guy because he was considering cutting out the 40-year-old dead weight known as Violence Jack. Ethan Knight... is he that much of a heat vacuum that a guy who does the kinds of things that he does still needs to be aligned in part of a larger unit in order to get any attention? Damn... I think the last time I was that uncharismatic was back in high school. Los Diablos -- Moving on. Killer Pirates -- Natch. We can't lose. I'm double-timing the training with Gabriel so we get out moves down right, and I'm trying to build up a greater resistance to neck drops. I can't fall to a freaking piledriver. Other guys take that in their sleep. Damn this. Of course, Zameer landing on me didn't help anything. Can't take much of that no matter who you are. And that's what I'm banking on. All that we have to do is let Azala frickin' fall his fat ass onto our opponents and run interference on the pinfall. And that ends another monologue by me. Yeah, this sucks. Yeah, it's short. But hey, when you're assured of a win, speaking much really isn't a worry. My new album drops next Thursday. Cop that ish! I'm out! ~V~
