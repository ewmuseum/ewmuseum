Jumping Through Hoops

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/31/05 The wheels of time are slowly turning. As the wave of talent splashes over the barriers of PRIME’s breakwater wall, the turbine begins spinning, crushing old faces against the millstone. Careers are ground into dust, and at their expense, new grain is produced. Baked in the oven of the locker room, and finally fed to the fans at ReVolution, the new bread -the new breed- of PRIME superstar has arrived. The mill of PRIME is churning, producing the goods left and right. Rumor has it that even more talent will join PRIME’s already talent-soaked roster at Culture Shock. A sponge, however, can only hold so much moisture before it is totally saturated. At this point, displacement takes place. So with the populace begs for more, can we continue to feed them our finest? As we saw on ReVolution with Emilio Rage and Ozric Mortimer fed to the dogs of new? Or will the old-schoolers put their collective foot down, fighting off both the Dark Age and the new enemies from within? An exemplary situation. Let’s boldly admit it. Vampir Nosferatu, man who’s had less than half a year in PRIME but has left a lifetime of notably insidious achievements. This is the man who ripped Tony Rolo in half last week on the top of PRIME’s very own program. He clearly dominated the fan-favorite in a match where many believed Rolo would win, hot off a momentous victory over Boda. The Dark Age, it seems, must be celebrating this. While they fume under their dark hoods of destruction and terror, one can’t help but get the feeling that while they publicly cry for justice -“Vamp deserves a title shot!”- that behind the scenes they are complacent. Waiting. Cold and calculating. Ready to strike when the time is right. As much as Emilio Rage, Ozric Mortimer, Killean Sirrajin, and Boda have bruised and battered them, the Dark Age continues to reign supreme: Vampir beat Boda at The Great American Nightmare. Tony Rolo beat Boda several weeks ago. And in a little over a month, Vampir has beaten Tony Rolo...twice. Any man or woman adherent to the laws of logic would surely draw the conclusion that he is the most dominant single force in the company. So although the Dark Age has of late sustained losses, it continues to thrive on, growing new limbs left and right. But can hatred sustain itself? Emilio Rage is banking on the tough bet that it cannot. For while Nosferatu may seem almost unstoppable as far as being a force for evil is concerned, it stands to reason that in the ring he is very, very beatable. Looking at the upcoming ten-man tag extravaganza on ReVolution, note that four of the ten involved were present at a certain match at the onset of ReVolution 52...where Ozric Mortimer and Emilio Rage beat both Furher and Nosferatu. It was fair, it was square, and it was in the middle of the ring. One. Two. Three. And for the first time since his arrival, Nosferatu was defeated. Soundly. As rumors swarm about the locker room, with the winds of discontent ripping at their already tired faces, the roster of PRIME must not fall to its knees. They must down bow down before the pressure of the still emerging Dark Age. Or, worse yet, fall under the false impression that they are getting weaker. Indeed, the reverse is true. Even if it is so, they must not recognize, must not legitimize, the power that the Dark Age holds over the masses. For two weeks in a row, Mortimer and Rage have found themselves on the winning end of the stick. Twice has Dani Furher’s blood stained the mat, and twice as he looked up at the bright arena lights as two sets of big, determined boots pummeled his unsavory carcass into the mat. After all that, was does the Dark Age do...but get stronger. With the seeming solidification of The Enlightenment, as seen last week on ReVolution, Vampir may make his personal gains. He may pick up his personal victories along his lengthy and tedious road, even at the expense of Shadow and Furher. But that dwindling road of personal glory leads nowhere. Without the backing of a strong, steady group, not even the darkness in his heart is glutenous enough to bind the weak particles of the one-time dominant institution of the Dark Age together...if The Enlightenment can persist. Just as Toshiaki Motoki fell by the wayside, so it shall be for the Dark Age. As far as Emilio is concerned, whereas ReVolution 50 was an event where he had to keep his eyes in the back of his head, constantly under the impression that his very being was in danger, Culture Shock will be a whole different story. It’s an entirely different environment requiring an entirely different realm of thought. Survival, some say, is the goal of the Dual Halo. Nay. Rage believes that winning, sweet victory is simply the end-all. For a victory entails the very luxurious reward of being able to demand anything you wish. Anything...you...wish. Emilio’s one wish at the moment of course would be to reverse the humiliating defeat at the hands of Los Diablos on ReVolution. Although the two Mexican wrestlers, led by the focused mind of Morales, are two known, well-respected competitors, the loss still hurts. It stings this very moment. Regardless of the fact that they cheated, that they used illegal maneuvers, that they used foreign objects, and that they simply outnumbered the duo of Rage and Mortimer, three-to-two, a win is a win. And a loss is a loss. There is no exceptions, no looking back. Make no excuses about it, Emilio is not happy about his recent experience at ReVolution. The night may have ended with a collective gain by The Enlightenment, but only at his own personal cost. And you can’t help but think that plays a big role in the scheme of things, the idea that while Vamp has been dominating all creatures between the four lines of ropes that we call the squared-circle, Emilio Rage has slipped down a notch or two. There is nothing more to it. It remains in plain sight: Going into Culture Shock, Emilio Rage has something to prove. He believes from the bottom of his heart that not only will he soon walk around the lockers of PRIME with the PTC Global title proudly displayed around his waist, but that he will come out of Culture Shock a winner; a living legend. For no man can defeat twenty-nine of the other greatest in the entire sport and not receive a slap on the back from the hand of History. Whoever wins this event, mark my words, will by consent, or by force, control the destiny of this promotion... So Before the biggest night of his career, before the looming figure of the massive metal structure called the Dual Halo, there is Furher and there is Vampir: Furher, it seems, shall receive his third beating in a row. As of late he’s served as the rag-doll of The Enlightenment. As for the master, Vampir has been felled before and he can be felled again. On ReVolution, Emilio looks to repeat history. Stake in hand. Emilio took his time making his way into the store. He dragged his feet, complained of chest pains, and even, on several occasions, cited his upcoming match on ReVolution as an excuse for added “training.” But of course he needed no such training. For five long months he has trained. He has struggled, he has worked, and he has made peace with himself again, pulling out of manic depression. Just last month, however, after the events of ReVolution 50 kicked his blinded self in the teeth, did he finally make the Pact though. That promise? To rid PRIME of the Dark Age. The upcoming tan-man tag team spectacular, followed by Culture Shock, will provide the perfect forum to do so. And do so in style, under the heavy lights, and watched by millions of hopeful eyes around the globe. Nonetheless, as his young, witty guide talked him through decades of fashion evolution on what could be described none other than a pilgrimage of garments, a crusade of clothing, Emilio yawned, looking down at his watch. They had been peeling down the rows of clothing for hours now, anxiously trying to find the perfect “post-victory” suit if Emilio did in fact bring the PTC Global Gold back to PRIME. A first ever in the history of the promotion. But the reality of the situation was much more melancholy in nature to Mr. Rage. He was there, accompanied by the fabulous Nancy, to pick up the suit Mr. Hin See had sent to Emilio in anticipation of the PTC Global Dinner that he was expected to attend later on in the week. It was early Saturday afternoon, twenty-four hours away from the kickoff part to his much anticipated PTC debut. At the request of Hin See, Blaine Blaire had hired this fashion expert to escort Emilio in his quest to find proper attire for his post-PTC dinner...what headquarters was hoping to be known as victory party, after what they knew Emilio would give as stellar performance. Later in the night he’d have a press conference, followed by a private party at the request of a mysterious, but no doubt important, PTC big-shot... Emilio couldn’t wait. ...To leave. “So,” asked Emilio, with a twinkle of playfulness in his eye as they strolled down the aisles, “what do you think about this?” He held up a crotchless pair of satin underwear over his jeans, thrusting his hips. The young woman’s face flashed red. Shaking her head no, she finally let out a sigh. “What? If Hernandez can do it...” began Emilio, laughing. “First off, you’re not Hernandez,” said Nancy “Fancy,” as Rage dubbed her, ripping the item away from him. “Secondly, it doesn’t match your bandana...” Emilio frowned. “Which,” she started, although he knew what was coming next, “will certainly have to go.” “Over my dead body,” Emilio whispered, turning to walk away. “What was that?” “Nothin’.” Emilio said. He let out a deep, wide grin, showing off his dimples as they made their way down the women’s aisle. Although Emilio wasn’t having a particular blast, at least it was keeping his mind occupied. With all the fray and discontent in PRIME, maybe the upcoming Global Contender’s Match would do his burnt out soul some good. Or, followed by a loss, maybe not... “Besides,” he cursed under his breath as they passed by a selection of festive masks, reminding him of Los Diablos, “no matter what happens, it can’t be as bad as losing to two nobodies...” The woman grabbed Rage by his shoulders, pulling him to a sudden stop. She stopped and pointed down a hallway, directing Emilio’s gaze toward the end. “Huh?” he asked, unsure if she was being serious: A glass shelf containing displays, some of them reserved suits, sat humbly next to a pair of dressing rooms. Inside the glass cases were clothes of all variaous genres, ranging from festive to formal...and from gaudy to atrocious. The particular dank piece of clothing Emilio couldn’t believe Linda was now directing his abused eyes on, were particularly crafty in the art of scuz. “You can’t be serious!” Emilio pleaded, furrowing his thick brows. But Nancy simply laughed, shaking her head. “Of course not, hone!” she said as Emilio let out a sigh of relief. “It’s actually that one...” Emilio turned to view the one behind the fore-mentioned suit...a one much, to his utter shock, much uglier. “Well...” asked Linda with a happy smile on her beaming face, “do you like it?” Emilio bit his lip. He turned to Linda and asked, staring down at the fabric seemingly made of bundled shards of what Satan must use as toilet paper....when he’s sick...“Honey?” “Yes?...” “Have you ever received a Pentafix on behalf of the seven-foot pile of living human waste named Vampir?” But the woman simply store back, confused. “Heh...Thought so...” Emilio snapped his neck to the side before reaching up to scratch his chin in careful consideration. “Yeah,” he began, looking over at the suit, “I was just thinking how, actually, it’s not that bad...” “Oh, you! That’s enough,” demanded Linda, tapping her feet in frustration. She wore a pair of long brown pantyhose down to her perfectly shined high-heel sandals. From the tip of her long dark hair, to the flawless red finish on her tow-nails, she was certainly one of the more professional individuals to be contracted by this company. She gave Emilio an angered grin. “Now I realize you’re not exactly a huge fan of this...particular...er,” she grit her teeth, looking for the proper words, “style of clothing....but nonetheless,” she added, nodding her head in false confidence, “there you have it.” “Oh, if I wear that thing, I’ll have ‘it’ alright...” “Oh, stop acting so childish. It is in the fine tradition of Hin See and his business associates...If it’s good enough for those who give you your paycheck, it’s good enough for you...I...uh...suppose,” she said as her voice trailed off, getting a good look at the piece herself now. “Quite logical, I suppose,” Emilio started, sarcastically, “but after I realized, inspired by new evidence, that my pay check comes from gigantic tropical birds...” for proof, he gestured back toward the suit, “I now feel as if I need to rethink my life.” Still, Linda persisted, only rolling her eyes. “Well let’s just try this on for size, shall we?” “We?” asked Emilio, “Are you sure? I mean, there’s room for at least three, I’m sure...” Emilio couldn’t help but give his own work a slight chuckle as he looked down at the ceremonial ancient Japanese gown. Together, they took another look down at the brightly colored cloth. Emilio scoffed. “Ki-MOAN-NO!, huh? I can see why she would...” “Shut up,” taunted Nancy, pulling out a magnetic key. She inserted the pin into an electronic reader, causing the clamps on the display to snap open. At this point, Emilio realized that he had no say in the matter. Apprehensively, he reached in and brushed his hand along the edge of the flower-embroidered cloth. “Well, it’s what it’s purpose serves, as toiletry, several hours after dinner that counts, I suppose.” “Oh, you mean it counts when we take pictures of you dressed in it, a perfect front cover of Hin See’s yearly Christmas cards to every member of the locker? Oh yes, what an excellent idea!” said Nancy, gleefully. A chill went down Emilio’s back and the hair stood up on his neck. Nancy smiled. The traditional Japanese kimono ironically had something in common with the ideology of Samurai who once sported it: Ritualistic suicide when dishonored souls could no longer continue to live. Sepuku. “Please...somebody...kill me,” it could be seen crying out, the hippy-ish golden flowers starting to whither before Emilio’s very eyes. He couldn’t help but grin at such a sight. But Hin See was Hin See. And Hin See the boss. Emilio noticed, come to think of it, over the last few weeks, the elaborate vests of Mr. Bojangles, the feline co-owner of PRIME. And whatever the cat gets, the roster eventually gets as well...Good for the goose, good for the gander... But how comes, Emilio thought, it’s only the bad aspects that get filtered down? If only Emilio had a bed adorned with golden pillows...or rose-scented kitty litter for his bathroom. Mmm... In the end, he simply couldn’t offend the boss. Publicity aside, it came down to making friends with “The Man.” Yeah, protest him when you have to...and as punk rockers do, fuck him when you can...but above all else, when the need arises, kiss his ass. Emilio cringed. The ancient outfit, this particular pattern worn by the warrior class in See’s family, would have to suffice. Besides, would it really look that shocking when the PTC Global Title Division crew ended up at arena: Emilio in a dress, but Evers with a puke-stained shirt, Somers with a title belt wearing a wig around his insidious waste?... Well, Yes! And doubly so. But at least he wouldn’t be the only one with red cheeks that night. Although Emilio gets the feeling the others aren’t as...well, conscious of their respective dispositions...Ahem. Somers is mucho loco... Emilio sighed, taking the old cloth. He made his way into the changing room where Nancy waited outside. Every once in a while, the fashion expert would give encouragement. “Wow, it’s kind of scary in here...all the mirrors simultaneously shattering, and all....” called Emilio from the safety of the room. “You just suck it up like a big boy and come out to get your picture taken with Santa, okay?” said Nancy, tauntingly. “Heh...” Emilio began to grumble, being out-done by a, like, Valley Girl and, like, stuff! As hard as it was for Emilio to dress himself in such attire, it was just as much work having to where the proverbial dunce hat because of his recent loss to Los Diablos. I mean, for a Hispanic, let alone an in-ring veteran, to be beaten by a rookie duo called, quite simply, “The Devils,” was too much a burden to bare. Granted, he teamed with a psychotic, drug-abusing clown/schizophrenic. But regardless, there could be no ex...well, okay. Teaming with a psychotic, drug-abusing clown/schizophrenic is ample material to defend his pride with. However, on that night, the cliche did of course prove to be true. Oz and Emilio got the hell kicked out of them at the hands of, well, devils... For a person of Rage’s integrity, in all illicit seriousness, the simple “they cheated” spin would not work. To Emilio, a winner is winner and a loser is a loser, plain and simple. If a ten-pound steel chair and a bit of double-teaming can knock you down for the count, quite simply, you probably should stay down. However, as luck would have it, it was probably good for Emilio to get out of the tag scene anyway...Although his upcoming match requires the same skills. Rage unhappily de-robed himself and slowly pulled on the kimono. He started to shake his head in disbelief. As annoying as this jumping through hoops gesture to please the boss was, his upcoming match was of much more dire consequence... And no, we’re not talking his Global Title Contender’s Match. That one’s a synch: Have a nice little four-way dance, put on a good show, get the one...two...three....and voila! You’re done. But this Wednesday on ReVolution would be a little different... While ten men will walk down to that ring, only five men will leave, and of those, only one will get the cover. This live, in front of millions of fans around the world, a predicate to the real deal at Culture Shock. Remember last time Vampir and Emilio were locked into the playground together, Emilio came away the “W.” Ozric Mortimer may have been the one who got the pin, but Emilio was like a relentless battering ram when he took the ring that night, driving back the once-dubbed unstoppable ambitions of Vampir, both in and out of the ring... Emilio finished putting on the robe and stepped back to view himself in the mirror. “Great,” he mumbled to himself, turning sideways to get an ever more entertaining view of his pitiful self, “Vamp should be able to get some great propaganda pics out of this little fiasco.” Indeed, as of late the Dark Age has really taken it to the people. Not in a popularity sense, but as far as getting the message out is concerned. And what a bad message...hate, self-gratification, the destruction of the human (and evidently clown) race... Of course they don’t expect to win over the hearts and mind of the masses who have seen them time and time again smash the hopes and ambitions of some of their favorite faces...Boda, Rage, Larkin...the list goes on an on. But they are, however, making their dominance known...even when they’re running out of steam! The most frustrating part, realize, on the part of Emilio, is the unwillingness of the Dark Age to admit defeat when death so blatantly knocks on their collective door. It seems that whenever The Enlightenment makes gains, taking the fight to the Dark Age, Vampir sees the reciprocal: Every failed opportunity to take down a member of the PRIME roster, turned into the beating of one of its own, the Dark Age truly sees it as another spark to add to their growing fire, another bold act for which they can later extract revenge. Remarkable. Talk about mind control! When you control the discussion and set the tone of the conversation, you really have the power. And when you have the power, the world is at the mercy of your hands. Bottom line. Emilio realizes this, and certainly wishes to address it. This fascinating hydra quality of the Dark Age -especially the resilient, thick-headed, and apparently un-killable Dani Furher- is not one to be taken lightly. As Emilio leads The Enlightenment on the proper path down to Culture Shock, it’s important that the other members recognize this distinction as well. Emilio had really gotten the feeling as of late that not everyone is on the same page. Sure, Boda and Ozric shook each other’s hands the other night, and sure they worked fairly effectively as a unit during the main event, preventing the Dark Age from interfering in the main event (which, come to find out, wouldn’t make a difference). But all that simply wasn’t enough. With Killean and Boda about to clash at Culture Shock for the most prized possession in the game, where was the unity of The Enlightenment? Surely Rage has always believed Boda was the most deserving of any on the roster for a shot at the Universal Title. But Killean, if he does in fact win...will it be Rage’s moral concern -nay, duty! to support him? It has been well documented that he can defeat Black Angel, at least in singles competition... Or should he stick by Boda, a true friend? As these thoughts and others raced through Emilio’s head, he shook out of it after a soft knock on his dressing room door. “Are you okay in there?” Nancy called out in a soft, concerned voice. Emilio took a good second to respond, buttoning up the remaining buttons on his undershirt shirt. “Yeah, I’m fine...” he called back. A moment later, Emilio stepped out of the dressing room. He blinked his eyes thrice at the loud flash of the camera lense. Nancy stepped back, giggling... “I’ve never met a paparazzi with a boob-job,” said Emilio, smiling. Linda’s nostrils flared as her own smile turned into a frown. Rage burst into hysterics. “You’ll think it’s funny when I sell these pics to the Globe,”warned Nancy, waving the camera in front of Emilio. Emilio lurched forward to snatch it, but Linda pulled back in the nick of time. “Fine. But can we please get this over with...I have a press conference in four hours and would prefer to split my remaining portions of limited dignity to the media.”said Emilio in a sardonic tone. Linda bowed her head, motioning for Emilio to follow her toward the front of the store. As embarrassed as Emilio felt as the burn of watching eyes began to penetrate his red cheeks, he also felt a slight lapse of relief. It was all going to be over soon...the Global Title match, ReVolution, Culture Shock...And maybe by this time next month Emilio would be sipping whine on the beach of some far away island, under the golden sun... ...While running from the agents, alerted the moment after the cameras picked him up when he stepped off the plane.... Emilio shook his head. Yes, he knew it. No matter what happens, win, lose, draw, over the course of the following weeks and months, Emilio would never -will never- live a perceivably “normal” life. At this point in fact, if its not the life-threatening, bone crunching, or potential poisonous matters which worry him...it’s the mundane. He’s been an adventurer for thirty-some-odd cycles of earth’s seasons, and probably -hopefully- many, many moor. For in between the short naps, light meals, quick showers and few walks...there was plenty of action. While few people claim to have the world in the palm of their hand, other than himself, none could brag of the world holding you in its palm. For every week it seemed he was escaping death. Granted, sometimes the world squeezed down too hard, sucking the life out of Emilio’s strenuous bones. But the majority of the time it was a fun, albeit bumpy, ride. And he wouldn’t trade it for anything. Emilio generously held the door open for the woman who, he supposed, was worthy of taking his next PRIME pay check, purchasing a penis, turning gay, and becoming a regular on “Queer Eye.” But as they walked out into the streets, he never mentioned this to her. Instead, he forced a phony smile as she walked by. “Thanks,” she whispered. Emilio followed her out into the cold Boston winter. “Man, I’m freezing,” he began with chattering teeth. “The wind blows through this damn thing like I’m wearing nothing, blowing cold air on my bal...uh...back.” he said of the kimono. “Yes, well the studio is just down the street,” said Nancy, adding, “I’m not exactly dressed for Antarctica either, so it’d be excellent if you’d hurry up.” “But...” Emilio began, racing to catch up with her... He shrugged, looking up at the foggy sky above. “Come on,” Nancy called back as the lethargic Alias Champ dwindled behind.”We’ve got to...Oh.” her voice trailed off as Rage finally caught up. Emilio rose an inquisitive brow. “What’s up?” he asked, concerned. “Oh, just...uh...” and that’s when Emilio saw it. “Well, well, well...” said Rage as he bent over to pick up a fallen copy of the New York Times, tucked away beneath Nancy’s foot. He gently shoved her aside, retrieving the paper. “What’s this?” he said, unfolding the battered paper to read the headlines out loud... “ ‘Rome: 3,000 Perish in Wake of Mysterious Virus’... ” he said in a voice that grew weaker upon the reading of each word....finally trailing off to nothingness. As Nancy backed away with a slight frown on her face, Emilio clenched his fists in tight balls. Emilio stopped to customarily snap his neck, a measure most undertaken in moments of distress. His throat felt dry. The PRIMEal Rage Champion looked down at the paper again and continued reading to himself... “Public officials and medical experts baffled. As the virus sweeps across Europe, US intelligence launches official investigation. But while terrorist cells of unknown origins are cited as responsible, religious groups around the world claim it an act of ‘Satan,’ declaring ‘The End of Days.’ The seriousness of the situation, not lost on the European Union, has even brought up dialogue on possible acts of ‘isolation’ in a last-minute attempt to contain the mysterious virus... In unrelated news, recent pools indicate increasing levels of violence as worldwide phenomena, as well as the recent rates of infant mortality...” Emilio crunched up the paper into a ball and tossed it aside. As the Alias Champion strolled down the street with a pale look on his face, his wary eyes contorted in a blank flare of rage. The snow began to fall softly around him as the sky began to darkened. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ Later on that night, Emilio set back in the comforts of his limousine, listening intently to the buzz of the Massachusetts highway. After a series of unfortunate events had just shaken his life, there was nothing to do but ponder the uncertainties of his tumultuous future. Earlier on the evening, he found himself in a deadly game of cat-and-mouse between himself and the Central Intelligence Agency. Apparently, something in his past had been distorted, and the feds were hot on his trail. After being saved by the hand of vigilant cop, Emilio had quite a piece to chew on in his already choking mind. Although the earlier press conference had gone okay -and thank God, Hin See insisted on him wearing the suite only after he has achieved PTC Gold- the smoothness of the lake, as you may well know, is inclined to bow down at the cast of one simple stone, causing ripples throughout the calm waters. This is the case in Emilio Rage’s life. Yes, he had a rough idea of what the feds wanted. And no, they were not the same reasons that he had given to the trustworthy officer Starr. But something much, much more sinister. Could it be, he thought to himself as the acid seemed to rip at his heart after it had sunk into his chest, that the feds knew there was some connection between the viral outbreaks and the Dark Age, and therefore, himself? Who knew... It was up to him now, he realized, after all this time. Boda or not, with or without the help of his new friend Ozric Mortimer, the complete dismantling of Vampir Nosferatu, and his fiendish friends, the Dark Age, was his predestination...or the fate of humanity, inevitable. Boda raised the hammer at The Great American Nightmare. At ReVolution 52, Mortimer planted the stake. Now it was time for Emilio to Strike.
