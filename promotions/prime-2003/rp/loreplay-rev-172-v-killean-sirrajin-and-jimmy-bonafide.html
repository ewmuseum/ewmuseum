Date: BORN ON SNIPER ISLAND, 100 SHOTS, 100 HITS! The lore of Mega Job is just as complicated, full of errors and transgressions, and unreliable as their history. In the interest of getting something up before the deadline, I've quickly compiled a list of things related to Mega Job that really shouldn't ever have to be explained and yet will be explained for no reason other than boredom. Nerva | A woman fighter formerly of the Asylum. She was the only female Asylum Champion in the organization's long and bloody history. During Nerva's run at the top of the Asylum, under her feminist "Movement" stable, she assaulted El Janito in a way that could only be described as vile and heinous and other Jim Rossisms. For a long time, El Janito would shriek in horror and jump into the nearest person's arms (or straight into the ceiling at times) upon the very mention of her name. Only years of therapy have calmed him to the point that the mention of the woman's name doesn't affect him any more. Well, that and if it was brought up today, nobody would get the joke. Apples | El Janito is deathly afraid of apples. We don't know why. Beefvile: City of the Annoyed, USA | Mega Job's hometown. It might be fictional, it might not be. People other than Mega Job have visited this city, ranging from the Codemaster to Steve Knox. Yet they can never remember how they got there or how they left. It's a magical place. Obviously, though, it's not powered by faeries, because then Chandler Tsonda would lose about half of his might. The Jobmobile | Mega Job's van/utter atrocity on the human eyes. It's a van with a crazy, ugly paint job that was obviously done by Beef while on acid. The Jobmobile is actually the second incarnation of the vehicle, after Beef drove the original off of a cliff and completely totalled it. Yet, somehow, still surviving. Beef doesn't have a driver's license, so the van is technically El Janito's. Villam Ender | Crazy motherbastard who punished El Janito for acting as a terrible human being with no common sense (it's no different from what El Janito is normally, in other words) by spanking him in the ass a couple of hundred times with a steel chair. Janito couldn't sit down properly for a while after the incident. It is commonly joked by Beef that Janito lost his anal virginity to Villam Ender and his chair. Janito's British Citizenship | During an utterly bizarre period of time when El Janito was actually the Asylum's United Kingdom champion, El Janito turned completely stereotypically British, which actually made him even MORE unbearable to be around, and during this time, he actually registered for British citizenship. Beef and Steve HATE El Janito's British phases to death and since it is randomly triggered after every blow to Janito's head, they actually encourage people to hit Janito again if he starts acting even remotely British. Also known for wearing an absurdly gaudy suit with the colors of the Union Jack on it. Speaking of Union Jack, Janito is at least twenty times more obnoxious than he is during this phase. You Have The Right To Remain Bad | A movie series that Mega Job produced starring El Janito as the good cop and Craig Miles as the bad cop, and yet they somehow fight aliens from the planet of Biaakazatron while seeking out the mystical "Light of Biaakazatron". Apart from Janito and Miles, Mega Job also cast Tim Walden, Jane Matheson, and both members of the Emo Kids in random roles. They have produced four sequels, all of them worse than the last. Perhaps to the horror of everyone else, Mega Job is attempting to garner support for a remake. Craig Miles | Previous star of Mega Job's movies and long-time associate of Steve. Steve describes him as being the coolest guy on Earth not named Chuck Norris. Beef and El Janito fear him, and the primary reason why they want to remake the very first movie they ever shot is because they want it to have "104% less Craig Miles". Really, I could go on. But this is just a small taste of that which is Mega Job. Now here's some water to get that taste out of your mouth.
