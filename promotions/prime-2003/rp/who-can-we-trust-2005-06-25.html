Who can we trust?

25 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/25/05 :::: When you are the champions, the Kings of the mountain, the bar that everything is set at then you become the target. Suddenly everyone wants a piece of you, everyone wants to take you down, and now proving that you were nothing to begin with becomes the order of the day. Things like this aren't new and they happen every other week in the wrestling business. One day you are the icon that everyone wants to look up to and strive to be better than until the following week rears it's ugly head around and the order of the day becomes bringing something bigger and better than you along. For Los Diablos their whole PRIME careers have been a struggle to gain the respect they feel is owed to them. They have fought every single team or challenge that has been put before them and some how they have managed to take the titles and make them their own. Los Diablos are finally the crown jewel of the PRIME tag team division and they earned it the good old fashion way. They WHIPPED someones asses for them essa! Thats right folks Jorge 'Dirty' Sanchez has returned to bring some life and love back to the pride of Mexico LOS DIABLOS! Welcome back to the fold Jorge. Even I was beginning to miss you. Now if we could move on about this tag team thing we do have some stuff to cover you know. Shut your mouth Barry. I've got some stuff to say and it is far more important than the dramatic crap you're going to spew out in order to build up drama holmes. So sit back, shut up, take notes, and realise the greatness of The Dirty Sanchez as I work this promo into something of true greatness. Now that I am back I want to say a big F*CK YOU to all those out there who didn't believe in Los Diablos. My masked Mexican buddies are truly proud men who proved you all wrong. So you can all sit on that fact and rotate as they continue to carry those gold belts to the highest mountain top they have ever been on. It is high time a group of truly great Mexican hermano's were given their due and Los Diablos, along with their great manager, Enrique Morales are going to provide us with that. You bias nature is astounding Mr. Sanchez. At any rate we are set to see the current PRIME Tag Team Champions of the World compete in a big sixteen man tag team match that has been ordered by Hin See himself. What do you think of this situation Mr. Sanchez? First of this is clearly an attempt to undermine the champions. If Hin See were anymore obvious with his bias toward OUR Tag Team Champions he would be as see through as clear ocean water essa. Signing all these 'big name' teams from the past to try and outshine Los Diablos clearly cause he never wanted them to have the titles in the first place. I'm onto that racist Japanese imigrant and I can tell you I don't much care for him at all. I don't believe that Hin See ordering this match was a slap in the face at Los Diablos. In fact if anything this will allow them the shot to prove that they are indeed the cream of the crop in the divsion by beating out four of the other seven teams in the division with them all at the same time. This is a golden shot for the team that wants to set the standards and be the example of the PRIME tag team division. I'm not surprised that a guy who is on Hin See's personal pay roll would come out here with a bullshit explanation like that. Fact is that Los Diablos are the stars of the tag team division in PRIME. They have the belts, they defeated and ended Manifest Destiny to get them, and they walked through more hell to get those belts than any other team in the history of the wrestling business, let alone PRIME, just to get them. Now Hin See goes out and pulls teams like Civil D and PWA out from what ever rocks they have been living under for the last several years, dusts them off, and expects everyone to be in awe of his division cause he managed to dangle enough money in front of a bunch of has beens to come in and try to outshine the main event act he already had here. It is bias at the most core level holmes and I defy anyone with any amount of real intelligence to deny that fact. No matter what the reasoning behind their signings Mr. Hin See has brought them here and they are here to make an impact in PRIME and sooner or later, yes, to try and take the gold from around the champions waists. In my eyes though this is nothing but a good thing for all involved as they talent level of the company just exploded to even greater heights and continues to make PRIME the premiere company in the business to be a part of. Just think about this Mr. Sanchez. The longer that Los Diablos holds onto those titles with this type of talent in the division the bigger and brighter they are going to shine in the grand scheme of things. Keep your fantasy to yourself Mr. Suck-A-Japanese Cock and I'll spill the truth when it comes to these situations. The fact here is that this is a travesty. Hin See has signed these teams to do nothing more than try and steal the titles from Los Diablos cause he never really wanted them to be champions in the first place. They are here to do whatever they can, be it triple teaming, attacking from behind, using fork lifts, or spraying their stupid little mists in the eyes of the ref in order to steal the titles that Los Diablos fought hard and honorably in order to get. We all know what the true plan and intention here in this deal is except you Barry. So cut your Hin See propaganda and get with the program of truth. Los Diablos are the champions though.. Yes and despite this openly bias plan they are going to remain that way. Don't you even begin essa to think for one second that Hin See has scared my masked brethern. They are going to be ready for war each and every time they come to the arenas. Whether that be a war in the ring or out of it will not matter to them as they set out on the next path of their journey. The time has come that Hin See, you, the fans, the world, and any other Jim Bob, hillbilly, American idiot who thinks otherwise that Los Diablos are in PRIME to remain as the top team in the world. PERIOD. Put a stamp on that and take it to whatever bank you like cause it is a check that simply will no bounce anywhere. If the PRIME Tag Team Champions of the world can bring as much fire, determination, desire, and confidence to the ring each week that their official cheer leader brings to us here then they are going to be a very tough team to every topple. Cheer leader? Essa you are treading on really thing ice already with your support of Hin See and his tyrant type ways. You keep getting smarting off like that holmes and you'll find yourself on the wrong end of a Dirty Sanchez and I don't think you'll enjoy it one little bit. You feel me there essa? Why are all of you people so violent? OH so now violence is a Mexican thing huh? Come here you little bastard! Screaming...suffering...then silence. We now move you to the scheduled promo.:::: The night air was cold and the high blowing wind didn't make things any more pleasing for those walking through the night in an attempt to either get home, to a movie, or in the case of a few men to clear their minds and talk about the events that have lead them to this point. Los Diablos, along with Enrique Morales, all walk along the streets of Anaheim, California. Normally it would be warm at this time but there is storm blowing in and the tempatures have dropped. Walking along with their large, black coats, pulled up to just below their mouths in order to keep warm the trio talks about PRIME. 'This infulx of talent is a problem Enrique. I'm not afraid to face off with any of them but it seems pretty clear that Hin See is using his money to screw with us by trying to simply over whelm us with greater numbers and odds.' Baltasar begins to explain as he pulls the coat a bit tighter around himself. 'I understand your feelings and dislike my boy. This is no doubt an event I never saw coming. But you should just forget the whole thing and not worry about it at all. Angelus and yourself held your own out there this last week and quickly proved just how dominate you are going to be even with this time of talent hanging around.' Morales replies with a little smile from under his coat. 'We need a plan of attack for this week though essa. I don't trust anyone we are partnered with this week in the least. Not after the way things went down on ReVolution the other night holmes. They are going to stab us in the back and I can feel it.' Angelus snorts as he punches a road sign as they walk by. Still angry over the loss to Gibbo a couple of weeks back and pissed that the division is now trying to shine brighter than its champions. 'A problem we have indeed my boys. This new 'talent', if you can call it that, will no doubt do their best to make names for themselves here in whatever way possible. Costing the current champions wouldn't be a bad way for them to start off their careers here. We must not allow that to happen.' Morales responds while taking his right hand and running it through his long hair. A look of intense thought on his face as the attempts to think of a way not to be screwed out of a win by over zealous team mates. 'I don't see why we have to take part in this affair anyway. We are the damn champions after all and I don't see what the hell it is we have to prove in this.' Baltasar roars with anger in his eyes. The fights with Team Zambo over the last couple of weeks have had him on edge. A shot at destroying Zameer was in his hands and the damned officials of PRIME stole that chance from him by ending the match in a no contest. 'The only thing I look forward to is keeping my ass in that ring with Zameer and teaching that big, dumb, snack loving tool who the real boss of this company is.' Morales stops, gets a big smile on his face, and jumps around like a kid who just saw his favorite toy in a shop window. He grabs both of them by the coats and begins to pull on them like a fool who is having a seizure from excitement. 'I've GOT IT! BY GOD BOYS I'VE GOT IT!' Morales exclaims happily as the two masked wrestlers simply stare at him like he is the biggest idiot they ever met. 'You've got something alright essa and I'm not sure I want to know where you got it from. If Hoyt saw you acting like this he'd surely smack you in the head and be trying to remove whatever demon is plaguing you at this moment.' Baltasar says and Angelus has a little laugh over the thought of Hoyt smacking Morales in the head and knocking him cold. 'Har, har, har. Laugh it up you two gringos. If I had more reason I'd send you both back to Mexico just to teach you a lesson.' Morales replies with a slight grin, never able to stay mad at them for anything for very long. 'Yeah sure you would holmes. Then what? You'd run around the ring like the Jumping Bean of old and take down all the giants, like Hessian, that you've pissed off while here all by yourself? I am pretty sure we can risk giving you some good natured ribbing.' Angelus replies with a grin of his own and an elbow to the shoulder of Enrique. 'My nickname is never to be uttered allowed,' Morales says with a scowl on his face.' You know that Angelus.' 'As much as this little 'thing' is intresting and all what exactly was the plan you brought up earlier? I mean you said you had a plan so maybe we should hear it...eh, Jumping Bean?' Baltasar says with a chuckle while stopping to look right at Morales and hear the plan he has. 'Alright. We'll continue this later. The plan I have in mind will allow you to keep the other teams from screwing you over and to prove to the world that you are better than all those other teams in PRIME. You simply only tag one another in and refuse to let the other teams get involved. This way they can do nothing to harm the outcome and you get to beat the other four teams all by yourselves. Good plan?' Morales says with a twinkle in his eyes. Truth be told he knew this could be suicide as beating four good teams by themselves would be a tall task for any team. Even one was great and experienced as his own. Both members of Los Diablos nod their heads in approval as grins form over their faces. The plan was put into place and in their minds it was the only way things could go down. No way they could trust the other teams not to screw them over in order to make impacts for themselves at their expense. It was the only move that made sense and all three men agreed on the plan. As the wind picked up they continued to walk down the street toward their hotels in order to get a good nights rest before trainning some more for the up coming war that they were going to have to set out to fight.
