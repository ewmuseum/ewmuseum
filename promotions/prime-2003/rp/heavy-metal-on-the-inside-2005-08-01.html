Heavy Metal on the inside

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st August 2005 A deafening echo slammed throughout the pyramid like a shotgun fired in the ears. Dust was thrown about and the attention of everyone was suddenly diverted to the source of the noise. Smiles abandoned shrinking faces and gloom built up in the hearts of the unfortunate witnesses as something spoken of only in hearsay was shown to them first hand. A large open-topped cube made of corrugated, pliant metal painted in bright lime green and electric purple stripes stood around 20 feet from the floor. A wooden door on contrasting stripes was crudely attached into a section cut out and hung on with poorly screwed hinges. The gigantic metal container was none other than the “office” of Pranks With Attitude, serving as their home away from home during wrestling events. Strategies were conceived inside, any side business such as hired pranking and so on would be conducted and occasionally, Tim Walden would be ejected in a humiliating manner through the “roof” during their PCW days together. “Alright” Gaz called out, peeking over the top. “All done, Squire!” “BASTARD!” Nerezza yelled as he opened the door. “Why did we have to ride IN this thing?” “It’s called making an entrance!” Gaz leaned over the edge, arms crossed and chin resting on them. “We have a day or two to prepare, so I thought we’d bring back the old metal crate for nostalgia’s sake.” “Gaz…” Nerezza said flatly, peering up at his companion. “Join me inside for a conference, would you?” The Jester came down from his perch, descending a ladder that had been screwed into the metalwork. Nerezza stepped inside, once again getting used to the surroundings. The focal point of the “room”, as it were, was a rectangular table, surrounded by 6 folding chairs. At the back, or furthest away from the door, was a wardrobe, which was never opened in fear of releasing Narnian chaos, but made an interesting conversation point. Just behind the door was a large sofa, presumably in the decades of age. It was good for lying on, but not sitting, as the back of it looked slashed to pieces and would easily give way. One of Nerezza’s rages had brought the situation about, leading to Gaz using it as a shield. Overall, it was a rather spacious and slightly cosy room considering the metal wallpaper and lack of carpet. “So what’s the problem, squire?” Gaz was answered by a thundering right hand to the face, sending him sprawling across the table. There were no crunched mints to substitute for teeth, no fake blood capsules, or cushions to land on. That was a shoot. Lost for words, Gaz didn’t get up for a second or two. When he finally raised his head, he saw a face from Nerezza he’d only seen when the situation was dire for his opponents. A face full of fangs, glaring red eyes and the vertical “Chelsea smile” ripping open along the black tattooed lines that sunk down from his mouth. He was hissing in a way that H. R. Giger and Ridley Scott would have instantly recognised, especially with the liquid dripping from his teeth. “Don’t you… EVER… do that again.” “…” Gaz had no reply. Nerezza was best left unspoken to in this state, until he had finished whatever monologue or lecture he had in mind. Interruption was a move as wise as shooting oneself in the head, although the latter would be less painful. “You ran off for a week. I had to put a brave face on. I had to come out with the intention of taking on everyone else without a partner. And when you come back, you’re spitting water, flexing your muscles and using Double Underhook Face Busters? What in Dio’s name are you THINKING?” Gaz waited, counting the seconds to make sure he wasn’t going to cut Nerezza off. Seven… Eight… Nine… “Believe me, Squire, there is method in my madness!” he replied, spitting a mouthful of blood onto the floor. His speech was slightly distorted from the swelling on his bottom lip. The infamous smile of his had avoided permanent damage of any kind, but Gaz would certainly not be doing publicity shots in a while. “HOW? Explain to me what you’re accomplishing by disappearing on me for a week then coming back like… THAT.” “Let me remind you, Squire…” Gaz began, slowly getting off the table. “That you know what happens to me if I stay as someone for too long. Why I like to imitate people and change things around every so often. Remember, that at one point, I wasn’t Gaz Green. I’ve never held the same name for long. As a child, my stepfathers changed faster than my socks did. My last name was something else before I learned how to spell it each time. And then I met that man…” “Gaz Green…” Nerezza stammered, shrinking into himself and unclenching his fist at last. “Yeah. You know at the time, I went by the name New-Blood in the ring. And then I met a man who I battled for months. He humiliated me in the ring, I took it outside the ring. I was obsessed with him. In the end, I broke him mentally to the point that he ran away. He left the business, the country, everything. But I still couldn’t get him out of my head. I hated his guts, but with him gone, what could I do? There was nobody to project that hate onto. So I took the name for myself. If I couldn’t beat him, I’d join him.” “You never told me that…” “And how am I to react, Squire? I couldn’t stand the thought of Gaz Green being no more. So I became him. As long as he was alive, still having fun and ruining else’s life, my heart was still beating and my lungs kept working. He had to live, so I could catch up to him one day. And I could pay him back, then. Yeah, it’s fucked up, but that’s what revenge does to you.” The Jester collapsed into a chair, tending to his bleeding lip, constantly wiping blood away from the cut. Nerezza fell silent… how much more to Gaz was there? And how much of it was the original Gaz Green, instead of the empty shell that held onto his memories? Behind the laughter and the pranks, the smiling and cheer, was Gaz actually the darker of the two? “I needed time out. Time away from Gaz. I wanted to spend just one night not having to be that bastard. I’ve been spending the last few days incognito in public. Just as me, not Gaz. I even used my real name when checking into a hotel. Wrote it there as plain as day. Name:-“ “I DON’T WANT TO HEAR IT!” Nerezza quickly yelled. “I know you as Gaz Green. I don’t want any secret identities. We go out there, we do our thing. It’s strictly business. And that’s how I NEED you. We have two matches ahead of us… I need you to get some ice on that jaw, I need you to sort your head out and I need you to be at 100%… clear?” “…crystal.” Mumbled Gaz. Nerezza awkwardly shuffled out of the door towards the canteen for some ice. He looked down at his hand, then raised it to his mouth and pressed his tongue to his knuckles. “And Rezz!” Gaz called out behind him. “Hm?” The vampire turned, looking at his partner. “I hope your punch won’t be that weak next ReVolution.” Smirked the Jester before disappearing back inside. Nerezza glanced back to his hand, then licked his lips before whispering under his breath. “Really bitter…”
