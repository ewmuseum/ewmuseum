The [name withheld] Series Presents: View of the World From the Bottom of A Bottle (ReV 49, vs. Karina Wolfenden & Bastion Cossack)

27 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/27/05 The camera fades in to a shot of an elderly gentleman sitting in an armchair in front of a glowing fireplace, wearing a red robe, white slippers, and smoking a pipe. A warm smile creeps over his face. “Hello, there! I'm Michael Gough. Although trained as an actor in the Shakespearean tradition, most of you will know me as ‘Alfred’ from the first four ‘Batman’ films.” Mr. Gough looks quizzically into the camera as he puffs delicately on the pipe. “I bet you're wondering a few things...more precisely, what am I doing here, and what the blazes is the [name withheld] Series. I'll answer the questions in reverse order. ‘The [name withheld] Series’ is the name chosen by one Mr. Chris ‘Nova’ Vega to be the title of a series of early PRIME efforts by the aforementioned, efforts that have sadly fallen by the wayside in the aftermath of the immediate closing of one Mr. [name withheld]’ theewe.net web domain hosting, which housed the original PRIME archives.” Mr. Gough grins knowingly. “Hence, ‘The [name withheld] Series.’ Now, as for what I'm doing here...I was busy handling voice work for Tim Burton's upcoming ‘Corpse Bride,’ when I received a call from...I guess I can call him ‘Nova,’ can't I? Nova was interested in hiring me to narrate his proposed series of refinished works.” Mr. Gough's smile takes on a hint of self-admiration. “I tried to refuse, but Nova was simply adamant about having me. In the end, my self-admitted weakness to flattery was my proverbial Achilles Heel, and I acquiesced...and that brings me here. So allow me to set the tone for you, as is my ultimate job description in this venture.” The firelight dims mysteriously as the camera begins to slowly zoom in. “It was the week before ReVolution 49, around Thanksgiving of 2004. It was Nova's debut match, against two other debuting PRIME athletes, the forgettable Bastion Cossack, and the Negasonic Lupine herself, Karina Wolfenden. As history dictates, the K-Wolf pinned Cossack that night and kick-started her storied winning streak, but Nova turned more than one head with his in-ring ability and promise. Here is the effort behind that match. Sit back, and enjoy the first installment...of The [name withheld] Series.” The image of Gough fades out, one of a young man in the crack of a doorway fades in. “Nova?” Sean Page cracks open the door to the apartment, and immediately swings his head back, repulsed. “Holy God…” The overwhelming stench of cigarettes, weed, and vomit hits Page’s olfactory glands like a sack of bricks wrapped in a migraine teleported into the very center of his mind. He puts a hand over his nose and mouth before swinging the door open and stepping into the darkness. “Where the fuck is the damn light-switch in this dungeon?” Sean fumbles on the wall for a moment before running his fingers across the switch and flicking it on. A disgusted sneer creeps over his face as his surveys the wreckage of the one-room domicile of his enemy-turned-ally. “Nova, wake up, you asshole! You have an interview in forty-five minutes!” Sean shouts into the room, looking around for his friend. Sean’s eyes dart around the room…and stop, suddenly, on a pair of Clark Wallabes poking out of the doorframe of the bathroom. “Shit…oh, shit,” Page groans, “you fucking bum…” He steps over an over-turned stool, a stack of scattered newspapers, and an empty pizza box before reaching the bathroom. He looks down to see Nova sprawled out, his head near the toilet, one hand resting on the lip of the bath-tub and the other clutching an empty plastic handle of Barton’s vodka. Sean discovers the toilet un-flushed, the remnants of what appears to be a large, half double-cheese, half meat-lovers pizza floating serenely on the surface of the water. “Nova, you bastard,” Sean grumbles, reaching down and lifting Nova up under his arms. Nova’s feet slide across the ground, and he emits a low “unngh.” “Come on,” Sean urges, trying to hoist Nova to his feet and drag him towards the door, “damn, Nova. You HAVE to go to this interview. Seriously. Help me out here.” Nova groans, and his feet slap across the floor as Sean helps him towards the door. Sean reaches down, turns the knob, and kicks the door open. He starts to drag Nova out the door, but Nova lurches forward of his own accord and hangs over the railing of the stairwell. “Ugh…gluuhg…,” Nova moans, spit dangling from his mouth over the side of the stairwell. “Oh, just fucking great,” Sean says, his arms crossed over his chest as he leans against the doorway of Nova’s apartment. “UUUGH!” Nova’s head snaps back before shooting forward, vomiting spraying in an impressive all-drenching spray that covers several cars that are parked below the balcony. “HOOOARGH!! GOOOAGH!!” “Ya done, there?” Sean asks, a small grin on his face as he watches his friend gasp for air. Nova nods slowly before Sean moves forward, grabbing him under the arm. Sean leads him down the stairs towards his rent-a-car, a black Toyota Camry. He opens the passenger door, and Nova collapses into the seat. Sean slams the door behind him and walks around to the driver’s side. Inside the car, Nova, eyes still shut, fumbles to secure a blue bandanna around his long, blonde hair before reaching into his pocket for a half-crumpled, booze-soaked pack of Camel Lights. He pulls one out of the pack and puts it between his lips. The end of the cigarette hangs down…Nova used to call these “Gonzo Noses.” “Now, let’s examine where the fuck you would be without me,” Page states matter-of-factly as he starts up the car, “face-down in your nasty-ass bathroom missing a mandatory interview just after being formally hired by the company I got you into.” Nova lights the cigarette and exhales, the plume hitting the windshield in front of him and dissipating. “You never say anything anymore,” Sean grumbles, backing the car out of the apartment complex. Nova closes his eyes. **** He opens them briefly as Sean pulls up to the offices of RAGE Magazine, a wrestling insider publication. “We’re here, Sunshine,” Page says, getting out of the car and slamming the door loudly on purpose. Nova winces and tries to lift a hand to rub his throbbing temple…but he can’t summon the energy. Sean walks around and opens his door. “Listen up,” Page says, taking hold of Nova’s arm, “I can do the talking if you just sit in the chair. Deal?” In response, Nova leans over and lets spill another round of whatever delectable culinary concoctions he indulged in the previous night. Sean leaps backward quickly. “Whoa!” Page shouts. “A little warning, please? These shoes cost more than your entire fucking hobo-ass wardrobe, Nova!” Nova fumbles in his pocket for his pack of smokes…and drops them right into the puddle of bile beneath the passenger door. He makes as though to grab them back up. “Oh, give me a break,” Sean says, grabbing Nova and pulling him up, “let’s just go inside.” Sean helps Nova inside into the lobby and quickly plants him in a chair before walking over to the receptionist desk to ask for directions to Studio 4C. When he receives them, he walks back over to Nova and helps him to his feet. They head towards the elevator, the receptionist openly staring at them. “What’s the matter?” Page snaps, “Never seen anyone in the final stages of lung cancer before?” “Oh, my!” the receptionist replies, “I didn’t mean to…” “Ya know what,” Sean interrupts, “just don’t, okay? Just don’t!” The duo makes their way into the elevator and Sean sets it for the right floor. When they get out, Nova stumbles over to the water fountain and begins gobbling up the stream, gasping for breath in between slurps. He steps back, wiping his mouth, his chest rising and falling quickly as he pants for air. He follows Sean into Studio 4C, which is empty save for a table and a few chairs. Nova slumps down into one of them, holding his head, his eyes tightly closed. Sean takes a seat in one of the others, slinging his legs up onto the table and getting comfortable. A few minutes pass. “Hey, guys! Sorry I’m late…traffic was a bitch, ya know?” a man says as he walks into the studio. “Sure,” Page says, rolling his eyes. “I’m Terry ‘The Technician’ Thomas, head reporter for RAGE Magazine,” Terry says. “Sean Page,” Sean says simply before gesturing to his comatose comrade, “this is Nova.” “Uh,” Terry says, obviously confused, “is he…?” “Asleep?” Page finishes, “yeah, I think so. Poor guy. Sick as a dog. Hell, I told him not to even come in, but he knew it was a mandatory interview, and it would reflect badly on him so close after being hired if he didn’t show up.” “Wow,” Terry says with a respectful nod. “That’s some ambition.” “Well, he’s an ambitious guy,” Page replies, a hokey smile covering his face as Nova begins snoring audibly. “I can answer your questions.” “Okay,” Terry agrees, “well, there’s no real getting around it. Rumors have been floating around, each more outlandish than the last…rumors about Nova’s personal life in the past month or so, since the abrupt closure of NEW…” “I figured as much,” Page interrupts, “you want the story?” “You know what happened?” Terry asks, leaning forward in his chair. “Yeah, I know what happened,” Page replies calmly. Dollar signs flash in “The Technician’s” eyes as a grin spreads from ear to ear. “But if you want me to tell it, you’ll need to do two things,” Page says confidently. “And what are those?” Terry asks quickly, eager to get the story that has eluded the other mags and websites that have covered pieces of Nova’s career. “Nummer Eins,” Page says in a mocking German accent, “Keep your trap shut. I know the story, so I’ll do the talking. Nummer Zwei…” Page pauses, and Terry leans forward again, his hands clasped together in front of him. “…clear your schedule.” **** “So around the time Nova came back to NEW…August, I think…he met this chick named Liza Davis in the lobby of the hotel he was staying at. Idiot gets wasted drunk and passes out in the elevator. Liza finds him and takes care of him, but suckers him into helping her out with this book she wants to write about professional wrestling…ya know, get her backstage access to NEW, give her the scoop on what it’s like inside the business and all that other good shit that people on the outside have a difficult time getting at. Now, me, I would have pimp-slapped that bitch as soon as she tried any of that guilt trip bullshit, but Nova’s always been nicer than me about stuff like that, so he agreed. He let her sort of tag along, and they became quite the pals…” “Ah, I gotcha,” Terry says, chuckling devilishly and winking at Page. “Not like that,” Sean corrects, “Yeah, if it was me, I would’ve stuck that shit before my hangover wore off and then kicked the bitch out of her own room…but we’re talking about Nova here. His wife got pretty in with Liza, too, since she would stay over at their house in Detroit sometimes just to get a feel of how a younger couple with a small child handled the high-stress pro-wrestling lifestyle. And what did I tell you about interruptions? I’m gonna lose my train of thought here.” “Right,” Terry replies, holding his hands back in the “my bad” gesture. “Where was I?” Sean asks rhetorically, “right. So Liza and Nova are getting to be real tight, and one night Nova goes to pick her up at her hotel room, and she’s waiting for him in the lobby…” Nova swings open the glass double-doors to the lobby of the Comfort Inn, and sees Liza sitting on one of the couches in a black dress, waiting for him. She waves. “Ready to go?” Nova asks as he approaches. “Yeah,” Liza says, standing up. “You look nice tonight,” Nova compliments. “Thanks,” Liza replies. “You too.” They walk toward the doors, but Liza stops suddenly. ”Oh, I almost forgot!” she exclaims, “I got proofs back on the first three chapters of the book! You wanna see ‘em?” Nova looks down at his watch…they were running late, but he had been waiting to see some proofs on the book for weeks. “Eh, I think we better get moving, Liza,” Nova says regretfully, “You can show me later.” “Oh, come on, Nova!” she pleads, “It’ll only take a minute! You’ll love what you see.” Nova caves. “Alright, Liza, but we need to hurry. We’re already half an hour late.” Liza grins. “Fine, let’s go, then. It’ll only be a minute.” “So Nova follows Liza up to her room on the fourth floor, anxious to see how she’s transformed his words into writing. He walks into the room behind her…” ”Come in, come in,” Liza says amiably. Nova walks into the room, looking around. Truth be told, he’s nervous being up here with Liza. It seems so…personal. But he doesn’t want to offend his friend by suggesting that this is inappropriate. He doubts she’s even taken it into account. “I know they’re here somewhere,” Liza says, looking around the bed. “Can you help me look for a manila envelope?” Nova steps into the room and scans around the bed with his eyes. Liza climbs to her feet in front of him, and he notices immediately how close she is to him. She steps backward, away from him, and he also notices how heavily she’s breathing, a wild look in her eyes. “Liza?” Nova asks, growing uncomfortable as she stares at him. Without warning, Liza brings an arm up, smashing her nose with her fist. She stumbles backward and bounces off the bed to the floor. She immediately rips one of the straps on her dress with her hand. Nova’s world stops. He stares dully at his friend on the ground, unable to comprehend what has just happened. “STOP! PLEASE!” Liza screams loudly as blood begins to pour from her nose. “HELP! SOMEBODY PLEASE HELP!” Nova lingers for only a moment, staring in horror at Liza, before he turns and runs out of the room. He runs down the hallway as a security guard hustles toward Liza’s room. All Nova cares about is running. Run. Run. Run. “Hey, you!” the guard yells as Nova tries to pass, grabbing one of his shoulders. Nova tries to shirk free of the grip, and when he can’t, he turns and lands a stiff jab to the guard’s cheek, sending the middle-aged man careening into the wall. Nova takes the elevator down, and sprints through the lobby out into the parking lot. “No shit?” Terry asks incredulously, “she was hustling him?” “It gets worse,” Page continues, “Nova tries to drive home, but he sees police by his house, so he parks down the road a ways and waits for them to leave. When they do, he sneaks over to his house…” Nova opens the front door to his house quietly. He shuts it behind him softly, and when he turns around, he sees his wife sitting on the back of the couch, staring at him. “What happened?” she asks simply, calmly. Nova walks briskly over to her and grabs her shoulders. “I didn’t do it, Ariel,” Nova says desperately, “I know what it looks like, but it isn’t what happened…she…she…” Ariel puts a finger up to his mouth. “Shhh…I believe you.” Nova looks at her, amazed. “You do?” When she nods her head, Nova gives a sigh of relief and embraces her. “Thank God…” She pushes away for a minute, and looks him in the eyes. “I just need to hear something from you.” “What is it?” he asks. “I…I need to hear that you’ve never…never cheated on me,” Ariel says slowly, as though each word pained her to say. And the world explodes, little fragments of blue and green splattering across the canvas of the Milky Way. Nova hesitates…and remembers. It is September of 2003, and the Forsaken are living large. Sean Page, Supernova, Derek Lowe, and Jamie Zero are on top of NEW, and ratings are seeing improvements. In the VIP area of Club Vertigo, all hell is breaking loose. Sean holds a bottle of champagne as two dark-haired European women crawl all over him. Nova stands by the wall, the cigarette in his mouth glowing in the sexy darkness of the room. Derek Lowe stands next to him, Canadian beers in each hand. Both of them survey the debauchery. Lowe says something, but Nova can’t hear over the music. “What?” Nova yells to Lowe. Lowe just grins and points over towards the far wall, where a young svelte blonde stands by a black curtain, staring at Nova. “Oh, fuck,” Nova says with a gasp. The girl has been watching him all night…and, truth be told, he’s been watching her, too. He loves his wife, but things at home have been very tense recently, with Ariel’s pregnancy getting underway… …and hey, he was still allowed to look. But the drinks keep coming, and coming, and coming, and in the haziness of the vodka stupor, Nova can see the girl leading him by the hand beyond the black curtain… “No…,” Ariel gasps, bringing Nova back to reality, “Chris, no…” “Ariel, I…,” Nova begins, but is cut off. “Don’t touch me!!” Ariel screams, backing away from him. Somewhere in the house, a baby begins to cry. “I didn’t do this, Ariel!” Nova protests, “I’ve been set-“ “Shut up!” Ariel screams over him, tears beginning to pour down her cheeks, “How could you, Chris?! How am I supposed to believe anything you say now?” “Ariel…,” Nova stammers, tears beginning to well up in his eyes as well, “Please…” “Get out!” Ariel yells. “Ariel, please…” “GET OUT!!!” “Wow…,” Terry says, spell-bound, “what a screw-job…” “You’re telling me,” Page says, “St. Nova the family man goes buck-wild one night, and it catches up to him over a year later…” “So she kicked him out?” Terry asks. “Oh, yeah,” Page replies, “Liza filed a report with the police, fabricating an attempted rape story, and Ariel kicked Nova out of the house. The trial was avoided, as there was a settlement out of court where Ariel paid Liza a given amount out of her and Nova’s joint account, and basically kept the rest. Nova knew that, to the public, he didn’t have a leg to stand on. He had a bruised-up bitch in a hotel room, and a wife at home that he basically admitted infidelity to.” “God, that’s the shits,” Terry says, shaking his head. “Yeah, but it ain’t the money that really got to him,” Page continues, “it was never getting to see his infant daughter again. Ariel filed for divorce and got custody over the baby.” “Jesus,” Terry whispers. “Yeah, it was a real kick in the nuts, and the timing couldn’t have been worse,” Page explains, “Amidst all this NEW shut down-big fuckin’ surprise-and Nova was left unemployed. He basically holed himself up in his one-room drug den in the slums of Detroit, and that’s where I found him the night he almost made the biggest mistake of his life…” The bottle of Jack hits the wall and shatters. Nova leans violently to the left and right, struggling to keep upright, his vision swirling as “White Light/White Heat” by The Velvet Underground plays frantically on a small CD player. Nova wipes tears away from his eyes as he stares at a photo of his daughter, asleep in her crib. He tosses the photo to the ground and stumbles over to a nightstand by the mattress in the corner. He opens the drawer and pulls out a 9mm pistol and a loaded clip. Shakily, he slams the clip into the butt of the pistol and staggers over to the door. He flings it open to see the dark sky flash with lightning as heavy, thick rain blankets everything around him. He turns… …and sees a figure shrouded in shadow approach him. “What were you doing there?” Terry asks. “I had heard that he was in kind of dire straits, and I figured I would go and just toss out the olive branch to him after our falling out about a year ago,” Page explains, “I wasn’t prepared for the shit I was about to see…” The figure emerges from the darkness, shrouded in a beige raincoat, brown hair soaked and clinging to his skull. “Nova?” Page asks, “wha…what the fuck is going on?” Nova can barely speak or stay upright, the liquor slowly depriving him of coherence and mobility. “Liza…fuck…fuckin’ bitch…,” Nova says slowly, “fuckin’ bitch ru-ruined my life…” “Oh, my God,” Sean whispers, “Nova, put the gun down.” “I’m ‘unna kill ‘er,” Nova mumbles, as though he’s speaking through Sean, “I’ll fuckin’ kill ‘er.” “PUT THE FUCKING GUN DOWN!” Sean screams. When Nova doesn’t comply, Sean leaps forward and grabs Nova’s arm, quickly wrestling the gun out of his hand. Page breathes a sigh of relief as he tosses the gun over the balcony into the dumpster below. Nova stands for a moment, staring at nothing, before collapsing against the outside wall of his apartment. He slides down to the ground, and though it’s impossible to see it in the rain, he begins to cry. Page stands there, staring at his friend, dumb-founded. He can do nothing, say nothing to help his friend. “Come on, man,” Sean says gently, “let’s go inside.” “He was gonna kill Liza?” Terry inquires excitedly. “Or himself, in the process,” Page answers, “Hell…I could give a fuck if he offed that Liza bitch, but I didn’t want him to ruin his own life.” “So what happened next?” Terry asks, eager to know more. “The rest is history,” Page replies coolly. “Brad and I found PRIME, and I eventually convinced Nova to follow us there.” “That’s one of the most amazing stories I’ve ever heard,” Terry says, his eyes lit up, “I think it’ll be cover story easy.” “Fuck,” Nova moans. Terry jumps in his seat as Nova sits upright in the chair, holding his head. He stands up without acknowledging the interviewer and walks over to the garbage can in the corner. Page smiles at Terry as the sweet sounds of the almighty up-chuck can be heard from the back of the room. Nova blows his nose a few times and coughs before standing upright and shaking his head. “I guess you’ve got everything you need?” Sean asks Terry. “Absolutely!” Terry replies enthusiastically, “Thank you!” Terry gets up from his chair and extends his hand, but Sean just gives him a once-over with his eyes before scooting back in his chair, standing up, and walking behind Nova to the doorway of the room. “Sure.” The elevator stops in the lobby, and Nova lights a cigarette. He and Page walk out past the receptionist, who stares gapingly at the cigarette in Nova’s mouth. “He’s cured! It’s a fucking miracle!” Page shouts to the receptionist, a sly grin on his face as Nova pushes open the double doors to the building and steps out into the sunlight.
