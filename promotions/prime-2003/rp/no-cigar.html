No Cigar

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Post-SB's Match at UV SB, still bloody as hell, is shoving EMT's off of him as he walks backstage after his brutal Falls Count Anywhere match with Tony Rolo. EMT #1: Mr. Rossi! Mr. Rossi, you need to get on this stretcher and get to the hospital, please. SB: Fuck you! I'm walkin' outta here, you got me? A second EMT grabs SB, and Sb decks him! The first EMT just stares at his downed colleague, and he eats a right hand from SB as well! The rest of the paramedic team backs off, and SB is left on his own, slumped against the wall, his chest heaving. His hair is matted from blood...his eyes blood red from nearly bulging out of his sockets after that 20 foot splash off the top from Rolo. A young PRIME gofer walks over to him with a concerned expression on his face. The kid can't be older than twenty, and has thick glasses on his face. Gofer: Are you all right, mister? SB: I'm pretty fuckin' far from all right, kid. Gofer: You need anything? SB spits up some blood. Gofer: Jesus, are you bleeding internally? SB: Relax, kid. It's my lip. Just get outta here, I gotta get to my car. SB stumbles forward a few steps before flopping forward, his hands just barely breaking his fall. He drives his knuckles into the floor and forces his way back up. There it is...the exit. He drags himself to the door, shoves it open, and disappears behind him. We can't see him throw up, but we sure can hear it. A few seconds of silence follows... SB: FFFFFUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUCCCCKKKK! Something heavy and metal slams against the wall, and then the CRASH! of breaking glass is heard. We hear slow, dragging footsteps, and then a body falling onto the concrete. As the camera fades out on the door, we hear the sirens of an ambulance...
