Fear Of Jones In Las Vegas

28 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28th June 2005 “It’s only right that I be formal. My name is Nascosto Nerezza. And for a little under a year, I’ve been… away. Everything began 11 months ago with the closure of my current employer, Rush Pro Wrestling. It came as somewhat of a shock to many – the fans, the wrestlers, even to some staff. Even now, I still remember the glass nearly breaking in the door as it was slammed on Gaz and myself. Our careers were once again on hiatus, but we had already decided our next step. What I will talk about… of course, if you’d care to hear… is what we did next. Pull up a chair; the comfier, the better. Get another beer, or coffee, tea, coke, your own personal poison and if you can, try to picture what I’m telling you.” ~ From whatever quiet environment you may have imagined me to be telling you from… a fireside in a stately home, a candlelit mausoleum, a dimly lit basement… throw yourself away from that. Find yourself hurtling towards neon lights, jazzy music, ladies and gentlemen of the upper class and entertainers from every corner of the globe. The splendour of the most glamorous hotels and a casino that only Willie Wonka could have had a hand in the construction of. Because let me tell you now… there’s no place on earth like Las Vegas, Nevada. And that’s where they are. Dressed in the permanently attached bottle green 3-piece suit, Gaz Green captures the attention of a small crowd. Among the tuxedos and cocktail dresses, no outfit quite stands out as much as his. The smile on his face was more than a million dollars – each tooth would be enough to cash in for more than every chip in the casino combined and tripled. And the merry laughter that came as a chorus to his every sentence carried a message resting on top – this boy was home. Nerezza, the antisocial misfit had even dropped his shield for the night, although his taste remained unchanged. He sat at a poker table, looking more like an off-duty entertainer in his attire – gone was his bare essentials attitude to clothing, now bearing a more olde worlde vampyre look. If Anne Rice had taken 5 minutes from bringing herself closer to a heart attack, she probably would have claimed credit for his creation. His outfit was black from toe to head with the exception of a pristine white shirt and pair of gloves. The fine top hat, mantle and even the elegant cane resting against his leg gave him all the credibility needed to dance on a tunnel roof. However, the ladies on each arm gazing at his cards kept him suitably immobile. Another round of laughter, acknowledged by a bow, saw Gaz leaving his small party and sauntering to his partner-in-crime. Giving a quick cock of his head to one side, one of Nerezza’s companions shuffled across to make room for Gaz. She was quick enough to attach herself to his arm instead, raising her leg enough to draw the Jester’s attention to her fishnet-covered thigh as it peeked out from under the slit of her wine-red dress. Clearing his throat and loosening his collar, Gaz addressed his friend without taking his eyes off the visual treat. “Mr. Nerezza… how goes your luck tonight?” “She’s quite the lady…” came the double-entendre reply. “And your evening?” “Oh, superb, great, ace!” Gaz jovially chirped. Nerezza was as sharp as his teeth and noticed the hint, rearranging his cards. “I’ve just been speaking with a few people and it turns out that there’s going to be a certain guest entertainer tonight. “I already have entertainment of my own, but thank you for the notice.” Nerezza had his mind on the ladies. He couldn’t wait to find a secluded, safe, private spot and begin to nibble their necks. Almost nothing would be able to shift him from his seat. Almost. “What if I said that it was someone who gets pelted with women’s underwear regularly?” Gaz continued, his eyes wandering over the appropriate places of his new friend. “I have no interest in seeing Steve The Laundryman Carnage on stage.” “What about… Tom Jones?” … Nerezza’s cards fell to the floor. His hands balled up into fists and the grinding of his teeth could have cut diamonds. Gaz reached down to pick up the cards, neatly pulling them into a uniform stack and fanning them before passing the hand to the leggy brunette to his right. A smirk appeared on his face as he saw an inverted version of it appearing on Nerezza’s. Tipping his hat, Nerezza gave his apologies to the ladies and hurried away. Gaz was quick to follow, with a supreme and satisfied grin. Hopefully, the blonde would have noticed the small slip of paper with his mobile phone number wedged between the King of Clubs and the 2 of Hearts. Outside, the black sky was kept pressed away from falling by dazzling spotlights sweeping through the air. Nerezza stomped along, barely allowing Gaz to keep up. “One thing, Rezz!” Gaz panted as he jogged alongside. “You never told me why you hated Tom Jones so much.” Nerezza stopped for a brief second, finally allowing Gaz to get his breath back. As the Jester regained his composure, Nerezza looked up to the sky and began to reminisce to himself. “Do you remember Fluffy? My little pet that followed me everywhere, from Detroit, to Great Britain, Japan, France, North Carolina… who ate every scrap I gave him and never did anything wrong? My adorable little Fluffy who had a habit of chasing cars that were coming towards him instead of driving away from?” Nerezza came close to shedding a tear. Gaz merely looked on, straightening his jacket. “Yeah…” “Well that’s the bastard who killed him. We came to LA for that show and Jones was singing the night before. Fluffy caught a mysterious odour from Jones… ran in his direction… Limo driver failed to notice him… oh, Fluffy… he was such a rare breed.” “I can never pronounce it right.” Gaz added, trying not to show a lack of emotion. “Chupacabra.” Nerezza corrected him. “No, I’m sure it’s that small little thing with the big eyes and the bloody intent to kill everything around it, compressing 50 megatons of explosive murderous rage into less than 12 inches square of toothy death.” “That’s a Chihuahua. Anyway, the fact is, that underwear-fuelled crooner killed my pet and we have a perfect chance to ruin his name. And where better to do so than right here, in the city of sin?” Nerezza continued his self-righteous and motivated speech while Gaz looked at him in doubt. “Squire, in all seriousness… we’ve been living the high life here for months. We’ve got our own show from 3am to 5am every Thursday, we have friends, we even have women saying our names without shouting YOU BASTARD! after it, why start the pranking again now?” “One, because I want to scalp him and make him eat his own afro until his throat is permanently deformed by the wiry texture and two… because it’s fun.” Gaz tapped his finger to his chin for a moment. His eyebrow quirked up followed by the corners of his mouth upturning. The smile of the Original Prankster sat on his lips once more. “You know, Rezz…” Green smiled. “I think you may have something.” “Cheeky, you sleep around far more than I ever do! Honestly, you spend so much time on your back that you look like Boris Nakobi waiting for the 3-Count!” Nerezza roared, attracting the attention of a few bystanders. Gaz quickly grabbed him by the collar and led him along the street, hastening the journey towards Gaz’s beloved Shelby Cobra. Almost hurling him into passenger side, Gaz quickly ran around to the driver’s seat and slammed his foot down, causing them to speed away with a screech of tyres and the smell of burning rubber. “Don’t worry about the comment, Rezz, it’s not the reason I’m going so fast.” Gaz reassured his partner. “But you gave me an idea that will make Stephen Hawking look like Terry Schia… wait… well it’s a damn good plan, anyway.” “Do tell…” Nerezza said, staring out of the window at the bright lights of the famous strip. “Well, Old Tom loves the tsunami of thongs he gets at every show. Look at his face. The man must have an entire room of his house dedicated to being a panty-shower. So all we need is a good disguise so we can get close enough to him. And I just happen to have a brilliant idea. We just need top floor seats, a tent and a little skill…” “I’m not sure I follow…” Nerezza sounded a little confused by the plan. “Why don’t we just run onto the stage and put him 6 feet under the Green, Green Grass Of Home?” “We have a reputation to uphold. Besides, what could possibly go wrong?” Sometime later… The Voice himself stood proudly Stage Left, with “What’s New Pussycat?” providing the entertainment for an amassed crowd of thousands as they dined, drank and gambled the night away. However, unbeknownst to anyone, something sinister lurked in the rafters. With evil eyes, Nascosto Nerezza stared down at Tom Jones, salivating at the opportunity of revenge. Behind him, Gaz worked feverishly on something with a sewing needle. “Is it done yet?” Nerezza hissed, leering at his nemesis as he began to shake his hips about… taking most of his stomach with them. “I’m beginning to feel sick and the height we’re at has nothing to do with it. Not to mention your idea of getting in here. I mean, men also him perform.” “Yeah, I heard about that on the grapevine… apparently, his voice isn’t the only reason people talk about him having the best mouth in Britain. Anyway, my plan was awesome! Who’d have thought we’d stumble onto a bunch of girls seeking butch lovers?” “First of all, there are plenty of straight women here too. We’ve had no shortage of them. Second, I am never wearing that dress again!” Nerezza growled, yanking down an imaginary skirt. “Anyway, are you finished with that?” “Almost!” Gaz replied, biting the end of the thread and dropping the needle carelessly below. “Right, finished. I’ll sit on that side, you sit there.” Nerezza followed the instructions, pulling a large white cloth over his head and backing into what appeared to be a huge sleeping bag. Gaz followed, pulling down some sort of safety bar like the kind found on a roller coaster car. “And this is?” Nerezza looked at it with disapproval. “Steering bar. Put your weight on it to go left, I do the same to go right.” Came the explanation. “Now get ready, the song’s almost finished.” “So is he…” gloated Nerezza. Tom held onto the last note before the grand finish. A deafening roar of applause resounded throughout the entire building, encompassing clapping, whistles, screaming and of course… the unavoidable hail of female underwear. “That’s our cue, dive!” Gaz ordered. And with a hefty shove, the pranksters plummeted from their perch… and soon found themselves floating towards Tom from on high inside a hang glider constructed to resemble a gigantic pair of panties. The shadow swooped over the unaware audience towards him like a hungry shark closing in for the kill. It wasn’t until a split second before impact that Tom noticed the colossal knickers set to engulf him. A painful “OOMPH!” managed to get through the microphone before the mother of all underwear collided with him, forcing the stagehands to hurriedly close the curtains. Luckily, the mic was killed by remote before the sounds of the savage beating could shave 10 minutes off the expected heart attacks of his biggest fans. A few weeks later… “What could possibly go wrong…” Nerezza parodied Gaz. “It wasn’t my fault!” Gaz defended himself. “Oh please, the Leeroy Jenkins excuse won’t work on me!” Nerezza interrupted. The two of them were busy loading boxes of all sorts into the thankfully spacious rear of Nerezza’s customised purple Hearse. “Squire, how was I to know that at the very same time we nailed him with the Pants Glider, someone had successfully used a slingshot to get him in the mouth with their G-String?” protested The Jester. “I thought the way her was turning blue was from the way we were beating him black and blue!” “WELL IT BLOODY WELL WASN’T!” came the thunderous exclamation. “He was in surgery for hours while they tried to stop him choking to death.” “Hey, I’m not the one who disconnected the safety bar and used it to ram the offending undergarments into his lungs, screaming TAKE THIS YOU DRACONIAN PET MURDERER and started to throttle him with the first thong he could grab!” “Well, I… I… forget it.” Nerezza began, but simply darted back inside the door to their home. Or at least, their old home. Following the fee for the fine and medical bills on top of their spending sprees in the casinos, the pranksters had lost their home and job. Never to see the nightlife of Las Vegas again, sadness overcame them as they climbed into the car, taking one final look at their social paradise in the mirrors before they slowly drive off. ~ “And that’s how we began our long road back to the ring… Of course, it wasn’t a straight road at all. Many twists, many turns… but I’ll leave all that until next time…”
