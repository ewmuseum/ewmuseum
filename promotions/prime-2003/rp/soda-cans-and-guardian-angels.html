Soda Cans and Guardian Angels

undated

Date: Who cares December 19th, 1996 Mary Mayweather had garnered a reputation as the senile old bat on Brooks Drive. She sat on her porch all day watching the world pass her by. When someone gave her a greeting, she would retort with indecipherable gibberish. At random points in the day, she would scream for felines to get off her lawn when none were there. Once nightfall presented itself, she littered the spaces under her windows with plastic bags full of empty soda cans. What she called an efficient alarm system, everybody else deemed ridiculous. However, when Mary was startled to life by the sound of cans crunching, she suddenly felt like the genius. Quickly, her instincts came into play and she clutched the phone. A couple of seconds later, she was on the phone with the 911 dispatcher. "There’s an intruder at 15 Brooks Drive, come quickly." Mary said before hanging up the phone, not giving the lady on the other end the chance to respond. Meanwhile, downstairs, an eighteen year old Devin Shakur was kicking the plastic bags away while quietly swearing. "Son of a bitch, who in the fuck puts soda cans all over the goddamn floor?" The rebellious teenager rhetorically inquired. Ten seconds elapsed before Devin’s eyes were able to adjust to the darkness. Rumor had it that Mary had very valuable diamonds somewhere in her house. Devin had watched the residence for a couple of days to get her routine. To say that it was the most boring time of his life would be a massive understatement. He deduced that going in through a back window would avoid mass detection, and having honed his skills he would be able to glide up the stairs and investigate the whereabouts of the diamonds. Now his plan was going to shit. Devin knew that he had to get out quickly. Even a deaf person could hear the ruckus that he was making. He made a visor with his hands, looking for an exit out the back door. Once he was confident with the route, he started to make his exodus. The light turned on behind him, and Devin heard the sound of a shotgun being cocked. "Don’t ye move yer hide no more." Mary said, moving some of the bags that blocked her path from the nuisance in her house. "I done already called the poh-lice and dey gon’ come and take ye to the big house." Barely audible, Devin muttered his sentiment "Goddammit." "God done gone asleep for the night so ye got me instead. Now turn around, I wanna git a good look at you." Reluctantly, Devin slowly turned his body around to face the elderly woman that held the gun to him. Once their eyes met, Mary raised the gun up to Devin’s chest to further emphasize her being in charge of the situation. "Why you gotta come and burglarize my house? I ain’t done nothin’ of no kind to you." A long, long, long moment elapsed before Devin decided to dignify her question with a response. "Why? Because you are a waste of space, taking up the air society could use on more productive matters. You just take like all the rest of the fiends and never give anything back. You sicken me as a human being." The exasperated look upon Mary’s face told the entire story. She went to slap the taste out of mouth, but blue police lights pierced into their lives and Devin knew that the end was near. Just a hop and a skip later, the police knocked on the front door. "I’M IN HE-" Devin stopped her from emitting another word by kicking at the barrel of the gun, propelling the gun up and back into Mary’s head, knocking her to the ground. The astute policemen heard the commotion, and slammed into the door to get it open. Three men rushed into the house with their guns drawn to find Mary looking up at her kitchen light. Out the window they saw the back of Devin Shakur, hopping over fences like a man possessed. The lead officer talked into his walkie talkie to alert other officers in the area that the intruder was fleeing the scene. Devin figured that he wouldn’t be able to get away from the army chasing him, but he had to try anyway on the off chance that he hit Mary in the right place, rendering her incapable of remembering his appearance. Five minutes later he was apprehended just one mile from the Smith residence. ------------------ August 25th, 2007 "I’m on the highway to hell" "On the highway to hell" "Highway to hell" "I’m on the highway to hell" "No stop signs, speed limit…" The concoction of Devin Shakur and Bon Scott was not exactly something that would receive any awards for best vocals. Devin was at peace on the road, just mere miles from his home and the woman inside who would alleviate all of the stress that had built up in his life. Right now he felt on top of the world having captured the Intense Championship, being able to headline DDT, and he just knocked off Nathan Murray. It's the good life." Suddenly, a violent force slam into his back. His eyes turned around to see what the disturbance was, but the only thing that he could see was a square mass accelerating forward to slam the back of his vehicle yet again. "WHAT THE FUCK?" Devin attempted to speed away from the scene, knowing that his car could go up to speeds of 180 miles per hour. Much to his chagrin, the car stayed right with him every step of the way. Next, the car pulled alongside of Devin, to which he more than willingly rolled down his window so he could bless them out in ways never before comprehended by the English language. "WHO THE F-" Before he could finish his exclamation, Devin saw a shotgun in the passengers hand, and ducked underneath the door while shots flew in overhead. He lost complete control of the car, causing it to spin around four times before the front slammed into a steel barricade, flipping the car repeatedly. It finally came to a stop about five hundred feet from where the first shot occurred. Devin did a fast body check to ensure that nothing on his person was damaged. The seatbelt actually saved his life. Then reality set in, someone had just tried to kill him. A car came to a screeching stop not far from where Devin rested. He deduced that it could only be the people who attempted to just take his life. Nobody would help out this quick. Devin decided to resort to a tactic that he knew well, now being in the wrestling business for close to eight months. He played dead Footsteps became audible after a few seconds, getting closer and closer to where Devin feigned his appearance. No movement from inside the car, so the person slams the barrel of the gun into the window, smashing it into many tiny pieces…Again, no movement from inside. "I think he’s dead." A feminine voice said, glancing over her shoulder at the driver of her automobile. Another feminine voice from inside the car responded, "Shoot him again. I want to be sure. You know what the boss said." The gunwoman turned her body back to the car and pointed the gun down at Devin. Devin wondered if he would feel anything or if he would go in peace. The gun cocked back… But the bullet never left the gun. A shot from off in the distance knocked the gun out of the woman's hand. Her eyes quickly rose up to look for whoever managed to accomplish that feat, but she couldn’t determine a location. The second shot whizzed right by her left ear, causing the woman to turn around and rush back into her car. The driver floored it away from the scene, leaving Devin and his car in a destructive heap. For the longest time, Devin didn’t move because he had no idea what in the hell was going on above him. ------------------- Elsewhere… The phone at Christian Daniels house rang and he quickly picked up the receiver and put it to his ear. "What’s the word, Rach, Devin alright?" Apparently news traveled damn fast "The car pulled up beside him and someone stepped out to off him. I got to 'em before any extensive damage could be done." "The Braintrust did this to him I’ll bet." "That’s the theory." "Motherfuckers, alright call the ambulance and bounce." She dialed 911 before driving off into the darkness. "A totaled car at mile marker 178 just before Exit 13B, in the ditch, the driver inside will require medical attention but his injuries are not life threatening." Rachel Smith, the lone Smith family member assigned to watch over Devin Shakur during the war, had just saved his life.
