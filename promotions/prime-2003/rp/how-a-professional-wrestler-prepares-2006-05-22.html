How a professional wrestler prepares

22 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 22 ### Disclaimer: Most of this will be untelevised. There will be a lot of spots where Gideon and/or Neil are not being wrestlers. I’m going to be using the camera as a 3rd person to be introduced to the scenes when relevant. This is to show Neil and Gideon off camera as well. By separating things into televised and non televised Neil Whittaker and Neil Fracas act similar but are different in many ways while Gideon will consistently be a ruthless asshole. ### Ultra Violence was an interesting PPV. There was no expectations of doing anything but as they say “S#$t happens”. There were some rumors on the Internet and there was speculation that certain people might show up. As you can tell most rumors and that are just that rumors with no more foundation or fact then a republican promise. You can list the names by the dozens that were rumored to appear. People that have been kicked out of feds. People that became free agents due to feds going bankrupt. People that came out of rehab and want to prove themselves again for a fan base that abandoned them. People that are such big assholes and egomaniacs they flutter to whatever fed will put up with their antics. People that are born again of born a 3rd time. Of course there is the Alliance’s favorite, Movie stars just looking for a TV spot to get exposure. Neil and Gideon have been this long road of hell many times. They have come to PRIME after talks with Motoki. This was to be their fresh start and their chance to make wrestling mean something again. This was to be their legacy to professional wrestling to once again make wrestling matter once again. This was not going to happen. Though there is a glimmer of hope. Gideon had a match with Lindsay Troy. He had heard of her before in other feds and for a female wrestler to draw his attention there has to be something to her. He saw one of her matches in another federation. He hadn’t expected her there dismissing the rumors on the net to be just like the other rumors that have no facts behind them. Then of course they talked. Neil referred to her as a self-absorbed bitch after they talked. Gideon has heard enough about her that he has some doubts of that being totally true but has to admit she was a total bitch to her when all he was doing was being polite and introducing himself. Of course there was no way that Gideon was letting disrespect like that slide. You want to talk shit and run your mouth then prepare for a long night. They had a match later and to say that he was impressed is putting it lightly. He did not hold back because he knew he didn’t have to. Neil knows full well what a woman wrestler can do. His wife Stephanie took the fight to you like few men could do. Gideon on the other hand has had no actual experience with a woman wrestler of that caliber. Sure he knows Stephanie and what she is capable of but actually experiencing it is another. To actually see that she can compete on a level like that is a shock because you kind of assume that the woman wrestlers were made to look good rather then them actually being that good. It’s a reflex action out of years of seeing women wrestlers meaning next to nothing. Their match was a draw and Gideon enjoyed it having an actual wrestling match for the first time in years and actually trading a few heavy strikes. Of course that glimmer of hope having a new tag team with an actual legitimate wrestler was crushed and buried by reality when the A-List became the tag champions. This was a cluster F$%k from the very start. Everyone was saying the A-List was getting the tag titles so you know there was something that “wasn’t quite right”. The Alliance had their cluster f#$k tag match to see who would be the first of 2 teams to face the tag champs. That match wasn’t even aired. That is how important that match was. The Alliance didn’t even know how Titan won the match. They just heard that he was announced the winner. Then of course comes the Tag title match. Danny Ferguson’s good ole buddy T-Nel came through in spades. It was never “official” but you could see the fix was in. Tchu was banned before the match started. Think about that. A tag team champion BANNED from defending his titles. Then you have Titan St. James VS Clint Poteet and the A-List to win the tag titles. Were you betting on Titan beating 3 men by himself to get the tag titles? Watch the match. There was absolutely no contact between the tag champion Clint Poteet and the A-List. It was just 3 men taking on Titan St. James the entire match. The closest it ever came to “fair” was when Tchu took Clint Poteet out of the match. The A-List won the match. T-Nel’s good ole buddy Danny Ferguson finally got the belt he “earned”. Neil and Gideon come from the days where you earned the titles you got. If you were the “man of 1001 holds” you got the respect that was due for a man of your skills. Now you have a man that is a tag champ that is the “man of 1001 rest holds”. How does a man like that become a champion? Is it through hard work and dedication? There are other ways to get to the top. There will always be the easy path when wrestling means nothing in professional wrestling. Now they prepare for the match this week. ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### Neil and Gideon are at the Fontaine mansion in L.A. preparing for the match. Gideon has found out earlier that there has been a card change. It was tentatively supposed to be the Alliance VS Business and Pleasure. Gideon was looking forward to this match a hell of a lot. Of course things fell through. There were commitments that made it so Mr. Melton could not make the TV taping. Troy and Neil were booked in singles instead and Gideon has the week off. Neil and Gideon are going to drill and drill and drill so Neil is working like a finely oiled machine……… Neil: Do we have any promo plans? Gideon: Would it really matter? Neil: Good point. Neil and Gideon are walking around a wrestling ring in Gideon’s gym. There is a lot of weights and heavy bags as well as other training gear. This is not about muscle mass however. Neil lunges to take Gideon down with a dbl leg sweep. Gideon tries to counter but as fast and good as Gideon is Neil has already anticipated the counter and the counter to his counter. Neil thinks farther ahead then most. He knew how Gideon would counter the shoot. He also knew how Gideon would counter his counter to the shoot. Neil took it one step farther then that. He countered Gideon’s counter to his counter and took Gideon down. Neil is a top notch grappler and when he shoots a takedown it is very hard to stop him. If he had the discipline he likely could have competed on an Olympic level. Gideon: Thought I had you stopped on that one Neil. Neil: I know you too well Gideon. I knew you had my shoot countered and I knew you had a counter to my countering your shoot. Gideon: (Laughing) I’ll take you down yet. There are 7 more tries but Gideon never quite takes down Neil though he came very close 4 times. Neil would be a pain in the ass to try and take down at his weight and size but Neil is a bigger and stronger man. The saying “size doesn’t matter” may be valid and heaven knows it has been proven many times in the past. There is another saying by Neil that goes like this. “In an even fight the big man will usually win”. This is an even fight. You might be wondering what drives Neil and Gideon to push themselves so relentlessly. One word pride. Neil: Let’s go for submissions I want to tap him out. Gideon: Ah. Now we go to my element. Neil goes for an armbar but Gideon manages to use a hip toss. Neil lands on his feet and drags Gideon down to the ground. As Neil starts to straighten out the arm for an attempt at a Painkiller armbar Gideon rolls and uses his other arm to break the grip. Both Neil and Gideon get to their feet. Neil: Nice Gideon: Not a good idea to try and catch me in my own submission hold. Neil: I’m pretty damn good with the Painkiller myself. Neil goes for a single leg takedown and then starts trying to flip Gideon overwhile holding the kneebar on his left leg. Gideon blocks the attempt and with his free leg he swings it at the back of Neil’s right shin. While Neil is staggered Gideon gets up on his right leg. Neil goes for a legdrag on the left leg but Gideon rolls with it getting to his feet. Gideon: Are you prepared for the luchadore moves? Neil: Yup He goes for the flying head scissors I can counter it with a powerbomb, Style’s Clash, of Hangman’s powerbomb. Probably end up snapping Cruz’s neck if I hit him with the Hangman’s powerbomb. Neil and Gideon go over to a pedestal near the treadmills that is about 3 feet high. Neil stands in front of it and jumps to the top. He hops off and goes flat on the ground. He goes from flat on the ground to his feet then hops to the top of the pedestal. Neil keeps repeating this over and over drilling it. Gideon: He tries for a low dropkick jump it and land on him with an elbow or splash. Neil: Yup Neil keeps on drilling going from the floor to standing to high jumping to the top of the pedestal. The speed that Neil is doing this is unbelievable for a man his size. Neil is nearly all muscle and works out heavily. While other wrestlers may take shortcuts to build muscle Neil has been doing this for years and never needed a crutch. He hit the weights and hit them hard to build up more and more power. He did road work and things to increase his agility and speed knowing that size and power without sped is nothing. Still anyone sees a guy Neil’s size they go into the roid references because so many people have done it before. Neil: We have been drilling for a couple days now. You think Cruz is going to be that big a threat? Gideon: I only know a little about him and most of that is second hand at best. I know this. If you are fully ready you will be very damn hard to beat. I don’t think he will be prepared for anyone like you. Neil: We can’t afford a loss. Any idea about the tag titles? Gideon: Truthfully the tag titles mean so little that I couldn’t give a damn what happens to the things. What happened to the tag titles at Ultra Violence was pretty much a funeral for the tag titles prestige and credibility. Even if you manage to completely gloss over the fact that Face Eater is a jackoff you still have T-Nels good buddy, best friend, and god knows what else Danny Ferguson. The man is literally the master of 1001 restholds and thinks he is actually a great wrestler. Neil: The belts mean nothing to me either. They might as well have a F$%kin funeral for the tag titles if they are going to shit on them that much. Neil continues going through the reps and you can see that he is starting to sweat a bit from the exertion. That doesn’t stop Neil because that is the way he works. Give all that your body can then just continue giving more and more. Build your endurance by going past your limits more and more a little each day. Neil: I’m going to take a shower. Gideon: Toss your workout clothes in the hamper and I’ll have Jessica have them ready for the next time you are over. There are fresh towels there. I’ll give you about half an hour while I make a few phone calls. Neil: Sure. When Neil walks off towards the shower room Gideon walks towards his study. On the way he makes a phone call on his cell phone. Gideon: Amanda have you uncovered any information yet? Amanda: Yes it wasn’t a serious injury he should be ok. Gideon: Good. How reliable is the information? Amanda: It was in the medical report submitted to his insurance. Gideon: Good. Thanks for being so quick. Amanda: (Laughing) No problem that’s what you pay me the big bucks for. Tell Neil I said hi. Gideon: (Laughing) Of course. Gideon is in his study looking at various things on the computer and thinking about many things while waiting for Neil to get done showering. Catherine is still visiting Liz. He has heard that someone is actually crazy enough to sign Caleb Alan to a wrestling contract. You wonder just what is going on in the world these days if Caleb Alan is actually approached by a wrestling promoter to work a match. Then he thinks about PRIME and the tag team championships. They are going to have to make some plans and call in a few markers. Gideon gets his cell phone and dials a number while waiting. While he is waiting for an answer on the other line he is thinking he isn’t going to believe who is calling him up. Gideon: Hello……… With those words a very interesting conversation starts……… ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### Neil and Gideon are in L.A. going to see a friend of theirs Caleb Alan. When Neil heard that he was signed to a new promotion starting up he thought there was a punchline. Then he realized that Gideon wasn’t kidding someone actually is nuts enough to sign him. The Alliance and Caleb Alan have a bit of history. In professional wrestling friends are rare and Caleb is a friend. Unfortunately Caleb is also a very dangerous psychopath and complete womanizing jackass. This is Caleb’s “off time” at his penthouse suite. The room smells of sex and Caleb is sitting on the couch in a bathrobe drinking a glass of wine smiling. Caleb: Ah Gideon and Neil. Welcome. Are you sure you are still not willing to join me in entertaining some of my many fans. Gideon: There comes a time when every man settles down Caleb. Caleb: Of course. Of course. I hope that you will say those very words the day I pass away. They are so heartfelt and sincere I believe that you actually believe them. Neil: So what’s up with the wrestling gig anyway. Caleb: Pretty much the standard. Some guy had heard that the Canadian Heart Throb is not wrestling and decided to make his federation great by hiring me. It is a flawless strategy and will bring in millions of female fans. Gideon: What about the psychiatrist you had to deal with your anger issues? Caleb: She decided I was very well adjusted? Gideon: WHAT!?! Neil: Take it you overlooked the “she” part Gid. Gideon: Let me guess you nailed her. Caleb: I must admit her breasts weren’t all that great but you know how I love to make time for each and everyone of my female fans. Well barring the ugly ones of course because truthfully I only have so much time and I prefer to leave the ugly ones for their husbands. Gideon: Do they know about your anger problems? Caleb: Nonsense I do not have any anger issues Gideon. Though I do seem to have horrible luck when it comes to opponents. I am so physically gifted and well trained that a lesser man simply gets hurt trying to compete on a level he cannot ever hope to obtain. I have never understood what it must be like for someone to have to face a man as great as myself but I have accepted it like the poodle that runs after the bus in a vain attempt at personal glory when the bus will never even acknowledge it’s existence. Neil: Have you done any training? Caleb: Train? My god Neil you really need to get out more. Have you seen what passes for a professional wrestler these days? My god the obesity of these wrestlers today is truly appalling. I can understand them not being in as great a shape as men like ourselves but my god some of these men haven’t seen their toes since they looked at their baby pictures and even that is a secondhand account. Gideon: Any idea when you are debuting? Caleb: I have people that keep track of things like that. I really can’t be bothered to keep track of all those trivial little details. The tireless hours I devote to meeting the fans alone leaves me very little time for meaningless details. I’d invite you gentlemen to stay longer but you know how draconian these fire laws are Gideon. If you allow more then 50 people in the room it is considered a crime. Neil: I know I’m going to regret asking. Anyone you took notice of? Caleb: Likely but my god once you go through the bother of trying to remember a name they feel like it is a commitment and they start stalling the line up wanting to be friends or something like that. Truly I could never understand the friends bit myself but if it is someone they can talk to after we are done then it is a good idea. Heaven knows I can’t waste my time talking to everyone after I am done or I’d never get to the end of the line. Gideon: Later Caleb. Give us a shout when you debut. Caleb: But of course. The return of the Canadian Heart Throb will likely be documented by every major news show. After all wrestling history will be made and I suspect that billions of women will once again have a reason to dream again hoping that one day that I may grace their home town to spend some time. As the Alliance walks away Caleb continues to drink from his glass of wine. Neil is shaking his head and Gideon is rolling his eyes. On the elevator ride to the lobby Gideon turns towards Neil. Gideon: I pity the poor SOB that signed him. Neil: I pity your lawyers trying to keep him out of jail. Gideon: It’s like a car wreck. You know that there is going to be pain and suffering and things went horribly wrong but you look at it anyway and try and figure out how in the world something like that ever happened. Neil: Then you realize that people are more stupid then you ever imagined could ever be possible. Gideon: Got George Bush a second term. Neil: Why I gave up on voting. ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### ##### Neil and Gideon are in the PRIME arena. They appear to be a bit angry. It has been a long trip and they have been thinking things over all during the trip. As soon as they are given the countdown they are prepared to let loose. Gideon: We have to cut a promo for Neil’s match. As of today we are no longer wrestling as a tag team in PRIME. We do not want anything to do with the tag titles. To the tag team or two that chooses to stay good luck. Over the past few years in this business a tag belt has meant absolutely nothing and has been something that owners toss their friends. This has not changed any in PRIME. Neil: We aren’t playing the politics game to get ahead and we do not kiss any ass. Gideon: This will give Neil something to look forward to in his match against Cruz. As you can tell from Johnny Noble’s focus on his singles career anyone that can escape to singles will. Those that stay here simply bide their time until they can go into singles. Neil: We know that Motoki isn’t going to be happy hearing about us breaking up the Alliance. Gideon: We were signed as the Alliance and with that no longer being the case Neil Fracas VS Damien Cruz may be our last appearance here. If that is the way it ends assume that any talks between us and Toshiaki Motoki did not end well. Neil and Gideon just walk away not saying anything more and let the camera fade to black after they leave the room.
