A reason to hate (CS Halo RP)

10 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-10-05 Enrique Morales sits in the bed of his hotel room examining a picture. A picture that unlocks memories to his past. A past of both excitement and disappointment. Memories of a time when the world seemed perfect and nothing could go wrong. A picture of bliss...of true happiness...of a world that is all on a equal plane. A picture... A picture of a young, vibrant, beautiful spanish woman floods his thoughts. Her dark brown hair flowing past her shoulders like the mane of a horse running in the wind, bouncing with full body and life. Her emeral green eyes sparkling so bright they seem to be shinning a light into his very soul, making his heart skip and dance as if he were still sixteen years old and awaiting the moment that he finally sealed his manhood. The feel of her full, luscious lips as they pressed softly against his own...sweeping away any and all other thoughts, other than those of that very moment he is currently in. His only thought being that he wished that this moment would last for the rest of his life. Nothing every changing. No one ever moving, walking away, or leaving this very moment. That very moment when he knew, without a doubt, he had found the one and only true soul mate. HIS...one and only...true soul. True love had finally pierced his otherwise very cold heart. Her love had made him feel, trust, and believe in any and all dreams. Everything in this world could be made possible if you only believed in it with all the fire and passion that love brings with it. His dreams...his passions...his accomplishments...her love. They all paled in comparison to how bright her love shined down upon his very being...as if he was the only other person on earth. 'I love you Enrique...and I always will,' the words resounding through every single fiber of his body as he stood there smiling. Her white dress fitting her perfectly in all the right spots and the smile on her face warming his heart. 'I love you as well Maria. Always have and will forever till my dying day,' were the words that he had spoke to her. Complete sincerity, honesty, and trust in every single word. He had never loved another person in his entire life, outside of his family, and that wasn't this type of love. No other woman had been able to reach him the way she had. Connecting with him on every possible level that a man and woman can connect on. They say that there is always one perfect match for every single person in the world...that one person to complete their being and fullfill their dreams. Maria was that woman to Enrique. She is the woman he loved, even if he didn't realise it at first, from the very moment he laid his eyes upon her. The daughter of a successful business man in Mexico City had been able to draw Enrique to her like nothing else had other than wrestling. Wrestling had always been his passion, his life, and his reason for getting up every single day. Maria changed all that. Wrestling wasn't gone, of course, it just wasn't the only reason to get out of bed every morning anymore. It wasn't the only thing that drove his very soul any longer. Wrestling had become a means of making life as easy as possible for the one person that truly mattered to him...Maria. 'You don't have to become some huge star to please me,' she had told him. Her eyes conveying that she was being completly honest with him. The Enrique that she had married only a mere few months prior to this moment was more than enough for her. He had always been more than enough for her, even if he was just some other luchadore on the card in his fathers promotion. 'You don't understand Maria...I don't do things cause I have to. I am doing this cause I want to. Becoming a huge star in a big time promotion in North America will insure that you will never want for another thing in all your life,' he had explained to her. Holding her tightly in his arms, allowing her to rest her head on his shoulder. Just feeling her against his body, standing in the bright sun of that day, made him more happy than he could have ever hoped to be in his entire life. 'Enrique...you promise you won't forget what important to you. You won't forget what really matters to us,' she had questioned through tearful eyes. Enrique had smiled at her while wiping the tears from her cheeks. 'I'll never forget that your the most important thing to me. Being faithful, my dear, will never be in question with me. Your the only senorita for me and that is how it has always been,' he had told her. She had shook her head, knowing that he was true in his words. Enrique Morales had never been a man to lie. When he told you he would do something, he did it. When he said he was going to accomplish something...thats exactly what he did. With those thoughts and traits in his mind Enrique had hoped on a plane to come to the United States. A short term deal with the World Class Wrestling Association had been offered, with the chance to move more into a full time contract...should Enrique provide the goods. The goods were something that Enrique realised that he could no doubt provide, and in doing so he would do it in a way that no other Spanish wrestler had ever done so. On his own terms. Things were beatiful in his life during that time. He had the love of the most beautiful and perfect senorita in the entire world, a contract with WCWA for a shot to make it big, and the fire and drive inside of him to accomplish it all. Making it big in WCWA was a sure catalyst to being called up to the big time company in the area Lone Star Wrestling Association. That is where Enrique would position himself as the top draw out of Mexico in the history of professional wrestling. 'I've been called up Maria. My dream of giving you everything you can dream of is at the tip of my fingers,' he could clearly remember telling her over the phone. His voice and just jumped with joy and enthusiasm. He'd been away for merely four months, even though to the two of them it did feel like a lifetime, and he was already on the way to where he had always wanted to be. A star...THE STAR. 'I'm so proud of you Enrique. I love you so much and I can't wait until we're able to be together again,' she had said with as much joy as possible. She was proud of her husband, no doubt, but her longing to be with him was tough. He could feel the tears filling up her eyes, coming through the phone in the way the words had rolled off her tongue. 'Do not cry, Maria, for soon you'll be by my side for good. I love you my darling and I always will,' came the words from his mouth, soothing both of their hurts in one motion. The rise up the ranks of LSWA was a slow one, but a profitable one. While he wasn't shooting to the top of the card as quickly as he had hoped, the money he was drawing was more than enough to move Maria to Texas to be with him. They were enjoying life and all the fruits that his hard fought labor was bringing to them. He was a star, to many, and he was raking up the wins despite a few bumps in the road. But then came the curve ball that started his downward spiral. The word from the top of the LSWA that he'd be let go...if he didn't let go of his principles. 'You were fired,' Maria had questioned in total shock the night he came home and told her. His body was still numb over the fact that this was actually happening to him...to them. His perfect world had taken a serious blow, yet he remained optomistic. 'Well I was pushed out, you would say. It was either go back on everything I believe and become some comic side show luchadore, or be let go. I should have thought more about it, I know, and you should have been consulted before I decided to take the latter...but Maria,' he had begun to explain before she put her fingers on his lips and smiled warmly into his face. Her radiant smile was always something that could brighten his demenour, even in the darkest of hours. 'Sssshhh. It's not important. I respect your decision Enrique. Truth be told I was ready to go home to Mexico anyway,' she had told him as she sat down on the bed next to him. A look of confusion had covered Enriques face as he sat there staring at his beautiful wife. He hadn't even thought for one moment about going back home over this one minor set back. 'Maria...I'm not going back to Mexico over this. One set back is no reason for me to give up,' Enrique had told her, tears now welling up in his eyes for one of the very few times in his life. No way could he face his family or even begin to continue considering himself a proud man if he tucked his tail and ran like a coward back to his home land. No, on bigot wasn't going to force him out of the business...that Enrique was sure of. 'Enrique you said yourself that he was going to black ball you. That if you weren't willing to be a luchadore, other American promoters simply weren't going to take a chance on you,' she had pleaded. Maria was scared to death that her husband would risk his entire career trying to please American promoters that were never going to give him a fair shake. 'He said that...yes...but we're going to prove him wrong,' Enrique had responded with his own warm, giant, loving smile. She once again had rested her head on his shoulders and given into the fact that she would back her husbands decision. She was beginning to doubt his chances in the United States, but she would stand proudly behind him none the less. Proving someone wrong has been the theme of Enrique's career since it began. The constant stereotyping of him and his Spanish heritage with in the United States scene was one that he was willing to face. The willingness to face that problem, however, began to cause more and more problems within his personal life. As he professional life continued to go along with very strained results for a couple of years...a booking here and there...being told that they could only use him if he would change his style...the questions of whether he would be willing to dawn a colorfull mask...all these things were taking a toll on his personal life. A major toll that would finally come to a head on a cold, dark, and painful night. 'Enrique it's time to stop! We're going back to Mexico,' Maria had shouted the moment he walked in the door, a busted open head, and the word that despite his superior performance, his services were not needed with his current look. 'I'm tired of you killing yourself for this country only to have them spit in the face of it with stupid bigotry,' she had exploded into him with her full wrath. Up until this point she had been willing to back down, to try and see his side. Not this on this night, however, and this was the last straw. 'Maria I will not give up. I simply can't let what a few promoters say dictate how I respond. Did you see the show, did you see the way the crowds continue to go crazy for my performances? It's not about being a luchadore, just cause I am Spanish, Maria and I believe those fans are proving it,' he had shot back to defend his stubborn nature. The fans, true, were behind him one hundred percent despite what the promoters were telling him. He wasn't wrestling like a luchadore but he was taking an insane amount of risks to prove that he could get over with the crowds without resorting to that sort of stuff. 'Those fans don't love you, Enrique, I DO! They only cheer cause your willing to give your body for them every night. They aren't behind you cause they believe in your cause, they are behind you so they can see you do something to make them shout that ridiculas holy shit chant all the time,' she had screamed back. There was no changing her mind on this night. Enrique had to leave North America and it had to be now. 'You're just jealous that I am proving them all wrong,' Enrique responded while exploding to his feet. They had rarely fought in their entire time together but now it appeared they were on the brink of a huge blowout. 'Jealous? Is that what you think? Your a fool Enrique. A damned fool and you're refusing to notice that your losing everything around you that was suppose to be important to you...TO US,' she had screamed at the top of her lungs. By this point Enrique was enraged. He didn't like being called a fool, never had, but had really begun to hate the phrase after the owner of LSWA had refered to him as a fool when he refused to change his style for him. 'You're just a dumb bitch whose ready to give up,' Enrique shot back. A pain shot through his head, both from the fact that he instantly regretted saying that, but also from the slap of her hand across his face. His anger was still boiling, however, and he couldn't allow himself to admit he was wrong. 'Enrique you have to make a choice, right now. It's either going to be trying to make this stupid pig headed theory of yours true, or it's me. You no longer get to try and juggle both, Enrique, it has to be one or the other,' she demanded with her hands on her hips. Shock rang through his entire body. He knew she was unhappy with the risks he was taking to make his point, but he had never expected this. 'I don't believe what,' he had begun before she cut him off in a fit of anger. 'NOW ENRIQUE! CHOOSE!,' she had exploded once more. He stood there with pain in his heart and soul. He knew what he should choose, in fact he knew what he really wanted to choose, but his pride wouldn't let him. He stood there, silent. 'I see,' was all she said as she turned around and walked out of the room to their bedroom where she slammed the door behind her. With tears welling up in his eyes Enrique had slumped back down into the chair in their living room. He could hear her sobbing in there as she spoke to her mother on the phone, making arrangements to come back home and file for the end of their life together. Suddenly Enrique awoke and shot straight up into a sitting position in his bed at the hotel once again. While looking at the picture of his lovely former wife he had fallen asleep and drifted into dreams of the past. Some of them wonderful and some of them very painful. Much like the way his professional career was going at the moment...some good moments and some that weren't so pleasant. 'Damn the fans,' Enrique mumbled to himself as he sat in the bed staring off into the vast black night that filled his room. The only light in the room was that of the a light red from the glowing light illuminating off the numbers on the bed side clock. Enrique ran his hands through his long dark hair as he tried to shake off the thoughts that were once again plaguing him. 'Happens every damn time there is a set back,' the muffled words of Enrique came forth and were lost in the darkness as quickly as they left his lips. No one there to reassure him that he was still fighting the good fight. No one there to yell at him for making some selfish decisions. Over the last several years he had learned to be comfortable with the complete lonelyness that the darkness always brought with it. 'Complete isolation...in the cold of the night and the dark...all for the love of those damned racist American fans,' Enrique began to kick himself at the thought of his decision making. Trying to prove so long ago that the fans weren't ignorant bigots, but people who truly did back him had, at least in his mind, caused him to lose the one thing that truly mattered to him in his life. The love of his life and the pure joy she had brought to him were gone, and had been for several years now, yet he still longed for the ability to return to that night and change his past. But that is the thing about your past, your unable to change it. As much as Enrique knows he would give anything to change the way things went in his life, he also knows that it simply isn't going to ever be possible. The moment that fact sunk into his skull was the very moment that he shut out most of the rest of the world. Entering into the cold and bitter state that he now lived his life in...his only true happiness in seeing that Los Diablos made all the fans of the wrestling world pay for what they did to him. Pay for the price they forced him to take part in for their love and understanding. Los Diablos was his salvation...his ultimate weapon to strike revenge at those who had wronged him...by any means necassary his 'boys' would take apart any and all things that the fans took pride and joy in. He would take their love from them. Take their souls from them. Take everything they wished, hoped, and dreamed and flush them down the toliet. He would do so for... 'Maria,' came his final words before drifting off to sleep once more.
