eVERYBODY'S gOT oNE

30 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30*09/05 eVERYBODY'S gOT oNE 'Uck-pweh!' As Karina lifted the black, red and silver crash helmet, it hit her nostrils, instantly. The back of a gloved hand guarded her nostrils, using the tangy scent of leather to hep cut through the decaying, organic stench that wafted across the parking lot from the sewerage treatment works. Only 50 feet or so of neglected wasteland separated the so-called arena from the unsavoury neighbour, and the discarded bottles, vehicle parts and furniture did little to absorb the putridly sweet odour. As Kari's face soured, her tongue pressed hard against the roof of her mouth, trying to force the decaying flavours from her taste buds as lips pressed defiantly closed. 'Sometimes I need to slap myself...' was heard only as a mumble outside of the K-Wolf's thoughts, which became increasingly negative with haste. Resting the helmet on the seat of her scarlet Ducati, Karina freed her rucksack from its restraints, then contorted her shoulder through the tight straps, wriggling within her leathers... which she zipped up a little further as protection against the unappealing environment. Still encased within their gloves, Kari's ran her hands through choppy blonde and pink hair, trying to tackle the tufting of her mane. It took her all of 5 seconds to form an opinion, which given stubbornness that made the most stuck-in-his ways Ox sigh, pretty much set it in stone. A hair tie twirled between fingers, before restraining all but Kari's pink fringe, which cascaded down past startling emerald eyes, and over prominent cheekbones. With the early evening sun hanging depressingly in the sky, as if what was over the horizon was an even bleaker backdrop, Karina rested mirrored sunglasses upon the bridge of her twice-broken nose. 'And here I was thinking I'd never self harm,' danced around Kari's thoughts as her lithe frame extended fully, stretching out the slump that had come hand-in-hand with a 4 hour ride to the show. The air seemed murky, leaving a jaundiced-tinge to the dull brickwork of the building, while a heat-haze rippled through Kari's vision. After patting her bike on its rear as a parting gesture, the K-Wolf took a gulp, let out a sigh through her nostrils, then set off towards the entrance. Sneaker soles felt bogged down in the tacky parking lot asphalt, while no other vehicles around the building seemed remotely road-worthy... mainly due to tape and rope being used as bonding materials in most. 'I should have asked what shots I needed when I took the booking,' her thoughts scathed as Kari began to get too close to the building for comfort. There were no billboards, no names in lights, just a chalkboard sign with the promotion's name - WFW - scrawled on in a way that could have made it a scruffy logo, or a lazy scribble. Seeing her name chalked in below couldn't help but make the K-Wolf feel as dirty as the entrance's windows, furred with dust. No matter how many times she'd butted heads with PRIME management, nor how many times she'd clashed with PTC over expenses, it was moments like this that made her truly grateful for what she'd go back to the next day. Just to be awkward, in all honesty, it was made clear in her PRIME contract that she had freedom to book any non-conflicting Indy dates... and to justify it, well, here it was. No names written in the narrow gap under Kari's and the edge of the board rang even the remotest of bells with her. Admittedly, Kari was abysmal at remembering names, or even paying enough attention to the conversations to catch them, but these were nobodies. Not even fleeting with the bitter-sweet title of has-beens, and wallowing in the anonymity below even the never-weres. Mon$y McMahon-Helmsley, s00per Kr@zE, HaRdKorE lEgEnD, 'Tha Showstoppa' Bret Michaels, 'Big Poppa' Brock Goldberg, Co-Vice-Prez Commissioner Gary. Nobodies. PWC. OSW. RPW. PTC. tSC. PRIME. fWo. ACW. Connections had spread her across the wrestling world, daredevil displays had crawled her to the tips of everyone's tongues, name-value had crashed through the roof of anywhere but the 'name' federations, where natural talent had never left her short of gold. She was a somebody. 'I need a shower already...' continued her inner gripes as Kari hopped up the rusting steps towards the entrance doors. The travel information bundled in with the cheque - which she'd half expected to bounce - called the venue 'The Rowan Arena'. Apparently, Arena was used a little more loosely by some, and hardly seemed to fit in with appearence. Looking so short in stature than fans standing on their chairs would headbutt the ceiling, and unless it had tardis-like dimensions, barely enough room for mats around the ring, let alone fans and a backstage. This was going way beyond the likes of Hollywood actors returning to the stage, showing respect for their roots. This was like accepting a bit-part in their old school's nativity play. Non-speaking, of course. Most likely the Donkey. Moss had engulfed the building's mortar, criss-crossing dull brickwork with a brownish green, while the tracings of scrubbed-off graffiti left those who cared to squint reading every curse word under the sun. With a hint of a captured chuckle, the K-Wolf tried to tell herself that appearance wasn't everything. After all, a federation didn't need a flashing building, huge advertising hoardings, and a bunch of 'name' talent to put on a quality product, right? 'This place is a fucking hole...' Yeah, keep telling yourselves that.
