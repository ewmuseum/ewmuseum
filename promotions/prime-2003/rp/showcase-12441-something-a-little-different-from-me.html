Something a little different from me...

23 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Taken from a recent PRIME roleplay. II - I Dance Between Worlds. She was reborn of primal instinct. Millennia old, yet with no life to tell of. Like a newly born calf drawn to its mother's teat, she knew what it was that would quench the scorching acidity of her soul. Eyelids shot open to show burning balls of emerald flame, long having since incinerated her eyeballs. Sight wasn't needed, she could sense movements as they brushed against the fine bristles of her thoughts... like whiskers. She wasn't of this world, or any other world for that matter. She danced between worlds, swam between particles, and divided her existence between trillions of protons, electrons, and neutrons when she slept. She existed, and that was the ends and the means. The question and the answer. Starved of what she desired, the hunger no longer confined to her stomach, but creating a vacuum that agonised nerve endings, she let out an impassioned scream. Part unparalleled pain at missing the one thing that would make her whole, part releasing destructive sonar that levelled centuries old buildings as sonic waves crumbled their pathetic, steel foundations. "Where are you..?" she spoke, not through her mouth, but by resonating the air around her... letting existence to her talking. Rising to her feet, she then straightened up, a mane of blonde and pink skipping on the disturbed air, like smoke. The sonar she had released then hurtled back at her with destructive speed and force, blowing clouds and lightning bolts in her direction, and almost causing the gasses that made up the air around her to implode. "I know..." she then whispered, using the bristling rattle of the charred leaf litter as her mouth. Whether it was a mile or to the other side of the universe was inconsequential. Her thoughts, spanning the stars, where then pulled back in. Concentration centred on one spot, super-heating the air so that the oxygen split and burnt. A pin-point of pure, brilliant white appeared, sending ripples through the atmosphere surrounding it. "More..." was all she could comment, screwing her eyes up and stiffening her muscles to force the ripple to widen. Slowly, her arms began to move out to her sides, bones bending as the universe repelled them. Tendons threatened to snap, and ligaments barely held their grip as she conducted a deafening orchestra of raw, primal power. Her unrivalled desire tore a hole in the fabric of reality itself, causing the blinding white to consume her, then spit the creature back out at her instinctive coordinates. The void between time... tickled. The blast crater where her consciousness landed hinted that she was the end of life on the planet. It's atmosphere was raging with acid storms, and the seas were superheated to boiling point, choking the air with steam. Eyes opened once more, showing the flaming emerald that lit her path. As natural as the touch of skin against fingertips, she could feel the gold that her existence relied on. She was anaemic, incomplete without it. Anything that stood between it and her primal instinct was on borrowed time. One blink, one click of her fingers, and it would have never existed. "That's far enough," a mortal voice warned, stepping out from behind the rubble. Following him out was a wall of defiant human bodies. Bound to the earth, but heavenly at the same time. They had the power of Christ behind them, the guidance of almighty God, and the weapons of heaven at their disposal. But they didn't crave what she required, they simply wanted it. A trophy opposed to a beating, pumping internal organ. They stretched for over a mile in front of her, over a hundred ranks deep. Their faith was pure and unfaltering... but ultimately futile. A hand reached out to them, palm turned up. Transfixed, their curious eyes focussed on it. Like the initial sparks of fire when flint is flicked against itself, her fingers ushered in the flames. Contained in her grip, the fire grew, but was squeezed back together... focussing the flickering orange into a marble of scorching white. The gravity started to suck in dust and smoke, but the guardians stood firm. "The gold belongs to the RISP," was hurled in her direction, but the deafening physical forces ravaging the air around her swallowed the words whole. Unmoved by their protests, her fist then closed around the ball of raw fusion, before her wrist turned and hand unravelled into a wave. Before they'd even seen her fingers extend, their bodies and thoughts were consumed by the raging intensity of the heat. Nobody would ever know whether even their bones were reduced to a fine dust, or whether the unnatural heat simply wiped them off existence’s canvas without so much as a smudge. It was so close... tantalisingly brushing the extending whiskers of her thoughts, but always darting back to safety. No door, and barely any frontage at all remained of its housing. Leaving her free to stride in, pacing in the calm of the storm while all around her was whipped into the upper atmosphere to be digested by the acid sky. Her breathing caused the crumbling brickwork to pulse, sending a fine rain of plaster dust washing down over everything. The creaking of wooden beams mimicked the slight whistling from her nostrils, while the thumping of her heart cracked the concrete of the floor with its elevated pumping. As the flaws in the dusty, grey floor spread under her, her feet seemed to float her to safety, leaving her suspended in the air through pure concentration. Far from graceful as she hovered, her body was being ravaged by the primal forces threatening to erupt from it. The soles of her boots began to blister, spewing forth clouds of choking black smoke as they desperately tried to ground the power being channelled through her. Blonde and pink hair was standing on split ends, while eyebrows began to look singed as the emerald flame of her eyes licked at them. Through the dust storm, she could see the shimmer of gold as lighting punched down into the ground beside it. Her hand reached out, dust and dirt forming a paste with her perspiration as the violent winds mixed them. Fingers fired lightning that attached to the gold plating, causing the metal to buckle as she ripped it from its leather housing. Just being close to it she felt revitalised, like a shock to a dying heart, or pure oxygen being sucked into asphyxiated lungs. The raw force of creation, the birth of the universe, replayed itself as she grabbed the golden plate. Under her, the ground crumbled as the planet turned itself inside out, while the intense spike in gravity caused the sky to collapse.
