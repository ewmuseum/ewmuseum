His arm obliterated and now equpped with a sour demeanor to greet every stranger he’d met during his hospital stay, Sonny Silver had no choice but to stave off boredom by inaudibly counting some of the odd-looking pin-sized holes in the ceiling of the cab. “524… 525… 526… 527… Eww, what is that nasty white shit on the ceiling?” A pungent combination of Pine air freshener and some half-eaten turkey sandwich the cab driver had been working over in his spare moments filled the back seat, making Sonny wretch just a little bit. Sonny got his arms up in time and protected himself from nearly going face-first into the glass wall barricading the backseat, but a surge of pain traveled up his arm in the process. “AHHH!” Sonny let out a howl and grimaced as he started cradling his arm close to his own body like a parent protecting their child. Adjusting his legs to get more comfortable, Sonny spoke up again. “Yeah… This girl we were talking about not too long ago? Whenever I think about her, all I can see about her is that stupid smirk. She thinks she can get away with doing this to me and living to tell the tale. Sonny rubbed his temple with his good hand and glanced up at the driver. “You’re…” He stammered. “You’re kidding, right?” “I kid you not. You speak of her like she’s somebody you still care about in some way if she riles you up this much. So what do you plan on doing about it? Fighting with her doesn’t seem to be a likely answer, seeing that arm bothering you this much.” What COULD he do? He didn’t answer the question any further, instead choosing to shrug off the question. “I’ll… figure something out. Just… I dunno, wake me when we get there.” “Aye.” As Sonny drifted back in the rotten-smelling cab, his angered boiled over in his head. He had to settle his nerves somehow and he needed to do it quickly. He let the ideas of revenge cross his mind and eventually, anger surpassed sleep and carried him off into the Land of Nod. This, of all times, was no time to doubt one’s self. Today was going to be the first day of the rest of Sonny Silver’s life. It was time to reconstruct himself. First, his hand. Then, his body. Soon, his career. ~~~~~ The seconds became minutes, minutes became hours, days, weeks, months, and years as I searched the high heavens for somebody with half the strength and courage you exude.
