Into The Abyss: Part IV - My Way Or The Highway

2 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-02-09 His face ached as he held back the tears. His jaw was on the verge of cramping up as he grit his teeth and he held his sunken, bruised eyes shut so tightly that ghostly images in bold splotches of purple and grey flew around the insides of his eyelids. Von Kelsig's face was filled with pain that he had brought upon himself, but even as his mandible cramped up and a ball of tangled muscle bulged beneath the skin it could not save him from the creeping euphoria that crept up his legs through his toes and swirled around his torso like a lingering orgasm. For a few torturous moments the feeling washed over his body while his head throbbed in agony, and finally the tears came as a rush of energy forced its way up his throat and into his brain, massaging itself into the nerves and finally overcoming all his mental defences. A twitch of his mouth caused the cramping to stop with a surge through the muscle that tickled and itched, and as he opened his eyes he knew he had finally succumb to the deadly allure of the drug that had been pumped into his system for god only knew how long. "How are we feeling now, friend?" Came the raspy welcome. Reuben stood in front of him, propping himself up on a table stood between the two giants. Rolls of flab and a large belly pressed on the thin silk of a custom shirt adorning his ample frame, reminding Von Kelsig of a sack of laundry. The initials 'RB' were embroidered onto a breast pocket, and together with a fresh pair of black trousers, suede shoes and an expensive-looking overcoat hanging off his shoulders, Reuben looked like a prize slob indeed. Von Kelsig's head rolled on his shoulders, his eyelids fluttering momentarily as a warm rush of pleasure rolled through his body again. "Sure is something, huh? For a day or two there I didn't think you'd cave, but by god I'm glad I'm here and dressed in my Sunday best to see you go over the edge so spectacularly!" His sarcasm grated Von Kelsig's nerves momentarily before another wave of ecstacy came over him. He chortled and a sliver of drool escaped the corner of his mouth, which he wiped away with his thumb and rubbed off on his thigh. Von Kelsig groaned as adrenaline began coursing through his veins at a phenomenal pace, stopping suddenly and bringing with it another rush that stirred in his loins and threatened to spring an erection on him. Doing nothing to stop it he watched as Reuben moved his hand over something lying on the table. "Today is a new day Von Kelsig," Reuben paused, his eyes narrowing, "One you're going to remember for a long time, my friend!" He began arranging the objects on the table now as Von Kelsig's head lolled forward and he glanced at his hands, still bound palms-up on the arm rests of the chair. On several occasions they had been treated with lotion and were routinely bandaged, while a regular dose of pills had eased his suffering, albeit adding to the reeling nausea that came with a fresh hit of smack. Gone were the oozing blisters and blackened flesh, his fingers now covered in scabs and fresh scar tissue, and as he thought of the girl that had administered his antibiotics the threat of the erection grew until his pants bulged. "I gotta say I was relieved when I heard your condition had changed. If I had come in here and seen you shitting yourself one more time I was ready to just take you out back and put a bullet in your head. You'll be glad you finally cracked now that you know that, hm?" Reuben rubbed his hands together while Von Kelsig held a straight face, too overwhelmed by this new feeling to really take in what his captor was saying. Gradually though he came around, noticing first that he was no longer sitting in a mire of waste. Somehow he was clean, and wearing a fresh pair of jeans that could only have belonged to Reuben. Seeing his hostage take note of this, an equine whinny of laughter escaped Reuben's throat. "Oh yeah, you noticed you're all fresh and clean now? Just goes to show how fucked up you are that you didn't even remember being moved to get this place smelling sweet again. Just don't get any crap on my clothes or you'll know all about it pal!" The friendly tone he took made Von Kelsig stir, holding a gaze in Reuben's direction and paying more attention now. It was then that he saw what Reuben had been playing around with on the table. There in front of him was a byro next to a piece of paper that looked like a contract of some kind. Next to those however was something that made Von Kelsig's stomach turn. A syringe, bigger than the ones that had been used on him so far and filled with a carefully measured larger dose of the lethal drug he was slowly lusting after. The quiet groan escaping his throat quickly rose to a pained howl. Reaching forward, Reuben slapped Von Kelsig hard across the face, the sensation leaving him stunned silent as the two-inch jerk of his head to the side felt like a hundred mile trip on a tidal wave of rainbow-coloured lava. "Hush now. As you can see I have a little proposition laid out for you, and I'm fairly confident we can strike a deal here..." Von Kelsig shifted in the seat, trying to move away from the table. The cable ties only dug into his wrists and ankles as he struggled, and eventually he stopped and took a deep breath, letting the frustration go. "Now I left you in here and tried to condition you to accept your situation, and yet you fought against it. I was content to let you suffer then send you out to start repaying your debt to me. The more I thought about it though, the more I realized I'm going to be waiting a long damn time for my money with the amount you owe. So......I sent a friend of mine out to that little property you own downtown, and he did a little surveying and came back to me with this..." He paused, pointing to the contract. "What'urre you talkin' 'bout?" Von Kelsig slurred, leaning forward and reading the contract. "I'm talking about the nightclub you own. Remember? It was the last thing you saw before my boys carted you away and brought you here..." Reuben gestured to the word 'Underworld' typed in bold halfway down the contract, next to a large collection of numbers that could only have been the amount of debt owed. Ouch. His eyes scrolled down the contract as an image of Underworld came to him. The nightclub had been his pride and joy and was for four years a glorious home for the damned risen from the ashes of an abandoned office building. It was then he wondered what had become of the club since his kidnapping, realizing that someone must have surely realized something was wrong when it didn't open for business. "What happened to my club?" Von Kelsig said with more clarity as his head began to clear of the euphoric daze. "Oh don't worry, apparently it's closed for further renovations...and not one soul has given a shit since. Hell, I could keep you here for several months before anyone realized something was wrong!" Reuben chortled, reaching across and slapping Von Kelsig heartily on the shoulder. Staring blankly at the contract, hope seemed to drain away with the slowly fading high...until he caught sight of something written on the contract. A name. A name! Reuben Blanco. His heart skipped a beat as he repeated it to himself over and over. There in black and white was the declaration that the property hand-over would be made out to a man by the name of 'Reuben Blanco', of 'Blanco Holdings'. Finally he had the name of his captor! Suddenly everything was in perspective and thoughts ran clear in his mind. The only assurance he had that this was the name of the fat bastard standing in front of him and torturing him endlessly was a slip of the tongue. She had shown him kindness and helped him to heal after Reuben had burnt his hands. He realized then that he hadn't seen Gloria in some time, or else had been too comatose to remember her being there, though he recalled every little detail down to the last wiry hair on her head from their time together. It was her slip-up that gave him the information he needed. Taking a deep breath he exhaled and maintained his composure, making sure to give nothing away as Reuben continued. "But I don't want to waste my time and yours by holding you here when the solution has presented itself so clearly. You want out, and I want my money...so you can start by signing over the deed to that nightclub of yours, and let me tell you that will clear a nice chunk of debt away right from the get-go!" His words stunned Von Kelsig. Ignoring the hell he had endured so far, the thought of giving up his creation to this man sent a silent rage rippling through his bones. As far as he was concerned the debt had died with James Carlisle, and just because that little rat bastard was lying six feet under, Reuben saw fit to cash in on all the unclaimed debt from Carlisle's clients by squeezing Von Kelsig for all he had. He would have spat in Reuben's face and screamed "NEVER!" until his lungs gave out...if a little voice in his head hadn't reminded him of the syringe lying next to the damned contract. At once his mouth filled with saliva and his gums itched as the needle beckoned to him, to penetrate his veins and release him from the prison of his mind. Though he tried he could not shake the desire to tear himself free of his bonds and shoot up the sweet amber. The decision facing him was becoming clearer as the urge for the drugs became stronger. Obey Reuben or become a drug addict. The choice was clear, even though it was not his own. He had been forced into this, forced to give everything up or become a petty junkie. He couldn't believe he was going to have to choose but he knew he must, the only thing stopping him was the growing temptation to say no and take another hit of smack. But he had a name now! It wasn't fair, damn it! If he could only get out of here he could plan his revenge, all he needed was a name and he had it! As he struggled to think of a solution, Reuben had become a little impatient and cleared his throat. "Don't mean to push you big boy but I'm going to need an answer, I've got business to attend to shortly so don't expect this offer to be on the table for long." He folded his arms and began chewing on his thumbnail as Von Kelsig's heart pounded faster while his mind worked hard to find an answer. He had a name. "I, uhh..." He began. He had a name and a face. The only way out though was giving it all away to that man, then the information would be useless. If Von Kelsig was going anywhere it would be on his own terms. There had to be a way... "What happens if I don't sign the contract?" He muttered under his breath, to which Reuben clenched his teeth in frustration. He needed a way out! Reuben wasn't offering it. There had to be another way...some way to get out of these bonds. Out of this room. How? He had a name!! "Then nothing changes. You stay here and you stay high. I'm offering this now while the hunger is weak, but it will get stronger. Then I'll be back." Reuben replied. There was no way out without losing it all. He thought desperately for an idea, using what he had or what he knew! There had to be a way!! ".........No deal." The words suddenly escaped his mouth. Reuben shook his head, not quite believing what he had heard. No one had refused him before, not when faced with the same deal. No one. His mouth was slack and he quickly shut it tight, his eyes darting back and forth as he came to realize that his plan had failed. He was sure Von Kelsig had become too weak to negotiate, but how had he refused? Why? A red mist descended on him and suddenly Reuben roared, throwing his arms up and tipping the table over in front of Von Kelsig, the contract and syringe sent flying as he turned and threw a fist into the wall. A loud crack emanated from the punch, and Reuben turned back and threw another at Von Kelsig's face. He stopped as suddenly as he had exploded with anger, and saw that the syringe was lying on the floor at Von Kelsig's feet. Grabbing it, Reuben held Von Kelsig's arm down and carelessly jabbed the needle into the flesh, injecting the drug and tossing the syringe aside as a sliver of blood trickled from the tiny hole in his arm. Wincing in pain and still reeling from the punch, Von Kelsig groaned as Reuben stepped back and glanced at his watch, then quickly made his way to the door. "You'll regret that Von Kelsig, with your fucking LIFE you'll regret the day you turned me down!" With that he left the room, slamming the door shut with such force Von Kelsig felt a whoosh of air pass over him as the ground shook at his feet. Once more he was alone, and once more the drugs were creeping through his veins and crawling to his brain to forcibly release him of all his worries. Wrapped up in a blanket of thorns he was comfortably numb. He didn't give a damn though, didn't care that his decision would prolong the torture and drive him deeper into the poison womb of addiction. Until the very last possible moment before he had refused Reuben's offer the answer had finally come to him. He had a way out....he had a name. ...He had Gloria.
