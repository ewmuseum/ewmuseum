Preggos are plagued with hormones and easily confused.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: CWMC Round 3a Brock Shepherd, to say the least, feels lost for words. Arriving at his apartment for the first time in months, the sight of his home in its tarnished and disgusting state causes a disturbance in the pit of his stomach. Trash spread across the floors. The glass surface to their coffee table shattered into pieces, embedded in the shag carpet. The permeated stench waiting weeks for some unlucky sap to catch a whiff. After the moment of shock sinks in, he begins to tip-toe around the layers of trash decorating the floors. He peers carefully around the room, hoping to a hint pertaining to what may have happened in his absence. Upon returning, Brock never imagined everything to be exactly as it was before he left. He's been a ghost for far too long. But that doesn't mean he expected his home to be turned inside out with April no where to be found. The uncomfortable feeling in his stomach worsens and begins to overwhelm his system. Painfully forced to realization upon finding every room empty, Brock plummets to his knees and begins sobbing, tearfully calling out, "APRIL!" He knows she won't reply. He knows that no matter how loud he screams she still won't answer. That no matter how much he cries she still won't comfort him. That he can punch, kick, and destroy what few items haven't been thrashed in their home, and she still won't return. April is gone; their unborn child with her. Understanding his life is ruined, he hangs his head in shame. Only for a second, because he knows what must be done. He needs to find April and take his life back. In the face of overwhelming odds, Brock tries his best to remain clear-headed. Already his list of allies is non-existent. He knows there is hardly anyone he can call for information at this time. April never made friends in the city, as she always imagined their stay there to be temporary. Apparently she kept that promise, even if Brock wouldn't. Also absent is the luxury of his own contacts, as none of them even know he's back yet. They aren't supposed to. Brock must remain a ghost, if he is going to keep his wife and child safe. And to remain a ghost, he must trust those he otherwise would never even dream of associating with. But no man ever truly grows without sacrificing something they hold dear. Withdrawing a pay-as-you-go cellphone from his pocket, Brock dials a number recalled from memory. It's a number he wished he would never call again; however, considering the circumstances he doesn't have a choice. After less than a ring, a voice answers. "Speak." "You actually answer the phone like that?" Brock replies to August Monday, static filtering his voice. "Why did I know you'd end up calling me sooner or later." The way August speaks, he doesn't ask questions. Even when he's asking questions, he doesn't ask questions. It's a very distinct 'ass-hole' quality, one that contributes to the notion that he does indeed know everything he talks about and you are a dumb shit in comparison. "Quit acting like a prick; you don't know shit. Where's your sister?" "Maybe you should pay a little bit more attention to current events." "Maybe I should come to your house and test your gag reflex on the shit-encrusted urinal cake you have in the guest bathroom." "That would consist of you leaving whatever dark, embarrassing corner you've crawled into this time." Fucking August. Brock can hear him taking a drag from his menthol cigarette through the receiver. "I can't--" August's comment puts him off-guard. It takes a moment for Brock to think of the proper way to say what needs to be said. "I have to find her, August. No one wants to hear this, I know--I don't even want to fucking say it--but there isn't time to explain. Right now, I need to find April and know that she's safe." Brock takes a deep breath, closes his eyes, and silently prays to himself that August could dispel the piece-of-shit inside of him long enough to help out his sister. "Can you tell me anything that could help?" After another long drag, August croaks, "Only because I love it when we spar. And this new guy of hers is an even bigger tool than you." "'New guy'?" It takes too long to jump to a conclusion. The first reaction is always disbelief. "Like I said, 'Current Events.' You'd know all about this shit if you'd turn on a fucking television." "Yeah, well not all of us have time to watch TV." Brock replies. "Keep talking." "He's another wrestler. A Primetime fella. Face-Eater on the PRIME roster. You know 'im?" Face-Eater? The name rings a bell, only for its sheer ridiculousness. No one forgets a something like that after hearing it for the first time, but still, no detailed mental picture pops into place. No specific encounters and certainly no personal accounts. "Never met him before in my life." "Hmmmmmm, well, he's a real obnoxious prick. Apparently when Ape went to go pull you out of the Memorial Tournament--y'know, due to the fact that ya went fucking missing--this guy went and took your place, claiming to do it in your memory or some shit like that. Ever since then, sis' has been feasting like a fucking pig outta the palm of his hand." Another cancer-inducing breath. "Guy even went as far as to vow to find out where the fuck you were, too." The story is already too bizarre. Brock sits with one hand covering his speechless mouth, while the other holds the phone to his ear. "Still breathing?" "I'm here, I just--... fuck." "That ain't it." "What do you mean?" "Current events," he replies. Through the phone, Brock can hear August Monday grinning, that shit eating bastard. "Two weeks ago, I was flipping channels when I pass CNN and what do I see, but press-footage of Police, escorting a five-foot-nothing twig in a vinyl mask with who else but my sister, your fiancé, clinging tight to his arm. The text underneath read in very bold type: 'Fake Ransom Scam Foiled'." "What the hell?" "Apparently, some fucking hoodlums dropped a ransom note to Ape's place one morning, demanding eighty-eight grand or else you'd be dead. April, obviously knowing I--like everyone else with some sense on this planet--hate your guts and would never willingly help you, had to call someone else for assistance. So she called a total stranger." "But I wasn't fucking kidnapped!" Brock screams. "Obviously." "Well who the fuck would do that? No one even knows where the hell I've been." Brock finds what hasn't been destroyed of their dining room set, a lone chair, and takes a seat. This information is far too fucked up. "It was that Face-Fucker, wasn't it?" "That's the weird thing. See, the dudes that set up the fake ransom? They say it wasn't them. They keep sayin' this Face-Eater got them to do this. That he was setting the entire thing up." "So he's trying to get some money out of April? But she doesn't-" "April doesn't have any fucking money; you know that." August spits. "This is where shit gets confusing. Because the Face-Eater guy was spending his money. According to April, he was gonna use his own funds to buy your freedom." Brock doesn't know what to think. His shoulders sink and his head hangs low, still in the pit of his palm. "Sound like something April would appreciate? A stranger going so far as to donate that much money for someone he's never even met?" "Th-the cops." Brock stammers. "What did the cops say? They have to know he set it up--August, something happened at that fake drop. Something that made everything go wrong. If it was supposed to be a fake drop, police never should have came." The jagged facts aren't fitting together correctly. Missing pieces still remain. "What happened at that drop?" "No one's said anything." "No one? Not even a word from the cops? What about April?" "The cops?!" August laughs. "As far as 5-0 is concerned, they have their perps. Even if they can't tie 'em to your disappearance, they'll still pin those kids for something to keep 'em off the street. After that, it's out of sight and out of mind." Another long, shit-eating drag of cancer shit that Brock prays will kill him. He knows at this point he isn't hating his girlfriend's brother for his audacity. He's merely the herald of bad news. "I guess the same phrase could be applied to my sister's approach to the situation," August exhales. "What the fuck are you getting at?" "Ever since the day shit went down, she's been shacked up with him at his place. From what I hear, she doesn't even leave the fucking house. She's completely enamored with the guy. And like I said, frankly, he's a fucking tool." Finally, Brock lifts his head and his eyes pierce forward. They stare at nothing in particular; they simply read of determination. "So... August... where can I find the Face-Fucker?"
