Date: Who cares Living in a postmodern society that fixates its philosophical approach upon the cosmological is unto itself problematic. The common man encapsulates himself in superficial situations, allows said situations to exacerbate in his mind, and refuses to believe that there is an internal structure within his being. No single society has been able to function without some sort of metaphysical thread woven into the fabric. There has to be some avenue of thought that answers the essential questions of life: What is its meaning, what happens to us when we die, how did we get here, and so forth . Traditionalists who sought answers with the metaphysical are fighting harder than ever to get on the same pedestal as postmodern cosmologists. With the injection of technological advancement, postmodernists can provide answers in the form of “factual proof” whereas traditionalists fail in that regard. The only advantage traditionalists have is that materialism cannot ale an inquisitive mind, one that yearns for the truth behind the proverbial locked door. They beg for the common man, after receiving his knowledge, to yearn with all of his heart and eliminate his distractions. Then, they feel, he will be one step closer to achieving true happiness. Eliminating distractions by any means necessary is Devin Shakur’s second rule in accordance with his true happiness categorical imperative. Reflecting: December 14th, 1993 Time: 3 PM My heartbeat had never been as high as it was at this specific point in time. I could feel it beating vehemently, as if it were inside of my skull. My hands had never trembled as violently as they were right then. It took five different occasions just for me to be able to dial the correct numbers to the destination that I wished to reach. Every second that passed, my stomach crept ever closer to my throat. Having never been in the situation that I was currently in, I had no precedent to reflect upon. After what seemed to be an eternity, the voice that I yearned for answered on the other line. He nonchalantly inquired “Hello?”, as if this was nothing more to him than a run of the mill call. My voice trembled, unable to speak in a coherent manner befitting of telephone conversation. After a second of heavy breathing, I shouted back in return “Christian…getoverherenow”. The concern in his voice picked up, inquiring what was the matter? Maybe it was just my opinion at the time, but I don’t think that the situation constituted being told over the phone, “JUSTGETOVERHERENOW!” I screamed back, unaware that my voice could more than likely break glass if I were just a couple of inches closer to the window. “I’ll be right there” were the last words that I heard. I placed the phone back on the receiver and saw my handprint stain the handle. I looked down at the floor to once again. A puddle expanding under a heap of mass. An eight inch piece of steel coated in rage. A body at my feet. Reflecting: December 14th, 1993 Time: 2:45 PM I felt something boiling deep down in my soul that I never felt before. It was able to take control of my mind, manipulate the embryo cells inside, and coerce my body to do things that I would never dream of doing in a million years. I had a new found sense of confidence and something that I would later deduce to be self-respect. No longer was I content with being the one whipped for my infinite sins. I wanted to rise up and stand tall so that one day I could be judged not by the scars on my body but by the content of my character. In today’s vernacular, the term is called power. I sat down with my father in the living room to tell him a story… Reflecting: January 21st, 1991-December 14th, 1993 Ever since being under the tutelage of Christian, I started to become someone with an identity, not just a body for anger to fuck whenever it had the urge. I learned how to defend myself in situations in which violence was called for. At 4 PM sharp each day after his confrontation with my father, I released my aggression onto a large tube shaped object that hung from the ceiling. When my fists first connected with the punching bag, I cringed in pain because I was weak. I had no physical strength, so therefore I had no mental strength, nor will. Christian never took it easy on me either, screaming like my father used to in my ear whenever I wanted to quit. Believe me, many a time did I want to throw in the towel. Why didn't I? I did not want to give my father the satisfaction of seeing me labeled a quitter in someone else’s eyes. Christian did not have to take the time out of his schedule and make me his student, that was his choice. He also did something that he probably considered mundane, yet I saw it as an action that warranted death, standing up to my father. If he was to put forth enough consideration of me to do that, then disappointing him was something that I could not do. So I put forth that much more energy in every punch that I threw. Christian once worked me until my hands bled. His argument was that whenever I saw blood on my hands, I knew that I had put in a good day’s work. I decided to take it upon myself to see that blood before I called it an evening. Whenever I woke up the next morning, moving my hands was next to impossible, Christian called that "satisfaction". After enduring a long trial of stagnation, to my astonishment, the bag started to move from the force that I put into it. A smile developed on Christian’s face the first time that it happened, and even at the young age I was, I knew that I had made a step in the right direction. The process of kickboxing, judo, and various other forms of martial arts training followed suit. Before I knew it, my physical training was complete. I no longer had flabby skin, but instead I had muscle mass. I actually felt happy with myself for the first time. With the physical evolution at its culmination, my next endeavor constituted in being brought up to par in terms of intellectual knowledge. The person to do that would not be Chris, Jason, Brandon, or Matthew, but instead someone of the opposite sex, their sister Rachel. This, much like everything else, would be a learning process to culminate in a way that I never expected. After she completed her daily school chores, she would take the very same homework that she had just completed and bring it forth into my hippocampus. In her teaching methodologies, she was the complete antithesis of what Christian was. Instead of chastising me for not being aggressive enough, whenever I got something right she would give me some kind of reward. I was particularly fond of a candy piece named Starburst. She introduced me to the concept of mathematics, science, history, and various other subjects. Her particular favorite was English and she went through a painstaking process to make me appreciate the language . The mantra she always emphasized was "Patience is a virtue", something that her mother taught her while she was alive. My mind often reflected back to that once I hit a seemingly impossible bind in my studies. Nobody ever said that this life was going to be easy. The last assignment in English that Rachel gave to me was to pick out a book from their library to read and to write a report about it. One week would be the allocated amount of time, and would get Christian to grade the assignment. This did not sit well with me as he would more than likely pick out the little idiosyncrasies of the report, but she assured me that he would grade it like someone who was writing their first report. A book that I took a particular interest in was The Outsiders by S. E. Hinton. I felt that, for all the wrong reasons, the title described me down to a T. Arduous would be the accurate word to describe writing out the report.. The book took three days for me to complete, and the writing process equal in length. I wanted to give Christian the impression that I was not just going through the motions, that writing this report was a symbol of my development. Two erasers and four broken pencils later, the words were on paper and in Christian’s hands. Twenty minutes later he slapped me on the back and said "Brilliant job, kid". The intellectual training was complete…Or so I assumed so. There was something about Rachel that I could not coin. I will admit that I enjoyed hanging around Christian and the rest of the guys, and even considered them my fellow brothers, but I did not view Rachel like a sister. Whenever I saw Christian crack a grin at one of my accomplishments, I simply nodded my head like my father did. Yet whenever I saw a smile from Rachel, my instincts told me to smile back. Looking back on it I obviously see that I was attracted to her, but my mind had no idea what to do with the attraction. I garnered the impression that she also enjoyed being in my company and would often tell me things that special secrets about herself. She called theses times "intimate moments". With Christian and the guys there was never any surprises. They always gave me everything in a straightforward, no bullshit manner. Rachel had a bit of hidden meaning in almost everything that she said. I could not deduce that these were triggers that told me she “liked me, liked me”. On one particular day, December 14th, a bit of frustration developed on her face after one of her intimate moments when I did not give the appropriate emotion in her opinion.. Lacking common sense and a guide to her mind, I asked her what the trouble was. Her lips came into contact with mine. In the moment I had no response in my regime on standby to use. I felt a surge of energy rush through my body that dissipated once her lips removed themselves. She asked “Is that straightforward enough?”. I missed the feeling and the only words I could retort with were “I enjoyed that”. If I could go back through time and meet myself at that age, I would lay him out with a snap kick. Much to my pleasure, she once again brought her lips up to mine. This time I took an initiative and responded in stride, imitating her actions. I was now up to par. I could not wait to tell my father about what had happened. I actually thought he would be proud of me… Reflecting: December 14th, 1993 Time: 2:57 After I told my father of what happened between Rachel and I, he did not seem at all content. Hell, he did not even attempt to fake a congratulatory smile. Instead he reverted back to the dictator that I had come to know for the first decade and change of my life. He started into one of his rants. Multiple times he compared Rachel to Tituba, the woman many believe started the Salem Witch Trials by manipulating Abigail Williams and Betty Paris into witches. While it is true that Rachel might have changed the makeup of my mind, I did not resist in any capacity like I did with my father. He proceeded to tell me that I was never to see her again because who knew what she was capable of. In a rapid fire defense of myself, I shouted "You can't control me anymore! If I want to see her then that is my right!" The look upon his face was one of shock and awe. One thing that I was taught under him was never to talk back to an elder and to always respect them. Christian on the other hand taught me that I had a right to speak my mind. Naturally, I was inclined to go with the person that did not mutilate my body because he thought I was Satan's son. When my father stormed out of the room into the hallway, I knew exactly what he was going to do. I ran the opposite way toward the kitchen. A couple of seconds later we met back in the living room. He brought forth the one object that I never wanted to see again in my lifetime, the ever infamous belt that my back had grown accustomed to. Unfortunately for him, I held in my hand an object that made his eyes grow bigger than saucers, a knife. The grip upon its handle was that of a vice, tightening each second that I looked into his eyes. This would be my reparations, one way or the other. Whenever my body moved forward his would backup. He had the look upon his face like I did the first time I saw a snake in the basement, absolute fear. When he was backed up against his bedroom door, feeling for the handle, I made my move. After the first cut, his hands immediately went to cover up the spot, in hopes of keeping every ounce of blood that he could inside of his body. I looked over at the knife and had a sensation that it had not done enough to damage him. With blood coming out of his mouth, he looked at me and said “Devin, please don’t do this, God doesn’t appreciate this.” I couldn’t fucking believe it. Anthony Shakur was still trying to manipulate me with religious vernacular. Seeing as how I had the advantage, I decided to take charge verbally as well “IF GOD EXISTED THEN HE WOULDN’T LET YOU DO THIS TO ME!” Another cut into his body, right above his hands. He attempted to grasp a hold of the blade, but the strength that I had acquired prevented him from doing such. He knew that he was going down, but damn if his mind didn’t try to keep to fight. “God does exist Devin, and he thinks that-” I wouldn’t let him. “GOD DOESN’T APPROVE OF WHIPPING PEOPLE UNTIL THEY FALL ASLEEP! GOD WOULD NOT MAKE HIS CHILDREN SLEEP IN A CASKET WITH THEIR NAME ON IT!” I continued to vent my belligerence into his body. Three more to the body and then I proceeded to cut at his face. When he went to protect his face, I saw that this left his entire back wide open to attack and I took to it, plunging the knife deep into his back, hoping to inflict so much damage that he would not be capable of moving. Reassuring words did not emit from his mouth this time, instead he was just going to try and take the pain until I was tired. What he did not know was I had the power now. I had gone through the trials and tribulations in the basement, with the punching bag. I knew that if I could make him start moving, much like the punching bag, I had him right where I wanted him. In hindsight most of the wounds were very superficial, not capable of going that deep because I thought the weapon would get stuck inside of him, he would pull it out, and use it against me. I wanted to have the control, so I compensated by slashing him so many times that to this day I cannot recall the exact number. His last begging statement to me was “Your mother would not appreciate this”. At that point I lost the last of my rational thought and unleashed with a blitzkrieg assault upon him. I shouted in rage “MY MOTHER WOULDN’T LET YOU DESTROY MY LIFE!”, “YOU HAVE NO RIGHT TO CALL YOURSELF MY FATHER! I HATE YOU! I HATE YOU! I HATE YOU!” I continued to tear into his body until his eyes were involuntarily shut. Reflecting: December 14th, 1993 Time: 3:02 PM Christian stood over my fathers body and put three fingers right underneath the left side of his chin. He held them there a second and looked up at me, “Not dead yet, but I wouldn’t give him twenty minutes to live”. He then gave me a glance and saw that my shaking had not stopped. He stood up to his full height and put both of his hands on my shoulders “You did what you had to do, Devin…You did what you had to do…” The words alone were not enough to convince me that I had, but the look of finality in his eyes proved to be the great equalizer. The incredulous look that I had upon my face soon evaporated, but the shaking did not stop. “I did what I had to do…” I said quietly to myself, “I did what I had to do…” Christian once again chimed in. “What do you think he was going to do to you with that belt? I don’t think he was going to teach you how to use it. He was never going to change his ways with you Devin. The only thing that he looked at you as was a subordinate. I would doubt if he ever really thought of you as a son. Real fathers do not subject their sons to the shit that he put you through. They help them through the pain that they are having and they help them grow up into men and make their own legacy. He was just trying to hold yours down. You understand me?” At this point I thought that I would end up crying my eyes out like the first time that I came into contact with Christian. The situation was somewhat similar, a drastic revelation had occurred that altered the lives of everybody that was around me. This would call for an entire new scheme to be concocted in order for my life to have a chance of survival. Not one tear fell to the floor. “He was going to end up killing me at some point wasn’t he?” “Much as it pains me to say so, yes. Granted with me around he wouldn’t have gotten away with it, but I don’t think his intentions were ever deterred by my presence. Now what we are going to do with him?” I stood there a second and pondered all of the possible options. Everything from leaving him there to putting him in the closet, under the bed, or some other place that was hidden in the house crossed my mind. The one major advantage that I had was he had no real social network of people that would come looking for him. It was then that I got my first sadistic idea. “The basement…In the big casket that he made for me.” “Alright, you get his feet and I’ll take point.” The shaking in my body had eliminated itself when I had to grab a hold of the feet. I made my best effort to control my other nervous body reaction now that I had a couple of seconds without the knife in my hand. Christian’s comments resonated through my head the entire way down the hall and into the basement. I did what I had to do… Once his body was placed horizontally in the casket, Christian stood up and kept his arms at his side, “Once we get back to my house, I’ll call up some of my people and have them come over. They’ll go through the house from top to bottom and make sure that everything is secure. I’ll also make sure that they lock the basement door so that if somebody by chance comes over they will suspect that he is just down there working. That will give us a couple days to get our shit squared away.” “What is going to happen to me?” “I honestly don’t have a clue. Optimally I want you to stay with us, won’t be that much of strain since I’m boss around the house. You’ll have to share a room though as I’m not going to upgrade on the house. Hopefully, after a little while you can end up living life like Chris and everybody else.” “We can’t tell them about this can we?” “Not for a good while. I’ll think up something before they end up start suspecting. We’re going to have to rush out of here though because they are going to be home any minute. Let’s go get cleaned up.” When Christian’s back was turned, I was able to look down at my father for the first time. There was still a glimmer of life left in his eyes. I could not exactly make out the words that he was trying to mouth, but at this point I did not particularly care. He no longer had the stature nor the intimidation to call himself my father. He was just a mere distraction to me that would soon be in my rear view mirror. I then drew my conclusion Listening to his screams… Hearing him beg for his life… Seeing the blood flow out of his body… Looking at the cuts between where his skin used to be… Watching his life slip from his fingers… I enjoyed it I gave him a cold smile before slamming the casket lid down.
