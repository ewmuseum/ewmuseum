Oxios, You Dummy! (Part Two: ReV 69, vs. Masters vs. Gaz

3 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/3/05 Finally. *I* have the chance to break out now. No disrespect to Oxios or anything we have achieved thus far in the realm of PRIME, but my blood has now been permeated with a rush of adrenaline. A feeling I haven’t felt in a time since the Wrestling Battle Ground. Before Gabriel. Before that Invincible piece of Gaz. Before all of that, I was merely a sixteen-year-old kid in a regional place known as the WBG. I’ll be honest with you. I was a joke yet again. Gabe GAIJIN was the name. Take one element of your stereotypical Japanese-based wrestler in America (Read: Tajiri, who’s more talented than that, God bless him) and another element of what Nick Dinsmore is doing right now as Eugene and that said it all. It was utterly and mind-numbingly retarded. Having to sit there and perform like a damn circus midget. I mean, I’m short, but there’s plenty of smaller wrestlers than I…though being 5’5” at the time didn’t help my case much. People loved it. Just like Invincible Gabe did before me, it garnered me the WBG’s secondary championship, the International Title. Held it for seven months, too, earning the longest reign of any champ before that. I mean, yes. I didn’t hate it half as bad as RUSH Pro-Wrestling. The impetus of the WBG? I was on MY OWN. There was no Scott “Rampage” Matthews to trade banter with. There was no Forsaken to get into the most ludicrous feuds with. Just Gabe, albeit not exactly the one I just wanted to be. Now, I find myself on that cloud nine I’ve wanted for a little while since journeying to PRIME. Now, I get the chance to show people what The Real Gabriel Brown can do. There’s a reason that the Internet community have dubbed me with the moniker of The Near Future. Apparently, they view me as a ‘human Swiss army knife’ or something. Comedy, straightman, all-round great wrestler in the ring. All at the tender age of 20. I’m enthralled to know that my first test isn’t going to be a pushover. My personal feelings of Gareth Green aside, the man has that nasty penchant for submission and counter-wrestling. Masters, I witnessed first-hand. He and Hawke. Cut from the same cloth and blanketed in a proverbial fog of bitterness and hatred that their time had come and gone in OSW. Speaking of OSW, guess what I got in my little RPW excursion, guys? OSW Tag Title sound familiar? Beat the Forsaken nice and proper to get them. Granted, it’s a defunct belt and it was more of a psychological blow to those dinosaurs, but it was still an honor to hold them. I realize you don’t care. I recognize that you guys have pretty much taken a big fat dump on everything that OSW used to be, but I brought them up to prove a point. Anything you can do, Seth Masters, I can do better. If I can win these belts as a crowd-playing simpleton, imagine what I’m capable of now. Don’t think I’ve forgotten about you, either, Gareth. Your months of tormenting me on end and putting my face through a brick wall with that gimmicky splotch of suck you call Nerezza haunted me for some time. I thought with the closure of RPW, I wouldn’t be able to find you again. But that sound you hear is me kicking the door down. I wait for no opportunity to present itself, I make my own. I’m not Angelo Deville, nor do I aspire to be, but as the horribly drawn-out, overused, tired, beaten, dragged, and kicked-around cliché goes. The devil eventually comes to collect. No, I don’t mean Angelo. My personal respect aside for the man, I won’t need a sphinx-like reputation to defeat the both of you. June 22nd, 2005Seattle Height Condominiums, Seattle, WA Well, we were standing in front of the door. All Vangellus had to do was stick roughly eight ounces of metal into the lock and we would be in paradise. Then again, you obviously missed the part where this was VANGELLUS trying to open the door. “So, G,” Vangellus said as he fiddled around with a key. “What’re YOU gonna do with your space in here? It’s pretty roomy.” You know. Hadn’t thought about it. Gym? Game room? Burlesque room? Wait. Sorry, channeled the moron for a moment. Wait… “Why the hell don’t you just open the door already?!” I shouted at him, feeling the strain of having to lift two surprisingly heavy cases. Both belonged to Van. He merely looked at me and grinned sheepishly. “Well, dawg…” he furrowed his brow as he showed me the most OBNOXIOUS set of keys ever. “I’ve…well…got a lot of keys, dude.” “You…what?” “Seventy-five.” He continued to grin weakly as I felt my blood pressure literally rise to heights it had never been. “WHY do you have so many keys, you moronic chucklehead?!” He shrugged before he showed off several keys. “Rhonda’s house. Janie’s house. Jennifer’s house. The Dahm Triplets’ house.” He grinned like a moron at the last key. “Oh, I know this. Penelope’s house.” “Cruz?” “I thought you wanted to see the house first.” I SWEAR to you I heard my blood boil. “OPEN THE DOOR! HEAVY BAGS! NOT GETTING LIGHTER!” I couldn’t help it. He was on my LAST nerve. Finally, he fiddled with the door and kicked it open to reveal… “What the…” I said. I must have been delusional. What was inside was beyond most mortal comprehension. Few seconds later… Cages. Cages as far as the eye could see. It was…by all means…against EVERY law of nature ever. The condo was of a decent size, but THIS? This was just crazy. The first thing I did after I picked my jaw up off the floor was glare at Vangellus. “WHAT. THE. HELL.” He acted as if this was normal. Or he was REALLY high. I DID leave him alone for a few moments at Dick’s Burger Joint while I went to the bathroom. Maybe he did it then. “I thought this would be the shit, dude! I call this…The Fortress of Pimpitude!” Naturally, I was STILL in shock. “But…how…when…why…” “Well,” he looked at me as he pointed to the rows of cages. “We need to study our opponents, so I put together this kick-ass portion of our new abode together when I took a brief tour of this prior to showing it to you. See, there are endangered animals here and I thought it’d be great to take them all in.” “There’s a gas leak, isn’t there? I’m actually dead and am being punished for being ungrateful in spite of my success in the field of professional wrestling at such a young age. You can tell me, Angel Shaped Like Vangellus.” Vangellus walked over to the first cage. On the inside was the shape of what appeared to be a green elf. Or something. Couldn’t tell you. I still think I’m knocked out from a gas leak in the condo. “That’s the endangered Jester Elf,” Vangellus told me as he pointed to the cage. “They say he works great in packs, but on his own, he may be past his prime. If he were to attack you now, I’m almost confident that you would be able to outlast him.” The elf shot a funny look at me and mouthed something. I think it was “YOU’RE DEAD.” But, again. Gas leak. I looked back at Vangellus. “This is symbolism and that’s actually a representation of Gaz “The Jester” Green, right?” “How the fuck should I know…oh, yeah. Right. Yes. Well, let’s move onto the one next to it.” In this entire laws-of-physics-breaking scene. I find myself looking on at all the various creatures locked up in cages. In the one three cages away from me is what appeared to be a mighty wolf. Its extravagant fur coat had been lined with a splendid rainbow of colors. Gold, moreso than anything. Then firing another glance at the cage next to that, there was what appeared to be three beasts in one cage. One of them looked like Oscar the Grouch in a trash can, another like the Wal-Mart ball and one that seemed to be just black. No other way to describe it. Then a weird one particularly caught my interest. It was a Hoover XL. Seriously. A goddamn vacuum cleaner locked in a cage. The sign was marked “Human Vaccum Cleaner.” “What’s that?” I ask of my partner-in-crime as he glances over to the vacuum cleaner. “Oh, that one I haven’t paid much attention to. I just named it Boda. Seemed appropriate.” “Ah.” Both of us made our way to the next cage. Inside was the form of two snakes. Cobras to be precise. Both adorned in the color green with the same eerie-looking pattern on their bellies. Red and orange like flames. The two slithered along the ground, both powerful in appearance. But also treacherous like the animal associated with the name. “Those…” Vangellus continued. “…are Disobedient Cobras. They tend to stray away from all other connections. They’re on their own. They have fealty to no one but themselves.” Shocked at his lexicon, I look at Vangellus with a raised eyebrow. “Hooked on Phonics. Reading IS educational!” I look at the floor and sigh. “Maybe it’s not ME who’s whiffing gas fumes.” Thinking to myself again, I simply take another gander at the wide and wondrous world before me. At second glance…it’s kind of charming. You know, in a far-out, farfetched, whacked-out, almost pot-induced kind of way. Oxios tapped me on the arm and winked. “Want to see this one cage I just put in? It’s awesome.” Why not? I figure I might as well see something cool before I eventually cross over to the other side. Maybe Hoyt has taken on the form of a cockroach and I may crush him underneath my boot. Or Vampir. Dark Gimmicks never fail to piss me off. Personal feelings out of the way, I ventured over to where Oxios was pointed. Inside? THE RPW TAG TEAM, SWEET ILLUSION. For those not in the know, these are who I first won the RPW Tag Titles from. They were a group of four women, all joined together for the purpose of bringing pain to men. Carys, Sam Alotti, Alyssa Sanders and a fourth woman of South American heritage. Cruz, I believe she was. “This is my favorite part,” Vangellus said with a wink as he cleared his throat to look inside the cage of the women, all grabbing the bars and calling for our blood. “SHOWER TIME, LADIES!” Okay, that’s it. I’m outta here…well…maybe after one peek… WAKE UP! Huh? Where am I? Back in the car. Well, at least it wasn’t gas fumes and I’m not dead, I think. I look at my surroundings. I’m lost in the middle of some weird ghetto area in the middle of Seattle while in my peripheral vision, I see Vangellus moving around like he’s hopped on goofballs. Turning my head, I actually see the goof asking somebody for directions to the Seattle Height Condominiums. Sighing to myself, I simply fold my arms and chuckle to myself. I have the stupidest dreams, I really do. I’m in a feeling of euphoria right now. Mere days time until I get my turn to show PRIME why I’m called The Near Future. And most importantly…mere days until I get my hands on Gaz Green. He was infatuated with making life a living hell for RPW’s former curator, Christian Kannon. He never saw what he did to me as Invincible Gabe. I was a footnote to he and Nerezza’s revival. And I don’t blame him. Invincible Gabe made people laugh. That’s all. His impact rivaled that of Pepsi Crystal. But you don’t realize who you’ve stepped on, Gareth. You really don’t. The simpleton is gone, Gaz. And from that rubble, Gabriel Brown has risen. When you and the vampire leapt into that ring and made me a martyr for your return to the professional wrestling world, you answered a prayer. I never got to avenge any of my losses to you back then because you were seeing green yourself. I mean no pun. You were envious of everything that Kannon held dear. You passed me over on numerous occasions to get to Kannon, but he’s gone now. We all had to hear about it. We’re tired of it. Now the only sound coming from your mouth is going to be your screams when I’ve ripped your arm out of its socket and the only envy you’ll feel will be that for *ME* because you can never be what I have become, Gaz. True to myself. Let’s see you utilize a hand buzzer properly when your hand has been contorted into funny balloon animal shapes. As for the other, Seth Masters is a caricature. He’s merely an empty vessel that used to be embodied by the likes of a youthful, talented, brash, yet respectable wrestler. Now he’s a monster. He and Trevor live only for suffering. Their best days are behind them and come the next ReVolution, Seth gets bounced out of that ring like an OSW check. When the ReVolution comes, I live up to the hype surrounding me; placed on me by the observers. PRIME’s Golden Child will shine.
