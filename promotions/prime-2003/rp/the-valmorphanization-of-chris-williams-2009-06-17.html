The Valmorphanization Of Chris Williams

17 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th June 2009 Poking at his dinner with the fork in his left hand Chris let out a huge sigh as he glanced down at the slop in front of him. It had been intended to be some form of fish pie, however it looked distinctly devoid of fish and tasted even worse. This was often the problem with microwave food, the pretty packaging drew you in then BAMN! Crap for dinner. Holding up his chin with his right hand his eyes, whilst not vacant were not where he was, to put it in simpler terms he was bored. He was bored of microwaved meals and felt that his life had come to a bit of a standstill and he was stuck in a rut in his career and in his personal life. He had been left alone now for the best part of a week, Claire having been called away on business, it was the problem of having such a successful woman involved in his life, yet when she was away he almost felt weaker without her. Not just mentally but physically, as though there was something missing when she was not there. He had always put it down to the fact that her cooking was the missing ingredient and based on the congealed mess in front of him this could well be the case. Another problem was that when he was along, he had time to think and question everything about his career, he became nonchalant about his training schedule and would after leave things till the last minute and it was reflecting in his work drastically. When he wanted to show up and put on a performance, there was no doubt about it, he could be as electrifying and exciting as they come, his sheer strength bringing screams of satisfaction from the crowds. Yet when he was tired or when his heart wasn't in it, he looked like a lumbering beast in the ring, someone you could dance around and take several shots at, whilst all you had to do was watch out for a lethargic swing of his club like arm. He didn't intend it to be like this, but he hadn't really been ready for the step up in companies between Just Wrestling and PRIME. Just had been perfect for him, he had ruled the roost and by the time he left being begged to stay, a draw for a company that although steady was never going to reach a national level, hell it wasn't what they wanted. He had been a big fish in a small pond and loved the sensation. He loved the theoretical spotlight being on him, main eventing the gyms they played at yet PRIME was a far far cry from Just. The first difference was the professionalism. Lisa Tyler ran a very tight ship and if you so much as looked at anyone in the wrong way, you were out the door. It had thrown Chris off at first, far from the slack demands that Just threw at you, show up when you feel like it, put on a show cash in your pocket see you next week. PRIME was a maze of red tape and contracts with a thousand and one ways to lose money. All of which baffled Chris, he was not a thinker, not in that sense anyway too easily he became confused which is why it was always handy for Claire to be around. What she didn't have control of was his performances. Since arriving he couldn't help but feel like an outcast. The majority of the roster had been their years together and been through thick and thin together. There was a tight nit bond that so far he had need been able to break. Ok sure maybe he could have put himself out there a little more, but that wasn't the sort of person he was. He often just sat back, let people work him out and then go from there. The only problem was that everyone in PRIME was so caught up in their own lives and experiences, that hadn't happened. Hell he could count the number of encounters he'd had with his colleagues on one hand, most of them had been hostile if not entertaining, Natas had tried to tear his a new hole verbally and Hessian and Nerezza had tried to tear him a new hole with a chainsaw....as you do. The point was, that in nearly two months and heading towards PRIME's biggest Pay Per View of the year, he had only had one maybe two matches that actually meant something. A match that wasn't put on so the crowd would have additional time at the vendors to buy the merchandise that was a huge profit making machine for PRIME. A match where in the back of his mind he had thought, why am I even here why am I bothering? The final straw that had pushed Chris over the realms of wondering to realisation was his assignment this week. He hadn't been at Revolution the week prior having tweaked his back a little in his match with Nerezza, whose career in PRIME was extremely short lived so the win over him meant next to nothing. As a result of this he had been left a voice message from one of Tyler's cronies, advising him that his services would be required in Richmond, Virginia. Against Finn Roberts. Now Chris didn't know anything about the guy. This was not a problem in itself, hell he didn't know a lot of the new signings that had broken through, his knowledge of the wrestling world as a whole was not fantastic. What had perturbed him somewhat, was the fact that Finn Roberts was a member of a tag team. In wrestling this was a cardinal sin and as a singles wrestler, you knew you were on the bottom rung of things if you were placed against a tag team wrestler split from his partner. In short it meant that the management had no-one else available for you, there may have been some other wrestlers available, but none for you. You weren't deemed enough of a draw to go against the big guns, so they pull a man away from their natural environment of tag wrestling to make up the numbers. One thing was for sure. At Revolution 197 PRIME would be selling a lot more merchandise than usual because no-one apart from the most hardcore fan would stick around to watch that. “Maybe I should lose the name.” Chris mumbled to world in general the now clay cold meal in front of him, looking as though it was about to crawl off the plate of its own accord at any moment. Either that or reply to him. It was something he had been considering for some time now. Meat just didn't seem to fit, sure he was build like a slab but it just didn't work. He could hear the other people snickering around the locker room whenever they thought he was out of earshot. It didn't upset him, it damn right pissed him off. Yet what was he supposed to do? He was by all accounts the towel boy of PRIME, sure he got to hang out at the luxury pool and stare at all the women, but he never got to go anywhere near them, except on the rare occurrences when he was berated by them and they were supposed to be happy days? Another thing that had annoyed Chris more than it should have done was seeing Chainz storm ahead of him in stature. He could understand that he had been in the company before and had that sadistic edge that appealed to the more violent of teenagers, maybe he had paid his dues in the past, but this was now and the last time he had see Sloan , he was pummelling his face with a baseball bat at Ultra Violence, now all of a sudden his is receiving the high profile matches, which in his current state he could only dream off. Bryan Dawkins? Jonathan Rhine ? These sorts of matches were nothing but a pipe dream. Stabbing his fork deep into the fish pie, which surprisingly didn't squeal in pin he picked up the plate and took it over to the bin emptying the remains slowly as though every stroke of the fork was a strained effort. As he stood up he noticed his reflection in the mirror that was sat above the sink. For a moment he was captivated by the person staring back at him. In one moment of pure realisation he noticed that he didn't know who he was, not in a literal sense but a figurative one. His name for instance had not fit him ever. He didn't look like a Chris, nor a Christopher. It just didn't seem to fit. It didn't fit in PRIME either. Meat had made him a laughing stock, yet Christopher Williams. Chris Williams. However you disguised it, it simply didn't jump off the page at you. Christopher Williams was never going to be a Universal champion of PRIME. People may say that a name doesn't make you, but in an industry as heavily marketed as wrestling it really did. Take Hessian for instance, a name synonymous with success, it was simple and jumped out at you it rolled off the tongue far better than Angelus Von Helsig ever did, as a result he was able to be marketed to a broader audience. Meat was just a dull one syllable name that held no fire power. It was just his name though, everything else was there or there abouts. He was nearing the three hundred pound mark by week by week and reaching, what he felt would be level of height to muscle perfection. As he strode down to the ring everything about his body and stature felt right, but every time the announcer called out the name Meat..... It tore through him. He wanted to be thought of as more than just a piece of Meat, but a wrestler in his own right. Sure Claire was a fantastic manager and she had turned his world upside down for the better. Presenting him with opportunities that he could only ever previously dream of yet she had always referred to him as her piece of Meat. He needed to become more than that if he was ever going to be a success in PRIME though, he needed to take that one final step to distance himself from everyone else. To become more than an also ran His entire life he had sat back a let things happen to him instead of actively seeking things out. It was time for a change. Now if only Claire was there to help him. He allowed himself to stare into the mirror a little while longer, taking in every line and crease, the colour of his eyes, the shape of his nose, even the size of his lips. “Who are you?” He whispered quietly to himself as his mind began to race a million miles a second. <><><><><> Sat on a park bench as the sun beamed down were Claire and her father or boss depending on her mood. Today despite the sun in the air and the eagerness with which the world deemed it should be a good day, Claire was in a foul mood. She didn't like turning her attention away from Meat, especially not with the new serum in place, the last time she had done so he had ended up in cardiac arrest. “I would say the initial signs are good, but he's not the sort of man you can tempt easily. Hunting this new target is a million miles away from someone like Chris, you have to play it smart.” Claire turned to Steve who despite the immense heat was still wearing his plain black suit, liking to keep up with the social strata that his role within the company emphasised. Whilst it had never been revealed to Claire exactly what he did, she knew he had some degree of power but was far from being the top dog, if he was he would be able to call all the shots. He was more a middle management guy, the person who didn't mind brown nosing and getting his hands dirty to further their own personal goals. “I understand Claire, are we going to be pursuing things this week? We are very anxious to have him on board with us.” Steve had not looked at his daughter for the entire meeting thus far, sensing the overwhelming hostility from his daughter oozing from every pore. “No we let him come to us, in time he will. Any way I have to ask....why are you after him? What's wrong with Chris?” “Who?” Claire felt her knuckles tighten at the obvious disregard to Chris. To the company he was literally just a slab of Meat, someone they had poked and prodded to fulfil their own means. She couldn't help but wonder when exactly she would be deemed surplus to requirements and tossed to the side, did her own father simply see her as another piece of meat? “Meat.” she replied through clenched teeth, trying to not let the anger seep through too much and maintain some modicum of calm. “Oh right yes, well lets be honest he's not proved one hundred percent reliable and on more than one occasion has become a liability.” “And you don't think that the same would happen with him? He's stronger and considerably more dangerous than Chris, if he was to go rogue.” “That has been considered, Claire you don't need to worry about that. All you need to do is get him to us. As of now he is your prime mission. Meat becomes a secondary priority do you understand?” “I understand, it doesn't meant I like it though.” Rising to her feet Claire begins to walk away from her father, like she had done a million times before, not once looking back because she knew that he wouldn't be looking at her. Not with the eyes of a loving father, nor of a judgemental boss. There would be no looks at all and that hurt her more than being a piece of Meat and the thought of being discarded.”
