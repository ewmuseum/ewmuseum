The Inevitable

7 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/7/08 From TheSmokingGun.com Here’s the call placed from the 6138 Briarcliff Rd., Apt# 7, registered to PRIME Wrestling Universal Champion, Chandler Tsonda. On the tape, a frightened woman, who identifies herself as Jeri, tries to convey the situation to a 911 operator. TSG has not altered this public record in any way, except to place emphasis. An MP3 of the call will be released tomorrow. OPERATOR: Police operator 721, what is the emergency? JERI: My boss doesn’t look like he’s breathing. I need an ambulance, please. OPERATOR: Calm down, miss. What’s the address? JERI: Oh my god, there’s a pill bottle and [unintelligible] OPERATOR: Miss, I need you to tell me the address so I can dispatch someone right away. JERI: Oh god. Um. It’s 6138 Briarcliff. Apartment number is 7. OPERATOR: What city? JERI: San Diego. OPERATOR: Is that six one three eight? OPERATOR: [to dispatch] Six one three eight Briarcliff. Be advised there is an unconscious male. JERI: Yes, please please just send an ambulance. OPERATOR: Miss, what’s your name? JERI: Jeri. Please, you need to send someone now. If you just send someone. OPERATOR: Jeri, can you stay calm for me? JERI: [crying] No you don’t understand I think it’s all the pills and I can’t tell about breathing or not. OPERATOR: Jeri, the paramedics are on their way. Right now, I just need you to take a deep breath. [LONG PAUSE] OPERATOR: Jeri, are you there? JERI: Yes. OPERATOR: Can you see your boss’ chest rising and falling? JERI: I don’t. He’s not. OPERATOR: You can’t see or you don’t know? JERI: Don’t know. He’s on his stomach. OPERATOR: Does he have any injuries? JERI: His back. It’s why he’s always [unintelligible] OPERATOR: Is there blood on his back? JERI: No. No. It’s something he did before. OPERATOR: Jeri, I need you to be very clear with me. What happened to your boss’s back? JERI: He didn’t hurt it tonight. OPERATOR: Okay, that’s good. Can you flip him over to try and get a look at whether he’s breathing? JERI: It’s gonna be too heavy. OPERATOR: Okay. You’re doing great, Jeri. Just stay on the line with me until the paramedics get there. They should be there any minute. JERI: I can hear him breathing, I think. OPERATOR: He’s breathing? [LONG PAUSE] JERI: Yeah, yeah. He’s breathing. It’s just slow. OPERATOR: [to dispatch] Victim is breathing faintly. OPERATOR: Jeri, can you tell me your phone number? JERI: [deleted] OPERATOR: Apartment number 7, right, Jeri? JERI: Right. [ANOTHER INAUDIBLE VOICE] JERI: He’s waking up. OPERATOR: Are his eyes open? JERI: Kind of. OPERATOR: Okay, Jeri. The paramedics are pulling up right now. JERI: Thank you. Thank you.
