pith & vinegar works two ways

Date: 4dec08 Running as fast as I can, I attempt to hurdle a wooden fence but my foot catches the top and I fall flat to my face into the slimy mud below. Push to my knees, and then my feet slink to upright beneath me. Momentarily, I stand before the fleeting sensation of alluding my predators overwhelms me and I am running again. Hearing the rev of the V8 engine behind me, crashing through saplings in the woods, my life flashes before my eyes trying to envisage what would cause this vehicle to be upon my tail. Only thing that comes to mind immediately is the recent departure of the Just Wrestling personal assistant, Samantha Gideon, who removed her hand in a coke-fuelled abyss to appease her yearning to be just like me. Could picture her father, sick and twisted, full of pith and vinegar, with a mad vengeance like some sort of Jason Vorhees, sitting behind the wheel and laughing hysterically until it forced him to cough for fresh air. SMASH! went the fence as the V8 engine roared furiously on it’s way through it, twisting it’s wheels in the mud as it fought for traction. KRA-KOOM! The sound of a warning gunshot startled me to fall to my knees, covering my arms over the back of my head and it was only now the cold seemed to lap at my skin. Kneeling, in mud, in my boxer shorts, I wondered if this were the way I would see the end of my world. If the last time I would see my son would be him peeping over his mother’s shoulders as August Monday guided her from that courtroom which delivered my fate with a conniving gavel crash. Driver’s door opens and closes. Followed by the passenger side. I can hear the squelch of mud beneath the boot of those that approach me. The butterflies in my gut try to burst free like an alien creature birthing in a sci-fi flick. The fearsome click of a hammer being pulled back and the chamber of a gun revolving it’s next customer in the path of said hammer made my arsehole pucker and I feared I might shit myself, but be saved the embarrassment by the mud slicked over my skin. “If it isn’t the big movie star, Brock Shepherd.” “Wrestling.” “’scuse me?” Pause, realising this was not the most opportune moment to be correcting someone. Particularly when said someone has some sort of weapon at the ready to pierce through the back of my skull and create a puzzle for the forensic team at the local PD. “What’d you say, fuckhole?” “CHUD” is the noise that the barrel of a gun makes when it is dashed into the rear of your skull. “UNGH” is the noise I make as I feel my brain slap against the front of my skull like a bug on a windscreen. Instantaneously engulfing me with an unbridled thumping within my cranium which tortures my belly, rising nausea and bile in my throat. Fight with my vomit and answer the nice man. “Wrestling.” “You look like yer in the position to be providing me with feedback upon which that I am speaking of? Huh?” CHUD! “Do ya?” The second blow to the back of my skull forced my hands to fall into the mud, trying to support my body as the thumping grew to an enormous barrage of seismic proportions. The vomit fight yielded and I let any partial digestion in my guts into the mud below. A foot planted itself in my ass and pushed. Land belly first in the barf and moist earth, sliding a few inches before coming to a halt. Might be filled with disgust if not for the man with the gun scaring me half to death. “I ain’t worried in the delicacies of your ego, Shepherd. I’ve got grander plans and if ya help me out with these you will come out of this alive. If you don’t have what I need then I’ll seriously need to make some negotiations with my conscience as to the withdrawal of your life from this earth.” The man was considerably poetic for a guy with a gun, which unnerved me. He clearly had a vast intellect and was either a complete and utter sociopath borned to this earth to enjoy what he was doing right now or extremely cold and calculating. I was etching toward the latter. “See, we’re after Surly, Shep. You know where he is?” The name echoes inside of my mind with an almost numbing aftershock resonating in it’s wake. “Surly? My father?” Push up onto my feet in a state of complete and utter confusion. “He’s been feeding worms for the past seven years, feller. You’re chasing ghosts.” He shakes his head, not in disbelief but as though I was not giving him the information that he deserved. “See, I gots the word that it differs to the information which you are passing on. Found us a little snitch in Quantico that squealed after losin’ three toes to a pair of bolt cutters.” The man puts the knuckle of his free index finger between his teeth as if he were stifling a laugh. “You ever seen a bolt cutter cut off a toe? I have to say, despite the situation, I had to laugh. Those things shoot off with a fair bit of speed. It’s something you have to see to believe. Would you like to see it?” Shake my head. “Spoil sport. Anyways, ol’ squealy goes on to tell me about the cover up fer ol’ Raymond ‘Surly’ Shepherd. Guess Quantico better start trainin’ there fellers in the art of keeping their fucken mouth shout, eh?” The man chuckles as I stand shivering in the cold and the fear. The wind briskly laps at my skin with its icy tongue. I try to keep my attention to the man with the gun but the cold is wearing at my attention span. “You know what yer old man did, Brock-O?” he queries in all his cocky glory. Shrug at him. “He was a grocer.” The man laughs. Not the kind of laugh that is contagious and gets everyone going around him but the kind that sends a numbing fear through your central nervous system and trickles a little wee out your pee-hole. Well, that wasn’t completely true, the big guy began to laugh horribly which made my arsehole pucker one more time. “A grocer? HA! Nice try.” He raises the gun to aim at my face. “Try again.” Put my forearms in front of my face as if they might have been bulletproof and protected me from a barrage of bullets hurtling through the air toward my demise. Shout in response from behind my force field of flesh and bone. “A GROCER! HE WAS A FUCKEN GROCER! I SWEAR! THAT’S ALL I KNOW!” I scream in attempts to create some persuasion. The guy lowers his weapon in confusion. Scratches at his chin and looks at his companion. Big guy shrugs at him and walks over to me, drilling my sternum with a rib shattering punch like an explosion out of nowhere, expelling all the breath from my body and dropping me to my knees. Axe-handle down on back of my neck pushes me to all fours again. “Don’t fuck with me, kid. You know damn well who your father was. Maybe I oughta get those bolt cutters,” remarks the gunman. I gulp at my fear, trying to swallow it back down into my belly. “You’re tryin’ to tell me that ya old man was a fucking green grocer? Do I look like an idiot?” Stare up at him in pure confusion. I heard of stranglers that offer “protection” in return for payment, as in “we don’t fuck your joint up if you gimme 20%” kinda style, but seven years was a long time to hold a debt for a dead man. Why hadn’t they come at me earlier? Especially when I was earning a bit more of the folding stuff. What these guys had to do with my old man was something I couldn’t make any sense of. Particularly when the guy with gun makes references to “Quantico” and the “FBI”. Not the usual topics of conversation in regards to a green grocer’s career. “I swear, on my father’s grave…” I trail off, realising where that was leading to. Then another thought struck out at me. “Did you say cover up?” He scratches at his chin with the muzzle of his gun. “You really don’t know? Fuck. I seen your wrestling shows. You’re not that good an actor. I’d say he was tellin’ the truth. Whaddaya reckon, Dominic?” Dominic shrugs. “If my beatin’s don’t get nothin’ from the motherfucker I’d say ya pretty close ta bein’ right on the cash, Bruno.” Bruno, the man with the gun, slowly nods. “You’re tryin’ to tell me ya didn’t know yer old man was a Fed? The travel never threw a clue to you?” “He said he was the head of the Washington Green Grocer’s Association. Why wouldn’t I believe him?” Bruno nods. “Why wouldn’t ya? Fair question.” Stands there in thought a moment before shooting a shrug in my direction. “You’re no fucken good to us then.” I rise to my feet, covered in mud and teinted by my own sick upon my chest. My blood is chilled to the bone and my body bruised by Dominic’s brutality. Most of all, my heart was on fire and rage boiled within my belly. Pure fury was rising through my body as everything of my childhood was coming through to me as one big, fat lie. “I’m very fucken good to you.” “How so?” Wipe my face on my forearm and realise that there are tears running down my face. Can hear my teeth grinding upon one another inside my mouth. My pulse echoes loudly in my head as the anger begins consuming me. “Because now Surly has a real pissed off motherfucker on his tail. I want in.” Bruno shakes his head and makes his way back to the car, opening the door and leaning on the frame, preparing to get back inside the vehicle. “No. Not happening.” Thumbs himself in his own chest. “This ain’t the place for some softcock wrestler faggot.” I step forward; Dominic tries to get in my way by putting his palm on my chest. I slap it away and he swings with his other fist. Aiming to put some hurt in my lips. Instead, I duck into it, dropping my shoulder into his ribs and pulling him up into a Fireman’s carry. Swing his feet behind me and drop down for a neck breaker. Dominic squeals like a little girl from the stiffness of the neck breaker. Clutching at his neck in agony, frightened it might be broken. Bruno reaches for his gun but four quick strides forward and I kick his car door hard into his ribs. His gun spills free in the mud. It’s right between the both of us. “Pick it up, tough guy.” He looks at it. Then looks at me. Then back at the cannon lying helpless in the mud. Diving, he makes his snatch at the gun. Stomp on his wrist and then the other as it fleetingly grabs forward at the gun. Bruno clutches at both his wrists. I pick up the gun. Lower it. Aiming it at his head. He presses his back to the car and sighs heavily, closing his eyes. “Get it over with.” “I’m not a killer. That’s your job remember.” “Fuck you,” he replies. Shake my head. “Tsk. Tsk. Tsk. Don’t speak mean to the man with the cannon now, Bruno. Always wanted to a shoot a gun, see what it feels like.” “Heard ya liked choppin’ paws of broads.” Pull the hammer back and take my aim. This time he is the one using his forearms as a force field. “Listen in, Bruno. You include me on this or I change my mind about me being a killer.” Bravely, he peers around his forearms, with a look of confusion on his face. “You wanna find my father. I wanna find my father. You give me five fucken minutes with him before you do what you gotta do and we’re square.” “Square? No such fucken thing.” Shrug at him. “Maybe not in your world but there is in mine. Five minutes and I can help you find him.” “And if I say no?” “I waste two bullets. One on you and one on the whiny bitch over there,” I tell him, motioning to Dominic who rolls around clutching at his neck in the mud. Bruno thinks for a little while, pressed up against the side of the car. He plucks a cigarette from his jacket pocket and lights it with a Zippo, taking a long draw on the smoke before pluming a large cloud out before him. He shrugs. “Deal. Gimme my gun.” “Fuck you. No deal. Drive me home and I’ll consider it.” He smirks. “Remind me to never fuck over my kids. You got balls, Brock-O.” Shake my head. “You ain’t seen nothin’ yet.” Nothing at all. - | s h e p | - The man’s phone rings. He quickly peers down at the table in front of him to check the caller ID on his mobile. He flips it open and puts it to his ear. “Speak to me.” “We’ve got him.” “Got who?” “Shepherd.” He smiles to himself, raising his latte to his lips and take a quick sip of celebration before responding. “Excellent. Was it difficult?” “Dominic had to rough him up a little but everything occurred as expected. He was pushed into a corner and then he bit back. Dominic’s in a bad way.” “How bad?” “Broken neck.” “No shit.” “Bruno reckons he’s just like his old man.” “Compliment to him, I’m sure. Oh well, you gotta break a few eggs to bake a cake.” “You sure he’s the right one for this?” He chuckles to himself. “Dead set certainty. Where are they now?” “They’re on the goose chase.” “Good job, Paullie.” “Thank-you. Please to finally repay my debt to you.” “It’s not paid but it’s a start. Gotta go. Talk soon. Don’t mess this up.” “No way, Surly. Won’t let ya down.” Surly Shepherd folds his phone up and rests it beside the latte. Snatching up his caffeinated beverage to take a long sip in thought. “You’ll be safe with me, my son,” he whispered to nobody in particular. “I promise.”
