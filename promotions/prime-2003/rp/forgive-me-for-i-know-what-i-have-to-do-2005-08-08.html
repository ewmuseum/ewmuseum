Forgive me for I know what I have to do.

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/2005 ~The power of prayer. Prayer might be the one universal thing that links all people together. In every major religion, there is prayer to the superior being of that particular religion. Kano, believe it or not, is a religious person. He thanks God every day for his God-given ability to entertain thousands of people at a time. He spent about three years following his brother's death, being pissed off at God. He was convinced that God hated him, and Kano hated him back. It was an easy cop-out for Kano. Then prior to his entering PCW where Kano made himself a legend, something happened. Kano was sitting in his office at Kano Towers and began thinking about his life. He thought about how he and his brother played together when they were growing up, how they dominated the sports at their high school, how they served together in the Army, and how he cried when his brother died. He leaned back in his chair and in the midst of thinking of his life, he fell asleep. He dreamt of walking with his brother inside of a church. He and his brother conversed about God, life, and their past. When Kano awoke, he was so moved by his dream, that he dropped to his knees and prayed for forgiveness. He spent hours praying to God to be forgiven for what he had said and done to and about God. After doing this, he went to his old church and gave a hundred thousand dollars to his church. In the wrestling lifestyle led by all wrestlers, it is nearly impossible for wrestlers to lead the good, wholesome life that all people should lead, according to the Bible. Kano doesn't even try to lead the ideal life. Kano is an alcoholic, a violent man, and egotistical. Kano is probably one of the poster boys on how to go straight to hell. Yet, as bad as he is, he still prays to God for strength, when Kano feels he needs it. Kano has been to hell and back. He realizes that this ReVolution could very well be the last straw and he might just quit wrestling in disgust for what he has become. Many people doubt this, given that Kano breathes wrestling. Kano bleeds wrestling. Kano sweats wrestling. Kano and wrestling are one in the same. But those same doubters also said Kano was just a fad and he would never amount to anything. Kano has proved them wrong before and on ReVolution and beyond, he plans on proving them wrong once again. He plans on beginning another chapter in his legendary career and further cement himself as a legend.~ *The scene opens up inside of a large, beautiful Catholic Cathedral in Salt Lake City, Utah. Jon Kano, dressed to the nines, as always, in a red Armani suit, is seen kneeling at the altar at the very front of the church, just below the huge sculpture of Jesus Christ, bloodied and beaten on the cross against the backdrop of stained glass. As the camera slowly makes its way towards Kano, it takes shots of the beautiful architecture and artwork on the ceiling of the cathedral. Finally the camera stops about fifteen feet behind Kano, where it can just barely pick up what Kano is saying. Kano, obviously, does not know that the camera is recording everything he does and says.* Kano: OUR FATHER most holy,our Creator and Redeemer, our Saviour and our Comforter. WHO ART IN HEAVENin the angels and the saints,giving them light to know you,since you, Lord, are light;setting them afire to love you,since you, Lord, are love;dwelling in themand giving them fullness of joy,since you, Lord,are the supreme, eternal good,and all good comes from you. HALLOWED BE THY NAME,may we grow to know you better and betterand so appreciate the extent of your favors,the scope of your promises,the sublimity of your majesty,and the profundity of your judgements. THY KINGDOM COME,so that you may reign in us by your grace,and bring us to your kingdom,where we shall see you clearly,love you perfectly and,happy in your company, enjoy you forever. THY WILL BE DONE ON EARTH AS IT IS IN HEAVEN,so that we may love you with all our heart,by always having you in mind;with all our soul,by always longing for you;with all our mind,by determining to seek your glory in everything;and with all our strength, of body and soul,by lovingly serving you alone.May we love our neighbors as ourselves,and encourage them all to love you,by bearing our sharein the joys and sorrows of others,while giving offence to no one. GIVE US THIS DAY OUR DAILY BREAD,your beloved Son, our Lord Jesus Christ,so that we may remember and appreciatehow much He loved us,and everything he said and did and suffered. AND FORGIVE US OUR TRESPASSES,in your immeasurable mercy,by virtue of the passion of your Son,and through the intercession of Mary,and all your saints. AS WE FORGIVE THOSE WHO TRESPASS AGAINST US,and if we do not forgive perfectly,Lord, make us forgive perfectly,so that, for love of you,we may really forgive our enemies,and fervently pray to you for them,returning no one evil for evil,but trying to serve you in everyone. AND LEAD US NOT INTO TEMPTATION,be it hidden or obvious,sudden or persistent. BUT DELIVER US FROM EVIL,past, present or future, *Kano pauses and sighs deeply, not unclasping his hands, nor opening his eyes, and keeping his head bowed down.* Kano: Lord Jesus, give me the strength to carry on. Father, forgive me for what I am going to do. I have trials and tribulations, Lord. I have spent the better part of six months wondering if this is what I have become a shell of my former professional self. But I now know what I must do. Everyone wants the old Jon Kano. Everyone wonders where the old Jon Kano is at. Everyone includes myself. You gave me this ability to entertain millions of people and for that I am grateful, but my ability has been adversely affected in recent months and I pray for my ability to return. I know I must meet my ability half way on this and return to the Jon Kano of old. I must become the man I once was in PCW, when I was king. At ReVolution 72, I face a newcomer by the name of Damien Cruz. He has quite a history in wrestling, but never on a stage as vast and impressive as PRIME. I will... *Kano takes a deep breath and another moment to contemplate his words.* Kano: I will make an example out of him. He will be my stepping stone, my turning point. I feel for him, oh Lord, do I feel for him. It is nothing personal, and Jesus, don't let me hurt him too badly. I know to make an example out of him, I will have to make him wish he never got into the ring with me, but I don't want to hurt him so severely that it might impair his career. *Kano unclasps his hands, opens his eyes, and runs his hands through his long, blonde hair. He is still unaware of the camera videotaping him. Kano closes his eyes and clasps his hands once again.* Kano: I know that the Old School Jon Kano will get the job done, he almost always did. I also know I will lose fans, friends, and popular support when the bastard Jon Kano comes to the forefront once again. I am willing to accept this. Damien Cruz thinks he is going to get one over on Jon Kano and make a splash in PRIME right off the bat by knocking off a legend. I have made a career out of giving people like him a reality check. From what I hear, he is as quick as lightning, but speed does kill. I am not the slowest person in the world, and just when he thinks he might have me, I will have him. And before he knows what the hell just happened, he will be flat on his back. He will be looking up at the lights on the arena ceiling, and like almost in a dream, he will hear the referee count one, two, three. The only difference will be he will feel the pain, unlike a dream. It will be pure reality as the thoughts run through his mind like so many other victims of mine. He will wonder if it was real, he will wonder if he can actually move, he will wonder why he ever got in the ring with the legend of Jon Kano. He will be just a statistic after our match, and Lord, he will, just like all the others find out what REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. Jesus, forgive me, for I know not what I do, but what I must do. Amen. *The scene fades to black as Kano stands up and in Catholic fashion, crosses himself, his eyes transfixed on the sculpture of the cross.* ~If I am allowed one small pun...Jesus Christ, Kano seems motivated. What exactly did Kano mean by, 'The Old Jon Kano?' Has Kano truly found the last piece or two to reclaim his gift that made him so great in years gone by? Does Damien Cruz know what awaits him at ReVolution? Is Jon Kano prepared to take it to the next level and do what is necessary to regain his former glory? I guess we will all have to watch ReVolution 72 to find the answers to all of these questions and probably more.~
