Is this the final time?

13 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05-13-05 I can hear the sounds of the traffic speeding by the outside of the truck as I sit in the back and wait for it to arrive at the border between Mexico and the United States. I've already whore myself out trying to destroy everything in this damned prison, my frustration pouring from my very soul as I do. Once more I'm reminded just how devious the mind of Angelo Deville can be. I've seen first hand now, on several occasions, the fact that he'll stop at nothing to make sure my life is a living hell. Some would say that I've brought it all on myself, and in all honesty, how could I disagree? If not for deciding to help Rage in the Halo, Deville simply wouldn't even be a factor in my career at this point. Had I but made a few different decisions, in my PRIME career, then I’d more than likely already have gotten my hands on the World Tag Team Championships...that have eluded my grasp to this point. I've allowed myself to be manipulated into missing the goals that I have set out for myself here in PRIME. I've gone about all the situations that have been set before me with a terrible attitude and my eye off the ball so to speak. Being so wrapped up in everyone wanting to screw me simply cause I'm Mexican has made me forget the fact that no one in PRIME is attempting to screw us. I've lost my shot to be a champion here in this company on two occasions already, worrying about other people, and not focusing on what I should have been. Every since I joined PRIME in the middle of January I've allowed myself to be defined by the fact that I am Mexican and, along with my manager and partner Angelus, I was going to rid the world of all the racism and stereo typing of Mexican wrestlers everywhere. The thing I've failed to realize is that this cause simply doesn't win matches. It doesn't impress any of my opponents and it sure as hell doesn't make them fear Los Diablos. Winning is the only thing that demands respect. As of late I've been very up and down when it comes to winning and losing. In fact, if the truth is told, I’ve lost more than I’ve won thus far since coming into the spotlight of a big time wrestling company. I've lost two high profile World Tag Team Title matches; I've lost a high profile singles match up, my singles debut in fact, to Angelo Deville. Worse yet I allowed Hessian to throw Enrique around like a rag doll without so much as stepping up to him and extending him the same respect. There can simply be no more of these types of things. The time has arrived that I step out of the shadows of fighting this false battle of racism. Time to come out from behind the shadows of being a Mexican wrestler and simply become a great tag team wrestler. If I am to be feared I have to start acting like, and proving, that I am a man to be feared, respected, and taken seriously. That type of respect and honor will not come from running out to the ring unexpectedly and beating the hell out Deville in order to cost him the chance to win matches. Sure it felt great at the time, and I am not sorry I did it, but the fact of the matter is that it did nothing to make him respect me. When he looks into my eyes during our match at OverKill he won't be showing fear, respect, or anything that I've strived to obtain. He'll only show hatred, contempt, and fiery anger toward Angelus and myself. The Tag Team Championships of the World are the prize that is on the line officially. But this match is about, and is for, so much more. It's for that honor, pride, respect, and to some extent fear, to be taken by Los Diablos. Thus far all we've done is bellowed about how it's owed to us just because we put our names on a couple of PRIME contracts. The world simply doesn't work that way and it's time that we all woke up and realized that. Angelo Deville you have been a thorn in my side for a long while now, and a thorn that I'm sure will be in my side for the rest of my career. Even with a Los Diablos win and the Tag Team Championships firmly in our possession at the end of our match at OverKill, I've still got this feeling that down the line we'll be crossing paths again. People have been saying in the weeks leading up this Pay Per View that our match would be the one. This being the final match up, so to speak, between the teams of Manifest Destiny and Los Diablos. In some way I can see how that would be possible in the eyes of those who don't know any better. All those gringos out there who simply don't understand how being disrespected by someone who you have little to no respect for on a personal level can drive a man to his wits end. I'm sure that in the near future that our paths will discontinue to cross, momentarily, should Los Diablos finally do what they've set out to do all along. Capture the World Tag Team Championships. Make no mistake about this fact, Deville. While I may not like you on a personal level, and I'm sure it's quite clear that I do not, there is no disrespect for you on a professional level. You've been to the top of the mountains more times than I've been in big time matches thus far. You’ve accomplished more in your career already, then I’ve had the opportunity to obtain. So on a professional level that respect is there. I'm sure you've got a certain amount of respect for myself, even though I've accomplished little thus far, simply by the fact that you've been in a battle every time we've faced. Hatred is a funny thing, Senor Deville, as it can drive a man to his very grave. Destroying that which is the target of his hatred can be the thing that consumes him so greatly that he can't even begin to imagine a day in his life when his soul purpose isn't attempting to destroy the object of his burning rage. I've allowed myself to be consumed by just such a rage, my whole life, when it's come to stereo types and racism...at least the idea that I've been facing them anyway. OverKill is our proving ground Senor, and I'm not just talking about the team of Los Diablos. I know Manifest Destiny has plenty to prove themselves as well. Everyone knows what the two of you have accomplished on your own, but you've both got something nagging you on the way into this one. Williamson needs to prove to himself, just as much as he needs to prove it to the world, that he is more than some simple hardcore attraction. He has that fire in the pit of his stomach to walk out of Mexico with the gold still around his waist in order to prove that big match situations haven't gotten to be too much for him just yet. Angelo Deville you've got more than enough to prove to yourself as well. You might not have to prove anything in anyone else's eyes, at this point, but I can look into your eyes and tell that there is something driving you and its more than simply sticking it to me. That has become a great side joy for you during our months here thus far, but I can tell you're now driven by another purpose. The loss to Aimz, my attack of you not with standing, has lit a fire inside of an already dangerous man. I can see it, feel it, and know it deep in my soul as I looked into your eyes at ReVolution tonight. That loss last week has you so pissed off that you want nothing more than to prove it was an absolute fluke. Now you're off to Mexico to face the bane of your PRIME career, thus far, and that is eating at you as well. Its not just the fact that we’re in Mexico, though I imagine for a man like you that is enough reason to be pissed off. I can only imagine with the fact that the person who you clearly value more than any other in this world, except maybe yourself, is now going to have to face Aimz all alone is an even greater problem for you at this point. Do you fear for The K-Wolf? I've been in the ring with the former Universal Champion and I can tell you that she has nothing to fear. She doesn't need you, Deville, and I'm sure you have to be smart enough to realize this. That is why I know, deep down, that it isn't even Karina being there to go it alone. You have distaste for Amy Campbell that is rivaled only by that distaste you have for me. Costing her the triple threat with Karina and your own partner had absolutely nothing to do with Karina, and everything to do with you. That hatred for Aimz has to only be burning worse inside of you as you think about the fact that you won't be getting your hands on her this week. This week, my dear rival, is all about us. It’s all about the two of us going at it one more time, in an attempt, to destroy one another. This thing between us, it will never be over, only in a temporary sleep mode. For now, Deville, though we'll look at this as the final page in this chapter of our little history. The final page of the third chapter when history is written in stone for the whole world to know forever more. The history making event that we’ve been long over due in accomplishing since joining the PRIME federation. Los Diablos...World Tag Team champions...finally. May 8th, 2005: 10 AM 'Are you ready for this evening big man?' Asked Enrique as he finished putting on his warm up suit, preparing to go out and train with me for my big Atlantic City triple threat match with Munson and Almasy. 'As ready as I could be I suppose essa,' I replied with a shrug of my shoulders. The honest truth of the matter was that this match, while important and all, wasn't really the first and most important thing on my mind. OverKill and another shot at Angelo Deville was the only thing that seemed to make its way into my thoughts every day. 'You don't seem very convinced my dear friend. What has your mind all in a clutter? Is it more of this OverKill stuff?' Enrique questioned, just as he finished tying his shoes. As he stood up I could only smirk. I had to give it to the guy, since we'd met several years ago in his fathers wrestling facility; he'd learned to read me like the back cover of a book. At times he seemed to know me better than I knew myself, kind of like the words to a person’s favorite song. 'You have a way of reading my mind Senor Morales. I can't seem to shake the thoughts of beating Deville and Williamson, not even long enough to focus on a tough set of opponents like Munson and Almasy. Its stupid, yes, and I know it will wind up being a major pitfall for me in this match essa.' I respond as I stand up from my chair at the kitchen bar, having just finished my bowl of cereal. Crackling Oat Brand was the only thing that could get me going right of a morning, and if I don't have that, I'm just a very piss poor person to be around. 'Big man, in all honesty, you're as ready as you will ever be to face off with Deville. Angelus has trained harder than ever and he is more than ready to finally help you capture those elusive tag team titles. Today you should have nothing more than performing before the Core Wrestling fans and proving to them just why you're one of the fastest rising stars in this business today. A win over the likes of Almasy and the Core Wrestling Champion will put you on the map for all the world to see,' Enrique replied with a pat on my shoulder. His confidence in Angelus, and myself for that matter, was unwavering. No matter how many set back we seemed to come across he'd never turn his back on us, berate us, or even lose an ounce of confidence in us. At times it was almost as if the guy simply believed that by believing in us, we'd come out on top. 'Besides,' he continued while pouring himself some orange juice,'you've been training hard for a shot like this your whole life. Just remember that winning or losing in an event like this isn't what is important. You’re an outsider going into a foreign promotion, so you're not expected to pull of this type of upset. If you do that is wonderful but even if you don't all that matters is you go in there and prove that you belong in the ring with anyone in the world. A great showing tonight will give you leverage within PRIME to get a bigger shot at the spotlight.' 'That all sounds great and even what we've all worked so hard to achieve, but it hardly seems right. I mean Angelus is my partner and we've busted our asses to get shots at being the best tag team in the world. I sort of feel guilty for taking a spot in a deal like this and possibly getting a bigger name then him in this business.' I replied with a heavy sigh. While I knew that Angelus would never deny me the right, nor would he ever get bitter about such things, to make a bigger star of myself, it still just never seemed right. 'You know better than that Baltasar. You're like a brother to Angelus and he'll always be more than happy that you've made a bigger name for yourself. Besides all that he knows that the bigger attraction that one of you is, then the bigger attraction the two of you are going to be together. Angelus will have his time to shine and prove himself on his own as well, but he knows his time hasn't arrived yet.' Enrique replied before downing the remaining contents of his juice glass. He then gave me a big smile and waved toward the door. “Now come on we’ve got some serious training to get in before we head to the arena later today. It's time to work out some of those butterflies and worries,' he says as he heads toward the door, grabbing the keys and his jacket. All I can do is smile as well and follow him out the door and toward my future it would appear. May 8th, 2005: 11pm The arena is almost completely empty by this point as I sit alone in the locker room, still hurting from my match with Munson and Almasy a little over an hour earlier. The pain comes mostly from the physical side of things, as Munson and Almasy both worked me over pretty good, but the emotional side is pretty strong as well. I gave a hell of a performance tonight in front of a crowd of many whom has never seen me before. To them I was just some other gringo from another promotion that only believes itself to be bigger and better than Core Wrestling. Still, despite that fact, and despite the fact that I came out on the losing end those fans still stood up and showed their appreciation for what I had done. They all stood up and gave me one of the loudest, in fact probably the loudest reactions I've ever received from a crowd in appreciation for the heart and soul I put into winning this interpromotional match up. I can't lie to myself, if felt pretty damn good to have them taking notice of what I had nearly accomplished in the ring tonight. I was able to take two of the worlds best wrestlers today to the very limits of their abilities. Dominating huge portions of the match up and coming up just a little bit shy of the victory I could just taste. Suddenly my thoughts are interrupted as Peter Vetra walks into my locker room. He can't see the pain on my face, as its hidden not by my mask, as it would normally be, but by the towel I have hanging down over my head. 'Hey champ,' Vetra began with the energy of a fan,'you sure looked great out there tonight. Man I thought you had that match under wraps on about ten different occasions! It was simply awe inspiring to see you in action tonight. If you'll bring that kind of intensity, turned up just a little bit more, into OverKill this next weekend you'll be sure to take home the tag team championships.' At first all I do is simply grunt in response, not really in the mood to talk at the moment, but without even hearing him say a word I can tell he is a bit hurt by my lack of conversation. Vetra and me haven't been friends for very long, but since forming The Imperium with Hoyt, we've become pretty good friends. He was the one there when Deville and Williamson got off with their win last time around, the guy who came to our defense when Deville tried to have us deported illegally. In short Vetra, despite being one of the toughest men alive today, has been quite the good friend. 'Thanks PV, ' I reply as I extend my right hand forward so he can give me five. 'I appreciate your being here tonight, in the crowd, to cheer me on and watch my back. I only wish that I'd be there at OverKill to extend you the same respect essa. Unfortunately this thing with Deville has grown to a far bigger problem then I could have ever imagined, and it needs to be taken care of in the most brutal way possible.' 'Don't even worry about that type of crap! You've had my back plenty already, and you'll have it many more times down the road. Besides I've got OverKill in the bag and I'm sure to bring some more gold to The Imperium,' he began before a sly grin crossed his face. 'It will look real nice around my waist as we stand in the ring the following ReVolution and show off your tag titles as well. We are fast approaching the moment when The Imperium takes its rightful place as the strongest force not only in PRIME currently, but in the history of PRIME.' 'Yes I suppose we are due to make some history making of our own in the next couple of weeks. I imagine that most people will feel like its about time, since we've been talking a huge game as of late, but only Hoyt has had any gold to back it up at any point. It will be nice once we've gotten that monkey off our back essa and put ourselves firmly in the history books of PRIME as champions.' I reply as Vetra walks across the room and sits down on one of the benches along the far wall. As he makes his way over to his seat I simply let out a quiet sigh, with my thoughts no turning back to their original focus. The focus that has never really completely been out of my mind as of late. The match up with Manifest Destiny in a Mexican Prison match. The shot, possibly for the last time, to take the gold off the waists of the men who have kept me from them for far too long? Possibly my final shot for a while to put the big one over on Angelo Deville. The time is fast approaching and the window of opportunity, for proving myself to be the equal of Angelo Deville, is quickly closing. At OverKill it's going to be now or never time. At OverKill it is going to be Los Diablos obtaining the status of champions or fading away once more to be mocked for our inability to win the big one. OverKill is going to be me or Angelo Deville. May 13th, 2005 { 3:42 am } Sweat pours down my face, chest, and back as my eyes shoot open and I begin to stare around the dark room. OverKill is a mere two days away now and still I am unable to get any real rest. My whole body is wrecked with pain and fatigue from the events that have transpired over the last several days. 'What about you, Senor Deville? Do you sit up tonight and wonder about our upcoming match? Does the thoughts of the violence that will occure in that prison keep you up at night, nightmares keeping you from being able to sleep peacefully?' Are the questions I ask out loud in order to try and make myself feel better about the fact that despite going toe to toe with Deville on several occassions now, and more than holding my own, I still have a certain amount of fear in me when it comes to facing off with Deville. 'You can smell that fear can't you Deville?' I sigh as I pull the covers back up to my chest, trying once more to grab the hard slumber that continues to elude my spirit. My heart begins to pound a little faster and harder as more and more thoughts about my encounters with Deville over the last few months flood into my brain. Bright and viberate images of being beaten down with brass knuckles, chairs, locked in trucks, tasered, and thrown into handcuffs. Is it possible that I'm just never going to over come the shadow of Deville that I've created for myself? Will attacking him during the Halo really haunt my entire PRIME career, as he said it would? When will I be able to over come this fear of failure that I seem to attach to walking into a match with Deville? Will beating him for the titles with Angelus this weekend be the thing I need to make it all end? These are questions that I must get answers too when we step into that prison for our Pay Per View match, and they are questions that I must put to rest once and for all. 'No matter what the answers turn out to be.'
