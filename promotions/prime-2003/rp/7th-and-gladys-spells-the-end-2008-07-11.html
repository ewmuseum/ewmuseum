7th and Gladys Spells The End

11 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/11/08 "It’s getting late," Melissa reminded Brandon with a subtle shout. She was lying on her back, her body relaxing into the couch cushions as her eyes moved towards the ceiling. After thirty seconds without an answer from her husband, Melissa straightened up against the armrest her head had previously rested and stared towards the kitchen. "Maybe we order some Chinese?" Outside of her view, Brandon rolled the Percocets in his hand and filled a glass with tap water. He closed his eyes, shoving the four tablets in his mouth before taking a hearty gulp. Wobbling his head, he looked in the general direction of the living room. "If you want." "You want the mongolian beef?" she asked as she slinked back into comfort, her loose fitting t-shirt riding up her back as she pressed into the couch cushions. Anxiously bopping her bare feet over the couch’s edge, she rubbed the stretched skin of her protruding stomach as she waited for a response. Brandon pondered the question for a moment as a sudden bout of heartburn started to grip his sternum. His left hand aggressively rubbed against the contour of his nose as the nails of his right scratched the formica. "You know what?" he started, lingering on the rhetorical question for a few moments. "I think I’ll pass. If that’s what you want, go right ahead though." She focused her eyes on her stomach as she heard Brandon in the other room. "So where you off to tonight?" Melissa couldn’t help but grow bitter for being abandoned once again. Brandon hung his head as he appeared from the kitchen. "Don’t be like that," he started as he walked down the corridor to the bathroom. Shutting the door, he flipped the light switch and opened the medicine cabinet, fishing through the shelves of messily placed make-up for Melissa’s lipstick case of Dermablend. "I have to go out and see some of the kids at the show." "What show?" she yelled as she rolled her eyes. "You closed that wrestling school weeks ago." He rolled the bottom of the stick and brought his eyebrows down so he could stretch his forehead taut. The fresh pink of newly minted horizontal scar tissue darted in a three inch line near the crown of his head. Staring at his reflection in the mirror and applying a rudimentary touch with the stick across the scar, he tried to maintain his concentration despite Melissa’s griping. With a sharp wince, he continued to dab the scar before it disappeared underneath the cover of chemicals. Reeling the stick of make-up back inside of its container, he put the Dermablend back where he found it and cautiously shut the cabinet. He judiciously examined his forehead one last time to make sure it looked normal before he exited the bathroom, moving back into the bedroom to grab his backpack. Melissa tightened her lips together in a scowl as she stared at Brandon from the doorway of their bedroom. He caught her look with a glance as he zipped his backpack shut, trying his best not to look her in the eye. "I thought you said you were done with this thing," she relaxed her shoulders, letting her mood shift. They’d had some form of this conversation thousands of times, and each one was just as pointless. It wasn’t about wrestling for Brandon. "Look, those guys need me to look after them." She cast her head downward toward the wooden floor, making sure she was gazing away from her husband. "Come here," she ordered as she rested her hands underneath her jutting stomach. Brandon slung the backpack over his shoulder and ambled slowly towards his wife. "What?" She grabbed his wrist, guiding her husband’s palm against her bulging bulb. Seconds passed with nothing, but then he felt the faint tremors coming from the inside. He narrowed his eyes as he looked towards the door. "It’s kicking," she whispered as she released Brandon’s hand. He looked down to her eyes, his hand still hanging in the air. "Yeah, he is." He brought his hand in, pressing his body against the door to avoid bumping into her as he crossed past her in the entranceway. Brandon tried his best not to slam the door shut as he left. *** Arms wrapped around his chest, Brandon fought a losing battle against the Los Angeles breeze armed with only chattering teeth and gooseflesh skin. Once the stoplight turned green, his arms snapped into action and his hands gripped the steering wheel. He’d avoided the Historic Core, roving without any problems until he saw the sign for Gladys Avenue. When he went to turn, he faintly saw the LED sign underneath a bank logo say it was ninety-three degrees outside. With a furious stomp, the brakes screeched to a halt "Bullshit," he spit through haggard breaths, his left hand rubbing mere inches away from the make-up on his clammy forehead. "It’s freezing in here." His attention more focused on fiddling with the climate control console in his car than the surrounding traffic, he was soon jostled from his stupor by a cacophony of car horns from disgruntled late-night Downtown travelers who didn’t take kindly to having someone's car in both the incoming and outgoing lanes. Stiffening up, Brandon stomped the accelerator pedal and jerked the wheel hard to the left. As he turned onto Gladys Avenue, gravity pressed him into the upholstery of his Lincoln Town Car, his attempts to remain rigid proving futile as vibrations began rattling through the car’s interior. He finally relaxed as he passed by the Taiwan Seafood and Fish Corp, his eyes glancing toward the clock near his dashboard. Eight o'clock. This was more of a suggestion because Brandon never knew which clock to set his car's to. Every bank seemed to be running their times a few minutes later than the one he had on his cell phone, and radio programs were all over the three minute map on both ends of the spectrum. The sun was already starting to make its descent, the shadows cascading from buildings bathing the concrete valley mere feet away from Skid Row in shade. Brandon once ran a training ground for those wishing to become involved in professional wrestling mere blocks from here, trying his best to lend his guidance to the barrel full of multicultural slop that called this destitute hole a home. He’d promised twelve young men that they could escape the chains of poverty if they stayed quiet and let him steer them in the right direction. After driving them to the point where they could work small shows, he took their meager wages and funneled them into his pockets. His final betrayal was the unannounced ‘closed’ sign he left festering behind the glass entrance door of their former training ground. He’d avoided Skid Row since then, choosing instead to tease his presence in its outskirts. He slowly drove on Gladys, his eyes tepidly examining the refuse which had filtered onto the sidewalks. They were all light mocha, the vinyl of their skin tight miniskirts short enough to show their garter straps. This block was for those seeking the flavor of people from south of the border. There were a mixture of body shapes and sizes to choose from, all within earshot of their pimps. They looked ominous as they sauntered across the sidewalk, their eyes transfixed on his car as it moseyed down the street. Brandon eased to the side of the road, decelerating as he studied some latinos who caught his fancy. When his eyes caught sight of a piece he’d never seen before, his heart raced. He moved to a complete stop, pressing the button to the passenger side window down. "Hey," he stammered as he put the car in park. "How much you charging?" "Thirty for a hand job, fifty for a blow job, one hundred for thirty minutes." Brandon unlocked the doors of his car, chewing his nails after moved back to his seat. "You a cop or something?" Youngblood looked away for a moment. "What do you think?" After a few moments, his door opened. "Don’t worry, I ain’t no cop." "I know," he told Brandon. The male prostitute’s hand gently massaged the outer portion of Youngblood’s lap as he began to drive, weaving through the dilapidated colon of Los Angeles until he happened upon the Evenox Motel. It was a one story stretch, the white namesake letters standing on the roof thanks to steel supports held in place by rusted metal brackets. The walkways leading to the rooms were caked with grime, the white paint crusted over with cobwebs and traces of exhaust from cars. Parking the car next to his room, Brandon reached into the backseat and collected his backpack before exiting. His partner followed him out the door. "What’s your name?" Brandon asked, unable to look at his prostitute. "You know what? Forget I asked," he quickly interrupted before he’d received an answer. Youngblood fumbled in his jean pocket for his room key as a reassuring hand touched his free shoulder. With a subtle rub, his prostitute was doing the best he could to relax his client. "My name is Pedro. And don’t hurry. You aren’t wasting time on my clock." The encouragement caused shivers to crawl up Brandon’s spine. When he finally found his key, he quickly pushed it in and opened the door. "You told me more than I wanted to know." "But you asked." It was as if Pedro was playing a role, the octaves of his voice hitting the nasal droll of a stereotypical homosexual border jumper. "I don’t like knowing that sort of shit. Makes things personal," he responded as he held the door open for Pedro, motioning with his head for the Mexican prostitute to enter the motel room. His demeaning nature grabbed hold of his psyche to ensure his stomach could stay settled. "Then why you—," Pedro tried to say before Brandon grabbed him by the collar of the shirt and yanked him inside. With a slam of the door, Youngblood rolled his shoulders and sized him up. It didn’t take much; Pedro stood almost six inches shorter than he did. All he had was a backpack to go along with his cut off jean shorts and his checkered t-shirt. "Look," Brandon started as he stabbed his finger into his prostitute’s chest. "Let’s not make a big deal out of this. Do you want to make a big deal out of this?" Youngblood was sure to let Pedro see his biceps as he tensed them, lazily folding his arms across his massive chest. Pedro looked away, slipping the backpack off as he took a seat on one of the twin beds. "No sir. I’m new to this country." Youngblood could have hit him with any number of scornful responses, but chose not to. His lips fidgeting, he closed his eyes as he walked the practiced path to the wooden table in the middle of the room. "It’s alright." Letting his blood pressure simmer, Brandon relaxed and cracked his back after he put his backpack on the table. "You have anything to take the edge off?" Pedro’s question was rather direct, causing Brandon to crane his head toward him. "What do you mean by take the edge off?" "You know what I mean, sir." Brandon turned his body around fully so he could look at Pedro, his arms once again reaching for another relaxing fold. "No, actually, I don’t know what you’re talking about." "Any party favors?" Youngblood chuckled through his pursed lips. "For someone new here, you sure know your slang." Pedro looked away, his arms squirming as he puckered his lips and rapidly blinked his eyes. Brandon waved him off, turning back around to his backpack. "I ain’t got that kind of stuff." Brandon steadied his grasp against the soothing varnish of the table, more interested in the contents of his bag. "Tell you what, Pedro. I figure I pay you two hundred even, and depending on performance, I can give you a tip. And with that tip you can get yourself some chop, some black tar, some crank, or what the fuck ever. That’s up to you, amigo." Unzipping the main pouch, he pulled his prescription bottle out and tipped it over, allowing a narrow plastic vial that had been sandwiched between the pills to fall into his index and middle fingers. "What you have to say to that?" he questioned as he pulled the plastic stopper from the top of the vial, pushing the flask it into his nostril and snorting the finely cut white powder from inside. Brandon dropped it the moment he was finished, shimmy his arms to and fro before fervently petting the contours of his skull. The nervous energy of Youngblood’s body twitched away as he sought refuge on the bed. He never heard the prostitute’s bag unzip because Pedro had carefully masked the sound. He wasn’t aware of the movements Pedro had made because he’d turned his back to him. But Brandon was all too aware of the pistol’s steel barrel as it was pressed against his temple. He was in a compromised situation; he was completely at the mercy of someone else. "Look, alright," Brandon stammered as he tried to steady himself. "Alright I have almost a thousand dollars in my wallet. Take it. It’s yours. Just don’t shoot me." Uneasy seconds ticked away, the barrel remaining in place. Brandon closed his eyes, his heart beating in his throat as his fingers began to freeze. He couldn’t conceal his whimpering or the quaking of his body as his face shriveled up. Tears began cascading down his cheeks. "Please don’t do this. I got a wife. I got a child. Just take the money. Just take the money, Pedro." "Sir," Pedro started, his tone changing completely. "You’re under arrest for soliciting a prostitute as well as possession of illegal drugs." The barrel was no longer pressed against his temple. "You have the right to remain silent. Anything you say can and will be used against you in a court of law." It was as if a serrated blade had been shoved through his stomach and yanked upward with jagged pulls. The idea of being shot in a seedy motel near Skid Row was dangerous but was at least more honorable than this. At least he wouldn’t have to see his name in the papers. At least he wouldn’t have to live with the consequences. "A grand. I can take you by an ATM and pull out more. Much more," Brandon stuttered as he straightened his posture. "Ten thousand. Twenty. You don’t have to do this." But the gun was still trained on him. Brandon could have taken his chances with the cop, could have tried running for it. He could have fought him and taken his chances on the run. "Fuck that. I can give you fifty thousand dollars out of my fucking bank account." The color drained from his skin, the remains of his stomach left sputtering across the over-starched carpet of the motel room. There were no options for escape. "All you got to do is name a price." Pedro rose from the foot of the bed, keeping the gun on Brandon. "Roll over and put your arms against your back." Youngblood struggled to catch his breath, his head becoming too heavy to hold. He collapsed against the bed, haphazardly rolling onto his stomach. He placed both of his wrists against the small of his back. "Tell me something," Brandon wheezed as he looked upward towards the pillows. "What?" Tears stinging his eyes, he couldn’t help but smile at the stupidity of what he was about to ask. "Is your name even Pedro?" *** Youngblood remained stoic as he sat in his holding cell, slowly strumming the tips of his fingers against one another. His eyes were now dry from all the tears he had spilled while in this caged oblivion of sober awareness. Each blink felt like coarse sandpaper was being raked across his sclerae. "Want your phone call?" he was asked through the speaker system. He didn’t answer. He wished time moved slower, because the second he moved from this space, life as he knew it would be over. He’d never be allowed into a wrestling organization again. The old guard would never allow one of his ‘kind’ to ever be successful. And while he knew each locker room he’d ever been in contained at least one homosexual, they wouldn’t allow him the same private respect because his laundry had aired before the masses. He’d never be able to step foot into a church without the whispers of his faggotry following every soft footfall forward. Then there was Melissa. The woman he’d ended one marriage with because of a sharp blow to the jaw. She was the only woman in this world who seemed to look upon him with anything but disdain. Melissa was the one who had forgiven him in spite of the pleas from everyone she ever loved telling her how foolish she was for taking him back. She was carrying a child. Their child. His son. He couldn’t deal with anyone else knowing this secret. They could know him as a cancer, a drug fiend, but he couldn’t have everyone knowing this. But they would know. They would all know that the same Brandon Youngblood who three years prior stood before a television audience decrying others for their lifestyle choices was himself completely guilty of the same acts. That he had partook in drugs. That he had laid with other men. He pondered long and hard how he would break the news to Melissa. No realistic answer came to mind.
