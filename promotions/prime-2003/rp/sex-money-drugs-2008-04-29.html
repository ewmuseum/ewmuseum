Sex,Money & Drugs

29 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/29/08 MONEY Bonafide is seen kneeling down next to the bed counting stacks of money while his wife sits on the other side of the bed putting rubber bands around the stacks that he passes her, and she threw them into a old Nike shoe box. “Where did you get all this money honey?” “Let’s just say it fell into my hands, love.” “Okay! You don’t have to tell me twice.” “So what are we going to do with it?” “We are going to save it for a rainy day.” “You and your smart answers.” .::Bonafide phone rings::. “Hello?” “Hey what’s up man, hows it going?” “Everything is going good man, doing the family thing right now.” “Oh okay, well I’m sorry for interrupting your family time, What’s up for later on tonight? We are going to catch another basketball game?” [Laughs] “Sounds good to me, another basketball game it is.” “Okay, I will meet you at SHAKERS in two hours.” “No problem.” .::Call ends::. “Who was that baby?” "Oh, that was mark.” “What did he want? He needs to go get a job and stop trying to shine off of you.” “Hey, that’s my friend. I know him since we were little. He wanted to catch a basketball game later on.” “Okay, sounds good. I’m going out with the girls.” “Nice, have fun. Where are the kids going to be at?” “I’m taking them to my mother’s house.” “Oh no, when you get there make sure to tell her, that kids already ate.” “What are you trying to say? That, my mother cannot cook?” “No, you said that. I’m just telling you to tell her that the kids already ate.” [They both laugh] “Well I got to go ma.” Jimmy Bonafide stands up, stretches and walks to the other side of the bed here his wife is sitting down still placing rubber bands around the stacks of money. He places a kiss, amongst her beautiful Latin luscious lips of hers. “I will see you later.” “Okay, baby.” He reaches into the old Nike box, and pulls out two stacks. He kisses her again and hands her the stack, he gets up from the bed. And walks out the room as she continues putting rubber bands around the stacks. -Scene fades. SEX 2 Hours later. Music is blasting in the shakers strippers club, we see Bonafide sitting down at his usual V.I.P spot with Mark and his other friends in the background. “Hey man, how’s everything been going for you Jay?” “Man, everything been well. Everything looks like it is all falling into place.” “That’s good to hear man.” “What about our friends at prime? Mr. Jack & Jill (Jeeves).” [Laughs] “What about them? Like I told you before in time they will get taken care off. Just you wait till I get my hands on him, if there is one thing I hate, that is a liar and a cheater. And Union Jack & Jeeves are exactly that. That why this time at One-Five-Nine, I will take care of this little matter.” “Yes sir, these two butt buddies need to be put in their place.” “Yeah, real soon. Don’t worry about them.” “Hey, do you think you can get me a tony rolo autograph?” [Bonafide, looks at Mark with a stunned look] “I’m going to make believe you aint just say that.” [They both laugh] A beautiful young woman walks up to them and sits down on Bonafide lap, with a smile on her face. She is topless the only thing she has on at the moment is her red thong. But that’s nothing a little money can’t change. “Hey big boy, How are you doing?” “I’m doing good sweetie, how are you doing?” “I’m doing good, and yes! You do feel good.” “How about I take you upstairs, and show you some of my tricks.” “How about, you take me and my friend upstairs and you let us show you a couple of our tricks.” “Let’s go, big boy.” She grabs them both by the hands and begins to walk to the stairs, but Bonafide phone rings…… “Hello?” “Meet me in the hotel in an hour.” “Yeah, okay. No problem.” “What happen Jay?” Mark asked. “Nothing, you going to have to take care of this one yourself. I have some business to attend to, I will be back later on if anything. Just hit my phone up.” Bonafide rushes out the strip club, to his jeep. He jumps into his jeep turns it on, and pulls out of the shakers parking lot. -Scene ends. DRUGS We see Bonafide sitting down at a wooden round table, staring his boss Bid D in the eyes. Wondering why he needed to see him so quickly. “What’s up Big D. What is the problem?” “Nothing man, I been having a shitty weekend.” “Oh man, that sucks.” “YOU DAMN RIGHT IT SUCKS.” “Me having a shitty weekend, means I’m losing money. And I can’t have that happening, it’s not good for a business. Especially for a business man such as myself.” “Yeah, I feel you. Big D.” “So, why did you call me here?” “Yes, I need you to handle this situation for me. I had these two guys’ run off with my work. I want my work back, or the money.” “Okay, so that’s where I come in?” “Damn, you are getting smarter by the minute.” “Well I have to talk to you about something.” .::Flashback::. BLAM BLAM BLAM BLAM we see Poster Boy kneeling on the ground over his dead cousin’s body. Poster Boy holding his cousin close to him begins to speak to him knowing these are the last words that they will probably speak to each other. "Don’t worry….Everything it going to be all right junior." Poster Boy says’s as he tries to calm his cousin down. "I…..I…..I don’t want to die." Junior mumbles out of his mouth. "Don’t worry you’re going to a better place junior." He tells him. Sirens in the background begin to get closer as the eye’s of his cousins closes one last time. .::Flashback ends::. “I don’t think, I can do this anymore. This shit aint me!” “What, I know you bitchen up on me Jay. The same Jay who use to get into fights for someone stepping on your Nikes? The same Jay who shot that guy on the roof.” “It aint me anymore. I got a family, I need to think about them.” “Well, I just can’t let you go just like that.” “What do you mean, you can’t just let me go?” “One more.” “One more job and I’m done.” “Yeah, and your done!” Bonafide, get’s up from the chair with a pissed look on his face as he exits the hotel room. -End
