Adam.

31 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 31, 2007 In the Begining... To accompany the newly created earth, man was given birth. Perfected to serve as the father of many generations to come, this first species of man was donned the name Adam, meaning Humans amongst both man and woman. Amongst the harvestful fruits and evergreen plants that preceded him,this man...this breed of species stood triumphant over them all in terms of sovereignty. His assignment was simple: tend to the Garden of Eden...the most beautiful, luscious, and deluxe Garden in all of this new world. The essential tools necessary to carry out his assignment were graciously provided for Adam with only one command in place; stray away from the tree of knowledge and evil. With this command in place, two elements that corrupts today's modern society were given form... Betrayal and Disobedience. This tree that Adam was prohibited from eating of harvested fruits of death in which he was forewarned. Adam embraced this warning graciously. Yet, as it came about, Adam was deemed unable to carry out the form of life destined for him in a lone state. In order to expand the perfect society that had been presented before him, he needed a partner...someone to share in the overall responsibility and assignment of population the earth. With this in mind, all species of this new earth were gathered and presented before Adam. It was then that he was presented with free reign to name and position them all. With this motion in place, another element which corrupts today's modern society was given form... Ungratefulness. -- Day One 11:00 p.m. I have a new prey. I know this guy...his face is all too familiar, but his overall presence in a place such as this seems rather strange. Vangelus Olsig, I believe his name was. I'm not sure however...his appearance varies greatly in terms of contrast compared to the last time I had the pleasure of witnessing him at a local IWCCP house show here in Arson. A low cut darkish style replaces his once long and hazel fluent hair. His once girlish, soft face now covered with cuts and bruises, accompanied by influential earrings and a dry makeup that has nearly peeled itself off. Somethings not right though. A guy of his stature, homeless and broken in an abandoned alleyway? 11:04 p.m. The scrub present on his face suggest depression, and his stench unfortunately reaches all the way up here. With an aura of somberness present in his demeanor, he props a cardboard box against a lone garbage can. Oh wait, now he's digging in the can. He's looking for something, food would be my guess. What's this? Looks like some sort of torn blanket. I can't make it out. Like an Iraqi refugee, he's wrapping it over his head. Is he trying to suffocate himself? The smoggy weather makes up a relatively warm atmosphere, so he can't be cold. Wait, now I see it...He's using it as a makeshift pillow. Obviously he walked here and is exhausted. 11:07 p.m. Three minutes, give or take, have passed and he's now sleeping. The kill looks tempting, but it's too early. He's too fresh. I shall wait until he's broken, hungry, and weak. Then...then I shall go for the kill. This should be fun. Dravente the Hunter, Signing off. -- The soaring, winged species that he saw flying through the clouds he named Birds. The tamed, furry critters that roamed though the garden he named Lions. So many species, but yet Adam could not find a partner to satisfy him. The animals that roamed were greatly created, but didn't seem to align with the image Adam saw in his inevitable partner. In coming to this conclusion, Adam was given rest in the form of sleep, and in the midst, a rib was withdrawn from his body. From an element of his very own body, Adam's partner was created and given the conjugated name of Woman. The creation of this woman saw another corrupting element placed in today's modern society... Persuasion. Naked, Adam united with this woman whom took on the label of Eve. The same form of nakedness that shames today's society was not found shameful between Adam and his partner Eve...for there society was yet still perfect. The animals were perfect, the environment was perfect...their world was simply perfect. It was that same perfection, however, that drew the attention of a envious figure known as Lucifer. This devil once stood as an elite angel among the God. His praises of the God were second to none and so was his faithfulness. Envy struck the Angel named Michael, however, sending him into rebellion. No longer did he want to serve, but instead he wanted to be served. This arch-angel, of sorts, gave birth to the most cruel element of all in today's society... Greed. -- Day Two 12:34 noon I've sat here for two hours waiting for him to awaken. Finally, he has. Pretty deep sleeper, but it's evident what he's trying to do. Obviously hunger has taken it's toll and now he's trying to sleep through it. It's now evident that my theory is correct as he's now trying to return back to his slumber. He can't do it, however. He's starving, and it's preventing him from even being able to stay still. I hear something. Sounds like rummaging. He hears it too. He lifts his head and looks inside of the trashcan. Something catches his eye. It's obviously moving because his eyes are following it. He's reaching in...it's a cat. A brown cat with white streaks. The cat is desperately attempting to fight from his grasp, but he's not letting go. He's going for the kill...it's obvious. He's reaching for the blanket around his face...he yanks it off. Now he's wrapping it around the cat. He ties it tightly...he's trying to suffocate the cat. Now he's placing the cat back inside of the trashcan with the blankly tightly wrapped around him. He turns the trashcan over, trapping it's opening and thus providing the cat with no escape. Smart move. He lays back down. 1:13 p.m. He's found a broken shard of metal. Ferociously, he's scraping it across the brick wall of the alley. 1:20 p.m. It finally catches fire. He yanks the shirt off of his back, leaving nothing to clothe him but a torn wife beater. He quickly lights his shirt, watching as the fire amplifies. The muffled purs of the cat softened about ten minutes ago, and now they completely cease to exist. He's now walking over to the trashcan. Slowly and gently he lifts it up and takes a peek. The cat is motionless. He slowly drags it from underneath the trashcan. He unwraps the towel and finds the cat dead. My heart sinks a bit. He throws it into the fire. 2:01 p.m. Footsteps from outside the alley. That's rare...no one hardly ever comes by this place. Quickly, he takes cover behind the trashcan. That ensures me that he's still alert, thus the kill must be postponed even further. Behind the trashcan, he suddenly dozes off into sleep. His fatigue is decreasing rapidly. 5:29 p.m. Finally, he wakes up. He quickly hops to his feet now and runs over to the fire which has greatly died down, leaving nothing but a crisped version of a former cat. He attempts to eat, but it's nothing but burnt ashes. He throws his back against the wall and buries his face in the palm of his hands. I can't tell whether or not he is crying, but he's in a great deal of pain. If I had to take a guess, he's trying to construct a plan for food. He falls into a deep sleep once more. 9:31 I've just awoken from a dirt nap and now I find him stirring through a trashcan, possibly for food. He's way more relentless and built for this type of environment than any of my past prey. I managed to kill David Hosteff, my last victim, in a matter of hours. This guy is relentless however. His search for food only provides him with a few crumbs of food I can't really identify and a waste of liquid which is equally unidentifiable. He stumbles back to the wall. He is tremendously weak. I sort of feel bad for him. He sleeps once more...he wont be waking up for the rest of the night. Dravente the Hunter, Signing off -- With a plan of vengeance in place, this Devil took on the form of a snake, the most clever species of all the animals in the garden. He set his sight on the most vulnerable half of the duo known as Adam and Eve...the woman. The woman was nothing more than the rib of the man and therefore stood weaker not only in physical terms, but in mental and spiritual terms as well. This devil sought to expose this weakness by approaching the woman and disclaiming the command given to her and her husband concerning the forbidden tree of knowledge and evil. "Look at the fruits on that tree in the middle of the garden" the devil insisted "Why do you eat amongst this peasant fruit when you could eat of that which is beautiful and luscious?" "We have been commanded to not even touch that tree." Eve responded,but her response only served to add more fuel to the snake's burning fire. Taking on the corrupted element of persuasion, the snake began to enlist the advantages of eating from that tree, weaving his words together to suggest a switching of roles amongst himself and the God. With confidence heavy in his rattling voice, he spoke against the God, stating condemning him as not wanting to be matched in terms of sovereignty. The snake explained the origin of the tree of knowledge of evil to the couple, and being of naive nature, they accepted it. He told them that if they were to eat of the tree, they would gain the knowledge possessed only by the God and thus make them greater than him...something that he did not want. The woman believed the snake and thus gave birth to the factor in which all of the above elements are known as.... Evil. -- Day Three 2:11 a.m. His moaning and groaning awakens me from yet another cat nap, no pun intended. He's obviously still sleep, but he's so weak and in so much pain that he cannot even...what's the word...tame himself? Whatever the right word is. I've got a reassurance, though. Two O'clock in the morning, he'll be highly unexpected, and way to exhausted and weak to fight back. The time is right... I'm going in for the kill. Dravente the Hunter, Signing Off -- Satisfied with the sweet, forbidden flavor of the fruit, she shared with her husband, sending them both spiraling into immediate damnation. It was at that very moment that their perfect world took a turn of darkness. Suddenly their nakedness offered them great shame, causing them to hurry in a manner to cover themselves with the leaves and twigs provided by the garden. They had yet to realize it, but sin and evil was among them. When the God approached the garden, he was greatly angered. Both Adam and Eve attempted to hide, but found their efforts falling short. Pleading to the God, the woman insisted that it was the snake who tricked her into eating of the fruit. In knowing this to be true, a curse was placed on the snake. No longer would he walk on legs, but instead he would crawl on his belly and become a common enemy to women. His head would be crushed, and his spirit would be envious. But the woman was not off the hook. The God looked to Adam and questioned his disobedience. Adam placed the blame on the woman, stating that because she was a product of the God's creation, he believed her when she told him of the fruit. A curse was then placed on the woman. Her birth would be painful, she would be caused much trouble, and she would be ruled over by man against her will. Then he looked to Adam... He condemned Adam for believing the woman over him and thus cursed Adam with the burden of caring for a family and working hard to provide for his family. He forced Adam's work to be painful and tiresome. And in unison he cursed them both and all of mankind with the greatest curse of all... Death. -- Slowly, the hunter descended from the top of the building and crept unto the alleyway. With knife safely equipped in his back pocket, he approached the groaning Olsig from behind, reaching down to grasp him by the neck. Olsig reacted. SMACK! A kick flew connected with the lip of the hunter, sending him spiraling backwards into the brick wall as Olsig kipped to his feet, immediately bull-rushing the hunter by snatching a hand full of his shirt and forcing him up against the wall. Breathing heavily, he stared into the eyes of the hunter with enraged eyes of his own. "Oh m-...I'm so sorry. I didn't mean to startle y-.." "...W-who are you?" Olsig stated. "I-I heard some noises and I ju-..." "Leave." "I'm sorry, but you need help." "I don't need anything, just le-..." Suddenly, Olsig's grasp loosened as he crumbled to the ground. The weak nature that had taken over was being shown clearly as he could barely stand. "Oh my God, are you okay?" the Hunter asked, placing his palm on the shoulder of Olsig. He attempts to help Olsig up, but Olsig swiftly snatches away. "Why are you doing this to yourself? A young star such as yourself with such a successful career should not be torturing himself in such a manner." "...Y-you know who I am?" Vangelus asks in a widely surprised tone. "Vangelus Olsig. Longest reigning Intense Champion. Original Alias League alumni. First man to defeat Doctor Curiosity in PTC competition. I'd say I have a pretty good idea." Olsig shook his head as he attempted to push himself back to a vertical position. "Don't flatter yourself nor me with a list of meaningless achievements from a meaningless aspect of my life. That stuff means nothing to me anymore." "What are you talking about? You have it all. I'd kill...and I do mean kill...for your life." "No...no I don't. I don't have peace. Every time I turn around, someone wants to kill me. Someone's always out after me. I can't sleep, sometimes can't eat. I have to carry a pair of eyes in the back of my head. This world has corrupted my life. I no longer won't to deal with people nor the evil inside of them. I've isolated myself from the world." The hunter glanced away for a while, lingering over the words that had just been expressed before him. "Tha...That's deep." "It's true. I'd rather just lie here and die. Surely hell can't be any worse than this." The hunter placed his hand on the shoulder of Vangelus once more, this time finding it able to stay there without reaction. "I feel your pain, Vangelus. I feel the same way at times, but this is not the way to do it. You're only allowing the world to defeat you. You're playing right into their trap. This is what they won't...a broken man...and that's exactly what you're giving them." Olsig simply brushed off the comment and began to slowly walk off. "You cannot defeat evil if you never challenge it...Arthur Essex." He stopped. Those powerful words ran circles through his mind. So many memories erupted from that very statement. They told him he couldn't defeat Curiosity, but when he finally challenged him, he did. They told him he could never be a champion, but when he finally challenged for it, he not only became champion, but became the longest reigning champion in history. They told him that he could never compete on a main event level, but yet when placed in the challenge, he gained a victory over the current Universal Champion, Nova. So many times he'd been told he couldn't, but the result of the challenge proved otherwise. "I can't believe I'm saying this, but you're right. Why should I suffer because of the ill will of others?" The hunter smiled. "Indeed. Nothing is unbeatable...even the evil of man." For the first time, Olsig looked into the eyes of the hunter with an absence of rage. "You've really inspired me. I don't say thanks too often...but thanks." Olsig extended his hand. The hunter quickly shook it and pulled in Olsig for a hug. The two embraced. The hunter withdrew his knife. Olsig patted him on the back. The hunter yielded the knife behind Olsig's back... ARGHH! The hunter crumbled to the ground, dropping the knife while holding his groin. A perfectly placed knee by Olsig found it's spot. Olsig looked down at the ailing hunter and proceeded to yank up the knife. He glanced at it and simply shook his head. "Truth that's told with bad intent beats all the lies you can invent...William Blake." Suddenly, a snake waddled across the grounding, passing the hunter along the way. With mild enthusiasm, Olsig stabbed the knife into the body of the snake, watching as it fought death. He smirked and stepped over the snake, set to re-enter the world in which he had isolated himself from. Braced to challenge his Evil. -- In the beginning, Adam gave birth to evil. Since then, that very evil has corrupted the lives of many, mine included. The title is meaningless to me...I'm fighting for something far greater. Triumph. 118...I conquer Adam. 118...I conquer my evil. TBC.
