U.S.S.S.S. & T.I.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Previously Pre Recorded "So, you think they'll notice?" Flyer quipped, sitting in a comfortable leather couch in the middle of nowhere, America. He had a solemn look on his face, and was flipping through television channels indiscriminately. "I know the Eagles lost Fly," Mary-Lynn said, standing over top of him. She held a clipboard in her hands and looked like she always did, as if she were ready to go to court. "But you can't reuse an old promo for this match." Flyer threw his arms in the air. "Why not!? It's not like they'll even notice!" "Of course they'll notice!" Mary-Lynn screamed, pulling down a flow chart out of thin air. "See, this is you in 1998," She points to a man who looks more like a Ninja rather than a rainbow'd Lunatic. "This is you in 1999." She points to an image of Flyer wearing all black and looking like a cult leader. "This is you in 2000...." Mary-Lynn said, specifically pointing out his short crew cut like hair style and camouflage pants which said 'Snowman.' "Do I need to go on?" "Listen," Flyer said, leaning in. "My hair's grown out. My look's the same it was in '03. Why can't we just recycle some promo where I'm all God-like and be done with it!?" "Jack!" Mary-Lynn sighed. "I wouldn't be there, hell, I wouldn't have even been mentioned in 2003." "Oh," Flyer groaned, annoyed. "I guess you were LEARNING, or whatever it is you do in school back in the past." "Yes Jack. I was learning. Which is precisely the reason why I now know it's impossible for you to reuse a promo from five years ago and make it look like it's from today. It's just impossible!" Mary-Lynn crossed her arms over her chest. "But, you don't know my after effects skills. See, I've been doing this all day!" Flyer said, spinning a side table over to Mary-Lynn's side. It has a promo from his days in the fWo, except every fWo logo has been blurred out, and Mary-Lynn has been digitally implanted into the background of the wrestling ring. She looks herky jerky in some sense, and the shadows don't really match up from the original Flyer in the frame and the super imposed Mary-Lynn Mayweather. "See?! Fail proof!" As the previously used and reimagined promo runs, Flyer continued to speak, pacing back and forth. Suddenly, a second Flyer walked into frame, and began to do the same, but this time with much different dialogue. And a third. And a fourth. "TONY!" Flyer shouted. Peering around the corner wearing a hat that gave him elephant like ears, Tony Davis smiled. He held a tray of crisps in his hands. "Yes'um?!" "Why'd you activate all these timelines? Are you trying to screw with my ingenious plan as well?" Flyer huffed, shaking his head in disappointment. "Well, no," Davis said, cautiously. "I just wanted to see if someone could watch six promos on top of each other. Turns out they can't. Or at least I can't. I got bored and started to play Micro Machines." Flyer quickly does a few computer key strokes. "See, no harm no foul. Alright Tony. You can go back to being ignored now!" Tony smiled. "Sweet." And quickly ducked off screen. "Alright! RUN THE CLIP!" Flyer, in a guilty motion, jerks his head from side to side. "I MEAN RECENTLY PRE TAPED INTERVIEW! ROLL IT!" [Fade to black. Quickly, a screen of static is shown, and then on comes a dimly lit gymnasium screen shot, where it seems fifteen people are currently standing in the audience.] ("Kick Out the Jams" by the President of the USA plays over the "sold out" ICW arena as High Flyer comes out the dressing room. He's slaps the hands of the fans as he walks down the aisle.He jumps up and over the top rope. He's handed the mic.) [Editors Note: At this point, High Flyer has a long ponytail and is wearing a Gi. That is about all you need to know about him.] High Flyer:Hello ICW. Insane Championship Wrestling. Now how did I get mixed up with people like this. For those of you who don't know me, I'm the best High Flyer the Professinal world of Wrestling has EVER seen. That just might be why I'm called High Flyer. I'm six feet, 217 pounds of risky, high flying action. A lot of people in Insane Championship Wrestling know me from EWA. Edward Glide, Josh Cagle, Oz, and Wrecker are all in EWA, and all will know me very well by the time my career is over, which may have just been shortened by five years. I am here to introduce myself to the entire ICW audience, and to all the wrestlers in the back. You will eventually see my face on a poster as the ICW champion. I know I have to climb the ranks, but eventually, I will become the champ. I'd like to make a challenge, to anyone. I'd love to take any of the top wrestlers in ICW to the limit, and back. I'm only 22 years old, but that gives me the age over almost anyone in professinal wrestling. I'm not afraid to go insane, well, you got to be insane to join ICW. (High Flyer gets out of the ring. He goes over to the time keeper and asks for his chair. He folds it up and tosses it under the lower rope. He goes in through the lower rope as well. He picks up the mic again.) High Flyer:This chair is going to be through anyone's head who steps in my way. If you want this chair to go right through your head, come out now. (Ryan Crystal comes down the ring to no music. He heads straight to the ring and goes through the middle ropes.) Commentator:The bell rings as High Flyer locks in a grapple with Ryan Crystal. Crystal wips him off the ropes, but High flyer jumps onto the second rope, MoonSault right on Crystal. Both men get up fast. High Flyer goes off the ropes, Flying Leg Lariot. Flyer's going for the legs. He crosses the legs, takes the arm, Modified Bow and Arrow, or Surfboard. The referee is asking Crystal if he wants to submit. He wont and somehow kicks out of it. Crystal wips Flyer off the ropes, he put his head down too early, Powerbomb. He goes outside and gets the chair. High Flyer goes back in through the middle ropes and set the open chair by the ropes. Crystal is slowly getting up. High Flyer off the ropes, he jumps off the chair, off the top rope, another moonsault, square on Crystal. Both men are down. The referees are counting them down. 1------2-------3------4------5. Flyer is on his knees and gets up after another 2 count. High Flyer goes off the ropes as Crystal gets up. Flying leg lariot once again. High Flyer goes up top, FROGSPLASH off the top. He pulls up Crystal, goes up top again, Monnsault once again. I believe that's the Flying Moon Shot. The cover, 1-------2-------3. Flyer wins it! (High Flyer picks up his mic again.) High Flyer:You see that, that was my Flying Moon Shot, the finisher that will bring the entire ICW world to my feet. I'm may be underweight, but that makes me able to throw my body around like no one else in ICW. By the end of the year, I will have left my mark on Insane Championship Wrestling, as well as EWA. Just watch and see. (Kick Out the Jams replays over the sold out ICW arena. High Flyer goes up and over the top rope. He walks down the aisle, slapping the hands of the fans. [Quickly, the camera cuts to static, and fades back into the living room house where High Flyer and Mary-Lynn Mayweather stand in their place.] Flyer looked nervously from left to right. "Uh... that wasn't noticeable, right?" Mary-Lynn felt she doesn't even need to bother to respond. "I mean, EWA and ICW, they could be failing bank corporations. No one knows what I'm talking about! Hmmm... and I still can't believe I'm one of those guys who has a squash in the midst of an interview just to make it longer. Man. I really sucked back then." Flyer hopped up onto his feet slightly disturbed. He began to pace. "But my hair. Why didn't we rotoscope out my hair and my clothes! God! I'm so stupid! I should have bearded him!" Flyer lowered his head in defeat, and began to rub his chin stubble. "Or I coulda shaved. That woulda been cheaper slash easier." "What about your broken nose?" Mary-Lynn said, as Flyer quickly hid his nose from the camera. "It was broken against Tyler Rayne and, right when it was about to heal, Slugger broke it open again." Mary-Lynn smiled. "You've never had a broken nose before, AND been green at the same time." "THEN GODS DAMNIT, I'll dye my hair and change into one of my many other outfits!" Quickly, Flyer walked over to a closet, and opened it up to reveal every outfit he's worn in wrestling dating back to his start in Mexico. "I'm a frakin' chameleon! Shit son, if I wanted, I'd wander over to the Time Machine and just grab the retro version of myself and stick him in present me clothing and get him to do things. But wait, I'd have remembered having had that done to me in the past if I were to do that. Hey Mary, why can't I remember having been told to do stuff by future me in the past? TONY!" Flyer shouted, as Tony quickly rushed to his side. "TO THE TIME MACHINE!" Mary-Lynn raised her hand to stop them. "Uh, remember Jack. You smashed it to bits because of that day you were against continuity errors?" "But," Flyer frowned. "I'm no longer against continuity, so, TO THE TIME MACHINE!" Flyer pointed, heroically. Flyer and Davis quickly rushed off screen, leaving Mary-Lynn Mayweather standing there confused. "That... can't possibly work... can it?" A few moments later, groans escaped from the basement. Shouts followed. "HEY MARY!" Came the voice of the Lunatic, sounding more and more like a child each whine. "Can you get us the Time Machine's repair number? It should be fridge magneted and labeled experimental United States Secret Service Space and Time Initiative?!"
