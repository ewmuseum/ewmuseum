The Help Part 1 (Condensed Version)

Date: Who cares This is a bit of a condensed version of what I was going to post for 122. This doesn’t count for 123 in any way. December 16th, 2006 "Tony Gamble" The name snapped off the tip of his tongue with a hint of venom behind it. This would be the man that he would have to go to the ends of the Earth to try and assassinate so that his own problems could be alleviated. Devin Shakur looks down at the documents that have been provided for him by the Braintrust. His most recent addresses were scattered about, never once coming back to a specific place. Shakur, thus, draws the conclusion that Gamble does not have a set place of residence and lives in various hotels. This would be of little use, unless Devin was able to know ahead of time where Gamble would be. Gamble's phone numbers on the other hand would provide great assistance to Devin, as whenever he wanted he could call up Gamble in the future. If Gamble is this low maintenance to begin with, Devin draws another conclusion that he would not go to the trouble of replacing his cellular phone. Now all Devin had to do was alert his own means of support and go from there… And that is when it hit Devin like a ton of bricks. The name Anthony Gambillini… In much younger days, Devin Shakur was once part of Christian’s Death motorcycle group, granted it was a short period of time. When there, Devin overheard the name Gambillini twice and this always infuriated the entire group. When Devin went to ask why, the boss at the time said that it was none of his concern. This was in 1996, right before Devin was sent off to prison for seven years. Could Christian still hold a grudge over ten years later? Devin remembers the partial paranoia in Christian’s voice when Christian inquired whether Devin had used somebody else’s cellular phone upon their first contact. He fears that this is not going to be a friendly conversation. Regardless, he picks up the telephone and starts to press the digits. Elsewhere… A giant man stands on the walkway between a beachfront house and the beach itself, staring out at the waves crashing on the shore. He is rocking a red bandana, jean jacket vest, and has tattoos covering both of his arms. He stands at about seven feet tall and looks to weigh in at about 330 pounds. Thirty eight years of hell have aged itself upon his face. His name is Christian Daniels, Devin Shakur’s last resort to try and achieve nirvana. "Do you ever get tired of it?" A voice to his left says, also staring out into the same ocean that Christian is. This man is significantly shorter, by almost a foot, and weighs about one hundred pounds less. His hair goes down to the middle of the back, and he’s wearing the quasi-punk look of a short sleeved black shirt outside of a long sleeved shirt of the same color. For almost thirty years he has worn the look of a quiet rebel. His name is Chris Smith, and once upon a time in a previous life he was an independent wrestling legend known as Icon. Now they are both wanted by more law enforcement agencies than one could count. "Get tired of what?" Christian says without taking his eyes off of the waves. "The chase, always having to look over your shoulder, the thoughts of death." "Ah man, you know we’re all gonna die one day, might as well piss off as many people as we can before going down you know?" Chris gives a half frown "I just wish that we could get Rachel down here, at least that way we should know she is safe." "You know she wouldn’t stay down here for long. Look at her occupation, assassin, she doesn’t stay in one place longer than she has to. She doesn’t do it for the money with us around. She does it because she’s that way in the head. Hell, we all got a bit of that in us. We made out pretty good with it wouldn’t you say?" "I know she can handle herself, but if she goes down then we’ll never know. You get where I’m coming from?" "We all have the same DNA Con, you know deep down she’s not going to jeopardize anything. She’s the smart one." A faint ring is heard behind the two of them, which gets both of their heads to whip around toward the house. Inside, a dead ringer for Michael Vartan holds his hand up to indicate that he’s going to answer the ringing. Both of the brothers still keep their eyes on the house. Chris wonders "Who in the hell could be calling us this fucking early?" Christian replies "Well it’s either Rachel, which is too coincidental, or Devin Shakur." "Speaking of that bloke" Chris runs his hands through the back of his hair before locking it in a ponytail "How’d your research on finding his oppressors turn out?" "I got it down to one of two groups. One has mafia ties and the other doesn’t. If he ever coughs up the name then it shouldn’t be hard to deduce-" Both brothers turn their heads to the side in bewilderment as the Michael Vartan knockoff throws the telephone across the room and slams it into the refrigerator. They start walking back toward the house, preparing their best consolation statements that they can contemplate. Christian inquires "What in the hell would make Jason Fucking Hardy, mister CIA, throw a phone across the room?" Chris replies "Must be a big name then." Chris opens up the door to see Jason Hardy looking at him like he just received word that his girlfriend is cheating on him. Christian steps in behind Chris and shuts the door. "That Devin?" Christian says "OHHHHH YEAH" Jason replies, looking like he wants to kill someone in the face. "Did he give out a name by chance or was that your hormones kicking in?" "OHHHHHHHHHHHHH YEAH" Christian walks over toward the phone to survey the damage. It will definitely need replacing, that’s for sure. "And who was the individual in question?" Jason seems too eager to answer the question "Anthony Gambillini!" Just like that, at the drop of a hat, their paradise has been invaded. The smirks collectively come off the faces of Christian Daniels and Chris Smith. They are now replaced with the rage that Jason Hardy has on his face. Christian slams his seven foot frame up against the wall and stares down at the floor. "Man, we fucking should have taken all of those shits back out in 98. It was right there for the fucking taking, and we blew our opportunity." Chris is still a bit perplexed "Waitaminute, waitaminute, waitaminute…So these other people, who I assume by now you know CD, want Devin to whack Anthony Gambillini?" Christian shoots back "Fuck that shit, I can deal with those douchebags at the snap of a finger. I’m more worried about Devin, boy has no idea what he’s stepping into." Jason drops the phone down to the ground, ignoring the clang it makes. "So they know that Devin knows us, and they also know then that Devin was in your group, and they also know that this-" Christian holds up his hands to stop Jason’s moment of revelation. "Jesus Fucking Christ man this shit ain’t Cold Case, yes this "Braintrust", or whatever the fuck they call themselves, knows that he knows us, and that’s why they fucking picked him, personal motivation on our behalf or some shit." Jason wonders "So then why didn’t they just get us to do their bidding?" Chris has a look of stupidity upon his face after hearing that question. "Man you really need to get in the fucking game. Do you know how stupid that would be to come after one of us directly?" "Well then who the fuck is it? Who is getting Devin to try and assassinate someone?" Jason blurts out, slamming his fists down on the table "Anderson and Wilkerson!" Christian shouts back Jason has an incredulous look upon his face "What do they have to do with anything?" "Aite, lemme break this down for your stupid ass so we can ALLLL be clear about it." Christian says before holding his right hand up "One, they are a competing business that Matt ran against back when we were in the States. Two, they don’t like to deal with problems. Three, they have mafia connections, and apparently one of them ran through DeAngelis. Four, they really didn’t like it when Matt almost put them out of business last year. Five, whilst Matt and Devin don’t have the history that you, me, or Con does, they got history. Get the picture yet, Newton?" Suddenly a door slams open and another combination of a seven foot man and a six foot man enter the room. The taller man is carrying a big bucket in his right hand and the shorter one just looks like a sleazy corporate executive. The taller man has a big smile on his face "I GOT’S THE FRIED CHICKEN!" Meet Michael Blaze, the happiest member of the group and the one time stock guru turned wrestling trainer of Chris Smith. The shorter man holds up a big box that contains happiness for all "And I’ve got the liquor." Meet Matthew Smith, the apparent side target of the Braintrust. Both men’s eyebrows burrow down when the three men in the room completely no sell their happy time antics. Matthew is the first to ask "What’s wrong?" He then looks over at the refrigerator "Why is there a dent in the refrigerator?" His eyes then looks over at where the phone usually is "And why is the phone ripped out?" Christian half frowns "Well we know who is holding Devin at literal gun point, and we also know who they want him to whack…Wanna add some root to your beer and guess who is behind it all?" A look of pain comes over Matt’s face as he hands the liquor off to Michael Blaze. "Those sons of bitches. I thought they said they wouldn’t go after us again." "Well they know we’re on the run from practically every guy with a badge, patriotism, and a gun, so maybe they thought it’d be nice to give it a try one more time." Chris chimes in "That and they probably don’t think Devin would put himself at risk by trying to contact us. Because if they knew that we knew it was them, and that we were planning on doing something about it…They probably not gonna like it too much." Michael Blaze puts the bucket of chicken down on the table "So what are we gonna do?" Christian leans up off the wall "Well the first thing we’re going to do is get Brandon’s ass down here so he can get our computers up and running, preventing any of those knobs back in the States from tracking us down, then we’re going to get our shit updated, call Devin back, give him instructions, and then we’re going to have fun like the old days. BRANDON! GET YOUR BILL GATES WANNABE ASS DOWN HERE!" And with that, we depart. Tune in for Part 2 of this tale to be posted sometime before the deadline for 123. It will feature actual back story that makes this condensed piece of shit make legitimate sense.
