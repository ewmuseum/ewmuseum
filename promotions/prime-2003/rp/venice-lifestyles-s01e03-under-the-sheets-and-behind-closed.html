Date: February 10-12, 2009 Tuesday, February 10, 2009 Cold drinks. Warm weather. Cool beaches. Hot cars. Smokin' hot women. Yep, that was Nicholas Donnelly's personal check list when he arrived in Venice Beach and certainly he had experienced each one in the month he had been there. But as the Boston native was standing outside a pair of large wooden double doors, he felt something that he had barely felt since his plane landed. Boredom. A meeting of unknown importance was taking place inside and here was Donnelly, decked out in his sleek suit, sans tie of course standing watch outside. It was his job and he was definitely paid handsomely to perform his tasks. Surely he wasn't about to complain of the simple work and the great coin he was raking in. However, the one thing Nitz Donnelly didn't handle well was boredom. Ask his friends and his family. No really, ask them and they will all tell you the same thing. He gets antsy when he's bored. So there he stood, scratching his head and glancing at his phone from time to time to try and keep sane. The meeting had extended past the three hour mark now and Big Van was called away for another job leaving The Ego all by his lonesome. He paced a little, slapped his hands on his hips and took a seat in the chair opposite the doors. His mind couldn't help but wonder what was being discussed inside, how much money was changing hands and how many people would walk out exponentially richer just from attending a meeting with drinks and expensive catered food. He licked his chops at the simple thought of the food as he made the mistake of not grabbing a bite to eat before the job started. He tried to text message Leticia only an hour before and unfortunately there was no response. The finals of a tournament was taking place at the gym and of course the owner needed to be in attendance. Donnelly hoped to step in before it ended but now he was cutting it rather close. Just as he was starting to lose the last of his patience, his phone began to vibrate. His childhood friend finally replied to his original message but he'd might as well not said anything as her response had nothing to do with the discussion Donnelly was trying to start. “Jaime won the tournament. A cool 10 G's in his pocket.” Nitz smiled. Jaime showed up in Venice only a couple months before Donnelly. He had slacked on his training and was rather out of shape for an aspiring fighter. Vance trained with him a few times and with a couple months of non-stop action and a couple tournaments down, his efforts seemed to finally pay off. He really needed the money too as he drained his bank account for the entry fee and had nothing left to pay his rent. A top three finish would have earned him more than enough money for the month but his first place finish had set him up for at least a couple months. Nitz got lucky, falling into a well paying line of work while Jaime worked hard to earn his keep from beginning to end. It was almost unfair. Almost was acceptable for Nicholas however. The minute the meeting extended past three hours, his night profited him a cool four hundred fifty dollars plus any tips the high rollers sucking back expensive booze and custom made sandwiches left him. Three nights a week like this and in just a couple short weeks, he was already pulling in more money than plenty of people were pulling down in a month. He tested the job market outside of Mr. Mendoza's business and he didn't get a single bite. Turns out the pay was better with what he ended up with anyway. It left him some time to repeatedly check and recheck the items on his aforementioned list minus the hot car. That was a little bit in the future for him yet. Donnelly was proud of Jaime though, and expressed his happiness for the young fighter in his response to Leticia. “Dinner's on him when I get outta here.” “Actually, it's on Vance. He just got here and it's his turn. He'll be picking you up when things are done there.” Nitz sighed, immediately wishing that the large doors would burst open at anytime and he could end his night walking to the parking circle out front. He almost willed the doors open, for the light from the full conference room to shine out into the hall and the shadows of the suits approaching to leave. He could picture it, he could see it in his mind but it just wasn't happening. |~o~| Leticia had a huge smile on her face. Not only did she manage to secure the proper licenses well ahead of time for the tournament, but she also made a nice handful of cash to put back into the club. New weight sets were required and as she thumbed through the bills in her hands, she knew all it would take now was ordering the equipment and many of the clubs members would be happy. Indeed, they were craving some new equipment to use and stuck by Leticia and the club while some money was gained. Now they will get everything they want and possibly more. The Latin Princess couldn't be any happier. “Good night?” “Great actually.” Leticia turned around and greeted Vance with a hug. He put his keys down on the desk and sat opposite her. Leticia knew that when Vance sat down, he wanted to talk. “I'm concerned.” “It's Nicky isn't it?” He nodded his head. He had taken off his suit coat and was rolling up the sleeves of his white dress shirt. “I've been working with him for two weeks and I've managed to keep him away from the... let's call them the “improper” aspects of my job. It's not gonna work that way much longer. He's getting curious.” “Van...” Leticia sat behind her desk as she quietly said his name. “I'll be honest with you in saying that it's inevitable. Working closely with you he's gonna see things and he's gonna wonder. I knew the day he started working with you that he would eventually see some of the things you do and it's up to him whether he sticks around with this work. That is if you're comfortable with all this.” “I'm not doing anything considered illegal.” “You haven't been caught doing anything illegal.” Leticia responds. “We'll just have to wait and see.” “Yup... Dinner?” “You bet. Lemme text Nicky to see where he's at. Don't worry about him though, things should end up fine. I like to think I know him.” “Probably yeah.” Yet Vance still wasn't sure. |~o~| Friday, February 12, 2009 It was dark out and in the background the waves of the salt water could be heard battering the beach. There was a single light on outside of the beach house Nitz was staying in. The breeze was cool and would bring a slight spell of goosebumps to anyone not wearing long sleeves. But things were just a bit warmer inside. OK, a lot warmer in fact. Date night came and went and while it was only a couple minutes before Midnight, Donnelly was just getting in a groove so to speak. He started off with dinner, a bit of dancing and then some talking. Oh, and the alcohol intake was quite impressive as well. Kimberly, the beautiful redhead he met on the beach a couple weeks earlier finally agreed to accompany him home. The heat factor increased as soon as he walked in the door as he realized that Kimberly wasn't wearing any undergarments beneath her very short dress that already left little to the imagination. “Is your neighbor home?” Kimberly enquired as she removed her top leaving only the bed sheet to cover her. She had spotted Vance's trailer in the open lot next door. “Nope, he had business to tend to.” “Good, cause I can get rather loud.” She smiled brightly and all Nitz could do was laugh as she kissed his neck. She slid down and helped him remove his shirt and then pressed on his chest to make him lay back. She kissed his chest and slowly she slipped under the covers. Nitz couldn't believe he actually had Kimberly in the position she was in. She played a little hard to get after the day on the beach but tonight she had broken down all of her defences and allowed Nitz to get to know her personally and intimately. He just wondered if she was as good in bed as she was as a tattoo artist, her chosen profession. She slid lower until there was only movement around the midsection of Nitz and the smile on his face grew wider. An audible bang went unnoticed by the fornicating couple as they were too involved. Perhaps the loud bang of the bedroom doorknob crashing through the plaster wall would disturb them? “Nick, we gotta...” Vance paused. Nitz began to flip out and covered himself up to his chest and Kimberly jetted out from under the covers. She however was too drunk to really care all that much and as soon as she got an eyeful of the designs on the bare arms of The Tortured Artist, she smiled. “Great ink! He joining?” She turned to Nitz who looked like he was about to lose his dinner on the floor. “We gotta go, just got an important call and I need help. You're the only one I could reach?” “REACH?! My phone is right there and I know I didn't get a call.” “I had to pick something up from home.” Nitz sighed and pulled himself up, dragging the bed sheet with him leaving only the blanket for Kimberly to cover up with. Her inebriated state didn't allow her the ability to care about covering up, giving Vance an excellent look at her assets. “I swear to Christ the next time you don't knock before coming in here...” Nitz started as he was attempting to pick up his clothes from the floor without dropping the sheet. The last thing he needed to do was visibly wang his friend. “Relax Nicky, I'll have you back in no time.” “Thanks cutie!” Kimberly said as she rolled out of the bed and trotted to the bathroom in the other direction. Being stark ass naked obviously didn't bother her all that much. “I take it your friend is stickin' around?” “Fuck it, let's go now so we can get back please.” Nitz bolted from the room still with the sheet around him and an arm full of clothes. Vance looked down and observed the lack of underwear amongst the dress Kimberly wore. |~o~| “What's this call about anyways?” Donnelly asked as she slipped on his coat in the front seat of the car. Vance sped around the next corner, obviously heading down the beach toward the hotel Leticia's father owned. “Couple guys got a little drunk and out of hand at the tower. No one's around and we're the only ones Letty's Father trusts enough.” “Isn't this more for the cops to deal with?” Raymes just shook his head as he turned the final corner into the nearly empty employee parking lot he came to a stop at the back door. “Nah, this might get a tad physical though so watch yourself. You go that way and I'll take the back door.” He pointed as he quickly exited the car. “Oh, yes boss. I live to serve especially after you cockblocked me.” Nitz closed the door and jogged off to the main employee entrance. As soon as he swiped his card and disappeared through the door he gave Vance the finger to finish his previous statement. Vance paid it no mind though as he walked back to the trunk and opened it quickly. As he closed it, he could be seen carrying a tire iron in his massive hand. What Donnelly didn't know though, was that Vance just sent him on a wild goose chase to basically keep watch as the big man had to take care of business. A few minutes lapsed and Nitz was already outside the office of Mr. Mendoza. Normally his rounds included the basement if he had to show up for some random reason however the janitors had the basements closed off as the floors had been soaked from a good cleaning. There didn't seem to be any action happening in the office at all and he was beginning to get curious as to why he was even there. The doors outside had been locked and all the lights were turned off. He checked the door to the main office and it was also locked. He didn't have access to that set of rooms so he moved on, around the corner towards a stairwell that connected the two floors of offices. As he ascended the stairs, he heard voices coming from above. He distinctly heard the voice of Vance as he took the last few stairs. A light illuminated the hall coming from a room at the end where he assumed he would find Raymes and any other people who happened to be in the building uninvited. He checked the other doors on his way down to find they were all locked. Vance's voice grew louder but he just couldn't make out what was being said. A loud bang on a table in the room caused Nitz to avoid the last few doors and jog down the hall. He could now make out Leticia's Father's voice and another male voice along with Vance. It took him no time at all to make it to the door and fling it open, stunning the other men in the room. He didn't talk nor did he move as soon as he laid eyes on what was happening in front of him. Mr. Mendoza was sitting at the conference room table facing a man. Vance had his back to the door before Nitz burst through it and the tire iron he carried into the building was above his head. The other man hopped out of his seat and glanced back at the door, wondering just who the intruder is. “Fuck...” Vance lowered the tire iron to his side. Mr. Mendoza didn't look too surprised to see Nitz show up and calmly finished his conversation. “Now you must understand my commitment Mr. James. Eye for an eye as I see it. I need those units available as soon as possible.” The odd man in the room nodded and shook the hand of the hotel tycoon. “Just don't hit me with that thing and you got a deal.” “Good, I'll walk you to your car. Vance, your night is done.” Mr. Mendoza stood and slipped his overcoat on that was hanging on the nearby coat rack. “Sure you don't need me to walk you down?” “No need Vance. You and Nicholas here have a good night.” Mr. Mendoza led the obviously nervous Mr. James out of the room as Nitz stepped to the side. Vance slapped his hand on his leg in frustration at Nitz seeing what was going on. Donnelly turned to glance down the hallway at the businessmen leaving and then he looked back over at Vance again. The big man shrugged. “Dude, never again should we both catch each other in the same night with our tools in action.” Vance chuckled at the joke his friend decided to crack. He approached the still somewhat stunned Ego and patted him on the shoulder, leading him from the room and cutting the lights with the tire iron as they leave. |~o~| Three friends, three lawn chairs and three bottles of beer. That was the situation outside of Vance's trailer at 2 AM. They were all laughing and sharing their stories of earlier in the night. “Look look look, the one thing I gotta know is why a monster with claws the size of frying pans needs a tire iron to look intimidating!” Nitz was feeling pretty good as he already had a couple empty bottles lying beside the full one he just picked up and took a swig from after he stopped laughing. “Perhaps he's compensating for something.” “Oh thanks Letty, Nick and I have had enough dick jokes for the night believe me.” “Really?” “Long fuckin' story...” Nitz paused and took another swig of his brew as Vance finished off his. “Your date still inside?” “Heh, she passed out in the washroom praying to the porcelain gods... her night is fucked, as was my night when you burst in on us.” “Wait what?!” Leticia said with her mouth open. “Walked in on Nicky's girl rippin' on the trumpet.” Vance smirked as he put his beer on the table beside him. Nitz's face turned bright red at the vulgar mention of his activities he was caught participating in earlier in the night. Leticia just laughed. “Oh geez! Would have loved to see the absolute joyful skank you brought into my beach house.” Just at that moment, the nude woman appeared in the doorway of the house. Her red hair was all messed up and she still had a bit of vomit dried on her chin. He looked quite dishevelled. “Nicholas, aren't you coming back inside anytime tonight?” “You were sleeping somewhat comfortably last I looked.” He winked at Vance who just shook his head, wondering if she even realized she passed out by the john. “Well I'm taking a shower, why don't you join me?” She said seductively as he bounced away from the door in her birthday suit. All three friends looked on as she left and Nitz downed the last of his third beer in a hurry. “Isn't that the lotion chick from the beach that one time?” Leticia asked. “Yep, perhaps my night isn't over after all. Van Ray, you're forgiven.” The Latin Princess shifted her weight in her seat and looked at Vance and shook her hand from side to side. Vance gave her an odd look. Nitz excused himself and jogged into the beach house for a nightcap. “Don't tell Nicky, but I was wrong. If I swung that way, even I'd take that home. Yikes.” Leticia exhaled sharply as Vance practically fell out of his chair. “Holy shit, if he was to hear you say that he'd never let you live that down.” “No doubt. So he seemed alright with what happened.” “Yeah, we talked in the car on the way back and he figured something of the like was going on. The amount of money he was getting paid was his first tip off. He knows things can get a tad bit more messy at times too but it's part of the business. He gets it.” “A tad huh? Heh, so things will be OK then?” Vance lightly grabbed the hand of his good friend sitting in the chair next to him, smiling genuinely. “Things are lookin' good hun. Lookin' good indeed.”
