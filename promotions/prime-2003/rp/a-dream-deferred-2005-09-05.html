A Dream Deferred

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/5/05 Ferguson3 - A Dream Deferred A DREAM DEFERRED By Danny Ferguson FADE IN. INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - DAY The Ferguson mansion is as gaudy as you could ask for, with gold in inappropriate places and enough size to house almost 100 homeless persons. It only houses one person, however, and that's our star, Danny. NARRATOR Danny Ferguson had returned home from Hawaii and a successful turn at PRIME's Tropical Turmoil Pay-Per View. Now seeking his first winning streak since returning to the ring, he was preparing for the next week's match by watching his own movie. 'Hard Money' plays on a garishly large television as Danny sits in a lounge chair in front of it. He chomps away on handfuls from a bowl of popcorn that sits in his lap. A bodyguard sits on either side of him. Both Jim and Dametreyus are out of their wheelchairs, but sport knee braces and crutches. Neither man looks terribly enthused to be watching Danny's film again. DANNY (onscreen and in person, simultaneously) I think my luck's about to change. Danny (in person) cackles evilly as his celluloid counterpart enters yet another fight scene. Jim Pibb, staring off in the distance, absent-mindedly mouths the line along with his boss, then catches the eye of Dametreyus and looks at the floor. NARRATOR It would still be at least 24 hours before Team Ferguson would leave for the upcoming ReVolution, as Danny still had not looked forward to his match. He had left that up to his wrestling manager, Reggie Delray. The toilet flushes from off in the distance, followed by the sound of clumsy footsteps and frustrated mumbling. For several awkward moments, there is only the sound of the movie playing and Danny's reactions. NARRATOR Unfortunately, Reggie was still making the adjustment to the Hollywood lifestyle, including the steady diet of juices that took the place of his usual drink of choice, Cherry Coke. His bladder had still not recovered. Finally, Reggie appears from around a massive column, having made the marathon-like walk from living room to restroom. REGGIE Fuck, now I've gotta piss again. He turns on his heel and heads back in the opposite direction, footsteps echoing like they had done just moments before. As he disappears, Vicki Siguchi walks in the front door, chatting on her cell phone as usual. We'd make that into dialogue, but she's honestly not saying anything important, and you know that. While she walks in, she notices the movie playing and rolls her eyes, looking at Dametreyus and giving him a 'gag me' gesture with her finger. Almost immediately, Dam starts to stand and unbuckle his pants, but a silent intervention by Jim Pibb avoids what would surely be trouble. Back at the television, Danny glances over his shoulder at his chatty manager, then raises the TV remote in an exaggerated manner, turning up the volume. NARRATOR If it weren't for the anticipated collection of his Storm The Beach reward, Danny might not have even checked to see if he weas scheduled to be at the show to begin with. CUT TO: I/E. Danny sitting in the same chair, with the exact same lines from 'Hard Money' playing, except it's quite clearly a different day and time, as noted by the clock and calendar on the wall behind him. DANNY So listen, do I get this thing mailed to me? My reward? I never needed to win anything before, so I don't- On the other end of the line. BLAINE BLAIR Um...I think you're supposed to draw it at the show. DANNY Draw? I'm a fucking actor, I don't draw. BLAINE BLAIR I mean pick it. You pick it from a hat. DANNY You can't do that for me? BLAINE BLAIR Well, that kind of ruins the point of 'Choose Your Own Destiny' DANNY Wait, so now I'm supposed to be able to read and draw? I didn't go to college, dickface, I'm too talented for that. BLAINE BLAIR You're thinking of 'Choose Your Own Adventure.' DANNY They're not the same? BLAINE BLAIR Well, one of them avoids copyright infringement. DANNY I hear you, dawg. So I'm going to go hit the library to brush up on my 'choosing.' You just send me my thing in the mail. BLAINE BLAIR No, you need to do it at the show. DANNY We're filming this week? BLAINE BLAIR No, you're drawing. I mean...crap... BACK TO: INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - DAY Reggie finally returns from the bathroom, again. REGGIE I don't think I have an ounce of liquid left in my body. NARRATOR In fact, Reggie did hav...you know what, you don't need to know this. Reggie walks out into the living room a little further. NARRATOR As Danny's wrestling manager, Reggie was prepared to use his extensive expertise in tag-team based matches - CUT TO: I/E. Shots and clips of Reggie managing several high-profile tag teams, notably The Midnight Train To Georgia, in all seven of its incarnations. The appropriate song plays along with these clips, and ends with a picture of Reggie holding a pair of lavish tag team titles. BACK TO: INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - DAY It fades into Reggie in the same pose, arms outstretched, except now they are empty, save for an unidentified book of sorts in one hand. He looks more confused than anything else. NARRATOR However, as Danny's wrestling manager, Reggie also knew that the further he kept conversation away from wrestling, the less frustrated he became. After all, he still got a paycheck. Reggie tosses the book down at Danny, who looks at him like he just interrupted his mother's eulogy, then hits 'Pause' on the DVR and looks at his lap. It's a movie script, with the title 'Double Down' printed across the front. REGGIE Found that in the shitter. Something you want to share? Danny looks at the script, then back at Reggie, then back at the TV as he tosses it over his shoulder to the manager. DANNY It's a script, so what? REGGIE (looking over at the bodyguards) It's not just any script, though. DANNY Yeah, big fucking deal, it's a sequel. They happen all the time. CUT TO: I/E. A growing stack of movie sequel scripts, including, most notably, The Lord of the Rings IV and Ray 2. BACK TO: INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - DAY Reggie picks up the script and flips through some pages. REGGIE Well? Doesn't this have some sort of effect on your career, and therefore my career? Danny looks at him again. DANNY Keep your pants on, old man, I'm not filming that piece of shit. It's career suicide. Reggie looks up at the TV screen and the paused 'Hard Money.' The onscreen Danny is slamming a thug into a slot machine with gritted teeth. REGGIE Yeah, don't want to ruin what you've built. Danny hops up onto his knees on the chair, facing Reggie and the guards, oblivious to the sarcasm. DANNY Exactly. The last thing I need is to get typecast. Besides, that thing's getting a rewrite. It took like five Mulligans on Hard Money before we could talk about filming. Fuckin' writers, you know? Everyone exchanges a look. DANNY I mean, it's not like it takes a retard to write on of these. CUT TO: I/E. NARRATOR Hard Money had begun as an independent film in the vein of Rounders, where a drug-addicted card-counter tricks his way onto the World Series of Poker tour to repay his mother's mortgage. It was written by a recent USC graduate and was pegged as a favorite for Sundance. A doe-eyed film-student-type sits behind a big desk, surrounded by suits. He swallows hard. NARRATOR Howeve, after selling the rights to major studio to help finance the film, the writer was pushed out and the script was given the 'Hollywood treatment,' that included several rewrites. Cut to several harrowed-looking writers toiling in front of computers and typewriters, cursing and drinking heavily. Then cut to a shot of a young adult with Down's Syndrome punching keys on an ancient Dell. NARRATOR The final workable version came as the result of the studio's charity work-release program. BACK TO: INT. FERGUSON ESTATE - DAY DANNY Needless to say, I won't be filming that thing for quite some time. Reggie and the bodyguards exchange a look. DANNY Don't I have a match in two days? REGGIE We'll talk about it on the plane. Danny shrugs and turns back to the television, hitting play and settling back in. The movie booms once again, and Danny recites the lines and jukes along with the action scenes. Reggie sighs, then stops and grabs his crotch, dashing from the room with the script still in hands. The bodyguards just settle in, knowing full well that today was the rule, not the exception. FADE OUT.
