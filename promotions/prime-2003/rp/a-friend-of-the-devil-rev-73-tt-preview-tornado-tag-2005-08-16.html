I mean, if that doesn’t cement me as Mr. Tag Match, then fuck you all. And the competitive brilliance keeps on trucking, as I take a guy whose ass I’ve kicked twice, Dani Furolsig, and turn him into a winner as well, trumping not only one Brandon Youngblood, but then Uni champ Killean Sirrajin. I was kind of surprised that I didn’t get more props for that. It’s alright. I gave myself plenty. Now we arrive at Tag Match #5, and, barring any catastrophic mistakes on mine or Angelo’s parts, my fifth victory in my undefeated tag career here in PRIME. Hallelujah! Aimz. Honestly, I don’t have that much to say here. The Red Raver and I have said a grand total of probably two words to each other in the nine or so months that we’ve both been a part of PRIME, though who knows, she’ll probably have a mouthful to say about me. … Yeah, I’m not coming up with much. This is really Angelo’s territory. I just…don’t care. I will, however, offer my premature condolences to the Red Raver for her upcoming loss at Tropical Turmoil. And I don’t think you’ll lose because you’re a woman. I don’t think you’ll lose because of who you’ve chosen to romantically involve yourself in the past with. I think you’re going to lose because you aren’t as good as everyone else. Brandon Youngblood. Obviously, I’m a little more vocal here. Let’s end the humor and pettier shit-talking for a moment. I’m pissed off. I’m enraged. Truly, I am. Why? Look no further than ReVolution 72, to Youngblood’s 5-Star Title “Defense,” if you’re one of the people kind enough to call it that. What the fuck, Brandon? What. The. Fuck. It was a travesty. That was no title defense, and with every chair-shot to Cross’s head and body, you ruined a little more what I worked so hard to create. I gave my life to that title, Brandon, very literally. My wife begged me to stay in the hospital bed, recuperate, and come home for the week, but instead I chose to go to ReVolution 66 to defend the 5-Star Title…and seeing that I wouldn’t change, Ariel left me. I wanted so badly to make the 5-Star Title the envy of PRIME, to make people see the belt the way it used to be seen when Troy and English made it the talk of the federation…and I wanted to be its champion. I admit, I wanted the glory. But as angry as I am at you, I’m even more pissed at myself…for not finding the resolve to fight my way out of the Gridlock for the second time. I gave up…but do you want to know why? Everyone already knows, but I’ll remind you. I gave up because I knew we had created something special in the ring that night. I knew we had stolen the show, eclipsed the Main Event, and become bigger than we were when we walked into the Silverdome. And the 5-Star Title had become bigger than either of us. I gave up because I saw that the 5-Star Title would still go to a worthy champion, because as much as I loathed you, Brandon, I knew that you had that spark that drove you to succeed, to excel, to create things that people would talk about for years after you were gone. I saw that in you when you returned to PRIME looking to fight anyone and everyone who were considered to be above you. I saw that in your amazing matches and unshakable resolve. That’s why I chose you to face at Colossus II for the 5-Star Title. That’s right, Brandon. I chose you. None of the mind games mattered, none of the stupid bullshit run-ins or promos or insults... I’m not an idiot, Brandon. I know when you have something that people want, they bait you to try and get it. If I didn’t think you were 5-Star material, I would’ve brushed you off and looked elsewhere. I always choose the challenger, Brandon. I did it with Hoyt, and was right. I did it with you, and was right again… …but maybe I wasn’t as right as I thought, Brandon. Maybe you really aren’t 5-Star material. I thought you were. We certainly told a story in the ring in the Silverdome. But that was Colossus II, the big show, the big fantasy extravaganza of the year. Now it’s back to real life. Now it’s back to the week-in, week-out task of not winning the championship, but being the champion, and maybe you’re not so good at that, Brandon. Because that bullshit you pulled on ReV 72 was pathetic. It was pathetic, and it wounded that belt you wanted so badly to champion better than I ever could. And I won’t stand for it, you lunatic, chair-wielding asshole son of a cock-gobbler. I won’t stand for the 5-Star Title to be brought back down, not after I’ve given everything to see it lifted up. I know you have your big chance now, Brandon. Your big shot at the Universal Title, the same shot I have. But don’t you dare forget about the 5-Star Title. Don’t you dare shove it off to the sidelines while you go in search of your precious fucking legacy. You better make good on all the shit you talked about being the “5-Star God,” about showing pathetic druggies like me what a real champion is like. You better practice what you preached all summer, what you promised the fans if you won at Colossus II. Because if you don’t, I swear, I will get my rematch, and I will find the strength to kick out of a thousand Gridlocks, and I will take that belt back. Because honestly? I’m starting to think you don’t deserve it. The last stop before Tropical Turmoil, and all of our colliding destinies…it’s me and the Crimson Angel, and it’s you and the Red Raver. Rampage, Oxios…take notes. This is what Teh Pwn is supposed to look like.
