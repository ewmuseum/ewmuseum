Ire

29 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29Nov05 “In this story…everyone dies but the hero.” And there it … Everything you could ever imagine, just metaphorically hanging by a thread in front of your eyes. A choice so simple to determine that the power held within you almost consumes your every move with a stale heartbeat and forged breathing. It’s too easy. Take the moment to take your eyes off of the target... …your goals… …your purpose. A glance to you’re your left enlightens a look into the future; a future possibly only a few minutes away and you are seen taking a step back. You feel that you have moved in too quickly, for this is not your moment. You prefer to move in cautiously and slowly for you would undoubtedly acquire what you rightfully deserve, right? Foolish you. It’s unrealistic, for you are too ignorant – too selfish – too impatient. “The hell with that” you say as you shake your head in discontent. You live for the moment. You’re ‘bad ass’ and if it doesn’t rock, it’s not for you. Therefore, you take a look on to the other side and again, it is a look into the future. In this foretaste, you do not take a step back, but you go for that destiny that hangs right in front of your face. Without hesitation, you swipe your hand and grasp it. It is yours and you hold it within the palm of your hand. Bask in the glory of what you fight for and raise your fist in the air… …this is what you live for. A flash back into the present and there it hangs; the moment directly in front of you. You reveal a convincing smirk and grab it. As you raise your fist in the air and smile, for you believe that you have made the right choice— BANG The shunt of a bullet shatters the back of your skull and a mist of blood taints the air of which we breathe. You collapse face first into the ground with your hand still clenched shut. And there I stand, with a smoking gun in hand. “You didn’t look behind you.” Irony so deceit that it would bring a God to his knees. Don’t predict that you’ve got it all won before it’s all over, because before you know it, you will be lower than you have ever imagined, and I will prevail. ----------------------------------------------- Off the streets among the sidewalk, a man walks tall, destined to fight for what he believes in. The man is Tyler O’Neill and today his hair is burdened by a blue baseball cap with an unrecognizable white logo on the front. A pair of blue jeans, a polo shirt, and a possibly fake leather jacket accessorizes a man who walks graciously down the streets of San Diego, California. He’s got one hand in his pocket, and the other one’s… (Give me a moment to consult Alanis Morissette.) …and the other one’s by his side. (Haha, I dodged copyright infringements this time.) A cool autumn breeze fills the air, but an enjoyable afternoon in Southern California is never one to discriminate against. Turning a corner to an apartment complex, Tyler stops at the stairway and takes a seat on the third step from the bottom. Reaching in his jacket pocket he pulls out a pack of cigarettes and opens the box to reveal three cigarettes. He pulls one out and places it in his mouth and places the cigarette box by his side. He then reaches in his left pocket in pursue of a lighter, but comes out empty handed. Tyler stands up and checks the pockets in his jeans, but still finds no lighter. Tyler pulls the cigarette out of his mouth and throws it on the sidewalk out of anger. Sitting back down with a disgruntled look on his face, Tyler reveals a large sigh and stares at the cigarette. He stands up and looks around and makes his way down the steps. Pacing onto the sidewalk, Tyler swiftly and in stride picks up his cigarette and places it back in his mouth. Three second rule, right? Determined to fuel his nicotine more efficiently to ingest it into his lungs, Tyler patiently waits for someone to approach. An older man walks his way and they meet eyes with Tyler. This causes the man to look uncomfortable to Tyler standing in his way and crosses the street. Tyler shakes his head as if the day couldn’t get much shittier. Suddenly-- Whoa. Check this girl out. (I know it’s not that easy checking her out in a text format, but seriously, hear me out.) With a light fall jacket covering her blouse, but a nice mini skirt is easily noticeable at the peak of her tall tan legs. Tyler’s eyes shift and scan her body more than any machine would with your retinas. She walks with her head down, quickly scurrying to her destination. She was cold and the goose bumps lining her legs and the faint redness on her nose was a sure way to know. She didn’t seem like a smoker, but he was determined to get her name. Only a few feet away she stops and looks up at him. Before he could even release a friendly gesture, she speaks in the deepest voice he has ever heard. Hey do you have a light? He should have noticed from the bulge in her crotch and the fact that those weren’t goose bumps on her legs, but it was actually leg hair entrapped by the leggings (s)he had on. A tear rolls down Tyler’s cheek and the (s)he disgruntled, continues to walk away because of the mute response they received from Tyler. Tyler takes the cigarette out of his mouth and makes his way back to the porch at the foot of his apartment and grabs his cigarette pack. He places the cigarette back in the pack and drops it on the ground and then stomps on it. “I need to quit” he says as he kicks the crumbled pack into a sewer and prepares to walk into the building. Shaking his head in discontent, he puts his hands in his pocket and walks up the stairs. Tyler suddenly stops and his eyes sharpen with anger. Releasing his hand from his pocket slowly, a lighter is revealed in his right hand. ”That was my last pack.”
