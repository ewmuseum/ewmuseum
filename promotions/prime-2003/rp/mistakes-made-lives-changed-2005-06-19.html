Mistakes made, lives changed.

19 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 19th The stifling heat was radiating from the clear skies in Winnipeg. Killean had made a big decision and had already called PRIME headquarters to advise them of the situation. Between events, Killean would be moving from San Diego back to his hometown of Winnipeg. Yes that’s right, The Supreme Machine was coming home, for good. Sirrajin had taken plenty of time to think about it. After all, he had to think of his wife who is currently buried in San Diego. But after a few talks with Tchu, he figured it would work wonders for his recovery and Kira would want that for him anyways. If he would be happy doing it, then do it. That’s the way Kira always thought about things. She wanted to see her husband happy. This decision made him happy. It all started when he arrived back in Winnipeg and was being shown around the city by Rebecca. He had fallen in love with the area all over again. He remembered all the good times that he had when he was last there and he wanted to live it all over again, for good. When he came out from behind the curtains into the MTS Center on ReVolution 66, the crowd had basically made the decision for him. As A Perfect Circle had played his entrance theme, the crowd literally exploded and greeted the hometown hero with the loudest reception of the night. It felt like it went on forever. Inside, Killean was broken down, inwardly weeping because the fans showed their love for him. It was simple really, he had to be home. He had to return to the place he loved so much and he had to do it now. No more of this visiting every now and again. His house in San Diego would still be his. He had no reason to get rid of it really. Plus he didn’t want the burden of moving all of his belongings back up to Canada. He would use the house now and again. He had also made a deal with Vivian. She would get full use of the house anytime she wanted. Basically, she now got to live there. It was a decision that really wasn’t that hard for him. Vivian was like family to him and that’s what family does for each other. Killean would now be in the market for a home in Winnipeg. At the moment he was staying in the Fairmont in Downtown Winnipeg. But the name of that hotel kind of brought another recent memory flooding back. Just that same day, Killean was caught red handed so to speak in that very hotel. He had to go and hide in the bathroom as Dave Gibson came knocking on Amy Campbells door. But the question arises, what was Killean doing in Amy’s room in the first place? It appeared that Killean and Amy had consumed plenty of alcohol the night before. It wasn’t enough to be debilitating but it was definitely enough to cloud their judgment. Well it just so happened that Killean had woken up the next morning alongside a rather naked Amy Campbell. At that point they had known what had happened and they began to joke about it a little bit. The only thing that Amy didn’t know is that Killean had been thinking about this day for quite sometime. He really never thought it would happen but now that it has, he didn’t have any regrets to be totally honest. He knew how Amy felt about it happening, he just didn’t know what she was thinking about their future. Could it be that something was to evolve between the two of them? Could it be that their relationship could grow beyond a good friendship. PRIME had been kept in the dark about most of their friendship to this point and no one really knew that there was much interaction between the two superstars at all. The awkward part about it all is that Dave Gibson, a very good friend of Aimz, knew about their interaction from the previous night. The friends walked around completely shrouded in secrecy and no one was the wiser. But what would Dave Gibson do? Would he go back to PRIME and spill the beans? Would he shun Amy for her actions? What would he do? Killean would have to find out sooner or later. But the one thing that he constantly had on his mind is whether Amy would want to move forward or just forget the night ever happened. He was curious and he would have to find out for sure. Meanwhile, before ReVolution began last week, Killean had run into a special guest in his locker room. The guest was a man that he really never thought he would see again. It had been many moons since this individual dared show his face around the Supreme Machine. But what was it that made Jin Daikusa return to face his former prodigy? Jin was a very selfish man at one point and anything he usually did was for his own benefit. But Killean could not look back and not give Jin his just deserves. The man trained him to be a wrestler when he really had no where else to turn. Everything that he knew was basically an extension of the training that was given to him. Now, he was standing in a small gym facility in the Tec Voc High School in Winnipeg to meet with his former trainer and to show him his moves. When Jin Daikusa rolled out in his wheel chair being accompanied by two large men, Killean knew this would be more than just your typical workout. Jin wanted to test the Universal Champion; he wanted to see if he was really good enough to lay claim to the title that he so proudly wore. “Killean, glad you could make it.” Jin began, stopping just a mere two feet from the Supreme Machine. “These are my bodyguards, Gene and Fredrick.” Both men extended hands for Killean to shake. Backing away a little, he could not help but feel a little threatened being in the company of two men who were six feet seven inches tall and weighed next to four hundred pounds each. But he shook their hands anyways, one after the other and the bodyguards went about their warm ups. Killean on the other hand came close to Jin and stood over him, crossing his arms and meeting the wheelchair bound gaze of the smaller Japanese man. “I haven’t seen you in a few years. Forgive me if I am not completely happy to see you.” Killean began, piercing a hole through his red goggles into the beady little eyes of Jin Daikusa. “You screwed me over once before. May god help you if you decide to screw me again. I’ll break your arms so you’ll have to push that controller for the wheelchair around with your ass, you get the picture?” He knew he sounded quite harsh, but Jin just smiled at him. “Killean, if I was going to screw you around, then I definitely wouldn’t have come all the way up here to do it.” He stated, sounding much more relaxed than anyone else would if Sirrajin had threatened to break their arms. “You have it all, or so you think. Do you even have a submission move in your arsenal?” Killean thought to himself for the moment and realized that Jin was right. He didn’t have a proper submission hold to use in the ring. He had never made anyone tap out and any good champion had at least one submission to use. As Killean was working out with the two bodyguards, he had other things on his mind. He had been told by Amy to leave the hotel room and not be there when she returned. She had definitely come back now, unless she was looking for Gibson. It really had been an awkward situation for her and truth be told, she was the only thing on his mind right now. He had been working with these two bodyguards and even though he was handling them with little to no effort; Jin Daikusa still noticed that something was wrong. He figured he had better get this workout over with quickly and show Killean why he brought him here. “OK boys, enough. Gene, get on the ground on your stomach.” Jin rolled over on the mats to where Killean was standing. He had yet to break a sweat. “I am impressed at how far you have come Killean. You were the best student in my class before, now you outshine anyone I have ever taught. But I have your trump card Killean and I will show you what it is now. Get on the back of Gene like you are going to apply a camel clutch.” Killean did as he was instructed and sat on the bigger man’s back. “Now, first of all reach around his head from right to left with your left arm.” “You mean like a Dragon Sleeper?” Killean questioned as he completed the first step. Jin nodded and the bigger man on the bottom was in quite a precarious position already. But Jin had more up his sleeve. “Now even though this hold alone can make anyone squeal like a child, I want you to fold his right arm back behind him and hold it in a chicken wing position.” Killean performed the action as Jin was speaking, almost instinctively knowing what was going to be asked of him. It brought back some old memories for The Supreme Machine and they weren’t really all bad. “Now as soon as you have that synched in, lean back and pull as hard as you can.” If Gene was in pain before, it was nothing compared to the pain he was faced with now considering he lit up the entire gymnasium with screams. Killean had sat back and synched the hold in tight as he could and the yelling followed not a split second afterwards. The hold had maybe been applied for a full couple seconds when Gene was tapping the mat furiously. He wanted out of the hold and that second was not even soon enough. Killean had gotten to his feet a second later and watched the big man roll around on the mat like a fish out of water. He looked down in awe that he could possibly inflict that much pain on one individual with little to no effort. “Surprised?” Jin smiled at Killean. He could do nothing but nod as Frederick helped Gene off the mat. The pain had almost caused the bodyguard to black out. “It’s a dangerous hold that I have never taught to anyone before. Some people wouldn’t even understand the magnitude of a hold this deadly. No matter the strength of the person it is being applied to, they cannot power out of this hold. Once locked in, you could argue that it is unbreakable.” Killean watched as Fredericks tried to revive his fallen comrade. Jin Daikusa smiled once again, having taught Killean the final piece of the puzzle to make him an unstoppable killing machine. Later that evening, Sirrajin was walking down Broadway in Winnipeg with his hands buried in the pockets of his jacket. Amy was still the only thought flooding his mind but he had something else to do and it really didn’t involve the dynamic redhead that he had grown attached to. He was thinking about ReVolution this coming week in Buffalo, New York. The road to Colossus was well underway and Hoyt Williams stood in the champion’s way. Boda and Jon Kano were to be involved in the match, but with Boda being hit by a truck, their involvement in the match is undoubtedly in question. But before that, Killean has an obstacle to surpass. “Peter Vetra… the official lapdog of Hoyt Williams and the Imperium. You are just like what Dani Furher used to be. Vangelus Olsig is a dangerous individual and I can attest to that. I was his first opponent. I had to work very hard to retain the Universal Title against him. But now I have you Vetra. I am competing against a shadow of a man. Has this so called ‘God’ done anything for you lately? Of course not, so why hang around with Hoyt and the Imperium? But I don’t expect you to wake up like Vangelus did.” Killean arrived at the end of Broadway leading on to Main Street. He looked forward into the train station that has been around for many, many years. It’s a landmark so to speak. Killean was only a few short blocks away from the hotel now and he was debating on heading back there to talk to Katie. “Peter, I realize that you are hungry for the win. Underneath it all you are probably still your own person. But Hoyt Williams has gotten to you. Why fall for all his rants and ramblings? Why fall for all his false prophecies and hope? Why not ask him to prove that anything he preaches about even exists? That would be smart because he would never be able to prove it, but I know you won’t do that. So I will be forced to make an example out of you in front of a packed house in Buffalo. You don’t know who my trainer is, no one in PRIME has even heard of him, but he taught me something today that will be the key to my keeping this title for as long as I see fit. Vetra, I will unleash this hell on you come ReVolution. I call it “The Last Call” because your screaming will be the last call before the bell rings. I promise you Peter Vetra, I will make you tap out on Wednesday. And I have never gone back on a promise.” Killean was still looking down Main Street from Broadway. If he was to turn left, he would be heading back to the hotel. If he was to turn right, he would be heading farther away from the hotel and possibly further away from his friendship with Amy. He really cared about her and he didn’t want to do anything to put that in jeopardy. But he felt like a heel inside because he had allowed himself to drink enough to impair his judgment. He doesn’t regret wanting to get closer to Amy but if it was to harm their friendship, he wouldn’t have done it. He snapped out of his thoughts when his cell phone started to ring. He looked at the screen before flipping the phone open. “Amy…” He flipped the phone open and greeted her. “Hello?” “Killean; it’s Amy. I am sorry for earlier when I told you to leave but I had to think and clear my head.” “That’s alright… listen I am going to…” “I want you to come back, we need to talk…” This was Amy’s last response before she hung up the phone.
