Date: Great American Nightmare A man walks in with a stern look on his face as his eyes scan the room carefully. His hands rest on his hips the entire time that he's in the room, a small one with just a few lights inside of it, and all of the amenities that you could imagine. With a meeting like this it's the norm. This man checks out every inch, every corner, and every nook and cranny trying to make sure it's as secure as possible. Dressed in black glasses, a black suit, and black shoes he looks every bit the job that he's been hired for; a bodyguard. Satisfied with the state of everything he walks back over to door before pulling the door open and ushering in the man he's been hired to protect. Alexander Lewis. His demeanor is rather calm as he enters this high-profile meeting with no agenda attached to it. Frankly, he didn't have the time to put one together as it was called hastily three hours ago. All he knew was that his presence would be required immediately, and judging by the person who called him he knew better then to just ignore the request. Well, not so much a request as a demand, and when certain people demand things, they get exactly what they want. His brow is furrowed as he looks around at the smallish room, not impressed in the least bit. "Is this all they can afford on such short notice? Maybe they don't quite understand who I am," he mutters under his breath as the only person in the room is his bodyguard who could care less about the size of the room. Alexander rolls his eyes as he sits down in the leather chair provided for him and patiently waits for his guest to arrive. The seconds roll by slowly, and Lewis, generally a patient person, is quite squeamish in his chair. His impatience could be attributed to many things, but the clear winner would have to be the meeting. Two minutes pass before Alexander checks his watch and just lets out a large sigh. As he does so, another door in the room opens and a large man, as wide as he is tall, walks through it quickly followed by a coffee-skinned woman. They scan the room rather quickly before their eyes land on Alexander. "Is your boss planning on coming anytime soon or should I have to wait like the lapdog that she thinks of me?" he asks them, but the pair provides no response, just an amused smile from the woman. They turn their backs to him, splitting up and standing on opposite of one another by the door. Seconds later, a person walks through that threshold wearing a long trench coat burnished red, hair up in her hat. A thin pair of sunglasses, perched on her nose, completes the outfit. She places a bag down on a table while Lewis only watches. "Frankly, it's about time. If Mr. Williamson had told me that you wouldn't quite be so punctual then I would've adjusted my schedule accordingly. Nonetheless, he tells me you want to talk business, so let's get down to it," he tells the person as they unbuckle their trench coat, settling the hat onto the back of the chair carefully. Every motion is controlled, every twitch of a muscle precise. A fall of red hair kisses the woman's shoulders. Alexander just sighs again as he shakes his head, not a fan of being ignored. "Would you like to start with names?" Alexander asks, but she simply shakes her head, folding the coat neatly over the chair before sitting. Thin, gloved fingers pinch off the shades, setting them carefully down on the table. "I know your name, Mr. Lewis. You know of my reputation, my power in seeking you out. Not my name. It would be... impolite... not to share," the woman says carefully, her soft whisper like a stiletto in the night. "My name is Alexandra Pierce." ~~~~~~~~~~~ "I NEED SOME HELP DOWN HERE!" Your memories for our lives," Samantha continues from Derek's statement. "That...," Derek begins, but his voice cracks. "That's why you can't remember any of it. You told Kristen to just go. That it would be better this way. Almost like you might be absolved of your sins...," Samantha picks up from where Derek leaves off. Craig nods his head before closing his eyes. Could he have been that big of an idiot? "But, I couldn't be absolved of those sins," he tells them, knowing how painful the past two years had been to him. "No, you couldn't. Life wouldn't let you. You had made far too many enemies. Alexandra, she wiped your mind. Left you in an alley somewhere, with nothing. Not even your dignity. We wanted to come and save you, but Kristen told us not to," Samantha tells him sadly. The tears are starting to build up in her eyes, but she swallows hard and in an instant they're gone. "She told us that you knew this moment would come. Told her to stay away from you," Tyler says his first words in a few moments. "If so, why am I here? Why were you looking for me?" Craig asks, looking each of them in the eyes. Derek is the first to chime in. "Because, she told us if anything ever happened to her, that we had to come to get you. That..." "That if things had gotten that bad that it would only be the precursor for the future. That you would be the only person who could stop it all," Samantha manages to finish as she crosses her arms across her chest. "She wanted us to give you... this...," Derek tells him before handing him a CD. Craig takes a long look at the CD before looking back at Derek. "What am I supposed to do with this?" he asks him as Tyler and Samantha start to walk out of the room, hoping that he would be able to pick up on everything quickly enough to have an impact sooner than later. "Watch it. It should help you with your memories..." Derek tells Craig before he starts heading for the door too. "What is this, Star Trek?" Craig yells out after him. "It's just what we were told to do. Something about your mind being conditioned a certain way..." Derek says before the door closes behind him. Craig takes a deep breath in before entering the disc into the computer console. Quickly, the computer displays a face of Kristen who is smiling at her old friend. "I hoped that you would never have to do this. I wanted to allow you to have a normal life. It seems like I've failed you...," she says as Craig takes a seat and begins to watch the video. The holes that had plagued him for so long would now be filled. ~~~~~~~~~~~ Mary-Lynn Mayweather didn't need for this to happen. Not the evening before Great American Nightmare. Tossing and turning in the wrinkled sheets while the air conditioning kept bothering her and causing her to stay awake instead of falling asleep wasn't her ideal phone number. She sits up in her bed and scratches her head as she sighs under her breath. Days had become much more stressful then she could've ever wished. Taking care of Jack and Tony had been the least of her worries. Training to be a sufficient wrestler and watching after Craig had taken forefront. Things had become crazier and crazier for her, and she wasn't quite sure they would become normal ever again. CRACK! Her eyes quickly dash across the room, trying to find the source of the noise, but finds nothing. Slowly, she gets up to her feet and starts inching towards her bedroom door when she hears footsteps inside of the house. Fear strikes her as she opens the door and peers out of it to find nothing. Mary-Lynn then races down the hallway and turns into Jack's room when she's tossed into the door of his room! She strikes as hard as she can, but is unable to do anything as shadowy faces look at her before looking back at Jack and Tony, the two of them awake with guns being held to their heads. "You think they'd want them dead or alive?" one of the men asks as another man moves closer and closer to Mary-Lynn. "The idea is to send a message to Craig. Having them missing wouldn't send nearly as good of a message as him finding their dead bodies," another voice speaks out. Mary-Lynn looks on in fear as her life and fate is being decided right before her very eyes. "We've done that plenty of times though...," he says, his voice trailing off. "Then, how about we try a new one on for surprise," another voice enters the discussion before a scuffle breaks out. Mary-Lynn tries to make sense of the action as an elbow collides into another man's jaw. Gunshots are then heard which is quickly followed by silence. A light comes on inside of the room as Mary-Lynn immediately looks over at Jack and Tony, and breathes a sigh of relief to find them alright though a little shaken. Then, Mary-Lynn looks over to see a man and a woman holding out guns, pointed at the three of them. "Now, you're coming with us." ~~~~~~~~~~~ "How do you think he's holding up in there?" Derek looks over at Tyler and just shrugs his shoulders. "Honestly, I have no idea. It's been nearly two hours now. You know how his mind works. It's scarred beyond belief. It's been prodded and toyed with to the point of no return. He could die in there; he could have a mental breakdown. Or, he'll be like Craig always is and come out ready for a fight. Until he opens that door and is ready to assume his spot as leader, I'm not quite sure how he'll respond." "I bet that just eats you up inside," Tyler says cockily, and as Derek is about to respond the door to the room opens to show Craig standing there, his head bowed low, and his hands inside of his pocket. Samantha walks up to him and puts her hand on his shoulder once again to comfort him. "It's a lot, isn't it...," she whispers in his ear as he just nods his head. "Trust me, Craig, we believe in you. Otherwise we wouldn't have come and brought you back in. Things are starting to get really bad out there," she tells him as Craig once again nods his head. He looks up at Derek and Tyler, before ushering them into the room. As Craig takes a step inside of the room, he can feel a large weight landing squarely on top of his shoulders. The world around him, the one he had once fought so hard to protect, was starting to burn down to the ground and at a rapid pace. The enemies were staring him straight in the face. Alexander Lewis. Alexandra Pierce. They had a huge head start on him. He would be playing catch up. "So, now what?" Craig looks at them, but before any of them have a chance to answer him, the door to the room opens up and a woman walks in. Even with his memories intact, he doesn't remember this one. However, Derek immediately runs over to her with this worried look on his face. "Eliza, did you get them?" he asks them immediately to which Craig shoots them both a look "Who's them?" Craig asks them, but before he gets to hear the answer to that question, in walk Jack Harmon, Tony Davis, and Mary-Lynn Mayweather. Craig hadn't even thought of them through this whole process and walks over to them as behind them walks in another man. "Craig," Mary-Lynn Mayweather whispers under her breath before hugging Craig as hard as possible. "Craig, man, they filled us in on everything," Jack tells him before throwing his thumb backwards towards the big hulking man that walks in after them. "Craig, this young woman here is Eliza. The big guy in the back is James," Derek tells him and Craig acknowledges both of them. Derek then turns his attention to James. "Did you guys get there in time?" "Barely," he tells him before Derek looks back at Craig. "We had some solid intel that someone was going after these three. Figured it would be best to get them out of there," Derek tells them as Craig just looks over at his closest friends and his hands turn into fists as he's ready to fight. "Got there and found a crew there ready to take them out. If we hadn't moved, they wouldn't be here right now," James tells Craig who just nods his head before looking back at Jack and Mary-Lynn. "I'm so sorry," he tells them, his emotions wrecking havoc upon his body. Yet, Jack holds his hand up and looks Craig right in the eyes. "I'm pissed at you," he begins to tell Craig who knew this was coming. "I'm pissed that you have a group of assassins and you NEVER TOLD ME! Dude! ME! I'm like fucking fuck-you-in-the-ass-ninja-style! And you never invited me?! Dude, I thought we were like this!" Craig can only laugh as he shakes his head at Jack who is ready for a fight. He then looks over at Mary-Lynn. "Look, we owe you so much. We're a team, Craig. If Desade is behind all of this, then it's time to fight back," she tells him and Craig can only nod his head as Samantha and Tyler walk up to them. China's first," he tells them to some rather perplexed looks. "Why China?" Tyler asks him. "One word. Tian," he tells them as he walks over to the computer console. "We've got a war about to be waged, and we've got some people we need to see. We need to stock up and get as much information as possible. Tian."
