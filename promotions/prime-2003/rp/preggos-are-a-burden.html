Date: CWMC Round 2 At the top of her third trimester, April Monday never anticipated her life at a point this low. Right now she should be married to a wonderful man, sharing with him a two-story house complete with marble counter-tops and shag carpeting, awaiting the birth of their beautiful and healthy boy. All of these possibilities disappeared with the father of her son, Brock Shepherd. Three weeks ago the man went missing, and ever since April has been a shell of the person she was when she was with him. Every aspect of her life has always revolved around Brock, whether she was playing the role of his on-screen manager on shows or the role of his pregnant girlfriend at home. When he went missing, she was reduced to a mere portion of who she's supposed to be. Her life descended into constant affliction; self-tormented over the possibilities of him being alive or dead. She stopped showering, stopped leaving her home, and began wallowing in her own pitiful filth. Her apartment quickly became dirty before an even quicker transition to disgusting. Truly an unbearable fate. This morning, however, hope is presented in the most insidious of ways. In the wake of receiving considerably devastating news, April lay sprawled out on the floor of her single bedroom apartment, sobbing uncontrollably with its source crumpled up tight in her hand. Some part of her knows she should feel happy, but the other half would be disgusted. How can she be happy after seeing something like a ransom note, no matter what possibilities they present? There was no one else to turn to. No one else that could help. No where to go, except for the arms of a complete stranger. Her front door bursts open by way of Illustrious Face-Eater. "I CAME AS SOON AS I GOT THE MESSAGE! IS EVERYTHING ALRIGHT? IS YOUR FETUS BEING STUBBORN? FEELING THE NEED TO BE INDUCED? "Don't you worry, baby. We'll get you outta there." "It's not the baby. It's Brock." "Who?" "Brock! Mm-my husband!" She cries, finding the topic very difficult to speak about, much less have to endure it. "OH, right. THAT GUY. What about him?" Her face collapse conveniently into one hand while the other hand presents Adam with the very note she received this morning. Because seeing it with his own eyes would be a better way of understanding the pain she's experiencing. He rips the ransom letter from April's grip to examine it closely as she stomps to her sofa. She can't focus on anything else, anymore. All of her energy must be devoted to be being miserable. Reading aloud the letters cut-and-pasted to the paper, he says, "deer april if u one a c ur huzzy again ull broght 88thousant dollerz n unmarkt billz or maybe swiss bank acount untrasabel n shit or else brocky junoir gunna be an bastard orfin conprinday. brin da $$$ 2 da drop @ da dress b low n NO KOPZ or i kyll dem N ur huzzy." Adam can't help but look at the note as if it were written in an alien dialect. "So, wait, what's this supposed to be?" But she ignores his question. "I didn't know what else to do!" April says in a fashion indicating she'd been waiting to speak to someone for an awfully long time. "There's no one that'd be willing to help me--." "Well, that's why I'm here, M'lady; TO PROVIDE YOU WITH THE SERVICE NO LESSER MAN CAN!" "So you're going to help me?" "I didn't bring any rubs but I guess I could use some saran-wrap just as well, but you can't get pregnant if you already are, can you? I don't wanna be bringin' no Mini-Dicks around." "Adam, please help me!" "Alright, alright, don't start popping babies out your crotch and just settle down. Let's think about this for a moment..." Taking a seat next to April on the couch, the Face-Eater places a hand in his chin, placing himself in deep-think-mode. "So it's your baby's daddy who is kidnapped?" She nods. "And these people want eighty-eight-thousand dollars for him to be returned?" She nods. "And you're sure you want to pay?" She nods, uncontrollably breaking into a fit in the process. "But... I don't have that kind of money! I don't even know where I'd start looking for it. I can't-- EIGHTY THOUSAND DOLLARS FOR MY HUSBAND'S LIFE?! WHO WOULD DO SUCH A HIDEOUS THING?!" He's gotten quite annoyed with the woman, what with her incessant crying. Not to mention the fact that she keeps mentioning her baby's daddy. "You don't have the money? Well what the fuck are you going to do! THEY'RE GOING TO FUCKING KILL HIM AND YOU'RE JUST SITTING HERE WHINING!" "I KNOW." Something's not sitting right with Dick. And no, it isn't because of the awkward position his balls are resting inside his pants. Well, it is, but it isn't just that. It's the combination of many aspects. Like the fact that he has to deal with a Ransom drop off. Or the fact that he's withdrawn $88,000 from his own bank account to pay for the safe return of a man he doesn't even know. For a woman that isn't even his girl. Most importantly, it's the fact that said woman is pregnant and he has yet to bone her despite exhausting all of his resources. Well, maybe not all of his resources. Needless to say, however, the Face-Eater is fed up. If paying eighty-eight grand to get this bitch's husband back isn't going to get him laid, there is very little left on this that planet that would. This grim outlook was beginning to upset Facey, causing his face to crunch up as he stares at the path in front of him, stomping down the storefronts lining Main Street with little regard for anyone or anything along his path. Men, women, children, three-legged dogs and half-retarded midgets all feel the wrath of his stiffened shoulders as he stays his steady course. With an amount just shy of a hundred-thousand-dollars sitting in the Samsonite briefcase clutched firmly in his right hand, it'd be hard to get Adam's grip loose. But if anything could him to sacrifice what little amount was left of his money, it's the good fucking of a pregnant woman. That's not to say Adam still couldn't be pissed, despite the eventual baby-poking he looks forward to. I mean, dude's losing a lot of money! And for the sake of a woman's happiness, no less. Some would argue that such an act is selfish. Not those who know the Illustrious Face-Eater. He walks towards the drop; a shitty apartment complex one block north of the intersection of Main and Center, the heart of Downtown. Only a few yards away from where he knows he's supposed to be, Facey stops in his tracks for a moment and examines his surroundings. He looks to a point across the street, right in front of the Tired Oaks Living Community that shares the same address on the ransom note he clutches in the hand opposite the briefcase. Standing there, waiting for her cue, is April Monday. Her eyes are red, worn, longing to be anywhere else than where she is. Regardless, she stands on her mark as Adam takes a position on the opposite side of the street that allows him a clear view of the path she'd take. With the briefcase still tight in his hand, Facey nods and motions for April to "go ahead." She allows herself a deep breath before summoning the courage this moment requires. In..... Exhale. Sniffing away the snot and tears that have been persisting all week, April walks the path that she's supposed to. See, every action, whether they belong to her or Adam, has been predetermined by the plan the two concocted prior. At this point, there is no room for mistakes. Everyone knows that kidnappers are worthless, lying pieces of shit. To walk into this situation without a plan would be suicide. Fucking this up is something neither party can afford; whether at the sake of April's husband or Adam's money, there is a lot to lose. Luckily, the Illustrious Face-Eater is a crafty mother fucker. If anyone can think of a way to walk into a ransom situation and walk out with both the money AND the kidnapped party, it's the Mother Fucking Face-Eater. He observes silently from his position across the street, watching as April navigates her way through the open courtyard in the complex. She makes her way to Apartment Number 15 as designated on her ransom note. Even before she can go to knock is she startled as the door swings open revealing a large, intimidating black man standing in the frame. The man's large presence serves to intimidate her further, rendering her frozen in her tracks. Adam, from across the street, oversees their interaction despite being able to hear their exchange. He watches, from what it looks like, as April fearfully attempts to explain herself to the large man who doesn't appear to be happy. It's probably due to the fact that she has no money on her persons, when the ransom note explicitly instructed the opposite. She points at Adam across the street, still holding the money-filled briefcase in his hand, attempting to tell him that the exchange would have to be in public to go down. Apparently, the thug doesn't particularly enjoy what she suggests him to do. So he replies by glancing at Adam with the meanest sneer one could possibly have written across his face, before gripping April by the shoulders and shoving her inside the apartment. The exchange occurs so quickly that Adam doesn't even have a moment to react before the door slams shut with April Monday captured inside. "Fuck," he mutters to himself before sprinting across the street, dodging the coming traffic then spilling into the courtyard. Within a matter of seconds, the entire situation has gotten out of hand. "I've got to quit smoking before I plan out shit like this."
