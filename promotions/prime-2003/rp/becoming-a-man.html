Date: UltraViolence There was honestly little for David Noble to do in Minneapolis. Sure, he had thrown out the first pitch at a Twins game (Twins even offered him a contract afterwards), and had seen the largest mall in America. Yet, as he sat in his hotel room he was bored out of his skull. Two months had passed since he joined the organization known as PRIME and in those two months, he had won 5 of his first 7 matches. However, the ultimate test would be in two night’s time when he had to step into the ring with three other competitors and compete for a chance to be the #1 Contender for the Intense Title. Things had actually been good for Noble. His relationship with his family had grown stronger over the previous weeks, something that was a stark contrast to the previous three years. Finding success in the ring had given him an extra gear of motivation, and spending his time with the lovely Mary-Lynn Mayweather had been an added bonus as the two kept finding ways to keep each other company on the road. Yet, there was still a large hole in his heart and he found it eating away at him in the fancy hotel room he sat in. He walked over to the nearby mini-bar and pulled the door open with his eyes set on finding anything with alcohol in it. Thankfully, the hotel management knew just how the PRIME wrestlers enjoyed partying it up, and had stocked the rooms rather efficiently for their multi-night stay. David eagerly grabbed a few bottles of Vodka, Rum, and Gin, and began to tear the tops off of them before he returned to his spot on the edge of the bed. As he turned the television on, his lips met the cold plastic and felt the vacant alcohol mesh with his tongue and slide down his throat with no resistance in the least bit. David closed his eyes as he felt the sweetness overtake him and a sly smile appeared on his face. The channels flicked through as David tried his best to find something to hold his attention for more than a second and watched as it landed on ESPN. Nothing good was on, but it didn’t matter as he downed another bottle of alcohol and tossed the bottle into the small cylindrical trashcan next to the television. The third bottle went down faster than expected and David rose from his seat with an urge for more. He pulled the door open and grabbed a glass from the counter above it. In the back of the refrigerator was a large bottle of Sky Vodka and a smile plastered itself across David’s face. As he filled the glass to the top, David took it, and finished it off in the shortest minute ever recorded by mankind. The alcohol had started to have some effect on David, but he could care less as he felt more alive in the past few minutes then he had in quite some time. "Seem to be enjoying yourself." David spun around in a hurry and found himself face to face with a tall man who had a sunken face. Noble’s eyes narrowed as he shook his head and looked the man up and down. "What the hell are you doing here?" The man chuckled at the sight of David and looked at the strewn bottles on the floor. "Glad to see you’re making good use of your time. Becoming a drunk? Man, I thought you would’ve surely turned your life around by this point, but I guess not," the man continued and it was obvious that he was disappointed in David. David had begun to get angry as he held the empty glass in the air and pointed at the man. "You’re not supposed to be here! YOU’RE NOT SUPPOSED TO BE HERE!" The rage filled the room as the intruder slipped his hands inside of his pocket and shrugged his shoulders. "Why not?" the man questioned David and David’s free hand started to ball up into a fist. "Because," David began as he tried his best to calm himself down. "Because, you’re supposed to be dead, Dad," David finished as he looked at his father who stood there in the mirror with a grin on his face. *** Four Years Earlier "Seriously, how could you even say that?!" His eyes had to been deceiving him. That’s what he kept telling himself. David paced around in the driveway as he shook his head in disbelief as he tried to accept the reality of it all. His father had cheated on his mother, the only two people in the world who he felt like he could trust more than anything. David’s hands shot inside of his pocket as he heard the front door open and Shawn rushed up to his son, this time clothed. "David--," Shawn began but David held his hand up to his father. "Dad, tell me this is just some kind of joke. Tell me I didn’t come home to find some woman grinding into you. Just tell me that. So, just, please, just tell me that I saw things wrong," David pleaded with his father but from the look in Shawn’s eyes he could tell it was the complete opposite. Shawn looked down at the ground as he felt ashamed and embarrassed. "David, I made a mistake," Shawn tried his best to explain it to David, but David’s eyes grew wide as his own father couldn’t even lie to him. David pushed his father away from him as the door opened up and out came the woman that had been the mistress in this relationship. "Shawn," she began, but David held up both of his hands to her. "Oh, please, do me a favor and just get out of here," David started as the woman looked at him in disbelief. "How dare you? Looking at himself through the fragments of glass. *** Four Years Ago David looked at the clock. Thirty minutes had passed since he had parked the car three blocks down from the woman’s house. His fingers tapped on the steering wheel and he looked anxiously out of the window, but still no sight of his father. For some reason, David had decided to trust his father on this day. Shawn had told David that he would end it with this woman, but David knew that the only reason Shawn was doing it was because of the fact that David had told him if he didn’t end it that he would tell Mom everything. As another minute passed by, David decided to take matters into his own hands, and turned the car back on. David turned the corner and moved the car ever so slightly to avoid another vehicle. Then, he felt this weird sense come over him. David brushed it to the side as his eyes continued to scan the surrounding neighborhood and parked his car on the side of the road again before he opened the door. Noble stepped out and began to walk towards the house where Shawn had been in for the past half-an-hour, and strode across the lawn.
