Into The Abyss: Part XXI - Rhode To Colossus: Jekyll

26 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26-08-09 A distorted shimmering smile beamed back at him. Running his fingers over the polished metal he gazed in wonderment at the prize in his grasp. He bathed his slack face in the golden hue, enamoured by the clarity of the engravings and losing himself in the complex markings. His smile melted and he bit his lip, staring beyond the hypnotizing gold at the reflection looking back. Violet bruises marred the flawless finish, and he gently dabbed his brow and winced as the marks throbbed from the pressure. Mirror, mirror on the wall would have told him he was an ugly brute but in the light of the Elite Championship he was something else entirely. One man's back pressed to a canvas mat for three insignificant seconds was all it took and over thirty thousand roaring voices silenced all doubt with a chorus of three syllables bellowed into the Boston air. HESS-EE-AN! HESS-EE-AN! HESS-EE-AN!! Running the leather strap across his palm he breathed in the sweet musky odour and gripped the belt either side of the dinner plate-sized medallion bearing his name. War had practically broken out in the pursuit of this prize, and Von Kelsig had walked in on his own amidst a crowd of Sin City natives eager to snatch it up for their side. One PRIMEate standing tall against the odds, outnumbered and out-gunned but never out-wrestled. He chapped the gold, a solid thunk meeting each strike. He swooned over the title as though it were the one Ring, his once stern visage long since put to rest. A strange thing had happened when the bell rang and his name was called out. In the days leading up to Colossus he had moped around the city of Boston melancholy. Only when he trained did his rage surface, and on several occasions came close to breaking his personal weight records. In the fortnight since his split from Gloria he tried to focus his energy on his two night stand at the upcoming pay-per-view. When personal appearances and interviews beckoned he channelled his anger into Hessian and gradually absorbed into the hype for the show, pushing the bad memories from his mind and in the final hours before Colossus forgot Von Kelsig and all his problems and became the Murder Show. Stalking the corridors of Fenway Park he felt symbiotic with this character he had created nearly a decade ago. Hessian was on everyone's lips, he had a shot at Primetime Central's greatest prize (after already having previously won the Global Title years before) and to top it all off he had finally broken that glass ceiling into the main event, he was a viable threat now. His three year hiatus was well worth it given the success of 2009. Von Kelsig was a failed businessman and he was unlucky in love, had no casual friends and no other skills to speak of as prominent as his wrestling ability. Von Kelsig was half the man that Hessian was. He thought it pitiful that a television character had a better life than he did, even if he was the man behind the character. Before winning the Elite Championship he could not foresee any hope for salvation in the aftermath of the break-up with Gloria. Now as he fawned over his title and cooed like a child with a treasure, he appreciated what the win had meant to him. His entire body trembled with excitement even now, days after the win over Wyatt Connors and Desade. His face was wrinkled from almost constant smiling and his heart fluttered not with love for some two-bit hussy, but of the memory of all those thousands of fans finally chanting for him, not against him. He was PRIME's Elite Champion, the people's Champion. Just over a week had passed since Colossus and he was still ecstatic. Just over a week before Colossus however, was a much different story... ---- Thursday, 13th August Glancing up at the sky, Von Kelsig shielded his eyes as swelling clouds rolled over the late morning sun like cloth soaking up condensation on a window. When they passed the sun seemed to shine brighter and warmer as afternoon approached. The time on his cell phone read a quarter to twelve. Von Kelsig had landed at Logan International nearly twenty minutes ago and now he was loitering on Lansdowne street outside Bill's Bar waiting on the arrival of one Michael Sloan. He waited impatiently for another ten minutes. Kicking dust over the bar's closed shutter he glanced up and down the street for any signs of his fellow PRIMEate and frowned when the few cars that appeared just drove on by. Grumbling under his breath, Von Kelsig started a text message asking Sloan where he was. His flight had been delayed and it was Sloan who suggested meeting at this particular bar. Suddenly the sound of keys jangling broke his concentration and he glanced at the side door of the bar as it scraped open over the concrete step. "What the fuck are you doing out here?" A voice asked from within. "That you Sloan?" He replied, inching closer to the door and cocking his head. From the shadows beyond the entrance Michael Sloan emerged, ducking under the doorway and emerging onto the street, nodding in response. Von Kelsig shuffled on his feet, his sulking demeanour all too clear. Sloan eyed the giant and clucked his tongue against his teeth, almost pitying the giant in his current state. Almost. He wasn't sure of just why Von Kelsig wanted to meet up but the way the big man's shoulders slouched and his tired eyes cursed the sun suggested he just wanted to forget why himself. "What’s happening?" asked Michael. "What's happening is I'm thirsty. How'd you get in if the bar's closed?" Von Kelsig queried. Sloan flashed a wry grin, lurking around the side door like Grendel. Motioning for Von Kelsig to follow him they entered the bar, Sloan shutting and locking the door behind him. "I've been...hanging around this neighbourhood," Sloan said with a sinister twinkle in his eye, "and let’s just say that the owner just couldn’t refuse someone like me when I asked for special privileges." Von Kelsig uttered a grunt of approval as they made their way into the lounge where a slim brunette was busy cleaning tables. A pine fresh scent filled the air, a welcome change to the smell of stale alcohol that he had become accustomed to over the past few days. Recently he seemed to be turning to the bottle to numb the painful memories and as a consequence had to endure the dissatisfying atmosphere surrounding a session on the firewater. Taking a seat at the bar Sloan pulled a stool out next to him and gestured Von Kelsig to take a seat before turning to the girl cleaning the tables. "Hey Keeley, we could do with some service over here and a beer or two would be nice" He ordered chauvinistically. The girl glanced up at them and sighed, finishing off one table and sauntering over to the bar. Von Kelsig studied her eyes as she leaned on the bar before them, while Michael focused on her breasts pressing against her Red Sox t-shirt. "What'll it be gents?" She groaned. Sloan hadn't been around long but she was already accustomed to and offended by his manner. "Beer me." Sloan grinned, while Von Kelsig shuffled on his seat and grumbled. "And for the lady?" Keeley asked, cocking an eyebrow. "Whiskey sour." Von Kelsig replied, ignoring the lady comment as he pulled another stool next to his and positioned himself comfortably on both of them. "Coming right up." Keeley grabbed a Bud from the fridge and slammed it onto a beer mat in front of Sloan then went about pouring the whiskey for the bearded man. Ripping the cap off with his teeth Sloan spat it at Keeley's feet, relishing her frustration with disdain. "So..." He turned to Von Kelsig and took a gulp of beer, "what’s eating at you?" Keeley dropped a couple of ice cubes into the glass and placed it before Von Kelsig and cleaned up around the bar as he swirled the drink around. "Eh...I've had a rough couple days." He paused to sip the whiskey, and noticed the taste, "Hey this isn't a Whiskey sour..." He motioned to Keeley. "It's a Boston sour, big guy. Figured you'd want your protein being a wrestler and all." She smirked and returned to her duties while Von Kelsig shrugged and carried on. "Yeah," He continued, "so I got some personal shit on my mind and I don't want it to interfere with this title match at the pay-per-view. That's where you come in." He nodded at Sloan and raised his glass. "What, you wanna shoot the shit or something?" "Something like that. I don't know about you but I love this job, but it seems there's never anyone around to talk about it with. Got no time for it at home and I usually travel alone so-..." "So why don’t you bring your girl along. When shit gets annoying Tracy listens or performs other services I may need at the time. I’m sure you’ve got a lovely young thing that loves you and wants to be with you," Sloan said, cutting him off. Von Kelsig sighed and downed his drink, sucking the residue from his lips and clearing his throat. Sloan watched him like a shark, happy to let the giant vent and play along with the drama. "Like I said, there's never anyone around. I just want to kick back and shoot the shit, like you said." Von Kelsig said before motioning for another drink from Keeley. "Make it a double, would ya?" He added, content to order the girl around as Sloan had done. Reluctantly she poured another, furrowing her brow as she served it to the giant. "You paying for these?" She said, realizing no cash was on show for the drinks. "You got a mouth on you girl, maybe I should put it to good use? If not, shut it, and give the man what he asked for." Sloan replied, his expression hardening as Keeley squinted at him. Von Kelsig clasped his hands and stared at the quartz of the bar top while Sloan eyeballed the girl. Eventually she placed another glass in front of him and washed out the first one, before instinct told her to fetch Sloan another beer as he slurped down the contents of his bottle. "Anyway...Rhine and Noble. How long is this match going to take out of my night?" He asked, grinning while his eyes flashed almost demonically. "Those two?" Von Kelsig replied with a half smile, glad that Sloan had initiated conversation, "Rhine's worthless man, he can't even earn appearance money never mind a cheque for actually wrestling a night. If I wasn't sky-rocketing to the top I'd be giving him a piece of my mind for pissing all over that belt. Guy needs to learn some respect. Noble's on his way up I reckon, seems like he's got a few problems to deal with but I think he's got potential...judging on how he handles a beating anyway. You'll coast through Colossus man, Chainz will look good with Intense gold around his waist." Sloan pursed his lips and glanced away at the thought. "Yeah, it's been pretty tough trying to work a program with a non-entity and a faggot..." "How do you think I feel?!" Von Kelsig blurted, "My guy's off his face all the time and the other two are too busy organizing company pep rallies to confront me! This is the Dual Halo winner we're talking about here! He's a bum!" He chuckled and took a drink, his throat warming as the liquid sloshed down. Sloan managed a smile too, chugging down his beer before grunting and pointing a finger at the giant as he swallowed. "Mhm, talking of sky-rocketing Nelson may be someone worthy of our attention." "He is that." Von Kelsig replied. "I mean don't get me wrong, there isn't a hole on Lisa Tyler I wouldn't bust a nut in but Nelson is one ruthless son of a bitch. I like his style and I know he likes ours..." The notion hung in the air for a moment before Von Kelsig lowered his glass from his lips and sniggered. "The fuck is up with all the Tyler's in PRIME?" Sloan stared at Von Kelsig for a moment, undecided on what to make of the apparent joke. When Von Kelsig's bemused expression stuck, he snorted and chuckled along with the giant. Without glancing up at him, Von Kelsig could tell Sloan's mind was at work. He didn't expect Sloan to just be drinking buddy material but he was thankful to have this reprieve away from Gloria and all her crap. Just forget about it, he thought. Just relax. It'll work itself out. It always does...just relax... ---- Some hours later... "An wherr eh fuck duzz sh geh hoff gowin behind mah back? Bish. I give hurr evurfin in sh fucksh hoff wi some faggit hairdrezzer??" Sloan dipped his fingers in his glass and flicked the drops back into the icy liquid. Von Kelsig groggily turned to his colleague and furrowed his brow then lethargically slapped him on the shoulder. "Wuss eh mattur wi you then?" He slurred, taking a shot glas full of some pungent colourful alcohol and downing it in an instant. When Sloan gazed up at him Von Kelsig froze, then opened his eyes wide and stuck his tongue out. "Waaaaahhhh," He groaned, "nuffin (hiccup) left." Between drunken ramblings, Von Kelsig had through the day had a lengthy chat with Michael Sloan on their thoughts of PRIME as well as other companies and the industry as a whole. Somewhere along the line around the same time he started on shots, Von Kelsig began slurring about home and what Gloria had done. Sloan listened intently, and every time he ordered a drink Von Kelsig noticed he didn't seem to be getting any more drunk. Careless of how inebriated he was or wasn't the giant poured his heart out while the alcohol took effect. "Cow duzznt desurve me anyhow. Sh duzznt deserve..." He paused, a tear welling in his eye, "To fuckn breev. Dijoo know sh wuz some druggeez hoor bifore ah tukk her away fr'm it all n' give hurr a propr life?" Sloan perked up. Taking a sip of his quote unquote gin and tonic he faced Von Kelsig and swayed a little on his bar stool. "So… she was a whore?" He asked. "Fuck aye! Sh yoosed ta look like fuckn Bride of Chucky b'fore she cleend up!" He gasped, his cheeks rosy and his forehead beaded with sweat. The temperature in the bar was cool, though there were a lot of empty glasses in front of Von Kelsig now. Keeley sat at one of the tables twirling her hair and reading a magazine, ignoring the two pro. "Damn, and still you loved her though?" Sloan said, prodding around Von Kelsig's vulnerable state. Saying nothing, Von Kelsig reached into his jacket pocket and produced the thousand-dollar diamond engagement ring he had intended on giving Gloria before his world collapsed around him. "Yeah, I can see you did..." Sloan trailed off, taking another drink. Suddenly Von Kelsig's head hit the quartz bar top and he began sobbing into his folded arms. "Iss not fair...ah nevr did nuthn buh luv hurr...nuthn..." After all he had heard from Von Kelsig on the subject of his failed relationship the word 'nothing' told him much more than he believed even the big man knew. Continually sipping his drink he watched as Von Kelsig reeled on his seat and finished his double vodka before nestling his head in his arms and quietly sobbing. "Fucken bitch. I hope sh gess wuss comin ta hurr. Bitch." "I’m sure she will. Things like this have a way of working themselves out." Sloan patted Von Kelsig on the back, a smile spreading across his face. Slowly the smile twisted into a sadistic grin. As quickly as it grew it disappeared when Von Kelsig reared his head and locked eyes with Michael. "Cheersh mayn. Yer alrite." He grinned. "Hey," Sloan returned the grin and winked, "what are friends for?" ---- Meanwhile, back in Memphis... "Baby you can't let him get away with that! Call the fuckin' cops on him!" Larry yelled emphatically. Pacing back and forth in his lounge he cracked his knuckles, sighing and trying to comprehend the situation. She lay on his sofa cuddling a cushion and staring vacantly into space. Running a hand through his streaked blonde hair Larry dropped to his haunches in front of her and carefully caressed her cheek just below the large cut which was still bandaged. "Look what he did to you." Larry said softly, "He's a goddamn coward. You need to send his ass to jail. You don't deserve this Gloria." She held her breath. Larry had a point. Von Kelsig had stepped over the line when he did what he did to her. She didn't plan on it ending the way it did, and her withdrawn state stemmed from being caught in the act before she had a chance to let Von Kelsig down gently and leave for a better life with Larry DeLancy. Von Kelsig was a good man, didn't understand his priorities but he was a good man nonetheless. She was the one who went behind his back and it left her feeling guilty and it was this that stopped her from going straight to the police. But the more Larry drummed the severity of the beating into her the more her remorse dissipated, leaving only a burning vengeance in her heart. "You're right..." She stammered eventually, "I'm going to call the police." "Good." "I'm going to call the police...and a lawyer..." "Alright." Larry grinned. "I want his ass in jail...and I want every cent he's got..." She hissed, gripping the cushion tight between her chewed nails. Larry tried to stifle the smirk on his face, and kissed Gloria's forehead softly, looking her in the eye. "It'll be OK, sweetheart. Everything'll be alright. Trust me."
