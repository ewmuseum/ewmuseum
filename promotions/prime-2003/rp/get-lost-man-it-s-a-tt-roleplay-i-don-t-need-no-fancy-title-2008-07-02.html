Get lost man, it's a TT roleplay! I don't need no fancy title!

2 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2nd July (FADE IN: A PRIME “Shooting Stars & Armbars Red, White Black & Blue Independence Day Spectacular” branded wrestling ring in the middle of a jarringly quiet Mid-South Coliseum. JASON NATAS, the Intense Title challenger, provides the only sign off life in the otherwise dead arena. Clothed from top-to-bottom in his ring attire – a black and white patterned shirt opened up to reveal a torso so coarse and craggy it may as well have been chiselled from granite, some fingerless leather gloves and a humble pair of blue jeans – NATAS stands in the centre of the ring, his face a portrait of fortitude.) NATAS: Alright, Troy. I give up. I tried, I really did, to drop down to your level and have a good ol’ fashioned verbal boxing match, but your jabs were so weak I switched the channel and watched me some Leno instead. Don’t get me wrong now; it’s like I said earlier, this shot does mean more to me than I might’ve let on. And shit, maybe this match is kinda personal. After all, you’re standin’ in the way of a big ol’ paycheck, Troy, an’ that ain’t somethin’ I take too lightly… (JASON pauses for a moment. He backs into a corner, leisurely resting against the top turnbuckle.) NATAS: Just so we don't have a misunderstandin’ ‘bout shit, lemme tell you somethin’ Troy. I ain't "Special"; I'm not stuck with the same delusions of grandeur that so many of the rest of the pawns in PRIME got. I ain’t flashy; I'm not spoutin’ out catchphrases, blastin’ pyrotechnics and helping contribute to the pantomime trash that plagues this industry. I let my one finger salute and his 4 knuckled buddies do the talkin' for me, and it gets the job done, which is more than you can say for all that flashy bullshit. (The hand gestures of the ANTI-SUPERSTAR mirror his words.) NATAS: But you, Troy… you’re “special”. You’re quickly becomin’ one of the more popular dudes around, an’ I’m sure ol’ Cantrell has big plans for ya eventually. Take a look around this place; I’m sure ya know the surroundings like the back of yer hand. You’ve been in the business for a long-ass time; I know that… hell, I bet you’ve probably even “graced” this very buildin’ with your presence on more than one occasion. I'm still stumped though Troy, it's taken you this long to get to the top? You're, what, 32? Most of the "big names" in this business are looking to kick back and take a load off on that fat wad of cash they racked up by the time they get that old, and you're just now getting there? Shit, I might not be the most refined guy on the roster, but here I am, seven years your junior, standin’ toe-to-toe with you in the biggest wrestlin’ company in North America. I’ll let you do the math on that one. Experience doesn’t mean a thing though, come ReVolution it’ll all be irrelevant. In my NINTH match as a professional wrestler I’m gonna walk all over ya, boy. No matter how long you’ve been wastin’ your career for, I will break your resolve, take yer title, and get a nice raise outta the deal. (Pushing himself away from the turnbuckles gently, JASON slowly makes his way back to the centre of the ring. His nostrils begin to flare as his brow furrows and the intensity of his address increases.) NATAS: So one minute ya say you’ve no real interest in facin’ me, and the next you’re callin’ me “Public Enemy Number One”? C’mon man, that’s fuckin’ weak… make yer fuckin’ mind up already. You wanna jibber-jabber about Kaiser Vashaun, fuck off and do it on someone else’s time, ‘cause at the moment yer blowin’ more hot air than Hilary’s concession speech. That's when I had to cut the shit, man; I'm your damn problem, and you better get that through your head right quick, 'cause I won't hesitant to set ya straight on it. If I were a lesser man, I’d be accusin’ ya of not maintainin’ yer focus here, Douglas. I know you and I have already crossed paths twice before, but this time’s gonna be different, I can guarantee you that. That guy pinnin’ PRIME’s much-loved tag team champions a fortnight ago? Yeah, that was me. I like to call it “foreshadowing,” Troy. I could make a habit of this whole “make the guys with gold squeal like bitches” thing… (NATAS allows a smirk to etch its way across his features.) NATAS: I know hearin’ Vince Howard announcin’ yer name as champion is music to your ears Troy, but me? I've always been a fan of percussion, and the drummin’ I got penciled in for you is bound to make you appreciate a good solo when I'm holdin’ your pride and joy over my head. Want some advice, fucker? Keep that strap warmed up nicely. Might as well crack out the ol’ Turtle Wax and give that sucker a good polish while you’re at it, ‘cause I swear, for every single grubby paw print I find on that damn thing, I’m gonna take a tooth. Bottom line. Revolution 165, Troy Douglas and Jason Natas, Intense Title. Bring all you got, bring your very best, ‘cause one thing is inevitable… Jason Natas is going to ruin you. (FADE OUT.)
