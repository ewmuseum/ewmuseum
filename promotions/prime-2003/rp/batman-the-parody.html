Batman: The Parody

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ~1966 INT. NOBLE STUDY - DAY Johnny Noble and Jethro are sitting on opposite sides of an oak desk in Noble’s brightly coloured den, doing a crossword together. Jethro and Johnny are both wearing beige slacks with button-down collar shirts and brightly coloured sweater-vests. The den has books everywhere. It’s like a small library… or a study! On the left-hand side of the desk is a red phone with a single button in the middle and at the other end a bust of George W. Johnny: Egyptian Sun God? Jethro: How many letters? Johnny: Two. Jethro: Um… Ray? Johnny: Too many letters… R-A… wait, that fits. Jethro: Don’t doubt. Johnny: How about a seven-letter word for “Victory”, the third letter’s an “I”. Jethro: Um… *ring ring* Jethro: THE INCREDI-PHONE! Johnny; No time to finish this clue now! The red phone at the end of the desk is lighting up, flashing and buzzing away. Johnny picks it up and holds it to his ear as quasi-suspense music plays in the background. Johnny: Yes, commissioner? I see. Really? Oh no! We’ll be right there! Jethro: What is it? Johnny rushes to the bust of George W. and flips his head open to reveal a secret button! He presses it and a bookshelf slides out of the way, revealing two fireman’s poles. They are labelled, likely for Jethro’s sake, “Johnny” & “Jethro”. Johnny: It’s the commissioner! FIRESTARTER is on the loose again! Quick, old chum, let’s hurry! The Tremendous Tandem are off! Down the poles they go! INT. INCREDI-CAVE - DAY The pair appears at the bottom of the poles, fully dressed in their super-outfits! Johnny sporting a red, white and blue jumpsuit with an American flag as a cape. Jethro is wearing a Lone Ranger dollar store mask and has his underwear over top of his pants. The Tremendous Tandem whisk themselves off to the Incredi-Car (A ’55 Bel-Air convertible) and fire up the engine! Off they go! INT. COMMISSIONER GEORGE’S OFFICE - DAY O’Gravy: Begorrah! George: I know! Mr. Incredible and Lone Streak arrive at the Commissioner’s office. Mr. Incredible: What’s going on? George: Firestarter’s lit the old convent on fire! O’Gravy: Begorrah! Mr. Incredible: Quick, old pal, let’s go now before those nuns get hurt! Streak: Holy… nuns… Mr. Incredible…? The Tremendous Tandem disappear together out the door! EXT. THE OLD CONVENT - DAY The Incredi-Car pulls up and myriads of people cheer for the Masked Marauder and his Kid Krusader. Mr. Incredible reaches into his Incredi-belt and pulls out a vile clearly marked ‘Incredi-Fire Stopper’. He pops off the cork and launches the vile at the building. In a poof of smoke the building is fire-free and the nuns are saved. Then! On the grass! Mr. incredible spots the envelope. Streak: What is it? Some sort of… letter? Mr. Incredible: Yes, it must be a letter from Firestarter, no doubt! It will likely reveal information crucial to his plans cleverly disguised in a riddle of some sort! He picks up the note out and reads it aloud. Mr. Incredible: I am at the old gunpowder building at pier 43. Firestarter. He lowers the note and look pensively at the sky. Mr. Incredible: What do you think it could mean, old bean? Streak: Could it be th-- Mr. Incredible: What’s the clue? The first letter from each word? Iaatogbap!? Iaatogbap 4? What does that mean!? Streak: Or it could be as easy as-- Mr. Incredible: Maybe the words are jumbled! I am building the pier-- no! At 43 I am old-- no! Building the old gunpowder pier-- dammit, no! Streak: Should we check out the old gunpowder buil-- Mr. Incredible: I’ve got it! Maybe he’s trying to throw us off by telling us exactly the right information! How massively diabolical! Streak: Yeah. Should we go? Mr. Incredible: Just charge in with no chance of survival? We’ll need to get a lay of the land first. C’mon, old billox! Mr. Incredible starts towards the car again and fires up the engine. Streak: Billox? Lone Streak runs up and hops in the car as well. EXT. OLD GUNPOWDER BUILDING - DAY Mr. Incredible and the Lone Streak are standing behind some dumpsters looking at the building. Mr. Incredible sums up the scenario in a handy monologue. Mr. Incredible: So, there’s the building. Looks like about 15 stories. We’ll have to our Incredi-rangs to climb it’s outer wall, so as to be inconspicuous! Once inside we’ll likely encounter several unarmed, nameless, faceless, lackies who we will beat senseless before we find the Firestarter and bring him to justice, ready, old cohort? Streak: Yeah. Let’s do it! EXT. OLD GUNPOWDER BUILDING - WALL - DAY The Tremendous Tandem are rope-climbing the wall when none other than Adam West and Burt Ward pop their heads out of a nearby window. West: Hey guys. What are you doing? Mr. Incredible: Chasing down some bad guys… you wouldn’t be able to relate. Ward: I don’t know about that. West: Yeah, heh heh heh… anyway, d’you hungry, hungry heroes want a soda? Mr. Incredible: Do you have any Apple Juice or maybe some milk? West: Sorry, no. Mr. Incredible: We’ll have to pass, then, gotta keep fit. West: Yes… I suppose… you… do. Well, then, keep up the good… work, fellas. Streak: We shall! Thanks, guys. Adam West and Burt Ward close the window and head back inside as Mr. Incredible and Lone Streak keep on scaling the wall. INT. OLD GUNPOWDER BUILDING - 15TH FLOOR - DAY Mr. Incredible & Lone Streak climb in through the window and look around. Nothing. Mr. Incredible: This seems a little too easy, old ally! Keep your eyes peeled! They check down the corridor, and then Streak spots something of use. Streak: Look! Streak points to a sign that Mr. Incredible checks out as well. Mr. Incredible: Hmmm. D’you think he’s just being obvious again? There’s a sign on a door that reads. “I’m in here.” Mr. Incredible: Oh, whatever… let’s just open it and check it ouuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu-- The Tremendous Tandem fall, quite ungracefully, two floors into a giant net where a gas fills the area, rendering them unconscious! INT. OLD GUNPOWDER BUILDING - LATER Mr. Incredible and the Lone streak find themselves bound to a giant matchstick. They are tied to the end that is held by a fantastically over-sized hand, at the farthest point from the huge sulphur tip. They spot Firestarter in the control tower on the catwalk opposite them. He smiles. Mr. Incredible: Darn, you, Firestarter! You’ve foiled us! Firestarter: Well, I’m surprised you managed to decipher my clues! I thought they were quite disabolical. Mr. Incredible: Oh, they were. Firestarter: Really? Mr. Incredible: Oh yeah… Firestarter: Goooooooood. Now. Mr. Incredible: You’ll never get away with this! Firestarter: My evil plan is-- Mr. Incredible: They catch you! Firestarter: I’m planning to take all-- Mr. Incredible: One day your evil regime of power will be brought down and then you’ll see that good always win the-- Firestarter: Look! Before I can set this ghastly over-complicated contraption in motion and leave you for dead I have to tell you my entire plan for global domination. So how about you shut up, hey? Evil glares are exchanged. Firestarter: I’m going to take all the lighters and matches in the world and destroy them! Then everyone will need to come to me for fire and for heat and I, FIRESTARTER, will rule the WORLD!!! Bwahaahahahaahhahahaha! Streak: What about flint sparkers? Firestarter: Right, I’ll take those too! Now, this contraption. I’ve set a mouse loose in a cheese maze. When he finds it he’ll eat it, setting off a mousetrap. Then the sudden vibration will jar that marble which will roll off the ledge and travel through a complex series of pipes. Upon reaching the end of that tunnel the marble will land on a switch which will spark an electric current that will light this small match on fire, which will light the match beside it, which will light the match beside IT, which wil… look, anyway it does that for a few dozen matches THEN! The last lit match will set this piece of VERY, VERY dry kindling on fire! It will burn until it lights this rope beside me on fire. And when that burns through it will release the hand that is holding the match you are tied to. It will swing down across that pit of sandpaper and lit the tip on fire and it will burn it’s way up to you, and you’ll be burned alive!! Ahhahaahhahahahahhahah! Now, I’m off to steal all the matches, lighters and flint sparkers before I go to my hideout at 1254 Hudson Street to revel in my amazing feat of villany! Good bye Mr. Incredible!! Streak: What are we gonna do, Johnny? Mr. Incredible: I don’t know, old affiliate. Announcer(V.O.): Will the Tremendous Tandem make it out alive? Can Firestarter get away with his ultra-evil-villainous plan!? Will the campiness of this RP ever stop!? Stay tuned! Same Incredi-Time, same Incredi-RP! INT. OLD GUNPOWDER BUILDING - LATER - DAY The mouse has found the cheese, the ball has rolled and the electric current has sparked. Now the matches are lighting in sequence! Mr. Incredible: Can you feel that, old associate? Streak: What? Mr. Incredible: There’s a jagged edge to this matchstick! He must not have had enough time to properly fashion it! A-ha! But we shall take full advantage of his careless errors! Mr. Incredible begins rubbing his ropes against the jagged edge and soon frees his arms. Then he reaches for his utility belt where he pulls out a packet of Incredi-Termites. He releases them and they eat through the matchstick, allowing it to fall to the ground where the Tremendous Tandem can untie themselves. Streak: We should probably head to Firestarter’s hideout at 1254 Hudson Street, yeah? Mr. Incredible: Make it so, old comrade! EXT. FIRESTARTER’S HIDEOUT - NIGHT Mr. Incredible: On my count, old crony! One! Two! Three! They rush in through the front door… sneaky! INT. FIRESTARTER’S HIDEOUT - NIGHT Goon: Hey! It’s Mr. Incredible! Get him! The fight sequence starts. A goon comes after Mr. Incredible with a double axe handle, which is ducked and countered with a crazy punch! Biff! Clank! Another Good comes after Lone Streak who does a double punch to the face! Clash! Touché! Kayo! Ooooff! Two more after Mr. Incredible but he hits a dropkick from one into the next! Boff! Zap! Splatt! Sock Whamm! One can be heard yelling in the background! Eee-yow! Three of them gang up on the streak and hold him down, but Mr. Incredible is there right away and lays in a couple lefts and rights! Zlonk! Bloop! More fighting, with excess kicking of arse! Glurpp! Slosh! These are words actually used in a ‘60’s superhero show! Awk! Splatt! Whamm! Mr. Incredible and the Lone Streak make it through the barrage of men and find Firestarter in his kingdom of matches, lighters and yes, even flint sparkers! Streak: Holy ignition sources, Mr. Incredible~! Mr. Incredible: Indeed. Look, Firestarter, there’s no way you can win! It’s over! We’ve got you surrounded! Surrender now and we won’t have to work very hard! Firestarter: I can see how that would work well for you! But I think not! Firestarter whips out a futuristic weapon. A ray gun! Mr. Incredible: A ray gun!? Firestarter: Yes. Meet Ronald! Mr. Incredible: You named it? Streak: Holy Ronald Ray Gun, Mr. Incredible! Mr. Incredible: Ronald Ray Gun!? The Republicans would never screw America like this! Firestarter: You’ll see, Mr. Incredible!! He fires the gun, but it jams just long enough for Mr. Incredible to rush in and handcuff him with his Incredi-Cuffs! EXT. FIRESTARTER’S HIDEOUT - DAY The commissioner and the police chief are there as Firestarter and his men are escorted into the paddy wagon. O’Gravy: Begorrah! George: How can we ever thank you for this, Mr. Incredible? Mr. Incredible: Just keep America free, Mr. Commissioner! George: But wha-- where’d he go? Mr. Incredible and the Lone Streak had vanished into thin air! O’Gravy: Begorrah! George: Say, O’Gravy, d’you wanna go get some potatoes? O’Gravy: Whatever. Announcer(V.O.): The point? Anti-American arsonists cannot win against the people and freedoms of America! Billy Joel said it, “We didn’t start the fire” but we’re damned-well going to put it out. This Wednesday, come see the fire extinguisher in action. Welcome to the big-leagues, Brian Arse-On.
