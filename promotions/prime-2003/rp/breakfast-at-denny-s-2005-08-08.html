Breakfast at Denny's

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/8/05 'More coffee sir?' asked the waitress. A pretty young thing, probably just out of high school working at Denny's to pass the time until college starts in the fall. A nametag on her blouse proclaimed her name as Colleen, and the bright cheeriness of her smile proclaimed that her shift hadn't been on for very long. She didn't quite have that 1000 yard stare servers of both sexes get when they've gotten sick of fetching uncaring customers endless refills in hopes for a couple of lousy bucks left in the messy debris they invariably leave on the table. She's answered by a noncommittal grunt that apparently she's takean as an affirmative as with a toss of her head, she swings her long ponytail away from where it was resting on her shoulder and leaving it to sway down her back as she bends over with a pot, refilling her customer's cup. 'Is there anything else, sir? Are you done with that plate?' Answered again by a grunt, something in the timbre or inflection has informed Colleen that in fact there wasn't any thing else and indeed, he was not finished with his plate. With a beaming smile her parents had spent several thousands of dollars in orthodonist bills to pay for, she left the untalkative figure hunched in his booth and proceeded to the next table to repeat the coffee refilling ritual. Now we could follow the bright eyed waitress throughout her day. If we were to stick around with Colleen today, we'd see a wonderful young woman who would come to terms with her boyfriend's decision to leave her for a man in a spectalularly violent act of defenestration. One that would lead to a prison sentence of two years that would be suspended because Colleen reminded an old sentimental judge of his favorite granddaughter who had perished the year before in a tragic drunk driving accident. This would be a story of high drama, action, tragedy, and redemption. But no, we're not here to read about Colleen, are we? No, let's focus in on the large figure in the bright orange plastic booth, picking away disinterestedly at his Moons Over MyHammy. With a sigh of discontent, he reached over and picked up the last two sugar packets out of the little square sugar tray, shaking thim in his orange gloved hand before tearing them open, pouring their granulated sugary contents into his coffee. Tearing the foil lid off a single serving container of non dairy creamer, he stirs in the contents, completing the metamorphis from shitty Denny's coffee to something almost palatable. Taking a small sip of the scalding hot concoction, he nods at the heavy bitterness being partially masked by the sugar and cream before replacing the cup on the table before him. He sighs heavily and rubs at the bridge of his nose, trying to massage away the pounding headache. And trying to drive away thoughts of the flickering image seated in the opposite seat. 'C'mon Trash, you know I'm not going away anytime soon. Why do you keep trying to drive me away?' The Ghost of Ed leans forward, resting his forearms on the table as he stares intently into the eyes of the Trashcan Man who's trying his damnedest to ignore the spectre of his best friend. 'We've gotta talk about what you did to Mr Smiley Face.' For those of you who just joined us... first of all, shame on you. The last RP was a good one, well worth reading. If you'd have read it, you'd know what happened to Mr Smiley Face at the hands of the Trashcan Man. So go on. Read it. Do it. We'll wait...............By the way, for those that don't know... the definition of defenestration is an act of throwing someone or something out of a window. Now you know something new...............Ok, we're going to assume you've gone back and read it by now. If not... well, it's your problem, not mine. Back to the story. 'No, no we don't Ed. I've got bigger things to worry about now. I've got a match against...' Ed shakes his head, interrupting the Trashcan Man quiet explanation. 'Bigger things? You just killed part of yourself, Trash. You performed the first case of psychological suicide, man! I know you're crazy, but.... ' The ghost is interrupted in turn by the Trashcan Man, voice still a whisper, but showing more emotion. 'Don't call me crazy, Ed. Smiley was a tool of Kriegman's, it just makes too much sense. He sensed I was finally getting some success on my own merits in PRIME without anything to do with Alan Kriegman, and he took over and sabotaged it. He lost me my title!' 'Trash, my friend, he didn't take over, he was forced to take over. He was just trying to protect you like he always has. When Lord Crom Griffin Dagoth went after Kremmy, he took over. When you were afraid that Alan Kriegman was going to be able to take you back over and go after Tempest, he...' SLAM! Silence fills the restaraunt as the Trashcan Man brings his fist down hard onto the tabletop, sending the dishes jittering and almost knocking over the coffee cup. Glancing around in sudden embarassment, he notices that he's the center of attention. Lowering his head, he mutters something resembling an apology to the restaraunt before fiddling with his ham. Conversations start to strike back up around him as the restaraunt fills back up with it's customary noise. The Trashcan Man glares over at Ed and whispers again. 'He did not help me, he let Kriegman walk all over him. Just like he let that punk Vangelus Olsig hang me with that damn noose. He let me hang, Ed. I don't know who would call that help, but I sure don't.' The ghost of the dead hardcore legend shrugs his shoulders in defeat. 'Ok Trash, you win for now. I won't getcha thrown out of Denny for throwing one of yer tantrums. So what are you going to do this week?' The Trashcan Man ponders for a bit, idly drawing his old flame design in the ring left behind by his plastic water glass. Beads of water glisten in the flourescant light before he wipes it way with the palm of his hand. 'Well, I've got a falls count anywhere no DQ match this week. My favorite beating toy in PRIME... 'Besides Token Weed' both men say at the same time, the same small smile on their faces. 'Actually, First Blood. That Daniel Pierce, for a guy who calls himself 'Our Hero', he gets the crap beat out of him a lot.' 'This from the guy with the 4-9-1 record.' 'Yeah, well, this guy hasn't beaten me yet. Although now I think about it, I haven't gotten a clean win over him either. Both times we were in the ring together, we both loss to a third person. HIT and Olsig. Both men I know I could take one on one. Not this time. I'm taking out Pierce as early as I can, make him a non factor in the match instead of the slight distraction that he normally is. And kicking his ass will send a message to that little tramp Eve. She tried to set this whole thing up for Pierce to take the belt from me with a surprise victory. I'm glad THAT blew up in her face like Steven Smith sucking off the entire Ream Team. Even if I didn't win it, her boy toy didn't either.' 'You sure you're not overlooking him? He's a tough guy Trash, and he could come out swinging.' 'Nah, he goes by First Blood for fuck's sake. Only two men have ever been able to use the word Blood in their names and NOT be giant pussies, and that was the Blood Brothers.' 'What about Brandon Youngblood?' 'Whiney overpushed and overestimated Chris Storm wanna be. Nah, Pierce won't be a problem at all. As for Ken Kaze...' Trash reaches down and puts a manila folder on the table. He slides it open, showing some pics of Ken Kaze in various high flying situations. 'Did a little research on the Laptop of Hardcore(tm). Apparently this little guy graduated from the P.W.A... god, remember when the Phantom Booker tried to get me to run that?' 'You've already done that bit, Trash.' 'Yeah, but I'm REALLY glad I was able to dodge that responsibility. No way I'd be a good teacher. First sign of backtalk and I'd've set someone on fire.' 'Anyway, back to the subject at hand?' 'Why in such a rush?' queries a confused Trashcan Man. The ghost looks around carefully, making sure no one can hear him. Even though, you know, he can't be seen by anyone BUT the Trashcan Man. But it's the thought that counts. Ed motions Trash in closer to him. Trash leans forward and Ed whispers in his ear. Trashy's eyes widen as he checks his watch. 'Holy shit... deadline's almost here... and Walczak put up a pretty decent one...' 'SHHHHHHH! Ixnay on aeakingbray ayfabekay!' cautions the spook. Trashy nods and continues on. 'So, Kaze was also in PCW for a bit, as well as a few other feds. Apparently he's... Hardcore, Innovative, Submissionist, Suicidal... H... I... S... S...' He trails off in confusion. 'What, he's in COBRA?' asks the dumbfounded Ed. 'Or he's got a really bad lisp. He calls himself the Hero of Hardcore...' 'I didn't know Hardcore had a hero. Well, I guess now I know!' 'And knowing is half the battle!' Both men raise their fists and proclaim 'G.I. JOE!' Once again the restaraunt gets real quiet as the big 6'3' long haired brute in the booth by himself becomes the center of attention. He quiets down and drinks his coffee, waiting for the restaraunt to ignore him again. Once again the clamor slowly rises to it's normal din as the Trashcan Man continues his conversation with his dead friend. 'So, what about Walczak?' asks Ed. 'What, the Ivan clone that sucked so bad that Ivan kicked him out of the Red Army?' 'Yeah, the guy that lawn darted you out of the battle royale. Oh, and did something you never did, won the OSW World title.' 'Bah, that belt was a sham, I didn't want it anyways.' 'Yeah, that's why you got so pissed when Ivan and Alexei stopped you from beating Boda for it.' 'No, I got pissed because they stopped me from beating Boda. Boda for God's sake. I've got a loss against Boda on my record now. That's the kind of shame not even Lava soap can wash away.' 'Oh, heylo, who's this?' Ed looks behind the Trashcan Man, a glimmer in his... well, his glimmering eyes. They glimmer already because he's a flickering ghost, but there's another glimmer in there too. A glimmer of interest. Like there's a glimmer of hope you guys are entertained by these little asides I've started to put in my RP's. 'She's cute... and I think she's staring at you Trash. Check her out...' 'What? What are you talking about... ' Trashy turns to see a young lady, maybe in her mid 20's, straight chestnut brown hair flowing over her shoulders as she suddenly looks away, knowing that somehow the man she's been watching for the past twenty minutes has realized she's been gazing at him. 'Great, another person who thinks I'm a freak... ' His voice trails away as she turns back towards him, her hazel eyes meeting his dark brown ones. One corner of her mouth lifts into a small half smile as she waves shyly at Trash. The hardcore legend, flustered beyond believe, waves back for a moment before turning back and staring straight ahead, face starting to turn a bright red to the sheer amusement of the ghost sitting across from him. 'She's cute, Trashy. Really cute...' 'But why's she looking at me? I didn't fart or anything...' mumbles the Trashcan Man as he lowers his head letting his shaggy brown hair fall over his face. 'Trash, you gotta realize, you're not this hideous freak you think you are. Sure you've got some outlandish scars, but they're not that bad. Your broken nose really kinda adds character to your face... and you do have nice eyes.' 'What, when did this turn into Queer Eye for the Crazy Guy?' 'Oh, about when she stood up and started walking over here. Good luck, big guy!' Ed fades away as the Trashcan Man sputters a protest. He feels a presence next to him and looks up through his mane of hair at the young lady who's smiling nervously down at him. 'Excuse me sir... I've got this weird feeling I know you from somewhere...' Thank god, her boyfriend's a wrestling fan. I can just sign an autograph and she'll leave me alone 'Aren't you... Michael Kriegman?' Trashy's jaw drops open in shock as she uses his legal name. 'How do you...' 'Oh, I'm sorry, my name is Suzanne. I work at the Museum of Contemporary Art in Chicago. I recognized you from a couple of your metal sculpture shows you did there last year.' 'You do?' 'Oh yeah, I loved your work... especially the Three Faces Of Me. The faces imprinted in the car hoods, one with a maniacal grin, one with that cruel scowl, and the third looking so very sad... it was amazing.' 'You... liked that one?' sputtered the Trashcan Man. 'Oh yes, definately.' She glances down at her watch and looks disappointed. 'Oh shoot, I've got to run, there's a deadline I've got to meet at the museum...' Like how I threw that deadline in there again? Pretty funny stuff... 'Well... it was nice to meet you Suzanne...' 'Listen... I'm going to give you my card, call me sometime, ok?' The Trashcan Man looks at her amazed as she fishes out a business card and places it in his orange gloved hand. 'My cell number's on there, I'm usually free after six most nights. Please, give me a call, we can go grab a coffee or some dinner or something, I'd love to talk about your work with you. Oh, I've got to get going. Bye!!!' With that flurry of conversation, Suzanne departs, leaving the Trashcan Man speechless. Staring down at the card in his hand, he pokes it with a finger to make sure it's real. His match this week forgotten, he now prepares to embark on the greatest decision of his life... Does he call the girl tonight or wait a few days? Booya, one minute before deadline, baby!
