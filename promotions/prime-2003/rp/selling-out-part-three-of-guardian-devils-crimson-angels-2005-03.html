Selling Out : Part Three - 'Of Guardian Devils & Crimson Angels.'

March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March, 2005 Selling Out : Part Three - 'Of Guardian Devils & Crimson Angels.' As Karina Wolfenden left the PRIME offices, every step was heavy and laboured, while the sigh she expelled almost left her lungs hanging from her mouth, such was the relief. There was a good reason why she'd never done PRIME promotional appearances, publicity gigs, or merchandise stuff... and today was it. Now, at every show she'd wrestle, the K-Wolf would have to look out into the fans and see that money had been wasted on her cheap, nasty merchandise. Pride was not a feeling that Kari would experience for a long while. But, at least she was now feel to focus on Seven of the Best, while the rest of the wrestling world got to see her jabbering on like a moron about how she was going to win it. Repeat the above pride comment. 'Well, well, well,' a voice tickled up her spine, playing each rib like a xylophone, 'if I knew you liked it in front of a camera, I'd have asked you back to my place a LONG time ago.' Angelo Deville, the Crimson Angel. Karina knew that she didn't want to hear them, but no matter now much she squirmed, or tried to close access to her ears, the lubricated words slithered into her. 'Lunch?' his silver tongue then enquired, a mixture of jest and menace in his eyes as he strolled up to Karina's side... neither stupid nor arrogant enough to even consider putting a hand on her shoulder. 'Aaaaanything you want.' Dropping her head to the side without breaking stride, the K-Wolf let Deville down in her usual, less-than-subtle way. 'Okay, I'll have the train ticket home, and you can have the crushed glass surprise.' 'Ouch,' he winced as the verbal right hook smashed against his stylised jaw. 'You really need to make this a match tactic. Just ask Oni to be nice to you at Seven of the Best, and two minutes later he'll be on his back wondering what hit him.' 'Oh, I've won it already,' Karina groaned, having had to destroy the friendly competition aspect of the event with her own words. 'Didn't you hear me say so?' Giving her a sly nod back, Deville was almost knocked over by the hefty dose of sarcasm exuded from Karina. 'Yes, but let me give you a little tip when it comes to cutting a promo. At least try to sound like you mean it.' 'You walking into that post would make my day,' Karina then stated back at him, the sparkle returning to her eyes. 'Hmmm, I think I can manage to belief thing, just need the right inspiration.' 'One day you'll say something that'll really hurt me,' Deville sniped back, breaking off towards the car park. 'And then who will you have to abuse?' 'A hurt Angelo Deville,' she offered up as an alternative. 'So, not going to wish me luck?' 'You need it?' The Deville enquired back. 'I don't NEED to abuse you with every breath that escapes me,' Kari shrugged, having needed another jovial joust with Angelo to lift her spirits. 'Doesn't mean I don't appreciate having you as a verbal punch bag.' 'Fine,' Angelo appeased his razor-tongued equal with, 'you go and show those 13 guys just how high you can get your legs, and teach them a whole new meaning to the term stiff pounding.' As Angelo turned to unlock his car, he waited for Karina's retort to stab him right between the shoulder blades... but he was left disappointed. 'Oh, do I hear a rare verbal knock-out for myself?' Angelo asked, turning to watch the departing Karina. 'Hmm, that's a new tactic.' Eyes narrowed as he watched Karina swat her shoulder, as if to dismiss him, before dropping down to her knees. Curious, Deville approached, only for Karina to slump forward, then roll onto her front, arms and legs shaking. 'It wasn't that funny,' Deville shrugged, 'there is such a thing as over-selling.' But instead of a typically barbed comment coming back from Karina, all Deville could hear was a loud wheezing, causing him to explore closer. 'What the...' he exhaled, seeing that something wasn't right. Her usually pale face swelled with a dangerous red, disguising the subtle freckling that ran over the bridge of her nose. As if every pore was being squeezed, cold perspiration added a sheen to the alarming crimson, while Karina's eyes darted all around, panic obvious to see. She was scared... and not from the kind of fear that she overcame on an almost daily basis. This was serious. 'Kari,' Deville's concerned words again probed, still sounding as if he was holding a little back, half expecting her to smile and shoot a playful wink his way. 'If this is a joke, fucking quit it, okay?' Her pleading, emerald eyes looked right through Deville's iced blue irises, staring her words right into his brain while just managing to point to her left shoulder. Her lips couldn't move apart to speak, but alarm bells sounded inside of Deville, jarring his soul and letting him know that she wasn't playing around. 'Your shoulder,' he picked up on, scurrying around the downed K-Wolf to where her twitching finger rested next to a Bee's stinger, embedded in her mottled and swollen skin. 'It's just a sti-... oh, shit...' Instantly, Deville flicked the stinger from Karina's skin, then looked on in horror at the sickening red and violet marbling that was creeping out from the pin-prick like tentacles. Everyone had heard of people who were extremely allergic to Bee stings, or had channel surfed onto some documentary of 'near miss' fatalities... but as the saying wisely goes, you never think it'll happen to you, or someone you know. Deville went to stand, trying to fish his cell phone from his jacket pocket, only for Karina's hand to clamp itself around his wrist. The woman he knew as fiercely independent, able to go toe-to-toe with anyone, and never back down from anything, was helpless... looking up to him as her savoir, while half wondering if her life was about to fizzle out, lacking any kind of glory. 'You're going to be okay,' his dry words told her, stripped of any Deville persona, and without the merest hint of his usual, arrogant inflection. 'You're Karina Wolfenden.' Fighting the muscles locking down her face, Karina tried to prise open her lips and exhale something in a wheezing whisper, but just as they crept out, her body began to convulse, almost biting the tip of her own tongue off as teeth dropped like a guillotine. 'Kari,' Angelo called out as his ice-cold demeanour bubbled and evaporated away, cell phone pressed so hard against his ear that the skin turned an almost luminous red. 'Yes, ambulance.' Every beat of her stuttering heart felt like a hammer blow to the head, the pressure threatening to explode out through the crown of her skull. Another convulsion ravage her body, causing her to gag as vomit crept up into her mouth. She couldn't isolate any of the words Deville was yelling frantically down the phone, getting louder as he threw himself down beside her, turning the despairing K-Wolf onto her side. A mixture of mucus and vomit hung from Karina's bottom lip as she curled up into the foetal position, stomach cramps threatening to turn her inside out. 'They're coming,' Angelo tried to calm her with, pulling the hair from her face, leaving his hand sodden with perspiration. 'Just... just try to breathe, you'll b-' Deville was then sent tumbling backwards as Karina's curled up body exploded, nerves randomly triggering muscles, making it look like her heart was being shocked. A whimper escaped from Karina's lips as she closed her surrendering eyes, fearing the next agonising spasm. 'Hey!' Deville yelled, cupping the K-Wolf's head in his arms and slapping her across the puffed-up cheek, 'keep your eyes open, okay? No sleeping... you're not drifting off on me, understand?' Despite the burning red pigment to her skin, Karina felt frozen in his arms, filling Deville with the kind of dread that he never knew could permeate through his tough exterior. Caring wasn't something he tended to do a lot of, to the point that he could barely pluck the right words from his vast vocabulary to help keep the shivering Karina at ease. Once again, her body was ravaged by a spasm, forcing a high pitched squeak to force itself from between her tightly pursed lips. Keeping tight hold of her, Deville tried to absorb the reaction into himself, but Karina's tightly toned body almost knocked him backwards once again, such was the ferocity. 'Where the hell are they,' Deville seethed, mumbling softly in case Karina heard. Looking back over his shoulder, hoping that the ambulance was hurtling down the road towards them, despite the lack of any sirens, Deville spotted something on the sidewalk beside his feet. The delicate form of a Bee, devoid of its stinger, lay discarded on the concrete... so light that the subtle breeze threatened to roll it over. Scowling, Deville slammed his heel down into it, then looked back to Karina, who's eyes were almost staring up into her skull. 'The ambulance is coming,' Deville forced into her unreceptive ears, expanding on the truth to try and reduce her panic. 'I can hear the sirens now, you'll be fine. Up and about again by tonight, you'll see. Kari?' Another slap managed to drop her eyes back down from under her eyelids, but under their glazing, Deville could barely tell what she was looking at. 'Listen to me,' Angelo urged, keeping her head cupped as he dragged the matted-down, stray strands back. 'I'm not going to let you die, understand? Fuck allergies, fuck whatever your body is doing to you right now... I am NOT going to have you die in my arms. You're... you... I...' As his piercing blue eyes spoke to Karina through the fever and muscle contractions, he struggled for the words that slipped through all his defences, before the welcome screech of sirens sliced through the refreshing, midday air. 'Hear that? They're here, not long now...' Karina again tried to force words from her dry and cracking lips, but another spasm once again caused her to gag, while her hand sharply gripped Deville's, causing an audible crack to make the Crimson Angel wince. 'Don't let go,' Karina finally managed to gasp through her swollen airway, eyes watering and barely able to focus. 'Don't, please...' As the medics surrounded Karina, her eyes finally gave up the fight and retreated back behind her eyelids, causing her hand's grip to loosen, an act that Deville wasn't about to mimic. She could still hear his voice forcing itself into her ear, not able to make out any words, just a fluctuating blur of noise. Other frequencies were added as the medics began to question him, the occasional exchange just about managing to pierce through. '-u the husband? Boyfr-' 'No, just a fr-' '-ome with he-' '-l follow, and phone her fam-' '-kay, we've got adrenaline inhal-' '-ove yo-' Then, the pounding that ricocheted around her skull ceased, as did her heart's beating.... As if to spite the sign warning against doing so, Deville flipped open Karina's cell phone and hunted through the recently dialled numbers, looking for her brother's. 'Jesus Christ,' he blasphemed, scrolling down the names, 'how can someone as infuriating as you still have this many people to talk to?' Finally, he found the number, and checked to see that nobody was paying any attention to him, before hitting the green phone key. 'Hey, Sis,' Max greeted him with, assuming from the Caller ID that it was Karina asking for a loan, or legal advice, or any of the other numerous requests she tended to plague her contrasting brother with. 'It's not Kari,' Deville revealed, before going on to explain where he was. 'What's happened to her? What's she doing there?' the panicked Max hurled down the phone at Deville. 'And who are you, anyway? She hasn't mentioned a boyf-' 'It doesn't matter who I am,' Deville shrugged the awkward latter question off with. 'If you're on my sister's cell phone telling me she's in hospital, you bet it matters,' Max shot back, his voice wavering up and down as the news slowly sank in. 'Look,' Deville coldly stated as his paces up and down the hall became more potent. 'She's had an allergic reaction to a Bee sting... a bad one. Her heart stopped, her heart stopped while I was holding her, okay? They've given her adrenaline, and have started something called Immuno-Therapy, or something like that, to deal with what's left in her system. She'll be fi-' 'Oh shit,' Max then gasped down the phone, a rare lapse of language for him. 'Yeah, I know,' Deville agreed, 'it was pret-' 'No, you don't understand,' Max cut him off with, 'she isn't insured. Her father, our father, paid for it, and cancelled everything like that when they stopped talking. FUCK! I was nagging her about it the other day, and she just shrugged it off and said she'd sort it when her next pay cheque came in.' 'Well, she hasn't needed surgery,' Deville assured him, 'and they say she'll be out of here tomorrow, and only need a follow-up course of injections, so we're not talking heart surgery here.' 'Still, it's more than I've got to hand,' Max sighed, struggling to prepare a life for his own family, without having to keep financing his sister as well. 'And certainly more than she's got.' As Deville wandered back up the corridor, he passed the window looking into Karina’s room, where the blinds hadn't been fully closed. She looked a shell of the woman he'd began to enjoy the company of, and for more than the odd insult-exchange. He'd never seen her sleep before, and often wondered if she ever did, but it certainly wasn't the way he'd wanted to first take the sight in. Fragile was not a word he'd ever affixed to the K-Wolf, but he'd seen the vulnerable side she'd kept so well hidden, looked into her fearful eyes, and felt his own heart stutter and stall when hers stopped. 'I'll take care of it,' Deville then bluntly informed Max. 'You'll what?' he needed a double-take to absorb. 'But it's... who are you, exactly?' 'If you ever need to, you'll find out,' Deville critically told him, not sure whether he'd be excited or hesitant about being entwined further with the Wolfenden family. 'I'll pass your number onto them, just get here as soon as you can. I know she looks like everything just washes right off her, but I've never seen her like I did today... it might take more than a few shots to make her better.' 'Look, I do-' 'Bye,' Deville then ended the call with, slapping the phone shut as his eyes remained locked on the sleeping Karina. Nobody was passing, and he could have easily gone into the room, taking Karina's hand in his, waiting for her to wake up to his face. A part of Deville wanted to sneak in, fix the bedclothes that had wormed their way down her body, and brush the hair from her face, in case it tickled her nose and woke her. But Angelo managed to resist, keeping the cold barrier of glass between them. He wasn't ready, wasn't comfortable, with being that close to her. 'What have you done to me?' he muttered under his breath, bemused at how what used to be simple spite between them had grown, blossomed, into an attachment that neither could quite fathom... and neither had the personality traits to openly explore. 'Don't make me love you...' Having to force himself to look away, Deville felt his eyeballs nearly being wrenched from their sockets before Karina's visage finally released them. Striding back towards the desk, Deville looked as if he needed to escape, a feeling like claustrophobia taking over him, stifling his breaths. 'Karina Wolfenden's cell phone,' he announced to the desk clerk, his fixed expression not offering up even a hint that he'd just been using it in a banned area. 'Her brother, Max is on the way... and, she isn't insured.' That actually made her look up from the monotonous database filling. 'This is the account you need to charge the bill to,' Deville then told her, matter-of-factly, openly putting his details on the notepad on the desk in front of him. 'Whatever she needs, she gets... and whatever she doesn't need, she gets if she wants it, okay?' There was a sight hint of suspicion in the woman's eyes as Deville hurriedly handed the details over, before retrieving his wallet. 'When her brother arrives, tell him that as far as anyone will know, he paid,' The Deville then commanded, his words having the kind of natural authority that overcame peoples' reservations. 'And this is for carrying out my request to the letter.' With the kind of subtlety that showed a vast experience in doing so, Deville rolled a number of bills in his palm, extending a handshake to the desk clerk that finally brought a look of acknowledgement from the hospital employee. 'She's special,' he added, as the clerk flicked through the bills under the desk, her smile growing wider as each revealed its value. 'Treat her as such.' Loosening his tie as his lungs begged for fresh air, Deville strolled out into the evening, looking up at the startling blue sky, where then sun was being sucked towards the peach horizon. Sitting on the dusty curb, Angelo cared little for his suit's trousers, as there was only one thing on his mind... Karina. She'd be up and about in no time, and would be at 100% for Seven of the Best, but still, she died. Her heart stopped. And what if she'd died without knowing... without either of them having the guts to... before they'd... 'I need a drink,' Deville grumbled, pulling himself up and striding off to the car park, leaving Karina Wolfenden to awaken, the memory of her Guardian Devil remaining etched into her thoughts. The End... Of The Beginning.
