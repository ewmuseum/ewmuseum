Fairy Tales For 'Tards

21 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 21st, 2005 Once upon a time, there lived two men. But these two men were not your average men, no. They were known far and wide for their wit and bravery, not to mention their skills with the fairer sex. And that’s without mentioning their impossible beauty. Oh, how they were beautiful. Women from around the world wished they could have but a glimpse of these men, and all the other men wished they could be them. These were two very special men, indeed. And so it came to pass that one day a proposition was made to these men. All they needed to do was become in our world what they were in their own. It seemed simple at first, nothing impossible to men of their stature. After all, these two men could move mountains with just a passing thought, so how could something so simple cause them any trouble? But it did. What had sounded so simple in the beginning soon became an improbability, and then an impossibility. And before they knew it, they were gods no more. They were simply men among men, in a world they didn’t understand or belong. Unable to return to their own world, and with their godly powers diminishing, they found refuge in a place they knew no one would find. First it was OSW, and then it was RPW, and even tSC for a show or two, and finally PRIME. With a fabricated backstory to carry them through idle chitchat with the beings who shared their new world, the two hid within the professional wrestling industry, hoping to avoid their prosecutors at all costs. What they weren’t counting on, however, was being found out. Perhaps they became too confident for their own good, or maybe it was just fate. Either way, the time had finally come to make their true selves known. What follows is the true account of what took place on that fateful day. Darkness. A match is struck. The glow illuminates a circle around two bodies, casting shadows that trail off back into the darkness. One body huddles against the other, but is quickly pushed away as the match drops to the ground and is extinguished amidst a muffled curse from the one who had been holding it. “Goddamn it, Clyde,” the voice growls, as a new match is attempted to be lit. “We only brought two matches, so we can’t afford your repeated screw-ups today.” “But Jaaaaames,” Clyde whines in response, “this place scares me. Why are we here, anyway?” You can’t see it, but the sound of James Farwell slapping himself in the head cuts through the otherwise silent room. “I already told you, you moron. We’re here because someone else left us a note to go on some trippy adventure, and since we don’t have anything else of particular interest to do, we went along with it. Now, be a help and hold your hand out for a second. Palm down.” And again, you can’t see it happen, but Clyde obliges James and holds his hand out. Farwell reaches out into the darkness and takes hold of it, with a moment passing before the striking of a match is heard, followed by a girlish scream from Clyde as the room lights up once again. “There,” James smiles, proud of himself. “That’s the last time I use matches that have been in your pocket.” Don’t get the reference? No worries, neither does anyone else. But if you would like to get it (not that anyone does, but a girl can dream…no, wait--a guy can dream…a guy!), please refer back to this. I’ll wait while you read back over the hilarity that is me. Meanwhile, I’m gonna go grab a joint. brb lolz! Done yet? Good, because I am. If not, too fucking bad. I’m the man, and when the man’s done, everyone’s done. Because that’s just how I roll! Yeah, I use that phrase more than Christian actually does. I’m a sad, sad little man. Anyway, getting back to The Forsaken and the task at hand. Lighting a torch that was on the wall with their sole source of guidance, James brings the piece of old wood crudely adorned with straw and perhaps paper down off its resting place, exchanging it for the quasi-wet match. But it turns out that they don’t have to go far, after all, as a terrible shock is delivered to Farwell and Walkins once the torch illuminates the entire room. “My god…” James utters, staring in disbelief at the sight before his eyes. “Wh-who…what…are you?” “We, my good friend,” the hoarse voice across from him speaks, “are you. Or rather, will be you. After we take care of a few things first, of course.” And then a pause, followed by an awkward silence. And then a break of the silence. “Foo’!” Beside him, another voice is heard. This one, however, is distinctly not human, but rather…robotic. “WEEE are GOO-iing to TAKE o-ver yoUR liiives.” James looks puzzled by this prospect, but decides to go along with it for the sake of dragging some sort of story out of this. “Why the hell would anyone want our lives? And especially the two of you. I mean, you’re a robot, and your friend over there…well, he’s Mr.-freakin’-T, for Christ’s sake!” “IIII’M a phy-SI-cist, you jack-AAASS!” And then the wheelchair rolls into view, piloted by none other than Stephen Hawking himself. Standing to his side, the now visible Mr. T folds his arms across his chest, both men looking more familiar than usual. “Foo’! We needs to be takin’ yo’ lives so we can live free of persmercution!” Farwell’s eyebrow lifts slightly. “P-persmercution?” Mr. T remains unfazed. “I pity da foo’ who don’t know English!” And then he turns to Hawking. “Tell dem da plan, yo’!” “Riiight a-WAY, my ill-IT-ER-ATE friiieeend.” Stephen rolls forward a little more, and then it dawns on James. They really do want to be The Forsaken. After all, they’re dressed just like them. Mr. T as James, and Stephen Hawking as Clyde. “WEEE were HI-red by DAN-ny FER-gus-ON to play YOUUU! But weee’ve been foll-ow-ING youUU since you first be-GAAAN. WEEE ca-AAN MI-mic youuu per-fect-ly! WAA-tch!” Turning his chair slightly in order to swing his head over in Mr. T’s direction, Stephen Hawking gives him his cue. “I’m the Innovator of Old School, and by the time I’m through with PRIME, they will know what true wrestling is about!” Mr. T says in almost perfect Farwell fashion. “Also, I apparently don’t know what deodorant is.” Having said nothing at all during the entire exchange, Clyde giggles like a little kid at Mr. T’s impression of James. “He’s pretty close, I’d say.” “He’s pretty close, I’d say,” James responds in mock tone, slapping Clyde in the back of the head for good measure. “He sounds nothing like me.” And then he turns back to Hawking and T. “And you’ll never get away with this! They’ll know you aren’t us!” “PER-haps,” Hawking replies, “buuut per-HAPS they wooon’t ca-AARE.” Well, it is the truth. As harsh as it may be. And James knows it. “Ok, ok,” Farwell says, while trying to think of a better rebuttal. “They may not care, but I don’t think you have what it takes to be us. After all, only Mr. T seems to be into the whole idea. I think you’re just riding on his coattails.” “Hey, yeah!” T shouts. “Foo’! How comes you’s not gettin’ in character?” Hawking is taken aback for a second, but his genius enables him to trick Mr. T without much effort. “BE-caaauuse they dooon’t de-SEERVE to hear it! Nooow, de-stroooy the IN-fi-DELS!” But T doesn’t stay fooled for long. “No, foo’! Dis ain’t ‘bout dem! Dis ‘bout you an’ me! Now say sumtin Clyde-like!” “UHHH…WH-yyy dooon’t youuu BUYYY a BI-ke FROOM a black MAAAN?” Hawking pauses for a second, digging back into Clyde’s RPW days of trying to get over as a heel using racism. “BE-caaaauuuse it miiight BEEE yooouuurs! Ha. Ha. Ha.” Mr. T stares at him for a second, a blank expression on his face, before it’s replaced with one of anger. “BITCH!” Raising his hand to beat down Hawking, Mr. T only relents when the genius begs for a second chance. Finally, he comes up with a line that’s related to Clyde all too well. “IIII’M Cly-DE WALK-ins, biiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii-” And if you’re wondering, that word does keep on going, as Hawking repeatedly tries hitting the stuck key with his only usable finger. Which amounts to jack-shit. Again, just in case you’re wondering. As Hawking tries desperately to turn his voice off, with Mr. T helping as best he can, James nudges Clyde and the two begin to slowly back out of the room, shutting the door behind them as they come back out into the open of the outside world. “Well,” James starts, having dropped the torch in the bushes beside the house. “That was…strange.” “Yeah, but at least it filled that empty void.” “What empty void?” James asks, eyeing Clyde. “Why, the one above Tyler Lopez’s promo, of course!” And as the two leave the area as quickly as possible, a fire begins to rise up from the bushes beside the house. Inside the four walls, the two men who were once gods are now trapped in the hell of mortals, with nowhere to go once they hit the bottom. As for Clyde and James…well, that’s another story.
