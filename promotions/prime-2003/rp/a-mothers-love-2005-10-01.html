A Mothers Love

1 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/10/05 “It looks like God is smiling down on you don’t it?” Damien Kahn looks sombrely from across the table as he sips on his glass of scotch in the smoke filled bar. His opposite number and partner in crime, Paul Cain, sits casually on his chair looking around the room. His attention obviously goes to the ladies, his eyes on stalks as they scan from one fine looking body to the next. With a big smile across his face, he takes a long drink of his bottled beer and taps it against Damien’s glass. “Fortune favours the brave D-Money and I’m not running from anything anymore.” Having observed Paul’s adventure through to redemption, Damien had become clearly aware of Paul’s new resolve and steel. However he had grown weary of the constant travelling back to Denver that they had been doing over the last fortnight. When Paul proposed that they would celebrate his Revolution 77 victory in style, Damien had grown hopeful that maybe they would actually do just that. Instead they found themselves in a dimly lit bar slap bang in the middle of a now very familiar Denver, Colorado. Damien felt like a fish out of water in any place which wasn’t the Big Apple but this place was growing very tiresome. It might have been Paul’s old hunting ground but how Damien longed to be back in his. He lit up a cigarette and casually gazed around the bar before redirecting his attention to Cain. “You might not be running FROM anything anymore but why the hell do we keep running TO here? I thought you sorted out your beef with this place?” Paul sat sipping his beer as he watched the door to the bar open as various members of society passed over the threshold. His smile had evaporated as he refused to take his eyes off of the bars entrance. Damien’s question went unanswered for a time as Paul just stared on. One woman caught him in the act and she moved closer to her boyfriend as this gaze unsettled her. “After all this hard work, you really think I’m going to give Cruz an advantage? I got a phone call about something that might give him a slim chance and as per usual, the source of the problem is a female.” Kahn turned around in his seat and followed Paul’s glare towards the door. He moved back around and leaned forward in his seat. “YOU promised me that this was over and that we were done with this place. YOU said that you were fine now and that all your problems had been taken care of. Now, SURPRISE SURPRISE, we’re back here AGAIN, chasing ghosts. Tell me what the fuck is going on here, Paul.” Cain glanced down at his Rolex watch and turned his attention to his colleague across the table, leaning forward to talk in a low but aggressive tone. “My past has been out to get me for God knows how long now; YOU better than anyone should know that. Now do you think it’s ANY coincidence that as soon as I start getting my shit in order, my business life begins to improve? Yes, I thought I’d gotten everything taken care of but then I get the call to say that someone is back here in Denver, someone that has been screwing with my head for more years than some of these people in here have been alive. There is NO fucking way that I’m missing an opportunity to exorcise this demon and move on! I’m not going up against Cruz with half a mind on the job, those days are done man, the days of taking one eye off the ball are gone. YOU might not like this but I have NO fucking choice!” Just as he finished speaking, a waitress approached the table and leaned over to speak to Paul. Damien’s concern was briefly forgotten about as he focussed on the impressive double D’s that filled her blouse. She caught him out of the corner of her eyes and winked at him before addressing Paul. “Sir, we have someone in the managers office who seems rather keen to talk to you. Keeps yelling your name and seems pretty out of it though. If you like, I can show you where they are sir?” Paul nodded and set his beer down as he hauled himself up from his seat. Damien rose too but Cain held up his hand to stop him from moving. “Stay here, this shit is personal and I need to deal with it…ALONE.” Damien appeared disgruntled to be playing second fiddle again but begrudgingly sat back down again. Paul turned his back to Damien and followed the shapely figure of this waitress as she pushed her way through the crowd that filled this morbidly dark place. One drunken man grabbed at her arm but Cain quickly moved to swat him away and send him staggering backwards. She smiled a silent thank you and they continued to shove their way forwards to the door in front of them. A bouncer blocked the doorway but she waved him away as she indicated for Paul to go through. He paused and took a deep breath as he pulled a carton of cigarettes from his pocket and put one to his lips as he threw the door open and stepped in. As he closed it behind him, a voice immediately screamed out to him. “WHOA! THERE’S THE BIG WRESHLING STAR!” Cain simply raised his eyebrows as he calmly lit the cigarette and took his first drag. His response was both flat and emotionless as he spoke. “Hello, Mom.” It had been years since he had laid his eyes on his own mother and despite having been told of her return to Denver, he had been sceptical as to whether it was really her. Looking at her now she certainly seemed a lot different to the woman that he vaguely recalled to have made an attempt at being a mother. She now had very short boyish hair and she was wearing a very short blue dress with sequins that made her appear more like a hooker than a woman who was in her mid fifties. Her hair was flying out at the sides and she was drastically swaying around the room. So much so in fact that she grabbed for the desk that was planted in the middle to ensure she didn’t fall flat on her face. Her speech was slurring and she mumbled quietly to herself before yelling loudly again at Cain. “WHASH THE MATTER?! DON’T RECOGNISE YER DEAR OL’ MOM?! GIMME A HUG!” She propelled herself forward and flung her arms around his neck before falling forward and only Paul’s body stopped her from collapsing on the carpeted floor. He roughly grabbed her by the sides of her dress and pushed her backwards until she was propped up against the desk. His response was one of disdain and disgust. “If you throw up on my new suit, you can pay the cleaning bill.” Her face changes to one of exaggerated sadness like a pouting little girl with a puppy dog expression. “Ish that any way to talk to the woman who suffered unbear…unbear…[she hiccups]…unbearable pain shoving you out of my pu—“ Cain screwed up his face and waved her comment off quickly with his left hand before turning away from her. “I don’t want you to go there for so many reasons!” A tense and uncomfortable silence filled the room as she swayed against the desk as she focussed on her son. Paul however kept his back to her as he puffed on his cigarette whilst sifting through the thoughts in his head. He then looked over his shoulder at her and smiled to himself. “What the fuck you smilin’ at eh? There ain’t nothin’ to smile at, this ain’t no fucking happy reunion! I don’t even fuckin’ recognise you, you ungrateful bastard!” Again Cain takes a calm and lengthy drag watching as the smoke billows out of his nose and mouth. Idily he flicks the ash away and continues to smile at her. “I came here looking for my mother because I was told she had resurfaced but I don’t know why I bothered, my mother died when she was giving birth to me. I don’t know who you are but you’re not her, you’re just some sad neurotic middle aged alcoholic.” Her eyes grew wide with aggression and she began to screaming uncontrollably like a banshee at him. “OH I GAVE BIRTH TO YOU ALRIGHT, YOU BASTARD! YOU USED ME! YOU LEECHED OFF ME FOR NINE FUCKING MONTHS AND PUT ME THROUGH HELL WHEN YOU WERE BORN! YOU WERE PUT ON THIS EARTH TO MAKE ME SUFFER FOR SOMETHING, FUCK KNOWS WHY! YOU WEREN’T SATISFIED WITH FORCIN’ ME TO LISTEN TO YOUR POISON BUT YOU TURNED MY OWN MOTHER AGAINST ME! YOU FORCED ME AWAY WITH YOUR VILE HATRED! YOU RUINED ME! YOU DESTROYED MY LIFE FROM THE MOMENT YOU WERE CONCEIVED!” The force of her anger and the energy she used to furiously scream at him made her nearly fall over again and she quickly grabbed at the corner of the table in order to steady herself. Cain simply stood with his arms folded and cigarette hanging out of his mouth. He cocked his head to the side and studied her for a moment as he watched her regain her composure. He then withdrew the cigarette and dropped the final ashes to the floor before he moved over to her and ground the butt down into an ashtray on the table. They were close together and Cain slowly turned his head to glare intently at this woman who was his mother. “Have you QUITE…finished?” She moved away from him and spun around in a circle with her arms outstretched. “Look at me…This is what YOU did to me!” Cain scoffed at her and shook his head at himself more than anything. “To think that my business suffered, I lost sleep and nearly my mind over THIS? You’re pathetic and to think that I felt guilty for what I did and that I stored up so much anger towards you. Now I look at you and the only thing I feel…is stupid. You’re NOTHING…you’re a nobody…My grandmother did what was right and she took care of me because she knew her own daughter was a mess! I had NOTHING to blame myself for…And I wasted my life being angry at you when you weren’t even worth wasting the energy on. This place is your home is it?! You and it are WELCOME to each other.” As he went to walk away towards the door she lunged at him and grabbed at his arm with both hands tightly and roughly. Her eyes were maddening and her face was one of a human being who was seething with rage. “YOU….” She spat out the word as she uttered it. “I NEVER WANTED YOU! I NEVER ASKED FOR YOU TO COME ALONG! YOU MADE ME FUCKIN’ MISERABLE! I HAD A LIFE BEFORE YOU CAME BUT THEN MY LIFE BECAME NOTHING BUT AN ONGOING CHORE! THEN YOU REPAID ME BY ACCUSING ME OF THE VERY THING YOUR FATHER DID!” Paul’s expression was cold and heartless as he looked at her dismissively. “As a child I wanted to be loved by my mother…But…as a child…I also knew you were no good for me. Now…take…your…hands…OFF…me!”For those familiar with the life and times of Paul Cain, they would have recognised this same look appear on his face as he went to strike Claudia Lopez down. His eyes had narrowed and he was breathing heavily through his nose as his long hair touched her face because they were so close. She was not daunted though and spat out one more venomous sentence towards her own flesh and blood. “Go…to…HELL!” She screamed the final word loudly into his face as her spit flew into his eyes. He pushed her back with his free arm and then slowly wiped away her phlegm. He then slowly began to smile and almost laughed under his breath as he surveyed this pathetic sight that laid claim to being his relative. “I’ve been there once already thanks to you…But now I’ve seen you again…I know there’s no reason to go back there…My life won’t be ruined by ghosts anymore…Because my mother truly is dead…And whatever YOU are…I hope you find a comfortable gutter to die in with her…” With that he turned away from her and grabbed the handle and threw the door open to the bar. Her legs buckled underneath her and she fell onto them as she watched Paul Cain walk away and close the door on their family ties. On the other side, the waitress watched as he shoved his way back to his table where Damien Kahn raised his glass to greet him from a distance. She quickly rushed over to Paul and called into his ear. “How was she? Are you just going to leave your own mother there?” The waitress chased him down as he walked up to the table and drained the bottle of its contents. Ignoring the woman next to him, he looked across to his associate. “I’m getting bored of Denver, D-Money. I think it’s time we gave Damien Cruz some attention.” Damien rose from his seat and finished his scotch, slamming the glass down onto the table. “Please tell me we don’t have to come back here again, man. I’m sick of this shit hole.” Sympathetically, Paul smiled at Damien and shook his head. “Don’t you worry, amigo, this is a ghost free zone now.” Behind Paul, the waitress was now growing impatient and she slapped her hand on his shoulder. He slowly turned around and looked at her with a lack of interest. “Sir, your MOTHER? You’re not just going to leave her there are you?!” He smirked and laughed at her as he pointed towards the door. “I don’t know WHAT she told you…But my REAL mother died in childbirth…THAT woman…I don’t have a clue WHO she is…” Paul rummaged around in his pocket and took out his wallet, rummaging it around it for some cash. Eventually he pulled out large wad of cash and opened the waitresses’ hand, dropping the money into it. She looked down at the money and appeared completely bemused. “Keep the change; you’ll need it to pay for the cleaning bill...But then, you must be used to old drunks. Sorry I couldn’t help you with her but…she’s not my problem.” He shrugs at her and the duo leave the waitress behind before fading away into the crowd that occupy the bar. She groaned to herself as she put the money into her pocket and glanced at the door where Cain’s mother was hidden away.“God I need a new job.” Scene fades out to black.
