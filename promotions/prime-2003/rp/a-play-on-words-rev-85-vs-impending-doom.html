Date: Over the stretch of several days... “It’s kind of an interesting story how the whole project came about, actually.” A man sits in a chair that looks like it could have come out of your living room. He’s middle-aged and overweight, with thinning black hair and thick-rimmed glasses that magnify his eyes slightly. His t-shirt reads: “RPW/tSC, King of the Indies, September 11, 2004.” Behind him on a shelf sit at least two squadrons of Space Marine miniatures. A name appears beneath him on the screen: GABE MCMANUS – FREELANCE INTERNET WRITER & WARHAMMER 40000 ENTHUSIAST “I had written a little article for…for, uh, www.projectfedmark.com, if you’re familiar with them at all…an article about Nova, after the Main Event for PRIME’s ReVolution 85 was leaked.” Cut to a computer screen, the homepage of www.projectfedmark.com: Who Is Nova?25 Dec 2005 Hello out there in cyberspace. I nabbed some pretty phat loot from ol’ Saint Nick (the phattest of which was the delivery of the Xbox 360 that I reserved in April of 2003), but one unexpected gift arrived on Jesus’ birthday that trumped them all. The leaking of the ReVolution 85 Main Event…and PRIMEates will not be disappointed. Ready for a little extra Christmas cheer? Well, in the paraphrased words of DMX, “Gabe gon’ give it to ya!” Hoyt Williams. Angelo Deville. Nova. Tyler Nelson. It’s been dubbed the “2005 Showpiece Match,” and it might be the best booking since the Tropical Turmoil Preview Match (Nova/Deville vs. Aimz/Youngblood). Too often the PRIME booking committee avoids placing the company’s top names against one another on weekly ReVolutions in hopes that the inevitable confrontations will play themselves out to the tune of big Pay-Per-View bucks. That’s all well and good, but when PRIME PPVs are sometimes two months apart, waiting for your favorite stars to butt heads can be a painful endeavor. It could be argued that the focus of the weekly shows is more on the vignettes and backstage segments, but isn’t that losing sight of the real focus of a competitive federation like PRIME? PRIME is numero uno because of arguably the most competitively driven roster in the industry. But what good is that if you only really bust it out for the PPVs? I’m generalizing, but you see my point, right? Okay, so we’ve got this solid booking, and I’m analyzing the participants. We’ve got Hoyt Williams, God’s other Son, Our Personal Jesus, the Pontiff and former Universal Champion of PRIME, son of Duke Williams, a PCW icon. We’ve got Angelo Deville, a legend in the sense of the word that hasn’t been ruined yet. Long history related to WO/PTC, inaugural Universal Champion, active in OSE, RPW, PWC, a list of accomplishments and tournament victories to rival any combined number of men you could assemble. We’ve got Tyler Nelson, one of the best-known, most dastardly heels in the history of the industry, former PCW CEO and two-time PCW World Champion. And then we have Nova, an enigmatic character in this list of established stars…no past history remotely related to PTC, no major accomplishments within PTC, or truth be told, PRIME, since his arrival within the community from the failed revival of NEW. Not to say he hasn’t accomplished anything…the only two-time 5-Star Champion, PTC Extreme Champion, to name a few…but unlike the others, he has no pinnacle achievement in the PTC community, no ultimate prize. So it got me to thinking…who is Nova? His face has been all over PRIME TV for the better part of this last year, and yet I can’t escape the feeling that we don’t know much about him at all, not in the sense that we know the others. We’ve watched the others for much or all of their professional careers, seen them evolve, progress. Not so with the Rising Star, who appropriately was nominated and elected to receive the award by the same name only weeks before his career-threatening injury at Tropical Turmoil. Who is he? Since his return, he’s appeared randomly, almost in renegade fashion, on PRIME television, without the specific goal that pushes so many other roster members. We all saw him headed into TT, sights set on the Uni Title shot, and then derailed spectacularly by Brandon Youngblood. So now what from the mysterious Rising Star? Now what from the man apparently destined to give us flashes of brilliance from the darkness? I don’t know. So, in closing for today, who is Nova? I’m hoping he’ll show us on the 4th. - Gabe “Maniac” McManus “And I, I don’t…uh…it wasn’t twenty-four hours after my article was posted I think that I got the e-mail from…” “GABE! DID YOU PICK UP ANY MILK AT THE STORE?!” McManus turns his head to look off-camera, his thick brow furrowing irritably. “Ma! I told you we’re filming for television! Don’t bother me!” Turning around, he scratches the back of his head for a moment before continuing. “I got the e-mail from…” “DID YOU GET TWO PERCENT? YOU KNOW TWO PERCENT GIVES ME THE SHITS! I ONLY DRINK ONE PERCENT!” “Ma! For the love of God in Heaven, leave me alone! We’re filming for television!” “ARE YOU WEARING THAT SHIRT I PICKED OUT ON YOUR BIRTHDAY? THAT WAS A NICE SHIRT I PICKED OUT ON YOUR BIRTHDAY!” “I’m wearing the damn shirt, Ma, and to be honest, it itches like hell! Now you’re ruining my interview!” Gabe turns back to the camera, his face beet-red from the exchange with his maternal overseer, and makes his right hand into a pistol. “One of these days, ya know? BANG! So anyway, I got the e-mail from Trout…” The scene switches again to a computer screen: From: [detail withheld]To: [detail withheld]Subject: Yes. Your article was fascinating.
