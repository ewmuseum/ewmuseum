Date: Doesn't Matter All it took was three seconds. Three seconds to end one of the most personal battles of my life. Scandal, steroids and stupidity. I left myself open to so many attacks because I just didn't care anymore. All I wanted to do was clear my name and as soon as I did, the axe fell. Hung from a ring post with a chain like a convicted terrorist was my fate. But it took three seconds to make the pain go away. Three seconds to move on. The young buck didn't think I could hang anymore. The old guy thought the new age had an advantage over experience. But my opponent made one crucial mistake... He made my battle with someone else into his own... and he paid for it. Three seconds to re-establish myself. Three seconds to become important again. |~o~| Tuesday, August 12, 2008 “Daddy I can't see!” The little girl mumbled into her fathers shoulder. Desiree was being carried through the crowd of people bunched into the aged terminal of the Richardson International Airport in Winnipeg. Some people were calm, just wanting to see the arrival of the hometown hero and his family. Others weren't quite as sedated, pushing and shoving to get their own view, some waving around Sharpies and pens looking for a John Hancock. Some of the crazed people got their wish. “How many more times am I gonna have to stand and watch you sign another woman's breasts?” Jenny said with a smirk as they got into their car after strapping Desiree into the child's seat. “Breasts? I signed old programs and DVD's of Pay-Per-View's.” “I've seen you sign cleavage before.” “I've only done that a few times, a couple when you were there. Was I supposed to say no?” “No.” “Hey, I'll sign yours next time if it would make you happy.” They both shared a hearty laugh. Many cars followed as they pull out of the lot. Unknown to them, this was the starting point. |~o~| Tuesday, May 8, 2007 Brandon Youngblood, beaten beyond what anyone thought possible. A physical dissection if you will. The new 5-Star Champion returned home, greeting fans at the airport after one of the biggest wins of his career. The terminal wasn't full, but there were enough people around for one superstar to barely handle. Giving in to the pressure, Killean sat at a table belonging to an airline and signed autographs. He really didn't have much else to do for the rest of the day. One fan really caught his eye. She had been around before at some events, autograph signings and other open to the public events the Hall of Famer was involved with. She seemed to be a loyal fan, never wavering and always wanting a scribble on a picture or a magazine cover. Even when Sirrajin was the baddest man in PRIME, she was still a fan. Killean thought it to be an unhealthy obsession at first, but thought nothing more about it when she walked away with a smile after a simple hello and signature. Killean figured that he signed three to four dozen items for her and her demeanor was always polite which made things easier. Today, she was once again at the airport to greet her hero. But this time, she held no paper in her hands or program in a book bag. She was standing at the end of the line, speaking to another fan while waiting. When the end of the line approached, The Supreme Machine recognized her, giving her a smile and a wink. She blushed and broke a grin of her own. Once it was her turn, there was no one else behind her. “Congrats. I saw the match, you were great.” “Thanks.” Killean replied. “How are you?” “Been better... got over the flu just recently.” Killean pursed his lips, never expecting to actually make conversation with the woman. He rested his marker on the table, trying to figure out just what was happening. “So... anything you need?” He smiled again, trying to end the strange situation. “Yeah, just wanted another autograph to add to the collection.” A normal request. Plenty of people had numerous signatures of his, but none so many as this female. But instead of producing paper, she opened her blouse, exposing her cleavage to the wide eyed champion. Having only done it once before, he was taken off guard. But he offered no resistance and she offered no explanation. Without anyone seeing, he performed the task at hand, making sure to dot his i's. It only lasted for a couple seconds as she smiled and said thanks before walking away from the table, covering her new souvenier. |~o~| Tuesday, August 12, 2008 “No wonder why I thought that blue car was following me. It's right over there.” Killean points across the parkade to a blue Chevy. A woman gets out wearing a business type suit and a classy hat. “You're too observant Kill. Other people do live here.” Jenny said as she took Desiree's hand. Killean took her other as they unloaded the rest of the car. Both Jenny and Killean were wheeling suitcases with one hand while they swung Desiree back and forth in between them. Mere Seconds Later... “He's coming this way Fergie.” Jordan said as she shut her door hard. She took off the business coat and looked at the dark grey cat as he hopped up on the stool beside the door. He meowed and looked at her. She thought he looked concerned. “Don't look at me like that. You know how long it took to get this place after I found out where he lived.” The cat sneezed and scratched it's ear. Jordan stood by the door and took her shoes off, waiting to hear the elevator doors open from down the hall. An long mew eminated from the cats stomach. “I know but she isn't right for him.” The sound of the elevator bell ringing from down the hall signalled Sirrajin's arrival on the floor. She took a peak out of the peep hole in her door as she watches Killean walk by with Desiree in tow. Jenny was leading the way. From outside the door, she could hear his voice. “I don't know Dizzy, we can make some macaroni for you.” “YAY!” The little Sirrajin exclaimed, jumping down the hall with her father. Jordan opened her door and peeked out at Killean going down the hall only three doors over. She picked up her newspaper, having purposely left it there in the morning. As Killean disappeared into his loft with Jenny and Desiree, Jordan did the same. She shut the door and turned her back to it, sighing in content. Her blouse was falling open and a black tattoo was partially visible on her chest. A large 'K' and 'S' accented it. She moved from her door into the main room where numerous pictures and magazine covers were framed on her wall. She sat on her couch and looked at all the memorabilia around the room and then reached for an empty frame on her table. She took the newly signed picture and placed it in the middle of the frame. Fergie the Cat moved over to his bowl, placed just under a cluster of different autographed items. He nudged it with his nose a bit, signalling that he was indeed hungry. Jordan walked over to the area and placed the frame on the wall on a nail that was already strategically placed on the wall with the other frames. She admired her work before walking to the nearby cupboard to get the small bag of cat food. “Aunt Nicole will be looking after you next week big guy.” Jordan said as she scooped the food into the bowl. She gave him a gentle pat on the side, winning a purr from the animal. He started to eat his food as she put the bag away, humming while she worked and only stopping to talk. “I've got a plane to catch to Charleston.” She nodded, happy with herself for getting the plane ticket while she was at the airport. The cat meanwhile minded his own business eating his lunch. “Don't give me that, scalpers will have good tickets outside the building when I get there.” She was still talking to the cat who frankly didn't care what she was saying at the moment. But she continued anyway. “I think I'll trim my hair a bit and dye it red, just like her. I haven't done that yet. He won't recognize me anyways with the makeup on.” She observed herself in the mirror, curling her shoulder length black hair in her fingers. “No silly. I'll be back after the show. I just have to see him fight in person again.” She looked over to the cat who was still eating and paying no attention to her. She stuck out her tongue anyways. “We can watch him the week after on TV.” Shr grouped her hair in a makeshift ponytail and took the scissors off the table in front of her. She hummed quietly while she trimmed her own hair in only a few slices of the utensil. The cat stopped eating for a moment and glanced over at her mostly because he heard the scissors cutting. When he realized it wasn't a bag of treats for him, he turned back to his bowl. The motion however was not lost on Jordan as she admired her handywork. “Yeah, red'll work, right Fergie?”
