My Mindset > Your Credibility

17 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17 Dec 05 [The Metaphor] Your eyes are closed as you find yourself deep in meditation. Your heart rate is steady as you lose yourself in thought. Then it happens. You fill the cold course texture of the object pressed against your head. Your mind begins to race as you try to think your way out of it. Do you open your eyes? No. Any quick movement may leave you lying on the floor in a pool of your own blood. Do you try to strike quickly, hoping to catch your assailant by surprise before he has an opportunity to strike? Possibly… …but what if you miss? What is the right reaction? What do you do, when a man, holds a loaded gun to your temple? [The Irony] There is always silence before the strike; a momentary pause where the victim knows and understands what is about to happen. But he can’t stop it… …and neither can you. Everyone has those dreams of gander; born with stars in their eyes. Whether it’s the urge to become a doctor, a lawyer, a banker, or even an all star athlete, you believe that nothing can stand in your way of reaching the mountain top. Until one day; one day when those dreams seem to have turned into a nightmare--a nightmare because it is now apparent that you are no longer looking through the eyes of a baby nor a child, but into the mirror of reality. There is an even larger question that may have to be answered; what do you do when your dreams have been swept away by an unforeseen accident--a bump in the road, a splinter in the lion’s foot, or an event that not only could end your career, but the entirety of your life as well. Though unclear, you still smile. You will soon find yourself at a spot in life that you never wanted to dream of ever being at; on the edge of death as it stares into the whites of your eyes. Running thoughts will run wild through your mind of not being able to hug your spouse, or kiss your children to sleep one more night. This is all you will have; just a few seconds to ponder on how well you want to live them and suddenly… …suddenly you’re dead. Push away the ignorance and you must realize that this has been the greatest thing ever to happen to you within your life. You have been cleansed of all offense, sin, and wrongdoing that have hugged tightly around your neck for most of your life. You can live the way you want to prefer to live now— --alone. No lies. No cheating. This is all for you. This is your happy place, Deville. [The Odds] All the signs are pointing to no. The critics say that I can’t get the job done. A mountainous plateau that seems unreachable and most of you have foreseen me losing focus; assuming that my self doubt will creep into my head. Something surely will bring me down. Something surely will shake my foundation, right? Wrong. It’s the sign that you’ve been waiting for, PRIME… It’s the moment you weren’t ready for Angelo… Now you stare me into the eye. [The Story] “They fight in the openness of his space with bright jagged waves of electricity. The two tango, back and forth, neither giving an inch making is possible for each stroke to be seen for miles. What magnificent seas of color; jumping to and fro just knowing that each hit creates a monstrous wave that produces a sound familiar to herds of elephants stampeding down a moon lit alley—yet peaceful.” And there I stand--staring into the night from a place where I used to come to alone as a kid during the tough times-the times where you feel completely broke down and you think to yourself why you keep going. The sky is lit with the full moon glistening across the horizon of the water from a dock of which I stand; as close to the edge as possible because it feels like I’m flying. The water glistens ever so beautifully as the wind blows a few locks of my hair out of my eyes. “The silence of the night makes it even more powerful now. As the tears of our ancestors pond the black tar-marked pavement and as earth-shattering stillness consumes every creature, understand that His work is at hand. Indulgence becomes washed away from those whom have passed us by, and yet we are still looking ahead into the future curious of when our time will come. Oh how beautiful the grayness is that swallows us whole. Asking no questions, giving no answers, but only pondering the mysteries of life’s ever changing ways.” I rub the haze from my eyes with my course hands. “I stand gazing out at you as you go about your work and all I can say… …is what a beautiful sight.” As I stand here, I try to sink my mind into the thoughts in life that usually are found forgotten. I try not to think of the downside of things of life, but to acknowledge the reason why I find myself where I am today. “I am respected, I am strong, and I am smart. I have the attributes to excel in life but I too, like every living thing on this earth, have my downfalls. I am not perfect. I do not always know the answers to life‘s questions, I find myself insecure at times, and there are many times where I find myself defeated.” I pull out the pack cigarettes from my pocket and take out the last one. I crumble up the empty pack and put it back in my pocket. I reach in my other pocket and pull out my Zippo. I snap it open and on the third flick, I am successful on lighting it. Pulling the flame up to my cigarette, my face glows and you can mark out the scars from my existence. I suddenly snap the Zippo shut and put it in my pocket. The cigarette glow is faint yet distinct. It glows brighter as I breathe in the toxins that do nothing good for me, yet they relax me because not many things do. I exhale a cloud of blue smoke and stare back out into the distance “There are many things in life that make me take the time to think. In a few days I find myself stepping into the ring with one of the best there ever was, Angelo Deville. He’s won barrelfuls of matches like the one scheduled to open The Great American Nightmare this weekend and a loss to him is a complete rarity. I don’t shake, I tremble. It’s comprehensive that fear will be running through my veins, but unlike any other frightened man, I do not cower and I do not back down. I lift my chin higher and hope to smell my opportunity of a lifetime linger within my reach. I want this more than any single human being could imagine. I take another drag of my cigarette and release a small chuckle as I exhale ”I realize I must start focusing on the things that matter. I no longer can my ego overcome my mind and my desire to fight. But why do we fight?” My eyes clench as I try to distinguish that answer. “Is it just to prove that I’m right and my opponent is wrong? Is it to prove that I’m a better athlete than my opponent; more seasoned in the arts of war and better trained in technique? What about to prove that I am stronger, faster, or more agile?” I extinguish my cigarette on the edge of the rail and hold it in my hand. “No, that’s not it at all. Mankind fights for the right to believe in a cause, a movement, an event for freedom. I fight, with the very same purpose to defend my right, my freedom, and my cause to lead a movement for the ones that I love. Entire worlds have been conquered under this very same premise. When men go to war, they believe that their fighting to protect the innocent--their babies, their wives, their mothers, their fathers, son’s, daughters, friends, and numerous other loved one’s. Sometimes when I think of my opponents, I think about how they feel about this topic. I wonder if Angelo Deville believes in fighting for what he believes in? I wonder if he believes in the phrase ‘by any means necessary’.” I look up at the star blanketed night as something pops in my mind. “Oh yes, that is correct. He fights for the sole purpose of revenge and only revenge for some reason regardless or not if he knows who he finds himself toe to toe with. Revenge, a dish best served as the old saying goes. What happens when he walks the path of vengeance only to be foiled by determination? What happens when his wrath wasn’t enough to get the job done? What would happen if his target proved him wrong… …again?” I smirk as I realize that making sense with my words and focusing on the playing field prior to running on it clears a lot of unanswered answers. I turn around to a lit path back into what seems to be a quiet city about two miles down. I start making my way off of the dock and soon reach a dirt path. I pull the balled up cigarette pack in the garbage can nearby including the cigarette butt. “There are no rules of engagement when it comes to war; no right way or wrong way to take the fight to those who may oppress you. All you have is your morals and values for thy fellow man--in the art of war do those words even exist. When your enemy doesn’t focus on those things that matter in order to achieve anything in life, or when their actions go far beyond the ideas of winning and losing, far beyond purpose, far beyond a movement and when their actions border on insanity is when you know that you are one step above them. He pleads for me to stop him, and that plea I can respond to because it is something that I enjoy to do. “ I begin to walk through the lit path as I stop and turn my head once more and gain the relief that the view of the night over the water creates on me. I turn my head forward and begin to walk again until I fade into the darkness a bit further down the path. Confidence hurts, Angelo. [The End]
