Date: I hate life The long recognized old school blue and black PRIME logo flashes three times. An abridged version of "Sound of Madness" blasts through a computer screen while various clips from ReVolution and Pay-Per-Views of the past fly by in rapid succession. PRIME PRESENTS... A WEB EXCLUSIVE... SEGMENTS FROM THE VAULT Matt Mills is seen in PRIME Headquarters shuffling assignments between his fingers and laughing heartily with a sound technician. Despite all of the constant abuse he receives from the much larger and more athletic wrestlers, he really does have a passion for the business that is worth sticking around for. How many people get to hang out and interact with the wrestlers on his level? Not many, and not for such a consistent duration. Time to make some more Internet magic happen. Mills puts the papers across his lap and fully turns, acknowledging the camera and PRIME web audience. Matt Mills: Good evening to everybody on the Interweb, this is Matt Mills coming to you from PRIME Headquarters with another exclusive episode of "Segments from the Vault". In this edition, the heated controversy between Tyler Rayne, Devin Shakur, Christian Daniels, and Lindsay Troy will be highlighted. Over the past two shows, they have made conscious efforts to hide from one another either inside the building or in separate venues. 193 would be the first time in three weeks that nobody attempted to deceive anybody in location. And folks, let me tell you, the first encounter of the night ensured nobody would avoid the other. ReVolution 193 Minutes: 25:02 to 28:49 With one step into the Sprint Center, an unmatched roar shakes the foundation and buckles every quivering female knee in attendance. These women are fainting like they just saw the Beatles saunter off their plane back in February of 64. This man's other military boot graces the ground and sends a shock wave of intimidation through the locker room. After the hideous actions committed last week, all of which the backstage employees saw, this figure has regained his status as being the violent and cynically witty douchebag. RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH The one and only, Tyler Rayne. His face still holds bruising and cut marks from the torture of 190 and 191, but his spirit and confidence are back to full strength. After the way he left Devin Shakur last week, not many folks are going to stand in front of him and desire to engage in a battle of fisticuffs. Cutting around two employees bringing a table in from the parking lot, Rayne gives some much necessary dap to some of his fellow roster members. One who happens to be the host of Segments from the Vault, Matt Mills. Matt Mills: Tyler Rayne, long time no see compadre. Tyler Rayne: 'Ey kid, how's life in the fast lane been? Matt Mills: I don't think I'm the one living in the fast lane. I'm just the backstage guy with a stick. Tyler Rayne: You trying to hawk my gimmick there, Ace? Both men give a light chuckle while Mills drops the microphone behind his back, this being an unofficial meeting despite being on camera. Matt Mills: No, really, how are you man? You've been pretty busy the last couple weeks. Tyler Rayne: Yeah, one way or the other. I'm chillin' like always, kid. None of this shit is ever going to break me. Emo and Easy Rider can punch and kick all they want but as as always- The Underground Pimp slips into his best 1984 Terminator voice. Tyler Rayne: I'll be back. Mills gets a good laugh out, holding up a finger to get Rayne's attention and stop his talking. Reaching into his side pocket, the interviewer busts out a pair of "These are supposed to make me cool" glasses. Tyler Rayne: Shiny, you'd be better than that Bale fuck going around these days. Matt Mills: Really? Tyler Rayne: No doubt. Speaking of fuck...Where's LT stationed tonight? Matt Mills: Other side of the building I'm afraid. Tyler Rayne: Son of a- Before Rayne can conclude his sentence, a hand descends down and taps him on the shoulder. The Underground Pimp turns around to meet his acquaintance. One hulking chain wrapped fist sends him to the ground. Mills doesn't decide to be a hero, having encountered this beast before, and bolts off for higher ground. Christian Daniels stands atop the recovering Tyler Rayne, straddling over his body and ensuring he doesn't get far. Christian Daniels: What, you thought I was going to let you walk around here free of charge tonight, jackass? Christian swings his hips into the second punch and furiously pounds the back of Tyler Rayne, collapsing him fully onto the concrete. Daniels employs a back mount, locking the legs for security, and goes to work on the ribs with alternating rights and lefts. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO Nick (OSV): Normally, I would be in the position to say this is a travesty, but this war has gotten so out of hand that I can't really comment on it anymore. Richard (OSV): I can. POUND HIM LIKE THE SICK PRICK THAT HE IS! Christian switches tactics and drops an elbow across the shoulder blades before reaching up to the neck and slipping his left arm underneath the chin. The entire arm being wrapped in a chain does not enhance the fortune of The Underground Pimp in any capacity, but he still has a desire to try and liberate himself from the hold. Christian has to struggle and deliver a forearm shiver to the head in order to fully cinch the submission hold. His left arm snakes around the neck and onto the right bicep while the left hand pushes down on the back of the head. Rayne tries to roll from his position, but Daniels tightens his grip and keeps Rayne in position. Christian Daniels: THIS IS WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU FUCK WITH FAMILY YOU STUPID PRICK! Gurgled break escapes The Underground Pimp, and he makes one last ditch effort to escape. All of his reserves are flaring, fighting against the chain and 300 pounds atop his body. Those supposedly fresh wounds are now agitated, more and more of Rayne's body aching with every passing second in the choke. A mountain of bodies bolt from their designated locker room, hoping they can make it to the scene before its too late. Rayne throws an absolute last instinctive effort into escaping before slipping into unconsciousness. Most in this predicament would release their victims back into the wild but Daniels doesn't want to give Rayne any satisfaction whatsoever. He's not relaying a message, he's going for the kill. Flying around the corner, Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas and Enemigos I-V finally arrive on the scene, immediately using their collective strength to remove The Biker. Dam puts his massive arms around Daniels waist and pulls, but ends up yanking Rayne off the ground in the process with Daniels attached. The Biker is stuck to Rayne like glue on paper. Three of the Enemigos rush around and start messing with Daniels arms, trying to release the grip. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: Let it go, boss. The Biker refuses. All five Enemigos are now fix on unlocking Daniels' arms. Dam goes over to assist them, working like a referee does to untangle wrestlers stuck in the figure four. Ten seconds after their collective minds are on the same page, Rayne is relinquished from the grip and rolled against the wall for safety. Christian is shuffled by Dametreyus out of the scene, being scolded with his deep based voice all the way down the hall and presumably out of the building. The Enemigos stay by Rayne and call for medical assistance in broken English. Segments from the Vault Matt Mills shakes his head and continues reading off the teleprompter just behind the camera. Matt Mills: That was truly a heinous attack by Daniels, extracting revenge for Devin Shakur and ReVolution 192. Lisa Tyler was determined to have her stranglehold on this show, saying the inmates had ran the asylum long enough. She was the head honcho and should be in command. While she couldn't get that first one just right, now her eyes and security were going to pay a little closer attention to Daniels and Rayne, plus anybody who might show up with them. ReVolution 193 Minutes: 60:04 to 62:18 After being reprimanded by the Enemigos and given a temporary suspension from the building, Christian Daniels throws open the parking lot door and steps back inside the Sprint Center. He is met with a massive wave of heat, thanks to his only act of the evening. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO Christian doesn't appear to have an expression that says, "I'm looking to stay around". While unattended in the parking lot for the last 32 minutes, nobody came out and attempted to make his acquaintance. He could be surprised at this, but considering what happened last week and the kind of people he's been dealing with since arriving in PRIME, nothing surprises him anymore. The Biker strolls into his locker room, grabs a duffel bag, and throws it over his right shoulder. Without bothering to look around, Christian whips open the door and heads toward the exit, a mission accomplished and night fulfilled. He could get Troy on ReVolution 194 when Devin was around to distract Rayne with the dog and pony show. He had to make sure Gamble and a soda machine were nearby. Or...He could get his dose of Lindsay Troy right now, considering she is standing in front of him with a steel chair in hand. CRACK RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH The Biker doesn't drop, instead stumbling backwards and looking at Troy like she's out of her mind. Perhaps she is, or this is part of a greater scheme Christian hasn't deciphered just yet. Regardless, he trudges forward, throwing a right hand into the chair. Troy ducks underneath the shot, pops behind and slams Christian in the leg. The Biker stumbles out into the main corridor, the worst place he could have gone into. RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH Because it's where Tyler Rayne stands, ready, and eager to fight. Rushing forward with both legs, The Underground Pimp slams his feet into Christian's stomach, moving the Biker back into the wall and sending his head against the concrete. Troy doesn't give him an ounce of leeway, slamming steel against skull, dropping Christian on his backside against the wall. Nick (OSV): It appears Troy and Rayne aren't going to let Daniels off the hook tonight! Troy casually places the chair in front of Christian's face while Rayne rushes across the corridor, full head of steam, screaming like a ninja, and puts both feet into Christian's face, bending the chair at an awkward angle. Christian slumps further against the wall. YEAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH The Underground Pimp takes the chair from The Queen and goes to wallop Shakur's running buddy one more time for good measure. When a burly security guard and his trained staff of backup rush into the scene to make the save. The chair is taken from Rayne and both superstars are sent from the arena with the Enemigos pushing ahead like a riot squad. Tyler Rayne: Yeah, he doesn't get kicked out but we do, the fuck Dam? Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: I done gave him the same time out I givin you boss, half an hour. Tyler Rayne: That shit's not cool Dam. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: Ain't my call, boss. Tyler Rayne: Fuck this, cuntbag has to come out sometime. Shit ain't going to make it out of here if I have anything to say about it. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: Not happenin', boss. Tyler Rayne: He comes out into the parking lot it damn sure is gonna happen. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: You or Daniels attack each other again tonight, yo asses goin'to the slammer. Tyler Rayne: That's just cruel. At least let me stab him once. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas: Still ain't my call, boss. The Pimp gives a one finger salute to Christian before shoving through the Exit door where Lindsay Troy has been tapping her foot waiting. Segments from the Vault With folded arms across his lap and no papers anywhere nearby, PRIME's number one interviewer is ready to wrap the evening's festivities up. Matt Mills: Dametreyus gave out the firm warning that the next superstar to strike the other party would be arrested. With Rayne and Troy now on the outside looking in, what would happen next? How would Daniels respond to being, as he would say, "punked" by his two rivals? Where was Devin Shakur in all of this? All those questions would be answered in the final showdown for the evening, with a few surprises in store. ReVolution 193 Minutes: 95:40 to 98:58 Lindsay Troy and Tyler Rayne stand on the other side of the Exit doors, having an odd conversation. The words "Amsterdam, Duke Valentine, transvestite stripper, and Hitler lookalike" are referenced, so one can assume The Underground Pimp is narrating the story. Some of the lower card superstars looking to make a name for themselves in PRIME file out in a group, heading toward their parked cars and giving the veteran superstars a wave. They respond in kind, indifferent to the plight and more interested in telling/hearing their story. Troy might only be hanging around since there isn't anything to do for her tonight with Christian being on the defensive. Staring down at their watches, Troy opens the Exit door and heads back inside, prepared to get her stuff and check out for the night. The Pimp follows behind and they head for the locker room section. Well, let's make a correction, they were heading for the locker room section until a certain seven foot behemoth annexed himself into the picture. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO The nefarious Biker pops out from behind a corner and lands a big right hand on The Underground Pimp, setting him backwards and by the Welcome mat near the doors. Troy grapples for position with Daniels, but finds his strength unbearable and gets launched against the wall. Christian puts a boot to Troy's midsection and unloads with furious lefts and rights. Her toned midsection doesn't find much use other than being there against the bowling ball fists of The Biker. Her knight in shining armor rushes from behind and leaps on Christian's back, slipping his arms around and looking to apply the same choke hold which put him down earlier in the night. Christian attempts to fight back, launching Rayne from his back, but finds the annoying, and very sharp, legs of Lindsay Troy strike him in the sternum. The boot sends him rolling forward through the door, inadvertently conking Rayne's head against the steel door. Both superstars go tumbling into the parking deck and a phone call is made inside Lisa Tyler's office. The police, stationed half a mile away, start up their vehicles and book it toward the Sprint Center. The Queen puts another kick into Christian's leg, sending The Biker down on one leg while Rayne jumps off and locks Christian in a Muay Thai clinch. One knee hits him square on the forehead. Troy rifles her foot into Christian's ribs and The Biker is on the verge of getting punked two times in one show. Lucky for him there are sirens wailing in the distance and a black Ferrari who has the five second jump on them. With tires squealing down the deck, all three superstars look over and see said Black Ferrari charging in their direction, with the intent of running Rayne or Troy over, whichever the front end finds first. Troy rolls out of the way, but Rayne isn't so lucky and gets jacked up on the car, thrown overhead and put back first against the concrete. BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO Christian takes the free opportunity to boot Troy in the side of the head before seeing the wave of cop cars. When the passenger door shoots open, The Man in Black sticks his head out the window. Devin Shakur: GET IN! MOVE! MOVE! THEY ARE RIGHT ON MY ASS! Troy tries grabbing a hold of Christian's leg, but The Biker wiggles free and hard sprints across the parking lot into the driver's seat. Shakur drops both doors with a button and the duo flee into the night. The Underground Pimp, who doesn't give a damn at all about the police, rises up from his position and finds a pipe near the dumpster area. Looking to send one final message, he grabs the pipe and Hail Mary's the object down the parking lot. RAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH Through the back window of the Ferrari seconds before the vehicle rounds the corner. Devin Shakur: GO! GO! FORGET IT! Rayne gives one last fuck you middle finger to the duo before looking over at the cops and sighing. He knows the drill by now and rather than be the pompous douchebag, worn arms go behind the head and Rayne allows himself to be arrested. The police are even nice, not slamming his head into the top of the car and claiming it to be accidental. Tyler Rayne is aware there will be a next time Lindsay Troy walks over to her vehicle and prepares to bail The Underground Pimp out with her checkbook. Segments from the Vault Matt Mills: Well fans, that does it for another edition of Segments from the Vault. I'm Matt Mills. End
