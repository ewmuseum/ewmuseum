Date: 5/12/06 Without Eye Contact It was a rare and welcome circumstance to be able to walk down the street, holding hands, and thinking about nothing but the hand wrapped in his own. He stole a peek at her occasionally, and was enchanted by the way her face was turned upward slightly, smiling at the sky that swam with bright stars. He was grateful for the chance to drape an arm over her tiny shoulders, and pull her into an embrace as they strolled up Broome Street on the outskirts of Manhattan’s Greenwich Village. Damien Cruz often fantasized about being ‘normal’, and in moments like this, the fantasy was easy to inbdulge. An hour before, like normal people do, he and his girlfriend made love in the shower, and spoke lazily about what they wanted to eat. Like normal people, they sorted through take-out menues, and eventually decided that it was a nice enough night to take a walk. And, like normal people do, they interupted their dressing to sneak another session of love-making, and she protested only at first, and not very adamently. So now, like normal people do, they were walking, arms clasped, conversation light and playful, toward a Thai restaurant they had both heard good things about. They spoke of what they were hoping to eat, Damien making it clear that they could serve him shit on a Ritz, and he wouldn’t care so long as they made good Thai Iced Tea. She, more discriminating, suggested that they both order something they have never eaten before, and then trade half and half. Damien, deep in the fantasy, agreed, and the conversation shifted into how much weight she was trying to lose; and of course he playfully reprimanded her. He, on the other hand, talked about getting another tattoo, and she promised him that if he put another mark on his body, than she would leave him. The one thing that never came up, with some amount of effort, was work. Hers or his, or what the morning held for each. But they both knew. She had a noon flight back to Santa Fe, where she had moved to escape the many places that reminded her of him a year before. And, of course, he was going to be on a flight to Someplace Else to compete in one of the most important matches of his PRIME career. So, the conversation was avoided, and they walked, in ignorant bliss, into The Siam Palace, arm-in-arm and decidedly happy. Invading The Ether He closed his eyes, and listened to the sound of her breathing. He ran his fingers up up her, which rested across his chest, and committed it to memory one last time. Somehow, they had made it through the night with no real discussion of what came next. In fact, it was a promise that they had made to eachother that they would not ask what came next next. But as she shifted where she lay, and rested her face into the crook of his neck, the familiarity of it made him question it. He lay there, the taste of her skin still fresh on her lips, the wamth of her breasts pressed against his body, the light scent of Liz Claiborne and baby powder sending his memories into flight, the sound of her light snoring in the room, and the moonlight casting shadows over her beautiful body. He kissed her softly on her chin, and slid away from the bed. He sat up on the edge of the bed, and walked to the downstairs kitchen. Standing by the sink, he poured himself the last bit of the wine. The harsh light of the kitchen made everything seem surreal. He washed his face, trying to clear away the racing thoughts, the swimming doubts, and the suffocating confusion he felt. Something was changing in him, and he knew that it was her. The decision was made. He was going to do it. He was going to go upstairs, wake her up with a kiss, and tell her that he chose her. Her. He was going to call the PRIME HQ, and tell them to sell him his contract. He was going to call his lawyer, and draft up a resignation statement. He was going to give her the life she deserved, and the life that she was asking for. Whatever it took, he would offer it. His eyes watered with the clarity of it. His breathing came easier, and every nerve in his body felt alive with his decision. Damien Cruz, The Latin Assassin, was going to walk away from the life that had served no future purpose for him. It was time. It was, despite everything he had assigned to that fucking ring, nothing compared to what she was offering. He dropped the wine glass into the sink, and walked upstairs, kissing her on the back of her shoulder, and tracing his lips to her neck, and then she rolled over, half-asleep and meeting his kiss with her lips. He kissed her desperatly, greedily, and with a resolve that made him come to life. 3:26 am or not, he was walking into a new life; one that he was ready to embody with all that he was. He swam around her body, nudging her thighs apart, and kissing her deep. She, in a moment of responsibility, reached over to the dresser, searching in the dark for the little plastic wrapper. Nothing. “Damien…Baby, wait.” He laughed through the kiss. “Dammit!” “I think I have a spare in my coat. Hurry back.” Damien grabbed his sweat pants, and ran downstairs like a teenager. Fumbling through the dark, he walked to where her coat was draped over the back of the couch. He checked each pocket, three times, and found nothing. Her purse. Her purse sat on the kitchen table, half open from when she dug through it to find pictures of her nephews at Disneyland. Damien ran to it, hoping that he would find them there. At this point, he was willing to use the emty M&M’s wrapper that she had stuffed in there. Then Damien noticed something else. It was a small white envelope with his name on it. Curious, and with a sense of mischeif, he took it out. It was a small sidetrack, but he had the rest of his life to get back to other business. He then heard her walk down the steps, calling his name, and asking him where he had disappeared to. Sylvia tied back her hair and steped into the doorway of the kitchen holding the package, and calling him back upstairs with a wide smile. The smile faded quickly when she saw him holding the letter. “ No, Damien….” Damien turned and looked at her, smiling, but now confused. “ I was was going to leave it for you to read in the morning. Please…not like this.” Damien looked at the letter, and then at her. Her eyes were cast down, and she pulled her tee shirt down, feeling indecent. He dropped the letter on the counter, and walked to her, taking her face in his hands, and kissing her. She pulled away. “ I can’t anymore.” His mind felt like a bundle of stripped wires. He reached out to her, and she lifted the letter, placing it back into her bag. “ Sylvia. What’s going on?” He took it back immediately. “You know what? It doesn’t even matter. I don’t care. Baby, let’s just go up stairs and sleep. In fact, baby, let’s sleep late.” Sylvia looked at him, and understood his meaning. Tears welled in her eyes, and Damien was ready to cry with her. He was so fucking happy, and it was so fucking obvious that he hated himself for waiting this long to realize it. He reached for her, and she stepped away again. “Damien…I’m sorry.” She wiped away a tear, and then opened her purse, pulling out a small plastic bag. In it was a gold band, and another ring with a modest diamond. Damien’s vision went blurry as she slid it onto her left hand, and then brought her hands up to her face and cried into them. Rage and confusion and disbelief overcame him, and Damien swung his fist hard into the cabinet, breaking it off the hinges. “What the fuck were you thinking?” “Stop it! I don’t know. Okay? I don’t know what the hell I was thinking. I just knew that I wanted to see you. I wanted to be with you one last time. I knew that I wanted to pretend one last time before I accepted that everything was different and we could never have what I wanted. That’s as far as I thought it out. I didn’t want to hurt you, but I knew that I couldn’t wait for you any more.” “So you just hopped on the first guy that came along after me? That’s pathetic!” Sylvia’s eyes widened at the words, and Damien hated himself. “David was not the first guy that came along. He was the first guy who was willing to be there for me, unconditionally, and without asking me to share him with anyone else. I met David, and I fell in love with him because I knew that when he and I were together, I was his priority, and it was ME who he wanted to be with.” “Then why did you come out here? Why did you agree to even talk to me again? You’re a married woman now and you had no fucking right being with me, or hiding this from me. You needed only say the word.” Sylivia buried her head into her hands again, crying harder. “No! Don’t you dare shed a fucking tear. You used me. You lied to me. So please spare me the dramatics. Why are you here, Sylvia?” “Because he isn’t you.” It came out a whisper, and Damien was disarmed. His anger washed away in a moment of pure, unquestionable pity. He tried to remain angry, but felt like a hypocrite. “I came here because I knew that this would be the last time.” “How long?” Sylvia looked at Damien through hollow eyes. “We were married in August.” Damien took it in, and shrugged. “That means that when you and I met at the Oasis…” She nodded. “And that also means that everytime we spoke on the phone, and flirted with the idea of giving it another shot…” “I knew that it was all talk, Damien. I knew damn well that you could never walk away. You have spent your entire life in that wrestling ring. Like you always reminded me: you were a wrestler before we met, and would be long after I was gone.” The remark, said often in the depth of his anger, stung him. He would not begin to speculate how it must have stung her. “So what was tonight?” “It was goodbye.” “And what if I didn’t want to say goodbye? What if I was ready to put everything aside for us? What if?” Sylvia wiped away the last of her tears, and looked at Damien, searching for any sign that he was being more than hypothetical. When she saw it, she chuckled painfully. “I guess I should have given you more of a choice. I suppose I had spent enough time convincing myself that you had already settled the issue, that I literally eliminated the possibility.” “That wasn’t fair.” A tear rolled down his face, and the sight broke her heart. She walked to him, and kissed him hard on the lips. Despite his every desire, his every instinct, and the swelling love he was trying to understand, he allowed himself to be kissed without kissing her back. Her decision had been made. Damien sat on the couch, and didn’t sleep at all. When the sun came up, and she came downstairs, she was fully dressed. She walked to where he was sitting, and placed a cup of green tea in front of him. “Damien, I hope you can one day understand. I hope you can one day forgive me. I know I was wrong in everything I did. I know that. My only defense is that I never set out to hurt you. I was being selfish. For that, I am willing to let you go and hope that you will not be angry with me. Please, try hard to remember that I love…loved…you.” Sylvia was putting on her strong face. He knew it. It was the façade she hid behind everytime she backed out of a promise. It was the defense she put up whenever he seemed to choose being The Latin Assassin when all she wanted was Damien, her husband. Damien Cruz, who had sold his soul to the world in that ring, refused to be angry at her for doing what he had been doing all along. He turned and faced her, seeing her for the first time for who she was. She was not the angelic vision of patience. She was not the celestial care-taker. She wasn’t the guiltless virgin wife or the beautiful healer or the long-suffering mistress. She wasa woman who wanted to have it all. At the very least, she was more like him than he had ever realized. She rose, and traced a finger along his chin, committing it to memory for the last time..She hefted her bag, and walked to the door. Damien thought of a million things to say, a million of which would have brought her back. A million of those things would have stilled her hand on the doorknob, silenced her doubts, and made her other life disappear. There were a million things that he could have said to take back wht he had lost. He knew that the words would have come so easily. But as she walked out of his life he remained silent, knowing that she deserved more than words.
