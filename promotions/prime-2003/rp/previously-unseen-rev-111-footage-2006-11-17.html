Date: 11/17/06 Oxios: Ha! Vangelus Olsig blinked at the approach by Sky High Oxios. Oxios: I ate 20 pies before you! So that's how he walked up -- with red, purple, and orange filling sauce and pie crust all over his face. He didn't say hello or bow respectfully or nothin'. Just sauntered his narrow ass into the scene, like a sex machine (ah, James Brown... we miss you). And as Vangelus Olsig was just minding his business, feasting upon the virgin flesh of a baby girl and watching his favorite syndicated television show Sliders... Or whatever he does for fun... The Whole Freaking SHO made his presence known, and it was quite unwelcome. Then again, a lot of wrestlers find his presence unwelcome. They're mean. Olsig: Of course you won, you didn't tell me until now. Not that I'd participate anyway, foul glutton. Triumphant laughing came from The Mouth of the South (of Canada). Oxios: You're just upset that I'm the winner. Face it, I'm better than you, I'd have beaten you in the GTT, and now I beat you in pie-eating. You're upset, you're jealous, you're pwned. That's when he saw the baby flesh and TV. Oxios: And come on dude... Sliders? What the hell is wrong with you? Ignoring that last part, because really Olsig was just standing there minding his own business and I filled in those fallacious details about what he was doing (and damn you, SHO, for using that), the former Intense Champion gotthis party started and fired back at his "challenger." Olsig: Jealous? Of you? That would be as the king being jealous to his court jester. No, I'm generally annoyed by your very existence, though in this case I'm elated that you lacked the foresight to schedule this AFTER your match. On cue (seriously, he was just waiting on the side for a good line to come in on), the guy in charge of making sure that everybody reports for their match walked up. Stage Manager: 2 minutes 'til your match with Hoyt Williams, Mr. Oxios! Oxios: .... Olsig: I recommend bringing your own mop. And some sanitary wipes. And just like old segs from RPW, only in the opposite way now, all those requisite black guys (as well as Dr. Gregory House) who were on Oxios's side were now forced to go "OH SNAP!" at Van's ill-planned attempt to get one over on Vangelus Olsig. And now you know the rest of the story. ......... And Yaph-- nah, that joke's getting old now.
