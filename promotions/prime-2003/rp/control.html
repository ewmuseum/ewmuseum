Date: Couple of em December 11th, 2002 Time: Following Devin Shakur’s fall Parting is such bitter sorrow for Devin Shakur, which is why he decided to take his own life by plummeting twenty five feet down onto concrete. Now he is in a place unlike any he has ever experienced before… He can see her radiant green eyes full of exuberance and life staring back at him… He can feel the warmth of her touch on his inert skin… Devin Shakur does not like anybody else having control over his life for even a facet of a second. Nobody can intrude into the world that he is in now… He can feel the rush of ecstasy going through his body at a breakneck pace when their lips meet. He can feel the beating of her heart against his ear… He can see her body rising and falling at a comfortable pace… She has life… She has life… Kimberly Crawford is with him and Devin’s happiness cannot be contained. They are reunited in death... The liveliest are the ones who have no barricades, no borders, and no boundaries to stand in between them. Devin can begin to like this place… Because somebody amputated their chances of euphoria without mercy All of the cuts on her face are no longer… She is in the purest form that God could have created… An angel… His angel… Now nothing can come between them… Devin Shakur can say he has won the battle against her killer by sacrificing himself. Their love will last forever… Only in fantasy …A bright light emanates from an enigmatic source that beckons to Devin.. The force that it contains is trying to pull on him… He begins looking around frantically… "NO!" Devin shouts at the top of his lungs, looking for something to grasp a hold of. He then reaches out to grab the hand of his beloved wife to keep him in his own personal heaven… Only to find Kimberly pull her hand back, much to the shock of Devin who is now being pulled closer and closer to the light. "WHY? WHY?" Devin once again screams at the top of his lungs, now only mere feet from the light. "It’s not time for you yet, Devin." Kimberly says in a quiet tone "You still have much to do…" "I’M USELESS WITHOUT YOU, KIMBERLY! DON’T DO THIS TO ME!" Devin says while staring into her green eyes. "I’ll be waiting for you Devin…" "KIMBERLY!" KIMBERLY! "I love you" Kimberly’s angelic voice emits before fading completely away from Devin’s eyes. "STOP THIS! I DON’T WANT TO GO BACK!" Devin pleas, attempting to grasp serenity for even another nanosecond. Life wants to play another round with Devin Shakur… Beep… Beep… "He’s back…" An unknown voice says. Devin Shakur knows exactly where he is and he does not like it… …In a hospital… …He has been brought back into a cold world where he had no real friends… …And no real purpose… "Devin, can you hear me?" The same unknown voice inquires, now staring down at Devin with a look of concern on his face. Devin cannot speak back to him because his face has been mutilated by the fall, but the beeping ensures that the question does not need to be answered. The only question Devin can bother asking himself is… Why? He now has to re-enter hell. December 12th, 2006 Location: Pullen Park, Raleigh North Carolina Time: 3 AM If life was going to deal Devin Shakur the hand that it was currently dealing him, then he was going to fight back. Devin would not succumb that easily to defeat after all that life has given him so far. This would be just another battle that Devin would have to fight through... And he knew just the people to give him the upper hand. He is out here in a public place in hopes that the braintrust, whoever they are, cannot follow him. Hell, it is the dead of night and he is in a park, he is hoping that nobody followed him or knows that he is out here. He had run a risk by keeping his headlights off the entire way and going every scenic route that he knew just to avoid detection. Devin had asked to borrow one of his former co-workers cellular phones for the evening, but he had no intention of returning it. He would say that he misplaced it, and he would buy them a new one. If someone found out that he called these people, Pandora’s Box would be opened, and Devin would be forced to spend the rest of his days back in prison. Their escapades are far more known around the state, hell the country for that matter, than those of the braintrust. They were public figures just like Devin Shakur was, and all of them had some involvement in the same wrestling business that Devin Shakur is currently involved in. Devin was also one of two people that these people gave their new phone number to. He was told to call them if he ever had a problem...And he definitely has one. They were no where near the United States because every agency was looking for them from the CIA, to the FBI, the NSA, all the way down to the Raleigh police department since four of their own were just a few of the victims that are in the ground now because of these people. Devin decides that he could wait no longer. He would soon have to play the game of strategic warfare, and he is not an expert in that department… These guys sure as fuck are though. Devin starts to dial the numbers on the cellular phone, the lump in his throat getting larger with each finger pressing down on each number. Once he finally is done, he stares down at the phone for a long second before pressing ‘Talk’ "This is it Devin" He quietly says to himself "Gotta do what you gotta do." Once he hears the ringing sound coming from the phone, Devin feels his hand starting to shake, and he almost drops the phone because of it. He used to know these people very well but he does not know what to expect once a voice on the other end picks up. "How did you get this number?" A voice riddled with some aging says. "Christian..." Devin says, having a hint of familiarity with the tone. "Devin? Devin Shakur, holy antichrist, we never actually expected you to call." Christian, apparently being his name, says, still with a bit of shock in his voice "How the hell you been?" "Not good man, not good," Devin says before letting out a long sigh. "What's the matter? Christian says, taking an optimistic tone "You sold all of your stock in Apex Technological, made yourself a shitload of money, and now you are going to the wrestling business. What's not to like? You are going to be fucking famous, man." "You and I both know that fame has its pros and cons. Look what it did to you." Devin replies back, his voice now becoming a bit calmer. "I don't necessarily think that fame did that to us." Christian quips "That was moreso what happens when you fuck with me and my crew." Devin has nothing to reply back with. "So why are you calling me in the dead of night? Christian inquires before taking a pause "It's what 3 or 4 in the morning there? You should be sleeping or watching some videos on those PRIME wrestlers you are going to be facing." "There's a reason..." Devin says, trying to think of the right way to word this "That I'm in that organization and that I'm no longer the CEO of Apex." "And that reason being…" Christian inquires. Devin takes a deep breath "Well you know how I used to invest some of your guys money on the side sometimes whenever you wanted to make some quick scratch?" "You done did well with that." Christian says before the sound of ice hitting together indicates he is taking a sip of drink. "Well there was a group that wanted me to invest a hundred grand of theirs on the side, real secretive about it. They sent people to my office one day with a briefcase, and told me that if I did not invest this wisely, that they would deal with me accordingly-" Devin says, wanting to continue but being interrupted. "You had to do something right, you aren't dead." Christian says before once again pausing to take a sip of his drink. "The only reason that I'm not dead is because I had to beg for my life. I saw a couple of stocks that looked really hot so I threw their money into those, and well in the next couple of weeks they took a nosedive. Then when I'm in a hotel, somebody knocks me out, I wake up and I'm in the middle of a fucking warehouse with a guy standing over me. Soon as he sees me recuperating, he starts to bash me in the head with the gun, telling me how I'm as good as dead. He cocks the gun back and I go into panic mode. I said that I would do anything-" Once those words leave Devin’s mouth, he wishes that he had not of said them. He can only hope Christian is in a good mood. "Why the hell would you..." Christian says with discontent streaming from his voice. He does not sound like the man to negotiate. "Never mind, I'll ask when you are done. Keep going. You said you'll do anything." "Yeah" Devin continues "So he brings me into another room with a spotlight fixated on me and three voices coming from the far back of the room, two girls and a guy, telling me how I'm going to become an asset to them. They then go on and tell me that I'm going to have to join this wrestling organization called PRIME because they have somebody who has done far worse things to them than I have. They tell me that I have to keep tabs on this guy, real close. Then when the time is right, they want me to take him out." "I assume by calling me that you agreed to this in full." Christian says. "That I did...And you know..." Devin struggles to finish his sentence. "I know you aren't the killing type Devin." Christian affirms, taking control of the situation "You might want to, might think it, might say you'll do it, but you really won't. You've tried before, but you just can't fathom taking another person's life unless they have done you significant harm. Shit, s'why you never made it past the first level of my group." "Yeah, but that was the reason that I got put in a fucking max security prison." Devin says, now gaining some confidence in his being "Just because I was an honorary member and couldn't even finish that one guy off. The cops would have thrown anybody, even the fucking doorman on the closed meetings, into Central from your group. I just happened to be out robbing people trying to survive and I had ties to your group. Lose, lose situation at the time." "Yes, but you were a misguided little lad back in those days." Christian adds "Prison was probably better for you anyway, made you into a man. If you stayed around me and my boys any longer, who knows what would have happened to your mind." Devin gives a silent nod, "Suppose you are right, though you were able to raise Chris, Brandon, Matthew, Rachel, Charlie, and Jason when they had no where else to go when you weren't even 21." "They are my blood, Jason is Chris’s best friend, and I didn't get them involved like you. I would have gone down the Ganges burning rather than let them deal with the business. As I said, you were misguided and a lost soul. It was a learning experience for you. Shot you to the top of a company in three fucking years, not many people can say that with how deep the pool was at Apex." Christian says with some pride in his voice. "That is true also." Devin agrees. "Alright back to my earlier question, so what is the purpose of calling me? I don't think you are calling me at 3 AM to catch up." Christian says, getting back to business. Here goes nothing "...I need you and your people to do some digging on these people that are trying to make me their personal mercenary..." Devin reluctantly answers back. "That's code for get rid of them isn't it?" Christian says, now with some invigoration in his tone. He likes the sound of death. "My life was almost perfect...The only thing that I needed to do was to find Kimberly's killer and my life would have been complete. I could have faded away into the sunset a happy man..." Devin says, trailing off into his own thoughts of what that could have been like for him. "Somewhat just like us." Christian chimes back "We got rid of all the problems and now we are living in paradise...But I suppose since we've rolled for a long time, not to mention that Jason and Chris would flip if I turned you away, that we can deal with it. You have to make sure though that you don’t tell anybody else about this. I don't want this shit coming back to us." "Trust me, nobody is going to know." Devin says, now breathing a sigh of relief because he’s gotten Christian to agree to the mission. Christian’s mind now starts going to work "Alright, so we are looking at three people...Possibly more. You woke up in a warehouse right?" "Yes" Devin answers. "Two women and a man...What do you remember from the conversation? Did you get a make on them?" Christian inquires. Devin mulls the question over for a second. "Well the stocks hadn't plummeted that long and they had somebody on my ass quickly. They also said that they knew all of my dirty little secrets and they could tell me what really happened to my father." "Hmm...And not many people know about that so they have to at least have a rep..." Christian says, still drenched in thought at the same time he is speaking. "I know a couple of their stories too. They are about as bad as you guys. I’ve heard a bit more about them ever since you guys left town back in June." Devin adds. "Could you make their faces at all, what about their voices?" Christian asks before taking another sip of his drink. "Did not see their faces, but their voices weren't hidden. Two of them sounded in state, and one of the women sounded Californian." Devin answers back, going into a bit of thought himself. Christian takes one last sip of his drink and starts talking, "...Hmm...I might have an idea as to who you are talking about. Alright, from what you have told me I can tell you a couple of things just in general: They are running a public business, it is legit, they are figureheads, they have a lot of money and power, people at their disposal obviously, and they are working for somebody above them. Having three different people as the boss usually does not work out that well..." "So you think you can stop them?" Devin asks, trying his best to mask the jubilation in his voice. Christian chuckles at the question "I don't think I can, I know I can. If they are working in state, the same state that I still have a shitload of people rolling through, then that is their biggest mistake. Since the only thing that my group is doing right now is just sitting back and collecting the money, I'll tell them to make this a high priority." "How long will it take?" Devin questions, before looking over his shoulder to make sure that nobody is around. "It's not going to happen over night that much I can tell you. Are they supposed to give you anymore information any time soon? Christian questions. Devin thinks for a quick second "I assume they are going to give me the information on the guy that they want me to take out soon." Christian is about to bring this one home, "Alright, what I'm going to do is this: I will have my boys do some preliminary sweeps through the area just to pick up some information. They'll relay it back to me. Once the three of them give you the information on their boy, call me back up and I should be able to tell you who you are up against. If we have to go bigger to get information based off of that, Jason and Rachel got international connections practically everywhere, Chris still has a lot of connections throughout the States, Matthew and Brandon can get some business-related connections if they have to, so we'll have you covered on all bases." "I appreciate it" Devin says with satisfaction. "So have you been able to go back to your place since this happened?" Christian asks, now just making sure that he has covered all of the bases. "I haven't stayed there more than a couple of hours at a time and I've been packing things up for the jet. I'll probably have to live there for a while won't I? Devin pessimistically says, he really does love the mansion. "They've probably bugged the crap out of the place, got some shit on your cellular phone...You got somebody else's to make this call right?" Christian says, a bit of worry in his voice. "Right" Devin affirms. Christian can breathe easy now. "Ok, good. Yeah, from my experience on this they are going to want to be in control over the situation completely, hence why I asked about the bugs." "They also said that if I thought about going outside the parameters that they would start dropping bombs." Devin adds on. "Yeah, they've got your shit bugged. Just make sure to keep secure lines of communication and to keep yourself on the move. You'll have to start going where PRIME goes at some point, so do that. When do you think you can get back to having even a remote time to rest?" Christian inquires, and the sounds of writing on a notepad are heard coming into Devin’s ear. Devin stands up and starts to get ready to move "Well I'm not supposed to start until the New Year, and their next Pay-Per-View is going to be in March." "We should have shit covered by then, bank on it. Call back once you get some more information." Christian says, like he is about ready to jump on the phone the next second and make some calls. "Will do...Thanks, Christian, I really appreciate it." Devin says, now walking over toward where his car is. "Don't mention it. We'll have your life back to normal before you even know it." Christian says before hanging up the phone. And just like that, with the click of the phone... War has just been started Devin Shakur can smile because now he once again has control over the situation...Every aspect of it. He concludes the moment by dropping the phone down on the pavement, getting into his car, starting it up, and running the cellular phone over before backing out and pulling away from the park. The evidence has been destroyed.
