The Development of Habits and Characters

6 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07-06-2008 "...So you run and you run to catch up to the sun but it's sinking..." - 'Time' by Pink Floyd His sunglasses had an orange tint that contrasted harshly against his pale, albino features. At that moment, Rhett Locke could've cared less. His thoughts, however erratic and paranoid were focussed squarely on PRIME. This was the break he'd been looking for the past few years. How quickly he had stumbled. Six years ago he was the World Champion of Old School Wrestling. A federation in which he was one of the main reasons it became the legendary wrestling company it was. At 30 years old, this would probably be his last endeavor in the world of wrestling. His last chance to make something of himself. The sun was still bright in his eyes. A cigarette dangled, almost unheld entirely between his lips. He took it between two slender, pale digits and flicked it to the ground. Exhale. What a fucking day. He opened the door to his little studio apartment. Once he had a house, a big ass house. Before he went crazy again... Stepping in he looked over the garbage, clothes and alcohol bottles (some empty, some full.) He went straight for his couch and removed an envelope from his pocket. Emptying the contents onto a small mirror resting on the coffee table, he multi-tasked and pulled a razorblade out of a box of 500 sitting by the mirror. He never understood why people had such a problem-- It wasn't even physically addictive! AND he was buying the high-priced kind! The good kind. He smelled it and his eyes rolled into the back of his head for a pleasurable but split second. It had that coke stink. You know that your coke is good when it holds just a slight tint of yellow. As he sat there, cutting away at the rocked up yet soft pebbles of powder he could not help but grin at it. It was a beautiful sight, three big gaggers waiting to be inhaled. He had an entire cocaine kit right there in his ratty apartment. Laying by the box of blades was a little clear package with a few different cut up straws inside, each serving a particular purpose. After tucking his long, blonde hair behind his ears he pulled out the regular cut straw. Placing it in his left nostril and gingerly placing a finger against his right, his head went down quickly and he inhaled deep... ------------------------------------------------ Locke's body was shivering a little as he opened the door to the adjacent apartment. A big, at least 300 lb. black man was sitting in a chair watching the Braves get their asses stomped by the Phillies. He turned quickly to Rhett, an angry look on his face. "Knock on the GOD_DAMN door. God damnit. I told you about that shit. Just 'cause a nigga aint got no lock don't mean he aint gonna put a cap in your fuckin' ass!" Rhett grinned, not saying anything for a moment. His sunglasses hid his dilated pupils. The guy in the chair eased up rather quickly, a smile spreading across his face as well. "Pop a squat, nigga. I know whatchu need..." It was funny how a simple smile on Rhett's face could say everything he wanted to. "Yes you do, Cluster." Rhett never asked Cluster if that was his God given name... it never really came up. Soon Locke had two white lines placed in front of him. Cluster offered him a rolled up dollar bill. Rhett only shook his finger and pulled out the same straw from earlier. For someone who's life was ever changing, he kept certain things obsessively consistent. "It's lucky, you understand." Cluster grinned, "Stupid ass." Rhett snorted it. He sat up, head craned back with the straw still in his nose. He continued snorting until it completely cleared his nostril. He coughed a bit. "Yeah. That initial drip is killaaaa." Rhett didn't say anything. Cluster continued. "You know I got that persian..." Persian? Fuck. "Naw. No thanks." Cluster laughed, "You don't like that herr-ron? Butchu Rhett Locke... Shit, nigga I knew you's a bitch. I knew it the whole time. 'EEEEY, LOOK AT THIS BITCH SITTIN' ON THIS COUCH RIGHT HERE!" Nothing like a drug dealer peer pressuring you to get hooked. "If I didn't have anything to do... man, you know I would." "Well if if was a fifth, nigga--" "Yeah, we'd all be drunk." Cluster smiled, cutting out a line of brown powder and snorting it up himself with the afore mentioned bill. "So, what do you have to do that's so fuckin' important?" "I have to catch a plane." "Oh, my bad." Cluster said in quite possibly the most sarcastic fashion he could muster. "I forgot you a wrestler and shit again." Cluster tossed a full prescrption bottle Rhett's way. "Call it a gift, nigga. You always straight with me, so..." Rhett grinned. "You're a good guy Cluster." "Don't go tellin' people that shit." "I don't tell anyone about that time you got too drunk and pissed all over your bed." Cluster frowned, "Why you gotta bring shit up?" "Just sayin' man, you woke up covered in piss." Cluster couldn't help but laugh. "What! I'm a rich millionaire with two beds!? Now go catch ya plane... Bitch." ------------------------------------------------ He lied to Cluster a little bit. True, he had to catch a plane but there were other matters at hand as well. Laura. They laid there under the sheets. His pale skin, tattooed and scarred looked dark under the blacklight. Which was strange to him, because the white sheets glowed. Her hair was red and he his fingers ran through it. She liked him cause he was dark and twisted. He liked her because she'd fuck him. She kissed his lips. He barely kissed back. He was coming down from the drugs detailed before, however he didn't want to do anymore before he got off the plane to wrestle. Rhett could party a bit before his match maybe, if everything happened on schedule. "I'm gonna miss you Rhetty." Cheesy little bitch. "Yeah." It wasn't that he was a total dick and refused to get close to someone because he was such a man or some macho bullshit like that. It's just sometimes, things are more complicated and you have to protect your feelings. "You probably need to go soon, Baby." She said in a girly-fake pity voice. "My husband's going to be home soon." Ugh. Steve. Fucking... Steve. Steve was an all around nice guy. He woke up early Monday-Friday and would work 6:00am-6:00pm to support his family of four. He never complained, never even argued. Rhett always thought that's why Laura liked him so much. Steve was just so boring. And Rhett... well, he was Rhett. A mystery. Every bitch loves a good mystery. He got up and threw on his clothes. On TV he dressed like a rockstar. In real life he was uniform. White hanes t-shirt, blue jeans and sandals. "Yeah. I don't need to be making an album in here." "Huh?" Rhett frowned. "R. Kelly?" "OOOOOOH, 'Trapped in a Closet'. I get it." She said, forcing a laugh that was disgustingly fake. God damn whore. Soon, he drove off in a 2000 Dodge Dakota on his way to Hartsfield Airport. It was a white truck with pink and green decals on the side. It was dented all to hell from several accidents. None of which he considered his fault. Whatever. It got him where needed to be. ------------------------------------------------ On the airplane he was rather calm. His skin always caused a few different looks from people but that was to be expected. When he was in OSW, he handled it by drinking rather heavy amounts of Nyquil and falling asleep. But in his older age he let it go. Truth be told: Though he was only 30, he felt like an old man. That was the problem. That was why he was so relieved to be wrestling again. He worked so hard to get that World Title run... and when he finally got the ball, he fuckin' dropped it. This old man won't let that go again. Ever. "...The Sun is the same in a relative way but you're older. Shorter of breath and one day closer to death..." -'Time' by Pink Floyd.
