Date: June 6, 2007; June 15, 2007 Bob Seger's whiskey-smooth, melancholy voice slipped through the speakers in the cab of the midnight blue Ford Super Duty, the tinning of the cymbals falling into rhythm with the beating of Troy's heart. She drove through the darkness on a road she had traveled so few times, yet knew like the back of her hand; far past the city lights of downtown Houston and the hubbub of civilization, following a wooden fence and an endless line of trees that were well off the beaten path. She'll turn left a mile up the road and follow a gravel path to the ranch her sister, a city girl if there ever was one, now called home. The tires kicked up stones as she made the swing in the over-sized truck, careful not to accidentally clip the fence as she navigated the beast through the opening. In the distance, lights twinkled from the inside of the main house, awaiting her return. Her foot, acting on instinct, pressed the gas pedal further down. The Ford roared ahead, cutting through the inky blackness like Alto Reed's saxophone solos. Here I am On the road again... ~**~**~**~**~ "Should've known you'd still be awake by the time I got back here. You're home, for once. Why aren't you sleeping?" Dan Ryan shot Lindsay a crooked grin while he calculated the exact moment to remove the teapot from the stove before the shrill whistle woke up Alaina and Cecilia. Troy sat in the kitchen, throwing proper posture to the warm Texas wind as she slouched over an empty mug, head in her hands. "I'm not sleepin' because I'm not tired," he replied in the know-it-all tone that made him one of the best promo-cutters in the industry. He looked inside the teapot at the water, which has almost reached its boiling point. "I don't know why you wouldn't just let me nuke the water in the microwave, Dan. Not that I don't appreciate the hospitality and all..." "If it didn't decide to stop working earlier tonight, I'd let you just 'nuke the water.' But seriously, listen to yourself. You go off and win another title and you think you can be Miss Bossy in my home. Your sister's got that territory well marked, Lindz." "Alright fine. Boil the fucking water, Danny Homemaker. Want me to get you an apron too?" Lindsay smiled while Dan shot her the finger, lifted the teapot from the stove and poured a steady stream into her cup. "Thanks." He pulled a chair out and took a seat next to her. "Uh huh. See if I let you stay here the next time some other company has a show in Houston." "Pffft," she smirked. "Like my sister would deny you the privilege of seeing me more often than you already do." "And what a privilege it is indeed..." "My sarcasm detector just went off. Louder than a teapot, that's for sure." She stirred in powdered Chai mix until it had fully dissolved, then took a cautious sip. "You're due for a last stand soon, aren't you?" Lindsay quirked an eyebrow in surprise and gave her brother-in-law a sidelong glance. "You actually watched the show tonight?" "I may have stolen a peek while looking over contracts as Alaina watched in the den." "Uh huh." "Just a peek though." "Right." She took another sip. "You know me, always have to get involved in something just to shut up a bunch of pricks." Dan smiled and patted her on the back. "You do it so well, though." "This'll all be over soon enough," Troy said, eyes narrowing out of habit. "Silver's not going to know what the fuck hit him come the Seventeenth..." There I am Up on the stage... ~**~**~**~**~ "You want another drink?" Killean asked as he made his way to the kitchen. His half sister Cadence was sitting on a chair in the living room. "Sure... uhhh you know this still seems a little awkward," she said, looking out the window into the streets of Winnipeg. "You know, this being my first time here and all. I only met you a short few weeks ago." Killean had to agree. It was weird. He wasn’t even sure he actually wanted to deal with the situation at hand but he knew that there were people who needed him now. His father’s funeral was back on May 25th, but it seemed like it was only yesterday. To him, Cadence seemed like a troubled soul, like a puppy who was lost and was trying to get back home. He had to be there, mostly for her. Sure her mother was alive still, more than he could say for his own mother. But she needed more than that. She admitted to Killean that she had only one serious relationship with a guy and he turned out to be abusive. Since then, she never had that kind of companionship. Killean needed to be with her, to help her get past the absence of their father so she could move forward with her life. He needed to be there, like a brother should be. "You’re gonna be around here a lot when I am finished with PRIME this weekend. You know I’m here for you and your mother when I’m back around here." What was going on with him? A few months previous, he would have told anyone associated with his father to rot. But now, now was different for some reason. "About that..." Cadence started as she got off the chair and walked to the kitchen. Killean was pouring her another drink. "You don’t need to quit PRIME for us. We barely know you and you’ve been in wrestling almost all your life. In the last year or so, it was normal for Dad to have us all in the room Wednesday nights to watch PRIME. We were watching you." He almost dropped the bottle of cola on the floor. As he got his grip straight, he turned to look at Cadence, who was looking directly at him. He finished pouring the drink and put the bottle safely on the counter. He took a couple steps to Cadence and put both his hands on both her shoulders. He looked into her eyes and saw pure innocence. He must have had a look of surprise on his face. After all, he just heard that his father, his step mother and his half sister were watching him every Wednesday night in PRIME for the last year. His father had passed, but the old guy still had plenty of secrets up his sleeve. Meanwhile, Cadence tried to decipher what Killean was thinking about. He saw the innocence, and immediately thought of Summer. His actual sister. He never spoke much of her anymore but in private he missed her all the time. She had passed away years ago due to complications from cancer. He saw her in the eyes of Cadence. He didn’t believe much in religion or anything, but he saw that as a sign. He knew now, more than any other time, he would have to be there for Cadence. "I had no idea you guys watched, which mean now that I made the right decision. Don’t you see Cadence? You’ve known me for quite some time, indirect as it may be. But I’m your brother and I’m going to be here as your brother. You can count on that." He gave her a hug, feeling closer to her than he did before. He was sharing a moment with his sister when the phone rang. He released her and smiled as he walked over to pick up the phone. The club's number showed up on the call display. He answered but didn’t have time to say anything. "For fuck sakes you pig, you never told me you slept with Stacey again!" Vegas yelled. This was the last thing he needed at that moment. He sighed out loud and put his head in his hands. Meanwhile, Cadence was sneaking a little extra rum while Killean wasn’t looking. "Dude, listen, I was drunk in the bar. She came and picked me up. I was totally wasted and all I can remember is waking up, going to the bathroom and walking out. She was there in the bed naked under the covers. I passed out. When I came to, I made it blunt that she’d better get out." "Yeah, after you tapped her ass. I know you had a thing for her before but come on." He started laughing. It was all a rib and he wanted to laugh too, but he couldn’t bring himself to it. After all that Stacey had done in the past, he couldn’t laugh at anything involving her. "Yeah yeah, OK. You got the plans for the club?" "You bet." He replied. "But you’d better get a move on it. Your plane leaves in 2 hours and you need to stop here to get the plans first." "SHIT! I lost track of time! I’ll be there shortly." He hung up the phone and rushed into the bedroom. He grabbed his already packed gym bag and suitcase and made his way for the door. Cadence rushed out of the kitchen and met him. "I’ll be watching Sunday, Killean." He gave her a hug. He picked up his bags and opened the door. "I’m counting on it, Cadence. Stay here if you want. Your choice. I’ll see you when I get back Tuesday." She said goodbye and closed the door. He walked to the stairs and he suddenly stopped. He turned to look back at his door. He stood there for a moment, looking dumbfounded. Here he was, in the hallway of his apartment, getting set to leave the city for Oklahoma and Overkill. It was the last time he would be doing it. The next time he was at home, it would all be over. His career in PRIME was done. He would be a retired wrestling legend, at 29 years old. He stood, thinking of PRIME and then thinking of Cadence. He smiled. He knew he made the right choice. Here I go Playin' star again... ~**~**~**~**~ "Stupid plane, now I'm late..." Sirrajin strolled through the lobby of the hotel in somewhat of a panic. He was already forty-five minutes late and the clock never stops ticking. His rolling suitcase hit many bumps in the hard tile floor, causing it to try and alter his course. He came through the doors of the lounge, immediately looking around to find Lindsay and finally, he saw her sitting at a two person table near a window. He approached her and sighed audibly. "I know, don't say a word. The plane landed late," he said as he put his suitcase to the side and took a seat opposite the Queen of the Ring. "I hear cell phones are all the rage nowadays," came the quick retort. Troy plucked her phone off the tabletop and waved it in front of Killean's eyes, cracking a smile while his eyes rolled behind red-tinted lenses. "OK, I'm sorry. I mean, when a plane is having a small mechanical problem trying to land, you aren't supposed to use your cell phone. But I'll take the blame. I guess nothing stopped me from calling in the ten minute drive it took me to get from the airport to this seat," he smiled at her and ordered a scotch on the rocks from an arriving waiter. "Hey, all I'm saying is that it wouldn't have killed you to push a few buttons. But at least you made it in alright." Lindsay paused to take a sip of her water, then leaned back in her chair. "So, what's on your mind? You said you had some plans you wanted to show me?" Killean received his drink and took a nice long sip, savoring it inside his mouth before swallowing it down. He thought that it went down just a bit too easy but he shrugged that off and reached into the front pocket of his suitcase, removing a copy of some blue prints. He slapped them down on the table between them. "I figured I would ask someone besides Vegas or someone who worked there. What do you think of this set up?" He proceeded to point out all the new areas on the blue print of the new club he's working on with Vegas. "Something didn't gel with the stage being designed like it is, but I can't figure out what the heck is missing from it." "What kind of club is this supposed to be again? I heard rumors you ran a strip joint before." Killean laughed a loud, winning some unwanted attention from other patrons in the lounge. "Heh, a rumor? What is this... some Internet smark thing with your sources? Yeah, it used to be a strip club, but the place has been torn apart, waiting for my approval on the plans. It's going to be a new night spot. We're gonna have live bands from around the city and sometimes a big show. We're also gonna have a comedy night once a week and some other special events that we are working on." "Oh, I see," Troy said with a smirk, "so you're going from the cliched 'wrestler-owned strip joint' to something less seedy? Bet you're still going to keep the skimpy clothes for the women, though. Am I right?" She took the plans to hand and started looking them over, feeling an incredulous look from Sirrajin seeping through the floor plans. He had observed her mannerisms and the tone of her voice when she talked and he sensed that something didn't feel quite right. But he decided to save it for a moment and address her other comment. "Kinda I guess. I talked with the girls and most of them wanted to be waitresses in the past, you know, something more down to earth than stripping for money. So in a recent meeting, I gave them carte blanche on what they wear. Hey, at least I'm kinda moving towards a more sensible environment!" He defended himself, something that he knew he didn't have to do, but he did it with a smile on his face. "Relax, Kill. I'm just ribbing you. And the problem with the stage is that it juts out too much. Look." Troy placed her glass and phone on the windowsill and laid the plans back out on the table. "This place isn't too huge to begin with and if you make the stage as big as it is here, you're not only cutting off walking space but you're cutting off dance space in case people want to get up near their tables or dance in front. Plus, you're pushing the tables further back towards the bar this way. You got a pen on you?" "Uhh, I don't think so. Wait, maybe..." "Nevermind. I got one." She pulled a Bic out of her purse's back pocket and starts drawing. "You've got all this space on either side of the stage going toward the sides. If you build it out this way instead of front-ways, you're going to give bands more room to move. You could even tier it a bit if you build it up and make it sturdy." "Geez, you should move to Winnipeg and run this place instead of me. It makes perfect sense and I just couldn't see it." Killean rubbed his hand through his hair and took another long sip from his scotch. "Thank you very much, Lindz. I just have to fax this back to Vegas after and they can start the work. Were you going to get something to eat?" Lindsay shrugged, replacing the pen. "I wasn't planning on it. I guess I could if you wanted company." "Of course I want company. Heck, I'm done after this show and I enjoyed spending the time with you, Matt and even Danny. It's been a hell of a ride. So consider this part of my retirement party." He smiled and took one last sip of his drink, draining it. He picked up a menu and noticed a little bit of a vacant look in her face as she did the same. He figured it was now or never. "Is something bothering you?" Troy let the menu fall away from her eyes and studies Sirrajin's face. "It's just now starting to sink in that you're really all done after this show." Killean nodded, looking down at the silverware on the table and the half full water glass sitting in front of Lindsay. He took off his sunglasses and put them on the table. His eyes then met hers. "It hit me a little bit when I left home. Cadence was telling me that I didn't need to do this. But I had to. It's just really hard to deal with. I have family again, it's kind of a shock and I'm still not sure if it's what I want. But Cadence was so... lost... when our father passed away. She didn't know what to do. She reminds me a bit of my other sister. Her name was Summer. But enough about that, why is it bugging you? Heh, I thought you'd be relieved to have the Canadian out of the picture?" He smiled brightly. "I can think of other Canadians I'd want out of the picture before you." The honesty floated across the table with ease, and Troy took a breath. "It's not really bugging me, per say, as much as it's something that's finally happening. You've given everyone enough time to prepare for it, so it's not like you're dropping the news like a ton of bricks right before you cut out of here, but I haven't really thought about it until now. You're talking about this club you're opening and your family and something clicked just now. This is really the end of the road for you and you've got your ducks all in a row." "Mmmmm duck..." Killean replied, more to himself than to Lindsay as he glanced down at the menu again. "I haven't had duck in a while. But seriously, I appreciate the honesty in the first statement, even though it is a rib against me. But don't consider it the end of the road for me. It's more like... moving off of a gravel road onto a freshly paved, smoother one. Yeah, I'm leaving something that I have been involved in for almost three years now. I have Desiree to look after, a business to help run and an extended family to get to know. But wrestling will always be around, it'll always be in my blood. So may someone help you should I ever decide to come back and tear that Universal Title away from you sweetheart." He laughed heartily. He took a sip of his water, waiting for the waitress to come back so he could order more alcohol. "The hard part is not leaving PRIME. It's leaving the people I have gotten to know. Matt will always be there, always has been. But there are some other people that I don't wanna lose touch with. People like..." he glanced back straight into the eyes of Lindsay, almost asking her about the future. He shifted uncomfortably in his chair. "... you." "Well, I'm not going anywhere, y'know." She gave him an amused look. "I'm just a phone call away, when you remember to pick it up and dial." He smiled again, wondering if he should avoid any further hinting. Sure he had some feelings for her, but he had no idea how she would take it. Last thing he wanted to do was create a sense of awkwardness that was unrecoverable. "Well, I can assure you, I'm going to be picking the phone up more often. I can't get enough of your wit. Not getting insulted by you day in and day out will start to make me suffer from withdrawals." He tapped her on her hand. She jerked a little in surprise, but he quickly withdrew after a couple taps. OK, now it turned awkward. Maybe he shouldn't have done that. Quick, recover you big lug. "You know, I've had the salmon skewers here before, you really need to try them." He hoped that did the trick. "We're in Oklahoma and you want me to have fish? You really do need to retire, because I think you've just gone crazy, Canuck." They both laughed at the comment, getting rid of the awkwardness quickly. They both leaned down to look at their menus again. Killean sighed quietly, glad that he was able to spend this time with Lindsay before the events on Sunday. It'd be his last time sharing the ring with her. It would be the last time sharing the ring with PRIME. It would be his last time sharing the ring with the world... for now. There I go Turn the page...
