Life, Death, Resurrection, Ascension?

17 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17th December 2005 Christ has died,Christ is risen,Christ will come again. He was burned,He returned,He will fight again. “A reading from the Holy Gospel, according to Mark…” As the snow fell unstoppably outside, the congregation of the St. Olaf Downtown Catholic Church in the heart of Minneapolis, Minnesota sat warmly huddled together in the midst of a heaving sermon in the build-up to Christmas. The stained windows glowed bright white, shedding the church in a cold glow, but Angus Marshall felt a warm vibration on his insides as he sat between his mother and sister in the second row of the church. “Immediately Jesus made his disciples get into the boat and go on ahead of him to Bethsaida, while he dismissed the crowd. Leaving them, he went up on a mountainside to pray…” Father Brennan was recollecting the story of Jesus Christ walking on water, probably the man’s finest moment before the Crucifixion, his words reverberated off the freezing walls like pebbles dashing across water, but Angus could not help but daydream about PRIME’s Great American Nightmare pay-per-view that he had already ordered for later that evening. “…When evening came, the boat was in the middle of the lake, and he was alone on land. He saw the disciples straining at the oars, because the wind was against them. About the fourth watch of the night he went out to them, walking on the lake.” Thrilled at the prospect of his returning hero Ignatius Lisieux battling his greatest foe to date, Ozric Mortimer, Angus was having severe difficulty concentrating on the words of his Priest, as was always the case on a cold winter’s morning. Granted, he was excited for the presents that Christmas would bring but he did often wonder if all of the Church beforehand was adequate punishment for that one day of joyous wonder. “…He was about to pass by them, but when they saw him walking on the lake, they thought he was a ghost. They cried out, because they all saw him and were terrified…” Awakening from his daytime slumber due to a sharp jab in his upper-arm from his menacing older sister, complete with braces and straggly, angry-looking red hair, Angus was eventually forced to listen to Father Brennan’s words, but then found himself once again unable to hold his attention down. He passed his eyes over the wondrous decoration of the church, the statues and the alter, the lights and the paintings on the windows and the walls… he had never noticed how attractive St. Olaf’s was. “Immediately he spoke to them and said, 'Take courage! It is I. Don't be afraid.' Then he climbed into the boat with them, and the wind died down. They were completely amazed, for they had not understood about the loaves; their hearts were hardened…” He dragged the balaclava from his head and ruffled his red hair around a little bit as his mother looked down on him with a glowing smile; Angus returned the pleasantry as a few local rogues pelted the Church window with snowballs, causing the sermon to be halted for a brief moment as the congregation muttered angrily between themselves. ”Let them have their fun, after all, it is Christmas time…” Father Brennan let out a hearty laugh as the congregation were silenced in agreement and Angus took a moment to consider the Irish-American Priest. A tall, strong-looking man with a receding grey scalp, dressed in traditionally lavish Roman Catholic garb, Brennan commanded the respect of the community - much like Ignatius Lisieux had commanded the respect of fans and peers alike, once upon a time. “When they had crossed over, they landed at Gennesaret and anchored there. As soon as they got out of the boat, people recognized Jesus. They ran throughout that whole region and carried the sick on mats to wherever they heard he was...” The young boy twisted and turned to find comfort on the cold oaken bench his butt was pressed against, but to no avail, it’s impossible don’t you know? He finally resigned himself to listening to the Gospel story of Jesus Christ, but deep-down he knew his mind would still much rather be taken away by some good old PRIME action… but that could wait, he supposed. “…And wherever he went - into villages, towns or countryside - they placed the sick in the marketplaces. They begged him to let them touch even the edge of his cloak, and all who touched him were healed.” Realising that the story was ended, due to the fact the whole congregation, young and old, rose to their weary winter feet as the organ piped-up, Angus had a prayer-book thrust in front of his face as he and the amassed voices chimed heartily in with the classic Christmas carol “Silent Night”. The Church seemed a far warmer place with dozens of voices chorusing in unison and Father Brennan smiled proudly at his community before him, until he settled back at his pulpit and prepared for the final part of the ceremony. “That was delightful, now we must march onwards with proceedings…” The rest of the Mass was something of a blur to Angus as he later found himself with a window to speak with the Priest as his mother and sister chatted away merrily with various friends and acquaintances. “How are ya, young Angus?” Father Brennan beamed brightly towards him… “Still watching that wrestling that you used to love so much?!” “Oh boy yeah, tonight it’s a pay-per-view, the Great American Nightmare! Ignatius Lisieux is back to fight the man who burned him on the cross and there’s Emilio Rage, Nova, Angelo Deville, Hoyt Williams… who is Jesus’ brother, you know!?” the young boys thoughts couldn’t keep up with his mouth when he talked PRIME, he was simply an enthusiast when it came to wrestling. “Jesus’ brother, eh? Well isn’t that something!” the Priest responded valiantly… “Yeah but he’s like an evil brother I think and I ain’t never seen the man walk on any water!” Angus chirped back. “That doesn’t surprise me, Angus… now you say this Ignatius fellow was burned on a cross? Seems awful similar to the story of Jesus, to me…” “Really?” enquired the young boy… “Well you know Jesus was persecuted all of his life by people trying to get the better of him and the only way they ever could was by nailing him to a cross and killing him… but he came back three days later in what we call “the resurrection” and then ascended to Heaven afterwards!” Brennan enthusiastically continued. “Wow, well I knew that… but I never noticed Ignatius was just like Jesus Christ! He came back recently too for what he calls “revenge” and “unfinished business”…” Father Brennan let out a chuckle, “Well it seems he’s at least partially like Jesus, as long as he fights for what is right and what he believes in. You seem to be quite taken by him.” “He’s the greatest, I saw him win the title in his sixth match right here in Minneapolis! He beat Zyfloi, Lance Jackson and even Tony Rolo!” enthused Angus. “Seems an awful lot like when Jesus walked on water to me, the most amazing thing people had ever seen, it made them believe in him… just like it made you believe in Ignatius when he won that match. Jesus hasn’t yet got “revenge” on his oppressors though… you say Ignatius has the chance to do that later on tonight?” said Brennan, seemingly being interested by the PRIME goodness. Angus let out a big smile, thrilled to be allowed to talk wrestling so much, “He’s fighting the man who burned him in a Last Man Standing match! Ozric Mortimer is his name - he’s like the devil, I HATE him!” “I see, I see…” before the Priest could respond he was cut off by an elderly group of ladies just gagging for their fix of Father Brennan, he was quite the stud with the older generation. Angus’ face fell, but Father Brennan slipped him a wink and thumbs-up to reignite the fire of smile as the young boy ran off to find his family. “Mom, Mom!” he shouted in her general direction, “I was telling Father Brennan about Ignatius and how he’s like Jesus and it looks like he’s going to watch the pay per view later on, I think he liked the sound of it, man that guy is REALLY cool!” Looking flustered by her son’s excitement, the mother let out an appreciative nod and waved towards her Priest as Angus’ sister sneered at him from beneath her black-rimmed glasses… “I’ll be sure to watch that Angus, we can talk about it next Sunday on Christmas!” the Priest hollered over as a look of disbelief spread across the mother’s and sister’s faces. “I told you so! Father Brennan loves PRIME now, all because of me!” and Angus revelled in his success all the way home… …The shocking image that ended ReVolution 50 is projected in black and white onto a huge screen in a large auditorium. In the picture there are flames licking at the toes of Ignatius Lisieux as he is strapped to a huge wooden cross with the Dark Age members looking up at him. A tear can be seen rolling down the cheek of Lisieux who has his face contorted in fear and anguish and his eyes held tightly shut as his life flashes through his mind. The auditorium’s previous lively banter descends into hush as people observe the image and take in what they see and a female speaker prepares her middle-aged self to give a lesson to the pupils amassed before her in the University of Alberta in Edmonton. She regards the image with a look of disgust and disdain before she begins addressing the students… “THIS is exactly the kind of image that we at the PTA are demanding be removed from international television in order to prevent our children from growing up well before their time.” Her voice is shrill and cold as she derides PRIME for literally stoking the fires of blasphemy with what happened to Lisieux over a year ago. “This blasphemous image, beamed directly around the world by an organisation affiliated with the equally moral-less Playboy TV, is exactly what we should pray that our children never have to witness, especially on free television broadcast.” The grey auditorium remains quiet for now as the students allow the female speaker to indulge in her crusade against professional wrestling and the apparent negativity that it creates within our global society. “One year ago this week, Ignatius Lisieux of PRIME was literally burned on a cross by men going under the aliases of Ozric Mortimer, Dani Furher, Shadow, Vampir Nosferatu and Tiamat: right here, in Edmonton, and the whole incident was broadcast on Playboy TV in the United States and around the world.” She tips her glasses back to the top of her nose as she flicks over a sheet of paper and continues to talk, “PRIME as an organisation received over five thousand complaints from all corners of the globe and agreed to eventually remove Ignatius Lisieux and the Dark Age from their company.” Some restless teenagers shuffle around in their seats as another image is projected onto the screen to keep their attention for a little while longer. This picture shows Ozric Mortimer’s new look on the day of his return to active competition in PRIME over a month ago. “Shown now is Nicholas Brocken, also known as “Ozric Mortimer”, who recently returned to television with PRIME, despite assurances to the PTA and various religious organisations that he would never be seen again. Mr. Mortimer is widely thought to be the main perpetrator in what was witnessed at ReVolution 50 and PRIME have gone back on their word that he would be released from the company and never reinstated.” She rolls her eyes a little and flicks over another sheet of paper before clicking a button on her laptop computer to show an image of Ignatius Lisieux returning last week in Lexington on ReVolution 84. A few comedy cheers ring out from inside the auditorium that causes a few muffled “shhh” noises and an angrily raised eyebrow from the lady speaker. “This image shows Ignatius Lisieux, also known as Richard Larkin in the past, just last week returning to television within PRIME in order to have revenge on Ozric Mortimer for the crucifixion a year ago. Now, although Mr. Lisieux is widely thought to be the victim in the whole matter, it was also agreed by PRIME and the PTA that he would not return to active competition with the company for at least a five year period in order to let the catastrophe that he helped to cause last December.” The PTA speaker looks angrily towards the auditorium as a few students begin to up and leave, despite urges from their usual lecturers not to. She clicks another laptop button as a picture of Toshiaki Motoki appears projected onto the screen - she regards the image with a frown and turns back to her pulpit to address her dwindling crowd… “Toshiaki Motoki, former PRIME owner and current PRIME adviser… who also recently returned to PRIME despite numerous calls for his eternal resignation from the PTA and various other welfare organisations. This is a face of evil and the man behind much of what PRIME does that the PTA and other groups deem to be seriously inappropriate.” Some members of the audience do actually jeer Motoki’s image, which actually brings a smile to the speaker’s face, which makes a welcome change… “Basically all I can ask is that you be aware of the reputation that PRIME as a company has and the reputation that the individuals I have mentioned have, especially on the day of a pay-per-view PRIME event, such as this. This concludes today’s lectures about the PTA and sensible censorship and I thank you all for taking the time to listen to what I have had to say… I now open the floor up to any questions you students may have…” A lot of hands are raised, from both the male and female variety and both the mature and regular students, the speaker looks towards a young lady with designer glasses and blonde pigtails… “Yes?” “Hypothetically, who would the PTA be rooting for later on when Ignatius Lisieux and Ozric Mortimer fight each other in a Last Man Standing match?” asks the blonde girl with a cheeky smile to boot… The guest speaker looks obviously flustered by the question as she re-adjusts her hair and glasses, “I should think that the PTA as a group would condemn such an event outright and side with nobody in the matter… next question, yes, you…” “What is it about professional wrestling that gets up the proverbial nose of the PTA so much?” asks a young man with messy brown hair and a pink tee-shirt… “Where can I start?” replies the lady speaker, “The glorification of human beings battling one another, the constant use of profane language, the sexual references, the drug references, the alcohol references, the blasphemy… the sadistic acts of violence, simply how can anybody regard this company as a good provider of entertainment!?” “But isn’t that what makes it all so great, the realism involved, the good versus evil… PRIME tells stories that have been told throughout the ages in new ways and with new characters and ideas? Toshiaki Motoki is actually something of an innovator…” continues the questioner as more and more of his peers begin to make murmurs of agreement… “The man is something of a lizard, he exploits other people to make himself money and he broadcasts his foul ideals to a worldwide audience with no fear of punishment or justice because of his and the company’s bank balance, he has been allowed to get away with too much for too long” she responds with vitriol. “Well why not just let Ignatius get his hands on him, then, an eye for an eye?” “I can assure you that violence is the absolute LAST method the PTA would advocate for use in dealing with PRIME and its employees, the whole idea is so ironic that it becomes preposterous!” A growing tide of support for PRIME emerges from the people within the auditorium and an older man, perhaps even a lecturer, wearing a black shirt with a dark blue tie smiles and waits for the microphone to hover above his head and then asks, “Who else here hopes that Ignatius Lisieux beats the hell out of Ozric Mortimer later tonight and drives him out of PRIME!?” Much of the auditorium joins the man in rising from their seats in uproar of cheering and agreement as the lady speaker looks on in horror at the scene unfolding before her. She rapidly bunches her clumps of paper together and begins to shut down the laptop as the auditorium is engulfed in an “Iggy” chant. A lot of people look around in disbelief at the scene as about a third of the attendees join in with the chanting and noise and finally a “PRIME” chant breaks out. The speaker turns her back fiercely on the crowd and heads for the exit of the auditorium, laptop and papers in tow, with a face of utter disbelief at what she had just witnessed. Lecturers and university staff all rise themselves to try and calm the scene as she leaves the room and things begin to return to normal, a janitor remarks to an eager student towards the highest entry point of the arena… “Do you suppose that there lady knew that she was in Lisieux country?” The student laughs back at the question and shakes his head with an amused smile. “I didn’t think so, either…” …”Welcome back to The Wrestling Point on TGWAN Kentucky, where we have just been looking at the strides made by GCW over 2005, now it’s time for our next “Wrestling Point” as we focus on the blood feud between Ignatius Lisieux and Ozric Mortimer over in PRIME which may or may not be settled at the Great American Nightmare later on.” A crackling radio buzzes and hisses at the back of a quiet Lexington bar, inhabited only by two elderly gentleman, one black, one white, and a miserable and portly barmaid. “Turn that damn radio up Goddamnit, they’re talking about PRIME…” the drunken white sloth shouts towards the barmaid, who turning away from her copy of the regional newspaper, flicks a couple of switches on the PA system to raise the volume levels of the wrestling talk show. “Why in the hell are you still watching that shit!?” his black associate asks, “I mean okay it was fun when we had the Rolo guy and the Troy guy and that Rock Startling guy, he represented, but who they got now? They ain’t got shit!” The white man shakes his head and takes another sip from his bottle of beer, “Man you’re missing out if you ain’t seen what’s been going down lately, there’s old faces popping up all over the place, it’s the Great American Nightmare later on you know? Damn man that’s gonna be one hell of a match rights there, that Ignatius fella against that giant Mortimer bastard.” “Well Johnny, I hate to say it but I can’t see anything other than an Ozric Mortimer win later on tonight, it took one punch, just one closed fist to knock Lisieux off his feet last week here in Kentucky… how can anyone expect Ignatius to go the distance with that monster?” “Jee-sus, they got guys who get knocked out with one punch there now? Damn I’m glad I ain’t watching no more…” the black gentleman uttered as he threw back another whiskey chaser and beckoned the barmaid for a new glass. The white man again shook his head as he scratched the collar of his faded green shirt, “You ain’t got no damn clue what you’re yakking about Claude. This guy Lisieux comes back from being burned at the damn cross after a year of no wrestling, the guy’s a mess, his heads a mess… he damn sure looks a mess…” “You saw a mirror lately?” “Shut your yakking… anyway, this guy is not right and then this monster blindsides him and busts him wide open, when the guy is in a pair of damn handcuffs!” “Is this brother a black brother? ‘Cos if you got a man getting whacked while wearing handcuffs I gots to give ya good odds that the brother is going to be chocolate.” Claude chimes in with an angry scowl… “No, you fool… he’s French-Canadian or some shit, but that ain’t the point!” the white man reiterates, waving his arms around aimlessly in the air to try and back himself up, he’s cut short once more by the radio… “I can’t see any other outcome either, David, if Lisieux manages to survive this bout and fight again then it’ll be a miracle. Mortimer has been rolling like a stone since he came back and I don’t see Lisieux being in any fit shape to stop him, it’s gonna take divine intervention or something to go down any other way.” “Listen to these damn disbelievers… young ‘uns these days can’t see that good ALWAYS triumphs over evil, no matter what the odds are looking like you always have to back the good guy! Stacy, get me another damn drink…” Claude looks puzzled for a second at the enthusiasm being displayed by the seventy-year-old John sitting opposite him, “You tellin’ me you think a scrawny little white punk from CANADA, who gets knocked down on his ass from ONE DAMN PUNCH can beat a guy who people call “monster”!? You must be up out your mind, how much of that shit have you swallowed?” “No more than any other day and I damn sure ain’t for being crazy! Listen here, Claude, ignore those radio jockeys for a minute and quit staring down that young girl’s shirt… This guy comes back from severe burns, right? Really bad shit like he might never walk again, all he’s got left is revenge… imagine having nothing but revenge to live off? No family, no friends… no money, all that you got to wake up to every day is the hope that one day you’re gonna get justice for the wrongs that people did to you back in the day… that ain’t no kind of life to have now, is it?” John leaves the question hanging as Claude takes a moment to consider it… “Well damn, I can’t see how I could live like that… this kid ain’t got nothing at all? He used to have it all, right?” the aged citizen enquires… John nods his head before responding, “Clothes, cash, cars, houses, women… beautiful, beautiful women… and PRIME gold too, this man had everything you could dream for… imagine having all of that one day then BAM you ain’t got shit. Not to mention some sick bastards are gonna string you up on a damn cross and then light a fire underneath you on international television… I don’t know how those guys weren’t locked-up or something.” Claude looks puzzled again, “So you’re meaning to tell me this was no stunt or shit like that? These dudes actually crucified a man and lit a damn fire underneath him!? On damn television!? And then they got away with it?”“Yup…” Claude takes a long, strong drink of whiskey before shaking his head in utter disbelief at what he was hearing, “That company must be loaded something rotten to be able to get away with shit like that.” “I’ll drink to that!” John and Claude clink bottle and glass together and share what could well be their millionth drink together in the same bar in the same part of Lexington that they had been coming for the past fifty years. Both are family men with strong values, but little money and a big appetite for alcohol - both so alike yet so different. “PRIME have sanctioned this bout as a Last Man Standing match and we’re hearing rumours that the opposing wrestler has to actually signal for the count to start, there can be no draws, no ties, this one has to end tonight and it has to have a winner… It damn sure isn’t going to be a pretty sight.” “That bastard Motoki is just begging for someone to be killed right there and we all know which guy he’d rather see the end of…” “Oh yeah?” replied an interested Claude… “Well hell you must know of the trouble that son of a bitch has had with Ignatius, the hell he’s put him through… seems to me like this feud with Mortimer is all born out of that sick bastard’s head!” Claude perches forward, “That’s the Jap guy, right, the guy with the money and the power?” “Well yeah, but not so much anymore, he’s pulled some bad shit in his time but tonight might show up to be the worst yet if he gets his way. That Last Man Standing shit will finish somebody, that’s for sure, you can’t send two guys that hate each other into a ring together with no rules or regulations and expect those two guys to not try and tear each other apart!” Claude nods his head along with his friends’ words, “Damn maybe I do have to watch this… Stacy, you showing the wrasslin’ here later on?” The barmaid looks up from her magazine with a scowl, “Yeah we sure are… does that mean I have to entertain your company for the whole damn night?” “You love me really baby girl, I can see it in your eyes…” “Quit that shit, Claude, I ain’t finished filling you in on what you been missing… anyway, this Lisieux guy comes back, thing’s like a damn resurrection if you hear what I’m saying… you got all these religious groups in disarray and whatnot when they burned him up but they musta been paid off by the company. Now he’s back and it’s started again, they been very careful not to call it a resurrection but damn me to hell if that ain’t what it is… You got a guy burned on a cross who surfaces from damn near nowhere in order to judge those that burned him…” “Sounds a hell of a lot like the Bible to me, John…” …It was hard for Ignatius Lisieux to put into words what he felt about Ozric Mortimer, the Dark Age and ReVolution 50 - he had already spent a year trying to get over it all. Numerous specialists had tried their utmost to make Lisieux move on with his life, but each and every one of them were eventually resigned to the fact that the French-Canadian could not find peace with himself until he had his revenge. On the back wall of his lavish room at the Liberty Falls Complex was a list that he had etched as soon as he had regained consciousness; carved into that stone wall were names… Ozric MortimerDani FurherShadowTiamatVampir NosferatuToshiaki Motoki …A sort-of little black book of revenge that he had looked at every single day for almost a year with a hot burning sensation that rose up inside of him every time his eyes scanned up and down the carving. It all started with Ozric Mortimer, he was the natural choice, as the man who lit the fire that put a former PRIME Global Champion into a coma for three weeks. Ozric Mortimer had an awful lot to answer for. “How’s the head today, Ignatius?” asked the nurse that had been personally assigned to tend to Lisieux between ReVolution 84 and the Great American Nightmare. “You mean the inside or the outside?” smiled the French-Canadian towards a knowing return glance. “You know fine well what I mean… the stitches, how are they doing? Want me to take a look at them?” said the nurse, inching towards her patient. Ignatius shook his head, “No thanks, they actually feel pretty good… it’s the inside of my head that always seems to hurt me the most.” “Well I wouldn’t know too much about that…” smiled the nurse, “I’m only here to keep you physically right… I’m no quack, that’s for sure.” Lisieux laughed again, “You must have heard the rumours though, right? ‘Crazy Lisieux’ and all that stuff?” “Well” she sighed, “People have got wicked tongues, it tends to be the way in this world we live in, people take any chance they find to knock a man down. I’ve heard what people say about you, but I’ve seen no evidence myself to prove the words right.” “Thanks Jen, that means a lot.” Replied Lisieux warmly while shifting his legs outside of his bed to stand on his feet, only to be held back by the aching pains he felt down the charred carcasses he had for limbs. “For a guy who has perhaps the biggest match of his career later on today you ain’t looking too ready to me!” commented Jen, trying to mask the seriousness of the allegation with a subtle tinge of humour. Lisieux sighed to himself once more and held his head in his hands for a brief moment, “Just get me some painkillers fixed up, every two hours for the rest of the day - that should just about see me through.” He dragged himself up to a vertical base in order to look out of the window of his third-storey PRIME-provided apartment. “It’s a nice day to die - at least, I love fresh winter days like these.” Nurse Jen looked worried at the comment as she joined Lisieux by the window, “There’ll be no dying later on for you, young man, just make that guy get what’s coming to him and bring yourself back here to this apartment. I’m sure I can still look after you, no matter what state you might find yourself in come tomorrow morning.” She placed her hand on his shoulder as Lisieux bowed his head with a solemn smile. “Have you heard what they been saying about you in the papers and on the radio? Some folks are comparing you to Jesus Christ, can you imagine that?” Ignatius struggled not to scoff at the comment from Jen, but he managed it, instead simply turning to her to ask, “You for real?” “Hell yes I am. It’s been all over the news - you’re causing another royal stink with the PTA and the extreme religious groups – people are calling you the second coming and those folks don’t like that one bit!” replied Jen enthusiastically. “I suppose you get a guy who does apparently great things before getting put on a cross and practically finished before coming back from an apparent nowhere and crazy comparisons like that are bound to be made.” Pondered the French-Canadian while gently rubbing the left of his stomach. “I suppose so… I guess that if you’ve done the whole life, death and resurrection thing like Jesus did then all you have left to do is the ascension before judgement day thing.” Laughed the Nurse, “We might never see you again after tonight!” Struck by the harsh reality of Jen’s apparently innocuous and innocent comment, Ignatius Lisieux began to realise that perhaps tonight may be his “ascension” of sorts. Jen realised that her words had struck some sort of chord with her patient as she settled herself tidying up around the apartment while Lisieux stared intently out of the window, thinking to himself. “Surely it makes no sense for me to follow the exact same path as Jesus did… but the similarities have been pretty severe so far… Nah, that’s crazy talk right there Iggy, tonight is all about judgement, there will be no ascension involved… Well Jesus is still yet to come back and it’s been two-thousand-and-five years… Damn, you wouldn’t find me waiting that long to judge those who do wrong, tonight it starts with Ozric and then I continue down the list…” “I didn’t mean to scare you, but you do realise the severity of what you’re about to do later on, don’t you?” asked Jen cautiously… Lisieux took a moment to ponder before looking his nurse directly in the eye and issuing a response, “Of course I do… tonight it’s either me or him, he wants to finish the job and I want to take an eye-for-an-eye, either way he walks out of there alive, but the same can’t be said for me… I realise how much is on the line, but do I really have anything to lose?” With that grim revelation Ignatius Lisieux finally opened his eyes to his true reality. Nothingness. Jen had never been there at all, she was merely a figment of his imagination brought about by the incessant amounts of morphine he’d been getting pumped with since last Wednesday evening. Nobody had been there bar the visits from the doctors and nurses who only came when he was passed-out with the pain or with the effects of the various drugs. Right at that moment Ignatius found himself back at square one. He seemed a million miles away from redemption, but the truth was that it was under twenty-four hours until he would stand toe-to-toe with the first name on his list, perhaps the most important name… Ozric Mortimer. If Lisieux could pummel and disfigure Mortimer until he was no longer able to stand then maybe, just maybe, it would make all the months of pain and anguish he’d suffered seem worth it. Maybe it would make life seem worth it. If not, then at least he trusted Mortimer to be as good as his word, which was that he would end Lisieux in Michigan. Either way, in some warped reality, it seemed that the French-Canadian couldn’t really lose no matter what the outcome may be. “Let us pray…” Dear Lord, Please provide me with strength and honour so that I may not shame myself tonight in Auburn Hills. Allow me to utilise and then diminish all of the ill-will and vengeance bubbling inside of my being in pursuit of peace and harmony with my true self and the world. Please allow me to return to some form of normality when this night is over, whether that be peace with life or peace with death, all I ask is that the pain be ended… forever and ever… Amen.
