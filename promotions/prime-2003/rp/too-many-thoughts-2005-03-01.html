Too Many Thoughts

1 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 1, 2005 It was difficult to understand at first. Trying to figure out why Boda did what he did at Culture Shock was not fun for Killean. But it was rattling his mind and he had to figure out what made the new Universal Champion do what he did. He turned his back on everything that everyone thought he believed in. He sold his soul to the Dark Age and hugged Vampir. He hugged Vampir! He had welcomed the devil himself into his life. That was unbelievable in itself. Sirrajin finally came to the realization that Boda will do whatever it takes to hold on to the Universal title, even if it involves the Dark Age in any way. But Boda did not know what was coming for him. But Killean had other things on his mind as well. Sitting in the office of his friend and doctor Tchu, he was thinking about Aimz a lot. She and Killean had attended a couple of appointments with Tchu together earlier in the week. She had begun to make progress and Sirrajin was happy for that. They had talked a little and gotten to somewhat know each other. There was so much more that Killean wanted to know but he was a little shy when it came to talking to someone he hasn't known that long. Plus, he just wanted to insure that she was getting the help she needed. He didn't take things into his hands at Culture Shock for nothing. Avoiding everyone was a must. If it got out that Aimz was having a breakdown of sorts, who knows what would happen. She could be put on leave from PRIME or even worse. Killean knew that Aimz wanted to be in PRIME and he wanted to ensure things stayed that way, no matter the price. 'So have you been writing in your journal as of late Killean?' Tchu interrupted his thoughts with the question. Killean sort of shook out of the trance he was in to acknowledge his friend. 'Yeah, every now and then I guess. I haven't written in the last day or two.' Killean said shakily, still trying to focus and break loose from his thoughts. 'Well you don't need to write something every day. Just so long as it....' Tchu could see that Killean was in a far off place. He wasn't even making eye contact with Tchu. 'Is anyone home?' He knocked on his desk to get Killeans attention and Killean snapped back to the present. 'Sorry Matt, just so many things going on right now. It is getting hard to concentrate on just one thing.' Killean went on like Tchu didn't know what was happening. Yet a few minutes before, Sirrajin spilled his guts about everything. He definitely was having a hard time dealing with it all. 'Look its ok, perhaps you should go home and rest, clear your head.' Tchu suggested, clearly showing concern for his friend. 'I would like you to bring in your journal next time.' Killean had looked like he just saw a ghost. He knew exactly what Tchu said and showed a look of concern on his face. His worst fear was someone reading the inner most thoughts that he had written on the paper. 'I thought that was going to be.... ummm.... private.' Killean stuttered, obviously showing a sign of fear about having his personal writings read by another soul. 'Well I do need to see what you are writing to ensure that it is helping you; word is not all I can go on. That would not be very professional.' Tchu explained, but he saw the look of discomfort coming from the facial features of The Supreme Machine. 'Don't worry about it Killean. I will just browse through what you are writing. I will be the only one who sees it.' That made Killean feel a little better but he was still worried. All the deepest darkest thoughts that went through his head were logged on that paper. No filters were present; it was all the real thoughts. No one had ever known what exactly was going on in his head but him. He could feel one thing, and show something completely different. This journal was an outlet that he surely thought would be kept with him. Tchu was a good friend after all and he is not one to judge. But if Tchu saw something and decided to ask Killean about it, what would he do? He couldn't very well explain exactly why the thoughts were running through his mind. Even he didn't know at times. Killean just shook it all off really and got back to what was bothering him. Later, Killean was sitting at home in his backyard by the swimming pool. He was sipping on a cold beer still wondering how a man that seemed so genuine could do what he did. Boda was not really a friend to Killean, but they had a mutual respect. Sirrajin always stood side by side with the Big Bad Boda Daddy until he decided to become Chris 'Vampir is my Daddy' Sloboda. How could he side with someone like Vampir after all Vampir put him through? Was it all just a ploy? All along could it be that Boda and Vampir were planning this? It couldn't be because if it was, Boda would be sicker than even Vampir himself. How could he put his family through everything and then turn a blind eye? Sirrajin could not understand, but he hoped that Boda would explain himself and his actions at ReVolution. Ahhh yes ReVolution. Episode 56 to be exact. Killean Sirrajin was in the main event that he asked for, but he did not expect it to be a Glass Ring match. It all reminded him of the hell that was the Syndicate Structure at Colossus. Kincade and he could barely move when that match was over. But the main point is that Killean still walked out the Intense Champion. Now the Dark Age is banned from ringside and it is one on one. Oh how Sirrajin wanted the opportunity to face First Blood in a brutal match. He got what he asked for and more. Killean didn't really know if First Blood knew what he was getting into. Sure a shot at the Intense Championship was great, but to endure absolute hell just 2 weeks after the Dual Halo? Was it all really worth it? Killean had debated that himself but he was a fighter and a fight was what he was going to get. First Blood turned on him not too far back and retribution was in the cards and it was due to take place at ReVolution. More so than anything though, everything was for the fans. Killean could still recall the ovations that he received at the Sapparo Dome in Japan. He could also remember his match in Canada where the fans were behind him 100%. That is why Killean does what he does. He feeds off the energy of the fans if he needs 'A Pick Me Up' in the middle of a match. The fans meant so much to Killean; they all really had no idea just how much. But Boda turned on his fans. He let all the people down and to be honest, it broke a smile on Killean's face just to think of the boos and the garbage tossing that will take place at ReVolution this coming week. Boda deserves all the bad treatment and garbage from this point on and it will not stop even when that Universal Strap is off his waist. He has tainted the Boda persona and many will never forgive and forget. Aimz on the other hand was trying her best to show PRIME that she does indeed belong. But having to face a phenom like Vampir could even be too much for the Electric redhead. Killean knew that she would definitely be a problem for Vampir but he just didn't want to see her get hurt or perhaps worse. She had to be totally focused on Vampir and use her speed and smarts to beat the demon. But Killean knew that Vampir was on a roll. He was a wrecking machine in the Dual Halo. He even eliminated Sirrajin to end his night. But Aimz had a very large task at hand, a task that Killean was afraid that she would not overcome. He had taken a liking to Aimz and really wanted to see her safe, especially after the events behind the scenes at Culture Shock. ReVolution was to answer many questions, but it was posing even more at the moment. First Blood had better be ready, for The Glass Ring is unknown territory to him. Killean has been put through plate glass many times before and he knew how much it hurt. The pain is sharp and fast you get used to it, but it wears you down very fast. He had to give it all he had at Colossus against Kincade, Shadow and Poet. Nothing was lacking here either and he knew First Blood was ready to kick off the work load early. But Killean will not give in. Even deep in thought, Killean was debating on taking this match on. But with the Dark Age banned from Ringside, it would make things easier and a lot less messy. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Journal EntryMonday, February 28, 2005 So much was on my mind. Aimz was worrying me first and foremost. I had taken a liking to the attractive redhead and I know that in the future, we will be good friends. It didn't help that she has to face Vampir this week. After seeing her break down like she did on the road, he even debated on suggesting to Aimz to stay home. She would have none of it though. She wanted to wrestle. Something will be happening at ReVolution 57. Regardless of the outcome of the Intense Title match this week, I have big plans for ReVolution next week. Boda needs to be confronted and after all, Boda pinned Black Angel and never pinned or beat me. Boda better have that loud and clear. HE NEVER BEAT ME. Not to mention that First Blood is intent on getting his hands on my title. That is my belt and not even First Blood will be the one to pry it from my hands. The Glass Ring will definitely show why the Intense Title is just that, Intense. Who even knows if we will survive this match? Glass is very damaging and I took a look at the ring that they would use. It is an unforgiving piece of equipment and is trying to steal my health and well being. But First Blood needed to go down and go down hard. No more games and no more words, it is go time. But all of that didn't hold a candle to the news I received later in the day. I received a phone call from The Police department and found out some rather disturbing news. I took the phone and through the rather long introduction, I finally got the hint. I was rather shocked and appalled at first. I mean the words hit me like a sack of bricks. Vandals had broken into the cemetery and had damaged and destroyed many tombstones. Kira's was one of them. I remember that stone very well, including the flowers that bordered the tombstone and some unique designs that I had thought up on my own before were carved into the middle of it along with her name. I really couldn't stand the fact that people could do something so heartless and pointless. It goes to show that people don't care. I really would miss the old stone but I had already promised that I would come up with a new design for them. I cemented my love into the first stone and now I had to put my soul into this new one. I loved my wife and no one will ever tell me or prove me different. Even with the investigations going on, I will come out with more love for my wonderful wife than I ever had. All in all it was a pretty bad time this past week. All of this on top of everything else that was running through my head made me tired. I had slept a lot recently and I was still beat. Tchu apparently wants to look over this little journal of mine. Surely I had wanted it to remain private but I am sure that Tchu will be ok with all my thoughts. My main issue is ReVolution. I needed to find a way to get through the main event without killing myself. I would definately be walking out of there with my title, but with how many limbs intact? First Blood better be ready because I am and he will regret the day he caused problems with Killean Sirrajin.
