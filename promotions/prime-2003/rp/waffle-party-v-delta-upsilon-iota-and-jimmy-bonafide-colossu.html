Waffle Party (v. Delta Upsilon Iota and Jimmy Bonafide, Colossus Night 1)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Tomorrow. Beefville, City of the Annoyed. According to those native to the city, Beefville really existed "within one's own heart". To those who have visited the city, they often acted as if they weren't sure whether Beefville really existed or not. Beefville was henceforth renowned as the "City That May Or May Not Exist". Three of Beefville's most prominent citizens were the three men who commonly squatted in an abandoned warehouse hidden somewhere in the city, which they converted into their epic studio made out of materials they scavenged from every place they could reasonably scavenge things from. The three members of Mega Job, Beef, El Janito, and Steve, were currently putting aside their "job" as a camera crew. Today was a different day. It was close to Mega Job's first pay-per-view match in about three years, and that was something to celebrate for some reason. Possibly because Mega Job got a whole advance on their pay for that pay-per-view. That's right. It's a waffle party. "Dude," Beef said, "We have WAY more waffles than even WE can eat." "Which is why we organized our party in the first place." El Janito said, just as he said that, almost as if it was on cue, the door was knocked. Steve simply stared at the door and it opened. Beef and El Janito stared Steve for a moment. "Okay, seriously, we are turning Steve into some supernatural force of nature that can't be stopped by any mortal man and can only be fought to a stalemate by Chuck Norris." El Janito commented. He turned to look at the new guest, "Oh. Hey, it's Mr. T and the gang." "You know, *I'm* supposed to be the leader of this group." said Texas Kid, a luchador who wore a mask of the Texas flag, and had spurs on his wrestling boots. He also was wearing a poncho and a cowboy hat, neither of which were typical parts of his ensemble until recently, when he decided that he needed to appear "more Texas-y". It was often asked why he didn't just say "more Texan", but then Texas would just superkick him hard enough to achieve a .2 on the Dusk superkick scale. Which would totally kill a mortal man, by the way. "Since when?" Waru asked to a nearby brick wall. Waru was a guy who wore a pink... thing on his face which pretty much just rendered him blind. "Jesus flipping Castillo!" Beef exclaimed, pointing at Waru, "He's STILL wearing that on his face? I thought you guys would've gotten sick of that whole 'I'm blind! I'm blind and I'm too stupid to take this thing off of my face!' thing by now." "If I knew who said that, I'd punch them." Waru said. "I'd just no-sell it, anyway." Beef said in response. "I PITY DA FOO' WHO DON'T BRING NO CHILI TO A WAFFLE PARTY!" Mr. T shouted, abruptly. Beef and El Janito looked at Mr. T for a bit, before they simply turned back towards Texas Kid again. "I see he's back to normal, again," El Janito said, "Or at least as normal as T gets." "I think he's only back to normal because someone thought it was just easier for people to understand if Mr. T spoke like Mr. T rather than speaking like Angelo Deville." Texas Kid said. He then paused, and then he added, "Wait. Who the hell is Angelo Deville?" >=) "And what the hell was that?" Texas Kid asked. >=) "Seriously," he said, with increasing panic in his voice, "It's creeping me the hell out." "What worries me more is the prospect of chili with waffles. Can you say 'ew'?" Beef said. He shuddered to complete the effect. La Parka shimmied. Yes, we're calling him La Parka even though he lost the rights to that name some time ago. Beef nodded in agreement, "Glad you agree with me, Parka." Anyway, a squishy knock on the door was heard. Steve once again opened the door using only the power of his awesome to reveal Adam Nowell, a former member of Ringside Wrestling who was best known for being half of the first Fellowship Championships, standing there. He was not alone, however, as he was holding half of Ken War's crushed skull, still attached to his body (amazingly), in his left hand. "I don't know how I end up at these things, or why this guy is always around for them." Nowell said, gesturing at Ken War. Ken War gurgled something, perhaps something to the effect of "I am hardcore" except in his odd language, but he wasn't really in too much condition to talk at the moment. Which was probably a good sign for the rest of this roleplay. ...Did I say roleplay? I meant "waffle party". Waffle party was what I meant. "Adam. Glad you could make it. Please don't kill us." Beef said, saying his last four words in a low whisper as if he was chanting some sort of incantation rather than actually outright telling him not to kill them. "Please tell me you brought liquor." Nowell said. "No liquor store in town will sell to us," El Janito said, "Something about Yugoslavians." "Yugoslavia isn't even a country any more, you douche," Nowell said, already feeling a headache coming on. He dropped Ken War's corpse and sighed, "Fine. I brought my own, anyway. None of you can have any." "Dammit!" Beef exclaimed, as Nowell brought in a handful of tiny bottles of tequila, went to a corner, sat down, and began to consume them. Meanwhile, Ken War crawled over to Texas Kid, almost as if he wanted Texas Kid to help him. Texas Kid responded by embedding the spur of one of his boots into his head. Ken War died, if only for a moment. "So, how has life after fWEo been treating you guys?" El Janito asked. "Well, Mr. T found World of Warcraft." Texas Kid solemnly explained. "I'm sorry." El Janito said, seemingly sympathetic. "He thinks he's using a Night Elf Mohawk. None of us have had the heart to tell him it's actually a Night Elf Warrior," Texas Kid said, "Anyway, now he's all 'I PITY DA FOO WHO DRAWS THE AGGRO AWAY FROM ME!' and 'I PITY DA FOO WHO CASTS POWER WORD: SHIELD ON ME!'. Seriously, it's like he's become a different person." Beef nodded, "I see. I don't know what any of that means." There was an awkward pause. During this time period, Texas Kid and Beef idly turned to the left and were startled to see a black man standing there. He wore a snazzy green and white suit, had a strange visor covering his face, and he had a coffee mug in his hand. As soon as they saw him, a smooth jazz theme suddenly filled the air. "Ha...!" he said, "Just goes to show you, the T knows what's up when you gotta tank the mobs." Yes, the Codemaster was here, too. "We didn't even INVITE you. How'd you get in?" Beef said, exasperatedly. "It just simply isn't a waffle party without the Blackest Brother in the Hyrule Kingdom." he said. He gave a nod to Steve, who simply threw one of these up: o/ The Codemaster blinked. "Oookay," he said to himself, before he turned to Beef, "Brother, tell me you got an Xbox." "Dude. Since when in the hell could we afford one? We get paid in waffles, remember?" Beef asked of the video game addicted black man standing next to him. The Codemaster decided to ignore him and speak to the only man in the room he felt had a speck of intelligence to them. "Brother Parka," he said to La Parka, "Guide me to the Xbox 360." La Parka shimmied. The Codemaster nodded, and then followed La Parka to a corner of the warehouse. Seconds later, a figure majestically flew in from the huge, Ken War-sized hole in the ceiling. He flew in with such majesty that you would think that he were regal. Then he landed with both bird feet on Ken War, killing him just as he reanimated again. And then he spoke. "MUWAKEKEKEKEKEKEKE! I AM THE MYSTERIOUS BIRDMAN 0¿0 AND I GO CLUCK AT YOU! FOR I AM THE FATHER OF EXTREME AND THE MOTHER OF HARDCORE, AND THOSE WHO SAY OTHERWISE WILL HAVE A BEAK-SHAPED HOLE IN THEIR SKULLS! MUCH LIKE THIS ONE GENTLEMAN I MET ON A MID-SUMMER AFTERN--" "Birdman." Beef said, interrupting the Birdman. "WHO DARES INTERRUPT THE MYSTERIOUS BIRDMAN IN THE MIDDLE OF ONE OF HIS LEGENDARY SIX PAGE INTRODUCTION RANTS?!" the Mysterious Birdman asked, clearly ruffled. "We have waffles. You're welcome to some of them." Beef said. The Birdman... paused, and looked up at the ceiling as if he was pondering whether to accept Beef's generous offer, or "............THE MYSTERIOUS BIRDMAN ACCEPTS YOUR OFFER, UNFEATHERED ONE." the Birdman said, before he marched straight to the waffles, shoving some homeless guy out of the way as he marched. Beef whispered to Texas Kid, "That guy scares the crap out of me." "I didn't know you invited a hobo." Texas Kid said. "Uh, yeah... he has Jason Natas' invitation to this party. I found that strange, but I assumed Jason just used some sort of shapeshifting powers." "......Shouldn't this roleplay end now?" Texas Kid asked. "Roleplay?" "Yeah. I mean, it feels like we've been at this for days now." Texas Kid said. And as anti-climatically as possible, this thing came to an en-- "HOLD IT!" What? "That's it?" Adam Nowell asked, suddenly looking up at the sky all confused-like, "We're just gonna gather some guys together, make them say stupid crap, and then we just abruptly end it? What kind of stupid crap is that?" Dude. This is like my third roleplay for this show, and this is the joke roleplay at that. What do you expect? "Why am I talking to the writer, anyway?" Nowell asked. You're drunk. "Oh yeah." Nowell said, after examining his small bottle of tequila. Okay. So. That's it. You don't have to go home, but you can't stay here.
