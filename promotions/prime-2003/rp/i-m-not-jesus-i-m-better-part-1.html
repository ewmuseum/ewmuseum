Date: Jnauary 8, 2010 Chapter One: Excuse The Disturbance ---- "Ladies and gentlemen..." The words started out mellow and disheartening, just like any good sermon should. While the room was not large, it was filled from the front to the back... and from side to side. A diverse group tried to hold their composure, fighting back weeps and sobs as the preacher didn't look away from his podium. "We are here today to mourn the loss of Jesus Sanchez, better known to the world as lucha libre star Dragon Mask I. Growing up just outside of Mexico City, Jesus started training in what would become his life's work at the young age of 8... the common practice for many of the world's greatest lucha libr..." CREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEK The painful screech of an old door to the Miami funeral home stopped everything in its tracks. Even the preacher took a look up from his podium to find out what all the commotion was about. It seemed as if the Earth stopped rotating on its axis as she walked into the room. Wearing a gorgeous black dress she was sure to make herself the center of attention, just like she always is. While some people gawked in perverted curiosity, the mood in the room for the most part seemed to greet her the same way. She wasn't wanted here. "She has some nerve showing up here..." She could hear the voices around the room and couldn't help but smirk as Kazys Jankauskas followed in tow. All the would be remarks were met by intimidating stares by The Iron Wolf as they passed. "Who is that?" "That's Elise Ares." "She said on a radio show last week that she didn't even know who Dragon Mask was." Not wanting to pass up the opportunity, she couldn't resist the urge to walk all the way to the front of the room. Again, it wasn't in her nature to pass up the chance to get maximum exposure. Meanwhile Kaz feared for her life the deeper they went into the group in mourning. "She was his favorite student in my academy class." "She used to wrestle as Dragon Mask II when she got her start." "She was a talented girl, it's a shame she'd turned into such a whore." Every comment made the smirk on her face grow bigger and bigger as the preacher tried to cover up the obvious hostility by continuing on with his sermon. Yet the biggest confrontation was only moments away. Finding two seats available in the second row, she quickly took a seat and pretended to be only interested in what the preacher had to say as Kaz took the one beside her, but she knew full well who the person in the seat behind her was. Jose Guerrero. Better known to the world of professional wrestling as the great luchadore El Demonio de Azul. Better known to Elise Ares as her father. The glare coming from behind her could even be felt by Kazys, who couldn't help but turn around and look into the eyes of the beast in discomfort. His hatred was not focused as Kaz, but at his own daughter who he hadn't seen in over five years. Not since she deserted Cuba in the middle of the night to start a new life. But the God fearing girl that he raised was not the woman that sat in front of him today. The woman in front of him was an arrogant, selfish, ignorant whore. Reclining her chair a bit was sure to piss off her father, but poking the lion with a stick has always been one of her go-to moves. The anger on her father's face was obvious to everyone around them. No one was paying attention to the sermon anymore, they were focused on one thing. Hoping that Jose would finally snap and rip her head clean off her shoulders. That wasn't going to happen though. At least... not while Kazys was around. Now wearing a Cheshire smile from ear to ear, Elise looked over at Kaz who is simply staring back at her. "What?" She whispered, acting like she wasn't trying to take away from the service. "Oh, nothing." Kaz commented, "You just forgot to mention this entire room wants to kill you." "They can get over it, I have as much right to be here as anyone else." "Yeah, but..." "Hey, has the guy behind me stopped glaring at me yet?" Kazys tried to look behind her without causing a scene. "No." Kaz's answer brought a giggle up from Elise. "He's not even paying attention to the service is he?" "I don't think he's heard a word of it." "Good." Elise leaned back in her chair a bit farther, "I love this city." Even in the death of another, Elise had managed to make herself the center of attention. She wouldn't have it any other way as the preacher spoke up... " 'For your sake we are being killed all day long; we are accounted as sheep to be slaughtered.' " He paused for a moment to let the line sink in, "No, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him who loved us. For I am convinced that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor rulers, nor things present, nor things to come, nor powers, nor height, nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. Corey Taylor ---- Chapter Two: Confessions Of A Harlot ---- The women's bathroom always seemed to be crowded immediately after the service. One by one, the grieving girls filed into the bathroom to take a look into the mirror and fix whatever flaws in their makeup the loss of a loved one had made. It was almost like the first step in moving on with their lives, erasing the scars left behind from the loss of a loved one. This ritual was almost done for the evening thankfully, leaving only two girls in the bathroom. The first was Ayumi Nguyen, wife of Jose Guerrero. Famed nemesis of Dragon Mask. Looking in the mirror she was wiping away the mascara laced tears from her cheeks, trying her best to make herself presentable to the world. The first step in a long process that she was sure to remember for the rest of her life. Amazingly enough not for the loss of a loved one, but for what was about to happen because the second person in the bathroom was... "That might've been the longest funeral I've ever been to in my life." Elise Ares spoke out loud, thinking she was in the bathroom by herself but quickly stopped in her tracks when she saw her mother looking into the mirror. It was her father who she ran away from years ago, not her. But the look she got back from her mother was one of betrayal. "Sorry, I thought I was alone." Elise spoke when she saw the look on her mother's face. She'd be lying if she said she didn't miss her mother. It might be the only person on the planet besides herself who she legitimately loves. Deep down inside that annoying facade she puts on in front of PRIME audiences every night, there was a heart. Just not one she particularly likes others to see. "How could do this?" Ayumi said, "I raised you better than that." At first she didn't have anything to say. She was speechless. There were so many words and sentences formulating in her head but nothing seemed to make its way down to her mouth. "You prance around at a funeral, disrespecting Jesus." Ayumi said, "And not just Jesus Sanchez, but Jesus Christ! Walking into a house of God and making it all about yourself. And not to mention your father. You're lucky he doesn't even know you're here." "I don't owe anything to my father." Elise spoke up for herself. "If it wasn't for your father, you wouldn't be alive!" Ayumi spoke up in defense of her husband. "If it wasn't for my father I wouldn't have broken my leg when I was 11." Elise answers, snapping back, "If it wasn't for my father I wouldn't have to lie to the nurses at the hospital and tell them I got hit in the face with a baseball when I was 5 years old. Maybe you weren't around every time he beat me within an inch of my life mom, but I was far from a pampered child." "But your father still loves you, he just doesn't know how to..." "Stop making excuses for that asshole, mom! He continually forced a little girl to lie to adults to make excuses for him beating the piss out of her!" Elise argued back, "I don't care if he loves me. After everything he's done to me... the last thing I feel is love for that sick bastard. My only regret is that this was Jesus' funeral and not his." The tears started to roll down her mother's face before she realized what she'd put her through, but it was far too late to go back. She felt bad that her mother was crying but didn't regret a single word that she'd said. She meant it, it was how she felt and had been wanting to get it off her chest for years. “The day he dies will be the second happiest day of my life.” Elise spoke. "It's like I don't even know who you are anymore. What happened to you Elise?" Ayumi spoke through the tears. “I grew up.” Elise answered before breaking eye contact with her heartbroken mother. Even she couldn’t look back at the tears streaming down her mother’s face, so she simply hung her head as she reached out for the door handle… but it wasn’t there. Looking up she realized the door had been opened, and standing in front of her was quite possibly the last person in the world she wanted to see at this moment. It was her father.
