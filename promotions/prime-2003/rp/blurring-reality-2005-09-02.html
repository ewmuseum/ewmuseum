Blurring Reality.

2 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02/09/05 “Are you ready to go on a journey of discovery, Paul?” Damien Kahn had shed the prison robes that had branded him a violent criminal and replaced them with a white suit jacket, pants and shirt with a matching tie. This was unusual for him and it seemed to signify Damien turning over a new leaf. His past had been one dominated by a need to survive and protect his now deceased younger brother by any means necessary. Damien knew all about turmoil and heartache with his prison term being the culmination of many months of hell. Now that he was free, he was determined to resolve some outstanding issues and one of these was Paul Cain. They had been associates for some time now and it was Paul that had led Damien to the sometimes equally dark world that he lived in. It had offered Kahn some protection from the more dangerous lifestyle that he had been trapped in and for that Damien was grateful. However, Paul was not able to protect him all the time and eventually Kahn succumbed to more violence which led him to prison. However now, it was Paul who needed protecting, protecting from himself. “I said, are you ready to go on a journey with me, Paul?” Cain looked down at himself was somewhat puzzled by what he saw. The black clothes that he had been wearing recently to mark the trials he had faced were gone and replaced by something a little more normal. He was wearing a sand coloured tank top and denim jeans with sneakers, he almost didn’t recognise himself. This was not something he would wear at the moment and he didn’t feel comfortable with it either. “Why am I wearing THIS? I don’t remember putting this shit on, it’s just not…Me…at the moment.” Damien smirked at Cain and shook his head in disbelief. “That hangover must have been worse than we thought. See, once you got used to the idea of me being back around, it didn’t take long for us to hit the bars and the clubs, drinking like we’d never seen alcohol before and showing people how D-Money and the Serial Thrilla burn it up, remember?” A frown crosses the face of Paul at the mere mention of the name ‘Serial Thrilla’. “I’ve told you before, don’t call me that, that person no longer exists.” With his hands on his hips, Damien rolls his eyes dismissively over something that he has heard continually to the point where it is becoming exceptionally boring. “That right there is why you need me around Paul, because you’re always living in the past. You continually blame yourself and everyone else for things, you cry about the injustices done to you and you waste your life away wishing things had been different. That’s not how Paul Cain used to be, not the Cain that was winning titles and respect for his work ethic, his abilities and most of all, his morality. You need to me to show you where you keep fucking up Paul, because unless you change your attitude, Nerezza will be just another opportunity passing you by.” Behind Damien are two black rucksacks and he turns to pick them up. He throws one in the direction of Paul who seems surprised and almost drops it. “Where are we going?” He ignores this question and throws the bag onto his shoulder, motioning for Paul to do the same. Once Cain has followed this order, Damien turns his back to his companion and begins to walk away from him, leaving him standing.“I SAID, WHERE ARE WE GOING?!” Damien’s reply is cool and calm as he continues to walk on. “We’re going on a journey to save you from martyrdom.” ******************************************************************************************************************************************** When Damien had told Paul that they were going on a trip, he had not quite expected it to be something quite as grand as this. He looked down at the ticket in his hand and his attention was drawn again to the destination printed on it; Denver, Colorado. This had been where he had grown up yet now it was almost like a foreign place to him which held no meaning anymore. However Damien had insisted that they were going there even though Paul was adamant that he had no time to be chasing shadows. Nerezza was an opponent that required the utmost attention and taking impromptu vacations was the last thing a man in Paul’s position should be doing. As usual though, Damien had proved himself to be exceptionally forceful in his demands and eventually for the sake of his already tired mind, Paul backed down. The departure area of the airport was particularly busy with people milling around everywhere. Some looked lost whilst others were excitedly checking they had everything ready for their break away from normality. There did not appear to be anything unusual about this scene except the fact that Paul felt almost invisible among these people, free to move around without the harassment that television brought with it, something he had not felt for a long time now. Lost in his own thoughts, he was startled as Damien’s hand touched his right shoulder from behind. “Looks like we’re all set to go Paul, ready to take you back to where you came from.” Paul looked quizzically again at Damien, completely confused by this whole situation. “There’s nothing there for me in Denver, why the hell would I want to go back there?” The smiles that were appearing on Damien’s face were knowing ones, as if he was sharing his own private joke with himself. “You don’t want to go there, that’s the whole point for us going now. Denver is where your past is, the thing that you must confront. For you though, it seems the past is everywhere, stalking you like a predator going for its prey. This airport is no different is it Paul? Take a look over there.” Damien pointed to the left and Paul could not believe what he saw before him. The vast throng of people that were busily checking their travel arrangements had parted leaving an empty space where a group of people were standing isolated from everyone else. Instantly he recognised them all, a family that he knew all too well. Claudia Lopez stood with her arms folded, her long hair cascading down past her shoulders however it was not long enough to hide the tears that were falling from her eyes. In front of her was Maria, her sister, with a set of luggage either side of her legs. “Claudia...Maria…” Cain whispered their names underneath his breath as he watched Claudia throw her arms around Maria and sob heavily against her. Paul looked away for a moment and then struggled to take in this scene once more. They were hugging tightly and inaudible words were being exchanged between the two siblings. Cain felt a sick feeling developing in his stomach yet he could not quite understand what he was seeing here. They had not been in the same space for so long; Maria had been recovering from difficulties of her own whilst Claudia and Paul had been on tumultuous ride for some while now. Now the sisters were together and he simply did not understand what he was seeing, yet it still struck the same familiar chord in his heart. “Do you remember this Paul?” Damien’s voice broke through the sinking feeling that Paul was having as he watched the sisters cry into each other arms. He swallowed hard to allow himself to answer Damien. “What…is this?” He moves to stand side by side by Paul as he watches the emotional scene in front of them. “This is your past, Paul. You stood here and you watched this unfold as Maria left her sister behind to follow a career in wrestling. From the moment that she walked through the departure gates with you, you felt guilt and it stayed with you, didn’t it Paul?” Cain’s eyes were glazing over as he struggled to watch the two women hold each other hands with tears falling down their faces. “This was my fault, I broke up a family, they were the best of friends and I ruined that. I never wanted it to be that way, I never wanted them to be hurting like they did. If it hadn’t been for me, they would still have been together, now all the suffering they feel is because of me, my…fucking…FAULT.” His words ended in an angry tone as he showed his frustration at his own actions. Somewhere beneath the man that had inflicted pain upon his colleagues, was a hint of remorse and sadness as he blamed himself for the problems he felt he had caused. “This is what is holding you back Paul for it is but a distant memory to them. They both made their choices and in the end, it was Claudia who made hers out of love for you. She loved you and chose you because that made you both happy. Yet you prefer to blame yourself for something that happened naturally and the more you blame yourself, the more people like Nerezza get the chance to walk all over you. Let go of your past Paul because it’s poisoning your future.” Cain bows his head and then slowly looks back up at both Maria and Claudia. They turn to face him, smiling through their tears as they address him in unison. “It’s okay Paul, it’s okay…” Then they were gone. ******************************************************************************************************************************************** Paul remembered nothing of the trip that took him to Denver; his mind was still fully focussed on the image of Claudia and Maria together. None of it was making any sense to him and he could hear their words to him even now as they absolved him of the wrong-doing and guilt that he felt. Paul’s eyes were closed as he tried to make sense of all that was happening around him now. Once again it was Damien’s voice that distracted him and caused him to open his eyes and once again, shock and confusion gripped him. “You see Paul, now you understand that you were not responsible for dissolving a family, it is now time to understand your own. This place should be familiar to you too, for this is where you were raised my friend. Here is another part of your past that is affecting your judgement for while you excel at blaming yourself for things you are not responsible for, you have proved yourself highly skilled at blaming others for your own problems too.”Cain’s eyes widened as he saw the two bedroom townhouse that served as his home throughout his childhood. He had not been back here for many years and it gave him a chill up the spine as he took it all in. His family had long since been separated with his father having moved abroad and his relationship with his mother being non-existent. Although he had happy memories of this home, there were many that he pushed to the back of his mind in order to forget. Now those events were confronting him again as he walked on automatic pilot towards the steps leading to the porch. As he put one foot on the bottom step, Damien called out to him again. “In there you will find the reason for the death of Paul Cain. It wasn’t the fault of any Dark Age worshippers; they were just the trigger whilst the real reason has lived inside your mind for many years. Walk through the door Paul where the answer will lie and when you do find it, put it back in the past where it belongs. Only when you do that, can you defeat Nerezza.” He doesn’t look back at Kahn but instead slowly walks up to the porch and approaches the front door. He nervously puts his hand on the doorknob and takes a deep breath, feeling a sense of dread about this place. Surprise hits him as he realises the door is already open as he pulls it open gradually, hearing the hinges creaking as he does so. The darkness of the corridor hits him as he enters the house, his footsteps loud on the wooden floor. To his left are the stairs that would lead to the bedrooms where the family slept, he remembered being dragged up there many times when he had misbehaved and it was something that happened on a regular basis. This place was haunting. He walked down the passageway ignoring the staircase for now as he headed towards another door that was ajar. The house had been covered in a blanket of silence and he had heard nothing up to this point except the beating of his own heart. As he reached the door he noticed a sliver of light coming underneath it, something or someone was here in this house with him. Gingerly he extended his hand and pushed on the wooden door, holding his breath as he wondered what would greet him. There in front of him was the kitchen where his mother had regularly begrudgingly prepared their meals. Everything was as it stood all those years ago, the old fashioned glass table stood in the middle of the room as the centrepiece and the work surfaces still held old utensils. Surely this was not really happening? In the middle of the room was a small boy aged around six years old. He wore red sweatpants and a blue jumper, terrible colour co-ordination but being a child he got away with it. His hair was very untidy as if he had been playing but the sad and terrified look on his face told another story. His eyes were red and puffy with his jumper stained by tears that had been freshly cried. As Cain looked to his left, he saw something that shocked him even more; there stood his own mother with her shoulder length brown hair and skinny build. She suddenly started screaming at the child furiously who cowered back against the wall. “HOW DARE YOU SAY THAT TO ME?! HOW DARE YOU?!” Cain stepped forward and blocked the path of his mother’s furious gaze. “Stop it, stop it!” She looked straight through him and continued to scream in the child’s direction. “HOW DARE YOU!” The child was crying desperately and ran to his mothers legs, clinging to them but she pushed him away. He tried for a second time but again she rejected him, making sure to turn her back on him so she didn’t have to look at him. She breezed past Cain and stormed down the passageway with the child running after her, screaming as he moved. Paul held his hands to his head with his fingers entwined in his hair and his eyes screwed tightly shut. He heard her footsteps going up the stairs to the top floor and her feet stomping around the bedroom above. Then he felt that familiar hand on his shoulder again. “You remember this too, don’t you Paul?” He swung around to see Damien Kahn standing behind him with a solemn look on his face. “How did you…?” Damien shook his head to dismiss the question posed by Paul. “That doesn’t matter Paul, what matters is that this event has stayed with you since you were a child. She turned her back on you didn’t she, Paul? You made a mistake based on something that you had seen on television, monkey see, monkey do, right? You accused her of an affair, something that a child wouldn’t understand anyway yet she still walked away and left you alone didn’t she? However you forever blamed her for it didn’t you, Paul?” They both watch as she storms down the stairs and throws open the door, slamming it shut on the child angrily, leaving him all alone. “You did to her what you did to the whole of PRIME, blamed them for something that was not in their control. Your mother was emotional and shocked to hear those words from your mouth, yet you held it against her and it ruined your relationship forever. In PRIME, you blamed all of them for leaving you to apparently die at the hands of the Dark Age, when really it was not their fault. Now you’re saddled with this burden which eats away you continually to the point where you lashed at the closest thing you could find, Claudia, just like you continued to do as you grew up. How long will it go on for Paul, how long are you going to continue to make the wrong choices because of the past you lived through?” Paul points in the direction of the child who stands at the door, wailing loudly. “Look what she did to me, the way she treated me, walked away from her own son, turned her back on me! It’s all I’ve ever known!” Damien moves in front of Cain and shakes his head defiantly. “None of that matters Paul. You went after Peter because he REMINDED you of the past not because you opposed who he was. You came back to PRIME to damn everyone around and for what, what did you gain from it? Nothing, you gained absolutely NOTHING. Look at him, look at this child…LOOK AT HIM!” He grabs Cain by the hair and points at the child. “You were a young boy, this was years ago! Your mother is gone now and this is all in the past. Whatever hatred you have for her, do not let it consume you and ruin your life. This feud with Peter is over now and you have been given an opportunity to turn your life around, Nerezza is that opportunity. It’s time Paul; it’s time to bring him back. If anyone is going to turn their back to anything, it’s you who will turn away from this vengeful, vile creature that you have become. Bring back the man that made you; for he is not dead, he’s just sleeping.” Cain stares wide-eyed at the younger version of himself and then back to Damien. “I’m not ready…” Damien’s face turns to one of fire and intensity. “You have to be, you have to be….” ******************************************************************************************************************************************** Paul Cain’s eyes shot open as he sat bolt upright in his bed. The sheets were soaked with his sweat and it was glistening on his forehead as he ran his hands through his hair. He looked around the room and was reassured to see his bedroom as he had left it. His heart was pounding like a drum in his chest and his hands were shaking with fear. He desperately tried to control his breathing as he fought to remind himself he was in his own home. “It was just a bad dream…Just a dream…Damien isn’t here…Mom’s long gone…None of it was real…” He leaned forward and gripped his hair tightly trying to shake off what he didn’t understand. Then a voice spoke in the room where he thought he sat alone. “Something on your mind, Paul?” Into the rays of moonlight that entered the room walked Damien Kahn dressed all in black. “What the…?” Damien stood with his hands clasped together in front of his stomach and looked down at Cain with a stern expression. “I think we need to have another talk about your actions Paul, don’t you…?” Scene fades to black.
