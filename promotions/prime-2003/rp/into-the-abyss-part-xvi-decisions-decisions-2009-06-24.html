Into The Abyss: Part XVI - Decisions, Decisions

24 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 24-06-09 Beads of sweat curled around the dense wrinkles and lines in his forehead, rolling over his dry skin and moistening it as his pores opened and more droplets oozed through to the surface, catching in his thick eyebrows and sliding down his cheeks. He took great gulps of air as his muscles tightened and he heaved upwards, taking the weight on his hips and groaning. She doubled over, biting her lip and reaching out before arching her back and straightening again. She held the weight on her shoulder and moaned as it bore down on her. Together they panted as they worked, exchanging little loving glances as Von Kelsig shifted the concrete blocks and Gloria wearily carried bags of cement mix from the drive around to the side of their house. "Shouldn't the contractors be doing this, Ange?" Gloria asked, huffing as she dropped another bag onto the pile accumulating by the stacked blocks. "They won't be here 'til later this afternoon, I'd rather pay them to build than to do work that could already be done for free." He replied, working his hand under his shirt and pressing his head to the fabric, wiping away all the dirt and sweat from his face. He jogged back a few steps away from the stack and glanced around at the front of the house. Of the four hundred blocks and twenty bags of cement mix only two small piles remained of around seventy blocks and four bags. "Come on there isn't much left, if you want I'll grab the bags and you can go in and have a rest." Gloria smiled, shaking her fringe out of her eyes and clapping the dust off of her hands. "Think I might just do that. I don't get why you don't just leave it to the builders, we're not desperate to get this extension built, and it's not like we're strapped for cash to pay them to move this stuff and put it up." "True, but after what happened to Rezz on the last show I don't want to risk my finances in case the next pay packet can't cover whatever we need it for." Von Kelsig added. "What do you mean? What happened to him?" Gloria asked. Von Kelsig pondered for a moment on the situation before a fitting phrase came to mind to best serve it. "He's a write-off." Gloria shook her head and turned her nose up, while Von Kelsig started off for the last of the building materials. "So what does that matter?" She asked, tagging along behind. "Well, I was looking at a hefty bonus for starters. After my work in the Intense Division people took notice and when I expressed interest at moving into tag work they were all excited. That division hasn't ever had the kind of heat I would have been able to bring to it, but the bosses wanted me in there and I was supposed to be doing it with Nerezza." Von Kelsig groaned, hefting another slab onto his shoulder. "So what happened?" "Well, I get called into the office on the day of the show last week and told I'm working one segment with Rezz at the top of ReVolution and the tag deal is off. I don't know why and I haven't even been able to get in contact with him to find out what's going on...and..." Von Kelsig paused, suddenly realizing he was about to mention his investment into Nerezza's debauched Las Vegas club, which he hadn't even talked to Gloria about yet. If she found out he was even contemplating moving back into that line of business she would definitely put her foot down on it, after the uneventful circumstances surrounding his last venture. "And...what?" Gloria pushed, eyebrow raised. He had to think quickly. "And...I'm really pissed off that a guy I've worked with for years has just up and vanished. I don't get it. It's happened in the past, and it's really strange. People I've worked with and known for years just disappear without a trace, no one can find them. Whatever happened I just hope the guy is alright...Jesus, I'd hate to think something happened to him like what happened to me..." He shivered at the thought of Rezz being tortured in the same way he was. "Don't be silly. Anything could have happened, I'm sure he'll turn up somewhere safe and sound." "Let's hope so. Point I'm making anyway, is that without him that fat bonus I was expecting is gone. I've been re-allocated to work something else and until that gets going we need to watch our spending, OK?" Von Kelsig placed the slab of concrete next to the others and straightened up, groaning as he held his back and stretched the kinks out of it. "Oh well, better get a move on then. I'll go make a start on lunch." said Gloria, heading back into the house as Von Kelsig continued to toil under the baking Tennessee sun. As midday approached Von Kelsig heaved the last of the slabs around to the side of the house, wincing as the concrete scraped against his arms, leaving red welts peppered with flakes of torn skin. Brushing off the excess dust and skin he gazed across to the drive and sighed with relief at the sight of one last bag of mix waiting to be moved. Dragging his feet across the lush lawn he skipped over a small rockery next to the driveway and thudded onto the criss-crossing brickwork pattern that made up the road leading to the front of the house. Bending over, he grabbed the bag in his large dirty hands and hoisted it up onto his shoulder and marched back to the stacks of materials, dumping it beside the others. As he straightened up once more and held his back, breathing in the warm dry air he heard the front door slamming closed after a sudden breeze, followed by the thwack-thwacking of flip-flops on the driveway. Gloria appeared around the corner, hugging the side of the house as she called out to her man. "Ange, lunch is ready!" She began. Seeing the last of the building materials lying at his feet she whistled in admiration. "All done. When the contractors arrive they can get started straight away, told you it was a good idea." He called out, still catching his breath. "Well done, Muscles. Now get your butt in here before this soup goes cold!" She warned him, sticking her tongue out before disappearing back into the house. Von Kelsig chuckled and booted a bag of cement mix closer to the others. He stopped dead, hands on his hips and head tilted back. His eyes closed he momentarily lapped up the peaceful atmosphere, waves of tranquillity washing over him. Losing himself in the quiet of the dying morning he never noticed the sound of footsteps on the grass, silently approaching. The figure had emerged from behind the house, gliding across the lawn and tightly gripping a hand scythe. Von Kelsig stretched one last time as the figure neared him, the scythe lowered to the side as though ready to slice upwards and shank him in the groin. Before any such strike could be made, Von Kelsig turned around and gasped. "Jesus George, don't sneak up on me like that!" He said with a start. "Sorry boss, didn't mean to scare you." The gardener replied. Pulling the scythe up, he tipped his farmer's cap in greeting and wiped his hand across his glistening tanned chest. Clad in dungarees and shorts George still sweated in the heat of the day, even with the breeze and the lack of a shirt. "I cut back them nettles for ya sir, the pond's all clear now. I'll start on the gutters after I gets a bite to eat if that's alright by you?" George asked, squinting in the bright sunlight. Von Kelsig shook his head in agreement, glancing behind the burly gardener to the rose bushes peeking around the rear of the house. "How's the view out of the conservatory against the roses?" He asked, nodding towards the bushes. "View's fine if I say so myself." George replied, sucking air through his teeth and tapping the scythe against his thick legs. "OK, great." Von Kelsig began before suddenly pausing. "Actually there's one more thing George...I need to head into town later this afternoon, if Gloria asks can you tell her I'm just picking up some new equipment for you for the gardening?" George scratched the back of his head, staring at the ground. He pouted his bottom lip out whilst in thought, and for a moment looked simple. After a short pause he locked eyes with Von Kelsig once more and nodded. "A'ight boss, I can do that. Don't think she'll bother though, it's hella-warm out today." "Thanks George." Von Kelsig muttered, patting the worker on the shoulder and turning back to the house while the gardener went back to work. ---- "What took you so long?" Gloria asked between careful slurps of her home made minestrone. "I was talking to George about the garden out back. He says it's coming along nicely." Von Kelsig replied, tearing off a chunk from the crusty loaf situated between them. Dabbing it into his lunch he jammed as much as he could into his mouth, letting soupy drips run down his beard. "For goodness sake you eat like an animal." She tutted, swirling a slice of her own around the bowl and carefully tonguing the spongy bread into her mouth, careful not to dribble. "I am an animal." He replied stoically. For a while they ate in silence. Every so often Gloria would glance up at Von Kelsig, lowering her gaze back to her meal when he looked up at her. The air tensed gradually until finally Von Kelsig rattled his spoon over his bowl and clasped his hands, licking his lips and staring at his woman. "So what's up with you?" He asked. "Hm? Me? Oh...nothing." Gloria stammered, trying to feign control over her emotions, though she knew Von Kelsig knew something was the matter. "Well, actually..." She continued. "I was just thinking about us. We've been together for two years and now we have a home together, and I was just wondering......" A wry chuckle escaped Von Kelsig's throat, silencing Gloria. She tilted her head and furrowed her brow while Von Kelsig studied her nose as it wrinkled. "Where do you see this relationship going?" She didn't hesitate to say it, snapping out of her stare to deliver the point thus. The emotion slowly drained away from Von Kelsig's face as he thought about what she had asked, eyes darting down to his soup bowl before returning to Gloria's bright green eyes. His heartbeat quickened and he swallowed the lump in his throat before picking up his spoon and swirling the last of his soup around. "Well." It was a start. "I don't know. I love you and being with you...what do you mean exactly?" He asked coyly. "I mean are we just going to live together and be boyfriend and girlfriend or...are we going to become anything more?" She asked quietly. He smiled sweetly at her and reached across the table, wrapping his thick fingers around her hands and stroking his thumb along her wrist. "I do want more. I'd love to come home to Mrs. Von Kelsig and the kids but..." "But you have a career?" She butted in and immediately Von Kelsig understood why there was that awkward silence at the start. "Baby, if I don't have my career then there's no way we can have all of this." He raised his arms and gestured at the grandness of their surroundings. "And...I don't really know how to do anything else. I've been a wrestler for ten years, I'm not cut out for anything else." Gloria couldn't find the courage to tell him otherwise, though the expression on her face said more than words could. "So you don't want marriage and kids until you retire?" She asked, straining to stop her voice trembling. "No...well...no. I...don't know. There's no way I'd have kids with the schedule I'm on, and I wouldn't want a family on the road some three hundred odd days a year and never settling anywhere. I guess kids would have to wait until I quit wrestling." He shrugged. "What about..." She paused, pulling her hands free from his and stroking her ring finger, a twinkle in her eye. "Guess that all depends on how OK you are with my work. Do you want to be married to a man who's slowly breaking over time and can't find a whole week to stay home with you? I mean, what we have now is great...but..." "You know what..." "No, wait let me finish..." Von Kelsig knew straight away his opinion didn't sit well with her, and he was about to succumb to that inescapable hole that men dig themselves when asked a question by a woman, where the only correct answer is their answer. "I'm not upset." She rose to her feet, snatching up the plates and pacing towards the kitchen. "I just know what I want in life and I assumed you did too. I was just asking is all, don't worry about it." She stormed into the kitchen, her feet thudding against the marble floor. Von Kelsig closed his eyes and buried his heads in his hands, cringing with every accentuated clatter made by Gloria in the kitchen. Above the noise he could hear her mumbling erratically while tossing the bowls and cutlery into the sink. She continued her incoherent rambling for a while, unnecessarily cleaning surfaces and milling around in the kitchen. Von Kelsig took his cue and escaped the dining room, disappearing into the bar room and closing the door tight. He lumbered forward and threw himself onto the sofa, dissolving into the cool leather and staring at the blank screen of his HD television. Gloria had a point, and the crazy part was he agreed. This was his most successful run in a wrestling organization to date, and life was working its mysterious way to try and prise him away and force him into a normal routine. "Christ, if Dawkins can do it..." He whispered to himself, burying his face into the seat of the couch and holding his breath. Plenty of guys on the roster had families and he couldn't for the life of him work out how they did it. For all he knew theirs was a dysfunctional nightmare, with only the fallacy of a "family" being the veneer masking a hellish existence, and suffering the loneliness of the road. As it was he was right there in his own home, with a mortgage and a loving girlfriend who wanted that family life with him, and yet wrestling was in his blood. He remembered what he had said to George earlier, and sighed heavily. Glancing at the coffee table in front of him he noticed a scrunched up envelope sitting next to his cell, and raising an eyebrow he sat up and grabbed the envelope, removing the letter from within and reading it aloud for the nth time. ---- Dear Mr. Von Kelsig, You have been selected to participate in a company trial which aims to investigate physical and mental performance under different set circumstances. If you wish to know more please contact our representative, Ms. Saunders at your earliest convenience. Regards, Impega Inc. ---- A defeated pout developed and his heart sank as he flipped open the facia and dialled. Checking that Gloria wasn't lurking around in the hall, he turned away from the door and bowed his head. "Hi there, this is Angelus-..yeah Hessian......I'm fine thanks. I was wondering if you could help me, I'm looking for a contact number for Claire Saunders......Yeah Chris's manager......No, no it's her I need to speak with......yeah that'd be great, thanks..." A moment of silence ensured while the secretary on the other end of the line searched out the number from the online directory. Again, Von Kelsig checked the bar door, holding his breath so that the room was void of noise. "-...Five-Two-One, Seven.." "Oh, sorry can you repeat that again?" Von Kelsig snapped out of his daze to return to the phone call. Holding the cell up as she called out the number again, Von Kelsig keyed it into the screen and quickly saved it. Exchanging a few pleasantries he ended the call and rolled the cell phone in his palm while staring at the envelope, occasionally glancing at the door. He had an idea what the letter meant, but only Claire could clarify what was involved. While Gloria made some lemonade and sat out on the porch, Von Kelsig sat for a while in the bar thinking about what she had said. The wrestling business kept him away from this life at home which Gloria obviously pined for. For the first time in many years he had marked territory to bide down, buying up this magnificent country house outside of Memphis. What lacked was the love to fill the home, and without him here it felt empty, yet without the career there would be no house or home or family. He didn't know why she held her tongue when he mentioned being a wrestler through and through, though he imagined she had at least contemplated the same ideas as he had. One day down the line he would have to bite the bullet and make a choice between his family and his career. He couldn't juggle both, and he couldn't imagine being away from home for so long without being there for his family...on the flip side he couldn't imagine hanging up his boots to commit to caring for a family. Scrolling through the address book in his phone he came to a local number, and biting his lip he pushed Call. "Zales Diamonds...this is Claude speaking, how may I help you?" "Claude, this is Angelus Von Kelsig. I called in the other day about a ring order, I was wondering if it had come in yet?" He asked, unaware a smile had begun to develop on his face. "One moment..." The clerk paused to check the books. "Happy to tell you sir that it has. Will you be coming down today for it?" He continued. "Yes I will, in the next hour or so." Von Kelsig added. After he had hung up, another sigh passed his lips and he rose, leaving the bar and heading down the hall to the front door. Looking out the open front door he could see Gloria relaxing with her lemonade watching the surrounding countryside, and his heart swelled to see her fit in so nicely with her surroundings, like she belonged. Summoning up some courage he sauntered right out the door and stopped beside her. "I need to go into town for some equipment for the gardener." He said, maintaining a guilty hush over his tone. "OK." Gloria replied, her eyes stuck on the tree-line at the end of the driveway. "You alright?" He asked, leaning down to place a hand on her shoulder. "I'm fine. Don't worry about me." She took his hand and stroked it, the corners of her mouth turning slightly. He knew she would keep it on her mind until the miraculous day his pigeon's instinct kicked in and he said all the things she wanted to hear. Kissing her goodbye he walked across to the garage and roared away down the drive in his 2008 F-150. Sunbeams shot at him through the trees as he rounded the corner to the main road at the same time that a large white truck trundled into the drive; the builders. Waving, Von Kelsig passed by the truck and as soon as the wheels hit the tarmac he put the foot down. The growing paranoia in his mind eased when he thought he was far away enough from the house to bring out his cell once more and dial the newly saved number. After a few rings a sultry female voice came across the line, firm in tone. His heart skipped a beat as he made his next decision. "Hello, Claire? It's Von Kelsig here, I want you to tell me more about this trial......"
