Captain Justice, Issue #2: Invited

1 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-1-08 I Come in! Mi Casa es su casa. Bienvenue. Willkommen. No matter how you say it or how it’s said to you, an invitation is a grand show of respect. To welcome somebody into one’s home is an olive branch extending a feeling of friendship and trust to that person. Of course… "What? No?! She’s not supposed to be here yet!" Silver exclaims, nearly on the brink of a heart attack. Looking through the peephole of the Silver Lining Gym’s rear entrance, Mr. Silver sneers as he starts nervously chewing at his own fingernails. The visage of a beautiful young creature stands impatiently, bag in one hand, the other placed on her hips impatiently. As Silver utters quiet curses under his breath, Captain Justice peers through the peephole and inquisitively raises an eyebrow. "Mr. Chairman, all that I see is some young – a very fetching, might I add – damsel. She looks possibly distressed!" "She’s nobody, Cappy!" Silver retorts as he tries frantically to hide the blueprints entitled "FUCK KANNON OVER MARK TWO" involving a crude drawing of Sonny blasting Kannon in the face with a steel chair while a red, white, and blue-clad giant stands tall over him. More knocking. "Hold your goddamn horses, lady! Like you’re the first woman this desperate to come inside the humble abode of Silver!" … That doesn’t mean that the person standing on the other side of the door will always trust you. II Having the dubious distinction of being the first-ever member of the Kannon Clan to find herself at the gym of Son-MR. SILVER, CHAIRMAN OF PRIME-ny Silver, Isabelle furrows her brow as she waits for somebody to answer her persistent knocking. "What the…?" She blurts out in the presence of her white-washed nemesis, agitated tone increasing with each syllable. "Sonny, you asked me to come here!" A few more loud bangs just to infuriate Sonny. "I HAVE THE COSTUME FOR ROIDY RODDY PIPER! THE ONE YOU WANTED! OPEN UP!" On the other side, she can hear the shuffling of papers and whispers mentioning something about "screwing Mannon" or something to that effect. Before she has time to decipher any of it, numerous locks are heard on the other side being undone, including that of a safe knob turning. Perplexed, the sound of what one could call a retinal scanner is heard firing off a beam. The door finally bursts open and out juts the head of Mr. Silver, looking completely natural as a sweaty middle-aged man hiding plans to screw over his current adversary could look. "Heya… Iz… what’s… oh, sweet!" Akin to that of a certain Christmas-stealing monster (and I don’t mean the writer’s strike), Sonny’s lips curl into a devilish grin as eyes the package in Izzy’s left hand. "That what I think it is?" "Yes!" Isabelle sarcastically remarks. "It’s the key to your brand newlemmeinyouidiot!" "Hey, hey, hey!" Mr. Silver sternly advises her. "Next outburst like that, and I SWEAR, I’m gonna send your narrow little Kaley Cucuo-looking ass right back to developmental. Lord knows those breasts of yours could sure use a little… ahem… development." "Okay, we’re at your place. You don’t need to keep up the character?" He looks at her quizzically. "What character?" "Ugh. Nevermind." Letting herself in, Izzy shoves past Mr. Silver and throws the package on the table. "We also need to discuss business pertaining to my services in this whole matter." "Yes… busin-ass… er, business…" He says as he slams the door behind her, playing "Follow the Bouncing Ass." III TEN MINUTES LATER… "All right, all right, all right," Mr. Silver throws his hands in the air and concedes to hearing what Izzy has to say. "What’s this business we have to talk about?" Finding herself seated in a rather uncomfortable steel folding chair, Izzy tries to adjust her position in it to no avail. "Can’t I at LEAST get a padded chair?" She complains. "This thing is seriously killing my back! I went to a lot of time and trouble and… well, MORE time and trouble to get Captain Justice’s costume done and I deserve better treatment." "Heyheyheyheyhey!" Silver spews forth. "The chair you’re sitting in was powerful enough for me to stun an 800-pound giant in front of 120,000 people in the Orange Citrus Fruity Bowl of Two-Thousand and Yesterday! The dent that’s currently being covered by your ass was proof of this very feat! Now… enough of this nonsense. What business do you have to discuss?" "That UltraViolence thing." Izzy peers over both shoulders, then glances back at Silver. "Wait, is Captain Justice here?" "Nah, I just sent him out on a "Top Secret" mission. The very fabric of time, space, and the world’s overall safety is at stake, so we have at least a good thirty minutes." Fighting through the horrendous afternoon traffic that Seattle’s streets have to offer, Captain Justice tries valiantly to find the gumption to cross the street. "Citizens!" He screams, but the noise is drowned out by a cavalcade of honking horns and fellow residents shouting over one another. "Please! If this traffic doesn’t scatter, the world will surely perish!" He tries to jump into the road, but speeding cars hinder any sort of progress. He cups his hands over his mouth and tries again to reason with Seattle’s citizens. "Citizens! Excuse me! Please wait for me to cross the street so I can make it to 7-11 and bring Mr. Silver his double-chili-cheese Nachos! Your very lives depend on it!" "Before we get to anything else," Sonny gestures to the package marked "Kannon Designs" on the table. "Let’s see the costume, Izzy. It has to be EXACTLY to spec." "Hold your horses, already," Isabelle opens the box and undoes the white wrapping paper. In it, a shiny new costume looking like something Uncle Sam would barf out. Waving the costume in front of him like a Barker’s Girl showing off the BRAND NEW CAR~!, Izzy waits for a reaction. "It took a good while to finish this," Isabelle says. "Got the design exactly how you want it, matching star pattern and all. I’m a little disappointed I didn’t get to do Wolverine claws, but I feel it’s one of my better works. If this were any more patriotic, he’d have a flagpole rammed up his poop chute come Fourth of July." Nodding approvingly, Sonny leans back in his chair and laughs with volume that rattles the windows. "Perfect. The big lug’s gonna look great going into UltraViolence." "Speaking of." Eager to get right down to business so as not to endure this painful experience longer than necessary, Isabelle puts the costume away and sits back down, crossing her legs before pressing onward. "UltraViolence." The only word to come out of her mouth as she looks at Silver intently. "What about it?" "Well," she begins. "We know the plan. Xavier and your boy dance around for a few weeks in a little game of one-upmanship, wave their wangs around to see whose pen is mightier, then comes the big dance of UltraViolence. Your boy lays down for our boy and everybody goes home happy." "Yeah, that about sums it up. Why?" "On paper, everything sounds simpatico. But since we are neither, deaf, dumb, blind, retarded, idiotic, stupid, and we aren’t blissfully ignorant to your reputation. We made you this costume, but we’re not bringing condoms to the UltraViolence show just so you can screw us." "Your point?" Isabelle leans against the table, not taking her eyes off Silver. "We want some kind of compensation… a gesture of good faith if you will, just to make sure that this entire thing goes through and that we STAY on the same page." "Oh, I knew it!" Throwing his arms in the air, Silver shouts incredulously. "You and the Kannon wife are just trying to shake me down! This is a load of bullshit, you know! You think just because I’m made of money, I’m sexy, I’m an owner, a pwner, a businessman, and Hugh Hefner’s my homedawg, you think I’m gonna get you bitches into Playboy?! Please! Nobody’s gonna see you bitches there! You guys are just like Lost… once you open the hatch, it’s just not interesting anymore!" "No, dumbass," Isabelle tries to halt his ranting train of thought before he spirals out of control. "I just meant… Compensation. Otherwise, we out you to Cap J and he finds out what a scumbag you are." Settling his nerves, Sonny shifts his ugly gaze into a smile again. "Oh, you dirty little Kannons. Trying to blackmail me now, Iz? That’s kinda cute. But… we are on the same wavelength, Isabelle." He throws a red satchel on the table that nearly catches her off-guard. "Your talents and your part in this will not go unrewarded. Captain Justice needs the experience and no better than you guys." He gestures to the sack. "In there is 25K. I know Xavier just got back, but a home in Merlin Bay goes can’t be that easy to maintain before you get your first cut of Pay-Per-View scratch. Tell him I said Merry Christmas, Happy Birthday and have a delightful Kwanza." Checking to make sure it’s all there, Isabelle thumbs through each wad of cash. Satisfied with what she sees, she picks up the bag and beams. "Generous," Izzy shoots his way before shaking hands with Silver. "We’ll keep in touch. I trust the lug’s still in the dark?" "He’ll believe whatever I tell him," he assuredly tells her. "He’ll take a dive when the time is right." "He better." Those are the last words Isabelle utters before she slams the door behind her. Silver chuckles to himself, rubbing a hand through his thick beard. "Hehe, hatch… that was pretty good, even for me." IV "Mr. Silver?" Captain Justice’s voice rings out as he returns. "I’m back, sir, with your nachos as you commanded!" "Excellent." As he places the food on the table, Captain Justice jerks a thumb to the door. "Who was that woman I saw leaving?" "Nobody important, Captain," he unflappably tells him. "Now, let’s get back to your training." Nodding happily, Cap J goes to work on the speed bag while Mr. Silver parks himself in his seat and ponders his charge’s future. He was new to the business. He wouldn’t understand any of this, so it was best to keep him in the dark to what would happen. Perhaps, one day, young Soren Knox would be invited into Sonny Silver’s circle of trust. But for right now – just like Sonny had in his youth – he had to fall before he could begin to truly rise.
