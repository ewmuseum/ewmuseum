A bullet to the head and a shot to the heart

3 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 3, 2005 Leaving Amy when he received the phone call was hard for him. But the phone call was important because it involved a long time friend. It had been a few years since he had seen Seph in person. Killean remembered how Seph chose him in tU to replace him after he injured his arm. Sephiroth was a smart man, a great athlete and a dominating champion. But all the belts and awards in the world could not stop the assault, the cold unforgiving bullet that had put him down, perhaps permanently. The phone call was from Matt Ward. Killean had not heard from him in quite sometime. Sirrajin had moved forward in his life and rarely visited him anymore. He became accustomed to seeing Tchu a few times a week, but the need grew smaller. Killean was pulling his body out of the shady depths of depression and creating himself a whole new world to live in that he hoped would be free of turmoil and worry. But this one phone call instantly caused his life to sink into the icy cold, dark depths of depression once again. The car ride couldn’t have been any faster from Anaheim. Killean had it in his plans to go home to Winnipeg to unpack his belongings and truly have the opportunity to feel at home there. Instead, he was driving as fast as he could trying to enter the busy streets of Los Angeles at peak rush hour times, not a good idea. The drive, albeit short removed all the energy from the body and mind of the Supreme Machine, yet he pushed on, like a fish that migrates down a fast flowing river trying to battle the current all the way. It had taken most of the day and into the evening before he finally pulled up outside the UCLA Emergency Medicine Center. He made his way to emergency parking and didn’t even take the time to set his car alarm before running towards the emergency entrance at a frantic speed. As soon as he entered the hospital, a disgusting shiver went down his spine. A hospital was not Killean’s favorite place and he wouldn’t be here if it wasn’t for the urgency of the situation. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ A cold shiver ran down my spine as I walked to the reception desk. I saw a chart on the wall listing each patient’s room. The plain white walls made the environment seem extra chilling as hospitals always are. But I never saw his name on the list, surely he was here. This is exactly where Matt told me to go. I rang the small bell outside of the protective window and a younger nurse pulled the sliding window to the side and greeted me. “How may I help you sir?” The brunette was stunning and her voice was quite soothing to my ears but I digress. I was here for something far more important. “I am looking for my friend. His name isn’t on the list. It’s Sephiroth Traves.” It felt odd saying his last name. I didn’t really learn what it was until later in our friendship when I received a letter from him. “Yes, he is in intensive care. We have tried to stabilize him. I’ll take you to him.” She was kind and knew by the rather dismal look on my face that I was grateful for her help, but I just couldn’t feign any sort of smile. I am sure she understood. She led me down the back hallway into the Intensive Care Unit. This specific area had an even colder feeling to it than the rest of the hospital. I was asked to stop and wash my hands and remove my leather coat. I hung the coat on a hanger in the side closet close to the sink. I washed my hands with soap and water and the nurse instructed me to use the sanitizer too. I did that but had another shiver run down my spine. Could a room really feel so hard and cold? As we made our way through the security doors, I heard the beeping of the many different machines in the unit. The constant beeping would drive me mad after awhile, but I was here to see a friend who has been hurt. I had to put aside my petty complaints and be strong. But strong was not in my vocabulary as I circled the curtain alongside his bed. I saw Matt and Mary standing at his side. I almost fell ill when I took my first look at a once powerful man. He was lying on his back, arms resting motionless at his side. His chest was rising and falling at a slow pace. Surely he was sleeping. I saw his silver hair, soaked with his own blood and knotted into a total mess. Some dried blood was still spread across his forehead as the large white bandage encompassed his skull just above the ears. His face was motionless as well but very relaxed looking. I really was losing it all at this moment as I looked at my friend and almost felt like I was going to break down into tears. I approached Matt and Mary and gave Mary a hug. I also turned and shook Matt’s hand as the question about how he was seemed as if it was trying to beat its way from my soul. I couldn’t have said it soon enough. “What have they said?” I released his hand and he placed it on his face. Matt was obviously just as distraught as I was as he rubbed his cheek and eye, moving it back to brush his hair away. “He was shot point blank in the head. Only reason he lived was because the bullet didn’t gain the momentum to completely destroy his brain. It’s currently lodged in there and they fear removing it since it’s quite deep. Currently, as we have been here waiting for you, he has slipped deep into a comatose state.” The words tore at my heart strings like it was your typical puppet. Every key word affected me in a negative way. But the one that hit me that hardest was comatose. It was like a slap to the face of my soul, a kick while I was already down. How could it possibly be any worse at the moment? I turned away and had a tear rolling down my cheek. I sniffled as I tried to regain some sort of composure. I wiped the tear away and turned back towards the man I very much considered my brother. Matt however, continued speaking. “They… they aren’t sure whether he is going to make a full recovery or not. They don’t really even know if he will live at all. But they said he appears to be fighting.” Even worse. I never thought I would walk in to this hospital and be told that my friend, a guy whom I considered a brother, was in a coma and may never come out of it. I couldn’t even squeeze a word out, but I am certain my actions and looks spoke volumes about my current condition. My thoughts were running through my mind a million miles a minute and almost couldn’t put two and two together anymore. *Beep… beep ... beep* The machine set the tone of my thoughts. The beeping reminding me of what I was facing. *Beep… beep… beep* Continuously it was ringing in my head, mesmerizing me as I took the seat right next to his head. *Beep… beep… beep* It was the only sign that he was still alive. His breathing was so shallow I could barely notice the blanket over him rising and falling. I took the chair right beside his head, not taking my eyes of the mortal body of my fallen comrade. “Listen, we have been here all day. We need to get some rest. Need a place to stay?” Mary’s voice was breaking up as she tried to hold back the emotion. Matt was nodding in approval of the offer to stay with them. But I had to decline, my place was here. “I am not leaving his side, not yet. I just couldn’t…” “We understand. Keep us posted if anything else arises. You have our numbers.” Matt was even showing signs of mental and emotional weakness. It would take a truly cold hearted individual to not show some sort of emotion in such a devastating event. He was fighting, that fact was the only remaining thing keeping Mary’s head above water as well. As they left the room, I took another look down at my friend. I had never seen him so frail and so powerless. I could honestly feel the forces of life and death in the room tearing at his very soul, trying to decide which would win. I looked down and saw the top marking of a tattoo on his chest. ‘That was never there before…’ I thought. I pulled down the top of his hospital gown and I saw the tattoo in full. It was a tribal type design. I looked at it for a few more moments and grabbed a piece of paper off the table beside me. I then searched for a pen in the drawer beside me but only came up with a pen. I began to scratch the design down on the paper. I had hoped that if I took a drawing of the tattoo to a parlor, they would be able to recognize it. I had to get this tattoo, not out of want, but out of respect to my friend and to the man who I very much felt was my brother. *Beep… beep… beep* The machines were still going. It was the only sign I had that I still had my friend in this world with me. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The small, ratty hotel room couldn’t have been a first choice for him. He had to get some sleep but when there are roaches crawling up the walls, it may make it hard. Adding to the abomination that some would call a back alley, liquor bottles and beer cans lined the floor. It had been three days since Killean arrived in Los Angeles to see Sephiroth lying comatose. Most times as they days past people would begin to feel better and hope for the best. But apparently Killean was feeling worse. It was as if the devil himself was dragging the Supreme Machine further into the dark depression that had overcome him. Thinking drinking would solve the problems, Sirrajin laced himself with almost every alcoholic beverage known to man. He had showed up at the hospital the night before drunk out of his mind. He even tipped the cab driver Five Hundred Dollars when he finally arrived back at the hole in the wall he called a place to sleep. His mind was gone and his thoughts were elsewhere. But the drinks kept coming. He was lying on his side on top of the bed, covers all over the floor and pillows at his feet. The clock beside the bed read ‘3:34 PM’. But Killean was so out of it; all he knew was that at five in the morning, he was starting on another bottle which was now lying empty on the floor under some other beer cans. It looked like the life of a raging alcoholic who was unemployed and spent half his welfare check on booze. But this was the life of a rich man. Not even all the money in the world could buy him happiness at the moment. Suddenly, a knock came at the door. It was loud, but it was not enough to wake the sleeping Sirrajin. A second knock soon followed, but this time Killean stirred on the bed, knocking another bottle off the bed where is broke into quite a few little pieces. It was then silent, but the sound of keys jingling was heard instead. The door popped open and the sunlight filled the room. Stepping from the light, Tchu was seen thanking the manager for opening the door. He managed to weave his way through the bottles and cans towards the bed. Sirrajin was beginning to snore again as Tchu sat on the corner of the bed. But the movement seemed to awake the sleeping drunk. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I awoke quickly when I felt movement on the bed. I tried to open my eyes to look around but I had to shut them as the sun was too much for them and my aching head. I stirred around and began to rub my eyes when I heard a soft voice. “I bo… hospital yes evening.” What the fuck was that? It was all I heard and it wouldn’t have even made sense to someone entirely sober. “What?” I managed to say as I tried to sit up, the dizziness taking effect and sending me for a sideways tumble off the bed. I had only hit the floor two seconds before when I felt this person trying to help me up. I co-operated as best as I could and soon I was sitting on the bed facing the same wall he was facing. He spoke once again. “I said I know what happened at the hospital yesterday evening.” Ahhh, so that was what he said. I recognized the voice to be Tchu’s but I was not exactly thankful he was here to see me like this. After all he was my shrink and after all, he was a closer friend to Sephiroth than I was. But too many people close to me have gotten hurt and/or died and I just couldn’t stand having to add another person to that ever growing list. “Ugh… I didn’t know you were… were you there?” I was confusing myself even more when I was trying to talk, but the words just seemed to slur out and I couldn’t even conjure up a single bloody sentence. Why do I do this to myself? “Yes, Mary and I were both there and we finally saw the security guard throw you out. You know it will be hard to get you back in there after the disturbance you caused. I hope you don’t mind if I stole some money from you to pay for any damages. When they wrestled you to the floor, they took out your wallet and checked your ID. They put it on the table and I pulled out about a thousand dollars. You really caused a problem in there. But I happened to talk to some nurses and guards and explained to them that I was your doctor and everything would be taken care of.” Oh gee, that was just super wasn’t it? After all, I always wanted to go on a drunken rampage in a hospital, break some equipment and then get tossed out on my ass. Boy it must have been a lot of fun. Unfortunately I couldn’t remember it, much like plenty of the events from last night. I had seemed to think that I could hide away in a liquor bottle and all my problems would not be able to find me. Damnit what have I done? I recoiled in anger at myself and got up only to whip another bottle across the room. It smashed against the wall and broke into a million pieces. Tchu looked disappointed in me and that made it feel even worse. I buried my face in my hands and sat on the edge of the bed. As I began to openly sob, I slipped into a seated position on the floor. My hands were pressed so hard against my face that some pain was noticeable but I just didn’t care anymore. I was a wreck and I was an embarrassment. Oh if only Kira, or Summer, or Seph could see me now. I was sure at that point that I didn’t have a friend left in the world. But I was wrong. Tchu slipped onto the floor beside me and had me lean on him. I was still weeping and I could barely make out the words that I just had to get out. “My god what have I done to myself?! What have I done to deserve this?!” And cue the uncontrollable crying from a broken shell of a man. Sure I was trying to be tough and trying to keep everything together, but I don’t know many people who could. It all hit me like a freight train and it ended up breaking the back of my courage. I may be courageous when I climb in the ring and I may be courageous when it comes down to my physical health. But with all I have been through personally, my emotions are susceptible to attack. Meanwhile, I am sitting here in what most would think to be a precariously gay situation. Yeah, yeah, yeah. I am just happy that I have a shoulder to cry on at all. When I was dealing with the passing of Summer and Kira, for the longest time I was sobbing into a Kleenex or my own arm. Now, I have people to support me and for that I am ever grateful. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Sunday was a very long day for The Supreme Machine. He had gone to the tattoo parlor right when they opened and paid a very large sum of cash for the tattoo that he requested on his chest. Two artists worked on it for almost three hours to complete it. After it was completed, he went to go see Sephiroth in the hospital once again. He originally had no plans to attend ReVolution this week due to the gravity of the situation. He realized that he had an important match but it was not important compared to the health of his friend. However, he continued to think about it, trying to communicate somehow with Seph as he was lying still in the bed. He knew what Seph would say. He would want him to go to ReVolution and kick some ass. He wouldn’t want to hold Killean back in any way from his hopes and dreams. Most of all, Sephiroth would never stand in the way of Sirrajin’s successful career. Killean knew all this and silently decided that it would be best if he left that night. He didn’t want to have Seph on his mind when he climbs in the ring. Gratefully, Sephiroth was resting comfortably and Killean was trying to get on with everything. He had come back to the hotel room and began cleaning up all the empty bottles and beer cans. The television was on cranking music videos from MTV throughout the room. Killean finally finished cleaning everything up to make the little “hole in the wall” hotel room look acceptable for a checkout. He took a quick rest on the bed and leaned back against the head board. Seether’s Remedy finished playing and they went to commercial. Killean ran his hands through his hair and releases a sigh of relief. What a long week it had been. Friday was Canada Day and he couldn’t even spend that day in his home country. He loved saying that he was back in Winnipeg and he loved being a Canadian. But now he had to spend July 4th in The USA and he was going to see and hear all the celebration. He had silently resented living in the States for well over 5 years now and he always wanted to go back home. Now, he didn’t have to be down south but here he was. He had nothing against America and the fourth of July, but it reminded him too much of some unpleasant times. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I was sitting on the bed, running my fingers through my hair and thinking about taking a shower. Just as I looked towards the TV, MTV was now beginning to show the new video for The Backstreet Boys new song, Incomplete. No fuckin’ way! I thought the group was dead. Yet here they are on my TV. I had to admit, the song had a decent tune but there had to be something better on. I reached for the remote on the night table and tried to change the channel. No dice, the batteries seemed to be dead. I kept trying and trying but it was hopeless. I had a quasi-gay singing group on my TV and I couldn’t do anything about it. I didn’t want to get up just yet so I just tossed the remote across the room and it came apart against the wall. But that still didn’t solve my problem. I sat and listened to the song and it actually started making some sense to me. I immediately thought of Amy and how I had left her suddenly. She must have been a little bit worried. SHIT! I hadn’t even bothered to call her! What the hell is wrong with me? I am supposed to be the man in a relationship and I haven’t even called her in three days to tell her where I am or how I am doing. I left so suddenly and barely gave her an explanation. If I ever learned anything from watching late night television, never give the woman a reason to resent you. I might have just done that. One thing was for sure, I thought about Amy quite a bit. She was always on my mind, regardless of where I was or what I was doing… well, except if I was heavily drinking of course. The fact that I was drunker than a hundred and ten pound wuss boy at a frat party was irrelevant, or at least I would like to think so. But I knew that I should have called Amy as soon as I settled here in LA. Gee, what a great way to start off a relationship. I just hope Amy knew how much that she meant to me. Now, this Wednesday I would be in the same ring with her. It would be the first time since Culture Shock that I was in the same match as her. This time, we would be teammates and I had no complaints about that. Hey if we can work together in bed a wrestling match should be a piece of cake huh? I had noticed Ellis Easton getting a little over zealous with her in the ring during their match and to say I was getting a little upset with it was being far too kind. I had almost felt like running out there, but I thought against it. After all, Amy had said that once our relationship came out in public, it was finished. I hadn’t agreed but I was certain that we could make it work anyways. The Backstreet Boys had just finished their song on MTV and a song by a group called the Black Eyed Peas began. Well, there was no better way to get my mind off of The Backdoor Boys than to think about my stunning red headed vixen. But then again, I had only began to think about her again when the song was on. The name of the song did it I think. But I realized that I had been falling harder then I ever thought I would for a woman who had more piercings on her body than Michael Jackson had little boys at his ranch. I was falling harder than I ever thought I would for a woman who considered routine sex as common as walking. Boy was she an animal. But I really noticed; that I was falling harder than I ever thought I would for a woman who was there for me no matter what. That was the most important thing of all and I had to be there for her too. I felt Incomplete… empty and alone, but Amy Campbell made me feel whole and happy again.
