Into the Void

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-27-05 Kobe, Japan. June 12, 2005. 11:00 PM 'You're sure the caller was legit?' The phone pressed to his ear, Trevor Hawke paces back and forth in his motel room, walking out to the furthest reach of the cord in one direction before spinning on his heel and retracing his steps to the other side of the room. A murmur can be heard on the other end, though the exact words cannot be deciphered. Hawke's eyes sweep over the off-white walls, the blue-sheeted full-sized bed, the dresser with the mirror sitting above it, the tacky watercolor landscape hanging on the wall. He looks, but from the faraway look in his eyes, it is clear he is not really taking anything in. The entirety of his attention seems to have been consumed by his conversation. 'No, baby, of course I trust your judgment. I just don't want to get into things with Seth if there's even the smallest chance of this turning out to be a scam.' His face is damp with sweat, not surprising considering the room is lacking in central air. An archaic-looking air conditioner in the corner is loudly attempting to perform the job it was designed for, but considering the 75 percent humidity nearly visible in the air, it's fighting a losing battle. Judging from the look on Trevor's face, it looks like fighting losing battles might be the theme of the night. 'Well if you're sure, then I'm sure.' His forehead wrinkles and his mouth screws up in a frown. 'Seth, on the other hand...' Catching a glance of himself in the mirror as he passes, Trevor turns to face it, examining himself distractedly while listening to the person's response. He brushes a piece of lint off the front of his white button-up shirt, before readjusting his collar. Jean shorts and tennis shoes complete his ensemble, though they barely get a glance from him as he suddenly turns away in mid-appraisal. Hawke reaches up his hand and runs it through his dark brown head of hair, resuming his anxious pacing as he listens intently to the speaker on the other end. 'Well, I'll try my damnedest. I guess that's all I can promise. You know how stubborn he is, though.' A pause. 'Mmmhmm. Thanks for calling. I'll be sure to give you an update as soon as humanly possible.' Another pause, this time with a half-smile. 'Yeah, I'll talk to you later, babe.' A click can be heard, followed by the shallow droning of a ring tone. Placing the phone back on its hook on the dresser, Hawke's gaze slowly rises to meet itself in the mirror. A glimmer of purpose has returned to his eyes as he examines his reflection, almost looking as if he is sizing himself up. He sets his jaw in a look of determination....but only manages to hold this expression for a couple of seconds before it is broken with a sigh. Shaking off the lapse in confidence, he snatches his keys off of the dresser, swings the door open, and steps outside, almost visibly wilting as he steps out into the heat. He shuts the door behind him, jimmying the handle to make sure it's locked, before heading down the concrete walkway to the next room over. Standing outside the door, he readies himself for what's to come, though he can't help but think of all the things he would rather endure. A self-performed root canal, for example, or perhaps a Jon Kano segment. No, he thinks to himself. Nothing could make him sit through five minutes of Kano. 'Well here goes nothing,' he mutters, raising his hand to the door. The fist hangs in mid-air, as if second-guessing the commands being issued to it, before coming down upon the wooden surface with two stiff knocks. 'Hey, it's me, Trevor!' 'It's open!' shouts a muffled voice from within. Twisting the knob, Trevor pushes the door inward. With one final deep breath, he plunges himself across the threshold, entering the sanctum of his tag team partner of five years, Seth Masters. Taking in his surroundings with a sweeping glance, he quickly realizes that the room decor is basically a mirror image of his own. Same beige walls. Same dresser. Same noisy, ineffective air conditioner. Same bed, which Seth is currently lounged upon, looking rather relaxed as he sips his drink. A Jack and Coke, if the half empty bottles on the dresser are any indicator. He's wearing a black tank top and a dirty pair of jeans, clearly not expecting any visitors tonight, though from his tone, certainly not upset that his friend has decided to put in an appearance. 'Hey, hey! What's up, my brother?' Seth exclaims, smiling as he lifts his glass in acknowledgement. 'Help yourself to a drink if you'd like' Glancing down at the bottles on the dresser, Trevor ponders the offer for a few seconds, before deciding that sobriety is definitely the way to go tonight. 'Nah, not tonight.' 'Suit yourself,' Seth says with a shrug, swallowing the rest of his drink and placing the empty cup on the floor. 'So to what do I owe the pleasure of this visit?' 'There's actually a matter of business I wanted to bring up to you,' Hawke replies, managing to sound half-confident. Seeing his partner raise a questioning eyebrow, he leans back slightly against the dresser, continuing carefully. 'I just got off the phone with Shanda' 'So there's some business going on with Shanda? I don't get it.' 'No, no, not with Shanda,' Trevor says, letting out a weak laugh. 'Actually, I guess somebody called the house wondering how to get in touch with us.' 'You and Shanda?' 'No, me and you.' 'Yeah?' Seth asks, leaning forward with interest. 'So who was it?' Trevor hesistates, clearing his throat. Here was the moment of truth. No sense in putting it off any longer. 'It was this federation. PRIME. They've been on the rise as of late over in North America.' 'Yeah, I've heard of them,' Seth snorts incredulously. 'More sports entertainment bullshit. What the hell did they want to talk to us for?' 'Well...' Trevor stops, biting his lip. 'They extended us an offer.' Seth's jovial demeanor turns cold almost instantaneously. 'Ha...no.' He adverts his gaze, staring at the floor for a second before snapping his head back up to make eye contact with Trevor. He repeats himself, more forcefully. 'No.' 'I knew you would be dead-set against this...' Trevor begins, but Seth cuts him off in mid-sentence. 'Good. Then I hope you've already told Shanda to turn them down.' '...but I think this is an offer we should actually think about considering. From what Shanda was telling me, the talent recruiter she talked to was trying to assemble one of the most prestigious tag rosters in the history of the business.' 'Prestige? Who cares. We've got prestige right now.' Seth frowns at the incredulous look Trevor gives him in response. 'What? It's not like we're hurting for bookings. We could wrestle twice a night, seven times a week if we wanted to. They love us here.' 'A fucking boxing kangaroo could get bookings, but that doesn't make him prestigious.' 'What the fuck is that supposed to mean? That we're no longer the big names we once were?' 'No, that's not it at all...' 'Hey, no need to backtrack. Shit, it's probably true. That doesn't bother me, though. If I gave a fuck what the American fans thought of us, I would have never suggested we start working over here to begin with.' Disgust hangs thick in Seth's voice as he glares across the room at his partner. 'Don't tell me you miss wrestling over there, pandering to all of those low-class assholes.' Trevor shakes his head, remembering why they had come to Japan to escape in the first place. He certainly can't argue on that point. 'Nah, Seth. Fuck that.' 'So what is it, then?' Seth asks, in the manner of one posing a riddle to which he expects no answer. 'What can...PRIME, was it?....what can PRIME possibly offer us that we can't find here?' Masters is greeted with silence, and a smug look comes over his face, as if he has settled the issue with that statement alone. Trevor opens his mouth to speak, then closes it again, wondering if he should really go all out on this chance. Was it really worth placing all his cards on the table? Yes, he decides. He had already gone this far. Might as well go all the way. 'Let me ask you a question first. What exactly are we gaining by touring Japan from one end to the other, back and forth, hitting every small venue wrestling event we can find along the way?' Seth wastes no time in answering him. 'Peace of fucking mind, that's what.' 'No, seriously.' 'Can you cut the bullshit already?' Seth spits, a scowl enveloping his face. 'We've had this conversation a thousand times, so do I really need to spell it out for you one more time? You know as well as I do that we're here because there's actually some respect and appreciation for what we do for a living. These people, they don't demand for us to go out there and do swan dives headfirst onto concrete in our umpteenth ladder match of the month. For the first time in our careers we're treated as actual athletes, and not just the latest expendable pair of stuntmen. I don't know about you, but I sure as hell sleep more easily at night.' Trevor puts out his hands in a sympathetic gesture. 'Look, I can appreciate all of that. But I just can't help but shake the feeling that we're just wasting our time out here. I mean, shit man, our 'extended tour' has stretched out to thirteen months now...and we can still barely speak the fucking language!' 'Hey man, if you don't like it, nothing's keeping you here. I wouldn't want to force you to do anything against your will,' Seth says, resentment heavy in his voice. 'That's just it, though,' Trevor pleads. 'Nothing's keeping us here. It would be one thing if we signed a contract with a major promotion over here and started to establish ourselves again, but instead it's like we're just renting ourselves out. A couple of gaijin for hire, to spice up the card. We're not accomplishing anything.' 'We're wrestling matches we can be proud of, on a nightly basis! Isn't taking pride in your work enough? All the fame and fortune we got in OSW, and where did it take us? Nowhere. We were fucking miserable.' Trevor exhales deeply, clearly getting exasperated. The back and forth arguments are clearly escalating, and it's too late to stop the progression now. He fires back passionately. 'It's one thing to not give a fuck about fame and fortune and all that...shit..., but I don't want to look back, after spending fifteen, twenty years in the ring and have to deal with the fact that I've left nothing behind.' 'Legacy?' Seth spits venomously. 'Do you remember the last time we tried to create a legacy? Remember OSW? Remember those house shows we wrestled, where I could barely limp over to the showers, and those bastards still forced me to wrestle a match. At a fucking house show! No, they couldn't give a fuck less about wrestling. It was all about making money off of grown men killing themselves in the ring....and I was just another dollar sign to them..' Seth rolls up the leg of his jeans, revealing the surgical scars marring the landscape of his knee. He points at it with his right hand, continuing on vehemently. 'Well that was three years and four knee operations ago. And I don't think I have four more knee operations in me. I'm not going to let those sports entertainment bastards shit all over me again.' 'It doesn't have to be like that,' Trevor mutters, clearly at a loss for words following his partner's impassioned rebuttal. 'But it will be like that, Trevor. It always is.' Hawke pauses, closing his eyes, as silence descends over the room. Seth seems to be unsure of what to say next, rolling his pant leg down again and staring at the wall straight ahead in a sulk. Hawke can tell the conversation has just about come to the end, but before he leaves he feels the need to make one last statement. A final thrust toward victory; the verbal equivalent of a Michael Jordan jumpshot at the buzzer. 'Like it or not, in OSW we did make a name for ourselves. And it wasn't just getting over with the fans, either. We made waves in the industry. Let's face the facts, we can wrestle a thousand five star matches over here, and nobody will care. They may clap their hands and show us respect, but ten years from now, none of these people will remember our names. Those OSW championships, though, they meant something. And if you really want to be honest with yourself, then you'd realize that they're the only reason we're getting the bookings we are right now. OSW made our career, and without it, now...we're stagnating.' Seth shows no reaction to this comment, continuing to stare at the wall unflinching. Trevor turns to leave, taking a few steps toward the door. Placing his hand on the doorknob, he stops, turning to face Seth again. 'I'm sorry to throw this all on you all of a sudden, but...I'm just tired, you know. Tired of wasting my potential. Our potential. We'd have a chance to face some big name teams in PRIME, overrated teams that I know we're better than. Now we have a chance to prove it. Finally here's a chance for us to make an impact and solidify our legacy. Is this something we want to pass up?' Trevor swings the door open and takes one last look at Masters before he steps outside. Seth's gaze has not wavered once from it's unflinching stare at the wall. 'Just think about it, man. That's all I'm asking.' Trevor cannot tell if his comment even registers with Masters. Finally taking his leave, he returns to his room without another word. * * * Trevor's sleep that night is an uneasy one, haunted by dreams of fleeting glory. Many scenarios play themselves out in his slumbering head, a mish mash of a thousand individual thoughts. The backdrop is the OSW ring, the OSW locker room, the opponents, the wrestlers in the back a mixture of former OSW talent and the PRIME tag roster related to him over the phone. He and Seth are wrestling in a three way dance with PWA and Da Westsidaz. He watches as Dave Gibson turns on Azala Zameer, reforming the Throwbacks as Clay Vendetta runs to the ring and the two viciously stomp down the African Nightmare. History repeating itself, with a twist. He is in the ring now, having just reversed a move by Covenant member Horace Tully. He crawls to his corner, seeing Seth reaching out for the tag....and suddenly his partner begins to fade away, turning ethereal as Trevor's tag passes straight through his ghostly hand, making no contact. The next instant, Masters has disappeared altogether...and then the darkness consumes the scene. A knocking at the door jolts Trevor awake, and he is surprised at how heavily he's breathing. Not one who usually remembers his dreams, he is shocked by the particular lucidness of these. The knocking continues, undeterred by the lack of response. Glancing at the clock on the wall, Hawke realizes that it's almost four in the morning. Rolling out of bed to walk over to the door, he is too distracted by the content of his dreams to even register the oddity of the situation. Looking through the peephole, he sees a serious-looking Seth Masters standing in front of his door, looking rather impatient. Impatient and...something else. He'd seen that look on his partner's face before, but what did it signify. Suddenly it hits him. The look, it's a look of determination. Opening the door, Trevor acknowledges his partner with a nod as the two stand looking at each other across the divide, one man in and the other man out. 'Well?' Trevor asks. And Masters only hesistates for a second before giving his reply. 'I'm in. But only on one condition.' As groggy as he is, Trevor can feel the excitement welling up inside of him. He tries to keep from showing it, but a hint of a smile still manages to break through. 'What's the condition?' A sudden viciousness appears in Seth's eyes. No...it was there from the beginning, Trevor merely had not noticed it until now. 'This time around, we're the ones calling the shots. Fuck the PRIME management and fuck the PRIME roster. We take what we want, when we want it. And if anyone stands in our way, we show them no fucking mercy.' Trevor lets out a laugh, surprised at the callousness he hears in it. Visions of the future again float across his waking thoughts. They are defined by their complete lack of ambiguity. He sees that this is the way it has to be, and so he accepts it without argument. No fucking mercy was now a given, an elementary law and first principle. Nodding his head in agreement, Hawke swings the door open. With purposeful steps, Masters crosses the line that divides them, entering into the room, and with a slam the door closes behind them. And then...only silence, as the night outside the hotel runs its course. There is no sunrise in the morning. The storm clouds have obscured it.
