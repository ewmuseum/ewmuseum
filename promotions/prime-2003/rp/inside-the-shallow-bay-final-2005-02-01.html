Inside The Shallow Bay [Final]

1 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/1/05 Authors note: This roleplay might seem vague, however, the storyline will be filled in to a certain extent in the very near future. The original intention of the series was to build from the simple to the very complex issues dealt with in this roleplay. It's how when you think everything is okay, that it actually isn't what it all seems. The 'sins' will be explained better in the next roleplay. This is the building block to a very ambitious storyline, and is critical in that way. Hope you enjoy. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- It is simplistic in a way. Almost completely oblivious in another. For those in the know, the information is all there. The events have started to align in a mystical sort of cataclysm. Whether it is the current down turn in the wrestling, or if it is the surges of organizations that open and close, open and close, open and close, it is of no consequence. The seeds were planted and the plant has begun to sprout before us, this, our calm before the storm. And if you are on the outside, if you are outside the sphere of influence, you have no idea of the dark riders about to come and snatch you up from your place of slumber akin to the boogeyman would a fearful child. Whether it was the man who changed his face to simply appease his plans, whether it was the nicotine stained bare foot lunatic who spit whiskey and fire for simple joy, whether it was the man on the bed with only a coma to come out of his dream, it really does not matter. They all play their parts. It is just that some did a much better job than others. AOL Time Warner removed a critical obstacle. The simplicity is the heart of the design. When Craig Miles sold New Frontier Wrestling, he vowed that night to bring the greatest battle ever seen to professional wrestling. Whether they were ready or not. Rashard Clark told at his end days that the industry was on the brink of civil war and got into direct contact with who he thought would be the critical pieces. It's what drove CWL out of the United States. What sunk PCW. What killed Realm and OSW and Rush Pro. What removed the obstacles of WCW, of tSC, and basically devalued CSWA. This industry, professional wrestling, is not at war. And when the war does happen, it will not be organization against organization. It will be brother and sister, wife and husband, friends turned foes and foes turned friends who will engage. Everyone is involved. Every single person who has ever stepped foot in a canvas ring will be on the front. It's just where you are. Who you are. What you are doing. It's what you are hiding. And those are only the first to fall. Taste the milk & honey, the blood of your hateStep into shadows, where God gives no lightChrist will not save you, but the angels awaitThis is no heaven, just creatures of night. Wars have been started, deceptions cloud the airThe cold of Winters, OH MAGNIFICENT!Anger & Hatred, jealousies shall FlareThe end of days come, Young bloods circumvent. Whiskey & Fire blazes the chosen trailLost brothers and friends, enemies and moreA journey you face? a trial I fail?One city by the bay, where the lions roar. But that is a mere start. The first cannon shot tearing flesh apart and crushing into powder what was once bone. From there, a pair. Two men, on the edges. One on the outskirts of the industry, one right in the thick of. They are not pawns in this war. No, they play key roles. They will be standing long after the dragon fire peters out. They will be battling over the desert that the wolf once owned. The war isn't theirs, oh no. They are mere fallen angels, battling over the final battle ground. It's novel, their paths being so closely knit. But that isn't just it. The duel of the fates, it starts here. -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------Date: 10/7/04 Hers was an apartment in the heart of New York City. The building was atypical, with an aesthetic flare to it. It was somewhat pricey, but when you have four people living in the same room, the payment plan gets a little easier. Her favorite song played audibly from her compact disc player in her room, that being U2's 'Hold Me, Thrill Me, Kiss Me, Kill Me'. The place wasn't all that bad, with the white paint job and plush carpeting. However, the place was a mess of amps and beer cans, something that didn't please Brandon too much. On the couch before the television lay the bane of Youngblood's existence at the current moment, Kyle. Lindsay quickly ran over and jumped on top of him, causing an audible groan to come out of his mouth before he looked up at her. 'Hey, you need to lose some weight!' 'Shut up and kiss me, bitch.' And with that, the two lip locked. Brandon decided to break up the scene as it was making him physically ill to see his little sister making out. 'Hello, we are on a tight time budget.' Lindsay looked over to Kyle. 'He's goin' to let us stay in San Antonio. Isn't that great?' Kyle's face flushed with that of joy. He looked over to Brandon, his long curly hair in a huge mess and his only clothing being a pair of boxers. His eyes were bloodshot, probably from lack of sleep. 'Dude, that's great. I hope it is not any trouble.' Youngblood gritted his teeth. 'No. As long as you guys work, I got no beef. And no sex. That's important too.' Youngblood trailed off, as Kyle got up and gave him a huge. Brandon cringed in response. 'Dude, you have no idea how much this means, bro.' 'Trust me, I can't believe I'm allowing it. But I got to take care of the family' Suddenly, there was a knock on the door. Kyle nonchalantly walked over and opened it up, not really caring for his attire. The sight on the other side of the door shocked him nearly to death. A rough voice began to speak. 'Any you kids got a belt you can spare to a junkie?' ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The owner of the gruff voice was a strung out Japanese man, with long black hair and tattoos almost completely covering the arms. His eyes were bloodshot, and the lids sagged. He wore a beard, with some dried spit on it and his lips. His skin was pale, and there was a huge bandage over his right arm. He wore a pair of tattered jeans, with urine stains and stains from the outdoors, a pair of sandals, and a flannel shirt. He even wore a stocking cap over it all. His fingernails and skin were dirty, and he simply looked like a train wreck. You could see an open gash on the left side of his head that was probably infected. He was just a bum. A bum who used to be one of the greatest young wrestlers in the industry. He was Youngblood's former partner, and the meal ticket for most of the west coast for a short period of time. The man had taken part in the past FWO Survivor competition. That was a story for another day. He was also of Action fame. He is the very essence of wasted talent. Of what drugs and bile can do to a man. He was Alastair Nagatsuka. But man in the wrestling community knew him as Neko Case. The pair were now in a back alley, Brandon quickly taking his former friend out to find out what the hell had happened. He hadn't heard from him in almost a year. 'I guess this is what happens when you mess with the fires of Hell, right Al?' 'Your sin mirrors my sin Brandon. You've made deals with the devil too. You wouldn't BE on top without them! So before you lecture me about who I've fallen to shit, maybe you should reevaluate yerself!' Alastair was obviously strung out, but his words were not without their merit. Sobriety is the ultimate clear vision for some, where as for others it is just another state of self dilution. Brandon merely fiddled in his pockets for a couple seconds, before finally breaking the silence. 'Yeah. We all got to do something to make anything of ourselves in this industry.' Alastair laughed, but coughing took over the faux mirth. 'You a funny mothafucka Brand. Don't let anyone tell you any different. You know the rabbit hole, and how deep it goes. You are one knee injury away from bein' me. You are one revolutionary away from getting hung out to dry.' 'OCW.' The letters poured off his lips with relative ease. For Nagatsuka, they pierced. They ripped him open and tore him asunder. The synapse routing the messages in his brain most have skipped a nonchalant beat, because the joking manner Al had exuded suddenly changed. He got up out of his filth and grabbed Youngblood by the chest of his hoodie, forcing him into the wall. 'DON'T YOU FUCKIN' SAY THAT SHIT AROUND ME! EVER! YOU UNDERSTAND? WE GOT FUCKED! ALL OF US! PROMISES OF GREENER PASTURES AND PARADISE! WE DIED FOR THEM! WE STOOD TALL AND THEY GUNNED US DOWN LIKE CATTLE!' Alastair's erratic behavior was akin to that of a delusional Vietnam veteran. Somewhere between the cerebral, Al was still fighting in that 'war'. He was in his mind a freedom fighter. Ending the tyranny of Disney and get wrestling back on track. Making the Fans Wrestling Organization pay for selling out and becoming the enemy. The OCW was a way, in his mind, to avert what he thought was an impending industry wide war. As if this one thing was the make or break. A peace accord. But the FWO still stands, and the OCW does not. Alastair had fought for nothing but ideas and dreams. He let go and eased himself back. 'Look, Al, there is no reason to be this far gone. You think that just because one thing slips up that it is the end? Al, you and I carried the west coast independents with an iron fist. You know how we did that? Not giving up at the first sign of something bad.' Somewhere within Youngblood, he knew his friend was too far gone. But like most things, we allow our own sense of good will get in the way. He chuckled once again. 'Yeah, but you haven't been blacklisted. All we tried to do was change the industry. To make it more than just sweaty guys grabbing each other. Wrestlin's art man. My father showed me that. We put ourselves on the line to paint pictures of gladiators. We tell the stories of the great battles when to go out there and bump. It's not simple.' 'That has nothing to do with wrestling as an art. And this doesn't either. Fuck, you are strung out Al! You stink of crap and alcohol. Get something straight, just because your little world crashed DOES NOT MEAN YOU GO INTO THE GUTTER AND KILL YOURSELF!' Brandon was trying. He was trying to understand the situation his friend was in. But it was of no consequence. Veterans who have seen their comrades die before their feet are not easy to bring off the outskirts. And that was where Alastair was. Firmly planted on that outskirt, in the far east, while the rest of the world kept moving on in the far west. 'Look, Al, you need to get to rehab. Clean your self up.' Brandon had wanted to say that for years. Only now, when he felt this much superior to him did he dare say it. 'What I NEED is my dealer. What I NEED is another hit. What I NEED is an overdose, so I can finally sleep.' Crazy talk. 'Jesus Christ Al, look at yourself. Two years ago, you'd be liable to piss on yourself. Two years ago you were on top of the world, with money and all that. Not that much can change.' Alastair grabbed into his jean pocket, pulling out a crumpled cigarette. It lacked a filter, and was bent back and forth. The white paper had turned a shade of yellow. Al grabbed into his pocket and pulled out his cheap BIC lighter, lighting the cigarette and inhaling deeply the cancer into his lungs. 'Yeah. Right. Tell me that when you lose the last bit of cash you got on loan shark.' Brandon was getting more and more shocked by the situation. 'LOAN SHARK?! What the hell you dealing with those people for?' 'Look, Brand, some shit is better left untouched.' Before him, this person was a child. Brandon was going to support his sister and his girlfriend, and do it very well. To see someone completely screw off like Alastair was inexcusable to him now. It was time to drop the gloves. It was time to get into it with him. May that include a shovel to the cranium or not. 'Look, you piece of shit, grow up. Things didn't go your way. Wallow away. Real men deal with that stuff.' 'WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE I AM DOIN' BRANDON? PLAYIN' PATTIE CAKE? I'M DEALIN'!' Youngblood quickly retorted. 'The HELL you are Al! All you are doing is getting high and pissing all over yourself!' Al coughed as he sucked in more smoke. 'Some people deal differently.' 'Look, what if I told you I got connections you a booker down in Florida that might get you work?' Brandon was trying to get Al back into the business, calling on a favor from an old friend. Alastair just shrugged his shoulders. 'Miami is a long ways from here Brand. And last time I checked, I was dead ass broke.' 'You want back in the industry, right?' Silence. 'RIGHT?' Alastair flicked the cigarette away at this point, somewhat reluctant to admit that out of all the things he needed, he needed the industry more than anything else. 'Yeah.' 'Well, all you got to do is get clean and they'll take you in. There is a place in Port St. Lucie that you should know from Rashard. Morgan is there. She'll probably take you in just for the company. Besides, she went through the same stuff in the Asylum.' Alastair coughed. 'So, using the sins to your advantage? Using sin to eliminate sin does not do away with what you have done. Ever thought that me being dead might be the best thing? If I was to get to my feet, you know how I am.' Brandon knew. He knew too well. He would hunt down those he felt had done wrong to him, and make them pay ten fold. The war that Rashard told Brandon about would have a firecracker. Alastair back on his feet, after being in the bottom of the hole, would be the worst thing the wrestling industry had ever seen. Al had the fortitude back when he was eighteen to kill a pair of promotions because they didn't pay him what he felt he was worth. He had manipulated people into ending careers. And that was only on a small scale. If put out there, he'd take on Durden like status. He'd be a general. He'd build the army and wipe out entire global promotions at his mere whim. However, for Youngblood, there was that part of him that was still his friend. That he felt could change. Al just need to get back onto his feet, he thought. Once he did that, he'd realize just how foolish revenge would be. In ten years, they'd be able to sit at a table and laugh about this. He wished. He hoped. 'Look, it's a raw deal. But time heals all wounds.' 'Time heals all wounds. Even the deep lacerations that inundate blood like a geyser. Some gashes do not heal as painlessly. And some warriors strike back without any indication.' Alastair giggled like a moron afterwards. Brandon didn't know if it was the drugs, or Al giving an outburst like it was just always there in his head, rattling around. Either way, it scared Brandon. Youngblood dug into his pocket and pulled out a hundred dollar bill. He put it against Al's chest, and he grabbed it away. 'Al, go to Miami. That should be enough for the Grey Hound. Check into rehab. Become the man you were meant to be. Just because you got a rough start doesn't mean you can't come back.' 'You are right, you know Brand. I'll clean up. I'll take you up on the offer, but after that, I give no promises. And if I do stand on top, do not stand in my way, or else I will end you. Charity is nice, but my soul is empty now, Brand.' The future is a mystical thing. You can predict it, but until it happens, you do not know what will happen. Brandon had given his friend a new start. A new lease. As long as he keeps his promises. For Brandon, the last thing Alastair Nagatsuka said to him on the seventh of October two thousand four was not the greatest thing. Brandon knew Al like the back of his hands. He knew he always kept his promises. Always held his grudges. Never forgot. Never let a detail slip that he could control. If Brandon ever saw Nagatsuka again, he knew what it meant. And while nobody else might understand, it would be enough to keep Brandon up nights. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The car was packed. His and her suitcases neatly put together in the back of the Hummer. Brandon looked at the beautiful New York skyline as the stars of night beamed heavenly down upon all. The lights still hummed and the streets still bustled. The city that never closed its eyes for even a slight nap was living up to its name. Brandon looked over to shotgun, where his sister slept peacefully. In the back, her boyfriend was gaining some much needed sleep as well. Brandon was all alone now. In more ways than one. He had pushed his deals to the background. He wouldn't let Melissa or anyone else know exactly what he knew was coming. Or what his role was. But for a impressionable youth, you'll sign anything away to get what you want. Brandon wouldn't be here without what he had done. Without what he had given up. He was a success. His only problem was the guilt of being able to prevent an onslaught before it ever happened. Wrestling rosters are akin to family. Over time, they bond and become a self supporting unit. There wasn't a union around to take care of wrestlers. It was them and them alone. Owners and such had their rosters. But with the number of organizations around now, it was impossible for talent not to find homes. Which makes the future so dangerous to Brandon. He knows people. He knows the twisted folks who feel that the perversion of wrestling has gone far enough, that it is time to scour the landscape and start anew in their image. These people were talented enough that they had to be employed. Brandon looked at the two. How blissful they were as their dreams took them wherever they wanted. It must be nice to do such a thing, Youngblood thought. But tomorrow, it will be different. He'll push the thoughts tormenting his very being further into the recesses of his mind. He'll lie to himself that there is nothing wrong. That all he has to look forward to would be a prosperous career. And maybe he does. It's not a conspiracy theory when you have foresight.
