The Spoils

19 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 19, 2008 The dull, pulsing pain of his left knee slowly drags Tyler Rayne from his fitful slumber. Everything aches. Even his eyelids are throbbing under the pressure of fluttering open. Hell, who knew eyelids even could ache? “Son of a fuck.” Sitting up feels like wildfire spreading over the muscles of his abdomen. Swinging his legs over the edge of the bed is such a task they may as well be made of lead. Each breath feels like someone poking his ribs with a pointed stick. His arm’s so stiff it barely moves at all. It takes a legitimate, concentrated effort to straighten his fingers enough to run them through his hair. It is said, that, to the victor go the spoils. Tyler Rayne’s rewards for surviving the Dual Halo certainly feel spoiled. A ceaseless, unrelenting ache that pulses through his whole body, both in waking hours and sleep. The searing fires of agony that shoot from muscles he’d long forgotten ever existed. The ragged, pained breathing. The occasional, completely involuntary muscle spasm. Oh yes. The spoils of Dual Halo victory were about as rotten as day old milk left to boil in the Tatooine suns. Despite the intensity with which his body screams for mercy, however, these sensations are not exactly foreign to Tyler Rayne. Pain has always been his constant bedfellow. Suffering his unending legacy. Have the atrocities of the Dual Halo taken that legacy to new and unimaginable heights? Yes. Has the pain and agony of his everyday life been amped to an nth degree? Absolutely. But Tyler Rayne wouldn’t have persevered the tortures of PRIME’s most malicious invention if not for his ability to overcome. Just as now he overcomes the pain and ache of war to slide a pair of ragged old jeans over his Spider-Man boxers. Just as he ignores the agonized cries of muscles as he stretches on an Aqua Teen Hunger Force t-shirt. Numbah One in da hood, G. With an unpleasant sigh that has little to do with his current physical condition, Tyler reaches for the cell phone on his nightstand, quickly pressing the green phone icon twice to cue up the last dialed number. He patiently listens to the personally chosen hold music of his intended, lips mindlessly mouthing the words he’s grown to know all too well in the past few days. After the usual twenty seconds or so of waiting, Tyler closes the phone, swallowing yet another disappointing attempt. Fortunately, his mind is so distracted by thoughts of the ill-fated phone call that he barely notices the pain of standing. The door from his room creaks on its hinges, opening him to a rowdy and raucous outburst from the common room. Tyler shambles down the hall, ignoring his haggard reflection within the frames of his treasured Star Wars posters, (some of which have been autographed by the likes of Mark Hamill, Carrie Fisher, Billy Dee Williams, Frank Oz and Ian McDiamird). He stumbles into the kitchen, hand reaching for the handle of the fridge before a shiny glint catches the corner of his eye. Now that he’s noticed the kettle on the stove, there does happen to be a faint, sweet aroma wafting about the air. “Tea?” “Figured you might need it.” Katterina Wylde just manages to squeeze out the sentence between bites of sausage and scrambled eggs. “You’re a doll.” Tyler can’t help but wince at the pain that is reaching for the cupboard, but a mug full of Japanese herbal tea will do more than a little to soothe the pulsing ache that is his body. He leans against the stove, sipping tea from an Avengers coffee mug and watching quietly as the raven-haired vixen shovels up the remnants of her eggs. “Any left?” Cheeks puffed with the last of a very tasty breakfast, Katterina shakes her head in the negative. With a roll of her eyes she nods her head backward, indicating the common area just behind her. The lithe little minx slides off her barstool, removing her empty plate from the breakfast nook and gliding into the kitchen next to Tyler, where she deposits the dish in the sink. Tyler barely pays the woman any mind as she begins to wash up the pans from breakfast, only acknowledging her enough to move from the stove when she gives him a less than gentle shove out of the way. His focus is fixated upon the near seven foot frame of blond-topped Brit lounging across his couch, television remote grasped firmly in hand. An empty plate lies abandoned on the glass coffee table. The fork upon the plate gives a little rattle as Duke Valentine lets loose with another entertained whoop of excitement. “Con was right, ya know? This TiVo shit is bloody brilliant.” “Yes, Duke. It’s quite amazing the entertainment my money can buy for you.” With an amused sigh, Tyler turns his back on the Brit, now leaning against the nook to watch Katterina wipe up the last of her mess. She does her part to pretend not to notice. “Don’t you people have your own houses to loiter in?” “Of course we do. But you have better food here.” “Ah. Love you too, kid.” “Says the man who stabbed me with a sword.” “Hey. You know, there are some cultures in the world that say I love you with the point of a sword.” She stops, throwing the dish towel over her shoulder as she turns to face and challenges him with the raise of a skeptical eyebrow. “Is that so?” “Absolutely.” “Name one.” “Oh. Well there’s the…um…you know…from the…place that…uh…the place that... Yeah. I got nothin’.” “Didn’t think so.” She flashes a victorious smile and smacks him with the towel on her way out of the kitchen, throwing herself into the plush comforts of a chair in the common room. Tyler takes a moment to refill his mug before joining the party. “The fuck’s so damn entertaining anyway?” The answer comes in the recorded image of himself pacing about the second floor of the Dual Halo, laying waste to any unfortunate soul stupid enough to walk within the reach of his heavy-handed steel chair. As he rarely takes the time to relive his own accomplishments, or failures for that matter, it’s a somewhat odd sensation for Tyler Rayne to be watching himself on TV. He watches quietly as the doors of the Halos open and he tears sprinting through the tunnel. Something gnarled and unpleasant grows from a knot in his stomach as each step brings his recorded visage closer and closer to the demise of one Danny Ferguson. At the time there’d been so much adrenaline pumping, so many other thoughts and worries cluttering his brain, that he hadn’t really noticed just how brutal the attack had been. But now, days removed from the event, he could watch this replay, the impact of his knees against the steel chair, the blunt trauma force that sent two grown ass men hurtling from a height of fifteen feet, and…and the look on her face when she drops to his side. The expression of disgust and horror and betrayal and utter disbelief when Lindsay Troy looks up to the second tier and the man responsible for…for what, exactly? There’d yet to be any word released on Danny Ferguson’s condition. No doubt his agents and Hollywood caretakers were keeping mum on the situation until they knew for sure exactly what it was they wanted to announce. No sense in risking further damage to his career by inducing an unnecessary scare amongst potential employers. “That, sir…” The accent pulls Tyler from his reverie and back to the screen, where he fully wishes Duke hadn’t been as daft as to pause on Lindsay’s horrified expression. “…That was fucking fantastic. I can’t believe you actually did that to someone. Fuckin’ wanker got laid out.” Despite his personal feelings on the subject, which were somewhat less approving than Duke’s, Tyler couldn’t help but laugh at the Brit’s response. Duke had been living in the States for years, making a fine living off professional wrestling and, more recently, steady gigs as a quasi-popular star of straight-to-DVD action flicks. The man was, for all intents and purposes, Americanized. Every now and then, though, a bit of the Queen’s English, or whatever the fuck he called it, would slip, making him sound like some sort of ridiculous parody of the proper Englishman he used to be. With a shake of his head, Tyler removed himself from the scene before Duke could rewind the program and watch that “brilliant” move again. He shuffled over to the brand new sliding glass door, slipping through the scant opening and stepping out onto the warm sands of his very own private beach. The temperature was perfect. Warm, but not too hot, with the occasional ocean breeze blowing up to ease the convoluted mess of emotions within. He sensed her presence a full three steps before she was close enough to put a friendly hand at his elbow. “You okay?” “Yeah. Just…got a lot on my mind.” “Bullshit. You’ve got one thing on your mind. Just like always.” He laughs. “S’pose so. Wish it could have rolled a little different. That’s all.” He takes a sip of tea, momentary silence falling between the two. “The fairy’s supposed to be your friend, right?” Another laugh. “Tsonda? Yeah. Well…mostly. Bit of a selfish prick at times, but when it counts…yeah. He is.” “Ya know, I’ve lost count of the number of times you’ve saved my very sexy ass from some ugly and terribly untimely beatings. In and out of the ring. But I know, you had it to do all over again…you’d do the same thing. Even knowing the consequences. You’d still do it again. You’re an annoyingly loyal little asshole, Ty. A hundred times over you’d save that little pillow biter’s career. Just the way you’re programmed. And anyone who’s really your friend ought to be able to appreciate that. Regardless of who was on the other end of that chair.” “I hate it when you get all introspective and sentimental, Katt. It goes against the natural order of things.” “You just hate it because I’m always right.” He rolls his eyes above a smile. “Yeah, well, there’s that.” Before either of them can work into the next lovingly clever exchange, a chime reminiscent of the Super Mario Bros. theme song echoes from within the condo. Both turn in unison, training a gaze on the front door, tracing a line over to Duke Valentine’s childish grin and complete infatuation with the television, and then back once again. Tyler takes a step forward, but Katt puts him at ease with a shake of the head and a raise of her hand. “I got it.” The sand is warm and soothing between his toes. Another of those cool breezes blows up from the ocean. For a moment, the world is perfect. Which is something of a cheap reminder how very imperfect everything is. He digs into the pocket of his jeans, fishing out his cell phone and quickly dialing that number up again. This attempt doesn’t seem to be any more positive than the last. “OH! MY! GAWD!” The shrill cry rings out across the condo, an unexpected outburst from an even more unexpected guest. H e hears the little pitter patter of rapidly moving feet behind him, and turns just in time to catch Angelica Brooks. Her overzealous attempt at a hug turns into something much more akin to a Thesz Press. As, it would seem, they tend to do. He hits the sand hard, not completely unaware of the hundred pound impact of a beautiful twenty-something on his groin. Her blazing red hair falls about her face as she smiles down at him. “I can’t believe you fucking did it!” “Um…ow.” “Huh?” “Falling. Hurts. A lot.” “Oh. OH!” She quickly scrambles off of him, even attempting to help pull him up. Angelica Brooks, of all the things people have and will call her, is certainly not a powerhouse. However, the attempt is appreciated, and he does his best to make her feel like it’s necessary. The pain shooting through his spine is too great to attempt to stand, so he does his best to pass off the sitting position as more of a choice than it really is. The bubbly little ball of energy doesn’t seem to mind, though, and quickly plops down right next to him. “This is so fucking amazing! I mean, really. YOU! You won the Dual Halo!” “Yeah. Well…I told you I would.” “Yeah, but…you don’t sound very happy about it.” He shrugs. “Why aren’t you happy? This is…this is AWESOME!! You should be celebrating. Why aren’t you celebrating?” “Don’t feel like it.” “Please. You’re Tyler Rayne. You’d celebrate successfully zipping your pants if it gave you an excuse to drink. This is about Lindsay, isn’t it?” “Do you have to say it like that?” “Like what?” “That…tone.” “I don’t have a tone.” “I distinctly heard tone. Besides, I still don’t know what the fuck it is you’ve got up your crawl about Uni, anyway.” “Not you, obviously.” “Pardon?” “Nothing. I just don’t understand why it’s such a big deal that she’s pissed at you. She should be happy for you. You just won the biggest match in PRIME history. Fuck her if she doesn’t want to congratulate you.” “Fuck her?” “Well…not literally.” He laughs. A genuinely amused little giggle. “You’ve been hangin’ out with me a little too much, B.” “I don’t think so.” “ ‘Course you don’t. Look…it’s complicated. Just forget it.” “Try me.” “Okay. Fine. I’ve got titles. Lots of ‘em. You’ve seen the Manor. They’re all over the fuckin’ wall. I’ve been given all kinds of credits and adulation in my time. Won all sorts of big matches and fancy rewards. Got more kudos than I’ll ever know what to do with. But ya know what I don’t have a lot of?” He points back into the condo. Duke Valentine has, at the very least, managed to sit up so that Katt can squeeze in on the couch next to him to watch the show. “Friends. Good friends. Real friends. That kinda thing don’t come ‘round often in this business. When I go out and party. Take you or Pierce or Uni out for a drink…that’s what I’m celebrating. Good beer and a good friend…life doesn’t get better than that. It’s stupid, maybe, how much I lean on that. But I’ve had a long life of being fucked over and knocked down. Anyone willing to stand next to me like that, like you…that’s worth more than anything I’ll win in the Dual Halo. May have a Golden Ticket, but what I might have lost…that’s worth more than any wish PRIME could ever grant.” “She’s not the only friend you’ve got, Ty. And you may not have noticed, but the rest of us are pretty damn happy for you. And proud. Now let me tell you something. Most people who come through PRIME look at me and see a nice ass and a fake pair of tits.” “Those’re fake?” “Wouldn’t you like to know.” “Eh. You do have a nice ass, though.” “I know. But you…you took me in. Saw through the surface and…you’re actually a friend. A very good friend. And I don’t like my friends to be sad. Even if it is for a stupid reason. Or over some stupid girl. So get up, get your ass in here, and pour me a drink. Then we’re gonna watch you win this Halo again. And you’re going to enjoy it. You got me?” He smiles. “Yes, ma’am.” “Good. Now hurry up. I wanna get fucked up.” With a smile and a kiss on the cheek the bouncy red-head pops up and damn near skips back into the condo. Unable to keep himself from laughing, he just watches as Angelica throws herself onto the couch next to Katt. Far from anticipating the jolts of pain it’ll bring, Tyler Rayne prepares to push himself from the sand. But as he hand slips between the grains, his finger passes along the smooth edges of his cell phone. One last try couldn’t hurt. A breathless moment passes before it becomes obvious that this call will be no different from the others. With another heavy sigh, he listens to the recorded message, apparently the closest he’ll be getting to talking with Lindsay Troy any time soon. "Yep, you found me. Leave the details at the beep."
