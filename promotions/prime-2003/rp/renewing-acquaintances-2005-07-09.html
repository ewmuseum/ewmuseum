Renewing Acquaintances

9 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9th July 2005 Many months ago Paul Cain would not have sought the solitude of a dark and empty bar. “The Serial Thrilla” would have longed for the throng of beautiful women with their defences lowered by the aphrodisiac of alcohol. He used to love the feeling of one behind him and one in front of him, rubbing close with their bodies pressed against his as the music would pound out across the club. In fact, he was all about the adoration and the image that he had, the mirror was his best friend. He would keep a beautiful woman on his arm wherever he went and he would always smile that mocking smile towards the men that weren’t so fortunate. This was the life of a “Serial Thrilla”, money, women and self-love, a simple but pleasurable recipe for a fulfilling existence. All it takes is one event to make that existence crash down like breaking glass. Cain: Get me another fucking drink, and make it a double. Paul Cain sits on a bar stool looking up a small television playing classic sporting re-runs. He is dressed in a black hooded jersey and black jeans with his long hair covering a face marked with disdain. He tosses his empty glass towards the bartender without even looking at him. The bar itself is deserted save for Paul Cain and the unfortunate owner of this establishment who looks with an air of heavy caution as he goes to pour another shot. This is not the image of the “Serial Thrilla”, this is the picture of a fallen man desperate to claw his way back up. Danger surrounds him and the bartender himself was dreading the prospect of calling time on Cain’s wallowings. Cain: You might want to stop eyeballing me too, ask Jackson Darilek what happened to the last person that got under my skin. I don’t take kindly to people eyeballing me. Jackson Darilek had become the first innocent victim of the reborn Paul Cain, a man who was now black of heart and devoid of conscience. He was displaying an air of inapproachability and he was grateful for that as it seemed to him that people were becoming very overrated. The bartender approached Cain and set the glass of whiskey down in front of him cautiously. His grey and receding hair was enough to tell that this man had probably enough violent drunks in his time to not be phased by Cain. However, he still showed an air of caution in the way that he addressed his customer. Bartender: I’m going to be calling time on this joint shortly, you’ll have to make that your last. Slowly turning his head in the direction of the bartender, Cain glared at him with a look of absolute hatred for another innocent party. This whole scene had the potential for disaster and the owners face changed to one of fear as he took in the response to his polite request, recognising the threat. Gradually Cain brought the glass to his lips and sipped it excruciatingly slowly not taking his eyes off his opposite number for a moment. The tense and awkward silence was broken by the door of the bar opening behind Cain. Into the premises walked Claudia Lopez, dressed in a long white trenchcoat, covering her denim jeans and white blouse. As she walked into the door and her jacket flapped open, it exposed the bump of pregnancy blossoming from her stomach. Claudia: Its okay, I’ll take care of him. The bartender backs away and goes back to his duties as Claudia cautiously approaches Cain, standing behind the barstool next to him. Claudia: Is this seat taken? Paul stares down into his glass and swirls the contents around and he never takes his eyes off the whiskey. Silence fills the room again. It is said that when women are pregnant, they give off a glow and a radiance; well even that cannot brighten the darkness that seems to follow and surround Cain like a shadow. Cain: First Tyler comes out of nowhere and now you refuse to take a hint. Those people who share the name ‘Lopez’ are persistent like an untrained dog. You just don’t know what’s good for you, do you? I ruined Tyler’s night during Nightmare on Elm Street and I ruined YOUR life in an attempt for you to quit hanging around my neck. What is it that your kind don’t understand? She pulls the barstool back a little and takes a seat on it, ignoring his dismissive and aggressive tone. She rests one hand on her pregnant stomach and the other on her right knee, looking at him with sad eyes. Cain refuses to visually acknowledge and takes another long sip of his drink before looking back at the television. Cain: I thought I told you to flush that ‘thing’ down the toilet. It only took two utterances from Cain to make Claudia look away and swallow her emotion down. She could not believe the horrors that PRIME had brought to this man that she had loved. He had been accused of so many vile things in his life but none of them had been true, she had known that and stood by him each time. However, since Vampir Nosferatu had put his hands on Paul it was as if something dormant had been awoken. She had tried everything and bearing his child had been the final attempt at bringing humanity back into the man she loved. Yet now, as she nervously looked back him, the sting of those words struck her and brought reality with them. Claudia: What has happened to you, Paul? You would never have said something like that months ago, never. Look at what you’ve become, a lonely drunk sat in a bar where nobody knows you and nobody cares. Your family are sat right here and they want you home, they want you to come back to them, come back home and we’ll talk all night if you want to. We’ll get all this straightened out and you won’t have to go back to PRIME, you won’t have to deal with the past again. She reached out her right hand and grasped it around Cain’s arm. Claudia had now managed to get Paul’s attention and he stared furiously now at her hand. Cain: One, get your dirty hand off me. Two, I never asked for you to waste nine months of your life giving birth to something that would have been better off in the sewers than living in this world. Three, if you’ve come here looking for the love of your life in a desperate hope to bring him back, don’t bother because he’s dead. Her eyes were red from tears that had been cried before she even got to Paul. Now they were welling up again and her bottom lip was quivering. Her stomach was churning and she was watching the love between them die and begin to decay. Cain dipped his hand into his pocket and pulled out a cigarette, lighting it up and turning to blow the smoke in her face. Cain: Want a cigarette? I hear it really helps pregnancy. Chills shoot up her spine as he laughs softly under his breath displaying an attitude that most sane and well rounded people would find repulsive. She stood up from the stool and her tears were flowing freely down her cheeks as she tried to wipe them. However they would not stop falling and tumbled down quicker than she could brush them away. Her eyes were pleading and her body shaking with distress as she cried to him. Claudia: Why in Gods name have you gone back? Why have you let them do this to you? This isn’t you, this isn’t what you’re about! Have you forgotten Rictor Arkane, remember the man that did everything you hated? He terrorised and abused my sister, remember? He was everything that you stood against and now you’re becoming that very person! For what, huh?! For Vampir Nosferatu?! For a scar that’s on your chest?! You want to give up your life for THAT?! For fuck sake Paul, don’t you go and let our relationship die on me now, I need you! WE need you! Cain set both hands outstretched on the bar and stood up slowly, pushing the bar stool away with the back of his leg. He held his glass in his left hand now and cigarette in the right as he looked at her with a cold and empty expression. Cain: What do I have to do to make it clear to you that your true love is D-E-A-D, dead. Now shut the fuck up and walk away. Defiantly, Claudia shakes her head from side to side quickly with tears still streaming down. She has both hands on her stomach now almost protecting her unborn child from a father that he or she will never know and that Claudia does not recognise. Claudia: Paul, PLEASE! PLEASE DON’T DO THIS! In all his years of bartending, there is very little that the owner has yet to see which would shock him. However he watches now in stunned silence as Cain lunges forward and throws the whiskey into the eyes of Claudia Lopez and then violently smashes the glass against her face sending her sprawling down to the floor. She crashes down and then doubles up into the foetal position, clutching her stomach and blood pouring from the left side of her face with glass everywhere. Cain stands over her at first looking down numbly at a woman he does not recognise either. He then walks towards her crumpled form and crouches down over her. Cain: I told you to shut the fuck up and you wouldn’t let it go would you? See, this is what happens to Lopez’s when they get themselves in too deep. They keep gnawing away at you trying to make you crack, trying to get you to see their point of view. They’re not satisfied until they get you to see their point of view. Now Tyler, think of it like this, if this is what I’ll do to a female Lopez carrying this....germ that she says is mine....what would I do to you if you push at me? He turns his face away from the whimpering Claudia and looks towards the ‘invisible camera’ with a sick and twisted smile. Cain: That felt so good you know. I can’t imagine how good it will feel to see you again Tyler. It’s been too long hasn’t it, Mr Future Of Wrestling. Like fuck you are, the only reason you’re this next big thing is because you turned your back to the atrocities and you hid away, thus avoiding the fallout of my death. Why? Because last time I remember it, I wasn’t blotting your record book, I was pissing all over it and you wouldn’t have liked that. So while all of PRIME masturbates over your success, I would like to remind you that you’re not invincible and you’re not immune, just like SHE wasn’t. I’m back and now I’ve seen the truth, I’ve seen what I have to do in PRIME and that’s making me stronger than I have been in years. He casts a casual glance at Claudia and smirks. Cain: Ask yourself what goes through the head of a man that can beat up a pregnant woman and feel satisfied by the results. Ask yourself whether your cheap insults and self-promotion will work on that man. Sit back and consider to yourself whether your attempts to weaken the resolve of that man will affect him. Ask yourself whether your confidence and words will affect that man, Tyler. Turning back to the ‘invisible camera’ Cain restores that cold look symbolic of a lack of emotion that is rarely seen in the human race. Cain: Ask yourself whether I give a fuck about you, Tyler if I can’t give one about her. Claudia begins to stir and her head moves slowly as she tries to look at Paul. She is sobbing quietly and desperately holding her stomach. Her voice is hushed and whispering as she speaks. Claudia: Why Paul...why? He rises to his full height and looks down at her as he begins to walk past her. Cain: Because I’ve never been one for renewing acquaintances as Tyler will find out too. Now get this through your head DARLING...I’m dead to you so don’t try finding me again...Because as PRIME is about to find out...If you do seek me out...You won’t like what you find... Cain spits on the fallen woman as he walks away from her, leaving her with blood running down her face. The bartender leaps over the bar and rushes to her aid, his hands grasping her reassuringly on the arms. As he goes to leave the bar, Cain mutters to himself as he leaves. Cain: What is it with these Lopez people? They just won’t leave me alone.... [Scene fades out to a broken Claudia wondering how to pick up the pieces of her life.]
