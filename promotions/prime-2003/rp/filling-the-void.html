There is no line between what most people refer to as “good and evil.” It’s all just one big empty void. We’re all just wandering aimlessly within, until one day Tyler Rayne looks up and realizes he’s wandered too far. He’s surrounded by darkness. He’s done terrible things. Unmentionable things. And the guilt of his actions has burrowed deep within his abdomen, creating a nest of blackness and despair. A void. He turns to what he knows best. Fucking and drinking. He knows that these vices will do nothing to lead him down the path of forgiveness. These actions are nothing more than fleeting moments of pleasure. Ecstasy absent the pain of memory. He has become, once again, the one thing he hates the most. One of them. The twisting, searing irony of the situation is that he wouldn’t change a gods damned thing. He does what he does because he has to, because he’s done it before and he knows, no matter how bad it eats him from within, that he can live with it. Much to his own personal chagrin, he will survive. He cannot wash the blood from his hands, but he can live with the stain. This is his gift. This is how he shows his love. He bloodies and bludgeons and puts the fear of Rayne into fearless men. He does not kill. Men like these do not fear death. Fortunately for him, there are worse fates than death. He can do much more to a man than merely kill him. Maim. Torture. Cripple. Whatever it takes to protect the ones he loves. He will do whatever he has to do to ensure that men without scruples, without remorse, think twice before bringing blood to the doorsteps of his loved ones. He does what he does to keep Katterina and Milena clean. If he spills the blood of his enemies, then they won’t have to. He does what he does to protect former loves from retribution at the hands of his enemies. He does what he does to protect newfound friends from the price his deceased ones have had to pay. He does what he does because he has to. Because no one else should have to. He has accepted this as his role in life. His meaning for existence. Pain is eternal. There are worse fates than death.
