Date: All over Shit was just about hit the fan for Gabrielle Ke’ala this week as her eyes fell upon a blank Word document. Some old song on the radio from some rapper guy named "Eminem" on the oldies station was measly ambient noise as she sighed. Oh, how could she ever hope to end her junior year at Manteca High School on a good note with a modest 3.0? Let us count the ways: (1) US History Final to cram for. What did she care about a bunch of wig-wearing cherry tree-chopping dead guys, anyway? (1) Trigonometry Final that was running her mind ragged (2) More specimens necessary to complete the set of her Biology assignment on lizards and their amazing regenerative abilities. Ew. Slimy little things. (1) Character Analysis assignment for English/Lit that most likely wasn’t going to write itself. With every second ticking down and the pressure of being bombarded by all sides with big words, cellular regeneration and exceedingly complicated theorems, she buried her face in her palms and brought herself to the brink of a light sob. Peering up, the blinking cursor only served to taunt her further, almost imagining the little fucker teasing her with each flash. "YOU. CAN’T. WRITE. YOU’RE. GONNA. FAIL. HA. HA. HA." She considered herself lucky that she was even able to narrow her list of options down to one assignment she could put to rest. The Biology assignment, she could just poke her head at that shoddy old pet store down the street and pick up a couple of those retched lizards that made her skin crawl. Her friend, Shelly, swore up and down earlier that day in class she’d let Gaby borrow her notes for Trig. She thought she’d pieced together a nice little study guide to cover all the fine points Mr. Bergman went over for the test, so maybe there was hope yet. She’d use her study breaks, homeroom, and lunch to properly absorb the knowledge of the War of 1812 and the Hero’s Formula. That took care of the little annoyances. But… goddamn it. She’d given little to no thought in her final days before the deadline just what she was going to write her story about. Her precious time had been consumed by a multitude of daunting tasks. Assignments, part-time job on the weekends, Cheerleading squad, Mom breathing down her neck, friendsclassboysparitesschoolinfuckinggeneralAHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! As her own screams echoed through her head, Gabrielle beat herself about the skull lightly with one of several books she’d been assigned in English/Lit. Thus far in class, her assignments were "okay" to "slightly above average" but that ever-elusive "A" was still just out of reach. She did fine in her other classes and had seen a perfect mark here and there, but this writing thing was a pain in the ass. Prose. Theme. Characters. Plot. There was no joy in it for her. You could fluff something up with cornucopia of beautiful words and tie it all together with a neat little bow called symbolism, but such ideas were lost upon her. Now, she realized that she wouldn’t have to write the next Catcher in the Rye or Grapes of Wrath, but her analysis of the character had to be spot-on. Bergman had a reputation for being a notoriously tough grader, but he was a fair man and it was only fitting to bring him something that would make him stand up and take notice of her abilities. Doing this would ensure her GPA would end the year on an excellent note. Putting her hands behind her head, she looked down at the aforementioned book and wondered aloud. "Moby Dick, huh? Well, why not? Not like I have any other brilliant ideas. GOOD ones, anyway." Staring once more at the blinking cursor, Gaby tried to make her way through the cloudy fog of confusion just long enough to put some semblance of an assignment together. No longer was she going to worry about whatever crap twenty-nine other students were clacking on their keyboards at the time. She was trying to earn her own grade, not theirs. "Captain Ahab…" That was about as far as she got before folding her arms and staring at the screen longingly. "Captain Ahab is one of the main characters in the story of ‘Moby Dick.’" Oh, yeah, THAT was gonna win her a Pulitzer. Frantically hitting the Backspace key, she deleted each letter and started up again. "Captain Ahab is a man depicted in the story of ‘Moby Dick’ as being ‘grand and ungodly’ by his peers." A little better, she thought. "Grand in the fact that he had a high opinion of himself; almost portraying himself as larger than life. Ungodly because he never once swore fealty to a higher power other than his own." Nice. She leaned back in her chair and folded her arms. Not a bad start by any means but it’s time for a little break, she decided. She didn’t know how she would get there, but she remained confident she’d input those fantastic words on the pages and walk away from Mr. Bergman’s class with her first "A" just yet. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 1st RAMADA INN, BOSTON, MASS., 2:40 PM "196… 197… 198… 199… 200! On the floor of the hotel suite, rivulets of sweat trickling down his body splashed on the towel beneath his form. At the apex of each sit-up, he kept constant vigil on his television playing various matches with "The Queen of the Ring" Lindsay Troy. The match being viewed was her infamous ladder match against Troy Windham at EPW’s Wrestleverse II. A miniscule neon green "MUTE" sign remained displayed in the corner to prevent inane Dave Thomas/Mike Neely banter from ruining his thought process. Putting up with the Nick/Richard tandem was punishment enough. His participation in ladder matches was few and far in between, but he had to gain every last bit of insight he could on how she was going to operate in a match of this variety. He was not adept at flying around like she was – skills he’d need to study in excess to compensate for his own lack of ability in that department. He’d have to inspect every movement on this tape closely, looking for any inconsistencies and chinks in her armor. Momentarily, he stayed seated up to watch this boaster Windham guy drive a Guillotine Legdrop from off the top of a ladder across the throat of the Queen. A smile etched across his face to see her in such agony; the exact kind of agony he would have to put her through to finally wrest the title away from she whom he perceived as "one evil bitch." Windham’s sweaty palms began to wrap around her throat and ebb the life from her as Sonny continued with his routine. "210… 211… 212… 213… 214…" "Fuckhead." Huh? He stopped his sit-ups and paused to glance around the room. Nobody there but him. He dismissively scoffed and repositioned himself to start his push-ups. Just hearing things again. "1… 2… 3… 4…" "You REALLY think this will help you?" His eyes returned to the television where Lindsay groined Troy Windham on the top of the ropes. The Loose Cannon had no choice but to stop and clutch his own testicles in sympathy pain. The poor bastard. "Crazy bitch." Back to the workout. "Crazy bitch that beat you what… three times now?" As he shifted his gaze back to the television, his eyes did not deceive him. Double, triple, quadruple takes were met with the same results. On the screen, the visage of Lindsay Troy let her devilish smirk shine, making his blood boil. "You keep underestimating me," Sonny said to… whatever this apparition was while seething. "You keep looking past me, woman, and you will pay in spades." She fended off a second assault from Windham by delivering a jaw-shattering Spin Kick – one not unlike the many he’d taken from the Queen – and turned her attention back to the man with title aspirations. "No, YOU keep underestimating me. You treat me like I’m beneath you and yet, I’ve come out on top every single time. I’ve beaten you at my best, at my worst, when I’m crippled… in fact, all I gotta do is beat you while I’m having a so-so day and I’ll have run the gamut." That did it. He pushed himself to his feet and pointed an accusing finger at the television, hate burning in his pupils. "You REALLY don’t want to trade words with me. Not this time. SuperTroy slipped last week and her team lost. I prevailed last week… not you. I’ve taken down Wade. I’ve beat Ferguson. Now all that’s left is to complete the trifecta of fags and I’ll have beaten YOU." No fear was shown in her eyes. Not a single bit. Her control was unwavering and she even allowed her smirk to grow a tiny bit wider just because she knew it pissed him off. "You had the Midget and Emo kid to hand you both those victories. You won’t have them climbing a ladder for you. Face it, Sonny… you’re falling… well, not really falling. You have to amass SOME success before you take a fall. And even then, you know that you’re taking another one…" CRASH! He’d had enough, so Sonny’s trick arms acted up, hurling the TV across the room and watching it explode into hundreds of wires and plastic parts against the wall. "PAY ATTENTION, LINDSAY! THIS IS NOT YOUR PRIME ANYMORE! DO YOU HEAR ME?!" Taking a reprieve from his apparent delusion, The Schemin’ Demon slumped back on the bed with a heavy groan. Sonny, realizing the error of his outrageous aggression, errantly reached an arm out to the nightstand and proceeded to take his wallet from the drawer. There would be many hotel staff to keep quiet, lest he wish to find himself as a "fined and punished" story on the PRIME website. He grumbled under his breath as he walked to his closet for a change of non- clothing. "No way are people gonna call ME Nitz Donnelly Jr." "Obsession was his only drive; treating the white whale like evil incarnate, his sole purpose for living was to slay the beast that taunted him merely by existing. Some even deduce he was driven to sheer lunacy because of it." ~ ~ ~ FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 2nd 24 HOUR FITNESS, BOSTON MASS., 1:02 PM The gym was a hub of activity in which everyone ranging from the fat to the athletically gifted worked to either maintain a superior build or sculpt globules of fat into mounds of muscle. Thank God Sonny was one of the former because dear GOD did he detest the latter. Finishing a set of 15 reps with the 30-pound weights, he threw them down on either side of his seat and wiped beads of sweat from his forehead. Next to him, Tony "The Grin" Gamble completed a set of his own with slightly smaller 20-pounders. Once he was finished, the permagrin pointed in the direction of the third man of their pseudo-stable. Devin Shakur – dressed for the occasion in all-black gym attire, of course – leaned against the mirror with something weighing heavily on his mind. Sonny downed the last remnants of water in his bottle and glared at the Commie Emo. "What’s got your panties in a twist, dude?" Sonny prodded. "Yeah," Gamble interjected. "What happened? You enter a Dashboard Confessional contest and come in fourth or something?" He shook his head. "Can’t imagine losing out in an emo contest. If the winners cut their wrists, what do the losers do?" The silver tongue of the Grin does little to faze the weightiness pervading through Devin’s veins. "Admit it." "Admit what?" Shakur looked at him crossly. "Just admit it was you who blew up the car, dude." "It wasn’t me! I’m telling you. If it was me, I’d be singing it up and down in the streets. I plan on taking the Universal Title to drive my point home. I don’t need to blow up her car to send a message." Sonny, Devin, and Tony found themselves sharing a seat at a bar the day his wife left him. Downing what had to be his eight or ninth Bud Light, Sonny slammed his glass on the counter. "Know what I should fucking do for her sending me those pictures of my wife and daughter? I ought to FRAG her fucking personal property! Like her car or something! "HEAR-HEAR!" The three men tapped glasses before taking hefty swigs from their respective drinks. "I was kidding when I said that." Back to Boozeland one more time. "No joke, guys. No fucking joke. Seriously, I’m going to blow up her car and I’m gonna laugh the whole way." Sonny started up a second set of reps while listening intently to his conversation with Shakur. "Do you have a point, Devin?" "Yeah… if in ANY way you’re linked to this explosion and my name is brought up… I refuse to be dragged down with you. The three of us are at the cusp of big things here and we can’t afford Tyler or Blair sticking their noses where they don’t belong. Not now." "Oooooooooooh!" The Grin snickered in the face of the tense situation as he wiped himself dry with his own gym towel. "I got ten bucks on the crazy old guy." Neither Sonny nor Devin pay mind to the Jewel in the Crown winner’s caustic wit, but rather keep their attention saved for one another. "Well," Sonny told him, trying to maintain his stoic composure in the wake of Shakur’s threat. "Good sir, you are free to leave my stead at any time you see fit." After reaching the fifteenth rep, Sonny let the weights collapse to the ground as his eyes darted toward Devin. "However, Dev, indulge my long-winded speech for a second. It would serve you very well to know that questioning me will only get you nowhere and your ire is best saved for the fuckheads that mock you day in and day out. Your fight isn’t with me. Your fight…" He turned to exchange a glance with Tony. "Gamble’s fight…" And a finger pointed directly on himself. "My fight is with every last naysayer that says that I don’t have it anymore. Your fight, Devin, is with everybody in that ‘upper management’ of PRIME that accuses you of not being ready for the limelight. Your fight, Scarface, is a two-front war with that sheep-fucker Wade telling you you’re a coward and with those GCW idiots wasting airtime that belongs to ME… Us." He was careful to avoid the Freudian slip. A snort from Shakur cut Sonny off. "Something funny, Oompa?" "Yeah. Your nostrils, like, really flare and shit when you rant." "I’m with him," Gamble jerked a thumb. "You make valid points, as usual… you could be a motivational speaker if it wasn’t for that whole ‘Rar! I hate humanity!’ thing." The Loose Cannon laughed. "I did try once. The only good that did was give me a new outlet to tout my abilities as a cunning linguist. There were hot chicks at some of those seminars, you see." As the trio known by many as "The Fuck-lets" all shared a laugh, Sonny folded his arms and grinned. His brother, Timothy, was right. This "cool-headed mastermind" thing was a lot more useful than people gave it credit for. "Far beneath a man trapped in madness was a man of great profundity. The sound of his voice carried an undeniable charisma that made disciples out of doubters. Rousing speeches and incomparable bravado were the tools at his disposal to incite the masses and spark interest in whatever course of action the Captain decided to undertake. Even the loner Ishmael and the soft-spoken Starbuck found themselves captivated by the sound of his mere voice. The crew saw him as their highest authority even though his goals remained purely monomaniacal." ~ ~ ~ SATURDAY, OCTOBER 3RD PRUDENTIAL SKYWALK, STILL IN BOSTON. 11:53 AM The Prudential Tower (known as The Pru by local Chow-dah-sucking morons) boasted a magnificent view of the rest of the city from on high. The John Hancock Tower notwithstanding, said building stood higher than any other structure in Boston and was host to the Prudential Skywalk, a fabulous observation deck on its 50th floor and to this day, remained the tallest desk in New England available to the public. "Can we go now?" …Of course, all those interesting factoids were lost upon Sonny as he bundled himself up in his black leather coat, feeling the effects of the frigid air well above the ground. "Oh, hush, child," Maria Silver told him as she admired the view from one of the viewing posts overlooking the downtown area. Her gaze traveled every which way something visually stimulating was spotted. Signs, Billboards, other buildings… "Huh," she muttered. "There’s some woman out there that’s giving some guy a mean ride." With the piquing curiosity of a six-year-old, Sonny dashed over to the telescope and knocked his mom out of the way to peer at some sort of debauchery going down. "Seriously?" "No." Frowning, Sonny lifted his eye out of the telescope and glared at Maria. "Mom, I hate you with a passion reserved for Republicans, Vin Diesel movies, and homosexuals. The latter two are interchangeable." Her arms now placed firmly on her hips, Sonny’s agent started chewing the gum in her mouth more intensely. "Why don’t you quit your whining and just enjoy yourself. You don’t have to wrestle until tomorrow night and you promised me you were going to spend some time with me AND not act like I’m dragging you to your death." She turned away to take another gander through another post. "I swear, even your father didn’t do this much kicking and screaming when I represented him on the road… well… there was some screaming of a different sort…" That did it. Sonny jiggled vehemently with some of the iron bars to prevent lookers-on from falling off the tower and becoming street pizza. Maria turned to her son. "What the hell are you doing?" "Before you scare me with the "talk about my past sex life," I’m gonna get mentally prepared by swan diving into the concrete. Cool?" "Oh, knock it off," she snickered at her son before both of them turned their attention back to one of the viewing posts along the edge. "So what’s going on between you and Eme?" "I still have to meet with a divorce lawyer. Go over how much money I’m gonna be out, I’m sure." "Sorry." She brushed a hand down his arm sympathetically and chose her next words carefully. "So… Son?" "Yeah?" "What’s gonna happen to you if you don’t win this match?" "No more title shots or contention of any kind until Lindsay Troy finally loses the belt." He read the expression on her face, knowing that’s not the answer she sought. "No…" Her gaze wanders. "What’s gonna happen to you if you don’t win this match?" He raised a finger, prepared to throw out some glib answer that would both satisfy his ego and her need for the truth. He saw the look in her eyes that told him "you better tell me right this instant or you’re taking a header to the street below." Try as he might, no meager answer would escape from his lips. With an exasperated sigh, he let his finger down and turned to the sunlit Boston skyline, not sure how exactly he would answer the question. "I… I just don’t want to think about it. All I know is that 100% or not, I’m winning the goddamn title and if I have to put her face through a ladder rung or six, I’m going to do it. If I have to break every bone in her goddamn body to prevent her from climbing, I’ll do it. Whatever it takes." The grave tone of voice chilled Maria to the bone. "You…" She sputtered incredulously. After the initial shock subsided, she stood next to her son. "You… how could you stand there and say things like that, about another human being, no less?" "Because she insulted me!" His screams reverberated throughout the Skywalk, making a few passers-by jump. Sonny paid them no mind, instead treating his mom like she was the only person for miles. "She’s taken my pride, she’s taken my blood, she’s taken my arm, she’s taken EVERYTHING from me! She started this war by injuring me and GODDAMN it, she’s going to pay. If I can’t win this match, I’ll make damn sure she won’t live to see another day in her career." "YOU injured HER!" Maria fought back. "YOU were the one that turned your back on her, YOU were the one that plotted against her, and YOU were the one that lost your marriage because you were too damn stubborn to let anything go. You’re really surprised that she came after you? She was defending herself. I’d have done the same thing, and you would’ve, too." "I would strike ANYONE that has caused me as much strife as that woman has, no matter where they stand on the food chain. As a man and as somebody that has given so much of his life to a sport that’s been less than kind to him, I will not take any more. I am NOT somebody to be walked on." "She…" "SHE’S A MONSTER!" Maria felt the hot, venomous breath on her skin and shuddered. Sonny, not wanting to exchange another word with his mom, started to walk towards the elevator. "Nobody controls me, nobody walks on me, and nobody trifles with me. I am a human being with his destiny laid out in front of him and will not let anybody else deter or stunt it." On the verge of pulling his hair out, Sonny took a reprieve from his wild fit before composing himself. "You, Eme… everyone else in my life… you guys can all wait one more day. I need this. I need this more now than I have ever needed anything in my life; I need to know that somehow, someway, that every decision I’ve made in this lifetime wasn’t in vain. I need to know that I can win this and not have twenty years of work gone down the toilet. I can’t turn back. Not now. Not when I’m so close." "Sonny, wait…" But it was too late. Sonny was long gone, ignoring the pleas of Maria calling for him to return. He’d have no choice but to live with every last biting word he’d thrown at his mother and every decision he’d made up till that point since the day he turned his back on Lindsay Troy. It consumed his thoughts as he traveled 49 floors down to the lobby. The awkward cab ride home with Maria was no better. It attributed to a near-sleepless night involving much tossing and turning, and any moment he could grab of sleep, the thought of failure in the face of his career’s biggest test haunted him. When he showed up at the arena the next day to ready himself for battle, Maria was not there beside him. Gamble and Shakur were busy with their own agendas, thus were not able to aid him in his time of need. Even his only true friend, Nova, was nowhere to be found. In his final moments before showtime, he would feel pressure like no other. As if his very fate had already been sealed. Starbuck was the sole voice of reason in his otherwise turbulent and maddening world. Seeing through every last attempt at guile and immune to his silver tongue, the first mate refused time and time again to be a party to the blasphemy of hunting a creature whose only transgression was following nature and defending itself. While the captain was lost in a sea of his own insanity, the faithful first mate tried time and time again to talk the captain off his pedestal, to no avail. In his own mind, he was fighting evil under the guise of an innocent being; a force that sought to control him. But the captain was his own master and nature be damned, he would not have it any way that wasn’t his own. Catching sight of the beast, his lust for its demise only peaked. As the final three days of his voyage left him with little more than his own thoughts, a lot is learned about the captain. He was a tortured, complicated soul that took on the world with an unfaltering hand. He knew he was mad and had no scruples with using his crew to his own twisted ends. As many of his crew and enemies alike would admit, he wasn’t ‘normal.’ Some hypothesized he wanted to be normal, even. However, the choices he made and his defiant nature condemned him to death… a death he’d gladly take over submission. ~ ~ ~ "Damn," Gabrielle patted herself on the back as she clicked the Save icon on her Document before closing it out. "I didn’t think I had it in me… profundity…" After shutting off her computer, she spun around in her chair, sat up and made her way towards the bedroom. She was exhausted. Racking her brain thinking of some amazing shit to write really took its toll on her mind and now, she’d had enough of this writing thing. She would go to bed and pray go the high heavens that the assignment she’d spent a grueling night or two to complete would finally give her the result she wanted.
