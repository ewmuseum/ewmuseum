Double Hockeysticks

3 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/07 Jagged spires of obsidian stretch towards the sooty red sky. Geysers of fire explode all around, filling the air with smoke and an overwhelming stench of sulfur and brimstone. The ground is hardened and cracked clay, baked to an almost diamondlike hardness by the oppressive heat all around. The air is heavy and thick and completely motionless, offering not even a hint of a breeze. There is nothing stirring on the land or in the sky, no sign of life anywhere... except for one lone traveler. The heat had baked all the moisture out of him hours ago. His head hung low as he trudged on, his tongue thick and leathery in his mouth, unable to even produce the slightest amount of saliva. Time had no meaning here, but he felt like he had been walking for days. His muscles ached with fatigue, his lips were cracked and peeling, his feet screamed in pain with every step as he walked on blisters that had formed and broken some unknown time ago as he searched for any relief from the desolate landscape. While no stranger to heat, to pain, to torture that few people could withstand, there's only so much that even the Trashcan Man can take. He stumbles over a crack, falling to his hand and knees. "Gotta keep moving," he muttered to himself. "There's gotta be a way out of here." Normally there'd be some sort of response from one of the several personalities that lived inside him, but ever since he arrived here, silence had reigned in his head. At first he had counted the lack of internal dialogue a blessing, it had been years since he had been alone in his own mind. But as time dragged on in this place, he came to realize that he counted on his eternal companions, the unexpected loneliness was in some ways even more oppressive than his blasted surroundings. He rose to his feet, a slow trickle of blood from his scraped right knee running down his torn and filthy jeans. Taking a deep breath to attempt to steal himself, he instead gags on the foul air. He collapses back on his knees as his body shudders with dry heaves. He finally stops and falls over onto his side, his last remaining reserves of energy spent. “Where… the hell am I?” he croaks. “Exactly!” The Trashcan Man cracks open an eyelid to stare at a pair of brown boots. His eyes trail up past the jeans, the flannel shirt, and rests on the familiar grinning face of his old friend and murder victim. “Ed? Is that you?” he whispers in disbelief. “Heyo Trash!” replies the smiling Ed. He reaches down and grasps the Trashcan Man’s orange gloved and raises him to his feet. His fatigue and suffering now ignored, he stares at the man he once had a hand in killing when under the control of his “master” Alan Kriegman. “What are you doing here? And where the hell are we?” “Exactly again, Trash. You’re in Hell. And I’m the guide with “it”, baby!” Trashy stares at his old friend in disbelief. “Hell? What the hell am I doing in Hell?” Ed throws a companionable arm around his old buddy. “You’re dead as Kyle Lamen, baby.” “Dead? Since when?!” “Last night. You had a brain clot from that mind control chip in your head and you died in your sleep. Sorry Trash, but that’s not the kind of thing you can no sell like a chair shot.” “Son of a bitch.” “Yep. You need a minute.” “What I need is something to drink.” “Ok, that we can do.” Dust swirls around them as they vanish and reappear at the edge of a pond. Trash, ignoring his surroundings for the moment, breaks away from Ed with a hoarse cry and plunges into the pond, wading in water up to his knees before falling into it. He rises back up and starts sucking it in desperate gulps, his lips and tongue aching with the sudden rehydration. He stops drinking long enough to see a man standing chest deep on the other side of the pond. He watches to see the strange figure jump up and try to reach one of the apples on the branch that’s hovering over him, his outstretched fingers just barely missing the lowest of the hanging fruit. He then suddenly bends over, trying to bring his lips to the surface of the pond, just to see the pond recede from his reach. Trash turns towards the shore to see the smiling Ed. He trudges back through the water and asks “What’s with that guy?” “Oh, Tantalus? That’s his punishment. He’ll never be able to drink or eat ever again, no matter how hard he tries.” “Wow, that’s kind of rough.” “Well, it is Hell Trashy. And it could be worse. You should see what Hitler gets.” “Do I want to know?” “He’s constantly being boiled in Jewish semen.” “You’re shitting me!” “Nope, that’s what he gets for failing in God’s plan.” Trash’s brow furrows at that one. “Failing… in GOD’S plan?” Ed nods seriously. “Oh yeah. God came to him in one of his paintings and told him to wipe out the entire Hebrew race for not acknowledging his son. Oh, and for being money grubbing shysters.” As the Trashcan Man stands with jaw agape, Ed’s solemnity is broken by a burst of laughter. “Hah! The look on your face… no, Hitler was a dick, dude, he deserves everything he’s getting.” He looks down at his bare wrist. “Well Trash, tour’s over, time for you to meet your final reward.” “Wait a minute…” stalls the Trashcan Man, backing away as he holds his gloved hands before him defensively. “I might not be a saint, but I’m not bad… Lazarus is the evil one, he’s the demon that belongs here… I’m the good guy!” Ed grins as he stalks towards backpedaling Trashcan Man. “Oh, I dunno Trash… Laz wasn’t in control when you helped kill me, was he?” “Hey Ed… I said I was sorry about that… that was Kriegman and the chip… I didn’t mean to do it…” Ed’s grin grows… uncomfortably wide, as if there were too many teeth in his mouth. “And face it Trash… Laz is just another part of YOU, isn’t he? All the… “ He makes quote marks with his fingers, the nails dark and growing… “EVIL that he’s supposedly done was all you, wasn’t it?” Trash shakes his head wildly as he trips and falls on his ass, staring up at the bumps growing on Ed’s reddening forehead. “No… Laz is the demon… I’m the good one… I’m not bad, I’m not bad!” Ed’s bifurcated tail slithers around and wraps around the fallen hardcore legend’s ankle. His bright yellow eyes blaze with a not so inner fire as he reaches down with a taloned hand. “Oh Trash, you’re bad… and bad boys must be punished.” Trash screams as they are surrounded by a whirl of flames and ashes, vanishing from view. He staggers out to find himself… In a locker room? The Trashcan Man glances around to see the room filled with wrestlers, the steam from the nearby showers filling the room and obscuring their faces. He spins at a tap on his shoulder. A scrawny intern, barely out of his teens takes a startled step back, raising his clipboard defensively in front of him. “Sorry sir… but you’re on.” “Oh, my match with Devin Shakur for the #1 contendership for the Intense title, right!” The intern shakes his head. “No sir… you’re in a much bigger match then that! Your match is going to make history… It’s you vs El Rayo Azul in the first match for the first card of PRIME the Second, the new league after the roster split! Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” He glances over at a wrestling magazine on the nightstand. His eyes widen in fright as he gazes at the headline… PRIME announces roster split! Trashcan Man first man announced as part of the new roster! Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” An arm falls across his chest. He looks over to see that the naked form of his alter ego, Jumpin’ Jack Flash is sleeping next to him. Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” An arm falls across his chest. He looks over to see that the naked form of WWE Diva and Playboy Playmate Ashley Massaro is lying next to him. Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Dammit, come back! Son of a bitch…” Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” Suddenly Charleton Heston runs in, screaming at the top of his lungs… “People! Soylent Green is made of People!” Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” He glances out the window to see that there’s a light drizzle outside. Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” He lays his head back down. Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Goddammit, when’s this going to stop?” Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He jerks awake in his hotel room, drenched in sweat. “Whew, it was just a dream.” He glances at a Wizard magazine. He shrugs and picks it up, reading an article… until he gets to a certain page… “The Punisher might be the new Captain America?!” Trashy throws his head back and screams. ”NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!” He looks around, realizing he hasn’t woken back up yet. “Son of a bitch, that one’s not a dream. Goddammit.” We fade to black.
