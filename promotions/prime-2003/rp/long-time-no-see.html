Long Time, No See

8 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4-8-08 Drip Drip Drip. Damn leaky faucet. This hotel room sucks. But it’ll have to do, because a certain company owes a certain wrestler a late check. Not this one, don’t worry Mr. Blaine. But it’s been keeping Andrew Rossi up all night. Every time he gets up to turn the damn handle, he can hear it again once his head hits the pillow of one of the most uncomfortable beds he’s ever slept on. He’s working on NO sleep right now. SB rolls over in his bed to check the time. SB: Three AM? Son of a bitch! Whose wife do I have to fuck to get some peace and quiet around here? SB hurls the covers off of his bed and gets up to pace a bit. He considers going into the bathroom and just taking the sink apart, but he’s not about to pay for the damages if all he’s getting in return is a decent sleep at best. SB: Hey…I wonder if the bar is still open. Grinning, he throws a coat over his sleeping clothes and heads for the door. ==================================================== When SB gets down to the bar, there’s only a couple of guys there. One of them is an old friend of his, in town to work an independent promotion. SB doesn’t notice him right away, but the other guy gets up and starts walking over to SB. Man: SB, you old son of a bitch! SB looks at him long and hard, but then it hits him just who it is. SB: Tom! What the hell are you doin’ in Wichita? Tom doesn’t answer right away, he just gives SB a big hug. They practically grew up together in Staten Island. They even went to training school together. Tom: I’m with K-State Pro Wrestling now. Y’know, I’m just trying to keep my name out there and see if somebody notices. SB nods knowingly. Tom’s always had a tough time holding down steady jobs both in and out of the business. First it was the drugs, but that was a long time ago. Then it was the whores, but he’s moved on past that too. He found a good woman two years ago – actually she was sitting at the table beside Tom. But there was no way she was getting up to say hello to SB. She’s heard enough stories about that “womanizing son of a bitch” to NEVER want to meet him in person. SB: Well, you heard PRIME is in town right? Tom: Why do you think we’re in Wichita on the same day? We’re running a mid-day show so we can get a full house of your fans! SB: Man, indie promoters still do that? Tom: Don’t fix what isn’t broken, right? SB: Hey, maybe some of the PRIME scouts will drop in. SB smiles at his old friend, but Tom just shakes his head. Tom: I’d have to tell ‘em that I’m not interested. I’m just not about money anymore. As long as there’s a hot crowd, and as long as I’ve got Susan. SB: Who? Now it’s Tom’s turn to smile. Tom: My wife. SB’s eyes go wide, but then he grabs his friend by the shoulders and gives him a big, hearty hug. SB: Why the hell wasn’t I invited? Tom: We eloped. Her parent’s weren’t too thrilled with her marrying a wrestler. SB, who’d been eyeing Tom’s wife as he spoke, looked up at him and nodded his approval. SB: Her parents got money? Tom: Loads of it. But we don’t want any of it, and we don’t ask for any of it. We’re just fine living this way. SB: (smiling) Well, why don’t ya come to the show? Tom: Your show? I don’t have a ticket. SB: I can pull some strings and get you backstage. Tom: No thanks. I don’t wanna have to owe you a favor, you’d hold it over my head till my funeral! They share a laugh. SB shrugs his shoulders and pats Tom on the back. SB: How long’s it been, Tommy? Tom: Oh, I don’t know Andrew. 2005? Yeah, about three years. SB: You never even call, brotha. Tom: You don’t either. SB: Yeah, because you don’t. Tom: What’s the matter with you? I haven’t seen you in three years and this is how you react when I run into you by chance this far from home? SB: Well, what do you expect from me, huh? Tom: Listen, not in front of Carol, okay? SB: What? Tom: Let’s take this outside. The childhood friends walk outside into the street. A car passes by and bathes them in its headlights, but passes on just as quickly. Tom: I’m sorry we fell out of touch, pal. SB: I am too. Look, I’ll tell you what - Tom: Look, Andrew, I really gotta get going. I didn’t realize how late it was… SB: What’s the matter, you gonna turn into a pumpkin? Tom: Very funny. Listen, let me get your number and I’ll give you a ring and we can set something up in some other town. SB: But we’re here now! C’mon, let me take you guys out tomorrow. Tom: I don’t think that me and Carol will be able to, I’ve gotta get right to the next town for the show the day after. SB: Jesus, how many shows do you guys ru…you’re lying. Tom hangs his head down in shame. His silence tells the story better than his own words can. SB: What the fuck is goin’ on? Tom: Just give me your number, and we’ll set something up down the road, all right? SB: You’re full of shit. Now come on, tell me. What the fuck is your problem? I run into you after all this time and you give me this bullshit? Tom: Would you look at yourself? You’re still the same old asshole! Goin’ out, chasin’ tail! I spoke to Suzanna after everything that happened last year, and she told me what the fuck you put her through! SB stands dumbfounded. People who remember him from NAPW will know what Tom’s talking about, but PRIME fans are in the dark completely. Tom: The reason I can’t go out tomorrow is because my wife knows how much of an asshole you were. And THAT’S WHY YOU WEREN’T AT MY FUCKING WEDDING! SB: You son of a bitch. SB goes to take a swing at him, but Tom blocks the punch and LEVELS SB, knocking him out into the empty street. Tom: You have my number, Andrew. Get your fuckin’ shit together, and then you call me. My number hasn’t changed. With that, Tom walks back inside, grabs Carol, and heads over to the elevator. Outside SB, in shock more than hurt, lays on the ground rubbing his chin. Now he’s really not gonna get any sleep.
