A Flawless Road to PRIME -- I

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 22nd, 2005 PRIME. Five letters that embody the pinnacle of the industry. KELLY FLAWLESS. Four syllables that instill envy within the eyes of every man, and orgasmic bliss within the being of every woman. Mmm- touching. Their association is one driven by fate, you could say. Well, not really. If fate were money… then yes, their association may very well be driven by destiny. But last time I inquired, fate had much more to do with someone’s – or something’s – agenda, than it did with paper and ink. All things aside, however, PRIME was on the Road the Colossus- it was merely two days away, in fact. And now, the promotion touted by many as the best has staked claim to the entity that is Perfection… my honorable apology to all of the readers of PRIME Chapter I: Change of Heart.Weeks earlier; post-Courage The evening had just concluded- another favorable show for the competitors of All-Star Championship Wrestling, and there was not a soul in the locker room fearful enough not to gloat about it. The boys were riding high. PPV buyrates were up, as were merchandise sales, and not to mention ticket sales. Continuously over the past few months the wrestlers had perceived a dramatic change in event attendance. The crowds were growing increasingly louder, which affected the morale of the wrestlers considerably. After all, when somebody has paid their hard earned money to observe you display your talents, and they cheer you - like a god, none the less - it’s always a little uplifting. He sat in the driver’s seat of his rental car. Nothing immaculate- a Toyota Camry. It wasn’t top of the line, but he felt secure within its confines. In the background the dull roar of the radio could be heard- set to a local rock station, Kelly nodded his head a tad to the beat as he concentrated on making it back to his hotel room without any automotive mishaps. He took a glimpse down toward the gas gauge, before averting his focus to his cellphone that was on the passenger’s seat. It had been set off by an incoming call. He picked it up with his right hand placed it near eye level to check the name reading on the display. PHIL CELL He pried the device open after letting it ring one final time. “Hello,” Kelly began. “Hey Kel, it’s Phil.” The man speaking into the other end of the line was Kelly’s agent- Phil Mickelson. … No, not that Phil Mickelson. “Howdy,” Kelly replied. “How was the show tonight? ACW still treatin’ you well?” He said with a tad of apprehension in his voice. “It was… good.” Kelly began, “And, yes- ACW is treating me immaculately.” “That’a boy,” Phil said encouragingly. “I’m havin’ a good time here, Phil, you know that.” Kellen Cutright spoke, reassuring Phil everything was alright. “I’m just worried about you, you know, after what went down with Sars and the whole Alice thing.” Phil said with a sigh. “That coupled with the knee thing, of course.” “It’s not a big deal. So they’ve got one guy on their payroll who’s a little off his rocker.” Kelly smirked. “Well, I must say, Kellen, you’re handling it a lot better than you were the other week,” Flawless’ agent said, rather surprised. Again, Kellen Cutright smirked. “Things change, Phil. I’ve got no hard feelings against ACW- I love that place. Hell, as far as the fans go, I’m royalty in those hallowed halls. Well, Kelly Flawless is.” Clearly there were two sides to every story: Kellen Cutright was a far more modest person as opposed to the character he portrayed. “I’m glad you’re feeling differently. I was a little worried you were gonna fold up shop a little while back.” “Rage is as great a mask as any, my friend.” The two shared a laugh as Kelly pulled the car into the parking lot of the hotel. “Okay, well I’ll be talking to you later then, stay positive…” Phil said, pausing, “it’s a lot more pleasant.” Again the two shared a chuckle as they exchanged farewells before hanging up their respective phones. It had been made evident by the native Alaskan that he was no longer bitter toward the company that employed him. Although, no more than a handful of souls knew about his grievances. They weren’t so much with the company itself as they were with Sars the Clown. A feud that had escalated- gotten personal, and eventually ended with Sars injuring Kelly’s knee, as well as Kelly losing – potentially – the love of his life. No hard feelings. Kelly muttered beneath his breath as he entered the hotel room. He was more than content in ACW- it truly was his home. Then again, he wasn’t at all aware of the opportunities that would soon present themselves… Opportunities that would seem nearly impossible to a blind eye the way of.
