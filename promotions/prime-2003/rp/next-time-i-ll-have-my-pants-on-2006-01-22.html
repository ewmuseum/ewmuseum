Next Time I'll Have My Pants On.

22 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/22/06 --------------------------------- Fuck, Fuck, Fucking Fuck! I win right? That ain’t how it worked where I come from. I’m not here to preach, that gimmick’s been done. I’m a wrestler, and I love this business. At times I could have said it was my life but I got a lot more to live for now. I still think there should be some respect shown for the business. I know, this is PRIME, we’re on Playboy TV. I have no problem with profanity, you’ll hear me use it, but cursing should be used sparingly and to place emphasis on the things you say that really matter. If all you do is spout a few cuss words on TV every week, then nothing you say should really matter. --------------------------------- My name is Jake Keeton. For 10 years I’ve traveled the world getting beaten up and beating people up for money and I’ve loved every minute of it. 6 Months ago I won the heavyweight championship of one of the greatest independent promotions in North America. I beat an almost unbeatable champion, then my world fell apart. I haven’t been in a wrestling ring since the night I won that belt, haven’t seen my 6 year old son since then either. PSW closed a week later and they took my little boy. Nothing else mattered to me. I didn’t think I’d ever end up jerking curtains in PRIME, but I got bills to pay. I’ve made plenty of money through my career but I want the best lawyers money can buy and they know I’ve got it which means if I don’t work I’m gonna run out. Which brings me to Revolution 87. I signed my contract that day and was just wandering around backstage when I heard my name being cursed by a brain dead Mexican. Wait… my bad it’s his partner that’s brain dead right? I know he’s just paralyzed and not brain dead, but it sounded better and he might as well be. Anyway, this jackass finds out I’m his partner then questions who I am when I should have been asking the same of him. I got smart with him. He tossed me through a wall. End of story. It’s not the end though see. Seems me, him and a Mexican Elvis impersonator gotta be partners this week. I don’t know what I did to be punished like this but my girlfriend works at Walmart and she said dealing with Mexicans ain’t that hard. I don’t know anything about a couple of our opponents and don’t care too. I’ve known Nova for a long time. We haven’t exactly been the best of friends, but back then I didn’t do much to make friends either. I was an asshole, still can be sometimes, but the last time I was in a wrestling ring I walked out of Rupp Arena in my hometown with thousands of people cheering like the University of Kentucky had just beaten Duke. I spent 9 years making people hate me, it only took 6 months to make them love me. When I first contacted PRIME I’d been hearing the buzz about how far Nova had come, but it wasn’t anything new to me. The guy’s always had it, I might not have admitted back when he was part of me getting tarred and feathered in the ring, but I knew it. People like Vetra, Gamble, Baltazar and El Vice come and go like the seasons change. People like Nova and I, we create legacies. By no means am I comparing myself to what Nova’s done in PRIME because he’s established himself as one of the top guys here. Me? I’m just a nobody like Blatazar said. Win or lose I get paid either way, but Nova knows what I’m capable of. If I wanna be where he’s at bad enough I’ll get there, just won’t be by the same means I got there back when he and I were bitter rivals. There’s a reason Baltasar focused on Nova and I did too. We both know he’s our only competition in this match. Good luck Nova, I can promise you’ll get a fair fight out of me. I can’t say the same for my partners. (The scene opens to water pouring from a shower head and Jake Keeton standing under it his hands up to his face and his elbows against the wall of the shower letting the water run over him. He cuts the shower off and grabs a towel. He wraps the towel around him and walks over to a mirror and wipes the steam away with his forearm. He stands staring into the mirror for a few moments, his short brown hair still glistening with a combination of the water from the shower and condensation. He runs his hands through his hair and drops of water fly into the air. He walks into the bed room of the 4 bedroom house he shares with his girlfriend and his mother.) “Jacob! There’s someone here to see you.” (The sounds of Jake’s mother Kathy can be heard coming from downstairs. He runs over to the stairs still wrapped in only a towel and sees young lady in her mid twenties in a grey and white business suit sitting on his couch. He quickly tucks his head back around a corner.) Jake: Mom, could you come here a second. (His mother walks to the top of the steps.) Jake: Who the hell is that? Kathy: She’s a paralegal with that law firm you contacted about getting Jonathan back. Jake: I don’t have any damn pants on, tell her to hold on. Kathy: She’s been here for 20 minutes and said she couldn’t wait any longer, we thought you’d drown in there. Jake: Nah, I was just thinking. Go ask her if she can just wait a few more minutes I’ll be down as soon as I’m dressed. (As Kathy makes her way back downstairs the young woman has already gotten up and started towards the door.) Young Lady: I’m sorry Mrs. Keeton, but I can’t wait any longer. Kathy: Please, he said he’d be right down. Young Lady: I’m sorry, but I have to meet with another client who really hates to be kept waiting, and I know the feeling now. Kathy: I’m sorry too; Jake’s got a lot on his mind right now. Young Lady: He’s known about this meeting for three weeks. Regaining custody of his son must not be much of a priority. Kathy: I beg to differ! That little boy is the most important thing on earth to him. Young Lady: Just tell him I’ll be back at the exact same time next week. We have to get these proceedings underway soon though or he’ll have to wait 6 more months. Kathy: Thank you, I’ll let him know. Young Lady: Goodbye. (Not 30 seconds after the door shuts Jake comes running down the steps. He completely misses the last5 and falls flat on his ass. He goes to get up but just stays down when his Mom bends down and places a hand on his shoulder.) Kathy: She’s gone, and you look like you need to stay right where you are. I’ll get you an ice pack. Jake:(groggily) Is she coming back? (Kathy answered from in the kitchen.) Kathy: She said that she’ll be back at the same time next week and to make sure you have your pants on. (Just as Kathy says that Jake’s girlfriend Tasha steps through the door. Tasha: Who is “she” and why didn’t you have your pants on? (Fade to black.)
