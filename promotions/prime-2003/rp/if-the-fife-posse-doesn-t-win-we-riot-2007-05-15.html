If The Fife Posse Doesn't Win, We Riot

15 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/15/07 Hey, guess what? Yeah, you. The guy who decided that he’s going to spend a few minutes reading this garbage. What the HELL is the matter with you? Seriously. I’m Slash McCoy. My cracker brother/tag team partner Burnie McCoy’s probably off somewhere making sure all the buttons on his extensive collection of tacky 80’s shirts are still all where they need to be. I’m going to take a few minutes of your time to tell you just why the flying fudge you should be calling me and Burnie the next Tag Team Champions of PRIME. Firstly, let’s take a look at the competition. LINDSAY TROY AND DANNY FERGUSON Pssh. You cowards only got the drop on us the first time because you CHEATED. Yeah, that’s right, I said it. You, Lindsay Troy, distracted me with your gargantuan, misshapen woman head while the suck off Danny Ferguson was radiating so much; it caught Burnie off-guard and blinded him completely. You two pastyfaces aren’t worthy of picking my cotton. Yes, I am aware of the hilarious irony of a black man telling two white people that they should be picking his cotton, but it’s all true. If Slash McCoy gives you the business, he’s gonna put your asses to work. You two shouldn’t even BE a tag team, yet here the both of you are trying to sit here and shit on our division. Ferguson, you can sit there and refer to this division as a “peanut gallery” but when’s the last time you even SAW your peanuts, you roid-head? You aren’t a movie star. I’ve seen better actin’ in a bottle of Tough-Actin’ Tenactin. Lindsay Troy, I’m sorry. I’m sorry that I have a life and don’t understand the pop culture references that spew forth from your mouth. You no doubt have LOADS of time on your hands and probably don’t have a man in which to keep you from sitting on your ass all day watching daytime Soaps. Big ones, skinny ones, kids who climb on rocks. Tough kids, wimpy kids, even one with chicken pox. Beef, I think his name is. But nevertheless, it’s a Three-Ring Circus full of disgraces to this sport. The only one I have any respect for is Coral, but I wonder how much respect I could truly have for a man whose wasting his God-given talents playing second fiddle to a video game-obsessed Negro. The fact of the matter is that when it came to crunch time, you two folded worse than the Yankees this season. Back of the line with you and you guys can probably have a shot at Burnie’s half of the belts after we win it. CAPTAIN SULEIMON AND JACK MURPHY Same goes double for you two. You guys have already bungled things up TWICE. That’s two… Count them. Two. Bwa-ha-ha-ha. Rather than watching you two complete the trifecta of sucking, I do believe that the Fife Posse will be taking your spot now and will look forward to taking those precious titles from FergTroy. But something else has been weighing in on my mind: Where in the name of DOLEMITE does some Borat-looking piece of trash get off telling ME how to wrestle? Last I checked, he was the love child of the aforementioned foreigner and Jeff Hardy. You flop around like a dead fish and I’m supposed to take YOU seriously? I completely sympathize with Murphy’s sense of direction in wrestling, but when you can tell me the difference between a German Suplex, your ass, and a hole in the ground, son, you don’t get to say anything. PRANKS WITH ATTITUDE Hey, as long as we’re digging up old material, we might as well not leave out Pranks With Attitude. I wonder if Nerezza knows that Vampires officially became the lamest thing to parody when a certain man by the name of Kevin Thorn came on the scene. When you guys are done spinning your old-ass Offspring records, come join the rest of us in the real world where you’ll find The Fife Posse… you know, AMOUNTING to something this year. SPANGLISH UNITED XCHANGE No… just… Oh. HELL. NO. To defeat you guys, all I gotta do is think like a pair of Spaniards… Excuse me. After this phoned in attempt at trash talk, I’m gonna go get a girl pregnant, drink some Tequila, eat a burrito, sell some oranges near the off-ramp, then go take a nap. NEXT. DAS KORPS You know what, I’ll talk to you two overfed Nazis when I want a Bratwurst. THE DETONATORS Wow. A pair of guys from the WWA. Color me impressed. Seriously. I’m going to cream my pants in sheer anticipation of what two guys name TNT and Dynamite are going to do. They are practically blowing me away as I speak. Ha ha. Ha ha. Ha ha. After you’ve all drowned in a torrent of my sarcasm, allow me to get to the nitty-gritty. MAYBE you two were hot shit in WWA and MAYBE all three WWA fans gave a flying fudge about you over there in bush league central, but on the Fife Posse’s turf, there’s a rule that’s been cleverly said by a bunch of nerds crapping their Star Wars underoos on the Interweb… WE ARE THE RULE AND YOU ARE THE SUCK. And if you aren’t down with the Fife Posse, we got eight words for ya! Well, THAT sucked. ...Damn link. :( Can we have the tag titles, please?
