Date: September 1, 2008 He played with her hair. The Atlanta Summer, though the tail end, was a humid and unforgiving kind of torture. Even with a ceiling fan spinning so fast that one eye could not even zero in on a single blade's whirl, they needed only a thin, green velvet blanket. She was a beautiful creature, proof that though there may be no God that hears Rhett Locke, there is one that hears the calls of Man and heeds to his primal sense of art. Her name was Laura Doris. She was a strung out angel, a rare beauty and an alcoholic. Her eyes were closed and her head was rested gently upon his chest, temple covering his right nipple. Locke made a blank statement, "My nipple's cold." Her hand lifted from around his waist and covered the other. They both giggled in quiet breaths. He loved her. He kept his distance but he loved her. She never would want to admit it but she knew he did, as well. It was the kind of affection she'd received from everyone her entire life. Every little girl is a princess in their hearts, causing them to be bossy and obnoxious unknowingly. She was no different, though when she hit puberty she became rather shy and mousey. The few boys in High School who'd take interest in her would find one of the most gentle, easily crushed human beings alive in their hands. As it was in that time and surely still is, she was consistently crushed. She lost her virginity and stayed with the same boy, through slaps across the face and being called names she stayed with him. Because she loved him. The typical excuses, all trite and annoying to most who aren't a prisoner of that mindset. Perhaps the most easily captured of prisoners, she stayed with him. When he broke up with her for silly and horrible reasons, Laura cried her eyes out. When boys dated her in between break ups with her prison guard, she'd use them dry until he came calling. With sorrow and tears in her eyes she'd try and explain to those boys who truly cared for her, who loved everything about her, that they simply weren't him and that was why she could not stay. She would stay on the phone and cry with them and those boys would sit through a consistent wave of "I'm sorry, I hope you can forgive me someday." Then Steve Mayner came along. He swept her off of her feet and truly made her feel like the princess she was at the age of four. True, she put him through the same treatment after two months of a wonderful relationship once that asshole called again but he never gave up on her. For three years, he tried and tried again with her. Eventually she would grow up, realize who really loved her and she married him. They had beautiful children together, a house and lived the proverbial American Dream. At that moment, however, it was in Steve's bed that Laura Doris and Rhett Locke were laying. A microscopic look inside of either of their nostrils would find tiny speckles of cocaine, covering drug created lesions and nose follicles like snow covers a child's gloves on a cancelled school day. To blame drugs for her decisions would be unfair. It is not as uncommon as a father would hope for a depressed, teenage girl to love alcohol and everything it made her feel. To her, sex wasn't as good unless she was drunk. Thinking was something she was forced to do, you understand. Whenever it was when she was so fucked up she was no longer having thought, she would be at peace with herself only then. She looked in the mirror and saw an ugly, fat whore. Even though she was over model weight, time and time again people would tell her she was beautiful. "You're just saying that," was a common reply. The door to her bedroom opened and Steve Mayner stepped in... -------------------- Well I love you so much, but do me a favor baby don't reply. Because I can dish it out, but I cant take it. -- Limousine by Brand New -------------------- Steve blinked for just a second. Was this really happening to him? "Laura..." She scrambled under the covers ashamed, embarassed and already crying. Rhett knew he was caught and there was nothing he could do but lift his hands in the air and step out of the bed, exposing his pale and naked flesh. Steve's face was beet red. Who was this horrible bastard? Why was he with the love of his life, the mother of his children? "LAURA WHAT THE FUCK!?!" Laura was sobbing, "I'm sorry! Steven I'm sorry!" "Get out from under the blanket, Laura!! How the fuck would you do this to me!?!" Rhett had his boxer-briefs on and was slipping into his basketball shorts. Laura was crying and she lifted her cover finally away from her, exposing her naked body. "HOW COULD YOU DO THIS!?" "Steve... I'm so sorry... please just understand, I'm so fucking--" "--slutty. Yes, we see that now... you god damn whore. I've done every. fucking. thing. I could for you. And you fucking throw it in my face." "Steve... please..." "In my FUCKING FACE, Laura. It's all so clear. I suspected this, you stupid bitch. Those long nights you were out with no call home. YOU THINK YOU CAN JUST FUCKING TAKE ADVANTAGE OF ME YOU SLUT!?!" For the first time in a, well, ever. Laura looked scared of Steve. "...And with this fucking... fucking... WEIRD, ALBINO FREAK!?!" Locke shot Steve a glance. He probably deserved that one, Rhett thought. He was knowingly sleeping around with another man's woman. Not only that, having a near full-blown relationship with her. However, something happened that shocked even the weird, albino freak. "He's not a freak, Steve..." Steve glared. Was she really going to make that an arguement right now? After taking his heart, throwing it on the ground and taking a huge shit all over it? "What did you just say?" "He's not a freak." Steve did the only thing he could do right then and he sat indian style on the bedroom floor and cried. Rhett did the only thing he could do right then and told Laura, "I love you." Laura did the only thing she could do right then and jumped out of bed and ran to Steve, holding him. She looked up and mouthed, "I love you" back to Rhett as he walked out of the door. As he walked out of her home... her nice, American home, he could only feel malice toward her in the moment. It was his defense and a way to handle the three words few would ever utter to Locke his entire life. The last and only person to do that to him before was his mother. She was a crazy bitch who held a gun to his head frequently as a child before putting it in her mouth and actually pulling the trigger later in his life. As far as he was concerned, he was cursed. And the cursed are always destined to be alone. Aloof and misunderstood, they travel by themselves. Rhett got in his car.
