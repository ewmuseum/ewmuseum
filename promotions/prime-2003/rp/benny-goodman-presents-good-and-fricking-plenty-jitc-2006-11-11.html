Benny Goodman presents: GOOD AND fricking PLENTY JITC

11 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/11/06 Duke was busy making pasta spilling red sauce distaining his white shirt. On the floor full of disdain was Duke’s fat cat Guthrie, who was bitter at the world around him. Hoyt was sitting at the kitchen table playing Solitaire bitching about the woes of life. Hoyt: So they canceled the two hours of NYPD Blue reruns, for a flipping ROSEANNE Marathon. Duke: Yea? Hoyt: Are you grasping the gravity of this situation? I mean instead of Sipowicz; I get some fat, filth mouthed, sinner, with dyke daughters. I mean hello!! Duke: Wasn’t John Goodman on that show? I always liked John Goodman. Hoyt sighs a loud sigh as he gathers up the cards and puts them back in the box. Guthrie a big Roseanne Barr fan strolls out of the kitchen to see if she’s still on the TV. Duke goes on with his cooking half listening. Hoyt: HELLO AGAIN!! This isn’t about John Goodman, or Benny Goodman, or even GOOD AND fricking PLENTY. It’s about the fact the TV guide said one thing, and the TV had something else on. I was lied to and that’s a sin!! I’m going to talk with God tonight and make sure Roseanne, the people who pick the shows on the USA network, and TV guide’s staff; burn in hell. Duke: Yea Benny Goodman had some good jazz licks. Duke stirs the pasta sauce, while Hoyt stirs the conversation. Hoyt: WHAT ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT? Duke: WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT!! Hoyt: Injustice man!!! We only have so many words to get to the point today, and your wasting a lot of them. Duke: I’m wasting them? I simply don’t know what the hell your babbling about. Hoyt calms down as he walks over to the sink and pours cold water into a glass. Hoyt: I lost the first round of my return party at the GTT, now I have to prepare for another big tournament. This time in my home fed of PRIME. If I couldn’t win the GTT, which is full of much less talent. How the hell am I going to win this tournament? I mean, just by showing up I should be the number one contender in PRIME! BUT NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! I have to prove myself and beat guys who don’t even have names, they are just question marks. Duke: I think those are mystery spots. Hoyt: Well if they can’t come up with a catchy name, then they are blood spots on life's tampon. I was so prepared for the GTT, how could I, the greatest wrestler of all time and God’s son lose. Duke turns off the heat on the stove killing the flame. He then pours himself a glass of water, and sits down at the table across from Hoyt. Duke: Let me give you some advice. When I was a kid my father used to tell me the tale of Peabody’s tomb. Hoyt: This Peabody must have come from the same nerd parents who named their kid Vangelus. Duke: Shut up and listen. Peabody was a monk of great powers in the Chicagoland area. Tales were told of him giving the blind back their sight, healing the sick, and even raising the dead. One day Peabody was stuck by lightening and died. Hoyt: God hates show off’s. If this Peabody was so powerful why didn’t he heal himself? If he did, he would still be living, and I wouldn’t have to be bored listening to this story. Duke: Never mind all that. So anyway, Peabody was buried. Hoyt: Was his first or last name Peabody? Was it like John Peabody, or Peabody Williams? If it was Peabody Williams I would think he was a pimp instead of a monk but, if it was John Peabody I could believe he was a monk. Duke: Shut up and listen damn it!! So out of respect to Peabody they built him this underground crypt that could be visited by climbing down some stairs. In the center of the crypt was Peabody in a glass coffin filled with formaldehyde. Hoyt: I knew he was a pimp! Duke: One day me and my buddies were hanging out and we all started telling the story of Peabody. Each of our fathers had told the same story. They said if you visit the crypt of Peabody you can see into the future by looking into his eyes. But all the monks protected the cyrpt and if they caught you they would shave your head. Hoyt: Shave your head? Duke: Just like a monks’! Hoyt: That’s lame. Duke: This was the 1970’s it was a different time. Hoyt: Whatever. Duke: So we all had the idea to find this cemetery and look into Peabody’s eyes. We packed up the van and the ten of us headed out to the country just outside of the city. We drive for about an hour, and keep in mind none of us know where this cemetery was. We passed by this creepy looking bone yard with candles on all the graves. We figure this must be the place. We all grab weapons, I grabbed a tire iron. Hoyt: Were you going to smash his glass tomb? Duke: Don’t be silly we had the weapons to fight off the monks. Nobody was cutting my damn hair, that’s for sure. So we slowly walked to this one area where there were some stairs going down. We get closer and closer, and all of a sudden about half the guys pussed out. All that was left was four guys and me. Hoyt: What does this have to do with me going out in the first round of the GTT? Duke: Shut the fuck up and you’ll find out. Ok, so all of a sudden we hear this mighty war cry; and this scene straight out of Braveheart is thrown upon us. Hoyt: Angry monks? Did they have their hair clippers with them? Duke: No it wasn’t angry monks. Hoyt: OH NO, don’t tell me they contracted out and got professional barbers? Duke: It was about 40 Indian guys in their twentys’, wearing pizza hut uniforms. Duke is serious in his delivery (no pun intended) as Hoyt laughs out loud. Hoyt: Like the Indians we killed with diseased blankets, or the ones we killed for their oil? Duke: Sandniggers. Hoyt: That’s very racist! We call them Oil Niggers in heaven. Duke: Well what ever the PC term is, I love everyone. I just call different people different names. So the Indians surrounded us, and next thing you know a bunch of cops show up with their guns drawn. We get on the ground and the cops question us. We tell them we were looking for Peabody. They tell us we’re a long way from Chicago and any goofy tale. Turns out we were in a Muslim cemetery that had been vandalized a few nights before. Hoyt: Do the Indians work at Pizza Hut, or was that like a gang thing? Duke: I never found out. The cops searched our van then let us go with the stern warning to stay the hell out of Lake county. You understand? Hoyt: NO, I’m more confused now then ever. Duke: You see son, sometimes you can be ready to go, prepared, and determined only to find you were in the wrong cemetery. I think the GTT wasn’t what fate had in mind, it was the JITC. Hoyt: Thanks for wasting my time.
