No infringement intended.========================== SUMMARY: ==========================Matt and Killean’s intense rivalry escalates further as Tyler cunningly organises a three-on-three benefit game of basketball on the River Court that pits the two against each other. Ignatius, Karina, Adam and Vangelus are also dragged into proceedings. Ignatius, in particular, is faced with a dilemma as he is torn between his oldest friend and his new “allies” – both of whom he has experienced recent troubles with. Confusion and chaos abounds amongst the six, but who emerges victorious when all is said and done?========================== PRIME TREE HILL 1x95: THREE-ON-THREE ========================== FADE IN: IGNATIUS: (v/o) Previously on PRIME Tree Hill… [EXT. TREE HILL HIGH – BASKETBALL COURT – DAY] (Tyler stands across from Killean, ball in hand.) TYLER: This is just how it has to be, Killean… I want Johnny handling the election. (Killean turns away, his pain contorted with anxiety.) KILLEAN: I don’t agree with it Ty, but if it’s what you want… (Tyler places his hand on Killean’s shoulder, nods and then smiles deviously.) CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH – CORRIDOR – DAY] (Karina and Ignatius bump into each other.) KARINA: I was impressed with you last week. (Ignatius scowls.) IGNATIUS: That impressed you? CUT TO: [EXT. TREE HILL - RIVER COURT - EVENING] (Karina approaches Ignatius, who is practising on the court.) KARINA: You gonna play it that way with Matt? (Ignatius carries on playing, he does not look up.) IGNATIUS: I don’t need to cheat to beat him… (Matt enters the scene from another angle.) MATT: …Is that so, Lisieux? (Ignatius stops dead in his tracks, he looks mortified.) CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH – CLASSROOM – DAY] (Ignatius is in discussion with a staff member; Matt approaches him cautiously from behind.) MATT: Lisieux? (Ignatius winces and then turns to look.) MATT: That election win was BS, man - and we both know it. CUT TO: [INT. NELSON RESIDENCE – TYLER’S OFFICE – EVENING] (Tyler is speaking on the telephone.) TYLER: Change of plans. It’s not one-on-one anymore - I want to make it a three-on-three match… Matt, Karina and Ignatius against Killean, Adam and Vangelus… (Tyler grins to himself.) FADE TO BLACK:END OF PREVIOUSLY ON:OPENING CREDITS ROLL: FADE IN: [EXT. TREE HILL – STREETS – EVENING] IGNATIUS: (v/o) Robert J. Sawyer said, “You can’t choose the ways in which you will be tested.” We are not masters of our own destiny, we are puppets in the games of the powerful… (Shot fades down from the sky past trees blowing gently in the wind. It’s raining heavily. Close-up shot of a stray cat prowling the streets. Shot cuts to a passing car, which promptly brakes and pulls over towards the side of the road.) FADE TO: [INT. ADAM’S CAR – EVENING] (Adam turns keys in ignition and faces Ignatius.) ADAM: You sure you wanna do this man? (Ignatius nods and makes a move to open the passenger door.) ADAM: Well man, just be careful okay? Don’t say I didn’t warn you… (Ignatius nods again, opens the door and swiftly and rushes from the vehicle.) CUT TO: [EXT. TREE HILL - SIRRAJIN RESIDENCE – EVENING] (Ignatius rushes up to the door, drenched, rings the doorbell.) IGNATIUS: (muttering) Come on… come on… (Door slowly pulls open, Killean appears.) KILLEAN: What the hell are you doing here? CUT TO: [INT. SIRRAJIN RESIDENCE – HALLWAY - EVENING] (Ignatius walks in uninvited, Killean steps aside.) IGNATIUS: Just stopping by to see how you’re doing… (Killean grins and closes the door behind him.) KILLEAN: (still grinning) And very nice of you it may be… but forgive me if I don’t believe you. (He hands Ignatius a towel and takes a seat on the stairs.) IGNATIUS: Forgive me if I don’t keep the charade up, then… (shrugging) I guess you know why I’m here. KILLEAN: (solemnly) The three-on-three thing… (Ignatius nods and then ruffles the towel against his wet hair.) KILLEAN: …don’t worry about it man, it’s nothing between me and you. I’ll do my best to avoid you. (Killean sighs as Ignatius removes his head from out of the towel.) IGNATIUS: (looking relieved) Music to my ears man, music to my ears. Say, you got anything hot to drink in this big ol’ house? (Killean stands from the stairs and motions for Ignatius to follow him.) CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL - GYM - NIGHT] (Karina is going to town on a ceiling-mounted punch-bag. Matt enters the scene from behind.) MATT: (inquisitively) Say… you aren’t picturing that as Ignatius Lisieux, are you? (She immediately stops, but does not turn to face him.) KARINA: Him amongst others… yeah. MATT: Who else? …Killean? …Vangelus? (Karina fires off some more right hands.) MATT: (cheekily) I’ll take that as a “yes”, I suppose. KARINA: (aggravated) Just what are you doing here, anyway? (He shrugs and takes a seat on a rowing machine a few feet from her.) MATT: Nothing I guess… looking for someone to talk to, maybe? KARINA: (sighs) …I “guess” that lucky “someone” is me? (Matt laughs, scratching at his head nervously as he goes.) MATT: Looks that way! (She sighs again and begins to remove the boxing gloves.) CUT TO: [INT. OLSIG RESIDENCE – VANGELUS’ BEDROOM – EVENING] (Adam and Vangelus sit beside one another, X-Box controllers in hand.) ADAM: God dammit… (veering left) take that! (veering right) You asshole… COME GET SOME! COME GET SOME OF THE FACE-EATER! VANGELUS: Dude, that gamer tag is still uber gay… (Adam throws the pad down in a brief fit of rage.) ADAM: NEVER, EVER SAY THAT AGAIN! I AM THE ILLUSTRIOUS FACE EATER! (Vangelus cowers, looking increasingly fearful.) VANGELUS: (timidly) I’m sorry, I’m sorry… Facey rules all. (Adam immediately raises his arms in the air in an extremely dramatic and exuberant fashion.) ADAM: PRAISE THE FACE! CUT TO: [INT. NELSON RESIDENCE – TYLER’S OFFICE – EVENING] (Tyler sits slouched over his desk, sleeping. He is immediately woken up by his phone ringing.) TYLER: (confused) Uh… hello? (He nods along with the voice on the other end of the phone.) TYLER: Uh-huh… is that so? Does he know you’re calling me now? (There is another pause as he listens.) TYLER: Good, that’s good… just keep it how it is. He won’t suspect a thing. (He places the phone down and sits back in the leather recliner with a sadistic grin etched onto his face.) CUT TO: COMMERCIAL BREAK FADE IN: [EXT. TREE HILL – STREETS – EVENING] (Rain still beats down heavily on the sidewalk as the camera focuses on the exterior of the Sirrajin residence in the heart of Tree Hill. Wind disturbs the shot.) CUT TO: [INT. SIRRAJIN RESIDENCE –KITCHEN – EVENING] (Killean and Ignatius sit opposite one another at the breakfast bar.) KILLEAN: So what do you make of it all then? Me against you for Valedictorian? (There is a brief pause as Ignatius takes a sip of hot chocolate from a mug.) IGNATIUS: (quietly) It still hasn’t sunk in just yet… it’s a shame it has to be between me and you, but at least we finally get to settle that old argument. KILLEAN: (grinning) Hell yeah… it’s about time that we knew for sure just who is the better man. (Both men stop to take sips from their respective mugs.) IGNATIUS: (anxiously) I hate to ask you this bro, but you are going to play me straight, yeah? (Killean stops drinking and his eyes narrow a little.) KILLEAN: (swiftly, definite anger is his tone) Of course man. I wouldn’t do that to you. (Ignatius is taken aback by Killean’s tone, but is nevertheless quick to respond.) IGNATIUS: …You did it to Matt… KILLEAN: (still slightly mad) Matt was different. Matt’s always been different. I’m giving you my word here… the best man will win. (Another pause ensues as Ignatius finishes off his drink. Wind blows violently past the shuttered windows.) IGNATIUS: (timidly) What about Tyler? KILLEAN: (confidently) What about him? I’ve got this whole thing under control. Trust me. FADE TO BLACK: IGNATIUS: (v/o) Trust… that everlasting covenant between friends. The thing that stands above all else. Knowing who to trust is an essential part of growing up, make a wrong choice and the repercussions are immense… FADE IN: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – GYM – EVENING] (Karina and Matt sit beside each other on a black leather weight bench.) MATT: (inquisitively) Why’d you have to rag on him so much, though? (She shuffles her feet a little and shrugs her shoulders.) KARINA: (exasperated) The guy is just such a square, he’s such a goody-two-shoes… how can I not make fun of him? MATT: (extending his arms outwards) But he’s a good guy! He’s done a hell of a lot for this place (he looks around the gym)… If it wasn’t for him- (Karina cuts him off immediately.) KARINA: -exactly… “Iggy” represents the past… I’m the future… (She turns to look at him, he is staring intently back towards her with a raised eyebrow.) KARINA: (correcting herself) We’re the future… and how can you be so full of praise about him after what happened last week? (He shrugs his shoulders and sighs.) MATT: …Wasn’t his fault, I guess. How can I blame him for it? KARINA: (scolding) You’re WAY too trusting of people, Ward… WAY too trusting. (Matt sighs to himself a little and rubs through his short, dark hair.) MATT: Nah, that’s not me at all… just with Lisieux I know that he’s good at the core. KARINA: (cheekily) Like you “knew” with Killean, you mean? (He shakes his head and motions to throttle her, playfully.) MATT: Jesus – don’t remind me… anyway, it’s getting late. I’d better jet. KARINA: (with raised eyebrows) Mary? (Matt nods a little as he stands up to leave. Karina simulates a “whiplash” sound-effect as the pair share a laugh.) CUT TO: [INT. NELSON RESIDENCE – BATHROOM – EVENING] (Tyler is in the bathtub, submerged under a mountain of bubbles. He is alone.) TYLER: (to himself, satisfied, sipping champagne) Ahhh… this is the stuff. FADE TO: [INT. OLSIG RESIDENCE – HALLWAY – EVENING] (Adam Dick is preparing to leave Vangelus’ house, he stands in the doorway ready to face the weather.) ADAM: (excitedly) Man you’d better play ball better than you Halo, I pwned you tonight, I PWNED YOU GOOD BOYEEEE!! VANGELUS: (whispering hastily) Man, keep it down. My parents are sleeping. (Adam shrugs his shoulders.) ADAM: Why did I even come here tonight? Do I even like you? (Vangelus looks puzzled.) VANGELUS: (confused) Good point… I don’t even know myself. (Adam shrugs again and then drives a right boot into the crotch of his host.) ADAM: Nah, just remembered… I don’t hang around with foreign fag-goths… see ya on Thursday!! (As Vangelus writhes in agony on the floor, Adam skips merrily away into the night.) FADE TO BLACK: IGNATIUS: (v/o) See what I mean? Trust the wrong person enough to let them into your life and you could be left flat on your ass… clutching your balls and squealing like a pig… COMMERICAL BREAK FADE IN: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – CORRIDOR – MORNING] (Ignatius is walking through the halls alone as Matt approaches him from the opposite direction.) IGNATIUS: (anxiously) Morning. MATT: Morning. How goes it? (Tension in the scene as both men stop to talk.) IGNATIUS: Fine – look I want to apologise- MATT: (interrupting swiftly) –save it. I understand. You didn’t plan it, it wasn’t your mistake. We’re cool. Let’s just concentrate on beating those idiots this Thursday. (Ignatius smiles, looking greatly relieved. Many extras pass through the scene.) IGNATIUS: That’s perfect man, thanks for being so exciting. What class you headed to? MATT: (looking at his watch) Maths, I guess. You? IGNATIUS: English. (Both men enter an awkward moment as they try to get past one another.) MATT: You go stage-left, then we don’t have a problem here… (they move past each other cautiously) There we go. CUT TO: [EXT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – COURTYARD – MORNING] (Karina is rushing in to school, the clock on the entrance wall reads 8.55am) KARINA: (to herself) Ugh, gonna be late again… (She bumps into someone, it turns out to be Killean.) KARINA: (sarcastically) Great! Something else to brighten up my morning! KILLEAN: (impatiently) Watch we’re you’re going… (Karina squares up to Killean, looking him directly in the eye. Passers by stop to observe the scene between the ex-lovers.) KARINA: (aggressively) Out of my way Killean. KILLEAN: (goading her) Or else what, Kari? Don’t make me drop you like a bad habit all over again… (They continue to square up as more and more rubber-neckers appear. Teachers swiftly appear on the scene from all angles to interject.) MR. ROBERTS: (with calm authority) Move along people, move along. You two (he turns to face Killean and Karina), I suggest you attempt to stay out of each other’s way… at least until Thursday. (Karina scowls towards Killean, who returns the favour, but both back-down and go their separate ways.) CUT TO:[INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – MEN’S BATHROOM – MORNING] (Whistling can be heard from one of the cubicles, Adam Dick enters.) ADAM: (shouting excitedly) GOOD GOD! WHO DROPPED A DOOKY-BOMB IN HERE!? (The toilet swiftly flushes and the cubicle door creaks open. Matt Ward appears.) ADAM: (apologetically) Ah Matt, it’s you… (shuffling his feet a little) I didn’t mean any offence there Matt, I hope you understand. (Matt stays silent as he walks past Adam to wash his hands.) ADAM: You know this thing on Thursday is just tearing me up… I remember when we were a team, me and you… and that other guy… we beat Ignatius, dammit! We beat him and that old guy he calls a friend… why did Tyler do this man? Why’d he have to make us fight again? MATT: (wiping his hands dry) You’ve got three seconds to get out of here… ADAM: ACK! (Adam’s eyes grow wide with fear and he immediately makes a bolt for the door.) CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – INDOOR BASKETBALL COURT – DAY] (Ignatius Lisieux is shooting some hoops as Vangelus hesitantly walks into the shot.) IGNATIUS: Well, well, well… if it isn’t Dani Furher, all over again… (He doesn’t acknowledge the intruder with a look.) VANGELUS: (respectfully) I come in peace, Ignatius… I didn’t realise you were here. (Ignatius frowns before shooting off another jump-shot.) IGNATIUS: Well now you do know… so hadn’t you better be moving along? (Another jump-shot falls through the hoop.) VANGELUS: A frosty reception indeed, not exactly as humble as I expected… (In a swift movement, Vangelus rushes forward and steals the ball from Ignatius before slamming it impressively into the hoop. IGNATIUS: (stunned and angry) Seriously Olsig… you’re best off not reminding me about what happened a few weeks ago… VANGELUS: (snappily) FINE! Have it your way Ignatius… if you want this to return to being personal, then fine… I’ll see you on Thursday. (Ignatius turns to challenge Vangelus, but he is already leaving. He sighs and picks up the basketball before angrily slamming it against the concrete wall of the court.) IGNATIUS: (v/o) When we are reminded of a time when trust and loyalty abandoned us, we lose our grip on reality… sometimes we even lose our grip on sanity. But there are men in this world who everyone knows better than to trust… FADE TO: [EXT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – BLEACHERS – DAY] (Matt is watching Mary’s cheer practice as an unwelcome visitor appears.) TYLER: Hello Matt… (Matt immediately shuffles left, shocked at the appearance of his nemesis.) MATT: (sharply) What in the hell are you doing here? TYLER: (raising his hands soothingly) Now, now Matt… for once I come in peace. (Matt raises his eyebrows a little, ever suspicious of Tyler’s motives.) TYLER: (creepily) Boy, Mary sure does look good in that little uniform… (In one swift movement Matt grabs Tyler by the neck, but soon pulls back as police officers arrive on the scene.) MATT: (confused) What the hell, Tyler? (Tyler stands up and begins accusing him.) TYLER: Well, Matt (he grins)… I think I figured out who broke into my dealership last week… I think he might have been caught on my security cameras… OFFICER #1: (calmly) Please come with us, Sir… (Matt sighs and gives an agonised look towards where Mary is. He stands up and is placed in cuffs by the second police officer as Tyler places an unwelcome hand on his shoulder.) TYLER: (sadistically) I’ve got you by the balls now, son. CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – CAFETERIA – DAY] (Killean Sirrajin is waiting in the lunch-line as Chet Worth clumsily stumbles past him. Chet taps Killean on the nose…) CHET: (playfully) Bink! (Killean looks confused at first - but then he seems furious.) KILLEAN: What the hell do you think you’re doing, retard? CHET: (touching Killean’s tray) Bink! (touching Killean’s plate) Bink! (touching Killean’s shades) Bink! (Killean’s eyes narrow a little as a member of the school support staff rapidly approaches to defuse the situation.) HELPER: (apologetically) Please excuse Chet there, he likes to “bink” everything in sight… it soothes him. KILLEAN: (sarcastically, under his breath) I’d like to “bink” you, doll-face… (The helper stutters a little bit, grabbing Chet and then motioning towards Killean…) HELPER: I’m sorry, what was that? KILLEAN: (being completely false) Oh, I just said no problem… (he looks at Chet behind the helper’s head and motions a “cut throat” sign as the OCD-sufferer gulps.) CUT TO: COMMERCIAL BREAK FADE IN: IGNATIUS: (v/o) …When your trust in someone is rewarded, be it via an act of kindness or a well-kept secret, you gain a debt… It is your duty to one day repay that debt. Today was that day. [EXT. TREE HILL – POLICE STATION – EVENING] (Rain still relentlessly pounds the ground as the camera focuses a wide-angled shot on the Tree Hill Police Station, a car pulls up in front of the station and grinds to a halt with a slight “screech”.) CUT TO: [INT. KARINA’S CAR – EVENING] (Karina turns the key in the ignition to kill the engine as she turns to her unlikely passenger, Ignatius Lisieux.) KARINA: (exasperated, tired, agitated) Okay… (she sucks air in through her teeth) …I brought you here and I lent you money, now you can go and get Matt… (to herself) Jesus, what am I doing. (Ignatius smiles back at her.) IGNATIUS: (cheekily) I KNEW you had good bones in your body somewhere, Kari… I just knew it! (She scoffs a little as he begins to leave.) KARINA: You seriously need to get some wheels man - you’re such a bum… (Ignatius does his best to ignore the cutting comment, deciding it best to swiftly exit the car and brave the rain.) CUT TO: [EXT. TREE HILL – POLICE STATION – EVENING] (A wide angled shot of Ignatius running into the station, doing his best to beat the rain.) CUT TO: [INT. TREE HILL POLICE STATION – RECEPTION] (A wide-berthed black man sits behind the reception desk as Ignatius cautiously approaches.) OFFICER: (raising his eyebrows) Can I help you, son? IGNATIUS: (nodding) Yeah… I’m here to bail out Matt Ward… FADE TO: [INT. TREE HILL HIGH SCHOOL – CAFETERIA – MORNING] (Ignatius Lisieux, Karina Wolfenden and Matt Ward sit around a small table in the far corner of the dining hall, nobody eats. The hall is a hive of activity and background noise.) KARINA: (through gritted teeth) So, Ignatius… are you ready for Thursday? IGNATIUS: (laughing) How hard do you have to try to be nice to me? (Karina fires an angry glare towards Matt, who merely chuckles upon seeing it.) MATT: I guess that means “very hard”. (Facing Iggy) I’ll be honest with you man, I asked her to cut you a little slack this week… we do need to team, after all. IGNATIUS: (sarcastically) Oh how nice of you both! Being nice to little old me just because we have to team-up… KARINA: (scowling) I can just as easy stop… (Ignatius raises his hands apologetically.) IGNATIUS: No, no, no… I’m sorry… I get it – only you are allowed to do sarcasm. (Matt laughs again and then, in turn, receives another fiery glance from his female team-mate.) KARINA: (still scowling towards Ignatius) Don’t you have to be somewhere, or something? MATT: (interjecting) Ah come on now… we’re here to plan… Killean, Adam and Vangelus aren’t exactly easy meat on the court. (Karina scoffs, Ignatius fires her a frustrated glare.) IGNATIUS: (agitated) Lemme guess… Kari thinks we’ll beat them no matter what… KARINA: So what if I do? (she pops a gum bubble) (Ignatius frowns a little as Matt once again is forced to attempt to keep the peace.) MATT: Can we please just put the claws away for five minutes? We’re never gonna win this game if you keep this up… and we need to win this… KARINA: (raising her eyebrows) You mean you need to win this… it’s just another match to me… IGNATIUS: I’m with Matt (staring at Karina)… we can’t lose this… not the three of us together. It’s unthinkable. (Kari faux-yawns a little at Ignatius’ typically straight-laced response - causing him to again fire her a cautionary glance.) MATT: (shaking his head) Oy, oy, oy… (The shot cuts to the far side of the hall, where Killean Sirrajin, Adam Dick and Vangelus Olsig are approaching with Tyler Nelson in-tow…) IGNATIUS: (worried) Uh-oh… incoming… (Matt and Karina both look up, the former immediately rising to his feet upon seeing the intruders. Ignatius stands up beside him and places a cautious, soothing hand on his shoulder as Karina pops out another obnoxious gum-bubble.) KARINA: (sarcastically) Great! This should be fun… (The four men arrive at the foot of the table as numerous staff members immediately begin to flock to the scene.) MR. GILES: (warning) We’ll have no trouble in this food hall – save it for the game… MR. JAMES: Agreed… anything you all have to settle can wait. This isn’t the time or the place. (Tyler Nelson stares at both of them nonchalantly and waves his hand a little…) TYLER: Nonsense… there’ll be no trouble – I’m merely here with a warning to jailbait and his two loveable allies… (Mr. Roberts and Mr. O’Reilly also appear on the scene and take up positions in between the rival factions.) ADAM: Dude, can we just kick their asses? I want the girl if we’re gonna do that… definitely the girl. (Karina narrows her eyes and scowls towards him, he instantly backs off.) IGNATIUS: (pleading) Killean, what’s up with this man? (Killean merely extends his arms as if to say “nothing to do with me”.) TYLER: Button up Lisieux and listen to me good… you too Mat, you too Doll-Face… this Thursday you three are going down. Everything you stand for, everything all these idiots love you for… it’s not going to matter. My guys are going to tear you apart in front of everyone that matters. (Matt’s right arm begins twitching with fury, causing Ignatius to tighten his grip on the shoulder. Spectators to the scene begin to mount up around the shot.) TYLER: You’d better pray to God for a miracle, you’re going to need one… (Karina, surprisingly, is the first to bite back…) KARINA: Is that a fact? (another gum-bubble pops) (Tyler narrows his eyes at her audacity.) KARINA: I say that on Thursday that the three of us are going to tear your guys apart and spit them out for everyone to see… (A brief “roar” bursts out from the gathered pupils.) KARINA: (confidently continuing)… and if you think that by placing me together with these buffoons that disharmony is going to grant you an easy win, then you’d be wrong. Last night me and Iggy over here bailed Matt out of the police station… TYLER: (shocked) YOU did that!? (Karina nods, delighted at his reaction.) IGNATIUS: (interjecting) Hell yeah we did… we’re together on this one, Tyler… you and your boys are going down this Thursday… (Another murmur of approval sweeps the hall as back-and-forth shots of the two teams fill the screen…) (The shot zooms in on the contorted face of a frustrated Tyler Nelson.) IGNATIUS: (v/o) In the end though, trust is all we have... trust is all we need... trust will see us through. FADE TO BLACK:END CREDITS ROLL: (Resource used for transcript template: www.twiztv.com)
