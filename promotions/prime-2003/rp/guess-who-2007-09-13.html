Guess WHO!

13 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-13-07 Like a fart in the wind most of what we do in life is meaningless. What gives are lives meaning, is the chances we take in between the normalcy of our everyday life. I could have stayed retired and lived on my legend forever. But being the GOD that I am I have returned to wrestling to show the world I was no fluke. Gambling is a byproduct of diseased, I don’t gamble as I already know I’m going to win. Hoyt Williams: There is no way you can beat me at Guess Who. Big Al: You maybe a savior but dude, I don’t lose at Monopoly or Guess Who. My manager Big Al appears to be challenging me to a game of Guess Who, while sucking on M&M to get to the peanut core. Why not try a salad and save his stomach and soul? I will do all I can to save him one day but he may be a lost cause but here on earth he’s a hell of a manager. Hoyt Williams: You know how many calories are in those M&M’s? I mean look at you just sucking them down like their water. I envy yet pity your gluttony. My God man. Big Al: Are you trying to get out of a game of Guess Who? Hoyt Williams: If I ordered a pizza would you have some? Big Al: Sure. Hoyt Williams: The candy isn’t enough? Big Al: Fuck you its candy not food. Hoyt Williams: I’m just saying man. There was only one thing to do and that was to get the game Guess Who which was buried deep under Battle Ship and Mouse Trap. I pull it out and toss it on the table like a jouster using his glove to slap a challenge. Big Al: So are you ordering a fucking pizza or not? Hoyt Williams: It’s 9am. Big Al: Amato’s is open. Hoyt Williams: Mastering the game of Guess Who takes deep concentration. Chandler Tsonda does not have concentration and that’s why he is a disgusting human being. That is why he will be struck down by the grace of Hoyt Williams in the slick laid back biblical vengeful style of Revelations. You understand. Big Al: No, and if your not ordering a pizza I will. Hoyt Williams: No food till after the game. Big Al: Are we going to put some money on this little game of guess work? Hoyt Williams: I don’t gamble I know. So being the fact I know I’m going to win I’ll put a grand on the game. But really I’m conflicted since I know I’m going to win isn’t this stealing? Big Al: Doesn’t the church always make the most on the bingo games? Hoyt Williams: Well yea, but that’s just us taking it from the limp dicked blinded sheep Tyler Rayne. Morons whom throw their foolish coin at the feet of God thinking their shine will get them into heaven. It doesn’t work that way you know. Big Al: SO ARE YOU PUTTING MONEY ON THE GAME OR NOT? Hoyt Williams: To teach you a lesson I will gat a G on the game. Big Al: G? What are you gangster now? Hoyt Williams: Everybody knows I’m the G…..O-D from the H-SIDE! Word to your heavenly father! Big Al: I think you should change you’re in ring persona to be more like that!! Then you can pick up the whole urban market. Hoyt Williams: What are you talking about?? The blacks already love me!! Everybody loves Hoyt don’t fall for that liberal spin the PRIME media wants you to believe. Clearly they doctor footage and pipe in the boo birds because when I look out into those faces of believes I WITNESS millions of my fans paying homage to the Hoytness!! Big Al: You’re a regular Bob Hope. Hoyt Williams: I like that, despite your sarcasticness! My now moniker will be Hoyt “Hope” Williams because that is what I am, HOPE. When people wake up in the morning with their backs aching and eyes glazed with boogers heading off to their low paying jobs they now have something to look up to. I AM a man whom has riches, nice beds, and no eye boogers. In today’s day and age I am the alpha and omega male! Big Al: Are you ready for your fist PPV back? Hoyt Williams: Does the pope pray to Hoyt? Damn Skippy I’m ready to rock and roll showing the world what an active member of the Hall of Fame looks like. I’m sure Satan is playing his games hell just last night I couldn’t find my car keys. You know why? Big Al: Satan?? Hoyt Williams: The one and only. I have been gone way to long. Since I have returned to PRIME Rosie O’Donnell was tossed off the view, the Stock Market has reached new highs, AND the NFL has returned. Coincidence? I THINK not. Clearly the work of a heavenly body. That body being me. Big Al: Wasn’t the NFL going to return anyway? Hoyt Williams: Was it? The world never has to wonder thanks to my return. Big Al: I’m pretty sure it would have anyway. Hoyt Williams: That’s why I’m in the know and your not. IS your sinner wearing a hat? The game had begun. Big Al: No. I’ve also noticed the gas prices went down as soon as you returned to PRIME. Is your suspect a man? Was he toying with my ego trying to read into whom my suspect is? It really doesn’t matter because as I eliminate the sinners with hats my suspicions grow toward an evil bald men with the devil in his eyes. Hoyt Williams: Yes, man is always the sinner. IT goes back to Adam and Eve we are naturally the bad. The women are the light and givers of life while the men are the takers of life and the darkness. Big Al: Are you speaking in jive or are you saying your card is a dude. Hoyt Williams: Are you listening to me in this ever important game of deduction? I said it was a man, and it is. Now listen here tootsie roll more people need to my words. Maybe even writing them down in a book so people in the future can share in my brilliance and insight. As a matter of fact I think I’m going to get 12 friends to follow and record all my words. Big Al: That’s a great and original idea!!! Hoyt Williams: That is why I’m a savior and you’re a manager. Now allow me to ask is your sinner Jewish? Big Al: What? Hoyt Williams: I told you to listen up tootsie roll why must I keep repeating my self. I asked if your sinner was Jewish or not. Big Al: Who the hell am I supposed to know? Hoyt Williams: Look at them. Big Al: That’s racist. Hoyt Williams: What I asked you if he was a black guy? Big Al: But a black guy can be Jewish?? Hoyt Williams: Now your just backtracking. Big Al: Are we going to order a fucking pizza or not? Hoyt Williams: This is a thousand dollar game focus!! Big Al: Ok but I can’t tell you if he’s Jewish or not. I really don’t know. In anger people give up the simplest of clues as they always show their cards. Al in anger just said ‘he’ so the sinner is surly male and I’m yet to even ask my question. Before I ever get into the ring I shake the cage till the opponent shows his cards. It never fails and this game is no different. Hoyt Williams: Forget the question. Is the sinner bald? I smile as I watch in shock Al’s face as he knows I know. Big Al: Yea. Fuck. Hoyt Williams: Only pure women my friend, only pure women. Not dirty tramps like that so called champion of ours. Thank God the fans now have a….ahhh….HOPE like my self with God’s title to save them all. Big Al: Does your person have red hair? Hoyt Williams: Maybe he does his hair so I really don’t know. Big Al: Quit fucking around. Hoyt Williams: Well if you can’t tell if somebody is Jewish how can I tell if he dyes his hair or not. Big Al: Is his CURRENT hair color RED? Hoyt Williams: No. Big Al: FUCKER. Hoyt Williams: I would like the make a guess Watson and end this little charade and collect my thousand dollars in peace. Big Al: You can’t know already. Hoyt Williams: I being whom I am do know. Its really elementary my high calorie manager. Picture the words that enter your way and you will see the path. Now I need to escalate a hurricane to drive those gas prices up to punish the sinners whom have booed me. Big Al: I thought you said that the boo’s were being piped in. Hoyt Williams: Then those whom released the boo’s shall cause us all to pay. Now at the Pay-Per-View PRIME will also feel my wrath. Now give me my thousand dollars. Big Al: You didn’t guess. Hoyt Williams: Do you not trust your savior? Big Al: Well this is a thousand dollars. Hoyt Williams: SO BE IT SINNER!! Do to your mistrust I have unleashed a cop killer in Florida! Oh its Willard! Big Al: FUCK! Hoyt Williams: Another one crucified and saved! =w=
