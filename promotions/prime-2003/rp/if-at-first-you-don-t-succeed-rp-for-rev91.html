If at first you don't succeed... (RP for REV91)

3 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/06 It has been about a week since my major run-in with the Ishuu Kuroi and more importantly the Godaikoku. My body still isn’t one-hundred percent from my last encounter with them and the Dual Halo crashing down on me didn’t help matters. Nonetheless, I stand before the “Five powers”, ready for battle. This time I evened the odds a little bit in my favor. I am but one man and I cannot stop this army alone. That’s why I called in some back up. I’m damn good at what I do, but even Superman calls in the Justice League when he can’t do it alone. There is nothing dishonorable about admitting that you need help. Not saying that I am Superman either, but some ladies would say otherwise in the bedroom. Off to my left is the one half of the rescue squad that saved me from being put in a pine box. Her name is Lorna Campbell. She’s a former black-ops who specializes in firearms and covert tactics. The redneck is as deadly as she is fine. She may very well be the toughest bitch in the land and if you don’t believe me, tell her how nice her ass is and she will show you. You better believe she will put a pair of red dots on your forehead faster than you can fucking blink. Her job was to make sure none of the pee-ons in this place decided to jump into the action. She was the one who took care of me when I was coughing up blood and the first one Officer Funakochi called when he hadn’t heard back from me. I owe her my life, period. Next to her, stands the fucking ginormous killing machine from the United Kingdom, Nottingham. He’s the Vatican’s top mercenary. The Vatican has mercenaries? You damn right they do. You think those terrorists and cults just vanish on their own? It’s no accident that they are taken down so swiftly and never rise to prominence. He’s the reason why. We are childhood friends, as his uncle and my grandfather were best friends. He has a heart of gold, but that doesn’t mean he isn’t a little quirky. He likes to carve little crosses into his body when he sins or uses the Lord’s name in vain. He is a warrior through and through despite it all. To my right is the only relative I have left in this world, Wong Kei-Ying. He’s named after our ancestor and father of the Chinese hero, Wong Fei-Hong. Like his namesake, he is a master of kung fu and the “shadow kick”. I’ve seen him put his foot through things I thought were impossible to kick through. He was the other half of my rescue squad in case you were wondering. We may not see eye to eye on a lot, but blood is thinker than water. I have come to his aid before and now, he is here to assist me. We’re the remnants of a dying breed of Asian people. We believe in using our skills to better the world. That’s the one thing we agree on and that’s more than enough. With them all identified, it was time to get down to business. We all paired off, while Lorna had her pair of Uzis drawn, ready to pop a cap into anyone who felt a little froggy. Fei-Hong flung the side of his jacket around behind him and motioned for Hitosume, the enforcer of the Godaikoku, to come get him some. A sword gripped tightly in his hand. Nottingham chuckled, as he looked at the ichiranseisouseiji, not even carrying a weapon while they each have a pair of bos in their grips. For me, I wanted the leader of this villainous horde, but beggars can’t be choosers. I got that sadistic son of a mother fucking bitch Onryou. It was abut time I see how good I have become with the sword, as I fight the modern day master with Reido watching it all. “I am coming for your fucking ass,” I announced and pointed to the leader of the Godaikoku. “Just words, Darkness,” he replied back, as he took a seat in a large throne like chair. “Prove it.” Onryou drew his Hanzo sword and took a traditional samurai fighting stance, looking much like a batter standing at the plate. Unfortunately, I had to fight him at his own game, as my manriki-gusari has been missing since I had my ass handed to me. I never was very good with the sword, so I may never get to Reido. But who knows, right? Maybe I could get lucky and beat him right? Yeah, I am not really buying it either. I’m allowed to hope, though. Three battles commenced. I can’t really give you all the specifics on what happened with my two allies outside of knowing that they came out on top. Kei-Ying was able to topple the gargantuan Hitosume by using his speed to counteract the big man’s power, finally putting him down with a kick to Hitosume’s patched eye. As for Nottingham, the twins tried their best to attack from all angles and had things in their favor, until Taki got too aggressive. She had her bo caught in mid-strike and Nottingham gripped her throat with his other hand. Yoko went on the offensive, but Nottingham used Taki as a human shield. Yoko struck her sister in the spine solidly, taking her out of the match-up. From there, Nottingham had a fucking cake walk with Yoko, who he knocked out with an overhand right that shattered her pretty little nose into millions of pieces. That’s what they told me happened. As for me, you ask? Let me tell you all about it… That fucker Onryou was confident as all hell. You could see it on his scarred face. He was better than me and he damn well knew it. This was primed to be an easy and powerful win for him. I had gained a serious bounty on my head from the Triads, Yakuza, and Tongs for being a thorn in their side, so being the man who took me down would be quite the honor. Yet, I wasn’t ready to be worm food and my skills have improved since he last saw me. Maybe, just maybe I could keep up with him. I drew my blade and held it out before me. I quickly tossed my sword into the air and caught it in an underhanded fashion with the blade extending from the bottom of my fist. I took a deep breath and looked up at my foe, finally gathering up enough courage to face my apparent death. I brought my arm across my body and bent at the knees, taking what most would call a stereotypical ninja stance. “Your sword and life will be mine,” Onryou enlightened me, standing fast in his stance. “Come get some,” I snarled back. Without warning, I darted towards my enemy. Our swords crashed together, sending sparks flying in all directions. I unleashed a series of attacks geared towards his upper body and he deflected or dodged every single one. I spun quickly, hoping to catch his throat. He dipped under it. I kept rotating and spun right into a slice at his ankles to which he showed his great speed, hopping over it and delivering a kick to the side of my head. I rolled back up to my feet and found him right in my face. I almost fell on my ass, as I backpedaled away from him, blocking his strikes best I could. His rampage was capped off by a upward slice after knocking away. I saved my body from it, but my shirt was sliced open. With a few paces between us, we locked eyes once more and the motherfucking sadist was just eating it up. He knew he had my number. We began to circle one another, each keeping our stance, just in case the other decided to charge. Both struck by the same use, we race towards one another. Our blades slammed together and for once, I gained the upper hand. Being larger than him, I was able to back him against a nearby wall. He tried to catch me in the shin with his foot, but I countered by pulling my leg back to avoid the kick and headbutt him in the nose in one clean motion. His head snapped back harshly and I was able to catch him in the jaw with a straight forearm, as I pulled my sword back. My knee found his gut and things looked to be on the up and up. I figured that if I could keep the swords out of it, I could very well win this duel. I tried for another left forearm to the head, but Onryou ducked. Luckily, I was able to spin with my miss because it saved me from very well having my throat slashed. Instead, my back was split wide open. I fell to one knee and had to place my hand on the ground to steady myself, as pain shot through my body. I didn’t have time to tend to it though. My enemy tried to take my head off with a leaping slash. I was able to roll back and get my blade up in the nick of time to stop his overhead strike. He pushed down with all of his might and from the positioning and massive gash on my back, I could only slow his blade down, as he pushed it into the skin covering my shoulder. I growled loudly, as struggled against him. “I am going to make you scream for mercy before I skin you alive, Darkness,” Onryou confided in me, pushing his blade down harder. He very well might have been right. There was nothing stopping him. I wasn’t his equal to start and as injured as I am, he had the advantage increased. Victory was within his grasp. Yet in all his confidence, he forgot about one key factor to this fight, the will to win. Justice must be served and I would not be denied my vengeance for what they did to my dog. No one should ever have to suffer at his hands again. I would make sure of it. I let go of my sword with my right hand and despite Onryou’s blade cutting through my glove and into my hand, I began to push upward. I watched, as his eyes opened in horror. He could not believe that I was rising up to my feet from what seemed like sure death by his sword. He quickly pulled his katana back and spun into a slash. Just like he had done to me earlier, I bobbed under his attack and sliced his back wide open while he spun. He roared with agony and spun to his left in an attempt to impale me. I brushed aside his attack and caught him right in the mouth with a left hand that knocked him back. I marched towards him and he tried another flurry. With all of them blocked, he found himself being struck in the temple with the butt of my sword, as I spun with the last deflection. Each time he tried to go on the offensive, I defended against them and struck him in the jaw. Adrenaline was fuelling me now and I couldn’t feel either of my injuries. He was going to die on this night. My body and soul had become one and were focused on that sole objective. He was never really that tough physically and his right eye was swelling shut from my repeated attacks. He clenched his sword tightly before sprinting at me. He put all he had into his next few strikes, but momentum had changed. I blocked one of his strikes and tried to counter, but the cagy swordsman dipped behind me. I quickly fell to my left and placed my hand on the ground, so I could avoid his attempt to make me a few inches shorter and smack him in the side of his head with my feet. I used the recoil to rewind back to my feet, as he fell to the ground. Faster than you could blink, I my sword rained down upon him, taking his arm clean off at the shoulder. Onryou cried out in horror and clutched at his wound while crimson life flowed out of it like the Mississippi. “I should make you suffer like you do to everyone,” I announced, as I placed my boot on his throat, “but I have bigger fish to fry.” He tried one last time with his other arm. However, I caught it with ease and slammed my blade down into his mouth and through the back of his skull. My adrenaline was still pumping so much, that all the blood I lost didn’t stop me from turning my attention right to Reido. He just chuckled at me, as the rest of the Ishuu Kuroi closed in around us. “Eat hot lead, ya rice-farming assholes,” I heard Lorna yell in the distance, as she unloaded round after round into the mob engulfing us. Reido wasted no time in taking off down a hallway away from the main lobby of this underground hideout. I told him I would get his ass and I was not going to back out on my word, so I took off after. My friends could handle the foot soldiers. The hallway was long and winding with very little light. Doesn’t anyone ever have proper lighting in evil hideouts? It hinders them as much as anyone else. Anyhow, back on track. I finally made it to the end of the pathway to find it opened into another large room. This one was full of various machines and other scientific shit. These guys were never rocket scientists, so this is a little odd. I paused at the entrance way and examined my surroundings closely. I didn’t have any idea what those fuckers were doing with all this shit, but I was about to find out. “Vell it is about time zhat zie hero shows up,” a familiar voice chimes in. “You gotta be shitting me,” I say to myself, as Reido steps out with a cybernetic device attached to his right arm and the infamous Doctor Curiosity right behind him. “Now, I have seen it all. Has the Ishuu Kuroi become such a pathetic mess that you would deal with that reject from a bad scifi flick?” “Make jokes now, funny man,” Doc C. says with confidence, “but you vill not be able to stop zhe combination of his fighting ability and my super genius! Show him, Reido!” Reido clenches his metal-covered hand and from it, extends a laser in the shape of a katana. “Okay, two things,” I bought to light with a raised brow, “Doc, that haircut is just… ridiculous. Two, when the fuck did Reido become Luke fuckin’ Skywalker?” “You like zhat, yah?” Doc asked, as he was proud of his contraption. “I made zhat especially for him, so he could destroy you! I shall have my vengeance for you making a fool out of me on national television!” “Dwell on things much?” I replied sarcastically and pointed my sword at Reido. “Let’s see what that hardware can do.” We took off towards one another as fast as we could and swung our weapons towards one another. A funny thing happened though. I was expecting some sound effect like crash, clang, or bang. However, the only sound was the humming of his sword, as it sliced right through mine like I was fighting with a wet origami sword. “Oh shit,” I exclaimed, as I saw the energy blade come flying towards my head. With no way to stop the blade, I had to use every last bit on my agility to keep from becoming Reido’s newest and might I say, most handsome victim. He was relentless, in his attacks, I narrowly escaped them. He swung at my thighs and I had to do a back handspring just to avoid the attack. Knowing I was not going to win this battle by just dodging his attacks, I took off running. He kept steady pace behind me, as I leapt up onto a nearby lab table. He tried to take my feet off at the ankles, but I hopped over it and had to quickly drop to my back to save myself from being cut in half. He tried an overhead strike aimed at my legs, but I curled my legs up just in time, so he only took off the end of the table. Reido turned a full three-hundred sixty degrees and tried to remove my head from my shoulders. I just barely rolled off the table before he brought his sword down, cutting off another section of the table. I shoved the remnants of the table at, him catching him in the guy just as he brought he lifted the sword over his head. Reido doubled over with his upper body laying on the table. I noticed Curiosity trying to sneak away and kicked a rolling chair at him. The seat struck him right in the back of the knees and when he sat down in it, it fell back, sending him crashing to the floor. “I’ll be with you in just a second, Doc,” I informed him before turning my attention back to my former leader in the Ishuu Kuroi. I took a short hop and thrust my hand down at the back of Reido’s neck. Too bad, I only drove my hand through table, as he rolled out of the way. He tried to slice my legs while he was down on one knee and I leapt up onto a nearby rolling tray table. It took me a second to catch my balance and Reido took full advantage of that second, rushing me. He thrust his energy blade at me and I spun to the right, taking the steel tray table with me. He wouldn’t just give me a second to catch my breath. He tried to amputate my legs and I had to jump up and lay out flat. I held the two metal handles tightly, as I kept my body perpendicular to the ground. My arms supported my weight all by themselves. Said arms would have been detached at the elbows by Reido’s energy blade, if I hadn’t pushed off of them. As I feel behind the tray table, I kicked it and it crashed into Reido. I rolled up to my feet as fast as I possibly could and watched, as my foe launched the cart to one side in a fit of rage. The leader of the Ishuu Kuroi roared loudly and just missed slicing me right down the middle. I had dipped behind him and as soon as he turned around, I blew a giant cloud of green mist right into his face. He didn’t see me slip a capsule of green dye into my mouth, as he chased me around the laboratory. With him blinded, the time was right to bring the fight to my foe. I stepped in close, eliminating the reach advantage he had with the sword. I gripped him by the collar and slammed my knee into his gut as many times as I could. He swung at my with his metal-covered arm and I captured it under my own, further taking it out of the equation. I unleashed a trio of punches to his chest before sending him into a back flip with a lariat like strike that struck him right in the jaw. Reido scurried to his feet and held his jaw, as I came dashing towards him. I thought he might lead with his new weapon and I was dead on. I caught his wrist mid-move and trust my other hand against his elbow hyper extending and possibly breaking it. With his right wrist still captured, my elbow crashed into the right side of his dome and a chop found his neck on the same side. I twisted his wrist harshly, flipping him to the floor where I drove my boot right into his jaw. He was out of it, as I quickly bent his injured arm back, sending the energy blade right into that main artery in his throat. I am not a doctor, but you don’t need the exact name to know which one I am talking about. Doesn’t matter anyhow, as name or no name, his ass was dead. Blood gushed out of him like a geyser and I rose up to my feet. Doctor Curiosity couldn’t believe what he just saw. His invention did nothing to help stop his hated foe. However, he wasn’t going to stick around long enough to find out happens next or so he thought. I found that white-haired bastard trying to crawl away quietly to some escape hatch on the floor. He had just gotten it open when I step on the lid, crushing his poor little fingers under it. He cried out in pain and it was music to this ninja’s ears. “In such a big rush to leave?” I asked upon crouching down in front of him with my foot still firmly on the escape hatch. “No hard feelings, yah?” He said with fake grin, as he was trying not to show how afraid he was of me at that point in time. “I just vanted to test you! That’s the ticket! I wanted to see how good you really are!” “Is that right?” I smirked evilly and put a big thumb print on each lens of his glasses. “It is very true, Mr. Xiong,” He had to stop himself from growling before he spoke, as everyone with glasses hates that. “And you have surpassed all expectations! You reward is the cybernetic sword your adversary was using! He could tell I wasn’t buying a single bit of this utter bullshit. “Please don’t hurt me,” he sobbed while looking up at me with big sad puppy dog eyes. “Don’t worry,” I smiled cheerfully and patted the top of this head. “I won’t.” With one clean shot, I knocked the dastardly scientist out cold. Outside of that one strike to send him to dreamland, I kept my word. I didn’t harm a single shaggy white hair on his head. However, I can’t say the same for the citizens of who found the wily doctor who they found laying in the middle of the street. Why would they harm him you ask? He did nothing to them, you say? Why wouldn’t they hate a man with a German accent in a Klu Klux Klan grand wizard uniform? Oh did I forget to mention that part? My bad…
