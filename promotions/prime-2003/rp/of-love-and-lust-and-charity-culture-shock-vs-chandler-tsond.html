Date: March 6 and 7 Thursday, March 6, 2008. Houston, Texas. Out of all the things two-year-old Cecilia Ryan loved most in her young life, she loved these the most: Her mommy and daddy. Naps. Her Auntie Lindz. And right now, two out of three ain't bad. The littlest member of the Troy/Ryan family sat in her high chair, fresh off of her afternoon nap and laughing up a storm as her aunt fed her some strained squash and shredded chicken from the night before. Lindsay held the baby's spoon in her right hand, playing 'airplane' with her niece, while cradling her cell phone between her shoulder and left ear. "Yeah, I'm in Houston for a few days visiting my sister," she said, keeping an eye on how high she was lifting the spoon so as not to spill anything on Cecilia's head before swooping it down towards her open mouth. "She came up to Boston last week to help me with some stuff and I figured, might as well stop over before heading down. What about you?" "Eh, I'm around Monterrey town. Not much going on here just yet," Tyler Rayne's tone of indifference came through the phone's tiny earpiece, and Troy could picture the shrug of his shoulders as he made the comment. "Didn't know if you were down here yet and wanted to rustle up some barroom fun." "Hah. No, I'll be there Saturday. My sister's got this charity thi--" Lindsay's explanation was cut short by the slamming of the front door and the loud whump of bags and a briefcase being dropped. Cecilia looked off in the direction of the noise and loudly proclaims "MOMMY!" before the figure eventually trudged into the kitchen with the weight of the world on her shoulders. "Hey sweetie pie," Alaina Ryan sighed, bending down to give her daughter a kiss on the head and to oblige her when she lifted her arms skyward to be picked up. Cecilia wrapped her arms around her mother's neck and Alaina rubbed her back gently. "Hang on a sec," Lindsay instructed into the phone, lowering it from her ear and resting it against her shirt. "Something up?" "I just had three people cancel on me." "What? Why?" "Oh, it's just a mess," Alaina pulled a big girl chair away from the table and sank heavily into it. "Who're you talking to?" "Tyler. One of the PRIME guys." "Ah. You want me to wait to spill?" "Nah, he's not going anywhere." She lifted the phone back to her ear. "You're not going anywhere, right?" "Nope." "He says no," as she lowered the phone back down. "Well, Maria and Carmen had a death in the family and they're on the first plane to Barcelona in the morning. And Angie's mother broke her hip so she's going to be with her in the hospital." "That's terrible, Lanni." "I know." Alaina set Cecilia down on her lap, took the plate from the high chair's tray and extended her hand for the spoon, which Lindsay gave her. "So, I was wondering..." "Oh no," Lindsay held up one hand defensively, a mortified look crossing her face. "Please, Lindz," Alaina looked at her sister with weary eyes and a full-fledged pout. "I wouldn't ask if I wasn't desperate, but I want at least ten people to do this. I can't call anyone else on such short notice and I'm going to be busy enough tomorrow setting things up and I don't want to have to scramble." "Alaina, this is a bachelorette auction benefiting Susan G. Komen and featuring cancer survivors. I don't have cancer, thank God, and this isn't something I feel comfortable doing." As soon as the words left her mouth, Lindsay Troy knew the exact reaction she was going to get. The pout turned into a full-fledged puppy-dog look, complete with quivering lower lip, and Alaina's bright blue eyes grew as wide as the plate which held her daughter's dinner. This was the look Alaina perfected at the age of five and since that time, few have been able to resist its hypnotic powers. Least of all, Big Sister. "Pleeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeease....PLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEASE..." Lindsay sighed and stared down at the cauliflower blue and sunshine yellow tablecloth, shaking her head at her complete lack of willpower when it came to anything family related. This is why I'm such a bitch to everyone else, because all my sappiness I save for my sister... "You freaking owe me, Alaina. And I use that F word in place of the other one I should be using because you're holding my niece right now and I don't want to be blamed for corrupting her." Alaina jumped out of the chair and ran around the table to hug her sister with one arm. "Thankyouthankyouthankyouthankyouthankyou..." "Alright alright. ALRIGHT!" Troy said, pulling away from her baby sister. "And I'm only doing this because it's going to charity." "Yeah yeah," Alaina grinned and skipped back to her seat. "Don't be such a grump. Who knows," Here it comes... "You might actually meet someone outside of a sports arena." Lindsay rolled her eyes while Alaina got back to feeding Cecilia. With an exasperated sigh, she lifts the phone back to her ear. "You're never going to guess what I just got roped into..." ---- March 7, 2008. Westin Galleria Hotel. Houston, Texas Standing at the front of a short catwalk off the main stage, dressed in a hunter-green wraparound dress and black peep-toe shoes, Lindsay Troy knew exactly how Chandler Tsonda must have felt during his days as a primary fashion model. And she didn't like the feeling one bit. "Let's open the floor to bids," the cheery auctioneer proclaimed with her bleached-white smile and tacky gold jewelry. Alaina told her earlier that she was a professional, did this kind of thing all the time. Lindsay wanted nothing more than to punch the woman in the face after ten minutes in the same room as her. She was the last one to go on and the rowdy crowd was even rowdier by this point, courtesy of two free drink tickets and the hotel bar being just a short walk away from the main ballroom. "Three hundred!" The catcalls and whoops from the men in the audience were to be expected, she knew. She even figured that those who were fans would be high-fiving the other men at the table and would be completely orgasmic over the prospect of taking the Queen of the Ring out on a date. She wanted to crawl into a hole. "I have three hundred, threeeee hundred dollars from the man in the blue sport coat. Three fifty? Three fifty, do I hear three fifty?" "Three fifty!" came a shout close to the stage. One of the orgasm guys. "Three fifty, let's make it four, who'll make it four?" "Four hundred!" "FOUR hundred from the gentlemen to my left, let's make it four fifty, four fifty for this striking young woman.." "Seven hundred!" "Whoa there, we're raising the stakes like a Vegas casino. Do I hear eight? Eight hundred dollars..." "EIGHT!" "Eight hundred dollars from our opening bidder. Do I hear nine? Nine hundred dollars for this lovely cause and this lovely lady..." "Nine!" "Nine from the gentleman in the middle with the orchid shirt. Do I hear a thousand?" "Fifteen hundred!" came a shout from the far corner of the room. Heads turned to see who made the bid and for the first time all evening, Lindsay genuinely smiled at her savior in the tight black tee and blue jeans. "Didn't think you'd go through this embarrassment alone, did you?" Tyler Rayne smirked as he held a beer between his fingers. The orgasm guys hollered again, and now bumped chests with their fellow fans. "Well now, we've got a wild card in the mix!" proclaimed the auctioneer, eyes twinkling under the crystal chandeliers. "And that card says fifteen hundred dollars! Fifteen hundred dollars is the bid, who will make it sixteen?" "Sixteen!" yelled orchid shirt. "Seventeen," Rayne coolly replied. "Eighteen!" "Two grand," the Underground Pimp countered. "Twenty-one hundred!" said Sport Coat. "Twenty-five hundred," said Rayne, rubbing his knuckles against this chest and inspecting the shine off them. "Thirty-five hundred," a new voice calmly stated from the doorway. All eyes shifted from Rayne to the mysterious new entrant into the bid war and once Troy's eyes followed, gravity took over and caused her jaw to drop to the floor. Alaina's instantaneously followed. Not again. Not him again... "I said, 'thirty-five hundred'," Joey Melton breezed into the room, his eyes a turbulent ocean as they locked on Troy, freezing her in place. He walked toward the stage, not breaking his gaze. Rayne approached too, unaware of the relationship this man had with Troy but not ready to just let an outsider walk all over his game. All other bidding ceased. "Four thousand." "Five." "Six." "Seven." "Ten." An audible gasp escaped from the crowd as Melton's voice calmly stated the bid. "Please," Lindsay finally whispered, not to either man in particular, but to Fate to stop this scene from unfolding. "Twenty." Rayne scowled harshly, yanked his wallet out from his pocket and tore a bank receipt from the billfold. "Twenty four thousand, eight hundred twelve dollars, seventy-two cents, and..." he tore a plastic card out from a slot, "a gift card to Steak n Shake." Silence hung in the air. The final gauntlet thrown down. Melton finally looked at Rayne, sized him up and smirked aristocratically. "Thirty thousand." ---- "How did you even know I was here? Huh? Did Alaina tell you?" Lindsay stormed out of the ballroom, humiliated and rattled, after the winning bid of thirty-thousand dollars was put in the books. Melton had hurried out after her while Alaina, having never met Tyler before in her life, rushed over to calm him down, explain the situation and apologize profusely. "No! Well, not exactly..." Troy narrowed her eyes. "She kind of...forgot to un-share her Outlook calendar with me after the divorce." "Joey, you don't even know how to turn on a computer!" "Hey, I may have been born in the Stone Age, but even I'm smart enough to keep Evans around to do those kinds of things for me." Melton jerked his thumb in the direction of the hotel's bar where Adrian Evans, midget wrestler extraordinaire and Joey's heterosexual life mate, sat chatting up a buxom redhead and a striking brunette. "I gave him a biscuit, a couple Benjamins and told him to wait there for me. Want to go say 'hi'?" "Absolutely not. I can't believe you," she starts to walk a few paces towards the lobby then whirls around to face him again. "First Vegas, now this? I thought you would have gotten the hint when I didn't want to talk to you then. And now you just show up here, thinking...what, that there's the possibility I'd be in town and you could play 'catch up'?" "Vegas was a coincidence, but yes! Look, I figured you'd stop to see Alaina and Dan before you went to Mexico. I figured if you did, you'd at least make a showing here out of sisterly support. My finger isn't completely off the pulse. I play the fool, but..." "Don't give me that line." Venom spits from her mouth, landing pointedly on his cheek. "Don't you DARE try that self-deprecating crap with me, Joey. It's not cute anymore and I'm not going to wilt like a flower over it. You can't just waltz in here, feed me some lines and expect me to want to have anything else to do with you." "Then what else should I try?!" he roared, counteracting her poisonous words with a lion's ferocity. "You dumped me, remember? On a fucking pay-per-view, I got served with divorce papers that I never wanted to sign. You ignored my phone calls up until you changed your numbers. You ignored my emails...well, the emails that I had Adrian type for me. You moved and no one would tell me to where. Not Dan. Not Alaina. No one. You cut me out of your life and not a day goes by that I don't regret everything I did to push you away and everything I should have done to fix what we had but was too selfish to do. And if the only way I'm going to get you to understand my complete and utter need for you in my life is to crash a charity event and make you see me again, then I'll gladly clean out the rest of my accounts and be the penniless pauper you first met all those years ago. She stood there in shock, frozen in time as Melton closed the distance and took her lightly by the wrists. "I know I shouldn't give a shit about you anymore. I know I should unlock my dick from the box you shoved it in when you left. But I can't. You're it for me, Troy. I didn't want to fight to keep you then. But I do now." "Wow, man. That's...that's really touching." From behind Melton, Tyler Rayne wiped an imaginary tear from his eye as the barb flew across the room and hit Joey right in the back of the head. Melton's head snapped back to throw a death stare at Rayne and he released Troy from his grasp. "Tyler," she started, face beginning to crack, but she was quickly interrupted by Melton. "Why don't you go back to whatever meat grinder spit you out, irritant." "Joey..." "Man, at least it's better than the vagina that housed you. Never figured Troy was into pussies..." "Rayne!" "Listen Chuckles, I'm sure you've got a great story to go along with the bouquet of flowers you've got shoved up your ass..." "ENOUGH!" Lindsay's scream echoes off the walls, bounces up to the ceiling and then ricochets off the marble tile. All conversation stops, from that in the ballroom all the way to the bar. The look on her face: reddened cheeks, clenched jaw, throbbing temples, coupled with balled fists and a rigid posture is enough to take Melton and Rayne aback and for Alaina, who finally arrived on the scene, to look terrified. "I am not anyone's fucking ring in a Cracker Jack box," came the deadly declaration from a thoroughly pissed-off Troy. "Lindz..." "Don't," she directed toward Alaina, then spun on her heel and stalked towards the exit, her feet leaving a blaze of fire in her wake.
