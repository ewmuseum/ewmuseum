Date: Today MISPLACED MISPLACED By The A-List (Chandler Tsonda, Danny Ferguson, and your Super Fucking Duper Champion, The Illustrious Face-Eater) FADE IN. EXT. JUNGLE - DAY We open on an eye. A green eye, slightly bloodshot, the result of tearing up. A drop trickles from the side as it blinks a few times. SHOT OF: KYLE LAMEN, the owner of said eye, slowly sitting up. He's dressed in street clothes, which, like Kyle, are slightly torn and ragged, but otherwise intact. Content that he's alive and in one piece, he begins to look around. He's in a jungle, surrounded by vines and bamboo trees. Nobody else is around, and we can tell by the look on his face that he doesn't know how he got here. This is a strange place, and for all intents and purposes, Kyle is a stranger here. VOICE (O.S.) MEEEEEEEEOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWWWWWWWWW! Kyle looks around to find his unexpected company. Mr. Bojangles strolls through the jungle a few feet away from Kyle. I know the angle is that we don't know where Bojangles is, but I think it's important to tell you right away that this is a parody, so bite me. And yes, we are going to use your character without asking. Lamen takes a few steps towards Bojangles, who hisses and scampers away into the jungle. He considers for a moment whether or not to purse. That cat is fucking annoying. Reluctantly, though, he gives chase. Crashing through the jungle, Kyle's jog breaks into a run when he hears something in the distance: CRASHING. WHIRRING. SCREAMING. He doesn't know what it is, but he runs towards it, because that's the kind of guy he is. Crashing through the bamboo, he bursts out of the jungle and emerges onto... EXT. BEACH - DAY ...a beach. A sweeping shot around Kyle shows the dramatic change from the green jungle to white sand and the deep blue ocean that extends beyond it, all the way to the horizon. He pauses for a moment to stare out into the water, then gets brought back to earth by a particularly loud scream. Dashing across the beach, Kyle encounters a lot more than just sand. Huge pieces of SHRAPNEL and WRECKAGE litter the land in front of him, and he darts between them as he runs toward the source of the screaming. It's a plane. The various chunks of metal and debris strewn across the beach are the leftovers of a plane – particularly, the PRIME charter to Tokyo, which doesn't exist, but does in this scenario. LISA TYLER, PRIME interviewer and erstwhile bitch, is the one screaming, and she continues doing so as Kyle bounces over a row of seats and tackles her to the ground. As the two of them crash to the ground, a flaming piece of a wing slices into the ground where Lisa just stood. KYLE LAMEN You okay, Lis? She looks at him for a second, then looks around...then starts screaming again. Kyle stands up and looks around again. KYLE LAMEN She's okay. He takes off down the beach again, on the lookout for anyone else in trouble. Throughout the wreckage, various PRIME employees and wrestlers are running around like chickens with their heads caught off. As he walks, we close in on his face and this really intense WHOOSH sound brings us to: INT. THE PLANE - EARLIER Kyle is listening to his headphones while Lisa Tyler taps her fingers nervously on her tray table. His eyes gradually wander over to the nervous clapping, and he notices Lisa's trepidation for the first time. He slides his headphones off. KYLE LAMEN Something wrong? LISA TYLER I'm not a big flyer. I can handle the short flights, but we've been in the air for at least six hours. Not to mention that all I can see is water below us. KYLE LAMEN Heh. Well, if you wouldn't give it so much thought, you wouldn't be ready to crawl onto the ceiling like this. LISA TYLER Yeah, but the second I stop thinking about it is the second we hit turbulence, and then I've gotta change my panties in mid-flight. KYLE LAMEN I can tell you without hesitation that I will never look at you the same way again. Above the two of them, the 'Fasten Seatbelt' sign dings on. Lisa flings a crazed look at Kyle, who sighs. KYLE LAMEN I've made this trip more times than either of us can count. We ALWAYS hit a little rough patch in the middle and fasten seatbelts. It's nothing ou- With a quick slam, a crash, and a tail-end-of-your-plane tearing-off-and-flying-out-into-space, he's cut off. Panic and screams fill the cabin and Lisa joins in, looking at Kyle as if to say that she'd never look at HIM the same way, either. WHOOSH TO: EXT. THE BEACH Kyle breaks into a sprint, seeing someone in need at a distance. He leapfrogs another chunk of metal and skids to a stop on his knees. In front of him, Damien Cruz is lying on his back, not moving. Kyle checks his pulse, then launches into CPR. KYLE LAMEN Come on, dammit. While he continues the attempt to revive Cruz, a new voice growls from behind him. DANNY FERGUSON I'd let 'im go if I's you, boss. The shot swings around to show Danny Ferguson sitting in a first-class seat with his legs kicked up on a suitcase. He's dressed more casually than usual – a black button-down shirt and blue jeans – and sports a dark stubble. He's holding a cell phone up above his head, looking at it instead of Kyle. KYLE LAMEN I'm not just going to let him die. DANNY FERGUSON Why not? I wouldn't tell nobody. 'Sides, once he's out of the picture, you got the monopoly on that snazzy haircut. (cursing to himself) How hard is it to pick up a damn signal bar? Ain't like there's a skyscraper in the way... KYLE LAMEN Are you going to help at all? DANNY FERGUSON I did my helpin' when I stole some sound tech's seat cushion and went fishing for this here phone. Once we get picked up, the Pay-Per View's gonna need its stars. Who all's gonna care if Paco there ain't in the Dual Halo? His momma? Lamen doesn't even respond to the last statement. Danny stands up on the chair and tries again for a signal, then walks off, still staring at his phone and cursing. Suddenly, Damien coughs to life, and Kyle breathes a sigh of relief. He sits back and wipes some sweat from his brow. KYLE LAMEN Thank God. DAMIEN CRUZ (sputtering) Where am I? KYLE LAMEN We don't know. I lost you there for a minute, but- Cruz passes out. Kyle checks his pulse to make sure it's just a fainting spell, then pushes back to his feet and drags Damien into the first-class seat next to him. CUT TO: Across the beach, KARINA WOLFENDEN is carrying BLAINE BLAIR to safety by his collar. She tosses him in a heap of bodies who she's already 'saved.' Although she doesn't show any direct concern for her peers, the mere act of saving is enough to know. And of course, the act of pseudo-kindness – perhaps the act of not having blood on her hands – does not go unnoticed. Listen, I've gotta jet - pun intended - and find my guitar, which is not really a guitar at all, but a guitar case filled with more pot. I need to make it there before the freeloading bitches do. Karina wasn't listening, because she was already walking out towards the wreckage again. WHOOSH TO: THE GATE, PRE-FLIGHT Karina is checking the clock as an announcement about flight times crackles over the airport PA. She's standing at the check-in desk, and has that bored look on her face that we've come to regard as normal. The woman behind the counter is tapping away furiously on her keyboard. TICKET COUNTER GAL Ah, here you are, ma'am. Seat 45A. KARINA WOLFENDEN 45? Is there something we can do about that? TICKET COUNTER GAL Excuse me? KARINA WOLFENDEN I just prefer sitting on the front of the plane. You know, last one on, first one off. Is there another seat I can have? TICKET COUNTER GAL (tapping on the keyboard) I'm sorry, Ms. Wolfenden, but we're fully committed. KARINA WOLFENDEN Well, it certainly seems like you tried your hardest. The attendant gives Karina a dirty look, which the K-Wolf graciously returns. As she pushes away from the desk, we overhear the conversation from one computer down, where PETER VETRA is also checking in. OTHER TICKET COUNTER GAL Here you are Mr. Vetra, seat 15C. PETER VETRA Tjank you I wouldliket o check my bagge. OTHER TICKET COUNTER GAL We already did that, sir. PETER VETRA Nevemnd me iam fukced up. Vetra takes his ticket and walks away from the desk...right into the outstretched arm of Karina. KARINA WOLFENDEN Petus, you got a minute? (beat) I was thinking you and I could chat a little about our dear friend Baltasar. It wouldn't help to be on the same page for the Halo. PETER VETRA Iwast hinkin the sam thing, but dont tink iWIll carry u threw it. KARINA WOLFENDEN Of course not. Equal partners, right? Anyway, I'll catch up with you on the flight. She pushes away from him and the two walk off in different directions. We follow Karina as she produces a ticket from the hand that was draped across Vetra's shoulder. KARINA WOLFENDEN (reading) 15C... WHOOSH TO: EXT. THE BEACH Karina walks towards the shouting without a hint of emotion on her face. CUT TO: Across the beach, Lamen is trying to lift a piece of wreckage up to let some trapped survivors escape. He's being assisted by BALTASAR, but 'assisted' is a relative term. BALTASAR What I don't understand is how I have to be the Iraqi guy, esse. KYLE LAMEN B, I'm really not in the mood to- BALTASAR I mean, I'm not any effing dothead. I have to deal with enough anti Mexican feelings, now I have to deal with anti-Arabian ones, too? KYLE LAMEN Could you just- We hear Danny's voice, and the shot changesto shor him sifting through some suitcases and observing the two men. DANNY FERGUSON Simple as cake to me, 'holmes.' TOSHIAKI MOTOKI Where is Tyler Nelson, dammit? TAUN PHAM Until we find him, Motoki-san and I are in charge! PETER MAXWELL Nothing. TOSHIAKI MOTOKI WE'RE SPEAKING ENGLISH! Pause. Chet and Peter look at each other. CHET WORTH Yeah, it's complete gibberish to me. PETER MAXWELL I never noticed how intense the language barrier was before. BACK TO: EXT. THE BEACH. Danny is still leaning against the wreckage. Baltasar is still looking at him. Kyle Lamen is still trying to SAVE EVERYONE. DANNY FERGUSON ...so I'm afraid that's just the lot you drew, Skandor. Hope you know how to fix radios. Baltasar nods in concession to Danny's point. BALTASAR Fair enough, esse. KYLE LAMEN Guys, do you mind? DANNY FERGUSON Nah, we're cool. Baltasar gives Kyle a hand and they lift the piece of wreckage just enough for the survivor to slip out. As they let it slam back down, Kyle's eyes burn a hole in Danny, who shrugs and goes back to his collecting. Finally Kyle turns away, but only to see PSYMON walking a little too close to one of the plane engines. KYLE LAMEN Hey, man, watch out! The man looks up at Kyle, and straight into the camera. WHOOSH TO: INT. SOMEWHERE - SOMETIME Psymon does some crack. WHOOSH TO: EXT. THE BEACH. He makes that crazy-eyes-smiley-face thing, then gets sucked backwards into the jet engine. It explodes in a fiery...um...explosion. In fact, it's such a big crack infused explosion that you can actually smell our special effects budget burning up in it. Kyle hits the sand and waits for the explosion to subside. When it does, he looks up at the motley crew of PRIMEates who sit before him on the cusp of the jungle. The look on his face can't quite be pinpointed, but the person who rolls up on him next offers some insight. KARINA WOLFENDEN I know what you're thinking. She holds out a hand and helps him back to his feet. KARINA WOLFENDEN You're wondering what the hell we did to deserve this. KYLE LAMEN More or less. They both stare at the remainder of PRIME's cast and crew, not quite sure what else lies ahead. FADE OUT. FADE IN: EXT. THE BEACH - LATER It's about midday on the island and no hope of help has arrived. The de facto leaders, Lamen and Wolfenden, have gathered the rest of the group to discuss a long-term plan for the future, which is kinda funny, because this joke only has legs for so much longer. DUKE WILLIAMS steps up out of the crowd and tips his hat to Kyle. DUKE WILLIAMS No problem hoss my son has it all taken care of why look at him now. Everyone stares at Duke for a half-second longer because they weren't quite sure when the sentence ended. Frustrated, he gestures towards the water. There, HOYT WILLIAMS is standing, shin-deep in the water - and wearing his tights, mind you - holds out his arms and looks towards the sky. CHET WORTH, CHIEF EXTRA You do talk kind of loud. DUKE WILLIAMS Whatever hoss my boy can turn sand into sandy tasting bread and get fish from out of nowhere hell he created that polar bear over there. HOYT WILLIAMS Got it. People look to their left again and the bear is gone. DANNY FERGUSON Can your boy conjur hamburgers, Merle? We were brought here for a reason, and that reason is to be fodder for a low quality parody roleplay.
