The Starr

12 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6-12-10 Steve Starr was the kind of guy that absolutely hated losing. “I get like this sick feeling inside of my stomach. My mind won’t allow me to easily displace a loss.” It had always been that way. Before Steve Starr was jumping off turnbuckles, he was catching pop fly’s for the Kansas City Royals minor league affiliate club, the Burlington Royals. While Steve had talent, he never put in the necessary work to advance. Baseball really wasn’t his love anyway. “I honestly preferred hoops to the diamond. But there were many more opportunities playing baseball than there were playing basketball. If you weren’t 6’5, 220lbs, it was hard getting noticed on the court. Baseball though? All you had to do was hit.” “I’m a guy that really harps on effort. If you are gonna do some shit, you should always go hard. I spend a lot of time in deep thought, and my mind will never rest if I’m always battling “What If?” This would explain the sudden return to PRIME. Realistically, Steve Starr probably could have exercised many options. Despite all of the travel and having to live out of a suitcase, traveling as an independent was still a lucrative venture. What it lacked though, was stability. There was no chance of Steve Starr ever getting to compete for championships. He was never around long enough and no promotion was thrilled about the idea of a traveling champion. “I always wonder ‘What If’ in regards to my first stint here. I was poised for big things. The place was still new, there really were not many established superstars my first time here. Had I stayed and committed myself, I’d probably be a 7-time Global, Universal, Intergalactic or whatever the hell they call their top crown now.” Steve Starr, ever the optimist. Considering that no PRIME superstar has ever held the coveted crown 3 times, 7 may be a stretch. But that’s the thing with the Steve Starr, he puts his mind to things and he normally gets his done. “I don’t want people to get it confused, I came back to win a championship. No scratch that, I came back to win championship(s). I’ve finally bought me a house. A very big house, with lots of space. I got a bunch of awards and trophy’s from my high school days, but really nothing to cement my legacy as a professional wrestler. That’s going to change.” Oh Steve, if you’re serious about winning, the opportunity shall come. You’ve made it back to the big-time. Here’s hoping that you have not lost a step, seeing as you have not been seen in a PRIME ring for nearly 6 years. Of course you have competed actively against lord knows who, but the fact of the matter is this is PRIME. Throw all of that other stuff out the window, only the strong survive here.
