Date: 11-27-07 The bible warns us clearly that our bodies are our temples and we should not worship false idols. But man is weak and the devil is creative, plus he runs with the times. Friends when you read the bible you get the feeling it was written a long time ago. That’s because it was. It’s always been my mission to modernize the bible so that the kids of today can understand it. Why? Because the devil already has revised his tactics to coincide with today’s generation. When’s the last time you read a comment on Myspace? When’s the last time you read the bible? The devils technically advanced, and that’s scary. Christmas used to be a religious holiday, but thinks to the genius of Satan; it’s now a commercial mockery with a fat, out of shape elf taking over as its icon instead of a happy savior baby. Santa plays to man’s greed disguising it as “giving”. He encourages kids to consume mass amounts of calories while conforming to the man’s word in order to make the “good” list. This year is going to be different as my forces are in full combat mode. I’ve taken down Santa aka Satan…I mean come on isn’t the name as obvious as Anderson Coopers sexuality. Nothing was hidden in Satan’s attempt, yet the world fell for it. Disgusting. Ethan Frost is clearly a minion of Satan sent out to stop me before I get started. Frost? Again with the name, as clear as Clay Aiken’s sexuality. Now a normal man would be scared to death with fear over stepping into the ring with one of Satan’s top soldiers. But I’m not a normal man, I’m Hoyt Williams. Monday was the day the planning and the execution of project “LickMyCandyCane” began. One step forward in burying Satan, and one giant leap forward to becoming President of the United States. It all goes hand and hand like nipples and sex. My research and public relations man Hoss Brooks arrived Monday with a game plan that would lead to the down fall of the fat elf and the preparation of the elimination of Frost from the tournament. Let me take you back to Monday at my corporate office. ----------------------------------------------------------------------- [A well dressed man rushes into Hoyt’s Oval office with a manila folder in his hand. Hoyt is kicked backed watching a rerun of Run’s House on MTV2.] Hoss Brooks: Hoyt this isn’t good! Hoyt Williams: You mean the vending machine is out of that cheese popcorn I love? Hoss Brooks: Worse. [Hoyt ponders for a moment what could be worse then a popcorn shortage.] Hoyt Williams: Oh my gawd, they cancelled Dancing With the Stars? Hoss Brooks: No, our research says that Santa’s public image is so high it would damage your image to go after him. [Hoyt laughs loudly.] Hoyt Williams: No way is Santa more popular then me!! I’m in the PRIME Hall of Fame, is he? Hoss Brooks: No but…. Hoyt Williams: Is he God’s Champion and winner of the Greatest Wrestler ever award? Hoss Brooks: NO! BUT HE’S MORE LOVED. [A soured look flashes over Hoyt’s face.] Hoyt Williams: Villainous coca-cola corporation of brain washed brilliance. Damn you! They have marketed and created this vile fat man brainwashing children everywhere. We must destroy his public image immediately. Hoss Brooks: But how? [Hoyt looks at the television for a moment then turns back to Hoss with a smile on his face.] Hoyt Williams: How good are you with this intro-net thingy? [Hoyt points to his laptop.] Hoss Brooks: I’ve been known to surf the waves of Gore once or twice. ------------------------------------------------------------------ It wasn’t an original concept, but it was what was needed to be done to take down the fat elf. The night before I talked with Hoss Brooks, I watched the “To Catch A Predator” marathon on MSNBC. This is what gave me the idea to catch Santa Claus. I had to entrap Santa into getting busted by Chris Hansen. I called my insiders at the network and found that PRIMETIME was working with Perverted-Justice in Long Beach, California for their next edition of “To Catch A Predator”. So I had my buddy Hoss Brooks create what the kids call a ‘screen name’ and hunt for children in Long Beach. The following is a transcript of one of the many exchanges that happened with our fake Santa and the Perverted-Justice agents. ------------------------------------------------------------------ KidPeteCool: 14/m/longbeach JollyCandyCane: Hey! What’s going on? KidPeteCool: Not much wut up wit u? JollyCandyCane: Just looking for sex. KidPeteCool: Whoa that b blunt. JollyCandyCane: To fast? KidPeteCool: No I kind of like it. JollyCandyCane: Would you lick my candycane? KidPeteCool: What do u mean? JollyCandyCane: You know, my ripe jolly old stick? KidPeteCool: I’ve never been with a guy before. Can you teach me? JollyCandyCane: I’m the master of the south pole. I can blow your mind with what I know. How about I show u, dressed as Santa Claus. That’s always been a turn on to me. Could you be a little boy waiting for a gift? KidPeteCool: I’ve always wanted to role-play! Sure. What will you bring me? JollyCandyCane: Erotic Rubbing oils. KidPeteCool: Cool! Pic? --------------------------------------------------------------------- A quick picture of some random penis and we were set. But how did we get Santa to walk into the trap? That’s where I stepped in. --------------------------------------------------------------------- Dear Santa, My name is Peter and I’m dying of a rare heart condition. My heart will explode in ten days. I will not make Christmas this year, and I accept that. What would make my last few days on God’s green planet more tolerable would be a visit from you Saint Nick. I know you are really busy this time of year getting the toys together for all the good little children across this great land of ours. But if you could find the time in your busy agenda to visit me for the last time, it would make my leaving that much easier. You see besides my condition I’ve also recently lost my father. He was killed in a tragic traffic accident. I miss him greatly. I was going to follow in his footsteps and become a great massage therapist. But given my condition, and his passing, I know that will never happen. Gee if it wouldn’t be to much to ask, that if you do indeed pay me a visit you could bring me some erotic massage oils? I know it’s a strange request but, yea. This would bring back great memories of my father and his profession. Perhaps mama will even let me be buried with them. Also a candy can in your front pocket would be really neat as well. Thank you so much Santa for considering this. You really are a swell guy and I cherish the warmth you bring. I hope to see you before I go to the after life, but if not have a great life. Love, Little Pete --------------------------------------------------------------------- One side was on the hook for certain the other side was a wait and see situation. I knew my letter was brilliant since everything I write is borderline genius. But would Santa have the time to visit the dying kid? I didn’t know which made me nervous since it was such a major part of the plan. I asked God for some help, and he made sure Santa got the letter but outside of that he couldn’t’ help much since Santa is a creation of Satan. The world is a dark place, full of dark ally’s, full of dark cats. But every once in a while the world lights up, the ally’s find sun shine, and even a dark cat has a bright day. Like when my buddy John Katz won the lotto. I’m still waiting for my cut for spiritually fixing the numbers; just as I did with Silver so he could beat Troy. It’s a thankless job being savior that’s for sure. You see dark and light is the very Yin and the Yang that drives sanity into our souls so that we can continue on in the darkness never really sure what we’re searching, or traveling for. Is life a spiritual quest of inner peace? Is life a chance accident that we must enjoy as best we can till we all step on a rainbow and go the way of Elvis and Milton Friedman? Is life nothing more then a test? All good questions that no human can answer. When somebody’s question can’t be satisfied, like the question of life it leaves an opportunity to be conned. From the very moment we are born we look for comfort. Somebody to tell us everything is going to be ok, and life will march on. But will it really? Not only do individuals look for unanswered questions as opportunities to con but so does the mind. Think about it, your very mind will create an oasis mirage in the middle of the dessert as you lay dying. Why? To comfort you into believing it’s going to be ok. Am I the crazy one here, or the enlightened one? Santa is comfort to a world freighted by the thought of a savior. Jesus does comfort but, his tale isn’t comforting. The image of God sent down in human appearance murdered by humans to comfort the government? The people were scared. Scared of their own mortality and of a vengeful God. Now do you understand why I have such a hard time proving my legitimacy as the Second Coming? The Coca-Cola Corporation in conjunction with the Prince of Darkness created Santa to comfort and con the needy public. It was a rather brilliant strategy that took the focus away from the baby Jesus and put it toward merchandising. Remember money is the devils greatest tool. I did some research and it turns out that the ad executive working for Coca-Cola in 1923 was Charles Frost, Ethan Frost’s great grand father. If that bit of frost doesn’t send a chill down your spine, then I don’t know what will. You see Ethan Frost is really Charles Frost a re-spawned demon who is actually a part of Satan much like Jesus and I are a part of God in the Holy Trinity. It’s confusing to those not in the know, but everything I am stating in my journal here is accurate. This week on ReVolution I will remove the little toe of Satan as I destroy his earth structure in Ethan Frost. I will melt him like aerosol on the o-zone! I will diminish his chances like the Christian right on John McCain in 2000. I have studied my opponent and know all there is to know about this Frost guy. I being a student of the game who has destroyed every big name ever thrown at him will continue on marching in the tournament to crown me the Jewel in the Crown, which everybody already knows that I am. So now, it’s just a matter of showing the world live on ReVolution. First let’s find out what happened to Old Saint Nick, shall we? ---------------------------------------------------------------- [A young male is sitting at a table with cookies and milk when all of a sudden a commotion is heard from the chimney. After a bit of struggling a fat man exits the fireplace with a sack over his left shoulder.] Santa: HO HO HO are you Adam?? I have a candy cane in my front pocket for you like you asked!!! Adam: Yes!! Sit down and have a cookie while I change into something more comfortable. [A confused look flashes across the jolly red face of Santa as he walks over and picks up a cookie. Before the cookie reaches his lip Chris Hansen exits from the back wearing a suit.] Chris Hansen: How is that cookie? Santa: I better be going. Chris Hansen: Why don’t you have seat. Are you Santa? Santa: Yes. Chris Hansen: Also known as “JollyCandyCane”? Santa: Jolly what? No I’m old Saint Nick. Chris Hansen: What’s in the bag? Santa: A present for Adam. Chris Hansen: Erotic Message oils? Santa: Yes, how did you know? Chris Hansen: We have all the transcripts. How about this? “I can make the tip of my candy can as bright as Rudolf’s nose.” Is that appropriate conversation with a 14 year old child? Santa: Well no! But I didn’t write that. Chris Hansen: Oh, so you showed up dressed like Santa, with message oils, just as you stated in your chat conversations with our model. This is all a coincidence? Santa: YES, and I think I’ll be going now. Chris Hansen: Soooooo this is all by chance. You don’t want to “lick his candy cane till it shoots fresh eggnog”? Or “eat the nuts out of his fudge”? Come on sir! What is your real name? Santa: Kris Kringle! Chris Hansen: Your real name sir. Santa: I am Kris Kringle, Saint Nick, Santa Clause. Chris Hansen: Well if that’s the story you’re going to stick with then fine. Santa: Who are you? [Camera’s spill in from the back and front all pointing at the old man.] Chris Hansen: I’m Chris Hansen from Datelines’ “To Catch a Predator” and we’re doing a special on man who meet up with youngsters. This we’re calling ‘Miracle on 35th Beach’. Santa: That is a very catchy title, but I’ve been set up. Chris Hansen: With erotic message oils for somebody you thought was 14? Come on Santa. Santa: I’m leaving. Chris Hansen: You’re free to go. Santa: I will. [As soon as Santa exits the house about 15 strapping brave police officers take the jolly man to the ground and arrest him for meeting a juvenile for sex. They also impound his reindeer and sleigh.] ------------------------------------------------------------ This Christmas will be all about Jesus Christ and dying for your sins. Not a fat Godless elf giving “gifts” to corporations. Sure the technique I used was, by modern stands, wrong. But what is law and who created it? Man. The same man that creates corruption, murder, rape, theft, and porn sites. I do what I have to do to win and that’s why I’m a legend and others are just passing through. God is my only law, and his work is my command. Soon enough I will be the Jewel in the Crown then President. I already am your savior and now is the time to hop aboard the salvation train before we leave the station and your left with a faithless soul in a world gone dark. Don’t say I didn’t warn you, as I’m not a conman but rather a savior and the true comfort you desire. Good bless you and Marry Christmas from the Williams family. =w=
