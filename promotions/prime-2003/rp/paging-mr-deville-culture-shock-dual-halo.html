Date: Timeless Fuck the Halo. Seriously. Even if it wasn’t the bingo game of professional wrestling, the sort of sweepstakes clusterfuck I live and love to mock and despise, why would I get all worked up striving to win it? To improve my marquee? Would winning the Dual Halo suddenly deliver little old me from anonymous obscurity into the limelight? Would it finally make the world sit up and take notice of my mad skills? Of poor, nameless Angelo Deville? I need sensationalist press like "Cap’n Crutch" Suleimon needs classes in hackneyed rhetoric. To prove I’m still at the top of my game? Sorry, that doesn’t apply. I’ve never in my life won a match like this and I imagine I never will. Know what that means? Absolutely nothing. Conquering something like the Halo is as indicative of wrestling supremacy as winning the lottery is of sound financial strategy. To become part of the proud heritage? Last year’s winner was Chet Worth, now a stuffed (jogging) suit making his living off the skills and sweat of other men. His ring legacy amounts exactly to that victory. The year before? Karina Wolfenden. She has accolades and celebrity galore, but ask her what has happened every single time we’ve squared off one-on-one. Every single time. The year before? I know who it was, but I’m ready to wager most people have to look it up, and that’s exactly my point. To wield some influence over PRIME, to ensure one of my whims comes to pass? Think about that for a minute. Think about it. Would anything really change if I won? ~><>...<><~ Not Outside Kansas Anymore?
