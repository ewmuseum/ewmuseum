Getting Back to It(Ultraviolence RP)

11 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-11-10 Steve Starr’s return to PRIME has been met with little to no fanfare. Asking random wrestling fans how they felt about his return were met with answers such as; “Who’s Steve Starr?”, “Didn’t he used to make porn?” or the ever popular “the guy who lost to Ian English nearly 7 years ago”. Yes people, that Steve Starr. You think people would remember a guy who was close to setting this organization on fire in his brief stint here. Then again, there are normally many flash in the pan talents that walk through that people have a hard time remembering every one that laces up the boots. Even the powers to be in the federation do not think that highly of Steve Starr, at least not as of today. Proof of that can be found on the next PRIME Pay Per View extravaganza, an event that promises to deliver a new Universal Champion. There are other enticing battles as well found on the card in addition to the triple-threat main-event. How about the 5 Star Championship contest for those of you more interested in just the headliners? Champion Elise Ares defending the strap against GBJ, should be worth the price of admission alone. Always a crowd pleaser, the Intense Championship bout is sure to please your hardcore passion. Tchu vs. Hush. Not much else needs to be said about that one. And oh yeah as for Steve Starr, who’s he taking on his debut? As you already tell, he won’t be competing for a belt as reported by some wrestling observer type website out there. The guy has yet to even reappear on live TV, did you really expect him to already receive a title shot? For all we know Steve Starr can be a washed up shell of himself. That is highly unlikely. Those who have seen Steve Starr train contend that he is in the best shape of his life. Then again, people close to Mike Tyson also said the same thing before he got whooped in his last fight against some Irish guy. That is probably one of the only times that you will ever see a Steve Starr-Mike Tyson comparison. But hey, you cannot expect guys that take these massive layoffs to be able to return at will without any rust. But as for Steve Starr’s opponents at Ultra Violence, he was taken for a bit of a loop. Here’s a brief recall of how the situation came about. First there was a phone call. Steve Starr answers. “Yo.” “Hey Steve. You got a minute it’s Manny.” “What’s up man I was expecting to hear from you hours ago.” “Yeah sorry I had my hands tied. Those fucks at PRIME were giving me the run around. I’m starting to think they believe they made a mistake when they accepted you back.” “What do you mean?” “Well for starters, they suck at booking returns to the organization. They didn’t buy my idea that we should wait until after this upcoming PPV to get you a match.” “Manny you know I don’t care who I have to wrestle, I’m just ready to get back into active competition.” “I hear ya Steve but the fact of the matter is I’m your manager. I’m supposed to listen to what you want to do, but at the same time I probably understand what’s best for you. Especially now, you are just returning so we sort of want to ease you back into the swing of things.” “Ok enough spin Manny, what’s the deal?” “You got the opener on the upcoming PPV; Ultraviolence. I though it would be best for us to just skip that event, because you are just being thrown into some match with no build-up. The crowd is barely even gonna notice it’s you out there. “ “Bullshit, I’m Steve Starr. I know how to work a room ya idiot. And opening a PPV is never a bad thing, the following matches are going to have to somehow live-up to the bar that I’m going to set after the first match. I like what I’m hearing.” “I’m glad you do. My preference would be to get you back amongst the Intense Championship division. After all, you technically never lost that championship you were stripped due to a situation that was out of your hands.” “Who are my opponents?” “I definitely was no supporter of this decision; you’ve been thrown into some clusterfuck triple threat. It’s bad enough that the main-event is a triple threat and that is surely to garner most of the hype for this event, but then they have the nerve to throw you in as some fill-in against some other guys. Ever heard of Skylar Montgomery and Shawn Hart?” Skylar Montgomery was a nobody as far as Steve Starr was concerned. Shawn Hart on the other hand was a very interesting opponent. He was of a similar build to Steve Starr. Their styles were pretty comparable, both had high flyer instincts with a bit of technical savvy added in. Their histories differed completely. While Shawn Hart was new to PRIME, he has established himself as a force in other organizations. He has an extensive title history, going strong for the last decade plus. Steve Starr on the other hand has one championship credit to his name, and he never even defended that. Honestly, Steve Starr is sort of like Lane Kiffin in the college football world. He has a tremendous amount of hype, but he really has done nothing to show for it. Shawn Hart has pride himself on being a champion. Steve Starr’s wrestling career revolved around making money. Had Steve Starr actually applied himself to an actual organization, instead of his ill-fated attempt as an independent traveling wrestling sideshow, he’d probably have a lavish trophy case as well. But he probably wouldn’t have a sandwich named after him in Slovenia. “Manny I don’t really see what the big deal is. I get to make my return at a PPV. I get to work off whatever rust I may have against a couple of non-factors as far as THIS organization is concerned. I couldn’t have come up with a better scenario.” “Well Steve I’m glad you feel that way.” See in the past Steve Starr did everything to the max. Had this been 4 or 5 years ago, he probably would have stormed into the PRIME front office, demanded a title shot or some or high profile storyline, all while requesting his own private jet to and from each arena. He really was that bad. Now? Steve Starr seems to understand his place. He has not wrestled once in the past 20 or so months. While he has maintained decent shape playing in little recreational type sports leagues, he’s probably not in professional wrestling shape. You know, the kind that requires you compete at an insane level in front of thousands of people in basically your boxers? Steve Starr has not done that in awhile You do not exactly get into professional wrestling shape schooling has-beens in a 25 and over softball league for the park district. You get into professional wrestling shape by being a professional wrestler. End Scene Steve Starr was the talk of the town, and for good reason. He was about to announce his decision to return to professional wrestling, signing a contract with the last promotion that hired him on a salaried basis; PRIME. And in typical Steve Starr fashion, he goes above and beyond. He does not call a reporter and ask him to come over for a beer and do a little interview. That would not be enough for Steve. “I’m in Miami BITCH!” Steve shouts, a popular slogan from some song by some band. Honestly when you’re in Miami, or the “Yayo” as Steve affectionately refers to the city as, the beautiful surroundings take over your soul. Lil Wayne may have said it best; “Palm trees, beaches and short sleeves, fly ya bitch down now she don’t wanna leave.” The truth was fly anyone down here and you would not wanna leave. “Just like old times huh bro” Steve’s longtime manager/assistant/confidante/friend Manny Rivera asks. The two of them have been making the annual pilmilgrage to Miami for years now. First it began in 2002, then again every year from 04-06, and in 08. Steve missed the 09 trip after battling a case of pneumonia that saw him lose 15 pounds in a 10 day span and eventually spending a night in the hospital hooked up to an IV. “There’s something special about this, I cannot really put my finger on it. Miami is a crazy city if you come and visit on a regular weekend, but this one is different.” There was no denying Steve’s statement. Ask anyone who actually lives in Miami and they can tell you how the nightlife can leave you broke and sleepless. But it can also can you laid and drunk. So many beautiful women, you have your pick of the litter if you are confident enough. Or rich enough. But on this particular weekend, Memorial Day Weekend 2010 Miami is littered with people. In an annual event that has come to be known as “Black Beach Week” hundreds of thousands of tourists and citizens litter the streets. 3 of South Beach’s premiere avenues, Washington, Collins, and Ocean collectively known as “The Strip” are taken over by a mass amount of consumers. The city experiences an enormous economic boom during this weekend, with prices jacked up considerably for every establishment that profits are reaped by everyone. It’s a rare win-win situation that normally never happens. Usually when you get that many people in one area, consuming alcohol, using drugs, displaying their sexual interests’ trouble is bound to happen. The police do a relatively good job of maintaining the order. They are faced with an enormous task because how are you supposed to contain 300,000 individuals? Traffic is also a bitch so do not expect to see the cops in their vehicles. They will roll up on you on bikes in a pack about 15 deep. Cops on steroids are the best. Steve Starr was probably going to be in Miami this weekend regardless of whether or not PRIME allowed him to return. When you have parties being thrown by rap superstars such as Young Money Entertainment, P. Diddy, Young Jeezy, Jadakiss, the list could go on forever. And let’s not forget about the other superstars. Walking up the strip you might look across the street and see Chris Brown being mobbed by thousands of horny groupies. Turn around you might see the sickest Lamborghini of your life. The overall atmosphere is just incredible. Announcing the return to PRIME is something that has to be done efficiently. Or should I say done in extravagance. Either way Steve Starr was sure to both in this city. “I hope you don’t plan on getting any sleep, Manolo, we are going to run this city. I’m doing all the stuff that I normally forget to do while I’m here. You know like getting on the jet-skis, I’m definitely gonna rent a motorcycle because driving a car sucks here.” “I just hope we don’t run into any trouble here.” “Damn Manny stop acting like a puss. It’s not like I’m some famous rapper or something, I ain’t gotta worry about people noticing me. I’ve been off the scene for awhile, and plus when I throw the slick shades on no one is gonna notice me.” Miami is a very interesting city. On one hand you have the very beautiful South Beach, surrounded by the Atlantic Ocean. If you’re into that type of stuff, watching the sunset on the beach is one of the more peaceful images ever. Not in a romantic type of way or anything like that, but just being out all night and then seeing that sun rise by the water. It is crazy. “Well if we are not gonna be protected by security, I am at least glad that you’re not going to be wearing your watch, chain, earrings etc. That type of flashy shit pisses me off and it draws attention from these thirsty Dade County thugs.” Although Steve hated to hear Manny talk and act like a bitch, he was telling the truth. For those who may not be familiar with Miami, it is home to some very rough and intimidating individuals. Earlier today, Steve and Manny saw this light-skinned dread-head kid with a white t-shirt that read on the front “Welcome to Dade County.” Cute right? Until you see the back and it reads “Home of The First 48.” You know, the Police TV show that revolves around getting leads during the first 48 hours of a murder investigation. These guys out here are hardened criminals. They got a lot of guns, and they are shooting to kill. Steve Starr rarely ever worried about these type of things. “Look I’m not saying that I’m tough but I could care less about that type of shit. I’m from Killer City, Kansas City, I been around this type of shit my entire life.” True while Kansas City probably pales in comparison to the level of violence found in Dade County, it is still a pretty rough city in some parts. There’s still a lot of old-school organized type of crime in Killer City. There’s more of a hierarchy similar to the Mafia. That’s not what you’ll see in Dade County though. These kids just don’t give a fuck. Steve Starr was only going to be here for a couple of nights. Not like the old days where he would fly in Wednesday Morning, and stay until Tuesday. Not this time. Just a trip to do a bit of promoting, have a little fun with old friends, and then it’s back to business. It’s finally time for Steve Starr to return to work. End Scene Steve Starr was stripped of the Intense Championship one week exactly after the day that he won it. He competed in a back and forth hard fought competition against The First, a bout so vicious that he suffered 3 broken ribs during the contest. The injury was so severe it made routine habits such as breathing and walking a chore. Conscious of his condition, Steve Starr thought it would be best to rest as opposed to risking further injury. If you asked him back then what the reasons were as to his freak injuries, Steve would answer bad luck. It’s possible that luck had a lot to do with Steve receiving these nasty injuries, and one can also say that Steve did a horrible job of preparing for matches. A diet? Steve Starr would chow down on McDonalds and Burger King minutes before a match. Getting enough rest the night before the match? Not Steve Starr, who was often found partying into the wee hours of the morning, stumbling into his hotel room a druken mess, sleeping until 5 PM and then sauntering into the arena just a mere minutes before his actual match began. While there was no denying the talent because Steve Starr normally put on a show once he made into the squared circle, wrestling purists always wondered “what if?” What if Steve Starr actually trained? What if Steve Starr cut out all the wild all night partying, and got some sleep before his big matches? What if Steve Starr actually scouted his opponents? Well folks, we are about to get our answer. Not only has Steve Starr learned the benefits of a rigorous training complete with a strict diet, he has also taken up the art of scouting his opponents. “Ya see, one thing I learned when I went on my tour, was to watch video on my upcoming opponents. Since I was usually hired for only a couple of nights per organization, there was little to no chance of gaining a familiarity with my opponents. I had to become a student of the game.” It’s true, one week Steve Starr was in Japan battling a behemoths such as Oko Yono, and The Destroyer. The next week he was in England taking on strongmen like LiVerPool and Mr. Smith. Not to mention he also did a fair amount of shows in Mexico, taking on Lucha-Libre sensations such as El Conquistador and Trey Hysterio III. “There was hardly any time to even meet my opponents, let alone watch an actual match of there’s in person. Each time an organization invited me in for a match, it was a requirement that they sent along enough hours of matches so that I could successfully learn everything there was to know about my opponents.” All that constant travel also left Steve Starr battling extreme bouts of jetlag. “I learned quickly the importance of getting a good night’s rest.” Steve Starr has spent a considerable amount of time working out. Not competing actively, he watched his weight balloon up to 235 lbs. He needed to drop 20 lbs to get back to his in-ring weight of 215. Steve Starr was about to find out if all of his work was worth it.
