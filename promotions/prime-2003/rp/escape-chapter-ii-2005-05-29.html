Escape - Chapter II

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-05 Escape.By Nick Brocken 'Gather ye rosebuds while ye may. Old time is still a flying. And the flower that smiles today, tomorrow will be dying.' Euthanasia. I could hear the serrated sound effects of the butter knife digging its way into my flesh. The gooish scum beneath my skin made it that much more difficult to cut it appropriately, without having to puncture my whole arm with one blow. I could feel an intense sting, like the sensation you get when you place a limb near fire. Not at the sear, but moreso at the singe. Closely eyeing the brutalization, I remove the knife and gaze toward the gaping slit. The thin, dense periosteum had been exposed, along with a film of maroon colored liquid that masked the bone. The small force of wind coming from the window to my right whistled an echo to the bone and sent a bone-chilling pain throughout my body. I collapsed. I could see colors and the stain of humans marked amongst my walls. I could see brilliant black shadows climbing my ceiling like mountaineers venturing up the Rockies. I felt my body tremor for a couple seconds as I shifted my attention towards my arm, which had become more and more lifeless as time past on. The pulse monitored at my wrist was beating at such a preposterous rate that I suddenly had to match my breathing with each undulation. I could hear the off-the-hook alarming 'BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP'-ing of the phone that I kicked when I was on my first layer of skin. Relapsing in a series of conceptual daydreams, I thought of the individuals who scorned upon 'cutting'. Eventhough, this was my first experience of cutting and I certainly hadn't planned on living through it, it could definitely be termed as 'cutting'. I could remember several case reports on television discussing this violent 'stress-reliever' and I would jump into the march and echo what they were all against. I sat in the hot seat and visualized my conclusion. I had no right to echo, for I succumbed to it, believing that death would've came by now. It's been an hour and twenty minutes. Blankets, sheets, rugs, wood floors, the night table; covered in blood. I felt drowsy and certainly incomplete, yet, I was one-hundred percent sure that I wasn't dying, or even close for that matter. 'Fuck it. Just, fuck it all!' I maliciously screamed to myself, smacking the side of my head a couple of times in the process. For this wasn't how it was supposed to end. I came to the encore of my illnesses and fought them off in a series of pivotal battles. The illnesses were gone and now everything is slowly being re-constructed. I'm a walking bowl of euphoric art that people choose not to recognize. I've forced my girlfriend to endure the bullshit I splash at her and enjoy it, if only because I threaten to end it all if she were to leave me. I'm Mr. Wonderful at his finest hour, where he rips off the skin that perfects him and morphs it into contagion, sickness and dirge. Two knocks struck the door to my hotel room, but I ignored both as if they never happened. My head bellowed below my knees as a faucet-drip of saliva oozed down my bottom lip, to my chin and to it's inevitable homeward bound; the floor. Three more knocks hit the door as I tried my best to scurry them away. 'I had a late night. Come back later...' explained Brocken as he grabbed the white sheet that covered his mattress and wrapped it around his arm. At this point, he just didn't want to bleed any more. If it wasn't his time to die, he wasn't going to die. He didn't want to waste all the blood for no reason. His eyes darted toward the doorknob which was being twisted to the right and left. It was locked and even if they gained entrance that way, the chain was on the wall as well. Suddenly the door opened and the stream of sunshine that hit me felt like a series of cannon balls bouncing off my sternum. Well, I thought I put the locks on. 'Jesus Christ, Nicholas. What the hell happened?' hollered Nick's uncle who covered his mouth with his hand and noticed the blood seeping from Nick's arm. Half naked and sedated from the blood loss, Nicholas looked dead and was nearing the actual thing. The uncle, Roger Moss, quickly walked over to Nick and grabbed one of his pillows, pulling the pillow-case off of it and wrapping it around Brocken's wounded arm. Roger, who was rampently repeating 'OH GOD' out loud, tried lifted Nick's head up in an attempt to wake him out of his nearly unconscience state. Mumbling meaningless words, Nicholas struggled to stay awake as Roger pulled his cell phone out from his back pocket. 'H..ho...hospital...' Nick muttered as more drool slid down his mouth and onto his bare chest. Roger walked outside of the hotel and address the worried crowd on the bottom level that nothing serious had happened. Of course, it was definitely serious and dangerously close to a tragic end. He reached a 911 dispatcher and quickly told the female officer the situation. 'My nephew apparently tried to kill himself by cutting his wrists, and he's bleeding really bad ... Yes, well I would if I had a car.. I took a taxi over to his hotel... an ambulence? Good, an ambulence is good. We're at room 15 at the Farmingdale Inn just off of Route 26... Okay? Okay.' Roger sighed as he shut his cell phone off and put it back in his pocket. Nicholas lifted his head up slowly and noticed the frightened uncle staring at him. 'Hey Roger..' Nick said as his eyes slowly shut and quickly opened back up. '...wake me up when we get there.' gasped Nick as his body lifelessly dropped to the floor in a heap of bloody linens and things. The Infirmary. Six long hours later... 'I feel certain that I'm going mad again. Voices from the underground told me to follow them, thus, I'm killing myself in the act of obeying them. Farewell...' My eyes opened and scanned the room like infared sensors. I could smell latex and peroxide and saw three middle-aged men in lab coats. What a familiar FUCKING scenario. Did they lock me up again in some wretched mental hospital? I intended on finding out as soon as possible as I couldn't get up because of the IV's that were stabbed into my veins. It's unbelievable how quick life can end -- It sort of exemplifies nicely in the wake of my friend, my doctor, Jack Klingman's death. Though it was a legitimate car accident, it all still had a sketchy glaze to it. I wanted more answers than were given because I knew there were more. I wanted to know what exactly killed him in the accident because it wasn't like Klingman didn't have hitmen hunting him down for protecting me. I suddenly become no longer concerned in that matter as I propel my way into a blur of thoughts about PRIME. The pain post-match was incredible. My back felt like it was tied in a knot, and I felt like I stepped on Mars without a helmet. In my first match back, I felt alive, but, felt more pain than ever in that ring. Exhilerent waves of wonder stormed my head as I heard the bell ring and the announcer declare me the winner. What a brilliant way to re-invent myself and it was just beginning. This, being the first and foremost incident that's turned everything upside down. This uncomfortable bed I lay upon, the blinding lights that gaze at me from above, the mindless nit wit doctors that do nothing but heavily medicate the patients. It's all bottled up corruption, disguised by the government and made out to trick people into believing that it's better that we're medicated rather than feel natural pain. Then again, maybe that's why I'm in this sport? Maybe I've figured it all out long beforehand and never realized. Medication comes and goes. Natural pain? It stays within us and reminds us that we are still alive, still breathing, still kicking. I notice two nurses entering my room. One seems to be carrying a needle and the other's hands are protected with purple gloves. They both reminded me of Lucille Ball; dressed down in white, skimpy dresses with red hair. Freckles autograph who seemed to be the assistant nurse. In front of them lie a cloudy mist of oxygen. I could hear my lungs collapsing as I exhaled. 'This'll only pinch, Mr. Brocken.' the registered nurse stated as she started to stick me with the needle. Suddenly, a spark of adrenaline brought me to life, forcing me to sit upright and wig out. 'What the fuck are you putting in my arm? Get away from me! Where the hell am I?!' I screamed, pushing the two nurses away who giggled for a moment, placing their hands in front of their mouths as they fluttered their eye lashes towards me. 'Now, now, Nick. Allow us to help you. You don't want us to hurt you, do you?' the assistant nurse said, followed by a hissing laugh. In just a moments time the medication powerfully knocks me down and shoots through my veins. I can sense something wrong as I can feel my body get sucked through a black tunnel at hundreds of miles an hour. I can see my alarm clock ticking at rapid speed. My dog, sitting beside my entertainment center, chewing a dog toy and staring towards me. What the fuck was going on? Why was I going through this soap opera again? Suddenly, everything quickly changes and I'm on an abandoned street in an abandoned town. It's dark as hell out and cold to boot. I can see a washed out moon through a line of fog that had descended to just above my head. I can see the large, NY TIMES clock that I had seen when I was younger and it's on fire. People are suddenly running as a wisk of heat hits my neck. I turn around and noticed an avalanche of fire coming my way. I jump to my left and escape the wave of fire. As I roll along the sidewalk I notice my girlfriend being escorted into a vehicle. Three men, around my age, have guns pointed toward her back. Right there, I figured out the level of scum bags these guys were. Not one gun to the back, but three just to define their grimey selves. I tried to scream but only air came out. The root of all evil had grown like a fungus upon this unknown city and this premonition had slowly grown to much more of a reality than I had ever expected. Premonitions. 'Dreams are the obligatory red curtains of preparation for what's to come in the future. I am prepared..' 'Nothing is getting through to me. Feedback builds up like cateracts around my eyes as I focus in on my girlfriend being escorted into the car. These familiar men were the culprits behind the disapearences of my mother and father. They're going to take my girlfriend away now? Over my dead, cold body. I'll risk my own life to safe hers. Was this all a part of my fucked up mind or could it have possibly been some kind of premonition to save the collapse of time within another individual, another loved one. I just won't allow another tower crumble in my life.' Static shock. Nicholas' eyes sporadically opened and closed as the nurses looked down; helpless and without a clue as to what to do. His hands, shaking and gripping onto the bed sheets as his mind is violently driven into a massive lucid slumber. 'I won't let her GO!' Nicholas screamed as he lifted his arm up, tearing one of the IV's out. 'Everything has been continuously rocky and crucial. I've never had a breather in my life and I don't intend on ever getting one. For I live in the fast motion and fast lanes life and that's why I will be victorious once again come ReVolution. I want this more than any of you motherfuckers and that's the truth. Nova, I don't want to hear your shit about 'wanting' this title -- You've experience the wonders of holding a title. You've experience the extravagence of being a household name ... I HAVEN'T. So, before you go on and on about how you deserve this title or have earned it, think about me for a second. I was a struggling, schizophrenic clown without a purpose of direction. Losing here and there but never truly giving up until I had enough of the illness. Not a single title or deserving, significant award. Do not stand up to me with the impression that you're the man to beat in this match because you'd be lying straight through your fucking teeth. I owe this victory to the ones who stood up for me. I owe this victory to Killean Sirrajin. I owe this victory to Emilio Rage, and even Nova in some aspects. That joint we smoked will forever burn in my heart as the beginning to a friendship. But, at ReVolution, it's all business and whatever friendship we had shall be put aside.' I felt a rush of emotion jolt into my body as I stared directly towards the wall directly twenty-feet or so in front of me. I felt like a stone statue, reaching my arms out to try and move myself, but to no avail. I reached to pull the remaining IVs out of my skin, but noticed that my fingers were slowly contorting into a mound of flesh. My fingers were disapearing and the energy was just minutes away from depleting. The nurses ran to get help from doctors and security. Nicholas' face grew beat red as he collapsed back against the mattress. I'm no longer the head-hanging direlect that I seemed to be locked inside of. I refuse to down myself any longer and succumb to those who word out jumbled predictions on who's the better man in the ring. I'll tell you what, I may not be the best in that ring, but I am the one with the most heart, the most soul and I want this victory so fucking much. I can feel that warmth, euphoric sense of anticipation in my stomach, fluttering like butterflies trapped amongst the contorted twirl of my intestines. I may be edgy and bruised by dementia, but isn't it about time I use it all to my advantage? Isn't it time for ME to shine in the spotlight instead of some over-rated, sob-story called Aimz? Isn't it time for ME to grow amongst my peers on the roster and blossom into something that Tyler Lopez and Trashcan Man were never meant to be? I want to be the guy holding the gun in front of someone's face instead of being the guy on the floor in a panic, screaming for '911'. I want to be the guy struggling to climb the rope through the mist of camera flashes going off in my face. It's taken me SO GOD DAMN LONG to be the loaded weapon and this time .. this time.. I'm ready to fire it... In the midst she was standing. Darkness through me into a dress of obscurity, as the white blanket covered my quivering body. My dreams had gradually become dangerously real, and to the point where I'm convinced that they're. It's calm and desolate and I'm surprised to still be breathing. This isn't a hospital. It's a prison made out of steel and whoever ran this place didn't intend on anyone leaving. If this was, indeed, an average hospital, then the medication they were giving me didn't sit well. A cool breeze hit my face as I notced both large windows to the my right were open wide. I quickly pondered out dramatic plans before noticing a classic spin-dial phone. '...Gwen.. I should just check in and see if she's all right.' Of course, I knew it was in the middle of the night but, like I said, these dreams were that powerful. I closed my eyes and grabbed the receiver as I dialed her home phone number. Ring.. Ring.. Ring.. Ring.. Ring.. ..Ring..? Six rings without a pick up at two in the morning? Not like Gwen at all. Not like her at fucking all. I notice the IV's were still stuck in me. Without a care in the world, I pulled them out one by one. A singe of pain thrushed throughout my body as each one 'plopped' out. I can feel my body breaking. I can feel my body breaking. I'm ready, to let it all go. I can feel my body shaking. Right down to the foundation... The root of it all... These weren't dreams, but moreso premonitions. Gwen was in trouble and it's all that mattered at this point. With one quick inhale I leap out of my bed and head for the open windows. If there was any kind of strength that helped me face another day, it was her. Losing her is all it would take. I looked down and saw the rain splashing against the pavement below. My eyes stared at my dying soul, which looked like a wounded angel with its hands placed near its heart. My mind was being strangled in thought as I stretched one leg outside and grasped onto the angel. I'm coming Gwen... I'm coming...
