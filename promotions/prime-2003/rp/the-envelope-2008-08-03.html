The Envelope

3 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/3/08 “So, what did you feel right before you got your revenge against Anton Dufresne?” “A little nervous, and I think I had a cramp in my leg that I had to rub out…” “Seriously. I mean the actual feeling associated with taking someone who caused you and your loved ones so much pain, and making them feel the same pain. The feeling that you got when you finally pushed back after being struck down time after time. I just want to know what it felt like to be the last man standing.” “That? Oh, yeah, that felt right. That felt like destiny. That felt like home.” ---- Jonathan Rhine has been running his last conversation with his trainer Foster Nackedy over and over again in his mind. This is partly because he is using it as a motivator to give him the strength required to exact his revenge on Xavier Kannon on PRIME’s biggest stage. However, it is mostly because there is no one else for him to actually talk to. In the past 24 hours, Jonathan has gotten into a fight with said trainer over Foster’s breakup, cannot get in touch with any of his stablemates to give him support or advice, and scared away his own girlfriend because she saw him sitting with another girl. So for the past twelve hours, Jonathan has lifted weights, watched tape of Xavier Kannon’s recent victories, and gone over his conversation with Foster over and over again. Not because it will help him win, not because it’s something he truly wants to do, but because Jonathan Rhine, a man who has thrived on having a chain of support that helped him through his entire career, is all alone for the first time in his life. And that scares the shit out of him. ---- “I understand what you’re trying to say, but you’re cheating me.” “What do you mean, Jon?” “I mean that ‘feels like home’ is a nice little cliché that conveys a certain idea, but it doesn’t really tell me anything. I want to know how you feel the minutes, days, weeks leading up to the moment. I want to know every sensation, every mood swing, every thought that goes through your mind. This is one of the most important things I’ve ever had to do, Foster, and I’m not going into it unprepared.” “Fine. Uh, let’s see if I can do this; it’s been like five years. How do you feel right now?” “Angry. Focused.” “But you’re not. You have no idea what focused is. And the closer the day gets, the more you’re going to find yourself getting distracted. Not because your life suddenly gets dramatic right before a pay-per-view matchup, but because you’re letting yourself think of other things. Because the passion and rage you’re feeling is so strong now, it scares you. So you pray for anything to happen, anything to take your mind off the madness.” ---- Jonathan is in the middle of his 452nd sit-up when he hears the front door open. There is no knock; just a small click and the prolonged creaking of a slowly-opened door. He sits up and smiles, because the fact that he hasn’t seen his girlfriend in two days is something that has done more than merely distress him. Grabbing a towel and beginning to wipe himself off, he plays out how he will approach this conversation. “I know what it looked like, and I understand you have trust issues because I cheated before. That’s totally my fault. But I swear, baby, I never touched Shweta. She’s just a friend.” “I know, baby, I’m sorry for suspecting. I’m just really nervous now after everything that’s happened. I’ll try to be more understanding.” Jonathan smiles thinking of the hypothetical result, knowing that all he has to do is acknowledge that Katie has a right to her suspicion and she will forgive him. If the conversation doesn’t go exactly in that fashion, it will share many of the same words at the very least. But the conversation is not like that at all, mostly because the person who entered Jonathan Rhine’s home isn’t his girlfriend Katie Malick. “Hello, Jonathan.” It is the woman who tried to tear him apart from her in the first place. “Desade…what are you doing here?” ---- “And when that distraction does come, that’s when the human brain really starts to fuck you over. Because at first you’re thinking it’s a good thing; you’ve got something to let your focus rest for awhile, and you can test yourself by dealing with whatever unexpected thing has come your way.” “But it’s not a good thing?” “It’s only a good thing if you can deal with it before your big moment comes. Because a distraction leading up to your moment is one thing. A distraction that is still unresolved at the time of your moment? Yeah, that’s why they’re called distractions.” ---- “I was merely coming to offer a proposal.” The Director of Sin City Championship Wrestling stands nearly five feet from the former SCCW Universal Champion, her hands at her side and her face completely detached of human emotion. Jonathan, however, is showing plenty of emotion in his sneer as he says, “I know your proposals, Desade. I don’t play your dirty games.” “But you do play dirty games with others, it seems. Xavier Kannon brought the beast out in you when he took your eye. If I had known how easy it was to make you lose your angel façade, I would have taken a limb a long time ago.” “What do you want?” Jonathan sighs. There is no lead-up to the proposal, no further sidestepping of her demands. “I want you to lose to Xavier Kannon.” Jonathan tilts his head to the side, unable to stop himself from pantomiming a man who lacks hearing. “I’m sorry?” “You will lose to Xavier Kannon.” Desade finally moves, and it is to cross the room to the sofa that Jonathan and Shweta had shared two days ago when Katie found them together. “You will give an undoubtedly genuine effort, and you might even get a standing ovation from the audience of PRIME for your near-accomplishment. But you will fall to the ground, and you will lie there for ten seconds.” “Fuck no,” Jonathan immediately says, turning around. After a moment, he shoots a lock over his shoulder. “Why?” “Because you have two big matches in the coming weeks, and although I would be more inclined to have you lose to Amy and Lance in the Universal Title match, I know that your failure to become a three-time champion will not shake you nearly as much as knowing you spent months trying to exact revenge on someone who has cut you deeper than anyone has ever done…and failed. I know that in addition to this, if you not only failed to defeat Kannon but were faced with the reality that you stopped yourself from it, the anguish would be considerably greater.” Jonathan turns around and steps towards the Spider in the Web, crouching to her eye level. “I’m coming down here so you can clearly see me do this,” – he shakes his head slowly, deliberately – “and hear me say, again, FUCK NO. Desade, I am and have always been the maker of my own destiny. If I lose to Xavier Kannon, it will not be because you told me to do so. You are not Marcellus Wallace, and I am not Butch. I don’t feel the sting of pride fucking with me, I feel the sting of annoyance after listening to your strongarm bullshit tactics over and over again. You do not write the ending of this chapter, Alexandra Pierce – I do. Thank you, and kindly exit my house, now.” After a few seconds of silence, the woman known as Desade nods curtly, then stands. “Very well, Jonathan. I will see you at All In.” As she heads for the door, she takes with her the rest of Jon’s confidence. Watching her go, he shakes his head quickly and tries to sound tougher than he feels. “That’s it? What about the envelope?” Desade feigns confusion. “The envelope?” “Yeah. The catch. The ultimatum. Last time you proposed that I hurt my own teammate, you had an envelope which was full of pictures from my affair. You said that if I didn’t do what you wanted, you’d show them to Katie. You don’t offer me something and then not have a punishment in place when I say no. That’s not how you operate.” Desade draws her hand up from the couch and begins to examine her hand lazily. “I’m sorry if I am not adequately playing my part in your opinion, Jonathan. I am not telling you what I will do in response for a simple reason.” She begins to move her fingers slowly, and Jonathan notices that between them is a long piece of black hair. His eyes flare for only a second, but Desade sees it and smiles. “If you knew what I was doing, you’d be more inclined to stop me from carrying it out.” Jonathan can’t keep himself from shouting. “What did you do with Shweta?” “Goodbye, Jonathan.” ---- “When I was getting ready to face Anton and get him back for the pain he caused me, me and my wife had a falling out. You see, an old girlfriend of mine came back and started talking to me again, and during sex with Magen I called her by my ex’s name.” “You’re a moron.” “Don’t I know it. Anyway, I was distracted from the impending match with Anton because I had to deal with jealousy and my own strange feelings towards Jasmine, my ex. So that all happened maybe three or four days before the Pay-Per-View.” “Well, what did you do?” “I fucking paralyzed Anton Dufresne, same as you’re gonna do to Kannon.” ---- “SHWETA!” Jonathan yells as he bounds through her already-open door, his fists at the ready. He starts turning, hands up, ready to take on anything that comes his way… Except a stool, however. Jonathan Rhine collapses on the ground. He starts to get to his feet but Roderick Ashe, one of Desade’s Dead Man’s Hand, drops to a knee and punches him in the mouth, knocking him back down. Jonathan can hear Shweta scream from the other room, but he can’t do anything but remain pinned under Ashe’s knee. “Boss tol’ me y’weren’t gonna have a clue. Why ya haveta be the hero?” Ashe punches Jonathan again, then smiles as he blows cigar smoke in his face. “Ain’t fer carin’, got no time limit for the Injun, an’ you gotta wrassle.” “Don’t…you…dare…” Jonathan breathes. “Oh, I won’t, Not now at least. Since I ain’t fer havin’ y’think you got one over on me...” Roderick Ashe removes his cigar from his mouth and pushes the lit end to Jon’s forehead, smiling as The New Life screams in pain. “Nighty night, Rhine.” The big man of Desade’s group stands up and exits the door. Jonathan is able to roll over, still growling from the burn. “Jonathan? Jonathan are you okay?” Shweta calls from the bedroom. Jonathan attempts to get to his feet and finally does, then stumbles to Shweta’s bedroom. “Oh my God, Jon!” “I’ll be fine. I’m just happy he didn’t do anything to you.” “Me too.” Shweta shudders. “He was talking about some terrible things, and I am just so glad…thanks for coming for me, Jon.” Jonathan nods, then hears an engine start in the driveway. “He’s still here. I’m not letting him leave.” As Jonathan runs outside to stop Roderick Ashe from reporting to Desade, he sees a small Toyota parked across the street. He stares at it for a second before realizing whose car it is. “Katie? Katie, wait, no, this isn’t what it…” But she never hears the rest of the clichéd phrase because she peels off in her car. Jonathan sighs and turns around, turning right into Shweta. “What happened?” “Katie saw me here. She thinks I’m cheating again.” Swheta sighs as well. “I’m sorry. This week has been really tough for you. Come on inside, let me make you some soup.” So for the second time in three days, Jonathan Rhine is caught in a shady-looking position. For the second time in three days, he can only turn to a woman he barely knows. And for the second time in two days, he willingly eats food she prepares and doesn’t taste the drug she slips him.
