The Return (5-Star Title Match)

8 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08-08-05 You've come back after a long time away, from the sport that you loved with more passion than really anything else in your life, and you've been thrust into a title match with one of the best that has ever laced up some boots for your return. To say that you're going in with a lot less confidence than you normally would is an understatement. Normally a match like this would have you watering at you're mouth for a shot at one of the sports best. A man who is known world wide as one of the toughest and most feared mat technicans in the entire game. You would normally feel right at home, on the mat, with a man like this. Going move for move, hold for hold, thought for thought, with a guy of this caliber. It is the stuff that dreams are made of and you couldn't possibly think of anything better. That is how you'd normally feel about it. Right now you feel over whelmed, some what reluctant, and a bit older than you really are. It's been a while since you stepped foot in a PRIME ring for an actual match. It's not that you're in terrible shape or you've been sitting at home doing nothing and getting fat while you've been away. You've stayed in shape, worked out in your personal ring, in your personal gym, to perfect your craft that much more. Problem is that there is only so much improving one can do against local boys who just want to get in a ring and learn as much as they can. It's kept you in shape but not in ring shape and certainly not in ring war shape. Brandon Youngblood is a warrior and he is relentless. You know that and so does the rest of the world so there is nothing less desirable then having to step in the ring with a man of his caliber for your PRIME return debut. Title or no title this isn't exactly the way you had invisioned having to try and work off some of that ring rust. Still, ring rust or not, you've got no choice at this point. You are going to have to step foot in the squared circle and face a man who is possibly the top talent in PRIME today. He might not have the fancy records, the top title, or the press clippings to prove it, but frankly who cares about that? No matter who the man has faced in his PRIME, or his wrestling career period for that matter, he has come with the same relentless and aggressive style for each and every one. He beat Karina when it was believed that he had no shot in hell of winning. He took Angelo Deville, one of the best of all time by all rights, to the absolute limit of his game when no one gave him a real shot to do so. He best Nova a man whose star has been steadily on the rise since joining PRIME for the very title that he now puts on the line against you. A man who hasn't wrestled a match in two years, hasn't locked The Cross Effect on a legit contender in two years, and hasn't even seriously thought about wrestling for more than a few months now. Enrique Morales, through some quick thinking on your part, got you back into the game that you love and have been absent from. He is your manager, albeit not of his own will, and you've come to expect that he'll do the best he can for you cause it's his job. Even if he doesn't particularly like it. Yet some how he managed to allow Hin See to out smart him and book you in a match with a man that you know Hin See is just hoping will destroy you. You can see it now, that look of pure joy, when Brandon beats you into the mat and forces you to submit. You can hear that familiar chuckle, or cackle if you prefer, as Hin See slaps his knee at the thought of you being humbled in your first match up back. Your hat has to go off to the old timer cause he made a brilliant move. A move that could easily rid him of you for the rest of his life. Who is to say that in such an important match with such an important opponent that you won't get a leg broke, have an arm shattered, or have some muscles torn to shreds as Youngblood looks to pound you into oblivion. You can tell by the booking that is what Hin See is hoping cause Lord knows you weren't given this shot cause he wanted to see you win and have one of his titles. The first time around in PRIME, despite being undefeated and one of his best and brightest stars, he did everything he could to keep the titles away from you. Never giving you even one shot to be so much as one half of the tag team champions. Now, when you're returning after so long, he wants to give you a shot at the 5-Star title but with a hefty price tag on it. To win you've got to best a man who quite honestly might just be the better wrestler even if you were on you're best day. You can never tell cause the sport of wrestling is a funny thing and anyone can get a pin fall on any given night. Look no further than Zyfloi pinning Rock Startling for a perfect example of this fact and you'll know that no matter what, ring rust and all, you've got a shot at winning. You could literaly walk into ReVolution this week as just some thorn in the side of Hin See, just returning and being punished for doing so, and still walk out with gold around your waiste and one of the biggest moments of your life. How great would it be for you, and that ego of yours, if you beat Youngblood and took the title that he worked so hard to get, in your first match, when Hin See booked it just knowning you'd be smothered and destroyed? The pain and suffering that you'll no doubt be feeling during this match would be well worth it if you put one over on the old man and took the title that he dangled in front of you, taunting you with it, with the knowledge, in his mind, that there would be no way you'd beat Youngblood for it after such a long lay off. Thing is, as you already know, wrestling is a strange sport and strange things have been know to happen. Could this be the upset that PRIME hasn't seen since Zyfloi shocked the world and took the title from Rock Startling? Probably, even given how great Youngblood is, be on par with that type of shock but it would certainly turn a lot of heads toward taking notice of you. It would turn a couple of faces green in illness as well and that is worth even more. 'You've got a shot at a damn title belt so you should be more than happy with me gringo. This is one more title shot than you ever got without me at your side.' Enrique Morales says to you with a gleam in his eye and a hint of arrogance in his smile. You know damn well that given the nature of the relationship between the two of you that he is more than likely just as tickled as Hin See at the thought of you being locked up into some sort of human pretzel hold. 'This might be true, and even worthy of praise, if I was ready for a match of this caliber like I was before. Now I've got to prepare in one weeks time to face a man who is in the best shape of anyone in the business. I would say that you must be attempting to make me look bad but I think that is just a tad bit over stating the obvious.' You respond with a slight grin on your face. Despite the odds being against you there is nothing you like more than proving people wrong, even when the odds are seriously not in your favor. 'Why Senor Cross you make it sound as if I'd intentionally hurt an investment of mine. Whether I like the fact that I've been swindled into this deal or not you're still now a person under my name and there for a reflection of my management duties. The last thing I need is someone making me look bad so I haven't got time to intentionally put you in bad situations. This one is, unfortunetly, all of Hin See's mind and creation.' He responds with a bit of an arrogant grin. You can tell he wants you to win, not lose, but he won't mind seeing you go through a bit of pain to get to that point. 'Guess I'll just have to live up to my billing as the only great technical wrestler in this business. The name isn't all that flashy, and to be honest I don't even like it, but I didn't give myself that name, my peers did. Unlike some people who run around this world calling themselves Diamonds and technical marvels, and all that great jazz. Personal hype seems to be a huge part of this sport, and of course I can do that with the best of them, but I don't mind allowing what I can do in the ring speak for itself. We'll let this week be just that type of situation where my actions speak for themselves.' You respond with just a hint of your old cocky attitude starting to come back. You know that in a month or so that attitude will be back in full force but for now you've got more important things to do then talk about how great you're going to be. 'You shouldn't be so damn cocky essa. This isn't the PRIME you once were king shit in.' The voice of Baltasar booms into the room and announces the arrival of the PRIME tag team champs as Angelus follows him in. Baltasar and Angelus both look you up and down and just grin, wide, and Baltasar runs his right hand over his chin. 'See I don't know what all the hype was in the first place gringo. I dont think you could last five minutes in a technical match with me much less a technical wizard like Youngblood. Hell I'd be willing to beat a thousand dollars and a case of beer that you make us look back and lose your return match this week.' He responds once more with a huge grin. 'I'd be willing to up that bet and put a thousand and another case of beer in on it myself.' Angelus chimes in as he slaps Baltasar on the shoulder and just stares at you. The hatred coming from these two men could be detected even by someone who had never met any of you before in their lives. They just stare through you as if setting their gaze into a ray that is burning the life right out of you. Instead of being angry you simply grin. 'A bet you say? Well in that case who am I to ever turn down the chance to gamble. I'll see your bet and raise you an extra thousand and another case of beer just cause I don't like being out done. You'll learn, much like many others this week, that even at less than my best I'm not someone to bet against.' You respond with that trademark arrogant grin you're known for spreading wide across your face. 'I can't wait to see you fall flat on your face.' Angelus responds coldly as he simply takes a step toward you only to have Baltasar put a hand on his shoulder and shake his head no. 'Angelus that is no way to treat a new team mate. We should be encouraging Cross to go out there and break a leg and all that great jazz. I mean after all he represents us now, and just as importantly, The Imperium. We don't need another person running around here losing all the big time matches they are in.' Baltasar responds with a gleam in his eye and back hand to your chest. It wasn't a full blown one, or he'd have knocked you on your ass for sure, but it was enough to get your attention and remind you that he is the boss. At least that he believes he is anyway. 'If you only knew how little I like being pushed...' You start to say as you ball up one fist. You see a gleam flash in the eyes of both members of Los Diablos that tells you there are just dying for you to make you're move. Instead you take a deep breath and let it slide for the moment. 'Instead of being a jug head, however, I'll simply go out here and win the 5-Star title in my first match back. You'll need to pay attention cause this week is a clinic in how to win a title in your first shot, not your fourth or fifth.' 'We'll see gringo. Some how I got a feeling that the deck will be stacked against that type of return for you.' Angelus responds with anger at your remark. You simply chuckle at his anger and turn to walk away. 'The deck has always been stacked against me 'gringo' so this week shouldn't be any different. If that remark meant one of you big Mexican ingrates is going to try and screw me out of my match then there will be hell to pay...for all of you.' You respond as you simply walk out of the room and close the door behind you.
