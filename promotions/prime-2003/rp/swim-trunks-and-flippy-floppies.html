Swim Trunks and Flippy-Floppies

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReV 187 -February 13, 2009 “Gods damn it’s fuckin’ hot out here.” The heat was absolutely intense. The earliest days of February still harbored a sometimes bitter winter for most of the United States. At least the parts PRIME had been touring through recently. There was at least a fifty degree difference between where Tyler Rayne stood yesterday, waiting for his plane to arrive, and where he stood today, on the bow of a very expensive pleasure yacht. Fifty degrees before factoring the humidity and reflection of the sun’s relentless rays off the shifting waves of the Atlantic. “I’m on a boat mother fucker take a look at me.” His sweat-gleaned body glistened with the same diamond twinkle as the ocean below. A black pair of Oakley shades shielded his eyes from the brilliant sunshine. Black and red board shorts and a thin layer of SPF 45 were all he could stand to wear in the blazing heat. Which was still more than his associate had bothered to produce for attire. “You can’t stop me mother fucker cause I’m on a boat.” Katterina Wylde lay face down on the deck. Her thick hair is draped forward over her head. The black mess looked infinitely darker sprawled out in the blinding sun and glistening ocean. She tanned well for someone who usually crept through the shadows and darker corners of Detroit’s underbelly. The bronze sheen of her backside was nearly enough to match Tyler’s own California gold. No tan lines for the Dark Angel, either. Oh no. That would require something to actually be covering her skin in one spot or another. She was having none of that this afternoon. “How much longer do we have to wait?” “Just got the call from Milena. Shouldn’t be more than a few minutes.” “I got my swim trunks, and my flippy-floppies.” “You do realize how cliche this is, don’t you?” “Wasn’t my idea, love.” “I’m flippin’ burgers you at Kinko’s straight flippin’ copies.” His eyes wandered to the horizon. Far off across the endless ocean waves he could see a dark speck against the otherwise pristinely blue sky. “You’re not going to put on any clothes, are you?” “Should I?” He scoffed. Damn girl was incorrigible. Tyler marched across the deck and popped open a cooler, pulling an icy cold Corona from within. He reclined into one of the deck chairs nearest a small plastic table. The metal briefcase on the table shone like its own brilliant little star. “I’m on a boat mother fucker don’t you ever forget.” The speck on the horizon grew quickly. Wings sprouted from the side. Two pontoons grew from beneath. The deafening roar of a propeller engine drowned out even The Lonely Island blaring from the yacht’s overly expensive sound system. The white seaplane touched down with a hefty splash and glided to rest next to the yacht. The wings grew massively large as the plane slipped closer and closer. Closer. Tyler took a sip of the Corona and watched as the aircraft stopped just inches short of chipping the paint from his favorite yacht. “Our friends have arrived.” “Thank Christ. If I have to listen to this damn song one more fucking time.” Tyler laughed. “You think I’m turnin’ that shit off for them? Song’s fuckin’ hilarious.” “Take a picture, trick. I’m on a boat, bitch.” She responded by moving for the first time in a half hour. Katterina rolled to her back, exposing all sorts of wonderful goodies men have been drooling over for years. Her hand lifted just enough to give him the finger. Even with her annoyance, she still couldn’t be bothered to open her eyes. He laughed. These little arguments could go on for days, but business called. The door to the plane had already opened and a young man with shaggy brown hair climbed across the wing to the yacht. He wore only a pair of white slacks and leather sandals. His muscular frame was hardly enough to rival Tyler’s, but for someone outside of the profession… respectable. The man smiled as he stepped across the deck, pulling down his own designer sunglasses to give Katterina a long once over. “I always did think the scenery was beautiful here.” “Yeah. Too bad you didn’t give me a number. Could’ve let you know we had a landing strip right here on the deck.” “I’m ridin’ on a dolphin, doin’ flips and shit.” “Nice song.” “I thought so.” “For the seventh time in a row.” “Katterina Wylde, I presume. Nice to see your reputation is well earned.” “Let’s hope yours is as well.” “Of course.” Tyler opened his hand and gestured to a nearby deckchair. His guest nodded graciously and accepted. “To business then.” “Unless you plan on starin’ at Katt’s tits some more.” “They are rather fine.” “Why thank you.” Tyler sighed and pushed the metal briefcase across the plastic table. The man smiled and opened it. “What’s this?” “Ten large.” “I’m the king of the world, on a boat like Leo.” “That’s half, Mr. Rayne. The instructions we gave you were very explicit. You know how these things go. We were very clear about--“ “You’re right. I do know how these things go. I know you’re just as likely to fuck me over when you get your money as you are to live up to your end. So you get half in advance. The other half when I get what I’m paying for. And if your information proves to be satisfactory, I’ll throw in another ten as a tip for services rendered.” “I’m sorry, Mr. Rayne, that’s just not how business is done. We agreed—“ Tyler pointed behind the young man. To the plane on which he’d arrived. The dusty haired informant turned as directed. There was an Asian woman sitting atop his plane. Dark hair rustling in the breeze. She sat peacefully, eyes closed, a katana balanced across her legs. “The assassin.” “She’ll kill your pilot before you even get up from that seat. And I know you can’t fly that plane yourself.” “You could, though.” “Not if you intended to land.” “Still never finished those lessons then?” “Hard to find the time these days.” “Of course. Well, as you can see, I know a great deal about you, Mr. Rayne. In fact, it is my sole task to keep as many tabs on you as possible. Keeping those tabs straight has proven to be an… interesting career path.” “I’m sure. You have my information?” “Straight to the point.” “I’ve lost the luxury of fucking around as of late.” “Yes. You’re quite busy lately. Might I ask you a question, Mr. Rayne? Why so many enemies? The ones we’re here to discuss I can almost understand. But this wrestling business. Devin Shakur. Lisa Tyler. Wade Elliott. Why do you insist on infuriating so many people at once?” “It keeps life interesting.” “I’ll bet it does.” The informant smiled. The gesture was genuine, if a bit condescending. Amused, really. He pushed a thick manila envelope across the table toward Rayne. “Fuck trees I climb buoys, mother fucker.” “Everything we know about the Jade Scorpion and the Son of Nine. Business ventures. Real estate properties. Known associates. I believe there’s a particular set of warehouses there you might be familiar with. As well as some establishments in Hawaii…” “Looks good. Synopsis?” “It turns out the gentlemen you refer to as Number Nine did, in fact, have a bastard son hidden in Okinawa. The boy was far removed from his father’s Yakuza ties, seemingly, by his mother. The two lived in near poverty for some time. It’s most likely the boy never even knew who his father was. Other than that… he’s practically Jesus. I could tell you where he was born. Who was at the hospital. I could tell you the poorest borough of Okinawa where he grew up. After about the age of eight… there’s nothing. Until recently. Most of that is just unfounded rumor and hearsay. Other than your own testimony, there’s nothing of particular merit to prove he’s even still alive.” “Never thought I’d be on a boat.” “As for the Jade Scorpion… well she popped up a few years back. The ridiculous nature of her name is intended. Seems she thinks that the name in conjunction with her lowly status as a woman serves as a feint of sorts. Allies and enemies alike enter her arena with lowered expectations. They come ill prepared. Then, much like the scorpion from which she takes her name… she devours them. She is an ambitious little minx, but cautious. Fingers in all sorts of illegal honey pots. Extortion. Kidnapping. Human trafficking. Drug running. A jack of all illegal trades. There’s nothing that considerably ties her to you or Se7yn. Which we found very interesting, seeing as she hired you under the pretense of stopping Se7yn from disrupting her… affairs.” “So the Son of Nine… there’s no properties? No aliases or accomplices?” “Nothing. No. There were a handful of his father’s properties bought up a few years back. All legit purchases. We traced them to the Jade Scorpion, interesting enough.” “So they are connected.” “Somehow. Though the threads are so finely woven even we could not connect them. There was… one other thing I thought to mention. It’s all there in the envelope, of course. You did know the boy was adopted, didn’t you?” “Yeah. Those were his foster parents got killed in NC.” “Yes. Tragic. Well we had to do a bit of digging… but we thought you might find it interesting to learn where he really came from. His father was a wrestler, turns out. Even spent some time in Tokyo, right around the time you were there.” “Is that a fact?” “It is indeed. Paperwork’s all there. You can verify for yourself.” “I will.” “I believe this concludes our business then.” “I believe it does.” “And my payment?” Tyler nodded toward the plane. Milena was standing atop the wing, passing two metal briefcases along to the pilot. The informant smiled and stood, pulling his own briefcase from the table. His eyes wandered along the smooth curves of Tyler’s Asian counterpart as the two passed each other on the wing. Katterina sat up to wave a flirtatious goodbye to the informant as the plane carefully pulled away. “Believe me when I say, I fucked a mermai-ee-ai-ai-ee-ai-aid.” “Fuck me running.” The envelope fell from his hands. Loose sheets of paper, blueprints, official court documents… everything fluttered in a cascade to the deck. Melina scrambled to recover what she could. Katterina sat up. There was no denying the hint of revelation in his voice. Betrayal. She knew him well. The Dark Angel could derive implications from even so simple and crude phrases. “What is it?” “His name is Logan. Drake fucking Logan.”
