Into The Abyss: Part I - The Turning Tide

29 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/29/08 He bit down hard on his tongue, as though it would take him away from the nightmare and into the fuzzy warm reality he was accustomed to, a place where he made his own decisions and the fate he brought upon himself was just that: his. His throat was hoarse and dry and tired, it felt like he had screamed his last, his wrists pulsed painfully from the shredding bonds that tied him to the arm rests of the chair and dug deeper into the raw flesh from the constant struggle he had put up. Now as he opened his eyes cautiously to pitch black he sighed with relief, thanking whoever or whatever was watching over him for the momentary reprieve. With a simple click the dream was shattered once again as blinding white light struck him like a rattlesnake, causing him to wince and whimper as a footstep sounded from what seemed like a couple of feet in front of him, just behind the searing light. Another, then another until eventually stopping directly behind him. Silence. "This was never going to be pleasant." A warm, pungent breath caressed the back of his neck, the voice sending the hairs on the back of his neck on end. He closed his eyes again, shaking nervously as a tear developed, praying it wouldn't so much as blur his vision. The dark was his only solace now, and the silence in between. Anything else was a mark left by his tormentor, another memory to stain his thoughts. Holding back the tears as though they should flood from his eyes and drown him if he looked onto the world, he grit his teeth together and took a deep breath as a large hand clamped down on his shoulder, the thumb grinding into his shoulder blade in circular motions and sending sharp pains shooting into his back. "You didn't have to be here you know, it could have been anyone else...but you had to involve yourself. What were you thinking? Nothing you have experienced so far has even scratched the surface...and you were their last great hope. You were everything they needed and now you're nothing to no one. Sure people got hurt and now there are repercussions, but let's not pretend that's going to be a problem. The only issue right now...is how far can I push you before I get what I want?" Two thick, greasy tasting fingers forced their way into his mouth and pulled at each corner, the skin stretched and his cracked lips splitting as his mouth was pulled back into a grotesque smile. Two thumbs pulled the corners of his eyes back, reducing them to a slant and allowing what few tears he could now produce to trickle down his nose and drip off. His face seemed to ignite and burn from the pain, then suddenly the fingers slid out and his head was pushed down. His lips definitely felt torn, and running his tongue between them to the corner of his mouth he could feel through the burning that his tongue was poking into a little fissure of flesh past where his lips had ended. He moaned and tried to cry, but no more tears would come. The pathetic noise escaping his throat brought down an open hand, smacking him across the face and dazing him as the light seemed to shift in front of his eyes, dancing in shades of red and purple. "Guess you really don't have anything to smile about if I can't even force one out of you...so by my reckoning you are less than comfortable here, or you care rather deeply for the cause you're trying to protect by refusing to tell me anything...so be it..." The voice trailed off as the light blinked into darkness and for a brief moment the orb of purple phosphorous light danced before him. Taking a moment to bask in the peace his reprieve was shattered as a piercing beep echoed throughout the room, changing tempo until one long and shrill scream bore into his head, causing him to groan and bite down in an attempt to tighten the muscles in his head and somehow drown out the white noise...to no avail. Something in his head snapped and in an instant he embraced the torture, allowing it to fill his head and shake his blood. His eyes rolling into the back of his head, he opened his mouth and bellowed and hollered, using the burning sensation that filled his dry throat to roar into the dark, his world filling with a blotchy red mist as an imaginary Anaconda wrapped itself around his torso and squeezed his lungs so tightly even the last rasping scream before he inhaled seemed to rock his foundations. The entire time he had yelled he could not once make out his own voice over the high-pitched noise that battered around in his head and scrambled his brain. His ears rang and buzzed on as the noise ceased, and a row of blunt bony knuckles cracked him across the nose, sending a clot of blood flailing to the ground. "Understand this. I am the night, I am in the shadows. I am watching your every move even when I'm asleep. If you won't give me what I want then the situation becomes a problem, and you become my problem. Ergo, and pay attention because this is something you should take great care to remember...no matter where you go or who you involve yourself with or what you do down to the littlest most insignificant action...I will be watching. I could break your bonds and let you go but the fact of the matter is...it doesn't matter. You will never be free again. Give me what I want and you can go back to your shitty existence where only you and the tax man care what you do...or try living your life when every waking moment will be like holding a loaded gun to your head and being unable to pull the trigger when that is the only thing left in the world you desire. Do you understand what I have just said?" From the thick globule of blood, sweat and dirt clotting around his mouth and nose a sharp exhalation escaped his nostrils, sending claret spraying across the floor. His lip quivered as he looked up into the darkness, imagining his captor standing over him and what he might look like. Sighing nervously he swallowed and turned his head away from the bodily fluids oozing onto the floor before taking a deep breath. What must have felt like a moment to prepare himself to answer was a mere couple of minutes and ample time for his captor to boil with anger. Grabbing the back of the chair with one hand and his victim's head with the other, the tormentor pulled him to his feet. From the size of his hands and strength the victim was struck with more fear as the realization his captor was a mountain of a man gripped him as tightly as his captor's hand around his head. Propped back to an upright position he took another breath as his captor cleared his throat. "I want to hear you agree to my conditions or I will begin a rigorous and painfully slow process which will end, and this I promise you, in the proverbial trigger being pulled. NOW TELL ME WHAT I WANT TO HEAR!" It was this moment he chose. Grimacing menacingly and using the pain coursing through him as a stimulant he spat at his attacker and replied in a bubbling, guttural tone. "If you wanted me dead I'd be six feet under by now. Do your worst you son of a bitch coward!" Dead silence. Then the sound of footsteps. The door to his prison opened and slammed, reverberating around the walls and letting him know the room he was in was small, nothing else. Time seemed to drag on as he sat wondering what was to happen, and in what was no time at all the door opened and two silhouettes entered and approached him leaving the door ajar. The next sensation was of coarse burlap sliding over his head and something being strapped around his arm. Exchanging words in a hushed tone one of the figures quickly left again, flicking a light switch before closing the door behind them. "You will wish you had told me what I wanted. However you wanted the worst and I have it right here..." Twisting his wrist around with one hand and grating the sores on his skin against the rope binding him to the chair, his captor tapped his arm with the other and then took something from his mouth. He heard a faint spurting noise before a stinging sensation filled his elbow, followed by the flow of some kind of fluid... "Forget what I told you before. Your defiance tells me I won't get what I want unless I take action here and now..." Suddenly pins and needles exploded up his arm, filling his chest and his head until his eyes felt like they were popping out of his skull. A smile forced itself upon his lips and in a moment his worst fear was realized. "Diacetylmorphine. Heroin. Pure skag. On the street one bag of this will set the junkies back $20, but I'm willing to forego the cost for this pursuit. Until you tell me what I want to know, I'm going to pump this shit into you every single day until you're so strung out you'll tell me your dirtiest little secrets for one fucking hit of the good stuff. My only piece of advice is to enjoy it, there's nothing worse than being addicted to something you hate. Remember, any time you want to you can give me my information and I'll let you go...hell I'll even help you go cold turkey. But until you do you'll be spending all your time here in your own personal hell." As his heart pounded faster than ever before and his eyes rolled into the back of his head he heard the light switch being flicked again, followed by the burlap tearing up over his head, the sensation rippling through his skin and leaving it crawling as though a thousand spiders were scuttling across his face. Opening his eyes a blurry silhouette stood in the doorway, a hulking form of muscle holding the syringe and bag in hand. Chuckling at the sight before him, his captor cocked his head and reached for the door knob. Salivating uncontrollably and grinning without cause he stared at his tormentor, rage bubbling away beneath the soft, warm glow that took grip of his body. His captor took one final look at the sorry mess before him and turned to leave his victim in darkness. "You strike me as someone with no regrets. Perhaps a change of character is in order...sweet dreams Von Kelsig."
