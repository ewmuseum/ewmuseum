See You In Seattle.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday The impact of my body crashing down across another, and then my torso across the guard rail from somewhere up in the lights broke my two already cracked ribs, and left me with an ever increasing whirlwind of incertitude. “Should I have wrestled twice in one night at Culture Shock?”, “Have I been training hard enough?” all these questions swirl around in my head, each revolving around a solitary central doubt, the eye of the storm if you will. “Am I good enough?” Now on any other day before now the automatic computer generated response formulated inside my head would be “Of course”. Even before such a ridiculous thought could be conceived. Since being signed to PRIME my confidence has swelled and deflated like boat sails in the changing winds. In other words, without notice. One minute I’m the shit, then next thing you know I’m doing something stupid like missing a double shooting star press and crashing through a table, or eating guard rail. Now it seems as if all the boasting I did when I first got signed has come back to haunt me as I’m left to wonder the question again, this time audibly, as if I truly don’t know. “Am I?” I whisper to myself in an inquisitive tone as I ease back in my chair. Well, in actuality the chair isn’t mine, it’s Matt’s... this being his place and all. But seeing as how I squat here when I’m in town, I’m sitting in it and he’s not, it’s mine for the moment. So here I sit, blinds shut, wasting the day away in the darkened living area of Matt’s apartment while quickly approaching full blown inebriation and doing something I’ve never been prone to before going pro, doubting myself. Which I’ve had a lot of time to do as of late due to an injury knocking me out of action for a few weeks. The injury could be chalked up to stupidity I guess, over zealous to win a shot at the Alias title I jumped into the fray with whomever else entered, even held my own for a while, but came up empty. Damn cluster matches, too many things to watch and worry about. Going in I could’ve sworn I’d come out on top as I knew I could take any of those guys one on one. Not like it mattered though, it was like being in one of those money booths where they blow dollar bills all around but you just can’t seem to grab any of them. On the bright side, I didn’t get pinned, but I mean seriously, I matched those guys move for death defying, jaw dropping, hair raising, eye popping move, and then I got serious... It was evident, at least in my eyes that I was overwhelming them. Then to come up empty handed like that? This coming ReVolution I will be looking to claim my spot in the pecking order and prove that I am truly the new face of PRIME. “I can’t lose.” I murmur to myself as I lean forward in the chair, causing the foot rest to once again collapse back into the front of the chair. Normally when such a thing comes out of my mouth it’s said with an air of confidence, but this time it comes as more of a warning. I can’t afford to lose, not again, and especially not with what is rightfully mine on the line. Once fully upright I reach out onto the table in front of me and take a half emptied bottle of Yukon Jack in my grasp. As I lift the bottle up from the surface of the table a small square of moisture can be seen where little droplets of water cause by the frost melting from the base of the bottle have begun to accumulate. I quickly look over both shoulders to make sure Matt is nowhere in the vicinity before wiping the wetness from the table with the cuff of my hoodie, as he would most likely have a fit if he thought a ring were going to develop on his table. After opening the bottle I slowly fill the lonely shot glass sitting next to where the bottle previously sat. As I stare into the thick brown liquid filling the translucent black shot glass with the silhouette of a zero lined in gold the smell of what I am about to ingest fills my nostrils. A less than enjoyable aroma to say the least, so horrid in fact that most anyone else who has smelled the same thing would put the glass down. I simply open my mouth and toss the shot into the back of my throat. That much closer to intoxication, I gently set the bottle back down on top of the table. This time on a coaster. A burning sensation slowly works it’s way down my throat then grinds to a scorching halt at the center of my chest as I pull my hood up over my head and lean back once again, now feeling sufficiently buzzed. A deep sigh rumbles up from the pit of my stomach, squeezes through my esophagus, and makes a mad dash for open air through my partially pursed lips before echoing through the large empty house. I chuckle to myself a little at the faint noise repeating over and over. Just then, nature screams. I try to ignore it as am in quite the opposite of the mood of someone who wants to get up, but apparently not only does nature know where I am, but that bitch has my cell phone number as well. I lean back further into the chair and pull both of by legs into the air as I press both my palms flat onto the armrests. Once I feel I’m in a sufficient position, I thrust all of my weight forward in an attempt to use the chair to aid in the task of catapulting me to my feet. To my relative amazement, it works, and if only for a moment, I feel like I’m flying. But then again, that could easily be attributed to the fact that it’s two thirty in the afternoon and I’ve been up drinking all afternoon. Eh, oh well, it’s still fun... while it lasts. As I coast forward into what on any other day would be a five point landing I find that my landing gear has yet to be deployed and that both the pilot and co-pilot are in the midst of a drunken stupor at the helm. If it weren’t for those damn unions I’d fire both their asses. And if I weren’t hurtling toward the ground right now I’d probably shake my fist in protest of their existence, something to the effect of what an old man does toward the kids throwing the frisbee in his yard. Finally the rust riddled copper cogwheels of my slow motion descent grind to a screeching halt as the front of my right foot strikes the floor with the rest of my body coming down at an extremely odd angle behind it. At this very instant I realize that I am not buzzed. But I’m not sober either. As a matter of fact, I’m drunk. Indeed, I’m actually pretty fucked up right about now. What a time to find out. Somehow I manage to plant my right foot flat on the ground and use my left arm to catch the rest of my weight, landing in what could be considered the red headed stepchild version of the three point stance. Picture spider-man... but drunk. That how I look right about now. Who knew such a simple task as getting to one’s feet from an easy chair could be such an accurate gauge of how fucked up you are. When my foot hit the ground it felt like I was stepping down into a vat of watered down jell-o pudding... needless to say, Bill Cosby would not approve. Rather than muscling my way up into a standing position I opt to simply drop down onto both knees and work my way up from there. Too bad the trip from knees to feet include a pit stop in Layingonthefloorville, USA. I don’t know if it’s due to my equilibrium being entirely maladjusted at the current moment or for the simple fact that the floor seems to resemble a water bed filled with freezing cold water on a hot summer day, but that’s where I’m headed. No finesse or gingerly working through the motions about it, I just keel over to my left side and collapse on the floor. Soon after I assume a fetal position, complete will full range thumb sucking motion. Babies don’t sleep this good... Suddenly and excruciatingly rudely I’m awakened from my alcohol induced, daydream or reverie or what have you by the sound of nature once again screaming in my ear by way of a police issue megaphone. My eyes pop open as if pried by crowbars as I quickly roll over onto my stomach and use my arms to hoist myself up, much like your standard issue push-up. I then push myself off the ground and pull my legs up under myself and during my landing transform into a world class sprinter then take off in a mad dash for the bathroom. Now I know I’m fast, hell, everybody knows I’m fast. But I don’t think I’ve ever run like this before, not even when being shot at or finding myself late for class. I fly out of the living room like a bat out of hell being chased by a Russian race horse with a glue truck behind it bordering on the speed of light on a three day crack binge. Once I hit the hallway I get the same feeling I do when I realize that I’m home free, usually the same one I get around the time a ref counts three in my favor. Probably the same one a junkie gets while taking his first hit fresh from detox. I tear down the hall kicking up rugs the whole way until finally I reach the door. Once there turn the nob while still trying to bring myself to a complete stop. Much to my disappointment... it doesn’t turn. Doesn’t even budge, nothin’. With the situation continuously more critical by the second I proceed to jump up and down in an attempt to somehow stall the inevitable, the draining of the main vein. Still in disbelief of what is going on I try the door once more only to hear the words “I’m in here.” come through the door in a muffled voice. Ugh, Matt. One word comes out of my mouth before I race back up the hallway, and that word, ladies and gentlemen is “Fuck.” Although I doubt he even hears it until I’m a good three quarters of the way back down the hall. I burst back out into the front room of the apartment and nervously look back and forth across the area evaluating options. Finally something clicks and it dawns on me to go outside. If lightning were to have struck the exact spot I was standing, at the exact time I looked toward that door as my only hope... it would’ve missed. Words can not describe how fast I got from the end of the hallway and out the front door. With time being of the essence I decide that in this instance finding an optimal spot for the outdoor deployment of urine is less than necessary as long as it gets out. Basically, as soon as I open the door I let it fly... just as Matt walks into the living room. Startled by the footsteps I jump, skewing my aim and dousing his shoes on the welcome mat. I just know he’s gonna kill me for this. After being ripped several new assholes over the shoe incident and with the passing of several hours, a hotel room now lies in ruin as a result of one of the wild parties that have a tendency to occur anytime I visit home. Inside, amid empty liquor bottles and a myriad of half crushed beer cans lay several partially clothed bodies. Each one strewn about the floor and furniture in reckless fashion... and snoring in unison. Though stretched and contorted into the oddest of positions with limbs draped at widely varying angles, each person’s face conveys an overwhelming sense of comfort and well being. Euphoric, utter peace. This is true of everyone in the room, except, ironically, the person who should be at the absolute epitome of comfort. The only person in the entire place occupying a bed, me. Unlike the smiling faces of my unconscious friends, mine has twisted into a visage of unrest. Here I sit alone in bed, back pressed against the cold wall, bent knees hugged to my chest as I gently half hum half sing to myself. Rocking back and forth ever so slightly I continue on over and over “I’ve been, workin’ this grave shift, and I ain’t made shit. I wish I could, buy me a spaceship and fly... past the sky.” That’s right, the doubt is back. Stopping for a moment, I Look down at my unconscious friends, who’ll all no doubt be sporting spectacular headaches once the sun breaks dawn in half, but I can’t help but envy them. They’ll all wake up and go about their business with nothing extraordinary expected of them. None of them will be expected to show up to yet another arena in which half the people inside it, including staff, co-workers... if you could even call the rest of the roster that, and those in attendance haven’t a clue as to who they are. None of them would be expected to bring a crowd of thousands to their feet. None of them will be faced with the challenge of putting on a harrowing display of daring do as they face off against a much larger opponent, especially not one that just might be the opportunity needed to finally become something. But then again, I’m not them. None of these things would probably ever be expected of any of them, but all those things have suddenly become my life. For the past several weeks I’ve been traveling from city to city, arena to arena, often times showing up several hours early in anticipation of the quickly becoming commonplace “Jackson Darilek?” posed by event staff with a cocked eyebrow when I try to enter the building. More often than not, the question posed is soon be followed by being forced to use general parking and enter through the front door. On one occasion I even had to purchase a ticket at the door in order to gain entry... I lost that night. Now go ahead and tell me that’s not supposed to hurt. I guess all that hassle I’m forced to endure just to get backstage to the locker room is all worth it to hear “Bring Em Out” hit and be able to run out there in front that crowd of screaming fans. That is, until I gets out there and half the crowd is sitting on their hands while the other half use the opportunity to score nachos at the concession stand. Humiliating to say the least. It always comes back full circle though. My anonymity can’t obscure the ridiculous risks I take. Wether they know who they’re clapping for or not, everybody loves to see death being openly defied... even if it doesn’t result in a win. I personally could go without the part where it doesn’t amount to anything though. Ugh, I’m so lame... still sitting in this bed, still humming to myself, staring up at all the beer caps that have been embedded in the room’s popcorn ceiling. All I can do is hope, desperately at that, that it isn’t the case on this outing. If only there were a star shooting past my window, I’d wish on it. Hell, I can use all the luck I can get. My next match serves as a double edged sword. One side could force me deeper into the depths of obscurity, while the other could catapult me into stardom with a huge upset over the former 5-Star champ. More importantly though, it would prove to me that I actually belong here in PRIME, and furthermore that I made the right decision by skipping out on college and using tuition money to get here. With each glance at my snoring, drooling friends my secret envy for them swells. If only they knew how much the guy they all think has “the life: wished he had theirs right now. Doubts swirl around me as I continue humming ... I still can’t get this damn song out of my head. I can practically see myself in the ring with Nova right now, right down to our opening moves. As powerful as my army of doubts had become, there’s still one thing that could crush them all. Winning. The only acceptable outcome. Realizing that there is but one option, my expression becomes one of determination as scenarios continue to play in my head. He goes for a suplex? Flip out of it, win. He locks me in a submission move? Grit my teeth, take that shit like a G, win. He nails me with a chair shot? Suck that shit up, win. He goes for a pin? Kick out, WIN. That’s all there is to it. Tomorrow, the extraordinary will be expected of me, and I intend to rise to the challenge. See you in Seattle.
