Date: ReV 225 The creature slinked across the distant dune, not quite stalking but… pacing. If such a thing could be said of a panther. Or leopard, perhaps. He was no zoologist. The beast padded across the sand, muscles rippling beneath a dark pelt that looked like velvet. Occasionally the dark lips would curl back, revealing jaws of absolute destruction. He swore he could hear the creature’s soft growl before it turned to retread the prints it had just made. The majestic black predator did not walk a perfect line. The shifting sands made that more than impossible. Each step carried the immense weight of the animal, which even in measured percentages, was enough to disturb the delicate balance of millions of little grains stacked atop each other. Here and there, where the sand wasn’t packed as tight as the step before, the desert would disperse beneath the creature’s paws. Fluid and adaptive, the cat would drift along with the erosion, ultimately weaving a slow but serpentine pattern across the dune. So it had done for what felt like the past hour. He had watched, of course. There was little else to do while stranded in an endless sea of sand. The dunes stretched for miles. As far as he could see. Other than this strange oasis and that distant panther… leopard… whatever… there was nothing but desert. On and on forever. The sun was blinding bright. Too intense to look at. It illuminated the landscape. The horizon lit over the cusp of yet another dune. He could see the shimmer in the farthest distances of his vision. That strange filter of heat that made the air visible. Made it dance. The sort of sweltering hot that fucked with the mind. Twisted the world. Hallucinations. Panthers. Leopards. Whatever. “She’ll come back, you know. Just give her time.” Her voice was smooth and confident. An undercurrent of care and devotion flowed beneath the words. An inflection of love in the paced manner of speaking. He sighed, and for the first time in that hour, removed his glance from the pacing beast. His palms were flat against the surprisingly cool marble stone. The ledge was maybe three feet tall. Almost as thick. Pure marble, same as the half circle of stone laid out around him. Muscular arms stretched before him, supporting a lithe but powerful two hundred (and change) pound frame. He hung his head. A single, disappointed shake the prelude to his argument. “It’s been months now.” “You killed her father.” “Yeah. I recall. I also recall her showing up at my house and going all Inigo Montoya on me, so I figured we were kinda square.” “Square? Remind me… what was it you did to the people that killed me?” Tyler Rayne pulled his hands from the stone ledge and rose to his full height. He turned, with more than a bit of annoyance, at the beautiful young Japanese girl sitting on the ledge just a few feet away. Her dark hair was pulled into a loose ponytail that hung over her left shoulder. The black lengths stretched out over the front of her white dress. She had somehow pinned a handful of cherry blossoms throughout the luxurious locks. Green eyes blazed back defiantly at the scarred, yet handsome, man. “Well?” One dark brow raised over her right eye. The jade orbs narrowed. Her jaw set. She presented the challenge. Quite unlikely to back down. “That was different.” “How so?” “I didn’t even actually kill that fucker, so I don’t see what she’s so bent about.” “You stabbed him with a sword and set him on fire. And kicked him off a cliff.” “Well he’s a fucking vampire, isn’t he?” “Is he?” “I… shit.” “Thought so.” “Fine. I killed him. Gods know that fucking bastard deserved far worse than… ” His finger was extended in her direction. Emphasizing the point. Which was lost at the distraction of discoloration. The index finger was not quite the color he had expected. The usual bronze hidden beneath a muted veil of darkened red. He opened his fists. Turned both palms open. Sure enough. Stained with blood. “Lin, darling, why is it that every time you’re here… this shit happens?” She smiled. “You tell me.” Remarkable beauty glowing behind that simple smile. She nodded toward the center of their stone oasis. A glance over his shoulder revealed a white marble basin he had not noticed before. He turned a skeptical look back to the Asian marvel. She shrugged. Resigned, he marched to the basin. His boots sounded like thunder coming down on the marble. It was then that he realized how quiet it was. No wind. No birds. No sounds at all other than their own voices. The basin was full of the clearest water. He dipped both hands into the reservoir. He washed quietly. Wringing and rubbing at the dark splotches across his skin. “It’s quiet here.” “Peaceful.” “Heh. Yeah. Guess I wouldn’t know much about that.” Her smile is more subdued this time. As if she were aware of some grand secret he has yet to discover. He did not notice, though. He could not focus on anything but the oddity of his hands. He stared at the back of his right. Then turned to inspect the palms. The same stunned examination was given to the right. Check between the fingers. Beneath the nails. “Well fuck me runnin’.” “What’s that, dear?” “They’re… clean.” There was no exact explanation for his confusion. Though he most certainly was confused. Something about this seemed wrong. Off. He could remember a dozen other times when a similar scenario had presented itself. Never had he been able to wash off the blood before. “You notice anything about this place, Ty?” He does not look up from the fascinating circumstance of his blood free digits. “It’s a fucking desert, Lin. Only thing to notice is the gods damned sand.” “Well there is that panther.” “I thought it was a leopard.” “Whatever.” “There’s nothin’ here, love. Just me and a ghost and a jungle cat we keep acting like’s my best friend. Look around. No buildings. No people. No ninjas falling from the ceilings. Serial rapists wanting to skull fuck me. Retarded Polish steroid junkies trying to ruin my career. Again. We’re all alone out here. With jack shit to do. No bullets to dodge or swords to swashbuckle against. Not even a good bottle of imported beer to crack. We’re… ” He dropped his hands. Mouth agape. He stared at her blankly. Incapable of forming more than a single word of revelation. “Oh.” That subdued but secret smile returns to her face. “Peaceful.” He laughed. “Yeah. Guess I wouldn’t know much about that.” A smile spread across his face. Not the usual cocksure and brash, panty dropping grin that has wet the pants of women the world over. A legitimate expression of pleasure. The sudden warmth he felt from inside radiating outward. It was a strange feeling. A bit unfamiliar. He strode over to the marble ledge and leaned there next to her. She extended a hand. The warm touch of her fingers around his wrist. “You’ve been fighting for a long time, dear.” “It’s really over, isn’t it?” “There’s no one left to fight.” “So… what now?” “What do you want?” “Uni.” He said the word without even thinking. Without even realizing. The question registered and he answered with the first thing that came to his mind. Automatic. Almost instinctual. “This is fucking weird. These past few months I just felt… lost. Confused. Like the life I was living wasn’t my own anymore. Nothing made fuckin’ sense. I’m goin’ through the motions and… all this time. All this fucking time I … I’m a dumbass. Shit. This is… really disturbed. I don’t know how to live my life without— ” “Ninjas falling out of the ceiling.” “Something like that. This is my second chance. A real chance to make— ” “… a stop before we get to … ” The voice did not belong to Lin. He paused for a moment. Mind working through the strange revelation to identify this new speaker. Familiar. She was… “Ty, you’re drooling.” Well that’s just a strange fucking thing for an ethereal voice to comment on. ************************* “Ty. You’re drooling. It’s sorta gross.” The sun shone bright through the windshield. He cracked his eyelids open just a schmidge. Too bright. He groaned. His neck was stiff. His elbow was sore. His knee felt like someone had been pounding it with a hammer for the past hour. Where the fuck was he? Reluctantly, Tyler opened his eyes to embrace the day. A car. The passenger seat of a car. He’d been sleeping. He rolled his neck, ignoring all sorts of shooting pains down his spine, to the Chinese-American girl in the driver’s seat. Not the first time he’d woken next to a cute Asian girl. “The drool.” It was the first one, however, that complained so much about his bodily fluids. He wiped his mouth on his shoulder. “Better?” “Much.” “Where are we?” “Just crossed into Arkansas. Thought maybe you’d like to stop and get some food before you fall asleep again and leave me with all this damn driving.” “Well you are my assistant.” “Assistant. Not chauffeur.” “Well… assist me by driving.” “Ass.” A moment passed in silence. She was not as mad as she pretended. Nor was he as crass as he pretended. The moment ended soon. Juli could not hid her concern for long. Even if her boss was an asshole. “Seemed like a sort of intense dream. You were mumbling a lot. You OK?” “Shiny.” He smiled after answering. Still stunned at just how true the statement was. Self-revelation could have that sort of impact on a person. “You sure?” “Quite, kid. Five by fuckin’ five. No worries. Let’s grab some grub and get to this fuckin’ show. I think they booked me in a bum fight or something. I hear those bootlegs make mad money on the interwebs.” “Yeah. Does he, um… still put his balls on people’s faces?” “I sincerely fuckin’ hope not.” “Like it wouldn’t be karma coming to kick you in the ass.” “Now every person I put my balls on their face… they enjoyed that. Thoroughly. I don’t think ol’ Johnny boy could say the same.” “Really? All of them?” “Yes. Absolutely.” “Even that Magnum guy? The one you and Nathan pissed on.” “Yeah, but… he was a douche. And a half.” She laughed. “Riiiight. So where ya wanna eat?”
