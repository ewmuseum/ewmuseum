Border Jumping (RP for ReV vs. CPFTOC)

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-29-05 The scene opens to a dark room, standard, a shifty, mysterious figure sitting at what appears to be a control panel, standard, a maniacal cackle breaks the silence of an otherwise serine surrounding, standard, and a loud snore, audible only through speakers, then a booming blast of flatulence, priceless! The figure sitting alone, in the dark at the control panel is none other than Mr. E, his bizarre aura chills the dank room as it does any place he inhabits for longer than just a moment. The swelling, pungent smoke from his cigar lifts angelically upward as Mr. E. just looks on truly enjoying his term at the helm. “When the rest have fallen, my time will come.” Mr. E broodingly admits to no one other than himself. “While these weakened wills rest, it is I who plot the future of our creation, and soon the time will come where devastation and destruction will once again rule our motives. I can feel, deep inside my being, the winds of change are upon us, the rest grow complacent, weakened by a new comfort level, while I grow strong, motivated by the memories the rest have forgotten. Soon…………Soon…………….Soon………..” Suddenly, Mr. E.’s reflection is cut short as a door behind him creaks open slowly, and in a puff of smoke, he’s gone, letting the odor of his smoke permeate the room for only a few moments before it too is inhaled into the vacuum which he had escaped through. “Soon” Azala Zameer snaps up, waking himself with his own loud snoring, but finds himself not in bed, but in line, strange indeed. Dusty looks on quizzically for a brief moment, “I’ve never seen anyone fall asleep standing up before, I thought only rhino’s, cows, and horses did that?” “Wha? Who? Huh?” Zameer confusingly retorts, “Azala not remember…..?” “We are at the City Hall man, remember, we have to get you a passport so you can get into Canada for ReVolution this week?” Speaking with a certain frustration in his voice Dusty shakes his head and looks away. “Even though I told you a thousand times that you don’t need one to get into Canada or Mexico. Dummy.” “But Azala not going to Mexico?” Slapping his hand to his forehead, Dusty stares up to Zameer for a moment to make sure he’s serious. “Yeah, I know, but you don’t need a….” Dusty’s explanation, for what seems like the millionth time is interrupted by an annoying, high-pitched voice, “NEXT!” The massive form of Zameer and his new little buddy step forward to the desk, Zameer barely being able to squeeze through the opening supplied by two metal posts, and Dusty nearly unable to peer over the wooden table behind which sits a middle-aged white lady wearing a name pin that reads Jean, who appears to be in a very agitated mood. “What can I do for you today sir?” Questions Jean. “Azala need passport.” “Ok, well I’m going to need a birth certificate,” Jean commands promptly, trying to keep things moving whilst constantly look down to her watch. “Azala not have certificate of birth.” “Well, then no passport for you……NEXT!” Jean screams in the same annoying voice from earlier. Dusty jumps up, propping himself to stay at eye level, with his feet off the floor, looking Jean square in the eyes. “Isn’t it enough that he’s standing right here in front of you lady, I mean, what more proof of his birth could you want!” Growing increasingly more agitated now Jean staring at the young child, “and just who are you to tell me how to do my job? No birth certificate, no passport, simple as that……NEXT!” “But……” Dusty tries to interject but is rudely cut off. “But nothing!,” motioning over to a bulbous white man in an imposter police uniform playing the role of security guard, “you can leave now, or we can make you leave!” “Like that fat ass is going to be able to do anything to my friend,” looking up to Zameer and taking in his massive size, then looking back to Jean, “He’ll squash that fat piece of shit just like he did that clown and Pi….” “That was you! Security!” Without another word said, Zameer hoists Dusty up to his shoulder and like a running back, charges through the citizens standing in line and directly over the security guard who offered only a feeble attempt at capture, to the outside. “Why Dusty say Azala did that to clown?” Dusty squirmed out of Zameer’s grasp and plants his feet firmly back on the ground, “cuz that bitch pissed me off, that’s why!” “Now Azala have to sneak into Canada, this not going to be easy.” “Easier than you’d think.” Responds Dusty as the scene fades to black. We open back up to Zameer and Dusty driving along a rural highway in a light blue 88’ Caprice. The sun was just about to set to the west, but I’m not a huge fan of over description, so you can create your own picture in your head. “Do you even have a driver’s license?” Dusty inquires “Azala need license to drive car?” “How did you get this car without a license?” Dusty continues his barrage of questioning. “Azala…….. look 10 miles to Canada!” Zameer squeals as his head swivels to stare at the sign, causing the old car to almost go into the ditch, and it probably would have had Dusty not grabbed the wheel and steer them to safety. “Eyes on the road big guy, eyes on the road.” Pulling up to where the Canadian border patrol was visible, Azala nodded to Dusty in concurrence of their previously plotted plan to get in. Although Dusty was wary at best about Zameer’s plan, he really had no choice but to proceed at this point. Sounding simple enough to Azala, he had plotted to exit the car, having Dusty drive across the border. He would travel by foot, off the beaten path across the border and meet up with Dusty and the car about a mile down the road on the other side. It had occurred to Dusty, being only 13 years of age, that driving a car was completely illegal, but it was a chance he was willing to take to have the opportunity to view his first PRIME show live, accompanying Zameer and meeting other superstars. Azala on the other hand, had obviously not thought of this, as a matter of fact, it hadn’t even entered his mind that Dusty might not make it across, as his concern was for his own safe passing. Pulling the old Caprice classic up to the border patrol barricade, Dusty swallows hard while rolling down the diver’s side window. Seated upon a pile of clothes to give the impression that he’s quite taller than he really was, he greets the Royal Canadian Border Patrol Officer with a friendly, “Good evening sir.” “Good Evenin,” the officer responds. “Goin into Canada eh?” “Yes sir,” Dusty replies shortly trying not to allow his voice to shake from his nerves. “Meeting a few college friends to do some…….um…..fishing.” “Fishing eh? Don’t see any poles.” Thinking quickly over his rapidly pounding heartbeat, Dusty stares up to the officer looming over him, “My college buddies came up a few days ago, they brought all the equipment in their van. I had to stay late and finish up some papers for school.” “And what school would that be, eh?” “Um, University of Vermont, sir?” “You don’t sound so sure aboot that young man,” the officer interrogatingly admits, “What color van were your friends in?” “Red, it was a big red van sir.” “Big red van eh,” the officer repeats, “I’m going to have to ask you to step out of the car” Reluctantly Dusty opens the car door and steps out. The officer’s eyes bug almost completely out of his head when he first notices the pile of clothes the young boy had been sitting on, and secondly how young the boy actually appeared. “How old did you say you were eh?” Dusty pondered for a moment, him being in college was never really a thought that entertained the 13 year old boy that often, let alone how old he would have to be to actually be enrolled, “Um, I’m 26 sir.” “26 eh? Can I see your driver’s license then?” Thinking ever more quickly as his heart rate increased at an exponential rate Dusty offers a brief chuckle. “Funny story sir, I forgot my wallet at home, I didn’t’ realize I would need it to come to Canada and I was already two hours on my way and I didn’t want to go back and I didn’t think it would matter.” Speaking now as hastily as his heart was beating the officer picked up on this. Being Canadian didn’t mean he was completely retarded, and when things seems plainly spelled out, this officer eventually started picking up on the signs. “Left it at home eh?” “Yes sir.” “Do you have registration for this car then eh?” “Um no, it’s my dad’s car and he has all the papers with him back in Chicago.” Dusty was starting to feel this whole situation was spiraling downward out of control, and in the officer’s reply, his worst fears were confirmed. “Please come around the front of the car kid, I need to find out what this is all aboot.” The officer commanded whilst retrieving his walkie-talkie form his side holster. “Yeah, Bill, come in Bill.” An inaudible response is heard through the static of the obviously outdated CB system, but never-the-less garners a response from the officer at the scene. “I have a bit of a problem out here, I’ll be bringing in a suspected border jumper, potentially underage in about five minutes.” With that being said, the officer places Dusty in handcuffs and sets him in the back of the border patrol car, then pulls the old Caprice Classic into a safely parked position next to the patrol shed. Climbing back into his own car, he looks back over his shoulder at Dusty sitting in the back and shakes his head, “Not sure what you’re trying to pull here kid, but the Royal Canadian Mounties will get to the bottom of this.” “Right,” replied Dusty daftly, “You guys are fucking morons.” The border car pulls away a little more aggressively than was necessary and the tail lights soon disappear in the distant darkness. Looks like Dusty won’t be making it to his first show after all. Picking up a few miles down the road, Azala Zameer sits contently in a Denny’s (I sure hope there are Denny’s in Canada), shoveling food in his face, and waiting for his friend that unbeknownst to Zameer, isn’t going to show up. How will Azala get to Rev in Toronto? What will happen to Dusty in custody in Canada? Will the officer in charge of border patrol get a big promotion for making the biggest bust of this century for the Canadian Royal Border Patrol? Will Zameer have enough money to cover his massive Denny’s bill? Find out the answers to some of these intriguing questions on the next PRIME ReVolution!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
