What's My Line Part 2

22 July 2005

Date: 22/7/05 “John, I’m pregnant” It had only been three little words a few days ago but they had torn through John, like a hot knife through butter. As the words had left my mouth I could see him crushed in an instant. That hadn’t been my intention, it honestly hadn’t. It just sort of blurted out. The uncontrollable force of an argument, which causes you to say things, you really shouldn’t say. You’ve heard John’s side of the story…now hear mine. I suppose it began when John didn’t make it into the Ideal four competition, it was as though someone had dangled a sweet in front of a child, before cruelly whipping it away and eating it themselves. He had become quiet reclusive, which I could understand. The money was tight and we had no real source of income. It became an obsession of John’s, he had to get back in PRIME no matter what. It began that he would be away for a few hours a day, the hours turned into days, which of course turned into weeks. He always tried to reassure me that he was doing it for us, that if everything went to plan, things would be normal once again. Normal, that was a laugh. Life had never really been normal, but I carried on, I tried to be strong well as strong as I could be. There were times when I really noticed the difference though, having to sit around the house all day alone. It was worst at night though. I had started to fall into a depression you see. A woman needs to be held, to be touched in a way that only a man can do, yet my man was out painting someone’s car pink. I felt worthless, like the whole world was staring at me and laughing, laughing at the fact my husband didn’t care. That’s when I turned to the drink. It was four in the morning as I rolled over and padded the bed, the position where John would normally be, instead of a warm loving body I wanted to cuddle up against, I felt mattress and sheets. Despite it being hot outside I suddenly felt incredibly cold, a cold shiver just ran through me. I allowed my feet to find the floor, the soft carpet wrapping itself around my toes as I sighed. It wasn’t a content sigh, more one of despair, of loss, of realisation. At that very point, part of me thought that John would never come back, that this is how it was going to be forever. I don’t know what possessed me to do it, but I walked into the lounge and opened the liquor cabinet glancing at the treasure trove of alcohol inside. Reaching in I didn’t care what I pulled out. I just needed some relief, some form of end to the bitter loneliness that was encapsulating my heart. My eyes fell upon the see through liquid, which seeped like oil over the edges of the bottle. This elixir would prove to be my one, my saviour. The first gulp was sharp, unyielding as it attacked every one of my senses. As I continued to drink, the world around me span as it so often does when inebriated and for just a few blissful moments, I forgot about the sadness. The loneliness, everything that had driven me to this. In a way I forgot about John, what’s more I did not care, for about five minutes that is, then came the tears, the unfathomable tears of sadness. Why wasn’t John here? Why didn’t he love me anymore? Was he with another woman? Why was he cheating on me? I held that thought with me, until I lapsed into that sweet abyss known as unconsciousness. A place where I would be safe from thought, the dangers of my own mind. Unaware of the tiny life inside me. ~~~ “John, I’m pregnant.” The words echoed around my head like a fart in a church. I could feel the beads of sweat begin to develop on my forehead as I stared at Louise’s stomach. In there was a child, that someday soon would be born and depend on us for everything. Louise gently ran her hands over her stomach, the way that pregnant women do, she was smiling happily. I on the other hand couldn’t help but feel like there was something amiss, yes something was definitely wrong. Louise’s smile had faded, it was now a look of anxiety and worry, and I had not said anything for a good few minutes, fully aware that this was the case. I could not tear my eyes away from her stomach. ”Well? Aren’t you happy?” The desperation in her voice should have been the telling sign, my heart was wrenching inside of my chest. They describe it as someone walking over your grave, when you shudder uncontrollably. This was considerably worse, a feeling of dread that you know is coming, but you can’t possibly prepare for. “How far gone are you? How old is it?” I tried not to allow the emotion in my voice get the better of me, I won’t lie it was tough, especially whilst trying to avoid the gaze of scorn after referring to her child as ‘it’. She told me that the unborn was roughly two months old, that she hadn’t told me before because of the work situation. Now everything was ok though, everything could be fine, a nice and happy family. I inhaled deeply, the question was on the tip of my tongue, but I was scared of the repercussions, could I honestly survive alone? Louise was the woman who had pulled me back from the edge on numerous occasions. The doctors and psychologists had dubbed her ‘my angel in disguise’ but an angel couldn’t do this. “Is it mine?” The question was out there now, there was a joker in the pack, the dealer had dealt the cards and he wouldn’t take them back. She stepped back and raised a hand to her mouth, visibly shaking, either with rage or the shock that she’d been rumbled. The blistering left cross, which ricocheted across my jaw, nearly sending me sprawling across the carpet indicated it was rage. “You shit!” She screamed as she began lunging at my face. I raised my arms frantically blocking the blows, which came my way, before finally grabbing her wrists, just tightly enough to restrain her. “I only ask because I was away with PRIME then, remember Ideal 4? We had sex once that entire month so I simply put two and two together and…”Louise’s eyes softened slightly as I spoke, had I finally caught her out, surely she would have to admit defeat now. I vaguely remember seeing the stars, an intense burning sensation in my crouch. Louise’s knee imbedded deeply in an area that God only intended to be treated nicely. As I dropped to my knees, the tears involuntarily welling up, she screamed at me whilst leaving the house. “Well maybe you were lucky and managed to hit the spot for once in your life!” I may have had a swollen face and my testicles in my mouth, but that hurt. I managed to painfully and tenderly pull myself up into a sitting position, even though Louise had long gone, I felt that I needed to get the final word regardless. “Yeah well, you’re not so great yourself, with cellulite and sagging tits. I pity the baby that has to suck off of them!” Strangely enough, it didn’t make me feel better. ~~~ “And that was a week ago. I haven’t heard from him since.” The voice belonged to Louise, sat alone at the edge of the bar in the Rafferty’s pub. The pub was the sort of place where everyone was made to feel welcome. A real family atmosphere. Stood opposite her was the landlord known to one and all simply as Baz. “I’m sorry to ask you this and please don’t hit me, but it is his isn’t it?” Baz approached this extremely carefully, he need not have worried though, the rage and anger had passed. All that remained now was the sorrow and emptiness. All she had ever wanted was to be with John. She thought that a child would put the spring back into their relationship, that John would spend more time at home. She hadn’t expected to be accused of cheating on him. “Yes, it’s his.” She sighed as her words became muffled into the bottom of her glass, shooting back another mouthful of liquid. There was something about not drinking alcohol in a pub that she found depressing. When sober you can paint a picture of what you must be like drunk, it’s a bit like ‘The Scream’ only no one wants to steal you. “What am I going to do Baz? How am I even going to convince him that it was all just a horrible argument? Something, which simply got out of hand.” She returned her now empty glass to its position on the bar. He head slumped forwards resting on the palms of her hands. The past week had drained her of her strength, she had attempted to call John a few times, he simply hadn’t answered. She had come to the conclusion that he would be getting ready for this weeks show. It was the big one after all. As the slogan went Colossus II, nothing else compares. Although she tried to ignore John when he started to waffle about wrestling, but you couldn’t escape something the magnitude of Colossus. “Leave him to cool off for a bit, at the end of the day he’s a man and stubborn. Just give him something to realise that everything was a mistake and he will be fine.” The words of Baz were wise and true; Louise nodded sagely in agreement and sighed. “Well may as well have another drink Baz, lets be wild and have some ice with the orange juice this time” She forced a smile as Baz nodded and prepared the drink. She may have been reckless before, but she was not going to do anything that endangered this child’s life. It could turn out to be her last link with John. “If you don’t mind, I’ll get that…” ~~~ “And that was about a week ago, so guys what do you reckon?” John’s eyes glanced over to his friends who sat, Playstation 2 controller in hand. The men were Matt and Rob, friends from John’s childhood. A lot of wrestlers often became friendly with their colleagues, in his experience however this usually turned sour. Rob and Matt had simply never let him down. “I can’t believe she beat you up man.” Rob grinned as he spoke. They also had a mean sense of humour, the type where you weren’t accepted until they were taking the piss out of you. Matt sniggered into his control pad as they both tapped away furiously. “Hey come on lads, I know a few girls who would kick your ass.” John managed in reply, rather weakly I might add. ”Ellie could kick my ass any day she wanted and that Aimz chick DAMN I’d just like to grab onto one of those piercing and… “Wolfenden! Oh man extreme isn’t the word, then there’s Callisto Bane…” “You realise that she’s a lesbian right? With a girlfriend and everything!” “Oh yeah.” “GUYS” Immediately Matt and Rob stopped their rapidly descending conversation and paused the game on their Playstation 2, turning to John who by now was clearly distressed. He had never been that good at making decisions, he was a man who stabbed himself and jumped off a cliff. Nuff said. “Look John, we all know that Louise is the best thing in your life. So why don’t you just eat humble pie and accept that you were wrong.” These were the wise words of Rob who could tell just be looking at John, just how bad things were in that messed up head of his. “Another thing, if it turns out that it isn’t your child. Will you be prepared to love4 it as your own? It will still need a father figure, it will still need a dad.” John’s face distorted briefly at the thought of this. Did he really love Louise enough to live that much of a lie, could he really… “Stop thinking about it and go find her!” bellowed Rob who had grown increasingly worn with John’s dilly-dallying. A look of determination covered John’s face like a harlequin mask, this was short lived however as it was replaced with a look of despair. “ I can’t, I have to catch my flight to Michigan. There are a lot of press conferences I need to attend promoting Colossus II, I can’t really afford to not show up” John said, gutted at this realisation which had struck him like a man in a tinfoil hat in a thunderstorm. “With all due respect John, fuck PRIME and fuck Colossus, this is your wife and your life we’re talking about!” Rob pleaded with John who stood there shaking his head frantically. “But PRIME IS my life” John hastily retorted, his emotions starting to take control. “If that’s what you REALLY think John. Why the hell are you here? She didn’t leave you because of this baby business. That may have been the straw that broke the camels back sure but she left you because you don’t have time for her. Everyone can see that you care about your job more than her and that’s just not fair. She’s your fucking wife John, sort your fucking life out!. ”But…” “No fucking buts we’re tired of buts. You can do two things now, you can catch your flight to Michigan, do your press conferences and stay in your plush hotel or you can get your arse out of here find your wife and prove you’re the man that we know and respect. John could have said something. He wanted to say something but he knew that they were right. Immediately he turned on his heel and headed towards the flat door, in his mind he didn’t fully know where he was going, he just knew he had to leave and make a decision along the way. ~~~ “So what’s your name?” Asked Louise who had been joined by a male companion sat at the bar. Baz sneered at him from behind the bar when he had a spare moment, he could smell letchy scum from a million miles and this guy was their king, yet for some reason Louise had allowed him to sit down and talk to her. ”Leroy Jenkins” He almost seemed to purr as he spoke, immediately running a finger down her arm. Whilst she didn’t object to it, she certainly didn’t want to encourage it and ruffled out of reach slowly. Leroy was not put off though as he leaned in towards Louise. ”I have to say, you’re a pretty young thing aren’t you. All alone in a bar like this, that truly is a crime.” The slime was almost dripping from his words, like a snake through the long grass. Now Louise was feeling totally uncomfortable. Not only could she see Leroy the letch leaning in towards her, but she also caught a glimpse of Baz out of the corner of her eye. He was just about ready to put a pool cue through his skull. She shook her head lightly at Baz who relented, just about, before she turned to face Leroy and showed him her wedding ring. “Look Leroy, I’m sure you are a lovely person and all but I’m married so I don’t need your attention so thank you for the drink, but you are really wasting your time.” Louise spoke in a calm controlling voice as she looked into the eyes of Leroy, who didn’t take too kindly to this news, he leant forwards as his voice and face took on a much more sinister tone. “I don’t know who the hell you are, but I don’t see any ring or any husband for that matter, so quit screwing me around, you know you want a piece of me.” Louise glanced down at her hand, it was true she hadn’t been wearing her ring, in the middle of her rage she’d removed it. “Now what say you give me a little kiss” Leroy sprang forwards and locked his lips on hers as Louise tried to force her way out of it. It lasted for a few seconds until Leroy went limp and fell to the floor, snoring, he’d been knocked out. Louise looked up at Baz and wiped her mouth in disgust. Baz however was nowhere to be seen. Instead she turned around and stared into the eyes of John. He’d made the decision to come and find her, this was their usual haunt and he was right, however he had just seen her kissing another man and all the pain and hurt had come flooding back. “John…” Louise managed to utter as she stepped off of her stool and moved towards John arms outstretched, in reply John stepped backwards shaking his head. He looked down at the body of Leroy who was just beginning to stir again before sending a swift boot to his ribs, causing him to cough frantically. Immediately he turned around shaking his head. “John!” Louise cried out, she knew what he must have been thinking but that wasn’t the case, it wasn’t the case at all. John just kept walking he couldn’t turn around, if he saw her and him again. He’d kill him, he knew he would. Instead he just walked. Walked out of the pub and hopefully out of her life forever. “John!”
