Starting Over

13 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 13th, 2007 “Nothing great in the world has ever been accomplished without passion.” - Hebbel She knocks ever so gracefully on the large oak door. Behind her, several cars line the street, and a moving truck sits in the driveway with every single door open. With her back to the door, she looks out at the beautiful view of the city below her. He's picked a beauty, she thinks to herself. Then, she turns around after hearing the creaking of the door and smiles when she sees her assistant manager with dirt and sweat all over him. "Having fun, Dave?" she says with a chuckle to her voice. He just shakes his head and rolls his eyes, knowing that she’s come after all of the work was done. She takes a step inside of the house for the first time and is floored by the beauty of the house. "Wow...," she says, and frankly, it's all she can say as she takes a few steps in, maneuvering her way around the various boxes that clutter the floor. She walks over to a counter and places her purse down on it while she just takes another long look at the house she’s heard about for the past few days. "Yeah, he picked a nice one, Nicole. Huge too. I should know since I've carried most of the boxes in here. Some eight bedrooms, seven bathrooms. I'm pretty sure I hate each one of them too," he says, the memories fresh in his mind of having to unload the moving truck. She glances over at him and just laughs before moving her beautiful brown hair away from her face. "Where is everyone?" she asks him. "In the different rooms, trying to set things up for him. Julie, Rick, and Adolpho are here. Shawn and Lucy should be back in an hour. They went to go pick up dinner for everyone. You sticking around?" he asks her as he goes back to unpacking one of the boxes. She shakes her head, her brown eyes still looking around at the house. "Nah, I have to get back to the restaurant. Someone's gotta run the show there while you're here," she says jokingly, but he seems to be elsewhere. She takes a few steps away from him as she takes a good long look at the living room. "Where is Craig?" she asks him after a few moments pass by. "Oh, in his room, I think. To get there though, I'll have to draw you a map," Dave tells her. They walk over to the counter and Dave starts drawing on a piece of paper. ---- He looks at himself in the mirror and focuses on the one feature that most people tend to focus upon. His red eyes. There's a certain dullness to them this evening and he figures it's from the splitting headaches he's had for the past few weeks. Craig walks over to the sink and turns on the faucet, wishing they would finally go away. Weeks ago, he took some time off from PRIME. He had told management that he needed to refocus, but the truth had been that these headaches were starting to get the best of him. Craig looks to his right and grabs an aspirin bottle off of the bathroom counter, and pops off the top. He takes a few of them and throws them into his mouth before taking a long drink from the running water in his sink. As his eyes close, he can feel the pain worsening, and has to grab the counter to prevent himself from falling over. "Anyone home?" a familiar voice says from afar. He somehow is able to push the headache away, and turns around. As he walks out, a small smile appears on his face when he sees the most beautiful woman in the entire world. At least in his mind. "Hey...," he says, his voice trailing off as he can't think of another word to say to her. Two months he’s known her and from the moment he saw her he’d been taken by surprise. She walks over to him and the two hug. "Love the house," she tells him and in the back of his mind, he knew she would. "Kind of big, don't you think?" he asks her and she nods her head as they break their hug. She walks over to the bed and sits on the edge of it before crossing her legs. "Just a tad. I think you might need road signs to get over here," she tells him and he cracks a smile as he walks across his palatial room to the sliding glass door hidden by a curtain. He pulls it back and opens it to let the lovely Los Angeles air in. "Got a contractor already drawing it up," he says jokingly. There's a brief pause in the conversation, but long enough to cause Craig to turn around. Nicole's eyes are no longer looking at him, but at her shoes. She bites her bottom lip and then looks back at Craig. "I guess we need to talk," she tells him and he nods. "That's normally what people do after they kiss each other out of the random blue," he responds, referring to the evening ten days ago when Nicole surprised him with a kiss outside of his restaurant. It had been opening night, and one of pure mayhem. Yet, they had been able to pull it off, and as they walked outside to take a breather, she grabbed him by the arm, and kissed him. He returned the favor. Then, he had to catch a plane, and they hadn't talked about it since. "Look, I know you've got a lot going on in your li--," she begins to tell him, but he holds his hand up. "No, not here. We'll do dinner. I've got a thing to get to and I don't want this conversation in the back of my mind. But, I want you to know, I’m interested in seeing where this goes," he tells her and she understands. She nods his head as she gets up off the bed and walks over to him. His words though are what she had hoped to hear. "Training for your big match?" she asks him, moving on, and he sighs for a few moments. He hasn't even thought about it. "Oh, yeah. Brandon Youngblood in the main event. How fun is that?" he asks her sarcastically and Nicole just chuckles. Youngblood would be his first opponent since returning, and it would be in the Jewel in the Crown tournament; his first step towards greatness. No longer does he care about the trivial things. He only cares about being the best ever. "So, what is it that you're doing? What could be so important to drag you away from your new house with your friends here, ready to eat food?" Nicole asks him and he takes a quick glance at her before looking back out at the window. This would be exceptionally difficult for him as he hates having to lie to her. "I've got a meeting with Josh," he lies to her, referring to his agent. "Fun," she says sarcastically. He then turns around and looks at her to see if she believes him. Thankfully, she does, and he takes great comfort in that knowledge. Yet, he wouldn't be meeting with Josh. Instead, he's taking a meeting with the judge and attorney who look to end his life if they had it their ways. He starts to walk towards her and hugs her again. "Look, I'm not going to be around for a few days as I'll be up in Canada doing some promotional stuff and then I've got my match. When I get back though on Friday, do you want to do dinner?" he asks her and she pauses for a brief second before giving an answer. "Who's going to watch the restaurant?" she asks him. She hates having to leave the restaurant. He laughs at her comment before shaking his head. "Put Dave in charge. He'll be fine," he tells her. She thinks about it for a few more moments before nodding her head. "Yeah, that sounds good," she tells him and he kisses her on the forehead before rushing out of his room so he can make his appointment on time. Meanwhile, Nicole just stands there in the room all by herself and takes a quick look around. She walks to the window and looks out at the view that could've easily come from a postcard. In the back of her mind, she asks herself a simple question. Why'd he lie to me? ---- Craig sits in the large conference room wearing a pair of slacks and a black sweater. To say he's nervous is an understatement. At any given moment, he could be sentenced to jail or death for the rest of his life. The past months have given him a chance to come to terms with what he had done, and while he wasn't in complete control of himself when these crimes took place, he accepted responsibility. Yet, in the midst of it all, he’s found reasons to live, and he wants to see them through. A door opens behinds him and as he turns his head, he sees the man that's destined to ruin his life. Daniel Santiago, the lead detective from the FBI on his case. The pure hatred spews from Craig’s eyes as he just wishes Daniel was dead. The tension in the room only gets worse as several attorneys walk in followed by the judge proceeding over the case. Something seemed different about her today as she sits down in front of everyone. She slams the file onto her desk and looks at the prosecution. "For months, I've been asking for this evidence that you felt would incriminate the defendant beyond all doubt. This is what you give me? Information from a man who is largely regarded as the boss of the criminal underworld?! You expect me to accept this?!" she asks them and Daniel is taken aback by this piece of news. The prosecutor looks at Daniel and the judge before standing up. "Your honor," she begins to say, but is hastily cut off by the judge. "No, I'm done with these private proceedings. You're lucky that these hearings weren't made public because if they had been then you two would be embarrassed. I'm throwing this case out. Good night," the judge says and in one swift movement, she walks out of the conference room. Craig just sits there, stunned by this piece of news. It had felt like a dream, an unrealistic dream at that, but it had all been real. Minutes pass by as the room empties out, and Craig just sits there, a new man. This isn't one of the outcomes he planned for when he woke up this morning. Slowly, he gets out of his chair and begins to walks towards the exit. It was time for him to put his life back on track and focus on the thing he loved the most. Wrestling. King of Kings could do that once and for all for him as he looks to become the king he’s been dreaming of since the age of thirteen. Not the king of a title or anything, but the king of wrestling. No longer does he have to worry about trivial bullshit. Now, he would have a chance to become what he knew he is capable of, becoming the best; becoming the greatest; becoming the king. As he walks outside, he sees Daniel just standing there, an intense hatred in his eyes as he watches Craig walk down the steps. Craig's red eyes look at him for a brief moment, but as he walks past him, he does the only thing he can. He closes this chapter of his life.
