Momma Don't Like No Small Scene

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Revolution 166 No fancy fade, just picture. We start uncomfortably close to the angular features of the Faceless Fighter, yin/yang eyes sparked with the kind of mad intensity only she can muster. Cozen: It still works -- YAYS! Cut. When the picture returns, the room is so dark, sound plays a bigger role in discovering where the Crazy has us than sight. A faucet leaks nearby. Vinyl crinkles. The camera rests on a marble countertop. A woman groans. Cozen: Goodie, you're up. I thought you were gonna sleep all day, lazyface! Two orange pinpricks of candlelight bring the scene into focus; if you guessed "bathroom" earlier, good on ya. It's rather spacious, decorated in reds and whites; the camera points at the bathtub (it's got jets!). It's also got a woman in it, judging from those long, tanned legs. Eagle-eyed viewers will notice she's wearing pantyhose -- chances are, she's not taking a bath. The shower curtain hides her identity from the view and whatever's kept her from struggling thickens her voice beyond recognition. Prisoner: Where...Cozen? I...what...why? A dark-haired woman steps -- skips -- onscreen, in blue jeans and a white blouse. Cozen: That's an awful lot of questions. I need you to take a deeeep breath and try to chillax. Prisoner: You want me to relax? You abducted me! Cozen: Oh, c'mon. You're being all "RAR". You're at about a seven; I need you at about a four. The former champion's "guest" doesn't seem eager to calm down. A long-fingered hand grips the tub's side, weakly attempting to climb out. Cozen perches at the edge of the fiberglass, firing in an elbow. Cozen: Okay, hon, you're being kind of a selfish bitch right now. Here I went and got a camera and everything. Prisoner: You fucking hit me! Cozen: Uhm. Duh. Prisoner: Nobody told me you'd hit me! There's a great deal of indignation in the woman's voice for someone lying drowsily in the Crazy's tub. Cozen: Nobody told you a lot, sugarbear. Prisoner: But... your match with Shakur is this week. You're supposed to be training, preparing! There's a strategy worked out and -- Cozen's head tilts slightly to the side; it's been described as like a dinosaur or a bird. Really, it's like she's considering if you're the crazy person. Cozen: I ate those pages you sent me. I'm not a good soldier, babystuff. I'm not excited to goosestep to your drumbeat and win back my belt and march into Colossus twice the Universal -- She covers her mouth in a playful yawn, lips contorted in a smile that couldn't possibly be wider. Cozen: It's, like, hello! I'm so over your gobbledygook Prisoner: Don't lie to me. I was there when you beat Troy -- Fists accompany words. Cozen: YOU! DO NOT! SAY HER NAME! The shout reverberates throughout the bathroom; the play and light leaves the Crazy's voice when she shouts. Her victim scrunches down, groaning. Prisoner: You need me -- need us. We keep you on the path. Cozen: You mean the path you want. The path leading through Devin. I had him, you know. I had him. I told the Beautiful Lie; his body believed. I would have stood up, but that BITCH.... The woman's voice softens further, ragged from thirst. Prisoner: Don't you hate him? He has what's yours. The former champion looks down. Cozen: I'm not marketable. You won't sell dolls with my face on them -- any of them. No catchphrases or wit. Too dangerous to go on Rachel Ray -- I like her. Cantrell wants Chandler. Cantrell wants Wade. Someone yummo to bite into and have a good taste. Prisoner: But Al- Her captor shakes a finger. Cozen: [singsong] Fiiiiiilming. [eyes to the camera] You needed to see this. Prisoner: All of us believe. Together, we can regain your place. Silence reigns. Cozen: I'm tired. Tired of being "cute". Of being "wacky". I'm not a good soldier. I'm not a good champion. I'm not even a good crazy person. Dusk hasn't seen me. Bitchwhore... hasn't seen me. Not really. Saw what they wanted, what was safe. Keep it secret; keep it safe. The Crazy rises, lifting the camera until her yin-yang gaze fills the screen. Cozen: I don't need you. Don't need anyone. If you love something, set it free; if it returns, it's yours forever. If not? It was never meant to be. I like Devin Shakur; he's just in the way. As long as I have the belt, I have something she wants. I keep it from her... I win. The brow above her black eye slowly rises. Cozen: What I do on Wednesday... isn't personal. I don't want to hurt Devin. Grind his bones to make my bread. But I have to, you see? No, silly -- not to prevent Chandler from doing it in Devin's hometown. She's walking away from the tub. Feet slap along tile wetly. Cozen: But to keep it away from you, Lindsay. I know you're watching, as well. Probably on DVR, right after Gossip Girl. I'm not the loveable maniac. I'm not the friendly nutjob. I'm a teacher; class is in session. I'm taking Devin's title, because you took my toy from me. The camera swings, finally peeking back, upside-down, at the woman in the tub. Her name is Siena van der Wal. Cozen: And I'm never giving it back. Fin.
