Bringing The Band Back Together (Part 3, Alexandria Malone)

20 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: February 20th, 2007 Alexandria Malone. Essentially speaking, this young girl was the half-sister of a wrestler named Joey Malone. To be perfectly blunt, Joey didn't know she existed for about fourteen years, until his estranged father told him on his deathbed. Yeah, I know, that's called "jumping the shark", but then again, much of Joey Malone's life consisted of jumping the shark, and then going off to find more sharks to jump over. Joey was twice a World champion, once in the Internet Wrestling Organization and once in Action! Wrestling. He then retired from wrestling in May of 2003 in the interest of family, and currently was the head of a construction crew in Phoenix. It's an odd change from wrestling, but he seemed to be happier building things than risking further injury to the left leg that always nagged him. Joey was known for the very small amount of wrestlers who trained under him... in point of fact, there were only two true trainees under Joey. One was a young man who currently wrestled in Japan. The other was currently knocking on his front door. "I don't think Joey's going to like this." Robert Falk commented as he leaned against the suburban home of Joseph Tyler Malone. He was standing behind Codemaster and his brother, and generally looked around the suburb. Joey's neighborhood was fairly quiet, outside of the sounds of children playing. In fact, the only unusual thing in the entire neighborhood happened to be Robert, himself. The Codemaster glanced behind him, evaluating the situation, before he came to a conclusion, "Yeah. You're right." He turned to Scott, "Maybe you should wait in the car, Scotty." Scott Falk did a double take and nearly fell off the path to the door and into the plants placed next to the door's alcove. He managed to recover, however, and he pointed an accusing finger, "Hey! Fuck you, it's a van! Anyway, why the fuck do I stay the fuck behind and he doesn't?!" "Well," the Codemaster said, as he placed a hand on his chin, "If I had to venture a guess, Scott, I think maybe it's that mouth of yours." Scott Falk crossed his arms and began the walk back to the Codemaster's car, "Man, fuck this. I didn't come all the way out here to get insulted by Allen McFuckface. Come on, Robert." Robert Falk sighed and rolled his eyes, before he ceased his lean against the wall and walked back to the car as well. This left the Codemaster alone to deal with the Malone family. The Codemaster, being the first of Joey's two trainees, had a certain amount of comradeship with Joey. Even though as a wrestler, he was radically different from both Joey and the other trainee, the Codemaster kept up contact with Joey Malone throughout his career. After all, to be trained by Joey Malone was, essentially, the same as being let into his family. The door opened. It was neither Joey nor Alexandria who opened the door, however, but it was a woman. This woman's hair was brown and curly, though it was clearly only dyed brown (her natural color being blonde). She wore a simple white shirt and a pair of jeans, but she also appeared several months into pregnancy. Her name was Keri Malone, and she was Joey's wife of roughly five years. Keri was Joey's manager in the old days, but she was also his girlfriend at the same time. She despised both the wear and tear that the business put on Joey, and the constant travel associated with wrestling. She was the first of the couple to quit the business, quitting about five months ahead of Joey himself. The Codemaster nodded towards the smaller quarry, "Hey, Keri." "Allen," Keri said, with her own nod of recognition, "What brings you here?" "Ahhh... how should I put this?" the Codemaster asked, lamenting on the subject for a few seconds. If he made a wrong move here, he could forget about bringing Alexandria to the fold. He had to be careful. He had to be precise. He had to... "I'm looking for Alexandria so I can get her to join my party consisting of a ragtag band of misfits." the Codemaster stated, flatly. God *dammit*, Codemaster. Keri blinked at the man known in some cultures as the Hardcore Lunar Legend. She sighed, before saying, "She's in her room. Come with me." So, suffice to say, and just to save myself some time and energy describing the Malone household, the two went straight to the room occupied by Alexandria Malone. Right away, the Codemaster knew he was entering territory that did not accomodate black video game addicts who excelled primarilly at trickery and deceit, and this was without opening the door. That's because there was a sign on the front of the door, written in ominous letters. "STAY OUT." Not very accomodating, was it? Keri nodded to the Codemaster, "If you need me, I'm going to be in the kitchen." The Codemaster's attention snapped away from the ominous sign in front of Alexandria's door and towards Joey Malone's wife who was already walking away, "You're not going in there with me?!" Keri didn't even look back at the Codemaster, but she instead said, "Added stress would be bad for the baby." The Codemaster rolled his eyes, breathed a heavy sigh, and stared at the doorway that would be either his salvation or his damnation. Slowly, the Codemaster raised his right hand and lowly rapped on the door. There was a pause, before the doorknob on the other side of the door was harshly grasped, turned, and then pulled out. This caused the door to come flying away from the Codemaster's face, and the door was soon replaced by the visage of a young girl. Alexandria Malone. The Codemaster lamented to himself that he had first met Alexandria when she was still thirteen years old, and about to turn fourteen. She still clearly looked and acted like a child at the time. The Codemaster remembered that the little girl had such a crush on Marcus Alexander Dox, also known as Max Danger, one of Coral Avalon's best friends. What a difference four years made. Alexandria had grown up, now an eighteen-year-old beauty and the sister of a reputable wrestling legend. Her black hair tied into a ponytail, wearing a green tanktop and a pair of black pants, Alexandria had grown to be quite the attractive girl. However... Whamf. That's the sound of Alexandria rushing towards Codemaster, wrapping her arms around her, and giving him what could've amounted to a spear. The Codemaster, caught off guard, fell back and hit his back on the wall, causing the house to shake a bit. Alexandria looked up and smacked the Codemaster right in his chest. "You're late." she told him. "I am?" the Codemaster asked. "Darn right you are." Alexandria said, as she backed away and crossed her arms in front of her chest. She cocked her head to the side, giving off that same aura of attitude she always gave even as a child, "Big brother said you'd come by at some point." "He did, did he?" the Codemaster asked. This was one of the few times where his usual wit, which was often as sharp as a katana (we're thinking Masamune here), was ineffectual. Alexandria was as strong-willed as she was beautiful, and given how straight-forward she can be, even the Codemaster's flowery speeches about how one could defeat Emerald Weapon with a cardboard tube had no effect on him. "Yuuuup," Alexandria said, "He was all like, 'Allen's probably gonna come by and try to recruit you into some of his evil'. And he kinda made this 'grr' face when he said it." Alexandria mimicked the aforementioned "grr" face as best as she could. "I think he was either trying to talk me out of trying to tag along with you because of his stupid 'sense of moral code', or because he doesn't want me to be involved in that business that ruined his knee and almost ruined his family." Alexandria said, before she shrugged, "But I'm not real fond of that whole 'thinking' thing anyway." She gestured, upon saying thinking, as if she were quoting something. The Codemaster scratched the left side of his face in confusion, before he decided to open Pandora's Box, grow fifty feel tall, and fight Aries, the God of War, to the death. "This means what?" he asked. "It means, you big dummy, that I'm gonna come along with you regardless of what anyone says. I want to see for myself what the hell my dad and my brother nearly killed themselves over. If you're not okay with that, then I've got some super glue and I'll glue myself to you." Alexandria said with a narrowing of her eyes, as if she were extremely serious about the super glue thing. The Codemaster sighed in relief. This was easier than he thought. He leaned against the wall that he had previously been slammed against, and looked forward at the pretty tomboy who stood before him, "Well, get some things together. I'm driving along with the Falks right now, so be prepared to be hit upon by a guy who can't say a sentence without throwing in something foul." Alexandria smiled, utterly oblivious to the description of Scott Falk's behaviour, "Great! Be out in a second!" She quickly turned, entered her room again, and slammed the door shut. The Codemaster turned to his left to see Keri Malone standing there with her hands on her hips. To Keri's right, clinging to her pantleg, was two-year-old Holly Malone, the Malones' first daughter. Keri seemed slightly disapproving. "Joey definitely won't like this, Allen." Keri said. The Codemaster walked towards Keri and passed her by, but after passing her, he stopped walking and glanced back at Joey's pregnant wife, "Maybe." He turned back to walk out the front door, "But all cute little chicks will have to leave their nests at some point." Then the Codemaster walked out the door, and back out into the hot Phoenix sun that he had grown used to all those years ago. Afterwards, he pulled out his little notepad and pen, and wrote in a new name. Robert Falk. Scott Falk. *Alexandria Malone*. With this accomplished, the man known in some cultures as the Tethe'allan Rattlesnake headed for his van.
