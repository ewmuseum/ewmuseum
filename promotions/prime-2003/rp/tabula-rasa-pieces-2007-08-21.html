Date: August 21st, 2007 Tabula Rasa: Eternity Pieces Part Two of Thirteen December 31st, 2007 She walks in wearing a white blouse stained with blood. The skin directly under her eyes look soft, fresh with salt tears, and her eyes are blotchy and red. She walks without much life to her step. Just from her stance you can tell that things are downright grim for her. The house that surrounds her is dark except for the moonlight beaming in through one of the open windows. Her eyes scan her surroundings and she looks ready to break down again. However, somehow she is able to keep it all inside of her. Slowly, her fists ball up, and the anger she is feeling is a stronger feeling than anything else. Each step drags her over to a desk where she sits down and lets her thoughts stew for a moment. Then, she pulls out a pen and begins to write slowly until she finds her steam and pace, and let's go. For as long as life has been around, there have been stories that we categorize as mythologies and fables. Yet, we allow them to be known as that because we refuse to accept anything else. Just like the stories of Zeus and Ares were considered true thousands of years ago, we consider the stories of Adam and Eve, and Jesus Christ to be true now. Thousands of years from now, are they going to look at our stories and consider them mythology and folklore? Will there be a new set of literature out there that is considered the gospel and to be held in truth. Will religion go down a different road just like it has time and time again in history? The truth is that the stories that we read are more than stories. They're truths. Because sometimes the imagination cannot just cook up certain things. The story of Zeus and Athena is as real as you and me. The words behind the birth of Jesus Christ, the parting of the Red Sea for Moses, and the creation of our universe as told in the Bible is a factual tale of the world around us. There have been Gods and Goddesses who have inhabited this world. The only downside is that we refuse to let ourselves take a look out of the window and see what is really there. We think that if we read the gospels that we'll be safe, but we're ignoring the present by reading the past. I'm writing this as a promise to somebody who should be considered in the same breath as Zeus and God. Thousands of years from now, I hope his story is held in the same breath as our mythology, but instead of acting naive when you read it, you'll realize that what I'm writing is the truth. Because, there's a man, a God, that fights without care to himself. He looks for answers and has sought out to find the truth about not only our world, but himself and the universe around him. We read stories of heroes today and fail to see the heroes that are really there, doing things that we can only imagine of and see in the movies. This man is one of them with a story that is interesting to boot. This is why I write. To inform, to be a storyteller, but to never forget. Never forget the man with the weight of the world on his shoulders. This is his story. The pen rests in her hand for a moment as she thinks about it. Then, the memories of the past few months hit her like a truck racing at a hundred miles per hour, and it nearly knocks her to the ground. So much has happened that it's difficult for her to make sense of it all. She asks herself where she should start and then it's all perfectly clear to her. At the beginning. ---- August 7th, 2007 As my eyes open I ask myself if it was all a dream. I tell myself that it's not possible that I had been arrested and that I had dreamt it all. Yet, I know the truth. I know what actually happened. I swing my legs around and get up out of the bed before I walk out of the room and walk down the stairs to my kitchen. Sitting in there is Katie who is waking up with what would appear to be tea. She smiles at me and I nod my head as I refuse to stop on my way out to the garage. Over the years I had turned my garage into one of those gyms that you would see in a boxing movie, like Million Dollar Baby. In one of the corners is a rather large punching bag and my anger is starting to boil over. I walk up to the bag and swing at it with all of might. As my fist collides with the anger I can feel everything just unleash as the chain snaps into two pieces and the bag falls to the ground. My eyes land on the bag and from behind me I can feel Katie's eyes on my back wondering what's wrong outside of the obvious. "Everything okay?" she asks, but I can't answer. Not now. My throat is constricted, the rage starting to rush through me. I had done this to myself, I knew that much, but it didn't mean I couldn't feel angry at all. I had been controlled, tricked, and not in my right mind. Hell, according to Santiago I didn't even have memories of it all. Now, I'm facing charges of the murder of two hundred people, and a vindictive detective wanted to put the needle in my arm myself. Things could be worse though. He could be locked up in a jail cell, his career having been ruined, and wouldn't be able to find out the answers that gnawed at him. Even with time running short, he had a few leads he could play with, and that made him happy. He needed answers desperately. He turns around and looks at Katie, and nods his head. "Things are better now," he tells her and from the look on her face she wants to laugh. "Well, that's good. I figured that punching the bag would help," she responds and I walk up to her, tapping her on the shoulder, and then walk back inside of the house. I immediately walk over to the counter and pick up my cell phone. I see several phone calls that I've missed, but I ignore them all. "So, what route is next?" she asks me and I look at her for a brief second, putting my words together. "Last year, when I seemingly awoke from a long sleep, I went to Europe and met with a man that seemed to have some answers. He told me a story about another world, almost as if it was a fairy tale, but something tells me that it was far from that. He knew me from years prior and I think there're things he's not telling me, things he's keeping from me on purpose. If he doesn't have the answers, he will know who does though," I tell her and she nods her head. "And what about Daniel? You think he's just going to let you out of the country?" she asks me. I hadn't thought of that. "We'll have to figure something out. I can't sit in waiting while this goes on, especially when I know I'm guilty. It kind of defeats the whole purpose," I tell her and she doesn't like the answer. She takes a sip of her tea as her eyes begin to narrow. "That's your solution? To just walk down the gallows and let it all be over?" she asks me on the verge of tears. I stand there, knowing no words will be good enough to calm her down. I bite my bottom lip as I look at my cell phone and then decide against jotting down a note to my manager. I have to start acting as if I'm being watched constantly. Instead, if I'm going to get out of the country then I'm going to have to do it my way. I look back at her, the silence filling the room, and shake my head. I walk away and leave her there for better or worse. Eventually, she'll come to terms with it all. I know I have. ---- You wake up; sweat pouring down your face. A dream is what you tell yourself; just a dream. Yet, it didn't feel like a dream. It felt like real life, too vivid, too realistic, too much. Your left hand reaches up to your forehead and you try to wipe the sweat off of your forehead, but there's too much. It's drenched as is the rest of your body. Your breath is shallow and you try to calm yourself down with no avail. Slowly, you try to move your legs off of the side of the bed, but you're unable to do so. No energy. Your tongue washes over your lips and your eyelids close as your tepid mind works overtime to move your muscles. They begin to move, but with much pain. An old adage crosses your thoughts, no pain no gain and you toughen through it. You can feel them moving and you do your best to block out the pain. As your feet touch the soft, recently cleaned, carpet you see a flash before your very eyes. An attempt to scream is made, but your throat is constricted from dehydration. Images race along your vision and you remember them from your dream. The pain becomes worse and worse as the memories begin to flood back to you. It's like a movie is being played for you, but unfortunately you're unable to turn it off. At first it's just small things. You can't control it as if the pictures are a separate entity. A dark cave; a woman dressed in a white dress with fear etched on her face. Blood, trickling out of her wrist, and there is a group of people huddled in front of her. Black cloaks. They're intimidating. Reason enters your thoughts and you fight with all of your might to stop thinking about the dream; the nightmare. Instead, you focus upon just getting out of the bed. Your soft blue eyes glance over to the clock and make a mental note of the time; 3:30AM. Far too early. In the back of your mind you begin to think about what you had before you went to sleep when you realize that you didn't have anything; you just need a reason to explain this explicit dream. Once again, you try to get out of your bed, and this time you have better results. Your muscles don't ache as badly as before and you feel the strength beginning to return to you. Each step is a little easier as you continue to brace yourself upon the nearby wall. As you reach the leather chair in the room, the flashes return and your right hand grips the armrest; if it had been an arm you would've broken skin, and possibly bruised one of the bones. There's a knife in one of the man's hands; you're unsure of which one. A mark on his left hand near his rest. He takes the knife and makes a small incision on his left wrist. You feel a pinch on your left wrist as if he had cut your wrist instead. Your left hand curls into a ball from the reaction. He is speaking, or at least his lips are moving, but there are no words coming out. Your ears strain to hear him, hope to understand what's going on, but it's no use. Silence fills your mind and it begins to frustrate you. Your eyes glance over towards the woman and see the fear still etched on her face. You get the idea that she has no idea why she's here. With desperation having soaked you, you want to help the woman out, but find that the energy in your body has left you. The flash disappears again and you can feel even more strength in your body. You take another step and another, each step an accomplishment. Every inch of your body is glad at the ability to move after feeling helpless just seconds ago. There's a certain freedom that washes over you and as you step into the bathroom you're able to flip on a light switch with such ease that you want to cry. You look at yourself in the mirror and wipe the strands of hair away from your face. Your eyes are drenched with exhaustion and the sweat has covered your entire face. The right hand moves towards the towel, but as you touch it you're blinded by another vision. You're lucky to catch yourself before you collapse on the hard tile floor. Another man steps forward and for the first time, you see fear etched in their faces. Something has happened, something not expected in the least bit. You're unsure of what this could be, but you can tell one thing. It's not a good thing, whatever they're fearful of. You then are able to feel pity for them as they've been driven to an end that they had hoped not to go down. There's actual pity in their hearts from having to do this to the woman. Then, a searing hot pain comes over your entire body. You feel like you're being watched, but you know there's nobody there. Yet, red eyes enter your mind's eye, and your entire body begins to tremble. It's what they're scared of. A beast or an animal. Something with red eyes, it's coming to get them. You now understand why they're doing what they're doing. Preservation. As you snap back to reality you contemplate just downing a whole bottle of pills to end it all. It's becoming far too much and you don't have the slightest clue as to what it's supposed to mean or when it will stop happening. Instead, you pull the towel off of the rack and wipe off your face. In the back of your mind you try to remember what the woman looks like with little luck. Instead, you're completely focused upon the red eyes, menacing and hateful, filled with rage. Part of you begins to feel like if you saw those red eyes in person that you would die right there on the spot. The fear is so strong that a wave of nausea hits you. Then, it happens again. You can see the woman's face. Similar to your own, but unique in its own fashion. Tears are streaming down her face as the knife is placed against her throat. Then, she's lifeless. Nothing. Emptiness. The pity you once had for them is replaced with rage. You know why they fear the thing that they do. Because they brought it upon the world. It's pitiful. You wish the animal upon them. Then, you see a scenario much like what's happening to you now. A woman stands in the bathroom, pained and tired as she tries to wash her hands. Then, a hand comes over her mouth. And there's darkness. ---- August 9th, 2007 An ancient house sits on top of a hill, trees surrounding it to give it a rather haunted and rustic look, and a gate sits 100 yards away from it to deter visitors from even thinking about coming up to look at it closer. Just looking from the outside you wouldn’t think that anyone even lives here as there are no visible lights on, no cars in the driveway, and you certainly wouldn’t think that this house is legendary. Yet, inside of the house sits two men who’re agonizing over a painful decision, one that doesn’t come lightly to them. They sit across from each other, a large wooden oak table separating them, as they ponder over a very simple question. "We need to bring them back together, Samael," one of the men says in a rather hushed town. Samael, sitting across from him, looks at him warily. He wonders as to what Samael is getting at and sits back in his chair. "I already told you, Abel, that they’re dead. The Order is dead," Samael responds and immediately, Abel shakes his head at him. He gets up out of the chair and begins to walk closer to Samael. "No, not them. The Illuminati," he responds and Samael looks at Abel flabbergasted. He gets out of his chair and walks up to Abel, wondering if he is delusional or something. He looks Abel right in the eyes and stands there for a moment before looking away. Surrounding the two are paintings that had been painted centuries ago by artists like Da Vinci and Michelangelo. "You’re not in your right mind, Abel. Not in your mind--," he begins to say before he’s cut off. "So, the right mind should be to let Craig just come and kill us?!" Abel yells back and Samael scoffs at this notion. "You know that not to be. Even if Craig is able to figure out who we are, and who honestly knows that, he’ll never be able to find us. We have centuries, millennia’s, of history working in our favor. Craig’s powers are infantile compared to ours and you know that, regardless of the scriptures and prophecies. On top of that--," Samael begins to say once again before he’s cut off by Abel who seems anxious and nervous all rolled into one. "Don’t you dare say it,’ Abel tells Samael, offending Samael. "Don’t I dare say it?! Don’t speak the truth?! Craig isn’t like the others, Samael! He’s far too angry!" he yells back at Abel who doesn’t seem to be listening, instead in his own world. "You’re far too naïve if that’s your reasoning behind it, Samael. The fact that the anger inside of him is at this point is the biggest reason that we have to bring the Illuminati back together! That anger isn’t from him, it’s from--," Abel says before he’s cut off this time. "Don’t. You’re putting too much weight into him. You fear something that you shouldn’t fear. On top of the anger that clouts his judgment, he’s unable to use any of the powers that have been bestowed upon him. He will never be able to use them, and those are needed if he wants any chance to defeat us!" he yells back and the two are at a standoff. Abel turns from Samael, needing a moment to himself. "So, that’s your advice? Just sit around because he doesn’t have the ability to find us or the powers to kill us? Great planning if you ask me. You don’t understand how determined he is right now! You don’t have that access like I DO!" he yells back, turning back towards Samael and spit flying at him. "If you understood him at all then we wouldn’t be in this predicament and you know it," he finishes saying and Samael stands there for a moment, unsure of what to say. After a few moments of silence, the pictures seemingly watching them, he nods his head before speaking. "Fine, bring them together, but bring them together at your own cost. You know that they don’t like to be summoned for things like this. But, if you insist, fine, go ahead. Just know that it’s your death that is coming if you do," Samael tells Abel and then walks away. Abel stands there, watching as he walks away, and nods his head. Once Samael is out of range of his voice, he says one last thing, one thing that if Samael heard him say then it would be Abel’s death. "It’s your death that is coming Samael. So it has been told," he says and then he turns on the spot, and walks the opposite way, knowing that preparations must be made if he is to summon the Illuminati. ---- August 11th, 2007 The room seemingly engulfs me as I step inside for the first time in eight months. None of it has seemingly changed. I take a few steps, taking it all in, and wonder to myself where everyone could be. The silence is alarming to me as it was never this quiet in my time here. My red eyes glance across the room and strain my ears to find some semblance of life. As I begin to walk up the nearby stairs that leads to where the Master is, I feel a pain explode in my back as I drop to my knees and nearly pass out from the tension in my back. "Welcome home," a voice whispers in my ear before the back of my head is grabbed and slammed into the stone stairs. I grunt from the attack and try to turn over, but another shot of electricity is sent through my body and I can feel my body begin to spasm from the voltage being sent through my body. I can barely even control my body as I begin to yell out without any warning. Immediately, I hear the footsteps against the stone floor and can feel the eyes looking at me. In the back of my mind, I already know who's attacking me and as my eyes land upon her, I see the woman that has wanted to kill me from the very day that I walked in here ten months ago. "Melody," I manage to say through gritted teeth and a smile appears over her face before she kicks me in the face. I can feel the blood begin to trickle out of my nose and she kneels down in front of me. "So nice to see you again, Craig. Sorry it has to be the last time we see you though," she whispers into my air before she jams a metal rod into my throat and another wave of lightning is sent through my body. I continue to shake and feel the pain building up. Some of the students in the room watch on in awe while others are fearful of the display before them. I try to fight back, but it's no use as she has complete control over the situation. I feel the cold metal leave my throat and I can feel the skin, now raw, over my throat ache from the pain. "Nice... to... see--," I begin to say, but can't speak any longer. The pain is too much as I feel my very consciousness slipping away from me. I try to move my hand, but I've lost all feeling from the top of my head to my toes. I'm thankful that my eyes are able to move and that I haven't passed out because I'm certain that Melody intends to kill me. She walks away from me for a brief second and picks something up that I can't see. As she turns around, I see another rod, this one much larger than the one that was in her hands before, that she intends to use upon me. There's a claw on the end of it and I just know that I have no desire to be attacked with that one. She slowly walks up to me and kneels before me again. "Why have you come back, Craig? To cause more damage? More pain?" she asks me and I try to form the words, but she knows that no words will be coming out. She stands back up and lunges forward with the rod as it begins to rip at my stomach. She twists and turns as the claw starts to rip away my stomach. Once again, my body begins to involuntarily spasm as she enjoys the pain she's causing me. My eyes begin to close as I feel myself losing my grip on reality. I continue to fight though, refusing to go down this easily. As my eyes open back up, I can feel the fire inside of them as they land upon Melody who just looks at them with no fear. It's one of the few times that I've seen someone look at them with no fear, and it startles me slightly. "Oh, you're going to have to do better than that," she tells me before she looks at the students. As she looks at some of them, I can see those who must be her favorites. One of them is a boy who is several years younger than me. He looks at me as if I was his prey. "Jonathan, pick him up," she tells the boy and he walks over to me eagerly. He's stronger then he looks as he picks me up off the stone floor and holds me up. I see Melody walking away from me and she picks up a wooden stick this time. In the back of my mind I wonder how long this will go on before the Master puts an end to this or until she just kills me. Her arms swing back as she smashes the stick across my face and I nearly fall from the pain. If it wasn't for Jonathan I would have fallen, but he holds me up as Melody swings back and nails me in the face once again. My eyes begin to flicker again as I know I can't take much more of this. The blood begins to drip down my face and I know I've lost a good amount of it. She looks at me and she continues to smile as she swings the stick across my chest and I can feel the blood rising in my throat from the internal injuries. Part of me wants to die right here, wanting it all to be over. However, the other part of me is roaring with fury, and wants to fight back. Yet, my muscles don't move as they're lifeless. "Jonathan, would you like to try the electrocuter on him? That way you can see how it works on a person's body," she tells him and I just know that he's nodding his head. Another one of the students moves down the stairs and takes Jonathan's place. From the feeling of the hands I can tell that it's a female even though I can't see her face. Melody then places the metal rod in Jonathan's hands and he looks at it in awe. It's sickening. She whispers something in his ear, and then backs up as he looks at me with a thirst of blood in his eyes. He pauses for a second and then runs at me with the metal rod aimed for my face. I think that this is going to be all over, but something happens. My right arm rips out of the female's hold, and I grab the boy's arm with the electrocuter in it and jam it into her face. It only takes a few seconds as I can hear her last breath leave her. I let go of the boy as I drop to one knee. He looks at the fallen body with tears in his eyes and I can see his body begin to tremble with anger. He turns towards me and lunges the object at me again. Somehow, I'm able to move away and slam my other knee into his stomach. I feel two of his ribs shatter from the impact. Behind me I can feel Melody moving towards me and I immediately snap my body around and nail her with a roundhouse kick that floors her. Truthfully, I have no idea where this sudden burst of strength has come from, but I let it take over me. The boy tries to attack me again, but I grab his body and slam it onto the ground. I drop to one knee again and Melody gets back up to her feet. I look at her as my right hand grips onto the electrocuter and she only looks on in fear. Her eyes wander over to her pupil and I pause for a second before I lash out in anger and jam the object into his heart. I only hold it there for a brief second as I can feel his life leave him too. "You brought this upon yourself," I tell Melody after a few moments of silence. She runs at me and I try to get up to nail her with the electrocuter as well, but she slams her elbow into my throat and I drop it without warning. She then kicks me in the midsection, but I'm able to catch her fist as she tries to punch me in the face. Then, I knock her legs out from under her and her back collides with the jagged edges of the stairs and she loses her breath. I then slam my foot against her back and send her rolling down the set of stairs. Two people are dead already and I'm certain a third will join them tonight. I just hope it's not me. I limp down the stairs and she is back up on her feet as she lunges at me, but I'm able to dodge her. As I duck from her attack, I'm able to pick up the claw rod and with my left leg, I slam my knee into her face and create some space between us. As she turns back towards me though, I lunge with the rod and the claw wraps around her throat. For a brief moment, I can see the fear in her face as the anger inside of me takes over completely. I grip down on the claw and can see the life fade from her eyes as I rip out her throat with the claw and I can only watch as her body collapses. Slowly, the wave of anger passes over and I can feel nausea sweep in. Everyone in the room can only watch and I can see fear in some of their faces while others are elated at this turn of events. Then, I feel somebody else behind me, and I hope I don't have to fight. "Thank you," a feeble voice says from behind me and as I turn around, I see the person that I've come to see. "Master," I mutter and he smiles at me. It's the last image I see before I pass out from the pain. ---- London, England May 1347 A haze covers the already darkened sky. Screams fill the air as people continue to run from the mayhem that surrounds them. The scent of fear is evident in the atmosphere as another explosion is heard in the distance. A man stands in the center of it all, amazed by the confusion and the hectic pace. Something about it all makes him feel alive for the first time in a many of years. He licks the bottom of his lips as he looks down at the ground and sees something silvery laying there. His legs bend at the knees as he lowers himself to see what it is and picks it up in his hands. The item has some weight behind it, but he doesn't care. It feels like it's an extension of him, an organ that's been missing for a long time; too long. As he stands up he feels a power rush through him that is foreign to him. "Maria! MARIA!" a yell pierces through his thoughts and turns around wondering where the noise is coming from. A man runs towards him and he can see the fright in his eyes as if he's lost something or someone very dear to him. "Sir, sir, my name is John, and I need to find my wife. Have you seen a small brown haired girl with a scratch on her face? Oh, please, tell me that you have!" he tells the man and he shakes his head. Never does he think to form words because to him they're foreign. John looks at this mysterious man and his head drops low. He had hoped that someone had seen her in all of this pandemonium. Instead, there was nobody. "Thank you," John says to the man and begins to walk away. After four or five steps though, he pauses and turns back to the man holding the sword who hasn't moved except to look at the sword. There's a look in John's eye, one of caution and hesitancy. He begins to walk back to the man and asks himself who this random person is. "Aren't you the man who lived on Culver Street?" he asks the man and as the man turns towards John there is a definitive look in this man's eyes; one that makes John think twice about standing there any longer. Yet, his legs aren't moving. Another explosion goes off near them, but even the bomb going off doesn't scare John as much as the man before him does. With each step that the man takes, a sense of death begins to wash over John. "Please," John whispers to the man and a million different thoughts fill John's head as to what to tell this man. He has a family, a wife, children, and his whole life to look forward to. He tries to tell the man that as he pulls back his sword, but nothing comes out. His mouth is dry and even as he tries to make the faintest sound, it's all for nothing. He feels the sword tearing through his abdomen and feels the pain wash over him. The man across from him looks at John and sees his eyes get wide. This look is surprising to the man as he's never seen a look like this before. It amuses him. John's hands grab onto the blade, but it's no good. His life is fading before his very eyes and there's nothing he can do about it. The man can only watch as John collapses to his knees. It's only by instinct that he pulls the sword out of John's abdomen and he looks on as John falls face first into the dirt. His eyes look at the stained blade and he is intrigued by it. He raises it to his face and looks at it with wonder and amazement. His fingers graze over the blade and the blood stains his hands. He then moves his hand closer to his face so he can see the blood up close, and for the first time in a while, he feels a sense of calm come over him. It's like he finally has found his purpose in life. He takes a step and another explosion goes off behind him. Yet, it doesn't startle him. He takes it for being part of life. A world of confusion. A world of destruction. As he takes another step, he gets the feeling that somebody is behind him. He turns around and a man stands 50 yards away from him looking like he owns the world around him. Another bomb goes off and yet neither of the men flinches. Even with the sword in his hands he feels compelled to bow to this man. It doesn't make any sense to him, but as the man begins to walk towards him he drops the sword and kneels before. He lowers his head and waits for the man to come to him. The man is then in front of him and looks down upon the man, his eyes gleaming with hope as he sees the blood on his blade. "You have arrived, haven't you? Oh good, oh good," he begins to speak in a rich baritone voice. There's a regality about him that makes him seem like royalty. "There's much for me to teach you, and yet so little time to do it in. But, so far," he continues to speak before looking off at John, dead in the dirt, "You have done well." The man finally looks up and feels like a child to the man standing above him. He welcomes it though instead of fighting it. He's never seen the man before, but it feels right. "Now, rise, and take your place at my right hand, Adonis," the man says and slowly he rises. Adonis looks out at the world before him, a stranger to it, but feels a part of it nonetheless. In his heart rages anger. Anger for a man. Anger for Ares. To Be Continued.
