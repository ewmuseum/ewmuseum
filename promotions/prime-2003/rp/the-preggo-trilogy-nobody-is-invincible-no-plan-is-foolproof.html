The Preggo Trilogy: Nobody is Invincible, No Plan is Foolproof.

Date: CWMC Final c the present... He's ready. Sure, it's only taken three hours, two changes of water, four grams of marijuana and eighteen tokes from the bong; but the Face-Eater has finally smoked himself courageous. Most of the tasks he usually finds himself tackling necessitate no more than a blunt and a beer, then he's ready to go. But this isn't a usual task. Every time the Face-Eater's nerves got the best of him, all it took was a little substance abuse. When he needed to be in the right frame of mind to find victory in the 24th Round of the Infinite Gauntlet, Mary Jane carried him all the way. Last year, when he faced Nova in the Bong on a Pole match at Colossus III, it was the mid-match smokefest that enabled him to edge out the big W. A few weeks ago, when he needed weed to prepare his mind for the big Unified Title Match with Jason Snow, his dealer was completely dry. We all know what happened there. Needless to say, Adam Dick and Marijuana's relationship can be described as intrinsic at best. And after the smoke session he just treated himself to, a betting man would be called foolish to go against his determination. For three months, the Illustrious Face-Eater has been unsuccessful in the bedding of a pregnant woman. Tonight, he knows, will be different. As he travels down the sidewalk with a skip in his step, he can't help but think about all of the success he's experienced in his very short lifetime. What began as an attempt to win a father's love has since transformed into a lavish lifestyle that he could have never imagined. Money, notoriety, women and extravagance follows him wherever he goes. Countless foes have fallen under his size ten-and-a-half boot. Whether they be Jason Snow, Jacob McKail, Ember, John Covel, or even the mighty Kimbusa; each of their shoulders were on the mat for three-seconds, all because of Adam Dick. Though success has come in many forms, the perks of celebrity have still been unable to sate the Face-Eater's thirsts. Victory, money, even a surplus of drugs: all of these things can contribute to his being, but they cannot make him whole. What can complete him, however, is the precise thing that has been evading him for the past three months. That ends now. In a few hours, Facey will have his last match in the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, in the final bout with Digital Mortality. Antonio Vargas (del Valle) is the final step in achieving wrestling immortality. Unfortunately, this is the same man who defeated him in the last round—the only man to give him a loss in this tournament so far—nicknamed the PRIME Killa for a reason. Whether the Face-Eater can defeat Digital Mortality is not a question he should be asking himself, it's THE question. It's too bad Adam Dick has never been one to worry about wrestling matches. If he can put his mind in the right place, there is no questioning his ability to defeat such a formidable opponent. But the Face-Eater could give two shits about winning the match. All he wants to do is fuck April Monday. "How could I be stupid enough to walk away? I was this close!" he thinks to himself while traveling the pavement path before him. He's become so engulfed in his thought that he does not notice the elderly couple ahead of him, set for an on-coming collision course. In a last ditch effort of survival, the two break their holding hands and dive off of the path. And despite the old farts dusting themselves off and saying something predictably ancient like, "Kids these days," while shaking their fists, Adam still pays no mind to his surroundings. All of the apartment buildings' occupants that happen to be outside bear witness to this arrogant punk clad in a strange mask, whether they be on their porches or getting the mail. He makes no effort to hide his actions, which raises the question of whether or not he knows he's committing them. Adam has only been to this place once before: when he picked up April to take her to a Ransom Drop for her boyfriend. When Adam returned to his home where he left April, all traces of her being there had vanished. That's how he knows she's gone back to the place she shared with her baby's daddy. He quickly runs up the staircase to the second floor of the building, still carrying the gaze of various neighbors who care more to gossip than be concerned. The thought of a man in a wrestling mask walking about in public is ridiculous in itself, but this situation is even stranger. Some have it in their minds that it could lead to a drug deal or, even "better", a domestic dispute. Reaching room 219, Adam beats heavily upon the door. So comes the time for the Face-Eater that all men must eventually face: the Moment of Truth. He's pushed the clock toward his own Eleventh Hour... and now it has arrived. Whether or not he achieves true success in his lifetime hinges on this very instance... ...if April would answer the fucking door. Facey knocks a few more times while also screams into the peephole. "APE!" But she does not come running to the door. She has to be here. With nowhere else to go and very little money, her other options are practically invalid. "APRIL!" He screams again, accompanied by knocks. He's seconds away from kicking the door down (which the neighbors would fucking love to see) when the peephole goes dark. FINALLY. She's ready for some serious dicking. With an apology already loaded, he begins to gush before the door swings fully open. "April I jus-" "Hey, Richard. Sorry I didn't recognize you earlier. It's just that, y'know, you were wearing that mask." Surprise, surprise; someone he definitely was not expecting to see, Brock Shepherd, stands in the doorway. ---- I'm sorry. There's nothing more for me to say, other than 'I'm sorry.' I took our relationship and I threw it away, I understand. And I really can't blame anyone but myself. I can't blame you, and I can't blame the child. You've always been so proud. So poise and strong. But I couldn't match your strength. September Twelfth. That's supposed to be the due date. Every second that has passed, leading up to that day, has made me sick to my stomach. It's despicable that I'm not at your side, and I know you don't want me there. I can't not be there for you, April. I can't let you go through this alone. For being such a caring, generous woman, you did not deserve to go through the Hell that you have. And I know the blame rests solely on my shoulders,which is why you don't want me around... That was before, when we didn't know the whole story. Everything is my fault, yes, I know. But this mother fucker, this Adam Dick... I can't believe I was so stupid. This is where I've been, April. It all started three months ago, when we first got the house. Remember how your eyes lit up when we walked inside? Of course you don't... Anyways, the Illustrious Face-Eater... He's a man notorious for doing whatever it takes to achieve his goal. This fucking asshole, he doesn't give a shit what the repercussions are, as long as the final result is achieved. By any means necessary. He ruined my life, April. He sent me on a wild goose chase in the middle of the fucking desert. That's not an analogy; he actually sent me on a wild fucking goose chase in the middle of the Tohono O'odham Indian territory. Next thing I know, I'm captured robbed by a pick-up trick full of these Injun fuckers, and they spend the next 9 weeks taking turns in beating the shit out of me and feeding me hog shit, keeping me locked up in some cellar, stuck in the middle of nowhere. I swear to God, April, I wanted to die. I'll be honest when I say that the heat exhaustion from the sun was unbearable, but the thought of leaving you alone with our child was even worse. Luckily, those mother fuckers would have kept me alive on the bare essentials for as long as they needed to... Only, I escaped. When I came and found you, I was certain these assholes would follow me, that they'd try to clean up their mistake. That's why I tried to get you and go. I didn't think there was any time for explanations. But it seemed that I was replaced by Adam fucking Dick. So I left, and I stayed underground for a few weeks, and no one came for me. No one even batted an eye. Not the Indians who kidnapped me, not my friends. Even GCW had forgotten about me. When I decided to check up on the loose ends I left, see what's up with the Chris Williams Memorial Cup, whose name did I see in place of mine? Adam. Fucking. Dick. Immediately, I understood something wasn't—isn't—right. And that's when it hit me. This mother fucker has planned it all from the start. He took advantage of me, April—no, he took advantage of us. I admit that I was weak, that I couldn't be as strong as you. I got involved in some bad people and that's what truly brought me into all of this. That's why I know I can't blame anyone but myself. But that doesn't mean I can't hate the asshole that put me over the edge. He took me away from you, April. And for what? So he could get in some fucking tournament? Well I hope he finds success in that tournament, and I hope that all of the pain he put me—I mean, US—through is worth it. Because at the end of the day, he'll never have what I know we have. I love you, April, and I know you still love me. ---- "Do I know-" "Playing fucking cute." Brock says, feigning a smile. He's wary of the neighbors watching and carefully places a hand on Facey's shoulder. "Get inside." Shoved through the door against his will, it shuts behind Adam who now stands face-to-face with a man he's fucked over more times than he could count in recent months. The funny thing is, half of the time he wasn't even meaning to. Brock's large size intimidates the Face-Eater, but not as much as the memory of their last encounter. His raw strength enabled him to break Adam into pieces with ease, and surely Brock has caught on to his antics by now. He's probably waiting to tear his throat out. In a moment where his fears could take the best of him, relief comes through the doorway, belly-first. "Adam?!" "April, I'm sorr-OOMPH." Brock immediately floors him with one solid hook to Adam's jaw. "HOW DARE YOU SPEAK TO HER." April's face is flushed red. Her stomach looks as if it's about to burst from the belly-button and she doesn't appear to be healthy. But still, the look of such a beautiful pregnant woman is enough to keep the Face-Eater inspired and on track towards his goal. "...I'm.... sorry, April..." "SHUT THE FUCK UP." Brock screams, kicking him in his side. The scene is very similar to what happened weeks ago. "Brock, STOP!" "Stop? Stop? Are you fucking serious April? HE TRIED TO KILL ME. HE TORE US APART. ALL OF THE PAIN THAT WE HAVE GONE THROUGH IN THE PAST THREE MONTHS HAS BEEN BECAUSE OF THIS PIECE OF SHIT!" "Can I just... say... something?" Adam asks. "Fuck you can!" Brock punches the Face-Eater in the spine, driving him back to the ground. Not leaving him a chance to breathe, he grips the back of Adam's head and begins to slam it into the floor, bludgeoning his face against the tile. April cannot watch this. For all the torment she might have suffered in waiting for her child's father to return, it is the man that he's trying to kill that offered her comfort. He offered something tangible when she needed it most. Not just concerns and "I love you"s, but actual affection. She runs to Brock and forces him to back down, knowing he'd never put himself in a position to harm her. "April, what are you doing." "What am I doing?!? What are you going to do to him, Brock? Kill him?" "Fucking right I am." "I don't think that's such a good idea." Adam huffs from the ground, blood sputtering from the back of his throat. "What about everything you told me? How it's all your fault?" Brock is quick to retort, but April continues. "Those were all just words, weren't they? Words, because you can't let it go! Even when you were telling me how you know you were wrong, you still insisted on blaming someone else. You're so selfish, Brock." April turns around, as if she's going to allow Brock to continue beating him. "Selfish?! EVERYTHING I'VE DONE HAS BEEN FOR YOU. FOR THAT CHILD. There isn't a god damned thing on this planet that I wouldn't do for you, April." Back to face him, April delivers a cold, piercing stare to the father of her child. "Except spare the man who you're blaming your problems on." He's been defeated. No matter how he tries to explain it, she's essentially right. Brock has relegated himself to a hypocrite. At the moment, it's hard to tell what hurts most: the fact that he can't be a good father or the fact that he'll never be with April again. Brock Shepherd no longer considers himself a man. April once again heads through the doorway, going back the way she came, but says to Brock before departing, "Do what you want, Brock. I thought you came to me because we were going to pick up our life. But I see that there are more important things to you than that." His shoulders shrink, his head drops. Just like that, his life is put back into perspective. He's about to call out to her, plead for another chance to make things right. But she disappears through the doorway. And then the Face-Eater kicks him in the balls. "DICK RUBBING PIECE OF SHIT! YOU KNOW WHAT I'VE DONE FOR YOU!?! WHAT I'M STILL DOING FOR YOU!?" Facey uses his momentum to drive Brock to the ground, slamming him into a coffee table in the process. Particle board and photography books form the rubble under them, providing Adam suitable material to strike his opponent with. "I KICKED MICHAEL KILLJOY'S ASS FOR YOU! I KICKED KIMBUSA'S ASS FOR YOU! AND JOHN COVEL AND ALL THOSE FUCKS!! AND TONIGHT I'M GONNA BEAT THE FUCK OUT OF THAT DELUSIONAL DEITY-LOVING PRICK! ALL OF THAT IS FOR WHAT?!? SO YOU CAN TRY AND KILL ME!" After two blows to Brock's head, he manages to find himself with an opportunity to boot Adam in the stomach and send him flying. He gains a moment of breathing room, but uses this advantage to get to his feet and knee Adam in the face. April runs back into the room, a hand clutched on her stomach and tears rolling down her face. "You stupid FUCK! You didn't do shit for me! Richard Adamson, Adam Dick—what, you take me for a FUCKING RETARD?!" Another knee to Adam's face, absolving him of his will to fight with the impact. But the adrenaline is surging through Brock. He ignores the pain in his testicles and the gash left Adam left on his face, and instead opts to remove a large porcelain vase from its shelf. With the vase held high overhead, Brock's upper lip curls as he prepares to unleash it. A final blow would put to rest all of the fury that's been swelling inside of him for months. THUD. "April?" he says, turning back to the door to see April hunched over on the ground... a pool of liquid soaking underneath her. Brock tosses the vase aside and dodges the debris as he makes his way to April's side. "APRIL!" He screams, rolling her to her backside. She shakes lightly, vigorously, with her eyes closed and biting her lower lip. "She's having contractions!" The Face-Eater yells, crawling back to his feet. He runs over to the downed Preggo as well, resting a hand on her stomach to the chagrin of Mr. Shepherd. "The fuck are you doing?" Brock asks. "It's coming, you idiot! Your fucking kid is on its way. If you want to sit there like the fucking 'tard I know you are, fine. Go ahead. But if you want to help the woman that you claim you love so much, you'll call someone and get some fucking help!" Brock doesn't realize his inaction is costing them. "Those Indian fucks stole my phone, you prick. The line here is disconnected because no one's lived here for months!" "WELL THEN YOU'D BETTER GO GET SOME FUCKING HELP WHILE I TRY AND GET HER THROUGH THIS!" Brock, confused by the commotion April is making and the words that Adam speaks, climbs to his feet and heads to the door. A thought jumps into mind and he stops himself. "You go get some fucking help, psycho. This is my kid-" "DO YOU KNOW HOW TO DELIVER A BABY?" Brock stares quietly for a moment. "Oh, I suppose you do?" "TWO SEMESTERS OF MED SCHOOL. GOT KICKED OUT BECAUSE I STOLE THE MODEL VAGINA. I THINK IT'S SAFE TO SAY THAT I'M AN EXPERT ON THE SUBJECT." Brock's eyes go wide, and he turns down to his girlfriend laying on the floor. In pain. "I'm not leaving you alone with her, you sick fuck." "IF YOU WANT YOUR GIRLFRIEND TO GET THROUGH THIS, YOU'LL GO GET HELP!" Through with arguing, the Face-Eater turns back to April. He's a man of action and when the call of duty arises, he won't puss out (assuming enough pot is in his system—luckily, there is). Placing a hand on April's stomach, he places the other in her palm and allows her to squeeze. "Alright, April, I need you to breathe! This is only going to last a few minutes, okay? BUT I NEED YOU TO BREATHE! COME ON! HEE-HEE-WHOOOOO. HEE-HEE-WHOOOOO." Slowly beginning to cooperate, April mimics the breathing exercise that Facey demonstrates. The pain isn't quick in subsiding, but at least he's made the effort to help her. Facey turns back to Brock, finding that he's still standing there. "THE FUCK ARE YOU STILL DOING HERE?! GO!" "...Apr-" "SHE'LL BE FINE, GO GET SOME FUCKING HELP." Brock finally bolts out the door, allowing Facey to turn his focus back to April. "Alright, the contractions should be dying down any moment no-" "You came back." Lifting his eyes from April's... situation, he looks her in the face. She appears pale, sickly. It isn't completely attributed to the labor process—April has been ill for quite some time. At a certain point in this entire ordeal, she stopped taking care of her self. "I had to, April. I think... I think I-" "Adam?" "Yes?" "Did you really learn how to do this in Medical School?" Adam smiles his shit-eating grin which, for some reason, always seems to put April at ease. "Nah, but I did see a movie in my High School Biology class where this chick with a hairy cooch squeezed one out. If it's anything like that, this'll be a fucking cake-walk." ---- It took Brock many attempts to find someone in the complex who was home AND willing to help, but on the bottom floor, two buildings over, was a single mom with a home phone handy. Brock was on the line with a 911 dispatcher seconds later. Waiting for more than ten minutes, he sits in the courtyard near the parking lot for what seems to be an eternity. Right as his hope begins to fade, an ambulance arrives. The firetruck and squad cars are not far behind. "THIS WAY!" He screams at them, flagging them towards the heart of the apartment complex. The EMTs bring their stretcher and med-kits, properly suited and ready to deliver the child on the scene if need be. All of the neighbors watch from the safety of their porch, even those that aren't of the nosey-kind. But like flies to a light, even the sheltered come out of their hiding places to view a spectacle. Guiding them all the way down the path and up the staircase, up to the second floor and in Room 219, Brock Shepherd is the first through the door. The sight he sees is enough to knock the wind out of him. "...shit..." one of the EMTs mutters, but quickly brings himself back into the moment and orders the rest of the squad. Whatever the kid says, it's lost on Brock. He slowly approaches April Monday, collapsed on the floor... covered by her blanket with a pillow under her head... and a brand new baby boy wrapped tight in a beach towel, snug in her arms. She rocks the child back and forth as he cries uncontrollably, gently caressing his tiny body. While the EMTs quickly work around her, rolling her on to the stretcher to get her to the hospital, Brock forces his way to her side. He can't fight the tears any longer, but there was never a reason to fight them in the first place. All of the shit he's gone through has all come down to this moment... His Moment of Truth. "April, are you alright?" he asks, his eyes more fixed on her than the child. She attempts to speak, but her voice has gotten so weak from labor it's hard to hear. "What? What're you trying to say, hon?" She quietly mutters, "I named him Adam." Brock's heart shatters into many irreparable pieces. And of course, the Illustrious Face-Eater is nowhere in sight. ---- Epilogue After childbirth, April Monday remained very sick. Though she never fully recovered, she insisted on raising her son until she was physically unable to do so. Three years later, she was admitted to the hospital for a viral infection, where she passed away shortly after. She never married. Her child was put in the care of his father, Brock Shepherd. Brock became a quiet man after the birth of their son and was involved minimally during the first part of his life. When he began caring for the boy, his resentment towards April took the better of him. He retired from the wrestling business shortly thereafter, and raised the boy for much of his life. With a big match just hours away, the Illustrious Face-Eater bailed and never looked back. April was no longer pregnant, so why the fuck would he want to?
