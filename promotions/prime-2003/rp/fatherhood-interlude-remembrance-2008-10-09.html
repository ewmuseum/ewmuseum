Fatherhood - Interlude - Remembrance

9 October 2008

Date: October 9, 2008 Life is pleasant. Death is peaceful. It's the transition that's troublesome. - Isaac Asimov The pain was different than anything else I had ever felt. It stung. It burned. It was constant. It felt good. Tattoos are interesting things. They can tell a story. They can be a reminder of a person, place, time, or thing. Tattoos can be a fashion statement, a way to buck the system. For some people, tattoos are even a way to improve themselves. Personally, I was here for one reason and one reason only. Closure. As I sat in the recliner-like chair with Steve Cotton hovering over me laying down the stencil, I couldn’t help but wonder how I was gonna go on. Nikkie was gone, and I was left to care for Nicholas, all the while staying with PRIME and making money so that he can have a good life as he grows up. It really seemed impossible. I kept wondering how I could pull this balancing act off. I had to find a way to both continue to provide for my son while also actually taking care of him at the same time. The ideas kept popping into my head, and sadly, none of them seemed like winners. When I came to him with my idea, he had already heard about the tragedy. Bruh was totally up front, giving his full condolences and bein’ the overall nice guy that I had always heard about. We went over a few sketches before deciding on a final design, but I was confident in his work and was giving him full creative reign. “Alright Bryan, ready to go?” No. “Yeah bruh, all set.” I glanced down at the work Steve was doing, and with each stroke of the gun, another jolt of pain and pleasure shot into my body. Cotton is a renowned tattoo artist on the island, probably one of the more talented bruhs I’ve ever seen do tattoo work. A couple hours passed, and when I glanced down at my new work of art, I was relieved. Nikkie would now never leave me.
