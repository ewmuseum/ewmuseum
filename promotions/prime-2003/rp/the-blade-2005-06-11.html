The Blade

11 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/11/05 I finally managed to break out of my shell. I’ve finally found a place where I can be free to be me. Being young, I needed to shed that last shell otherwise I’d be doomed. Doomed to a guise I no longer wish to become. For those of you who do not know my name. They call me Gabriel Brown. To many, I should not be. My existence is irrelevant and I am to be their puppet in this life. I made people laugh. It had its charms and it wasn’t without its perks. I was granted riches beyond my wildest dreams on being an entertainer. I loved it all. Key word. LOVED. I can’t take it anymore. I just can’t. I’ve been traveling through these blackened tunnels for ages, searching for something that I can call my own. The light is atypical and the various shades enshrouding me as I walk further into this tomb grow in number. But I know it’s here. My salvation can be found here. No longer will this life I detest cover my eyes. It will be gone whether my voice is heard by many or by few. Scratch that. Screw the many. Fuck the few. I can’t keep making them happy. I just can’t. I wanted so badly to be able to, but I just can’t anymore. My mind is empty because THEY want more. They want and want and want and never give me a break. That’s why I walk this tunnel now. It’s dark. It lies underneath a sea full of fish that I’m not ready to return to yet but provides me with perfect breathing space for the time being. I hear no sound but that of my own heart beating hastily. I’m away from their gaze and away from their presence at long last. But when I reemerge, the world will once again know my presence. Their malfeasance will not go unpunished. I will destroy these dark tunnels and my resurgence will send ripples upon ripples throughout the sea. It will unlock a cataclysmic event never before witnessed by the eyes of the mortal. But it all begins with that light. --------------------------------------------------------- Eagerly awaiting the red light of the camera going off in mere moments from now, Gabriel Brown took one moment to look at his surroundings. The signature PRIME backdrop behind him. Then a glance at the red light on the camera. In a second, it’d turn on and the world would tune in to see the face of this youngster exploding on the world with his own brash opinions of everything he’d taken in with PRIME weeks prior to signing with the place. How he loathed Hoyt for exploiting religion in order to get ahead in wrestling while being a Catholic himself. His very hatred of men like Vampir Nosferatu tainting the sport with such blatant disregard for humanity, it sickened Gabriel to his very core. Was this the way that Gabriel really wanted to go when starting out in PRIME? Make a few funny potshots, possibly be some smark’s line of the night. It was all well and good, but thinking back on that night, it made Gabriel sick. One week and he was already compromising his personal modus operandi of just getting into PRIME for the competition. And he was about to do it again. All The Golden Child wanted to do was wrestle. That’s it. He didn’t want to make many friends (Though an eccentric bastard in Vangellus Oxios was his own tag team partner now, though he could truly call him a friend) and not bother anybody. Just wrestle. But as is the story with life. Conflict breeds interest. It’s how PRIME puts the asses in the seats and how it’s become the monolithic success it is now. Then of course, Gabriel gave into his emotions. Human nature. Something got on his nerves and he opened his mouth about it before the show. Not only did he vent, but he used the names of others to make an impact and make his intentions known. He was going to rise to dominance in PRIME and nobody was going to stop him and it would all start with grabbing for the tag team titles coveted by great teams of the past like Manifest Destiny and Clique Clique Boom. Los Diablos finally succeeded in wearing down Manifest Destiny to the point where the Mexican tag team could claim they were indeed greats by stopping the unstoppable in Angelo Deville when Williamson was fed up with the deceiving. Now they were the champions and looked to fend off the likes of pirates, trapeze artists and hungry Africans galore. But there was one new team thrown into that mix. Gabriel, along with Vangellus Oxios, had a target in Los Diablos. And they would reach it one day. But for now, it would start with the relentless and aggressive former champion of one of the sickest pits of Hell on Earth in The Asylum. Token Weed. The presence of Token in PRIME made Brown’s stomach turn but he’d be allowed to vent. Oh, how sweet it’d be. Just one more week, I think. One more week, THEN no more talking. But the light was about to go on in 5…4…3…2… Blink. Awkward silence filled the screen as Gabriel Brown’s visage appeared. His blonde hair was spiked up and his face a pallet painted with intensity. Cold blue eyes stared into that camera as it closed in. Wait for it… Here it comes. Token was going to get the verbal bashing of a lifetime. Gabriel ached at the chance to tell the former tA champion how stupid his name was. How Asylum was ECW on Government Crack-Cocaine. How he was making the biggest mistake of his life by facing both members of Teh Pwn at once. That his tenure in PRIME was only good for a laugh. A bead of sweat hit the floor near Gabriel’s Adidas sneaker as he continued to stare. It was then that the former multiple-time RPW titleholder looked down at his clenched fist. Was he really ready to once again compromise his integrity by lowering himself to bashing others? “Token, prepare yourself. That’s all you get.” And with a wave of the arm like his time had been utterly wasted, he left. Simple as that. --------------------------------------------------------- If I’m going to get anywhere in life to be the success I want to be, I have to go against the grain of others. Oxios, his lifestyle be damned, is still my friend. Let him do all the talking. I did enough of it in a past lifetime. My action will indeed speak louder than my words. You may take that old cliché out back and shoot it now for all I care. The light is close in this cavern. I have to reach for it and I’ll grab it at all costs. It’s the only way to be able to rid myself of that shadow that still looms over me every place I go. I feel it getting ever so closer now. Its energy is the substance that legends depict. It overcomes me as I proceed onward, not giving into apprehension. Finally! Etched in stone lies the blade that shall put to rest that of my former self. Reaching for it proves highly difficult. The hilt is broad and powerful and the blade is embedded into the deep rock. But I can’t stop now. I just can’t. If I do, then my journey can never truly begin. It becomes a true test of my physical strength to secure the blade into my possession. I stop. I have to catch my breath. With one big gob of spit, I fire it into my palms before meshing them together. I’m not going to give in. I’ve never surrendered once. Once again, the blade proves to match my hereditary stubbornness. Brown family trait, you see. It’s unforgiving. Unwilling to let itself be handled by anyone. But it WILL be mine. I rebuff its obduracy by continuing despite the strain it’s putting on my arms. It budged. I could feel it. I was winning. The blade was closer to becoming mine. With one final and commanding pull, the blade rises and becomes free of its former prison of rock. I take one look at it and the smile becomes etched in my face. I’ve won. Waving it around was a task, but none too great. It was finally mine. Now the shadow near me rises. It wants me to go back to it. That…PUPPET. I want nothing more to do with it. With one mighty thrust, I jam the blade into its beating heart. One mighty scream of profanity later, the beast falls to the ground and dies at my feet. After a year of trying to escape it, I can hold my head up high and say it’s over. I’m true to myself now. And being true to oneself is the greatest power that any man can hold. Far greater than any blade.
