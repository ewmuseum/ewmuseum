Psychic or Psycho You be the Judge (CS)

11 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/11/05 Journal Entry 5.0: The Past= The Future There are some things about a person you can automatically tell by judging their parents. That my friend is scientific, also know too many people around the world known as genetics. Before a newborn is even born, people have a good idea what the distinctive traits are going to be. To make a baby it takes two people, and the baby is made from parts of both these people. So as I see it, along with many scientists, genetics can give you a good idea what a newborn will look like before it is even born. Both parents have blonde hair, most likely, or should we say that the children will have blonde hair, same with any other statistic or trait. Now what does physical appearances have to do with my involvement with PRIME and the Duel Halo? Many would say nothing, I am just rambling, but I would say a large part of my game plan. Children do not just inherit physical traits, they also inherit mental traits. If you go and look at all the entrants of the Duel Halo’s parents, you would learn a lot of each entrant. We could easily find out who, has the drive, motivation, competitiveness, aggression, and most importantly we can find out who the real winners and the real losers are, from their parents and previous life experiences. The statement “The apple does not fall far from the tree,” is something I am going to take into deep consideration this week. Science is something I have looked to in the past, and this week I am going to try and use it to help me get out of the Duel Halo alive. Tuesday February 8, 2005: Finding the “Real” Me -The Skies they were ashen and sober;The leaves were crisped and sere--The leaves they were withering and sere;Taken from “Ulalume” by Edgar Allen Poe Those three lines from such an in depth meaningful poem, describe the scene we currently seeing right now. The dark skies are getting darker and darker by the moment. The scene displays an area of sadness and destitute, while black cats and other animate objects you could/would associate with sadness and horrific sites are roaming around the area. A man standing no more, no less then five foot nine, wearing dark clothing with a desolate look upon his face is walking the streets. He reaches the one dimmed street light on the whole block. The man leans against it as if he was the only support this pole had, but in reality it’s the pole that is the only stability for the man. “A life of solitude is what I live, darkness is the only time where I seem to be myself, the person I truly am. Maybe I am a point in my life where I should show the world the real me, maybe I should stop hiding in the darkness and stop putting on this charade of being some sort of loveable guy. I might not be the most liked, hell I might not even be liked by anyone here in PRIME or their fans but it is surely time for the truth. This weekend the truth will be unfolded, a different type of person will be seen in the ring, a different wrestler all together, but the only thing that matters is the outcome.” The darkened figure steps more towards the dimmed light, he looks up towards the sky as if he was looking for some sort of sign or answer from the sky. When look upwards towards the sky enough light was shown upon his face allowing his identity to be revealed to any that paid enough attention. The man, Peter Vetra, standing all alone looking for an answer, nothing seems to come to him. Peter just looks down after staring upward for what seemed to be an eternity but in actuality it was only about 45 seconds, a rather short period of time in a person’s life. “Throughout what you may like to call a career many things have changed. I went from a man that once fooled the world into thinking I was a member of a secretive elite group. I was supposedly their only link to society and making them known and getting recognition for their accomplishments, well that failed. Then I was sent into this last spin I just had with a supposable friend Oscar Hernandez. That man truly screwed me and my career up for good, but I will try and bounce back. If I can he will be the first one out of the Duel Halo this weekend, his time is due, and I would like to be the one that gives it to him.” Peter Vetra again looks up to the sky but this time for only a mere moment, he then looks back down at the ground and with the cold air slowly moving in we can now see every breath Peter Vetra takes. He turns and starts to walk down the street towards the dead end. “As I take each step I try and relate it to a point in my career, I try and relate it to a good time in my life. While with each step it hurts me to try and remember my past, the time where I was unloved by my family, the ones in your life who are suppose to care for you the most. Was it a hurtful moment in my life? Yes; but you know what? I have gone through it before, and l once again I will fight my way through it. Duel Halo will be my time to shine, at Culture Shock my life is going to make a turn for the better, only time will tell me what happens but I see it as being something good.” Journal Entry 5.1: Some of my Opponents! When you actually sit down and think of the probability of one of us winning the Duel Halo at Culture Shock it is not good. I mean think about it only one winner when there are thirty entrants. So being a mathematical genius I am I get a one in thirty chance of winning, not very good if you ask me. But of course there are always things you have to take into consideration, I mean you will have alliances and then you will have the blunders of the year. I mean we are going to have the alliances that are already know of, the likes of the tag teams; Los Diablos, Clique Clique Boom!, Jeff Garvin and Youngblood, along with some others. But then you always have to fear the likes of the Dark Age, you never know what these sick sadistic people are actually capable of, and you know they are going to be watching out for each other’s backs. But then we will have the other petty friendships form who are looking to help each other just for the two hours they are in this contraption together, but if they are just forming how good could they actually be? But those are the people to worry about because you might think you have formed some kind of alliance but in actuality they can turn their back on you. Now many people will work together but yet there are some who are going to go right after each other, so that kind of makes some sort of balance in this crazy match. We all know HIT and Karina Wolfenden will not have had enough of each other earlier in the night so we can expect them to go right after each other, along with the Vampir and Boda going right against each other. So the probability of this match is not something you can really figure out. One thing I do know is I will be prevailing as a winner from the evening. Tuesday February 9, 2005: Visiting the Psychic or should I call her the Psycho? Peter Vetra is being his dark gloomy self and is sitting at home in front of the television. There is minimal light in the room; the sun is down as you can see the moon through one of the windows. The alarm clock on the be shows 12:35 am. His attention affixed on the screen of the television, he is watching the Late Show on NBC. A commercial comes on for the local Chevrolet Dealer; Peter is not at all interested in this advertisement and changes the channel quickly. He changes the channel into the higher digits somewhere near the 90’s. Besides changing from the local channel where he sees his mayor speaking on some bullshit, he turns it one more time where a commercial gets his attention…. Voice of a Lady: Are you sitting at home alone? Peter Vetra: Yes! Voice of a Lady: Are you in a dead end job? With your future not looking good? Peter Vetra: Why yes. Voice of Lady: Well then you need to call Lady Cleopatra at 1-900-UR-FUTUR. Peter Vetra: Hell the lady can’t even sell future correctly what makes her think I am going to call her and lose a shit load of money to listen to her tell me lies. Voice of Lady: You might be skeptical but you should know your money is 100% Refundable as long as what I predict doesn’t happen to you! Peter Vetra: Ah what the hell do I have to lose? A couple of minutes of my night, it is already 12:36. Peter then picks up the cordless phone that is next to him, he starts to dial the number. After it rings for maybe three minutes or six rings whatever came first he really lost count an automated answering machine answered. Pre Recorded: Welcome to Lady Cleopatra’s Psychic Hotline, if you are a male please press one, if you are a female press two. Peter Vetra: What the hell does this have to do with my future? BEEP… Peter presses the number one waiting to see what the next step was. Pre Recorded: Thank you sir, Lady Cleopatra will be with you in a moment, as you wait please fill out the following survey, when you are done she will await you. Peter thinks to himself how is she going to know if she is handling this on a case by case basis but then again she is a psychic or maybe a psycho that is still undecided. Peter continues to answer the silly little questions. Upon the finishing of the survey a ladies voice comes on the phone. Lady: Good Evening Sir; you are a sir aren’t you? Peter: Well yes, I did press one but I guess you are a psychic. At this point Peter is skeptical about this, wondering how much money he has already wasted just on the “survey” Lady: Sir to get the full experience you have to be completely open. Now just sit back and relax, close your eyes. I see an uphill battle in your future, sir do you have a challenge ahead of you? Peter: Why ye ma’am I do, I am currently in a huge career breaking match in PRIME wrestling. Lady: Ah… that explains the jogged lines in the ball you are a wrestler, I thought you were a man that was very fierce and competitive. Now this match, are there many men you are going to be facing? Peter: Well there are about 29 other wrestlers involved. I am not sure if that is considered a lot as I have been in larger matches but yeah there is a good amount. Lady: Well Sir, I am sorry but I don’t see you being victorious during this match. You have been given the death card, and by this I don’t mean fatality it means no success. Peter: Well that card is a sack of shit! Are you sure the death card is not for my opponents? Lady: No Sir, the card has been dealt to you, I don’t see you coming out of this match a winner, your destiny has been chosen by the cards. I am sorry. Peter: Let me ask you a question, how many times have you actually refunded someone’s money because you were wrong? Lady: Never Sir, the cards do not lie. Peter: Well ma’am I will be getting a refund on my money, and I am sick of hearing these stories from you, you are nothing but a charade, you are a phony a fake. Right then something hits Peter, he has just realized that everything he ever did when he was younger was fake, phony, not his true self. He says his final words and good byes he explains to her that she will be hearing from him again, and next time she will be paying him back instead of taking his money. The phone is then hung up in disgust. Peter: How dare her! I will show her and the rest of the world this Sunday, my time will come and it is now I swear! Journal Entry 5.1: Who are my opponents? So there are some opponents during this Duel Halo thingy that are going to be the target of my aggression. First we have the man I have mentioned many of times before, Oscar Hernandez, I will never be fully done with him, not until I run him out of this federation and out of the career of wrestling altogether. Most of the other people in this match have not really crossed my path before. I mean only a few people I have faced before I mean we have; Karina Wolfeden and Tessa Windsor which was in a tag team match, Emilio Rage a couple of times the same with Ozric Mortimer. Then we have Jeff Garvin and Brandon Youngblood which I lost of course because of Oscar, again. Then we have the man I lost two last week, Big Poppa, he will get what is due to him last week, I should have won that and he knows it. There really are only a few on my list that I really want to throw out. But on top of that list there has to be two men that I have faced a couple of times since I have been here in PRIME. First and for most would have to be Emilio Rage, the man who has beaten me a couple of times, lastly of which was a match I should have won. The rest of the world, along with him knows that title is mine, the truth will be revealed some day that I am the true Alias Champion. Now the second man who I really would enjoy to throw out would have to be Ozric Motrimer I’m not sure if he remembers back to Revolution 44 where he gave me my first loss. Now on this night I am hoping to give him the most important loss of his career. Next a man who is new to this place we call home, a man who has yet to make a real impact to the federation with his actions. But always actions speak louder then words, and this week I am going to show him that. Jackson Darilek, was quoted saying his is the best cruiserweight in the business today. Well my actions of throwing him out will show him that I’, actually the best cruiserweight not just in this federation we call PRIME but also in the wrestling world today. When I look down this list there are many men I would enjoy to throw out just due to the simple fact of sheer enjoyment or maybe it is the status I will get from throwing one of these big name people out. I don’t know but if I get a chance to throw out Vampir, Tony Rolo, Boda, Hoyt Williams, Killean or any of the bigger more known names of PRIME I will not hesitate to do it. So everyone better watch their backs while I am still in the ring. Tuesday February 10, 2005: The last day in the United States Peter Vetra is out in public during the day an sight which not one to be seen as of late. The man has been hiding in the dark, unknown to many of people why. He is just seen leaving his house getting into his nice beat up 1994 S-10. He throws a couple of suitcases into the bed and starts it up. As he starts it up a thick rich cloud of black smoke leaves the exhaust. After forcing it into gear Peter starts to drive away. There is no music coming from the speakers of Peter Vetra’s sound system. But he easily keeps himself occupied by talking to himself. “So the part of this match that is really going to get me are the people that I do not know about. Think about it, people I have no previous knowledge of. People like Razor Blade, Xavier Kannon, and hell even Los Diablos I am unsure of, but these guys really do scare me. Even the guys who are unannounced to any of the other members of the fed, provided a real threat” People are starting to look into this broken down piece of shit car and wondering why this man is talking to himself. Many people raise their eyebrows to him, but Peter Vetra is not worrying just going about his business. “I know there are people I have forgotten, I know there are some important people like Nova who is a champion, then there are other people who I am not really afraid of like Shane Wethers, Tyler Lopez, First Blood. All of these guys are good wrestlers but I think if we do happen to cross paths I will end up on the better end of the deal.” Peter just continues his drive until he arrives at the airport for his plane departing for Japan in a few hours. The young man is eager and ready to go, he pulls in the parking lot and pays the fee. Peter grabs his bags and heads for the terminal. “Hopefully I will have better luck in Japan then I have had in the United States. I guess only in a few days we will see, let’s hope for the best.” Peter Vetra enters the terminal knowing that this could be the turning point in his career, on his return trip from Japan he might be a completely different man, with completely different goals and recent achievements.
