What Say You?

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/20/05 The on-air light turn’s red as the hall of fame wrestler flips the mic switch to “on” coming out of his last break. At one time in his life Duke’s mind was full of desire for only three things cocaine, whiskey, and most of all women. But now after being blessed with a successful back surgery, a clean sane wife, and the prospect of patching up a long worn relationship with his only known living son, Duke has become pleased. His only true passion nowadays, besides family, is hosting his popular afternoon drive radio show on WCKG in Chicago. He does still wrestle on occasion but works more in the role of a manager, or a figure head, then a brooding blood hungry warrior. His body is wreaked like the American pop culture after the British invasion, part two. The first time the Brits invaded our pop culture musically they took a commercial viable sound spearheaded by a young Mississippi boy and added art too it. The Beatles gave rock music something Elvis or Jerry could never and that was passion. Unfortunately the latest incarnation is more so the work of the devil then Apollo the Greek God of Art; and a historic New York dance theater in Harlem. The new invasion is called “American Idol” a TV show imported from England by the evil man known as Simon Cowell. Cowell has wrecked havoc on the charts by unleashing his minion’s known as Clarkson, Justin, Hung, and when he’s not stuffing his face Studder. I guess you can say Duke’s body is like an ill faded dream of a rusted out automobile on the side of dessert highway. It may have a few miles left but isn’t worth the work needed to restore it. Duke Williams: Deacon Duke with you this afternoon on WCKG 105.9. So I heard somebody in the office bitching about talking behind peoples back. I’ll be honest I haven’t heard that term since high school so I nosed around to see if it exists in the real world. I found it does among the low class still living in that high school mentality. Listen if Jenny the receptionist blows a stinker in the break room. “Malice Man” searches the computer in front of him quickly for the needed sound effect. With a quick click of the mouse a 50,000watt fart is blown for the listeners. Duke Williams: And I go tell Steve what Jenny did, am I talking behind her back? What am I doing? I’m sharing a story with somebody. If Jenny has a big ass and I comment on that, sure it’s mean, but I’m talking to a friend about somebody we both know. It’s conversation. Is that talking behind somebody’s back? I’m really unclear. 591-WCKG. The phone lines light up like a Christmas tree on a late December night in a goyam town. You can almost feel the Yule tide log burning in the fireplace, if only I knew what the hell a Yule tide log was I’d explain more. Duke like a pro pushes up line one and fires away. Duke Williams: Billy, from Oak Lawn, am I wrong? What say you? Billy: Hey Duke this is Billy here and I think you ARE wrong anything bad you say about somebody with out them in the room is talking behind their back. Duke Williams: Well Billy, what if I said Jennifer is the best worker we have. Do only bad things qualify as talking behind somebody’s back? Because look, if we are going to have to qualify what is good and what is bad we might as well end communication all together. Who is to judge what is a complement and what is an insult AND, AND, AND what business is it of ANYBODY else a conversation I have with a fellow worker. What if we talk bad about President Bush doesn’t that mean I’m talking behind his back by your standards? Billy: He’s a public figure. Duke Williams: AND in our world, community, and ecosystem so is Jennifer! What she does has a direct consequence on me. If she decides to dump people’s calls that need to go to my voicemail into the garbage I’m SCREWED! Thanks for the call Billy. Duke pushes the release button dropping the call from the line as he moves on to his next point as a skilled orator like himself does. Duke Williams: What if I told Steve I think Jennifer has great boobs? Can’t two guys talk about a coworker like that? Don’t ALL workers who deal with others of the opposite sex do this? It’s a man thing and its part of our masculinity. It’s a cultural habit of the man species. Now what if I said Jennifer is an asshole and I didn’t like her. Again this is conversation between two people who have contact and effect with the person being talked about. Duke takes a quick sip of water with out even missing a beat on the air. Duke Williams: Steve may agree that Jennifer is an asshole. He release some stress about her attitude and its over with. Now if I went up to Jennifer and told her it would do no good. NONE! NADA!! So by finding out Steve has a problem with Jennifer I know I’m not the only one. Again this is not talking behind somebody’s back that’s what kids do at lockers who are yet to learn social interaction. The Malice Man’s slightly crooked pointer finger moves slowly toward taking another call as the show is winding down Duke Williams: Another thing, ANYTHING I’ve said about somebody else, I’m always willing to say to somebody’s face. If I think you’re an asshole I’m not going to blurt it out but if it comes to it sure I’ll tell you you’re an asshole. That’s just the way it is. You keep mean and hurtful feelings to yourself until they feel the need to be told or are out of control. Some people are assholes and don’t have a clue so you need to nudge them and explain the simple concept of humanity to them. You see I’m an asshole but I can acknowledge it. Let’s take another call Sam from Streamwood you’re on the air. Sam: I think you are a red-neck racist. Duke Williams: But you don’t know me. You have no idea who I am. Sam: I know enough to judge. Duke Williams: Ahhhh so you don’t know the full story but you are willing to judge. Pre-judge also called prejudice. You are prejudice. Sam: What ever. You are a stupid asshole. Duke Williams: Really? Because we don’t agree I’M STUPID? Sam: No, because you won’t listen to my side. Duke Williams: Sure I will and let me give you my side of an issue. Sam: I don’t want to hear your Bush pushing propaganda. Duke Williams: But I won’t listen? Come on you are a hypocrite! Sam: You are a stupid moron. Duke Williams: You’re quick to judge anybody who doesn’t fit you’re imagine of the world yet you are the first to call foul when somebody calls you on the way you look or act. A product of the “not me” generation the generation that feels the world owes THEM! They can sit around, swear, flip the bird at authority because they were wronged. NIKE and MTV told them so. The whole culture is built on them being an individual that the world has screwed. Good luck in the real world with that attitude. One more caller before we end another exciting addition of “Malice”. Dave from Prospect Heights you’re on with Deacon Duke. What say you? Dave: Dude I can’t wait to see Youngblood kick your ass!! Duke Williams: GOOD!! IF he CAN’T then we’ll talk. I’m a retired wrestler with a son his age!! I can barely get up in the morning. If Brandy can’t take me out then he is pathetic. He is not worth a title shot, which we don’t think he is regardless of if he beats me or not. This a test to see where he is. If he can’t beat a retired part-time wrestler then he SHOULDN’T even be in PRIME!! If he can, then GREAT that puts him at about Kano’s skill level. Not anywhere near the Universal Title. Dave: He’s going to destroy you Duke he’s this months PTC’s number ONE! Duke Williams: Proving it’s a flawed system. How can Hoyt be number one the month before for winning the Universal Title then Youngblood gets it for winning a number one contenders match with one guy getting injured and the other guy being softened by my son and I? How can that be? PRIME can’t be so dominate that no other federation had a game player high enough to be number one. Now Hoyt had a slow month he shouldn’t be ranked Youngblood should be. But number one? Come on Dave you’re smarter then that aren’t you?? This is the Deacon of Rock signing off. Do it Malice Style if you’re going to do it at all. “Living in America” by James Brown play’s off of a cart as Duke’s bumper music out. He takes his headphones off his dyed brown hair. It’s a tough decision in a man’s life when he reaches a certain age. Should he let the silver streak in or should he cover it up. Duke covered it up as that’s what he does. He never lets his emotions show he covers them up. Right now he’s covering up a feeling he hasn’t felt since the forever before Sunday. A feeling I’m not sure he completely recognizes as his relationship with it faded like my fathers old jeans a long time ago. No question what that feeling is, its fear. He’s old, out of shape, and lacking the passion he once rode to world titles. He’s stepping in the ring with a submission professional that could break his brittle old body. What fun would the golden years be sitting in a stainless steal chair? These are emotions the Malice Man must conceal in his proud mind. In through the door walks Duke’s son the Universal champion Hoyt Williams. Duke’s Cadillac is in the shop so Hoyt offered to pick him up a ruse for a little time to bond. Hoyt and Duke exchange pleasantry before walking silently to Hoyt’s Ford truck. Both men’s minds racing with those little things that keep us from talking as we focus on them. Hoyt Williams: I’m going to stop at Taco Bell you want anything? Duke Williams: Taco Bell? Hell I’ll buy. Hoyt Williams: Thank, you’re a big spender. The old man chuckles to Hoyt’s friendly jab at him. Duke Williams: Anything on the menu. Hoyt Williams: Anything? Duke Williams: I’ll buy anything! Hoyt Williams: I can eat a lot of Taco Bell. Duke Williams: Nobody can eat that much. There is a moment of silence as Hoyt seems to be pondering something as he keeps his eyes on the rode. Duke kicks back and starts digging on the radio for his favorite country station US*99 despite their seemingly endless loop of George Strait songs. Hoyt Williams: I bet you I can eat ten dollars worth of Taco Bell. The elder Williams makes a face like his son is nuts. Duke Williams: There is no way, that could kill you. Hoyt Williams: A thousand bucks says I can eat ten dollars worth of Taco Bell. A mean that’s like a few seven layer burritos, a few Mexican pizzas, a drink or two. Duke Williams: Drinks count? The Malice Man raises a curious eyebrow as he considers the bet. Hoyt Williams: You said ANYTHING on the menu. Duke Williams: Fine but you have an hour and half to eat all that Taco Bell and finish all the drinks. The young Williams sucks his tongue in his lip as he now thinks about the stipulation. Hoyt Williams: It’s on! The two blood relatives shake hands as the wager is on. They pull into the nearest Taco Bell as Duke pulls a ten spot out of his bad ass wallet. The purple and green sign spits out a squeaky voice of uneducated adolescence. Taco Bell Sign: Welcome to Taco Bell would you like to try our Super Grande Mega Burrito value meal? Hoyt Williams: No, I’ll take three Dr. Peppers, four double Decker Supremes, ahhhhhh, ahhhhh, ahhhh, three seven layer burritos, and a Mexican pizza. Can I get a total? Taco Bell Sign: 10.79 sir please drive to the second window. Hoyt Williams: Hold on. Take off one seven layer what’s the total now? Taco Bell Sign: 9.79 Duke Williams: We take off five minutes from the one and a half hours you have. Hoyt Williams: Fine. Duke gives his son an acceptable nod as the order is placed and they drive up to the window where a squeaky voiced uneducated adolescent with a bad mustache and a home made tattoo awaits. Guy: You want any sauce?? Hoyt Williams: Mild. Guy: We are all out. The oversized savior sighs loudly as he shakes his head in a disapproving fashion. Hoyt Williams: Then give me whatever…… The man puts a few packets into the bag. Hoyt Williams: More…….. The man puts a handful in. Hoyt Williams: MORE….. The man puts a ton in as “Your Personal Jesus” gives the guy the thumbs up taking the bag and trading it with Duke’s ten. They then drive away. Duke Williams: We are going to need Police Academy movies to make this night complete. Hoyt Williams: Blockbuster is three minutes away but its 11:55pm and they close at Midnight. Duke Williams: Oh ball said the queen if I had two I’d be king! They close when you’re done getting your movie. If you are in the store before the stroke of Midnight they have to wait for you to finish before you can leave. Hoyt Williams: No way. Duke Williams: Yup, One night I could not decide between Die Hard 2, Die Hard 1, Die Hard with a Vengeance, and Lethal Weapon. Took me all night to decide the clerk had to stay with me till 4am. Now don’t get me wrong he was vacuuming and shit trying to get my out of their but I stood my ground. Hoyt Williams: What movie did you decide on? Duke Williams: Patch Adams. And it made me cry like a baby. Hoyt laughs loudly at the way Duke said he cried at the end of Patch Adams. He was dead serious and didn’t appreciate the laugh. Duke Williams: I need you to sign a wavier before you start eating all that Taco Bell. I mean you may get through the night but tomorrow you WILL feel hell. Or at least your asshole will. The father and son duo laugh as the car parks in the Blockbuster parking lot at 11:57pm. This is where we leave them as I run off and get some Taco Bell. =w=
