Date: undated The BecomingWhen one man seeks a destined futurehe looks to his past for rejuvination. Prelude 'Num ye rah wrang ye ku.' The chants came from the mouth of a young man sitting indian style in the center of an open field. A torch stood implanted into the ground beside the young man with a flame dancing at the very top. 'Num ye rah wrang ye ku.' The young man sat with his eyes closed and his hands outstretched as he chanted out loud as if calling amongst some higher power. But that is far from the case. This man, from the sound of his voice and the trademark scar on the side of his face, appears to be the Dark Age's own prodigy, Dani Furher. 'Num ye rah wrang ye ku.' As Dani continued to chant, a tall dark figure appeared behind him, looking down upon him. 'Young Furher.' Dani looked up from his positioning to see the masked face of his Lord, Vampir Nosferatu. 'Yes Mi' Lord.' 'You have a very important task that awaits you at Revolution. The chance to not only take the first step in bringing more gold into the Dark Age...but also the chance to slay that betrayer...the clown.' 'I understand.' 'But you don't Young prodigy. You see...in order to unravel a bright and destined future...you must first take a journey into your past. Take a journey into how you became one of us. Do that, Dani, and then...then you will truly understand the task at hand.' 'Yes..Mi' Lord.' And with that, Vampir slowly walked off of the scene leaving Dani to revisit his past. Revisit the Becoming. Revisit how he became a Prodigy of the Darkness... The Becoming Dani steadily walked along the pathway of the ocean shore. He had just returned home from a night out with the Tsar's daughter Elizabeth. The clouds in the sky gave off a forbidding darkness as a dark seam of smoke suddenly sweeped from the sky, attempting to sweep young Dani from the ground of earth. Dani rushed home and jumped into his bed looking for some sort of covery from the Darkness, but the dark smoke came through the creaks of the door and wrapped around Dani sucking him into the darkness that was... -- The thick atmosphere of this dark, abandoned underworld. Its stench reeked of spilled blood, scornful cries, and a raw chymical smell that reduced the eyes to tears and made the throat burn. This place, no, this Hell Hole played the role of some sort of Haven for the children of satan. The smoke swirled about as a figure, crawling on the tiled floor screamed out in pain, blood gushing from his tortured and scar filled body. Flames bind up all around this man as he struggles, slowly crawling as if in search of some sort of Salvation from his horrid state. A search that, by the looks of things, seemed corrupt. '..wh-where am I?' His words floated through the air, echoing, as he looked down at the crawling man on the tiled floor. Through looking at the man on the floor, Dani noticed something strange. The blood that gushed from his body was not of the normal red color...but instead a dark black liquid. The skin from his bones began to peel off, revealing the skeleton of his temple. Loud cries began to fill the room as a man dressed in all black and a cloak emerged from the shadows and behind the barren soul of Dani. 'Wh-what's going on. Why am I here?' Dani turned to face the man as the tall broad man looked deep into Dani's eyes with a glaze the would send Lucifer himself to his knee's. 'That...my child, is your father Czar Olsig, Leader of the land of Tahj. He is the reason for your being, as well as your presence here.' '...Ex-excuse me?' 'Listen my child. You were brought here by Lord Nosferatu to take your father's place as Leader of Tahj after his death. As his only son, its is your duty to claim his throne as our Shepherd in the land of Tahj.' Upon hearing that, Dani slowly turned from facing the man and crumbled to his knees at the side of his dying Father whom's only remains is the rusty skeleton in which his battered flesh once covered. Dani sunk in the dark blood as the man stood tall behind the now weeping Furher, tall and broad with a presence of dominance evident in his appearance. 'Of course there is the option to reject young Dani. If you are to reject, though, your proper positioning, then you shall charish in the same fate as your father...' Slowly, Dani looked up into the eyes of the man whom's face now shelted a wicked and devious smile. -- Elden, The tall dark figure from earlier, stood in front of Dani as the various gaurds assembled to his side seeking to guide him through this grand march of sorts. Elden slowly unvieled a small glass bottle from his inner pocket, popping off the cork that secured the liquid inside and gently dabbing the liquid unto the tip of his index finger. Softly, he annointed the oil on the forehead of Dani as Dani looked at him...fury in his eyes. 'You are now ready my child.' Before a word could escape the mouth of Dani, the man turned around and began to walk slowly down the hall as the four men walking by the side of Dani followed pushing Dani along the way as well. They steadily walked down the hall, speaking not of any words, just quietly marching down the dark hall that lead to the entrace of the Temple. Not long until they reached the end of hall where the entrance door opened and the sun came piercing through. As the door opened, Dani saw hundreds of people assembled in front of the Palace all cheering and screaming for a reason unknown. Slowly they continued to walk until they reached a spot directly in front of the hurd of people. 'Who are all of these people?' 'They have come to share in your ordainance as Shepherd and Ruler of Tahj.' Dani stood by the side of Elden as Elden reached, once again, into the pocket of his long black robe pulling out a blade that glimmered in the sun's light. Dani looked at the blade terrified. Not wanting anything to do with what it had to offer, he quickly spoke up. 'I'm not going through with this.' Dani turned to walk away but suddenly he felt the tigh grasp of Elden's hand on his shoulder. After turning Dani back around, Elden looked deep into his eyes without saying a word. As Dani looked into the dark eyes of Elden, he began to witness flashes of the Underworld in which he watched his father helplessly die. His mouth peeled open as he watched the skin fall from his Father's bone landing in a pool of the dark colored blood that covered the ground. Slowly, Elden blinked causing the image to fade. 'I'd hate to see you in the same position as your father my child. So, are you right handed or left?' The crowd drew silent as the hundreds of pairing eyes glanced up at Dani giving Dani an uncomfortable feeling. He held out his right hand as Elden took it and drew the blade straight across Dani's palm as Dani snatched his hand back immediately responding to the pain. The pain of the blade was so elevated that he was left speechless. He looked down at his palm to see the same dark blood that escaped his father's torn body, escaping his wound. 'Repeat after I my child.' Dani glared up at the Elden and unto the crowd as Elden began. 'I, Lord of Tahj...' 'I, Lord of Tahj...' 'Hereby accept the duties formed against me as such...' 'Hereby accept the duties formed against me as such...' 'And with the spirit of the Holocaust in heart...' 'And with the spirit of the Holocaust in heart...' 'I shall defend my throne...blood for blood.' 'I shall defend my throne...blood for blood.' And with that, Elden grabbed the wrist of Dani and raised it high into the air for the mass of people to see. The dark liquid blood began to drip from his hand and unto the snow covered ground, staining it and robbing it of it's usual white color. The people began to attempt to fight through the guards holding them back in order to reach Dani. Dani looked upon them with a bewildered look in his face, but one person out of the hundred caught his eyes the most. A man whom looked dead at him with a glare so threatning, words could not describe. While the rest of the people attempted to battle through security making themselves to look as fools, this one man stood calm, staring at Dani with that same cold glare as the Guards grabbed Dani and began to set for the Palace of Tahj. -- The dust swirled in the air as a young woman, seemingly in the range of 19 and 24 as far as age is concerned, stood over a wooden table dusting it with a spark in her that was rare in young children of today's society. The expression on her face gave evidence to the fact that she did not want to do this nor did she want to be here, but it also gave evidence to the fact that she had a serious task at hand and was determined to complete it at any and every cost. An older woman walked unto the scene and to the side of the young housemaid, startling the young housemaid as the woman placed a hand on her shoulder. The young housemaid grasped her hard in relief as she looked unto the old woman's unwelcoming face. 'What are you doing Nikia?' 'Mother I'm just dusting the table as you said. Is there something wr--' Before she could even finish what it was she was attempting to say, the older woman brought a back hand across the face of the girl sending her to her knee's on the hard floor. The girl held her jaw as she struggled to get back to a vertical position. 'Don't even Think about talking back to me Nikia. Now Lord Furher is on his way and there is much work to be done. Go up to his room and begin preparing his bed. Everything has to fall in place for his arrival.' 'Y-yes mother.' The woman stormed off of the scene as the girl gripped the table using it as support to help her reach her feet. A tear rolled down her cheek as she quickly brushed it away and began to head up the stairs. She stepped slowly up each step, one at a time, before she reached the top where she entered a room. The room was much fair in size as well as in design. Truly a room built for a Shepherd such as Lord Furher. She walked over to the bed and began to smack the covers beating it free of the dust. She looked to her side where on a dresser sat a beautiful painting of a young boy peering out of a window into a beautfiul and peaceful world. She stopped what she was previously attending to and walked over to the picture, picking it up and smiling at its beauty. Surely that must be a picture of Lord Czar Olsig's son, the new Lord of Tahj. She thought. A loud knock came at the door nearly making her drop the picture as she hurriedly placed the picture back on the dresser before hurrying back to her regularly scheduled task. 'Are you almost done in there Nikia?' Came the loud voice at the door. There was no doubt in Nikia's mind whom's voice that belonged to...her mothers. She had been dealing with it for too long not to know. 'Yes, Mother' Nikia said, blatantly lying. She heard the footsteps of her mother fade going down the hallway as she let out a big sigh of relief. Seeing that as a warning, she began to do her task as assigned to her. She stopped suddenly to rub her arms...a chill had entered the room, one that was not there before. She brushed it off though, figuring it as maybe a open window or a figment of her imagination. The chill returned once again though, this time with an more fierce breeze as Nikia began to get the feeling that someone is in the room with her. She turned around and began to look all over the room but she found no one. 'Who's there?' She called out but no one answered. Suddenly, she began to hear the sound of a fierce blowing wind. 'Nikia...' came a deep tonic voice that had the bones inside of Nikia's body vibrating from its deep tone. She turned around and looked at the mirror that stood on the dresser. A Black and purple hole had not emerged from the mirror, sucking up wind and giving off a cold breeze, colder than a winter night. A face appeared inside of the hole, a face torn and battered with bruises and scars. 'L-Lord Czar Olsig? I-is that you?' 'Yes it is Nikia. Now, I must speak with my son.' 'I'm sorry sir...b-but he has yet to arrive.' A lound roar of thunder shook the room as Nikia trembled and fell unto the floor. 'Well then you must release me from this portal in order to speak with him.' 'M-me? Why me?' 'Because my dear...' Without saying another word, the face of Lord Czar Olsig sunk into the portal once again this time, taking the body of Nikia in full with it. She traveled down a dark portal until suddenly she reached a place. This place reeked of crawling bodies whom had been tortured and beaten and now searching for a common cure. 'Wh-Where have you brought me?' 'This is what has become of me Nikia...I need you to release me from here so that I may speak with my son. Immediately.' 'Please let me o-out of here.' 'Help me, Nikia...Help...Me' His voice faded as everything around her went into a blur until suddenly...She hit the ground of the bedroom floor with a loud thud. She attempted to stand but it failed as she slowly seeped into a deep dark sleep... -- The midnight sky gave off an unforgiving darkness. They had been riding for hours now, on this journey to the great White Palace in Tahj in which Lord Furher would call his home. They had tied there horses now and stopped in order to take a break from the long rough and hard journey. Elden sat on a log over an open fire, throwing various tree branches and cones into the fire in order to keep its flame growing. He hovered his hands over the fire, rubbing them together for warmth. Dani slowly walked behind Elden and took a seat on the log next to him. He copied Elden's actions, placing his hands over the open fire as well seeking warmth. Silence dominated the communication between the two until Elden finally spoke up. 'Tommorrow, we'll arrive in Tahj where you will gain your father's inheritance Lord Furher.' Dani stopped rubbing his hands together for a second as he slowly peered over into the face of Elden who payed him no attention and instead continued to embark in the warmth. 'So what exactly does that entitle me to? More bloody brawls? More unholy cermonies? More gory acts of violence?' Elden gave a deep sigh as he shook his head in shame. 'What was that cermony about anyways?' said Dani. 'It was a contract to seal your ordainance as your father's replacement as Lord of Tahj.' 'If I go through with all of this...do I get my freedom? I want freedom to return to my homeland of Bogota and do what I choose.' 'You'll have your freedom sir...but that would not be a wise move in this situation.' 'Why not? Didn't my Father meet my mother in Bogota?' 'Your father had no blood feud to settle when he met your mother.' 'Blood feud? What type of Blood feud do I have bound against me?' 'Whenever a Lord is murdered, whether in his bedroom or in his great hall,' Elden grabbed a cone and tossed it into the fire. 'his replacement takes up the responsibility of finding the murderer and exacting revenge.' 'Revenge eh? And what if I choose not to participate in this clan war.' 'If you choose not avenge the murder, you father's soul shalt not rest in peace and this land...our land will rot. Our animals will die, ours crops will die, and this unforgiving winter shall never end. The honor of vengeance falls upon you Mi' Lord.' 'So you're saying that I must kill my father's murder or else all of these people, including you, could very well starve to death? Is that what this whole thing is all about? You abducted me, basically stripping me from the confines of my family without notice..all because of a stupid and barbaric Blood feud?' After stopping to think of what Elden had previously stated of Dani seeking his father's murderer, he began to flashback to the cermony that had taken place earlier. More specifically, the man he had spotted in the crowd. The man whom's cold glare shot straight through Dani without relent. 'Are you okay Lord Furher?' Dani shook his head and regained his focus. 'Y-yea..I'm fine. But you said earlier that no one know's whom the murderer is...how do you expect to kill him.' That intense and pale look of cold anger from that man in the crowd crossed the mind of Dani once again. 'People talk...it'll eventually leak out one of day.' The fire that burned within that man's eyes...it just would not leave Dani's thoughts. Elden stood up and began to walk off but not before speaking his final words to Lord Furher. 'No one will harm you Lord Furher. You will be well protected in Tahj.' Giving Dani a pat on the back, Elden walked off leaving Dani sitting on the log with the memory of the man's deep gaze left to dry. Dani began to hug his knee's as he thought to himself. However 'safe' Elden proclaimed him to be, he still had come to a conclusion that Elden had not. Someone had betrayed and murdered his father. Somone whom hated all of his father's blood. Someone who was out there...waiting for him. -- 'Did you hear the news Lilly? Someone summoned the spirit of Lord Czar Olsig.' 'Whoever did that is sure to have some serious hell to pay.' Nikia stood at the wall, peeking into the kitchen where her two sisters sat at the table gossiping to one another. The talk over Lord Czar Olsig deeply disturbed Nikia...especially since she knew who it was that summoned the spirit of Lord Olsig...her. She slowly turned the corner and walked into the room mildly shaking but not enough for it to be clearly seen by her other siblings. The room grew silent amongst her presence. Without looking up at the other two sisters, she pulled a chair from under the table and took a seat with her head still hanging low. 'Are you okay Nikia' She quickly shot her head up from the peering at the ground and into the eyes of her sister. 'I-I'm fi-fine.' 'You don't look fi--' 'I SAID I'M FINE!' The two sisters were taken back from the sudden outroar. A moment of silence occured as one of the sisters broke the silence by beggining to chatter to her other sister. 'Anyways, Danna, I can't wait until Lord Furher arrives. I hear he's a nice young man.' 'So true sister girl. I hear he's a nice looking gentleman as well.' The two began to giggle amongst each other in a female manner as Nikia sits at the far end of the table, nervously biting her nails and bouncing her legs. What if they found out that it was indeed she that had released the spirit of Lord Olsig. What if Lord Furher found out...what if Elden found out...or worse...what if Lord Nosferatu found out. She had seen teh things he had done to others, the slays that he had commited to anyone whom messed with his plans. Lord Furher had been called by Lord Nosferatu personally to take his father's place in his Ministry...and if she was revealed as a virus to that plan, she could very well suffer the same fate as some of the others. That was enough to make a tear slowly stream down her face. That thought, alone. Quickly though, she wiped the tear away so that he sisters may not see it. 'Nikia...' The voice scared Nikia as she jumped and turned around to see her mother standing over her with a cold glare. 'Y-yes..mother?' 'Sir Roman would like to speak with you downstairs.' Her eyes grew big. Sir Roman was the executor of the Palace, the man whom ordered execution until all whom distracted the works of the Powers that be. No one ever had a good reasoning for visiting the Roman...and she knew that she would be no different. The fear of what might occur in that dungeon was like Hell thinking about. Her mother guided her down the stairs and softly knocked on the door of the room. Hearing no answer, she slowly creaked the door open and guided her daughter inside. The entire room was dark except for the spotlight that shone in the middle of the room illuminating a high chair in which an old man with a long white beard and a bald head sat. Nikia and her mother slowly walked through the darkness and partially enter the light where they faced The Roman. 'Nikia...young Nikia. I'm sure you've heard about the recent sightings of Lord Czar Olsig. His spirit has been released from his portal meaning he cannot rest in peace placing the land of Tahj in deep danger. It has also come to my attention that the sightings began after you had finished preparing the room of Lord Furher, which by the way was not finished. How do you plea?' Nikia held her head down looking at the ground as he mother tapped her on the back. She looked up at her mother with a puppy dog gaze that would have Dracula weaping. 'ANSWER ME!' Crying was too tempting right about now, but somehow she held her ground. 'Sir...I-I would never anything to harm the land of Tahj, Lord Furher, or Lord Olsig.' The man remained silent for a moment before looking into the eyes of Nikia's mother. 'Does she know Sophia?' 'How could she Roman? I have held my ground all of these years just as I sworn.' 'If I find out that you have been lying to me young Nikia...you know exactly what will occur.' 'Yes sir.' 'Now get out of my sight.' Without another word, the two turned around and began to make their way out of the room. Once they had exited, Nikia looked up at her mother. 'What did he mean mother?' 'Nothing Nikia...now wipe your tears. Lord Furher is nearly here.' -- The days passed and Lord Furher had arrived at the Palace now. He lied in his bedroom channeling the events of the day and his new positioning as the Lord of Tahj. His role was one that he had not approved of, nor did he ask to take part in...but instead he was forced into this situation. He had to claim his father's throne and serve his dues to Lord Nosferatu. But as he sat on his bed, peering at the war torn land of Tahj from his window, he knew that someway...somehow...he had to get out of this mess. Someway. Somehow. -- As I look to my past for rejuvination, I realize just how massive the battle at hand truly is. The time of friendship between me and you Ozric has long past And now comes the time where you learn the other side of me. The side of me that others have feared Loathed And prayed they would never have to meet. The part of me that can go longer, harder, and faster, Than any other. The part of me that can take the pain, turn it into pleasure, and return in ten-fold, Vice Versa. The part of me, That lays in waiting for the right place, and the right time. Perched above, looking downward onto all that I consider prey, and watching, waiting, and smiling..as the time slowly arrives, And my chance is given. I wish it didn't have to be this way But you decided to become curious of things outside of the Dark Age. And you know what they say. Curiosity killed the Clown.
