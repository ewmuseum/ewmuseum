Date: 10/10/05 Let’s get physical. Physical. [Recorded 10.07.05] The scene opens up in what is very clearly a doctor’s office. It’s a small room, with two chairs that seem to be upholstered in turquoise felt. There’s, of course, the phone that is always blinking but never ringing. On the wall sits the sinister-looking needle disposal bin, right next to all the cheerful children’s drawings that say things like “DRWING OF A TURTLE” or “THANS FOUR FXING MY FUT” or “Thanks for helping me achieve my goal of having my lips surgically attached to Hoyt’s ass cheeks. Signed, Peter Vetra.” Enter Chandler Tsonda. The new PRIME signee steps through the door, accompanied by a short, brunette nurse with a clipboard in hand. “Take a seat, Mr. Tsonda,” comes the sweet, patient voice of the twentysomething nurse. She flashes him a small smile without opening her mouth (Chandler’s Note: who wouldn’t? I’m a fucking fox). Today, the Model Citizen is well-dressed, but slightly casual. He’s wearing a grey sweater, with the white collar of a polo jutting out from underneath, and loose-fitting jeans that hang down over parts of his LaCoste sneakers. Suffice to say, even on his off-days, he still looks like he spent a minimum of half an hour preparing his outfit. I don’t even need to touch the subject of the hair; it’s beautiful. Assume as much unless notified otherwise. For the first time in his life, Chandler does something quietly. He sits down in the chair, immediately realizing that it’s tremendously uncomfortable. While the nurse turns her back to him, scribbling on her clipboard, he makes no effort to hide the fact he’s checking her out. “So, Laura, how long have you been working here at Dr. James’ office?” The nurse turns around, surprised at hearing her own name. Before she can ask the most clichéd question in the history of books, film, theater, and adult movies, Chandler interjects his own sappy, gawd-awful romantic comedy response. His voice is a sweet melody in the air. Yeah, sweet like choking to death on maple syrup. “I looked at your name tag. I always notice a pretty girl.” She flashes the ol’ pearly whites and her face opens up in a larger smile. Laura is one of those girls who is immensely prettier when she smiles and her cheeks perk up and her whole face follows. “Thank you, I’ve been working here for about three months. I haven’t seen you here; are you a new patient?” She’s smarter then she looks; she makes it seem like the question mattered more then the compliment. By the way, are we having an “I’ll buy you a drink and then ask you how often you come here” conversation in a doctor’s office? Jesus, Tsonda’s gone so bush-league on us. As she waits for the answer, Laura turns back to the clipboard, noting a couple of things, or pretending to so it makes her seem less interested. “Well, I’m a professional wrestler. Back when I was wrestling in the Jersey area, Dr. James was my personal physician. And now that I’m signed up with a fed, a much more important nationally renowned fed, I need to get cleared again before I get back in the ring.” No reaction. The professional wrestler bit hasn’t hooked her yet. Never fear, our hero never met a piece of 7.5 and above ass that he wouldn’t pursue. Still, Chandler knows that only a rookie would get overexcited and mishandle this situation. He leans back and says nothing, allowing the silence to settle in the air. “Hmm…well, Dr. James is very busy today.” Dagger. Laura doesn’t believe this PRIME newbie is the type of superstar he makes himself out to be. She thinks he’s not at the top of the doctor’s list. But two can play at this game. A cocky smile spreads on Chandler’s face, a look that usually accompanies some sort of mischief at a wrestling show, but now it only says that his interest is piqued by the nurse’s challenge. “Why don’t you let him know I’m here? I’m a fairly important patient; he’ll want to know if he’s keeping me waiting.” The words are as condescending as possible, but Tsonda tries to keep his voice in a flat tone, to disguise the fact they’re now playing a game against one another. “That’s not really the protocol here. It’s first come, first served.” Well, Chandler would beg to differ, missy. Still, he has a look of amusement on his face as he charmingly replies to this direct insinuation that’s he’s just not really that important. “Just call him.” Three words. Equal to a slap in the face. Maybe he’s losing the possibility of a date, but there’s a certain pleasure to be derived out of putting a nay-sayer in their place (Chandler’s Note: That slut cadet gave me attitude. Besides, she wasn’t going for anything until she believed I was the fucking cat’s meow. Which I am.) He opens his mouth to speak again, but then decides better of it. He leaves it at just those three words, basically daring our friend Laura to test the sway that Chandler Tsonda holds where she has been working hard for three months to help pay off mountains of school loans and he has been sitting for two minutes, grinning like a cheeseball asshole and hitting on her. She takes the dare. She picks up the phone, dials three numbers for the doctor’s extension, and speaks into the receiver, although we can’t hear what comes out of the other side of the line. “Dr. James. Yes, there’s a Chandler Tsonda here to see you,” she begins, making sure to add just enough change in her voice to notify both the doctor and Chandler of her growing displeasure with the situation. “Yes, sitting right in front of me.” There seems to be a flurry of activity from the other end of the line after she says this, but with sufficiently better acting skills then those displayed in the movie The Island, she keeps her face exactly the same, betraying nothing. “Okay, I will.” Laura hangs up and turns away, keeping Tsonda in suspense. “So, what’d Doc James have to say to his ornery little strumpet?” Okay, the date is definitely off, but you’re talking about the pettiest human being on earth. This is a guy who has broken off a relationship with a friend (he used to have them, yes) because he was beaten at the paper football game. Of course he’s going to make a huge deal out of nothing and let the unbelieving nurse become his nemesis over something like this. “Well, I’m dying to hear the answer, Laura.” It takes all the will-power in her body for Laura not to tell Tsonda to fuck off and die. But that’s unprofessional. Still, there’s no way to save face. “Doctor James will be in in a minute.” The tone is solemn, defeated, and angry. And music to Chandler’s ears. “BOOYAH! I KNEW…ahem…erm…like I said, I’m kind of a big deal around here. People know me.” Chandler rubs his victory in Laura’s face without care for her feelings. “You stole that from a movie, jackass.” But this isn’t enough to stop the victory celebrations of Chandler Tsonda, in whose head there are trumpets playing and fanfare of all sorts thanks to this grand conquest. Laura opens the door and leaves the room without another word. It’s another couple of minutes before Doctor James arrives. He’s a white guy with a full head of graying hair and strange ear hair that protrudes in tufts. He’s slightly taller then Tsonda, putting him at about six feet even and he’s not fat, but he’s burly and takes up a lot of space, especially in the small space of the examination room. “Chandler, my friend!” This man is genuinely excited to see Chandler Tsonda in his office, whether he’s a wrestling fan or just aware that Tsonda has signed a PRIME contract that will allow the doctor to siphon more money from him. The two shake hands. “How have you been doing since I last saw you?” There’s a slight pause in the reaction from Tsonda. This is not an arena in which he wanted to be conversing. “Well, I’ve been doing okay. The industry went through a slump, so no one had the funds to sign me to the deal I wanted. I did a couple of ad campaigns for Hilfiger and Ralph Lauren, but I basically just trained and made myself a more complete wrestler.” The latte part, true. The former, not so much. Tsonda couldn’t get signed because he was known throughout the industry to be an asshole of epic proportions to everyone around him. He was universally blacklisted and the only work offered had been in unknowns or indies. “Hmm…didn’t Hoyt Williams and Karina Wolfenden sign pretty big deals over at PRIME?” “Hey fu...yeah, I suppose they did. Well, I looked at PRIME and I just recently decided that I wanted to go there, so then we were able to reach a mutual decision about things.” Chandler almost slips up and lets his fiercely competitive nature get the better of him. The doctor suspects nothing. “Well, that’s good to hear. Let’s get to work here and we can get you in and out, right quick.” He looks at the clipboard left by the late, great Laura, and mumbles out loud. “Well, the PRIME office sent over weight, height, and eyesight and hearing tests. I don’t need to reconfirm those for the type of physical they requested. In fact, I only need two things. First, we’ll need to perform blood and DNA tests. They’ll do that in the lab as you leave.” Well, that leaves one other thing. Tsonda waits eagerly to hear what it is that he has to do before he can get the hell out of here and back to the hotel in Kansas City to prepare for a pretty-fucking-important match as far as debuts goes, going up against the PRIME Universal Champion Hoyt Williams. Not only the Universal Champion, but noted rival of Tsonda from their stint in PWC. But first, there’s one obstacle. “I have to check for lumps.” Chandler recoils in horror. “WHA?” All I have in this world are my balls and my word. And I don’t let anyone check either of them for lumps unless absolutely necessary. That’s Tsonda’s thought process via Scarface, for those of you dumb enough to miss that (Chandler’s Note: …or Imperium members. ZING!). “Chandler, it’s part of going to get a physical. I realize we used to forego those formalities but we’re working with a different third party now. If there’s any sign of cancer…even down there….I have to let PRIME know, by law.” Chandler almost lets loose an exclamation of exasperation but decides better of it, gulps, and realizes his fate. He steps towards the doctor and drops trough. The camera below Tsonda’s lower half becomes blurred out (Chandler’s Note: If it weren’t blurred out, this would be the most sought after Internet video ever. Don’t act like it wouldn’t.). The doctor lets out a small cough. “What the fuck was that?” The doctor looks caught unaware by this eruption from Tsonda. “I’m not sure I catch your meaning, Chandler.” “That cough. Were you trying to stifle a giggle?” Looks like someone has penis anxiety and his name rhymes with Sandler Fonda, if you catch my drift. “You better pray to Jesus you didn’t just laugh at Mighty Kong Johnson.” Yes, he’s pathetic enough to name his penis (and no, this wasn’t supposed to be stolen from Anchorman, but it is. So sue me.). “Chandler, it’s nothing to be ashamed of. It’s a…good size.” Tsonda’s still fuming, but he relents and his face turns to utter horror as he feels the most awful feeling a straight man can feel: the prodding fingers of another man groping every inch of skin. The camera doesn’t show it, but from Chandler’s reaction, it’s fair to say that the last time someone did this, there was cheap champagne involved and a very awkward conversation the next morning. Finally, the doctor pulls his gloved hand away. Chandler gives a shiver and pulls his jeans back up. He looks at the doctor, who is disposing of the glove at the moment. Chandler awkwardly offers his hand and the two men shake again, without a word between them. “Yeah, so…I’ll just get those tests done and hit the road. I’ll be fighting Hoyt on Wednesday on...the Playboy Channel. After that little exchange we just had, I’m gonna pray that you do have a subscription to said channel and that I don’t have to worry about just having been victimized.” The doctor just nods while checking off things on his chart. Chandler breathes a big sigh of relief and steps out through the door. FIN
