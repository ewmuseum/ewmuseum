And On The Seventh Day... (Match RP - w/ Tessa v Dark Age)

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st January, 2005. And On The Seventh Day...Written: 31st January, 2005. 'In this white wave,In this silence,I believe.' - Sarah McLachlan. I - Tuesday. Every action - be it physical, chemical, or biological - has a threshold. An amount of energy, no matter how vast or miniscule, that needs to be put it to 'prime the pump', as it were. To create energy from formation of chemical bonds, they first need to be broken. To initiate combustion, a level of vaporisation has to be achieved. But Karina Wolfenden's problem was not on such an industrial scale... all she wanted to do was sleep. 'Ughhhh,' was her laboured groan as she once again checked her bedside clock, where 5am taunted her in LED red, burning itself into her retinas. Over four hours ago, she'd collapsed onto her bed, barely having the energy to stagger over to it. Physical excesses, flights, delays, and the mentally straining family obligations had taken their toll on the natural reserves of energy the K-Wolf usually possessed in almost selfish amounts. No position she'd warped her flexible body into would allow her to drift off... some barbed part of her thoughts always managing to hold onto consciousness. 'Grrrr,' Kari wearily growled, grabbing an unfortunate pillow, thumping it against the wall in frustration, before holding it over her face. 'KNEW I should have gotten those sleeping pills...' Determinedly, Wolfenden then sat up, swinging the pillow from over her face to under her head in one fluid motion. Tired of pulling the covers with her, Karina kicked them off the bed, satisfied that a white vest and pyjama bottoms would keep her warm enough. Reaching back, she put her hands under the pillows, and nestled her head down into them. Deep, cleansing breaths brought her entire upper-body to life, admittedly humble chest rising with every inhalation. Comfort was complete, every strand of her pink and blonde mane tightly tied back into a pony tail so it couldn't tickle her pale skin. After the week she'd had, and the early hour of the morning in which she dwelled, the K-Wolf should have been out like a light... but still nothing. Behind their lids, eyes were still very much alert, to the point that she'd willingly prise them from their sockets with a rusty spoon. 'Maybe I should just bang my head against the wall until I pass out,' an almost agonised groan forced out, Karina stretching her tall frame out across the bed. Blinds were down, and curtains were drawn, letting none of the ambient light in through her ceiling to floor windows. Anything with noisy electrics were over the other side of the apartment, too far for any hum to drift. Her two cats, Psylocke and Betsy, were holed up in their usual slumbering places, offering no nuisance to their exhausted owner. Even the phone was off the hook, in expectation of Tessa's like clockwork 8am call to make sure she showed up for ReVolution on time the next day. For a woman who had seemed to have mastered her body, extorting its full potential in every activity, she was now feeling it fight back. The last 6 days had each taken their toll, with little or no time set aside in-between to recover... so on the 7th day, she did not rest. Tightly clenched fists thudded against the innocent mattress, venting some of Karina frustrations, before she rolled over and hugged the pillows tightly enough to send and flurry of feathers bursting into the air. 'Just...' she then sighed, sandwiching her head between the pillows. 'FUCKING...' was then added in uncharacteristic language as her feet kicked against the mattress. 'Go to...' followed on as she took the pillows and dumped them on the floor. 'SLEEP! Kari finally ordered herself, rolling off the bed and landing in the mass of covers and pillows on the floor. Hardly the best preparation with possibly her two biggest matches in PRIME thus far approaching. But if being held against her will in the waking world wasn't bad enough, she still had the ranting lecture from Tessa to look forward to if she turned up looking like an extra from Night of the Living Dead. 'Just... just... don't do anything you'd normally do before next week, okay?' Tessa had told her before leaving ReVolution 54... the 8 inches she lacked in height beside Kari more than made up for with the measure of her words. Knowing that she wouldn't even speak to Tessa again for nearly a week, Karina didn't exactly promise to obey. 'Ugh?' she'd remarked back as she strode out the doors. 'Er, okay, I guess...' Wishing that she'd vacuumed more often, Karina wrapped the covers around her, encasing herself in their warmth. She'd tried to catch forty winks waiting for her flight out of Bern, but when you're gradually delayed from 8am through to 11pm, never knowing if the wait will be 30 minutes or 6 hours, there's never a quiet time to drift off. 'Mom! Mom!' a kid had been screaming as her eyes had crept closed, almost ushering forth a nap. 'He keeps teasing me!' 'Jimmy,' the woman had then throatily yelled, sounding at if at least 40 cigarettes worth of smoke had polluted her lungs every day. '*HYUKK!* Behave!' Coffee after coffee had kept Karina's bloodstream topped up with caffeine, getting her through the agitation and tiring farce that was her flight delays. The airport seats did little to soothe the dull throb from her right thigh, where a rather over-expectant Snowboard trick had left her plummeting down onto the compacted snow. Knowing how to land had kept Karina out of many a hospital, but still... she had to land on something. The blanket of pure white below her gave no perspective, and no judgement of when she'd hit the icy surface. Knowing enough not to land with her hands and feet and shatter the joints like sugary glass substitute, and that her angle of plummet ruled out a rolling landing, Kari took the thud on her fleshiest part... which had since brewed up a deep, brooding purple bruise. Karina thought that if only she had the crisp, alpine air to quench her lungs, then she'd have drifted off to sleep in an instant. Her apartment, despite its less-than-humble dimensions, seemed stuffy and enclosing. Standing atop the peaks, she'd felt the thin air in her mouth crystallise before it had even been exhaled... everything just feeling cleaner. Pure. 'I didn't do nuffin'!' the boy had exclaimed in retort, nearly tempting Karina to butt-in and correct his double negative. 'Give him a bloody slap,' Karina had muttered under her breath, after scanning the terminal to see that there were no other spare seats... anywhere. She'd been tempted to try and walk off the throbbing tightening of her thigh, but didn't exactly fancy standing for 34847495 hours when someone snuck in and stole her seat. 'Heh,' had been her response when it was suggested people sat on the tiled floor... mainly because she hadn't seen a cleaner in the 6 hours she'd been loitering there. It was meant to be a quick, relaxing jaunt. A day in the snow, before flying back for a family dinner, then home to prepare for ReVolution and the tag title tournament finale. It had turned into anything but. As her eyes were almost rolling back into her head, Karina even managed to find the constant updating of the departures board a welcome moment of excitement... instantly ruined by the fact that her flight was never one of the ones soon to board. 'I want a burger,' the ever-so-delightful children had then pestered their mother, causing her to drag their bags towards the nearest fast-foot outlet with the brats in tow. Seeing them leave, Karina instantly dropped her travel bag on the 2nd seat across, then swung her legs up onto it. Her slender, 6 foot frame stretched across all four seats, finally easing the dull, distracting pain. 'Excuse me,' one man had enquired, about 5 minutes after Karina had first noticed him sniffing around... nervous about saying anything. 'Can I si-' Staring out with tired, bloodshot eyes from under her choppy pink fringe, Karina let it be known within a fraction of a second that the seats were not going to be taken from her... the frustrated flash of burning emerald almost sending the grown man scurrying off with his tail between his legs. The same stare was given to her ceiling as Karina remained wrapped tightly in the covers, not even feeling the subtlest of tugs towards slumber. 'Gahhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh,' Karina proclaimed, throwing the covers off and pulling herself up to her feet. She didn't know what it was. It might have just been that she was physically too tired to sleep. It might have been that she was so used to being active, that she'd simply transcended the need to sleep... even when tired. Or maybe, just maybe, it could be pre-match nerves? Before teaming with Tessa, the K-Wolf barely bothered to find out who she'd be facing before arriving at the show. She'd turn up - usually late - do her thing between the ropes, leave - usually early - live her life for 6 days, 22 hours, then show up again. If she won, she didn't celebrate or gloat. If she lost, she didn't curse or question herself. Karina relied on nobody else, and had nobody relying on her. That was, until she - for some reason - agreed to partner Tessa Windsor in tag team action. Tessa's determination to disprove all her critics should have set-off Kari's self-preservation warning signals, but Karina knew what it meant to her friend, and lacked the heart to say no. Little did Karina realise that every action she took could warrant a nagging reaction from Miss Windsor. 'Ugh,' Kari groaned, stretching as she took wide paces across to the kitchen. 'I'm going to NEED rest if we're facing the Dar-... wait, did I just? Oh, GOD no...' Whether it was the constant thought keeping her awake or not, acting as ballast to weight her down, Kari had her upcoming match on her mind. 'I'm going to kill her for this...' was let out with a sigh, aimed at the absent Tessa. Kari's eyes squinted as she opened the fridge, and stared into the harshly lit reminder that she never really had anything grocery-wise when she was there all week... let alone when she knew she was going away. Still feeling the meal that she'd pulled herself away from early sitting heavy on her, Karina just grabbed a bottle of Evian and took a couple of quenching sips. Feeling the ice cold liquid wash down her throat, the sensation of standing atop the frozen peak came back to her... attempting to usher in a vision of serenity to help her drift off to sleep. Standing and walking had left her head feeling heavy, and a dull, insular pounding was felt from deep within. She'd lost track of how much she'd drunk earlier. In fact, she'd lost track of most of the evening. 'How much longer?' she had kept asking the driver, to the point that he must have wanted to stop the cab and throw her out. The delayed flight had knocked her schedule off, and rather than a relaxing afternoon before a stuffy family engagement, she was left travelling straight from the airport to the restaurant... already over 15 minutes late. Hardly dressed the occasion, Wolfenden must have been the only woman to have ever been allowed in wearing a baggy purple sweatshirt and overly-baggy, near-engulfing black cargo pants. 'This way,' was delivered with a slightly suspicious, displeased tone as her appearance attracted the attentions of the other smartly dressed diners. 'Dare I ask?' was her father's greeting, met with an apathetic shrug from his less-then-talkative daughter. All she'd wanted to do was go home and collapse into bed, feeling slumber take subtle tugs at her. But the dinner wasn't about her, or her parents, or their expectations, or her weary state of mind. It was for her brother... her lone confident in the Wolfenden family, and the only person she'd adopt a false front for. 'Your brother has been promoted... again,' her father boasted in such a grandiose tone that Max seemed embarrassed. 'He's taking on the partners' clients now.' So different that there was still a lurking, jovial afterthought of adoption or infidelity tucked away at the back of their minds, Max and Karina shared little but a surname. But despite the vast distance travelled in opposite directions since they chose their paths, there was still mutual pride and respect between the Wolfenden siblings. 'Awesome,' Kari offered as congratulations, already filling her wine glass to the brim, attracting a rather disapproving glance from her father. As much as she wanted to let Max know how proud she was of him, such events always made the K-Wolf uncomfortable. From a young age, such expectation had been pressed down upon both of them. Constant talks and willing connections had moulded Max into his father's son, while the pressure had forced Karina from the mould all together, sending the stubborn young woman to pursue her own dreams. Every time they met, Max seemed to have achieved something that Karina would never have had the attention-span or focus to aim for. He'd graduated in the top five percent of his class, while Karina dropped out just after her first Christmas, after already having flunked most of her courses on attendance alone. By the age of 20, he'd had work experience at the kind of firms most of his class wouldn't have a shot at until they were 30, and very, very lucky... while her wrestling booking fees were the first taxable income she'd ever earned. 'Why, thank you, sis,' Max raised a glass to from across the table. 'Try to leave at a few weeks before asking for a loan, please?' She knew it was in jest, and flashed him a sarcy grin as such. Looking around the table, she couldn't help but notice the smiles radiating from her usually stoic parents. They'd have probably relayed the news throughout their social circle already, giving Max at least a week of politely taking congratulatory call after congratulatory call. Every achievement of his sent ripples through their corner of society, felt days, often weeks after. What was Kari going to offer to the celebration? 'Hey, dad, I've got a shot at the PRIME tag team titles this week!'? While he was at least willing to accept another of his daughter’s unconventional life choices, it still wasn't something he'd exactly welcome as dinner conversation. 'Are you alright, Kari?' her mother enquired, seeing her look distant as sip after sip was taken from her glass. 'You don't look well.' 'Lonnnnnnnnnnnnnnnnng week,' Kari sighed, looking awkward at becoming the conversation topic, and wanting to quickly shift focus. 'So when's the pay rise kick in?' As the meal went on, Karina's focus grew more distant, and the threads of conversation weaved into one inaudible patchwork quilt... only interrupted by the splashing of wine which regularly topped up her glass... and would later leave her feeling groggily dehydrated. Another glug of the icy water helped stave off the sensation, before Karina returned the hand-numbingly cold bottle back to the fridge. As the door swung closed, her jaw acted in opposition, dropping over with an uninhibited yawn. As memories of her exhausting week were replayed by Karina, the feelings of being just on the brink of sleep clawed their way to the surface, where they were most needed. As soon as the whole-body action of the first yawn had subsided, another was initiated, almost straining the K-Wolf's jaw. Dragging her feet along the tiles and rug, Karina pulled her reluctant body back towards her bed, just about reaching the mattress as she collapsed forward. Before her head had even bounced back off the springs, Karina's eyes were closed, fused together as she let go of her strained grip on the world of the awake. Now all she had to do was wake up in time for the ride to ReVolution...
