Preggos are not going to take abandonment lightly.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: CWMC Round 3b What the fuck's a Face-Eater to do? He's facing an ongoing investigation with pending charges of extortion. He's out $88,000, being held as 'evidence' for said investigation, doubting he'll ever see it again. Some hormone-ridden Preggo (the worst kind) has decided to crash at his home because she's too scared to be alone. Worst of all, he's out of weed. Facey sits in his laundry room, a tiny space in the corner of the basement that has become his retreat post-Preggo. To compensate for his lack of marijuana, he's opened a Grape White Owl and filled it with various stems, seeds, lint-laced pocket buds, and joint roaches. Two puffs later, and he's already more miserable than when he wasn't high. "JESUS CHRIST BITCHING ON A MESSAGE BOARD ABOUT PPV RESULTS!!!!" Followed by hacking, wheezing, and getting extremely light headed. "Aside from the blistering migraine located directly behind my left eye, this ain't half bad." Even clambaking in shit-smoke is more amusing than hanging out upstairs with that abysmal blackhole of depression. Adam was first under the impression that Preggos were blessings; gifts from the heavens for men who like cute-but-not-disgustingly-fat-chicks. But this horrid experience is quickly turning him off to pregnant women forever. He knows that not ALL Preggos are this whiny. She's merely reacting how most docile, emotionally frail objec—I mean women—would react in this situation. But come on? How much can a woman bitch about her missing boyfriend before she realizes that he ditched her? Get past the denial already, and move on to the stage where you feel the need to fuck like a rabbit in retalliation. But Adam can't be pushy because everything depends on this Preggo. She's very integral to the Police's case. After the incident at the drop, she almost went into a state a shock was and released without making a statement. However, between two conflicting stories from Facey and the hoodlums he ALLEGEDLY paid to set it up, as well as the confusing addition of Facey's $88thousand, her version of how the events transpired is what will decide whether he faces a Jury Box. He isn't worried. All he needs is the patience to let her stay for as long as she needs—or until she makes her statement. For some reason he can't place, she loves him. But not in that "I want you to fuck me until my kid jumps out" kind of way; the kind of love that makes you go "OH I COULD JUST TELL YOU ANYTHING!" While this type of fag-shit isn't normally Adam's forte, he can play the part for however long he needs to. Inside the laundry room, he hears the door at the top of the basement staircase open. His shoulders sink at April's impending entrance. "ADAM." He sighs plumes of smoke. "WHAT?!" "THERE'S SOMEONE AT THE DOOR." "SO? ANSWER IT!" ".........MAYBE YOU SHOULD." "JUST ANSWER THE DOOR!" "BUT I'M--" The door swings open to the laundry room. Adam exits flanked by a cloud of smoke, causing April to quickly retreat from the basement.. "You're not still scared, are you." Facey follows her to the main floor. "It's just--"" "You don't have to explain. I understand, I just hope you learn to deal and don't become a crazy hermit lady." He says with a smile, passing her and heading towards the front door. "By the way, could you please make me some chocolate milk? Just take some ice-cream from the freezer and put it in cup on high for, like, five minutes and it'll be good. THANKS." April heads to fulfill his request, disappearing into the kitchen, as Adam reaches the door knob. Upon opening, the sight he sees isn't familiar: a particularly large stranger with an angry look on his face. Must be one of them recovering crack addicts trying to get donations for the local church's youth group, those pesky solicitors. "Are you the Face-Fucker?" he asks. "Are you--" Adam can't even work in a witty retort before the man clocks him in the left side of his face. "MOTHER FUCK! I HAVE A FUCKING MIGRAINE, YOU ASSHOLE!" The man exhibits complete disregard for manners and enters Facey's home, gripping him by the throat and forcing him to his knees. "WHERE IS SHE!" he screams, his mouth two inches away from Adam's face. "DO YOU NEED TO BE THIS FUCKING CLOSE? SERIOUSLY, YOU'RE SPITTING ALL OVER ME!" "WHERE'S APRIL?!" "Brock?" The man looks up and with the sight of the Preggo his face immediately swells with tears. His grip on Adam's neck loosens, allowing him to adjust himself. April Monday stands before a man she never knew if she'd see again, a cup filled with Chocolate ice cream in her hand. "Wh- What are you..." "April... I ca-- We have to leave here. Now." "NO NO NO!" Adam screams, pointing at her. "YOU HAVE TO MICROWAVE IT FIRST!" Brock turns back down to Adam, driving his fist into him one more time in an effort to shut him up, not knowing that nothing short of cutting out his voice box will make him be quiet. "Brock! Stop it!" April screams at him, herself now overcome with tears. "What the FUCK is your problem?" Face-Eater attempts to crawl away, but Brock pins him to the ground with a boot on his back. "You think you can get away with this? Huh, you piece of shit?" He kicks Adam in his ribs. "You think you wouldn't have to fucking pay?" "You're hurting him, Brock!" "He's a fucking scumbag! Can't you see through the bullshit? He's fucking manipulating you! Ape, c'mon. You can't really--" "Oh, SO YOU'RE THE ASSHOLE THAT I MADE THE MISTAKE OF DEFENDING YOUR HONOR?! I'll have you know, prickhole, that if it weren't for me, NO ONE WOULD EVEN GIVE A SHIT ABOUT YOU BEING GONE. While you were out playing Foxtail or whatever the FUCK you were doing, I WAS FIGHTING FOR YOUR NAME IN THE TOURNAMENT YOU BAILED ON! I WAS OUT ON THE STREETS, TRYING TO FIND OUT WHAT HAPPENED TO YOU! Where were you, huh? Working for GCW? Paying your rent? No, you FUCKER. Was it you that saved April's life a week ago? HUH? DID YOU SMOKE OUT OUR CAPTORS AND LURE THEM INTO A FALSE SENSE OF SECURITY BEFORE BLUDGEONING THEM UNCONSCIOUS WITH A PORTABLE DVD PLAYER? NO, THAT WAS ME." Brock doesn't know why he's listened for so long, but enough is enough. He boots Facey right to the dome, dropping him flat and limp in an instant. "You're a sneaky piece of shit and I should break your fucking neck! You were the one that capitalized on my absence. Trying to take advantage of a poor young woman? What the fuck is the matter with you? You think I don't see through your bullshit?" April is sobbing at this point, completely broken in the situation. "What are you talking about!?!" "HE FUCKING LIED, APE. He set the whole thing up so he could... so he could..." "'SO HE COULD' WHAT, BROCK?" she screams, dropping the glass of ice cream. "SO HE COULD FUCK ME? IS THAT WHAT ALL THIS WAS FOR?!" "Probably! I don't fucking know what this sick fuck wanted!" "Well where the hell where you? WHERE THE HELL DID YOU RUN OFF TO?" Facey, on the ground, begins stirring to consciousness. "I'll tell you on the way. Right now, we've got to get the fuck out of here." "How long was I out for?" Facey asks. "On the way? Like I'm going ANYWHERE with you?" "April, I'm not joking. We have to leave this place NOW! There isn't time!" "What happened to my fucking milk?!" Face asks. "So there's time to beat the shit out of the guy who's trying to fuck your ex-fiancé, but not time to tell her where you've been?" "It's complica--'ex-fiancé'?" "Whoa. Burn." Facey says. Brock Shepherd looks at April with sullen eyes, the weight of pain inside of them almost unbearable. There is little that is keeping him from breaking down and crying right now, but he manages to keep his emotions intact. April has since stopped crying and regained her composure. "If you're so fucking pressed for time, Brock, you'd better be going." In respect of the woman he loves, in respect for their child she harbors, Brock leaves. But not before swearing he'd return. *Tea parties with Mike Wade excluded.
