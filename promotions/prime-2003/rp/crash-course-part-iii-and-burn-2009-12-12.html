Crash Course: Part III - ...And Burn

12 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-12-09 25th November “You're awfully quiet...” Her words bore into him like a tick, furthering the resentment he already harboured towards her and everything else. ReV 213 had only finished fifteen minutes before and he was back in the saddle of his brand new 2010 Ford F150. All the mod-cons and luxuries couldn't settle his brain after suffering second hand rentals for the past couple of months. Dropping out of the Jewel in the Crown to Chainz didn't help matters either. After such a successful year, losing his last three matches in a row had unnerved Von Kelsig terribly. Once to Gamble felt like a fluke, being beaten again was a problem and on the last ReVolution losing to Chainz proved that something was amiss. “Hey, you OK?” The angelic voice to his right could not sooth his temper as he lamented on his recent bad luck. Losing was a bitter woe, but that could be remedied. He was the largest and most powerful wrestler in the business, it wouldn't be long before the wins would start pouring in again. Under the crushing realization that everyone around him seemed to be against him however those losses were grating on his mind. “I'm bored...” He sighed, refusing to address the girl in the cab with him. Among the aches and pains of the main event a dull throbbing emanated from his temples, a scolding manifestation of the stress building up inside. His chest tightened as he thought about the truth Tyler Rayne had dropped on him. Under the employ of Tyler Nelson he felt a different sense of power, working alongside a man who could potentially make him should he somehow overthrow Lisa Tyler to regain his powers in PRIME. As a mercenary it shouldn't have troubled him so much that the Greediest Player in the Game was just using him as he had so many others in the past. Breaking the contract with Nelson would leave Von Kelsig on his own, and while he had no problem working alone again he had felt a sense of camaraderie working with the likes of Chainz and the Wolves of Slaughter though it hurt his image with the fans. Through his war with Shakur and those before him, the giant had tried to save face where Nelson had tainted his reputation with attacks on the more popular wrestlers in the company. If one good thing could come of leaving the Army it would be the chance to elevate himself over those pulling his strings and allow him to pick his own battles. The unfinished business with Tyler Rayne would be the closure he needed before venturing out on his own again; as painful was it promised to be. “God...” she groaned, throwing her head into her hands and rubbing her face before tossing her hair back and glaring at the giant next to her. “What is it?” he snapped back, snarling at her jaw slackened and she adopted a who, me? look of astonishment. “Jeez, calm down.” she whimpered with a petted lip, “What's up with you?” “What's up with me?” he chuckled stiflingly, “What's up is that my work is really pissing me off right now, I'm driving across the country every week just to be stabbed in the back and held back by people I should by rights be crushing underfoot...and to top it off I've got a stowaway riding with me who's going to get me jail or worse.” Christy was silent for a moment as Von Kelsig adjusted the air conditioning and cracked the window open a little to let the cloying heat out. A deep exhalation wafted out of the slit as a cloud of steam as the F-150 trundled around a corner onto a street filled with bars and restaurants. His hotel was a couple of blocks away but Jack and the boys were calling out to him from each of the pubs. “What are we doing here?” she asked, casting an unsure glance over the bustling street. “I've had a rough day and I need to unwind,” Von Kelsig drawled tiredly, “you however can make your own way from here. I don't want any part of what you're doing.” “But!” She yelled out desperately but the giant had already slammed his door shut and begun meandering around to the passenger side. Even as he opened the door for her her eyes were welling up with large beaded tears. “I don't have anywhere to go...” she pleaded. “Not my problem, girl.” Von Kelsig replied blankly, “If you want to run away from home or whatever you do it on your own.” “But you don't even know why I left! You've barely said a word since you found me!” she cried, tears falling freely down her face, at which Von Kelsig grumbled at the girl's obvious ability to cry on demand. “The hell do you want me to say? C'mon get out...” he motioned to her, taking her by the arm only to have her wrap both arms around his and pull him close. “Look,” she began, firing words like a Tommy gun between sniffles, “I already told you back in town why I wanted to get away, OK? My parents don't give a crap about me, and my boyfriend nearly killed me in that accident. I want a chance to make it on my own; my rules, my way. I want the kind of life you have, you're rich and famous aren't you? You did it all on your own?” Von Kelsig narrowed his eyes, clenching his jaw as he released himself from Christy's grip. “I don't care about any of that. I'm tired, I'm sore and tomorrow I need to leave for North Dakota. By now there's probably a search party looking for you around Waco and if you're hanging around me then it won't be long before someone recognizes you and calls me in as some kind of kidnapper or something. I don't need that in my life right now, I've got enough crap to deal with.” “But, but!” she stuttered. “But nothing. You don't want the kind of life I've lived. Come on...” he nodded to the street and waited for her to clamber out of the truck. As her dainty feet touched down on the cracked pavement, Von Kelsig reached in and grabbed his cell from the compartment under the stereo facia. Backing out he shut the door and locked the truck up, turning to face Christy and stopping in his tracks. Her lip trembled as she stared up at him out of big bleary eyes. She cut a lonely, pathetic figure, clutching her bag and imploring silently for sympathy. Looking her up and down, the grizzly giant couldn't help but fall for her act. “Alright, look...” he said eventually, clinging on to his last shred of contempt, “I'm going across the street for a pick-me-up. In an hour or so I'm calling it a night so you can go do whatever you want and meet me back here then.” “Then what?” she pipped, perking up almost immediately and marking her relief with a little hop on the spot. “I'm booked into the Holiday Inn a couple blocks away. You can stay the night but tomorrow we need to sort this out, because I'm not going back to Texas again.” She squealed with glee and leapt at him, hugging him tight before releasing the embrace and looking over the many clubs on the street. “OK, two hours...” she uttered, taking in the sights around her, “I'll be here, BYE!” She was off like a shot, bounding past groups of students and other revellers before deciding on a nightclub dripping with neon called Havana for her first taste of freedom. Her movements were subdued as the world passed her by, though inside she was bounding around like a deer on a vast meadow. Her eyes darted back and forth between the nightspots and even between blinks her gaze whipped over each new avenue as she came to it. Clutching the strap of her purse with vigour she smiled sweetly at the older punters, then lowered her head as she passed by groups of guys around her age, flicking her gaze up at the more handsome gents in a sexy submissive way. Havana had no queues waiting to enter during the week, and issuing a quiet greeting to the bouncer she slipped inside and took a deep invigorating breath. Running a hand over his tired features, Von Kelsig watched her disappear into the club before he trudged across to the street to a small redbrick affair called Lafferty's, where some of Tennessee's finest malts were waiting to help take the edge off. ---- The vacant odour of past revelling clung to the cool air as salty sweat and stale alcohol stains clung to the upholstery of the booths lining the dancefloor beyond the bar room. Random chatter clattered around her from the many busy tables as grafters enjoyed a refreshing post-shift bevvy with their mates or neighbours or kin. The louder noises came from the few booths occupied by the younger and more boisterous publicans enjoying the time between classes and studying. One such table was especially crowded, with chairs adopted from other booths to accommodate the extra students cawing and hawing over their beers. Already a part of their group in her mind she opened her mouth as though to offer conversation, pausing only to realize she was still alone in the middle of the floorspace. Quickly she turned to the bar and skipped over, taking a seat and taking another glance around the room as though waiting for a friend to raise their hand or show up at the door. “Getcha somethin' hun?” asked the barmaid, a slim blonde not unlike herself, with the exception of the work-worn wrinkles in her face. “Um...Bacardi Breezer, any kind.” she replied, pausing initially to figure out how much of her own money she might have to spend should she have to fly solo for the night. She daren't return to the giant to ask him for a lemmy. The girl behind the bar served up the drink and broke the twenty handed over, paying no mind to Christy as she dumped the change into her purse and sighed, peering past the aqua alcopop into the blurriness beyond the bottle. “Howdy chick.” Stopping short of her first drink, Christy turned to find a rugged looking guy smiling down at her. Tufts of blonde hair peeked under the rim of his suede cowboy hat, while his muscles pressed against the fabric of his shirt and jeans. He reminded her of Heath Ledger, and his smile even crinkled at the corners like the late actor's. “Hi...” she swooned in retort. “You waitin' on somebody or y'all just here on yer lonesome?” Her heart beat hard in her chest. Far and away from her boyfriend she was lost in the cowboy's smouldering gaze as he sucked in his bottom lip and furrowed his brow, giving her a once-over. Barely free from the constraints of her family she was all ready to let this handsome stranger take her away and care for her, though he had only just begun existing to her. “Um, I was here with someone but I kind've, uh...” she struggled to come up with some excuse as the cowboy studied her big blue eyes. A few tables back a bulky guy with a crew cut in a Titans shirt nudged a skinhead next to him in a black wifebeater and motioned to the pair at the bar. “On yer own, huh? Damn tragedy in this day and age when a pretty thing like you is left proppin' the bar by herself. I'm just over there,” he pointed back to the table where the fat guy and the skinhead were seated, “if you wanna join us fer a couple drinks?” “Sure.” she beamed. “Killer. I'm Clyde...” he offered his hand and shook hers gently when she accepted, glancing down at the soft skin to make sure that it wasn't velvet he was holding. “I'm Christy.” she replied, impressed by the large paw enveloped around her dainty hand. “You gotta be a Texas gal with that accent.” he smiled again, sending butterflies swirling up and around her gut. “Yep, I'm from Waco.” “Then I'll be damned if you don't know how to have a good time in a bar!” he laughed heartily, the booming chortle commanding the attention of several patrons even though there was no effort spent in it. Nodding his head to the table and laying out that gorgeous smile of his he walked away from her with the air of a leader about him. Christy followed suit, taking after him like a lost puppy. ---- Head hung over a half finished pint of ale, Von Kelsig's state was a far cry from the usual affair when a celebrity entered a bar. No fanfare awaited his arrival, no punters clamoured for his attention or autograph or even to listen to his road stories. Instead they simply sat and stole sneaky glances at the famous man at the bar. Stirring a pool of froth with his finger he sighed, hand in head trying to figure out where his life was headed. One long tragedy after another seemed to befall the giant, whether it was the death of a loved one or as now, with a hapless teen stowing away in his truck hoping for a better life. The last shred of joy had been capturing the Elite Title from Wyatt Connors, and even that was career-based. It had been a long, long time since the real world had thrown him a bone and Von Kelsig was beginning to feel the burn. Letting out a messy belch he rose from his bar stool, which creaked under his bulk as he worked the kinks out of his neck and took his leave of the bar. As he walked out onto the street he took a quick look to either end of the street expecting the girl to run up in tattered clothing expecting a handout. Rather he found the street quieter than when he had entered the bar an hour earlier. Getting into the F-150 he turned the ignition and almost hit the accelerator before contemplating Christy's whereabouts. The world was a big, bad place and he had already seen what it could do to an innocent, as in Gloria's case. Grumbling under his breath the giant pulled out of his space then quickly braked as a car pulled up on his rear. Shifting into reverse he swung the truck back into its space and got back out, cursing under his breath and stomping across the pavement towards Havana. ---- “Alright.” he nodded to the bouncer subtly. Though a six and a half foot brick shithouse of a man, the doorman was still dwarfed by Von Kelsig. Looking him up and down he flicked his hand up and took a step towards the giant. “How can I help you?” he asked, unsure of why a scarred and bearded monster would want to enter a hot nightclub like Havana. “Came to pick up a friend.” Von Kelsig retorted dryly, “In and out in five, man.” Furrowing his brow, the doorman nodded and stepped aside to let Von Kelsig in. As the giant ducked under the arched entrance the bouncer pulled his collared mic close to his mouth. “Security standby, keep an eye on the big feller.” ---- Wading through the small crowd with their heads turned skyward to catch a glimpse of the grizzled giant, Von Kelsig's gaze crawled over the many heads bobbing up and down on the dance floor to the sound of some Country remix tune. When several blonde heads spun around to reveal a less than attractive non-Texan, Von Kelsig made his way to the bar where a trim barmaid was serving up a cocktail. “Hey stranger!” she yelled over the din. Von Kelsig simply nodded and smiled. “What can I getcha?” “I'm looking for someone,” the giant began, ignoring the offer of a drink, “short blonde chick...” “Lotta blondes in Dodge, fella.” “Um...” he paused, trying to recall any distinguishing features on the girl, “...she had a white coat on with one of those furry hoods.” “Yeah I seen her, she was hanging out with a few guys. One in a cowboy hat, one a skinhead and the other some big guy in a Titans shirt. They should still be around.” “Thanks.” Von Kelsig whacked the bar with his fist and thanked the barmaid before turning back to the crowd and looking for any of the individuals described, including Christy. Out of the corner of his eye he spied the bright orange of a shirt clad upon the rotund frame of the man described by the barmaid. Pushing past the group on the dancefloor, Von Kelsig tailed the fat man as he headed to the men's toilets. Leaving the main area he trudged along a brightly lit corridor painted with a countryside motif and followed the Titans fan up a flight of stairs where he disappeared into the first door ahead marked 'Mens'. Without hesitation the giant pushed the door open and felt a colossal weight shift behind it, before it jammed against the obstacle. Muffled noises emanated through the gape in the door, and Von Kelsig pushed harder against it to find something pushing back. “Don't let nobody in, dammit.” came a voice from within. Growling, Von Kelsig took a step back and heaved into the wood with his full force. The weight behind it buckled with the door as it flew open, revealing the scene within. The man in the Titans shirt was on the floor for a second time after originally falling down after the first shove by the giant. Standing in front of the cubicles, the lanky skinhead as described by the barmaid looked in shock as the giant stepped over the fat man, before stopping in his tracks as a soft moan broke the awkward silence, originating from within the cubicle directly behind the skinhead. “I been looking for you,” he snarled, pointing at the skinhead, “where is she?” Another groan. Cocking his head, Von Kelsig pushed past the skinhead and peered over the cubicle door, and all of a sudden felt a chilling rage climb up his spine. There below him, hunched over one another were the guy in the cowboy hat and Christy. Her face told the whole story, her features glazed over and her mouth agape as the man behind her undid her zipper and slipped his hand down her pants, too drunk to realize he was being watched. Hopping up onto the toilet on one foot and ingoring the creak of the porcelain, Von Kelsig slammed his massive paw down on the cowboy's head, flipping the hat off before wrapping his fingers around his head. Letting out a horrified yell, the cowboy released Christy who went tumbling into the toilet door as Von Kelsig drove the cowboy's head into the cubicle wall next to him. Staggering, the cowboy fell onto the toilet before again stumbling and wedging himself between the toilet and the opposite wall as he fell. As he did so his two friends made a beeline for Von Kelsig, slamming into him and driving him into the back wall of his cubicle. Landing on the seat, Von Kelsig pushed into the skinhead with both hands, sending the lanky drunk flying back into his overweight comrade and together they hit the deck yet again. Exiting the cubicle, Von Kelsig reached into the adjacent one and picked Christy up off the floor as she carried on giggling, unaware of exactly what was happening. Hoisting her over his shoulder the giant planted a firm boot square in the face of the cowboy, his nose bursting with a torrent of claret before he turned away and hastily made leave of the bathroom before his friends could get another shot in. A look of unbridled anger scarred the giant's face as he carried Christy straight past the other publicans to the front door, where the doorman was gazing in anticipating the giant's return. Stabbing a finger into the bouncer's chest, Von Kelsig boomed at the man, uncaring of who heard. “Three of your fucking charges just tried to rape this girl you prick!” he roared, “Get into the men's room and fucking deal with them before I do something stupid and go back in myself!” Not waiting for a reply, Von Kelsig left the doorman aghast at the abuse received and stomped down the street with Christy babbling incoherent nonsense at his back. He practically threw her into the back seat of the F-150 before veering out of his space into the road, too angry to be concerned with the cars he cut up getting out of the street. “You want to make a new life for yourself??” he sneered at the weary lump lying along the back seat in the rear view mirror, “You couldn't go one night without getting into trouble! Girl, I don't care how shit your life is, I'm taking you to the hospital and then getting in touch with your folks. Be damned if I have any part of this fucking charade any longer...” Clenching his fists around the wheel Von Kelsig sped towards the nearest medical facility, his heart racing at the thought of what could have happened hadn't he shown up at just the right time. Shit like this was why he kept himself to himself and made his career his life. Taking a final glance at Christy's wasted form, he flipped the rear view back up to the back window and put the foot down as he raced away from the shit storm. ---- A pounding drumbeat brought her around. Her entire body aching and her mouth dry, as though stuffed with cotton. Trying to clear her throat was enough to bring a sharper headache, before she rose an arm and rubbed her eyes. “She's coming 'round...” a faint voice mumbled to her right. Opening her eyes she winced at the blinding light emanating from what was nothing more than a dim energy-saving bulb overhead. Several attempts to blink away the haze revealed two figures standing now at the foot of her bed. As he eyes focused her heart dropped at the sight of her mother and father standing over her. “What in the hell were you thinking, girl?” Bob Wilson groaned, moving back to her side anxiously and taking her by the hand. “Christy you gave us such a scare!” her Mom added, holding back tears with a tissue clasped in a shaking hand. “Wh-...where am I?” Christy asked groggily. “What answer are you looking for? The hospital? Or fucking Knoxville, Tennessee?” her Dad replied, traces of his anger seeping back through the concern. All at once Christy froze up. The last recollection she had of anything was passing through the neon cloud into a nightclub, and next waking up here. “What happened?” “According to the nurse you were brought in last night and had to get your stomach pumped. The man who brought you in didn't say who he was but the nurse says she recognized him off the TV. What in blazes were you thinking??” her mother cried, letting the tears fall now. “I...I don't know.” she stuttered, trying to juggle the crushing feeling of being dragged back to hell with the empty hangover racking her body. “You're coming home right now Christy,” her dad growled at her, “where you belong.” Though full of hate and dread, the tone with which her father laid down the law felt new, almost reassuring. She most definitely did not agree with her parents, and she knew in all likelihood her life would be hell until she redeemed herself...but she didn't have a choice in the matter. The giant had made sure of that. Still, beneath the grinding pain of the alcohol and, unbeknownst to her, the drugs...beneath all that the allure of that new life was pining at the back of her mind...
