My Knife For You (ReVolution 120 - 5-Star Choice v. Jumpin' Jack Flash and Jason Snow)

Date: Timeless For the criminal, deceit is a virtue. Not on a level with madness, surely, but virtuous nonetheless. In the kingdom of chaos confusion controls the throne, mischief its crown, doubt the noble heir it sires. Battles won and lost are ambiguous, as opaque as the doctrine proclaimed. Any attempt to understand the motivation driving such a force is an exercise in numbing futility, for it changes whenever need arises, and often for no reason at all. Its impetus is ephemeral, its lunacy sublime. Eyeing the King of Kings – a man entirely too eager to constantly point out how he attained that title – with a wry smirk, Deville could almost see the strings extending from his fingers into that royal mind. The Universal Title match he’d stolen was rapidly approaching, and The Grin was in stark defiance of his namesake at the prospect. "How do all you guys I beat get a shot before I do?" he raged, pacing across one of the Fuck You luxury boxes as if trying to ignite the carpet. Alone with The Deville, he fancied it a sort of bonding, quality time to inspire the kind of closeness Deville seemed to share with the champ in both their crosshairs. "Who cares if you kicked some paki’s ass? I’m the goddamn Jewel in the Crown!" "Which is why you get the honour at Culture Shock," Deville explained with wearied amusement, not for the first time. "It’s the privilege you earned, not a tragic slight. Next to Colossus there is no bigger stage, and while these ReVolution matches like the one I have tonight help solidify the Fuck You legacy, you get to cement and polish it." Gamble snorted, flapping a hand like a beak. "It’s an insult is what it is. Especially to you." Whirling around, an impudent smile stealing onto his face, he folded his arms with smug superiority. "Imagine you win tonight. That’ll make two consecutive payperviews I have to own you on." Sighing, shaking his head as he rose from the sofa, Deville held out a hand of his own, incredulous at his protégé’s interpretation of the role. "Look . . . Just be patient. You remember why you’re here, right?" Another snort, followed by a nod. "And you remember the first rule?" "Of course." This time Gamble allowed a snicker. "You of all people should know how well I embraced it." "Keep that at the front of your mind as you watch me tonight," Deville grated, almost forcing himself to growl. "Pay special attention. If you’ll indulge me in another cliché, actions speak louder than words, and I’m about to put on a clinic." ~><>…<><~ A red sun beat low on the horizon, swathing the sky in dreamy hues of pink and blue. The impenetrable water slapping against the ship, stretching immeasurably in all directions, sparkled in the eerie light, glimmers of a divinely muddled mind. The squawk of a nonexistent shithawk sounded impossibly, too far from shore for it to be real. "You know you’ve gone crazy, right?" Spinning around from the oversized steering wheel, The Deville came face to face with his carbon copy and quirked an eyebrow, querying with delightfully upbeat dementia, "Gone, did you say?" The other Deville nodded, his precariously tilted captain’s hat adding emphasis. Another voice, remarkably similar yet dryly amused, accosted them from the side. "Wouldn’t that imply he was something else at one time, hmm?" Both Devilles turned to spy the third, resplendent in black ninja garb, a rapier hanging at his side. Technically, another Deville, but also The Dread Pirate Roberts, shamelessly plagiarized and recycled from the past. Why? Because Pete likes pirates, damnit. "And you think," began the original, apparently intent on keeping up this game of constant questioning, "that you two somehow skipped out on the journey?" "Of course not," sneered the captain, "I was using you in the maniacal first-person." "Impossible," refuted a fourth, storming onto the scene. The DPR nodded his assent. "Quite." "I have to agree with me and me," Deville intoned, glaring at the captain. "That doesn’t make any motherfucking sense." "But we do it all the time!" "We? Make up your damn mind." "Aha! That was first-person, wasn’t it?" "Did I say I?" "If by I you mean we and you, absolutely." "Aye." "Argh!" "Now you’re getting it!" cried the captain. "No, I’m not!" The Deville roared. "What the hell are all of you doing here?" "Isn’t it obvious?" The Dread Pirate laughed and nodded that it was. "You’re coming to terms with your insanity, and this is the most effective way to show it." "Is not." "Is too." "Is not. He could just as easily shout at or threaten himself, or maybe treat himself to some mutilation. He could even be haunted by his past." "That’s already happening!" "Shut up! Shut up!" "Hell, maniacal laughter is still in style, ain’t it?" The captain barked a fake laugh to test the theory. Nodding affirmation came from Roberts. "Ooooh. How about possession? That one hasn’t been done before." "You’re an idiot." "Ha! You just called yourself an idiot!" Thunder boomed, startling the collective Deville. Opening his eyes, glaring around in the darkness, it slowly dawned on his groggy mind that the thunderclap was actually the slamming of a door. The idea that it could be one of his staff tried to firm itself, to lull him back to dreamland, but he knew none were stupid or suicidal enough to make noise after he’d retired. Impossible as his security was designed to make it, he had an intruder. His feet hit the carpet without a sound, then his knees as he retrieved a gun from under the bed. It was a sweet deal, bribed away from the IDF, a prototype for their next generation urban warfare. The size of a sawed-off shotgun, it had a built-in laser scope and fired fifteen rounds per second. After seeing a presentation for it on the Discovery Channel, he had to have one. Without bothering to dress, he loped to the door, trying to forget the dream. He knew perfectly well what it meant, but he also knew it made for a better roleplay if he pretended otherwise. His perked ears picked up the sound of something being banged below, then muttered cursing, arguably female. Stopping at the top of the stairs, he slowed his pace for three reasons. One, going down the stairs with speed would alert the invader that he was awake. Two, it was dark, and despite his cat-like agility, I wanted to have three reasons and couldn’t think of anything better. Three, didn’t I mention he was nude? Bounding down the stairs like that he’d likely put out an eye. At the bottom of the stairs he spotted the trespasser, fumbling forward with arms outstretched. The red dot skirted up a leg and found a flat chest. Hmph. A man after all. He’d have to go get his ears cl--oh wait. No. Sun Tzu. Deville sighed. "What the fuck do you think you’re doing?" "Sweetheart!" exclaimed The Artist of War, intentionally ignoring Deville’s outrage. "Our schedules have obviously been clashing during business hours, so I thought I’d make a personal visit." She strode forward without fear, ignoring the gun in his hands. Then her eyes spied the other gun. "Oh! You’re in the buff! You knew I was coming!" Ignoring the nonsense, Deville raised the gun so it marked her between the eyes. "How did you get past security?" She actually feigned a giggle. "I’m your girlfriend, silly." "No," Deville corrected, "you’re fucking insane." "Exactly! We’re the perfect match! Kismat even!" She pulled out a pistol of her own and aimed it at him as if to prove the point. It took a great effort not to splatter her across the walls and hallway, but somehow Deville found that restraint. Sighing again, he shook his head. "You’re not going to give this up, are you?" She mimicked his gesture. "Never." "Yet you know that I know it’s a ruse, don’t you?" "Y--What? Angie, honey, dearest, I’d never try to toy with a great mind like yours." "Mmmhmmmm." Clucking his tongue, he sighed yet again. The bitch was definitely nuts, even moreso than himself if she thought she was going to put anything over on him. He’d been abstaining from dating because no woman deserved the torment that came with the territory, but maybe, just maybe, he’d found the exception. She licked her lips with exaggerated slowness, then frowned, pointing. "Shouldn’t that have made it grow?" Yeah. She definitely had it coming.
