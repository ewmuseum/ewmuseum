Date: 1/31/05 Lucky’s Diner, January 30, 2005… “Oh, man,” Ron says, running a hand through his hair, “you would not BELIEVE the night I had last night…” “Oh, yeah?” Fred replies, his interest piqued. “Jesus, yes,” Ron answers, “I was at the bar, and I saw Allison…God, that ass...its beauty is comparable to 14th century Florence…so I said…” “Ron…,” Fred butts in, but Ron brushes the interruption aside. “No, listen, man,” Ron continues, “so me and Allison are pounding doubles of Jack, right? And we’re dancing and shit, and she starts nibbling on my ear, right? And I’m like ‘Holy shit!’ So we…” “Uh, Ron…,” Fred attempts, and again, Ron cuts him off. “Quiet, Fred, and try to understand the stank-nastiness of what I’m about to tell you,” Ron continues, “So we go back to my place, and before I can even put any moves on her, she’s gone and fuckin’ RIPPED her clothes off, and she’s bending over my couch and, I swear to God, I’m not making this up, she was…” “Oh, God, Ron…,” Fred says, grimacing. “I know, man! Crazy!” Ron interjects, “she was BEGGING for anal…I swear on my grandmother’s grave. She was yelling for it so loud I thought everyone in the building would hear. So I tear that shit apart, and Fred, you would NOT believe it. She was squealing and squirming all over the damn place, knocking over lamps with her legs and shit! She LOVED every min-“ “RON!!” Fred screams, shoving back his chair and standing up, “for Christ’s sake, Allison is my sister!” Ron stares at him for a moment. “Yeah, I know, man. Hey, look! Our food is coming!” Across the restaurant, outside on the patio, a man in a brown trench-coat and red bandanna approaches a table where a man with long blonde hair dressed in a black coat is smoking a cigarette. “Hey, you made it!” Nova says to Emilio Rage as he pulls back a chair and takes a seat. “Man, why outside?” Rage asks, shifting uncomfortably, “it’s cold as hell.” “It’s no smoking inside,” Nova explains apologetically, holding up his cigarette in an admission of guilt. “Swell,” Rage replies sarcastically, pulling the collar of his coat up perhaps more flippantly than usual to show his disdain for the climate. “So tell me about this show, man,” Nova says, taking a drag before putting the cig out in an ashtray already overflowing with butts. “Pretty interesting stuff, man,” Rage replies, “didn’t happen before last year’s Culture Shock. Ten-man Tag Match…” A waitress approaches the table, and Rage respectably stops for her. “What can I get you boys?” she asks amiably. “Coffee, please,” Rage responds. “Well…‘Marge,’” Nova replies, looking at her nametag. “That’s my name, don’t wear it out!” Marge says, laughing loudly at her joke. “Don’t worry, I won’t,” Nova replies dryly, “I’ll have a coffee, light on the cream and sugar, please.” “The cream and sugar are right in front of you,” Marge replies, pointing to the condiments before turning to walk back to the kitchen. “If you need me to come back there and fry up my own omelet, just holler!” Nova yells irritably at her. “So, anyway,” Rage continues, “ten-man tag match…obviously, it’s you, me, and then there’s Aimz…” “That’s cool, I guess,” Nova says with a shrug. “And Oz,” Rage adds. Nova raises an eyebrow. “Don’t worry about it,” Rage replies, “he’s cool. Trust me.” “If you say so,” Nova says with a shrug. Marge comes back with two steaming mugs and sets them down, shooting Nova a dirty look before walking away. Nova gives her his best shit-eating grin. “And then there’s Killean rounding out our five,” Rage says, adding, “not bad at all.” “Respectable,” Nova agrees. “But then, we’re up against First Blood,” Rage goes on… Nova’s satisfied grin turns into a frown. “Hoyt Williams,” Rage lists, blowing on his coffee before lifting the mug to his lips. Nova winces. “The HIT,” Rage adds. “Shit,” Nova curses. “Vampir-goddamn-Nosferatu,” Rage spits, his personal feelings for the unsavory Dark Lord made perfectly evident. “Oh, man,” Nova groans. “…and Tyler Lopez,” Rage finishes. Nova stops trying to light his cigarette and stares openly at the PRIMEal Rage Champion. “Wait…what?” “Yeah, I reacted the same way,” Rage says with a shrug, “but nonetheless, that’s our competition.” “So what’s the plan, assuming there is one,” Nova asks. “Well, you know what happens when you assume*,” Rage admits, “I don’t think there is much of one. The best we can hope for is that the other team is as disjointed as we are. Besides, any strategy we come up with is gonna evaporate as soon as we’re all in that ring.” “It’ll be a cluster-fuck, for sure,” Nova agrees, exhaling a cloud of smoke into the air before sipping his coffee. “And,” Rage adds, looking at his watch, “I’ve got a ton of stuff to do before I fly out…um, should I leave some…” Nova holds up a hand to silence Emilio. “Don’t worry about it…I’ve got it.” “Cool, thanks,” Rage replies gratefully with a nod, “so until Monday, then?” “Until Monday,” Nova echoes as Rage pulls back his chair and stands up. They shake hands, and Rage walks away from the table. Nova looks around for a moment before pulling a flask of whiskey from his coat and pouring some into his mug. He holds a hand up to signal the waitress. “Refill, please!” Nova’s hotel room, that night… Nova finishes the last of his Chinese take-out and sets Pussy & Provolone: A History of Fondue Kits in Pornographic Films on the night-table. He packs a small bowl and torches the green with a Bic lighter, blowing the smoke out of his nose. He sets the piece down and turns out the light, leaning his head back on the pillow. He doesn’t feel his eye-lids grow heavy. Nova opens his eyes. He’s sitting in the audience of a syndicated game show. He looks around for a moment…circular room…keypad for punching in answers in front of him…awkwardly tense synthesized soundtrack… “Where could I be?” Nova whispers to himself. AWKWARDLY TENSE SOUNDTRACK… “No, I got that bit,” Nova snaps, “I STILL have no idea where we are…” Nova’s question is answered for him as all the spotlights focus on the entrance to the table in the middle of the room. The audience cheers enthusiastically at the behest of the CHEER ENTHUSIASTICALLY signs hovering above the stadium seating as Regis Philbin makes his out to the middle of the room. “Ha, ha! Allllright!” Regis says, giving a wrinkly, varicose vein-infused thumbs-up to the camera before taking his seat, “last time on the show, Tim was competing for the sixteen-thousand dollar question, so let’s bring him out…TIM!” The crowd claps appreciatively at the behest of the…oh, fuck it. Tim comes out and takes his seat, waving to his wife. “Okay, Tim…,” Regis begins, “for twenty-thousand dollars, fill in the blank for me. Twenty thousand dollars. A blank. Fill it in. ‘Eeny, meeny, miney, blank…’ “‘Eeny, meeny, miney, blank?’” Tim repeats. “Yes, Tim…‘Eeny, meeny, miney, blank…,’” Regis confirms solemnly. “Is it A) The Battle of Verdun, B) Moe, C) Drink Smirnoff, or D) Listen to subliminal messages?” “No lifelines,” Tim mumbles to himself. “Absolutely none what-so-ever,” Regis echoes. “Well, funny story,” Tim begins nervously, sweating like Jennifer Beals’ character in Flashdance, only without the pedophilic sexual attraction, “I had this dog when I was a kid…” “Just answer the goddamn question,” Regis snaps before politely covering his tracks, “I like that tie, Tim.” “Thanks!” Tim replies before continuing to ponder his answer, “Hmm…uh…well…I guess I should go with my first instinct on this one…” “Absolutely no doubt about that,” Regis quips. “So I’ll go with the Battle of Verdun,” Tim says confidently. “Final answer?” Regis asks quietly. “Final answer,” Tim says with a nod. “Well…,” Regis begins before pausing dramatically, “…I’m sorry, Tim, the correct answer was ‘Moe.’ Looks like you get…TWO DAYS IN THE BOO BOX!” “AGGGHH!!” Tim shrieks as his chair flips around, sending him into the black abyss. The snip, snip of scorpion claws can be heard as Tim frantically begs for medical attention. A man in a suit and dark sunglasses walks briskly onto the set and approaches Regis. “Regis, we have people on call waiting from Spielberg’s office,” the man whispers, “apparently, using the ‘Boo Box’ is copyright infringement from Hook, their 1991 blockbus-“ “TWO DAYS IN THE BOO BOX!” Regis declares loudly, and the floor opens beneath the man, sending him tumbling to his doom. Regis takes a moment to get a hold of himself, smoothing back his toupee until his hairline starts behind his ears. “Well…looks like we need a new contestant on…” He turns and points into the camera. “…WHO WANTS TO BE A MILLIONAIRE! Ladies and gentlemen, the fastest fingers question is…what PRIME superstar likes to pretend he is acclaimed songwriter Dan Fogerty from the 1970s rock/folk group Creedence Clearwater Revival when he masturbates to interracial pornogr-“ “THAT’S ME!” Nova shrieks with excitement, standing up after punching in the answer. “Nova, come on down!” Regis yells as the crowd cheers loudly, “you’re the next contestant on WHO WANTS TO BE A MIL-“ “I know what the name of the show is,” Nova interrupts as he climbs down awkwardly through the audience, “sorry. Whoops. Excuse me. Sorry. Owww! Excuse me! Comin’ through!” Nova hops over the guardrail and walks up to his chair, shaking hands with Regis before sitting down and lighting a cigarette. “Um, Nova, we don’t smoke in the stud-“ Regis begins. “Just read the question, Reg,” Nova snaps. “Ha, ha! Okay, then!” Regis agrees with a hearty laugh that allows anyone with high-definition television to see his Adam’s Apple bobbing lifelessly like a corpse lynched from a tree in Asswater, Mississippi. “For one hundred dollars…who played the role of ‘Chachi’ on the popular 1980s sitcom ‘Joanie Loves Chachi?’ Was it…” “‘Popular,’ Regis?” Nova says, raising an eyebrow. “Okay,” Regis admits, “on the 1980s sitcom…was it A) Scott Baio, B) Baio, Scott Vincent James, C) Scott Baio, or D) ‘My stools are bloody.’” “Umm…shit…,” Nova murmurs, scratching his beard, “Regis, I’d like to phone a friend.” “Okay,” Regis nods accommodatingly, “who do you want to call?” “I’d like to call Big Poppa,” Nova says, and, thanks to some TV voodoo, only a moment later the audience can hear a phone ringing. “Hello?” comes a tired voice. “Hello, Big Poppa, this is Regis Philben,” Regis says cockily, “you’re on WHO WANTS TO BE A MILLIONAIRE~!” “I’ve fallen for this before,” Big Poppa says warily, “how do I know you’re the REAL Regis Philben?” “You don’t,” Regis replies plainly. “Well, there’s no funny way to respond to that, so just ask me what you’re gonna ask me,” Big Poppa says. “Okay,” Nova begins loudly, “Poppa, who played the role of Chachi on the 1980s sitcom ‘Joanie Loves Chachi?’ Was it A) Scott Baio, B) Baio, Scott Vincent James, C) Mr. Baio, or D) ‘My stools are bloody.’” “Oh, God,” Big Poppa cringes audibly, “I’m sorry to hear that, Nova. I warned you that with your destructive lifestyle, dysentery would be a prob-“ “No, that’s one of the choices,” Nova clarifies. “Ah,” Big Poppa replies, “…mmm…Scott Baio. What a dreamboat.” “I ONLY HAVE THIRTY SECONDS!” Nova shouts. “Okay, okay,” Big Poppa says gently, “I’d go with Baio, Scott Vincent James…but don’t come crying to me if it’s completely wrong!” “Fair enough,” Nova replies, and the line is disconnected. “Well?” Regis asks. “He didn’t sound too sure,” Nova says weakly, “looks like I’ll have to eliminate two of the wrong answers, Regis.” “Okay, that leaves you with B) Baio, Scott Vincent James, and D) ‘My stools are bloody.’” Regis explains. “Shit!” Nova curses, “it eliminated the only two I KNEW were wrong. I guess I’ll go with my friend on this one…D) Baio, Scott Vincent James.” “Final answer?” Regis asks. “Final answer,” Nova confirms. “AND YOU GOT IT!” Regis yells happily, and Nova pumps his fists in triumph. “Okay, moving on, then…for ONE MILLION DOLLARS…who is gonna win the Dual Halo match at Culture Shock? Is it A) Tyler Lopez, B) Boobies, C) Anyone But You, Asshole! or D) Boobies?” A hushed silence falls over the crowd. “Oh, man, this really IS a million-dollar question,” Nova says with a startled gasp, “Umm…uh…I’d like to poll the audience, please!” The audience feverishly types in their answers on the keypads. “Let’s see the results!” Regis declares. “Well, at least we know where the one vote for Tyler Lopez came from,” Nova mutters, scanning the audience for the Scientific Apparatus. “What’s it gonna be, Nova?” Regis pressures. ”Lay off, old man,” Nova growls, “I…I guess I’ll go with Boobies.” “Final answer?” Regis asks carefully. “Final answer,” Nova answers. “Hmmm,” Regis says, drawing out the suspense, “…aaaaannd…I’M SORRY, BUT THE CORRECT WAS ‘ANYONE BUT YOU, ASSHOLE!’” “WAIT! WAIT!” Nova shouts above the awkwardly tense soundtrack, “I meant that! I meant that!” “TWO DAYS IN THE BOO BOX!” Regis decrees. ”NO! NOOOOOO!!!!” Nova cries as the chair flips around, sending him spiraling… …spiraling… …spiraling… “NOOO!” Nova shouts, bolting upright in bed. He looks around at his hotel room, panting heavily from the scare. “Just a dream…” He fumbles for the pack of Camel Lights and removes a cigarette before lighting it in the darkness. Well, looks like we’re in store for a Preview of Coming Attractions on Wednesday… …because that match, that Main Event, is gonna determine whether or not I even have a chance at winning the Dual Halo match come Culture Shock. I mean, if I can’t overcome five wrestlers, with the aid of four on my side, in a regular PRIME ring, how the fuck can I expect to beat twenty nine by myself inside one of the most insidious structures I’ve ever had the pleasure of stealing the schematics for? “Halo Hype Match,” indeed. This match will set the stage for who will ultimately triumph at Culture Shock, and who will fail. I met with Rage, but he doesn’t have any more of a clue as to what’s going on than I do. I don’t know where the hell Aimz or Killean are. And I have a psychotic clown on my team that only a month and a half ago was carving the same symbols on my team-mate’s body that young, unloved, pimple-faced Slipknot fans are probably scratching into their desktops as we speak. Lovely. But then, I doubt very highly that The HIT or Hoyt Williams are exactly itching to hold a conference meeting with Vampir. They’re douchebags, but they aren’t madmen… …okay, The HIT isn’t a madman. … Alright, they’re all madmen, but that doesn’t mean they’re organizing together or anything. That’s the logic that will keep me sane going into this thing…my first Main Event, and against some of the top competitors in PRIME, no less. In the words of the Blues Brothers… “IT'S 106 MILES TO CHICAGO. WE'VE GOT A FULL TANK OF GAS, HALF A PACK OF CIGARETTES, IT'S DARK, AND WE'RE WEARING SUNGLASSES. HIT IT.” … Okay, scratch the part about it being 106 miles to Chicago. That’s irrelevant. But keep the rest…it sounds kinda catchy.* 'When you assume, you make an 'ass' out of 'u' and 'me'.'
