A quick little venture

21 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/21/2005 'Mr. Matthews, please sit still.' Scott sat on the all-too-small stool and fought the urge to fidget. He just sat there, thinking about how not to do anything. This was something of a stretch for him. He didn't like to just sit and think on things. He liked to be up and moving around. And he was sitting here because this 'Peter Jackson-wannabe' wanted to make a computer-graphic animation of him. The thought just irritated the hell out of him. 'Okay, Mr. Matthews, I think we've got one more set to go,' said the tech behind the mountainous console of equipment. Scott sat there and nodded. His mind had finally drifted: the bodyguards had caught someone outside of his mom's house. The guy had been carrying a machete and had actually gotten up to the house. According to the girl, Scott thought that Van had called her Emi, she had been trailing him since he was halfway down the ridge on the east side of the house. It was reassuring and she'd have no reason to lie about that. But he was still there. But what was he going to do? How could the actions of the Cult be stopped? How exactly could he manage to stop this without looking bad or breaking laws himself? It was something he didn't have a clue what to do about. One thing he could take comfort in was that they seemed to be at a stalemate: neither he nor the Cult could really force the other's hand. 'Holy crap! Peter Jackson?!' screamed the tech. Itwas true. Peter Jackson, taking a break from some of the CGI editing for the upcoming King Kong, had stepped in to get a look at the facial capture equipment. He was smiling a warm, friendly grin and made his way over. 'Hey, how are you? What is it that you're up to?' Are you seeing the problem yet? Yeah. Scott didn't hear that it really was Peter Jackson. All he heard was the name, which he'd heard in conjunction with the sumbitch that had him sitting through this whole mess. Scott whipped around, his nostrils flaring. He glared to see the only new man in the room. It wasn't hard; it had been just him and the tech in there for a while. Scott charged, kicking up dust if there had been any on the floor... just pretend it's CGI'ed in. Scott hit Jackson with the kind of force you'd have expected out of the Balrog that damn near flipped him over. Jackson hit the floor with a thud and Scott continued right out the door, eager to get the hell out. 'Wow,' Jackson muttered. 'I've GOT to put that in the King Kong movie.' What no one realized was that, across town, there was a meeting taking place that was very pertinent to Scott's life. There sat the news director for 48 Hours. There stood, across the desk from him, was a man in a blue pinstripe suit. What you couldn't see was the cross with the protruding twisted branches that the Cult had adopted as their sigil. 'So you see, not only is wrestling accelerating the degeneracy in our youth, but they're idolizing a psychopath,' the cultist said. 'What makes this psychopath so special?' the director asked, setting a stack of papers aside. 'He attacked a religious retreat,' the man replied indignantly. That made the director's eyebrows climb. 'Not only that, but he attacked women that were there as well. I have video footage.'
