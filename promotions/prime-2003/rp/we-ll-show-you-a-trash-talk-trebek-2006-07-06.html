We'll show you a trash-talk, Trebek!

6 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 6, 2006 "I'm not gonna lie and say that I'm thrilled to be here and am grateful for this opportunity. Maybe I would be...under different circumstances." Detroit in the summer summer summertime (summertime!). T minus three days until gold was up for grabs. Really, would you expect anything less from this duo? Opting for a video clip to promote PRIME's special One-Hundred edition of ReVolution to be put on the company's swank new website (Right Ross? RIGHT?!), Lindsay Troy sat in an easy chair in her hotel room, somehow managing to bend her long, slender legs into an Indian-style position. Behind her, Joey Melton stood looking smugly into the camera. TROY: It's not a wonderful feeling having to assess the situation and recognize that Joey and I are in this position by default. Being the only other tag-team in this company isn't a laurel I'm content to rest on or even be pleased about. My ball is still on the court and I'm not ready to go home just yet. I haven't really made many friends since being here; not gonna lie, an attitude thing probably has something to do with it. Probably? No. Definitely. But then again, I didn't come here to make friends and influence people. Barging into Nelson's office back at UltraViolence, I had said Joey and I were here to 'save the tag division.' From what, at the time, I didn't know, but somewhere along the line the bottom dropped out. I must have blinked and missed something. Something important... Something like...the tag team division. Troy snorts, shakes her head and then laughs to herself. TROY: So there's no division to speak of left to save yet here we are, not too far from a tag title match against Captain Resthold and the Midget Stoner with a mask and a cape. Whoever had those on the Wrestling Gimmicks Bingo cards, feel free to cover up the squares. MELTON: This is our chance. Our shot to become the first of two relevant teams in PRIME and win something together, as a couple, and validate the choice of not buying a two-seat bicycle or investing in human trafficking. There’s good money to be made in slavery these days. Oh sure we’d like to believe in equality, you know the “all things are created equal” startup shit they hammer home when you’ve first been entrusted with the big decisions in life like when not to shit your pants. As I asked Lindsay when we first met, “How do you feel about saying fuck you to charity? Because I believe in haves and have nots.” We have the opportunity to win something that truth be told will be disbanded and sold on Ebay in 6-8 weeks, and 95% of the roster does not. Apparently there are two types of people in the world. Those who enjoy beating the shit out of Ferguson and his homoerotica co-pilot, and those who don’t need the extra check. Beating a face-eater for belts only two other men could possibly cherish, isn’t exactly being King for a day, but the alternative is an awkward ride home, and deep discussions about who’s dick to suck next and what Culkin gem is left to parody quite badly. Troy and I are a team because neither of us can Garden, or decide which stamp to collect next. Basically we’re here in PRIME until McDonald’s starts their Monopoly game again and we have months to kill as a couple trying to run the board. This is our Myspace page. The four of you who we’ll keep beating are our friends. Rest assured we’ll be blogging from a Hilton hot tub an hour after walking out with the Tag Titles. Current Mood: Aloof. Listening To: The Dulling of my conscience. If you cup your hands together and raise them over one ear, you can hear the sounds of the Ocean. It doesn’t matter. Just listen. Imagine. It’s not hard if you try. You’re on the beach. A seagull flies overhead. The salty air reminds you of vacations taken long again. It’s pleasant yes? Living a lie isn’t such a bad thing. We all need the clowns to make us laugh. If you believe, you can hear the Ocean. Can you hear what we’re whispering from low tide? Will any of it matter when we’re the only tag team left? Rome is burning and we’re the proof such a time period produced revolutionary results. Or maybe I’m wrong and both teams left are fucking their partners.
