Date: August 8th, 2005 “Your winner…and NEW Intense Champion, Vangelus Olsig!” -- I did it.Months of struggling, months of pain, months of tribulation…And on the grandest stage of them all…I did it. But now I am faced with an even greater force.With the dawning of a new chapter in PRIME comes my chances to prove the critics wrong.Those doubting my abilities at ever being anything…Those doubting my abilities at ever making it anywhere…Those doubting my abilities of every being somebody in this business…Now is my time to prove them wrong.And I’ll do it, by defeating two of the best in this business.I’ll do it by defeating an upcoming star in this sport.I’ll do it by conquering this mountain which I have been greeted with.My name is Vangelus Olsig.And now is my time. Bogotá, Columbia; 9:00 P.M. Nocturnal period in the deep center of Columbia. Swathed in Darkness upon the hills of Bogotá is where I resided, racking my brain with various thoughts of exhilaration. Wednesday nights seemed so cliché’ in this city, all consisting of Gang Wars, Blood, Violence, all draped around the all so familiar themes of Greed, Money, and Power. The papers stacked upon my Office Desk pleaded for recognition, but my desire sought something different. Coming off of a big win at Colossus II, claiming the Intense Championship as my own…tonight needed to be a night of celebration. A night, free of Violence…free of Rage…free of Worry. A night where the party animal inside of me mounted from its deep dark grave. That’s what I desired on this night. “Too Busy? When are you going to let loose, man? Alright.” I slammed the phone down. Fourth call tonight and the same response every time. This city is so tainted by crime that it’s become a hassle simply trying to find someone to hang out with. My father died two yeas ago, leaving me with his throne of General Attorney, not once did he mention the heartache that would come along with putting your life on the line every night trying to save that of another’s. A night of spontaneous pleasure suddenly became foreign. With a new chapter dawning in my life, though, I needed this chance to celebrate. “Come on…can’t you put that off until another time? No? Alright.” I slammed the phone once more, after the fifth decline of the night. My already petite list of friends in this city was leisurely waning with every phone call. I implored Hin See with requests of an off week and he gave it to me, but now it seemed like nothing more than a waste of time. What I thought would be a blissful night of jamboree had become nothing more than another night at the office. I sighed, pushing myself up out of my chair as I glanced outside of my window unto the Bogotá city. There was fun somewhere out there, and I was determined to find it. I snatched my trench coat off of the coat rack, making sure to retrieve my .45 glock off of the cabinet desk beside the coat rack. In this city you can never be too prepared with crime around every corner. I tucked it inside of my Coat and opened the door, flicking off the light to the room. I was headed into the Blood City. To have some Fun. The Bar Room door creaked open as I stepped inside, drawing immediate attention from the small core of people inside of the Bar. I brushed off their usual gazes, grabbing a chair from an empty table and pulling it out as I took a seat. Maybe not an average man’s idea of Fun, but I was not an average fun. While it surely wasn’t a high class dine, a few drinks would probably do me good while trying to come up with a proper excuse to give Hin See as to why, exactly, I wanted this week off. “May I get you anything?” I took a momentary retreat from my deep thoughts to glance up at the woman standing before me with a pen and pad in hand. Her hair was blonde, and her eyes were a glossy emerald shade. She wasn’t the most beautiful thing to ever grace my presence, but she was average enough to garner my attention on such an average night. I gave her a swift smile as she returned one back before placing the tip of her pen on the pad in an “I’m ready to take your order now” manner. “Ugh…A scotch will do fine for now.” She jotted down the order on the pad, as if remembering to bring me a scotch was too heavy for the brain. But then again…she was a blonde, so it wasn’t exactly Guinness World Records material. She gave another smile before walking off leaving me to immerse in my deep thoughts once more. This night wasn’t going to be the night of spontaneous bliss, as I so previously projected, but any night where I could escape the mean streets of Bogotá was a night I could certainly deal with. I ran my hands through my hair, taking another deep breath upon waiting for my scotch. I took a glance at my watch, wondering to myself what was taking the blonde so long. Nonetheless, I closed my eyes taking a moment to soak in thought. Thought of the city in which blood stained every corner, and how I was going to save it. Thoughts on my Intense Championship win, and what I would do to capitalize on it. Thoughts on what heinous act I would portray among this Blonde if she didn’t hurry with my scotch. Hahahaha… The deep toned sound of laughter caught my attention as I peeled open my eyes to glance at the table in which it came form. Around the table sat around 4-5 gentleman, all with their eyes turned towards a man standing at the bar hovering over a woman who seemed less than interested in him. I took a deeper glance at the man standing over the woman realizing that he was Burt Jones, a man with the strangest accent who had been the target of my justice law many times before. He always hung around the streets, preying on random single women with his foul stench and physical zeal. Guess he’s taken his acts into Bar Rooms now. I paid it no attention, promising myself that I would not jump into crime-fighting this night unless serious vigilance beckoned. I took an oath to my father amongst obtaining this throne that I would rid this city of crime at any given chance, but tonight I had given myself a night off so to speak. I turned my head and closed my eyes once again, focusing my attention back on my deep thoughts. “I SAID NO!” The high-pitched scream of the woman caught my attention, as I peeled open my eyes once more to glance at the situation. This time I had become more interested than before. My previous endeavors with women possibly killed my chances of ever having another one to call my own, but with it came a life learned lesson that no woman should ever be physically harmed by man. From the looks of this situation and from knowing Burt Jones like I know him, I knew that pretty soon this interaction would turn physical. “Come on babe, ya know yooz want ta hook up with meez tonight.” “If I have to tell you one more time to leave me alone, a slap to your face might come along with it.” I sighed, shaking my head as the drunken men gathered around the table continued to tickle their fancies with enjoyment of the situation. Burt was a man that sickened me, but his friends seemed to love his actions. They followed him as if he was some sort of god to them, and judging by their actions, he most likely was. Burt seemed to only adorn pleasure out of his men’s laughter, so he continued to participate in the act. “All I’m asking is f-…” “I said leave me alone!” In an attempt to stay fond of her promise and hopefully peel the creep off of her back, she swung an open hand in an effort to slap him, but quick reflexes on Burt’s part led him to catch the wrist and begin twisting it. I cursed under my breath, knowing that I could not sit there and allow this to go on without even bothering to lift a finger. Looks like my night of spontaneous bliss had gone right down the drain. “You heard the woman…leave her alone.” I stated in a calm deep tone. Burt kept hold of the woman’s wrist as he turned around and looked at me with that oh so familiar look on his face. That look that clearly displayed his hatred for me and his desire to have my blood on his hands. Not on this night, though. He caught me in a pissed mood, ruining my night because of his craving for harming woman. I had no problem ending him right here and right now, but first I wanted to give him a chance to take his lackeys and leave so I could enjoy atleast some of the night. “Lookey what weez got here fellas. Mistah good guy Olsig. Listen, bub. Meez and the woman here is minding our own bizness, so howz bout yooz do the same.” “Look, Burt. I’m trying to enjoy a stress free night, tonight. Please don’t make me get physical.” I spoke with a still calm voice, taking a moment to decipher his outlandish accent into English. I heard the murmurs coming from the table behind us where his lackeys sat at…murmurs of how Burt could take me out with once punch, argued by murmurs of how Burt stood no chance against me. I paid them no attention, focusing my main attention upon the situation at hand and how I was going to go about ridding it and keeping a peaceful night in the same motion. “Awww, fuhgetz yooz. Meez and the lady is having fun, eh sweetcheekz?” Blatantly ignoring my demands, he turned back around facing the woman trying to pull her closer to him by her wrists. I took a deep sigh, knowing that words weren’t going to cut it in a situation like this with a man like this. As stated before, you can never be too prepared in a blood city like this…so I reached inside of my coat jacket, retrieving the .45 gun that I stored before leaving the office. The table gasped as I placed the cold barrel against the back of Burt. Suddenly, he released the wrist of the woman and held his hands high in the air as his buddies all jumped up, scattering from the table and out of the bar in fear. “Heyz! Wherez yall going!?!” “There hightailing it…and I suggest you do the same unless you want a silver bullet to pass through your back.” I could see the disgruntled look on the face of Burt as he cautiously ran out of the bar, yelling at his lackeys who had by now made there way down the street. I smiled, satisfied that the clown was out of my hair and back into the hair of Bogotá officials. I slowly placed the gun back into my coat pocket, turning around to face the woman whom I had saved. I had become so busy with trying to rid Burt that I hadn’t even taken time to look at her face…but when I did, my jaw nearly dropped to the ground. Back when I first took over the position of General Attorney, I was dating a woman who I had practically fallen in love with. Her name was Dawn Blasky. We met on the streets…surprisingly enough she was a lead detective for the B.O.B (Bogotá Officials Bureau), which is the lead Detective department in this city. We were teamed together to shut down Roman Esposito III…well known Mafia Dun and the Cities lead crime boss. Of course, in a matter of weeks, we shut down Esposito, but as usual, his money put him right back on the streets. We continued to team together until we officially became Partners. But suddenly, Partnership in the office turned into partnership outside of the office in our personal lives. We became involved in relations outside of the office that no one inside of the B.O.B was aware of. I loved that woman with all of my heart, and one day sought to marry her. That quickly changed on June 19th. Prior to that date, the Emerald Jewel, a very valuable diamond within the city, had been stolen from the museum of fine arts. We had been paired together to find the jewel, but it was night and our search had ended for the day. We returned to my home where she had been spending the night every night the week. She walked inside of the bathroom, leaving me to undress in the bedroom. “Honey, Can you hand me my purse off of the bed?” “Alright.” I took off my coat jacket and tossed it unto the bed, walking over to snatch the purse off of the bed. Suddenly, it slipped out of my hand and fell unto the floor, all of the contents inside spilling unto the bedroom floor. I looked down to witness the Jewel lying on the floor. I reached down and picked up the jewel examining it and realizing that it was the exact same jewel that we had been looking for. I placed the jewel inside of my pants pocket, hurriedly going through my bedroom dresser and retrieving my trademark .45 guns and slipping it inside of my pocket. Also, I snatched out a pair of handcuffs and quickly tip toed behind the bathroom door. “Dawn…I need you to come out here for a moment.” “Okay, Just a moment.” I heard a drawer slam closed inside of the bathroom as she turned the knob and stepped out. That’s when I made my move, pouncing on her and wrestling her down to the ground as I grabbed her wrists and folded them behind her back. I slapped the handcuffs around her wrists as she screamed in apprehension. “What are you doing, Vangelus!?!” “I found the Jewel. You have the right to remain silent…” The police sirens rang throughout the area as a couple of Police Officers escorted her to the squad car. A tear fell out of my eye. Words could not describe how I loved that woman…words could not describe how broken I felt as I watched her turn from lover into convict. …Words could not describe. “Dawn!?” Her expression met mine in total shock. I did not know what to say nor do in this situation. Upon seeing her beautiful face, it was if old feelings began to erupt…but upon reminiscing on the events that kept us from spending our lives together with each other…another feeling erupted. A feeling of disgust, a feeling of hatred, a feeling of… Betrayal. “Vangelus…I-I don’t know what to say.” Obviously she saw my face as I fought off Burt, but was in denial. Upon hearing my voice and actually being able to look at me with a straight face, her shock was just as immense as mine. Seeking beyond the veil, though, I could see that it was more than shock. Fear plagued that shock…fear of our previous most recent uprising. I wouldn’t have expected her to be free out of jail so early…obviously she was working for someone, and it wasn’t someone good. “Wow…it’s really nice to see you again. Have a seat” She stated. I did as told and took a seat next to her, smiling simply because it was too much of a burden to hide. Our past was evident, of course, but that still didn’t change the way I felt about her. She was the one I wanted to spend my entire life with and one simple flaw couldn’t take that away from me. I thought I would never see her again, but I would be lying if I said I wasn’t ecstatic at the sight of her face. “Bartender, get her a thunderbird.” “Well…It’s nice to see you again, Vangelus.” “Yeah it is. How have things been?” “Pretty good…you know. Trying to make it.” I didn’t say a word as I began to sink into the pool of her crystal blue eyes. Suddenly, in the span of five minutes I began to fall for her once more. It was as if she held that special key to my heart that boasted the fact that no matter what she did to me, no matter how she betrayed me, no matter how much she lied to me, she could still wrap me around her finger. Admitting that she was right was the hard yet inevitable part. “I’ve really missed you Dawn.” “I’ve missed you too Vangelus…I wish it could have worked out between us.” Emotion beckoned, realizing that it could have worked between us if not for one single motion. If not for my inelegance, I would have never dropped the purse, never found the jewel, and never ended up having to arrest her. We would have lived life together with a love that no force could break. A bond that feared nothing in life. All of these emotions, all of these feelings, all wrapped around one question…What if. “Yeah…” “You know, maybe we could head over to your place and catch up on old times…” “Well, I do-...” “You saved me from that creep, Burt. I owe you something…and this is my chance to pay you in full. What do you say?” That look. That look that took me by the neck and drew me in with no remorse. How could I turn back that look? Simple…I couldn’t. I smiled, refusing to decline such an invitation, an invitation to make it work this time with the woman that I loved. I reached deep into my pocket and pulled out two singles, slapping them on the bar as a tip. I pushed myself off of the seat and stood in a vertical position, opening my hand to take hers. “Let’s go.” She smiled and placed her gentle soft moist hand, allowing me to help her step down from the step and unto the floor. She grabbed her purse as we both made our way out of the Bar room. As I exited, I saw the blonde finally bringing my scotch to my table to discover that I wasn’t there. A smile crossed my face as I saw her throw a tantrum. In the span of Five minutes… I had fallen back in love… Maybe this night wouldn’t be so bad after all. This scene had become rather familiar…me undressing in the bedroom as she did whatever it is women do inside of the bathroom. I had just taken off my coat, tossing it on the floor as I removed my shirt. It didn’t take long for her to swing the bathroom door open, exposed in nothing more than her bra and panties. I had forgotten how beautiful her tan skin was, but I was more than reminded amongst seeing her before me. No words were spoken as she pressed her perfect round breast upon my chest, connecting her soft lips with mine as we allowed our tongues to do all of the talking. Her gentle hands danced across my chest as he fell back unto the bed, allowing the moment to take over. Deep down inside, I knew it was wrong for me to be doing this, but I could not stop. The voice inside of me pleaded upon my deaf ears. This was the moment that I looked to embrace every night if we were to get married, but that was shot to shame… So I looked to make up for the lost time right now. Hours stacked upon hours passed as we dwelled in the moment, knowing that the chance may not present itself again after this night. Finally, our glossy bodies peeled off one another. We both took the rest of the night as a chance to gather some rest after such a passionate moment. I had to return to the office in the morning so I needed the sleep. The alarm clock beeped as I woke, feeling as if I had just drifted into sleep. Nonetheless, I knew the consequences that would come along, so I pushed myself out of bed and grabbed my trench coat before making my way down stairs. I heard a long forgotten sizzling sound as I walked down the stairs to see Dawn in my kitchen, cooking breakfast. I smiled, reminiscing on her unbelievable home cooking. She looked beautiful down there. “Hey Honey. You made me happy last night, so now I’m going to make you happy with a breakfast that will blow your mind.” She said, smiling as she stated it. I smiled right back, finally planting my feet on the ground as I slowly walked into the kitchen. She pecked me with a kiss on the cheek as I greeted her with a smile. I walked over to the refrigerator and pulled out a carton of orange juice, pouring its contents into a glass before placing it back inside of the fridge. She spoke as I downed the orange juice… “Honey…why are you wearing that Trench Coat? I’ve already seen you naked, hehe.” “Yes…and unfortunately it’ll be your last time ever…” BANG! In one swift motion, she fell to the kitchen floor with blood pouring out of her head. The blood flowed like a river unto my kitchen floor as I stood over her, kicking her softly a few times with my foot to make sure she was dead. I shook my head in shame… She betrayed me again. I walked into the living room and snatched her cell phone off of the table. I flip through until I reached the Text Messaging section. I flipped through until I read the last text message which was sent to her last night. Good Deal. Put the bullet in Olsig’s heart right before he leaves in the morning.” The phone number was that of Sal Francois, the Mafia Dun who took over Roman Esposito’s throne as the lead dun in the city. It was all a setup to have me killed…she was going to kill me, but I got to her first. She betrayed me again… Damn. Bogotá, Columbia; 10:00 A.M. Somewhere on the south side of Bogotá sat Sal Francois, grinning from ear to ear. Olsig thought he spoiled his planned, but he fell right into it. With a phone up to his ear, Sal spoke… “Yes, I would like to report a murder.” His Smile broadened more as he prepared himself to inform the police… “Victim: Dawn Blasky……Culprit… Vangelus Olsig.” TBC
