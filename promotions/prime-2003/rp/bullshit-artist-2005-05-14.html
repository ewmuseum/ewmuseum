Bullshit Artist

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/14/2005 'Thank you all.' Tyler Lopez smiling. Lopez: You know what the saddest part of this whole enterprise is? You people realized what was going on, and you didn't stop it. How many times did everyone keep saying it? 'Tyler Lopez is just looking for attention.' 'Lopez is a braggart with nothing to back it up.' 'The Scientific Apparatus is nothing more than a sap.' Lopez points at a finger after each sentence. Lopez: And yet you just kept coming. Every time one of you would tell the world you had figured it out, and that you wouldn't bother to deal with he who is Tyler Lopez anymore, the next one would come out of the woodwork and say the exact same thing, rendering it more and more stupid each time it was said. From my PRIME debut to everyone on the other side of me, you ALL addressed me! How sad is it, when a scrub like Jackson Darilek comes closer to the answer than anyone else did? He finally figured out how well I had sucked you all in, and as a result displayed himself to be the smartest of all of you sorry bunch of BS artists. I ought to hire this boy as my PR agent, since he managed to get across the point better than I can. Too bad he couldn't come up with something else to add to that.' J.D., if saying certain things makes being knocked around feel better, than knock yourself out, Scrappy. A grin. Lopez enjoys the misery of others. Lopez: Some of you here already have your accolades. But Peter Vetra. You came to impress the PRIME viewing public with your smooth words and wonderful stories to tell, trying your best to make an impression and maybe even be the magical 'underdog who makes good.' In a month or so, nobody is going to remember anything you said. Your little attempts to make yourself into some sort of big shots will be long forgotten. After this match you are going to go back to your little federation, you're going to compete for your petty titles, and you'll NEVER reach these sorts of heights EVER AGAIN! Me, they'd be a fool not to give me another title shot after this. So even if things don't go my way and I should lose this match by some terrible misfortune, I can just go back and bide my time. Win or lose here, I've stolen away ALL of your chances to impress. And every single one of you helped me to do it. So, again, thank you very much. Lopez laughs. Well, cackles, really. Lopez: Let me establish something here, Peter. You've spent the better part of a week going on and on about how you've proven over and over what a superstar. Now, now that I'm picking up on the oversights in YOUR speeches, now all of a sudden let's drop that and say, 'What does any of this matter? It doesn't make us worse wrestlers because of it!' No, it doesn't. But it does make us inconsistent bastards, now, doesn't it? Chuckling to himself, Lopez shifts in his chair, which we keep forgetting to describe to you. It doesn't matter for this scene, though, so we still won't. Lopez: If it doesn't matter, boys, then what in the HELL have you two been doing? Stretching your tongue muscles? Exercising your jaw for another self-promoting fan-ass-kissing interview? Or just finding yet another way to inflate your egos besides telling us for the quadrillionth time about great you are, and how it vindicates you from any further criticism of your sucky careers up to this point? Lopez just won't let that one go, folks. He's like a dog with an old sock. Lopez: If it doesn't matter, boys, then why are you still talking? Is it because you've humiliated me so terribly? Gee, let's see how you've done that, shall we? Here, folks, in a nutshell, is what my opponents have proven about me this week: They've proven that I don't have as many accomplishments as they does. They've proven that I like to brag a lot. They've proven that I crave attention, and will go to any lengths to get it. They've proven that I'm at a disadvantage this week because of the stipulations on this match. Hey, everybody watching this! Raise your hand if you DIDN'T know ALL of these shocking, news-worthy facts before we even started! Lopez raises up in his chair, looking for hands in his living room. Considering no one's there, he probably doesn't see any. Lopez: Congratulations! You've proven yourself half right. What YOU have said up to this point doesn't matter at all. Because it's contributed to proving nothing but what people with even the slightest knowledge about my career already know. So there's the other one out of eligibility. You see, I find it interesting that I do nothing but examine your own words and find your inconsistencies, and that makes me a liar. But when you come up with that bone-headed 'hypocrite' argument, then suddenly you're just 'taking things at face value.' This isn't a tag match, anyway!' I shouldn't expect consistency. He loves to do this. It's like a cat toying with a wind-up mouse. Lopez: See, guys like you two, they'll just take that as an opportunity to take the self-appreciation up a notch, and stroke themselves in public by mentioning their glories yet again. Half of your arguments, Jackson and Ventra, end with, 'Well, it doesn't matter, because I've won more matches than you, and am therefore better than you.' I may be the kid who whines like somebody's taken his candy, but you're the kid who sticks his fingers in his ears and says, 'LALALALALALA! I CAN'T HEAR YOU! LALALALALALALA!' Lopez waves a hand dismissively. Lopez: And if that's the way things are going to be, then I'm done with you. Go ahead. But don't worry. After this is over, you can go back and get back to work on it. Plenty of time to worry about it then. Lopez is nothing if not optimistic. Lopez: And now on to the other talking head. Jackson, to be honest, you're almost not worth talking to, considering you've proven yourself to be more full of @#%$ then all the locker room. That's what's known as an 'inside joke.' Lopez: When it comes to your own history, Jackson, you're the champ, obviously. But when it comes to your opponent's, you're a little shaky. Sit down and watch. And I want you to notice something very subtle about the way I operate. A little something that may have clued you into the fact that every match I lost I wasn't really trying to win. I'll give you a hint: it was in my offense. Lopez sits for a second, looking at his watch. Lopez: Give up? Well, here's the answer. The subtle clue in the offense is that THERE WASN'T ANY, YOU SHIT-FOR-BRAINS EXCUSE FOR A HUMAN BEING! I never once tried to destroy Munson, or Jessica Devine to name a few. Never once. These are all facts. Not lies. Facts, the ones you love so much. I went into that match and just when you think I'm down and out my final 'fuck you' is when the ref counts three. Are you even paying attention at this point? He shakes his head. On the one hand, he likes it when they make these kinds of errors. On the other, he hates having to beat them over the heads with it. Lopez: Regardless of ability level, I always enjoy kicking asses. But let's get to the real heart of the issue, here, Jackson. The real crux of the matter. Lopez takes a deep breath. Lopez: I've asked you this question approximately five times. I even cut a promo where I did nothing BUT ask you this question. And you still haven't provided me an answer. I been called a braggart. Well, Jackson and Ventra, you see... *I* have an excuse. Since nobody is ever any better than their greatest accomplishment, I have yet to hold any PRIME titles. By the way, Vampir Nosferatu has made more of a scene then both of you combined. I beat him. Just throwing that out there, no reason. He snickers. Lopez: I've been called a braggart, because I've inflated things I've done to massive importance, without much evidence to support it. Lopez sighs. Lopez: As much as I hate both Jackson and Peter Ventra and all his babble, I have to admit that, of all people, it would be my distinct honor to kick their asses and win my first PRIME title. But maybe I'm missing something here. Maybe there's something hidden from my sight that proves beyond a shadow of a reasonable doubt that you are the men who can claim responsibility for destruction in PRIME. If there is, you'd better bring it up quick. Try as you might, you can't distract me with your dead people, burning people, or walking people. I will not quit until you have given me, and everybody watching, a satisfactory answer as to why you should win the Alias Title. If you can't provide a single, rational explanation for why YOU, either of you, and not anybody else, then you're just as much a bullshit artist as I am, and I welcome you into our fold. Lopez chuckles to himself. Lopez: Maybe, you should stick with gay jokes. Fade out.
