Danny Ferguson in 'Green Hawaii'

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/26/05 Ferguson2 - Danny Ferguson in Green Hawaii Danny Ferguson in 'Green Hawaii' By Danny Ferguson FADE IN. EXT. HAWAII - DAY The sun is high and bright as the travel version of Team Ferguson is emerging onto Hawaiian soil for the first time. As we fade in, Danny's head emerges from the narrow door of a private plane. He's visibly squinting behind his aviators. NARRATOR It was several days before PRIME's Tropical Turmoil Pay-Per-View, and Danny Ferguson's second match since returning to professional wrestling. Looking to avoid the first losing streak of his career, he opted to fly in early to avoid any unwanted jet lag on Sunday. Danny steps onto the tarmac and yawns, then exits, stage left. A wide shot shows him walking away from the plane and off of their airstrip. Slowly, the other members of Team Ferguson deplane and begin collecting bags. Jim Pibb is visibly struggling with many bags. As Danny walks, Reggie Delray scampers behind him, leaving his own bags behind to be picked up by the bodyguards. REGGIE (continuing a conversations from inside the plane) And you just decided to come back? DANNY (walking, and constantly looking forward) I'm here, aren't I? REGGIE Well sure, but I don't know WHY. Danny stops and looks at Reggie out of the corner of his eye, but we can't see it behind the aviators. DANNY You know, that's something I don't understand. REGGIE You mean you don't have a reason? DANNY No, I mean I don't understand why someone like you thinks I owe you anything other than a fucking paycheck. He starts walking again, but Reggie falls behind, his face showing a mix of speechlessness and disgust. Slowly, he starts after his protege. NARRATOR Ever since he was hired as Danny's personal wrestling manager, wrestling legend Reggie Delray was confused why Ferguson was back in wrestling to begin with. After all, it was only a few months ago that his movie, Hard Money- CUT TO: SHOT: DANNY'S 'HARD MONEY' POSTER. BACK TO: NARRATOR (CONT'D) -was the top grossing film of the spring season. Problem was that no one outside of Danny's personal advisors knew his reasons why. And even though Reggie could be considered a 'personal advisor,' he still wasn't in the loop. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas and Jim Pibb struggle to carry all of the suitcases towards their car. As they do, Vicki Siguchi walks briskly by, twirling a purse and chatting on a cell phone. She looks at both men as she goes by. DAMETREYUS (under his breath) Yeah, keep walking before you get something up yo' ass you won't enjoy. At least not the first time... JIM Dude, my knee's killing me. DAMETREYUS (snapping, annoyed) I ain't takin' another fucking bag from you, Jim. Cry about it in the car. Pibb winces and heaves forward with the bags as both men struggle to keep up. NARRATOR In fact, Jim's knee WAS killing him. Or rather, he was killing his knee. CUT TO: I/E. Still shot of the X-ray on Jim's knee, making an effort to point out the troubling areas. BACK TO: EXT. HAWAII - DAY Outside the private airport, a regular limousine idles. A small crowd mills about, but it's mostly barren. By the time Jim and Dametreyus reach the rest of the group, Danny is standing by the door of the car, looking pissed. DANNY Some dipshit fan came up and hassled me. Where the fuck were you two? There's a pause as both bodyguards just look at him, still holding the suitcases from the entire party. Finally, Danny sighs and gets into the limo, mumbling angrily. Once he's out of earshot, Dametreyus speaks up. DAMETREYUS 'The fuck's up his ass? VICKI The guy thought he was Astin. Dametreyus lets out a low whistle. Jim looks shocked, but more concerned with getting his bags loaded up in the car. His face is strained with discomfort. Reggie, meanwhile, is confused by the revelation. DAMETREYUS Damn... REGGIE Wait, what's the big deal? Dametreyus and Vicki exchange a look. CUT TO: A MOCK-UP BOXING POSTER Danny is on one side, in ring gear. One the other is Sean Astin, of Rudy fame. NARRATOR Danny had a longstanding feud with actor Sean Astin, who he claimed was responsible for his career slide years ago. Few others regarded it as a feud, though, because Astin didn't actually KNOW who Danny was. BACK TO: EXT. HAWAII - DAY REGGIE Damn... DAMETREYUS That's what I said. Suddenly, Danny pops out of the limo's sunroof, looking more agitated than ever. DANNY Hey, did you mistake Hawaii for the fucking employee lounge at Albertson's? Because that's where you'll all be ending up if you don't quit dicking around on my time and get in the car! He looks around at all four, who start to shuffle around. Unfortunately, the bodyguards had been holding those suitcases for all of the above segment. NARRATOR Unfortunately, fate and Jim's bum knee both chose that exact moment to twist. JIM Shit! Pibb lets out a yelp as his knee buckles and he tumbles forward, dropping his suitcases and plowing head-and-shoulder first...into the knee of Dametreyus. DAMETREYUS Shit! Both men tumble to the ground and begin holding their knees amidst the strewn suitcases. Danny looks at them and closes his eyes. We zoom in on him as we... FADE OUT. FADE IN. INT. HOTEL - LATER THAT DAY. A loud screeching sound is heard, like a disaster film where the creaky old metal sign starts to swing in the wind...except in stereo. We start back on Danny as he walks down a wide hallway, showing the same look of disgust that he had when we left. Now, however, it's mixed with a touch of anger, the kind that comes about from only sheer embarrassment. Behind him, Vicki Siguchi is trying to keep up, still talking on the phone. Reggie Delray is a few paces behind, looking like he doesn't understand what he's gotten himself into. Notably absent are the looming shadows of the bodyguards. As the group continues down the hall, the shot pans out to show everyone in full view, and we finally find Jim and Dametreyus. Both men are in wheelchairs with braces and wraps on their left knees. They're both too drugged up to feel any pain, so it's only dejection and a sense of duty that moves their squeaky little wheels along. NARRATOR After a very long trip to the hospital, and a very expensive ride in a handicap-accessible cab back to the hotel, the guards returned to the side of their charge, who had spent the interim sleeping off his jet lag and watching softcore porn simply because he could. Now that they could safely protect the area between Danny's feet and his upper thigh, it was time for a trip to the gym. They paused in the lobby and waited for the car to come around, because no one had bothered to call it beforehand. Reggie took the opportunity to push his point from before. REGGIE I can't guarantee I can be of much help in a match like this. 12 people out there at once is already a bit much. DANNY That's assuming they all show up. You didn't see too many PRIME names on the hotel registry, did you? NARRATOR This was a stupid statement for many reasons. First, Reggie had no reason to look at the registry, hence his confusion. Secondly, Danny had chosen NOT to stay at the PRIME hotel, and had, in fact, moved Team Ferguson's reservations to a more expensive locale on the other side of Honolulu. And third, most wrestlers who truly considered themselves wrestlers used fake names when signing in. In fact, Ellis Easton had checked into the very same hotel that they were in, but had used the name Token Weed to ensure no trouble from fans. Reggie finally stops thinking about all that the narrator detailed and turns back. REGGIE My point is that this is a pretty good match concept for somebody who obviously needs to shake off a little ring rust. NARRATOR The comment was meant to incite Danny, and it succeeded magnificently. DANNY What the fuck's that supposed to mean, old man? REGGIE I'm just saying that you didn't look your best out there against Olsig. DANNY Are you kidding? I would've had that match in the bag, were it not for the miscues of my crack security team. Both men look over to see the bodyguards spinning wheelies, then bumping into each other and simultaneously grabbing their injured knees. Dametreyus' chair rolls back and over Danny's foot, triggering a series of expletives from the Superstar. REGGIE My point is that you shouldn't need it- DANNY (grabbing his foot in pain, hopping slightly) Who said anything about need? SHOT OF: Reggie's face as his eyes suddenly widen. CUT TO: I/E. NARRATOR Danny had said a number of incendiary things to Reggie over the course of the trip, but this marked the first time that he had cut him off. In his moment of flippant juvenility, he had forgotten where Reggie earned his reputation... Series of video clips and still shots depicting Reggie in a state of red-faced screaming. Some shots feature a microphone, and most of the audio includes excessive feedback and censoring. And every part features the same fully extended, neck-strained pose with a chunk of spittle hanging in the air just in front of him. NARRATOR ...for talking. Or rather, something several decibels above talking. It was Reggie's penchant for the wrestling promo that earned him his fame in the business, and likewise, his nickname - 'The Lip From Alsip,' in reference to his hometown of Alsip, Illinois. The last shot is a map of Illinois with an arrow pointing to Alsip, then a scale comparison of the size of Reggie's mouth to the city itself. BACK TO: INT. HOTEL - DAY Reggie snaps to life, his nostrils flaring. REGGIE (rapid-fire) I'm the one talkin' bout need, son, and if you'd shut that sewage pipe you call a mouth and listen to the man you're paying for the first time since I put your fancy-ass money in my wallet, you just might learn somethin'. Danny opened his mouth to speak, but it was just left hanging. He gingerly tries to put his foot on the ground and winces - from both sources of pain. REGGIE Now I said 'shouldn't,' not 'didn't' because I sure as shit knew you could've added a fucking kamikaze battalion to this bumblefuck team of yours and you'd still be struggling for the W and the end of the day. Boy, I watched you long before I got that phone call, and I knew you have - or maybe just had - the talent. Now of course somebody in your position needs a little help here and there, but I'm tellin' your spoiled ass that I can't do shit for you - other than drain that bank account - unless I know why the FUCK you came back to the business to begin with. Don't piss in my breakfast and call it lemonade, son. Everyone in earshot crinkles up their face at that line. NARRATOR It should be noted that the one thing keeping Reggie from being a superstar manager was his inability to finish off his trademark long winded promos with a clever line. REGGIE Fuck, kid, you know what I mean! He turns away and kicks at the air. Danny just looks back at him and then hangs his head. NARRATOR And Danny realized that Reggie was right, that he finally needed to accept his weaknesses instead of passivly letting his actions speak. If he was to get a wrestling career started again, he would need a father figure like Reggie Delray showing him the way. He looks up and has a genuine expression for the first time that we've seen him. DANNY Reggie... Right then, Vicki steps in between them, slapping shut her cell. VICKI Car's here! She skips off towards the entrance of the hotel. Reggie just looks back at Danny. DANNY Car's here. He grabs his gear bag off the floor and tosses it onto the lap of Jim Pibb, who grabs his knee in pain. Reggie watches Danny hobble his way out the door cursing Dametreyus, then shakes his head and continues towards the limo. FADE OUT. INT. GYM - LATER It's a renovated-but-still-worn-down gym, with a boxing ring serving for wrestling at the moment. A smattering of people work out on the limited machines and free weights on the other side of the place. NARRATOR Without a lot of advance planning, Danny was unable to find a top-of the-line gym in Hawaii. This was also due to the distinct lack of top-of-the-line gyms in Hawaii. Danny's in the ring, in custom workout gear, working out the limp in his stride thatwas created by the wheelchair mishap earlier. Across the ring, an anonymous workout partner is hidden by his padded boxing headgear. DANNY ...and all I think you really need to know is that I'm here to fucking wrestle, Reggie. I want to get better because I'm a wrestler. NARRATOR The drive to the gym had expunged any feelings of remorse that polluted Danny's mind in the hotel. Reggie just shakes his head, pacing back and forth at the side of the ring. REGGIE Whatever you say, Dan. I just don't think that's good enough for me to help you. DANNY Well, maybe I could find Mickey Meehan and see if he wants to be my manager. CUT TO: I/E. The exact same 'fight card' poster as before, but with Reggie in place of Danny, in his trademark strained w/spittle pose, and a gray-haired man in a sequinned sportcoat pointing to his head with an outstretched thumb. NARRATOR A quick analogy: Mickey 'The Mind' Meehan is to Reggie Delray what Sean Astin is to Danny Ferguson. BACK TO: INT. GYM - DAY REGGIE (immediately) No way. Sorry. I'll keep quiet. Danny snorts, happy to have made his point. He motions towards his practice partner. DANNY That's wat I thought. Besides, you don't think I could just do this for love of the spor- At that moment, his partner tries a collar-and-elbow tie and catches Danny mid-sentence, once again setting off a chain of expletives. Except now they're more muffled, because he bit his tongue. DANNY (bear with me) Wha ga fuk eu hink eur gooing? PRACTICE PARTNER (behind the mouthpiece & headgear) Aye bus dus tyin to estle. DANNY GahGAMMIK! He stalks around the ring a little, limping but trying to fight back the pain and at least return his speech to normal. DANNY I on't know wut you sed, but it better've been 'I sorry, mr. Ferguson, i go leave now,' 'cause you're fukkin' done. Get out da ring. The partner reluctantly agrees as Danny heads to his corner to recoup. He waves Dametreyus over, and the burly semi vehicular guard squeaks his way over. DANNY (unnecessarily nursing his tongue) Dam, go fin 'nother patner. Sum tick-hedded Pasiffic Eyelader who can take de punishent. Dametreyus listens to the message and thinks it through, then looks at Reggie for approval. Fed up, Delray agrees. Dametreyus squeaks away towards the meager crowd. NARRATOR As Dametreyus sought out a suitable replacement for the practice partner, he encountered a new addition to the gym crowd - a local camera crew, filming a story on the lack of top-of-the-line gyms in Hawaii. The crew had already filmed some of Danny's exploits in the ring, and while Dametreyus tried to convince a gymgoer to take the kind of abuse Danny was prone to dish out, the reporter recognized him. A woman in a business suit/skirt combo approaches the hobbled Dametreyus with a microphone. The camera follows closely behind. REPORTER Excuse me...aren't you...Obsidian? Dametreyus looks up at the woman with an expression that says he hasn't heard that name in a while. CUT TO: INT. ARENA - YEARS EARLIER Dametreyus in a red, white and silver spandex uniform, sporting some matching knee and elbow pads. Except he wasn't wrestling. NARRATOR Years earlier, Dametreyus had been known as Obsidian, the very large and very dark character from Colisseum 2000, an athletic contest of sorts that pit normal-sized citizens agains juiced up Superman such as he. Many short clips show Dame...uh, Obsidian competing in several ridiculously contrived events, such as a tug of war, a sumo wrestling contest and a game of table tennis. NARRATOR Despite being massively popular on the show, Obsidian ran into trouble during a later season, when his constant anger went over the edge. This shot shows a contestant in purple spandex, bouncing off a trampoline to dunk a basketball. In midair, Obsidian flies in and spears him out of the picture. BACK TO: INT. GYM - DAY NARRATOR His reputation had never quite recovered. Hence the bodyguard work. DAMETREYUS Um...yeah, yeah that's me. You want an autograph? REPORTER Mmm, not quite. We just noticed you in this gym and wanted to see if we could do a little piece on you, maybe a 'where are they now' kind of thing. DISSOLVE TO: BACK IN THE RING - DAY Danny watches the crew talking to Dametreyus and looks at Reggie. DANNY You call them? REGGIE Nope. DANNY Huh. Dam must have set it up. Good PR move, getting the media catching me in training. Phyllis will love it. Almost makes up for that whole running-over-my-foot thing. REGGIE Almost. Come on, I think you're done in the ring. Let's do some cals to get your heart rate up and call it a night. Danny consents and climbs out of the ring, keeping an eye on the cameras as we... FADE OUT. FADE IN. INT. HOTEL - NIGHT The lobby is empty save for one man as Team Ferguson returns from the gym. Danny looks confused as he walks in. DANNY I don't understand why those guys didn't interview me more. They kept saying 'we've got enough. Dametreyus says nothing, and looks away. NARRATOR Dametreyus had never told Danny that the story was about him, not Ferguson. He had just hoped to avoid the TV once they returned. Unfortunately, their company in the lobby prevented that. As the team walks in, they see Allan North standing alone, his neck craned at the television in the lobby. DANNY Hey, Allan! See anything interesting on TV? ALLAN You could say that. As they approach the TV, Dametreyus' worst fears are confirmed. Their footage is on the local news, but it's worse than it could possibly be. 'HANDICAPPED FORMER TV STAR HELPS RETARDED CHILDREN LEARN TO FIGHT IN LOCAL GYM' The story showed Dametreyus, in the wheelchair, watching Danny and his partner argue in their gibberish. Danny limps and hunches his back as he holds his mouth, letting a little drool slip out. MEanwhile, in the lobby, Danny's face is plain white. DANNY That's not going to be good. ALLAN Nope. DANNY Well, at least it's publicity. Once again escaping to avoid the laughs, Reggie walked up to the front desk, smiling at the attendant. Looking away, his eyes catch the hotel registry. Only one name can be made out, in bold green ink. DANNY FERGUSON, ROOM 425. NARRATOR And suddenly, Danny's return to the ring started to make a little more sense to Reggie. It had never been about anything more than the regular Hollywood goals: Attention, attention, and attention. FADE TO BLACK.
