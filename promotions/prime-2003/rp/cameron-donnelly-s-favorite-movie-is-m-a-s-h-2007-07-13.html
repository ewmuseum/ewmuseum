Cameron Donnelly's Favorite Movie is M.A.S.H.

13 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Friday, July 13, 2007 He was doing everything he could to avoid his brother. He got back from Los Angeles late on purpose so he wouldn’t have to face Chad right away. He used every excuse in the book to prevent the unwanted meeting and where did it get him? A few minutes away from what was always inevitable. Even with the delays and the ignoring the phone calls from his sister, he was still making his way to his parents place to see everyone. Chad hadn’t even bothered calling him. Instead it was Hailey or his Mom calling to find out where he was. He turned off his phone once, preparing the excuse that it had died but he reconsidered given the fact that he was worried about his Mom. Three years back, Chad received a job offer from a large corporation as a Working Client Supervisor, fancy name for a paper pusher who goes for lunches with clients all day it seemed. A year later, he was promoted to be an assistant to the CEO and he was basically making a six figure salary. But all that money and power could not make the backlash from his brother just go away. |~*~| “Look Nicholas, this is something I have to do and it’s that simple. Offers like this only come once in a lifetime and I’m only twenty to boot!” Chad explained, trying to talk some sense into his brother. Nitz wouldn’t stop trying to talk Chad out of leaving, no matter how much Chad got upset. “You fuckin’ selfish prick. You’re leaving all of us behind and you’re leaving Rachelle behind. She loves you and you’re just gonna leave her here. You know she can’t leave with the chance she received with the medical center.” Nicholas said, clearly upset with his brother. He was only eighteen at the time and perhaps just a little immature. But he was quickly discovering that family and close friends were the only people who mattered. But Chad was ruining that for him. “If I ever spoke like that a couple years back Dad would have beaten my face in.” Chad replied to the crude comments from Nicholas, but he wouldn’t have any of it. “He should punch your lights out now you pussy. You are abandoning your family and your friends for some fuckin’ golden road chasing bullshit.” “You are the only one who has a problem with it Nicholas. I spoke to Mom and Dad and I talked with Hailey too. They didn’t have much to say except congrats, which is more than I can say for you.” Chad was sounding upset, trying to turn Nitz into the bad guy by playing his hand. Chad was like that. He manipulated people for his own personal gain and always played any kind of sympathy card he could, even if it didn’t involve the truth in any way. “No one at home will say a fuckin’ word to you cause you’re nothing but a big baby about it. I remember when I was much younger and there were times you wouldn’t get your way. You would sit in one spot and cry for hours, like a fuckin’ baby. Mom wouldn’t buy you a toy, you would throw a fit. Dad wouldn’t take you to the fair, so you flipped out and got grounded for it. But you were always let off the groundings cause they were sick of your incessant whining. You haven’t grown up one bit Chad and if someone besides me said anything, you’d throw such a big fuckin’ fit you’d probably have a heat attack. At twenty, that’s not a good thing.” After he was done the speech, he took a deep breath, but it was like Chad wasn’t listening. He completely no sold the rant from his brother and he had already made up his mind, regardless of what Nicholas or the family may think. Chad got up from the kitchen chair and went for the door. Nitz threw his face in his hands for a moment and then stood up from the chair with an angry look on his face. He stared death into the soul of his brother. Chad was looking concerned for a moment, but quickly wiped the look of worry off his face. “Chad, I promise you, if you leave here and do this to everyone, you’ll never find me calling you again. If I ever see you coming around here looking for compassion like the god damn baby you are, I will tear you limb from fuckin’ limb. I swear I’ll hurt you the same way you’re hurting everyone here. Don’t make me despise my brother with everything I am. Don’t make me hate the brother who I grew up with...” He pauses, long enough for Chad to shake his head. Nicholas finally thought he got through the thick skin of his dickhead brother, but just when he thought he would come back and sit down, he reached for the doorknob and quickly disappeared outside. |~*~| So what would a brother do? Well Nitz went on with his life like his brother didn’t exist anymore. In the three years leading up to the present, he hid all the pictures and memories of his brother so that coming across anything wasn’t a real option. But the hurt had always been there. He put an act forward from that point, like he had truly moved on with no remorse for the entire situation, but that’s all it was. But it wasn’t Hollywood. He didn’t want some kind of award for making people believe him. Instead he constantly thought about that fateful day, when his brother revealed his true colors to their complete shining glory. It was obvious before, but proven that night. His brother was selfish beyond all reason and there was nothing anyone could do to stop it. The family went on without Chad although no one treated the situation quite like Nicholas did. Hailey had kept in contact with Chad shortly after he left because she partially admired him for following his ambitions. But she also knew where Nick was coming from and after some time, the calls stopped. Nicholas wouldn’t say he won her over to his side, but she slowly understood more and more where he was coming from. So Hailey and Nicholas grew closer while Chad was becoming nothing more than a memory to them. That is until the cancer arose in their mother. Meanwhile, their father couldn’t care less really. He had enough of Chad’s games and complaining in the past and saw him leaving as somewhat of a vacation from the garbage that his song brought with him. He was always a decent father, until his wife developed the disease and he suddenly threw himself into an alcoholic based depression thinking he was going to lose his wife. There was no end in sight for him either, except for when he saw the bottom of a bottle. Cameron needed help but before it could be given to him, he had to accept it. But through all the problems and the entire ruckus that was caused when Chad left, Trina Donnelly stayed somewhat loyal to her eldest son. She was sad he was leaving and upset that Nicholas took it so hard, but she also felt somewhat proud of him for his success. She always wanted to see her kids doing what they wanted to do. Hailey was the loving caring person she envisioned who followed her dreams and became a veterinary assistant. Chad was a businessman through and through. He was always interested in investments and was offered his job in Portland after only a year and a half of college. After he left, he attended night classes three nights a week after working during the day. Nicholas meanwhile was a different person. He chose wrestling from the beginning. After watching all his heroes on TV growing up, he got into training early. He had visited facilities in Japan and Mexico to learn from the best before mastering his craft. He was always very athletic and Trina thought he would be a football player or a baseball superstar. She couldn’t envision him wrestling for a living but it’s what made him happy and as long as he was happy, Mom was happy. Pulling up to the front of his parent’s house, he took roll call based on the vehicles parked out front. His Dad’s truck was at the head of the driveway and figured that he had enough alcohol since it didn’t look like it had been moved for a few days. He knew Mom’s car would be next as she recently left the house for not much else except to get a few groceries and to go to doctors appointments. Hailey’s Explorer sat on the street in front of the house. But that was it. “This is a fuckin’ tease.” He thought to himself, forced to be optimistic his brother packed up and left already. He knew that wasn’t the case, but one could only hope. Given all the attempts to miss this family reunion of sorts, he wanted nothing more than to avoid dealing with his brother for another three years. Oh if luck was only on his side. |~*~| “Chad went to go see a couple friends earlier. He should be here shortly.” Hailey explained while rinsing a dinner plate in the sink. She shook it off and tucked it inside the dishwasher and closed the door. Nicholas sat at the kitchen table, even though he wanted nothing more than to disappear out the very same door his brother left through three years earlier. “How was the event hun? We watched it on TV.” Trina said while rubbing her son’s shoulder. “I had no idea you guys got FX.” “I had them get it.” Hailey said as she made her way to the table with a drink in hand. “How could we miss a chance to see you on TV?” “Yeah and you know Mom and Dad are on a fixed income. Who’s paying for it?” “I am.” His sister sits and crosses her legs while taking a sip from her milk. “I’m playing for their entire cable bill, mostly because I spend more time here lately than at my own apartment. Been staying in the basement on the hide a bed for the past couple days actually.” “Eating all the fu...” Cameron said, trying to get their attention from the living room in the midst of an obvious drunken cough. “... the fuckin’ food too.” Everyone at the table shook their heads trying to ignore the rants emanating from the other room. “Mom, when are we going to get Dad some help? He is digging himself into an early grave.” Hailey spoke softly so that only Nitz and their Mother could hear. The Ego audibly sighed and began to pat his hand on the table, drawing the attention to him. “Good luck trying there Hail. The drunken lazy ass won’t accept any help, so we can’t just throw it at him and expect him to put up with it.” “Hey, that’s your Father you’re talking about. Stop with the mouth Nick.” Trina said while shifting in her seat. “Sorry Ma.” Nitz got up from the table and went to the cabinet to get a drink. As he closed the cupboard he swore the click was not the only thing he heard slamming. Moving to the fridge, he looked at the window facing the driveway of the house beside the table and saw a shadow walk by. The thin curtain was closed but he was sure beyond any doubt that Chad was about to walk through the door. He poured himself a quick glass of juice and returned the carton to its spot inside the fridge. He leaned on the counter facing away from the door as the knob turned. Hailey and their Mother braced themselves for impact as Chad finally made his appearance in front of his brother. Nitz stayed quietly still, closing his eyes and listening to the rustling of a coat and a bag hitting the floor. “How ya feeling Mom?” Chad said as he kissed Trina on the top of the head. Moving over to Hailey, he put his hand on her shoulder. She didn’t push it away but she looked like she wanted to a little. “Hey sis.” He said while turning and removing his hand. He looked across the kitchen at his little brother. Memories came flooding back to both of them. But through all the good times that swam through their minds, the one that stuck out the most moved to the forefront. Nicholas finally opened his eyes and took a sip of his juice. He rested the glass on the counter and turned to face his older brother. He was almost as he remembered him. Blonde hair, just like their mother had before the chemotherapy started. It was a little longer than he remembered. The stylish frames of the glasses were resting comfortably in front of his green eyes. No one had any idea who he took after for needing glasses as both their Mother and Father and respective Grandparents didn’t require corrective lenses until they were much older. Nicholas scanned every thing about his brother. All the way from the suit he was wearing to the wing tipped shoes. Chad did the same, from Nitz’s spiky black and silver hair to the tight track shirt he wore, down past the loose fitting cargo pants to the Dockers on his feet. They both mostly started into each others eyes, not saying a word. Hailey got up from her seat at the table to move between the two but Trina rested a hand on her arm, stopping her. She gave her daughter a look of “Let them settle this on their own. They’re adults.” Hailey took the hint and stood beside her Mother. The tension began to loom over them all like a large dark cloud. Hailey was constantly wondering how bad it was going to be. “Can someone ge’ some icthe for meeee?!” Cameron slurred his words from his chair in the living room. “You look good little bro.” Chad said lightly. Nicholas was taken back by the comment, innocent as it was. He fully expected the snot nosed spoiled brat to return in full force right off the bat. Instead, he fire off a basic and complimentary remark. But Nitz saw through it right away. It was just like when Chad left. He was making Nicholas look like the bad guy again in an effort to abolish the bad thoughts about himself. Nitz was guessing, but he knew he was probably right. So he did the only thing he could accept doing. “So Hail you said you saw me on TV this week. What’d you think?” He ignored his brother. He took his glass and boosted himself onto the counter, much like Chad did in years past and almost in the exact same spot. Nitz normally sat by or on the stove when he was younger and he was doing this to try and get a reaction from Chad. The older brother just dropped his shoulders which won a slight smirk from Nitz. Hailey meanwhile was shaking her head at her youngest brother’s ignorance. She knew this wouldn’t be pretty but she thought there might be some good to come out of it. Nitz lost the smirk quickly after looking into his sister’s eyes and shrugged. “Ok fine! What the fuck am I supposed to say?! I told this selfish bastard when he left that I’d hurt him if I saw him around here again. So he shows up and I do nothing. I thought you’d be fuckin’ ecstatic that I didn’t knock his lights out and mess up the floor tiles when I break his pelican sized beak!” Nitz finished and Hailey just rolls her eyes. Trina turns around in her seat. The breaking of glass is heard in the living room, not an uncommon sound when Cameron falls asleep in his chair with his drinking glass in his hand. They all ignore it. “What’d I tell you about that language in my home Nicholas?” She sounded stern, so much so that she surprised all three children. Nitz threw his arms up in the air. “Fine, tell ya what... let me stew over this while we go to Mikey’s. I told him I’d come see him when I got back and I’d feel more comfortable after having a chance to unwind my damn mind after seeing this wimp in a monkey suit.” They all nod their heads, Chad staying oddly quiet that whole time he heard his little brother ranting and calling him every possibly thing in the book. He moves to the table and sits for a moment while Nicholas downs the remainder of his juice. Once again, the breaking of glass is heard from the other room. Once was normal, twice was not. “What the hell you doin’ in there ya silly drunken...” Trailing off he began to walk from the counter into the other room, to face the other family member he had not shared time with recently. |~*~| The sounds of theme song to Bewitched began to play throughout the living room. A little bit of sunlight shone through the window although the drapes were pulled mostly shut, leaving the room in quite a bit of darkness. Cameron Donnelly shuffled around in his chair, hearing what he thought to be his eldest son greeting his Mother. Looking at his empty glass through his beer goggles, he called out to the kitchen “Can someone ge’ some icthe for meeee?!” He liked having his drinks on the rocks. But hearing no answer from anyone inside the kitchen, he signed and put his glass down on the table. Cameron was sitting in his recliner, thoughts running through his mind. He had no idea what he was doing so wrong and why everyone was ignoring him or talking to him like he was the worst person on the planet. Alcoholism did that to a man. He had no idea he had a problem and whenever someone tried to talk to him about it, he could barely understand them. As far as he was concerned, his wife, who he loved dearly, was dying. He became greatly depressed and had been put on medication for it. But he still turned to the drink to calm his nerves. But without his ice he wasn’t happy. Without his wife he wasn’t happy. Most of all, with his family ignoring him for the most part, he wasn’t happy at all. “Fuckers...” He mumbled while reaching for an empty bottle on the TV tray beside him. “I’ll show them...” He heard Nicholas ranting and yelling in the kitchen but paid it no mind. He took the bottle and broke then bottom of it off on the chair, the sound of breaking glass barely audible to him over the sound of the television. Winning no attention to the kitchen, he looked at the broken bottle, sharp pieces of glass sticking out all over the place. He brought the glass down on his wrist roughly, drawing blood but no pain through the drunken state. He continued to dig the glass into his arm, blood beginning to flow down his arm and onto the chair, eventually making its way to the floor. Part of him didn’t know what he was doing. But part of him was trying to extract revenge on his family for writing him out of their lives for the most part. A vacant look occupied his eyes but a disgusting smile was visible from his mouth. He began to feel weak, not showing much control with the bloody broken bottle. He finally gave up, looking at his arm and seeing a large amount of blood on the chair and the floor. Growing weaker, his other arm fell, causing the bottle to crash on the wood that was on the front of the chair, the sound of glass once again ringing out. He finally heard some rustling in the kitchen, his eyes rolling around and his eyelids growing heavy. |~*~| Nitz made his way into the living room, not expecting to see what he saw. Hailey took a peak around the corner and Chad also came to stand in the doorway. Trina was still sitting at the table and she was lightly sobbing, keeping it quiet from her children when she looked and saw the broken glass on the floor among the puddle of blood. “FUCK!” Nicholas yelled, ripping his shirt off and kneeling beside the recliner. The knee of his pants was quickly soaked through with his Father’s blood. Chaos erupted in the Donnelly home as Nitz took his shirt, rolled it up and wrapped it around the blood covered arm creating a makeshift tourniquet. Hailey was crying and screaming for her Daddy the whole time while trying to console her Mother and Chad stood shocked in the doorway. Nitz turned to him with tears in his eyes. “DON’T JUST STAND THERE YOU FUCK! CALL 911! CALL AN AMBULANCE!” Chad broke out of his trance as Hailey ran for the phone in the kitchen. Chad saw this and made his way over to his Brother and Father. He kneeled down beside Nicholas and grabbed the shirt from his hands, trying to help how he could. The younger brother meanwhile put the chair upright and put down the footrest, trying to help his Father retain his consciousness. “What the fuck old man? WHAT THE FUCK DID YOU DO?!” He yelled, tears streaming down his face. Chad was just as emotional, never seeing such a disturbing scene. Nitz tried to look into his Father’s eyes. He was alive but was quickly losing the fight to stay conscious. “Don’t you dare old man! Don’t you leave us now! I won’t let you! I’ll never forgive you!” He continued, looking at his brother now and reminding him of three years ago. These words were spoken much faster and without thought, but similar in nature. Having the bleeding mostly under control, Nicholas grabbed the other hand of his Father’s willing him to stay alive through the ordeal. Cameron flashed a slight smile in his face, it wasn’t much but Nicholas certainly noticed. “Don’t leave us here you old fuck! Stay with me!” Sirens could he heard in the distance, the ambulance obviously not that far away anymore. There was an eerie silence hanging in the air as the sirens came closer. Red flashing lights were now visible through the curtains, reflecting off the walls of the dark living room. Sobbing from the kitchen grew louder as Hailey hugged her Mother. The door burst open and both brothers move away from their Father, Chad still staring in disbelief and Nicholas walking backwards in shock. He wiped his forehead, drenching it in his Father’s blood. He backed into the TV and then slumped to the ground with a look of shock on his face. The paramedics quickly did their job and the eldest living Donnelly was quickly being wheeled out of the house. Chad offered a hand to Nicholas to help him up and without a thought, the youngest Brother took it. He was lifted to his feet and he ran to get his coat from the kitchen, escorting his Mother and Sister out the door. Chad followed, locking up the house behind him. Another eerie feeling crept into the house after the family departed the premises. The TV was left on and after the red lights of the ambulance disappeared into the distance, the glow was much more visible in the room. Blood was dripping from the chair onto the carpet, broken glass was littering the ground around the chair and the theme to the M.A.S.H movie was the only sound heard after the sirens faded away. It was Cameron Donnelly’s favorite movie. “I realize and I can see... that suicide is painless It brings on many changes and I can take or leave it if I please.”
