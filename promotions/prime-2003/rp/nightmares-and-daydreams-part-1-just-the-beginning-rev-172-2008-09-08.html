Nightmares and Daydreams - Part 1 - Just the Beginning (ReV 172)

8 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Monday, September 8, 2008 Monday, Sept ember 8th, 2008 “How old are you Dizzy? It's time for a big girl bed.” The littlest Sirrajin was being a stubborn cookie. She was lying current bed, a modified crib and was refusing to get out of it at the pure mention of it disappearing. She was well over two years old now and certainly it was time for her to take the next step in life into a normal bed. But Desiree was having none of it. “No! No new bed!” She sprawled out on the thin mattress and grabbed at the side rail, refusing to let go. Everytime Killean or Jenny reached down for her, she would scream bloody murder. Had the neighbors not known a child was next store, the police would be called. “Way to take care of it.” Jenny joked, referring to a previous statement from Killean asking specifically to “Let me take care of it”. “I didn't do any worse than you.” “She wasn't screaming into my ear before you came in here!” Jenny retorted. “Kids should come with a god damn training manual.” Killean leaned against the wall, watching Desiree relax as both adults took a step away. Desiree stuck out her tongue at her father, following a tradition of one upsmanship dating back almost a full year when Killean did it to Vegas and Desiree caught on quickly. Most parents would see it as an insult, but Killean knew better. It was Desiree's way of playing around in the situation, lightening the mood if even an ounce. “I'm gonna go pick up the B-E-D.” He spelled it out, knowing full well that Desiree couldn't spell just yet. “Be careful.” Jenny replied, taking a step closer to Desiree again as the small child grabbed hold of the railing in a rebellious manner. Killean left the room and made his way for the door. |~o~| “It's almost sounding not worth it to rent a new car all the time to make sure he doesn't recognize me.” Jordan was walking through the lot, only mere seconds behind her target. Killean was leaving to get Desiree her new bed and Jordan had every intent on seeing her master plan unfold. The creepy thing was, she was talking to herself, or so it looked like. Out from her over sized handbag popped the head of not a cat this time, but a small chihuahua. The dog looked around a bit and licked it's lips and ducked into the bag again for just enough time to grab another treat. Jordan pressed the button to unlock the doors, her newly dyed red hair falling over her sunglass covered face. One would also come to the conclusion that her physical features had slightly changed as of late. “Can't control the kiddo huh? Losing control of your family? Maybe Jennifer just can't hack it as a Mom. Have you ever thought of that?” She said, starting the car after letting her dog out of the bag and onto the passenger seat. She waited for Killean to pull out of the parking lot before putting her rental car into drive to follow. “He really should face facts Trixy. He needs a motherly figure to take care of Desiree all the time and I really don't think Jenny is up to it. I know what happens when he's away and he doesn't.” She explained to the little animal who hops up on his hind legs to watch the world go by through the tinted glass. “That guy I hired cost me a lot of cash. Hope he did what he was supposed to do. It's amazing what you can do with computers.” |~o~| A kid with a medium build wheeled out the new bed Killean was buying for Desiree. As the Supreme Machine was digging in his pockets, a woman wearing a hood with red hair could be seen standing far enough away from the conversation to not be noticed but close enough to know what was going on at the register. She was mindlessly searching through some merchandise, looking into her handbag to see the dog laying down inside, making sure her furry friend wasn't about to bring attention their way. “I'm sorry sir, this credit card has been declined.” The man behind the register said with a scowl on his face. Why did people always do that when a card gets declined? Like the customer wasn't humiliated enough when it happened? But Killean had nothing to be humiliated about because nothing was wrong, or so he thought. A smile could be seen on the face of Jordan... and it was only the beginning. “You'd better call the card company and find out just what the problem is.” “Already done Mr. Sirrajin. Your card was closed by upper management at the company.” The snarky man said matter of factly. It was his universal way of thinking 'They don't want anything to do with you anymore'. “Yeah, I'll get that straightened up after I leave here. But you'd better drop the attitude before I take my cash elsewhere baldy.” Killean pulled out his wallet and started to thumb through the bills. Jordan was looking over and flashed a look of disappointment on her face. “Oh, I apologize sir. My tone of voice was not meant to sound as such.” The clerk said backtracking quickly. Killean just shook his head and opened his cell phone to dial a number as he followed the young gentlemen outside who was wheeling the cart. “He hasn't carried that much cash on him in weeks!” Jordan loudly whispered to her handbag. The dog lightly barked in response. “Oh come on, how was I supposed to know he'd be carrying that much today. So much for saving the day by sparing the cash.” She had intended to initiate a friendship of sorts with Killean today, offering to lend him the money to pay for the bed short term. Meanwhile, as the new bed was being loaded into the trunk of his car, Killean pressed “SEND” on his cell phone. A staggered ring followed, odd for just making a local call. A person picked up on the other end, but it wasn't who he dialed. Instead, a message from the phone company was heard. “We're sorry, but this line has been temporarily suspended. Sorry for the inconvenience. Message 324.” Killean flipped his phone shut in disgust, wondering just what else could go wrong on the day. |~o~| Killean roared into the parking lot of the club, almost hitting another parked vehicle in the process. He was in a rush to see Vegas and this time it was more than important. It had been a day of trial and tribulation for the PRIME Choice. In a few short hours, Killean had come to discover that not only were his credit cards all maxed out and closed, but his cell phone service had been turned off due to a supposed lack of payment and his joint bank accounts were frozen for fear of what was called an “Account Breach”. In layman's terms, someone has stolen some money. Killean got out of the car and slammed his door in anger, marching to the back door and typing in the code to enter. He quickly closed the door behind him as Jordan pulled up to get a quick look. “No point in trying to get in there Trixy. Bouncers at the front and no access to the back cause of the darn code. Looks like it's the end of the lone for us.” Jordan said as she slowly pulled away from the establishment. Inside, Killean marched into the office to see Vegas peaking his head out into the club, admiring the female lead singer of the house band playing for the evening. Her voice was wonderful and it had him somewhat mesmerized. Killean had to slap him on the shoulder to get his attention. “Ow, what the hell was that for?” Ok, so the slap was a little hard, but Killean was working out some aggression the only way he knew how. Hey, he felt better already... sort of. “We need to talk big man.” Killean replied as he took a seat behind his desk and put his feet up. Looking at his demeanor, you really wouldn't guess Killean was having such a terrible day. But Vegas could see it in his eyes. “Sure man, what's the story?” “Well I remember that I told you to slap me silly if I brought this up so soon after my legal battles but it needs to be done.” Killean said, not even having to spell it out for his tall dark friend to catch on. “You're not serious?” “Dead serious dude. I've had problem after problem arise today.” Killean put his feet back on the ground and leaned over as Vegas took a seat on the desk beside him. “Something has happened and money has gone missing from my private account, our joint account and or credit cards have been maxed out. The bank froze all the accounts and the cards have been suspended. Hell, even my cell phone service was turned off. I don't even wanna know what else could be wrong that I haven't checked. They froze Jenny's accounts too and she is piping hot about that one. It's a damn good thing I signed majority ownership of this place over to you before so everything is in your name.” “But man, the overseas accounts? Someone could still be watching those kinds of transactions. That's an assload of cash to transfer man.” Yep, and you only thought these kinds of illegal activities took place in the movies. Think again jack. “We'll have to do a bunch of smaller transactions from the numerous accounts to avoid large tracking. Spread it out and make it look minor. It's been a while since I was jailed and stuff so I don't think anyone is monitoring anymore. I was clean after all. Just call the guy, he's the cleanest and fastest around for this type of issue.” Killean got out of his chair to look out the window into the parking lot. It wasn't that he was paranoid, he was just looking for any reason to punch anyone square in the temple at that moment. “I'll see what I can do man. In the meantime, use the business cell phone for yourself and I'll get you some scratch to use just as soon as I hit the bank machine. But what the fuck man? Someone is messing with your shit? That's brutal.” “Someone has a fuckin' death wish. Although the banks said they are on top of the issue, but I've heard this shit can take months to clear up. I don't wanna wait that long or mess with those hidden accounts much. It's just begging for trouble.” Killean moved back over to his chair to ponder the situation in silence a little more. His story from earlier in the day popped back into his head. “Speaking of this crap, one of the credit card companies said there was a large amount charged to the card before it was closed, some kinda private transaction, used for under the table types of surgeries and such.” “Surgery?” “Yeah, cosmetic and the such when you don't wanna have long waits for procedures. People are going to private health care more and more for the cosmetic things.” Killean explained. “Well they had to grow an extra set of balls to mess with your life man. Heh. Perhaps they had to get them removed as well.” “Very funny dude.” Killean sighed. |~o~| “I think they look great!” Jordan said as she looked in the mirror in her washroom. “The scars healed up nicely and are barely visible after only a couple weeks.” She reached down to cradle her new D cups in her hand, admiring the cleavage that she never had before. Fergie the cat just looked on, not really knowing what exactly his master was talking about. Trixy meanwhile was no where to be found, most likely sleeping under the bed after a long hard day balled up in Jordan's handbag. “You think he'll like them?” Jordan questioned the cat. He just jumped off the counter and went about his own business. Jordan wasn't really looking for a response anyways. “They were quite expensive. It's just a good thing I didn't have to put out that kind of money. I couldn't afford it.” She smiled at herself, proud of her new found fake beauty. She looked into the hallway at Fergie washing himself and a small frown developed on her face. “Do you think my nose needs some work too?”
