Date: Today ***The Future*** He sat there inside the bus terminal. Alone. Welcome to Detroit, with snow flakes dropping rapidly from the pitch black backdrop. We had to be talking about it at least being twenty-five degrees outside, causing most to simply cover up with their best winter wear and blankets. As the flakes descended ever forcefully, he looked up. He blew. And a haze of smoke scattered a few delicate snow flakes within a breaths wake. He brought his head down, placing his Kool cigarette back onto his lips, where it dangled loosely. In the dead of this bitter November night, he was slightly under dressed. A pair of worn denim jeans along with a Patriots hoodie were his main defense against the elements. Add a pair of white nameless sneakers to the mix and a white Reebok knit stocking cap with a small New England logo in the lower middle and you had the basic appearance. The cap had a waffle texture design with a drying polyester lining that would allow for quick drying. And considering the down pour going on now, that would come in handy. The knit cap played a stark contrast to the long jet black locks of hair coming from his skull. They seemed messy and rather unkempt. One good look at his face denoted his ethnicity. Japanese. Semi-slanted eyes that burned like deep blue flames. The eyes are said to be a gateway into the soul, and if that were true, the gateway one would take would be to the deepest pits of Hell. The contrast to the eyes would be the silver ring in the corner of his right eyebrow. He need not talk to anyone, as his iPod kept him company. Arc Arsenal by At The Drive In. The current song on the play list. He brought his right hand up to once again fiddle with his cigarette. He ashed the end of it, allowing the embers to float helplessly into midair. He was currently at the end of his cigarette, meaning he'd get to enjoy the taste of the filter shortly. But before he could enjoy even that much, he took a double take. How did he get here? From the apparent cream of the crop to the bottom of the barrel? From the toast of Tokyo to the dust upon an ABBA record. From taking the reigns of perhaps the most notorious character in all of Japanese wrestling lore to throwing it away, just to destroy his own heritage? He did not know. But as he tasted the filter, he threw the cigarette out before him carelessly. He then felt a sharp pain enter his right knee. He took the time to rub it, the cold obviously getting to his joints. He wasn't an old man by any stretch of the imagination, but his body hid many secrets. Like the heavily tattooed pair of chiseled arms and the right knee that twice had to be surgically repaired. Below a man's attire, his story is told through the scars he has earned. And this man had earned plenty. The wrestling organizations stopped calling. Hearing of the Japan debacle, no one could willing risk someone who was willing to expose the business just to settle a grudge. He hated his father and brothers, so he destroyed their legacy. With one removal of a mask, he brought shame to his family name. Someone willing to do that, destroy his own heritage, was a person without limits. Without boundaries. And nobody wanted that. No matter how great he might be when it came to wrestling. But it was of no consequence. Wrestling was slowly dying from his perspective. The Fans Wrestling Organizations and Squared Circle's he had seen were dragging in the ratings. The heroes on those shows could not pull a buy rate. And even with all the people like Craig Miles to the Coral Avalon's of the world fighting their hardest, it made no difference. He pulled his pack of cigarettes out of his pocket. Kool Mild. That meant blue. The box was longer than most. That meant 100's. He pulled out another cancer stick and lit it up with a flip of his lighter. And as he put the pack directly back into his pocket, the song faded into Capricorn by 30 Seconds To Mars. So I run and hide...And tell myself I'll start again...With a brand new name...And eyes that see...Into infinity... The words he'd heard a thousand times over, but until now, they never meant anything. How could he have been so foolish? He was black listed, but still wanted in the back door. But hearing a voice crow on in his head, the thoughts of why began to unfold. He didn't want to wrestle conventionally. No. He wanted to be a weapon. The atom bomb. The chemical agent that would kill thousands...NAH...a million in his wake. A cold blooded apocalypse. The end. A razor thin smile began to form upon his weather worn face. There was a corner of the wrestling world that still had a buzz going on within it. All he had to do was infiltrate it. A plan began to spring forth in his head. And as the bus approached, November became December. Night became day. And Heaven would become Hell. *** 'Allen Bertruger...?' The voice of the secretary stated, obviously puzzled. 'It's Bertruger. No Bert.' He responded with a deadpan at the person serving him. She looked over his face, before she blurted, 'Must be to be more accepting of an American heritage.' He looked at her, not flinching. Not blinking. Not even a smile to reassure her comment was necessary. She shifted with unease within her chair as she stared at the man across the service window. 'Bertruger is a odd name. Any chance you'll tell me what it means?' A flirt. Trying to make up for her past transgressions. 'It's German.' 'But why take on a German name with your heritage?' She interrupted, now becoming somewhat interested in her subject. 'Heritage means nothing to me. As I was saying, it's German. Bertrug simply means fraud. Trickery. Double-deal.' He stated it coldly. Heartlessly. 'And why would you want a name like that?' She asked, being overcome with the queerest of vibes from this person. 'Let's just say it fits.' And with that, a smile broke out on his face. He laughed. And she looked at him puzzled. 'I had you goin' there for a minute...didn't I? Sorry, it's just been a long day, and I wanted to pull a gag on someone to lessen the tension. Know what I'm sayin' hun?' A smile came across her face. She obviously was comforted by the fact that this guy wasn't being serious. 'I was gonna say...gosh...you seem like a rather mean spirited person.' 'Well, I try to make the best out of bad situations. See, my folks just passed away, and my brothers are some of the most moronic people on this Earth. I'd rather just become invisible to them, you know? Not deal with their shit on a daily basis. Finally get away from all that Japanese hoopla and make a livin' for myself in America.' She smirked. 'I can understand how family can be. Did you just move back from Tokyo? I mean, your information mentions a change in residence from LA to Tokyo then Detroit? Doesn't look all that long either.' 'Yeah. Had to take care of the fam for a while. But I'm back, ready set the world on fire.' His blue eyes mimicked his words as they seemed to burn bright. But with what? 'That's so sweet. Just give me a couple minutes to file all the necessary paperwork, and you'll have your new alias in no time flat.' And after a few minutes, it was over. A new beginning. With a brand new name. Allen gave the women a lazy salute with his right index finger, and said his thank you. He left the Secretary of State's office nonchalantly, as if not a care in the world could change his mood. The second he stepped outside, he reached into his pocket and pulled out a cigarette, sticking it in his mouth and quickly lighting it. He breathed in the cancerous fumes and chuckled. 'Rashard, if only you could see the gullible people. If only you could be here to see me take your name and bring our world to its' knees.' he said to himself silently as he kicked his iPod into gear once again, placing the small ear phones in his ears. 'But being six feet under kinda prevents that kinda shit from happenin', now doesn't it?' ***The Present*** 'No Paul...the cuts aren't that shocking.' Brandon trailed off. The kitchen in the Mojave desert abode of the Greatest Wrestler Alive Today, the Only Diamond In The Sport Brandon Youngblood was a myriad of appliances and fixtures. The kitchen had a fine wood floor, varnished a deep amber. The walls were in a white hue, with the ceiling being the same with thousands, if not millions of jagged points imbedded in in thanks to the ceiling being pop corned. The counter tops were all pearly white marble, giving the reflection of the hot desert sun from the window just above the stainless steel industrial sized flat grill at which Brandon was standing at. It was something you'd see at a restaurant. Just above it was a window, looking over the burn pit along with the vast sand dune and mountain landscape. In his left hand was a cordless phone, pressed against his ear. And in his right was a steel spatula. The batter below was sizzling as the tremendous heat from the grill. He was focused in on the twelve circular spots of batter, making sure to peel each one diligently. 'Look...I know Kidman had talent but he was always hurt Paul. What do you want me to say? It just makes sense. Now he can go seek a place out that will actually use his talent.' The food was in the wooden cabinets below the marble counter top, with doors there to open to gain access to the good stuff. There were also wooden cabinets above the counter tops, nailed high into the wall with glass midsections on the doors. Both were varnished in a lighter shade of brown. Brandon flipped a pancake into the air, having it land perfectly after it spun and cascaded downward. He then looked away from his breakfast momentarily before looking up through his window in an almost annoyed fashion. 'No Paul...the Circle ain't coming back...' Brandon was in a pair of yellow shorts and a black Iowa Wrestling shirt fitting snuggly on his upper body. He flipped two pancakes, this time stopping with the theatrics and doing it all simple like. 'No...I wouldn't tell you even if it did. The internet can talk all they want about it coming back, but it's dead London. It died when Harvard did.' London had been on Brandon for a month about the rumored rebirth of the famed Squared Circle, an organization that had revolutionized how people look at wresting forever. 'Melissa is doing fine, by the way. Maybe a woman would calm you down man.' He laughed for a moment before continuing his flipping duties. 'So...Matt was on Raw...huh? Is he actually under contract?' Brandon lingered there for a moment as he listened in on his phone to his good buddy from the WWE. 'That's not fair London...NO...I'm not telling you if the Circle is coming back to find out! Look, if it was coming back, you couldn't compete anyway. You're stuck in that stupid deal you signed with the E.' Melissa came into the scene, in a pair of blue jeans and a tight black shirt, her hair a mess. She was with a pair of glasses on her face, and had yet to put any make up on her face. She really did not need to, as she had a natural beauty to her. She could pull off the look with relative ease. She walked over, her bare feet smacking against the fine wood floor as she came over, hugging Brandon from behind and kissing him on the neck. 'What you cooking?' she asked as Brandon looked over his shoulder with a smile on his face. 'Pancakes...no that was Melissa... She then broke off the hug and walked over to stainless steel refrigerator, grabbing a gallon jug of two percent milk, then walking over and grabbing a glass from one of the cabinets. She poured herself a glass and then walked to the wood table across the kitchen, pulling out a chair and taking a seat as she tired to fight back her tiredness. The table was situated before the glass doors to the deck on the outside, and sun poured in through the doorway. 'Dude...you against Avalon would rock, but you'd need to get out of your contract first.' Brandon had a giggle at playing around with his friend like this. 'Who's that on the phone Brand?' Melissa asked as she wiped her eyes lips clean before taking a drink of her milk. 'It's London hun...' he trailed off as he flipped the pancakes over again. 'Look...you could always go to Jolt.' Melissa almost spit up her milk at that one as Brandon laughed out loud, still managing to keep some semblance of attention on his and her breakfast 'Yes, that's a joke Paul...Gawd...you take shit too seriously man...Melissa, if you could get a plate, these are ready.' Melissa got up from her chair, walking over and grabbing a ceramic plate and setting it beside Brandon. Brandon ran the spatula under each pancake as he carried it over the small distance and put it on the plate. 'The Academy is comin' around. I'm just taking guys and gals who want in now...' He then turned the gas off for the grill, then took to leaning against the kitchen counter. 'Dude...Amy Campbell could survive a day training with me. I'd break her down and she'd be too spent.' He stood there as Melissa pulled down two more plates, and a pair of forks. She then stabbed about four pancakes and put them on her plate. She then made the trek over to the table, sitting down and digging in. Brandon just sighed as he shook his head, his focus now completely shifted to his Texas Wrestling Academy cohort. 'VERY funny Paul...you know making those comments about her being flat on her back is so damn carbon dated.' Melissa slowly ate her food as Brandon continued his talk. 'Yes Paul...have fun with the house shows, I'll see you when you get time off.' He then pulled the phone away from his ear, turning it off and looking at Melissa. She simply looked back at him and fired off on the usual training regime. 'Got that last bit of Gibson film study before dinner.' 'Already went through them.' Brandon responded as he grabbed the syrup from just above, pouring a generous amount on the pancakes on his plate, not even taking the time to put them on the one Melissa had just brought down for him. 'You know...that Paul...you'd think that you're dream was so good and everything. But to him, it's like he's in hell. I mean, he's got the Cruiserweight Title for Christ sakes!' But Melissa was not even tuned in to that. 'Brandon...when could you have possibly gone through that last two hour block?' Brandon stuffed his face, chewing his food as he responded. 'Had trouble sleeping, did it before the run.' And now, Melissa took the disappointed tone. 'Brandon...' 'What...I had trouble sleeping...I like to make use of my time...so sue me!' Brandon fired back as he swallowed. 'Don't worry, we'll get on the flight tonight and I can catch up on the z's. Besides, opens up some free time.' And that free time would be used for more training. David Gibson was a unique challenge, a man who was actually a great technical wrestler. He wanted to make sure his preparations justified someone of his technical skill. *** David Gibson. Nice enough guy. But he's a dirty cheap shooting bastard. But when it comes to pure technical ability he has very few peers in this world. But the fact of the matter is, he isn't my peer. He's a wily veteran who can get away with things. But when that bell rings, he won't find the opening to use those tactics. I won't let him breath. Endurance is the factor. Gibson, I've studied. And he can go, but can he go when someone is not just trying to beat him into mush, but actually trying to sap that energy level? Can he react or have time to think when I'm in his face after every movement he makes? When that bell rings, I'm a damn bull. I go for the kill every single time. Maybe I don't hit as hard as Sirrajin at first, but after ten minutes of me grounding your every move, I will hit harder then anyone you've ever known. It's about wanting something more than someone else. And Gibson, I want this more then you. You know why? Simple. Momentum. Momentum going into Colossus II would mean the difference between myself and Nova. He's game now. He's ready for a war. And so am I. But if I lose, I risk the chance of over exerting myself. I don't want to break my routine here. If I lose, something is wrong in the routine, and it is hard to get a groove in. The roll up from Nova proved to be a blessing in disguise. But the fact is, Gibson, it isn't disrespect. I respect you for what you are. But you are not going to go into that ring and prove anything to the world. You realize what you are dealing with? I'm the Greatest Wrestler Alive Today. You...you're Old School. But that shit is old hat. I've wrestled in some of the biggest matches over the last three years and proven without a shadow of a doubt that I am everything I say I am. I make everyone sweat at night. They are afraid of me. And who could blame them? I want it more. I train harder. I'm faster. I'm stronger. And I have an iron will. Nobody has destroyed me. Nobody. I might have tapped, I might have been beaten to a bloody pulp, but I will always come back for more. I was an amateur wrestler since I was six years old. I've watched Mike Randalls. I've faced Mike Randalls. I've faced the Angelo Deville's, the Karina Wolfenden's, the Jeff Garvin's...and they all know one thing. They know that it is a game of inches and timing. Like a football game, inches and timing is all the difference. Unlike others, I never have an off night. I don't come out flat. I don't let you dictate the pace. And I damn sure do not let you go without punishing you for getting booked against me. I'm getting excited just thinking about it Gibson. Unlike so many, I love this business. I sleep and eat it. I will give my soul and my life to the squared circle before anything else because I believe in this art. I believe in what we do in there gives people a mirror into our souls. Those people see that I am like a rabid animal. And even if it does not pay off...even if someone uses it against me...I do not relent. I do not stop. I fight and fight until my heart stops beating. People need hatred, or something to fight for. I don't. I'm dangerous no matter what. If hatred or a belt is up for grabs, it doesn't matter. I go in there and fight no matter the stakes. No matter the costs. A while back, someone I overlooked damn near stole the two greatest things in my life from me. Never again will I overlook a man or woman, no matter who they are or what they have done. Thank Angelo Deville. Thank Nova. Thank Karina Wolfenden. Thank Peter Vetra. They are the reasons why you are going to tap like a little bitch at Madison Square Garden. *** He stood there, looking into the mirror. The white athletic tape was in his right hand, his left arm pushed out. He took to firmly taping his left wrist with white strip before yanking the roll, tearing it off. He grabbed his left wrist, rubbing the taping to make sure it would stay firm. He was already in his singlet, his shaved head with a thin sheen of perspiration glowing from the reflection of the bright lighting. He had already done his usual jaunt in the backstage area, making sure to have his body loosened up and ready. He grabbed at the water bottle, popping the cap off with his thumb and began sucking down the liquid refreshment. He needed to make sure his fluids were up, especially before a match. He did not want to risk running out of energy at a key moment, especially not tonight. It was Madison Square Garden, and if anything, this was a wrestling town. The audience was smart. They respected the guys who could really go and hated the ones who could not. Brandon grunted as he looked at Melissa. 'Just remember, check him. If he even looks to be saggin', you relay that to me and I can go in for the kill. I want to test his wits and his stamina.' Melissa shrugged. 'Make sure you reserve something for Nova. He's going to be coming for us.' Brandon smirked at that. Nova was a war he had waited for. He wanted to see how the man would respond. He wanted to see if he could avenge himself after the brutality and the psychological games he played with him. 'I'm dedicating this one to him. And he can get a sneak peak at Colossus, when his hand hits that mat and he taps out to get me official possession of this.' He rose it high. It was the 5-Star Championship, all polished. Brandon gave the belt a smack on the face plate, feeling the name plate with the name 'Nova' etched into it. Nova had held this thing for a very long time, and was the only man to ever win it twice. And in just over a week, he would have another record. The only man to lose the 5-Star Championship twice. Being a big time wrestler wasn't something he was physically ready for almost a year. His endurance was not high enough. He was too green. But since then, he had became one of the most feared wrestler in the world not only because of his talent but because of his stamina. He could go for nearly an hour every single night. He could take the unholy ass beating and still survive. He had won a World Championship because of that. Where as before, he would be nervous, now he was simply mind numbingly bored. Youngblood simply sat back in his singlet and boots with a water bottle in his hands. He sat back and relaxed. 'You know...this building ain't so big and bad.' Melissa looked at him puzzled. 'Brandon...you've done a lot...but this is still the Garden. This is still where legends are born and excel.' Brandon just smirked. 'I've never lost in this building. Home field advantage, hon. It's the best thing in the world. Gibson...I'm ready. I don't need to be hyper. I don't need to yell and scream. I'll save my energy and put it to him in the ring. It's a waste to do that here...you know?' She nodded her head. 'Man...a year ago you were fidgeting.' Brandon simply laughed. 'That was eons ago. Yesterday doesn't matter until the career is over. Then we can gloss over it. This is my stage. This is where I shine.' Brandon then went and threw on his lucky pair of baggy blue tear aways and simply walked away, his boots echoing off the holy halls of Madison Square Garden. Time to put the last of effort into Nova. But no mistake about it. He was ready for Gibson.
