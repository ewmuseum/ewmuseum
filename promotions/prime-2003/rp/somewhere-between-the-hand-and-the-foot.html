Somewhere Between The Hand and The Foot

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReV 139 S.A.T. Pictures and PRIME Entertainment Present A Wylde/Rayne Production of an O’Mac Original "Somewhere Between The Hand and The Foot" Starring Tyler Rayne as The Unlikely Hero Pierce Lavelle as The Good Friend Angelica Brooks as The Damsel and Dawn Stanton as Uh…Pierce’s Girlfriend with special appearances by Lindsay Troy Wade Elliott Angus the Dog and A Shitload of Mother Fuckin’ Ninjas ---- "…so Dawn pulls out her gun and almost gives the little old lady a heart attack!" Pierce Lavelle can barely finish the sentence without laughing. He’s laughing so hard his head falls toward the table, dangerously close to mopping the remainder of his Italian dish with his hair. Tyler Rayne responds in kind, slamming a half-full mug of beer to the table, rattling used silverware and shaking the third pitcher of beer for the evening. It’s all he can do to keep from spitting the alcohol in his mouth all over Pierce’s new blazer. Tyler struggles to swallow, the two men sharing a healthy, and perhaps overly enthusiastic, laugh at the outcome of the story. Sitting to the right of each man, the four tucked tightly into the booth of some random pub in Baltimore, are two women slightly less than amused by the story. One, the young and beautiful Angelica Brooks, offers a cordial smile, though she’s obviously completely unamused by the tale. To the right of Lavelle is Dawn, the unfortunate star of the comedic tale, who rolls her eyes at the two men, though she is unable to keep the good-natured smile from creeping across her lovely features. Tyler raises a finger to her, attempting to collect himself enough to speak. "That’s…that’s one hell of an introduction. You treat all your landlords like that, or just the elderly ones?" It’s not even that funny, but both he and Pierce are thrown into another fit of laughter at the comment. A few of the pub’s other patrons are beginning to turn their eyes toward the quartet. Angelica seems to be the only one who notices. Or cares. "Very funny, boys. Just wait till we get outside, and I bust both your asses for public intox." Dawn gives Pierce a loving nudge on the shoulder. "C’mon. Move. I gotta go to the ladies’." "Oh. I’ll go with." The three of them turn to Angelica, who for a moment seems embarrassed to have spoken up at all. Dawn just shrugs and motions her to follow along, leaving the two men to retake their seats at the booth and attempt to regain some sort of control over themselves. "That’s fuckin’ hilarious." "You’re telling me! Try being there to see the look on that old lady’s face!" The hilarity finally begins to simmer, Pierce reaching into his pocket to pull his wallet. Tyler puts a hand to his arm to stop him, the other digging in his own pocket for a couple bills. "I got it." "Tyler, I can—" "I know. I’m just tryin’ to be a nice guy. ‘Sides, you got the girl and the house and I’m just a dude with a Batcave." Pierce still doesn’t seem convinced. "I’ll let you buy us ice cream on the way home." He laughs, but places the wallet back in his pocket. With a satisfied nod Tyler throws a hundred dollar bill down on top of the check and slides it to the edge of the table. Pierce looks to the far corner of the pub, ensuring the girls are still powdering their noses, before he turns the question on Tyler. "So…did you tell her yet?" Tyler sighs. Pierce had concocted this whole double-date idea as a way to give Tyler a chance to explain how he felt to Angelica. He meant well, and Tyler knew it, but it just wasn’t as simple as blowing the girl off. This wasn’t just some clingy one night stand or some randomly obsessed fan. He liked her. Honestly. He just didn’t like her quite as much as she wanted him to. "No. I…I don’t know…I don’t wanna hurt her." "I think it’s a little late for that, friend." Another sigh. "Yeah." "Just tell her the truth. She’s tougher than you give her credit for." Tyler nods, though he’s not entirely convinced. He hears Angelica’s youthful giggle from across the pub, and turns to see the two women exiting the restroom. "Let’s roll." He snatches her purse from the end of the booth and slides out, marching over to meet the two women at the exit. Pierce takes the cue, grabbing Dawn’s purse as well and quickly following behind. Tyler hands off the purse, then makes a quick stop at the bar to chat with the bartender. Pierce just rolls his eyes, taking Dawn by the hand and leading her out of the pub. Angelica waits patiently in her white dress, the black dots matching the large black belt that cinches up around her abdomen instead of her waist. One of those odd fashions Tyler would never understand. Fortunately, every attractive woman wearing a dress one size too small happened to look damn fantastic, regardless of the ridiculous size of her belt or sunglasses. He finishes his goodbye to the bartender and hurriedly takes Angelica out to catch up with the others. "So how do you know him again?" "Clint? We, uh…" He pauses just outside the door. Even through the generic music pumping through the speakers outside the pub, he thought he’d…nah. Probably nothing. "Sorry. He’s a friend of a friend. Guy named Sean." "Oh. So who’s this Sean guy then?" "Just an old friend. Died a few years back." She doesn’t respond. It seems like every time she tries to learn something new about him, an answer like that comes up. She’s surprised he still has any friends left alive at all. He shrugs and offers a smile, his way of ensuring her it’s no big deal. It’s almost sad how comfortable he is talking about the deaths of his friends and family. Tyler pulls a pack of Camels and his lighter from the pocket of his jeans, eyes turning to the rooftops of the buildings across the street as he lights his cigarette. "What is it?" "Nothin’. I thought…I thought I saw somethin’. Prolly just a cat or somethin’. Don’t worry about it." He nods down the block, where Dawn and Pierce have stopped to do a little window shopping at an antique store, and begins to lead Angelica down the sidewalk toward them. "So…I guess you finally got that date with Troy, huh?" "It’s not a date. Just a drink." "Right. But I mean…um…are you excited? About going." Yes. "What’s there to be excited about? It’s a drink. Doesn’t mean anything." "So no?" Yes. "No." "Good." He wisely chooses to ignore the comment. Besides, they’ve caught up to the cute little couple down the block, which means it’s time for the quartet to walk the half a dozen blocks to the parking garage. Sure, they could’ve parked a little closer to the pub, but it’s a beautiful evening, and Tyler had insisted that they get out and enjoy it. "What took you so long?" "Just had to say bye." "The bartender. How do you know him again?" "Don’t ask." "Uh…okay." "So, Tyler, Pierce tells me you’ve been getting some pretty big wins lately." "Eh, when you’re losing every match you’re in, any win is a big win. But yeah, I feel like I’m finally getting my stride back." Tyler stops in mid-stride, once again passing his eyes over the rooftops of the nearby shops. He narrows his eyes, peering into the shadows of the alley across the way. It is only now that he’s noticed they’re the only four on the street. "Uh, have you guys noticed anyone else out walking since we left the pub?" "Yeah, I…um…actually…" "No. I haven’t." Dawn’s answer is much more matter of fact than Pierce’s. Tyler notices her hand instinctively pressing against the side of her jacket, ensuring that the service pistol is still strapped exactly where it should be. All four of them are searching the unnaturally quiet streets for signs of anything out of the ordinary. "I’ve got a bad feeling about this." C’mon. Someone had to say it. "Shit." Depending on where one gets their information, there are various rumors involving Tyler Rayne and his ability to survive, some much more…unbelievable than others. Like, for instance, the belief that he actually has superhuman senses. Acute senses of sight and hearing far beyond that of the average human. This is, of course, entirely untrue. He’s simply trained himself to pick up on certain things that others are like to miss. Like the sound of a shuriken splitting the air. Though, on this evening, he hears it almost too late. His reaction time is a bit slower than usual. Three pitchers of beer will do that to a man. His fingers move at the last second, catching the shuriken between his fore and middle fingers just before a point pierces Angelica’s left eye. She mumbles something under her breath, far too stunned for her words to make any real sense. The weapon came from the direction of the pub. He sees nothing but darkness. Somehow the four of them had failed to notice that all of the streetlights behind them had gone out. "Um…fuck. Car. Now." He doesn’t turn to face them. He just begins to back peddle slowly, eyes still searching the darkness for sign of their attacker. "Uh, Ty…we, um…uh…" They may not have known each other long, but Tyler knows that it’s never a good sign when Pierce is at a loss for words. He turns to his friend, eyes falling upon the same unsettling sight that has frozen both Pierce and Dawn in place. Seven men have appeared, seemingly from nowhere, in the middle of the street. All seven men are covered from head to toe in the black cloth stereotypically reserved for the ninja. Yes, that’s right. Ninjas. Pierce turns a questioning eye to Rayne. "Pub?" "Pub." The four turn, only to find twice as many black clad assassins waiting behind them. "Shit." "What now?" Fifteen, maybe twenty ninjas stand between them and the pub. Three blocks of complete darkness and a contingent of assassins blocking their retreat. Three blocks in the other direction will get them to the parking garage. Only the nearest block has been blacked out. Seven…make that ten ninjas blocking that route. Both contingents begin inching closer. The four of them form a circle, back-to-back, watching as their silent assailants close in. Dawn trains her service pistol on the nearest ninja. "I am Detective Dawn Stanton of the New York Police Department. Stop where you are and lower your weapons to the ground." The ninjas don’t even pause. "I don’t think they’re listening, sweetheart." "Guess that means we’re fighting." "No. We need to do this by the book." "Hate to break it to ya, kid, but it don’t look like they’re up on the reading. Anybody ever fought a ninja before?" "Can’t say I’ve had the pleasure." "Never worked Chinatown." "I’ll ignore that comment for now." "I have." The three oldest turn to look at Angelica, shocked looks on all their faces. "Not the time for jokes, I guess." Tyler laughs, and even Pierce manages a smile, in spite of the situation. The ninja are closing in on both sides. Suddenly Tyler stops laughing, turning on a dime and extending his arm over Lavelle’s shoulder, a flick of the wrist sending that shuriken on a collision course with the lead ninja’s shoulder. The ninja stutters. Dawn fires off three shots at the next in line. One in each shoulder, one just below the knee. Before she can fire a fourth, a shuriken lodges into the barrel of her gun. Cursing, she tosses the weapon aside and rushes to attack behind Rayne. The odds of beating ten are better than that of fifteen. Dawn’s taken out one by the time Tyler makes contact with the first. He pulls the shuriken from the ninja’s shoulder, ducking a katana slash before striking back, jabbing a point of the shuriken into the ninja’s eye. From there a back sweep puts the ninja down, where Tyler uses the ninja’s own katana to slice at the biceps and quads. Pierce has now joined the fray, fighting side-by-side with his girlfriend as the two duck and weave between the slashes and jabs of katanas. Things are going about as well as one could hope. Except for the fact that Angelica hasn’t moved. Tyler tosses the katana, catching an unsuspecting ninja through the shoulder before he can land a blow on Brooks. The splash of blood against her face seems to have awakened her from the shock of the situation, and she finishes the ninja off with a shot of mace. Tyler moves to her side, taking the ninja’s katana as well as the one from its shoulder. Now with a weapon in each hand, he turns to defensive tactics, parrying the attacks of the handful of ninjas that have broken ranks from the darkness. He notices that ten of them wait patiently in the road, blocking any retreat back to the pub. They’re being corralled. He didn’t have time to worry about why. When he could spare the second, Tyler put an elbow into Angelica’s rib, nudging her toward the growing stack of bodies around Pierce and Dawn. The four meet up once again, Dawn, with sword in hand, taking a stance beside Tyler as the ninjas drive them back. "You know how to use that thing?" "They offered a few classes at the academy." The two of them, with three katanas, are able to fend off the attacks of the ninjas. But a defensive strategy will gain them no ground in this battle. It will only serve to wear them out. Unable to turn his attention away from their attackers, Tyler must rely on Pierce and Brooks to watch the road behind him. "What’ve we got, kid?" "There’s a Chinese place down the road! The lights are on!" "Shiny." He strikes without warning, turning the katanas downward so that he may stab the nearest ninja through both shoulders. He pulls the blades out immediately, turning them back to a more effective fighting grip before diving straight in. Tyler slashes and parries, spinning around bodies and ducking under the swipes of enemy blades. He drops to a knee in the middle of the group, spinning the two swords about his body, lashing across thigh muscles and slicing open knees and calves. "GO!! NOW!" The three of them run for the welcoming neon lights of a Chinese restaurant. The surprise offensive has given Rayne the advantage he needed to defeat this first wave of ninjas. But as anyone who’s dealt with ninjas should know, there are always more on the way. He jogs down the road to catch up with his friends, who have already entered the restaurant. Tyler slows as he approaches, noting the raggedy looking dog resting in the bed of a big ass pick-up truck. Something familiar about… "Hello, nurse." Call it fortuitous, or kismet, or a simple matter of coincidence, but however one chooses to refer to the circumstance, there is no denying the oddity of her presence here. Through the large windows of the restaurant he can see her, obviously surprised by the unexpected appearance of both Pierce Lavelle and Angelica Brooks. Lindsay Troy. PRIME’s very own Universal Champion. And damn does she look good. Pain races up Tyler Rayne’s back, the familiar searing flash of a blade splitting his flesh reminding him of the more immediate problem at hand. Ninjas. He stumbles forward, turning to face his attackers though he’s dropped both weapons, only to receive two simultaneous kicks to the sternum. Then he’s in the air and… CRASH!! Glass sprinkles across the linoleum floor of the Chinese restaurant, Tyler’s body barreling through chairs and tables before coming to a painful halt at the feet of Lindsay Troy. She’s wearing a very nice pair of pumps. He’s trying very hard not to look up her skirt. Pierce grabs a chair from the dining area and launches himself at the ninjas coming in through the window. Dawn snatches a broken chair leg from the floor, spinning it in her hand as if it were a baton. Not quite what she’s used to, but it’ll do in a pinch. She jumps in after her lover. Angelica is hiding behind an overturned table, the Koreans who own the restaurant throwing kitchen knives and tongs at the ninjas pushing through the hole. Then there’s Tyler, still lying in a pile of broken glass, staring up at the perplexed expression of Lindsay Troy. He flashes her that infamous grin. "So you, uh, you come here often?" "I didn’t think you’d be at my feet this soon, Rayne. Must be a record for you." "Eh, you seemed like the domineering type. Just figured I'd save us the bullshit hassle and skip right to the kinky part. Nice heels." Troy's crimson lips curl slowly upward into a smirk, but her smoldering look doesn't hold for long as another CRASH! is heard. Lindsay instinctively throws her arms over her head and ducks as more glass comes flying her way. "What the shit is all this?!" "This…this is a ninja fight." He slides forward, a sort of front-seated dropkick that takes a ninja out at the knees. The ninja falls as Tyler bends his own knees, jacking the ninja’s jaw before it hits the ground. He gives it an unpleasant kick to the head before turning his attention back to Troy. "What, you’ve never been in a ninja fight?" Lindsay looks at him, dumbfounded. Speechless. Two things that everyone with a pulse knows rarely happen to her. "Two minutes with me and you’re already speechless? Didn’t realize I was so good. So that, what, must be a record for you or somethin’, right?" Another break to bust on some poor ninja fool. "Oh, real fucking cute, Rayne. Never a dull moment with you." "Yeah, just imagine what our date’s going to be like." "It's not a date..." A flash of silver slices through the air, causing Lindsay to cut her sentence short, stumble backwards and almost trip over her own two feet. A katana blade lodges itself into the wood of the bar. Troy immediately grabs a half-empty beer bottle and smashes it over the attacker's head, watching him slump to the ground as consciousness leaves him. "...It's a drink." "Same diff. So what brings a girl like you to a place like this anyway?" "Drifter wanted Chinese food. And since Danny and I are in a snit, my PRIME buddy list is seriously depleted." "Yeah, what’s up with you two anyway? Seemed like you were such good—" The thing about ninjas is their innate ability to interrupt a good conversation. Years of professional wrestling will help sharpen a person’s senses more than they realize. Even in the midst of the chaos, Lindsay sees the shurikens coming, giving her enough time to roll backwards and out of harms way. Relatively speaking, since she is now hiding behind the same table as Angelica Brooks. The door to the restroom opens, and Tyler turns to stare up at the contorted face of Wade Elliott. He’s a bit confused, and well, who can blame him? It’s not every day you step out of a public restroom and into a scene from a Bruce Lee movie. "Rayne? What the hell is goin’ on in here?" Tyler shrugs. "Ninja fight?" "Ninjas? You mean them fellers in the black pajamas?" "Yeah." Wade snorts. "Where’s—" Tyler nods his head in Lindsay’s direction. Both men turn in time to see two shurikens slam into the counter, right between the heads of Angelica and Lindsay. Angelica, eyes wide with fear and shock, turns to her Universal Champion for guidance, completely forgetting the fact that she hates her bitchy, smarmy, holier-than-thou guts. "Oh God, oh God, I've got blood on me and my dress is ruined and my hair's gonna go flat and my face is all shiny and...and...this is madness!" Lindsay laughs. "Awww come on now, Angie. Don't get your g-string in a twist. Madness? This isn't madness." A shuriken slices the air between Lindsay’s legs, splitting a slit in her business-like skirt. Another takes her much closer to the thigh, actually pinning the fabric of her skirt to the counter. Lindsay Troy isn’t laughing any more. In fact...Lindsay Troy is fucking pissed. "THIS IS PRADA!!!" She stands, unable to keep from ripping the skirt even further. The fabric unravels, leaving her in something shorter than even Angelica would dare to attempt. But the Uni Champ hardly cares. "YOU FUCKING FUCK!" She kicks a table, sending it sliding across the linoleum and into the gut of a ninja. The ninja stumbles backward, allowing Lindsay the time she needs to roll over the table and plant a firm high-heeled foot into his sternum. The ninja staggers backward, back slamming into a support beam between the two broken windows. She boots him in the balls, grabs him by either side of the mask and drives his face downward, smashing his nose into her rising knee and effectively removing the ninja from the fight. Only like a hundred more to go. "Damn." Tyler nods in agreement with the succinct comment from Wade. "Yeah. I could so fall in love with that girl." With that definitive entrance, Lindsay Troy has officially thrown herself into the fray. Black-clad bodies are everywhere. Some are jumping. Some are falling. Some, like the ones who get too close to Wade Elliott, are thrown. Regardless of how or why, the bodies are everywhere. Dawn, Pierce, and Lindsay are doing a well-enough job of keeping the ninjas from flooding through the windows, though there is simply no end to the number of them that keep coming. The door, however, is another matter. While it is a small opening, it is an unguarded opening all the same, and the ninjas keep pushing themselves through. Rayne and Elliott are doing a fair job of laying them out once they get in, but a better alternative would be to keep them out altogether. "Wade!" Tyler grabs the attention of the drifter, motioning toward a soda cooler in the corner. Wade nods and moves along with Tyler, the two of them dragging it away from the wall and then pushing it toward the door. Well, Wade does most of the pushing. Pierce sees their plan and moves to try and limit the amount of bodies pouring through that doorway. It’s not easy, but after a bit of a struggle, they block the door. The ding of a bell turns Tyler’s attention back to the counter, where the head chef has placed a large brown bag. "Hey, Uni, I think your dinner’s ready." Lindsay turns to the counter, amazed at the fact that the Koreans have been preparing her food all this time. Pierce can’t quite believe it, either. "Now that’s service!" "No shit, kid. I’ll have to remember to come back here sometime." "If there’s a here left to come back to." "Point." There are no more ninjas coming through. The few left standing either scurry back out the window or fall at the hands of their would-be victims. Everyone takes a moment to catch their breath. "Is that it?" Tyler shakes his head, snatching a pair of nunchaku from the floor. He pulls up a chair to sit in, the only chair not broken in the whole of the dining room, and lights a cigarette. Noting Wade’s own futile search through his pockets, Tyler tosses the pack to the drifter. "Thanks." Tyler shrugs. "Get ready." "There’s more?" Another shrug. "There’s always more." With cigarette in hand, Wade Elliott steps to the center of the wreckage, staring straight out into the night. He can see the glint of light off their weapons as the newest wave of ninjas approach. His smile widens. The Drifter is always itchin’ for a fight. His booming voice rings loud over the quiet march of ninja feet. "THIS IS WHERE WE FIGHT!!" The ninjas quicken the pace. They’re coming through. "Wade!" Lindsay Troy’s cry from the back of the room brings everyone’s attention. She tosses the bag of food at her traveling companion before a ninja can slice through it. They’re coming in through the kitchen, too. "Shit." Tyler Rayne rolls out of his chair, leaping over the counter with a kick to the throat. Meanwhile, the ninjas have begun anew at the broken windows, Dawn and Pierce throw themselves headlong into the throng. Wade catches the take-out bag with one hand, while smacking the shit out of some unfortunate ninja with the other. "THIS IS WHERE THEY…ATE MY APPLE PIE!?" He’s paused just long enough to peruse through the bag’s contents. And, alas, there is no apple pie. "WHICH ONE O’ YOU SONS A BITCHES ATE MY APPLE PIE!!" After that, it’s kind of like Arnold Schwarzenegger swatting flies. In the kitchen, Lindsay and Tyler are fighting side-by-side, doing their best to keep the ninjas from progressing into the dining room. "You gonna tell ‘im they don’t serve apple pie here?" She looks to the dining room, where Wade has a ninja in the military press, just before he tosses him through the window and into about five of his friends. "I would, but he’s on such a roll." "Point." And the fighting continues. More ninjas are pouring in through the backdoor, and though the kitchen is narrow, limiting their numbers advantage, Tyler and Troy can both see that they’ll soon be overwhelmed. The former falls into a defensive position, using the nunchaku to block attacks while Lindsay backs out of the kitchen first. She hops over the counter and down to the floor, now face-to-point with the shurikens that ripped her skirt. She pulls them from the wall, amassing a fair collection in her hand, then pops back over the counter for a surprise offensive. Three shurikens fly from her hands at the same time, each catching a different ninja somewhere in the chest. All three go down. Tyler turns, unable to hide the shock from his face. This girl just got hotter by the minute. With Lindsay providing cover, Tyler made a hasty escape from the kitchen, stopping at the counter to pull two daggers from the wall. He can’t keep his eyes off Troy for more than a second. She smiles. "Impressed?" "It is very Bullseye of you." "Huh?" "Bullseye. Bad guy from Daredevil. Um…Colin Farrell in the movie." "Never watched it. Fuck Colin Farrell. I was actually going for Gambit." "You need playing cards for that." She shrugs. "Yeah, well, they’re fresh out." She leaps from the counter to the dining room, throwing the last of her shuriken into the kitchen. Each one hits the intended target, which is, still, pretty fucking impressive. Tyler jumps to his feet, running at Wade with a dagger in each hand. "Yo, drifter! FASTBALL SPECIAL!!" Wade turns a questioning glance at Rayne. "Huh?" "Just throw me!" Tyler leaps into the air without waiting for a response. Leaps right at Wade, and without much other choice, Wade catches him and puts adds his own impressive strength to Rayne’s momentum, tossing the smaller man right out the window and into a group of ninjas. Again he leads with two blades to the shoulder. Tyler lashes out with the daggers, stabbing at the joints shoulders and thighs of any ninja unfortunate enough to be nearby. But there are too many. Way too many. One of them, the only ninja with red trim around his uniform, puts a solid boot to Rayne’s chest and knocks him back into his compatriots. Everyone has stepped out of the restaurant and back outside, but that’s where they’ve stopped. Pierce helps Tyler to his feet, where Rayne can get a better look of the army that stands before them. There must be a hundred of them. Maybe more. "Well fuck me." "Us." "Well fuck us." The ninja with the red trim steps forward, placing himself in the front of the pack. His eyes narrow on Tyler. The ninja looks only at him. The ninja speaks only to him. "The son of Nine sends his regards. This is only the beginning, Rayne. More to come. Much, much more." "Who the fuck is—" "More ninjas? Cause I don’t think I can do more—" Without word or warning, all the ninjas raise their arms in a single, cohesive movement. Every single ninja throws a smoke bomb as a single, cohesive unit. That’s a lot of fucking smoke. When it’s cleared, and the dust has settled, there are only six people standing in the middle of a deserted street. Six beaten and exhausted people listening to the wail of sirens draw closer. "Now the cops come." Wade begins to shift a little where he’s standing. By some miracle, he’s still holding the take-out bag. "There a problem, drifter?" "Me ‘n the pigs don’t exactly see eye ta eye. Usually best I just avoid ‘em." Despite her exhaustion, Dawn steps up to take control of the situation. "No one’s leaving. This is a crime scene, and every single one of us is a witness to—" Or so she thinks, until Rayne steps in. "Sorry, kid, but I’m cuttin’ ‘em loose. You’re just gonna have to deal with it." "You’re cutting them loose? On who’s authority?" He flips open his wallet, revealing an identification card that certifies him as an agent of the Japanese government. "This is an international matter, kid. What with the ninjas and all. Which makes it a Fed case. Sorry to say that makes police authority worth jack. It’s not personal, kid. Really. But I’m not about to let Wade here get busted because of some bullshit I brought down on his head." He’s right, of course. For as much as Dawn might hate that. She storms away, no doubt to go and tell Pierce all about what a wonderful friend he has. Tyler turns back to Wade. "Go ahead, drifter. Roll out." Wade nods. "Guessin’ I owe ya one, then." "Nah. You helped me out here. This just makes us square." He nods again, mulling the situation over in his mind. Sure was a damn good tussle. With a smile and a final clap on the back, Wade Elliott heads back to the truck, where he’s surprised to find his ol’ dog Angus chewing on the leg of an unconscious ninja. Okay, well maybe it’s not that surprising. He looks at Troy, who's trying her best to tie her Prada suit jacket around her thighs and restore some form of clothing decency to her person. She gives Rayne a wary look, then rights herself. "You know Lisa and Blaine are going to find out about this, right?" "Oh yeah. Can’t wait for that meeting. After all, Lisa likes me so much already." "I'm pretty much number one on her Christmas card list." She looks over to Wade, who's kicking the ninja away from the truck and trying to get Angus back in it. "I should go. Drifter's my ride and I'm probably going to have more shit to deal with than I bargained for because of this." "You have no idea. Look, I’m…um, sorry. I got…I mean…it’s…this kinda thing happens to me more than you’d think. Most times I try not to get people involved but…if you want to reconsider that drink, I’d…I mean it’s not a big deal…ninjas and everything. I’d totally understand." "You're stuttering, Rayne." A quick glance over to Pierce, Dawn and Angelica and Troy drops her voice low. "And Angie looks pleased as punch after that statement. Might make you hold up your end of the deal just to irritate her." "Wow. A drink for spite. After getting my ass kicked by a shitload of fuckin’ ninjas, that’s exactly what I wanted to hear." He looks to see Wade climbing into the truck. "You should, uh…you should probably go." "Yeah." Lindsay gives the others a wave then turns to the truck. As she walks, she throws a comment over her shoulder. "Oh and Rayne?" He’s already looking at her. Of course he was watching her leave. "Uni." She doesn't even turn around. "Don't be sorry. There's not much excitement in my life outside of the work place. I might be buying you a drink after all." He sighs as he watches her go. Not exactly the best of impressions. Pierce steps up beside him, and they both watch the big pick-up pull away from the sidewalk and drive away. Wade makes sure to honk as he passes. "Hell of a girl." "Yeah. Dawn pissed?" "You could say that." "Sorry." … "Well this was a pretty fucked up evening." "Yeah." "So you’ll probably like, never get to go out with me again, huh?" "I’m not even sure I want to." Tyler laughs.
