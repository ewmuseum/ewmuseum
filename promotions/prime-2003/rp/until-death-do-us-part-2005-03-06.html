Until Death Do Us Part

6 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/6/05 Until Death Do Us PartChapter IIMarch 4th, 2005 My arms felt like wings. Wings that could fly me to a land undiscovered. Spread-eagle beneath the damp patch of grass that lay before his wonderful home, Ozric looked towards the majestic skies. A midnight run-in with his 'friend' and a quick escape to the outside, where he felt as if the land beneath him was slowly morphing into quicksand. He could envision himself being lowered into the ground. Chronically blinking his eyes to protect the dirt from collapsing onto his face; along with the small stones and compact mounds of hard-soil that followed. He could hear extravagant voices singing Broadway tunes at the top of their lungs. It felt slightly refreshing to him and sadly, he couldn't figure out why. The ground is moving with the decibal of song. Internally, I feel numb. Externally, I feel reborn. It became evident that Ozric was slowly becoming sick. The drugs became an exit-wound for all of his problems. The mental effect took him down with the current of the ocean. Yet, the drugs seemed to give him enough hope in life where he wouldn't have to end up killing himself, which slowly drifted past his mind-set as being an option. For Ozric didn't want to end his life. He was happy with what he was doing now. Happiness came at rare occasions for this twisted clown and when it was at his finger tips, he never seemed to let it go. Until the inevitable gravity of eyelids grew like a fungus; sending him through a wild series of dreamscapes. The victories. They were beautiful. They really seemed to have a positive effect on his motivation. He wasn't sure how to take these past two weeks. At war inside a cage, pouring out every ounce of energy onto the field of twenty-nine other wrestlers who wanted the same exact feeling of victory that Ozric wanted. It became obvious that Ozric wasn't spiratually drawn to it, as he was eliminated after a long run in the action. It felt good when he returned to his hotel that night. Resting his bones on his bed while watching Paula Zahn talk politics. He wasn't sure about what kind of politics she was refering to but, for some strange reason, her voice kept him safe. No matter the aches, worries and strains of the hours beforehand, Ozric felt at touch with God as he lay on the bed with his eyes halfway to a misty land of unconscienceness. Dark Age. The only miserable moment that seems to corrupt the twinkle in my eye. Dark Age, the only poison that seemingly eats away at me. I can't control it, for I am alone in a forest that is slowly becoming infested with familes of wolves that to dislike me. In the sky, he could see the constellations gleaming back at him. Stars slowly etched out a chalkboard with 'Ozric Mortimer' written in the middle of it. A whip of a ruler startled Ozric who continued to stare at the chalkboard. The grass now felt like a sensational pool of warm water and it was getting more and more difficult to control his bodily movements that awkwardly forced him to jerk his body to the correct position every couple of minutes. The light sounds of traffic in the background and the soothing simpatico of crickets churping through the night seemed to become muffled by the emphatic intonations of rhymes and giggles. Strikingly curious, Ozric sat up and swallowed another 'tweak'. He lay back down and continued to watch the indiosyncratic presentation before him. A young lackey of a group of wolves seems to find himself becoming more and more powerful. Smoking out a harmless soul who's been criticized by his master. Determined to crush him. Determined to beat him. 'Prepare for a series of uncomfortable miracles, Ozric.' a middle-aged, white bearded teacher said as his shaggy hand gripped Ozric's right shoulder. 'You're going to need them if you want to pass this grade.' stated the teacher in a bold voice as Ozric continued to write the same sentence; row after row. It became evident that he was in some form of detention and through the magical bliss-splitting smile across Ozric's face, he couldn't care any less about his situation. 'I'll never be mean to my peers again, I'll never be mean to my peers again, I'll never be mean to my peers again'. What a load of hogwash! Ozric Mortimer wasn't the 'social' figure of his class; spending a usual day covering his face in a barricade of folded arms. He didn't want any part in the lessons his teacher was giving, nor did he want to associate with kids who frequently tortured him at recess-periods and after school. The will power wasn't there. He couldn't walk up to his teacher and snitch out kids that were just going through a time in their life where they follow in the shadows of those that interest them. Some of the bigger kids were doing the exact thing the younger kids started doing just this past year, but for generations. It wasn't hard for them to find the weakest link out of the class's twenty-seven students. There was also a young boy who traveled through the forest where the wolves lurked. Undismayed, he walked through this forest and just when he was reaching the sunlight of open ground, he heard the snarl of a twisted creature. This young boy turned around and snarled right back... 'That was incredibly rude of you, Ozric. There was absolutely no need to throw a wooden building-block at Michael's head.' the teacher said as he sat back down at his desk and started to correct papers. Oz glanced at the clock and realized that he had only been there for fifteen minutes. Time did, in fact, fly by when you were having fun. This was a true example of what time did when you weren't. Michael was one of the six kids that gave Ozric a hard time on a daily basis. He would suck up to Mr. Wylder, their teacher, which would give him brownie points. The teacher thought of Michael as an angel and nothing else. The thought made Ozric cringe as he slammed down the piece of chalk and pointed at the teacher who stopped grading. 'How would you know if it was rude?! How would you know?! What could possibly make me do something like that, Mr. Wylder? Maybe, JUST maybe, Michael did something?' The teacher laughed and shook his head. Standing up and walking over to Ozric, he crouched down. 'Michael's a straight-A student, Ozric. You're lucky to get C's and you skip school like it's an addiction. He's got promise and you obviously don't. Therefore, I know Michael wouldn't pull a stunt like that. He's too intelligent for me to just presume that he would pull such an ignorantly intolerable antic. Now, keep writing.' Quickly, Ozric threw the chalk across the room and stared at the teacher. For a string of a few minutes, he felt incredibly empty; as if part of him had died. Through the cloudy mist of decay that wreaked around him, he felt as if he could do anything. Walking up to the teacher, Ozric pulled a gun out from his back pocket and pointed it at the teacher. Smacking his head with the other hand, Ozric started to sob uncontrollably. He turned his head and noticed that the door was shut, along with the window blinds. 'Get on your knees, My Wylder.' Ozric boldly stated as he pointed toward the floor. Alas, the teacher wouldn't budge, shaking his head and shooting a look of grimace his way. Ozric was in no mood for desperate looks of last-minute disapointment. 'I SAID GET ON THE FLOOR! Don't make me blow your head off, My Wylder!' screamed Ozric as the teacher finally complied; nodding his head and getting down to his knees; hands up and his eyes like lasers being shot through those of Ozric. Shortly after the boy and the wolf snarled, a fight developed. Screaming and shouting, the boy lept on top of the wolf and started to roll around in the dirt. The wolf, continuously biting and scratching, seemed to have the upper hand until Ozric grabbed the wolf's head and held it's clamp-like mouth open, as if training an alligator to obey. Night was falling and the aroma of death slowly became evident... The scene suddenly altered to that of a hospital. Lying on a stretcher and being strolled through a long hallway that was painted in checkered black and white was Ozric Mortimer. He could see three or four doctors above him, looking down at Ozric and trying to talk to him. Oddly so, Ozric was deaf, as he couldn't hear them or anything else for that matter. Slamming into revolving doors, the stretcher reached one of the emergency rooms and the doctors promptly lifted the dazed Mortimer from the stretcher to the bed. 'What state was he in when the psychiatrist found him?' asked one of the doctors who grabbed the clamps of The FR2 AED defibraillator. A nurse jumped into the room and answered the question as well as she could. 'Violently shaking and foaming at the mouth on the front yard of his house. Didn't respond to the male who found him and seemed incredibly dilusional.' the nurse answered as she left the room and went back to the waiting room, where Dr. Klingman was sitting. The doctor prepared the clamps by rubbing them smoothly together before placing them against Ozric's chest. 'CLEAR!' At this point, you'd think the wolf would've gotten the better of this young boy, who was fighting for his life. Through sticks and stones and punches thrown, the boy was still hanging by a thread. To take the opposition down by the limbs, it was time to be the smarter, more intelligent gladiator in this forest! For a wolf will always have a family to rely on, while this young boy has nothing but his own knuckles and his own bravery to fall back on. 'Ozric, you don't want to do this. Where'd you get that gun?' questioned the teacher as he remained on his knees; acting like a negotiator in a hostage situation where, in fact, he's the hostage as well. Ozric turned each question down without an answer and started to trash the classroom; knocking down desks and ripping up papers. 'Don't ask me stupid questions, Wylder. After all, you're a teacher. Grade my presentation.' clamored Ozric as the teacher became perplexed. Grade what presentation? He thought to myself as he scanned the situation out. Being held up with a gun by his own student who's already threatened to blow his head off? Grade that presentation? He couldn't even deem an option to jump out of this. Ozric slammed his fists down on the desk and shouted once again. 'GRADE IT, DAMNIT!' See Furher, I've lost everything. My life is slowly slipping from the tips of my fingers and I've got nothing else to shoot for in life but this victory. The wolf inside of you is scared and the boy inside of me is ready. Let's make this one a classic for old time's sake, right? My heart, sproadically beating. My eyes, faint and groggy. My drug, the only friend. My soul, a force to be reckoned with. 'CLEAR!' screamed the doctor as he placed the clamps on the chest of Ozric for the second time. Ozric's body responded with a jolt, arching his midsection up in the air for a split second and falling back down to the bed. Still motionlessly flatlined, the doctor charged the clamps again and turned to the nurse. 'Time?' asked the doctor as he prepared the third jolt of electricity. 'Sixty-eight seconds, doctor.' answered the nurse who followed up her answer with a question of her own. 'Are we out of options, doctor?' asked the nurse who jumped back when the doctor got directly in her face. 'Nurse, I will do as much I can to keep this guy from dying. 'Optionless' is a word I'll work very hard to keep myself away from.' Jumping back to the classroom, the teacher was allowed to stand up, verbally grading the presentation going on before him. Traveling through a different vortex of time, Ozric started to pose with his gun as the teacher jotted down letter-grades of, well, whatever. Simply put, he was going through all possible obstacles to prevent him from being shot. Before Ozric could finish his final pose, which could've been compared to those of Action Jackson, the teacher placed the clipboard down and rested the pen on the block of paper attached to it. Through stormwinds of panting and gasping, Ozric looked down at the gun in his clamy, right hand and walked over to the graded-assignment. To the shock and awe of Ozric, he envisioned a dark-red 'F' planted in the middle of it. 'Failure'. He could see the sign hanging over his neck with this negative word stamped against it. He knew that if there was any word to describe him, it'd be in the lines of that one. He covered his eyes with cupped-hands and started to cry. Stomping his feet down on the ground, Ozric wailed. 'Failure! Failure! Failure!' he screamed at the top of his lungs as the teacher slowly interupted his tantrum, trying his hardest to keep him pastoral enough; if only to keep red and blue lights from shining into the windows. The teacher didn't want Ozric to hurt himself and unfortunately, that would be the case if the police were to get involved. Comforting him with a bold expression on his face, the teacher slowly realized that he knew absolutely nothing about Ozric and that seemed to scare him the most. A child with mental deformations? A flat out juvenile? Questions went rampant through his head like a dog chasing a rabbit in a circular motion ... for hours and hours and hours beyond. Drugs are delightful. They take away pain, stress and needless emotions. They put you in some rare form of carelesness and for some strange reason, it feels wonderful. Half the time, I don't care about what I'm getting myself into. I just do it for the sake of being me. The little will power inside of me is all I have left in this life and I'll be damned if some cult, tail-hanging bastard tries to take it away. I, see lights gleaming down upon me. I, feel as if I'm floating into some spacey world beyond this land. I, have no memories of what has just happened to me. I, am dying. As if every moment in time seemed to collide like two trains riding the same tracks at opposite directions, blackness took over. Suddenly, Ozric could be seen holding the pistol to the head of the teacher. The grade assignment, now crumbled in shreds with the clipboard snapped in half. Ozric's arms were violently trembling as he pulled the trigger without the slightest last word to top off the vicious hype of it all. The hospital could be seen once again as Ozric lay on the bed. His pulse at a steady rate and his breathing no longer collapsed. 'Is he going to be alright?' asked Dr. Klingman, who was allowed into the room immediately after the doctors revived Ozric. The main doctor hesitantly nodded as he turned is head toward the resting Mortimer. 'That is, if he stops doing the methamphetamines.' stated the doctor as Klingman quickly nodded. My life depends on less drugs and more success. The choices are, I either defeat you and go on to the final level in my journey to become a champion, or, I die a poor man with the same drug that gave me the happiest moments in my life in my lifeless hand, with brutal carvings along my wrists stating, 'Till Death Do Us Part...' ..Which one would you choose? The doctor scurried away from Jack Klingman who walked over to the side of the bed, looking down at Ozric, who's eyes were closed. Klingman lowered his head and said a quick prayer before turning around and walking out of the room. Just as Klingman was opening door, he heard a murmur come out of Ozric. '..He-Hey J..Jaack?' Ozric said in a whispered mumble as his eyes remained closed. Klingman turned around and jogged over to the bed. 'Yes, Ozric?' Klingman answered as he noticed Ozric giving a 'Thumbs up' and a then a 'Thumbs down'. '...How .. How'd ... How did I do?' asked the delirious Ozric Mortimer who waited for Klingman to answer. It took a few minutes for the question to actually hit the psychiatrist, who seemed to be taken aback. For lack of a better way to state it, Klingman spat it out the hard way. Ozric felt a peace with himself. Dangerously close to dying, but what did matter? If anything, he could laugh at it now. It was one of those moments he would never forget. Those moments where suspense oozes out of every pore. Those moments where life passes by you for a moment before driving to the side of the road, backing up and letting him back in for another series of rides; no matter how good or bad they seem to be, this is life. Ozric will walk into ReVolution a new man with a new agenda. To bury the haunting hatchet that the Sentinels relentlessly continue to swing at him. The first chapter in extinguishing the most evil of forces in PRIME? Dani. Furher. '..You overdosed on methamphetamines, Ozric.' The room grew quiet, with the exception of the heating vents. 'I think I knew that. But, am I alive?' Ozric asked anxiously with Klingman responding immediately afterwards. 'Yes, we're all alive for the moment, Ozric.' answered the psychiatrist. A bright smile of relief drew on Ozric's face as he sighed, keeping his eyes closed and mumbling one last thing before drifting into a deep state of sleep. 'I did wonderful then. Until morning, doctor.' Fin Good luck, Dani...
