I was gone for a minute, now I'm back with the jump off.

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today That's right, I went Lil Kim on your asses. But hey, you know you all love to see the V-A-N... oh, right. There's some confusion. As soon as The Whole Freaking SHO debuts on PRIME television, that Dark Age groupie Dani Fuhrer decides that he wants to be named Vangelus, too. That's 'Ban-HEL-oos' for you pronunciation-impaired jackasses who think that his name sounds like mine just because it's spelled almost the same way. Did just about EVERYONE ignore the fact that this guy is from South America? Why wouldn't he use that native pronunciation for his language? Even in German it wouldn't sound like my angelic 'Van-jell-us.' But let's not forget Imperium monkey and member of Los Diablos: Angelus. He was happily minding his own business and BAM! Someone with a name like his comes along. I mean, except for the b/soft v in Fuhrer's new name, his and Angelus's names sound EXACTLY alike. The nerve of this guy. I mean, I know that I rock so much that even Jon Kano would fall off the boat, but what gives this run-of-the-mill I R X-Deveshun! moron the right to pack his bags and hop on the Oxios Coattails Express and try to get famous off of MY name?! I mean, yeah, it's his birth name too, but he wasn't even using it for all those PRIME matches. He had his chance to use it, he passed on it, and now he's trying to say, 'No, wait, I wanna use my name after all.' Since when did PRIME allow take-backsies for anyone not named Boda? But I won't dwell on that too much. I've got a plan. It'll fix you all. GOOD. Bwahahahahahaha! Oh, wait. I forgot. Did you all hear the good news? Brown and I are apparently another in the long ling of 'good guy' tag teams that got drawn into PRIME because Hin See has like no foresight. You can't have one heel team in Los Diablos, a bunch of face teams in Teh Pwn, Team Zambo, and Flying Vergomovs, and then filler like the Pirate-Clowns from a Halloween Party Gone Terribly Wrong. I mean, if it was a dominant heel team, but to have the tag team champions be a team that couldn't win the tag team titles until the champions disbanded? That's gotta just suck some bigass balls. But hey. Teh Pwn is here, so the tag team division can be ready to get an injection of Vitamin V. Gabriel is a tag team specialist, and I'm a specialist at just flat-out stompin' nuckas (We did it, Jamar! We on fire, baby!) so you know that things are gonna get a lot more interesting around here. Karina Wolfenden is going to get chain by the neck to the digitally reanimated carcass of Two Ton so that she'll have no choice but to let herself heal, so that's my eclipse out of the way. Eclipse? Oh yeah, by that I mean the person who was always stealing my spotlight in RPW. For some reason she always got the attention. Was it the never-ending feud with Magnum Wolf that did nothing except prove that neither one of them had a life (or any imagination to figure that there's a whole roster full of people to annoy?) that got everyone on her side, or was it the breasts? I mean, hey, I got a lot more attractive honeys in my entourage if you just want girls to jump around in tight shirts. But this week, it's not about any of that. Apparently it's about punishment. Not of me, no no... PRIME loves me. My phone's already ringing off of the hook for promotional appearances. So many wrestling federations are trying to book me that I don't have a PDA, I have the frickin' Library of Congress. No, I'm not being punished. Token Weed is. Why would he get put up against both members of Teh Pwn, after all? Gabriel Brown, a multi-time champion in various federations. And myself, RPW's best kept secret (and STILL Eclipse Champion)! Weed doesn't stand a chance against one of us, let alone the combined threat? Congratulations, PRIME, you're essentially getting an exhibition to show off what we can do. Weed is an unfortunate punching bag in all of this, but hey, he shouldn't have slept with Hin See's daughter/wife/sister/niece/whatever he did to get on management's bad side. This is short, I know. Most of you are saying, 'I don't get out of bed for less than 5 pages!' But then you're wondering what pages have to do with anything. Hehe. Just rest assured, the beatings, both physical and verbal, get into full swing next week when Teh Pwn debuts properly. In a REAL tag team match. See ya'll until then. HOLLA! ~V
