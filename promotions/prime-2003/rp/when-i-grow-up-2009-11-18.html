When I Grow Up

18 November 2009

Date: November 18, 2009 "We're late!" Kazys Jankauskas looked down at his watch as he announced the obvious, hoping to motivate the habitually tardy. He knew that she was already moving as fast as she could, but for as fast as she was she certainly could never manage to make it to a venue on time. In the hotel hallway he tapped his foot impatiently with a backpack slung over his shoulder and a rolling suitcase propped up against the wall. "Shit! Shit! Shit!" He heard from inside as Elise Ares scrambled to get everything together at the last second. You could use a lot of words to describe The Swaggeriffic One, a lot of them not as nice as others... but organized was certainly not one of them. If you were on the outside like Kaz, you'd almost believe that Elise thought shouting a profanity or two would make her move faster because that's what her vocabulary consisted entirely of while she was late. Frustrated Kaz leaned up against the wall and sighed looking down at his watch. Every week before Revolution the same thing. He gets up early and make sure his stuff is packed while she sleeps the day away. Waking up at the last minute there's a mad scramble for her to throw everything together and rush to the arena. Just once, he's like to arrive to the arena on time and receive some food from catering that didn't require a microwave to taste edible. Opening his eyes he realized that he was blocking the path of a young woman holding her child by the hand as they walked down the hallway. Kazys took a deep breath and stepped the other way to make room for the two to get through. She smiled at the colossal Iron Wolf as her little boy looked up at him in awe. The boys mouth dropped, to him Kaz looked much like a super hero with his muscles flexing. Kaz smiled as he looked at the boy before... "Awww... fuck!" was screamed from inside the hotel room and the young mother glared over at Kazys, who took this opportunity just to shake his head awkwardly. Of course she'd shout a profanity just at that point in time. There was a child around and Elise just couldn't miss an opportunity to get Kaz in trouble. "Miss I'm sorry about her lang..." The door swung open and slammed against the wall of the hotel. From inside Elise Ares exploded out of the door with a cargo bag over her shoulder and two rolling suitcases behind her. She almost ran over the young woman and her child, forcing Kazys to jerk the two out of her path as she went speeding by. "Let's go! We're late Kaz! Don't just stand there!" Elise screamed as she went racing down the hall towards the stairwell. No time for elevators, they had an arrival time to make. Realizing he's being left in the dust, Kazys acknowledged the woman and her child with a nod as he grabbed onto his rolling suitcase and gave chase. She was so much faster than him, it was almost a lost cause as he went jogging down the hallway. He could feel the second flood shaking as he made his way towards the stairs with no Havana Harlot in sight. "Wait up, Elise!" He screamed as he opened the door to the stairwell. He couldn't see her but he could hear the sound of her suitcases jumping down the stairs. As he continued his pursuit, he knew there was a very good chance that she may very well start the car and start driving to the event without him inside. Giving her the keys last night was a terrible idea in hindsight, because when she's like this she's scatterbrained enough to leave without her. Good thing he wasn't booked. Getting to the bottom of the stairs he still hadn't seen her... like a flash she just disappeared over the horizon. Lowering his shoulder he plowed through the exit door of the stairwell to the first floor. People on the other side jumped as The Iron Wolf was moving and wasn't slowing down for anything. He felt all the eyes on him as he ran towards the front exit of the hotel, but he didn't care. All that was on his mind was getting to that car. "Don't leave. Don't leave." He repeated to himself over and over again as he pushed open the tinted front door to find himself surrounded by a media circus, right next to The Swaggeriffic One. Cameras were pointed in her face and tape recorders blocked her view of the parking lot. Great... the media. That's all they needed. Camera and tape recorders were always a great bet to stop The Swaggeriffic One in her tracks, but looking into her eyes Kaz could tell that even she was a little surprised by the attention. Not that she minded exactly, just took her a bit off-guard. In her mind though she quickly concluded that she had no idea why she was so surprised, because who could resist the urge to give the Jewel In The Crown of PRIME an interview. She didn't need to win the tournament to be called that. In her mind she'd been the jewel in PRIME's crown ever since she walked in the door of the promotion. "Elise, we're late... we have to go." Kazys quickly grabbed Elise on the shoulder as smiled in the limelight. "Not yet." She answered without breaking her smile, "I don't even know what they want." "It doesn't matter." "Miss Ares!" One of them finally spoke after getting their tape ready. "Que?" She answered, not even realizing she answered in Spanish. There were all sorts of nonsensical things floating around in her frantic brain at that moment, replying in the correct language wasn't exactly a priority. "I don't know if you heard or not but last night Dragon Mask died in a tragic in-ring accident last night during a match in Mexico City." The reporter continued, "Do you have any comments you'd like to make about his passing?" That was a bomb. Everything in her brain that was going on came to a screeching halt as she processed the news she'd just heard. Her mouth almost visibly went slack jaw when she looked back at Kaz. The cargo bag on her shoulder fell off onto the ground as her body went limp for just a moment. "ONE! TWO!" The crowd shouted before he finally shot a shoulder up off of the mat and they all went crazy, including a young lady in the front row who would later become known to the PRIME viewing audience as Elise Ares. At nine years old, she stood leaning her chest against the security railing and clapped enthusiastically as her father got a shoulder up. Back then she was far from behind The Swaggeriffic One that she is today. Back then she was just known as Elise Aresel Guerrero. The great (but not so much late because he was still alive today) El Demonio de Azul was legendary around the lucha libre circuits in Mexico and Latin America. Although he'd had several offers to move up north to the United States to be on national television, he'd turned them all down. He believed in the sport of lucha libre and was very passionate about it. So much so that he'd never attach his name to something known as "Sports Entertainment." He thought it was blasphemous and an insult to his business. It isn't hard at this point to figure out why the relationship with his daughter is strained, to say the least. It wasn't so much wrestling in PRIME for a national audience as it was that she was wrestling at all though. In his opinion, even back in 1993, his beautiful little girl was too precious to step into the squared circle. She belonged traveling the world as a producer or gracing magazine covers as a model, not fighting men twice her size and trying not to get killed. But at this moment in time his little girl was honestly one of the last things on his mind as he was pulled up off the mat by a man who'd been known as his bitter rival for over a decade, Wearing all black and green, the man who now held him in a headlock was his arch-nemesis Dragon Mask I. Both legends in their own right, Dragon Mask made a name for himself by brutalizing his opponents. Matches often times ended with his victims leaving on a stretcher due to an after match beatdown, a clear spit into the face of the sportsmanship and history behind lucha libre. El Demonio was doing much more than trying to win a match for his little girl in the front row. He was trying to avoid embarrassment in front of a sold out audience in Mexico City and leave on his own two feet. He knew any loss would surely result in him leaving on a stretcher to the nearest hospital. He tried to break free but the hold was tight, any attempt to push off was blocked by Dragon Mask attempting to rip off his mask. A clear break of the rules that the official failed to even noticed. Since pushing off didn't work, El Demonio did the next best thing by picking Dragon Mask up off of his feet and bringing him down with a huge back suplex. The crowd roared in approval as Dragon Mask arched on the ground and got back up as El Demonio also reached his feet. Elise smacked the barricade in front of her rooting on her father as he attempted to whip Dragon Mask into the ropes, but the move was reversed and Dragon instead sent El Demonio into the ropes against his will. As bounced off he somersaulted under a clothesline attempt from Dragon Mask, but didn't lose momentum as he hit the opposite roles. Coming back, Dragon Mask leap frogged him sending him to the other ropes. Dragon Mask got a running start and leapt into the air at El Demonio who did the same. Both attempting a flying cross-body press. Both succeeding. They collided in mid-air with a sickening thud before both fell helplessly to the mat. Elise covered her nose and face with her hands as she watched in fear for her father. Dragon Mask kicked his legs in pain, curling over on the mat favoring his chest and abdomen. Although in pain, Dragon Mask rolled over onto his back an in attempt to breathe and get some air into his much depleted body. It'd been a grueling forty minute match and he was certainly feeling the effects. On the other side of the ring, El Demonio de Azul lay stomach down on the mat... seemingly unconscious. In Elise's young mind her father was done for. She didn't want to spend the rest of the night crying in the hospital wondering if her daddy was going to be okay. Many times running into the ring to save her father had ran through her mind. Usually he came out on top, but sometimes he didn't. She often wondered the times he didn't what would've happened if she'd just helped. Would things really be different if she jumped over the barricade and gave him a hand. Looking over at her Japanese mother sitting next to her, the fear on her face was obvious. She, much like Elise, was worried about her husband and what the night held in store for them. Biting her lip, Elise nervously held onto the barricade as both luchadores lay on the mat... not moving as the crowd went insane trying to lift El Demonio de Azul to his feet with their cheers. Unlike the fans from all over the country she wasn't content with hoping and praying. That was her father in the ring. She had much more stake in this match. "Elise... no!" Her mother Ayumi screamed as she reached out for her child, but she was too late as her little girl cleared the barricade with one jump. Now on the other side, the crowd in attendance looked on in curiosity as the nine year old girl sprinted up the stairs onto the apron, sending security into a frenzy as they left their posts in the crowd to catch the little wannabe luchadore. As they got to the barricade, Elise was already on the apron climbing up to the top rope. Neither luchadore even noticed as the crowd went crazy, rooting on the little Elise as her heart beat faster and faster. Once up top, everything seemed to go in slow motion as her heart tried to beat out of her chest. She looked around at the fans stood up from their seats cheering her on. It took almost all of her concentration to breathe. She was so nervous and had never done this before, what if she got hurt? It didn't matter though. The adrenaline pumping through her from the crowd would make the pain go away and she had a father to save. Leaping into the air, cameras from all around the arena went off as she spread her arms out and landed a slightly awkward frog splash right across the stomach of Dragon Mask. The crowd went wild as Dragon Mask sat up after impact holding his stomach once again. Elise hadn't done much more than knock the wind out of him, but it was completely unexpected as she rolled to the middle of the ring, The bell sounded as she looked over at her father pulling himself up to his feet trying to figure out what happened. El Demonio looked into the eyes of his little girl as it's announced over the PA to the arena that he'd just lost the main event by Disqualification. "Hoja!" He shouted, which is Spanish for leave. Elise saw the anger in her father's eyes as he writhed in pain. "Get out of here!" "I was just trying to he..." That was as much of the sentence as she could get off before security grabbed her from behind. Kicking and screaming she tries to break free but it's no use. Her dad watches and doesn't do anything as they drag her out of the ring. Ashamed and embarrassed, El Demonio de Azul simply fell to his back and rolled out of the ring hanging his head in defeat as his little girl was dragged backstage by security. For just a second she felt like a super hero. This must be what all the movie stars feel like, being the center of attention and doing big things. In her home she was never even the center of attention to her parents, who were always so busy they didn't have time to raise a little girl but instead paid someone else to do it for them. In a way, the attention she received that night was like a drug where her first taste was free... but she wanted more. She wanted a lot more. It was an addiction that Elise was going to have to pay for, for the rest of her life. ---- Chapter Two: No Girls Allowed ---- The once thunderous roars of a full house were now gone, making everything in the arena seem so empty. Or maybe it was the solitary confinement she found herself in. Either way it was a very lonely place backstage. Staying in a room by herself ever since she was forcefully drug away by security guards three times her size, a child has not much more to do than stare at the clock hanging ominously over her head. Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock. It seemed every second went by slower and slower. Her eyes were still pink and swollen from what seemed like hours of crying, but in reality was probably closer to thirty minutes. The tears had since dried up. Crying was useless if there was no one around to hear, or if they did hear they just didn't care. It'd been over an hour since her last bit of human contact. All she wanted to do was help and this is what happens to her. Her father was going to be so pissed. The time dragging wasn't so bad when she thought about what she was in for at home with her dad. Never in her wildest dreams did it cross her mind she'd cost him the match. She felt terrible. Just like any other little girl her only real agenda was getting her dad's attention and hoping that he'd be proud of her. This time she had screwed up big time though. If she'd just waited the split second her mother would've been able to stop her and none of this wouldn't happen. If she could just go back in time... KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK! The bang on the door startled her as she perked up. Eyes wide and at attention as the doorknob started to move. Someone was definitely outside this time and it wasn't her imagination. As the door slowly started to open she was actually hoping to see security again, because it was the lesser of two evils after all. If she could only be so lucky. It was the greater of two evils. Elise stared in complete silence as the legend known as El Demonio de Azul walked into the room. Still wearing his mask and his entire wrestling gear, the amount of anger pent up inside of him was obvious from the start. His knuckles were caked with dried blood, presumably from hours of releasing his anger on the closest concrete wall he could find. His breaths were shallow as she slowly closed the door behind him, taking his eyes off his daughter for just a second. She wanted desperately to break the silence by apologizing feverishly, but she knew begging and pandering wouldn't get her out of what she'd found herself in. It was better to not speak at all. Anything she said was bound to make the situation worse. The match had taken its tool on El Demonio though. He had a limp in his step and any sudden movements were sure to make him scream out in pain. His opponent that night was the real demon. Many said after a match with Dragon Mask they'd rather have been in a car accident. Sitting down across from his daughter, he slowly began to untie the back of his mask now that he was fully concealed from public view. One by one the laces were pulled out from the back, getting looser and looser until revealing the face of an intense man. He was younger but very rugged, wearing scars from the legendary battles he'd fought. The mask offered no protection and was nothing more than a sacred tradition. A tradition the man cared about more than anything in his life, including his family. Elise couldn't make eye contact with her father, instead choosing to look away at the clock. Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock. "Look at me." He spoke softly, but loud enough that Elise could hear. Slowly she moved her head to look into his eyes. The eyes of a bitter man. She'd seen that look far too many times in her life. More than a little girl should. "Do you see this?" He pointed to a gash on the top of his skull, covered in caked blood that resembled his knuckles. Her heartbeat so fast that her breath staggered. Anything that she could've said wouldn't come out, it was like she was being strangled from the inside. The lump in her throat felt like the size of a potato. So her only choice was to nod her head with a yes as she felt the tears welling up in her eyes again. "This is what I had to go through tonight. I have a concussion Elise. I was fighting injured." He paused before violently lashing out, "FOR NO, GODDAMN REASON!" Elise flinched almost expecting a right hand to come across the table but it didn't, thankfully. "Because you had to disobey your mother. Disobey me and go against the only rule in this family that I've told you from day one was UNFORGIVEABLE!" He fumed as his little girl cowered in front of him, "I told you since the day you were born that you never get involved in my business." "I was just tryin..." "You're just lucky we're not at home or I'd beat the shit out of you." He interrupted her before she could even finish. "I just want to be like you dad." Lord only knows how he'd react if someone had found out what had just happened to her. She was convinced if someone found out he'd kill her where she stood. It probably wasn't very far from the truth. Looking over his shoulder he took one last glance at his little girl... crying, battered, and beaten on the floor. It had been almost ten years to the day since the last time she'd even watched a wrestling event live or been backstage, but that doesn't mean she was completely cut out from the business. Growing up her father regularly had the boys over for drinks and she watched her dad compete every week on television as Senorita Rosa kept an eye on her, far away from the squared circle. Just where daddy wanted her. Maybe it was better that way. Although things were never completely normal, things seemed to be much more normal when she wasn't always around her dad's business. You might even say that he was content with the life she'd led for herself. She excelled through school, getting high marks along the way and even secured a job as a Hawaiian Tropics beauty model. Traveling the world was pretty fun, as was gracing the covers of magazines, and starring in the dreams of teenage boys all around the globe. At the end of the day though, it wasn't enough for her. Even though it'd been almost ten years she could still vividly remember what it was like at the wrestling shows when she was a little girl, and even more so that one time she went into the ring. The cameras flashing. The roar of the crowd. She hadn't felt that kind of rush since. It was something that was missing in her life. Something she needed. It almost felt like it was her calling. That's how she found herself where she was today... Miami, Florida. She arrived just the night before, defecting from Cuba in the middle of the night so far had paid it's dividends. It was a whole new country and a whole new start for her. The first thing she did was throw her cell phone away into the ocean, if you're going to get a new beginning you better do it right. At least that's what she'd tell you. The real reason was an acute and very real fear of her father. Lord only knows the kind of abuse that would be thrown her away if he found her after she up and left in the middle of the night. Even at nineteen she was no where near the size and strength to escape her father. Her looks could fool a lot of guys, but not him. He was an entirely different animal. She'd looked into his eyes enough times to know that he wouldn't think twice about ripping her in two. Maybe that's why she found herself where she is today, following mapquest directions by foot to what used to be a warehouse but was currently being used an elite school for training professional wrestlers. Sure part of it was to learn how to defend herself, and part of it was to use it as a catapult towards the fame and adoration she'd dreamt of since she was a child. But it wasn't what the place was as much as who was inside. "The Dragon's Lair..." She said out loud to herself as she walked through the parking lot. Behind those doors was the very man she'd frog splashed as a child, the one and only Dragon Mask. He'd left the Lucha Libre scene for the limelight of American wrestling a long time ago, yet unfortunately without the mask he'd become a victim of the industry. Working his way through the independent circuit, he'd never got his big break. Never got the phone call up to the big leagues. To this day he's still traveling the US, although in his age not as much as he one did, doing nostalgia acts under his old guise of Dragon Mask I. He spent most of his time at this school training the next big stars of the industry, hoping to leave his legacy in a way that is far different than the one he envisioned when he left Mexico a few years ago. Maybe someone inside would get that call up to the big leagues some day. Maybe that somebody was her. Walking through he front door, the facility really wasn't as elite as she thought it would be. Nobody was there training at all, it must be a day off for the students. Yet the lights were on, dimly lighting the one run-down ring that sat in the middle of the huge complex. Surrounded by gym equipment and plastic folding chairs used presumably to watch. But in the world of professional wrestling, who really knows? "Hello?" She spoke loudly, hoping someone was there to answer her. Silence. Taking a few steps she decided to check out the facility. Even though some of it was run down the equipment was nice. Everything she could possibly think of that she'd seen in her dad's gym back home was here. She just wished it was... nicer. She wasn't much of a hand-me-down type of girl. She'd grown up the only child of a wealthy family, she had never learned to share. It was only a matter of time until she approached the ring. She ran her hand across the apron. It'd been a while since she'd seen one in person, not to mention touch one. It was so much smaller than she'd remembered. She thought it'd be a bit intimidating but it's not that bad. Nothing that she didn't think she could handle. The problem is with living on the streets the past two nights with not a dime to her name, how in the hell was she going to pay for it... "Hello?" A voice startled her, causing her to swing around and greet the owner of the voice that resonated through the building. Standing in the doorway of an office off to the side of the building, a middle-aged Hispanic man with long black hair started walking over towards her. He seemed a bit upset and very confused. He was not a large man, but his body was very well toned. If Elise had to guess what he was, she'd supposed he was a swimmer of a gymnast. "Who are you? What are you doing here?" The man asked as he approached her. He couldn't help but smile as he came close. Elise had turned into an extraordinarily beautiful young woman, a big smile was the reaction every man gave her when they met. Her beauty was even a bit intimidating to some, but this man at least seemed to have been around women like her before. "You're not Dragon Mask, are you?" She questioned checking out the man, he was so small. Couldn't be any taller than 5' 10". "I am." He answered, "I own this facility. Were you looking for something?" "Wow, you're tiny." Elise was never one to hold back comments, "Don't you remember me, it's me... Elise. I'm Jose's daughter. Remember? Little girl, this tall? Hit you with a frog splash?" "You've got to be kidding me! Elise!" The man's eyes lit up when he realized who it was, "I haven't seen you or your family in years. That was the last match I had in Mexico." "Mine too." She responded, "So I've come to train to be a professional wrestler." The man known as Dragon Mask smirked before breaking out in laughter. Elise didn't seem to catch what was so funny, scrunching up her nose before shooting him a look. His laughter quickly died down when he looked into her eyes. She had the glare of her father. "You're serious?" He asked. He didn't get an answer, but he did get a glare. "You are serious. Did your father send you." "My father has no idea where I am." Elise answered quickly, "I plan on keeping it that way. Last week I left Cuba. My dad thought I left on a trip for Hawaiian Tropic but I'd quit that job weeks ago. If he finds out he'll kill me." "I'm sure h.." "No, he'd kill me." Elise interrupted him, "Honest to God, murder me. I swear." "Sounds pretty serious." He finally agreed. He knew the man to be a violent and tenacious competitor in the ring but honestly didn't know the first thing about the man behind the mask. Her father had a few close friends but kept pretty much to himself. He was a man who enjoyed his privacy. Thing were probably better off that way. "I have a class starting off tomorrow, but I have a spot open next month if you'd like to come bac..." "That won't work for me." Elise interrupted, "I don't have a house. I've never been to Miami before, I don't know anyone here..." "No, no." The man spoke with a smile, patting Elise on the shoulder. "It won't be a problem at all. I have an open office where you can make yourself at home while you get back up on your feet. I'm sure you'll be a natural just like your father. You'll get some money coming in no time..." He paused for a second smiling and looking into her eyes. "We'll worry about payment later." ---- Chapter Four: The Price Of Fame ---- Time to hit the showers. After a long session, Elise felt like she'd been hit by a truck. Not just a truck... a very, very large truck. Probably carrying anvils or something equally silly. With a limp in her step she made her way into the women's locker room, a room she shared with no one. Being the only woman student had it's advantages... the best of all being privacy. This was certainly new to her. The locker swung open after the short battle with the lock. After talking to a few of the students she'd learned that he preferred to keep it that way.
