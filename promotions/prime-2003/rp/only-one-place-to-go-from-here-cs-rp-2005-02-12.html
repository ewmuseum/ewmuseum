Only One Place To Go From Here. (CS RP)

12 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 2/12/05 A voice echoed in the small room. 'Broke.' ... Broke-Broke-Broke. 'So that's why your coming back?' another voice asked. A cigarette cherry lit up and smoke blew out of the man's mouth. 'Yeah, that's why.' 'Ya know Raz, I just cant seem to understand how in the hell you lost all that money.' Out from the corner of the room the man with the cigarette stood up and revealed himself to be infact, Razor Blade. Razor took another drag off the cig and then put it out on the palm of his hand. 'Well Bob, Depression's a bitch!' He said as the cigarette sizzled in his hand. Bob, Razor Blade's personal manager and friend over the years stood across from him. 'And gambling too right?' Bob replied. Razor cracked a small smile. 'Wanna bet?' 'Razor I ju-' Before Bob could finish he was interupted. 'Yeah , yeah, I know. I gambled it all away, but you know it was never about the money, you do know that right?' Razor asked. 'I do know, I understand it somewhat, but I just wish it would have brought some closure.' 'Maybe it did.' Razor crossed his lips. 'Maybe it did.' He repeated softly. You could sense the mystery surrounding those statements with the hidden subleminal messages. Bob opened the door to the room and some light lit up half the room. 'C'mon, let's go.' He said. Razor grabbed his jacket and put it on as he followed Bob outside. They had just walked out of a gas station's bathroom. 'Why dont you atleast sleep in my car tonight.' Bob asked Razor. Razor smirked. 'I dont know, I've started to grow fond of the nothingness that surrounds my life.' 'It's pretty cold outside tonight, you sure?' Bob asked once more. 'I'm sure.' He said. Bob shook his head at Razor. 'Well I'm going to the hotel, I dont see why you dont just sleep in the rental car, it's Japan for christ sakes, you know they dont take to night walkers.' 'The plane ride over was enough luxury in my life for the moment Bob. I'll find a park bench, or something.' Razor replied. Bob stopped. 'Fine, I'll stay out a little while longer.' He said. 'We got some things we need to go over anyways.' Razor flashed a questionable look but it was quickly replaced by the gas station's clerk. Razor was returning the key to the bathroom when the clerk had a ear to ear smile on his face. 'I know hoo joo R!!! I know hoo joo R!!!' The japanese man yelled out excitedly. 'No you dont.' Razor replied. The man shook his head as he disagreed. 'Ya I do, Ya I do!' He insisted. 'Joo harkore legend, Lazor Brade!!' He said excitedly. Razor looked over at Bob and Bob back at Razor. 'What? It wasn't me who stayed in Japan for over five years!' Bob snapped at Razor. Razor rubbed his chin a bit. 'Your right.' The japanese man began a chant. 'LA-ZOR BRADE!! LA-ZOR BRADE!! LA-ZOR BRADE!! LA-ZOR BRADE!!' He yelled out loudly and motioned his arms in the air. 'You know, in a weird way, I sure have missed that.' Razor spoke out. 'Stadiums used to erupt in your chants over here.' Bob replied. Razor shook his head. 'That they did, but now its different. In just a couple of months I'll be forty-six years old. Sure I can get hardcore and down right dirty with the best of them, but its my experience and ring savy that has to pay off now, not my act first think later strategy. Now I work smarter, not harder.' 'Nothing wrong with that, it comes with being a veteran.' Bob said. Razor flashed a devious look. 'I tell you what tho, I'ma give em' hell in that cage match.' He said. Bob let out a small chuckle. 'I know you will.' The Japanese man with broken english still stood there waving his hands around chanting. 'What about him?' Bob asked. Razor flipped him the keys and signed the desk with a marker that lay near by and started walking out. 'Groupies.' Razor said as Bob laughed. The two walked further when Bob spoke up. 'So, you a little worried about getting back into all this with a dual halo cage match?' Bob asked. Razor laughed. 'Hell, I dont even know what a dual halo cage match is, but I betcha it aint too far away from that get-up at Sole Survivor.' Razor replied. 'Your right, it's not, but still.' 'I know you may be a little worried about me jumping back into the mix of things, especially within both PRIME and fWo, but what the hell, if I'ma go out then it might as well be in a ring.' 'Why do you even think like that?' Bob asked. 'Well I dont, but its always a possibility.' Razor said back to him. 'You know there are a few people you cross paths with here in PRIME right?' Bob asked. Razor smirked. 'Well, yeah, I mean there are a few people I cross paths with in fWo as well.' 'Yeah, but besides the likes of Brandon Youngblood or even Garvin for that matter, there are people here in PRIME that you REALLY cross paths with!' Razor's head moved back an inch a little confused. 'Poppa .... Nova ....' Bob muttered. Razor released a heelish smirk. 'Oh, yeah, them.' He chuckled. 'My buddies.' He laughed a little. 'There wont be any problems in PRIME I cant handle, or have I not handled , or in there case, mahandled in my past.' Razor paused. 'Things should run rather smoothly here.' 'Good.' Bob paused. 'Now if we can just win that damn Halo match, what an entrance that would be.' 'Hey, it's hard being the best, but somebody has to be it.' Razor smiled as he responded. Bob chuckled. 'How can you be so confident all the time, even when your at such a low point in your life?' He asked. 'Well Bob, It's when you have absolutely nothing that you then have everything to gain.' Bob stopped smiling, the words of wisdom really set in. 'Well, then, how about that hotel room? King size bed.' He poked at Razor. 'First of all, I'd never share a bed with you, King or not. Secondly, I may be on the up and up but I plan on staying at my lowest until I have something really worth while to gain.' Razor said. 'Like winning that halo match?' Bob asked. Razor smirked. 'Perhaps.' It was then Razor pulled out another cig and lit it up as he waved goodbye to Bob as he walked off in the night and Bob went his seperate ways.
