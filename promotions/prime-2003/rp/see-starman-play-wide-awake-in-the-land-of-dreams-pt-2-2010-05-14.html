Date: May 14, 2010 See Starman Play: Wide Awake in the Land of Dreams Pt. 2 By William K. Spades Orlando Vacant Staff Writer Thursday, May 14, 2010 HOLLYWOOD – Getting to Castor Strife’s house wasn’t the tough part- and considering the barista’s incorrect address had me sweating my way through Laurel Canyon Blvd until divine luck brought me to 2256, that’s saying a great deal. No, it was the man’s penchant for keeping his guests in the waiting area for an excessive block of time that was the real difficulty. Forty-five minutes went by before I approached the platinum haired receptionist who let me in the door to begin with. "Not to seem rude, but I actually have to be somewhere in an hour. Is he busy? I could come back…," I told her, lying of course. Truth is I had nothing better to do, but fuck it- I was bored. "Could you wait another twenty? He just paged me and said it would be twenty." Who does this guy think he is, Brian De Palma? "Twenty, got it." I sat back down and waited another forty-five minutes. Castor Strife is starting to make me think Dustin Diamond was simply caught in traffic. Is he even in there? I half-think he’s dead. Seriously, I’d feel a lot better about the whole thing if he were dead. "He paged me again to apologize, and said it would be another twenty. He promised. Please, have a seat," she said when I approached a second time. My reply was in the form of an uncomfortable silence- you know, the kind where it’s either ‘say nothing’ or ‘make a giant scene.’ I’m not the scene making type, so I locked her in for the stare. She looked back, blinked a sarcastic blink, glanced left (like I had five fucking heads and she wasn’t sure I knew), then went back to typing on her desktop. I should have left; should have gotten up, kicked in his door, pulled out my dick, said, "THERE IT IS!" and left like Clint Eastwood leaves a saloon after killing his man. But you know something, I told myself to sit there and wait. Wait as long as he wants, because it’ll all be worth it just to get one look at this asshole that wound up making you wait three hours. I had to know what that kind of nerve looked like. *** Laurel Canyon Blvd. takes you on a spiraling trip down one of Hollywood’s more prolific neighborhoods. On one side of the road are houses separated by what seems like quarter miles, hidden away behind gates, palm trees, shrubbery, and a dash of old Hollywood mystique. Narrow driveways lead to the portion of the homes which are visible from the street, the rest disguised behind the flora and fauna like a woman that allows herself to be seen only from good angles. On the other side of the road and behind every house: hills, everywhere hills. This place was a human paradise carved out of the ground. Each unit was a pair of crossed legs that only hinted at what was inside, daring you to get in its pants. As the doorknob to Castor Strife’s office turned to open, I could hear the zipper pulling down. "Mr. Spades?" That’s me; been living in your hallway the past three hours, how ya doin’? Not even a ‘Hello’. Bastard. Castor Strife was a tall man, very muscular, and long blonde hair to go with sharp green eyes. "Yeah, from the Orlando Vacant, thanks for seeing me. Mind if I…?" I said, holding up my digital recorder. "Of course. Have a seat wherever you like." Very funny, there was only one. Does he really meet with people one at a time? The room was completely white, too, with only the black chair and his oak desk providing any hue. "Decorator on vacation?" I asked, immediately realizing how cliché and void of wit that sounded. "There are many rooms in this house, but this is where visions are envisioned and re-visioned and un-visioned. It is my study, but we call it Tabula Rasa." He spoke like a man distracted by the birth of his ego. "Ah, the clean slate, huh? I gotcha." Could I think of nothing interesting to say? I hate when every sentence out of my mouth is a desperate attempt at a first impression. "A canvas is always white." Isn’t this the porn guy? Fuck it, lemme ask him. "You direct porno films, correct?" I said to a now stone-faced Castor. His face was now red, but not with embarrassment. He locked his jaw a moment before answering. "Who told you that?" said he, with the seriousness of an outbreak. "Honestly, it was some girl at a coffee shop. I wasn’t even…" "Who told you that?" he interrupted, this time whispering. "Umm…Sasha?" What was I supposed to say? The man wanted an answer. He looked mildly comforted, sitting back and quietly laughing to himself. "She resides in a faction of people put on this earth to do one thing: lie. She’s a liar; life is full of liars. Good luck to them." Spoken with flawless devotion. I picked the name ‘Sasha’ off the top of my head, and it’s a rare name at that. The fact he even knew a Sasha who indeed was spreading rumors around town about him being a pornographer was both amazing and startling. I have no clue what I’m going to learn from this guy, but let’s roll with it. "Calling what I do pornography is just a clever way of calling it a nigger. It is a slur, heaped upon my artwork by those who don’t understand and are therefore…frightened by it. Are you frightened, Mr. Spades?" "Uh, no sir. Disney World is frightening." "Disney World is frightening. Walter was a grey and tortured man." I assumed he meant Walt Disney, and he probably did, but it was said so matter-of-factly that he could have been referencing Walter his brother-in-law who was just arrested for scoffing parking tickets.
