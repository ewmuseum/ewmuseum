Turn Your Head and Cough (Revolution 115, tag-team match with N vs. The Covenant)

8 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 8th, 2007 "Alright, now open up. Say 'aaaaaaah.'" "Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaah." "Good. Aaaaaand, that should just about cover it." The physician, Dr. Allen Hathaway, tossed the tongue depressor into the trash, followed by a pair of rubber gloves. Hathaway was a good sized man, about six foot one. Older too, with a balding head and a wrinkled face. He wiped his hands off on his white doctor's coat, picking up a clipboard as he walked across the room. "So I'm lookin' good n' healthy, doc?" asked Asa 'Fro' Fountain, the Most happenin' cat in Prime, sitting on a physical bench, clad in one of those surgical gowns that they make you wear. "Well, despite your heavy drinking stint..." replied Hathaway, taking a seat in a small swivel chair. "...and your new smoking habit, you're one of the healthiest forty-year-olds I've ever met." Asa smiled wide and clapped his hands, excited to hear the news. "That's what I like to hear, my man! So I'm good to get back in the ring?" The doctor paused for a moment, scribbling something on a clipboard. "Asa, you're not getting any younger," replied the doctor. "That's what they tell me, but I got half my life in front of me, doc! I tell ya, I feel great!" "You might feel great now, Asa, but tearing a ligament will be one awful wake-up call. Look, I know you have half your life in front of you, and yes you're in great condition for your age. But you're an athlete, Asa, not a business man. A lawyer can do his job until the day he dies. How many athletes do you know kept going after forty?" Asa rolled his eyes a little as he start to get dressed. "I ain't most athletes, brotha," he replied, pulling a t-shirt over his head. "I'm aware of that, but this isn't something you can just ignore! This year you might feel spectacular, but it's not unlikely that next year you could do some serious damage to yourself. The forties are unpredictable." Asa just smiled as he pulled up his jeans and tightened his belt. "Look doc, you know what I did after I retired four years ago? I drank a lot, smoked a lot, and had a lotta sex. Weren't the healthiest decisions, I know." Asa slipped on his shoes, tying them up as he spoke. "But ain't it possible that gettin' back in the ring is what this cat needs to get his life back on track?" Hathaway nodded, setting his clipboard on his desk. "Yes, it's very possible." "Now you're jivin' with me! So, doc, are you gonna give me the O.K. to bring the funk back? Or are ya gonna tell me to go home?" The doctor sighed heavily. He stood up in front of Asa with a poker face that would make Chris Ferguson fold. "You have to cut back on this drinking habit, and you have to quit smoking immediately." Asa smiled wide, slid his aviators to the bridge of his nose, and clapped Dr. Hathaway on the shoulder. "Thanks doc, I knew I could count on ya. Now I gotta catch me a plane for Georgia. See ya next time, cat daddy." Asa grabbed his jacket and headed out the door. The doctor followed, calling to Asa down the hallway. "You'd better hope "next time" you're hear for a routine check-up and not a heart attack!"
