Tattoos and Notes (match RP for ReV 80)

7 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/7/05 ~Monday, November 7, 2005. 2:03P.M.~ In the ring, he may have been the King of Kings, but in the real world, he was struggling in a way that was sickening in its insolvability. For two months he had soaked himself in the addiction of the wrestling industry and had done so behind her back as much out of necessity as out of cowardice. And then, finally, he had told her exactly what he was doing… put it all out in front of her, and he might as well have stayed behind her back. The ‘shock’ of his confession had done nothing to snap her out of her amnesic state. Hiding it from her hadn’t been hard, but it hadn’t been productive either. Telling her was equally worthless, the amnesia had still basically hidden her from the truth, and even after she had written the note to herself so she would remember, things had still been miserable. So fuck it. Nothing more needed to be said other than just fuck it all. Not that Matt was, or ever had been, a big fan of the ‘F’ word. In fact, he had managed to make it through his years in college using it maybe once a week, while surrounded by roommates that used it as an adjective or adverb about once for every noun or verb that ever escaped their lips. Still, recently, it was the one word that would best express the workings of his everyday life. That’s why he had readopted his old philosophy that had served him so well in the Underground… “FTW”. And that renewal of ideals was why he found himself in the tattoo parlor doing something he had vowed he would never do. Then again, if you were to ask Mary, she would probably say that he wasn’t much for sticking to the things he vowed not to do. The buzz in his ears stung as much as the piercing that traveled across his throat. Things in the room looked rather blurry, though mostly, his vision was consumed by the artist who was gracing his skin with a permanence it was too late to regret. “Jesus man… I gotta say, I’ve never had this particular request before. I’ve done the back of the neck more times than I can count, and even did the front once… but damn, never both.” “Yeah, well… I didn’t really give it much thought. Just was out for lunch, walking down the street, passed this place, next thing I know… I’m standing inside making a request for…” The needle struck a particularly sensitive spot and Matt winced for a moment, “… this.” “So this was a spontaneous decision, huh?” “Entirely” Matt paused for a moment, “Should I be talking?” “Probably not.” Taking the advice, Matt silenced himself and focused on the pain that scrolled across his neck. Each point of anguish was the same as the one before it. There was nothing to distinguish one moment from the next. There was only the sting of repetitiveness that originated from just beneath his vision. After KoK, his spotlight had brightened ten fold, and now, what had been a battle to see through the darkness he had slipped through under the radar, had become a battle not to be blinded by the light that was being cast his way. To debut in the JITC was a good spot and a fortunate situation. What luck had come his way with a debut spot in the tournament had ended with his first match. From that point on, luck had given way to the actual battles. At first, his rise through the tournament went rather unnoticed. It wasn’t until he had made the semi-finals at the King of Kings that much talk had popped up about his success and abilities. Still, even at that point, it was unlikely that much of anyone expected him to be standing with his armed raised in victory at the end of the evening. After just six matches he found himself undefeated and the number one contender for the Universal championship. In two months, he had gone from a nobody rookie to the top of the PRIME mountain. Matt had joined PRIME in the middle of a unique and tragic event in his life, and his arrival in PRIME had been as much, if not more, about escaping from the tragedy as it had been about proving himself. But now, he found that he had already begun to prove himself, and merely getting away to the world of PRIME was quickly becoming a distant goal, trumped the possibility to do so much more than just escape… he had the chance to thrive. The only real problem with a quick rise to the top was the lack of respect he was sure to find… not that he had gone out of his way to earn any. Cracking open the skull, in just his second week with the federation, of one of the most beloved PRIME superstars wasn’t going to earn him any favors with the fans or a lot of the boys in the back. In fact, Killean, the very victim of the grotesque attack, had probably been the most sympathetic and forgiving about the entire thing. And it was pretty obvious that finding himself the number one contender for the top prize in the business wasn’t going to win him over any fans in PRIME either. He was just waiting for the first “unproven” to fly his way. So, as the drone of the artist’s paintbrush echoed in the small, rather warm room, he figured that he should prepare himself for the realization that he was going to end up with a lot more enemies than just the man he had first targeted when arriving in PRIME. It was likely the case that he didn’t need any more adversity in his life than he already had. And making more enemies in PRIME was just going to make Wednesday nights all that much longer. With his sights set on Hoyt and the Universal title, their battle scheduled for The Great American Nightmare, he was going to have to watch his back, or get someone to watch it for him. That much he knew… “Finished” the words filled the air of the room as the buzzing came to an abrupt stop. “That didn’t take too long.” For the first time in quite some time, Matt was able to hold his head up, not leaning it forward to the floor, or backward to the heavens. “They were pretty simple tattoos, nothing too complex. And rather small. Had you come in here wanting some personalized tattoo that spread from shoulder to shoulder and all the way down the back… you’d have been here a while longer.” “I’ll keep that in mind, just in case I ever get the urge for another of these. I here they’re addicting.” “Can be. So, want to check it out. The artist pointed to a mirror in the corner of the room. “Looks damn good, but then again, its my work… I might be biased.” “Naw, I trust you.” “You sure, man?” “Like you said, it was pretty simple. FTW on the back, Mary on the front. So long as you can spell, i’m sure its great.” * * * ~Monday, November 7, 2005. 2:34P.M.~ As he turned down his street, heading home, complete with new ink, Matt reached into his pocket and pulled out his cell phone. He turned it on, checking to see if he had missed any messages while at the tattoo parlor. In fact, he had one message, from the office of PRIME, informing him of the card for the week’s ReVolution. As the message came to an end, he cursed and slammed the phone shut. Somehow, the idea had come to members of the PRIME staff to book a rather intriguing tag match that would have enough underlying tension and possibilities to keep anyone guessing every second of every minute till the match actually went down. Killean Sirrajin and Dave Gibson would be teaming up to take on Tchu and Aimz. Again, he flipped open the phone and again he played the voice message, checking to make sure that he had heard correctly the exact structure of the match and the names of the individuals involved. The second time, nothing had changed. Killean and Gibson vs Aimz and Tchu. It was enough to give him a headache. For starters, he would be facing off against The Supreme Machine, and although it was possible thanks to the tag rules of the match that the two would never actually step foot in the ring against one another, the likelihood of the situation was that the two would collide, even if only briefly. On top of the potential collision between himself and Killean, was the fact that he would have to team up with one of his opponents from King of Kings. It had been only a matter of days since Matt had squared off against Aimz in the finals of the Jewel in the Crown tournament. He had figured it bad enough that with the battle with Hoyt officially scheduled that he would have to be constantly looking over his shoulder, now it seemed, as early as this Wednesday, and entirely separate from any conflict over the Universal title, he might have to guard his back from his own tag team partner for the night. Perhaps it was time to start making preparations to ease the bumps in the road. Maybe the best way to handle enemies in multiple forms was to turn those enemies against one another. It was time to make the right moves to make sure he wasn’t having to look over one shoulder at Killean and over the other for Hoyt, meanwhile never knowing who might jump out in front of him ahead on the road. Maybe he needed to meet with Killean, talk a few things through. Hoyt and The Supreme Machine had a past, and not one that was much more pleasant than the one between Killean and himself. Though he hadn’t been a part of PRIME at the time, Matt had been a large part of Killean’s life, and that connection left him with a close watch on PRIME. When Killean had achieved his greatest success, climbing to the top of the federation and claiming its highest prize, it had been Hoyt who had stolen it from him. Matt hadn’t forgotten that, and he doubted that Killean had either. Wednesday would be a good opportunity to find out. Matt parked his car in the drive and headed into the house, Mary was sitting on the floor, playing with Georgie. Both were laughing and smiling. He stopped, noticing that Mary hadn’t heard him enter the house, and for a minute he simply watched his wife and child and they worried about nothing and laughed at less. “You two look like you’re having quite a good time.” Matt walked into the living room, and sat down at the couch, his family right at his feet. Did I miss a really funny television show, or was it just a really good joke.” “A magnificent joke.” Mary looked up at him and smiled, which was enough to let him know that she hadn’t glanced at the note in the last half an hour. Since she had written down that he was wrestling and she had amnesia, he had spent every moment wondering when she would stumble across the note and rediscover his confessions all again. It had led to at least a half a dozen fights in the last three days alone, and he was beginning to wonder how much more either of them could handle of having the same fight a thousand times over, as if it had never occurred before. “Ahh, and which one of you told the joke?” “It was Georgie.” Mary laughed at his good humored question. “Sounds like she might have a future as quite the comedian.” “Gets it from her father.” Mary pulled herself up and crashed on the couch by Matthew. “How’s your day going?” She leaned in and kissed him, and in doing so, noticed the tattoo. “And what in God’s name is that?” “Tattoo.” “I thought you had said you weren’t the tattoo type?” “Didn’t think I was.” This time, it was Matt who got down on the floor and started playing with Georgie, rolling around in her laughter. “Please tell me there’s only the one.” “No, there’s two. Your name on the throat, and another on the back of the neck.” “Were you were afraid you were going to forget my name or something?” “No, but I have recently discovered how much something as simple as remembering can really mean. Anyways, I just wanted to have my angel with me at all times. So now while we’re apart, you’re still with me.” “Apart?” “Nevermind.” He looked her way for a second, then back to Georgie. Mary joined them on the floor. The baby giggling between her two smiling parents. “Anyways, the other tattoo was just an old state of mind, from back in the days in San Diego. Kind of a current reminder of the way to embrace my business.” “What’s that?” “It’s…” he stopped as quickly as he had begun, deciding to alter the form of his response, “… nothing you haven’t already heard a hundred times. Probably be reading about it by the time dinner rolls around.” Before Mary could even begin to think of a response, the baby’s laughter gave way to a large, long yawn, followed by a small frown. “Uh oh, looks like somebody could really use a nap.” She picked up Georgie and went to take the baby to her crib. Matt watched the two disappear into the bedroom, wondering what Mary would have asked had Georgie’s mood not taken a quick turn of grumpiness. Would there even have been a point in telling her? Would there be any point in not? Either way, she had made the note to herself, and regardless of what he told her, she always had the note to come and go, bringing a mass of arguing with it. Every time she found the note, all he could do was brace himself and wonder if it would tears or screams on that particular occasion. He wondered if she would find the note when she was tucking Georgie in to bed. Was it in the bedroom? Matt suddenly realized that he wasn’t sure where the note was, no more so than Mary, who’s very amnesic state which had caused her to need to write the note, kept her from ever remembering right where she had put it. Neither of them knew where the note was, he at least had the advantage of knowing that such a note existed, which gave him the ability to search for it. It would be safer… better in his possession. Maybe he could stumble across it before she could.
