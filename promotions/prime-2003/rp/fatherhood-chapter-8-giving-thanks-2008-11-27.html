Fatherhood - Chapter 8 - Giving Thanks

27 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 27, 2008 THURSDAY NOVEMBER 27, 2008 SIVEC HOUSEHOLD 4:00 PM The house had been filled with the aroma of fresh turkey, gravy, cornbread stuffing, baked corn casserole, and cranberry sauce. The murmur of various conversations drifted into the kitchen from the living room. Laughter rung out from another direction in the house, signifying that the children were getting rambunctious and eager to partake in the holiday feast dedicated to giving thanks. Mike and Pam Sivec’s house was the beacon of activity on most holidays throughout the year, with family members from near and far venturing to the house in Hilo to celebrate. Today was no different, aside from the fact that there was one rather important person missing. “This would’ve been our first Thanksgiving together, ya know?” Pam Sivec listened to her would-be son-in-law as she prepared the last of the food for the holiday meal. Hearing Bryan talk like this just wasn’t right, but his demeanor was understandable. He was right; Bryan and her daughter didn’t get to spend one of the two meaningful holidays together, and that’s enough to make anyone down on this normally joyous day. “I know, Bry. But you’re doing so well…you’re so strong.” Her words of encouragement could do nothing but make Bryan feel at least a little bit better about the whole situation. She was right; throughout everything that had gone down, Bryan was doing exceptionally well, and the family admired him for that. They knew that Nikkie would have it no other way. “I’m tryin’. It gets hard, but Nicholas needs me.” Bryan hadn’t gotten used to saying Nick yet. It would slip every once in a while, but more often than not, Nicholas was the name that came to Dawkins’ mind first. “Nikkie would be proud, you know that.” Pam finished garnishing the dinner salad and set it atop the table, alongside all of the other luscious foods prepared for the day. She pointed to the living room. “Alright Bryan, tell everyone it’s time to eat.” With a nod, Bryan exited the room. Pam turned quickly, discretely wiping a tear from her eye, making sure it was gone before anyone entered the room. * * * As everyone filed into the room, all eyes were fixated upon the amazing spread prepared by Pam, with contributions from various family members. It was truly a sight to behold for those who yearn for the holiday meals. The adults took their places at the elongated dinner table, and the children took their places at the smaller square kid’s table off to the side. As everyone settled into their spots, one spot was left open, yet no one said a word. Everyone knew who it was for, and why it was left empty, and not even the children questioned the vacancy’s existence. Mike stood up and gazed around the room, filled with friends and family who had been attending this dinner for years, and extended his hands out. His burly bass voice echoed through the dining room. “Thank all of you for coming. It’s great to see most of the family gathered around here again for the holiday, especially in the midst of the tragedy we have been forced to power through. At this time, I would normally ask you all to bow your heads and pray to the good Lord above and give thanks for all that he has given us, especially this bountiful meal. But instead, I would like to call upon my son-in-law, Bryan, to lead us in the prayer for thanks this evening.” Lifting his head up whilst sporting a rather unexpected look on his face, Bryan peered at Mike questionably. This was his first Thanksgiving with the family, and Mike was asking HIM to lead the family in prayer? Without any more hesitation, Bryan flashed a somber smile and nodded. “Thank you, Bryan.” Mike sat down and embraced Pam’s hand. The two in-laws of Bryan peered up at their would-have-been son-in-law and smiled. Whether it was official or not, Bryan was a real part of the family to them. He loved their daughter and they knew it. Bryan stood up and looked around the room, examining the faces of the family members that would’ve also been his, had it not been for the terrible accident that plagued this family a few months ago. Their expressions were various; faces filled with anticipation, joy, love, and compassion. Although they weren’t all familiar with the new face, they embraced him because he was to become one of them. Bryan took a deep breath. “First, I’d like to thank Mike and Pam for offerin’ for me to come into this house today and lettin’ me be a part of your holiday, even though I’m not officially family now. Before I begin with the prayer, I just wanna say a couple things. This right here is the reason we all should be thankful. Followin’ the tragedy and heartbreak this family went through, we could’ve all crumbled and fallen apart. Family members could’ve broken apart, phone calls wouldn’t be exchanged anymore, and long-standin’ relationships could’ve pretty much been destroyed because of a really unfortunate event.” Bryan exhaled deeply and then drew in a big breath. “But this family is strong. You guys don’t give up, and ya all realize that even though we lost Nikkie, that’s no reason for an entire family to self-destruct. Had that happened, Nikkie would have been disappointed in ALL of us. She loved every single person in this room, I’m sure, and to see somethin’ like that tear this family apart would’ve made her furious. So tonight, let’s think about how lucky we are for each other, and for the things God has left us with on this day today, not what he took away from us on October 1st. If Nikkie’s watchin’ over us right now, and I’m sure she is, I guarantee she’d be happy to see that she wasn’t a reason to break this family apart…” Looking around the room again, Bryan saw many faces with eyes welling up and tears rolling down their cheeks. The interesting thing was that no, those tears were not of sadness, hate, or resent; they were tears of joy. Pure, unaltered joy. And as Bryan made the sign of the cross to begin the actual prayer of thanks, he couldn’t help but look beside him and imagine the image of Nikkie sitting there, peering up at him with a smile on her face. * * * THURSDAY NOVEMBER 27, 2008 HILO MEDICAL CENTER ROOM 3233 7:00 PM The routine was a usual one for Dawkins. Coming in after visiting hours wasn’t even an issue anymore. Marnee didn’t even question him anymore. He signed in, flashed a quick smile, and made his way down the long hallway to room 3233. Making a quick left, Bryan walked into the room and slid the nearest chair over to Nicholas’ bedside. The little guy was sleeping, and Bryan wasn’t about to wake him. Plus, watching Nicholas sleep was one of Dawkins’ new favorite pastimes. There’s just something peaceful about watching a newborn sleep that makes it seem as though all is right in the world. “He’s a real cutie, you know…” The Flyin’ Hawaiian had become accustomed to her voice by now, and was starting to take quite a liking to Marnee. Although it was way too early to go jumping into the dating scene again, Bryan was comforted by her presence and felt like it was good to have someone to just talk to. Someone, that is, who doesn’t want to talk about what’s happening in the world of PRIME, anyway. If he wanted that, there were fans all over the island who would be more than willing to oblige. “Yeah, Nicholas is gonna be a good lookin’ kid.” Bryan smiled. Talking about Nicholas really brought out the best in him. “Just like his dad, right?” With a playful wink, Marnee pulled a chair up to accompany Dawkins, who was back to watching the newborn sleep. “Ya know, she would’ve been overjoyed to see him. He’s a fighter, just like she was. From what the doctors tell me, it took a big struggle form her before she finally became overpowered that day. I guess they said that it should’ve only taken a couple of minutes for her to pass, but it took nearly 45 before her body just couldn’t fight anymore. She would’ve been so proud to see him fight and make it through the whole premature birth thing.” A lone tear slowly slid down Bryan’s cheek. “He’s all I’ve got now. Sure, havin’ family and friends is good, and the guys at work, like Chan, are great too…but Nicholas is my responsibility now, and seeing him be so healthy and strong really shows me how much I have to be thankful for. I mean, I could’ve lost ‘em both that day…” Marnee simply listened. “Not to sound like I’m happy about the outcome or anything, but seriously. I could’ve lost my fiancée AND first born son that day. I’d have loved for her to have survived, too, but I guess God just didn’t have that in his plans. All I can do is be thankful that he’s here, and that he’s healthy.” Reaching over to caress Nicholas’ tiny hand, Bryan saw the little guy turn his head, opening his little eyes just a fraction of an inch, making eye-to-eye contact with his dad. His little legs kicked up in the air and, getting a hold of Bryan’s finger, applied that vice-like grip that Bryan had grown to love from his son. “Well, I’m gonna leave you two for some alone time for the holiday. If you need me, you know where to find me.” With a quick wave goodbye, Marnee left the room, leaving father and son in that oh-so-familiar position. Nicholas with a tight grip on his father’s index finger, with Bryan simply staring down at the little guy, trying to figure out what was going through the infant’s head at that moment. “So, what’cha think about comin’ out on the road with dad, eh? I mean, ya still got a while to go before the doctors clear ya to get outta here, but I think comin’ out on the road with dad and Uncle Chan will be good for ya. It’ll also give me a chance to get to know how to do this whole ‘bein’ a father’ thing.” Nicholas, having no idea what his father said, simply stares up in amusement at Bryan. “But shh, little bruh, I haven’t talked to gram and pap yet, so let’s keep this between you and me, ‘kay? Don’t want them breathin’ down our necks before we even get ya outta here, ya know? I know they want the best for ya and bein’ out on the road with a bunch’a wrestlers might not be the most ideal place to grow up, but hey…I did it, and I guess I turned out alright. I mean, I made you, right?” Bryan couldn’t help but smile. Looking down at Nicholas, Bryan could see the distinct facial features of the Sivec and Dawkins families; he had Nikkie’s eyes, nose, and chin, and Bryan’s hairline, cheekbones, and smile. He was a spitting image of the perfect baby the two could have made, and Bryan couldn’t be more proud. Bryan Dawkins had so much to be thankful for. A beautiful, healthy baby boy, a loving family, and a steady career. After all he had gone through, he was finally coming to terms with the fact that although things wouldn’t be easy, he was confident that like with anything in life, a little hard work can even make the most impossible things seem very possible. But for right now, Bryan Dawkins was concerned about nothing. It was Thanksgiving night, and the thing he was most thankful for was lying in a bed not a foot away from him. Only one thing, or person, to be more accurate, could make his Thanksgiving complete, and he’d have liked to believe that she was there, watching over the two of them with a smile.
