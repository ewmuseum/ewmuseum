The Homecoming (vs. Vangelus Olsig)

13 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 13, 2005 It has been a long month. It all started when Boda came out on ReVolution and revealed his choice of stipulation for the Overkill main event. A buried alive match was definitely unique for PRIME. No one had stepped up to the plate and participated in one before and the fans of PRIME were ready for the history making match. The cheers were resounding for the hero of PRIME, the Universal Champion. Immortality had engulfed The Supreme Machine once the pin fall took place back at Ultra Violence. The arena was electric back then and the Universal Championship came home. It took a long 10 months for Sirrajin to achieve that specific goal and he in no way disappointed. After Ultra Violence, it took some time for Boda to rear his ugly mug once again. Buried alive was his choice, but after Killean got wind of a plan in which Boda was to prematurely make the champion eat mud and worms, Killean reacted and it was not what most were expecting. The Supreme Machine snapped and had Boda swimming with the fishies in Maine. Boda came back around after the near death experience to die again according to Sirrajin and that is exactly what happened. Boda was buried at Overkill regardless of the interference of Hoyt Williams and Tony Rolo. Now, it has been announced that The Supreme Machine will be defending his Universal Title at Colossus against Hoyt Williams. Boda will only be included if he can defeat his arch-rival Jon Kano. But why give Boda another shot? Everyone wants the Dark Age gone but yet Hin See finds it necessary to give him yet another chance to regain the Universal Title. Sirrajin himself has a little bit of an obstacle to sidestep. He offered to put the Universal Title on the line against Vangelus Olsig (AKA Dani Furher) when they fought to a draw after using steel chairs on ReVolution 65. It will be a hard task and Killean himself says he sees some major talent in Mr. Olsig. But the champion has a distinct advantage. Not only because he is the champion, but because he will be wrestling at “home” for the first time in his career. The MTS Center opened in November of 2004 and it is a beautiful facility. Killean has only seen it in pictures though. But at ReVolution 66, he will see it up close and personal. His plane landed at Winnipeg International Airport on Monday the 13th of June. This was one of the only events that he didn’t drive to. He decided to return to San Diego after ReVolution in Toronto and then fly out to Winnipeg. He wanted to get there with plenty of time to spare before the event. It was after all a homecoming of sorts and he hadn’t seen the city for quite some time. Besides the new arena in the downtown area, he heard the city had changed quite a bit. The beloved owner of the Winnipeg Goldeyes baseball club, Sam Katz, had become the new mayor of the city and had apparently cleaned up the now thriving metropolis. The Supreme Machine had stepped out of the terminal and into the baggage claim area. Many other people were fighting for their bags and Killean thought it wise to hang back and wait for the crowd to diminish. As he stood against the far wall watching the bags go around the conveyer, Killean was approached by a young boy and his mother. It didn’t take long for some one to recognize who he was but the boy was pleasantly quiet. He didn’t really want to attract much attention to himself. He shook the hand of the mother. “Killean Sirrajin, how do you do?” He politely introduced himself to her as the boy tugged on his long leather trench. “And what’s your name?” He messed the kid’s hair a little bit trying to be playful. He boy laughed. “My name’s Bobby and this is my mom, uhhhh…” the boy looked confused as he made a “come here” signal with his finger to his mother. He whispered into her ear. “Mom, what is your name?” The relatively young lady blushed and laughed lightly. Killean couldn’t hold back a smile. “My name is Rebecca.” She blushed again as her son blurted out her name after hearing it. “Rebecca! That’s it! I forgot!” The boy repeated as Killean smiled and shook his head.“Rebecca is blushing! OOOO!” Her son blurted out and she playfully smacked his arm. “Bobby! That’s mom to you, got that mister?” Bobby smiled and nodded his head as he ran over to the conveyer to look for their suitcase. Rebecca was left with Killean. She was searching for the words but struggled to talk now that she was left alone with the Supreme Machine. “Ummm, Bobby pointed you out and I have seen you on TV. Are you really from here?” She was having problems looking into his eyes as she turned her foot repeatedly, pivoting it on her toes. “I am originally from Winnipeg. I do reside in San Diego now however. Where are you returning from?” Killean was trying to make conversation as the boy was now chasing after the bag he spotted on the other side of the conveyer. “We just got back from Texas. We have family down there and my husband lives there.” She replied in a rather quiet tone. ‘Ugh, guess I won’t be able to get her to show me around town now. She is married.’ Killean thought to himself as he tried to not look disappointed. She was a beautiful lady. She was probably around the same age as he was. She had long red hair and a fair figure. She was a little bigger here and there but she made it look good. “My ex-husband I mean.” She corrected herself as Killean was thinking. ‘Jackpot!’ He thought. Maybe this would turn out to be a good night after all. She turned to find Bobby lifting their bag off the conveyer and pushing it on its wheels over to them. Killean figured he had better work quickly. “Listen, I haven’t been here for quite sometime and I would enjoy it a lot if you could show me around town. We could have dinner as well, it would be on me.” Killean blurted out. He sounded desperate but Rebecca was caught quite off guard and really didn’t notice the tone of his voice. “UHHH, ermm sure. That would be amazing. I hope I can find a sitter on short notice.” She looked to see Bobby now standing beside her with a replica of the Universal Title in his hand. “Sign this Mr. Sirrajin!” The boy exclaimed. Killean took the belt and dug around for a pen in his jacket. He instead pulled out a sharpie as a pen really wouldn’t do the trick. He also pulled out a piece of paper and placed it on top of the belt without Bobby seeing it. He scribbled his “John Henry” on the belt and wrote the name of his hotel on the paper along with the words, ‘Meet me in the lobby @ 6 PM.” He handed the belt to the boy and held on to the paper which he slipped to Rebecca. Bobby didn’t notice this and she looked at the paper and blushed again. She nodded to confirm to Killean their plans. “Thanks Mr. Sirrajin!” The boy yelped as he looked at his new collector’s item. “Not a problem little guy, but call me Killean please. Mr. Sirrajin was my father.” He replied. “What are you doing this coming Wednesday night Bobby?” The boy took no time in responding to the question. “We’re going to watch you kick some butt on TV!” The kid must have been an avid watcher of ReVolution. “You aren’t going to the arena to see it live?” Killean questioned, mostly looking at Rebecca. “Well we really don’t have the money for that. I am a single mother now and I only work as a waitress. We spent out savings on this trip because my cheap ex husband wouldn’t pay for it.” She was sounding kind of down and pissed off at her ex. After all, he wanted to see Bobby. Least he could have done was paid for it. “That cheap skate, look, how would you like it if I could get you some front row tickets? PRIME always reserves seats just in case an employee has family that wants to come. I’ll also throw in some dinner and some merchandise. What do ya say to that?” Killean was being rather generous. He actually was quite fond of the boy and Rebecca, especially Rebecca. “WOW! That would be wicked!” The boy exclaimed and started to jump up and down. Luckily the crowd that was gathered not too far away was too busy with their bags to really make a fuss over the boy’s attention grabbing behavior. “That’s much appreciated Mr., errmmm... Killean. You really don’t have to go through the trouble.” Rebecca sounded very nervous at this point. “Nonsense, I’ll hear none of that.” Killean retorted and smiled. He messed Bobby’s hair again as he spoke. “I’ll see you guys there.” He winked at Rebecca as she nodded in return. She took the hand of her jumping son and turned to walk away. Her hair waved back and forth as she looked at the replica Universal Championship in her son’s hand. Killean found her extremely attractive and had to capitalize on his luck in running into her. ‘I’m a bad man.’ He thought to himself as he grabbed his bag off the conveyer and made his way over to the rental car station. Later that evening, Rebecca was pacing in the lobby of the Fairmont hotel in downtown Winnipeg. She was looking repeatedly at her watch and was talking to herself which made the hotel clerk behind the counter stare at her. “6:15, he isn’t coming. No wonder he didn’t give me a room number.” She mumbled to herself as her strapless black dress swayed with her movement and hugged her figure tightly. She was a very attractive woman and Killean wouldn’t dare stand her up. Sure enough, as she was asking herself if she should leave, the elevator bell rang and the doors opened swiftly. Out stepped Killean and he was dressed to kill. He wore a red silk button up shirt without a tie and left the neck open. He wore a black suit as well. A friggin’ suit! Not bad for the guy who always said he wouldn’t be caught dead in one. His hair was hanging loose and he actually shaved down to his own custom goatee. Of course he was wearing his red sunglasses as it was rather bright outside still and was a tad too bright in the lobby, what with the fluorescent lights and all. He made his way over to Rebecca and pulled his sunglasses down his nose to take a better look. She blushed hard and it was even visible through the little makeup she had on. He returned the sunglasses to their regular resting spot and nodded his approval. “Sorry to keep such a beautiful lady waiting.” He took her hand and pulled her to him and he gave her a small kiss on the cheek. The couple made their way around the various sights in Winnipeg. Having not seen the city in such a long time, Killean felt like a tourist. He had never seen some of the new additions to the city. He was happy to take a long walk through the Exchange District at Waterfront Drive on the banks of the Red River. He remembered back to when he was in the city last and claimed this area was ‘a waste spot which contained the lowest of the society’. But now, the city had taken over and created a beautiful parkland atmosphere. This was also the location where the River Rouge and Paddlewheel Queen were now docked. The boats were available for lunches, dinners and weekend parties on the water of the river. Some new condominiums were in the process of being constructed in the area too, a welcome addition no doubt to help rejuvenate the area. Just a couple blocks away, they then visited Can West Global Park, the rather new 5000 plus seat baseball stadium that houses the Winnipeg Goldeyes of the Northern League. It may be a minor league team but Rebecca had her good words about a favorite pastime in the city for many. “I have been to a few games and I have to say it is a lot of fun.” She went on telling Killean about the stadium and how the Major Leagues are not all they are cracked up to be. “Plenty of the guy who play for our team play for the love of baseball. Not much money is thrown around and when a game is being played, it feels like a family atmosphere. The food is reasonable and the prices are not steep at all. It’s a lot of fun that you should really experience before you leave the city again.” “Maybe I’ll hold you to that.” Killean genuinely smiles as Rebecca giggles. Moving on, Killean received directions on how to get to Broadway. Not like he needed them really, this was the place Killean used to go to look at all the women in tight business suits as they left their workplace for lunch. Plenty of businesses were in the larger office buildings in this epicenter of the city. But something that really caught Sirrajin’s eye was the numerous different polar bears that lined the street on the boulevard and on the property of some parks and large office buildings. “These bears were sculpted and sponsored by many different companies.” She began explaining to him. “This bear over here looks like a truck driver and another to your right looks to be gold and covered with wheat. The wheat board sponsored that one. I hear they have done similar things in Chicago and Calgary with cows and in Toronto with a moose.” Killean was really surprised that a city that looked to be on its way down since he had last visited was well on its way to reinventing itself, making it a staple city in today’s society. Some other small landmarks were visited but nothing impressed the Supreme Machine more than arriving at The Forks Market. Built on an old warehouse ground, the Forks was the major settlement in Winnipeg in the early days. Settlers arranged themselves at this location because the Red and Assiniboine Rivers meet there, hence the name “The Forks”. After the warehouses closed down and vacated, the buildings were restored and inside a Market with restaurants and groceries and many different souvenir shops made themselves at home. Today the Forks is a major tourist attraction with short tours of major landmarks in the city are guiding along the rivers in small boats. Also, restaurants have many patios outside in the summer to sit along the riverbanks. Killean had quickly made his way out towards the riverbank patio that belonged to Branigans, a local restaurant inside the Forks Market. Rebecca and he were greeted and accompanied to the patio where they were shown a little seat overlooking the Assiniboine River. Killean sat across from Rebecca and smiled, holding her hand genuinely in his own. “Rebecca, I have to say this.” Killean spoke, a sincere tone present in his masculine voice. “I honestly don’t know how to thank you for everything that you have shown me today. To come back to my hometown and know next to nothing about it was hard for me. I felt outcast and felt like I didn’t belong. Because of you I now know quite a bit of facts and events that have taken place since I have been gone. I no longer feel like an outcast and I feel accepted here once again and I thank you. You have no idea how much it meant to me to be with you around the city tonight.” Rebecca was mesmerized by Killeans deep voice. Killean was also surprised that he could really be that sincere. He hadn’t spoken that way for quite sometime and it was honestly surreal for him. Rebecca held his hand in hers and spoke back to him in a soft tone. “I am happy that I was able to reacquaint you with your hometown. We are all proud that such a successful person could be from here. You would be seen as somewhat of a role model here. I can only imagine what everyone will be thinking Wednesday night when you show up in front of an arena full of fans. But having said that, I am honored that you asked me to show you around and you have thanked me enough. This wonderful dinner is more than enough thanks.” She was blushing again. Killean smiled because he had taken a liking to her blushing. He had noticed that she did it a lot. But was it because she was happy with Killean or was it because she was nervous around someone of his stature? He really thought nothing of it because he considered himself a normal guy who likes to wrestle. Killean had no special designation for himself in society. He was just another human being. Rebecca smiled again as the waitress approached the couple and began to ask for drinks. They then settled into their seats and after deciding what they wanted to eat, folded their menus and began another conversation. Later that night the couple had returned to the hotel and Killean had then invited Rebecca to stay with him for awhile. After a short deliberation and the offer of an ice cold drink, she gladly accepted. “I do have to be home shortly though. My sitter was only told till Midnight.” Rebecca stated as she accompanied Killean into the elevator. Killean secretly had another concern, which was trying to figure out how much to pay the sitter till the morning. As Rebecca made her way into the washroom to freshen up, Killean took his freshly mixed rum and coke out to the balcony and stood, looking down at the lights of what he thought was a beautiful city. He had always loved his hometown and now that he was back, he really didn’t want to leave. But he also had history in San Diego. Kira was there and she was always fresh in his mind. But she also would have wanted him to move on and do what made him happy. That was the kind of person that she was. She had even expected him to remarry. They had a discussion about what each would do if the unexpected happened and one of them was lost. Killean could never agree to remarry. It just wasn’t in his thoughts. The Supreme Machine was scanning over the city, seeing if he could recognize some landmark locations in the dark. The task proved to be daunting however until his eyes scanned over to his right and found the MTS Center nestled snuggly on Portage Avenue. He himself could only imagine what the atmosphere would be like come Wednesday at ReVolution. He had never wrestled in front of his hometown crowd and he was excited and nervous at the same time. He closed his eyes after taking a sip of his drink and began to imagine the inside of the MTS Center and what it would be like full to the rafters with fans wanting to see ReVolution, fans wanting to see PRIME and fans wanting to see The Universal Champion, Killean Sirrajin. The lights began to flash in his mind as the fans were on their feet with the loudest ovation of the night. His music was playing but was drowned out well by the crazy Winnipeggers. He remembered those kinds of fans at Winnipeg Jets hockey games. In the middle of his daydream, Rebecca made her way out on to the balcony and put her hand on the shoulder of Sirrajin. He snapped back to reality, almost dropping his drink in the process. “Ahhh, thinking about something are we?” Rebecca questioned, knowing full well what he was thinking anyways. But she figured she knew Killean pretty well after today, she just wanted to test herself. She knew what it was… or so she thought. “Well I was just thinking about returning home and wrestling in front of my hometown.” He started slowly, smiling a bit. Rebecca grinned as well; she knew that was what he was thinking. But she didn’t think there was something else running through his mind, perhaps more so at that exact moment. “But I have been running something else through my mind. If you don’t mind my asking, how much are you paying your sitter until midnight?” Rebecca was taken back a little but she kept grinning a little. “Well, she will have been there for 6 hours so I was thinking between forty and fifty dollars. Why do you ask?” Rebecca sounded curious yet cautious at the same time. She had never been asked a question like that by any date before but she also welcomed the forwardness of Killean remembering that anyone she dated in the past was rather shy. “Forty to fifty dollars?” Killean questioned with a confused look on his face. “Well that makes it a little harder for me to figure this out. See I was wondering what you would then pay her for the entire night until sometime tomorrow morning.” If her jaw was not attached to her skull, it would have fallen quite a few stories to the ground below. She was trying to clear the cobwebs and figure out what he just said. She ran the words through her mind about a dozen times before finally finding it in her to respond. “Ummmm… I’m sorry I am just… phew. Ok, I would have to call her and pay her later… but why?” She was really flushed and trying to catch her breath. Just then Killean came closer to her and gave her a short but effective kiss. When their lips parted, she was then sucking in a deep breath and her eyes were rolling into the back of her head it seemed. Killean then spoke to her softly while walking back into the suite. “Call her; tell her two hundred dollars for the night. I’ll take care of it tomorrow… and I’ll take care of you tonight.” Killean was straight forward and to the point as she was now putty in his arms. They disappeared behind the curtain so she could make her phone call… yeah right. Wednesday, June 15, 2005 3:00 PM Fir the first time in his career in wrestling, the Universal Champion has finally set foot in the arena in his hometown. The MTS Center still had the smell of fresh paint… well not really. But since it was finished in November 2004, it needed to be rocked to it’s foundation for the very first time and Killean couldn’t think of a better night. He made his way through the backstage area and came to the entrance ramp area. Dropping his gym bag by the steps, he took the Universal Title and put it on his shoulder. Step by step he was about to see something that he had never seen before. The main bowl of the arena was beautiful according to pictures that he had seen but seeing it in person would be the ultimate rush of adrenaline. Step by step he ascended the base of the stage to the curtain. He stood for a moment before splitting the two curtains and taking his first look into the main bowl of the MTS Center. It was something that affected him emotionally and it almost brought a tear to his eye. He was standing in the exact middle of his hometown and he was standing in the one place that he would be later that night during the main event Universal Title chair match. As he looked into the empty seats and made his way down the aisle, he was definitely thinking of Vangelus Olsig. Yes that was his name now. Vangelus looked like a whole new person last week when he was making his case with Hin See for a rematch against the Champion. He was confident, but then again who wouldn’t be after going the distance against Killean Sirrajin? But that was neither here nor there. Why did it take facing Killean for Dan… erm Vangelus to stand up for himself? He basically told the Dark Age where to go and he was now a loose cannon. ‘Dani… Vangelus, whichever you prefer. Remember that just because I have given you the chance to become a champion, doesn’t mean you will. I respect you and I respect you for telling it how it is in regards to the Dark Age. But remember, you have just stepped out. You need to reinvent yourself and complete the turn around. You need to show the world that you belong. I will be ready come main event time and the chairs will be thrown, the blood will be poured and the tears will be shed. You will not only be in the ring with the Supreme Machine this time. You will be in the ring with the immortal himself, the Universal Champion. Immortality beckoned Vangelus and I answered the call. The powers that be have spoken and I am the immortal being and I will not be dethroned. My legacy is to hold this title for as long as my soul feels. Let’s give them a show, but a show is all it will be. There will be no upsets, just a lesson and perhaps, sadly, some disappointment.’ He was thinking it and he swore that Vangelus Olsig heard him. A shiver may be flowing down the challenger’s spine and damnit; the boy will realize what he asked for tonight when a chair is bent over his head repeatedly. As Killean found the ringside area, he looked over to the seats that he had reserved for Rebecca and Bobby. They would be there cheering him on as the rest of Winnipeg would be doing. He began to climb the steps and the environment felt like it was closing in on him. He felt like the fans were surrounding him, chanting his name as he stood in the ring. He stepped through the ropes just as he was imagining the pyro going off in his head. A Perfect Circle’s Passive was playing in his head as well as he looked into the empty seats, thinking the fans were there. He was in awe at absolutely nothing at the moment except for his own thoughts. He thought about the crowd, the music, the announcer practically immortalizing him as the hometown hero by introducing him from Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. He was seeing Vangelus standing across from him, looking straight into his eyes and telling him with the facial expression that he deserved to be there with the Champ. He saw… He saw someone reaching out and tapping him on the shoulder. It was a ring technician who was no doubt there to do some final checks on the ring. But he was also trying to get The Supreme Machine’s attention. “Sorry Mr. Sirrajin but you have a guest in your dressing room. I was just sent out here to…” The tech named Cory stopped and backed up a little when Killean looked directly at him, burning a hole through the young mans eyes with his own. He pulled down the sunglasses and looked at the boy, looking for who it could be that was sitting back there right now during his night. This would be his night and he would not let anyone ruin it. He then slipped the glasses back onto his face and made his way out of the ring, leaving the dreams in the ring to blossom into real life later that special night. He showed up only mere minutes later in front of his own locker room door and the individual who interrupted his time in the ring to think. He had asked not to be disturbed unless it was important. It had to be something serious otherwise Cory would be running through the halls, trying to escape the silver baseball bat he had tucked away inside his coat. He stepped to the door and took the knob in his hand and wasted no time in turning it. The door opened swiftly but Killean stopped just as swiftly as he saw the person who was there, literally sitting in front of him. The wheelchair bound man was in his electronic wheelchair with a smile on his face. He was the man who was Killeans mentor in wrestling. He was the man who helped lead him into the Underground. He was the man who ran him up the ladder towards the Immortality Title in the Underground. He was… “Jin Daikusa…” Killean hissed, shutting the door behind him, leaving everyone else out in the cold.
