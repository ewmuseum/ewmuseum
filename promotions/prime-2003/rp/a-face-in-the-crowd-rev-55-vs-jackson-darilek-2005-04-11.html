A Face in the Crowd? (ReV 55, vs. Jackson Darilek)

11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/11/05 “For when you need maximum protec…um…ya know, I’m...I’m having a difficult time really getting into the script here. I, uh…yeah, I just don’t think I’m really the guy for the job. Thanks for your time.” - Nova, on the set of shooting for a Kotex Maximum Protection tampons commercial Nova stumbles back against the ropes, holding his gloves over the sides of his head to block against the relentless head-shots…and pays for it as he feels his opponent’s fist collide with his ribs. “Hmmmrrphh!” The mouthpiece muffles his scream, and his arm shoots down to cover his side. Without pause, his opponent swings wide, cleaning Nova’s clock and sending him stumbling backward against the turnbuckle. Nova can’t see much of anything anymore, his eyelids swollen shut as he desperately attempts to defend himself. The turnbuckle post is death…he has to get away. Nova stumbles out into the middle of the ring, tears pouring down his face as he fights the pain in his eyes from trying to open them wide enough to find his bearings. WHAM!! No need. His opponent had enough for both of them. Nova hits the mat hard, spitting out blood with his mouthpiece, a white circle around his temple fresh from the right hand of his opponent that’s slowly beginning to turn pink. Nova sees his opponent walk away through the slit of his right eye. He sees his opponent climb out of the ring, take off his gloves…and grab a steel chair. As his opponent calmly climbs back into the ring, all Nova can think about… …is the smell. What the hell is that God-awful smell? Nova’s eyes open and he shoots upright…but the doctor catches him. “Steady! Steady there…you’re alright. You’re alright I used smelling salts to wake you.” Nova looks around…and sees PRIME backstage staff busily attending to their posts, all trying to make the Pay-Per-View finish up as smoothly as possible. Fleet Center, Boston, MA, the night of Ultra Violence… “Now hold still,” the doctor says authoritatively. Nova takes a deep breath and complies, trying to relax his muscles as he lies back on the bench. “Ya know, I work all the PRIME shows…I must’ve fixed you up, what…gosh…” “I’d reckon around four times,” Nova says bitterly, the tone in his voice indicating that there’s no “reckoning” about it. “That’s right!” the doctor replies jovially, satisfied with his recognition of the number, “four times…wow…” The doctor picks a few pieces of glass and plastic out of Nova’s hair, careful to tread gingerly around the bloody oval of ripped skin and blood. Nova grits his teeth, letting out only a “tsssss” sound when the doctor’s tweezers come too close to the wound. “If you don’t mind my asking,” the doctor continues, “is there something about you that puts people off? I mean, I’ve been cleaning you off the floor every other week for a while now…” “Doc, if I knew the answer to that, you probably wouldn’t be picking glass out of my hair,” Nova replies humorlessly. “True,” the doctor concedes, “well, that looks like the big hunks. You can run a fine-tooth comb through the rest later and get out what’s left, but don’t shower tonight. I need to put a bandage over the wound that must stay on for twelve hours to make sure there’s no infection…after that, it’s just a migraine headache for a day or two, and we can give you something for that.” The doctor puts a salve over a bandage and pins sections of Nova’s hair back to expose the wound. He places the self-stick bandage over the injured area as Nova lets out a muffled grunt. “Leave the pins in your hair to hold it back from the bandage for a while, alright?” Nova nods as he gently slides his feet around to the same side of the bench and sits fully upright, the world spinning blurrily as he does. It feels like someone is pouring warm water over his head, only it hurts too, the pain unsettling his stomach. “Here’s something for the pain,” the doctor says, handing Nova a prescription bottle, “I’ve gotta go take care of other business. I’ll see you later.” “Hopefully not,” Nova grumbles in response. The doctor begins to walk away, and then turns back. “That noggin of yours has taken quite a beating recently. Try to take it easy, eh?” “I’ll be sure to tell everyone to downsize from the chairs and bats to Sock ‘Em Boppers and Super Soakers,” Nova replies with a roll of his eyes, and the doctor chuckles before walking away. Ugh…fuck. Nova feels down inside the band of his pants and grins as he grabs hold of a pack of cigarettes. He flips back the top of the box, removes his lighter and a cigarette, and re-secures the pack. He lights his cigarette and dangles his feet back and forth on the bench, letting blood circulation return to normal before hopping off the bench. He had seen guys collapse in the hallways of arenas after sustaining moderate blood loss because they wouldn’t sit still for five minutes after getting patched up. Nova finishes his cigarette and slowly slides his feet down from the bench onto the ground. He stands up wearily and taps a passing PRIME road crew manager on the shoulder. “Hey, boss…you know where the pisser is located around here?” “Take the first right after the coffee spill,” the manager replies, pointing down the hall a ways. “Much obliged,” Nova says with a painful nod before following his directions. He stops in front of the “coffee spill” on the wall, reflecting Nova’s approximate position when Vetra Nolan Ryan’ed the coffee machine at Nova’s dome. Opening the door, Nova stops in his tracks, noticing the mirror hanging in front of the sink is broken. A large shard lies intact on the ground, and Nova carefully steps over it as he goes to drain the lizard. “Ahhh…hoo, boy.” The sounds of satisfaction. “Hey.” Nova nearly jumps at the sound of another voice. “Damn, man…didn’t hear you come in.” “Hey, chump,” the voice says again. “Chump?” Nova echoes as he pulls up his tights and turns around, “if I didn’t have a busted-up…” He stops as he realizes no one is there. “What the fuck? Where the hell did he go?” “Down here, asshole,” the voice mocks. Nova peers around until movement catches his eye. Something is flickering in the broken mirror shard on the ground. Upon closer inspection, Nova gasps. It’s his reflection…and it’s waving at him. “Yeah, there ya go. ‘Bout time, genius.” “What the…,” Nova murmurs. “Yep, it’s your reflection, and it’s talking to ya,” Nova’s reflection says, with a slowness that suggests condescension, “and right now it’s got some advice for ya.” “Ad-advice?” Nova repeats incredulously, still in utter disbelief. “Damn right,” the reflection replies with a satisfied nod, “get outta PRIME, man.” “Get out of PRIME?” Nova says, raising an eyebrow. “What are you, in playback mode?” Nova’s reflection fires back scornfully, “yeah, I said get out of PRIME, Jesus…” “Why the hell would I do that?” Nova replies, “I love it here.” “Oh, oh, you love it here, excuse me,” Nova’s reflection says sarcastically, “well, pardon me for raining on the love parade, but my advice stands. Get out of here, Nova. Go back to NEW or something…” “NEW?” Nova repeats before catching himself and quickly adding, “why would I go back to NEW?” “If you went back to NEW, you could be World Champion in two months or less,” his reflection answers, “you’d be one of the top guys. It’s more your…your league.” “My league?!” Nova replies angrily, not caring that he’s still repeating everything his reflection is saying, “whaddaya mean, my league? I can hang here!” “Can you?” his reflection prompts, “you really think so? Let me enlighten you, sir. You’re shit here. You’re…” “Wait a second,” Nova interrupts, “I’m still stuck on the ‘not my league’ thing…I’m seven and two. That’s nothing to sniff your nose…” “Cut that seven and two crap,” his reflection interjects, “substantial victories? You’ve beaten Big Poppa, a chump too far gone in his madness to remember what the Poppa Droppa looks like…” “Tony Rolo! Jon Kano!” Nova protests, “that’s just not true! I’ve stood toe-to-toe with…” “Save me the clichés, I deserve better,” his reflection cuts in again, “so you’re the master of the cluster-fuck match. Congratulations. Those are your ‘big’ wins, right? The TLC match and the Wetherlution Invitational? BIG DEAL! Those matches are half-luck anyway…it’s all about being in the right place in the right time.” “I set a record,” Nova offers. “Oh, right…which one was it?” his reflection ponders mockingly, “longest winning streak? Most titles held? Hmm…oh, right! ‘Superstar Who Won A Title In the Least Amount of Matches,’ right? What an accolade! I guess maybe you forgot about the whole completely random match assignment thing…ie ANYONE COULD HAVE GOTTEN THAT MATCH, YOU CHODE! It was luck! Luck that secured for you your precious 5-Star Title reign of two months. Refresh my memory…what was your first official title defense?” “You know what my first official title defense was,” Nova growls. “No, no I don’t,” the reflection replies innocently, “what was it? I must’ve missed that show.” “Hoyt Williams,” Nova mutters. “I’m sorry, what? I couldn’t hear you,” his reflection replies, cupping a hand to his ear. “Hoyt Williams, for fuck’s sake,” Nova answers angrily. “Ah, right, Mr. Williams,” Nova’s reflection echoes, scratching his beard, “a worthy competitor, no?” “I would guess so,” Nova says, fumbling in his tights for his cigarettes. “You challenged him, right?” his reflection asks. “Yes, I did,” Nova admits, his irritation with this embarrassing interrogation growing by the second. “Sorta wanted to shut his mouth, didn’t ya?” his reflection says with a faux-sympathetic grin, “wanted to take all that irritating smack talk and cram it up his ass, didn’t ya?” “I suppose I did,” Nova says with a sigh. “Hmmm…didn’t work out that way, did it?” Nova’s reflection asks rhetorically. “Is there a point to all this?” Nova replies, the level of his voice raising. “After that, you were made number one contender to the 5-Star Title, weren’t you?” his reflection ignores him, continuing, “I mean, that’s usually what happens…the former champions get to be number one contenders after they lose, sort of like a last hurrah or something, I guess. But you never got a match, did you?” Nova doesn’t answer, mostly because he knows his reflection will keep talking anyway. “Yeah, you didn’t,” his reflection continues, “in fact, without ever having a contendership match, you lost your status to Ozric Mortimer. Interesting. So basically, the only title you’ve come within a hundred yards of since joining the ranks SIX MONTHS AGO is the one shot at the 5-Star Title that you drew out of a hat! Ha, ha, ha, ha!” Nova flicks his cigarette into the toilet and runs a hand through his hair, wincing as he comes close to his wound. “Ha, ha, ha!” his reflection guffaws, “look at you! You’re a mess! You’ve done nothing but beat mid-carders and get the shit kicked out of you since you got here! Ah-ha, ha, ha! You’re a gimmick wrestler, man! They’re hiding your crappy card placement with gimmick matches! ‘Face in the Crowd Match?’ You are a fucking face in the crowd! You don’t mean shit to anyone, and worse, when you’re gone, no one is going to remember you! You don’t even have time to establish yourself, even if you could rise above the upper mid-card! Your leg is a fucking ticking time-bomb! You…” “Wait, what?” Nova interrupts, “my leg? What about my leg?” “Ask me again in three months!” his reflection cries gleefully, “oh, this is too good! Too good! A fucking face in the crowd! A face in the…” “SHUT THE FUCK UP!” Nova yells before extending his elbow and dropping, “die!!” THUMP. “Ohhh…oww…” “Ha, ha, ha! Ohhh, ha, ha, ha!” his reflection cackles as Nova lies next to the mirror shard holding his elbow, “you missed! You missed! I can’t believe it…this is classic! You fu-” “Fuck you,” Nova groans, rolling over and punching the mirror shard with his fist, shattering it. Nova lies on the floor of the bathroom, panting, staring at the fragments of mirror now wonderfully silent…and then he looks down at his hand. “AAHHHHHH!!!” Nova stumbles down the hall, his hand bleeding profusely. “Doc! Yo, Doc!” The doctor who attended to him earlier turns around. “Hello, Nova…I was just…holy crap! I said I’d see you later, but I didn’t think it would be…well, nevermind. What the hell happened?” “I…uh…I fell down some stairs,” Nova says sheepishly, feeling like a Mob boss’s wife. “Right…,” the doctor says, rolling his eyes, “tell me, Nova…are other people in PRIME this…weird?” Nova takes a moment to prepare a thoughtful response. “Well, Doc, that depends…does a deifying egomaniacal complex count as ‘weird?’” “Yes,” the good doctor replies. “Then yes, there are,” Nova says with a grin, “yes, there certainly are.” … His hand wrapped in gauze, Nova sits on the steps to a side loading entrance to the Fleet Center. A face in the crowd… …I can’t accept that. I won’t accept that. I have to believe that the time will come when I get my shot…I have to. I have to believe that this isn’t all for nothing. I had two goals when I joined PRIME…two goals that I promised myself I would never tell anyone until they had been achieved. I still have them…and I will make them realities. It’s what keeps me going. It’s what makes me keep coming back and suffering through the backstage attacks and the back-breaking match-ups. It’s how I justify leaving my wife and daughter to fly around the country and get the crap beaten out of me and leave my mother-fuckin’ soul in that ring every week. I have my dreams just like everyone else, and I’m entitled to believe that I can achieve them, whatever the fuck my reflection in some broken piece of mirror tries to tell me. I’ll have mine, or I’ll kill myself in the process, because if I can’t make it here, then I just can’t make it, and I refuse to acquiesce to mediocrity. It’s selfish, and bastardly, and completely irresponsible… …but I feel it’s what makes me more than just a face in the crowd. “It’s all happening!” - Penny Lane, Almost Famous
