There were few people in the First Class cabin with him, but those that were exchanged quick whispers between themselves, each sparing a glance in his direction when it could be afforded. Tyler Rayne remained unconscious, oblivious to the hushed excitement around him. These moments were too few and far between. Plane rides were one of two places where he felt comfortable enough to sleep. True sleep. The man had traveled the globe a dozen times over. Maybe more. He had slept in some of the most expensive and luxurious hotel rooms in the world. Owned some magnificent properties on the most coveted beaches. Hell, he'd even slept in a Mid Eastern palace once. Of course there were the low sides, too. Nights spent huddled on the cold stone of a Turkish prison. Holes dug in the jungle floor. Freezing in the cold nights of the desert. Once he'd even been forced to use a moose as a tauntaun when he got stuck out in the Canadian wilderness. Point being, Tyler had slept just about anywhere and everywhere a man in this world could think to rest his head. For years, though, damn near twenty of them, he'd been running from one thing or another. There were always threats lurking in the shadows. Ninjas. Sociopaths claiming to be vampires. Jilted one night stands. Matt Mills. He could never allow himself to get too comfortable and was always sleeping with one eye open. One hand on the gun beneath his pillow. Unless he was on a plane. There was nowhere to hide on a plane. No shadows to lurk in, no space to hide that wasn't filled to overflowing with some old couples' vacation bags. Planes were the damn near the safest place he could think of. So he took advantage of these opportunities to get some true rest. That didn't, however, mean he was dead to the world. He woke quickly, though remained in complete control of himself so as not to look like he had woke. Eyes remained closed. Breathing remained even.
