Team VIAGRA Presents : The Invisible Price Line

15 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-15-08 High Flyer: Why do we always do this? (FADE IN. Luxurious living room of the Harmen "estate." Jack is pacing over the hand woven rug, as Tony Davis ignores him, playing Nintendo DS in the corner. High Flyer stands next to a computer, which is set up on a small table, almost looking like a tv tray.) High Flyer: I mean, why do we ALWAYS wait until the last minute? Can't we ever NOT procrastinate? (Flyer slams his hands onto the table, shattering it in the process, and causing a loud crash of computer electronics. They clatter to the floor as Tony sighs.) Tony Davis: It's not my fault. High Flyer: Oh. Oh, I see. It's not your fault. How could I have been so blind?! It's someone else's fault, of course. I'm sorry I almost yelled at you. Because, you know... I FUCKING TOLD YOU LAST WEEK... but NOOooooooOOOO. (mocking)Look at me. I'm Tony fucking Davis. I'm a complete fucking retard who doesn't listen to anything going around me. If I were in war, I'd be treated like a deaf person. Please help me. Guide me with a walking stick! Tony Davis: Dude. Harsh. (Tony shuts the lid on his DS close.) Tony Davis: Like you're without fault. Cast the first stone and you're house will be condemned. High Flyer: What does that even mean! You say shit like that like it means something. Revenge is a porkchop best served microwaved? THE FUCK MAN. How do you have a wife and I don't? Tony Davis: Cause you're too jealous of my wife. High Flyer: That's my sister. I'm not jealous of my sister. Tony Davis: Maybe you just wish the relationship you had with Kate was like the one me and your sister got. Eh? Eh? I hit a sore spot, didn't I. Like on a bruised orange. Man, at least you're not ruined like that orange would be, eh? High Flyer: Tony. Tony Davis: Yes Jack. I'm right here. (Tony leans back, flipping through a magazine now, just to annoy the Lunatic. Flyer sits with his arms crossed, tapping his feet impatiently.) High Flyer: Did we cross a line with each other? Tony Davis: I think so. But I just moved the line elsewhere. High Flyer: Good... I just... I've been waiting for this. Tony Davis: I know. High Flyer: I have very little joy in my life. Granted, we have the shiny tag team titles, but there's only so many times you can look at them without defending them before they lose their sparkle. Tony Davis: That's cause you gotta polish it. (Flyer ignores Tony.) High Flyer: If this were the old days, I'd start a war that would echo throughout eternity. As it stands, I'd much rather just raise my fist and curse their name. (Flyer promptly raises his fist.) High Flyer: Damn you fandango. Being sold out of Batman tickets. I DAMN YOU TO HELL! (Flyer screams, turning beat red. He falters back to a sitting position.) Tony Davis: Okay then. Less crazy now? (Flyer glares at Tony, and crosses his arm.) Tony Davis: Let's just move that invisible line one more time... (Flyer and Tony promptly return to silence. Fade out.)
