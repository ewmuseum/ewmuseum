SUX Flashback Numero Dos!

15 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/15/2005 The first promo as part of a four way challenge with three teams from Lights Out Wrestling. This is the first time White Tiger appears in a PTC promo. Pajaro de Presa's name is correctly spelled as "Pájaro" here, eventually the accent would be dropped out of laziness. The Masked Monkeys in... You Got Therved! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Starring... The Masked Monkeys... White Tiger and Pájaro de Presa! Like many great cities across the country and all over the world, Chicago has its famous landmarks. The Sears Tower. Wrigley Field. That barn where that cow started that fire that one time. And the UWF Arena. Yes, the UWF Arena, that ultramodern architectural wonder synonymous with the Universal Wrestling Federation. Probably because it’s named after them. Besides being a frequent venue for their events, the UWF also has its offices at the arena. And like all facilities of its stature, the UWF Arena has something else… dumpsters. It is outside one of these dumpsters that we find UWF jobber, I mean, superstar White Tiger. Holding a package and some letters, he seems to be looking for something. Or someone. White Tiger: Pájaro! Hola, Pájaro! You got mail! Hey! There is no answer from anywhere around or inside the dumpster. White Tiger keeps looking and spots a note taped to the back of the dumpster. White Tiger: “Amigo, I’m at the gym training. Pájaro” Having discovered his partner’s whereabouts, White Tiger heads inside the arena to go to the gym located within. Reaching his destination, White Tiger sees Pájaro de Presa by the treadmills. He is giving words of encouragement to a duck waddling on a treadmill. Pájaro: C’mon, Plucky! Feel the burn! Plucky: Quack! White Tiger: What are you doing? Turning towards the new voice, Pájaro de Presa sees his partner and gives him a big dumb grin. Pájaro: Hey! I got Plucky training for his big cockfighting debut! He’s gonna kick booty! White Tiger: Don’t forget we have a big match of our own against those three teams from LOW. Pájaro: Don’t worry, amigo! We got it made! We’re the Greatest Enhancement Tag Team in PTC! Plucky! You’re not slowing down on me, are you? Plucky: Quack! Pájaro; I can’t believe you just said that! Don’t you know there’s no “I” in “quit!” White Tiger: Err… actually, there is. Pájaro: Hmmmm… hey! You’re right! What was I thinking of? White Tiger: Maybe that there’s no “I” in “team.” Pájaro: That does make more sense. So whatcha got there? White Tiger: A package for you. Taking the package from White Tiger, Pájaro de Presa is thrilled when he sees where it’s from. Pájaro: At last! It’s here! Plucky, go ahead and hit showers! I got other business to take care of! Plucky: Quack! Hopping off the treadmill, Plucky the Cockfighting Duck heads towards the showers. Pájaro de Presa tears open the package like a kid on Christmas. Inside is a plastic doll that looks somewhat familiar. Pájaro: Sweet! It’s my Karina Wolfenden action figure! I’ve been waiting weeks for this! White Tiger: What are you going to do with that? Pájaro: Keep it in my pants forever and ever! White Tiger: But you don’t have any pockets. Pájaro: Don’t need ‘em! With that, Pajaro de Presa shoves his Karina Wolfenden action figure down the front of his tights. Then he starts gyrating his pelvis in a circle. Pájaro: That’s right… feeeeeeeeeeeel… the MACHISMO! Oh, Karina! That’s quite the kung-fu grip you have there! White Tiger buries his face in his hands and shakes his head. Pájaro: You know, this reminds of that match we had against Clique Clique BOOM and those other teams a few weeks back. White Tiger: Really? I can’t remember anything about that other than Clique Clique BOOM winning. Pájaro: Now that you mention it, neither do I. SPOOKY~! But at least we can beat those LOWlife teams we got coming up. How can a pirate and a clown compete against us? Pirates are cool and clowns suck! There’s no chemistry there! White Tiger: I really don’t know how to argue with you there. Pájaro: Exactly! It makes perfect sense! White Tiger: I didn’t really mean it like that. Pájaro: And those other teams, the Flying Whoevers and A Something or Other. We own them. White Tiger: You don’t know their names? Nice scouting, champ. Pájaro: Well, I don’t get the Internet in my dumpster. But how can the Greatest Enhancement Talent Tag Team in PTC lose? White Tiger: Because that’s what we do! Enhancement talent loses! Pájaro: Details! The Masked Monkeys will stand victorious! White Tiger: I don’t think the “Masked Monkeys” will be standing anywhere soon. Pájaro: What are you saying? Did Richard Hayes get us comfy chairs? I could use a comfy chair in the dumpster. All I got is a busted car seat. White Tiger: Remember that lawyer, Johnny Cockroach? The one working for the Gay and Lesbian Association of Monkeys? Pájaro: Uhhhh… not really. White Tiger: The insect with the injunction preventing us from calling ourselves the Masked Monkey because we’re not real monkeys. Pájaro: Oh yeah. He was cute. White Tiger: Well, do you think it’s cute that we have to go to court and face Johnny Cockroach? Pájaro: Actually, seeing that little cockroach in court would be kinda cute. White Tiger He wants to permanently ban us from using the name! White Tiger hands an open envelope to his partner. It contains a summons to appear in court. Pájaro: Ohhhhhhhh… that’s not good. White Tiger: Thank you for joining us on Planet Earth! Pájaro: Hey, I thought you hated being called a Masked Monkey? White Tiger: I do! It’s a stupid name! But it’s the name people know us by! That’s the name we got booked in that match with those LOW teams with! Pájaro: Plus I just got those bowling shirts made for us. White Tiger: How cold you afford to get bowling shirts made? Pájaro: I have my ways. By the way, can I borrow some money for bowling shirts? White Tiger: Get serious. We need to find a lawyer. Pájaro: I know just the man! Come with me! White Tiger: Why do I have that report card day feeling in my stomach? Dragging White Tiger by the arm, Pájaro de Presa heads out of the gym. The luchadores head down a hallway, stopping at a door marked “REFEREE DRESSING ROOM.” White Tiger: What are we doing here? Pájaro: We need a man who is tough under pressure. A man that will stand up to anyone for what he belives in. A man who has a mind like a steel trap. A man who will navigate us safely through the treacherous waters of the legal system like… I dunno, Boba Fett’s dad flying through those asteroids or something. White Tiger: You actually saw Episode II? Pájaro: Dude! Natalie Portman in a belly shirt! White Tiger: True… but back to this man you’re talking about... Pájaro: I give you our attorney, the sharpest intellect in the UWF… senior referee Jerry “Pops” Kohler! Pájaro de Presa throws open the door. There stands an old man in a referee’s shirt. Just a referee’s shirt. “Pops”: Have you guys seen my pants? White Tiger: Madre de dios… To be continued…
