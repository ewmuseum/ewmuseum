Schrödinger's Cat: Chapter 1

20 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20th, 2007 It was one strange world he found himself in. Everything seemed familiar but at the same time, completely alien to him. His eyelids felt heavy as he tried to make sense of everything around him. He began to feel around with his hands. The mattress was soft and the sheets silky smooth. The light of the sun hit him directly in the face, which is what was causing his woe. He was able to sit up. For some reason he could not remember what he was doing before he fell asleep or even where he had been. It felt like his head was splitting in half. After a few seconds, he was finally able to take his surroundings in. It was a generic room. Nothing that really set it apart, except for a sole picture on a mirror that was set up opposite the bed. His vision wasn’t good enough to see the picture. But he was able to discern a bottle of water and two pills in the table right besides him. He grabbed the pills and swallowed them without water. His throat was parched though, so he opened the bottle of water and took a sip. After a few minutes of sitting there, he was finally able to stand up. The air smelled strangely of sulfur, as if a piece of hell had appeared on Earth. He grabbed the picture on the frame of the mirror and saw himself in it with another person right besides him. Their smiles where joyful and whatever it was that they were doing, it seemed like a great time. On the backside of the picture there was a date written. 8 de enero de 2000. Antonio y Burton en el show. Trying to remember in the state he was in was too much. He recognized the date, it was his own birthday. The man he was standing behind was his best friend since high school, Burton. They had passed through thick and thin in their lives and they would continue to do so until the end. The memories came flashing back. There was no memory of that birthday though. There was a sense telling him that there was something going on. He still had no idea where he was or how he got there and he needed answers. Antonio pushed the door to the hallway and realized that it had been slightly ajar. It all seemed surreal as he walked down the hallway and saw all the different pictures of himself with people he didn’t remember meeting. As he looked around and made sense of everything, he finally knew were he was. It was his house. He recognized the little things that people normally don’t notice but the owner knows. There was a certain picture of himself with someone and he moved it a little bit and found a hole he had made trying to hammer the nail that held the picture. It was definitely his house. When he finally reached the dining room, he heard a voice. “Ah! Finally, you are up.” The man stood up and pointed Antonio towards a chair. “I’ve been waiting for you to wake up for quite a while,” he said as he sat back down. “I hope the accommodations were to your liking. I didn’t think there would be a place more comfortable than your own bed,” he said with a laugh. Antonio was looking straight at the man that he had seen before in the picture, except that there was something that differentiated him from the Burton he remembered. Maybe it was an air about him of dominance and self confidence that he had never seen before. Antonio wondered how he could have changed so quickly and so deeply. He walked towards the chair and sat down. There was food on a plate in front of him, but he ignored it. His stomach wasn’t already agreeing with him and he didn’t want to upset it again. “What happened?” he asked. “Don’t worry about what happened just yet. There is much that must be explained,” he said while trying to chew on a piece of bacon that was on his plate. Burton’s voice was cold and distant but there was a hint of humor in it as well. “You know, it’s been a while since we sat at this table together. You left us so abruptly back then. It was very bad.” Antonio couldn’t help but feel more confused than ever. With every word that came out of Burton’s mouth, Antonio’s bewilderment grew. “I left? Where? I don’t have any memory of ever leaving you or anyone.” “You died.” Antonio’s jaw dropped. It surely couldn’t be true. He was there. He was breathing and he had a pulse… he could feel it. There was no way he could be a ghost. “I… it can’t be… how is it possible?” he asked no one in particular. He put his forehead in his hands and sighed deeply. “What is going on?” “It is not that hard to explain. You made the jump. Well, in a way, I might have had a hand on you making the jump, but you first have to hear my proposition,” Burton told Antonio. “I know it might be a bit difficult for you to understand, but I have a machine that creates what are called ‘soft spots’ in the fabric of space and time,” he said as he continued to eat his breakfast. “So, I’m in some sort of future world? That still doesn’t explain my death.” Antonio lifted his head from his hands and looked at Burton as he devoured the plate of food in front of him. “Your death is easy to explain, but I will get to that one in a minute.” Burton took a sip of orange juice. “You are not in a future world, but in a parallel world. Basically, you are in the same time, just a parallel universe. As for your death, I meant that the Antonio Vargas of this world died a few years ago.” “What makes you so sure? Maybe I am just me and you are just mistaken,” Antonio said trying to reach for straws. “I should know. I killed you.” Antonio stood up and backed away against the wall in sudden fear. “This can’t be… it just doesn’t make any sense. Keep away from me.” The mix of the drugs he had been given, the confusion and the grogginess made him feel like shit. He fell to a seated position with his back against the wall. “You know, this gives me an unprecedented opportunity,” Burton said as he got up and approached Antonio. “When I killed you, there was really no pleasure to it. I wasn’t like one of those villains in movies that put you in some weird contraption and then explained the whole thing to you,” he told Antonio as he sat in front of the man he was, in a way, torturing. “I didn’t get to tell you the reason why I shot you in the head.” “Stop. Please. What do you want from me?” Antonio pleaded. “I shot you because I was tired of you always being the center of attention. I was tired of being in your shadow in everything that mattered. You were always the man. I was just that guy who hung out with you.” He stroked his black beard as he counted the reasons for killing him. “You never let me shine.” At that moment, Antonio’s laughter was loud. There was a madness in his eyes as he heard what Burton had to say. “You killed me because you were jealous?” Antonio’s amusement continued to grow. “That is probably the most pathetic thing I have heard in a long time,” he said as he stood up. Burton didn’t waste any time, he took out his handgun and stood up. He aimed it at Antonio’s head. “I can just kill you again. You have no idea how much satisfaction that would give me,” Burton threatened. Antonio smiled at his former friend. “You know, I think your failures were more your own fault than mine.” “I don’t think it matters anymore. You are dead and you will do whatever I want. Before, you were the man. Now, I am the man,” he said as he grabbed a cowboy hat that was sitting in a chair and put it on. Antonio didn’t believe that he was in another universe at all. He didn’t believe what Burton was saying about parallel universes and machines and all that stuff. It wasn’t possible. He also knew that Burton would never kill him. He walked towards the door. “Where are you going?” Burton asked. “Outside. I need some fresh air. Your lame stories left me completely exhausted,” Antonio answered as he opened the front door to the house. When he did, it was as if he had been punched in the stomach. “Welcome to my world, Antonio. In this world… I am King.”
