The Half-Assed Reconstruction of Mr. Silver (USE THIS ONE!)

5 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/5/07 Oh, so you were expecting to open up this roleplay and see some whack-ass adventure from Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME, were you? You were really thinking… SERIOUSLY… really thinking that after that EMBARRASSING loss to Danny Ferguson, he was actually going to show his face around these parts and dazzle you with some hilariously horrible ripoff of some event going on in World Wrestling Entertainment? What a load of crap. You know, he wouldn’t even HAVE a job right now if Vince McMahon didn’t exist. Seriously. It’s a load of bullshit. I – Sonny Silver, his past self that he left dead in a desert in that piece of shit roleplay he posted at the very last minute – am going to be running this shit, not Mr. Silver, Kennedy-microphone-ripping-off-ass-bitch. See, he’s gone and tarnished my good name for the longest time. I mean, the guy’s only had ONE belt in the year he’s been here… ONE! An Intense Title reign consisting of messing with some black guy for two months. And instead of curbstomping him like I would’ve, he went and lost the belt to him two months later and that guy lost the belt to Devin Shakur in HIS first defense. Now why can’t Sonny be like THAT guy? That guy goes around, beats up whoever he wants and rubs their face in the dirt to improve his marquee. I mean, guy, rip off somebody GOOD. Now this would be the part of the roleplay where he decides that he’s going to learn some inner lesson about himself, kill somebody, watch a loved one commit suicide or go on some wacky adventure that has little to nothing to do with facing off against a woman named Jasmine Drogan. What I’m going to be doing here is telling you millions of reasons as to why Jasmine Drogan is going to get the beating of a lifetime. No fancy tricks, no gimmicks, no nothing. Old school, trash-talk at its finest. So now, Sonny Silver proudly brings to you: THE TOP TEN REASONS WHY JASMINE DROGAN WON’T AMOUNT TO SHIT AGAINST ME ON THE NEXT REVOLUTION 1. Sonny Silver has amassed many wins over girls and has only lost once to a woman, but it was Lindsay Troy so the loss to a "woman" is still very much debatable to this day. And he beat her in a singles match way back when, so he’s already got a singles match victory over the PRIME Universal Champion. He fought back from the forces of death to beat up a crazy Asian girl that jabbed a stake into his head to win the match. He recently played an integral part in kicking PRIME’s sole female Hall of Famer out of here and he ended the winning streak of the girl that kicked HER out of PRIME, thus the chain of command is as follows. Sonny Silver > [Your Name Here, if You’re a Chick. Actually, guys, too.] 2. It’s a proven fact that Sonny’s great grandfather, Richard Silver the first, was the actual founder of the He-Man Woman Haters Club and not the Little Rascals. And the original purpose of the He-Man Woman Haters Club was not actually to hate on girls who have cooties, but girls who have STDs. Jasmine Drogan herself is a card-carrying member of the "I have more STDs than Paris Hilton and Nicole Richie COMBINED" Club, so she is Public Enemy #1 in our club. By Public Enemy, I don’t mean Flavor Flav’s punk faggot ass. That faggot’s so broke, he couldn’t afford a freestyle. I’ll let you guys think about that joke while I move onto number three. 3. As a n00b, Jasmine Drogan’s place on the food chain of this mighty jungle called PRIME is as such: N00b < Easton Hall < Fans < Janitor < Guy running the ticket counter < Concession Stand worker < Sellers of Alcohol < Ticket Scalpers < the wrestlers < Lindsay Troy < Me. 4. While Jasmine Drogan’s trying to make up her mind as to which way she’s going to come at me off the top rope, I’ll have used my superior technical acumen to detach her legs from the rest of her body so that way doing these croo-zer-wayt maneuvers is a non-factor in what will be my absolute and total dominance. She prides herself on being a brawler, but honestly, who’s going to win in a slugfest: The five-foot-nothing woman or a 6’3", 250-pound man with several years experience dabbling in Mixed Martial Arts? Do the math, kids. 5. The odds of Miss Drogan making it out of ReVolution alive – let alone a win over myself -- are comparable to winning the lottery seven times, back-to-back or Karina Wolfenden actually sticking to one hair color instead of the Amazing Technicolor Jizzbang Hairdo she sports every three days. 6. A win for Sonny Silver is much more necessary than a win for Jasmine because Mr. Silver’s been punked so bad these last few weeks, I actually hear Ashton Kutcher a-callin’. And oh, how I fucking LOATHE Ashton Kutcher. I wish for him to walk into the path of my Tacoma pick-up truck in the middle of the road. 7. The show is taking place from Houston, Texas. That really has little to zero bearing on me since I’m from Seattle, but a lot of my niggaz from That Dirty Souf will be in the house, hence Sonny Silver must represent and keep it real by jocking the bitches, slapping the ho’s, and keeping stuff straight-up gangsta n’ shit. Fo’ sheezy, his neezies, keep his arms so breezy. 8. Sonny Silver MUST win this match to maintain his rep. If he loses to a girl that’s got more dicks in her than a Tyler Rayne promo, the universe will collapse upon itself and we will have nothing left but a gaping hole where a proud blue planet once stood. 9. I’m pretty sure that trying to use the trademarked nickname of somebody that’s already used it for years is a crime punishable by, like, prison or something. Although with the way today’s justice system works, people get punished in other countries for murder, but if you live in SoCal and your name is OJ Simpson, you walk away scott-free with a book and movie deal from Fox. Lord knows Tchu’s punk-ass should’ve been thrown in jail for indecent exposure every time he cuts a promo. I’m the only Silver Lining in PRIME. Deal with it. And the tenth and final reason as to why Jasmine Drogan won’t amount to shit against me on the next episode of Revolution… 10. In reality, Sonny Silver is the most precious commodity you have in that I’m always entertaining, I put on great matches, my voice doesn’t sound like I’m gargling bongwater like Jason Snow, and most important, I fear comeuppance from no man, woman, or child. I always win in the end and I back down from no challenges, especially that one flamer, Killean Syringe or whatever. You know, Killean, the guy that’s got more shit on his dick than Jasmine Drogan has on her resume. So there you go. Either Mr. Silver Sonny Silver gets to win this match or else there will be riots in the streets. You really don’t want that, do you? I mean, if people are rioting, that means FUSE is going to lose out on millions of bucks because people will be too busy you know… stealing shit. Hell, I’ll probably be LEADING one of those mobs. … Okay, so you’re not convinced that I should have victory declared over Jasmine Drogan, are you? A top ten wasn’t good enough for you? Well, how about I go REAL old school. Yes, folks. I’m talkin’ script-style, Negroes. Like, SUPER old school trash-talk style script. ~~~~~ The scene fades over to Sonny Silver walking in front of the slowly-packing Toyota Center with hoards of fans in the background. In mere hours, PRIME was going to deliver a spectacular show in which would be the final chance for redemption and one-upmanship heading into Overkill. The opener itself was of great important to Sonny Silver, as he would be doing battle with PRIME newcomer and uber-talented female wrestler "The Silver Lining" Jasmine Drogan. With only that faithful cameraman following around his wacky exploits, the man who claims to NOT be Mr. Silver paces around the sidewalk with lots of gusto traveling through his veins. He takes a few breaths, then decides that he’s going to speak on this. Odelay. Silver: Welcome one, welcome all, welcome ladies, gentlemen, fuckwits, and morons. Salutations and thanks for watching this instead of egging on my stupid doppelganger, Mr. Silver, Chairman of Sucking. My name is Sonny Silver. But for those that don’t know me, I’m what Sonny SHOULD be coming into this federation and not posing as some horrible gimmick with little to no longevity beyond poking fun at Wuh-We booking. Tonight, I look forward to getting myself back on the path to greatness when I take on Jasmine Drogan. He continues to pace a hole through the ground, seemingly lost in thought for a few moments before jerking his head back to the camera, almost looking solemn. Silver: I’m sorry. I’m sorry that you had the shit luck to draw against me, my dear. No man, woman, child, or tranny should have that kind of incredibly bad fortune to look on that booking sheet and see the name "Sonny Silver" staring them in the face. You’d have been better off facing a Killean Sirrajin, an Easton Hall, or a fellow newcomer in Tyler Rayne. Instead, what you have drawn on this night is going to be nothing short of a massacre. Tonight, you won’t even have the chance to become the "Silver Lining." Shaking his head, he starts to chuckle now that his energy is being built back up with each word. Silver: Instead, you will be the woman known throughout PRIME as "The Shining Example." Why, you may be asking yourself? Because you will be just that. For you, Jasmine of Chicago, Illinois, the one-hundred thirtieth edition of ReVolution will be a return to form for myself and to use the newcomer rookie as a stepping stone to get myself back on track. You will be the first in a line of many bodies that I stack up as a message to send to the rest of my fellow PRIMEates in this business… STAY THE FUCK OUT OF MY WAY OR GET TRAMPLED. Shifting gears now, Sonny Silver starts his walk through the parking lot as the camera continues to fixate on just him. He turns his back to the camera every few seconds to make sure that every word that comes out of his mouth is heard clearly and efficiently. Silver: But I am to understand that she’s already not without accolades. She managed to pick up some hardware during his FUSE tenure in the form of their equivalent to a Hardcore Championship. Custom Shit title or something, I dunno. That’s all well and good, lady, but what are you doing to do when you’ve got to face a man much bigger than yourself within the confines of the rules? The use of weapons and other hardcore garbage will be null and void, so if you’re gonna beat me, you better pray in the seven days leading up to this match, you’ll have learned to wrestle by then. Better still, what the FUCK are you gonna do when you’re pitted against a man in which the rules mean jack shit to him? He finally comes to a stop when he reaches the backstage entry area for wrestlers and other PRIME staff. Turning back to the camera, he lets an eerie grin spread across his face and leaves the people on the final word. Silver: Tonight, we’re gonna lock up, Miss Drogan. You’ll be trapped in a prison in which there are four corners, three ropes, two blood-thirsty combatants… and one winner… Hint: Not you. I… "HEY, HEY, HEY!" Silver: Aw, crap. Before he can enter the arena, the hideous scowl of MISTER Silver gets up in Sonny’s grill looking incensed at his appearance. Mr. Silver: What the FUCK are you doing here? And what is this script trash-talk bullshit? This ain’t Geocities so by the powers vested in my by the PRIME Board of Directors, we’re going back to past-tense novel-style, NOW! With a snap of his fingers, all is right again in the RPing universe and we find our adventure going back to the wonderful world of Script. "NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Sonny screamed into the sky before Mr. Silver slaps him back to sanity. "Didn’t you read my last RP?" Father PRIME asked, angrily. "I shot you in the leg before the count of ten, a bullet through the head, and then I drove off leaving the reader with the sense of feeling that honor is not a commodity I care for anymore! It was brill, man!" "Brill?" "Yeah. BRILL. Short for "brilliant," something you would know nothing about you curtain-jerking twerp. But what are you doing here?" Sonny rubbed his temples, not believing that Mr. Silver was not only there, but annoying him with his belligerent Chairman-like attitude. "Did you REALLY think you were gonna off me like that, broham? I don’t think so. I’m trying to repair the damage you’ve done to my good name in lieu of your loss to Danny Ferguson. Remember that guy? You lost, dickface." "No, I put him over, MORON. Can you imagine what the masses would say if I were to go after the Universal Title? Madness, MADNESS I say! I’d have people accusing me of Jarrettism and that’s not some shit that even *I* would get involved with." "…Yes, you do have a point." Mr. Silver tugged at the collar of his Armani suit to make himself more comfortable, then folds his arms. "Of course I have a point here, you pusillanimous pencil-pushing pissguzzler! I’d like to thank you for handling most of the dirty work here, my past self, but I’m afraid your services are no longer necessary. I do feel I can take over from here." Akin to a pouting child, Sonny folded his arms and stood his ground. "I’m not going anywhere." "Yes, you are." "Who the fuck’s gonna move me? YOU? You haven’t had a spine of your own since you donned this moniker." "That may be so, dopplefucker, but I’m afraid that’s blessed me with the gift of knowing when to have other people handle the dirty stuff. SECURITY!" In a few moments, several ripped men sporting "Event Staff" shirts and coats surrounded Sonny Silver. While he looked around incredulously, Mr. Silver smirke at his past self. "This fan’s been stalking Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME for weeks now. He just told me he wanted to wear my skin like a suit to his next birthday party. Do your collective jobs and ensure this ruffian spends the night in a jail cell, if you will." "WHAT? WHAT?!?! NO! NO!" As security escorted Sonny off the premises, he left Mr. Silver with some ominous words. "I’LL BE BACK! OH, I’LL BE BACK! WHEN YOU DON’T HAVE A SINGLE RP IDEA IN YOUR HEAD, I’LL BE BACK! COUNT ON IT!" Mr. Silver chuckled like the evil genius of a token secret agent flick and prepared to enter the arena for combat. Tonight, no matter how he sliced it, was going to be a difficult task in upend Jasmine Drogan’s aspirations of securing her first victory within the hallowed halls. Would he have enough to do just that? Or was Sonny Silver right? Had he spent so much time hiding behind his bravado that he was looking towards a loss? No, he needed this win to redeem himself. He HAD to win. … Hopefully. Or something. Yeah. See what I did there, making him truly think about his doubt so this RP didn't appear to be a piece of phoned-in shit in which it is truly not?
