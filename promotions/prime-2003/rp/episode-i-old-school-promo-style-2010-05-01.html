Episode I: Old School Promo Style

1 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Saturday, May 1 The scene is sick and depraved... yet strangely alluring. LoC Champion, NEW Champion, EPW Intercontinental Champion, 2010 A1E Pier Six Brawl Champion, and 2010 ACW End Game Winner (Whew...) SHAWN JESSICA HART, PHD. appears before cameramen naked as a jaybird, save his sweet, yellow and black, zebra-print top hat. His naughty parts are, of course, blurred in the name of decency, but his arrogant mug, draped with an expression most malicious and projecting SUPREME confidence, is seemingly beaming through your TV, inner-nets, or whatever the heck you're using to get your PRIME fix. After taking a totally deep, albeit totally unnecessary breath, Hart licks his lips and speaks. "Hey now! Pro Wrestling's SJH here..." Charmed. "...and I'm PLEASED as punch to be comin' atcha for the veeeeeery first time in the PRIME universe!" The self-proclaimed "Bangbino" pumps his fist victoriously in celebration of this benchmark achievement for all mankind. "Some of you may know me as the guy that makes the WWR's top THREE or so every week the list comes out. To others, I'm probably just some JACKHOLE that forgot his underpants. Some even know me as the guy that stole Lindsay Troy's panties. Buuuut REGARDLESS of your pre-conceived notions about me or, for some, the lack thereof, the one UNDENIABLE truth as relates to yours truly that you simply MUST know is this..." Dramatic pause. "I'm into scat play." Grin. "That and the fact that I'm the greatest sensation to hit this fiiiiiiine nation since mechanically-induced orgasmic vibrations! A veritable ICON in sports, entertainment, music, and more or less KICKING ASS like no other dreadlock-wearin' whiteboy can. Which brings me to the reason I'm here. My CALLING here in PRIME and in this tournament..." He removes his hat, runs a hand through his flowing locks of blonde, and then returns his whimsical gaze to the camera's lens. "To channel my inner Sid Vicious... and become the ruler... of the WWOOOOOORLD..." The Prime Minister of Gettin' Sinister clearly exudes EVIL. "......HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE!!" Oh right, the gold. "Y'see, I've got a trophy case bigger than J-Lo's dump-a-bump, and BELIEVE YOU ME, it's gettin' more crowded by the day. But resting on my laurels has never been something that tickled my pickle. So with GOD as my witness... and Mohammed n' Buddha and OBI-WAN too... I WILL NOT REST until that bitch is full!" SJH nods with self-approval. "Now, what does all of that have to do with the tag team bout that marks my debut with this promotion? Heh, not much... I honestly don't even know who I'm teaming with. Probably another anxious tourney-goer? My manager sent me the line-up sheet, but until just now, I've been COMPLETELY unable to pull myself away from this Ghost Adventures marathon on the Travel Channel to peep it." The camera pulls back just enough to reveal a flat-screen television, upon which Zak Bagans taunts dead people. "NONETHELESS, the steak n' eggs of the situation here is that PRIME is about to become my personal pic-a-nic basket... and there's not a good-golly-GOSH that ANYONE can do about it." Hart edges in close. "Call it a mockery, call it an OUTRAGE, I call it the TRUTH! And sometimes, the truth HURTS!" More head-nodding action. "Say what will and do what you must. In the end, none a' y'all are gonna FLY as HIGH as the PHENOM!" He glances pensively upward. "Except maybe birds, but after all... they ARE the world's LARGEST tetrapod vertebrates!" Who knew? "Anywhoo, I think that's enough wingin' it for one night. My NetFlix disc ain't gonna stream itself! So until we meet again, allow me to say to all you PRIMEates out there... that I look forward to changing the game on your asses!" Deep breath.. Big Finish: "The NEW STANDARD of all that should be in this company has left the building!!" Fade.
