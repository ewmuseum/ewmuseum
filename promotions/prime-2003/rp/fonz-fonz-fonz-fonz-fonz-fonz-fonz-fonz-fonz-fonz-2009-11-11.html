Date: 11/11/09 Success? Making a name for myself? Glory? Being admired by a nationwide audience? Yeah, I'm gonna stop you right there, 'cause you know, I don't really care about what you're talking about. I don't really want any of that. So why am I here, doing this self-monologue in front of my bedroom mirror in my head for practice, in hopes that I'll get to say it in front of a live crowd on network television? Well, it's simple, folks. And here's where I take a deep breath and bite my lip so people know to listen closely -- and then: "I love Arthur Fonzarelli from the bottom-bottom-bottom of my heart-heart-heart!" And I know he'll see it someday, 'cause the news loves a heroic story of a small-town girl from Milwaukee who rises to the top against all odds, and someone -- Ritchie, or maybe Ralph Malph -- will e-mail him the YouTube link and then, THEN he'll know that Jennifer Leanne Picciotti is the one to tame the panther behind those denim pants! And we'll ride away on the back of his Honda road hog, right into the sunset! Just him and me. He'll carve AF-heart-JP into a picnic table, and then make love to me on top of the picnic table! And then, we'll head to Arnold's, because everyone knows that making love gives the Fonz a major appetite. And if I see that little tramp Pinky Tuscadero there, I'm gonna be so mad, because EVERYONE knows she's a tease. And then, we'll live happily ever after! Because this plan, this one's gotta work. It's not some dumb little girl-style attempt to be with him. No silly love note or declaration of how beautiful he is during a public appearance, no way, Jose! That kind of thing doesn't work on Arthur Herbert Fonzarelli! I know, because I um...I tried both. Dear Fonzie, I caught you trying to peek up my skirt in Mrs. Schmidtstein's class yesterday, but don't worry! You don't have to be coy with me, Arthur Fonzarelli, because we're both seniors now! I mean, I know you've been in high school for eight years, and I know that you're probably looking for someone with a little more meat in the seat -- but I'm wild about your style, you hunk! I want to bop with you all night long. Even on weekdays! Enclosed are some naked pics of me. Call me later on my cellphone @ [detail withheld]. C-ya, Jenny (with a heart for an e) So, the letter didn't exactly work, because I never got a call...so yeah, I was a little depressed. Then, after a month of staying in my room and crying into my teddy bear (I call him Zonf, which is Fonz spelled backwards,) it dawned on me! What a square I had been! Arthur Fonzarelli, that silly goose, wasn't ignoring my letter! He was just playing hard to get! What a card he was. I knew then that I was going to have to grab the bull by the ring-thingy in his nose: I needed to see him in person. So, I did what any normal, rational girl would do: I "borrowed" my mom's pink Cadillac she'd won for selling Mary Kay cosmetics, gathered up my life savings, and drove down to Chicago where the Fonz was making a public appearance! (Of course, it was billed as a book signing for "Henry Winkler," which is what Arthur calls himself now in order to keep a low profile. I know better, though, unlike everyone else.) Cut to the big day. I was sure to bring my cutest outfit to wear just for him. I stood in a HUGE line of about fifteen people, mostly middle-aged women, ready to meet my one and only. I had on my white tennis shoes, white bobbie socks, off-white skirt, off-white long-sleeved sweater with an "S" embroidered onto it (the S no doubt stood for "Sex Me In The No No Place" because Fonz likes that,) and my hair looked great tied back into two pigtails. All these other old hags wore dungarees, which is SOOOOOOOOO tacky! HA! They didn't stand a chance against my love! "Aren't you excited?" A woman standing in front of me asked. I nodded back and smiled politely. "I just love the Fonz," she yipped (yes, yipped, like a dog) back, and I could tell right then that she'd come for his mighty loins. That bitch. According to the police report, I "screamed" (read: voiced sternly) that if she "put her filthy paws on Arthur" (read: touched him with foul intent) that I would "use her face as a welcome mat" (read: use her face as ANY kind of mat.) Then, I "struck her violently" (read: slapped that gangly beast back into 1842 when she was born) and "strangled her while forcing the back of her head into the ground repeatedly" (read: the only true statement in the police report.) The worst part (besides the year of house arrest) was obviously the fact that even after traveling all that way, I still didn't get to tell my beloved Fonzie how I felt! Dammit! The judge recommended that I take up some kind of sport to channel all of my violent aggression. Since only weird people take karate and stuff, I chose the obvious. Wrestling. You can get on national television with wrestling, not just ESPN 57 with karate. Training was hard, but I knew that the only way to get the Fonz's attention was through the media: kids' stuff just wasn't going to cut it! And now, here I am. And boy, do I hope this works, because if it doesn't... Well, I don't think the Fonz is particularly fond of living in a cellar. Tied to a cot. No, I don't think so.
