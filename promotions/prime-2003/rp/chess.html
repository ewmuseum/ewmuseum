Date: Inside August 5th, 2007 I know that once I take a step out that hospital door, everything is going to be different: The sights, the smells, the tastes, and most importantly the feelings. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Reflection For the past 28 years I have been entrenched in a malicious game of chess with life as my opposition. Whenever trouble arose, I often looked over to the chess board that my father gave me and considered it the battlefield. Sixteen pieces: Eight pawns, two bishops, two knights, two rooks, one queen, and one king. Every attempted shot at victory never materialized. The punishments for my failed conquests were macabre and I carry their results around with me even today. The voices in my head wished for me to succumb in hopes that the violence would decrease. Much to my chagrin it never would, and for the longest time I led myself to believe that it was my destiny never to receive liberation from the endless struggle. The light at the end of the tunnel was always there, but miles away. For all of my problems to be excommunicated, I must defeat life at one game, just one, easier said than done. There was always a mismatch when it came to this game. For starters, my pieces had the mind of someone who was incapable of strategy. The pawns were just inept pieces sent to the slaughter. They knew going into the match that they were not going to make a difference, the same with every other piece on my team. From the very beginning I never had a stalwart queen, let alone a queen at all. She was never there to be the last line of defense or to be the mask that shielded me from the horror life threw in my face. Imagine starting the game without one of the most vital pieces in a limited arsenal and being expected to defend the territory. To boot, until thirteen years of age when I had a leash around my neck and was able to leave my quarters, I had no proper instructions on how to play the game. My sensei, Christian, and the Smith family allowed me to glue myself back together, and to formulate a theory or two on how to turn the tide. My feet gained momentum and my mind believed that it had a shot for glory. Life cut me down by putting me behind bars. Prison should have been the ultimate checkmate, and I knew that it would be in my best interest to cease trying. The amount of work that I was putting into this was absurd and unnecessary. The sword was just too goddamn difficult to pull from the rock. However, my heart refused to see me go down like that. It prevented me from using the ‘Get out of Jail Free’ card, turning my bed sheet into a noose and hanging myself. I was able to take some time out and re-evaluate everything in my life, along with my current situation. While in my isolation, it seemed that I actually had another chance once Kimberly Crawford, a woman who I had only met once after a long exchange of letters, decided to marry me. I finally gained a queen that would be there. Another day Another dollar Another town Another war… Another death She was left to die inside of her home right before I re-entered the real world for the first time in seven years. Twenty five years in and I still hadn’t won a fucking game. Most men would quit, but I couldn’t. At this point I had invested way too much time to throw in the towel. I went back to the east coast, found myself a job, and used the newfound ability to gain status and to gain wealth, a massive amount of it. Before I could step back and see what I had become, I was in PRIME and the last pieces fell into place. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The Present: Analysis Thanks to life, inadvertently, giving me a new hope in the Braintrust, I have the feeling that this is going to be the final round. Confidence is pumping into my veins for the first time ever. Right now, I am sitting on top of the world both mentally and physically. For once, I have a complete wall of protection around me. The pawns are my material resources. I will use them wisely instead of in vain ala the previous battles. Brandon Smith and Michael Blaze are my rooks. Their computer and business skills will make sure nothing gets out of hand. Jason Hardy and Matthew Smith are my knights. Their past work connections are more than enough to keep tabs on the other side. Chris Smith and Christian Daniels are my bishops. Their hearts are cold and they have no problem in sending the opposition a stern message. Sun Tzu is my queen. She is not like the two that came before her, for she is someone who is just as sinister as everybody else in my army. My opposition stands tall and very proud. On the surface they might wear different team colors, but I cannot distinguish that superficial color. I only see the one on the inside and they all match. Their pawns are the same as my pawns. One rook, knight, and bishop combination is the Braintrust. They are under the impression that defeating me is easy by holding information over my head that incriminates me. An x-factor that their camp holds is that I do not know the true identity of their bishop, and according to Nathan Murray, I never will. The other combination is the DeAngelis family. They have employed me to take out Tony Gamble, just as the Braintrust has. Realistically, I do not believe that they are going to allow me to live after dealing with Tony. I refuse to become someone else’s permanent lackey and that will upset them, thus leading to me being the next one on their hit list. At the top part of this food chain are Cynthia Berkman and Tony Gamble. He is the one that my side wishes to execute just as much, if not more, than the DeAngelis family. The one advantage I have at this point is that Tony does not take me seriously. Sure, we might have met once outside of a PRIME arena, but the duo will focus on killing their squad rather than exerting energy with me. How naïve you two are. I just took down the Braintrust rook and whether life likes it or not, I’m ready, willing, and able to take down its other pieces. But this time it’s different because life is not ready for me. I respect the fact that life thought tried and true old tactics would work on me like every other time. I just hope that it doesn’t make any excuses when there is nowhere to run and nowhere to hide. Achieving ultimate victory is the destiny that I shall to carve into the world. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I am sitting on a wheelchair about to get the hell outta Dodge. The doctor just deemed me “well enough” to leave the grounds of Northwest Hospital. During the match with Winters, Lavelle, and Dusk I sustained cuts to both my legs and arms that required stitches. All in all there are a total of thirty five that don my anatomy now. Every time one of the staff walked into my room, they could not keep their eyes off of the Hieronymus Bosch painting that is my body. I will admit that I am not the most attractive man in the world when it comes to what Hollywood deems to be attractive, but at least give me some common courtesy and respect. I should sue those fuckers for malpractice! I swear to God if those stupid little bitch nurses told anybody anything about what they saw… No, I bet you are telling that shit to other nurses in the break room right now. Hell, before I know it I’m going to be on the six o clock news as the top story. The valet parking kid, who will never make enough money to fix his face, which really proves evolution exists, hands Sun Tzu the keys. Thankfully she retrieved my car from Safeco Field and drove it here while the doctors were busy poisoning themselves with prejudice over my life, one in which they know nothing about. The passenger door flies vertical. Once at its apex, I rise up from the wheelchair and enter the vehicle. Sunny swings the wheelchair around and shoves it at the valet guy, leaving him to catch it while she enters the driver’s seat and floors it away from the hospital. My eyes turn around and I go into a trance. The hospital is not captivating on the surface, but just below my exterior it holds a special place. Whenever I think about chess, a hospital is not that far behind in the thought process. It was where the genesis occurred when my first queen died… And I’m going to make sure that the last game ends here with me pulling the plug on the king and queen.
