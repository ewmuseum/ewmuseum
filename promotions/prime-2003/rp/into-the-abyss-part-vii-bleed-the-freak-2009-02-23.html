Into The Abyss: Part VII - Bleed The Freak

23 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23-02-09 "Because...I believe yours is a life worth saving, Gloria..." The words of Von Kelsig reverberated in her mind, her inspiration as she marched on desperately to reach her destination. In the rush to get out of the building she hadn't thought to grab anything from her room and so still wearing her ripped jeans and leather jacket she burst onto the streets of Detroit and ran like the wind to the nearest train station she knew of, the closest being Eton Street. Once there she threw herself up the platform stairs and lost herself in the crowd. Moving through the mass of commuters it was several minutes before she noticed someone wearing similar fatigues to Reuben's accomplice. At that moment she realized any number of his cronies could be in the crowd looking for her, and in the company of casual citizens and high-powered smart-suited businessmen she would be easy to identify. Her eyes scanned the stands as people waited on the trains to come though no one seemed to offer anything of use to her, until she spied a busker hunched over his guitar case counting the change by one of the ticket booths. Clad in baggy stonewashed jeans and a white t-shirt underneath a black hoodie with a red barbed wire design running all over it, he was her solution. "Hey! Hey buddy!" She called, zig-zagging between human obstacles and stopping sharply in front of him. "Uhh...hi." The busker glanced around, wondering why she had suddenly sprang out on him like that. Whipping a hand over the lid of the case he began closing it over, for fear she was just another mugger. "What's up?" He continued, satisfied his takings were safe. "Listen, this might sound strange but, um..." She paused for a moment, scanning the crowd again until she spotted an old woman with a red scarf and a jolly face shaking a donation box in aid of Cancer Research. Time was of the essence but she had to convince him nonetheless. "I'm doing a Barter Challenge for Charity. We need to go out and haggle people for stuff, getting something bigger and better each time until the challenge stops this evening. Then we take what get got and auction it for charity!" Wow, she'd never thought that fast in her life and the light-hearted half smile she shot him convinced him she was telling the truth, although he was suddenly aware that while she had a conscience, he had a guitar. "Umm...I don't really have anything to-..." He began, only to be interrupted. "Oh don't worry I don't want your guitar, your hoodie would be fine!" She said, thinking that if she really was working for charity she would have been affronted by his selfish attitude. "Uhh...what are you going to give me?" He asked, opening the case again and placing the guitar on top of the money before closing it over and clipping it shut. Gloria realized she didn't fully understand the meaning of the word barter. Then again when it's only use was found in Reuben's act of "bartering" a kilo of cocaine in exchange for a bullet from a rival dealer it was no wonder she was confused. "How about my jacket? Straight swap?" It was a shot in the dark but she was desperate. He thought for a moment, regarding her jacket and her overall appearance. She looked like Nancy Spungen, only much thinner. Not once did he imagine her to be a scummy drug addict like Nancy herself, given he a stoner who leaned heavily into the grunge scene, the busker shrugged and began removing his top. "Ok...if it's for charity." He sighed, as Gloria removed her leather jacket and traded it for the busker's hoodie. "You're a star, babe!" She quipped and turned tail, scarpering off and leaving the busker slightly bewildered look on his face to accompany the junkie's jacket. As she raced towards the platform for the train that would take her north to Port Huron she threw on the top and pulled the hood over her wild blonde hair, allowing her to blend in with the norms. Shoving her hands into her pockets she fiddled around for a moment before producing her lighter and smokes, a piece of paper with some essential contact numbers for Reuben, and some crumpled paper money. Stuffing the rest back into her pockets she unfolded the notes to find she had almost forty bucks, enough to get her at least quarter of the way there if not a little further. As the northbound train rolled into the station she glanced around the platform to find no one had noticed her or appeared to be actively looking for her, though in such a dense crowd there was always the possibility she was wrong. Getting onto the train and finding a window seat near the middle of the carriage she nestled into her seat and kept a watchful eye on the platform as the last of the passengers boarded and with a quick succession of blasts from the horn the wheels started turning again, carrying Gloria far away from the hell of Detroit on to greener pastures, though she never put herself at ease or risked resting for too long. Unrelenting fear gripped her every second of every day that she was on the move and in the days since she had escaped Reuben she had never once removed the black hoodie she wore for fear of being recognized. Reuben was sure to have eyes and ears in many different places and she daren't let her guard down for a second. As if that wasn't bad enough the money had only gotten her as far as Port Rowan and from there she had used her cunning to dupe local beer-swillers into forking over cash for sex. Half of the perverts were too drunk to notice she had slipped out their bathroom window where she promised she would "freshen up" for them. The other half she fooled into blindfolding and binding themselves to their beds while she took the money and ran, notably leaving one middle-aged, overweight fisherman in Grimsby to explain to his wife upon her return from bingo why he was in such a predicament. Avoiding any major population densities where possible, Gloria sailed through Rochester and chose to travel through Oswega towards Camden rather than chancing a trip to Syracuse. Fear was the angry piston pumping her heart to its limit and she barely noticed as the shakes and cramps began to set in, the power of the adrenaline shrouding the hunger for drugs every time she imagined herself being followed. By the time she got to Boonville however the hunger had become ravenous and in an attempt to quell her cravings, for the sake of Von Kelsig, she took up sticking herself with a diaper pin to relieve the stress. Chemically the best she could do was consume lots of energy drinks and smoke far too many cigarettes in a day. On really bad nights vodka and codeine helped her sleep, and where she couldn't obtain that particular drug she had to resort to substitutes like Co-codamol. A couple days travelling through the Adirondack Park Preserve, keeping an eye out for any suspicious reptiles as she passed Lake Placid, Gloria caught a fishing boat from Essex to Charlotte and arrived in Vermont at long last. The first true leg of the journey was over and she had succeeded unnoticed, Von Kelsig would have been proud of her for coming this far. All that remained now was to find Wyatt Manor, and as she ventured forth she couldn't help but wonder what had become of the man who saved her. For all she knew the two gunshots and ripped his head off his shoulders, but not once did she give in to that ugly thought, she'd sooner take solace in the churning pain of her habit calling to her before imagining the worst. When curiosity eventually got the better of her she focused on the moment before she ran away and ignored the sound of the pistol firing at her saviour... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Back in Detroit, MI.... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ...The noise had jolted Von Kelsig but the adrenaline rush was too great and he carried on regardless, barreling into Reuben and sending him hurtling out of the room and across the corridor, slamming into one of the fake doors on the opposite wall. Smacking his head against the wall, Reuben slumped to the ground as Von Kelsig turned and screamed to Gloria once more to run, snatching her by the arm as she darted past and pushing her away for added momentum. Sprinting down the hallway in tears she descended the stairs onto the first floor and disappeared out of sight while Von Kelsig lifted an arm into the air to strike down on Reuben and smash his face with unadulterated power and rage. Suddenly a piercing pain shot through his arm and he snapped it close to his chest, looking down to find a big black bullet hole in his right bicep which quickly began dripping blood. Stopping in his tracks he cradled his arm and noticed a red stain spreading across his gut, sending another shooting pain through his stomach. Glancing up he saw the anger in Reuben's face subside to that of shock at what had just transpired. Another rush of adrenaline suddenly tore through his body and raising his hand once more Von Kelsig roared as he intended to cave Reuben's face in once more. Taking the gun by the barrel, Reuben deflected the fist as it came down upon him and smashed the butt into Von Kelsig's temple, knocking the equally giant man out and leaving him unconscious on the floor as blood began pooling around him. Getting up, Reuben placed his gun back into its holster and nudged Von Kelsig's face with his boot as Joe came bounding up the stairs. "I heard gunshots!" He exclaimed, panicking at the sight of Reuben standing over his hostage. "No shit." Reuben replied, his tone devoid of emotion as he observed Von Kelsig bleeding out before him. "Drag this fuck back into his room and get a hold of Menendez, I want him over here immediately with his gear. Tell him he's got two holes to plug." With that Reuben took off again, still a little unnerved. The image of Von Kelsig charging him stuck in his mind and as he jogged down the stairs to the first floor he suddenly realized something. While Joe struggled to pull Von Kelsig through a coagulated mess of blood on the floor, he glanced up to see Reuben storming towards him once more. "Forgot all about why I came up here in the first place, my goddamn phone!" He remarked. As Reuben headed into his office Joe simply chuckled and pulled Von Kelsig back into the room, seeing for the first time that the many cable ties were lying broken on the floor. Beyond those lay the chair, two of its legs broken off and a third cracked through the wood. Exhaling in wonder at what must have happened, Joe released the seven foot dead weight and produced his cell phone from his jeans pocket. Scanning through the address book he came upon the name of Gil Menendez and called the number. "Gil? It's Yousef. Blanco asked me to call, he needs you over at the factory office immediately, there's been an incident and-...no no, gunshots...two of them...in the arm and the stomach......... how soon? Make it fast, I'll meet you at the door......no he's not bleeding too badly. Ok, see you shortly." Hanging up, Joe took off to meet the man who was a precious commodity to people like Reuben Blanco, a mob doctor. Von Kelsig remained unconscious and lay bleeding out on the floor, while Yousef ran to the front entrance and Reuben stood in his office with an uncertain look on his face. Holding his cell in hand he read the words '1 Missed Call' over and over, and jumped with fright as the ringtone suddenly began playing. Checking the number on the facia he was relieved at first to see his answer machine calling him back. "......You have...one...message. To call this person back, press 1. To delete the message, press 3." The robotic voice held hopes of some kind of good news to follow what had been an unfortunate and unforeseen event. What followed however left Reuben's heart in his mouth, pounding rapidly. "Blanco, you're late for our meeting. Don't bother showing up, we're coming to you. You better have a good excuse." The assault by Von Kelsig had shaken him so much he had simply forgotten he was on his way to a meeting. A very important meeting. Now the realization that his peers were on their way over sunk in and Reuben imagined the grief he would receive for his unprofessional attitude. It was one thing to undermine him in his own house, it was another to cause him to undermine the people he worked with and for. Von Kelsig had unwittingly thrown a spanner into the works and Reuben's blood began to boil at the thought of what this giant waste of smack had just cost him. Storming out of his office he walked straight past Von Kelsig's room and made his way to the front entrance where Yousef was waiting impatiently for Menendez to show up. "The second he patches Von Kelsig up I want that big cunt tied down and ready to suffer. He may have just cost me my connections in this town and if he has...he's going to wish the bullets had killed him." Reuben snapped, burying a finger into Joe's chest. Nodding agreeably, Yousef waited on while Reuben disappeared back into the office. He had seen what Reuben did to people who were uncooperative, and had he not been desensitized to the punishment that was dealt out he would have prayed for Von Kelsig. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Out on the Road again... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ She had no idea how long she had been travelling, it felt like weeks. Coupled with the agonizing reminder that her habit would not go quietly into the night, Gloria's trip had been hell. After reaching Vermont, Brayston still took another two days to find and after quizzing countless Vermonters who seemed strangely reluctant to talk about the village she eventually found her way there. The sky seemed to darken the closer she got, and a chilling wind froze her to the bone. Somewhere in the distance an ominous clap of thunder boomed in the blackening clouds. The residents there were equally suspicious, and threatened her with violence when she asked of Wyatt Manor. Stumbling innocently into the town she made her way over to a group of people conversing beneath a scratched though polished sign that read 'Wicker Street'. Amidst the crowing and chattering gossip of the group she asked quietly if anyone knew of Wyatt Manor. As one elderly lady clad in a frumpy black dress raised her basket of fruit to strike Gloria, the wild-haired addict turned and made off as the old woman's friends began cursing at her and moving as though to give chase. Heading north as Von Kelsig had instructed however Gloria came upon a dense forest of dead trees, the locals howling at her and crying madness as she disappeared into the tree-line. Passing through the woods she made her way up the hill that brought her to the Manor itself. Surrounded by a broken wrought-iron fence that had all but sunken into the mud around the grounds, she made her way up the cracked concrete driveway past ominous, vine-ridden statues of creatures that were marked as "The Great Old Ones". The hideous beasts seemed to stalk her as she crept up the driveway towards the looming Manor that had all the airs and graces of a Hammer house of horror. Gloria noticed flickering lights in some of the windows along the front of the Manor, produced by many candles within. Approaching the grand front door she nervously reached for the golden door-knocker, hanging from the mouth of a grotesque gargoyle that bore a hole through her own eyes. Lifting the knocker she allowed it to drop thrice to the maple wood, the sound echoing through the hall beyond the door. Moments passed before whispers filled the air, and finally the door creaked inwards to reveal a tall gentleman completely shrouded in a black ceremonial robe. His eyes were cast in shadow by the hood, while she could make out his hooked nose and small pursed lips in the low light of the entrance. It was at this moment that Gloria realized where the path had led her. Tears began welling up in her eyes and her lip quivered as she wrapped her arms around herself and gazed at the hooded figure, who stood as statuesque as those effigies in the way behind her, wary of her presence here. "Are you lost?" He said in a whisper, though to Gloria he was as loud as the whistling wind. "I'm here to...to see Bruce Shanahan? Von Kelsig sent me..." She replied cautiously. At once the robed figure stood erect and spun on his heels, disappearing into the Manor as Gloria stood confounded on the front step. She hadn't but a few minutes to wait before another figure approached the front door, standing before her clad in the same style robe as the previous man, the difference being this one was tailored with gold lacing into an intricate pattern across the chest. Amulets and pendants hung from his neck and rings bearing similar symbols adorned his fingers. Peeling back his hood Gloria stared straight into the eyes of the man she had come to meet. The name of Violence Jack was spoken in hushed tones in the town of Brayston, and now he stood before her. A half smile crept across his face as he reached out an open hand to Gloria. "Come, we have much to discuss..." And with that Gloria took him by the hand and wept as he led her into the realm of Wyatt Manor. Her journey was over, she had made it. She only prayed Von Kelsig would be as fortunate. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Meanwhile in Detroit... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ He awoke in the midst of a never-ending bright white corridor filled with doors. His chest felt tight and his lungs ached as he tried to gulp down air. His vision was blurring and before him he saw that wild-haired angel once more, reaching out to him with a smile that comforted him even as he suffocated. His vision began reddening and his chest tightened painfully as the angel of mercy reached closer, only to suddenly start drifting away from him. All at once his head pulsed as the red mist descended and the many doors in the corridor burst open. From each a giant emerged, clad in a tattered black suit that clung around folds of greasy fat that bulged through the seams. They began clamouring for the angel, lashing out as her and tearing her wings apart as she floated deeper into the crowd of obese monsters. He tried to engage his legs but his muscles would not respond, and the wrath of the roar he unleashed shook the walls around him. Thrashing his limbs wildly he smashed the walls around him, reducing the doors to splinters and revealing a black void behind each. Try as he may he couldn't move forward one step as the angel almost willingly floated further into the maelstrom as her wings were plucked from her back and the gross beasts began ripping her hair out, while some began licking her face and groping her breasts and groin, slavering all over the once perfect, unblemished divine being. Suddenly his throat closed up and he could no longer breathe. Ceasing to destroy the walls around him he clutched at his own throat, desperately trying to tear it open. His vision blackened slowly, while the angel began to scream. Louder and louder she called to him as she was reduced to a mutilated pulp, but her cries only faded as the world around him fell into the void beyond those doors until there was nothing. "Wake up..." The faintest calling in the darkness was accompanied by a sharp sting around his head. Again, the distant stranger called to him. "Von Kelsig...wake up!" He opened his eyes abruptly and took a sharp breath with the memory of suffocation still fresh in his mind. No longer was he in that damned corridor, there were no slovenly giants and no angel. His vision was clear, if a little bleary and the only individuals around him were strangers...all except for one who stepped forward into the room and stood behind them all, arms crossed and a vicious stare in his eyes. Reuben Blanco. Struggling forward Von Kelsig tried to lunge at his tormentor only to find that once again he was bound, this time by four thick leather straps that held him tightly against a large stretcher. Four smaller straps wrapped individually around his limbs tied him to the rails below him. He felt the desire to roar as he had in his dream, though the only noise to escape his lips was a whimper as Reuben stepped forward and placed a hand on the shoulder of a much smaller Hispanic man wearing a white overcoat and latex gloves which Von Kelsig found to be covered in blood. "Excellent work Menendez, what's the verdict?" Reuben queried as the man began removing the gloves and wiping sweat from his forehead. "He was lucky. The bullet in his arm passed clean through his bicep and tricep, grazed the bone. I was able to remove any grit as well as the bullet" Menendez replied, squinting his eyes at something on Von Kelsig's arm. "And the other?" Reuben asked. "Even luckier. It passed between his spleen and liver...god only knows how, if was three millimetres to the left it would have severed his aorta. For someone that wasn't aiming straight those were a couple of crack shots, Blanco." Menendez quipped, turning away to light up a cigarette as Reuben regarded the bandages and the faint pale face of Von Kelsig. Drawn by the look on his face Von Kelsig turned his head to find his right arm bandaged up, a faint red blotch stained beneath the surface. His bicep throbbed as blood pumped back through the veins, and feeling a similar sensation in his stomach he glanced down to find another bandage wrapped around his torso with a similar stain seeping through the dressing. Gritting his teeth he held his eyes shut and let his head fall back onto the pillow beneath as Reuben chuckled, turning to face his three accomplices, of whom Yousef was present. "Joe, take the good doctor to my office..." He began, turning to the man in fatigues and buddy. "You two go wait out front for our guests, inform them of what happened and assure them I will make amends." All nodding in understanding, they left the room leaving Reuben with Von Kelsig. The air that had once reeked of burning flesh was now stale with a hint of iron invading Blanco's nostrils as he bent over his victim. "The fuck were you thinking? Hm? Did you really think you could get out of here?" Reuben's eyes flashed with anger as Von Kelsig listened in discomfort. "There are only two ways out of this, you refused one and you survived the other. I'm done. I've tried to break you and to your credit you held on long enough to fucking corrupt one of my people to help you escape..." He wrapped his hand around Von Kelsig's bandaged arm, applying little pressure though enough to make his victim squirm. "Now this is how it's going to go. I've wasted too many ounces on you and even more weeks. My time and supplies are better served elsewhere than fucking with a piece of shit like you...so today things change." Grabbing Von Kelsig's hand in his own so that his fingers pressed down on the knuckles while Von Kelsig's fingers were balled up in his palm, Reuben sneered before gritting his teeth and with a quick snap jerked his hand forward. Von Kelsig screamed as his fingers broke at the knuckle, pain buzzing through his hand as Reuben released it and took hold of his other hand. "Shut up!" He roared, holding his free hand over Von Kelsig's mouth as the big man's eyes rolled into his head and he bit down on his tongue. Von Kelsig's screams became desperate groans as he writhed on the stretcher. "You're going to sign over your whole fucking existence to me and then you're going to tell me where Gloria is. You've still got one hand to write with and two choices; obey me and be done with this or watch me break every other bone in your body. What's it to be?" The words seethed from his lips as the desperation built in his body, eager to hear Von Kelsig finally break under his might. Reuben stood up for a moment and cracked his knuckles as Von Kelsig's broken hand left him shaking, his heart beating violently and a cold sweat glistening across his face as the sharp pain in his hand refused to fade away. Banging his head off the pillow he grit his teeth until his jaws ached, his eyes shut tightly. Reuben's words rattled around in his head amidst waves of confusion and panic and pain. The more he hurt the louder the words were in his head until finally he snapped his head forward, eyes wide and mouth slavering red. "NEVER!" Reuben closed his eyes and pursed his lips as Von Kelsig began laughing hysterically through tears. "NEVER! YOU HEAR ME?! NEVER! EVER...EVER!" Reuben sighed and leaned over Von Kelsig again, grabbing hold of his pinky. "YOU WILL NEVER BREAK ME! YOU HEAR ME?!" Snapping Von Kelsig's little finger back into the knuckle, he held on tight as his bellowing captive screamed louder than before as the pain shot through both hands now. With a calm measured approach, Reuben released the pinky finger and grabbed the ring finger, cracking the bone and smiling as the sound brought more tears to Von Kelsig's eyes while his screams became raspy squeals. As Reuben wrapped his hand around Von Kelsig's middle finger however, the door behind him burst open and in walked three men. Two were gnarled, thick-necked brutes in black suits with a hand poised in their jackets ready to pull a weapon at a moment's notice. The third was dressed in a similar fashion to his cohorts, with the exception of his high-collared jacket and a fedora tipped low on his head, casting a shadow over his face. Von Kelsig's screams had hypnotized Reuben and he didn't notice until a hand tapped him on the shoulder at the same time as he wrenched back on the middle finger, revelling in the gore as the bone snapped and clicked against the palm of his hand. Turning around, Reuben's look of sinister joy turned to surprised panic at the sight of the intruders. "Did we catch you at a bad time?" The man in the fedora asked. Reuben brushed back a few loose strands of hair and opened his mouth to welcome them before he was stopped. "You had the audacity not to turn up to our meeting in favour of this?" He continued. "Oh no, not at all sir...Mr. Doe. Please, you must understand I was incapacitated! I make no excuses but as you can see I had a pressing matter to take care of!" Reuben exclaimed rather worriedly to his superior, known in his own little sphere of influence as John Doe. "Please..." John began, holding a gloved hand up to the level of Reuben's face. "...don't insult me with explanations. What is he, family?" Came the sinister tone as he quizzed the seven foot man lying on the stretcher, shaking more violently now as his hands felt the agony pulse through them. Von Kelsig didn't know what was worse, this or the wielding torch. In a short few moments he succumbed once more to a blackout as Reuben trembled in his shoes, all eight foot of him was stepping on eggshells around this man called John Doe. "No this is the one I was telling you about. Von Kelsig." He said, trying to hide the fear in his voice. "Oh?" John regarded the quivering mass on the stretcher more closely. "Why is he so special?" "He's going to help me to help you! You're aware of the trouble with our funds, this man is the cause of it all. Sir I've tried everything to make this bastard pay what he owes but there is no getting through to him. He's unbreakable!" Reuben couldn't control the tone of his voice, and Doe knew his words to be true. He approached Von Kelsig, gesturing for his henchmen to stay put as he measured Reuben's victim up where he lay. "I think there is a very simple solution you have overlooked in all of this..." John turned to Reuben. "...You know if you wish to continue your business in this city, and indeed any other...especially with me, then you had better get a handle on your own business first. If anything like this jeopardizes our operation again, I'll make what you've done to this one look like a slap on the wrist. Do you understand?" Doe's words were clear, concise and shook Reuben to the core. "Yes sir. You have my word." He replied. He paused for a moment before nodding to Von Kelsig. "What's to be done with him?" John smiled a devilish smile, hidden by the shadow across his face. Reuben felt it and it scared him. "He's not your concern any more. From what you've told me it's too much for you to handle. You see Blanco what you've failed to realize is every man has a price, your misfortune was finding the one man who values nothing." "I know this now, sir." Reuben said, with a pained expression on his face. "But what's to be done about it?" "Nothing in life is worthless, Blanco. The solution lies with finding what this Von Kelsig holds dear, what he holds true...and we exploit it. We exploit it, Blanco." They watched as Von Kelsig lay on the stretcher, lost in limbo. All around him nothing but darkness except...except for that wild-haired angel. Except for her.
