Out Of My Element

4 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 4th, 2005 Chapter One: In A Funk There I was, walking through another hotel corridor with no real sense as to where I was headed. My room? His? I didn't even have time to worry about drugs or booze... I'd fucked up far beyond my means this time. My head was far from being the even playing field it was, not even months ago, when I knew exactly what I wanted and how to get it... in the form of a K-Wolf, of course. Now, well... everything was wrong. Dave probably hated me, I'd decimated Karina's knee to the point that I had no playmate in the forseeable future and there was this strange man in my bed who just didn't know the rules. ''Look,'' I feigned interest in myself. This was practice for the speech I was about to give. ''...You need to know that this doesn't go an--JESUS!'' There he was. Chest-first into my nose, which proceeded to bleed quite wonderfully. ''Shit, Amy! I'm so sorry! I was just walking around, practicing what to s--ah, forget it, I sound like an ass...'' An ass who's still talking... He paused, ''Are you, uh... you okay there?'' ''Bleeding,'' I managed to sniff a good portion of that first bood spurt straight back into my skull, ''But alive.'' His hand was still hovering close to my face, indicating some kind of move... something I wouldn't be too receptive to, without a doubt. When I lifted the breast of my t-shirt up to soak the blood up, I noticed the flash of a few cameras; The perfect time for press coverage! How long would it take for this to get on the internet as some sort of peer abuse scandal? ''Cameras...'' he mumbled, as if I hadn't noticed well before. With one hand planted below my nostrils, I shielded my eyes from blinding flashbulbs with the other while snarling ''Little privacy for the cripple?!'' at one of the sweating slabs of lard that held his camera within lens-busting distance. Killean played the part of concerned pal, staving off the onlookers with one arm while he pressed his free hand into the small of my back - well BELOW the 'just friends' line that I'm pretty sure most in-the-know adults would have known about by now. Needless to say, they chased us all the way back to my room. The LAST place I needed them to see him, of course. ''What's so god damned interesting about a girl with a bloody nose?!'' I lashed at Killean while I high-tailed it into the room, hoping he had the presence of mind to lock the door behind us, ''I mean, really... fuckbag tourists just don't have a clue.'' ''Are you sure you're okay?'' he queried, finally noticing my distaste toward the whole situation. ''A bunch of would-be photographers just rallied around me in hopes of getting pictures to sell. Pictures that involved me standing - bleeding - beside the guy I don't want people knowing I'm involved with. Should I be okay?'' ''I d--'' Fuck you, I'm talking. ''I don't HAVE to be okay, Killean. I've heard people telling me that you and Dave aren't looking too friendly.'' ''What does that have to do with this?'' his head cocked to one side, as if he had no idea what the hell I was talking about, ''Amy, y--'' ''I'm gonna keep interrupting you, so make it less painful for us both and shut the fuck up for a second,'' I snapped, ''Not only am I hearing that you guys are a little less than friendly, I'm hearing fans and wrestlers alike wondering why... why, oh WHY does Killean seem to be following Dave Gibson around anytime there's a show? I left it alone when I heard what happened the other week, figuring you two would say what you had to say and leave it at that. He's a stubborn bastard, but you are provoking him, and don't even think to pretend that you aren't. I know you two aren't about to stand around talking friendly, but I DO NOT need that battle fought for me. If he wants to be a man, he'll come to me. You don't need to be policing my social life, especially given our little... privacy issues.'' He tried opening his mouth, but knew it was best not to bother. Instead, he sarcastically raised his hands to let me continue... because that's exactly what I needed. Go figure, a man giving me what I really want for once. ''Look - I like you, okay? I do. Having whatever it is we ha... no, having THIS, and having it private... it's a breathe of fresh air. I don't have to worry about people interfering, I don't have to worry about what the fuckbags outside of these leased hotel walls think. I know you like it, too. But I assure you... if either of us fuck up and let it out? It's dying right there and then.'' ''You don't have to hide...'' ''That's why I'm not,'' How the hell could he say that? Who's hiding, here? ''I'm simplifying, which is a concept you don't seem to have grasped since, from what I'm hearing, all you want to do is further complicate the situation between my former best friend and I by your special, brute force brand of help. I don't ask you to take stock in ANY friendship I have, and you shouldn't. Quite frankly, I'm not willing to marry myself to the concept of sticking with one hair color, MUCH less some kind of relationship right now.'' He looked hurt. That wasn't quite the intention, but I was past caring. He, of course, still did... ''Amy, is this some kind of breakup?'' I steadied myself, head spinning enough to put me down, ''No - it's a confession. I'm not in this for the long haul. Maybe someday I will be, but that day isn't today... or any in the forseeable future, for that matter. I've just become a tiny bit more comfortable with who I am, but learning who that IS will be a long process. I'm not a whore, but I don't need baggage. Having you... that's a great thing, but understand that I CAN'T have the public, too. I can't do this on any scale that can't be contained to these four walls... see what I'm saying?'' Despite being a rather typical male - wrestler, much less - Killean's eyes had a gleam of understanding in them. My terms were hardly pretty, but something told me that he didn't mind being told for once. Something deep down in him wanted so badly to be possessed by somebody else... whether it had anything to do with his wife's death was nothing for me to judge. ''I understand,'' there was that little smirk that first caught my eye about him, ''As long as this can continue, I just know I... we... can make it work as it is.'' ''But for now...'' I had something on my mind, ''I'm gonna leave the room first. Wait ten minutes and come down to the dinning room, I'm gonna die if I don't have a dinner date.'' He offered a tiny peck on the lips and a nod in accordance with the orders, and I couldn't help but find myself smiling... It might just work after all. ...And there was that smile again. Chapter Two: Teamwork''Can I get the number to Trashcan Man's room?'' ''...Who, Miss?'' Insanity makes it so difficult to find a hobo's hotel suite. I mean, really. ''Uhh... Smiley?'' ''I'll need a real name, ma'am...'' ''How about you just give me the number of the last schizophrenic maniac who checked in?'' ''Well, there was a young man who came through a little earlier,'' he fumbled, ''Let me see... ah - Hoyt Williams, ma'am?'' Close enough, but no dice. ''No. I know it's hard to believe, but there's someone SLIGHTLY more disillusioned in the building.'' ''There's also a rather large African gentleman wafting about...'' Impossible. Pointless. Forget it. ''I'll find him somehow.'' When I turned around, I was actually THANKFUL to find that Killean was chatting with some of the ring crew across the lobby. We'd already had dinner... I occasionally had to stop and question whether or not he was stalking me. Right now, though, he could be useful. ''Kill!'' I laughed when the clerk at the desk dove to the floor, ''No, fuckbag... that's THAT guy's name. Killean.'' He stood up and tried chuckling off embarassment. Poor kid. While he was busy brushing himself off, Killean ran over like an obedient puppy. It was adorable to see him try so hard to be casual with me, while on my end... well, I was casual with pretty much anybody. Even though seeing his chest pump with breath underneath that wifebeater brought a little extra color to my cheeks, our interaction was carefully calculated - hardly out of the ordinary between greasy wrestling types. ''Any clue where Trashy is? He's kind of hard to find around here...'' ''You're telling me!'' Killean chortled, ''I've been looking for him since dinner. He's a recluse, there's no way we'll be able to get together as a team before the show. All we can do is hope he's on the same page...'' ''Actually, we HAVE to hope he decides on an efficient personality this week. It's gonna suck if we get stuck with Asshat The Clown or something.'' ''Amy, don't be ridiculous - pretending to be Hoyt's nearly impossible, even for TCM.'' I smiled, ''Ellis Easton's worked up for this, and I'm not letting him have the win again. Let's try to be a little more serious about... my wins aren't exactly speaking for me lately.'' ''I hate saying it,'' he was whispering, ''But I've thought about it a little... and it kinda seems like the K-Wolf motivates you. You seem slack when she's not in the buildi---OOMPH! Jesus Christ!'' He took that punch to the gut curiously well, and I could only smile, ''Sorry... instinct. Let's avoid that subject, shall we?'' ''Shit...'' he was cradling his stomach, ''Fine, we'll avoid reality as usual.'' Judging by the sarcasm in his tone, Killean hadn't taken my fist QUITE as well as I'd hoped. Now was the time to high-tail it, especially with him looking increasingly unhappy under the stares we were starting to get. ''I've gotta go, Kill. Uh, sorry about the... y'know...'' I had a classic Amy Campbell hit-and-run exit, ducking into the elevator as quickly as I could. Third floor's the charm. When I got to my room, it was little more than a blessing that housekeeping had already cleaned up. That place had been a fucking brothel when I'd left it, but it looked good as new now... no thanks to me, of course. And housekeeping meant that the mini-bar was restocked with the same nice wines I'd seen - and experienced - last night. I opened the door and saw my reflection smiling back at me from a dark bottle of merlot. Well hey, it was PRIME's tab. Speaking of that tab, using my jackknife to pop the cork off just cost the company upwards of fifty bucks... and that's American dollars. Here, EVERYTHING was American... and everything had to do with me being Canadian. Fuck the Fouth of July. Fuck this veiled sense of democracy, and fuck Texas. Why fuck Texas? I don't really know, it just sounded right at the time... I mean, it is the biggest state, and I'm hardly known as a tight. Recovering from what had to be the worst joke I'd ever made against myself, I started taking the wine down my throat straight out of the bottle. It was terrible, of course - most wines were - but a bottle of this, maybe followed by a couple of beer would do the trick I needed tonight. I was a one-woman party, as always, but my boozing got momentarily distracted as I looked to the black case across the room. It housed the thinline Tele - the gorgeous guitar I'd recieved from Ellis Easton almost anonymously, his motivations questionable... until I gave it a little thought. What was that little case doing? Distracting me from the wine. What would it do during my match? Distract me. Easton's questionable motives would have me zeroing in on him, trying to determine whether he was friend or foe... it was the perfect trap for a little Canadian who hadn't put down that instrument since childhood. Was he wooing me? Was he making an offer of peace? 'Sorry that I barged in on your shower'? Nah, it was just another fool with another tool to try bringing me out of my element in the ring. I couldn't promise myself that it wouldn't work, but I'd already recognized it as an evil. He might've been a tiny bit more intelligent than I'd thought... or seen... through the past few weeks. It hardly seemed possible but then again, I'd never thought Karina Wolfenden to be particularly bright until I got in the ring with her and ate my words. That thought called for another swig of wine, and a hurried effort to find where they'd buried the rum in that tiny hotel fridge. No rum?! Just... Coors Light and Red Bull? Christ, was this place trying to kill my tastebuds or what? Coors was Gibson's favorite drink, and the sight almost tempted me to try calling him. He wouldn't have answered anyway, and I was HARDLY the one who needed to make the first move to correct the failed friendship. After all, I had simply acted as I normally would have. It wasn't ME who overreacted. I deserved the apology, not that crybaby. It was his fault we weren't speaking. I tried explaining myself when I didn't even HAVE to. *I* made the effort. What the fuck did he do in return? Shatter glass on my floor and break the doorknob. Fucker. I was allowed to live my own life, right? I didn't have to answer to my buddies. I didn't have to answeer to anybody. My life, my rules... it was just a matter of getting those around me to play by them. Killean was starting to, and that was something I could toast to - he was making life a lot easier for me, ESPECIALLY while management was unknowingly encouraging a relationship by booking us together. Now we had an excuse to be around one another, to be in each other's rooms... you know, 'planning' and all. Practicing our holds, learning new moves and what not... Yeah, the wine was a little too strong tonight. Strong enough to give me the sense of pride I needed going into ReVolution, only hours away now. Hours I'd spend preparing for once, because there wasn't any way my image would sustain another loss. Not with Karina returning, now in the perfect position to laugh in my face. Not that it was about besting or impressing her, of course... I've just gone to shit lately. Too many outside distractions and such. People like Dave Gibson throwing temper tantrums. Things I'd rid myself of easily this week. ''No no, we'll meet tonight for the... uhh... the training. Just come in around nine, that's not too late... nobody'll think anything of it.'' The only problem I could find in being with Killean privately? It made me think of Angelo Deville. Angelo Deville and Karina 'Frostybox' Wolfenden; The most sickening pair you could imagine procreating. They took the 'Ick' factor of any relationship - Kellen Kinkade or Brand Frontier - I'd been in and multiplied it by ten, throwing in Sopranos episodes and lesbian beach volleyball with the freakish imagery. The fact remained, though, that she somehow snuck into every aspect of my life. Even the pleasurable ones. THAT was the most sickening part in it all.
