Date: 12/8/78 and 12/7/06 December 7th, 2006 Location: Four Seasons Hotel Raleigh, North Carolina Time: 8:55 PM Gratification Celebration of achievement is a bliss that is often frowned upon in excessive doses. One would consider a person who does the aforementioned to be a greedy and arrogant individual who is self-centered. The humbleness of the human mind says that one should accept the credit but never accept the praise, for the praise will bring forth an ego that can never be repressed. Devin Shakur, the twenty-seven year old CEO of Apex Technological Institute, is not a believer in that philosophy. He sits atop the balcony of a hotel room that he rented for the evening to celebrate his first full year of becoming CEO of ATI. He is staring out at the skyline with a bottle of water in his right hand. He is simply enjoying the fact that he can buy whoever or whatever he wants at the current moment. Nothing can disturb this moment of bliss. He is so entranced in this bliss that he does not hear the door to his hotel room open and shut quietly. He is so entranced in this bliss that he does not see the giant of a man wearing a ski mask sneaking up behind him with a lead pipe. Devin takes another sip of his water and cracks a smile as he looks out to see the one hundred and fifty foot building that is ATI. He can barely make the building out from this distance, but it is in his line of vision. Devin would say that he’s done alright for himself. *THWAK* The giant man would also think that he has done alright in knocking Devin Shakur across the back of the head with that pipe. A second man enters the room, much smaller in size and stature, also wearing a ski mask. He runs over to where the giant man is and looks down at his handiwork. “Let’s get this motherfucker out of here and back to the warehouse so you can deal with him accordingly,” The smaller man says. “Just make sure the stairs are clear, Captain,” The larger man says. ~~~~~~~ December 7th, 2006 Location: An empty warehouse in Raleigh, North Carolina Time: 9:32 PM Fucked It is what happens when one is in a situation in which there is no viable alternative solution to avoid verbal or physical mutilation. It is where one has done an incredible injustice that has holes which cannot be repaired by time or any other means of healing. The salt burns slowly from the deed, coursing through the bloodstream until it gets into the brain. Upon arriving there, the individual who has been fucked will either decide to inflict the punishment upon the fucker by themselves or hire another party to do so. This situation involves the latter. Devin Shakur is laying face down in the middle of the warehouse with blood pouring out of his mouth after being hit for the sixth consecutive time in the mouth with a 9 millimeter pistol. He is trying to get back up to his feet, but at this point he is wondering why he bothered doing so in the first place. He knows that this is one investment deal that he will never get back. His genius has failed him. The man standing above him is a six foot seven inch behemoth with muscles bulging out of the dark coat that he is wearing. He stands there with his arms folded, looking down at how pathetic Devin Shakur is. A man of such great wealth and power is now at the mercy of a man who lives as a bouncer and hitman for hire. The man now looks down at Shakur with a look of sheer venom and points the gun at him. “YOU ARE DONE SHAKUR! I’M PUTTING THIS FUCKING BULLET THROUGH YOUR SKULL!” The hitman screams. “NO, NO! LISTEN!” Shakur begs vehemently. “NO YOU LISTEN TO ME MOTHERFUCKER! YOU SCREWED MY PEOPLE OUT A HUNDRED GRAND! THEY DON’T LIKE TO GET SCREWED BY ANYBODY! THOSE ARE YOUR LAST RITES!” The hitman fires back. The sound of the gun cocking back, Devin Shakur can see his life flash before his eyes. “NO! NO! YOU HAVE TO GIVE ME A CHANCE TO EXPLAIN! GIVE ME TWO MINUTES!” Devin says. “FUCK THAT! HAPPY BIRTHDAY, MOTHERFUCKER!” The hitman shoots back. The memories start to Devin’s eyes like a tidal wave. “NO, NO, PLEASE! YOU DON’T WANT TO DO THIS! I’VE GOT FRIENDS! THEY DON’T DESERVE THIS,” Devin shoots back. “AND MY PEOPLE DON’T HAVE A HUNDRED GRAND, THE TWO EQUAL OUT!” The hitman says, informally denying Devin’s request at living “PLEASE! DON’T KILL ME! I’LL DO ANYTHING!” Devin says, now trying to be reasonable, considering at this point he doesn’t have anything to lose. “Hmm…Anything you say?” The hitman inquires, now mulling over the potential options that he could have here. “Yes, anything” Shakur responds. “Maybe we can work something out then…Come with me.” The hitman responds. The hitman starts his walk deep into the abyss of the warehouse darkness. In the process he pulls out a cellular phone, flips it open, punches in a couple of numbers and waits a second. Devin is reluctant to move with the man, considering that he could turn around and have to deal with a barrage of bullets coming in his direction at any second. The hitman starts talking to another individual on the phone “Yes, I’m bringing him to you now…He says he will do anything to keep his life…Right away…Yes, we’ll be there shortly. COME ON DEVIN, MOVE IT!” What was the first moment that popped into Devin Shakur’s head when he stared at the barrel of the 9 millimeter? Perhaps it was being on the other end of this heartwarming encounter, his birth. ~~~~~~~~ December 8th, 1978 Location: Raleigh Community Hospital Time: 8:34 PM The delivery waiting room of Raleigh Community Hospital is not one of lavish settings. When people sit in this room, the only oxygen that they are breathing in is nervous tension, no matter who the individual is that is breathing in the air. No filtration system can deter the particles from entering the anatomy in those crucial moments before birth. In the left corner of the room sits a man who stands out from the other two people in the room due to his dress attire. He is dressed in working clothing: A white dress shirt, a pair of overalls, blue jeans, and a pair of working boots. He is not that of a farmer, but instead that of the Puritan belief system. He is staring down at a Bible, flipping through the pages frantically, trying to find the right verse to settle his soul. He can find no such one. For Anthony Shakur, this is a sign that things are not going according to plan. They are not going the way that he envisioned that God envisioned they would go. A mahogany door opens on the other side of the waiting room and out steps a man wearing a red and black bandana that is looking directly at Anthony Shakur. He looks to be like the doctor who just performed the childbirth on Maria Shakur, Anthony’s wife. Once their eyes lock with one another, Anthony can tell that there is something wrong. The evil thoughts that Anthony has tried so long to suppress are now pushing themselves through to the forefront of his mind. The doctor, whose nametag is now lucid enough to read Dr. Simpson, now stands in front of the Puritan and looks down at him. A deep breath is emitted by the doctor before he starts. Anthony can barely keep his eyes focused on the doctor. “I am sorry to inform you Mr. Shakur…But Maria did not survive the birthing of the child. We tried to do everything that we possibly could, a lot of extra staff was brought in to try and contain the situation but we were unable to keep a grasp on the situation. There was a heavy amount of bleeding during the childbirth and we were lucky enough as it is to save the child. Unfortunately, Maria lost too much blood to even see the child after he was extracted,” The doctor says, trying to be as easing with his words as possible. The silence of a broken man is the most uncomfortable feeling in the world. “I’m terribly sorry, is there anything that I can do for you? The doctor says, putting his comforting right hand on the shoulder of Anthony Shakur in an attempt to soothe the man’s pain. Anthony’s eyes have gone to the floor, staring downward at the blue on the carpet. “Can you call my reverend up here, please? His number is [detail withheld]. Tell him that it’s Anthony Shakur,” Anthony manages to muster, with what seems to be all of the strength that his body can release. “Right away sir and to reiterate, I’m terribly sorry for your loss.” The doctor says before turning around and walking back towards the mahogany door from which he entered. Before he does, he turns around to get one last look at Anthony Shakur whose body seems to be that of just a skeleton. He hasn’t moved and Doctor Simpson does not expect him to for a good amount of time. Anthony is not grieving like somebody who just lost the love of his life. There are no tears coming down from his eyes, and he is not throwing fits of emotional rage like people of his elk have been known to doing in strenuous situations. Instead he keeps a stern demeanor. “You did all of this didn’t you? She died from complications due to the birth alright, because you are a product of the devil!” Anthony says, presumably talking to the child who he has not even seen yet. “We are going to cast the devil out of you before the night is done, so help me by the grace of God. You didn’t deserve to come into this world if this is the pain and suffering that you are going to cause the woman that I love, your own mother for Christ’s sake. What kind of kid does that to his mother, killing her by exiting the womb?” Twenty Five Minutes Later Anthony Shakur still has not moved from the position that he was last in. He is still a lost soul in the world without a purpose. “Anthony” Father Patterson says, quickly rushing to the side of one of his church members and sits down in the chair next to him on the left. Anthony moves his head upward and over towards Father Patterson so the two are making eye contact. “Ah, Father Patterson, thank you for coming.” Anthony says, barely able to muster out the words. “I’m so sorry for your loss,” Father Patterson says, now moving his arms into a hugging like manner around Anthony. The hug really does not seem to have much effect. “Why would something like that happen? She didn’t deserve to go,” Anthony says, now with some life back into his voice. Father Patterson responds, trying his best to combine the words of God and rational thinking. “I really can’t answer that Anthony. God sometimes does things that really cannot be explained by our human logic.” “She was a good follower, did everything that God asked her to do, why did she have to go the way she did?” Anthony inquires. “Well, look at it on the positive side. She exited this world by giving birth. That is the ultimate contribution to society that we can make. Her legacy will live on in the child,” Father Patterson says, doing his damnedest to put a positive spin on the situation. “Forgive me father, but I’m going to have to disagree with you on that last portion,” Anthony says “I’m sorry, on what grounds?” Father Patterson responds quizzically. “I believe that the child was the reason for her death,” Anthony calmly says. “Well there were complications from” Father Patterson manages to emit before being interrupted. “No, not in that way, I mean he was the reason of the death. She died because the child coming out of her was a product of the devil,” Anthony says, with the most conviction that he has emitted all evening. “Anthony, listen to yourself, do you really believe that in your heart?” Father Patterson says, utterly shocked by what he has just heard. Anthony replies with a bit of defiance, not particularly fond of being questioned by others. “Yes, father, I do. You have known me for over thirty five years. I have been brought up in the Puritan faith from the day that I came out of my mother’s womb. I have believed in the faith to the fullest and done my hardest to do every solitary thing that God asked me to do. So did my wife. She didn’t deserve this. That kid is going to grow up to be the son of Satan and I am convinced of that-“ “Anthony…” Father Patterson attempts to say, now at a loss for words with what he is hearing. This is completely out of character for Anthony Shakur. “That is why I have asked you here today father. I have a request of you…I want you to give this child an exorcism tonight,” Anthony says without any regret in his voice. “I will do no such thing!” Father Patterson shouts in retort. “This is something that God will want! Once he sees…” Anthony says, trying to be convincing in his argument now. “God would not want the innocence of a child shattered by an exorcism!” Father Patterson jumps in. “You don’t have any evidence to support this claim either!” “Spectral evidence was good enough to convict those devil worshipping witches in 1692!” Anthony retorts back quickly. Father Patterson is now enraged beyond belief. “Even Puritan leaders found that to be a rush to conclusions, which is what you are doing. I will have no part in this! Good night Anthony!” Father Patterson stands up and storms out of the waiting room, more appalled than he has ever been in the sixty four years of life that he has been given by the ultimate creator. He doesn’t even bother to look back at Anthony, who he now considers one of the most cynical individuals he has ever encountered. “Well if Father Patterson won’t exorcise the devil out of you…I guess I’ll have to do it…My way,” Anthony says with a scowl to the open air. ~~~~~~~~~~ Now we move to the conclusion of our warehouse adventure December 7th, 2006 Location: A different room in the warehouse Time: 9:35 PM A door slams, leaving the beaten Devin Shakur with only one companion, darkness. Devin is frantically searching around for some kind of object to support himself with as he is already groggy to begin with, and his eyes are unable to adjust to the darkness right away. He takes a few stumbling steps forward before being blinded by a spotlight that comes down from the ceiling. Shakur’s body freezes in place, but his eyes are still wondering throughout the room in an attempt to maybe find some means of escape or to find the person who made the spotlight fixate upon him. “Mister Shakur” a voice calls from the darkness. The voice is deep, feminine and authoritative in tone. “Yes, that would be me,” Devin nervously replies, using his ears to fixate his eyes upon the voice. “You are aware of the damage that you have done to me and my clients. We chose you to invest our money wisely, a hundred thousand dollars, and all of it is now gone…And we are aware of the reimbursement that you are willing to deliver in exchange. You are going to make us very happy, whether you like it or not,” The enigmatic female says with a sneer to conclude off the remarks. “I intend to do whatever I have to do to stay alive,” Shakur says with a bit more steadiness in his voice. “Good answer, because you know what my two associated and I can do. You are not going to get a third chance with us Devin. If you do not perform the task that we require down to a T each and every day that you are asked, we will dehumanize you in ways that make what happened at Abu Ghraib with those prisoners look like kindergarten bullying,” Another female voice says, coming from a couple of feet away from where the first female voice was emitted, this one sounds even more threatening in tone than the previous yet is not as deep. “Yes, I’m very aware of what you are capable of. I have heard the stories and know they aren’t pretty,” Shakur says, like a child who is being disciplined by a parent. “And we know all of your dirty little secrets Devin,” says the first female, which gets Devin’s eyes to focus back on her, “We will expose them to the highest tabloid bidder. For starters, we’ll tell them what really happened to your father. Then we’ll shatter that public image that you have gone to great lengths to protect. It’ll be a fun little endeavor for us Devin.” “So you had best follow our instructions down to a T,” Says the third voice, that of a male who sounds like he is in between the two females. “We are going to keep you on a very, very, short leash. If you even have a nanosecond thought about going outside of the parameters, the bombs will start dropping and you won’t have a second to get the fuck outta dodge either.” “So are we absolutely, one hundred percent clear Devin?” The male voice inquires. “Yes, we are clear,” Devin responds with a bit of relief in his voice. “Splendid. Now let us inform you of the task that we wish you to partake upon. You see, there was a man who screwed the three of us, collectively, over a long time ago and we have never managed to get back at him. We have had nobody capable of getting close enough to him without the bastard having a sense of security, and getting rid of them,” The male voice says, now having taken over the oratory role for the triumvirate. “This man did an injustice that we cannot speak of to you. He has always had the right people around him, sort of a security blanket if you will. However, we have it on good authority that now is an opportune time to go after him. His guard is down and I’ll bet that he’s got enough problems that he’s not going to see us coming.” “What we are asking you to do is to follow this man around. We want you to follow him around very, very closely. We want you to keep tabs on his everyday life. We want you to see how he operates. We want you to become part of his extended network,” The male concludes. “That’s it? You want me to stake some guy out?” Shakur asks, seeming somewhat surprised at the simplicity of the task. Taking notes is something that Devin is accustomed to doing as a businessman. “Oh, trust me, there’s more. Once the time is right, we want you to move in on this man and deal with him accordingly,” The male voice says. A quiet chuckle is heard coming from the male after finishing that sentence. “Wait…You want me to kill this guy?” Devin asks, now realizing the severity of the situation. “To avoid getting killed you have to kill someone else. Somebody in this country dies every thirteen seconds, and you were supposed to die a while ago. We figure that we can balance out the equation down the line with him instead of you,” the male says, never missing a step in the demeaning process. “What in the hell did this guy do to you?” Devin asks, almost appalled by what could possibly have been done to these people. He actually seems to have some legitimate sympathy for them, even if they are threatening to kill him. The deep voiced female voice interjects “That is neither here nor there Devin. You are going to become his personal incognito stalker. You have to become camouflage, and we think that we have the perfect situation for you to do this. You see…” “He works for a wrestling organization named PRIME. They are a pretty big organization. We have gotten you signed on with them. The process is going to take a couple of weeks to get you on television, but once you get there you will be able to keep tabs on him. Your background in martial arts will help you nicely when it comes to the fighting aspect so we don’t have a problem with the actual competition that you will end up facing,” The deep voiced female concludes. “So you want me to follow this man around and when the time is right, knock him off?” Shakur asks, now seeming to have an understanding of what is being asked of him. “Precisely, now will you do it?” The male voice asks. “Do I really have a choice?” Devin responds. “Now, you will need to do a couple of things first. You really do not use your private jet that much. You will need to do so frequently as this organization travels on a consistent basis,” The male voice says. “I can do that,” Devin responds with a tad bit of enthusiasm in his voice. “You also need to do one other thing…” The male voice says “Which is?” Devin replies “Sell all of your stocks at APT,” The male voice blurts out. “You are out of your fucking mind!” Devin responds. “Pulling a trigger for me only takes a second Devin. I have no heart and to you I have no face. Nobody will be able to identify me. Nobody has a grudge against you. I would never pull the trigger again of a gun in my life. I’m a model citizen. They aren’t going to suspect me of a crime since I will have no known connection to you.” The male voice says with supreme confidence in his voice. The look on Devin Shakur’s face says it all. He does not know who this is. He does not know where to find this man. Shit, he doesn’t even know where he is at this point. The light female voice calls out. “There is no way out of this Devin. Accept your fate or accept your death, your call.” “Very well then,” Devin says. “I will get to work on an exit strategy for selling my stocks and getting prepared for PRIME.” End
