Fatherhood - Chapter 15 - Revelations (JitC 2009 Round 2 RP)

16 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 16, 2009 AUGUST 16, 2009 COLOSSUS VI: NIGHT TWO He used the steel cage to ascend to the top turnbuckle with over 40,000 fans erupting, praying that Bryan Dawkins would finally end the reign of terror brought upon PRIME by Devin Shakur. He stood atop the turnbuckle, staring down at his fallen opponent before flashing his "hang loose" gesture to the thousands upon thousands of fans screaming his name and attempting to will him to victory. They all return the gesture happily as Dawkins shifts his focus yet again to Shakur, but this time morphing that "hang loose" gesture into the much more widely known middle-finger. Then, Dawkins took to the air, hoping to land the final blow and limp out of Colossus the victor. However, a "cameraman" from ringside intervened, somehow catching Dawkins in the knees with a thin pipe, knocking the Flyin’ Hawaiian off-target, sending him crashing to the mat violently. That cameraman, of course, was Shakur’s brother, Christian Daniels, and it just went to show that no matter what the circumstances, Dawkins was still subjected to play the numbers game against Shakur and Daniels. Dawkins lay in a crumpled heap in the middle of the ring, next to a now stirring Shakur. Daniels was now upon the ring apron, encouraging his brother to quickly end the match to minimize the damage that had already been done. Dawkins was physically drained, and now, possibly without his greatest assets: speed, quickness, agility, and high flying ability. The beaten and bloodied Devin Shakur slowly limps toward the Bruh amidst a sea of boos, which actually seemed to fuel the Man in Black. Picking Dawkins up and landing two hard knees to the head, Shakur locked his hands around the waist of his foe and planted his head into the ground with an intentionally botched Tombstone Piledriver. Carefully positioning Dawkins’ body and, more specifically, the head and neck area to assure a violent collision between Dawkins’ head and the canvas, Shakur perfectly calculated the deadly move, which resulted in Dawkins collapsing immediately onto the mat. Dawkins was instantly out cold, and was deathly motionless as Shakur covered him for the win. Following the bell, ridiculous amounts of debris poured from the fans into the ring, showering Shakur and Daniels after the win. Dawkins, however, was still motionless, and EMT’s were on their way toward the ring as Daniels retrieved his brother from the cage. Almost immediately, the EMT’s secure a neck brace on PRIME’s Resident Hawaiian as they begin their preliminary tests. At first, Dawkins is still out like a light until after a few moments, his eyes open just enough for the paramedics to see the glint reflect from his eyes. After a few more moments, his extremities began to regain feeling and the Hawaiian began to show signs of life with each passing moment. Finally, Dawkins is strapped onto a stretcher and slowly wheeled across the field and toward the dugout where close friend and uncle to his first child, Chandler Tsonda, is watching with a concerned face. Dawkins’ stretcher is loaded into a nearby ambulance and is immediately being tended to by more EMT’s than you could shake a stick at. The ambulance doors shut quickly and the vehicle speeds off into the night, lights-a-flashing, as the paramedics continue to attempt to tend to the Flyin’ Hawaiian. This would be the last that the PRIME world would see of Dawkins for almost three months. * * * NOVEMBER 5, 2009 AFTER REVOLUTION 210 Darkness shrouded the entirety of Bryan Dawkins’ locker room upon the close of PRIME’s visit to New Orleans for ReVolution 210. This day marked the return of the Flyin’ Hawaiian to PRIME television for the first time in nearly three months, and he surely did not disappoint, defeating Johnny Raindance to advance to the second round of the annual Jewel in the Crown tournament. Normally the atmosphere inside a Bryan Dawkins locker room post-Revolution would be filled with tropical music, pineapples, and the other two-thirds of the Three Amigos. However, this time was different. Rayne hadn’t been seen on PRIME television for longer than Dawkins, and Tsonda hadn’t been seen anywhere near a PRIME event for more than a few weeks. Dawkins was back to being a loner, which is how he was when he entered the company almost 20 months ago. The only thing that tipped off the fact that Dawkins was actually in the locker room was the beacon of light emitted by his iPod, which was plugged into one ear. From that light, it was apparent that Dawkins was either asleep or having a migraine; his face was covered with a damp towel and he was laying lazily atop the couch. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK The knocking broke the silence of the moment, but didn’t stir the Flyin’ Hawaiian. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK This time, the knocking caused Dawkins to flinch, but other than that, still no response. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK "Bryan! You in there?" Finally, Dawkins mumbled from underneath the towel. "…depends on who’s askin’." Carefully, Dawkins removed the towel from his face and peered toward the doorway. The door was cracked slightly and all he could see was a faint outline of a figure standing in said doorway. CLICK "SHIT BRUH!" The figure turning on the light was not something Dawkins was prepared for, apparently, judging from the reaction. Wiping the blurriness from his eyes, Dawkins stood up from the couch and approached the figure. "Sonjay, why didn’t ya tell me it was you?" Sonjay Taku was a close friend of the Dawkins’ for many years. He started as Kenneth Dawkins’ personal trainer and grew to be one of his closest friends. However, around the time Bryan turned eleven years old, the two were rarely seen with each other. Bryan, however, came to become very close friends with Sonjay when the two met, partially through Bryan’s father, in HCW, where Sonjay served as one of the head trainers. When Sonjay heard about Bryan’s injury at the hands of Devin Shakur, he made sure to offer his services to Bryan. Knowing Sonjay’s track record with his father, Bryan happily agreed. "Sorry Bryan, didn’t think you’d be zoning out in the dark like that. Actually figured you might’ve left." The two share a quick handshake, but Dawkins winces in pain on his short walk back to the couch. "Nah bruh. Movin’ a little too slow at the moment to leave this quickly." Dawkins gingerly lowered himself onto the couch, letting a sigh of relief slip out after becoming semi-comfortable. Sonjay took a seat next to the Bruh. "So what’s on your mind? Saw you shot me a text, so I got over here as soon as I could." Sonjay offered to come out on the road with Dawkins for the first month or two into his return to wrestling, just to offer any assistance or rehab that the young Hawaiian might need. Again, Bryan happily agreed, especially since Sonjay’s expenses would be paid for by PRIME. Dawkins winced as another pinch shot down his spine and spread through his entire body. Needless to say, Sonjay noticed. "I told ya, Bryan. Should’ve waited another month." Bryan attempted his best to play the part of the innocent. Unfortunately, you can’t bullshit a bullshitter. "Bryan, ya damn near died a few months back. Ya should be happy you can walk after what you went through." "This is what I do, bruh. I don’t know anything else." Sonjay slouched into the sofa and let out a sigh. "Seriously, bruh. You know how hard it was for me to sit around for almost twelve weeks doin’ no physical activity?" "Bryan, you have Nicholas. That should be more than enough to keep you occupied for twelve YEARS, much less twelve months." Again, Dawkins attempted to play the innocence card. "Believe me, bruh, Nicholas is my life and that’s never changin’. I love the little guy to death, and I go home to see him every week. That’s about as much as I’d see him if I had him out on the road with me anyway. I’m doin’ this so he can have a good life. And while I’m aware that doin’ this might shorten my life a bit, I’m pretty sure I’m willing to sacrifice that for my son." Again, another shot of pain caused the Hawaiian to flinch sharply. "All I’m sayin’ is that you could’ve used another eight weeks or so on the shelf. You had one of the worst concussions that I’ve seen in a LONG time, and your neck was about as mangled as it could be without requiring any sort of drastic repair. Seriously, kid, you’re lucky." Dawkins began to flash that famous smile before having it wiped away by another shot of intense pain. "I know, bruh. And I thank you for comin’ out here with me for a while, makin’ sure I’m alright and everything. Otherwise it’d be up to that Fihlguud and his group of merry men, and to be honest with ya, bruh, I trust them about as much as I trust Shakur and Daniels." Sonjay gave a slight nod to Dawkins. "No problem, Bryan. I did this with your father for how many years, so why not take on the better half, yeah?" For the third time in as many moments, Dawkins began to flash that signature smile, only to have it interrupted by another attack of pain. "Thanks, bruh. Now what can we do about my neck? Feels like three months ago all over again." "Well, at least you’re movin’ and talkin’ this time. I guess for the time being, we might have to put you on some pain meds and just hope that it’s something that will go away with time." Again Dawkins winces, but this time, not from the pain. "And it looks like you need it pretty badly. You’ve winced almost a half dozen times since I walked in here." "No bruh, it’s not that…" Sonjay peers at Dawkins with a questioning look on his face. "What’s on your mind, Bryan?" "The painkillers. I’m not a big fan, to be honest with ya." Sonjay shifted in his seat. Dawkins turned to his trainer and put on a serious expression. "Why’s that?" "Come on, bruh, don’t play dumb." Now Sonjay was the one attempting to play the innocent role. "Seriously, Bryan. I don’t know what you’re talking about." Dawkins stared at Sonjay straight-faced. Finally after a few moments of awkward silence, Sonjay sighed. "Alright. What do you know?" "Sonjay, I was there that night. When dad faced El Drago." Sonjay sighed deeply this time. "I know." Dawkins shot a shocked glance at his trainer. "You did?" "Yep. You were a ringrat for HCW for how many years? You had to have been there." Shaking his head, Dawkins went to speak, but was stopped in his tracks by another shot of pain. "No?" "Bruh, I was there as a ring rat for more years than I can remember. But after the match, I went to see dad, and I saw what happened between the two of you." An eerie silence crept into the room as the proverbial cat had been let out of the bag. Or the everyone finally noticed the elephant in the room. However you want to put it, the truth was now out there for everyone to see. "Sorry you had to see that, Bryan." Shrugging, Dawkins forgave Sonjay. "Hey, it happens, bruh. But if ya could, tell me what actually happened?" "Well, it goes back further than the El Drago match, but that was when things really ramped up…" * * * Kenneth Dawkins lay face down on the cold leather upholstery of his living room couch after retaining his HCW Championship in the first of many matches with El Drago. The match was an instant classic, one that is still being downloaded thousands of times every day by wrestling enthusiasts around the globe. The technicality of the match was breathtaking, the flow of the match was perfect, and the ending was one of the truly spectacular endings in the history of independent wrestling. The match was back and forth. Dawkins, the company’s ultra babyface and most well-rounded wrestler, was matched up with El Drago, the most talented of the fleet of Mexican wrestlers to make the trek to HCW to compete. El Drago’s moveset was the high flyer’s dream, with a multitude of aerial attacks aimed at slowing bigger, more powerful opponents, keeping them on their toes at all times. This match was no different. Kenneth and El Drago went back and forth, neither really gaining any type of advantage until the halfway point of the match, where Drago’s superior endurance began to become the x-factor in the matchup. Little by little, Drago’s speed and athleticism was getting to the older, much more powerful Dawkins. Even when Dawkins would become luck y enough to catch the Mexican high flyer, El Drago would find a way to slither his way out of harm’s way or completely counter Dawkins’ attack. After almost 45 minutes, it seemed as though Drago had completely worn down the champ, and it seemed as though it was only a matter of time before his hand would be risen to proclaim Drago the new HCW Champion. However, on this night, it was not to be. Dawkins finally caught a whiff of luck by catching a flying body press attempt by Drago, finishing him off with a devastating sit-out piledriver for the win. That was the first of many meetings between the two HCW powers, and throughout their series of matches, the wins for each wrestler evened out over time, to the point where each competitor had the same number of wins and losses. However, this first match drove Kenneth Dawkins to take other measures. * * * "Here ya go, Ken." Sonjay handed a syringe and a bottle of medication to Dawkins, who stashed the medicine bottle quickly into his bag. "Thanks Sonjay. This should put me over the top." "Ken, in all due respect, I don’t think ya need this. We don’t really know what all this stuff does, except for the obvious." Kenneth rolled his eyes and clicked the cap from the needle and ejected any air bubbles from inside the syringe. Taking a deep breath, Dawkins plunged the needle deep into his buttocks and ejected the liquid testosterone deep into his body. "We’ll see, Sonjay. We’ll see." * * * For years, the steroids and painkillers became common for Dawkins. He was using the steroids religiously and the pain medication was being eaten like candy. In the ring, Dawkins became even more dominant than he had been at any point in his career. The fans couldn’t get enough of their hometown wrestler and he couldn’t get enough of their support. Every show put on by HCW was dominated by Dawkins t-shirts, signs, and the like. Kenneth Dawkins was the face of HCW. And if any matches were a prime example of what HCW stood for, the Dawkins/El Drago series fit the bill. The matchups between the two most talented stars in the company were instant classics no matter what the situation. It was one of those rare cases where there was no underdog and no favorite. Just good guy versus bad guy. Dawkins, the good, and El Drago, the bad. Until that night in 1996. * * * The match had been going for nearly 40 minutes and for the first time in the series, Kenneth was struggling. El Drago was pushing the pace aggressively and shockingly, Kenneth just simply could not keep up. No matter what Kenneth did, it seemed as though El Drago was one step ahead of him at all times. But like the champion that he was, Kenneth showed no signs of frustration. Dawkins had been thrown over the top rope and was crumpled in a heap on the cold padded concrete floor. He was physically drained and El Drago was preparing to go in for the kill. Most of the fans in attendance were certain that the match was only mere moments from ending, and HCW would end the night crowning a new champion for the first time in nearly three years. The referee began his count as El Drago rested against the ropes with the hope of winning the match by countout and beginning his reign as champion. As the ref reached the count of eight, Kenneth rose to his feet with the help of the guard rail and El Drago lept from the ring to meet him. Unfortunately for the Mexican superstar, Dawkins saw it coming and rolled out of the way, leaving El Drago to crash throat-first onto the railing. It seemed as though Dawkins would now take control of momentum and end the match. Taking hold of El Drago’s mask, Dawkins began to manhandle his much smaller foe, slamming his face into the ringpost, steps, and barricade. The ref had begun his count and was now at the count of nine. Dawkins peered at the ref and shrugged, continuing his assault on El Drago. The ten count was reached, and the bell was rung. The match was over. Or was it? Dawkins decided that the ten count simply wasn’t enough and left a beaten El Drago leaning against the railing in search of a steel chair. After retrieving the weapon of choice, Dawkins reared back and sent a massive chair shot into the skull of his nemesis, prompting the bell to be rung yet again. However, yet again, Dawkins refused to stop, sending numerous chair shots crashing into Drago’s skull. Eventually, after what had seemed like an eternity, Dawkins tossed the chair aside and made his way to the back, leaving El Drago a beaten and bloody mess on the padded concrete floor. * * * "I was there for that, Sonjay. I saw you and dad fight afterward, and that was when I realized dad was on the drugs." Sonjay sighed and shrugged simultaneously. "I’ll admit I was part of the problem. We looked into the possibilities together, and figured it wasn’t a bad thing to do. I’ll take my share of the responsibility for it, but it was ultimately Ken’s decision to keep doing it." Dawkins peered blankly at Sonjay. "So now I know that it was true, I think I’m starting to realize why he is the way he is now. I mean, dad won’t even talk to me anymore. Shit, Sonjay, he tried to take Nicholas away from me." Sonjay frowned at Bryan, but extended a hand to pat the young Hawaiian on the back. "Well maybe now it’s time for you to get his side of the story." Dawkins half-smiled at Sonjay before being interrupted by a shot of pain. "Yeah bruh, ya might be right. But I think we should figure out what’s goin’ on with me, first." With that, Dawkins grabbed his bag and followed Sonjay out the door. Obviously the pain medication wasn’t the way to go, in Bryan’s mind. Becoming his father was the one thing in life he knew that he didn’t want to do, and taking those pain medications was just too much like what his father did. The bottom line was simple: Bryan Dawkins would not become Kenneth Dawkins.
