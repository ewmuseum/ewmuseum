Conquering Mount Goliath

6 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 6, 2006 'Conquering Mount Goliath' 19 B.C. The land of Negev had bore witness to its greatest tragedy to date. The rise of King Worth II had resulted in division amongst the Negev Kingdom, isolating the kingdom into two hemispheres…the western, populated by followers of King Worth, and the eastern, populated by those who cursed King Worth’s throne, accusing him of fraudulence against their former ruler, Sir Sebastian. The consequence of this division amongst territories would come in the form of holy wars, violent crusades, and radial invasions. A municipal war was brewing between the Spartans (citizens) of the Negev Kingdom. Five years of hostilities and combat would pass between the two rivaling territories with no end in the foreseeable sight. The Spartans of the eastern hemisphere had appointed King Nelson as their ruler, exalting his name above that of Worth’s, but during the midst of an invasion war, King Nelson was slaughtered. With their fearless leader having met defeat, the eastern hemisphere’s future looked bleak. For the first time in the span of five years, the war between the two territories seemed to be nearing an end, and the restoration of the Negev Kingdom seemed all but completed. But just as things began to reach a pivotal point of return, the eastern hemisphere fell before a newfound power, one even greater than King Nelson. This figure would come to be known as Emperor Curiosity. Emperor Curiosity was widely recognized around the kingdom as a sovereign figure, an unbeatable one even. He’d participated in many Spartan competitions, but never met defeat. He was the greatest of them all, and his ascension to the top of the eastern hemisphere was heralded as a gift from the fathers above. Meanwhile, King Worth and his western hemisphere were grooming soldiers for the inevitable grand war that was set to occur between the two territories. The plan was to destroy Emperor Curiosity, for without him, the eastern hemisphere was destined to fall. But then realization set in…Emperor Curiosity was an undisputed, unbeatable beast that no man had ever defeated. His powers and abilities were far greater than the human mind could comprehend. Simply put, he could not be defeated. In the midst of the grooming, however, the King Worth was met with a highly unexpected round of news. Emperor Curiosity, in all of his glory, was marking a treacherous path towards the kingdom, seeking to take out the exalted ruler by his own hands. Worth panicked. He was in no shape, form, or fashion equipped to carry on a battle, especially with someone as great as Emperor Curiosity. He had to decipher a plan, and he needed to decipher it quick. It just so happened that visiting the land that day was none other than Vangelus the Great, son of the Almighty Czar Olsig. Vangelus the Great had come to Negev in pursuit of his sister, but those plans would find themselves short lived. Upon arrival, Vangelus found himself ambushed by a round of guards, all of whom would subsequently carry his stunned body to the palace of King Worth. Upon arriving, Olsig found himself embraced by the King…a feat that not many had gained the pleasure of experiencing. With even more irony, the King fell to his knees before the feet of Vangelus the Great, pleading his assistance in taking out Emperor Curiosity. The guards watched in amusement. Besides the fact that they’d never watched their king engage in such a humiliating act of begging, Vangelus was hardly the typical choice to take on a challenge as great as Emperor Curiosity. No not Vangelus the Great. He was nothing more than a peasant. His rags proved that. He’d been defeated countless times before with his only true claim to fame being an impressive feat in a hardcore competition held on the mounts of Olympia. Add to that the fact that'd he suffered defeat at the hands of Sir Tchu of Gallus and Sir Nova of Mount Enoch simultaneously. He was a failure, through and through. Even Vangelus himself stood amused by the fact that he, of all Spartans in Negev, was chosen by the kingdom to take on Emperor Curiosity. He knew that his life would be greatly threatened by this, but he accepted. To reject an offer by the king would be sure blasphemy. Thousands of Spartans laughed and joked before the face of King Worth, stating that he should have saved himself the embarrassment of using someone like Vangelus and instead should have simply submitted. They all doubted Vangelus’ abilities…some even jumped over to the Eastern Hemisphere because of such a foolish decision…but Vangelus the Great yet fought on. He met Emperor Curiosity on the hills of GTT (the Grand Teresa Territory), and stood toe to toe with the giant. His boldness was met only with laughs from Emperor Curiosity who accused Vangelus of being a fool for accepting such challenge. Vangelus the Great responded in terms by declaring the fact that he’d been called a fool during his entire voyage to these hills…he’d become immune to such lament terms. He went on to question Emperor Curiosity’s methods, asking if the true fool was the man who showed acts of courage or the man who underestimated his opponent. Such insults caused Emperor Curiosity to strike, wielding his long and powerful sword. Vangelus, on the other hand, had no weapon. All he could rely on was his wits. The swing of the sword was so powerful that when ducked by Vangelus, Curiosity fell to the ground…a fall that caused a huge shake in its foundation. With momentum in his favor, Vangelus trounced on Emperor Curiosity and pried the sword out his hand. He swiftly lifted the sword above his head and brought it straight down, piercing it into the heart of Emperor Curiosity. Blood pelted from his heart, staining the hills of GTT in mass amounts. Thousands of Spartans watched in amusement… Vangelus the Great had defeated Emperor Curiosity. In a seconds notice, all of the Spartans rushed to the helm of Olsig, ascending them above their heads as they proudly boasted his feat. The same man that they'd laughed at and spoke ill-will of was now the same man with whom they'd all come to kneel before. He was their hero. The feat was so great, even, that it reunited the hemispheres. With the eastern hemisphere all but beaten without their fearless leader, they accepted defeat, reuniting the hemispheres. Vangelus the Great had gone from zero to hero in one stab of a sword. One man, however, was not as impressed. That man... Elder Snow. Elder Snow was a Negev legend. His “tank” democracy made him a pivotal figure in the land's history. He'd only recently made his return to Negev, causing mass waves of praise. To have a man as honorable as he reject Vangelus the Great's victory astonished many, and even persuaded them to do the same. One stormy evening, Elder Snow stepped out on a limb, gracing Vangelus the Great with a challenge at the stroke of midnight on the plains of JITC (the Jabez International Territory Crossing). With every stroke of momentum in his favor, Vangelus the Great accepted the challenge. So at the stroke of midnight, the two clashed to the sound of roaring thunder and the sight of illuminating strokes of lightening. The two warriors were so ferocious and determined in their quest that the battle raged on for seven hours...but on the seventh hour, it ceased. Vangelus the Great defeated Elder Snow. He was now widely recognized as the Universal Sovereignty. --- “Sir, wake up! We've landed!” With sudden awe, Vangelus Olsig awakened from his deep sleep, mind still lingering over the strange dream he'd just endured. “Come on, sir! You've got a big match to get ready for!” He rubbed his eyes and pushed himself out of the seat in which he'd sunk into. He'd re-entered the real world, hoping that the one in which he'd just exited would resemble it in every way. With confidence, he smiled. “Yeah...guess I do.”
