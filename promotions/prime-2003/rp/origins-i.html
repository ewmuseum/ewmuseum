Date: March 2002 “Don’t back up Ricky! You’re not in there with an eggshell. Get that right hand underneath!” Masahiro Takeru barked the Gospel whereas the Apostles took dictation like court stenographers when saving the world. Takeru was entrusted with more than a dozen souls. The irony of being a religious figure on the island, Masahiro’s reverence and good work would never spread West. He would raise the sons of the mortals whose back he climbed to become a deity, and shelter the fallen running from grace. “Lock it! Lock it!” Takeru’s truth coated an empty Saitama Super Arena. In six hours Tokyo would enter the pores of the Arena for the next sermon. The World League, the Tokyo based promotion owned by legendary American promoter Chad Merritt to give him a foothold in the Asian market and groom potential talent for the CSWA, was celebrating its fifth Anniversary next month. Takeru was the only man who could keep the promotion running in Japan, and to say he was deeply respected for it would be an understatement. “Great counter Troy. Use your leverage, yes!” A spattering of workers roamed ringside watching Ricky Ohno, a native son, and a new kid to the World League, Lindsay Troy, spar in the ring. Troy’s debut was three days ago, a loss in a 6-man tag match, but she’d impressed the crowd if not the God himself. Troy’s desperation was too evident in the ring, as Ohno was making her look helpless. She’d missed a crucial spot near the end of the match and, while easily covered, hadn’t slept since. Women in a man’s world aren’t forgiven rookie mistakes, less so when they’ve been in the business six years already and should have known better. Troy and Ohno locked horns again. She slid between his legs as soon as he leaned in and took Ricky’s feet from out under him. He tapped to a Fujiwara arm bar seconds later. “Beautiful, Lindsay. Beautiful.” Troy turned to Takeru, bowed in reverence to the promoter, then took a gulp of water in a neutral corner while ignoring Ricky’s eagerness to square again. Fifteen minutes of hard-sparring into the workout and she was barely winded, a testament not only to her conditioning but to her controlling the workout’s pace to her liking. Lindsay nodded toward one of the guys leaning against the apron to get his attention. “You sure we’re not taking up your time, Keiji?” she inquired in Japanese, the frequent trips to the country as a youth and the hours of study she put into learning the language were once again proving to be helpful. The chestnut-haired fighter shook his head and gave her a small smile. “Susumu’s late, as usual. You take your time; none of here are going to rush you, although Ricky’s getting impatient.” Turning halfway to face Ricky again, Troy gave him a wink. Ohno swiftly charged into the corner and was greeted with a chestful of turnbuckle. “Where are the elephants and pedophiles making balloon animals? Personally, I always love it when they make the number 8.” Mashiro’s heart sank at the voice riding up behind him. “This is a circus, right? Girls wrestling boys. Look Chief, if she wants to be immersed in a large glass tank of water and hold her breath for seven minutes, great. You can probably sell that. But what are we doing here? Have we really stopped to answer that question yet?” “Joey,” Takeru greeted the hard slap on his back with disdain. “Miss me?” Joey Melton fell into an empty seat next to Takeru with all the grace of a dog finding a sinkhole. Takeru shifted his eyes to see the mess: black lived-in slacks, and a white dress shirt missing the top three buttons. Joey pushed his gray shades deeper over his eyes and scratched a hole through his five o’clock shadow. Melton married out of obscurity over twenty years ago. That it found him like true love a year from his fortieth birthday gutted the last of his conscience. If he were two inches taller he could put on a suit and rob every bank on the island, but he was sort of nothing now. No more important or famous than someone who guesses the number of jellybeans in a jar at a fair. 7684. Melton knows the number before every contest. Always has, but there’s romance in dancing when your own mortality is a joke no one’s heard. He’s in Tokyo because he’s not needed anywhere else. If Joey went home he’d drink himself to death. In the bed of Earth where the carnival ran the night before, he has denial and the element of surprise when he cops to being a drunk. “Ten days later than I was told to expect you. What is it this time? Shingles? Another radioactive man-eating dog you had to corral and put to sleep?” “Hey, that one was real. The government confiscated the film. No, it’s these new fucking security measures since 9/11. Six months since and the airport is still tougher to get out of than a Concentration Camp.” Masahiro turned his attention back to the session in-ring and forgot Joey for as long as he could. “I’m contractually obligated to book you. That’s why you’re here. Every other body I’ve got is busting ass because they believe in what we’re doing.” “You’ve got two guys that can work anywhere else Takeru. Don’t kid yourself. That’s why I’m here. To make ’em say ‘Ow’ when hit right and teach ’em how much to tip. Sorry if it’s not on your schedule.” “You’re a disgrace.” “Am I?” “The shame is someone will inevitably look up to you and wait like Christmas morning to find substance that burned out years ago. When was it exactly, Melton?” “You’re king of a world I’ve been sent to because I’m forgotten. What does that say Masahiro? It means your line has been in the water for fifty years but you haven’t caught one fucking fish. Don’t bait me with your shit.” Takeru’s stare held out of pity before turning back to the ring session. “Sober up before you come back tonight….” In ring, Troy dropped Ohno with a stiff forearm that reverberates through the Arena. Flesh slapped hard against the canvas, the result garnering a grin from Lindsay and a groan from Ricky. “Whoa!” A suddenly interested Melton howled from his hole, “Ricky, if you sold that well when the gate was in you wouldn’t be driving American cars.” Joey’s jab drew a quick laugh from most watching and embarrassed Troy. She gave him and Takeru a sidelong glance while Ricky got to his feet, then held up her hand to give pause to the fight. “Is it true?” Troy asked, her voice cutting through the space that separated her from the respected promoter and the obviously wasted Melton. The rest of the boys turned to look at Joey while someone tossed Ricky a towel and a bottle of water. “Is what true, dear?” “That you’ll work for food,” she said, leaning against the ropes. “Because if so, I can feed you for the night but the catch is you get your ass kicked.” “Oh, shit,” Ohno spat out, laughing with the crowd and politely stepping out of the line of fire. “Honey, this is how a man is supposed to look,” answered Melton, pointing to his appearance. “Not how a pimp looks.” “Can he do anything besides drink?” Troy asked Takeru. “Can he roll over and send me to the moon as well?” “Look doll,” Joey said in a cold, hard voice, rising to his feet and walking to the apron. “Don’t talk about me like I’m not here.” “I’m sorry, I’ve been here a week and you haven’t been. But you were big once, right?” Lindsay smirked, knowing the more white she showed the hotter Melton got. “Princess, I was big when men wrestled bears in Main Events, and I’m big now when apparently they wrestle Amazon dykes who dressed their baby dolls in blue jeans instead of sun dresses and revealing mini-skirts. But choices are always made for us that define who we are. Instead of being under me sucking my dick, you’re in my ring trying to prove you have one. Whatever works.” Troy seethes, Joey’s last remark got to her. “Why don’t you get in here with me and show me you have some balls if you’re so worried that the size of your dick might pale in comparison.” The tension stuck in everybody’s throat. Joey cleared first. “I don’t have my gear handy, sweetie.” “Fair enough,” Troy turned to walk back to the center of the ring, “At least I know who to go to when I need to borrow a dress.” “That’s it bitch!” Melton dove through the bottom rope in a fit of rage, stood three feet from Troy and lifted off his shirt, careful not to break the remaining buttons. “A little respect will take you a long way in this business!” “Joey…” said Ricky as he tried to step-in-between. “Out!” Melton’s stare was foreboding. “Just two co-workers sparring. Right, Princess?” Lindsay smiled. “Queen. ‘Princess of the Ring’ is too wordy and doesn’t fit down the side of my pants. And if this is what we’re doing, why not make it interesting?” Melton’s left eyebrow rose, intrigued. “What’d you have in mind?” “Shoot rules. No blows to the head. Three hundred dollars to the winner.” ”I hate owing a pimp money. But, I reckon this is a safe call. You’re on darlin’.” “Who wants action?” Ohno frantically called out for bets. Everyone, including Masahiro, got in. “You irritants are getting in on this?” “Boys.” Troy chuckled, pulling the curly wisps that had escaped from her hair elastic back into place. “Fuck me, what’s that?” Joey’s jaw dropped as he looked behind Troy. “Oh, do you really think I’m that stupid?” Melton cautiously backed away from her, giving every indication that he’s not joking around. Godzilla really does exist, and he’s standing right behind her. Squinting in speculation, she threw a glance over her shoulder. Forearm shot! Heeling right from the start. Classic Melton. Lindsay stumbled forward, falling to one knee and cursing herself for being played for a fool. Grinning like the Cheshire Cat in Alice in Wonderland, Melton dragged her up to her feet by her pony-tail and prepared to shoot her across the ring with an Irish-whip. Unfortunately for him, Troy held on. Pivoting her hips, she planted her feet and used the momentum to send Joey against the far ropes. On the rebound, Melton got a first-hand feel of what size 9 Italian leather felt like. KICKS! JABS! STRIKES! OH MY! All Muay-Thai and all unchecked and uninhibited. “JESUS...” Joey cried, but his plea to the son of God went unanswered. He tried to cover up, but Troy was much, much too fast for him and worked him back into a corner. “Time out! TIME OUT!” With his upper body falling halfway out between the ropes Melton desperately made his hands into a T formation, petitioning the boys at ringside to grant his request. They didn’t, but Troy did back up a bit...only to spit in his face once he brought his body completely back into the ring. Joey pawed the splutter from his cheek and shot her an angry look, which she returned in kind. “Go fuck yourself, Hilary Swank,” Melton walked past her, breathing hard. Troy didn’t bother facing him; with her back still turned, he lunged at her like a suburban ninja...with none of the stealth and a lot of hot air. Not about to fall for another cheap shot, she quickly spun out of the way and brought him down with a drop toehold. Instinctively, she hooked in an STF. “FUCK!” “Mama hurting you?” “You bit...GODDAMNIT!” Joey cried as Lindsay cinched the hold in tighter. “C’mon Joe-Joe do you give?” Keiji asked with a smile, while Ricky taunted Melton with the towel. “You want me to throw this in for you, big guy?” The ropes were out of reach and Joey knew he wasn’t going anywhere anytime soon. Far be it for an Angel of Mercy to descend to the ring and convince Troy to let him go out of the goodness of her heart. Embarrassed, he tapped the mat, and once Lindsay got off him he rose on shaky legs to his feet and kicked the bottom rope. “Well, I must say you’ve given me quite the workout,” she said as the guys all climbed into the ring and crowded around her; the new girl in town was damn sure lookin’ good! “But I believe we had a deal.” Melton watched her long, tanned arm uncurl in front of him, palm extended awaiting the spoils. He dug into his back pocket for his wallet. “It’s fucking prom night and my ass is Carrie,” Joey snarled as a handful of bills were thrown at Lindsay’s feet. Not wanting to see her pick up the money or look at her any longer, Melton rushed out of the ring and stormed past Masahiro. “I’m taking my vacation days, Takeru, and that’s the last time I’m in a ring with Scarlett O’Hara. I promise you that!”
