Greetings

23 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 23rd November 2010 The view changes pace with a flicker to a much more peaceful surrounding that I expect you are used to, in fact a rustic Victorian restaurant is the location from which Prime's newest star is to make his first appearance. At other tables couples and groups of people are chatting, drinking and eating. Our subject sits alone, his body sat adjacent to his table as he welcomes us with open hands. ???: “Hello. My name Dr. Clay ‘Chainsaw’ Krueger. I would like to issue my welcome to all of you Prime wrestling supporters, the rather simple production workers, the questionable ‘talent’ on the roster and especially the management. I admit I do hold the management of this spiffing conglomerate in the highest of regard.” Dr. Krueger’s appearance is almost immaculate. His hair is obviously highly conditioned, not a single hair is out of place on his mane and he naturally looks down at his audience behind a slanted nose. He is wearing what appears to be a very expensive suit, custom-made surely. His accent is a British one, obviously phony but easy on the ear. Krueger: “Allow me to explain who I am and why I is here in this company. I was once a respected psychiatrist. In fact, I was considered one of the best in all the land. My views were respected, my work lauded and my opinions sout after. My peers would see my name credited to an article in a journal and immediately the sentiments would be accpeted as fact. I was a saviour to many people less fortunate and less gifted than one’s self. I provided salvation and I helped others through their problems, in some cases even reformed their character. But what about me? For where was the assistance when I myself needed it. I had no peers on my level. I was used by the system and abused by the very system that I swore to serve.” A cup of coffee is delivered to Clay Krueger’s table, he examines the contents and proffers a word of thanks to the waitress. As soon as she turns her back, his demeanour changes. Krueger: “What a ghastly beast of a woman. Back to my tale, I needed salvation but it was not forthcoming. I was dismissed from my practice for… well, that revelation is best saved for another day. Let us just say that my reputation was both shattered and assinated. So in my infinite wisdom, I have sought refuge in this enterprise. I will wrestle. I will release all of my bitterness and frustration in your squared circle. I am the very epitome of a “thinking man’s wrestler”, how could I not be when I am the ultimate “thinking man”. My mind is second-to-none and you will bear witness as I outthink all of your current roster members. How smashing!” Clay takes a sip of his coffee, gently stirring before lifting the cup to his lips. He takes in the aroma, sips and then carefully replaces onto the saucer. Krueger: “Soon I developed somewhat of a dual-personality but one I am in full control of. Dr. Clay is a rather nice, a handsome and pleasant chap. He'll build up his opponents and admire their talents. Dr. Clay is rather personable... but 'Chainsaw' Krueger is not. My moniker of ‘Chainsaw’ was handed to me like a baton to an Olympic athlete. I enjoy opera and collect fine wine. I speak six languages and enjoy a successful business portfolio. I may appear professional and business-like but beneath the surface lays a tyrant for which your ‘sport’ has never seen. When that bell rings, I don’t become a nice person despite my Harvard and Oxford education. The boys in the locker room will soon refer to me as 'Sir' or 'Mr. Krueger', that I assure you of.” Somewhat proud of himself, Krueger spends a moment recalling his university days in his mind before continuing. Krueger: “I shall expand upon my background. I have become a master in manipulation. I see other people simply as tools to use and abuse, I have trust issues and will rarely allow others into my circle. On the few occasions that I will willingly team with others, my true motives are well hidden behind the assertion that they are simply extensions of my own ego and an opportunity to assert myself over the weak. I was informed earlier this week as the ink on my illustrious contract was still wet that currently there is a process in action to crown a new keeper of a Jewel in the Crown Tournament. I will be watching and I shall indeed participate. My warning to all is to be heeded and it is simply ‘do not get comfortable in your position’ for soon I will grace your canvas and like a Monet, one will be immediately respected.” Krueger stands, picking up his long overcoat he drapes it over his forearm and drops a few dollar bills on the table in payment of his coffee from a wallet brimming with cash. Krueger: “The Doctor is in session… ” With that said the restaurant scene fades into oblivion and the screen switches back to regularly-scheduled programming.
