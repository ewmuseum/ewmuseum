Friends In Low-Down, Dirty Places

26 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26/08/08 “CANTRELL!“ The swollen face of Xavier Kannon glows in patches of beet-red between the mottled bruising. Blood pressure feeling like concrete is being pumped through his veins, the bandages plastered across his forehead begin to have Rorschach blottings of red seep into them where stitches loosen their grip with every throb. “Is this really how you want it to go?” his seething words hiss into his cell-phone, any more serpentine and he’d have spoken them in parseltongue. “I don’t know if you bothered to read up on the wrestling business before you took over PRIME or if you just wiki’d a few pages, but the history books are full of names who fell to the wayside with a knife in their back… and my fingerprints on it.” While those strolling up the steps to the Charleston Plaza Hotel shuffle away from the sneering Gold Patron Meritorious, one petite blonde watches and listens, licking her lips with lustre. “Don‘t think that suit and tie is going to protect you any, either,” his snarling rant threatens, devilishly contorted face spitting the words from his split lips with disdain. “I took the Sin City champion’s eye, and what did I get in return? I got a personal invite from Desade to their shows. I’m untouchable, Ceep. So if I choose to make an example out of you, who’s coming after me? Think they’ll see it as a sickening assault on a defenceless, untrained man… or one of the locker room dealing with an outsider the same way we have since this business began?” He fakes a lunge at one of the bystanders who gets a little too close for his liking, sending them scurrying back down the steps and almost face-planting on the sidewalk. “But no need to go and get fresh underwear yet, Ceep,” a mischievous grin settles across his lips as he delivers. “You’re not worth the laundry I’d need done after setting you straight. What I’m going to do is walk up to Tyler Rayne this week, tell him to show some respect for one of the men that paved the way him to set foot in a ring today and that I’ll be taking the slot in the Roulette I deserve. Then I’m going to walk out of the Great American Nightmare as your worst nightmare… a Gold Patron Meritorious with PRIME’s big Gold around my waist.” Just as Kannon goes to hang up, he snatches a few final words. “Oh, and just in case you’re planning any more little surprises to stop me, I’d be careful at ReVolution this week,” he warns in a devious chuckle. “It turns out I might have a helping Hand.” Those little surprises he talks of? Well, rewind a few minutes… With his bruised and swollen face seemingly held together by stitches and blood-blotted bandages, a battle-scarred Xavier Kannon could be forgiven if he looked a little less-than-cheerful. But, despite having his face, already pulverised by Jonathan Rhine at Colossus, further tenderised by the granite fists of Jason Natas, the Gold Patron Meritorious looks positively… chipper. A jubilant smile formed from puffed and split lips suggests victory can be far more soothing than a treble-measure of painkillers with a whisky chaser. “What?” About that jubilant smile? It’s not gonna last. Despite looking most respectable from the neck down in a sharp gunmetal suit, the scowl that wrinkles the Scientologist’s bruised face causes the Charleston Plaza receptionist to tremor, worried that a clawed glove is about to slash him up. “Um, er, you see,” the pitted-faced employee mumbles, his nervous, squinting eyes circling around the Gold Patron Meritorious’ bruise-warped face. “A, um, Mr Cantrell called a couple of, um, hours ago and, well, he, um, cancelled the reservation. Said something about you wanting alternate accommodation and not standing in your way.” Biting down on a pursed bottom lip, Kannon lets out a stress-venting breath through his nostrils. He counts to 5 in his head to calm himself, before raising an index finger to politely excuse himself from the reception desk so he can count about another 95. The shuddering receptionist’s fearful look, together with Kannon’s Gollom-esque hunched hobble attract bemused and disapproving glances from those in the foyer of the Plaza. “Um, hello?” Ellie snipes at one such snooty lady. “Haven’t you ever, like, seen someone who’s totally had their face pounded into a hella fugly mush before?” “Thanks,” a sensitive Kannon sarcastically snipes, breaking his count. “You should, like, totally have a spare face if you’re gonna keep totally messing up that one,” she then recommends, obviously having been keeping up on the latest advances in medical science in-between shopping trips, running up cell phone bills equal to the gross national output of an African country, and watching The Hills. “Ooh! A mask! You could, like, totally be Enemigo XK!” “No, really, thank you!” snarks Kannon, suddenly finding out that his wife‘s bluntness is a lot more fun to see others subjected to. “It means a lot.” He’s half tempted to lie out of spite and say she looks like she’s gained a quarter-pound, but he’d much rather get a good night’s sleep - wherever that may be - rather than listen to his wife sob and vomit in the bathroom all night… … like last time. Self-consciously wondering just how much she‘s exaggerating, the Gold Patron Meritorious leans a little to his left, trying to check his reflection in the nearby mirror without making it obvious. Unfortunately for him, his wife is to looking in mirrors what Rhett Locke is to Factor 99,999 sun-block. She could find her reflection blindfolded, in a black hole. “Um, you’d better, like, not,” she advises. “7 years totally bad luck when your face breaks it.” Abandoning his count somewhere around, well, let’s say -82, a fuming Kannon retreats from his wife’s teasing and returns to the reception desk. “Look, Peon,” he commands, hunched over the desk and in the face of the nervy, gulping receptionist. “I don’t care that Cantrell cancelled the reservation, I’ll be taking the room, pouring the mini-bar down the sink, ordering every movie on offer just in case there’s one I want to see five minutes of before going to sleep, and you’re going to charge it all down to the card that it was reserved on. Did you get all that? Because I‘m happy to write a list for you. Unless, of course, you want a religious discrimination case dragging this hotel‘s name through the mud.” “Well, er,” the receptionist stalls, timid eyes gradually creeping up to Kannon’s swollen face. “I’m afraid I’m, um, not allowed to do that, Mr… um?” “That would be Gold Patron Meritorious Xavier Kannon, actually,” he grandly states, although it’s not quite the same when his head looks like it’s been through a meat-grinder. “Who, I should explain, has spent the evening earning vast sums of money for the aforementioned Mr Cantrell by having THIS done to his face. So, you charge my room to Cantrell, and I’ll make him pay. Okay?” “Yeah!” Ellie can’t help chiming in with. “Like, okay?” Just as the receptionist goes to hold his shaky hands up and risk the wrath of the Gold Patron Meritorious once again, his colleague’s interest is piqued. “Did you say the name was Kannon?” she breezes onto the scene asking, exuding an air of icy confidence that her tremoring colleague lacks. With volumous, flowing raven hair, crimson lips and dark eye-shadow, the hotel’s deep red uniform seems made for her. From around her neck dangles a web of silver chains, a spider pendant straddling the glistening strands. And no, she isn’t. She’s just a fitting messenger. “Yes,” Xavier coldly growls, not breaking his death-stare on the now sweating receptionist. “Before the Cantrell reservation was cancelled there was a call from a… one moment… a Miss Pierce about the room,” she reveals, the news prompting Kannon to finally release the quivering employee from his gaze. “I couldn’t confirm you were a guest, but she reserved one of our finest suites for you rather than your standard double room… should you happen to be staying here, that was.” The Gold Patron Meritorious snaps his attention back to the nervous male receptionist, ready to enquire as to why he couldn’t have said that in the first place. However, the sound of a fainting body collapsing on the floor lets him know there’d be little point. “Now that’s a kind of service a like,” a reinvigorated Kannon declares, sly grin unfurling across his beaten face. “It’s nice to feel… wanted. Now, if you‘ll excuse me one moment, I just have to make a quick phone call.”
