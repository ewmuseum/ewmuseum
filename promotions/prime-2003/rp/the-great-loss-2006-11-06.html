The Great Loss

6 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 6, 2006 The Great Loss The hustle and bustle of the people around him had slowly started to aggravate him. The constant chatter of the ignorant people on their cell phones as they walked around the man had started to grate upon his basic instincts. Were these people unaware of the simple truth that they were in the midst of a deity? A god? Their god? Part of Malachi wanted to tear the throats out of a few innocent bystanders and make an example out of them, but he decided that wouldn’t be the best decision. Not here at least. For now, they needed to think of their God as a loving man, and not as one who takes lives on a whim. He shook his head ever so softly so as to not bring attention upon himself, and glanced upwards to look at the building he was standing in front of. For nearly twenty minutes, Malachi had stood there looking at the building, and waiting for the doors to open for him so he could enter. However, they hadn’t, and Malachi decided to go in himself instead of waiting for these gentiles to open the door for him. As he pulled the door open, he felt a blast of cold air hit him in the face, and he walked in to see a man dressed in a priest’s uniform standing there before him as if he were waiting for him. Malachi had entered a church. “Good afternoon,” the young priest said to him with a fakeness that almost made Malachi throw up at the priest’s feet. He once again had to resist his urges to rip the man’s eyes out of his head, and decided instead to smite the man who had ordained him. Malachi would send him to hell for this decision and let him suffer for it by being anally violated by Satan’s worse demons. As Malachi walked up to the man, the man placed a hand on his shoulder. Malachi just simply looked at the hand on his shoulder and wondered if he could rip the man’s hand off with nobody noticing. He decided against it once again. “How may I help you my brother?” the priest asked, still faking his sincerity. Malachi looked into the man’s eyes and decided to feign his niceness too by just smiling at him. “What is your name, my brother?” the priest asked him, interested in how come the man hadn’t spoken since he entered the magnificent church. “God,” Malachi answered and the man smiled. “Why yes, God is here,” the man responded to Malachi. Apparently, the priest thought he was dealing with a retard and Malachi decided that he would make his first son a retard as he walked away from the priest and entered the sanctuary. As Malachi looked around, he found exactly what he was looking for, and walked up to it. Along the way, he did his best to ignore the others around him that weren’t worthy of his presence for fear that he wouldn’t be able to stop himself from wanting to rip each and every person’s brains out of their skulls. After a few seconds, he reached his destination with little effort. Before him stood a large cross with his son, Jesus, on it, and a smile appeared on Malachi’s face. “My son,” he whispered softly as he looked up at the cross. “How dare they remember your ugliest hour and treat you as such so you have to look down upon them, and see them praying to a statue of you on a cross after they accused you of lying. For that, I apologize. Because of them, I lost my only son as he was tortured for an eternity in Heaven,” he continued to whisper to the statue. Behind him, people were looking at him peculiarly, wondering what this man was doing, and why he was whispering to a statue of Jesus Christ, their savior. “I have experienced loss in my days, son, and it was something that I had never wanted to heap upon you. However, I needed them to understand who I was, and I needed you to be my messenger. In the end, I lost you, and I can never forgive myself for it. I’m sorry my son, I’m so sorry to have put you in this world as a human, and allowed you to be persecuted in that fashion. I hope one day you shall forgive me,” he continued to speak so softly. “I’ve lost my people. They no longer look towards me for guidance, but looking to celebrities and sports athletes as their role models. They do not know of the ignorance these people show and how flawed they truly are with their perversions and inability to come above their shortcomings enough to even obey the law. People worship them and it makes me sick,” he continues. “I know where I lost them though. I lost them when I put you on this Earth to right them. I made you human and gave you all of their flaws. Now, they worship a false idol, and it’s your entire fault!” he yells out loud, startling everybody around him. “You, this is your fault! No longer do they worship a God, a deity, but they worship a human. They worship you and you don’t deserve to be worshiped. You nearly ruined it for me and I had to interject to make sure you didn’t fuck it all up for me! You nearly ruined it, and for that you make me sick. I gave you one simple task of being God’s messenger, and what do you do?! You get strung up on a cross, have nails slammed into your hands, and then have the audacity of DYING! YOU WERE SUPPOSED TO BE GOD LIKE! YOU DIED ON A CROSS YOU GOOD FOR NOTHING PIECE OF SHIT!” he yells at the top of his lungs, and now everyone is looking at him in fear. He then turns and looks at the people looking at him. “All of you are idiots and don’t deserve to live! You’re flawed and you have FLAWED YOURSELVES! I created you to be perfect and you’re too fucking dumb to do that! I programmed you and somehow you fuck that up just like the rest of the things I’ve given you worthless infants! Idiots, the whole lot of you! You will die at my hands and then be tortured for eternities as I laugh at the sight of your naked, pathetic, godless bodies dance in the fires as you try to worship me. Then, you will realize that you’re too late!” he finishes yelling as the same priest from before rushes up to him to stop him. As he places his hand on Malachi’s shoulder, Malachi stares at him in such a way that it frightens him. “Remove your hand from my shoulder before I turn you into a Catholic priest and make you a faggot and you’re sucking dicks and taking it in the ass for the rest of your pathetic life,” he tells the priest who immediately backtracks, and falls over himself in doing so. As he falls, Malachi realizes that the priest has pissed his pants. Malachi then begins to walk out, but then he turns around and looks at the cross again. The feeling in his stomach turns into venom as he must rid this very image from his sight, and he rushes up towards the ten foot tall cross, knocking it down and destroying it in the process. As the dust slowly clears, he looks at his work, and is proud of what he’s done. He’s accomplished enough in one day and decides to rest for the rest of the day. Tomorrow, he’ll do it all over again until they get his point. For all that he has lost, his people, his son, and his world, he has so much to gain back. He has so much to prove. That there is only one God, and he is it.
