Date: ReVolution 158 Soft skin. Grazing hands. Subtle kisses. Memories flash before Craig's eyes at an alarming rate as he takes a step back and takes a good look at Nicole. She stands in the threshold of the door, a different look on her face then the last time that Craig had seen her. Color was lost while lack of sleep was evident. Her hands rest nervously at her side as she waits for Craig to say something. Behind Craig stands Katie, a shocked look on her face while other people are lingering about lost in their own conversations and not even aware of the fact of what's going on. "Can...," Nicole begins to speak, but her throat is dry. She swallows hard, nervous in her own right about being here, and then takes a deep breath before continuing. "Can I come in?" At first, no one moves. Craig's right hand consciously moves into his pocket as he tries to pull himself together. He can feel the fatigue setting in rapidly, but he continues to fight through it. Not now, he thinks to himself. He couldn't fight the MS while dealing with Nicole. His eyes slowly move towards Katie who hasn't said a word in a few moments and hasn't move either. He then takes a look back at Nicole, her eyes looking to be on the verge of tears now at the hesitancy from Craig. Katie finally takes a step forward and looks at Craig. "Craig?" she asks Craig, wondering to herself what does he want right now in this moment. He licks his lips, thousands of words filling his head while emotions fill his heart. Anger. Hate. Sadness. Elation. A full range of mixed emotions. He looks back at Katie and then at Nicole once again. "Let's go to the bedroom, Nicole. We can talk there," he tells her, his tone very still and hushed. She nods her head before stepping into the house, and closes the door behind her. She begins to move towards the stairs that lead to his bedroom as Katie places her hand on his shoulder. "I'll clear the house," she says rather simply. Craig just nods his head, unable to talk at this moment. She walks away, her heels clicking on the hardwood floor. Craig then turns back to Nicole and she begins walking up the stairs. At first, all Craig can do is just watch her, wondering to himself if love has found it's way back to his heart. His thought is quickly interrupted though as she reaches the sixth or seventh step before she realizes that Craig isn't following him and turns back to find out where he's at. "Coming?" she asks in this rather silky voice. He hesitates for just a moment before nodding his head and begins to walk up the stairs. She waits until they're one step away from one another and looks him in the eyes. Months of sadness passes between them as time around them freezes until Nicole breaks free and walks up the rest of the stairs and Craig just follows right behind her. A few seconds later they're standing inside of his bedroom. Nicole moves to the bed and sits down, bracing herself for the Armageddon that's sure to follow while Craig walks into the bathroom and closes the door. He immediately walks over to his medicine cabinet and opens the door to find the bottle of pills that he's looking for and takes three of them before grabbing a glass of water from earlier and downing them in one sip. His eyes remain closed for a minute, trying to mentally prepare himself. The fog has started to creep in, slowing his thought process, but he knows that the drugs will take their effect sooner rather than later. The only thing that scares him though is that he took three instead of the recommended two, but he needed the extra one if he expected to get through this without collapsing in front of her. Questions fill his brain in seconds. Would he tell her about the MS? Would he bring her back into his life? Will it only get worse tonight? Do you tell her about the past? "Are you okay?" the voice slices through his thought process and he's brought back to reality. He walks to the bathroom door and pulls it open before walking back out into the bedroom where Nicole is still sitting on the bed. Even with the lack of sleep and loss of color, she still looks as beautiful as ever; something that he didn't think to be possible. "You left," he finally manages to say, his mouth feeling dry as he speaks. The moment that the words leave his mouth he knows that this is the wrong way to start this off. He wishes he could take them back and he opens his mouth to do so, but she beats him to the punch. "That's what you want to say? You haven't seen me in four months and you want to start off with that? You might've forgotten that I left moments after a guy committed suicide in your living room! Did you forget that?" she yells back at him, her eyebrows furrowing, and her temperature raising with each word. He closes his eyes and swallows, knowing that he's in for a battle now. "You didn't give me a chance to explain myself," he responds. Once again, he cringes, wishing he could pick what he wants to say a little better. "Once again, Craig, a guy committed suicide in front of me! Here, in this house! He told me how you killed dozens of people, of how you were basically an assassin for years! I fell for a guy who murders people, and you expected me to just stick around so you could explain what happened? Does that not sound, oh, I don't know, a little insane? I was scared out of my mind, Craig, because you weren't the guy I originally met. You kept that from me! I--," she continues to go on and on before Craig cuts her off, his own anger starting to build. "Did you ever consider why I kept it from you?! Maybe I wasn't proud of my past, Nicole! I was trying to protect you from the person that I used to be! Do you understand that? Used to be! I'm sorry I didn't reveal that tidbit of information in the few months that we had been dating, but I was fairly certain that you would've left me if you had known rather then letting me tell you when we'd been together longer!" he fights back, but in the back of his mind he can see that there's no winning this battle. The truth stared him straight in the face. He'd killed somebody. That hadn't changed nor would it. He'd killed dozens upon dozens of people and there was no correcting that. Silence. It comes sooner than either one of them had expected. They glance at one another periodcially, but there's still a mountain of tension between them. They each desperately want to tear it down, but with each layer gone, there's another wall of questions standing in between them. A lifetime of questions. Would any of them ever get answered? Would either one ever be satisfied with the results of them? In the back of Craig's mind, he's not certain. In Nicole's mind, she desperately needs them to. Her life was changing by the minute and she needed Craig right there with her. Knock. Knock. Knock. The rapping of knuckles against the bedroom door wakes both of them out of their stupor. Craig glances to the door before clearing his throat. "Come in." His voice is somber and still as the words come out. The door opens up and in comes Katie with a tired look on her face. "The house is clear. If you guys, you know, wanted to do this elsewhere," she says, obviously uncomfortable with all of this. Craig doesn't hesitate though as he walks out of the room, hoping to get away from it all. He rushes down the flight of stairs before walking into the living room. The house looks like a mess, but in this particular moment he could frankly care less. If anything, he wants to punch something right now. "So tell me," her voice comes from behind him. He swiftly turns around and looks at her as she walks down the stairs towards the living room. "Tell you what?" he asks her while she sits down on the couch, glaring at Craig by this point. "About your past. About these people you murdered," she responds rather bluntly. Craig sighs as he sees Katie walking down the stairs, nervous, and in awe. He begins to pace a little bit, the details crystal clear to him, but in no way wanting to delve into them. "You don't want to hear them," he replies softly, hoping that she would just move on to another subject. "I do, Craig. I honestly do. I'm trying to give you that chance that I never gave you," she tells him and he stops mid-step before looking at her with great interest. The question that should've been asked from the very second she had approached the house hits him squarely in the face. He begins to walk up to her, the interest written on his face. "Why'd you come back?" "What?" Her face shows that she's taken aback by the question. Nicole's head turns over her shoulder and looks at Katie who's face has changed in the past few seconds, trying to figure out the answer to the question herself. "I haven't called you in months, Nicole. I gave up on us because you walked out. Then, all of a sudden, you're back. You're here at my front door, ready to talk. You tell me that you're trying to give me that chance now. But, why? What brought you back here?" Craig can see the fear in her face as she inches forward to the edge of the couch. Her elbows are planted firmly into her legs and she begins to shake a little bit. Her back is hunched as she looks at the floor. All of this strikes Craig odd as he approaches Nicole even more before she tilts her head up and looks at Craig with a certain softness in her eyes. One that shows how much she's been crying as of late. "I'm pregnant," she says, her bottom lip trembling as she speaks the words. Almost like it's an admission that she's been too scared to make to even herself the past few weeks. The words hit Craig full on. In the background, Katie's sitting on the stairs paralyzed but she had said. Craig is almost frozen, but he fights through it before kneeling in front of Nicole and looking her in the eyes. Within those seconds, a few tears have raced down her cheek. With his thumb he gently wipes away some of those tears before looking Nicole in the eyes. "Really?" he asks her, not even having to ask if it's his. She wouldn't be here if it wasn't his. She wouldn't put him in this position if it wasn't him. If there's one thing Craig knew about Nicole, and he knew many, it would be that she hadn't been with anyone since him. She knew exactly who the father was. "Yeah," she says, her voice now trembling as well. "Four months now." Craig stands up, a little woozy from the bombshell, and looks around the room. A child. The one thing he doesn't need or want in his life. Nicole reaches out and grabs his hand. He doesn't pull away though as he looks back at her. "I need you, Craig. I never stopped loving you. I never stopped caring about you. I want you in my life," she tells him and Craig can only stand there at first. He nods his head after a few moments have passed before he looks back at Nicole. "I need some time, Nicole. I just need some time, some air," he says before pulling away his hand and beginning to walk towards the front door, his mind numb to everything around him. He looks back at Nicole though as he reaches the door. "Stay here, relax, I'll be back." He then looks at Katie, but only briefly before walking out of the front door. The weight of the world were on his shoulders again, and he could feel the pressure increasing with each passing second. A child? Could he honestly do that now in his life? With the demons in his closest, fear sets into his heart for the unborn child. Fear. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Have you ever wondered how the world would've been different?"
