It Happens To The Best Of Us (UV RP, with Danny Ferguson)

4 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 4, 2007 At the end of an ordinary hallway in the not-so-ordinary Renaissance Marriott hotel in St. Louis is an absolutely extraordinary sight to behold, marking one of the few documented times that such a scene is visible to hotel patrons unfortunate enough to witness it. A tornado has passed through. Most of the time, they happen behind closed doors where the cleaning staff are the only witnesses: bed sheets strewn asunder, furniture shattered, empty beer bottles and purchased fifths of liquor decorating the floor, various articles of clothing found in piles, clumps and mounds. Like hurricanes, these tornadoes have names and some turn out to be more famous than others. Today's tornado is not the product of an argument or a fist-fight, of infidelity or slander. It's the simple result of something very important being missing: a hotel key card, which gives its holder access to temporary, and very comfortable, lodging. This tornado's name is Lindsay Troy. And right now, she needs the quiet sanctuary of her hotel room that the missing card is not providing her. Resting on her knees in the middle of the corridor, she hovers over her nearly empty gym bag and pulls objects out without giving them much of a second thought. "Please be in here, please be in here..." she pleads while pulling out another roll of tape and tossing it over her shoulder. It lands momentarily on top of a T-shirt, then rolls innocently down the pile of clothes, bumps against her heel and comes to a rest on its side. A pair of wrist-wrap gloves are next, and are flung off to the side where the contents of her purse are in a heap by the door to her room. As a pair of shoes are yanked out, Troy is convinced the door is mocking her, so she throws them against it. The resulting bang is enough to startle a passing bus boy, who suddenly appeared from around the corner pushing a tray of half-eaten food in front of him, on his way back to the elevators. "Something wrong, ma'am?" Troy jumps in surprise and looks up at her visitor, who is eying her quite curiously. She feels her face turn red, not noticing before just how much the hallway looks like a FEMA-caused disaster area thanks to all of her stuff. "I, err...ugh, this is so embarrassing." Lindsay pinches the bridge of her nose in frustration and sighs. "I can't find my key card and I thought I put it in my purse and it's not there...andthenIthoughtIputitinmybagbutit'snotthereeither..." The words hurriedly escape her mouth the more flustered she gets, her cheeks now matching the comforter of the hotel bed that she can't lay on. The bus boy, also a little nervous by the scene and the irritated state of its creator, shuffles his feet a little. He reaches down to help clean up, but Troy, less protective and more embarrassed, starts grabbing everything, hoping to gather her pride up along with it. The serviceman abruptly stands back up and turns pink himself. He stammers a little before managing a sentence. "Aiyuh, Ai yuh, I uh, I uh, I can go get you a new key card, um, ifyoulike, so you don't have to, uh, y'know, lug all your stuff back downstairs." Lindsay offers a small smile, trying to relax her posture so she appears less likely to pounce, cat-like, with claws bared. "Would you mind?" "I, um...no, I wouldn't...ok." He takes off down the hallway, starting in a jog and then breaking into one of those nervous fast walks. Lindsay, meanwhile, softly groans at her less-than-tactful handling of the situation and returns to cleaning up after herself, nerves temporarily eased at the promise of a solution. Of course, that was before the elevator door opened. The anonymous staff member taps his foot and looks nervously back at Troy, waiting for the lift. The metal doors slide open as he's surveying the damage, and as he turns around he finds himself slammed up against a wall. He lets out an "EEP!" and finds himself staring at the meaty forearm and "don't have time for this shit" glare of Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas. And you know what that means. Troy, hearing the squeal of her timid sort-of-hero, looks up to watch her tag team partner, Danny Ferguson, step out into the hallway. She closes her eyes and mouths a four-letter word. Meanwhile, back at the elevator, Danny looks the bus boy up and down, his face not cracking from a dead expression. "That's a good disguise," he eventually volunteers, producing a Sharpie from inside his jacket pocket. With Dam still holding the kid up, PRIME's Superstar leans in and autographs his cheek. "I work here, sir," says Troy's Knight in Food-Flecked Restaurant Uniform. "Come on, kid. Just because the act worked on security doesn't mean you should try it with the pros. Now scram before we call someone." The bodyguard yanks the kid off the wall and throws him into the elevator car in one fluid motion. The doors slide shut on his motionless form and Team Ferguson (which, in its abbreviated form, only includes both guards and Hollywood super-agent Allan North) progresses down the hallway. Of course, they only make it a few more steps before Danny catches sight of our scene. His walk, which already had a pretty cocky hitch in its giddy-up, slowed to more of a Vince McMahon grapefruit-smuggling saunter as he slowly wandered up to the Queen of the Ring. "I don't want to hear you say one thing," Troy snaps, her mood turning sour again. "That's probably for the best, because I was having a hard time settling on just one." Troy half-heartedly shoves her gym clothes back into her bag, not bothering to fold or otherwise arrange them. "I just lost my key card." "You know they can get you a new one, right?" She sighs. "That's what They were doing before you signed Them and threw Them like a rag doll into the elevator." "Hrm." Danny looks back at Dametreyus, who shrugs. "You've got a long ways to go as a White Hat, Fergdor. Lesson 1: Fans cheer you when you don't manhandle them." "Whatever. At least now he's got a story to tell his friends." "Or a lawyer." Ferguson shrugs, then just hovers a little while Lindz continues to clean up her mess. Finally fed up by the peanut gallery looming over her maid act, she looks up. "Don't you have somewhere to be? I'm sure the Hard Money marathon on TBS is much more appealing than watching me pick up after myself." Danny glances to his agent and gets an affirmative nod. "Probably. I can never get enough of watching myself detonate some high-powered explosives." "Naturally." "You sure you want to be chilling out in the hallway by your lonesome?" Danny jerks his thumb back in the direction of the elevators. "There's a lot of fans hovering around this place since they leaked it as HQ for the weekend. You could be 30 seconds from a mob of Hancock-seekers." "That's one of the last things I need right now. I can cross you finding me in this state off the top of the list." "Hey, just looking out for a teammate." "I appreciate your sudden concern two days before a title defense. It's really touching...gets me," she taps her chest, "right here." Ferg doesn't offer a jab in return, but quiets down a little, looking away. He's - perish the thought - a little wounded by the insinuation, and it shows. Troy reconsiders the venom in hindsight and slouches her shoulders in apology. "I'm sorry. I'm out of my element which has made me a little wound up right now. You've been trying; that's more than I can say for some of your predecessors." Ferguson slowly slides down the wall opposite Troy, getting to eye level. "So I've heard," he says, eliciting a slight chuckle and eye roll from Lindz. "Youngblood earns a paycheck by talking out his ass and miraculously, on occasion, backing it up. He likes to find the right button ahead of time so he's ready to jam on it when it suits him." "Sure, but he's not the problem." Troy gathers up her shoes and the tape and jams them on top of everything else. "I'm not really into talking about the problem right now." Danny's hands go up in confirmation. "Gotcha. He did seem like kind of an ass, though." Troy hangs her head at his inability to drop the issue, but can't help but let another slight chuckle out. "That's Joey." --- The elevator opens again, and the same bus boy breathes deeply before stepping out. The permanent marker on his cheek is smeared a little but still very much present, and he seems a little unkempt after the dust-up and the rush. As he hurries down the hallway to finish his task, he hears laughter echoing. What was once a mass of clothes and personal effects was now just a mass of persons, as the entirety of Team Ferguson is seated on the floor across from Troy. Everyone is yukking it up about something as he approaches. "So then Jean-Claude is like, 'wait, he's the President?'" Ferguson sputters, breaking up into laughter as everyone else rolls. The kid approaches and clears his throat, putting an end to the affable atmosphere. Everyone turns to look at him. "Here's your card, Ms. Troy." Smile still radiating, Lindz gestures for him to hand it to her. "Thanks, kiddo. I'll make sure I hang onto it this time." "Oh, uhh, yeah...let me..." He steps over the crowd a little, trying not to keep Dametreyus out of his sight, slides the key card in the lock and opens the door for her, giving a picture-perfect customer service smile. Lindsay stares at him in surprise. "I just wanted to make sure you made it in your room," he starts again, redness starting to spread across his face again. "Hey, wow, and here I thought chivalry was dead." She laughs and kicks her bag over to act as a doorstop. "I'm good, really. I'll put the key in my pocket and everything. Promise." The hop is momentarily speechless and Danny laughs, leaning over to pull out a wallet. "Come on now, Killer Queen, the boy just wants a tip." "Oh no, I ju-" "It's fine, kid. You look like you could use it." He peels off a $100 and holds it out. The hop considers protesting, but changes his mind and reaches for the bill. Just as he gets there, Danny pulls it back. "First, though...who ya got, Oscar or Floyd?" "I, um...well, De La Hoya is kinda-" "PSSSSH!" The group starts laughing before he can finish. Danny peels out another Franklin and hands both bills to the young man. "Here. Order the fight tomorrow and make sure you take notes during the Mayweather clinic." "You see, I think that-" "Excuse me?" Danny interrupts, feigning anger just enough to be believable. "Two hundred and suddenly you're Larry Merchant? I paid you for silence, not analysis." "I-" "Listen to Steve Miller and take the money and run, kid. And we won't tell that you argued with a customer," Troy adds with a wink. He hesitates momentarily and Dametreyus starts to stand up, prompting him to take off at light speed. He murmurs a thank you as he disappears down the hallway. Once up, Dam pulls Danny up as well, and the rest of the posse follows suit. Troy slowly follows, taking Dam's hand as it's offered, finally coming back to the reality that she can move her bags in and take a much needed nap. "You got this taken care of?" Danny asks, gesturing to the bags. "I'd have the guys help, but I don't know if Jim should be touching your delicates. He's got a thing." Jim Pibb closes his eyes and flares his nostrils involuntarily. With a 'thanks for that' look, Troy holds the door open, kicks her bag the rest of the way in and plucks her purse off the ground. "I'm good," she replies. "Alright," he says, and turns to head down the hall, entourage in tow. As Troy disappears in the door, he stops and turns back. "What about you?" Lindsay pokes her head back out. "Huh?" "The fight," Danny says. "Oscar/Floyd. You got plans?" "Are you being serious?" came her incredulous response. "No, I'm asking you for my health. Well, maybe I am," he says, which garners a shrug from Allan. "Yes, I'm being serious." "Oh. Well, I dunno." She moves out into the hall again. "Night before a show and all, so I didn't really plan on going out, you know?" "Aw, come on, don't be a shut-in." Danny replies with a grin. "It's St. fucking Louis. We could put two coats of red paint on the town and it'd still be boring as shit. I'm not talking about sharing milkshakes and picking some songs out of a jukebox or getting completely shitfaced and partying it up all night. I just want to watch my boy Junior get paid. So, you in?" There's a pause before the reply. "Sure," Lindsay shrugs and returns Danny's grin with one of her own. "Why the hell not." "Aight. I'll buzz your room tomorrow. Don't lock yourself out again." "And don't you assault any more hotel workers."
