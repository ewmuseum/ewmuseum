The Journey Home (Match RP - v Walczak, Battle Royal)

1 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/08/05 The Journey Home.Written: 1st August, 2005. 'All I need is a little time,To get behind this sun and cast my weight,All I need is a peace of this mind,Then I can celebrate.' - Air. I - Lost In Translation. 'Here is your winner... ANGELO DEVILLE!!!' 'Kari, wait... don-' 'Just get off me.' 'Where are you going?' 'Just... leave me alone, okay?' Never taking losing that well, Karina didn't stick around long after Colossus II, neither in the arena, nor in the country. Rather than think about the moment she came to, hearing Angelo Deville announced as the victor, Kari knew she needed to get away from him, away from everything. For a whole week, she refused to afford her body the luxury of recovering, instead pushing it even further, until every joint soothed itself with its own acidic heat. Sleep was grabbed in short spurts, Kari being happy to slam herself against her limit, knowing she'd have 3 days left to relax before ReVolution. Back to back losses against Brandon Youngblood and Angelo Deville grated the highly competitive athlete. Nothing less than a win would do for her, while her pride didn't stop there, wanting to repeat the double she achieved at Culture Shock. She'd done the hard part, and her often suspect body had endured all the determined K-Wolf all but tortured it with... so just getting back home should have been the easy part. Well, 'should' doesn't really exist in Karina Wolfenden's universe. 'Are you alright, Miss?' Denying herself any rest, Karina's usually rapid healing decelerated, and the bruises from her emotional battle with Angelo Deville remained. As Karina checked in, the woman across the desk from her, almost caked in foundation so that no natural skin tone could even be guessed at, seemed transfixed by the marbled bruising. 'Er, yeah,' Kari shrugged the concern off with, the visual remnants of Colossus II having slipped her mind. 'Fight with the boyfriend, y'know? Then again, I did ask for it.' The woman's eyes shot open, but bound by the duties of work, couldn't blurt out what was banging against the inside of her head. 'Um,' the self-conscious Kari then enquired after looking behind herself, awkwardly, wondering what the redheaded woman was looking at. 'Are me eyes bleeding or something?' 'Look, we have a few seats in first class left,' the woman, rife with concern and worry, revealed. 'I'd be happy to upgrade you, if you want, that is?' 'Sure,' the K-Wolf breezily accepted. 'I'm all for leg room.' As she slid Karina's ticket across, pity seemed to drip off the well-meaning woman. 'And about your boyfriend,' she began to advise. 'Maybe you sho-' 'Oh, he'll be fine,' Kari dismissed, having taken a different turning at the conversation's first junction. 'He just gets all emo sometimes, and uber-violence is his release.' The woman's lips waver, trying to form words out of shock, but none are audible. 'Anyway,' Wolfenden smiled, oblivious to the wide-eyed and aghast expression across the desk from her. 'Thanks for the seat.' II - The Undesirable Profession. 'And this is one of my son and his second wife in Hawaii...' Suddenly, economy wasn't sounding so bad to Karina Wolfenden. She'd gotten the window seat that she always craved, had instantly found her groove in the seat, was all set for a relaxing, lazy flight... and then she was reminded that aircraft don't really fit in with her desire for privacy. 'She wasn't a gold-digging whore like his first wife,' the elderly woman then followed, with no shame. Plenty of thousand feet too far from the ground for even the gravity-defying K-Wolf to bail, she was trapped in a photo slideshow. Legs, draped in baggy black cargo pants were hugged to her chest, where a simple white vest let slip a pair of lilac bra straps. With blonde and pink hair neatly tied back into a short braid, Kari couldn't even hide behind her fringe, as was the usual tactic. 'I'm glad I started those rumours about her being a call girl,' the elderly woman refused to cease, leaving even the K-Wolf almost wide-eyed in disbelief. 'That served her right for messing with my boy.' The pre-flight shots of courage that the woman beside Karina had downed not only shed her of doubts, but eroded away inhibitions, freeing her tongue. To look at her made it all the more baffling. In a conservative, flowery dress, and with thin glasses perched at the tip of her nose, she looked a perfect fit for the role of a dear, beloved grandma. Dark grey hair was frizzy, tied back, but with a batch of curls for a fringe. 'Yeahhh,' Karina hesitantly released, not entirely sure what to say to the woman. 'That would do it, I guess.' Having reached the second batch of photos, Karina had already found out more than she really wanted to about the elderly lady and her family. But, for some reason both unknown and infuriating to her, the K-Wolf couldn't do her usual, disinterested exit from the conversation. Despite the language spewing forth from her row-mate, it felt... rude. 'It's not often I use such words, dear,' the slightly-slurred, but truthfully direct speech carried on, 'but she was a Grade A BITCH!' Karina was so close to bliss. Her chair was reclined to just the right angle, her long, almost awkward legs had stretched out, and earphones were ready to hammer trip-hop lullabies into her brain. Then, just as her head was about to nestle in the inviting headrest, it began. 'Hello, dear...' That was 90 minutes ago. 'She got the house, the kids, and my boy STILL has to pay her every month,' the elderly lady seethed, words still sounding somewhat delicate with age, despite the intention. 'All she did was sit at home watching TV, cooking, and cleaning... and between us, it's not like she did the last two that well.' The scene from Daredevil wouldn't leave Kari's thoughts, and inside she prayed for an offer of peanuts. 'That... um... sucks?' the K-Wolf offered in return, doing her best to stay on the outskirts of the conversation. Upon hearing Kari's words, the lady scoffed. 'Well, rumour is she did a lot of that too,' was spat out in disgust. 'With the gardener, the plumber, the mailman...' Kari gave a wince that just screamed, 'too much information.' What was it about human beings and conversation? Was it that hard to just sit back, close your eyes, and go into shut-down for a few hours, trying to get the sensory deprivation thing going on? Not everyone was a highly social creature, and one of those people was Karina Wolfenden. 'Sounds like he's better off rid,' Kari then hurriedly decreed, before making a hasty grab at her earphones. 'But I'm a bit tired, so I think I'll ju-' 'So, dear, are you married?' was politely enquired, thwarting Kari's plan. 'Um, no,' she sighed, dropping the earphones in her lap, somehow unable to allow her usual, aloof rudeness to kick in. Karina's eyes betrayed the fact that she'd hardly slept since Colossus II. Never one to take defeat well, sporting in every sense but the losing kind, she'd been in a sulk since storming out of the building. A few snatched hours on buses and at airports did little to soften the reddening of her eyes, while dry, tired skin was turned almost arid in the heat. 'Oh,' the elderly lady remarked, shocked. 'I thought you'd be beating them off with a stick.' 'Nope,' the K-Wolf mumbled under her breath, too soft for the lady's hearing to pick up. 'Just beating them...' Pausing for a few seconds, the elderly lady took a moment to look Karina up and down, having finally tucked the extensive photo collection back into her bag. 'I'm sure you'll find yourself a nice man, dear,' she assured the K-Wolf in words too warm to just shrug off. 'You just need to make yourself a bit more appealing. No mother wants their boy to come home with a girl with hair like yours... it's such a lovely blonde behind that nasty pink. And maybe you should try dressing a little better too, dear. I'm sure you can find a nice skirt, rather than those trousers which, let's face it, don't really fit now, do they?' Wondering if her exhaustion was causing hallucinations, Wolfenden just looks at the smiling, butter-wouldn't-melt lady next to her. 'And do tuck those brassiere straps under something,' she then helpfully suggested, tugging a white vest strap over the nearest one to her. 'The last thing a respectable young man will want to see is your underwear showing. Nobody likes a girl who looks like one of those 'club kid' whores that always used to have on Rikki Lake, dear.' The man in the aisle seat drifted his gaze across the old lady towards Karina, who gave him a look of m'aide, only for him to hold his hands up, signalling that he wasn't going to take over the attention for her. 'And maybe you should try smiling a little more,' the suggestions continued to flow as the elderly, uninhibited woman caught sight of a scowl Kari directed towards the aisle seat. 'Nobody wants to see you looking like a sourpuss all the time. My boy's first fiancé was like that, and, well, she was a frigid bitch.' While a passenger in the row behind them finally tuned into the conversation, only to spurt their drink back out their mouth in shock, Karina tried to formulate any reply that wouldn't follow a word-expletive-word-expletive pattern. 'I'll... um...' she stumbled over her own words with. 'I'll take that those thoughts on board.' 'You do that, dear,' followed with a warm smile, as if the lady had just showered Karina with compliments and cookies for the last hour. 'So, what do you do for a living, dear? A lot of men nowadays don't like these 'career woman', no desire to settle down and start a family. They prefer to leave it too late and have those caesarean things...' Almost squirming into the nook of her seat, the K-Wolf began to realise there was no escape, her window seat now her shackles. 'Well, um, I snowboard a bit,' she began to explain. 'Skate, base-jump, bit of other stuff, but that only really brings in sponsorship money... and, yeah, I'm a wrestler.' As if Karina Wolfenden had just spat out obscenities to trump those of the elderly woman, her face soured, almost shuffling across in her seat to get further away from the rightfully bemused K-Wolf. 'Hmph. Well, no wonder you're not married.' The wide-eyed Kari almost had to do a double-take, her lower lip drifting south down the shock gradient. 'So, young man,' the lady then diverted her attention to the man in the aisle seat with. 'What do you do?' III - Thewhowhatwherehow? A day, and barely 5 hours of sleep later, Karina Wolfenden collapsed back into her seat. Long, gangly legs fidgeted to find a comfortable position, before Kari lifted them up to her chest, then extended the limbs skywards. Satisfied there was enough room to do her anti-DVT stretches, the K-Wolf pulled her hooded sweatshirt off, then hugged it to her like a cushion. 'I need a 'do not disturb' tattoo,' she groaned, ushering in a yawn as her whole body extended. Nuzzling up against the cabin wall, she let out an exhausted sigh, relishing the thought of hours of uninterrupted sleep. Even the clobbering footsteps of the other boarding passengers couldn't break through the defences her craving for relaxation built up, while the chatter sounded barely above a distant whisper. Somewhere in Karina's cynical mind, she should have known it was all a bit too perfect. And when bliss clouds a cynical mind, naivety drifts in. 'Karina?' shattered through her aural barriers as she felt a jolt of her seat. Reluctantly, her right eye crept open to catch a blurred image of a young man smiling at her. Hardly comfortable with the attention, Kari shuffled upright, rubbing her eyes. 'Um, do I... er, know you?' she enquired, the effort of forming words triggering another yawn. 'Karina Wolfenden?' he excitedly followed the initial query up with. 'Y'know,' the strained words went from the K-Wolf, 'I kinda don't like it when people just say my name at me.' Kari nudged him back with a widening of her eyes, the young an quickly taking the hint and recoiling his eager poise. 'Oh, sorry... I must sound like such a mark!' The look of dread deep in Karina's emerald eyes was for a reason. The word 'mark', lower-case m, used as a noun, meant only one thing... a wrestling fan. Worse, a 'smart' fan. And she was trapped by him. 'Yeahhhh,' she just agreed, disinterested, before tucking her sweatshirt behind her head as a pillow. 'You just... er... keep doing that.' All Karina could think was that even from her confined position, she could kick him in the head. 'This is awesome!' he did an A+ job of not taking the over-acted hint with. 'I was watching PRIME the other day! Is it true that Hoyt is really a Scientologist, and he's not even Duke's son?' 'The whowhatwhere?' barely formed words above the whine that the K-Wolf released, forcing her eyes back open. Rubbing his hands together, the young fan settled into his seat, getting comfortable for the long haul. 'And there was this website that said Brandon Youngblood shot all over the PRIME writers so was suspended for a month, which is why he lost to Vetra!' continued his verbal onslaught, each one setting off a numb thud in Karina's heavy head. 'Was that true? Because the same site had some crap about Joshua Taro Freedom walking out because he was tapped-out to Youngblood, and lost his belt to Nova... wait, did he? You'd know!' A blank look was chiselled deep into Karina's face, while eyes told of her despair. 'I have... no idea who that is,' crept out as eyebrows migrated down towards her nose, baffled. 'Do PRIME make you dye your hair all the time?' the questions continued to spew forth. 'Er, no...' Kari continued to answer with the least possible energy expended, in the slim hope that he'd suffocate on oxygen or something, sneakers tapping hurriedly on the floor as she did her best to endure the questions. 'Is it true that Aimz and Killean are with each other, 'cause I heard on this other site that she was with Gibson...' was rattled off as the deluge refused to be stemmed. 'What does Vampir look like behind his mask? I heard he was the same guy that used to be Nerezza. Or was it Hessian... I can't remember. Oh, speaking of masks, is one of the Flying Vergomovs El Spiffy!?' 'Elwhohuh?' Kari barely fielded as a reply, unable to keep up. Never liking to be on the back-foot, the weary Karina's head recoiled, almost forming a double chin against her neck. 'Man, this is SO cool meeting a wrestler!' he exclaimed, amplifying his boyish side. 'I was going to train, but my parents were all, like, you can't be a wrestler! and wouldn't give me the money. It must be awesome getting to travel around the world with all those PRIME guys, meeting all the fans, huh?' 'Oh, yeah,' Kari's words slashed sarcastically through the cabin's atmosphere with. 'It rocks... with a tilde or whatever that thing is called.' 'I'd still want to do Indy dates though,' the fanboy continued to rattle on. 'Because that's where the real fans ar-' Finally having endured enough, Karina narrowed here eyes quizzically, leaning with her ear towards the young man, then formed a cup around the feature with her hand. 'Sorry, I can't hear you...' Karina blurted out, doing her best to look bemused. 'I think I might be going deaf, so I'd better put in these special, medicinal earphones.' The man finally paused as Karina put her earphones in, then tried to re-adopt the comfy, snug position she'd found before being interrupted. He took a few more stabs at starting another question, but seemingly able to see through her eyelids, Kari raised a disapproving index finger, wagging it at him whenever his lips parted. 'I think you need to have your eyes closed for these things to work,' the K-Wolf continued her blunt blanking with, leaving the fan rather miffed. 'Oh, and the answers to your questions were... um... yes, no, yes, a zebra, Lima, 42, Bobby Stormbringer, and only on Sundays. Yawwwwwwwwwwwwwwnandbuymyt-shirt...' IV - Dream Job/Lame Pun. The final home leg was underway, Albany the destination, ahead of two days of pure nothingness before hopping on to ReVolution. Her own bed, her own sheets, her own pillows, and her own alarm clock to smash against a wall if it dared go off. No buses to catch, no check-ins, no annoying passengers, no hauling bags through lounges. Just sleep, lounging, and then even more sleep... with maybe a walk to the phone and some strenuous take-out dialling for exercise. She'd endured two flights from hell, so the third, if the universe was anything but a sadistic bastard, would be a cake-walk, right? A breeze, a milk-run, surely? 'I have to apologise in advance...' were the words which lassoed Karina's attention as her row neighbour squirmed into her seat. 'I get really nervous on planes, so I tend to talk quite a bit.' There was a tear trying to force its way out of Kari's dry, glassy eyes. 'Oh, and I'm Janet, by the way,' the woman breezily introduced herself with, extending a hand that Kari did her best to ignore. 'I work for an airline, which is the ironic thing! Anyway, what do you do?' Deep down, Karina was on her knees, screaming to the Lord, 'WHY?!' 'I'm a wres-...' Karina went to reel off out of instinct, before a sudden flash of genius back-lit her temporarily revitalised emerald eyes, accompanied by a smug smile. 'I'm a professional aircraft sleep tester.' The woman mouthed the job title back, bemused. 'It's very important work,' the K-Wolf then assured her fellow passenger, doing her best to sound serious, without mockingly overacting. 'I get paid to sleep on flights and grade how comfortable it is. Biiiiiiiiiiiig business is aircraft comfort.' Sold by the forced sincerity of the K-Wolf's words, the woman nodded along, her mouth reverting to a smile as a passenger then took the aisle seat. 'So, yeah, if you'll excuse me,' Kari yawned, actions amplified, 'I need to nod off, or I won't get paid, and nobody will be comfortable, and the world will end, everyone make a frowny face...' With a shameless smile across her lightly freckled face, the K-Wolf took great delight in sliding down into her seat, arms hugging herself to sleep as she heard the introduction repeat itself to the unfortunate woman in the aisle seat. It was all almost enough to make her stay in the same country for more than a fortnight. Almost.
