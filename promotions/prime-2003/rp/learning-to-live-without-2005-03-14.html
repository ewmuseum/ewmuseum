Learning to Live Without

14 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 14, 2005 Thursday, March 10, 2004 4:15 PM EST Located about 4 blocks from the scene of ReVolution 57, the Joe Louis Arena, Killean Sirrajin and Amy Campbell decided to take a walk in Graphix Park. Spending an extra day in Detroit was not an idea on the top on either of their lists but it became a reality when Killean asked Aimz out for a day of relaxation. A walk through the park seemed harmless enough and Killean believed he could drive to ReVolution the following week in Anaheim and perhaps stop by home in San Diego as well. Killean came around the 2005 Chrysler Crossfire and opened the door to let Aimz out. Killean then closed the door behind her and armed the state of the art car alarm. Sirrajin was wearing his usual black trench coat and has his hand in his pockets. He drew a few looks as the sky was rather overcast yet he still wore his trademark sunglasses. They walked along in the park rather silent for a few minutes, taking in the scenery. It was not really a cold day, about 21 Fahrenheit and the wind was almost non existent. The cool air seemed like a relief to the two compared to the night before in the stuffy arena. The silence lasted for only a couple minutes longer. Killean’s goal here was to get to know the Red Raver and perhaps shoe would get to know him better as well. “What is Halifax like this time of year?” Killean started with not removing his hands from his pockets. Amy smiled, trying to find the words to answer the question. “Well the warm air coming off the water keeps the extreme cold away. It does seem to be cloudy quite a bit and there can be some large storms as well. Nothing to serious really, probably nothing compared to your hometown.” Aimz answered and looked for some knowledge from him. Killean wanted that and wanted to know if Aimz really wanted to know more about him. He was pleased that she did. He also laughed aloud about the mention of the cold in Manitoba. “Ha-ha, yes that’s right. Winnipeg is well known as Winterpeg at this time of the year. I always like to keep up with my hometown but I am glad I am not there right now. Forty below zero is not something I enjoy. But I do miss the snow when I am home in San Diego.” Killean seemed anxious to tell Aimz things about himself. But this was no regular discussion with a member of the opposite sex for him. He did not find himself attracted to her per say nor did he find himself lusting for her. This was quite different. He really wanted to get to know her and get closer, but not intimately. “How did you end up in San Diego anyways?” Aimz questioned with a smile that made Killean crack one as well. He was a little out of his shell with her. “Well I joined a training school for Professional Wrestling and they were located in San Diego. Not to mention the Underground was based out of there. It was my first real wrestling job and the show didn’t travel. Always being in San Diego for work made it easy to move there. I just haven’t really found a reason to leave.” He was quiet yet punctual with his answer. He really wanted to build a proper picture for Aimz as he was sure it would lead to more discussion. “So is that where you met Kira?” Aimz dared to ask and Sirrajin’s face sort of sank at the mention of his wife. But he kept his composure. After all, he did want Aimz to ask questions and it was an honest one. “Yeah, I met her while I was wrestling with the Underground. A previous job allowed me to save quite a bit of money. I had saved more money than many even make in a lifetime. So we could afford a house when we decided we would get serious. We had been married in June of 2003 and we were living how we wanted. I was not even working at the time and I was sure I wouldn’t return to the squared circle again. But after the accident, it sort of changed my previous plans and I decided to move on. PRIME was there and I jumped at the chance.” Aimz seemed a little taken off guard with the detail that Killean went into, but she wasn’t complaining. Looking at him as he was talking was proof enough that he was really beginning to get comfortable with her. His facial expression was not hiding any feeling and his voice was not nervous sounding. But Killean himself knew he was feeling nervous around her. But he was being confident as he could get. “So tell me a little about yourself. Was Halifax always your home?” “It has always been my home. But I am on the road so much now so it feels like I don’t have a home. I miss it a lot. I also miss my son and I know he misses me too.” As the words slipped through the bright pink lips of the Red Raver, Killean paused in his tracks and Aimz didn’t notice it right away. She looked back with a smile on her blushing face. Her multi-colored hair was blowing around, sometimes falling to hit her between the eyes. She brushed the hair away quickly. “I had no idea you had a child. You don’t seem the… well…” Killean was lost for words until they were unwillingly given to him. “Type?” Aimz smiled at him again as they began to walk side by side once again. “No, I hear what you are saying. I am young and I am a child at heart. But there are certain times when I feel my age.” “I understand completely.” Sirrajin whispered out, trying to ensure Aimz that he truly did. “I felt that way sometime ago as well. When I was in the Underground I was a child at heart as well. I was immature and childish as you can get. But I realized that soon I would have to be better to myself and to others. I had to begin to move my entire life into adulthood. I began to save the money and then I met Kira. She took me the rest of the way. She was the main focal point in my life and it began to turn my focus to family instead of other things I just didn’t need.” Aimz was a little shocked that he would reveal that kind of information to her. They hardly knew each other and she finally realized that the wall was completely gone and he was feeling comfortable with her. He felt comfortable saying anything to her. “Wow, maybe things will turn out that way for me too. I am glad you were able to share that with me Killean.” Aimz said, blushing once again as she didn’t want to sound too friendly just yet. But she felt a connection with him, something that she couldn’t quite explain “I am sure they will Amy, it just takes some time and patience.” Killean assured her with his gentle yet convincing tone. His voice was deep yet very friendly, something Aimz noticed and it intrigued her. Here was a guy who was a killing machine in the wrestling ring, opening up to her. He didn’t care who he hurt in the ring just so long as he stood on top of the mountain when it was all said and done. Yet he seemed like a totally different person. Maybe he wasn’t like this with everyone, but what made her so special? She couldn’t come up with the answer to her own question. She didn’t think she was anyone special. But this man, this… monster of a man who looked like a cold, deadly machine actually had another side to him that she was sure was not normally out in the open like this. “I am just curious, is Killean your real name?” Aimz said with a bit of laughter. Sirrajin actually broke a smile as well and turned to her with a look that said ‘Are you kidding?’ “What? It was just an honest question.” “Of course it is.” Killean replied with a giggle in his voice. Aimz found that rather entertaining. “You really don’t think I just came up with it on a whim do you?” “Well how am I supposed to know that you smart ass?” Aimz playfully punched his arm. What was going on here? She only did that with her best of friends. Was it wise to be so relaxed with him this early on? “Heh, well I didn’t. It is my given name. Apparently it is a take on my grandfather’s name. His name was Leon and I guess my parents just saw it fit to change it up a bit.” He looked a little disturbed at discussing his parents with her. They were the people he hardly knew and from what he learned about them, people he didn’t want to know at all. “Don’t bother asking about them though. I know little to nothing about them. It’s a rather touchy subject.” She was quite impressed at this point. He just told her some things that he wouldn’t tell his own friends. She probably knows more history about him now than almost anyone. She knew of one person that probably knew more but unfortunately, she had passed on. “You know, Kira sounded like a great person. I’m so sorry Killean. I couldn’t imagine that kind of loss.” Oh no, did she just overstep her boundaries? She noticed his face sink for a second, but he respectfully turned to look at her with an honest face and a smile that could melt ice. “Thank you Amy, I really appreciate that.” Killean even surprised himself. Where was this conversation going? They continued to walk around the park as the snow began to fall in big fluffy flakes. Some landed on Killean’s hair and Aimz attempted to brush them away. Killean stepped back a little as he wasn’t really expecting that kind of physical contact. “Sorry, I just figured the snowflakes might bother you.” Aimz said, turning her head to the side a little. She felt embarrassed. “That’s… ok. Really it is; I’m sorry about my sudden reaction.” Killean apologized. What was he apologizing for? Aimz thought he might not appreciate the contact. But Killean cleared his face and smiled at her again. This was turning out to be a strange day. But Aimz and Killean seemed to be happy about how it was all coming around. They were silent all the way back to the car. Monday, March 14, 2005 9:45 AMSan Diego, California “You have a worsening case of “ explained Doctor Harvey. Killean had been going every now and then to see the eye doctor. The sunglasses were not always a part of the Sirrajin persona. There definitely was a legitimate reason behind them but it just so happens that no one knew about it. The doctor continued, “I have no choice Killean but to prescribe you a pair of specialized goggles to wear when you wrestle. It will prevent the situation from worsening.” Killean was stunned. He only came in because he had a little bit of a spell on the drive home from Detroit. He never expected that he would need a prescription. “Listen Doc, I am just having difficulty with all this. Has it really gotten that bad since my last visit?” Killean was trying to grip the reality of what the doctor was telling him. He never thought that one day, he would have to wear protective goggles when wrestling. “Well let’s put it this way, once I looked at the test results and took a closer look at your eyes I could swear I was treating someone other than Killean Sirrajin.” The doctor’s words hurt Killean. It had the feeling of razor blades sliding across his skin. “Ok, I understand. But I have a very important match coming up in a short couple of weeks. I want to keep this problem secret so no one preys on it. Is there any way that I can put this off until that match is over and done with?” Killean had desperation in his voice. He really did not want to reveal his problem to the wrestling world this soon. When he reports to Ultra Violence, he did not want to give any advantage to his opposition, whether it is Boda or Karina Wolfenden. Knowing the smarts of both of them, they would try to expose the weakness any way they could. “Well, it goes against my better judgment I hope you know.” The doctor was writing out the piece of paper as he was talking. He did Killean this one favor. “I will write the prescription for April 4, 2005. That is one day after your big match. Please fill it as of then. I would hate to see someone as young as you make that eye sight worse. It will not cause blindness, but more exposure to bright lights can cause the situation to worsen to the point where you will have to wear protective eyewear 24 hours a day.” “I understand Doctor.” Killean was grateful. He knew the doctor would make the exception. Although the exceptions in the past for certain other things no doubt made this current situation what it was. The sunglasses were no doubt a part of the Sirrajin persona in PRIME, but they were about to become much more. However, no one will know soon enough. Monday, March 14, 2005 3:30 PMJust Outside San Diego Killean was walking out under the shining sun. Of course he was wearing his trademark sunglasses but he was minus a coat. It was a little warmer out today and Sirrajin decided to just wear a plain black t-shirt. Although, he was not walking on the beach behind his home. It just so happens that Killean had to slowly weave his way through a bunch of tombstones. As the mortal bodies of the deceased lay undisturbed and unable to hear, the angels above surely caught wind of the weeping coming from Killean Sirrajin. The Supreme Machine was no doubt shedding a few tears on this day. Carrying a large bouquet of flowers, he was no doubt making a beeline to a certain tombstone. “It just so happens that I will have a tune-up match this coming edition of ReVolution. But there is a little problem.” Killean spoke, trying to hide the small sobs that were trying to escape. He was focused, just not entirely on one thing. “I am not afraid to admit that I am not as sure about this match as I want to be. Brandon Youngblood is a rising star here in PRIME. He took Emilio Rage and Angelo Deville to the limit last week. How could that make me feel?” Killean stepped his way around another couple of tombstones before coming to a stop. He was looking down as he began to speak again. “I feel as if I could very well lose this match. But I need to be confident. So in order to make myself completely confident, I have a little surprise come ReVolution for Youngblood. No it’s nothing that will hurt him. As a matter of fact, it will help the both of us. Not to mention that it will give me the confidence that I need to win the match. My focus is on Brandon Youngblood, but I will also be watching Boda and Karina. I will be awaiting the winner of that match come Ultra Violence. I am getting my one on one shot, finally. Even though it would be my pleasure to destroy Boda for all the fans, I respect Karina and I would be glad to wrestle her for the title. However, no matter who it is, it will not change the fact that I will be there to take the title. I will lay claim to the grandest prize of them all, The Universal Championship. No one can stop me now.” Still looking down, Killean bends onto one knee and stares at the tombstone. Just as expected, the grave is that of Killean’s beloved. Kira lay quietly below his feet. Killean places the flowers against the tombstone as he sobs lightly. It is a powerful sight to see such a strong and confident individual bending on one knee, sobbing for his beloved wife who left him so tragically. It was almost an uncontrollable sob as he spoke quietly to Kira, knowing she was watching every action and listening to every word. “Dearest Kira, I have finally won my first battle. I have come to terms that I have lost you and that I am forced to live without you. Oh how I wish you were still with me, but with the help of a dear friend, I have begun to heal. I know you only want to see me happy. So I shall work down that road now. I will be happy for you, whatever it takes. I will be happy. I love you my darling, and I will definitely have you in my heart always. I will do it all for you. I will fulfill a dream April 3rd and win a championship that I desire so much. It will all be done for you. I will not be doing it on my own as I will have you with me. Listen to my words as you Rest In Peace my beauty.” Killean rubs his hand on the face of the tombstone, tracing the letters with his fingers. The letters spelled Kira Sirrajin. If anything, this victory would help him move one step closer to his dreams and desires. That is having a happy life and possessing the one piece of wrestling history that will truly make him a legend. “Listen to me nowI need to let you knowYou don't have to go it alone And it's you when I look in the mirrorAnd it's you when I don't pick up the phoneSometimes you can't make it on your own” -Lyrics from “Sometimes You Can’t Make It On Your Own” by the legendary U2
