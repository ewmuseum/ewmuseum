Turning the Corner

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 196 Andrea’s eyes looked carefully at a few documents that rested on her desk. She stifled a yawn before she reached over and grabbed her cup of coffee. A moment later, Andrea glanced over at the nearby clock, and found that David was running ten minutes behind schedule. A sigh left her lips as she fired on the computer and began typing away furiously. “Morning,” the groggy voice of David crept into the empty silence. Andrea looked up for a second from her computer screen and waved David in before she continued to type. David looked at her for a minute before he sat in a chair across from the desk. “Sorry, just needed to shoot off this email confirming your availability for the next few weeks to Lisa. Though, it would be helpful if this time around you didn’t do anything crazy backstage. Don’t really need her yelling at me again over the phone.” Andrea looked over and saw him dozing off. “David.” The stern tone of her voice managed to stir David who eagerly sat up in the chair even though it was evident that he was still tired. “Sorry,” he mumbled under his breath as the palms of his hands wiped off his face in an attempt to wake up. “Late night. You know how those go.” The look from Andrea spoke differently though. “Or, you know, not.” “How’d the date go?” “I’d have to say it went well. Really well.” Andrea groaned. “Okay, no. We’re in a business meeting right now. Don’t need to know that you scored on the first date. Try to learn some tact, alright? Especially since you’re not only dating someone else who is in the limelight, but your ring partner as well. Don’t need People magazine reporting on all of your latest life details as if you were Paris Hilton or Nicole Ritchie, understand?” Andrea asked David in a rather professional tone. Deep down, David was already regretting agreeing to this arrangement, but he took a deep breath in and found that it calmed his nerves. “Yeah, sure, whatever you say, boss.” “Good, because your professional image is going to go a long way over the next few months, especially as PRIME starts to look at you for a bigger spot in their company. There are bonuses you have to consider as it pertains to big-time matches ala Colossus, which is coming up very soon; also if you win championships. You start doing things like that, and Lisa is going to want to lock you in to a long term contract. But, if you’re no better than a Tyler Rayne or Wade Elliott, then she’s going to show you that door because she already has enough of those,” she continued on, the glasses that rested on top of her head earlier now firmly planted on her nose. David could tell why Andrea had been regarded as one of the better lawyers in her law firm, although he was still a little confused as to why they let her go. “Sounds like you’ve got everything taken care of then. Don’t even need me. Get those contracts signed away and make me my money,” he informed her before David began to get out of his chair. Yet, he was stopped cold as Andrea held her hand up. “We’re not done quite yet. While the PRIME side of things seems to be going well, I’ve started to get some preliminary contracts from several companies looking to see if you’re interested in doing commercials or endorsing their products. I mean, we’ve got some pretty good companies here like Gatorade--,” He cut her off. “Look, Andrea, as far as I’m concerned, that’s your job. You make the decisions that you think would best fit me. Want me to be in a Gatorade commercial or do a promotional event? You sign the contracts, get my calendar together, and get me there. That’s how I work and that’s how I operate. Leave time in my schedule for me to relax, work out, and get my stuff done. That’s all. But, I’m not really interested in how this side of the business works. That’s all on you,” David retorted just as quickly and it left Andrea a little surprised at the nonchalant attitude her older brother was showing her. “Alright.” Andrea paused for a moment. “Well, in that case, we really need to talk about Dad’s will. I did what you asked in terms of contacting his lawyer and he’s insisting that we meet together to go over it.” David sighed. “Look, can you meet with the guy? Talk to him? Just, let’s get this over with. This family doesn’t need to be dragged around any longer. Got it?” David asked his sister and she simply nodded her head. David then moved to the door, but was stopped again. “Do you know what’s wrong with Mom recently?” David looked back at Andrea and shrugged his shoulders. Not quite the response that Andrea was looking for. “Alright, fine. Do you want to talk about the mirror you broke at the hotel? Lisa had to call me about that one,” Andrea continued, but the look that David shot her answered that question as well. Without another word spoken, David disappeared, and left his sister to herself. She looked over at the computer screen and sighed. “Want to talk about anything?” she muttered to no one in particular as another email hit her inbox. She glanced at it and sighed before placing her hands on her forehead. Things were only getting worse. *** “So, Jack, he thought he would be really crafty and smart about it all. He just waited and waited, it must’ve been hours. He was so certain that it was going to happen, and then Tony tapped him on the back. Jack turned around, and low and behold, Kanye West was on the ground knocked out.” Mary-Lynn and David laughed at the same time before David took a drink from his cup and lifted the slice of pizza into the air. “So,” David began. “How’d you guys end up putting Kanye in a box?” The question tickled Mary-Lynn as she shook her head before she coughed into her napkin from laughing so hard. “Jack, don’t ask me why, rented a crane and lifted Kanye into the box. Then, took him down to the arena, and gave Kanye to Craig.” David had to fight back the tears that welled in his eyes. “Man, that’s crazy stuff,” David said and then took a bite from a slice of scalding hot cheese pizza. It tasted wonderful though as he moaned from a mixture of the taste and the pain from the heat. Mary-Lynn pulled the pizza away from his mouth and placed it back on the plate as David quickly took a sip of Pepsi from his glass. “Those were the good days.” “Not so good anymore?” “Just not the same. Back then, it was crazy. Now, everyone’s so spread out and doing their own things. For me though, those were some of my best memories of being in wrestling. I mean, I’ve got good memories now, but back then it was just so funny.” David agreed with that sentiment as he had seen it for himself. Craig was always on the go and Jack had seemingly slowed down from the hectic schedule he had managed to keep for the first half of the year. Mary-Lynn and David were the rookies of the group and they had somehow discovered the way to keep surviving in the business. “Oh, MacKenzie asked about you today,” David told her and Mary-Lynn looked surprised by that revelation. “Oh, really?” “Yeah, she wants to meet you. She’s seen you on TV and wants to find out if your hair is really that red, you know? I think you’ll love her though.” Mary-Lynn blushed. “I’m game whenever you are.” Another bite of pizza disappeared into his mouth. “Well,” David began before he lifted the cup to his lips and took another swig of the Pepsi. “How does tonight sound? Get to meet the family and get it out of the way.” “I think that’d be a really good idea I’ve been dying to meet your sisters and Lord knows that I need some friends around here,” the red-head quipped, in between a bite of pasta, and David took a deep breath in. He felt the pressure on him now, but somehow found the strength to shrug it off. “We’re on our second date and already interested in becoming friends with the sisters, huh?” “I get a very good feeling, that I’m not going anywhere,” Mary-Lynn whispered and he nodded his head confidently. Their eyes locked with one another and moved in to kiss each other. Electricity nearly came off their lips as they embraced each other and felt one with each other. As they broke apart and looked into each other’s eyes they agreed with one another on that very point. “Alright then, let’s get the hell out of here,” David said as his hand found the small of Mary-Lynn’s back and she smiled, happier than she had been in quite some time. *** The door to the Noble house opened with little effort. In through the threshold walked David and Mary-Lynn, holding hands, and they looked around at the house to see a considerable amount of work had been done in sprucing up the place. “Wait here, let me see who’s around,” David told Mary-Lynn who just nodded her head as David broke out into a half jog as he entered one of the side rooms where he found MacKenzie as she sat on the ground, dressed for bed, coloring. “Hey MacKenzie. Isn’t it time for you to go to bed?” MacKenzie looked up from her picture and David saw she was coloring a picture of various Disney princesses. “Yeah, but Andrea and Samantha were in the kitchen talking, and I’m waiting for them to tuck me in,” she confided in him and David simply chuckled. “Here, take my hand, and I’ll go tuck you in. You know, there’s someone here to see you.” Her ears perked up at that, and then she remembered something. “You know,” MacKenzie said as they walked towards the living room. “There’s someone here to see you as well.” Those words didn’t sit well with David. They rounded the corner, where David had left the beautiful Mary-Lynn, but she wasn’t there. “Well, Mary-Lynn was here,” he muttered under his breath as he looked around hoping to find a glimpse of her bright red hair. Yet, he didn’t see her anywhere close by. “Hm.” “MacKenzie, who’s here to see me?” David asked her inquisitively, and MacKenzie shrugged her shoulders. “Beats me. Just some girl. Said she needed to talk to you. I think Andrea talked to her for a moment,” As he stepped up a few stairs he saw Mary-Lynn’s auburn hair and a smile came across his face. Yet, as he came down the stairs, he found Andrea and Samantha in chairs as well, and a mysterious woman who sat on the couch by herself. “Hey guys,” he said slowly as he noticed the fear in Mary-Lynn’s eyes. Something was definitely wrong as Andrea rushed up to him. “David, we need to talk.” It came from Andrea. “Come on ‘Kenzie. Let’s go put you to bed,” Samantha said as she rushed her out of the room. “But, I wanted to see David’s friend.” “Alright, you’re worrying me. What’s going on? Who’s that?” he asked her and the look only grew worse on Andrea’s face. “You really don’t know?” David shrugged his shoulders as he moved past Andrea who tried to grab his arm in the process, but missed as he walked closer towards Mary-Lynn and the other beautiful woman who sat in the house that supposedly knew him. “Hey, what’s going on?” David asked the gathered crowd and immediately Mary-Lynn shot him a look of contempt. It took David by surprise as he took a step back. “Just was chatting with… Philly, over here. Had a quite interesting conversation in the two minutes I’ve sat here with her. She look familiar to you?” Mary-Lynn interrogated David who looked over at the woman named Philly and shook his head. Philly didn’t offer any words as Mary-Lynn cut in right away. “Okay, let me ask you then,” she began once again before she pointed over at Philly. “Notice anything unique about her.” David chuckled as he looked over at Philly and gave her a once over, but noticed nothing unique about this woman who had yet to speak. Noble looked over at his younger sister for some sort of explanation, but a look of panic was on her face as she couldn’t even look at David. He returned his gaze to Mary-Lynn who was slowly starting to get upset. “Nah, Mary-Lynn, I really don’t.” “Look again.” David did as he was told and as he looked at Philly, he did indeed notice something different. Something that troubled him greatly, especially as to the reason that she was here to see him. She was pregnant.
