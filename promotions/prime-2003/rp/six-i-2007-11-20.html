Six (i)

20 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 20, 2007 Where I’m supposed to be is in the back of a limousine. Where that limousine is supposed to be going is to the airport. From there I’m supposed to take a flight to Toronto, back to my home. Back to the home of so many bad memories and nightmares. Where I actually am is far from where I should be. Where I actually am is a small motel room in the middle of nowhere. Where I actually am is sitting alone in the fucking dark. Where I actually am is nowhere. I take a look at myself in the shattered mirror. I see myself, my face scattered everywhere, fractured by the shattered glass. I can’t even remember how many days ago I broke the fucking thing. Oh, the joys of alcohol abuse. Anger… Check. Depression… Check. Rage… Check. I stare in the mirror and staring into my cold, dark eyes, I realize how much I hate myself. They say the eyes are the doorways to one’s soul. You think maybe that’s why when I look into my own, I see nothing? Existential meltdown in six… I turn my face away from the mirror, I can’t bear the burden of looking at myself any longer. I look back at the dimly lit hotel room. I look back to the empty bottles scattered all over the floor, I look back at the mound of white powder on the night table, I look back to the stains of hooker sweat on the cheap blankets. I look back at all of this, and I hope that the next time I open my eyes, I won’t be able to. I look back at this and I hope that the next time I close my eyes, they stay shut forever. I clench my eyelids shut. I try to open them. Unfortunately for me, they open. I’m brought back to the motel room, brought back to the dimly lit hotel room. Brought back to my cage. Brought back to my hell. Five… I walk across the hotel room, my bare feet dragging across the rugged, cheap carpeting. I throw myself onto the bed, slumping myself onto the hard, uncomfortable mattress. I’ve barely managed to catch more than two hours sleep a night on this piece of shit. Serves me right, what the fuck do you expect for twenty dollars a night? I close my eyes again, hoping that sleep will wash over me. After a few seconds, I know it’s not happening, not now at least. I open my eyes and drag my worthless ass up to my feet. I take a few steps to the night table, I bend over, and bury my nose into the powder. I don’t even bother to cut out a line, fuck it. This kids, is what you call a drug addiction. I snort and the powder shoots up my nostrils, sinking into my bloodstream almost immediately. I feel the euphoria begin to set in, but not even the massive amounts of cocaine running through my blood stream are enough to make me feel any better. This kids, is what we call the end of the rope. I stare across the room and notice the chess board sitting at the far side of the room. I find solace in the memories of the board and I can’t help but move to it. I stand to my feet, my legs wobbly, my balance feigning. I reach my hand out, pressing it softly against the grimy walls of the room. I feel my balance returning and I stand myself up. I look at the wall, I can see clearly the imprint of my hand. I look down and my hand is covered in dirt. Hooray for the janitor of this place. This kids, is what you call utterly fucking disgusting. Four… I wipe the dirt onto my pants and walk slowly towards the chess board. I stare at the board, the pieces still set up almost identically to how they ended, almost exactly as I remember them. The only one not in place is the black king. The charcoal colored marble piece lies tilted, fallen, defeated. I stare at the board and I think back. ”Check mate.” I look into his eyes, and I wonder how the fuck he always wins. I take my focus from his eyes and turn it to the board. Everything was going so well, he was almost down and out. And now check mate? What the hell had happened? Salaok looks at me, he brushes his raven black hair away from his face as he smirks. His face is filled with charisma, his very presence makes me feel uneasy. He pulls a small cigarette from the package on the table, he offers me one, but I tastefully decline. If I’m going to kill myself, I’m doing it in a blaze of blood and gore, not one dose of Carbon monoxide at a time. He pulls his lighter out from his pocket and lights up. The smirk doesn’t leave his face as he puffs on the cigarette, his teeth glinting in the light as he inhales on the smoke. The cigarette embers a radiant orange, like the sun and then it’s gone. He flicks the embers off of the cigarette into the small ash tray, the ash tray already containing a beach worth of embers, a plethora of butts litter the beach. “Isn’t it beautiful?” he asks, as he stares down at the board once again. “What the fuck are you talking about?” “Chess,” he responds, his voice calm but enthralling. “It has to be the world’s most beautiful game, but at the same time morbid and filled with primal violence.” Sometimes I wonder how the fuck Salaok Davis ever got me to join the Nevermore. Sometimes I wonder if Salaok Davis even knows what day of the week it is. But still, I feel compelled to listen to the rest of his story. He looks up and flashes that sinister grin that I have seen all too many times. He knows I’m intrigued. “Think about it. An entire army of men, who’s only purpose, is to search out, trap and kill the opposing king. The only purpose of the game is to topple the mighty, to bring down those at the top. The only purpose of the game is to strike down the king, the emperor. Kind of makes you feel like having second thoughts about being at the top, doesn’t it Christian?” I stare at him. My mouth open and my face somewhat puzzled. No wonder our psychopathic leader is good at chess, it has everything he desires apparently. The ultimate chance to break down an opponent’s defense one by one and crush him. The ultimate trap. I take the black king into my hand and hold it in my hand. I hear Salaok’s words echoing through my mind once again. I can’t help but feel like the king myself. Global World Champion, Global Master of the Mat. Everyone in the company is gunning for me. The only difference is I have no pawns to protect me, no knights, no queen. I have no one. I stand alone, against them all. And now I will do it again in PRIME. In the Jewel in the Crown tournament, I will not be the king. Not yet, I will start out as merely a pawn. A pawn in the greater scheme of the tournament, but by King of Kings, I will be the King once again. I will be on top. And I will welcome all those who will try to bring me down. This kids, is what you call a metaphor. All of this starts this Wednesday, November 21. All of this will start at ReVolution 145. All of this will start where my life began. That is if I even make it there alive. I put the chess piece back onto the board. I stare at the game board. My mind continues to play his words over and over, the thoughts echoing through my mind as I look at the perfectly placed 64 squares in front of me. The perfect trap. No escape. Three… Any time you want to get off this train of depression and self-pity, feel free. Me? I’m riding this motherfucker straight into the ground, straight into hell. I turn back to the shattered mirror, staring back at me I see the reflection of a hundred lost souls. Staring back at me I see the reflection of a hundred dead eyes. Staring back at me, I see the pitiful man I have to live with every day. Or do I? I walk to the table. And I slam my fist against the cheap wooden table. I turn my attention away from the mirror, away from myself, and away from the hatred. That’s when I catch a glimpse of it; the cold, black steel of my revolver. It sits, lying seductively on the television stand. A sick smile spreads across my face and the euphoria of the cocaine continues to numb my body. This kids, is what you call getting a bad fucking idea. I stare at the revolver, knowing that beside it is the box of ammunition. I step towards the gun, unable to take my eyes off of it. It calls out to me, begging me to pick it up. Begging for action, begging for the chance to do what is does best. I can only hope tonight, it gets the job done. The box of ammo is tipped over, bullets scattered across the table. I reach out and pick one of the bullets up. I let the bullet roll around in my hand. I stare it with longing eyes as I watch the dim lighting of the room bounce and gleam off of the copper jacket. I stare at the cold copper bullet sitting in my hand. Somewhere, deep down I know this is wrong. Somewhere deep down, I know this is really stupid. Somewhere deep, deep, deep down I know that one day this is going to kill me. Somewhere not so deep down, I hope that day is today. With my right hand I take hold of the revolver. The metal of the gun is icy cold and just the caress of it against my skin sends shivers racing down my spine and leaves every hair on my body standing straight up. My hands are shaking as I swing the shooting cylinder out. My hands tremble as I struggle to force the bullet into one of the six chambers. One in six. Seventeen percent. This kids, is what you call a lesson in mathematics. Today’s mood is angry, depressed and suicidal, with a seventeen percent chance of blowing my fucking brains out. Two… I run my fingers against the cylinder and with a flick of my wrist I send the chambers spiraling. The bullet whizzes by my eyes and I can barely make out the difference between a bullet and an empty chamber. I slam the cylinder back into the gun and cock the hammer back. I clench my eyes shut. I grit my teeth. This might be messy. Mom… Dad… Know that I loved you. As for the rest of you? I’ll see all you fuckers in hell. Fingers still trembling, I slide my finger against the trigger and allow my index finger to caress it. I take the barrel and press it against my temple. The shivers come back through my body, washing over me. I feel my life getting ready to flash before my eyes. One… I squeeze the trigger and wait for the sound. Wait for the thunder of the gun shot. I wait for the light to wash over me. Instead I get the sound of the hammer clicking against the empty chamber. No bullet today, no salvation. Nothing. This kids, is what you call a waste of fucking life.
