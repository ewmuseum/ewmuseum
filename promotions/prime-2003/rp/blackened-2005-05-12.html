Blackened

12 May 2005

Date: 12-05-05 Beaten by Boda. My confidence took a massive hit when I looked up and saw him standing over me. The man who was sure to run through Killean Sirrajin at Overkill to win that coveted Universal Title beat me. How could I ever face Vampir knowing I was unable to succeed against his stable boy? I wonder if I clung onto the belief that that 6’10” growth that hung off the end of Ivan Stanislav’s throbbing member for all of his PCW tenure was nothing more than a spotlight-seeking, lowly cheating sponge then…actually I began to feel better already just thinking of it like that. Let the Big Bad Boda Daddy have his cake and eat it. I was nothing more to him than a big obstacle slowing him down on the way to his Buried Alive match. I should’ve expected such underhanded tact, no matter…when he gets a nice helping of dirt shoved down his throat and tastes bitter defeat seething through the muck I’ll be cheering for the Champion. How odd that the bastard brother of a band of dark miscreants has the privilege of a Buried Alive stipulation bestowed upon him by the management. An oddity amongst an idiosyncrasy has the honour of potentially burying a man alive. In the back of my mind I couldn’t help but wonder if the Dark Age had planned it as a means to prevent me from engaging in a match of that calibre with Vampir and sweeping him under a 6ft carpet of dirt and worms. There would be plenty of time to have fun with the Soul Stalker though, as I said before his fate would be a painful lasting one…Overkill would be our first encounter, it certainly wouldn’t be our last. I had dealt with Fuhrer and Boda, and their futile attempts to cease my progress only served to feed a raging inferno burning within. Every time I imagined that cage trapping us inside the ring, locking us in and everyone else out I shuddered with excitement. I didn’t even care that Vampir would be in the same situation as me; the prospect of a beating at his hand meant nothing. All that mattered to me was that cage stopping anyone getting in…and a world of hurt coming to Vampir. Then, whatever the outcome…it would begin. I had spent the past few weeks trying to get to Vampir only to have the underlings sent to stop me. After our match at Overkill it would all change. The moment I got to Vampir there would be nothing stopping me, not even the Dark Age. Even if they came after me, Vampir would have already felt the sting of defeat, and demand he take care of the matter. I would seek to humiliate my mentor, give him a reason to want my head on a pike. I wanted to put everything on the line to make this worthwhile; after all there was no satisfaction in a victory without a fight. Only then would I be able to truly appreciate the demise of Vampir Nosferatu, knowing he worked to prevent it… And failed. ---------- Two fading welts adorned his forehead, and remembering the brutal shots by Boda, he twisted the final bolt into place, exerting a little extra force as a means of releasing the frustration. Tapping the fixture, Hessian backed away and grinned in admiration of the structure now in place within the last chamber. From the fitting he had just secured hung a leather shackle, suspended by a chain fastened to the rock wall. Another shackle adjacent to it and two at ankle level on either side of the chamber made up the restraints, while two small pits had been dug out of the rocky ground by Hessian himself, separated by a ridge big enough for someone to stand on as they were shackled between these pits. Residing within these holes appeared from first glance to be two large black cylinders, but with two massive pillars standing ominously at either side of the ridge between the pits they had an obvious use. Pity the poor soul who found out what it was. The stagnating fetor of all ungodly discharge emanating from the sacrificial body by the entrance to the basement had seemingly soaked into the stone. A vague odour of death clung to the walls yet was barely noticeable to the average entrant into the basement. The perfect atmosphere for the purpose the room would serve, and with the interior finally finished it would soon see its first guests, or captors as the equipment within the saline walls would suggest. As Hessian paced back and forth along the dark cavernous excavation he struggled to catch a glimpse of each nightmarish surprise waiting within the walls of each chamber. Looking to the ceiling the grin residing at the corner of his lips extended to a full smile as he felt his way along the wall back to the entrance, running his hand over something and stopping. Flicking down with his thumb the object, a light switch, brought a flickering white luminescence to the room. At first it flickered slowly, appearing to struggle, until suddenly the flashing became faster to the point where a strobe effect filled the entire cavern. At once all the dastardly contraptions, the modified torture racks, iron maidens and what could only be described as sex swings lit up within their cubbyholes. Rubbing his hands together and chuckling maniacally, Hessian extended his arms and let loose a triumphant roar! The walls echoed as the strobe lighting breathed life into the underground nightmare. Taking one last glimpse at his own personal Hell, the Goliath made his way out of the basement, slamming the large newly reinforced door shut and jogging back up to the first floor. He caught himself chuckling once again, gazing over all that he had built. In just a couple of months the disused office building of a crooked loan officer had become a grand epicentre for all the depravity the most sickened individual could put to a list, as well as dancing and drinking. The Mercenary of Satan, the dealer of fate to the immortals had since the turn of the year ventured into unknown territory within the business realm and now, stood on the brink of success or disaster. Before the dawn of June, the Slaughterhouse would open its doors to the filth of the city looking for a new place to congregate. Before those doors opened however, there was the small manner of staff. From the beginning Hessian knew the club would be too big to manage himself, but the issue of staff never seemed a problem…in that Hessian knew exactly who he wanted working for him. The lounge looked as welcoming as ever, and as fresh as the day it had been installed. Checking over the minor details in his head Hessian headed for the large stairway that led to the second floor where the core of the partying would emanate from. Stepping from the polished marble stairs onto the hardwood floor the difference was noticeable immediately. While downstairs the lounge choked with an exquisite air of grandeur, the second floor felt grittier. Rather bland looking, half of the room was designated to a dance floor with a raised floor near the back, while the other half played host to another larger bar with simple black tables and chairs, separated from the dance floor by a barrier crafted from oak, painted to a black finish and decorated with bronzed barbed wire wrapping around the pillars of intricate carved designs on the barrier. With plasma screen televisions mounted around the walls and a couple of pool tables and amusements within the bar area the second floor was the ideal place to let it all hang out. The silence only captivated Hessian, as the room seemed to pulsate with life, realizing its purpose and waiting in anticipation to fulfil its needs. After putting effort and pride into making this work, it seemed less inanimate to him. An aura of sadism similar to his own was present within the building, most likely rising up from the brooding underground Hell beneath the club. It felt like home. Win or lose it was an experience, and if all else failed Hessian had gained property in the process. Not unlike the troubles with Vampir, where it was a case of win or win, there was no lose. Just as he had crafted a masterpiece from a tower of insipid stone, Vampir had warped a war machine from the broken parts of that dishevelled body of the young Von Kelsig that fateful night he lost his family. The transition to abnormality began when everything normal had been destroyed. Having his family cut from existence was like severing the main artery from his heart. Everything he held true, his beliefs, emotions, his pride and love all bled from him through the cracks in the pavement and into the void beyond. Like a Hermit crab stumbling upon an empty shell, Vampir came upon Von Kelsig and claimed him as his own. The impact of the massacre at the hands of James Carlisle had left Von Kelsig scarred as emotionally as he was physically. Lying in a hospital bed recovering he felt like he was trapped in his own mind, made to suffer the horror over and over again with no release, like the young American soldier of Dalton Trumbo’s classic “Johnny Got His Gun”, with the sole exception that he had full use of his body…only he was numb inside. All Vampir needed was a mindless drone to make his own warrior. Like Frankenstein’s monster, but with the body already perfect only the mind needed a drop of poison to tune it into his way of thinking. Rehabilitation was an arduous process, but Von Kelsig recovered well. Though Vampir had his own rehabilitation in mind, a detoxification of sorts to cleanse the Hessian of all that was pure and tarnish the young man’s mind. The process was successful, but it was not without its sick consequences… -------------------Detroit, MIMay 6th, 19978:05pm------------------- “There he is, standing in the window.” The voice whispered gruffly into his ear. It almost sounded like he was talking with his throat slit, but Angelus had learned to ignore the awful disfigurement in its entirety, focusing instead on the wishes and promises of his saviour. “Who? Why are we here?” Angelus asked, peeking over a hedgerow on tiptoe. Before losing his balance he could make out a well-built gentleman in a string vest and dress pants by the window combing his hair. Turning to the cloaked figure beside him he quickly jerked his head to the left a little, hiding the hideous scars on his face from view. “Truly you must have suffered a blind rage not to have identified him, Von Kelsig. The well-to-do gentleman you see before you was the culprit who thought it humane to deliver that explosive into your family home as a means of repayment to his employer.” Vampir growled under his breath, and a musty, vomit-inducing breath it was. “He…he set my home on fire? He’s the one that killed my family?” Angelus asked frantically, promptly being shushed by the Soul Stalker. “He was the first, two other explosives followed but yes it was he that led the attack.” Vampir confirmed, moving around the hedgerow to a 1994 BMW 7 Series parked in the paved driveway. Without a second thought he reached out and opened the back door of the car, motioning for the Von Kelsig boy to enter. “He is a foolish man, the epitome of capitalism. He believes in a neighbourhood of wealth the need for locking any material possessions is irrelevant, why worry when he has a nice sum resting in his bank balance. Angelus he would sooner call the car company to reserve their latest model than report a stolen vehicle. After tonight he won’t need a car…now get in.” Vampir motioned again, pointing a stern finger to the back seat. “But…what-…why am I-…” Von Kelsig stuttered as he climbed into the car, tucking himself down onto the floor. “He assisted in the death of your loved ones. He watched as they burned alive, breathed in the aroma of their flesh melting off the bone. You know exactly what you’re doing here Von Kelsig.” And with that Vampir closed the door, backing away into the shadows as the front door of the house opened and the prey appeared. Glancing up out of the car, Angelus could see him mouthing something into the house before closing the door and jogging to the car. Ducking back down, he held his breath as the door opened then slammed shut as the lackey to Carlisle plopped into the driver’s seat. Watching from the shadows still, Vampir grinned as the BMW reversed out onto the road and took off. Not a quarter mile down the road the car suddenly braked hard, jerking to the right across the road and into a streetlight. The crash itself seemed very anticlimactic, almost orchestrated, but it would to someone who wasn’t hiding in the backseat, watching the driver’s eyes in the mirror for that moment where he looked to the rear window, eyes wide with horror as the disfigured face lunged forward, arms wrapping around the headrest and choking the throat. As the car swerved the crack sounded from his neck, and as it crunched into the streetlight the limp body thrust into the steering wheel, crushing bone and teeth. Angelus sat in the back gripping the seat, staring at the head resting against the steering wheel as wheezing breath escaped from the lungs, ending abruptly. The thrill of the kill had enveloped him completely, and as the heavy breathing subsided a smile graced his face. The back door opening beside him drew a shocked breath from his chest, only for Vampir to look in, a smile surely upon his masked face. No one said a word, Angelus left the car and together they walked away from the wreck. “What about the body? The police will find out won’t they?” Angelus asked, trying to overcome the excitement that had left him shaking in the warmth of the night. “One thing you will learn in time Von Kelsig is that hiding one’s tracks is very much easier than first anticipated. Allow me to demonstrate.” Vampir hissed, wafting back towards the crashed car. Angelus watched closely as the Soul Stalker reached through the broken window, something metallic glinting in his clawed hand. Waiting for a moment, Vampir tore a piece of the dead man’s shirt off, before retracting his arm and walking around to the diesel cap, ripping it off and inserting the shirt. As if by magic, fire suddenly burst from Vampir’s palm, latching a deadly embrace around the torn cloth and working its way into the tank. Vampir and Von Kelsig had already disappeared from sight as the BMW exploded, sending shards of metal and balls of fire hurtling into nearby gardens, setting trees on fire and calling to attention every resident at home in the street…including the victim’s wife and daughter. Amongst the panic and the screaming, Angelus and his mentor watched on hidden from the light. Glancing at the house the young Von Kelsig watched as the wife and daughter ran barefoot screaming and crying for their father and their loving husband respectively as he burned to ashes. Seeing the tears streaming down the little girl’s face, the wild look of horror upon the mother’s…Angelus smiled. He felt nothing. ------------------- A single candle flickered weakly in the dark, only the face of Angelus Von Kelsig and the mask of Vampir barely visible in the thick black limbo. “You must remain strong Angelus. Insensitivity brings strength, if you are dead to the world the world cannot kill you. You have done well my son, many have fallen by your hand and deservedly so. You have left wives widowed, husbands widowers and children orphaned in the name of vengeance. You wrath has only just begun to unfold upon humanity, and in years to come you will be spoken of in legend just as I am now. Before I can unleash you there is one thing you must do first…” Vampir sat Angelus down, taking a hold of his hand and slicing his index finger open with a thumb claw. Years had passed since the massacre of the Von Kelsig family, and time had been spent contorting the man into a demon beast. His mind was now all but trained to the word of Nosferatu, a disturbing tunnel vision taking over. He had been blinded by the error of his ways, learning first to kill for his own gain, slaying for a purpose. When the time came that Vampir instructed Angelus to “tie up loose ends” by doing away with the widows, widowers and orphans…Von Kelsig simply nodded and struck the final blow as commanded. As blood trickled down his finger, Vampir took hold of it, placing it upon Angelus’s scarred face and marking it with religious symbols of his own cult. “It is your will that our lifeblood mix, that our differing paths come together as one road?” Vampir asked, cutting into his finger and tracing over the symbols again in his own blood. “Yes. It is my will.” Angelus replied, in a zombie-like trance. “That your mind blackens, that the poison course through your veins and the weakness of your soul leaves your body. Is this of your free will?” Vampir asked, slicing another cut into his palm and gripping his wrist, forcing blood through the wound. “Yes. It is my will.” Von Kelsig replied once again, his eyes rolling into the back of his head. Placing his hand on Angelus’s face Vampir smeared the blood in, mixing it with that of his student’s. From his throat spilled words of ancient worship to a deity, to the only entity Vampir would ever bow before. As the complex language slithered off his own tongue, Angelus began repeating by ear almost instantaneously. Abruptly the prayer ended as Vampir thrust his hand into the scar on Angelus’s face, holding his head tightly in both hands as blood spilled from the wound in his palm, oozing from his hands onto Von Kelsig’s face. Releasing his student, Vampir turned away into the shadows as Angelus’s eyes rolled back around. Focusing, the warped student stared straight ahead into the abyss, struggling to find Vampir. “So it is written, so it shall be done.” As the words came forth from the night, something glinted in the candlelight; catching Angelus off guard as it flew threw the air and stuck to the side of his face. Jerking back, the Von Kelsig lad raised his arms to remove it until his hands came into contact with those of Vampir. Lowering them immediately, Angelus felt with his face what had been pressed against it. “Forged of the Dark Age, let this mask strike fear into all who oppose you. Let this be your true definition. Let the lore of the Dark Age empower you; carry you to victory in battle.” Vampir smiled, securing the silver steel half-faceplate to his student’s face. “Now arise………Hessian.” ------------------- He wore the mask to this day as a constant reminder of what Vampir had done to him. Loyally when he was a part of Vampir’s cult, and grudgingly as he plotted his mentor’s death, Hessian held everything close, maintaining the power Vampir had bestowed upon him as a means of some day using it against the creature that had taken his life from him. Certainly in time he might have recovered from the death of his family, but before he could even be given the chance the Soul Stalker had done as his namesake suggested and sucked any remaining life from him, reducing him to a shadow of his former self, to a machine programmed for death and destruction, knowing love only as the violence he perpetrated. He had been groomed for vengeance, and then cold-blooded murder. PRIME’s Overkill Pay-Per-View would see the perfected treatment finally unleashed upon that which cultivated it. To say ‘bite the hand that feeds’ would be an understatement. Hessian was going to tear it from the wrist, and that was just for starters. His mind so deep in thought, Hessian completely ignored the phone as it rang. Jumping up from one of the barstools he ran around the bar and grabbed at the receiver, pulling it to the side of his head just in time to hear the forlorn beeping of a call missed. Hanging up, he lifted the phone again and dialled to find out who had rung. Rubbing his eyes and yawning Hessian waited for the monotonous voice on the other end of the line, feeling strangely tired after having been in thought for so long. “You have…two new messages…and…no…saved messages.” BEEP. “Uh yeah hey there this is Mr. Williams calling about the…stains on your momma’s-…advert in the paper for the position of doorman at the…Slaughterhouse? Shit goddamn why does a fuckin’ abattoir need a doorman? Ain’t no cannibal cows been spotted in Detroit since the Government cover-up of ’58! Um…excuse me, the advertisement said book an interview and bring a résumé. If you could give me a call back and let me know when would be a good time to…FUCK YOU IN THE-…book an interview I’d be very grateful. You can call me back at this number, thank you. Goodbye.” Taken aback by the message, Hessian shook his head and forced a breath of laughter from his lungs. Had it been anyone else he would have deleted the message, but the voice sounded familiar. Forgetting a second message had been left, Hessian was even more shocked to hear another familiar voice sound after the beep. “Um…hey Hess…look don’t delete this please, I need to say something. Actually I need to say it in person. I caught your ad in the Times, thought I’d call you. We need to talk, call me back…erm…or, I’ll call you back. Just don’t delete this, please? I guess I’ll catch you later…bye.” Lowering the phone, Hessian’s jaw dropped as the squeaky voice rung through his head. It had been a long time since they had seen each other, and the last thing Hessian ever expected was an apologetic phone call. On the eve of one of the most important battles he would be a part of, he received a call from one of the more important figures in his life. Vindication never factored once into how Hessian imagined they would meet again…yet it seemed that would be just so. Stunned by the message, Hessian could only utter one word… “Minion.” To Be Continued.
