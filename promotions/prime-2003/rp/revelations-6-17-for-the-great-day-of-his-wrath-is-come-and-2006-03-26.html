Revelations 6:17 For The Great Day Of His Wrath Is Come; And Who Shall Be Able To Stand (match rp)

26 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/26/06 [u]Sunday, February 19th, 2006 Hoyt Williams returns to dominance at the Duel Halo at Culture Shock.[/u] -Hoyt lasts for over an hour and twenty minutes.-Hoyt ends the dreams of six sinners including a geezer, a drunk, a stoner, an obese oaf, a nobody, and a follower. -Hoyt destroys Rich Rollins proving to the world he is PTC’s Top Champion.-Hoyt proves to the WORLD why he is the PONTIFF of PRIME. -Hoyt Williams eats a tuna and cheese sandwich. [u]Sunday, March 26, 2006 Hoyt Duke Williams, Jr. is born to the free world at Lutheran General Hospital in Park Ridge, IL.[/u] -Baby Hoyt weighs in at 12lbs and 6oz.-Mother Mary Williams labors for 6 hours before delivering at 4:20PM-The sun shines bright as the future king of the world is born.-Hoyt Williams weeps. -God smiles.-The baby passes gas. Life is fleeting as time marches on to the beat of a different drummer. It comes and it goes like the ice cream man on a hot summer day taking your money for a short pleasure before it pulls away in musical surrealism. If you’re not lucky enough to have the money to pay the man in the truck your experience is less then pleasant. Hoyt Williams, Jr. is a multi-millionaire at less then a day old. His father and his grandfather are famous wrestlers who have built a pleasant nest egg for generations to come. He is a privileged child who according to his father Hoyt is a miracle and the third and final coming of Christ. If Hoyt’s master plan plays out he will be the ruler of the world until a great evil force comes to destroy him. It will be the battle of revelations that will destroy Hoyt, however his son will rise up and destroy the evil force once and for all, opening the gates of heaven to the earth for ever lasting peace. Such a large title to live up to for a child who is still shitting in his pants and staring oddly at the lights above him in the hospital room with amazement. With the birth of his son Hoyt Williams’s game plan kicks into gear. Time to rid the world of the filth so that the final showdown of good and evil can begin. Hoyt Williams: I now bestow onto you my young son the weight of the world. Your uncle Jesus carried the burden of sin, I have carried the burden of conquest, and your mother brings balance and the burden of judgment, while you my boy will ride a pale horse and bring death to those who don’t comprehend the words of God and live in sin. But you also bring redemption to those who seek it and heaven to those who deserve it. The small child giggles as he looks up at his babbling father. They are in the nursery surrounded by other fresh born children. None of them as perfect as Hoyt’s baby. Hoyt Williams: Oh it’s no joke you have a lot of work ahead of you. I mean I have to do the conquest stuff first but you really need to get ready. This is no time to laugh we need to get you right and ready. First things first I’m going to destroy Ignatius Lisieux. The baby again giggles loudly as he grabs his fathers thumb with his tiny hand. Hoyt Williams: You my friend are right the guy is a joke. You see I’m in the Hall of Fame which means I’m better then everybody else on the roster. Iggy is also in the Hall of Fame which drastically lower the real estate and the validity of the Hall of Fame. I mean listen little man everybody in PRIME and the wrestling world understand I the best the game has to offer. How many other guys are sent to earth from God? The baby looks confused. Hoyt Williams: I’ll help you out. Just me. ONE. That’s it I’m the alpha and omega and everybody else is just crap. I have been the PRIME world champion and I honestly couldn’t tell you who holds that distinction now. Why? Because nobody cares since I’ve lost the title cheated by poor ventilation in the air ducks. The baby again laughs. Hoyt Williams: That’s not funny somebody was messing with the air and I was suffering from acute affixation. Tchu cheated and he knows it but that’s not important because I’ve moved on. I have a few unfinished activates I need to finish before I return to claim my crown as King of Kings and the Pontiff of PRIME. People have lost respect for me because I’ve been taking it easy worrying about you little man. But now you are here and seem alright so I the King of All Media can return my attention to amateurs like Olsig and anybody else who claims victory’s over a distracted son of God. The baby begins to cry loudly. Hoyt Williams: What’s wrong? Did the mention of less then talented wrestlers upset you? You and me are a lot alike I hate feeble minded nothings as well. Hey kid look at that fit baby in the crib next to you. That child is going to hell. The baby cries even louder. Hoyt Williams: You need to get some tough skin because a lot of people are going to hell. Anybody who eats fried chicken or shops at Whole Foods is certainly going to hell. Fat people will never get into heaven even if they are babies. That child’s mother should have been eating less what a sin it is to see baby fat. You on the other hard are perfect. Hoyt Duke Williams, Jr. begins weeping louder. Hoyt Williams: I know its hard being perfect but that’s the price you paying being the grandson of God. All your life people will be jealous of your perfect body. In many ways its curse but I think you’ll do fine. Duke Williams the earth grandfather of the baby comes rushing into the nursery. He is wearing the hospital issue green scrubs and looks a bit out of place in a sea of crying babies. Duke Williams: He shit himself look out. Hoyt Williams: What? My baby would never shit himself there must be some mistake. Duke Williams: Maybe Ignatius sent in an operative and planted a shit in this baby’s diaper. Hoyt thinks that idea over a bit even though his father was being sarcastic. Hoyt Williams: Impossible! I have been with this baby for the last three hours. Nobody has been in hear accept a few other parents and the rest of these imperfect babies. Duke Williams: Well then he must have shit himself. Hoyt Williams: Never my baby would never take an Olsig on himself. I know maybe I was drugged and they snuck the Tony Rolo in and placed it in his diaper. That would be the must logical solution. Old man Duke rolls his eyes as he changes the soiled diaper of his grandson as his son rants on. Duke Williams: How does it feel knowing PRIME is leaving Playboy TV? Hoyt Williams: This just shows you the incompetence of management. For the last two years I have been filling out the little cards and submitting them into the suggestion box back stage begging management to get us off that pornographic network of smut and sin. Finally they found somebody who can read and have obviously taken my suggestion to heart and we are finally moving. Duke Williams: Cable TV you guys are going to have a bigger audience then ever before. Hoyt Williams: Nobody saw me lose those BS match on a worthless network. From here on out on national TV I will be undefeated. I’m going to preach and profile my way to the heavens. I’m going to show the world why Hoyt Williams is the best thing since Jesus Christ and much better looking and a hell of a better wrestler. I’m going to strut over the sinners and simile while doing it. AMEENNNNNNNNNn A nurse comes rushing in. Nurse Ratchet: Can you please keep it down this is a nursery. Hoyt Williams: What the hell are you yelling at me for that ugly baby over there has been screaming this whole time. Somebody needs to tell him to shut up first, he’s babbling like an Illustrious Asslover promo at least I make sense. Oh and lets not mention this little bald booger over here who’s been drooling like Boda at a preschool full of boys this whole time. If this kid doesn’t grow up stupid then I ain’t the son of God. Nurse Ratchet: Sir PLEASE! Hoyt Williams: Please Dear! I would like to file a complaint somebody broke in here and placed a Tchu in my sons diaper. Nurse Ratchet: A Tchu? Hoyt Williams: A south side slider, a nugget of brown, a Tookie Williams, Mississippi Mud, a Clinton, a Rolo, a Olsig. Nurse Ratchet: An Olsig? Hoyt Williams: POOP somebody snuck a shit in my sons diaper. Somebody my guess is Ignatius Lisieux broke in here, drugged me, and placed a turd in my boys backside. Who do I talk to about this. Nurse Ratchet: A shrink would be a good start. Hoyt Williams: Well then you get Mr. Shrink in here so I can have a word with him. You know you are going to hell don’t you? Nurse Ratchet: Sir, who are you and what are you doing in here. Hoyt Williams: Who am I? My brother only died for your sins, my father only created you, and I’m here to save you and you don’t know who I am. Lady don’t you read Pro-wrestling illustrated? Watch Playboy TV? Visit www.primetimecentral.com? Nurse Ratchet: No why would I watch Playboy TV or read some magazine I’ve never heard of. I’m calling security. Hoyt Williams: Thank you, that’s all I wanted was you to get security so we can get to the bottom…pardon the pun…of this poop pilferer. Let them know I want to press charges. Duke is cracking up as he is watching this odd conversation unfold in front of him. Duke Williams: Why don’t you go and check on your wife while I fill out this ahhh police report for you. I’ll stand guard. Hoyt Williams: Fine, but make sure nobody gets in here. I can’t wait for next weeks Revolution when I present the world my baby and the NEW FATHER HOYT WILLIAMS! Duke Williams: Amen son, Amen. Hoyt exits the room just before a slew of security comes pouring in. Duke explains what’s going in to them and they all have a good laugh. [=w=]
