Real Life - Part 1

12 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/11/06 The wonderful thing about the human animal is that it varies in so many different ways yet at the same time, it can be so frighteningly similar. For one thing about us all remains true, we all yearn for safety and comfortable familiarity. No matter how adventurous we may be, we always look for a place we can call home with people around us that can protect and nurture us, no matter how old we are. Whether it be older generations, close friends or the families that we choose to create for ourselves, every single one of us craves that security of love and understanding. It is that familiarity and stability that gives us the freedom to spread our wings but the ties to keep us from wandering down the wrong path. Anybody who thinks they do not need that is fooling themselves because nobody can walk the road of life alone, no matter who they may think they are. If we lose that which holds us together, our families and loved ones or our grip on what we need weakens, then we are in danger of self destruction. Welcome to the world of one such person. ---- “I was thinking to myself the other day…” The casually toned voice of Paul Cain can be heard musing to himself as the scene slowly raises up from black. He pauses as he flicks his lighter and slowly draws it close to the cigarette. It’s not uncommon these days for him to be sitting in darkness and this occasion is no different. The brief glow of the flame accompanies the moonlight that glares through the window behind Cain, bathing the back of his head but keeping his facial features under cover. He tucks his lighter away into his jacket pocket and takes a slow drag on his newest accessory. Tilting his head back, the smoke billows from his nose and mouth as he folds his arms across his chest. “…How the most overused question in the English language is, why? Questions, questions, questions, that’s all people have. Why did you do this, why did you do that? Whatever happened to the term ‘because I felt like it?’. I shaved my head because I felt like it, I gave the Spic my wedding ring because I felt like it and I’m stalking Tony Gamble because I feel like it. There doesn’t have to be some deep psychobabble as to why Paul Cain is feeling a bit different these days, could it not be possible that I might have just fancied a change? Maybe I can’t handle the pressure of being a poster boy and a moral guide to young Americans?” Suddenly there is a very strange mumbling noise that can be heard from around Cain’s vicinity. He doesn’t move for a moment and can be seen to pretend that it doesn’t exist. “It’s not easy being a pillar of the community and someone that everyone looks up to. There’s a lot of responsibility and you can’t do a thing without being judged…Maybe I just woke up one morning and decided that wasn’t the life for me…?” There it goes again, the same muffled whimpering and groaning that Cain tried so hard to be blissfully unaware of. He takes a long drag of his cigarette and then looks down to between his legs that are seated wide apart. The thick smoke passes swiftly from his lips as he looks sharply back up to the camera. “I mean, it doesn’t have to be anything to do with the fact that the individual who has spent two years of his life trying to take my family away from me, is bound and gagged on the floor here and he is serving as a very comfortable seat.” The camera view pans slowly out to reveal what is a small bedroom, suitable for only one person. It has everything in it that a young man could possibly want, a comfortable bed, a computer and access to all the sport you could wish for on a decent sized TV. Of course the standard pornographic material was all hidden away and the alcohol safely tucked away for a rainy day. A typically average room in an average home on an average street in an average middle class town. It hardly radiated potential for a home wrecker yet appearances, according to Paul Cain at least, were clearly deceptive. Of course the broken lamp and tipped over furniture were a slight give away that something may be amiss here. Now the individual in question can be clearly seen wriggling on the floor of his own bedroom, face down on his stomach. His hands are crudely tied behind his back with rough looking rope and the mouth is gagged by cloth tied at the back of his head. Cain suddenly shoots him a look as the whimpering and groaning gets louder from the youngish male. He puts one finger to his lips and then draws his thumb to the throat, implying quite clearly what will happen if the victim continues. The murmuring dies down to an almost silence as Cain paces around the room, staring around it with his mind clearly somewhere far away. He then stops with his arms folded and his back to his captive. “You despise me because I have what you want…But you know what, I despise you for trying! How do you feel when you look in the mirror and realise that you are trying to take a wife from her husband and a daughter from her father, do you feel fucking justified?! This isn’t some childish tantrum about how you lost your favourite toy, this is fucking real life and you’re screwing with people’s futures! So…you know what?” Cain now moved around to face a man who was no physical sight to behold; he was slightly overweight with a look that screamed potential forty year old virgin. For an individual who had bravely spent his time chasing a female that could not be caught, this courage had suddenly vanished at the sight of Paul Cain. He had never truly believed that they would ever meet yet now he witnessed first hand, the monster that he had created through his blind pursuit. Cain’s boot was now directly in front of him as he watched Paul go down to one knee so he could see for himself the fear in this man’s eyes. He then stands back up to his full height and launches his boot into the face of his rival, slamming it hard into his nose. The blood instantly bursts out as he falls onto his back with the force of the blow, causing a small smile to appear on Cain’s face. “it’s time to show you and the world what hatred and contempt can do to a man.” Scene fades to black
