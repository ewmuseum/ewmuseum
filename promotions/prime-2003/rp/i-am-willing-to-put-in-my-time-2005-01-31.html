I am willing to put in my time...

31 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/31/05 January 30, 2005: Arrival, this place looks familiar. Darkness is slowing surpassing the amount of light; people are trying to shelter themselves from the cold air. With the darkness starting to surround the area, street lights are starting to glow, giving minimal light to cover the now empty streets of Pittsburgh, PA. Pittsburgh usually quite a party town has been silenced ever since the loss of the Steelers to those damn Patriots. Everyday life has changed in the surrounding areas of Pittsburgh. Many faces are no longer glowing with glee but are wallowing in sorrow, things like this happen, just so happens this time it’s to the citizens of Pittsburgh. The emptiness of the streets gives a lonely sad feeling, until a single silhouette appears. A man slowly moving closer and closer to the light is shown; from the spray of light coming down from the street lamp we can see that it is Peter Vetra. Peter Vetra is dressed in all black with a black trench coat on. The look upon his face is one of desolation; Vetra looks up at the night sky and sees that the moon is completely darkened, not a single star showing through. He continues to walk down the street, until he reaches upon the dead end. Now Peter Vetra stands at the dead end all alone, in front of an abandon house which seems to have seen its better days many years before. “This home is rather familiar to me. No I never lived here but it appears the same way as I look inside, worn. This house looks like it has been around for many centuries and I, myself have only been around for almost two decades and a half. I have probably only a quarter of the age of this house, but yet as it appears to me is the way I feel inside.” Peter opens the fallen gate that leads to the home, with a slight creek and gate falls off the hinge and Peter throws the gate aside and continues his walk. Peter reaches the steps which were once painted white but it is starting to flake from the wood. Peter takes one last gaze up at the house and then takes a seat on the stairs only hoping that they don’t fall through on him, with his additional 190 pounds. “Tomorrow marks my twenty-fifth year on this plant. Exactly twenty five years ago my mother gave birth to a young Peter Vetra. Who was to know that on that day this young boy would be condemned to a life of hell and abandonment? That day my parents made a choice, their decision one that would change the way I was raised, and the way I would act in the world, once I was going to set sail on my own. That day my parents made a choice, they made a choice that not only affected them but it also affected their young newborn, Peter Vetra. Placing me up for adoption was something they felt was needed, but all it I did was give me a life of being an outcast. A person no one wanted to affiliate with, who wants to hang out with someone that does not know their past?” Peter places his face into his hands, showing the amount of sorrow he is currently feeling. You hear the young man or as he might soon be called old man start to cry. From beneath his hands the following words are muttered, “Strong men also cry… Strong men also cry.” Journal Entry 4.0: Tomorrow is doom day. Most people are looking forward to that day each year, the day that they were “born on”. Not me this day is just a time for me to realize that twenty-five years ago my parents decided they made the wrong choice by having me, placing me in the hands of the foster system. I am product of the United States Foster Parents system and look what has happened to me? A loner, a man without a home, I have no one to call a friend, last week that was proven. The only person in my life that has ever taken the time to try and show me some compassion, time and time again I have spoken about this person, Oscar Hernandez. I have given you many different occasions to try and not only prove yourself but also prove your friendship. Oscar Hernandez, you my friend have failed at doing such a thing. I can only give so many chances before I have to end it, last week my so called friend was your last chance, you blew my only chance at gold here in PRIME, now I am thinking about ending your only career you ever had a chance for here in PRIME. The time to forget about that Mirror Man has come, we may meet paths again, who is to know, but my energy and thoughts have to be focused on something or someone else. This week I placed once again in the ring, this time without my counterpart holding me back, I will again prove that I am the better wrestler of the two. Big Poppa, a man whose name is as generic as the brand Wal-Mart puts out as their soda, is about to face reality. Last week after have his first match here in PRME, against Tyler Lopez, a man by no standards is any better then me, he is stuck in the ring against me. Quite a tough streak of bad luck, if you ask me, first you lose to Tyler Lopez, a man whose talent just barely outweighs the talent of a dog, and now being stuck in the ring with me. Being a newcomer in a federation usually is a something that sucks, but what sucks even more is losing your first two matches in a new federation. Big Poppa I am sorry but that is a reality you are about face, and actual live next week. January 31, 2005: Yeah, it’s my birthday….notice the sarcasm The sun is starting to reach the horizon, this creating what many people like to refer as dusk. The time somewhere near 5:00 am, Pittsburgh is slowly starting to get moving, with every couple of minutes there are more and more cars on the road. Off to the sides there are many stores and coffee shops starting to open. Inside one of these small shops sits a single man all alone with the server. Both men with their attention fixed on the television, the channel turned to ESPN. “With less then one week before the Super Bowl is about to air, how do you think the Pittsburgh Steelers are feeling right now?” Stuart Scott says as they head to a segment about how the Steelers missed the Superbowl by one game this year. The man behind the counter turns his attention from the television towards his customer and offers him more coffee. The man sitting at the counter is Peter Vetra, who is still dressed in the clothing from last night, he seems like he has yet to get a wink of sleep. He accepts and looks at the man working the counter. “Tough break, I thought they had it this year, well at least there is always next year.” “Yeah, I know but I was hoping this year was their year. I guess we will just have to try again next year. That of course is if no one else leaves the team.” The person behind the counter sighs and pours the next cup of coffee for Peter. “Yeah, but there is one good thing to come from this,” Peter tries to convince the Steelers fan. “What is that?” “Well think about it Big Ben got more experience then any other rookie ever has. So next year he will be that much better.” Peter is easily speaking his mind about the subject and sipping his coffee at the same time. Without even realizing it he has reached the end of his cup. “Well I have to be going.” Peter gets up and pulls a couple of bucks out of his pocket and drops it onto the table. “Thank you, sir. I have to agree with what you say about Big Ben, but we will have to wait till next year to find out.” Peter gets up from the counter and heads for the door still wearing the black trench coat. Journal Entry 4.1: The day has arrived… Most men by the age of 25 have achieved some sort of success, not me. I guess I am under estimating myself, I have been in PRIME for a while, and I have had two shots at the Alias Title. But if you sit down and think about it I even failed at both of those attempts. Shots I have been given, hell I think I have even earned most of them, but yet once again I am placed lower on the list wrestlers. I am now stuck on rookie duty where I am facing the newcomers of the federation. That is ok, I can not be kept here to long I will prove myself once again. Twenty Five and yet to achieve anything will not be true for long, because by the end of this day next year I will have held Gold, what type of gold I do not know. I am not picky anymore I will take Tag Team, 5-Star and even Alias gold, but one thing I do know I will have held some sort of gold. Big Poppa it all starts with you. A man unknown to me but soon to be my next victim, is someone I feel pity for and soon he will feel my wrath. Many times in my life I have felt alone, many times I have tried to turn to a friend and most of those times there was no one there to help me. I have grown as a human being and learned that friendship is only a bonus in life while the things you really need are what you need to worry about. This week I am worrying about Big Poppa, and my wrestling career. I have reached a time in my life where I have to start worrying about more then just the relationships I have built but I have to set my eyes on the prize and take it home. This week it has to start with Big Poppa. Will I succeed no one knows until the moment it happens, but I do have a strong feeling about this week, so be aware PRIME I am coming up in this federation, it just takes time, time I am willing to put in.
