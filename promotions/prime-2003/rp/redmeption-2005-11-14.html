Redmeption

14 November 2005

Date: 11/14/05 Redemption Time I have spent many hours watching the tapes; I have spent my time wisely learning what I did wrong and how to fix the problem. But all I hear in my head and reiterate through my brain are Richard’s words, “I think Vetra really hurt himself. Let him go, Brandon!” For a couple of weeks my work in PRIME has been almost substantial, not by the terms of my contract but more of my own personal terms. I need to become active, I need to become the Peter Vetra I know I am, I need to be a champion. The man that sidelined me for days upon days, forcing me not to wrestle for three straight weeks, not only missing two ReVolutions but also missing King of Kings the tournament I should have won. Now I come back and expect to reap my revenge on the man who sought to have me out of the sport for the rest of my life, but I find he is gone. Long gone, Brandon Youngblood, no longer a champion, no longer a competitor in PRIME wrestling. What am I to do? I have built up anger, anger I want to take out on the son of a bitch that hurt me. But I can’t. He is no longer around. So I will have to go after the next best thing. Killean Sirrajin The name sends chills up my spin, he entered PRIME shortly after I did has achieved much more success then I have, and yet is still unbeaten against me. It is time for a change, the streak is about to end and it is going to end this week on ReVolution 81. It all started earlier this year, in a simple singles match. I thought I had him beat, I should have had him beat but for some reason I didn’t have it in me. Maybe I wasn’t prepared, I thought I was. I guess I was wrong. Then it happened again within the last month Jewel in the Crown Tournament 2005, a chance for the winner to shine, become someone. I thought it was my chance, I was wrong. First round I lose to Killean, it shatters my hopes and dreams I continue on trying to get somewhere in PRIME. I am faced against Brandon Youngblood, Five Star Champion. Maybe if I win I can get another shot, this time for the title. Well I had high aspirations but this time I overlooked the competition, and he hurt me. Redemption, it is time for Peter Vetra to get his redemption, not only on Killean Sirrajin but all of PRIME. Oh Imperium how I have let you down. Imperium a group of elitist, the greatest stable in the industry today, currently the home to Hoyt Williams the Universal, God’s and PTC Extreme Champion. Then we have Jesse Jamester the newest member who is now the Five Star Champion. Then we have me, Peter Vetra the man who had not done a thing since he lost he Alias Championship. Hell that belt no longer exists. I guess it’s time for a change.
