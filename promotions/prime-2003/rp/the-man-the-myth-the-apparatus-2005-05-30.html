The Man, The Myth, The Apparatus

30 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 05/30/2005 Up. Close up on the face of the Apparatus. He looks well rested. We are zoomed in on him, his eyes are the foreground of our scene. Lopez's hair looks freshly washed, so it is less wet than usual, and a shade lighter. It hangs down in fine, smooth, blades. His bangs hang slightly over his eyes as he stares at us. His face is smooth beyond what seems natural. He quirks an eyebrow at us. The camera obeys his unspoken order, Lopez commands obedience like few others. He is sitting on the sundeck of his hotel room, he is wearing shorts and no shirt, obviously getting some sun. 'Jackson Darilek makes the oh so astute statement that I am a tool.' Lopez laughs, and raises his glass and nods. Cheers! He is obviously humored. 'Very nice Jackson. Incidentally did you also know that the sky is blue, water is wet, space is big, and Hawaii is a state in the American Union? I have never made any bones about who I am, I have never said I am unbeatable, but since you wanna talk about my being a tool...lets.' Lopez seems almost in too a good a mood, too relaxed. There is something wrong here. This is not the norm for Tyler Lopez. 'I have never depending on anyone for anything in my professional career. Have I used people as stepping stools. Yes. Will I continue to step on people on my way to the top. Absolutely. Do I give a damn that you or everyone in every arena around the world consider me to be a tool... HELL NO!' Lopez drinks, it looks like lemonade. Below you can hear the sounds of the beach. Lopez's entire torso is visible here, his feet are up, but they extend off camera. It appears that he has, Dear God, sun tan oil on. 'Is this a sign of being soft you think? If you honestly believe that I lose my desire, then you are a fucking fool. If you honestly think that after all these years, all the losses I have had to recover from, that I am somehow soft? Then you are going to get hurt badly here, and you really are one of the world's foremost buffoons. Jackson I'm not sure that you are ready for PRIMEtime, but I'll bring you into PRIMEtime... head first! He shrugs now. At least he is frank about his failures and shortcomings. It's refreshing somewhat for a bad guy to admit these things. 'And yet Darilek, here I am, where I have always been. With Money. With Power. With Respect. I have always recovered. There is not a wrestler in PRIME history who has had a tougher road than me. I have marched a road of bones, over, and through obstacles that destroyed lesser men and even one woman... Hello Jessica. I say that my greatest triumph and the most obvious indicator that I am, indeed, one of the greatest ever will be when I destroy you and show you why I am indeed a tool.' Now his voice starts to lose the cool, and he begins to growl slightly, lower, more threatening. 'And as one of the greatest, kid, winning the Alias title at the biggest PRIME pay per view event is not a matter of merely being in the right place at the right time. Getting the grudging respect around PRIME that EARNS you a shot at a prize in one of the rare biggest shows IS NOT something that is stumbled into. Okay? You simpering little toad. To get one of those shots, is something that I earned before you were even thought of in PRIME. Face it Jackson. I have all the makings of a great Champion. I have overcome THE longest odds, recovered from the most devastation, I have taken the most beatings, and now I am standing before you. If you, so called, good guys were man enough to give me the credit I deserve you'd see that I am the owner of a heart every bit as strong and brave as your heroes. I have as much grit and desire as anyone you admire.' The lemonade is gone. He drops the glass at his side with a clink, he folds his arms and leans back, shaking his head. He still hasn't moved his legs. 'I go soft? No, I have had amazingly bad luck. But you never heard me complain, I only talk about these things when pressed. When I lose, I get up, find those responsible and I pound them into dust. You certainly wouldn't catch me dead tapping out and then saying I got screwed over. You wouldn't catch me blaming someone for exploiting a weakness I showed. I take responsibility for my losses. Talk about all my losses, like all my other opponents have ever done. Find me striking out in the big game in little league. Find me sobbing in a darkened locker room after losing thinking I was finished. Find all of those things. All of those setbacks. And you will know why I am now so strong. Because I have gone through hell to be where I am now. Then compare my career to yours, and tell me who is stronger, tell me who's demonstrated greater will, tell me who is a Champion and who has a title shot he all but admitted he didn't earn. Ultra heel Lopez versus babyface Darilek? Snort. Lopez is ranting now. ---“I must admit, I really have no idea what the Management really thinks about when they go to sleep every night. All of a sudden, I get thrust into a Intense Title Glass House Match against a man who lives in a Trashcan, Aimz, Nick Brocken, Nova, and someone who no one knows who he is. Slight pause. “Or something.” Either Lopez’s tolerance is really bad, or he just doesn't give a damn. “Hey, by the way, nice performance last week Brocken. You know, you and I haven’t really had much interaction with each other, and if we did, well who cares. You see, there are some things that I have gotten sick and tired of. Every thing I see on television nowadays involves the same damn statements, and frankly, enough is enough.” Lopez takes another drink. “Fortunately for me Brocken, I didn’t expect that out of you. However, I did expect something. This is not a place for hugs. Consider for a moment what you are getting yourself into. I still don’t anticipate anything but victory. Just like I have a hard time keeping a straight face during your comments. You will never main event unless I allow you to, and don’t you ever fucking forget that. I hope you are enjoying my scraps, independent pilgrim of mine.' Lopez rolls his eyes. 'Nova you want to know the difference between us? The REAL difference? You think it’s your supposed talent, but it’s not. The real difference is the inspiration of hatred among our fellow wrestlers. I count on you being very, very successful some day, but never as successful as me. And do you know why? Because I can create hatred with the drop of the hat, and that is what you need to do around here to stay in the spotlight. Sure, it’s easy to get hotheads like The Dark Age upset. It takes no effort. You want to talk about puppets? These are the real puppets. Men like me can pull their strings whenever I want. I've driven opponents insane enough to take steps that actually got that goofballs booed.' He smirks. He is remembering past accomplishments, his actions on one Jeremy Allen. 'Guys like Trashcan Man hate me way more than anyone else in PRIME. And do you know why? Because when I’m doing what I do, he can look into the crowd, and despite all the booing...he’ll see some smiles here and there, and it eats him alive. When you look into the crowd, how many kids are wearing my t-shirt? All sorts of ‘em. I do it with a smile, I do it with wit, I do it with Pizzazz, and I do it with all the women that these guys jack off to thinking of hanging on my arm. When people see me why in the hell do they want to have honor? I have one-liners and nice hair, and THAT is cool enough for the masses. They boo me out of obligation, but they aspire to be me with every breath they take.' Lopez shifts; he looks a tad more intense. 'But come on now, this match, the Glass House Match, this is something people could get excited about. This is something at least more than 100 people want to see. I mean one of the best in PRIME today, Tyler Lopez, against some other losers. Who doesn't want to see 'the Man, The Myth, The Apparatus' in the main event. The fact is, I am looking forward to this week.' Lopez ponders this thought. “But really Aimz, all I want to say is good luck. And may the best man win.” Lopez tips his glass of lemonade. “To us. To us.' Tyler Lopez is somewhat anxious about his match on Revolution. For the second time in less than a month Lopez headlines Revolution. He begins talking faster now, his voice is sharper. 'Saying something is routine is not to say something is effortless. I am not underestimating any of you, I know exactly what you are capable of, I have watched, studied, and prepared, and I can make the conclusion that you are not nearly as special as you think you are. See I think you'll all agree, the underestimated opponent has a great advantage, one which Jackson Darilek has had previously and will not again. Let me tell you something...' Now Lopez leans forward, the relaxed look is gone. Tyler can make himself menacing while getting sun. 'Just because I don't think you are that good Jackson, doesn't mean I have underestimated you. It might just mean, shock, you aren't that fucking good.' Lopez relents and leans back. He sighs, and looks out over the horizon at something and stifles a giggle. 'Try as you might Jackson, you can't take away what I am, what I have been through, and who I am. I have sacrificed more than you can imagine, paid a higher price than you would have the courage to. If anyone has ever earned their spot, I have earned mine. If anyone ever was in a position they didn't deserve to be in, it's you. These are facts, straight facts. This is not me tainting the truth like you, so honorably, do when talking about my lack of 'quality'. I'm getting another Lemonade, want one?' Lopez clears his throat, and the camera looks back at him, standing in the doorway. He shakes his head in a tsk tsk type of way. Heh, I'm even more honest than you.' Lopez enters the room, and slides the glass door shut. Fade.
