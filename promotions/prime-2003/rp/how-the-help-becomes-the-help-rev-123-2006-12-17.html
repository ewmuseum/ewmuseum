How the Help becomes the Help (Rev 123)

17 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/17/06 This has a direct connection with Part 1 that I posted earlier. I made a couple of revisions to it, so if you want to make any sense of this one then you will need to read that one. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Allow your eyes to accept a story drenched in immoral righteousness. The tale of how eight souls vandalized innocence just to sanctify their souls before fading into the abyss. The torment's genesis begins in the year of 1989: A happy family consisting of Chris, Matthew, Brandon, and Rachel Smith, along with Christian Daniels, living under the guidance of Jacqueline and Robert Smith. A nurse mother and a real estate father at the top of their professions with kids dreaming of being a psychologist, doctor, lawyer, and a detective respectively, as for Christian...He was doing alright for himself. The perception was that this family was living out the American dream to its apex… …Until a seemingly “injured” patient at Raleigh Community Hospital took Jacqueline’s life with one bullet on March 11th. The family was devastated. Robert Smith battled with alcohol to the point of unemployment and homelessness. The family responsibilities of raising four siblings fell to twenty year old Christian Daniels. He would also have to raise Jason Hardy, Chris’s best friend, whose parents died in a car explosion on the same day. To solve his problems, Christian joined an upstart biker group by the name of Death. He always believed that there was a conspiracy surrounding the deaths, and vowed to avenge all three of them one day… He would have to wait a long time… Fast forward thirteen years later to 2002: Chris Smith, on the verge of independent wrestling legend, Matthew Smith, high level corporate executive, Christian Daniels, the top dog in the motorcycle group Death, Jason Hardy, working for the CIA, Brandon Smith, technological genius, Rachel Smith, professional assassin, along with Chris’s childhood crush turned wife Mallory Daniels, and stock guru turned wrestling instructor Michael Blaze living inside of a massive mansion in Raleigh, North Carolina. The group was united and looking toward what the future presented. The amount of information the group had about that fateful night in 1989 was limited, but enough to justify motivation. This much was fact. Robert Smith, unbeknownst to anybody in his family at the time, was a massive gambler in debt one hundred grand to the North Carolina mafia. When they came to collect the money, Robbie only had 1/10th of it. The result was that he had to sell his wife and best friends, Jim and Jennifer Hardy, up the river for the remaining 90 grand. There was the belief that three mafia associates: Mike Connors, Lance Robinson, and Marcus Galloway made sure that things went smoothly, sending out their best hitman, Renshaw Prescott, to play the injured patient that whacked Jacqueline, and to wire the Hardy’s car for maximum explosion. The Smith household decided to draw first blood. On May 29th, 2003, Chris went to see his father after Robert announced he was remarrying to a woman named Courtney LaMont. This infuriated the household as Robert had promised never to marry again after Jacqueline’s death. Chris killed both of them with a shotgun. The first detective to get there was someone who went by the name of Mike Connors, Courtney’s ex-husband. The second to arrive was Matthew Smith…An eerie coincidence. Considering that this was obviously murder, Mike Connors, along with his two brothers who went by the names of Marcus Galloway and Lance Robinson, requested the case. Their focus quickly shifted to two people, Chris Smith and Christian Daniels. Their father, veteran detective Daniel Wilson, assisted in a supervisor’s role over the case. Later that evening, after getting a reliable tip from one of Jason’s contacts regarding Renshaw’s involvement, Chris physically beat the hitman to death. The detectives never looked into it. Due to lack of legitimate leads, the case went officially cold to the Raleigh Police Department in September of 2003. It never went cold to the trio of brothers who could think of nothing but justice. When they weren’t working on current cases, the double murder was their obsession. They carried on, each day getting closer and closer to the prize. In June of 2004, they received some assistance from an enigma who had an agenda to destroy the Smith household. This man was able to get inside of the Smith’s home while the group was vacationing and plant bugging devices. These devices gave the detectives enough incriminating evidence to get the Smith’s lynched by the courts. On November 23rd, 2004 in Denver, Colorado, the detectives showed up in the front row of one of Chris’s wrestling shows. After his first loss in the federation, due to politics, Chris saw the three detectives, but more importantly he saw that they were carrying weapons. They weren’t going to go through jurisprudence. They were going to kill him. Chris decided that he had only one option…Run, and run like hell. He managed to get back to Raleigh and had to continue his option of running like hell by fleeing the country. Quickly, a bag of belongings was gathered, and Chris fled in the middle of the night to his private jet. His destination would be Shanghai, China. The only problem was that nobody else in the house knew about this, and when they awoke on November 24th, all they found was an encoded note. Each person in the house felt some sort of guilt for not being able to resolve the case faster. To shatter the fragile state of the remaining household, Mallory Daniels would die in a car crash on December 19th. The house made sure that the information of Mallory's death did not reach outside of the state, as this would be the perfect trap for the detectives to bring Chris into custody, since he would want to return if he found out. Before the end of the year, the man assisting the detectives had found Chris's location, but chose not to relay this information to the trio of detectives. He wanted to burn Chris when there was no adequate time to prepare another escape from the country. He also had a theory that sooner rather than later, Chris would return to the United States. While the Smith household struggled to get back on their feet emotionally, Chris was growing into his new role as a lawyer's assistant under the alias of Horatio Austin. He also found a new love named Kirsten Davis, and married her on May 1st, 2005 to further encode his true identity. There was just one problem with this...Their wedding picture ended up in the Shanghai Star, a newspaper that Christian received. Christian blew up and managed to get Chris's cellular number from a reluctant Michael Blaze, who Chris had called twice after meeting Kirsten in February. Christian demanded that Chris return to the United States because if he did not, soon he and the new bride would end up dead. Nine days later, the newlyweds took flight back to the States with the help of a mysterious pilot. One that only Christian Daniels knew the logic behind...Daniel Wilson. Why would the detectives own father assist a wanted fugitive in returning to the country, which ensured his death just 11 months later? In 1991, Daniel Wilson was a part of the force investigating mafia like activity in North Carolina. He ran across a group named Death. Daniel attempted to raid the gang a day before an official raid was scheduled because of personal disdain. Unfortunately for Daniel, a youngen from the group by the name of Christian Daniels, out on a beer run, caught Daniel from behind and proceeded to beat him to a pulp. To save the life of his family, Daniel agreed to one favor that Christian could call in whenever he desired. Ensuring Chris’s safe return to the States was that favor. When Chris returned, he faced the death of Mallory, and the sense of paranoia began to kick in once again. He vowed to avenge her death, and to bring closure to his mother’s case that was over 16 years old at this point. Chris also made the decision to return to wrestling, knowing full well the repercussions that it would bring. On the other side of the coin, Mike Connors got suspended for a year in February of 2005 due to his bouts with alcohol, and his obsession over the case involving Chris. Marcus and Lance resigned from the department in protest and started up their own private detective agency. Marcus and Lance were too swamped to give a real damn about the Smith case at the moment, considering it was more Mike’s deal than theirs. The Smith household would get a free pass to resume their normal lives until February 1st of 2006 when Mike managed to talk Marcus and Lance into one more round of battle, the final round. A week later, the mysterious man and woman decided to make their presence felt upon Chris specifically. During a PPV in Phoenix, Arizona, the woman attempted to run him over in the parking lot, only for the man known as Icon to jump out of the way at the very last instance. Chris could not make the woman because she was wearing a Ghostface mask. Once Christian got wind of this, his suspects were limited and in one triumvirate: Connors, Galloway, and Robinson. The game was definitely back on. A subsequent robbery of Connors’s office on February 10th gave the group all the information they needed to burn the detectives in terms of misconduct. There was only one question that they still could not answer, was the trio involved in the death of Jacqueline Smith? Over the next couple of months, Christian managed to clear the detectives from the car incident. Instead, a far greater horror began to grow in his mind. He feared that one of their own was working with the detectives. The suspect list was Brandon, Matthew, or Jason. On June 1st of 2006, the mysterious man and woman decided to reveal themselves. When only Chris and Christian were in the mansion, the woman entered the house much to the horror of Chris. When Christian came out from his room to leave for a meeting, he saw the woman and instantly knew who the other person was. That man knocked Christian unconscious from behind with a pipe and revealed himself to Chris…As Jason Hardy. The woman was Chris’s believed to be dead wife, Mallory Daniels. The two would explain that jealousy of Chris since the preteen years drove them to forge an alliance, and fake Mallory’s death by staging the car crash. Chris was given an ultimatum, leave the country by June 10th or face his own systematic death. Later that evening Mallory and Jason would turn against the detectives, thus making themselves an independent party in the battle. Christian did not believe that Jason and Mallory would stick around long enough to give Chris the systematic death that Jason preached. Christian drew the conclusion that they wanted a fight to the death, and he was more than willing to oblige, as were the detectives. On June 8th, the three factions consisted of Jason and Mallory, the Smith family and Michael Blaze, and the three detectives with the help of Lance’s wife, Rachel Robinson. Before the battle started, Jason had planted two bombs at different ends of the meeting place with a sixty minute timer on each. Inside of Chris’s childhood home, the three parties fought to the death. The first casualty was Marcus Galloway by Christian. The second was Lance Robinson, shot in the back of the head by his wife. To the shock of everybody there, she was actually Rachel Smith, Chris’s sister. The third death was Mike Connors, shot by Chris twice in the chest. The fourth and fifth deaths would be Jason Hardy and Mallory Daniels, who died when the house exploded. Everybody else got out at just the last sliver before becoming dust. That is how the story was supposed to end…But not so fast...There was one final twist. On June 10th, 2006, sitting on a beach in an unknown part of the world were Chris, Michael, Christian, Brandon, Matthew, Rachel…And the duo of Jason and Mallory. It would be revealed on a video tape that from day 1 everybody was coordinated, and the entire thing was a setup to completely obliterate the world of all their problems. The plan was thought up and put into action on 10/31/02. Between then and June of 2006, the body count between the eight of them is believed to of been around 70 to 75. All of it just to rectify one person’s death. They were supposed to fade into oblivion after this, to get new identities, new faces, and walk amongst the criminally innocent… That was the case until December 16th, 2006 when Devin Shakur revealed the target that he was designated to assassinate, Tony Gamble. With the scent of potential kill in the air, the demons inside the seven present at the beach house could not be contained any longer. In their minds they were protecting one of the innocent. Devin was just a pawn to Anderson and Wilkerson, but to the Smith family he was one that they could not turn their back on. He needed their help in order to right his life. Christian knew first hand that while Devin was smart, his conscience prevented him from being ruthless and handling all of this by his lonesome. The Smith group lost their conscience and heart so long ago that they forgot the date. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- December 17th, 2006 00:00 EST (Devin's time) Two Hours after Part 1 Ends The fog of rage has been lifted from the house, and the group has firmly taken the reigns of control once again. It is unlike this group to ever falter in any aspect, but dragging up the past is never a pleasant experience for any soul to endure. They didn’t want theirs seeping into the stigmata of public winds for long. Everybody from the Smith clan, sans Rachel, is sitting in the living room. Christian looks at a computer screen that Brandon is pointing at. Michael Blaze flips through the USA Today. Matt stares down at some pieces of paper. Mallory is making drinks in the kitchen. Finally, and most importantly, Chris and Jason are talking over by the telephone. A look of apprehension fills Chris’s face. He would much rather take the burden upon his own shoulders than to leave it in the hands of one man without a shield around him. “Do you really think the kid should go through with this?” Jason looks over toward Chris with his usual confidence, the antithesis of his state of mind two hours ago. “What do you mean exactly?” “I do not doubt that Devin can handle himself in a wrestling environment…But everybody in this room knows how that is. We’ve all been in the ring at some point in our lifetime. The media loves to get on any minute story and suck the life out of it. If they even find one emotional aspect of his life out, they are going to drive him back to the asylum.” “So you think that time he did in the joint did nothing for him?” “That only made him tougher, but it didn’t abolish the emotional issues. It's not going to be pretty for us if we send the kid out to his death.” Christian has been keeping his ears perked on the conversation the entire time and now looks in the pair's direction. “What do you suggest that we do then? A & W aren’t going to let Dev get out of their sight many more times. I’m surprised they didn’t catch on the previous two times. I wouldn’t be surprised if tomorrow they have somebody tailing his ass everywhere he goes now that he has their information. If we give him the necessities now, then he should be good to go.” Chris still does not believe that it can be as simple as they are saying. He is not, and never will be, the master manipulator of planning. “How do you know they aren’t just going to off him the minute that he becomes accessible?” “That’s exactly what they probably are going to do!” Christian exclaims “But it ain’t going to be for a while. If they want him to make it clean, no trace back to them, and no trace back to him, then they are going to give the boy some time. If they know his emotional state like they are claiming to, by proxy of knowing what happened to his father, they have to expect time. That is our advantage and their disadvantage.” Brandon diverts his attention from the computer to join in on the discussion. “Because look at it this way, and Jason, you can back me on this, I really doubt that they anticipate our involvement. The emotional maelstrom is already in Devin’s head, and with their annexation, they aren’t really expecting him to have rational thought. They are expecting a puppet on string with a robot brain to conform to whatever they say." Jason nods in agreement “I’ll bet you 100 out the pocket they don’t have any type of contingency plan in case of emergency. A & W think that there is no chance anybody can bring them down. Fuckers have been doing backhand deals for years and years, and only one entity called their bluff…Us. We became dominant throughout the state of North Carolina without opposition after that. Now that we are gone, who is to stop them? They don’t think we are going to put our asses out there just to take them down once and for all with this much heat on our asses. ” Christian folds his massive arms across his chest in a defensive manner. He’s trying to bring this chat to a close now, as he is not the kind to get in debate of the verbal variety. “They think us coming back into the fold doesn’t make rational logic, but they don’t know our logic system. The law can’t fucking touch us, otherwise they would. If you put us behind Devin Shakur’s meticulousness, yeah, he’s good for that, then ain’t nothing going to happen to any of us. I got faith in the kid's mind when it comes to work.” Chris bends over and puts both of his elbows on his knees. Jason smacks him across the back, still unable to comprehend the amount of regret Chris has. “You didn’t worry about shit when we were working our plan while you were in China.” “That was because A) I knew you guys weren’t going to fuck it up, and B) we got to meet up on some weekends. We always had eyes everywhere, but we don’t have any in this situation. We’re all sane and Devin flat out ain’t." Christian grits his teeth. If he had his way, Jason and he would do all the planning for this project, and just make sure Chris carried it out. “Aite, aite, aite…What if we get someone to go dark and keep an eye on Devin wherever he goes, someone that we trust. Would that make you feel better?” "It would I suppose." “And to calm another one of your fears, I’ll get Rachel on it.” A genuine smile graces the face of Chris for the first time all day, and he finally concedes his worries. “Get Devin on the horn and let’s get this over with.” Jason looks over at the caller ID, hits the speaker button, and starts to dial the numbers without looking A few seconds transpire before Devin picks up on the other end. Christian takes his cup of wine from Mallory who goes around handing out the drinks. “Devin, we got the information that you should need to keep your head above water.” “Alright good, everything over there alright, I didn’t expect to be hung up on in such a fashion.” Christian looks over at Jason “Yeah, we just didn’t think that would be the name you spat out. You get what I mean?” “Right, right, I remembered it, just didn’t think that it affected Jason as well.” Matt looks up from his documents and over at Christian “You want me to go first?” Christian shrugs “Might as well.” “Alright Devin, first question that you probably have is who the Braintrust are? Well, they are the corporation known as Anderson and Wilkerson. You might remember them as the company that went down a while back-“ Devin interrupts “What in the hell are they doing going after me?” “Well” Matt starts off. He really doesn’t want to tell him this next part “That is our fault. The reason that they went under is that they tried to make a move on our territory back in 2005, threatened us, and well let’s just say that we don’t take kindly to people coming onto our turf.” There is a fifteen second pause on the other end before a response can be mustered. Everybody starts to feel a little bit of guilt for making Devin take this task on. “So you are telling me that this is all because of you guys? You guys are the reason that I’m getting targeted?” “We’re only part of the reason.” Matt explains “They do have a tie in to their target, Anthony Gambillini, and more specifically Michael DeAngelis. Those two groups had a spat a while back, with DeAngelis managing to put the screws to them, and they never forgot. My theory is that they want you to kill Gambillini before their guy can do it…” “Ah” Christian says, with a smile on his face “Now we get to the fun part.” “So they have a guy that is going to try and kill Gamble also?” “Well” Matt says “Yes and no. You see-“ Christian holds up his left hand “I got this one from here. A while back they asked us to do the job on Gamble, knock him without any questions asked. Our original plan was to send someone that looked like Jason to get some basic information about Gamble, relay it back to us, and then we would send Jason in himself for the kill. We even had the look-alike show up in front of DeAngelis, but we pulled back on that idea ultimately.” “Now” Christian says, clearing his throat “We’ve still been lingering around with them, screwing with their heads a bit. They know how fucking good we are. What we plan to do is this. We are going to send in you to be their guy to kill Mister Gambillini.” “I don’t follow” Devin replies, the sound of his head being scratched is heard over the speaker. “As far as you are concerned Devin…” Christian says, a flare for the dramatics coming over him, “You work for three organizations: Anderson and Wilkerson, the DeAngelis Family…And us…Until we eliminate Anderson and Wilkerson, then you work for two. Then we eliminate the DeAngelis people, and then you work for us exclusively. At that point, once we have complete control over the situation, then we can actually bring you down here and get some real good strategy talk going. You get what I’m selling?” “I believe so” Devin says, like the good little line soldier that he is. “I take orders from them, send them back to you, and abide by your commands only.” “Damn straight. Alright, do we have anymore information to give him about Anderson and Wilkerson before we move on?” Christian asks Matt. “Um…Hmmm…Well you know about the company they run, electric, front obviously, and they usually have three people do their bidding. I have confirmed that they have a head boss but I can’t get anything other than that. The name he uses is an alias, and all that bullshit. Unless you fuck up really bad, I would not count on ever seeing him in person…” “Question, what happens when I don’t knock Gambillini off? Won’t A & W just kill me?” Christian takes a swig on his wine and cracks a massive smile. “By the time they realize you aren’t going to knock him off, we got something real special planned for them, and more special for the boss man. This shit is going to be a stroke of genius if I do say so myself. ” “Got it” “Aite, that’s one down, one to go. Matt, give him the DeAngelis part and how there is a connection to Gambillini.” “Alrighty, alrighty” Matt shuffles two papers to the back of the pile “The man who runs the “Family” goes by the name of Michael DeAngelis. His father ran the business before him. Anthony’s father was the right hand man for him. The father is dead, and DeAngelis’s considered Gambillini a son moreso than Michael himself. So he wanted Gambillini to inherit the legacy of the Family. Michael doesn’t like it, wants to kill Gambillini, he can't get his own people to do it, so we reinvigorate our relationship with DeAngelis via you." “I have some information from the Braintrust on Gambillini. Can we compare?” “Lemme have it” “He doesn’t have a set residence. He lives in hotels where PRIME travels” “Half right, half wrong, he does have a residence, a large apartment, but does travel around with the PRIME organization and stays in nearby hotels. We can get you the address on the apartment if you want.” “I might need it later. Alright, and he has a woman that is his associate, someone by the name of Violet Rayne.” “That part is true” Jason interrupts “Show me that picture again. I swear to God I know her from somewhere but I can’t place it. I think I almost got it narrowed though.” Matt hands Jason the picture. He studies it with intent and waves his hand at Matt telling him to continue on. “They have been spending a lot of time together recently. Paul Cain, a wrestler slash businessman, cut her face some time ago. The reason was because Paul and Violet had some kind of working relationship where they oversaw a tag team in PRIME consisting of Anthony, and someone by the name of Damien Khan. According to this, Violet and Gamble had a secret motive for control and eventually broke free from Mr. Cain” “SHIT” Jason shouts out loud, breaking his quiet studying “Secret, she is or was working for the CIA. We worked on something for a couple weeks, and man she's good at what she does. Violet Rayne isn’t her name though. It’s um…da, da, da, da, I should know this… Jason goes into the tank for a good thirty five seconds before reaching an answer. "CYNTHIA…Berkman, hang on let me check my computer. That sounds right though” He takes the computer from the table in front of him, types in the name Cynthia Berkman into a search engine and comes up with a hit. The woman in the picture looks exactly like Violet Rayne. “Yeah, she’s CIA all the way. The fuck could she be doing with Gambillini…Christian, how international was DeAngelis back in the day?” Christian thinks for a second “Pretty damn, I’d say. People knew who he was in a lot of places. Right now, the family is pretty much living on the existence on Gamble. What in the hell does she want with DeAngelis now? Fuck what the dad did-“ Michael Blaze decides to interject “It’s gotta be personal, really personal. People don’t act that ballsy unless they’ve got some serious head issues.” “Ok, Devin” Jason says, now leaning over to the phone “Do not ever, EVER, make personal contact with this woman. I ain’t joking, don’t. If you see her in the street, go the other way. She’s bad fucking news.” “Stay away from Rayne, got it, anything else regarding them I should know?” “What you are going to do with them is this. You are going to stall. If they set a plan for you, ignore it, they just want to get Gamble dead so they can have vindication. I don’t want that. I want them to suffer for a little while, get nervous, and then when you are about to make the hit on their behalf…” Christian snaps his fingers “That’s when we knock DeAngelis off and eliminate him from the equation.” “Then you will be working strictly on our behalf...” Jason jumps back in “At said point, you make the real move for Gambillini. We’re talking about a long project for you here, but trust me the benefits once the smoke has cleared will be beyond your wildest dreams, Devin.” “You know that I only have two underlining problems in my life.” Christian nods “Finding out who killed Kimberly and becoming at peace with your inner self. I can put a couple of people on the first one once we move along in the process, think of it as an incentive, and when we find out who it is, we’ll give you first dibs. As far as the second one: You’ve got the money, the lifestyle, all you need after that is something that you love enjoying and somebody to enjoy it with. Then it’s all gravy from there.” “Alright, so I’ll report back every couple of weeks to give an update on progress?” “Actually, that’s a little something we are going to throw in free of charge. Con man over here is a bit worried about not having eyes on your situation, since well we can’t exactly make public appearances anymore. So we’re going to have the only freelance person on this that we can completely trust, Rachel. You remember her right, our sister?” “Will I ever see her, or she going to stay hidden the whole time?” "I sure as hell hope you don’t ever see her. You don’t need to be paranoid and look over your shoulder every second with her looming. She’s the best that I know. If someone is following you, she’ll take care of them. If she suspects something is wrong, she’ll make sure to right it. Otherwise, if you ever get in a jam you can always give us a ring. We ain’t got anything better to do.” “Thanks guys.” “Keep that smirk on your face as long as you want. When we’re in control, shit always goes down how we plan.” “I’m going to sign off. We’ll keep in touch.” Christian stands up and begins to walk over toward the kitchen. “Oh, and one more thing…Make sure you don’t make any friends or close connections in that PRIME place.” Devin chuckles “That shouldn’t be hard, night guys.” Click …If only they knew about an artist by the name of Sun…
