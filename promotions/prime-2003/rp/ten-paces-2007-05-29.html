Date: 5/29/07 He was scatterbrained. Vision was blurry. The pungent stench of blood mixed with both dirt and sweat penetrated his nasal passages. The pain sent a shock that traveled through the entirety of his leg; it wouldn’t move. He tried to crawl away, but the more he did so, the more futile it became. The panting of his breath matched the steps of the oncoming attacker as he flicked the sweat outside of that all-too-familiar jet black beard. “You knew it would come to this.” His tone was unchanging. Fervent words reverberated through his head. He was at his mercy and it appeared he would have zero. “You knew that I couldn’t be escaped, you son of a bitch. You have everything I want and right here, right now, Mr. Silver, I’m taking it all back.” The gun cocked in his hand. “Peace out.” And then… nothing. ~~~~~ The dust devils were in full force as he surveyed the endless stretch of arid land. His eyes scanned for any sign of life, but other than the roaring winds and sand crunching underneath his good Armani shoes, Mr. Silver was by his lonesome. Looking down at his cell case, he picked up his phone to see the time. He had a very important business meeting with some of the next PRIME event’s sponsors later that afternoon and he wasn’t about to waste either time or money. Once he put his phone back in its holster, he scanned the vicinity once more for anything. The phone call said there was an urgent, life-altering matter to be dealt with, but he assumed it was Burnie McCoy bothering him for an autograph for one of his “seven kids” who just so happen to coincidentally share their father’s name. He ran a hand over his neat, slicked-back hair and released a sigh from his mouth as he began to lightly kick away at dirt clods beside him. “Goddamn it, Burnie, if this is you again…” Mr. Silver didn’t even get to finish the thought as he folded his arms and shook his head. Just what WAS he doing here, anyhow? He was Mister Fucking Silver, the man with all the power and the influence. He didn’t get dragged to some desolate shithole off the side of the highway, he had bodies taken here… at least, he made much mention of bodies being here. Turning his heel and walking back to his black Dodge Charger, he silently fumed at all the dirt he kicked up onto his brand-new detailed car to make this unnecessary trip to nowhere. Another day, he would figure out just who the hell left that message, but until that day came, there were profits to be made and lives to be ruined by the hand of the great Mr. Silver. The car sprung to life when he hit the button for the door to unlock. As he rested a finger on the door handle, the sounds of his customized “SILVER PWNS YOU” ringtone was literal music to his ears. He even took a moment to listen to the beat he placed together in the studio because he was just that egotistical. For those not in the know, its sound was reminiscent with that of the chorus to 50 Cent’s “Wanksta.” You said it’s a fight you itchin’ But all I hear is your bitchin’ You say that you got my numba But y’all can’t handle my thunda You comin’ round my block But y’all run when I’m settin’ up shop Cuz I’m tha one… one… one… one… Wiki-wiki-fresh. Aw, shit, reppin tha W-A, nigga! Man, that was a fun afternoon, he thought to himself before he finally picked up the phone. “Speak to me.” “Do NOT move from that spot.” Before he could even come up with a quip for the joker on the other line, the line went dead. He flipped his phone shut with his chin and gritted his teeth when something caught his eye way out in the distance. Almost out of nowhere or like a scene in a movie where danger was afoot, a massive cloud of dirt was speeding towards his particular direction. Mr. Silver caught sight of sunbeams bouncing off the silver roof of the oncoming Dodge Tacoma. Thinking quickly, he slapped on his sunglasses to admire the nice four-wheel ride. “Oh, yeah, that ain’t Burnie… maybe Slash… I pay him enough...” The pickup came to a dead stop a mere ten feet or so away from his ride. Mr. Silver tried to take note of the driver of the vehicle, but tinted windows were making it nigh impossible. All he figured was two things. First off, this person had some SERIOUS gumption to make him come to the middle of a field. Secondly, he had good taste in cars. Maybe after he heard this guy out and beat his ass for wasting his time, he would get the Fife Posse to swipe the truck for him. Cautiously approaching the truck, Mr. Silver gazed at the driver and folded his arms. The window of the Dodge rolled down, but he didn’t look him in the eye just yet. His gaze was reserved for the sky in that “I look like I know all, but I’m going to stare away from you in case you look at me funny” kind of way. “You, sir, have some brass balls,” he began as folded his arms and paced around the truck. “Summoning me away from my business is a privilege reserved for my subordinates, those who offer me massive-ass cash bribes, and those who are privy to getting their faces smashed in. Which one are y…?” In an incredibly rare feat that ranked up there with lightning striking seven times in the same place and Jason Alexander getting a successful vehicle after Seinfeld, the words were taken right from Mr. Silver’s mouth. The driver of the Dodge looked down at the brazen man in the business suit and chuckled to himself. “They don’t come bigger than mine, SIR,” The last word was accentuated in a mocking tone. “From what I understand, yours shriveled up and fell off the moment you told a certain Hawaiian ring rat, ‘I do.’” Ballsy as the comment was, Mr. Silver was still taken aback by the mysterious stranger that was in the driver’s seat, both figuratively and literally. “You… you’re… you’re… familiar, somehow.” The sound of palm striking forehead echoed through the vehicle before he gazed at Mr. Silver. “You could say that. I know all about you and what you’ve turned into… you sicken me.” Mr. Silver backed a step when the door suddenly swung open with great force. The stranger, adorning a black Slayer t-shirt, blue jeans, and causal work boots, hopped out of the vehicle and came face-to-face with one of PRIME’s most notorious figures. With each step, the nearly-frightened Mr. Silver couldn’t do anything other than take notice of the well-developed figure advancing upon him. Both me come to a dead stop and he sizes him up. “Vince McMahon? VINCE McMAHON?! Out of ALL the people you could model yourself after, you chose the head of a Sports Entertainment shithole that produces NOTHING but metrosexual tattoo-sporting roidheads whose greatest claim to fame are raising their hands halfway above their head and taking a shit in a bitch’s bag.” He walks paces around Father PRIME and finally ends the circle by spitting at the ground by his high-quality shoes. “I can’t believe… I’ve… you’ve… I just… this is completely fucking unbelievable. Where did I go wrong with you, sunshine?” Stammering over his own words, this unabashed, mealy-mouthed, shaggy-haired beast pulls at his own hair frantically before snapping back at the cause of his current angst. “You’ve become nothing more than a fucking puppet. After all the times I’ve preached to you about valuing your own independence as a human being and as a superior being, you’ve allowed The Man to jam his hand up your narrow ass and work your limbs the way he sees fit. I bet if I were to wipe the shit off your dick right now, I could get enough for a stool sample. The test would tell me that there’s a 99.99999-recurring-percent chance that you are a Grade-A pussy.” Before any sort of sound resembling a response could be uttered from Mr. Silver’s mouth, his verbal attacker sustained his assault. “And you… you’re running around with these “FU” people. Well, you know what I say to that? Fuck fuck you, MISTER SILVER Fuck Fuck You. YOU and you alone should be the star of this show. You don’t need Nova, Snow, Gamble, or anybody else riding your cock. You’re hiding behind thugs, you’re ducking competition like Asa Fountain, you’re letting yourself become a slave to the whims of others… We… I… you toiled for fifteen years before you saw any kind of a reward and now? Look what you’ve degenerated into.” “Now, look he…” “NO! YOU look here.” He walked over to the truck and procured a silver box from the back of the pick-up truck. Patting it down like his baby, he drops it at his feet and goes back to eyeing Father PRIME. “I’m sick of this. I can’t live my life anymore knowing that you exist… that the future of this business rests upon the caricature of what I once stood for. You’ve become nothing more than the domesticated animal of a horrible machine known as Sports Entertainment. Do you not understand this? “No.” “I’M YOU.” “You can’t be me… for one; I’m not some ugly-looking fucktard riding in the back of a pick-up truck. Two, you don’t look like you’ve achieved one IOTA the success I have. Three, you look like you’ve tossed more salad than Emeril Lagassi. And, four…” SMACK! Mr. Silver’s hand collided with Sonny’s, sending the apparent doppelganger tumbling backwards in the dirt. A hand wipes across his face, checking for blood, but when none is found a boisterous laugh flies through the open stretch of desert. “You even hit like a pussy. If you’re done proving my point further, you fake, open the box.” After wiping his hand on his good sleeve, Mr. Silver approaches the box cautiously with the possibility of this nut setting up some sort of contrived booby trap. “What manner of bullshittery is this? What kind of an idiot do you take me for, you imposter… er, SONNY?” Sonny picks himself up and gestures to the case with his hand. “You know what it is. Your sixteenth birthday… the last one we got to share with Dad. Remember? That was the day you told him you were ready to start a career in professional wrestling.” Gazing upon the shiny steel briefcase with piqued curiosity, Mr. Silver holds it in his hand while listening to the rest of Sonny’s story. “Big Richard told you that the business would consist of everyday duels in which not every man survives. You’ve got to want it more than anybody else out there and you’ve got to be willing to show the world your pride. Because if you allow yourself to slip, get injured, or even show signs of falling off, there’s a hundred other young guns that are willing to take your place. He said most importantly, the best thing for you to do was to give it your all and be willing to risk your life in this profession if you want to succeed.” Flicking open the dual switches in the briefcase, Mr. Silver pops it open. What he finds is something that does ring a bell. “These… these… Dad’s twin revolvers.” He turns to Sonny and screams belligerently. “These were a gift from him, you fucking douchebag! These have been passed down from generation to generation! How’d YOU end up with MY possession, you fake-ass Mr. Silver wannabe? ANSWER THE CHAIRMAN, NOW!” “Oh, you don’t get it yet, do you? I’m you. I’m tired of hearing about all your exploits and besmirching what was once a proud and renowned name. For years now, I’ve sat back and watched you make a mockery of yourself… fWo. ACW. LoC. Now PRIME. I’m you from the Squared Circle era, the place that respected wrestling at its finest. I’m a cocky son of a bitch, but goddamn it, I love this sport. You treat it as a prop now and it sickens me. You, Mister Poserbitch, have offended my honor by continuing with this Chairman charade that’s made you go softer than Star Jones’ husband’s penis. So right here and now, we’re gonna end it.” Sonny nudges him aside long enough to hold both guns in his hands. He teases pointing them at Mr. Silver, who tries to cover himself up while his past chuckles. “Look at you. To steal a phrase, you’re yellow. You’ve even become a coward. I had zero fear of anybody that tried to take me on in the Squared Circle. You, you just go around and let grunts do your dirty work for you and skate by.” “I’ll have you know, sir, that I am a man held in the highest regard in Sports Entertainment today! You can sit there all you want with your big, bad threats but what you’re seeing before you now is what you WISH you could be. I’ve won titles everywhere, even in countries you can’t pronounce.” “Yeah, I know… the Intense title. That’s real hardcore.” He finishes the statement with a roll of the eyes. “You defended it, like, once in three months. But enough pussyfooting around this. I called you out here because it would be so easy to just blow you away right now with these, but I have something you’ve forgotten all about; a little something called honor. I didn’t have much, but I have just enough to give somebody like you a fighting chance.” He tosses the gun into Mr. Silver’s hand as he starts to fiddle with the chamber on his. “Each of these has enough to take out the other. In olden times, as you may know, there were duels where people would be willing to put their lives on the line if it meant restoring such a thing as honor. This is one of those times.” The words seem to go in one of Mr. Silver’s ears and out the other as he continues spinning the chamber to make sure the bullets are working. “What, are you mad, you fuck? Dueling is illegal in the United States! I won’t consent to th…” CLICK “Then your life is forfeit.” “Wuh… buh…” “You’re me in some ways. We both know that next to wrestling, we’ve had some experience with firearms. I’ll kill you well before you get the chance to fire that gun. Last chance.” Seeing as he currently had a loaded gun aimed at his head, the duel seemed more than fair to him. “Fine, you NRA-lover. You can have your one duel. When I do away with you and this madness, I’ll be sure to bury you faster than I did Carlito.” “We’ll see.” ~~~~~ Back to back. The breathing of the two combatants matched up perfectly as the sands started whirling around them like a scene you would see in one of those old Westerns. A bead of sweat trickles down Mr. Silver’s forehead. The rapid-fire heartbeat that Sonny feels in his throat almost burns. GULP from Mr. Silver. SIGH from Sonny. Sonny turned his head just enough to make sure that Mr. Silver wasn’t trying any funny business behind him. “All right. We both count ten paces and start walking forward. When the countdown concludes, turn and fire. Last person standing makes it out of this desert. You got me?” “Yes.” “Good. Let’s say we end this.” And with that, the duelists began taking what could potentially be their final steps, miles from civilization. In perfect unison, the two men started up the count. “ONE…” “TWO…” “THREE…” “FOUR…” “FIVE…” Each step was aligned with the breathing patterns of Mr. Silver as he thought about everything that rested on this. In his own deluded mind, he’d hit the top of the professional wrestling hierarchy with the fans glued to his every word and enough money to provide for him and his family, estranged as they were. “SIX…” In the same moment, thoughts raced through Sonny’s mind. He was alone on this Earth and that was just the way he liked it. When he shot Mr. Silver and left him to die in this land seemingly untouched by the hands of man, he would head back to civilization knowing he can stop a desolate future from happening. “SEVEN…” Just three more numbers and he could do away with his past once and for all, making him food for buzzard or gophers or whatever the fuck eats people out in the desert. “EIGHT…” Sonny licked his chops. The second shot found its mark perfectly, piercing through the skull of Silver and sending him back to the sand with a dull thud and a small cloud of the Earth kicking up beneath the spot his body landed. With a sigh, the remaining Silver dropped the pistol at his side and nudged the other with his foot to ensure the task he set out to do was finished. “It was nice knowing you. He turns to leave, but stops himself for a moment to look back at the newly deceased. “My life only goes up from here.” Satisfied with his handiwork, he picked both guns up again and calmly placed them back in the case before locking it shut. He couldn’t believe it, but it was finally over. His future was no longer a bleak cesspool in which it seemed to be an absolute certainty that the name of the Silvers would be desecrated by some ranting imposter. What seemed like nothing but a few steps was a walk of fame for Silver as he opened the door of his vehicle and sit inside, briefcase slung into the back seat. He finished off the Aquafina he started prior to this daunting task and smiled. The sun was overhead and it was a perfect day to start anew.
