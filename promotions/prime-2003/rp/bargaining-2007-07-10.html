Bargaining

10 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10th, 2007 Bargaining He gazes up at the stars above him and can’t break his stare. Slowly, he begins to count the stars. One Two Three In the back of his mind, he continues to count them. Every now and then he loses his number, and he has to begin all over. Yet, he doesn’t care; not a single ounce of his body cares. Not anymore. His bloodshot eyes continue to glaze over the stars, desperate to find out how many stars there are in the universe. Deep in his heart, he believes that if he figures out how many stars there are that he can unlock a mystery of some sort. His insides gnaw at him, ready to explode, but he just stands there calm and patient. It could take him an eternity, but sooner or later he would finish counting. Eight hundred and forty three Eight hundred and forty four Eight hundred and forty five Hours pass by; he doesn’t even realize it. His thoughts are silent, as silent as the middle of the desert on an empty night, except for the numbers that keep forming in his mind. Slowly, he counts, making sure that he doesn’t miss one. He can’t afford to miss one. If he misses one, if he misses any of them, they could be the difference between life and death. Every one of them has to count, has to mean something, or else it’s for naught. If it ends up being for naught, then he’s failed in a lifetime of failures. He can’t fail again. Not now. Not when everyone is looking at him to stand up and be strong. Yet, he asks himself, how can you be strong when you feel so weak? One thousand, four hundred and seventy seven One thousand four hundred and seventy eight One thousand four hundred and seventy nine Strength is overrated. Everyone looks to people to be strong in a time of need. Why? Why do we have to be strong when our natural, basic human instinct is to be in shock, to be weak? As human beings, if we go against the grain it’s not bravery or strength. It’s a flaw. He knows this even though time and time again he has had to be strong. Even now, in this moment of time, he tries his best to be strong. It’s what he knows. However, what he knows has failed him so many times that he has to go into the unknown; basic human instinct. For so long he’s been flawed, and for the first time he finally realizes it. He’s not a God, no matter how many people look at him like he is. He’s human. He’s basic. He’s mortal. Two thousand six hundred and eighty Two thousand six hundred and eighty one Two thousand six hundred and eighty two In reality, astronomers admit that they don’t know how many stars there are out there. In the Milky Way alone there are well over 400 billion stars. Some have speculated that there are 200 billion stars in our galaxy and 3 thousand million stars in the universe. NASA claims that there are zillions of uncountable stars. Even in the back of his mind he knows that he’ll never know how many stars are out there. Yet, he believes that if he continues to count, somewhere along the way he’ll find something to save him, something to rescue him from the pain that he feels. If he’s able to find out how many stars there are in the universe, then maybe, just possibly, he can offer them to whoever it took to bring her back. To bring his daughter back. Until then, he counts, praying that the stars give him answer; an answer that nothing else in the world can give him. Until then, he counts, in pain. ---- “How does a mere mortal conquer someone who is eternal?” he asks out loud rhetorically. Silence. Perfect. He sits there and stews, the words replaying over and over again in his mind. Even though his tone was rhetorical, he still desperately needs an answer. The past weeks had been hellacious upon him. There was once a time that he had been the king of the underground world, but now he is nothing more than a mere man forced to look at himself in the mirror and ask him the same question he has asked himself for days now. How does a mere mortal conquer someone who is eternal? For so long, he has seen himself as something more than man. He had been deemed untouchable as his hands dripped in the blood of the world he was happily destroying. With everything he had done in his life that was his greatest achievement; to be treated as something more than a man, a king, or a ruler. He had been thought of as a God. If that was the case though, how had a God been scared into submission? Truth be told, he was hiding in the darkness, cowering from that which he had created. A monster. “Daniel,” a voice cracks through his consciousness. His dark black eyes dart around the room, trying to find the source of the noise. Slowly, his eyes land upon the culprit and the hatred grows in his heart. The face of the man is unidentifiable to him. Instead, all he can see is the man he had created many years ago, the man who was determined him down at this very moment. He pushes himself away from his desk, the wheels on the bottom of the chair moving without much effort, and he pulls his body out of the chair. Now, every set of eyes in the room are upon him, and that feeling of being a God rushes back to his blood. It was not something you created, it was what you inherited. The ability to command the gaze of millions, the gift to strike fear in the hearts of men, and the talent to move mountains with your words was not something that came from the outside, but instead internally. Those traits created men, not the other way around. “You speak when not spoken to?” Daniel asks the man, knowing that the fear has seized the man’s vocal cords. The sweat runs off the brow of the ill spoken man as he tries to find an escape, but is paralyzed to find one doesn’t exist. This was his world and they were all just living in it. Even as he had retreated into hiding after killing the daughter of the man he had crafted, he still made sure that those who surrounded him realize who they were dealing with. This, the situation he finds himself in, was a mere setback. Soon, the tides would rise and he would find himself standing tall amongst the men of the world. He was almost certain of it. Almost. “I apolo--,” the man begins to speak, hoping to calm the anger from the beast standing in front of him. Yet, it was far too little too late. Daniel’s left hand strikes out and wraps itself around the man’s throat. Daniel was far from being the strongest man in the world. However, seven years travelling the world and studying with the finest martial arts teachers had taught him the ways to kill a man with barely even flinching. Slowly, his fingers began to tighten and the man tries his best to breathe. His lungs fill with emptiness though. With each passing second, he can fee a burning sensation in his chest. Desperately, he tries to fight out of it, but feels his body weaken before hearing a snap. It was his trachea collapsing from the immense pressure being put on it. Seconds later, the larynx shatters into millions of pieces. Daniel’s dark black eyes, eyes reminiscent of Satan, stare ahead refusing to break the lock it has on the man’s eyes that he slowly kills. He can see it in the man’s eyes, the fleeting seconds of the man’s soul before it was extinguished. The man’s body grows limp in his hand as he lets go without warning and doesn’t even flinch at the lifeless body colliding at the ground below him. With one fluid motion, he turns around and begins to walk back towards his desk. His eyes flash around the room at the remaining four people who have just watched the sickening display unfold before their very eyes. Secretly, he smiles knowing that he hasn’t lost his audience. Not yet at least. “Anybody else want to interrupt my thoughts?” Daniel asks and is met with silence. As he sits down in the chair, he finally smiles. Then, the thought of the beast he created, the beast determined to hunt him down, creeps into his mind ruining the happiness that he was feeling. With the happiness being drained out of him, an idea strikes him. The only way to destroy the beast you’ve created is to remind the beast who is in charge. He would have to find Craig and he would have to kill him. If only things were that easy. ---- In the back of his mind he can feel the stinging pain from his eyes. For hours they’ve been fixated upon the stars above, not even taking a mere second to blink. If he was to do that he would lose valuable time. Time was against him. He was the furthest thing from immortality. Across the horizon he can see a faint glare. Silently he curses to himself knowing that it wouldn’t be long before he would run out of time. The sky above him is vast offering millions of stars for his taking. They’re within his reach yet he can’t grasp them in his hands. As the sun continues to rise, he knows he has failed yet again. “The stars won’t solve your problems for you Craig. The stars won’t bring her back,” a voice breaks through his concentration, causing him to lose count. His head drops, knowing the words to be right. Even with that thought he can’t bear to look at her, to realize that his failure is a reality. For now, he wants to live in a fantasy because reality has taken so much from him. He can feel her gaze upon the back of his head, almost pleading with him through her eyes to turn around and look at her. She knows though what he is going through; having lost so much starts to push you on the brink of insanity. “You don’t know that. You can’t know that,” he mutters back at her, speaking softly that in his own mind he thinks that the words can only reach his ears. Yet, she hears him without a single problem. Her tongue grazes over her lips knowing that she can’t save him any longer. He had told her that he had to take care of the problems in his life, the problems that he had allowed to fester. She knew that when he said those words that there was no coming back from them. Deep in her conscious she knew that two nights ago Craig had walked out of the hotel with the single intent and purpose to kill. It was in his eyes; eyes that were meant to kill. Just from the way he walked, Katie could sense what he was going to do that evening. And he did indeed kill. She asked him about it and he was silent. It scared her; not so much because Craig was a talkative person, but more so from his demeanor. The sweat rolls off of his forehead when he walks in and she knows from that very moment that something is wrong. There is a look about him, a sickly look, that all is not right in Craig’s world. Slowly, the frayed edges are starting to unravel before his very eyes. “You want to bring her back and that’s commendable--,” she begins to say, but her words are stopped as he turns around and looks at her. The words remain stuck in her throat like gum she had swallowed. His eyes are fierce yet dull at the same time. They send a chill up her spine as this is no longer the same man that she had been told was destined for great things. This was a mere shell of that man; a shell that couldn’t be cracked. “She’s come back from the dead before. How?” he asks her, referencing the fact that his daughter should’ve been dead all along; that she shouldn’t be seventeen. She stares at him, wishing she had the answers that would satisfy him. Yet, she doesn’t no matter how hard she tries. Katie bites her bottom lip, knowing that there is so much uncertainty here, realizing that there is so much that both Craig and she can’t possibly understand in terms of how the world works. “I don’t know. I know that she’s not coming back, Craig. That’s something that eventually you’re going to have to accept,” she tells him, knowing that her words are just going to wound him even more. “Never! I can’t accept that. I refuse to accept that,” he tells her before he walks past her and enters into his hotel room. With each step he can feel his body tire, wanting and needing sleep. “If I could, I would bring her back Craig. Trust me, I would,” she yells after him. He instantly stops in his tracks and looks back at her, and sees the sincerity in her eyes. “Then tell me, what do I have to do? Because, trust me, I’ll do it. I’ll do all of it. Just tell me how I can do it,” he pleads with her. She looks at him, wondering if he truly believes that she’s withholding information from him. She just shakes her head as she’s unable to form the words in her mind. Katie can’t fathom the idea that he’d think there was something she could do and was just refusing to do it. “Please, I need something. I need to be able to save my daughter. For so long, I tried to find my memories, find out who I was. In that time, I’ve learned who I was. Who I once was, I no longer am. I’ve found out about the things I’ve done and am sickened to see the person I was. Sarah, she made me see that I had a reason to live, that I wasn’t as bad as I thought I was. Without her, I’m that person all over again. I’m nothing more than a beast in the form of a human. Without her, I’m nothing,” he tells her, each one of his words breaking her heart. He then turns from her, but before he can even take a step, she beings to speak. “If she changed you that much, then you should let her guide you in your heart. You think that you can only be different because she’s around? Just think of the damage you’re doing to her by reverting to the man that sickens you. Fourteen people. How many more have to die before you realize that her essence is still inside of you? You crave to be so different yet you’re still doing the same damn things you hate about yourself,” she tells him, hoping that her words finally give some wisdom to Craig. He doesn’t even turn to look at her, instead walking towards the restroom and stepping inside of it before slamming the door shut. He then looks at himself in the mirror and realizes that she is right. He’s a shell of the man he’s supposed to be; resolute, strong, and tough. Instead, he’s a man who wallows in his own misery and pretends he doesn’t know how to be a better man. He swallows, feeling the saliva coursing down his throat, and turns on the faucet. He splashes some water on his face and the fire in his eyes return. He’s far from being perfect knowing that he has demons still in his life. In his heart, he can feel the pain, and he desperately needs to dull the pain. The pain that is Sarah. ---- “Check this shit out, man,” he yells out. The boy sitting next to him glances over; his eyes telling the world that he doesn’t give a shit about whatever it is that his boy is trying to show him. As he looks at it, his reservations are found to be true, and he looks away without so much as muttering a world. “That’s great Mario,” he responds before focusing whatever the hell it is that he’s so interested in. Mario looks at his boy and just blows him off. He looks down the street and sees a man walking up to him. He smiles as he recognizes the man as one of his faithful customers. He lays back on the bench, taking in the scenic view, and takes in a deep breath. Now he knows that today is going to be one of those good days. The night was fresh and already people were looking to score from him. As he glances over again, he sees the man approaching him. “Mario, man, tell me you got some good shit,” the man asks Mario who just gives him a look that answers the man’s question. Mario reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out a small bag. He then glances around to make sure no one is watching him and once satisfied he places it in the man’s hand. Seconds later, Mario is handed a few bills that he just sticks into his pocket. The man though doesn’t walk away, instead looking at Mario as if he is crazy or something. “You don’t give change or something?” the man asks Mario who just looks up at him with a smile on his face. Mario’s friend just sits next to him, ready to burst out laughing from the question that the man just asked him. Mario glances over at his friend and elbows him in the back as he realizes that he’s seconds away from laughing at this man. Mario had learned more than anything that people don’t like to have their pride attacked like that and had suffered a few bullets to the chest before he figured that one out. “Look man, I told you from the very beginning that I don’t offer change. I told you the second time that I don’t offer change. This is your fifth time coming to see me and the fact that I repeated myself twice should tell you plenty. I’ve got a good memory obviously and since you can’t bring me the exact change, the remaining amount will go towards your next buy. Good?” Mario asks the man, his business sense quickly taking over to calm the man down. The man looks around, upset at this bit of news, and then he relaxes. He knows that Mario had told him this before and just nods his head. “Sounds good then. I’ll see you soon man,” the man says before walking away. Mario smiles as he looks up at the fading sun and wonders what else the night will bring him. His friend next to him just continues to toil away, wasting the night to the nothingness that he’s engaged himself into. Mario then looks around and enjoys the view when he sees a man walking his way. The man has a hat on his head and his head is to the ground. He was taken aback by the stature of the guy as he was a rather big dude. Slowly, he walks up to Mario, his eyes unable to stare him in the face. Mario looks back at his friend who is completely oblivious to the fact that there’s another person in the picture. Just by looking at this guy, Mario gets a sickening feeling in his stomach that this isn’t going to end well. “What can I do for you man?” Mario asks as his head turns back towards the enormous man standing in front of him. Yet, the man’s head doesn’t come up at all and Mario notices that the man’s hands are shaking. To Mario, this guy must be a big time user or something, but that’s never scared Mario from selling to somebody. “I just need your finest,” the man says, his voice darker then a cave. It just bellows as he speaks his words and Mario just stares at the man not knowing what to make of him. However, for Mario, this is a business and he doesn’t concern himself with things like that. He reaches into his pocket and starts to take something out of it. The man in front of him continues to stand there, his head still bent towards the ground. “I don’t normally do this for first time customers. Normally you gotta prove yourself to me, but it seems like you need somethin’ man so I’m gonna help you out. Oh, yes I am,” Mario tells the man as he pulls out a bag. The man barely even acknowledges it as he extends his left hand and gives him some cash as he takes the plastic bag. Immediately the man sticks the bag in his pants pocket before turning away and begins to walk back the direction he came from. Mario looks at the cash in his hands; ten one hundred dollar bills. Without hesitation, he looks up at the man and yells after him. “Hey man, I don’t give change! How about next time you just get something on the house?” Mario asks the man, still shocked to see how this is going down. Yet, the man keeps walking. “I won’t be back,” he mutters under his breath, knowing that Mario wouldn’t hear him. He keeps walking until the darkness takes him away. ---- Night is starting to fall as Daniel looks around trying to unleash a plan that would put him back in the spot of prominence that he had enjoyed not so long ago. His memories begin to flood his mind, reminding him of the evening when he had to go into hiding. Word had started to leak out to him that the job on Craig wasn’t done in the least bit, that he was very much alive. Daniel knew it would take something more than a bullet wound to stop Craig, but he had hoped that Brady would at least be able to put him out of commission long enough for Daniel to put an end to Craig. Yet, that wasn’t the case. Within mere days Craig would be healthy enough to get up and fly home. However, when he arrived home he wasn’t supposed to see the blood in the carpet. That was all a mistake, a mistake that he was paying costly for. The whole reason for going dark wasn’t because he had paid somebody to shoot Craig. Nothing about that scared him in the least bit. It was when one of his associates decided to kill Sarah that he knew things were about to come crashing down for him if he didn’t go into hiding. The minute he had found out about it he knew that things were about to get dangerous in his world. Everything he had created he had to destroy in order to live. It saddened him, but he knew he had to do it. Daniel knew what Craig was capable of and that Craig would go to no end to destroy everything that Daniel had created. The Lord of the Underworld had been reduced to nothing. Reports continued to leak out to him about Craig’s actions. Twelve of his finest men had been killed by him. Craig was trying to send a message to Daniel, but without the backing of an extra ordinate amount of money and the right set of information, Craig would never be able to find out where Santiago was. Santiago had made sure to that. Then, there’s a knock on the door. Daniel looks up and sighs that once again he is being distracted. He slowly pulls himself out of his chair and walks over to the locked door. He pauses as his hand grips the doorknob and wonders to himself if that’s Craig on the other side of that door. He takes moment for himself before taking a deep breath and pulls open the door. In walks one of Daniel’s top agents who doesn’t look good in the least bit. The past few weeks hadn’t been good to anyone as they had to keep on top of Craig and see where he had been and try to figure out where he was going. The first part of that had been no problem as Craig was leaving a trail behind him. It was the other half of that statement that had been proving to be a problem. Craig’s behavior had been erratic at best as he went from city to city without any sort of rhyme or reason for it. Santiago knew that Craig had always been eccentric; wanting to do things his way, but this had taken the cake. Craig was striking out just to strike out, and that couldn’t be further from the game he would play. “Daniel, we have news,” his agent says as he looks at Daniel who just stares back. Then, Daniel walks over to the chair he was sitting in before and makes himself comfortable. If anything, he enjoyed the reports on Craig’s actions. Ever since the day the two met years ago he had seen something special in Craig’s eyes. He was unique, that was for sure, but he had an eye for detail and an ability to keep himself alive no matter the situation. In their line of work they needed that ability more than anything. “Let me have it, Sergei,” he tells his agent who just simply nods his head. He looks around, obviously uneasy about all of this, and wonders what it could be that Craig has done now. Daniel, now intrigued, sits forward in his chair ready to find out the latest bit of news. “Brady is dead,” Sergei tells him, his eyes dashing to the ground as he finishes his statement. Daniel just sits in the chair dumbfounded by it all. He knew eventually Craig would find Brady, but nowhere close to this soon. Daniel had intended to hide Brady and then let him out into the world when he needed that insurance card. Now though, Craig had found Brady on his own, and knew that this wasn’t starting to become dangerous, but it was already dangerous. His hands meet each other as they clasp and Santiago starts to think about his options. Brady had been the first priority after keeping himself safe and that had been compromised. If he could get to Brady, then he could find Daniel sooner than he had expected him to do so. This wasn’t looking good at all for his empire and for himself; not at all. “Go get the dossier and send the black files to this person,” Daniel tells Sergei who walks over to him as Daniel pulls out a piece of paper and hands it to him. Sergei looks at the piece of paper and then back at Daniel, wondering if Daniel is certain about this. Daniel just looks at Sergei and begins to feel angry that Sergei is even questioning him. “Yes, I’m certain! Send it!” he yells at Sergei who promptly walks away and exits the room. Daniel sits back in his chair and sees the end of the road coming up so soon. Yet, if he were to go down, he wouldn’t be the only one going down. The world would go down with him. It was all part of his endgame. ---- Craig walks into his hotel room and realizes it’s empty. Good, he thinks to himself knowing that he needs time to himself. As he watched himself in the mirror earlier, he was disgusted by the person he saw there. He had found himself in a fit of depression as he had hit rock bottom. This wasn’t how he solved his issues. Before, he went around the problem killing those connected to Santiago. That wasn’t his style; it never had been. Instead, he was the kind of person to go straight to the source. That’s exactly what he intends to do now. He double checks to make sure he’s alone before walking into the hotel bathroom and turning on a light. He closes the door behind him and looks at himself in the mirror. His potent red eyes were dull, duller then he had ever remembered them as. That would change soon though, he thinks to himself as he pulls a bag out of his pants pocket and tosses it onto the bathroom counter. The white powder just sits there as he stares at it, making sure that he wants to go down this road. In the back of his mind, he knows if he goes down this road that there is no coming back from it. However, he made his mind up a long time ago to do this, and he knows he has to. This ends. This ends now. He opens the bag and pours the contents onto the marble counter. He stares at it for a second, preparing himself for the journey he’s about to go on. Kamikaze is a Japanese word that means divine wind. In ancient times, the word applied to the samurai whose intent after losing everything was to avenge those that had lost his life. Many times, the warrior knew that this was a forfeiture of his life to bring honor and respect to those names. For so long, Craig knew that this day would come. In a world of honor, pride, and respect there is no question if that day will come, but when. Even with so much his memory missing, so much of it shrouded in darkness, he knew that he was created for a moment like this, a moment when he would have to restore honor to those he had failed. This was his failure. He looks at the white powder and closes his eyes before kneeling right next to it. He breathes in through his nose as hard as he can as the powder flies up his nostrils and into his blood system. He could feel the cocaine doing its job as it takes him into another world, and numbs him to the reality that existed right outside of his conscious mind. His head slowly pulls up off the counter and he just stands there with his eyes closed, allowing his body to be taken over. He can feel a burning sensation all over his skin as he can just feel it changing everything that he knew about himself. Finally, he opens his eyes. He looks into the mirror and sees a man that has long been dormant. He knows this is what he was created for. From day one he was created to be a warrior, a soldier, and instead of trying to deny it any longer, he’s determined to let it be who he was destined to be. This would be his final mission. ---- “Mary, can you bring the files on the Mitchell boys?” a voice booms through an intercom system. The woman on the other end rolls her eyes as she picks up a stack of files and walks into her boss’s office. As she walks in, she notices that he is sitting behind his desk. His back is to her, watching the rolling thunder clouds darken the sky above. With each step, the heel of her shoe clicks against the wooden floor, giving away her position. Finally, she reaches his desk and drops the files on his desk before turning around and walking out of his office. “There you go, Jesus,” she says before closing the door to his office. Yet, he doesn’t move. Not an inch. Instead, his blue eyes are focused upon the darkening of the clouds. To him, this is symbolism, a sign that the times are about to change. With his nemesis Daniel Santiago out of the picture it was time for a new power to take over. Things had gotten stale under Santiago’s watch as he enjoyed the power far too much to allow any kind of changes. Now though, after Santiago had taken the underworld from his grasp, he would be able to step in and do the things he wanted to do. The things that needed to happen for people to be successful like they should be. Santiago was greedy, manipulative; instead of sharing what he had, he horded it for himself. Jesus couldn’t stand the way Santiago did business and plotted for years to kill him. Now, he had just disappeared. The rain starts to come down like soft footsteps on carpet. It’s soothing as he turns around in his chair and looks at his files. He starts to read one of them, learning up on the players in the underworld that he had yet to meet or see. With the times changing, he was going to make it his personal mission to change the way business was done. The black market was one that was always changing, never allowing for it to solidify. Of course, in some instances this was a good thing as the things that were done on the market were strictly illegal. However, other aspects of it proved to be doing more harm than good, and he wanted to eliminate those problems. The Mitchell Brothers were part of that problem. As the rain starts to beat harder on his window, he notices something has changed in the mood inside of his building. He couldn’t quite put his finger on it, but it was quiet; too quiet for him. He straightens up as he then hears a commotion outside of his office. “You can’t go in there sir!” he hears his secretary yell before his door explodes open. Instead of opening the door, it is simply kicked in and shattered to pieces. Jesus can only watch, stricken with fear and horror, as the man seemingly glides to him with confidence just radiating from him. As Jesus looks at the man he sees something that frightens him to his very core. A pair of red eyes. “No, it can’t be,” he mutters, having thought it was surely his time. Instead, the harbinger of Death himself stood at his feet looking like he could throw Jesus out of the window whenever he felt like it. Yet, he just stands there instead of looking like he’s about to fling Jesus around the room a few times. There’s a calmness to him, which puts Jesus off because of the fire in his eyes. The man stands there wearing all black and with an odd smile on his face. Jesus had seen this man a handful of times and every time he had seen this man it was never a good thing. “What do you want? Santiago is gone, Craig,” Jesus finally manages to squeak out. There’s a noticeable amount of fear in his voice, but Craig could care less. He’s not here to harm him and finally Jesus comes to that realization. Instead, he was here to help Santiago. “I know that and I want to find him. It’s time to put an end to this. You want to rule the underworld? Fine. Just help me get to Santiago. You give me your resources and I’ll give you mine,” Craig speaks his voice with a certain edge to it. For Jesus, this is a no brainer. You do what Craig asks mainly because of his ability to change the momentum with his very presence. This was what Jesus wanted. This is what he needed. Sure victory. “You got it,” Jesus says before he stands up. He extends his hand and Craig just merely looks at it. Instead of ignoring the man, he returns the favor and shakes the man’s hand. A smile creeps across Santiago’s face as they break the handshake. For Jesus, his dream was coming true. For Craig, his nightmare was coming to an end. To Be Continued
