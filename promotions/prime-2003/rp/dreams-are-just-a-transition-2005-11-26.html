Dreams are just a transition.

26 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-26-05 10:26:15 P.MMonday 'Excuse me, Nicholas?' No response. No response at all. 'Helllo?' Eyes wide open. Skin's as cold as ice. The air within these walls seems to suffocate the innocence. 'Perhaps you need another shot?' The words crumble like rainfall clashing against his skull, drowning his sight and manipulating his hearing. He has zero control over what's to come considering the circumstances. He lays there; mindlessly channel surfing through the pistons of his imagination, discovering places that he never knew existed. The nurse walks towards his elevated bed -- a snooty expression planted on her face -- with needle in hand and places a gentle hand on his strapped down arm. He closes his eyes and winces in anticipation. These weren't ordinary flu shots. These hurt more than anything. 'You're not as brave as you were when those hands were free. This'll only be a pinch more than what you're expecting, you know that.' A mumble or two shot out of Ozric's mouth as he opened his eyes and glared towards the nurse. His lips were trembling, battling the coldness in the room and the fear beating him into the ground. He was no longer being helped, but moreso being tortured, and what? For being sick? To doctors and psychological experts, he was inhuman and could no longer be helped. Thus, these 'doctors' chose the option of injecting medicine into him, if only to keep him from talking anymore 'nonsense' than they wanted to hear. 'What was that, Mr. Brocken?' She tested the needle by shooting the liquid into the air twice, followed by flicking the body of the syringe to rid the bubbles. Just as she was about to stick him, a massive yellow glob of goo hit her directly in the left eye. She shreiked and shouted for Dr. Beurin. 'I said, FUCK YOU! In a swift motion, the booger-eyed nurse jammed the needle into the arm, forcing his head to shoot up from the suspension, screaming loudly as a multitude of veins protruded from his neck. His pupils ran a marathon to the back of his head as his head slammed into the support of a pillow. The nurse took in a deep breath and exhaled it loudly as she wiped the mucus from her face. She bent over and got directly in his face and started to whisper. 'Let this be a warning to you, Nicholas. There isn't a single person in this hospital that wants you alive, including me. You've done harm to all of us and people outside of these walls, so now it's time for you to pay your dues, understand? Don't turn this into anything more than what it already is, it does nothing for you in the end. It just twists the needle a bit more...' She grinned as the intercom turned on and announced her name. She kissed her index finger and placed it on his head before turning around and walking out of the room. His eyes opened slightly, but just for a moment. In that moment, an entourage of reflections jumped in and out of his head as he began to realize that this might be his final resting place. He was suspened by belts that looked fit for a lethal-injection bed and had been heavily medicated for almost a month now, may be more. He didn't even understand the concept of time at this point and that, for once, scared him dearly. He closed his eyes and let the darkness swallow him whole for another night. 3:04:35 A.MMonday I had escaped? I couldn't believe it. I had managed to rip these seemingly durable belts wrapped around me? Uncanny. Crawling along the tiled floor in hopes that I'm making almost no sound at all, but my uncomfortable inpatient uniform is crumbling up every few feet I glide against the floor. I look up and notice a light turn on outside of my room through the closed blinds. I quickly shift through my options and roll underneath my bed, in hopes that they don't even check the room. The shadows of two doctors or nurses were in clear sight for a moment and then drifted off down the hall. I crawled out from underneath the bed and got to my feet. 'Nicholas?!' Shit -- That was the bitch I honked on. I quickly ran to left side of the door and placed my back flat against the wall as the door opened. She, in all her dreadfulness, entered the room silently and walked over to the empty bed. She shook her head and pulled out a beeper. Fuck it, I was going to be caught whether I liked it or not, but the feeling that it was just me and this whore in the room made me very comfortable for a split second. She shoved the beeper in her pocket and I studied her movement for a couple of minutes before violently running toward her, screaming my name over and over again as I charged into her. BROCKEN.. NICHOLAS.. OZRIC.. OZRIC.. OZRIC! 3:10:23 A.MMonday Two doctors and one administrator of the hospital had reached Nicholas' room and took complete caution before entering. 'Tom.. Greg? Take the outer sides of the door in case he runs out. I'll open the door and if he doesn't try to escape, we'll all run in and do the deed. Understand?' Both of the doctors nodded as the administrator, Gabe Longthort, pulled out a rather large needle from a black case he had strung over his shoulder. He counted to three in his head and twisted the knob to the left, slowly opening the door. 'You come at me with another needle and I'll break the bitch's neck.' The door, now wide open, welcomed all three men inside the room. 'Jesus Nicholas, don't do anything you'll regret.' 'I'll regret? What haven't you fuckers done to me that I'd regret besides kill me? Do you honestly think I'll regret you guys killing me? At what point will there be time for me to regret what's actually happened? Kill me! Shoot me dead! C'mon Gabe, you motherfucker!' 'Calm down, Nicholas. Just don't hurt Christine..' 'I've broken her nose along with two fingers and a cheekbone. This isn't about hurting anyone anymore. I've done that pretty well, I'd say. Do you know what she's done to me in the past month, Gabe?' 'I'm afraid I don't, but that's a non-issue until you let her go.' 'A NON-ISSUE? What you guys are doing to me is highly illegal and could put all of you away for a long time, and it's a non-issue?' All three men understood what was at stake now. They all realized that Nicholas wasn't going to take anymore of the crap they'd pulled to keep him off of society's shoulders. They knew he would kill Christine and it'd only take one snap of the neck for investigators to fly into the hospital and demand further information as to why this had happened in the first place. 'Discharge him and he won't kill me or do further damage to the hospital. Listen to him, Gabe..' Gabe shook his head; his eyes wide open throughout the scenario unfolding. He threw the needle across the floor, rolling itself underneath one of the many tables scattered around the room. 'You win, Nick, just don't hurt her anymore than you've already done and we'll work this out.' Christine started to cry. Whether it was the pain or the realization that they were caught in the act of corruption was unknown, but tears were dropping like rain from the sky. He let go of Christine and she quickly shot up and ran to the doctors and administrator before collapsing on the ground. 'You win, Ozric.. You win..' 10:36:12 A.MMonday I woke up in the realm of a hospital bed, no longer strapped to it, though. A nurse walked in with a tray of assorted fruits and a bowl of cereal. It wasn't Christine. A doctor entered as well, holding a chart of my statistics, along with a stethscope. It wasn't Tom, nor was it Greg. A dream of earlier days when everything seemed to go wrong. A dream about a sign on a highway that I had passed long ago. I was being discharged after a year and three months inside North-County Medical Facility, buried inside the walls of its psyche ward. 'So Nick..' The nurse saud. 'Any plans after you leave this place?' I smiled and slammed my teeth into a golden delicious apple. I swallowed the scattered chunks of apple in my mouth and replied as well as possible. 'I've got something in mind..'
