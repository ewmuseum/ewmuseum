Into The Abyss: Part VI - And That's How It Is...When You're A Soldier

17 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17-02-09 Scuffing her heels against the sidewalk, she quickened her pace as a cold chill ran up her spine. The cool wafting night air would have been refreshing weren't she so skinny and even the slightest breeze made her shiver. Clutching the rucksack close to her chest she glanced behind her and, on finding herself alone on the street turned sharply into an alleyway, carelessly splashing through puddles on her way. Another quick check to her back confirmed the coast to be clear and slowing to a stop beside a large rusted dumpster she caught her breath before reaching out and knocking sharply on a large steel door side entrance. Resting against the side of the dumpster her head lolled to the side as she heard the sound of a locking mechanism disengaging before the door creaked open a couple of inches. Taking another breath she peeked around the side of the dumpster once more before entering through the door as it shut behind her, the locks grating back into place once more. "Did you get it?" Asked a short chubby man in a leather jacket and jeans. "Yeah...where's Reuben?" Gloria replied, noticing that he had one hand tucked inside his jacket, resting on a pistol. He was of middle eastern descent judging by the tan and the thick eyebrows, goatee and balding head though that didn't stop him growing out what hair he had and tying it back into a greasy ponytail. His name was Yousef, but in this capitalist land he went by Joe. "In his office," He said, removing his hand from his jacket and folding his arms. "You're late." He added. She grunted and took off, brushing past stacks of cardboard boxes and trotting up a flight of stairs onto the first floor. The corridor stretched to a set of silver steel double doors at the end that led back down to the small lobby and front door of the establishment. The hallway surrounded a series of what were once small office rooms that now housed more goods belonging to Reuben, as well as a couple belonging to guys like Joe who used them as their own personal space. To her left another set of stairs led to the second floor, and she headed off towards her destination. The stairs stopped at the foot of one long corridor, again running the length of the building though there were only two doors leading to two rooms on this level. The usual theme of steel was present in the door at the very end of the corridor, though it had been veiled with a thin layer of plaster and painted white to blend in with the colour of the walls. Halfway down on the right hand side was the other door, made of heavy oak and also plastered and painted white so that it too matched the colour scheme. What was strange about this however was that on either side of each door and subsequently running all the way along the corridor were brown wooden doors that had been rigorously shaved down to thin board and fixed to the walls, giving the impression that the second floor was once more filled with offices. She had asked Reuben once and been told that it was a precautionary measure. In any case she wasn't too fond of this trippy decor and favoured her little dorm among the offices on the first floor to this strange faux labyrinth. If she was high she would make sure to avoid going up to the second floor for fear of her head bursting at the thought of not being able to find the actual doors. Pulling her jacket tighter over her body Gloria made the long walk past the fake doors and skipped past the first actual door on her right, from which the faintest aroma of burnt meat could be detected. Her stomach turned as she thought about what lay beyond that door and carried on to the end, chapping the handle-less door and pushing against the heavy springs holding it shut to find Reuben sitting back in a large luxury leather chair with his feet up on a very formal desk. This room was different from the rest. The floor was covered in navy blue carpet tiles and the walls, though a dull beige were decorated with pictures of the city and other exotic locations. A large filing cabinet covered the length of the wall to Reuben's right, and to his left a safe and a bookshelf. As the door gently closed shut behind her she placed the rucksack on the desk next to some paperwork as Reuben gestured for her to wait quietly, while he talked away on his cell phone. "...Glad to hear it......yeah United's stock is rising fast, I'm surprised they're not capitalizing on it and making more transfers.......well why buy out when they can just trade for better? Yessir, my '09 kit was delivered today as it happens. Oh, before I forget...the guys are meeting up for the game next Sunday, are you free to join us? We'll be 'round Mary's house......ok sounds good......yeah looking forward to it...ok take care......buh-bye..." Closing the facia over Reuben placed the cell on the desk and lifted his feet off, sitting forward and resting his elbows on the table. He smiled quaintly at Gloria as she shifted awkwardly on her feet. "...I never figured you for a sports fan." She muttered. "I'm not," He began, opening the rucksack and checking its contents. "I want you to find Joe and tell him to send word to the Chief that the meeting has been set up for next Sunday, and tell him to ensure his funds are in order or the deal is off." Sitting back with the rucksack on his lap, Reuben produced a few brick-sized blocks of brown powder wrapped in cellophane. Raising his eyebrows and curling his lip in satisfaction, Reuben placed them back inside the bag and tossed it onto the desk once more. "Good. Did you get an idea of what they're packing?" He asked dryly, clasping his hands together and placing his index fingers over his lips. "There were two of those big removal vans in the loading bay and another three Transits inside. Enough gear to fill each one twice..." She replied, producing a pack of Marlboro and lighting up as Reuben stroked his chin at the thought of all those vehicles filled to the gunnels with more of those bags. "Anyone notice you taking this? Were you followed?" He asked again, his gaze flitting between her and the bag. "No, no I made sure." She replied, taking a long soothing drag. Her reward was one of the blocks from the bag. Reuben's massive hand closed over it and as he tossed it to Gloria she stumbled forward to catch it, holding both her small hands out together to prevent it falling to the floor. Her eyes were wide and she began salivating as she gazed upon the compacted goodness waiting to be cooked up, so much so her cigarette almost dropped from between her chapped lips. As she glanced up at Reuben he held a hand out and gestured for her to leave as his cell phone rang once more. Checking the name on the display he sighed and gestured once more before flipping it open and answering the call. Clutching the kit like a newborn child to her breast, she turned tail and skittered out of the office. As the door closed behind her and she made her way along the hallway, beaming at the thought of getting nice and high before bed she came once more to the only other door on the second floor. Stopping in her tracks she gazed at the door and gulped, remembering what had happened the last time she had stepped foot inside. Looking back at Reuben's office she imagined he would be busy on the phone, and with a deep breath reached out and pushed the door open. The ray of light that crept into the room ahead of her cast an eerie silhouette over the figure seated in the dark. Her heart began beating a little quicker as the figure's head rose up and its face turned to the light. His eyes were as dark and sunken as ever, ringed by a deep shade of red. His nose was crusted with mucus and his beard was slightly matted where saliva had drooled and dried. His face appeared gaunt, and his wrists and ankles were covered in sores where he had been trying to break free of the cable ties holding him down. He had been crying. ".........I need...help." Von Kelsig nearly choked on his words as Gloria moved into the room, closing the door behind her and for a moment only the red ember of her burning cigarette filled the darkness. In another the light above Von Kelsig's head burst with energy and tore at his eyes, forcing them shut. Gloria approached him, tucking the block as best she could into her jacket pocket. It squeezed into a tight fit, filling the pocket but leaving her unable to zip it up. Noticing this, Von Kelsig shuddered at the thought that had the block been in his possession he would have been all too tempted to abuse it. She stood next to him and placed a hand into his open palm, stroking the side of his head with the other. It was a few minutes before the combination of the stinging light and the burning pain in his eyes subsided and he could look up at her. In the bright light her peroxide blonde hair glowed and her pale skin seemed luminescent. His heart skipped a beat as the holy aura embraced him, until he blinked and in an instant the angel before him was reduced to the cadaverous junkie with the wild hair and the big black eyes. He sighed and rested his head in her hands as she thought about the last time they had spoken. She never had a chance to tell him what she had decided before Reuben had burst in and thrown her out. She thought about the bag in her pocket, and the words Von Kelsig had spoken. She couldn't remember when she had taken her last hit but she remembered the time and day that Von Kelsig had told her her life was worth saving... "I'll help you...I'll help you get out of here..." Her eyes stared blankly ahead as Von Kelsig looked up again, his face drooping in submissive disbelief. He was mentally shattered and had for a moment thought he had dreamed those words. "What?" He croaked. "I said I'll get you out of here...and you can take me with you..." She couldn't believe what she was saying and as strong as the urge to cut open that bag and cook up a storm was, the desire to be free of Reuben and this excuse for a life was greater. She knew this now. A smile crept across Von Kelsig's face and any trace of pain seeped from his pores and evaporated into the stuffy air. He looked haggard but those few words had rejuvenated him again, and he felt a shiver run up his spine at the opportunity Gloria was presenting him with. She held his head in both hands and lost herself in his eyes for a moment, while he gazed back and together they grasped the full understanding of their importance to each other. Eventually she relieved her hold on him and took a step back into reality. "Just how do you plan on getting us out of here anyway?" She asked, squinting her eyes and clinging on to the hope they could make it. "You tell me how to get out of here, cut these fucking cable ties off then follow me out and try to dodge the bodies on the way..." He replied, a sinister twinkle sparkling in his eye as his grin grew wide. "Ok...well there's two floors to this building and-..." She jumped hastily into her speech, only to be shushed by Von Kelsig. "-...No, no! Not now. He could come in at any minute, wait until you know the coast is clear before we plan anything." He insisted, to which she bit her lip and nodded in agreement. "Now go, come back when we have time to talk." He gestured to the door and smiled, to which Gloria reciprocated one of her own. For all her drug-addled features, her smile was strangely pure. She skipped out of the room and turned the light out, leaving Von Kelsig in the dark once more. All he could see now though was the light at the end of the tunnel. His chance had come and the surge of adrenaline that burst through him upon hearing Gloria agree to help him was stronger than the choking grip that the poisonous heroin had around him. He began shaking with excitement, his heel inadvertently twitching and causing his foot to thump off the floor. He realized too that in the time since Gloria had left the room he had been smiling in the pitch black, grinning like a fool in the night. While Von Kelsig deliberated on just how he could go about his escape, Gloria had made her way back downstairs to the first floor. Emerging from one of the rooms two tall slim men, one in slacks and a dress shirt and the other in urban fatigue pants and a black vest sauntered down the hallway towards the double doors leading to the front of the building. They took no notice as Gloria entered the room two doors down from theirs and closed the door tight behind her, locking it in the process. Her quarters were bare, but bearable. The walls were a dingy amber, while the once white and black tile-design linoleum was now stained and marked with splotches of red and brown and dark green where fluids of some sickly origin had made their impression. A mattress lay on that dirty floor next to a duffel bag filled with clothes and make-up, while her toiletry bag lay at the foot of the bed. Scattered around that was various drug paraphernalia; packets of sterilised needles, a few empty syringes, a dirty spoon, a roll of tinfoil and a bag of cotton balls. Needless to say to a junkie it was home sweet home. Kicking off her boots and dropping to her knees on the mattress she fumbled the block out of her pocket, her fingers straining to pull it past the zip that bit into her skin. Eventually with minor frustration the cellophane-wrapped packet popped free and she smiled, holding it up and admiring it in all its glory. She was never usually rewarded with a bag this big and she imagined the errand she had run was of great importance to Reuben. Hers was a world of fleeting moments where few details stuck fast, she could have been sent to assassinate the president and not realized it as long as a pay-off like this was waiting for her on arrival. At once all aspirations of getting out and saving herself disappeared with the ominous block sitting before her. Placing it on the bed she took the dirty spoon lying to her right and held it by the blackened bowl. Forcing the handle into the cellophane she pushed down until it split, and carefully tore it down the middle. The sour smell greeted her like a lost love, and sighing with relief she went about the process of preparing a hit. It wasn't until she had removed her belt and fastened it around her arm that she observed just how the habit had been an affliction on her. The crook of her arm and subsequently her forearm was scored with numerous little crusted sores where she had repeatedly injected. Beneath those her veins had begun to take on a darker tone and looked very much worse for wear. With the syringe fully loaded and held between her teeth she slowly regarded the rest of her body, scanning down her gaunt body past her faded features to her bare feet which to her surprise were also pock-marked with sores like craters where she had tried to inject into the veins there. It hit her at once just how much she had destroyed herself with this junk, and as her heart sunk and her stomach began cramping up she had no choice but to give in anyway and shoot up. From hopeless and lifeless piece of shit to a burning supernova of energy glowing with love and laughter and comfort in an instant. She truly hated to love it, but love it she did. She couldn't tell how much time had passed when she was rudely disturbed from her paralytic state of euphoria by a large boot digging into her ribs. Her eyes rolled around in their sockets as she tried to focus on the culprit, moaning softly as her stomach twitched and tickled. Scratching her arm she lifted her weight off of the mattress and leaned on her elbow, her head drooping to her shoulder as another boot nudged her in the side of the face this time. "Get up. I've got a meeting to get to and I need you to dose up Von Kelsig while I'm gone. Come on, move it." Reuben said, wrapping a hand around her dainty arm and hauling her up onto her feet. Swaying slightly, Gloria ran a hand through her rat's nest of a hairstyle and nodded obediently. "Ok...how long you g'nna be...hm?" Gloria muffled, licking her lips and turning her face up at the faint taste of drugs on her lips. "About an hour...so straighten up and get your head right or no more treats." Reuben said it with a straight face before leaving with the two slim associates Gloria had seen earlier on. Sighing, she collapsed back onto the mattress and reached out for her boots, sluggishly pulling them on before falling forward onto her knees and rising to her feet. A rush of blood to the head brought on a dizzy spell that left her smiling as her head pounded with a pleasant rhythm and finally she made her way back to the second floor. "Gone already?" Came the enquiry as she opened the door into the abyss where the monster lurked. "Yeah, he has a meetin' so he won't be back for...an hour?" Gloria replied, scratching her head as Von Kelsig smiled at her. Stepping into the room she closed the door and turned on the light, both of them squinting as their eyes adjusted. Sauntering over to Von Kelsig she leaned against the chair and swivelled onto his lap, giggling girlishly as she did. Butterflies battered around in his stomach as he watched her slump over him. "Now...let's start with the layout of this place. Where do we go once we're out of here?" Von Kelsig asked, his face becoming more serious. Straightening up, Gloria turned and gazed back at him. "Well...you turn left, go down the stairs...then straight down the hall and turn right down some more stairs and out the back door. That's the quickest way..." She smiled again, and his heart skipped a beat. They were really going to do this. "Anybody other than the fat man that I need to worry about?" He asked, already playing out the escape in his head. "Reuben...Joe...and a coupla other guys that work for him. Everyone else works next door in the factory, 'bout forty guys or so..." Gloria replied once more, breaking into a yawn as she finished. Contemplating this information, Von Kelsig went through the motions in his head figuring out the next step. The state Gloria was in threw him a little, he knew she was high and had access to heroin and for the first time it troubled him that he didn't have any and wasn't getting injected until later. The thought only made him focus more on the task at hand and after a moment he took a deep breath and let it out slowly before looking back up at Gloria. "Ok...let's do it. Cut me loose. If he's gone I'm not wasting time, hurry!" Von Kelsig snapped at her, and leaning back in surprise at his vehemency she got up and checked her pockets, patting herself down for any kind of tool to cut the cable ties around his wrists and ankles. "I...I don't have a knife." She muttered. "Check Reuben's office." Von Kelsig replied. "No, he always locks it when he's out on business..." Gloria said with a hint of panic in her voice. "What do you have?" Von Kelsig said as Gloria thrust her hand into her jacket pocket and produced a lighter. For a moment they both observed it and the slow painful realization hit them that this was perhaps the only solution. "Ok just use that, melt the ties off." "No way," Gloria replied holding the Zippo up. "You'll get burned!" "Burn the ties underneath the armrests and the back of the legs, then I won't get burned! Just do it while we have the time!" Von Kelsig shout this time, driving the message home. Gloria hurriedly dropped to her knees and snapped her finger over the wheel of the lighter, holding it to the cable ties around Von Kelsig's right arm as a flame danced to life. Tediously focusing on one area of the tie she watched as the plastic first turned a rusty brown before beginning to bubble. Little tiny bubbles, frothing from the plastic until smoke began rising from it and then finally the cable tie began to visibly reduce as it evaporated and melted simultaneously. Finally it broke apart, oozing at the ends as Gloria went to work on the next one. "How's it looking?" Von Kelsig asked nervously. "Got one, but there's like twenty in total...this is going to take a while." Gloria replied, holding the flame closer to the plastic. Closing his eyes Von Kelsig felt the beat of his heart quicken and lost himself in its rhythm while Gloria continued burning off the cable ties. He didn't know how much time had passed though he imagined not long due to the fast beat of his heart, but still he opened his eyes and forced himself to ask. "What about now?" He hoped the answer would be good. "This side's done!" She chirped, and moved across to the left armrest and began burning it. Tugging on the cable ties on his right arm he was relieved to see them come away with such ease that he couldn't help but let out a cheerful bellow. Shaking his arm and moving all the joints in his fingers he stretched them out and flexed his massive bicep, feeling free already even though he was still bound by three other sets. Reaching forward he felt the ties around his feet and wrapped his fingers around the top tie, gripping tightly and pulling up with hall his strength. To his surprise the tie stretched and contorted before snapping loudly, and again he let out a joyous laugh. "We'll be out of here in no time!" He yelled, ignoring the deep welt left imprinted in his palm from the tie digging in as he pulled on another. Meanwhile, outside the building Reuben was stepping into a black Chrysler 300C with fully tinted windows among other criminal-friendly modifications. As he sat down on the leather seat he ran a hand across his pants pocket and cursed. "Fuck, I left my cell on my desk!" He grit his teeth and growled angrily, quickly jumping back up out of the car and hurrying back into the building, trusting only himself to deal with matters relating to his belongings. Jogging up the stairs to the first floor he flicked a few beads of sweat from his brow nervously, never one to be late for a meeting. Hauling his near-8ft frame at a hefty pace along the corridor he swung up onto the second set of stairs to the second floor and leaped the final few steps before jogging on to his office. Meanwhile Von Kelsig had ripped through another two cable ties and stopped as the stress burst the skin on the palm of his hand. Clenching his fist he thumped it against the armrest as Gloria looked up with a smile on her face. "Got it!" The words had barely left her mouth before Von Kelsig ripped his left arm away from the chair and found the ties fall away as he did so. Without hesitation he got to his feet, wobbling slightly as they were still bound to the legs, and grabbed hold of the seat. Flexing hard he began pulling up with all his might and to Gloria's surprise the legs began rising up off the ground, grinding against his legs as he pulled them over the cable ties. "If there's trouble on the way out..." He began, pulling harder on the chair as the bottom ties on each leg popped freely away. "I want you to keep going...get away from this place..." Another two cable ties popped away as he lifted the legs into the air against his own, grimacing as the solid wood scraped his skin through his pants. "Go to Brayston, Vermont......head north and find Wyatt Manor......when you get there ask for Bruce Shanahan, tell him I sent you. Wait for me. Promise?" He groaned as the wood shaved into his skin, and tore his clothing. A slice opened up along his calf and with a final surge of power he hauled the chair up from the cable ties and tossed it back. "Yes. Ok, I'll wait." Gloria said loudly as the chair clattered off the floor. Standing free for the first time in a long time, Von Kelsig stretched his legs and flexed his muscles. Still wobbling, he fought through the fatigue with the adrenaline pumping through his veins as he prepared to release himself from this prison. The clawing addiction was powerless against the will to be free of this hell. Jogging on the spot he worked blood into his limbs, before stopping and turning to Gloria. Opening his arms he didn't wait for an invitation, pulling her close and nuzzling his face into her hair and kissing her on the head. Taken aback momentarily she soon returned the embrace and rest her head against his stomach, and for a moment they were at peace...but only for a moment... "Gloria, you in here? I forgot my-..." Reuben's voice accompanied the creaking of the door as it opened wide. A jolt of horror ran through both Von Kelsig and Gloria's bodies as Reuben stood in the doorway aghast at the scene before him. To say his world stopped was an understatement as Von Kelsig snapped his head around to find Reuben glowering at him. In an instant he roared at Gloria to "RUN!" before spinning on his heels and charging at his captor with the power of a stampeding bull. An animalistic roar forced itself from his lungs as he sped across the room with the intention of spearing Reuben to the ground and beating his head to pulpy fragments of bone. With lightning fast reflexes however Reuben reached around to his back and slipped a hand under his jacket, pulling it back immediately with something in his hand. A 96G-SD Beretta. Gloria spotted the handgun first, and shrieked witlessly as everything seemed to happen in slow motion. Von Kelsig was only a couple of feet from Reuben as he pulled the gun up, and he barreled forward regardless as the shots rang out at point blank range. BANG!! BANG!! Suddenly, everything went dark... To Be Continued...?
