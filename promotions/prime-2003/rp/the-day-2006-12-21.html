The Day

21 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: December 21, 2006 CUE: "Suicide" by Jedi Mind Tricks ---- The murder of Rita Sammael was headline material for months. In a shoddy little apartment building, the heiress to a vast media fortune had been slaughtered, shot through the face with a .45, buck naked and stuffed into a closet. No one knew exactly what had gone inside there, but everyone – especially the girl's family – wanted to know. Everything hinged on two men: Easton Hall and Brian Jackson. Jackson had gotten off, released, found to have nothing to do with it; the police decided that he was too inept to have formulated a plan like that himself. Easton had said his goodbyes to his old friend, thinking he'd never see him again. Brian Jackson had never told Easton why he had ran that day, why he had come to that particular apartment where everything had gone to shit. Easton had never asked. It wasn't that Easton didn't want to know. Somehow, every time they met, it simply slipped Easton's mind. Easton remembered the days and nights of interrogation, fiery Stan Hepton – curiously enough, a former professional wrestler in his own right – in his face for a confession. Hepton had told him that the Sammaels were going to bring suit against him for the murder of their child. Eric W. Sammael himself had come to shout at Easton, to fling words at him that had utterly no meaning, no base behind them. Easton was the only man they had, but there was no evidence that he had done it. He didn't have a gun registered to him and that day was the first day he had even been seen in that building. Easton called it the Day of the Beginning of the End. Hepton was the only thing between Easton and his release and Easton knew this. Easton knew why he was being kept, too; he was smart. He was a tool that Hepton could use, because even if the man wasn't cunning enough to out the guy who had really done it himself, he could use Easton to do it. Armed with this knowledge, Easton kept his mouth shut and his eyes ahead. Patiently, he waited out those cold days in a cell, alone. And then, of course, came the call. "Listen, you fuck," Hepton growled, leaning to him over the space between their seats. Hepton drove an unsophisticated black sedan with leatherish seats and a dashboard console straight out of '95. Dressed in a black trenchcoat and dark, shabby clothing underneath, Detective Hepton didn't seem like the man who cared about being as up-to-date as James Bond. He fixed beady, hateful eyes on Easton, his snarl as accusing as a pointing finger. "You belong to me, and don't you forget it. When you get out of this car, your chain is not off. You're still property of the police force. Understood?" Easton nodded his head and brushed a wave of brown hair away from his face. Hepton nodded in return. "You're getting out of this car and walking up to room 316, which will be your home base of sorts. There's a cell phone in the room; don't let that sonofabitch out of your sight, because that's the phone I'll contact you on. You can put any other calls on it you like – if you need to, you can jerk off to phone sex using it – just as long as you pick up any call I make to you and you don't fucking block my number. Capiche?" "Capiche." Easton's green eyes didn't waver from Hepton's. He didn't know what the man had to gain by cussing so much, but he didn't ask. "Now listen. I've got you a job with PRIME. You need to be public, Hall, you need to be on TV. All the time. Every week. They need to see your face, know you're out there. That's the only way this will work. But just understand: Chet Worth owns your soul just as much as I do. He doesn't know it yet, and if you're lucky, he won't. But you piss him off, you get yourself fired, so help me GOD I will rip your throat out and feed it to Eric Sammael. Capiche?" "Capiche," Easton repeated. Stan didn't look Italian, but Easton didn't bring it up. "We pulled a lot of strings to get this to work, Hall. A lot of fuckin' strings. You will bring me whoever is behind this ready for the fucking noose. We got a lot of bad fuckin' press over this and we are going to make it right. But don't mistake me, Hall; you are expendable. I will hunt you down if you fuck this up, Hall. I will hunt you the fuck down." Stan's growl grew meaner and his face grew closer. "Do you FUCKING UNDERSTAND ME!?" Easton grit his teeth behind his lips, keeping himself contained. "I un – " "I have killed people with my bare fuckin' hands, Hall," Stan breathed dangerously. "Bare fuckin' hands. So don't you fuck with me. DO NOT. FUCK. WITH ME." Stan trembled with pure hatred. Easton trembled with pure anger. Their eyes locked, a death struggle, a war of attrition. Easton wanted to reach up and strangle this man, knock his head off his shoulders, smash his face into the dashboard, through the dashboard. Instead… instead, Easton breathed. All the anger swept out of him. Easton still wanted to stick Hepton's face through the dashboard, but calmly now. Click! went the opening door handle. Easton stepped out of the car and threw it shut behind him, his face free of scowl, free of emotion altogether.. He reached into his pocket and pulled out the single key for the room he was staying in. He had decided he would call today the Day of the Continuation of the End. It was catchy enough. Two stories later, Easton pushed open the door into his apartment. He looked around and scratched the back of his head idly as he pushed the door shut. It was a modest place, at least in size; the floor was bare hardwood and the only furniture was a shitty little desk and chair. Atop the white desk was a tiny silver phone. Easton didn't pick it up. He didn't even look at it. He walked across the room and pulled open another door, into what he hoped was the bedroom. He wanted to sleep in a normal bed for a change. So, set upon the path. It was an odd feeling, being bait. Easton didn't completely understand what his gut was trying to tell him. He was free of jail, true, but how would he dodge all the questions? Upon hitting PRIME, people were going to want to know what happened to this girl. They wouldn't be satisfied with "I don't know" and Easton wasn't about to say that he was being put there as bait to draw the killer out. If he did that, Stan Hepton would likely kill him with his bare fuckin' hands. Bare fuckin' hands. Easton wasn't that stupid, anyhow. Besides that, though, Easton had his own reasons for wanting to know what was going on. He had managed to put the mystic out of his mind for his jail term, but that was only to preserve his sanity, to keep from beating the walls in anger. Now that he was out, he couldn't help but turn his thoughts back to the man. Couldn't help seething internally at him. Couldn't help remembering that Kelly was likely still captive, still under his control. What bothered Easton was everything that he had heard in whatever state he had been on the Day. That conversation he had heard while wavering in and out of consciousness had disturbed him then and he just remembered what it was about it. They had been talking about something called the Babalon Working and summoning. It wasn't something that Easton had ever seen done or even believed possible, but the mystic knew too many things that Easton didn't. He didn't put anything past him. With everything weighing down on his mind, Easton pulled out the chair and took a heavy seat. He rubbed his hand over weary features and then looked up. So much to think about. He had to get back in shape for competition, too; he'd been in jail maybe a month and hadn't really been able to do his normal workout. He needed to be in tip-top shape for PRIME. This was his biggest stage. He had been handed an opportunity and he meant to take it and make it his. That was all he had ever had.
