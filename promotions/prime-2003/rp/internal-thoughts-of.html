Date: Today Discretion truly is the better part of valor. Silvio Fiore defeated me a few weeks ago. He broke my nose and wounded my pride. Since then, I have not appeared on PRIME television. A blessing in disguise, it seems. Chet Worth’s managed to get the company in hot water, and all sorts of rules have come down from the powers that be at FX. I think you all know how much Kenjiro Ito likes rules and guidelines. So, I don’t think you can blame PRIME for deciding to hold me back a bit. Give me a few weeks to calm down so I don’t start dropping f-bombs all over the place. Give me a few weeks to lighten up. It’s times like this that I wish we were still on Playboy TV. I had to join this place just after they stopped being able to have fun. But now, I’m back. My nose has healed, as has my pride. Fiore will pay eventually, but he can wait. For now, I have to face Kevin Sandusky in the ring. I’ve already been told of the changes. That I’m going to have security guards accompanying me when I get my Ito on. FUCK THAT SHIT! FX can blow me. I am KENJIRO FUCKING ITO. The Future of Wrestling. THE MAN WHO WILL TAKE PRIME INTO 2007. I don’t need security. Unlike that pussy Worth, I can take on twenty drunken frat boys single-handedly. I don’t need FX. PRIME doesn’t need FX. At ReVolution, I fire the first salvo for a company scared too shitless to respond. At ReVolution, I reclaim the path that I was once on, the path of the next big thing in this sport. At ReVolution, Kenjiro Ito ceases to be Kenjiro Ito. Because, really, no one calls me Kenjiro. I’m known the world over by three letters. I-T-O. So why not go by it? That’s right, kiddies. Drop the Kenjiro. Because ITOsan’s coming to ReVolution. And ITO’s Ice Age is coming with him.
