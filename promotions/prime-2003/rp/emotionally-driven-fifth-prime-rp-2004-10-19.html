Emotionally Driven [Fifth PRIME RP]

19 October 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10-19-04 I never meant for this business to get in the way of me being a father to my girls. Hell, I didn’t think it would have ever cost me my marriage, but it did. But even after the divorce, I made time for my girls. I took them fishing. We went on vacations together. When I was in Chicago for a match, I made sure they had front row to see me. I made sure I took time off to be with them, and to be a good father. I made sure they knew that they are number one in my life. I never imagined in my worst nightmares, that this business would end up hurting them. I sure as hell never thought, never considered, never fathomed the possibility that they would be damn near killed, by an enigma from my past....... VAMPIR NOSFERATU I had forgotten all about him after he was banished or killed by Hessian back in Old School Wrestling. I may have forgotten about him over the years, but there is one thing a father doesn't forget. The year was 1996. Me and Stacey hadn't been married yet. I was still a rookie on The Orlando Magic. And we were expecting our first child. I tell you what......I was more scared those 9 months leading up to the big day, then the first time I ever wrestled in a ring. And then the day came. It was August 27th, 2 am, and Stacey had woken me from a deep sleep. I drove her to the hospital, and after she put up a brave fight, Mackenzie was introduced to the world. That was 5:15 am, on the 27th of August. That afternoon, on my way to team practice, I heard a song for the first time, that was making it's radio debut, and it's been mine and Mackenzie's song ever since. Hell, it makes me cry when I hear it still to this day. And I know, when Mackenzie pulls through this tragic time, and in 10-15 years when she gets married, she'll choose this as her song to dance with me. 'Butterfly Kisses' Still to this day, when ever I put her to bed, we give each other butterfly kisses. And since that fateful night two weeks ago, I've made sure to give her butterfly kisses every night, hoping that this brings her out of the coma. Kenzie is my first child, and she's always been daddy’s little girl. And I won't let her become a minion to Vampir. I've fought through to many sicknesses, to many broken bones from playing in the trees, and been to to many cheer competitions to let her go so easily. She'll fight out of this, she's strong willed like her father. Carleigh, the baby of the family, is very special. She was brought into this world January 25th, 1999. She caused Stacey alot of problems during delivery, and at one point, the doctor thought she had lost both my wife and unborn child. Needless to say, it all worked out in the end. Carleigh was fine. Stacey was fine. She's my little tomboy too. Rough and tough. Hell, she's just as big as her 8 year old sister. And she's a fighter too. Let me tell you. She'll tell you her mind too, even using bad language too freely. But even being a tomboy, she’s still a little girl at heart. She’s my ‘Care Bear’. We get that from the Care Bear characters, her favorite thing in the whole world. Same as her sister. I won’t let her succumb to Vampir’s powers. He will not use my girls to his advantage. I won’t let it happen. I’ll die myself before Vampir has his way. Maybe the emotions of what I’ve been through is too much for even me to handle. But, hell….. I’m a father before I’m a wrestler. My kids are #1 priority. Wrestling ain’t got nothing to do with what I’m going through right now. ===============================================================ARNOLD PALMER’S CHILDREN HOSPITALORLANDO FLORIDA.INTENSIVE CARE UNIT ROOM 301 Even after two weeks of coming to be with my girls, I still find it hard to walk into the room, see my angels lying still in their beds. To see all the machines and tubes hooked up to them, hard on the eyes, even harder on the mind. I’ve sat back for two weeks, watching doctors and nurses do their things, trying to bring my girls back from the comas they are both in. Athena has been very supportive. She stayed here last week when I surprised Vampir in San Antonio. Hell, I surprised a lot of people last week. Doctor: Good evening Mr. Sloboda. Boda: Good evening Doctor Greening. Any updates? Doctor: Well, nothing as far as mass improvement. But the good news is they are not going backwards. Boda: Well, thank you Doctor. I’m going to go sit with the girls for a bit. Thanks again. The whole staff here at Arnold Palmer has been 100 percent great. This hospital loves children. I really hate stepping into this room. I feel like I have let them down, allowed them to be easy access to anyone. I can’t help but feel that I am responsible for this. If I had never become a wrestler, I’d never have put my kids in this position. Hell, I feel a little responsible for Tony Rolo being eliminated last week. After all, it’s not his fault Vampir ran like a scared bitch when I hit the ring. Yeah, I might have been a little ‘rage blind’, but come on, it was all emotion driven. Rolo, as a professional, needs to know that. What happened last week, was not personal. Athena: Hey Chris. It’s been hard on Athena too. She took to my girls almost instantly. She’s been here, around the clock with me. She’s been the only bright spot in this dark tragedy that I’m going through. Boda: Hey. How you doing? Athena: Good. A little tired. Didn’t get much sleep last night. How are you? Boda: Great… considering. Still can’t believe the bullshit stipulation Motoki put on this match this week with Tony. Telling me to show up and wrestle, or else be fired. I have half a mind to kick his stupid ass for hiring Vampir in the first place. Secondly…. I showed up in San Antonio, unexpected, with my kids still here. How does he not think I won’t be at the show this week? Seriously, this is Orlando. My home. Of course I’m going to be there. Even if it is against Rolo, I’ll be there. I’m sure there will be Rolomaniacs in the T.D Waterhouse come Wednesday night, and I’ll be there. And even when he’s attempting to hulk up, I’m sure those Rolomaniacs will cheer him on, and I’ll be there. My girls are laid up in this hospital, and I’ll be there. I’ll be there because Vampir will be there. And before or after I wrestle Tony, I’ll be all over Vampir. Boda: Look at them. I point to my girls, lying calm in their beds. Athena smiles, what else can one do in this situation. As a tear comes to my eyes, I try to imagine my life without them, and I can’t. Watching them there, reminds me of my debut in PRIME almost four months ago. As they lay there helpless, I remember helping two men my first night in this company. Two men that didn’t ask for my help, but had I not been there the outcome would have been different for them two. Joey Troy and Tony Rolo. Athena: So, you spending the night with them tonight? Boda: Sure….. you go to the house. I’ll crash here. Wednesday night, they’ll be under guarded care with the Orlando Police stationed in the hallway. And as soon as me and Rolo finish tearing down the house at the TD Waterhouse, I’m coming right here…….Hey, you look really tired…. go ahead, take the car and go home. I’m okay. Athena tries to hold back a yawn. As I take my nightly position in a chair between both beds. The short beeping of the life supporting machinery has helped me, calmed me to sleep for weeks. Even with a huge match upcoming against a stellar opponent like Tony, I’ll find it easy to fall asleep sitting in this chair with both the loves of my life on each side of me. Athena walks to me, kissing me on my cheek. She then places a loving kiss on each girl’s forehead before leaving the room. Boda: I’d like you to be at the show this week. I’m going to have to give a lot to the fans this week, and it’ll be great for you to be there with me. Athena nods her approval before disappearing down the hall. As she goes, I’m left to wondering how this week will play out. Tony….. you and me aren’t best friends. We are merely associates, professionals in this business. I always say hi to you, and you always manage to return the greeting. I never expected anything in return when I saved your ass the night I arrived in PRIME. It’s just the way I am. Last week, I was out of control from my emotions. If you took the Bounced Czech personally, I’m sorry. It was an accident. This week though…. it won’t be. I promise.
