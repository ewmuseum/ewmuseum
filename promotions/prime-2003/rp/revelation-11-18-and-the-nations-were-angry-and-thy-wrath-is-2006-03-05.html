Date: 3/05/06 Snow fell like the hopes and dreams of so many immigrants who migrated to the land of plenty in the 20th century. Hoyt watched as the white accumulated and covered up the bleak colors of the dead winter grass. The blanket of white made Hoyt smile as he thought to himself that’s exactly what the world needs. Many believe for all intent and purposes, Hoyt Williams is done with PRIME. His name is now hardly mentioned in rants over the greatness of the roster, and nobody hangs on his words anymore. Fuck them all Hoyt thinks as closes the blinds on the window to the world. Hoyt Williams: They think I’m done Mary. They think I’m washed up. They think I can’t win back the Universal Title. They think repugnant comedy, foul humor, and dramatized bullshit is the wave of the future. They think a lot of things Mary. Most of what they think is wrong. Mary was thinking about a lot of things herself, as the birth of Hoyt’s first child was days perhaps hours away. With her legs kicked up on the recliner and head resting upright on the chair she really wasn’t listening to her husband, but more so enjoying the movement of the life inside of her. Hoyt Williams: What they fail to realize is I’m not done, I’m just getting started. Its a war crazed world and baby I’m the craziest of all of them all. I will do what has to be done to get what I need. Baltasar left Imperium? So what, big deal, why are they wasting air time on him. Face-eater and Nova talk up a drug culture that has populated our world and destroyed it? That’s as original as a Peter Vetra promo. Oh I shouldn’t rag on old friends should I? Hoyt snickered as he picked up the latest issue of Mad magazine and worked to fold the back cover together to waste three minute trying to read a joke that is never, and has never been funny. Sometimes in life we do thinks out of habit and continue to do them because they are a trademark of what we are. You don’t have to be a magazine, or a TV star to be typecasted. Millions of American sheep fall into it every single miserable day. Hoyt’s plan of world domination was calumniating to a peak and it wouldn’t be long until he unveiled his wrath on an unsuspecting world of followers. But even as powerful of a man as he is, he was still insecure in his dealings with PRIME. Hoyt Williams: What these simps, blimps, and gimps of PRIME do not understand is I have chosen the path less traveled. I have been putting my efforts in other world activity’s and not as much in PRIME, or the PTC. Well baby doll that is all about to change as I provoke my enemy’s to try and match me. I challenge those who think they are better them me in a simple match of wrestling to pin my shoulders to the mat. I call out the names who think they are better then me like Olsig and others. BRING IT ON SINNERS. This is the start of a new day for HOYT WILLIAMS and PRIME...... Mary Williams: Honey its time! Hoty Williams: Dinner already? Mary Williams: No, BABY! Hoyt Williams: Yes, what do you need...OH GOD...you mean baby! Mary Williams: Get me to a damn hospital or you will be visiting GOD!!!! To be continued......
