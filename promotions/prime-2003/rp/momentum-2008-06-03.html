Momentum

3 June 2008

Date: June 3rd, 2008 mo – men – tum (noun) 1. force or speed of movement; impetus, as of: a. a physical object The car gained momentum going downhill. b. a course of events Defeating Jason Natas at UltraViolence 2008. * * * ULTRAVIOLENCE 2008 BRYAN DAWKINS VS. JASON NATAS FINAL MINUTES OF THE MATCH Stunned, Natas stands erect and catches an attempted kick to the gut, which is followed with Dawkins’ other leg thrusting into his sternum, sending Natas into the ropes. Dawkins runs towards the opposing side and yet again uses the ropes to his advantage, springboarding into the air and catching Natas with a reverse springboard DDT! Natas neck and head snap back and plant into the mat, much to the enjoyment of the crowd! DAWKINS DAWKINS DAWKINS!!! Richard: Natas is just playing possum. He’s fine. Nick: Say what you want, but Dawkins has yet again turned around and taken the momentum of the match! Without hesitation, Dawkins has found his way to the top turnbuckle, and has also found the time to show his praise to the 50,000-odd fans in attendance by sending them all the "hang-loose" hand gesture that all but few of the fans enjoy immensely. Richard: If he keeps that up, the only thing he’s going to win is a trip to Chicago’s finest emergency room… Nick: What are you talking about? I love it! Richard: You would. Nick: Dawkins is now showing his appreciation to the crowd, who has no doubt been his motivation through the match! And it looks like he’s gonna end it right here! Richard: Fat chance. After showing his appreciation to the fans, Dawkins springs from the top rope, arching high in the air before crashing down onto Natas with his patented Shooting Star Press, which Dawkins has dubbed the HAWAIIAN HANGOVER! Nick: And Dawkins nails it! It’s all over, Richard! What was that about a fat lady singing for Dawkins? Richard: … The Flyin’ Hawaiian hooks the leg of Natas and holds on… ONE... TWO... THREE!!! As the referee calls for the bell, the crowd erupts with cheers for the Hawaiian, who has pulled off his first victory with the company. * * * ULTRAVIOLENCE 2008 BACKSTAGE SHORTLY AFTER DAWKINS/NATAS “So babe, d’you see it? I pulled it off!” “Congrats, Bry! You’ve worked hard for this, now you should go off and celebrate. The last thing you should do is worry about how I’m doing…” The voice on the other end of the phone trailed off. Nikkie was nearly four months pregnant, and getting bigger by the day. Of course, Bryan, being on tour with PRIME, has not been around for any of the process, which wasn’t making things any easier on either of them. “No no, babe, I’ve missed so much. Tell me, what’s new?” questioned Dawkins, who was still in his wrestling gear. The match had drained the Hawaiian of his energy, and stripping the tights off in exchange for some more loose, “comfortable” clothing wasn’t a viable option at the moment. He was completely content with his state at the moment. “Bryan, you’re having the night of your life so far. I’m not going to bore you tonight about this. Go out, have a good time, and be safe celebrating the big win. Love you, kid.” Dawkins sighs. “Love you too. Call me tomorrow, ‘kay? Peace.” Just as the Flyin’ Hawaiian closes his cell phone, his locker room door bursts open and Dawkins is met with a barrage of fruit (mainly pineapples, for the sake of our story), lei’s, and… …Polunu? Bryan looks on in awe as his best friend bursts through the door with a multitude of gifts. What the hell was his friend doing here? Did Dawkins get him tickets and forget to remember? Who knows. “Polunu! What the hell you doin’ here, bruh?” The portly Hawaiian smiles, showcasing a mouth full of freshly-cut pineapple, and replies to his longtime friend. “Oh, a little bird got me tickets for tonight…” Polunu looks around the room innocently and continues eating the fresh pineapple he has brought along with him. Dawkins, still in his tights and looking mightily confused, stands before him in a state of confusion. “Uh…what?” “Nevermind that! What a win tonight, bruh! For a second there, I didn’t think you had a shot. Natas, like I told you the other day when we were out lookin’ for coconuts, was throwin’ your ass around that ring like a ragdoll, bruh! Didn’t think ya had a chance!” Now that he was reminded of it, Dawkins felt the soreness from his previous match creeping in and settling in his muscles. Every square inch of his body felt like it had been beaten, and suddenly moving around and being active simply was not an option. It was appearing as though celebrating the “big victory” would NOT be on the agenda for tonight. “Now that you mention it, I do feel like hell, bruh. Natas is a tough dude. Big guy, too. Not real easy to get him down…” “Yeahhh, but you did, bruh! What a match! I mean, think of it. The guy was a full-out STREETFIGHTER, and you took him down. Natas is probably one of the meanest guys in PRIME, and it didn’t phase you one bit!” Dawkins just shrugs and continues to tend to his aching body. “Now that you’ve taken on this first speed bump on your…path to glory…it’s about time we start shooting for some higher goals!” Peering awkwardly at Polunu, Bryan questions his friend. “…we?” Polunu smiles and nods his head wildly. “Yes, WE! How about this…the FLYIN’ HAWAIIAN BRYAN DAWKINS being accompanied to the ring by THE ONE AND ONLY…POLUNU!” Dawkins sighs and picks up another ice bag to place on his neck. Laying down on the bench of his locker room, he looks over at Polunu and smiles. “That, bruh, we’re gonna have to save for another day. Right now, I just wanna recover, and carry this momentum into next week. Ya know, maybe pick up win number two? That’d be good, wouldn’t it?” Polunu smiles and nods his head. Dawkins repositions himself on the bench and motions to his friend. “Now if you wanna get a head start on this whole…manager…thing, how about you make yourself useful and send one of those pineapples over my way. I mean, we need to have SOME sort of celebration, right, bruh?” The scene fades out with both Polunu and Dawkins enjoying some abso-fucking-lutely juicy pineapple as Dawkins recovers from the hellacious war that was Dawkins/Natas.
