What's My Line? Part 1

10 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10th Occasionally in life you can become so focused on one thing, that you forget everything else. Even those things, which you hold most dear. It’s not intentional; it’s just that the mind of man being the most complicated thing on the planet (the first being the mind of a woman), it often has an overwhelming ability to focus on just one thing. A Quest A Goal A Mission The mind over powers the heart, so even if you aren’t trying to hurt someone you inevitably do. Yet you will not see their pain, or their agony, nor will you see the tears that they cry, simply because you won’t be there. It’s only when you reach that goal, which you have strived for, for so long, that the world becomes realigned and with it you can see the terrible truth, the things that you have done. Will it be too late to change everything? To make amens for your mistakes through tainted eyes. All the wanting in the world won’t change what has passed though, it simply doesn’t work like that. Through rose tinted glasses, I spend my days watching them perform. Putting their bodies on the line in the name of entertainment. The thrill of the crowd, their cheers for hearing their home town mentioned, the boos as the bad guys used a chair illegally, something which the referee missed due to being knocked over, unconscious like a sack of potatoes. This of course is the world of wrestling, the land of luchalibre wrestlers were their masks tell a history. Seven foot men, boosted by the little bits of plastic in their shoes. A world where even the thunder gods themselves can sell themselves as wrestlers. The world around me thrived as I sat silently on the bench. The park was full of life, family outings, with children wrestling playfully on the grass. The grass had turned almost golden due to the suns rays. It had been a frightfully warm summer so far, the air almost tasted of electricity in the storm, which was clearly brewing, in a time some way in the future. It was the world that had been a part of John’s life for the past four years. True the times he spent there had not always been golden, in fact since joining 4WF, all those years ago, his life had entered a rollercoaster, which he could not climb off of. In the space of four short years, he had watched his brother die and as a result attempted to take his own life, still baring the scars to this very day. The scars a constant reminder of how close he came to making a fatal mistake. I had been sat down for sometime without moving considerably. The occasional flick of a wrist to dismiss a curious fly. The slow and careful uncurling of my toes as cramp set in slowly, it had been happening a lot more these days. I had allowed my focus to fall directly opposite me. A small river ran through the park, a number of aged bridges providing passage across. You get caught up in the whirlwind of it all, although you are just playing a character, although you know that it is just an extension of your darkest feelings. It does begin to consume you; the lines between character and reality become blurred and jagged. That said, the entire business was not all doom and gloom. Upon the river was a family of ducks; I at least presumed that they were a family. The two little ones at the back were pecking at each other like children bickering. The ducks weren’t my main focus though. It was the river itself. Granted you couldn’t see the bottom the river, discarded shopping trolleys, used prophylactics. Yet the entire river continued regardless. It didn’t care about the shit that was in it. It knew its destination, that through its entire struggle, for every amount of semen that had fallen in and tainted it, it knew where it was going. For a start the money was good, once you had your face out there and people recognized you the world practically became your oyster, endorsement deals came through your door everyday, the opportunity to sell yourself out if you wanted to, to bring in that little extra income for saying how great a chocolate bar is, it’s the thing of champions. Maybe that’s why I couldn’t avert my eyes from it, as it babbled effortlessly in front of me. Every inch of water there would eventually reach the sea and complete the circle of life…for water. What I’m trying to get at is that it had a purpose; I had direction in its life. I myself was fortunate to find the love of my life, a woman who radiates pure goodness the second she walks in the room. A woman who without a doubt had saved my life. She had pulled me back from the brink, when it seemed as though life had one too many tasks for me, without her I would have crumbled. She was my angel in disguise, the one who bought the truth to my eyes. This was something I felt that was slipping out of my control. I had it all planned out you see. My attention was pulled away from the river as a small balled rolled and brushed the side of my leg, stopping a few feet in front of me. The stomping of feet following it quickly at speeds, the sound of dry grass being crushed beneath feet. A small blond haired child appeared in front of me. His eyes full of youth and wonder, not yet worrying about what the world had to throw at him. He smiled a semi-toothless smile picked up his ball and dashed back to his mother and father, clutching the ball tightly against his chest. His legs flailing unceremoniously as children running often do. Turning back to the river, I noticed that the ducks had disregarded him as interesting and continued their journey down stream. Reaching into the gravel, which rested just below my feet, I idly picked up a few rocks and began to skim them across the surface of the water. It was very much a time wasting exercise. All was not well in the world of John, not well at all. Her name is Louise, I met her in my very first company. Sure it wasn’t the best of beginning, effectively stealing her off a co-worker. But its always been written that the course of true love never runs smooth. A calm personality who stood by me through everything, the happiest day of my life, was when I got down on one knee and asked her to spend the rest of my her life with me. Marriage, it was a scary prospect, the only thing in my life I was ever prepared to commit to for a long period of time. It’s well documented that I was very much a make and move person. I used companies. I squeezed every ounce of money that I could out of them, but with PRIME I had felt something different a desire and a hunger to stay there, so when they threw me out. It tore through the very sinews of my heart. Lately though, this had begun to cause friction between us, she couldn’t understand that I needed this job, that we needed this job. Without it we had no money coming in. I appreciate that we weren’t seeing that much of each other, but there was no need for all of the arguing. I sighed a large unfulfilled from the very bottom of my lungs as I glanced at my wristwatch. The sun would be setting any minute now and I would have to return home, to face a barrage of shouting, accusations of not loving her anymore. With a certain level of apathy I pulled myself up to a vertical base, wearily and rolled his back, a number of vertebrae cracking gently as I did so. I felt my face squirm into a ball as the arcs of pain rushed through my spine, before subsiding, waiting once again to attack me. My back had been an issue ever since day one, it had been manipulated numerous times by various doctors who always said the same thing, if you want to walk when you’re forty, tone it down. So I had, no one wants to spend his or her life in a wheel chair. In the past I would happily throw myself off of ladders, be the man who was called for the crazy stunt. I was an Extreme Champion representing PrimeTime Central. PRIME was different though, PRIME was elite in a way, the roster was beginning to compete with the legendary fWo, just to be a part of it, meant big money in the long run, as well as the chance to be seen on the international stage. So why couldn’t she understand that? I was only doing this for her now, I may have appeared selfish in my intent, but it was always about her. Always… “Well” I mumbled to no one in particular as I turned lightly on my heel and left the picturesque image of the park behind me. I had avoided Louise for long enough. ~~~ Back at the Edwards residence, which currently consisted of a relatively small flat, Louise Anne Edwards. John’s wife and by his own admission the love of his life, was pacing up and down the living room clutching a small white plastic stick in her hands. Two things should be noted about this situation. There was a thin blue line on the plastic stick. Louise’s hands were shaking. She shook her head and allowed her gaze to fall upon the blue line, which appeared to be growing, encompassing her very world. In a rush of panic she ran into the kitchen and buried the stick at the bottom of the bin, trying to incorporate the theory, out of sight, out of mind. Returning to the living room she sat on the sofa, rocking back and forwards lightly, allowing her hands to rest on his stomach. They had never talked about children, but it had always been a dream of hers, as it is with most women, she was just scared about how John would react, who at heart was still a child himself, spending free time playing on his Playstation. It was as though he wanted to clasp onto his childhood. The door to the flat opened slowly, Louise allowed her eyes to fall upon the clock quickly checking the time before pulling herself to her feet. She dashed forwards and flung her arms around John who less than enthusiastically wrapped a single arm around her, his back was still hurting, he didn’t need it playing up so close to Revolution. “Hey, hun how are you doing.” John asked as he kissed her, more out of habit than love and brushed past her, sitting down on the sofa, once again a dull crack rang through John’s ears, causing his face to wince in pain. Louise did not notice this however as she had decided she was going to make the most of this, she had to tell him her news, their news. It could be the thing to finally bring them together. “John honey, I’ve got something to tell you. Why don’t we get dressed up and go out for dinner. It’s been so long since we ate in public, now you are back in PRIME, we can afford to treat ourselves.” Louise had moved behind John and was running her hands against his chest lovingly. John let out a small sigh and checked out the time. His back was shot, which meant he would have to be up early to get it manipulated once again and time was already pressing on. “I’d rather not Lou, I really need to just focus on Revolution a good showing this week will really help me in PRIME.” He slowly and painfully pulled himself up and walked into the kitchen, receiving a bottle of water from the fridge, twisting the top open and taking a number of large gulps. “Really, it seems all you are focusing on is PRIME. Do I even count anymore?” The tears were already welling up in Louise’s eyes, her arms flailing uncontrollably to exaggerate her speech. John sighed and hung his head, he knew this was coming, he was reasonably impressed though forty two seconds, some sort of record. “I only do this for you Louise! Do you think I like having my spine wrenched every week, just so I can walk and train? Do you think I like being away from you for so long? The only reason I’ve been away is you…no that didn’t sound right.” John fumbled his way through his hurried talk. He had gone through this conversation many times in his head, but it had never gone this badly before. ”John, I love you, but you’re just making this too hard! I can’t cope being on my own all this time, how am I going to cope when we have our child!” Louise paused mid-sentence. Her face turned a shade of crimson, usually associated with blood, her head hung limply. All of the thoughts in John’s head disappeared and were instantly replaced with one simple word. What… “What?” he barely managed to say, as though he used his very last breath to utter the word, it was emotionless, just full of confusion. “John, I’m pregnant.” Fin…
