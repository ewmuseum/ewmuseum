Enter the Deville (Match rp for ReV.56)

28 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02-28-05 ==== Culture Shock...what can be said about it that will do the event justice. 'You could start off by praising the greatest tag team not only in the history of PRIME, but in the history of pro wrestling...LOS DIABLOS!' I suppose I could do that. Afterall the guys really busted their asses and made quite an impact on the landscape of the Duel Halo match, not to mention PRIME itself. 'Exactly, holmes, cause thanks to Los Diablos that little cheating tart, The K-Wolf, was able to secure a victory and get anything she wants from Hin See in return.' That is partially true, though, Rage had a real good shot of winning the match still. What do you suppose the K-Wolf with ask for? 'First off you were correct. Senor Rage had a great shot of winning. Unfortunately our Spanish brother had to start off the match and had to endure almost two hours of punishment, while K-Wolf, simply sat back and strolled her way into the win. So props are a major order for our Spanish friend. Secondly, and most important, let's hope that the sexually frustrated Karina doesn't ask for something stupid...like the right to give Hin See free blowjobs every single ReVolution for the next year. What she should really ask for is a rematch with Los Diablos and face them in a fair match up.' I can pretty safely say that I don't believe we'll be seeing that happen anytime in the near future. Specially since her partner, Tessa Windsor, and her seem to have had a bit of a falling out at Culture Shock. 'True, essa, but that is really of no concern to Morales and the boys. Karina could simply pick herself another partner to help her lose the titles to their rightful owner, Los Diablos, anyway. Tessa doesn't have to be there for the inevitable to happen.' Well since we won't be seeing that happen this week perhaps we should discuss the upcoming match for ReVolution 56 when Baltasar goes one on one with Angelo Deville, the man that Los Diablos cost the Halo, in Deville's singles debut... 'Just stop right there essa! Why does everyone only want to acknowledge the fact that Deville is making his singles debut in PRIME? Can some one who isn't fricken bias toward the Spanish culture please get a position of authority around here? I mean for God sakes, holmes, it's also Baltasar's singles debut for PRIME. Does anyone care to mention that fact?' I guess it was a slight over sight. 'Sure. It's always a slight over sight until affirmative action is threatened.' I really don't believe that the oversight was done out of prejudice intent by PRIME officials. I know it certainly wasn't that way on my part. 'Don't try that bullsh!t with me putá! It's been nothing but one big parade of bigotry toward Morales and Los Diablos. It wasn't until they arrived that Enrique Morales was finally able to realise just how much PRIME was screwing him simply for being of Spanish decent.' How the hell can you say they did that to Rage? He is the Alias Champion and one of the most respected superstars to step into a PRIME ring. 'Then why the hell did they throw him to the wolves of the Dark Age? Can you tell me that? Why not their little hero Tony Rolo? Or maybe that turncoat prick, Boda?' I don't really believe that the 'threw him to the wolves' as you put it. 'You don't huh? Did you catch the interview when Rage finally realised what was going on and told them to stick it? They were his words essa...his words.' I believe those were said simply out of the frustration of Boda leaving the team the The Enlightment having some struggles as of late...nothing more. 'Think what you will, moron, and I'll keep speaking the truth like a truly proud Spanish man. I bet your one of those many fools out there who believe that a Deville win is a for gone conclusion this week, aren't you?' I never said that... 'You don't have to say it! I can smell the attitude just illuminating off your very carcuss right now!' I believe that smell is you, Sanchez. 'No, that smell, is the smell I got earning my nickname with your wife early today. So shut your gringo trap up right now, before I have to shut it for you!' At this point I believe it's just best if we go to the rest of the promo for Mr. Morales and Los Diablos, since we are getting no where. 'Oh sure, holmes, change the subject when you find out that a fine and successful Spanish man has taken your wife into his bed and shown her the true meaning of Latin Loving...DIRTY SANCHEZ STYLE!' Why you!!!!! KA-BOOM!!!!!!!!!!!! (Voice over interruption: We're sorry folks. But we are experiencing some personal difficulties at this time and we'll just be moving along to the remainder of your Los Diablos promo. Thank you for you understanding and have a nice day.) ==== 'Did you see it? Did you see how great we were at Culture Shock?' The scene begins to focus in on the smiling and extremely excited face of Enrique Morales...the manager of Los Diablos and one of the thirty plus men and women to take part in the 2005 Duel Halo. He is jumping around, quite excitedly, in the same type of gear that he had worn into the Halo during his highly unexpected particpation. Morales: Boys we were all able to make a HUGE impact upon the face of PRIME with what we all accomplished in the Halo. You should be very proud of yourselves. He pats both men on their massive backs, as they sit foward on the incline benches they were busy working out on. Both men give him a nod in respect to his words. Angelus: Well, holmes, I didn't really accomplish much for us. I let you both down and I will not allow that to happen again. You two, however, really did Mexico proud with your showing. If not for that damned Motoki screwing you, Senor Morales, your strategy would have no doubt allowed for you to pick up a huge victory at Culture Shock. Morales frowns a bit at the thought of his perfect strategy being ruined by that devious Motoki, who had entered the Halo under a mask and a false personality. Morales simply shook his fist in the air slightly at the thought of it. Morales: It's no matter. We accomplished pretty much exactly what we set out to do. You two proved your domiance as a tag team by really dominating a great portion of the time you were in there together. And Baltasar...my boy...you more than proved you have to be one of the toughest and strongest men on the face of this earth. That ring shattering suplex you planted that big retard Eugene with was a sight to behold. Angelus: I agree...and I should know...I was on my way to the damn mat as well the whole time. Angelus just smirks a little bit at his ill fate in the Halo while Morales smiles wide at Baltasar. The Red Devil, however, isn't showing any signs of being in a celebratory mood of any kind. He simply stares past his friend and manager, as if he hadn't heard a word he was saying. Morales: Big man are you paying any attention to me! Baltasar snaps out of the semi-trance he was in at the sound of Enrique shouting at him. Baltasar: Sorry, senor, I was simply thinking about this weeks ReVolution. No time to really celebrate what is now in the past. Angelus: Baltasar you have nothing to ponder about this week. You've already seen first hand what Deville is all about. Morales: Angelus is quite right...Deville is talented, and I won't take that from him, but he isn't some great God of the ring like so many proclaimed him to be. Baltasar: Maybe so. The fact still remains that without myself and Angelus coming out and kick his ass, there was a real good chance that he would have eliminated Senor Rage...then possibly won the Halo all together. There must be a reason they call him the Devil himself. Angelus simply chuckles at the thought of anyone refering to Deville in the same sentence as Satan. While he chuckles he slaps his partner on the back and just smiles. Angelus: Cheap merchandise tag by Deville himself. That man isn't anything to fear when you step into the ring with him. Your the Red Devil and a much greater threat between the ropes than Deville will ever be. Baltasar: Possibly. But I'm a tag team specialist and you both know it. I haven't particpated in many one on one matches in my career. For my PRIME singles debut to come in such a high profile match up, well, frankly it's a bit of a overwhelming experience. Morales (cocking his eyebrow while speaking): Does this have something to do with your fear of Satan? If it does then you can put that worry to rest right now...Deville IS NOT Satan. Baltasar: Oh...he isn't? I guess you two truly believe the guy is all hype and no substance? Please don't try and paint Deville out to be some piece of crap, second rate wrestler, like Tessa Windsor. The man is for real and we all saw that in the Halo. I am not afraid of him, anymore than I am of anyone else with any wrestling company, but facing him is a challenge that I want to be well prepared for. Baltasar shoots up to his feet and heads toward the door, quite angry. Morales quickly jumps into action, shock written all across his face. Morales: Big man, chill out! I understand your nervous about making an impression on PRIME by defeating one of it's biggest names. But it's no reason to worry in this manner. You have the skills, strength, and determination to beat anyone in this world on any day of the week, let alone this company. Angelus: You know, brother, I'm always here to help you get prepared. I always got your back essa. Baltasar (Stopping at the door and looking back to his two friends and allies): I know. But this is a situation I have to work out in my mind before hand...and I have to do that on my own. Baltasar walks out of the door and heads back toward his room, three floors up from where their personal workout gym is located. Once he reaches his room he walks inside, closing and locking the door behind him, and then walks over and sits down on the end of his bed. He places his hands over his face and begins to shake his head slowly from side to side, as if trying to clear his head. Finally he simply lays back, placing his head on the pillow, and begins to think to himself about the upcoming ReVolution. { Angelo Deville...one of the biggest names in the history of professional wrestling. That is quite a draw for the first singles match of your career with a company. The kind of draw that could make or break a career. It's not even really about winning, necassarily, as simply pushing a man of Devilles caliber, to the limit, can easily make you a huge star...even in defeat. Fear...that isn't even a consideration, nor is worry. This week is all about respect and proving myself. I'm a tag team specialist and I don't claim to be anything else. But a victory this week...a victory would allow me to prove that I am a force with in PRIME to be taken seriously...even on my own. A guy like Deville he has everything. He is already considered to be one of the biggest threats in all of PRIME. He is able to just waltz into PRIME and have everyone talking about him, everyone writing stories about him, and everyone respecting his abilities...before he even steps foot in the ring for the first time with PRIME. The name Baltasar, however, hardly even seems worthy of a mention at this point...unless they are speaking about the ring shattering suplex in the Duel Halo. Is that really want I want to be remembered for? The fact that I suplexed a seven foot man with the brain of a four year old, and it caused a ring to shatter? Might sound like one of those moments you'd be happy to have your name remebered for...throughout the history of the PRIME federation or your own career. But...it's not a enough for me. Being thought of as a legit threat to not only the tag titles, that should be ours already anyway, but to the top prize in PRIME is what matters. Having a shot at the Univeral title, at this point, isn't what I am after. It's the idea, in everyones head, that if I had a shot at it I could win. The pride of Mexico is on my shoulders this week. The honor of a nation, the heart of a city, and the determination of a team is all riding on what type of showing I can provide for them. It may not seem like something to worry about for a near seven foot man...but it is. Being big doesn't mean having nothing to worry about or fear...just like being Mexican doesn't mean you have to be a damned luchadore. Beating Deville, this week, would instantly prove that Senor Morales was right all along. I could once and for all prove to the world that he was right in sticking to his principles of not becoming just another pinball Mexican. If I lose...the thoughts of that can't even be allowed into my mind. I must not allow a thought like that to break into my mind as I prepare for this huge responsiblity. Losing, quite honestly, isn't going to be an option for me this week. } A knock on his bedroom door shakes Baltasar from his thoughts. Looking at the door with a bit of confusion he yells out. Baltasar: Who is is? 'The Devil.' The words echoed through the door and into Baltasar's brain. Quickly his body froze...fear taking his body over. The one thing the big man had always feared, his whole life, had been Satan. Sure he called himself, 'The Red Devil'... and his team was known as The Devils...but simply put it was his own way of attempting to overcome his one nightmare. By becoming that nightmare himself. Mainly for the fear that with all of his lifes past actions he would eventually be ruled by the evil that was known as Satan, and the fear of having something rule him made him nuts. By becoming the nightmare which he had feared he had hoped to, at least in his mind, make himself more feared than Satan himself. Something that would make him believe, once again at least in his own mind, that Satan was somehow truly beneath him. At the moment, however, it appeared that it hasn't quite worked. Baltasar: Angelus, you prick, making comments like that will get your skull kicked in! 'Angelus isn't around right now, Baltasar...you puss.' The voice of the supposed Devil came through once again and this time angered Baltasar enough to make him shoot back. Baltasar: If your the Devil, and as bad as people claim you are, you'll just come through the door without me opening it. Baltasar smiles, content in the thoughts that he is simply freaking himself out once more with his age old fear, until his fears come true. Suddenly the Devil himself stands at the foot of Baltasar's bed, smirking at the giant man who is trembling in fear at the mere sight of him. 'Stop shaking like a little bitch!' Baltasar (Attempting to remain tough as possible): Go to hell! 'I've been there...done that...in fact I rule that.' Once again The Devil simply smirks and grabs ahold of the giant and yanks him to his feet. Quickly Baltasar, more out of fear than anything else, grabs the Devils hands and pulls them off of him. 'Don't try and fight me, boy, simply embrace the fact that I am real and I am superior to you in every single way.' Baltasar (looking at Satan as if he is nuts): Are you retarded? I know your real and that is why I am not exactly beaming with pride that your here right now. But I also must not allow myself to fear you. 'Oh you know, do you? Then why is it that the mere thought of me makes you sweat like a fat chick in the middle of a Billy Banks Boot Camp session?' Baltasar: Your evil...more than I am...more important in the fears of many. I don't want...I don't want to be ruled by you! 'Am I?' Baltasar looks at the Satan with some confusion. He hadn't expected to be asked that question, cause afterall, this was the Prince of Darkness...or so he had been lead to believe by all the hype and stories. Baltasar: It's what I have been led to believe...by the population of those who come before me. 'Do you always fear things cause someone tells you to?' Baltasar stopped, once more, to ponder this question. Once again The Devil had thrown him a curve ball that he hadn't quite expected. Finally he shook his head as he answered. Baltasar: No...I do not. 'Then why do so with me? Do you know me?' Baltasar: I don't know you personally. Though I feel like I have known you for years. Those damned stories, articles, and all the hype. Stupid people like you making you out to be the worst thing to ever walk the earth. 'I'm the stupid one? Your the one who is afraid of something that you've both never seen and have no idea of the validity of my hype and legendary stature, so to speak.' Baltasar: I can see that your real. I've seen first hand the type of being you are. I have been right there next to you and seen it. 'Have you?' Baltasar: Jesus Christ! No wonder Hoyt wants everyone to go to Heaven. Spending an eternity answering idiotic questions like this would be a horrible after life. The Devil gets a wide grin across his illuminated face. 'Then why do you feel the need to fear me?' Baltasar: Your supposedly something to be feared. A man, or enity, that is as evil and heartless as any to ever walk the face of earth. Would it not be stupid to completely ignore the facts that have been laid out in front of me? 'Don't always believe what your told, or what you read. If I was so 'evil' would I be playing second fiddle to God? Besides what facts have you really been given, other than words and warnings?' Baltasar ponders this question for a moment before he shakes his head, slowly, as The Devil, who is surrounded by a bright red glow, begins to grin. 'Exactly. Your fear and worry is manufactored by what others tell you to believe. Do you understand why you must stop being a silly bitch now?' Baltasar: Silly bitch? Holmes your about to really piss me off. 'Good. Your going to need that attitude.' Baltasar: Need it for what? 'To overcome this silly fear so that you can win against me this week.' Baltasar: Win against you this week? Baltasar once again looks on in confusion at the being before him who has plagued his thoughts for the longest time now. 'That's correct. For some reason I have become the enitity that you view everytime you are coming up against a challenge that you are worried about. Now you'll have to either fight me and win...or lose right now and be taken to the darkness.' Baltasar looks on, at first in fear, and then finally with a scowl as he stands to face his tormentor. Baltasar: I will not be ruled. Quickly the giant, mexican wrestling, superstar raises his fist as if to defend himself from the attack that he knows shall come from the evil before him. Prepared to finally, once and for all, defend himself from the fear and the history of the tormentor. With those words and Baltasar's actions, the room fills with a blinding red light and engulfs the entire screen and we can hear Baltasar screaming, not in fear, but the way a person would scream as they charged into war. Suddenly he shoots straight up in his bed, sweat pouring off of him, as he tries to adjust his eyes to the darkness in the room. Outside we can hear Angelus and Enrique screaming for Baltasar to unlock the door. Finally, with no response from their friend, Angelus busts down the door and the two storm in to find Baltasar sitting up in his bed and smiling. Angelus: You fuck! What the hell are you yelling like a Mexican revolutionist for? Morales: Are you alright, essa? Baltasar: Perfectly. The two simply stare at him for a moment before looking at him with great confusion. Angelus: Then what the hell were you yelling about? Baltasar: The Devil. Morales (with a smile): Another one of your nightmares? Baltasar simply grins before slapping his manager on the back. Baltasar: Not exactly. Angelus: What do you mean? Baltasar: Let's just say that I worked out that subconsious fear of Satan and The Deville all in one quick motion. There isn't anything to fear there...any longer. Morales and Angelus simply look at one another confused then shrug their shoulders as the scene begins to fade away and out on a shot of Baltasar smirking. A dark smirk... A Deville-ish smirk, you might even say.
