Argh, Canada

12 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/2005 ~Friendship. It has been said that friendship is the one thing that makes humans human. It separates humans from any other animal. If you would take away friendship, humans are not human but are just another species within the animal kingdom. Friendship makes human beings feel like life is truly the great odyssey of all time. When you break down all relationships in life, it comes down to one common denominator, friendship. Wives and husbands are friends. Fathers and sons, when you take away the blood, are friends. Brothers, sisters, and all remaining people in one's life are friends. Jon Kano and Chris Sloboda have been friends since the day they met. They have evolved in two different ways. Kano was the man during Chris Sloboda's first PCW tenure. Kano is the man who knocked Chris Sloboda out of PCW and down into PCW2. This is perhaps the best move Chris Sloboda ever made in his career. While Kano continued to reign terror in PCW, Chris worked in PCW2, and he worked hard. He worked harder then he ever worked in his life for anything. When Chris Sloboda returned to PCW, he was no longer the jobber known as Boda, but the superstar known as Boda. Kano and Boda feuded for a short while when Boda returned. But this was at a strange time in both men's careers. Ivan Stanislav had just come into the scene, formed the Red Army, and broken Kano's neck. When Boda joined the Red Army, Kano saw it as an act of treason against himself. Kano thought with his heart, instead of his mind, and went after his former best friend. After their infamous Cold War match, Kano and Boda reached an understanding. This match was so brutal that it has never been televised, both men bled profusely, both men gave it their all, and Kano, in his injured state with his C-4 vertabrae having a compression fracture, could not overcome Chris Sloboda on that day. Boda explained that the Red Army was just a wise career move, and Kano understood. It was not that long ago that Kano had made a similar choice by making the deal with the devil himself, Tyler Nelson. Kano and Boda soon renewed their friendship off screen, and when Boda finally broke away from the Red Army, Kano and Boda reeked havoc for a short while on PCW in celebration of the liberation of Boda. After PCW ceased operations early in 2004, the two friends went their different professional ways. They still kept in touch, but Boda went to PRIME while Kano stayed home. Kano felt he had to stay home, his wife was pregnant with their first child and he thought he needed to run Kano Enterprises a little more hands on than before. Kano wasn't even watching wrestling, he was retired and was perfectly content. He was a hall of famer, had held more titles than something utterly bombarded with titles, and had nothing left to prove. But just like the Mafia, once you thing you are out of wrestling, it pulls you back in. On this week's edition of ReVolution, PCW lives again as three of the most decorated PCW alumn and the son of a PCW legend engage in the first ever PCW Reunion match. Now PCW was known as for its innovation and entertainment value, and many expect this match to continue this style with many cumbustable elements in this match.~ *The scene opens up at the border of Canada (otherwise known as America, Jr.) and the United States of America. Jon Kano is driving his trademark jet-black 2005 Lincoln Navigator, with 'RLLGND' on the license plate, as he slows to be questioned by the border patrol. The rather large, disgustingly fatbodied border patrolman approaches Kano's Navigator as Kano rolls down the window.* Patrolman: Welcome to Canada, sir. As per certain safety precautions, there are certain questions I must ask you. Kano: That is fine. *The patrolman takes a quick peek into Kano's luxury SUV, just to survey the interior.* Patrolman: Sir, is your visit to the great country of Canada for business or for pleasure? *Kano stops to chuckle to himself.* Kano: A bit of both. I am up here for business, but I take great pleasure in what I do. Patrolman: And what is that, sir? Kano: I am a professional wrestler, among other things. I am sure you have seen me on television. My name is Jon Kano. Patrolman: I can't say that I have watched much professional wrestling since that incident in Montreal in '97. *Kano sighs.* Kano: Oh lord. Well, my current promotion is doing a show this week in which we have a match involving myself and two other wrestler whom have quite a history. We have known each other for quite some time and the other combatant in this match is the son of a man whom I know very well. All four of us will be going at it for the sake of old times. Patrolman: That is all well and good. Are you bringing any weapons into Canada? *Kano seems a bit annoyed that the patrolman could care less about Kano's particular situation.* Kano: Only myself. You see, there is only one objective in this match. A man whom is one of my opponents was an old friend. He was my best friend and he turned his back on me. He made me feel a pain in my soul that I haven't felt in years. I am a very forgiving person, but I cannot let this slide. This man got into my home, came within inches of my sleeping wife and child, and stole my most prized possession. My only objective is to make him scream like the bitch that he is. Patrolman: Yeah, I have noticed that you have several luggage bags in your vehicle, have you been in possession of these articles at all times? *Kano is even more annoyed at the patrolman's continued disrespect.* Kano: Yes, at all possible times. I don't take them with when I go to the bathroom. Anyway, is this about over? I really need to get back into Canada and get to the arena ASAP. Patrolman: I think I have all I need. Enjoy your stay in Canada. Kano: Yeah, right. *Kano now officially enters Canada and leaves the entrygate in the distance.* Kano: Chris, I am on my way. I really don't care about the two others in the match. Trashy and me get along just fine. Hoyt ain't even worth my time, plus it would be hard to explain to Duke why I beat the living hell out of his boy. As for you, Chris, we will dance the dance once again and there is no cage for you to fall through this time and pick up a tainted win. Chris, this will be a nice little appetizer for Colussus, won't it? You and me once again going for broke, wanting to make each other bleed and feel the pain again. Oh my God, Chris, you and I are definitely going to give everyone their money's worth. Chris, there is going to be one difference, though. This time, instead of you having your arm raised in victory, even as bullshit as that was, it will be I standing over you, as you bleed and gasp for air, wondering why you have done what you have done, as you have fallen victim to like so many others to me. I'll see ya at ReVolution, buddy o' mine. *The scene fades to black as Kano puts the pedal to the metal on his way to ReVolution, smiling the whole way, knowing that he will once again get his hands on Boda.* ~Jesus, with friends like Boda and Kano, who the hell needs enemies.~
