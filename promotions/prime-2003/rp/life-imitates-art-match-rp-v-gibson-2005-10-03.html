Date: 03/10/05 Life Imitates ArtWritten: 3rd October, 2005. 'And now I'm flying, like an angel, to the sun.My feet are burning and I grab into another world.' - Guano Apes. I - Between Embracing & Detaching. To some, wrestling is their life. Men like Brandon Youngblood, drawing pride from the honour of the wrestling business at it's most pure. The Five Star Title strapped around his waist is a stamp of approval from the wrestling business for his ways. Wrestling shouldn't need to be showy... it IS the show. His PRIME matches may only be 20 or 30 minutes out of the week, but every breath, every mouthful, every muscle flex beforehand is geared towards victory. Who would he be without the wrestling business? Maybe still Brandon Youngblood by name, but surely not by nature. Like a reptile's discarded skin, just a hollow casing with nothing alive inside. And then there are those to whom the wrestling business is peripheral. Striding into PRIME, the two words uttered in repetition by Danny Ferguson weren't 'Pro Wrestling', they were 'Hard Money.' Actor first, wrestler second, every greeting is more Hollywood than Madison Square Garden, every gesture more sound-stage than wrestling ring. Between the two extremes is a rare medium, where wrestling and life neither overlap or separate. As two separate entities, they share common ground. Each has their own sensations, rushes, territories... but that familiar feeling can't be shaken. Between embracing and detaching... is imitating. II - Only Just Breaking The Surface. Despite the raw, scorching heat of the sun, it took only the slightest of subtle breezes to trigger goose-bumps up Karina's bare forearms. Her naked feet danced on the sizzling stone, where just a few seconds of unbroken contact left skin feeling like it was about to blister. Perched on the edge of the rocks, emerald eyes were hypnotised by the shimmering waters beneath. The purest of copper-sulphate blues highlighted from beneath by vivid, natural green, all blended by reflective ripples across the surface. She could feel the contours of the cables through their plastic wrapping and her own thin sneaker soles. A night of sweat had left the coating slippery, and a discrete shuffle of the K-Wolf's feet was needed to react as the slight give of the tense ropes sent her toe shooting forward. A expectant, slow, rhythmic applause had already began as Karina's vision recoiled, up from the waters beneath, along the vines and overhanging branches, and back to where shocking pink toenails all but hooked around the ledge. After 2 hours, the compact building's air carried a stale, sweaty taste which infected even the atmosphere above the squared circle. With a slight stinging of her eyes leaving the K-Wolf squinting, she lined up the fallen opponent sprawled out on the stained canvas below. 10 minutes of kicks, knees and elbows had left his face swollen, and a slither of blood ran from a busted lip. With the plain black one-piece swimsuit clinging to her lithe, toned body, the K-Wolf straightened her form, tilting her head back for the sun to bathe it in dry heat. The remnants of a pink fringe that had squirmed free of its tie ticked the bridge of Karina's lightly freckled nose, but such a distraction was lost as nerves began to cloud her senses. Somehow, Wolfenden heard each drop of blood hit the canvas, amplified through her thoughts, bursting free of the murmur that had become of the fans' cheers. Gloved hands ran through her hair, scraping away the strands that had become matted down against perspiration-soaked skin, the arena not even producing a teasing hint of ventilation. As the claps increased in tempo, Kari's toes flexed nervously, before she shuffled that little bit further forward, so that the ball of her foot pressed on the curved angle of the drop. Feet secured themselves on the cables, leaving the K-Wolf perfectly balanced, able to extend her frame fully. Over the past hour, she'd watched dozens make the same cautious walk along the sizzling rocks to the ledge, before shaking their head and backing away... forfeiting a slice of pride rather than a whole chunk of their health. A confident nod, despite pounding heartbeat and fluttering stomach, told the spectators that they weren't in for another disappointment. As the crowd continued to grow in volume, the K-Wolf extended her arms from her sides, conducting the symphony of pops and cheers. With a pointed finger on each leather-encased hand, the K-Wolf motioned for a corkscrew, letting the fans know what they were in for. Thumbs hooked the straps of her swimsuit, pulling them an inch or so further in from her shoulder, before he explosive knees that helped propel her endless legs bent. Always self-conscious, Wolfenden took a moment to check her pants were still tied tightly, before her explosive frame recoiled back towards the ring post, like an elastic band being wound tightly. With the clapping reaching its crescendo, and Karina recoiled, the starting gun went off through her mind, causing her to explode out. Flinging herself from the rocks, Kari tucked her tall, almost awkward frame in, corkscrewing through the arid air. The fierce sun was such that she could follow her rotations by which part of he body was touched by the heat. Heaving in lungfuls of tepid air, the K-Wolf launched, flinging her fragile-looking frame high up into the arena. With eyes staying open, her vision became a wash of blinding white, cobalt blue, and the organic weave of the surrounding trees. As the volume of the crowd swirled around in her head, the K-Wolf's rapid rotation flashed harsh arena lights and dull grey canvas in front of her eyes, until they merged into a blur. Her rapid rotations left Karina only hearing the whistle of displaced air, any claps or cheers from above drowned out before they even hit her ear. The faster she spun on her athletic body's axis, the further the volume of the crowd decreased, until only the whistle of her own rotation could be heard. Battling against the often unstoppable momentum of her mind and body, Kari burst herself out of the corkscrew, shooting into a rigid arrow as she locked onto the deepest part of the water below. Feeling muscles tense to almost painful extremes, even Kari's teeth pressed achingly tight against one another so not even the strongest breeze swirling through the rocks would knock her a millimetre off course. In her head, Karina counted down the number of spins she knew herself capable of, usually dynamic body frozen in flight, tight, not wanting the merest muscle flex to affect her rotations. Eyes hadn't blinked since her feet had left the baking stone above, and despite ploughing down through a thin blanket of insects that migrated to the pool below, they refused to close as the shimmering surface below tried to dazzle the young woman plummeting down to break it. The already hefty man below grew large in Kari's sights as she pulled her shoulders in tighter to her body, eyes looking hopeful that she'd manage the last few degrees to land the move cleanly. Then, as she could almost catch the scent of the water through the air ravaging her nostrils, the K-Wolf battled to push her eyelids together against the force of the displaced air. For a split second, the water's surface felt like concrete as her tense fingertips impacted against it, before the flat javelin on the K-Wolf's body punctured the surface tensions. Twisting under the swelling sun had felt like being roasted on an open fire, so the cool, shadowed water below felt icy against the extreme. As the heavenly lights above threatened to pierce through her retinas, the K-Wolf went limp, just a fraction of a millisecond before her body ploughed down into her opponents chest. Even with her body loosened to prevent any impact injuries to herself, Karina could feel the natural give of the human body, before defiant ribcage sent it grounding to a halt. Blanketed senses then drifted into deprivation as only the calming whoosh of the water soothed its way through Karina's head. With her plummeting weight then supported by the purest of waters, Kari simply went limp, letting herself float back to the surface, at peace. The air was expelled forcefully from Wolfenden's lungs upon impact, leaving a heady feeling creeping in as she remained atop her beaten opponent, sensation only heightened by the spotty glow of the lights above dancing across her vision. A gasp of air was then expelled as her head finally broke back through the rippling surface, eyes shooting open as stale air was quickly replaced by a quenching heave. Spread-eagling on the surface, Karina basked in the sensation as the cheers from those watching above filtered down to her. As Kari freed her hair from its tie, blonde and pink blanketed the surface, forming a crest above her head as she just looked back up at the beaming faces peering over the rocks. She could feel the slapping of the referee's hand against canvas resonate up through her opponent's body, the count in time with his slowed heartbeat. The eruption of the crowd sent a warm feeling of comfort out from the K-Wolf's stomach, spreading through her body to massage tired muscles. 'I so need to do this every week...' near-breathless words were expelled in the enthusiasm that was hypercharging her respiratory system and metabolism. 'That was better than sleep...' went the joyous exclamation as lungs burned and yearned for air to fill them once more. The subtlest kicks of her feet then propelled Karina to the pools edge, where her slender form wriggled up onto one of the rock formations bordering the crystal waters in time to watch the cannonballing body of one of the locals explode against the surface, showering her once again with the pool's purity. III - Hustle, Young Woman... Hustle. The smooth, varnished wood of the pool cue glided back and forth against the soft skin of Karina Wolfenden's chin as she lined up her shot. Just a few inches away from her cueing arm was a stack of bills that had grown by three lots of double-or-quits, each at the expense of the seemingly off-form K-Wolf. As an ominous omen of bad fortune, the jukebox took leave of 70's rock to pollute her ears with country, while the confident smirk of her opponent infected her peripheral vision. As if their bodies carried opposite charges, Karina Wolfenden spun and ducked through the blows being flung her way. Bulbous, clubbing fists swung at her head and body, but every time an arch or twist left nothing but a breeze making contact with the Negasonic Lupine's toned body. 'We ain't got all day, Missy,' his raw southern accent warned, cowboy boots shuffling him further around the table, following a gradient of expectation towards the money. 'Fuckin' stand still,' his frustrated drawl ordered, tired wings becoming increasingly wild as he desperately tried to finish off his plucky opponent. Kari's eyes subtly reacted to the patronising words, narrowing in greater focus as her aim began to zero in. Minute adjustments hovered the tip of her cue around the cue ball, before her cueing motion again heralded her shot. Seemingly reacting quicker than he could think, the K-Wolf's lithe frame danced through his arms, waiting for just the right moment, and thinking enough moves ahead to know exactly when that would be. Having left the K-Wolf with no clear sight of the 4 ball, the man tried to stifle a throaty, mocking chuckle, rubbing the thick, harsh stubble that covered his chin... looking as if it would sand any cue rubbing against it. Only the man resting against the ring apron, zooming in on Kari's face with his handheld camera, caught the confident smile she flashed as she closed to within a Matrix arch and a defecting spin of her trap being sprung... the opponent staggering blindly into it. Then, quicker than he could shoot his eyes open in astonishment, the K-Wolf rose from her tight cueing stance, and casually drilled the tip of her cue down harshly against an extremity of the cue ball. Her opponent's jaw lowered further with each degree that the cue ball curled back, catching the 4 ball at the precise angle to propel it into the corner pocket. Trying to keep up the confident demeanour, the man went to throw in a cocky remark, only for the topspin that Karina channelled into the ball to bring it back to life, shooting towards the 9 ball with just enough power to send it trickling into the adjacent pocket. With a desperate lunge, he tried to grab a hold of his elusive opponent, only for the K-Wolf to leapfrog his lowered, bulky body, hooking her powerful legs under his unsavoury armpits. Catching him well and truly off-guard and equally off-balance with the strength contained in her athletic thighs, Karina snapped him over onto his shoulders as she propelled herself forward, landing seated. The smug grin she'd been saving for the last half hour finally broke across the K-Wolf's pale pink lips as she yanked her hair free of it's tie, then scooped up the winnings up from the table. Before the lumbering ape of an opponent even realised his shoulders were down, the referee had slid down beside them and fired off the deciding three count, much to the delight of the fans. 'Well,' she began to mock in his own, over-exaggerated accent. 'I ain't got all day.' 'Still enough for yah?' went the taunt Kari was too petty to hold back. After waving him goodbye with a hand stuffed full of notes, the K-Wolf slipped the winnings down into her figure-hugging leather jacket's pocket, then yanked the zipper up to just under her chin. Those who'd watched on from their bar stools, always entertained by the routine they saw so often, gave knowing nods to the departing K-Wolf, having seen the outcome from the second money touched table. After rolling free of his breathless, bemused body, the K-Wolf executed a lightning-fast kip-up, returning her worn sneaker soles to the equally worn canvas. The fans, all too used to watching Wolfenden lead a hopeful opponent on a merry dance, rose to applaud, having seen the end coming the second Karina convinced the man that she was reacting to him, and not the other way around. 'Hey, no fuckin' way,' he growled as the realisation hit him like a slap across the stubbled face. 'I ain't goin' down ta that...' he growled, shoving the referee down to the canvas. With thunderous steps, he stormed towards Karina, then thrust both hands into the K-Wolf's back, sending her stumbling forward and crashing into a vacant table. Even through the padding of her lip, one of Kari's lower teeth jarred against an ashtray, while the bitter, desiccating taste of ash polluted her mouth. Before the rise in crowd volume could warn her, Karina felt a clubbing forearm smash down across the back of her neck, its momentum then being channelled through her own body. Sent flying forward, Karina's mouth smashed against the turnbuckle, feeling the bolt smash against an incisor as the stale taste of the pad drew a sour look across her face. 'I think I'll be takin' that back,' he advised Wolfenden, a little part of his inner-sense wondering why nobody was stepping in to be chivalrous. 'We have a sayin' back in mah home fed,' was spat out with a scowl. 'If yah the only one standin', yah tha real winnah.' Spitting out a wincing mouthful of putrid, grey saliva, Kari clamped her fingers around the side of the ashtray, then counted heavy steps towards her. 'Now, let's not make a scen-' 'Now, just do yahself a favah an' fall down,' went mocking words of advice as he strode up behind the stunned K-Wolf. With as much spite as vindication, Karina flung her right hand viciously towards the man's groin, ploughing the corner of the square ashtray into his unfortunate genetalia. As the weapon dropped onto the dusty floorboards with a satisfying clunk, all her defeated opponent could do was let out a gurgling wheeze, while vomit slowly refluxed up his gullet, joined by tears welling up in previously angry eyes. Hands that could smother Karina's face then reached for her, only for Kari to spin on a pin-point, pumping out her potent right foot into her beaten opponent's groin. Any male within sight of the kick instantly winced, crossing their legs as they suddenly felt uncomfortable sitting. To his knees the man dropped, pleading eyes staring up at Karina, who shot an anything but benevolent look back in return. With dry heaves, he tried to drag himself up, only for Karina's foot to threaten a second blow, causing him to wisely back down and focus on whether or not he still had the most important parts of his reproductive system in solid form. Both the man's hands grabbed his groin out of instinct, but nothing he could do was going to dull the agonising throb that crept up into his abdomen, leaving an acidic taste sweetening his mouth. Her shoulders then circled within her tight jacket, as if skin was creeping under where his unwelcome hands touched. Not quite having finished with him yet, Karina dropped into her kick stance, and went to fling an angry right foot towards his chin... only for the man to wince away, before coughing up a stomach full of vomit onto the floor. The pulsating techno onslaught of 'Velocity Shift' then blasted out around them, the bass resonating up through the ring only causing more discomfort for Kari's fallen attacker. Without wasting so much as a word on him, Kari simply dropped out through the ropes, patting her mouth for blood as she retreated to the sanctuary of the curtain. Plucking a loose note from her pocket, the K-Wolf slapped it down on the bar to cover the cleaning, before making a wordless exit, effortlessly drifting back into low-key. IV - Achy, Wakey Heart. In the dreamworld behind her eyelids, Karina Wolfenden was oblivious to the waking world outside. Few people could find comfort in a discarded packing crate and a cheap plasterboard wall, but somehow, Karina Wolfenden managed. With a mass of pillows fighting her for bed space, slumber rocked Karina, while fidgeting limbs wrapped the sheets in knots around her. Through the protective cushion of sleep, the lashing of rain against the windows was muffled out, while a defiant roar of thunder prompted nothing more than fingers gripping pillow tighter for a split second. With a balled-up sweater as a pillow, Karina embedded herself on the nook between crate and wall. The ambient euphoria from the earphones which had acted as a sedating lullaby had been drowned out by the murky silence of slumber, which managed to filter out any footsteps and passing, throwaway conversations. Karina had drifted so deep into sleep that it would take the sky being torn open and the Second Coming of Christ arriving in all its heavenly splendour to even prise eyelids apart. Distant curtain calls slithered through the labyrinth-like backstage corridors, but were instantly repelled by the subconscious sentries that guarded the K-Wolf's sleep. But, retreated comfortably within herself, the K-Wolf wasn't privy to the knowledge of her alarm clock as it counted, ominously, down the seconds to midday. Each re-arrangement of LED numbers mattered little to Wolfenden, until the 5 and the 9 became two lots of 0, and a deafening alarm tone to rival that of the most shrill Banshee ripped through the world Karina had immersed herself in under the covers, shattering the dreamscape around her. The calls zeroed in on Karina, until the stressed-out PRIME employee all but had to yell directly into Karina's ear. Eyes shot open as fists clenched by instinct, before the familiar, sterile, pale beige of backstage soothed the racing senses. 'C'mon,' he then ushered with a clap of the hands that carried way too much pep for the waking Wolfenden's liking. 'Act like you're employed.'
