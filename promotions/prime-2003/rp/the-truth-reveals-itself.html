The Truth Reveals Itself

Date: ReVolution 161 A bird sits silently on the window sill, eyeing its prey rather carefully. With a steady eye, the hawk never flinches while the mouse below is ignorant to the fact. Within a blink of an eye the bird spread its wings, soars through the air, and snatches the rodent with its talents before returning to the window sill it was previously perched on. As the bird lands on the sill, his neck turns from left to right a few times curious at the sight of the open window and the man lying on the ground. He’s not moving as the bird hops through the window and looks down at the man. Maybe the bird doesn’t understand the entire scope of everything going on around it, but the imagery is unique enough for it. Seconds later, the bird is flying towards the man, mouse in tow, and lands on the back of the man. TAP! TAP! TAP! The powerful beak of the hawk slams itself into the back of the motionless man. As the violent poking rips through the man’s nervous system his head starts to poke up, trying to figure out what exactly is going on around him. The palm of his left hand moves slowly to his forehead before rubbing it to get rid of the headache from sleeping on the hard floor. His eyes move around the room swiftly before he turns over slightly to find the hawk with mouse in talons there. Not willing to believe his squinting eyes at first he opens them to fully reveal the burning red eyes there. The sight of the eyes sends the hawk flying and it lands on top of the man’s bed. The bed of one Craig Maloof. “What the—,” he begins to say before the headache he’s experiencing causes him to bury his head into the hardwood floor. With his head buried there, questions begin to surface to the top of his thoughts. Why is he here on the floor? What happened the day before? Slowly, he raises his head again, and looks up to his bed where the hawk is eyeing him. “I’d be careful making me your prey.” His voice is stern as he grabs the foot of his bed and pulls himself up to his feet. He looks around, his muscles sore, and his mind seemingly blank. A feeling he had once experienced and one that he wasn’t interested in returning too. No, he tells himself, he couldn’t be experiencing amnesia once again. For one, he remembers who it is that he is, and knows certain details such as that this is his house and is aware of his surroundings. No, for some reason he just doesn’t vividly recall the day before. His brow furrows as he tries to recall the day before. It only takes a few moments though before he hears the voice of the doctor invading his mind. ”You only have a year to live.” As the memory comes back to life, he can remember the calmness he originally felt, and then the planet-shattering pain that he felt in his heart. He then flashes back to when he received the phone call to come to the doctor’s office, but then there’s just blackness there. He digs deeper, hoping that the loose memory will show itself, but nothing’s there. He honestly doesn’t recall why that memory is missing. Quickly, he returns to the moments after he left the doctor’s office, and remembers walking into his house. Then, he sees Katie approaching him with an accusation, but the words are missing. Craig can see her lips moving, but it’s just not there. He grips the foot of the bed and his fingernails begin to dig deep into the wood leaving indents there until it rips through every other thought he has at the moment. ”What are you hiding? Haven’t you done enough lying already?” The lying. He had for so long been lying to everyone around him. For five months now, he hadn’t been able to tell the truth as to what he was suffering from or even his true relationships. Nicole was pregnant with his child and he couldn’t take her back for fear that he would be dead sooner rather than later and didn’t want to hurt her more than he already had and would do so in the future. Craig hadn’t been able to reveal his darkest secrets to those closest to him, but that would change. Except, he had already come to the house with that intention before being accused, and he wasn’t certain as to what had happened afterwards. “UGH!” Craig yells out in frustration before pounding his fist into the foot of the bed causing the entire bed to shake. Yet, the hawk doesn’t move an inch as the now lifeless mouse just hangs from its talons. Craig glances at the hawk and shakes his head. “How’d you get in my room?” Almost as if the hawk understands him, the hawk looks to his left and seemingly points to the open window. Craig glances at the window and shakes his head as he begins to walk away from the bed. He stops dead in his tracks though and looks back at the hawk with great interest. “You seem to be rather comfortable.” He walks around the room a little more while he feels his head begin to mount with thoughts. Too many thoughts. “Why can’t I remember things? Why am I blacking out? This fucking disease, for far too long, has dictated my life. My style. My attitude. The past few weeks I’ve been angrier than ever before and pushing people who I consider close to me away from me. At PRIME, I’m more destructive then I could’ve possibly imagined and here at home, I can’t share my heart with those who want to help me. Now, I can’t remember what I told them last night. I can’t remember if I saw Katie before I went to the doctor’s, and I couldn’t tell you why. I couldn’t tell you if it’s the disease or something else, and it’s starting to piss me the fuck off!” His final words are nearly at a shouting level and as he feels the anger rise all around him, he looks back at the bed to see the hawk still sitting there and Craig rolls his eyes. “And I’m talking to a hawk. Great.” Craig then begins to walk towards the bathroom. “Don’t get mouse blood all over my sheets or else we’re going to have a problem.” His left hand grabs the door as he walks into his bathroom and slams the door shut. The hawk just sits there for a moment, never flinching, before flying out of Craig’s bedroom. An interesting morning. ~~~ “What do you mean you’re not ready?” A man stands in the center of a small, dark room, with a long, narrow knife in his left hand. That knife is resting on a rectangular table as the man examines it with a cautious eye wanting perfection from the knife. Sitting across him is a bald-headed man, a little older, and with a long scar that starts at his forehead and runs vertically to his chin. You can see the impatience in his eyes as he looks at the fitter man. “I mean, that this isn’t a process you just rush into, Malik. You don’t just jump into everything and hope it all works out for the best. Not with him. You have one shot at this and that’s it. If you don’t do this correctly then everything will be lost, and Malik, you’ve paid me a lot of money to make sure that this isn’t fucked up.” Malik nods his head for a brief moment before walking up to the man as he continues to work on his blade. “Then, what’s the plan, Draifus? What’s the plan?” Draifus then puts the blade down gently and looks up at Malik. “My plan, Malik, is one that will give you much pleasure when it’s all said and done, and you’ll be able to go back to your boss with the sweet sound of success. You hired me for a job, let me do it.” Malik smiles as he looks at Draifus before walking around the table and slamming his left foot into the back of Draifus’s knee bringing the hired killer to both of his knees and his jaw slams into the wooden table. “You’ll have to excuse the lack of respect here, Draifus, but my boss is a rather important person. Yet, the problem that we’ve ran into calls for a swift response to. He’s not an impatient man, but in this instance, he’s not willing to wait any longer. For far too long, he’s seen this man live his life as if there’s nothing wrong in it, and that can’t happen any longer. He must be taken out and your little games are not going to last much longer if you don’t indeed take care of it. Do you understand me?” Draifus rests there for a moment, the anger doing its best to fight to the surface, but he somehow manages to push it to the side. His head tilts back and he looks at Malik for a long, hard second before responding to him. “I understand. Now, you’d do well to get off of me, and allow me to get back to work.” Malik simply nods his head before removing his foot, and Draifus climbs back to his feet. He stares Malik in the eyes for another second before surrendering that respect to Malik. The scar on Malik’s face says it all and tells of the long, arduous road that the man has indeed traveled. He grabs the blade and presents it to Malik. “This blade, I promise you, will have his blood in due time. Due time. Tonight is just the beginning and he won’t know what hit him. It won’t be enough to take him down, but it’ll be enough to rattle him. Something that hasn’t been done in a long time as you will remember.” A smile creeps across Malik’s face. “That’s why I came to you. I look forward to hearing the news later on tonight.” Malik then turns on his heel and walks away from Draifus. Meanwhile, Draifus continues to glare at the serrated blade. “And here shall lay the blood of Craig, the fool who thought he could get away.” Then, in one swift movement, he takes the blade and stabs it into the wood with pure frustration. ~~~ Night time in Los Angeles is well alive. A premiere in Hollywood has captivated a town that has long left them cold to the average celebrity. Yet, on a small street away from the glare of millions of eyes, rests a small bar that finds a celebrity of its own walking in, hiding from the naked eye. He glances at a few of the patrons before finding a place at the bar and waits patiently for a bartender to approach him. His left hand rests on the top of the bar uncomfortably as it continues to shake back and forth from the nerves of the day behind him. The day had left him wondering what he had said to Katie and Nicole as the two had been oddly absent from the house throughout the day. “Hey, Craig.” His head snaps up immediately as he looks at the bartender and finds it to be the same girl who had helped him the last time he was here. “Kristen, didn’t think I’d see you here tonight.” She just chuckles before shrugging her shoulders. “I don’t have much of a life outside of this bar to be quite honest. It’s just who I am, you know?” Craig looks around and then back at Kristen before shaking his head. “Not the kind of place that I’d center my life around if you ask me.” She inches forward to look Craig dead in the eyes. “Who was asking?” “Point to you.” “Thanks. What can I get for you?” Craig looks at the selection behind her before settling on his usual favorite. “Let me get a Rum and Coke.” With the mere mention of the words, she grabs a glass and fills it up with the aforementioned with drink before setting it down for Craig. He wraps his hand around the glass and takes a sip from it before feeling all of his nerves relax. He closes his eyes for a moment and when he opens them back up he sees Kristen still standing there. “Looks like you’ve had a rough time since I last saw you. Everything okay?” He takes a moment before answering her. “It’s been rough. Life just hasn’t been what I’ve expected from it I guess and now I’m losing out memories.” “That’s… interesting.” Her words are uneasy and don’t come right away. She looks at Craig with a sincere interest in her eyes before moving towards him a little bit more. The bar is relatively empty at this point and gives her the freedom to focus her attention on Craig. He lowers his head as he looks at the alcohol. “I wasn’t ever handed the easiest life, Kristen. I had to fight for what I believed in, and that’s affected who I am and what I do. One of those things is that I don’t tend to trust people very easily and when something dangerous to me is about to happen, I begin to push them away. The only problem with that is that the latest problem that I’m facing isn’t one I should be handling by myself. It’s one that requires the help of those that I care about.” She nods her as Craig takes a sip of his drink. “One of those girls the one having your kid?” Craig nods his head. “Yeah. Yesterday, I found out a piece of news that could change my entire outlook on life, and I remember going to them to tell them that piece of news, but then it’s black. I don’t remember if I told them and I can’t tell if it’s because of what I’m dealing with or if it’s something else. It’s troubling and I wish I could’ve told them before hand, but I didn’t. Because of whom I am. But, why would it be that I can’t remember telling them?” For a moment, Kristen just stands there, looking off in the distance as if she’s no longer interested in what he has to say. That moment passes though as Kristen looks back at Craig and smiles at him. “Maybe it’s this thing that’s changed your entire life or maybe it’s because you’re scared of hurting them, and you’re blacking out those painful memories. Maybe you’re blocking out those memories so you don’t have to remember hurting them. It’s natural, but you can’t shy away from those memories. You have to open yourself up or you’ll die alone. It sounds like to me that you have people who care about you and you shouldn’t die alone. It’s not fitting for someone like you.” Craig sits there, lost and aimless. He takes a look at his drink before looking back at Kristen. “How?” “Go to them and tell them the truth. Open yourself up, and you’ll see the truth staring you right in the face of what happened. It’s there Craig, just waiting to be unlocked.” The words sit with him for a moment before he stands up and looks at Kristen. “Thanks.” She just offers a smile as he looks around at the nearly empty bar and just shakes his head. He couldn’t believe that this was the place he had found some sense of direction and guidance, but he had. As he looks back at Kristen, he sees something in her eyes. Something he hadn’t seen in quite some time. A blood thirst. ~~~ “Well?” Malik sits behind a small table, a glass of scotch resting on the table. In front of him is Draifus, and as Malik sits there, he’s waiting for some kind of answer that will give him the feeling that Draifus had promised him. “The man sent after Craig, he’s missing. I don’t know where he is.” Malik just shakes his head as he looks at Draifus. “Strike one, Draifus. Strike one.” Malik then stands up and takes the blade out of Draifus’s hands. He looks at it for a second before slamming the tip of the blade into Draifus’s shoulder. A loud yell is released from his lips as he falls to his knees. Malik then moves his lips right to his ear. “Don’t fail us again or next time it shall be worse. I promise you.” Malik then leaves the bleeding Draifus to himself as he walks out of the room. ~~~ Craig stands outside of his own house, scared, and alone. For so long he had held his secrets to himself, and for the first time in a long time, he would have to let them loose. No longer could he hide from them or hope to protect those around him. Now, he would have to believe in that they were indeed strong enough to help him shoulder this burden. With a year to live, he would have to start doing some things differently. This was one of them. His hand grips the doorknob and he turns it slightly before walking into the house. The light in the kitchen is on and he takes a deep breath as he closes the door behind him and walks towards the kitchen. Sitting in there is Katie and Nicole, and as he looks at them, he can see some apprehension on their faces. “Hey.” His voice quivers from the fear he’s feeling right now. “You calm down?” The shot hits him hard as Katie starts to pull back the shroud of what happened the night before. He still doesn’t remember anything though, but he accepts that whatever it was wasn’t what he had intended. “Yeah. It’s time that I’m open and honest with you two. There’s something that I should’ve told you a long time ago, and I just haven’t. For fear or what not, I should’ve told you.” Nicole looks at Craig carefully before walking up to him. “What is it?” He licks his lips and swallows hard before looking at Katie and then back at Nicole. “Five months ago, I was diagnosed with Multiple Sclerosis. Yesterday, I found out I have a year left to live.” ~~~ The night has ended for Kristen as she locks the door to the bar. Empty at last she thinks to herself before walking to a small closet by the back door. She opens it up and inside of it is a man bound and gagged with fright in his eyes. She smiles at him before walking up to him. “You think you’re going to hurt Craig? Think again.” Her words are strong as she moves within inches of him. “I’m sending a message to your boss. Make sure you remember this one carefully.” She then removes the gag from the man’s mouth. He looks at her, not moving, wondering to himself what it is that she’s going to do. Kristen then licks her lips before moving in for a kiss on the man. At first, the man seems to enjoy it, but the longer it goes on, the more it feels like something is wrong. His eyes grow large as he looks at Kristen, wondering what it is that she’s doing to him. Then, all that’s heard is a thunderous clap. And he stops breathing. To. Be. Continued.
