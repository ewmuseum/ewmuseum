Date: June 17th, 2008 The deepest human defeat suffered by human beings is constituted by the difference between what one was capable of becoming and what one has in fact become. ---Ashley Montagu The last few weeks have been difficult ones for Colby Korver. But it wasn’t supposed to be this way. It was supposed to be different. Although Korver and his partner Hank Cobb have competed in other federations: Lights Out Wrestling; the Atlantic Wrestling Club; and GLOBAL; for one reason or another they have never realized their full potential. It may have been inexperience. It may have been have been nerves. Hell, it may have been alcohol. But whatever the case may have been, when the team signed contracts with PRIME, it was with the intention of leaving their failures far behind. But thus far, this has not been the case. And it is certainly not the first time that Colby Korver in particular has tasted the bitter elixir of loss, loss that he himself must shoulder the ultimate responsibility for. You see, Colby Korver hasn’t always been the poster boy for alcohol abuse and notches on a bedpost. Not so long ago, in a high school not so very far away, Colby Hughes Korver was the boy most girls are still waiting to sweep them off their feet. As a junior, he captained the football team as its starting middle linebacker and dated the head cheerleader. Although many assumed otherwise, he remained abstinent voluntarily, controlling his hormones for the good of his future. He spent Sunday mornings in church and Sunday afternoons at her family’s lake house. It was the ideal relationship. They never fought. They never cheated. They never knew saw it coming… Then along came senior year, and with it, Brooke Baker. The product of a broken home, broken parents and a broken public school system, Brooke Baker just in time for the start of the school year, but not soon enough to select her class preferences. So the guidance counselor did what she could, and just managed to squeeze in one final elective, 7th period Athletics class. And while Korver and the rest of the football team used this time to lift weights and watch game film, Brooke Baker used it for something entirely different. She was there to find her own golden ticket. And her very first day, as she watched Korver nearly pass out after squatting an inhuman amount of weight, only to bring his teammates to tears under a minute later with a timely quip, she knew that he was as golden as they came. And so her relentless pursuit began. When her seemingly innocent flirting came up empty, she moved on to “accidental” brushes of the skin and more blatant sexual innuendos. When those were ignored, she would leave notes in his locker detailing the “perks” of giving in to her wiles. But amazingly enough, Colby Korver did not concede, even under a barrage of propositions and suggestions the likes of which would make the cast of Nip/Tuck blush. And if only the story ended there, Korver probably would have lived happily ever after with his beautiful wife, a white picket fence and 2.5 children. But Brooke Baker didn’t believe in fairy tales. And like many stories, this one shall be told…in due time. The turning point in the process of growing up is when you discover the core of strength within you that survives all hurt. ---Max Lerner, The Unfinished Country, 1950 So with Korver’s heavy heart worn so prominently on his sleeve, it is no wonder that Hank Cobb has taken note and called an impromptu timeout during their training session to question his partner. Hank Cobb: Korver, what’s gotten into you man? I know the road’s been a little rough these last few weeks, but it’s like Coach Spear used to say, "You’re never gonna win ‘em all, but that don’t mean you shouldn’t play like it.” Or somethin’ like that. You know I’m ‘yer boy, so what’s the deal? Colby Korver: I told you not to worry about it. It’s just something I’ve gotta deal with on my own. It has nothing to do with you anyway, so let it go. Hank Cobb: Nothin’ to do with me? Don’t forget we’re a team, Korv. We got a big match coming up on ReVolution and a losing streak to get off our backs. We’ve been wrasslin’ like a couple of blind baboons lately and I’m gettin’ pretty sick of it. Colby Korver: Well no shit Cobb. But you going Jack Bauer on me isn’t going to fix that either. So unless you’ve got a matter of national security you haven’t told me yet, I strongly suggest you get out of my face. Hank Cobb: Listen jackass, you know I’m just tryin’ to help you out. I know how hard it can be to get over someone like Brook… Colby Korver: You finish that sentence and I swear to god I’ll knock your big ass the fuck out right here and now. Hank Cobb: Alright, alright. I’m done. This obviously ain’t helpin’. But then again, neither is the pity party you’ve been hosting for the last couple of weeks. I’m fixin’ to get back to work, but you better get ‘yer ass in gear or the only company you’ll be keepin’ is ‘yer buddies in the unemployment line. I’m out of here. Colby Korver: Don’t worry about me; I’ll be ready to fight. Hank Cobb: It ain’t ‘yer body I’m questioning. You just better get ‘yer head screwed on straight. And fast. Colby Korver: The only screwing you need to worry about are me and my fucking groupies after I drop the bomb on those bitches tomorrow night. Hank Cobb: I hope so, boss. Colby Korver: Don’t hope. Know. I fear we have awakened a sleeping giant and filled him with a terrible resolve. ---Admiral Isoroku Yamamoto, December 7, 1941
