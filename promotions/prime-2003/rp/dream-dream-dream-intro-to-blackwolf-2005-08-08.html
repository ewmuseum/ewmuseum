Dream, Dream, Dream (Intro to Blackwolf)

8 August 2005

Date: 8/8/05 'Please, don't do this! Please I don't deserve this' It was happening again, the dream that he had in his head for the past 6 years. He could see his parents alone with that madman. 'SHUT UP AND GET DOWN! ON THE FLOOR NOW! BOTH OF YOU!' The crazed lunatic was pointing the loaded Smith and Wesson .22 Magnum at his mom's forehead. 'Please, we have a son, he'll have nothing left. We're all he has! 'I SAID DOWN! NOW! He switched his aim so it was now pointing at the head of his father, his hand was twitching slightly. 'Please, you don't need to do this!' Mark was starting to rustle around, trying to find a way to stop what was going on or wake up somehow. But it was no use 'FREEZE! DROP THE WEAPON! GET DOWN ON YOUR KNEES AND PUT YOUR HANDS IN THE AIR!' The Police had arrived and we're now stationed outside the bank as the commanding officer screamed out into his megaphone. There we're three Police cars all stationed outside the bank and the officers we're pointing their firearms straight through the bank window. 'Listen to them, you don't need to do this! I can give you everything I..... 'SHUT UP!' The lunatic's hand was twitching even more now as he pointed it back at he's Mother's head. 'Please, I..... 'SHUT UP! BACK AWAY AND GET DOWN!' Tears began to roll down his mother's face as the lunatic, clocked his gun back, loading a bullet into the chamber. 'Please, just give me the..... 'DROP THE WEAPON OR WE'LL BE FORCED TO SHOOT! The commanding officer was now becoming as nervous as the three stationed inside the bank, he already had two deaths on his hands and he didn't want another two to add to his record. '.....gun! Please you don't need to do..... 'BACK OFF! GET DOWN ON THE FLOOR!' The madman continued to switch aims from father to mother and back again not sure what to do. They weren't doing as he ordered, but he also didn't want to be caught shooting two innocent victims in full view of the Police. 'Its over.....there's nothing you can do! Give yourself..... 'DROP THE WEAPON AND GET ON YOUR KNEES! NOW! 'Please, listen to them. Its over! You can't do..... BANG! His mother hit the deck as the gun fired off, smoke billowing out of the chamber that had just released the deadly bullet. 'NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!! His father leaped forward to try and grab the gun from the assailant, wrestling back and forth to try and free the gun from the man's hands. The lunatic cocked the gun again, releasing another bullet into the chamber as he reached up and pointed it point blank into his father's face. 'NOOOO! DROP IT! DROP THE GUN! BANG! The bullet fired off and connected with his father's face from point blank range. As his father started to fall towards the floor the dream suddenly became blank. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 7th August 2005 6:45am Mark Blackwolf's head shot up off the bed. He hated it when he dreamt of his parents death. He had'nt even been there at the time, but the dreams he'd been having these past couple of weeks made it seem as if he'd been there right next to them. He looked over at the bedside table at the picture of his parents having the time of there lives, out in the sea on there first family holiday. He reached out and slammed the picture frame down onto the table, shattering the glass that enclosed it, just like the caskets that now enclosed his long lost parents. He hated the fact that he hadn't been there with them. Things could have been so different if he had just gone with them that night. The thought had haunted him for the past six years now and he didn't know if it would ever go away. He knew it wasn't his fault, but that didn't make it any easier. The scene he had to endure that night in the morgue would never leave him and it isn't something he would ever wish on even his worst of enemies. The look on his mom's face as the bullet ripped through her skull, creating a hole the size of a moon crater at the back of her head, still adorned her lifeless body as she lay still as a mouse and as cold as an ice pack on her death bed. His father's body lay unrecognizable, but he knew it was him, the 'Sandra' tattoo on his left peck was unmistakable. He had gone to the funeral, but had his eyes closed through almost all of the ceremony, only opening them to say goodbye as their bodies dropped down into their final resting place. He knew they would always be with him and he also knew that they wouldn't have wanted their son to deteriorate the way he had these past couple of years, but he didn't know what else to do. Mark crawled out of bed, letting his feet hit the floor, as he reached out and grabbed the necklace he had had created just two weeks after that dreaded night. He opened up the locket and looked at the picture inside, his mother and father smiling back at him helped keep him sane over the years, otherwise he didn't know what he might have done. For all he knew he might have been up there right now with his parents in heaven after killing himself, God knows he wanted to do it for months after. He knew his parents wouldn't have wanted that though and thats all he needed to know. He wrapped the chain around his neck, standing to his feet and making his way across the room. He looked in the mirror and boy did he look like shit. He hated looking at himself in the mirror and today was no different as he turned his back to the wall and made his way over to the radio stationed on top of his chest of drawers. Maybe some music could get that horrible dream out of his mind. He was a PRIME-ate now and he didn't need these thoughts running through his mind at this moment, he needed to calm down and relax and maybe some light music could be just the thing he needed. He switched the power on and the radio came to life. 'Bang Bang, he shot me down, Bang Bang, I hit the ground, Bang Bang, that awful sound, Bang Bang my baby shot me down, down, down, down' Audiobullies feat Nancy Sinatra was the worst song that could have ever come out of the radio at that precise moment, but it didn't stay on long as Mark grabbed the radio, pulling it out of the plug socket and tossing it through the room, the radio coming to a crashing halt at the far wall as it shattered and slid down to the floor, no longer the radio he had come to love, but now a mangled heap of junk. He needed some food and a drink, it was still only 7am and not a single joint in town was open yet, but he knew just the place where he could get a bite to eat, this was the place he spent most of his time when he wasn't travelling the world kicking ass and Paulio the owner would certainly open up for him when he realized who it was. Mark had known Paulio almost all his life, well ever since he could walk. Paulio was one of his fathers best friends and would do anything to help Mark out when he needed it, even going as far as to offer him money and a place to rest his head when he didn't have the money to buy a place of his own. He would certainly shoot out of bed to make Mark something to eat and Paulio was always great at keeping Mark's chin up when he felt down. Boy did he need something like that right now. Mark grabbed his long black leather coat off the rack and slipped it over his shoulders, letting the long material slide down his back. He grabbed his hair and put it in a pony tail, no one was going to be around but he still didn't want anybody to stare at him anymore than they did already, he needed to look the best he could. He took one last look in the mirror, snarling to himself as he made his way over towards his bedroom door, swinging it open and making his way downstairs. He made his way into the kitchen and opened the fridge door. He hardly ever used his fridge and one look inside would tell you just that. The fridge was empty save for a few small energy drinks and a tray full of eggs. He grabbed an egg, cracking it open with the palm of his hand and tipping his head back, mouth open, as the contents spilled out and into his mouth. He shook his head, letting the egg whites slip down his throat. He did this with another three eggs before grabbing an energy drink, cracking it open and swilling it down to rid himself of the taste. He wanted to try to get himself in shape now he was in PRIME, but the amount of food he was eating didn't help any. He crushed the can in his hands, throwing it across the room as it just missed the bin on the far side of the room. Blackwolf: FUCK! No wonder they turned me down when I tried to enroll on the basketball team. I couldn't hit a damn shot to save my life. Mark ignored it, as if the place needed cleaning anyway, there was only him there and he didn't care one bit. He slammed the fridge door shut and made his way through into the living room. He grabbed the remote and switched the TV off, he must have left it on last night when he went up to bed and he didn't need to be paying extra on his electricity bill right now. He threw the remote back down onto the couch and grabbed his keys off the coffee table situated in the middle of the room. He opened the front door making his way out into the morning light and slammed the door behind him, locking it shut. One click of his car keys and the door popped open as he made his way over to where he had parked. He opened the driver's side door and slumped into the seat ready to make his way to Paulio's cafeteria, The Green Dragon. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 7th August 2005 7:15am The car pulled up outside the Green Dragon as Mark slipped out of the car, one more click of his keys and the car door clicked shut. He looked up at the neon glowing sign and a smile spread across his face. He was here and the lights we're on upstairs which mean't he wouldn't be standing around forever trying to wake Paulio's lazy ass up. He looked around on the floor for something big enough to make a loud enough sound on the top window, but also not that big that it would fly straight through the window and shatter it. He found a pebble on the floor and tossed it up at the window, but it drifted off and hit the window next door instead. Mark seemed shocked that his shot was way off and jumped straight under the shelter of the Green Dragon as a light came on in the window he had just connected with. He didn't want to be caught this early in the morning throwing stones at people's windows. Blackwolf: FUCK! No wonder they turned me down when I tried to enroll on the baseball team. I couldn't pitch to save my life. The window upstairs shot open as an old looking grey haired man popped his head out of the window, looking down to see who was stupid enough to throw a damn brick, or so he thought, at his window at 20 minutes past 7 in the morning. Old Man: HEY YOU LITTLE SHITS! I'VE HAD ENOUGH WITH YOUR STUPID GAMES! I'M TRYING TO GET TO FUCKING SLEEP HERE! KNOCK IT OFF OR I'LL COME DOWN THERE AND TWAT YOU WITH MY DAMN WALKING STICK! STUPID LITTLE SHITS! YEAH YOU WILL RUN! He slammed the window shut as Mark creeped out of his hiding place, looking up to see the light go back off in the room. He let out a sigh of relief as he again searched for another stone to throw up at the window. He found another pebble not far from where he found the other and threw it up at Paulio's window, connecting flush this time as he threw his arm in the air in celebration, like his team had just won the damn Super Bowl. The window opened and a tall looking man popped his head out of the window, looking down at the street below. Mark shot his hand in the air to try and get Paulio's attention. Blackwolf: YO DUDE! IT'S ME MARK! COME ON MAN LET ME IN WE NEED TO TALK! Paulio: YO MARK! WAIT THERE AND DON'T MOVE, I'LL BE RIGHT DOWN! The window slammed shut and the light went off upstairs, signalling that he was on his way downstairs. Mark made his way over to the front door just as the light came on in the big room and the door slowly unclicked and opened up. Paulio threw the door back and motioned for Mark to come inside, which he did without hesitation. Paulio pulled the door closed and locked it, turning back to Mark, who had now made his way over to the bar, grabbing a stool and sitting himself down. Blackwolf: Hey man, I think I may have pissed your next door neighbor off, he was screaming his head off. Paulio: Who Old Joe ? Don't worry about him he's going a bit senile and the kids have been pissing him off lately. They won't stop throwing stones at his window. So why was he pissed off at you ? Blackwolf: Erm.....nevermind about that. Listen I needed someone to talk to and you are the only guy around here that I can trust. Paulio made his way behind the bar and placed his arms down on the surface, looking at Mark who now had his head down, looking directly at the floor. Paulio: Why what's wrong ? You can't pay your bills again ? Do you need some money because I can..... Blackwolf: No it's nothing like that. I've been having that dream again, you know the one with my parents death. I just can't seem to get it out of my mind and I just needed a place to come and get my mind of things you know ? Paulio: Hey dude, you know I'm always here for you. You need a drink ? You look like you could use a drink and something to eat. You want something to eat ? Blackwolf: I wouldn't say no. Thanks a lot man, your the best, you know that ? Paulio: Yeah I know. Paulio turned around as he grabbed a pint glass off the shelf and poured Mark a tall glass of Coors finest lager, placing it down in front of him on the bar. Mark went into his pocket to grab some loose change to pay Paulio, but Paulio put his arms up in front of Mark stopping him in his tracks. Paulio: What you doing ? You know your moneys not welcome here. Put that away. Blackwolf: No man, I can't do that to you. You've got a business running here I have to pay for my ale. Paulio: Don't be stupid. Like I said your money isn't welcome here. Now what do you want to eat ? Fries and burger ? A hot dog ? Some waffles ? Blackwolf: Yeah they'll do nicely. Paulio: All of them ? Blackwolf: Yeah, what do you think I mean't ? Paulio: You don't change do you Mark ? Even after joining this wrestling organization. PRIME is it ? Blackwolf: No I think it's Premium. Paulio: Not the lager, the wrestling organization. Blackwolf: Oh yeah, PRIME, sorry, my mind just isn't with it at the moment. Paulio: I understand, don't worry about it. Paulio turned back around and reached into the glass containers, we're all the pre-made food was kept, grabbing a fries, beefburger, hot dog and three waffles before placing them on a custom 'Green Dragon' dinnertray and handing them over to Mark. Paulio: And again your moneys not welcome here. Blackwolf: Oh come on Pauly. At least let me pay for my food. I have to pay for something. Paulio: I said no and I mean no. Now how's the wrestling been going ? Blackwolf: Not very well to be honest. I'm still to make my debut yet and I'm not even booked for next week's show. You know I'm sure they don't want me, just like when I was in college. Mark bit into the burger, letting the juices run down his chin as he took half the burger away with one bite. Paulio: Don't be stupid man, of course they want you. Who wouldn't want someone of your stature in their organization ? Blackwolf: I suppose so. I just feel left out, although Deville did make me feel somewhat a home last week, with erm....what was her name now ? Paulio: You can't remember her name ? Blackwolf: Wait it'll come to me.....erm.....shit.....Clara, thats it Clara. She was a lovely girl, although she ran off after 20 minutes. Just like everybody else does. Paulio: Man stop dragging yourself down like this, you just need to be more sensitive with the ladies, don't scare the crap out of them. Blackwolf: I can't help it man, it's the way I look. I treated her well, even better than my own mothe..... Paulio: Look man, I'm sorry. Paulio puts a reassuring hand on Blackwolf's shoulder as his head makes it way to the floor once again. Blackwolf: It's ok man, it's not your fault. I need to get over this, Its been 6 years. Paulio: Yes I know, but it was your parents man, it can take a whole lifetime to get over things like this. When my dad died, I couldn't speak for months, to nobody, not even your father and he was my best friend. Oh shit sorry, I forgot. Blackwolf: Don't worry about it man. It's time I got out of this shell I've been in for the past 6 years anyway and moved on. It's just so hard. Paulio: I know man, I know. So you have any ideas who you could be facing next week ? Blackwolf: I don't have a clue man, and I really couldn't care less. I just need the money badly. I don't even have a damn carpet in my home. I'm walking on concrete. Blackwolf finishes the last few fries and takes a huge swig from his lager, downing the rest of it in one gulp. He wipes his mouth as he places the pint glass back down on the bar. Blackwolf: Listen thanks a lot for this man, I really needed it. I don't have anything to eat in my damn house only energy drinks and eggs and I mean how many ways can you cook eggs ? Paulio: Fried, Scrambled, Poached, Hard Bolied, Soft Boiled..... Blackwolf: Ok I get the picture, but I can't live of damn eggs alone. I needed some proper food. Paulio: Not a problem man, listen anytime you need anything you know you can come here, your always welcome Mark, always. Paulio looked at the clock on the wall and saw that the time had drifted by and it was now 7:55am and nearly time to open up the cafe. Paulio: Wow, look at the time, time really drifts by when your having fun. I have to open the shop soon. Blackwolf: Ok man, I was gonna get going anyway, thanks a lot for chatting to me Paulio, its always a pleasure man. Listen I'll stop by sometime next week and we can chat again. Paulio: No a problem Mark, like I said your always welcome here. Mark got up of his stool and took the last bite of his hot dog before waving at Paulio and making his way towards the entrance. He looked back at Paulio as he made his way to the door. Blackwolf: Be sure to watch ReVolution this week man, you have the playboy channel right ? Paulio: What are you trying to say ? Blackwolf: That you're insecure and you like your porn. Paulio: Well that true.....just don't let people know man, its our little secret. Blackwolf: Right.....I'll see you around Pauly. Mark turned towards the entrance, trying to open the door, but he goes crashing straight into the glass, nearly falling straight through it. Paulio: HAHA! Oh shit, let me open that for you. I forgot I locked the door. Paulio makes his way round from the back of the bar and makes his way over towards Mark, who was standing there rubbing his head. Blackwolf: You'll give me a damn concussion before I even have my debut match. Thanks a lot Pauly, I'll see you around. Pauly unlocks the door and lets Mark make his way back outside and onto the sidewalk, into the hot morning sun. He turns around and gives Paulio another wave before hitting his car keys again, popping open the drivers door. He slides in and winds the window down, leaning out and looking back at the entrance we're Paulio was stood waving him off. Blackwolf: I'll see you around. And thanks again mate, you've been a huge help. By the way, the food tasted like shit. Blackwolf starts up the engine and leaves his hand hanging out waving off Pauly as he makes his way off down the street and into the rising sun. Paulio: Cheeky shit! See you around Mark! Paulio waves off Mark as he speeds off into the distance before turning the 'closed' sign around to reveal the word 'open'. Paulio: Another busy day. God I hate this job. Paulio closes the entrance as he makes his way back inside the Green Dragon to another hectic day.
