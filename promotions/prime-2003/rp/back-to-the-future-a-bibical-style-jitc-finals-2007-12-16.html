Back to the Future: A Bibical Style JiTC Finals

16 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12-16-07 “With the breakdown of the medieval system, the gods of Chaos, Lunacy, and Bad Taste gained ascendancy.” --John Kennedy Toole from, “A Confederacy of Dunces” There is something sexy about a drug addicted woman. The vulnerability makes them almost childlike mixed with the explosive nature of a full grown person brainwashed by the devils venom. One minute they have the innocence of a child, and the next minute they are raging violent whirlwinds with breasts. The girl on the sofa’s arms were so thin and her skin as pail as freshly fallen snow it mixed into a disgusting creation of anorexia, with an odd sex appeal. Her breasts were still bulbous hanging loosely out of her black night gown. Hoyt Williams admired the tattoo of the joker on her leg. It was the old joker Cesar Romero from the old 60’s Batman TV series with Adam West. Ceaser was known as the Latin lover and refused to shave his mustache for the role. The Joker on the tattoo also had the mustache. The Joker was smiling a devilish grin while he fingered Batwoman who was spread eagle in front of him. Hoyt Williams: Nice Tattoo. Girl: For fifty bucks you can be my Joker. Hoyt Williams: I’m celibate, but thank you. The girl stared blankly at Hoyt with emptiness in her eyes. She admired the golden skin and muscular build of the wrestling legend. It’s been a long month of Sunday’s since she felt the touch of a sober sensuous man. Girl: I think I have something in the medicine cabinet for that. But thanks for being up front I don’t want to catch anything. Hoyt Williams: ummm, no problem. Hoyt searched the bleak apartment of closed shutters and empty McDonald’s bags for a place to sit. Chicago has two types of apartments. Much like dogs they are either small or large, nothing in between. This was one of the smaller fair, one step above a studio apartment as it did have a tiny bathroom and what appeared to be a bedroom. Hoyt Williams found a place to sit down after moving a package of White Castle from the sofa. While sitting down Hoyt noticed a needle on the table which was clearly used for narcotics of some sort. Hoyt began to consider leaving but the thought of buying a laptop for 300 bucks held him down. Every man loves buying shit they think is stolen and Hoyt Williams, savior or not was no different. You see Hoyt saw an ad on Craigslist the night before and called some guy named Brad. Brad gave Hoyt directions to his apartment and told him to bring the money in cash. Now Hoyt with a pocket full of cash is at Brads wondering what’s going to happen next. Hoyt Williams: Where is this guy Brad, who I spoke to on the phone? Girl: He’s getting the laptop right now. Hoyt Williams: 300 bucks only? Girl: Cash only. Hoyt Williams: I’m not getting mugged am I?? Girl: Somebody your size is worried about that? Hoyt Williams: The world is nuts. Girl: I’m allergic to nuts. Hoyt Williams: In many ways I’m allergic to this world. So what do you do for a living? Girl: I used to be the head waitress at this Hollywood themed restaurant. We had all kinds of fancy dishes like Pulp Fiction Milkshakes, Fried Green Tomatoes, High Noon Eggs, and so on. Hoyt Williams: Yea those places are annoying. Girl: Tell me about it. Well anyway, they put me in charge of naming the new dishes and having the menus printed up. So I did my job and had the menus all printed up with in budget. Everybody told me what I good job I did. Hoyt Williams: So what happened? Girl: About a week into the new menus this old bitch starts yelling for the manager. Seems she was offended by my selection of Schindler's List Ribs. They fired my ass after having to replace all the menus. Hoyt Williams: I’ll be damned. A moment of silence took over the room like a popular Simon and Garfunkel tune. Hoyt sat wondering if the woman named the ribs out of stupidity or racism. Some times people get labeled racist when in reality they are just stupid. In many way’s they go hand and hand. Hoyt Williams: So did you name the ribs because… The door whipped open and a panicked Hispanic man rushed in. He was holding a case which held the laptop that would cause so much trouble for Hoyt Williams later on. The man slammed the door behind him locking it up like it was a vault. He then rushed over to the window peaking out at the street below before turning around and noticing Hoyt sitting on the sofa staring at him. Man: Who the fuck is he? Girl: Somebody allergic to the world. Man: What the fuck is he doing here? Girl: He’s the guy here to buy the laptop. Man: You showed up! Hoyt Williams: You must be Brad? Man: Listen man just take this laptop and get the hell out of here fast. Hoyt Williams: What about the money? Man: Oh yea, give it here….FAST! Hoyt reached into his pocket and handed the man three hundred dollars. The guy snagged it up like a drunk at the liquor store grabbing a bottle of Mad Dog 20/20. He shoved the cash into his pocket as he quickly unlocked the door he just locked. Hoyt Williams: Does this have a warranty? Man: As is man, now please for you own good get moving. Hoyt wondered about the suspicious behavior of the man and his junkie girlfriend but he figured he knew where they lived if trouble did arise. He got into his Cadillac as he cranked up the new John Fogerty album and sang along to “Gunslinger” as he created a path as he drove through the freshly fallen snow. Back at the apartment the fate of the ill willed often end up tragic, and this was no different. Shortly after Hoyt left the apartment a police call was heard over the radio describing two gun shots heard. When the police arrived they found two dead junkies. The place had been tossed as the killers were looking for something. What the killers wanted was the suitcase what they found was nothing except an e-mail from Hoyt Williams which they grabbed up and deleted. The police wrote it off as two drug dealers killed over no doubt stolen drugs. The police like anything starting with poli as a prefix were often wrong. Case closed, so they thought. Back at his mansion Hoyt, like a small child on Christmas ripped open the suitcase which the laptop came in. He opened it up and was surprised to find how high tech it was. It was slick and small with a gray casing. Hoyt plugged it in and to his surprise it fired right up. Hoyt’s son, who was now almost three years old crawled over to his father as his daddy played with his new laptop. Hoyt picked him up and put him on his lap bouncing him as they both looked puzzled at the computer screen. Hoyt Williams: What’s going on here? Little Hoyt: I don’t know? Hoyt Williams: Look it’s some weird screen?? Little Hoyt: I don’t know. Hoyt Williams: It says I can witness anything in history all I have to do is punch in the date?!? Little Hoyt: I have to poop! Hoyt Williams: MARY!!!! A moment later Hoyt’s wife the beautiful blonde with the giant cans arrived. She picked up the baby glancing briefly at her husband who was enamored by this weird screen on the computer in front of him. Mary: Are you playing games again? Hoyt Williams: No this is my new laptop; I’m trying to get past this screen. Mary: Looks like a game to me. If I have to help Little Hoyt to the bathroom while your playing games there is going to be issues. Hoyt Williams: Honey I swear I have no idea what this is. Mary: It says type in a date and watch history. Help those who need help, and change history. I don’t know this sounds like a fucking game to me. Little Hoyt: Fu—king! Hoyt Williams: Oh no. See what you did!!! Mary: Darn it. We’ll worry about that later. So try it out. Where are you going to go in history? Hoyt Williams: I could go to the theater and stop Booth from shooting Lincoln, or even save that sissy east coast elitist Kennedy! Mary: Or save your brother Christ! Or MLK. What about 9-11? Hoyt Williams: All great ideas. Wait I have it!! Let’s go back to December of 2005! Mary: Why? Hoyt Williams: That way I can watch me kick the hell out of Brandon Youngblood one more time as I defend my title at King of Kings!!! Little Hoyt: I pooped! I fu-KING pooped!! Mary: Have fun, I’m going to change YOUR son. Hoyt looked at the screen one last time and typed in the date of 2005 edition of King of Kings. He didn’t expect must to happen as he hit enter. A moment later the screen let out the brightest light Hoyt had ever seen. He began to feel dizzy and sick as the light overcame him. Moments later he was on his back waking up from what seemed like darkness. Hoyt Williams: What the hell? Youngblood ‘05: Hey man you ok? Hoyt Williams: Oh you! What do you want? Youngblood ‘05: Dude relax. You just look a bit sick. Hoyt Williams: You better hope I’m sick, because tonight I’m going to kick your ass. Youngblood ‘05: Tchu are you ok? I’m not in the tournament I have a title shot against that phony champion Hoyt Williams. The guy is as over rated as it gets and hides behind management. He NEVER defends his title and has no talent. Tonight his ass is mine and I’m going to take it. Hoyt was confused, as the man in front of him was definitely Brandon Youngblood. But something was different. Youngblood did not seem to be threatened by his most hated nemesis. Plus Hoyt was puzzled by why Youngblood called him Tchu. Hoyt Williams: Maybe I’m NOT feeling too good. Youngblood ‘05: Well good luck tonight. Hoyt Williams: Umm thanks. Hoyt got up and looked around noticing that he clearly was in 2005. Panic struck him like an animal in a trap. Perhaps the laptop was real and he had in fact traveled back in time. With out missing a beat Hoyt walked over to the mirror by the sink in the dressing room. Hoyt gazed into it and was frightened by what was staring back at him. It was not the ultra sexy Hoyt Williams but rather the inferior Tchu, the man Hoyt allowed to take his Universal title when he bored of it. Hoyt again became light headed and had to sit back down in the nearest chair. Aimz ‘05: Hey you don’t look that bad! Hoytchu Williams: Umm hey, ahhh this isn’t a good time. Aimz ‘05: Just wanted to wish you luck tonight. We may face off but I just want you to know we’re cool regardless. Hoytchu Williams: Hey thanks. The King of King’s locker room in 2005 was a very tense place. Back stage issues had heated up to the boiling point between members of the roster. Hoyt was leading the charge on one side with the help of Deville and a few others. On the other side were Youngblood and Aimz with some help from their friends. It was a locker room divided. That same tense feeling of a fight possibly breaking out at any moment still existed. Nobody knew if when Youngblood and Williams when they stepped into the ring together if the match would turn into a brawl with both guys trying to kill each other. Security was tight backstage which was another rarity in the sport. As Hoyt sat in his chair reminiscing about the past, which oddly was now the present, he started to freak out. Questions raced through his mind about how long he will be stuck in this new body, and what he should do while in this formation. Just then Hoyt Williams noticed, ahhh Hoyt Williams walking by. Hoytchu Williams: Hey! The Hoyt Williams of 2005 turned around and looked at what he saw as Tchu sitting in a locker room alone. He cautiously entered though the doorway never taking his eyes off of Tchu who was really him from the future. Hoytchu Williams: Listen to me, this is going to sound really strange but I’m you from the future. Hoyt Williams ‘05: Kid, keep doing what you’re doing and somebody it’s true you may be almost as good as me. Hoytchu Williams: YOUR NOT LISTING TO ME I’M YOU! Hoyt Williams ‘05: This is some kind of trick isn’t it? You’re trying to get into my head. Did that weasel pin dick Youngblood put you up to this? Hoytchu Williams: NO man, I really am you. I’ve traveled back in time. Hoyt Williams ‘05: Really? Tell me about my future. Hoytchu Williams: You leave PRIME for a while and float around in FUSE. You return but struggle, as I, or you, seem to have lost a bit of your mojo. Currently you are in the Jewel in The Crown tournament which you must win to get back into the title picture to reclaim your greatness. Hoyt Williams ‘05: First off I would NEVER leave PRIME. Second, no way am I, in some silly tournament and not the main event. Hey aren’t you Killean’s buddy. I bet he is still pissed how bad I embarrassed him taking the Universal Title off his waist. I heard you two are some kind of homos, and I want nothing to do with THAT! NOW GOOD DAY TO YOU SIR!! Hoyt walked away leaving the Hoyt Williams from the future alone and stranded in the body of Tchu. A moment later a man walked through a wall holding a small calculator looking gadget. The wall walker was a fat man with blonde hair and blue eyes. He had a rather folksy warm charm to him. Hoytchu Williams: Did you just walk through a wall? Wall guy: I sure did. Hoytchu Williams ’07: I’m going to call security. Wall guy: They can’t see me, only you can. My name is Al and I’m from the real future. Hoytchu Williams: What? Al: Yes, I’m your handler. I’m here to guide you on your journey into the past. Hoytchu Williams: So you’re from the future? Al: Kind of yes. You see I’m your creator and my reality is much different then yours. You see we are from the same time but parallel universes. When you turned on that lap top which in affect turned on the Quantum Leap accelerator which cased you to vanish .... You just woke to find yourself trapped in the past, facing mirror images that were not your own but rather Tchu’s. You are now driven by an unknown force to change history for the better. Your only guide on this journey is me, Al, an observer from your own time, who appears in the form of a hologram that only you can see and hear. So now your stuck leaping from life to life, striving to put right what once went wrong and hoping each time that your next leap will be the leap home. Hoytchu Williams: Wait one gosh darn minute! Is that the intro to the TV show Quantum Leap? Al: It sounded more poetic then anything I could come up with you see I’m not Dave, Matt, or Jeff, I’m Al. Now understand the only way you can get out of this is by helping Tchu win the Jewel in The Crown Tournament then take the Universal title from, well, you. Hoytchu Williams: So where is the real Tchu? Al: He’s hanging out in our waiting room. He won’t remember anything you do here but will only recall them as his own memories. Hoytchu Williams: So I need to help this closeted homo, and not get any credit for it? I don’t swing that way. Al: According to Zippy my computer you have no chance of ever returning to your own time and own body if you don’t help him. Zippy is telling me you have a 15% chance of returning home. Hoytchu Williams: If not, I say here and get bonked in the ass by Killean? Al: According to Zippy there is a 76% chance of that happening. You MUST beat K-wolf and Aimz tonight to be crowned Jewel in the Crown. Hoytchu Williams: Well there is one plus side to all this after all. Al: Now you’re seeing the light. Hoytchu Williams: I really don’t want to get nailed in the ass. Just as Hoyt said the comment out loud, seemingly to himself K-Wolf walked in seeing only Tchu talking to himself. She looked him over with a glance of distain. (CREDIT TO MAT FOR THE CAMEO APPEARENCE!!!) K-Wolf: Excuse me while I gag on your 'aura of invincibility'... or whatever that smell is. Hoytchu Williams: Listen I’m not who you think I am. I need you to let me win tonight so that I can get home. It’s a long story but I’m not Tchu. K-Wolf: That sure beats Hoyt's 'I only stole your used ring-gear to stop a plague that would kill all of mankind' excuse. Hoytchu Williams: Hoyt Williams and his genius put aside understand this Sinner, I’m not Tchu I’m better then him. But I don’t have my body, I have his, and its not fair that your in your body. Now if you’re willing to have an out of body experience this thing might be fair but, that’s not going to happen. So I need you to forfeit. Do it for the good of mankind, do it for Tchu! K-Wolf: Actually, come to think of it... admittedly, this thing doesn't happen everyday, but I THINK I know a way of helping you get out of Tchu's body and back into the future. Hoytchu Williams: This better not involve Christopher Lloyd and a flux capacitor. K-Wolf: Only if we start giving nicknames to parts of our anatomy. See, if you stand here, feet apart, and I kick you as hard as humanly possible in the snowglobes, then maybe, just maybe, I can make Tchu's body dry-heave you out... into THIS can of mace. Then, I set a reminder to myself to mace Hoy-...you in the face in a couple of year’s time, and you just breathe your peppery self in. Bingo. Hoytchu looked over at Al the hologram from the future to see if that option was a reality. Al just shook his head and his three chins in the process. Hoyt hung his head for a moment before turning his head up as he looked into K-Wolf’s eyes as Tchu. Hoytchu Williams: You know what sinner I have had about as much as you as I’m willing to have. I’m going to win this thing for Tchu and I’m going to show the world just how great Tchu is as me! Al watched on as Hoyt now playing the role of Tchu argued. He made notes and checked back in with Zippy. The odds had increased for Tchu winning the Jewel in the Crown tournament via Hoyt Williams. This made him smile as he wished Hoyt luck and exited through a bright light shaped door. Hoytchu went out to the ring in the body of Tchu and kicked ass. He took down K-Wolf, and then he took down Aimz. He did everything he was asked to do, yet nothing happened. He was still backstage as Tchu and not as Hoyt in the future. Hoytchu Williams: I won the Jewel in The Crown AND Hoyt Williams just beat Youngblood but I knew that. So no history has changed and I bettered Tchu in the process carrying him. So why am I still here? Al: Zippy is working on it. Seems like to really leap you must embrace Tchu in the process. You must be him to leap from him. This is your chance to walk in another man’s shoes. Hoytchu Williams: Yea but, he wears a different size then I do, and the dude has no taste in shoes. I mean look at these things so plain and no style. Disgusting! Al: Well that is what I mean. Perhaps you can learn from others by becoming them. You really have a chance to look into another man’s soul. Hoytchu Williams: That’s gay. Al: Have it your way Hoyt but you may never leap if you don’t come around to the barrel of the wheel that is spinning your way. Hoytchu: But if I stay here that means I have to fight myself? Al: The only person who can beat you is you. Hoytchu: Now you’re fucking with my head. Are you saying I am where I am because I don’t listen? So in some actual yet metaphorical way I have to beat my self to change back to myself? Al: Right? Hoytchu: Now you’re really getting into my head. I don’t know how I’m supposed to beat me when I’ve never even jerked off. The door swung open like a whore’s legs after handing her a fifty dollar bill. In walked the ’05 Hoyt Williams who was not to happy about Tchu winning the tournament making him the number one contender. Hoyt Williams ’05: You know something you sinning sack of Satan’s excrement. Aimz was supposed to win so I could take out both Youngblood and Aimz as I march toward greatness. I hope you don’t think you are going to take this title from me rookie. Yea you’re on some winning streak right now but you don’t impress me and never will. I may have a reputation as a nice guy back stage but understand this. I like to play politics and I will crush you not just in the ring but also out of it. I will make it so you never work in this federation again. Now if you’re a smart man you’ll forfeit your little victory and pass the win off to Aimz. You follow me sinner? Hoytchu Williams: You know you really are starting to get on my nerves. I’m going to give you a little bit of advice. First, never talk to me that way again. Second never buy a laptop off of craigslist. Hoyt Williams ’05: Who the hell is Craig and what about his fucking list? Hoytchu Williams: Thirdly get the hell out of this locker room before I get really pissed because I’m not in the mood to beat myself up. Hoyt Williams ’05: You should beat yourself up over the disgust and sin you bring to this world. Hoytchu Williams: You just don’t know when to stop do you? Hoyt Williams: ’05: I always get the last word. Hoytchu Williams: You know I like getting the last word too. The two men went face to face but that didn’t last long. Hoytchu reared back and socked Hoyt ’05 in the face hard. With out missing a beat Hoytchu jumped on top of Hoyt and pummeled him until staff came and pulled the two apart. Over the next few weeks Hoytchu prepped for his match against the older version of him. He got Tchu’s semi-perfect body into Hoyt like greatness working out every day. A month or so later the two met in the ring and Hoytchu beat Hoyt Williams ’05 sending him more or less out of PRIME for good. Depression hit Hoyt hard at first, as days turned into weeks and weeks into months. He gave Tchu greatness not only taking and maintaining the Universal Title but also getting into the Hall of Fame for the second time, different body. Hoyt was miserable trapped inside a body that wasn’t his. He thought more than once about suicide, but the thought of never seeing his son again kept him strong and living. Al came around less and less as Hoyt started to forget who he was. After a year or two Hoyt became Tchu and took Tchu to great heights that he never could have accomplished with out Hoyt as him. Hoyt became more and more comfortable with who he now was. He did things he never would do as his former self like smoking dope and sniffing coke. Then the calendar turned to December 12, 2007 and just a few days from another huge fight. This one would seal the deal for Tchu’s greatness. A win would make him a three time Universal Champion and would reclaim the title moving into ’08. Oddly Hoyt Williams has also returned a win by him and a win from Tchu would reverse the situation of ’05. Creepy and odd but so is life which I too think I’m a bit allergic to. Hoyt thought for a moment how he was going to destroy Sonny Silver. In his mind Hoyt had a game plan all laid out. It was going to be easy and just the high Hoyt needed to keep on living day to day. As Hoyt turned around he saw something in the corner of his eye. Hoytchu Williams: You scared me. Al: Sorry. Are you ready for your match with Sonny Silver? Hoytchu Williams: You know I have the thing in the bag. I’ve come a long way haven’t I? Al: I would say you’re a new man. Say are those new shoes? Hoyt chu: Yea, I just wanted something basic. Al: Really? Before Hoyt could answer a bright light flashed in front of his eyes. His body hurt and the white brightness soon turned too black. When Hoyt awoke he was staring in the face of Al who was dressed in an emergency workers uniform. Al had two paddles in his hand and was zapping Hoyt’s chest. Mary: Oh my gawd I think he’s breathing!! Al: Can you hear me. Hoyt Williams: Yea, but Al where am I? Al: You are home sir. Hoyt Williams: Home? An eerie feeling come over Hoyt Williams, who had spent the last two years living in the body of somebody else. He looked over at his wife who had a look of concern on her face. She was holding Hoyt’s son who was crying, frightened by the happenings. He was home yet he was not two years from the point when he left but rather ostensibly the exact same point. Hoyt Williams: Home? Al: Yes sir, you got zapped by a loose wire in your laptop. It stopped your heart but you appear to be ok. Hoyt Williams: You’re not my handler? You’re a medic? Al: Ummm, yes sir I am a medic. Hoyt Williams: But your name is Al? Al: Yes. Hoyt Williams: How long have I been out? Mary: About fifteen minutes. Some men in suits also came by and took the laptop away so you can forget about suing. Hoyt Williams: Forget about suing I’m just happy to be home and be me!! I love you! Mary: I love you two. Hoyt got up picking up his son for the first time in years. Tears fell down his face as he kissed his son on the forehead. The child stopped crying in the arms of his father. Hoyt Williams: I’m just going to be me from this point forward. First things last as I need to win the Jewel in The Crown tournament and prove to the world that I, HOYT WILLIAMS, KING OF KINGS, PONTIFF OF PRIME am certainly HOME!! Like Christ I have risen from the dead paying for others sins. The time is now and now is the time for me to be me and send baptize and bless the rest of the roster. Al: Umm, ok Hoyt Williams: I have been making history for two years and nobody knew it was me. That’s very humbling but now I need to show the world again what they already know. NOBODY IS BETTER THEN HIS HOYTNESS. CROWN MY ASS! =w=
