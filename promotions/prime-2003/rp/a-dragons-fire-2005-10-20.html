A Dragons Fire

20 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20/10/05 “Welcome to Dragons Lair, where Heaven and Earth meet in one place!” Ryu Hirohito smiled broadly as he walked with his arms around the shoulders of two exceptionally attractive young women. It had taken him a number of years, dodgy deals and mysterious disappearances in order to create this masterpiece that surrounded him. Even as a child he had not believed in the straight and narrow, from the moment he made a sideline out of stealing other children’s lunch money he knew that this would be his life. He was a man with big dreams and at thirty five years old he had finally accomplished his biggest dream. “This club will provide the very best entertainment for the men with dancers of all colours and ages. However don’t you worry because I’ve not forgotten you ladies, oh no I haven’t, because you will have all the music, alcohol and men that you could ever dream of in one place!” To his left is a blonde twenty something wearing the tightest dress and to the right an Asian late teenager dressed in equally complimentary attire. Both were giggling like schoolgirls at his comments and they were nuzzled in closely to the suited Japanese ‘businessman’. The club itself was vast with a black and red colour combination for décor which made it look very suave and sophisticated. Ryu had been heavily promoting opening night and sure enough the response had been massive with the club absolutely heaving with the upwardly mobile members of society as well as your Average Joe. Although Dragons Lair had been built on the foundations of a crooked lifestyle, Ryu now had a legitimate business to cover his alternative dealings. As he revelled in this glorious night, Hirohito speculated that there was nothing that could possibly ruin his evening. “Mr Hirohito! Mr Hirohito!” At least that was the plan but all good plans tend to have a habit of going wrong. Ryu sighed heavily, took his arms from the women and looked at them with an apologetic expression on his face. “I am SO sorry about this ladies…You just CANNOT get the staff these days…Now why don’t you check out our bar and get yourselves a drink, the first one is on me tonight. Once again, I apologise for the rudeness of my colleague…He WILL be dealt with…” Despite his firm tone he smiled politely to the women and pointed them in the direction of the bar to the right of them. He waited until they have left before turning around to the large although somewhat dopey looking security guard, casting an icy glare at him. The employee looked extremely sheepish especially as he was holding his hand to his right cheek and looking in a certain amount of pain. “Why do I get the distinct impression that I am not going to like what you’re about to say?” Ryu stood with his hands on his hips with a look of complete impatience and frustration. His shaven headed colleague glanced at the floor and then with nervous eyes returned his attention to intimidating Japanese superior. “Um…Mr Hirohito…We have a slight problem…There is a woman who insisted on a personal appointment with you…I tried to explain to her that this was the opening night of Dragons Lair but she wouldn’t listen to me…She forced her into your office and she refuses to leave until she’s seen you…She says it’s urgent business that she needs dealing with and you have a vested interest in it…?” Hirohito groaned to himself and barged past the man that seemed to be playing at being a security guard rather than actually being a successful one. As he made his way towards the metal stairs that led up to the second floor of the club, he quickly put on a smiling face as customers shook his hand and attempted to speak with him. It was essential that he portrayed himself as the good businessman with the public’s best interest at heart. He had had far too many close shaves in the lead up to opening this place and as he made his way up the stairs to his office, he mused that the last thing he needed tonight was a scene. He threw open the door and stopped in his track as he witnessed the beauty that was stood before him. “Confucius says every cloud has a silver lining…” His office was spacious with one wall completely made of glass so that he could look out onto the club and survey his kingdom. Not taking his eyes off the woman for a moment, he slowly made his way around to his desk and sat down. She was wearing a long red leather trench coat and red leather pants with a white cropped top and boots. Her hair had been dyed flame red too and she had clearly gone for a striking appearance in order to be noticed. All of Hirohito’s movements were slow and deliberate as he pulled out a cigar and methodically unwrapped it before putting it to his lips. In the background the security guard continued to look like a wounded animal as the woman turned to him and smiled with a hint of satisfaction. “You!” Suddenly Hirohito spoke up as he leaned over to glare at his employee once more who nervously stepped forward. Ryu then pointed at the female as he spoke. “Did SHE do that to YOU?!” Again the sheepish expression was on the guards face as he nodded slowly with great embarrassment. “Ugh…I do not have the patience or the time to explain to you how you have failed me tonight…So it gives me great pleasure to tell you…You’re fired! Take your wages on your way out and don’t let me EVER see you again…Because bad things happen to people that let me down!” Although feeling somewhat aggrieved, the guard decided discretion was the better part of valour and he quickly retreated out of the office with his tail between his legs. As the door slammed shut, Ryu muttered under his breath and lit his cigar taking a deep inhale into his lungs with his eyes closed. That was the last time he would take a chance on someone he did not know and trust. He leaned back in his chair and kept his eyes firmly shut as he smoked on his cigar doing his very best to ignore the irritation that was stood in front of him. “So I finally get to meet the great Ryu Hirohito who I have heard so much about.” He didn’t even bother opening his eyes to acknowledge her as he grumbled about the incompetence of those around him. “It was only out of great luck and great stupidity that you managed to meet me at all. I have enough women to cater for my every need and I do not recall having any requirements to hire a prostitute.” She scowled furiously at him as he sat basking in his own arrogance and cigar smoke. Determined to get his attention she leaned forward on the desk with both hands open and snarled at him. “I’m no prostitute, I’m Claudia fucking Lopez.” As Ryu’s hand drew itself to his cigar, he stopped in mid movement as he heard the name of the woman before him. His eyes flashed open as he examined her from head to toe and began to recognise her face.“Well, I never thought I would see a Lopez woman in one of my establishments again. How IS Maria? I trust that your sister is still busy living her better life in a penthouse suite somewhere?” When Claudia Lopez arrived at the Dragons Lair she had vowed to herself that she would rather negotiate with this devil in an attempt to remove her biggest demon. However as she looked at his arrogant posture and listened to his condescending words, she realised just how repugnant she found him to be. Never had she understood how and why her sister could possibly have become involved with a creature like this and now she was in front of him, she understood even less. “My sister is in a mental hospital thanks to the MEN in her life and I’m sure that you did VERY little to prevent that happening.” Ryu appeared somewhat puzzled as he listened intently to what she had to say as he puffed out the cigar smoke into the room. “As much as the doting sister act is cute, where WERE you when your sister was on the street? I GAVE her a job, I gave her an OPPORTUNITY…Without me, she would have been wandering from soup kitchen to soup kitchen, begging for a meal because her PRECIOUS family were nowhere to be seen…So I thought you had no right to come barging into my club in the first place but you now have even LESS right to come in here and attack my reputation and integrity.” She laughed at him and folded her arms defiantly as she glanced at him with a look of disdain. “You a man of integrity and decency? Puh-lease! You gave my sister a job alright, a job as a fucking ESCORT! You pimped her out to anyone that would pay you good money and I’m SURE that she was the one that paid for some of the bricks that built this place of yours! You OWE my family for what you did to my sister and I have come to collect payment!” He idly ran the cigar around the rim of his ashtray as he calmly listened to her ranting. She was clearly a woman of fiery temperament unlike her sister who had been a meek little thing in the time he had known her. However he was feeling particularly non-plussed by Claudia’s antics and he found himself paying more attention to the ashes in the ashtray than her ranting. “I owe your family nothing.” Even his reply was dismissive and casual as he looked up from his ashtray to her seething expression. To add insult to injury he then shrugged his shoulders at her shoulders which caused her to become even more riled. “You SOLD my sister to a man named Paul Cain who was the cause for her going crazy! Everything that has ever happened to her has been a result of your little business deals and it’s about time YOU made it up to her!” Ryu simply rolls his eyes and stubs out the cigar into the ashtray and leans back, placing his feet up on the table. “It was HER choice to leave, not mine. She was making me a LOT of money and seemed to be having a good time until her knight in shining armour rode up and said he was going to set her free from all this. He was an easy way out for her and they both saw each other coming. You can blame me all you like but Cain CONNED me and forced me to let her go for peanuts, I got NO compensation so don’t think to yourself that I have used your sister because I got NOTHING out of her treachery!” He then took a long and deep breath after that mouthful which Claudia viewed with great interest. “Face it Claudia, they deserved each other and I want NOTHING to do with her. In fact I do not want to hear another mention of HER or HIM again, do I make myself crystal clear?” Claudia Lopez had always been a defiant woman and there was no way that she would ever take no for an answer. For whilst Ryu Hirohito was a renowned Triad gang lord with a penchant for violence and prostitution, Claudia Lopez knew that she could appeal to his wounded ego when it came to Paul Cain and she was not afraid of the consequences of infuriating him. “Paul Cain made a mockery out of you by taking away your prized possession from you…Now I don’t like you, in fact I despise you for pimping out my sister…BUT…You and I have a common hatred for the man that swindled you….And we BOTH know…that you have the power and the influence to…How shall I put this…DEAL with Cain…?” Ryu simply smiled at her and feigned a look of complete innocence. “But I’m an honest businessman trying to run my establishment…I don’t do ‘that’ anymore.” With that, Claudia perched herself on the edge of the desk and looked down thoughtfully for a moment. “If you are the businessman you claim to be, then you’ll see the merit of removing Cain from my life and everyone else’s…I wouldn’t be the only one to be particularly grateful for your work…I have colleagues such as Hoyt Williams, a man of great social influence and Vangelus Olsig, who would be more than happy to see Cain ‘disappear’ and who has friends in high places…They could be very useful to your…enterprise…In fact, I would go so far as to say we would all be…indebted to you.” Tapping his thumbs together, Ryu considered this for a moment and then stared quizzically at Claudia. “And you had the courage to walk into a club belonging to a man who you knew very well would not have thought twice at snapping the neck of someone who disrespected him in the way you have behaved tonight?” She slowly nodded her head affirmatively and whispered the word “Yes” under her breath which caused Ryu to smile to himself. He extended his hand to her and maintained that expression that proved he was impressed by what he saw. “You have balls, unlike your sister…How can I possibly refuse you? Welcome to the family.” Now it was her turn to smile as she leant forward and shook his hand cautiously. Karina Wolfenden might have been tough but Paul Cain was about to find out just how dangerous a woman scorned can truly be. ******************************************************************************************************************************************** Far from the dark underworld of the Dragons Lair, Damien Kahn had finally completed his search. Since having lost to Karina Wolfenden, Paul Cain had disappeared into the night and not been seen since. Having remembered vividly the mental state of Cain upon his return to the squared circle, Damien had become concerned that Paul had retreated back to the darkest corners of his mind. When Revolution 79 had concluded Cain departed the arena in silence, he would not speak to anyone including Damien, such was his frustration at defeat. From there he faded into the night to lick his wounds and to contemplate what this result meant to him. Now though there was no more hiding as Damien Kahn had finally found Paul Cain however he was not propping up an inner city bar, he was instead communing with nature. “So this is where you’re hidin’ then? Campin’ out under the stars?” Paul Cain was sat in the lotus position in front of a crackling fire warming his hands on the flames. Damien’s eyes scanned the crudely constructed camp which included a tent which appeared to have seen better days. Then his eyes caught the glint of a blade shining against the moonlight. Paul had remained in silence all the time that Damien had been there but he felt Damien’s stare become fixed upon the ceremonial dagger that was lying by the fire. “What is it D-Money, you look like you’ve seen a ghost?” Kahn walked around the camp to where the dagger rested and he crouched down to get closer to it. He ran his hand along the blade and then down to the hilt which he then took hold of and lifted slowly off the ground. “I haven’t seen this since….” Cain turned his head slowly towards Damien and watched as Kahn gently moved the blade through the air albeit with a degree of respect for the damage it could cause. “Since the Dark Age carved me up with it?” Even after all this time and the events that occurred since Paul’s return, he could still clearly remember the events that led up to his temporary retirement. For although he was a changed man and no longer controlled by the scar on his chest, he had not forgotten a single strike or a single stab from that very same blade. The Dark Age was not a distant memory as they were still very fresh in the mind of Paul Cain. He saw Damien drop the dagger down onto the grass as he stood back up again. “What are you doin’ with that thing man? We got unfinished business to be dealin’ with like Blackwolf and getting you another shot at K-Wolf!” Cain resumed his focus on the fire in front of him and went into deep thought for a few moments as he listened to the crackling of the flames. “While I was gone everything became crystal clear to me. There is someone that PRIME is trying to protect and the only way that they could do it was by throwing distractions in my way. Kyle Lamen, the Battle Royal, Karina and Imperium were all ways at making me forget that this person existed. PRIME knew that if I got my hands on him, there would be a bloodbath and they couldn’t risk it. I just can’t believe it took me so long to realise what they were doing. Now though I know the truth and I won’t let PRIME keep me from him anymore.” Staring at Cain as if he was speaking another language, Damien appeared both shocked and confused by this tale that he was being told. “What the hell are you talkin’ about? They were just givin’ you opportunities!” Cain breathed deeply as he whispered the name on the night air. “Vangelus Olsig.” Now Damien’s eyes widened at the mention of another PRIME champion. “Whoa, whoa, whoa…You want to go after HIM after what you went through with HER?!” Slowly Cain rose to his feet as he walked over to the dagger and picked it up in both hands. He used the blade to snap open the buttons on his shirt and he then ran the tip of it along the scarring of the pentagram on his chest. “They tried to hide Olsig from me and then they tried to break me by taking everything I could fight for away from me in one night, at the hands of Karina Wolfenden. He is the link between PRIME and Vampir’s rebirth…And that link can NEVER be established again…I won’t allow it…I won’t allow PRIME to stop me doing what is RIGHT for them…For me…and for the people…Blackwolf and Williams can wait in line because I have a job to do…And if I don’t succeed…” Kahn watched in horror as Paul pressed the tip of the blade into the scar, causing a fresh trickle of blood to appear on his chest. “I am willing to pay the price of my career…And my life.” The scene begins to fade slowly out as Paul throws the blade onto the fire and then smeared the blood across his heart and chest as Damien Kahn watched on fearful of where this would end.
