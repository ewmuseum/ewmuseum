Is This Thing On?

2 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 2 FADE IN... [PRIME Intense Champion Troy Douglas sits inside his Mid-South Coliseum locker room, finishing up his traditional pre-match wrist taping routine just before PRIME's uber-patriotic "Shooting Stars & Armbars (etc.)" edition of ReVolution hits the air. Troy cuts the last excess piece of tape away, balls it up and tosses it into a trash can across the room, then looks up and smiles at the camera, looking like the very embodiment of steady confidence.] Troy Douglas: JASON! DO! I! NEED! TO! TALK! LOUDER! [Troy pauses, picking some imaginary lint out of his ear.] Troy Douglas: OR! CAN! YOU! UNDER! STAND! ME! [Finally done shouting, the Intense Champion rises from his seat and walks to his locker room door -- which is NOT dented -- and leans against it.] Troy Douglas: Hopefully I keep your interest this time, you ADD-riddled asswipe. I sure hope you can hear me this time, Jason Natas, because from what you just said, I'm pretty sure your ears were a little bit clogged up the last time I came out here and talked to you. Of course, you're DIFFERENT FROM EVERYBODY, so I'm sure actually, y'know, listening to me wasn't all that high on the ol' list of priorities, was it, Jason? Because if you had been paying at least a little bit of attention, you might have actually comprehended that little thing I was making that us rationally-thinking people like to call a point. Because I didn't say I've got no interest in facing you, Jason. I said, quite plainly, that there's a man I want in that ring just a little bit more than you. But, just like Mick Jagger told us, you can't always get what you want. But maybe, Jason, just maybe, I did get what I need. And what I got, son, is you. Which makes you my problem, Jason, and I understood that from the first goddamn moment that C.P. Cantrell announced this week's show. You are my ONLY problem, Jason, and I'm gonna do absolutely everything in my power to make sure you're not my problem for very fucking long, you arrogant little shit. [Troy moves from his relaxed position and now stands straight up, looking straight through the camera and speaking directly to his opponent.] Troy Douglas: You see, Jason, you like to talk about being the man who just doesn't give a shit about convention, about pomp and circumstance, about the thoughts and opinions of anyone watching and anyone who shares a locker room with you. Frankly, I couldn't possibly care less if you're telling the truth or not, but after listening to your little display of rampant noise pollution, I can tell you one thing without any pretense. You saying that you don't give a damn about those "delusions of grandeur" that you said everyone else has is pretty much the largest wad of absolute bullshit since "I did not have sexual relations with that woman." And yeah, that reference is pretty dated, but we're doing a nostalgia show here, folks, so forgive me if I reach into the time capsule for some of my material. Because while you might not have all the frills, all the pyro, all the fancy-ass ballyhoo that's long been associated with this industry, you've still got a hell of a lot of delusions, Jason. They're just manifesting themselves in a slightly different way. Mainly in that cocky-ass attitude your sporting and those oh-so-witty little wisecracks you've been spouting over the last couple of days. Oh, and in your completely unfounded belief that you're going to be walking out of Memphis with my Intense Title. Make no mistake, Jason, as much punishment as you think you can lay on me, that's still not happening. [Troy begins pacing back and forth, seemingly anxious to get the talking over and let the ass-kicking begin.] Troy Douglas: You were harping on the rather canyonesque difference in age and experience between the two of us, right? Let's get into that little debate for a moment. I've been in this business for eight years. You? You've had a grand total of eight matches, all right here in PRIME. I've been in wrestling rings around the world since the turn of the fucking millennium, putting my ass on the line night-in and night-out, giving absolutely everything I have to this industry. You showed up, talked down to Bryan Dawkins and FAILED when the spotlight was on at UltraViolence. So forgive me, Natas, if I'm taking all your talk about "ruining" me in your ninth professional match with a grain of fucking salt. Because I've heard a lot of talk from a lot of annoying little bastards over eight years, and in my long history of field research, I've managed to come to a rather startling conclusion. Most of that talk never, ever gets backed up inside the squared circle. And considering the rather glaring fact that I've kicked your ass before -- and this time around I've got a HELL of a lot of motivation to kick your ass again -- I've got more than a little evidence to believe that you're just going to be the next man to get lumped into the pile of trash talking never-will-be's. But, Jason, in your ninth professional match, you are standing right across from me with a shot at my title. And regardless of how much of a stupid little bullshit artist you are, that still makes you the most important man in my life tonight. [Troy finally finishes walking around and moves back to the bench in front of his locker. He pulls the Intense Title out from his duffel bag and holds it across his lap, the faceplate reflecting occasional glare into the camera lens.] Troy Douglas: And because of that, I'm going to make sure that your whole "make the guys with gold squeal like bitches" thing was a one-time occurrence. You pinned the tag champs in a match with NOTHING on the line. There's something on the line this week, Jason, and there is nothing in the world more important to me than keeping what's on the line between us. Because I am 32 years old, Jason. I've had a busted knee, a torn shoulder, concussions, broken bones, ligament problems, anything you want to toss out there, I've probably had to live through it. Hell, if not for a major spinal injury ending my football career, I probably wouldn't be here today. But I am, Jason, and at 32 years old with an medical history that'd make most respected physicians piss their pants, I know that my time can run out at any moment. Which means I'll do ANYTHING to keep cocky-as-shit brawlers who've had nothing but a fucking cup of coffee in this industry from taking what is rightfully mine before I've got to ride off into the proverbial sunset. And whatever you might think you are, Jason, you're not the guy who's going to put the old cowboy down. So drum all you want, Natas. Fuck, bring the entire goddamn drum corps from the University of Tennessee marching band with ya and have 'em join in if you want, because at the end of the night, the sound of whatever pounding you have in mind is going to be completely drowned out by the ringing in your ears you'll be hearing after I drop you on your grizzled head 15 or 20 times. I'm not flashy either, Jason. I may give the fans a good show because I appreciate what they mean to this business, but in the end, I don't need the pyro, the music, the gigantic sets, the national television, any of it all. I do this because I love professional wrestling, and I'll go to the ENDS OF THE FUCKING EARTH to be the best I can be. And to do that, I've got to teach a little fucker in his ninth professional match a very, very harsh lesson. Don't let your mouth write a check your ass can't cash. Because when the debt collectors come calling, all that swagger, all those requests to wax up my belt, they're not going to do you a damn bit of good. Flashy or not, I'm still going to WASTE you, Jason. And I don't need pyro or music or Vince Howard or C.P. Cantrell for that. Although, I'm keeping the fucking catchphrase, asshole. Ready for it? See you at the end of the road, Jason. There. Did THAT hold your interest? Hope so, because if not, you're in for a rude fucking awakening when the bell rings, son. I'll be waiting for you. ...FADE TO THE PRIME LOGO
