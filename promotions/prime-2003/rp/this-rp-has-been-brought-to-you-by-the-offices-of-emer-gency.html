Date: Twenty Minutes Ago As Adrienne and Emi sat quietly listening to the story, Scott laughed aloud. You read that right. “You've got to be kidding me, Van!” he roared, smacking his knee. “Oh yeah,” replied Van, “right up the middle.” “Van!” Alyssa said, giggling but trying to sound scandalized. “Dude, I told you wouldn't believe it.” Oxios smiled and sat back on the couch, sipping at his glass. Scott leaned forward and picked up the drink off the glass-top coffee table. It was his place, his and Alyssa's, in Lexington. Scott had pretty much taken up living here with Alyssa between shows. He'd visit home now and again, but this was just... easier. Plus, with him being away from home so much, the incursions from the Cult had diminished. It was a nice place. Alyssa had taken care to make sure that both of their tastes had gone into the decoration. Not that Scott knew he had tastes so much, but his responses to her questions had created a pretty comfortable atmosphere. The TV sat off in the corner in the entertainment center he'd put together. On the wall were his half of the RPW tag titles and OSW tag straps that he'd won in his RPW stint. Alyssa had put in some frills here and there, but it all felt right. “Okay, okay, anyway,” Scott said, taking a sip of his whiskey, “have they got the big setup going for the PPV yet?” “Oh yeah,” Van responded with a grin. “Tag champs get adjoining suites, my brother.” He looked at the two of them. “I might ask that they not be adjoining. You guys just don't let a man get his sleep.” You might have expected that they'd get embarrassed, turn red or quickly changed the subject. But Alyssa responded with a throaty laugh and Scott had a deep bass chuckle in his chest. “What's the matter, Van?” Aly asked, wrapping an arm around her man. “Can't take a little screaming?” “A little?” he shot back with a laugh, “you folk don't go to sleep until four in the morning. Damn, Scott, you must be made of hydraulics.” Scott smiled and kissed his woman's cheek. “So waitwaitwait,” Van said, asserting control of the situation, “when did you guys get engaged?” “Yeah, I was wondering when that would come up,” Scott sighed. Alyssa smiled and very purposefully laid a hand on his chest so the ring would sparkle, then winked at Van. “Well,” Scott began, “we were back in Pikeville and I took up her up to the place called Lover's Leap. We got up there and you can see the whole town. So I got up there, and she said the view was so beautiful, I said something sappy and gave her the ring.” “Oh, Scott!” she said, smacking his arm. “There was more to it than that.” She turned towards Van, her face positively beaming. “He looked at me and said I was more beautiful than any view could ever be. I never expected to see him get on one knee, but this luscious mountain of a man got down on his knee and pulled a ringbox from his pocket. It was so sweet.” Vangellus nodded his head. 'I think I saw that mov--OW!' Emi smacked him on the leg with her boken. She never leaves home without it. 'Nice,' he said. Aly cast her eyes to the two lovebirds Emi and Adrienne. A nine year age difference didn't seem to matter to either of them, as they operated on the same level. The two of them were more alike than one would think on first glance. They're both nature lovers, both are vegetarians, they're both wild in bed... (hehe). About the only thing that they disagree about is how annoying Vangellus is. 'How did you two come to your understanding?' Alyssa asked of the other women in the room. Adrienne hugged Emi close, leaning over her in a loveseat as the younger bodyguard sat legs folded on the floor. 'I can't say, really. There was just... a connection. We got along from the start. We just were attracted to each other. Dated a couple of times, I don't know. Then we were an item without really saying anything about it.' Emi turned her head upward and shared a kiss with her girlfriend. Scott and Aly could appreciate this. Vangellus, not so much, but he and his sweetheart were still pretty close. Scott raised an eyebrow. 'And what about you?' Vangellus held the dog in his arms as it licked his face. 'It was love at first sight.' That, for a joke, wasn't so bad. Even Emi cracked a smile. Calm. Happiness. If things could be this way forever....
