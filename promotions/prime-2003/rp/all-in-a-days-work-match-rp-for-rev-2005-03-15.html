Date: 15/03/05 Prelude: Life, one of the world’s most invaluable products. Gifted with life; we are expected to live our lives in happiness and love one another, yet that is just a dream because of a deadly combination of sins that have caused this world to become a less than pleasuring place. The Seven deadly sins make the picture a lot clearer; Gluttony, Greed, Sloth, Envy, Wrath, Pride. So the time comes where amends must be made and someone must be chosen to come forth and become an Eternal Leader. Shane Wethers has been chosen as this one, gifted with Telekinesis and the ability to haunt peoples mind. More viscous than any deadly disease seen by mankind, Shane must step forward and punish those who have done wrong, those who kill for pleasure. No one has the right to take anyone’s life, no matter what religion or path you follow. So hold hands contact the living and let’s wander into the life of reality as we experience a little something that could be haunting you if you follow those in Eternal Sin. If you’re easily offended… then either grow up or turn away now because you’re about to enter the twisted world we live in. Time 21:03Date: 02/30/05Location: Wakefield, England It’s another typical cold damp night in Eastmoor, Wakefield. The streets are quite on this saddened estate, full of council houses for the low budget parents trying to get by. Every so often the faint sound of car slowly passing can be heard as it approaches before disappearing into the distance. Inside one of the many red-brick thatched houses lives a young mother and he 7year old son. The Mother, Julie is only 24, having her child when she was 17. She flunked her GCSEs when she was younger as she was due to be giving birth and never had the chance to re-do them. Yes, life as a young mother really finds its way of destroying lives, yet who is to blame? Julie stands in the scrappy white kitchen, with a cup of tea in her hand. The faint orange wallpaper has begun to peal off of the wall and the tiles below it have various cracks in them. Julie is a pretty young lady, long shiny blonde hair, attractive brown eyes and a very sexy smile. She wears rather large round earrings that looks like a bargain from the market, they have already began to lose some of there golden colour. She has one of her hands in the pockets of her blue jeans and is wearing red small t-shirt with ‘FCUK’ written across the front in wavy black letters. It’s difficult to see why a lady of her beauty would have ruined her life with such an early pregnancy, living on nothing but the dole. All her dreams of becoming a nurse died along with her future and happiness. The only joy she wakes up to now is the sight of her young son and the arguments with the boy’s drunken father, her ex-boyfriend. She split with him a year ago and recently discovered that he was trying to introduce their son to alcohol. When she found out about this she was less than happy and told the father that he could no longer see their son and if he tried to he would be reported to the authorities. The father pleaded with her and begged her to change her mind, before slapping her hard across the face; the scar can still be seen today indistinctly upon her left cheek. The restraining order has not done much to keep him away, but at least she’s kept the chain lock on the door. As she stands in the kitchen her son runs into the kitchen wearing his blue ‘Thomas the Tank Engine’ pyjamas. He runs open armed and hugs her with a big smile on his face. She chuckles. Julie: Daniel! Why aren’t you in bed? It’s after nine o’clock! Daniel feels his mothers grip losen Daniel: I’m going to bed now mummy. Julie sighs and places her empty cup down on the side of the white unit. Julie: Did you brush your teeth? Daniel nods his head in the usual cheeky child-like way Julie: Let me see Daniel extends his cheeky grin by showing his teeth at the same time. He has two teeth missing; one to the bottom left and one in the middle at the top. Julie: Good. Now lets have you to bed, you have school tomorrow. Daniel groans as mother wardens him off out of the kitchen, leaving him to go to bed. As she does, Julies mother ‘Anne’ walks in a cream blouse and a long black skirt. She folds up random items of clothing that she has taken off the radiator and folds them. Julie smiles gratefully at her mother. Julie: Thanks mum, you’ve been a real help today. Especially with all the problems that are going on right now, it’s great to know you are here. Anne smiles and walks over to her daughter, putting her hand over hers Anne: You know I’m always here for you anytime you ask. I’m not going to leave my daughter and my grandson on their own am I now? Julie smiles along with Anne. Anne points to a set of large books on the table in the living room Anne: Just you concentrate on studying and soon we’ll have you back on track. Julie lets out another sigh and walks across the room, picking up one of the books and staring hard at the front of it Julie: It just seems like a far step for me. I should have been a nurse already, but who’s going to want to take me on now? Anne: Don’t be down sweetheart. Things will come round, you’ll see. You’ve always been a smart girl and I know things will work out, I guarantee you that. Julie sighs and picks up her mug of tea from the side of the counter and sips on it like an overly cautious child. She is interrupted by a knock on the front door. She places her mug down, looks at her watch and then looks at her mother as she walks through the hallway towards the front door. Julie: It’ll be the Avon lady; she usually collects around this time. Do you want anything? Her Mother shakes her head as she continues to fold items that have been placed over the warm radiator. Julie approaches the blue front door, pulling back on the lock and un-hooking the chain. She opens the door to see a tall black man with messy blue ripped jeans and a large white Ben Sherman coat. His hair is braided, his eyes look blood shocked and his pupils are dilated. Julie stares at him with an expression of terror. She attempts to slam the door in his face but he puts his hand inside and stops her. He pushes the door against her as she leads out a tearful scream. She walks backwards, trying to keep her distance from him. Julie: Craig!? What are you doing her?! You know you’re not welcome here! Craig continues to walk her way slowly, forcing her to retreat back into the living room. As she walks into the living room her mother rushes across. Anne: What’s wrong? I heard screaming! Anne turns and sees Craig walking towards them. She steps in front of her daughter. Anne: Get out! You are not allowed here! Craig doesn’t stop. He just continues to walk towards them with the blank, mindless expression on his face. Anne refuses to retreat as he walks straight up to her. She attempts to block his way. Anne: I said…. Craig suddenly pulls his hand from behind his back, jabbing forward at Anne’s stomach, and then pulling his arm back. Anne pauses as she staggers backwards into Julie’s arms. She looks down at her body to find blood soaking her clothing. She gasps in shock as Julies eyes widen in horror. Julie screams as her Mothers weight gives way on her and she is forced to let he mother fall to the ground. Julie: MUM!!! NO!!! She looks up at Craig with tears filling her eyes. She sees Craig holding a large sharp kitchen knife with a 7 inch blade.Julie: What have you done you sick bastard! He walks over to Anne’s body. Julie inhales and takes a deep breathe of courage as she walks over to him and begins to hit him in the chest with closed fist. He stands and laughs, before jabbing forward once again, hitting Julie in the chest. Julie gasp and falls back next to her Mother, finding that she two now has a bloody patch, but this time on her chest. The panic grows inside her and her breathing becomes heavy and fast. Her mother lays motionless. He walks forward and begins to jab away at Anne, the knife plunges in and out of her flesh like some cheap meat. Julie tries to scream and shout, but all that comes out is faint words Julie: stop…stop...you sick bastard…stop Julie’s words turn into tears. All of a sudden the sound of feet running down the stairs can be heard. Craig looks up at the stairs and wipes his hands. He places the knife cleverly in the back of his jeans. He begins to make way to them. He finds his young son standing halfway down the stairs with a weary tired look on his face. Daniel: Daddy? What’s going on? I heard Mummy and Nana shouting. Craig walks over to him and takes him in his arm, carrying him back up the stairs. Craig: They have just had a few drinks. Why don’t you watch some TV whilst they finish? Craig carries Daniel to his bedroom. He opens the door of the blue bedroom, decorated with ‘Thomas the Tank Engine’ wallpaper. He lays his son on the bed and takes a black remote from the side of the bed. He points it at the television, presses a small button on the remote and the TV comes on. He messes around with the silver Lodos all-in-one TV/Video player. Bob the builder suddenly appears and Daniel smiles. Craig hits the volume button, turning it up so it becomes quite loud. He turns to his unsuspecting son and smiles. Craig: Now you watch this and don’t be coming downstairs. Your Mother and Grandma are sorting some important stuff out and they won’t be happy if you are wandering around at this time of night. Daniel nods his head innocently Daniel: Yes daddy. Craig walks towards the door and opens it Craig: Night kiddo, Daniel: Night Dad.Craig walks out of the room, closing the door behind him, He pauses and takes a deep breathe, he then begins to walk down the stairs. He reaches the bottom to find Anne still lying motionless on the floor. Her old face has begun to turn pale and her eyes are still open wide. Craig looks around trying to find Julie and suddenly hears a faint voice coming from the living room. The sound of a silent crying whisper comes from the room as Craig walks in to find Julie knelt on the floor with the phone to her ear. Her other arm is covering her bloody chest. Julie: Yes…he’s here now…please come quickly, I beg you…. Please. She looks up to see his cold dark face staring her right in the eye as he walks closer. She pauses with a terrified look. She attempts to back away with the phone still in her hand, but it’s no use as Craig gains closer to her. He snatches the phone from her hand before hitting hard in the face. She falls back feeling her young mouth jilt and become swollen. She holds her face and wipes the blood from her lips, letting out a loud whine. Tears run down her face profusely as Craig steps forward once more, grabbing her by the hair, reaching into the back of his jeans and revealing the same blood-covered knife that he had used earlier to kill Julies mother. He puts it to her throat and smirks before running the blade smoothly across her throat. Julie gasps as she grabs at her bleeding neck. With one evil smirk, Craig plunges the knife at Julie in the jabbing motion she had seen only moments earlier. Julie feels every jab as if she’s been punched hard in the ribs. Julie gasps as she begins to feel faint. Craig walks to the kitchen and over to the sink, he begins to wash his hands under the silver tap, leaving small traces of blood over the tap handle. He watches the blood drip down the sink, almost mesmerised by the sight of it. He turns off the tap and grabs a small blue kitchen towel, he dries his hand. Craig then starts to walk back into the room and sees Julie’s body is now like her mothers, Motionless He stares for a moment, lost in his careless world. He suddenly hears a voice from behind him. Voice: How’s it feel? Craig turns around surprised. Shane Wethers stands before him wearing black baggy jeans and a black leather drench coat. Shane stands looking at Craig with wide angry eyes. Craig: Who the fuck are you? Shane smirks Shane: I’m your worse nightmare.. you might have heard that before…but I REALLY AM. Now tell me… How does it feel to have fucked up your son’s life? Craig grits his teeth and charges at Shane with his knife. He gets up close to Shane and finds he goes straight through him, almost like Shane was only an illusion, yet he still appears to be in the room. Craig: What the hell is going on? What are you? Some illusionist? Shane sighs and walks over to the bodies. He turns and looks at Craig Shane: No, I’m just here to torment you for the rest of your pathetic life. You see, I aren’t an illusion, I am actually here. The only thing is ‘I’m in your head’. I will be in your head for a long time because you have just destroyed your son’s life. Can’t you see the headline in the paper tomorrow? Muppet of a father kills the Mother and Grandmother of his child, And kills them in front of his child. You are one sick little man, that’s why I’m here to destroy every hope of happiness you ever have. Craig shakes his head Craig: I did what I had to do, I weren’t gonna let that whore fuck with me! Shane shakes his head with disgust Shane: You just don’t get it do ya? All the British Wethermaniacs across the nation are gonna be reading about your actions in tomorrows paper. They’ll be reading about how a sick, relentless, cold-hearted father killed the mother of his child and HER mother, right in front of their small child. Now what pisses me off is that they should be spending ninety-nine point nine percent of there concentration on Wetherlution fifty-friggin-eight! The night where the Eternal Punk talc’s up his hand and bitch slaps the scrawny faces of four PRIME Pickledicks in the Wetherlution ‘Shane just handed me my ass’ Invitational! Craig attempts to speak but Shane cuts him off Shane: Shut up! I’m speaking here Skippy, you’ve already had your say and you’ve just set a record by boring the whole nation. Now listen to me, get a pen and paper and write this down. Maybe I can project some Eternal light into your boring, waste of a life. Next Thursday in the morning papers you will read the headline ‘Shane Wethers victorious’, because on Wednesday I’m gonna take out each and everyone of those no-hopers who step into the ring. We’ve got a guy named after a shitty Vauxhall! I mean who calls himself Nova? Is he as slow as one? Cos if he is then I don’t want to embarrass him too much by kicking him around the ring like an over-used football. Craig nods his head and slouches in the corner of the room. Craig: Nova is quite a good athlete. Shane frowns at Craig and walks over to him, his big shadow belittles Craig. Shane: Did I ask you to speak? I don’t like people interrupting me Craig, don’t do it again. You’ve already pissed me off, I’d hate to have to remove each one of your limbs as a punishment to repent your sins. Shane turns away Shane: Now where was I? oh yes… Shane looks into space with a big smile on his face, beginning to picture a thought in his head. Shane: … ‘The Wetherlution Invitational’. My friend Tony Rolo will also be part of it, I’m sure he won’t be too disappointed when I climb that ladder and grab the bag because business is business. Also Tyler Lopez. Shane smirks and begins to sing in to the tune of J’Lo’s ‘Jenny from the Block’ Shane: He’s Tyler from the block with a tiny microscopic…. Shane pauses Shane: …career? Shane: Now who else do we have in this tournament. Craig looks up and responds Craig: Jon Kano. Shane turns to face Craig and scowls. Craig becomes immediately intimidated. His eyes widen as Shane closes in on him. All of a sudden blood begins to drip from the nose of Craig. He brings his hand to his nose and looks at his hand. Craig: Wha-what the hell is this? Shane shrugs. I’m no doctor but I’d say it looks like blood. Shane looks up at the dainty walls. Shane: This place reminds me of my first place when I was young. I used to live across the road from a Special Ed school. By the side of the road it had a sign that said ‘Slow, Children’. I used to think to myself ‘that can’t be good for their self-esteem’. Shane pauses and ponders Shane: Then again, they can’t read. Craig looks up at Shane, still in panic. Craig: What the fucks going on! I’m bleeding here. Shane turns Shane: Am I supposed to care? Shane stares at Craig as blood now begins drip from his ears as well. Craig brings his other blood covered hand to his ear and looks at it. Craig: What the F*CK! WHAT ARE YOU DOING TO ME! Craig begins to whimper in panic Shane: You are scared aren’t you? How does it feel to be scared Craig? Just like Julie and Anne were before you took a knife to them. All of a sudden the faint sound of Police sirens can be heard in the distance, getting louder as they approach. Craig attempts to stand but finds his give way. He continues to try to run away but his legs fail him. He cries out. Craig: What have you done to me? Shane shakes his head with a cold angry look on his face Shane: It isn’t what I’ve done to you. No, you did this to yourself Craig. Enjoy your time in hell. Shane turns and heads for the doors as the Police sirens grow louder and the sound of car doors slamming can be heard. The scene ends.
