Farewell

Date: Various As he stepped into Madison Square Garden, twelve hours before the live telecast of ReVolution 200, Craig felt the history course through his veins. Not only though of the pristine legacy that was the Garden, but that of the industry leader known as PRIME. Having served in that ring for two years, he remembered his time very well, but never did he feel as nostalgic about the history of what had taken place in it. Craig took a step closer to the ring and watched the various people who put their blood, sweat, and tears into making it all happen. Tonight, there was a certain aura about the way that everything just came together. The special design and decorations for the milestone telecast was certain to make it feel even more mythical. This was something that you didn't see that often. Places like PRIME, they closed up all of the time, and he had been witness to quite a few of them. Yet, PRIME set the marker, and so many failed to live up to it during that time. He had left under the worst of terms last time, but now he was being granted the chance to rectify that. Competing in front of these fans might not be any different than doing battle in SCCW, but there was something unique about it here. Craig had spent two years breaking his back for the company, fight after fight, feud after feud, and now they were showing him respect for that. Two hundred episodes of ReVolution had come to this. Tonight, would be a chance for the old and new to mingle, and yet, Craig didn't know where he belonged in that group. He had seen the list of returning superstars and knew he didn't belong with them. However, a new order had taken control of PRIME in the form of Jason Snow, Kaiser Vashaun, and Hessian. Craig knew for certain that his moniker, Dusk, didn't belong in that group either. In a way, he was in his own world, and he liked it like that. Most importantly, the fans liked that. A man, in his thirties, approached Craig with a soft look in his eyes. "Morning," he spoke in a hushed tone and Craig turned to look at him. "Need me to leave? I know you guys are working, but this might be the last time I ever get to see this happen. It's... different, I guess that's the word I'm looking for." The wave of nostalgia that Craig had felt while standing in the arena just stumbled out of his mouth while he spoke. The man though shook his head and put his hand on Craig's shoulder. "No, no, quite the opposite. Just wanted to come over and say hey. I've been putting together these sets since the very first show, but I could tell in your eyes how much this means to you." Craig could only nod his head. During his time here, he had just seen so much, and the memories he had made here always remained with him, whether they were good or bad. “For what it’s worth,” the stage hand continued. “I think Cantrell firing you was shit. These fans, they loved you.” Before Craig could open his mouth to respond, a young woman with a headset on her head approached him. “Excuse me, Mr. Maloof? Lisa Tyler would like to see you in her office.” Craig looked over at the stage hand and shrugged his shoulders. “Gotta go when the boss calls for you.” He shook hands with the man before he followed the young woman up the steel ramp and to the backstage area. *** “Look, Bruce, you get back on the fucking phone and get me my old contract back! Sure, PRIME might be the shit these days, but I don’t care. I’m this close to being at the top of Global!” Craig walked around his apartment, fury burned into his retinas. “Look, Craig, I would love to get you your old job, but I don’t think it’s going to happen. I just don’t know if it can be done! They fired you and they were pretty strict about it,” his agent informed him over the phone, but Craig wasn’t in the mood to hear it. “They fired me because I was going to upset the natural order over there. This is my comeback, Bruce, and I need to make it. I need to finish what I’ve started over there. PRIME isn’t going to allow me to do that. Not with this stable war that’s starting to brew and this overflow of new people they’ve got coming in. I’m going to be lost in that sea. I promise you that.” He continued to pace around his apartment, with each word coming from his agent sounding worst then the last one. “I think you need to seriously consider this offer, Craig. If not, I’m not sure I’m going to get you a better one. They’re willing to give you a chance even considering what went down with Global. They’re trying their best to blacklist you from the industry.” “Then put me on the phone with whoever I need to over there so I can kick someone in the face!” Craig’s voice raised a few degrees on the intensity scale. “I’ve put too much time and effort into turning that place around. When I stepped in, it was a cesspool of failure. I gave life.” There was silence on the other end, a silence that gave Craig the chills. “I don’t dispute that. I watched you seriously come alive over there, but at the end of the day it’s their decision. You might have just banked a shot at the World Championship, but they don’t care.” Little did Craig know that they were going to make sure no one remembered that event when they edited the show the next day. He just stood there in his apartment, facing the toughest decision of his life. “Fine,” he started as his head dropped to the ground. “Go ahead and fax over those documents for me.” “Good to hear you’re coming to your senses,” Bruce responded in a cheerful tone. Craig hung up the phone and threw it into the nearby couch as pure rage seemed to overtake his body. A moment later, he heard the beeping noise on his fax machine and walked towards it as he thought about how his career in PRIME would go. The pages started to come out and he picked them up and read it. The money was good, but he never truly cared about that. His career to this point had afforded him the ability to choose where he wanted to compete without fear of not having any money to his name. Everything he had heard about PRIME was good except for the fact that to get to the top was on the verge of impossible. A war had been brewing in PRIME and had exploded over the past couple of weeks, Fuck You going up against the anti coalition. It didn’t truly interest Craig though. The players over there, Nova, Sonny Silver, Angelo Deville, and Lindsay Troy was a who’s who of successful and nearly legendary competitors in the sport. Part of his fear was that he would be swallowed whole in this kind of environment. He had heard of the numerous signings that they had done as of late and he didn’t want to be another fish in a giant ocean, having to fight towards the top again. He was in the midst of doing that already. Another part of him though knew the challenge ahead of him was one that would push him to that next level. Competing against the best in the business would put his name everywhere and he could very well become a legend in the process. The only problem that he had with that is that he didn’t know if he was ready for that next step. Four years he had spent off the shelf. He needed time still, he was sure of that. Yet, this opportunity might never come again. Craig walked over to his kitchen table and sat down before he put his signature on the contract. It wasn’t like he had much of a choice left. *** Before Craig even had the chance to knock on Lisa’s door, it came flying open, and he came face to face with the large Head of Security, Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas. “Damn, Dam, give someone a warning next time,” Craig said as he took a step back from the monstrous frame of a man that could kill him by breathing on him. “Only Dam, not Dam Dam. I’m not some Flinstone’ character, aight?” he responded in the only fashion that Dam could. Craig started to open his mouth to explain that he wasn’t saying Dam Dam, but decided against it as the man left the office and left Craig alone with the woman in charge of PRIME at this exact moment. She smiled at him and sat back in her chair as he stepped in and closed the door. “You know, once,” Craig started as he walked over to the chair and took a seat in it. “I had to guess a password from him just so I could see Lindsay Troy. Never going to get over that.” Craig looked around her office before his red eyes fixated upon her. “Anyways, what’s up boss?” “Good to see you,” she told him and Craig had to do a double take as he couldn’t believe the words that were coming out of her mouth. “Are you talking to me? Weren’t you the same person that was pretty set on letting me just quit about six weeks before the Roulette was about to happen? You know, the original one.” She cracked a smile, a rarity in the world of Lisa Tyler. Craig was actually slightly frightened. “That’s the past, at least, that’s how I’d like to think about it. Plus, I just had Tyler Nelson hi-jack my show. Trust me, coming face to face with you is much better than having to deal with that again and the forty phone calls I got from Prometheus.” She seemed to be more relaxed on this day then Craig ever remembered of her. “So, this is pretty great, huh? ReVolution 200?” Craig nodded his head. “It’s truly something else, I guess you could say.” “I never thought I would be around to see it,” Lisa confessed. “Now that I am, it’s kind of bittersweet.” Then, silence filtered into the room as Craig felt the awkwardness as it settled into the room. “So,” Craig threw out there before he sat up in his chair and folded his left leg over his right one. “Yeah, let me just go ahead and cut to the chase. I don’t think it was fair to you that Cantrell fired you in the manner that he did it in. I think last year was a really good for you. You won the Intense Championship, placed fifth in the Dual Halo, headlines another Colossus VI, and if it wasn’t for Jason Snow, you probably would’ve made it to the finals of the Jewel in the Crown tournament. Unfortunately, he was in charge at the time, and you two never truly hit it off,” she rambled on and Craig did his best to follow along. “Well, thanks,” Craig offered, but could tell that Lisa wasn’t done. “I think you should come back. Actually, I’m prepared to offer you a contract to come back and wrestle exclusively for PRIME. You might not be the best employee backstage, but this is where you belong. I watch you in SCCW, and you’re doing great there, but this is your home, Craig. This is where you should be retiring and spending your final years. We might have done you wrong, but let me make it right to you.” Craig was taken by surprise and didn’t know what to say. “I, um, I love it there, Lisa.” She smiled. “I know you do, but you went there because you got fired from here. I want to offer you more money than you could possibly imagine, and a contract that is for a lifetime. There are few that would be synonymous with PRIME that’s still competing today. I truly believe you’re one of them.” Craig wanted to refuse right there, but Lisa didn’t give him the chance. “I know this is a lot, Craig. Do me a favor and think about it first. Just truly think about it. Let tonight sink in and remember what you loved about this place, alright?” He couldn’t refuse her that. “Sure, I’ll think about it,” he told her and got out of the chair. She smiled at him. “It’s odd, but it’s good to see you back here, Craig.” Instead of saying anything else, he just left the office as he felt this oddness come over him. *** ReVolution 116. Maraana Stadium, Brazil. It’s the last place that Craig ever expected to be. The contracts were only a few weeks old, but as he stepped into the arena an hour before he was expected to be there he got this sense of dread in his stomach. Part of him thought that he had made the wrong decision in joining PRIME. “Hey, Craig?” a young, female voice exploded into his mind and he looked around to see a smartly dressed woman walking towards him. As she reached him, her right hand came out, and introduced herself. “Lisa Tyler, it’s nice to see you’ve made it.” Craig accepted the handshake as he looked around the arena. “Nice to meet you Lisa. You’ll have to excuse me, but it’s been a while since I’ve been in Brazil and the last time that I was here wasn’t exactly the best time for me.” Lisa looked at him and was about to see if he would elaborate, but judging from the look in his eyes, she thought twice of it. “Anyways, I was asked by Chet Worth to go ahead and give you a tour, kind of walk you through our process, and help you feel like you’re at home. Now, if you’ll follow me,” she began, but Craig cut her off. “You’re the interview lady, right?” She nodded her head. “Okay, just wanted to make sure. Continue.” Lisa began to walk through the arena and Craig followed behind her. “Over here is our makeup and wardrobe department. They’ve been hard at work on getting everything together for you so you can make a successful debut this week. You’ll meet with them in thirty minutes to make sure everything fits right. Over there is the doctor’s office. Try not to fight in there, please.” They turned the corner. “Now, over here is Chet Worth’s office. It’ll change from week to week, but he likes to stay close to the doctor’s office. Some of the guys have a running joke that it’s because he likes to get the blue pill from him. Anyways,” she continued as they walked past threw a few of the newest signees to PRIME, including Pierce Lavelle. “Catering is always done by the best in the area, something that Chet has deemed more than necessary.” A few more steps and they turned down another corridor. “Normally, if you need to find me or one of the other interviewers, we’ll be in this office. You know, if we’re not already outside of your locker room. Now, fair warning, I’m the best and the others you’ll be dealing with here are simply bush league. So, try and get me for everything.” Craig smiled at the suggestion. “Pretty busy tonight,” Craig quipped as he looked around and saw the faces of Nova and Jason Snow passing by him before he was nearly tripped by the shortest man on the PRIME roster and the most recent Jewel in the Crown champion, Tony Gamble. “Nova and Jonathon Winters in our main event, so of course, things are tense for our Universal Champion. Don’t worry; you’re competing against the Illustrious Face-Eater and Garbage Bag Johnny.” Craig instantly recognized both of their names and nodded his head. Lisa stopped for a moment and looked at Craig. “The rule of the land here is to watch your own back. Nothing made that clearer when Nova turned his back on all of us. You’re going to learn pretty quickly that when you step into a PRIME arena, you’re never going to be safe. People make enemies very quickly around here and they will not stop until you’re done.” “Why is that?” Craig wondered? “Simple, PRIME is a revolving door of the top talent in the industry. People want to be at the top, because when you’re the top here, you’re considered the best around. That kind of platitude makes me people do things they wouldn’t normally do. Trust me, you’re probably already considered a threat to someone around here,” she told him and Craig just nodded his head. “Good to know.” Lisa began to walk off again and Craig followed behind her. She walked at a quick pace and Craig had to run to keep up with her until he found himself walking out onto the stage. “And this is where the action happens. All of it. At least, the good stuff. Once again, no expense spared as that’s one of the best rings available. Top notch productions qualities too, something that will be a marked difference from Global.” Craig chuckled. “Thanks.” “So, if we head back this way,” Lisa began and as Craig turned around to follow her, he ran smack into another man who sent a scowl his way. “Watch where you’re going, cunt face.” Craig stood there for a moment and watched the man walk down the ramp with an anger he had never quite seen from someone. Craig looked over at Lisa. “Who’s that?” She paused for a moment. “New guy. Think his name is Devin Shakur.” Craig nodded his head, making a mental note of him. “Maybe I’ll run into him again.” *** Four hours until show time, Craig sat in his locker room and looked at himself in the mirror. Years of battles had made him look tired even when he wasn’t. He lifted the black leather bag off of the ground and began to unzip it before he found his outfit for the evening. Craig’s intent for the evening had been to say his final goodbye’s, but Lisa wanted him around for longer. It was something that Craig hadn’t anticipated. “Look, I brought you a ham and cheese sandwich,” a familiar voice said behind him. Craig looked over his right shoulder and was surprised to see Devin Shakur standing there in the doorway. In his hands was a pre-packaged ham and cheese sandwich that one would find in a PRIME vending machine. He tossed it to Craig and then Devin showed him that he had one of his own as well. “Thought it would be fitting if this is your last show.” Craig just laughed as he opened the package and watched Devin walk to a seat across from him and sat in it before propping his legs up on the bench that Craig was sitting on. “Heard you were in the building, so thought I’d come see you. Not, you know, in a homosexual, Pierce Lavelle kind of way. Plus, with GBJ back in the house, whole crew is back together.” Craig took a bite out of his sandwich. “It’s sweet, especially considering the source.” “Don’t tell Dawkins about this or he’ll shit a pineapple.” Another laugh between the two former enemies was shared as they took another bite of their sandwiches. “So, what’re you wearing for the show tonight?” Devin asked Craig before he snatched the bag out of Craig’s hands. He looked around and held up the white jacket that he now wore. “Good, better than that emo trench coat shit you used to wear.” “Sorry, didn’t mean to infringe on your copyright,” Craig replied back quickly. “Damn straight.” As he looked through the bag, he found a contract in there, one that Lisa Tyler had given him before he left her office a few hours earlier. “Seriously? Are you coming back here for good?” Craig could only shrug his shoulders. “Lisa would like me to. Don’t know if I actually want to do it though.” “You know,” Devin said before he finished off his sandwich. “It’s weird not having you around. I mean, I hate you and all except when you do cool shit like eat a sandwich with me, but we started at the same time. Eight months we spent tearing each other’s faces off for one of the best feuds this place has ever seen. Even though you would be competition, it’d be good to see you come back.” Devin’s words filled Craig with thoughts he hadn’t thought before. “Though, fair warning, it’s not the same place like when you left here. It’s gotten nasty around here. Troy leaving took away most of the so called good guys around here, though your boy Noble is trying to hold down that fort. I mean, GBJ is back, but he’s just pure evil man. Got Hessian on his side. Jason Snow and Vashaun doing battle for the Universal Title. I'm being the top level douchebag now. A new order’s taken over.” Craig remained silent as he looked at the remaining pieces of his sandwich. “Not trying to scare you, but that’s just the way it is. You come back, you better prepare yourself for something completely different. “Thanks,” Craig responded as Devin got up and Craig finished his sandwich off. “Good to see you kid, fans might enjoy it also. Just don’t superkick me in the face.” Craig chuckled. “I’ll do my best, man.” “Good, because then I'll have to kill you” With that, The Man in Black left Craig alone in his locker room as Craig found himself looking at the contract again, trying to put the pieces together on his decision that Lisa Tyler would surely want tonight. *** The evening of March 15th, 2008 is one that Craig would always remember. As he laid in the doctor’s office, pain coursing through every part of his body, it was probably the moment he felt proudest of in his PRIME career. Two doctors looked over Craig and were seriously concerned about the condition of his shoulder. “Hold still,” they told him and Craig just nodded his head. Most wouldn’t be proud of finishing fifth in the Dual Halo, but for some reason, Craig did. Most didn’t make it as far as he did, especially after winning the Intense Championship the evening before, and that was an accomplishment in and of itself. The past few weeks, he had seen this marked improvement in his skills in the ring, as his time in PRIME had been a mixture of up and down moments. The fans, they loved him though, and that’s all that really mattered to him. He felt hands on his shoulder and cringed from the pain. “How does that feel?” “Well, doc, um, I think I’m going to go with that it hurts, a lot.” They laughed, but Craig didn’t. As he sat there in the office, the door opened, and in rushed High Flyer followed by Mary-Lynn and Tony Davis. “You alright man?” High Flyer asked, concerned for his closest friend. Craig nodded his head. “Sir, please, we need time to work with him.” Yet, Jack just ignored them and was beaming from ear to ear. “Dude, they’re talking about you. The fans, the wrestlers, the staff. They’re talking about how amazing you were out there. They saw you do things that they’d never seen before, sacrifice yourself, and become a true warrior in the Dual Halo tonight! It was amazing! I couldn’t be prouder of you if I tried!” Craig smiled, double the length of High Flyer’s, and laid his head back. This is what he wanted. For people to talk about him. “Move out of the way,” he heard the voice of Blaine Blair as he walked into the doctor’s office and walked over to Craig. “Is he going to be okay?” The doctors nodded their head before Blaine looked at him. “You five did an amazing job. Longest match ever. Most grueling match ever in PRIME history. Tyler Rayne, Cozen, Xavier Kannon, Crucifix, and you, you five have got the people talking. Rayne, of course, because he won it. Crucifix and Cozen for being the two unknowns in that ring and Kannon because he returned. But, you, they’re talking about how gutsy you were out there. I can’t believe what I saw. You earned your spot tonight, sir.” Craig didn’t know what to say and he looked around, proud. In the upcoming weeks, he would do battle with Wade Elliott and walk out the winner with the Intense Championship. Afterwards, he would square off against Tyler Rayne and come close to pulling off an upset. He would enter the Jewel in the Crown tournament and put on a strong showing. He would face Bryan Dawkins and barely lose to a man who would be considered the Rookie of the Year. “So, what’s next?” High Flyer asked him and Craig didn’t know the answer to that. “Time to take PRIME by storm, my friend,” Craig responded, still smiling. Tonight, would be the night that Craig remembered for the rest of his life. *** “Ready, old man?” Craig, dressed for tonight’s show, which would begin in less than twenty minutes, looked towards the door and saw the Rookie Sensation, David Noble, standing there with a huge smile on his face. “I think so,” Craig responded as he began to feel some butterflies enter his system. “I’ve gotta say, Craig, it’s great to see you here, back in PRIME. It’s where I became a huge fan of yours. This is where you belong, no question about it.” Craig shook his head. That’s what he had heard all day long, that PRIME was where he had belonged, but for the past few hours, as he sat in the hallway and watched the wrestlers enter, he didn’t feel that way. Devin had made a solid point in that the face of PRIME was changing. “Lisa offered me a contract,” Craig confided in David and the young man couldn’t believe it. “You’re coming back?! Alright!” David exclaimed, but Craig shook his head, which sobered the Rising Star. “The opposite, this is it for me. I came here to help you out David, be your teammate, but I don’t think this is the place for me. Far from it if you ask me. This place is not the place that I helped build for two years. The people here, they’re so far removed from the purpose of why you compete in PRIME,” Craig told David and David stood there for a moment. “What is it?” Craig smiled at him. “To compete for the fans. You have your Jason Snow’s and Kaiser Vashaun’s, they’re here for the glory and titles. So many of these guys, they lost sight of what matters the most in the end, and you know me. Competing for the fans, that’s the most amazing feeling you’ll ever have in this sport.” David didn’t agree though. “Then prove that to them. Come back and show them why they’re wrong. You can compete with these people, I know you can. They don’t hold a candle to your abilities and your drive, I’ve seen it this year more so than ever before,” David told him and it was kind to say, but didn’t change Craig’s mind. “No, it’s your turn to show them. Your excitement when you get in that ring, the fans feed off of that, and because of that, you feed off of the fans. They want to see you out there David, and it’s going to be amazing to watch you grow. You’re the future of this company, if you let yourself be. Maybe not this year, but I see you compete, and you’re on the verge of something great,” Craig told his pupil and David smiled at his mentor. “Thanks,” he said sheepishly and Craig just grinned. “So, ready to do this thing?” David asked Craig and the former PRIME Intense Champion nodded his head. He was ready to do it in a PRIME ring, one more time. Fin.
