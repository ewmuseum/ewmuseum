Meet Dom

17 February 2006

Date: 17 Feb 06 “Alright I’ll tell you the truth!” It’s funny how they all say that eventually. No matter how hard they try to be some tough bad ass and no matter how much they don’t want to be the snitch. I guess it could be my incredible negotiation skills, I mean throughout high school and in my time in the military women just could never say no to me. Could be my intimidating features, all 6’2” 245lbs of me, rock solid. Maybe it’s the long dark hair that does them in? People usually think a guy with long hair, wears dark clothing, must be crazy right? Or it could be the berretta that I have pressed up against his forehead. “Well that’s really good pal because I would hate to get brain matter all over my new shoes.” I chuckle softly and take a seat right across from my hostage, making sure the weapon in my hand never leaves its spot on his forehead. “This doesn’t have to be difficult Angel, it really doesn’t. I am going to ask you a few questions and all you have to do is answer them to my liking. Got it?” “I got it.” Angel was just a two bit clown, some young punk that got his kicks from dropping pills in young girls’ drinks and helping them find their way to his car or his apartment. A crime this is, actually a crime that I hate very much but for now he has a much more important purpose to me. “Well first off I think we both know exactly why it is that I am here. If we did not both know why I was here, you would not have run when you saw me isn’t that right?” He doesn’t say anything. I scoot my chair closer, dragging it on the tile floor beneath me. “Angel THAT is when you talk.” “I was only going to get a small portion of the shipment. I am no big time seller man and you know that.” “Right your drug of choice doesn’t quite get your victims high does it?” I crack my neck before taking in a deep breath. “Where were the rest of these going?” “Look I was only taking them so far, after that I really don’t know who…” “TRUTH!” I say digging the barrel of the pistol into his head. He closes his eyes and grits his teeth. I know what he wants to do to me, it’s all over his face and written in those eyes of his when they slowly open back up. Unfortunately for him he doesn’t speak again fast enough for my taste. “Fine.” I cock back and send the pistol upside the side of his head, letting out a sickening crack of his skull. His head jerks to the side and he lets out a muffled whimper. It was a sound that I relished, a sound that could really make my fucking day. I replace the pistol at his forehead. He stares at me with that fire in his eyes, even as blood from a gash over his eyebrow trickles down. “You must not have heard me the first time Angel. You need to answer me, not only that but these answers better sure as fuck meet my damn requirements!” I calm myself, forcing a smirk. I guess I should try to ease his nerves. “But I’ll give you another chance; after all I am an equal opportunist. Now where were those drugs going?” “I only have a first name.” “Then spill it.” “David. Some big time mother fucker from Detroit. He has been doing this shit for years and the police have never been able to take him down.” “What does he look like?” “Sorry I’ve never met him.” “Look you son of a…” I press the pistol to his forehead harder. “I have no problems sending a low life like you straight to hell!” “LOOK I SAID I NEVER MET HIM!” He was telling the truth this time. Not sure if it was that look of hopelessness in his eyes, the tears, or that lump in his throat but I figured I could cut him a break. It’s not often that assholes like this get breaks from me. I smile at him. “Alright I believe you. Why can’t they take him down?” “Lots of reasons, money, power, lies, deceit. Look you lived in Detroit when you were a child and YOU never even met the guy. The best you can hope for is to continue to bust his shipments. You cops aren’t going to catch this guy.” “Really?” I raise an eyebrow. “Look at me you little piece of shit, do I look like a cop to you? Does it look like I am ready to “go by the book”? All of the pawns for these criminals say the same thing and guess what? They all fall eventually. This “David” character will do the same. It’s all a matter of time.” I glance at my watch quickly, 11:33 PM. “Well look at the time. I must be going, I am sure those boys in blue will be here any minute. Angel I suggest you try and make it out of here before they roll in here and catch you with all of these drugs.” I stand up and begin walking away. I can tell by the lack of footsteps coming from behind me that young Angel hasn’t moved yet. Probably surprised that I let him off with just a small gash over his eye. Normally our run-ins end up a little bit worse. I stop and turn to face him, he quickly stands up. “Angel stay out of trouble, before something unfortunate happens.” BANG! The gunshot rings through the dark warehouse, it’s like music to my ears. Angel crumbles over holding himself where the bullet pierced his skin. Let me tell you as a man, you never want to be shot in this area. I can see the blood dripping from out of his hands and falling onto the floor. I guess I should feel bad … but I rarely ever do with boys like this one. “Like that.” I put the pistol away and walk away. 3:24 AM Motel 6 The room wasn’t much but it was all I had at the moment. Well that and my Camaro but it wasn’t exactly the most comfortable thing to sleep in. I lay in bed staring at the off white ceiling and the broken light that dangled from it. Rooms like this one had become my life and up until a few months ago my only companion would be the random woman, be it a hooker or just someone who needed to be fucked right and my bottle of Jose’ Cuervo. Let me tell you something about old Jose’, unlike most people he will never turn his back on you. When you need Jose’, he will be there to take all of your sorrow and pain away. Speaking of which I grab the bottle without looking over at the desk it rested on and bring it to my lips. I felt the warm liquor run down my throat, past my chest and let it rest in my stomach. I take one more swig before slamming the bottle down back on the table and taking in a deep breath. The air around me was suffocating. The air tasted old, if that is at all possible. It felt like death really, something I had seen plenty of in my day. Hell for all I knew I could have killed somebody tonight, fucking Angel. Just the thought makes me chuckle out loud; really I didn’t even realize I was doing it. “Rest in peace you little shit.” “Well he is not dead.” I tried to sit up quickly, the voice startling me so. It did not help though as instead, with a mixture of it being dark and too much Tequila I rolled right off the side of the bed, my head hitting the night stand. Of course this did not help the small problem of impaired vision. “Who…Who are you?” “Get up Dominic, its Stephen.” Fucking Stephen. I fight to get to my feet which I do get to albeit a bit wobbly. All I really see is a silhouette of a man but the voice told me all I needed to know. “What is it Stephen?” I ask, trying to hide the annoyance in my voice. “What was that tonight? You know you should have called us before doing anything. This was in MY jurisdiction…” “Look I don’t work for you Stephen, I don’t work with you, and that isn’t going to change.” “Those men need to be tried and convicted. You cannot continue to take this stuff into your own hands. If you do …” At this point it all kind of turned into an episode of “Charlie Brown”, how when adults begin talking and it just sounds like something incoherent. My brother was a good man but he had this problem. He believed in people. It would more than likely be his downfall. “Get over it Stephen. We are brothers but that doesn’t give you the right to tell me what to do.” I sit down on my bed. “Now if you’ll excuse me, I have a long day tomorrow.” “Fine Dominic … do me a favor though?” “Whatever you want.” “Meet me at Denny’s at 7AM.” “7 AM!?” I let out a sigh, knowing I won’t be able to get rid of him without agreeing. “Fine … 7AM it is.” I hear the door shut as I lay my head down on the pillow. Again I am left with Jose’ and the smell of death. I guess Dorothy was right. There’s no place like home. 7:12 AM DENNY’S “You’re late.” “It’s only twelve fucking minutes Stephen, chill.” “Just letting you know.” “Look Stephen if you are mad about last night I’m sorr…” He slams a manila envelope on the table. I raise an eyebrow as I stare down at the unmarked envelope. My eyes move up to Stephen’s with obvious questions. “It’s what we have been talking about Dom.” He says with a small smile finding its way on his lips. “Big time.” I grab the envelope and rip it open. A Contract. Two to be exact. “How the hell did you manage this?” “Does it matter?” He leans in closer. “All we have to do is sign our names. What do you say?” It didn’t take much thought. How could it? “Got a pen?” PRIME here we come.
