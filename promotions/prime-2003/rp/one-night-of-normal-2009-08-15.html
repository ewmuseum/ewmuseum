One Night of Normal

15 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8/15/09 The night air is crisp in Boston, mere hours after the first night of the biggest pay per view on PRIME’s calendar. Shivers and goose bumps would generally be the norm for someone as they sat on the balcony of their hotel room in nothing more than a pair of boxer shorts. Not for Tyler Nelson. Not on this night. Sipping from a crystal flute full of champagne, the Greediest Player in the Game savors the moment. The lounge chair on the balcony is comfortable, allowing his mind to wander. It was good to feel normal again, even if he had to empty his bank account to do it. This was a night to be celebrated, and he certainly knew how to do that: a VIP table at Centerfolds, a rented stretch Hummer limo, followed by a suite at a five star hotel. What was once a nightly occurrence was now a one-night-only delicacy. He was like a junkie in full relapse, high once again off his former lifestyle. But things weren’t all peaches and cream coming out of Colossus. As he continues to sip on the bubbly in his glass, breathing the midnight air into his tired lings, he ponders the ramifications of his deal with Lisa Tyler. With his victory over Killean Sirrajin, he was now an active wrestler again. While that drug him out of the backstage interviewer gutter, and put some extra money in his pocket, it also put him in a perilous position. He would be subject to the booking whims of one Lisa Tyler, and given their recent history, that would almost certainly cause problems. But that was a worry for another time. “There you are.” The voice purred like a content kitten from behind him. Her blonde hair is mussed, the recent victim of the throws of rapture. She holds a bed sheet loosely around her naked form, allowing various glimpses of her wonderfully proportioned body to be seen as she moves out onto the balcony. Tyler smirks as she runs her soft hand delicately around his neck from behind. “Why don’t you come back inside?” she asks, bending down to playfully nibble on his earlobe. “I thought I already did that,” Tyler muses. He downs the remainder of the champagne and tosses the flute over the balcony. The woman releases a slight gasp, then giggles quietly as Tyler climbs out of the lounger. “I had a good time tonight.” He leans in and kisses the woman gently on her ample, collagen enhanced lips. She reaches up and hangs her arms around his neck, allowing the bed sheet to drop to the ground. She’s caught off guard, however, as Tyler quickly disengages and walks into the hotel room. She stands there, looking quite puzzled. After a few moments, she reaches down and retrieves the sheet, pulling it back up around her body. She wraps herself a little tighter this time, clutching the sheet close to her body. Tyler flicks the light switch on, illuminating the multi-room suite. The decorating is rather contemporary, with bright white walls and carpet serving as the backdrop for the black leather furniture in the sitting area. There are clothes sprinkled across the floor in a trail from the entrance of the suite to the oversized leather couch. The King of Greed makes his way to the wet bar and pulls the refrigerator door open, retrieving a bottle of water from inside. “So, you had a good time?” Her voice is slightly agitated. “Is that supposed to mean that we’re done?” Her head whirls toward the door as several raps are made against it from the hallway. She looks back at Tyler, who casually takes a drink from the water bottle. “Yes.” The words sting her, even though she’s been in this position before. Many times she’d gone home with someone from the club who had thrown their money at her, and almost every time it ended like this. She always hoped to catch that one big fish, but the big one always seemed to get away. Little did she know that the night’s activities weren’t the norm anymore for the former mega-millionaire, so he was probably doing her a favor. Nevertheless, she haughtily scampered around the room, gathering her clothes before heading into the bathroom. The door slams behind her as Tyler walks over and answers the door. “I thought I told you to come up and hour ago?” Tyler’s annoyance is evident as he allows Mr. Simmons to enter the suite. “You seemed like you were having a good enough time.” The bodyguard shrugs his shoulders, a large manila folder tucked under his arm. He’s dressed down a bit, sporting a black t-shirt, blue jeans, and tennis shoes. “I figured I’d give it a little extra.” “Don’t figure next time.” Tyler points toward the bathroom door. “Make sure she finds her way out.” The bodyguard nods his head and takes a position outside the bathroom door as Tyler disappears into the bedroom. Soon the young woman reappears, fully clothed, if you can call it that. She fulfills the stripper stereotype in a tight, white halter top, black leather mini-skirt, and knee-high black leather boots with a four inch heel. She clutches a small purse in her hand, firing a glare at Mr. Simmons. “Oh, he can’t bother to show me the door himself?” Simmons simply outstretches his arm toward the door. “Ma’am.” She snorts disgustedly, tears beginning to well up in her eyes. She can’t decide if she’s angrier at Tyler or herself. It’s a familiar cycle that will more than likely play itself out again in the future. She storms out the door, disappearing behind it as Simmons latches it shut. “She’s gone.” A few seconds later Tyler emerges from the bedroom, sporting a pair of black warm-up pants and a white t-shirt. He passes Simmons on the way to the large leather couch and snatches the folder from under his arm. “Were you able to find someone to look over the contract?” Tyler plops down into the comfort of the supple leather. Mr. Simmons grabs a bottle of water from the fridge for himself. “Yeah. Everything seems to be in order.” “There are no loopholes or hidden clauses?” “Not that he could find.” Simmons takes a seat in the black leather chair at the end of the couch. “It looks like it’s just a standard wrestling contract.” Tyler flips the folder open and scans the pages inside, his brow furrowed. After several minutes of intent reading, he reaches the final page. On cue, Simmons leans over and hands Tyler a pen. The Greediest Player in the Game never even looks as he reaches out and takes the pen from Simmons. After a brief pause, he scribbles his signature on the document and closes the folder. “Have this sent over to her directly.” He hands the folder back to Simmons and tosses the pen onto the glass table in front of the furniture. Simmons places the folder in the table, then leans back in the chair. He takes a long pull from the bottle of water. It’s been a long night for the both of them. The bodyguard looks over at Tyler, whose head is leaning back against the sofa. “You know she’s going to try and break you, in more ways than one.” The King of Greed doesn’t flinch. “I’ve got my own agenda, Simmons. I’ll not allow her to force any deviation from that. When all is said and done, I’ll be the one left standing. I can guarantee you that.” Simmons finishes off the bottle of water and rises from the chair. “I’ll let you get some sleep. We have to check out by noon.” He gathers the folder and pen from the table, then heads toward the door. Tyler stops him, however, just as he reaches for the door handle. “What about that other thing I asked you to do?” Simmons looks back over his shoulder. “It’s almost taken care of.” “Good.” Tyler waves his hand dismissively at Simmons, authorizing the bodyguard to leave. He slips quietly through the door, barely making a sound. Tyler closes his eyes as he rests on the couch. Goose bumps start to crawl over his skin, and his body shudders slightly. It’s not from being cold, however. The Greediest Player in the Game has a plan, and the thought of its outcome sends chills up his spine.
