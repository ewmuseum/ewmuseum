How We Roll...

Date: Read it Previously in the Devin Shakur story… Battle lines were drawn Michael DeAngelis sat at his desk with anger traveling through his veins, and demanded a meeting with Devin Shakur. Anger was put to the side Shannon Austin and Valerie Swanson reluctantly agreed to play defense and not attack Shakur after he murdered Nathan Murray. An unexpected general took his throne. Michael Blaze revealed that he had got a scheme cooking in his mind. …And death warrants were signed Rachel Smith purchased a rocket launcher from an unknown dealer. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Smith Time: 10/09/07, 19:35 Christian Daniels leaned up against the blue wall of the Smith’s international casa with his arms folded across his chest and a glass of Jack Daniels at his left side. His attention was fixated squarely upon the eyes of Michael Blaze, the man who had just proclaimed victory like he was Thomas Dewey, circa 1948. "Alright Washington, what is this ingenious Tet Offensive you got in your brain?" The other two players for Team Smith, Chris Smith and Jason Hardy, turned their attention to Blaze, ears burning with interest. They also wanted to know what in the world this psychopath could formulate in his mind. "Well…It goes a little something like this." Michael took a sip of lemonade, drawing out the moment just a little bit longer. He knew the entire room was hooked like deep suburban teenagers on crack cocaine. "What do we know about our opposition?" And much like the same teenagers having sex for the first time, the suspense fell flat prematurely. "Man shit, you ain’t got a plan." Chris blurted out, pulling his hair back into a ponytail before emitting a huge sigh. "It’s a real question Con." Blaze said, now mimicking Christian in folding his arms. "We know that they know." Jason replied, slamming both of his feet up on the mahogany table. Michael stood up and walked around the room like an animate professor getting to his favorite part of the semester, "Ah yeah, but do they really KNOW or do they just know?" His audience, much like the students, didn’t give a fuck for the dramatics and just wanted to get on with the show. Michael remained undeterred. "The only thing that they know is about Devin. They have no idea that he’s still in contact with us. For all they know, he’s hired some independent mercenary to deal with them while he hogs the spotlight with the reduced cost scarred male prostitute midget and a geriatric plagiarist who has a fetish for long…microphones." The three stone cold assassins stared at one another in disbelief before giving a look of death to their orator. Christian decided to speak on behalf of the group seeing as how they were all on the same mental wavelength. "They…Don’t know about us…That is your big plan…You wasted this much time just to declare that they don’t believe we still roll…Allow me to speak for common sense when I say…DUH!" Unharmed by the scathing backlash, Michael just cracked a smile and stared back at the Death gang leader. "They cannot go outside a specific parameter. They cannot be independent and subjective. They will drink the poisoned cup because it tastes delicious." "If you say "use soap" next, I’m going to shoot you in the mouth, Durden." Chris said, walking past his compadre to get more alcohol. He had a feeling that it would be required to get through the evening. Michael deduced that it was time to give away a piece of the puzzle. "My specific plan involves 50,000 dollars, a rocket launcher, some Oscar worthy acting, and getting some LEGIT inside information." And the collective ears yearned once more. "I was just on the phone with Rachel, and she’s going to get in contact with a guy who has some really powerful shit..." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Five Minutes Ago Within the confines of a small apartment, the lone watchdog of Devin Shakur sat and waited patiently for a phone call. She lived a block and change away from him, with a view good enough to see the majority of his residence. Once Rachel felt the vibrations of the phone, she whipped her hand across to snag it. "Alright Rach, we’re going to do things a little bit different this time." Michael said. With a bland tone, Rachel replied "I’m listening." "I want you to kill Valerie and Shannon ASAP." She brushed the comment off like it was a simple hello, "So we are moving that up? Any specific requests or just off them?" Michael took a deep breath before he continued. "I want this to be big, huge, send a message kind of killing." "Getting his attention so that he knows this isn’t a joke?" "Ding" "How do you want me to go about it?" "Are you any good with a rocket launcher?" The first sign of exasperation was expressed on Rachel’s face, "Where am I going to get one of those? They don’t exactly grow on trees." "Don’t worry about getting it, just worry about shooting. Can you do that?" Rachel sighed again, "You know I don’t like having to go outside my arsenal to other people. Is it really necessary to use something that big?" Oozing confidence from his voice, Michael replied "Shooting them with a gun is so 2006. I want to start a new trend. Now, I need to know if you shoot a damn rocket launcher." "Yeah, I know how to shoot an RPG." "Fantastic, here is what we are going to do…" Michael leaned up against the porch outside of the house, kicking pieces of the rocky driveway beneath his feet. "You know how to do the basics, watch them and get their routine down pat. You’ve followed them before so you have the places they like to hang out…" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Rachel Recap: 10/10/07-10/15/07 For the past week, Rachel sat four parking lots away from the Braintrust headquarters with binoculars during the mornings. She noted any pertinent movements. During lunch, she watched from across the street while they drank coffee. After work, she followed them to the bar where they blended in with the normal crowd. Just before 10 PM, she watched the duo enter their two-story home. End Recap ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Michael continued, "After you have that down, then I’ll have you meet my guy somewhere." "How much is the RPG going to cost?" "I would say about 50, no more than that." "How much time am I going to have to get the money?" "A couple of days I’d say. I’ll call you back on Friday or Saturday with the information, give you enough time to get a grasp for the weapon and find a good location to shoot from." Michael moved around in a circle now, pacing a trail in the rocks and dust beneath. "I don’t know the specific layout of their house, but according to Google Earth there is a park nearby and close enough in distance to where you can set yourself up." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Rachel Time: 10/16/07, 20:00 The only activity in the park was some people sitting on benches, and others walking through to enjoy the serenity of the scenery. Rachel sat quietly with a bright red wig and a long jacket to accommodate for the chilly weather. In her lap was the same carrying case which contains the harbinger of destruction. She had been waiting for about an hour, listening to her iPod and looking for her targets to come within her line of sight. Her objective with the flashy exterior was to throw people off the scent just in case anybody looked at her suspiciously. Finally the women appeared in front of the house and took a gander across at the park. A quick second later, they scurried up the stairs enveloped in conversation. The blonde reached for keys, and wanted to open the door, but an element of the conversation halted her. For a couple of minutes, they continued to talk and laughed merrily. Rachel had decided to make her move, no longer content with sitting anxiously on the bench to wait them out. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: ??? Time: 10/16/07, 20:05 Two women dropped their bags behind a door after a long day of doing back breaking labor just to keep their careers and dreams afloat. The brunette woman, continuing her jolly laughter from outside, walked into the kitchen and opened the refrigerator. She pulled out a bottle of beer and went to a shelf to pour the liquid into glasses. While she was in the kitchen, the blonde woman let her hair down and removed her jacket. "Do you ever think that they are going to get that building finished?" The brunette said to the blonde The blonde walked into the kitchen and took her glass of beer off the table. "I really hope so because I’m damn sick and tired of having to hear that awful noise all the time." "You know what we ought to do?" The brunette asked, before leaning up against the refrigerator. "Go over there and threaten to kill them like we do everybody else?" The blonde said before both busted out into hyena like laughter. The brunette cracked a beautiful smile "You read my mind, girl, cheers." Both girls clanged their glasses together and took a sip of liquid courage. Sometimes one’s gut becomes sick with eerie premonition and tries to alert its guardian. The blonde suddenly had a searing pain in her stomach and almost doubled over to the floor. Immediately, the brunette rushed to her side and attempted to console her. "What’s wr-" KAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAABOOM! The RPG slammed into the front door. From a respectable distance, a cacophony of horror rose into the atmosphere. Strangers stood with jaws agape at the destruction in front of them. Their individual voices were inconspicuous but their intent was clear. "HOLY SHIT" "SOMEONE CALL 911!" "DIDN’T TWO PEOPLE JUST GO IN THERE?" "JESUS CHRIST! SOMEONE CALL 911!" Like the kid who picked the most creative place to stow himself in Hide N Seek, Rachel appeared from behind a bush with the carrying case slung over her shoulder in a large tennis bag. The red wig was still on her head and she grinned like a Cheshire cat at the destruction before calmly walking the opposite way into the darkness of the park. Nobody in the crowd suspected a thing. The audience was frozen with fear staring at the house. Elsewhere From their firehouse eight miles away, the 22nd saw the carnage and knew that it was too massive to be ordinary. All of the firemen rounded themselves up and got into their respective trucks. The men knew that they wouldn’t be getting to sleep early this evening. Half An Hour Later at the scene Two women emerged from around a corner, rushing to the scene like distraught parents being informed that their daughter was just taken away by a kidnapper. "THAT’S OUR HOUSE! WHAT THE SHIT?" "FUCK, VAL! An overwhelmed Valerie Swanson grabbed a spectator and spun him toward her. "WHAT HAPPENED?" "It…It…Just went up into a ball of flames!" The man, not really sure not to react, responded. The two women joined the throng of onlookers, only capable of whispering a sentiment here and there. "All of our shit is in there…" "How could someone do such a thing…" To shock these women into stunned silence was an accomplishment unto itself. If assassinating Nathan Murray enraged the women, one can only imagine how vicious their forthcoming assault will be on the responsible culprit. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Smith Time: 10/09/07, 19:43 "Wait, wait, wait so your masterful plan is just to blow their house up with a rocket launcher, and run away." Christian said, now bringing the situation back to reality. "The only thing that is going to do…" Chris chimed in, "…Is get Devin killed that much faster. Even if you take out those two, the boss is still going to have money. If the boss man has money then he can hire people. If he can hire people, well shit is going to happen. I’m not going to go through with this unless we have some sort of safety net in place." Jason and Christian firmly nodded their heads in agreement while Michael just withstood all of the criticism. "You guys are seriously underestimating me here. Do you think that I would go through all this trouble, have this entire plan set up, and not have a safety net involved?" Everybody in the room looked at one another and shook their heads in a yes manner. "Wow, I really have to lay off the chicken if I come across as this much of a joke. The bottom line is this... All of what I said will be an advantage for us and Devin once it’s all mapped out. But blowing their shit up with a rocket launcher…Ha, that’s not the fun part… Two slender female individuals, who look like characters that the story is very familiar with, entered the room. Everybody and now realized that the funny man actually knew a thing or two about the art of war. Michael pointed behind him to them and gave a condescending grin at his brothers in arms. "That is" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: ??? Time: 10/16/07 8:40 It also appeared that Rachel missed her target and took two innocent lives down in an attempt to gain a strategic advantage in a war over the lives of Devin Shakur and Tony Gamble… Inside the burning wreckage… Two petite bodies burnt to a crisp, identification will only be capable via dental records once the flames are extinguished. …Or did she? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Team: Shakur Time: 10/16/07, 20:45 Devin Shakur sat on his couch and stared blankly into the television, waiting on the pointless commercials to conclude. Most of the wrestlers in PRIME aren’t still relaxing in their home on a Tuesday night. Then again most do not have the luxury of a private jet. Devin continued flipping channels with the remote. He eagerly awaited one of his favorite shows, NCIS, to delivering its culmination for this week’s episode. Once he passed the opera music channel, something caught his attention. News 14 Carolina, Your News 24 Hours A Day Breaking News: Massive Home Fire Always a worrisome bloke or perhaps more intrigued than anything else, Devin forgot about Gibbs, McGee, and the damn cute Abby to focus on this story. An anchorwoman named Jill Hansen appeared on the screen. "For those of you just joining us, this is a story that broke a couple of moments ago. In downtown Raleigh, a house fire started at 8:30 this evening. Witnesses said that they have no idea how the fire started, but the house erupted into a massive fireball. We’re going to go live to the scene with Matthew Williams. Matt what updates have transpired since we last checked in?" At the scene of the blaze, a black man in his mid thirties stood a good distance away from the burning inferno. He made sure to get the fire and his own arrogant self in the camera shot. "Thanks Jill, firefighters are still continuing to arrive on the scene from neighboring counties. Some witnesses believe that two individuals had gone inside the home just minutes before the explosion." A couple of moment earlier, Matthew had a microphone in the face of a sleek businessman who was walking through the park. "I done saw two people walk in dat house. I’s on my way up to da store, they gone in the house, and then all a sudden the house go up in flames." Another witness, a woman who could pass for a college student, appeared next. "I didn’t see how it happened, but two women were talking on the porch a couple minutes prior to the fire." Back to Matthew Williams "In a brand new development, the two owners of the home have just arrived on the scene. Their names are Valerie Swanson and Shannon Austin." The names sent a shockwave through Devin. Most of the puzzles pieces immediately fell into place. This was not some sheer coincidence anymore. This was the first step in a long journey… Devin thought he knew… The house phone rang, and almost caused Devin to hit the ceiling. He reached over and took the phone before placing it up to his ear. "Kid, we got some splaining to do." The raspy voice of Christian Daniels said on the other end of the line. But he didn’t really have a clue…
