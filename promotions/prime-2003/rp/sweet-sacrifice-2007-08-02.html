Date: August 2nd, 2007 Sweet Sacrifice by Craig Maloof Dedication: To those who have believed in me. This piece is for you. Thank you. ---- Chapter One: The End Draws Near Night begins to fall, but the man behind the desk doesn't even realize that at all. Instead, his eyes scan over documents like they've been doing for months now. He can feel himself moving closer and closer to his final destination, knowing that he can start to see the light at the end of the tunnel. His right hand slowly moves over to his Styrofoam coffee cup, which is branded by Starbucks. Internally, he's focused on the words before him which causes all of his motor functions to slow dramatically. The instance that his hand touches the cup though he sees something that surprises him. His right hand flinches ever so slightly causing the cup to tip over and pour out its contents. Any normal man would start to clean up the mess, but this man, a man so obsessed with another human being, is oblivious to it all. Instead, the clues start to piece themselves together almost like the most difficult jigsaw puzzle known to man. "You plan on cleaning that up, Daniel?" a voice echoes from across the room. However, he hears not a syllable of it. Instead, his dark brown eyes, with the aid of a pair of glasses, continue to read a piece of paper that seems to put things in context much more than he could've ever imagined. Nearly eight months had gone by as he investigated the death of Jocelyn White. His prime suspect was becoming a wrestling superstar before his very eyes while Daniel continued to try and prove that the man known across the world as Dusk, but known to Daniel as Craig, had murdered his wife. For the first six months, he felt like his efforts were being wasted, and he would never be able to link Craig to this. Then, he found a statement. A very simple statement that he had somehow glossed over; a statement from a seemingly normal village dweller. However, the words on the piece of paper had finally struck him as being odd. The man would come around here time and time again, a hallowed look upon his face, as he would go up the mountain and battle through the hells that the Gods have unleashed upon that place. He would be there for a few days, and then you would see him again, looking worse and worse. They say that they teach you about hell up on that mountain, and he's the first one I've ever seen go up on that mountain willingly. There is just something not right about that mountain, something that just doesn't gel with me. The description the man would give to Daniel was one that seemingly matched Craig, but he had yet to find any proof that Craig had even visited the mountain once, much less several times. However, he continued to look down that avenue, hoping to see something that he hadn't seen before. It wasn't until he had read the statement of a flight attendant, one of the few to travel to that city several times over the course of a few months, that he was able to place Craig there. Craig was far craftier then Daniel had given him credit for as he had been flying under an alias. Jack White. A simple fairy tale character that had fooled the attendant. Even with Craig being as smart as he was though, he had still charged the tickets on a now cancelled credit card. The dots were starting to connect for Daniel. But, it all came together when Daniel was able to sit down with the attendant again after it started making sense to Daniel. There was one airline ticket missing, the one that would connect him to returning to Los Angeles the evening that Jocelyn White was murdered. It had been the attendant that had placed Craig on that airplane earlier the same day. This all made sense especially because of a conversation he had with a woman who was part of the so called hell mountain. She had been secretive, Daniel knew this going in, but had given him some clues as to Craig's mentality leading up to his wife's murder. He had found Craig to be in an extremely dark place; one that even the woman had called suicidal and homicidal. Now, he had found the missing airline ticket; after several subpoenas he had found it and finally realized why it was so difficult to track down this ticket. He had paid for it with cash. "Daniel!" the woman yelled again and this time he looked up at her. She looked rather stern standing across the hall. Slowly, Daniel removes his glasses and rubs his eyes, the pressure starting to get to him. He then notices that he has finally knocked down his coffee and groans rather loudly. The look on the woman's face is priceless, almost as if she wants to smack him back into reality. "I'll clean it up Lindsay, thanks," he tells her and gets up to get some paper towels. As he walks across the office, something catches his eye. A manila folder. He stops himself and finds himself lost in his thoughts again, the idea of cleaning up the spilled coffee leaving his mind behind. Instead, the folder now interests him. It simply reads Confidential: For Daniel Stark's Eyes Only. He looks up at Lindsay whose face doesn't even register that she knows something that Daniel doesn't. She had been good at keeping him appraised of new information, and knows that something like this wouldn't have slipped past her attention. He picks it up and glances back at her once again. "When'd this come in?" he asks her and she looks up at him, and notices the package in his hands. She simply shrugs and he purses his lips together before walking back to his desk. She watches him, wanting to say something to him, but thinks better of it. Then, she hears a ripping sound, and knows that he is opening the package. At first, there is just stark. Silence. Then, there are three words that she'd never forget. Three words that would change her future forever. "Oh my God." ---- Chapter Two: Split In his right hand rests a glass cup with a brown liquid in it. "You, a man that has conquered death, have nothing to fear," Sergei tells him, but just from the expression on Santiago's face, he isn't convinced. Far from it. "Death comes to us all eventually, Sergei. It's just my luck that I've been staring death in the eyes for all these years thinking it wouldn't bite back. Now, it's ready to rip me to shreds," he says, almost as if he's already succumbed to defeat. Then, there is silence as they look up at the stars. Santiago feels lost in them, the vastness starting to swell in his chest. With each breath he takes, there is a heaviness there, and he knows he can feel the walls starting to close in around him. He doesn't like the feeling. For so long, he felt like he was in control. Now, he felt helpless. He needed that control back. He lived off that control. "We'll talk about the dossier later. Tell me though, are we ready for him? Because, if I do take my last breath, I'm not going down without him going with me. Do you understand?" he asks of Sergei who simply nods his head before finishing off his drink. Then, he looks at Santiago who continues to gaze at the stars. "Trust me, we've been preparing for this day for years now. If he makes it to the final step, we'll pull the plug right then and there," Sergei tells him and Santiago responds with a slight nod. "Good," he says before walking away from Sergei, needing time to himself.
