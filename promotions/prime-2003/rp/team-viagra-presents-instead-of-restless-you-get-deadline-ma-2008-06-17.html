Team VIAGRA Presents: Instead of Restless, you get DEADLINE MAN

17 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-17-08 (High Flyer sits on his living room couch, slowly changing channels.) High Flyer: Boring. Boring. News. Bad News. the Shield. Syndication. Syndication. Syndication. (Tony Davis lies down on the floor, playing his Nintendo DS without a care in the world. Mary-Lynn Mayweather walks in, carrying a plate of drinks.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather: So... (Mary-Lynn points to a naked man huddling in his own fear. He is painted the midnight color of green.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather: What's with the b-s-d-m fetish? High Flyer: Oh nothing. He's just Deadline. Tony Davis: Yeah! We hug Deadline! (Tony gets up and walks over to the shivering man. Davis wraps his arms around him, as "Deadline" man jumps in his seat and lets out a yelp. He shakes violently.) Tony Davis: I love you Deadline man. Deadline Man: Unclean! Mary-Lynn Mayweather: You know, the majority of PRIME had their work done three days ago. You're like that retarded kid at the sixth grade science fair. High Flyer: Hey. Those kids aren't always retarded... One was drunk once. (Mary-Lynn Mayweather sets down the tang, crosses her arms, and blinks.) Mary-Lynn Mayweather: I can see why Kate wishes to have nothing to do with you. (Mary-Lynn turns to leave.) High Flyer: Oh yeah? I hope you know that those kids she lugs around are half Harmen! Yeah! I'm totally all up in there genetically. (Flyer turns to Davis, who has still not let go of Deadline Man.) High Flyer: You can let him go. You're making him tint lime. (Davis lets go of his hug, and color slowly returns to the skin pigment of the Deadline Man.) High Flyer: So. Deadline Man. What special powers do you have? Besides curling in the fetal position of course. Tony Davis: And crying to an insanely high pitched degree. (Deadline Man doesn't answer. He continues to slowly rock back and forth like a pendulum.) High Flyer: Uh. Does Deadline Man hug his own deadline when it concerns procrastination? Do you have the ultimate power of putting things off!? Because if you do, perhaps we are two kindred souls, forever lost amongst the cosmos, split up from birth like Tia and Tamara and reunited here at this exact moment to extract vengeance upon the ontimers. Come with me Deadline Man. Let us unite against the struggled oppression of the working class. Rebel. LOOT AND REBEL! (... Nothing. No reaction.) High Flyer: Well, he's not moving. I just gave him a free pass to worldwide Disneyland and he turned me down. I guess I'll just have to go looting myself.... First things first, I gotta loot me a loot bag from a hobo. TO SANTA MONICA! Tony Davis: Don't we have a match this week. High Flyer: Tony, Tony, Tony. (Flyer walks up behind Tony and places his arm around his shoulder.) High Flyer: We loot objects so we can break them over our opponent’s heads. Haven't you been listening to those tapes I made for you while you're sleeping? Tony Davis: Well, yeah... but the only change I notice is throwing up when you light up a cigarette. High Flyer: Hmmm... Wrong tape perhaps? Tony Davis: I dunno. I've been sleeping better than ever! High Flyer: Then let's keep you throwing up. (Flyer and Davis walk off out of the front door of their house.) Tony Davis: Ooo! Wanna loot Radio Shack? High Flyer: Only way I'd ever shop there is if I was looting! (They go off to loot Radio Shack)
