An Umbrella? A Frickin' Umbrella?

1 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03/01/05 Well, It was a 'Showdown' of epic proportions. Double cage structure, Dual Halo .... Though I didn't fair too well, I let my prescence be known. This week however, may just be more than that. This is a week where I get to show all of PRIME what I truly have instore for them. *The scene opens up and the view is that of the inside of a car, a cadillac sedan deville to be more specific. Razor is seen sitting shotgun while Bob drives with Bald Wall in the back. They drive by a few establishments with one of those reading 'Houston Dolls'. A strip club it looks to be, but more importantly the name Houston lets us all know the location is Houston, Texas.* Bob: I dont know why you waste so much money Raz. Razor Blade: How so, Bob? Bob: Well we're still in Houston when we should be in Arkansas Razor Blade: I got me a little signing bonus and seeing as how I dont give a rats ass about the cash, I'd rather waste it where I deem necessary. Bald Wall: Yeah, Money doesn't grow in gardens Razor! Bob: It's 'Money doesn't grow on trees' you jackass. Bald Wall: Oh .... Razor Blade: Bob, I got a good bit of cash to throw around. Bob: I hope so, because as your manager I dont work for free. Razor Blade: And with Soultress' death I cut back alot on groceries. Bob: Yeah but you double that on eating out and beer. Razor Blade: What can I say... I love my coronas. Bald Wall: I love kool aid! Bob: Whatever... Anyways, So how did it feel Raz? Razor Blade: Shit it felt great, she was boppin' like a pogo stick! Bob: Huh? Razor Blade: Oh, thought you meant the celebration at the strip club. Bob: Uhmmm no. I meant to finally be back in this full swing.. Razor Blade: Well it felt great to know I still had it... But It's not like I won anything. If I want to pick up a win this week I will have to go all out. Bald Wall: Yeah, Supernova is the best! I love him! Razor Blade: First of all, I'm not fighting Supernova, and secondly, if that's how you feel about the guy who sold me out why dont you just go hang around with him then! Bald Wall: Really!? Can I!? Can I!? Bob: Yeah Bdubbya, we'll drop you off to spend the night. *sarcasm implied* Bald Wall: That's great! I'll go pack my bags! Razor Blade: We're not even home yet you dumbass. Bald Wall: Oh... yah. Bob: Anyways, The HIT and TCM are not to be taken lightly Raz... you think some training will help you out? Razor Blade: Training? Shit Bob, come on. I'm Razor Blade, since when have I ever trained? Bob: Well that's true, you hardly train. Razor Blade: True, but sometimes there is a need too. However I didnt just win all these matches on luck. My progress is based solely upon S&S. Bald Wall: Skittles and Snickers!? Bob: I dont know what S&S is but I'm damn sure willing to bet my manhood it's not skittles and snickers! Razor Blade: Well actually.... Bob: DO WHAT!? Razor Blade: Haha, Just playing Bob... and no, it's not Skittles and Snickers... It's skills and Strategy. Bob: Well, we're nearing the house Raz. Razor Blade: Well until we get there I will educate you two fools... Skills and Strategy, Once your a vet in this sport you got skills to outdo your opponent who is just isn't prepared to see you counter his every move. Bob: What about strategy? *The cadillac pulls in the driveway* Razor Blade: There is no strategy towards the likes of The HIT & TCM, just bring your A game. Bald Wall: And your going to bring it right? Razor Blade: I've been bringing it for over fifteen years.. what do you think? Bald Wall: So I can play the game? Bob: It's not a game Bdubbya, it was a figure of speech... god did you ever go to school! Bald Wall: Yeah, I was third grade spelling bee champ! Razor Blade: What he fails to mention is that third grade was the furthest he got. Bob: Spelling Bee Champ my ass! You cant even spell 'Heaven'. *Razor gets out the car and Bob and Bdubbya follow suit* Bald Wall: Yeah I can... H .... E Bob: Dumbass. Bald Wall: V .... no... A-V ... H ... E .. A .. V ..... Razor Blade: He also fails to mention he failed the third grade three times. Bald Wall: I.. N! ... HEAVIN!! Bob: What an idiot! *Razor walks up to the front door of his large two story house and goes to open the door...but it's already opened and cracked and a voice is heard inside faintly.* Razor Blade: What the fuck... Bob: Oh man... you didn't leave it unlocked did you? *Razor looks back at Bob with a look that reads 'Are You Kiddin' Me?'* Bob: I'll go use the car phone and call the cops... Razor Blade: Nah... I got a better idea.. Bob: What's that? Razor Blade: Bald Wall, go around back.. Hurry! Bald Wall: Ok. *Bald Wall takes off sprinting to the back of the house* Bob: Why did you tell him to do that!? You could get him killed! Razor Blade: Yeah but then he wouldn't bother us anymore. Bob: Haha.. your bad Raz... *Razor walks back over to the lac and pops the trunk.. he reaches under the compartment where the spare is located and pulls out the long steel metal pipe looking thing that jacks the wheels up when changing a flat.... he looks in the trunk and there sits an umbrella... he grabs it and throws it to Bob* Razor Blade: Here, take this. Bob: What the hell is this for!? Razor Blade: For the burglars, duh. Bob: What the fuck Raz, you think they are going to rain on me!! They probably have guns and stuff! Razor Blade: Look Bob, it's all I got for you... Bob: Why dont we just call the cops! Razor Blade: By the time the cops show up they will be long gone with all my valuables. Including all my Gold title belts! Bob: Dude, I understand really... But u want me to defend for my life with a friggin' umbrella!? Razor Blade: Bob, Jackie Chan has used an umbrella on countless occasions proving it to be a great weapon. Bob: This aint no Shanghai Knights Razor... I dont know that Kung Fu Jit zu whatever! Razor Blade: Well now is as good a time as ever to learn... Now C'mon! *Razor grabs bob by the collar and heads back to the front door of his house.* Razor Blade: Ok... you first. Bob: What!? Me first!? Why!? Razor Blade: Cause I said so! Bob: But your Razor Fuckin Bladdddddeeee!!! Mr Whole Fuckin Shooowwww! Razor Blade: Are you mocking me!? Bob: Uhhh-- Razor Blade: Look, if you dont get your ass in there I'm going to conk you over the head with this here steel pipe thinga-ma-jig. Bob: Heh, you said Thinga-ma-jig. Razor Blade: Well what would you call this.. Bob: Uhmmm uhhh a Thinga-ma-jig I guess. Razor Blade: Quit Stalling! Go! *Razor shuffs Bob in the door.. and follows behind... The fridge is wide open in the kitchen and footsteps are heard coming down the stairs... it's Bald Wall... and he is decked out in Supernova apparal from head to toe... Supernova Hat.. Nova AW World Belt replica, Nova head band and wrist bands and a foam finger that reads 'Smell My Finger!'* Bob: It's Bald Wall... Razor Blade: What the fuck are you wearing! Bald Wall: You said I could spend the night with Nova! *At this time a crash is heard in the room adjacent to them... Razor reacts quick and Bob follows with his umbrella... they open up the door to the room and low and behold stands........... Jason Blade, Razor's brother with an apple in his hand. Razor is standing weilding his steel thingy-ma-jig that is used to change a flat while Bob has his umbrella cocked over his head like a baseball bat.* Jason Blade: What the fuck are you two doing? Trying to perform a musical? Razor Blade: What the fuck am I doing!? Bitch, what the fuck are you doing! Jason Blade: I'm eating an apple, what the hells it look like. Razor Blade: Look Jason, you got some serious issues thinking you can come down here and break into my house. Jason Blade: Heh... Do I? As if the house didnt have tape around it, seized? *Bob sets the umbrella down and Razor pulls his weapon down to his side.* Razor Blade: You damn right you do! Dont worry about the tape, its still my house! You get out of prison and you come here thinking everything will be like it used to be... and that your going to ride along on my gravy train but your absolutely mistaken becau--- *Jason cuts off Raz* Jason Blade: You see Raz... you got it all wrong... *At this moment Bald Wall bursts through the double doors with his Supernova outfit stil on, the purple Nova hat, the blue AW Nova t-shirt, the yellow Nova headband, the red Nova wristbands, and the black AW replica belt.* Jason Blade: Who the fuck is that colorful looking bastard!? Bald Wall: Why I am Bald Wall! Supernova's number one fan! Jason Blade: Super who? Never mind that... Look here you walking talking rainbow, I suggest you scram because you dont want to get inbetween this mess of mine. Bald Wall: Why? I love messes! Jason Blade: No, this is one mess you dont want to get involved with... this is between Me , Razor and Bob. Bob: And Bob!? Why the fuck am I involved! Jason Blade: BECAUSE! Bald Wall: Hey... why do you have rubber gloves on? Bob: Because why, I didnt do anything! *At this moment Jason pulls out a gun and points it at Bob* Jason Blade: Because I SAID SO! Bob: Holy shit... Uhmm Ok , sounds good to me! Razor Blade: What the hell do you plan to do with that. Jason Blade: What do you think! Razor Blade: Kill yourself? Jason Blade: Oh very funny. Bald Wall: What are the rubber gloves for? Jason Blade: So I dont leave fingerprints, you reading rainbow look alike! Bob: Oh god, tell me you didnt just say those two words.... Razor Blade: Oh man... Bald Wall: READING RAINBOW!! I LOVED THAT SHOW!! *Singing* 'Butterfly in the skyyyy, I can go twice as highhhh ... Take a look, it's in a book , Reading Rainbowww!! Razor Blade: You just had to say Reading Rainbow didn't ya!? Bald Wall: *Still singing* I cannn go anywhere! Friends to knowww and ways to growww , Reading Rainbowwww.... Jason Blade: This shit is tripping me out. Bob: Oh god, make him stop! Bald Wall: I can be anythingggg!! take a look... It's in a book... It's Reading Rainboowww!! Jason Blade: LOOK HERE DAMMIT IF YOU DONT SIT DOWN AND SHUT THE FUCK UP I'M GOING TO BLOW YOUR BRAINS OUT! *Bald Wall stops and Bob starts to snicker* Jason Blade: What the hell you snickering about!? Bob: Heh, Nothing... you just said 'SIT DOWN AND SHUT THE FUCK UP!' Jason Blade: So... Bob: Nothing you just sounded alot like Miss Crabtree from South Park. Razor Blade: Heh.. you did. *Just about this time a knock is heard on the front door...* Jason Blade: Who the hell is that.. you expecting company? Razor Blade: You know, I dont know why the hell you want to kill me. Jason Blade: You were my big brother, you were supposed to watch after me, but you were too busy looking after yourself to realize what was happening! Razor Blade: I didn't make you rob that chicken joint that day... and I sure as hell didnt make you pull that trigger. *The knock is becoming louder* Jason Blade: Who's at the door! Bob: Can I go answer it and find out? Jason Blade: NO! Razor Blade: It's the cops... I called them before entering. Bald Wall: Bad Boys, Bad Boys... whatcha gonna do, whatcha gonna do when they come for you! Jason Blade: Another day, another time Raz... *Jason jets out through the back room door and you then hear the backdoor of the house slam. Bald Wall walks over to the front door.* Bob: Dude, I thought you didnt call the cops? Razor Blade: I didnt. Bob: Then why are they here! *Next to Bald Wall stands two Houston police department officers.* Razor Blade: What are yall doing here? Cop 1: Alarm of the house went off, and ADT security called us. Cop 2: Yeah, got here as fast as we can. Razor Blade: As fast as you can? It's been close to probably thirty minutes now! Cop 1: Hey, dont raise your tone with us! Cop 2: We had to make a stop first... Cop 1: Anyone in the house? Razor Blade: No. Cop 2: Good, then I guess our job here is done. You gentlemen have a good evening. *Cop 1 looks over at Bald Wall* Cop 1: Right on! Nova rocks! Razor Blade: Screw you! *The cop gives Razor a glare...* Cop 1: Come on Jeff, let's go finish those donuts. *The scene fades to black*
