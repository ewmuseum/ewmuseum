Neither a drunk dial nor a booty call.

27 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 27, 2006 -Laundry. -Lunch with Elise and Brent. -Food shopping. -Talk to Joey about the tag thing in PRIME. .................For real this time... "Fuck..." With her chin resting agonizingly on the kitchen island's marble counter-top, Lindsay Troy gazed at the bright screen of her palm pilot with tired eyes. It was an early Monday morning, and a rare one at that. The still-humid early fall day found the Queen not only home in Tampa but found her as the only soul awake inside her spacious palace walls. The Prince or Court Jester (depending on the situation), was still asleep upstairs and Lindsay just didn't feel like lying still when she had things on her mind. Taking her electronic gadgets with her, she quietly tiptoed downstairs to the kitchen, where she proceeded to pour herself a glass of orange juice and stare blankly at the small, three hundred dollar screen and wonder about paths, choices, and two championship belts that suddenly weigh more on her mind than they do around her waist. In a situation such as this, it's best to talk to someone about what you're feeling. Lindsay didn't want to wake Melton up to discuss what was bothering her because not only would Joey be incoherent and groggy, but he'd probably be cranky after being forcibly woken up to discuss, and make, important decisions before his first cup of green tea and an hour of the Today Show. So instead, she picked up her cell phone and scrolled through her contacts until she reached the "N"s. After all, someone owed her. Big time. ************* In a hotel room in Dallas, near the Mavericks Suck Dome, lies Nova, alone, passed out with his clothes still on. Somewhere between plummeting with no parachute to his death in the Grand Canyon and the real world, "Brick House" by the Commodores started playing. This may be weird to some, except it's the sound of the ring tone the Rising Star assigned to Lindsay Troy's number in his cell phone. The sound, and the vibration are coming from his foot, where his cell phone mysteriously ended up in his sock. Nova shot upright, hand still stuck in a bag of Cheetos and bandanna half-hanging over his left eye and fumbled for his phone in his smelly foot. "I don't normally take booty calls before my morning joint, but for you I'll make an exception." "Good thing this isn't a booty call then," Troy said on the other line, fingers tapping the marble impatiently. "Were you passed out...I mean, asleep?" "Something like that." Nova groaned, and collapsed back against the pillows, the effort exhausting. "What time is it anyway?" "7:45." "PM, right?" "No." He grimaced. "Fuck. Normal people aren't up this early." "I imagine that stoner slackers have the luxury of sleeping until at least eleven, but normal people generally past the age of twenty-two with normal jobs are up this early." "So what's your excuse, then?" Nova inquired, lifting the bandanna up across his forehead, restoring vision to his eye while Troy rolls hers. "Very funny. I'm an insomniac, you know that." "Yeah but, even so," a provocative lift of Nova's eyebrows accompanied the statement, "I wouldn't let you leave the bed." "Oh, I'm pretty sure I couldn't get INTO your bed even if I wanted to. You probably have paraphernalia scattered about the bedsheets and fall asleep with your hand still inside a half-eaten bag of Cheetos." Nova looked down, took his hand out of the bag and licked the cheese powder off his fingers. "So, what are you trying to say?" he replied, eating a now-stale chip and crunching it obnoxiously into the phone. "I'm a Doritos girl. It'd never work between us." "It might. What's your fancy?" "Cool Ranch." Pause. "Damnit, I'm a Spicy Nacho kinda guy. I've been foiled by flavor." Troy's laugh was accompanied by a shake of her head, which Nova sensed over the phone. "You must have had a reason for calling besides feeling particularly cruel and unusual." "Not any more cruel and unusual than usual. Heh. I wanted to get you back for dragging me to that grease-pit you call a restaurant last week." "Come on! We had a moment there!" "We had many moments, Nova. Embarrassment. Disgust. Me wanting to kill you..." It was Nova's turn to laugh and shake his head. "Alright, alright. I get your point. Seriously though, why'd you call?" There was a bit of a pause before Lindsay, reluctantly, admitted, "I have a problem and it has to do with Joey." Suddenly, a heavenly chorus appeared above Nova's head. Angels started playing guitars and smoking doobs, one in particular bore a shocking resemblance to Jimi Hendrix. And to think, Nova hadn't rolled since the night before. "You mean...", he started, wistfully, coaxingly, "You're finally leaving him..." "No, you ass! This isn't anything relationship related." Abruptly, the heavenly chorus stopped and angel Jimi *poof*ed out of existence. "Oh." Dejected, just another emotion in the never-ending gamut. "Let me rephrase then. Why'd you call me" "Alaina and Dan have a newborn baby and Sonny would kill me if I called him now when he's on Hawaii time. And besides, you're the only one who has the best idea of what's going on in PRIME." "Makes sense then. Continue." "Well," she started, "Chet called me the other night and you know he never calls anyone unless it's important." "Or when he's drunk." "Right." "Although, he's never called ME while drunk..." "Uh huh," Lindsay deadpanned. "Ca-..." "Do you think the basis of a push is dependent on whether you get drunk calls from the boss or not?" "If it is, and you were the CEO, I'd be the Universal Champ by now. Can I continue?" No objection from the Rising Star. "Okay, so, Chet called me, sober, wanting to have a chat. I'll spare you the minute details, but the gist of it is that after I ask him if he's received his endorsement check and eaten fresh from Subway today, he tells me that three teams does not a tag division make and that, for all intents and purposes, the division is suspended. So now, I'm a co-champion of a division that's barely breathing and I have to tell my partner what's going on. And the thing of it is, I already know exactly what's going to happen when I do." "And what's that?" "He's going to see this as his way out." Heavy silence. Nova reached for his cigarette carton, which had ended up, somehow, in the adjacent pillowcase and his lighter, which thankfully remained on the nightstand. Troy sighed. "He agreed to come to PRIME on my suggestion, because teaming in Empire or New Frontier wasn't going to be possible. But I don't think it's any secret that the Business part of the team has been doing the brunt of the teamwork." "So he wants out?" he asked, and examined his cigs. None were bent or broken. "And then what?" "And then he'll want me to come too. This was supposed to be a way for us to test the waters of a professional teaming outside of a manager/wrestler dynamic and I know that if there's no division for us to do it in then he'll want to cut the losses and have that much less road-stress in his life. But I don't think I'm ready to pack it in just yet, Ceas. It's easier for Joey to sever good-standing professional relationships than it is for me. He makes a phone call, signs a few papers...poof, it's done. I can't say the same." She takes a sip of her juice. "This whole situation has been frustrating, knowing that Joey's nonchalance a lot of the time has resulted in me doing more and making a bigger effort. Getting him to come to a company where he's a complete stranger was a milestone in itself. But I know he's going to want to leave and I'm stuck in the middle somewhere between wanting to stay and see just how well I can do in the singles divisions or leaving and taking a little pressure and stress off of myself." "Well, I can't tell you whether to stay or go, obviously, but the bottom line is that you don't wanna wind up staring up at the ceiling in bed at night regretting your decision. I think the chances of that happening are significantly higher if you go." Nova put a stoge in his mouth and lit up, inhaling the sweet tar before blowing a stream of smoke out of his mouth. "You belong here, Lindz, much more than Melton ever did. And if you leave, I'm going to take over ReVolution and sing shitty renditions of Teddy Pendergrass hits until you return." "I don't think the world is ready for nationally televised proof of how bad you are at karaoke." "All great artists are misunderstood and under-appreciated while they're alive." "Some. Not all." Troy allowed another silence between them as Nova's sober words sunk in. "Well, I guess you're going to leave me with little choice than to stick around and drive Noble's head into the canvas a few dozen times. I just hope I don't get accused of ageism after this week is over." "Beating old people don't count as a hate-crime. Is Noble gay?" She shrugged. "Deville came back, Fuckhead showed up out of nowhere and Chet morphed, overnight, into the long-lost brother of Jon Lovitz. I'm convinced that anything in the world is now possible." "Obviously, since I'm in the Main Event picture, you're right." The sound of loud thudding filters into the open-air kitchen. Troy looks toward the entryway, and smiles softly. "I hear Sleeping Beauty rising and stomping down my staircase. I should probably wait and break the news to him after he's slightly more coherent." "Why don't you let me do it," Nova stated, then made the face: >=) "Ha. Nice try, but to him you're still the Enemy of the New Frontier." "He just knows that one day you'll be sleeping with the enemy." Another roll of her eyes was granted to Nova. "Go roll a joint and drift back off to dreamland, Patrick Bergin." "Your will is my command."
