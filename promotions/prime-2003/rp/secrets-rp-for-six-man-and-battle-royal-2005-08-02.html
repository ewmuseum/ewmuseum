Secrets (Rp for six man and Battle Royal)

2 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 08/02/05 At Colossus II you did exactly what you intended to prove to the entire world you could do. You walked into the show as the PRIME Tag Team Champion and you walked out of the match as the PRIME Tag Team Champion. You fought through seven other teams that were hell bent on your destruction and walked out with the titles held high above your head. Every single team in PRIME was there, locked in that cage with you, and they all wanted your blood and your title. They proved it time and time again by making sure they didn't allow you to get pin falls even when you were dominating. They proved it by every single one of those men attacking Angelus and yourself and trying to take you out. Fourteen other men with no other common ground other than wanting to see the end of Los Diablos reign coming together in attempts to end you. Yet they failed. They weren't able to obtain the ending that they all so desperately wished. Everyone one of them no doubt wanted to win the titles for themselves, and who could blame them, but you could tell by their actions...by the looks in their eyes...that if they didn't win the titles there was nothing they wanted more than to make sure that Los Diablos didn't walk out of the match with the belts either. Fourteen men coming together with the goal of finally ending the reign of a team that no one has been able to even come close to beating since Manifest Destiny was able to some how survive against them in the Mexican Prison Match up. No other person has been able to lay claim to putting one of the champions shoulders to the mat for a count of three since Angelo Deville was able to. They all tried, they all failed, and you simply moved on once more proving that you've got more than enough reason to walk around with your heads held high, proud that you've been able to dominate the division that you set out to build. Every single team with in PRIME that wants to see you fall, to see you be tripped up, they all have no respect for the fact that they are only here cause of you. Without the heart, desire, and determination to take the PRIME Tag Team championships and make them worth holding once more, that Los Diablos has displayed, then none of these teams would be making their PRIME appearances. None of these teams would be making any money off of wrestling right now and they would all still be floundering around in nothing companies or still enjoying their forced retirements cause no one wanted them. They are all wanted now. They are all looked at as something worth talking about again, something worth having on the shows. Every single thing that teams like PWA, Team Zambo, and Civil D have right now is cause you've allowed them to have it. You made what they did a few years ago worth talking about. Sure they were doing it in the big times here in the United States before you were but they haven't even been thought about once in the last two or three years. No one cared about PWA and their five PCW title reigns anymore. People vaguely, if at all, remembered that Civil D once ruled the vaunted Old School Wrestling tag division. They were all dead, buried, and forgotten until you came along an revived the world of tag team wrestling. With your desire to prove that holding tag team titles is something worth more than simply mentioning from time to time as a boasting tool you've single handily brought back people's interest in teams that were going the way of Rock Startling, Ian English, and Tyler Nelson. Superstars who no one even bothers to think about any longer. Yet how do you get repaid, not only by those teams who you've helped get back into the spotlight, but by the company you've helped make into the biggest and most recognized company in the world today? Did you get a celebration for walking out of the Fallen Halo with your pride still hanging over your shoulder? Did Hin See and his son come to you and say thank you and give you a raise or the night off? No. Instead they decided that it just meant they could use you even further. Now you're expected to walked into ReVolution this week and compete in a six man tag team match with Peter Vetra against some newbie fuck and one more damn time with Team Zambo. As if that aggravation wasn't enough but then both Angelus and yourself have to go back out to the ring in the main event to compete in a thirty six man over the top rope battle royal for...you don't even know what for. It could be for nothing more than the simple bragging rights of having out lasted thirty six other men. 'I've got some real concerns about this week hermano.' You say to Enrique as he jogs around the track with you. He turns his head, slightly, to give a glance to you. 'What for? The titles aren't on the line? This is just another chance to prove your dominance in PRIME, both in a tag match and as an individual competitor.' He responds while continuing his way around the dirt track in the back half of your yard. 'First off in the tag match we've got a little problem. When it is just Angelus and myself I don't have any worries. We've beaten Team Zambo on two straight occasions already, so they don't concern me any longer, and this new guy means exactly zero to my career. But it is our partner that I have to be concerned about.' You reply as the two of you pick up a little more speed around the track. How in the world could you be facing Team Zambo again? They have had two shots at you as a team already and neither time have they been able to get a W against you. They have given you everything they have and you've walked away both times clutching your titles. Why is this week suppose to be any different? 'Peter Vetra is your problem?' He asks almost as if he is going to laugh at you. Vetra has been your stable mate and buddy in The Imperium for so long now it is hard to even remember a time in PRIME that you haven't been concerned with his career. 'Yes. He is a liability in the ring for us.' You respond as cold and matter of fact like as you possibly can. You don't want to think that way cause you genuinely like Vetra but you have to face the facts and speak the truth. 'So you've got concern that the is going to cost you a match involving Team Zambo and that it will make it seem like they deserve another shot at you?' Enrique asks with a raise of his brow. It is clear that he is a bit confused by you having some concerns about a match cause you're the one who rarely every worries about this type of thing. 'I'm not concerned with Team Zambo getting another shot. Far as I'm concerned they have already proven themselves to be less than worthy of our time. We've beaten them not once, but twice, and that more than proves they don't belong in the ring with us. Vetra, good a guy as he is, seems to have trouble stepping up to the plate and winning matches that he is suppose to. He loses to people that he should be dominating and he always seems like he is just on the cusp of breaking out and making a name for himself only to allow some fuck like Edwards to best him.' You reply with a bit of aggravation in your voice. A tone that is very unusual when speaking about partners and friends and usually only reserved for people like Angelo Deville, Dave Gibson, or the K-Wolf. 'I see what you're saying. Peter has been dropping the ball a lot of the time but he is a young man Baltasar. He works hard and he really is getting better with every week, even if he isn't winning more, and he does command respect from his opponents.' Enrique replies. You slow down and stop and this causes him to stop as well. He looks back at you with a confused look. 'What is the matter? I know you're not tired by the twenty minutes we've been jogging.' 'No. I'm fine physically. But I'm tired of some things mentally. I'm a patient man, you know that Morales, but my patience is wearing thin with some people. I'm growing tired of having to prove that Team Zambo doesn't belong in the ring with Los Diablos. I'm more than tired of The Imperium being just on the cusp of being the greatest force in the world only to have Vetra fuck it up by dropping matches to losers. I'm also tired of this whole deal with Jacob Cross. Don't think for a moment that I haven't noticed the fact that you've had to deal with him a lot more lately and you've been less that forth coming with information on the situation.' You respond, almost as unloading the world from your shoulders, to your dear friend and manager. You see the compassion in his eyes but you also read the fear and hurt behind them. You've known for some time now that there was something very serious to the deal with Cross that he just doesn't want to tell you about and now seems like the perfect time to make him spill it. 'You've got to start talking essa cause I'm more than tired of explaining myself and getting no response from it. So stop dragging your feet and come out with it already.' You say with more than a hint of annoyance. It's more like a truck load of it that comes flying off the words as they leave your mouth. 'Alright hermano I know I owe you that respect. The fact of the matter is that I've screwed up and in a major way essa. I've allowed myself to be duped just as you told me I would. I let the greed of fame, success, and fortune in the Cruiserweight Tournament blind me and allowed myself to sign a deal that wasn't quite what I had thought.' Enrique replies to you in one of the most defeated tones you've ever heard in your life. 'Let me guess that favor you owe Jacob Cross has now got something to do with him being part of PRIME as one of your charges huh?' You ask with a roll of your eyes, a crossing of your massive arms over your huge chest, and a great deal of annoyance in your movement. 'Unfortunately, yes. He is going to be part of PRIME starting this week and...'he trails off and turns his back toward you. You can tell that he isn't happy with himself at all, not only for this happening, but for not telling you about this sooner. 'We're going to have to team with him aren't we?' You ask with out a hint of giving away any emotion to the situation any further than you already have. Enrique just slowly turns back toward you and you can tell by the look in his eyes that the answer you already had in his mind was true. Jacob Cross is now going to be a partner of yours, along with Angelus, and you'd just have to live with the nature of this deal. 'I see. Hermano I can't say that I'm not angry about this. I'm more than displeased with the fact that I have to team with a man that I not only don't trust but more importantly I don't even like. He reminds me a lot of another jackass I can't stand in this business and this situation wasn't something I needed on my mind going into a week like this one.' 'I understand that essa. That is why I had only intended on telling you the full truth after this week. I wanted to get past The Fallen Halo and now this Battle Royal thing before giving you this news. I'm sorry essa as I've let you down.' He drops his head and just stares at the ground. You're angry, there is no doubt about that, but you can't stay angry with Morales. He has been like a older brother to you every since you first met him and it just in you to take anger out on him. You know, already, that you're going to roll with this situation and make the best out of it. 'Forget about it hermano. It isn't worth holding grudges or getting down about. We'll just move on and make the best of this shitty situation and keep a smile the whole time. Who knows maybe you'll think of something and Jacob Cross will become more useful than I realize. Just don't worry about it any more and roll with the punches so to speak.' You smile at your friend while patting him on the shoulder. He looks up and you can tell that there is a peaceful calm and relief in his eyes as he pats you on the back as well. Then you both turn and begin your job again with the thoughts of ReVolution, Team Zambo, and Jacob Cross on your mind as you begin preparing for a week that just got a little more crappy for you. It didn't happen the way you had thought it would. Sure in your mind you always knew that walking out with the titles would be more than possible for Baltasar and yourself. You always felt in your mind that you'd make sure and pull you weight and do everything you could to ensure that this would happen. Still there was a part of you that hoped it would go down a little differently. You wanted the win, that is certain, but you wanted the win to be with some sort of consequence to gaining the win. You only wanted the win cause you wanted it for your best friend and tag team partner and never wanted it for yourself. You wanted to leave the Fallen Halo a champion but deep down you didn't want to be able to walk out a champion. You wanted to be carried out of that match a champion. You wanted it to all be over. Going out on top is all you've ever wanted to accomplish in this business and the Fallen Halo seemed like the perfect place to have that situation present itself. If Baltasar and yourself could get the most decisions and leave as champions, along with you being injured just serious enough to have reason to give up the wrestling business, then the night would have been perfect. You don't want anything too serious, no broken neck or anything like that, but something that could be just serious enough that you'd have good reason to just walk away. You've made your money, made your country proud, and proved yourself while being part of the world of wrestling. The nearly eight months that you've worked for PRIME now have been more than enough to prove that you are indeed one of the best tag team wrestlers to ever walk into a wrestling company and so now you've accomplished what you set out to accomplish. Why can't it all just be over now? Why couldn't one of those teams finally do what they all run around claiming they would do? Is it possible that you are being cursed to not have something like this happen to where you never have a ready made excuse to tell Baltasar and Enrique you are ready to walk away? Is God doing his best to force you to man up and just tell them yourself? You could never do that, never just walk away from it all without being able to have some excuse. You owe Baltasar too much to ever just simply up and quit on him like that. If it wasn't for all the great things he'd done for you in your life then your career would have ended at Colossus. You could simply just walk away, on top of your game, and be remembered for leaving as a champion. If you didn't owe him so much you could just walk away right now and let him have the career he deserves to have. 'I wish that Zameer had crushed me in that Halo. I wish that Gaz would have gotten 'pumped up' enough to have just taken me out. Then I could have left Colossus that evening with a actual smile on my face.' You say to Maria. She is the love of your life and you want nothing more than to just spend the rest of your life with her. You also want nothing more than to be able to tell Baltasar and Enrique that this woman is the real reason you want to just give it all up. 'Honey you don't need to stress yourself out over this. Things will happen when they are suppose to. We're always going to be together and I'm patient enough to wait till the time is right.' She responds in that way she has of soothing your nerves and making things right for you. She has never, not one, in the over a year you've been together forced this issue. She let you know that she wanted to go back to Mexico, one day, and hoped that it would be with you after your wrestling career was over. You have known that you wanted to leave this business forever even before you signed with PRIME. You signed back then cause of Baltasar but also cause deep down you still had the desire to make some real good money and prove yourself a little more. The night you won the tag team titles from Deville that desire died. You'd reached the pinnacle that you'd set for yourself. It wasn't the lofty goal that most set for themselves, including your partner, but it was all you ever wanted. Make some money, win one half of the tag team championships in a major company, and then walk away with your health and sanity still in tact. 'If only I could just tell him how I felt. Let him know about you and know that this isn't the life I want anymore. I know he'd understand he is my best friend for God sakes. So why can't I just tell him?' You ask Maria. She just smiles warmly as she begins to rub your shoulders for you. 'Some where, deep down, I've got a feeling that there is some unresolved guilt or something that you feel you've not repaid him for, or gotten past, or something. There is something in your life that you've still not moved past to where you can tell him you'd like it too all be over. When you finally make peace with whatever that issue is then you'll be able to walk away and say you did everything just the way you always wanted.' She responds as she starts working on the stress that has tightened up your muscles more and more often over the last few months. Winning matches, beating Team Zambo, and putting your body on the line isn't the problem you're having either. That stuff is second nature to you now and it just comes natural. The problem is that is all that it is anymore is a second natured reaction to something you once loved but no longer even care the slightest bit about. Wrestling just isn't in you any longer and the thrill it once provided is gone and has been replaced by the thrill of possibly one day having a family and being married to the woman of your dreams. 'You're right as usual. I'm not sure what the issue is myself but I've got to find out what it is so that I can end this. So I don't have to force myself to get into the right frame of mind for many more weeks like this. As if having to go out there and beat Team Zambo, again, and this new chump Lamen isn't bad enough but then I've got to compete in that damn Battle Royal later in the night.' You say more to yourself than anything. Nothing could make you hate wrestling each week more than having to wrestling more than once each week. 'You could always just get eliminated early and call it a show.' Maria responds with a grin. You give her a grin back and then a quick kiss on the lips. She grins cause she knows even though you hate your career that you're not going to do that. 'I could but then I'd be making a fool of myself. I have to keep pushing myself when I am in the ring so that when I do get out of this business that I leave with the respect of being someone who was always willing to give it his all and I do that. I don't love it, hell I don't even like it, anymore but I still give every match every thing I've got. Besides I want to see Baltasar do great in this Battle Royal and if I'm there to help watch his back it just makes it that much easier for him.' You respond as you lay flat on your stomach allowing Maria to be able to really work your stressed body. 'Some how I knew you were going to say that.' She replies with a little chuckle. You simply close your eyes as a smile comes over your face once more. You know that soon enough this will all be over and nights like this right here can be your reality. You've just got to hang on for a while longer cause you've got more things to make sure that you carry your weight in to accomplish. Baltasar is on his way to being a break out star in PRIME and you've got to stick it out long enough to make sure no one screws that up for him. Just a little longer is all you have to endure this business... Just a little longer. 'Did Enrique tell you about our new business associate starting this week?' You ask Angelus as the two of you sit down in a booth at the back of your favorite restaurant. The Mexican food here isn't as good as back home but it is close enough and the quiet atmosphere makes it worth the drive that few other places do. 'Yeah. He explained the situation to me yesterday after he told you about it. He is pretty wrecked inside about it still even though he knows he has your blessing on this whole thing.' Angelus replies and then turns and tells the waitress that he'd like an ice cold beer. You do the same before turning back to your best friend. You can see the writing on his face that there is things he'd like to tell you but he won't. You've thought of asking him about, pushing the issue, but you don't want to drive him away from talking about it any further. 'He has my blessing on this only cause there is really nothing more that can be done about it. I'll just have to live with it I suppose. At least it is out in the open now and it can be dealt with and not be tip toed around any longer.' You reply as you pick up a chip and dip it into the hot sauce before eating it. You read the eyes of Angelus as he simply nods his head and does the same. You know he wants to tell you that he is ready to retire that is why you slipped that little shot about being out in the open about it. You know he wants to tell you but for some reason simply doesn't. It is written in every action that he takes when it comes to wrestling. You've never worried about him letting you down in the ring cause you know he loves and respects you too much to do so. Still all the same you can tell that the fire that was once there simply isn't there anymore when he slams an opponent into the mat. 'I can understand why he wouldn't want to tell us. That was a pretty bone head deal that he cut with Cross and I'm sure he was afraid we'd blow up about it. Guess he should know us better than that by now.' Angelus replies just as the beers arrive. He thanks the lady for them, as you do as well, and then takes a big swig of his. You simply take a small drink of yours. Still trying to read him, trying to figure out why he won't tell you what you already know, and what it is that has given him this change of heart about wrestling. 'Things could always be worse I suppose,' you begin with out a hint of knowing what is going on. 'I could be facing this situation on my own and then I'd be really pissed off. Can you imagine him cutting that deal and forcing me to have to be partner with Cross?' Angelus grins, but it was after a hesitation, and it was just yet another give away. You know that deep down he is ready to give this all up, to fly home to Mexico, and start his business he always dreamed of. You can read it all over his face and that delayed smile only made it more obvious. For one moment you think about simply telling him that you know. Letting him off the hook and just telling you that you realize he is ready to retire and that he has your blessing. Instead you just sit at the table with a grin on your face and watch. He does have your blessing if he wants to walk away, and he should know that, but you aren't going to give it to him that easy. He is going to have to tell you himself and let you know what his reasons are. You don't much care what they are, they make no difference, as his happiness is all that matters. But it has to be him that gives himself that happiness, not you, cause you've given him too much in life already. It is why he won't just stand up and say what he wants right now cause you've made things too easy for him in the past. Now it is time for him to stand up and be a proud man and let you know for himself. When he does that you'll move on in wrestling without him and never harbor any ill will. Till then, however, you're going to wait it out and see what finally comes of this whole situation. 'I've been wondering, the last couple of days, about how you want to approach this Battle Royal at the end of the night hermano. I know you seem to think you've got to help make me look good all the time but I was thinking what would you think about us going out there and trying to get you the win?' You say while staring straight into Angelus eyes. You can tell the question has just about made him choke on the chip he was eating and he quickly downs the rest of his beer to get the chip the rest of the way down. 'Me? Baltasar you know I am a tag team guy only. I have no care for individual accolades and such so winning this Battle Royal would do nothing for my career. I'm where I should be as one half of the best tag team in the world. This Battle Royal could really catapult you to the top of PRIME though so I couldn't take that away from you.' Angelus replies as he looks around for the waitress to try and get another beer. You can tell you rattled him and it was the reaction you had hoped for. Sooner or later nights like this would lead to him realizing you know something is on his mind and he'll just spill it. 'True it could help a singles career for me later on down the road. Still I don't know that I want to even worry about such a thing for a while. I really think a win in this type of match could solidify you and make you the force to be reckoned with in PRIME. Wouldn't you like that for one of your career highlights?' You ask with a grin. The goading will begin now. It will take some time, no doubt, but you'll start trying to crack him here tonight. 'No I don't think I would. I'd rather just be one of the tag team champions and root for guys like yourself, Hoyt, and Vetra to get all that singles stuff that you guys get into. It isn't for me and I don't want it to ever be for me thank you very much. I don't care about it in the slightest.' He responds and this time he does so with just a small hint of frustration and annoyance. You are getting to him a bit and you know it. Time to turn up the pressure just a little bit. 'Well I've made up my mind. If comes down to use then you are going to win this match up and receive whatever reward there turns out to be for doing so.' You respond as you put on your best stubborn face and sit back and cross your arms over your chest. Angelus just shoots a glare at you and you can tell he is now getting really pissed at you. 'No. I don't want it, I don't need it, and I will not accept it. You will win if I have to eliminate my own ass before it even comes down to the two of us but you WILL WIN if it comes down to us. Simple as that and you will not change my mind Baltasar. I'm a tag team specialist and I do not care to be anything more than that. So just drop it.' He responds in a heated manner. 'But..' you start but he cuts you off. 'NO!' He explodes before getting up from the table and heading toward the bathroom. Once he is out of sight you just chuckle to yourself. This is going to be more fun than you realized to try and get your best friend to spill his guts. The next few days, weeks, months or whatever amount of time this takes is going to be very fun. Very fun for you, indeed, and a lot of uncomfortable moments for your best friend.
