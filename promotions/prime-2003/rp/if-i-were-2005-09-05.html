If I were...

5 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: September 5th 2005 “If Nascosto Nerezza was…” There is one thing that, make no mistake, I have always loathed about mankind. Nothing so deep as their destructive and self-destructive nature. Nothing so trivial as how most of them smell. Not even pop music. The single itch I’ve never quite been able to scratch is their misconception of me. Yes, quite a selfish notion, but a valid one nonetheless. Much like Ozzy Osbourne prior to his little dramamentary, if you’ll forgive that little portmanteau, people assumed I slept in a coffin situated in the bowels of some lavish Transylvanian castle, emerging only to suck blood or suck heat. The truth, obviously, is quite different. Because if I was what they all said I was… that would be quite the cause for alarm. But vampires are creatures of the imagination… often subject to the desires of the minds of others. Let me show you what, thanks to your designs, I have endured… “If I were the king of the undead…” The howling of wolves. If you were ever to hear it outdoors, you’d be hoping you had a gun and good, speedy reflexes. No such worry here, though. We’re indoors, although your safety is just as low. It’s time for a ritual that takes place every night when the sky is drenched in indigo ink, when rain seeps through the cracks of the clouds. Welcome to Castle Nerezza, home of the nobleman and lord over a small European village, isolated from the world by being sunk into a deep valley. Right in the middle stands the focal point of this lost town… the aforementioned castle. A twisting nightmare of black, spiny spires and rough stone walls, it was whispered by the locals to be a representation of its master’s spirit. The few of the seemingly lucky populace requested to serve inside the castle itself would thoroughly agree. However, it was in a room that the serving-girls and petty entertainers would never see under correct circumstances that the ceremony took place. A series of mirrors that cascaded in a network of precise and painstakingly arranged sequence provided the light of the room, manipulating the moon’s illumination into a globe at the top of the room that released it as an eerie glow. The light revealed the objects and yet another side to the master. His decadent and self-indulgent tastes were reflected in the luxurious wine-red velvet drapes and golden engraved crests and shields that decorated the walls. But the envy of everyone’s eyes was in the dead centre, no pun intended, of the room. A gorgeously illustrated casket, greedily bearing pearls, emeralds, diamonds and sapphires around the edges lay in smug splendour, the hand-painted panels and lid bearing the likeness of an odd yet intriguing man. With ashen-grey skin and soft-looking plum hair, clad in scarlet robes and bearing a wise-looking and modest face, the lid itself foreshadowed his presence, showing him posed on the back of an ebony horse and brandishing a lance. As the moonlight grew stronger, the servants of the master were forced to avert their eyes from the shine of the coffin. The light produced a strange effect as the colours of the jewels began to reflect along the walls and mingle with each other. The surreal display was always the first sign that he would soon awake. The second came much more sharply and direct. Three loud knocks on the lid, followed by it gracefully sliding to the left and opening up. And then… THUD. THUD. THUD. BANG! The top of the coffin was flung aside unceremoniously and the clumsy flailing of limbs followed. The coffin itself tipped over to one side, spilling out a vulgar mixture of coloured thrashing and frustrated noise. As the master finally picked himself up off the floor, he slung his locks of purple hair back, adjusting his red glasses and surveying the scene before him. In a circle around him was a collection of ladies in outfits designating them as serving wenches, while behind them hung the lowest of those in his employ, mere rats of the food chain, limply dangling in manacles fixed to the wall to be snacks upon his awakening. Clearing his throat and allowing time for the information to sink into his skull, Nerezza expressed himself in the manner he felt most appropriate. “Oh, BOLLOCKS, I’m at an Anne Summers party!” In a moment of confusion, the servants looked up to him. The most daring of them, situated out of his eyesight, leaned across to the one next to her and whispered; “The master’s not himself tonight.” “He is and always has been in league with ‘El Diablo’, as are we! Worry not!” Whispering around Nerezza was as effective as trying to make a snake flinch. “You work for El Diablo?” Quick as a flash, he snatched up the coffin lid and hid behind it. “Stay back! I swear, I had nothing to do with those male escorts or the fall of his career following the scandal! It was all internet rumours!” “But Master… you are the lord of this house and we are merely your followers. We fear not your wrath… for no man as charming, charismatic, beautiful, generous and graceful could stain himself in our eyes. At your command, we will give up our bodies to feed you and satisfy your every desire…” Nerezza’s mind was reeling. The last thing he remembered was collapsing onto his mattress in his temporary East London home after having spent a day trying to ram Skitzo down a manhole to get him unstuck after a personality of his decided there was buried treasure in the sewers. However, trying to fit himself and the large sack of equipment he was carrying through the hole at the same time was the tricky part. Nerezza sunk into his pillows, praying that Skitzo would not try to exit the sewers in the same manner. Then he awoke to this… a bizarre situation even to him. Had he been kidnapped in his sleep and brought as a replacement for some hedonistic aristocrat? Was some unseen force guiding his destiny and pulling the strings of his fate, making him a simple marionette dancing to a tune he had no part in the composure of? Suddenly, a loud clicking noise startled him. Like the clockwork noises of springs and cogs moving in harmony, the wall behind him began the move. The maids shrieked and quickly stood up, running to Nerezza’s feet and cowering at them, grabbing his legs and clinging to them as though they were the only things stopping them from plummeting into Hell itself. The wall shuffled forwards, swallowing up the carpet beneath it and grinding it into shreds with a ripping noise that maid everyone cringe. One of the sacrifices chained to it screamed in horror as she found herself being pushed forwards, picking her feet up and hanging suspended to prevent them from being pulled under and crushed. Nerezza himself flung the coffin lid at the advancing wall, only for it to bounce off and meet the same fate as everything beneath it, being ground up into bejewelled splinters. “HELP!” Nerezza roared. “THE FOURTH WALL IS CLOSING IN ON ME!” “Master, there is only one way to save you!” cried out one of the maids through a face of tears. “Forgive me, Master…” KLUDD!! The vampire found himself on the floor, stunned and mumbling groans. Over his body stood the same maid, her reddened and tear-soaked face in her palms, weeping loudly for having to raise her hands to her master… and bring a huge typewriter down over his head. Nerezza faded in and out of consciousness, trying to pay attention to the little stimuli he was offered. The sound of rapid typing, the occasional flash of fluorescent lights, the feel of tight leather over his skin… tight WHAT!? “If I were a modern vampire…” Nerezza floated out of his state of semi-awareness, slowly being brought into the new world by the thumping of drums and randomly sweeping lights. Silhouettes of people writhing and swaying all around him in time to the music crowded him on all sides, dancing to the aggressive techno that swamped them. And on each side of him, two women easily pigeonholed into the Goth rooted themselves. The trio were sat on a leather couch that encircled a round table, split in four places to allow people to enter the circle. Nerezza and his newly found companions had the area to themselves, with the girls sprawling across his lap and lower body. Both were wearing an arrangement of leather, fishnet, mesh, PVC and other fetish-flavoured fashions, one with long dark red hair, the other with a jet-black bobbed haircut. The typical corpse-paint style of contrasting black and white makeup adorned their faces and to Nerezza’s surprise, his too. He was never one for makeup… lipstick was for women, or so he though. However, it seemed the events of this world were beyond his control; a fact further emphasised by the wall closest to him seeming to shudder and rumble every time he felt like acting differently to how he was portrayed in his attire and setting. It soon dawned on Nerezza that the area was not as alien as he first thought – after a moment of familiarising himself with the surroundings, this was The Slaughterhouse: Hessian’s nightclub. More notably, he even recognised the immediate area… across from him Skitzo sat on his own with enough alcohol to keep Duke Williams sedated for a day or two, gazing up at a huge grid of screens that displayed a slasher flick. Skitzo was clearly far too drunk to be in good health, but keeping all of his multiple personalities happy with anything less than liver-rupturing quantities of booze was a challenge. On another table was Gaz Green, accompanied by his usual Temporary Girl Of The Week™. His attire was the usual green suit and spiked hair, except the suit bore a dual green and black shiny two-tone effect. His date wore a bottle-green, off-the-shoulder opera gown and matching gloves. Her eyes were a bewitching blue with devil-red lips, all capped in spun golden locks. Definitely Gaz’s type. The three were definitely in the VIP area – Vampires, Idiots, Pranksters. Allowing it all to sink in, Nerezza stretched out an arm and picked up the goblet from the table. It didn’t matter to him that he was drinking from a goblet… what mattered was the contents. Thick, dark red, metallic smelling and sweet: Just how blood should be served. Of course, in this stereotypical setting, there was only one way to drink it. Tilting his head all the way back, mouth gaping open, Nerezza slowly poured the liquid into his mouth with a gluttonous moan, allowing it to overflow and spill down his chin, neck and chest where the girls at his sides proceeded to lick up the scraps, leaving bloody red smears over his body with their tongues. Gluttony, lust, pride… how many times over could he die tonight from sinning? Suddenly, something caught the corner of his eye. A bright neon green, far too luminescent for Gaz’s tastes, lit up a distant corner of the area. Turning his head sideways, he raised an eyebrow at what he beheld. Hardly the appropriate clientele for The Slaughterhouse, he thought. An emaciated, almost anorexic-looking boy, maybe 15 or 16 years old at the highest, huddled himself into a corner. He was wearing a black t-shirt with some obscure band name printed in an undecipherable font and decked himself in countless junk bracelets on his wrists. His dark, curled hair couldn’t decide which direction to frizz in and his thick glasses were only barely held together with an abundance of tape, reflecting the green glow that lit his face up. Pulling himself from his seat, Nerezza further investigated the boy, drawing closer. In a world of his own, the boy was tapping away furiously on a laptop resting on his ripped black jeans, his feet resting comfortably on the table. The untied laces and masses of gum stuck to the soles were the icing on an already repulsive cake. Regardless, Nerezza moved in undetected by his senses, which seemed to be as sharp as a pool ball. “Hey, kid…” Nerezza hissed sinisterly, planting a hand on his shoulder. The boy almost fell from his seat in shock, dropping the laptop on the ground. “This is the slaughterhouse, not the library…” “Nyeeeh, oh my god, this is so crazy!” replied the nerdy boy. “I, like, SO never gave you this much intimidation, man!” A little too much had been given, it would seem. Without a second thought, Nerezza had clamed a claw around the boy’s neck, squeezing tight and lifting him from his seat into the air. Standing at possibly 5 feet and a half and weighing a little over 3 bags of sugar (soaking wet), Nerezza’s +10 strength bonus was not needed at all. With malicious glee, the vampire reached into his pocket and pulled something out. Slowly opening his palm in front of the terrified child’s face. “We’re going to play a game, kid.” Laughed Nerezza, putting the average comic-book villain to shame. “I roll the dice… if it scores seven or more, you win. Six or less… and I feed.” Unable to fight the crushing pressure on his windpipe, the boy stayed silent involuntarily as Nerezza scattered the dice over the table. The first one landed almost flat and dead on the table… 4. The second one spun around on one of the corners, a little cyclone of fate. Gravity took effect painfully slowly, eventually revealing the other number. 2. With a thumb forcefully digging into the edge of his jawbone and tilting his head sideways, Nerezza stepped forwards, opening his mouth and leaning closer to the pencil neck of the nerd. Crushing the laptop’s screen underfoot, his fangs scraped the surface of the vein before the lights of the club suddenly went out and a dead silence slammed over the world. Nerezza felt numb and dropped to the ground in total darkness and silence. Deprived of all senses. It was the second time that happened in one night… but it only took him those two times to notice the pattern. First, a typewriter slamming into his head. Then, the laptop breaking. Each time he awoke, in a new world and personality. When the next change occurred, he’d be ready. “If I were a classical vampyr…” Nerezza rose from his slumber to the tick-tock of a grandfather clock. The room was rather stark and bland, with walls papered in a faded pale orange and white. The technology was ancient by the comparison of the last sequence… electricity was not present in any shape or form, for a start. Nerezza sat in a rocking chair, his head lazily bobbing with the rhythmic creaking sound. His outfit this time was more stylish and pronounced than ever before. The most noticeable thing was his hair – a cascade of violet and black, beautifully arranged into helter-skelters of silk. His clothing was stolen from the classic novels of the era – Pride And Prejudice, Jane Eyre, Sense & Sensibility… shaking his head and rubbing his eyes, he noticed the lacy, pristine white frills emerging from his sleeves. Royal blue velvet encased his arm in a fine tailed coat. His trousers were creamy beige and tight, tucked into fine, brown shiny boots that came far up his calf. Under the coat was a white shirt, ruffled and bearing a large frilled front piece that spilled from his collar, marked with a brooch that glistened in the light of the oil lamps. “What ghastly hour do I awaken at?” Nerezza made a dramatic gesture, placing the back of his hand on his forehead. “What place of man do I come to find myself in? My mind has no recollection of my arrival here, have I been spirited away? Cry for shame, my soul beckons to this blackness of doubt to clear and show mine eyes the light of truth! How might I accept this new world? Shall I long and pine for times past and gone? How many ages can I make such a hollow, angsty and repetitive monologue last?!” Nerezza collapsed to his knees, hands clinging to the top of a table and knocking over a pile of books and a pot of ink, which “bled” over the carpet in a suggestive and macabre manner. His fingernails clawed at the wood and agony washed over his face, choking back tears of anguish. In a stagger of madness, he crashed from wall to wall, tripping over his own feet as he headed for the gun cabinet and snatched a rifle from the racking. His hands trembled in desperation as he tried to load the gun, fingers quaking and sweating as they clutched the barrel. He continued to stumble down the hallway while loading, jaw wide open and groaning out a hymn of regret and turmoil. A powerful kick fuelled with indiscriminate rage opened the bedroom door opposite his room and he stood in the doorway, breathing raggedly and red in the face with sweat dripping over his brow. And that was when he saw her. Dressed in the frumpiest of clothing imaginable and in dire need of exercise, she sat in the chair with a stern yet proud look on her face. The presence of Nerezza carrying the rifle seemed to bear no threat. In fact, all she did was sit and type. Each press build up, faster and faster, until the barrage of drumbeats ended in a single exclamation point of the bell ringing. “You may as well put that down.” She said with a cool, knowing confidence. Truly enough, Nerezza lowered the barrels of the gun and began to sob uncontrollably. Pushing her glasses back up her nose and peeking over her shoulder, she smiled in a satisfied yet cold way. “I knew you wouldn’t really be able to kill in cold blood, would you… my reluctant dark prince? You wouldn’t be able to bear the shame, the emptiness, the suffocating guilt.” “Evidently, fair lady, we are not the perpetually whining sacks of excrement you take us for.” Nerezza smirked, baring a toothy grin from one side of his face before cocking the gun and squeezing the trigger. A single BOOM later and Nerezza wasted no time in grabbing the back of the chair and shoving it forwards, dumping the overweight carcass onto the floor. As he took another round of ammunition from his pocket, his eyes began wandering over the typewriter. The very concepts horrified him to the core… disgustingly inaccurate portrayal. A fatal accident leading to Nerezza making Gaz a vampire to sustain his existence. A metric truckload of angst following. An energy-filled rapier duel in a field of roses and the subsequent beheading. All smothered in thinly veiled homosexuality leaking from every pore. “In Dio’s name…!” Nerezza recoiled in terror. “What kind of mentally diseased freak could honestly get enjoyment from writing about two gay vampires having a lovers’ tiff lasting centuries?” Without another word, Nerezza stuck his eyes over the barrels of the rifle and yanked down hard on the trigger. BANG! Nerezza’s eyes snapped open. He was back in London, lying on the floor of his bedroom. It was still early morning, with a cloudy sky giving way to a gaudy pink. Breathing in deeply, he sighed and scratched his chin. Today would be the same as any other ReVolution. Another person misjudging him, expecting to be ripped apart by razor-sharp claws or bitten into to leak blood all over the ring. But Nerezza never needed to do that. There was no need to slash people up. It was just wrestling. No satanic deals, no six-six-six. All he ever needed was the same as anyone else. A one-two-three. “What if I… was just a little different?”
