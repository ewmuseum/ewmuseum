'Beating Dan Ryan' Presents: A Day at Mount Rushmore

16 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/16/2010 [FADE-IN: The Welcome Center at the Mount Rushmore National Memorial. Dan Ryan is perusing a brochure while Lindsay Troy checks something on her Blackberry. People are milling about, some snapping pictures of the famous memorial while others are snapping pictures of the two stars. Suddenly, Castor V. Strife runs over, pushing children out of the way as both he and a camera man approach.] STRIFE: Sorry, sorry I'm late. Lard ass behind me was dragging anchor. [Both Troy and Ryan look over to the camera man. Troy has a puzzled look on her face.] TROY: What is Tom Adler doing here? ADLER: I'm interning with Castor to earn community college credits. RYAN: I see that a life after the New Frontier has really panned out for you. ADLER: Hey pal, not all of us were lucky to get out of there with sweet gigs. STRIFE: Look, if you shut up and do a good job, maybe I'll put in a phone call to Merritt and have you booked on Fish Fund XVMCMMIIV. ADLER: THAT WOULD BE A DREAM COME TRUE. STRIFE: Yeah. Whatever. Where the hell is everyone else? I TOLD PEOPLE THAT THEY NEEDED TO BE HERE BY 1:30. RYAN: You didn't get here until 2:00. [Castor stares at him.] STRIFE: You're not even supposed to be here. Dan Ryan isn't in "Beating Dan Ryan." TROY: I wasn't just going to leave him at the hotel. Is there even anything to do here besides come see this? STRIFE: Toss buffalo? TROY: Those things are best left to the Skywolf Fathers and Shaman, from the Forests of Kansas. STRIFE: Forget it. Look, we have a scheduled tour, that we've probably already missed. Give me the updated head count! TROY: James Irish is busy hunting for guild and killing Elf Knight Mohawks named Mr. T on World of Warcraft. Big Dog is praying at the Shrine of Peyton Manning. I got a text from Nova that said, "Watch out, I'm coming in hot"--whatever that means. And Jecht is camping out to be the first one in the theater for the midnight showing of Harry Potter. [There's an awkward pause, one worthy of the Late Late Show with Craig Ferguson.] STRIFE: It's Tuesday. TROY: I know. STRIFE: That doesn't open until Thursday night. TROY: I'm just saying, that's the info I got. STRIFE: How are we supposed to have a Team Building Excursion when there are only two team members here, one inept camera man, and the guy that the band was named after? RYAN: I can play a mean drum. TROY: On Rock Band. RYAN: ... I can play a mean drum. STRIFE: Great. This is just great. I'm going to have to cancel the WHOLE AFTERNOON because of you idiots. TROY: We can still go on the tour. Or improvise, like the time we had to get a stunt double for the Lindsay Troy: Wrestling Linebacker skits because I was pregnant. [CUT-TO: An office set, with Castor sitting in a director's chair.] STRIFE: OK, all you have to do is just run down the hallway, tackle the guy with the misspelled birthday cake, and deliver the line. Got it? [The person he's talking to nods her head and takes her mark. From off-camera, a familiar voice is heard.] TROY: Hey guys, sorry I'm late, hope I didn't miss the start of film-- [Her body double whips her head toward Troy's voice. The Queen of the Ring clenches her fists as she locks eyes with Cozen.] TROY: YOU.... [CUT-BACK] STRIFE: Yeah, I'd rather not. RYAN: Suit yourself, then. TROY: Dan and I will just go on a tour by ourselves. STRIFE: Fine. TROY: Fine. STRIFE: Fine! TROY: Fine. STRIFE: FINE! INNOCENT BYSTANDER: Hey look over there! [The crowd turns to see that four Enemigos have rolled a giant cannon through the entrance gate and have it pointed at Abraham Lincoln.] TROY: Is that the cannon from Colossus V? [From inside the cannon's mouth pops Nova, wearing his red and white jumpsuit from the same show. Underneath his arm is a helmet and in his mouth is, of course, a joint.] NOVA: Hey guys, the 'Migos are gonna try and shoot me up into Lincoln's Beard. Last one there's a dead wolf carcass on Sarah Palin's bed! [He pops the helmet on his head and disappears back into the cannon. Enemigo III grabs a match and starts to light it. TROY: CEASAR! NO! [She runs over while Castor leans over to Adler.] STRIFE: Make sure you're getting this. [FADE-OUT]
