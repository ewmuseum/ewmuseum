Can’t get it the same way down here.” “We’ll go to Mikey’s and enjoy a bowl of his classic, if that’s what you want.” “I was hoping you’d say that Nick. But I should go. Got a long Sunday ahead of me before I come home. See you on Monday.” She blew a kiss into the phone and The Ego returned it. He said goodbye and turned off the cordless. He was almost ready to give up on the baseball game. The Sox had a great chance to tie the ballgame but they never pulled the trigger when the time presented itself. He knew they were a better team than that. Monday, July 2, 12:45 PM Nicholas stood calmly by the luggage conveyor for his sister to show up. Her plane was on time and many people had made their way through the doors and received their bags. His sister was still not there. He was watching a little boy try and lift his heavy bag off the conveyor. He decided to actually do something nice for a change and help with the bag. He walked over and helped before the poor boy was dragged onto the belt and out the exit. “Here ya go little guy.” He put the bag on the ground and put out the handle for the boy to grab so he could roll it across the floor. The boy smiled and started to say thank you when his mother ran up yelling. “GET AWAY FROM MY BOY!” She grabbed the bag and her boy, checking to make sure he was ok, but Donnelly wouldn’t release his grip on the bag. “LET G...” “WHOA!” He interrupted her, releasing the bag and letting it fall to the floor where the boy picked up the handle. “The kid needed help and I was helping. You might wanna check yourself lady. Think you left that tampon in too long you frigid patsy.” The woman was shocked and grabbed her child’s hand. She proceeded to drag him away in a rather rough manner. Her son could be heard asking, “Mommy, what’s a tampon?” as they were further off, which won a satisfying smirk from Donnelly. After a couple more minutes, Hailey finally appeared through the doors. Nicholas had already grabbed her bags and had them sitting beside him as she approached. She gave him a big hug. “Hey bro! You miss me?!” “With each and every gunshot thus far.” He responded sarcastically as he squeezed her tightly. Her shoulder length reddish-brown hair tickled his face when she pulled away and playfully hit him in the shoulder. He laughed and lightly coughed, obviously doing much better from the flu than he was Saturday. After they had left the airport, Mikey’s was not that far off. Both went to sit down, absolutely famished and a rather large Italian-American man burst forth from the kitchen. “NITZ AND HAILEY!” He quickly came to the table and shook Nicholas’ hand and lifted Hailey from her seat by hers. He took her and proceeded to spin her around in dance moves which she laughed at.
