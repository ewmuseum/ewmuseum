ROAD TO PERDITION CONTINUES.

26 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/26/05 As memory may be a paradise from which we cannot be driven, it may also be a hell from which we cannot escape.' -John Lancaster Spalding, Aphorisms and Reflections “Tyler, I’m stuck.” “Passion, didn’t we discuss this before we started?” “Yeah, but, [sad] I just had to try it once” [Sigh] “I knew I should have hired professional movers…” “…Tyler, where are you going?” “To go make sense of life Passion, and pack up my reason for living.” “Tyler, but how am I going to move” “Just take the moving tape rolls off of your nipples Passion, we went through this…” And so it begins, we fade inside the Former Catholic Cathedral and now home to wayward wrestling legends. Today looks a little bit different than most days, because there are moving boxes and tape rolls, scattered all over the place. The kitchen is filled with boxes, the foyer area covered with them as well. One would get the impression that this is a place that’s going to be barren in a few days. We follow our hero as he walks towards the large confessional in the middle of the church. Lopez carries in his hands a large platinum brief case. Lopez opens the curtain to the confessional and reveals quite the stunning site. Some replicas, and some originals, but all gold, championship gold that is. Yet this just isn’t any normal championship gold, Lopez’s most prized gold, all of the Professional wrestling variety. To his left, the AWF Television Championship belt, The AWR Tag Team Championship, the AWR World Heavyweight Championship. To his right, the ASWF Heavyweight Championship, an older version of the ASWF World Heavyweight Championship, an older version of the ASWF Tag Team Championship. Lopez, as he stares to the middle of the confessional, and there, sitting brightly, an older version of the AWF International Championship, the more recent version of the PRIME 5 Star Title Championship, and finally, above them all, the most recent version of the PRIME Universal Heavyweight Championship. Lopez slowly lowers his head, and then begins to carefully grab each title, one by one, and place them into their respective spots inside of his multi-tiered Halliburton. The camera awkwardly turns in front of Lopez, as he sighs and then stares/ “Hello again my erstwhile opponent, this time yes, I’m referring to you, Tchu. With the impeding changes of the administration of PRIME, I no longer have a need for my current humble abode, quite sad actually, I felt right at home here. PRIME: A place where God’s are worshipped, and evil is cast out, almost felt like this is where I belonged all along, you know, with my peers. And hence what seems to be the problem with all of my current opponents, it seems to be the point of confusion for everyone involved but myself. So as the most sane and lucid person involved in this match, let me clarify for all involve, one major point that seems to have been overlooked…” Lopez blinks. He had started of the tournament on the right foot. He was beginning to see the top of the mountain. He started fresh. And with him starting fresh has plans on a being on a streak, a streak that he plans on continuing for a very long time, a winning streak. “Tchu you are a quality athlete, who have accomplished enough to be notable people in this tournament, you are my peer and thus can relate on a level of peers. Now listen closely, because here’s the sticking point, once “The Scientific Apparatus” Tyler Lopez was added to this tournament, you remained peers with all those other men who are going to be considered second best this go around and I remained what I was before the match, and what I will be after the match…Better than all of you.” Lopez sighs. He didn‘t care who his opponents were. Ever since he came in almost two years ago, Lopez became another name on the roster. Looked over. He wanted the spotlight on him. He liked being in the center of attention“You see I’ve grown tired of repeating the same points over and over, so what I’m going to do is cut to the chase with each and every one of you, and be done, we’ve had our fun, and now it ends. Let’s be honest with everyone, all of us want the same thing, championship gold. We all want to be the top man, and Tchu, in this business, make no mistake about it, if Tyler Lopez or anyone else has to slap the taste out of another man’s face, and ram it into a steel cage, they’ll do it, gleefully, no questions asked. Tchu, I’m supposedly the delusional one of PRIME, but you’re the one making absolutely no sense. You can lecture about no dignity and no morals as if it’s a shock to you, as if me suddenly looking out for the greatest asset in this business, myself, is a new thing. But that’s not where the fun begins, no the fun is in attention to detail, first of all I am the greatest wrestler in the PRIME, period. Second of all, I am the 4th best wrestler in the Universe, semantics yes, but an important fact that shows you don’t pay attention to detail.” Lopez smiles. He is convinced. He was back to his old ways. He had to be. He was forced. He has a plan, a plan that unimaginable, a plan that could change the entire existence of PRIME. Dominance had to be present in this match, and Lopez knew that. “Because if you did, you wouldn’t be so shocked with my ability to be what is called a 3 Dimensional Character. [Lopez feigns mock shock] I know that must just blow your world, considering you operate completely in a 2 Dimensional world. [Mocking] “Me Tchu, I fight tell I have no more breath in my body, thus I am a better wrestler than you, goodbye now, return to your regularly scheduled life” Lopez rolls his eyes. He would stoop depths unimaginable to win. Lopez was starving, ravenous for a win. Tyler Lopez was more prepared than he had ever been before. “Spare me. I’ve seen your kind come and go, time and time again, you’ve been here and you haven’t evolved one bit. You’re the same stupid little kid that walked in here, spouting the same boring shit. You can make a character turn, and nothing will change. You can’t understand me, because I’m everything you want to be. I can rip you apart with humor, I can rip you apart with common sense, and I can rip you apart with underhanded tactics, all in which I’ve done and or plan to do, and not think twice about it.” Lopez shrugs. He is not convinced. He knows that this man is not a threat to his chances on continuing his path to King of Kings. He has to do any and everything to get it. It was impossible to make everyone comprehend his thoughts, his emotions; he would have to use his actions to speak for him. “You look in the mirror every day and you stare at a man that has boundaries, boundaries on his potential, boundaries on his skills, the man people look at now, is the same man they saw four years ago, and will be the same man they will see eight years from now. It’s not that you won’t change, it’s that you can’t. You’ve written me off as washed up, but honestly, if we compared points in our careers, You vs. Me, I think we’d find whom exactly the washed up one truly was. While I could easily bounce between Tag Team Success, Singles Success, all in very different manners, the one thing that never changed, was the success, but I evolved, I didn’t get old, I got great, I didn’t grow bitter, I grew better. Contrary to your life path, change is indeed good; you’re either evolving, or letting it pass you by. Hence, you’ll be the same two dimensional characters you’ve always been, you’ll just find a different person and place to look important at. And that’s why you have such a problem with me…” Lopez blinks. “In every generation, I’ve been a success, and I have evolved, reinventing myself, but the success rate increased, you, you’ve just seem to be the same old, same old. But, if you paid attention to detail, or even had a grasp of what it meant to be a real legend, you’d understand that, but I guess one cannot comprehend that in which one can never attain. [Lopez nervously chuckles] Look back throughout history, hell; look back at my matches. I’ve been everything you’ve wanted to be, multi-faceted. Some traits remain the same, I am just a man, but something’s always different. There’s no desperation here, I can rest easy knowing it’d take you 4 more LIFETIMES to achiever what I’ve achieved Tchu, and hence where your confusion comes in at. They call me crazy, but, as the old boring saying goes, Insanity is doing the same thing over and over again, and expecting a different result.” Lopez sneers. Suddenly his voice is soft, and amused. His eyes take a glint. Lopez has a prophecy. He wants glory. Nothing can come in the way of his future, his spirit. “So I guess the child lyrics stay true on your side too, I know you are, but what am I? [Lopez smiles] If you’ve seen one match involving Tchu, you’ve seen them all.” Lopez sighs. Lopez realized the reputation that he was getting in the locker room. He didn’t like feeling that he wasn’t a threat. He didn’t like feeling that he was not a factor. He hates these thoughts. “There are few things in this business that confuse me, and you’re lucky enough to be one of those. Not because you’re overly complex, but because the more you talk, the less sense you seem to make. You may note that I haven’t won a championship in my PRIME career. Lopez sighs. He is bored by the opponent that lies before him. Just another hurdle that doesn’t need to be jumped over. He plans on just pushing it down. “So let’s try and make sense for you. First off, I have always earned to respect that I sought hence my entrance music. I have always felt like I have to earn respect, no not from you boys in the locker room but those who sit in the administrative offices. A record that still stands. But lets exam your claim a little further, is it that I can’t win a championship for a year, or haven’t had the shot. “Because you see, I’ve only had a couple of shots in my career here in PRIME, and if you do a little research it all has been in the last four months. But lets be honest Tchu, you’d kill to be in my kind of slump, you’ve never been successful enough to garner a couple of DQ’s as a slump. You know why you can’t compare your accomplishments, or hell, any of yours to mine…” Lopez stares up. His rant is demanding. His rant is informative. His rant is truth. “Because there’s no comparison. I have no equals, and for damn sure not you. But of course you want to come clean, correct? Let’s do that. Everything I’ve ever wanted to accomplish, I’ve done, or had a hand in doing. You think I’m a failure, but am I really? “So while the face has changed, the threat remains the same. Riddle me this, when the show this week closes, and my mission is accomplished, regardless of how it comes to be, won’t I have won, and people like you have lost? And in the end, isn’t that all that matters.” Lopez shrugs. He doesn’t care. He knows he is on the path to his destiny. He knows no one can stop him but himself. It’s like the devastation of his home, he knows that New Orleans will rebuild, and his rebuilding began two weeks ago. “So come clean, is it that you’re so fixated on the past that you refuse to acknowledge the very immanent future in front of you? Or is it that I’m so fixated on the past that I’m well aware of the future that awaits me. You can’t knock me for the past, cause it’s something you know nothing about. It’s either one or the other, and sadly, when it’s all over, you’ll be left standing dumbfounded and empty handed, and you won’t have the option to quit before it’s all over, because for you, it’ll already be over. Because sadly, without the past, there would be no future, and for you Tchu, the future looks as bleak as your past.” Lopez slowly has packed the championship belts inside the brief case. He stares longingly as he places the PRIME Universal Heavyweight Championship inside of the briefcase. He slowly rises to his feet, and turns towards the aisle way. “It’s quite simple, between you and I. Cliché as it may be, the enemy of my enemy is my friend…” Lopez nervously chuckles and then stops. “…For now.” Lopez walks away from confessional, leaving the Halliburton in front of it, open. Passion bounces in and glances down at the suitcase, and stares up and yells at Lopez. “Tyler, Tyler, you forgot to put a title in here, there are only 12…” Lopez turns and arrogantly smirks. “…I didn’t forget babe, lucky 13, I’m saving the best for last.” Passion stares down confused, Lopez chuckles as he walks away and continues to pack and think, as we slowly fade away, knowing that this saga shall continue… ROAD TO PERDITION CONTINUES.
