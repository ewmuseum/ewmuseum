baby envy, flapping teen gums & a half full bottle of courage

19 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19 sep 08 Adam sat on the blanket and shook a tiger with a rattle in its belly. It jingled and jangled as the infant “gooed” in appreciation of its sounds. Watched him but I felt nothing for him. Here he was. My own flesh and blood. And all he did was make my blood boil. Central Park was nice this time of year but I still felt cold. Was my emotions that made me feel cold and starin’ at my son sent a shiver up and down my spine. April sipped coffee from the lid/cup of her thermos. Her eyes were fixed on me and she was trying to do that thing where women read your mind and assume. I fucken hate that. “You don’t feel anything for him, do ya?” she finally divulges. Say nothing and keep my mouth shut. Just watch the kid and take a swig from my own coffee. April was kind enough to bring a cup for me. She made good coffee. Kind from a perculator machine. Preferred that to that instant shit. “Why can’t you just feel something?” Turn my head and stare at her. Send her that “like you don’t fucken know” look. She just echoes her last question with an eyebrow raise that called for an answer. “What do ya fucken want me to feel, Ape? You turn ya Goddamn back on me for this Dick feller and shun me. Then ya go and slap me in the face by namin’ the kid after him. Howd’ya think that’d make me feel, Ape? Huh? Think I’m gonna go down the street, buy streamers and throw a Goddamn parade for the sumbitch?” April never appreciated my sarcasm OR my acidic tongue. She watched Adam and I watched her. “Why do ya always gotta call him ‘the kid’?” Stupid questions deserve stupid answers. But I owe her more than that. “I can’t stomach his name, alright? I just can’t.” “And that’s why you get an hour with him on a weekend, Brock. Don’t you want to spend more time with your son?” she demands. I search inside my heart but I know the answer there isn’t the answer she wants to hear. Just shut my mouth and keep staring at the kid. Watching him play with the tiger as he torments it with the continuous shaking. “Every time I see the kid I just feel anger, Apr-“ “ADAM! HIS FUCKING NAME IS ADAM, YOU SONOFABITCH!” she shouts. She batter my shoulder with a few uncoordinated slaps and pushes up off the bench. The kid is picked up weighted on April’s hip and she begins gathering her belongings. “It’s only been forty-five minutes, April. I still get fifteen more.” “Tell me a reason I should let you seem him for fifteen more, Brock. Give me one good reason!” More demands. My eyes fix on hers and see the fire deep inside her belly raging and hungry for more fuel I can give it. Searching inside myself again I continue to find no answers my eyes wander lower until they stare at the ground, to ashamed to look at April’s. Hear her voice break and her throat crumble. See her eyes blink as they fight the waterworks and one solitary and betraying tear rolls down her pretty cheek. “I thought so.” She never really said it loud enough to be called anything but a whisper but the pain and torture in her tone made it seem like it shouted at full capacity in my head. Gathered her things and left me standing there with the spare cup of coffee in my hand, just watching her and the kid cross Central Park. Everybody. -- ] = } s h e p h e r d { = [ -- Scotch burns the back of m throat as it washes down my throat. Cringe and wipe my mouth with the forearm which lacks a hand. Other hand clutches the bottle real tight. Trying to hide inside of that bottle. “I gots the bluesh,” I sing to myself. “The Goddamn bluesh. I gots the baby’sh named after fucktard bluesh.” Swig. “I got fucken bluesh fuck. Oooo-wooo-woo the blue-hoo-hoo-hoo-hoos! I gots where the fucksh me damn hand go bluessssshhhh!” Longer swig. Stop singing. My own voice grates against my own teeth and I let go of the bottle to put my forehead in my hand. I poke at the bottle with my stump regretting ever opening it and anticipating the end of it. I feel my soul grumble for more so I remove my forehead and swig again. Push up onto my feet and stagger through my apartment and into the lounge room. There, standing upright on the table I see him. The Illustrious Face-Eater. Action figure. From the PRIME Intense Champion series. He stands there with his shit eating grin mocking me. “You!” Swig. Point at the opposing figure with hand holding the bottle. “Funk… fuggggg… fucken swine! Don’t you unnerstan what yous doned to me Fashey? HUH?” The bottle flies from my hand and knocks the figurine flat on it’s back on the table. It’s pose never seems to change as it stares up at the ceiling as though it were done staring at me. Like it were too good to look at me. Mocking me through not recognising my presence. “Is… is… are yous to scared to look at my eye? Look me in the eyes? Hmmm? Huh? Yous are scared to look at me then? Can’t faced me Fashey? Yeah?” Stumble as I cross the lounge toward the figure, tripping up on my own feet and falling to my knees before the laid out enigma which plagued my mind twenty-four/seven, never begging for a rain cheque. Shuffled across the floor on my knees toward the web of hate on my coffee table. The symbol of wretched despise my life had become. “Why’d yous do it?” I had to ask. “Why’ded yous steal them? Steals m-m-my familililies?” I needed an answer. “Why?” I got no answer. -- ] = } s h e p h e r d { = [ -- I stand there with my shoulders pressed back as far as they can go to puff out my chest to show my confidence. My confidence in the ring had been fuelled with the destruction of the Bard last time. My good hand clutched the mic and my eyes twinkled with pride beneath my neon green shades. “Jacob Moreno… Just Wrestling… a rebirth was witnessed. The rebirth of the One-Armed Wrecking Crew when I didn’t even break a sweat making a point with the Bard. All I broke was his ego and tore him limb-from-limb in front of the Just faithful. Shouldn’t of even fucken bothered. I mean… I’ve had harder work outs takin’ a shit and I’m pretty regular.” Choke a laugh and continue on. “I mean let’s take a look at what’s happened so far… I had the rust politely knocked out of me by some bum I’m itchin’ to get back inside a ring with and then they throw this faggot at me. I kick ass his so hard he thought he’d been broken in by railway sleeper. And then they dish up Jacob Moreno?” I hold my hand and prosthetic out as though I awaiting an answer. “Who is he? I’ll tell you what he is. Some damn greenhorn steppin’ out into shark infested water with blood flavoured under pants on. You’re making a big move, kid. Gettin’ inside a ring with a proven fiend. I’m not takin’ hostages anymore. We’re lookin’ at casualties and bodies. There’s a war goin’ on. A war I plan on winning.” Look down at my waist and make the cliché belt gesture. So fucken what. I’m not reinventing the wheel here. I’m about entertaining. “We get closer and closer to wrappin’ the belt ‘round somebody’s waist in Just. Findin’ the first Just Champion. Some might say the competition is tight. Neck and neck and shit like that. Anybody could come away with it. I don’t say that at all.” Shake my head, to add to the effect. “I say get it engraved right now. Put my nameplate on the title and prepare the tickertape parade. Strike up the band. Train up the cheerleaders. Close of the streets. The champ is here. Hand it over and save everyone the embarrassment of lookin’ up from the canvass at the Just Champion.” Point down the barrel of the camera like I’m pointing directly at Jacob Moreno. “Moreno, I dunno who you are, dunno what your capable of but there is one constant round these parts and that’s if you step inside the ring with me you leave it on a fucken gurney. I’m the face of Medical Insurance. They pay me fucken commissions for all the work I give ‘em. And you’re just another pay-cheque, Jake.” Push the shades off my face with my prosthetic hand and stare down the camera. “Think about it long and hard. Think about the life you could leave. Think about all the things you could do if you just stay home. I don’t wanna hurt you. You think I enjoy this? Think I enjoy tearin’ fucktard punk like you a new one every time they make the stupid decision of getting in a ring with me? You think that’s something I like doing?” Shrug and nods my head. I can hear his answer without hearing the words. “OK. You’re right. I do enjoy. I fucken LOVE it. I’d stomp your head on weekends if I had the chance. I’d chuck your innards at my wall tryin’ to paint it red. Hell, I’d hollow out ya rib-cage and buy a fucken canary. Pain is my game. And you’re comin’ to play, Moreno. You’re comin’ to play and you’re comin’ to lose.” A leer of frightening proportions is sent down the camera lens to Jacob Moreno. “Mark my words.” Nod at the camera operator and make a cutthroat gesture to suggest I was done with my banter. Let it fade to black or something like that. You know how it goes.
