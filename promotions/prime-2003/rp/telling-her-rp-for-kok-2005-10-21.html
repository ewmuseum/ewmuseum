Telling Her (RP for KoK)

21 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/21/05 ”They made an ingenuous and disorganized effort to escape from captivity but ultimately failed, because they were unable to find the bars of the cage. If you can’t discover what’s keeping you in, the will to get out soon becomes confused and ineffectual.” Ishmael by Daniel Quinn Some day, sometime in the evening The comforter stopped just below her breasts, covering her legs and stomach in a warmth that seemed foreign to him. As she read, he sat at the computer, watching her dance with the pages of a book he could only dream with lust of creating. Her attention was held by each of its words the way his was held by each of her breaths. One poured into the next, never breaking flow, growing into a story that had one life… only now, it would be short life, destined to repeat itself every twenty minutes. It was nearly impossible to fathom… a half hour life. From time to time, she would move the book, blocking her eyes from his viewpoint. It was only during those times that his mind shifted from each breath she took to each that had passed him with the breeze of sunken skies that filtered in line by line through the blinds covering the window. He thought about how many times she had laid there in that very bed, under those same covers, reading a book, watching television, her heart heavy from the unfortunately true tales of tragedy so many millions dealt with each day. She would cry for them. How many times she had laid there shaking her head with sympathy for the countless rape victims that swallowed their stories and blamed themselves, or cringing for the realness of Rwanda. And yet, recently, selfishly, how many times he had laid there beside her, hating what she read, ignoring the glow of the television, and cringing for the realness of their problems…just their own misfortunes. That was all. Unlike most nights, when the animal in him would watch her lie there in a t-shirt and a pair of panties and wish she were naked, covering him in her existence, this night it was he who wished he was naked, exposed to her in the truest form. But as he wondered whether she was smiling behind her book with each page she turned, he could do nothing but crack his neck and scratch his thigh through jeans worn thin. Like any man who actually loved a woman, and looked at her with more than visions of sweat and sex, there were times when he doubted himself. Some nights he would lie awake doing the typical self-loathing routine. He asked himself if he made her happy, wondered if he was right for her, thought that she deserved better and he too often came up short. Always though, he had been wrong… just playing the part-time pessimist that was a given with any existence. Those nights lying awake were unnecessary. His doubts were typical but were in fact just doubts, not reality… at least that had been the case until recently. Somewhere along the way, he had corrected what was right into a mistake. What exactly had happened, or when it had occurred… those facts were likely rather clear. But, now, after what he figured was at least two months that seemed like more setting suns than his brain could comprehend, when and how things had started their descent no longer seemed of any sort of importance. By this point, he wondered only how to get back, and where exactly ‘back’ was supposed to be. He knew that she needed to know exactly what it was that had made him so unable to be anything other than what he was…he had simply spent the last few weeks figuring it pointless. It was futile. So he hadn’t tried, hadn’t told her. Hadn’t told her what she would have desired to hear, what she deserved to hear. But what was he supposed to do? How was he supposed to put any sort of sequence or storybook order to information she couldn’t grasp for more than a dying moment? She knew most of the details, most of what happened, she had just lost it somewhere in the back of her mind where no thing disturbed. Maybe it was squashed in short term memory storage, defied by mind to flourish and suppressed out of existence. Or maybe it was all side-tracked, made a wrong turn, got stuck somewhere in that ninety percent scientists say people don’t use. She deserved more than that, but the tragic event that had stolen her memory the past few weeks, had stolen something of his too, and now, his senses were dull. No story he could tell seemed worth hearing in his own opinion. For, however, the first time since he had stepped foot back into the ring, sitting there at the computer watching her read nothing while he booked tickets for a flight to an event he probably didn’t belong at, he knew he owed her. He owed her more than to give up just because his self had recently abandoned him. For another moment he watched her sit there and read, wondered when she would put the book down for just a moment and lose her place forever, despite the valiant efforts of a bookmark. With each passing minute she would turn the page, not knowing at any moment that the story might make reference to something she read thirty pages before and wouldn’t remember. He’d recognize its arrival by the puzzled look on her face followed by a slow motion to set the book down on the covers, casting its words away from her glance while she tried to figure out what she had missed and how she had possibly missed it. He waited for that moment… and it arrived. “Mary” “What is it, sweetie?” she pulled herself up, leaning her back against the headboard, propping her elbows on the feather pillows that had rested beneath her head just seconds earlier. “I…” even though he knew it wouldn’t help, she’d forget it all in minutes anyway, he felt the need to tell her. Nothing else was helping things get any better. The words came out, “…book’s not any good?” “I dunno, just… bits and parts of it don’t make any sense.” “The past few weeks, I’ve been away from time to time.” He made no bridge from one subject to the next. It didn’t seem there was an appropriate one. “What?” It was enough already to confuse her. She didn’t even have any memory of him being gone at least one night every week. “I’ve been wrestling again. A couple months ago, I joined PRIME… the federation Killean has spent the last year or so as part of. I’ve already wrestled at a few shows and…” “What? Why? You told me you were done with wrestling. Retired. You… gave me your word. Why?” It appeared to him that tears were already forming in the corners of her eyes, though he hadn’t expected much less. “Because I… its like there’s nothing here for me. I need something, even if just one night a week, to get me living again. To feel pain, to cause pain, to compete. Something to give the week some sort of form so that I at least know what the hell day it is.” “What are you talking about? What do you mean… there’s nothing here for you?” The tears he had suspected in the corners of her eyes had now confirmed their existence by finding their way down her cheeks. “You have Anterograde Amnesia. Short-term memory loss. A few weeks ago, there was an accident at the café you were having lunch at, and…” His words began to trail off, not exactly sure how to break the news to her, not exactly sure it mattered. “And so you went back to wrestling. You broke your promise to me?” “I had to, I was going crazy here at home…” “But… you lied to me.” Her words choked through sobs, which tried to steal each of her breaths for themselves. “Think of it as… changing my mind, instead of ‘breaking my promise’. Does that make it any better?” The words were declared with a clear cynicism. Her tears eroded his patience. The effort he was making to be honest, to be straight forward, was being met with watery eyes and accusations that he couldn’t handle, no matter how true they might be. “Does it meet with your approval then?” His question was answered with nothing but tears. “I’m sure when, if, you snap out of this and get some sort of memory back, this will all be really tough for you. But right now, I’m the one who has to wake up every morning to the same god damn day I just had! I’m the one who has to kiss you goodnight every evening, then wake up the next day and answer questions as to why you can’t remember the last time I kissed you, or told you I loved you. And now I’m some bad guy cuz I decided to do something to get away from it all one night a week, and god forbid that might go against some promise I made ages ago!” He slammed his fist down on the desk, causing the cup that held a variety of pens and pencils to rattle and roll on its edge before falling over. “Matthew… please stop…like you said you would… please…” “Please fucking come back to me!” As he finished the sentence, he pushed himself away from the desk, the momentum knocking the chair to the ground. The sound of its collapse offered only a seconds relief from the sound of her crying. Matt left the room, grabbing the doorknob behind him, shutting off the room from the rest of the house. Distance and doors, anything he could find to silence her tears. * * * Writings: Part I When you sit, you daydream and you often tell stories. Stories of your own life, or maybe stories of what your own life would be in the perfect world. Sometimes they’re just random thoughts that fly through your mind in an effort to keep you preoccupied from the boredom of a rambling speaker at a banquet, or a tiresome dinner with the in-laws. Other occasions, they command time all their own. They consume a space and energy that exists solely for them and every effort they receive belongs to them, is dedicated to them with great deliberants. In those times, some of us, we write those thoughts and dreams and fears down on paper. We call them journals, though ‘medicine’ or ‘self-therapy via pen and paper’ might be more accurate descriptions. His, though, weren’t really journal entries. Journal entries start with a date and time. Something like, “Saturday, August 11th 2005. 9:00P.M” or maybe, “Monday the 28th of June 2004. 10:05PM.” Whatever the case, they began with a date, to forever mark the event that is about to be recorded, then jumped to “Today I was out and…”. His didn’t start with any of that. They just jumped straight to the “Today I…”, so that in a couple years, or a couple months, or maybe even a couple weeks… he wouldn’t remember when they occurred. They wouldn’t be important dates in his life, they would just be words of something that had happened sometime. Today I went out for lunch with Seph. He had seen what had been going down in PRIME lately and wanted to talk about things between Killean and I. It must be hard for Seph, sitting at home, still not fully recovered, watching two of his close friends finding themselves on opposing sides of an ugly feud with a long and dark history. Killean was his protégé, the man he brought into the business to follow in his footsteps. I’m the old friend, the man who was his tag team partner and only ally in the business when his name and stock caught fire and rocketed through the roof. Now, he has to sit and watch to men he considers to be nearly brothers of his, torn apart on national television. He seemed more concerned than angry as we ate lunch. I tried to explain to him where I was coming from, and he seemed to understand, though he asked me on more than one occasion that no matter how much right I may have to want to seek revenge against Killean, to please just let it die. He said it would be best for everybody, especially Mary. I told him, I thought what would be best for Mary is for her memory to come back. That seemed like the most logical response, but he just kept telling me what would be best for Killean, for me, for Mary, and even though he had it all wrong, and he seemed genuinely concerned, he didn’t seem to be listening to what I was telling him. I thought about reaching across the table and punching him, but Seph was too quick, knew me too well. He stopped me before I even really had the notion. I didn’t take much away from the lunch. The food was good, but not too much else. I did, however, discover that Seph was very openly concerned about what this feud between Killean and myself might lead to. He told me he’s worried about ‘The Supreme Machine’, and my own, well being. I’ll have to remember to pass that information along to Killean. * * * Some other day Together, they stood in the kitchen, cupboards open, loading them with the various groceries they had just returned home with, loads of bags in his arms, Georgie in hers. Mary put away various foods in the pantry. The loaf of bread, the hamburger buns, a bottle of ketchup. He loaded the cabinets under the sink, placing away the dishwashing liquid, the steel wool pads, tablets for the dishwasher, anything to cleanse the filth from their lives. He put it all away. Georgie began to cry, much like she had done throughout the grocery store as Mary had carried her, while Matthew had filled the shopping cart with whatever they needed. Mary stopped loading the cupboards and headed into the bedroom to comfort their child. He looked around at the rest of the groceries, grabbing a couple things he knew belonged in the fridge. As he reached to open the door to the refrigerator, he saw an old note from Mary saying that Killean had called. He looked carefully over the message, listening in the background as he heard Mary soothing George, calming the child, probably working her magic and getting Georgie to fall into a peaceful sleep. She had a way with their child, always able to say and do the right thing. When she wanted George to stop crying, she could do it in an instant. When she wanted George to smile or giggle, the baby’s laughter would fill the air within a minute. Mary had the baby close to heart, able to offer it the ultimate comfort. It was the only genuine comfort in the house the last few weeks he thought. When she returned from the bedroom, he noticed the look she gave him as he studied the note on the fridge. Mary came over his way, wrapped her arms around his waist, kissed him on the back of the neck, and rested her chin on his shoulder. “Whatcha lookin’ at?” “Just a note from Killean from a message he left a few weeks ago while I was out of the house.” He pulled it off the surface of the refrigerator and crumpled it in his fist. Tossing it towards the trashcan in the kitchen. “It’s nothing of importance now. Just forgot to take it down after I first read it.” “What did he have to say? I don’t seem to remember taking that message.” “Just wanted to talk a little business, I guess.” “Talk a little business? I didn’t know Killean was still stopping by for appointments. I thought he had gotten himself back on his feet and was doing really well.” “Not therapy, sweetheart. Wrestling business.” “Wrestling business?” She unwrapped her arms, releasing his torso, stepped back from him a bit. “What do you mean ? Why wrestling?” “About PRIME. He’s back in the business. Back in the ring.” “Oh” it was a simple response, and the one syllable escaped her lips, he turned to face her, their eyes only a few inches apart. “And so am I.” Like a battering ram, the words pushed her back several steps, causing her to bump into one of the chairs at the small table. “What?” Before he could even truly begin to form a response, she demanded an answer again. “What?!” “I joined PRIME a couple months ago. You…” “You told me you were finished with wrestling. Retired! R-E-T-I-R-E-D. It means to be done with for good, Matthew!” Her words nearly shook the contents of the freshly stocked cabinets “I’m sorry. You were involved in an accident and lost your memory” his words lacked any of the power and anger hers did. He said it almost without courage, only a bit of shame in his tone. “I didn’t know what to…” “Lost my memory?” She was squinting, her eyes nearly shut in a piercing form. “What the hell are you talking about?” “There was an accident at a café. You were there for lunch… the shock, it caused you to… you can’t form any new memories right now. She wanted an answer to the question, and the truth was, her memory hadn’t recovered a bit since the accident, and the suffocating feeling he had at home, reliving each day the same as before was still very much present. Just a nuisance to be dealt with in passing while she straightened things up on her own. * * * Writings: Part II They weren’t really journal entries. Journal entries fall in consecutive order in a notebook specifically for such a purpose. His were written on loose pieces of paper, napkins, the inside covers of books… whatever he held in his hand when it hit him. They weren’t journal entries, they were a tired and confused mind spilling as ink into words. Some were short, jotted down on the couch in front of the television during a commercial break. I want to be like Danny Ferguson. I want this to be a script I’m just following. I want to be an actor, want to wait for the ‘aaaand cut!’ that I know is coming to bring this all to a wrap. This is all too real if you ask me. You know what would be nice? Acting some script given to me that I can mess up and just laugh and say “damn, I screwed up! Let’s try that one again, fellas!” Danny’s got it all figured out, and that’s more than I can claim. He’s got the right idea. The bodyguards, the drinkin’ and laughin’ and bar room brawling just for the hell of it. Those movie roles and sitcom appearances… lots of people seem to long for that fame and that seven figure pay off for that blockbuster film. I’m sure that’s all nice, but I think the best part is the part that no one ever talks about, nobody ever longs for or dreams of. Ferguson probably gets it, just most people don’t. I don’t need the number one summer movie spectacular. I want the script. I want it all to be for fun and games, maybe a little money and fame. I want none of it to matter when I go home at the end of the day. I want to act. I want her to be acting. I want it all not to be real. Others were longer, starting on a piece of paper, finishing up on another, or maybe on a paper towel laying on the oak dining room table where he would sit and let his hand flow at the control of his mind. I see Karina and I see something beautiful. I see a powerful woman, a strong woman, and I think back to what Mary was when she still had her memory. That passion and strength, unmatched by any man, untouched by any adversity, refusing in its own unique way to be scarred by the tragedy and misfortune of the human race. I think sometimes, for no reason, as I am now, of Mary’s smile and in one moment it eases my pain spawning a smile of my own. Then, in the next moment, it terrifies me, leaving me awed in its merciful power. Her smile and the words and thoughts behind it… all possessing a strength I spend most days searching for. Unsuccessfully. In Karina I see much of the same, though I doubt her strength and power exhibits quite the same merciful manner. I can’t say that at King of Kings I truly want to step into the ring with Karina, or Aimz, or Danny, but I can’t say I truly want much of anything anymore. Doubt has given way to indifference over the last few days. Talent wise I can’t imagine I’m Karina’s equal; it’s likely I’m not even quite in her league or the league of Aimz for that matter. Two women, one I’m destined to face, the other all too real a possibility and maybe all too familiar a face thanks to Killean… and I’ve found myself a step below their power and command. Maybe if I could face the woman I spend every day of my life with and walk away having actually made a difference, changed something, I’d feel a little better about my chances against two very dangerous women come Sunday night. I can’t find the right way to reconnect with Mary, to bring her back with me, to be any of what she deserves, and it’s left me apathetic. And that sole fact may be my saving grace in a few days when I find myself standing across from… her. I can’t seem to fix things with Mary in any way, shape or form. I can’t snap my fingers or scream and yell or whisper and kiss and just bring back her memory. Neither my love nor my absence can just rid of her amnesia and bring back everything we know. I can’t beat it, so I’ve spent the last two months outlasting it. I guess it’s what I expect from KoK. What you can’t beat, you outlast till it can’t take anymore. I wonder if outlasting the King of Kings will be as painful as these last two months with Mary? At least it can’t be any more impossible. * * * Another day “That’s why I’m doing this.” “You’re wrestling because I had an accident?” Her words were stoical. “I’m wrestling because you have short-term memory loss… amnesia. You can’t remember a damn thing since the accident I just told you about and it’s killing me or numbing me, I can’t figure out exactly which one it is. That’s why I’m wrestling. I don’t know how to make it any clearer than that.” It was the third time that week he had told her. This time though, his words didn’t strike her as violently as her hand did him. In one swing, she let out an aggression he had not seen from her. Ever. The sting carried through his cheek, where her palm print had left its mark. From just below his eye, blood flowed from a cut her diamond ring had manifested as it had slipped around her finger while dicing across his face. She turned and walked away from him. “Mary…”A solitary tear found its way into the trickle of red that flowed down his cheek, and there, that sole drop of emotion cleansed itself in the blood. “Where are you going?” “To write this all down. So I’ll remember.”
