Date: December 07 and March 08 December 1, 2007. Night. "Yep, you found me. Leave the details at the beep." "Hey Lindz, it's Matt. I know Wednesday at ReV was kinda crazy with Sonny and all, and I ... (sigh) ... I know I should have said something ahead of time, but Blaine...anyway, it's not important. I'm sure we'll talk about it later. I'm really calling to tell you that Mary went into early labor today and, well, it's a boy! Hunter Dean [name withheld]. I'm just...God, I can't even tell you how happy we are. Mary's doing alright and we wanted you to be one of the firsts to know. Call me when you get a chance. ---- I'm really not a jealous person. Perhaps, once upon a time, back when I was some greenhorn who had just fallen off the turnip truck all doe-eyed into the wrestling world, I had felt Jealousy's ivy fingers snaking around my heart. Worked my ass off too hard and too much only to see opportunity after opportunity get passed off to some schlep with the right genitalia and the workrate of a wet noodle. The unfortunate curse of my family's Italian stubbornness and my own personal desire to give the world a big "fuck you" made me keep on keepin' on for longer than anyone was willing to fathom until all that I wanted was finally in my grasp. Only took me nine some-odd years, but greatness is not always recognized or rewarded when it's deserved. Now in my twelfth year in this sport, I find myself clamoring for something worth far more than any belt I've ever held, any prestige I've been awarded, any recognition passed my way. It's more important than any "favorites" list, Hall of Fame nods and lasting legacy. It's tangible, precious, and not easily come by. I nearly had it once. Matt [name withheld] has it now. And I want it so very badly... ---- March 1, 2008. Cambridge, Massachusetts "I can't believe you've been in this house for six months and you [i]still[/i] haven't unpacked everything yet." From her seat on her bedroom's Brazilian Cherry hardwood floor, Lindsay Troy looked up from an unlabeled box of miscellaneous knick-knacks to her younger sister, Alaina Ryan, who was seated on the bed and going through a box of her own. "Well Lanni, it's not like I'm home on a regular basis to do any of this. I unpacked the important stuff. Clothes, dishes..." "Trophies, belts..." "See?" Lindsay replied with a wry grin as she unwrapped a picture frame and tossed the bubble-wrap into a corner. "I appreciate you coming up to help, though. At this rate, it'd be Christmas again and I'd still have boxes abound." "I needed a vacation anyway. Of course, my idea of a vacation didn't include snow and ... decorative candles?" Alaina held up a candle and candle holder for closer inspection, scrutinizing the metal holder to see if it was a figment of her imagination. "I had no idea you actually had stuff like this." "I'm not completely helpless in the 'Suzie Homemaker' department," Lindsay slapped her hands against her thighs and hit the exaggerated eye-roll. "Could've fooled me. This is very Martha Stewart of you," Alaina chortled. "Will I be finding the stenciled hand towels next? Or some seashell bookends?" "Y'know what?" The older Troy woman climbed to her feet and stalked over to her sister, whose gleeful chuckle now morphed into a full-blown belly laugh at Lindsay's thunderhead of a scowl. "Give me the goddamn things if all you're going to do is make fun of 'em." "Nonono, c'mon Lindz,," a snort from Alaina, "I can see it now: 'Today on Martha!, we'll be featuring exquisite decorative candles from the collection of PRIME Universal Champion, Lindsay Troy. Who would have thought this rough-and-tumble woman would have such delicate taste in home accents?'" "How is it that your two-year-old is more mature than you?" Lindsay climbed onto the bed and lunged for her sister, who squirmed out of the way, still laughing hysterically. Alaina stuffed the candle and holder under a pillow and fell on top of it. Tears formed at the corners of her crystal blue eyes as a result of her mirth and streamed down her face once her sister starting tickling her underneath her arms. "AHHHH! Stop it! Lindz! Stopppp! I hate...I hate being..." Alaina's writhed away from her sister, but Lindsay kept right at it. "Oh, you hate being tickled? You should have thought of that before you started ragging on my friggin' candle!" Alaina kicked her feet and howled, still trying to escape Lindsay's clutches. Her left foot struck the box and sent it careening off the bed and onto the floor, contents spilling and scattering all over the place. Lindsay stopped her assault and stared at the mess, while Alaina wheezed and tried to get her bearings. A moment passed in silence as something by the closet door caught Lindsay's eye. She scooted off the bed and onto the floor, feet mechanically walking to the spot her eyes refused to leave. Alaina watched, the apology caught in her throat while Lindsay bent to the floor to pick up the black box. It was opened only a slit before the top was snapped shut and thrown forcefully back into its hiding place. "Lindz?" Troy merely held up a hand and squeezed her eyes shut before walking slowly out of the room, down the hall and down the stairs. Alaina watched her go, then slid off the bed herself to find what could have possibly changed her sister's demeanor so rapidly. A resurfaced wedding ring. Guilty as charged. ---- I'm really not a jealous person. But you get to be a certain age and you start to wonder about things. I've started to wonder how many more mornings I'm going to keep waking up with the aches and pains and keep pushing myself to forget they exist. How many more times I'm going to want to keep fighting my friends to assert my dominance in this sport. How many more times I'm going to stretch the boundaries of friendship to get what I want; to stay on top. I've started to wonder when someone's going to tell me to stop putting myself through the wringer and when, not if, I'll actually listen to them. When that someone will be a person I care the world about, who I'll want to start a family with. Who I'll want to be with. Who will make me want to throw my gear in a box and never touch it again. I thought I had that once. But I threw it away like I threw the reminder back in the box where I buried it. I've started to wonder if it's something I'm ever meant to have for more than a moment in time. I think of Alaina and Dan, I think of my aunt and uncle and I think of Matt and Mary and I wonder...maybe it's enough to just live vicariously through them. Even if it means forcing Matt the hard way.
