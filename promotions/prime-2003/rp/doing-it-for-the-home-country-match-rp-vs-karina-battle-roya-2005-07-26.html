Doing it for the Home Country (Match RP vs Karina, Battle Royal)

26 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/26/05 Darkness is all that is seen. Suddenly a couple of creeks are heard in the wooden floor. A door is opened and light floods into the darkness. The outline of a massive man is seen in the door and he has to duck to get into the room. He throws his keys on a table and turns on the light. Now we see who the man is. It is none other than Tom Walczak. The last that we saw of Walczak was in court facing a prison sentence of two years for drunken assault. Walczak makes his way over to the couch and turns on the television. He checks the clock to see that it’s just in time for the Ten O’clock News. “Good Evening, I’m John Cavanaugh reporting tonight with Jenny Groves.” “Alright John, later in tonight’s broadcast a vicious gang shootout, but first our Top Story. The still unknown whereabouts of PRIME co-owner Taun Pham. He was kidnapped on PRIME’s biggest event of the year COLLOSUS and has not been seen since. Pham was taken away on a GLOBETECH helicopter by a large man whose identity is unknown. Our field reporter Trish Roberts was lucky enough to get talk to some of the people who witnessed to dramatic event. We now send you to Trish who is standing outside the Pontiac Silverdome in Detroit. Trish.” “Thanks Jenny, the events have no doubt shocked the fans of this esteemed federation, but have also shocked much of the non-fans, and it has been given top-priority by the Detroit Police Department. I was lucky enough to talk to one of the security personnel that was at the event.” The camera cuts to earlier in the day still outside the Silverdome. “So you witnessed Taun Pham being carried away on Sunday night?” “Yeah it was horrible. He was a lifeless lump on the back of that beast. There was nothing we could do it just happened to fast.” “Did you have any idea who the man was?” “I definitely seen him before. I just can’t put my finger on who he was. He was scary looking though. Just business as usual didn’t even break a sweat.” “Did you by chance see who else could have possibly been on that helicopter?” “Nah it was too far away I couldn’t make nothin out.” The camera now cuts back to Trish standing outside in the darkness of the Silverdome. “That was the best that we could do Jenny. We tried to reach Taun Pham’s father Hin See for a comment, but a representative said an interview could not be set up. Back to you Jenny.” “Thanks Tri….” The television shuts off, and Walczak heads towards the kitchen. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK The thud resounds on the door. “Who is it?” “Police open up.” Walczak opens the door and two detectives walk in. Both of them pale in comparison to Walczak, and they are almost taken aback by the immense size of the man. “Can I help you?” “Uh…yeah. We’re here to investigate something that happened Sunday night. I’m detective Norris, and this is detective Williams.” “What’s that?” “I don’t know if you’ve heard, but the co-owner of PRIME wrestling, Taun Pham, was recently kidnapped.” “Yeah of course I heard.” “Well the man who kidnapped him fits your description, and I don’t think that there are too many people who fall into that category.” “Yeah, but why the hell would I kidnap the guy?” “Well we just heard that you recently started to work for the promotion and just found it to be a coincidence that a guy just like you kidnaps him.” “You should have your answer right there you just said it. He just hired me. I just got out of prison and this guy gave me a chance, so why in the fuck would I kidnap him.” “Walczak, we know your past, and we know that you’re not the cleanest guy. To put it in terms you might understand. You are a scumbag, and you could have other motives Walczak.” “You better stop talking down to me. My whole life people like you have thought you have been superior to me, and never given me a chance. Pham gave me a chance, and you think that I’d just take it and throw it down the shitter.” “I’m just saying you umm… seem to be living life pretty good for a guy who just got out of jail.” “Yeah, and what does that have to do with anything?” “Williams is this guy really this dense?” Walczak gives a disdainful snarl towards detective Norris, but holds in most of his anger, so as not to get into trouble. “Maybe he really didn’t do it?” “This guy is guilty. Walczak how in the hell did you make enough money to buy this apartment. It’s right outside Chicago, and last time I checked not to many ex-cons have enough money to afford a place like this. What about the Range Rover sitting outside this place? Did that just magically fall into your lap to?” “You think I didn’t work for this shit? I’m not just some poor schmuck who didn’t take care of his money before he went away to jail. The first time I had all my shit taken from me, and this time I was gonna make sure that it wasn’t going to be the same ” “You know what I don’t doubt that, but while I was doing my research I noticed a rather large deposit placed in your bank account shortly after COLLOSUS was over.” Walczak pauses for a little bit “Yeah, I transferred it over from my account in Gdansk.” “It just seems like it’s a little too good to be true Walczak. I don’t trust you and I’m going to be checking up on you. You had something to do with this one way or another, but we can’t book you just yet, so you can enjoy life a little bit more.” “Alright fellas have a nice day, and Ill see you guys again I assume.” “Don’t be such a wiseass Walczak. No one likes a wiseass. Just don’t get too comfortable with life out of prison because if I have my way then you will be back there fairly soon.” Without saying another word Walczak closes the door behind the two detectives, and then makes his way to his bedroom. He turns on his air conditioning, and makes his way over to the bed. Walczak pulls away his covers, but stops before he gets into the bed… What the fuck is going on? Didn’t I just get out of jail? Why am I getting involved in the same shit again? I can’t keep going in this same vicious cycle again. I just got out I can’t go back in. Why did people have to see me doing that? Millions of people saw me there with Pham on my back. I can’t just avoid it forever. You know what it doesn’t matter. I got paid to do it, and I’d do it again. These damn Americans are such hypocrites. They always look for a way to get ahead in business, and that’s exactly what I did. An opportunity came across for me to get back into the “biz” and I took it. I have nothing to feel bad about. Anyone else in my situation would have done the same thing.
