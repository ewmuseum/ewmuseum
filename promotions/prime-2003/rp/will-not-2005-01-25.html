Will Not

25 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/25/05 There are days which can, without even the slightest bit of uncertainty, be termed “good days”. The kind of day where you find an extra $20 in your pants pocket, where that test you were dreading gets cancelled because the teacher fell ill, where that asshole of a boss you have to put up with gets chewed out by their superior. The sort of day where everything seems to be going your way, when life goes out of its way to help you out, when the universe seems to revolve for you and only you. It’s safe to say that, for Tyler Lopez and Passion, this is not one of those days. Hell, this probably wouldn’t even qualify as one of those years. Five days ago. SHIT! There’s a certain level of force behind the uttering of this word that would probably stop even the most road weary trucker in his tracks. This is not the expletive of someone who’s just lost a video game or seen their car keys slip down the sewer drain. It isn’t even the swear word of choice for someone who’s just seen a vengeful ex light their personal possessions on fire. No, this is a unique kind of swear word, a rather special brand of expletive. There’s certainly anger behind it, you kinda take that for granted with a good old hearty SHIT! But it’s the kind of anger that’s got a fair amount of pain behind it. Passion is currently standing stock still in the middle of her driveway, her sneaker clad foot frozen in mid-stride. Her breathing is shallow and rapid, her eyes closed. The look on her face is speaking volumes. Tyler slams the door of the car behind him, running up to his soon to be wife, black backpack slung over his left shoulder. He catches up to her quickly, slowing down as he pulls up alongside her. He takes her left hand in his right, gently so as not to disturb her. He turns his head towards his wife and a look of infinite tenderness crosses his face. We’ve seen Lopez scowl, we’ve seen him yell, we’ve seen him bellow, we’ve seen the veins on his neck pop out so far that it wouldn’t have been much of a stretch for him to suddenly turn green and even bigger than usual. Right now, Tyler Lopez looks as though he would break if you breathed on him too hard. Lopez: Are you okay? Where have you been? Why haven’t you called? Passion slows down her breathing enough to be able to answer her husband. The dark look on her face lightens but not by much. Passion: No. Lopez: What… Passion: Not right now. We can talk about this later… Passion is trying rather desperately to sound lighthearted. She’s failing spectacularly. Lopez: You are going to have to take it easy for awhile. Just one week off your feet and you’d be good as new. I need you to stay home and communicate with me Passion. Passion: I don’t need time of… don’t you get it. Tyler: Baby, please…Right now, you are my only concern. I am gonna make sure you are all right. Passion: Promises, promises. She’s getting better. That almost sounded like a joke. Lopez: I’m not kidding. I love you too much to let anything else happen to you. Passion: I love you too, baby. Lopez: It would kill me if anything else happened to you. Passion carefully opens her eyes again, carefully turning her head to look at her fiancé’s face. There is a level of pain on his face that she has only seen a few times before…and her own face immediately melts in response, tears welling in her eyes. Despite her obvious pain, she manages a smile. Passion: Aww…look at my big, strong man. Lopez chuckles softly in response. Passion: Don’t worry, baby, I’m not going anywhere. The duo make their way slowly across the driveway, taking each step carefully. It takes them about five minutes to cross a distance that would normally take them one but neither of them seems to mind all that much. Lopez opens the front door for Passion, gingerly helping her into the house. Passion tenses slightly as she enters the house, as if she can sense some bad energy present in the house. But just as quickly, the tension ebbs away from her shoulders. Passion walks slowly over to the living room, passing by the pile of mail that has accumulated on the front table. Lopez stops, putting the backpack down on the floor. He begins quickly sorting through the mail, occasionally placing the odd envelope or two aside from the rest of the mass. With that accomplished, he heads over to join his lady. Lopez calls out to Passion in the next room. Lopez: You want anything, babe? Passion: Glass of water, please. Lopez walks over to the well-appointed kitchen, opening one of the overheard cupboards and pulling a glass out from inside it. He then opens the refrigerator door, pulling out a bottle of what looks like Evian. He unscrews the top and puts it aside, pouring water into the glass. He manages to get it roughly halfway full before the phone begins to ring. Hurriedly putting the bottle down, he heads over to answer the phone. He has it off the receiver by the third ring. Hello? Hello? Click. Lopez hangs the phone back up, not getting frustrated by the obvious crank caller, and going back to finish filling up the glass of water for Passion. Once done, he takes it over to the living room where she is currently snuggled into a large, slightly overstuffed leather armchair. Lopez hands her the glass of water, settling into the chair next to her. Passion: Thanks, babe. Who was that on the phone? Lopez: Dunno. They never said anything. Passion looks nervously. Passion: You don’t think… Lopez: No. We changed the number remember. Anyways don’t worry about it. Passion: I hope you are right. Lopez: Well, we’ll see…hey, sports is starting. The world goes white for a few moments. If pressed, they’d be able to tell you that they only remember vague fragments and snatches of the next few moments. Passion: I just wanna take a nice long bath. And then I wanna sleep for a year. Lopez walks over to Passion, giving her a kiss before walking back towards the hallway. Lopez: Have fun, babe. Passion: You ok? Lopez: Yeah, I’m just gonna go through the mail. As Passion gets gingerly on her feet, Tyler walks over to the entrance hallway. He stops briefly by the pile of mail before crouching down to the level of the black backpack on the floor. Opening it up, he withdraws a videotape from inside. Videotape in hand, Lance walks back into the living room, making sure that Passion is nowhere in sight. Before he turns to tv on he speaks. I have never once claimed you to be an inferior wrestler, Big Popa. I have never once said that I viewed you as irrelevant or as merely a “speedbump”. I do not think little of you. I believe you to be a skilled wrestler, a talented athlete and…at heart…a good man. Why you ask, bcause you recieved a PRIME contract and now it is time to prove yourself. You will not be walking out of the ring as the victor come Wednesday. I am sorry that it had to be you this week. Under any other circumstances, I would have no problem with seeing you become “The Next Big Rookie”. But after what happened a couple of weeks ago…after what Katrina and Nova tried to do…I can’t allow myself to lose. I cannot allow myself to let anyone believe that they can stop me. I cannot give anyone even the slightest sliver of hope that they can ultimately succeed in what they aim to do. And so I can’t allow you to beat me. Lopez: Does that make overconfident? Perhaps. Does that make me arrogant? Maybe? As I said earlier, I never claimed to be perfect. And no, saying what I did does not mean I think you're not a worthy competitor, that you won’t give me a hell of a challenge, that we won’t put on a hell of a match for the fans in attendance in Raleigh. Hell, Poppa…I was never expecting anything less from you because I thought we were operating on that base level of understanding at least. But with all that being said? You will not beat me. I will continue to view you in the same way I do now…a worthy adversary and a worthy man. But you will not beat me. He turns the TV back on, careful to quickly mute it. He then slides the tape into the VCR. The VCR clicks and the screen briefly turns black. As the image resolves, Lopez finds himself wondering what might be on this particular tape. It’s safe to say that what he was expecting to see was not an image of the very same room he’s now standing in. Tyler watches as his video double walks into view on-screen, hair wet, clad only in a towel. The frown on his face deepens as Passion’s video double walks onto screen. This is a moment Lopez very much recognizes. It is certainly not a moment he expected to see on tape. It’s hard to gauge what must be going through Tyler’s head right now. Which makes this a very bad time for Passion to come back into the room. Passion: Hon, have you seen the bubble bath? I could swear we just… She stops short as she catches the image on the TV screen. She looks as though she’s about to be violently ill. Passion: What. Is. That? Lopez starts, noticing Passion. Lopez: Baby… Passion: What the hell is THAT?! I know you haven’t started…wait a minute. Passion begins shaking. Lopez: Baby… Passion: They were in our HOUSE as well! Don’t tell me to calm down, don’t tell me it’s no big deal, don’t tell me everything will be okay, THEY WERE IN OUR DAMN HOUSE AGAIN!!! Tyler walks over to Passion, who is now openly crying. Saying nothing, he just takes her in his arms and holds her close to him, wrapping her sobbing, shaking body in his strong arms. There’s no telling how much time passes before Passion stops shaking. Lopez gently takes her face in his hands. Lopez: Do you trust me? Passion: You know I do. Lopez: Go pack a bag. I’m gonna take you out to dinner, you choose the place. Then we’re gonna go spend the night somewhere…you choose, I don’t care. And in the morning, I’ll take care of this. Ok? Passion just nods. Tyler kisses her gently on the forehead. She walks off unsteadily, heading in the direction of her bedroom. Tyler waits until she disappears before heading off to the kitchen. Once there, he picks up the phone and dials a familiar number. The phone is answered on the third ring. MPR Inc., this is Carmon speaking. Carmon, hi, this is Tyler Lopez. Oh, hi, Mister Lopez. How can I help you, today? Can you patch me through to Michael Normand? Sure thing. Just a moment. Hold music. This is Michael. Mike, it’s Tyler. Tyler, hi. What’s up? I need a favor. I need you to book a week at the ritziest spa that has space available. Canyon Ranch, Golden Door, whatever, I don’t care. But I need it immediately. What’s going on? I need to get Passion out of town… And as Tyler’s conversation continues, we pan over to the TV where the video is still playing. All we can see now is the back of the large leather couch and the head of Passion sticking out from the top of one end. Video Passion smiles, opening her mouth to laugh before her head disappears again beneath the shield of the back of the couch. Such a shame it couldn’t last. Cut.
