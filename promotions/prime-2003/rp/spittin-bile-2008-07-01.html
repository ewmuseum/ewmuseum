Spittin' Bile

1 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1st July NATAS (V/O): I’d be lyin’ if I said this ain’t a big deal. (FADE IN: In an otherwise empty bar, JASON NATAS, PRIME’s ANTI-SUPERSTAR, takes a lengthy, cathartic drag on a newly-lit cigarette. Sitting alone at a table, NATAS’S shaggy, dirty blonde hair is sheathed in a black bandanna, and the black t-shirt he wears adorns the logo of NYC hardcore punk band Murphy’s Law. Pursing his lips, JASON blows a long plume of smoke from his mouth, before cleansing his smoky taste buds with a quick mouthful of whatever cheap liquor has taken his fancy this evening.) NATAS: I mean, shit, this IS the Intense title after all. Lotta important dudes have held this strap. Talkin’ ‘bout Hall of Famers and World Champions, guys like Vangelus Olsig, Nova, and Killean Sirrajin. As far as your average wrestlin’ fan is concerned, that’s a whole lotta prestige right there, an’ I’m sure a dude like Bryan Dawkins or Union Jack would jump at a chance like this. In most cases, it seems traditional that the Intense Champion goes on to have a stellar career here in PRIME. Kinda makes me wonder how a punk like Troy Douglas got hold of it, then. (The ANTI-SUPERSTAR allows self-importance to curve his lips into a smirk. He pauses momentarily to scratch his calloused fingertips across his sandpaper-rough chin.) NATAS: Guess I gotta hand it to ya, Troy, you’re not doin’ too badly here. Been here, what, two, maybe three months and already you got a pretty little title wrapped around yer waist. You’ve caused somethin’ of a stir by beatin’ a Hall of Famer… hell, you’ve even headlined a broadcast of ReVolution. If I were one to care about such accomplishments, I might be attempted to be a lil’ impressed right now. But guess what? I ain’t. (NATAS shrugs lightly.) NATAS: Accomplishments and reputations never meant shit to me. I don’t care who you’ve beaten and who I HAVEN’T beaten, Troy; the only thing that matters to me is rippin’ that pretty little belt of yours from your grubby little fingers and shovin’ it right back in yer face. Intense? Kid, you don’t have a fuckin’ clue about intense. (After tapping the tip of his cancer stick against the edge of an ashtray, JASON sucks in another mouthful of toxic fumes, before turning his steely gaze back towards the camera.) NATAS: So this is the big one; your first real test as champion, your first defence. Bet you were hopin’ for somethin’ a little bit more easy-goin’, huh? Bet you probably thought you’d end up facin’ an insufferable little shitcake like Jimmy Bonafide. A nice lil’ pat in the back for ol’ Troy; warm the new champ up with a nice, easy match-up to ease him into his tenure as champion. No such like Troy, no such luck; ‘cause instead of takin’ on some lightweight, "sports entertainment" jackass, you done got yourself booked against a terror. (He cocks his head back, polishing off the drink with a final gulp.) NATAS: Way I see it, Troy, we ain’t so diff’rent, you and I. Seems like we both let out fists do the talkin’ instead of pullin’ some long-winded, self-important Xavier Kannon bullshit on everyone’s ass. If had an ounce of admiration for my fellow "professionals," Troy, maybe I’d even respect you a lil’ bit. Experience sets us apart, I guess; from what I understand, you’ve had yerself a nice, long career. Captured your fair share of gold, and put away enough bills to make for a comfy lil’ retirement fund. Bet you’ve faced all kinds of grapplers in your career, Troy; big guys, small guys, fast guys, slow guys. Bet you thought you’d faced every kinda opponent imaginable, right? Well, I’m afraid yer mistaken again, Douglas, ‘cause you just ain’t ready for the shitstorm ol’ Jase is gonna throw at ya. (JASON leans forward out of the uncomfortable wooden chair and across the table. His nostrils visibly flaring as the intensity in his voice builds, he begins to accentuate his key phrases by pointing at the camera.) NATAS: I might not sell as many t-shirts as Lindsay Troy, and I might not have Tyler Rayne’s win/loss record, but make no mistake, Troy Douglas; I’ll wipe you off the fuckin’ map. For every punch I throw, you’re gonna need to throw five. With every big move you hit, you’d be hopin’ I don’t get back up, ‘cause if I do, yer fucked. (He reclines almost as quickly as he had leaned forward, his concentration unwavering.) NATAS: So Troy Douglas… come out here, flap yer gums. Tell me I don’t matter, or I’m not a worthy contender, b’cause at the end of the day, I’m gonna tear yer fuckin’ head off. (FADE OUT.)
