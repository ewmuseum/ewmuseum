Two if by Psychic and Pizza, and Chicken.

13 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5-13 Almustafa, The Chosen and the beloved, who was a dawn unto his own day, had waited twelve years in the city of Orphalese for his ship that was to return and bear him back to the isle of his birth. And in the twelfth year, on the seveth day of Ielool, the month of reaping, he climbed the hill without the city walls and looked seaward; and he beheld his ship coming with the mist. Then the gates of his heart were flung open, and his joy flew far over the sea. And he closed his eyes and prayed in the silences of his soul. But as he descended the hill, a sadness came upon him, and he thought in he heart: How shall I go in peace and without sorrow? Nay, not without in the spirit shall I leave this city. Long were the days of pain I have spent within its walls, and long were the nights of aloneness; and who can depart from his pain and his aloneness without regret? Too many fragments of the spirit have I scattered in these streets, and too many are the children of my longing that walk naked among these hills, and I cannot withdraw from them without a burden and an ache. It is not a garment I cast off this day, but a skin that I tear with my own hands. Nor is it a thought I leave behind me, but a heart made sweet with hunger and with thirst. Yet I cannot tarry longer. The sea that calls all things unto her calls me, and I must embark. For, to say, though the hours burn in the night, is to freeze and crystallize and be bound in a mould. Fain would I take with me all that is here. But how shall I? A voice cannot carry the tongue and the lips and gave it wings. Alone must it seek the ether. And alone and without his nest shall the eagle fly across the sun. Now when he reached the foot of the hill, he turned again towards the sea, and he saw his ship approaching the harbour, and upon her prow the mariners, the men of his own land. And his soul cried out to them, and he said: Sons of my ancient mother, you riders of the tides, How often have you sailed in my dreams. And now you come in my awakening, which is my deeper dream. Ready am I to go, and my eagerness with sails fell set awaits the wind. Only another breath will I breathe in this still air, only another loving look cast backward, and then I shall stand among you, a seafarer among seafarers. And you, vast sea, sleepless mother, who alone are peace and freedom to the river and the stream, Only another winding will this stream make, only another murmur in this glade, and then shall I come to you, a boundless drop to a boundless ocean. And as he walked he saw from afar men and women leaving their fields and their vineyards and hastening towards the city gates. And he heard their voices calling his name, and shouting from field to field telling one another of the coming of his ship. And he said to himself: Shall the day of parting be the day of gathering? And shall it be said that my eve was in truth my dawn? And what shall I give unto him who has left his plough in midfurrow, or to him who has stopped the wheel of his winepress? Shall my heart become a tree heavy-laden with fruit that I may gather and give unto them? And shall my desires flow like a fountain that I may fill their cups? Am I a harp that the hand of the mighty may touch me, or a flute that his breath may pass through me? A seeker of silence am I, and what treasure have I found in silences that I may dispense with confidence? If this is my day of harvest, in what fields have I sowed the seed, and in what unremembered seasons? If this indeed be the hour in which I lift up my lantern, it is not my flame that shall burn therein. Empty and dark shall I raise my lantern, and in the guardian of the night shall fill it with oil and he shall light it also. These things he said in words. But most in his heart remained unsaid. For he himself could not speak his deeper secret. And when he entered into the city all the people came to meet him, and they were crying out to him as with one voice. And the elders of the city stood forth and said: Go not yet away from us. A noontide have you been in our twilight, and your youth has given us dreams to dream. No stranger are you among us, nor a guest, but our son and our dearly beloved. Suffer not yet our eyes to hunger for your face. And the priests and the priestesses said unto him: Let not the waves of the sea separate us now, and the years you have spent in our midst become a memory. You have walked among us a spirit, and your shadow has been a light upon our faces. Much have we loved you. But speechless was our love, and with veils has it been veiled. Yet now it cries aloud unto you, and would stand revealed before you. And ever has it been that love knows not its own depth until the hour of seperation. “The Prophet, The Coming of the Ship” by Kahlil Gibran This was a passage read often to Azala Zameer as an infant and well into his young childhood. The passage was read to him daily until the time came that he had memorized every last word. It was one of many things that oft cast a shadow over Zameer’s memories allowing him a brief moment of tunnel-vision into the depths of his soul; and it was that passage the enveloped his train of thought a moment before………. “Thith ith reary goooood!” Azala Zameer stammers through a mouthful of what one can only guess as fried chicken and mashed potatoes, with maybe a half bite of corn. Swallowing hard, most likely due to his lack of ever chewing his food a proper amount, he continues, “Anyway, Azala have big match at pay per view this week, winners get PRIME contract, and losers, losers get…..” “No more snacks” interjects Dusty half joking. With his mouth hanging agape, Azala Zameer stares blankly with horror as if though watching some terrible news on the television, giving what Dusty just said a chance to sink in. Suddenly, as if all at once, it hits Zameer like a ton of bricks. “Dusty right” Azala softly agrees. Now most men in this situation would readily attempt to find a way to remedy their quarrel within by either hitting the weights, finding a quick training partner, or watching video of their opponents. Keep in mind I said MOST MEN. Without thinking, The Snack Daddy throws a hundred dollar bill on the table and promptly removes himself and walks out the door, well, squeezes out the door is a more proper description. Dusty hastily follows in pursuit, and finds himself having to jog to keep up with his new enormous African friend. “Where are we going?” inquires Dusty through broken breaths. “Hey, slow down” Kicking at the back of Azala’s heels so Zameer’s legs cross and Azala practically trips and falls on his face, Dusty has resorted to his last option. Zameer regains his balance however and spins around, seething through his huge gold teeth. “Azala must not lose this match!” “Well where the hell are you going!” demands a frustrated and tried Dusty. Continuing forward Zameer paid no more mind to his new little buddy, and Dusty, with no sufficient answer has no choice but to follow blindly. The street lights lining the paved roads below the L train in Chicago reflect eerily off the now wet sidewalk and the reflections of the puddles along the curb are sent rippling as Zameer’s larger than average feet splash down into them. Azala’s head swings frantically from left to right as if searching for something, someone to offer him an answer and as he turns the next corner on the town’s south side he’s hit head on by a ……………………… Man in a wheel chair? Spilling over into the street a station 6 transit bus narrowly misses our favorite candy stuffed PRIME super star, and in rolling out of the way, Zameer is offered a chance to look back at the middle aged man in an electric wheelchair. “What the damn hell do ya think yer doin ya crazy bastard!” The handicapped man demands. Zameer regained his composure and without losing train of thought responds as quickly and intelligently as he could. “Azala sorry mister. Azala have match, and if Azala lose match he get no more snacks~, and if Azala get no more snacks~, then Azala, Azala just don’t know what he do.” Being wary straight away of the humongous African’s possibly defective and deteriorating mental state, the man offers only a minor insult before carrying on his way. “Ya crazy bastard!” About this time, Dusty manages to catch up to Zameer and finds him dusting himself off and stepping back to the sidewalk. “What the hell was that all about” asks Dusty once again through heavy breathing. Ignoring Dusty yet again Zameer’s head continues to frantically swivel from left to right, at one point almost appearing to rotate a complete 360 degrees. Dusty wipes away the sweat running into his eyes to ensure they were not playing a trick on him when he notices a broad smile cross Zameer’s visage. “What, what is it?” Dusty inquires, feeling somewhat like he’s playing a twisted game of twenty-one questions in which none of his questions are never being answered, and yet again, this question goes, well, unanswered. Instead, he glances up to where Zameer is staring and immediately contradicts what Azala is thinking. “No way, you gotta be fucking kidding me man!” Dusty yells trying now to physically hold Zameer back. “These places are a fucking scam!” Paying no mind, and dragging Dusty along like a small dog doing nothing more than annoying him, Zameer traipses forward and directly into….. Madam Zorn’s Psychic Readings “Fuck” is all you can here spring from Dusty’s rather dirty mouth as he’s dragged through the door. The interior of Madam Zorn’s is exactly what you would expect. Violet silk sheets are hung from the ceiling, connected to the wall to form some sort of a sultry, porn-esque teepee. A mixture of incense and oils cause an eerie fog that encircles the lamps and burn ones nostrils, which are also covered with smaller sheets for added effect. The carper below their feet is a shaggy black and a disturbing audible, twangy music can be heard as if coming from the other room, yet there is no other room visible. Zameer stands stone cold for a moment taking it all in, for to him this is something he would expect to see in a movie. “Velcome, velcome, come in von’t you” Stammers an old wrinkly lady from amidst the shadows. Silently the Big Snack Daddy steps forth, dragging his little companion with him and manages, albeit uncomfortably, to take a seat at a small round table. Upon the table sits a clear glass ball, many flickering candles, and immediately across from him, appearing from almost out of nowhere, sits Madam Zorn. “Vat is it I can do for you tonight?” Zorn asks in her obviousle fake gypsy accent. “Azala need to know, need to know his future! Azala need to know if he can keep getting snacks and stay in country. Azala need to know!” Zameer growing increasingly distraught is brought back to his senses by a well places elbow to his side from Dusty. “This is a really bad idea” the kid whispers through closed lips. Before Azala can respond to Dusty’s accusation, Madam Zorn replies, “You no believe vat I do is real young man?” “It’s not that,” Dusty replies back peddling now, “it’s just that, well, all you guys are fakes, you can’t really tell the future………… can you?” “I sense much apprehension in you young man, and through you,” Zorn turns her attention to Zameer, “I sense much concern, much, trepidation, and great worry.” “Yes!” Azala retorts. “Azala very worried about snacks!” A cold breeze rushes in through the room causing a few of the candles to blow out and ruffles the silk sheets above them. This disturbing occurrence even seems to shock the great Madam Zorn a bit as she looks warily on, as if only for effect, I guess we’ll never know. “No, I sense much concern, but it’s not about your snacks, it goes far beyond that, to a much greater ordeal in your life.” “Azala not get any free pizza from Pizza Hut either?” Zameer inquires while burying his face in his bulbous hands, “This much worse than Azala thought.” Shaking her head, Madam Zorn reaches across and grabs Zameer’s hands, one of hers in each one of his. Her tiny hands disappear inside Azala’s and her eyes snap wide open. “You, you are not who you say! There is much more to you than you reveal and this causes you great sadness. Yet, you stay, even though you may leave at any time, for you are not a prisoner in the most common definition, but, indeed a prisoner never the less. You reach out blindly into the night, hoping to grasp onto anything substantial, you scream in horrific attempts for anyone to hear you, yet you remain unheard, even to your closest peers. I, do sense that you have sullied your memories, and they are trying as mightily as possible to present themselves to you, to make you remember, remember what you have done.” “Vat?” Zameer responds, shaking his head at the fact that he now has responded in the same gypsy accent as she’s been using. “Azala just need to know that next week at this time, next week,” he repeated, “Azala be enjoying more snacks and eating more chicken.” “I don’t think you understand” states Madam Zorn whilst releasing her grip on Zameer and pulling away, looking almost afraid. “You are far more important, much greater than you realize……” “Psssssssst” Snapping her attention over to the kid, Madam Zorn is beginning to look more and more concerned. Dusty tosses Zameer the last of the Now & Laters to keep him occupied and coyly slips his small villainous hand into Zameer open pocket, just far enough to remove one of the many hundred dollar bills Azala has in case he needs a late night treat, or as he would put it, emergencies. “Look here.” Dusty states as plainly as possible and slides the hundred dollars across the table. “Just tell him that if he works hard, his wildest dreams will come true, and that next week he will be swimming in a pool of Ju Ju B’s and lemon drops, and eating all the free pizza he can handle.” “But…..” “But nothing!” Dusty replies as Azala finishes scooping the last of the sticky candy out of his gold teeth with his tongue and begins again to pay attention. “Just tell him.” Looking quickly down to the bill she’s now holding in her hand Madam Zorn simply shrugs and looks to Zameer. “I Zee in your future, pools of chocolate, with licorice slides and cotton candy beach balls,” her accent is so sporadic now one can easily tell she’s faking it, but with the money in hand, she continues, “and pizza hut cars lined up outside your house, with French fries raining upon your roof and …….” “That’s quite good” Dusty cuts her off. “Can we go now? Pahlease.” Zameer stares across to Zorn, then off into space, then back to Zorn, then to the empty candy wrapper, then back to Zorn, then nods, “So Azala have nothing to worry about?” “Nothing at all” Zorn responds still looking from one eye at the money in her hand, “absolutely nothing at all.” “Yippeeeeeee!” Zameer shouts as he jumps from his chair, knocking her table over in the process, sending her crystal ball rolling across the floor of the room. Without another word said, Zameer spins around, not once but twice on his heels, much like Michael Jackson would do in a room full of young boys to take it all in, and smiles, “Then it time for Azala and Dusty to eat!” “EAT!” Dusty exclaims. “Didn’t we just….” “This different, this better, Azala take Dusty to best place in town!” ~~~~~~~~ Flash forward to Zameer and Dusty standing in front of a Pizza Hut all you can eat buffet~~~~~~~~~~~~~ “This, is the best place in town?” Dusty asks facetiously Looking over to Zameer, as if expecting a response, he just finds the gigantic African doing the cabbage patch, circa 1988, and drooling all over himself. “I have a feeling this is the beginning of a fucked up friendship”
