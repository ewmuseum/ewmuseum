"All Games Must End" as it was MEANT TO BE SEEN! Panic erupted in the back, as word got out Hessian had taken a circular saw from the industrial crew. A man that size shouldn’t be walking around with a weapon that deadly. Except these were trying times; and he wasn’t walking. Soon enough the cameras found Hessian, pace quickening, no clichéd yelling for his nemesis but words under the breath, chanted, the saw clenched by fists that sweated with anticipation and excitable fear. Now the locker room of one Castor Strife was in view again, door open this time, and he made for it with increasing speed. All obstacles were on the cusp of removal; frustration painted over with urgency and- SMACK! Hessian blacked out immediately. There was a trip, maybe, or a stumble as he turned the corner, and his head was brought with momentous force into the very cage he sought to cut open. The details of the accident left his mind where blackness prevailed, and now: only a voice. "This isn’t good- we’re running out of time," it called. "Wake up!" His eyes opened to a blurry image, though it was becoming clearer. Before him stood Castor Strife, PRIME Universal Champion, peering at from behind steel bars. Except…something wasn’t right. Castor wasn’t on the inside, and Hessian found himself this time, a prisoner. "Drop something?" Castor said, holding up the circular saw before letting it drop. "Won’t be needing that. Your toys are far too dangerous for me." "Let me outta here you bastard. See how rough I like to play…" Hessian replied, still hazy. Castor let out a condescending laugh. "The time to hesitate is through…" he sang, quoting from that famous song. Laughing more, he continued in normal voice, "Now I set your world on fire. You remember my cast, don’t you?" "Those artsy degenerate freaks? Yeah, I remember revoking their right to fuckin’ walk," said Hessian. "Hmm. Sadly they couldn’t be here to witness this. And the boy, you remember him too? Reconstructive surgery couldn’t make him pretty like before. Now if you ask me, there’s an eclectic beauty in dismantlement…" "Glad you see it that way…" "…but I’m afraid my associate doesn’t see it that way. This boy was his treasure. Of all the ones he’s had, and he’s had them all, this one was his very favorite. HE LOVED HIM, Hessian. And you took that away. Now, we’ll make this things right." Music started in the background: "The Crying Game" by Boy George From the doorway crept a large, muscular man in a sparkling golden dress. It was TOM ADLER, determined and barefoot, French whore’s makeup caked over his face. "It’s true that I can grant dreams; but I can also grant nightmares. Since you took his boy from him, it was Mr. Adler’s sincerest wish that I deliver you to him, in his place. From this moment on," Castor said as Adler licked his lips at the sight of the big man, "you belong to HIM. Enjoy!" And with that, the champion departed. Adler approached Hessian’s caged prison with a sexual ferocity seen only in the red light districts of Bangkok. Adler began to sing the lyrics, loudly, "I KNOW ALL…THERRRRRE IS TO KNOW ABOUT THE CRYYYYIIIING GAME!" "GET ME OUT OF HERE!" yelled Hessian. Swinging his golden hips side to side, Adler pulled up his dress to expose a gray, spotted phallus. "I’VE HAD MY SHARE…OF THE CRYYYYYIIIIING GAAAAAAME!" he sang. Hessian began to shake the cage. His attempts to escape only aroused Adler more, but the diva kept his wits about him. After all: how does one urinate with a hard-on? Horrified at the prospect of being pissed on by another person, let alone another man, let alone Tom Adler…Hessian did all he could to break free. It was no use. The golden shower ran from Adler’s half-swollen member into the cage and onto Hessian’s wrestling tights. "FIRST THERE ARE KISSES…" he continued to sing. "HELP!" screamed Hessian. "THEN-THERE-ARE…SIGHS…" "I’m gonna kill you when I get out of here. Do you understand that?" Hessian said. "Just you hush yourself now," Adler said placing his finger to his lips. "SHHHH. Adler-plex" he whispered, smiling deviously. "ONE DAY SOON, I’M GONNA TELL THE MOON ABOUT THE CRYYYYYING GAME." Walking by the locker room door is The First. He stops, looks in, raises his eyebrows, and quickly moves on. The warm piss was being showered on him with indiscretion, but Hessian continued to struggle until finally...some luck. The top of the cage door began to come off his hinges, and Hessian kicked furiously. Within moments, the door was off his hinges. "Uh oh," said Adler. Immediately, Hessian grabbed the circular saw from the ground. Before he could lunge at Adler and do his worst, a familiar voice yelled from the back. "SON OF A BITCH! WHERE’S MY WALLET!" It was none other than Charlie Sheen, who stumbled in naked. "THINK I CAN’T COUNT? MY WALLETS BEEN STOLEN AND MY MONEY INSIDE IT! YOU TOLD ME FIVE HUNDRED AN HOUR, NOT HUNDRED PER LOAD!" Hessian looked at Sheen, then looked at Adler. Without another second of deliberation, he brought the saw blade to Adler’s penis, severing it with ease. "URRAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!" Adler dropped to his knees in the Platoon pose. "NOOOOOOOO! WHAT DID YOU DO TO HER? SHE WAS MY FAVORITE!" yelled Charlie Sheen. "YOU DON’T FIND ‘EM LIKE THAT IN A.C. ANYMORE!" Hessian swatted Sheen aside and proceeded out of the champion’s locker room. With the camera in his face, Hessian spoke a clear message: "Maybe we’ll need that casket sooner than later." He shoved the camera aside, and the scene faded to static.
