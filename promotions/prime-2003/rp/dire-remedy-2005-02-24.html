Dire Remedy

24 February 2005

Date: 2/24/2005 Dire RemedyChapter IFebruary 24th, 2005 To be completely honest with you, It's comforting. It's comforting to be involved an immense battlefield where only one individual could claim that glorious victory. It's comforting to know that I was fighting along side the rest of the pack. It's comforting to be able to navigate these feelings and emotions. These controls being one of the many things I've suddenly gotten back from the diseased captor that wounded me beyond all imagination. I entered this federation believing that it was a game. This isn't a game. This isn't a fun passtime. This is business and an ass-busting job. People tend to enjoy seeing us fall on our skulls, fracture bones in our bodies and shed our own blood onto the ring mat. People enjoy seeing us internally bleed, pull ligaments in our necks and get betrayed all at the same time. It's the aggression from the PRIME fans that fuels the fire and the need for this kind of entertainment, right? Or is it the quiet, sequestered concerns behind the curtain that keeps the adolescent, blooming crowd on its toes and roaring? It's puzzling. It's strange. It's tiresome. The frozen perimeters of New England have really uncovered my eyes. Not so much for the need of high-dosages of rest, but for the comfort that the cold sends through weak bones and fragile mindsets. A white-out, snow-front was on its way and I was perched on a stool, looking outside the off-set window in my master bedroom. Flurries were occupying the cloudy skies and marking their spots on the lukewarm concrete. Freakishly panicked residents of Boston fled to local markets to gather flabs of meat, flats of eggs, boxes of canned milk and countless jugs of water. I never understood the hysteria. A snow storm isn't the end of the world. The war of elements would, in fact, be the end. A wall of firey damnation would, in fact, be the end. Everything is a dilemma suddenly. Shambles of disorganization are now lying around my ankles and it's almost as if I can't reach down far enough to push the rusted foolery away from me. I have this unintentional sense of incompentancy puddling around me and I'm slowly sinking into it. So how I do I leap away from this pit of fire; the same pit of fire that I jumped from approxamatley a month ago. My associates of PRIME have all left me in the middle of the ocean. Holding my hands up in surrender to the current. As I slowly allow the open waters to determine my every move. The stratosphere flirting towers that seem to bully and cover most air space around my condominium. I can see men working in their respected offices; phone at ear and backend of pencils being grinded by their teeth. There is a sense of clarity around this part of Boston. It seems to fight off the ugly, sewer-dwelling side of this city. My face, free of colorful designed mask paint. My system, free of deadly, chemical toxins. Something that I can rightfully and honestly state that I'm heavily addicted to. I could've sworn my body was cleansed; tyrants, the likes of my father tried to slay me; tyrants, the likes of my father killing himself. A doctor who jots down my thought patterns and hands out medication that he's observed and tested out just isn't enough anymore. I don't know what's enough anymore. The Dark Age has brought me back to my original state. Boda and Emilio Rage have both walked away from what was promising. I won't allow myself to be succumbed by thin-skinned bastards like that. People who notice trouble up ahead and decide that it's better to exit through the hatchway of your own boat rather than go down with it. I'm not here to be someone's voodoo-doll. People aren't going to continuously poke pins into my sides. I am as much a human being as most are and I am here to do a job. I go out there and waste every ounce of energy on hopeless saps who are doing this to be 'something'. I'm doing it for a paycheck and that's it. I joined The Enlightenment to show my respect for those taking back PRIME from The Dark Age and that has suddenly stopped. Let's go after Dark Age individually. Let's plan this out as more of a gauntlet rather than a war of good and evil? What? Ignorance is bliss and it's starting to glaze over former Enlightenment members. Moreso on one than the other. Leaving the window spot and now sitting in a chair, watching television and looking at several 'tweaks' nested on the end-table just a couple feet away from the recently installed king-sized, crimson-patterned bed. Drowsy with a chronic headache that had the comparable effect of a hammer continuously banging against the backside of your head, had seemingly dragged Ozric down to a state of obliteration. His eyelids were halfway shut as he shifted his head toward the drugs once more. He could feel a whisper crawling into his ear and exiting the other. Continuously doing so, Ozric rested his head against the support of his hand; closing his eyes and letting a queue of dreams filter in. I'm here now, by your side. Hush now little child, it's time to say goodnight. Behold my son, reach for the vision that fills your mind, just let go and run. Ozric's eye opened as he sat up. He looked outside and noticed that the sky was dark. The chair he was sitting on was gone and was replaced with a bright-red bean-bag chair. He turned his head and realized the pills were gone. They were replaced with his wallet, a pair of keys and two plastic-cards sponsoring clubs. The phone that was once resting against the wall on the other side of his bed was awkwardly and oddly ripped out of its socket and smashed into pieces near the television set. Ozric whimpered out a giggle as he rose to his feet only to trip through his own vortex of dizziness and knock him back down. His condo looked like a run down shed that some train-hopper often lived in. He held out his hand and gazed at as streaks of his skin color slowly tagged along as he whisked his hand around. He was tweaked out of his mind and the gitterishly suspicious Ozric slowly realized this. There's a strange machine staring me. The machine consists of two heads, two egos and enough glitzy wretch to keep me entertain for a lifetime. What can this machine produce? A bang at his door startled Ozric, but it didn't seem to change anything. A gigantic, two-headed machine was in the center of this run-down shack he was sitting in. He wedged past the machine as the two-headed monster continued to eyeball him. He got to his feet and looked through the peep-hole. Two young gentlemen could be seen, wearing star-trek spandex suits and holding the 'Prosperity' sign up towards the peep hole. Mortimer shook his head and became confused. One side of him knew them and the other side seemed to realized how uncanny this was all unfolding to be. 'Who's there?' Ozric kept close guard of the door as the high poured onto him like nothing he ever felt. Various warm sensations kept him collective. He could feel like thumps of non-existant warm water splashing against the upper portion of his neck. It was limited as thoughts of Dani Furher eclipsed past him like a high-speed jet hovering above a helpless town that's be scarfed up by brush fires. There's anger howling deep inside of him and he can feel his fingernails gripping into the flesh of Furher. He gathered his natural senses and questioned the knock, again. 'I said' wailed Ozric, who's voice grew to enormous decibals heights. 'Who's there?!' Temptation, drugs and anticipation didn't mix inside of Ozric. It put him on egde and made him get incredibly jumpy. Looking to find the answers in all the wrong places while frantically waiting for them to come to him. Of course, by the time he'd find the answers the whole reason behind looking for them in the first place would mean nothing. Fortunately, this drug kept Oz on his toes; fully aware of everything. Including his match against two back-stabbing critters that seem to walk the Earth with one mission on their minds: To never have enough enemies. 'It's Morky and Otis! Open the door!' shouted a young male from behind the door. Strangely, Ozric didn't know anybody by the names of Morky and Otis. In fact, he didn't know a single soul Boston. He came here to sight-see and bought a condo in the process. He wasn't a sucker for coffee shops, movies or event-groups. These two men were simply nobody's when it came Oz. Yet, for some odd reason he felt obliged to open the door and greet them. He turned his head and noticed that the strange machine was still sitting in the center of the room. Its two heads protruding out from top with a long, bendable neck holding them in place. He noticed the two heads were, in fact, those of Dani Furher's and Big Poppa's. Strangely so, he ignored it and twisted the door knob, opening the door and letting the two Star Trek dressed men enter. 'All right, guys! We need three cameras near the window, one aerial camera in the middle of the room and two corner cameras ... and make sure they're all accessable via remote control!' yelled Mork who could've passed as Spock's body-double, as he walked into the room strolling a cart of cameras behind him. Six other men wearing shirts with white lettering saying: STAGE CREW followed Morky and Otis into the room. Whilst, Ozric shifted his head back and forth as he stared at each individual who came into his house. He swallowed hard as he started to walk over to the condo entrance. Peeking his head, Ozric suddenly gets smacked in the head with a microphone as two other crew members enter. Ozric didn't understand it at all. Frankly, he was getting nervous and frightened. Spun out on drugs and visualizing your house being destroyed and taken over by a team of Hollywood crew dwellers would definitely do that to you. 'Excuse me guys but, I'm not sure what's going on here. You caught me at a .. uh, bad time.' exclaimed Ozric as he held his index finger up in an attempt to catch somebody's attention. A super-mario looking crew member bumped into the backside of Ozric while carrying a step ladder into his room. 'This is YOUR show, Oz! You've been chosen out of one-hundred thousand randomnly picked individuals and you're getting a... TWENTY MINUTE SEGMENT ON TELEVISION! Are you excited? Can you feel my excitement? I CAN SURE FEEL YOURS!' hollered the worker as Ozric took a few steps back. His eyes open wide as a spacious smirk came over his face. He noticed that the cameras were all set up now and the lighting was just being finished up. 20 minute segment on television? There was only one question that could be forced out of Ozric's chapped lips. 'Wait, why? What's the sense of putting somebody on television when that 'somebody' never asked to be on?' questioned Ozric as the Super-Mario worker clasped his stubby hands together and smiled. 'That's the thing, Mr. Mortimer. You did, in fact, sign up for this! It's in the contractual agreement you signed!' Ozric quickly objected. 'Contractual agreement?! I signed a contract?' The worker nodded and patted Ozric's shoulder. 'Yes you did, Mr. Mortimer. Twenty minutes of air-time is now yours! Any other questions?' Ozric thought about it for a moment as he noticed the contract hanging out of the right pocket of the worker. He looked down at it and then noticed that they were breaking the windows of the condo. Pondering raising his voice, he shot it down as the effort wouldn't of been worth it. They were broke already. 'Yes, can I rip that contractual agreement? Or somehow void it? I'm not much of a social person and if I ever signed anything it was either forcefully done or I was drugged.' explained Ozric. It just occured to him, after that speech, that he probably was drugged. The problem being that he was the perpetraitor! He drugged himself up, and went prowling for some kind of strange activity. Hence, the television segment. The more Ozric thought about it, the more he realized that airtime may, in fact, be brilliant. He shifted his attention back towards the machine which now was being towered over by a wooden statue of Nova! It all just didn't make any sense and it forced Ozric to mentally trip even more. This trip had 'salvia' written all over it and he couldn't help but laugh. 'Mr. Mortimer, it's show time!' yelled Otis, presumably the director of this event, as he was sitting on a folding chair with a loudspeaker and a long 'Kool' ciggarette in his mouth. The excessiveness of the situation was enough to force Ozric to shrug and shake his head as he walked over to where the cameras were pointing at. Which was directly in front of the bed; just a couple feet in front of the two-headed machine and wooden Nova. 'All right, All right! Stop yer' yappin'! We start the segment as soon as a shooting star crosses the Boston skyline that is visible through the left shattered window! It should be arriving in twenty-seconds!' yelled Otis who seemed to be elevated in clouds that even Ozric never saw. The shock treatment was enough to force Ozric sweat off a good portion of the high prowl that he was on. The lights that were now shining on him felt like rays of the sun, shooting bullets of heat onto his body. The cameras feigned him to become un-easy and scattered in thought as a large clock lowered down from the ceiling and read off the twenty-second wait, which was already down to ten-seconds. 'Can we stop the clock and take it from the top? My palms are dripping with sweat and I've got cottonmouth.' requested Ozric who didn't get a reply, nor did he even get a flinch as he suddenly realized that time had stopped around him. The clock was frozen at one-second and everyone seemed to have paused in mid-motion. Ozric hearing a scourging sound, as if something was caught on fire. He turned and looked at the window as a bright light quickly passed by. The clock hit zero and everything went back to normal. Ozric blacked out and returned again standing in front of an American Flag with a cowboy hat on and a dark-red trenchcoat covering his body. The national anthem started to hum in the background as a microphone crept against his lips; a breif, awkward smile came across his face. Ozric wasn't himself. He couldn't control his mouth. The world felt like a simulation that he was viewing behind a plate of glass. 'Isn't the world unfair? Isn't the world just like some whore who get on the streets for a night? You pay them to do what you want them to do and then it's over before you know it. It may not be as easily comparible to the world as other things are, but it definitely proves the point. We are a nation KILLING for resources! I am a human being KILLING for a paycheck. I'm out there on the holy grail of the wrestling circuit putting my body on the line for a check stub. Not for a some two-timing slut who I personally love and have NO IDEA she's banging two other guys behind my back. Not for trip to the Carribean where I'll end up being eaten by a shark after a rare scuba-diving incident where the boat leaves me stranded in the middle of the ocean. It's not for the so-called fans who show me their thumbs raised high and their appreciation JUST BECAUSE I got away from Dark Age's grip, because they're fake and with one wrong move comes an arena load of scorn, ex-fans who treat you as fish bait while you wander down to the ring, being hit by bottles and other snack-bar schrapnel. It's for the money and it's for respect!' A strange chant from the mock-audience of crew-workers stirs Ozric's excitement as he jumps up and raises his arms high. He lifts his cowboy hat off and throws it across the room. 'I'm not here to be ridiculed and made fun of. I'm not here to be the Dark Age's punching dummy. I'm not here to be left alone by my own friends who have other agendas to deal with. We had an agenda, Boda. We had an agenda, Rage. You both seem to be blinded by title defenses and title reigns and the God-damn glory hole of wrestling. What happened to people who could trust in this federation? Oh I forgot, YOU CAN'T TRUST PEOPLE. They'll either kick you straight in the ass when you're not looking, or they'll up and leave you in a storm cloud of dust where you're wondering what happened and how it happened and what went wrong for all this to merge into a disasterous point where the resistance suddenly implodes at the knees. Furher.. Big Poppa.. This has gotten way more personal than I thought it would. See, Nova is a man who shoots for his dreams and ... and ... and.. latches on to those dreams. I am similar in the sense that I am looking to destroy both of you. I'm looking to be the one that stops both of your marks of sin. You've disapointed me, Furher. I expected a lot more from you. I expected you to be the superstar. The one that people seem to love, no matter what happened. You failed. You became a lackey for that cynical son-of-a-bitch, Vampir, and now you're nothing but a follower, living in the shadow of those who are so much better than you are. ..And another thin---' Suddenly, a loud 'CUT' could be heard from Otis as everything stopped. The cameras started to roll out of the condo, as did the wooden Nova, the american flag background and lighting. Confused and bedazzled from it all, Ozric noticed that the machine was smoking and split in half. Everyone was suddenly gone, and it seemed as if normality was back in the rear-view mirror. Drugs appeared to be Ozric's enemy when situations like this came into play. Situations that he just couldn't control due to his tweaking of speed. He thankful of two things at this point. * The television segment was over, or at least he thought it was over. *He didn't have anymore 'tweaks' on him. As Ozric was about to sit down on his bed, he noticed a small piece of paper on the floor. He picked it up and giggled. '..Well, almost perfect.' A check for three-hundred dollars signed by an unknown television company with a little note on the back saying: 'For the window.' Fin
