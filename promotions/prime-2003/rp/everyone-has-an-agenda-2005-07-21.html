Everyone has an agenda

21 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/21/05 The time is slowly ticking away toward the biggest night of your life. Every single hour, minute, second, hell every moment drags by as you prepare for the night when you just might not be the top dog. You're a champion, you always have been, but the gold belt with the shiny metal plate on front with your name etched into it serves as further proof that you're the best. Those titles that Baltasar and you fought so hard to get are now possibly only a few short days away from no longer being yours. Every single team that PRIME has signed lately has the chance to make Colossus be their moment in the spotlight. Fourteen other men, all great in their own rights, are going to walk into Colossus with the sole focus of dethroning the current cream of the crop. Losing isn't even an option in any of their minds and they are all equally confident and skilled, enough to be the ones to snatch that gold right off your shoulder. The time you've put in, the beatings you've taken, the blood you've spilt, the muscles you've pushed to their limits don't mean anything to these men who are hungry. Hungry for the world to point at them while saying that they are the best team the world of professional wrestling has to offer. It is a title that you've proudly held, along with Baltasar, for nearly two months to this point and one that you'd rather not give away at this time. The problem is that the choice isn't up to you anymore. This match up isn't designed to crown the best tag team in PRIME, with the honor of the titles, but to crown the team that can simply be the most lucky and walk out of a structure, that is meant to break bones, cut flesh, and shorten careers, with the most falls. This type of environment can lend itself to being the complete advantage of everyone in the match, except the champions. You're a target, as you've been since you won that shiny gold strap, but in the Fallen Halo you become THE target. In the Fallen Halo it will be nothing for five or six guys to team up and take out the champions of the world. Why not? It makes for the most logical game plan if you're going in to become the best team in the world it would only make sense to take out the men who currently hold that status. When you're the biggest dogs in the fight it isn't hard to see the reason behind being the ones that everyone feels the need to remove from the equation. Take out Los Diablos equals far greater chance of becoming the PRIME Tag Team Champions of the World. You know people look and start to think that you've had an up and down career in PRIME, and the reality is that you have, but when it has come to winning with the pressure and gold on the line you've been able to step it up. Team Zambo was supposed to take the gold away from you and be walking into Colossus with your titles. They were suppose to be the ones that put Los Diablos in their places, moving them down a peg and proving to the world that you're nothing more than a flash in the pan. They were wrong, just like the rest of the world. Stepping into the Fallen Halo, however, is a different beast entirely. Being locked in a cage like a bunch of animals is a recipe for disaster. People are going to get banged up, bones are more than likely going to be broken, and people are going to bleed. There is nothing that can be done about the simple truths of the Fallen Halo as it was designed to do nothing more than house pain. Walking in has no champion’s advantage, as the simple rules of the match were put in place to screw you over and take the titles away from you in the first place. You might record ten pin falls or submissions over your opponents, and never be pinned or forced to submit even once, yet if another set of those fourteen men can garner eleven decisions they are going to walk out of the match with the titles. You walk in the champions, having had to pin the former champions for the honor, yet now you could walk out of Colossus without your straps and you won't even have to actually be beaten by anyone. There is no champion’s advantage in this match at all but you do have one advantage over all these other teams. That one thing that absolutely sets you apart from all the rest in the match up is going to be needed to help you through the match up. Everyone walking in has the heart, desire, drive, and need to win but not all of them have the one important factor that just might be the difference in whether you walk out as the champions or not. Experience. It is on your side this time. You've been inside this demonic structure before and you know what it is all about. You've been thrown all around the inside of it; hell you are one of the reasons that it is now the Fallen Halo and no longer of the dual kind. The experience you've gained being in a made house like this has given you an edge that can't be explained, can't be given, and can't be taught. You simply have to live the moment and take part in the mayhem in order to understand what it means to be locked inside the structure. Your status, you’re meaning, your gold is all on the line in this one and you simply can't slip up. A win in the Fallen Halo cements you as the best team, by far, in the history of the PRIME federation. Winning over all the talent that fills the PRIME tag division, at one time, is your stepping stone to immortality. You know that when this tag team ride is over that your career is as well. Tag team wrestling is all you've ever known and the only thing you have the proper skill to succeed in. One day your partner is going to move on and continue to have this type of glory in the individual ranks, but for you this is it. Colossus is your proving ground, your legacy, and your immortality. Seizing that immortality is going to be totally up to you...and that little element of luck. 'You have got to find that edge Angelus. You need to have that motivation, the drive, that something that makes you tick and bring it every single time you step into the ring. Frankly you simply don't have that every time you step into the ring and at Colossus you're going to need it if Los Diablos is going to walk out of the match up with the belts.' Enrique preaches to you as he ties the string of his black Nike Cross trainers. He has been working you both over time these last two weeks in preparing you for the rigors of thirty minutes locked inside of Hell itself. 'I agree completely senor. The problem is I simply don't have that natural drive to be the best that Baltasar does. I like being the best, I give my all, but the desire, the need, the...how do you say...dying to have it all attitudes for wrestling just isn't something I possess essa. It is unfortunate but it is a true fact of my nature. Wrestling just isn't in my blood the way it is for Baltasar, it is simply just something I'm really good at.' You reply with a shrug of your wide shoulders and sigh just a bit. 'I know that my dear friend. I'm not asking you to love the business like it is the love of your life, or even to treat it as your true passion. Baltasar has always been the one who clearly lived for this sport while you simply did it for the money. No one can fault you for that cause it is something you're great at, but he is going to need you to come into the Fallen Halo with that killer attitude. With the desire to walk out as the champions written in your every movement, you’re every breath; with ever beat of your heart. The desire to beat out fourteen other men has to be the most important thing on your mind or you'll be no good to Baltasar in that cage.' Enrique replies with absolute honesty. He knows it may not be what you were looking to hear but it's the truth none the less. 'Which is my problem at this very moment Senor Morales, I can't seem to get my drive to kick into gear. I'm walking around this week with no motivation, no desire, and no will to make sure I don't let my partner down. It just seems to me that it won't matter anyway as no one is going to notice if I help Baltasar retain those titles or not anyway.' You reply with a heavy heart and a very deep exhales of breath. This is a feeling that has been plaguing you for months but you are finally starting to open up about it. 'Why would you think something like that? What do you think you've been doing by holding onto those titles for nearly two months now? What do you think beating Team Zambo was all about two weeks ago? Where is that attitude coming from?' Enrique asks you with a great deal of shock, and of course concern, as he looks at you like you might just be mentally challenged or something. 'I can tell by simply looking around. We took the titles off of Angelo Deville and Justin Williamson and did anyone take notice? No they were all to busy talking about all the 'new blood' that Hin See managed to bring into PRIME. No talk about how Los Diablos had finally conquered that mountain they had been climbing, it was all about what PWA or Civil D was going to do now that they had signed. Then we beat the team that was suppose to be the greatest thing since sliced bread, when we defeated Team Zambo, and yet no on noticed. All they can talk about is how all these other great teams are going to walk into Colossus and how one of them will be walking out the champions. No one even bothers to notice that we actually are walking in as the champions because it's like we aren't even here anyway.' You tell him with a whole lot of built up emotion. The anger that has been building inside you finally starting to boil over and explode out of you like it has needed to for quite some time now. 'The fact is Enrique that no matter what we do we're not going to get any respect for what we've done. We'll walk into Colossus with the titles and even if we walk out of the match with them no one is going to notice. All the talk will shit to whatever gringo in the match through his body around like a pinball, or how Angelo Deville and Karina tore the house down, or maybe even about how everyone should be patting Brandon Youngblood and Nova on the back for beating the hell out of one another. Nothing that Los Diablos does ever gets noticed cause no one gives a shit about a couple of Mexicans in masks who are trying to bring honor and respect to their country by doing something other than being a fucking human high spot reel.' You shout out as your face turns even redder with anger by the moment. The heat coming off your facial skin would almost fry an egg if you were inclined to compare it to such things. You know that you shouldn't care about what others think, believe, or feel but it still burns you up. Everyone wants respect and to be noticed for his or her talent and what they accomplish; but not everyone gets the recognition and respect that they deserve. Enrique walks over and puts his hand on your shoulder and nods with understanding written all over his face. His dark brown eyes seem to be filled with a storm of mixed emotions as he searches for the right words to try and make you feel better about a situation that really seems to be sucking the heart and soul out of you. Finally he pats you on the back and sighs deeply, letting out a lot of breath as he waits for as long as possible before having to try and speak again. 'Angelus you're a wonderful athlete and one half of the best tag team in the world right now. You're never going to get the respect you deserve, nor is Baltasar, in this world for a couple of reasons. Number one you are not out there kissing babies and making people love you for what you do, and you probably won't ever be able to do that anyway. Secondly you guys are just two guys who like to beat the hell out of people and it isn't about those catchy little phrases the fans can chant along with like it is for so many others. You guys aren't out there flipping over the top rope, splashing into piles of people, and acting like fools for the gringo masses. As long as you continue to respect yourselves, and your country, too much to do those things then you won't ever find people giving a crap about you. But really why does that matter anyway? If you know, in your heart and soul, you’re one of the best that PRIME has to offer then why does it matter if any of those gringo tools in the audience, or your fellow wrestlers, are honest enough to express the fact that you are one of the best?” He asks you as he slumps back down into his chair on the near side of his oak desk. 'Senor Morales if I knew the answer to those questions then I don't think this would be an issue. For some reason, though, I can't seem to shake the feeling that we don't matter, and never will matte, to the whole of PRIME or what it is able to achieve and accomplish in this industry. Maybe it is time that I start thinking about moving myself out of the picture and allows Baltasar to continue on his way where he might be able to get the slightest amount of notice that he deserves.' Your words trail off as you turn and look out the large window of the office, across your yard, to the large track and training gear that covers the back corner of your very large yard. Being home always makes training, during moments of this type of doubt, far easier than being on the road for it. 'Angelus we've got work to do and you've got to clear you head of this none sense. I don't know how you're going to do that but we'll think of something. Now let us get out there and hit that track for our run, shall we?' He asks with a smile as he starts toward the door. You sit there, for another lingering moment, before letting out one more deep breath and then getting up and heading out to join him. The doubt will go away, it always does, but it is just a matter of how quickly you can bury it and get on with your career. 'What are we going to do essa? We can't have you continuing to team with a guy whose head isn't into it anymore. What are we going to do about Angelus not being able to get his head straight?' Enrique pleads with you. He has been raging on you about this for the better part of an hour now as you've simply ignored him to think about the situation. 'I'm thinking about that very thing right now Senor. I'm not really sure at the moment what to think about this whole situation.' You finally reply as you rub your temples with your index fingers. The stress of being a champion, the loss of focus by your partner, and the yelling of Enrique is beginning to get to you. 'We don't have a lot of time to drag our feet in thought here hermano. We've got a very serious situation brewing here that could cost us the belts.' Enrique responds with anger. Not anger toward you but toward the situation at hand. Things have been tense like this every since Hin See brought in the new crop of talent to the PRIME tag division. Now everything has become about how great PRIME is going to be with Civil D or PWA or Teh PWN leading the charge of the tag division. How quickly everyone has forgotten that you are part of the big dogs that have been here since before there was a division. How quickly the fact that you didn't pack it in and find a new federation, with more competition in their tag division, is one of those things that are forgotten as quick as the ink can dry on the contracts. When the owner can sign a few 'big names' to his roster all they think about is how to promote the old and forget the new that they were trying to create for themselves just a few moments before. Loyalty and dedication don’t mean much in the world of pro wrestling when there is bigger things to consider like bragging rights and money when you can say you control the division with all the big names from two or three years ago. “The fact is this isn’t a situation that can be easily fixed by a simple plan or set of fancy words, Enrique. Angelus is feeling the strain of being a champion that doesn't mean jack to the company. Hin See doesn't see dollar signs in Los Diablos the way he does in PWA, Civil D, or Team Zambo. We're just the guys who go in and get the job done when it means something, and they are the teams that everyone wants to see and get behind. This is a situation that we'll always be in if we don't step it up another notch and take everyone by the throat and force them to notice us. We need that extra push and that extra something that makes us more and more marketable to the company if we're ever going to be noticed the way Angelus is hoping for.' You respond, almost as an after thought, as if this whole situation doesn't really faze you. 'In all honesty I've seen this attitude coming for a while now. Angelus has always said this business is about the money and fame for him, so when he isn't getting the attention he believes we should get then his drive begins to fade.' 'This is a fine time to have that kind of attitude show up though essa. We don't need him worrying about recognition the week of your biggest match to date. There is a time and place for all things and this certainly isn't the time or place for this to become an issue.' Enrique responds almost as if he is pleading with you to do something about it like you’re all the sudden the master of the world or something. 'We may not need him doing it but there isn't going to be anything that we can do about it. Angelus will be fine and he'll come into the match at one hundred percent. If I start worrying about what he isn't doing then we are going to be in even worse trouble. You just need to worry about focusing yourself and making sure you keep him busting his ass in training between now and then. Everything else is going to work itself out.' You reply with a smile crossing your lips. You believe in your heart and soul that your boy is going to be there full force for you at the Pay-Per-View, even if there is cause to be a tad alarmed. 'Well in that case I do have some other business I need to take care of. I'll be back in a couple of hours because I need to clear my head and think.' Enrique tells you as he heads toward the door to leave. Once he walks out of the door and leaves you alone in the house, to think, to collect your thoughts and feelings you realize that you’re now worried too. For the first time in your career, of teaming with Angelus, you are actually thinking that there might be something to worry about. Something to fear and have constantly on the back of your mind during the Fallen Halo match up isn't exactly what you need to have your teaming with Angelus mean to you. With the titles on the line and your back against the wall you don't need to be sitting here wondering if your partner has his head screwed on right and is going to have your back. 'Well at least things couldn't get any worse right now.' You say to yourself as you push yourself up off the couch and head out the door, across your yard, and back to the training grounds to work off some of this frustration and ill thinking. The time to get your head and body in the right place is here and you don't have an extra moment to spare on thinking about situations that you have no control over. 'What should I do Jorge?' You ask your long time friend and confidant. He has been the man to help you through many a tight decision and he is the guy you always go to when you need advice on the worst of situations. “So let me get this whole thing straight in my mind. Angelus is having doubt about himself and the team’s place in PRIME; Baltasar is behaving as if there is nothing to worry about toward the whole situation. On top of all that you have signed Jacob Cross to a deal with PRIME that begins after Colossus and you’ve not told either of them yet? Are you smoking dope again Enrique?' He asks with a stern look on his face. The lack of a smile proves to you that he is dead serious and obviously thinks you might be smoking the doob again as a way to cope with the stress. 'No you damn fool! You know damn well I haven't smoked that stuff in seven years and I don't plan to start again now. This is a real mess I've gotten myself into here, essa, and I really don't know what I'm going to do to fix it.' You reply with a great deal of emotion in your tone. Clearly this whole situation isn't exactly going the way you had envisioned it. 'You've got to come clean with the two of them and tell them before it is too late. If Cross just walks in the ReVolution after Colossus, and they aren't expecting it, then you are going to have a far worse situation on your hands. You could be looking at a walk out by your boys if you surprise them in such a manner.' Jorge replies with a shake of his head. You know that he is disappointed in you for even getting yourself into this position but you can't seem to figure out how to stay out of them. For a long moment you simply watch the road in front of you as you drive down the highway, neither of you bothering to speak, as you both think about the situation and what needs to be done to correct it. Finally after several moments of silence you simply can't take it anymore. Not surprising since you've never been good with the whole quiet scene. 'I've got to think of something. I can't just say, hey Jacob is part of the team now cause I owe him, enjoy. They will look at me like I'm the biggest idiot to ever make my way north of the Mexico border. There has to be a better reason for him being with us then simply my screwing up and making a mistake. What could I do to make it out like I'm not exactly the bad guy here?' You ask as you sit further back into the driver seat and try to get more comfortable for the drive to Jacob's house. Today is the day when all things are decided, complete, and ready to go with him coming in after Colossus and being part of the movement. Deciding what to do for the explanation between now and when you arrive at his house is all the time you have left. 'Good question Senor. This is a touchy situation. You're going to need a miracle from God to get you out of this jam I'll bet.' Jorge replies almost shy like. The lack of ideas that he has for you seem to almost embarrass him but your face is all lit up. A plan has come across your devious little mind and there is nothing better than when you can foot the blame on one of your own mistakes, off on someone else. An act of God why hadn't you thought of that before? This is perfect and you smile wide as you turn your head to look over toward Jorge. 'My dear friend once again you prove yourself to be the best man for giving advice that I know. You are quite simply a genius.' You exclaim as you hit the gas and start speeding faster toward the Cross home. 'I am? What did I do?' He asks as you continue to look straight ahead, your destination and planning now complete, and you simply smirk as you continue to drive. 'You gave me an act of God.' You sit there in stunned silence as you listen to the explanation that your new partner is feeding you. Being a long time business man, and having had to do to having a child at a young age and coming from parents who were successful in the business world, you can smell a load of bullshit that someone is trying to feed you from a mile away. You sit there, in your business suit, and simply stare into the bright brown eyes of the manager to Los Diablos as he excitedly tells you about the new developments of your deal with him. 'So you see Mr. Cross everything is all set as long as you agree to be the money behind the RISP. Hoyt doesn't know you, or owe you anything, so your membership into Imperium depends on this deal.' Enrique states as matter of fact like as he possibly can. You can tell that he isn't new to trying to lie or con people, but all the same it is obvious he isn't very good at it either. Good news for him that he can manage a wrestling team far better than he lies, because if he'd tried to become a lawyer he'd be homeless and starving right now. 'I see. So what you are telling me is that I am going to have to fund this religion thing that Hoyt Williams heads up in order to take my place in PRIME. In order for me to receive the favor, that you owe me Mr. Morales, I have to give money to another man's cause that I truly believe is complete bullshit. This is what you are telling me, Senor Morales?' You ask with your face masked completely. Not an ounce of emotion is showing through as you continue this conversation with the 'Jumping Bean' himself. 'Those are the facts as stated to me by the honorable Hoyt Williams.' Enrique replies with a confident grin. 'So you see my hands are clearly tied in this manner unless you agree.' 'First off all JB,' you begin as you watch his face turn bright red. Clearly he got the jest of the idea behind the initials and he now sits forward a bit further as he prepares to listen to what you have to say. 'Don't bother with all that honorable crap with me. We're partners now Morales and trying to put on a false show of pride and service toward a man like Hoyt Williams will not do. There is nothing honorable, holy, or even remotely genuine about that man. He is a fake, of the worst kind, and one of the true plagues of this world. If he wanted to be a truly honorable and noble man then he would simply tell you all that there is no God and you've all been duped. Secondly don't think for one second that this bullshit you are trying to feed me is going to fly. I know better than to believe that a dolt like Hoyt came to you with a plan to have me be the man with the money behind his false prophet business. You are a terrible liar Enrique and it shows through badly when you are speaking with me.' He just sits there, steaming, for the next few minutes as he tapes his fingers on the desktop and just stares at you. More accurately he is probably staring a hole right though you, wishing he could simply end this whole badly handled mess by putting you through the near by wall and ending any future involvement he might have to endure with you. You smirk, only slightly, as unfortunately unlike the normal day this type of situation isn't amusing to you. Normally letting a man know that you see right through his lying is something you enjoy, but when it comes to your furthering your wrestling career then the fun is all sucked out of it. The attempted side step at keeping you out of PRIME for that much longer has more than annoyed you and is now getting to the point where you'd like to put him through something and be done with it all. Unfortunately you realize that as much as he hates you, and as much as you detest him, that you actually need him, at least for the near future anyway. 'Look Cross this is the deal, the real one. I have no idea how I’m going to tell my boys that I let you weasel your way into PRIME through me. When you consider the fact that it happened after they told me to steer clear of you, and I’m simply trying to find a way that the majority of the blame is going to be on someone else. I like Hoyt, despite the fact that I don't really believe into all his bullshit, but he is an easy target for this type of thing. His large ego and the fact that he pays very little attention to many things makes it very easy for me to convince him that this was his idea and I can do that on the spot the moment I need to. This is mainly to give me a reason to point a finger and say that it is someone else’s fault.' Enrique explains to you. Finally, for the first time since you met him months ago, there is some actual truth coming out between the two of you. A tiny smirk begins to tug at the corners of your mouth but you hold back the urge to smile in any manner. The anger of being played with is still sitting on your stomach the wrong way. 'Enrique it is purely out of sheer desire to shut some people up in PRIME that I am going to agree to do this favor for you. This is a one-time favor that comes with no other strings attached though and you'd better remember that. I'll go along with that idea, and I'll actually even give the RISP the money to back their cause. It won't just be some lie you tell your boys, as I'll make it real. Besides religious donations of large amounts are a tax write off and I rich guy like me can always use another one or two of those. From this point on though you'd better be straight with me or you'll find that my good mood and generosity only stretches so far. We clear on that?' You ask him as you lean forward, with your arms folded on top of your desk, and look him directly into his brown eyes. 'I understand. Mr. Cross you've saved my career, and life for that matter, once more. If I actually liked you I'd be really grateful at this point but since this whole mess is your fault then I can't exactly be the most thankful little Mexican. You understand of course.' He replies with a little bit of a sneer. This does make you chuckle, ever so slightly, as you think about his words. 'Am I really suppose to give a damn if you are thankful or not? I'm going to be working for PRIME again, making lots of money, and wrestling. That is all the thanks I need from a bottom feeding, no good, Mexican bastard like you. Since when should a great American like me give a flying fuck about what a damn low life from Mexico thinks in the first place? There is a reason that Mexico is located south of the United States and that is because if there was God even he'd know that Mexico is below the United States in every way. So take your little I'm not the most thankful little Mexican speech, and shove it up the ass of Hoyt. Then you can help make his ass as big as his head and there for make him for more proportionate.' You respond without batting an eye. To think that someone that you know is clearly beneath your level would even hint that you have reason to give a crap about what they think is pathetic. As he gets up, red faced and angry, and walks out the door, you realize that you do feel pretty pathetic yourself. Having to rely on a man that you know is clearly beneath you on the food chain is something that simply doesn't set well with you. 'Curse my good nature.' You say to yourself as you think about the event that has led to your needing Morales to get back into the PRIME federation in the first place. If it wasn’t for your good heart and desire to help your friend Rock Startling solidify himself, as the top dog in PRIME, then you’d be the one in the PRIME Hall of Fame right now and you’d still be the one dominating the Universal title scene. That one moment, agreeing to help out Rock, proved to you exactly why you only look out for yourself in this world. 'Nice guys really do finish in fucking last place.' You say to yourself with disgust as you pour yourself a shot of whiskey. Never again will you allow a weakness like that to get the best of you. PRIME is now going to be yours for the taking, just like it was two years ago. Only this time there is no friends around to hold you down or have you thinking with anything other than yourself in mind. Rock Startling got the benefit of the run he wanted, by you allowing him to use you as a stepping stone, and now it is your turn to use someone else as your stepping stone to an even greater ride. Rock Startling was the man of the hour two years ago. Jacob Cross will be the man of PRIME history...and that begins the week after Colossus. Through out the history of the PRIME federation many men have come and gone and many more will come and go in the future. Few men have been able to place their names so deeply into the minds of all those who follow the federation that they have a legacy that lives on for eternity. Anyone can walk into a company and make a small name for themselves by winning a title or two and beating a few people with name value. Any regular Joe Wrestling can walk through the door of a company, even one as great as PRIME, and pick up a couple of lucky wins and have people remember who they are for the short haul. Building a legacy, a lasting one, is something that only the people with that special 'it' factor can accomplish. Colossus is the breeding ground for men to become heroes and for heroes to become legends, as far as the PRIME federation is concerned. Colossus is the biggest stage of them all and the show where the biggest tests of ones career will take place. There is nothing that can compare to knowing that your going to walk out to that ring and compete in front of one of the biggest crowds of your career and in the biggest match of your entire career. The gold is on the line and there is nothing that can be compared to putting everything you have all your hopes, all your dreams, and the future of your very career on the line for just one chance to reach that next level. The levels where only the finest athlete's in PRIME history are allowed to dwell, boast, and live on in the memory of PRIME forever. In all the history of PRIME no team has been able to dominate the tag team division in the way that Los Diablos has been able to since joining PRIME in January. They have beaten some of the biggest and brightest shining stars while on their quest to becoming, and remaining, the PRIME World Tag Team Champions. To most the belts are nothing more than a trinket; another award to place on the shelf and remind everyone about how they once were the holder of one half of a tag team championship. For Los Diablos those titles are so much more, they have far greater meaning then simply being shiny gold placed on a leather strap for the entire world to look at and admire. The belts are the centerpieces, the corner stone if you will, to providing Los Diablos place in PRIME history. Keeping those belts around their waists is as important to them as the simple act of breathing. The titles are the very life blood that keeps them going and fighting to be the best. Without the titles they are just like every other “team” in PRIME’s tag division that are just trying to get to the top. Climbing the mountain is something that Los Diablos has already done and they don't wish to go back and do it again any time soon. They always say that the hardest thing to do is keep a championship after you win it and that is partially true. The hardest part, however, is coping with the fact that at some point you are going to have to live without the titles you worked so hard to obtain. To go on and try to climb back to that mountaintop after you've been knocked back down to the base of it by the hottest thing at that moment. Team Zambo was the team everyone believed was the team on the rise. They had fought their way into position and were within a heartbeat of taking their place on top of the mountain. Everyone, Hin See included, was ready to stand up and cheer that there were new champions in PRIME. Everyone was just waiting to see the lovable tandem of Zameer and Gibson put the final nail in the Los Diablos title reign. They were turned back, however, just like Los Diablos said they would do with their challenge. They turned away the hottest tag team to enter PRIME, since the signing of Los Diablos themselves, and managed to hold onto the straps that mean more to them than anything else in their entire careers. Team Zambo gave it every thing they had and came up just short of being able to beat the superior unit of Los Diablos. Now the Fallen Halo waits on the horizon. Just around the corner at Colossus Los Diablos are being forced to step into the ring, inside the steel structure, and put their titles on the line in a match that allows them to lose their belts without ever being involved in a single decision. A masterful plan by Mr. Hin See to try and pry the death grip that Los Diablos has on the belts and finally be able to put them on a team that he can mold, control, and turn into a set of corporate champions. It is no secret that Hin See has never liked the fact that Los Diablos have been anywhere near his tag team titles. They aren’t the type of champions that he likes to have cause they don’t do what he wants them to. They aren’t going to walk into his office each week, kiss his ass, and make sure that they get the breaks they want just handed to them. Instead they go about simply making their own breaks, beating every obstacle put in their path, and winning despite the fact that Hin See would love to see them fall flat on their faces. Colossus is the ultimate test of will for the current champions as they will be forced to out last every single team in the entire company along with making sure that they do not allow any one of them to gain more decisions then they do. Winning at Colossus isn’t going to be easy, in fact it is quite promising that there is no chance for Los Diablos to walk out as the champions. It would be nothing for all those involved to simply screw the champions over, take them out of the match, and then fight it out amongst themselves. Los Diablos would certainly deserve that, given all the crap they have pulled on people to not only get to the titles but to also keep them. It is no secret that they are willing to pull out any and every single trick in the book to make sure that they continue to hold the gold over their heads after the matches they walk into with them on the line. It is no secret that The Imperium looks to impress its will on the whole of PRIME; every single time they walk into an arena. Colossus could wind up being the greatest night in the history of the Imperium, if they all walk out successful, or it could wind up being the worst night in their history if any or all of them should stumble. Gold is on the line in every single match that The Imperium is involved in and for Vetra and Los Diablos keeping that gold is the order of the day. Colossus marks the beginning of their reign of supremacy or their fall back down the mountain and the climb that they will have to face to get back to where they once were. Colossus means many different things to many different people that work for PRIME and for Los Diablos and The Imperium it means even more than the simple pay check or glamour of being part of the biggest show of the year. More than just retaining and winning more gold is at stake for the greatest stable of talent ever assembled in PRIME history and they all know this fact to be true. The very reason for their arrogance, for their ego’s, for their abilities to brag about how great they are and what all they have accomplished is on the line at Colossus. Like I said anyone can be great once in a while but it takes a special type of athlete to be great every time that something important is on the line. There aren’t many who can honestly claim to be in that league of special wrestlers who are able to step their games up a little bit more each and every time they go out and perform in front of the huge crowds, under the bright lights, with all the focus and attention on them. There are very few...but... Los Diablos is going to be known as that type of athlete and they are going to prove that fact on the biggest, brightest, and most important stage of them all. By walking into the greatest wrestling show ever assembled and beating the legends of the past and the stars of the future. There is five reigns of Primetime Championship Wrestling pride walking into this match wanting to prove themselves against the best that PRIME has to offer. There is two times the honor of Old School Wrestling history on the line for another team just dying to make a name for themselves at the expense of the forgotten and over looked champions. There are three teams, six men, who are on the cusp of being mentioned as great with in the confines of the company that look to use The Fallen Halo as their stepping stone to the next level, the next platform of history and prestige. Yet still there will be two more teams coming in as even great underdogs then the champions themselves who want nothing more than to walk in and steal a win under the radar of the fans and the experts of the wrestling world. The Fallen Halo is their chance, possibly their only one, to shine brighter than they ever have before and slip through with a completely unexpected victory. The champions are just one team out of eight with something to prove and loads of reasons to win. The only difference in them and the rest is that they also have absolutely everything in the world to lose while all the rest can focus on the everything to gain aspect of their career. The board is set, the pieces are in place, and the game is ready to begin. The king has made his move and his intentions known and now all the little pawns are going to be rushing out to see who can accomplish the orders first; while the enemy simply plans to make the move to check mate as fast as possible and end all the bullshit lack of respect for their place within the history books and their abilities to make their mark in those books. Welcome to Colossus.
