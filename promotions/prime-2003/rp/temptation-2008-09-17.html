Temptation

17 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/09/08 Temptation Shrouded in the shadows, Xavier Kannon hears the ebb and flow of the SCCW crowd and knows everything that’s happening. In the shadows he could be anyone, but a single, purposeful step out into even the faint light renders him unmistakable. Bright red hair whores itself out to any illumination it can woo, amplifying even a few stray beams to glow in the shade. Though the corona of his vivid mane pierce two sharp emerald eyes, irises seemingly swirling like storms. Barbed tattoos snake aggressively up each arm, stabbing under the arms of a black t-shirt that urges Praise Hubbard. A collective, vocal wince rumbles like thunder after the crack of lightning that was Tyler Rayne collapsing Mayhem’s facial features with a chair (and a liberal assist from his knees). A shrill ringing cuts through the crowd, only for a furious chorus of jeers to spill into its wake as Devin Shakur introduces the ring bell to the back of Lance Marshall’s skull. The King of Wrestling has developed a sixth sense - like sonar - that lets him read the arena through its reactions. It’s how he knows it’s his time. “Don’t do this, Kannon…” He also knows he wasn’t supposed to hear that. “You do this, and you’ll be a juicy chunk of meat in the Lion’s den when you walk into ReVolution,” warns an all-too-familiar, patronising, smarmy voice. “And I won’t be able to protect you… or your family.” Off Kannon’s left shoulder, a powdery white halo highlighting him against the shadows, stands Chadwick Peter Cantrell, his neck craned so he can whisper straight down the Gold Patron Meritorious’ ear. “It doesn‘t need to come to this,” the soft-glowing form of the Executive Producer prods. “You turn around, go home, and show up at ReVolution in a PRIME t-shirt like a good little employee, and I’ll forget you even came this close to fucking over everyone you share a backstage with.” The sickened jeer that the SCCW crowd spilled out for Devin Shakur still hangs heavy in the air, a dull ringing within its frozen form. “I know you’re not running out on me now, X,” starts a mischievous voice in opposition. “Not now we’ve come this far.” Chin perched on his shoulder, a devilishly grinning Amy Campbell tries to tempt Kannon to complete his crusade of belittlement and humiliation. Here by his side, an Angel. Here by his side, the Devil. But no way to know if they’d come dressed as one another. “You’re the one who came to me, remember?” the fizzling image of Aimz reminds him. “We sat up ‘til 3am, carving up the destruction of Jon Rhine’s career between us… and we’re so nearly there. So, why not join me through that curtain and we’ll finish him together.” “What can she do for your career, Kannon?” asks Cantrell. “You think once she gets rid of Rhine, her and Desade are still going to want you hanging round the place? No. All they want is their Judas, their betrayer in the PRIME ranks to show the reach their Hand has.” “The Dead Man’s Hand rewards wrestlers for their loyalty, for their belief,” Amy snarls across the King of Wrestling’s frozen shoulders. “Desade is at the whim of no veteran, Desade is hostage to no ‘locker room leader’. Fulfil your part of the plan, and The Hand will see to it that your name is written in Sin City history as a war hero.” “And I’ll see to it that you’re branded a traitor for life,” hisses a fork-tongued Cantrell, a flash of hellfire to his eyes. “All I’m hearing are threats, X,” sighs Amy, her kinetic visage not only settling down, but settling into the serene. “Did Alex say she’d ruin you? Did I hold a gun to your head? No. We let people be themselves, rather than whipping them into obedient followers. And this… this is what Xavier Kannon was born to do.” “And it’s what’ll get you killed,” Cantrell decrees through grotesquely contorted features… before fire is sucked out through the cracks that form in the creases, leaving the image of the Executive Producer to burn out from within. “But, since I’m not from your side of the tracks, I know it’s your free choice, X,” Amy tempts as she floats through the heavy, suspended jeer and towards the curtain, leaving a trail of inviting light in her wake. “So, you coming out to see me?”
