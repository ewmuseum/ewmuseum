Honest Abe (pt. 1 of 3)

6 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Thursday, Nov. 6 Abraham Harris had secluded himself into the darkest recesses of his mind long ago. Some, before he retreated to his current alcove, had known him as "Honest" Abe. It was a joke that everyone loved because it worked two different ways, both due to the historical significance of the nickname (as Abraham Harris was an African American) as well as the fact he was brutally honest. Blunt, others might say. Still, the vast majority had always felt the appropriate adjective was "asshole." It was of Abraham's belief that those who called him an asshole were only irritated. After all, it wasn't his fault that when they looked in the mirror they found themselves staring directly at a big, giant pussy. Honest Abe was walking through the streets of his hometown of Philadelphia (which, a Philadelphian calling someone an asshole is kindof a simulation of an asshole calling someone an asshole, Abe thought) when he fell witness to a jewelry store robbery. If he could tell you the story himself, it'd sound something like: Ol' Abe remembers it like it was yesterday. August 1977, I was pissed off and madder than hell, 'cause some mothafucka stepped on my shoe. I told that bitch he need to watch where he step. He came at me talkin' some mess, so I popped him right in his dumbass forehead once and he fell to the ground like a bitch. Forgot I was wearing my ring. Anyway, I was just stepping away from jiving Homeboy like that, when out of nowhere a colored man who looked a lot like myself burst out of McNabb and Son's Jewelery like some kindof crazy, terrible thang. I said, "Sir you better get the fuck out of my face." You know what that crazy bastard did? He dumped that bag of jewelry right in my crossed arms. It was an unfortunate stroke of luck that a witness had called about the break in, describing the burglar as a "large, angry, black guy". Equally unlucky was the fact that Abraham Harris seemed to befit the description in a very precise fashion. And so: As soon as he dumped it into my arms, right, I was closed into the street by officers. I nearly shit myself! According to Chief Dobson, there was no "nearly" to it. Also, Chief Dobson noted, that Mr. Harris' story was so unoriginal and filled with holes that those who knew him should consider changing his nickname. Chief Dobson's just irritated. It's not my fault that when he looks in the mirror he sees a big, giant pussy. Abaham Harris saw eight years inside. Once he was released in 1985 he found himself surrounded by crackheads and dopefiends. A mere product of his environment, Abe too became a crackhead dopefiend. He was into that sortof thing for quite a while in his life, until he joined a cult during the Y2K ensaga. Harris ran like a girl and hid in the woods in the deep south once he realized the world had NOT been hit by a bunch of aliens riding on an asteroid. In those woods, Honest Abe built his cabin. He has been there ever since, living peacefully and waiting on God to call him home. --------------------------------- "...made a tar of your woe and your flesh where in. Have you gone half dead? Yet, yet have you let the worst most be as if it were Atlas to your world of cope." - 13 and God's Soft Atlas Rhett Locke's neck was tightly held in a noose. His feet kept him from choking or possibly breaking his neck from a fall as they stood in his desk chair. The rope itself dangled from the ceiling of his apartment. A firm believer in karma and doing what was, ultimately, right in your own definition of morality (a kind of existential stance the Author feels is sorely missed in this world), he had certainly gone against those beliefs in killing an innocent hooker. Regardless of whether or not it was right to do so in order to save the lives of your friends is not a question. Things are much too black and white in the guilty Albino's eyes. No murderer deserves to live. Of course, a bottle of vodka had helped him come to this decision. Or rather, it didn't hinder him from making it. Rhett Christopher Locke always was a sad drunk. Always would be. For the next few moments or so, anyway. He grinned. There would be no suicide note. There didn't have to be. The few that he'd call people he cared for would miss him, but they definately wouldn't be surprised. He kicked the chair out from beneath himself. Rhett Locke was strangling. As he dangled, finding his body fighting for air despite the fact he didn't want to, he pondered if this was the right decision. Then... ...he began seeing red... ...and he became very fond of the decision he'd made. Even though he was drooling uncontrollably. He was beginning to convulse, he noticed. It was really uncomfortable. There was a sequin on the carpet... ...he liked it when his eyes drifted off like that. He could see a bright blue. He pretended it was his soul. --------------------------------- One and The Other were badasses. If you asked them, they'd tell you so matter-of-factly that you'd think it had to be true, or else. Generally, people agreed to do what they say. Who tells two suave men in dark suits holding briefcases (probably containing something as badass in Pulp Fiction) what is and what isn't? They had just successfully robbed a jewelry store (something Abraham Harris was incapable of doing), and were feeling rather giddy. So giddy that as an old lady walked by, they kicked her right in her belly. She fell and they laughed. One was so happy about kicking the old lady that he did it again. A man ran up to them and tried to stop them. They laughed and beat him with their briefcases. The Two Lunatic Jerkwads entered a jaguar, that was black and fast as fuck, and hauled ass to Rhett Locke's house. --------------------------------- Abraham Harris had long since forgotten anything before Y2K. Having heard of the Mayan Calendar and how it ends some time in 2012, he'd constructed a story so horrific as to what will happen to the planet on the day that calendar ends that he had set up what was, essentially, a walking mine field outside of his house. As well as a razor wire fence and trap nets for anyone who happens to not set off a mine. How he got this ammunition was simple. A man he'd known on the inside joined the cult Abraham had started about this event, The Happy People Of a Certainly Doomed Future, and had means in the black market. More specifically the Brazilian black market, but that didn't seem to matter much. The Happy People of a Certainly Doomed Future, which had no sort of cool, relating word made out of it's acronym, didn't mind. Some in the upper court of the cult suggested that this acronym problem may be a reason they weren't very successful in the aquirement of new members. Others suggested it was that many people could not truly handle the ridiculousness of what would happen on that puppy-dog slaughtering, demon rising day in 2012. Abraham Harris didn't mind, however, as those who believed him considered him a prophet. The kind that everyone gets a hard on about due to their obscure and absent style of writing. And so, on a near by tree just outside of the mine field, a banner that said: puppy dogs get neck slit, demons rise from ground like angry flowers! those who do not know will either get stab in heart by demon with triton or something much worse. the prophet abraham said this, let it be known! let. it. be. ...known. Abraham Harris thought he was very smart. Most didn't understand why he never used capitals. Abraham Harris cared little for their tomfoolery. Most dissapeared. --------------------------------- Rhett Locke came to, realizing that he was in Heaven. Wait, no, his kitchen. And someone was cussing and holding him up. "Good. Little fucker is awake." One removed the noose from around his neck while the other carried him into the living room and threw him on the couch. Rhett Locke landed cushioned, "Thank you?" One replied, "You're welcome! NOW WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING!?!" "Excuse--" "HE SAID WHAT. THE. FUCK ARE YOU DOING!?!?!" The Other screamed. He screamed so hard that he had to cough afterward and adjust his tie. "It's kindof obvious, isn't?" One slapped Rhett Locke right across his mouth, then drew a gun to ensure that Locke would not come at him. It seemed to have slipped One's mind that the Albino had JUST tried to kill himself and probably was still in a fashion of mind not to care about a bullet flying through one side of his skull and out of the other. This was One's near-fatal (maybe) mistake, as Rhett Locke kicked him right in his head and hopped down. He took the gun and pointed it right at One's face. Unfortunately for Locke, in his state with the lack of oxygen entering his brain, he'd not even remembered that the Other was there. Rhett Locke's jaw dropped when he was clocked over the head with a gun. --------------------------------- The Prophet Abraham had been gone for several weeks and cult members at the secluded alcove were getting a bit antsy. So when he, led by several Brazilians on jeeps carrying ammo in a caravan of six pulled in, his people (fifty six to be exact) welcome him like a king. He stood, standing like Rambo if Rambo were a pissed off old man with a machine gun in his ebon hands. He said few words, but his subjects took them like the flesh and blood of Jesus Christ. "The War of 2012 may come early, my friends. Prepare for war." There was an uproarious applause. His people loved him. The look in his eyes told a story of Machiavellian-like proportions. Considering what he did to aquire these weapons, lots of people were going to be pissed off. Lots of people with lots of money. "Yes! I will prepare for the next step in the morning!" The party began and didn't end well into the next morning where, the Prophet, Honest Abe Harris, was already planning his next line of defenses. --------------------------------- When Rhett Locke woke up he reached to rub the knot he felt painfully aching on his head. This was a pointless endeavor, as he found himself tied to a chair with the very same rope that was around his neck previously. He looked up as he noticed two feet in his struggle. He said, "oh fuck." That was when he realized his mouth was duct taped. One and the Other both looked mad. Mad and very fucking serious. He didn't say anything. One screamed, "ARE YOU READY FOR YOUR NEXT JOB!?!" The Other ripped the tape away from Rhett's mouth. Rhett yelled, "OW! WHAT AR--" The Other taped his mouth back shut. One screamed, "I SAID ARE YOU READY FOR YOUR NEXT JOB!?!?" The Other once again tore the tape from Rhett's lips. Locke screamed again, "CHRIST! FUCK! I DON'--!" Again, the Other taped his mouth shut. One screamed even harder, "DAMNIT YOU IGNORANT BASTARD FUCKING BASTARD! I SAID ARE YOU READY FOR YOUR NEXT JOB!?!" The Other slapped Rhett across his mouth and ripped the tape away again. "YES!!!" Rhett screamed. "Good." Replied One. The Other chuckled. "FUCK!" Rhett yelled, "WHY THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING THIS TO ME!?!" One and the Other smiled. They said nothing and merely began opening seperate briefcases. Rhett was exasperated. Were they playing a fucking game? "I SAID WHY THE FUUUUUUUUUUUUUCK ARE YOU DOING THIS TO ME!?!" One replied calmly as he presented the documents. "I'm sorry, I thought that was a rhetorical question." The Other laughed when he said that. One, once again, noticeably dodged the question anyway. "You're going to kill Abraham Harris." "No." Rhett said. "No. Fuck you. You don't tell me what I want to know, I'm not doing fucking shit." The Other shook his head, "Wrong. You will do fucking shit." One agreed with the Other, "Unless you want everyone you know to die. Also, if you try to kill yourself before our relationship is through, you're friends will die before they get to see your funeral. So let's just do what we say from now on, alright little buddy?" Rhett was feeling overwhelmed. How did he find himself in this shit? My God, what have I done? "Now, you will kill Abraham Harris. I believe you are scheduled to wrestle in Savannah, Georgia. I'm sure you'd like a big win. And hey," The One smiled. "We're rootin' for you buddy. But between Savannah and Jacksonville, he's nestled with a shitload of guns and all kindsof fucked up, awful land mines. We know this might result in your death. You should know that." Rhett was so sick to his stomach, he was on the verge of throwing up. Guns? LANDMINES? He was dry heaving in a literal panic. The kindof feeling a man gets when he realizes things will never be what they used to be. "Calm down," interjected the Other. "The guy is insane. He runs a cult. He's practically harmless, but our employers don't like cult dudes suddenly collecting guns. Really freaks them out. So, we're putting you in as our first wave, so to speak, of fucking up whatever he might be doing. You have to be smart. You have to infiltrate. You've got to convince them you're one of them, otherwise he'll feed you to their pigs... or something fucked like that. Then, like, poison the guy or something." The One was trying to calm Rhett down as well, "Here, we got some poisons. You'd like some poisons, wouldn't you?" And so, this was life. Rhett Locke was going to kill another person. And he was being blackballed into it once more, with threats that may or may not be idle. The only thing that really convinced him there was no trickery going on his life was that sometimes, when One and the Other mentioned killing, or weapons, or anything. Their eyes lit up, like they've seen what happens and they really enjoy seeing these things. Once Rhett was freed from the rope by the butterfly knife of the Other, he was told that he needed to go ahead and get in the car and they'd follow him to Savannah. With a noticeable look of helplessness, the kind of look One and the Other loved seeing in their minions, he got in his car and began driving. Win or Lose at Revolution, sometime between then and the next one... Rhett Locke was sure he'd be dead.
