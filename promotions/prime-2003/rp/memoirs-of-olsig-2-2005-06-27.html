Memoirs of Olsig [2]

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6.27.05 Time was winding down. Revolution was just under three days away and with it came my chance to revive the glorification that I could once call my own. What bothered me most though was my current state, not just psychically, but mentally. Psychically I had taken a lot of abuse and bruises both inside and outside of the ring.. Mentally I had been presented with painful images of my past. I had been battered and bruised mentally by these distortions and quite frankly, I didn't know whether or not I would be able to survive Revolution in this current state. That is why I called my very close friend, a shrink named Thomas Washington, to come and help me deal with this mental torture in which I had been put through lately. Hopefully he could restore some sort of mental stability in my life. Keyword there being hopefully, because with my current condition, I didn't know if anything would ever be stable again. I had turned to the Demon Drug for some sort of brief comfort, but it only brought forth more problems than before. Simply put...if this didn't work, I wasn't so sure if anything would. I began straightening up the place a little bit. It was very late and Thomas didn't normally make house calls at such a time, but I held a special place in his heart which made this an unquestionable decision. I atleast wanted to make the place look nice for him. During my reign of terror, so to speak, I had torn this place into shreds. The rebuilding process was far from over, but hopefully this could play some sort of subsequent role in it all. I looked over at the glass display that stood in the center of my living room. It was the spot in which I planned to place the Universal Championship. To witness its absence...it struck me with a great deal of grief. In the end, I knew that the belt was what this was all about. All of the images, all of the visual scenarios, all of the nightmares, they all centered around me bringing the Legacy Championship home. It was destined to happen. All of this pressure and turmoil that I have put myself through over the past week and a half would soon payoff on that faithful night when I shall walk out the undisputed Universal Champion. For that moment, I continued to fight. The doorbell rang as I immediately rushed over to answer it knowing that my salvation from this abyss could very well be on the other side. I opened it and there he stood, the man I hoped would save me from this madness. I invited him in as he accepted the warm welcome and sat down on the couch. I began to pour out my heart and soul unto this man, telling him off all the problems that have penetrated my torn soul for the past week and a half. The Drug Additction. The Painful images of my past. The demon in which I possessed. …Dani Furher. He offered me various amounts of advice, telling me that it’s just a figment of my imagination and that in the end, my thoughts are controlled by me alone. He even told me that I may be viewing these painful images and experiencing these demons because I want to...because I have to...because I need to. These things he was telling me, though they seemed awkward and insecure, they began to make a reasonable amount of sense. Maybe the only way for me to reach my destined future is to overcome my horrid past. Maybe. Suddenly I felt that feeling once again. The feeling I felt when I was on the verge of snapping. This is what the demon drug had done to me...it had taken over my body creating out of body experiences in which I began to become something that I am truly not...some sort of demon...some sort of monster. And the worst part about it was that I had no control over it...it was if it had a mind of its own. That is what bothered me the most. It casted my spirit and used my temple as a host to its own. But why was it activating itself now? I had a man here attempting to help me exercise this demon and yet it wanted to take over now? I couldn't stop it though...it grew inside of me and began to take over. I knew that I was in for some trouble. Before the serum could take over my body fully, I managed to tell Thomas Washington about how I felt. I told him that I believed I was turning into some type of uncontrollable monster. He laughed, viewing it as some sort of humorous joke...but what he didn't know was that it wasn’t a joke. He was about to find that out first hand. He shrugged off the comment and began searching through his briefcase for something with his back turned to me. My veins began to press against my skin as if they were about to pop out. My eyes strained, creating a mild ache on the left side of my head. My fingers cringed, craving the chance to strangle some helpless victim. My face crumpled creating the image of a pure demon. Thomas didn't know it, but I was transforming into this monster right before his very eyes. The images came like lightning bolts, chronicling the past weeks in the form of flashes. ”Call Me”came her voice. The woman I had met at the café…the woman who had turned my life around. ”You’ve become weak, son”Came my father’s booming voice as I stared at his painful reflection through the mirror. ”Why did you do this to me!”approached Jessica’s screams as I felt the ground shake with the pulling of the trigger. Her body sank in her own blood as mine sunk in my own tears. ”No matter what you do, no matter what you change your name to…you will Always be Dani Furher” said Needles as I grabbed a chunk of my hair with both hands at cringed at the sound of his hideous voice. Thomas turned around to hand me some papers expecting to see the same Vangelus Olsig he saw when he first came in...but no. I was no longer Vangelus Olsig...I was a demon that the serum had created. Thomas's eyes grew wider with fear as he dropped the papers and attempted to run, but I grabbed his shoulders bringing him closer in. I wrapped my right hand around his neck and began to strangle the life out of the man whom was supposed to be my salvation from this madness. I sent straight forward knuckle shots into the bridge of his nose, smiling with every crack that his nose took. This was what the drug had done to me. This is what it had turned me into. I was not myself anymore, and that revelation scared me like none other. I dropped Thomas's helpless body to the ground below watching as his lifeless body lay there with blood flowing out of his mouth resembling a crimson waterfall. The effects began to wear off. My eyes reached normal state. My face washed off all of the wrinkles. My hands relaxed and my veins sunk in. I was Vangelus Olsig once again. I looked down at Thomas and immediately knelt down beside him holding his head in my arms. The Demon Drug had struck again and this time it had taken one of my best friends. I screamed out knowing that no one would hear me...but it was the only way to release the torture which had set in. His blood stained my hands. His blood…at my hands. “Slash one up for ol’ needles, eh Vangy?” I looked up to see Needles standing before my very eyes. I squinted at the sight of him. Here is a man who claimed to be my alter ego, but yet he appeared before me in a physical form? It was obvious that the drug hadn’t worn off. ”Go to hell. You aren’t real…” Needles smiled revealing his hideous fangs, some even stained with a red fluid that I automatically assumed was blood. A scary sight indeed, but I held my ground, remembering what Thomas had told me. It’s just a figment of my imagination…just a figment. ”Vangy…I’m as real as you make me. And judging by your recent acts of lunacy…I’m as real as a heart attack. HAHAHAHA” His laughs, they were so uncontrollably horrific…a sound I had never heard before. I swung looking to strike him…but he was gone. All that was left were the wretched cries of laughter that tormented my soul. Just a figment of my imagination…but as real as I was. -- With Revolution on the horizon, the question plagueing everyone's mind is whether or not the underdog team of Nova and Vangelus Olsig stand a chance agaisnt the Universal Champion and a man who some deem nearly unstoppable. Killean...you defeated me. It's a fact that I will never forgive myself for letting happen, but I do offer you proper credit. I can't say I like Nova. We've endured coutnless battles and my hatred for him grows with each contest. But he has been placed as my tag team partner, and whether or not he decides to have my back, Killean...I will get my revenge. I'll never forgive you for pushing me to this state. I'll never forgive you for wrecking my life. Never. What I did to Hoyt last week...the way I manhandled him for the third time straight...it holds no comparison as to what I am prepared to do to you. Hoyt may be the known as the man that everyone deems the top contendor in PRIME. Killean may be known as the toughest man in the business. Nova may be known as one of the most proven stars in this business. Brandon Youngblood may be known as the uprising force. But post-Revolution... Vangelus Olsig will be known as the man who toppled them all. (Fin.) ...The best I could do [detail withheld].(no pun intended)
