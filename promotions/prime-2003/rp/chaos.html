Date: Colossus V "How else did you think this would end?" The voice booms through without any kind of filter, much like the obnoxiously loud bass of a car driving through a neighborhood not giving a damn as they listen to the most recent Ludacris song. He can feel the long, cold nuzzle resting comfortably against the back of his tender skull. Darkness surrounds him though as he's forced to wear a blindfold while this man stands to the side of him berating him as if he was a little kid stealing from a candy store. With silence comes abuse and he feels an elbow slam into the back of his head. Anger rises in his throat, but he fights off the urge to retaliate. "Let me ask you again, Craig, how did you think this would end?!" Just like that, the darkness is filled with an array of lights as he squints his eyes while they adjust to the recent flood of colors. Everything around him spins though as if he had just gotten done spinning around in a chair. A rotten, toxic smell fills his nostrils as he can feel the urge to throw up coming on. "You always thought you were just so smart, hiding, and scheming. You thought you could get away with what you had done without any sort of repercussions. I think this, this moment right here, proves the complete opposite, Craig. You can't run away from us. You can't disappear from your demons, because demons, they have this funny habit of just popping up at the most inopportune time. Kind of like right now, wouldn't you think?" The voice is vaguely familiar to him though he can't quite put his finger on the trigger. His head screams of pain as he can feel trickles of blood slipping past his skull and onto his shoulders. His knees rest uncomfortably on the hard concrete as he feels the pressure of the situation increasing with each passing second. He has to do something and has to do it now. The world has quickly crashed all around him and he has to do something now or else he would too. "For years Craig, you were trained, and then one day you just decided you would up and leave it all. You thought you could be a wrestler for the rest of your life as if you hadn't committed the crimes! Life just doesn't work that way, it never works that way. You have debts that need to be paid up Craig and this debt, with me, can only be paid with your life. For far too long, you've tormented the people that you eventually burned. You ruined the world that we live, eat, and sleep in, and then just left. People answer to me Craig, and they asked me how I could let you get away with what you had done. I'm here to tell you and show them that you just don't." With each passing second, he can feel that long, hard nuzzle dig deeper into his skull, pushing him closer and closer to his breaking point and into complete insanity. He grits his teeth while biting his tongue hoping to keep himself from reaching the edge of the cliff, but the man holding the gun intended on making him snap into pieces. "Do you just love hearing the sound of your own voice?" A soft laugh in his ear sends chills down his spine. "Tired of hearing my voice, Craig? How about you see my face instead?" Seconds pass before the blindfold is removed from his eyes and at first, the bright glare causes him to grimace in pain while his eyes adjust to the sudden change in lighting. As he looks up at the man though, he can feel a sensation that he's never felt before. He had been tricked. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ A hard, cold night in Moscow, Russia wasn't the way any person should die. Unfortunately, it seems inevitable with the stakes as high as they were at this particular moment. Snow litters the ground as a lone fire in a barren wasteland keeps two people warm enough to live throughout the night, and if they're lucky into morning. "You forget that I make the rules around here, Katarina. The bosses, they trust me, not you." "Rules?! Craig, rules?! Don't lecture me about rules when we KILL people! She sits there, stewing in her own anger, as she holds out her hands over the open fire. Craig looks at her before biting his bottom lip, possibly feeling sorry for having yelled at her the way that he had just done. He can feel the tears in her eyes, but knows that she'll hold them back like she's so good at doing. He walks over to her and puts a hand on her shoulder. "Go over the details. Tell them to me." "Katarina’s" eyes look up at Craig and she nods her head before licking her lips and clearing her throat. "Tomorrow evening, we go--" "Date, Katarina." "Why do you think the date is so important?" "Because, if you don't remember the date, Katarina, you'll forget it." "What do you mean?" "Just remember the date, okay?" She bites her tongue as she wants to lash out at him, but just does as she's told. "Fine. December 27th, 1999." "Thank you." In the background, watching the scene unfold, Craig just stands there like a third person being privy to this very private discussion. A dream. That's all it was and instead of fighting out of it to write down the details, Craig stays in it hoping to find the secret that eludes him. For three weeks, he had seen different dreams and scenarios play out before his very eyes without the slightest clue as to why he was dreaming of these moments and who that woman was. He had racked his brain trying to figure it out, but thus far, nothing. He remains though, hoping to unlock this secret, not realizing that the secret wasn't in the dream, but in reality. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Morning, possibly the most important morning of his entire life.
