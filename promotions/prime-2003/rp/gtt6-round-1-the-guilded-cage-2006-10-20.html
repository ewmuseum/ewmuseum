GTT6 Round 1 - The Guilded Cage

20 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/20/06 Ok boys and girls, take out your calendars and go back in time. It really doesn’t matter what date you choose. One year ago or ten years ago, it wouldn’t make a lick of difference. All throughout his life, Killean Sirrajin was never completely and totally helpless. He had been through some tough times before, but as an independent person, he didn’t need the sympathy and help that he was offered. It was there for the taking but rarely did he ever indulge. This is what made him a unique person. Most people who desperately require the assistance of others in bad times just don’t have that luxury. Killean has had it but if he could give the excess to charity in some sort of donation he would. But as he sat in the dirty wooden chair behind a cold metal table, he only wished his friends and loved ones had any pull at that particular moment. *TAP TAP TAP…* It was official. The man who was all powerful in the world of professional wrestling was now officially powerless. *TAP TAP TAP…* The man who always had the finances to get himself out of any situation was now quite possibly poorer than a man who spent most of his days in a soup kitchen and making blankets from old wet newspapers. *TAP TAP TAP…* He was having problems thinking for himself. How could he let this happen? One slight indiscretion has finally come back to turn his life into an upside down roller coaster ride of helplessness. *TAP TAP TAP…* “COULD YOU STOP THAT INCESSANT TAPPING?!” His body shook like a leaf blowing in a cool autumn breeze as he spat out the rather loud question. His handcuffs and leg shackles begin to ring together like wind chimes with the shaking. He looked over at the all too primped figure of the questioning officer who had the gall to look down his nose at Sirrajin past his extremely shiny and annoying reading glasses that sat on the edge of his beak. He hated that, it reminded him of his terribly prudish and snarky grade school English teacher. Yeah, you know the kind who has the terrifying smokers voice that sounds deeper than your fathers? Yeah, she haunted Killean for three full years in grade’s one, three and six. He kept moving up grades and she seemed to change grades with him. It was awful. “I could, but I don’t think you are calling the shots right now big guy.” His voice was too high pitched for an older man. This guy had grey hair surrounding his lightly wrinkled facial features and yet he sounded like a teenager who just went through a voice change. Killean mouthed a few obscenities before thinking briefly what it would be like to wrap the short chain of the handcuffs around the neck of Officer Daniels and choke him until he heard the last gasps for air come crawling up from his lungs. *TAP TAP TAP…* Needless to say, that thought ran through his mind for many a minute before another officer opened the door to the room. Killean sighed as he recognized the officer as Lynn Jacobs, a middle aged and rather polite lady who seemed to be the only officer to believe in the innocent until proven guilty pledge. She finally put a stop to the annoying tapping by grabbing the pen out of Daniels hand. She sat down at the table and wrote a few words in her notepad before smiling at Killean. “Thank you for joining us this morning.” “Yeah, to what do I owe the pleasure of sitting across from Richard Gere here? I was pulled from a warm but dry as hell breakfast for this. By the time I get back I could use the pancakes to dig my way out of my holding cell.” At least his sarcasm wasn’t gone after all the negative things that have encompassed his life in the last few days. Officer Jacobs actually smiled but Officer Daniels continued to look down his nose at Killean. Once again the thought of strangling this schmuck ran through Killean’s mind. Really, he was in deep anyways. Might as well go for the full six feet under right? “Well we happen to have some relatively good news for you this morning.” Lynn began. “We have received numerous phone calls from one of your places of employment. Apparently a Mister Blaine Blair of PRIME Wrestling has spent a few hours on the phone with us and many other contacts trying to at least help you to get out of here. But as it turns out, we don’t have the evidence to freeze your assets. We have to keep you under our watch but your assets are available to you. So as part of code, we have notified your lawyer who has already spoken on your behalf. Thank him for this, but you are going home.” Fireworks went off in the depths of his mind. After a few days of nightmarish hell, he finally found the silver lining. He was silently screaming for joy in his mind but the look of absolute control in the face of the extremely annoying Officer Daniels told him there had to be a catch. “Wipe that fuckin’ smirk off your face before I buff my shoes to a shine with your lips.” Officer Daniels took some offense to the derogatory remark but Lynn happened to keep him quiet while she could reveal the conditions of the release. “This is not a full release back into society. We figured that since your job makes you travel all over the continent that you are a considerable flight risk. Therefore, your lawyer once again talked the crown into granting you house arrest with an electronic bracelet. One plus for you though is that you will be granted a pardon to attend work. Your own company however does not fall into this category. Instead, the work pardon only involves wrestling with PRIME. We are also aware that you expressed interest in participating in a tournament called GTT. So the pardon includes that event as well. However, you will rarely be staying in hotels on the road. Instead, you’ll be accompanied by an officer where ever you go. In some cases you will also have a local officer from wherever you travel following you as well just in case. On top of it, you’ll be escorted to the airport and immediately taken home on a plane or if your next event is within a day you’ll be taken there and housing for the night will be arranged with top security. This is not a free pass Mr. Sirrajin. The only time you will ever find yourself alone is when you are at home or in housing on the road. You may not feel alone though because security will be stationed in locations all around you. The bracelet tracking will accompany you wherever you go so if you find yourself in an unapproved location, you can bet you’ll be tailed.” Her voice was gruff yet somehow soothing to the emotionally tired mind of The Supreme Machine. That was why he liked talking with her so much more than Officer Daniels. Once she finished the very long speech, Killean sighed. Sure he was getting lucky enough to be sent home and sure he was lucky enough to be getting a work pardon. But an ankle bracelet? Really now, he was being treated like an animal. “So since I’ll basically be thought of as a damn dog with a collar, who will be holding the leash and taking me for my walks?” He didn’t want to sound so disrespectful, especially around the pleasant demeanor of Lynn Jacobs. But what choice did he have? Flight risk? This is a guy who has paid his taxes to the dollar every year. This is a guy who has never been issued a parking citation. Yet here he was under house arrest and being led around by an officer with an electronic leash. “The duties will be split by a few choice officers. I have been selected as one of them.” “And HIM?” Killean nodded his head in the direction of Daniels and Jacobs smiled a little. “No, he will not be part of the process.” Finally Killean could crack a smile of victory over Scott Daniels. He was the arresting officer after all and his ego really didn’t need another push in the direction of being obsessed with seeing Killean suffer. Finally, Killean had some leverage in the fact that he would be away from this douche bag that he wished would roll over and die already. “In fact, Officer Daniels will no longer be working your case anymore. His statement as the arresting officer was taken by our superiors. Effective now, Officer Daniels has a new assignment.” Daniels gets to his feet with a smirk and gathers his papers. “I think I have done enough to put you away for a while. It’s scum like you that ruins our city.” Meanwhile, another officer enters the room and approaches the still shackled Sirrajin. He removes a small silver key from his pocket and unlocks the confining and rather embarrassing steel that was controlling him. Killean gets to his feet and rubs his wrists, glad to be free. He then eyes Daniels and begins to make his way around the table. The officer who unlocked him follows closely, trying to make sure Killean didn’t snap. He gets real close to the much smaller frame of Daniels and is mere inches from the barely visible wrinkles in his face. Killean sneers and continues to look down at the shorter man. “What are you going to do? Hit me? Go ahead. You’ll just end up right back in those shackles big guy.” Daniels smirked but still backed down a tad from the menacing look on the face of Killean. “Hitting you would be too easy.” Killean said quietly, stomping his foot on the ground and leaning forward a bit, causing Daniels to recoil a bit and trip on the leg of the chair he was sitting on. He tumbled to the floor, taking his papers with him. They littered the space around him, floating to the ground like fresh snow flakes on a cold winter’s day. Killean smiled as Daniels looked ready to burst at the seams. “Did you see that?! He assaulted me!” Daniels screamed from his seated position on the cold floor. “Give it up Daniels, he didn’t lay so much as a finger on you.” Jacobs said as she led the way out of the room. Killean followed but turned back to take one last glance at the officer on the ground. “Looks like you aren’t calling the shots anymore… little man.” He then turned his head and proceeded to leave the room, followed by the officer who unlocked his shackles. Frustrated, Daniels swatted at the papers on the floor and causing some to flip into the air again. ~|o|~ Now granted, Killean had been released from prison and had already taken a new lease on life. Something that he really didn’t have however was the same freedom that he had before. It would take some getting used to, not being able to walk down to the corner store for a carton of milk or being able to walk to the vendor to get himself some beer so he could plunk down in front of the TV to watch football. It was something that he wasn’t quite grasping yet. “What do you mean I can’t get out to get a hamburger?!” See? “Killean, I already explained this to you. You are under house arrest. You can’t just go anywhere you please. You are cleared to travel for your wrestling work and you are cleared for things like doctor’s appointments and funerals. I don’t think going down to the diner on the corner would be considered something of equal importance.” Lynn Jacobs spoke quietly from the passenger seat of the police car. The officer who earlier unlocked his chains was driving the car and during transit Killean learned that he was a 22 year old rookie on the force named David Carlyle. It was a situation that Killean was grateful and disgusted to be in at the same time. What could he really do though? He didn’t have any choices and already considered himself lucky that he was out of that stuffy holding cell. If they really wanted to bury him under clauses and laws, they would have by now. The fact that he has the ability to set foot outside of the jail is something that he doesn’t take for granted. “I have been enduring those plates of food that I wouldn’t feed to a dog for three days now. I am just craving some good cookin’.” It was sad. Killean almost sounded desperate in his pleas. But he was a smart man. He figured that he would be able to rattle some compassion and a positive response from Lynn and he ended up succeeding. “Dave, pull over up here. There is a small take out place on the corner. I’m hungry myself anyways. But I’ll be the only one going in. So Mr. Sirrajin, I’ll need to know what you want.” Lynn Jacobs always kept professional remembering that she was talking to a convict and not a friend. But Killean cringed at being called Mr. Sirrajin. It reminded him of his father… his terrible, annoying and hate filled father. Ugh. “I’ll have a double bacon cheeseburger platter with a coke. And please, call me Killean. Mr. Sirrajin is my father.” “Alright… Killean.” The car comes to a stop and Lynn proceeds to open her door. “Dave?” “The same sounds fine actually. Thanks Lynn.” She exits the car and closes the door. “So tell me, what is it that got you into this line of work anyways?” Killean queried the younger officer. He was really trying to avoid any period of awkward silence. He had never been in this situation before. “Nothing really, decided that I wanted to do it after high school. I was in good enough shape and took the tests, did the physical training and testing and here I am.” Blast… he was hoping for a longer discussion but instead Officer Carlyle was as blunt and basic as possible. Killean slumped back into his seat and sighed. He was not used to being treated as anything less than a member of a civil society. He had a funny feeling that wherever he was to go, this treatment may follow him. ~|o|~ He shook his ankle as he made his way back into the main room. The surprisingly light ankle bracelet that he was to wear was just fitted to him by Lynn. He had no intent to try and get out of it. No, he was smarter than that. He had already been granted enough and the work exemption for wrestling in GTT 6 and PRIME was just icing on the cake. He was just thankful to be at home. Lynn Jacobs exited the bedroom with a briefcase in her hand. Officer Dave Carlyle had set up the home monitoring system in an undisclosed location in the building. Killean was just sitting down to his lukewarm hamburger when Lynn grabbed her bag and was about to make her way out for the day. “Why don’t you stay and have your meal before you go? I know you’re hungry.” “Dave is waiting for me outside. We have to get back to the station.” “Hey, come on and sit. You get a lunch break just like anyone else. You have your food so eat it while it’s warm. French fries from the microwave are not as good. You know that.” Killean really had a way with his words. He could convince anyone to stay and talk with him for a little while, no matter what plans were preventing it. Lynn just smiled and shook her head. She pulled out one stool from the island table that connected the kitchen from the living area and put her bag back down. “I really shouldn’t be doing this, but I’ll be honest. Ever since I smelled those French fries, I’ve been drooling over them.” “You? I have been craving this burger for three days! You have no idea what that food is like in there. I mean hospitals are worse, but still.” “I have no idea? I work there. I HAVE forgotten my lunch every now and then you know. And when you’re in a hurry to eat and run, you really don’t have much of a choice. I just grab my Tupperware container with rubber food and leave.” Lynn laughed as she took a sip of her drink and then ate a couple French fries. Officer Lynn Jacobs was a woman of about 37 or so, at least that’s what Killean guessed. She was not the most attractive of ladies, but she had something about her, something that would make even the most cynical person like her and perhaps in some cases become attracted to her. But Killean wasn’t feeling that. What he was feeling was that this woman had a heart the size of Texas; that she would really care about her friends, family and almost any person she got to know. In truth, Sirrajin was worried that his social life would never be the same after the arrest and after his name was plastered on almost every television set and newspaper in North America. Lynn Jacobs however, gave him hope that it might not be the case after all. She had no real reason to help him at all or be fond of him. Even though she might even believe his plea of innocence, doesn’t mean that she is even allowed to show it. As an officer, she must be impartial for the sake of being non-bias towards the court case that was pending a date. So what was it that Lynn saw in Killean? Maybe, she was just a nice person. He intended to find out. “So, I never thought I would see the day that an officer would be sitting down and having lunch with me after I committed a crime.” He was being simple yet blunt. She finished a bite of her hamburger and nodded slowly. “You may see some changes in the way people act towards you now. You are someone who is rather famous all across the continent. I have seen it many times that some people wish they would die rather than have to deal with the criticism and being treated like someone with a disease. It’s sad really. Even the people who are exonerated of charges walk around experiencing it. The fact that their name is even mentioned in the same sentence is enough to change the views of most people. Me? I’m just a nice person.” Lucky guess. “I don’t look at you and see a psycho. I can’t speak about the case to you but I can say that the chances are quite good that you could walk away from all this.” She brought a smile to the face of Killean, something that had been a rarity in recent days. “What do you think? Do you really think I’d…” He stopped. He knew he shouldn’t be asking such a question. He knew that she couldn’t answer it so why did it slip out? He didn’t want to offend her. She should know better. “I can’t say, you know that.” Her voice, it was warm and soothing. Not to mention that she said that with a smile on her face while she looked directly at him. He was certain that she thought he couldn’t do such a thing unprovoked. He did beat the guy within an inch of his life, but the reasons given were way off the mark. The kid was selling drugs on his legit delivery route. Meanwhile, when he was questioned, he told authorities that Killean did what he did because he screwed up the sale. He said that Killean himself was the dealer and the kid was just making the drop. Killean would be ok with an assault and battery charge, but no, this little shit has to go out and lie to get his own ass off the hook after he made the error. Yeah, shows how selfish and irresponsible people can be. So now, Killean, the guy without a smudge of any type on his record, now had a black eye in the face of the public. Sure, some people would not think any less of him, namely most of his friends and fans. But then there were other people, people who don’t have an open mind. They’re the people who don’t understand the idea behind forgiveness and people who don’t think that everyone deserves another chance. *KNOCK KNOCK* “Lynn? You ready yet?” A voice called out from the hallway of the building making Lynn Jacobs began to laugh with a bite of her hamburger in her mouth. She raises her hand to her mouth to avoid spitting the food out in a fit of laughter. Apparently, Officer Carlyle was finished with his task. She turns towards the door and attempts to speak with food in her mouth. “Uhhh yeah! Having an issue with the bracelet. We should be done soon!” “You need some help?” “Nope! I can handle it! Gimme ten minutes to get everything organized!” “Alright, I’ll be down in the car.” After they hear the door to the stairwell close, they both laugh a little. “You think we should’ve invited him in to eat?” Killean asked but that was quickly quashed by a wave of Lynn’s hand. “You kidding? The guy is 22 and still wet behind the ears! He goes by the book in regards to everything. He wouldn’t think twice about reporting this to the Constable. No worries though, he’ll be good for another ten or so minutes before he really begins to wonder.” Both of then shared a hearty laugh as Lynn asked for more ketchup for her French fries. Killean gladly passed it across to her as they continued to discuss things other than his criminal existence. ~|o|~ “Ouch! Damn man, put me down!” Vegas’ voice boomed throughout the loft as he was set back on his feet. “I thought you wanted me to help you with your back?” “I do, but hell I don’t want my ribs slipping out of place. You had your arms wrapped around me tight!” “It’s to help crack your back and stretch it out though. But your choice.” “HAHA, yeah, my choice. So back off bro.” Vegas moved towards the couch rubbing his lower back as he was walkin like an 80 year old hunch back without health insurance. Killean went to the fridge in the kitchen and got a couple beers for the two of them. He handed one to Vegas and slapped him on the shoulder quite heartily, winning a funny glare from his best friend. “So man, it’s gotta suck being cooped up at home all the time huh?” It was Killean’s turn to return a quirky glare. “Ya think? I mean hell this is only the fourth day and I feel like a fuckin’ caged animal. Although it beats the jail cell, don’t remind me that I’m stuck here. I sure am glad I have GTT coming up on the 20th of October. Let’s me get out before I go back to PRIME. They threw some guy named… Yori Yakamo Jr. at me. Apparently this guy is so messed up the white coats want nothing to do with him. This fucker seems to be beyond help. We’re talkin’ full blown five alarm fuck job here.” A quick chug of his beer later, the phone rang. Killean checked the call display and decided to let it ring. ‘Mr. and Mrs. Manale’ was on the call display. He knew that it was Charity’s father calling because Charity and her mother were away at that women’s retreat they had talked about numerous times before. He just wanted to spend some uninterrupted time with his friend. “Heh, Charity’s dad is lonely without her mom around.” Killean said as he took another quick swig of his drink and wiped his goatee with his hand to remove any excess. A beep was heard in the room and the answering machine kicked in. “Killean… it’s Frank. Listen I was just wondering if you had heard from the girls. They said they would call before they left the retreat so I would know what time to pick Betty up from the airport. If you have heard from them, can you call me and let me know? Thanks…” A click was heard and the machine finished recording with a beep. “So Charity’s dad is named Frank and I suppose her mom is Betty?” Vegas questioned while flipping through TV channels with the remote control. “Yeah, Francisco and Betty. Her parents are great. He is watching Desiree while they are away. In two weeks, they’ll be out this way and they will bring her back. Vivian will be coming out from San Diego for a few weeks to help out while I get back into the swing of things.” “Where do they live?” “Outside of Toronto… Mississauga I believe. I’ve never been out to their place so I’m not entirely sure. But either way, I haven’t heard from the girls. They probably just got caught up with programs there and are running late.” “That’s cool bro.” Vegas finally got frustrated with flipping through channels and turned on a Canadian Football League game, otherwise known affectionately to Canadians as the CFL. “Ok, you need to explain something to me. I haven’t been around here long and I watch the NFL religiously. But what the hell is a ’55 yard line’?” “Ha! In the CFL, the field is 10 yards longer. Not to mention the goal posts are at the goal line instead of the back of the end zone. So if the ball is gonna be kicked from the 40 yard line, it’ll be a 40 yard field goal.” “Hum, I guess that’s cool. WHAT THE?! That end zone is massive! It’s gotta be an extra 10 yards longer!” “Yep. Some other differences are that if a field goal goes wide, but still ends up in the end zone since the posts are at the front, they can run it out, much like a kick return. Also, and this is the key, it’s a 3 down game, not 4 like the NFL or college.” “Dude, this is too different. I don’t think I’m gonna like this game very much…” But Just One Hour Later… Killean is in the kitchen checking the hot wings that he had been cooking in the oven. He was also tending to the potato wedges that he spread out on a non-stick pan to put in the oven. He then reached in the fridge to grab another beer for Vegas. He closed the door to the fridge and… “HOLY SHIT! GOD THAT WAS AN AMAZING CATCH! FOURTH… I MEAN THIRD DOWN GAMBLE! WOOOOO!” *CRASH…* Vegas basically exploded off the couch when he went into his psychotic football fan mode and Killean really didn’t expect it. It startled him and caused him to drop the bottle of beer on the hardwood floor. Needless to say, he now had a mess on his hands that he was considering cleaning up with his tongue. You just don’t waste good alcohol. “Yo, dude… what was that?” Vegas questioned as he returned to a seated position on the couch. “Beer… floor… tears… kill…” Killean barely grinned, but Vegas wasn’t even paying attention to him. He had already returned to being enthralled with the football game. Killean just shook his head and grabbed another beer from the fridge… “TOUCHDOWN! THAT WAS SWEET! I LOVE THIS GAME!” Killean bobbled the ice cold beverage but this time kept control. He figured Vegas would be like a baby, take his bottle and shut up for a while. He delivered the beer to his friend who was still ranting about the replay of the touchdown. “Man did you see that? He was deep in the end zone when he caught it. That would have been way out of bounds in the NFL.” “I told you earlier, CFL happens to have a more exciting game than the NFL. It’s the three down thing, more chances are taken and bigger plays are made.” Killean explained as he handed Vegas the beer. He grabbed his own off the end table and finished it off before returning the empty bottle to the box. He grabbed another out of the fridge and placed it on the counter beside the stove. He took his tray of potato wedges and put them in the oven beside the wings. The phone then rang again and Vegas took a glance at the call display again. “Dude, Charity’s dad is calling again.” “Let it ring, I got my hands full.” Vegas mutes the TV for the pending message on the machine. *BEEP* “Killean, Frank here again. Listen I still haven’t heard from the girls. It’s getting late, but gimme a call when and if you hear something.” *BEEP* “You sure nothing’s wrong?” “Yeah, like I said, it was a busy weekend for them and it’s probably running late. It happens.” Killean grabbed the broom and placed it by the fridge to clean up the mess from the broken beer bottle. He first took one tray of wings out of the oven and dusted them lightly with salt and pepper. He then replaced that tray and removed the second tray and drenched them in honey garlic sauce. Just as he was finishing… “INTERCEPTION!!! OH YEAH BABY! HE OWNED YOU!” … he flinched and got honey garlic sauce all over him. He slammed the bottle down on the counter and walked out to the living area with arms out stretched, sauce covering the front of his shirt. “Dude…” Vegas said as he looked at Killean and took a sip of his beer. “You’re a damn messy cook.” ~|o|~ The football game was now over and Killean and Vegas had just finished their dinner. Vegas always liked Killean’s chicken wings and this time was no exception. “Man, those wings were great. I think I ate my weight in them.” Vegas was licking and sucking at his fingers like a madman, trying to remove all the honey garlic sauce that seemed to be stuck like cement to them. Even the napkin was all ripped from sticking to his fingers. “Me too. Want another beer?” “Sure, but I’ll grab them.” The phone once again begins to ring. A full hour and a half after he last called, Charity’s father was calling again. This time, Killean thought the wiser of the situation and actually answered the phone. “Hello? Yeah… wait, what do you mean she’s crying? What’s… Hang on.” He covers the receiver on the phone. “VEGAS! Pick up the phone in the kitchen man. Something’s wrong…” Vegas picks up the phone and listens in to the conversation. Killean: Ok, what’s going on, why is she crying? Frank: I dunno, she said they were doing strange things at the retreat. They were going on about some bible thing and she says they were very pushy. Killean: Pushy? Meanwhile he could hear Betty crying in the background, yelling something about Charity. Killean’s heart sank. Killean: What’s she saying about Charity? Frank: Charity stayed behind, they offered an extended stay and she took it, but Betty was forced to leave by the staff. They said she wasn’t accepting enough or some garbage. Killean: Stayed behind? Why wouldn’t she call? During the conversation, Killean had made his way into the bedroom and was now looking out the window, standing right beside their dresser. He mindlessly bent his leg up and scratched around the electronic bracelet he was wearing. In his mind, he cursed the annoying device. Frank: I dunno what to do. They said they were moving the camp. Killean: What do you mean moving? Where the hell is Charity going? He glanced down at the dresser and noticed a pamphlet sticking out from underneath one of Charity’s jewelry boxes. As Frank was talking to him, he snatched it from the top of the dresser and took a look. The pamphlet was folded in three sections and on the front there was a rather strange image. There was a hill, with a bed of rocks on top and a hooded figure standing at the top. The figures arms were pointed at the sky and a light was shining down on him. A crowd of people are at the bottom, mimicking his acts and also on their knees. The text below the image said… “We’ll help bring you peace and everlasting life.” There was also a strange symbol at the bottom. It suddenly hit him, like a brick to the face it hit him so hard he flinched. Frank: Killean, put this together. Pushy… religious material… you don’t think…? Killean: No fuckin’ way! Don’t even use the word cult around me. S… sorry, didn’t mean to do that. He was flustered. He didn’t know what to say or what to do. He just knew something was wrong… Terrifyingly wrong. Meanwhile, Vegas had put down the phone and didn’t hear the last few lines that Frank and Killean spoke. Something had caught his attention on TV. No, he wasn’t that heartless. Believe it or not, it was relevant to the phone call… sorta. “Killean! Get in here now man! You gotta see this!” Killean was heard marching into the room and was a little distraught at being called at such a bad time to see something on TV. But when he saw the image on the screen, he froze, his eyes opening wide as saucers. “ReVolution 107 saw the forming of a new alliance. Although it was not broadcast with the show, our network got the exclusive video from PRIME. It shows Charity Manale making her way out to the ring just when Jonathon Winters won a tough fatal fourway to become the number one contender to the 5-star Championship held by Chandler Tsonda. Charity made her intentions clear. She wanted to manage Jonathon Winters and would do anything to help him out as you can see by the numerous flirty looks she’s giving him. Eventually Winters shakes her hand and accepts the deal. Thus, a new tandem is formed in PRIME. What will these two have in store for ReVolution 108? Will Jonathon Winters walk out the new 5-Star Champion? Will Charity be the difference maker opposite Ellie? What does this mean for Charity and the incarcerated Killean Sirrajin if anything? Tune in to find out!” Still frozen, Vegas turned back to see the look of shock on Killean’s face. Frank was trying to get Killean’s attention back on the phone but Killean paid no attention. Charity, the love of his life and the person he chose to be Desiree’s mother was managing someone else behind his back? Even worse, joining some traveling bible thumpers intent on doing something really screwed up? A mere few seconds later, Killean appeared to snap out of his trance and dropped the cordless phone to the floor where it broke open and pieces flew across the floor. He managed to form one sentence, a very obvious sign of anger and frustration in his voice. “Just what in the fuck is going on?!”
