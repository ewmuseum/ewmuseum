Trials and Tribulations (JitC Round 1, v. Chainz)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Early November "Blacker than a moonless night. Hotter and more bitter than Hell itself. That is coffee." Coffee was about all Allen Brown wanted right now. He had, for the lack of a better term, been bored. Since leaving PRIME due to what he referred to as a "difference of opinion" between himself and former tag team partner, Coral Avalon, he had been sort of drifting. On top of that, his secondhand video game business that he started years ago had been showing declines in sales, which forced him to also continue to wrestle despite hating it. Though he still worked on the independent scene with the remaining members of the Blue Rogues, he was somewhat itching to find a way to sneak back into PRIME, where the big money was to be had. Allen, known to most people as the Codemaster, was staring down at a future as dark and as bitter as the darkness in his coffee mug. His dream business was showing signs of fading, the business he actually hated and yet still had to take part in wasn't going so well either with all of the miscues he'd been faced with as of late, and he felt like he was floundering. As part of the advertising, the Codemaster was wearing a long-sleeved green shirt underneath a tan vest, with a white tie. He also wore a pair of black pants. Most significantly, he was wearing a strange visor with three red lines on them over his eyes. For the record, yes, he could see out of that visor. I don't know how. "Ha...!" he said. He drank the blackness in his coffee mug, and smelled the ruin in the air. The Codemaster had fallen pretty low. So low, in fact, that he had even agreed to be in one of Beef's crappy C-movies. As in, so bad that it couldn't even be a B-movie. The story went something like this. For Beef's birthday, some months ago, the Codemaster actually went out of his way and bought him a Nintendo DS, and along with it, a video game called "Phoenix Wright: Ace Attorney". In that game, if one was unaware, the player took the role of an attorney named Phoenix Wright. Wright used trickery in the courtroom to pull off miracle comebacks in the courtroom in order to get his clients declared "not guilty". Along the way, he would learn just what it was to be a defense attorney. The person the Codemaster was portraying was Godot, a hotshot prosecutor in the third game who happened to drink a lot of coffee. Like, seventeen cups a trial. The reason why the smell of ruin was in the air was because, at that moment... the defendant's desk on the set of Beef's new movie, "Phoenix Wright: Turnabout King", was engulfed in flames. El Janito, who had been playing the role of Phoenix Wright himself, was dangerously close to being on fire himself, but his survival instincts kicked in and he stopped, dropped, and rolled. However, at the conclusion of the roll, he was compulsively drawn into completing a Rolling Thunder, and thus, he fell on his back. Ouch. Meanwhile, Grundle McMiles, the only man so low on life's totem pole that he was BEEF'S assistant, tried in futility to operate a fire extinguisher. Grundle wasn't retarded by any means, but he had never operated a fire extinguisher before and found himself blasting himself in the face with foam before he could direct the foam at the defense chair. Beef had been frantically shouting cut for a minute, and he was tempted to fire the flames from the production, because it was such a frickin' drama queen. He sighed, threw down his megaphone, and went to his "trailer". In actuality, it was just a bed made out of cardboard and ragged sheets. He missed PRIME because he got money, he had a decent bed, and he could eat all the waffles he could freakin' eat. The Codemaster turned towards Scott Falk, who had been playing the role of Larry Butz. He played the role because Larry always dated hot models, and Scott always plowed hot sisters. So it was kind of the same. Except with a lot more sex on Scott's end. Even if he was just as stupid as Larry was in the game. "Man, how the fuck did we get roped into this?" asked Scott, as he picked at his ear, "Aren't you supposed to be the leader of this outfit?" "Ha...!" went the Codemaster, who still seemed to be "in-character", "To err is human, to forgive, divine. Humans aren't machines, they have souls. They live, they die, they love, they hate, and yes, they even make mistakes." "That would sound fucking profound if I didn't know you were quoting video games." Scott replied, crossing his arms and giving the Codemaster a rather cross look. "Actually, the first bit of it was Alexander Pope." Alexandria Malone, who was playing the part of Maya Fey and therefore was wearing some rather odd clothes more befitting a Japanese shrine maiden than that of a modern teenage girl. She walked away, leaving the Codemaster and Scott Falk to discuss it among themselves. "We make mistakes, Scotty. I made the mistake of letting that guy into the Rogues, you occasionally make the mistake of forgetting to ask some of the girls you have sex with if they're over eighteen." the Codemaster said. "By 'that guy', I assume you're not talking about that guy who's decided to take a nap in that god damn junkyard over there?" Scott asked, gesturing in the direction that Beef was now sleeping and rightfully no-selling the Codemaster's earlier comment. "No, of course not." the Codemaster said. He had yet to remove his visor, and looked a little reluctant to step out of character quite yet. Scott sighed and muttered to himself, "Man, when the bottom falls out... look, forget about Coral and that bitch he's with, alright? You're too fucking good for them, anyway." The Codemaster sighed, and stared longingly at the bottom of his coffee mug, "Maybe." "So, you made a big grand scheme to steal away Annabelle, and Coral was smarter than you thought he was. And he figured out what you were going to do. So what?" Scott asked of his visor-clad friend. "Well, now I can't use him as a pawn in my grand schemes because the next time he sees me..." the Codemaster said, but then he paused to take off his Godot visor and reveal that the area around his left eye had been obviously struck with someone's fist and became quite the shiner, "...He'll probably do more than this." The Codemaster thoughtfully put his visor back in so as to cover his "war wound". He had been telling other people that someone had stiffed him in the independents, but the truth was, he got into a fight with Coral Avalon, his former tag team partner. Though the incident did well to sully Coral's "nice guy" reputation, it also did some damage to the Codemaster's reputation. In fact, the only people who still had any faith left in the guy were the members of the Blue Rogues who had stayed behind... the three members of Mega Job, the two Falk brothers, and Alexandria. He took a swig of his coffee. "You know, I'm supposed to be king of this jungle." the Codemaster said. He gestured all around with his coffee mug, "I'm supposed to know the score, make the plans, take advantage of people for their naiveté, hit people with large heavy objects, and get through life with nothin' but my trickery and deceit. I'm not supposed to get punched in the face for my troubles." Scott sighed and leaned against the prosecutor's desk. He then reached into his pocket and pulled out a folded piece of paper, which he casually handed to the Codemaster, "Maybe this'll help take your mind off that." The Codemaster examined the paper for a bit, and then looked up at Scott, "The Jewel In The Crown tournament? Are you out of your mind?" "A little bit," Scott said, "That paper was never meant to reach you. I got it through Andy Sharp. You remember that fucking Canadian, right?" "We managed him to a World title." the Codemaster said with a smirk. "We did," Scott said, "Well, anyway, don't ask how I got it from him. What I did to get it sounded more like somethin' you'd have done." "You *stole* it." the Codemaster said. "Well, yeah," the older Falk said, with a scratch of his head, "But you've been itching to get yourself back into PRIME somehow, right?" "Little bit, yeah." the Codemaster replied. "This is your shot," Scott said, "So don't fuckin' blow it." The Codemaster thought about it. He had made more than his fair share of enemies while in PRIME, but the times had changed in the promotion as of late. The man who was dressed as Godot simply shrugged and pocketed the note, "I think I'll look into it. You guys will be along for the ride, of course." "We'd fucking better be. You're so fucking lost without me." Scott Falk said, getting back into character. "Ha...!" the Codemaster said, also getting into character... although a little bit different than what Scott was doing a moment ago, "Humans are complex creatures. They want to be selfish loners, but they require the help of other people in order to do what they want to do. They want to be loved, and yet they can hurt the ones they love as well. It's our jobs, as the prosecutors of the guilty in the wrestling world, to take advantage of human complexities and use them for our advantage. That's one of my rules." He took a sip of his coffee. "...Humans are afraid of the dark. And yet... at the same time, we're fascinated and bewitched by it." the Codemaster said with a smirk, "Maybe that's why humans drink the darkness that is coffee." Scott grumbled, "Stop being profound, jackass. You know I'm too stupid to get any of it." "Ha...!" went the Codemaster, with another sip of coffee. Robert Falk walked over to the Codemaster and Scott Falk, wearing black robes. He was wearing a fake beard and he had a gavel in his hand. He was messing with his fake beard, constantly adjusting it, "My beard itches." The Codemaster smirked, "Gonna give a guilty verdict today, Your Honor?" "I may declare this beard guilty," Robert replied. He then paused and looked at the Codemaster, and particularly the piece of paper that was in the Codemaster's left hand (the one not holding a coffee mug), and assumed that Scott had told him already, "So, are you joining that?" The Codemaster, after a smirk and a last sip of coffee, had just one thing to say, "Ha...!" He set down his coffee mug, having finished drinking its black depths, and took off his visor one more time to give Robert Falk a very serious look that was magnified by his "war wound" on his left eye, "You bet your robes I am." He tossed the visor aside, and gave Robert Falk one of those "I'm not even fucking around" looks that he gave whenever he was serious. It almost looked as if there was a glow of red in his eyes, even though that's coming from the fire that still hadn't been put out. and he said very simply: "I will be God of the new world."
