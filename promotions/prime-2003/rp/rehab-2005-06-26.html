Rehab

26 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: June 26th 2005 -6 Months Ago- 'Tell me a little about yourself?' The question was simple and direct. However its recipient struggled with his reply, having led a dual identity for the best part of eight years even he was uncertain as to whom he really was. The last time his angelic face graced a television set he had been hell bent on destroying the life of the man he once idolised, he envied everything about Xavier Kannon and his employment with the now defunct Rush-Pro Wrestling allowed him the opportunity to step out of his shadow. Like an Artic lorry he planned to steam roll over the RPW benefactor's legacy, one paved in gold… But it wasn't to be. Not from the will of trying but the untimely death of Rush-Pro Wrestling. This obsession with the decorated veteran engulfed his body like cancer, a twisted bitterness only witnessed at first hand by Tessa Windsor who was bonded to the monster contractually. His aspirations may have been shattered by the sudden closure of the Dale F. Halton Arena's entranceway but this roadblock in his career was the chemotherapy he deeply craved. 'Well, Mr. Green?' asked the voice with a manly tone. Mr. Green? Having earned a living through a medium of violence and having been away from this scene for what may have felt like an eternity it must've seemed gratifying that someone would utilise a little decorum in his presence. Or was it the wad of Benjamin's he left in the eager hands of his therapist's personal secretary. A pretty little thing, one that would have moistened her cherry red lips and fallen to all fours at the click of his fingers, had he not been prematurely taken from the limelight. Now, she wouldn't look twice unless she required a second glance to digest the fact that Gaz 'The Jester' Green had now been reduced to Gareth, Gareth Green to be exact. How the mighty hath fallen. A phrase so fitting that it could quite easily be a tailored business suit in emerald green. It's a similar case to the Dark Knight. On one side of the coin you have the Bat and his technical wizardry who everyone adores while the other side has Bruce Wayne, millionaire playboy who quite frankly no-one gives a toss about. The same could be said about Gaz and Gareth. 'Tell me a little about yourself?' began the Jester sarcastically. 'I am Gaz Green, alias The Jester!' he continued, however his voice had a level of uncertainty. Even Gareth Green was still trying to fool himself into believing that he was the same person who had exploded onto the wrestling scene all those years ago. Back then Gareth adorned his alter ego Gaz under the persona of The Jester, a cocky, egotistical and self-centred bastard who was only in the business to cater for himself. Three years later and how the tides have changed. 'I am the Emerald Menace, The Prince Of Pranks and King Midas Of Tag. Five time PCW Tag Team Champion and the LAST, I emphasise the LAST reigning PCW Continental Champion…' Quite the resume. Had he been standing on a soapbox people would've taken note and cared but his only recipient was his therapist and he peered right through the charade as though it were a pane of glass. 'I don't dispute that, you WERE everything you said!' retorted the Jester's therapist calm and collectively. There it was, like the final nail in the coffin. He could've used a number of words but instead he made 'WERE' the operative word in his sentence and deep down probably took great joy in verbalising it towards his client. With the closure of Rush-Pro Wrestling and a titanic movie career… titanic as in the infamous ocean liner, the once Emerald Menace had been reduced to performing in a cabaret act along the strip of his native Las Vegas, Nevada. Had it been the grandeur of Tom Jones or Celine Dion then the former Primetime Championship Wrestling starlet wouldn't be seeking help from some pencil pusher but fate had dealt him a different hand. 'Tell me about Gareth Green, not the gimmick Gaz or any other persona you have portrayed during your wrestling days. I want to know about the man himself, his history, and his relationship with his family if there is at-all one, likes and dislikes?' questioned the Jester's therapist. Gaz pondered momentarily before replying, 'Hello, my names Gareth Green, I have medium length brown hair and green eyes. I believe I'm a Leo and thus I enjoy long walks in the park and absorbing the surrounding beauty, golly a stroll through the countryside on a hot summer's day is just super!' Again Gareth Green struggled to differentiate between himself and the monster he unleashed on the business, Gaz 'The Jester' Green. Having tasted a finer life under the guise of a prankster 'Green' wanted desperately to hold onto his floundering fame, even if it meant playing to an audience of one. Not bad for someone who once sold out twenty-thousand plus arenas on name value alone… 'I come from a warm loving family background that encouraged me into sports and…' he continued before being timely interrupted upon. 'Look Mr. Green! You can come here and try to get around the things troubling you and I'll happily kick back while my sandwich and I listen intently for an hour. My alarm will sound after sixty painstaking minutes where I will then usher you through the door with a smile that masks as a frown and collect my reward… sorry, I mean fee!' the therapist shot from the hip down, there was no sugar coating just the same excruciating pain felt from the Soprano, a grinding boot to the groin. 'With that generous wad of dollar bills I'll then check myself into the nearest strip bar and drown my own sorrows. Some piece of talent will then prey on me and I'll relay every sob story I've endured during the day while slipping the occasional Benjamin into her dental floss sized g-string!' Had Gaz verbalised what he was thinking, it be something to the tune of, 'And your complaining?' but the Jester listened and noted. The therapist continued, 'After drinking the waterhole dry and financing the dancers crack habits like a secret benefactor I'll head on home to a wife who hates me and kids with limited conversation skills, 'Like whatever!' and 'Yeah right!' and you think you have problems?' 'So now do you want to tell me about yourself?' Gaz is what makes the world go round or so he thought but apparently there were 'people' out there who had real issues, momentarily an ounce of decency ran through his veins. The Jester either needed a toilet break or twenty solid minutes on a treadmill to work up a sweat in order to flush this virus from his system. As though this spark of decency was a submission hold, the Original Prankster had bare enough and tapped out… 'Okay!' he began. 'I suppose I should start from the moment I opted to join this crazy business we call professional wrestling. Well back in school I was more focused on my studies, but intellect isn't seen as positively as sex appeal. While I was fingering through books, most guys my age were fingering… well you know what I mean. I envied these guys, green by name and green by nature I suppose so I took stock of my life and decided that weights were more benefiting than Shakespeare!' Gaz continued, 'Anyway, after building my muscle mass and finally graduating I was at the point where you asked yourself, 'What's next?' and I decided I wanted exposure, with no training in movies or television well that avenue was a little NTICW, pointless. Wrestling was a viable option and it looked like an adrenaline rush so I opted to take my ass back to school squire, but nothing in this world is free, which I quickly learnt!' 'Oh, this option had it's repercussions. My family were dead against it from the word go, I always thought it was because I was the only child and that they cared for my well being, let's not kid ourselves, the industries has taken many lives over the years. I always thought they wanted to wrap me up in cotton wool but in reality they didn't want me to shun the family name, they looked at the business as though it were mud. It wasn't a case of what I wanted; they wanted a highflying businessman son in a suit, they got the suit all-right and it was green to match the family name!' 'Interesting…' was the only retort the therapist could muster. 'My choice of career resulted in my biggest match to date and the most hurtful; there were no punches traded or physicality for that matter. Just insult and profanity…' Gaz' opening up coincided with another interruption. -Ding. Ding. Ding.- An alarm sounded signalling the end of the session, sixty minutes had elapsed and this insight into the real Gareth Green hadn't even scraped the tip of the iceberg. 'I'm sorry but your time is up!' said the therapist. 'We've taken great strides here today and hopefully we'll continue down this path at our next session. Well that's a wrap as they say in the movies. I'll see you again real soon!' That's a wrap? After paying out all that money, money he no-longer could afford, the only thing the Jester wanted to wrap was the chair he sat on across his therapists head, something amiss in his life for the last few months. Gaz may have been pushed back in the mind of Green but he still was there to pull the strings. That was six months ago and with a contract reuniting the comic and vampire collective P.W.A. back together, the only question left to ask is, 'Can they justify the hype?' well, Team Zambo, Civil Disobedience, The Flying Veromovs and Teh Pwn will find out first hand during this weeks scheduled ReVolution 68.
