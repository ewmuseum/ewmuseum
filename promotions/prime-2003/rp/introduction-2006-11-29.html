Introduction

29 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 29, 2006 They said I should start from the beginning. Unfortunately for me, the beginning is thousands of years ago. So, instead of starting from the very beginning, I’ll start sometime recent. Specifically, a year ago. This book is the first in the series of three that I’ve been commissioned to write. For some reason, the life of a wrestler is intriguing enough to people that they’ll actually pick up books about them. Yet, it could also be the fact that they view me as being mentally insane. For those that are currently left in the dark, let me introduce myself. The name is Malachi and I’m God. No, not in the metaphorical sense but in the literal sense. I am the God that created this Earth, created the skies and the oceans, and created you, the person reading this book. I know that you more than likely don’t believe me, but hopefully when this book is all said and done I shall have changed your mind. The past year for me has been a tumultuous one to be on the safe side. The road I went down was one that frightened even me. To be a God, a deity, or revered one it’s easy to forget where each and every single person comes from. It’s easy to forget that there are so many temptations in the world and I have seen where I’ve failed. However, there are also fields in which you have failed me. It’s a street that is littered with not only garbage but much potential. When I was approached to write this book, I had gone through a rather traumatic and public experience that you may have read about by this point, or else you will go to Google and figure it out now. It’s one that I shall touch upon at the end of this book. I was trying to figure out why you had turned out flawed when I had created you to be perfect, and along the way I did find out why you turned out flaw. Yet, all of my efforts to convince you to go down a different road had backfired in my very face. Instead of being helpful, I had turned condescending. It’s sad to look back upon your times, see that you tried to do good, but you did so much wrong along the way. Wrestling took me in the seedy underbelly of the world and it nearly pushed me to do unimaginable things. Things that were even worse then what I did in the ring. I couldn’t help myself though. I had come down from the Heavens above with such a fury that I couldn’t control it. Only in time would I learn how to deal with that fury and instead of attacking the situation I learned how to help the situation. However, that didn’t happen right away. Instead, I had to experience the life I had created for each and every one of you. It was then that I learned that I had expected too much of you. The first year of my life on Earth brought me to the realities that this world is not the one I had originally envisioned. I take full blame for that as I lost sight of what I truly wanted and eventually grew lazy in my work. Now I know what I have to fix. Now, I know what I have to do to make you perfect. Before that is to happen though, I have to explain myself. I know, for those who believe me, you believe that your God doesn’t have to explain anything. Yet, I have learned from the errors of my ways and how would it look if I expected from you what I could not do? No, this is a story of trials and tribulations that came to an end that was unforeseen when this journey began. This could be viewed as an autobiography, as an inspirational piece of how tough it truly is out there, and as an apology for what the past year. In here will be things that I have to fully explain now that I’ve gone through them. Everything that follows is from my own perspective written in my own words. It is the truth that I speak from my memories. As you read this, remember I did this for you all. I carry my sins and shortcomings on my own shoulders. I have to thank those at PRIME who have supported me throughout my short, but successful career thus far in PRIME. This story may be of the past year, but it’s only the beginning of what’s to come as the life I knew is no longer the life I have. This may be the beginning of my journey, but the end of this road is just the beginning to many more. Malachi ---- An excerpt from the upcoming book, The World Through The Eyes of God, penned by Malachi. To be released in Winter 2007. Posted here with permission from HarperCollins Publishing.
