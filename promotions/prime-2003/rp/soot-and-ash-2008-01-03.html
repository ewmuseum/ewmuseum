Soot and Ash

3 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: January 3, 2008 The flame flickered in the cool night breeze. A macabre and mocking dance. Like a fiery jester putting the punchline to this grand cosmic joke. It flitted upward, licking at the cigarette clinched between his lips. Hungry. Starving. The fire wanted nothing more than to consume. Destroy. It was an unnecessary and unwanted reminder of the circumstances. The flame's unquenchable craving laughed at the crumbling walls. The soot on his fingertips. Amused by the irony of the cruel turn of Fate in which he stood. Addictions being what they are, however, Tyler Rayne tried not to think about all that. He pushed the thoughts from his mind. The sight of the fire itself from his eyes. He only wanted the release. The sweet embrace of nicotine. The embrace would be fleeting, yes. That wouldn't make it any less satisfying. The truth of the matter did not escape him. The spark of this flame would ignite a fire that would eat him from the inside. Consume and blacken his lungs like a similar fire had voraciously consumed the apartment in which he stood. The charred remains of something... a book, he thought, crumbled to ash beneath his boot. "You really think you should be smoking in here?" Tyler's eyebrows raised in amusement. The smile on his face carried little of the usual joviality. The adolescent mirth all but gone from the expression. The slight turn of his lips was more a grim acceptance of the situation. He could do nothing but laugh. The alternative was unacceptable. "What, you afraid I'm going to burn the place down?" Pierce Lavelle laughed. He laughed hard. The somber, blackened tomb in which the two men stood was a void for such things. It was cold and suffocating and infectious. The darkness seeped into the skin. Weighed down on the soul. The room just felt... heavy. As if one were not standing in it as much as supporting it. A metaphorical Atlas to this tiny world that once belonged to Tyler Rayne. The strain was almost unbearable. Anything to break the depression. Even a joke of such poor taste. A joke at all was such stark contrast to the seriousness of events that it put poor Pierce into a right fit. Admittedly, Tyler was glad for it. The one thing he'd always appreciated about Pierce Lavelle was the man's ability to find the silver lining. Even in some of the toughest situations, Pierce could always find something to smile about. It was a trait Tyler admired. Something he wished he possessed a bit more of himself, really. “Story’s set for tomorrow then?” “Going out with the morning edition. Unless you want me to have it pushed back to--“ “No. Sooner or later someone’s going to stumble upon all this, and I’d much rather have you do the reporting than some schmuck smark on a sugar high. Just trying to think of what I tell Lisa when she calls me in the morning.” “Tell her the same thing Dawn told her boss.” “What was that?” “Ninjas!” Tyler laughed. He didn’t want to. It didn’t seem appropriate, given the circumstances… but he did. It felt good. A fleeting feeling. His eyes opened back to a harsh reality. Scorched and peeling walls. The blackened remnants of a couch, contorted metal springs escaping through the melted cushions. The foreboding gloom of something lurking just beyond. Someone. Lurking. Stalking… “I take it that means they’ve got nothing.” “Won’t know for a few days, but it didn’t sound very promising. Whoever did this… they knew what they were doing. “ “They always do.” How many years now? How many wasted days fighting shadows in the dark? Running. Constantly running, just to stay one step ahead of the next mysterious beast nipping at his heels. It seemed a lifetime. Hell, it was a lifetime. A lifetime of restless nights. Always sleeping with one eye open. Casting paranoid glances over his shoulder. Watching the mirrors for suspicious-looking strangers. Every reflective surface hiding a stalker. A menace. A foe. Everywhere. Around every corner. Every dark, dank little corner of this globe. Always waiting. Always lurking. Ready to strike. Ready to kill. “Heard about the JitC. That’s a tough loss. Snow’s good, though. No shame in--“ “Great, actually. Almost as talented as me, the lucky bastard.” “Almost?” He was a good friend. Pierce. A damn good friend. Tyler was flashing that trademark smile before he realized it. His confidence and swagger returning for a brief moment to answer the question with the most obvious of Rayne answers. “Almost.” “What now? In PRIME, I mean.” His gaze wanders toward the door. It’s hard not to stop. Not to get caught up in the memories. The twisted frame of his favorite poster. The collapsed and broken remnants of his trophy shelf. The melded mass of goo that was once a Wiimote. Former fragments of times gone by. Nights he’d all but forgotten. Binges he’d hoped to forget. All of them inconsequential in the larger scheme. Now, though, in this barren waste of a former residence… they all seemed to impress a new meaning. As if in their destruction, in their near non-existence, these memories had gained some sort of weight. Puzzle pieces he only missed now that they were gone. It was hard to ignore them. It was hard not to get lost in them. He found a way. His eyes found a way beyond the disaster. Found a way to the door. To the woman outside. Bundled tight, her red hair billowing in another gust of wind. Lindsay Troy. He watched her. He stared at her as she stared off into something farther. Something beyond. Lost in the lights. The city itself. Lost in her own sudden rush of memories. He stared at her and, even in the slightest, the room brightened. Just a little bit. A bit was enough. “I really don’t know. Everyone seems to think I’m flat on my ass now. Two losses and suddenly I don’t have what it takes anymore. I… I haven’t really decided what the next move is. With everything going on, I haven’t really had time to dwell on it.” Once again, the blackness seemed to swarm around them. The depression of tragedy. Ash rising up to fill his lungs. The tattered ceiling collapsing to bury him beneath. The walls imploded. The floor surged. The entire room swelling in upon him. Suffocating. “Well, look at the bright side.” “What’s that?” “At least you can find a place in a better neighborhood now!” “You got one in mind?” “I could think of a few. Is she, uh… is she going to be alright?” “Yeah. She’s just… New York’s not her favorite place in the world. ‘Sides, I don’t think this quite qualifies as a romantic evening on the town.” “Was she there? When the other one…” “Blew up?” “Yeah.” “No. Just me. Me and a lot of very precious things. Things. They’re just things. Fuck it. They were my things.” “And now they’re gone.” “Now they’re gone.” “I’m sorry, Tyler. It may not mean much, but I’m sorry.” “Thanks, kid. I think more than anything, I hate sitting around and waiting for the next one.” “Next one?” “A week ago my place in Cali explodes. Shit, I was walking in the gods damned back door. Boom. Gone. Just… gone. Now I’m sittin’ in Boston and you call, tell me this place is extra crispy. I don’t… didn’t… even use this place that much. Just when we came through on tours. When I wasn’t up at the Manor. Thought maybe the Cali thing was a botched job. Assassination. Now… someone’s just trying to smoke me out. Burn it all out ‘til I’ve got nowhere left to hide.” “Maybe that guy. The one. You know. The guy. The one you told me about.” “Se7yn?” “Yeah.” “Maybe.” His pocket vibrates before the tone can ring loud and true from the phone. Duel of the Fates. “Now’s not really a good time, Katt. … … … You’ve got to be fucking me. … … … I can’t-- … … When did it happen? … … Fuck. … Yeah. … … I’ll be there. … … No. Go to Japan, will ya? I want someone there with Kai. … … Thanks, Katt. … … I’ll see you soon.” Silence filled the void. The quiet was nearly as devastating as the truth. “Another one. In Seattle. Fuck. That’s three. Three of my fucking… FUCK!!” “Ty…” Lindsay Troy was no longer lost in thought. An exclamation like that is enough to pull anyone from reverie. “Well, you said you hated New York. Good news for you. We’re going to Seattle.” “Another one?” “Another one.” "Christ, Tyler, it's a small wonder I can keep track of my two homes and all the things that come along with them. How many abodes do you own?" “Things keep going like this, you just might find out. Pierce, if Dawn hears anything…” “I’ll call you immediately.” “Thanks, kid. For everything.” “No problem. Just… be careful. Someone is definitely out to get you.” The smile. The patented Tyler Rayne smile, complete with a generous helping of undeniable charm. Confidence. It was all coming back. It had to. He couldn’t let this bring him down. This was vicious. An angry, vicious tempest. But he had weathered storms before. He would weather this as well. He had no choice. Someone is definitely out to get you. “What else is new?”
