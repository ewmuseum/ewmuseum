Brave/Liar. One In The Same.

25 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 25th, 2008 "I knee'd her right in the titties, man!" Rhett screamed. He was sitting in Cluster's apartment. Cluster the Big, Black Drug Dealer and Rhett's next-door-neighbor. He looked like he'd been beaten to a bloody pulp. In all actuality, he had been. Cluster couldn't stop laughing. "I'm serious Cluster! The titties! ...He's pissed." Cluster shrugged his shoulders. He was puffing on a blunt. After coughing out quite a bit of smoke he offered, "I know. I saw dat shit on T.V." He hit the unnecessarily large philly and passed it to Rhett. Rhett puffed, he seemed like he was in a dilemma. "I don't know WHAT the fuck I'm gonna do, Cluster. I feel fucking bad. That bitch could've had a miscarriage or something, you know?" Cluster laughed, "Man you're like, 'I feel so bad' and shit, then you call that bitch a bitch. Ahahaha!" Rhett couldn't help but smile. But a blunt of hydroponic marijuana can make nearly anyone laugh about some fucked up shit. ------------------------------- "I'm going to kill that motherfucker." Maybe famous last words. The truth was he wasn't sure whether or not Bryan was going to run him over when he peeled out of the parking lot. He felt his face and felt part of his pigment challenged skin that had peeled off. He drug his hand down his features. "Fuck!" Glass was sticking out of his scalp and face and it had now dug deep into his palm. Not to mention blood soaking his flesh, making it a damp, pink shade on an otherwise ghost white tone. Dawkins really worked a number on the Albino and promised more of the same next week. He leaned against the car he was thrown through, digging in the pockets of his shorts. A Camel Turkish Gold cigarette was removed from the packaging and placed in his mouth. A blue bic is struck and he inhales. Locke chuckled a little. He had too. An asswhooping was just packaged, handled and delivered to him via the Flyin' Hawaiian. Which is precisely what Rhett had planned to do to him. But the only thing he accomplished was knocking the Love of Dawkins' Life unconscious, on ACCIDENT. But that had apparently lit a fuse in the docile, pineapple eatin' asshole, Rhett noticed. A kind of fury that he hadn't ever expected out of the young guy. He had only taken that kind of beating from one other person in his entire life. Graphic Violence, in their infamous feud at Old School Wrestling. Rhett still owed on Hospital Bills from that one. It was also the second time he'd ever felt a particularly feeling he was not friends with... Fear. He was afraid. Afraid of fucking Bryan Dawkins. How pitiful! How pathetic! That Fruit Eating Bastard? Really? Really. Maybe he was too old for this. 30 years old and having his head slammed through passenger side glass. He thought he'd wrestle a few matches, win a championship or two. Now he was in what was no doubt the 2nd blood feud he'd ever been involved with. It hadn't reached that point yet, but all the pieces were there. "I sware on my life, bruh. If you live through this, I'm gonna make sure your life is HELL as long as you're walkin' the earth." What scared Rhett the most was that he knew Dawkins meant it. ------------------------------- Cluster ashed what was basically the end of a wrap. He exhaled the final bit of smoke left in his lungs. On the coffee table, buds of marijuana had been broken up and he used that to fill the next one. The scent of a blueberry blunt wrap hung high in the air, mixing with the high quality bud that had filled the room with a haze of grey. It was truly a wonderful scent. Cluster inquired as he rolled, "So what are you gonna do about it?" Rhett's eyes were more red and bloodshot than usual, "Hmm?" "Well, you gotta have a game plan man. You gotta get your rug back." "Rug?" Rhett asked. He wasn't quite attentive or smart when he was so high his vision was blurry. Cluster responded, "Yeah man. Cuz you the Dude." "The Dude?" Cluster sighed, "You know. Like the Big Lebowski?" Rhett caught on and coughed out a laugh, "I'm the Dude and I need to get my rug? What the fuck? Are you high? Don't answer that! But seriously, I didn't know you like Big Lebowski." "Yeah, that shit's funny as a muhfucka. Why? What else would I watch?" "I dunno. Friday?" Rhett offered. Seemed a reasonable answer to him. "Why, cause I'm black?" "...Well, yeah." "Fuck you, bitch! ...Yeah, I like Friday too. But that don't mean shit though..." Rhett pointed and laughed, holding his stomach with the other hand as he leaned back. It killed him to laugh at that point in time. Seriously, it fucking hurt and it was written all over his face. A laugh accompanied by an intense grimace and squinted eyes filled with salt water. Cluster continued, "But yeah, you like the Dude dawg. I thought about that shit a long time ago. You and The Dude man, y'all constantly gettin' fucked. Y'know, you gotta way of life and shit. Y'all don't need much to get by. Yeah, it's nice to have a lot of nice shit-- but y'all don't particularly care to have it all. Y'all just wanna do your thing. But people don't like it, you know, when you be yourself. So you get fucked. He got his rug pissed on and so did you. Only yo' rug is yo' courage. Yo' gut. And I'd metaphorically relate the piss to the beatin you took, but he prolly pissed on you too, shit. I aint even gonna lie, you got fucked up the other day." "Cluster, I'm well aware of how my ass was beat the other day. So... what fuckin' point are you trying to make anyway!? Jesus Fuck." "Well dawg, you know... when that guy told the Dude off, the Dude got him a new rug." "So you want me to go get a new face? Thanks, I appreciate it." Cluster shook his head as he finished the blunt. It was a process, a delicate one that he took time and care with. To him, the blunts he rolled demanded respect. Cluster never had much to take pride in. But his rolling skills were top notch and if you said otherwise you got your mouth busted. Bottom line. "Naw man... naw. Don't get a new face. Getcha balls back, kid. Getcha nutsack back and show that piece of muh-tha-fuh-kin trash that you aint TAKIN' NO FUCKIN' SHIT man, you know? I mean shit, muhfucka takes my head and slams it through a window!? Muhfucka's gettin shot right in the face, forreal. I'd take that piece on that table over there," he pointed to a 9mm gun on the kitchen counter, viewable from anywhere in that apartment. "And I'd put a clip right in that motherfuckers forehead. Pow." Rhett laughed at Cluster, "Man, I can't shoot him. Does every drug dealing black guy in Atlanta carry guns and shoot people in the forehead?" "Nah nigga, just me." Almost on cue, gunshots. powpowpowpowpowpow! powpowpowpow! powpowpowpowpowpow! Tires screeched. The Apartment was lit up. By the time the first three shots had been fired Cluster had ducked. Rhett had not moved a muscle. Was it shock? Whatever it was he still sat their frozen in time. Cluster got up from the couch. He looked at Rhett. Locke had not even trembled. He was just staring at the ground. Absolutely. Petrified. Cluster casually got back on the couch and lit up the blunt. "Man, I gotta find out who's doing that shit. Rhett? Rhettimus Pussyus?" Locke was still. "RHETT!" Locke jumped and turned to Cluster. Cluster laughed, "What the fuck is wrong with you, boy? I know that aint the first time you've ever been shot at! ...and you're actin' like a bitch." "Whatever man. I had my ass beat and was thinkin', and then the gun--" "Wah wah wah. Shut the fuck up, baby, and hit this blunt." Rhett took it and puffed while Cluster sat their and talked shit to himself about how much of a bitch Rhett is. "So you aren't going to do anything about that?" Cluster glanced at Rhett. "What?" Rhett hit it again before he passed it. Exhale, wheezy speech. "Find out who it was and take them out, I mean." "What the fuck do you think this is, nigga? Grand Theft Auto?" Rhett laughed, "I'm just saying... we should do something about this." "Oh, Mr. I'm So Scared I Can't Fuckin' Move A Muscle over here wants to "do something about this", eh?" Cluster scoffed. Rhett made a promise, "Man... You know I have to go soon. But by the time I get back, if you find out who it was, I'll help you get rid of them." Cluster thought for a second. "...Alright then. Thas was sup." Locke nodded, "Cool then, I'm gonna go." Rhett stood up and started to make his leave. He still needed to change and take care of some errands before catching a flight-- which was getting increasingly extensive. "Oh and Rhett," Cluster said. "Don't come back here withoutcha guts, man. Getcha rug back, you know?" "...Okay." "I'm serious." "I know." With that, Rhett was out the screen door. He thought about Dawkins. A shiver went down his spine. The intensity that Bryan had during that whole debacle was uncanny, and Rhett wasn't even sure he could match it. He'd be coming for him... he had to be prepared. He had to be a step ahead. He had to get brave. And he didn't have it in him, he didn't think. But he'd lie. Lie to everyone but especially himself. Just like he always did. If you tell a lie enough eventually it blurs with truth and they can't be seperated, or recognized as two different things. So he'd be Rhett the Brave. But he'd really be Rhett the Liar. So the lie is now my weapon. Like a bush dried withered in the sun. With this spark I'll go up in flames. I'd lay out my guts but they're too small to see. It's kinda cute how I pretend to be everything but the instrument I am. ... I'm just a bad actor, stuck with a shitty script. -- Let It Enfold You by Senses Fail
