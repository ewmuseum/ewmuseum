The Pegasus Chronicles: A Breeze of Conformity

4 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4th 12:16 A.M. EST ”This is what happens, when we try to become something we are not… This is the result, of a lost goal in the breeze of our reality… This is our awakening to another dawn, as the world deciphers our ignorance… This is… our final conformity, before our luck vanishes. In these hours, I appreciate the things I lost. I appreciate the things I never had. And most important of all, I appreciate the things I have left in life… My friends. Let there be rest to the fallen in life, and let life pursue freely.” Church bells ring, as a statue of the holy lord comes into view. Hung upon his sacrificial cross in a golden template of remembrance, the sanctuary to many Christians is seen. Walking through the entrance doors, two fairly large statues of bowls representing holy water changed into wine is seen. In one is seen water, in the other are leaves of purple color, tinting the water a light shade of purple for effect. ”It’s never been my favorite place to be, but now I suppose theirs no choice,” are words wondering in the mind of the Nemesis Warrior. His eyes shaded by a pair of sleek black sport sunglasses. Tugging his blank black t-shirt from his chest to allow some air circulation. Walking the aisle to the front of the church, nearest the podium to which most sermons are given, Jesse goes to the first row. Crouching down to a knee, he does the cross motion with his hand to his chest, before finally taking a seat. ”Dear lord, I come to you today for a simple wish… eh, sorry, I suppose everyone says that, but truthfully, I ask for your help, and in this hour I need it more than any other,” Jesse says before sighing,”I come here for a favor, if you will. My long time friend has been taken amongst an enemy of mine, unknown to me as of now, but soon their identities will be revealed. They wish to do harm to him shall I mess up. I only ask, in this time of need, your utmost support to ensure he is fine and goes back home when this is all done. Please lord, grant me this… and I shall confess to all sins I’ve ever committed.” These words echo like the church bell itself in his mind. For a long time had the Nemesis Warrior pledged his hate towards the sacrificial side of life, the side that made a meaning out of things that we did not see nor hear, but only believed in during our times of desperate hope. Now though, he wasn’t asking for help for himself, but giving his life’s confession in return for the safety of his friend. Nothing was a truer testament of friendship than this, for in return for support, Jesse was selling his soul to the devil by letting out the secrets of his life. ”And…” but no words followed. Suddenly, the Nemesis Warrior drew blanks, or was it something else? ”Maybe this isn’t what I needed to do. Truth be known, I’ve never been in the presences of god except to attend the funerals of those I loved most. What difference is that to this time in age, where I stand here waiting to drop to my knees and kill any chance of me making it to heaven, for a friend… I’m not greedy, but damn it, I’m putting my trust in the hands of someone who has never given me anything in return but ability to conquer.” Looking up at the cross in the middle of the wall above the podium, Jesse’s eyes flicker, as his mind races with doubts and misconception of his true purpose of being here. Shaking his head, he seems to have made his mind up, but on what grounds? Why was it so hard to pray for something, especially of the man who seemed to live a life of equality and balance? What was holding him back from bowing to God and asking for help? Just what? ”It’s not that I don’t believe, no that’s not it at all. It’s the fact that the only thing I’ve done for God in my time on earth, has been to bury the innocent for my own survival. It has not been to sacrifice myself as he did, to save others, but to kill others in attempt to save myself. Greedy? Yes, but also instinct. I can’t subject myself to dying for a fight I neither started nor believed in. To those who think different of my view, I can only relate to them by asking, “What would you do?” says Jesse, not spoken, but in thought, as Jesse peers to the floor with his eyes blinking in sync. ”When you have slaughtered the son of a Minister, devoured the heart of a demon with your own petty thievery and tricks, as well survived by murder of others. See that’s when you have no right to live or pray to someone you obviously respect little of. So to that I apologize for wasting the lord’s time in listening to my meaningless prayer. I bid you farewell father, and I hope one day my respect towards you grows. I hope you see me for who has renewed this warrior’s soul, and not that of who tainted it,” he rambles on in his head as though he can telepathically talk to God himself. As he goes to leave, he stands up and does the cross on his chest with his hand before saying, ”Amen.” Walking the aisle, he exits the church, and steps to the steps inhaling with purpose. Smelling the air, his body lets out a deep exhale, and then he sits down, just staring at the steps in front of him leading to the street. ”I don’t know the true thing to do. Following my heart has never lead to anything constructive, but it may be my only real option. All the rest seem like failures waiting to happen, even if following the heart also has the same reflection, it may just surprise me and not… Let’s hope so, for Mick’s sake, and for the sake of the Warrior’s in which are fighting this war with me… I will not rest till the message is passed, till the day a warrior walks amongst men as not equal but superior. I shall be the reason that life for our kind survives, and the race of warriors grows… That is my goal, and I will not fail… Not now, not ever…” His eyes close as he pulls out a cigarette and lights it, blowing the smoke, as the skies weary shades grow, and a glooming sky shadows the light. Peering off to the street, his vision blurs, and only thoughts race, as the Nemesis Warrior ponders on his next steps towards rescuing Mick, as well passing on his message. To what extent will he go? What makes him so sure that he will not fail? Is their more to this than what meets the eye, or is it all a fix? Only time shall tell…
