No Celebrations

13 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-13-05 Revolution 57 was a night that should have been all about celebration and big business. Instead it was tarnished by the acts of one evil man. Angelo Deville. The Devil Himself. Defeating two PCW legends, forming the greatest collection of talent ever in the history of PRIME, and making people look foolish were all paled. They were all tainted and ruined by his sickness. A deadly sickness. There was certainly no wine and women for Los Diablos after ReVolution on this night. There was a matter of life and death to worry about instead. 'I'll kill him,' came the thunderous voice that could be from only one soul. 'Baltasar, my boy, calm down,' the slightly calmer, yet clearly still rattled, voice of Enrique Morales chimed in. He had been trying to calm the beast that was flowing out of The Red Devil for the better part of the last two hours. 'Calm down? Calm down? Homes, I won't be calming down any time soon. Should, for any reason, that gringo doctor come out here and give me bad news about my best friend...well...all I can say is God have mercy on Deville's rotting soul. Cause I damn sure won't,' Baltasar blasted back as he slammed his right forearm against one of the waiting room walls. The wall, which aren't exactly steel you know, smashed in and left a giant hole where his forearm had slammed through it. Enrique quickly wrapped an arm around the raging giant to try and console him. 'Believe me, my friend, I know exactly how you feel. That essa, Deville, will get what is coming to him. You better believe that. Right now we need to worry more about Angelus than Deville though,' Morales said with the most calming manner possible. Finally the Red Devil, calmed, and sat down in a seat next to the Los Diablos manager. 'Your right Senor Morales. Tonight we will worry about Angelus and his health. Next week I'll worry about Deville and his fate,' Baltasar replied with fire dancing in his icy blue eyes and a slam of his left fist into the palm of his gloved right hand. In the rush to the hospital the men hadn't even taken the time to change out of their wrestling gear. The fear that waiting around to announce the forming of The Imperium, at the fighting requests of Angelus himself, might have caused their friend to be in even greater danger. 'You will worry about Deville? What do you mean by that,' Morales questioned with a raised eyebrow. Clearly he already knew the answer to his own question, but he was attempting to prove he was still the man to make decisions for the well fare of the team. He knew, however, in a moment like this he wouldn't be convincing Baltasar of anything different, nor did he plan to try. 'You heard me right homes. I'm going to take care of Deville personally this week on ReVolution. You are going to be at home making sure our friend recovers fully,' Baltasar answered with complete authority and cold demenour. Never before had Morales seen The Red Devil so determined in their five or six years together. The sight actually caused him to take a very deep breath. Suddenly the two jumped to their feet as the waiting room door opened and in walked the tall, skinny, gray haired doctor who had been running tests on Angelus. He had a semi warm smile on his face as he walked up to the two Los Diablos members. 'Angelus is resting comfortably in his own room on the next floor up, room 421. He will have to stay here for the next couple of nights for close observations, but all should be alright,' the middle aged doctor began to inform the two men. 'How long will he have to sit out of wrestling,' asked Morales with his arms to his sides and a look of relief on his face. 'At least for ten days. Clearly he won't be cleared for this upcoming week, that you mentioned, and I would recommened that he not wrestle the following week either. But we'll evaluate him closer to that time and I'll let you know that answer then,' the kind doctor, named Robert Jones, answered as he cleaned his glasses with a cloth. 'My friend is going to be alright then, gringo,' Baltasar asked in order to make absolutely sure that the doctor had given him all the information. 'Baring any serious relapse at this point, I'd say you go him here just in time. He'll be fine,' the good doctor replied while shaking Morales hand and smiling. 'Good. I've got a request for you though,' Baltasar said with a tone that would make your skin crawl. The doctor looked at him, some what confused, and asked, 'What would that be?' 'Keep his bed open and warm. Next week you'll have someone to take his place in it,' Baltasar began as he headed toward the door, before finishing his statement in a cold blooded tone, 'And that man won't be leaving after just a couple of days.'
