The letter

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Today A man sitting all alone at his desk is sitting calmly with minimal motions. He is not showing any emotion and is looking straight down. The man is writing vigorously, he might not be showing any physical emotion but he is placing all of his emotion down on paper. The man sitting there is PRIME wrestler Peter Vetra, the man who has seen more up and downs with in the last two to three weeks then a rollercoaster sees. The note he is writing goes something like this; Dear Melissa, Sorry we were unable to meet last time I was in town. Currently I am staying in Seattle, Washington. I am in the area for the next couple of days then I am on my way back to good ole Massachusetts, where I hope to meet up with you, this time in a more casual and enjoying situation. The actual meaning of this letter is not to ask you out on another date. I have enclosed a single ticket to the event being held at the Fleet Center next week. We are having holding Ultra Violence next week and I am unable to have any relative come to my event. I was hoping you would accompany me to the event watch my match and other matches of the evening and then you and I can go out after words. I hope you accept and I will get in touch with you once I have landed back in Norwood. Call me anytime. Sincerely, Peter Vetra Peter Vetra has just placed a lot of emotion on to this letter for this girl he barely even knows. The one thing he is certain about is he has an attraction to her and not just a simple physical attraction, they are connecting intellectually. As he looks up you can see the look of distress in his face, he grabs the paper and looks at it once time, just before he goes to crumple it he decides not to. He takes a final glance and then places it into the envelop that was next to him. Licking it and taking a deep sigh he looks at it with a grave emotion that he is going out on a line here, hoping he has the feelings for him that she has for him. With this letter he has put a lot on the line, he may have just put his final chance at a relationship in this letter. Will it occur? He can only wait and hope.
