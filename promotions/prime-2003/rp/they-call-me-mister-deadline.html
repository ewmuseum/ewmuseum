They Call Me MISTER DEADLINE!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now Darkness. A blue-tinged room, Cheetos bags scattered across the floor. The curtains are drawn shut. Upon the presidential mahogany colored couch, a body loafs in the horizontal position. The long-haired man stares at the television, watching the fifth episode on Sci-Fi channel's marathon of Firefly in honor of the movie that did so well at the box office that it was better of staying out of people's minds. Stepping into this filthy room, with pizza boxes stacked in the corner and dirty clothes littering the area, he huffed in disgust. 'He should be better than this,' the trenchcoated man thought as he took care to avoid the wet areas of the carpet (given that there were porn magazines on the side of the loveseat, one wasn't willing to risk that those stains were simply spilled drinks). Every explosion on screen for the action scene registered on his shiny black flood boots, every joke reminding him why Joss Whedon needs to get kicked in the nuts. 'What are you doing?' he asked of the 6'4' PRIME tag team champion lounging back with a pint of Haagen-Dasz chocolate and walnut ice cream in his hands. Vangellus Oxios, hair screaming out in agony for shampoo and a comb, held up a spoon and replied, 'Making weight. Gotta put on some pounds before my match.' The spoon got slapped out of Oxios's hand as the brown hat comes off of the standing man's head, revealing Justin Williamson, the trainer (I can't say former because he never left or was fired, though he's been absent from all material concerning them for the past few shows because I, dear people, am a lazy ass) of one half of only two tag teams in the whole federation of PRIME (whose dedication to the division is on par with Ann Coulter's dedication to the neoliberal agenda) stares quite angrily at his trainee, in the way that John Cena did earlier tonight when he bashed Chris Masters's left leg with a chair, twice, because he's gangsta like that and was angry at being so exploited and victimized throughout the entire match because it was part of Eric Bischoff's plan to... well, do something (not that I know since I got home late, what with these graduate courses all being at night because whether or not we've got jobs, the professors can't be arsed to fit them in at any other time of day) even though it always ends up backfiring, much like Wile E. Coyote's orders from the ACME company, and then we're all sitting there going I wonder what else is on tonight? and turn the channel to find out that Monday Night Football is much more interesting and the wor-- STOP! GRAMMAR TIME! Let it be known far and wide that we will never be able to escape the legacy of Dillon. NEVER! Anyway. 'Why didn't you show up for training last week?' Williamson asked of his trainee. Vangellus Oxios was making headway in his training, but for only doing it once a week just before the shows, missing a week is not a luxury that Van can afford. Vangellus shrugs his shoulders. 'It was Thanksgiving. Don't you spend any time with family or anything?' The answer: 'Families are for the weak!' A chilled silence came over the room. Tumbleweed rolls by. And somewhere in a far off land, only Montgomery Burns is nodding his head in agreement and understanding. 'Okay, but last week was my off week,' Oxios said. Williamson countered, 'A perfect time to train while you weren't booked to a match. Hell, even Matthews was able to pry himself away from his family in order to wrestle.' A burp from Vangellus. 'Yeah, but he needs the money.' Randomly, quite out of the blue, a woman walked into the picture. A beautiful redhead that we've never seen before (rather, that you've had described... after all, it's not like this is on camera or anything _>) is introduced. Justin Williamson has never met her before. Vangellus Oxios has never met her before. Her appearance here is a complete mystery to us. Only more mysterious, then, is her behavior. Stepping up to the tag team champion, she socked him right in the face. Without a word, she left. Just like that. Both Oxios and Williamson were in shock. Then, Vangellus got up from the couch after pulling the old Zach Morris Freeze! routine, then walked off of the scene to a black guy sitting at a computer while watching a rerun of Mind of Mencia. Oxios smacked him in the back of the head. 'Please, PLEASE! PCW is dead. And even if you do reference it, do something a little more notable than some esoteric reference to one of The Multiple Man's flashes.' The black man raised an eyebrow, though much less cooler than when The Rock does it. 'Flashes? They still use that word?' 'Well, he did at the time that he posted it way back when.' 'Wow, you learn something everyday.' Rob Lowe and Selma Blair (I think that's her name... the one who played the pyrokinetic in Hellboy) attempted to muscle their way into the scene outside of the fourth wall, which is still within the written convention capable to interact with because we're working in a tesseract here. Along with them came a bigass sign that reads, 'The More You Know.' Vangellus whipped out a Glock .40 and points it at them. 'Niggas, BACK UP! GET TA STEPPIN'!' The three (yes, even the sign), backed away slowly, making no sudden movements. Soon, they were out of sight. The black guy said, 'Hey, Van, you'd better get back into scene and character before people forget what was happening in the first place.' Oxios nodded. 'Good call, yo.' Michael Jordan shouted from a few weeks back, 'You're not black!' Vangellus returned, 'Shut the hell up!' Stepping back into the living room, Vangellus unfreezed the scene. I don't remember how Zach Morris did that. I assume it involved him snapping his fingers like Fonzi. Williamson, who picked the conversation back up from where he was left off, nagged, 'And you're underpaying your whores. You're slacking on all fronts.' And some guy pressed up against Van's window from outside, not seen through the curtains, and called into the house, 'And you're still not black!' The television had cut to a commercial. Kato Cailin was impressing upon the viewer the starmaking factor that comes with The Power of 4. Oxios complained, 'Apparently it isn't enough that you can't watch a single program On Demand from Comcast without having to watch that, now it's invading the Sci-Fi Network. Screw you, Schick Quattro. Screw you and the horse you rode in on.' Off to the side, Alan Ackart licks his lips. 'Scratch that last one.' Justin Williamson was rubbing his temples, trying not to go ballistic on Vangellus. 'LISTEN,' Williamson, himself a former champion on multiple levels in multiple federations, 'you're slipping up. There's only one other team to challenge for the tag team titles, but you will be facing them again soon. And I assume that you're still going to be putting together random opponents to challenge you so you need to work out, work on your wrestling ability, and work on your team with Scott. When you're on, you're an effective brawler, but you need more than that. Especially this week.' Vangellus looks (got-dernit... I keep writing this stuff in present-tense when I'm not trying to; but it's too much work to go back and edit, so I'll just let it slide this time) at his calendar. November 28th. He's in Windsor, Ontario, Canada, not at all expecting to go anywhere tomorrow. 'Why?' Van asked. 'What's happening this week?' A questioned expression on Justin's face. 'What? You can't be serious. You've got a match tomorrow against Vangelus Olsig!' Vangellus attempted to slap Justin in the face with his right hand, but it was blocked. Left hand, also blocked. Again with the right hand, this time it connected because a third hand appeared out of nowhere. And don't worry, Leslie Neilsen, the check is already in the mail. Oxios's mini-tirade went like this. 'That name will NOT be used in my house! He started as Dani Fuhrer, he will be known as Dani Fuhrer. He only changed his name to ride my coattails to fame!' And following this, he took in two big spoonfuls of ice cream. Williamson shrugged as the woman in the red dress morphed into a bunch of butterflies, signaling (somehow) that the show was back on. 'He was in PRIME first, and he's had more success and recognition within the wrestling world than you. You can't keep harping on that.' Vangellus points to his white T-shirt, which has his signature blue dragon logo emblazoned upon it. We'll ignore the stains of pizza sauce around the bottom left where he used it as a towel. 'Who's got his own clothing line? Me. Who's got a clandestine organization funding his every activity? Me. Who routinely goes to court to face murder charges by the estate of Kay and Faber? Wait... who routinely goes to court for those charges and actually has heat with the fans? Me.' Oxios does a random pelvic thrust in the direction of Reader Hell, which might as well be Forsaken's hometown they visit there so much. 'Your stupidity aside,' Justin brought everything back to the immediate scene, 'you've got a match against him. And I don't need to remind you that he won a rather one-sided match against your own tag team partner the week before King of Kings. And why did he win? Because Scott didn't prepare for the contest. You can't make that same mistake.' Van formed a sly grin across his face. 'Oh, can't I?' Williamson was getting angry. 'Okay, you CAN, jackass. But I refuse to let you. Every bad performance you give while under my tutelage reflects poorly on me as a trainer.' Oxios rolled his eyes. 'You'd think that after only two of your PWA students managed to do anything with themselves that you'd be used to that right now.' That's right, if your name is Ken Kaze or Skylar Green, you are the HIT's only success story as a wrestling teacher. Sad, ain't it? 'And anyway,' Van continued, 'I couldn't possibly have a match tomorrow. It's a Monday.' Again, rubbing the temples. Williamson huffed, 'What. The. Hell? It's Wednesday tomorrow!' Oxios pointed to the Firefighters of Windsor calendar hanging from his wall. Yes, there are all males on it. 'No, it says it right there, tomorrow's Monday.' Justin approached and examined the calendar. Aside from the hot, top-bare men, he noticed something seriously wrong with it in only a fraction of a second. 'This calendar is for the year 2004.' Vangellus scratched his head. 'Oh yeah. I should've figured that out after I paid some bills late. But that was just such a good year, you know?' An exaggerated sweat bead, anime-style, appeared on Williamson's head. Oxios pointed at it. 'Dude, what the hell is that?' Justin noticed it, then swatted it off of his head. 'Gah! What the hell was that thing?' The black guy off to the side, the not-Michael-Jordan one, said, 'My bad. Overactive imagination.' A knock at the door. Vangellus said, 'Okay, everybody, hold your thoughts.' Oxios got up off the couch, with Williamson shielding his eyes due to the fact that Van wasn't wearing any pants or underwear (at least his closed legs on the couch covered him enough). Stepping over to the door, Van opens it. Standing outside is a cameraman in full rain gear, equipment ready, wearing a small decal on his rain parka that reads ASFA. Vangellus is perplexed by the visit. 'Hey there,' the bearded Mongolian cameraman greeted the pantsless tag team champion. 'I'm here from--' '-- the Asian Shoot Fighting Association.' Vangellus finished. 'I know the acronym. What are you doing here?' The explanation from the techie was, 'I'm here to record your promo for your fight against Iwata Ichiro.' Oxios scratched his balls. 'That fight was four years ago. I remember, I called for you people to send out a camera so I could do a promo, but you never came. Where were you guys?' The camera operator shrugged. 'You have any idea how long it takes for someone to get out of Mongolia?' Back in the living room, Williamson shouted, 'For the love of God, man, put on some pants!' Outside in the rain, Ms. Billingsworth was walking her dog. She replied, 'No, it's okay.' And Mr. Billingsworth across the street added, 'Yeah, no problems here.' Justin stepped up through the other room and slammed the door in the Mongolian guy's face. He has been shamed, and must now be trampled by horses upon his ignominious return to his home village. But that's none of our concern. The trainer admonished his student. 'Look, kid, though it boggles the mind, you are a champion. And it's about time you started acting like it. You've got to change your attitude and you've got to shape up your work. You may not have noticed, but you're not in some bush-league like RPW. This is the majors now; even the heelish pricks in the place can maintain a certain level of professionalism, and you've got to match that if you're ever going to be more than the Ricky Morton to Rampage.' The black guy asked, 'What, you'd have it the other way around?' 'NO. There doesn't have to be a weak link to the team. And a great team doesn't have one. But Vangellus has myriad weaknesses that a competent opponent can exploit. If he were serious about his work, he could learn to cover himself on it. That's where I come in.' Williamson pushed Oxios toward the stairs. 'Apparently I've got to drag you kicking and screaming into the big-time. First things first, get some damn clothes on. And then we're heading to the nearest gym. I already know where one is.' Vangellus is sent upstairs, Justin following him to make sure he doesn't goof off. That's when Richard, Van's webmaster and computer monkey, returns from the bathroom. Upon seeing the couch, his eyes begin to tear up. The 5'8' 271 pound man (and that's not from muscle) {and ladies, he's single!} [for good reason] falls to his knees, mourning the loss of his friend. 'NOOOOO!' the balding middle-aged hacker cries out in agony. 'My ass groove! I only just got acquainted with it, and now it's gone!' And on that note, now that you're probably stringing the rope around your neck wanting this to be over, I release you. Good day, Citizen. *End*
