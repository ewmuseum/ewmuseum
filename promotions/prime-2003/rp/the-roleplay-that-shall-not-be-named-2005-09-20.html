The roleplay that shall not be named

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 20th September 2005 Have you ever had a point in your life where it feels like you’re not in control? No, not like the time you were unable to stop your foot when the old lady was leaning towards the stairs or the shiny little coin on your neighbour’s shelf was just a little too shiny. I mean when it seems like someone… fate, God, Joss Whedon (I think all three are the same thing, aren’t they?)… is creating certain situations just so they can see the look on your face when you think nobody else is looking. Because I swear, as I sat in the Intensive Care Unit with a card in one hand reading of my advancement in the Jewel In The Crown brackets and a bowl of grapes for Gaz in the other... green grapes, of course… I had this unmistakable feeling that someone or something was looking at me, smirking their little face off and rhetorically asking me… “What are you going to do now, Nascosto Nerezza?” I’d ask Gaz who started the fight between him and Trashcan Man, except they’re both suffering severe concussions among other things. You know how you go to the doctor and they start vomiting medical dictionary terms at you? That’s how I was when they told me about fractures and hairlines and molecular structures of tractor beams and whatnot. They need to know the quantity of dioxylithiumbolloxide in his bloodstream. All I need to know is his head is severely injured and he’s barely in any shape to talk. And how am I compensated for this? Karina Wolfenden and “The New Boy” as tag partners, Skitzo to practice in the ring with and new enemies. While Gaz is away, Rezz will play. Normally, Gaz is on my arm telling me to slow down, to just win the match and not hurt anyone excessively. Never take a joke too far. Bollocks to that. I’m going to pull no punches. I’m going to pull on any piercing I find in Aimz’ body. I’m going to shove Syko or Juan or whatever his name is into a corner and batter him like an Essex housewife. I’m going to get Rampage on the mat and tear his laterals and collarbones up until his finishing move hurts him more than it does me. Because he’s dangerous enough to collide with me full force. On further inspection, I’m sitting here with seven deadly sins. Me, embodying gluttony. Stop, Gaz says. Don’t do it any more. And my appetite for hurting people continues. But Gaz wishes he were out there with me, or at least, if he knew what the situation was, he would. All he can do is lie there on his back, the personification of sloth. And then there’s Karina, lust in its most literal form, a craving for bodily pleasure. Not for sex, but a high she only gets from adrenaline. Someone brave, stupid or callous enough to send herself flying, not caring about what’s at the bottom of a drop, but savouring the buildup. And finally on my team, Tchu. We all had the new guy treatment in at least one fed we worked in… we’d stare misty-eyed at our own main events, hoping for the day we’d ascend and claim the crown for ourselves. Right now, Tchu’s coming off a win in the tournament and has a lot to be happy about. But it’s like trying to deny being dumb as a teenager… he wants to be recognised, not simply The New Guy. Envy. Then there’s our opponents… Rampage. Nothing but wrath, anger, infinite rage. He can walk to the ring like in the space between being on the apron and then on the canvas, his house burned down taking his family, pets and possessions with it And God help the poor soul who fdeels that first punch. Aimz, constantly smeared with controversy, trying to uphold her pride. Nobody seems to understand that despite the red hair, the previous drama from other federations, the bulging members, the stalkers, the numerous relationships… that’s someone who is trying to wear you down and pin you, 1-2-3. All that ever mattered. And finally, Syko Arsonist. You couldn’t stick to being the infamous Syko Arsonist, so you have to go establish a second legacy and Juan Santos has to rise up to such heights too. Greedy bastard, to live the lives of two superstars… but who wouldn’t, given the opportunity? …I should really stop thinking about stuff like this, but as you’re wandering down a hospital hallway, your brain only fixed on a red line to follow to the exit and if you can’t be bothered to read all the health notices on the wall or peek in at the less fortunate, you wander. As I stepped out of the huge doors and over to the awaiting taxi, I took a deep breath in and out. It was an almost surreal feeling, like I’d left the back of my head in the hospital and it was stretched like elastic, as though I was going to topple backwards at any moment and stagger backwards, like a bungee. Just to make sure, I tugged on my ponytail and flicked it upwards to make sure it wasn’t attached to anything. I’d probably have been more relieved if it were. At least I’d know what was causing the feeling. The sun was pretty heavy that day. I’ve never been fond of sunlight, regardless of my age or… persuasion… at any time. All it does is exhaust me, makes me feel sleepy and spent. I fought to keep my eyes open in the back of the cab, hoping to not have another crazy dream. No more European castles, no more techno clubs, no more Mr Darcy and horses… all I wanted was to wander in, find the first comfortable surface and collapse like Charlotte Church after a shot of vodka. After fumbling with the keys and letting myself in, the smell of burned toast from a few nights ago still hung in the kitchen, wandering towards my nose to greet me. Not that I’d missed it, but it apparently missed me. Thankfully, there was no Skitzo to be found. As I sluggishly climbed up the stairs to my bedroom, I noticed a hole dug in the back garden and several small stones gathered around it, each with a small piece of yellow paper adhering to them. Even with my mind as taxed as it was, I gathered he had taken my suggestion literally about digging his own grave to cure boredom. A headstone for each personality… amusing. Before I slumped half-dead (if you’ll pardon the pun) into my bed, I envisioned as if it were foreshadowing something. Without Gaz, I’d have more freedom. Not only the freedom to come and go or do as I choose, which is the only freedom a man ever needed, but to show what’s inside of me. To show the monster Hessian raised and Gaz tried to suppress. To release that vile creature I kept locked behind a veil of silver hair for so long… to paraphrase Alice Cooper… He’s back. The man behind the mask. And he’s out of control.
