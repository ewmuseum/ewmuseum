Angel and Airwaves

Date: ReV 174 “Honey, you really should be getting some rest.” Mrs. Brooks eyed her daughter sternly, fading red hair pulled up into a professional but alluring bun, a do the matriarch of the Brooks family was wearing (quite successfully) long before Ms. Sarah Palin ever stepped before a national television audience. Angelica Brooks furrowed her brow in defiance. “All I do is rest, Mom. Stuck in this damn hospital bed.” “Language, honey.” “Mom, please. I’ve said much worse.” “Not in front of me you haven’t. And you’d better not start, either.” The elder Brooks female leaned over her daughter, offered a loving peck to the forehead, then straightened back to her full height. “I just don’t think you should be listening to the man, that’s all.” “Not this again…” “Yes. This again. It’s unhealthy. You being so obsessed with-“ “I am not obsessed. We’re just-“ “Just nothing. It’s his fault you’re in here, after all. The least he could do is-“ “Is what, Mom? What more do you want from him? He’s been here for the past month. Since the night they brought me in. He’s traveling over three-fourths of New England to keep up with PRIME and coming back here every day in-between. Sleeping on those Hoyt forsaken cots. Not to mention paying for your hotel ro-“ “I thought you said the company was paying for that.” A sudden and heavy silence falls over the room. Mother looked at daughter. Daughter found something terribly interesting about her hospital gown. Head hung low, fingers playing with the fabric… “I just thought that if you knew… you wouldn’t have accepted.” “Darn right we wouldn’t have. I don’t want anything to do with that man. Or his.. his… his blood money.” “Blood money!” Her outburst was loud enough to hide the entrance of a sheepish nurse with a radio tucked up under her arm. Nothing, however, could hide the appearance of an unwelcome visitor to the mounting argument. Perhaps an angel in disguise, the nurse’s presence seemed to bring their conversation to a halt. Even if it did bring an awkward silence upon the room. “I… I need to speak with your father. He’s probably down in that stupid cafeteria again…” There was a second’s pause as both mother and daughter struggled for the right set of words. Battled stubbornness against the obvious need to apologize. Pigheadedness won. Mrs. Brooks quietly left the hospital room without another word. The nurse set her small battery-powered radio on the stand beside Angelica’s bed and left just as quickly, just as quietly, as the mother. Angelica sighed. Her shoulders sagged. She carried too much. All of it just… just too damn heavy. She wanted to cry. Instead, she let her fingers fumble with the radio dial. Her mind was elsewhere. “-ere with Tyler Rayne, reigning PRIME 5-Star Champion and professional wrestling icon.” “I don’t know about all that.” “Aw, come on now, Rayne. You’re not really the type to be modest.” “I’m not?” “Well, I never really-“ “I’m a realist. Am I good? Yeah. Hell… I’m probably great. Better than most, as my current title reign can attest to. But an icon? Eh… I’m not even sure I want all that. I mean, it’s not really about the fame and money for me, ya know.” “It’s about the women.” “There is that.” “Which is one of the things we want to talk to you about. First, though, we really do want to thank you for coming on the show. I understand you’ve been spending a lot of time here in Pittsburgh lately.” “A fair bit. Yeah.” “And how is Angelica doing?” “Good. Much better, actually. B’s a tough kid. Tougher than even I gave ‘er credit for. She’ll be up and back on her feet in no time.” “So we can expect her back in PRIME sometime soon?” “I can’t really say, man. I’m just glad she’s recovered. She’s gonna walk again. Anything after that… is all up to her.” “Fair enough. So what about Lindsay Troy then?” “What about ‘er?” “Well you two have… uh… you know. I mean, haven’t you?” “Wouldn’t you like to know.” “That’s why I’m asking.” “You watch the show, right? I mean, you’ve seen Lindsay Troy in action.” “Oh yeah. She’s one of my favorites. Her match at Colosssus Thr-“ “Then you can imagine what would happen to me if I divulged any information about our personal life.” “Excellent point. Moving on then.” “Thank you.” “You guys are doing ReVolution in Philly tomorrow night, right?” “That’s the rumor.” “And you’re facing… who is it again?” “Troy Douglas.” “Who the eff is Troy Douglas?” “Cute. Uh… damn I bet that kid hates me for all that. Gotta give ‘im credit, though. That match at Colossus was amazing. One of the best I’ve seen. Gave that kid a lot of flack, but after that night… I’m not about to forget who he is. He proved a lot out there.” “Almost sounds like you’re worried about ReVolution.” “Don’t go that far. I said the kid’s good. Credit where credit is due. But he held that Intense Title for about as long as… anyone has managed to keep that Uni Title strapped around their waist. Neither of which has even approached nine months. Good is good. I’ll still be better.” “That’s more like the Rayne I expected. Speaking of the Universal Title, what about this Roulette thing? You don’t even put yourself in the match? I mean… seriously?” “What’s the point? This 5-Star Title is all I need to prove what I can do. I’m a main event player in PRIME. Universal Title or not. I don’t need that belt. And I don’t need to beat Tsonda to prove anything. I’ve been proving myself all year long. I’m one of the most dominant champions in PRIME history. That’s fact. That’s truth. Inarguable. Undeniable. Kinda like my uncanny marketing savvy.” “Man. Those Baby Dusk dolls… those things are pure genius. I mean the t-shirts. Sure. Fine. But Baby Dusk… those little dolls are harder to get hold of than a Tickle Me Elmo. Which, while I’m thinking about it, you think there’s any way you can get me one of those. Ya know, under the table or somethin’.” “I’ll see what I can do. Honestly, though, I can’t even… hell.” “Is that a phone?” “Yeah. Sorry. I… [bleep]” “Is that your phone?” “Just a sec, will ya?” “Is that…. the Power Rangers theme?” “Dawk, what’s up?” “Dawkins? Bryan Dawkins? Hey! Do you think you can get him to answer a few-“ “No. I haven’t heard anything. … Slow down, kid. You’re not makin’ sense. Yeah. Just… … No, I haven’t talked to Tink lately. … He what? When? … Jesus Christ … I’m halfway across the [bleep]in’ country. … No. Just sit tight. I’ll uh… I’ll call a jet or something. … Yeah. … Hang tight, kid. I’m … [bleep].” “Well, um… uh… Tyler Rayne has just ran out of the studio. Sorry folks. Um… Bryan Dawkins, I’m guessing, called him on the phone and then-“ The radio crashed to the floor. Angelica stretched awkwardly, more than she’d exerted herself for a few days. Reached out for the handles of her purse. Dumped it out across the bed. Dug through the contents. Her cell phone. Angelica Brooks waited in complete agitation for the god damn thing to ring. Even speed dial wasn’t fast enough. “Come on, Ty. Pick up. Pick up…”
