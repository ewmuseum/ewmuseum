Date: 6/18/09 Postmarked May 20th Dear Mister Tsonda, You don’t know me yet. My name’s Riley Booth. I talked to all them boys straightaway every time you leave. O said you’s a real nice guy, and I’m hopin that youl’l take the time to read this letter. I don’t write so good, so if its too much trouble to try and undrstand what I’m sayin, its okay. I just thought you might like some fan mail ya know from someone who cares. Howie said you was lonely. Dr Barius gets real shifty whenever anybody mentions your name. We been jokin’ that he’s gonna challenge you too a cage match one of these days. We all Id like it if you came to visit again Dr Barius said you could meet with maybe. Not if you don’t want to but if you do. I’m wishin you good luck against Jason Snow even tho I know you don’t need it. Only reason you lost at Culture Shock is bc you were ruffed up before. And you still gave him the match of his life! Amazing job man but I’m sure you here that all the time. Howie Hareington uses his good behavyour hours every week to watch ReV and theirs a bunch of us who gather round and he tells us who won and who lost and all that. Hes a real good storyteller gets you on the edge of your seat waitin to hear what happened. Been tellin us about how GBJ and Snow are messin with you, how Hessean’s been attacking people. Its too bad I’m missin it but I gotta stay here until Dr Barius tells the court that I been rehebiletated like my heads right and everything. I don’t hear voices or nothin I just done a bad thing and hurt somebody who was shovin me around at school. My mom says I need the lord in me but I secretly think shes just afraid I’m gonna end up like my older brother Ryan whose locked up for jackin some cars. But nah thats not me. Riley /~/~/~/~/ Postmarked June 5th, 2009 Dear Mister Tsonda, None of us couldn’t watch UltraVielence, but Howie used his good behavyour time to get on the internet and listen to it on PRIME radio. Way he tells it, that Hell in a cell match was somethin else. Said that just listenin to it he could hear the crowd everybody risin up everytime you did sumthin great. Next time my uncle’s here Im definetly gonna ask him to write down every move that happened so I can get a better idea of what happened. I’m sorry that you lost, Mr Tsonda. I really thought you would do it this time. Everytime Nick Wilson whips on me tries to start shit with me I’m gonna start using that golgutha drop you use it looks like it fucken hurts. You use that one like every match that’s what Howie says so it must be like one of your fave moves and I think if I did that to Nick he’d never mess with me again. Is Bryan Dawkins back? I heard he is and that’s cool I like him you always seem to be in a better mood when he’s around. He doesn’t really get along with his dad so I guess it’s kinda like your his dad. Not saying that your old but I’m sure you got a lot to teach him bein such a vetuhren of the biz. The real reason I wrote you, Mr Tsonda, is bc I wanted to say Im sorry 1. I heard about Tyler Rayne and I know that’s your other best friend. My mom says that god works in mysterious ways and that he takes the people closest to us sometimes because he wants to test us. Maybe that will help you. Sorry about that. 2. I’m sorry about the match. I believed in you and I bet you thought you were gonna win, and its just not fair. I know you gave it your all. 3. I think I might have jinxed you. Sorry. I hope you can still read these and maybe write back to me but if you don’t want to its no big deal I’m not like a crazy fan or something just a kid but I think you’re the best in PRIME and in the world and anybody who cant see that is just crazy if you ask me. But how come you can’t beat Snow Mr Tsonda? He cant be better than you you were champion for so long and nobody could touch you. Howie says he thinks your too hurt to ever be champ again but hes gotta be full of it he just uses that big brain of his too much. How come you can’t beat him tho. You’re the best Mr Tsonda and he shudnt have your belt its yours and you gotta protek whats yours thats what my uncle says ever since Ryan left. If you come back to the D I’ve got a list of suggeschuns for how you can beat him next time. Only if you think I can help. Riley /~/~/~/~/ 6/12/09 Dear Riley, Thanks for the letters. I don’t usually get a chance to answer them personally. Maybe next time I end up down there we can meet up and talk shop. I haven’t been in much of a talking mood ever since UltraViolence and it’s the busy part of the season. You understand. I don’t know why I can’t beat Jason Snow, Riley Booth. I’ve never lost to someone twice in a row in big matches in my life. But whatever I think about the situation, it’s over now. I threatened to quit, I put my health on the line, and I went out there and fought the good fight…and nada. I’m sorry if you think I let you down. I’m starting to feel like I let a lot of people down. Rayne, Dawkins, everyone cheering for me. It’s a weird thing about my job, Riley. Not that I’ve ever been a man of duty, but I feel an obligation to make sure the fans are happy. It’s a weird point in my career, but then again, you probably know that. I’m writing a letter to an incarcerated twelve-year-old because…well, because I’ll probably never send it. You get to be my age in wrestling and the injuries start to pile up. Mind you, I’ve been healthy as an ox my whole career, a real health stud. Keep that health up, Riley. It makes the ladies’ special parts do pirouettes. So maybe it was the injuries, I dunno. I don’t make excuses, and Snow beat me twice. But it’s weird, ya know? When things start happening–bad things–that didn’t used to happen, do you walk away John Elway-style, all “if I can’t do this at my absolute best, then I won’t do it all?” Or is there more honor, something more substantive about going more the way of Robert Parrish, being a symbol of the old guard and knocking down any young whippersnappers who don’t respect the ledge? I don’t have a goddamn clue, and I guess this Wade thing has me even more brain-scrambled because the guy should know better. I won’t lie. I think about retirement, I do. But the whole “hang it up at Colossus” thing is played out, and Killean already played the early bird. I can still take anybody in PRIME to the limit (although they’re returning the favor with alarming frequency), that’s a fact. And I’m not having goddamn surgery. That’s career-ending shit, and I always said that I’d keep doing this until some spiteful god split my ACL in two. No, I’m gonna be the grizzled good-looking veteran who stays putting people in their places of respect, kinda like John Stamos. Say what you will, but that’s a nice looking man. So call me the Uncle Jesse of PRIME if you have to, but I’m not leaving until they damn well pull me out screaming. That’s a promise. I can’t send this. Fuck. -Chandler Tsonda
