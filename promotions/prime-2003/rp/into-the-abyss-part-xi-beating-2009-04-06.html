Into The Abyss: Part XI - Beating

6 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06-04-09 30th December, 2006 They stood on the front step of Wyatt Manor in the cold, Von Kelsig extending a hand to Bruce Shanahan. Gone were the thick bandages, replaced now by thinner strips wrapped around his fingers, and Bruce took care to grip softly as they shook hands and bid each other farewell. "Good luck with your future endeavours Von Kelsig." Bruce wished him well regardless of the resentment felt after the unwelcome appearance of Tyler Rayne at the Manor. It was a simple parting between men and with a respectful bow of his head, Von Kelsig turned from the sanctum of Wyatt Manor and made his way down the broken, cracked driveway. A few yards away Gloria was regarding one of the old vine-ridden statues of Cthulhu decorating the courtyard. She was tucked up inside a cream puffa jacket with the hood pulled over her head as the wind picked up and sporadic flurries of rain turned to hail and then snow. The faux fur lining the rim of the hood was chocolate brown and caramel in colour, though spikes of peroxide blonde hair poking out interwove with the fur, and he thought of her as a little wild cat with a pair of big black eyes. A twinkling grin greeted him as he approached her, her once creased and wrinkled cheeks now a little chubbier and fuller. During their stay with Violence Jack she had savoured every meal, indulging her re-discovered appetite and filling the void left by heroin with greasy succulent dinners and a shot of Buprenorphine. He on the other hand struggled to finish most meals, eating what he needed for sustenance and spending most of his time suffering the great withdrawal. His lack of appetite was apparent in the way the cashmere overcoat hung from his frame, the black wool scarf wrapped thrice around his neck giving him a little more profile. It made Von Kelsig happy to know Gloria was at least casting off the shadow of her junkie lifestyle and bathing in the light of normality. "It's strange the way life works..." Gloria muttered, echoing Tyler Rayne's sentiment and indeed Von Kelsig's own thought as they traversed the woods towards the back road beyond. "How's that?" He replied, fixing the strap of his duffel bag as it slipped down his shoulder. All their worldly belongings filled that one duffel bag; shared toiletries, Gloria's make-up and a couple of shirts, pants and underwear each. Meager luggage befitting their circumstance, with the exception of a folded slip of paper tucked inside the leather wallet nestled in the breast pocket of Von Kelsig's shirt. That was their ticket back to a bog standard life. "People work their asses off in their rat race lives to be able to lavish and indulge like fat cat businessmen with helicopters and TVs bigger than their living room wall. They play the lottery and put away savings so they can taste the high life once in a while and feel like life is worth something. Then you got the sub-human society, all the addicts and scum huddled up in hostels or wasting away in the gutter and not even giving a damn. We've been a part of that life and I realized I'm so grateful to be able to wake up in the morning and have a cup of coffee and a nice breakfast and enjoy the day instead of craving a needle in my arm, y'know?" She continued, taking care as she crossed over broken branches and rocks on her way through the woods. "Yeah." Von Kelsig said following her, taking care not to scrape or knock his hands against any jagged surface along the way. He watched Gloria struggling over the natural obstacles in their path while he effortlessly glided over each with one gargantuan step at a time, his boots crunching in the snow. "To be honest if I can have a safe and comfortable life I wouldn't care if it was boring and monotonous. I don't need luxuries to be happy, I just want to be normal like everyone else...and not...not scum. Not a piece of shit under the boot of society." Gloria sighed, stopping as the edge of the woods came into sight. "Don't say that." Von Kelsig stood beside her and wrapped an arm around her petite frame. "That wasn't an existence, least not for anyone that didn't ask for it. We're going to have a normal life, Gloria, I'm going to make damn sure of it. I'm going to take care of you, of us." She giggled and felt herself glowing as Von Kelsig hugged her tightly to his side and let her go, carrying on ahead towards their new life. She felt desire creeping up her spine and tickling the back of her neck as she took off after him, her heart fluttering. ---- 6th January, 2007 "Where are we gonna go?" Gloria asked in a drained and tired sigh. She lolled her head back and forth across her folded forearms. A half-drunk bottle of Budweiser spackled with droplets of condensation sat in front of her. Von Kelsig stooped over the bar next to her, taking a swig of ale from a steel tankard and wiping his mouth with his forearm. A complimentary belch followed, and he ordered another from the landlord. "Can't go back to Detroit..." He muttered, chugging down the pint. The motel was their third stop since leaving Wyatt Manor on the 30th of December. Ttheir first destination was the Citizens bank in Montpelier, where Von Kelsig opened a new account and deposited the cheque when Gloria reminded him Reuben Blanco still had access to his old account. Their path would take them towards Detroit and it was upon that realization that they decided to settle in a new location. New Year had been spent in a hostel outside of Boston when all other accomodation had been taken by visiting family members or office parties or other such deserving reservations. "Reuben has influence in a lot of places, we need to be careful of where we settle down. He'll have feelers out watching for us and if they find us Reuben will be on our backs for good..." She took a sip of her beer and beamed politely as the landlord glanced at her before put down the last washed pint glass and headed for the reception. "Gonna do the books for the day folks, if you need anything just holler." He said, heading through to the kitchen in the back, disappearing for a moment. In another he was walking past them along the hallway that separated the bar and dining room from the reception and waiting area. Tipping his tankard to the landlord, Von Kelsig took another long drink and breathed a satisfactory sigh, licking the froth from his moustache. "Well I don't have any intention of setting up any more businesses, Blanco shot that dream to shit. No, we'll need somewhere far away, somewhere you can be free to do what you want and where I can get back to wrestling again." He reached back and pulled a barstool close to his rear before plopping down on it next to Gloria. "Wrestling? You've never mentioned anything about wrestling before, Angelus. All you told me about Rayne was that he gave us a second chance, remember? You wrestle?" She queried him with a fascinated look in her eyes, moseying closer to him on her stool, her mouth ajar and a smile creeping around its corners. "Ha! Oh you're in for a treat then li'l pixie, I've got enough stories to fill a biography!" Von Kelsig chuckled and swayed a little on his stool, finishing the last of the ale in his tankard. "Can't wait to hear them!" She sat up and downed the last of her Budweiser, unleashing a guttural burp of her own. "I'd love to entertain you with some tales from the crypt but it's getting on late and I'm feeling a little boozy..." Von Kelsig glanced up at a Felix clock above the bar with oscillating eyes and tail, according to which the time was nearly 1am. "Hm. Guess so. Unless you wanna come up to my room to stay and tell me a bedtime story?" Gloria asked, her heart beating a little faster at the notion. Von Kelsig took a short breath and huffed, staring into the bottom of his tankard and swilling around the dregs at the bottom. He wanted to take her up on her offer, and was thankful his beard hid the rosy red cheeks beneath. Several pints of ale made him blush, but the thought of spending a night with Gloria damn near coloured him beetroot. "I wouldn't last five minutes Gloria, the Sandman's working overtime on me..." He finally uttered after a brief silence, a single moment that lingered a little too long between them. Pushing himself away from the bar he got to his feet and stretched, letting out a deep yawn. "Guess I might call it a night as well then..." Gloria added, jumping to her feet and wobbling a little as the effects of the alcohol hit her all at once. Together they sauntered out of the bar, bidding the landlord goodnight and trudging up the creaky stairs to the first of two floors where their individual single rooms lay in wait. It was Von Kelsig's doing, feeling humility at the idea of sharing a room or even a bed with Gloria. In the days they had spent at Wyatt Manor his emotions were put into perspective as he realized his true feelings for Gloria. With this revelation came the overpowering urges of the libido. He had spent a few weeks observing Gloria and all her physical aspects, and had found some to be quite attractive. The way her nose creased when she looked puzzled or shot him that innocent look, or how her perky breasts pressed against any tight shirts she wore and revealed all her intimate curves. Earlier in the night she dropped a handful of quarters under one of the dining tables behind them at the bar, and on dropping on all fours to retrieve them arched her back in a way that put Von Kelsig into a trance. The way she looked at him sometimes with doe eyes brought the urge on stronger, he knew she felt something for him too. Yet to give in to those urges so soon in this relationship of sorts would cheapen it, or so he felt. He fought back the feelings and tried to stay strong while they searched for their new start in life. "Guess I'll see you downstairs for breakfast tomorrow?" Gloria asked, running a hand through her wily blinding white mane. "Of course. We'll decide where we're headed and draw out a plan from there." He winked at her tiredly, his eyes narrowing and almost struggling to open again as he succumbed to drowsiness. "G'night then Angelus..." Gloria whispered, stepping forward and wrapping her arms around Von Kelsig before taking his hand and kissing his knuckles. "Sleep tight Gloria..." Von Kelsig replied, and ran his fingers down her cheek, tapping her nose with his finger and smiling again as they both retired to their beds. Von Kelsig had barely hit the mattress before he was fast asleep. ---- crrrrreeeaaaak. The sound of someone on the stairs at the top of the hallway resonated throughout the first floor and woke Von Kelsig from his slumber. Lifting his head from the pillow he listened, his eyes still shut as another couple of creaks echoed across the floor as whoever it was made their way to their room. Imagining the landlord to finally be headed to bed Von Kelsig rubbed his eyes and lay his head back down upon the pillow, nuzzling his face into the comfort of the warm silk. click. click. The sound of something working the lock of one of the doors roused him from his rest once more. Listening closely, he heard the noise continue before a soft sigh followed a groan of disappointment. He heard it a few moments later, a little louder as whoever was outside checked another door. It certainly didn't sound like the landlord. Quietly sitting up in his bed, Von Kelsig leaned forward and checked the gap under his door. Though the light in the hallway was low, enough shone to cast a shadow off two feet standing outside his room. As another set of clicks subsided to silence, Von Kelsig made out a faint whisper beyond the door. Pulling aside the duvet and pressing his feet to the carpet, Von Kelsig carefully rose and ever so gently approached the door one step at a time. Pressing his ear to the door he held his breath and listened carefully. "She's in this one...this one here..." Came another whisper. His heart skipped a beat and he threw open the door. A white beam of light stung his eyes and he jumped back, startled. Rubbing his eyes and blinking rapidly, Von Kelsig held up a hand and peered out into the hallway to find two men clad in black tracksuits and balaclavas standing in the open doorway of Gloria's room. "GET AWAY FROM HER!" Von Kelsig roared, waking Gloria up in the process. The sight of the two strangers in her room made her scream in fright as Von Kelsig charged at them. Throwing their flashlights up at the rampaging seven footer in an effort to blind him again, the two intruders rushed into Gloria's room and slammed the door shut behind them, pushing against it with all their strength as Von Kelsig slammed his full weight into the door, only managing to force it back a few inches before the two men pushed it shut and threw the lock back over. Turning to find Gloria sitting up in bed, screaming and holding the sheets over her body they looked at each other and nodded. The smaller of the two turned his flashlight off and bent down in front of the door, jamming it under the gap as Von Kelsig charged once again. The door wouldn't budge an inch and he listened in the hallway as Gloria's screams became louder as the sound of a struggle erupted within the room. Roaring like an animal he took a step forward and leaned back, shooting a fist forward and driving it into the hardwood of the door. It cracked down the middle immediately, though held its place as Von Kelsig drew back a bloody fist. Inside the room the sound of Gloria's nightgown being torn off sent a chill down Von his spine as he reached back for another punch. "Not so fast my friend!" The shrill voice accompanied the massive paw that wrapped around his wrist and pulled him back across the hall. A figure as large as him swooped around and forced him against the wall with a forearm across the throat. Reuben Blanco. "I found you Von Kelsig. You and your little whore!" He spat in Von Kelsig's face and drove a knee into his groin, while Von Kelsig struggled desperately upon hearing Gloria shrieking through the groans of both intruders now. Her desperate sobs filled him with rage as Blanco headbutted him in the face, breaking his nose. "I know all about your little deal with that cunt Doe! You're dead, motherfucker! DEAD!" Reuben screamed, reaching into his coat and producing his Beretta pistol. Von Kelsig hardly noticed as he reached out to Gloria's room, desperately trying to force his way past Reuben to rescue her as her cries became frantic. The two gunshots rang throughout the hall, and only when he felt his belly burning did Von Kelsig stop and look down to find two thick streams of blood pouring down his body from the bullet holes. Gazing up at Reuben, he opened his mouth and uttered no sound, while Blanco stared back, the devil in his eyes. Holding his stomach, Von Kelsig raised it to find it sopping with blood. Suddenly Gloria screamed out again, pleading to her attackers as their groans became louder. In an instant the pain was gone and adrenaline burst through his body, giving Von Kelsig the strength to pick Blanco up and spin on his heels, throwing him into the ground. As Reuben bounced off the carpeted floor Von Kelsig tore a fire extinguisher from the wall and slamming it down on Reuben's face with bestial force. A sickening yelp escaped Reuben's throat as he dropped his gun. Again Gloria screamed, and Von Kelsig bolted into the door breaking it off the hinges. As he crashed through the door he caught the smaller of the two intruders lying on top of Gloria with his pants at his ankles, while the second man held her arms down. In a heartbeat Von Kelsig was over the two men, grabbing the larger man's head in his own two hands and twisting hard. His muscles bulged with the strain as he felt the neck snap in his grasp and as the first of Blanco's accomplices fell to the floor in a twisted heap he had already ripped the second off of Gloria and thrown him to the ground. Mounting the deviant, Von Kelsig ripped off his balaclava and unleashed a wave of animalistic fists into the man's face as he laughed back through broken jaw and bloodied nose. Three more punches split his brow and snapped his teeth from the gums. Grabbing the man's hair, Von Kelsig rubbed his face into the floor, smearing the blood over the carpet. Jumping to his feet another surge of adrenaline shot through him and he brought a heel up, driving it straight to the floor and cracking the man's jaw clean through with the first kick. His victim's distinct laughter turned to horrified shrieks as Von Kelsig lifted his bloodied heel and brought it smashing down on the intruder's face, shattering his orbital bone. As the attacker bled out on the floor Von Kelsig reached forward and grabbed the corner bedpost, using his ungodly strength to haul the bed with Gloria still lying on it over to Blanco's second accomplice before raising the corner off the ground and bringing it crunching down on the intruder's skull. The audible crack of the deviant's cranium snapped Von Kelsig from his animalistic rage, and at once he moved over to Gloria. Holding her head in his hands he knew at once she was soaked in blood. Fumbling with the bedside lamp, he clicked it on and burst into tears at the sight that lay before him in his arms. Her once brilliant white head of hair was matted to her face, saturated with blood still running from wounds in her scalp. Her once innocent and plain face was covered in bruises, her eyes closed over and bulging while her left cheek was sunken. Cradling her close, he held his breath once again and listened for signs of breathing, watching her chest for any movement. Nothing. "Wake up Gloria! Wake up!" He shouted, but took care to shake her gently for fear of her breaking like porcelain in his hands. "I'm gonna get you to a hospital..." He trailed off, turning to the door. CRUNCH! The same fire extinguisher he hit Blanco with slammed him square in the jaw, dazing him and causing him to drop Gloria and slip off of the bed. Holding his head he looked up to see several figures dancing in his vision, holding several fire extinguishers. Shaking his head he squinted up at Reuben Blanco as the fire extinguisher dropped to the floor and the giant criminal lord reached down and grabbed him by the beard, hauling him forward on all fours and pulling him to his feet. Spinning Von Kelsig around, Reuben spat blood across the room and held his grip on Von Kelsig's beard, forcing him to look at Gloria. "How does it feel knowing she's going to die?" Reuben demanded, thrusting Von Kelsig's head forward. "Don't do this!!" Von Kelsig yelled back, throwing an elbow into Reuben's gut only for Blanco to catch it and lock it behind his back. Von Kelsig groaned as pain tore through his shoulder, while his stomach felt like it was pouring out through the bullet holes. "I ain't done nothing. You did all this Von Kelsig, when you went behind my back with John FUCKIN' Doe!" Reuben took a step back, dragging Von Kelsig with him. "No! I don't know what you're talking about! Stop!! I need to get her to a hospital dammit!!" Von Kelsig pleaded with Reuben, his eyes filled with tears and blood. "You had your chance you fucker. Now your bitch is going to die and you can't do a thing about it!" Reuben hissed in Von Kelsig's ear. "GET THE FUCK OFF ME!! I'M GONNA-..." Von Kelsig roared out and stopped in mid-sentence as something stung him in the neck. All at once the adrenaline dissipated and he felt nothing but horror as the old familiar sting gripped his jugular. Reuben had shot him full of a double dose of heroin. In seconds the fear and rage subsided as his body melted in colourful comfort from the injection. Sliding the needle out of Von Kelsig's vein, Reuben dropped him and backed away to the door, holding a handkerchief to his burst nose as Von Kelsig hit the floor. A wave of euphoria shook his nerves, and a faint groan escaped his lips as he slumped back against the bedside cabinet on which stood the lamp, now splattered with claret. "Like I said, you can't do a goddamn thing about it. I'm coming back for you Von Kelsig. I will come back for you..." He spat a bloody gob at Von Kelsig and stumbled out of the room. Von Kelsig lay on the floor, staring vacantly at Gloria's dead body. The drug coursed through his veins and he felt amazing, lying on the floor staring vacantly at Gloria's dead body. He loved her, he knew it then as he lay on the floor staring vacantly at her dead body. He loved her. She was dead. Gloria was gone. He felt euphoric. Gloria was dead. His love. Dead. He couldn't save her. Dead. Dead. ---- "URGHH!!" Von Kelsig sat bolt upright in bed. His heart pounded in his chest and his entire body was soaked with a cold sweat. Looking around the bedroom he felt his face, neck and stomach for any pain or injuries. He was clean, he was fine. The bedside clock read 01:35 in luminous green. Taking deep breaths, he kicked the damp blanket off his body and sat on the edge of the bed. Grabbing the duvet again he wiped his face against the dry side, rubbing it over his body and dumping it back on the mattress. Holding his head in his shaking hands he took a few moments to clear his head from the nightmare. His eyes stung as they welled up, his eyelids fluttering against the irritation. Closing his eyes tightly he took a sharp breath and rose to his feet. Exhaling slowly he grabbed his room key and headed to the door, throwing on a tight-fitting robe and heading out into the hallway. The hall was as quiet and still as it had been earlier in the night. No signs of a struggle, the fire extinguisher was still hanging on the wall and the door to Gloria's room was in one piece. Standing before it, Von Kelsig knocked a couple of times and closed his eyes. Faint footsteps approached the door and it opened. Before him stood a sleepy Gloria, safe and sound and dressed in girly pink silk pyjamas. Her hair was still clean and white and free of bloodstain, she rubbed her eyes and looked up at Von Kelsig. "Heyyy big guy..." She paused to yawn. "What's the matter, can't sleep?" "I..." Von Kelsig began, rubbing his forearm and staring at the floor in an effort not to let Gloria hypnotize him with her gorgeous eyes. "...I was wondering if I could stay in your room tonight..." She beamed and took him by the hand, leading him into her room and closing the door behind them. He felt like a lost child seeking solace from a night terror, his heart pounding hard again as Gloria threw back the sheets and sat down, gesturing for him to join her by her side. Breathing a sigh of relief Von Kelsig sat down on the bed, removing his robe and dropping it in a pile on the floor before pulling the blanket over him, his feet hanging over the edge. Gloria lay facing him, and raised a hand to his face, stroking his cheek. "You OK? You look a little shaken." She asked, seeing traces of sweat on his forehead. "Yeah, I'm fine now." He replied, and as she turned away from him and lifted her head he placed his arm beneath her and cuddled her, placing an open hand against her stomach and stroking her tummy with his thumb and nuzzling his face into her sweet smelling hair. "Good night Gloria." He whispered, kissing the back of her head. "Sleep tight, Angelus." She replied. Gloria fell asleep biting her bottom lip to contain her delight. Von Kelsig held her close for a short while, waiting for his heartbeat to slow before he sighed a final sigh of relief and fell into a deep slumber with his girl in his arms and a trace of a smile on his face.
