Threads of a Day (with Danny Ferguson for Culture Shock Tag Team Title Defense)

undated

Date: Pick one, before the PPV "Well, if you look past our mutual dislike for the members of Fuck You!, Danny Ferguson and I are really nothing alike." Pens and pencils took to paper and tape recorders were thrust forward as as a loud murmur rose to the ceiling of the executive board room inside the Guandong Olympic Stadium. The morning Culture Shock press conference had been underway for close to ten minutes, its subject for this half-hour block sitting behind a small table, PRIME security standing against the wall behind her. Lindsay Troy smoothed the fabric of her black embroidered shirtdress over her knees, pausing to let the statement sink in via a translator sitting beside her. She had fielded the softball questions at the onset, 'How has your stay been so far?', 'Have you found the people accommodating?', 'Have you visited White Cloud Mountain yet?', and now the relevant questions were finally starting to trickle in. "Yes, we're a team; a thrown together one, but a team nonetheless. And if I didn't think we could get past our differences, I wouldn't be trying to make the best of it and find success regardless." The follow-up question came, long and brisk, from a member of the Chinese media whose name Troy had forgotten by now. "He says," relayed the translator, "'How worried are you about the lack of team chemistry between you and Mr. Ferguson?" "Where chemistry is lacking, ambition replaces it. Danny doesn't like to look bad and he sure doesn't want to look bad at the expense of a twerp and a mute. He likes pretty, shiny things and one half of the tag titles looks good on him, despite the circumstances in which he acquired it. This isn't an ideal situation for him, or for me. I can't lie and say that it is. But Fuck You! made him look bad once and him not being able to find another partner made him look bad twice. If he didn't want to take a stab at this tag team thing, he would have left me in the lurch weeks ago." "What's to say that he won't still?" Luke De Rosier, an American journalist from Troy's hometown of Tampa, piped up. "Wouldn't be the first time something like that happened, Luke." ### "Boss, you ok?" Danny Ferguson shielded his eyes from the sun as he stared out the window to the Guangzhou skyline. The China Hotel, a Marriott-run outfit in the downtown area, is a headquarter hotel for the PRIME cast and crew during the week of Culture Shock. Across the cityscape, the Olympic Stadium has a firm grasp on Danny's attention, but that doesn't stop his company from trying to divide it. "Boss?" Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas, Danny's senior bodyguard, sits at one of those token uncomfortable chairs that come with any room at a Marriott, suite or otherwise. Danny doesn't turn away from the window, but tips his head up enough to acknowledge that he heard the question. "I'm fine, Dam. There's just...a lot on my mind." “Boss, if you need me to explain how I gots back to China after the whole plane thing, I c-“ “It’s not that. A lot of stuff goes down this weekend. I’m...I’m not entirely sure I’m making the right choice here.” “...Do you want me to call the escort service back?” “No - What? No! Goddammit, Dam, that’s not what I’m talking about...Maybe later, I’ll let you know.” Another voice from the doorway catches their attention. “He’s givin’ ya’ll the gaddang emo queer treatment on ‘count of nostalgia.” Both men turn to see Reggie Delray, Danny’s wrestling manager, step into the suite. For the first time in the history of their working relationship, Danny smiles upon seeing him. “I’m glad you could make it, Reggie.” He and Dam exchange handshakes with Reggie’s perpetually damp palm. “Sheeet, I’s itching ta get outta my house fer once, figured if they’d let me cleer customs I could come help an old friend.” “I think you’re being liberal with your definition of friend, but whatever.” “I’da thought that leedin’ ya to yer first tag title reign just ‘bout a year ago would be enough to earn the title.” It HAD been almost a year ago, at PRIME’s UltraViolence, when Reggie led The A-List (FergFace Edition) to the PRIME tag team championships. That fact was what led to much of the strain on Danny’s mind at the moment. At the recognition, he sinks down onto the bed, getting distant. Both the guard and the manager look at him inquisitively. “One year ago I came into this weekend with an eye on gold, be it a ticket or a title. I was the 5-Star champ, but I hadn’t ARRIVED, you know? The Halo was supposed to put me on the map. When the dust settled I hadn’t gone anywhere, and the best I could do was a tag team championship for a dead division. It’s almost a year later, and where am I? Tag team champ of a rising-from-the-dead division, hoping the Dual Halo will put me on the map. All I can think about today is...I turned down the role of a lifetime for THIS?” Neither member of the entourage is able to offer a silver lining to the Superstar, so he just shrugs. The phone in Dam’s breast pocket chirps with a indeterminate rap song, and he checks the number. “Car’s ready downstairs, Boss. Lot to do today.” “Yeah.” He pauses once more, then pushes up from the bed and heads for the door. “Let’s go.” ### "Breathe, Blaine. Seriously. Breathe!" A hundred sets of eyes gaze to the back corner of the Banana Leaf restaurant, following the sounds of hyperventilation and ragged wheezing. Blaine Blair had his back facing the patrons in the restaurant, but he could feel all the eyes were on him, which only made his predicament worse. With reddened face, he thumped his palm against his chest and squeezed his eyes shut. "I c-...I ca--..." Lindsay looked up, helplessly, at the waiter standing next to their table, who then said something completely indistinguishable and hurried. As luck would have it, she and Blair had gotten the one waiter who couldn't speak much English outside of "yes," "okay," "it's good?" and "right away." "Okay, Blaine," she started, a touch of concern threaded in her voice, "I only know a little bit of conversational Chinese and I'm going to butcher this dialect if I try to speak it, so I really, really need you to calm down so the restaurant doesn't start laughing at me as well as keep staring at you." Blair allowed his eyes to open, gazing at his lunch date and at the rail-thin waiter hovering above him. Slowly, his breathing became less haggard and the red in his cheeks gradually dissipated. Lindsay reached across the divide and slid Blaine's water glass closer to him, careful not to cause the water at the rim to spill over. She looked up at the waiter and smiled. "He's okay." "Okay? Yes. Good!" And then, he walked away. Blaine took the water to hand, sipping it quickly and topping his glass off again with the bottle sitting by the edge of the table. "I'm sorry about that." "Don't be." "How embarrassing..." He took a quick look around the restaurant, thankfully noticing that everyone else had gone back to their meals. "I can't wait until everyone hears about this. 'Blaine Blair Has Panic Attack In Front of Entire Restaurant. News at Eleven.'" "I really don't think anyone can blame you." Troy took a sip from her own water. "You haven't exactly had a stress-free week." "I have no idea what I'm doing, Lindsay." His frankness caught her off-guard. "What? What do you mean?" "I mean, one minute I'm someone's gopher and the next I'm the figurehead. I go from running around like a chicken with my head cut off to running around like a chicken with my head cut off with a big office at the arena and people needing me to make all the important decisions. I don't have the slightest clue how to steer the ship." "Technically, isn't Nelson steering the ship again?" "Well, y-yes, I guess. I don't know. All this happened so fast, it still hasn't even sunk in. He's not coming back back, but I'm expected to be his voice and run things on the road." "You were basically doing that anyway, Blaine, after Chet lost his head." "I know." A sigh. "Chet probably hates me now." Lindsay shrugged. "Is that something you could have prevented?" "No." "Okay then." "I don't want him to hate me, though." "I know, but I think we all kind of saw his ousting coming in some form. I know I did; I didn't want to, but he started getting way too obsessed with getting the title off Nova. Once you set yourself up like that, the chances for downfall increase." "Any suggestions for me, then?" "Remain as neutral as you can. Chet tried to, in the beginning, but he had an agenda. Once you start pushing one, you're getting yourself into trouble." ### “Why the hell is there this much traffic?! I’ve never even heard of this damn city before, so can someone tell me where all these cars came from?” “Boss, the ever-expanding population of the country had had an adverse eff-“ “Is this a Social Studies class? Shouldn’t you be out there knocking cars over and frightening the locals with your amazing size and blackness?” “That’s fucked up, Boss.” “So it goes, man. So it goes.” Danny looks out the window of the vehicle. He was already annoyed that they couldn’t rent an SUV to travel in, so it only became worse as that travel slowed to a crawl going from place to place. Next to him, Reggie fidgets uncomfortably, seeming to never be able to sit still. “Danny, I waz thinkin’ bout some things for yer tag match, an-“ “I don’t care about the tag match.” “-d I...wait, you what?” “I don’t care about the tag match, Reggie,” he reiterates, turning his head away from the window to look forward, but still not make eye contact with his manager. “It’s a pint-sized Saddam fan and an oversized, overaged ‘grappler’. The little guy is too short to hurt Legsay and too weak to give me a challenge. The big one is pushing 300 pounds and 40 years old, so his knees are grinding to a fine powder with every step. He can’t keep up with the chick and I can give him enough fight on strength that his joints would crumble. It’s not a cakewalk, but it’s not worth wasting prep time on.” “Ye’r puttin a lot of faith in the lady you ain’t been a big fan of.” “What? No. Don’t be stupid. I think she...those two jokers are bad enough that I could defend the belts with El Rayo Azul as my partner. Certainly Troy can handle it.” “Interesting,” Reggie said, scratching his chin. “Guy I’s watchin on TV wouldn’ta even put her over the toaster oven.” “She’s not a liability, that’s all I’m saying. Besides, I’m more concerned about the Dual Halo and the Fuck Heads.” He looks out the window again, to see a small group of Chinese teenagers crowding outside it in the standstill traffic. He jumps a little at the sight, collects himself and begrudgingly rolls the window down. Immediately they begin shoving in markers and items to be signed, chattering in indeterminate Cantonese. “Hi, how are y- ok, ok, one at a time. Who is this for? Nevermind, I’ll probably butcher it. This is a lot of stuff, do you guys have an ChineBay here or something?...Are you all coming to Culture Shock?” “Uh-huh!” comes the unison response. “All right, all right,” Danny replies, handing back the last autograph and trying to roll his window up before more arms are shoved in. “I’ll see you there!” When the window rolls up, the smile fades. “Nice job on that one, Dam. What if one of those teenage girls had a knife?” “Boss, I don’t think they woulda tried to stab you here. Besides, you look like you were enjoying it.” “A fan is a fan, Dam. And a PRIME fan is a movie fan is a More Than Hardcore fan. After all, I’m wearing a white hat now, remember? That reminds me, get ahold of the Blaine’s office. I want to have dinner with him tonight and go over some things.” He dusts off his jacket and sighs deeply. The traffic breaks up slightly, and the car starts to move again. Danny’s gaze is drawn back to the window and the world he’s passing by. ### "I really don't trust Danny ... as far as I can throw him. But then again, ... I didn't trust Sonny ... as far as I could throw him either ... and we didn't ... kill each other." A loud guffaw from Killean Sirrajin accompanied Troy's statement as her body rose and fell with the movements from the ab machine she currently occupied. After the press conference and lunch with Blaine Blair, which did end rather nicely despite the rocky start to it, the two had agreed on finding a gym and getting a mid-afternoon workout in. A franchised Powerhouse Gym was discovered fifteen minutes from the hotel and after a quick change out of business attire, Lindsay met Killean in the lobby and they headed off in his rental car. Two hours later and a workout of cardio, abs, shoulders and legs nearly completed, the two were finally starting to talk about Lindsay's Tag Team Dilemma. "I've seen some forced tag situations before, but this might take the cake," Sirrajin remarked over the clink of the weights from the leg press. Third set, eight reps to go. "The man got into an argument with Chet over some stupid movie and then left the country. Gone. See-ya. Bye bye. Whatever," she grumbled, sliding off the machine and grabbing a towel and spray bottle. "Hal Jordan isn't a redhead anyway." "He'd dye his hair, y'know." "Pfft. Whose side are you on?" "I'm just saying!" Another laugh. "Maybe it's better if he goes off and does the movie. At least then you can find a partner that actually gives a shit." "You volunteering?" "Maybe. I could always just push him aside and pick up the torch. Chet's not in charge anymore, so maybe Blaine would be receptive to it." "I appreciate the thought but, I don't know." Lindsay wiped the machine down and turned to look at the Supreme Machine. "Part of me would be thankful for not having to tag his hand but Chet put us together for a reason. He wanted Danny on our side and he thought I'd be the best one to be able to reason with him. I'm going to make it work." "Even if you don't trust him." "Yeah, even if I don't trust him. Not every tag team needs this wonderful chemistry to be successful. Shit," she tossed the rag into a nearby bin, "I can count on one hand the number of teams I've been a part of that have had that kind of chemistry." "Hmm. Well, the offer stands. We need a united front, regardless." "Indeed we do." Sirrajin finished his reps and wiped the machine down. "You ready to get out of here?" "Whenever you are." They hadn’t taken two steps toward the exit when the front door to the gym swung open. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas steps in, wearing the usual sunglasses and black blazer...but a gray t-shirt and workout shorts underneath. Behind him puffs in Danny Ferguson, complete with custom-colored warm-up suit and his initials embroidered on the chest. “Christ,” Troy whispers on sight, noticing Danny just milliseconds before he notices them. “Hey there, Wonder Woman! Didn’t see the Inviso-Jet outside, but I guess that’s to be expected.” Having learned that the best defense is not to give him an audience, Lindsay grabs her bags and heads for the door, giving Danny a courtesy nod. Not to be defeated, he ribs a little harder, not grasping that “friendly banter” is something completely different when you’re not friends. He turns his attention to Killean. “Hey, I see you brought the Headless Horseman along!” he boasts, shuffling his feet and throwing a few fake jabs at Sirrajin, who is nonplussed. “I’m just kidding, man, drugs are serious business.” A playful slap to Killean’s shoulder elicits no response from the Supreme Machine. “Always a pleasure, this guy. Hey, you guys going out later, talking a little anti-Fuck You strategy?” “No.” Troy deadpans, eyes anywhere but on her forced teammate. “That’s cool. I mean, I couldn’t have gone anyway, I’ve got plans and all. Big stuff. Just wanted to make sure you guys knew I was all in.” “So long as the studio doesn’t come calling, right?” growls Sirrajin. The fake smile Danny’s putting on sours in a flash. “What can I say, Surge...it’s a cutthroat industry.” Killean lunges for Danny, but Troy grabs his arm and Dametreyus steps between them. Ferg ducks behind his guard and shouts to Troy, who’s nearly furious. “I can see why Chet paired me with you, Legsay - you’re the only one I can reason with around here!” Finally, Troy manages to yank Killean through the glass doors and toward their car. She pauses momentarily to throw the death stare at Danny, who is standing on the front desk. He shoots her a wink and a finger-gun before she climbs into the car and drives away. “I’ve had some screwed up teams before, but this one takes the cake.” Danny hops down from the desk and unzips his windbreaker. “I never thought someone would make me wish for the days of Tsonda and Facey.” Walking in to the gym proper and shedding the rest of his warm-up, he rolls out his arms a little, still steaming. “With Chet gone, you’d think she could just fucking pair up with Oakley McRoidRage and let me handle my own business. Provided I keep the tag titles, of course. At least then I could find a partner who gave a shit.” “Boss, don’t you think-“ “I try not to. Not before workouts.” He turns toward an elliptical machine and announces to no one in particular: “You ladies ready to see how a MAN works out?” He swings himself on to the machine and lowers his voice to address Dametreyus. “I’m thinking cardio, shoulders, legs, and finish it off with some abs. Two hours tops.” Dametreyus nods, then takes his position next to Danny’s machine as Ferguson pumps away. ### "Ooooh, he pisses me off," growled Troy through clenched teeth as Killean maneuvered the car in-between lanes, heading back toward downtown Guangzhou. Traffic had slowed to a crawl in spots, making the mood in the car become even darker. "You're not the only one," muttered Killean, hands gripping the wheel in anger. "He's such a spoon-fed little shit. Who does he think he is?" Her fist thumped against the closed window, startling a bike messenger who was pedaling past. The boy jerked the handlebars and nearly took a spill. "And I have to defend a title with him against two guys who have a good rapport with one another. Fuck." "You bang your fist against the glass one more time and you may break it." Lindsay let her fist fall to her lap. "Suleimon sees me as a speed bump as is, which given his past makes the thought incredibly ironic. And you know I could give two shits about whether someone takes me seriously or not." Sirrajin nods. "But the fact that between the two of us, I'm the only one who's focusing on the actual defense is making this whole ordeal a gigantic laughingstock. Danny's got his head in the clouds. Knowing him, he's probably already looking ahead to the Halo and the Golden Ticket." "And you're not?" "It's in the back of my mind, but I need to get through Day One first. Then I'll worry about the monster that lies ahead. One thing at a time, y'know?" Traffic started to break up, and the car began moving at a pace faster than that of a snail. Troy gazed back out the window, letting her eyes people watch for a spell before looking over at Killean. "I just hope he gets his head on straight between now and then..." ### “It’s really not a hard job, Blaine.” Danny peers across the table, staring the recipient of wrestling’s biggest promotion dead in the eye. “Just try to keep everyone happy, and they’ll be in line.” “Wait...you’re saying I should be objective?” “Sure, whatever. Chet was an asshole. Don’t be an asshole like Chet. Everything else is gravy.” “Gravy, okay. Yes. Good!” responds the waiter looming over their table. “No, no gravy, we’re going to need some more time.” “Good! Gravy, yes, okay!” Danny watches the waiter scamper away, disgust twisting his face in a sneer. He glances to Blaine. “You’re covering dinner, right? CEO’s salary?” “Well, actually, I-“ “Aw, c’mon, I picked a place that was cheap...ish. Besides, I heard good things about this Banana Leaf place.” “You know, I wanted to-“ “Let me ask you something - have you spoken to Chester since you, ya know, usurped his throne? I wouldn’t worry about him hating you; he’s a very angry man. Probably a little vindictive, too.” “OH, god...” “Relax, it’s not like you could’ve prevented this. A lot of people saw the end of the Worth regime coming. I know I did; I can’t say I WANTED it to happen, since he owed me quite a bit, whether he knew it or not. Anyway, if you don’t push an agenda like he did, you’ll be fine. That being said, if we’re talking about MY agenda, I-“ “I ca...I can’t....can’t...breathe...” Realization sinks in, and Danny rises from his chair while Blaine grabs his chest. His face turns red and he continues wheezing. Not good under pressure, Danny grabs Blaine’s water glass and throws it in his face. “Someone help him!” Danny yells to the wait staff. “He’s a new figurehead and he has no idea what he’s doing!” Blaine wheezes even heavier and bodies crowd around their table, shutting Danny out. ### You know I've been a good girl but I hit a limit. I know there's not a lot of logic in it. But my life's been feeling to me Like Lemon... "Why the hell do you listen to this shit anyway?" Sonny Silver threw the barb over his shoulder, scowling as he scrolled through the iTunes on Lindsay's laptop to find something a little less alternative chick rock. "Fuck you, Fuckhead, I happen to like Poe. I don't give you crap for listening to that hideous McMahon song. Maybe I should start." "My theme song is the sweetest ever, Amazon. Ahh, this is better." With a click and a cackle from Sonny, the song changed from "Lemon Meringue" to "Whatever" by Godsmack. "Remember these good times, Lindz?" "Yeah..." Lindsay gazed out the sliding glass door to the sun beginning its decent for the night. Pretty soon it would fall behind the stadium and brilliant pink and purple hues will slice through the sky. Sonny had called her forty-five minutes ago, just as she was about to get out of her workout gear, to see if she wanted to get dinner and find out what sort of things people do for fun in the city once the sun goes down. "Why'd you ask me to get dinner with you? Where's Mike and Chet?" "They're off doing the whole sightseeing thing and I'm not waiting around for their lameasses to get back. I'm frickin HUNGRY. So why are you just standing there looking all mopey and shit when we can be chowing down until they meet up with us?" "Today's been weird." "Weird..." "Yeah. Weird." Sonny sighed, feeling one of those "heart-to-hearts" coming on that Eme liked to have but he tried to avoid at all costs. You and I did that and did pretty well for ourselves." "This is different, Sonny. You and I may not get along half the time but at least I know you'd give your right arm for this business. Danny's not like that. Danny wouldn't give his right arm to do anything but flex for a photo shoot." "I heard he gives his right arm nothing but a workout in the men's bathroom." Lindsay visibly shuddered. "Too much info." She closed the curtain and turned to look at him. "I'm trying to give him the benefit of the doubt, but given his track record for being a Grade A Douche I'm not entirely sure that this isn't just going to blow up in my face." "You gave me the benefit of the doubt." "I did," she said, smirking. "I'm also still waiting for the knife to find its way into my back." "If you make me listen to "Lemon Meringue" your wish will be granted." "Quit bitching, I let you change it. Consider yourself lucky Ivy isn't here to kick you in the balls." She walked over to the computer, closed the top and tucked it away in the safe by her bed. "This whole weekend's either going to make us come to a better understanding or I'm going to end up breaking something over his head. Like a chair." "Or a chopstick in his eye. Can we go now?" ### "I actually haven't been to White Cloud Mountain yet. I'm hoping that I can stay a few extra days to celebrate my success this weekend, and visit it then." Beyond the microphone, a sea of eyes and tape recorders stared back at Danny Ferguson, baited for his next response. He flashes the winning smile as the translator next to him did his thing. His portion of the PRIME media tour for Culture Shock was one of the last of the day. The sandwich trays in the back of the executive board room were long since empty, and many of the reporters had grown tired of hearing the same canned responses. An American-looking journalist was acknowledged by the translator, who doubled as a moderator as the day grew long. "Luke De Rosier, Tampa Tribune. Danny, what do you say to the suggestion that you would abandon your partnership with Lindsay Troy when a better opportunity comes along?" "We're a team, Lake; a thrown-together one, but a team nonetheless. It's become pretty clear to me that I need to prove something to PRIME before they trust me. I have to 'audition', so to speak." He pauses for a flutter of laughter to make its way through the collected and delirious media. "Lindsay and I both have experience in the tag division in PRIME, and if partnering up with her and keeping those nice shiny belts warm is what shows I can be loyal, then I can live with it. It's not ideal, but if I was going to ditch the b...woman, it would've happened weeks ago." After the translator finishes, a booming question comes from a Chinese reporter one of the rear rows. When he finishes his short and jovial inquisition, the other local media in the room chuckle a little. Danny cocks an eyebrow at the joke, concerned that he may be the butt of it. "He asks, 'Does that mean you two are finding a lot in common?'" The English-speaking media can't help but react to the translation, and Danny throws up his arms in a mocking "whoa cowboy" manner, provoking a more exuberant reaction from the room. "Let's not go that far!" Danny shouts to the roaring audience, being sure to flash the pearly whites again for the photogs. "Don't get me wrong, we both hate Fuck You, we both look down on Captain Suleimon, and we both know what it takes to be successful in PRIME. But if you look past that...Lindsay Troy and I are really nothing alike."
