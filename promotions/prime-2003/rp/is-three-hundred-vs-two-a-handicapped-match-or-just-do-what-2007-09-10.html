Is Three Hundred vs. Two a Handicapped Match? Or: Just Do What You Do

10 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9/10/07 SOUND AND THE FURY You burned one, you poisoned another…still 298 left to go Ian Nackedy is walking down the white halls of a hospital corridor, looking quite hurried and distracted. “No.” he says quickly, then keeps walking. Trying to keep up with him, the young man known as 5 Year Old Integer Chris is nearly jogging. Looking up at his comrade, who is dressed in basically a loincloth and plate armor, the young man asks, “How about her? Can I get her number?” “You can’t have anyone’s number, 5 Year Old Integer Chris. This is time sensitive. We can’t waste any more time.” As Ian continues to hurry down the hallway, he begins rambling to himself. “We spent so much time making sure Gildenstern got better after the burns, and now Foster needs to get better after the salmonella. When are we going to get time to train, God? When?” Uh…I don’t know. “Not you, the other God. The deity and whatnot.” Oh. Hm…God doesn’t answer. Ian snaps his fingers. “Drat.” 5 Year Old Integer Chris finally catches up to Ian in his depression and tugs on his huge shield. “Uh, crazy guy who dresses up as something different every week, can we please at least stop so I can LOOK at the girls?” Ian, without stopping, unzips his manpurse (what, loincloths don’t have pockets) and pulls out a picture of Nate POTCROTCHDIRTMONKEY and a few of her friends. He hands it to the small kid. “There you go, look at that.” 5 Year Old Integer Chris smiles…until he gets a closer look at the picture. “Well, the two on the left are okay. But the third one kind of looks like a man.” And NOW Ian stops. Grabbing the picture, he finds the third one…and snarls. “Ahem,” he says, agitated. 5 Year Old Integer Chris looks up innocently. “Well, I don’t know. She kind of looks like a man, but the picture is a little far away. I mean, maybe she has some other good pictures…” “MY GIRLFRIEND HAS PLENTY OF OTHER PICTURES!” Ian shouts, once again nearly running down the corridors. 5 Year Old Integer Chris starts running to follow him, shrugging his little shoulders as he goes. “Sorry, but can you blame me? Also, her eyes are WAY TOO BIG.” When Ian doesn’t respond, 5 Year Old Integer Chris sighs. “Maybe that was wrong of me to say. Man, I hope I never make that mistake again in my life.” Ian finally gets to his destination and makes sure to close the door on his little friend, who huffs and then turns around, sees a cute nurse, and begins to cry. Ian shakes his head, then goes further into the room, which we now can tell is a hospital room. There are irritating beeps going off intermittently, but they have long since gone silent to Foster Nackedy, the patient who is lying in the stiff bed, his eyes slightly open. Ian’s nearly always goofy nature melts off of his face in a heartbeat as he sidles up into a chair next to the bed. He grabs Foster’s hand, and the CWMC semi-finalist opens his eyes. “Hey bro. How’s everything?” Ian laughs, and it’s not a ridiculous, over-the-top laugh. He shrugs. “All right, just preparing for the Unfuckables at the Pay-Per-View.” Ian frowns. “Man, this shit is hard, Foster. We came in here just wanting to have fun like we always did, but it got serious, and quick. I think we should have studied up on these gay guys, but I feel like we’ve wasted our time making these silly jokes and stupid gimmicks.” Foster nods. “Well, I always told you what you were doing was stupid and you should stop.” Ian shakes his head quickly. “Hold on, I said nothing about stopping. I’m going to do gimmick after gimmick until we’re so scrapped for ideas we come out to the ring as cock-rings. But what I’m saying is we kept being silly and putting everything off, and now it cost me both my tag partner and my brother.” The usually off-kilter SuperJerk sighs slowly. “Just kind of puts things in perspective.” Foster sits up a little, grimacing as he does so because the IV sticks a little further in. “Don’t worry about it, little brother. You’re going to do fine, because even when you’re busy being goofy, even when you don’t train for your opponent, you’ll always have something that they won’t have.” “A fifty pound shield and a broadsword?” Foster smiles. “No. The Nackedy skill and spirit. You’ve got what it takes, no matter how many family members they roast or how many costumes you wear. Just go out there, do what you do, and everything should be all right.” Ian smiles cheesily. “Thanks for the pep talk, Fos! That was motivational AND emotional!” It seems he can’t let the seriousness sit for too long, as he stands up and lifts his large shield. “I’ve got some other things to do now, but I’ll make sure they take care of you. Send you a great big dinner. You want some of the chicken?” Foster glares at him, and Ian chuckles. “Oh. Right. Bird flu. Sorry.” Chuckling, Ian leaves the room and once again his smile leaves. He stands there for a second, thinking about all of the things he has to face. The Unfuckables. The pressure. The build-up. The itching. Man, why are loincloths so chafing? Ian begins scratching furiously, then looks up in shock to see 5 Year Old Integer Chris and an older nurse looking at him awkwardly. The two are holding hands. “What are you doing?” asks Ian. “I could ask you the same thing, Pee Wee Herman.” “Come on, let’s go.” Ian grabs 5 Year Old Integer Chris and leads him down the hallway. “I’m sorry for saying your girlfriend looks like a man, but you need to take pictures with more zoom. It’s too far away for me to really tell.” Ian smiles. “You know, with the rate you’re going at now, ninety percent of the women you plow will end up looking like men.” “AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHWAIT,” 5 Year Old Integer Chris responds, then stops screaming suddenly. “Who uses the word plow?” Ian smiles. “You do in 15 years.” “What?” Again ignoring his little friend, Ian begins walking and eventually reaches another door. He goes in and finds two nurses standing around a full-casted Gildenstern, looking at him. “Hey, Gildenstern.” “Hey Ian. Hey Chris.” “It’s 5 Year Old Integer Chris!” the child screams as he kicks the cast, which does no damage. “NEVER ABBREVIATE IT OR PEOPLE DIE.” Gildenstern attempts to nod, impressed. “Fine little supporting character you’ve found yourself there.” “Just like smoking, hook ‘em while they’re young. You ready?” “Um, ready for what?” one of the timid nurses ask. One of them is a guy and is looking quite wary of the man dressed in plate armor. “Oh, we’re getting him out of the hospital, into these cool costumes, and onto training! We’ve wasted too much time being silly.” The other nurse starts to speak. “But I think you’re being silly now.” “Hush,” says Ian, knocking the nurse over with his shield. “Now, Gil, hold still.” Ian grabs an end of the cast and pulls hard. Suddenly, Gildenstern begins spinning around in circles, and ends up lying on the bed, completely naked. Thankfully, Ian is there to hand him the same armor he is wearing. “Sweet gimmicks,” says Gil, putting on his loincloth. “There’s no way we can lose this time.” “Excuse me,” says the first nurse, who is walking up to the two wrestlers with intimidation in his step. “You aren’t allowed to leave right now. This patient still has to go through a bunch of tests.” Ian steps up to the nurse and thinks of what Foster told him. How he has to go out there and do his thing, and not worry about anything. This is probably in a terribly different context than he intended, but whatever. “No, you filthy Persian. We will leave this hospital with no testing done and you can just give each other sponge baths for all we care.” Now the nurse looks confused, and somewhat repulsed. “That doesn’t make any sense at all.” “No sense?” Ian presses. “Don’t nurses give sponge baths?” “I…uh…” The nurse’s confusion turns into anger. “What the fuck! This is so random.” 5 Year Old Integer Chris, who has been roughly silent so far, laughs. “You opened up a can of worms, buddy. “Random?” Ian asks, smirking slightly. Then, he roars. “THIS… “IS… “SOUND AND FURY!” Ian sends the nurse over the hospital bed and crashing out of the window with a big front kick. Ian looks over to Gil, concerned. “Sound and Fury was too many syllables. Maybe it was too indirect of a parody?” Gil shrugs. “You defenestrated him, so it’s good enough for me.” Nodding, Ian and Gil run, with 5 Year Old Integer Chris tagging along behind them. Two nurses begin to chase them, but trip over each other, because this is a comedy and things happen that way. A man in a large white coat goes up to the second nurse and yells, “What happened, Nurse Jones?” “Uh, two men wearing Spartan gear and a baby just ran out of here. One was a patient who wasn’t cleared to leave, sir.” “Well,” the old man huffs, “were you able to catch them?” The nurse shakes his head sadly. “No sir. They were too quick from us. It seemed like they floated away from us like a fart in the wind…” The doctor looks at the nurse with something that can only be defined as pure rage. “A fart in the wind? Have you been skimming morphine again, Nurse Jones?” Nurse Jones can only smile and drift off, once again repeating himself: “Like a fart in the wind…”
