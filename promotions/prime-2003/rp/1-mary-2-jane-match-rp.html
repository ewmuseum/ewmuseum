Date: Today 3-12-05 3:35am BODA 1. Mary 2. Jane INT. HOYT AND MARY’S APARTMENT - BEDROOM -DAY The camera opens up on the solid gold RISP bible that sits on top of a wooden nightstand. Quickly the camera pulls back, exposing Hoyt’s shrine to himself. His walls are covered in pictures of him winning various matches and giving various speeches. The camera focuses on the Biblical Babes calendar that hangs on the wall with date April 3ed circled in red. The camera pans back exposing the entire room. The peacock blue carpet stands out, as the room is immaculate. The room is sparse with only a wooden dresser, a display case, a king sized bed, and two wooden end tables. Outside of God’s title that is in the display case the room looks very professional. On the bed in his mother Teresa boxer shorts lays our hero and Savior HOYT WILLIAMS. The extremely well built young man is sound asleep, no doubt dreaming of world domination.With out warning a loud knocking noise is heard from the door. Hoyt slowly rolls onto his back and opens his eyes. He says nothing hoping the noise will go away, but the loud knock is heard again. HOYT: Please leave me alone. The knock gets louder and more intense. HOYT (CONT’D): WHAT? A soft weeping is heard from behind the door. The weeping could come from only one person. Hoyt’s roommate the always teary eyed MARY WITH HER CHERRY. Hoyt trying to live like a commoner decided for two months he would live on an average person’s budget. To accomplish this he rented an apartment with his tearful eyed co-host Mary. Needless to say it’s been hell. In order to convince the landlord Mr. Farley, a deeply religious man, that a man and a women could live together with out sin they told him Hoyt was gay. That was the only way he would go for it. Mary is an extremely attractive and well-endowed woman of about twenty-five. Her hair is high and blonde and she isn’t shy about make-up. MARY (from behind the door and crying): Mr. Farley was here and he wants the rent. What are we going to do? HOYT (annoyed): Can we do this later? MARY: We have to figure this out now! HOYT: Ok give me five minutes. Hoyt rises from his bed with a yawn and a big stretch. He walks over to a CD player and turns it on. A southern gospel uplifting type of song starts to play and continues over the following shots: -Hoyt scratches his nuts.-He says his morning prayers and then beings doing jumping jacks for Jesus in rhythm with the music.-He enters a bathroom and takes a quick shower.-He shaves, combs his hair, brushes his teeth and winks at himself in the mirror then gets dressed in preppy causal clothing.-He kisses God’s title and exits his room. INT. HOYT AND MARY’S APARTMENT - LIVING ROOM - DAY Hoyt walks into the room and looks at Mary in disgust as he makeup is smeared and running down her face from crying. He calmly walks over to the red couch and sits down. The apartment is of average size with a slight 70’s feel to it. Tan drapes cover the windows and tan wallpaper dulls the walls. The only furniture in the apartment is one red couch and a TV of about 27 inches that sits on the floor. HOYT: So now what’s the problem here? Don’t you know I have a match this weekend that is going to score us some heavy money? Do you have any idea how violent and deadly this match is going to be?? Do you really think I, a member of Imperium, give two Satan’s about the darn rent? MARY: But... HOYT (interrupts): But nothing this stuff cannot happen THIS WEEK. The guy down stairs who is religious thinks I’m a homosexual while I have a match with a nut ball on SUNDAY. I JUST NEED TO RELAX. Hoyt shakes visibly disgusted by that notion. HOYT: I am God’s champion everybody in PRIME looks up to me. I even saw Aimz wearing a Hoyt rocks t-shirt the other day. Boda keeps asking me for my autograph and advice on how to beat a women. This Deville guy was begging me to join Imperium so he doesn't have to face the wrath of Los D. So again I ask you, what is the problem? Mary begins to cry and cry a lot. Her tears stream down her face like the hopes and wishes of Trashcan Man to take God’s title flowing away. Mary walks over and sits next to Hoyt in a manor to calm him and her. MARY: Well, Mr. Farley yelled at me. He said he was going to toss us out unless we had the rent money by Friday. HOYT (interrupting): ahhhh didn’t you tell him I was going to score some major cash this Sunday? MARY: He refused to listen so I invited this girl I met Jane to move in with us. She’s coming over in an hour I hope its not a big deal. HOYT: Not a big deal? You allow some whore to come into our home and you expect me not to care? MARY: She has cash money then we’ll only be 200 dollars short on the rent. Hoyt buries his hands in his face as he sits stewing in anger. HOYT: She is not moving in. I, God’s son, your boss, God’s CHAMPION will not live with some stranger….some heathen!! I have needs…ahhh you know? I need my space I don’t like the way some people smell. What if she stinks? Over my dead body is she moving in. MARY: She signed a contract. Hoyt is about to blow a fuse when there is a knock at the door. Mary opens the door and in enters MR. FARLEY. He is an older man of about 65 and looks like hell. He dresses in modern preppy Gap clothing and tries to stay hip despite his age and conservative views. Mr. FARLEY: Where is my money? MARY: Well Mr. F we have 600 for you and a new roommate. Mr. FARLEY: A what? I need another 200 dollar security deposit AND the 200 more that you owe me after I get this alleged 600 bucks. OH WAIT A MINUTE IT’S NOT A MAN, IS IT TWINKLE TOES? HOYT: This is not the time. MR. FARLEY: What is the little sissy going to get upset. HOYT: I’m not a sissy. MARY: No it’s another women and she is straight. MR. FARLEY: Fine but I want the rest of the money by Friday or out on the streets with the three Mary’s. Mr. F laughs as he exits the apartment and Hoyt is pissed. HOYT: I’m two days from the toughest match of TCM’s career, and I have to parade around as some sick homosexual savior? BOO!!! This is not right. I’m God’s SON!!! I’m going to go down stairs and send this old man Jesus bent and Heaven Bound! Just as Hoyt is about to go kick Mr. F’s ass another knock is heard at the door. Mary with her Cherry opens it up and standing there is Hoyt’s nightmare. A 20 something girl with black mesh stockings and dark mascara. She is wearing a Good Charlotte T-shirt and has long black hair. She has a rainbow patch on her green backpack along with a “God is dead” logo. This is Jane their new roommate. JANE: Hey cat’s I’m Jane. HOYT: OH HELL NO! JANE: Who’s this good-looking stud? A little lame, but a lot of hot. HOYT: I AM THE SON OF GOD AND YOU’RE A WHORE. JANE: Nice to meet you too. So what room is mine? HOYT: HOLD IT!! Where is the money??? JANE: You must be a bible banshee asking for money that quickly. Shit. Anyways I’m going to have a few freaks over for a party tonight you game? HOYT: We have bible study tonight. JANE: Fuck if I care go to the damn church or library. I need a pad to PARTY at. HOYT: DO YOU KNOW WHO I AM? JANE: Besides a first rate asshole that needs to lay off the roids? No idea. HOYT: I’m the son of GOD!! JANE: I’m the daughter of Sam Stone what’s your fucking point? HOYT: My point is…you know something I watched TV yesterday and they had a show with some JEW hosting with fags and weirdo’s caught in love triangles. I thought it was fake as wrestling until I met you. JANE: Are you asking me for a date? I’m Bi so if you and Mary ever want to make our own triangle I’m game. HOYT: AHH THIS IS DISGUSTING PLEASE LEAVE. The phone rings interrupting the culture war. Hoyt walks over to the phone, which is one of the only modern technologies in the apartment. Hoyt presses the speaker and answers the phone. HOYT: Thee Holy One Hoyt Here. What’s Your Sin? A rugged and aged voice is heard on the other end. Voice: This is thee father of the holy one. Hoyt falls to his knees. HOYT: OH GOD PLEASE LEAD ME. Voice (in a God like ghost voice): Go to the cook county lockup and bail out Duke Williams. Hoyt slowly gets up. HOYT: Is this Duke? DUKE: You can call me dad you know. HOYT: NEVER. Now what is it you want? DUKE: Well last night Leroy and myself broke in to the Lincoln Park zoo because Leroy thought monkeys watched TV when they closed the zoo at night. I said that’s bullshit and we got into a fight so we decided to see who was right. Jane sits down on the couch with a smile on her face as Hoyt rolls his eyes. DUKE: Well I’ll be God Damned it turns out Leroy was right the monkey’s do watch TV after they close the zoo. They were watching the Jefferson’s so I paid Leroy the 50 bucks I bet him and we went to looking around. There was a beautiful stud horse there and I decided to take it for a ride. After all I am a hero I preformed mouth to mouth and saved a bottle of Tequila earlier that night. HOYT: Get to the point. DUKE: Well anyways I got carried away and took the horse out of the zoo and went to riding it through downtown Chicago. Now the people seemed please to see a cowboy on a horse in the big city but the law was a bit peeved. They took me in and threw me in the slammer where I’m now calling you. Plus I had Mary Jane on me which made things worse. HOYT: That’s funny I have Mary and Jane here too. DUKE: I didn’t think you did that stuff! You learn something everyday. Now if you could bail me…… HOYT: Nice to hear from you dad…. Hoyt goes to hang up the phone. DUKE: WAIT!! There is some money in this for you. HOYT: Money?? DUKE: Yes. I only need five grand to get out on bail and I have ten grand hidden. You can have the difference. Get a pen and paper. Hoyt franticly searches for a note pad and a pen to jot the information down. DUKE: Well take 57 out of Chicago four stops past Kankakee where you’ll find a small truck stop next to it is a bar called “The Feisty Fiddler”. Go inside and grab a drink then walk over to the jukebox and play “B-6” I can’t remember what song that is. Anyways, a small key should drop from the jukebox. Take it to the men’s room and use it to open up the out of order condom machine. Inside is ten thousand dollars cash that Leroy and I hide there from the IRS a few years back. Take the money and come bail my ass out! HOYT: Will do. Talk to you later. JANE: All right, ROAD TRIP!!! EXT. The purpose 1 Corinthians 13 is not to give a 'long winded rundown' of love, but to break it down into 'tiny Aimz size' pieces so that we may more easily understand it and apply it in a practical way.So, our goal should be to demonstrate this kind of love one to another. That love is only between a white man and a white woman. It tells us clearly that where these attributes and motives are...there is also the true love of God and HOYT. Sadly, the opposite is also true. This passage is God's wake up call to those who find themselves lacking in funds. It is God reaching out to us to change our lives and make us truly what He has purposed for us to be. Servants of the RISP. If you don’t you will become like the Trashcan Man. You know lost and mentally retarded. Then like him you will also feel my wrath, which is God’s anger. JANE: CAN YOU PLEASE SHUT THE FUCK UP?!? You’ve been babbling about Jesus, Joseph, Mary, fat folks, sinners, Jews, Blacks, Hillbillies, Pollock’s, women, Imperium, the Pope, and the feeding tube lady for the last two hours. Enough already. You mind if I smoke a “J” to mellow out. HOYT: I don’t allow cigarette smoking in my car. JANE: Great because this isn’t a cigarette. Mary begins to weep. MARY: THERE IT IS!!! The Feisty Fiddler!!!! Hoyt pulls the car over as the three exit and enter the bar. INT. Feisty Fiddler Bar – Main Room –Night The bar is a hole in the wall and that’s being kind. The place is long with a bar lined up all against one wall and a few tables on the other. Pictures of John Wayne, George Bush, Billy Graham, and the confederate flag decorate the walls. A jukebox is playing a sad country song when the three walk in. The bartender is old and has a handlebar mustache and the patrons are all country bikers and wearing big hats. This is a tough country biker bar and our hero looks out of place although Jane fits in nicely. Hoyt waists no time and sits down in the center of the bar and his two lady friends follow. Every eye in the house is on the out of place Savior and his two pretty lady friends. BARTENDER: What’ll it be? HOYT: I’ll take a cherry coke and a diet sprit for the lady. The bar busts up laugh as the Bartender cracks his neck. BARTENDER: If you want FIZZ POP there is a Pizza Hut across the street. This is a bar we serve booze. The local yokels look to be getting fairly agitated by Hoyt and his friends. Jane clears her throat and steps in to save the day. JANE: I’ll take a whiskey straight up, the big guy will take a white Russian, and a long island ice tea for the chick with her hair up to God. MARY: Oh Ice Tea sounds great!! HOYT: There are no booze in our drinks is there? JANE: Nope. HOYT: Well I’m not sure about drinking anything named after a Russian but at least he’s white. ****TWO HOURS LATER***** HOYT: SO I kicked him in the ribs and said you’re the only trash here!!! Everybody in the bar is drinking and laughing. MARY: You want to see my boobs again? HUH??? WOO WOO WOOOOOOOOOO JANE: My bible says to have another women touch your breasts is an act of Godliness. HOYT: What bible says that? BARTENDER: King Ron Jeremy II. HOYT: Well if the bible says so. JANE: HONK HONK!!!! Everybody again cracks up as Jane squeezes the innocent breasts of Mary with Her Cherry. MARY: HA HA HA you made my boobs sound like a horn!! Jesus what is in these drinks? Are you sure there is no booze in this? JANE: No, none at all. BARTENDER: They are as virgin as you! Hoyt laughs uncomfortably. BARTNEDER: Tell us about your little rassling match. HOYT: Well I have this guy who thinks he’s good and hardcore. But what he doesn’t know is God has given me the strength to be even more hardcore. So this weekend I will beat the life out of this goon and become the greatest champion in Prime history. BARTENDER: Ain’t them rasslers queer? MARY: Not at all they are really nice guys. HOYT: ahh lets play some music shall we! Hoyt wobbles over to the jukebox and looks at the number written down on his hand. He places a quarter in the machine and punches up B-6. “YMCA” starts to play as a key drops to the floor. Hoyt steps on the key then picks it up. Everybody in the bar is now looking at Hoyt who’s lost any and all credibility he’s built up in the last two hours of unknowingly drinking by playing that stupid song. HOYT: I have to use the washroom. Hoyt heads into the boy’s room as the bar begins to get unruly. The girls start to slowly back up towards the door. BARTENDER: I think we got us a peter puffer. That boy is a queer. I done told you them rasslers are jizz buckets. Boy’s get the bats we going peter pitching. The men get up and grab loose weapons all around them. JANE: START UP THE CAR MARY! The Virgin Mary heads out the door and turns on the car. Jane stands back by the exit watching carefully as the men start to head to the men’s room. INT. Feisty Fiddler Bar – Men’s Room –Night The song “YMCA” is muffled through the door as Hoyt franticly sticks the key into the out of order condom machine. His hands are shaky and his vision is blurred from the drinks. He can hear the men moving in outside of the door and Hoyt knows he’s in trouble. Sweat falls from his brow as he wrestlers with the key finally getting it in and turning it. HOYT (TO HIMSELF): Here we go. Hoyt pulls the condom machine open as a few dozen old dried up condoms pour out. Hoyt franticly shuffles through them but finds no money. In the corner is a folded up piece of paper. HOYT (READING OUT LOUD): Dear Hoyt…..APRIL FOOLS….GOOD LUCK GETTING OUT ALIVE….IF YOU DO….GOOD LUCK GETTING OUT ALIVE WITH TCM….LOVE YOUR NOT SO HEAVENLY FATHER. Feisty Fiddler Bar – Main Room –Night The men are trying to kick the door in as Jane watches mindfully. JANE: OH MY GOD A BLACK GUY ON A HONDA JUST KICKED OVER YOUR BIKES!!!
