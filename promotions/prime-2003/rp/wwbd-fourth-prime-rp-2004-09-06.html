WWBD? [Fourth PRIME RP]

6 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09-06-04 Never in my wildest nightmares did I think I would be going through such torment and pain as I am being dealt right now. A broken arm is nothing compared to the broken heart of not being able to help my little girls, to hold them in my arms, to have them kiss my goodnight. I honestly had no intention of wrestling last week against Dragon Silver. Winning this Jewel in The Crown is not a high priority in my mind right now. Obviously, this kidnapper has other plans for me. He’s the one that wants to see me in the ring. He’s the one that forced me to take apart Silver this past week. I did as he requested. I showed up in San Jose. I wrestled a match I had no interest in. And what does this sick piece of shit do? Show me a video of my girls. Yeah, he’s playing mind games. He’s apparently very calculating and has put a lot of thought into what he’s doing to me. I watched a tape of Revolutions this past week. I heard Nick and Richard say over and over again that “Boda is wrestling with more intensity, more focus than ever. Boda isn’t his normal charismatic self tonight.” You’re damn right. I knew I was wrestling Silver. But that’s not who I saw when I looked at my opponent. I say my mind’s image of who took my kids. And I took out my frustration on that image. Hell, I didn’t even recognize the fans in the arena. All I wanted to do was beat the living shit out of the man in front of me, the man that took my kids. =============================================================== Time: Noon Date: Sunday, September 5th, 2004 Place: A very windy and wet Orlando, Florida. Inside Chris Sloboda’s home. =============================================================== Channel 9 has the best coverage of this monster storm. Now normally, I wouldn’t fly home between stops on the ReVolution ’tour’, but this week is different. Hell, my past month has been different. As you all know by now, my two little girls were taken away from me. I’ve been fighting this monster storm of emotion inside my heart and soul. Athena has been there for me, even coming to Orlando to help make sure my house is secure from Hurricane Frances’ impending doom. Even through all the storm preparation has taken my mind some what off of what my girls are going through at this moment, I can’t stop losing sleep over it. Athena: “Hey hon, how’s the weather outside?” I had just walked in from the back porch. As I stood there, I witnessed two of my big oak trees plucked right out of the ground, roots and all, and tossed around like a rag doll. Then with a thud, they land, one foot from the hole they spent the last 50 plus years in. Boda: “Wet, windy and miserable….. Kinda like me. Any phone calls while I was out?” Athena: “No. You thinking this asshole will call you, and tell you not to miss the show this week?” Of course, I can’t miss the show this week. I don’t need to give the jackal that has my kids any reasoning to hurt them. Or at the least, he better hope he hasn’t hurt them at all. Boda: “I figure he knows I’ll be there. Listen, I’m taking him very seriously. I don’t want to see my girls get hurt.” Athena: “Have you given any thought to what I suggested the other day?” A few days ago, Athena asked me if I felt to going to church, and talking to a priest. I thought about it, for a while. I honestly had no where else to go at that moment. I figure it couldn’t hurt to talk to a man of God. After all, it’s not like he’s going to tell me he’s the son of God, the brother of Jesus. This is a man that is a servant of God, not some loose nut thinking he’s the Son of God. Boda: “Well, honestly, it wouldn’t be a bad idea would it? But Athena, we can’t go anywhere right now. There’s a curfew. And, to be honest, I just want to sit here, look out the window and watch the world come crashing down like mine is.” Athena: “You’ll get through this Chris, I know it.” Athena is a doll. She’s been with me, every moment since this happened. Last week, I asked her to stay in the back during my match. I knew it wasn’t going to be pretty. I’m in no mood to be pretty right now. She understands that she’ll be left in the back again this week when I face Hoyt Williams in another Jewel In The Crown match. I pop my head into the living room, as the sound of my wide screen plasma television and my surround sound comes to life. Athena has flipped on Channel 9, to see the latest up to the minute news as Frances makes its slow and powerful trek across the state. Athena: “Airports are closed until further notice. Let’s hope we can fly to Buffalo Tuesday morning.” Buffalo……. The next stop on the path to King of Kings. Buffalo….. Another arena, another match, another chance to hopefully see my girls. Boda: “I’m sure it’ll be fine by Tuesday. After all, my fate is in God’s hand…. Isn’t it?” Well, not so much God, but the self proclaimed Son of God… Hoyt Williams. I can only hope that once I get my girls back in my arms, that’ll they’ll never grow up like Hoyt. Here’s a kid, whose father loved him, whose father took him to PCW show, after PCW show, so the kid could learn about this business. I remember meeting Hoyt when he was merely 16-17 years of age. He’d be backstage, with Duke Williams, his REAL father, at PCW shows. He’d look up to a lot of us back there. Ivan, Jeremy, Kannon, me. But something changed about the boy. He forgot where his loyalties lay. He turned his back on his past, and made up some cockamamie story about being the second son of God and the brother of Jesus. So far, that’s gotten him nowhere. He’s not become the best in this business like he said his ‘destiny’ is. He preaches about RISP. But tends to forget about the ass WHOOPINGS he’s taken over the years. Hoyt believes he’s bigger than life itself. That makes beating him so much easier. Yeah Hoyt and I have tangoed before. In Old School Wrestling, he’s never beaten me. I beat him the night I made my PRIME debut, but he hightailed it with some bullshit stipulation. That’s okay. He had no clue who was coming to face him that night. This week, he has no excuses, and he won’t be able to use any fucked up stipulations when I beat him pillar to post. Athena: “What’s going on in your head today?” Maybe I should tell her that all I can seem to focus on right now is taking Hoyt out of the picture for King of Kings this week. At least my mind is not 100 percent focused on my girls right now. But I know what I have to do this week. But my only problem with this is ‘is this what the kidnapper wants?’ Does he want to see me win this Jewel in The Crown tournament. Does he want to see me get my ass kicked by Hoyt? All he’s ever told me is he wants to see me wrestle. He’s yet to give me an idea as to how far he wants me to go before I get my kids back. Maybe he’s a sicko, that wants to see me destroy every opponent thrown at me the next few weeks. Maybe he wants to see me win the Jewel in The Crown. Hell, he probably wants to see me be the Global Champion. I fought with such intensity and focus last week, and it’s all because of this person that holds my girls in some log cabin somewhere. If he is indeed some sick fan, then he’ll want to see me win with authority week after week. Or is he just waiting for me to fall flat on my face when the time is right. I do know that Hoyt isn’t going to be the one to make me fall on my face. Boda: “Nothing much. Just thinking about K K and Care-bear.” Pet names. I’ve called Mackenzie ’K K’ since she was about 2 years old. Carleigh is ’Care-bear’ because the kid’s got a sick obsession with those little bears from Care-A-lot. I can’t blame her. She’s got the same obsession gene that I do. I’m obsessed with finding them right now. I’m obsessed with doing what ever it takes to make it through each Revolutions, and to seeing my girls. WWJD? What Would Jesus Do? He’d close his eyes, and not watch this weeks show. WWHD? What Would Hoyt Do? I don’t know, but I do know if I was Hoyt, I’d not take this weeks match too personally. I’m not in there with the Son of God…… I’m in there with the son of a bitch that took my kids away from me. It just happens that Hoyt’s the unlucky one this week that’ll take the wrath of Boda. WWBD? What Would Boda Do? Anything it takes.
