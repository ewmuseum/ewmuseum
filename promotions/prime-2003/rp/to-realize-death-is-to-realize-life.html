To Realize Death is to Realize Life

Date: ? I was watching it on TV from my own personal hell. But my own personal hell paled in comparison. “You still watching that?” I didn’t see the nurse come in to take my vitals. I moved the TV off to the side. “I just can’t believe it. New Orleans is completely buried in water. Look at the people suffering.” Even though I knew something was going to happen when I heard the original reports, I just can’t fathom what the people are going through. “I donated one hundred dollars myself to the aid effort.” The Nurse stated as she wrapped my arm in the cuff. My blood pressure had been a tad high since I was admitted. They were watching it closely. BEEP I continued watching the rescue efforts on the TV. I had experienced a rather dangerous situation with my health, but I am receiving the health care I need. Rescue efforts in the Gulf Coast can only do so much. So many people trapped and stranded, it makes me wonder what anyone could have done to deserve such a fate. BEEP No water… BEEP No food… BEEP No hope? BEEP BEEP BEEEEEEEEEEEP! The cuff deflated and my blood pressure was back to normal. “One nineteen over seventy-eight, very Mr. Sirrajin.” She took my temperature next. As she removed the thermometer, I tried to reason with her. “You think you could let me out of here so I can donate to the relief fund too?” I smiled as did she. But she wasn’t party to my game. “Soon enough Mr. Sirrajin. Now here is your pill and your breakfast will be here soon.” I grimaced as I stuck the foul tasting pill in my mouth. I couldn’t swallow the water fast enough and the after taste was just as bad. As she left the room, I looked at my arm. The swelling had pretty much dissipated from the botched CT scan the other day. I had been wrestling for many a year and never have I felt pain like I had that day. My arm felt warm, I was finally going to find out what was wrong with me. After I had suffered the last couple days trying to breathe, I had had enough. I couldn’t do the simplest thing without lacking oxygen. I was lacking the ability that most people take for granted. I couldn’t pull enough oxygen into my body to live. Lord knows I tried, but that only got me a heart rate that I could hear over the loudest of noises. I was a lucky person and I still consider myself lucky to this day. It was a shock to my system when my vein ruptured, allowing the warm clear liquid to enter my arm. I watched as the liquid was being pumped, blowing my arm up like a cheap party balloon. I was in extreme pain as the flow finally stopped but my arm was already damaged. I cringed as the IV moved around in my arm. A day later, I went for the scan again and it went off without a problem this time. Pulmonary Embolus… a blood clot in my lung. I was actually blessed. I was told numerous times that I was lucky that it didn’t travel to my brain instead. I was told that I was lucky that it didn’t go to my heart instead. Yeah, that made me feel wonderful in the end. I was basically told that I was lucky to be alive, that I was lucky to have a heartbeat. Sure I considered myself lucky, but I did not need to be told about it. Nothing ever made me feel the way this did. You never look at your life and realize your own mortality until you have an experience that could very well have left you buried six feet under the life line. I experienced the stress that came with it, wondering if I would still be around tomorrow. I had disappeared from PRIME after my disappointing loss to Hoyt Williams at Colossus. It crushed my will to fight, my will to continue in the career that I had loved for so long. What did I have left? I wandered my hometown for a week before tragedy struck me and I ended up in the hospital. I had wanted to leave PRIME, to leave wrestling for good. I wanted to be a normal person; I wanted to have a normal life. Professional wrestling can change your life and it can change you. I had decided that I would never go back. But after all this, I was not so sure anymore. As my so-called breakfast arrived at my bedside, I sighed. I sighed a little deeper as I removed the lid seeing the garbage presented to me. Malnutrition was not something I had wanted to experience, but it was either that or spend the rest of the day in the washroom. I returned the lid, covering up the poor excuse for food that they felt like shoving down my throat. I leaned back in my uncomfortable bed and I knew what I had to do. “I have to get the hell outta here soon.” ----------------------------------------------------------------------\n My first day at home was rather surreal. When everything happened at first, I wondered what was wrong with me. I had wondered at one point if I would ever return to my loft in the downtown area of Winnipeg. But here I was, in my home in rather decent health but I was to be on medication for months now. I felt like a relaxing week was long overdue. Even in the week previous to my condition, I had not relaxed and I had not slept well. My dreams were coming back to haunt me. I had lost to Hoyt Williams, I had lost my Universal Championship and I had lost my will to fight. No matter what I did, it seemed as if the bad guy always won. The wrong person was victorious and I felt cheated and alone. What was I to do now? I have been ordered to leave my physicality to a minimum and yet, I wanted to get up off my chair and jog. I wanted to lift weights. I wanted to get back in shape after this whole ordeal. But for the first little while, heavy lifting would almost kill me. I even paced the floor at one point wondering what I could possibly do. But I remembered what I had been watching in the hospital. Not that my tiny donations would really help, but I had to do something. The way I felt was nothing compared to those poor people down in the Gulf Coast. I had a home, I had food and water. They no longer had any of that and it was something that so many take for granted. I picked up the phone and dialed the 800 number that had been flashed so many times on the screen. The International Red Cross had asked for my help, so I had to comply, I had to do my little part. “Hi, I would like to pledge twenty five thousand dollars to the relief efforts.” The high dollar amount didn’t make me feel any better, but the fact that a hot meal would arrive to help nourish someone who was starving helped me feel like a million dollars. ---------------------------------------------------------------/ As the sun wrapped around my window and shined in my face, I woke up to find that I had dosed off while watching CNN. The relief efforts continued and I had to wonder when it would all end. As I rolled from my bed, a breeze came in the window. It was rather cool but I relished the air hitting my body. For once, I could open my windows and let some fresh air in. The curtains danced against the walls, always falling back to the same place when the wind died down. I made my way into the kitchen to grab a beverage. My laptop was sitting open on the island in the middle of the kitchen. I must have left it open earlier. As I cracked the seal on my bottle of Dr. Pepper, I sat in front of the display, the light reflecting off my skin. I opened the main website for PRIME. I hadn’t visited the site since before my medical troubles. I had missed the witty updates and the humor, but most of all I missed the scream of the fans. I opened the link to take a look at the latest brackets for the Jewel in the Crown tournament. The first name that caught my eyes was Aimz. Oh Amy Campbell. How we have had our difficulties in the past. All we wanted to do was live our relationship in the dark, away from the flashing of camera and the annoying voices of the public reporters. But how possible could it have been to live in secret? Someone was bound to find out and someone did. Could we have really expected any less? Soon I know everything will come to a head and I can understand the most difficult times or only ahead of us. I only have your smile to look forward to, don’t break my heart. As I read more on the page, I saw two names that surprised me. Tchu… Matt Ward in PRIME? The same Matt Ward that always said he would never return to the ring? The same Matt Ward who was my arch rival in the Underground? How is it possible? He had a life with Mary, he had a baby girl. What could possibly have made him come back to the ring? What else did he feel he had left to prove? The second name? KILLEAN SIRRAJIN?!?! I looked in shock at the screen. Surely PRIME knew about my current condition. No… wait. I didn’t even have an active contract. It expired shortly after Colossus and yet, here were the very letters that spelt my name opposite Peter Vetra. At that point only one name came to mind. The Imperium. Yes they were still an active force in PRIME. Maybe Hoyt had something to do with this. All I knew is that I don’t have any plans of returning. I can hear the fans calling, but the constant beeping that I heard from the machines in the hospital had drowned out the cries of the people who only wanted The Supreme Machine back. My health was the first thing on my mind Or so I thought… The phone rang and I knew right away who it was. I don’t believe in telepathy but it was like I heard her voice in my mind mere seconds earlier. She was calling my name and now she was dialing the digits… “Why is your name in the JiTC draw?” She spoke calmly yet with a purpose. It was like I was hiding something from her. Oh if only she knew how confused I was. “I wish I could tell you Amy, honestly.” I tried to sound as sincere as I could but I was honestly feeling like the walking dead. I had developed a head cold in the past day and I was only wishing for the time to pass away so I could get on with the things that I loved to do. “I am not kidding; I have no idea what my name is doing there. You know as well as I that I am not an active competitor.” Boy was she speechless for a moment. She thought I was lying to her, she thought I was keeping things from her. Next thing I knew, this was going to turn into the Maury show and I would be accused of cheating. “Well something is up and I am curious as to what it is Killean.” The ever inquisitive mind of that attractive redhead was really working hard now. I just wanted to tell her to call someone who knows about all this and same our time. But I couldn’t, I just didn’t want to cut off her beautiful voice as it captivated me and captured my very soul. I knew I belonged to her now, but honestly could we go further ahead without looking back? Suddenly, the line beeped. Almost as if on queue, I looked at the call display. The name flashed on the little screen and caught most of my attention, tearing it away from Amy for the moment. “Caller waiting – Taun Pham.” It kept flashing and I knew that soon, the beeping would stop and Pham would abandon the contact. But did I really want him to do that? Maybe I had changed my mind already? No, it couldn’t be, a name strewn across a call display couldn’t possibly change my mind. But my curiosity was peaked and I had to see what the call was all about. It could be just a get well wish but I had received a large arrangement and card from them already. “Amy I have another call, it’s Taun.” “Ok, I’ll be here…” she still wanted an answer. And hopefully after I talked to Taun Pham, I would have an answer for her, because I really didn’t know what the hell to tell her. I pressed the button on the phone to switch the line over. “Hello?” I asked, even though I knew full well that it was the troubled co-owner of PRIME on the other end, waiting patiently for me to answer. “Killean, its Taun…” I thought I knew who it was on the other end, so why was I still surprised to hear his voice? “I have a proposition for you.” ---------------------------------------------------------------------/ The invitation was there… The RSVP date was set. Could I possibly take the chance again? Could I really do anymore? The people were asking for it… Screaming the name they screamed so many times in the past. I could almost hear it in my head… KILLEAN! The lights on the stage… KILLEAN! The music penetrating the ear drums of so many PRIME-ates… KILLEAN! I could hear them again… KILLEAN! The constant beep of the hospital machines is fading from memory now. KILLEAN! What harm was there in trying again? KILLEAN! Will I answer the call? KILLEAN! Would I ever be happy again until I ran the gauntlet just once more?
