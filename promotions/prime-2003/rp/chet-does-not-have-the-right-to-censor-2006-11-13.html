Chet does not have the Right To Censor.

13 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 13th The scene: An empty arena, save for one man. The commentary desk being the focus, the man of the moment being the mysterious "N", who so boldly decided to plot an attack of PRIME itself by attaching explosives to the Jewel In The Crown trophy. Sitting behind the desk with the maniacal grin etched onto his mask, he adjusts his wide-brimmed hat and clears his throat. "And once again, I bid you, the viewers of PRIME, a good evening. I must be brief, for it is not often one gets the chance for these little one-on-one chats, especially since in a matter of minutes, the seats behind me and to my sides will be full of… you. Sadly, my latest actions, mostly unknown to so many of those of you sitting behind a television screen, have made me rather unpopular with the security here. Allow me then, for those of you without the benefit of being able to attend the last Revolution in person, to show you something that the people in charge of his company decided you shouldn’t see, which I liberated from the cutting room floor." The scene now: Revolution 110. Chet Worth stands beside a glorious looking assembly of gold and silver, standing on a marble base and decorated with regal purple ribbons. A microphone is in his hand but because of a lack of a recorded audio track, N’s commentary takes the place of his speech. "This would have been the moment our friend here, Mr. Worth, decided to unveil the new trophy for the participants to marvel and muse at. I’m rather sure at this point he was saying how a little collection of precious metals was a symbol of prestige and fame. And it is right… HERE…" Chet jumps out of his skin as a spark explodes from the trophy, leaping out of the way as a second one follows it. A third one signifies the countdown complete, with a small detonation sending chunks of hot and twisted metal flying in all directions, accompanied by shreds of burning ribbon. "HERE… is the moment where I tell the world what I think it is a symbol for. It represents a fine moment ruined by unfair play. It represents the moment where the winner of this tournament will be announced because he had his arm on the ropes for leverage and the referee failed to notice, or his manager or valet will be on the apron to distract the referee for just long enough to dig out some kind of weapon. All the glamour, the honour, the very things people seek in this game, shattered by an illegal chair shot." Men with fire extinguishers sprint to the wreckage of the trophy, attempting to get the situation under control. The look on Chet’s face could only be described in one way. :O And it is this frame of "liberated" footage that the video freezes on. "I know some of you may view me as a bully, using the threat of violence to attain my desires. While the ever-so-popular designation of terrorist may be dealt out these days with impunity, I object to this heavily. The usual modus operandi of a terrorist would be to threaten the people in pursuit of personal satisfaction. While I do have a personal stake in this, which would be the improvement of the spirit of competition, the result would naturally benefit the viewers of PRIME and set an example to other promotions." The speech is interrupted as a door far in the background opens, spilling light into the arena and disturbing N. His fingers wiggle in a moment of panic, quickly spinning his head towards the camera. "And regrettably, I must end things here. A good evening to you. Viva la RevolutioN."
