Sonny and Kenjiro ITO Own You In Three Small Tales (ReV 107 vs. Tony Gamble/???)

9 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/06 Ladies and gentlemen: Welcome to my world. My name is Sonny Gabriel Silver. If you haven’t heard of me by now, may you all get to first base with an electric socket. My exploits are many and my accomplishments are endless. I’m a notorious being in the wrestling world and my staples have already been left upon this great, yet fickle industry we call professional wrestling. But…why am I here? Why PRIME? I’ve been everywhere. From the thankless toiler of yesteryear that could barely scrape together enough cash for a loaf of bread and something to put on it. All the way to the thousands of fans calling for my head when I won the most prestigious wrestling title in The Squared Circle Championship in the middle of Madison Square Garden. To leaving the business a disenfranchised man with the promise of never returning. To hearing it call back to me like a mother calling out for her lost child. I’ve seen the best and I’ve seen the worst. And as the VERY tired cliché says… I’m back. I have a second chance laid before me now. A chance to show people the REAL me. My eyes have been opened for the first time in years. I see that path in front of me more so than I have in the past eighteen years of my career and it shines. I RULE THIS PLACE. Yeah. You fucking heard me. I am Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME. Primetime Deion Sanders doesn’t really know what the fuck he’s talking about. I built this house with my blood and my sweat and my own two bare fucking hands. My father, “Big” Richard Silver built this house before me and his father, “Bigger” Richard Silver started this whole brainchild. Then somewhere along the way, I think I got pretty drunk and lost PRIME in a card game to some fussin’ and a-feudin’ Asians. Hin Seemore Ass Ho or something. Then they all hired some big Polish guy in a game of “Let’s Kidnap One Another” and somehow, it all ended up with those wacky gooks arguing over who would eat that cat, Mr. Bojangles. Then Hin See ate it when nobody was looking, then the cat was all “FUCK, NO! YOU’RE GETTING INDIGESTION AND A FLAMING CASE OF DIARRHEA, BITCH!” Then Hin See died and only about two people were sad (I assume he had a wife and kid.) Then Tyler Nelson and his millions stormed in. He ruled the place with an iron fist. Then Chet Worth had his ass thrown out on the street with some Willy Wonka Golden Ticket thing. Probably got it from being such a huge fatass or something. I dunno. And now, here I stand once again. The voices of my forefathers spoketh unto me and they said, “Dear GOD, you’ve gotten sloppy. A couple years ago, you would’ve never let a OCD-ridden porker take over the house you built.” And I said, “Dear God, you’re right! I was too busy owning the wrestling world, I forgot all about PRIME!” Then they were all “Fucking A!” Actually, that might’ve been Kyle Lamen getting pissed that Jimi Hendrix wasn’t in heaven to teach him how to stop sucking ass at playing guitar. … Wait. Where was I? Oh, yeah. STAY OUT OF MY BOOZE! PRIME is mine! And now, watch the following blurb in progress where me and my CEO of Awesome, Mr. Ito, prepare for a battle… A battle with the midcard. SONNY SILVER AND KENJIRO ITO STAR IN MIDCARD BATTLESCHAPTER ONE: WE’RE GONNA CUT YOU. “Ito-san, I don’t understand the purpose of this. Why must I be blindfolded? Dear God… you’re not sending me to be raped by gangbangers, are you? I do not approve of this!” “Hush! We’re almost there!” Beautiful blue skies are the canopy for that great urban forest known as Seattle, located in Pioneer Square. Why the hell our story took us here… nobody knows. This certainly wasn’t where the show was going to take place, but this was all Ito’s idea. Silver was ahead of him with a blindfold tied around his head tightly. Ito was following him shortly behind, shoving him. A couple times when Sonny was yelling at him to take the damn thing off, Ito thought it would’ve been hilarious to shove him into traffic. Not get hit by a bus. Maybe a crappy Geo or something. Then he remembered that if he were to kill Sonny before he could officially make his in-ring debut, then Ito would have to wrestle again… something that the Cocky Osakan was too good (read: Too lazy) to do. “That’s it.” Sonny grounded himself. “Either the fucking thing goes off or you’re going back to Hong Kong in one of your people’s shitheap of a boat. A rickshaw!” “Son, I’m not even Chinese and you’re thinking of a junk.” “Yes, I know they’re pieces of junk, so stop trying to change the subject. Blindfold. Off!” The blindfold was removed, leaving Sonny to stand in the middle of a not-so-busy Pioneer Square. It was the middle of the afternoon where people were still killing themselves in traffic or struggling to meet a deadline. Silver took solace in that fact and smiled. “Good thing about being a wrestler. No paperwork. We just get to hit things. But to the point: why are we here?” Ito, decked out in a fairly nice pair of black pants and a powder-blue dress shirt, raised a finger. “I’ll tell you why: I figured you ought to start at home when you prepare for your descent into mid-card hell, my friend.” Sonny took in the all-too-familiar surroundings. The former Seattle native (now a resident of Honolulu, Hawaii with his wife and kid.) put his hands on his shoulders and eyed some of the passers-by. “Kenjiro, take a note.” The manager for Sonny Silver took out a small ball-point pen and notepad and began jotting down pictures of himself banging Jessica Alba while Sonny spoke. “I, Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME, vow to smite my enemies into oblivion and rule on high as the Lord, God, and King of Sports Entertainment. Then, I shall hold a Diva Search to get rid of all the ugly abortos and agfays running around. Tony Gamble… Chanandaler Ta-sawnda… those other people. Winters… you know. THEM. The mid-carders.” Ito stopped drawing his lewd picture to look up at Sonny. “What’s an agfay and what’s an aborto?” “Agfay is piglatin for fag and an aborto is a Portuguese word for people that should be aborted. They’re gonna be catch-phrases, too. Spread the word.” “Ah. I figure you might like it here since you know the terrain and it’s mostly filled with commoners and dirty underfed bums that are more than happy to fight for scraps.” An interesting idea began to form in the head of Sonny Silver. May God help us all. “I GOT IT!” Ito looked up. “What?” “I just had a great idea for a federation! Airing opposite PRIME every Wednesday Night… the WBF!” Ito suddenly dropped his pen and paper on the sidewalk. NO FUCKING WAY could Sonny be this deranged. The WBF? “You’re… not serious… are you?” Kenjiro asked incredulously. “I am! The WBF! The World Bumfighting Federation!” Whew. Ito let out a sigh of relief that Sonny hadn’t gone THAT far off the deep end. Now bumfighting, he could get on board with. “After my training, we’re gonna have our talent scouts look for some bums. Now… how do we train for the midcard, Ito?” The former Jolt Triple Crown champion stood there and scratched his head, taking in the surroundings as well. Several yards away, a very scrawny and dirty fellow was tossing out sunflower seeds to a flock of birds on a park bench. That wheel in his head began to turn and a look filled with nothing less than sinister intentions made him purse his lips. “Son… I’ve got a plan. Your first test begins now.” ITO pointed off in the distance to the old haggard-looking man. Mr. Silver takes notice and an evil grin also forms… MIDCARD BATTLES: CHAPTER TWO:I TOLD YOU WE WERE GONNA CUT YOU. BITCH. THE FOLLOWING SEGUE HAS BEEN BROUGHT TO YOU BY YJ STINGER. CATCH THE BUZZ. FEEL THE STING. ITO looks around Pioneer Square and scratches his head. “What the fuck was that?” “An unnecessary cutaway,” Silver commented. “I need sponsors to keep this going.” “Oh.” The two advance on the bum, who failed to notice the two very buff individuals observing him closely. The seemingly harmless man is still sitting on the bench, now pulling out a harmonica. With flawless effort, the bum started playing a very soulful tune on it. Passers-by even procured change from their pockets and tossed loose change into an empty Starbucks coffee cup. Looking pleased at his efforts to collect some much-needed moolah, the bum continued playing his song and tapped his feet to the tune. For a very irregular sight, it was quite peaceful sounds to most. Not to ITO. He plugged his ears and grimaced at the old man playing his sounds. “Ugh… who the hell would listen to this? Where’s the rap beats? I can’t even hear Snoop Dogg!” Next to him, Silver was singing along with the song that he knew all too well. “We’re an American band… We’re an American band… We’re comin’ to your town…” “SONNY!” “AH! GONNA BURN IT DOWN!” Regaining his composure, the Silver Lining of PRIME adjusted the collar of his suit. “Yes, right. Bumfighting. So… what are we gonna do?” “Well, Son, the mid-carder usually knows when to mind their business. They’re spineless guppies that get kicked around by the much bigger fish in the sea. That’s you and me. “Ah-ha.” “And what we’re going to do now is get ourselves ready for that Gamble queer and whoever he pulled out of the sewers to work with him.” Silver turned his head and asked. “How?” A wry smile formed in the face of the CEO of Awesome as he pointed a finger at the musically-inclined hobo. “YOU! YOU DIRT-SUCKING DINGLEBERRY HANGING OFF THE ASS OF LIFE!” He looked up to see a big, angry Japanese man charging full-bore at him. Before he could even reply, ITO tackled him to the ground and started putting a series of very brutal stomps into the rib cage of the bum. People looking on were scared off quickly for fear that this crazy blonde-haired Asian man was gonna jump them, too. While ITO was screaming incoherently… something about making the bum eat his harmonica for playing Grand Funk Railroad around him… Sonny was taking notes on a scrap of paper. “Kick bum… until he stops twitching… That’s a good way to do it.” Sonny looked at the pen and suddenly, the gears turned in his head. “ITO… stop.” ITO was ready to deliver a hard right to the head of the nearly unconscious hobo, but stopped and looked up at his cohort. “Yeah?” “I have a plan. Lemme see.” Sonny took the pen and took a moment to start sketching on the bum’s face. After a few moments, he backed away and admired his creative handiwork. A bigger smile was drawn around the edge of his face. ITO was laughing his ass off at this point while Sonny was raising his arms to the sky for all to hear. “I’M MR. SILVER, CHAIRMAN OF PRIME, DAMN IT! AND TONY GAMBLE IS GONNA GET THE SHITKICKING OF A LIFETIME! THIS IS NEW SON CITY, SONNY-DAMN IT!” Sonny and ITO continue to beat the living Hell out of the bum, making it look like something you’d see out of American History X. Fists, feet, knees, and elbows flew every which way and blood splattered on the concrete. The sounds of ribs and arms cracking under the pressure of a precision strike could be heard by all, but it seemed that nobody was lifting a finger to come to the aid of this man in need that was screaming for help. ITO stopped wailing on the bum and looked around at the very eerie realization that nobody was doing a thing around them now. “Son?” “Yeah?” “How come nobody’s doing anything?” “There’s a camera here. As far as they’re concerned, we’re filming for a promo or something. Yes. >.>
