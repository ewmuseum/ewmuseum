"All true. And I don’t mean to go all Ebenezer Scrooge here, but I have another question: why is our son born on March 14th? Fate’s got a fuckin’ perverse sense of humor." "You know why, Chan." Because I’m going to be the first Universal Champion to win the Dual Halo." "Because that’s what makes you happy. You love being the best. You’ve learned to love others, but what makes you happiest is winning." "Don’t they say that all great winners are defined by a near-psychotic need to win? "Fate," Marie laughed quietly, "doesn’t comment on individual cases." "And if I said you were wrong? That what would really make me happy is you and our son? That I would trade my life for ours? All the thrashing and fighting and praying and hoping wouldn’t keep this fantasy alive. I felt my own eyes drooping, but I could hear Marie murmuring. "Chan, will you say goodnight to him?" "Good night," I started, choked up and barely able to stammer out his name. "…Benjamin." /~/~/~/~/ Sunday, March 7th 7:31 A.M. "…fuck." Knowing from the beginning that something is a dream doesn’t make it any easier to leave behind. I shook the sleep from my head. "…fuckin’ Christ." But that’s the choice you make. You sleep, you dream when you’re dead. And you live when you’ve got the goddamn gift of being alive. Otherwise, you never make any choice that’s worth a damn because you’re too worried about missing out. "...fuckin’ dreams." So I woke up that Sunday, six days before I was gonna beat Jason Snow, seven days before I was gonna win the Dual Halo. I’d made my bed, and goddamn it if I wasn’t gonna get real cozy and sleep in that motherfucker. Figuratively speaking, that is. Best days of your life, worst days of your life, they don’t come around too often. And with one on the horizon, I wasn’t gonna rest on my laurels. It was time to bleed, sweat, and cry. To make the most of choices that couldn't be undone.
