Lethal Weapons

23 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 01/23/06 Central police headquarters is buzzing with activity. Uniformed officers and plain clothed detectives hustle about between the cubicles on the 4th floor in the crime unit, ringing phones providing an annoying background to the murmur of conversation. As we make our way through the office area, zig-zagging through the flurry of commotion, myriad people pass in front of the view in almost a synchronized fashion. Our journey finally culminates at the captain’s office, the name scribed onto the glass window next to the door reading ‘Capt. Ed Murphy’. The tall, salt and pepper (more salt than pepper) haired captain yanks open the door to his office and bellows out at full strength. Capt. Murphy: RIGGS!!! MURTAUGH!!! GET IN HERE!!! A few heads turn as the captain slams the door to his office, sending a crack through the glass with his name etched into it. Everyone is soon back to work, obviously used to that kind of outburst coming from the over-stressed captain. After a few moments, our view turns to two men approaching the captain’s office. Sergeant Martin Riggs (aka Danny Ferguson) struts along in an impeccable looking dark green Armani suit with a molten red tie that contrasts his fire colored hair. The handsome detective with the movie star looks wears a smug look on his face, but still finds time to be annoyed. Riggs: What does that blowhard want now? Doesn’t he know it’s almost time for our spa treatment? Walking stride for stride with Riggs is his partner, Roger Murtaugh (aka Chandler Tsonda). The slightly older, yet better looking (in his own mind, anyway) sergeant is garnished in an impressive ensemble hand picked from the runways of Milan, consisting of grey slacks and a white mock turtle neck. A pair of designer sunglasses covers his eyes even though he’s inside, because that’s the ‘in’ thing. You’d mistake him for a model if he wasn’t at police headquarters. Murtaugh: Does it really matter? We go in there, he hollers at us for a while, then we leave and go do our own thing anyway. It’s purely procedural in order to keep up appearances. Riggs: Yeah, but just once I’d like to go in there and tell the over-the-hill bastard where to pack the shit he tosses at us. Murtaugh looks over and grins at his partner. Murtaugh: We’re the stars of the show, Riggs. If you want to tell him where to go, tell him where to go. It’s not like anything will happen to us. Riggs: We do totally pwn, don’t we? Murtaugh: How do I look? Murtaugh stops, then does a spin befitting the end of the runway, finishing with a snappy pose. Riggs: You look fine, let’s get this over with! The two detectives continue on to the captain’s office, shooting a few looks of disdain for the other people. Murphy sits behind his desk, the cheap short-sleeved dress shirt and tie screaming ‘beat cop’. He takes a look at the two men and is immediately puzzled. Capt. Murphy: Who in the hell are you two clowns? I called for Riggs and Murtaugh, not a couple of insurance salesmen! Throwing his head back in mock laughter, Riggs points a finger at the captain. Riggs: That’s a good one, chief! I never saw that one coming. He quickly looks over at Murtaugh and rolls his eyes. Capt. Murphy: What’s with the suits? You guys going to a funeral or something? Riggs: Well, the ‘rugged, flannel shirt and blue jeans’ look is totally played out, and since I’m a star, I need to look the part. The captain points to Murtaugh. Capt. Murphy: And what’s your excuse? What happened to those cheap blazers and slacks you used to wear? Murtaugh gasps in horror, turning to Riggs. Murtaugh: Is he serious? Murphy brushes off the comments with his hands. Capt. Murphy: You two prima donnas think you’re hot shit, huh? Riggs and Murtaugh: Well, yeah. Capt. Murphy: You’re probably right. But anyway, there’s a big arms deal rumored to be going down at the docks next Wednesday. I want you two on it. Murtaugh: An arms deal? Wouldn’t that be better suited to someone a little more….gay? This ain’t no Captain Sulu over here. Riggs hits his partner on the arm. Riggs: He means guns. Murtaugh: Oh. Riggs reaches into his suit jacket and produces a cell phone, feverishly punching a number onto the keypad. Murtaugh digs his PDA out and removes the stylus and begins to tap on the screen feverishly. Capt. Murphy simply looks on, dumbstruck. Riggs: Hello, Reggie…what do I have on the schedule for next Wednesday, say around ten o’clock or so? As Riggs listens to the other end of the, Murtaugh shakes his head sympathetically. Riggs: I’d sure like to help you out there, boss, but I’ve got to wrestle that night. Sgt. Riggs slaps his cell phone shut, mock despair in his voice. Murtaugh: Same here, cap’n. Turns out I’m faced with the prospect of carrying the Rev. Jerry Fallwell in what must be some sort of ‘Wrestling with the Stars’-type of event. It’ll be a tough task, indeed, but one I’m most confident I can handle. The captain is thoroughly confused by now, shifting his eyes from left to right. Capt. Murphy: What in the hell are you two talking about? You’re cops for Christ’s sake! Riggs: Oh, right! *wink wink* We’ll be glad to handle that little errand for you! Roger turns to Martin, who gives him a reassuring look. Murtaugh: As long as it doesn’t mess up my manicure. Capt. Murphy: Well then get the hell out of here already! Riggs: Perhaps you should try some Cialis for that impotence problem, captain. The captain pounds on his desk, his face turning a neat shade of bright red. Capt. Murphy: GET OUT!! Murtaugh: You know, that color red looks terrible on you. Try something more vibrant, perhaps a purple or electric blue! ‘CUUUTT!!’ Ferguson throws his arms up innocently. Danny: What? Taking a different angle, one farther back, we see the movie set that had just been witnessed. The cameras and booms are strung all over the place, and the crew begins to scurry about now that the film has quit rolling. Richard Donner jumps out of his directors chair and walks onto the set. Donner: What’s up with all the ad-lib from you two? I think you might have actually gotten in three words from the script. Danny: It obviously needed a little touching up, Dicky-baby. I think what we just laid down was pure GOLD, wouldn’t you agree Chandler? Chandler: Most indubitably! You can’t hold down natural talent, Richard. We’re professionals. Danny: Well, I’m a professional. You’re still a little raw… Tsonda leers at Ferguson. Danny: …but, certainly a superstar in the making! Chandler: Thanks. Ferguson waves off the make-up personnel that are now swarming the set. Chandler doesn’t pass on the chance to get touched up. Danny: You see, Dick, I know you’re used to dealing with character actors like Gibson and Glover in these roles. I’m fine with that, but A-list talent like myself and Chandler here have to be able to breathe life into these dreary roles. “I second that!” The voice off camera is unmistakable as that of the King of the Nicaraguan Death Match, Tyler Nelson. The CEO struts on to the set, a navy blue Italian suit with black shoes and tie making up his wardrobe. He even offers a soft ‘golf clap’ to the A-Listers. Tyler: I thought that scene was dead on what we’re looking for. Well done! Ferguson and Tsonda offer shit-eating grins to Donner. Danny: Why thank you, Mr. Nelson! That means quite a bit coming from a man of your stature and status. Richard Donner sighs and walks over to Nelson, intercepting him before he reaches Ferguson and Tsonda. He turns Nelson’s back to him and speaks under his breath. Richard: Look, Tyler… Tyler: That’s Mr. Nelson to you, Dick. Richard: Whatever…Mr. Nelson…these two guys are totally butchering the screenplay. I’m not sure if this project is going to- Nelson cuts the director off mid-sentence. Tyler: I think you had better shut up now and listen for a moment. This movie will get made, with these two starring, and you’re going to make it huge. This movie will get PRIME some much needed crossover appeal, and as long as I’m bankrolling it things will get done my way. It may be your vision, but it’s my money. Do we have an understanding? The director pauses for a moment, then responds with resignation. Richard: Yes, we understand each other perfectly. Tyler: Very well, then I suspect you should get back to work at making my movie, no? Richard: Yes, sir. Suddenly a commotion is heard coming from the far side of the set. The sea of people begins to part as a slew of foul language fills the air. “Okay, okay, okay, okay, okay…..what in the fuck is this shit?!” Danny and Chandler both slap their foreheads simultaneously. Danny and Chandler: Face-eater! Through the crowd comes The Illustrious Face-Eater, pointing to a bleached blonde wig sitting atop his masked head. He looks to have come straight from his trailer, as he still has on a rope and a pair of flip-flops on his feet. Face-Eater: I am not wearing a wig! Why would I want to cover up this beautiful head with this….this….wig!? Richard Donner: You’re playing the part of Leo Getz. He has hair just like that….not a mask on his face. Face-Eater: I’m the fucking Face-Eater, bitch! The Super Fucking Duper Champion!! Don’t make me go back and get my belt, SUCKAAAAA!!! Donner just buries his head in his hands, shaking it slowly back and forth. Facey looks over and sees Nelson standing there, a slight smirk on his face. Face-Eater: Oh, hey boss. Nelson approaches the three members of The A-List, who are all standing in line on the set. Tyler: Guys, I’ve been keeping tabs on things and I think it’s going great. Just a word of caution…don’t screw this up. It would make me very, very, verrrry unhappy if this movie did not do well. I’m investing a lot of money in this project in order to get PRIME some additional exposure, and as you know I don’t like to part with my money in vain. Face-Eater: Don’t worry, Mr. Bossman, I won’t let ol’ goofy-eyes here mess it up. Facey nudges Tsonda in the ribs, forcing a shocked look to his face. Chandler: ME?! I have natural talent, something a hack like you would know nothing about! Plus I’m good looking, which is more than I can say for you! Face-Eater: Oh yeah?! Chandler: Yeah!! Face-Eater: Oh YEAH?! Chandler: YEAH!! As Tsonda and Face-Eater continue their back and forth argument, Nelson pulls Ferguson aside. Danny: What can I do for you, Mr. Nelson. Tyler: We just need to have a little talk about this match on Wednesday, Danny. I know you and I are totally capable of winning this match on our own…. Danny: Totally! Tyler: Don’t interrupt. Danny: Sorry. Tyler: But I’m worried of Farwell gets his miserable carcass in the match we’ll lose for sure. Danny: He does have that “Rock of Kryptonite” aura around him, doesn’t he? Tyler: Precisely. I think it would be in our best interest to keep him out of the match whenever possible, even if it means soiling our hands on that dirty rock singer or that skanky wench. Ferguson gets a gleam in his eyes. Danny: I wouldn’t mind soiling Wolfenden’s hair a bit, “Something About Mary”-style. Tyler: Stick with me, here. I might be able to get Mortimer to inflict some more damage on Olsig prior to the match, which leaves the crappy musician and the woman. Sounds pretty easy to me. Danny: Yeah, I hear Karina’s real easy. Tyler: Anyway, I see bright things in your future in PRIME, Danny. I’ve got my eye on you. Ferguson puffs his chest out and grins widely. Danny: I won’t let you down, sir! The two men return to the set, where Tsonda and Face-Eater are still going at it. Tsonda: YEAH!! Face-Eater: OH YEAH?! Nelson just rolls his eyes and then shakes Ferguson’s hand before taking leave of the set. Danny smacks both of his partners along the side of their head. Danny: You two just made total asses of yourselves in front of the boss! Chandler and Face-Eater: OH YEAH?! Danny: YEAH!! Toward the back of the lot, Nelson strides confidently toward his waiting limousine, the standard black stretch Hummer limo we have become accustomed to seeing him ride in. He looks around suspiciously, wondering where his chauffer has gone. He stands and waits next to the back door for a few minutes, waiting for someone…..anyone!....to open the door for him. Tyler: Looks like I get to fire someone early today. Nelson reaches and pulls the door of the limo open and begins to duck inside, but stops short as he notices someone sitting inside. A sexy female voice calls out from inside the limo. “I hear you’ve been looking for me.' A look of shock is quickly replaced by a big smile on the face of the CEO. 'Well, here I am....' Nelson eagerly climbs into the vehicle and slams the door shut.
