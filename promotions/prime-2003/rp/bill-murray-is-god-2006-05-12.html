Bill Murray is God

12 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/05/06 “Roses are red, violets are blue, I'm a schizophrenic and so am I.” – Bill Murray in “What About Bob” (1991) The Noble Path I – “Another Holiday Inn” Let’s try this again… Another day of solitude for the isolated Ignatius Lisieux begins with the electronic bleeping of his motel’s alarm clock-come-radio contraption. Weary after a restless night he slings his arm across in the general direction of the disturbance and thuds it down… once… twice… third time’s a charm. Basking in the renewed comfort of silence he emits a satisfied groan from his sandpaper-dry mouth as he recalls his recurring dreams over the past nights and rolls over onto his back, spread-eagle in the centre of the double bed, taking in the glorious spring morning that valiantly creeps through his drapes. He’d lost count of just how many days he’d enjoyed alone since taking an early exit from ReVolution 92 after his shock loss to Chandler Tsonda, but he knew it had been a fair while. Ultra Violence merely hours away, but it felt a lifetime down the line in his current mindset. The French-Canadian had perhaps never felt so content, his recent run of defeats were as far from his mind as humanly possible… he was just, dare I say it – happy (!). Hoyt Williams lurked on the horizon like an incoming meteor strike, but Lisieux paid him no heed at this moment, his immediate thoughts were of draining the old one-eyed trouser snake and then checking for the inevitable voice messages from his cohorts, Adam and Johnny (yes, the old guy DOES know how to work a cellphone, Lisieux had to teach him, though!) An aggrieved quick-quick-slow to the bathroom later (we edited that bit out, for obvious reasons) and we return as Lisieux sits clad in some black boxer-shorts on the edge of his bed, staring down at his awakening cellphone with bleary eyes. It’s just tiredness causing it, though, nothing more sinister. GO, GO SPEAKERPHONE!! “You have two new messages… first message, sent yesterday at eight-forty-three A.M…” At that time of the morning do you even have to ask who it’s from? “Iggy, how’s it going boy? I hope everything’s okay with you, feels like forever since the three of us hung out - I haven’t seen Adam for a while, either… Look brother… I’ll be honest - I’m starting to get a little worried, please give me a call when you get this message, if not then I’ll try and see you at the arena. Buh-bye.” The worry was evident in Noble’s voice, so much so that Lisieux needed not to wait for the automated instructions to reply to the call – instead, in a rare display of integrity (at least lately) for the French-Canadian, he just hits “3”… I won’t bore you with written-out ringing sound effects, but it rang a few times before an elated “Mr. Incredible” answered… “HEY!” comes the instant greeting from the veteran, “Nice to hear from you Buddy, how goes it?” Lisieux is a little hesitant (and tired) to match the enthusiasm levels - his tone remains not much more than a hush, “Hey Johnny, I’m doing really good thanks… where abouts are you?” “Jacksonville, of course!” responds Noble in due time, “Me and the family are all here, the weather is beautiful… man I love this country!” Ignatius chuckles a little, but instantly returns to Noble’s words with an interested inquiry: “The whole family is with you?” “Heck yeah, we’ve been to Orlando, Tampa, Clearwater, The Keys… man it’s been awesome!” continues Noble, “Say man, where the heck have you been lately!? No call, no nothing!” “Been clearing my head a little, getting myself ready for tonight” replies Ignatius, being a little less than truthful. Noble pauses for a moment to consider, but is soon talking again, “And are you ready? Hoyt stitched you up good and proper last week, I’m worried about the match stipulation…” “Hey, it’s what people have come to expect of me, I guess… get my ass beaten around, get cut-up and put on a show… I just need to win the damn thing.” Lisieux grins to himself as thoughts of recent losses to Vangelus Olsig and Chandler Tsonda rush back into his mind like rushing white-water rapids, flooding his consciousness. “Well man, you’ve caught me at a bit of a bad time… but if you’re in Jacksonville too then I guess we could meet up at arena later on, I’ll help you get ready for your match and vice versa, it’ll be just like old times!” Noble is notable excited at the prospect of seeing the French-Canadian again, though for a fifty-two-year-old to use “old times” to describe a few weeks ago is nothing short of surreal… Lisieux contemplates things for a brief, brief moment before issuing his reply, “Sure thing man, I’ll see you at the arena.” “Bye for now buddy!” calls Noble, before hanging up the connection and leaving Ignatius alone – again. Call it schizophrenia, call it bi-polar or call it whatever… it’s something, that’s for sure… because as soon as Noble ended the call a feeling of dread sweeps over our protagonist like nothing he has felt for some time. Speaking to “Mr. Incredible” brings everything back at once, the fact that he was facing Hoyt Williams in what could be his last match later that day does not help one bit either… He just sits there, half-naked, in silence… staring at the bedroom wall. The Noble Path II – “The Fish Bowl” After hours of deliberating, combined with a little cardio, Ignatius Lisieux has finally made his way to Jacksonville’s premier sporting arena in his economy rental car (poor guy) to meet up with Johnny Noble and prepare “properly” for their respective matches later that night. Stepping out of the vehicle in the wrestlers’ lot, the French-Canadian is glad to find no eager fans waiting for autographs or pictures, instead he has a clear route to the backstage area, slightly callous and not at all superfacey, but not surprising. He steps through the swinging doors that lead into the backstage corridors and is instantly greeted by his huge veteran amigo, with open-arms, no less… “There he is!” calls out Noble with a huge grin as he starts making stride towards the former Global Champion, “There’s my little buddy… come here big guy…” Lisieux’s eyes open wide with shock as Noble bears down on him; he drops his sports bag by his side and is immediately clutched in a vice-like grip of appreciation as Noble swings him from side to side and then swiftly releases the hold before it enters the realms of the homoerotic… too late? Never mind. “How goes it, champ?” beams Noble as Lisieux reels a little from the overwhelming greeting before issuing his response with a kind hand on “Mr. Incredible’s” shoulder… “Ah well, all the better with seeing you, of course! What’ve you got planned for me tonight, then?” asks Lisieux with a raised eyebrow, no doubt fearing the worst in the response. Noble smiles a wide, happy grin before patting the French-Canadian on the back and leading him off in the direction of the locker rooms, “Just wait until you see this… Hoo-hoo, you just wait!” I’d be lying if I said Ignatius Lisieux wasn’t a little apprehensive about the “surprise”. If I’m honest with you, the whole reason that he’s been AWOL since his loss to Tsonda on ’92 is mainly because of Johnny Noble and his overwhelming niceness, if you can wrap your head around that. You see, before a lot of you kids came and made this place what it is today Ignatius was a little more than crazy, he was a big old steaming cup of crazy in truth, and some of that crazy juice still resides inside of him to this day. He doesn’t particularly like being nice – especially when it costs him matches, but he’d been persevering because Noble was an old friend and again tonight, he would persevere, but he knew that it could be fatal against Hoyt. “What do you think!?” smiles Noble upon opening the door to Ignatius’ locker-room… “Umm…” cautiously begins Lisieux, “It’s… it’s…” he stumbles a little further in… “…nice… It’s nice, very nice!” and he ends with sparkling enthusiasm, an Oscar-winning performance! “Nice!?” reacts Noble with surprise, “Nice!? It’s a heck of a lot better than “nice”! This place is like heaven!” And it was, it was exactly like what Johnny Noble’s heaven would be. There was a rowing machine, a treadmill, an exercise bike, weights bench, multi-gym, a step machine, a massage bench… and then there were posters… everywhere. Posters of Noble, posters of Lisieux, posters of Adam, posters of fruit… just posters of everything! “Sorry, sorry… yeah this place is awesome, what a great idea Johnny!” beams Lisieux with the best faux-smile you’ll see this side of Hollywood, “I’m gonna be so well-prepared for Hoyt that I can’t lose tonight!” (Excuse me while I vomit.) “That’s the plan! I thought we could all hang here, we’ll be in PRIME condition for our matches, and tonight is the night that we start turning the “Goodship Goodguy” around!” Noble is undoubtedly proud of himself as he continues to observe the scene with an extremely goofy smile, he places a reassuring arm on the underwhelmed shoulder of his French-Canadian ally and leads him into the room. “What do you want to start with? A little light jogging? Some bike-work?” “How ‘bout a beer?” “jests” Ignatius with a sly wink, “No, no… just kidding (not really)… I think I’ll kick things off on the bike, ease myself into it a little bit.” Noble smiles at the “joke” from Lisieux and ushers him towards the exercise bike, “Good thinking, Batman! Oh! Did I show you this little contraption!? Barbara got it from QVC…” “A juicer?” smirks Ignatius unerringly. “Not just any juicer… an electronic juicer!” beams Noble again, dropping an orange into the top of the white machine… “Now watch this… one push of this button and we’ve got the best glass of Florida O.J. you could hope to have…” *Click* The contraption whirrs into life as Ignatius is once again forced to evoke happy expressions and impressed noises until the juicing is over with. Once the glass is half-full Johnny Noble grips it hastily and hands it over to his compadre, “There you go buddy, drink up and we’ll get down to business! This is great!” “Yeah… just peachy” grunted Lisieux in response, his mask slipping a little. The Noble Path III – “The Outcome” “Darn it, darn it and triple darn it!” shouts Noble as he slams his fist down onto the apron, “Come on Iggy, get up, you can do this!” He turns to the Jacksonville crowd and waves his arms up and down in the air like a conductor calling for noise from his choir. An “Iggy” chant is quickly forthcoming as Noble again beats on the mat, but Hoyt Williams most definitely has the upper hand in the huge bout. He drops down and hooks the leg of the battered and bloody French-Canadian… One. Two. Three. Silence. Everything stops as Ignatius Lisieux loses an unprecedented third match in a row and “Personal Jesus” by Depeche Mode starts up over the speaker system to hysterical booing and jeering from the Florida fans. Referees and officials flood the scene to free Lisieux from the barbed-wire net as a bloodied Hoyt Williams celebrates his impressive victory in front of a loathsome public. Johnny Noble is heartbroken and still. He waits for his friend to be loaded onto an emergency stretcher before heading towards him to place a sympathetic hand over his torn and scarred chest and accompany him to the backstage area. Nick and Richard are almost speechless at what they have just witnessed. Once arriving back through the curtain Noble is left alone with Ignatius to discuss the events of the match and to offer his heartfelt condolences to his fallen ally, “You might have lost out there Iggy but you lost the right way, you fought fair and square and that lowlife bum had to cheat to win… you’ve done yourself proud, heck I’ve never been so proud of you…” Of all of the empty words in the entire world that Lisieux could be hearing right now the last ones he would choose would be those uttered by “Mr. Incredible”, the man whose philosophy has caused the former PRIME Global Champion to embark on his worst ever run with the company. Unable to speak due to multiple internal injuries, never mind missing teeth and two fat lips, the French-Canadian is powerless to resist as the veteran chunters on… “You’ll bounce back from this Ignatius, you’ll bounce back and be better than ever – I’ll make sure of it. You just let ol’ Johnny take care of everything, I’ll fix you up and get you going again…” and he continues, on-and-on, with no riposte for minutes and minutes, making Ignatius Lisieux resent him more and more with every single word he says… then midnight strikes. I hope everything’s okay with you, feels like forever since the three of us hung out-” “What the-“ stutters Lisieux. Holy crap, is he catching on here? “…I haven’t seen Adam for a while, either… Look brother… I’ll be honest - I’m starting to get a little worried, please give me a call when you get this message, if not then I’ll try and see you at the arena. Buh-bye.” Come on, come on… Lisieux just sat there, stunned, he was certain that the message and time were exactly the same as they had been in the nightmare that he had just endured, but he soon fought off that feeling and decided to wait and see what the next message brought… See? Things were already going differently to the dream… “Second message, sent yesterday at fifteen-twenty-one hours…” “Hey man, what’s up? Just checking in on ya to see if we’re doing this Ultra Violence thing tonight. I wasn’t gonna go but I figured it’d be a good chance to catch up with ya, feels like ages since we spoke. Peace out, Adam.” Ignatius instantly hit “3”, he needed to speak to someone, anyone… “Well hello!” came Adam’s Scottish/American accent on the other end of the line, his answer swift, “How goes it on the Eastside?” “Man I’ve just had the weirdest little bit of deja-vu, but that can wait…” trailed off the French-Canadian, “It goes pretty good actually, just shitting my pants about the match later on tonight…” It wasn’t deja-vu dammit, it was Groundhog Day! Argh! I need a break from this. “Figures” comes Adam’s simple and honest reply, “That Hoyt is a big, scary, underhanded bastard, you’d better be on your A-game later. I was thinking I could be in your corner if you wanted, keep an eye on things.” A proposition too good to refuse given what Lisieux thought he could expect from Hoyt, “That’d be awesome, man, much appreciated… how come you haven’t asked where I’ve been?” Adam chuckles a little, “Reckon you forget who you’re speaking to, I know you man… two losses, big match on the horizon… figures you’d want some space. Johnny been pestering you?” “Yup, must’ve had about forty missed calls from the guy…” recounts Ignatius with an agitated grimace. The “Number One Son” laughs again, causing Lisieux to emit a wry smile, “Aye, I thought that might be the case… bet you wish you’d never taught him how to use a mobile, eh?” Lisieux grins again, swatting away a fly from the back of his neck, “Ah, he’s harmless… anyway, I take it you’re in town? Where you staying?” “Comfort Inn – it’s fucking boring… where abouts are you?” inquires the Scotsman with some foreboding menace in his tone, “Lemme guess, somewhere cheap and shitty yeah?” “Oh hell yeah… Holiday Inn…” replies Ignatius with yet another smile. “Aye, not far… I’ll be over in ten, I’m bringing beers though… no arguing” laughs Adam down the phone. “Whatever man, I’m looking forward to it!” comes Ignatius’ surprising response. Adam seems delighted at the old-school reply and at the prospect of a drinking session with his oldest pal - “Well fuck aye then, let’s get wasted!” The Number One Path II – “The Sick Bowl” “God-damn, how much have we had to drink!?” calls out the giggling Adam, slumped against the foot of his friend’s double bed. He’s calling out to Ignatius Lisieux, the French-Canadian guy who is emptying his guts up into the en suite toilet after downing twelve bottles of beer in quick succession – with vodka chasers. Adam’s face turns a less-than-attractive shade of green as he gulps and makes a beeline for the bathroom, “Watch out of the way there… AHHHHHUUUUURRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH!!!” (That’s a vomiting sound effect, right there!) Just then, Ignatius’ phone (which is resting nicely on the bedside table) begins to vibrate and edge it’s way across the wooden surface, much to the annoyance of Adam, “Is he fucking ringing you again!? HHHHHHUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHHHH!! How many fucking times is that today, man?” Ignatius chuckles and looks across at Adam, who’s wiping vomit from his chin, “About three hundred, I think? I dunno, I guess he just really likes me or something…” “I think he wants to fucking pack your fudge or something!” snarls Adam, before returning to his toilet bowl… “No way man, that’s you…” grins Lisieux deviously, “Urgh… how the hell am I gonna be ready to face Hoyt now? What time is it?” “Let me just ask the fuckin’ toilet… ‘What time is it?’… Pfffft…” retorts Adam sarcastically. Ignatius raises a drunken eyebrow, “Bitchy!”, and then continues, “…Lemme see here… four-twenty… ah, that’s plenty of time - I’ll be ready to kick some… wait a second… MOVE!!” He darts for the bathroom and shoves Adam to one side… “HHHHHHHHHUUUUUUUUUUUURRRRRRRGGGGHHHHHHHHHHH!” “D’you not think we should mebbes flush it?” grins Adam, vomit resting nicely on the bottom-left corner of his lip. “Aye, go on and flush it… fuckin’ stinks in here!” Lisieux does the honours as an afternoon’s-worth of vomit is swept down into the Floridian sewerage system, “Adios!” hollers out the French-Canadian as both men fall down laughing to themselves and at each other, like a pair of drunken schoolgirls – which is what Adam secretly wishes they were. “Ah man, I’ve missed this shit… it’s good to have ye back, Iggy, put it there…” instead of outstretching his hand for the shake, Adam puckers up and directs his buddy towards his lips… “Fuck that!” shouts Lisieux, hurriedly pushing himself upwards to a vertical (more or less) position, he delivers a stinging slap to the cheek of Adam, causing both to laugh out loud all over again… “Jesus… I’ve just thought, how are we gonna get to the arena?” Adam quits rubbing his cheek and abandons the pucker before dragging himself upwards, “I’ll drive!” he calls out impressively while heading for the door. Lisieux cocks his head sideways and shakes it a little bit, “Okay… but you better promise me that you’re not drunk!” Right on cue, as if to answer, the Scotsman walks slap-bang into the door (which he just pulled open) and ends up flat on his ass in the middle of the hotel room, Lisieux looks down on him with a grimace and shrugs, “Meh, that’s good enough for me… onwards to the Fish Bowl!” The Number One Path III – “The Outcome” Not exactly “fresh as a daisy” and certainly not entirely sober, the outlook is incredibly bleak for Ignatius Lisieux. Just nine short minutes into his “Hall of Fame Championship” match with Hoyt Williams he finds himself on his back in the barbed-wire net, covered in his own blood and unable to see straight. On the outside of the ring stands Adam, “The Number One Son”, in a very similar boat to his good friend. Backstage a distraught and hurt Johnny Noble watches from the comfort of his locker room as Adam takes matters into his own hands and charges into the ring. Hoyt sees the clumsy Scotsman coming a mile away and is ready to intercept – with a baseball bat, no less – he swings for a home run and connects with Adam’s midsection, causing a horrific scene as blood and vomit instantly shoot from his mouth. Hoyt looks down with a disdainful scowl as he plants the bat down again, this time across the back. Adam is left motionless in the centre of the ring as Hoyt turns his attention back to Lisieux. The Jacksonville crowd stands silent to a man as the “Son of God” makes the effortless cover, not a dribble of his own blood anywhere to be seen. One. Two. Three. Referees and officials flood the scene to free Lisieux from the barbed-wire net as a bloodied Hoyt Williams celebrates his impressive victory in front of a loathsome public. Backstage in his locker room Johnny Noble is heartbroken and still. He watches as his friends are loaded onto emergency stretchers before solemnly heading out towards the curtain to await their arrival backstage. Ignatius and Adam are loaded up and wheeled backstage as “Personal Jesus” by Depeche Mode continues to ring out around the seething arena; Nick and Richard are almost speechless at what they have just witnessed. “Are you guys alright!?” asks Noble, worry etched all over his face, “What the hell happened out there, are you guys drunk!?” “The MVP” and “The Number One Son” are both unable to respond. Ignatius still can’t see straight and his mouth and tongue are swollen up and out of action, Adam is barely conscious. Johnny Noble rests his hands upon both men as a tear wells up in his right eye, “If only I’d been there to help them… if only he’d responded to my calls. This would never have happened.” Hate to tell you this, but yes Johnny, it would have. I guess now you might be wondering who I am? The smartass behind the words? Well just have a little think to yourself, who’s the best smartass in the business? Who has starred in numerous box-office hits and made people laugh the world over? Whose dry wit and receding hairline have excited ladies (somehow) wherever he has been? I’ll tell you all who I am, if you haven’t guessed already… My name is Bill Murray and I am God. Now, this might shock some of you, the more enlightened amongst you will have realised long ago… but I tell no lie, I am Bill Murray and I am God. Bill Murray is GOD. Bill Murray IS God… you catching on yet, junior? Seeing how you’ve grasped that concept you might be asking yourself just what am I doing here, in this particular place. I’ll tell you that for nothing, too, (damn I’m so much better than the other God that all those idiots believe in.) I realised this was never gonna work, so I clicked my fingers again and assembled everyone outside the Holiday Inn, Jacksonville at five-thirty that night so I had time to perform the Jedi mind-trick on my three associates to make them “comply”. Twenty minutes later and we’re suited and booted in Ecto-1a and waiting outside of Ignatius Lisieux’s hotel room… he finally joins us around six-o’-five, complete with a super-goofy smile and star-struck eyes. Then it was a short drive to “The Fish Bowl” for Ultra Violence, where I would hope to take my front row seat to see the pretender to my throne Hoyt Williams get his butt truly and royally kicked, the only downside was that I’d be wearing a Ghostbusters costume… and most likely sitting next to Dan Aykroyd. Believe me, you’d understand if you knew him. So on the way we had some good advice for the young whippersnapper, we told him that we, as the Ghostbusters (he made us call each other Pete, Ray, Egon and Winston the whole time), liked doing stuff our “own” way, the “right” way as we put it. When we saved New York City we did it by blowing up an apartment block and creating a giant Stay Puff guy, then in Ghostbusters II we hijacked the Statue of Liberty… Now you see these kinds of things wouldn’t usually be accepted, but when you’re the Ghostbusters it’s alright, because everyone loves you anyway… chicks dig you and all that… So we drew him a little parallel so that he realised it was the same for him. His recent downfall has been due to listening to the goody-two-shoes Johnny Noble (come on, NOBODY is THAT good all of the damn time) instead of listening to his heart and his gut. Anyway, surprisingly he seemed to grasp the concept that we laid out for him and he promised to do things “his way” against Hoyt Williams, kind of like a less-cool version of Frank Sinatra. The Right Path III – “The Outcome” Needless to say, numerous “Ghostbusters” chants later and Ignatius Lisieux was walking out of the Fish Bowl as the NEW “Hall of Fame Champion” and Hoyt Williams was beating to within an inch of his life… Hey, I’m a vengeful God, alright? All it took was a crazy Groundhog Day concept that somehow mutated into a Ghostbusters story to convince Ignatius that the best way to go about things was by doing this his way, a little dirty-tactics never hurt anybody. Whatever happened, Bill Murray was involved every step of the way as the higher power and Lord of Earth. I’d greatly appreciate it if you guys could keep my secret, though. Maybe I’ll return again one day to right a wrong or make sure something that is meant to happen actually happens, but for now it’s back to heaven for me… which means white clouds, naked ladies and a whole lot of Bill Murray movies!!! I’ll leave you all with a brief summary of events though, in case you haven’t read the whole thing (and who could blame you, really?): I, Bill Murray (God), tried to use a Groundhog Day concept to make Ignatius Lisieux realise that he could only beat Hoyt Williams by doing it his way. He didn’t really “get it” so then I was forced to come along with a little bit of divine intervention: which included some fabulous acting on my part during a phone conversation and a cameo appearance from the Ghostbusters, albeit in retrospect. After all of this Ignatius was equipped with the self-belief that he needed to defeat Hoyt Williams and in turn make the world a much better place to live. Simple!
