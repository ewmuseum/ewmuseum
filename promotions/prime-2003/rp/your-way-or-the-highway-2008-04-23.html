Your Way or the Highway

23 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/23/08 Night Wednesday, April 15th The end of ReVolution Wincing in pain and wiping a stream of sweat from his forehead, the newly crowned 5 Star Champion pushed his way through the curtain, severing his existence from the sea of fans in the Pepsi Center. Kaiser Vashaun let out a sigh as he looked down at the title belt in his hand. With each step he took further into the depths of arena’s corridors, the sounds of “Jackson, Mississippi” grew fainter, until the lyrics were suffocated under the lone sound of his heavy breaths. The championship belt felt heavy in his grasp, and for a moment, he simply paused on that thought, not bothering to shift it to his other hand or contemplate the burden. “Kaiser!” The voice was unmistakable… Matt Mills, overly anxious and excited, typical for his style. The PRIME interviewer’s feet thudded down the hall, and without even lifting his head, Kaiser knew that Mills was barreling right towards him. “Oh man…” “Kaiser, how ‘bout a quick interview! Just a few words on your big title victory. Whaddaya say?” “Not tonight, Mills.” “C’mon,” Mills stepped to the side, blocking Kaiser’s path down the hall towards the locker rooms. “This is a big deal. Only four or five guys have ever held the 5 Star and Intense titles in their PRIME careers. Give us just a couple minutes about the victory… about how it feels good, classic match… all the standard stuff. We’ll put it up on the PRIME website tonight and give those insomniac internet geeks something to watch. Probably get a few thousand hits by morning.” “Yeah.” Kaiser looked down at the 5 Star Championship, hanging from his fingertips. Curling his arm, he hoisted the title belt up, slinging it across his shoulder, displaying it prominently. “Ok. Let’s go.” Mills turned to the crew who’d followed a step behind and signaled for things to roll. “Ladies and Gentlemen, just moments after the conclusion of ReVolution and the incredible main event ladder match, I’m joined by the newly crowned 5 Star Champion, The Next in Line, Kaiser Vashaun.” The interviewer, like a true pro, slowly turned his gaze away from the camera and towards the subject of the piece as he asked the first question. “Kaiser, what does it feel like to join a very small, elite group of company as one of just five wrestlers to ever hold both the Intense Championship and the 5 Star Championship?” “It feels…” Kaiser stared into the lens of the camera, not blinking or turning away, “…good.” Mills arched an eyebrow, probably more annoyed at the plain response than intrigued by it vagueness. “Care to elaborate?” “Being able to call yourself Champion… that’s a great feeling, Matt. It’s so great that I want more than this. I want the Universal Championship.” “Well, that’s quite a tall order, and a whole other iss…” “I crave it, Mills. I look at this 5 Star title on my shoulder and I know that I’m one step closer to my goal, but still one step shy of it. And I’m by no means a patient man. That’s a step I plan on taking very soon. Jason Snow… I haven’t forgotten who it was who eliminated me from the Dual Halo. One superkick right between the eyes.” Kaiser smiled as he plucked a finger against his forehead, signifying the spot. “There is only one man in the thirteen months I’ve been with this company who has pinned me cleanly, in the middle of the ring. And don’t think I’m not well aware of that fact.” “Kaiser, if I…” “Hold on tight to that Universal Championship, Jason… because I want my revenge. And despite my personal history with them, I’d gladly give the fans a reason to cheer me by kicking your ass and taking your title.” Kaiser spun on his heels and pushed his way past Mills. Making no effort to apologize for the abrupt ending, or the general dismissal of Matt’s premise, the 5 Star Champion trudged down the hallway, anticipating the sanctuary of his locker room. “That was interesting.” Mills tossed the microphone in the air, and as it toppled end over end, he watched Kaiser disappear around the corner. As the champ vanished from sight, Matt caught the falling mic squarely in hand. “I may have underestimated how many hits that one’ll get when we upload it tonight.” ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ “What the hell were you thinking?!” Matt Ward spun his laptop back around towards himself and clicked the ‘X’ in the corner of the screen, effectively banishing the PRIME website from his computer. “It was just a post-match interview.” “It was more than that.” “What’s the big deal?” Kaiser leaned back in the couch and bit at his fingernail. “It wasn’t even on TV.” “Do you have any idea how many millions and millions of people visit the PRIME website on a weekly basis? Even if only one tenth of them click on that link and watch your video… you’re talking about hundreds of thousands of people. Your comments may not have been aired on FX, but they weren’t exactly said in secret either.” “Who cares if a hundred million people have watched it? Like you haven’t done a hundred post-match interviews in your lifetime.” “That wasn’t a post-match interview. That was a threat. I don’t know when you were born, but for as long as I can remember, professional wrestling has been a world where you earn your way to the top and choose your words wisely. You can pretend you didn’t do anything out of the ordinary, but its not World Champions get called out a couple times every…” “It’s the Universal Title in PRIME. Not the World.” “I held the belt twice. I’m well aware of what its called.” Matt reached across his desk and closed his laptop. With the newly unobstructed view, he locked eyes with Kaiser. “You knew exactly what you were doing. You blew off the interview you were supposed to have, acted like things are finished with Flyer, and tried to jump ahead by calling out the Universal Champion.” “You’re right. I blew off the interview Mills was expecting. And you’re right, things are over with Flyer… why wouldn’t they be?” “Because wrestling is never that easy. And it’s not like you went out there and dominated. You snuck out a victory by a matter of inches… by a split second. You’ve got an ego, you dumped Mary-Lynn on her head, and you just stole a victory… you think its over in Flyer’s mind?” “I don’t answer to his thoughts and desires.” “And Lisa Tyler doesn’t answer to yours.” Matt folded his arms on his desk and leaned forward, sighing as he did so. “When I agreed to take you under my wing…” “Take me under your wing? You make it sound like some after-school special where you’re stepping in and keeping me off the streets and out of gangs.” “Whatever you want to call it, Kaiser… when I said I would help you, it was under the agreement that you would listen to what I had to say. You tried this with Lindz… tried to jump to the top and move too fast. I told you… If you want my advice… you take things slow. You do things my way.” “One year into your career, you were a two-time Universal Champion and had main-evented CIII.” Kaiser stood up, not for any particular reason, certainly not to leave the office. He just stood. “Yeah?” “So cut me some slack, cuz that’s all I’m doin’… trying things your way. Just like you did ‘em.”
