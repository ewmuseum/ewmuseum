God's Own Drunk (match rp)

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-05 “God’s Own Drunk” Dressed liked Buffalo Bob in an outrageous white cowboy suit is America’s hero the “Malice Man” Duke Williams. Despite being on an old front porch of your atypical suburban home in the dark Duke is wearing his patented chrome shades and big brimmed cowboy hat. Next to him is PRIME’S most hated man, your Personal Jesus, and your Universal Champion Hoyt Williams. His dress is more toned down in a black adieus sweat suit. Duke Williams: Keep your voice down we don’t want to alert the neighbors. Hoyt Williams: Nobody can hear us. A light flashes down into the Williams’s eyes blinding them for a moment. They panic for a moment then begin to laugh hysterically as they realize it’s a motion sensor light. Duke Williams: We need to relax, he should be home in about an hour or so. Hoyt Williams: When he gets here we grab him, roll him up in the carpet, and then dump him in the forest preserve. Duke Williams: Your Cousin Buck is going to have the best birthday ever! Hoyt Williams: I hope I get kidnapped and thrown into the forest preserve when I turn 30! The father and son team of living legends trade high fives as Duke pulls out a flask of whiskey. Cool, tan, Tennessee born, whiskey with an amber flavor and a smooth kick. Duke chugs down what seems like a gallons worth before passing the flask to his first born and the Son Of God Hoyt Williams. Hoyt Williams: You know I don’t drink. Duke Williams: One quick swig ain’t going to kill you hoss. Hoyt Williams: Well I guess the blood of Christ is wine. So the stuff can’t all be that bad can it? Duke Williams: Not at all, this stuff has never steered me wrong. Hoyt Williams: What about your six Driving While Intoxicated tickets? Duke Williams: Well that was just racial profiling by the police. Hoyt Williams: Bastards. Duke nudges his son with the flask and passes it over to him. Hoyt takes down a drink and damn near spits it out as the flavor grain of the booze burns his virgin throat. Duke laughs a deep bellowing chuckle. Hoyt Williams: How can you drink that poison? Duke Williams: Its like money, the more you have of it the better it gets. Hoyt Williams: I like money. Duke Williams: Then you’ll love whiskey. Come on try another round. Hoyt Williams: And it’s never steered you wrong? Duke Williams: Nope, never. Hoyt goes forth with the temptation of the devils juice and drinks down a full swing. He struggles but he gets it down. Just as Duke predicted the second round did seem better to Hoyt. He smiles a bit as he wipes some excess sin from his lips. Hoyt Williams: Gawd DAMN! Duke Williams: Now you’re talking Hoss! The two men sit sharing the flask like two hobo’s on a south bound train with no destination in mind. In many ways life is a rolling southbound train with an undisclosed destination. We know we’re going, and we have a pretty fair idea how we’re getting there, yet we have no idea where it is we will arrive. Heaven, hell, or simple extinction or perhaps something else entirely, we just don’t know. No use asking the staff because the porters and conductors aren’t sharing all we can really do is find the card car and enjoy the trip or stare out the window and watch the world pass you by. Cousin Garth is a window watcher who in his thirty years of living is yet to find that card car. He’s yet to leave his seat and explore the train. He just sits and waits, wonders, and hopes for the best when the train stops. That’s not how it works. So tonight Hoyt and Duke have decided to force cousin Garth out of his seat and get his blood pumping a bit in an old fashion mock kidnapping birthday bash. The plan is simple when Garth arrives they will kidnap him, make him think he’s going to killed, dump him in the forest persevere and tell him he has three hours to get back to his house. When he gets there a party will be waiting for him. Hoyt Williams: Why didn’t we just buy this guy a cake? Duke Williams: Because his life is a cake. He goes to a job he hates, comes home watches TV, and every birthday he eats a cake and drinks a bottle of wine passing out in his lazy boy. That’s dull. He needs to live a little you know? Hoyt Williams: Are we going to have cake later? Duke Williams: What is it with you and cake? Hoyt Williams: I don’t know this sin juice is making me hungry. Duke Williams: You want to get hungry I got something for you to smoke. Hoyt Williams: No I want to get unhungry. Not hungry. Duke Williams: Suit yourself I’m going to smoke a joint. Hoyt Williams: A joint? Duke Williams: Yea some Mary Jane. Hoyt Williams: That’s a drug of Satan. Duke Williams: It comes from the earth right? Its nature not something Satan would want. Besides I don’t look at it as supporting Satan I look at it as burning down his crops. “The Malice Man” lights up a joint as he takes a deep drag letting the world escape him as he exhales. Duke smiles an illegal smile and passes the sin smoke to his son, the Son of God. Hoyt Williams: Well I guess, I mean if it’s setting Satan’s crops on fire I can understand that. Duke Williams: Yup setting the devils woods on fire. The Pontiff of Prime takes a drag which chokes his harder then the hands of Boda. He recovers quickly and under then encouragement of his father he takes another drag. He starts to feel a bit light headed. Duke Williams: How’s that good shit? The world starts to “wow” Hoyt like an old record one speed to slow and scratched from age. Time comes almost to a stand still as Duke rambles on talking Hoyt feels like he’s been listening forever just to discover after a peek at his watch that it’s been less then a minute. All of a sudden the world will run full speed then will slow to drag. Hoyt Williams: Lets go to Subway. The words only spoken by that of a dieter or a stoned son of a deity. There is something about Subway when your stoned that makes their food taste better then good. A sort of urging to stuff your mouth with thick bread and three different kids of meats. No gay puns intended. Duke Williams: Hell no we must carry out this mission. I didn’t stop at Walmart to buy this night vision goggles for nothing. They cast cost five hundred dollars. Duke reaches into his kidnap bag and pulls out his Silence of The Lambs like night vision goggles. Hoyt begins to laugh and can’t stop as he find the thought of Duke Williams wearing night vision goggles hysterically funny. The laughter gets louder when Duke puts them on. Duke Williams: I’m pissed because when they have these damn things on TV you push a button and they “vummmm” then turn on. No noise with these. Hoyt Williams: No “Vummm”? Hoyt laughs uncontrollably as Duke is obviously perturbed by the lack of noise the goggles produce. Hoyt Williams: If you’re in the middle of the woods do you really want something to make a noise? Duke Williams: Well I guess not but still that’s what makes them cool, you know. They wouldn’t put the noise in movies and video games if it wasn’t cool. “vumnm”. Nothing. I just see in the dark. Hoyt Williams: But that’s cool. Duke Williams: Yea I guess. Hoyt Williams: Lets go to Subway. Forget this kidnapping I’m not motivated anymore. Duke Williams: But the goggles! Hoyt Williams: They don’t make noise. Duke Williams: Fuck it, you’re right let’s go to Subway. Fifteen minutes later Duke and Hoyt are sitting on the green and yellow chairs with football sandwiches in front of them. Hoyt is eating his sandwich like a madman getting shit all over his face but he can care less as this is the best sandwich he’s ever tasted in his drugged out mind. Hoyt Williams: My God this needs to be the official restaurant of the RISP. Duke Williams: Easy killer. I like Beer, I like Texas, I like Texas Beer but you don’t see me putting that on a bumper sticker. You know what I mean? Hoyt Williams: Not really. Duke Williams: Ahh….it’s like everything in moderation type saying. You can rope the wind but you can’t sell at as stock. Hoyt Williams: What? Duke Williams: You see the road goes on forever but the party never ends. You get it? Hoyt Williams: I think so. You’re telling me to invest in tech stocks? Duke Williams: Yea, ahhh you got it. Hoyt Williams: Far out. Duke Williams: You ready for the match this week? Hoyt Williams: With Christen and Uwga? Hell yes I’m ready although I don’t know about this Blackwell cat. Duke Williams: I hired him you have to trust me I know talent. I mean who can forget the members of the original Malice Crew. Hoyt Williams: Who was in that stable? Duke Williams: Ahhh a few guys and me. But the point is he’s the real deal. Hoyt Williams: Well then in the name of Jesus, Williams, and Buddha I will beat those amateurs. I mean are ANY of them the Universal Champion? The Son of God? The most hated man in PRIME? The man who will run Youngblood out of PRIME? I walk on water, piss fire, and save souls! Duke Williams: I pissed fire once and it turned out to be an STD you may want to have that check out. Hoyt Williams: Never you mind. I’m so cool they call me the artic. Nobody is going to stop me high or not….I’m God’s Own Drunk. Duke Williams: Good Jimmy Buffett song. Hoyt Williams: What? Duke Williams: Never you mind. You’re going to send those nerds whiskey bent….. Hoyt Williams: and heaven Bound! -w-=w=
