Heat (PTC Tag Title RP)

19 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 19/02/05 Heat.19th February, 2005. 'Things Fall Apart; The Centre Cannot Hold...' Beads of sweat were forced from Karina's pores as the unrelenting rays of the sun threatened to blister her pale, lightly freckled skin. Vibrant green eyes met their match as multicoloured, elusive blotches danced across her vision, forcing eyelids to lock down and protect against seared corneas. Between the idea, and the reality. The fiercely overheated salt-flats cured her bare feet, burning grains working their way into every crevasse and contour... leaving her with an encrusted sole that had to be ripped from the surface. Her light grey vest was already darkening as perspiration was soaked up into its absorbent fibres, leaving it stuck uncomfortably to glistening skin. Loose, blonde hair lightened as the sun bleached every vulnerable strand, leaving its usually silken finish frizzy and matted down. Between the motion, and the act. A sky so blue that jaws would drop in awe dominated all below it, so startling that not even Karina’s eyelids could properly block out its splendour... just one glimpse embedding the vision in her memory, perpetually being recalled. Woozy from the baking her body was losing the fight against, Karina dropped awkwardly to her knees, legs giving out halfway. As her head slumped forward, it send her frazzled mane cascading over her face, leaving the back of her neck at the sun's mercy. The almost unbearable heat eclipsed her thoughts. It was all she could think of... a sharp nagging that just wouldn't go away. It talked to her without words, and left her doubting every instinct. Falls the shadow. 'Once again, Karina Wolfenden has heat with the PRIME management,' one of the dirt sheets had proclaimed. 'For months, her Indy schedule has been a source of concern for PRIME's bookers, who have grown weary of fitting her appearances around foreign bookings and PTC commitments.' 'It's common knowledge that PRIME didn't want her to capture their Tag Team Titles,' another informed her. 'Most backstage feel that she values the PTC belts above PRIME's own championships, something which has caused growing resentment from those she and Tessa Windsor defeated to capture them.' 'Many have said they wouldn't be surprised if PRIME's booking committee were cheering on the Mucho Machos,' an 'inside source' continued the relentless tirade of rumours with. 'Some have already petitioned not to have the PTC Titles recognised on PRIME television, and have been upset that Wolfenden has been carrying both around as if they have equal standing.' 'PRIME were also wary of a Wolfenden/Windsor win due to rumours that the duo aren't on the best of terms,' one finally got uncomfortably close to fact with. 'It was Tessa who set up the shot at the PTC tag titles, and invited Karina to team with her... but has quickly grown frustrated at all the attention going to her partner.' For Thine is the Kingdom. Held in Karina's hands, the leather almost melted into skin, were the PTC and PRIME Tag Titles. Superheated by the sun, the golden plates were almost white hot, the heat haze coming off them distorting the air around the K-Wolf. Between the conception, and the creation. 'Well... here's the thing,' Tessa had nervously began her proposition with. 'As you know, I haven't really had the best of luck around PTC... which I know shouldn't matter, but it just seems like until I win one, I'm always going to be remembered as the girl who didn't.' It was her chance to finally win something of note in PTC. 'So where do I come into this?' Kari had asked back, not even considering the fact that a tag team could be in fruition. Six matches, six wins, and a pair of tag titles later... the pair hadn't said a word to one another in days. PRIME's jaunt to Japan hardly helped, as Karina's growing reputation from dates with Pro Wrestling AEON gave her a reception right up there with that of other frequent Japanese visitors like the HIT. For someone like Tessa, already feeling overshadowed by her tag partner, the attention being paid to Karina only heightened her frustrations. 'Always have to retreat into your own little world when things don't work out, huh?' the voice of Tessa commented, standing over her to give Karina's reddened face a respite from the blistering sun. 'I guess this is easier than talking it through.' 'Seems like the 'net is having the convo for us,' she rather flippantly responded, opening her eyes to make out the figure of Tessa standing in front of the sun... her features too shadowed to properly make out an expression. Between the emotion, and the response. 'Maybe they put all that stuff on the websites because I said it,' Tessa's argumentative words fired back. 'It's not like you listen to anything I say, you could spit in the fans' faces and they'd still cheer for you... at least some people don't buy into the myth that you're so perfect and all.' 'And where did this come from, exactly?' Karina demanded to know, aggrieved that her own friend and tag partner had been badmouthing her. 'The fact that you have to ask says it all,' Tessa bit right back with, the hands on her hips adding extra emphasis to her displeasured tone. 'Then again, this is all about how someone else feels, so I wouldn't expect you to care.' 'Hey,' Kari finally snapped, her voice coming from deep within. 'You came to me and asked is I wanted to team with you. I agreed, and we've won every match since. What exactly is your problem again?' 'But it's not WE, is it?' Tessa exclaimed, throwing her arms up into the air as the ferocity of the sun seemed to fuel her temper. 'It was you who won us the shot at the Shadow Riders, you who escaped the Cage last to win us the belts, you who got the pin to win the PRIME tag titles, you who was being asked for all the interviews last week in Japan, you who makes the PRIME rankings. This was meant to be my chance to win some respect for myself in PTC... not become a side-kick.' 'And how is this my fault again?' Karina enquired, staring up at the silhouette of her tag partner against the glorious sky. 'Maybe it's not your fault,' Tessa sighed, tiring of the debate. 'Maybe it's just who you are.' Falls the shadow. 'Don't worry,' Tessa scoffed, turning away to expose Karina once more to the disintegrating ferocity of the sun. 'I won't leave you in MY shadow any longer. We can't have that, can we?' Life is very long... Karina could - should - have tried to appeal to her, but she just couldn't relate to Tessa's point of view. 'There's some attention,' Tessa had spat her way during an argument on PRIME's Japan trip. 'Go seek it.' None of the attention was asked for, and if anything, the K-Wolf could have done with a few quiet days to relax. But how does she tell fans to dampen pops? How does she tell them who to ask for an autograph from? How does she tell them to aim the camera at the woman next to her? Between the desire, and the spasm. Overhead, the haunting chatter of vultures echoed out to the horizon, no wind or ambient noise to dampen it. Even the menacing flap of their tufted wings could be heard, each one thumping the air harshly from its path. With every strip of flesh torn from the skeletal remains littering the flat around Karina, they were hungry. The sight of the distasteful scavengers ahead began to make Pajaro de Presa a little more tolerable. He may have been sickeningly smitten with her, but was his annoyingly goofy persistence really anything to warrant her harsh treatment of him? He'd danced down to ringside, proudly presenting Valentines gifts, and only asking for one kiss in return. Hell, the thought of getting one nearly made him faint. So, what did she do? She did what Karina Wolfenden always seems to do; she lashed out. Between the potency, and the existence. People called her cold, overly intense, unsociable... up herself. There were those she was friends with, that she'd happily talk to for hours... and there were those she'd blank. Karina was entirely comfortable, blissfully content in the world she'd carved out for herself, a world she fiercely guarded. Tessa had been one of those who'd occupied it, but had grown tired of its formal restrictiveness... unlike Pajaro, who'd knocked himself unconscious trying to break the door down. All she wanted to do was live life her way, by her rules, enjoying her pastimes, and accompanied by her friends. No need for locker-room 'solidarity', no need to trying to sound interested when those influential backstage tried to strike up a conversation, no having to pretend she's interested in a masked goof just because she's stuck in front of thousands of fans. Between the essence, and the descent. The last few weeks had taken Karina to a whole new competitive high. She was undefeated since entering PRIME, hacking down men twice her size with stiff, crunching kicks, while flipping-off gravity and disobeying all its rules to put them away. But there's a risk to soaring so high... the feeling of plummeting back down again. Falls the shadow. Forcing herself to let go of the belts' leather straps, Karina winced as her skin peeled away, exposing a fresh layer to the relentless sun. They were belts that she and Tessa had captured together, won with double-team moves, and topped-off with shared celebrations. But they were just an object to help pummel the wedge between the duo with. For Thine is the Kingdom. What were a pair of gold belts really worth? This is the way the world ends... As Karina watched Tessa shrink, the horizon beginning to swallow her small frame up, she could almost feel the clock ticking down in her head. The unused parts of the brain which would eternally baffle scientists, regardless of expertise, formed the mechanism, causing the 'clunks' to echo within her skull. This is the way the world ends... 'Tes-' she went to call after her partner, her friend... but there were no words that should could follow up with. Dropping down into a slouched, seated position, Karina watched Tessa disappear, a few lingering seconds of focus unable to make her out anymore. This is the way the world ends... Leaning back into the crackling grains, the perspiration on Karina's sodden skin and clothing sizzled as it touched the scorched, arid surface. Eyelids felt their supports sever, and dropped closed as Karina retreated back into herself, wishing to abandon the sensations of life, just as her friend had abandoned her. Not with a bang, but a whimper. Where Tessa passed over the horizon, an intense ball of white swelled, a split second before the sound of pure, thunderous destruction perforated Karina's eardrums. Expanding exponentially in a split second, the blast radius turned the salt flat into a bubbling sea as it threatened to ignite the air itself. Taking a deep breath, Karina just spread-eagled herself, feeling the force and heat of the explosion rip the flesh from her bones... aided by thousands of swirling, sharp grains of salt agonising the wounds for a millisecond or two. Ring, ring... 'Asleep, dead, or screening,' Karina's voice curtly welcomed the caller with. 'It's Tessa. I just wanted t-... I'm just calling t-. Look, I'm done. We're done. I've got too much outside wrestling to keep me trying to force some motivation out. If that made any sense. I dunno what you wanna do, but, hell, you're probably relieved. Not like anyone else thought I did much for the team anyway. I know we haven't been on the best of terms lately, but good luck. And thanks, for what it's worth. Bye.' Credits: 'The Second Coming' by W. B. Yeats.'The Hollow Men' by T. S. Eliot.
