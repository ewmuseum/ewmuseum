More Fun Than One Man Can Handle.

16 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16/08/05 His disappearance was as mysterious as his return to the limelight had been. Two weeks ago, Paul Cain had found his smile for the first time in many months all because of another man’s suffering. Whilst he had convinced Melissa of his good intentions, he knew very well that everything he was doing, he was doing for himself. Peter Vetra had come into his life and reminded him of everything that had been wrong in his past and for that, punishment was necessary. This had gone beyond the revenge that Cain had promised himself to give to all those who had turned their back on him, this was more than that. He stood in front of the mirror of the nightclub’s bathroom area, rinsing his hands in the sink. Cain paused for a moment to marvel at his dress sense, a black smart shirt and matching smart pants to go with it. The week he had spent away from PRIME had been one of drinking, sex and indulging in all the things that the Devil would book him a place in Hell for when his time came. He called it a reward to himself after what he felt was his own form of divine retribution upon Peter Vetra. It was fair to say that Paul was immensely proud of himself and that for the first time in months, he was enjoying life. As the other nightclub patrons came into the bathroom they would take a glance at Cain and keep their distance. For although Paul was not the most physically intimidating specimen, news of his mental capabilities was traveling fast. As bitter and angry as he was towards the Dark Age and the events that occurred, as desperate for bloodthirsty revenge on PRIME as he was, this little game of cat and mouse was proving to be a temporary amusement that he had not been expecting. Now, not only did he have Vetra to toy with but John Edwards had also scurried into his path, making the game even more interesting. Cain finished rinsing his hands and then turned to look at a geeky young man fiddling desperately with a bottle of aftershave, he had clearly come to the club to try desperately to get laid. Paul then clicked his fingers in the air and whistled to the lad. Cain: I need time and space to think and you’re invading the later. The lad adjusts his glasses slightly and edges towards one of the stalls. Man: I-I..just need to use the bathroom…There’s this girl…She’s…she’s really keen man, you must remember what it was like, right? Cain smirks to himself and shakes his head at the clearly nervous first-timer. Cain: The day you start getting that worked up over pussy is the day you start on the slippery slope to decline like the rest of these desperados’. Seen one hole, you’ve seen them all. Do us both a favour, get a grip of yourself and leave me in peace. Sheepishly, the youngster moves slowly away and out of the door leaving a big smile on Cain’s face. Satisfied that he is in peace, Cain turns towards the camera and hops up on the sink, kicking his legs in the air. Cain: What a beautiful week it’s been, I’ve not felt this relaxed since…Ooh…let me think now…Oh yeah, since I had some bastard stick a knife in me and draw pretty patterns on my chest. I mean, I’ve had Peter Vetra spending time in a jail cell, his ex girlfriend thinks I’m like the Second Coming and I have managed to make a mockery out of Peter Vetra’s mind games. Slowly but surely I’m breaking him into tiny pieces and it feels so good. It is SO nice to know that someone else appreciates the pain that life can bring. My dark world isn’t so lonely now that Peter has joined me there, my brother Peter who now can’t deny we are one and the same. Paul smiles to himself as he looks down at his feet before glancing back up again. Cain: God I feel good. His happy expression then disappears as he rubs his chin thoughtfully for a moment and a serious look appears on his face. He sighs deeply then and shakes his head mournfully. Cain: How could I have been so selfish? Paul slaps his right wrist with his left hand to chastise himself as he returns his glance to the camera. Cain: There are some people out there at the moment that are not as lucky as I am, people who are not having the time of their lives right now. People such as… He pauses and then points at the camera with his forefinger. Cain: YOU…John Edwards…Because I think it’s safe to say that you’re not having a very good time of it are you? Your title reign lasted as long as a hiccup and all because of a woman who probably sucked a mean dick to get what she got. I told men like you before; don’t ever be fooled by a woman fluttering her eyelashes at you, because they all have the capability to be black widows when they want to be. You take your eye off the ball with a bitch like her and you’ll find yourself a victim of a woman just like many other countless men. Losing to K-Wolf isn’t anything to be ashamed of though right…? A pause for thought as he reflects on this statement and then smirks as if realizing his mistake. Cain: Of course you should be fucking ashamed of yourself, John! Whether she screwed her way to the title or not is irrelevant, because you sir, should have been better than HER. She is a FEMALE, she has no place in this damn industry and you let the business down John. When will the men in this place quit falling over their chivalry or stop yanking their dicks in time to her bodily movements, because believe me, she ain’t shit. She’s just another modern day woman trying to prove a point and to be quite honest with you, that’s getting old. Sad thing is, she got inside your head and made you fail. Me? Pussy or no pussy, I’ll break a person without even thinking about it. Mind you, it’ll be hard to break a man whose career has already been broken…By a woman.At the moment it seems incredibly difficult for Paul to remove the smile from his face. Under his breath he can be heard tutting as he shakes his head at John Edwards’s latest ‘achievement’. Cain: See, I realize John that there are some things a man just can’t beat. If she went out there and played the political game then you had no choice but to face her, I understand THAT. However what I DON’T understand is how you didn’t rise above that and keep something which should have been precious to you, your title. You have let yourself down John, you’re an embarrassment to yourself and those around you. Whilst I hate the fact that you weren’t there to save me when I was alone and dying at the hands of the Dark Age, I won’t let YOU down John. Because this week, in the “Pick Your Poison” match, I will save YOU and get your reputation back. When it’s all said and done, they won’t be talking about the guy with the title reign of a hiccup, they’ll be talking about John Edwards, battered, beaten but having survived the poison of Paul Cain! He hops off the sink and moves closer to the camera stretching both arms out and holding either side of it with his hands. His eyes have that far away look in them and despite his happy demeanor, his flimsy grip on the real world seems ever apparent. Cain: I can bring you back John, I can bring you back from the brink of obscurity! You are about to become the star that never was but I won’t let that happen, I won’t let K-Wolf haunt you for the rest of your days, no I won’t. Do you know why? Because I won’t let a woman destroy someone’s career when I can do it instead! For I will rid PRIME of the embarrassment that was John Edwards, your pathetic little reign will be long forgotten in time and the world will praise me for it! You will be a star because of me, your career will be remembered because of me, they will talk about your courage because of me! You will be heralded as the man that fought tooth and nail to survive at the hands of Paul Cain, Karina will be long forgotten and in defeat you will regain your pride and self-worth. Just remember John, whatever I do in that ring, I’m doing it for you. A disingenuous smile creeps onto Cain’s face as he winks at the camera mockingly. As Paul teases John from afar, the door to the bathroom smashes open allowing the loud bass of the music in the club to filter in. Sliding into the bathroom goes the same geeky lad that had appeared earlier which causes Cain to glance down at him as he moves. Following him is a large skinhead male wearing a white T-shirt and black smart pants carrying a baseball bat in one hand. He crouched over the fallen lad and tapped under his chin with the weapon. Thug: Put your hands on my fuckin’ woman again and I’ll make you pay you sorry sniveling little shit! He tosses the ball bat to the floor, clearly too drunk to realize it and staggers out of the bathroom leaving Cain alone again with this frightened looking nerd. Instead of taking an interest in the fallen victim, Cain becomes fixated by the bat on the floor. He slowly picks it up and taps the weapon in one hand with a look of great interest. Written down the size in red lettering is the name “Ms Alexis” which catches the eye of Paul. Cain: Now THIS…Is the only woman I need…[looking back at the geek behind him]. Thanks for your help son, word of advice though, stick to hookers next time though eh? Laughing mockingly, Cain saunters out of the bathroom door with his new acquisition in his hand. After a heavy night out on the town, the last thing you want is an uninvited guest waiting for you when you get home. However, for certain people, Paul Cain is willing to put in that little bit extra effort. As he steps out of his jeep, he spots Melissa sitting on the doorstep of his apartment and sighs lightly to himself. It’s already two in the morning and Lord knows how long she has been there. He looks down at her and then crouches down to her level. Cain: How long have you been here for? She looks up at him with sad and confused eyes, before hauling herself up to her feet. Melissa: I’ve just been sat here for ages thinking about things. I don’t even know whether I was waiting for you or not, I just needed somewhere to go, somewhere to clear my head. I just need to know…Whether I did the right thing or not? Paul rests a reassuring hand on her right shoulder and points towards the door. Cain: Why don’t you come in, we’ll talk? She shakes her head definitely with a soft smile. Melissa: You’ve done so much for me already; I’m not going to impose any further. I just want to know…Did he…Deserve what we did to him? For a moment, Cain glances away from her and bows his head solemnly before addressing her question. Cain: If anyone were to ask me whether we did the right thing or not, I will always say “Yes, we did”. What we did by having Peter arrested was demonstrate to every abused and vulnerable woman in the world, that they have the right to go where they want and do what they want. They don’t have to be scared of people like Peter Vetra ever again. We showed the world to stand up to these people and not to let them stop the vulnerable living their lives. You are an inspiration Melissa and you stood up to Peter, you showed him that you will go on and live your life without fear. I’m PROUD of you, Melissa. She looks far from convinced as she folds her arms across her chest and stares at the ground. Melissa: I just…I made him suffer…I just…don’t know whether I did the right thing Paul…I chose to make this happen…I didn’t have to come to that show…I didn’t…I made him suffer didn’t I? Paul takes hold of her arm gently with his left hand and leads her slowly towards the door. Cain: You’re not going anywhere like this Melissa. You did the right thing and you’ve got your life back now. Peter committed a heinous crime and now he’s learning from his mistake. Don’t let his mistakes haunt you hey? Now come with me, I’ve got a comfortable sofa with your name on. He opens the front door and leads her in before turning his back to her with a huge smile on his face. Paul Cain has never had so much fun. Scene fades to black.
