A-Tchu...

24 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11/24/2010 “So wait…you’ve signed a contract with PRIME? When the fuck did this happen?” [The surprised voice came from the beautiful Asian goddess known as Milena Takahashi, the voice of sanity within the shambolic, unbalanced world of she who calls herself “The Dark Angel”. Milena is clad from head to toe in black spandex, aside from her boots, gloves and belt which are leather. Oh…and she has a katana strapped to her back. Can’t forget that. The view is rather lovely on the rooftop they are located, as the also black clad Katterina Wylde looks down upon the city. Her style of dress is a little different to her assassin friend. She is wearing a pair of tight black leather pants, tucked into a pair of black leather boots which barely come over her knee, black leather fingerless gloves, and a tanktop with her own wacky Dark Angel symbol on the chest area, and a long black leather coat. Her long jet black hair hangs over her face, hiding that beautiful face of hers as she stares down at the city below. She pushes her hair behind her back as the deadly Asian assassin approaches. The Dark Angel is in her own little world, and ignores the question completely.] KATT: “I’ve been sitting her for about 20 minutes, and I feel at peace for the first time in years. I can’t describe…wait, why are you dressed to kill, sugar bear?” MILENA: “Because ‘m going to kill a pedophile Yakuza leader, kitten.” KATT: “Oh. Well…stab him in the cock for me, hun.” MILENA: “So…about this tournament…I mean, this Hessian guy is a monster.” KATT: “Yeah. But I’ve beaten monsters before. Psycho Bob…Jakob…Ezra…Tyler Rayne’s peni…er…” MILENA: “I’ll ignore that. And there’s also Tchu.” KATT: “Bless you.” MILENA: “No, your other opponent is called Tchu.” KATT: “That is a really nasty cold you have. Maybe you should let me take care of the nasty child molesting Yakuza guy.” MILENA: “…you’re an idiot.” KATT: “But seriously, do you honestly think I sweat this? Have you forgotten who I am already?” MILENA: “Not all all, but you might want to start caring about who these men are and what they can do, or you will be doomed to failure.” [It was then that Katterina’s voice darkened slightly and her eyes burned black. It’s a rather creepy thing that happens…something that is never fully understood. But it’s a nice indicator to figure out that deep down…this girl ain’t right.] KATT: “You have no idea of the amount of fuck I do not give about these two.” [She paused as a sinister smirk crept across her beautiful face. And then the darkness evaporates from her eyes and her smile becomes warmer and less aggressive.] MILENA: “Cut that shit out. It weirds me out when you do that.” KATT: “Sorry…I’ll save that side of me for the match. (laughs) Now you know why I’m not worried.” MILENA: “You can’t even control it half the time. And when you can’t, I hate what it turns you into.” KATT: “I’m getting better with it, though. And I’m actually getting off my lazy ass and trying to figure out what it is and how I can to have this…whatever it is.” MILENA: “Maybe it came from…” [Katt clamps her hand over her friend’s mouth to silence her, raising her finger in a “sssh..” motion. Milena nods and Katt takes her hand away from Milena’s face.] KATT: “Don’t you have a child molester you should be slicing up?” MILENA: “Fuck…” [Katterina looks away and turns her attention back to the beautiful city view.] KATT: “Better get going then…he’s not going to murder himse…” [When she turns back, Milena is no longer there. Katt lets out a chuckle and shakes her head, returning to her thoughts.] [FTB]
