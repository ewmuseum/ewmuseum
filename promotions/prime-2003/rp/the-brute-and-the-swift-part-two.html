Date: 1998 The Swift “I’ll see you later man.” Tyler said offering his hand to the boy standing next to him. The two boys exchanged a simple handshake/fist pound combination before going their separate ways. Tyler re-adjusted the backpack that hung from his shoulder as he walked away, looking around slowly. Without much trouble, he found what he was looking for. Just a hundred feet or so in front of him stood the body that held the nose he nearly broke just three days earlier. “Oh shit.” Tyler muttered under his breath. At eight thirty on a Monday morning the last thing he wanted was to get into a scuffle. But it was almost imminent. Tyler moved swiftly, dodging freshmen, sophomores and bumping into seniors he normally would have avoided. At that moment he didn’t care about anything but getting into the Trigonometry class he hated to no end. He was almost at the door, just feet from his enemy who was a little pre-occupied with another of his “clients” when a young busty blonde grabbed his arm. “Tyler! Oh my God! When are we going to go out again? I had a really good time last week, Tyler.” She said in an almost disgustingly bubbly voice. Tyler broke his stare away from the round C-cups that were trying to escape from their restraints and focused on the long blonde hair of Aaron. Megan had been so loud in her greeting that the bully had to have heard his name. To this point he was still collecting dues from some of the chess club and hadn’t turned around. “Megan, don’t say my name right now. Please.” He asked of her. She smiled and giggled loudly, slightly confused at his request. Her laughter soon faded and was replaced by a quite, flirty smile at him. As much as Tyler wanted to flirt back, he couldn’t take his focus off of the impending confrontation. “Why not? Is it some sort of game you want to play? How about I call you baby?” Megan asked. Tyler shrugged his shoulders not really caring. Suddenly Aaron was lost from his view as she reached up and turned his head to look at her. His eyes landed on her breasts once again, causing her to lift his head up to look at her face. She laughed a little. “Sorry.” He apologized. She ran her fingers across the slight stubbles on his face before leaning up to him and kissing him on the cheek. He smiled at her and slowly began turning back to Aaron’s location, but she caught him again. In an attempt to catch his attention for good she reached down and pulled her top down a little more, almost revealing a nipple. “We’ve been seeing each other for like two months now, so if you want it you can have it the next time we hang out.” She said with an enticing tone in her voice. Tyler’s attention was completely on Megan now. His adolescent mind was running through thought after thought of sexual acts and pleasure with the beautiful blonde in front of him. He smiled and leaned in, kissing her softly on the lips. “I’ll pick you up at seven on Friday night.” He said. She got giddy again and hugged him before pulling away and readjusting her bra. Tyler moved his back pack to a more comfortable position on his shoulder as Megan stared at him. “Great. I’ll see you then Tyler!” She said above normal volume. Tyler grimaced as she turned and walked away. Immediately his head snapped to the right, looking for Aaron. Just as Tyler expected, he had finally heard Megan’s obnoxiously loud voice and was heading towards his next victim. The Brute “Out of my way punk.” Aaron muttered as he shoved a freshman from his path. He ran his hand through his long blonde hair and let a smirk cross his face as his gaze landed on a target. He normally wouldn’t have a beat down in mind this early in the morning, but this was a special circumstance. Three days prior, the “scraggily” kid in his sights made him bleed his own blood. Aaron knew he’d get in trouble, but the Saturday detention he’d serve was worth it. “Tyler. Buddy. Pal.” He said cockily as he approached the smaller freshman. His target had his back turned and was taking his first step into a classroom when Aaron grabbed his right arm. Tyler was pulled back into the hall and spun around. Aaron knew Tyler didn’t have to wonder who it was; he knew. Intimidation showed through Tyler’s actions as he followed Aaron’s frame from the floor on up. “Hey, Aaron; what’s up?” He asked. Aaron bellowed a laugh that caused some bystanders to look. Class was going to start in ten minutes; his plan was to lay the beat down in nine. He folded his arms as he looked down to the “lucky” subject in front of him. “You know what’s up. It’s time for the receipt I owe you.” Aaron said sternly. Tyler cleared his throat and nodded before looking to the floor. The backpack fell from his shoulder into his hand and was tossed aside to the floor. Aaron stood there watching, somewhat in a state of disbelief as Tyler took a deep breath and looked back up. He extended his arms and locked eyes with his foe. “You’re right. I deserve it. The bullied never beats up the bully.” Tyler said. Aaron’s wide smile slowly disappeared as he listened to what Tyler had said. Aaron knew he should have taken the shot, but he was too busy thinking. Tyler was right about one thing, he deserved what was coming. “That’s right. You’ve just got one thing wrong. You never beat me up. It was a lucky shot.” He said. Tyler nodded in a way to say “You’re right.” Aaron nodded back and unfolded his arms to crack his knuckles. Tyler took another deep breath, waiting for the first blow to land. He had gotten lucky once, but Aaron was going to make sure it wasn’t going to happen again. “So, hit me.” Tyler said. Aaron laughed and took a step back to allow his arm room to gain speed. A crowd had gathered as in any high school fight, blocking the view of any nearby faculty. Aaron cockily looked at his balled up fist, kissing it before finally swinging forward and landing a shot into Tyler’s stomach. His lungs were emptied from the air they needed and never got a chance to refill before the second punch landed. “You like that?” Aaron muttered. He stepped back and shook out his fist as Tyler slowly began to straighten. He looked to his left as he did and locked eyes with Megan, who looked as if she was about to cry. Aaron caught their moment and decided to spoil it. “What? You think she’s going to help you? After I’m done with you she’ll be begging me to show her a real man.” He said. Aaron winked at Megan who responded buy covering her cleavage. The bully laughed and turned back to Tyler, who was fully stood up. Aaron measured him up and ran towards him, looking to land a knockout blow. As he swung his right arm, Tyler ducked and hooked his own arm around his neck before sweeping the legs out. Both men fell to the floor, Aaron hitting hard on his back, head and tailbone. “Fuck you.” Tyler muttered. He pushed up to his feet and looked down to his bully, who grimaced in pain. Tyler was still trying to fully catch his breath as he stepped over Aaron and grabbed his backpack, heading into Trig.
