A New Year, New person

9 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/9/06 I have been referred many of times as numerous things. Some have been true, some have been blatant lies, but nonetheless I have been called them. A Dark Horse…. An underdog… A walk over… Those three terms have been used often throughout my wrestling career. Actually all three of those have been thrown around since I have started wrestling; all three names have been thrown around on a whim since I entered PRIME. When the next card is released every member on the roster runs to see who they are facing, they see my name they illuminate with the knowledge of an easy win. Well this is two thousand six, it is time for me to stop being the walk over, considered an underdog, and this time become the dark horse. The person you least expect to win, the person you expect to get their ass kicked and do nothing about it. Well that is about to change. Peter Vetra, is no longer someone you should over look, instead you should fear him. I have spent numerous hours in the gym lifting weights as of late. I have spent even more hours on the mat, working on my skills and wrestling moves. The only thing I have done more then that is studied. I have studied my previous matches; I have studied where I have gone wrong, and where I have wrestled correctly. The truth must be known, I have not been the greatest wrestler in PRIME. Many have known, many have wondered what I was even doing in a federation with such a high caliber of wrestlers. I don’t know how many times I have been asked to go to AWC or some smaller fed to work on my skills. I don’t need another federation, I need the motivation. That is one thing his federation seems to lack in, giving some of their lower card wrestlers. I guess I shouldn’t place the blame on the staff or anyone else. I guess I’m the one to blame for being so lazy or even better not showing the ambition. I guess it is kind of hard when I look down the roster and there are people who have yet to put their time in and they are already in the upper card or the main event. I mean I have sat and watch many champions pass me by, I mean the Universal Title has flipped flopped probably more times then I have won a match during my tenure here at PRIME. I mean let me sit here and count, we have had: Black Angel, Tony Rolo, Boda, Karina Wolfenden, Killean Sirrajin, Hoyt Williams, Tchu, and Clyde Walkins. So the list is currently at eight, of course that is not including the ones that slip my mind right now, I’m sure I missed a couple of people due to them not having that great of a title reign or some of those drugs that I have been taking are starting to kick in. But nonetheless the current count is eight. If we go and look at my current record on the PRIME website, it states I have ten wins and twenty two losses. Now I will be honest and don’t think that is currently up to date but it is close enough. So that tells me during my almost two years here in PRIME there has almost been as many Universal Champions as there have been matches I have won. Now either that shows I’m a horrible investment for PRIME and the Tyler Nelson group, or someone somewhere thinks that I have potential. But that isn’t the only thing that comes to mind, is it possible that I have slipped under the radar? I mean has anyone ever seen Office Space? I mean look at Milton, all he wanted was his red stapler and his desk not to move, but he was on payroll for a couple years after a layoff. Now that is some computer glitch. Is it possible for that kind of problem to have happened here in PRIME? I guess anything is possible since Clyde Walkins is our Universal Champion. I mean look how the hell is he the fucking Universal Champion? He went from tag team to being a singles wrestler and blew up. I guess the world is coming to end soon, I mean as I look at the names on our roster, Illustrious Face Eater, what kind of guy is this? I mean really what a name. I almost feel like I am doing something wrong, and I’m not cut out for this federation. But I guess this is why I have had a recent revelation. I mean I look at my past tapes, I look at the matches won, and the matches lost. The differences are there but they are very subtle. Stupid mistakes, trying a risqué move that was not needed nor was it called for. But yet I tried it. I guess that is why so many people are always saying experience matters. I guess in my two years here in PRIME, I have got my experience, I guess it is just time I start showing it. Tedious hours spent, the reason why I say spent and not wasted is because I have learned. I didn’t just stare at the screen watching the match, waiting for the outcome. Why? Because for one I already knew the outcome, I had been in the match before I had seen the moves before. But this time I was paying attention to the missed opportunities, I was paying attention to the times I got overzealous and went for some moves I shouldn’t have. But I paid attention and I got the missed moves, the moves I messed up and I am going to take that with me and show why I have become a new Peter Vetra. But at the end of the day is it going to be worth it? Of course it is, even if I go 0-100 this next year I will have learned how to become a better wrestler. Possibly in those 100 losses I will have learned that my career is not in wrestling. But that is not my worry right now. Right now I’m worried about becoming a better wrestler and if I don’t become better I might bow out. But I’m not going to bow out with out a fight and that is what the members of PRIME’s roster is going to get. A new life from Peter Vetra, a new beginning I guess you could say. ReVolution 86, the time for revealing it. At this event I have my first match of the year, my first match of the new Peter Vetra. Well hopefully a new start, of course I couldn’t be some kind of easy match, you know a singles match or hell even a tag match or a small triangle match. No, my good buddy, I use that term loosely, Tyler Nelson decided to throw me in his wonderful idea for the new Due Halo, he wants me to be one of the seven members of the roster to compete in it. Looking at the other six I think I might have an advantage here. I mean I know I am the only one of the seven to ever compete in a match like this before. So that is already one thing that is on my side. As I glance over the list there has not been one member who has been in PRIME as long as me, so what? That means any one of them could crack under the “big league” pressure. We have the second longest member of the roster in this as Tyler O’Neill who is on my side, a fellow Imperium member. A buddy, a person I should be able to turn to in the event I need help. So once again I’m starting to stack the chips in my corner. I’m seeing the scales start to tip towards me, I think I might have a chance for a good 2006. This event might just be what I need to move on, and become the next real threat in PRIME. But as I listen to the numerous wrestling wires, while I listen to the rumor mills, I always hear that there are some names in this match that could be the next big thing in PRIME. I mean hell we have Chet Worth, we have Brian Arson, and then this new guy El Vice. From what I have heard any one of these guys might be the next Universal Champion, not that it would be hard to beat Clyde Walkins. These guys might fall under the pressure of their first PRIME event. I remember mine, what a time the lights, the fans screaming for you even though they don’t know who you are. It really is a great time, but that is the problem, people get caught up in a good time not realize their career might be on the line. So that is what I’m going to hope for, the deer in the headlights scenario. A chance for me to get a win that isn’t a match on the dark card. A chance for I to become more known and this might be the time and the place. I guess it is now up to me to capitalize. The tools have all been given to me, the information is all at my disposal, I just need to execute.
