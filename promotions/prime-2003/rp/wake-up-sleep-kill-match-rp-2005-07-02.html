Wake Up, Sleep, Kill (match RP)

2 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7-2-05 The last time Hoyt Williams got a good night sleep was back when Christ was a cowboy. Given the grim situation of lack of Cowboy’s in our culture, and the fact Jesus went to boarding school many camp fires ago, one could conclude that it’s been a long time since the Sandman has visited the savior. One would conclude right. Hoyt stares restlessly at the clock, while in his bed lying next to the plentiful Virgin Mary. The clock appears to be giving Hoyt the finger as it displays an angry red one o’clock AM read out. The number one of coarse is acting as the outlaw middle digit taunting yet smirking in its vulgar display of power. Sleep is required by the body to function just as water and food are. After twenty-four hours of sleep depravation the body starts to display similar symptoms of drug use. The insomniac begins to become paranoid and the world seems to freeze then speed up. Very similar to the trip marijuana provides. At forty-eight hours things become interesting as the subject begins to hallucinate and lose their mind. It’s not really the sleep that’s required by the body but rather the R.E.M. which is also what causes dreams. That’s Rapid Eye Movement for my less then technical friends, not the band. Dreams are what Hoyt needs right now, but that may also be the very thing keeping him awake. The dream of becoming Universal Champion weighs heavy on his mind. If he’s not careful soon his dreams will be dreaming him. Mary: Are you still awake? Hoyt: It’s too fucking hot in here. The Jew-lie heat gives an insomniac’s something to blame for the lack of snores. They become over sensitive to temperature and light. Mary: The air is cranking, it’s like sixty five in here. Hoyt: My God it feels like Auschwitz I’m covered in perspiration. Anti-Semitism airs its ugly head once again as Hoyt kicks off the thin white sheet leaving him with nothing on but his white undies. Mary: You need to stop plotting on how you’re going to destroy Killean and let fate run it’s course. Hoyt: Sometimes even fate needs a kick in the ass. Mary: If you kick fate anymore you’re going to bruise its ass. Hoyt: I’m sorry I can’t allow a world where entertainment Nazi’s like Killean stifle out all elements of fun from life. We live in a world where Canadian whores’ like AIMZ spread their disease through their unholiest of holes while promoting drug use and glorifying night life. Meanwhile human turds such as the Trashcan Man treat their bodies not as a temple to the lord, but rather a trailer park in south-east Tennessee. Trash has no welfare for his body or his mind which is a gift from God to him. No, I simply can’t allow such tomfoolery to continue. This is my world and I’m here to save it, not help destroy it. Mary: But by not sleeping you’re doing your body damage. Hoyt: In less then three weeks I will become the leader and champion of PRIME. All will look to me for guidance and my words will set them free. I will sleep some time from now until then BUT I will not rest until that belt is around my waist where it belongs. Hoyt sighs as he sits up frustrated with the world but more so the night. To him the darkness around him which creates an ideal sleeping environment is nothing more then sheer torture. Like being late for a job interview and having a bus pull up yet refuse to open the door for you. Hoyt: Killean has God’s title which was UPS-ed to me by GOD himself. How do you explain to your father that you lost an expensive jewelry gift that he gave you? It’s hard enough to do that with a mortal dad but try your hand with God. He’s a forgiving man but he likes to dish out the vengeful part first. Mary: You’ll get it back honey and do him right by bringing him the Universal Title as well. Hoyt: You’re damn right I will. The majority would look at the match this week as a cluster of contenders and nothing more. But I understand what’s at stake, as it’s the first face to face match of the champion and GOD’S CHAMPION. Even if it is cluttered with cunts like AIMZ, dirtballs like Trash, no-names like Ellis, and I don’t know what the fuck like Vanfurless. To win this battle would be huge. Mary: You must work with Furher and Ellis and get them to understand what’s at stake. Not just for your career, but for theirs as well. Hoyt: Yes, great idea a little brainwash and manipulation should work just fine to get those two stooges to join GREATNESS. But still I need to make a statement to Killean that I’m not to be messed with. Although she looks innocent and is called “Virgin” she may indeed be the whisper of temptation and vengeance in the ear of Hoyt. Mary much like Eve offers up the apples time and time again to her most powerful and skilled man. Mary: Perhaps you should take something of his. Hoyt: What like steal his UT title? Mary: You have to be tired you’re coming up with lame ideas. I’m talking about something more meaningful, something more permanent, something more biblically vengeful. Like you and God did with Boda. An evil and sick smile comes over the face of the man here to purify the world. He gets what she’s saying and agrees with her wholeheartedly. Hoyt: I need to kill off something or somebody Killean loves. I need to go the extra mile and deliver repercussions for his lies. Job didn’t understand what he had until it was taken from him. I agree babycakes, I’ll talk to the landlord of life tonight and have somebody evicted. Mary: Aimz would be a good somebody. Hoyt: You’ve been listening to my dialogue with GOD who tells me everything. He and I are the only ones who know about that. Mary: You talk loud to him sometimes I over hear. Hoyt: No, as much as I want to get rid of AIMZ she is better suited to be persecuted by my hands on camera not GOD’S in the shadows. Same is true of Killean; GOD has sent me to make a public display of his vengeance on TV not something that could be chalked up as accident or written off as coincident. I will deliver personally to them something very real. AIMZ will have her judgment day, but I will be the jury. Mary: Who then? Hoyt: Tomorrow morning call RISP headquarters and get me all the information you can on Killean. I want to know where he drinks, who his friends are, who he phones, who he flirts with, what he eats, when he sleeps, when he uses the john, what he’s allergic too, how many teeth he has, if he has any pets, and where he gets those stupid glasses. I want the whole nine yards. I’m going to destroy his life just like when GOD took his wife. Oh the games people play now. Hoyt rests his head back down on the soft pillow as the thought of ruining Killean’s life somehow puts him at ease. Mary: You are going to decimate Killean at the Pay-Per-View that is what they call you ya know. Mr. Pay-Per-View!! You will be the Universal Champion unifying the title with God’s belt then the holocaust of PRIME begins. The world will watch as you decimate evil in the name of GOD live on television. There will be no denying your existents because you will prove it week in and week out. Soon the world will notice and Armageddon will begin and when it ends you will rule the world in the name of GOD. It all starts now doesn’t it Hoyt….Hoyt…HOYT?? Mary turns over and laughs as she realizes she’s been ranting to herself. Hoyt is now finally sound asleep with a twisted smirk on his perfectly structured face seemingly at peace with the world. =w=
