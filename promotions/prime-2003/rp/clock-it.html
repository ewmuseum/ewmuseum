Devin calmly opens the balcony door, stops dead in his tracks, turns around, gives one last look at Kimberly, blows a kiss in her direction, turns back around to face the balcony, steps up onto the railing, takes a forward step and lets gravity guide him down to the ground with a loud thud that causes everybody in the front of the house to rush to the back of the house. "GODDAMMIT, GET A FUCKING STRETCHER BACK HERE NOW! HE MIGHT STILL HAVE A CHANCE!" The last thing he wants right now. And I’m the most flawed human being this world has to offer. End
