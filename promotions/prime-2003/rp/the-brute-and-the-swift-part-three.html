The Brute and The Swift: Part Three

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1998 THE SWIFT Tyler sat at the table twirling his pen. Every so often it would make contact with the paper in front of him, creating another line in his doodle. It was 11:47 in the morning on Saturday; and he was sitting in the library of Easton Area High School. He watched the clock tick away his Sunday detention for his actions Monday at school. “Tell me again why this didn’t happen yesterday?” He asked across the room. The woman sitting at the large counter at the front of the library didn’t look up to him. She didn’t even respond right away. She flipped a few more pages in her “Glamour” magazine. Tyler cleared his throat to give her a kick in the rear; it worked. “The power was out because of the storms.” She said. Tyler tapped his pen on the paper as he looked at the clock once again. Normally he was an easy going kid but he got stuck at school on a Sunday he started to get a little agitated; and if he was agitated then somebody else was going to be too. “This school doesn’t know what candles are?” He said aloud. A smile escaped to his face after he said it. The lady looked up from her magazine and over to him with an annoyed look on her face. This only made his smirk grow bigger as he looked at the clock again. “Mission accomplished.” He muttered to himself. At the same time he watched the second hand click away. No matter how hard he tried, the clock never moved any faster. Tyler just wanted to be home. In six hours he’d be gathered with his friends for Over the Edge. Stone Cold was going up against Dude Love for the WWF Championship and The Nation would square off against DX. “Are you ready?” Tyler muttered under his breath. His pen tapped the paper twice in rhythm. He continued to tap his pen as he began humming as well. Soon the D-Generation X theme song was broadcasting in full force from his mouth as he tapped the beat on the table. The woman glanced up as Tyler continued. “Just got tired of doing what you told us to do.” Tyler continued. The woman rolled her eyes and looked back down to her magazine. In his performance he glanced up at the clock and saw it was 11:58. He quickly closed his notebook and shoved the pen in his pocket, still humming. He went walking towards the door just as the woman stood up. “Hey, that bell hasn’t rung yet. You can’t go anywhere.” She said. Tyler broke his tune and laughed before dropping his notebook and thrusting his arms to his crotch in an X. “Suck it!” He said before grabbing his notebook and heading out the door. He ran down the short hallway and turned the corner to exit through a side entrance behind the building. He exited and began jogging down the alley-like passage; the main building to his left and the track facility to his right. Tyler approached a dumpster and took a nosedive to the asphalt, tripping over an extended foot. THE BRUTE Aaron stepped from behind the dumpster with his arms folded; a bellowing laughed escaped his mouth. He watched as Tyler turned himself around to look in Aaron’s direction. Tyler made eye contact with the bigger kid and slowly pushed his way to his feet. “For once, I wasn’t the one getting the weekend detention. It was kind of refreshing.” Aaron said. Tyler stood vertical in front of him, never allowing his gaze to move from Aaron. Aaron did the same, trying to intimidate his prey. As the past week had shown, it was going to take some work. Tyler cleared his throat and glanced at the blood dripping from his scraped elbow. “That sucked. I don’t know why you keep putting yourself in positions to be there every weekend.” Tyler replied. For the first time since they met, Aaron laughed at Tyler’s comment. It wasn’t a disrespectful laugh, but a genuine “that was funny” laugh. Tyler caught this and a small smirk appeared on his face as Aaron quickly regained his stern look. “You’ve got this coming, punk. You avoided it twice this week. Now it’s time to take it like a man.” Aaron said. Tyler shrugged his shoulders as the bully moved closer. Aaron balled his fist and began punching into his open hand, readying himself. Tyler sighed and closed his eyes, preparing for the blow that would land. “Let’s just get it over with.” Tyler said. Tyler stood there with his eyes shut, waiting for what seemed like an eternity for that first punch. Finally after nearly thirty seconds he felt breath on the top of his head and slowly opened his eyes. Aaron was standing face to face, or as close to face to face as he could with the shorter boy; it was more like face to chest. “You know you’ve got guts to just stand here and take this, dude. I’ll admit I was surprised as hell when you told me to hit you last Monday.” Aaron stated. Tyler smirked again, as if he had accomplished something. Aaron knew this thought just by the look on his face. It was time to put it to rest. Aaron backed a foot or two away and turned his torso away before turning back and landing a right hand to Tyler’s gut. Tyler keeled over, but was immediately brought back up with a knee to the face. “Fuck.” He said. Aaron laughed as he watched Tyler stumble backwards a few steps and reach to his nose. As he dropped his hand to his side, Tyler noticed the blood on it and a look of intensity came over his face. Aaron was expecting him to turn and run away, but he wasn’t expecting him to fight back. That’s just what Tyler did next. He ran forward and raised his leg into the air, driving a boot into Aaron’s chest. Tyler fell to his rear as Aaron stumbled backwards and turned around, trying to catch his breath. “I’m sick of your shit, Aaron.” Tyler yelled as he got to his feet and ran towards his attacker. Tyler leapt into the air before Aaron could turn around and latched onto his neck. Aaron’s left arm went upwards into the air as his right was driven downwards. He felt pressure on the back of his neck from the left and his throat from the right. He struggled momentarily to escape, but soon his breathing labored and he stopped squirming. He dropped his big frame to a knee as he slowly realized what was happening. The sunlight coming from the blue sky was beginning to fade as his eyes closed. He struggled to stay conscious for a few more seconds before he knew it was inevitable and gave in. As he slipped away from the real world, he fell to his side and lightly felt Tyler’s legs wrap around his lower body. And then it was over. When his eyes re-opened, it seemed as though it had only been seconds, but he knew it had been longer. His vision was blurry at first, but he could make out Tyler just a few feet away. The sweet taste of air rushed back into his lungs as his vision cleared. Tyler reached down and picked up his notebook before jogging from the alley and out of sight. “This is getting ridiculous.” Aaron said to himself. He had to do something different. He had to quit getting his ass kicked.
