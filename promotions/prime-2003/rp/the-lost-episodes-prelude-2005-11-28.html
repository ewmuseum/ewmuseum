The Lost Episodes: Prelude

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 28, 2005 The Lost Episodes: Preludeby Needles Scharf [Begin...] Vangelus Olsig was reported missing From Revolution this week... How are you going to continue under this condition... It is my discomfort to inform you that your father has died... Vangelus, You're going up for adoption... The winner of this match...VANELUS OLSIG! So it is written...so shall it be executed... How dare you betray me? I thought you loved me? You'll never be the man your father was... EXILE! You're not fit for life anymore... 'Arghh!' The slam of the book echoed throughout the room as his breaths grew heavy in an evident sign of anger. A masters degree at Huntigdon college in Montgomery, Alabama was his focus, but various voices echoeing through his head, preventing him from comprehending a communications test was his obstacle. In all of my years of knowing Vangelus, I've never known him to be a man of infinite wisdom, and the frustration being displayed here as he struggled to cram in some last minute studying boasted that fact. He took his hand and ran it through his hair as he tossed the book to the ground in one swift motion...just like his father...never working for anything but yet waiting for it to arrive on a silver platter and then when it doesn't, he throws in the towel. Thats one of the things I could never respect about Vangelus. I loved his courage inside of the ring, and I even admired his wisdom in the field of work he'd chosen, but the fact that he resembled his father in every aspect was the one thing that killed his image in my book. Damn, I hated his father. Olsig took a deep breath as he pushed himself out of the chair in front of his office room desk. He made his way over the nearby dressing mirror and leaned foward on the dresser using his hands as a mount as he stared deep into the eyes of his reflection. Those girlish crystal blue eyes, they reminded me of another aspect I hated about Vangelus, but I guess you label that as mere irrelevance. Anyhow, as he glanced into his eyes he saw a world that was crumbling down around him. A world that he thoguht he'd built on a solid foundation, only to watch it fade with the pressure of wrestling, school, and of course stardom. Carrying the Intense belt and competing with worldwide names such as Angelo Deville, Brandon Youngblood, Nova, and Hoyt Williams had brought forth a sudden brink of stardom...not enough to win him a popularity contest, but stardom nonetheless. 'Damn...' He shouted as he pounded his fist against the dresser top. Such a little baby at times, but I guess thats what makes him such a vulnerable being. Thats the reason he could never hang with yours truly...you see, I am a man of great courage, pride, and integrity...therefore I tend to stray away from weaklings such as young Olsig. Thats what makes Needles Scharf so great...yeah, thats it. 'FRANCIS!!' Olsig screamed. Francis is his butler, hired only a few weeks ago and already cracking under the pressure of serving such an ungrateful brat as Vangelus. Francis also served a brief term as butler for Olsig's parents as well, though, so I guess it was only fitting that he carry on duty in the family he'd grown a part of...even though I can almost gaurantee that the white collared fool is regretting it now. The door creaked open as in walked Francis, decked out in the usual black tuxedo and wh-...you know what? I always wondered Who in the name of mayonaise came up with the idea of butlers wearing tuxedos all day in the house? Must have been some sick freak who got some sort of pleasure out of it all. Nonetheless, Francis stepped inside of the room and began to tend to the aid of his master. 'Yes, Mr. Olsig...how may I help you?' He stated. 'Book me a vacation in the tropics...I need to clear my mind.' responded Vangelus. You'd think that someone who was under as much pressure as he made himself out to be would pick a better place to relax other than the tropics. *Sigh*...so young...so naiive. Francis nodded in approval and turned to exit the room, but halted in his tracks and turned around to inform Vangelus of something. 'By the way, Mr. Olsig, .the board called...you're booked this week against Vangellus Oxios' He stated, obvious fear in his voice at the fact that his master would snap before his very eyes for not informing him sooner of such an announcement. He was right. Vangelus peeled his glance off of his reflection and slowly turned to stare at Francis with a look of unwelcoming anger present in his eyes. 'Oh really? And now is the time in which you inform of me this? What the hell is wrong with you Francis!?!' Olsig shouted, slowly walking and getting into the face of Francis who was less than pleased with the manner, but held his cool. 'I-I'm sorry sir, it must have slipped my mind, I mean y-..' 'Slipped your mind? Well try not to let THIS slip your mind...the next time you decide to keep such news away from me, both you'll be slipping your mind in the Damn unemployment line...savvy?' If I didn't know any better, I'd swear it was that time of the month. Its not too often that Needles Scharf feels compassion for someone, but as I looked into the eyes of Francis who seemed as if he was on the brink of tears at the mistreatment that had been slapped in his face. Such a good man, treated in such an unjust manner. Suddenly, out of nowhere, Vangelus sends a fist straight into the nearby wall in anger, tearing a hole into the wall. Such an ill tempered man, destroying such an innocent wall. 'GOOD HELP IS SO HARD TO F-...' Suddenly, Olsig put a hault to his outrage and stared into the hole that he had created in the wall. He tilted his head to the side in quiry as he stared at the hole, obviously intrigured by something inside. In the background was Francis, holding his hand up to his mouth in shock...obviously knowing something about the hole in the wall that his Master did not. Olsig walked closer to the hole and begin to tear away at it, making it bigger than before. Finally, once the hole was big enough, Olsig reached inside and yanked out a bag of VHS tapes, each with a certain label on them. He pulled the tapes out one by one, reading the labels on them all. Hidden: Domination PlanHidden: Death of SalvadorHidden: Widespread Disease 'Mr. Olsi-...' 'These are my father's tapes, aren't they?' Olsig interrupted, not taking his eyes off of the bag of tapes that contained what I'd assumed to be around seven tapes or so. I was never a math major, but I believe thats a pretty good estimate. 'W-Well...O-Of course not, I me-...' 'Truth...Francis' Olsig interrupted once again, this time peeling his eyes off of the bag of tapes and into the eyes of Francis with an even more demeaning look in his face. Francis took a deep sigh as he held his head low in sorrow for what he was about to inform his orignial master's son. '...Yes.' Olsig kept his glaze on Francis, looking as if he is about to snap on the poor guy. If he was this intimidating inside of the ring, maybe he'd be a world champion or something. Olsig finally took his eyes off of Francis, who took a deep breath of relief. Olsig returned to the bag of tapes and began to fumble through them until he reached one in particular that seemed to catch his interest. He pulled it out and read the label. Hidden: Confidential HideoutM Olsig held the tape in his hand as he placed the bag with the rest of the tapes atop his office desk. He took the tape in his hand and inserted it into the VCR/DVD of his television, which wasn't very impressive to say the least. Looks like it came from a local walmart. Nonetheless, Olsig pressed 'Play' on the VCR and automatically the tape began to play. It scratched a bit at first, but then cleared up revealing Czar Olsig, Vangelus's father for those who have been living under a rock, standing outside of a hut that seemed to be underground. Operation Number 00219. Location...Sa'ad, Israel. Time...14:53. Date...January 1st, 2002. My Hideout is simple. Point 212 latitude, point 187 longitude. At the point of unity lies a small X marked in black. Under the X is where I will locate until the precedings of my legacy are adhered. The location of my hideout resides in a scarce part of the country. Most will believe me to be dead after allegations, so It's not likely I'll be hunted. Remember, these tapes are kept only for record and for my allies whom I deem worthy of knowing. All information gathered from this record should be kept confidential. Operation Number 00219...Out. And then Static. Olsig stood there in awe of what had just transpired on his television. Francis stood there, rattling in his boots in fear of what may transpire before his very eyes, but to his suprise Olsig hadn't snapped...yet. Instead, he ran his hand through his hair once more before taking a deep sigh and speaking without even bothering to turn around and look at Francis. '...He's alive, isn't he?' Olsig stated as he glanced at the ground below him with sorrow in his eyes. If i had a heart, I'd probably feel sorry for him...but thats a big probably. 'I-I'm...sorry, Mr. Olsig. You weren't supposed to find out...especially not like this.' Francis stated, his voice cracking under the pressure of having to inform his master of such a situation. What most likely scared him the most, though, was the embroiling anger that was shaping on the face of Vangelus. Such a hideous site indeed. The look became even more clear as Olsig finally turned around and faced Francis, red veins forming in his eyes as he firmly grasped his hair trying to keep his cool. ...It failed. 'WHY THE FUCK DIDN'T YOU SHOW ME THIS BEFORE!?!?' Olsig yelled out in anger, leaving Francis to coward in fear as the trembling in his boots became even more evident. 'I was g-...' 'Oh what? You were going to wait until he decided to resurrect, Huh? HUH? IS THAT WHAT THE FUCK YOU WERE WAITING ON!?!' Olsig shouted, jamming his index finger in the fact of Francis who didn't know how to respond...other than shivering in his boots like a naked whitney houston in the winter. Might I take time to add that Francis wasn't the most in-shape being. I mean, he was as skinny as a toothpick...his chest looked like a piece of cardboard, and not to mention that the guy was short with an annoying squeeky voice that made you wonder if he'd hit puberty yet. He had nothing going for him, so with a outraged sweaty wrestler, albeit not a very good one in my opion, yelling down his throat...you could just imagine the type of fear he was experiencing. 'You know what?' Olsig stated as he turned from the face of Francis and rapidly walked over to his office desk, yanking out the drawer. 'I've got something that going to teach you real good, boy.' He stated, as he yanked his arm out of the drawer...but not empty handed. ...A .22 Magnum. 'OH MY G-...' 'Shut up...' Olsig demanded as he aimed the gun at Francis, backing Francis up against the wall in fear. 'Please calm down mr. Ol-...' 'I SAID SHUT UP!!' Screamed Olsig. Francis was not pinned up against the wall with a .22 magnum pointed his way. That must be scary indeed. 'You've been screwing with me ever since I hired you...informing me of booked matches two days before they occur, giving me wrong locations making me miss Rev which result in a front column placed about it on the PRIME website...but this...this just tops the line. You know how I feel about my father, how DARE you keep something like this away from me!?!' 'Please...if you'd just calm down.' 'Oh, I'm going to calm down. Witnessing your blood scattered all across his cheap carpet is going to make me veeeeeery calm.' (Cue Suspense Music) Suddenly, as Francis covered up for his life and the time seemed right for the bullet to be placed through his heart....Olsig stopped. I knew the kid didn't have the balls. He lowered the gun and looked at it for a moment, before dropping it unto the ground. He then held out his palms, staring at them as if blood was rushing from their veins. In one swift movement, he made his way over to the dresser once more where he looked at himself in the mirror...not at his reflection, but at what he had become. As he glanced deep in his eyes, he didn't see Vangelus Olsig anymore...he saw Czar Olsig. He saw all of the deviance that his father boasted becoming a part of him. He saw the violence and bloodrage that made his father the convict he was. He saw himself changing into the man whom he swore he'd never be like... A tear tickled his cheek as it rolled down. I could see why he was crying. Resembling a man like Czar Olsig isn't something I'd wish on anyone, even my worst enemy....wait....Czar IS my worst enemy. Nonetheless, a sniff followed the tear, followed by another accompanied by even more tears unto Olsig collapsed to his knees, burying his face in the palm of his hands in fear of what he had become. Francis walked over to Olsig and got on his knees as well, wrapping his arms around Olsig in comfort. And as the scene faded, the tears of Olsig filled the room. Because with the prelude of a lost chapter in his life ending...chapter one... Had Begun... And with it came a desperate Vangelus Olsig who was ready to begin the massive manhunt... For his Father.
