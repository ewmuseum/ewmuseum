Date: Always in my dreams ~Jon Kano has had enough. Fuck it all. Fuck it all to hell. He can't take it anymore. Tropical Turmoil has drained him of all he is professionally. He has had enough of this bullshit. Kano is at his wits end, period. Kano has always been a man with little time to himself. He is the CEO of his own company, Kano Enterprises, a career in and of itself. Then he has to deal with family. He has a loving wife and infant son at home, whom he loves dearly. The only problem is Kano's wife, Kristin, wants him to quit wrestling. She is tired of him being gone so much and the injuries he has incurred in his ten year career. Then he is a world class professional wrestler. He has a certain standard of performance he has to maintain in order to keep people happy. To say Kano's stress level is high is like saying Hurricane Katrina was a bad storm, quite understated, don't you believe? Kano is in a foul mood right now, he is beginning to get back on top of his wrestling game, but at personal prices. At Tropical Turmoil, we saw Christopher, er, Boda, er, Chris Sloboda, er, Chris Allen, er whatever the hell he is calling himself these days return to PRIME. He thanked everyone for thoughts and prayers during his tough times of recovery. The only person who he didn't thank was the one who helped him the most, Jon Kano. That has really hurt Jon Kano and now put him into the aforementioned foul mood we find him this lovely evening in Miami, this site of this week's ReVolution. Speaking of ReVolution, Kano has a match. A match against one of the hottest wrestlers in PRIME today, Aimz. Aimz just got done with K-Wolf and won, not many people can lay claim to that. So Kano, needless to say, has his work cut out for him. Will he rise to the challenge or is his mind too focused on other things to concentrate and get down to business when he needs to?~ *Miami, Florida. 8:30 pm EST. Sunday, September 4, 2005. Downtown Miami is beautiful at night. Neon lights, loud music, a melting pot of latino, white, and african american cultures. Trying to enjoy all of this is Jon Kano, in tan khakis shorts, a plain white wife-beater shirt, and golden-rimmed sunglasses hiding his eyes (even though it is night). As people are in the streets dancing to reggae music with the apparent street party going on, Kano just keeps moving through the people, not particularily giving a shit about anyone or anything.* Kano: Miami, Florida. One of the great party cities in the United States. I have had many a good time here in Miami. How can you not have a good time in Miami? I'll tell you how. When you don't know whether to shit or go blind, who is on your side and who isn't. *Kano finally makes his way through the people and onto the next block, where there is hardly a soul, mostly parked cars and alleys that lead to godknowswhat. Kano continues down this street. Suddenly this man approaches Kano. This man is obviously, well, gay. He is dressed in pink hot pants and a skin-tight see through black mesh shirt. This man has short black hair and isn't a small guy. He is about 6'4' and 200 pounds, but Kano, being nearly seven feet tall and well over three hundred pounds, towers over him.* Man: Hey big boy. Whacha doin'? Wanna go have some fun? *Kano looks annoyed.* Kano: Do I look like I am in the mood for some fun, especially from you? I hate to burst your bubble, but I don't swing your way. Now back off before you make the biggest mistake of your life. *Kano begins to walk past the gay man, who grabs Kano's right arm. Kano simply grabs the man's hand with his left hand and you can actually hear the bones in the poor gay man's hand break as Kano literally crushes it inside of his own palm. The scary thing about that isn't Kano's short fuse at the moment, but the fact that Kano is right handed and the strength he just displayed was with his non-dominant hand. After doing this, Kano leaves the man laying on the ground behind him as he walks away.* Kano: Now where was I? Oh yeah, Tropical Turmoil could have been a lot of fun for me. And the Storming the Beach match was fun. Trashy and me kicked ass and took names in that match, the rest of our team just wasn't on top of their game. But Boda came back, right? No, Chris Sloboda came back. No, I stand corrected. 'Hardknocks' Chris Allen made his return. Nope, I just can't get it right. Christopher made his debut. Now I am glad Christopher seems to have found God and all. I am a reasonably religious man. I believe in God and I believe that I am forever in His debt for creating me. However, for Christopher to just write me off like nothing is bullshit. *Kano keeps walking as he runs his hands through his hair.* Kano: I am not a selfish man. I just cut a check for $15,000 to the American Red Cross for the fine work they are doing along the Gulf Coast. I have just sponsored a directive for the Las Vegas chamber of commerce to raise additional money for the same cause. I don't ask for much. And I have never asked Chris Sloboda for a damn thing in all of the years I have known him. However, I will not sit idlely by while he forgets that I was the one who was there for him when he needed someone the most. I know Chris well enough to know he knows and to know he is truly grateful. I will go about my business here in PRIME and elsewhere like only I can because I can. While Chris goes about his, I know he will need help from time to time. I will always be there for him, if not for him, for his daughters, and the newest edition, his son. *Kano turns another corner and continues down this block, another block of nothingness in the otherwise vibrant nightlife of the city of Miami.* Kano: All I wanted was a thank you. It isn't asking too much. I was raised that if someone did something for you, the decent thing to do was to say two simple words, thank you. Now with that said, and with all that went down at Tropical Turmoil, most people would voice their opinion that a strong man needs to go after Hoyt's Imperium. For those looking to me for this, look elsewhere. I must be honest, from all the stables I have fought against in my career, from MEDIA to the Red Army to the Dark Age, I am exhausted. Don't get me wrong, if Imperium walks toward me, I won't back down an inch. Just at this time, Imperium is too strong for me to lead the charge against them by myself, like I have always done in the past. *Kano is obviously disgusted by saying this, but knows the words he has just spoken are true, and lets out a sigh.* Kano: But there is no rest for the weary in this business. On ReVolution, is the beginning of the Jewel in the Crown tournament right here in PRIME, the annual tourney that can make or break a career. Now, who do I have the good luck of drawing for the first round? Aimz. F*ck. Now here is the thing, I hate wrestling women. I don't want to beat on them, it puts you in an uncomfortable position. Now I like Amy, I have known her for years. And she is probably the most talented woman in wrestling, next to Karina. Aimz relies on speed. I rely on power. The classic matchup, ladies and gentlemen, of speed versus power. Which wins? Aimz is on a roll, there is no question. *Kano turns yet another corner and seems to be getting in darker and deeper parts of Miami, parts that you shouldn't be in at night.* Kano: Aimz wrestles great, but I am a great wrestler. There is a difference. I don't know who is going to win the match this week, but I can guarantee one thing, it will be entertaining as hell, and a great opening to the tournament. Now a little more onto Aimz, she is a cute little thing. I mean if I wasn't a happily married man, I'd try to get with her, maybe. That is part of her success, the sexy assassin. Vicious vixen. Bloodthirsty bitch. Whatever you want to call her, it doesn't make a difference. She can still get the job done in the ring, so can I. She and I have never wrestled each other one on one before. And I can give the Jon Kano promise that she has never stepped in the ring with anyone quite like me before and vice versa. It will come down to who really wants to go somewhere in this tournament. And you know what, put your money on the legend from Sin City. At ReVolution, I will show Aimz and PRIME once again why I am what I say I am, I am WHAT REAL LEGENDS ARE MADE OF. *Kano reaches into his pocket and grabs a pack of his trademark Marlboro Menthol Lights. He picks one out of the pack and lights one, pushing the pack down into his pocket. He takes a deep breath of the cancerous smoke that one day will probably claim his life into his lungs and exhales.* Kano: You know, I catch so much shit for smoking. My doctor yells at me about it. My wife begs me to quit. I get letters everyday from parents asking me to quit smoking on screen because their kids watch PRIME. Some of my collegues say that I would have won a helluva lot more titles if I wouldn't smoke. They're all right. But have you ever tried to quit smoking? It would be like Hoyt suddenly not being stunning full of shit. *For the first time in this little vignette, Kano is actually smiling.* Kano: Nothing like an old-fashioned cheap shot at someone to get a smile across my face. *Kano takes another deep drag from the cancer stick.* Kano: I'll quit smoking one of these days, probably the day after I die. Now Aimz, you should feel a little bit better about getting into the ring with me. A few minutes ago, I was in a really bad mood, and I am mean when I am in a bad mood. I am a real bastard, we all know this. But now I am cracking a few jokes and starting to have a good time. Hell, now I feel like going back to the party and having a few drinks. *Kano pauses in midstep as he looks around a bit.* Kano: Now just one question remains, WHERE THE HELL AM I? *Kano's face looks a bit concerned as he tries to look up at the sky to negotiate his way back to familiarity.* Kano: Yeah, I don't think navigating by the stars like I was taught in the Army works too well in the city. Son of a bitch, where did I put my phone? *Kano begins to pat himself down, looking for his cell phone.* Kano: To hell with it, I can backtrack. I think....I hope. *The scene fades to black as Kano begins to retrace his steps slowly, hoping to find his way back to wherever he came from in Miami.* ~So Kano seems to be in a slightly better mood. Is this a good thing or bad thing for Kano? Is this a good or bad thing for Aimz? How will Kano and the new incarnation of Christopher Sloboda get along in the future? Will Kano rise above his animosity, or will he sink below it? Is this new era of PRIME the Imperium's to take? Will Kano change his mind and lead the charge against them as he has done with success in the past against the megastables of yesteryear? At ReVolution, the Jewel in the Crown tournament commences with Kano and Aimz as the first match. A hell of a curtain raiser, don't you think? Speed versus power. Speed kills but can equalize power. All of this can be somewhat answered on this week's edition of ReVolution only on Playboy TV. And yet another afterthought, can Kano even find his way to ReVolution? And at ReVolution, Kano will take his first step towards PRIME immortality.~
