Lovecraftian Musings

22 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 22, 2006 "I can't believe I let you talk me into being here." "Would you rather go watch the football team practice?" "..." "Well?" "Will the cheerleaders be there?" ".... Shut up and keep reading." Joey Melton, the Ringmaster, Cirque du Soliel Club Member and Tamer of African Elephants, heaved a defeated sigh as he turned yet another black-tinted page in the thick text that rested in his lap. "And take off those sunglasses, you look ridiculous in here with them on," Lindsay Troy chided, juggling scolding her man, water-sipping and web-browsing on her laptop with ease, while Melton pushed his shades further up his face out of spite. "Do you know how bad it would look if anyone recognized me in here?" Joey protested, looking around at the endless rows and rows of books on either side of him and sliding further down in his chair. "I knew I should have brought the fake beard too." "Joey, this is a library, not a Thai whorehouse." "A whorehouse is infinitely more fun. Diseases excluded. And, less embarrassing. At least the public wouldn't be shocked to find me there. But a library? It's the Final Frontier and I'm not William Shatner getting roasted on Comedy Central." "You said you wanted to help me do research and now you're going to bitch about it?" She whipped her head around, her curls lashing against Joey's cheek. "When you said research," Joey leaned forward and spoke in a harsh whisper, "I thought you meant watching tapes, not flying back to this state to delve into academia about this crackpot." It's true, the newly-crowned PRIME tag team champions were in a library late Monday afternoon. The John Hay Library on the campus of Brown University in Providence, Rhode Island, to be precise. Troy had gotten it into her head that the best way to understand the boys of the Covenant was to do a little research on the man who wrote the Mythos which they worshiped: HP Lovecraft. And what better place than to get all the necessary, and important, information on the eccentric horror writer than his birthplace of good old Providence, where PRIME had been only three shows ago. "Academia, nice. Where'd you pick that one up?" "Dictionary dot com." Joey reached across Lindsay to steal a swig of water. "But really, couldn't we have just done this by looking on Wikipedia in the comfort of the living room?" "No. Well, yeah, I suppose, but no. This library is going to have texts and documents and information that you can't find by just going to Wikipedia or by ordering a book off of Amazon. It may not be your method of choice, but you're not the one with a match this week, are you? And you wanted to tag along, so quit whining and hand me that book." He reluctantly forked the book in his lap over, then raised his sunglasses to the top of his head in order to rub his eyes. "All you're going to find there is a bunch of names that I can't pronounce about a bunch of creatures that don't exist. Seriously, I don't know which is more insane: these three screwballs waiting for some giant octopus-man-demon-thing to finally wake up or Tom Cruise's Xenu fascination." "HP Lovecraft is a respected author who I'm sure didn't have the foresight into the future to know that three professional wrestlers in the twenty-first century would take his fiction seriously," Troy remarked while skimming demon creature biographies. "L. Ron Hubbard created a religion on a bet which further spawned the insanity of celebrities and Cruise's couch-jumping." "I think the winner is clear..." Melton mused. "I've got tapes on Jack, Knight and Tully. I know people who've been around them on a professional level. But spiritually, I'm intrigued by this outrageousness. Sure, no one will probably ever find Cthulhu in a bar of chocolate or in the butter/brown bread combination of a grilled cheese sandwich, but I can't sufficiently mock the Covenant without knowing a bit more about whatever it is that makes them completely laughable. Humiliating Knight this week is only one plan of attack, but I like to have a multi-frontal assault in both word and action." "Well, I'll tell ya what. This place is closing in about..." A glance at the Rolex, "...ten minutes. If you think you've rallied enough Fact Soldiers for your cause, I'll buy you dinner downtown before we get back on a plane." Lindsay thought about it for a minute. "Fine, but I'm picking the place. I'm not letting you drag me to some Vegan Cafe again."
