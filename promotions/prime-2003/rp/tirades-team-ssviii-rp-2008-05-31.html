Tirades (TEAM SSVIII RP)

31 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 31st May PRIME ULTRAVIOLENCE WRIGLEY FIELD, CHICAGO 25 MAY 2008 (FADEIN: JASON NATAS, PRIME’s self-proclaimed ANTI-SUPERSTAR, strides furiously through the curtains, storming into the backstage area. With a demeanour so sour that it would probably scare the living daylights out of a grizzly bear, the mere presence of the big New Yorker is enough to cause all backstage workers and ringcrew members in the vicinity to give him a wide berth.) (SFX: CCCCRRRAAAAACCCCKK!) (A shrill metallic thump fills the air as NATAS sends a small black flightcase toppling to the floor with his boot. The cheering of those in attendance pounds away in the background; obviously BRYAN DAWKINS is still in the ring, milking the crowd’s reaction for all it’s worth following his victory over THE ANTI-SUPERSTAR. Clearly not happy about picking up a loss to a guy who he’d spent most of the months April and May pushing around like a schoolkid, JASON snorts as he is approached by a rather nervy looking PRIME staff monkey.) NATAS: “Outta the way shitforbrains!” (Before the blonde-haired kid has a chance to speak, he finds himself being brushed aside by the NYC native who tries his best to continue stomping on down the corridor.) KID: “Mr. Natas! Please, one moment…” (The PRIME staffer, who is clad in a white UltraViolence 2008 branded t-shirt and has a folded white sheet of paper in one of his hands, races after the 6’4” NATAS. He almost collides with the ANTI-SUPERSTAR who stops in his tracks without warning, flashing the kid a stone-cold “don’t fuck with me” look.) NATAS: “Boy, this shit better be worth my time! I ain’t exactly in the mood to be fuckin’ around.” (JASON leans in towards the KID, who is clearly intimidated by the presence (and reputation for being a bit of a nutcase) of the PRIME grappler. He extends a skinny, trembling hand towards NATAS.) KID: “H-h-here! This is for you…” (NATAS facial features contort with confusion as he takes the sheet of paper from the kid; JASON’s own name has been carefully written on the front in handwriting that might as well be straight from a calligraphy set) NATAS: “The hell is this?” (The staffer shrugs.) KID: “I dunno man, I’m just the messenger.” NATAS: “Alright, get outta here kid.” (With a flick of his wrist, NATAS dismisses the KID, who soon scurries off to join the group of inanimate ringcrew members that JASON had just passed. The ANTI-SUPERSTAR begins to walk down the corridor once again, this time at a slower pace as he carefully unfolds the A4 sheet. He begins to processes the hand-written letter, whilst carefully keeping one eye on where he is going.) Hey-yo! Jason, man, you are one tough sucker to track down! I’ve been trying to get hold of you all week… you been ignoring me or something!? Anyway, I come to you with a golden opportunity… well, it’s more of an obligation than an opportunity, but hey! You’ve probably heard of the TEAM interfed, yeah? Well pretty soon they’re holding a show, and they’re looking at the possibility of a PRIME vs. GCW futures match! I’ve been to Cantrell’s office already, and guess what!?! You’re in! It’s Union Jack, Jimmy Bonafide, my good self, and you up against a bunch of no-mark schmucks from GCW! I will be assuming the role of team captain, naturally! You’d best get yourself down to TEAM headquarters and cut yourself a promo pronto! Counting on you, man, don’t let us down… Signed, - S.B. NATAS: “Awww hell, another fuckin’ match?!” (Clearly not best pleased at the news from SIMPLY BEAUTIFUL, NATAS, without thinking, immediately scrunches the piece of paper into a small, compact ball. Paying no attention whatsoever to where it’s going, he tosses it aimlessly over his shoulder.) NATAS: “Just what I need at the moment…” (FADEOUT: As, under his breath, PRIME’S ANTI-SUPERSTAR curses various incomprehensible obscenities under his breath.) ***** JASON NATAS’ APARTMENT NEY YORK CITY 28t MAY 2008 (SFX: THUMP! THUMP! THUMP!) ”JASON! OPEN UP! C’MON!” (FADEIN: To the sight of JASON NATAS arising from a state of slumber inside the less-than-desirable dive of an apartment that he calls home. He sits upright in his bed, immediately regretting the situation as the Mother of all pounding headaches hits him straight-away, making the forceful thumping of his front door feel like a series of grenades exploding inside his skull. JASON growls under his breath, before gently rubbing his sinuses.) (SFX: THUMP! THUMP! THUMP!) VOICE: “JASON! JASON! Seriously man, open up!” (NATAS instantly recognises the muffled voice of MR. JEWEL as it filters through his apartment.) NATAS: “ALRIGHT ALREADY! Jeesh…” (The incessant pounding ceases; clearly the sound of JASON’S voice has briefly satisfied JEWEL’S enthusiasm for beating the living shit out of his front door. NATAS steps up from his bed and throws the curtains open, before grabbing the nearest pair of jeans on the exposed wooden floor and pulling them on. Not bothering with a shirt or even socks, he shambles out of his tiny bedroom, still half-asleep. He blindly dodges the minefield of discarded beer bottles and other pieces of trash, before eventually coming to the door.) NATAS: “Whaddaya want, Jewel?” JEWEL: “This TEAM thing, have you forgotten about that? We’ve got to get the promo done, NOW!” (Not used to hearing such a harsh tone spewing from the mouth of one of his three agents, NATAS shakes his head before twisting the latch and pulling the door open. The sight of MR. JEWEL always made JASON sick to the very pit of his stomach; put simply, this guy is the definition of the word photogenic. Not a single blemish adorns his picture-perfect face, his blinding white teeth shine like the brightest set of stars in the sky, and his recently-trimmed blonde hairdo is swiped to the side without as much as a single hair out of place. MR. JEWEL’S appearance is a stark contrast to that of JASON NATAS; the ANTI-SUPERSTAR, naked from the waist up, couldn’t look any more different than JEWEL if he tried to. His short but shaggy dirty blonde locks spring from his scalp sporadically, and the fact that he has an eyebrow piercing and a tiny star tattoo under one of his eyes tell you all you need to know about JASON’S choice of lifestyle.) NATAS: “Yeah, and what makes you think you’ve got the right to damn near knock my door of it’s hinges and stupid-‘o-clock in the morning, hmm?” JEWEL: “Oh come on Jason, its gone 11am already! That’s hardly unreasonable, is it?!” (JEWEL steps over the threshold, brushing past his client.) JEWEL: “Besides, this TEAM promo isn’t going to cut itself, is it?” NATAS: “Do I look like I’m in the fuckin’ mood to jabber on about some god damn match that I don’t give a flyin’ fuck for? I ain’t doin’ it, Jewel; I ain’t teamin’ up with the likes of Union Jack and Simply Beautiful, and I sure as hell ain’t doing some dumbass promo…” JEWEL: “Okay, fine, be like that Jase. I take it you don’t want to get paid this month then, eh?” (NATAS suddenly snaps his gaze upwards, flashing JEWEL a scowl. He huffs, exhaling heavily, knowing very well that his agent has him in the palm of his hand. JEWEL, meanwhile, glances at his silver Rolex…) JEWEL: “Best get moving, Jase! The car’s waiting…” (JASON shakes his head, clearly frustrated by the whole situation.) NATAS: “Ahh for God’s sake. Alright, lemme grab somethin’ to wear…” (FADEOUT: To the sight of Jason turning heel on his agent and heading back towards his bedroom.) ***** RANDOM TELEVISION STUDIO NEY YORK CITY 28t MAY 2008 (FADEIN: To a situation deeply unfamiliar to JASON NATAS. The ANTI-SUPERSTAR stands, microphone in hand, in front of a crudely-erected PRIME backdrop. Now fully clad in a pair of tattered blue jeans, some well-worn Dr. Martens, a black leather jacket opened up to reveal and white Agnostic Front band t-shirt beneath – and with a black bandanna tied around his forehead – JASON twitches a little as a TEAM employee focuses the lens of his television camera at him.) CAMERAMAN: “Alright, just a liiiiittttllee bit more… GOT IT! We’re good to go, sir!” (The CAMERAMAN glances over his shoulder and flashes a quick thumbs up to a fat, balding guy, who is also dressed in a TEAM t-shirt. The FAT GUY, who stands a few yards away from MR. JEWEL, suddenly turns his attention towards NATAS.) FAT GUY: “Alright, Jason, let’s go! Taping in 3… 2…” (He silently signals the 1 by holding up his index finger, and indicates for the permanently-scowling PRIME wrestler to begin. Clearly a little uncomfortable, NATAS opens his mouth to speak, but vocal chords refuse to respond. He sighs and shakes his head out of frustration…) FAT GUY: “C’mon! Let’s do this!” (The FAT GUY was starting to get on JASON’S nerves.) NATAS: “Hey, cut me some slack, porky! I ain’t ever done this before; this shit’s for self-obsessed pretty boy bitches, not dudes like me…” FAT GUY: “This is supposed be a damn promo, though! You’ve got to, you know, say stuff! (NATAS sneers, resisting the overwhelming urge to run over the smack the apparent DIRECTOR across his big, fat face.) NATAS: “Want me to start again?” FAT GUY: “We can edit this crap out, just get going, sunshine!” NATAS: “Alright…” (The man from New York clears his throat, still incredibly uneasy at providing a monologue for the rolling cameras. Nonetheless, he begins to regain his focus, before raising the microphone towards his lips…) NATAS: “So it has been decided. May 31st, two of the biggest wrestlin’ promotions in the United States will clash under the TEAM banner in a so-called ‘futures match,’ whatever the heck that is supposed to mean. PRIME, the all-conquering ratings-dominatin’ MACHINE, takes on the bush-league, no-name Global Championship Wrestling… GCW, I saw yer boy Bagwell flappin’ his gums about like he’s a part of somethin’ important, and you know what? I wasn’t impressed. Chris Bagwell, what the fuck have you ever accomplished to go runnin’ your mouth like that, huh? Boy, you ain’t shit. You talk about leadin’ GCW into “infamy,” you talk about bein’ the best… congratulations junior, you sound like every other identikit rent-a-gimmick asshole in this little game we call wrestlin’. ‘Obnoxious arrogant dude’ is so 1996 man; come back when you’ve got somethin’ a little more interesting to say. You honestly think you’ve got what it takes to tango with a guy like me? Last I heard you were gettin’ thrown around that GCW ring by an over-the-hill Scotsman with an injury record that reads longer than little Timmy’s list to Santa Claus! You’ve got nothin’ on me, Bagwell, and you got nothin’ on PRIME; maybe we’ll start takin’ you a lil’ more seriously when you bust out of the little leagues, if you’re even fuckin’ capable of that. Zacharia Taylor? From what I gather, you’re some kinda warped man of the cloth. Bitch, you’re gonna need all the prayers you can muster at SSVIII; because God ain’t gonna save you when Team PRIME descend upon you like a pack of hungry vultures. Brad Jackson… ‘The Machine’… PLEASE! That shit’s almost as tired as Bagwell’s ‘I’M THE BEST! HEAR ME ROAR!’ schtick. Thom LeStrange? Sorry, who in the hell are you anyway!? Shit, if this is the calibre of competitor that GCW goes about employin’, I can safely say that this match is already lookin’ like a forgone conclusion! My advice, boy; stay backstage with your crumpets, fine wines and poncy-ass cheese, you’ll be a lot better off, trust me… … shit, I don’t think I really need to be sayin’ much more than that, because lets face it; when it comes to PRIME and GCW, there’s no contest. It’s kinda like pittin’ a poodle against a pitbull in a dogfight, ya hear? The fans know it, the press knows it; PRIME fuckin’ pisses all over GCW, twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week, and we’re gonna prove it. Because when four of PRIME’s finest step into the ring with a bunch of GCW ‘talent’… well, the result’s gonna be the same every damn time.” (FADEOUT.)
