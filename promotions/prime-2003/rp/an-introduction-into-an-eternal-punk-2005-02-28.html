An Introduction into an Eternal Punk

28 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/02/05 Prelude: “That’s life that’s what all the people say, you’re flying high in April, shot down in May”, those are the words from a Sinatra classic, yet he never mentioned ‘Being face down in a gutter mid-august. That’s what Shane Wethers has to face more than two years ago whilst under the contract of Eternal Wrestling. A powerful Wrestling company that became so bitter with its own Big-named superstar that it threatened to wipe out his existence unless he sold his soul to the devil. That Devil was of course ‘Jax’ or ‘Joshua Xian’ as he was also known. Jax created a company in which he know the whereabouts of every wrestler who had the chip implanted under there skin, he was to call this company ‘Globe Tech’. Many wrestlers gave in to the demand that is apart from an alliance that included Shane Wethers. Of course the Wetherman told them where to stick it, but Jax was never going to let this lie. He would not be happy until he could officially own EW’s very own marketing tool, this wasn’t going to be an easy task. Today we follow the fateful day in which Shane gained the trademark name ‘The Eternal Punk’. (It’s a cold damp night in California, mid-august. Car engines can be heard nearby along with the faint sound of tyres moving along the damp tarmac of the road. Shane Wethers’ shadow stands tall over him as he takes a turning down a small narrow road. Shane holds a red bulky training bag in his right hand as he continues down the pathway wearing his blue baggy jeans and his top half is cover by a big black coat. His headphones can be seen bearing out from just under his hood and ‘Ashes’ by Embrace can be heard from his earphones. Suddenly bright lights shine from behind Shane; those lights seem to be coming from a car in the distance. The distant view of the car seems to grow as it approaches close to Shane. It now can be seen as a shiny black Mercedes bends compressor with blacked out windows; “We can see you…but you can’t see us”. Little to Shane’s knowledge, the car stops and now stands just a couple of metres away from him. The door opens and a tall dark figure dressed in black seems to step out of the car and walk to Shane without hesitation. The figure is huge and the shadow dwarves Shane. “This isn’t a man… it’s a monster!”. As Shane and the figure leaves our view we are left with the shadow of each of them, almost like David and Goliath along the red brick wall. The larger shadow can be seen raising its arms in the air, holding onto a rather large item above his head that looks like lead piping. He brings it down on the back of Shane’s head with a large thud, Shane’s shadows drops until it is no longer visible and the large shadow remains. The creaking sound of a car door being opened can be heard followed by shallow footsteps moving along the wet pavement. Another shadow appears on the wall, but this time much smaller.) “What the…?” say’s the mysterious voice. “I said I wanted you to knock him unconscious, not kill him!” (The voice sighs as the camera’s pan out to reveal the culprits. Joshua Xian stands wearing a navy blue suit and white shirt. Next to him stands something that can only be described as a Bigfoot. Joshua replies after a deep heartless sigh.) Xian: Inject him with the anaesthetic, it’ll keep him asleep long enough to fit him with the chip and then we can just dump his body where we found it” (Xian leans forward and looks at Shane in his dark, weary face. Xian’s face shines with a large Cheshire cat grin. ) Xian: Sleep tight Mr Wethers, for when you wake up you will be official property of Eternal Wrestling. You tried to refuse the chip, but you are going to have it even if I have to kill you. Wethermania will soon belong to Eternal Wrestling and Globe-tech! (Xian’s large hench-monster pulls an unconscious Shane to his feet and carries him to the car. Opening the boot, he throws him in the boot of the car and follows Xian into the back of the car. Shane feels his body float into the unknown as his body grows colder and his skin turns pale. He manages to open his eyes to find himself walking down a long dark tunnel with a bright shining light at the end of it. Shane looks around him with confusion and starts walking towards the tunnel. Familiar voices from Shane’s passed can be heard. Voices from his parents, his siblings echo along this long tunnel.) “Shane! Are you still in your room?”“C’mon Shane… are you coming to the wreck?” (Shane acknowledges this, trying to find out where the voices are coming from. He continues to look around in bewilderment and then begins to walk fast towards the bright light at the end of the tunnel. Shane keeps running, covering his ears from the voices. He continues to run before tripping and falling flat on his face. He groans and looks up from the floor to find he is now in a plain white room staring directly at this bright light he has seen moments ago. Shane gets frustrated with the confusion.) Shane: Damnit! Where am I? (A dark plutonic voice comes from the light)Voice: There shall be no cursing in these gates or you shall find yourself banished from the gates. (Shane frowns, shaking his head and shrugging.) Shane: Where am I and who are you? (Suddenly a face steps forward. A long haired fellow with a gentle smile walks forward wearing a long white cloak and holding a large mass-walking stick. The glow from the light surrounds him.)Voice: I am a disciple. (Shane looks at him confused still) Shane: A disciple? (The disciple nods)Voice: Shane, when you were hit on the head moments are go you passed to the other side. Shane: You mean dead? (The disciple nods again. Shane begins to panic) Shane: But that can’t be.. (The Disciple interrupts) The Disciple: Shane… I’m a Disciple, a messenger of gods. I’m here an offer that may reward you with Eternal Sunshine. (Shane smirks) Shane: You mean like the movie with Jim Carrey? (This time the Disciple looks confused as he shrugs) The Disciple: I can’t recall ever seeing that my son. But as I was saying, we would really like you to be an Eternal one, an apprentice of god, so-to-speak. By doing this we will reward you with life, as long as you play by the rules and make sure you’re assignments are complete. (Shane still looks on in disbelief. He picks himself up off the floor and brushes himself down.) Shane: So what would these assignments involve? (The face of the Disciple looks sad and full of sorrow) The Disciple: When god created this world he saw beauty, he saw happiness and more than anything he saw life. But as of the present times the world has become evil. Its full of deceit, murder, hatred and sin. We need you to become part of the Eternal ones, a recruited selection of apprentices that will be gifted with power of Telekinesis and the ability to get in the mind of those who have killed others in cold blood. We will assign you with a mission to complete. You are there to make sure these people understand how they will be punished, you must make these people see sense. Get inside there heads and then…. (Shane looks at the disciple, awaiting him to finish) Shane: ..and then what? (The disciple continues and looks down, begin to silence his word.) The Disciple: ..he or she will be passed to Satan himself. (Shane runs his hands along his face and tries to let it all sink in) Shane: Are you sure you want me to do this? I mean I’m not even religious? And I have sinned a million times over! (The Disciple smiles warmly) The Disciple: Shane you have never killed in cold blood. You grew up in a sinful family, but you were always different. The day you were born, God knew you would be an Eternal One. Shane… you will now be known as the Eternal Punk. You will return here every week and collect another assignment. If you have any questions you can ask then. (Shane considers for a moment and then nods his head) Shane: So what now? (The Disciple looks Shane straight in his eyes) The Disciple: Do you accept the offer? (Shane pauses before replying) Shane: Yeah… Hel…er….Heaven yeah! The Disciple: Your assignment will be given to you with in the next few days Take this, (The Disciple hands Shane a silver chunky chain.) The Disciple: I think it’ll be your style. (Shane smiles, Attaching the chain from his belt to the back pocket of his jean.) Shane: Nice! The Disciple: Be safe my son. (All of a sudden Shane falls back, plunging down the tunnel he found himself walking along not so long ago. He plunges at a high speed, plunging into nothing but darkness.) “THUD” (Shane suddenly sits up, finding he is in his bed. He wipes the sweat from his brow. Lexi, Shane’s long-serving main squeeze sits up at the side of him. She has he soft electric blue hair tied back and is wearing a matching silk night-gown.) Lexi: Are you ok baby? (Shane sits silently and nods his head) Lexi:: Let me guess, another flashback? (Shane nods once again) Lexi: What was it this time. (Shane: Some job I did whilst in EW. Something I’ve kept a secret for so long.) Lexi: C’mon baby, you need to keep you mind on your match against Kano at Wetherlution (A big smile develops upon Shane’s face) Shane: oh don’t you worry yourself about that. I’m prepared for Wether-lution and I’m prepared for Kano. Its once again time for the Eternal Punk to make headlines, this time Jon Kano is gonna have bigger problems ahead of him that just his lovers-tiff with his little boyfriend. The Eternal Punk is no Samaritan and will have no mercy for him at Wetherlution. This is my path and I’m gonna make it and walk it. This Wednesday is gonna be no different. (The scene fades)
