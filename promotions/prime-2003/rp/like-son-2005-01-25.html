Like Son!!!

25 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/25/05 Hoyt’s only friend in the world is his child hood buddy Aaron. Aaron is a normal guy with a normal job and certainly doesn’t buy into Hoyt’s speeches or the RISP but regardless they stick together. Today Aaron is visiting Hoyt at his home in Chicago. The two best friends sit on the couch and chat as friends often do. Aaron: I tried to watch wrestling the other day but that crap is so fake I don’t know how you can do that stuff. Hoyt: You think Jesus wanted to be a carpenter. Aaron: Hell yes that was a union job and paid well. Plus it was real. Hoyt: So now your saying wrestling is fake. Aaron: Lets not go down this road again messiah. While I was watching the little soap opera I noticed you had a crush on some hot ass babe. Your personal Jesus is a bit startled by the accusation as he gives Aaron a stern look. Hoyt: Nothing but sinners and scum in that federation. Females breed of Satan himself. Aaron: Yea but this chick is breed of big ass titties and a beautiful smile. Sinner or not she’s a fine piece of ass. Hoyt: I WILL NOT have you talk that way in my house I’m a man of God. I am a God. I am the second coming. Aaron: Then do like Jesus and turn some bread into fish or better yet order up some Domino’s I’m hungry. Hoyt: I DON’T have a crush on Tessa I just think she’s a good girl at heart. Aaron: Ok, ok, what ever I’m not here for the free soap opera I’m here for the free pizza. Are you going to call them or what. Hoyt: I don’t put that poison in my body but I will order you up a pizza. Aaron follows Hoyt up to his bedroom to grab his wallet and get some pies. Laying on Hoyt’s bed are three different wrestling outfits. From Speedo’s to a body suit all monogrammed of coarse. Aaron: Looks like somebody is picking out his outfit for a little roll in the hey with a certain lady. Hoyt for the first time ever is left speechless. Hoyt: I’m ahh going on a missionary trip and wanted to have a few jogging outfits to stay in shape while in the jungles. Aaron: What did you say you want Tessa in the missionary position? You dawg you. Hoyt: That is not funny. Don’t make me use a miracle on you and give you class. Aaron: Why don’t you use a miracle to get this fucking pizza ordered already. The son of God cringles at the use of the nasty language he his mechanic friend uses so freely. Hoyt: Watch your root’en toot’en mouth tough guy. Aaron: Alright Ned Flanders just get the pizza ordered. The Miracle Man orders up a pizza for his friend who is probably going to hell anyways. Hoyt: This is a big match in my career. I’m already God’s champion and now I have the opportunity to turn heads by taking the TAG TEAM Titles and winning a match against the hands on favorites. This could be a wonderful opportunity to advance my career. Aaron: It’s all scripted just talk to the booker. So how are you going to turn her head. Hoyt: Well I got this new cologne from Jesus…..WILL YOU STOP I’m not trying to turn any women’s head. Aaron: So your gay? Hoyt: THAT IS AN EVEN LARGER SIN!! Do not joke like that. Aaron: So wait a minute go back a second. Jesus wore cologne? Hoyt: Oh yea he may have been a hippie and a coward but the man could dress and always knew what the ladies liked. Aaron: Your saying Jesus was a ladies man? Hoyt: Thee original! His robes where the top fashion of the day. Jesus was like the puff daddy of the biblical area. You know what FUBU stands for? Aaron: For US By Us its some black thing. Your personal Jesus waves his hand in disgust. Hoyt: NO!!! They stole that from us white people I told you the blacks were sinners. FUBU means Fashionable Unitarian Blessed Uniform it was a company started by Jesus to have hip biblical robes. He even had his own line of cologne called SAVIOR. Aaron: Are you on acid? Hoyt: I’m high on Jesus. Aaron: You been sniffing his cologne? Hoyt: Mock me all you want but its all the gospel truth. Aaron: What ever. Hoyt: As I was saying this tag match is too important on a title front to be worrying about anything but winning. Plus with my partner I can’t be stopped. Aaron: I thought you weren’t talking to your dad. Hoyt: Sinner-friend that is not my dad that’s some old dirty boozer guy. I found him after contacting the A-team. Aaron: Four guys in a black van with a red stripe down the side? Your personal Jesus looks puzzled. Hoyt: Yea how did you know? I met this old guy who stunk of cigars he was in disguise as a homeless man. Then some crazy guy in a worn out bomber jacket singled me into a van driven by a nasty black guy with a Mohawk and some pretty boy yuppie guy. I told them I needed a tag partner and they referenced the ODB. They tell me he’s the best. Aaron: Don’t you see that is your dad. He set that whole thing up its right out of the A-team TV show. Hoyt: I don’t watch anything that doesn’t have Pat Robertson or FOX News in the corner. Aaron: Well I remember your dad loving the A-team and this reeks of Duke. Hoyt: The only thing that reeks is the stinky guy who I’m tagging with. But that doesn’t matter because I’m paying him well and the old dirty boozer works for me. He’s a mercenary. Aaron: Well tell the Old Dirty Boozer I said hi. Hoyt: I’m going to save some souls this Tuesday when I send those women crucified and SAVED!! Aaron: AMEN now lets watch some TV. Hoyt: I TIVO’d all the Benny Hinn shows!!! Aaron: I don’t think so. =w=
