Doing as von Doom said so...

25 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 25th June 2005 Two weeks is a long time in wrestling. Fourteen days is a lot of interviews, promotion and signings. Too many signings compared to training for The Flying Vergomovs. Scotty Killer was a marketing whore. Why were TFV doing commercials for Japanese television? PRIME was headed that direction anytime soon. Why bother? And promoting cream for piles... That’s just not cool. “You’re fired!” Igor and Constantino had been building the strength to fire Scotty for days. Two weeks after employing him, he had wrecked their training schedule. TFV could get nothing done without Scotty interrupting about their next HUGE meeting. Igor had embarrassed himself on Playboy TV for some movie thing and they had done a commercial for pile cream. PILE CREAM! The Flying Vergomovs were fed up. Ellis Easton was making a success of himself through the i-4competition. Dave Gibson and Zameer are now the #1 contenders for the tag team titles, for TFV’s titles. Gibson and Zameer are singles wrestlers thrown together. They don’t deserve any tag team shot. Okay, so TFV lost the actual match, but a mute on the team? That’s almost as bad as the WWE employing Zach Gowen. Political correctness has gone too far. Scotty looked up from the front seat of his car. The Flying Vergomovs were going to make it painless. No point inviting him into the house to force him to leave straight away. They weren’t friends. Scotty had no other reason to be in the house, so why cause him the humiliation. Mr. Killer looked at Igor, dressed in a red t-shirt and dusty blue jeans. He then cast his eye towards Constantino, dressed in a blue t-shirt and navy faded jeans. He looked back at his car and looked at the box of promotional fliers he had made up. “The Flying Vergomovs – Available for birthdays, christenings and bar mitzvahs.” A smile grew across Igor’s face as he realised the curveball he had just dodged. “Okay,” sighed Scotty. The promotional work for The Flying Vergomovs had meant more to him than just a job. If he had of played his cards right, he could have been back on television. Somewhere he never stayed long enough. Niggling knee injuries and his inability to communicate on an acceptable level with the owner of federations forced “The Predator” to cut his career short and he missed the spotlight. He was treating The Flying Vergomovs as an extension of himself, an extension that would fulfil his dream of being better known than Coca-Cola. The former NTICWer turned the key in his car and pulled out of the Flying Vergomovs house. It was lucky that PRIME was California for Revolution 68. The Flying Vergomovs got to go home for a couple of days. Igor waved goodbye. Constantino waved goodbye. Scotty did not. Scotty turned through the gates and was gone from The Flying Vergomov’s lives, forever. “Lunch?” questioned Igor, as he turned to his tag partner and strolled back into the house. Igor stopped at the door and admired the immaculate garden they had been welcomed home by. He then looked at Zergolov. Constantino continued to “watch” Scotty drive off. His hands were in his pockets and his shoulder hunched forward. Something was on his mind. “We had to fire him…” reassured Igor from under the doorway. He leaned back and propped himself on the side of the door. Constantino looked over his shoulders and nodded at Igor. “I know, it’s just… We earned so much more doing all the signing and commercials and things.” Igor nodded. Scotty was good for business. He had doubled their incomings over the past two weeks with all the work he had them doing, but he had cut their training time down to a third of what it was. The Flying Vergomovs liked the money but, as Igor reasoned it; “When we become the PRIME Tag Team Champions, we will earn money than we currently do. We need to train harder to become PRIME Champions seeing as these new teams have joined. Therefore, we need to cut down our promotion work.” The theory made sense to Constantino despite the headache he was suffering when Igor had reasoned it for him. 810 splashes from the top of PRIME’s entrance set can leave headaches. Zergolov had been suffering after that spot and hadn’t even realised the match that had been made for Revolution. “16 man tag… Are you sure?” Zergolov had questioned Igor as the pair made their way from Revolution 67 in Buffalo and towards the airport. Igor stood in the doorway and looked down the path to Zergolov. The money that Scotty brought in was a help, but recently The Flying Vergomovs were more interested in titles than money. It was as though they were beginning to feel a passion for the business rather than being the spot-monkeys many had stereotyped them as. “What about Jason?” asked Zergolov as he plodded back to the house. “He is coming round in about an hour. We will fire him then…” No one was safe. Jason was being let go as well. With James von Doom now agreeing to help TFV out, it made sense. Von Doom could train TFV up as well as being an accomplished wrestler to have watching your back during matches. Zergolov nodded and brushed past Igor. Constantino just didn’t have the stomach to fire people. He didn’t have it in him to be cruel. It wasn’t his thing. Despite only knowing the two wrestling “veterans” for two weeks, Zergolov still felt bad for letting them go. Igor sighed as Constantino brushed past. He didn’t much enjoy it either but it was necessary. James von Doom will set us up perfectly. He has won titles in UWF. He has won gold. Von Doom knows how to steer us to the gold in PRIME. With an experienced wrestler behind us, The Flying Vergomovs will be the Champions of all. The same thoughts floated in the minds of both Vergomovs. Igor stood still looking out, still admiring his immaculate garden and then considering James von Doom and his plans for the tag team. Constantino strolled into the house and then walked into the all-white living room. He kicked off his shoes, feeling the fur rug on his black sock covered feet, before dropping onto the leather sofa. He stared at the tall flowers, lying If von Doom can lead the Killer Pirates t holding the tag team titles for a group of nine federations, then von Doom can help us win one federation’s titles… Right? It’s really amazing how best friends can think alike. Zergolov continued to lounge on the sofa as Igor turned from the front door and pulled it shut. Both men still figured their thoughts on the Killer Pirate of the Outer Caribbean, the thorn in the side of The Flying Vergomovs’ plan to secure their new manager. Two teams can’t have the same manager. We need to get him away from them. Igor strolled into the living room after originally heading for the kitchen. “Is von Doom going to be with us this week?” questioned Arefiev and he strolled in and sat opposite Zergolov. Zergolov’s concentration on the vase is broken and he turns to look back at Arefiev. “I don’t know… He never mentioned it. I would assume so.” The two men sit in silence. Arefiev is perched on the end of the armchair that he sat down on to question Zergolov. Constantino, as before is lying back into the plush leather sofa that The Flying Vergomovs own. Is he going to be with us this week? Is he coming to California? Someone should ring him… “You should ring him…” The two men voice their thoughts at the exact same time. Arefiev smiles at the “coincidence” and then nods, getting up from the seat and heads for the phone table situated in the foyer beside the door. With the advantage of the studio plan house, Igor spots the lack of phone in the holder and stops only a step out of the living room. “Where is the phone?” asks Arefiev, racking his brain to remember the last time he used the land line in their home. Zergolov, who had returned to apparently staring at the flowers, looks up, his hair being messed in the padded sofa. “I think I had it this morning. When Jess rang, but that may have been my cell…” Arefiev still busies his mind with trying to remember the last conversation h had on the phone and where he left the phone. Igor surveys the living room that he stands 30 centimetres outside of. No phone. Igor turns on his heel and heads towards the kitchen area. Constantino decides to sit up in his seat. “We have to tag with another three teams this week!” “Yeah!” calls Igor as he turns the corner to enter the kitchen. “But what about the fact that we have to face the same teams if we want to win the PRIME Tag Team Titles?” There is no reply, but then Zergolov spots Arefiev returning from the kitchen with the house phone in hand. Arefiev steps into the living room and heads for the same chair that he just left. “I think that’s the point. They want to see if we can unite towards the same goal of winning, or if we will just self-destruct.” Zergolov shrugs. Seems like a pretty good idea to him. Three new teams have just entered the already high-quality tag team division. Seems fair to throw them all together and see what happens. Oh shit. He’s thinking like a booker. Arefiev reaches into his jeans pocket and pulls out a cell phone. Igor flips it open to find James von Doom’s number when the phone springs to life with a really irritating ringing tone. Zergolov casts his eye at the phone as Igor answers. “Hello…” He has switched his tone to sound receptive and nice. A little silence incorporates the room as the caller speaks for quite a length to Igor Arefiev. His eyes widen, looking a little excited. “Really?” “That sounds excellent. Absolutely. Although we will be leaving for a few weeks come Saturday, so can we do it before then?” A little spell of silence. The caller must speaking or else Igor is just refusing to speak. “That’s fine.” Yeah, it was the first option as Igor replies in an upbeat manner, “You can call back anytime. If we aren’t here, just leave a message.” Hang up. Igor sits in the chair with a smile on his face. “Who was that?” enquires Zergolov, as he has watched the smile grow on his tag team partner’s face. Constantino is perched forward on his seat staring with eager eyes at Igor. “Wrong number…” shrugs Igor as he just sits back in the arm chair. He tries to look calm and casual as though it actually was a wrong number. “Seriously?” Immediately, Zergolov doesn’t believe him. “You’re lying…” Arefiev lasts all of two seconds looking “normal” as though it actually was a wrong caller. A beaming smile grows on his face and he sits back up with the same enthusiasm as his best friend. “It was actually a television company.” Igor’s voice is full of excitement. “They want us to take part in Cribs!” Ad with that Zergolov is in the air with fists clenched. “HOLY SHIT!” But then, in the same breath, he notices that Igor is still seated on the chair wit the same grin. Zergolov begins to question the request to be a part of Cribs. “Are you being serious?” “No! Or maybe I am…” Igor shrugs, his smile is unwavering. A once elated, jumping Zergolov is immediately sitting again. “What?” A million things cascade through the mind of Zergolov. Is he lying? Is he trying to double bluff me? Was it really a wrong number and he continued a conversation with the dead tone sound? What about Cribs? Are we really big enough to be on Cribs? He’s lying. Look at that grin. He knows something… At the same instance, a million thoughts cascade through the mind of Igor Arefiev. Hehe… This is fun. He has no idea if I’m lying or not. How do I bring him down from Cribs to Pro Wrestling Weekly’s Home’s Edition? I can’t tell him… I could fake another call and really play the last one as a wrong number? He is too easy to confuse but even sometimes I can push it to the point when he loses its fun and I think this is that point. Isn’t it? KNOCK! KNOCK! Thank Hoyt that’s the door. Something to end this really un-funny wind-up. Hold on, did I just think Thank Hoyt instead of God? Mother will not be proud. “That’s probably Jason. We have to go fire him!” Igor steps up from his thoughts and heads for the door. Zergolov remains perched in thought on the edge of the chair. I want to be in Cribs! I need find some girls to put in the Jacuzzi so we look like players… Oh my god! I can’t even think the word player… I don’t sound cool at all! Zergolov stands up and walks towards the door, about five steps behind Arefiev. Igor and Constantino lock eyes for a spilt-second. Both of them hate this job. Face-to-face firings are not a pleasant thing to do, even if you have only employed the people for two weeks, especially if you have only employed the person for two weeks. Igor un-latches the door and opens it ajar to the beaming face of Jason Jameson. As the door opens more, Jason steps through in his tracksuit carrying a sports bag. He steps once into the house and then round to be met by unfulfilling expressions from The Flying Vergomovs. “What's up?” Within a second Jason has analysed the expressions of The Flying Vergomovs and also survey ed the fact, that unlike usual the pair are dressed ready for a workout. “We aren’t training today, are we?” asks Jameson, not really waiting for the answer. He drops his head to the floor and the looks up. “You guys are going to ask James von Doom to train you aren’t you?” And then, silence fills the foyer of The Flying Vergomovs. It’s an awkward silence that all three men are aware of. Zergolov can only manage to nod in response to Jason. Igor and Constantino liked Jason. He was unsuccessful on his pursuit of titles. He tried so hard in a couple of feds but always fell at the last hurdle and the same could be said of TFV. PRIME was their third fed, and despite so much apparent promise it had never happened for them. TFV trophy cabinet is still empty. “Its fine,” shrugged Jason, “I was watching Revolution. I saw von Doom turn up with you guys. It’s okay. Honestly. It’s fine.” And Jason is gone with a slam of the door. A small ear falls down the cheek of the former “Bush Tucker Man” as he stands with his back against the front door of The Flying Vergomovs door. The trainer meant more to him. It was his chance to get back into wrestling. He failed to get into the Ideal 4 but he mentioned that to The Flying Vergomovs. Jason wiped his cheek with the back of his left hand and stepped off the porch and walked away from TFV for the last time. “That was…” begins Zergolov as he breaks the silence. The Flying Vergomovs lock eyes and then both look at the slammed door. “Difficult,” finishes Arefiev, as he starts off towards the living room again. “I was going to say ‘That was not the way he deserved to be fired.’ But hey, difficult works…” “The Pro Wrestling Network Weekly Magazine,” interrupts Arefiev, “want us to be apart of their ‘wrestler’s homes’ edition. They will be coming round some time before next Saturday.” Igor is lying back in his armchair and looking towards Constantino. Zergolov starts towards the living room after checking the door was closed properly. [i]Pro. Wrestling. Network. Weekly. [/i] “We are going to be on the front cover of PWN Weekly?” suggests Zergolov, as a rush of eagerness feels his cheeks. “Probably… I suppose so… Maybe…” 'Hold on, noone phone von Doom.'
