The Morning After

3 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 4/3/08 So as it stood, Bryan Dawkins’ stay in PRIME had thus far been a rollercoaster ride. Spanning from his fairly impressive showing in the Dual Halo at Night Two of Culture Shock to his near victory over Xavier Kannon to start his singles career in PRIME, the Flyin’ Hawaiian had experienced a fair amount of success, but not without downfalls. Last week in a four-way scramble involving the likes of Crucifix, Jason Natas, and Union Jack, Dawkins was knocked out of the match early when Natas knocked him from his perch upon the top turnbuckle, leaving him a non-factor in the rest of the match. This week, Dawkins will be paired up with Troy Douglas, who himself had a great showing in the Halo, to face Natas and Simply Beautiful. Some may ask whether Dawkins and Douglas will be able to conjure up enough teamwork to defeat their opponents, but the real question is…will Dawkins even know beforehand that he has his match? Thursday, April 3, 2008 – Oklahoma City, Oklahoma The alarm on the radio sounded loudly, almost too loudly, inside the Oklahoma City hotel room where Bryan Dawkins spent the remainder of his night following ReVolution 155. It couldn’t be 9:30 already, could it? The aches and pains that come and go the morning after shows were expected by wrestlers around the globe, but this morning, everything seemed to be amplified as Bryan attempted to get himself out of bed. However this morning, there weren’t small aches and pains. Bryan’s entire body was aching. “Daaaaaaamn.” Bryan attempted to sit up once again, but the pain was simply too much. “Well, looks like I’ll be stayin’ here all day. Now where’s the remote?” Bryan examined the bed and floor surrounding him, finally spotting the controller against the wall on the other side of his bed. Attempting to retrieve the remote from the sanctity of his bed, Dawkins topples out of the bed, wedging himself between the wall and bed frame. “…great.” KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK “…uh, I can’t get to the door. Who is it?” After a few moments, the knocking repeated. KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK “Yo Bry. If you’re naked or somethin’ in here, I can leave.” “Polunu? How’d you get in here?” Polunu, holding a McDonald’s bag and hotel room key in one hand, and an Egg McMuffin in the other just smiled at Bryan. “Well I tried to get you up at 9, but there was no answer. So I went down to the front desk and asked for an extra key. I guess we look alike or somethin’ , bruh, cuz the receptionist gave me the key, no questions asked.” Bryan, still stuck between the bed and wall, peered up at Polunu. “So is that Egg McMuffin any good?” “Yeah bruh, fresh.” Polunu peered into the bag and at the half-eaten McMuffin in his hand, and grimaced. “Well, as fresh as McDonald’s can get, I guess. Got one for you in here.” Bryan sighed and slumped back down into the crevice between the bed and wall. “…maybe if you got me out of here, I’d be able to have my breakfast, bruh.” Polunu smiled gleefully and sauntered over to Dawkins, picking his limp body up and placing him back on the bed. “OUUUUUUUUCH, man! You gotta watch what you’re doin’ around me today. If you didn’t see it, I wiped out pretty bad last night. It’s the nature of the ‘biz, though, bruh.” In between bites of his McMuffin, Polunu managed to spit a response out. “Right, right. Totally.” “I mean, I’m gonna have nights like this. Things aren’t always gonna work out.” After finishing off the McMuffin, Polunu offered some more advice, before sinking his teeth into another delicious breakfast snadwhich. “Maybe if you paid attention to the card and knew when you’ve been booked, you might not be in this situation. You know, being mentally prepared can go a long way, bruh.” “Thanks for the valuable information, Polunu. I’ll keep that in mind next week.” “Speaking of that, you gonna try to go one on one with that Natas guy? I mean, as some payback for last night. He got ya pretty good, bruh. You can’t deny that.” “I’m not denying anything. I can’t move, bruh. Plus I’m hungry, which doesn’t help. Where’s my breakfast?” Polunu gulped down McMuffin number two just as Bryan questioned about his breakfast, and immediately blushed in embarrassment. Apparently there was too much conversation going on, and he just couldn’t take it. =( That was Dawkins’ expression. He felt like he had been hit by a big metaphorical truck, got stuck between his bed and wall while attempting to get his remote, and then his breakfast was eaten by his best friend. “C’mon bruh, I’ll take you back over to McDonald’s and I’ll get you thirty McMuffins if you really want ‘em.” “No thanks, Polunu. Feel free to go yourself, though. I’m not goin’ anywhere at the moment. It might take a few days to get to Kansas for ReV next week, so I’m gonna need all the rest I can.” “Well, if it helps, try to prepare for a match this week. No need to get blindsided again, y’know?” “Thanks bruh. Now go get my breakfast.”
