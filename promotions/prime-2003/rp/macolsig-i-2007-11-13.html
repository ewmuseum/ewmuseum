MacOlsig I

13 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 13, 2007 If I'm ever murdered, Hin See did it. That's what my father used to tell me when I was younger. My father was Vangelus Olsig. He may not have pulled the trigger, dad would go on to say...he may not have supplied the weapon...he may not have even been present in the country at the time, but somewhere in the midst of it all, Hin See was behind it. A college student at the time, I attributed these prophecies to paranoia and mental disabling, both common traits found in a professional wrestler. But last night... My father was murdered. Throughout the course of the night, I've constantly heard that prophecy replay itself through my mind over and over again. If I'm ever murdered...Hin See did it. With all spirits of mourning having ceased, I've now made it my personal objective to uncover the exact reason my father would view Hin See as a threat. And if he truly was a threat. A journal written by my father in which I'd never taken any interest in priorly would hopefully provide me with those answers I sought. With sorrow in my heart, and expectancy in my mind... I began reading the first page. -- Entry Number One: Exciting News on the Road to Colossus...Sort of. Well halfway through the Road towards Colossus, I had yet to receive a program. I'd began to think that management truly wasn't paying attention to the effort that I was constantly pouring out in the ring. Today was different, however. Today, I received a new ray of hope from my good friend, Joe Blanton. Joe Blanton is an Executive Assistant in PRIME. He works directly with the Hin See's of the company and therefore receives all of the juicy news before anyone else. By hook or crook, I managed to snag him as a close ally. At times, it's a bit troublesome...the guy talks A LOT. But other times, such as today, it can come in handy...sort of. Joe approached me as I entered the building of the Garrett Coliseum in preparation for tonight's house show events. By the gleam in his eyes and the smirk on his face, I could already tell that i was in for a dandy of a news ticker. "Vangelus, I have some great news." He blurted out upon approach. "I have a full year off with pay." I responded sarcastically, resuming my browse down the hallway as he traveled alongside. "Wrong, but close." "I'll take close. What's going on?" "Latest talk in management is that the Intense Championship is in line for a major overhaul. They want to put the baby on par with the Universal Title!" He shouted in as loud a tone as someone attempting to whisper could shout. I looked at him with a sharp glare in my eyes before rebutting. "And this has exactly what to do with me?" "You're being considered as the poster boy for the belt! I hear whoever they decide to let run with it is going to hold on to it for a LONG time and in turn receive a huge boost both on air and in pay. And when I say huge, I mean HUUUUUGE." I stopped in my tracks and simply glanced at him with amazed eyes. "You're lying, Joe." "Look at these shoes, fellow...would I lie to you?" I looked at his shoes. He had to be telling the truth. For the first time in a long time, a smile crossed my face. No, not a smile...a full blown cheese crossed my face. "That's the best news I've heard since joining this place, man!" "I'm sure it is...buuut there's one small problem." "Which is..." "Daniel Pierce is at the top of the list as of now. Nine times out of ten, he'll be the one receiving the boost...but you're number 2, man!" I rolled my eyes and resumed my brisk down the pathway. "Wow...way to tease me there." "There's no tease. Management is noticing you. Baby steps, my man." "Yeah...I guess." "Well, I've got some paperwork to handle. Keep your chin up." I provided him with a fake smile as he exited from my presence. Half of me was truly excited about the news, but my better half felt disappointed at the fact that there was a blockade in the way named Daniel Pierce. Pierce was a good friend of mine. He provided me a ride home one day after my car broke down. Our bond grew to great lengths after that. I couldn't hate the guy, but surely I was envious of him. I decided to express myself in an email to my wife, Karen, later that night. ---- Dear Karen, I received some good and bad news last night. The good news: Management is set to give the Intense Championship a major overhaul by placing it on par with the Universal Title and I'm being considered as one of the holders of the belt which would, in turn, provide me an insane boost in pay and air time. The bad news, however, is that Daniel Pierce, AKA First Blood, is being considered above me. It's a damper, I know, but i truly believe that by some miracle I can at least garner some sort of shot at possessing that opportunity. I am so determined! Karen, I would do anything for this opportunity! Well, anyways, I have to run. Respond if you get a chance. Love, Vangelus ---- I pressed send and jumped into a cozy little nap. Entry Number Two: Less of a Man This evening, upon returning home, I had a heated argument with my wife. Karen's an aggressive woman who longs after the material things in life. Therefore, telling her that I was in life for a huge boost in pay made her more determined than even I to see it come to pass. Her determination ultimately turned into aggression, and she took her aggression out on me. The conversation went something along the lines of this: "Vangelus...Honey! The moment we've longed for has finally arrived. Your time...your opportunity to rise above the ashes and proclaim your rightful spot at the top of the mountain is finally amongst us. With as grand an opportunity as this, I may never have to work again. I can quit my job and spend my time managing the elaborate fortunes in which we are about to receive." I sat there at the dinner table with a blank look on my face. Slowly, I pecked away at the green beans thus causing Karen to glance at me with pondering eyes. "It takes two to celebrate, Vangelus." "And there also has to be a reason, Karen. You're hallucinating over pebbles of information. We still have Daniel Pierce to consider. He's the mainstay for the push, you know. I'm only second in line." "And he which is last shall one day become first." I twirled my fork in the air sarcastically. "I forgot to attend Sunday School last week...care to elaborate?" "Daniel Pierce has been experiencing health problems as of late, has he not?" "A few headaches and mild tantrums...nothing to decorate an insurance policy over." "But enough to provoke some drub abuse speculation, eh?" "The answer's no, Karen." She paused for a moment as a look of confusion constructed itself upon her face. She was a grand actor. "Excuse me?" "We are not going to frame Daniel in order to make it seem as if he's part of the steroid accusations going around just so I can reap personal gain. He's a friend of mine, for crying out loud, Karen." She slammed the glass table with authority before pushing herself up from it. "But he's not your wife...I AM! He's not the one that you're BARELY managing to provide for...I AM! What would it profit a man, if you can still call yourself that, to place a feeble friendship above the love and well-being of the woman that he vowed to love until death's call. Was that just a ploy, Vangelus? A routine? You didn't really mean it, did you?" I didn't answer. Instead, I sat there in dead silence. "ANSWER ME!" "Yes, Karen. I meant every vow I made to you. But I don't recall vowing to sell MY soul for OUR personal gain. I don't recall vowing to forsake my religous beliefs just so we can live a life of grandeur." "To HELL with your beliefs!" That's when it happened. Emotion got the best of me, causing me to leap up out of my seat, grab her by the neck, and forcefully push her up against the wall. Rage was ever so present in my eyes as I breathed heavily. "DON'T...do that." "Oh, now I get it. You have just enough balls to grab your wife, but not enough to activate your means in order to provide for her. You're not half the man you claim to be. I'd tear my own child from my womb and eat it whole if it meant providing for you, but you wouldn't so much as place a BOTTLE on a DESK to do the same for me." Regaining my composure, I let go of her before running my hands through my hair in frustration and reclaiming my seat at the dinner table. "What happened to you? Earlier, you were beaming with excitement over this. Were you drunk or something? Have you been raped by some spirit of your manliness? Or is the coward that always lived in your heart finally revealing himself? It's a shame that THIS is what I have to call a husband." "Please, Karen. You truly don't know how much your words sting right about now. I'm doing everything I can as the man of this house to provide for the both of us, and for you to feel as if I am less of a man than that which you married...it hurts." "Oh really? Then GREAT! I hope this hurt breeds courage...the courage to stop crying over how less of a man I think you are and actually acting upon it. Resurrect the animal inside of you that I once knew. Show me the lengths you'll trek in order to save this marriage." Suddenly, she stormed over to the nearby medicine cabinet and snatched a bottle from within before proceeding back over to the table and slamming it right in front of me. "...Show me." I looked at the bottle for a moment, pondering to myself. None of Webster's words could define the feeling and hurt I was feeling at this point. I had to make things right with my wife. It was my husbandly duty. I didn't want to admit it at the time, but her ridicule and hurtful words caused me to give in. I was weak at the heart when it came to Karen. "But if we fail-...." "Failure is not an option. As long as you engage this with your full heart and follow my guidelines with full comprehension, we CAN'T fail." She walked off in a motion that seemed to last forever before pausing at the doorway. "Your plane departs in the morning. Your bags are packed...minus the...well...you know." I knew all too well. Not only the steps to concocting this plan... But also the consequences that could soon follow. Entry Number Three: Dreams of a Terror foreseen. As I sat on the plane headed towards the destination of ReVolution, I could feel Joe approaching me. He had this scent of cologne and beer that followed him wherever he went. "Luck of the draw, my man." He stated upon filling the vacant seat next to me. "Excuse me?" "The push...didn't you hear?" "Can't say I...have." "Daniel Pierce died of an overdose last night." My life flashed before my eyes as I jumped out of my seat thus drawing glares from everyone else on attendance. "WHAT!?!" "...I said Daniel Pierce was caught violating the Drug Policy. You're getting the Intense Title push my man!" It was an illusion. I couldn't speak. My conscious was getting the best of me. I sat there in dead silence...something which had become a common theme for me, it seemed. "I know, I know...you're speechless. I'll leave you alone in order to allow it all to soak in." "...Thanks." As he walked away, sweat began to pour profusely down my face. My hands began to shake nervously. I felt as if I was on the edge of a nervous breakdown. I was regretting what I'd done. -- I closed the journal. Things weren't clear as of yet, but soon the would be. Tomorrow, I'd begin reading again.
