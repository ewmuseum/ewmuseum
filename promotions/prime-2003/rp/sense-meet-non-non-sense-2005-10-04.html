Sense, Meet Non. Non, Sense.

4 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: October 4th, 2005 James Farwell likes to vent. To be honest, he probably enjoys being pissed off all the time, too. But that temper of his has gotten him in trouble on more than one occasion, and today would be no exception. Furious over the last couple of weeks, he decided there would be no better way to go out with a bang than to trash PRIME as hardcore as he knows how. If they fired his ass, fine. It couldn’t get any worse than falling into a seemingly endless loop of jobbing, could it? Well, it could…technically. Somehow he could die and be reincarnated as Chris Storm or someone, but that’s about as bad as it gets. Or so he thought. But in his mind, getting kicked out of PRIME would only open doors in other federations. And if they didn’t fire him, maybe he’d show them that he could at least work the mic. Or something. Anything. In order for it to be perfect, however, he needed to practice before the cameras arrived. And in order to practice uninterrupted, he had to send Clyde on a wild goose chase. Clyde on a wild goose chase, you say? Hilarity. Unfortunately, we’re not following that story. Too bad, huh? You just get to sit there and take the damn Farwell adventure. Take it, bitch. … Sorry. It’s late, and to be honest…I’ve had more than a few. And I’m a mean drunk. I can’t be certain, but I think I beat down an old lady in front of my house before I came in tonight. Or Tchu. … Because he’s a lot like an old lady. Not the incontinent part, but…well, maybe the incontinent part…but I meant he’s about as…ah, screw it. Tchu pees himself. Now where was I? Oh yes, Farwell ranting. Again. It’s almost like I can’t think of anything to do, and just fall back on him being a bitch to make it seem like I have a workrate… Oh, and I should mention that we’ve missed about half of it because of this opening monologue/whatever the hell you can really call it. Now it really seems like I’m just half-assing it, eh? Well, I am. And again, you’re just to take it. Like the bitch you are. Now squeal real hard for daddy. Farwell paused, looking up from the piece of paper held in his hands. Trying to get the right face down, he settled on something between disgust and pity, then continued. “Talking about the tag titles…we don’t want them. Why in the hell would we want to be known as the best of this shit heap? And PRIME is a shit heap, don’t kid yourselves.” He paused once more, scoffing at the thought of people actually booing that statement. “At least when we won the belts in OSW, it meant something. The greatest teams in the world held those belts, and we were the last to have them. Don’t give me any of that bullshit about Gabe and Rampage winning them, either--that shit won’t fly here.” He stopped again, this time skimming back over his writing to make sure everything looked and sounded like it should. It’s called proofreading. Try it out sometime--you might like it. “We were told to make those untalented hacks look good while beating their asses for the RPW straps, so we figured there’d be no better way to give them a bit of a rub than to hand them the belts on a silver platter. Eventually we’d just win them back and all would be as it should, because there’s no way anyone who’s been rejected as a sperm donor could topple a legendary team like The Forsaken, but then something went horribly wrong.” And there he goes, telling himself the losing of the belts was just part of what was meant to be a bigger angle, but in reality died out before it could come to fruition. What a big kidder. Ha, ha, ha. … “RPW blew its load on a few too many Magnum Wolf sexual harassment suits, and went under. Turns out he had a thing for the new fish in the locker room, and just couldn’t help but soap a few too many of them up.” And there’s another pause. However, it proved to be just for effect, as a slight grin formed at the corners of his mouth, before he began talking again. “That may or may not be true; my memory’s a little hazy there. After RPW croaked, Illiterateness~! ran off with our titles, and now Teh Stupid thinks they won them or something. Just like they think they still have the RPW belts.” A roll of the eyes followed a disbelieving chuckle. “But that’s perfectly fine, because both record books show The Forsaken as the final champions of OSW and RPW, and all Roid-Rage and Gilligan have to show for their troubles are the PRIME belts. Wow. You’re the champions of an entire two teams. Congratu-fucking-lations. I’m sorry, but right now you couldn’t pay me to hold those belts; those things are worth about as much as Vangy’s mother. It’s not like the rest of the federation is doing any better, either. You’ve got half the upper card on hiatus because apparently that’s just how they roll, not to mention the rest of the ‘big names’ are so on-and-off each show, it’s literally not even funny. People call the shit we do inconsistent, but that’s nothing compared to having to play Where’s Waldo? with your champions. Honestly, I’m not even upset about being out of the JITC tournament, because getting a win over anyone at this point is about as useful a footjob from Deville. Speaking of which, he’s mysteriously Killeaned off now, so I guess it’s up to the K-Wolf and Aimz to hold down the fort for us up there at the top. Yippee. The main event’s starting to smell more and more like potpourri and freshly baked cookies with each passing day.” Taking a moment to pencil something in (perhaps the last line of that paragraph?), Farwell was oblivious to a movement in the doorway, and just as he finished writing he was shocked into a near heart attack by the blaring of music from an unseen source. Hey! … Hey! Oh. Jesus. No. Mercy Drive’s “Burn In My Light” caused Farwell’s ears to bleed slightly as it rang throughout the vacant locker room, in what was to be the PRIME arena in a few short days. Almost even more unbelievably, a spray of golden showe-…pyro…fell from the ceiling, as two figures appeared in the doorway. Catching an ember in the face, James fell to the floor and rolled around in what could only be thought of as extreme pain, screaming while clutching his head the entire time. “Oh my God…my eyes!” Stepping into the room, the outlines of both men transformed into clarity, and James Farwell finally saw the two for who they really were. Well, when he finally pulled the fiery spark from his eyes. Also, this roleplay has magically been transformed to present tense script. Because that’s how I roll. Farwell: My…my vision…it’s… ???: Perfect? The cocky voice causes James to shudder to himself. Farwell: Hardly. Now I see two Clydes. Two…different Clydes. And how’d you make the music turn on and off at will? And the sparks? Why didn’t you tell me you had main event powers? ???: I’m not Clyde. ???: And neither am I. ???: Quiet, you. ???: Yes, sir... His vision not yet fully back, a confused James Farwell looks to each figure, and slowly but surely they begin to take shape. Now he sees them for who they really are. Farwell: My God, it’s… ???: Yes, it is I, Randy Orton. And this is my underling, cena. Farwell: Don’t you mean John Cena? Orton: No, just cena. Farwell raises an eyebrow, but says nothing. Meanwhile, cena stands off in the corner, not having said more than a few words since arriving. Orton: cena has not yet proven himself worthy of what you humans call ‘capital letters’, whereas I have become the youngest world champion in WWE history, not to mention a…Legend…Killer. I have killed them all, and I’ll kill anyone else who tries to stand in my way. Or at least shit in their bag. Orton and Farwell share a semi-awkward silence for a moment. Orton: Ah hell, I do that once a week for kicks anyway. Come on, let’s go for a ride. cena, carry my bags. cena: But you didn’t bring any bags, Mr. Orton. Orton: Then carry Farwell here out to the car for me. I need to find some diva’s stuff to take a crap in. Farwell: Nova’s locker room is down the hall. Giving a thumbs-up as he heads down the hallway, Orton leaves Farwell and cena alone. Standing across from each other for a few silent moments, cena finally squats down and lifts Farwell up onto his shoulders after hearing the distinct sound of bowels being released in the other room. Struggling to carry him through the doorway, cena manages to keep James comfortably on his shoulders, sort of hobbling from side to side as they near the exit. Farwell: So…how come Randy bosses you around? Aren’t you something of a bigger star than him? cena: Shh! Don’t say that…he might hear you. If Ra-…Mr. Orton even thinks I’m anywhere near being a legend, he’ll kill me. Farwell: What about all the others? I mean, sure, Orton’s living his gimmick, but Mick Foley looks pretty alive to me. cena: Body doubles. Vince uses them so no one catches on. Mick Foley’s really just Roseanne with a plaid vest. Farwell: But what happens when there’s no more legends to kill? cena: Then Mr. Orton will go for the man who made those legends… Farwell: You mean…? cena: Yes. That’s why Vince pushes me down the fans’ throats. As long as there are new legends to sustain Mr. Orton, Vince’s soul goes unleeched. Farwell: And here I thought he’d just gone crazy and decided to push whoever he felt like at any given moment… cena: That too. Between the insanity and soul-suckings…not a good work environment. Orton: ceeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeenaaaaa! cena jumps, nearly dropping Farwell in the process. Farwell: Hey! cena: Y-yes, Mr. Orton, sir? Orton: Why haven’t you taken him to the Orton-Mobile yet? I’ve nearly finished depositing my savings into a checking account, if you know what I mean. cena: I’m afraid I do. Orton: So why aren’t you two ready to go to WWE headquarters? Farwell: Wait, we’re going to Connecticut? Orton laughs to himself, even going so far as to place his hands on his bare cheeks. cena, not wishing to offend his overlord, meekly laughs alongside him. Orton: Bad cena. No laugh for you! And I’m afraid you’re mistaken, James. WWE headquarters is a little further south than what you might expect… Fast-forward to an hour later. Farwell: Hell seems nice this time of year. Amidst the tormented cries of the damned, the trio walks through the fiery pits of suffering, and the running rivers of molten sperm. Or whatever it is now. Maybe I should read a few Deville roleplays to check my facts…nah, I’m pretty sure I hit the nail on the head there. Hot sperm, it is. After a grueling journey (for cena, anyway…who was forced to alternate between carrying Farwell and Orton for most of the way), the three finally arrive at WWE HQ. Orton: Home, sweet home. cena: Gasp…puff…I wish I was dead…wheeze… Orton: That can be arranged, my legendary amigo… Orton laughs evilly to himself, while cena’s eyes go wide. However, this all goes unnoticed by Farwell, who has already rushed into the building. Farwell: Thank the glorious God! We’re here! We’re really, really here! No more PRIME and its shitty booking…no more putting over jobber after worthless jobber…no more Wednesday night Playboy timeslot! Orton: Yes, hail Satan. Farwell: H-hail Satan? Orton: Oh look, there’s Vince’s office! Pointing past Farwell, Orton smiles and starts pushing him towards the door. Meanwhile, cena shakes his head to try and warn James, but it’s too late. They’re already in the room. Orton: Hey, Vince! I brought another one here…and from the sounds of him, he’s a surefire legend! Sitting behind his desk, Vince grins as Trish Stratus and Victoria stand in front of him. McMahon: Ok, you two start kissing each other, and I’ll jerk off as long as it stays hard. The two hang their heads in shame, but oblige. Vince commences the whacking. McMahon: Dammit, this isn’t working! Stone Cold, Rock…you guys start making out, too. Having seen more than enough, Farwell slips out the door and makes a break for it, as Orton’s become too preoccupied with the combined bags of Trish and Victoria (no, not the fun ones, either) and Vince is furiously trying to achieve some kind of quasi-erect orgasm. As he races past cena, Farwell gives an apologetic look, and vows to never again give the poor guy a hard time about being pushed. After all, he’s trying his hardest not to get over. Gotta give him some credit there. And besides, he’ll be played by Jennifer Aniston before the year’s through. Poor bastard. Several long and difficult hours later, Farwell finds himself back at the arena. Coincidentally enough, the cameras are waiting for him, having just been set up, and his rant is still sitting where he left it. The only thing askew is the slight odor coming from Nova’s locker room. But that’s really quite normal when you think about it. … Chris smells. Cameraman: Alright, Farwell. We’re here on our off day, so this had better be as good as you said. Nervously coughing to himself, James quickly thinks whether the alternative of PRIME is actually better than the severe jobbings that are bound to follow, and comes to a conclusion. Farwell: I’d just like to say…PRIME rules. I love it here. That is all. Cameraman: …Son of a bitch.
