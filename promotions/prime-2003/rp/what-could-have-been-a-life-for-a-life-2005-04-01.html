What Could have been... a Life for a Life?

1 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 1, 2005 Monday, June 28, 2021 8:45 AM Pacific Time The music was ringing off the walls in the large master bedroom. The patio doors were open letting the warm summer air blow into the room and the sun was up, illuminating almost the entire space. Beige curtains were blowing around, their corners tap dancing against the wall. The king size bed was in the middle of the room against a wall. In the bed, 2 people were stirring, waking up from the deep slumber that was no doubt caused by the intense party the night before. It was rather unusual for people to wake up this late at the beginning of the work week. But they weren’t typical people, nor did the man of the house have a typical job. The man turned over in bed and gave the woman with him a kiss. She was his wife, made clear by the wedding band he wore, and her diamond with matching band that kicked the sunlight back to the heavens. The man rolled over to his side of the bed, sitting up on the edge. The long brown, wavy hair was falling into the man’s face. It remained there until his hands came up and brushed it all back. He paused, seemingly disgusted with the amount of grey that was trying to peak it's way out from behind the brown armour. Killean Sirrajin, looking and feeling older, rose from his seated position and moved towards the bathroom. It was quite different from the more humble place he had called home years earlier. Down in the kitchen later in the morning, Killean was digging for something in the refrigerator as his Kira was cooking some breakfast on the stove. A young lady made her way into the room to sit at the island table in the middle of the kitchen. She began to dig into the large stack of pancakes on her plate. The girl had a well toned body, long brown hair just like her mother, and baby blue eyes. She was a very attractive young lady, about seventeen years of age, and was getting set to start one last summer after graduating high school, before the coming semester at the University of Los Angeles. “Kira, where the hell is all the cheese?” Killean blurted out, seeming to break the silence of the family. “It was finished last night at the party.” Kira replied as she lifted the remainder of the pancakes off the griddle. Killean had slammed the fridge door as he made his way over to sit by his daughter. “So where are you planning to go today?” Killean was trying to make strides with his daughter in light of recent tension. “I’m just heading down to the beach today. I’m pretty beat after last night.” She cut the pancakes on her plate into pieces and danced them through the syrup, never bothering to look at her father. “Damnit DeeAnna, sometimes I don’t think you really care about working this summer. The university looks at things like that you know.” Killean began to raise his voice. He really cared about his daughter but he had a loud way of showing it. “Killean let it go. She’ll go find a job tomorrow. Isn’t that right sweetie?” Kira tried to calm the nerves in the room. “Whatever…” DeeAnna mumbled as she excused herself from the breakfast table. “You know Killean that’s not really a good way to make amends with her. She is leaving in a couple of months for university. Don’t you care about that?” “Of course I care, but I also care about how she applies herself over the summer months. Don’t wanna see her fuckin’ slackin’ in school either.” Killean spit with a mouthful of omelet that was made with care, specially for him by his wife. Surfaces could be deceiving. Things looked good for the Sirrajin’s. Big house, plenty of money and their only daughter was heading to university in late August. However, things were not as pleasant as they looked. After DeeAnna had been born, Killean and Kira talked about plenty of things. Killean was approached by a strange man in a suit about a business deal that sounded too good to be true. He got involved solely so he could save more money for the well being of his family, but one thing had led to another and Killean had become synonymous with many members of an ugly crowd. Nearly two decades later, he was running a show that he should have never gotten into in the first place. But things had exploded in a moment, and he had quickly found himself in too deep to get out, forced to just go with the flow. After Kira and Killean finished their discussion about their daughter, the phone rang. Kira was making no motion to answer it, knowing full well it was for her husband. Killean made his way over for the cordless in the family room. “Yeah…” Killean started, staying silent for a moment. “Did it get done? What do you mean there were complications? Damnit Tommy I told you, do I have to do everything by myself?!” Killean turned off the phone and slammed it on the counter. Kira was busy washing up a couple dishes before sitting down to eat her meal. Killean took the rest of the omelet into the kitchen area and laid some plastic wrap over top of it. “I need to go, there are problems.” Killean turned to give his wife a kiss on the cheek. “Of course there are.” Kira didn’t appear interested to talk about what happened with Killean and his business. After all, she had everything she had because of the decisions that she and Killean had made together. But sometimes she couldn’t help but hate the type of life they were stuck with now. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ March 28, 2005 1:35 PM Pacific Time “This is how it all started. I was approached maybe three weeks ago. It was a strange guy wearing a suit.” He paused for moment, his eyes darting back and forth across the floor which held his gaze. “No one else knows about this but me. Telling you this is difficult enough Matt, I just want you to know that.” Killean was speaking in a soft tone, trying to hide the emotion that he felt. He had only experienced this revelation a couple weeks ago. One evening he just sat down to think and it all seemed to come rushing to him like a tidal wave, and he was the surfer left riding the wave of emotion. “So you feel that if Kira had been alive, you would be in the situation to make good with this guy and take the job?” Tchu questioned, trying to dig out the reason why Killean felt that his life would have gone this way. “Probably wouldn’t have had a choice.” Killean began, making his voice stronger and gaining the confidence he needed to reveal his thoughts. “No job at the time, a wife to support and a child to raise. It would have been the perfect option. Think about that, no boring desk job and no physical stress. It would have been a dream because I still would have had plenty of time to be home with the things that meant the most.” It made a lot of sense in all those important aspects, but the reality was, it would have been a more dangerous and troublesome lifestyle. “Well it’s sad to think that you feel things would have turned out that way if Kira hadn’t passed, but what really makes you think that it would have become that bad?” Tchu had a serious yet apathetic tone. He was trying to show a little respect to Killean due to the fact that talking about his deceased love was very painful even after all this time. “It always goes that way with that type of lifestyle.” Killean knew deep down inside problems would have arisen. “Look at all the TV shows about it. Look at all the books and stories that you hear in the media. I think it could have been made worse considering I would have been endangering my family…” ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Wednesday, June 30, 2021 5:30 PM Pacific Time The much older Sirrajin was sitting behind a desk in a rather dusty, dark room. Looking out of his own mold, he was wearing a very stylish black suit complete with red dress shirt and a black tie. He had his feet up on the desk which showed off his shiny dress shoes that were no doubt recently polished. Having added a little extra weight really didn’t hurt his overall look either. He was dressed to the nines. “Now Tommy, tell me one more time why the hit wasn’t carried out?” It was apparent now what the Sirrajin family had gotten themselves into judging from Killean’s words alone. “I apologize, Mr. Sirrajin.” The young man began to explain himself to the boss. Tommy was about 25 and had begun to work his way up in the organization. He had only been unreliable for work a couple times before but this was the straw that broke the camels back for him. “I saw him and was advancing when I noticed an older couple walking in the line. I just didn’t want to draw too much attention to…” Sirrajin popped out of his chair and glared with anger over the desk. The stern look on his face was anything but reassuring to Tommy and the kid was legitimately scared when he stopped for a moment to consider the possibilities of the situation. But Killean just smiled a little and straightened his suit coat. “Well I have to say. You have done some good work lately for me. Why should I let this one single act of indiscretion get in the way of our working relationship?” Killean nodded in Tommy’s direction which caused the young man to perk up in his seat a little. He then reached to the inside pocket of his suit which caused Tommy some stress, but Killean produced only a white envelope. “Here, this is your cut for at least taking the job. It wasn’t a job that anyone really wanted anyways.” “Thank you sir!” Tommy reached over to the envelope and grabbed it. Tightly grasping it, he tore the end off and pulled out a rather large stack of bills. He joyfully whisked through the money, seeming to smile more and more as he counted the dollar amount in his head. Killean smiled again and walked to the window behind his desk. He straightened his suit again as he looked out into the street behind the club. The music was beating in the background. “See Tommy, sometimes it’s the little things in life that can pay off.” Killean put the sunglasses over his eyes and reached back into the inside pocket of his suit coat as the young man was paying attention to counting the money in his hands instead of focusing on the boss. “Then again, sometimes it’s not…” Turning around Killean held a handgun in his hand and targeted the forehead of Tommy, who turned to look up just in time to see the barrel of the gun burning a hole in his forehead. The beginning of a scream was the only thing heard before Killean released his anger all over the forehead of Tommy. But the bang was not heard. Instead, a silencer had taken most of the bite out of the inhuman action, but the desired result was reached. Blood running down the shock filled mug of Tommy, Killean just planted three well placed death seeds in his skull. He rocked back in the chair, the blows causing the necessary force to dump him backwards. He hit the floor with a dull thud and lied there motionless with the same look seemingly plastered on his face. “Bill, Davey… get him out of here, you know what to do. I can at least rely on you right?” Killean looked at them with the same stern look he originally gave Tommy. They quickly nodded and began to gather up the body. Killean returned the gun to his suit coat and sat back in the chair. He smiled a little as he lit a cigar and finished off the drink that had been sitting on his desk. As Davey and Bill began to cart the body out the door into the storage room, Vegas walked in and took one look at what was remaining of the promising young man he once thought would go far. “Fucked it up didn’t he?” Vegas shook his head as he sat with his drink and grabbed a cigar to join Sirrajin. He lit the cigar and looked at Killean with a knowing eye. “Vegas, you are like a brother to me, so I don’t even have to ask if you can handle this mess. We can’t risk this guy possibly seeing Tommy coming. It needs to be done tonight.” Killean puffed on the cigar and Vegas nodded with a smirk on his chiseled face. A young waitress walked into the room from the club area and brought Killean another drink as if she was reading his mind. The scantily clad woman then went over and gave Vegas a kiss on the cheek as she was returning to the bar. Her clothes looked a little tattered as Vegas playfully slapped her on her behind as she made a light yelp. She smiled at him anyways and pranced off into the club, obviously taking the actions of the big man as a thank you for some untold events from earlier. Killean shook his head at Vegas and both men raised their glasses up in a mutual show of respect. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Monday, March 28, 2005 1:55 PM Pacific Time “Sure things would have looked good on the outside, but what about the feelings? What about the constant fear of having to live in the shadows under a constant microscope owned by the FBI and such?” Killean was explaining to Tchu why he would have tried to avoid this situation. But even he knew deep down that it’s what things would have come to, as he would have supported his family at any cost. The last thing he would have wanted to do, however, was put his family in danger, something inevitable with such a lifestyle. “That’s a lifestyle that’s not easy to hold. It wouldn’t have made things any easier on you. The only thing it would have benefited would be your finances.” Tchu wanted to make it clear that a choice of such magnitude would only be logically linked with financial gain. Killean knew that too, but finances were what made the world go ‘round. Who wouldn’t take risks to make an extra million dollars? “That would be my only reason for making that kind of choice. The security of my family would have been in jeopardy but the influx of money would eventually sink those thoughts of insecurity.” Killean replied, with a bit of emotion in his voice. It was something he thought about a lot. What really could have happened if Kira was still alive? He thought about it so much, he had manifested an entire alternate reality in his mind. He just had difficulty putting it into words. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Friday, July 2, 2021 3:30 PM Pacific Time Killean Sirrajin had just finished talking to DeeAnna and they both walked into the kitchen area with smiles on their faces. “Well, that’s what I like to see.” Kira smiled herself, observing that her husband and daughter had finally made some peace. “We had a good time today.” Killean started with a somewhat surprising sense of enthusiasm. “We went to the zoo, and then I took her shopping for some new clothes and a few other things. Then we went to see a movie. We really came together today.” “Yeah, admittedly, I love ya, even if you do bother me sometimes… I can get over that.” DeeAnna gave her father a humorous grin, finally sounding happy instead of being standoffish. “Tonight will be fun. Dad and I’ll cook a full dinner and then we’ll all crash and watch a movie.” “Sounds like fun, I’ll get some wine and we can celebrate!” Kira was beginning to walk down the hall when she suddenly stopped and began to backtrack to the kitchen. Not a couple seconds later, 3 men in FBI uniforms stepped into the room with one man wearing a suit. Killean turned to look in Kira’s direction and he noticed the men standing there. “What the hell is this? Get out of my home!” Killean began to yell, walking to the men and trying to push them out of the room. The officers were having none of it. “Mr. Sirrajin, I am Agent Sharpe from the FBI. We have a warrant to search your home.” The man in the suit said. Sirrajin was not backing down however. Kira took her daughter into the family room while her husband dealt with the intruders. Killean was not at all impressed with the arrogance and attitude put forth by the much smaller man. “Search my home? For what exactly?” Killean snatched the warrant from the officer’s hand, reading it carefully. “We have reason to believe some stolen merchandise has ended up here. We are just doing our job.” As the agent was speaking, one of the other men began to search the house. Killean was standing in the kitchen, roughly handing the paper back to the officer when the other agent walked back into the room with a stereo system in a box. It was a bullshit charge, the stereo wasn’t even stolen. It was nothing more than the feds using methods that were just as dirty and underhanded as anything he was involved in. They were pinning some bogus charge on him all in hopes of getting him down to the station where he might spill the beans or they might be able to stumble across something legit that they could tie to him. Killean knew that wouldn’t be happening. “It’s one of the units chief, the serial numbers match” The young officer accused, making Sirrajin swear under his breath. Kira was trying to think of way to prevent her daughter from witnessing what was bound to happen next. But it was too late. “Mr. Sirrajin, you have the right to remain…” The agent started while pulling out handcuffs. He was interrupted by an enraged father and husband however. “Skip the formalities Sharpe. I have heard this shit more fuckin’ times than I can count on my fingers.” Killean put his own arms behind his back as he was cuffed. He was then led out of the kitchen with a screaming daughter not far behind. The water works had started. “Daddy! No, you can’t take him!” She ran towards her father, but Kira held her back. Killean turned to both of them and he held his head down for a moment, knowing that he and DeeAnna had come together earlier in the day. He didn’t want anything to ruin that. “I’ll be back tonight, don’t worry. We’ll still do everything we said we would, I promise.” Killean was almost screaming the words back to his daughter. DeeAnna finally broke down and began to cry hard as her mother held her. He was hoping that she believed him as he was led out the front door, being put on display for the entire neighborhood to see. Later that night, Killean emerged from the downtown station, rubbing his wrists where the handcuffs were sitting. His lawyer had posted bail electronically. Now all that was left was to wait for them to come up with some other ridiculous or phony charge in hopes of finding something worthwhile to pin on him. Killean pulled his cell phone out of his pocket and dialed his home number. He was waiting with anticipation for Kira or DeeAnna to answer the phone, but he was getting steadily impatient with the constant ring not being replaced by a voice. Finally, the machine had kicked in. Killean wondered where they were considering they had their plans later in the night. He left a message… “Hi, I’m out and on my way home. If you are there, wait for me.” And he closed the phone and put it back in his pocket. He straightened the suit he was wearing and awaited the arrival of his ride. Sure enough, only a couple minutes had passed when a blue luxury sedan pulled up. The tinted windows did not show who was inside, but Killean knew it was Vegas. He had called him five minutes earlier and left him a message to pick him up downtown. Killean approached the sedan and opened the passenger door. But he was not expecting someone to suddenly pop out of the door as it opened and smack him in the back of the neck with the butt of a gun. “Come on you fuckin’ oaf. Get in there!” The young man snarled as he picked Sirrajin up from the ground and opened the back door. Three other people were in the back, all of whom were unconscious. The young man threw Killean into the back with them and slammed the door. He then returned to the passenger seat and the car sped off as the door closed. In the middle of a forest, muffled voices were heard talking. Two men were standing watching something with Killean Sirrajin and his family tied up. Killean was kneeling on the ground, gagged and bound at the wrists and ankles. Vegas was unfortunately part of the same fate, kneeling beside Killean. Kira was tied to a tree; DeeAnna was there with her attempting to scream at the top of her lungs. The gag was enough to muffle the voice of the teenager. “Sirrajin…” One of the men hissed as he walked towards Killean. Killean’s eyes lit up in anger at the mere mention of his voice. “What do you have to say for yourself?” The man tugged on the gag Killean had and it came loose from his mouth, in effect breaking the seal of his voice. “You bastards, you will pay for laying a hand on my family.” Killean snarled and spit at the man. He circled behind Sirrajin and kicked him in the back, sending him toppling to the ground. DeeAnna and Kira could be heard in the background trying to scream for the man to stop. “You know why you are here. You were arrested. The head family in Los Angeles didn’t like that too much and decided to act quickly, and that’s why we are here.” The man explained, Killean returning to his knees, trying in desperation to stand tall. But it was hopeless as he was tied up tight. “We don’t know what you said to the feds and quite frankly, we need to protect ourselves and our assets. We know they pulled you in to get something, anything that they could bury all of us with.” The two men then both pulled a gun. They held them respectively to Killean’s and Vegas’ heads. “Fuck you! Let my family go!” Killean was so enraged; he was beginning to break through the ropes, leaving large cuts on his wrists. The men had to act quickly. Kira and DeeAnna both managed to spit out their gags. Simultaneously, two things came from their mouths, sounding like one word. “DADDY!” “KILLEAN!” But the final sound drowned out the screams. BANG! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Monday, March 28, 2005 2:20 PM Pacific Time “Could you honestly bare the thought?” Killean was talking quietly once again; obviously bothered by the thoughts of everything that happens in this alternate reality he created. “Having your wife and daughter tied up because of your actions, because of what you decided would be a ‘better life’. Could you imagine having them tied up to watch you and your family friend die? I could have been killed at anytime in my life, not the time that was right for me. I could have left my family without a father or husband through fault of my own because of the road I traveled.” “Are you coming to a conclusion? Because I am sensing that you are feeling that Kira passing away in the car accident saved your life. Maybe her life for yours?” Tchu was careful in the explanation, but it still came out in a way that made him cringe. “Oh thanks Matt, make me feel better.” Killean half snorted the response. “I could never think that Kira dying in some way saved me. Regardless of what I just told you, the thought never entered my mind.” He was lying, which made him feel emotionally broken down. “I was just saying is all.” Tchu wasn’t back tracking, he was trying to justify his comment. “Anyways, we have some time left. Tell me about K-Wolf.” Karina Wolfenden… hmph! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Friday, April 1, 2005 4:45 PM Pacific Time Killean had collected himself from the harsh workout that he had his body endure earlier in the day. It felt like his body was put through a meat grinder. But that was the way he liked it. It helped him relax and un-wind properly after such an intense workout. The Intense Champion was sitting, staring out into the ocean from one of his favorite places; the beach behind his home. He was sitting on the rock he is always found on, Intense Championship sitting on the rock beside him. He was ready to cut a promo for PRIME. But he didn’t hesitate pointing out that he may be the only one in the Main Event come Sunday who is actually willing to do something like this. “Karina, Karina, Karina…” Killean had a smile on his face, his eyes partly visible through the new red lenses that make up his sunglasses. “Why is it that you tend to stay away from things like this? This promo is not only meant to talk to you or PRIME, it is also meant to address the fans who put out their hard earned money just to see us compete in the career we all love. Imagine the dollars spent to buy a ticket to a Pay Per View. Imagine the people who are turned away because tickets are sold out. Their only option is to spend money anyways to watch on Pay Per View. But the main thing is that they are willing to spend their hard earned money to see you, to see me and to see all of PRIME. I cannot help but think that even though you are being cheered at all the shows and the people chant your name, they are doing it because they want more from the person they idolize. Yes Karina, believe it. You too can be an idol.” Killean shifted on the rock, the last line spoken sounding like a quote from a famous TV show that everyone watches. He picked up the Intense Title and held it in front of him, facing the camera. “See this title? I am the Intense Champion of PRIME, and I am recognized as such by the people who cheer for me. I will give them something to watch and something to enjoy. You do that as well, but in a different way. You do not wish to expose yourself by talking to them. These are the people who make you what you are. Remember, you are nothing without the fans.” Killean put the Intense Title back on the rock beside him. He was smiling a bit because he knew he was doing something he always liked to do. He was showing the fans that he appreciated them. “Three main things make a professional wrestler. Skill is number one, attitude is number two and number three is personality. Sure there are other things that help, but these are the main things.” Killean did not sound like he was giving the younger and inexperienced Karina Wolfenden a lecture, but the tone suggested he was offering some friendly advice. But he also knew that advice was something that Karina did not welcome with open arms either. “Now I am in now way implying that you do not have skill. You have plenty of skill; that is why you are the Universal Champion now. Sure, luck probably had a little part in that outcome, but who doesn’t welcome a little luck? I respect the skill you have Kari, as long as you use it wisely.” Killean had to pause for a moment, because he knew what he was about to say might strike a chord with a few people, mainly his opponent. “Now I will skip to the third part, because I feel in your case, this is the most important. No offense or anything, but your piece of the personality pie is not all that big. You have no personality is seems. You cannot talk to people you like without pausing and being near terrified. You also can’t talk to people you don’t like without being insulting to them or threatening physical harm. That is something that a wrestler needs… a personality. Third, you have a very bad attitude in most cases. You shook off PRIME to go snowboarding instead of choosing your prize from the Dual Halo win. You did not respect PRIME and the fans and you thought it better to chase a personal pastime. I won’t even mention the attitude you portray to the superstars in PRIME because I think I covered that with personality. In order to be respected, you need to respect others as well and I fear that will catch up with you in time.” The Supreme Machine then jumped down off the rock, slinging the Intense Champion over his shoulder. He then had a more serious look on his face. It was the same look he always has on his face when he enters the ring… it was calm and focused. “Enough of the free advice, because I am not sure how much of that you were willing to hear anyways.” Killean smirked a little but continued anyways. “I am now going to say something that you have probably heard a million times Karina, but this time I hope you take it to heart before Sunday. You don’t scare me Kari. You in no way strike fear into my soul the way you think you do. Sure you have these powerful legs that could knock someone’s head off, but I’ll say it now. Kick me all you want, even go ahead and hit my C4 Vertebrae that you are speaking of. If you try to injure me on purpose, nothing will be able to calm the ferocity of my backlash. I will do everything in my power at that point to put you down and make sure you stay there. Now you may say, no one has ever done that. But I am not like other opponents you have faced. Like it or not, you really know nothing about me. I know enough about you to give you the advice I gave earlier. But I also know you well enough to let you know that I am not afraid of the big bad K-Wolf.” Killean walked down the beach, towards his home where he will then pack up his belongings and head to the Pay Per View in Boston. He intended to close out this promo in a mere few seconds. “Let it be known that Sunday will be a night of firsts. It will be the first time anyone will see Karina Wolfenden and Killean Sirrajin wrestle one on one. It will be the first title defense for K-Wolf since beating Boda. It will also be the first time that a PRIME Pay Per View emanates from Boston as only ReVolution has been there. Hell it might also be the first time that some geek sitting in the third row in Boston will get laid because the girl will be dreaming it was me instead. But all kidding aside, it will be the first time that Karina Wolfenden loses in PRIME by pin fall or submission and it will be the first time that PRIME fans will see Killean Sirrajin holding the Universal Championship high along side the Intense Championship. Now Kari, try and stay on your snowboard while attempting to tackle that mountain of promises. I will be the avalanche to finally take the aura that is the K-Wolf down. I don’t make idle threats, I make promises.” Killean Sirrajin fixed his sunglasses and turned from the camera. He was kicking the sand as he made his way back to his house. This would be the final time that Killean Sirrajin was heard from before Sunday Night. His point was pretty clear.
