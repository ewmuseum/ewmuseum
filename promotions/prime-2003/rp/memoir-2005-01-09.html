Memoir

9 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1 - 9 - 05 ((Okay, there was supposed to be more to this but the deadline came up so quickly I wasn't even aware. It comes out well, but just keep in mind that there's more to it..)) --- There's been this theory spreading like a virus around me. ---- Irrationalists have recently been grilling me left and right with propaganda; comprised of rediculous evidence that implicates me as being unfit to be on my own in this big, old world of ours. Cameramen and stealthy hidden reporters have become so conventional now that I'm surprised they haven't set up a queue line on the side of my property in Trenton. Apparently, events that include vicious behind-the-scenes attacks on wrestling officials, a mooning incident to the president while he was doing a press conference, and being commited for being off-the-handle bizarre and outragiously demented, are some of the many reasons that have seemed to fuel these crtics. Did they not know I was spinning like a dradle on psychadelics when all of this crap happened? Tell me this, critic bitches, have you once seen me burn a single person after Ignatius Lisieux? Have I done anything politically and physically incorrect to anyone else with the exception of what I'm forced to do in my job? Haven't I contained my psychosis long enough to hold you needy pirates over for a while? Why don't you point fingers at someone else. I did my time in mental facilities and really feel that it's done wonders, especially with my backstroke. At least I didn't go the homosexual route Manson did. Can't you guys live with me being who I am? Can't I just continue being a creature hidden underneath the loafers of tolerance? Tolerate me, Goddamnit! TOLERATE ME! I suppose that's not going to happen. Afterall, I wasn't conceived to be normal and by the book. I was meant to live life on its ridge. The couple sockets short of a tool set thing? It's all true. I'm an intentional living example of it, and why? Because that's what makes life fun. If we're only supposed to live once, why hold back on the salt and butter? Cram that fatty yellow substance on your potatoes and blanket your steak with salt until it resembles a brick of coke. Live life the way YOU want to because that's how, whoever created this fucking place- that is, it wants us to be. Why bother going to school for seven years of your life to become a penis-headed, obnoxiously polished lawyer or worse, a doctor who works sixty hours a week? I won't do it. I just won't fucking let myself sink that low. I may be an alien to some, and alienated to a lot. I may come off as being strange and confusing. Some may describe me as a work of art, in the worse way possible, and some may not even go the length of even speaking to me. That's fine, it doesn't matter. I don't care about you, I don't care about them, and I certainly don't give a shit about these wannabe human analysts looking over my shoulder and picking at wounds I'd prefer were obliterated from even existing. You can't see through who I was once in life? Fuck you. You can't manage to accept that I've tried and struggled to become a new man? Fuck you. If you can't see that the enemy is within me then you're blind and ignorant. I can only wish decapitation by train on these individuals. They're nothing but passer-bys that shoot for thrill at the people with the biggest targets that DON'T want to be shot at. Then again, it's always nice to be a part of a theory. I feel like Darwin should be on his knees blowing me. Evolution means nothing until you've met me, Ozzy Mortimer. I'm what makes the whole thing different and unique. People have asked what defines the oddities of progression and scientists, experts and writers combined have all answered with one word. 'Ozric.' I'm no longer afflicted by external melevolence tracting its greasey acrimony like venom from a snake. I'm no longer hanging my body on the cross you've all stapled me against. I've lit torches and burned the grit off my hands, grit that resembles and articulates the wars I've been through; wars that don't necessarily include the rugged and sultry terrain or the machine guns being gripped by pale-faced draftees. Neither do they include ear-popping helicopters, or repulsive holes in the ground to defecate in. These wars aren't about miscommunications between two nations or infidels violently running amuck. My wars hardly exist for longer than hours at a time and they have no purpose nor a meaning. They're here entirely to be a method to my madness. I can control them if I focus hard enough. If I push through the pain that covers my body and the ruthless catatonic state that I'm swallowed by, I can, with every ounce of power within me, fight this for not only my well-being, but for my life. How much longer can my mentality hold under all of this pressure? It's no longer about toying around with my defects, it's about preserving my liveihood for as long as possible and taking on these monsters before my system is permenently shut down. Fuck the critics. As far as I'm concerned, they're a nonissue. Afterall.. This is my life and my issues. I've been rediculed for far too long. I've seen these complications run their course on way too many occasions to just sit back again and sigh through clattering teeth. The hours are closing like the collapse of a shadow from closed curtains. There is no one that can help me other than myself and with my cousin, Ziggy, living with me for a while, I don't know how much longer I can hide everything. Then again.. What's a man who hides from things he's slowly resigning to? A civil individual? Well, yes. A problem-solver? I guess that all depends on the kind of problem and how well you're actually solving it. A coward? Oh, touche. Wait, yes, that's the word I was looking for, forgive me. A coward. A small man; emotionally, physically, mentally, sensually, visually, promptly, etc., etc. There's a certain degree of aspersion inside the built of the word 'coward'. There's a lot of men who've been labeled a coward, but very few actually are in the end. The cowards are the ones who always have a bleak demise to their lives. The cowardly souls are the ones that either plummet from fifty-feet of scafolding, or are impaled - at the skull - during an auto accident by piping being carried by a flatbed on a foggy highway. There's nothing but a white blanket of haze in front of you and you're as cautious as possible until that very moment you see two red circles of light. The truck in front of you is coming to a screeching stop and now, you are too. In the event, the truck glides until you slam into it and bam.. Bam. The piping comes loose and two sets of them slide out from the back and come crashing into your windshield, violently thrashing against your body and puncturing your skull with such force until your lifeless hands fall to the floormat and your eyes roll, like two brown marbles, to the back of your head. No, this certainly won't do. I just can't become a coward and face the people I work with; the generous staff that gave me a third opportunity at a semi-charmed life. I'll never be dragged back into another facility, either. I will arrive at that fatefully burning day with gun in my hand and faith in my bloodstream. No, I can't be a coward and that's becoming so clear to me now. If I had taken a little time out of my schedule, like I'm doing now, to sit down with myself and retrace every mental step I've processed, things would've been easier to swallow by now. Granted, I'd still be swimming with the assailant but, at least, I would've had an advantage. It's finally coming to my attention that I'm almost thirty years old. Thirty years old and still fighting for clarity. I can pick up a newspaper and almost bet that I knew someone inside the obituaries; associates I went through for some of my most memorable and worse drug binges, individuals indirectly involved with some of my family members, and worse of all, some of the homeless I used to spend nights with talking to on the streets when I, too, didn't have a home. This whole 'life' thing isn't a joke. We all are going to die when either we or someone else decides it's our time to go. These homeless people barely have a pinch of hope throughout most of their existance and these drug addicts without family? Just the same. They'll die painfully with needle in hand because they have no direction to do otherwise. I had direction. It was there for the taking. I was just either too high or fucking stupid to care enough at the time. Well, I do care. I'm no longer high on hard shit anymore. I can be a good person and carry the torch of a good citizen like my grandfather and father did. My grandfather died while working inside a coal mine and my father blew himself away with a shotgun because of severe depression issues, but he was an incredibly good-spirited man for a long time. He even tried to run for mayor of Trenton on a couple of occasions. I don't have any of that. I don't work in a fucking mine and I can work past the Goddamn depression. Just let me have this one chance! Please? I've cleaned myself up and I'm ready to take the bull by the horns. I'm not dying with crimson regret painted on my face. It's all so vivid to me now. If I don't turn things around for myself, then I won't be here long enough to have another revelation. Irrationalists have cited that I'm irrepairable and dangerous to society. There's bimbo mothers out there that are hiding their children from me because of the possibility that I may harm them because of my disquieting clown makeup and past. There were a couple reasons behind the closure of clown makeup. For one reason, I was thrown back into the Cage (The grittiest asylum located just outside Bethleham, Pennsylvania.) and was forced to keep it off of my skin. Secondly, it did nothing but heighten fear of me. Generally, people in the entertainment business would absolutely get off on people fearing them inside and outside of their employment; it only increases their exposure which always pushes them farther up the ladder in wrestling. This emphatically was something I could've ran with in the long run, but how long can you possibly run until your disgustingly dehydrated and struggling for one last breath of life? I'm just not willingly ready for the whole world to look at me with bete noire, and that, that's how it always flies. You take the route of engrossing yourself within the gimmick you've made, and you're suddenly walking on a tight rope with starved alligators wading in the water, eyeing their lunch. After you cross that line there's always some over-dressed, handlebar-mustache styling ego-head with both of his arms behind his back. He's got a fly swatter hanging out of one of his white pant pockets and with a smile he says: 'So, you've made it past this point. You've been succumbed by some fake monicker and everyone is swallowing you hole with hate because they think you've stabbed them in the back. But, at least you're at the top of the ladder, right?' Then he pulls out of his fly swatter and smacks me in the face with it. 'Not for long, though.' He pushes me off and I plummet into shallow waters within a poke-a-dotted kiddie pool. I can hear the ringmaster above me cackling like Rip Taylor, throwing confetti towards my lifelessly floating body. No matter how small the print may be at the very bottom of the paper, it always guarantees that you'll be paying a price when it's all over. It's not worth it and if I even want to be worthwhile, I have to stay away from this option. I can't sit back and let these irrational motherfuckers get to me like I'm some kind of bait. I may be almost thirty, but at least I'm only thirty. There's people on their deathbeds regretting things to this day; dying without the wish of clarity being fulfilled, well, that's not happening to me. Everything is now clear. The clouds that constantly rest in front of my eyes are being substituted with sunshine. The stars are going to sparkle that much more and the world is going to start applauding my efforts at being a better person. Contrary to what everyone thinks and believes, Ziggy Moonshawt is not a brother but a cousin of mine. He was taped on one of the shows after visiting me the night prior to ReVolution with a bit of unexpected news. Apparently, he was in debt to his eyeballs with drug money. He had backers giving him money to buy narcotics and sell. They came to Ziggy because of his astounding amount of connects in the business, and Ziggy happily obliged with a request of ten percent of the profits made. Jay (For the purposes of keeping him annonymous, which is for the purpose of keeping my bones in tact, we'll call the backer, 'Jay'.) was close friends with Tracey, a high school buddy of Zig's, and that's how everything bloomed. Ziggy fulfilled and satisfied for eleven weeks and then that was it. He was given a final bulk order of heroin and he fled back to his home in Rhode Island to keep it for himself after hearing that the landlord of his apartment complex was evicting him. He figured he'd sell off what he had, jump ship, and hang low until he found a way to pay Jay back. Unfortunately for Ziggy, Jay wasn't a patient man. He beat the living hell out of Tracey until his face looked like a pasta dinner. Three days after the beating, Tracey located Ziggy at a diner and told him the news and that Jay was on the prowl, armed and dangerous, for him. Needless to say, Ziggy was mortified of the situation and quickly left the diner en route to his home. Two days of hopping around went by until Ziggy noticed a flashing newsbreak on one of the televisions inside an appliance shop. Tracey was murdered; throat slit from end to end and there weren't any suspects captured. Ziggy jogged home and locked himself in his bathroom with the napsack of heroin embraced in his arms. He shot up and sat in one of the darkest corners of the room until I got there and carried him out and into my car. Now I'm forced to shelter him until things slow down in his life. I'm forced to because he'll die otherwise. He'll die otherwise because he's a shallow little shit who would spit in Jay's face if he pointed a gun at him. That, unfortunately, would be followed by his point-blank demise. I feel bad that he has to endure his life spiraling out of control because he's the kind of person that doesn't take it lightly. The last falling out was followed by severe anger issues and drugs. What might this one have in store? I wasn't going to find out and neither were his family. I've organized a personal operation that revolves around Ziggy, his drug problems, and his dereliction. To fully establish the sobreity of these issues I've developed a collusion that will undoubtedly open eyes, especially his. Just as Ziggy sets his stuff down on the floor of the kitchen the phone will ring. It will ring once, and then hang up. The ring will then start up again and go approximately three times before I pick it up. On the other line will be an accomplice who's real will be unrevealed. I'll do a Hollywood job of giving off frightened tones and follow that up by slowly handing the phone to Ziggy in utter silence and walk out of the room. Ziggy will get a message that will entirely disturb him and he'll hang it up shortly after my accomplice hangs up. He'll follow me out of the room and explain the exchange between him and the man on the line. Then, somebody knocks on the door and my crimson-red panic alarm goes off. I tell him that we can't open the door and that there isn't an escape. This leaves Ziggy to think on his own with morbid thoughts of an angry dealer shoving a gun into his mouth haunting him in the process. I opened the door to my condominium and let Ziggy enter first. My intentions aren't to hurt Ziggy, but to give him a bone-chilling lesson of what may happen in the event that he is all alone, like he wanted to be, one day. I opened the door and led my friend into the start of an unorthodox teaching. The door closed and the game began.. To be resumed..
