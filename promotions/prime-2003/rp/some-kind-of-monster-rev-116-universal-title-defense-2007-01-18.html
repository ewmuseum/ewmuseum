Some Kind of Monster (ReV 116, Universal Title Defense)

18 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 18 Jan 2007 With a yawn, the Risen Star rolls over, and the shifting of his weight creates swishing noises in the silk sheets and down comforter that cover him like a protective shell of luxurious comfort. Nova cracks an eye, and when he sees that the hour on his alarm clock has one digit instead of two, he yawns satisfactorily and throws back the covers, swinging his legs around to a sitting position on the side of the bed. "Wakey, wakey, eggs…," Nova says as he reaches down next to the bed and lifts up a beautiful one-foot glass bong swirling with blues, whites, and purples, "…and bakey." He rests the bong in his lap and grabs a lighter off the table, holding his mouth over the end as he lights, relishing the pleasant blub-blub-blub-blub sound. While it’s true that smoking like this is kind of lonely, the bong is new and gorgeous and the weed is absolutely off the chain given how close Nova is to British Columbia…so it’s all good. "Hwooooeecchhhh-eechh-ecchh!" Nova retches as a cloud of smoke burst out of his lungs and into the air above his head. That’s obviously not all, so let’s take a quick break. SMOKE BREAK, BOSS MAN. BACK IN FIVE. Right on. Nova sets the bong back on the floor and stands up, blood rushing to his shaven dome. "Whoa." He grabs his smokes off the table and walks over to a coat rack where his bathrobe hangs in wait. Slinging it around himself, he walks over to a set of double doors where sunlight is creeping through the curtains and flings them open. He steps out onto his balcony, yawning widely and stretching one arm out above his head… FLASH! FLASH! CLICK! FLASH! CLICK! CLICK! *GENERAL RUCKUS* …and is immediately assaulted by a host of cameras and people milling around below in his side courtyard. Nova is temporarily stunned, but recovers and lights a cigarette before waving to them. "Good morning, media." "Good afternoon, asshole!" one man holding an expensive camera shouts up at the Risen Star, "you stood us up, Nova! You were supposed to do an interview at the convention center at nine o’clock this morning!" "YEAH!!" "YEAH!!!" "COCK ‘N BAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLSS!!" The rest of the media, and Nova as well, pause for a moment and turn to one man, standing with a notepad and looking extremely embarrassed. "Sorry." Nova takes this unexpected opportunity to interject, looking down at his watch before holding out a hand. "Look, guys, I’m sorry, alright? I slept in, no doubt. Drank too much last night. It happens." He looks at his watch again. "I was in absentia this morning, and it really does bother me. I’m disappointed in myself, and ashamed." One more glance at the watch. "But it isn’t all bad. At least something’s on time." The media look at one another, confused. After a moment the Risen Star elaborates, first taking a drag of his smoke and exhaling through his nose as a devilish grin spreads across his face. "My sprinkler system." Instantly spigots pop out of the ground, spraying water in all directions to blanket the pristine landscape of the courtyard. The reporters are also blanketed, and commence screaming and running in various directions. One cameraman falls to his knees, tears streaming openly down his cheeks as he cradles his ruined camera in his arms like a lover and sobs loudly. "WE’LL RESCHEDULE!" Nova shouts before turning and walking back inside as below, a dozen or more media folk run dripping towards their sedans. ------*~*~*------ The Vega Mansion, Olympia, WA, two days earlier "Isn’t this place great?!" Nova shouts gleefully as he stands, arms outstretched, on the front steps of the mansion purchased just one week prior. In front of him, Rich "The Renegade" Rollins and Angelo Deville step out of the Crimson Angel’s Cadillac De…well, you know which one it is. >=) "Nice digs," Rollins says with an impressed nod, his arms folded across his chest. Deville surveys the front of the property and grins. "I approve." "Son of a fucking bitch!" comes the unmistakable grumblings of the Original Villain as he worms his way out of a neon green Volkswagen Beetle. "What the hell are you doing?" Nova asks, cocking an eyebrow. Snow’s face is red, one vein poking out on his forehead as he thrusts an accusatory finger at Deville and Rollins. "Apparently two is the max limit in the Caddy!" The Deville holds up his arms innocently. "That’s the rule." "You knew this stupid shitbox was the only other car available to rent!" Snow growls. "Alright, alright," Rollins interrupts, "did we come here to drink or sit around crabbing about a car ride that I honestly thought was delightful – I found the conversation in the caddy to be insightful and heartful…" "Agreed," Deville offers. "Fuck you guys," Snow grumbles, seeing himself outnumbered. "Now you’re learning," Deville says with a smirk. "All of you, shut the hell up and bask in the glory of my crib," Nova says proudly, holding out his arms again to illustrate the magnificence of his new abode. "The bar is iNsAnE." That’s about all the motivation the rest of the boys need to get over their harrowing car ride from the airport and head towards the house. ------*~*~*------ Victory. Real victory. Real success, beyond anything I’ve ever experienced, only seen, before. To be the man, the champ, the benchmark…the biggest man in the biggest wrestling federation on the face of the planet… …it’s definitely as fucking sweet as everyone thinks it is. And my rise is my own. Of that I’m confident. "I like what you’ve done with the place," Deville says casually, looking around at the furnishings of the cavernous living room. "You deserve it, man. Seriously." "I appreciate that, Doc," Nova replies from behind the cherrywood bar as he pours gin & tonics for everyone. He slides one over the bar to Snow, and one to Rollins. He grabs his own and holds it up as his stablemates do the same, the glasses clinking together over the top of the bar. "Cheers, boys." They respond in kind, and everyone takes a long swallow from their glasses, the Bombay Sapphire disappearing into their gullets and creating its own party in their stomachs. Tchu. The Kannons. I made my own way, and in the end I proved what I preached, but people fell victim to Fuck You’s whirlwind in the meantime. Was I directly responsible? No. That’s the truth, regardless of anyone’s spin. But was I responsible…indirectly? Yes. Of course, yes. I held up a fucking flamethrower while my friends beat the unholy piss out of Alexander "Kannon." I knew well in advance that Snow and Rollins weren’t who they pretended to be. Of course, Deville will always be Deville. I knew that Xavier Kannon was walking. Into. A death trap. And I agreed to it, and followed through with it, and allowed it to help carry me back to the top after I lost at the Great American Nightmare. I’m not blind…for the most part. I didn’t know about Tchu. I know that I was deliberately led away from the scene as Tchu, my friend, was brutally assaulted at the hands of my comrades. You know what? I’m okay with it. In Detroit, a car door slams as Ariel Martiarra steps out of a Camry dressed in black and walks through the ominous entrance of a cemetery in Detroit. She stops and takes a deep breath before turning down the lane on her way to a grave whose location she’s memorized despite not visiting it yet more than a handful of times. In Olympia, Nova and the Renegade sit on a plush couch next to one another, passing a joint back and forth. "Dude," Nova says before ripping on the joint hard and coughing, "dude…*cough*…dude, you ever think about, about how, like, matter can’t be destroyed, so if someone used time-travel to go back and fuck shit up so that we don’t exist, right? Because the smallest change in anything results in that kind of shit, ya know? Well, what I think is that it must be an alternate reality, because we can’t just be erased, ya know?" "Dude," Rollins says as Nova passes him the joint, "you are blowin’ my mind right now." All of a sudden, they’re both sprayed in the back of the head by a solid, bubbly stream of champagne. Whirling, they spot Snow standing behind the couch, laughing as he holds an empty champagne bottle in front of his crotch. "Shut the fuck up, stoner fucks! Rollins, didn’t I…didn’t I hear you say…say these exact words…about, um, about coming here to DRINK?!" "Something like that…you fucking asshole," Rollins replies, ripping off his booze-soaked bandanna irritably. "Fine, you wanna drink?" "Let’s drink," Nova agrees, "I’ll toast to my ruined couch." "Fuck your couch," Snow replies sensitively, "look at this place." I’m okay with it because I’ve chosen my path. I have to see it out now. I champion this group. I have the fucking initials stitched into my fist-wraps. Ariel approaches her daughter’s gravestone, and tosses roses onto the platform before dropping to her knees, body racked with sobs, and putting her face into her gloved hands. The face she spent so much time tonight dolling up with make-up to go see a stone poking out of the earth. The remains of her baby, her future. In Olympia, Deville, Rollins, Snow, and Nova sit around the living room, a bottle of Woodford bourbon on the glass table. They talk shit to one another, the shit that pisses girlfriends off when they hear it because they think the boys are fighting. Nova pours the last of it into his glass and tosses the bottle overhead. It hits the corner of the doorjamb and shatters instantly. "LOOKS LIKE WE NEED ANOTHER ONE A’ THOSE!!" Whatever happens now, happens. Let come what may. FUCK YOU. Ariel stands up, wiping her eyes. She bites her lip and extends a hand towards the grave, reaching out for someone who will never turn when she hears her name. "Bye, Lulu. I love you. I’ll be…I’ll be back real soon." She wipes her eyes uselessly, and turns away quickly to leave the cemetery. Nova and Snow hang over the bar, arm in arm, laughing as Jason maneuvers more shots of vodka their way. What does it make me, that I’m actually happy? Winning the Universal Title didn’t leave me with some void after how long I struggled to obtain it. It left me fulfilled. Satisfied. My ex-wife has excommunicated me. My daughter is in the ground. And I’m happy. It’s scary, kind of. This is what I wanted. What does that make me? Ariel sits alone on the couch in the living room of the Old Vega House. Her body glows orange in the light of the fire, and a wine glass hangs out of one of her hands. She stares into the flames, the tears on her cheeks illuminated, and lifts the wine glass to her lips. Even with the fire, the house seems cold, empty. In Olympia, all is quiet in the Vega Mansion. Rollins lays on a downstairs couch, one hand still gripping the bong. Deville is asleep in a guest room. Snow is actually laying across the bar, drool running in a solid stream from the edge of his mouth into the sink. And in the master bedroom, Nova is passed out across his bed like a stone. No dreams. What does that make me?
