I was in a bar where people were smoking and I was addicted to 2nd hand smoke I smoked 34 cartons the next day then I started smoking weed in the evening because I was smoking that made me a coke addict the next morning and after lunch I started smoking crack because I was sniffing coke before then I went to bed and took some heroin because Im a mega druggie and way more druggie than Facey who I killed in the face with drugs. I have to have special heroin called coagulate walk the walk schnitzel hippopotamus couch potato fizzle tintinnabulation double-dog dare copious dubious heroin that is special. I want drugs like someone who likes drugs a lot and can not function with no drugs. I go hunt for them outside. I walk out the door like someone walking out of a door and I see my neighbour Dean who is my neighbour. 'FUCK YOU DEAN!' I yell because I want him to be fucked I hate Dean like someone who hates Dean with the hate of someone who hates Dean. I steal $389392953 from Dean to feed my heroin addict. Hah fuck you Dean I think. I phone PRIME. 'I quit!' I yell at them and slam the phone down like someone slamming a phone down only harder. Then I pick the phone back up like someone picking a phone up 'Can I come back?' They let me come back and I call them again. 'I am retiring!' I tell them and hang up. I pick the phone back up. I tell them I am not retiring. 'Im not retiring' I tell them. I ring them. 'I quit' I say 'And Im joining AWC!' I phone AWC to tell them I leave. 'I leave' I tell them. I phone PRIME. 'Oh sorry AWC I thought you was PRIME.' I hang up. I hear Dean outside. 'FUCK YOU DEAN!' I yell. I hate Dean. I phone PRIME. 'FUCK YOU DEAN!' PRIME hang up. I ring PRIME again. 'I am not leaving I am coming back as a tag team.' I hang up. I ring back. 'No I'm not.' 'FUCK YOU DEAN!' they yell. 'FUCK YOU DEAN!' I yell. I hate Dean. And then I quit again. And then I retire. And then I come back. And then I go to make a sandwich. And then I quit making a sandwich. And then I start making the sandwich again. And then I decide I want a Bagel instead. So I quit. Fuck you Dean. I hate Dean.
