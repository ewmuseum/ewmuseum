The loft was perfectly silent, allowing him the much sought-after freedom he so sorely needed from everyday life. Artwork was coming to life at breakneck speed as this morning. The second he awoke Nova suddenly sprang to life with the urge to create whatever floated across his mind. Ever since those remarkably vivid dreams in which he saw himself winning the Universal Title came to fruition, the poor, struggling painter from New York found himself blessed with the power of premonition – the ability to predict the future. The loft itself was engulfed in a raging inferno of lively color as a result of the sudden inspiration he received for his upcoming match with the likes of Team PRIME on PPV. Where was Nova himself, you ask? The former Universal Champion was busy working on yet another in a long line of dazzling masterpieces that were being bought by the likes of his good friend and benefactor, Mr. Deville. A splash of jet black later and soon enough, the beastly portrait of four magnificent individuals standing in the middle of a canvas was finally complete. All arms rose with thousands of angered fans calling for their heads like the days of successful Gladiators in packed arenas. Surrounding the victorious combatants were the dismembered forms of four of PRIME’s fan favorites. Tchu and Killean found themselves sprawled in separate corners, Danny Ferguson’s head trapped underneath the boot of Nova while Troy was beaten at Mr. Silver’s feet with the Universal Title back in said Nova’s grip. "Good shit," he nodded to himself with approval before setting down his paints. He took one last look around at all the pictures around him, depicting some of the trials and tribulations that he’d encountered throughout his storied PRIME tenure. Each one he painted had come to pass circa the time he actually discovered his power of clairvoyance. The time he’d ended the titanic year-long title reign of Vangelus Olsig whilst the pedophiliac smile of the Illustrious Face-Eater was lurking in the shadows. One massive black and white pencil sketch of dual Five-Star Title reigns while hovering over the bodies of Ozric Mortimer, Tyler Lopez, and some other faggot, JTF or something with the other being over PRIME Hall of Famer Hoyt Williams. He shifted his gaze to look at the banner hung over the door which he saw his near-imminent destruction at the hands of Brandon Youngblood following his botched spot that resulted in the Risen Star breaking his neck. Carefully rubbing his neck as he reminisced about that particular black mark in his career, he turned his attention to the floor and smiled. In the very center of the floor, a spectacular canvas of Nova pinning Tchu to the mat to win PRIME’s Universal Championship at King of Kings was displayed for all to see as they walked across his humble abode. Good times. Mendez looked back to his latest painting, nearly losing himself in thought. It was true to himself, he’d fallen on some hard times with the losses of his estranged ex-wife and daughter. But that was all in the past. Without anchors to weigh him down further, he had to pick himself back up and claw his way to the top of the mountain once again. Overkill would be the start of that and people the rose-colored glasses they saw the world with would be crushed to dust beneath his heel. Red. Blue. Yellow. Indigo. Violet. Orange. He was out here in the middle of this oxygen-ebbing atmosphere, miles away from the nearest signs of civilization to prove something to himself. The first step was always the toughest, but not too tough for the Grin to shake off any signs of doubt. Tony took one last gaze to the camera – he couldn’t help that the camera was his best friend – and began his ascent to the top. One feet down, 400 or so more to go. The seconds became minutes and soon thereafter, thoughts began to race. PRIME had been real good to him and he’d been riding a tsunami of momentum that carried him gradually over time. It all started when he was brought in as a run-in buddy for the likes of Paul Cain. He’d done a little bit to impress the brass, but the bookers, the fans, the wrestlers. All of them had Cain pegged to be one of the next breakout stars of PRIME’s new generation, surpassing the likes of Tony Rolo, Boda, Black Angel, and Ignatius Lisieux. He bided his time until Cain took a leave of absence, leaving the Grin able to carve his own niche. His wars will fellow newbie Jonathon Winters became news-worthy as both men fought tooth and nail as both started rising up the card. "One hundred down. Three hundred to go." The Internet/Alias Title was his first taste of PRIME gold and it was his pride and joy. "The Grin" shifted his focus on becoming "The Internet’s Favorite Wrestler." The nickname was debatable, but his intelligence and brilliant technical skill gelled together to surpass the opposition. Eventually, the shadow of Jonathon Winters enshrouded him and robbed him of his precious prize, but he would not go down without a fight. He rattled him at every turn, stealing his title and playing mind games with him. Winters would win the fight, but the war had been far from over. "AHHHHHH!" Violet let out a shriek as Tony tried to avoid the premature fall. A right hand snagged the ladder long enough to save himself the long drop back to the Earth. When the panic subsided, he continued to set his sights upward and resumed his climb. Violet sustained the camera’s gaze on Tony, following him every step of the way. Tony bounced back after the loss, defeating Winters himself for the hot potato 5-Star Championship not too long after Jonathon unseated Chandler Tsonda as champion. The Grin followed the waves further and come time for the Jewel in the Crown tournament that made names like Tchu into legends, he blazed through many of PRIME’s upper echelon. Stewart. Tsonda. Fellow stablemate Snow. Adam. All victims that burned up in the light of Tony’s meteoric rise. "Not… much longer… now…" Each heavy pant as the climb continued to take its toll upon him made him remember the battle. Student versus teacher was the name of the game when he faced off with his very own idol, Angelo Deville, in the finals of that tournament. One of PRIME’s most technical spectacles went damn near thirty minutes before Gamble submitted Angelo Deville to win the coveted Jewel in the Crown tournament. At long last, the Grin was at the top. Pacing himself after the lengthy climb, Tony took a moment to sit on high while he gazed off into the vast land of nothingness below. It was kind of scary, really. He felt like he belonged up here. The perpetual smirk of Tony shined brightly as he prepared himself for what would be his true test of faith. One Hail Mary and a quiet prayer to himself later and gravity demanded that The Grin return to the ground from whence he started. The ground was traveling towards him hurriedly and his mind continued to drift. Things lately hadn’t been so good. His Five-Star Title he retained against all forces was gone. The Jewel in the Crown Title Shot was all for naught when Gamble came close, but couldn’t unseat FU’s fearless leader, Nova. The normally impregnable wall of arrogance and skill that created Fuck You was slowly being chipped away until there was nothing left but him and harsh reality… And the Earth… CRACK! A large cloud of dust formed where the body of the prone Bennet landed. Violet dropped the camera and rushed to his side, making sure that something didn’t go awry. It took a few moments before Tony could find the air to speak, but as he did so, he scurried back to his feet and popped his damaged shoulder back into place. He took his wifebeater and wiped some of the stray blood off his face, showing no signs of a mark or scar, save his permanent one. "Are you all right?" Violet gave him a once-over to see for herself that everything was fine. "Yeah," was all Tony said to her. "You know how I do, Violet." Unstoppable. Indestructible. Invincible. Call it what you will, but The Grin embodied all those traits. Walking away from defeat, only to rise higher than any force around him became his specialty. He would pick up the pieces of himself and make all those that threatened his livelihood and everything he’d worked to achieve. They’d pay with their careers.
