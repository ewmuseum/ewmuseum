Date: Present “Are you proud of yourself?” David took a long chug of his beer and felt the nothingness take over him. He looked over his shoulder and saw his mother standing there with this look that a mother has when they look at their four year old child after they’ve done something wrong. “It’s 5AM in the morning and you’re wasted out of your mind. Not only are you wasted out of your mind, but apparently you don’t have a limit either. You might have paid for this house, David, but I’m not going to let you behave like this.” David snorted. “Not going to let me, mother? Get over yourself,” he mumbled under his breath, or she thought. Her eyes just narrowed and her stance intensified. “What the hell are you doing up so late anyways?” “Whatever I want to do, David. I’m a grown woman and you will not talk to me like that.” David took another chug of his beer, polishing it off in the process. “If you haven’t noticed, Mom, I’m the man of the house around here now. I know you don’t approve of that or much of anything that I do any more, but sober or not, I still don’t give a fuck.” His language shocked her just as much as his attitude towards her. “Who do you think you are, talking to me like that? I’m your mother, David, and you might be drunk off your ass, but I still deserve and command respect from you.” He began to walk towards her and she wondered to herself what he would do in the state he was in. As David reached her, he looked over at her and just shook his head. “Whatever.” Her arm shot out and stopped him. “Excuse me?” David looked at her with contempt in his eyes. “You know that Andrea is asking what’s wrong with you and me? It shows in your face, Mom, how much you can’t stand being around me. How you blame me for everything. It’s not a secret to anyone right now; I wouldn’t be surprised if MacKenzie hasn’t picked up on it yet. So, do me a favor, and don’t try to lecture me about rights and wrongs when you put Dad’s death on my shoulders!” His voice was louder than he had intended, but in this moment, he could care less. She looked around with a somber look. “You want to do this? You want to do this now?” He took a few steps back as his head bounced from side to side. “Yeah, let’s go ahead and do this. You tell me how wrong I am, and I feel like shit even more then I did before. I feel like shit because I apparently ruined your life, killed my father, dragged my family from Buffalo to Los Angeles, hurt MacKenzie in the process, got into a relationship with a girl I really care about, and fucked it all up by knocking up some chick that I had a one night stand with!” His words were full of venom and all of his anger poured out as Rebecca just took it. She laughed, that was the only response she had, and it didn’t make David happy in the least bit. “It’s funny how you think you’re twice the man that your father was, but you’ll never even come close to being like him.” Her words stung and she regretted them the moment they left her mouth. “Fuck you. Don’t you fucking compare me to that man, just don’t!” Rebecca moved closer to David. “Why? Because you can’t help but feel small in his shadow. Your Dad, he’d never stand out on a balcony for hours on end, drinking alcohol, just so he could feel sorry for himself. He was a good father and a good husband, something that I don’t think you could ever be.” His blue eyes locked with her’s and he wanted to hit her. A rage came over him, anger swelled up in his chest, and he held the bottle in his hands and threw it with all of his might against the side of the house. CRASH! Rebecca shuddered from the fear of her son and his might. The anger he had exhibited in just the last few moments was something she had never witnessed before. “Don’t you ever compare me to that shell of a man. I’m already better than him and you’re a fool for holding him on such a pedestal. If you only knew the things that I knew, Mom, you would never even think of him the same way again!” Her mind raced for answers, but there were none there. Rebecca looked into the face of her son and wondered what he knew, and judging from the look in his eyes, he knew something and it had weighed on his conscious for quite some time. “David,” she pleaded with him and when he saw the sadness in his eyes, reality started to close in around him. He shook his head and began to walk away from her, a heaviness coming down upon his heart. David found it difficult to breathe as he walked past the pieces of glass that laid on the wooden deck. “David!” Rebecca yelled again, but he could only look back at her. “Just know that the man you loved, he isn’t the man that you loved. Not the man you fell in love with.” He offered nothing else and walked inside of the house as she stood there, left with so many questions that she felt would never be answered. *** Morning in the Noble house had started off on the right foot this morning. Andrea sat in her office, memories still fresh from the loud yelling she had encountered from her bedroom morning as the sun rose. She had heard the shattering glass against the window, the threats and profanity tossed about, and the cryptic message David had left for his mother who would end up standing out on the dock for another two hours before heading off into the room. Andrea could only hope that no one else in the house had heard it all. Judging from the lack of people running around this morning, it seemed as if she had been the only witness to the events that took place. Her fingers tapped away on her keyboard before a figure stood in the threshold of her door. Andrea’s eyes looked up and landed upon the obviously pregnant Philadelphia who looked a little confused. “Do you know where David is at?” she asked in a very sweet, passionate tone. “He had to do some press junkets for the upcoming ReVolution 200. He probably won’t be back until late tonight,” Andrea informed her in the most professional tone that she had. “Okay, thanks.” Philadelphia began to turn to leave, but she was stopped by Andrea as she cleared her throat. Her brown eyes looked back into the office and saw this very serious face on the younger sister of David, one she hadn’t seen before. “Could you step into the office, Philadelphia? I actually need to talk to you for a few moments.” Philadelphia hesitated for a moment before she stepped into her office. “Go ahead and close the door.” She did as she was told to do and then sat down in a chair across Andrea. “Is there something I can do for you, Andrea?” “Look, I’m not like my other sisters. They’re generally pretty sweet people. I’ve somehow inherited a gene that basically requires me to be pretty straight forward and honest. Some people, they don’t like that about me. But, at the end of the day, all that matters to me is the well being of my family. Am I making sense?” Philadelphia paused. “No, not really.” Andrea sighed. “I don’t trust you, I think that’s what I’m getting at. My gut instinct tells me that you’re not carrying David’s child and I think you’re here because you know he’s a nice guy and either one of two things will happen. He’ll either trust you blindly, like the fool that he can be, or he’ll give you money to just leave. My brother, he’s savvy and smart, but you start messing with his emotions and his heart, he becomes unaware of things.” Philadelphia looked at Andrea and couldn’t believe the words that she was saying. “I… I’m sorry?” “Yeah, that’s the problem I tend to have. I catch people off guard. Used to work wonders when I was a lawyer,” Andrea remarked in a matter of fact tone. Philadelphia’s face was one filled with shock and despair. Andrea seemed to ignore that though. “You’re a fraud, Philadelphia, and I can see it very clear on your face. So, why don’t you tell me what you really want?” Philadelphia began to get out of her chair, but Andrea wasn’t about to just let her go. “Sit.” Philadelphia shook her head. “You might be some big-time lawyer, Andrea, but I’m not a slouch either. 4.3 GPA and was the Valedictorian of my graduating class. Before I got pregnant, I was at the University of Washington and in my final year where I had received all A’s in my class in Pre-Med. For you to insult my intelligence and my purpose for being here is one that I will not stand for.” Andrea opened her mouth to say something, but Philadelphia quickly put an end to that idea. “I came here because David is indeed the father of this child. I’m not here for some payout or to attach myself to his shooting star. I’m here because I think my child deserves to have his father. I don’t know what to do with my life any longer, Andrea, but I think I want to be a good mother. I think this child, she deserves it.” Andrea nodded her head for a moment. “I hope you understand the position I’m in, Philly. I’m David’s business manager, but at the same time, I’m his sister. His very protective, younger sister. David is the only in my family that I believe is my intellectual equal, but I do know he is the only in this family with a heart larger than the sun. When it comes to something like this, I’m going to protect him.” She smiled. A fake one. “So, you can take your 4.3 GPA, because I will raise you with a 4.7 GPA, a degree in Law from Harvard and a two year job for one of the top law firms in the world at their Paris office. I’ve seen it all. I know more than you could ever possibly imagine.” Philadelphia stood up and looked at Andrea in her eyes. “That’s nice and all, but you’re still wrong. Dead wrong. It’s sad though that you can’t be happy for your brother. Just because you have a problem getting a boyfriend of your own, doesn’t mean you have to burden him with your unhappiness.” Andrea was taken aback by that accusation and it showed on her face. “I see the way the people in this family look at him. It’s a look filled with contempt. You hate him for whatever he’s done to you in the past or because he’s far more distant now then you could have ever imagined. I didn’t come here for your approval, only his. He’s given it to me so if you have a problem with me being here, then you’re going to have to deal with it.” She began to walk away from Andrea. “We’re not done, Philadelphia.” Her hair flipped as she looked back at Andrea. “I’m done with this conversation. You can shove the rest of it.” Her words had a large bite to them as she opened the office door and walked out without even looking back. *** “What makes you think that you have a shot at coming off victorious at Colossus VI against Jonathan Rhine? Do you truly believe that you have it in you to pull off the victory that virtually nobody is expecting from you?” The question caught David by surprise as he sat on the wooden stool. His eyes scanned the room around him as there were five other reporters in front of him with their microphones on. “You know,” he began as he felt a dryness in his throat. “Many didn’t expect me to do much in fWo, but Mary-Lynn and I have taken on two legends without backing down inch. When I came to PRIME, the talk was that I had a huge learning curve ahead of me. I think I’ve done alright for myself though. But, you guys are the ones writing the stories so you tell me what you think.” There were a few chuckles from the crowd gathered around him and David flashed them a grin. His body ached from the last of sleep and the abuse of alcohol that was attacking him. “I think the problem everyone is having is that you had a chance to prove that you belonged on the same stage as Jonathan Rhine when you faced off against Jason Natas, Bryan Dawkins, and Hessian. In Hessian, he’s the man who lost to Rhine. If you couldn’t defeat him, what makes you think you could beat Rhine?” David didn’t wait long to respond. “I’m pretty sure I just beat a legend in Jay Phoenix, but you know, let’s keep overlooking that.” His tone matched the feeling he had been receiving from the reporters for the last twenty minutes, one filled with hostility. “Look, if I didn’t think I could step in that ring with Jonathan, then I wouldn’t. The fact that you guys think that I can’t compete with the man says more about you than me.” As David began to get out of his chair, another reporter stopped him. “Have you talked to Rhine as of late? He’s been pretty absent on the most recent ReVolutions, and thought you might be aware as to why that is.” “Yeah, the two of us are really good friends. The best.” His sarcasm rankled the man’s feathers, though the others around him laughed at his expense. “Look, whatever reason Rhine isn’t around doesn’t bother me much. He obviously thinks that he doesn’t have to prepare for our match or maybe he’s upset with Lisa Tyler for putting him in a match with someone that he knows he’s going to win. Either way, I’m going to Boston, and fighting for that title, whether he’s there or not.” Once again, the Rising Star attempted to leave, but he was cut off by another question. “Sorry, one last question, and then we’ll let you go. You’ve been in PRIME for three months now. ReVolution 200, what does it mean to you?” It was a question that made David think for a moment. “I haven’t given it much thought, you know? My schedule, it keeps me rather busy. However, you know, in the short time that I’ve been here it’s been something else. PRIME is one of the top places to work right now and has a place in the history of wrestling. There’s no denying it. For me, ReVolution 200 is another stop on the road to prove who I am. Doing it on 200, in the format that we’re doing it in, and the fact that a list of former PRIME legends will be here, will only make me strive to be that much better.” As they scribbled down their answers, Dave rose from his seat. “Thanks guys,” he said as he walked away and looked around the rest of the room. Over in the corner, he saw Devin Shakur talking to a few people and then saw Jason Snow just a few feet south of him. The next morning, they would be on a plane, and flying to New York City, Madison Square Garden. David darted to the backstage area and picked up a bottle of water before he was joined by the pesky Matt Mills. “So, I was thinking, you and me, we hit some clubs when we get to New York City.” David gave him a look that should’ve given Matt the hint to get away from him, but for whatever reason the PRIME interviewer seemed intent on talking to him. “Okay, Matt,” David began before he unscrewed the top of his water bottle. “Have we ever hung out before?” Matt thought about it for a brief moment. “Nope, don’t think so.” “Then why would you think that we would hang out now?” Matt didn’t have a great answer for that one, so instead, he kept pushing forward with his idea. “Listen, you and me, I could be your wingman, your number one companion. You obviously take the hot girls and I’ll take the slightly less hot girl, and we conquer New York City one girl at a time. You and me. It’s like the stuff written in the movies!” David took a long chug from his water bottle, hoping that it would wipe some of the toxins out of his body. “Look, I know you have this inert inability to actually get girls on your own, but I’m not going to help you out here. You’re going to have to find someone else to help you out on your pussy excursion in New York City.” Matt looked sad by the rejection. “It’s because of Mary-Lynn, right?” David shot him a confused look. “Aren’t you two together?” Instead of answering that, David decided to just ignore him, and when Matt and David rounded the corner they ran into the red-headed bombshell herself. Standing there with a tall male, she took a long look at David and then back at the man. It all played in slow motion for David as he watched Mary-Lynn reach up and kiss the man fully on the lips. “Damn, that’s just cold,” Matt decided to add as he watched with amazement. *** There was no other word to describe Noble’s mood at the moment as he had walked away from Mary-Lynn angry. Fifteen minutes had passed since the encounter where he saw Mary-Lynn laid her lips upon another man and it still bothered him. Yet, he hadn’t stopped moving. As he walked outside and started to walk towards his car, wanting to get out of here as fast as possible, David was surprised to find Mary-Lynn talking to the guy outside of the arena. “David!” Mary-Lynn shouted as he approached them and instead of introducing himself to the mysterious man, he decked him right in the face and then began to walk away with Mary-Lynn following behind him. “David!” she yelled at him again and this time, David stopped and looked at the beautiful woman. “What do you want, Mary-Lynn?! You want to embarrass me again? Get my blood boiling and racing? You did that on purpose and you know it!” he shouted in her face and the two were right on top of one another. She pushed him back and stuck a finger in front of his face. “Don’t you make it out to be more than it actually was, David. You just happened to walk in when I started kissing him. Nothing more, nothing less. Don’t flatter yourself,” she responded with a tone equal to his. David scoffed at the idea. “Please, don’t take me to be a fool, Mary-Lynn! Your eyes met with mine and then you kissed him. You wanted to make me jealous, and guess what, it worked!” She rolled her eyes. “I don’t need to make you jealous, David. Trust me.” David shook his head. “And what’s that supposed to mean?” “It means that apparently you can get any girl that you want! Philadelphia ring a bell? You had me and now I have to wonder how many other girls are out there that I don’t know about! You’re a slut David, plain and simple,” she insulted him and almost instantly regretted it as he moved closer to Mary-Lynn. “What do you want from me, Mary-Lynn? You want an apology?!” She flipped her red hair behind her ears. “That would be a good start considering the message that you left me last night. I couldn’t believe it when I heard it David, it was rude and unbecoming of you! You should be ashamed of yourself!” David looked at her with an incredulous look in his eyes. “Be ashamed of myself? Mary-Lynn, I’ve done nothing but care for you these last few months! I’m sorry that I slept with a girl when we weren’t even together. I’m such a bad person, oh, wait, I barely even knew you at the time! She didn’t mean anything to me at the time!” Mary-Lynn could care less though. “Is that supposed to make me feel better? How about me, huh? Remember that night when you called me back in April and asked me to come over? I slept with you that night. Did that not mean anything to you either?” “You know it meant something to me, Mary-Lynn.” “Then tell me how I’m supposed to know! Tell me what I’m supposed to think when I sit in your living room and see this pregnant girl just sitting there?! Let me tell you how I felt then. I felt used and nothing more than a cheap girl on the arm of a man who could get whoever he wanted. That girl is drop-dead gorgeous and smart to boot, David! I couldn’t help but wonder if I was going to turn into this girl.” Silence finally entered the room and David took a few steps away from her, angry about the entire situation. “You’re not just some girl to me, Mary-Lynn.” “So, is that supposed to be flattering? Am I supposed to come running back into your arms now? Because, the same situation still exists. You made me fall for you, David, and then I got casted aside by her. I thought I was going to have a future with you. I can’t be with you now because you’re about to have a child and how can I get in the middle of that?” David looked into her eyes and wanted for this to just be over. “Please, Mary-Lynn, just trust me.” “I can’t! I can’t trust you, David! You’re about to be a father. You’re obviously going to grow attached to this child and possibly Philadelphia. Why would I put myself in that situation? Even if I remove the fact that you’re a chauvinistic pig, that still doesn’t explain how I fit into your life?” “You know,” David began. “This used to be simple between you and me. Now, it’s not so much anymore.” She just laughed. “It might never have been easy, David. It might have just looked that way.” Her tone was one of finality, the closing of this chapter in life. “I don’t want to lose you, Mary-Lynn,” he pleaded with her, but for the first time ever, he saw coldness in her stare. “Too late,” she replied before the red head walked away and left David there by himself. There was a stunned look on his face as he watched Mary-Lynn disappear into the horizon while the sun set behind him. *** Twenty minutes had passed since David arrived at his house and he remained in the driveway. Today hadn’t gone the way that he had intended, and it was one that he would like to forget much sooner rather than later. He opened the door to his car and stepped out of it before he walked to the front door. His blue eyes looked around, hoping to find someone around, but the house looked virtually empty. He walked up the stairs and into his room where he saw Philadelphia lying on the bed, curled in a ball, and barely even moving. “Philly, everything okay?” She looked over at him and seemed startled to see him standing in her room. “Yeah, I’m just fine. Had a fight with Andrea earlier.” Her voice seemed depressed and full of sadness. David began to walk towards her, but decided to set on the end of the bed and placed his hand on her right leg. “Seems to think that I’m just here to take money from you or ruin your life. Something along those lines.” “Are you serious?” he asked her and she nodded her head. David shot up and began to storm towards the door. “David, they’re not here. They all went out.” He stopped dead in his tracks and looked at her. “Looks like we didn’t have the best of days today.” “What happened with you?” David chuckled. “What didn’t happen? Let’s see, I fought with my Mom, fought with Mary-Lynn because she kissed a guy, and came home to find out my sister’s been a complete dick to you.” “You saw Mary-Lynn kiss a guy?” she questioned him and he nodded his head. “Why’d that upset you? Because you still care for her?” Her legs swung off the bed and she stood up as David just looked at her. “Philadelphia, relax,” he began, but she cut him off before he had the chance to say another word. “Just don’t, don’t tell me to relax, David. I’m over here defending you and talking about how great of a father you’re going to be to Andrea while she talks about how I’m a fraud, and your biggest problem of the day is that you saw your ex-girlfriend make out with some guy? Save me your drama,” she lashed out at him. “I’m carrying your freakin’ baby over here!” He shook his head. “Philly, I didn’t ask for this. You can’t expect me to just drop my entire life in the drop of a hat! I was seeing somebody when you came around, and I’ve shown nothing but love and care for you since you’ve been here.” “Why? Because you think Andrea is right? That I’m just carrying someone else’s child? So, you think that you’ll just hang on to Mary-Lynn for a little bit longer?!” she screamed at him and David felt the anger that had just sat under his heart all day raise up once again. “You know what, screw you too. I didn’t ask for you to just appear here. I’ve done everything possible for you, but for you to come at me like this is ridiculous. I don’t know if that’s my child and you’re over here talking about how I’ll be a great father?! What the hell do you know about me that would make you think that?!” David’s veins were nearly popping out of his neck at this point. She pointed her finger at him before moving closer to him. “Don’t you dare raise your voice at me and don’t give me this pity crap. You’re a good person, David! A person with flaws, but still inherently good. So, of course I think you’re going to be a good father!” David shook his head though as his heart began to beat in his ears. “Based off of what, Philadelphia?! Let me fill you in on something. The two men in my life have been pure dicks. My biological father, he left me with this family when I was young. The man that raised me all of my life, he was a whore, plain and simple.” Philadelphia didn’t understand though. “What the hell are you talking about, David?!” “Right after I graduated from high school, I came home one day to find my dad in bed with another woman. Thinking nothing of it, I told him to end the relationship only to find out that this woman had a child, his child. My father had another family, Philadelphia, and you think that makes me qualified to be a good father?! The people who have raised me have given me no skills to even think that!” The shock on Philadelphia’s face didn’t help matters. “David, you’re better than them, I promise you that.” “Bullshit,” David replied as he turned around and began to walk towards the bedroom door. “David, please,” she pleaded with him. He turned around and shook his head. “No, I’m done with this shit. I’m not about to throw away my entire life on a woman who might not even be the mother of my own child! Have the child, find out if it’s mine, and maybe then I’ll be part of her life.” He wanted to leave, he did, but she wouldn’t let him. Philadelphia’s eyes pleaded with him and he almost broke down, began to tell her it was all over, but he couldn’t bring himself to do it. David wasn’t ready to become a father. David pulled the door open and as he stepped out, he ran smack into his family who stood there, returning from an evening out. Tears flooded from his mother’s eyes as Samantha held onto her. Sarah and Andrea looked at David with pure shock in their eyes. They knew.
