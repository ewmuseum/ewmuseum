Get Sonny a chicken suit

2 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 7/2/08 We’re back at PRIME HQ again, the same site of Tony Rolo’s previous promo two days earlier. Rolo stands there in silence, staring at the camera. He’s wearing a white sleeveless shirt, a pair of jeans, and white Hurley hat with red lettering backwards. He takes a moment before beginning. Rolo: So what the fuck, Silver? It’s been a few days since my last interview and we haven’t heard a fucking thing from you? I figured you normally don’t shut up, so I didn’t think there’d be any reason we wouldn’t hear from you leading up to ReVolution 165. What, got nothing to say? Are you… The Specialist puts his hands under his armpits and starts flapping his arms up and down and making noises. BAAAAWK BAWK BAWK BAWK BAAAAAAAWK Rolo: … chicken? Come on, Silver… I knew you were a coward, but even cowards feel safe hiding behind a camera and spewing off at the mouth. We couldn’t even get that out of you, you fucking pussy. You’re such a fucking pussy that I bet 10 to 1 that you taped an interview that’s gonna air after ReVolution this Wednesday… that’s the kind of chicken shit stuff you’d pull. Too worried I’d tear your every word apart, you cocksucker? He pauses and shakes his head in disbelief. Rolo: And I just don’t get it, Silver. Why? You’ve held the Intense Title… you’ve been the Universal Champion… you’re accomplished in that ring, I’m not trying to take that away from you. Why the kidding like a little scared bitch? I realize what Wednesday will be when we’re standing across from each other in that ring. We’re not talking about a match that could only headline a ReVolution… not just a match that could headline a pay-per-view… this is a match that could be the main event of Colossus. I realize this is huge. But you… you don’t realize shit. Keep hiding, Sonny… cause that’s what cowards do best, even the talented ones. Yeah, we may have a few things in common… having held PRIME’s top title… you’re probably even going to join me in the Hall-of-Fame one day… but for every ounce of you that is a coward and a sneak, I have a pound of pride, courage, and determination. Tony Rolo pauses once more for dramatic effect. Rolo: Wednesday at ReVolution, there is no more hiding, Sonny Silver. I’m gonna show you why I’m old school… and you’re just old!
