Redemption

2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/12/05 :::: What do you do when you've been partners with someone for years yet you feel like you are holding them back? How should a person justify asking their friend to continue to team with them, in pursuit of a dream that has been realised, when on their own they could possibly be something far greater than they are now? What should I do when the thrill of being simply known as one half of the tag team champions of PRIME starts to clearly grow old on Baltasar? Sure most people think that all teams, even great ones, are destined to split up eventually. It seems like that is one of those unwritten rules or something that teams are not suppose to spend their whole careers as a dominate team. Still even the idea that we are a dominate team is pretty realitive when you think about it. It is more than a plausible theory that teaming with me cost Baltasar at least two matches against his enemy The Deville. I've failed him many times over since we came to PRIME. Every time we had a chance to kick the door to greatness in and make ourselves known and feared, I've been the one dropping the ball. This isn't something that he would ever come to me and accuse me of. He has never, and more than likely would never, blame any loss or bad situation on me. It just isn't his style to throw the blame from his own shoulders to those of which the blame actually belongs. For every victory we have obtained in this company I've some how managed to cause us to lose two matches. My role in the team has always been the guy who backs Baltasar up and watches his back. I'm not the outspoken and more charismatic one. I accepted the fact a long time ago that I simply don't have that 'it' factor that he has. I'm happy with my role as the guy who makes sure nothing and no one is able to screw us out of things. I have failed at this role too often as of late, however, and it is a situation that must be fixed. I can't continue to not hold up my end of the team and expect Baltasar to continue to bail us out of everything. Every since we arrived in PRIME I've allowed him to do every thing, to make every move, to avenge every loss or remark. I've done little to help him out and in a lot of ways I've made things far worse. The Deville is a perfect example of what I am talking about. We attacked that man together in the Halo in order to save Rage. It wasn't something that Baltasar went in and done on his own, we did it together. Just the same way we worked in the Halo together that night, the last night that I feel like I have truly had his back in a long while. That night we attacked The Deville and started a war between Baltasar and Angelo that will probably never truly end. They hate one another too much to simply walk away from it all entirely and forget about it. Yet why wasn't I hated as much by him? The answer is pretty clear, at least to me, and that is that I acted like a coward. I'll admit that I was a little bit afraid of The Deville. I've heard all the rumors, listened to all the stories, and seen with my very own eyes how cold and evil that man can be. The moment he had a dog with rabbies attack me I simply froze when it came to him. It was all I could do to get myself into a ring with him and face the man who caused me to believe in true evil on this earth. Baltasar, on the other hand, never missed a beat. He continued to rush head long into attacking Angelo and taking him down. He became obsessed with being able to finally get one over on the man known as The Deville. Then once he got revenge on him, pulled on over on him, or was able to humilate the man it was never enough. Baltasar became obsessed with destroying The Deville and most of it all on account of the incident with that stupid dog. If I had just stood up for myself at that point and time this whole thing could have already been over and we'd have probably been tag team champions long before we were. Basically my fear, stupidity, and plain lack of respect for Baltasar has held us back. So I guess that explains why Dave Gibson wants to face me instead of facing Baltasar. He is clearly smarter than some people give him credit for and has chosen to face someone who is considered the weaker link of the team that Team Zambo would love nothing more than to dethone. I can't say I blame him, really, as it really is a great strategy. I'm sorta shocked a gringo came up with such a brilliant deduction, but hey things around here shouldn't amaze me any longer. Gibson has what he wants and it's a chance to prove his teams superiority to the current tag team champions of PRIME by beating the weaker link of the group. This attitude and situation is what makes this match absolutely pivotal for Los Diablos, not to mention myself. A win by over Dave Gibson by myself would help to solidify Los Diablos as the team to beat in PRIME. Sure the tag team titles are suppose to do that, and to some extent they do, but a man has to step into the ring and prove it as often as possible for those titles to really mean anything. A win over a man of Gibsons caliber is a huge way to get back to my old form. To pull my weight and prove that I am here to do whatever it takes to make sure that Los Diablos is the most respected and feared tag team in all the sport of professional wrestling. Dave Gibson needs to understand that this match isn't about hating him or wanting to see him out of the sport forever. I have no reason to hate the man, even with the knowledge that he wants to take my gold, cause this is all part of the business we all take part in. I'm not afraid to face him and his five hundred pound buddy who has the mind of a two year old child who grew up in a less than worth while country. I'd say like Canada but really with people like Aimz running around up there I don't think there is really any thing else that needs be said. They already know it for themselves. This week is my chance for the redemption and salvation of my own career. To prove that I can step up and be the strong link in the tag team chain of Los Diablos when I need to be. I've allowed others to get into my head before and ruin everything that I needed to be doing in my PRIME career and I simply will not be allowing it to happen any longer. Angelo Deville, in my mind, is no longer a problem for Los Diablos. He no longer matters and he never really should have. I know Baltasar is going to hold a grudge against him forever, and I'm honored to say that part of that was in defense of me, but should there ever need to be another meeting with Deville for Los Diablos then I will not allow Baltasar to have to take it all on his own. I'll pull my weight and stand up for myself like I always should have. The time to stand up, fight back, and prove myself has arrived and it is long over due. The road to proving myself as a strong link is about to begin and... It begins with Dave Gibson. :::: {Scene Opens} As the plane takes off into the clear blue sky I rest my head back against the back of my seat. A trip back home to Mexico is just what the doctor ordered for a man who has had plenty on his mind. This week is to be all about proving to Baltasar that I can be the strong link and relied on if Baltasar needed it, and now there is trouble back home for a man who we both respect greatly. A chance to finally prove that Baltasar doesn't have to do it all on his own and can rely on me if he wants or desires. 'So where are you headed young man?' A old man in the seat next to me asks as we are just getting up to our place in the sky. He has to be in his early sixties and probably has three or four grandchildren from the warm smile he displays on his face. 'To Mexico where I am from, and you?' I respond with a little nod of my own. I brush out a wrinkle in my slacks as he takes a breath and smiles once again. 'I'm headed to Houston where I'll spend a little vacation time with my son, daughter-in-law, and their kids. Two boys and a girl that are all eleven years old or younger and I have a great time the couple of times a year I get out there to see them.' He responds with a glow about him as he speaks. Clearly the man has found the pleasure in the simple things in life. Something I can only hope that one day I'll be able to look forward to once my career in this crazy business is over. 'That sounds like a wonderful time. Too bad you only get to see them a couple times a year, but I know how that goes. I see my family very little a year as well cause of all my travel and such.' I tell him with a touch of sadness in my tone. 'Lots of traveling huh? What do you do young man?' He asks with a grin. I simply smile at the fact that he doesn't reconize me. That is one of the more simple pleasures about wearing a mask to wrestle, when I travel without it on then I don't get hounded by people who reconize me. One of those simple pleasures I've learned to enjoy during my time in the ranks of pro wrestling. 'I'm a professional wrestler and my name is Angelus.' I tell him as we shake hands and he nods his head in approval. 'Professional wrestler huh? I used to be a big fan of that stuff when I was younger. I got out of watching it a long time ago cause it never seemed like anyone who should get anywhere, was actually getting anywhere. I guess I just got bored with it. Who do you work for?' He asks after explaining his situation with watching wrestling. 'I work for the big company known as PRIME. I don't blame you for not watching wrestling anymore, there are lots of things on our show that makes me shake my head. Plenty of people like Boda who hasn't ever done anything on his own, yet he gets title shot after title shot. Makes me shake my head.' I respond with a little sigh. 'Boda...that fool is still around? Man I remember watching him get his ass handed to him by Duke Williams back in the good old days of PCW. He still running around jocking the nuts of the top guys so he can stay in the spotlight?' He says with a little chuckle to himself. The words of the old man do nothing but make me smile and chuckle as well. I guess even the fans must be able to see what is really happening with Boda as well. 'Yeah he is. Now he is getting punked out on a weekly basis by Duke Williams son, Hoyt Williams. Hoyt is a close friend of mine and he is finally get his just shot at the top prize in PRIME. He has just had to wait forever so guys like Boda can continue to get shot after shot, despite not being worth opening the shows anymore...let alone main event them.' I respond with a hint of distain in my voice for the whole situation. 'Don't like Boda much do you?' He asks with a grin. 'I don't really think about Boda much one way or another. Just when I see him getting to stick around the main events, despite the fact the he has never been a main eventer on his own, sorta sticks with you, yah know? If not for something like that I'd never even notice Boda being around PRIME, cause he'd be forgotten like he should have been long ago.' I respond with complete truth to my words. 'So if you are friends with Hoyt you must be one of those Los Diablos feller's who have the tag team championships, huh?' He asks, more like states, with a grin on his face and a little wink. Instantly I know the old man still checks in on the wrestling from time to time, despite the fact he isn't a regular fan any longer. 'Yeah I am. Angelus, as I told you before. I simply don't wear my mask when traveling for the reason that I'd like to not be noticed by the fans if I can. It just makes traveling easier and more relaxing than if I wore it.' I explain as he nods his head in understanding. 'Can't blame you there one bit. I respect that fact as I'm sure you guys get bothered enough by those crazy fan types. If my memory serves me correct, last I checked, you are facing that Dave Gibson fella. He is a tough cookie from what I remember of seeing him some years ago.' He says to me. 'Yeah. That is my singles debut for PRIME this week. My chance to shine and prove myself for Baltasar and the team. I haven't been the strongest of Imperium, I guess, since we started. Time that I picked up my slack and proved some dominance for a change.' I say as I am handed my drink that I ordered before take off. The old man is handed a bottled water and he takes a quick sip of the ice cold, refreshing water, as I down my glass of champaigne. 'It is always nice to prove yourself to people. Just remember this, as it is the best advice I ever got. If you start worrying to much about the concern of what others think, then you'll let the things that you enjoy be ruined. Pretty soon you'll look up and it will be ten years down the line and you won't remember anything that was fun, cause you allowed yourself to hate what you were doing. Do what makes you happy and what allows you to prove yourself to no one other than you. If you do that, then you'll always be one step ahead of the rest.' He says to me as he leans his head back into the large white pillow he has just placed behind his head. 'Now if you don't mind I'd like to get about an hour or so of rest before our flight lands. It was a pleasure meeting you Angelus. Just another story to tell to my grandchildren and son, all of whom are wrestling fans.' 'No problem. It was nice talking to you as well. Something that I needed, actually, and tell your family hello for me.' I respond as he drifts off to sleep. As I sit there thinking about what he says I reach down to the bag I have on the floor in front of me. I unzip the small bag, with a few of my clothes, and pull out a few of Imperium T-Shirts I had packed to wear back home and quickly sign my name to them with a message to the old man and his family. Then I fold the shirts up and place them into the seat next to the man. He shifts slightly as he is slighty disturbed but doesn't open his eyes. 'Thanks for the advice.' I whisper to the man, knowing he won't hear me, and I then turn and look out the window as I drift off to sleep as well with thoughts of Dave Gibson and my redemption ahead, floating around through my brain.
