One Ticket To Hokkaido, Please..

6 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 02 - 06 - 05 'Just have a good time over there.' A consoling Dr. Klingman sat next ro Ozric at the inter-connected chair, waiting area. Ozric was focused, but still shaken up from it all. This was his job and he had to concentrate on what was in front of him. He was still in a phase of disbelief as well. The doctor that was thought to have died in his sleep ended up being the one in the end, picking up Ozric as he mindlessly walked on the highway; battered beyond belief and still sliding down the mountainous high he climbed to. Ozric rested his head against the awkward back curve of the chair that nested just around his neck region, which forced him to slouch for it to reach his head. His right eye, pinkish and puffy and just at the edge of being black and blue. He had butterfly stiches applied to the back of his head and three small bandages covering various abrasions on his face. Yes, he was a beat up guy and didn't mind admitting it, either. 'Have you heard anything about the situation up at the factory?' The doctor was hesitant in responding. He wasn't plan on bringing this upon their last conversation for the next couple of weeks. 'Well, Your father died and I'm sure you knew that. Jim Emerson is in Intensive Care right now. Everyone believes he's going to make it out of it. The factory is closed down after police found out that they were making and distributing various drugs. Just look up at those television screens over there...' The doctor pointed towards the television to the right as a young female newscaster spoke on the incident that occured at Lathrop Steel. Someone then walked over a nd turned it up... 'We now return to our headlining story; a tragedy, massacre and drug bust all in one at a local steel company. The owner of the company was reported to have killed himself in the police raid, along with two others who helped design the corrupted business years ago with the owner. One other male who was involved is reportedly in the hospital recoopering from a bullet wound and other various injuries. Police have stated that he was protecting an unidentified individual in the tragic incident. More once it comes in, now back to the Golden Girls marathon...' Ozric pulled out a napkin from his coat pocket and realized that there was a number jotted in ink on it. 'Jim Emerson - [detail withheld]' - He folded it twice and stuffed it back into his pocket as he heard his flight on the intercom. He nodded toward the doctor and picked up his luggage. He shook the hand of the doctor before he pulled him in for a hug. 'You go into that match with a potent attitude and you take them on, Ozric. I will be with you in spirit. We've been through quite a bit, sir Mortimer, and I looked forward to a ton more once you return to the States.' Ozric nodded as a half-smile came over his face. 'I'm sure we will, Doctor. As for now, this clown must giddy up on outta' here and get to Japan. We're all placed on this Earth for a reason .. Well, this is my reason.' Ozric patted the back of the doctor as he walked past him and handed the flight-announcer his ticket, she placed it into a machine as it popped out instantly. He pulled it out and walked through the tunnel as the Doctor waved once more before walking away. As Ozric walked through the tunnel all he could think about was the duel halo, and Dark Age. He suddenly mumbled under his breath... 'Here I come, you fuckers.' Ozric entered the airplane as the scene slowly came to a fading end...
