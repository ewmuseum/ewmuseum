Date: ReVolution 169 "Daaaaaaaaaaaamn! Dude, how much is PRIME payin' ya? Because, I need to get some of THAT scratch!" The voice of Jack Harmen rings throughout the house as he walks in and takes a good look around. Behind him comes Craig, his face stern and stoic as he looks at the brand new house he had purchased just days beforehand. As he places a plastic bag down on the counter and looks around at the 2.1 million dollar house in the heart of Beverly Hills he can only nod his head as he admires the beauty of it all. Then, through the door comes Mary-Lynn Mayweather with a simple back bag slung over her right shoulder and a handful of books in her hands. "I've gotta say," Mary-Lynn begins to say in an in awe tone, "That this place is definitely nicer than any place I've ever been, that's for sure." Craig shoots her a smile while Jack nods his head. "No joke. Thanks again for letting us move in here. After that fight Kate and I had, it was either leave and find some cheap motel that might as well be a cardboard box or stay and kill the bitch, so... Thanks for giving me a third option to avoid all that jail time. You really bailed us out," he says in his usual frantic tone. Craig just shakes his head, telling Jack that it wasn't any big deal. "You guys feel right at home here. Mi casa es su casa, or something along those lines. It'll be nice to have some people living here anyways," he tells them as he begins to walk towards the balcony with a view of the entire city. His right hand grips the door handle to the sliding door and pulls it open before walking onto the wooden balcony and takes a nice, long look out as he tries to control his emotions and feelings. It had been a few weeks now since the incident that had taken the life of Katie and Nicole along with his two children, and he hadn't known how to really react. Moving into a new house had been the first step. "You doing okay?" the voice of Mary-Lynn cuts through his thoughts, causing Craig to turn towards her and nodding his head. "It's not easy, that much is for sure. The scene has definitely changed," he tells her, his voice very low and cautious indicative of how his mood is. Her eyebrows narrow, almost as if she understands, before she walks up to him and puts her hand on his shoulder. "When you told me, Jack and me, everything, it really floored me, you know? I knew there was something different to you, but never truly realized how different your life truly is compared to ours. Well, I don't know about Jack. He still harps about that time he wrestled a dinosaur to the ground and then pissed in its mouth," she tells him with this mocking tone in her voice. "IT WAS A DRAGON!" Jack yells, seemingly hearing every word that they were saying across the room. Mary-Lynn just sighs as she ignores him and keeps her attention on Craig. "You've obviously got demons you need to take care of. Cozen being one of them. I think this is one of those times where it's just going to continue to get darker before the light at the end of the tunnel shines through," she tells him, trying to warn and comfort him all at the same time. Craig turns from her and walks over to the edge of the balcony and grips the banister with a certain intensity to him. So much of him is dead, void of feeling and emotions. It scares him because when he's in this state, nothing good can come of it. "You're going to pull through this, Craig, I know it," Mary-Lynn says before walking back inside. With those words, Craig nods his head before turning around and walking back into the house. His mind is focused on one thing as he walks to the front door. "Where're you headin' Craigster?" Jack asks around the bend, as Craig grips the doorknob to leave. "We're all out of Cheetos because you don't have any..." Craig returns a small glare. "Just keepin' you aware mang." "I'll be back," Craig responds sternly before pulling open the door. "Craig, what're you going to do?" Mary-Lynn asks, but never gets an answer as he shuts the door behind him. Even though he never answers back, the answer is right there for all to see. To take care of business. ~~~~~~~~~~ Dealing with death is different for many people. Some grieve while others completely ignore it. Then, there are some who just get purely angry and lash out in every single possible direction imaginable. When dealing with someone like Craig, who had experienced death in so many different forms, the feelings that a person like that goes through are simply unfathomable. Norman Cousins put it best when he said that Death is not the greatest loss in life. The greatest loss in life is what dies inside us while we live. For someone who has experienced death to the point where it loses its luster and feeling, what is there left to die? If each time there's something that dies inside of us when a person dies, then what happens when you see hundreds of people die? Is there anything left there at all? Just one thing. Humanity. For so many years, he had operated as an assassin, killing without thought and feeling. Then, when his conscious mind came together with his secret life, the guilt hung on his heart that it nearly became unbearable. The pain ripped through his body unlike anything he could've possibly imagined. Witnessing the death of those he loved the most though, those that tied him to this world, had done something that nobody had ever planned for. They had planned for revenge. It's what you would expect from a man like Craig, with his skill set. Instead, they would get something much worse than revenge. They wouldn't just give a vindictive soul, but they would get a man hell-bent on pain and agony. For them, they wouldn't experience the expected, but fear the unexpected. They say revenge is an act of passion; vengeance of passion. Injuries are revenged; crimes are avenged. But, what is the price of that revenge? How do you avenge a crime such as pure, cold-blooded murder? The death of two unborn babies. That's a question that can't be easily answered. Evening has begun to set as the sun is fading in the distance. The world of Los Angeles is around him, but for Craig, there's no one else around. Except for two people. Craig just stands there, looking out at the not the orange sun, but at the loss of humanity around him. "Last night, I had a revelation. Somehow I have to make you pay. It's all about manipulation, and what it takes to get my way. I don't believe in soft solutions. No one makes a fool of me without receiving retribution; no one hurts me and goes free," Craig says in this very stead, slow tone. He turns around and looks at the two human beings sitting in chairs, bound and gagged. "I'll play on your fears, I'll leave you in tears, and you'll never be the same, my friend. You're walking a line, it's a matter of time, and you'll never rest easy again," he continues to speak, his tone very steady as his eyes go back and forth from person to person. "I've got the power to bring you down. I've heard it said, to err is human. It's forgiveness that's divine. I thought about forgiving you, but I want revenge, I want what's mine. I think it's time to settle scores now and it's time to set the record straight. You'll know it's coming, you won't know how, or when, you'll have to watch and wait," he says, taking a step closer and closer to the man and woman, helpless against him. "I'll play on your fears, I'll leave you in tears, and you'll never be the same, my friend. You're walking a line, it's a matter of time, and you'll never rest easy again," the words continue to come out of his mouth steady and calm. Then from his coat comes a long, silver gun polished and pristine. I've got the power to bring you down. You know, it feels intoxicating, to be intimidating. It's invigorating to see you shaking. I've got the power to bring you down You know something, you see it coming, you know I will stop at nothing," he finishes telling them before he looks back and forth once again at those that would torture him; Silvia his psychologist and Warren his doctor. They look at him, pleading with him to change his mind, but the wind had set a long time ago on their lives. Then, out of his other pocket, he pulls out a tape recorder and stops recording himself. "Your bosses will find you up here and no one will ever know what happened to you two. Feel comfort and solace in that you two won't be the last," he says before putting the trigger up against Silvia's forehead and pulling the trigger. BANG! The blood and skull fragments fly everywhere as her body grows limp and lifeless. As he looks over at Warren and sees the fear in his eyes, Craig shakes his head. "You don't get to feel fear. You don't get to be scared," he says, anger dripping with each and every word before he puts the gun up against Warren's temple. Warren begins to pray under his breath, but never gets to finish. BANG! With the two deaths staring him in the face, the blood pooling before him, he pulls the tape out of the tape recorder and places it on the lap of Silvia before beginning to walk away. "I've got the power to bring you down."
