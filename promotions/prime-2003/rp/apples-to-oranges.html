Date: poca de soca On a need-to-win basis? Yeah fucking right, maybe last week. Maybe before my dismantling on behalf of the Inhuman Monster with the Heart of Gold. Maybe when I had lost faith in the Atlantic Wrestling Club. I wanted to believe I was God’s Gift to PRIME; that Tchu was the mistake they shouldn’t have made and I was here to save them from such a descent from grace. I wanted to believe that I’d destroy Tchu and make a mockery of his record by pressing on and peeing on everyone who tried to stand up to me. That I’d pick up the piece that Tchu left me to grab once Great American Nightmare had past, and then I’d challenge Hoyt Williams to a Universal Title Match (because we all know Tchu can’t write another roleplay about memory loss and get away with it [/bitterness]). You know what I actually thought? I actually thought I’d win the Universal Championship the second I could claim a shot. Had everything played out the way I originally had planned, the way it was “supposed” to, I’d be the one to show everyone ‘Hey, Hoyt’s a tosser and can’t fight his way out of a ball pit! I’m the true Champion, and didn’t even break a sweat to get here.’ Looking back, it was being a cocky son of a bitch; but before I liked to think of it as ‘Manifest Destiny’. It was me saying “God, you selfish prick, you’d better give me what I deserve!” And it was a reasonable request (that divine mother fucker won’t even let me in the Garden of Eden because some bitch ate an apple? I didn’t eat the fucking apple! so fucking let me in paradise you jealous-of-Jesus sack of hypocritical shit! how the fuck am I supposed to have “free will” if YOUR bitch-ass is all knowing?!) that I never should have let run around in my brain, because the more I thought about it—the more it sounded like gospel. And now? What’s changed? Meh. Call it being content, or call it giving up; I don’t really give a fuck what you call it. I could get into this bullshit about how you’re opinions don’t really matter; that I’m a careless bird of my own free will and flying on my own merit while nothing that you can say will bring me down. But then I’d be lying. Because next week is Great American Nightmare, and that means Danny Ferguson is going to want a piece of this exquisite physical specimen. That means, next week, it’s back to winning. For now, though, I can relax in between a week of destruction on Tchu’s behalf and another one spent dancing with Danny’s lackey, Chandler Tsonda. I guess beating him in that tag-match, wasn’t “pure” enough for him. I don’t know how he justifies that when he’s got the Tag-Champs and Captain Drunk on his team when all I had was the Forsaken. Who’s Chris Collins? So since he felt that loss wasn’t HIS fault (he’s right; it was mine), he felt the need to get involved in affairs that didn’t involve him. SUPER FUCKING DUPER AFFAIRS, with the K-Wolf. Again: Chris Collins who? So the second time wasn’t “pure” enough; that I had to worm the gold from his grasp; and now he’s trying to go for round three. I take it dude really needs this. Meh, I’ll play with him. I’m taking a different strategy for this one. A guy whom I’ve beaten twice doesn’t deserve “dedicated Facey.” He has to deal with “bored Facey.” And sometimes, that’s the worst case scenario (for those of you shitty with math, or have the distinct learning disability of BEING PADDY O’SHEA, I’ll teach you something called the “substitutionism theorem”: sometimes = ALL THE TIME). I digress. I’m thinking about it too much. Already I’m betraying my strategy for the week, which is “don’t think about it.” Think about finally getting home. Yeah, about how you can finally pull into your girlfriend’s driveway, hopefully in more ways than one. You’ll get home, hand her the movie she picked out (Breaking the Waves? WTF), give her the pizza that YOU ordered (it’s important to establish who does what around here), and hopefully you’ll get laid. HOPEFULLLLLLLY. Shit, it’s been a fucking long time since I was laid last. Probably not years, but a long time! That’d probably be the BEST way to take my mind off of my match with- fuck that, don’t even finish that thought. Just concentrate on getting home. “Yo bitch, I’m home.” There was no answer. Adam Dick, despite the incessant crinkle in the plastic bags that contained the movie and the chicken wings, was able to make out that the water was running. Putting two and two together (for once), he realized that Chelsea Vice happened to be in the shower, given that was on her to-do list when Adam left just an hour earlier. If Adam thought she could hear him call her a bitch, he never would have said something. But he sure would think it at her! Enclosing himself in the single bedroom apartment, Adam tossed the DVD across the living room and onto the couch that sat in front of a TV. He unwrapped the buffalo-wings from the bag and opened the container, shoving the first one he put his hands on into his mouth. Indulging his impatient child within, the Illustrious Meat-Eater sucked every piece of chicken off the drumstick; even the dark stringy piece of fat that hangs loosely to the side. Once finished and a field goal attempt to dispose the evidence later, the thought ran across Adam’s mind that told him “if it’s one thing that’ll get you laid, it’s pleasing your woman.” So Adam prepared. He grabbed a stack of paper plates, napkins, and sides of buffalo-sauce along with the pizza and wings, taking them down to the living room coffee table, right in front of the television. He pulled the couch in closer to the coffee table to provide a more comfortable means of sitting (couches are perfect for bonage!). He turned on the surround sound and DVD player and popped in the shitty foreign flick. He even turned most of the lights off to create a sensual, romantic atmosphere. Needless to say, Adam was working hard for his lady. Hearing the water shut off, Adam knew it was only a matter of moments before Chelsea’d come waltzing out to find his perfectly concocted love-fest. He took a seat by the couch, awaiting her arrival. Minutes later, when she finally came, it was the reasons that came packaged in a skimpy pink satin bathrobe (that did more than wonders for thighs that were obviously worked for, with short, wet spiky hair that Adam had a fetish for) that made him realize why he stuck around for a girl like this. “Why are the lights dim?” she asked. “Because it’s better for the movie experience! You hungry?” “Didn’t I tell you to order a pizza earlier?” She always instigated the sarcasm wars. “Well come have a seat. We’ll eat and watch our movie, together!” Adam was admittedly too excited for this very non-monumental occasion. “Well, I usually don’t like to eat food like a yokel in front of the TV,” Uh oh, we’re at Defcon 1, “but I guess I’ll make an exception for you. Let me get my blanket; I’m freezing.” Chelsea retreated to the bedroom to grab her comforter from the bed, and then began sprinting with it towards the dinner scene. She happened to pass and glance down at the kitchen trash-can, noticing a very bare chicken bone that stopped her dead in her tracks. “Couldn’t fucking wait, could ya?” Adam didn’t even remember eating the chicken wing. “What’s that?” “You’re too hungry to think that maybe you should have some manners for once, Adam? That’s your problem; you’re always so damn selfish; doing whatever you want to do.” “What the hell are you talking about, I haven’t eaten a thing, look!” In his defense, Adam holds up the empty plates and unused napkins. Chelsea scoffs at his rebuttal, due to the evidence he seems oblivious to. “Are you REALLY lying to me about this? Honestly? Did the pizza place decide to give us a completely dry bone and slather it in wing-sauce with hopes that you might just toss it in the trash without any thought of mind, which you just so happened to do? You’re a fucking tool, Adam Dick, and an idiotic liar.” “I’m not lying!” “Look in the trash!” Light-bulb. Adam quickly recalls the sweet, sweet buffalo-wing he happened to enjoy earlier, but the memory turns sour in the current situation. “Sorry?” Followed by the shrug. “Excuse me?” she replied. “Listen, I’m sorry babe.” Adam hit the play button on the remote control. “Come on and sit down. The movie’s starting and I don’t want to fight. I just want to relax on my time off.” Reluctantly, Chelsea pulls herself in the blanket towards the couch. At first, she sits on the opposite side of the couch from Adam. He reacts by pushing the pizza box towards her and scooting down, accommodating to her “selflessness”. He throws his arm around her shoulder and kisses her on the cheek. “Come on.” “What’d you get?” her nostrils flared as her brain caught on. “Half-pepperoni, half-cheese. Just like you like it.” “You mean just like you like it.” “Well, I like cheese and you like pepperoni.” Adam was suddenly confused. “How else would we do it?” “I really don’t see why you don’t just order a pepperoni pizza. You’re so scrawny and don’t have enough of an appetite to eat more than three pieces much less than half a pizza. Why do I have to sacrifice my potential meal because you’re afraid to pick off a few slices of meat?” Wow, her justification almost made sense. Almost. Recovering from the shock of her bluntness, Adam was barely able to stammer out “I-I’m sorry.” “It’s alright. Just don’t do it again.” Okay, things are going great. The movie previews are playing, woot. “Are you going to the gym with me tomorrow?” She asked him. “Nah, I figured I’d take a new approach since I’m facing a pawn like Tsonda this time. You know; take it easy, relax all week.” Adam didn’t make eye contact. He wanted to move on from the topic as quick as possible. Hah, as if she’d have that. “You mean like you’ve been doing for the past six weeks?” Adam takes a bite of his slice of cheese pizza and nods. “Kinda,” with his mouth full. “Exactly. That’s exactly what I’m talking about. YOU. ARE. SELFISH. Not only will you do yourself the extra good by actually TRYING to defeat your opponent, but you’d also be keeping me company—but NO, you’re too fucking into your own world to stand up and do anything for anyone but yourself, Adam Dick.” “I help out Mike Wade!” “You help him by getting someone’s attention to pull some half-assed prank. You still act like a child and that’s what you’re going to be classified as for the rest of your life. You’ll be wrestling joke gimmicks like yourself and Chandler Tsonda in PRIME’s house shows once they get sick of the one-trick-pony you are. You’re too aimless to do anything, and you don’t even take credit for the things that you actually do.” “Wait wait wait wait wait wait, hold the phone here.” Chelsea interrupts him by ripping the remote control from the table and pausing the movie. “You are not going to ruin my movie with your quitting attitude.” “One, I DO prepare for my matches. My preparation isn’t exactly orthodox, and contains various tasks like arson, espionage, and TONS OF PREPARATION! Two, I’ve had big things in the works since day ONE lady, so don’t you tell me I don’t have any direction! And Three,” Adam takes a breath. “what the FUCK is your problem?” “Adam, I am only trying to help you and your career. You want to get past this little jaunt of Super Fucking Duper Champion and move on to things like the 5-Star Title, or even the Intense Title.” “What’s wrong with the Universal belt?” “Oh come on, honey, you don’t actually think you can take Hoyt, do you?” “Not anymore.” “The point is-” Adam tries to console himself with another bite of delicious pizza, which he carelessly eats over her comforter. “AGAIN, my point exactly, you are so selfish! A fucking stranger would think you’re deliberately trying to ruin my comforter or that you’re the most attractive retard since Sean Penn.” Defcon 2? That was ten minutes ago. We’ve skipped the rest and gone to Code Red, mother fuckers. “It’s just like when we were Christmas shopping-” “When YOU were Christmas shopping.” Adam interjected. “Well, just like when we passed that pet shop, and I saw that cute little pug. He was the runt of the litter and he was sickly, practically on the verge of death! I was ready to spend my OWN money on the dog, but did you try to stop me so you could get it for me as a gift; like a gentleman would? NO, you fucking stop me from buying it because you said the damn thing was ugly. YOU CALL THAT BEING CONSIDERATE, ADAM? YOU CALL THAT BEING CONSCIOUS?” “What are you talking about?” He took a moment to think about everything, and finally gathered his words. “Okay, I was going to wait until Christmas to give you this gift, but because you’re so fucking bat-brained right now I may as well give it to you. I’ll be right back.” Adam walks outside. fast forward Adam walks inside, with portable pet-taxi. He opens the cage which shakes wildly, and edges the opening towards Chelsea. Out of the kennel falls the aforementioned pug, excited as all hell to finally be out of the kennel. “Oh my God. Adam, how long have you had him for?” She was surprised to have the dog, but more inclined to his current state ‘holyfuckingjesus iamsogladtherearesomefuckingpeople’. God knows how long he was locked in that thing. “About a week?” He didn’t even convince himself. Truth is he couldn’t remember. “WHY THE FUCK WOULD YOU KEEP HIM LOCKED UP IN THIS KENNEL FOR A WEEK WHEN HE’S FLIRTING WITH DEATH; YOU INHUMANE FUCK!” “Um, I’m sorry?” “No, Adam, you’re a piece of shit. You’re not sorry, you don’t even know what you did wrong. You’ll never think of anyone but yourself and even then you’re still slow as fuck.” “Who rented Breaking the Waves, huh? That movie is some shitty Scottish film about an ugly chick fucking a vegetable! No one wants to watch that shit, but I did it for you.” “It’s an ugly chick fucking a paraplegic, and she’ll be getting more action than someone else. You only rented it because you wanted to get laid, Adam. Now get the fuck out, and take your shitty pizza and your dog with you. You’re certainly not getting laid tonight.” “You don’t want the fucking dog?” “You should have asked me before you went and did something as stupid as buying a dog. Jesus, did you even think about WHY we didn’t get it? You said I’d be going on tour with you ALL the time once summer came; we don’t have the time to take care of a dog. Jesus Christ, Adam, you still don’t get it, do you?” “Can I call you tomorrow?” “Of course. There’d be a problem if you didn’t.” Ouch. Yeah, I gathered my shit. All of my shit. I actually just dropped the dog off at the compound’s security office. I don’t fucking know what they’ll decide to do with it, but it ain’t my problem anymore. And yeah, I finished my half of the pizza. Then I fucking smoked, and when I got hungry enough, I picked the pepperonis off and finished her half! Fucking bitch, seems like there’s nothing I can do to please her. She’s like some other people I know; always on the go. They can never relax and just let things happen. Always too fucking tense and too fucking bitter. Makes you want to slap a bitch, know what I mean? I’ll have my chance at ReV. It’s always the ones who’re controlling the events that benefit in the end. It’s simple, that’s why I like to watch from afar. I like to set things into motion. We’ll see where this train takes us, but so far it’s been a fucking trippy ride. We’ll see how long I can stomach this. After all, we are to very different people. Meh, apples and oranges; they can’t compare.
