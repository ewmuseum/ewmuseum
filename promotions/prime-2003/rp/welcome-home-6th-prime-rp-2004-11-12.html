Welcome Home [6th PRIME RP]

12 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11-12-04 “Flight 215 from Chicago now arriving at Gate 12” “Flight 215 from Chicago now arriving at Gate 12” The loudspeakers at Orlando International Airport pierce the sounds of the hustle and bustle of everyday travelers. The flow of humanity zooms by, as hundreds of thousands of travelers look for their gates to catch their flights. Their destinations unknown to each other. Standing just inside the airport terminal, Boda looks up to see what gate he and Athena are standing at. “Gate 2” Athena is quick to inform her man. Boda looks down at her, smiles and heads off to the right. A family of foreigners, looking at their tickets and not the walking traffic, bumps into the PCW Hall of Famer. The teenage boy of the family looks up. His eyes glisten, knowing who he just came face to face with. “BLIMEY…..I can’t believe my eyes…. you’re Boda.” shouts the prepubescent English lad. His markism is evident as he searches for a pen and paper. “Hey kid, how you doing?” Even with all the trouble in his life, Boda still finds time to please the fans. The big man shakes the hands of the family in front of him. “Hey Boda mate, you're like my favorite wrestler, do me a favor and kick Vampir's arse on Sunday' the teen begs for Boda to do what Boda already wants and plans on doing at The Great American Nightmare. 'Vampir's a complete tosser.' The biggest man in Prime smiles and nods his approval to his fan. “Damn right kid. And believe me, what I’m going to do to Vampir on Sunday is so bad, the name of the event aught to be changed to The Great Boda Kicked Vampir’s Fanged Teeth In Nightmare.” Boda signs the paper for the teen, letting him know that the match is going to be brutal. The family of English tourists head off, allowing Boda and Athena to continue their trek to Gate 215. “So, Chris…. you don’t think Stacey is going to be mad that I’m with you, do you?” Athena asks, knowing that it isn’t really that big of a deal. “No…. of course not. You’ve been there for the girls ever since this happened. She’s actually happy you are in my life and in the girl’s life.” Boda reassures Athena, stopping only momentarily on the way to Gate 215 to take a sip of water from the fountain. “Jódame. ... usted es Boda”.[“Fuck…. you’re Boda”] Boda turns around, and is approached by a family of foreigners, yet again. Athena smiles as the Spanish travelers sort of surround the two Prime Stars. “Soy su ventilador más grande.”[“I am your number one fan”] The elder of the family speaks, placing his hand out to shake Boda’s. The big man replies, shaking the outstretched hand. “En mi país, yo arreglé como usted para Víspera de todos los santos.”[“In my country, I was dressed as you for Halloween”] The father of the group continues, all the time, Boda simply nodding in approval. “Mi esposa finge soy usted cuando hago el amor caliente dulce de mono a ella de noche.”[“My wife pretends I am you when I make sweet hot monkey love to her”] The wife smiles, her eyes dropping down to look Boda over. Athena giggles, then tugs on Boda to remind him they have to get to Gate 215. Boda waves to the family, but the elder Spaniard stops him. “Por favor, patea ese hijo enfermo de un asno de Vampir de ramera este domingo. Yo no quiero que mi esposa pare pensando en usted como un semental grande, porque ella pararán tragar, y eso no será bueno para mí. Gracias.”[“Please, kick that son of a bitch Vampir’s ass this Sunday. I don’t want my wife to stop thinking of you as a big stud, because she will stop swallowing, and that won’t be good for me. Thank You.”] Boda walks off, smiling and telling Athena “I have no clue what that man just told me. But he seemed to like me, and told me something about Vampir. That’s all I could figure out.” Athena laughs, letting Chris know “That’s all you really needed to know.” Finally, the two come to their destination. Gate 215. Standing there, luggage in hand is Stacey, ex-wife of Chris, and mother of Mackenzie and Carleigh. She acknowledges her ex, and Athena, then walks off infront of the two. “She’s not in a good mode Chris.” Athena points out the obvious. “Well, her plane did arrive about 30 minutes ago. I guess we shouldn’t have taken our time to get her. Don’t worry bout it…. once we get to the hospital, she’ll change her demeanor.” Chris reassures his beautiful manager. “And she’ll be able to spend the whole weekend alone with Kenzie and Carleigh. Her first time alone since Vampir tried to decimate them. She needs this weekend to become herself again. Don’t worry about her Athena…. after I get done on Sunday, she’ll be able to have her girls back to normal.” Following the lead of Stacey, Athena and Chris walk back towards the terminal entrance. As the three step out of the terminal and into the warm November air of Orlando, the PCW Hall of Famer is once again stopped by a pair of traveling fans.“By Ghawd, yer thet wrestler Boda. Yer mah favo'ite.” The middle age man standing in front of Boda proclaims. Standing next to him, a beautiful, younger woman, who is smiling at Boda. “So, that’s a strange accent… what country are you from?” Boda asks. With a chuckle, the man replies “Heck, we’z fum Alabama.” Boda gives a sort of ‘oh, my bad’ look. “Well, I’m glad I can entertain you and your beautiful wife when I’m doing my thing in the ring.” Boda tries to cover up for his mistake. “Wal hell, she aught t'be purdy. She's th' bess lookin' of mah four sisters. ah warn't a-gonna marry up wif an homely one. We're hankerin' t'have right purdy chillun.” The Alabamian pulls his sister-wife in close, planting a half opened tongue kiss on her. Boda shakes his head, then starts to walk off, following Athena and Stacey, who are actually sharing a laugh together. “Do me a favo'. Kick thet sumbitch Vampir's ass fo' me. He warn't right fo' harmin' yer gals like thet, an' ah's hankerin' t'see him pay. You’z can getir dun.” Boda walks off, walking right past the two women with him. “What’s a matter Chris……” Athena chimes in behind Boda’s right side. “Don’t even go there Athena, don’t even. Let’s just get to the hospital, ok?” Chris steps out among the traffic, nearly avoiding getting hit as the little white walk sign gives the foot traffic time to hop across the busy road to the parking garage. As soon as Boda, Stacey and Athena are across, the vehicular traffic starts up again, zooming past. The roar of incoming and outgoing airliners is evident, but becomes duller and bassier as the three enter the parking garage. “Thanks Chris” Stacey looks at her ex-husband, giving him some sign of appreciation. It’s something that he’s not gotten from her in almost a year. “What for, Stacey?” Chris asks in return, wondering what she’s thankful for. “Taking care of that motherfucker’s servant last week. Thank you for showing him he can’t get away with messing with our girls like that. And, it looks like your fans are thankful too.” Stacey shows some emotion towards her ex. Chris rolls his shoulders. “Yeah Chris…. you seen it. The fans, today, they were from all different corners of this world, and they were behind you. You’ve got the momentum hun… this weekend, you can’t lose.” Athena tries to reassure him, reminding him of the sets of fans he spoke with earlier in the airport. “Hey….what Vampir did…. goes way beyond wrestling. There was nothing entertaining about his actions towards this family. Maybe entertaining to his sick, twisted minions and himself, but not to me, you, or all the fans. I won’t kick his ass this Sunday because I’m a better wrestler than he is. I’m going to kick his teeth in because I am a father. I’m going to annihilate that night dwelling bastard because I’M A MAN AND I’M NOT GOING TO DWELL IN MY EMOTIONS AND LET VAMPIR GET AWAY WITH WHAT HE’S DONE ANYMORE!!!” The veins in Boda’s neck throb as he is chocked full of emotion. His voice echoes through the parking structure, causing a lot of people to stare over at the black H3 that Boda and the woman are getting into. As the doors shut, the locks activate with the turn of the ignition key, securing the three inside the newest Hummer on the market. Putting the transmission in reverse, Chris backs the big SUV out of its parking spot and heads out of the parking garage. “At least the traffic isn’t too heavy today. It’ll be a quick trip over the 528 to the other side of town, and then only 10 minutes to Arnold Palmer. You’ll get to see the girls soon Stacey.” Boda smiles as he looks in the rear view mirror at his ex-wife. She returns a half hearted smile, knowing that seeing the girls is going to be great, but they are still in the hospital, clinging to life because of Vampir. The H3 tools down the 528, flying past The Florida Mall, past Orange Blossom Trail. “Hey,” Boda blurts out to the two woman in the truck. “I need to make a stop at the house first. I need to get something for the girls.” Athena nods, Stacey just sits in the back, anticipating seeing her girls again. Boda pulls the H3 off the Bee-Line, and onto the Turnpike. A short ride up to I-4, and over to the Windermere area of Orlando and the three are at the Sloboda residence. “The house looks good Chris.” Stacey speaks up from the back. She’s not been in the house in almost a year, since the divorce was finalized. “Thanks, I’ve tried to have it kept up a little. Hell, when Tiger got married, his new wife spent a ton of money on his house next door.” Chris laughs. “He’s so whipped.” Athena smacks Chris’ arm as he pushes the garage door opener. As the garage doors open, Stacey asks about the two vehicle in the garage already. Chris parks the H3 just outside the garage, flipping the ignition off. The engine comes to a dull roar as it shuts off, and the three climb out of the H3. “Whose cars are those?” Stacey asks as the three walk in through the garage. Stepping into the kitchen, the smell of fresh baked apple pie hits like a freight train. “Just a few people staying this weekend with you.” Chris steps aside, allowing Stacey to step from the kitchen and into the open living room. The view of Windermere Lake is breathe taking from the 4 large bay windows in the living room. Stacey stares out of them for a minute, before the silence is broken by some beeping. “What the?” Stacey asks Chris. A smile pierces the hall of famer’s lips. “We’re not going to the hospital to see the girls. And you’re not spending the weekend in some hotel.” Chris reveals some of his reason for coming home first. “What do you mean? I’m not getting to see my girls?” Stacey is angered, almost saddened at the same time. “Chris…. you know I need to see them. They’re my angels. That cocksucker tried to steal them from me, and you won’t take me to see them. Fuck you. Fuck you Chris.” Stacey storms out of the living room, and pushes open the family room doors…. and stops. “Like I was trying to say……You know I’m going to be in Los Angeles this weekend with Athena, fighting for all the right reasons. I just figured you’d rather stay here. And of course, you’d want some company.” Chris walks past Stacey and into the family room. “Hey Dr. Greening, how are my girls today?” The room is full of medical equipment and two beds. On one, Mackenzie, on the other, Carleigh. Both are sitting up, eating food being fed to them by nurses. Stacey just stands in the doorway, her eyes as big as saucer plates. “Their doing well Chris. Still not 100 percent. Heck, they still don’t respond to outside stimuli…. but they are eating and breathing on their own. It could be worse.” Dr. Greening walks away form the equipment long enough to come shake Chris’s hand, and receive a hug from Stacey. “My girls are home” is all Stacey can manage to spill out of her mouth right now. “Hey….. they’ll be a lot better after this weekend, I promise you Stacey.” Chris gives Stacey a hug, making the guarantee she’s wanted to hear all day. “Now go see them.” Boda pushes Stacey into the room. Dr. Greening walks in with Stacey, talking to her and updating her on the condition of the two special patients. Chris and Athena walk out, back into the living room. Athena plants a kiss on her big man. “What was that for?” Chris wonders. “For being a good human being. For being a great father, and for being you.” Athena praises the big man. Chris walks over, and sits on a leather couch. Athena walks over, standing behind the couch, and places her arms around Chris from behind. “When I sent Tiamat crashing to the ring two weeks ago, it brought some life back into my girls. I broke part of what ever curse Vampir is holding over them. I know, when I defeat Vampir this Sunday, that it’ll finally pull them all the way out of that state of catastrophic coma. Athena… I know Kenzie and Carliegh know I’m doing what ever it takes. And I’ll do everything and anything to take Vampir down, and bring my girls back to me.” Chris stares blankly out the windows. A deep breath later, The Big Bad Boda Daddy stands and walks back to the family room. Chris walks over, between the two beds and looks down at his girls. “Welcome home girls…. Daddy loves you.”
