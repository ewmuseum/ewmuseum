Movin' on Up

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Late August Kaiser's Place Jackson, Mississippi Sunday, August 30th Kaiser grabbed his car keys and darted across his living room, cursing under his breath at his inability to get anywhere on time these days. Not more than two steps from the front door, his hopes of making it to the pub by five vanished. Tap. Tap. Tap. "Shit. Perfect timing." As he bit his bottom lip in frustration, Kaiser opened the door, not bothering to look through the peephole and see who the guilty party was that would be making him late. "Kaiser, we need to talk." David Vashaun ran a hand through his hair, scruffing his mane and leaving it in messy fashion. "I don't have time, Dave. I'm supposed to meet Michelle at The Pub in like five minutes. If I'm late, she'll kill me." "You out-weigh her by over a hundred pounds. I think you can take her." Kaiser's older brother pushed his way into the apartment, slipping passed his sibling like he wasn't even there. "Besides, this is kinda important..." "What?" "I might have fucked up." "What?" "Could be bad. I dunno. Maybe not. I mean, it could be nothing. I really dunno." "Don't have time for this, Dave. I promised Michelle we'd get together and talk back before Colossus. And I promised her I wouldn't be late." Kaiser grabbed his hat from the hook on the door. "Did you kill somebody or somethin'?" "Jesus. No" "Is Mom dying?" "No." "Then you can let yourself out, and I'll talk to ya later." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The Pub About forty minutes later "I still can't believe you moved to Chicago." "I didn't. Just got an apartment there. Makes since, ya know, with PRIME headquarters being in the city. It's not like I'm selling my place here in Mississippi." "You're keeping both apartments?" "I'm making pretty good money these days, Michelle. I can afford it. You ought to see the bonus I got for wrestling the main event at Colossus. Made my head spin. Never seen that much money in my life. Kaiser rolled his beer bottle between the palms of his hands. He'd promised his little sister they'd get together for coffee after the big show in Boston. With a little persuasive help from his puppy dog look, she'd been kind enough to agree to a couple of beers instead of a cup of joe. "Just kinda sucks that you're gonna be moving up north." "I told you", he laughed, "I'm not really moving to Chicago. My whole families here. You, Dave, Mom... Ellie... baby's due in a few weeks. I'm just going to be spending a few days up there every month. Promotional gigs and all that junk. Just figured it'd be nice to have a place of my own as opposed to jumping on hotels.com last minute and having to hunt down a room at the Holiday Inn." "Guess that makes sense." Michelle took a sip from her drink, then looked at her brother, the alcohol doing nothing to hide her disappointment. "Are you sure ya want to stay in a place like Chicago? Isn't there crime and stuff? "There's crime here. I'm going up to the Windy City on occasion. Not headin' south on the Green Line in D.C. I'll be able to take care of myself. Besides, how much trouble can I get into when we're talking just a few days outta the month?" "With you? God only knows!" She smiled as she took another drink. "Go easy, just cuz I'm not the little angel you are doesn't mean I'm a bad guy. We can't all be the favorite child." "What?! Mom and Dad never had a favorite." "Uh huh... ask Dave... he'll tell ya." "Whatever. Just because I actually helped with some chores around the house." "Kiss ass." The siblings shared a laugh, before Michelle turned the conversation back towards its roots. "So when are you heading back up to the new place?" "Tomorrow night, actually. Got some business stuff to do Tuesday before I hit the road for ReVolution." "Things are going well with the wrestling and stuff?" "Eh... other than coming up a little short at Colossus, yeah. I mean, I've got a great spot with the company, and I'm making real good money. Way better than I ever dreamed of making when I was with the fire department. It's a little more travel than I'd like sometimes, but all in all... yeah, things are goin well." "Good. I'm glad its all workin' out for ya. Even if I don't care much for it pulling you off to Chicago no sooner than we'd patched things up. "I'll still be around plenty. Chicago isn't gonna change anything." ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The New Apartment Chicago, IL Monday, August 31st Kaiser yawned as he twisted the key and turned the knob, pushing open the door. Unlike his place in Jackson, it didn't take a shoulder ram to budge anything free at this apartment, and it was good thing. The late flight had warn him out, and he was dying to collapse on his mattress. Flipping on the light switch, he tossed his keys towards the table he kept near the entry landing, but as they flew through the air, they found no table, falling to the floor with a clang. All around, everything was a mess. Tables turned over, the television shoved off its shelf in the entertainment center, lying busted on the ground. His book shelf had been tipped, spilling a library across his living room floor. The place was trashed. Completely trashed. Immediately, Kaiser pulled his cell phone out of his pocket. He had already dialed '9' and the first '1' when he stopped. "Sonofabitch." Cycling through his contacts list, Kaiser found his brother's number and hit 'Send'. There were two rings. Then an answer. Hello? "Alright, Dave. Let's talk. What exactly did you fuck up?"
