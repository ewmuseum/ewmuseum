Date: 8/25/05 "Don’t ever leave it," he said. "Don’t ever leave it. When a man has found something he really loves, he must always hang onto it, no matter what happens, whether it loves him or not. Nova’s common sense here, folks…and I die a little inside every time I see that shit. Everyone has to do that, don’t they? It’s like an unwritten rule. Pay-Per-View match. Big shot. Gotta do the "epic" thing, right? And it wouldn’t be an epic if there wasn’t some retarded blurb at the beginning to let you know this one’s all artsy and deep and the guy or gal behind it must have really probed their inner self for this, must have really swabbed out the ol’ noggin to come up with this soul-searching gem. Nothing says "I put it all into this one" more than grabbing someone else’s words to explain what you can’t spit out yourself. … I mean, not to sound like a broken record here, but what the fuck? Why is it so much cooler to try and force someone to decipher the meaning behind some goddamn pop lyric or poetry excerpt than to just fucking spit out what you want to say? I mean, look at that passage up there. You wanna know what the point of all that bullshit is? I noticed it before I finished reading the first word, not just because it’s not the most eloquent selection, but because I rule. The point of it is that Nova should hang onto professional wrestling because he loves it, even if it kills him. And if it does kill him, he shouldn’t give a shit, because he had the chance to do something he truly loved. That’s the secret to happiness. Of course, you could also read it as a relationship thing…telling him not to let Ariel go at any cost, because he loves her and that’s worth fighting for. It could mean that. So why not just fucking say that, huh? Why not? I, for one, would welcome the frankness, for a change. A breath of fresh air.
