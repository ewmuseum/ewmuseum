Date: August 4th, 2001 August 4th, 2000 I'm gonna die by the time I'm 35. This guise of business sport, cash blood, Nasdaq sweat... it's just another walking funeral. Marching us out to our doomsday, our own clocks ticking down to our last days. Wrestling is a sport of ends. Yet every week I return to action none the same. I know, every time I switch to a new promotion, that's it's more of the same. Self sacrifice, tears, and above all else loyalty. Loyalty to an inanimate glob of an organization slowly USING you, until there's nothing left. And yet, I come in every week with the largest smile on my face, ready to do any and everything asked of me. Do I know nothing else, or wish to know nothing else? Worst than all, this immeasurable pleasure may one day take my wife from me. Because I already know it's taken my mind... Death goes hand in hand with wrestling, like baseball and the seventh inning stretch. -High Flyer IWO Tag Team Champion(w/Tony Davis) August 21st, 2001 I've had an unhealthy fascination with death since I was a young child. Since the death of my parents, it's one of the few things I can look upon with complete clarity. The end. Nothing more. Nothing less. Heaven is the good you leave behind on earth. Hell? Well, you leave your own little slice of Hell behind for those around you to nurture. And mom and Dad? I'd like to say, Nurturement accomplished. Stuck in a rock and a hard place. Repeat the sins of the past or suffer the consequences of the now? Few people ever have to make a choice like this. If I win the World Championship tonight... it'll all be changed. The cycle of the past will twist and contort to new form. And I won't be doomed to repeat my parent's mistakes. Death by accident and gunshot, fueled by ones love for this pseudo sport. No. I don't have to have genetically inherited my destiny... Cause even if I can't beat Anthason tonight for the IWO World title? Even if I can't change the present to protect the future... Then... I'm going to have to protect Kate, push her away. Hit her if I have to. Protect the future by destroying the present. Who knows. Maybe one day she'll thank me... -High Flyer IWO : Rolling around in a rocket powered wheelchair for kicks August 31st, 2002 Are we always meant to look at the past, and no matter what was going on then, think it better than the now? I've had a lot of time off to think recently, nursing my surgically repaired knee. Doctors told me it was spent. That I would never regain the speed I once had. And that's fine. Because I can find quickness between the moments, and pick up the pace. My career, just like my life and my marriage, it all has a clock. One we can't see, ticking down to its end. Einstein's theory of relativity is certainly in work in all facets of life. Being off from the ring, I've had the time to enjoy my family. Those tough decisions made and forgiven for in the past. I'm getting help, help that I need. I never got this sort of help as a kid. Maybe my childhood would have been happier? Maybe I wouldn't need Kate to be happy. -Jack Harmen Currently trying to be High Flyer again August 14th, 2003 The FWO. Fans Wrestling Organization. While I may be here alone, and I may have to start from the ground up, I’m more than willing. I’m hungry to prove to myself, my doctors, and even my family that my legs are not the downfall of my career. They’re the spirit that fuels it. I’ve been back in the business for a little over 8 months, and I’ve been enjoying every moment. Yet, every day, I feel a distance growing between myself and Kate. Maybe it’s due to the therapy, maybe it’s… Well, if I knew I’d be stopping it and just embracing her in my arms right now. I just don’t get her anymore, and she doesn’t seem to get me either. It’s frustrating. It’s like if you were living in suburbia and whenever you’d come home your spouse would leave, and when you leave they’d be walkin’ through the door. I’ve been reading the Watchmen, so I think we’re four minutes to Midnight. -High Flyer FWO CW Superstar August 28th, 2004 Well, I’m still alive, that’s a good sign. Recently defeating Xias, hearing the cheers of the crowd, I can see why I feel in love with this sport. No longer burdened with holding all of my issues on my own shoulders, the Legion of Dairy, while annoying, have been a much needed and trusted ear during times of strife. I’m even trying to make things work out with Kate. But I fear. FWO wants to pick up, they want to tour again. I told her I’d leave if that would happen, but I can’t abandon my commitments. I have a contract, I have the Internet Title. I can’t just up and leave. She doesn’t get that. She says we have enough money, which I guess is true, as long as there’s no crazy economic collapse coming around the corner. And I want to spend time with my kids… But this is the most fun I’ve had in ages. -High Flyer Ruling the internet by writing in a notepad. August 26th, 2005 I realize something, during my jubilant self celebration of winning FWO's Meltdown match last night, gaining myself the opportunity to be World Heavyweight Champion. My wife was the first person I locomoted. ... That's certainly worse than anything I'm doing right now. It’s probably partly why I lost her though. I think I'm in the clear, still, karmically. Sorry Ivy. You... You got too close. After everything, I'm still chasing that pivotal moment I had in my life four years ago. The day when I chose my wife over my relationship. If I can be the best, hold the Heavyweight title... would everything be different? Would it change? Would it... be better? I need to know. -High Flyer Burden'd about Crosses, Inglorious Meltdown April 3rd, 2006 Nope. -Me. December 17th, 2007 How will my story be told? Probably poorly and with numerous grammatical errors. Regardless, I’ve joined PRIME Wrestling recently, teaming up with my good friend and brother in law Tony Davis. While I had fun with the Legion, nothing held a candle to Tony. But he seems distant, aloof. Perhaps that has something to do with me dropping him on his head and shoulder three years ago and taking him out of the sport until now. I seem to have a habit of harming those around me. Or causing harm to come to them. Hopefully we can all stick together, and… come out of this tour alive. -High Flyer Team V.I.A.G.R.A. once again dose the Neilson’s. December 25th, 2008 I remember a time when I would rush to Kate’s side to ask her, what exactly she would do if she were in my situation. With King of Kings coming up, and the possibility of getting back into the ring with Tyler Rayne(and the potential face off with Snow later that night), I really wish I could talk to someone about strategy. Tony is completely disinterested, and Mary-Lynn is way too green to show me anything from an angle I hadn’t already perceived. While they help keep me human, they aren’t making me a better wrestler. Dusk told me today he’s leaving PRIME. My contract is up tonight. Now, Alundra Blayze would walk off to FWO land and toss her gay little white belt in a trash can, but not me. I respect this business too much to even consider that. And to even have a chance to fight Snow… it seems like Destiny. I sell what he is. Even if we don’t get together in the ring tonight, I see us tangoin’ in the future. And the only thing I can hope to come from a matchup with Jason Snow? Is everyone loving the living hell out of it. -High Flyer Living for Blood Sweat and Tears, Scavanger Hunting Jewelry.
