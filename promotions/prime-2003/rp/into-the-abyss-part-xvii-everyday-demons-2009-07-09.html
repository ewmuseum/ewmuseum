Into The Abyss: Part XVII - Everyday Demons

9 July 2009

Date: 09-07-09 Locked away in the back of the mind in every man is a No Man's Land of secrets. It's an area tucked way down deep where his darkest desires are restrained and controlled, or left to fester in that forbidden zone. All the demons resonate from this little cavity of the brain, held back by bolts of pride, strength and dignity snapping at them and forcing them back into the shadows. Whether it's the desire to squander money for personal gain before giving generously to another, or imagining what it'd be like to hear that cute girl at work screaming with you between her legs regardless of how attractive your partner is, these demons lurk in the primal pits of Man, forced into constraint for they would be his very undoing if ever unleashed. The key to containing it lies in love and honour. If a man doesn't have anything to pride himself on then the demons claw to the surface and he indulges, and quite often the wants of a man will anchor him, stealing him from the pedestal upon which he would lord his status weren't he so weak. Temptation can take many forms, and if it weren't for his resolve he would tip over the edge and lose himself. In a world where sex, drugs and the seven sins are all present in most facets of a man's daily life it's not so much a question of how does he stay on the path of righteousness, but how long can he stay on it for? ---- "Is it to your liking sir?" Asked the clerk, an air of grace about him as he held the emerald box on an open palm, presenting the treasure inside to Von Kelsig. The diamond commanded the light around it, sending out a shimmering sparkle that twinkled in his eye as he gazed upon it. "It's certainly beautiful." Von Kelsig replied, his eyes tracing the cut of the rock and examining the fixing to the band of 24 carat gold. "If any piece truly captured the fine taste of a connoisseur then it would certainly be this ring." Lavished the clerk, subtly cramming his sales technique into flattery, even though the ring had already been paid for before its delivery to the store. "Can't disagree there." He muttered, taking a final look at the ring before the clerk gently closed the presentation box over and went about wrapping it up. Glancing around the store Von Kelsig found himself to be the only customer, and took a moment to look over the other exquisite pieces of jewellery on show behind the display cases. A lump crept up his throat every time he thought about the ring he had purchased and what it meant, nerves jangling through his limbs at the thought of where this thousand-dollar symbol would take him next. "She's a lucky girl." The clerk stated, smiling quaintly and holding the packaged ring out for his customer to take. Stepping toward the clerk Von Kelsig took a deep breath and reached out, taking the velvet bag with the ring inside in his large hand and gripping the drawstring tightly. He locked eyes with the clerk as he turned to leave, struggling to find an emotion with which to paint his blank expression. "She sure is." With that he took his leave, and as he ambled down the street towards his truck he couldn't have felt less like a 7'2" murderous giant. The more he thought about his decision to commit the more he cowered at the idea of it, of the unknown and the trials and tribulations it would bring. Aside from the trappings of his life, the monotony and the pain, he had a comfortable life with a routine he was accustomed to. He got his orders week in and week out, travelled to the promotional venues and the arenas, put his skills to use and put on a good show and got paid for it. He could handle that life on the road, because he knew what was expected of him and what he expected in return. Aside from a traffic jam or a nagging injury it was pretty sweet. With this ring that would all change. He remembered what Gloria had said at lunch about their relationship, and his stomach turned at the thought of having to pick between his career and his love life, or worse having to juggle both. He excelled at wrestling, and a two year relationship with no major squabbles must have meant something too, but with stock rising in PRIME and the possibility of a family life looming on the horizon how long would it be before something gave out? Strolling through the parking lot to his truck his thoughts were loud, drowning out the buzz of the traffic and the nattering of the public. As he fumbled for his keys a shrill tone pierced his concentration, alerting him to the presence of a girl clattering towards him on unsteady heels. "Hey! Wait!" She cried, stumbling to a halt in front of him, a plume of carrier bags emblazoned with designer labels wafting in her stead. Von Kelsig held his breath, checking the girl up and down. She was gorgeous, and when she looked down to adjust her feet in her heels he instinctively stole a glimpse of her teasing cleavage. "Can I help you?" He asked, cocking an eyebrow as she flicked her flaming red locks out of her eyes and beamed at him. "Um, I'm sorry I was just wondering...is your name Angelus?" She asked back, biting her lip after uncertainly uttering his name. "Yes it is. Do I know you?" He replied, aware that she had probably seen him on ReVolution but played the familiar face card regardless. "No, but I know your girlfriend. I work with Gloria at the salon, my name's Donna!" She giggled, curtseying and staring up at him with big emerald eyes, like Gloria's. "Ohh, alright. Nice to meet you Donna." He began, extending a hand to her. Juggling her bags in one hand she accepted the handshake, taken aback by the size of his big bear paw. Mm-mm, he's a big boy. "Gloria hasn't mentioned you before, hasn't mentioned anyone at the salon actually. She told me she was getting training to cut hair but that's about it." He continued. "How did you know I was who you thought I was?" "Oh she told us all about you being a massive superstar wrestling on the T.V., and you're too bulky to be a basketball player so I just guessed you were Gloria's boyfriend. Can't be too many giants walking around Memphis, right?" She smiled, adding a glow to her radiance. He chuckled and ran his fingers through his thinning crop of hair. He couldn't get her looks out of his head, and in the back of his mind another demon was locked away behind bars never to see the light of day again. Is she checking me out? "So Gloria's mentioned you're doing a little re-modelling at home. Have you had a house-warming yet?" asked Donna. "Oh no, not yet. We've been at the house for a good few months now but we're holding back on any partying until the place is looking the way we want it, which is taking longer because I'm on the road so much. But yeah we will be having a party, in the next month or so hopefully. Did Gloria extend an invitation your way?" Von Kelsig leaned back against the wheel arch, folding his arms and nodding down at Gloria's colleague. Don't think I don't notice those wandering eyes girl. "Yep she's invited half the work down. I'm looking forward to it!" You have no idea just how much... Von Kelsig nodded, uttering a low groan of approval before a momentary silence came over them both. "Well I guess I better-..." "So what are you up to to-..." They tripped over one another's words, and Von Kelsig felt his cheeks reddening from the awkwardness. "Oh sorry, carry on.." "Oh sorry, I'll let you get on-..." They paused again. She giggled and he chortled and the confusion gripped them. "No, it's fine. What were you going to say?" Von Kelsig asked, trying desperately to get the conversation back on track before they turned into bumbling idiots. "I was just wondering what your plans for the day were, but if you gotta go that's fine. I'm sure we'll be seeing more of each other in future." Again the soft smile graced her lips and he grit his teeth before forcing one of his own. "Oh, well I just drove in to pick something up but yeah we'll speak soon I'm sure." Before he turned to the cab, Donna reached out and tapped his arm, motioning for him to lean in where she pressed her cheek to his and blew a kiss, repeating with another on the other cheek. Awkwardly, Von Kelsig just leaned, staring ahead and straightening up timidly while she took a step back and curtseyed once more. "Okey dokey, well be seeing you!" She beamed, turning tail and skipping through the parking lot. Von Kelsig took a deep breath, aware that as she sauntered off his focus was on her buttocks and the gentle wiggle of her butt as she went. Taking one look at the bag containing the engagement ring he stepped into the truck and fired up the engine. Hopping into his F-150 Von Kelsig cleared his mind before recalling the phone call he made on his way into town. If he had to bite the bullet and take a step back from the ring he had to make sure his income wouldn't take too harsh a dive, as well as cater to his abilities. Claire Saunders had offered little more detail, clarifying only the intention of Impega, informing him that more dedication was required before he was told any more about this mysterious offer. From what the letter suggested Von Kelsig imagined it to be some kind of trial or experiment involving isotonics or electrolytes or some kind of scientific sport supplement, or perhaps he was being lined up for some kind of endorsement. Whatever the case may be if it offered better options and the right amount of money then he might very well consider a change of career. "Can't make a living out of being a lab rat..." He muttered under his breath, clapping a hand over the steering wheel and sighing as he cruised home. Fifteen minutes later he pulled into the driveway of his stately country home. With all the troubles on his mind thoroughly thought through Von Kelsig took a deep cleansing breath, invigorated by the rose-scented breeze blowing through from the back garden and carrying the aroma of the flowers over him. "Honey, I'm home!" He bellowed as he walked through the front door, chancing a cheery welcome even though only a couple of hours ago Gloria had stormed off after their argument. There was no answer from any room in the house. "Gloria! Hello? You in?" Von Kelsig shouted up the stairs, freezing for a moment and waiting for any sign of activity. Nothing. Sucking air through his teeth he furrowed his brow and went to the kitchen. He didn't expect to see the note lying there on the table, corners turned up and slightly crumpled like some kind of gnarled hand. Gone out for a while, Back for dinner X He wasn't any kind of forensic expert, but that didn't stop him making the assumption that Gloria had left the house in as bad a mood as she had been in earlier that day, judging by the vague nature of her note. The kiss at the end didn't fool him, and crumpling the piece of paper up he tossed it into the bin and glanced up at the time to find that dinner would be another couple of hours away. A sense of defeat hung over him as he skulked through the house to his private room, closing the door tightly behind him and sliding in behind the bar. Gripping the wooden frame of a seemingly random portrait hanging above the spirits, Von Kelsig tilted it a quarter inch and pushed up ever so gently until he heard a click. At once the crooked picture swung away from the wall revealing a safe. Pressing his pass code into the keypad on the door, Von Kelsig opened it up and popped the engagement ring inside next to a hefty pile of cash earned from his merc work in PRIME. Gazing upon the cash for a moment he snapped out of his daze when he looked back at the ring case, grunting before closing the safe up and retiring to the sofa with a dry Martini on the rocks. "Gloria Carmichael......will you marry me?" He muttered between sips of his drink. For all the worries in his head the corners of his lips turning up slowly into a smile was the only sign he needed to reassure himself. Finishing his drink he turned onto his side and closed his eyes for a nap as another chill ran up his spine and he nodded off smiling like the Cheshire cat. ---- "It's just hard sometimes you know?" She said with a weary sigh. "I wouldn't know, I've never been in a situation like that." He replied, inching closer on his seat by a bare centimetre and gazing at her big bright eyes. "God I just get so angry!" She hissed, burying her head into her hands and staring at her cigarette, the smoke snaking up from the ashtray and swirling into a billowing cloud around her. "It's alright to be angry, just let it all out." He echoed, licking his lips and studying her features while she sighed and took another drag from the cigarette. Gloria grit her teeth and pursed her lips, looking up at the ceiling of Larry's office as her eyes welled up and he watched as she swallowed hard and fluttered her eyelids to dry her tears before they fell. Reaching out he placed a hand on her shoulder and pressed gently. She turned to him and managed a smile of sorts, her lips quivering. "He's tied to that damn career of his and no matter how many times he says he'll think about a change he goes back out on the road for days, even weeks at a time and I'm left to eke out a life by myself while he goes and wrestles on television." Gloria thumped the table with a flat palm and winced as the sting filled her hand. "I won't tell you to calm down, but just stop for a minute and think about yourself for a change. Why punish yourself? You're young, beautiful and talented. You're an excellent hairdresser and in no time at all you're going to be a professional cutter and stylist. I'm proud of you, the girls are all gushing about you and the only time I don't see you with a smile on your face is when you leave at the end of the day." Larry said, taking a much needed breath at the end and sharing a gentle chuckle with Gloria as she wiped her nose and took his words to heart. "That's kind of you to say." She whispered before an empty void came over her. It was the same feeling she got at the end of a working day knowing all that waited for her at the house was a ready-made dinner and a big screen television with nothing worth watching. Occasionally she would catch ReVolution on the FX channel or watch a previously recorded episode and watch Von Kelsig. Other times she milled around the house aimlessly doing what little chores kept her mind busy. "I mean it." Larry continued, staring her dead in the eye and watching as she came to realize it too. "You're a fine woman, Gloria and it's not right that you feel like this. In my opinion it's up to your man to sort things out at home but as far as everything else goes..." Larry paused and took Gloria's hand in his, holding it tight and staring at her with conviction. Gloria glanced at her feet before meeting Larry's gaze and taking a nervous breath. "If you need a shoulder to cry on or a friend or just someone to be there you can always call on me. OK. I want you to know that Gloria. You don't deserve to be left on the sidelines while your boyfriend the "T.V. star" gets to live his dream. Take control of your life and do what you want, be the person you want to be. Do you want to be lonely and sad at night for the rest of your life until the day you become a full time carer for a broken old wrestler?" said Larry, his tone writhing with pressure and disguised by the concerned look on his face and the loving hand on Gloria's. She took a moment to reflect, maintaining a look of hopelessness while Larry watched her. It wouldn't do any harm to spend a little time with a friend if her own partner couldn't be around to be with her. Larry was obviously concerned with her well being, and he had been strong support ever since offering to train her up to become a proper hairdresser. She studied him; his stiff upper lip indicating his sincerity, the watchful stare of his caring eye. She felt the hairs on her arms standing on end and her cheeks blush at being tended to by someone like Larry DeLancy. He was offering friendship and a body to fill the void by her side where Von Kelsig should have been. "You got a point, Larry." She said finally, stifling a final tear before composing herself. "I know. Now...how about tonight we go out for a meal and get your mind off things?" "Oh, well I-..." "My treat. Come on Gloria we'll make a night of it, have some fun. We can ask Donna or Becky if they want to join us?" offered Larry, navigating the split second maze of options to put Gloria at ease and have her join him at dinner. "Well alright, that's really kind of you. I'm OK with just the two of us going, I think Donna would probably just make things...loud." Gloria said with a chuckle, to which Larry laughed. "We're gonna have a great time Gloria, I'll see to it personally." The confident smile filled Gloria with all kinds of hope and excitement. A problem in her life had been solved, the gap could be filled and she didn't have to worry any longer about being lonely. Her boss of all people had handed her a literal lifeline, and as she got up and left his office she couldn't have been more appreciate of Larry DeLancy. ...Larry on the other hand kept his eyes fixed on Gloria's backside as she left the office, his blood pumping faster now that everything was set. Grinning from ear to ear, he straightened his face all at once back to the supportive pout and the inspiring nod as Gloria turned and waved goodbye. It was with one wink that the locks broke loose, the walls caved in and the house came crumbling down. Larry DeLancy's demons were running wild and free.
