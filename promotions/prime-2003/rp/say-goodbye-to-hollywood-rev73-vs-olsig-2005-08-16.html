Say Goodbye to Hollywood (Rev73 vs. Olsig)

16 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-16-05 Say Goodbye to Hollywood SAY GOODBYE TO HOLLYWOOD By Danny Ferguson. FADE IN 1. INT. RESTAURANT - DAY We see a flustered, 'flamboyant' MAITRE'D glancing at the clock, absent-mindedly tapping a menu against his stand. He's dressed as typically as the position calls for, and sports a pencil-thin mustache/soul patch combo. The kind that makes you cringe and wonder if you've ever made a decision on par with that mistake. A few seconds pass, and he sighs, giving a haphazard wave to a white-shirted waitress wandering by. WAITRESS Who's coming in this afternoon? The maitre'd gives a smirk/smile that seems too enthusiastic. MAITRE'D Danny Ferguson is having a late lunch meeting in the V.I.P. roooooom. He accentuates every syllable of the final two words, speaking with his hands, and of course, because he's a stereotypical, one-dimensional gay character, wishing he could do much more with those hands. WAITRESS Danny Ferguson? Didn't he just sign with some wrestling company? MAITRE'D (scoffing) Uh, try like a week ago, Suzanne. Read the fucking papers. Turning her nose to match his disgusted expression, the WAITRESS continues on her way, leaving our unfortunate hero to his own devises. As he continues his impatient demeanor and nondescript banter with passersby, a voice begins. The NARRATOR is a smarmy kind of voice, friendly but acerbic, very Matthew-Perry-esque. NARRATOR (V.O.) It was a Sunday afternoon in Los Angeles, where any worship was purely nonsecular... ANGLE - RESTAURANT, an overhead shot as disembodied voices gush about how great someone is. NARRATOR (CONT'D) ...and everyone was 'at rest.' This, of course, only meant that the topics of conversation were people who WEREN'T at the table. One such topic was Danny Ferguson, who'd been cause for his fair share of gossip around town in recent weeks. CUT TO: INT. CASINO - DAY We get our first glimpse of DANNY FERGUSON as he sits at a poker table, in costume and character for his breakthrough movie, Hard Money. That costume is a black polo shirt with the collar up - like a tool - and a pair of mirrored aviator sunglasses. The other players around the table - and in the right corner, part of the movie's camera - are reflected in the glasses. The movie's title appears at the bottom of the screen. DANNY (AS CONNIE BLACK) Hit me. He breaks into a huge fight scene as the narrator continues. NARRATOR Earlier that year, Ferguson hit it big by playing Conrad 'Connie' Black, a World Series of Poker competitor who uncovers a Vegas terrorist conspiracy during the tournament. The movie shocked most critics when it succeeded despite their best efforts. Standing amidst a pile of bodies as card flutter to the ground, 'CONNIE' slowly removes his sunglasses. DANNY (AS CONNIE BLACK) (enunciating awkwardly) Everything's. Coming up. ACES. NARRATOR (CONT'D) This came on the heels of several smaller roles in which Danny could only be described as 'That Guy.' JUMP CUT TO: A montage of clips from several other movies, named along the bottom like before, featuring DANNY looking virtually the same, aside from small differences like a wardrobe change or a fake mustache. NARRATOR (CONT'D) And those, of course, came only after he had spent three years as the star of the forgettable family sitcom 'Four Is A Crowd' CUT TO: A still shot of a very young DANNY surrounded by a family that, conspicuously enough, looks nothing like him. Oh, sitcoms. BACK TO: INT. RESTAURANT - DAY The MAITRE'D cranes his neck to look out onto the terrace, then begins squealing and waving his hands. As much as I hate to send you on another jump cut, that's where we're going. JUMP CUT TO: EXT. LOS ANGELES - DAY Eminem's 'Say Goodbye to Hollywood' cues up as a dark green H2 rolls to a stop in the cul-de-sac (giggle) just outside the restaurant. In SLOW MOTION, the front doors pop open and two masses of humanity pour forth. Like an overstuffed can of spam, the twin BODYGUARDS step into the LA sun and give the shocks on the H@ a breather. Let's step back a bit. Calling them 'twins' is a stretch. Aside from the matching black suits with green ties, they are a bit different. JIM 'POLAR' PIBB is Caucasian and flaunting it, not bothering with the fluffy Hollywood tan that every other 'white' person in town has. He stands at 6'6 and 300 pounds. DAMETREYUS FUQUEIAWYTAS is, um, not Caucasian. He is 6'7 and 315 pounds, though. The two men straighten their suits and adjust their sunglasses. Finally, DAMETREYUS, who was sitting in the passenger side front, turns and opens one of the H2's back doors. Out steps DANNY FERGUSON, the star of our show. SPLIT SCREEN: Live action DANNY vs. Still shot, sitcom DANNY. NARRATOR Finally tasting the success and fame he'd craved for several years, Danny had been treating it like a cheap buffet, constantly keeping his plate full. Considerably smaller than his hired protection, he squints behind his now-trademark aviators, then motions for PIBB to stand in the path of the afternoon sun, casting a shadow over the Superstar. He's not dressed as nicely as the bodyguards - in fact, he's not dressed appropriately for the restaurant at all - but he doesn't seem to care. NARRATOR Since the success of Hard Money, he simply could not be seen in public without at least three attendants. Today's meeting was intended to add yet another member to his personal staff...a wrestling manager. As he steps from the H2, DAMETREYUS shuts the door...accidentally slamming it on the fourth occupant of the vehicle. As he positions himself on the other side of DANNY, the star looks around, searching for his manager, VICKI SIGUCHI. The sound of a door slamming comes from the other side of the vehicle as VICKI appears, rubbing her head. DANNY What the hell's wrong with you? Thinking too hard? The foursome begin walking towards the restaurant. PIBB tosses the keys in stride to a valet. He then proceeds to shuffle along, speeding up to maintain a shadow on his charge, and then slowing down to allow DANNY to walk at the head of the pack. This dance proceeds all the way to the door of the restaurant, the name of which we never see. MAITRE'D (bubbling) Your table is ready, Mr. Ferguson! DANNY (blowing past him) Don't tell me that like it's news. Of course my fucking table is ready. CUT TO: INT. RESTAURANT - DAY DANNY brushes through the establishment without taking time to regard anyone. DANNY (CONT'D) If my table wasn't ready, then it'd be news. Then it'd be Mr. Whichever-One-of-Will-And-Grace-Was The-Gay-One over there calling home to see if they have jobs at McDonald's. Then it'd be something worth fucking talking about. I don't show up 15 minutes late so my table won't be goddamn ready... People look up from their tables as the group passes through, either catching wind of DANNY's tirade or having themselves spun slightly as the girthy BODYGUARDS try to wedge between the narrow passageways. VICKI SIGUCHI, however, dodges artfully between diners, shaking hands and keeping those connections strong. CUT TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY As the BODYGUARDS and DANNY push into the VIP Room, there's already a man seated at the table. Wearing a wrinkled baby blue blazer and a pair of plaid leisure pants, REGINALD DELRAY is in his mid-forties. He stands, dropping the piece of bread he was gnawing on and ignoring any crumbs it left behind. DANNY (wincing at the sight) You must be Mr. Delray. NARRATOR Reggie Delray was, at one time, considered on of the top managers in the professional wrestling industry, most notably for his achievements as manager of legendary tag team The Midnight Train to Georgia. JUMP CUT TO: I/E Several still shots of DELRAY posing, wearing the same 'suit' and holding his trademark 9-iron, with two not-so-musclebound men sporting blue tights with yellow locomotives on them. 'Midnight Train to Georgia' by Gladys Knight & The Pips plays. NARRATOR (CONT'D) The MTTG, named after the famous Gladys Knight & The Pips song, found its name after legal action forced the discontinuation of its previous choice. Ironically, the team never used its namesake as a theme song en route to its 17 World Tag Team Championships. They used Skynyrd. BACK TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY NARRATOR (CONT'D) On any other day, Reggie's attire, which never strayed from his in ring persona, would get him barred from the establishment. However, by informing the staff he was with Danny Ferguson's party, he essentially told them that he had no idea what the term 'proper dress code' meant, and thus granted himself immunity. REGINALD DELRAY Call me Reggie. The manager and the actor shake hands and take seats at the table. The BODYGUARDS remain standing near the doors. REGGIE eyes them cautiously. REGGIE You expectin' trouble? DANNY, fixated on the amounts of bread that have been deposited around REGGIE's mouth as opposed to in it, snaps to attention. DANNY What, them? Nah, I just don't like letting them sit. Makes them lazy. They need to be on their toes. SHOT OF: Jim Pibb's face close up, trying hard to bite his lip and mask some sort of pain. JUMP CUT TO: INT. DOCTOR'S OFFICE - DAY Pibb sits on an examination table while a doctor looks at an X-ray of his knee. DOCTOR Mr. Pibb... He pauses as he realizes the novelty of the name. DOCTOR (CONT'D) ...You need to keep off your toes, and your feet entirely. The strain on these ligaments may soon become to much to bear. BACK TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY A tear rolls down Pibb's cheek, but he's able to mask it as the doors to the room open up. VICKI SIGUCHI steps into the room, followed closely by another man who seems vaguely familiar. ALLAN NORTH is DANNY'S agent, and is dressed for the part. He strokes a goatee upon sighting REGGIE already seated. ALLAN Mr. Delray, nice to finally meet you. I'm Danny's agent, and this is his manager, Vicki. REGGIE stands and shakes hands with VICKI, then with ALLAN. On the latter, he slows down, giving an inquisitive look. REGGIE Have we met before? ALLAN (looking annoyed) Of course not. REGGIE Sure? You look familiar... NARRATOR Allan DID look familiar, but the two had never met. JUMP CUT TO: I/E. On a porn set, a blurred image shows what looks to be ALLAN thrusting away on a heart-shaped bed. Women pant and moan as a soundtrack. NARRATOR (CONT'D) Reggie's feeling came from the uncanny resemblance that Allan bore to his brother, aging adult film actor Peter North. DISSOLVE TO: I/E. A side-by-side still showed Peter and ALLAN. A magistrator style arrow points out the small scar on ALLAN's forehead, as well as the goatee. BACK TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY NARRATOR (CONT'D) Even though Peter's name was a stage name, Allan had adopted the same surname to avoid suggestions that he was his brother posing as an agent. It was complicated. ALLAN pulls his hand away and sits down. REGGIE, still giving him the eye, slowly returns to his seat as well. REGGIE Can someone explain to me why I was flown all the way to Los Angeles for this? REGGIE typically made his home somewhere around rural Indiana, but even he couldn't normally remember his address. VICKI I can handle that. Reggie, we flew you out here because Danny needs an experienced wrestling manager in his corner. There's no one on the current staff that understands the ins and outs of the wrestling industry. REGGIE And you picked me? VICKI We started at the top. ALLAN And the top three were unavailable. REGGIE thinks about it for a minute, the insult from ALLAN going completely over his head. REGGIE Well, what is it I'd be doing? I mean, do I need to speak for your guy? Danny took this opportunity to pipe in. Meanwhile, a young WAITER serving some appetizers entered the room. DANNY Fuck no, buddy! I can talk for my damn self! (motions at the waiter) Watch this. As the WAITER approaches, DANNY slaps the tray out of his hand, suddenly raising his voice to a yell. DANNY What the hell do you think you're doing, you inveterate MORON! I ought to beat your sniveling ASS. NARRATOR Ubfortunately, Danny's timing could never be considered impeccable, and it just so happened that he'd chosen one young waiter who'd been pushed too far. JUMP CUT TO: INT. KITCHEN - DAY 'Earlier that day' flashes at the bottom of the screen, as the waiter in question is gesturing wildly at the waitress we saw earlier. WAITER I swear, if I get talked down to by one more so-called big shot, I'm going to throw down. BACK TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY The WAITER tears off his apron and fires back at the Superstar. WAITER Why don't you just fucking BRING IT ON, nancy boy! NARRATOR Not prepared to back up his words quite yet, Danny did what he normally would. DANNY snaps his fingers. Nothing happens. He coughs a little and gives a head not, clearly looking back towards DAMETREYUS, who is clueless. He claps his hands and does the head nod again, gesturing to the waiter. DANNY (sighing) Dam...hit him. Instantly, DAMETREYUS lashes out and catches the waiter on the jaw, dropping him like a sack of bricks. The bodyguard returns to his posed position, and DANNY's staff shakes their heads in disapproval. ALLAN That's going to go on our tab, Danny. Another waiter's medical bills. DANNY Hey, he attacked me and you saw it. Besides, I can pay it off with video game royalties. NARRATOR Danny mistakenly assumed he would be receiving money for the hot selling Xbox Live version of the Had Money video game. This was despite the fact that he never did any voiceover work for the game. JUMP CUT TO: I/E. A screenshot of the Hard Money video game, and the digital version of the Connie Black character. Poor animation shows him throwing his cards down at the table. CONNIE BLACK (in a voice that is so clearly Gilbert Gottfried it's ridiculous) Hit me. BACK TO: INT. VIP ROOM - DAY REGGIE I'm still a bit confused as to why you need me. DANNY We'll get to that later, Reg. You're hired. The rest of the staff beams and begins clapping, even the bodyguards. REGGIE seems a little perplexed, though. He claps half-heartedly along, but mostly looks baffled. REGGIE So why did I fly out here? Everyone starts to rise from the table. REGGIE Aren't we staying for lunch? DANNY comes around the table to put his arm around his new manager. DANNY Reg, you've got a lot to learn about Hollywood... More nervous laughter as they begin to shuffle out. REGGIE I don't understand... DANNY I'll explain later, right now we've gotta get out of here before they notice the waiter's gone. The scene pans up and out to the wide shot of the RESTAURANT. Team Ferguson walks through the crowded restaurant, shaking hands and waving. JIM PIBB bumps into and drags a table for a few feet as we... FADE OUT.
