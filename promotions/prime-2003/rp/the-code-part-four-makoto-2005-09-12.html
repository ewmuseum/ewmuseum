The Code (Part Four: Makoto)

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 9-12-05 Bushido For the uninitiated, a code of ethics that prevailed among the warrior class in feudal Japan...the code of the samurai. This is the story of Kyle Lamen, the story of a man who unintentionally lives by this code. Not because he read it in a book, or saw it in a movie. But because inherently, it is his way. The Six-String Samurai. The seven virtues of Bushido: Jin (Benevolence)Rei (Respect)Chuugi (Loyalty)Makoto (Honesty)Gi (Rectitude)Yu (Courage)Meiyo (Honor) * * * * * THE CODE: MAKOTO 09.11.05. New Fucking Jersey. Kyle Lamen doesn't think when he talks, so sometimes, he keeps his mouth shut, even when a friend is yelling in his face. “Spill, will ya?” In Red Bank, New Jersey, there's a little restaurant off the beaten path, across the street from the epic Galleria mall, which is a renovated factory. If you wander a bit on Broad Street, you may see a small restaurant called 'Modern Osaka.' The design out front isn't ostentatious, but boasts the name of the restaurant clearly and distinctly. And if you happened to pass by the place on the night of September 11, 2005, you'd see two young men arguing in front of the restaurant. “I'm telling you, if you don't give it up right now...” Naota Mizuno didn't exactly look resplendent in his navy blue terry-cloth bathrobe, but to be honest, it did look a bit more formal than the ones he wore of the zebra or leopard-print varieties. His face was a little screwed up in anger, and a fair bit of spittle flew from his lips. “Naota, calm down. Your autographed poster of Megumi Hayashibara isn't damaged or anything. Ikko took it as a joke, and yeah, it obviously wasn't that funny. But it's a poster, chill out a little, okay?” Naota gave Kyle a weak punch in the bicep, and pulled his hand back in pain. Hey, he may not be terribly muscular, but Kyle's still pretty strong. “Oai, why couldn't you have told me in the first place, man?” Kyle shrugged. “It WAS kinda funny, seeing you freak out at first. Who'd've thought that a tough guy like you would get so worked up over an idol singer, y'know?” Naota screwed up his face, trying very hard to be mad at the likable, affable “eVolution of ReVolution.” “Fuck you, man. You sit out here for a minute, I'm gonna go get that fucker.” Naota stormed inside Kyle's restaurant, leaving Kyle alone in the slightly chilly September air. Touchy. Hey, had to be honest with the guy...wonder how Sarah's doing? Kyle sat on the curb, staring out towards the monstrous Galleria, all brick and majesty. He pulled out his black Razr (and in doing, no-sold Ellis Easton) and hit the first of the quick-dial keys. It buzzed for a few moments, before a light female voice picked up on the other line. “Kyle, I promise you, I am NEVER running out to pick you up Junior Mints after eight PM ever again, you nerd.” Kyle smiled a little, in spite of himself. “Sarah, it's not about that.” The voice on the other end of the phone line gave a deep, relieved sigh. “Thank God. If I've ever had to be around someone so-” Kyle just simply cut her off. “I'm really sorry.” The voice on the other line was quiet for a moment, then pressed forward. “Uh, okay. What'd you do this time?” Kyle's eyes rolled back. “Nothing. I mean I'm sorry that I'm so difficult to work with. I know I've got plenty of faults and, well...I'm sorry. I'm sorry.” The voice on the other line was silent for nearly half a minute, and Kyle was slightly afraid the line had gone dead, but he thought he could hear Sarah's faint breathing on the other end. “Um, I dunno what to say.” Kyle sniffed a little, clearing his nose and throat. “Well, anything would be good. I mean, I'm serious about this.” A cough came through on the other end of the phone. “Um...well, I guess it's okay. I mean, I could be stuck with worse, right?” Kyle couldn't help but frown a little in disappointment. “Yeah. Guess you could. Look, I'll talk to you later, okay-” Sarah's voice rang true through the phone line. “I'M SORRY TOO!” This, to say the least, was not what Kyle had expected in the slightest. “What?” A long, lingering sigh of distress came from Sarah over the phone. “You heard me. I complain a lot, but you're not the worst client I could possibly have. Granted, you're also my first, so I have ABSOLUTELY no frame of reference to base this on, do I? But you're not such a bad guy, Kyle. I give you too much shit over what you do...and I guess I'm sorry too.” Kyle was struck speechless (no mean feat) for nearly a split-second. He spoke the next few words in a hushed voice, almost a whisper of disbelief. “You're kidding with me, right?” “Don't make this any harder for me than this already is, okay? You know that the plane conversations have been easier recently, I've used less profanity in public...it's probably just getting used to being around you, I think. Anyhow, whatever it is, I'm not necessarily sure I like it, but...” Kyle grinned. “But you're actually starting to like me?” Kyle had to pull his ear away from the phone, in hopes of retaining hearing in his right ear. “KYLE LAMEN, I SWEAR TO FUCKING GOD THAT IF YOU HIT ON ME ONE MORE TIME, I WILL GIVE YOU A GHETTO-RECTOMY!!!!” A WHAT!?! “A WHAT!?! There was a brief pause, and both Kyle and Sarah started laughing uncontrollably. “Jesus, Kyle. Don't you know anything?” Kyle adjusted the leg of his jean riding up on him. “Guess not. Pray tell...what the FUCK is a ghetto-rectomy?” He leaned back, ready for a good explanation. “Well, I had this roommate at Rutgers, who told her boyfriend she'd give him a ghetto-rectomy if she caught him cheating again, so-” “Caught him cheating AGAIN? What's her dysfunction?” “Shut up and let me finish the story, okay? Anyways, I asked her what a ghettorectomy was, and apparently, that's reaching down someone's throat and pulling their balls out.” Dead silence for a few moments. I've GOT to use that in a promo. “Jesus, Sarah...I've GOT to use that in a promo on someone. Sarah laughed legitimately on the other end of the line. “If you put it on a t-shirt, I want 75%.” Again, there was another slightly awkward pause, as Kyle adjusted his collar, obviously a little uncomfortable. “Sarah, what're you doing tonight, where you're actually at home at this hour? The pause on the other line waas brief, and Sarah's voice was just slightly shrill. “Um, I've got a late date tonight that I'm getting ready for.” Kyle shook his head, reacting as if Sarah were right there in front of him. “Fuck that. I know you better than all this. It's ten-thirty...you're watching Desperate Housewives, aren't you?” Sarah hesitated on the other line, so Kyle continued. “AND...you're eating cookie dough out of the tube.” There was half a spitting noise on the other side of the phone. “ARE YOU FUCKING SPYING ON ME!?” Kyle couldn't help himself but to laugh. “Come on, you wish. You know I'm over at Modern Osaka, you could call and check.” “But...how do you know?” Can't believe her sometimes... “It's not like it's the same thing you were doing when we went over my September budget last week, or three weeks ago when you were having the really painfully bad dinner party...” Sarah cut him off, either not wanting to be reminded about the male escorts that Kyle invited over to the “party” she threw for her parents, or just because she had a different thought pop into her head. “Seriously, Kyle...what's the real reason you called me?” Kyle scratched absently at the back of his head, where a mosquito may or may not have made of with a piece of his blood. “Honestly? I called just because I miss you tonight.” Oh, fuck! Kyle quickly snapped the phone shut, and looked around with shifty eyes, to see if anyone just saw what he'd done. There wasn't anyone around, but Kyle looked no less relieved. What the fuck did I just do? Great. No way of getting back from that one. I don't even know why I said that. Yeah, I guess it's true...but...fuck! The phone buzzed to life in Kyle's hand, as his ringtone, a piece from Less Than Jake's 'The Science Of Selling Yourself Short' hit his eardrums. It's either that she's quitting, or that I'm getting a sexual harassment charge. One of the two, and I can't handle either. I can't fucking. He popped the phone open, ready to talk, but instead, it said that he had received a text message. Kyle crossed his fingers behind his back on his left hand, and pressed the “read” command with his right. I actually miss you too, jerk. Now I KNOW she's just fucking with me...right? Messing with him or not...one thing became very, very clear to Kyle Lamen, as he looked up into a starry Jersey night sky... Seth Masters isn't the only one who's got a friend this week.
