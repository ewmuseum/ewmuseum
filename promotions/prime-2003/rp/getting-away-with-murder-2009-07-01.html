Getting Away With Murder

1 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 1, 2009 One might ask themselves... what would a young woman in Miami living under her means need a Lithuanian war veteran to be her bodyguard for? Living in a crappy apartment on the east side of town, well under one that she could afford. Working in a restaurant from five till close, when she could live several years with her current bills without working a single day. Spending her free time putting her life in danger at bingo halls, where half the time she doesn't even get paid for her time. There was something that just wasn't right about that Elise Ares girl. At least... that's what they tell me. Some people say I must have a screw loose, and that my delusions of grander have kept me from ever reaching whatever potential I might've had. That in the end, I'm really just another rich bitch with her daddy's money making it by with a pretty face and a nice rack. I've been born into anything I could ever want after all, isn't that right? A spoiled little bitch. Why do I waste my time? You see, that's because I'm a winner. I get what I want, whenever I want... and there isn't a damn thing that anyone else can do about it. I might just be a little crazy, but perhaps I'm putting the pieces into place. One by one, building the puzzle that solves all of the questions the simpletons out there have been asking for years. But I'm just a little girl with a pretty face. What harm could I possibly do? Again, that might be what I want you to believe. The crowd is electric and on their feet in the bingo hall sitting in East Miami, Florida. Known as fondly as "The Furnace" the sweltering building is living up to its reputation this particular May afternoon as the temperatures reach into the 90s. Yet none of the nearly 300 people in the room seem to notice as they chant on their champion, "Marvelous" Michael Belfrey as he stands in the middle of the ring looking down on one of his two opponents. Bonecrusher was just a victim of the dreaded Marvelous Bomb... the devastating finisher of the Miami All-Pro Wrestling Champion. "Crusher is down! Crusher is down! With no one else in sight, this one has got to be over!" The sound of Doug Yentz, play-by-play announcer of the straight to DVD series of MAPW shows tries to scream over the small yet boisterous crowd. With sweat dripping down his face and soaking into the collar of his shirt, he's in the moment staring anxiously as the massive Michael Belfrey drops down for a cover. ONE! TWO! THREE... "Wait, what the hell?!" Could faintly be heard seconds before the once rowdy crowd deafens into silence and Michael Belfrey finds himself outside the ring staring at six foot five inches of pissed off. Staring eye to eye with the Lithuanian, Belfrey measures the stranger that stands in front of him... wondering who in the world he may be. "Who in the hell are you?" Belfrey screams, "What makes you think you can just come down here and touch me?! Huh?!" "I'm just doing my job." The man answers. His cold gaze is stoic. His face is emotionless as he looks back at the confused champion. "What job? What in the hell are you talking abo..." by the time he would finish his sentence it would be too late. ONE! TWO! THREE! A bell sounds over a stunned crowd who now looks on in silence. In the middle of the ring, even MAPW head referee Steven Blaine can't believe his eyes when his third hand hits the mat. "Swaggeriffic" by Verse rocks over the bingo hall as the winner rises to her feet, and what had just happened slowly began to sink in over the crowd. In disbelief Steven Blaire grabs the MAPW Championship and hands it over to the last minute match-throw in. In front of hundreds of fans and several scouts from big-time promotions all across the country, Elise Ares raises the title over her head with a smirk. Michael Belfrey looks into the ring in total and utter disbelief. This was to be his night to shine. This was to be his time to make the lasting impression on the talent scouts in the audience from SCCW, PRIME, GCW, and fWo. With his hands on his head, he doesn't even notice the nod towards Elise Ares from The Iron Wolf, standing next to him outside of the ring. Silence from the crowd begins to turn to hostility as The Swaggeriffic One prances around in the ring in a celebration that had to be seen to be described. The words from Doug Yentz summarized the moment. "Elise Ares has just stolen the MAPW Championship!" The choir of once joyous cheers turned into a dark symphony of hatred, anger, and disappointment. There was so much screaming that nothing stuck out in the hysteria. Soon the cheap chain-link barricades that surrounded the ring began to shake. Trash and cups still half filled with alcohol began to fly through the air in a show of disapproval. The smirk of the new MAPW Champion quickly turned to that of fear as she dove out of the ring, narrowly avoiding dozens of plastic folding chairs now raining from the sky. Grasping her MAPW Championship close to her chest, Elise looked over at Kazys Jankauskas who blocked any clear shot at her from the crowd with his massive frame. "You sure know how to please a crowd, don't you?" The Iron Wolf said as Elise looked up at him motioning towards the entrance. That was the only word to escape the lips of Elise Ares as she opened up the back exit of "The Furnace". Standing around were a dozen or so hardcore MAPW fans... and they were PISSED OFF. If their eyes were bullets, Elise would be on the receiving end of a firing squad. At this point she only wished she could have a blindfold on. If it wasn't for Kazys Jankauskas standing behind her, she probably would've ran back inside. Instead, she decided to go a different route as she showcased the title she'd just "stole" proudly over her shoulder. "Impressed?" She said with a smile, "You should be." "With your complete disrespect?" A fan near the front of the small crowd said, "You should be ashamed of yourself. How does it feel to rob a hard working, talented individual his shot at the big time?" Sometimes, Elise just doesn't know when to keep her mouth shut. "You know what?" Elise said patting the title on her shoulder, "I feel FANTASTIC." "You little bitch..." He said before winding up to punch the new Champion, Elise's eyes grew wide when suddenly The Iron Wolf leapt in from behind her and threw the man to the ground. The fan looked up in shock as another one went to attack Kazys from behind. Jolting his elbow back, the massive Lithuanian caught the fans nose... shattering it into a artistic display of crimson on a flesh canvas. "Any more questions?" Elise said as the fans quickly dispersed. A grin crossed her beautiful face as most of them ran, and some of them walked away... leaving their fellow patrons on the ground in pain. She couldn't help but giggle as she looked at he handiwork left behind by The Iron Wolf. "We better get out of here." Kaz stated, "We might be in trouble." "It was self defense." Elise said as she stepped over the man looking down at the blood in his hand, trickling out of his face, "Besides, this is exactly what I pay you for. Sometimes these fans can get a little over zealous. If you weren't around someone could get seriously hurt." "Like you?" Kaz couldn't help but answer. "I can hold my own." Elise rubbed the leather and gold on her shoulder, "It's these other lifeless slobs that you have to worry about. Spending all day rubbing one off to one of my posters in their mother's basements. I would've bloodied every last one of them if they thought they could take me on. I'm sure that wasn't all of their intentions though." At this point Kazys just wished she'd shut up. "I'm sure most of them just wanted a picture or an autograph... or God forbid touch me with their filthy, grubby little hands." Elise sneered in disgust, "But some macho guy always has to ruin everyone's fun. Trying to be Mr. Badass and go one-on-one with the champ. They never learn..." "I'm sure..." Kazys responded, wondering again how he ended up where he is. Oh that's right... the ridiculous salary. "You know..." Elise contemplated, "It's tough being me." "Imagine being me..." Kazys muttered under his breath. "What was that?" Elise questioned immediately. "Nothing." Kazys responded, "Nothing at all." ---- TWO: An Offer You Couldn't Refuse ---- Days like this is what make some people call beautiful Miami, Florida paradise. A blazing hot sun in the sky, beautiful white sand surrounding you, seagulls flying through the air and deciding to not poop on you. The sound of the ocean soothing your ears as the breeze blows across your bikini clad body. This was the life. With her eyes closed, the stress involved from her recent interview with the Miami Police Department involving the assault of two men outside of a bingo hall on the east side seemed to go away. Last week seemed like a lifetime ago, and now there were only two things in the world she cares about. Perfecting her tan and being the single greatest entity in the known universe. She dreams big. Starting to feel the burn on her stomach, Elise Ares sits up... brushing the sand out of her dark brown hair. Looking around she realizes that she's the center of attention for a group of teenage boys... usually just like she wants. Lowering her sunglasses she glares back at them with a pair of brilliant, large dark brown Asian eyes. "Do you boys need something or are you just gawking?" She finally spoke. The boys said nothing in return, just continued to stare at the former Miss Hawaiian Tropics beauty model turned professional wrestling extraordinaire. "Beat it, kids." She instructed, "You're blocking my sun." The boys turned around and ran away, one of them losing footing and falling face first into the sand. Boys not paying attention and falling down around her had become a bit of a habit in her life. Most people think she's just a pretty face, and trust them... she is a VERY pretty face. But beyond the beautiful face and perfect tanned Hispanic skin was what could only be described as a criminal mastermind. Certainly not graced with natural wrestling ability at birth, she chose to follow her father's footsteps into the industry with much resistance. The historic teachers of the honorable profession of Lucha Libre in Mexico aren't exactly willing to share their knowledge with a bikini model. It doesn't matter who their daddy is. Rolling over onto her stomach, she pulled the red bikini down to cover her butt all the way as she relaxed again under the sun. Her brown hair swept down over her face as she laid her head down to the side and closed her eyes. Taking a deep breath, she let the breeze blow over her body once again. Blowing away all the troubl... How can you call it a lifestyle if you don't live your life in style? How can you call it a lifestyle if you don't life your life in style? The lyrics of her entrance music "Swaggeriffic" by Verse made her eyes flash open as she reached over into her purse to grab it. The music continued to play as she shuffled through her belongings. Finally she grasped the cell phone buried at the bottom and flipped it open. "Yeah?" She said as she answered very unprofessionally. "Who is this?" A voice on the other side said. "This is Elise." She answered confused, "How did you get this number?" "I was given this number to contact Kazys Jankauskas through Miami All-Pro Wrestling." "You're not the police are you?" Elise quickly retorted. "No, no... not the police." The girl on the other side stated, "My name is Lisa Tyler. I'm Vice President of Talent Relations for PRIME." "And you want to talk to Kaz?!" Elise answered confused. "Is that a problem?" Lisa commented, "I would've called a different number but this is all they had." "No... no problem." "Is he around?" Lisa asked, "I'd really like to speak with him. I saw him in action at that show last week, the one where you snuck in and won the championship? He was pretty impressive. Certainly has the look and judging by the extra curriculars that went on with those fans he had to save you from, he has the skill to back it up." "Oh, he wasn't saving me." Elise chuckled, "He merely gets paid to protect me. He was a little over zealous." "So what is he like your bodyguard or something? Why do you need one of those?" Lisa Tyler questioned. "No, no." Elise lied, "I'm his... manager. Which means I'm more than willing to negotiate on his behalf." "Okay..." she answered, "We'd like to give Kazys Jankauskas a tryout match in PRIME. We'll pay mandatory minimum payment for participating. If he does as well as we think he will, he'll be offered a full-time contract to PRIME. Be on the road with us, television appearances. Give him a good shot to move up the ranks." "That sounds like a pretty good offer to me." Elise responds, "We just have two terms of service." There is a pause on the other end. Lisa Tyler certainly wasn't used to being told by the managers of possible recruits that they have demands of PRIME. "First off, Kaz doesn't go anywhere without me." Elise says, "Where Kaz goes. I go." "Alright." "Second off, we're a team." Elise chirps in, "Kaz would love to participate in PRIME, but only if I were to be his tag team partner." "The deal is for Kazys Jankauskas only." "Listen, you want the best performance possible out of your wrestlers... am I right? He has a bit of separation anxiety, you see. Being around me makes him more comfortable. When Kaz is comfortable, you certainly get a better performance. If you don't want to disappoint the board, I strongly suggest you give us a chance. I assure you Miss Tyler a team like us doesn't come around everyday. If you miss your chance to see what we can do in a ring together, you're really missing out. I'd hate for you to have to watch us on GCW Television and wonder what might've been." "So what you're telling me is the only way we're going to get to see Kaz in a try-out match is if he's in tag action with you?" Lisa Tyler verifies. "And you've already had offers from GCW?" "I assure you, you won't be disappointed." Elise responds, guarding the receiver on her phone from the breeze. "I better not be." Lisa explains, "I promised the board a tryout for Kazys Jankauskas, and if I bring them a tag team and you screw up... I'll make sure you never work for this business again. You understand?" "Trust me." Elise smirks, "I never disappoint." "I'll make travel arrangements for you to come out next week and tryout at our headquarters against another tag team." Lisa Tyler instructs, "I'll call you later this week to nail down the details." "Ciao." Elise states before she flips her cordless phone shut and jumps up into the air in celebration. "Yes!" "What are you jumping about?" The voice of Kazys Jankauskas suddenly interrupts her as he walks across the beach. "Where were you?!" Elise exclaims, "There were these boys here and they were just standing there undressing me with their eyes. I almost fell asleep, they could've raped me you know." "Right in front of a lifeguard?" Kazys says as he points directly behind Elise, to a lifeguard sitting on the top of a lifeguard tower about twenty yards behind her. "You don't know that guy, he might be a perv or something." Elise answers without looking. "It's a girl." Kazys answers calmly. "Anyway, that's not important." Elise changes the subject, "What IS important is that I just got off the phone with Lisa Tyler from PRIME. She wants me to come down to PRIME HQ and wrestle a tryout match. Said PRIME had scouts at that show you got us in trouble at and they were really impressed by my performance." "Seriously?" Kaz questioned. "Hey, I guess the scouts really do know greatness when they see it." Elise replied, "ANYWAY, good news. I pulled some strings and called in some favors, I got you a tryout match too... as my tag team partner." "We've never tagged before." Kazys answered, "What make you think we ca..." "Trust me Kaz, you'll do great." Elise reassured him before he could even finish, "Once we get signed to our big contracts. We'll win those tag team championships in no-time, and once we do... we'll be banking a ton of cash. We'll be able to get out of this place once and for all. And if you think I pay well... you should see the digits those tag team champions get." "I could certainly use a few extra bucks to throw around." "And you get to beat up people who can fight back." Elise egged him on. "I've always liked a challenge." Kazys smirked in response. "Sure, we'll go for it." "Super." Elise said before getting up, "Lets go swing by the boardwalk, if we're going to make a good performance, we're going to arrive in style. Plus I've got some cash burning a hole in my pocket." "Where'd you get that from?" Kazys asked curiously. "Remember that MAPW Championship I won?" Elise asked, brushing the sand off of her and adjusting her bikini as she picked her purse up out of the sand. "Yeah?" "I hawked it this morning." Elise grinned, "I'm never going back to that God forsaken hell hole again."
