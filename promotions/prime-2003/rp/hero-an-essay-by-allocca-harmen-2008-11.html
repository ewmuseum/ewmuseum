Hero : An Essay by Allocca Harmen

November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November, 2008 A hero is someone who never gives up, and never surrenders. My dad tolds(tells) me thats[that's] from some movie I am not old enugh[Misspell-enough] to watch. And I wonder why he don't let me watch it, when it has good messages to give. But, is maybe my dad being a hero by not letting me watch it? Anyone can be a hero if they want to be. It's just easier to chose[Misspell-choose] not to be. It's safer. I remember, running into the street to save a kickball from it's death by runway cars[runaway?], only to stop and chooose[Misspell-choose] to let it be sent sailing into the clouds. I never forgive myself for that, had it been a baby or something. A hero don't[doesn't] hesitate. I've hear my dad said[say] he "threws[throws] caution to the wind," which don't[doesn't] make sense to me. I think it is like the kickball thing. If I would have put my life on the line to save that ball I woulda have[would have] been a hero. But its[it is] a ball, not a baby. So a hero not only has to risk his life at a drop of a baby, but he had to know when to actually let something go. Something my mom saids[says] my dad doesn't understand. My dad's is a hero and a villian[sp-villain] on tv[capitalize-TV], but he don't let me watch wrassling[sp-wrestling] when he's being a foot. I dunno what my dad has done to have him disapear[sp-disappear] from my tv then, but it can't be that bad, can it? Can a hero stop heroing[not a word]? Can a man live as a hero, but be a baddy? I mean, y'know, you chose to be a hero. I says it before. Now... to become evil, do you just chose wrong? Is[are] bad just accidents? Or is bad on purpose, and accidents just, are what they are? So... I wish my mom would see my dad the way I see him. I wish my dad is the hero I see him as, and I just wish I'm not seeing something that's not there. -Allocca Harmen Teacher's Notes/Grade: B. Needs work on Tense and sentence structure. Spell Check is your friend. Choose. Not Chose or Chooose. Says/Said is a problem, as is doesn't/don't. If Allocca needs some one on one tutoring, I will be happy to provide it. More information to come on Report Card, the 12th of November. -Mrs. Amy Wastler
