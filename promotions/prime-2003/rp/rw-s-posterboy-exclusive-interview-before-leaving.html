rW's PosterBoy Exclusive Interview, before leaving

Date: ??? Exclusive Interview Joe Gibbs: This is James Gibbs with the first edition of rW’s new Insider Magazine. I am here with soon to be former rW superstar Posterboy. rW is a new federation on the rise in sports entertainment. The sky is the limit and to leave only after the first show, why? Posterboy: I was offered a spot on one of the top feds, today in wrestling. I could not pass this offer up, I wish nothing but good thing’s for rW and their superstars. James Gibbs: We are only weeks away from crowning rW’s first world champion. Rumors are you could have been a contender for the championship. You could go down in history as the first champion ever. Is that opportunity not good enough for the Posterboy? Posterboy: I am aware of that, and yes that opportunity is more than enough for me. Like I said this is not a offer that I can just turn down. I will be wrestling with some of the best superstars in this business today…Tony Rolo, Killean Sirrajin, Dusk, Xavior Kannon, Danny Ferguson, and also Tyler Rayne who just recently won the 2008 Dual Halo. I wish luck to all of the superstars who are in that championship tournament. James Gibbs: It could take years to get on top of an established federation like PRIME. In a matter of weeks you could establish a federation like rW yourself…is that not a fair offer? Posterboy: Yes it is. I guess it will just take me a little more hard work, and dedication to get to the top of PRIME. James Gibbs: If you made it to the title match and weren’t leaving rW, who do you see yourself facing? Posterboy: I really could not tell you. We have some good superstars in this tournament. But I have my eye on Arch Angel, he looks like he has what it takes to get there. Joe Gibbs: Angel definitely is a top superstars…in your eyes does Gunther Hunt have what it takes to walk away with this tournament? Posterboy: Yes…with hard work and dedication. Everyone in this tournament has a chance to win, it’s just who wants it more. Joe Gibbs: Well Posterboy it has been a pleasure to interview you and to have you apart of our roster. I wish you luck in your journey through the ranks of PRIME. Posterboy: No thank you rW. Thank you for giving me an opportunity that I know in a few years no one would give up. It’s been a great stay while I was here, good luck to everyone. Top 5 Gunther Hunt Arch Angel Silk Lucian Cane Posterboy -Fin.
