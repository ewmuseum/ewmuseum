Slow Burn [RP for PTC Elite Championship / ReV 196]

7 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sunday, June 7th, 2009 Let me tell you about the time I beat Wyatt Connors... ****** 3 Days Ago [12:55 PM] The terminal doors hiss open to reveal a threesome considered unnerving in most public settings, including this one. With tattoos running out of his shirt like a wild rash, Tom O’Connor, known around the PRIME circuit as Scraps, checks the hallway through a dark pair of sunglasses and a few loose strands of his long, red hair. The scowl caused by paranoia and unwarranted violent thoughts never leaves his face, looking for the nearest bar. Behind him is an odd pair, to say the least. The big, brutish Veljumin Rippa stands beside the beautiful, yet rugged and dark Ruby, who measures up to around his chest without heels. The big man’s blond mane seems tame at the moment, wrangled by a hair-tie in a clean cut fashion; he wears a loosely buttoned white oxford, exposing his hairy and muscular chest, with the sleeves rolled to his elbows. Her frame is strong and sturdy, sexy and striking, but in many ways – scary as Hell. ****** [37 Minutes Later…] "So wait a minute." Scraps says with a cocked brow, closing the rear hatch of the PRIME-supplied Suburban, and looking at his manager. Rippa sits in the passenger seat, sipping on a Slurpee with the window open, watching the two and trying to find decent pre-match coverage of ReVolution 196. "You’ve got a match. In two days. Against Wyatt Connors, one of the sickest bastards in this business and you want us to take a road trip?" Tom steps up to the curb where he notices the venomous vixen’s eyes narrow in retort to the advice she hadn’t asked for. She opens her purse, removing a pair of sunglasses, then looks to the man she is coming to consider trustworthy through the tinted lenses. "Not us." The tone is cold enough to cut anyone’s ego in half. But Tom makes the most of it. He grins and takes a deep breath, looking off into contemplation that maybe she needs some time for herself. Clear her head; think things over. Lord knows he does it far too often. While lighting his Marlboro, the Irish Lion looks to her with an understanding expression. "Look Ruby…" Before the words can make a sentence a black 1966 Chevy Chevelle pulls up behind the Suburban. Ruby watches it come to a stop then trades a curious look with Tom before stepping toward it. He looks over his shoulder, through the shades, and then takes a long pull on his cigarette. She walks to the passenger side as a big, bald man in a suit steps out and walks to meet her. He stands at about a foot over her – which means he’s seven-feet tall maybe more. "Miss Ruby?" The accent, thick with British snare, was tough to make out at points. "That’s right. You’re not Swisher. You seem a lot bigger than Bruno but-" "No. I’m not. Mr. Swisher sent me to make the exchange." "And you are?" "…" A few seconds of suspicious silence pass by. Ruby glances to the car and then back at the man. The big man holds the keys up in front of her face, as if to make her think about them for a very long time, and then jingles them once. "Two days." She looks up to his pale face with eyes covered by dark glasses, glaring down at her. He drops the keys quickly, causing her to react and look away from the big man. Ruby holds them in her hand, briefly, then looks to Tom. The redhead watches the big man walk toward the airport. "Hey, buddy. Where you goin’?" The big man stops his stride, seemingly annoyed, and looks for a long time at the smaller man without making a sound. He finally looks at Ruby through the dark glasses and then back to Scraps, who is still waiting for a response. "Back home." He looks to Ruby once again. "Where I hope to never see either of you two ever again." With that, the big Scot steps into the automatic doors and out of the immediate memory. Tom turns to Ruby, who is standing next to the black sedan with the door already open, and one leg already in. "Trust me. I know some people from around here and well… I’ve got business to settle… and a ghost to burry. You can bet that new gold strap your wearing I will be in Lexington and I will be ready. I didn’t spend my whole life working this business from the back door forward to let it slip through my fingers now. This time, I’m ready. Like Pierre, Montiero, and O’Neill know – Connors won’t last a round with me. … I just have to do this, Tom." He grins and then shakes his head, not knowing what to think or even how to think it. He stops laughing, looking up in all seriousness, and shoots an extended index finger in her direction. Letting the gesture settle in before speaking his mind, Scraps holds up two fingers on his hand instead of a straight index. "Two days." ****** [4:33 PM] The Chevelle stops and Ruby takes a deep breath, placing it in park, and looking into the rearview mirror with a deep sigh. She removes her sunglasses, realizing it is officially dark out, and runs her hands over her stress-riddled face. The sweat from the sun is enough to remind her that this is one trip worth making. She pulls on the turn key and the car rattles dead. Pulling on a lever below the seat, the back makes a sound that clearly means the gas tank was now open. She steps out and walks around to the pump. The credit card PRIME furnishes was paying off, recently, for small things like this. So there was no hesitation in the way she slid it in then out to fuel up. With the pump running, her hands were finally free of the wheel long enough to reach into her back pocket, removing an envelope with RUBY scribbled on the front. She rips into it and removes a letter. Reading it thoroughly, she falls back against the car in a deep trance. It is broken when the clicking pump startles her. She replaces the nozzle back into the pump, recaps the tank, and looks at the final charge to pretend she was being cautious of gas prices. She’ll argue the fact Premium saves the car that, in the end, doesn’t really matter to either party. It was a craft being able to bend and manipulate the pawns whichever way she chooses. Before making her way back to the driver’s side, she stops at the trunk. Looking at the handle with disdain, her eyes meet her hands, holding the keys to her curiosity. Did she really want to know what was in there? Did she really care? Her job was to take care of things in Morton’s Gap, Kentucky. Anything else was grey area. This was a black and white deal. Firing off the rule-breaking snarl, Ruby stabs the key into place. She twists like an executioner might flip the switch, sealing the fate of others by their split-second actions. The gaze of the Muse keeps on the contents of the trunk and its awe-inspiring form. Reaching in, she pulls out what she can only believe to be a Jungle Knife. A machete. Stains from past encounters, blood covers it in a tribal, gladiator-like pattern. The type of blood stains that fill Ruby’s gut with a sickening notion…followed shortly by a warm one. "One more time, kid. Like old times." She spins around, looking to see if anybody saw what she was doing. Putting it away, quickly, Ruby slams the trunk and darts for the door. She slides in and turns the key over to fire up the newly replenished vehicle. She pulled out the piece of paper and takes another few seconds to memorize the instructions once again. ****** 2 Days Ago [2:44 AM] The rip-roaring down-throttle of a Harley-Davidson flutters through the open door of the Devil’s Playpen. A lone bike tears off into the long stretch of nowhere that is Western Kentucky in the darkness of the night. The known hot-spot for bikers and scumbags fits the stereotype as motorcycles and classics fill the parking lot. – one classic; one glossy, black 1966 Chevrolet Chevelle. It is the lone four-wheel and it sticks out like a black tooth of the grim grin that is the bar one-hundred and forty-nine miles west of Louisville. So it should be known it is no coincidence that these type of low-lifes that make up 99% of the patrons present today are indeed members of a certain motorcycle club. The Devil’s Doormen A gang notorious for letting rival members and anybody else in general know that they, as far as this is world is concerned, are the men who send you to Hell. A group of men, whose whores usually come town to town, litter this establishment in a way mice litter an alley. Today, the gang is here after a long trek back from Montana where they made a large purchase for some semi-automatic and automatic weapons, fifty kilos of pure cocaine, sixty pounds of Canadian-fresh marijuana, and, last but not least, six new choppers, custom made. There was also an initiation. Today is a good day. A day that is jovial and uncut; filled with celebrating and bonding. A day in which a young Doorman begins his ride into Hell with the rest of these damned souls. A day that, to those who survive it, will forever be remembered as a day this bar and these men would live up to their given names. ****** 2 Years Ago [The Devil’s Playpen June 5th 5:09 PM] "Look, kid. I know what you’re sayin’. And by all means, do what you got to. But you been ridin’ with us for what, eight years?" The gritty snarl of a woman scorn is enough to crack through a short exhaust like her Harley usually does. "Eight years too fuckin’ long, Eddy. Eight looong years." He bows his head, knowing full well what Ruby’s seen in these eight years is enough to send any normal woman running for the cops in no time. Nonetheless, he rests his hands gently on her shoulders trying to comfort her. "But they were some good years, kid." She wrenches loose and spins on a dime, scowling in his face. The black bandana isn’t enough to hold back her sleek black hair but the expression is clear on her face – pissed off. "Good? You call this good? I wanted to get into the business. Not the BUSINESS but the wrestling business. You" (pointing a fiery finger at Eddy) "made me empty promises. You brought me into this death show and made me believe I have some sort of… power… around here. It’s ridiculous. You lied to me. And I want out." The words cut like the blade she uses in Eddy’s heart. His face washes away all emotion as he blankly stares like a lost boy. She tries to be strong and meet his gaze but feels too compelled to comfort him. She looks away and begins collecting her things. "You want… out out?" "Done. Finito. I leave for Utah tomorrow morning." "Utah?" "I’m going to be the fifth wife to a Mormon senator." Eddy crosses his ink-laden arms. "I found a gig wrestling in a state-wide circuit." One hand slowly strokes his goatee as he searches his head for any type of words. Slowly, a smile cracks over his face. Ruby finishes what she needs to and meets him near the door, with the same smile spread over his face. "Finally lose it, Ed?" "You go ahead, girlie. Try and wrestle. You’ll just see that it ain’t the same." "The same? I sure hope not." "You told me time and time before…" Eddy pauses, pulling out a cigarette. He takes his time to light it, knowing that whatever he has to say is keeping Ruby around long enough for him to think of something better to do. Or just wants a cigarette. He offers one but she declines. He lights it with a black metal Zippo. "You don’t do this for no reason, kid. You do it for the thrill." Ruby thinks over what he says, adjusting her bandana back in place. She grins at him, sarcastically. "The thrill? Yeah. That’s right. I just have a thrill of a time runnin’ from the cops and fucking murdering the people you claim to be our enemies. you know what, Eddy? You’ve got a lot of fuckin’ nerve. Tryin’ to sell me this bullshit, still. After all this gang’s been through – losin’ Hiro, losin’ Tonya, losin’ the fuckin’ take – for you to try and shovel me shit as we stand here, today, is evidence you don’t know what you’re doin’ here. Kingston never should have left you in-" A hard backhand meets her mouth as previous Head Doorman’s name comes out. She glares at him in patience, licking her mouth to taste around for blood. He’s lucky that she didn’t find any. "Watch your mouth, kid. You might get hurt." He takes a slow drag off the smoke, blowing it directly in her direction. "You just take yer shit and get goin’. I ain’t got nothin’ worth sayin’ that can change yer mind, can I?" She doesn’t respond. Ruby takes three steps and hits the doorway. "Hey, kid…" The two lock eyes once again. A few seconds pass by as he keeps her waiting, still. "When you said ya loved me… did ya mean it?" She stares at him in a moment of thought. A nod comes from her in a positive tone. "I meant it just as much as I love this life." A smile crawls over his face as she steps out and into the upstairs hallway of the Devil’s Playpen. Eddy Rome walks to the doorway, leaning against it, and watches Ruby make her way for the double-doors below. She stops, looks around at the usual crowd of drunks in black leather. Good riddance, she thinks to herself. Turning over her shoulder, Eddy and Ruby lock eyes one last time. He grins and nods at her, giving a solid salute. She just raises her right foot, slamming it into the door. Filling the entryway with light and her life with gleaming hope, Ruby places a pair of black aviators over her eyes. ****** 2 Days Ago [4:54 PM] The severed limb still covered in black leather drops to the ground with a wet thud. The wooden floors don’t make for very stealth entrances so this one was pretty messy. Much like the jobs in High Falls and Kansas City. Regardless… it was the homecoming entrance Ruby called for. "Jesus Christ!" She held the tip of the sharp as a tack blade to the tip of the young, newly brought in recruit Jester. His eyes were wet and innocent, the kind you find in a scared puppy that tries to wrestle the bigger, stronger dog before knowing what it’s done. Ruby barks. "Where is Rome? Tell me now or so help me God I’ll cut your hands off." He begins to cry as she rolls her eyes. Another approaching member comes in quick from her left flank. Ruby turns, cracks him in the mouth with the handle of the blade, and then swipes down across his torso. He looks at the wound and then groans in agony, falling to the floor. She looks back to the scared puppy. "Just. Fucking. Tell me!" A slow clap comes from the staircase. It comes from Eddy Rome. He also happens to be holding a handgun about the size of a Tonka truck and is pointing it directly at Ruby. She lowers her blade. "Quick, kid. Knock her teeth in." Ruby scoffs at the fact this kid could do anything worth value to a gang that, when it comes down to it, can kill anybody it wants to. This kid couldn’t kill a steak. "Nice recruit, Rome. He’s got real potent-" A right hand clobbers Ruby in the face, splitting her lip and busting her nose. Rome blurts out in laughter, maniacally, and Ruby staggers back into a broken table. Jester, as he’s been named, grabs her by the hair and repeats the punching process multiple times. Rome begins laughing and dancing on his way down the stairs. "That’s my boy! Go get ‘er, kid! Show this bitch who she’s messin’ with." Ruby drops to the ground, dropping her blade, and holds her face. She is losing blood through her nose and mouth and cups it to gauge the amount but is quickly rocked by a hard right boot to the ribs by the young pup. She groans. "Fuckin’ prick! I gave you a chance to live." "Thanks bitch." Jester lifts his boot and aims for her face. Before he can land it, a large right hand crushes his own face and sends him flipping backward over the bar. He crashes into the liquor bottles. Rome looks to his right but catches what appears to be the same thing, causing the gunslinger to drop his Ace. Veljumin Rippa stands over the weaponless miscreant and lifts him by his collar. Hoisting him into the air, Rippa turns over to Scraps, who is tending to Ruby. "What do we do with this one?" Tom sits Ruby in a makeshift chair. He looks up at the half-awake Eddy Rome. Tom recognizes the patch is the same all around him. "He must be the Boss. I don’t know. Ruby? Ruby, can you hear me?" Through the half swollen, half bloody gaze she looks around. Noticing who is talking to her, a look of concern comes to her face. "Tommy? What are you doing here?" "What? Let you have all the fun? Get bent, Rube. I ain’t gonna let you go off into Kentucky’s wilderness alone. We’re a team. And teams take care of each other. I’m makin’ sure you get to that Connors match and we’re gonna make sure you take home that belt. Got it?" She smiles. Taking a few short breaths, Ruby giggles to herself before she speaks. "Plus we don’t want to see that big Scottish guy, either." The two laugh a bit but is quickly cut short as a loud crash comes from Rippa’s section of the bar. He looks over his shoulder to the two of them, who notice Rome is in a pile in the corner. "He was giving me that look. Sorry." Ruby glares at the pile in the corner and Tom takes note. He pats her leg. "Look, Rube. We’re here. We’ll be outside. I ain’t gonna tell you what you’re doin’ is right or wrong ‘cause... clearly you know how to do it. But I will say… make sure it’s what you want in the end. And make sure it hurts like Hell." Ruby watches as Tommy gets up and walks over to Rippa. The two share a quiet conversation before glancing at her and then nodding, leaving through the door and closing it behind them. She has no doubt that it is just Rome and her in this place with little effort from him to stop what’s going to happen. Ruby gathers all her might and stands to both feet. She winces in pain, feeling her ribs, and has some trouble breathing as she moves toward the bigger body across the main hall. She seems stronger and more able half-way across and then comes to Rome’s limp body. She clutches his collar and looks up the staircase. Grunting and groaning, Ruby drag Eddy Rome by his traditional Head Doorman leather vest up the stairs and down the hall. She drops him in the office and slams the door behind her. Looking around, she picks him up, grunting along the way, and throws him in the chair behind the desk. Unplugging lamps and telephones, she ties his arms and upper-torso with haste. The limp body begins to rock around. Rome’s eyes open just before Ruby darts out of the door. He looks around, noticing he is bound, and begins to struggle for freedom. He begins to scream and yell. Shortly, he hears another scream. The scream of a young boy being slaughtered like a sheep is by wolves. Soon, the thud of imminent doom makes its’ way up the staircase, getting closer and closer to Rome’s office. "I’ve been waiting to do this for a long time, Eddy. Now I get paid to do it!" Ruby stands in the doorway, covered in blood, and smiles through a broken smile. Her teeth are smeared with her own blood thanks to his now-dead recruit. His blood and the blood of others cover her machete and shirt as she raises it to point at the Doorman. "You sick bitch. What? Wrestlin’ not work out so you got to go and pull a hit on your old Boss? Fuckin’ rat! I’m gonna kill you when I get out of this!" More squirming and screaming from the leader eases Ruby’s tension. She feels the two need to catch up, so she sits on the desk and baits her victim like a snake does a mouse. "Oh! No, no, Ed. I’m wrestling, doing it a lot. Even have a big match coming up in Lexington. So I made it my mission to make a visit." "What? I got out of that a long time ago." "No, not you. That’s why I’m here. I have a match in Lexington in two days. It’s huge, Ed. Huge. I face off against Wyatt Connors. And guess what, pal?" The two exchange a bitter glance. "I’m going to win." Ed laughs then shakes his head. "Crazy bitch. Think anybody cares? No! Nobody gives a damn about any of that shit anymore." "I care, Eddy. I care more about that shit than I’ll ever care about this lousy dump. I also know that I am going to be walking out of this with a bigger bank account than the Doormen or the Playpen have ever had, win or lose. The win will be a double bonus. Which means I’m going to win, Eddy." She points the blade at him once again, causing him to turn away in disgust, and thrusts it closer to his face. "And after I win, I don’t want any dark secrets or rats coming out to nibble away at my winnings. You understand me? No people from my past coming in to blackmail me out of my prize and fame. Something I know scumbags like you pull daily. Not on my parade. No rain. No way." "So you want a truce? Fine. Just get the fuck out-" "Dream on, biker boy. I want your head." Eddy’s eyes narrow, feeling the cold hand of Death settle on his shoulder. "But… you just said you didn’t want me to do any of that shit. So I won’t. Take the bar, burn it. Take my bikes, sell ‘em. I’ll go legit and run off somewhere." Ruby gets in closers and presses the blade into her ex-lover’s cheek. The tip draws blood. Eddy winces but not very much. "Didn’t you hear me, Eddy? I get rich win or loss. It’s far easier than just wrestling and winning." Ruby watches the naïve look of a young man lost in translation. She smiles at how fun this will be. "I’m getting paid to kill you." His eyes burst with suspicion. The leader of the Doormen squirms around more, kicking at her and cursing under his breath. She smiles and smashes her right boot into his chest, knocking the chair backward and sending him crashing to the ground onto his back. She hovers over him, inches away with the tip of her two-foot-long half long blade. Blood drips onto Rome’s vest at a dead pace. "I made a deal. Mostly to earn some extra cash but it was a fair deal. Fifty-five thousand large for your head. And there’s some real cute outfits I need for the next ReVolution. By the way, my boys are tag champs and plenty of people care about that." "Jesus Christ! Again with this wrestling shit. Just tell me one thing." She entertains the thought with a brief pause. "Who?" "It doesn’t matter, Ed. Russians for your information. But it was them or the Jamaicans on the North Side. This was on my Bucket List, old friend. It was only a matter of time before we had our last dance. To think that it’s for a fee is the irony, don’t you think?" Eddy shakes his head. A sharp, fleeting feeling of disappointment floods his mind. "You’re going to gut me for them? For those fucking Ruskies!? I trained you better than that. You were better than that, Rachel." Ruby’s ears perk up and teeth clinch at the sound of her real name. She brings the blade down into his stomach and wrenches it upward to its’ final resting place. She stares into his eyes and at the end of her past. "Heartless, kid … God… damn… heartless..." The sound of air escapes his lungs as Ruby sits back on the ground. She looks around her, noticing the office is the same way it was when she left. In fact, everything was the same. Her corner was still there and pictures of the two were everywhere. With her arms, resting on her knees, a soft sob grows inside the woman in red. She drops her head into her arms and cries audibly for some time. "It’s over, Rube." She looks up to notice the redhead standing in the doorway with a cigarette lit. He looks to the blood spilling out from the body nearby. When she notices he isn’t looking at her, Ruby wipes her tears and composes herself. Stopping to think of what to say, she looks at Eddy Rome’s body with a warm feeling of nothing in her thoughts. A man she met more than twelve years ago. A man she spent years of her youth with. A lover. A friend. The man she felt at her finest with. A dead man, who got too personal with the Red Devil, and lost his life for it. "No. It’s not." Ruby’s voice rings in shining optimism. Scraps cocks his head to the odd response, watching Ruby pick herself up off the floor. She sheds her blood soaked shirt and jeans, exposing her long, seductive frame. Covering her is a slender pair of black lace panties and bra. She takes a few attention-grabbing steps into the corner of the office and grabs a black dress form inside the locker reading ‘RUBY’ on the nameplate. She throws it on and uses a nearby towel to wipe the blood off her arms. She looks to Tommy. Realizing his manager is a Queen of All Trades (if he may be cliché at the moment) the redhead grins and stomps out his cigarette butt, shooting Ruby an ‘oh yeah?’ face. She storms past him and out the door, bumping his shoulder and knocking the bigger man into the door-frame. "It’s time to put this world on its ass. We started in Denver. Next stop is Lexington…" Scraps turns to follow but is abruptly held up when Ruby stops on a dime, spinning around with a look he’s seen in her eyes before. The same look she came to him with five months ago when PRIME had openings. The same look that she had when she told them to put Tyler Rayne through that double-pane window. The same look she had when she addresses PRIMEates about him and Rippa, stomping faces in. The look she has when she means business. "I’m going to put on a clinic against Wyatt Connors. Then everyone will give a damn who we are when I win that Elite Championship. For a long, long time." ****** ...because nobody will stand in my way. FIN
