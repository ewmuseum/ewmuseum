Fatherhood - Chapter 1 - Questions

9 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 9, 2008 HILO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT HILO, HAWAII JULY 9, 2008 11:00 AM Bags and luggage of various shapes, sizes, and colors fell through the opening of the chute and landed on the revolving belt. Bryan Dawkins stood nearby and waited for his own piece of baggage to fall into place on the slithering snake of luggage. After a few moments, a blue bag fell onto the belt and was picked up by PRIME’s Resident Hawaiian. Wandering through the airport, Dawkins’ cell phone began to ring. Pulling it out of his pocket and checking out the LCD display that read "Nikkie," he had a feeling as to what the call was about—his ride had arrived. Bryan flipped open the phone and was greeted by the sweet voice of his significant other. "Hey hun." "Hey Bry, I’m here. You ready to go?" "Yeah, I just picked up my bags and I’m headed toward the passenger pick-up area now. I’ll see ya in a few minutes." "’Kay. See you in a bit." *click* As he continued his venture through the airport, he felt the eyes of various passerby’s on him. A lot of the looks Bryan was getting were of the "What the hell? Isn’t he supposed to be in New Orleans?" variety. Yes, there are fans of PRIME in Hawaii. The fact was, the Flyin’ Hawaiian’s five and a half month-pregnant significant other was sitting outside in a car, waiting to take her "boyfriend" to one of her check-ups. PRIME was the last thing on his mind at the moment. Union Jack? Dawkins could care less about him. Chandler Tsonda? Bryan figured that if he needed anything, the Viet Viper might be there for him. Devin Shakur? The guy was odd. I mean, he wore guyliner BY CHOICE. Regardless, the guy had an "interesting" way to look at life. The only thing that mattered to Dawkins at the moment is the fact that by the end of today, he will have seen his unborn child. All things PRIME were no longer an issue; he was becoming a FATHER. --------------------------------------------- "So your trip was good?" questioned Nikkie, who was simultaneously cruising down the freeway on course to her appointment with her gynecologist. Bryan smiled. "Yeah, it was great. So how’s everything been going with you? Are you okay at home by yourself?" He was concerned, but it was understandable. Bryan was a full time wrestler, being out on the road pretty much all fifty two weeks of the year, only being able to make sporadic trips home, sometimes for only a day or two at a time. She lied. "I’m doing well. This whole ‘being pregnant’ thing isn’t as bad as they make you believe." In all reality, she was lonely. The only child of her family, she was living in a mid-sized apartment in downtown Hilo, not far from her parents house; it also wasn’t very far from Bryan’s family’s house. Coincidence? Possibly. Although she wasn’t very far from her own parents, or even Bryan’s parents, who loved her dearly, she still suffered from loneliness a lot more than she let on. She was strong, however, and she wasn’t about to let Bryan know that his being away was slowly taking its toll on her. "Well that’s good, hun. I’ve missed ya like crazy over the last few weeks. It’s been a while. Whenever I’m not dealing with work, I’m always thinkin’ about ya. Ya know that?" He looked concerned; like she didn’t know how much he honestly cared for her. Yes, this was somewhat of an accident; a result of a drunken hookup. But it’s not like the feelings weren’t, and aren’t there. Nikkie reached over and grasped Bryan’s hand and squeezed it tightly, while letting out a deep breath. "I know, babe. I know." This was getting too serious for Bryan. He had to break the ice. Holding onto his girlfriend’s hand and gripping tightly, Bryan lightened the mood. "So, is this guy gonna be shovin’ anything up inside you that I’m not gonna want to see? ‘Cuz if that’s what’s gonna go down here, I think I might just take a seat in the waitin’ room and wait ‘til you’re all done." He chuckled at himself; at least Bryan thought it was funny. So he kept going. "After all, shoving things up inside you is what got us here, isn’t it?" After slowing steadily and finally coming to a stop at a red light, Nikkie glanced at Bryan, sighed, and then let out a laugh. "Yeah Bry, they’re gonna shove all kinds of stuff up inside me…but I’m pretty sure none of it can get me any more pregnant than I already am, so I don’t think you’ve gotta worry, hun." Upon seeing the green light, Nikkie put her foot back on the gas and sped off onto the freeway. --------------------------------------------- Bryan lead Nikkie into the gynecologist office, where the waiting room was abundant with pregnant women and their husbands, boyfriends, significant others, and/or parents. Some second glances were given to Dawkins by some of the males in the room, who, again, must have seen PRIME at least on one occasion or another. Bryan flashed a quick smile to those who realized who he was, and proceeded to sit down in an empty seat while Nikkie checked herself in. Dawkins grabbed the latest copy of ESPN the Magazine and began to skim the articles and columns until seeing Nikkie approaching. She sat beside him and clutched his hand tightly as he went back to reading the magazine; she was nervous, and there were a million questions floating around in her head. How would Bryan react after seeing the ultrasound? Is the baby going to be healthy? Am I going to make it through the pregnancy? Is he going to stick around after the baby is born? If he does stick around, how is he going to keep wrestling AND be a good father at the same time? Was her apartment going to be big enough for her and the baby? There were so many questions, yet she didn’t have an answer to any of them. Bryan glanced at his partner, who had a look on her face that he had never seen before: fear. "Babe, you alright?" He gripped her hand tighter and looked deep into her eyes, and realized something that hadn’t hit him until that very moment. He loved her. Did she love him? Right now, there was no way to tell. Anything said by her during this nine month period could be fueled by the pregnancy and not fueled by her honest feelings about him. He wasn’t going to tell her; he’d rather wait until the baby was born, and her hormones were no longer effecting her judgment any more than they normally would. The only thing that was important at the moment was being there for her when she needed it. Right now was one of those times. "Talk to me, hun. What’cha feelin’?" His voice sounded genuine and concerned, which he was. This was the mother of his unborn child, and she obviously wasn’t in the right frame of mind. He gripped her hand tightly again and looked into her emerald green eyes. "Nikkie, talk." She looked back at her boyfriend. She loved him too. The problem with that situation is the fact that she felt the same way about it as Bryan did. Nikkie was afraid that any feelings that Bryan may be feeling could only be due to the fact that he got her pregnant, not because he really had true feelings for her. That being said, she wasn’t going to talk to him about it until after their child was brought into the world. He had enough on his plate, dealing with her, dealing with his job with PRIME, traveling all of the time, and keeping himself in shape, all the while trying to figure out how he’s going to balance the new addition to his family into the equation. It was a hectic time for everyone, and the questions just kept repeating themselves in her head. How would Bryan react after seeing the ultrasound? Is the baby going to be healthy? Am I going to make it through the pregnancy? Is he going to stick around after the baby is born? If he does stick around, how is he going to keep wrestling AND be a good father at the same time? Was her apartment going to be big enough for her and the baby? But something brought her back to reality. She snapped back to attention, and was looking at Bryan, who was just smiling back at her. He said something, but for some reason, it fell on deaf ears. She just smiled and gripped his hands tightly. Bryan let out a small chuckle. "Are we gonna go in, or are we just gonna sit in the waitin’ room all afternoon?" Again his smile graced his face as he peered at the mother of his unborn child. Nikkie just peered back emptily. She had questions. A lot of questions. And no answers. TO BE CONTINUED
