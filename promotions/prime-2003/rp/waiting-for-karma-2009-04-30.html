Waiting For Karma

30 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 30/04/2009 Sometimes it would seem like we are in control of our destiny. That we are able to change everything around us and ultimately take hold of our own lives. But this is often not the case, the world it is said is a strange p lace and often things happen for a reason. What if you were told that every single action you had taken in your life was part of a predetermined path, if this was truly the case then freewill is little more than an illusion. You can fight the tide as much as you\'d like, you can make your own conclusions as to the ebb and flow of the worlds doings. You can wake up each morning and fool yourself into the belief that you have control of your life. Yet the niggling question will always remain over what you actually are? Are we little more than helpless passengers that are along for the ride? Are we puppets of a larger game? No the big moments are going to come, you can\'t change that. It\'s how you react to those moments when they come. That\'s when you find out exactly who you are. Claire had been sat in the office for sometime now her hands had become more and more clammy with every passing second. Being around Stephen, the man in the plain black suit did that to her. The foreboding sense of knowing that she could be ended in one fell swoop by the small, quiet disconcerting man. She had been called in to see him straight from her mission to go see Kelly, Meat\'s wife. A mission which although had been a success, felt like nothing more than an extreme failure. It was in that small Colorado home, that she had seen a broken woman, a broken home and a family torn apart by nothing more than life. She had been sent there to reveal everything that had been happening and despite the fear that was flowing through her, simply could not bring herself to do it. The universe at large had already taken that mission on its own accord. With every minute that Claire sat with Kelly in her arms she grew closer to her, despite not really knowing her. She felt the pain that she was going through and could only sympathise. Yet now in the office, overwhelming fear was taking over. The company had ears everywhere, it was extremely feasible that Stephen had found out of her deciet and this was the final curtain. However the fact that Stephen was sat in front of her, made her doubt this. He had plenty of underlings ready and willing to impress who would be happy to do his dirty work. She knew the severity of the situation and yet, with every minute that passed could not help but think of Kelly, sat alone in that house with no-one there for her. The last she had heard of Chris, he was still locking himself away from the world and as far as she was concerned, nothing else had changed. “It would appear Claire, that we have a problem.” Stephen\'s words were calm and collected with a subtle hint of anger, which only people who knew him would have picked up on. Immediately Claire was on edge, her mind racing a million miles a second trying to process every possible outcome of the conversation, even planning exit strategies. “What do you mean Sir?” Whenever Stephen said there was a problem, there really was a problem. He was not a man to mince his words and knew how to spread panic and fear through those around him. If the black suited man had a problem, then everyone around him would sure as hell have a problem as well. Claire, who\'s eyes had not moved from watching Stephen\'s hands watching for any sudden movement could not help but thinking that she was to blame for something. She just wasn\'t exactly sure what. “It would appear that two days ago. Christian decided that he was going to quit. In his words he doesn\'t want to be a puppet any more. I take it that he hasn\'t mentioned anything of this to you?”Stephen\'s eyes burned deep into Claire\'s soul as though he could read her mind, prying away for any shred of weakness or hint of lie. Claire immediately felt herself tense up. What the hell was Meat thinking, how had he come to the conclusion to quit? Did he know something? How long had he known if he did? She tried to cast her mind back to every conversation, everything that he had ever said to her. Had he been trying to tell her all along that he knew that something was wrong? Why now though as soon as they had made the big time in PRIME. None of this made sense, then again Meat had not been making sense lately. He had been locking himself away for days on end, only coming out at night. Claire had tried to keep track of him as well as she could, but he had grown quite adept at getting away wherever possible. It had made her job particularly more difficult, especially....her thought process was cut by a stern cough from Stephen who had been waiting patiently for a response, having deemed he had waited long enough. “Chris hasn\'t been himself lately at all. He hasn\'t been talking to anyone let alone me. Truth is I think he suspects something.” There was an awkward silence in the room as the words leaked out from Claire\'s mouth. Whether it was the situation that had caught her off guard, or the weight of truth finally breaking free. The enormity of the sentence began to dwell on Claire. Wishing with every sinew in her body that she could withdraw the statement “And why exactly would he have reason to suspect?” Stephen leaned in with a knowing smile on his face as he interlocked his fingers. Instinctively Claire leaned back , wanting to keep equal distance away from Stephen. It was more a psychological thing than direct fear of recrimination. The way that Stephen had spoken to her had ground her down. It may have been the pressure of the situation, it may have been because she was so emotionally charged after meeting Kelly but something deep inside of her snapped, forcing her to retaliate in a way she had never done to Stephen. She snapped at him. “Because he isn\'t stupid maybe? He\'s a human being he\'d be able to feel the changes. He put on seven pounds of muscle mass last week, that is not normal. The mood swings the sickness, the hospitalisation. Come on it doesn\'t take a genius!” By the end of her sentence Claire was stood up her hands balled up into fists leaning on the table, her chest heaving up and down. The rage that was flowing through her now was almost uncontrollable with every syllable filled with bile filled hate towards her employer. The person who had made her life a living hell. Forced her to become something she was not, she was just as much a puppet as Christian was. The only difference was that she did not have the courage to take the step forwards and break the cycle of self destruction. The fear held her captive instead of pushing her forwards and breaking her free. “I do not appreciate that tone Claire and you will do well to remember your place.” It was the first time that Stephen\'s voice had raised in its inflection, even slightly. It was just enough to freeze Claire exactly in her place, fear overwhelming her. A man with such power being angry was not something that you wanted in your direction. Taking note of the tone and that it was being directed straight towards her, Claire graciously sat down and lowered her head in submission. Any fight that she had in her had been tossed out of the window in one fell swoop. “I\'m sorry sir.” “Now, what do you suppose we do about our little problem?” Stephen allowed himself to relax a little and recline in his chair, his fingers which had remained interlocked were moved to the back of his head and supported it in an vision of casual comfortability. Whether this was true or not was not a matter to be questioned. If he gave off this impression then it was the out and out truth of the matter. You believed what Stephen said. “If he has quit, there isn\'t really much more we can do is there?” Claire took notable consideration to not let her words be considered as back talk. She had already pushed things in that department one too many times, however her words for all intensive purposes were true. Once a person had decided on a course of action, there was generally no turning back from it. People could only be controlled for so long before natural survival instinct kicked in. In Meat\'s case it was both mental and physical. His body whilst becoming strong and more muscular, had truly felt the effects on the inside. His body had been rejecting food and of course, there was the very small matter of the heart attack which had hospitalised him as well. Yet his mind was what had needed saving the most. It was not natural for a man to lock himself away from everyone. To resent his family and to actually want to have nothing to do with his wife and child. To avoid his wife\'s phone calls repeatedly. Something though had clicked in Meat\'s mind and made him fight. That\'s something that you can never whittle out of a person. The fighting spirit in whatever form it manifests itself. It will always remain a part of you locked away tight inside. Nothing can ever get rid of that. “That would be where you are wrong Claire.” Stephen\'s words once again froze Claire\'s train of thought. She watched Stephen cautiously as his reached under the table and pulled up a plain black briefcase, which he clicked open and turned to face Claire. Her face contorted with confusion as she looked at the object that lay before her. What lay in the briefcase was quite simply a number of phials of clear liquid and a pristinely clean glass syringe. “What\'s that?” Claire\'s eyes didn\'t waiver from the clear liquid as she her mind began to piece together exactly where this was going, with every piece that fit. The picture that it presented was one that did not end happy for anyone involved. “It\'s.....” Stephen paused for a moment. It wasn\'t hesitation, more a case of pulling together the correct words for the grand occasion. He reached forwards and turned the briefcase towards him so that he could look at his creation. It was his finest piece of work and once this had been tried and tested, everything would be complete and in place. “It\'s an upgrade on the formula we have been experimenting with. Only this time there is an added extra if you will. At a small decrease in the effectiveness as a straight edged steroid. The formula, all being well of course will render the subject almost without any free will. Think if you will of a new born baby, its first instinct is to cling desperately to its mother. It learns to trust her and will do anything she says in time.” Stephen\'s words reflected those of a mad man or mass murderer attempting to justify his actions to others. In his own mind he had done no wrong, it was everyone else that had the problem. He wanted to create a better world. The path of which madness often derives and as Claire\'s eyes looked into Stephen\'s she believed that this route was well and firmly being taken. “That\'s horrific...you are effectively creating slaves!” The abject horror on Claire\'s face more than reflected in the tone of her voice as she pushed her chair away from the table and rose to her feet. Taking a few cautionary steps backwards she shook her head at Stephen, who smiled almost maniacally back at her. “We prefer the term drones, its far more politically correct in this crazy world we live in now.” He had finally gone over board, there was no hint of guilt or even feeling in his voice, just cold hard facts. Almost being upset at how the world had changed so much so that terminology needed to be changed. “You\'re being serious aren\'t you. This isn\'t some sort of sick joke.” “The only downside is that this needs to be administered directly into the blood stream, it needs to be injected. Which as you may have guessed is where you come in my dear.” Stephen raised his eyes from the syringe in front of him to Claire who was backing away quickly as possible, without making it look like a retreat. She had no idea where the exit was and didn\'t want to turn her back on a man carrying a syringe to find out. “Even if I wanted to, which may I point out I don\'t. There is no way I would be able to get close enough to him to inject him with this stuff. I won\'t do it.” “Yes you will Claire, you don\'t have a choice in the matter.” Stephen rose from his seated position, syringe in hand and took three methodical steps towards Claire, his eyes burning into Claire\'s who had felt her body begin to lose control, every word that she uttered was filled with fear and her body was shaking almost uncontrollably. “I do Stephen, it\'s called freewill its something you are trying to take away from Chris. He is a human being, not a piece of meat.” “Claire, you will do this because you have no choice. Think back Claire, how many times have you actually said no to us, then gone on and done it anyway. Claire you are as much a slave, sorry drone as Chris will be. Now off you go and report back when it\'s done. Stephen turned around and moved towards the window in the office surveying the world around him, happy with another job well done. Claire turned towards the door and strode forwards. As she reached for the handle, something inside her snapped, that survival instinct that kicks in when your whole being is in danger of being consumed by the façade you have painted upon yourself. Turning on her heels she took five steps away from the door and stared daggers into the back of Stephen\'s head and although she was scared, there was an overwhelming sense of power in the one word that she managed to get out from her shaking lips. “.....no.” For a moment, there was silence the low hum of the air conditioning suddenly becoming apparent. Stephen\'s head dropped slightly and shook slowly from side to side. “I\'m sorry you feel that way.” Claire took another step forwards and was about to take another before a sensation of paralysis rattled through her body. He chest seized up as she became short of breath. As she fell to the floor she looked up and saw a man standing over her and empty syringe in his hand, she slowly raised her hand to her neck where the initial wound had been. Her eyes rolled backwards into her head as silence and darkness overcame her, the bitter embrace of emptiness embroiling her soul. Freewill is an illusion. I\'m in control of my own destiny. Two arguments that will be thrown around until the end of time. The truth of the matter is that they are thrown around as justification for our lives. For the actions and choices that we make. If it was something which worked out well then it was a choice in which we were in control of our own destiny. Yet if it was a choice which led to a negative response then it was life\'s great plan unfolding in front of us. Stephen turned around from the window and looked down at the fallen Claire. He dismissed the man holding the syringe who nodding in respect turned and exited the office, closing the door behind him. “It really pains me to do this to you Claire. It truly does but I can\'t trust anyone else.” Kneeling down next to Claire he gently ran a hand through her hair. He leant in forwards and whispered softly in her ear. “You always were my favourite child....here is what you are going to do....”
