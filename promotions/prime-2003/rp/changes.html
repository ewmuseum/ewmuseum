Date: ReVolution 156 Los Angeles, California April 6th, 2008 "Go get the cake, and make sure you take off those candles, alright?" Balloons. Hats. Presents. Cake. Birthday Party. Katie stands in the middle of the room, her eyes wandering for a certain person, when she finally realizes just how packed the room is. Well, I guess he does have friends after all. Hours earlier, there'd been some fear in the back of her mind that no one would actually show up for this birthday party. Craig doesn't have friends she had thought to herself, but obviously that wasn't the case. As she glances around, her eyes stopping every few seconds thinking that she's caught a glimpse of the birthday boy, but still he's nowhere to be found. "Has anyone seen Craig?" she yells out to the crowd, but many of them are unfazed by her voice as they're elbow deep into their own conversations. A man in his mid-twenties turns around and looks at Katie with confused look on his face. "He was just here a moment ago," he tells him, his voice rich with bass. She looks at him with this odd look on her face. "Thanks. Who're you?" she asks him and he just shrugs his shoulder before his hand wraps tightly around the beer bottle in his left hand and he puts it to his lips to take a sip of it. Katie just shakes her head as she walks around the man before stopping a friend of her's, Mark. "Who's that guy? As a matter of fact, who're most of these people?" she asks him and he just smiles at her. "Katie, don't you worry about it," he tells her and she just gets this alarmed look on her face. "Did you hire these people?" the question comes out a little more bluntly then she would've liked. He just throws his head back and lets out one of those loud, bellowing laughs. "No, no, no! Just found them on the corner? Some of them might be friends of mine. I think one of those guys is homeless so he might try to get a room for the night," he tells her, but then wishes he could've retracted his statement after the look Katie shoots him, a look that could kill on this particular night. "Look, I'm two months late with this party. You couldn't have cut me a little slack? He's probably looking at some of these people trying to figure out who most of them are. Poor Craig," she mutters as she walks away from Mark trying to make sense of everything happening around her. As she glances over her shoulder, she sees Cathy, a waitress from the restaurant, walking out of the kitchen with the cake in her hands. "Looks great! Now we just need the birthday boy! CRAIG!" the sound of her voice carries throughout the entire house and stops every bit of conversation going around her. Yet, there's no response. The voice echoes throughout the house as the silence just lingers, wondering where the birthday boy is exactly. "I'll be right there," his voice slices through the uncomfortable silence. Where his voice is coming from no one really knows, but at least he's somewhere in the general vicinity. He walks out of one of the bedrooms in the house to walk smack into a male and female making out against the wall. He glances at them oddly and walks right up to them. "I'm pretty sure I don't know who either one of you are, but this is my house and I'd appreciate it if you got a room or something," he tells them rather sternly. They glance at him, trying to figure out who the man in the blue jeans and black sweater is. The guy couldn't be any older then twenty-one, grabs the hand of the girl of the evening and starts walking towards the bedroom that Craig just left. Craig looks around a little lost, wondering if he's in the middle of a reality series all of a sudden, and then grabs the shoulder of the rather clueless man. "Do you know who I am?" Craig asks him, the tone in his voice growing more and more serious with each passing second. The man glances at Craig for a second before looking back at his girl, and then back at Craig. "Did you want to join in or something, man? I mean, because you can, but just stay away from my ass," his words come out rather slurred and Craig's almost certain that the man is stoned. Craig's hand comes up to his forehead as he just wipes his face, trying to see if he has egg on his face or something to make this guy take him as a joke. Satisfied with the fact that his face was still intact, Craig looks at the man again with this unbelievable look on his face. "You ever heard of PRIME, by any chance?" Craig asks him, trying to jog the guy's memory. For a second, the word sounds foreign to the stoned man, but then he slaps his forehead and his eyes get really big! "Yeah man! That's that shit! The other week, I was watching that thing--what do they call it-- the Dual Halo? Yeah! And this guy, he kind of looks like you, he like injured his shoulder, right? And he leaped off the top of this platform and did this spinning thing from like 20 feet high! It was SO sick! You love it too?" the man asks him and Craig honestly starts feeling like he's in the Twilight Zone. Craig just turns away from the man and starts walking away. "I can't honestly believe what my life has turned into," Craig speaks out loud to no one in particular. He just continues walking, hoping to get away from this life and find himself in his actual life. This party had been a disaster, but he couldn't fault Katie for trying. She wanted to do something special for Craig, but with him out of town for his birthday and much of the past few months, there just hadn't been anytime to really throw anything. Of course, now he was back in town, and the idea of a party had been engrained in her mind. This couldn't be any worse of an idea he thinks to himself as he walks by the front door, on his way to the living room where Katie had been calling him from. As he walks by it though, there's a knock on it. He looks back at the door as then the doorbell rings throughout the house. "I got it! he yells out, letting Katie know that he'll be there in a few more moments. He wonders to himself who else could've been invited to the party as he grabs the doorknob and opens it slowly. "Hey," the voice cuts through his heart as the world around him disappears. ~~~~~~~~~~~ Earlier That Day "How have you been feeling as of late?" For a moment, he doesn't even hear the question, lost in his own thoughts. Eventually, this nagging feeling in the back of his mind that causes Craig to look at Silvia. She jots something down on the notepad unknown to Craig who sits up straight in his chair and closes his eyes for a brief moment before looking at her again. "Sorry?" he responds, obviously having missed a question somewhere. At first she doesn't respond as she continues to jot down a note to herself. "I asked how you've been feeling as of late. With the MS. I noticed that your schedule over the past few weeks has increased and you've actually been taking more risks," she asks him, obviously alluding to his recent performance in the Dual Halo and the subsequent matches. "It's been... tough to say the least. The fatigue is more noticeable then in the past. The medication I'm taking isn't exactly helping with the constant pain of being in the ring," he tells her before his voice trails off remembering this past week of the sheer effort it took just to stand up. "And your shoulder?" she asks him, before pointing at his left shoulder. He glances over at it and notices how swollen it is. He flexes his arm a little bit before looking back at Silvia. "It hurts, what do you expect?" he responds rather rudely. She makes note of this as she begins scribbling again on the legal pad. There are times that he'd just like to yank the pad out of her hands and read what she's writing, but somehow he's been able to control himself. So far. Then, there's silence. She's continuing to write while Craig just glances around the room, wondering if he should leave. Instead he stands up and begins to walk around the room, his thoughts just kind of stagnant. Exhaustion has hit him and he contemplates just taking a nap on the couch, but figures that his therapist wouldn't mind that. Moments pass before she finally speaks again. "How goes that list?" she asks him and he turns around a little shocked. "Oh, yeah," he says sheepishly, showing that he obviously had forgotten about it. "You don't have an infinite amount of time, Craig. You need goals and the drive to achieve them. If you're not going to take the time to do th--," she begins to lecture him, but he raises his hand to stop her. He walks back over to his chair and sits down. "Alright, let's do this then. Right here, right now," he tells her and she looks at him to start off the list. "Find my parents." She nods her head before opening her mouth, "Very good." She jots that down on the list before looking back up at him again, waiting for his next response. "Let's see...," yet his mind goes blank after that. What does he want in his life? What's there left to explore? Nothing, right? Maybe this was everything that his life was about. Isn't that in the least bit sad? Shouldn't he have goals, a life, something else outside of wrestling? The truth of the matter was that he really didn't. "That's it?" she asks him, a frown on her face. He nods his head and she sits back in her chair with this chastising look on her face. "That's not healthy. Can you do me a favor?" Craig nods his head once again. "Can you go home and just think about this? Get away from the wrestling world for a little bit and just analyze your life. You should have something more to live for then that ring because eventually, with that disease, you won't have a choice. If you're at home, then those thoughts of suicide will return, and then what? I think we both know that answer, Craig," she tells him and Craig just sits there before licking his lips. All good points she had made and yet he didn't even have a retort for her. "Yeah," he says before standing up, ready for this session to end. He begins walking to the door, but she stops him mid-stride. "You didn't mention anything about Nicole," she tells him and he just looks at her for a moment. "Yeah." ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Present Seconds had passed and yet Craig still hadn't returned to the living room. This had frankly surprised Katie who begins to walk towards the front door to see what's stopping Craig. As she reaches the edge of the room though, someone grabs her arm and spins her around. "Hey, do we have anymore food? It looks like we're getting low," the woman asks her and Katie just looks befuddled at the woman. "Do I know you?" she asks her before walking away and towards the front door. As she gets closer and closer, she sees Craig just standing there, not moving. "Craig?" she asks him, but he doesn't move, instead fixated on the person or object before him. Then, he moves to the side ever so slightly as a woman walks in, looking as beautiful as ever. As Katie looks at her, she can't believe that this woman is standing in this house once again. "Nicole." Black.
