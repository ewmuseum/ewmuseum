UltraVigilant

20 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 20th, 2008 As the quintessential frat boy Colby Korver and the good ol’ country boy Hank Cobb sit sprawled on worn black leather recliners, helping themselves to a seemingly endless supply of adult beverages from a gigantic cooler situated conveniently between them, the movie Old School rolls on a nearby television set. Beanie: 'Gentlemen, we're discussing a brand-new way to look at a fraternity.' That pretty much sums things up for the two founding fathers of Delta Upsilon Iota. One, the walking personification of every meathead frat guy you’ve ever had the misfortune of running into late on a Friday night. While some value academic degrees or annual salaries, he instead measures his worth by counting the empty beer cans on his floor and multiplying that by the notches on his bedpost. But there’s something to be said for this sophomoric nature, because deep down don’t we all need someone like this in our lives? Someone who can make us look good around our girlfriend one day and take our mind off that same girlfriend the next. Beanie: 'In other words, forget all the normal rules that apply to both college and society, because this is a very big idea, my friends.' The other is in many ways Korver’s polar opposite, with a firm belief in holding doors for women, Sunday mornings in church, and Copenhagen tobacco. Blue collar in every sense of the word, this North Carolinian sporadically rubs his sore right knee, the result of a career-ending football injury that consequently set in motion his circuitous route to Prime. What Prime, and the upcoming UltraViolence pay-per-view in particular, yet hold in store for these two blue chippers, however, remains very much a mystery. Beanie: 'We are talking about a non-exclusive, egalitarian brotherhood where community status and more importantly, age, have no bearing whatsoever.' Colby Korver is a 26-year old who only recently completed his 9th semester…of junior year. Measuring 6’2" and weighing in at a stout 232 pounds, Korver has obviously spent a great deal more time during college in the weight room than the classroom. While an impressive physical specimen, Korver is obviously overshadowed by his mountain of a tag team partner Hank Cobb. But at 6’8" tall and 348 pounds, Cobb towers over pretty much everybody. Korver: 'Alright brah, well there's still about an hour left in the movie and we're down to just one case. You want to pause it and make a beer run?" Cobb: "Aw hell man, I’m fixin’ to settle right back into this lazy boy for a nice, little nap." "Cobb! I told you I invited those freshman girls from Friday night over to barbeque tonight. I need my wingman. I need the big guy." "That’s fine, just wake me up five minutes before they get here so I can throw on some Old Spice and a fresh t-shirt and we’ll be good to go." "You are absolutely killing me. This is only the first half of our big afternoon. You’re clearly forgetting we still have PCU to go. But instead of manning up, now you’re demanding a freaking catnap? What happened to you? "Oh come on now. I already have a nice little buzz goin’ and just wanna rest my eyes. For cryin’ out loud, I was up until 4am last night luggin’ your drunk ass home from the dorms. Dammit Korver, I’m tired. And my feet hurt." 'But it's Saturday. Tomorrow's the day of rest, you should know that.' 'Well, when I'm up all night babysittin' you, it changes to today.' 'Babysitting? Last night was kickass. I don't know about you, but I had an awesome time.' 'I know you had an awesome time, the whole town knows you had an awesome time.' 'But so did you, admit it. I saw you talking to that hot little blonde number on the porch. Now don't you dare sit here and tell me that was just another example of your babysitting abilities.' 'No, you're right. I mean, it was great. Her aunt actually grew up going to high school with my 2nd cous...' "Cobb, I don't need the boring details. I need Hank the Tank." "Ok, ok......I’ll do one." ~~~Some Hours…and Countless Beer Cans…Later~~~ "Ok…ok…ok…I’ll do one more. But I’m fixin’ to lay off the shots from here on out, ya hear me boy? And don’t get sneakin’ any of that 151 into my beer no more son." "One more? You’re rolling about 50 deep and you’re already calling it quits? You better not pull any of this shit Sunday, or I’m leaving your fat ass in Chi-Town and beginning my long awaited singles career." "Korv, the only thing anyone has been waitin’ for around here is for your dumb ass to finally finish up junior year." "I’ll have your know that I’m on pace to graduate Cum Laude…all over your mom." "So I guess that we’ve finally reached that special time of the night when I will kindly remind you that momma Cobb shall remain absent from all future conversation?" And with that, Cobb finishes up his tall boy of Pabst Blue Ribbon and immediately delivers it squarely to Korver’s forehead. "Point taken. So what do you say we move our focus to the pressing business at hand? Namely, our match this Sunday at UltraViolence." "I’m listenin’." "Well it seems to me that for a match of this magnitude…" "Yep." "On this grand of a stage…" "Sure." "For this treasured of a prize…" "Obviously." "We may require some measure of assistance." "Well Korv, I’d say we’re pretty well-covered, considerin’ how we’re fixin’ to compete in a tables, ladders and chairs match. What are you lookin’ for? A nuclear warhead on a pole match?" "Au contraire my genetically gifted colleague, I’m merely suggesting that we call in some reinforcements of the fraternal variety." "Well color me red and call me Clifford, that ain’t a half bad idea." "No shit. So throw me my cell phone already."
