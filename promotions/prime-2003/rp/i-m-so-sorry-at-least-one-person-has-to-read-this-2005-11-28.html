I'm So Sorry At Least One Person Has To Read This

28 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: November 28th, 2005 Ooh, lookie here. It’s time for another fabulous Forsaken adventure! Isn’t that fantastic? I think it is. Just fantastic. Then again, I also think it’s fantastic each day that I don’t get kicked in the balls by some random person off the street, so perhaps my standards are set a little low. Either way, the adventure is still happening, even whilst I have to prepare for the opening round of GTT5. Thanks, Sebs; you really know how to put something in a guy’s ass when he’s not looking. Speaking of GTT5, however, I have to say it looks promising. After all, it isn’t everyday that I get to lose in the first round of a major tournament in front of all my peeps. So I’m excited about that prospect, to say the least. But what are Clyde’s feelings on the tournament, and having to face Tyler O’-where did he go now?-Neill in PRIME just a few days before it starts? Let’s find out, shall we? “iiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII” The robotic voice slowly builds in volume as it nears, causing Clyde and James to both turn their heads at the same time. Standing on the sidewalk outside of a corner store with a faded sign in the window stating simply that they do indeed carry Pokémon cards, The Forsaken are just seconds away from escaping with a bounty of rejected children’s television paraphernalia when they spot a familiar face accompanying the voice. Stephen Hawking. On fire. Wheeling through the middle of the street. And still frantically trying to fix the stuck button. Behind him, Mr. T tries to rid himself of the fire consuming his body, but succumbs to the flame when he refuses to take off the jewelry that’s seemingly feeding the inferno. He finally falls to his knees, then onto his face, only able to mutter a few words before his eyes close forever. Mr. T: I…pity…the foo’…who…don’t…stop, drop, and roll… Still wheeling along ahead of the now deceased Mr. T, the screaming scientist disappears further down the road, leaving Clyde and James to each slowly raise their eyebrows in unison. “IIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiii” Farwell: Well, that was…unexpected. Walkins: But still only half as unexpected as that time we met Angelina Jolie and Brad Pitt. Cut to the streets of Calgary, where a group of reporters are following the two around the city. Out from the crowd appear James and Clyde, who instantly figure out a way to get the attention they so badly crave. Farwell: Hey, Miss Jolie! Probably uncharacteristically, Angelina turns around with a warm smile to greet her fans. Angelina: Hi! How may I help you? And just as she turns around, Farwell drops her with a right hand to the face. Farwell: Pow! Right in the kisser! Now we return you to The Forsaken. Farwell: I don’t seem to recall that ever happening, Clyde. And come to think of it, you stole the punch line from last night’s Family Guy. You know we always wait at least a month before blatantly plagiarizing something… Farwell’s eyes shift back and forth nervously. Farwell: Just to be sure. Anyway, let’s get these cards loaded up into the trunk so we can try and take care of our little problem at home. Walkins: Problem? If it weren’t for him, I’d have been eaten, just like you. I don’t get why you want him dead. Clyde pauses for a second, before a thought pops into his head. Walkins: How was getting eaten, anyway? Is it like everyone says it is? Farwell: It’s highly disappointing. Unlocking the trunk, Farwell opens it as Walkins dumps two armfuls of Pokémon cards into the car. Farwell: I just hope this works. Sure, that…thing…saved you from being eaten faster than a preteen at Neverland Ranch, but is that really worth keeping him around for? Walkins: But he’s my new best friend! We’re even seeing who can grow their beards out faster! Farwell: You ever stopped? Pause to let the joke sink in. Continue awkwardly when no one gets said joke. Farwell: Anyway, I think he was the one who broke the leg off Mr. CW. Walkins: But didn’t his leg break off before Gabe got here? Farwell: No, no. In fact, I’m positive it was him now. James looks at Clyde for a second. Farwell: And he’s the one who stole Christmas from you last year, too. Walkins: He shall pay for his treachery! When he least expects it, his muffins shall cum. James doesn’t even raise an eyebrow this time. He’s just growing more and more accustomed to the stupidity as time goes on. Now, since I’m really just too lazy to give this thing any real substance or meaning, we’re gonna skip on along to the point of them already being in the car, driving the last leg of their journey back to wherever the hell it is they’re staying this week. Again, I’m lazy. Lazy enough to forego giving them an actual place to stay, and rather just using the world in general as sufficient description. Anyway, now they’re in the car. Hooray for patchy plots. Farwell: So, how is it you got into GTT5 and I didn’t, anyway? Clyde turns his head from the road to James, completely no-selling the fact that he’s driving. Walkins: Well, you see…um…when I went there to enter the both of us, I sorta…kinda…gave your application to Ethan Frost. James just narrows his eyes. Walkins: But before you get mad, he told me he needed to win the tournament for his sick son. I think he’s got the gay or something. Real heartbreaking story. Anyway, he said if he wins, he’ll split the prize with us. James takes a second to breathe in and out a few times, calming himself while collecting his thoughts. Farwell: First off, he isn’t going to win. The best we’ve come to expect from Ross is a no-show. Second, how are we going to split the prize? You tell me how we split fame and a domain name. What, is it gonna be www.frostwell.com now?! Damn you, Clyde. Now I get to watch someone else take my fame, my fortune, AND my free hosting for a year? I don’t think so! Walkins: So what are you going to do about it, Jamesy? Farwell: Start hitting you more often, for one. And then I’ll have to make sure you win that damnable tournament…even if it kills everyone else in it… Looking out from the corner of his eye, Clyde shifts a little further away from James as his partner evilly plots the demise of everyone else in GTT5, just for the sake of a free domain name. Meanwhile, the radio mysteriously turns on with the sound of some annoying host introducing the next song. DJ: Our next song is number two on the charts, and one we’re sure you’ll enjoy! And then… “I...AM…CLYDER-MAN! Do-do-dododo-dododododododo-do-do-do!” Walkins: DICKWRINKLE! The anger of only coming in second (don’t get the reference? Start reading my writing, you dumb bastard!) evident on his face, Clyde stares straight ahead as his hands slowly turn the wheel and force the car into the oncoming lane. Farwell: Clyde! The highway! Walkins: Technically, it’s a road. This time, rather than crashing into a forest, James manages to grab the wheel out of Clyde’s hands and pull them back onto the road, coming to a stop just a few feet from the…well…feet of one massive Gabeazard. Gabeazard: HOW DO YOU KNOW I’M MASSIVE? HAVE YOU BEEN SNEAKING A PEEK AT MY KING KONG DONG AGAIN? MY DICK’S BIGGER THAN YOU ARE UGLY! You’re breaking the fourth wall, dammit! Gabeazard: I’LL BREAK YOUR MOM’S FOURTH WALL LOL! … I’m just gonna let The Forsaken handle this one. Farwell: Oh no, you aren’t. We tried that idea you gave us. You know, the one with summoning other Pokémon from the cards to battle Gaybeazard. Didn’t work, huh? Farwell: Not at all. I even think we got the reject batch of cards. For some reason, Janet Reno’s apparently a Pokémon now, but her only power is being a man. Outside the car, Clyde continues hurling packs of cards at Gabeazard. Walkins: How do you like your cummuffins now, bitch?! Farwell: See? Nothing. Hmm…let me think then… Farwell: Think all you want, but he’s yours now. You deal with him. James turns to Clyde, motioning for him to get in the car. Farwell: I’m too old for this shit. C’mon, let’s go beat Tyler (n)O’(show)Neill. And then they’re off, leaving me with a stack of rejected Pokémon cards involving the likes of Gary Glittersaur and Ronald McFondlechu. And, of course, Gabeazard. That son of a bitch. Gabeazard: I’M HUNGRY, COCKSMEAR! FEED THE ALMIGHTY MASTER OF YOUR MOM! You can subsist off old Forsaken promos, because that’s where I’m copying and pasting you to, bitch. Ha! Who wins now, huh? Not you, that’s who! … …Yeah, I’m done here. Sorry you had to experience that. We’re all dumber now for it.
