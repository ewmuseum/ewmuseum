Microscopic Fireworks of the Depressed

Date: ... When raindrops fall from the sky, the sound they make is often overlooked. Most program their minds to alter activity plans and sit inside for a tediously boring duration of time. In some of those moments of forced leisure, the soul should drift off for a moment when the evaporation falls down, pinging against the window. Allow the imagination to run wild in thinking up a potential sound to correlate with the raindrops. At first, a conclusion is believed to be reached but it is only an illusion. Another moment of contemplation manifests, and finally the mind has reached its verdict. The body picks itself up and watches the beauty that is a rainy day. Right before their eyes is a personal firework show to cure their depression caused by the inclement weather. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~` Devin Shakur’s Mansion Raleigh, North Carolina Sun Tzu stares out into the firework show from behind the shield of Devin Shakur’s living room. He was once again away at work in the very same business that she was destined to become the queen of. She sometimes wondered what her sweet baboo indulged in on the road when he was by himself. Despite the rabid hatred people generally held for Shakur, there was a crowd who held him in a high regard. They wanted to remove the clothing from his body and get to know the man in his natural element. The temptations came in all shapes and sizes, attracted by his rebel image and independent nature. These were the very same things Shakur himself was drawn to for almost half a year in his quest for her. Well, that and she had a big juicy ass that Shakur couldn’t take his eyes off of. Sunny’s eyes catch an automobile’s headlights penetrate the driveway and the vehicle head toward the house. Nobody is expected to arrive at the quarters this evening, so this is definitely an anomaly. A moment elapses, the lights and engine shut off, and an individual steps out from the car into the porch light. An umbrella furthers the enigma while they walk toward the front door. With the blood of a belligerent communist still running through her veins, she grabs at her two guns aptly named Mr. Snugglepants and Kookoo Bear, and throws open the front door. The individual is startled and his rugged aged face is seen for the first time to Sunny. Sun Tzu: What in the name of Mao do you want you decrepit display of capitalism? The mystery man regains his smug grin and straightens out his Armani suit jacket. Mystery Man: Good evening, Ms. Tzu. My name is Daniel Demora and I have some information regarding your boyfriend, a Mr. Shakur. Sun Tzu doesn’t budge with her weaponry, cocking both guns back. Daniel senses weakness behind the exterior and leans in closer toward the guns, showing his natural bravado. Daniel Demora: My boss, Mr. DeAngelis, sent me to enlighten you on a couple of things regarding his past…May I come in, it’s awful dreary outside? Sun Tzu: You want to spread lies about my sweetie, and you expect me to allow you into our palace of hot all night rabbit sex? Is your mother by chance related to Britney Spears? Demora can only smirk at her defensive stance, and leans up against the brick wall under the carport. Daniel Demora: Very well, I’ll just talk here then. Did you know that he’s been a felon ever since he turned 18? Daniel pulls a cigarette out of his jacket pocket, lights up, and continues with the declassification of Devin Shakur’s life. ~~~~~~~~~~~ New York The fireworks continue to sparkle outside of an Italian restaurant in lower Manhattan, where the other half of the communist revolution is currently. Devin Shakur walks the opaque halls with two large men standing behind him, and a scrawny Italian bugger leading the way. Another turn down a hallway and finally the little man opens the door and allows Shakur to see his final destination. Michael DeAngelis’s office looks like an egomaniac’s utopia. The carpet is crimson colored with a giant image of three lions in the middle. All of the chairs are leather, and redwood dons every desk, proud to be contained within the office. Shakur’s eyes are fixated at the man sitting on his throne, The DeAngelis himself. He greets Shakur with a pseudo smile and points him to the chair seated in front of the desk. Shakur plops down and folds his legs perpendicular to one another. DeAngelis waves all three of his henchmen off, one of the mammoth men almost breaking the door behind him. DeAngelis puts his feet up onto the table, and begins to smoke the remnants of a cigar. He cordially removes the cigar a couple of seconds later, and blows the contents into Shakur’s face. Commie Emo closes his eyes and waves a hand in front of his face. Michael DeAngelis: So how was your flight here, nice I assume with you being a superstar now? Devin Shakur: I was in business class, stuck in the middle of a fat motherfucker who listened to ABBA the whole flight, and a five year old kid who kept saying WOOOOOOOOOO WOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Michael DeAngelis: I’m terribly sorry about that, blame Mikey, first day on the job. Shakur rolls his eyes back into his head. Devin Shakur: What is important enough to pull me out of my gym at noon right before I’m about to leave for Canada? DeAngelis takes his feet down from the table and closes the distance between Shakur and himself. Michael DeAngelis: You know me. I’m a businessman first and foremost. I’ll cut right to the chase…How would you evaluate your performance since joining our association? An incredulous look comes across Shakur’s face. Devin Shakur: Are you fucking serious? Michael DeAngelis: Everybody has to have job evaluation at some point in their career. Shakur shrugs his shoulders and almost looks away from DeAngelis. Devin Shakur: I would say that I’m doing a fair job at this point. I’ve got Gamble to fully trust me, so when I stick the knife into his back he definitely won’t see it coming. DeAngelis lightly chuckles before producing a remote from a drawer in his desk. Michael DeAngelis: I have a different opinion than what you’ve stated. Allow us to look at some video evidence. The don pushes a button on the remote and a moment from Revolution 141 appears on the television. Tony Gamble has just kicked in Devin Shakur’s locker room door. Tony Gamble: HOWCOULDYOUDOTHISTOME? Devin Shakur: … Tony Gamble: After all we have been through… Devin Shakur: I still don’t know what you are talking about. Tony Gamble: All those times I stood by your bed and made sure you were asleep before leaving your house- Devin Shakur: WHOA! What? How did you get into my house? Tony Gamble: Figure of speech. Devin Shakur: Are you sure, you don’t weigh over 200 pounds, I’m sure you can fit through some cracks in windows. Gamble lets out a nervous chuckle and puts his hand on Shakur’s shoulder. Tony Gamble: …Ok I could be lying. I have seen Sun Tzu naked. Devin Shakur: DUDE! Tony Gamble: Only kidding…OR AM I? Tony gives a >=J at Shakur Tony Gamble: No, no, really I’m kidding…OR AM I? Another >=J The television is turned off and the remote placed back in the desk drawer. Shakur sighs loudly, while DeAngelis firmly plants his hands into the desk and rises up, standing over Shakur. Michael DeAngelis: You have been nothing but a waste of space since I started paying you! Commie Emo goes to retort but DeAngelis holds his hand up. Michael DeAngelis: Don’t you dare make me show the clip of you and Gamble dancing down the hall with Oompa Loompas playing a stereo behind you. Shakur drops his head down in defeat, but DeAngelis doesn’t give him the satisfaction and yanks his face upward. Michael DeAngelis: I’ve had coffee boys be more competent at their job than you, AND THEY WEREN’T BEING PAID TO KILL SOMEBODY! A job like yours isn’t supposed to take months at a time, you are supposed to do it within a week or two. I can’t believe I trusted Christian when he said that you were a legitimate assassin. DeAngelis walks around the desk and looks down at his employee, fists clinched in fury. Michael DeAngelis: I actually boasted to my counterparts about you, said that you would alleviate the problem without much of a hassle. Now they laugh behind my back when I’m in the room, and my organization is the butt of every single joke made in the underworld! Devin Shakur: You never expressed- Michael DeAngelis: For God’s sake Shakur, you are almost 30 years old, and you need me to hold your hand? I know you’ve whacked before, so you don’t need to be taught how. The bottom line is this Devin… Dropping his face down to within inches of Shakur, DeAngelis delivers his closing line. Michael DeAngelis: Consider yourself on the unemployment line, and no longer the hunter…But instead the hunted. Taking serious offense to such comments, Shakur stands up and now stands eye to eye with DeAngelis. There is no doubt who would win a fight between the two, but currently DeAngelis has far more power than Shakur. From behind, Shakur hears a door open and footsteps moving toward him. DeAngelis points a finger toward the door. Michael DeAngelis: Now get the FUCK out of my sight, you worthless piece of shit. Boys, show him the exit. Two large hands grab the shoulders of Shakur. They lift him off the ground and out of the room. DeAngelis runs a hand through his hair and goes back to his desk. ~~~~~~~~~~ The last bits of cigarette are put out underneath the foot of Daniel Demora. He looks at Sun Tzu, giving her a courtesy nod, before casually turning away from two loaded guns and walking down the stairs. Her Chinese military background actually did her some good, because Daniel could not enrage her once he got rolling. However, he knows that once Shakur gets home, his domestic situation is never going to be as peaceful as it once was. Daniel smiles before opening the front door of his Mercedes and beginning his arduous journey back to headquarters. He leaves The Artist of War to stare deep into the darkness, letting her mind listen to the captivating sound that raindrops can make slamming against a window. ~~~~~~~~~~ Devin Shakur is still furious at the moment he just experienced with Michael DeAngelis. His feet are carrying him to the taxi he just absent mindedly called a moment ago. He was now officially off of the DeAngelis roster, no longer a needed asset to them, but instead a nuisance. Now, Shakur has a whole new category of similarities to Tony Gamble, he has the DeAngelis crime family breathing down his neck. Much like Gamble, however, Shakur is going to fight fire with fire. There will be no unrequited bloodbath here. This was just another obstacle in Shakur’s quest to become a king. A yellow vehicle pulls alongside of the curb and stops right in front of Shakur. He throws the hood of his jacket over his head and rushes toward the vehicle, throwing open the door and shutting it just as quickly. Devin Shakur: Kennedy Airport. The cabbie nods and drives away with the Universal title contender inside. Shakur forgot to bring his iPod with him for the trip, so his mind encapsulates itself into the majestic sound of raindrops falling.
