Depression has set in

7 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/05 Depression, a psychiatric disorder characterized by feelings of extreme sadness, dejection, and hopelessness. A state which many people in the world face daily, monthly, and some even deal with it throughout their whole lives. What is the cause of depression? Many people ponder this thought, and many times it is caused by something different in every person. Some people the cause of depression is a recent death of a close friend or relative, a streak of bad luck, and sometimes the feeling abandonment, each person to their own is the cause. Phones are ringing everywhere the surrounding areas are very busy. Clients are sitting and waiting patiently to see their doctors, and the doctors are running around the area with their clipboards trying to get everyone to their appointments on time. This is almost impossible to get out of their on time, as many of you know that going to the doctor’s takes time, more time then you want to give. “Peter, take this prescription and go down to your local pharmacy to get it filled.” Came from one of the many doctors, who is running around ramped. He is screaming so his patient could hear him over the racket of the surrounding area. “Sure thing Doc,” a Peter Vetra is seen trying to push his way through the crowd. Moving as much as he can to get away from all these people, seeming to have recently some sort of phobia maybe claustrophobia, who knows? Peter busts through the doors and makes his way to his car. BACK AN HOUR AGO! We see Peter anxiously sitting in the waiting room of the same doctor’s office, he is shaking nervously as he can not take waiting any longer. He looks at this watch on his wrist. “God Damn it, I have been here waiting for an hour already, why did I even make an appointment?” Peter whispers that angrily under his breath just as he is interrupted. A nice looking young blonde nurse stands up from behind the counter, reading off her clipboard she speaks, “Peter Vetra, the Doctor will now see you in room A2.” Peter who sick of waiting for this doctor to see him gets up eagerly. The nurse opens the swinging door for Peter, allowing him to enter the doctor-patient area. “Little busy in here today, huh?” Peter is just trying to make small talk as he walks to the nurse as he walks by her. Without a response Peter continues walking, while walking he looks at the plates on the walls as he tries to find his room. After finding his room he is seated on the bench awaiting the arrival of the doctor. Peter starts to whistle some kind of show tune to keep his mind moving instead of it becoming idle. Before he even hits the second chorus the door flings open and in comes a rather petite doctor. His glasses are around his eyes and he has a clipboard in his hands, he is all but in a joking manner. “Good Morning, Mr. Vetra. What is it that is bothering you?” “Doc, I have been down lately, I have yet to come out of this slump I have been hit with ever since facing Emilio Rage. I can’t get his match out of my head, and every other match I have I loss because I am worrying too much about Emilio.” Peter lets out a heavy sigh and seems to be breathing heavy now. “I see, after all those tests we ran on you this morning, I have come to a single conclusion. What I am about to diagnose you with is more a mental disease for you. Many of times it is a physical disease but I think you have made this a little more mental. Peter you are depressed.” The doctor who was reading off his clipboard looks up from the board after finishing his statement. Trying to judge the reaction of his patient but he gets no signals from him. “Not surprising, every morning I wake up wondering what I can do to end it, but before I even go to bed I wonder what is the next hit drug, what is the newest combination that can ease my pain? Is it Oxycontin and Vodka? Or what if I take that 750 Vicodin, a OC, and take 151 Bacardi. Each night testing the night before, trying to ease that pain, I don’t know when it will stop, maybe not till I get that gold. But I will crash soon, which is why I am here.” Peter takes his head and hangs it low, feeling ashamed for his actions. The doctor shakes his head; looking down at a piece of paper before handing it to Peter he notices what they proscribed him. “Peter, we have subscribed you to Paxil CR. Take the pills as proscribed and you should start to feel better.” “So this is all it takes for someone to start to feel better all they have to do is take a couple of pills?” Peter who seems a little taken back that only a few pills will be able to make him no longer depressed. He agrees and listens to the doctor but he knows the only way to no longer be depressed is to kick Emilio Rage’s ass and take his belt. Time will take for him to get there but he will. “Peter to be honest, it is a little more then just taking this little pill, but this pill will start you on the path of recovery. You will have to work on it and it is all included in this pamphlet I am going to give you.” The doctor hands the pamphlet over to Peter. Peter grabs it from him and takes a quick look at it. “Peter I expect to hear from you within the next couple of weeks to know how you are making out, set your next appointment up with the secretary.” The doctor is kind of pushing Peter out of the office as he is incredibly busy. “Alright Doc, I will speak with you later on.” Peter notices that the doctor is rushing him out. Peter accepts that he is being pushed out, and starts to walk out of the building. Journal Entry 7.0 My honor is on the line! Time after time I have faced many men in this place we called the square circle. From my start of PRIME I have been included in the division known as the ALIAS division. The division where the champion names the belt, whatever they want to name it makes it more like their own. I have worked this division since its creation, I have put in my time, and it is now my time to shine. I have since then had one chance at beating Emilio Rage for that belt, I may have lost but I have grown from that match and I have even held my contedorship as the number one contender. So why should step into the ring with three other competitors, none of which who deserve to be in this division? Well there is only one logical, well I shouldn’t even call it logical, there is only one answer for this simple problem and that is they are trying to take me out of the division. They feel I am too good for this division and they want me out, which is fine, but these three are no where close to being at the level they need to be to compete in this division. It is cool though, but I am not going to be leaving this division with out at least one more chance to win that title. My next chance will be this week against three men, Big Poppa, Tyler Lopez, and Jackson Darilek. Big Poppa is a man that I have faced before once in the ring, right after he suffered a loss to Tyler Lopez he rebounded with a win against me. A horrible match that was for me, it actually could be consider it the start of my demise, but this is going to start my recent up swing. Tyler Lopez is a man that started before in the upper card and he is now seeing himself being lowered to a opening matches. What does that say about him? Well when I think of things like that it reminds me of someone being washed up or past their PRIME. Is this the case with Tyler Lopez we will have to wait and see when I face him this week but I think it is true. Finally a man who has been rather bold since he has been in this federation, Jackson Darilek is on my mind the most. I have yet to really pay attention to any of his matches as they never have really been of interests to me. But the only problem I really see is by the statement he once said. The actual quote slips my mind but the means of the statement is he is not only the best cruiserweight in PRIME, but he is the best cruiserweight in the game today. This week I will be showing him that is a false statement, and he will see that Peter Vetra is the best in the game today. Actually I will not only make him see it, I will prove it to him. Rage, Big Poppa, Lopez, and Darilek the only men right now that stand in my way to achieving what I want and that is gold. This week it starts with the later of the three men named, but the following weeks to come I will take on the first person on that list, and in my eyes the most important, the current champion. Rage, your belt is not safe as I do plan on taking it from you one day. That day is coming sooner then later, buddy, I hope you are ready to part ways with it. Once I have a taste of gold I will expect nothing less. My time is now! You all will see…
