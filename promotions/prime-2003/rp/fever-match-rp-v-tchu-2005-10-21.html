Fever (Match RP - v Tchu)

Date: 21/12/05 FeverWritten: 21st October, 2005. 'Is this it?Is this it?Is this it?' - The Strokes. I – Her Own Worst Enemy. When you live your life at a thousand miles an hour, it doesn't take long before a full tank is expended, and you're left running on fumes. Fail to take heed of the warning light, try to eek out the last inch before it all evaporates away, and the grinding halt is sudden, jolting. The inertia of the world that spins around you leaves it slamming on the brakes, but unable to avoid the shunt. Knocked for six, the body, no matter how tough and toned, has to give on some level. Battling against the exhausted body's own desire to shut down puts a strain on its systems, stubbornness failing to accept that there are some things pig-headedness and refusal to quit cannot match. But while many in PRIME knew what Karina Wolfenden's body was capable of - most having woken up to the throbbing of a bruised body of their own - she was about to feel its wrath herself. The spiteful revenge of a body whose plea for rest fell on death ears. Given no mercy, it offered none in return, seeping through the K-Wolf's body, flicking every biological switch it passed off. It was her emergency shut-down procedure, which bordered on self-destruct. Too often she'd been called her own worst enemy. Say something enough... and it just might come true. II – Florence Nightingale, Eat Your Heart Out. A cold palm almost fizzled across her scorching brow. The tenderest of fingertips twirled sodden pink fringe around them, on their way to scraping it from Karina's perspiration-soaked face. A fevered moan stoked up from Karina's throat as the fingers massaged her scalp, before tucking stray bangs behind her elfin ears. With eyelids too heavy the prise apart, Karina instinctively rolled her head towards the comfort of the touch, as a finger broke loose to stroke down her cheek, swabbing a bead of sweat that was on course to drop off her prominent cheekbone. Kari's bottom lip dropped ever so slightly, leaving just enough of a gap for her heavy breathes to whistle through. A delicate breeze was then blown across Karina's red, burning face, freckles lost in the intense coloration. The lips hovered just above her, the bottom of which shuffled from side to side, aiming the exhalation so it washed all over. Under the covers, Kari's body squirmed, fidgeting to find comfort as she came close to cremating herself in an attempt to break the fever. Although it felt as if the saliva on her tongue was about to bubble dry, she shivered, an ill cold juddering up her spine. The covers were then pulled up to Karina's chin, tucked in tight around her, restraining Kari's fidgeting limbs. A fevered mumble escaped her dry lips, but no words could form from the murmured jumble. 'Shhhhhhhhhh,' softly danced across their air between them, soothing. As the peaceful urging helped calm Kari's fevered mind, a kiss was tenderly placed on her beaded forehead. A content sigh swirled out through Kari's nostrils as her head instinctively reached back, her lips drawn by a certain static towards those which had cooled her scorching brow. A denying hand once again petted Karina's hair, settling her back down into the mound of pillows underhead. 'Try and get some sleep,' was spoken, with every hint of a harsh pronunciation smoothed, while choppy blonde hair was twirled around a finger, from root to tip. The feeling of snug satisfaction was enough for Karina to slip effortlessly back to sleep, while a shadow from the bedside lamp remained cast across her, the sight of Karina sleeping being irresistible to her self-appointed carer. Hours passed as the cooling salt from Kari's sweat threatened to form a crust, binding her eyelashes together. Karina's body squirmed as feeling was given back to her, instantly shivering where the cold perspiration her body had exuded remained in contact with her skin. Her head felt anchored down to the pillows, groggy feelings acting like heavy gravity, restraining her body against the bed. The tips of her fingers felt frozen where the fever breaking had seeped all the heat out through her extremities, while her head seemed to buzz with every screwing-up of her stinging eyes. 'Ah, you're finally back with us,' a perky, playful voice remarked from Karina's bedside. 'And, yeah, you talk in your sleep... a lot.' Karina's eyes narrowed, adjusting to the heavy, hazy morning's sun, where an Indian summer was hanging perilously over the brink of cool autumn. A groan betrayed the sickly feeling that lingered, but the rise in curious pitch struck a chord of bemusement. 'Oh, but don't worry... not repeating a word.' As words were spoken under the disguise of a silhouette, Karina instinctively flattened her palm into a visor, attempting to lift the secretive veil of light. With her optic nerves firing back up, Kari managed to pick out a black cat staring right back at her with wide, curious eyes, pupils having abandoned their slit to nearly fill the eyeball. For a split second, Kari was forced to do a double take, wondering just how fevered she was to have Psylocke or Betsy talking to her. It was then, as the low sun began to duck and dodge through the thinning cloud, that the hand stroking the feline was seen. 'Oh, fucking kill me...' dribbled with self-loathing from Karina's lips.”Take pillow, push over face, hold, hold, hold, hold, holduntilihaven'tmovedforanhour.' A bright pink nail, flecked with purple, scratched just behind the black feline's ear, leaving the cat to push hard against the arm in pleasure. As hard as she fought her eyes' infuriating curiosity, Kari scanned up from the hand, past the wrist clad with an untidy mish-mash of bracelets, along the forearm criss-crossed by torn purple mesh, and over a shoulder donning too many straps for Kari's newly-woken brain to process. 'Sorry,' went the charismatic quip, followed by an almost-but-not-quite giggle. 'Decided on soup over suffocation.' Leaning through the curtain of illuminated dust, the beaming face of an all-too-familiar redhead broke into a smile, while Karina's eyebrows migrated down towards the bridge of her nose. 'That pleased to see me, huh?' Amy scoffed, then cupping a bowl of piping hot broth in her hands. 'Says a lot about my bedside manner...' Kari's lips parted, but her tongue stalled. 'Wh-... the fu-... eugh?' 'Why?' the redhead beamed, impersonating a Cheshire cat as well as a Nova Scotian girl could. 'Who the fuck else do you have?' True to character, the broad grin burned itself into Kari’s blotted vision, attracting all her focus while the body surrounding it faded with a shimmer.
