Chasing Riddles (146 v. JTF)

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Just after ReV145 Let's start with the obvious. The Codemaster was very, very unhappy right now. After he yelled at his scapegoats (Mega Job) over what happened on the 145th episode of Revolution, he simply shut himself in his hotel room and wouldn't come out. After a few minutes of pleading from the rest of the Blue Rogues, they gave up and let the Codemaster have what amounted to a temper tantrum. This temper tantrum, which basically involved the Codemaster putting on headphones and playing Mass Effect for a couple of hours, annoyed two people in particular within the Rogues. Alexandria Malone and Robert Falk. Alexandria leaned against the wall in front of the door to the hotel room, her arms crossed and her expression fairly huffy. After spending the last couple of days as the Maya Fey to El Janito's Phoenix Wright, she was seemingly comfortable again wearing her frilly black shirt and jeans, but she was now upset. She addressed her mood to the giant of a man standing nearby without really looking at him. "He does realize that we're supposed to sleep in there, doesn't he?" Alexandria asked. "Allen's not good at thinking about other people, Alex." Robert mused. Robert stood a foot and a half taller than Alexandria, but the differences in their moods made Robert feel smaller than her right about now. "I don't even ask for much, you know," the teenager continued, "Just a nice place to sleep once in a while, maybe get my hair done in a salon, buy some new clothes... make sure my cell phone is paid off so I can talk to my friends back home about what a crazy person I ended up working with, maybe find a teddy bear to send off to Anna, and maybe soak in a spa for like an hour and forget about my worries in the world." She paused, and reflected upon everything she said. "So, uh... when I said I didn't ask for much, I think I might've been fibbing." she said, after a moment. She crossed her arms and sulked. Robert thought about it, before he said to Alexandria solemnly, "You might not want to mention Annabelle around Allen. It's a touchy subject for him." Alexandria Malone hadn't been in the loop with regards to the Codemaster. She didn't know that he had harbored a crush on a girl named Annabelle Summers, who had been dating Coral Avalon. She didn't know that he attempted to win her over. She also didn't know that it was the main reason why neither Coral nor Annabelle were with the Blue Rogues any more. Robert knew all this. But he dared not talk about it to the biggest blabbermouth he knew, Alexandria Malone. That being said, it still piqued Alex's curiosity. With her hands behind her back, she leaned forward all coy-like and smiles at Robert, "Oh? Why's that?" Robert sighed, but he knew he couldn't tell her. Not yet. Not when Allen Brown was in such a chaotic mood. He knew the man for years, and had been working with him as a member of the Blue Rogues for a little over two years. He knew that there was something off about him, especially ever since the abrupt departure of Avalon. Robert knew of the incident, but since it was isolated to just between Coral, Annabelle, the Codemaster, and a promoter, he didn't really know any specifics. After that moment of reflection, Robert sighed, "Because it is." Alexandria scrunched up her face, as if she hated that response. She took her arms and resumed crossing them, looking up at the giant in front of her, "You're such a fuddyduddy." Robert frowned. He knew her brother, though not as well as the Codemaster or Coral did. Despite his history of acting like a goof, Joey Malone was a fairly easy person to understand. He always voiced his opinion clearly and precisely, and he always made his motives clear if people asked him about them. He wasn't one for being cryptic. If Joey had been in the Blue Rogues, there'd be a very good reason for it. Which led him to his next question, "Alexandria, why are you still here?" "Because that idiot locked himself in our hotel room and we don't have an extra key." Alexandria responded in a huff. Robert... suddenly felt like how Coral must have felt in hanging around Allen Brown for all this time, "That's not... what I meant." "Then what?" "Why are you still here... with the Rogues?" Robert asked. He had his hands on his hips, and looked down at the girl, which reminded Alexandria of that look Joey, who was about a foot taller than she was, gave her whenever she accidently broke something. Alexandria rubbed the back of her head. She let her arm fall from that position when she came up with an answer, and let it fall against her side with a plop, "You know why I came, right?" Robert thought about it, "Vaguely. Something about looking for answers about this business, I think." "That's right," Alexandria said. She looked up at the ceiling briefly, before she brought her gaze back up at Robert, "Well, I still don't know what I want to know. I want to know what it is about this business that destroyed my father and my brother, and seems to be destroying him as well." Upon saying the word 'him', Alexandria had pointed at the door of the hotel room where the Codemaster was, in her mind, likely curled up in a ball and crying his eyes out. He wasn't. In fact, the Codemaster had just shot some damn geth with his trusty shotgun and was on the road to feeling a lot better about losing. "Allen's problem has nothing to do with the business. It has to do with him being... well, him." Robert said, "His personality evokes a certain... unsettling... element." Robert didn't want to tell Alexandria that their employer had a bit of a sinister side to him, a side he's only ever shown a handful of people. Robert and his brother, as the most trusted confidants of the Codemaster, knew him well enough to be familiar with the other side of the man. He didn't want to tell her that the only reason why he appeared to be unravelling was because of what happened to him just a few weeks ago. And he didn't want to tell her about the real reason why the Codemaster wore the red-lined visor or the eye patch. Nevertheless, Alexandria was still curious, "He seems fine to me, but... at times, he just seems so erratic." "It's best not to dwell on it," Robert said, hastily enough that it made Alexandria think that the normally stone-faced Robert Falk was hiding something, "Instead, why don't we make sure that that those two idiots don't find any liquor." "You mean, Beef and Janito?" Alexandria asked. Robert frowned. "Look, I dunno about you, but after the yelling that Blair gave us when those two got drunk and wrecked half of our old bus, I think I'd want to make sure they never even think the word 'alcohol' again." Robert said, with a sigh. He turned around and started to walk away, and Alexandria quickly went into a jog so she could keep up with him. "Well, they're stupid," Alexandria said, after having caught up with Robert, "I wouldn't be surprised. Although, I'm suddenly mortified at the idea of the little one all boozed up." "You don't need to worry about Steve. The little guy can hold his liquor." Robert said. "I'd have never guessed." Alexandria said, impressed by the statement. Robert smirked and turned to look at Alexandria, "Well, it's not like you can tell if he's drunk or not. He only says one word at a time and he never slurs them. But, as far as I know, you could put a funnel in his mouth and pour a keg of beer, and the only thing the little guy will do is just stare at you afterwards." Robert paused, letting his statement sink in. "That's kinda creepy." Alexandria said, after she felt the pause was about to grow to uncomfortable levels. Robert nodded sagely as they reached the elevator, "I was just thinking that." Robert pushed the button for the elevator to come up. As they waited, Alexandria turned to Robert, "You were asking about why I'm still with you guys, right?" Robert only nodded to her. He had indeed asked the question earlier. "It's something Coral said to me. He said that this is a self-destructive business," Alexandria said, and upon seeing Robert nod in agreement, she continued, "My brother, Joey... he was alcoholic, he nearly lost his marriage and his family, and he can barely walk without a cane or somebody to help him now. And yet, all of that happened because of this business he supposedly loved. Coral was the one who told me this, and yet, when I prodded him about why he kept doing it despite knowing of its nature... he just said that it was his 'dream job'. I wanted to know what that meant." Robert nodded, and looked away from the girl with an almost sad look on his face, "That's right. This was his dream job." The sound of the elevator's 'ding' caught Robert's attention, and the doors slid open with a rolling sound. Robert stepped in, followed by Alexandria, who gave him a somewhat questioning look. "What was with the past tense?" Alexandria asked. This was another thing that Robert didn't want to tell Alexandria, so he simply corrected himself with a lie, "Sorry. I meant that it is. I probably just made the mistake because he's not with the Rogues any more." Alexandria clicked her tongue in annoyance, but accepted it, "Alright, fine." Inside his head, Robert Falk breathed a sigh of relief. He didn't want to be the one who told innocent Alexandria that Coral Avalon wasn't allowed to wrestle any more.
