Where For Art Thou? (Match RP - v Jameson)

Date: 05/09/05 Where For Art Thou?Written: 5th September, 2005. 'You wash your hands,You come out clean,You fail to recognise the enemy's within. You say we're not responsible,But we are.We are.' - Ana Johnson. I - 15:58. Karina Wolfenden's eyes focussed in on the piece of gravel lodged between the treads of her sneakers. Sat cross legged in the comforting arm chair, she used the plastic coated end of her un-tied shoelace to prise it free, watching it disappear off the mahogany coloured leather on its way down to the engulfing white carpet below. For 58 minutes it had been annoying her, and it felt good, in the lowest, petty way, to have finally scratched the itch. She was sure that the wall-mounted clock across from her had gotten louder since her last visit, and every tick had the slightest of echoes to it, mimicking the human heart. It was easy to notice the little things where she sat. Without the distraction of the gravel, Karina instead drummed her fingers against her calf, the rustling of pants against skin acting as the symbols. Despite the eyes resting upon her, the K-Wolf was engrossed in her own little solitary world. Over the past 59 minutes, her slender, toned frame had nestled itself into the chair so that her body was softly supported by the cushions, a comforting, almost womb-like experience... and one that, for once, didn't trigger claustrophobia. Over 6 months ago, PRIME had ordered Karina Wolfenden to go to this place as part of her 'anger issues'. Her overwhelming urge to respond to the HIT's jibes with violence almost left an official with a fractured cheekbone, and left Wolfenden 5 sessions away from having her contract torn up. She didn't say a word during those 5 hours, and every fortnight since, Kari had come back, doing exactly the same thing... or exactly the same nothing. Just another one of her many quirks, providing the entertainment of people's facial expressions when she fobbed them off with, 'sorry, can't, have to see my therapist.' Initially, she didn't really understand herself why she came back to just sit there. In the end, it became a psychological chess battle against herself, going back to hopefully one day realise why she kept going back. Maybe it was the smell that the room had to it, a lingering, sweet almond smell like Amoretto, carrying the bitter edge lingering from those allowed to smoke during their sessions. Or maybe it was the book shelves, stacked full of far too many volumes for any one person to read, that always seemed to have been rearranged between her appointments. Reminiscent of the hardest of 'spot the difference' puzzlers, it was equally as infuriating as it was intriguing. She was half tempted to ask how much for the chair, seemingly crafted for her form, but dragging it back for each session would just be more hassle than it was worth. And then, after 16 hours, 59 minutes, and 46 seconds, said her first words to Dr. Beaubier. 'This'll be my last session,' Kari remarked, breezily. 'Things change today, so, yeah... thanks.' II - 19:53. A month ago, she'd come to this place to throw a hopeful Hail Mary of a proposal Angelo Deville's way. Ominous thunder threatened to rupture the sky, while rain lashed down hard upon her. Karina had mimicked the atmosphere by storming in, refusing to let anyone or anything stand between herself and Angelo. With her emotions hanging by an ever fraying thread, she dropped to one knee and asked the Crimson Angel to marry her. Shoved uncomfortably onto the back foot, he couldn't even offer a yes or no answer, the stark black and white which ruled his life, as well as adorned his wrestling attire. He was swamped by grey, doubt, hesitancy, lightened by the hopeful thoughts of what could be. 'I... I don't know,' he'd confessed, looking down into wide, hopeful eyes. 'I need to, well... I need to think about this.' Her impulsiveness made his calm, calculated approach even more frustrating, but as he gave his hand to her, helping Karina rise into his arms, he'd decreed to give her an answer in a month's time. Karina's mind had become an egg-timer, pulse the hands of her clock. There were no days, just 24 hours closer to his answer. Knock, knock... The knocker made Karina wince as it hit, the instant sharp prod to the ears spreading out across her forehead as it resonated on further. The familiar slap of Deville's employees' feet on the hall floorboards failed to emerge as obedient and dutifully hasty as usual, while Kari sunk hands into pockets, waiting. 'C'mon...' she mumbled to herself, eagerness leaving fingers fidgeting in her pockets. Karina hopped down from the top step, still waiting after a minute. Striding back up, she slammed the knocker down even harder, nearly shattering teeth as the echo ravaged her skull. KNOCK! 'Hey!' she called through the domineering wood, not in the mood for delays as her very soul itched relentlessly for its answer, and hopefully, salvation. 'Angelo, get people who can do their job!' For another minute she waited, focus becoming more erratic as each unanswered second passed. Letting out a frustrated sigh, she hopped over the railings and down into the house's grounds, feeling her sneaker soles embed an inch or so into the moist lawn, refreshed from the early evening shower she'd just managed to miss. Putting the fact that she probably looked like a stalker to the back of her mind, Karina checked the windows, unable to see any hint of a light on, at least on the ground floor. As she encircled the house, Karina failed to spot a single window open, and couldn't pick up even the sense of movement inside. 'You're fucking kidding,' she hissed, already angered that Angelo's month had expired, and every minute of grace was digging him deeper into her bad books. Pausing, Wolfenden looked up to the bay windows that adorned Angelo's bedroom, not seeing the height as any real challenge. After cleaning off her soles against the house's abrasive brickwork, she found her footing in one of the close-to-ancient trees that dominated the grounds, and effortlessly began her ascent, rising in bounds rather than timid climbing steps. Betraying the fact that it wasn't the first time she'd taken such a route, Karina leapt the expanse between tree and house, finding her footing on the upper-floor's ledge, while securing herself on the window's shutter. 'Whatever you do,' the K-Wolf hissed under her agitated breath. 'Don't be in and suddenly decide to open the window...' Hanging from the shutter, Kari swung in front of the window, squinting to see through the blind. Scanning eyes back and forth, she managed to find some transparency, but the room was pristinely untouched. The bed was made, sheets not an inch out of place, and pillows stacked almost regimentally. No cupboard doors hung open, and no possessions were left scattered over any of the furniture. He was either just being his most anal, or he was gone. 'Don't you fucking do this,' Karina's doubt spoke for her as she spun across the window ledge to look in at a different angle. Not a hushed whisper, nor distant, cloaked footstep. Turning away from the window, the dejected K-Wolf stepped off the ledge, feeling her nemesis, gravity, lasso her leg and yank her fiercely to the ground. As the descent dilated nostrils with brief turbulence, the K-Wolf readied herself for the landing, using every inch of her endless legs to absorb the impact, collapsing into a forward roll to divert momentum from straight-downwards. Swiping her cellphone from its arm pocket, she frantically hit Deville's speed dial number, and waited for the ringing to permeate back out through the walls, and his voice to once again bless her ear. The last thing she wanted to hear was a click of disconnection, followed by a mocking, dead tone, which was all that greeted her. 'Angelo, Angelo...' lips softly mouthed to the twilight as her phone snapped shut. 'Where for art thou Angelo.' III - 23:33. Karina's heart was almost pounding its way out through her chest, ready to do its best reinactment of the classic scene from Alien. Each jolting pulse sent a sharp ache through her head, while merely crouching began to force beads of cool, nervous sweat out through her brow. Breathless words told the K-Wolf to calm herself as she gasped to get air in with panicking heaves. Something wasn't right, and Kari couldn't be sure if she really wanted to know what. With the back of her black glove, she mopped her brow, then tugged the hat that covered her multicoloured mane down to the tops of her eyebrows. It was a world she'd done everything in her power to avoid. Her sense of rampant curiosity had been deliberately sedated for both Karina's own safety and sanity, but now, to pull back what was hers, she was ready to get her hands dirty. Mud, sweat... blood, if necessary. Despite her own erratic schedule, Karina had learned Angelo's routine off by heart. She might not have wanted to open herself up to the reality of her lover's everyday life, but Karina wasn't naive... she knew that one day, she might need to wade her way through it, keeping one eye closed to the unsettling truth. It was just after 11:30, and in two minutes time, Angelo Deville should be getting out of a cab, and making his way down the shadowed alley. The environment was deliberate, as every time the streetlight was repaired, some object was ready to be hurled up and smash it. Barely giving away so much as a greeting, he'd shake a man's hand, then carry on through to the next street, where a different car was there to take the Crimson Angel on the next leg of his journey. Wolfenden couldn't even begin to think how many people Angelo shared out his 'income' between, but there were some things he liked to do by hand. These were the men he'd grown up respecting, obeying, and wanting to be... or as fate would have it, rule over. To send a lackey on the round would be to detach himself from the life he'd intricately sculpted around him. But, as the seconds ticked down, no vehicle could be heard approaching the all-but-deserted street. The sharp clunk of a bolt being slid open echoed along the damp, claustrophobic brickwork, and Karina again checked that the scant moonlight wasn't casting her shadow upon the open ground. Clad all in black, with her sternest look adopted, the K-Wolf asked herself every split-second what she was doing. For a woman who lived in the haze between dream and reality, sometimes she had to slap herself to gain clarity. This time, worryingly, she was free of doubt. A short, stocky figure then stepped out through what looked to be 10 inch thick doors, checking his watch in what scant light emanated from the store room he'd emerged from. A bald head and white suit contrasted the shadowy surroundings, and the flicking of a cigarette lighter gave a brief look at moss-covered, degrading brickwork. Angelo Deville was now thirty seconds late. A paltry amount, but for a man who replaced watches after 6 months in case they'd slipped a second, it was an eternity. 'Okay...' she whispered levels under her breathe. 'Anything I say now is going to sound lame, so let's just do this... damn, that sounded lame.' Rightly, the stocky man was sensing something in the stale air, polluted by festering drains from the restaurants opposite. Tilting his watch, he checked in better light, before he sensed movement just feet away from him. Faster than his synapses could hurriedly pass the message's baton along, the K-Wolf materialised in front of him, out-pacing a blink. Her knee zeroed in on his crotch, while the point of her elbow dug in under the Adam's apple. 'Where is he?' she seethed, staring down into the man's bemused eyes as her joints pressed harder against him. Rapidly the man blinked, still wondering where the possessed woman came from. Even the slightest struggle ground his right testicle between his own body and Karina's knee, only causing her elbow to further restrict his airway. But while his eyelids were dynamic, Wolfenden hadn't blinked, probing every dilation of the pupil. 'I said where is Angelo,' the K-Wolf continued to press as her victim choked out words. 'Ikif Ikhhnew ikwouldknot ucknot bkufh out uckhere...' he wheezed, forcing the words out and causing the kind of pain that the K-Wolf had no qualms in inflicting. Seeing an innocence to his shock, Karina hopped back, not dropping her hostile demeanour as she watched the man's 5'7 frame slump down against the wall, spluttering as he tried to refill his lungs and ease the pain of his groin. She had the jump on him, but in a fair stand-up, the K-Wolf wasn't egotistical enough to think that he couldn't ground-and-pound her. 'Not often I ever want to know this,' Kari then pondered, watching the stocky man push himself back upright, then dust the grime from the brickwork off his hands. 'Why no making with the revenge violence?' He looked back dumbfounded, as if she'd just asked him of one plus one really equalled two. 'Er, yah serious, lady?' he throatily enquired, before letting out a rasping cough to clear her throat. 'I know who yah are, and I only like the wife dishin' out the odd bit o'pain in the bedroom, if yah know what I mean? Not a life time o'agony if I lay a hand on yah.' 'Who am I?' she dryly asked in return, curious. 'What's with yah?' he replied, still trying to work out what was going on as his pains subsided. 'Where is he? Who'm I? I'm glad I ain't the only one confused here.' Karina didn't say a word, and the clear messages she could deliver with just the slightest tweaking of a facial muscle meant she didn't need to. 'You're Karina Wolfenden,' he told her. 'No offence, lady, but wearin' black and pullin' on a hat ain't a fool-proof disguise.' Taking another step back, Karina checked each end of the alley, neither book-ended by an awaiting car. 'Why hasn't he showed up?' Unbuttoning his shirt's collar, then loosening his tie, the stocky man was still recovering from Karina's elbow digging into his throat as he tried to find a less painful way to stand. Kari pulled her hat off and released her hair from its ties and clips, having suddenly felt self-conscious about her mock-worthy attire after the man's words. 'Hey, nobody's seen him all day,' the man revealed, holding his arms out in an open gesture. 'Still, I ain't gonna break routine. He don't show up, he don't show up. I don't show up? Then that means I gotta do some expalinin' if he shows up. I like the guy an' all, got a sense o'humour so dry it leaves me needin' a beer afterwards, but he's all caught up on routine, which I gotta obey.' She always knew where he was, at least that was what Karina had thought. When Angelo said he was heading out for an hour, it was just that, an hour. If she'd have set a timer, him being a second late would have only told her that the instrument was a fraction too fast. 'Has he ever been late, no showed?' Kari then pressed, her words still forceful despite the man's submissiveness, or even obedience. 'No so long as I been workin' here,' the man confessed, sounding as if he felt there should be a little more concern upon his behalf. 'But, hey, he's a busy guy. Maybe he got held up, yeah? I ain't sayin' I know much about anythin', but it ain't like anything woulda happened to him. I mean, he's Angelo fuckin' Deville, he's way past untouchable, he's intong-... er, whatever that thing is where yah hand goes through 'em?' 'Intangible,' Kari offered up through know-it-all instinct. 'Yeah, that's the one,' he seamlessly continued. 'Look, you want me to call you a car or somethin'? Droppin' your name around here'll get yah more than you'd think. Once he puts yah name on tha A-List or whatever yah wanna call it, anyone not treatin' yah like they would him, well, they're askin' for what they're askin' for.' Still taking it all in, the K-Wolf drops back against the alley's wall, immediately feeling all kinds of ugh soak into her black zip-up top. 'No, thanks,' she softly replied, distant with her thoughts. 'Bike isn't far from here.' 'I'll get a couple'a guys ta escort yah there,' he insisted, words polite and respectful despite the gruff, heavy accent. 'Some around here don't know the rules, so need a bit'o officiatin' sometimes. Jus' bein' curious though, how'dya know he was, well, not on time? I didn't imagine him keepin' yah in the loop an' all.' 'A month ago, at 7:53pm, EST, he said he'd give me an answer in a month's time,' Karina divulged, words heavy under the labour of worry. 'Time is up, and we both know him...' After a few seconds of not really knowing what to say, the gruff man looks back up to the K-Wolf, his rugged face trying not to blatantly show pity. 'Feel free ta land one on me if yah take this tha wrong way, but, maybe, yah think all this is his answer?' IV - Doesn't Matter. Bursting through the door to her apartment, the K-Wolf's wayward sense of reason had accepted that Angelo wasn't going to be there waiting for her, mischievous look able to soften her sharp defences. As she plunged her hand across the light switches, flooding the spacious apartment in the harsh amber glow, the shadows gave up their secrets... but not the secreted Crimson Angel. To the freezer she marched, yanking a bottle of vodka from the top compartment, then putting the frosted bottle to her lips. Jolting her head back, the K-Wolf sent a gulp of syrupy spirit scorching down her throat, before her face screwed up, mouth contracting to force the taste from the buds of her tongue. Not feeling numbed enough by the sudden hit, another followed, the liquor filling her mouth before she hurriedly gulped it down. Letting out a gasping breath, Kari should have been thankful there was no naked flame around to make the exhalation a fireball. Leaving the bottle open on the counter, Kari checked her answering machine, leaving a pleading glare resting upon the light for half a minute until she admitted defeat, accepting there was going to be no flash. Dragging her feet towards the floor-to-ceiling windows, the spent K-Wolf just stared out into the night, detail concealed by the glare from her lights. If, somewhere out there, Angelo was watching, she wanted him to be able to see her. Hands rested against the glass, followed by her forehead, then the tip of her freckled nose. Looking down to the street below, she longed to see the swept-back blonde hair that only she'd seen ruffled make its way towards the building... but as usual, the universe refused to bow down to her heart's demands. 'Why...' Karina questioned herself as much as the absent Angelo, looking to the moon, and half expecting to see the silhouette of his grinning face cast across it. Reaching to her right, she grabbed the chord for her blinds, hesitant about tugging on it, in case she were to obscure her vision a split-second before he could use the moon as his medium. But not even her deepest foray into a conscious dreamworld could detach her from the reality of it all, and as the blind collapsed, so did Karina. 'Fuck you,' wavered along with her bottom lip as Kari crumpled to her knees, dropping her uncaring front along with any hope of a buzz on the intercom. 'Fuck you, Angelo...'
