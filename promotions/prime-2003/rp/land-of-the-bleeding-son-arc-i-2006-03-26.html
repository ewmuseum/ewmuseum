Time flies when you’re having fun, or so they say. It’d become doubtful that Vangelus would find out anytime soon, seeing as his idea of ‘Fun’ in the Land of the Rising Sun rested on the shoulders of nursing Culture Shock wounds and watching a Japan oriented version of The Sopranos...although the latter wasn’t all that bad. The facial expressions made up diligently for the lack of language comprehension, and a year in Japanese Studies brought forth a vast understanding of the Japanese lingo...though minimum. Credits signaled the end of the fifth episode straight, crowning a breaking period in which Vangelus utilized, pushing himself through pain and ache alike, forcing himself into the kitchen of the highly unusual Japanese hotel room. He always knew their comic books were backwards...no one ever told him their households were as well. Nonetheless, he proceeded to open the mini-fridge, snatching up a bottle of Jack Daniels. Popping the top, he drowned the liquid, lingering its taste and dismissing the pain that conquered his body, even if only for a few seconds. Too much extension with his arm invited the pain back once more, though...and in the most unwelcoming sense. Vangelus let out a chronic groan that portrayed the pain he was feeling in the utmost respect. Cringing followed groans, forcing Olsig to flop unto the floor, pressing his now bleeding elbow against the emerald green carpet in order to halt its flow. As the pain took a slow yet sure decrease, a rapid vibration began to pound the glass table nearby, beating to the tune of “Get Stoned” by Hinder. His interest in that particular type of music had made it’s way to his cell phone’s ringtone. Vangelus sighed, knowing that on the other side of his Motorola SLVR was either one of the two; PRIME management wanting him to participate in some ridiculous promoting event or Chad Washington...His best friend and Agency Co-Partner. Through pain and annoyance alike, Olsig reached up and snagged the vibrating phone, flipping it open just as the tune reached it second go round of chorus. “Yeah...” he greeted. Not the most pleasant greeting, but then again he wasn’t the most pleasant being in the world either, so in the end it sort of configured nicely. “Vangelus, my boy!” An immediate deep sigh escaped Olsig as he realized exactly who it was on the other end of the SLVR. It wasn’t PRIME management, nor Chad...but instead the most unlikely of people. Hiro Shiro, Co-manager of the Brockens Investigation Agency (BIA), an investigation enterprise in which Vangelus was employed under. Realization brought him to figure out that he wasn’t as marketable a name as the Karina Wolfendens nor the Angelo Devilles, therefore he wasn’t garnishing the finances they were either. If he’d had a cat, he’d starve with the salary he was bringing in...therefore secondary employment almost seemed mandatory. “Hiro...my favorite Japanese Bigot. Other than that...the usual.” He stated, his secular accent drawing a chuckle from Vangelus. “I hear you’re in Japan.” Hiro states, obviously accurate in his quiry. Vangelus knew the direction this was going all too well, though. The sweet talked will be followed up by a sudden, last-minute demand. Olsig could do nothing but brace himself for what he’d be assigned this time. “You’d be correct, sir.” Olsig stated, annoyance hinted well in his tone. “Well I hope you aren’t too beat up after the fight with the clown, because I have a very special file I need you to cover.” Hiro stated, the ruffling of papers breaking through the speakers and unto the ears of a less than pleased Vangelus Olsig. Hiro was no doubt a firm business man, but his organization skills lacked in areas. How many areas should be accurately estimated by the constant ruffling of papers sounding through the mobile device, resembling the form of static. “Ah Hiro, I hate to interrupt you...but I have a match thi-...” “AH! He will explain the case in full detail to you. After he does so, I won’t you to jump right on it, you got me? This is a huge opportunity that we NEED to jump on. Just think of the affiliates we could gain from this project, baby! You can’t let me down.” Hiro continued to ramble on, completely oblivious to the fact that on the other side of the line, Vangelus had long since placed the phone down on the carpet, sparing himself and his ear tunnels the pain of having to endure his screechy accent and useless rambling. All Vangelus got out of the message was the where, when, and who...and from that point on, Hiro was talking to Emerald Green Carpet. “-in the Country...You hear me? Vangelus, do you hear me? Vangelus?” His screeches could even be heard with the phone lying flat on the carpet, although not as soundly.
