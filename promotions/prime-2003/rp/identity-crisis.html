Date: May To: [detail withheld] Subject: UltraViolence Dr. Ward, As I’m sure you probably know, UltraViolence is just a few days away. And, even though I haven’t been able to get in for an appointment for the past couple weeks… I’m assuming you’ve been following the show and are aware of my 5 Star Championship defense against High Flyer in a 2 out of 3 falls match. I have to admit, I wasn’t expecting this, but Tyler seemed adamant about the rematch. It struck me earlier today that during our last visit that you had remarked that things with Flyer were likely far from over. “Wrestling is never that easy” were the exact words you used, I believe. So, I was hoping, perhaps, you would have some advice on preparation. Last week, I did a little cross-dressing and pot smoking to try and get inside the minds of my enemies. Between hairspray and leather pants, snakeskin boots and dirty bongs, in the span of an hour, I lived the lives of an unhygienic Jesus and a gay cowboy (think Elton John meets Wade Elliott), and although I walked out of the match at 194 a winner… I doubt my stoned adventures in leather pants had much to do with it. Anyway, my time in PRIME has been spent mostly moving up and on, from one opponent to the next. This is the first true rematch I’ve had. Any pointers on how to approach the situation would be greatly appreciated. Also, switching gears a bit, it seems that in the last few weeks, things between Ellie and I have been tense. Actually, that might give the wrong impression. I should say, instead, that I’ve been on edge with Ellie as of late. In fairness, she’s been acting fine towards me; the issue seems to lie solely in my camp. Could it perhaps have something to do with the fact that I’m suddenly facing fatherhood in the face? I’m not sure exactly what the case is, and I know email isn’t the preferred method of communication, but until I can get in for another appointment, I’ll take your thoughts any way I can get them. Thanks Doc, Kaiser ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To: [detail withheld] Subject: Re: UltraViolence Kaiser, I must admit that I don’t care much for email. Might just be that I’m an old stick in the mud, who was getting a cell phone about the time other people were getting iPhone’s… but this really isn’t a form of communication I prefer. While I understand how difficult it can be to make time in your schedule as a professional wrestler, I’d really rather discuss these in person. UltraViolence is just a week away, but I’d be willing to do whatever it takes to squeeze you in for a visit. There’s so much that I know you want to discuss, and many things that I’d also like to speak about… I’m just not sure exchanging emails is the proper way to do so (besides, who wants to read a bunch of back and forth emails?) Go ahead and give Nancy a call to set something up. Or hell, fire me back another email for whenever works, I don’t care how early or late in the day, or what day… and I’ll pencil you in myself. - Dr. Ward Oh, and next time, please feel free to skip over any mention of you dressing up in leather pants. Those sorts of details are the type of thing that leave individuals in need of therapy. I know, because I talk to these people on a weekly basis  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To: [detail withheld] Subject: No can do Doc, First of all… did the guy who spent his career calling himself ‘The Inhuman Being’ just type a smiley face in an email? Seems like you’ve lost your edge. As for your wish for me to come in and talk things over face-to-face… I’m afraid that can’t happen. Ellie and I will be visiting her parents for the next couple of days, and then its straight in to a host of PRIME appearances promoting UltraViolence. I’d love to be able to get to Ohio between now and then, but it just ain’t gonna happen. To be honest, while I appreciate and understand why you would rather discuss things in person, I don’t see what the big deal is about offering up a little bit of advice by email. Is it ideal? No, but I doubt the world stops spinning on its axis because of it. They’re just a few questions or concerns I have. It’d sure be nice to walk out of UltraViolence one of the elite superstars of PRIME, gold still firmly around my waist. As someone who I know has experienced his fair share of blood feuds and big-time rematches… a little advice could go a long way. And meanwhile, I’ve just got a few personal concerns on my mind that it’d be nice to get your thoughts about. Lifting that weight of the world from my shoulders would certainly help me to focus on PRIME. I dunno. I guess I just feel like you’re not putting in as much effort to get Kaiser Vashaun over as you did to make a legend out of Tchu. All I’m asking is that you pay the same care and attention to my success as you did your own. What’s the big deal about that? - Kaiser ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To: [detail withheld] Subject: Re: No can do What’s the big deal? Really? I’m not sure where to even start with that one. First of all, you’re entire problem can be found in the first email you sent to me. Are you telling me you don’t see something disturbing in the fact that the first thing you sought advice about was a wrestling match, and that problem’s with Ellie were just an afterthought? You are making the same mistakes I made… getting too caught up in the competition of PRIME. It shouldn’t be the pinnacle of your life. You’re turning it into work, when in reality, PRIME should be fun… more like a hobby than a job. Is there something wrong with the fact that I don’t have the same burning, competitive spirit that I did just two or three years ago? Maybe in your mind, but I’m content with a life that doesn’t hinge on winning and losing, and if that means I put more effort into the success of Tchu than I am Kaiser… so be it. I don’t owe you anything, certainly not more than I’m giving. If that’s not enough, then I’m afraid you’re just going to have to live with that. I’ve proven myself… left my legacy. There are days I miss it, but those days are few and far between, and I’m satisfied with that. Do I really need to leave a second legacy? Make something of yourself if you crave the spotlight that badly. It’s not like you listen to me anyhow. You’ve become an arrogant, obsessive fool who can’t step back and look at the big picture because you’re too caught up in this game of winning and losing. Have it your way. - The Human Being ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ To: [detail withheld] Subject: (no subject) Matt, That’s quite convenient of you… act like you’re gonna severe ties and be done with it. We both know it’s not that simple. We’re connected… permanently. And you’ve got some nerve sitting there and painting me as this sick bastard. I’m not saying that you’re wrong… I’m sure there are many unsettling and ugly things about me, but we both know that I’m a monster you created. Your contention that I don’t listen to you is ridiculous. I do everything you tell me to do. I act at your hand, Matt. There is so much of me in you… that’s why you took me under your wing, gave me life in the first place. Of course I’ll repeat your mistakes… we share the same flaws. I am on the cusp of greatness, knocking on the door of immortality. You’ve been there before, and you want to play your role and do so again. That much I’m sure of. You can’t get PRIME outta your blood, and you shouldn’t be ashamed. Part of you still craves it, and so here I am. Are things perfect with Ellie? No. Are things between you and Mary perfect, Matt? Somehow I doubt it. That doesn’t mean that everything else needs to be pushed to the side and ignored. C’mon… you know that. The words you speak… the way ya try too hard to make it clear that you’re done… I can see right through it. And I’m not even the one with a Psych degree. So stop trying to fool yourself. Stop trying to fool me. They’re equally pointless. Help me defeat High Flyer at UltraViolence. Help me take that next step and capture the Universal title. Help me beat a guy like a Jason Snow and cement my legacy. Your hunger for success hasn’t vanished, Matt. Otherwise, you wouldn’t be sitting there writing these emails. - KV
