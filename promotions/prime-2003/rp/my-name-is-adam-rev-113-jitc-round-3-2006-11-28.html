...My Name Is Adam (ReV 113, JiTC Round 3)

28 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/11/06 It’s backstage at ReVolution, T-minus fifteen minutes to the third match, and Adam is pissed the fuck off. He pulls white tape round his right wrist tight then tucks it in under the previous few layers before launching his fist into the locker beside him, crumpling the door down. What the fuck did I do to have my bye skipped over? Clear as fucking day the line-up read I was to take on the winner of Sun Tzu and ITO; double DQ leaves a free space this round, and someone else gets it?! Where’s the fairness in that? He yanks his hand back out the locker, wincing as he feels a sharp rip. A quick glance at his hand reveals that, yup, he’d slashed it good on a torn shard of the flimsy metal. Fucking hell! One fucking injustice after another… He gives the locker a hard kick as he pulls his quickly-reddening hand in to his chest. Shaking his head in anger he heads for the sink and sticks his hand under the tap, unwrapping the tape once he has a steady stream of freezing cold water pouring into his sliced palm. I’m due this round out, but I’m sure some fucking smart-arse somewhere decided a count-out “doesn’t really count” and that some other cunt deserves the break over me. He glances up at the clock on the wall, cursing as he sees time marching on. Shit… I don’t have time for this pish. Ruining a perfectly good white towel, Adam wraps his hand in it and holds it tightly under his left arm, keeping pressure on the wound. Life isn’t fucking fair, I swear to Hoyt. Deciding to take a breather to calm down and let the blood-flow ease a little, Adam takes a seat on the bench. The Scot takes long, slow, deep breaths and attempts (as best he can, just minutes before his match) to chill out. What if life was fair, though? What if I deserved all this shit? He shudders involuntarily at this, the shake running the whole length of his spine. I sure as hell know I deserve my belt, I earned that… but what about all the shite that happens to me? What if I’d earned it all? His hand had basically stopped bleeding now, but the thoughts running wild in the Scot’s head now had taken over and he was barely even aware of the slowly-pulsing throbs of pain any more. A drug-addled wife, bratty kids, a so-called ‘best friend’ who’s just absconded and deserted me for the third time in as many years; have I done enough wrong to deserve that? Maybe… Noticing the dull ache emanating from his injured hand he squeezes more firmly with his other arm, grimacing a little with the twinge of pain. Is terminal loneliness something else I’ve warranted with how I’ve lived my life? The gash across his palm forgotten again, Adam starts to mentally review his life; every wrong-doing from age four up. It’s gonna take a while, to say the least. Okay, so I deserve some level of grief for all the shit I’ve caused… but that’s all personal. My actions here don’t warrant all the shit I get put through in and out the ring in PRIME. Sure, I’ve screwed around a little here, maybe been a little lax with the rules once or twice – but that was a long time ago. A stagehand’s head pops round the door, his knocking having been ignored. Adam briefly snaps from his trance-like space-out and registers the intrusion with a raised eyebrow. Two hands with spread fingers are raised, Adam simply nodding to register the universal ‘10 minutes’ signal. The belt, I earned my belt, I know that. He comforts himself with that thought as he stands up to tend his wound. But losing my bye; what’d I do to merit that? Is it karma? I suppose I did get a bye of sorts already, but that’s gotta be some human thing – someone, somewhere decided I didn’t warrant another one. After unzipping his bag one-handed (with all the difficulty entailed) he begins to rustle around for his mini-medikit. What exactly is that karma crap again? Damn, I knew I should’ve watched that stupid show… After a bit of a fight with the child-proof opening Adam gets in and finds a small bandage. Is everything I do offset with some consequence later? Is my life entirely cause and effect? Adam tears open the packing with his teeth and gingerly unwraps his hand, drawing a little more blood as the towel pulls at the slowly congealing lesion. Ahhh, damn that hurts… Where was I? The towel is briefly reapplied to stem the light flow while Adam re-boards his train of thought. I’ve beaten Killean before now: one, two, three. I don’t care what anyone says, I could’ve taken him again in the first round. Was that count-out some divine reward for something? What did I ever do to earn that? It’s awkward, but he manages to bandage his right hand not too badly using his left. The tape would keep it on snugly anyway. Maybe I need the extra match, though – maybe it’s a way to prove I should be at King Of Kings, to show that it’s not only because Killean couldn’t make it to the ring that I got there. Gripping with his right hand is a little sore, but bearable, as he holds the start of the tape that’s now winding round and round and round his forearm. Jesus… I mean, it’s not like I haven’t been paying my dues here. Week-in, week-out I take abuse in that ring – I’ve played fair for a long, long time; try and tell me that doesn’t merit more than a little good karma in return. He finishes up the winding and tucks the tape away again, abstaining from destroying another locker this time. I’ve worked my ass off for this company; why shouldn’t I get a chance for a shot at the man on the mount? The Eternal Underdog takes on PRIME’s Top Dog – now that’s a fucking billing. There’s a knock at the door, to which Adam responds with a grunt. Another head pops round and gives the ‘two minutes’ signal. Adam silently nods to him. Pity life isn’t fair, though – means I’m gonna have to work for what I’m owed. He straightens up, flexing his shoulders back and clenching his fists by his side. Well… fucking bring it.
