Shopping

23 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/23/05 I’m not a religious person, but I am convinced that if there is a Hell, it looks like a mall. Seriously, a mall looks like a concentration camp for everything that’s wrong with this country. Doughy blobs shuffling from one cookie-cutter store to another, buying whatever the airhead cosmetology-school-washout salesgirls tell them to buy because their too fucking lazy to go anywhere else. The only exercise these wastes of flesh get is the ride on the escalator going to the food court to gorge themselves on Chick-Fil-A and Mrs.’s Field’s chocolate chip cookies. And the worst of the lot is the fuckers on the scooters. I have yet to see ONE truly handicapped person use one of them. Personally, I don’t think an inability to see your own feet or take a shit without sweating qualifies as a disability. But maybe that’s just me. So why am I standing here in a place I loathe amongst people so pathetic and ugly that I wish Osama bin Laden would skip the skyscrapers and target anyplace that has the words “Centre,” “Shoppe,” or “Faire” in it’s name? Faith. God damn Faith. She’s so sucked into the Body Shoppe’s hippy culture that she insists on getting all her stuff from there. She ran out of tea tree oil and conned me into picking some more up for her. Hey, it’s just about impossible to say “no” to a hardbody like Faith when she naked, no matter what the question is. I mean, we were in her bed and she was sitting up, resting on her elbows. I was just looking at her. From that face that’s been on so many magazine covers… to those breasts just big enough to cup in my hand… to the chiseled lines of her abs and that navel I could spend hours licking... down to those few dark hairs just peeking out from above the whiteness of the satin sheet. Faith was talking, but I had no idea what the words were. Finally, something cut through the fog and I realized she had just asked a question. I hoped for the best and answered “yes.” BIG mistake. So I'm leaving the store after buying Faith's tea tree oil from some chubby chick that probably thins she's a witch or something. I'm getting my ass out of that damn place. When this frat boy looking mofo steps in front of me. He's staring right in my face. I'm about to knock this asshole aside when he suddenly gets really exited. 'Dude! It's you!' 'Uh, yeah...' 'You're that guy!' 'Uh, yeah...' I have no idea what the hell is up here, but other people are stopping to look at this idiot screaming at me. 'I've seen you on TV! You totally kick ass!' Oh. He must be a wrestling fan. PRIME. I get it now. I was confused at first because he didn't seem like the usual Gap-addict queens that recognize me from modeling. 'Thanks, bro.' Someone has recognized me from PRIME. This is great. I knew it would be a launching pad for me. I'm finally getting fans that don't TiVo Ellen DeGeneres's talk show. Looking at the frat boy, I play it cool so he doesn't realize that I'm probably as thrilled about this as he is. 'Dude, you gotta give me an autograph!' Normally I'd charge him about fifteen bucks for the honor, but I'm feeling generous since this will be my first wresting autograph. 'Sure. No problemo, bro.' 'Sweet!' The frat boy is looking for something for me to sign. He goes through is pockets, but comes up empty. Then he decides to check his shopping bags. Foot Locker. Game Stop. Sears? The sacrifices I make for fame… 'I know I got a receipt or something I can use...' As he searches, I remeber I have a match this week. Who is it against again? Uh... Quicksilver? No, wait. Youngblood. I knew it was an 80's movie title. That was a great flick. Hockey fights should be their own separate sport. And I don't get all the shit Rob Lowe got over that video tape... if there's grass on the field, play ball. 'Found one!' The guy waves a wrinkled sales receipt. Then his smile vanishes as his jaw drops. 'Dude, I don't have a pen!' 'It's all good. I got one right here, bro.' I take out a pen from my jacket pocket and click it. The frat boy grins and hand me the receipt. He turns around. 'Here, use my back.' I shrug a put the receipt against his back. I'm just about ready to press the pen down when he speaks up again. 'This is so awesome! So what's Katie Holmes like?' 'Uh… cool, I guess.” I don’t know why he’s asking about Katie Holmes. Sure, I’d give up a testicle to hit that, but I’ve never really hung with her. I think I met her at premiere once. I took my shot, but she blew me off because she was engaged to Chris Klein. I tried explaining the difference between “engaged” and “married.” Nothing wrong with a few test drives before you sign a lease. But that goody-goody was no-go. Her wholesomeness turned me on even more. I ended up picking a hooker with a passing resemblance to her and doing to her things that would make that farm girl run to confession. “So what was it like being around her all the time on ‘the Creek?’” “Huh?” “Y’know, ‘Dawson’s Creek.’ That show you were on.” ‘Dawson’s Creek?’ What? And then it hits me. This… this… FUCKER… thinks I’m James van der Beek! I mean, yeah, he’s cool and all that, but still. The guy has no idea who I am! He doesn’t want Ellis Easton’s autograph! He wants James van der Beek’s! The entire mall feels like it’s on fire. My eyes are watering as my mouth grows dry. I snarl and stab the pen into the fucker’s back, pinning his sorry receipt to him. He falls to his knees howling. I barely notice as I head to where I think the nearest exit is. The other pasty losers clear out of my way, looking slack-jawed out of horror as well as stupidity. I see the glass doors with sunlight shining through like the light at the end of the tunnel. As I throw the doors open and step out into the embracing air, I hear a hysterical voice. “Oh my fucking God! James van der Beek stabbed that guy!”
