Date: September 23, 2008 Slender, pale digits ran through beautiful blonde extensions, shining platinum in the pale light of the Admiral Benbow Inn. Named for Jim Hawkins' place of residence in Treasure Island, however nothing happened in this seedy excuse for a hotel worth treasuring. Her face had a streak of glitter and she was nearly as pale as Locke. If some lucky fucking police officer were to stumble upon the pair's room, the air of marijuana and dirtyer substances pouring out of the cracks of the door to the outside of the two story box known as the Admiral Benbow would sliver up his nostrils and alert him to the dangerous drug-abusers inside. However no officers ever stopped by the Benbow unless they were trying to reach a few of their informants, who all seemed to swim in the same little pond. So it was a perfect location for Rhett to take his newly purchased whore. This was, no doubt, a spontaneous decision. It was not the normal behavior of the relatively shy Locke to approah a slut. We say slut because she was a damaged, drug using shadow of what was once possibly a promising future. Sometimes the road makes a curve you don't expect. Most people react accordingly. She was too fucked up to notice and wrecked into a four figure salary of perverse wish-fulfilling, so long as the money was up front. He had enjoyed her and in fact even had a good time. If he asked and she answered honestly, she'd have said the same. Locke had a way around people, creating a mystery entirely on accident. His shy demeanor was always being mistaken for quiet confidence. Rhett supposed that came with the muscular, scarred frame... however pigment challenged the frame may have been. Though most of her proverbial clientele was polite enough to let her toke a joint or two, the Albino had a drug collection that'd make the hair on the back of Hunter S. Thompson's neck rise. We would give you a count but Locke wouldn't even know what all he had in that briefcase if he tried. Hidden between folded up pajamas and what have you were all sorts of narcotics. Some were to be snorted, others were to be injected and of course there was enough to smoke for a two way road trip to New England. Where exactly was he, anyway? That was a question that proved only that he needed to wait to leave. Not remembering where you are is always a good sign to stay put. Which he didn't mind. This beautiful, fucked up angel in front of him would not mind either. So long as the money was up front. He would've engaged her once more. Her smooth legs and coy demeanor was enough to entice any poor fool like Rhett. "How do you want it?" She asked. ----------------------------------------------- Rachel Locke, mother of one and a diamond to behold. Her lips were too kissable, unintentially inviting all sorts of assholes to drop their best lines on her. She enjoyed the attention. The difference between your run of the mill whore and an attention whore is that an attention whore does not cost money. Only time and patience. A compliment will get you as far with her as a ten dollar bill would with her counterpart. Her only son, Rhett, a quiet but intelligent albino child who'd one day no doubt overcome his "handicap" and make something of himself watched as she made love to the man. In a squeaky, hotel bed that was stained from protein and cigarettes, he watched her from a corner he'd made his bed in. He saw and he often times cried, though quietly. It was said a pin drop was louder than Rhett's cries for help back then at the age of four. This was one of the truer statements of the 1900s. He knew he would get in trouble for just watching but he saw an evil in this act that his eyes could not avert from. Though it happened so frequently, he could only perceive the man as hurting the mother he loved so much. Later in life, these memories that had blocked themselves in an attempt to protect his psyche would emerge from his subconscious. But for now, it was only something to be forgotten. Along with the rest of the night. She screamed. The man she was with, one Rhett had yet to meet, bit her shoulder. What kind of horrible creature would do this to his mother? Few people on the planet could've explained to Rhett in that moment what was happening to Rachel. Perhaps less than that, even. Rachel yelled a final time, "FUCK!" Her eyes were glazed over drunk, also known as a white flag of surrender to any piece of shit attempting to pick her up. They always did. Easy women have a way of conveying how easy they are without making much of an effort. "Rhett," she called from the bed. The little boy, blonde hair favoring his mothers, stood and bit a fingernail nervously. She bleached her own that way to make him feel better about his. "Could you get me my night gown?" He ran in an anxious fever to the coat rack, fetching her silk, teal gown and handing it to her hurredly. This was her signal, they were done for the night. "Aww, so soon? I'm not ready to go yet..." The man said. He was big. He towered over both Lockes in the room that night. Also, he imposed fear in their hearts. "Too bad." She grinned, placing a kiss on the man's nose. "My son has preschool tomorrow." Her jacket was on now, purse strung over her shoulder. He only laid back, grinning at the next joke he'd make. "Is he gonna brag to all of his friends about what he saw his mommy do tonight?" That crude question was followed by a sick, hoarse laugh. "Fuck you." They casually stepped outside, her comforting hand on Rhett's back, guiding him to the safety of their vehicle. In a time like 1982, even in the town of Truth or Consequences, you never knew what could happen in the middle of the night. He'd fall asleep in the car on the drive home.
