Doctor, Doctor, Give Me the News (Great American Nightmare, Universal Title Defense vs. Sonny Silver)

Date: November 2, 2007 "I want you to close your eyes and relax. Clear your mind." I'm trying very hard not to point out the obvious flaw in this plan, that being that I don't know how to relax and can't remember the last time that I did. But the voice is soothing in a way that I haven't heard in some time, and I figure...fucking aye, it can't hurt, right? The illuminated shadows from the overhead light dance around the back of my eyelids, and I try very, very hard to not have a conversation with the voice in my head, saying how completely stupid this whole thing is. I remember then that I've done this one time before, when my job put me in a seemingly insurmountable position that I didn't think I'd make it out of, and that it helped. So I put the voice to bed. I don't think of the road I took to get here. I don't think of breaking Sonny's arm, or my breaking the Colossus Curse. I don't think of my now-healed knee and how I worked through the pain. I don't think of the Roulette and the odds that I beat and I don't think of third times being charms at the Great American Nightmare. All of these thoughts I could think, but I don't. I watch the shadows fade, pinpoints of light winking into the blackness, and I nearly miss a very pointed question.... "When did you turn into Sonny Silver?" ~**~**~**~**~ "So that's gutter, drain and yard cleaning, including leaf raking, grass cutting and removal/disposal of the waste. Anything else, ma'am?" "Hrm?" FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 2, 2007. 3:15PM. CASA DI TROY: NORTHEAST. Lindsay Troy had been standing in front of her opened garage for what seemed like eons, oblivious to the cool autumn wind, the shouts of high-schoolers making their way down her leaf-lined street and the voice of Mark from Horgan Enterprises, whom she had hired to take care of her never-ending yard work. She snapped out of her reverie and turned her head to the slightly shorter man, a faraway look in her eye. "I'm sorry. What did you say?" Mark rubbed the back of his head, embarrassed for interrupting her private thoughts. "I was just asking if there was anything else you wanted done. I know you went over the details with Michael over the phone. I don't want to miss anything." Can you mow Sonny over with your John Deere? Or would his flesh ruin the blades? "No, uh, I'm good," she shook the thought from her head. "Thanks." "Alright then. We'll get to work." Lindsay nodded slightly, then set her gaze back to the empty space which once housed her father's '68 Lamborghini Miura, left to her after his passing. It was gone now...long gone, set afire in Providence after ReVolution 142. The police, after finally finishing their investigation which included rounds of questioning and analyzing security tapes, had a suspect in mind, but at Troy's insistence were holding off on making any arrests for the time being. Her snap judgment to wait until after the Great American Nightmare came as a shock to not only Rhode Island's finest but to herself. After the phrase escaped from her mouth, she caught herself doing a double-take...then realized after that she would rather use the fuel to her advantage than to undermine the potential to inflict her own form of embarrassment on the culprit. Misery loves company and, for some unknown reason, Troy lately has only felt happy when she could spread her sour mood around; mainly on Sonny Silver. The clanging of metal on vinyl siding again snaps Troy from her thoughts as the workers start their tasks. Engines are fired up in the front of the property and a too-tall ladder is scaled to reach the height of the house and the gutters found there. She watched the men for a time, then turns her attention to two figures walking up her driveway. "Heya Lindsay!" Constance McKenney shouts above the noise, her son Nolan walking by her side. "We don't see much of you around. Thought we'd say hello while the opportunity presented itself!" "I'm glad you did. How are you two?" "Oh, fine, fine. Keeping busy with the kids and work. Never a dull moment in my household." Lindsay smiled, and the two make small talk. Never one for neighborhood gossip, considering her Florida residence has no neighbors to speak of, Troy listened with half an ear. It's only when a vibration from the phone in her pocket was able to grant her a moratorium. "Would you excuse me for a second?" she asked, retrieving a white Blackberry Pearl from her back pocket. The number on the whole was unmemorable, save for the area code, which Troy regarded with a squint and a click inside her head. Constance gave her a nod of affirmation, one that wasn't needed for her leave to be taken yet she waited for out of politeness. Lindsay walked toward the deck, giving the workers' ladder another quick glance while her thumb pressed the answer button. "Yes," the opening statement was delivered coldly, and it took a moment for a reply to come through, either out of bad reception or shock. "Well," the man's voice on the other line uttered, straight-forward. No-nonsense. But not without a brush of bravado. "This is certainly a surprise." "Is it?" Troy's retort dripped with the smirk that was forming on her lips. "Considering who I'm talking to, I thought nothing would surprise you. King's to me, then." "So it would seem, for the moment." A chair squeaked. Leather settled. "Now that we've gotten the pleasantries out of the way--" "Which one is it?" Unwilling to play on any side but the offense, Troy cut right to the chase. An ominous silence filled her ear and she wrapped her arm around her torso, waiting for the caller to part the brewing storm clouds. He finally does. "You're of the impression that you can boss me around." "I am. But let's not pussyfoot through the pansies. Given the way this relationship is, you were given three scenarios as to when it should be deemed appropriate to call this number. So I am asking you, which is it?" Dead air. "None? How utterly shocking." "There's another matter..." "No, there's not," she could feel her hand clutching fabric, wanting to tear it in a rage. "I don't care how cute you think you're being or how pretty you think you are. You've managed to follow directions thus far. It's in your best interest to keep doing so." "You're in over your head," the foreboding snarl lodged itself in her brain and sent a tremor down to her stomach. "And you're not? My ass is more than covered. How's yours?" The same thumb that started the firefight, ended it. Lindsay jammed the phone back into her pocket, mustered up the most pleasant smile she could, and walked back over to her neighbors. "Sorry about that. Ex-husband. Seems he crawled out of the stone age long enough to remember how to dial a phone." "My friends like to joke that Joey Melton's one of the guys on the Cavemen show." "Nolan!" Constance exclaimed and gave her son a mortified look. "I'm so sorry. That was inappropriate of him." "It's not slander if it's truthful," Lindsay laughed. "Well, we should really be going," She clamped her hand on her son's shoulder. Nolan's face turned uncomfortable. "Given my son's budding knowledge of all things personal, I'm sure he'd love to report to my family just how he made dinner tonight." "Awww maaaaaaaaaan." "Let's go, Nolan." Constance guided Nolan down the driveway, but the teenager managed to throw a glance over his shoulder and caught Troy's knowing wink. ~**~**~**~**~ My eyes fly open, and I struggle to sit up. Shoot up. Whirl around and glare with heated eyes and tempered fury. But the weight of the world keeps me pinned to the couch, and I can only muster a spat of bile. "I'm nothing like Sonny Silver." "Oh no? Let's recap here. You hired a P.I. to tail his estranged wife to find out if she found someone else or was holding out hope that he'd come around. You broke his arm after he knifed you in the back...something you KNEW he'd do at one point. You dumped your husband in the second biggest swerve of your career on a pay-per-view, destroying a plot against you that you countered in silence. And that's without mentioning your toying with a different man's emotions, gaining his trust and love only to stab him in the back at the behest of his half-brother, your lover. Tell me that these are not comparable things that Sonny Silver has done or would do to remain one step ahead of the rest of the pack." I say nothing. I have nothing. And, the evaluation continues. "You may have out-Sonny Silver-ed Sonny Silver. And while that hasn't been necessarily a bad thing for your career, it eats you up inside. Eats away at you. I know. I know you don't want to be like that. "I know you know." I'm finally able to sit up, look my evaluator in the eye as she continues. "*YOU* are your own worst enemy. You found that out in a dream, just as you discovered that you're also your best and worst judge of character. Funny how those two parts of you haven't managed to kill one another." "How is it that everything always comes back to me? The dream before the Roulette? Here?" "Because this is our world. Everyone else is allowed to play in it. I'll never get tired of looking at my smirk. "I would recommend relaxing tonight. Throwing the headphones on. Take a load off. It'll help with focusing before the Nightmare." "And what a Nightmare it will be." Auxiliary Readings The original
