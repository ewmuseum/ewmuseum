Phoenix Bloody Phoenix (ReV 131, vs. Shakur/Gamble)

26 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 06/26/07 The Ford Center, Oklahoma City, immediately following the end of the Pay-Per-View broadcast… Staff medics support Nova from both sides as he limps slowly down the stairs from the gorilla position to the primary backstage hallway. A man approaches with a clipboard and a medical jacket. “How many times I gotta tell ya, enough taking these powerbombs and stacking our workload, alright?!” The Risen Star doesn’t have the strength to smile. “Hardy…fuckin’ har. Good to see ya as always, Ray.” “Yeah, well I wish I saw a lot less of you, pal,” Ray replies, motioning one of the medics to step away as he moves in and slips his arm under Nova’s left shoulder. “There’s an ambulance already burnin’ gas at one of the side entrances. Allow me to escort you, monsieur.” “I don’t need an ambulance, Ray,” Nova grunts, “I need a painkiller smoothie, light on ice and heavy on bourbon.” “Agh, God, don’t let me hear that shit,” Ray replies, wincing, “I’m a doctor, I know what that shit actually does to you.” “So do I,” Nova says, finally managing a grin, “and it’s maaaagical.” His grin fades as they round a corner, and what was a bustling, lively hallway goes dead silent. Conversations between loaders and technicians vanish into the air, echoes dancing around the walls. “…and these tits, man, unbe-fucking-LEE-oh, shit…” “I think we’re gonna be here all…” Every head turns at the entourage following Nova as he’s helped down the corridor. Blank faces stare openly at the former champion, except when he meets their eyes. Oh, well. I’ll take ambivalence over disgust. “What’s the matter, no one ever seen an injured wrestler before?” Ray says, loudly enough that everyone suddenly goes back to what they were doing. “Fuck ‘em, Nov,” he says quietly into the Risen Star’s eye. “You’re the man, Ray,” Nova replies tiredly. “Looks like we’re about there, big guy,” Ray observes, “just wing a right here…and yeah, you see that door up ahead, that’s us. Straight on through to the other side!” “Thanks for the play-by-play, Mr. Morrison,” Nova sighs, rolling his eyes, “You know I don’t think this visit to the ER is necessary.” “Well, thanks for indulging me,” Ray replies, returning the eye-roll, “now on we go, out towards the red…um…blue…lights?” As the assisting medics push open the doors to the side entrance, there’s no waiting ambulance, but rather three police cars, blue lights flashing, and practically an entire squad of police officers standing at attention. The vocal officer from the roof of the Ford Center earlier in the evening steps forward. “I think we can take it from here, guys, thanks.” The sneer practically drips off his round, double-chinned face. “What the fuck are you talking about?” Ray growls, “where the fuck is our ambulance?” “We gave ‘em the go-ahead to return to the hospital,” the officer replies, unfazed by Ray’s outrage. “Your charge is coming with us…sir.” “This man needs medical attention!” Ray protests, “He could have a spinal injury, he could have a concussion, he…” “He could have made the decision not to fly a private aircraft through downtown Oklahoma City,” the officer interrupts, his confident smirk still drip-dropping down onto the concrete floor. “Now listen here, Officer…Grayson,” Ray replies quickly, glancing at the man’s uniform, “he’s fuckin’ bleeding. Look at him. He. Needs. Medical. ATTENTION.” “No,” Officer Grayson fires back, “what he needs is to understand that we know he was intoxicated when he landed that craft onto the roof, and we aren’t currently investigating that aspect of the crime at hand. Currently.” Ray looks over to Nova for confirmation, to which the Risen Star replies with a tired shrug and another grin. “Let it go, Ray.” “Yeah, let it go, Ray,” one of the officers standing behind Grayson mocks. “I’m not letting this go,” Ray whispers into Nova’s ear, “I’m gonna do something about this, Nov, don’t worry…” “Hey,” Nova says, fatigue obviously kicking it up a notch, “does this…look like the face of someone who’s worried?” “Alright, lovebirds, time for Captain Nighthawk here to take a ride downtown,” Grayson interjects. Ray nods and they step away from Nova, who stands shakily on his feet. Grayson motions with his hand for them to “shoo,” and reluctantly they do, Ray offering the blue shirts both of his middle fingers as the medics all depart. “So…,” Nova says when he’s alone with the police, “they take Sonny away in one of these paddy wagons, too?” “That’s none of your fucking business,” Grayson snaps, all traces of civility held in check by the presence of the medics now evaporated, “get in the car, rock star. Now. Or we’ll put you in it.” Nova nods slowly. “So...I’m pretty bushed. Anyone else here know how to roll a joint?” Oklahoma County Jail, twenty-five minutes later… “I just can’t fuckin’ believe this man, really…” For at least ten minutes now, Nova has listened to the burly faux-Hell’s Angel on the bench opposite him rattle on about how his very presence in this communal cell is astounding, mind-blowing, extraordinary… …and the Risen Star concurs, honestly. “I mean, shit, man,” the man continues, waving his coked-out (Nova’s guess) arms emphatically, “a few hours ago, my kid was watching you on NATIONAL TELEVISION, getting the living SHIT beaten out of you no less, and now you and I are sitting in the same fuckin’ jail cell, man! THIS! IS! FUCKING! GREAT!” “Yeah,” Nova replies with a sigh, the blood from Overkill still crusted to the side of his face, “yeah, it’s pretty…pretty awesome.” His attention is thankfully diverted as a guard approaches and slides open the cell door. “Vega! You can go. Someone’s here for you.” The Risen Star raises his eyes listlessly, and they fall on the most beautiful thing he thinks he may have ever seen in his entire life: Lindsay Troy, with a set of car keys dangling from her fingers. “Well?” She says with a wink, “you comin’, or are you just gonna sit there with your new friends and bleed all night?” “DUDE!” the man across the cell shouts, standing up, “She was on the show, too! THIS IS THE BEST JAIL EVER!” Windshield wipers flick rain away as Lindsay’s rental car speeds on in the night. She turns to Nova, whose head lolls around against the rest. “Wake up, jerk,” she says half-playfully, “I called in a huge favor to get you out, considering in the lovely state of Oklahoma if you’re in the lock-up after twelve, you’re in for the night…so how’s about staying awake for the drive back to the hotel?” “Yes, ma’am,” Nova says groggily with a nod. “Listen, Caes,” Troy goes on, her voice taking on a serious tone, “I want you to know I didn’t want to throw you to the wolves out there. I...I wanted to run in, and stick both fists down Chainz’s…” “Lindz,” Nova interrupts, “Matt was right to hold you back. I deserved every inch of that ass-kicking, and twenty thousand people knew it. You told me I’d deserve it when it happened, and it happened, so don’t sprout any curly little gray hairs over it, okay?” In spite of the solemnity of their conversation, Troy cracks a smile. “I did say that, didn’t I?” “Yeah, you did,” Nova replies, nodding as much as his neck will allow. “Now that I’ve conceded defeat, can I pleeeeease smoke a cigarette?” “No,” Troy denies, “it’s a rental.” Biltmore Motel, Oklahoma City… “Come on, a few more steps…” Lindsay coaxes Nova as they near the top of the stairwell to the second floor of the motel. “You…just had to choose the second floor, didn’t you?” the Risen Star mutters grumpily as he continues to limp over steps. “Well, when I made the reservations I didn’t exactly have ‘Carrying an Injured Stoner’ at the top of my priority list,” Troy fires back. “Now, inside with you.” She knocks on the door, and Ray opens it from the inside. “Aw, come on, man!” Nova protests jokingly, “after two years I get Lindsay Troy alone in a hotel room and your worthless balding ass has to cock-block me!” Troy punches Nova softly in the ribs. “In your dreams, asshole. God, you do a guy a favor…” “Oh, you can do much more than that,” Nova continues, grinning widely now. “Geez, delirium is really boosting your confidence, isn’t it?” Troy says, cocking an eyebrow at him. “Inside, you two,” Ray motions with his hand, and they oblige. Gently, Nova lowers himself onto one of the double beds. “Owww, shit…” “I figured you, being the nosocomephobic that you are…,” Troy begins. “That’s ‘fear of hospitals,’” Ray offers helpfully. “…thank you, Ray,” Troy replies, giving him the ‘do NOT interrupt again’ look, “so I got in touch with Raymond and decided we’d just play doctor here in the hotel.” Nova looks up at both of them, his eyes wide. “I’m sorry, I may need someone to slap me or something. First, I get to come to Lindz’s hotel room, now she’s telling me she’s gonna play nurse all night? Is there an outfit for you to wear? I’m sorry, it’s just that I will GLADLY take the same beating I took tonight every single week if this is the pay-off.” Lindsay steps forward seductively, shaking her hips and running her hands down her sides, before leaning over the Risen Star, pouting her lips fully. “Mmmm…Ray’s crashing here too.” She snaps back up, probably to avoid Nova’s projectile jaw reaction, and gives the former champion a wink. “Still want that slap, hot-shot?” Somewhere closeby, outside... A man is taking his dog for a late-night stroll, when suddenly they both whip their heads around in the direction of the motel nearby, where a piercing cry has shattered the still of the night. “NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!”
