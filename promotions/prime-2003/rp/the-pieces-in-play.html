Date: ReVolution 164 ”Explain something to me…,” The man behind the voice is an oddity. As his rocky, gravelly voice rumbles inside of the room a single pair of eyes remains focused on the person and the words, intrigued by the veteran assassin only known by the name of Viktor. “What would that be, Viktor?” Malik rolls his tongue around inside of his mouth, slightly annoyed that his boss had sent him to this meeting. “Why is it that this man, this Craig fellow, is so important in the grand scheme of things? For years, that’s the name I’ve been hearing as the harbinger of death, but as I look at him, he’s just a mere human. Nothing more special than anyone you or I shall meet on the street. Yet, he’s the man that everyone wants. He’s the man that everyone fears. And, apparently, he’s the man that now everyone wants dead.” A moment passes before Malik subtlety coughs into the closed fist of his right hand. His eyes though never waver from Viktor’s eyes, wanting him to understand the weight of his words. “Have you ever met him, Viktor?” Viktor just simply shakes his head. “This is a man with a mind unlike any that you’ve ever seen. He was born with this purpose; it was engrained into him. What he knows, you can’t teach anyone, and that’s why he’s the best. Unfortunately, now, he’s broken. No use to anyone. His mind and soul are intact for the first time in a very, very long time. At one time, he was just a weapon. Now, he’s a weapon with a conscience, and a conscience in that kind of bomb is kind of like the atomic bomb knowing how to think and rationalize what it’s about to do. Worthless.” Viktor licks his lips as he looks at Malik. “How is such a thing possible? How is it that this one man is designed in this light?” His mind races through his thoughts for a moment before Malik just shakes his head. “The history of that man, Viktor, is far too rich. Far too complex. Let me just tell you this, he isn’t what you think he is, and he’s everything that you think he’s not. If I were to tell you his story, then your breath would stop before I finish my last word.” Viktor bites his bottom lip before he reaches into his shirt pocket and pulls out a cigarette. “So, what is it that you would like me to do? I mean, Malik, you’re a very accomplished man, and so is your boss. What can I do for you?” He then grabs a light out of his pocket and lights up his cigarette before taking a puff out of it. It’s almost like ecstasy for him. However, Viktor couldn’t disagree more as he looks irritated by the mere sight of the wrapped tobacco. “You’re our last ditch effort, our failsafe if I may. We have the necessary people in place, but with a man like this you never have enough plans to make sure all of your bases are covered.” Viktor takes another pull of his cigarette. “I’m retired, Malik, and you know that very well.” Malik simply nods his head. “I know this as well as I know that you still have contacts. I need an agent out there that can match step for step with Craig. I have a suspicious feeling that you have that connection, if I’m not mistaken.” A name instantly pops into his mind. At first, he’s reluctant to even mention it, but looking into the eyes of Malik tells Viktor that refusing to send him his best would mean certain death. “You know, I do have someone. Someone that might be able to pull off this job much more beautifully then you could even possibly imagine. I should warn you though, Malik--,” Before he can even finish his thought though, Malik stands up and walks over to Viktor. “I don’t want any warnings, Viktor. Just make sure whoever it is, is ready to do the job at the drop of a hat if need be. I need results, not warnings or excuses. Got it?” Malik then rips the disgusting cigarette from his lips and tosses it onto the ground before walking away from Viktor. He remains in the chair as he can hear the echoes of Malik’s shoes fade into the black. The annoyance is clearly written on Viktor’s face. “That might be the worst thing you could possibly do, Malik.” Yet, it’s not the cigarette that he’s talking about. ~~~ The sun beats down upon Craig’s head as he drives around in his brand new Audi convertible. A few days had passed since having a conversation with Katie and Nicole that could potentially change his life, and fortunately he’d been able to get away from Los Angeles because of work commitments. The days had started to feel longer to him as he spent more and more time in the airports, travelling from city to city and wrestling night after night. Frankly, getting away from Nicole and Katie had been good for him as it gave him to let everything just settle around him. He turns on his blinker and gets into another lane as a car goes flying past him. Today, the city seems to be more alive to him than ever before. Maybe it was because his day hadn’t gone sour yet or maybe his mind was processing things a little differently. He couldn’t quite put his finger on it as he turned the corner and pulled into the parking lot of his therapist. He quickly gets out of the car and runs inside of the building, glancing at a clock on his way in and curses underneath his breath. The flight from Chicago had gotten him back later then he’d expected and made him about 45 minutes late for his session. He sprints down another hallway and flies up a flight of stairs only to see Silvia locking up her office. “I’m here,” he yells down the hallway, but as she turns and looks at him he knows that she’s not going to really care. Craig begins walking down the hallway, sticking his hands inside the pockets of his Banana Republic jeans. She begins to saunter towards him before they meet in the middle of the hallway and she just flashes him this stare. “Sorry, but you’re late, and I’m going home for the evening. You can reschedule your appointment another day,” she tells him and then begins to walk past him, reshuffling the papers resting in the crook of her arm. Not really in the mood to be blown off, not at this time in his life, he grabs her elbow and spins her back around. “I know I’m late, but that’s my life. I go from town to town and sometimes, things happen to cause me to be late. I’m sorry, but I need to talk to you,” Craig responds to her, desperate in his tone, but angry in his eyes. She swallows for a minute and looks over him before shaking her head. “Sorry.” She then turns on her heels and looks back at him before shooting him another look that tells him if he grabs her arm again like that he’ll be spending the night in county jail. He stands there for a brief moment before jogging up towards and walking with her. “Alright, I missed the appointment. That doesn’t mean you can’t talk to me for the five minutes it takes for us to get to your car, right?” Silvia glances at him briefly before sighing. “Fine, it’s not like I have a life anyways. Talk. You have five minutes. What’s going on in your life?” Craig smiles at her as he taps the down button on the elevator and waits patiently for the door to open. His left hand reaches up and wipes the sweat off of his brow before he digs down deep and opens up to her. “I think we’ve already talked about the fact that the doctor told me that I have a year left to live. You’ll have to excuse me as I just rush through everything seeing as how I only have five minutes and would like to get your feedback on things. Nicole and Katie finally found out about the M.S., and basically told me that I either need to quit wrestling or stay at home to take care of my soon-to-come child. I can see their point to be honest, but if I give that up, what am I? What’s left of me?” The words fly by in a hurry as the door to the elevator opens and the two step inside. Silvia hits the button for the ground level before looking back at Craig. “You knew it was only a matter of time before they would find out. I have to imagine the constant headaches, the increasing medication, and some natural suspicion probably led Katie to do some investigating. That’s just based off what you’ve told me about her. When we first started our sessions, I told you to keep competing as it was something that would help you cope with the feeling of the M.S. Not the actual symptoms, but the reality of it. However, the scenario has changed, and now you’re felt with new aspects of your life. The fact that you have a child on its way and ultimatums coming your way. You need to show Nicole that you can be involved with your child’s life and still compete. You also need to keep examining ways to battle the M.S. and possibly get a second opinion.” As she finishes her sentence the door flies open and they walk out of the elevator and towards the exit of the building. “Do you think I need to quit? Do you think I can fix this problem before it gets worse?” Silvia thinks for a moment and rolls her tongue around in her mouth. “I’m going to assume you mean your situation with Katie and Nicole, and not the M.S. You have to try. You have to show them that you need them there by your side and not against you, and that they need to accept what you do in your life. At the same time, you need to be understanding of the fact that they’re going to lose someone they really care. In Katie’s case, it’s her dearest friend while in Nicole’s case it’s the man that she loves and the father of her child. You can’t ignore those feelings.” A few seconds later, they reach her car. Craig looks around at the fading sunlight and nods his head. “Thanks for the quick chat.” “No problem. How’re you handling it all?” His eyes shift to the ground before looking back at her. “Sometimes, I feel like I’m trapped inside of a box, and there’s no way out no matter how hard I try.” “That’s natural. Have you had any success finding your family? Learning more about where you come from? It’ll help you come to terms with what’s going on.” Silvia then grabs the handle to her car and opens it before throwing some of her stuff in. “Not yet. I guess that’s something that I need to do soon.” She nods her head. “Yeah, you do. You’ll thank me for it.” Silvia then gets into her car as Craig grabs the edge of the door and smiles at her. “I’ll take your advice.” “That’s what I’m here for, Craig. Night.” “Night.” Craig then closes the door to her car for her before walking off. Silvia sits there for a brief moment before reaching for her bag and pulling out her cell phone. As she sits back up, she sees Craig disappearing around the side of the building, and quickly flips open her phone before dialing a number on it. A moment passes before she puts it up against her ear. It rings twice and then a voice on the other end answers. “Craig came and saw me. Pushed him back in the right direction. He should go looking for his father very soon, I imagine, Malik.” Another second passes before she hangs up her phone, the person on the other line never responding. She sits back in her seat and just shakes her head before smiling to her, pleased with the job she’d accomplished. To. Be. Continued.
