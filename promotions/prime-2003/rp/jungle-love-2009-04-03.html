Jungle Love

3 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2009 Another drop of sweat formed just beneath Tyler Rayne’s matted hairline. The bead rolled down the fissures of his forehead until collecting in the bristly barricade of his left eyebrow. There it sat. Wet. Heavy. Really fucking annoying. Tyler’s chin hovered just centimeters from the damp underbrush. His hand cautiously pushed a large frond from his line of vision. Careful not to make too many sudden movements. No unnecessary noises. Nothing to give his position away. The jungle was dense with ferns and foliage. A thin mist clouded the undergrowth in some areas. Between the sweat and the heat and the thickness of the rainforest… it was damn near impossible to see much of anything. He waited for a full minute. Motionless beyond the slightest rise and fall of breath. Eyes glaring across the jungle floor. Through the treetops. Watching for the slightest hint of movement. An unexpected shadow or a trembling tree limb. Anything. Nothing. She was good. Better than he had anticipated. Tyler’s hand slowly descended from the frond. Vision once again obscured by green, he ducked his head (the tug of gravity thankfully pulling that sweat bead from his eyebrow) and trudged forward. Elbows and forearms pressed tight to the jungle floor. Dragged his shirtless torso across the mud and broken twigs. Something large crashed in the jungle to his left. The plant life cracked and crunched beneath the weight of… whatever was out there. Forty yards or so to the left. He just barely caught a glimpse of the shadow rushing from the corner of his right eye. Tyler slowly rolled to his back, hand crawling down to the quietly unbutton the holster at his hip. The revolver felt surprisingly heavy as he drew it from the leather and up to his chest. The usually cool metal was strangely temperate against his skin. Tyler cranked his neck just an inch or two from the ground. Head still hidden beneath the underbrush, he glared out in the direction of the sound. Waited and watched until… there she was. Her dark silhouette passed between the trunks of two far trees. He noted the outline of a very similar revolver in her hand. Tyler took a deep breath and prepared to make his move. He could still crawl on for another few yards before she might hear or see him. The advantage was his. All he had to do was- “It’s rainin’ men! Hallelujah it’s rainin’ men!” The miniaturized voices of The Weather Girls called out from Tyler’s ear with an unexpected buzz. His head vibrated under the intrusion. It would have to be ignored. Eyes darted upward. Gaze falling across those distant trees. The shadow racing out from behind them and across the jungle. “Fuck.” He bolted to his feet and gave chase. Military issues pounded across the jungle floor. Leaves and plant-like debris collapsed and crumpled beneath his mass. The jungle was suddenly alive with the sound of pursuit. The Weather Girls were still ringing in his ear. Tyler darted between a pair of thin trees. Hopped an overturned and rotting log. His hand reached up to the device in his ear and silenced The Weather Girls with a simple press of his thumb. “Really not the best time, Tink.” He jumped to the left to avoid a protruding stump and took a sharp branch across the forehead. Tyler winced but landed with a modicum of grace and continued the chase. “Please. You know better than that. I rarely look at the card when I’m actually in the arena. You think I’m going to do it on my days off?” The shadow dropped behind a large collection of ferns and disappeared. Tyler sprinted faster. “No shit? So what two douche nozzles they put us up against?” He caught a glimpse of her rounding around a tree twenty feet below. It wasn’t just his prey that dropped off behind that collection of ferns. It was the ground itself. Without thinking twice, Tyler threw himself into the decline and surfed his way down into the ravine. “Natas came back? When the shit did that happen? I really need to start paying attention to what’s going on. Alright. Who’s the other chump?” He almost lost his footing trying to round that tree. His boots lost all traction and slipped a half dozen inches across the jungle floor. Fortunately Tyler Rayne has impeccable balance. “Well me fuck me runnin’. Ol’ Country, eh? Uni’s gonna love that.” He stopped. “Fuck.” Lost her. “Not you. No, just… fuck it. Forget it. You called me about the match, right?” Shit. Where the hell did she go? Couldn’t have gone far. Tyler took a real random guess and cautiously marched to the south. “Nah, don’t worry about her. Nothing she can do about it. Sanctioned match… and I didn’t even ask for the damn thing. ‘Sides, I don’t think her and Wade have been gettin’ on so well as of late. Been lookin’ for an excuse to stomp Country’s dick for awhile, anyway. Not gonna let a girl get in the way of that.” Tyler lifted the revolver a little higher. Closer to his core. Ready, just in case something crazy happened. Where the hell did she get off to? “Yeah. I realize she did in the past. Which is why I’m telling you she won’t now. Just… take my fuckin’ word for it, will ya? Promise. Things’ll be just shiny.” The slightest hint of movement off to the left. He turns in the direction and stalks forward. “Now that’s a different matter entirely. Fuck all if I know what that kid’s gonna do after last week. Have my way, though, he won’t make it far enough through the night to interrupt our little main event extravaganza. Fuckin’ bitch thinks he can threaten to end my career and I’ll just-“ Leaves showered down around him. Tyler made the classically amateur mistake of looking up to see what caused the disturbance. Which meant that the cause of the disturbance was already descending upon him. Flaming red taunted him through the green. He turned and found the barrel of a revolver hanging six inches from his chest. “Bang.” Her voice came out a barely composed combination of triumph and glee. Lithe fingers quivered as they pulled back on the trigger. Smoke exploded from the end of the barrel, championing the black plastic rod that followed through the opening. White cloth unfurled from the rod and big red letters screamed a faux death at him. Bang! “You’re dead.” Tyler sighed. “Great, Tink. Thanks a lot. B just fuckin’ killed me.” Both revolvers fell to the jungle floor. Angelica Brooks flipped from the branch she’d been hanging from and landed on both knees in the foliage. She rose with a huge smile that would rival the likes of even Tony Gamble. “Nice move, kid. Look, Tink, I’ll have to call you back. Um… we’ll get together on Monday. Come up with a plan or something. Actually, let’s just have your interns come up with a plan while I drink and watch some strippers dance. That’s what you pay ‘em for, right?” Tyler tried to contain his amusement at the victory dance Angelica was stomping out in front of him. It was unfortunate such a natural beauty had such unnaturally atrocious dance moves. “Shiny. Later.” It took all of a second before she burst. “I SO got you! I can’t believe it! I got you! YOU! Big bad Tyler Mother Fuckin’ Rayne. Killed by little ol’ Angelica Brooks. You can eat it! Fuckin’ eat it!” “You done yet?” “No! I’m going to gloat about this forever. When you’re eighty years old and wheeling around in some retirement home, I’ll come visit you just to remind you of how I whooped your ass tonight.” “I can never invite you over again, can I?” “Nope.” The rumble of an overstressed engine interrupts his undoubtedly witty response. Both Angelica and Tyler turned toward the direction of the noise. A black ATV rolls up over the ridge. Tyler’s butler Alfred carefully navigates the rough terrain. Sitting on the back of the quad is none other than The Queen herself, Lindsay Troy. Alfred pulled the transport to a stop just a few feet from the duo and (seemingly relieved) removes himself from the controls. “Ms. Troy to see you, sir.” “No shit.” The Queen slid herself from the back of the quad and pressed, with a small bit of trepidation, to the jungle floor. She looks at the muddied form of Tyler Rayne. Eyes drift across to the scantily clad, sweat soaked figure of Angelica Brooks. "I seem horridly overdressed for the occasion." “Indeed. Perhaps you should shed a few layers and get comfortable, eh?” The question ended on that infamously charming smile. The Queen remained resolutely non-impressed. "Perhaps we should commence with the exercise, Rambo, and we'll see if I undress for you later." Alfred handed her a duffel bag that was strapped down to the back of the ATV. She turned to take it from him, giving him a smile, before casting her gaze back to the dirtball duo. She gave Brooks a nod--short, and without the usual nasty remark to accompany it--as she walked toward Rayne. “Such a tease. Alfred, don’t suppose you’d mind taking B back up to the Manor for a shower?” “Of course not, sir. Anything else?” “Just make sure she doesn’t get distracted by something else big and expensive I own. Wouldn’t want her missing her big photo shoot.” “It’s not that big. Besides, you worry too much. I’ll be fine.” “Probably. Still, though. Call me when you get to New York.” “OK, dad.” “Very funny.” “You are old enough to be her dad.” “Thanks, Uni. If we’re done with the jokes then perhaps everyone can-“ “Yeah, yeah.” “Seriously. Call me.” “I will.” Angelica Brooks leaned forward and wrapped one arm around Rayne’s back. She didn’t pull him into the hug so much as she clumsily fell into it. She gave a half wave to Troy without ever actually meeting her gaze, hopped on the back of the ATV and roared away. "The whole 'being nice to her' thing is still very foreign to me." Troy absently shuffled her feet as she watched Alfred steer the vehicle out of sight. "Even though I feel like I should be." “She’s good people. Never understood why you didn’t get along to begin with.” "I don't get along with other women in this profession, Tyler. It took me a long time to become what one would consider personable with anyone whose name wasn't Ivy McGinnis." She looked at him. "I'm trying with Brooks partly because I still feel bad about what happened, but mostly because of you." “I know. She’d never tell you… but you scare the shit out of the poor girl. She really loves PRIME. Got a lot of respect for what you’ve done there. Again, not that she’d ever tell you. Anyway, I believe you were here for a reason that wasn’t talking about B.” "You said on the phone you were doing some training. I figured I could help." She shrugged. "I can always give myself a self-guided tour of the Manor instead, since you never had the foresight to invite me here before." “I had other places before. Now… I just have this. We’ll train. Maybe you give me a workout, and I’ll give you the tour. I hear the master bedroom is a must see.” "Is that where you're keeping the Luke and Leia statue now?" She was teasing again, and she punctuated the sentence with a wry grin. Troy raised her arms over her head, stretching them out along with her back. "You still have my place too, you know.” “How could I forget?” ********** Angelica Brooks never called when she got to New York.
