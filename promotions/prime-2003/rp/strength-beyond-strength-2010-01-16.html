Strength Beyond Strength

16 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 16-01-10 There is one constant in this business that dictates more than any board of directors could. It straightens the curves, determining the future by shaping the past and creating something new and fresh that whets the palate to a craze. Lisa Tyler. Devin Shakur. Prometheus. None of them hold the power to accomplish this over another contingent of the industry. The fans. The millions-strong allegiance to a world of warriors and heroes are the key ingredient, always have been. There can be no show without them, and it is they who decide what sells. The richest men and women in the world follow the whims of the faceless masses, whether they can barely afford the cable subscription or have a ticket to the front row of every show in the yearly schedule. The powers that be bend to the beck and call of the average joe, because it's their business. The board knows it, the immediate charge of the roster knows it... ...But more than most, the wrestlers themselves know it. ---- Sitting in front of a stack of glossy prints and neatly folded t-shirts, underneath a large lit billboard advertising his special appearance, those very thoughts sludged through Von Kelsig's tired mind. Tired from the transcontinental flight, tired of the culture shock, but most of all dog-tired of his recent run of shit luck. The Tobu Dobutsu Kouen amusement park was bustling with as much traffic as Disneyland on a good day. A sea of perky, oriental patrons chattered over the roar of the rides and the laughter of countless children enjoying a day out at the park, with the added bonus of a special appearance by PRIME's own Hessian, with the added bonus of his Elite Title being on show with the upcoming Championship match against Brandon Youngblood on the card. Wistfully he ran his fingers through his wiry beard, subconsciously grooming his battered ego as he signed autographs and posed for pictures. As exotic as his surroundings were he struggled to tear his thoughts away from the events of the last year. He had started with such a bang, capturing titles and notoriety like it was shooting fish in a barrel. Then without warning his focus and determination curled up and died, much like his partner Gloria. Tracking the losses, everything seemed to stem from her infidelity. When the Elite title was firmly around his waist and the world remembered who the hardcore giant was, his luck seemed to turn sour. Gloria betrayed him and paid the ultimate price, however not at his hand. With her death fresh on his mind he was free to be beaten and embarrassed in his first loss in months by Tony Gamble. Of all the humilities. With that loss, the fire in his belly was duly extinguished. Nothing could spark another inferno in the giant's gut, not even the prospect of being crowned Jewel in the Crown 2009. The annihilation of Devin Shakur at the Great American Nightmare ran deeper than the events of the summer, his only win in the last quarter. Then before Michael Sloan had shown his true colours, Von Kelsig didn't mind too much being beaten past the usual course of contempt shown for a loss. At King of Kings he faced his most lucrative fight since Wyatt Connors and Desade for the Elite title: winning the Golden Ticket, and from Devin Shakur to boot. Already having a W under his belt the previous arc it was a sure-fire win for the giant...until Chainz interfered. Scratching his fingers down his cheek Von Kelsig sighed through another signing as thoughts of Sloan's agenda made him curdle inside. Supposedly a comrade and ally, the Monster from Hell's Kitchen had been playing him along throughout their entire working relationship, enduring a faux friendship with the giant simply to learn his weaknesses and prepare for the first strike at the King of Kings pay-per-view. That hurt the most. Gloria's unfaithfulness stopped her staying in his thoughts, and losses were par for the course in a career such as his...but to be stabbed in the back by a friend broke his heart. A wrestler for nigh on ten years, Von Kelsig knew life on the road would be lonely, and so to have companionship in the form of a fellow monster such as Sloan lifted the troublesome weight of isolation from his shoulders. With the ability to use hardcore tactics on the line against Shakur's Golden Ticket, it had to be a win for Von Kelsig. No ifs, ands or buts. Hardcore wrestling was his forte, and the reason why hundreds of fans had been queuing up since dawn to meet him. Without it he was as effective as two of Sin City's best against one PRIME giant. Just another face in the crowd, albeit towering over it, but still in the crowd. Then along came Chainz. Cost him the match, cost him his hardcore edge, cost him another match. Left him ripe for the picking. Equipped with a villainous mind, Sloan proved a sly foe. All the muscle and power in the world could never have changed the outcome at King of Kings, and it left Von Kelsig feeling lower than he had ever felt before. The entire King of Kings arc left him with the understanding that he wasn't the smartest man in the world, and everyone was beginning to realize it. Autographing another 4x6 print, he handed it up to a lanky teenage fan in a PRIME t-shirt, also freshly tagged with Hessian's signature. “I hope your journey was pleasant?” he asked the giant, his r's tripping over his l's in typical Japanese dialectal fashion, and tying it off with a respective bow. “Yes, thank you.” Von Kelsig replied, applying a false smile to his face which over the years he had perfected to mask his discontent at any situation that didn't involve disappointing the fans so important to him. “I am looking forward to your match with Brandon Youngblood,” he added, and Von Kelsig's face softened into a genuine grin at the sound of Blandun Yungbrud. “Yeah it's gonna be hellacious.” Von Kelsig said, patting the Elite Title sat folded in front of him between the prints and shirts. Too starstruck to ask any more, the lanky youth took his prized signatures and wandered off into the crowd as a father wearing a backpack brought his two sons forward, one wearing a wiry Hessian beard from PRIMEShop.jp and the other, slightly chubbier and wearing glasses, wearing a tight Hessian t-shirt with a print of his roster pose on the front. The sight of the boy in the beard made Von Kelsig chuckle, and gradually he began to feel his woes dissipate. Even with the foreign spectre hanging over him in the thousands of intruding neon lights, the cloying smells and the dense evening atmosphere of the busy park, the sight of these fans half a world away sporting his merchandise and showing that unique oriental respect helped ease him into the international flow. “Greetings sir. My name is Riku, my boys Jun and Daisuke are great fans of yours, sir,” began the father, pushing his children forward to the edge of the table while they stared up at their hero. “You are their favourite wrestler. The biggest and the strongest and the best.” He gestured to the Elite Title and bowed before saying something in Japanese to his boys as they bit their lips and fidgeted with their shirts. His first instinct was to just pick up his pen and sign a couple of pictures, but as the boys shuffled closer he reached out his massive paw and held it open before them. Again Riku nudged them and one by one they placed their tiny hands in his, Von Kelsig closing his thumb and forefinger around their wrists and shaking gently. “Fine lads you got here.” Von Kelsig smiled, signing a couple of pictures and picking out a couple of small-size shirts for them. “May we have a picture of my boys sitting on the shoulders of a giant?” Riku asked, producing a camera from his pack even though an assistant with a polaroid stood behind the Elite Champion. Nodding, Von Kelsig motioned for them to move around to his side of the table, where their father picked them up and placed them on a shoulder each. Stepping back around to the other side of the table he watched as Von Kelsig held onto Jun and Daisuke and rose up out of his chair. A round of applause and a chorus of cheers rose up from the queue behind Riku as the giant towered high above them with the two boys grinning like Cheshire cats on his shoulders. As Riku took his picture a barrage of flashbulbs popped behind him as the crowd joined in, taking pictures of the giant Hessian. Lowering the boys back to the ground he gave them a wink as they stared up at their idol with great smiles upon their faces. Called away by their father he uttered many thanks and ushered his children away into the crowd. Taking his seat again he felt a sense of pride at the status he had achieved in the business. Of course fans on this side of the world were much more appreciative of the sport and his talents as a wrestler, and away from the mundane chants and the same old cheers common in American arenas over here he could expect awe-struck clapping and a silence of concentration that let him know they were absorbing every moment in the ring. Of the hundreds of fans that graced his presence that day, very few showed any less than the kind of respect reserved for a God King. Their enthusiasm and their many questions took his mind far from the last couple of months. Even when the subject of King of Kings was brought up the interest was more in the psychology of Sloan or what the fallout of his actions would be. Not one soul even came close to bringing the tone down with mentions of his defeats. It was at times like this he realized just how important these simple people were, to him and all the industry. The sold-out arenas were often taken for granted, it was this show of face at a much more intimate level that renewed that sense of gratitude. Von Kelsig had his proof that no matter how many matches or titles he lost these people would be there for him. As night fell upon the amusement parks the queue eventually seemed to shrink. As if the sight of the unique lit up oriental architecture wasn't enough the sky was flooded with fireworks from somewhere in the park. Clouds of dazzling blue and warm reds and oranges flashed amid whooshing rockets and snapping bombers. Mesmerized by the Saitama night sky, Von Kelsig had to apologize when he heard his name being called somewhere in the distance, quickly tuning to a close distance as he focused back on his duty and turned back to the queue. A smartly suited woman with half moon glasses and her hair tied up in a bun with red sticks smiled politely at him, offering a curt nod and flashing her pearly whites. “Sorry,” he snickered. “You don't get shows like that much back home.” “Not a problem,” she answered, stepping forward and offering a hand to shake while moving a briefcase to her side. “My name is Natsumo Okabayashi, I represent the Tokyo office of Make-A-Wish of Japan.” “Oh, alright. Nice to meet you.” Von Kelsig said as he shook her hand. “I was made aware some time ago you would be visiting our shores and I had to take this opportunity to meet you.” “Oh that's-...” “Yes, normally this meeting would occur through different channels but as you were so close to the office I thought it apt to see you in person.” Natsumo continued, “I am here on behalf of an Ashley Lawrence. His parents are Mister Gregory and Missus Miho Lawrence. Young Ashley is eight years old and suffers from acute lymphoblastic leukemia. Unfortunately he is struggling with the treatment and our foundation was contacted about granting a wish for him.” “Damn...” Von Kelsig mustered. “After being contacted, it was simply a case of visiting Ashley and finding out what he wanted...” “And what does he want?” “Mister Von Kelsig, Ashley wishes to meet the great Hessian of PRIME. He was most enthusiastic about it. I understand you have a busy schedule but if we could arrange an appointment for the future to further discuss this then-...” “Hell, I'm free tomorrow.” “For a meeting?” Natsumo was taken aback. “No...to meet the little guy?” He replied quizzically, to which Natsumo's face filled with surprise. “Oh, Mr. Von Kelsig. That would be wonderful. Truly wonderful! Normally we have to wait a number of weeks for a personal appearance to be confirmed.” “Well tomorrow is a free day for me. Normally I'd be putting in some time at the gym but that's easily miss-able for something like this.” “You mean you can fulfil this wish tomorrow??” Natsumo asked, still in disbelief that a celebrity was actually available to grant a wish so soon. “Sure, just gimme the details and we can go see Ashley tomorrow.” He shot Natsumo a smile and in the gentle creases and twinkle of his eyes she knew he was sincere to his word. While the people behind her waited on patiently, she traded information with Von Kelsig and went on her way, leaving the giant to see out the evening entertaining the last of his fans. ---- Staring down at the complicated console, Von Kelsig blew air out his mouth and furrowed his brow, determined to figure out this complex piece of machinery with his inferior Western mind. Casting yet another gaze over his room he couldn't find a manual anywhere, and with such precise icons over each button he feared pushing any without understanding the consequences. “All I wanna do is take a fuckin' piss...” he muttered under his breath. Sighing, he lifted the lid and unzipped, making swirling motions to avoid splash back. Once relieved, he scanned the console beside the toilet bowl and groaned, before dropping the lid and bracing himself for the worst as he pressed all the buttons at once. The toilet seemed to come to life with this frantic use of its facilities. Surprised and a little scared, Von Kelsig stepped back as the seat began to vibrate, while a gushing sound mixed in with the unmistakable gurgling of a flush. Suddenly water began leaking out from the bowl, and darting forward the giant threw up the lid only to get a face full of the stuff. “Son of a-..!” he cried, slamming the lid back down and with his mouth and eyes shut tight felt his way across to the sink with only his memory to guide him. Stumbling upon it he twisted both taps and let the water fill his cupped hands before scrubbing at his face. After soaping thrice and soaking his beard several times after that, Von Kelsig stared into the mirror and caught his reflection looking back in disbelief. “Where are the three seashells when you need them?” he asked himself, before drying off and getting ready for the day ahead. In ten minutes the giant was cruising down a glass elevator past hundreds of patrons on his way to the lobby. Clad in a black leather jacket and jeans with a sleeveless denim coat over his jacket, he looked ever part the outlaw, save for the kit bag in his hand within which lay a replica Elite Title he planned on giving to the ill boy. Flitting a gaze over everyone around him he wasn't surprised to see the vast majority of them eye-balling him. The moment he touched down on Japanese soil there had been hundreds of eyes on him. Giants were a rare commodity here, unless they were a hundred foot tall, fire breathing dinosaur. He could pick out the wrestling fans among the crowds quite easily, for they always regarded him with a certain analytical squint to their stare. “How are you enjoying your stay sir?” asked a bellhop, bowing as Von Kelsig passed him by. “Love it so far. Just wish I knew how to work the john.” He chuckled quietly as the bellhop cocked his head, trying to translate the statement into a more palatable form. Who is John and why is this giant trying to work him? As he fished his cell phone out of his pocket and checked for new messages, someone barged past him almost knocking the phone out of his hand. “Hey watch your step as-..oh.” Halfway out the front door, a familiar face glanced back at him, a face that even with frustration etched on it had a warm, friendly undertone. “Oh, hey Matt,” Von Kelsig said to PRIME's senior mic jockey, “didn't know you were staying here?” “Makes sense Von Kelsig, it's the closest to the arena.” Mills replied, stepping back and allowing a small group to enter past him. “Where you headed in such a rush? You're lucky I ain't a Yokozuna or you might've gotten squashed.” “Yeah well...” “Hey I got an appointment to do some charity work but if you're free later fancy hitting up a karaoke bar or something?” Von Kelsig asked, taking the chance to cling on to the close comfort of a work colleague while the chance presented itself. “Uhh, sorry big guy I'm in meetings all day. Shakur's really taking this board of directors thing to heart.” Matt answered, half out the door again before stopping and turning back to Von Kelsig with a distant look in his eyes. “Look, don't take this personally but if I were you I would leave the charity work for now and focus on your match. Everyone's touting Youngblood to beat you...everyone. I don't know how closely you pay attention to the sheets but there's been a lot of talk about your attitude and your losses recently. Seriously man, you need to step it up a gear, unless you want to incur the wrath of your new boss.” And like that, he was gone. Von Kelsig felt practically violated as he stood in the middle of the lobby amongst the bustling patrons. Whether it was because a senior staff member such as Mills had just laid it out for him so bluntly or because the reality of it was hitting home, Von Kelsig lowered his head and left the hotel with a new found sense of self loathing. Of the entire journey from the hotel in Saitama to the Tokyo Metropolitan Kiyose Children's Hospital, Von Kelsig recalled only calling a cab and paying far more than he imagined for the lift to the hospital. The rest of the time was filled with dread and sickening butterflies, a dry throat and sweaty palms at the thought of what ReVolution 216 would mean for him. Brandon Youngblood. The Pariah had been on a tear of his own lately, a run not seen since his own this time last year. Was this the order of things to come? Von Kelsig had noticed a pattern develop as the year progressed. With the initial return to PRIME he had overwhelmed all but the best opponents with his smashmouth style and dominant power in the ring. Then the further into 2009 he got, the quicker his opponents were, the harder to counter. Then before he knew it losses were mounting and his reputation was sliding. Could the same happen to Brandon Youngblood? No, not this soon. Troy Douglas was once a force to be reckoned with, and now struggled to jerk the curtain never mind pull out a win. Was this the destiny waiting for him? It was never like this before. Not in PCW or OSW. His career had its fair share of wins and losses but never anything like this. This was different. Had the pool of talent evolved to a point where they could learn every move and mannerism in his being? Three years he had been away from the game. Three long years, and in that time wrestlers he once worked alongside had either disappeared or become legends. They had adapted with the new breed and kept up with the times to ensure their survival in this cutthroat world. Had he missed out? It seemed to be the case on the roster that adaptation preceded success. He shook his head as he found himself walking along a gleaming white corridor of the children's hospital, stopping only to glance back at the realization he had made the trip from the hotel without remembering it. Shrugging it off he walked on, contemplating his first title defence. Brandon Youngblood had been a great talent before. Now with his return he seemed to be even better. Had he spent his time away from the ring studying and adapting from the sidelines? Much to his chagrin, Von Kelsig imagined his own ignorance might be his downfall. Sure he scouted opponents and knew most of their tendencies, but it felt like something bigger had happened in his time away that he had missed. He did notice throughout 2009 that even if he wrestled the same opponent multiple times their strategy changed each time. It didn't help that Michael Sloan had helped strip him of his only true advantage. He was a fine wrestler, but with a weapon in hand he could face down anybody and come out on top. Anybody. He realized at that moment the problem. He had been caged and his wings clipped. His strategy had limitations now, so that even if he wished to adapt he couldn't truly do so without re-inventing his entire style. He was no technician, sure back in the days of OSW he could chain wrestle with the best of them but years had passed and now he relied on a heavy hand and a big stick to help get the job done. The spectre of death loomed over him the deeper he tread through the hospital. With it glancing over his shoulder he felt its cold, skeletal grasp at his throat. Once a proud, world class wrestler, he found with each passing day that a little of him was slipping away and could never be retrieved. It happened to Ivan Stanislav, to Xavier Kannon. Was he following them down the same steep road? This Elite Title match would decide his fate. If he could go toe to toe with Brandon Youngblood and just retain the strap, he knew he could thwart Sloan and rise once again to the top of the food chain. Should he be defeated? Then the roar of the monster that had shook the world for nearly ten years would quiver and choke, then die. “Ah, you're here! So glad you could make it!” a female voice called out from ahead. Drawn from his thoughts, Von Kelsig looked past a slew of medical staff at the prim and proper lady standing to the side, wearing a similar suit to the previous day and with those same red sticks holding her silky jet black hair in place. “Natsumo.” he waved over to her, and reciprocated her bow with one of his own. “Learning the custom are we?” Natsumo asked as Von Kelsig let the weariness drain away from his mind in preparation for the job at hand. Von Kelsig shrugged and smiled. “Ashley and his parents are just down the hall. If you would like to follow me please.” “Is there anything I need to know before we go in?” he asked. “No, just be yourself.” Natsumo smiled back, “Just...be Hessian. Is that your belt?” she motioned to the kit bag. “Not mine,” Von Kelsig replied, holding the bag up and patting it. “Ashley's.” “Oh, he will be ecstatic. You know if it wasn't for his terrible affliction I am sure he would force his parents back to America to watch you wrestle.” “Can he travel at all?” “He is very weak from his treatment, and so mainly bed-bound.” “What if I could arrange for him to see the next PRIME show in person? Y'know, get him tickets and stuff. Meet some of the wrestlers. Nothing invigorates the soul quite like the electricity of a sell-out crowd.” “I would have to discuss this matter with his parents, but if that is possible I'm sure it would make his dreams come true.” Von Kelsig pursed his lips together and nodded to himself as Natsumo rounded a corner and stopped outside a brown door with a single window high up in the middle. A small blind prevented him seeing inside. “Wouldn't want to ruin the surprise.” Natsumo smiled at him. “I thought he knew I was coming?” Von Kelsig whispered back. “Oh he knows, he just doesn't know when.” Doesn't leave much of a window for surprise considering he probably knows PRIME is on an international tour, he thought to himself as Natsumo placed a hand on the doorknob and cracked it open wide enough to firstly peer in, before she stuck her head through the gap. Whispering something in Japanese, she nodded and pulled her head back, turning to Von Kelsig and raising a hand. “OK, let's go in. He's been sleeping for some time. He will be so surprised.” Ducking under the doorway, Von Kelsig rose up again as Natsumo led him into the room. Closing the door quietly behind him he took in his surroundings. The room was fairly small, maybe fifteen feet by fifteen feet at best. The walls were hand painted with cherry blossoms and swirling colours depicting a sunny day on a meadow. Box kites filled most of the upper space on the walls, while children painted along the rolling hillside laughed and frolicked alongside one another. A television sat in one corner next to the only unpainted wall covered by three large windows overlooking the Tokyo skyline. Various medical equipment lined the wall beneath the windows, none of which looked remotely familiar to Von Kelsig, and he had seen his fair share of hospitals. Various monitors and apparatus lay by a bed positioned in the middle of the room, with a plastic tray hooked on to a railing running down one side of the bed, on top of which lay a plethora of different tubs and jars filled with drugs and creams. Only his parents and their doctor occupied the room. His parents Greg and Miho Lawrence waved excitedly to him and walked around the bed to shake his hand, while the doctor checked over the equipment by the bed. Greg had a strong, very much like Clark Gable without the moustache, and a build that suggested he enjoyed his cardio workouts. Miho's face was slender and had a glow about it that reminded Von Kelsig of porcelain. A set of piercing green eyes stared out at him through wisps of her fringe, her cheek dimpling every time she smiled. “Wow, I can't believe you're actually here.” Mr. Lawrence said quietly, shaking his hand fervently. “Our son is a great fan of yours!” pipped Mrs. Lawrence. “We are so grateful you could meet him.” she added, emotion welling up in her eyes. Placing an arm around his wife's shoulder, Greg put a finger to his lips and nodded to the bed as Ashley began to stir. Stepping to the foot of the bed, they gestured for Von Kelsig to move closer. Among the mussed covers, the giant saw for the first time little Ashley as his eyelids flickered and he gazed up from his slumber at the man towering over him. He could tell the boy had inherited his father's western features, but due to hair loss from his treatment the giant couldn't know he once had a his mother's thick black hair. The moment their eyes met, Von Kelsig felt as delicate as the boy, who lay still all except for the small movements of his head as he silently glanced at his parents, standing by one another smiling back and nodding to him. Staring back up at Hessian, Ashley brought his arms up and rubbed his eyes blinking the sleep away and making sure this wasn't a dream. Standing over him, smiling down at him was Hessian! With his big blue eyes he examined the giant, and in the next moment his lips parted, forming a smile so sweet Von Kelsig felt his heart break and melt at the same time. Here lay a bald little child, holding on to life with the help of others where his body was failing, and yet he had the most beautiful smile Von Kelsig had ever seen. His milk teeth spaced individually in his gums by a hairline, his tongue flapping behind them as he swallowed in disbelief that Hessian was here before him. “Hey little guy.” Von Kelsig said softly, caressing Ashley's shoulder over his Power Rangers duvet cover. His only reply were those large eyes roaming his features and the little smile growing larger every second. He's tiny, he thought to himself. He's eight years old and he's so damn small... “I heard you weren't feeling too hot kiddo, thought I'd come down and cheer you up some .” he said, composing his thoughts before he let the agony of a dying child seep into his heart amid the other emotions swirling around inside him. “Say hello then Ashley. Hessian's come all the way here just to see you.” Greg said from the foot of the bed, clutching his wife close and treasuring every moment of joy his boy must be feeling. “Hi.” he squeaked softly, glancing up at his dad before looking back up at Hessian. Miho ran a thumb under her eyes to catch two large teardrops. “Look at him Greg, our son is so happy.” she whispered into her husband's ear. “Well it's a pleasure to meet you young Ashley Lawrence. Y'know I brought some presents for ya. Don't let the beard fool you though, you won't see me driving a sleigh and bumping down chimneys all night!” A noise escaped Ashley's throat as his nose wrinkled up and he shrugged his shoulders. A laugh that demanded a smile from everyone in the room, just because it was such a precious sound. At the mention of presents Natsumo caught her breath and approached Greg, whispering to him and escorting him from the room while Miho watched on, captivated by the glow in her son's eyes. Making some room on the tray, Von Kelsig placed his kit bag upon it and unzipped it. Ashley's nostrils flared as he breathed in the musty aroma of Hessian's bag, filled with a scent garnered from life on the road. From the bag he first produced a small t-shirt with a print on the front of his roaring face, composed of fragments as though the volume of his yell had caused him to explode. Turning it around he showed Ashley the back, which had “The Murder Show” written in a torn script. Placing it in Ashley's wee hand, Von Kelsig watched as his fingers gripped the garment and felt over the fabric. “Thank you.” he said, his weary voice finding a little more strength. “You are welcome kiddo. So how long you been watching wrestling for?” Still captivated by the sight of the giant, Ashley glanced over at his mum. “He's watched wrestling as long as I can remember, anyway.” Miho said, “We get J:Com on Demand so now that PRIME has changed networks Ashley can still see ReVolution, isn't that right son?” He nodded excitedly, while Von Kelsig raised an eyebrow, impressed that his parents took such an interest in the program. “Well that's good. I'd hate to have to Hellevate someone if you couldn't keep watching,” he winked at Ashley. “So what's your favourite wrestling match?” “I like the Halo, and the Jewel in the Crown.” he replied, pronouncing Halo as 'Hallo' and the JitC as the 'Duel in da Cwown'. “Good choices. I like 'em myself, but they sure are tough...say, you looking forward to Two-Sixteen?” “Yeah.” Ashley replied, clamping his teeth over his bottom lip and nodding excitedly. “'Cos you know I got a big match...” With that he delved back into the bag and produced the gold. As if they couldn't get much bigger, Ashley's eyes widened even more at the sight of the Elite title belt as Von Kelsig showed it off. “...And I'm putting this on the line.” He added, lowering the title into the bed and smiling as Ashley's hands clutched the fresh leather strap, his fingers gliding over the shiny metal. At that moment Greg and Natsumo walked back into the room, Greg embracing his wife and whispering something into her ear, looking eagerly for a reply while Natsumo waved at Ashley and smiled at Von Kelsig. “Now I got a secret for you Ashley,” Von Kelsig began in a more serious tone, “We both know Brandon Youngblood is good, better than Wyatt Connors and Desade. There's a good chance he might win this belt from me...” Ashley's face began to frown, his eyes squinting at the mention of Hessian's opponent. “So here's what I'm going to do. This belt is too good to go around his waist, so I want you to keep it here. This is a special belt, reserved for a warrior, for a fighter. I could tell as soon as I came through this door you're a fighter Ashley, so who better to protect the belt?” Miho gave Greg a nod of confirmation, which he passed on to Natsumo before they both heard Von Kelsig's request and felt their hearts leap as Ashley's face lit up. “I can have it?” he asked, his eyes darting between Hessian and his parents while Von Kelsig chuckled and nodded in retort. “Yep. You keep this belt here and you can be Elite Champion too. I'll get the bosses to make me up a new one and you'll have this one. Deal?” Ashley nodded again, his breath hitching with the excited beat of his heart. Hearing it, his doctor finally pulled himself away from the equipment and produced a stethoscope, huffing warm air onto the cold metal before placing it over Ashley's heart. He had cropped black hair and eyes like an eagle. “You feeling alright, Ashley?” he asked, regarding his breathing. Ashley nodded and took a deep breath, leaning his head back to let more air in as his excited gaze became agitated and his breaths became shorter with a rasp. “It's OK son, just calm down.” Greg said, moving over to Ashley's side and stroking his head. “Calm down.” he said again as the doctor reached over to the tray and took a brown-tinted transparent tub of pills. Popping one onto his palm he retrieved a cup of water from a nearby cooler and offered it to Ashley while his dad began rubbing his chest and making soothing shushing noises. Von Kelsig waited patiently, winking at Ashley as the boy took time to let his excitement pass and whatever pill he had taken have its effect. While it happened, Natsumo leaned over and whispered into Von Kelsig's ear. “His parents have agreed to let him go to the show.” she said, pushing two thumbs into the air, her eyes softening with her smile. “Great. I'll make sure he's looked after.” he replied, watching as Ashley's chest began to rise and fall more slowly now while he kept his t-shirt and belt clutched in both hands. “Hey how about a picture together?” Greg asked, producing a digital Nikon from a coffee table next to the television. “Alrighty, where do you want me?” Von Kelsig asked. “Just at the side there, Miho you take the picture I'll fix Ashley up.” Greg replied, turning and handing the camera to his wife then moving back over to Ashley, holding him at the back and helping him sit up before plumping up his pillow closer to the edge of the bed and resting him back down on it as Von Kelsig leaned over next to him. “Raise a fist for me kiddo.” Von Kelsig said, holding his massive clenched paw next to Ashley as he lifted his arm up and tightened his hand into a little fist of his own. Side by side Ashley's entire hand was a tiny bit bigger than one of Von Kelsig's knuckles. Touching his fist to Ashley's, they both smiled as Miho took the picture. “Hey I got a good idea for another,” Von Kelsig said, glancing at Ashley and flicking his beard, “grab hold of this like you're tugging it and gimme a growl.” He giggled, reaching up with both hands and grabbing handfuls of Hessian's great Viking beard. He tugged on it, though his strength wasn't enough to hurt Von Kelsig. Then, twisting his face into a look of cartoonish agony while Ashley grit his teeth and sneered playfully at his idol, Miho took another picture. “Great!” she pipped, hopping on the spot as she viewed the picture on the little digital screen on the back of the camera. “Wanna see, Ashley?” Taking the camera over to him, she showed the first picture with him next to Hessian followed by the beard tugging. Ashley chuckled a sweet innocent chuckle before his mouth opened wide and he let out a short yawn. “Aw, feeling tired son?” Greg asked, to which Ashley nodded back. “You want to say goodbye to Hessian and get some more shuteye?” Miho asked. Shaking his head, Ashley petted his lip, but couldn't resist a second yawn, his eyelids drooping a little as the Sandman crept closer. “Don't worry buddy, we can always see each other again. In fact, I might be seeing you sooner than you think.” Von Kelsig assured Ashley. “Gimme a hug kiddo, I'll leave you to get some sleep. Us fighters need our rest.” Rising up over the bed, Von Kelsig leaned over until his beard was tickling Ashley's face. Gently lifting both arms, the little guy wrapped them around Hessian's neck and pressed his face to Hessian's cheek. “Whoa, whoa, not so tight buddy!” the giant jested, reciprocating the hug before being released and standing up straight again. “Don't wanna choke me out before my match do ya? Then Youngblood'd have to fight you...and I wouldn't like his odds with a grip like that!” Again, Ashley giggled and held his hands clasped over his chin, even as his eyelids got heavier and he became sleepier. “It was a pleasure meeting you Ashley.” Taking his kit bag, Von Kelsig shouldered it and said his goodbyes to the parents and the doctor before Natsumo escorted him from the room. Out in the hall he felt like a heavenly aura had enveloped him, his spirits lifted after peering into those big innocent eyes and hearing that cute little chuckle so often. “That was very kind of you, sir. He was very shy I think. His father was saying out here earlier it's the happiest he's looked in a long time, and the most he's moved. You gave him some of your strength I think.” Natsumo remarked, pushing her glasses up her nose. “Kid's a gem.” he replied, before fishing through his bag again and producing three all-access passes. “Listen, make sure these are on the tray when he wakes up, and let his parents know he'll get the VIP treatment at ReVolution. When they show these at the door they'll be ushered straight in by one of our staff and taken care of. Can you do that for me?” he asked, not so much condescendingly as ensuring her with Ashley's happiness. “Most certainly sir. Thank you again, and good luck in your wrestling match.” Natsumo said with a bow as Von Kelsig tipped his imaginary hat and took his leave of the hospital. Poor little guy's got nothing...nothing but hope, he thought to himself as he left the building and hailed another cab. It was heartbreaking to see a little boy who had never wronged anyone struck down with an illness as devastating as leukemia. Never had a chance to flourish in the world, and by the time Von Kelsig got back to the States he'd probably have lost his battle with it. And yet, throughout the entire visit he never once complained, never showed pain or fear even when his breath started to fail him. He just looked up with those eyes and smiled that big smile and ignored it all. As the taxi sped back to Saitama, Von Kelsig thought about Ashley's courage. The thought of an eight-year old boy fighting leukemia made him question his own resolve. If little Ashley could fight this, then he had a nerve to complain that the world was getting him down just because he was backed into a corner. The boy could die from his affliction, yet the worst Von Kelsig would suffer is a dip in his wages at most, his depression not even factoring into his analysis. He would live on, and he could survive this. He'd put on a show for that little guy; draw strength from him as Ashley had from the giant Hessian today. He would survive. No matter what.
