Something Is Better Than Nothing

Date: Culture Shock “It just doesn’t make any fucking sense.” THOOMPH! His shin crashes against the thick sparring pad with an intense force. The impact reverberates through the padding. Waves vibrate down her forearm. She staggers a half step back before catching herself. Juli Lee blows a loose clump of hair from her eye. The damp collection of strands does not budge. She hears the rippling air before she notices the movement. Juli braces herself for the next blow. Another bone shattering shin kick. This one drops her right on her ass. An amused chuckle echoes through the empty training center. She looks up with more than a bit of frustration creasing the lines of her face. The chuckling buffoon above her offers a hand. She takes it and allows him to pull her back to her feet. “Don’t you have someone else to do this with you? I’m not cut out for this shit.” He laughs again. Tyler Rayne can be a real asshole sometimes. “You’re doin’ fine, kid.” The smile he offers is more amused than reassuring, but somehow, it does make her feel better. Damn she hated that. Little twist of his lips and the bastard could manipulate the female populace on a whim. His scarred but well-defined upper torso was glistening with a sheen of sweat he’d worked up from beating on her the last hour or so. She expected him to keep at it. Much to her surprise (and relief), he opted to roll to the outside of the ring and start toweling off. “What do you care, anyway? Thought you were done with all this.” “So did I. These fuckers don’t seem to be getting the memo, though.” Things had been relatively quiet for the past few weeks. Tyler had finally managed to deal with… stuff… and more or less calmed down. As close to normal as he could get. Professionally speaking, things were good. The Sutherland kid was proving to be a pain in the ass… but otherwise things were considerably more calm than the normal tornado of violence that followed The Underground Pimp. And now that he wasn’t out picking fights with random biker gangs, his personal life was beginning to even as well. He could have been approaching a personal record for longest stretch of time without a brawl or getting arrested. That was until two nights ago, when yet another representative of the Thai underground came to appeal for assistance. As with the previous attempts, Tyler politely refused… but he hadn’t been able to stop thinking about it since then. Juli found the whole damn thing annoying. “What does she need from Thailand? She’s got the running the biggest racket in Japan. Doing very well for herself by not overextending resources. If she wanted to expand, there are certainly more lucrative ways to do so. Bangkok would be such a pain in the ass to take, let alone control, from where she’s at.” “I just don’t get why it matters.” “Look, I’ve met the Scorpion a few times, right? Doesn’t make sense for her. She’s a smart woman. That’s why she’s in the position she is now. Fucking with the Thais is just an unnecessary risk. She doesn’t take unnecessary risks.” “Apparently she does.” “There’s something there. Something specific she’s either trying to hide or tying to get. It’s the only thing that makes sense.” “Ty, I really think you have more important things to worry about.” He nods. There is that whole Culture Shock thing coming up. “Everything’s straight?” “Yes. Pinfalls Count Anywhere, just like you asked.” “Shiny.” “So what next?” “I watch tape. Kid’s got a handful of matches I can check out. See how to break him. You better put some ice on that forearm ‘fore it swells.” She grimaces. “Oh, by the way. Did I tell you about the odds they were giving you online?” “Nah. How bad was it?” “Seventy-nine percent chance you don’t even show.” “Fuck. Lost faith, have they?” “Well you haven’t exactly been yourself lately.” “True enough. Ah well. Works out better for me, anyway. I do so love beating the odds.” “Why don’t you concentrate on just beating Sutherland? We’ll work from there.” “Yeah, yeah. Get some ice.” “I’ll meet you in the study. Grab a couple beers while I’m getting ice.” “You’re a doll.” “It’s what you pay me for.” “Handsomely, I might add.” “Eh.” He laughs. Girl made more bank than Matt Mills. For real. He looked up Mills’ payroll records just to make sure. Culture Shock. Cyrus Sutherland. Tyler sighed and marched off in the direction of the study. Time to put this fucker in his place. Gods damned soulless gingers.
