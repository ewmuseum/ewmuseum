He grabs his sandwich and gets up from the table. “Stupid broad, it’s always about the money huh? Ahhh well. You’ll do just fine Sunday and when I buy you that drink I’ll buy one for Sun as well. It may seem outta character for me to say this, but it’s actually nice to sit and talk with someone about these talentless hacks while they share the same outlook on things as I do. We’ll have to do this more often. Needless to say I don’t get along with ANYONE in this business.” He slips his jacket back on and slides a pair of sunglasses up the bridge of his nose that he plucked out of the pocket. Shakur gives a nod in response. "You are an alright bloke, got your head on straight and your priorities in order. You’ll definitely go far in this business. After CIV I expect the two of us to bask in the glory, me with a belt and you a full fledged superstar. Wade won't last five minutes in the ring with you. He'll probably be blinded by the amount of spotlights, or be so stupid that he believes he sees God in them." Shakur takes another sip of his drink. "We're a rare breed in this business Nitz, people with actual talent." “Preachin’ to the mother fuckin’ choir my man.” He reaches out and shakes hands with Shakur, releasing the grip to straighten his coat. He gives a smile. “Say hi to Sun for me.” He proceeded to walk away from the table, leaving Devin to his task of waiting for his main squeeze. The gym was only a few blocks away, acting as the final aggression outlet before he gets to truly do some damage Sunday.
