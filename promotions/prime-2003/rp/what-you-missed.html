What You Missed...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Cataclysm RING! RING! RING! "Hi, you've reached the voicemail of C.P. Cantrell, executive producer of PR--" CLICK! "FUCK! Mary-Lynn Mayweather can only cringe in fright as she looks over at Craig who is throwing his phone onto his bed with a certain force behind it. His hands are firmly on his hips as his stance does nothing to hide the fact that he is insanely pissed off. Wearing a pair of dark blue jeans and a short-sleeved plain black t-shirt his days have gotten worse over the past few weeks. Ignoring the fact that his personal life was in complete shambles, but now his professional life had gone down in a bloody abyss. He glares around his bedroom before staring back at his bed. "Fuck you, Cantrell! Fuck you! Fuck you up your stupid fucking ass you no good, reality show whore! FUCK YOU!" he yells vehemently, only causing Mary-Lynn to recoil in her chair as she quickly starts to look for a way out of this precarious situation she's found herself in. Craig turns towards her as he begins to pace around in the room and contemplates his options before him. "Talk to me, Craig," she speaks in a hushed tone, hoping to calm down Craig before he flew off the handles and killed Cantrell or someone else. She thinks to herself of how in a funk that Craig had been when it came to PRIME, losing matches he shouldn't have even lost, ever since the war with SCCW had begun. His mouth had shut as the fans had started to let their feelings be known when it came to his rampant losses and his perceived refusal to join PRIME in the war. Mary-Lynn though had seen more than any of those fans and knew the position Craig had been put in. Things weren't exactly easy for him and they wouldn't get any easier until Cataclysm was far behind him. "What the fuck do you want me to say, Mary-Lynn? The fucker won't answer my phone calls any longer!" he yells back, beyond the point of frustration. The look in his eyes gave it all away; he wanted to hit something and he wanted to hit it as hard as possible. As he walks over to his bed and sits on the edge of his bed he places his face into the palms of his hands and bites down as hard as possible to not just lash out at everything around him. "Maybe he doesn't want to be kept being told 'I told you so." I mean, he's starin' at a huge ol' war in his mouth and there's a good sense among the people I've talked too that Cantrell's chokin' on having way too much on his plate. Desade is tenacious and intelligent. Even if she loses this battle, this war isn't over. SCCW's still gonna come after us, and she's comin' after Troy. And you warned him! You told him and begged and pleaded for us not to go down this path. But now, he has no one to blame but himself, for the position he's putting us ALL into now," she tells him, hoping to snap him out of that funk. "Chadwick doesn't think like that, Mary-Lynn. He doesn't take or admit blame. He doles it out the moment he lays his beady little eyes on it," he responds with a quick tongue, his words not helping the cause of beating Cantrell's face in with a lead bat. "He might not think like that, Craig, but he also realizes the dangers. The pitfalls of what he's doing. He's not a stupid man, and I know you're rather well aware of that. Talking to you right now would just continue to confirm his suspicions. He knows what your thoughts are and he knows how pissed you are at him! He's put you in a very awkward situation and there's nothing you can do about it now except stare it straight in the face and accept it. Desade will be there. Cozen might be there. It's time to face your demons," she strikes right back hoping to bring the fire back to the man who had disappeared into the fray unable to find himself. Craig looks up at Mary-Lynn and just shakes his head. "Desade is a different kind of beast in herself. I stand up to her, I get into her face and it's just going to get worse for me. I still haven't even figured everything out, Mary-Lynn. She's pulling Cozen's strings... is she pulling my strings? Does she have something to do with Nicole and Katie? How far is her reach? Damnit, Mary-Lynn, she got you involved in all of this!" Craig then walks over to Mary-Lynn and looks her in the eyes before his right hand grazes over her still healing injury from a few weeks back when her skull was cracked open. She grimaces in pain as Craig just looks at her with these soft looking eyes, eyes she had never seen before in her life from him. "Yeah, don't remind me," she remarks, her attitude souring at the mere thought of those evenings, joy coming out of her when Troy agreed to help her train and then the disappointment she felt in herself after she lost her debut match. Mary-Lynn had beaten herself up a few times thinking about it and just wished she would have another chance to show what she was capable of inside of that ring. "I should've been there, Mary-Lynn. I should've been protecting you, getting you out of harm's way--," he begins to tell her before she cuts him off with a fire in her throat. "I don't need protecting Craig! I should've beaten her down! Savant, I should've kicked her ass!" She yells at him while her hands turn into fists as she thinks about getting back into that ring and fighting with Savant one more time. Craig simply puts his hand on Mary-Lynn's shoulder to calm her down. "I know you don't, Mary-Lynn. Not in that ring. That's not what I meant. I meant with Lindsay...," he says before his voice trails off. She looks at him with this curious look in her eyes. "Lindsay? She was just trying to help me...," she says before her voice trails off as well. "Lindsay will never admit this, but she was using this. She doesn't know it herself. She's gotten herself into something deeper then she realizes something that she can't hope to dig herself out of. Troy couldn't do it on her own and instead of protecting people from the mess she had gotten herself into she decided to drag you and others into it. People want to blame Desade for all of this? One day, Lindsay will have to wake up in the morning, shrug off the injuries she's accumulated over the years, and look at herself in the mirror. For a while, she'll be able to ignore what she sees there, but eventually she'll realize her part in all of this. The only problem is that so many others won't be able to see the manipulative bitch that she tries so hard to hide," he says before sitting on the floor next to Mary-Lynn. The idea of this war brewing had left him exhausted as he did everything possible to put it to a stop. When it had exploded though, he wanted no part of it. Doing so would only draw unneeded attention to himself. He had to find out answers on his own, whether or not Desade was involved, and if she wasn't then he would only create one other enemy. Cozen had shown up to his house and helped aide with the kidnapping of not only him, but Katie and Nicole. Someone had been pulling Cozen's strings that evening, but he wasn't convinced that it was just Desade. Nonetheless, he needed to stay off of her radar for as long as possible. He intended on not showing up to Cataclysm knowing that the evening wouldn't end just there, but much further down the road. Time was of the essence and he was afraid it was slowly slipping from his grips. Mary-Lynn just sits back in the chair for a moment, thinking about everything Craig had just said, and remembered that Jack had told her the same thing two days prior. She just grounds as she realizes how right they were and how far gone she was. Still, honor meant something to her, and she intended to never let that part of her go; no matter how crazy this business got. "Jack said the same thing to me just a few days ago," she tells him, deciding that she could admit that one thing to Craig. Craig just laughs at that notion. "Jack and I've talked about it a few times. It's easy to get worked up in the heat of the moment, but you have to learn how to take a step back and just analyze the situation. You're still a rookie in that ring, in this business, and I don't want to see you hurt. Especially not because of Lindsay." "Y'know, I thought I was doing the right thing. Getting a chance to compete, to defend the honor of PRIME, and to help out my childhood idol. I mean, I'd have loved to put the nail in the coffin of this feud, but I just caused more problems. And I thought if I could take out Savant, y'know, my pedigree would be raised, I'd be considered, y'know, an up and comer. Greed and Pride. Seems like most wrestlers suffer from both at times... I just wanted to be in that ring so much. I just... Jack's taught me so much, but... it's nothing like being in that ring." "Let me help you. Let me help you get ready in that ring and you're going to be showing Lindsay how it's done in that ring. Jack has done a great jo--," he begins to tell her before his bedroom door flies open and in walks one Jack Harmon, otherwise known as High Flyer. He looks around in this daze, almost as if he had appeared in the room without even knowing it. "YO! Foo' need some food foo'! Craig, do you have an aversion to groceries?!" he yells Craig and all Craig can do is chuckle. Jack looks around the room a little bit, kind of pissed at the fact that Craig's room is larger than his while forgetting the fact that it's Craig's house that he's staying in. Jack then jumps into Craig's bed and lies out as he sees that no one in the room is quite in the mood to be moving. "See, I knew Jack had the same characteristics as Beetlejuice," Mary-Lynn says with some sarcasm in her voice that causes Craig to laugh full out, the first time he had done so in a long time. For the first time in a long time, the three of them just sit in the room on the same page. Their lives had all taken them down different paths, events changing their mindsets, and instead of working together they had each encountered this situation differently. Their connection to the war was deep, as deep as Troy's if not deeper. They were friends and not just friends for now, but for life. Mary-Lynn had been attacked. Craig's life had been put in shambles and Desade somehow had a part in all of it. Jack valued his friendships with them more than his own life at times and had to watch as his two friends were torn to shreds by this war. He had been in the room when Cantrell had almost threatened to blackball Dusk from PRIME and everywhere else if he didn't step up to the plate. It had become a mess, something that they couldn't handle by themselves, but together they could come out stronger in the end. With the silence lingering in the room, Jack finally pipes up. "Just thought you'd like to know that Mary-Lynn broke one of your vases." Craig's head shoots around to Mary-Lynn who is shooting Jack a dirty look that screams out "You dirty muthafucka..." Craig can only shake his head though. "I didn't know I had any vases in here." Jack then clears his throat as he lies flat on the bed and looks up at the ceiling. "So, this Cataclysm thing, it's going to get pretty ugly, isn't it? I mean, what the fuck has Troy gotten us into? My boy over here has been basically neutered and Mary-Lynn is being cracked in the back of the skull and then going off on me. Shit ain't right around here any longer! It's all fucked up! So, the question remains, Craig...," Jack begins to say before he turns his eyes to Craig. "When're you going to end this war?" Craig just kind of sits there dumbfounded for a moment while he thinks about it all. Then, almost as if a light bulb popped out of nowhere, he had his answer. "What the fuck are you talking about, Jack?" Craig asks him with this short tone as if Jack had smoked too much weed earlier in the day. "You know exactly what I'm talking about, Craig," Jack starts off. "I've told you week after week that it should be you in the front of this war, not Rayne. Rayne is only there so he can like Lindsay's twat at the end of the night. Elliott? Come on, he has no vested interest in this. Nova? Same deal. He just likes to hit things. The first person Lindsay should've come to was you. The first person Cantrell should've come to was you. You're dealing with a crazy woman in Desade, someone who's been playing Cozen. They might not know your personal life story, Craig, but obviously you knew Cozen better than any of them did. The kind of person she was. Desade was right down your alley and instead, they shrugged you off. They ignored you. They laughed in your face. You, out of anyone, can end this. Talk to Desade and let her know the consequences of her actions if she continues. Troy isn't going to put the fear of God into her, you will." Craig listens to those words for those moments but those thoughts are interrupted as Mary-Lynn pipes in, "He's right." Craig just sighs as he hears her say that and just shakes his head. "First off, I'm not fixing Lindsay's problems. I refuse to do that. She's going to fall flat on her face and I'm not going to be the one to peel her off the ground. Desade, she's already got Lindsay figured out and much better than Lindsay knows herself. Lindsay got herself into this issue and decided to blow me off again, like she always does, then that's her business. Second, I can't stop Desade on my own. But, I'll deal with Desade on my own time," he tells the two of them as they both open their mouths to retaliate. Yet, before they get a chance to get a word off, Craig's phone starts ringing as Jack grabs it and tosses it over to Craig. As Craig looks at the caller ID screen, he expects it to be Cantrell calling him back. However, the ID screen reads something else. Lisa Tyler Craig looks up at Mary-Lynn before showing her the screen. She glances at it briefly before Craig answers the phone. As Jack looks at Mary-Lynn wondering who it could be, Mayweather mouths the name back to Jack. It takes him a minute to realize that it's the PRIME Executive. "Lisa," Craig starts off, "to what do I owe this extreme pleasure? "Needed to touch base with you. I got wind that you've been blowing up Cantrell's phone as of late and wanted to talk to you personally. But, first, I need you to pack your bags. You're coming to Boston," she tells him to which Craig gets this bewildered look on his face. "I'm what? Why the fuck would I do that?" he yells back into the phone, not happy with this bit of news in the least bit. "Look, I'm not going to pussyfoot around with you Craig. You're a PRIME wrestler, a superstar even if you don't like to accept that term when you're on the losing streak that you're on right now, so I do reserve the right to have you booked in a match. More importantly, I need you to set the tone of the evening. Be that rallying call for everyone in PRIME. There's this twenty-man battle royal that opens the show. I've booked you there," she tells him and Craig immediately starts getting up to his feet. "No, Lisa, no! I told Cantrell specifically I wanted NO part of this stupid little war! I told him NOT to book me in a fucking match and I'm being booked in a fucking match! This is ridiculous! Lisa, I won't have it!" he yells into his phone as Mary-Lynn shrinks in her chair upset with the fact that Craig's upset again after she had calmed him down. Craig begins pacing around the room as his anger starts to build up inside of him. "Look, who the hell do you think you're talking to Craig? I'm your boss, not the other way around. You do what I tell you. I know you've done your best not to get involved in this business. I know you blame Lindsay for this war. I know you tried to tell Cantrell not to get involved with all of this and that he didn't listen to this. The fact remains, Craig, that I need you in this match, do you understand? You're easily the odds on favorite to win this match. I respect the fact that you don't want to get involved with Desade--," she begins to tell him before Craig cuts her off. "How the hell do you know that?!" he demands to know from her. "My job is talent relations, Craig. I know a lot that you'd rather that I not know. Like the death of Nicole and Katie. I have files on you Craig because frankly, you're dangerous and I need to keep a tab on you. I don't know what Desade did or did not do, but you need to stop backing away from her and stand up to her. This is your way to do it. Get on her radar. Lindsay's dropped the ball on this war too much. I need someone to put the fear of God into her heart," she tells him as Craig looks over at Jack and wonders to himself why it seems like Lisa is giving him the same pep talk that Jack had given him just moments ago. "Fine," he tells her begrudgingly. "Thank you, Craig. I hope you take a moment to confront Desade while you're at it," she tells him before hanging up the phone. Craig tosses the phone onto the bed again as he stares out his bedroom window. "Lisa's booked me into a match. Don't have a choice. 20 men battle royal," he tells the duo. Jack grits his teeth as he looks at Craig. "That shit is not going to be easy," he reminds Craig to which he can only nod his head. "She also wants me to sit down and talk to Desade," he tells them. Mary-Lynn begins to shift in her chair. "Well, are you?" she asks Craig and slowly Craig turns around. "If that opportunity presents itself, sure. We need to have a sit-down. I've got some questions for her," he tells them as he just stands there while Jack begins to get up grabbing his stomach. "Okay, this chit chat is all fun and stuff. We can talk on the way to Boston. But, shit, can we get some food?!" he yells out at the both of them to which they nod their heads and begin to walk out of the room. In the back of his mind, Craig has to wonder if he can use this war to his advantage, to keep Desade occupied. In a few nights, he could have some answers to the questions he desperately seeked. If they went the way he thought they would, he would have to wage his own war against Desade. "So," Jack begins to say as they walk out of the room, "is it true that Kanye broke a camera and that you bailed him out of jail?" The End
