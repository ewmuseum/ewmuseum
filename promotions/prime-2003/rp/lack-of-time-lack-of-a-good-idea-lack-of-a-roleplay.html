Lack of Time + Lack of a Good Idea = Lack of a Roleplay

Date: Does it Matter? When I was just a kid, I remember the presents I got for Christmas. Sure my late sister and I spent time with many a foster family, but that doesn't mean we didn't have fun at Christmas time. Summer always got what she wanted cause she was the youngest and she was the princess, you know how things go in families with an only daughter. I got what I wanted most of the time and yet there were moments after I just removed the wrapping paper from my last gift that I was disappointed. I remember one Christmas where I wanted a Big Wheel. Everyone had one and let's face it, peer pressure when you're a kid was huge. One Christmas I wanted a Nerf Dart Gun, but my foster family at the time thought it would be too dangerous. Yeah, sponge darts dangerous. Any kid who could truly get hurt with a sponge dart deserved to get coloring books for Christmas well into their 20's. But there was one Christmas in particular that I got exactly what I wanted. But today brought it all back to me. |~o~| June 25, 2009 Killean had just gotten home a couple hours earlier from a doctor's appointment. Ever since the attack from Tyler Nelson, he had a sore shoulder. That damn interviewer monkey could still swing a mean chair when he wanted to. Jenny was still sorting through a few boxes, some of which were damaged in the fire, to find certain items that Killean wanted. He understood and forgave Jenny for what had happened even though he lost many a precious item. But what Killean wanted meant just a little more on this day. “I got them over here!” Jenny said, opening a box to find a bunch of old records. Killean left what he was doing and sat down beside his fiance to look with her. He sifted through a bunch of records and thankfully the only ones damaged were against the inside of the box. He immediately sifted to the middle of the pack and found exactly what he was looking for. It was played perhaps only a dozen times back in the day and now it was wrapped in plastic, perfect as the day he got it for Christmas. “I remember the day I got this for Christmas. Summer and I were with the Johnson's back then. They gave her the doll she wanted and a new painting kit. I got a new bike with training wheels and this.” He said, holding the record up for Jenny to see. The word “Thriller” was clearly visible on the cover, the best selling album in the world. “I never was much of a fan, but you're not really gonna give your Christmas gift to Cadence are you?” Jenny said. Everyone in the family knew that Cadence was the biggest fan of the King of Pop and what was announced only an hour earlier was sure to break her heart. “Nah, I don't need to. After a quick visit to eBay my pocketbook hurts and seems a bit lighter but she's my half sister and she's worth it. It should be here late next week.” “What'd you get her?” Jenny said, looking at the back of the album to read the song list. “No way. You'll know when she knows. I hear you guys taking all the time and you two can't keep a single secret from each other!” Killean pulled the record from Jenny's hands and ran for the stairs. “Oh come on, you gotta tell me! Killean!” Jenny chased after him playfully. “NO! Stay away!” |~o~| Bet no one would ever think I wanted an MJ album for Christmas when I was a kid. Fact is, when it comes down to music, I have specific tastes. But back in those days, his music was revolutionary. Still is as a matter of fact. But today I'm nothing more than your average, run of the mill, humbled fan. Sure, I've got millions of fans of my own around the world, but it should be fair to say that Michael Jackson had many more fans, possibly numbering over one billion the world over. Back in the early to late eighties, the man was a God in the music business. Everyone wanted a piece of what he did. I thought I was a generous man, but I feel small when I compare my own charitable donations to the three hundred million plus dollars in donations he offered. Sure he was in debt up to now, but unfortunately, the man is worth way more now than he was alive and thankfully his kid's will be taken care of. As for Cadence, I learned during the almost never ending time we spent together after Dad died that she really didn't like much music. There was a point in time where she wanted to be in on the discussions about the music I use in PRIME. Let's just say her options were extremely limited and she quickly found herself listening to the discussion rather than contributing. She has very specific tastes in music, more so than myself. But the one thing I know, and the one thing I knew from the beginning, she was a die hard fan of Michael Jackson. I was a fan, but she was on a different level. She never made it a part of her every day life like some of the nut job look-a-likes that dress that way every day. But everyone who knew her knew just how big a fan she was. She owned every album he ever released but most were on CD's and cassette tapes. I knew she'd be crushed and I talked to her only moments after it was announced. It didn't hit her then but soon after it did and she was a wreck. I had to do something special for her. The Elvis of this generation had just died and if our parents taught us anything, Elvis is still prevalent today. I thought about giving her my record, but it would just be nothing more than a good gesture at that point. No, I hadn't really done much for my half sister recently and now was the time I could do something very nice for her. She was my world, reminding me of my late sister Summer. So I had to surprise her and make her happy. |~o~| Saturday, July 4, 2009 “Dude, where are you?” Vegas asked from the main family room. Killean was upstairs, getting the gift he ordered for Cadence which had come express mail just a day earlier. No one except Jenny and himself knew what he was doing. “Dinner is getting cold and I didn't barbecue this stuff for nothing!” Killean came down the stairs soon after Vegas called for him, He had a large item in his hands with a material cover over it. He placed it on the couch beside his half sister and everyone stopped what they were doing. Jenny just smiled sweetly. “What's this?” Cadence asked quietly, looking up at her half brother. “Well, it's a gift from Killean and myself to you. We knew how special it would be to you.” Jenny explained, as everyone in the room suddenly clued in on what the subject matter of the gift was immediately. Cadence stood up, trying to figure out exactly what was going on when Killean pulled the cover off the gift. Cadence almost collapsed in shock upon seeing what it was they had gotten for her. Inside of a gold frame with a velvet backing was the front cover of the Thriller album. Below it was the certificate of authenticity and finally was a platinum record, resting in it's place. “Only a limited amount of these ever made. I hooked up with a collector online and even though it cost me a small fortune, I knew right when I saw it that I had to get it for you.” Killean said as Cadence turned to him and jumped into his arms. She cried a little bit on his shoulder as Killean hugged her tightly. “But that's not it.” Jenny said, coming over to Cadence and hugging her too. Jenny grabbed the frame and turned it around, revealing something stuck to the back. Cadence reached out and grabbed the item but soon realized there were six items instead of just one. She could barely speak as she realized just what they were. “Six tickets to the Staples Center for the Memorial on July 7 for myself, Mom, Killean, Jenny, Dave and Hanna.” |~o~| Those tickets were harder to come by than the album itself. Cadence was stunned and I don't think she knew what to think. But the tickets were not just for her. The tickets were for all of us. I had the chance for the first time in many years to reverse my usual role and become just an average fan, instead of being the one under the spotlight. My time would come at Colossus, my final event as an active PRIME member. But as I sat in the Staples Center just a couple short days ago, I realized one thing. I had always wanted to be just an average person ever since I found success in PRIME. I was not cut out for being in the limelight all that often. But as MJ showed every one of his fans, he loved them all unconditionally and did what he did in music and in life for them and the rest of the people around the world. I hope come Colossus I could be even a fraction of what MJ was to his fans, to my own fans.
