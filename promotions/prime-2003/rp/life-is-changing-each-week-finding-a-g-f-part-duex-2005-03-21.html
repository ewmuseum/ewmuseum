Life is Changing each week (Finding a G/F Part Duex)

21 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/21/05 This evening is something I want her to remember for a long time. When she speaks of it I want her to use words like magical and enchanted to describe it. Will that be the final result? Who knows… but one thing I know is I am eager to find out. Peter Vetra is at his nice apartment in Norwood, Massachusetts, he seemingly is dressed to impress with his fancy clothes. We haven’t seen Peter this happy since he has joined PRIME; usually Peter is glum from his most recent loss on either a Revolution or the latest PPV. But not this week, his most recent loss to Dani Furher doesn’t bother him at all, worrying about Emilio Rage next week is the furthest from his mind right now. The only thing on his mind right now was her, her smile, he look, and her features. Ever since last week when the date had been arranged it was hard for Peter to get her off his mind. During Revolution 58 when he lost to Dani Furher, he was thinking of her, was that the cause? Maybe no one really knows. But since his return from California it had been all Melissa all the time. The win loss record, the title situation, even the nature of his depression all came second to her. RING… RING… RING… Peter’s telephone starts to ring and vibrate in his dress pant’s pocket. Overly excited knowing it was her on the other line and this was the call he had been waiting for all night. Peter felt a large amount of pressure come off his chest as he was relieved she actually called… the time seemed all to long though. “Hello!?” Peter answered his phone quickly and eagerly. Waiting to hear her voice on the other end, it felt like an eternity since they had last talked, but now that they are talking again it seemed like pure ectasy for Peter. “Good Evening Peter, I am glad you answered the phone. It’s about tonight. I am not going to be able to make it.” The subtle voice of Melissa was a comfort for Peter in one way but the devastating news was a killer. “How come?” Peter who is still getting over the initial shock is running many scenarios through his head. “Peter, I got called into work tonight. I am sorry, can we reschedule?” The voice of Melissa on the other end was soothing, and the reason she can’t go out is a valid reason. “Well…ok, is it ok if I come in and visit you? Then we can make another date?” Peter is trying to grasp onto something just so he can ensure he sees her again. “That would be fine; at least I will get to see you tonight.” Melissa relieved that he pursued the date so she might get a chance to see him. “Well, I have to get ready for work; I will see you a little later.” “Ok, I will see you tonight. Don’t work to hard.” Peter who is a little depressed and bummed out that he is not going to see this beautiful girl tonight on a one on one basis but he is content with at least getting to see her. A dial tone is heard and Peter just falls onto his bed which was near by. He closes his eyes and his mind just starts to go into every which direction.
