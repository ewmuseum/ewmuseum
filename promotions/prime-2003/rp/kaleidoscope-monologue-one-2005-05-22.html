Kaleidoscope Monologue. - ONE

22 May 2005

Date: 5/22/05 'There are no more barriers to cross. All I have in common with the uncontrollable and the insane, the vicious and the evil, all the mayhem I have caused and my utter indifference toward it, I have now surpassed.' Kaleidoscope Monologue. ONE By Nick Brocken For the past twenty some-odd years of my life I have been this numb, no-nothing motherfucker and I regret it. For the past twenty some-odd years of my life I have been oblvious to one and all and I regret it. For the past twenty some-odd years of my life tragedy has welcomed me into its home and I regret it. I'm a stronger person now, deep down inside, I am a stronger person. The Basement. Garbage men and twisted homosexuals beware, there may be a barrier between us when it comes to veterans and amateurs, but, I promise that we will go as easy as possible on both of you. You see, Lopez has the tendency to go all 'White Tiger' on his opponents and I tend to imagine gouging your eyes out with rusty coat-hangers after we take turns turning both of you into pink-fetishing bitches. So, in hindsight, everything matches and pairs well. Welcome one and all, to the basement. The hideaway lodge that dwells underneath the ground for the psychotic, embryonic tragedy that is, Nicholas Brocken. In his time under the alias, Ozric Mortimer, he turned the dim-lit crawl space into an upside down traveling circus; colored with crayon and finger paints. Decorated with horror posters and framed autographs of notorious serial killers and one-sided maniacs that have since been locked up or died. Many have believed and understood Nick's past but in that percentage that do, in fact, seem to understand don't realize that they're fooling themselves in the process. To most, this basement may seem like a breeding pool for some metaphorical psycho who enjoys strangling women. An 'Amityville Horror' poster is nailed against one of the walls, along with an oversized picture of Ed Gein -- Autographed and framed. It appeared that Nick had found a strange passion that deep down inside, made him feel complete and connected. As we draw closer to the framed picture, we can see a weight bench and a desk with three stacks of plain white paper upon it. 'Home sweet home.' Nicholas murmured to himself as he pulled his wife-beater off, exposing a scattered plethora of bruises, scars and artsy tattoos. A feeling of nausea thrushed through Nicholas' body as he closed his eyes and began his flight into euphoria. Jumping through a barage of obstacles to be on his own, crawling through the lovelessness of human kind that stained his soul within. Ignoring the warning signs that put him at risk with the public eye, Nicholas fought off the sickness that controlled him only to open-heartedly welcome a sickness that he, in fact, controls. 'Dearly beloved, we are here to congratulate and personify a work of art. We are here to congratulate the Embryonic tragedy and thank him for embracing his heart and soul into the most dangerous sport in the world.' hollered Nick as he pulled out a black and white colored top-hat and squeezed onto his head. To complete the look Nick shoved his hand into the cardboard box that lay in front of him and pulled out a 'magical' wand. 'Yes! I am here for all of you! Adulate me to the best of your abilities because you and I both know I'm worth the dignity loss.' screeched Nick who wound the wand and swung it against one of the scattered dirty windows, which shattered. 'Can you hear the marching? The trembling of the Gods above our heads? Can you hear the vibration of brass from the trumpets sounding off for my introduction? If you can't, then you're not officially dead to society like I am..' Klingman. I once had a doctor who practiced his counselling skills on me. It came to the point where I realized that he was an overpaid waste of my life and no matter how hard I try I'll always be a raging schizoid. I'm shoved under the microscope and stared at for hours upon hours. Drugged and battered by doctor's opinions. I'm locked up for hours upon hours with just the white cushion of the walls to look at. Thoughts are blinded out and I'm no longer treated for misguided sicknesses. I can use this all to my advantage and there's nothing John Kano or TCM can do about it. Twiddling his thumbs while looking through a mass of papers scattered along his metal desk, Jack Klingman's nerves were chewing him up. Migrating to a new city wasn't all that easy and having nearly half the patients he had before made for a much less strenuous job; therefore, the doctor was bored to brail and was clearly nowhere near the right part of the stream. Opening his desk drawer, he looked down at six packages of pencils; all sharpened. '...I sharpened them all?' questioned Jack as he slammed the drawer shut and followed it with a wolf-howling sigh, folding his arms and hiding his face amongst the darkness. As he pondered on whether or not to start looking for other placements, he thought of his most controversial and mysterious patient, Ozric Mortimer. Was he still alive and if so, was he cured and healthy? It hit a soft spot on the doctor and triggered him aggressively. Pushing the crimson red intercom button, a loud 'BWEEEP' sound alarmed the secretary sitting outside of Jack's office. 'What can I do for you, Doctor?' answered Tracey who hid her magnificent wad of speariment gum underneath her tongue so she could talk correctly. 'Tracey, do you mind looking up some of the current records on a former patient? His name is Nicholas Brocken and if you can't find anything on that, look up Ozric Mortimer.' suggested Jack as he released the hold on the button and sat back in his seat. He had several meetings squared away this afternoon that he was anticipating on canceling at this point. Memoir. Everyone has an apathetic side to them and there's no way to rid it. I've become part of this evergrowing sport to distiguish my apathetic side so I can turn it into something powerful and obstructive. Fact is, I nearly killed myself in the practice of it last time. So, I guess it's time to take the more simplistic route and just go in there full force and try my hardest, right? No. If I had the chance I'd gouge both of your motherfucking eyes out with a rusty coat hanger. May both of your pray that the scenario doesn't come into play.. The friendship between the doctor and his patient had always been mutual until their final session together. Where Nicholas lost himself and attacked the doctor, laying him out with a marble ashtray. It wasn't so much that the friendship had ended, but moreso that it was put on hold and automatically terminated in light of the events. Stiches covered the back of Dr. Klingman's head and put him out of work for nearly two months. In those two months, neither one had contacted eachother.In those two months, Brocken had reached another level of insanity... This insanity was much more definied, yet, very well concealed and leveled to the point where he seems realistic and genuine to the public eye but turns into a vivacious oddity while on his own. An oddity that most would cringe or hide from if they knew about. No longer hidden from society by strange clown uniforms and blotchy makeup that made him look like he was in drag. No longer in colorful cosmos written, directed and distributed by his own twisted and malicious psyche, but, Moreso in a bi-polar millenia of divine chagrin. Conformed with seas of maglomaniac teirs of anarchy that set him into a portal of assorted mind-sets that are slowly modifying him - Whether it be for good or bad. Brocken's screams were loud and meant to send chills down the asylum guard's spines. He never hurt a single soul besides himself and he was being treated like a fugitive who's gone on the run several times beforehand. His knees were chafed from ramming them into the walls for hours upon hours and his nails had been chewed down to the bone. He breathing was heavy, yet, he was frozen from the inside out. He felt drowzy from the drugs they gave him but not enough to send him into a wild slumber. He was confident of his innocence and he remained solid in his campaign to fight off the consistent march of doctors that continuously ridiculed him. The Meeting. I've been pegged as 'The Clown' on countless occasions and I followed it up by dressing like one and acting like one to boot. It helped my addiction and it helped my porose, mental bullshit. It, unfortunately, put a damper on my career and really dug me deep into obscurity. I'm not letting that happen again, ever! All of you over-rated, washed up pieces of shit choose to believe that I'll return as the same joker in the deck as I was before can continue to do so because you're the first batch I'm going after. Obnoxious pricks like Jon Kano and talentless imbeciles like Trashcan Man will get the pressure applied to them first. I've been hated for far too long; it's my time to hate. 'Doctor, I found Mr. Brocken's home phone number and mailing address. Do you want both?' asked the secretary. 'No, just give me the number for now, Tracey. Thanks..' Jack replied as the secretary came into the office and handed the doctor a yellow 'Stick-It' note with the number on it. Jack took the number and stared at it for a few minutes, hesitating to pick up his phone to dial it. He didn't know what he could possibly say at two months of nothing. He figured that Nick would end up calling him in the long run to apologize, but it never happened. It was the awkwardness that forced him to stick the piece of paper to the brim of his PC monitor for another day. The secretary suddenly walked into the office with a folder of assignments under her arm. 'Sorry to barge into your bussiness sir but, are you going to contact him?' asked the secretary as she set the folder on Jack's desk. Jack shook his head and grabbed the folder, stuffing it in his desk drawer. 'I need that mailing address, Tracey. I won't be returning any calls this weekend.' the doctor said as he put on his brown trench coat and grabbed his maroon-colored umbrella. The secretary scurried back to her desk and grabbed the address and walked back over to the doctor, handing it to him. 'I'm going for a drive.' Jack stated as he walked past Tracey and exited the room, leaving the secretary with a heap of unanswered questions. Kaleidoscope Monologue. I started the introduction of my life gazing at the lambent colors and shapes that molded my room to be what it had been at the time. Of course, the cliche effect of demographics shadowed the room in shambles of Sesame Street and Barney designs flourishing. It was the simple, more facetious way of living and it has slowly dawned on me that there is, in fact, no more facetious ways of life. I've reached this realistic peak, where the oxygen is everso low, and, I can't seem to find a way down. As I'm clinging on for dear life, praying not to fall, I start to realize that I'm once again a wrestler. I start to realize that this is nothing short of a miracle and just short of a dream. I can feel myself falling and planting my feet in the middle of the ring with my eyes glued to two challengers. I don't fear you, TCM, and I don't fear you, Kano. You see, I've reached the peak of my fears and am now descending to pass them by once again. You two? Just pit-stops along my journey. Two slabs of meat leftover without a chance of survival. The time is here now here. Chevy Aveon. Soaring amongst the rain-soaked freeway as two windshield wipers propel to the left and to the right. Jack Klingman sits awkwardly with one hand placed beneath his buttocks to comfort himself from the cheaply made brown leather. Without a single twitch of the neck to look back, the doctor headed back to his home ground. He had no intention of returning to his current workplace and that became much more obvious as stacks of clothes, boxes of books and other assorted items were seen toppled amongst eachother in the backseat. He had regretted not being there for Nicholas in the two-month gap and he intended on making up for it. Meanwhile, Nicholas continued to lose his mind in the privacy of his basement. He could slowly feel a rivine cluster of demons pushing their way through his sternum. Anxious for it to all stop, Nicholas lifted with a bit more speed, exhaling while lifting and exhaling loudly while releasing the push. Tear drops of sweat drooled down Nicholas' temples as he closed his eyes and slowly started to count. 1, 2, 3 - Demons get out of my head. 4, 5, 6 - You can't control me, I'd rather die instead. 7, 8, 9 - Weak are my bones as I stare into dread. 10, 11, 12 - No longer lambent colors, all I see is red. The scene returns to show Dr. Klingman gripping onto his cup of coffee. A bright smile covers his face as the thought of a 'cured' Nicholas Brocken fantasizes him completely. 'Here I come, Nick. Here I co--SON OF A BITCH!' Klingman is cut off by the steaming heat of coffee spilt against his right hand. Quickly, Klingman sets the coffee back down only to look up and find himself cruising into a dead heap of traffic. SCREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECH! The smell of burnt tire is the final scent that enters Jack's nostrils as the Aveon slams into the back of a navy-blue Caravan, sending Jack's skull directly into the windshield, rippling the structure along its sides and creating a gaping hole in the center. The honks of car horns, cries of witnesses and twisted metal were all that was left. 'Hello?' answered Nicholas who picked up his cordless phone. 'Nick?' a faint and fluttered voice replied. 'Yes?' answered Nick who reached over and grabbed his wife-beater to throw on. 'Nick, it's Gwen. Listen, your old doctor...' Gwen jumped anxiously into the reason she called. Through a couple waves of hesitation, she let it out. 'He was in a car accident.' The words echoed through Nicholas' brain, like an alarm clock going off at 4 a.m. His doctor, and friend, was in danger and he felt absolutely helpless. 'Is he going to be all right?' asked Nicholas who an obvious tone of caution in his voice. '..Gwen, is he going to be all right?' Nicholas asked again, trying to force an answer out of her. 'He was paralyzed, Nick.' Nicholas closed his eyes and released his grip on the phone. For that moment, he weighed two tons and was back to his old self again. He could feel the insanity in his eyelids twitching and he felt a breeze of hell storm through his body.
