Date: 05/11/06 Saturday, May 6, 2006 The sun was shining brightly in through the windows and reflecting off the hard wood floor inside the downtown loft. The air was briskly moving outside causing the curtains to dance in their place against the baseboards. It was a mild day, quite a bit warmer than normal seasonal temperatures in the Winnipeg area. Sadly, owning a loft does not leave much room for outdoor activities and yard work that could be used as an excuse to enjoy the warmer weather. However, broom bristles were working overtime as they moved across the small cement patio that made up the balcony hanging a good few stories up from the busy streets of the downtown area. It had gotten quite dusty from the winter season and really, Killean just wanted a reason to get some fresh air. Cleaning off the balcony was one step into pulling his patio chairs and barbeque out of storage. It gave him something to do. It also gave him something to take his mind off all of the extra stress recently. Too many things were just popping up, adding to the already long list of items that had been stress causers. Killean found himself constantly thinking of how his hopeful visit to Mary’s had gone bad. Even though it was three months earlier, he still felt it to be a burden upon his soul. What disturbed him most though, was the fact that his friendship with Mary did not improve even though he set goals for himself pertaining to the visit. He wanted it to be a good meeting to get some things off his chest and make sure that the water was under the bridge. But after all was said and done, the water was flooding the bridge and his little tantrum after Anne decided to slap him did nothing to help the situation. Inside he felt ashamed, but on the outside he was too proud to admit that he was at least partially responsible. Killean set the broom down and began to pull his new barbeque through the sliding doors and onto the patio. One of the wheels got lodged in between the track for the sliding door and he was struggling to get it free. Charity returned from putting Dizzy down for her nap when she noticed Killean having problems with the wheel on the barbeque. She walked over and made a wise crack, but she didn’t bank on the reaction she received. “Problems with the new, expensive barbeque? Let me help…” “Look, just leave it be, I have it now.” His tone was gruff and his body language said it all. He yanked with enough force to move a boulder and the barbeque came rolling out onto the balcony with a bounce. Looking frustrated, Killean rolled the equipment into place at the end of the deck and went to get the chairs and small table that made up his balcony set. “Killean, what’s your problem here lately?” Charity was curious, maybe it was the added pressure from wrestling. Maybe it was the events that transpired the week before with Stacey. It really could be a mix of things going on, but she knew beyond any reasonable doubt that if it was Killean with her, he was not his former self. “I don’t know what you’re talking about. I’m fine.” He replied, moving the first chair into place and quickly wiping it down with a damp rag. “Oh yeah, that sounded very sincere. But there are no prizes for sarcasm in this discussion.” Charity now stood with her hands on her hips, looking a little more serious about the situation. “Then what was with that remark earlier huh? New and EXPENSIVE barbeque, making it sound like I spend too much money all of a sudden.” Killean moved the second chair into place, a little harder this time mind you but he still took the time to wipe it down, before moving back for the third chair. “That’s not what I meant. Just that you are always the one worrying about how much money we have and yet our bank account is floating much higher than most peoples. We are lucky when it comes down to our finances and yet you make it sound like we’re strapped for cash at any given moment. Now if it was quiet, meaning quiet enough to hear a pin drop, you would have heard a small crack. That small crack would have been from some straw because that last comment was the straw that broke the camels back. In this case though, the camel was Killean. Not saying that Killean is a camel but… oh you understand… anyways… “Is it such a bad thing to be concerned about the well being of my family?!” Killean threw the other two chairs into place, not bothering to wipe them down after. The stress had been building up for quite sometime now and it was only a matter of time until it overflowed his mind. “I’m just trying to ensure that we have everything we need that’s all!” He grabbed his head and plunked down in one of the chairs. He slid deep down into the seat, still rubbing his head. It effectively ended one of the shortest outbursts in history but it did its job in confirming that Killean was indeed, stressed out. You are still thinking about the camel comment aren’t you? Well give up because if you don’t understand it yet, then sadly you never will. Let’s move on… “Killean, I’m not saying anything judgmental, but you’ve been acting stressed out recently.” HA! You think? “You just haven’t been you, that’s all.” Charity came to sit down beside Killean in one of the other chairs. She held on to his arm as he put it on the arm rest. He was currently looking up into the sky, almost reaching out with his mind to find the one thing that would calm him. He didn’t want to be like this, but things have not been going his way. Sure he received some new endorsements linked to wrestling so that was a good thing. But it was one of the only good things attempting to even out the list of bad things. Unfortunately for him, there were just too many ups and downs at the moment. He drew in a deep breath, trying to cleanse himself of his resentful nature. “I know… I know I haven’t been myself. Things have just been on my mind that’s all. I’m sorry for taking this all out on you. You’ve always been there for me, I know that. It’s just that business is slowing down and I am still getting used to being on the road all the time again with PRIME. I was off for sometime and was enjoying the leisure time. Not to mention, the crap with Mary has been bugging me and same with Stacey. I mean, where has it gone right lately?” Last Wednesday was the perfect example. Being in the ring with James Farwell was no big deal really. Before the match, he even made a “deal” with Tyler Nelson to lay down for the Supreme Machine. Everyone knows how that turned out. But even so, in the end, something still happened to prevent the pin fall win for Sirrajin. Clyde Walkins, trying to save a little face interfered and awarded Killean a disqualification win. Perhaps it’s because Walkins knows he is going into Ultra Violence as one of the only underdog Universal Champions in PRIME history. Either way, the night didn’t exactly go the way Killean wanted it. Same way with the previous ReVolution. Killean knew exactly who Adam was. There were tapes from his previous stint in PRIME and Killean took a brief look at some. Fact is, Adam changed a few of his traits that brought him down the first time. This time, Killean stepped into the ring with a new Adam. He didn’t expect Adam to come out gunning and in the end he upset Nelson’s Choice. Not going to happen at Ultra Violence. “Regardless, everything is going to look up. Stacey is gone, the situation with Mary just needs more time, but Matt isn’t gonna help that one. Really, all you have to worry about from now through Ultra Violence is Clyde Walkins. Everyone else is banned from ringside, including Matt. How could it get any better than that?” Charity made a point. What happened in the past is the past. The time is now and it could not look any better. But Killean could still not help but be a tad pessimistic. “Maybe, but still…” Killean broke a tad bit of a smile, knowing he was beat. Charity playfully punched his arm. “Maybe but still? What the hell is that?” She laughs and jumps out of the chair. She walks to the railing of the balcony and looks down into the street. She turns back and smiles brightly at Killean. “Tell you what, I’ll show you what can go right tonight. Fire up that new barbeque. We’re gonna have a good dinner tonight!” She dragged Killean out of the chair and then ran inside to prepare. Killean shook his head and smiled. He could always count on Charity to talk some sense into him. That was something he loved about her ever since he met her. He opened the valve on the barbeque, turned the burners on and hit the igniter. The flame burst forth from the base of the grill and then Killean shut the lid. He wondered what Charity had planned because he could really use a good dinner to lift his spirits even more. That was another thing he loved about her, she knew how to cook. But it didn’t matter what she made, Killean would be doing the grilling because Charity had always rejected it. So if the barbeque was in the picture, Killean would be manning it. Later on, Killean was standing outside with a cold beer, turning the marinated chicken wings cooking over the low heat. If Sirrajin learned anything from watching grilling shows on TV when he was off last year, it was that low and slow was the absolute best way to grill. Charity stepped out through the sliding doors and onto the balcony with a tray in her hand. On the tray sat about four shrimp kebobs under a layer of plastic wrap. She set the tray down on the side shelf of the barbeque. Killean looked at her with a questioning look. “Yes, shrimp kebobs. I figured I would surprise you.” She smiled and put her arm around Sirrajin. “How are the wings coming along?” “How do they look?” Killean moved some of the wings to one side of the barbeque to make room for the shrimp that were sure to hit the grill soon. “Pretty darn tasty. That’s my man!” Charity hugged him close. Inside, Desiree began to cry from her crib. She was up from her nap and Charity was quick to react. “Guess who’s up.” She disappeared inside to tend to Desiree. Killean continued to work at the grill and sip on a beer. Charity made Killean’s daughter laugh the first time she met her and from there, their relationship has been great. Desiree helps Killean care for Dizzy when they are around the home like she is the legitimate mother. Normally, a single man with a child is not the greatest catch for a woman, but Charity wouldn’t have it any other way now. Charity made her way into the main room and just as she did, Killean stepped through the sliding doors from the balcony and made his way to the kitchen. He passed by Charity and as he did, he lightly pinched the cheek of his daughter, winning a giggle. He went to the kitchen and put the tray that carried the shrimp in the sink to clean it up. Charity made her way over to the couch and sat with Desiree. She cuddled her close and switched on the TV to the Treehouse channel. Ants in your Pants came on the screen and Desiree locked her eyes to the screen. Meanwhile, Killean finishes cleaning the tray but before he walks back out to the living room, he stops and looks over at Desiree and Charity. He couldn’t help but stare. Desiree had a large smile on her face as she watched one of her favorite shows. Charity was holding her close, talking sweet nothings into her ear about the show. He really couldn’t believe how well Desiree took to Charity when she first brought her around. Killean had met Charity while shopping in the mall downtown. She ended up helping Killean make a choice on a pair of dress shoes. Killean took the first step and asked Charity out to dinner. The rest, as they say, is history. He smiled as he continued to watch Charity and Desiree enjoy the TV program. Walking slowly, he continued to take in the sight and crossed his arms. He came to a stop directly behind Charity and since she was pre-occupied with keeping Desiree entertained, she didn’t notice Killean approach. She turned a little and frightened herself, still thinking that Killean was occupied in the kitchen. “Jesus, don’t sneak up on me like that.” He looked back at Killean, who had a smile on his face, watching his daughter who was in turn, watching the TV. He then turned his gaze to his lovely girlfriend who wondered what the smile was about. “What’s with the grin?” Killean came right to the back of the couch bent down and put his large powerful arms ever so gently around both Charity and Desiree. Desiree’s eyes however, never left the TV screen. “I’m happy.” Killean whispered to Charity. It was a big change from just a little while ago. “I’m content and I’m ever so grateful to you for everything. You’ve helped me with Desiree, you’ve helped me with my PRIME career and you’ve helped me realize just how important you are to me and to Dizzy. Most important, you’ve helped me realize how important you are to me.” Killean continued to hold her close, smelling a perfume scent coming from the raven haired beauty. He smiled a little more and then released both the girls. After staring at her for another minute, Charity finally broke the silence, pointing out to the balcony. “Is there supposed to be that much smoke coming from the barbeque?” Charity questioned. Killean looked out at the balcony and made a split second dash for the door. “DAMNIT!!” Killean grunted as he opened the lid and the smoke rolled into the air. Charity sat on the couch laughing and to rub it in, got Desiree laughing too. It was gonna be a long night. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ They were sitting across from each other, just the island table separating them with Desiree sitting right beside Charity. Killean was chewing on one of the slightly burnt chicken wings, but still savoring the flavor the marinade injected into the meat. Then she placed her fork onto her plate and fed Desiree some of her food, albeit not as flavorful as the chicken or shrimp but she enjoyed it anyhow. Did you ever watch me on TV before you met me?” Killean shoveled a forkful of salad into his mouth, smiling a little as he awaited Charity’s answer. Charity meanwhile, smirked herself, blushing a little as well. Killean found that interesting. “Sure I did. I mean I didn’t tune in to specifically watch you, but I just tuned in for the wrestling.” Charity explained, reminding Killean that she used to wrestle as well. “I just wanted to see what exactly I was missing by stopping my career before it could actually stop itself. Although, you have to understand that I had the most difficult time talking my dad into changing the cable account to have Playboy TV included.” She blushed again and giggled as Killean nodded his head in understanding, unable to hide another smile. He took a shrimp from his plate and ate it, proceeding to wipe his hands on the napkin. “I could see that. I could… but I need to know, did you watch any other programming on Playboy?” He laughed now. Charity just shook her head, saying the first thing that came to mind. “You gotta one track mind, you know that?” She shook her index finger teasingly at the Supreme Machine, which brought a shrug forth. “Is that gonna be a problem?” Killean teased back which brought another blush to Charity’s already rosy cheeks. Even though she acted tough around the cameras and seemed to be in incredible shape, she was still a woman. She was a woman who embarrassed easily and she never hesitated to show her full feelings. Anyone else would cringe at the abomination but Desiree seemed to enjoy the concoction. Killean sank his teeth into another chicken wing, delaying his answer. Well when I saw you in that tight shirt, my “brain” took over.” He made the quotations motion with his two hands when he said brain and Charity figured him out immediately and almost spit her wine all over the table when she laughed. She then choked a little and playfully slapped Killean on the arm again. “In all seriousness, I was just attracted to you. I was attracted to you physically and I thought you would be a fun person. You seemed quite outgoing.” Charity recovered and nodded, appreciating the honest answer that Killean finally provided. “Actually, if you wouldn’t have asked me, I would have asked you instead.” Charity stated between mouthfuls of food. She reached for the wine and with a glance, questioned if Killean wanted more. He still had some food in his mouth and just nodded in response. Charity refilled both the crystal wine glasses and pushed the now empty bottle to the side. She took a sip and continued on. “As it turned out, I was extremely attracted to you when you came in to the store. I was ready to go on my break and I put it off when you walked in. I recognized you right away.” Killean broke yet another smile. He was a different person around Charity. It was the same when he was with Kira, Stacey or Amy. Perhaps all he needed all along was just some companionship. “Ok, so you would have asked me out. I’m usually the one to take the initiative with those kinds of things. So the role reversal seemed to work quite well.” Killean concluded, placing his fork down on his now empty plate. He then took a sip of the wine and savored the taste. “Regrets?” “No, I don’t think so. If there was anything I was sorry about, it was spilling my drink into your lap at dinner.” She burst out with a hearty laugh, yet Killean just winced and remembered the embarrassing moment. “That drink was damn cold. I swear my crotch got frostbite!” Killean stated matter of factly. Charity meanwhile tried to give Desiree some more of her food, but Desiree was having none of it. Instead, tears welled up in her eyes and she began to lightly cry. Killean and Charity knew that tone all to well. It was time to get out the wipes and the clothespins. Look at the penguin and all the baby penguins sweetie.” Killean watched as Desiree pointed delightfully at the penguins. Her smile and enjoyment made him smile and for even a few short moments, made him feel like he didn’t have a care in the world. Have fun and be careful.” She said as she moved from the front door to make her way to Dizzy’s room. “I always am… and I love you.” Charity stopped and she couldn’t possibly hide the smile on her face as she openly blushed. Charity stood and leaned on the door frame of Desiree’s room, looking at the front door for a good minute until Desiree cried more to get her attention. It was a night to remember.
