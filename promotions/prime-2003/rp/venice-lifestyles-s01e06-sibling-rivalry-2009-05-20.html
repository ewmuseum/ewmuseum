Venice Lifestyles - S01E06 - Sibling Rivalry

May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: May 2009 May 24, 2009 – Boston “I still can't believe you said all that stuff last night.” Hailey was trying on some new clothes in a change room. The store was buzzing with all kinds of people looking for the next big deal hiding on the rack. Nitz meanwhile was checking himself out in a mirror, tilting a new hat he put on. It really wasn't him though and he placed it back on the table, reaching for another as the change room door opened. “Hey, technically I'm regressing. Last time I saw him I punched him. I see this as an improvement.” “What do you think of this?” Hailey said, standing outside the change room now, modeling a new pair of extra tight pants and a blouse that left very little to the imagination. Nitz turned around with another hat on his head. “Not bad for a waitress in a strip club.” “Oh yeah and your hat is just great... for a 45 year old woman!” Sure enough, Nitz is wearing a woman's hat on his head, complete with rhinestones and a silk band hanging over the purple rim. “It's not me?” He asks as Hailey smacked him on the arm. “I needed some new clothes to go to my next meeting in. It may be a little low for me but I noticed if I dress sexier, I get noticed and people listen to me.” “That's because they're too busy hiding their hard-on's to say anything.” Nitz lifts up a pair of g-string panties and displays them for everyone to see as he purses his lips, remembering the last woman he was with who wore undergarments like that. “Don't talk about your sister like that.” “Yeah, like I don't know you're loose for just the right kinda guy.” “Sure and you're a saint... now THOSE are you!” Hailey says eying the pair of lingerie in Nitz's hands. She walks back to the dressing room and closes the door. “So are we still going for lunch?” “If you want.” Nitz was thinking a lot about the incident with his brother just last night. He was right, he didn't punch his brother this time, but some very strong words were said. Chad really didn't have the chance to reply, except to point out the fact that Nitz is resenting him now while he's living in Venice. Chad had a point, but Nitz still had the overwhelming urge to punt his brother's testicles across the room. Hailey came out of the change room after she had changed back into her clothes. She was carrying the items she just tried on. “Have to go look at shoes before we go to eat.” Nitz sighed at the comment, dropping the second pair of underwear he decided to glance at. “Shoes? We going for lunch tomorrow?” |~o~| Nitz and Hailey grabbed his old table at Mikey's. The doors had just opened at 11:00 AM and no customers had come yet, but they were happy they showed up earlier because Mikey's tended to get a bit busy around the lunch hour. Glenda came out from the kitchen and looked as stunning as she did the day before. For the first time, Nitz really started to look at his friend's wife in an inappropriate manner. The hug she was about to deliver didn't help things either. “Hey guys, if this isn't a throwback I don't know what is. I can't remember the last time I saw both of you together here.” Glenda placed some cutlery on the table with some napkins and poured both of them some water. She then reached over and gave Nitz a hug around his neck, practically shoving her newly enhanced chest directly in his face. She turned and gave a hug to Hailey as well and Nitz couldn't help but notice that his sister was staring right down Glenda's shirt. She turned to Nitz and mouthed the words “Oh my god” as the hug broke. “Mikey will be out in a second to grab the order for you guys. Want something else to drink?” “No the waters are fine I think.” Nitz replied while Hailey nodded in agreement. Glenda made her way back towards the kitchen. Nitz just tried to hold back some laughter as his sister was blushing so hard she matched the red table cloth. “Looks like you need some fresh air.” He said, unable to hold back a slight giggle. “I need a damn cigarette.” Her comment certainly broke the ice and won laughter from both of them. “Does she not realize that she's basically stuffing them up your nose? I know they're new and all but wow.” “I really don't think she cares. I learned something about them yesterday that explains a lot.” “What's that?” “They're swingers now. She likes the men and the women.” “Bullshit! Glenda?!” “That's exactly what I said to Mikey.” Almost if on cue, Mikey emerged from the kitchen with a couple of bowls. He approached the table with a smile, not having seen the two Donnelly siblings together in his restaurant for a long time. He placed the bowls on the table and took their menus, ripping Nitz's right out of his hands. “You don't need menus.” He said, placing the individual bowls in front of them. “How do you fuckin' know that?” “Name me one damn time you've ever walked in here and ordered something besides the usual.” Silence. “Exactly. Enjoy!” He smiled again, taking the menus away from the table. “So what else are you gonna do? How long are you staying for?” “Couple more days.” Nitz took a cracker out of it's wrapper and dipped it in the chowder, taking a bite. “I really don't know. I wasn't thinking about much more than seeing Mom, Dad and Chad and worrying about how that would go. I had no idea Mom and Dad would be like the fuckin' twilight zone so I guess I could've listened to you and not worried about them. But Chad...” “I believe you know him as asshole.” “Ha ha. Very funny. He is an asshole. How could he compare me being in Venice to his abandonment of everyone here. He left Rachelle high and dry and he didn't care how she felt. Mom was going through the worst of her problems and Dad was just starting his boozing.” “You don't have to explain it to me Nicky. I know how he can be. He's just looking for a way to turn this around on someone else so he can feel better about the mistakes he made.” As she finished speaking, she took a quick drink of water before going back for her spoon. “There isn't gonna be any talking sense into him until he realizes he's wrong and I dunno if that'll ever happen.” “It's bullshit is what it is. The damn guy thinks she can just walk around like normal, like he did nothing wrong and whenever I see him and open my mouth, he can't possibly think he did anything wrong. He's like a child.” “But aren't you being like a child by not letting it go and moving on.” “I...” Try as he could, he didn't know how to respond to that remark. On the surface and perhaps not too deep inside all he wanted was for Chad to pay for what he'd done in the past, to suffer. But deep down he knew that he would have to be the one to grow up and let it go, even though he was the little brother. Even though he knew it, his pride was not agreeing. Letting everything go would not be easy at all since his brother pushed every button of his, so he tried to hide it where no one could see it. “Don't give me that Hail. The guy did wrong and he needs to lie in the bed he's made.” “But everyone else has let it go, really Mom and Dad weren't all that upset at all. I was mostly siding with you but Chad's our brother. We have to forgive and forget.” “I don't have to do shit and now I'm done talking about it!” The tone in his voice indicated he was serious and Hailey backed off, deeply sighing and dropping her spoon in exchange for a napkin to wipe the corners of her mouth. Mikey approached just a few seconds later and sat down beside Hailey. He had a glass of cola with him and took a large drink. “OK so how long you really gonna be here for before returning to the pampered lifestyle of Venice ya prissy cocksucker? Must be hard living a hop skip and a jump away from the best beaches in the country.” “He's got a few of the women under wraps apparently from what he told me.” Hailey laughs a little with Mikey while Nitz scoffs at the comment. “I told you about one Hail... and yeah, living by the beach is alright but I'm not used to the weather. Too humid sometimes.” “Oh boo hoo. Poor baby. Sunshine and lollipops in supermodels mouths and you complain about the weather. When did you become such a pansy?” “Give it up Mikey. I know you're just busting my balls.” “Yeah I am. So what's the deal with Chad? See him yet?” “Loaded question Mikey.” Hailey replied, swallowing another spoonful right before. “There was a war of words. Actually, more like a war of Nicky's words. He never actually let Chad talk much at all. Plus there was the compliment of 'asshole' you threw his way.” “Aww buddy you broke out the cursing. What's the matter with you man? This really still getting to you after all these years?” “I thought I said I was done talking about this.” He pushed his bowl away and got up from the table in a huff. Mikey and Hailey leaned back. “Struck a nerve did I?” “Yeah.” Hailey said after a sip of water. “Chad is his kryptonite. Has been ever since he took off. I'm kinda indifferent and Mom and Dad really aren't concerned. Even Rachelle let it go even though she was the one left alone. But Nicky just won't let it go. Now it's just two people making family get togethers extremely rough.” “Yeah, tell that to hot head over there.” |~o~| Later in the night, Hailey and Nitz got off the elevator on route to her apartment. He had decided earlier in the day to cut the hotel he was staying in loose and took her up on the offer to stay with her. He wasn't planning on playing his usual games with the ladies so he figured it would be alright to save a few dollars. As they were walking the halls, their footsteps made the faintest of sounds on the lightly carpeted floor, but it was nothing compared to the sounds coming from down the hall behind them in the stairwell. “Must be some silly drunks forgetting the elevators worked. Who else would walk 21 floors up?” Ash she said it, the door burst open and they both turned to look out of curiosity. They really wished they didn't however as a drunken man stumbled from the cold, uninviting stairwell. “Heyyyyyyyy... 21st floor or is this 12... I swear it only said 12.” What was worse is they recognized the man practically falling face first into the walls. “Yo bro! Yo sis! Wait up, I had to get a cab to follow youuuuuuuu!” “Oh god.” Hailey ran up the hall to attempt to corral her other brother as Nitz just stood against the wall with his arms crossed, shaking his head. He couldn't believe that he was really going through this. Chad had a bottle in his hand and raised it up to his lips. Hailey jogged to him and ripped the now practically empty container from his hand winning a moan of disapproval from her intoxicated brother. “Hey, whaddya do that for?” Hailey grabbed him by the arms and dragged him over to her door and inserted the key, opening it quickly as possible to prevent Chad from disturbing the neighbors in her rather quiet building much more. All three Donnelly siblings stepped inside and Nitz was the last one, shutting the door and locking the deadbolt, figuring that a completely inebriated Chad was not hitting the road before sunrise. “Fuckin' idiot.” “Nicky! Don't.” Hailey led Chad over to the couch, taking his coat off and laying him on the couch. He immediately sat back upright, albeit awkwardly trying to fight the urge of his sister. She ran off to grab something from the kitchen to sober him up. “Maaaaaaaaaan, when you gonna gimme a god damn break?!” His comments were directed straight towards his younger brother and Nitz just let the comments slide right off his back. “I'm not listening to this.” “Ohhhhh yes you are... you don't get to control every conversation you're in. You talked my face off yesterday. Now baby bro you're gonna listen to me.” “Oh really?” “Yep...” Chad paused, looking at the hardwood floors for a second, mesmerized by the separations in the boards. He almost looked like he lost his mind and something shiny took his attention. “Well?” “Huh? Oh... I forgot.” Chad replied, lying back down on the couch. “Yeah, thought so. Just another waste of time.” Nitz shook his head again in frustration and turned to walk away but he was caught off guard by some quick deep breaths coming from the couch. He turned back and noticed a few tears rolling down the cheeks of his brother. “I just wanna have a normal damn life.” He started as Hailey emerged from the kitchen with a cup of instant coffee. It was awful but it was the fastest sobering beverage she had access to. She paused to listen, surprised to hear the weeping. “I've been living the last few years with guilt for leaving. There's no excuse. You left cause you could, cause Mom and Dad were doing much better. I left when things were at their worst, avoiding everything and... uhhhh...” He stopped, trying to sit up again as Hailey sat beside him, allowing him to lean on her. The smell of sour alcohol coming from him almost turned her stomach, signifying that he must have already thrown up his guts. Hopefully it took place outside the building and not in the stairwell. She left the plastic mug just below his face and he cupped it with his two hands and brought it to his mouth, taking a large sip. He cringed as it probably burned his tongue a little. “... man, what do I gotta do? I've said I'm sorry.” “No you fuckin' haven't!” “Nicky... no.” Hailey looked at him with disdain, trying to calm the situation. She was genuinely concerned with Chad. “Yeah Nicky, you heard her. I just want things back to the way they were. It's been years... it's...” He stopped again, this time to sob into his sister's shoulder. He looked up at Nitz who was stone cold frozen, unable to remove his stare. “I want my little brother back. I wanna play toy cars again.” His kid like comment threw his siblings off guard as he coughed a little after pouring more coffee in his mouth. He tried to inhale while drinking. Not a good combo. Hailey removed the mug of coffee and turned to lay her brother back down again, his eyes half closed as he was beginning to doze off before his head hit the pillow. “Nicky, say something.” He was stoic, not showing any emotion but his mind was running a million miles a minute. Chad opened up to him, even though at the time he had more alcohol than blood running a course through his body. But it was a valid display of regret and he couldn't ignore it. He just really didn't think it would ever come down to this. He immediately flashed back to earlier in the day, when Hailey herself said he'd never break out of it. But he did and that's what really counted. Nitz was just left in shock, turning to make his way towards the spare bedroom. Hailey was tending to her other brother and heard the footsteps of the youngest Donnelly getting further and further away. “Nicky!” A detour back into the living room was not in the cards. Nitz was actually scared, not of his brother, but more because his brother came clean before he had he chance to let it go like any adult should. He was going to let it go and was looking for the right time to do it, but this ambush was something totally out of left field. If it was anyone else, he'd have thought he did it on purpose and would have hated him for it. He just couldn't give Chad that much credit. It was enough he was giving him credit for coming clean even, albeit in a drinkers haze. It still counted. And that's what mattered. It did count and now Chad had another point up on his little brother. He could only do one thing to even the score. He closed the door and sat down on the corner of the bed, holding his face in his hands, sighing loud enough that his siblings would probably hear him. He threw himself back on the bed, his hands now resting over his face. He spat as he spoke. “Well fuck me.”
