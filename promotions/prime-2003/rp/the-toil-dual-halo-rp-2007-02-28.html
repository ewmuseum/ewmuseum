The Toil (Dual Halo RP)

28 February 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 28/2/07 Seven O’Clock Wake up and refresh mind with splashes of water to my face in the bathroom. Take a good look at it and prune my beard carefully. The tighter the better as it won’t get as bedraggled as I expected it to. Inspect my general body and discover a few minor imperfections that might hinder the process. There can be no room for error today as even one slip will mean it’ll all be over in a split second. I begin to wrap up my two knees in strapping, knowing that they’ll need it. The braces are next to provide even more support. It should be enough but at the same time I worry. I take one last good look at my body. Scarring and stitching litter the backs of my legs and around my arms. A lifetime of punishment and battering has taken its toll on this body but it’s not done yet. Some people make excuses for retirement but I make none. There is no excuse for me in reality; I simple do what I have to do. I pull on my uniform and focus on it to make sure that its clean and pristine. It must be if I am to make the maximum impression on the others. Life goes on, another day in the grind. Nine O’Clock Traffic is heavy and the gritty smog coming from the oil gulping machines fills the vicinity. I drink it all in with my nostrils flaring at the scent. Its blooming smell envelopes me leaving me no choice but to take it even with my own desired choice. It’s not like I’m doing anything else productive in the meantime. A woman to my left is busy checking her make up in the mirror, pouting her lips upwards in a ‘kiss me I’m a whore’ kinda way. She’s filling those pursed lips with bloody rouge, going for the scarlet look. The dangerous and seducing method to try and flirt her way through life it seems. She’s taking so much time to do it that the lights have turned green for her turn off the intersection. A couple of horn beeps are to be heard behind but once she flutters those eyes in her main mirror the beeps peter out. It seems some things in life always stay the same. Much like my traffic lights. Another car to my right catches the eye. In it are a couple, both looking straight ahead, studying the signals and waiting for the eventual changing of colour. The man sits patiently with his two hands on the wheel, balding head sweating from the heat of the entire affair. The woman meanwhile seems to be purring at his side, whispering sweet nothings. That is until he smacks her full force in the face. The look of shock on one of the drivers behind the car is visible and they seem to be getting out of their car. I stay motionless and watch, happy to let the entire ordeal play out in front of me. It’s none of my business and it won’t stop me from getting to my eventual destination. That man who has stepped out of the car is brash and young, almost too young to be driving the car he has, a powerful Chevrolet. His rapping on the window makes it come across like some traffic warden who wants to give the wife beater a parking ticket. Our traffic warden gets spit right between his eyes for his efforts. From the woman nonetheless. Words are shared between all three. At one point it seems there’s a misunderstanding being played out, but the flying fist of traffic warden soon ends the speaking. Wife beater is dragged out of the car and dumped unceremoniously on the pavement. The woman is screaming out of the window as the two go at it on the hard shoulder of the road. People in their cars notice; some get out and attempt to help, others, like myself simply ignore it all and wait for the signals. The green light finally comes and I leave our troubled threesome in the dust. Twelve O’Clock Quite tired right now from the early exertions of the day. With the ticking of the clock it means that I’m able to get a bit of a breather from the hard graft. Things haven’t gone exactly as they should have due to some minor niggles in the project. For a start, there’s the contractors, headed by a man with a receding hairline that only a middle aged man would be proud of. His vain attempts at stubble are haphazard at best and overall he looks like he’s trying, desperately trying to connect with a generation two times past his own. His constant referrals to conquests of women and phallic jokes are tiresome to say the least. As for his associates…well, let’s just say there’s a guy who can’t stop winking in the most awkward way possible, another one who’s obsessed with red and the last one is again, like the woman in traffic, too busy admiring herself to take much notice of the actual problem at hand. ‘We simply can’t let you impose this monstrosity next to the building that has been put in place,’ he says, sneering at the sheer fact he managed to wrangle monstrosity into the sentence. A hip thrust is quickly following the word. I respond with the fact that it’s just a gym, something that’s going to have so much use for the general populace, a means with which they can better themselves. ‘Why would they need a gym when they’ve got us to look after them with our tools?’ he laps with his tongue, looking at his associates and giving buddy fists to each and every one bar the woman who simply clucks her tongue derisively. He returns to look at me. ‘I don’t care, this place needs to be shown the proper way, and if I have to dismantle your structure along the way, then so be it.’ My words are sharper and venomous and he reels slightly like a verified dandy would in the older times. His hands reach up in dismay but he does not argue. He simply turns on his heels in a huff, clicking his fingers to get the rest to follow. So much for that gang of chumps but that hadn’t been the only problem of the day. We finally ripped out the section of the building where there was going to be my own private office and exercise…to find an infestation of the kind that I do not tolerate. As I tossed out the man all he asked for was something to cover his grossly thick, hairy body and the chance to be helpful. Just like the rest of his kind, he was tossed to the wayside. Still, the work was progressing and the obstacles to the completion of the gym were being tackled head on, one at a time and finally I was able to get a little bit of a break from it all. The clock in the background was ticking down all the time however and it was a conscious reminder that the job wasn’t done and it wouldn’t be done for sometime. Got to conserve your energy it tells you. Got to act alert and be aware of anything blindsiding you, it tells you. Got to remember and manage that time as best I can. These legs aren’t as limber and potent as they used to be. Two O’Clock The burn is starting to come, erupting from my legs and knees and travelling all the way upwards. I don’t give in though, preferring to gut it out as is always my wont. The task was progressing slowly, oh so slowly and the rest that I had received only so recently was little help. In fact it had more likely lulled me into a false sense of security. More obstructions had shown up, the most annoying being that of the civil servant who just couldn’t resist in poking his nose where he shouldn’t have. Apparently, or so he claimed, I was rebuilding on a particular zone of land that was purely for residential and not recreational. The man was timid and lacking the balls to really say what he wanted to. He was constantly pushing the bridge of his glasses from the tip of his nose back up, snorting as he did so which only added to the irritancy of the situation. I sent him away with words, threatening legal action but you get the sense that he would be back. Civil servants always come back. Seven O’Clock Ah the journey home, the last hurdle which needed to be traversed and I would have completed my ordeal. One last problem; the road was being blocked again by everyone in their blasted cars. Despite the possible problem I would be able to surmount just like I knew I always could. As I passed the road with which the wife beater and the traffic warden had scuffled I could see bits of blood and teeth lying there carelessly, like it had always been an aesthetic feature of the pavement. I didn’t get much further though as the blocking cars soon piled up. It was so close and I could see it; just a simple turn to the path that I needed. But there were too many people in the way and most of all I had to cross a couple of lanes to get there. And then in front of me I saw a man who was signalling to let me by. His tanned skin and funky looking moustache immediately made him stand out in my vision. He nodded in appreciation of my situation as I passed him by and he let me on through to the desired path. He understood…he understood. It wasn’t far from there. Nine O’Clock Drag myself back to the bathroom and splash water on my face. Take a good look. The exhaustion from the day had finally caught up with me and I had sat mindlessly in front of the television for some time, letting the emotions sink in. Tiring and draining were the two most apt words for the day, but then again, so were all my days. That’s what happens when you give your all to everything that you focus on. And this wasn’t even something I was particularly passionate about. Wrestling on the other hand…well, they’ll understand soon enough. When you give this much to something that isn’t that important, how dangerous do you think I’ll be in the ring? They’ll find out soon enough.
