Like The Angel (If Only)

11 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 11, 2005 Chapter One: And They Say Knowing is Half The Battle...They turn the lights down low,In shadows... hiding from the worldAnd only coming out when it gets cold. A faux-leather boot stamped the floor. ''I just came off of that four way! HOW am I supposed to fight her, let alone both of them?!'' Neon hairs clung to sweat beads on a forehead filled with faint scars. ''It isn't policy to change on the whims of the competitors. The card has already been announced...'' A blue eye narrowed in rage... ''And it's absurd!!'' Its brown partner followed suit. The last fifteen minutes had been a vicious sparring match against various members of PRIME's head office. The next words heard, however, won the battle... ''Miss Wolfenden's already agreed, and she just came off of a main event pay-per-view match.'' Was that necessary? On the part of a company that wanted one of its stars to get to work, of course. If you were Aimz, though, it was a deathwish on the part of management. Her wrath would be dealt on either a secretary or her opponents...but the voice over the phone seemed so much closer and more inviting to beat, given the news it was delivering. The irate redhead roared her usual slur of unecessary obscenities, and her curses weren't the only things flying through the air. ''Jesus FUCKING Christ!'' Phone? Meet wall. Wall? Here comes fist. Head? This won't hurt you... ''Whoa whoa WHOA! Amy!'' ...Much. Now her hair was sticky with her crimson essence, mixing with the junky comprise of sweat to make the impact wounds burn. The blank and metallic scent of the room faded alongside her eyesight for that one fleeting moment. A splash of colors so bright, they were indistinguishable filled the space behind her eyelids. She took a step back, back...down. Her brother slid a hand under each of her arms and propped her up against the wall of plaster she'd nearly demolished with her phone, hand then head moments before. As her eyes slipped open again, the tiny redhead cast an unconscious touch to her throbbing forehead and felt the first icy trickle of her blood that only served in furthering the disgustingly sterile smell of the foreign space. This was Paul's home, not her's. It was clean - obsessively so - and free of anything that would possibly marr the perfect, white walls and polished hardwood floors... except, of course, the redheaded bull that had spent the night in her brother's China Shop. Now one wall of the immaculate living room was dented and shattered in the shape of a hard-headed Campbell. Not his head, though... chaos in this world was almost always courtesy of Paul's little half-sister, Amelia. Both siblings were wracked with Obsessive Compulsive Personality Disorder. On one hand, Paul was incredibly tidy and neat. Amy? Organized chaos. As long as she knew the pieces to a puzzle, it didn't matter what an outside perspective brought. At this point, though, her life's puzzle was ridiculously jumbled to a point where she couldn't have isolated a piece if her existance depended on it. She'd stopped sleeping three days ago, eating was a trial in and of itself... drugs? He didn't dare to ask. This was his sister lower than he'd ever seen her - hugging her knees and sniffling back tears on his now plaster-powdered living room floor. She never cried, not in front of anybody... this was closer to tears than he'd seen her since she was five. ''Ames... just settle down.'' His tone carried twenty two years of supporting his youngest sister and being more like this girl who - through all of her trials - seemed like his absolute opposite than any of their siblings. They were two children among eight, but even Amy's 16 year old brother was ten times as stable as she could dream of being. For whatever reason, and despite her upbringing, she was the loose cannon of the family. There was always one... but she took it to levels beyond that of most young Canadians. When her siblings were bringing in six figure salaries, she was spending every penny of a much more modest income on alcohol and hard drugs, mixing in the occasional DVD or vintage Minor Threat vinyl. The factor that took it over the top? Their income was something to be proud of in their hometown, while hers was... well... fighting sweaty, steroid-injected men; A step down from porn-stardom, only with more sweat and uglier women. ''For what? I'd rather wear every inch of my fucking body down. Maybe then I could sleep. Maybe then...'' Paul had knelt down and rested a reassuring hand on her shoulder - one Amy jerked away from. ''Don't bother.'' ''I'm your brother. I don't sit by and watch strangers ruin themselves, why would I do it for my sister? Listen, I've got someo--'' ''I'm not ruining myself, I'm determined... it doesn't matter what you've got for me.'' ''You're not determined. You're addicted.'' That second voice rang out and Amy instantly inched away from her brother, staring daggers straight through him. He thought THIS would help? ''Jeph,'' she spoke shakily to the second presence without removing her eyes from her kin, ''Leave.'' Paul dared to bring him between their sibling bond? His eyes were pleading, but Amy wasn't giving an inch. She stood up swiftly and stood on guard in the center of the living room as Jephrey Howe walked into it. He'd abandoned her to suffer through herself days ago, and now her own brother brought him back to the surface?! Speaking of that brother, he was slipping out of the room. His words were a simple ''You two need to talk. I'll be in the office.'' ''We don--'' Before Amy could argue, he'd left she and Jeph - whose confident smirk was notably absent - alone. Alone and together. Amy's eyes rolled, ''Don't need this. Not now.'' ''I'm sorry,'' Jeph began, entirely ignoring her, ''I shouldn't have... shouldn't have just left like that. Hell, I shouldn't have crawled back like this... it's just that you're so frustrating, Ames. Paul called me because without someone, he knows you're only running on your addictions - whether that's coke or besting Karina Wolfenden. You were killing yourself even WITH me, I can't imagine being without...'' ''I don't need you,'' came the bold interjection, ''Not now... not at all.'' ''Because I'm not an obsession, Amy. You didn't have me long enough to get addicted.'' Campbell's teeth ground against one another. She knew exactly what he was getting at, but wondered if he dared to go there... ''What's gonna happen when you can't afford drugs?'' ...Needless to say, Jeph considered his daring a finer attribute. Unlike her in-ring opponents, though, he wouldn't smile at her disdain. He didn't wish ill upon her. ''What happens when Karina ends up beating you? You're trying to scrape up a purpose in life by dedicating it to the pursuit of these obsessions, your addictions...'' Amy's fists were balling. Her eyes grew glassy from the building tension, but she was standing her ground. This wasn't going to happen all over again, and she could do things on her own. She didn't need these... these distractions, so she tried to ignore him. When she collapsed onto the couch in her own frustration, however, it didn't do a thing to back Jeph off. No matter how tired Amy grew, he wasn't going to relent. ''Well?'' his words prodded through the silence and caused the little redhead to groan in her seat, ''What happens when they're all used up and you can't find the next fix to tide you over?'' His words struck her, but she wasn't about to let that show. Jeph knew it anyway. He even knew what she was about to do...and watched as she walked out the door, mouthing ''You're too far off, Jeph. You have no idea, and I'd better not find you here when I get back.''. When Paul heard the door slam, fearing Jeph's head might've been in it thanks to Amy, he ran out only to find his little sister gone. ''What the hell?!'' ''She won't be out there long.'' Amy's brother made no bones about showing his panic. ''I'm her brother, man... and I don't think she's gonna be back anytime soon.'' Jeph gestured to the white couch where fire-printed leather and a wad of money laid where Amy had been sitting. ''Can't do much without her wallet, can she?'' She'd be back. Chapter Two: No TransitoryThe seas part when they hit the floor,the voices carry on and out the doorand everything you touch turns into gold...''Campbell! The usual?'' ''Double it.'' They stood in the corner of a cut-off alleyway as the little redhead, growing cold in only her tanktop, made her order quickly. This was hardly a dinner transaction, though. A deathly skinny, pale young man in a black sweater with a coat overtop held himself tight to the walls to escape the chilled breeze as he pulled two baggies from his hoodie's inner pocket. He grinned at Amy underneath distant eyes and went to place the clump-filled bags in her eagerly waiting grip, but hesitated a moment when he saw no other action from her. ''Money?'' ''Ha! Almost forgot. Sorry, dude...just a se--FUCK!'' She'd reached nonchalantly into the back pocket of her cargo pants, fully expecting to wrap her shakey fingers around a tied sum of bills... but her hand scraped up nothing but lint. ''I forgot it at the apartment. Shit... I can't go back there, man.'' Her associate frowned, ''I love ya, girl... but this shit stays with me until I see money. I've gotta live, hon.'' ''What can you comp me, then?'' ''I've got a shot left. That ain't my bag today, so if you want...'' It was Amy's turn to frown. Injection, with her fear of needles? No thanks. ''I don't touch that shit.'' ''Then I've got nothing for ya. Sorry, Ames...'' Nothing? Impossible! There was nothing left in her bag. NOTHING. She'd either fill up tonight, or go through the hellish symptoms of withdrawl and weakness just before a match against Wolfenden and Williamson... This was a junkie's dilemma. One she didn't want to think about, but her friend saw that she was. ''It don't hurt or anythin','' he offered, ''Great trip, too.'' She backed against the wall and pressed her scalp into it, cringing for even CONSIDERING what she was about to do... ''Alright. But...you'll do it for me, right?'' ''I du--'' he was prepared to refuse, but saw her nervous gaze. It was like the hardcore druggie suddenly turned back into the little girl he'd gone to elementary school with. It was VERY odd to see the good that remained in Amelia Campbell... ''Fine. Sit down, gimmie your arm.'' He took his belt off and knelt down next to the little redhead, who'd quickly obeyed the orders she was given. One twist around her right arm was enough to bring about a visible vein as he pulled the plastic tip off of the syringe produced from his pocket. Amy was making no bones about her fear; She shook, and one look up made it clear that here eyes could only be pried open by a jackhammer. The needlepoint stung when it broke the wall of the plugged vein, but there wasn't a single recognizable feeling beyond that moment... Halifax was hardly heaven, and she was no angel... but before the drug set in, Amy needed to shed a few tears for herself. Chapter Three: Here's To Lives Lost...A beating heart and a microphone,a ticking clock in an empty home still tells of these times so long ago,and even though I've come so far I knowI've got so far to go and any day now, I'll explode. ''You guys have no FUCKING clue!'' Jeph and Paul had been watching TV rather peacefully when the flurry of red hair and tattoos filled their sight. It was screaming at them, but it sure as hell wasn't the girl who'd left hours before... ''A......Amy? Wha--'' ''NO CLUE. Do you know what it's like? Being this weak? HUH?!'' Her brother stood to catch her as she dropped to her knees, but Jeph simply stood. She didn't need help... she needed to be put out of her misery. ''Amy!'' screamed Paul, shaking her by the shoulders, ''What the hell did you do?!'' ''Fuck off. You and you...ALL OF YOU!'' she thrashed, ''Out of my way!'' The redhead didn't realize she wasn't taking the confident strides she imagined, but crawling and scraping her way across the floor. ''Karina fuckin' Wolfenden, man...KARINA! Where the fuck am I gonna get the juice to take that fight now?! I can't lose this! I CAN'T LOSE WHAT I NEED TO GO ON. If I'm sick when I fight her, what the fuck happens? I can't win! I'LL LOOK LIKE......FUCK!'' Jeph finally chimed in with a calmer demeanor than her brother. ''Amy, listen...'' He, too, came to his knees in front of her. ''You're not making enough sense.'' Howe looked up at Paul, who'd backed away to let him cradle his... ex girlfriend? ''I'm gonna take her home.'' ''The hell you are!'' the elder Campbell growled, ''She needs a hospital!'' ''What she needs is a few hours of sleep and some vitamins. Please, Paul...I've dealt with this before.'' The men regarded one another. Paul had known Jeph since he and Amy had been toddlers... maybe it wasn't such a bad idea for her to be in his care? ''Alright,'' he tossed Jeph a set of keys, ''Take my car, and call me when she's okay. If ANYTHING happens...'' Jeph spoke plainly with the acknowledgment ''I know'', standing up with the tiny redhead cradled in his arms as she rambled - her screams now reduced to tired mumbling that neither man cared to discern past the occasionally clear reference to ''Kari...fucking bitch...Wolf...''. As he carried her down the stairwell, Jeph growled to Amy, who was quickly growing unconscious, words she shouldn't forget... ''You can't live on this alone.'' You can't feed yourself with addiction. You can't be Amy Campbell, not as she is. Chapter Four: Independence Day, morning afterAnd each and everyday will lead into tomorrowand tomorrow brings one less day without youBut don't wait up, just leave the light on'Cause all the roads that I might take will all one day lead back to you... Aimz sipped at her water and scratched at the sore, crusty bruise in the crook of her elbow while her former flame stared plainly at her. He expected an explaination... one he wasn't getting. Amy glanced up from her position sitting on the mattress to make eye contact with him, noting that high and mighty expression. ''Don't pull this shit.'' ''Don't tell me what to do. Don't tell me anything,'' he bellowed, ''...not when I can see your arm so plainly.'' She looked down to glance at the tiny bloodmark and shrugged, ''So? It was once.'' ''...And you could have died. What if that needle was tainted?'' No answer. Amy dipped her head down towards her waterglass again. Jeph, on the other hand, roared. ''Are you THAT fucking stupid, Amelia?! You're tossing everything away. After yesterday, I'd be surprised if your brother doesn't force you into a six month detox! What then? Where's your wrestling? Where's Karina Wolfenden? Where's the money for all the demons you hide behind?!'' Amy cringed when his voice raised again, hoping he hadn't seen. She remained staring at the sheets and her water. ''AMY. Are you listening to this?! I told you once that I wouldn't just stand here and watch you ignore everybody but yourself. I TOLD YOU I WASN'T GOING TO WATCH YOU SLIP LIKE THIS. I came back only to be told to get the fuck away from you, and I saved your ass without thanks. Doesn't that tell you something?'' he screamed, ''People actually care about you. God only knows why, but they do. You have family. If only your PARENTS knew this shit...knew how you live. What would you do then? Just do a few lines and think of ways to bounce off of some fuckin' stupid plastic ropes in PRIME?'' No answer. ''Listen to me!'' Tears welled in Amy's bloodshot eyes. '''Stupid plastic ropes'?'' she mewled, ''You think everything I do is that petty?'' ''I think you're so obsessed with success and overstepping some lanky bitch that you're destroying yourself blindly to attain it.'' Now, Amy was the one roaring. She jumped off of the bed she'd slept in as quickly as she could without falling over from the faint after-effects of yesterday's heroin luncheon. With a few swift steps, she was face-to-face with Jeph. ''Go live your own life,'' came the statement, ''I just needed a last go at it so I have the energy for this match...and you will NOT take that away from me.'' ''You've said that before every match you've had in that fucking place.'' ''And I mean it this time,'' she pointed to the doorway, ''Now OUT.'' Jeph snarled, ''You know what? Kill yourself. Forget just sniffing lines, how 'bout you shoot up until your fuckin' eyes bleed? Not my problem, is it? That's right. I'm GONE.'' He stormed to the door, but Amy put in a final word before it slammed... ''Stay gone this time.'' With that over again, she sulked back into the mattress. The familliar whirring of her iPod's harddrive loading a playlist was ever-present, then music filled Amy's ears. One more time... that's all she needed. Just drug once more. You can't go without that energy. Match to match, back to back...speed to crack. Whatever you need, you've got to get if you intend on winning this. It's pathetic that you've been shot, stabbed, pregnant and divorced - some simultaneously - yet a bag of white dust is kicking your ass. It was taking hold again. That scratching. Aimz hadn't drugged in at least twelve hours now, and the K-Wolf was dealing bicycle kicks straight to her frontal lobe from the inside... She wanted to be so alone. She wanted to have that solidarity to concentrate on her fighting. She needed to stay on top. She needed... 'I can take anything she can have. Deville? Like that's any problem. Titles? Fine. Matches? She doesn't know what's coming...not so long as I keep from crashing before this fight. I'll stop drugging after that...I need to promise myself. I will. Just this one last time...' ...She needed to wonder what path to take, and if all of this self-destruction was really worth the payoff. How do you kill the voice of craving if you can't find the source? Is it in your brain or bones? Physical or mental... What part of her needed these needles and lines to put up a fight? She guessed it was the same piece that wanted - needed - to beat Karina Wolfenden once more. Just one last time. 'Please. Once more. My body can't fail me now...' Like the angel you are, you laugh, creating a lightness in my chestYour eyes, they penetrate meYour answer's always maybeYou never cease to amaze meThat's when I got up and left.
