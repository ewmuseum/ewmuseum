Date: Distant Past If asked to tell your life story, where would one begin? I suppose I could talk of how I was probably conceived thanks to a malfunctioned piece of rubber and an all-too enticing backseat of a car. Would you like to know that? Or maybe, I’d go into some psycho-babble bullshit about not being loved enough as a child. I could preach how my parents being too busy to nourish my pre-adolescent roots so I would blossom into a model citizen, one worthy of recognition in the halls of scholars. Would you like to know that? Or maybe you’d like to know that I’m serving a consecutive life sentence for the double homicide of my parents. I cut open their bellies with rusted hack knife, pulling out their warm intestines in front of their stone stark faces. How I watched the blood stained tears trail down their faces as I wrapped their guts around their necks and kicked the chairs out from underneath their feet? Should I tell you how I watched them swing slowly back and forth until their legs began to twitch and spasm until that too died? Would you like to know that? Ok, I lied about the grotesque killing…but the rest is the truth, scout’s honor. The fact is this world feeds on controversy. I could tell you my life story but it wouldn’t be that interesting. Not unless I really did kill my parents. No, I happened to have a normal childhood if you could define such a thing. But most people didn’t see it that way. Most felt I watched, talked, and obsessed too much with wrestling. I told them it was my passion. Most felt I had an addiction to the internet. I told them I was expressing my artistic intellect. Instead of beer, pussy, pizza, and football, I had gigabytes, forums, cheese balls, and pixels. I can’t say I turned out normal…but what is…? Johnny Fox stopped typing and stared at the computer screen. The look in his eyes was sad…or maybe…something other than sad. Fox slowly lifted a leg and leaned to the side in his chair. THUUUUURRRRRRRNKKKKK!!!!!!!!! It was gas.
