The Strip Club

11 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 11th Sep 2005 This is my big chance to prove myself. I can't let this oppurtunity slip through my fingers. It all comes down to this and if I screw up I can forget about getting in. Don't fuck up now Mark, don't fuck up again. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Mark lay in bed, the thoughts running through his head like a steam train on a one way ticket to no where. He couldn't relax. Wednesday was the big day and he needed all the rest he could get, but he was teaming up with the Universal Champion, God's son and the leader of the group he wanted to be a part of so bad. How could anybody sleep when so much relied on whether or not he could step up to the plate. He felt like he was going to throw a ball, but he wanted a strike. If Hoyt doesn't like what he sees then he could forget about being a part of Imperium and he wanted this so bad. He needed to get out to clear his head, but where the hell could he go at this time of night? He thought for a while, but he couldn't think of anywhere that would be open at this time of night. It was 4:00 am in the morning and everywhere was shut. Mark opened his eyes and sat bolt upright on his bed. If he couldn't find anywhere he would just walk around the block for a while to try and clear his head, but he knew if he did that the thoughts would still be running through his head and he needed to get away from them somehow. He was about to forget about it and turn on the television when it hit him, like a 400 lb linebacker straight to the chest. The stripclub, of course, boobs would surley take his mind of things and he knew just the place. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Monday 12th September 2005, 4:23am Waitress: 'Hi there. Welcome to the Glass Slipper, may I get you a drink ?' Mark was sat down at the nearest table he could find to the dancers. This was exactly what he needed and this was sure to get his mind back on track. He had been so nervous everytime a PRIME show came around these past couple of weeks and this week it was ten times worse. What would Hoyt think of me? Will he find me to be dissapointing and not want me in? What if he had already made his mind up and was just taking me for a ride? Waitress: 'Are you listening to me? There's isn't even a dancer on yet. Heeeellllllllllloooooo!' Mark: 'Sorry, my mind was somewhere else, what did you say?' Waitress: 'I said do you want a drink?' Mark: 'No I'm fine!' The waitress sighed before turning around and walking off. This wasn't the best start he could hope for, he came here to get his mind off ReVolution, not to be pissing waitresses off. Again his mind started to race. I've only been here five minutes and already I've pissed somebody off. What if that happens this week and Hoyt leaves me on my own? What if he doesn't even show up? What if he doesn't tag me and only wants to see me get booted around the ring? His father doesn't like me, does this mean his son won't? Announcer: 'Please welcome to the stage.......the faboulous and deserible SUNSHINE!' Mark looked up from the table and set his eyes on the stage as a gorgeous looking brunette girl came strolling out onto the stage, dressed in the tinyest schoolgirl outfit you could possibly fit on a full grown women. The males in the room began to cheer at the top of their lungs as she slid down on her knees and began to crawl to the end of the platform. Mark's head suddenley went blank of all thoughts for the first time all week as he stared transfixed at the dancer. She got up to her knees and ripped off her school tie, throwing in into the back of the room as one of the druken idiots, fell off his chair trying to catch it. Unknown Voice: 'Isn't she the shit?' Mark turned his head to the side as a young man made his way over to the table Mark was sat at. He didn't even look 16 let alone old enough to be in a strip club. Young Man: 'Mind if I take a seat' Mark didn't answer as he turned his head back to the stage. Sunshine had already removed her shirt and skirt and was now down to her bra and panties. She stood up to her feet and began to swing around the pole, positioned in the middle of the stage. She was a pro and took to the pole like a fish to water. Young Man: 'I'll take that as a yes then' The man took a seat at the side of Mark and placed his beer bottle down on the table. Mark didn't need this now. All he wanted was to come to the club and watch some dancers, he didn't need somebody trying to take his attention away now when he had just got his mind on other things. He knew the man would probably have recognized him off the TV and wanted to ask him questions about PRIME. This was the last thing hew needed right now. Young Man: 'She's beautiful isn't she? I can tell you like her, you can't keep your eyes off her. That's good though because she's one of my best' Mark finally turned his head around to face the young man with a confused look on his face. Mark: 'You own this place?' Young Man: 'Yeah. I know what your thinking, I don't even look old enough to be in here let alone own the place, right?' Mark: 'Well....yeah' Young Man: 'I knew it. My father passed away not long ago and I started to run the place after he died. This was his pride and joy and I couldn't see it get closed down so I decided to run the joint.' Mark: 'My parents passed away too, I know what its like' Young Man: 'Really? I'm sorry about that' Mark: 'Its ok. That was six years ago. I should really have gotten over it, but they was all I had left in this world, its hard to forget something like that.' Young Man: 'I completley understand. My father was the best friend I ever had and thats why I needed to carry on his legacy, I couldn't see it all crumble like a damn cookie.' Both Mark and young man sat next to him dropped their heads to the table and didn't speak for the next few minutes until Mark raised his head again and looked up at the stage. Sunshine was now topless and swinging around the pole like a monkey in the zoo. Mark: 'So how old are you then?' Young Man: 'Listen if I tell you this you can't tell anybody, if the authorites found out I would be shut down as quick as this place fills up' Mark: 'Go on, how old?' Young Man: '17' Mark: '17!!!!!!!' Nearly everybody who wasn't so mesmorized by Sunshine turned their heads towards the comotion as Mark screamed out at the top of his lungs. They soon turned their heads back though as Mark and the owner put their heads down again. Young Man: 'Keep your voice down! I told you not to tell anybody, not scream it out so everybody could hear' Mark: 'Don't worry about it they won't know what we we're talking about, we could have been talking about someone's sister for all they know.' Young Man: 'Yeah I'm 17, I shouldn't even be allowed in here, but the girls don't mind they still get paid and if they don't mind and the authorites don't find out then thats all that matters, right?' Mark: 'Sure, you just shocked me for a minute. I thought you might have been 19 at the least, but its a nice place you have here, really..........erm.........modern.' Young Man: 'Erm..............thanks?' Mark: 'Yeah. I can't say I've been here before, but I might have to purchase myself a memberhip. You do sell memberships right?' Young Man: 'Wait a minute! Ain't you on PRIME wrestling? On the Playboy Channel?' Mark: 'Yeah.........but I'd rather not talk about that, if you don't mind?' Young Man: 'No problem. Yes we sell memberships and seeing as you are a celebrity, I can't have you paying for one. Let me just go in the back and I'll grab you one. You can have private dances galore on these things and you don't need to pay a dime. Wait here.' The young man jumps to his feet and rushes behind the bar and into the back as Sunshine just finishes off her dance and shakes her ass one last time, before making her way through the curtain. Mark had missed the dance, but seeing as he was going to have a membership he would get her to give him a private dance some other time. Now that was something that could take his mind off anything. The young man makes his way back to the table and places a membership card down on the table, which Mark picks up in a flash, sliding it into his jacket pocket in lightning speed. Young Man: 'Thats a year's membership, enjoy it. And when the years up, just come and ask me for another one and I'd be happy to give it to you, I love the show and I'll be looking out for you........Blackwolf is it?' Mark: Yeah......Mark Blackwolf' Young Man: 'Great. You'll like it here, its a nice little place and the dancers are all gorgeous. If you ever want to see any of them, just make your way into the back, I don't mind at all.' Mark: 'Thanks a lot. I'll do that.' The young man was about to leave Mark alone, but turned back at the last minute. Young Man: 'Erm.....one last thing, if you don't mind?' Mark: 'What is it?' Young Man: 'Could you please sign this for me? It would be my first autograph of a celebrity.' Mark: 'Sure.' The young man looks like he was about to jump for joy at Mark's answer, but thinks better of it and slides a small piece of paper in front of Mark and hands him a pen. Mark clicks the pen and signs his name on the piece of paper. This was the first time somebody had asked for his autograph and he wasn't about to turn down the oppurtunity to put his name on some paper. Mark: 'Who should I address this to?' Young Man: 'Shane, the name's Shane.' Mark: 'Ok Shane, there you go, don't go selling it on Ebay now.' Shane: 'Oh I won't, you can trust me on that' Shane grabs the piece of paper off the table and shakes Mark's hand before leaving the table and getting back to work. Mark might finally get to see a dancer all the way through this time and he wasn't about to leave before he did.
