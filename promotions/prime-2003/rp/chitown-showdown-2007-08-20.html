Chitown Showdown

20 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: August 20th, 2007 “Seven ninety-eight is your change, sir.” Munson Monsoon opens his big hand to drop a handful of spare change into the tip cup just in front of the register at Tanino’s, a small, family-run Italian joint two blocks away from Wrigley Field. He shoots a nod to the cute brunette behind the counter and grins slightly. His eyes move to the door as she lifts her head. “Oh, well… thanks.” “Don’t mention it, doll. Just don’t make this you’re life, none of the bombshells do. Stay in school.” The girl watches the Big Bear lower a pair of black shades over his eyes and smile brightly at her before heading out the exit of the restaurant. A few onlookers look back to the girl and she blushes, somewhat. Outside, Big Bear walks through the parking lot, mowing down his roast beef sub like a woodchipper. He grins brightly and half-chewed pieces of meat fall to the hot asphalt. Munson shoves his giant left paw into his pocket for a few second, pulling out a pair of shiny, jingly keys that open his 1997 Discovery Land Rover with the press of a button. “Well, I… I uhh… I’ll call the cops!” Finishing off his sandwich, Big Bear glances down the small, remote alley that barely gets any sunlight during the day. He spots a young boy with blonde hair in the middle of a group of thugs. Big Bear scowls. “Silly kids, playing Gang Warfare all the time- wait a minute!” Big Bear drops the experimentally purchased Mountain Dew to the ground, knowing how much Tyler Rayne would hate him at the moment, he sprints down the alley with lightning speed and approaches just in time. “Look, kid. You don’t want a broken face, you’ll give up the cash. We know you’re from Keller Street. We know you got money.” “I don’t, I swe-“ “Bust him up, Billy. Real good. Make him remember how much lunch really costs.” One of the boys, Billy, steps forward and walks up to the blonde boy. Looking up with his baby blue eyes, the young boy notices the sinister and cold look in his attacker’s eyes. Then the fear… the fear? “You know, boys. Playing Batman can be dangerous but luckily I’m here to help you out.” Billy tries to yank his arm down, squirming to get free but then notice4s who has his arm. The Big Bear. “I’ve seen a few different acts before, but this one is a bit different.” “Look, Big Boob, you’re not in the ring. This is ChiTown. The Windy City. There’s no rules out here, not in these streets.” Big Bear grins, sizing up the lot of the young punks. One yanks out a switch blade. Big Bear looks the little bastard in the eye, grinning a little bigger. “Oh, don’t get me wrong, you’re all really scary. That’s not it. I’m just having a tough time understanding why all of you don’t want to be Batman? Because, well… he is the only one who walks out of here.” Big Bear winks at the kid with the blade as the blonde boy’s eyebrows raise. Big Bear lowers his stance, watching the kid charging forward. -- “Wow, Mr. Big Bear. My daddy says that wrestlers are fakes, no good losers. But you’re the best and toughest I ever sar!” Big Bear grins, wiping his brow of some sweat. He lifts the young fella up off the ground and rests him on the back of a truck parked near his Land Rover. He pats the kid on the shoulder. “You know, buddy, there’s more to life than being tough and strong. You’ve got to be smart, too. You want to tell me what you were doing in that alley, Corey?” The young boy’s head drops down. “You know, pal, I’m not gonna tell anybody. It can be our secret, ya know? It’s okay.” “Well… I like this girl at school and she’s older, in the 9th grade! They said she wanted to meet me down here. I guess I should have known better.” Big Bear chuckles. “Not everybody is as paranoid as Sony Silver, buddy. Just think twice, use your head, and think: whose the chump here? If you do that, you can make the best of any situation.” The kid smiles and perks up a bit. “Alright, now head on home. I’m sure you’ve got some work to do.” The kid jumps down and starts running down the street. “Kid!” The boy stops in his tracks, turning back to the burly behemoth. “Just ask the babe out. She’ll dig it.” The kid smiles ever brighter and tears off like the wind. Big Bear smiles and turns around. A police officer walks up to him, removing his hat in good manners. “Hey, Mr. Munson. I just want you to know how much the guys down at the station, and I, want to thank you for what you did today. To be honest, that was the chief’s kid, Corey. Really saved our ass and his, ya know? Anyway, we’re huge PRIME fans down there and, well, we want you to know that we’re behind you, all the way. When you go for the 5-Sta’ Title in New Jersey, you kick that Jason Snow’s teeth in.” Big Bear turns on his heel, grabbing his chin and staring out into the distance. “Oh yeah… Snow...” Fade…
