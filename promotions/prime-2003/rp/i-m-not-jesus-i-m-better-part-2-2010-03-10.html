Date: March 10, 2010 The women's bathroom always seemed to be crowded immediately after a funeral service. One by one, the grieving girls filed into the bathroom to take a look into the mirror and fix whatever flaws in their makeup the loss of a loved one had made. It was almost like the first step in moving on with their lives, erasing the scars left behind from the loss of a loved one. This ritual was almost done for the evening thankfully, leaving only two girls in the bathroom. The first was Ayumi Nguyen, wife of Jose Guerrero. Famed nemesis of Dragon Mask. Looking in the mirror she was wiping away the mascara laced tears from her cheeks, trying her best to make herself presentable to the world. The first step in a long process that she was sure to remember for the rest of her life. Amazingly enough not for the loss of a loved one, but for what was about to happen because the second person in the bathroom was... "That might've been the longest funeral I've ever been to in my life." Elise Ares spoke out loud, thinking she was in the bathroom by herself but quickly stopped in her tracks when she saw her mother looking into the mirror. It was her father who she ran away from years ago, not her. But the look she got back from her mother was one of betrayal. "Sorry, I thought I was alone." Elise spoke when she saw the look on her mother's face. She'd be lying if she said she didn't miss her mother. It might be the only person on the planet besides herself who she legitimately loves. Deep down inside that annoying facade she puts on in front of PRIME audiences every night, there was a heart. Just not one she particularly likes others to see. "How could do this?" Ayumi said, "I raised you better than that." At first she didn't have anything to say. She was speechless. There were so many words and sentences formulating in her head but nothing seemed to make its way down to her mouth. "You prance around at a funeral, disrespecting Jesus." Ayumi said, "And not just Jesus Sanchez, but Jesus Christ! Walking into a house of God and making it all about yourself. And not to mention your father. You're lucky he doesn't even know you're here." "I don't owe anything to my father." Elise spoke up for herself. "If it wasn't for your father, you wouldn't be alive!" Ayumi spoke up in defense of her husband. "If it wasn't for my father I wouldn't have broken my leg when I was 11." Elise answers, snapping back, "If it wasn't for my father I wouldn't have to lie to the nurses at the hospital and tell them I got hit in the face with a baseball when I was 5 years old. Maybe you weren't around every time he beat me within an inch of my life mom, but I was far from a pampered child." "But your father still loves you, he just doesn't know how to..." "Stop making excuses for that asshole, mom! He continually forced a little girl to lie to adults to make excuses for him beating the piss out of her!" Elise argued back, "I don't care if he loves me. After everything he's done to me... the last thing I feel is love for that sick bastard. My only regret is that this was Jesus' funeral and not his." The tears started to roll down her mother's face before she realized what she'd put her through, but it was far too late to go back. She felt bad that her mother was crying but didn't regret a single word that she'd said. She meant it, it was how she felt and had been wanting to get it off her chest for years. “The day he dies will be the second happiest day of my life.” Elise spoke. "It's like I don't even know who you are anymore. What happened to you Elise?" Ayumi spoke through the tears. “I grew up.” Elise answered before breaking eye contact with her heartbroken mother. Even she couldn’t look back at the tears streaming down her mother’s face, so she simply hung her head as she reached out for the door handle… but it wasn’t there. Looking up she realized the door had been opened, and standing in front of her was quite possibly the last person in the world she wanted to see at this moment. It was her father... and he was pissed. ---- I try to make it through my life In my way there's you I try to make it through these lies That's all I do Just don't deny it Just don't deny it Deal with it Yeah, deal with it You try to break me You want to break me Bit by bit That's just part of it If you are dead or still alive I don't care! Adam Gontier ---- "Don't you ever talk to your mother that way." He said with a snarl, obviously hearing the entire conversation from outside. You could almost see the flames in his eyes as he looked down at his once innocent and fragile little girl. The one he loved more than anything else in his life. The one he also almost killed when she was eleven years old. "I don't think you belong here." Elise responded, unable to make eye contact with her father. It had been so long but that feeling from when she was a little girl came back so naturally. She'd stepped into a wrestling ring against the likes of Chainz and Hessian completely unafraid. How could her father have such a profound effect on her. "That's not the point..." "That is the point!" "Now you listen to me little girl..." He closed in on Elise as she backed away from him. She could see the look in her mother's eyes as her back hit the cold aluminum bathroom stall door. Ayumi wanted to say something, but she couldn't. Elise knew that feeling all too well, "You can say those things about me, and I probably deserve every one of them... but you DO NOT say that about your mother!" "Honey it's oka..." Ayumi tried to interject herself, but it was useless. "It's not okay!" Jose raised his voice. The distinct sound of violence resonated through his vocal chords as he closed in on Elise. Ayumi wanted to say something, anything to stop the inevitable snap that was about to occur but when she opened her mouth she couldn't even force out the air she needed to speak. Tears flowed down her face and her throat was in a vice. Her body locked in fear she watched as an unwilling spectator as Elise looked down at the tile floor, feeling her father's breath on the back of her neck. "I shouldn't be scared of you anymore..." Elise managed to bark out through her own compressed vocals. "Are you suddenly a big girl now?" He slapped the back of her head, startling her a little but not enough to cause any real damage, "You get on television and run your mouth after you run away from home and suddenly you're a big girl? You're gonna come home and disrespect your old man?" Another jarring slap to the side of her head bounced her off the flimsy aluminum. Gritting her teeth together she balled her hand into a fist. "You're getting angry now, aren't you?" He said, "Are you gonna hit me back? Huh?" "Jose stop!" Ayumi screeched, now seated in the corner and curled into the fetal position. The paralyzing fear that crippled her useless kept her from being able to look away as he screamed back... "SHUT UP!" "She's..." "THIS DOESN'T INVOLVE YOU!" He said while giving Elise a shove, pushing her down onto the tile floor. One her hands and knees, Ares pushes herself back up. Her shoulders rise up and down with heavy staggered breathing. Her hands begin to shake as her fingernails dig into the palms of her hands with fury, almost breaking the skin. "Fuck you..." She muttered under her breath as she stood back up again. "What did you say to me you little bitch?" Jose responded, "Don't you fucking disrespect me." "I said FUCK. YOU." Elise turned around and looked into the eyes of her father. His nostrils flared and his eyes grew wide. Everything seemed to move in slow motion as she watched him wind back to throw a haymaker at her. Wincing, she reacts instinctively by throwing up her arms hoping to block the incoming hard right. A hard right that never comes. THUNK! The noise takes Elise by surprise as she quickly snaps her eyes open to see what it was. The aluminum wall that was once next to her was now dented and ripped off the brace that was screwed into the tile floor. Looking next to it she saw the unexpected site of her father on the ground, trying to cover up from the frenzy of punches being rained down from Kazys Jankauskas. Her mother screams in fright as Elise herself takes a few steps back. The scuffle continues on the floor as Jose manages to fight his way back up. A wild punch from Jose manages to make contact across the chin of Kazys, hardly slowing him down as he grabs the back of Jose's head and slams it into the mirror. Shards of reflective glass rain down onto the tile floor mixed with shades of crimson. Stumbling back away from the wall, Elise watched as Jose tried to pick the pieces out of his bloodied face. He didn't have much time however as Kazys jarred him with a right hook that sent him staggering towards the sink in front of the mirror that was just broken. "Don't you lay a fucking hand on her you worthless piece of shit!" Kaz bellowed as Jose embraced the sink, holding on for dear life. He was too beaten to know exactly where he was, he was just holding on for dear life... blood pouring out of his face down the drain of the porcelain white sink. The Iron Wolf took a few steps back, running the back of his hand across his busted lip to find out how bad he was bleeding. It wasn't anything that wouldn't heal in a couple days. "Elise, we need to get out of her..." Kazys looked at Ayumi still sitting in the corner. Her eyes closed and rubbed raw from the tears. Her whole body was shaking as she finally opened her eyes and slowly lifted her hand towards the sink, pointing at the barely conscious body of Jose Guererro hanging on desperately. The Havana Harlot was now standing behind him, gritting her teeth she grabbed a hand full of his hair. "What in the world are you doing?" Kaz questioned as Ares snarled, lifting the head of her father before slamming into the sink. THUMP! THUMP! THUMP! THUMP! Over and over and over again Elise slammed the face of her abusive father into the sink. She could feel facial bones breaking with every impact. Blood squirted out of the sink as she just kept going and going. The snarl turned into an emotionless glare as she had the intensity of a killer, feeling her father's life fade away in her blood soaked palms. She was going to kill him. Kazys ran over to Elise and pulled her away from her father's unconscious body as her mother wailed out in the corner, trapped in a nightmare that wouldn't end. Finally Elise relinquished her grip and was dragged to the other side of the bathroom. Kazys looked on in horror himself as Elise screamed at the top of her lungs... "FUCK YOU, YOU PIECE OF SHIT! I WISH YOU WERE DEAD." Elise was venting years and years of pain, abuse, and anger. A tormented childhood she'd kept to herself all these years, "I WANT TO FUCKING KILL YOU! YOU DESERVE TO DIE!" "Elise! Elise!" Kazys tried to talk some sense into his tag team partner as she burst into tears, looking down at the bloodstains under her fingernails. It was like a scene from a horror movie as she just wrapped her arms around The Iron Wolf and cried. As she buried her head into the chest of the man she would never tell you was her only friend, she lost all self control and years of emotions just poured out of her. She had nowhere else to go, no one else to turn to, no one in the world who would listen anymore... besides Kazys. Kazys was always the one who was there, even if he didn't want to be. Although through her own sobs it was hard to hear the world around her, she managed to catch a few words from the side of the room that would haunt her dreams that night and quite possibly for the rest of her life... "You're a monster..." Ayumi choked out, "You're a monster just like your father." ---- BEEP BEEP BEEP BOOP BEEP BOOP BOOP "Enter your four digit PIN number, then press pound." CLICK ---- The sound of running water filled the room, bouncing off the acoustics of the hotel bathroom. Looking down at her hands, she felt almost like Lady Macbeth trying to wash off the blood that was no longer there. Looking down at the blood covering her hands still haunted her dreams. Every night it was like reliving the moment. It felt so real, yet as time passed on and the details of the exact incident began to fade away her dreams warped into something much more dark and sinister. As a matter of fact the more she thought about that very night, the less she seemed to care. The more numb she became to the reality of permanently scarring her father and ending any sort of relationship she could ever have with her mother, Ayumi. Perhaps the constant jeering from the crowd had calloused her heart, or it could've been the years of abuse she endured from the man who was supposed to love her more than any other man would, or maybe even it was the fact that she never loved them as much as even she thought she did. They say that time heals all wounds and it'd been a long time since the last time she had to face them. In her heart maybe they were both dead all along. Turning off the faucet she wiped the steam off the mirror and looked at her reflection. While she still looked the same, so much had changed. The harlot had turned into the beast. In a way she was even scared of herself. She acted like an unleashed animal that night. Past emotions brought on an act of violence that even she never fully realized she was capable of albeit years of fighting. That wasn't even the scariest thing to Elise though. The real horror was how little she cared about it. Running it past herself over and over again, she knew that she'd do it again. Even worse, she knew she wouldn't stop again unless Kazys made her. The locker room in PRIME was probably right about her, she was just some crazy fucked up bitch who missed one too many hugs when she was a little girl. Yet none of them could deny her success. Walking out of the hotel bathroom wearing a bra and a pair of boyshorts, she walked past the pink cargo bag right outside of the closet that held the PRIME 5-Star Championship. It was the biggest accomplishment in her life, but she knew it was just the start of much bigger things. Perhaps Kazys knew too, quickly averting his eyes from Elise to the television when she looked over. It wasn't the first time in the past few weeks she'd caught him doing that. Something inside the big, bad wolf had changed. While her constant bragging and immaturity still drove him to the very brink of sanity, he wasn't contemplating suicide any time she was around anymore. No longer was being Elise's personal bodyguard a mean to an end until a real job came along. The Swaggeriffic One had grown on him in a way he wasn't comfortable in facing. He probably hadn't even admitted it to himself yet but through years of manipulation Elise didn't even have to think twice about it. Kazys Jankauskas had fallen in love with her. Even knowing her past he'd stumbled down a road that every man in Elise's life before him had done. She was crazy, annoying, and obnoxious... but strangely irresistible. There was something magnetic about her and it wasn't just her breath-taking looks. She had her own little version of charm about her, and she certainly knew how to abuse it. "Throwdown with Bobby Flay?" Elise blurted out, "Are you serious?" Kazys went to look over but already knew what she was wearing, and didn't want to get caught staring. "Nothing else is on." Kazys responded, glaring at the television. "And I'm not watching Jersey Shore." "You have no taste in television." Elise said with a smirk, "Plus we have a big match coming up at the Dual Halo. Shouldn't you be working out or something? Being productive? Thinking of innovative strategies that are needed to protect me?" "Shouldn't you be doing something more productive like putting on some damn clothes?" Kazys answered quickly, "You look like a prostitute." "Right, like any girl who looks like me would ever be a prostitute." Ares posed playfully, "Plus, I'm sure you don't mind." "I do." "You don't." "I do." "You don't, look over here." Slowly Kazys adverted his eyes from the television and looked over at The Swaggeriffic One, now sitting on the bed leaning forward a bit showing off the cleavage from her bra. She looked into his eyes and knew he was hooked, no matter how hard he tried to hide it. Her years of manipulation made her a master at reading people as Kazys turned over and flipped the television off, turning the entire room into darkness. "I'm going to bed." His voice rang through her ears as her eyes adjusted to the night. "If you get pinned by Nitz you're totally fired." "Shut up. Go to sleep." He said in frustration as Elise sat there on the side of her bed all alone. Truth be told she liked the new-found attention Kazys started to give her. The fact that he'd just turned the lights on was a bit of let down. Sighing she rolled into her bed and pulled the covers up to her shoulders. Staring up at the ceiling in the silence, it didn't take long for the images of blood and violence to start running through her mind like a horror film she'd just seen a night before. If she closed her eyes the dreams would come back and they'd be even worse than the last time. She needed to get her mind off of it and there was only one thing that worked every time. Pushing off the sheets, Elise rolled out of bed as quietly as she could trying not to alert Kazys or startle him. Now standing up between the two beds her heart began to beat a little faster as she glared down at him sleeping in his bed. She took a step forward in the dark taking a deep breath as she reached around and unlatched the back of her bra. Letting it slide off of her shoulders and onto the ground she took another step forward, biting her lip as the cool air blew across her chest. Now at Kazys bed side, she lifted up the end of his blanket with a slight shake in her grasp. Shaking? She couldn't figure out what was wrong as she dropped the blanket and looked down to verify for herself. What was wrong with her? She'd done this a million times before. You just slide into the bed behind them, they turn around, you kiss, you fuck, and they're yours for as long as you need them. Yet now there was hesitation, just like the first time all over again. Her heart began beating out of her chest and her breaths grew short as she took a few steps back. She couldn't convince herself to do it. Not to Kazys. There was something special about him but as much as she tried she couldn't quite put her finger on it. It was something that she'd never felt before... CLICK ---- CREEEEK CREEEEK The sound of footsteps on the aged hardwood floors was like a screech in the night as the moonlight cascaded in from the opened window, casting a shadow onto the wall as he made his way across the room. His breathing had turned into a pant as his hands thumped against the dresser. All along, his hands shook as he tried to open the top drawer. It almost seemed to fight back as his own shaking caused him to lose his grip several times before finally sliding it open. Quickly darting his hands inside he fumbled around with the contents, navigating through socks, underpants, and other keepsakes as he tried to find his objective. First he pulls out a picture and lays it down in the moonlight. He can barely see the image of him holding his little girl in his arms. So small in fragile, but she grew up to be strong. Often times he lost sight of how proud he was at what she'd become. If only he could put into words what he'd always wanted to say. A tear dripped down his and he grinded his teeth together as he reached back into the drawer and wrapped his hand around cold steel. His vision became a blur as he only could see the outline of the .45 shaking violent in his hand, grabbing his wrist with the other hand didn't seem to help. Running the back of his wrist across his eyes to wipe away the tears, it took every fiber of his being not to scream out as his mouth opened and the bitter taste of steel and gun-powder filled his pallet. With one last deep breath, he thought about all the missed opportunities in his life and closed his eyes. Here's to having better luck in the next one. BANG ---- The voicemail icon flashed on her cell phone the next morning, but it was left unanswered. With one quick press of a button it was gone. She didn't care anymore.
