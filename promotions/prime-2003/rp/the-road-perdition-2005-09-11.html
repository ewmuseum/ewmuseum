The Road Perdition

11 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 09/11/2005 'To whoever reads this letter, I ask for your understanding, and, perhaps, your forgiveness. Somehow, I knew he would end up here. I had hoped beyond all rationality that my part in this affair was over, but that afternoon, when he stepped into my office... I knew I still had a part to play in this sordid tale. For a moment, I didn't recognize him. But then, it hit me like a truck. I remembered those days, spent training with a man I once considered my greatest feud, my greatest partner than man who made me who I am. But now dead and buried, or at least I thought so. I've tried to use my skills. But he was the one exception. I shouldn't have gotten involved with it, I know I shouldn't have. But professional curiosity overwhelmed all of my rationality. The plan was so bold, so completely unheard of, that I believed I would be a fool not to be a part of it. And now, the hens have come home to roost. And I only pray that, once my dirty work is completed, that no more lives have to be lost.' 'So long ago. How many years has it been since I first walked into an arena? Memories are beginning to fade, even in my normally sound mind. The date escapes me. But what I'll never forget is the way they spoke of me. The way my mentor, Kash, looked at me his eyes darted around as he explained his 'million dollar' plan. The way his voice filled with vicious hatred when he mentioned the name. Right then, I should have known to tell him to leave. Forget it. I won't be a part of this vindictive scheme. But I couldn't help myself. Imagine what I could learn if his plan was a success. Imagine how the field of hypnotherapy would be advanced light years beyond its time. Imagine the accolades that would be heaped upon us if this man's near-insane goal was possible. The plan to take over an entire organization and have the organization being controlled by three wrestlers, three men who were out for three things. Money, Power, and Respect. I should have realized then, that they all would have called us madmen. Mad scientists tinkering with the minds of others. Evil. That's what they would have called our actions. Pure evil. But at the time, I was still young and foolish, but it was for my good. I said yes. And I taught him, and he taught me. Taught him all of my skills of hypnosis, he taught me all the schemes and techniques to be successful come hell or high water. When everyone is counting you out he taught me who to overcome odds. I taught him techniques most professionals in the field frowned upon. He taught me how to lead an open mind, make it draw completely false conclusions. We taught each other all we knew, and if I had had a single inkling of what it would lead to, I would have... I don't know what I would have done... forgive me for what I have to do.' 'One day, he came into my office again. Told me about what he had done. Told me all about the months he had spent training. Conditioning and believing he was another man. Programming an entire past into his mind, like a hacker uploading a malignant virus into a system. And then he asked me for one last favor. And said that he needed a 'backup plan' in case his victim didn't accomplish the job correctly. In case worse came to worse, and he ended up dead. He asked me to conduct one last hypnotherapy session for him, in order to place one last post-hypnotic suggestion into his mind. One that was totally beyond his own skills, that only somebody as experienced as I could handle. I didn't want to. Even in my lust for accolades and recognition, I realized the implications of what he asked me to do. Burying something like that in a man's subconscious, it was too horrible. It could have repercussions, not only upon the people in his life, but upon his own sanity. I tried to refuse. But he threatened me. He was the only one I knew could beat me. My reputation was all I had and it would have been ruined. There was nothing else I could do. And so, I placed the final piece of the puzzle that created the man known as Tyler Lopez. So much work had been done, but in the end it was my own words that caused the most damage. There isn't a day where I don't regret what I did. And now, Tyler Lopez must come back. He is back. The funny thing is they won't even recognize me. They won't even see a difference at a glance, but when I enter the ring Wednesday, everything will change. And it pains me so much to have to do this. I don't want to destroy anyone's life. I don't want to destroy Vangellus Oxios's career. He has a whole future ahead of him. And because of my debt to myself, to my friend, and to my fiancé' I'm forced to tear that all down. If anyone ever reads this, I beg your understanding, and your forgiveness. Whatever may happen, just know that I never meant for it to get this far. But I have to do what I have to do, because my debt isn't paid. My debt to my greatest asset, and my greatest tormenter. My debt to myself.' ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------'The only thing you take with you when you're gone is what you leave behind.' -John Allston THE PAST “So if we do this, if I agree to this, what’s in it for me? I mean you’re asking a lot, you’re asking me to forsake all that I’ve worked for, to throw it all away, and well, I’m not sure if she’d want that I mean...' His voice young and enthusiastic, yet pained and vengeful. 'What reward can be greater than supremacy? [Cough] Why would you continue to work for the people that have taken the most from you, have robbed you of your dignity time after time...' 'From Culture Shock...' He listens to the man who made him who he is. '...To Colossus...' He hears. '...To having to hide your face, one of the greatest in wrestling history, because you are not fitting the mold that the powers that be have set for a world champion, like some common wrestler?' He grows angry. '...Shaming you for putting you as a Alias Title competitor, when the new brass at that time wanted to usher in the supposed ‘new blood’ that still hasn’t come through, and probably never will. They asked you to drop the gimmick, and shamed you for wanting to fulfill your destiny, of being the greatest...' His hatred builds. 'But if we do this, we have to reinvent ourselves, I have more soldiers to gather, so I can’t be at the forefront, for now...But I don’t want this Tyler. I need you to dig down inside of yourself, find all that hatred, find all of that scorn, and turn it against the people that have made you this way, I need you at your worst, so you can be my best...' He believes. 'To pay the price you’ve already paid is enough, but I understand your concern, and for that your reward will be…Whatever you want...' His voice sickened and evil, yet insightful. '...Whatever I want?!' 'You name it and it’ll be yours, after all, I’m KA$H, and you’re the “Scientific Apparatus” you’re Tyler Lopez...' ...Leave the past, for now... THE PRESENT [KNOCK-KNOCK-KNOCK] 'Tyler ...are you okay in there?' [Groaning] 'Nooo...' 'Well okay then, if you don’t hurry up we’re going to miss our flight.' [Moaning] 'Just reschedule the damn flight Passion; we’ll leave tomorrow night...' [Whining] 'Tyler...' [Growling] 'Damnit Passion, just reschedule the flight, we’ll leave tomorrow.' [Sad] 'Why are you in such a stinky mood?' [Moaning] 'All that food we made the other day, made me sick...' We’re inside the Tyler Lopez's bathroom. Lopez sits on the toilet and stares at the framed picture above him on the wall, a picture of himself hands high, as thousands cheered on, streamers flowing down, as he won his first world title. Lopez runs his hands through his hair, and then leans back on the toilet. 'What are you doing? What happened to those days, then I was a hero, then I was respected and revered, then it was all different…Then, I was different.' Lopez grunts his voice a surreal calm, something we haven’t heard from him in years, almost a semblance of, sanity? 'How can your finest dream turn into your worst nightmare? What are you doing Tyler? You walked into this place six, dreaming of the moment you could walk away the greatest, you could help make this place a success, you can be the 'Foundation' in which this place builds upon, and lives to thrive long after you.' Lopez runs his hands over his face. 'All you have is the remains of a shattered life, you try and pick up the pieces, but you just walk over them, stabbing yourself in the foot, you walk on them long enough, you start to deny the pain, and even though the trail of blood follows you, and then you want others to feel this pain, because misery indeed loves company…” Lopez's voice fades in and out, between anger and sorrow, between vengeance and regret. '...And soon you start to enjoy the pain, and shortly after that you enjoy the site of your own blood, and at the same time you want to stop bleeding so badly, you beg at night for the pain to stop, but it won’t, because it can’t. Because the minute it ends, so do you. That’s when it has you, you want it to stop so you can die, but you need it to continue so you can live. You think you’re running from it, but in reality it’s never chased you, you chased it. And in that split second in which in turns around and stares you in the eye, and you realize that you’re staring at yourself, all the pain and all the sorrow, the sickness was all self-created, was all self imposed, it’s too late.” Lopez's voice trails off. 'These dreams, maybe they’ve been more than that, maybe they’ve been premonitions, I ushered in a New Era, and now it’s fitting that it will end in Purgatory. I sit here realizing, the man Tyler Lopez...' Lopez lowers his hands off of his face, his eyes red, filled with remorse and anger '...Is already dead.' Lopez closes his eyes, and then nervously chuckles as he opens them. 'Hello my opponents, they say that imitation is the sincerest form of flattery, and trust me I am flattered. I mean, who wouldn’t be flattered, that after a man is called boring and unoriginal, he then goes out and copies the premise of the more entertaining mans efforts, and becomes the first man ever to call me washed up, that hurt deep, because you were the first, yep, the first.' Lopez gives a mocking thumb up.' 'It’s like looking in a mirror, but not. [Lopez nervously chuckles] And honestly Dani, as a crusader for all that’s right, you shouldn’t bring up your past accomplishments to validate your current quest, those are the signs of an insecure man clutching for approval, hence why I don’t bring up my accomplishments.' Lopez sneers. 'Moving right along, I can honestly say I’m proud of who I am. Unlike some people, I have never done any copyright infringement. Lopez sneers. 'I’ve been a lot of things in my life, in my professional career most of them have been prefaced with, “Champion”, but alas, I’m too humble to name them. What I’ve never been is a person who’d lay down for the greater good of a friend. There are no friends in this business, just current opponents, and future opponents. You were always the later, until now.' 'And Ignorance, they say its bliss. And trust me come Revolutions, I’ll have one more reason to be even more arrogant. I’m feeling nostalgic now, so I’ll leave you with these words of wisdom.' Lopez leans in close. 'Sometimes in life you make a choice, but sometimes, [nervous chuckle], just sometimes, that choice is made for you...' Lopez sneers. '...Come Wednesday, let’s just say, the choice won’t be yours!' Lopez nervously chuckles as he stares up at the picture again, and sighs' THE FUTURE?! 'So what’s your decision Tyler?' [Cough] 'The World is at your fingers, you do this for me, and you can have whatever you want...' 'Whatever I want eh? For a place that’s taken it all from me, for a place that’s taken the only thing I’ve loved, for a place that made me sacrifice my life, and then took the rest, its disease on me, so when it dies, I don’t want it to ever forget me...' [Desperate] 'What is it, name it, it’s yours, what you want, together, we can make history...' [Nervous chuckle] “Make history, no, I don’t want to make history...' [Silence] 'I want to take its HISTORY!' ...And indeed like the missing pieces to the puzzle, the picture becomes clearer, no matter how disturbing the image is. And at least for a few more days, the saga continues... THE ROAD TO PERDITION BEGINS!
