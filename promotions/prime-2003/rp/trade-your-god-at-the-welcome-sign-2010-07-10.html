Trade Your God at the Welcome Sign

10 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: July 10, 2010 From down the hall where I slept, I could hear the noise; the cheering and the howling. You can try to ignore it, but 7:30am is some rough shit, and that’s exactly when Castor demanded I get up. Who doesn’t need their biographer at the crack of sunlight? Every time I walked through that hallway, no matter the urgency of errand at hand, I would take a glance at the paintings on the wall: dead puppet mice held on marionette strings by a smiling orange cat; Alfred Hitchcock missing half his face; a vagina; the Greek alphabet; Walt Disney/Mickey Mouse two-faced. “RAM-THAT-SPAM! RAM-THAT-SPAM!” Is that what they’re saying? They are louder as I approach, and my mind draws a blank as I open the door. “RAM-THAT-SPAM! RAM-THAT-SPAM!” they cheered, this Roman orgy, as a thin waif-like male penetrated the ever loving hell out of a bent-over fat woman in a Burger King crown. “RAM-THAT-SPAM! WOOOOOOOOO!” Fuck’s sake- is this real? I hope so, because it doesn’t say much for the intelligence of a man who dreams of this; that such shit would be wrested in his subconscious. “Let’s hear it for Castor’s biographer! WOOOOOOO YEAH! FUCK YEAH! AHAHAHAHAHA!” One of the nude youths approached me, almost in. Not that I was anything to worshipped, but that perhaps I’d seen something they hadn’t. “So is he coming?” the boy said. “Who? Castor?” A bashful smile slid across his face. “Yeaaaah I mean Castor! Will he be visiting? I really love when he…visits.” “Uhh, not tonight. I don’t think, anyway.” What else could I tell him? The boy changed his expression quick, now looking desperate as he came forward and took my hand. He shook it uncontrollably. “He hasn’t…mentioned anything to you? About me? I’m sorry, my name is Jonathan. ACTOR, at your service. And these…” he said, motioning to the rest of the orgy, men and women alike. The lascivious act had stopped, for now. “These are his actors too. We are all HIS actors, yes? And you’ll tell him that we’re his…won’t you?” “Hold on pal, I’m just his biographer. He speaks, I listen and write. Simple exchange, nothing too complicated-“ he cut me off quickly. “You really are lucky, friend! Holy my Jove almighty are you lucky! You ever hear the shit that comes out of his mouth? I mean you must…cause you write for him. Oh he’s just…just on another LEVEL, friend!” The look in his eyes told me no one was home. In fact, nobody had been home for quite some time, and that mind was long ago foreclosed on. He continued, “You know what he said to me yesterday? He told me…that he is Geppetto, and I am his wooden boy. And he made me so that one day, I might be real too. Man…,” he began to sob, “man it took me to another fucking PLANET. Do you get what I’m saying? When he speaks it’s like a gamma ray setting the fucking clouds on fire, and I’M ON IT! …we’re all on it.” They were on it, alright. Me? Just about my time to get off. Mid sentence the boy began to vomit, breaking the silence from around the room and spurning another go-around of depraved sexual activity that included but was not limited to electro-shock clitoris stimulation. And I’m not sure, but I believe there’s a tranny somewhere in the crowd. I turned, closed the door, and walked back to my quarter trying to forget the whole damn thing. Now a female screaming could be heard from the room, getting louder and louder; the breaking of glass, and several thuds. Whatever, let them drown. Let them die in a puddle of their bodily fluids. Castor appeared at my door in the morning. The clock says- 9:30; FUCK. “I let you dream, because dreams are important. You did remember to dream?” Even his compassion was offbeat. “Hard to get much rest in with the party going on down the hall. You know I heard them breaking shit too?” “They are my cast for the month, while I finish shooting two films. I am surprised you haven’t met them sooner,” he responded. “Yeah, well, your house is huge. They seemed high on something.” “GHB mostly. It works wonders for the orgasm. Whatever the want, I give them. The things they do, the things they put in their bodies…it’s all an escape. This world no longer holds their interest, so they travel into mine where they live forever and ever and ever. That is, until, they’ve outlived their use to me.” “At which point you kill them?” I said, sarcastically. After all, he’s Castor Strife, not Chainz. You think I’d follow around a guy who supposedly goes on sick murder sprees? “Their careers, anyway. Is there a worse death?” Yeah, ACTUAL death, but hey, this is Hollywood after all. “Our friend the sponsor has written me again. Things are becoming quite interesting as it concerns my athletic endeavors. This entire month of work has come out of his pocket, and there’s more on the way.” Castor still wouldn’t reveal this man to me. But the details become less cryptic by the day, and the tall grass is being cut shorter and shorter. Something big is about to happen, at least that’s the impression I’m getting. “And what does he get in return?” I asked, straight to the point. “He only needs one thing from me, and who’s to say I haven’t already made arrangements to secure it? Follow me out the door, there’s more I should tell you…” Follow Castor Strife. The flute plays on, and I do just that.
