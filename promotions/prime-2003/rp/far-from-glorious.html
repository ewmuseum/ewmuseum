Date: Today :Prologue:Cocaine is the best high there is, especially if you mix it with Ecstasy and a Red Bull. It's very intense, but so relaxed at the same time. The problem for most people is the redosing - they'd end up doing it without even thinking, especially if they thought the drug made them better in some sense, and would overdose very quickly. Amy Campbell was no stranger to minor O.D.'s, either; She always lived, but ended up having vivid and horrible nightmares - moreso memories - of her life up to the point when she took in that last line. When she came back to consciousness, it was always to very depressing memories... and this time would be no different. Aimz is the kind of person whose parents are even afraid of her, and FOR her. She has little in the way of what you'd see as common sense, and by little... well, it's essentially none. She inadvertantly does almost everything she shouldn't, be it drugs, her 'type' of boyfriend or hell - even her choice to take public transit after dark on the wrong end of town. Her capability to physically fend for herself is the only thing that keeps the girl alive, whereas her mental prowess is essentially useless given her lifestyle... and her choices in life require a survival instinct like only Campbell could have. The Negasonic Lupine was in for a fight if she wanted to one-up Aimz, much less defeat her. Everything was now in place for the little redhead to topple what was technically her worst enemy. Not cocaine or a beer bottle, but a K-Wolf. The means to this end were destructive and barely healthy, but at least the end was coming... Whether or not her other enemies would fall as accordingly remained to be seen, but for now they were helping her. An overdose was the test... it was a small attempt for cocaine to weaken her spirit going into this match, but it only served to parrallel the women and build up the glass case of emotion Amy needed.
