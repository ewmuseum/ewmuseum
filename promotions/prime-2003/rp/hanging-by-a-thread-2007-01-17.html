Hanging by a thread

17 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/17/07 The Place: TD Waterhouse Center in Orlando Florida. The Date: September 14, 2005 The Event: ReVolution 75. The Match: Gaz "The Jester" Green vs Trashcan Man in the first round of the 2005 Jewel in the Crown Tournament. The last time the Trashcan Man was ever seen in PRIME. Let's go. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Both men were a bit nervous, but that was to be expected for such an important match. Everyone with any sense gets prematch jitters, even established veterans like these two. The winner of this tournament would go on to King of Kings to take on the Universal Champion Hoyt Williams for the big gold, and both men wanted that very badly. Backstage, waiting for the commercial break to end and for the familiar "WHERE'S YOUR HEAD AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAT?!?!" to blast out over the Orlando crowd, signfying Gaz "The Jester" Green's entrance. The two men exchanged a short respectrul nod, knowing that even there was no bad blood between them, all the stops would be pulled out tonight. Turning away from the Emerald Menace, the Trashcan Man starts twisting his torso, testing for any tell tale twinges in his surgically repaired back that had been damaged two years before in Old School Wrestling by a combination of attacks by the Russian juggernaut Ivan Stanislav and Czech American Bad Ass, Boda. The lack of any sharp pains brought a slight sense of relief to the hardcore legend, who proceeded to throw some phantom punches, trying to stretch out shoulders that were tensed up like steel springs from the stress of the night. "Maybe I should've brought Bob," he thought to himself. "Or at least a trashcan or something." Mourning the lack of the nail studded, barbed wire wrapped steel chair he had inherited from Ed, he cast around looking for something appropriate to bring with him to the ring. He had been relying on Bob too much lately, but he was accustomed to bringing a weapon of some sort with him to matches, just as a security blanket if nothing else. A man who's been in the business as long as he had made enemies, and the list of enemies the Trashcan Man had made in PRIME was not one to be scoffed. Hoyt Williams, Vangelus Olsig, and Token Weed had all jumped him from behind at one time or another, and it was best to be prepared in case it happened again. He didn't have any of the allies he had gathered up over the years in PCW or OSW. No Asylum to watch his back, no Miyagawa, Bang Davenport, JJ, Timo Bolamba, Azala Zameer, or Six Finger Cisco... No Brock Newbludd, no Reverend Jon Hull, no Tempest... no Ed, the man he had helped murder when he was under the control of Alan Kriegman. Even Ed's ghost hadn't been around much lately. This would be the time he would be mentally conversing with one of his other personalities, the laughing buffoon Mr Smiley Face. But the dark one, the third personality, had convinced Trashy that the Smilin' Dude had been a tool, a construct that the supergenius Alan Kriegman had implanted in him along with the mind control chip to keep him docile. The Trashcan Man, furious at the betrayal of... well, himself, gathered his willpower and crushed his happier personality, mentally beating it into what he then thought was oblivion. He was regretting that decision. For the first time in years, the dark side of the entity that was Michael Kriegman had full access to the Trashcan Man with no one to interfere. Back in the Evanston Clinic, Alan Kriegman had named the three personalities after the three faces of Fate. The Trashcan Man was Clotho, Mr Smiley Face was Lachesis... and the dark one was Atropos, the old crone that cut the threads that measured out mortal lives. And Atropos was making the best of this situation, making suggestions that were sometime horrifying... yet somehow logical to the ever confused Trashcan Man. "Why not use that one over there?" The sibilant whisper of the dark one hissed in his mind, and Trashy's head turned towards a heavy steel drum. He walked over and ran his fingers over the lid for a moment before shaking his head. "That's too heavy. I'm not looking to kill anyone, dammit. Just something to even the odds... just in case..." he sent the thought back at the voice inside his head. Evening the odds is all well and good, Michael, but this is an important match. Look at that green fool over there... One shot from this and the fight's right out of him. You know you're going to win this match... why expend the energy? Take him out now and we'll be rested for the next round. For a much more important opponent. Disgusted, the Trashcan Man spins on a heel and takes a step away from the steel container. Heavy with contempt, he shot his answer back to the voice in his head. "That's not going to happen. I fight fair, and I win fair. I will not be what my "master" tried to make me!" Blain Blaire ducks out from the gorilla position, his headset askew as he warns both competitors. "30 seconds till the commercial break's over guys. Good luck to both of you." With that, he ducks back in, getting ready to order the transfer over from the commercial break back to the live feed coming from Orlando. If you won't... I WILL! With the dark one's exclamation echoing in his head, the Trashcan Man felt something uncoil within him before cold claws seemed to envelope his mind, dragging him down as the dark one, for the first time in a decade came forward, taking over the body. He stumbled back into the steel drum, almost knocking it over as the euphoria of actual physical sensation washed over him. The Jester turned around, confusion in his eyes as he watched his opponent shaking his head and muttering to himself. "Trash?" Gaz took a step forward. "You ok, man?" His lips lightly part in a crooked smile as the dark one reaches behind him, placing a gloved hand on the heavy steel lid of the drum. "Trash isn't here. Not anymore." Ignoring the panicked shouting in his head, the dark one grasps the lid and swings it, connecting with Gaz's face with a sick CLANG, knocking him to the ground. Chuckling to himself, the dark one stood over his fallen opponent, drinking in the sight. Kneeling down beside Gaz, he reaches down and grabs a handful of green hair, dragging his bloody face up so he can whisper into his ear. "You can call me Lazarus. And I'm done being buried. Now I'll bury you." Trapped inside his own mind, the Trashcan Man can only watch in horror as Lazarus drags the Jester to his knees. Rearing back, he SLAMS the heavy steel lid into Gaz's back with another CLANG, sending him back down to the ground. Raising the lid above his head with a sinister grin, he brings down the lid onto the Jester's back with another CLANG. The crowd of fans in the arena who are now watching this on the PRIMEtron (it was called the PRIMEtron back then, before the Wal Mart sponsorship) gasps in sympathy as Lazarus brings the lid down again, and again, and again, and yet again, his grin growing wider with each sickening CLANG. Stop it! For the love of God, stop it!! screams the mental voice of the Trashcan Man as he tries to claw his way back into dominance, but he's ignored by the dark force that is now controlling his limbs. Lazarus reaches down again, grabbing a handful of green hair, ready to gloat over his fallen opponent, when Gaz somehow springs to his feet, desperation giving him strength as he locks his arms around Lazarus's waist, legs pumping wildly as he drives him into a vending machine. Lazarus, having never actually experienced physical pain before, cringes as several cans of soda, dislodged by the enormous impact, fall out of the machine. Gaz, ever the innovator, grabs a can and shakes it, spraying Lazarus in the eyes, temporarily blinding him and providing the Jester with a brief respite from the struggle. The dark one stumbles away as Gaz reaches down and grabs another can, flinging with the accuracy of a Major League Baseball pitcher, connecting solidly with the back of his assailant's head, driving him to one knee, dazed from the impact. For a brief moment, the internal struggle in Michael Kriegman's body takes a new turn, as the Trashcan Man scrambles back into the dominant position. Snarling, Gaz watches Trashy stagger to his feet in confusion before his springs, driving his shoulder deep into the Trashcan Man's midriff, driving him through a door and onto a loading dock, triggering a fire alarm. Ignoring the loud WHOOP WHOOP, the Jester kneels on Trash's chest, an unaccustomed expression of rage on his face as he slams punch after punch into the fallen hardcore legend's face. Unfortunately, this is enough to jar Trashy out of the dominant position as once again he is sent screaming into the abyss that is his mind as Lazarus claws his way back into control. A sick grin on his face, the dark one takes his thumb and grinds it into the Jester's eyesocket, the thickness of his glove probably being the only thing that's stopping him from gouging it out. Gaz howls in pain and falls back as Lazarus rises to his feet. The dark one moves in, but Gaz, one eye clenched in pain and tears swings wildly from his knees and connects solidly with the Kriegman family jewels. Lazarus staggers back, teetering close to the edge of the dock and clutching his groin as the Jester moves in, fist reared back. He throws a wild haymaker but Lazarus ducks, swinging hin behind him, wrapping an arm around his shoulder and placing a leg in front of Gaz's. Trash realizes what Lazarus is trying to do and claws his way back into control, but not in time to stop Lazarus from diving forward with a reverse Russian legsweep, sending both men to the concrete below. With some quick thinking and unexpected grace, the Trashcan Man ducks both men over in mid air, somersaulting them so they land on their backs with a horrible thud, saving Gaz from a potentially fatal headfirst impact. Groaning in pain, he strengthens the mental barries holding the dark one in his mental prison. Evil laughter fills his mind as darkness washes over him, sending him spiraling into unconciousness... ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The Place: Rio Internacional Hotel, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil The Date: 1/16/2007 The Event: One day before ReVolution 116. The Match: Pierce Lavelle vs Trashcan Man The Trashcan Man's return match to PRIME. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Steam filled the bathroom, fogging the mirror and obsuring the form of the Trashcan Man as he stood in the shower, head bowed forward underneath the scalding hot spray, trying to wash away the doubts and fears that returning to the ring arose in him. "Why am I doing this again?" he quietly asked himself. For most people, talking to themselves was a habit, one that never garnered an answer. But when you are the Trashcan Man, you always have an audience. Mr Smiley Face, having recovered and reformed mostly due to the Trashcan Man's desperate need for someone in his head to keep the dark one at bay, filled his head with high pitched laughter. You're a glutton for punishment, old chum. And so am I, for going along with this. Trashy sighs and leans his head back, letting the water wash over his face, turning it red from the heat. "I'm getting worse, Smiley. I'm barely holding on. Did you see what I did last week? I caught Karina's bike on fire... and all I could do was sit there and watch it burn." The newest voice in Michael Kriegman's head decides to weigh in with his own opinionShe was a hot little number. You should've offered her a ride on the Jack Express... and by Jack Express... I'm talking about my penis. "Shut up, Jack." C'mon guys... we're in Rio! Let me out, let's go have some fun... "Last time you got out I ended up at the clinic getting treated for syphilis, gonorrhea, and crabs." I promise to use a condom this time, I swear. Even though it takes away the romance and kills the sensation. "Goddammit, no. You stuck my dick into someone called Penicillin Penny, for fuck's sake! Now shut the fuck up!" Killjoy... Jack's voice sputters into silence. "What am I going to do, Smiley? I can't afford to buy her a new bike... Between the back taxes I owed, the child support, and the lawyer I hired so I could..." He stops for a moment, leaning his head back forward, letting a deep sigh escape. "So I could convince Suzanne to let me see little Eddie, the little bit I got from the GTT and the signing bonus I got for coming back to PRIME, I'm pretty much cashed out." Mr Smiley Face answers, his mental voice unusually solemn. Still freaked out from what happened with Eddie, Trash? "Freaked out? I'm scared, Smiley. You saw how close... you felt his evil... He tried to rise up. The first time I ever get to hold my own son, and Lazarus almost came forward. The threats... Smiley, he was going to hurt my son. MY SON!" With that, the Trashcan Man SLAMS a burn scarred fist into the wall in front of him, cracking the tile... and sending him slipping back, his feet skiddering on the slick bathtub as he tried to regain his balance... and fails as reaches out, grabs the shower curtain, ripping it off the rod and pulling it down on top of him as he lands ass first. The only sound that can be heard is the shower spattering against the plastic shower curtain for a moment, then Mr Smiley Face's high pitched cackling fills their head. I suppose Laz deserves a spanking, but if you bruise his tailbone, you bruise all of ours, ya big dumb palooka The Trashcan Man abashaedly pulls the shower curtain off of him and slowly sits up with a light groan, rubbing the back of his head. "Seriously Smiley, what am I going to do? It's Karina, you know she's going to want to explode all over me..." I'd like to explode all over her face. And by explode, I mean... "Shut the fuck up, Jack" interrupts the Trashcan Man. "Last thing I need is to make new enemies on my first night back. She'll want to kick my ass, and then I'd have to put her down, and I don't want to do that." Don't worry, I'll watch out for ya Trash. After all, who can resist my beautiful smile? "Your smile is some ink on a yellow piece of cardboard." IRRESISTABLE ink. On IRRESISTABLE cardboard C'mon guys, give me five minutes with her and she'll be eating out of my hand. And I'll be eating... "Finish that line and I'll burn all your Girls Gone Wild tapes." Not my inspirational work out videos! Trashy's head enjoys some welcome silence as we fade to black...
