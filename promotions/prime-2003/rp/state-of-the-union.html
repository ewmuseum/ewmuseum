State of the Union

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Now God, Vice President Christ, members of the RISP, distinguished citizens and fellow citizens, every year, by law and by custom, we meet here to consider the state of the union. This year, we gather in this chamber deeply aware of decisive days that lie ahead. As a wrestler, a deity, and a living legend I have served you well. Make no mistake about the challenges I have over come to keep PRIME both strong and fair. With out Hoyt Williams and the RISP, sadly, PRIME would have gone the way of PCW, OSW, FUSE, AWC, and PTC. It’s true you have my word. However in these days of light we can not forget the forth coming darkness. We still have failed to have a champion in the universal ranks worthy of the RISP. My friends in the coming year I will change that. I will work to once again bring pride, dignity, and respectability to PRIME. When I was Universal Champion, any member of our roster from the best to the Olsig, all they had to do was mention PRIME and they would be accepted, respected, and known to the highest of the wrestling world no matter where they went. Men fought hard to join the ranks of champions doing all they can not to be a main player but simple to play in the arena of God’s known as Prime. Strict immigration policy was implemented to restrict and weed out those not willing to commit fully to PRIME. My friends those restrictions must be torn down to build forward. During my title tenure feds knew that PRIME was stamped by the protection of the RISP and the greatness of the Hoyt Williams leadership. They knew that any ill will placed upon a servant of Hoyt would be met with ten times the force they put out. We were the crown jewel and all glory started and ended with PRIME. I left PRIME thinking it was in good hands and I failed the people of the organization. Then upon my return I was forced into exile by a benevolent and evil dictator. That dictator is no longer and I have returned for the betterment of this federation. By the grace of Hoyt we have removed all political opposition from the stoner that was Nova to the management that was Worthless. Let it be known from this day forward that he whom messes with the Williams vanishes like the others. Some might call this a good record. I call it a good start. Tonight I ask my fellow PRIMEATES to join me in rebuilding the very structure we have eroded. The shine that was once PRIME has lost its luster. Some refuse to acknowledge it, others blame the wrestling economy. I blame us the roster. It’s true in the last year we witnessed the collapse of the once mecca of our community the PTC. We have seen a wide spread decline in our sport for the last two years. Some community’s are doing better then ours, but all are struggling. As the president of e-wrestling, the PONTIFF of PRIME, the Bringer of Brutality, the King of Kings, and the father of the modern trash talk I say we regroup and build not with the faces of the old, but rather the vibrancy of the youth. Besides MY history I say we forget the past, as we are not what we one were. I say tomorrow starts today. With a smaller roster all of whom no doubt throwing their alliance behind me, we shall start building the road of tomorrow tonight. I vow to work with the new members of the roster and teach them the ways of the RISP and the greatness that is Hoyt Williams. Imperium was the strength of the past in PRIME and friends tonight I announce that it will also be the strength of the future. The Fatherhood of God and the Brotherhood of Man we will ride like the four horsemen of the apocalypse policing the roster and ruling with an iron fist. Friends you have been warned; you ride with the RISP or you feel the wrath of no mortal man but rather the infinite and creative fist of GOD himself. Let it be known that as a former Universal Champion I have yet to receive my rematch on a Pay-Per-View card. I have a lot of lawyers and most of them are Jewish. That means they will prevail, and I shall receive the retribution that is not expected but rather earned and deserved. Sadly PRIME is no longer a superpower as all superpowers have gone the way of quality reading on the internet. We need to stop acting as if we still are and act like a hungry dog fighting for meat or a hooker on the street fighting for a penis. God is watching and Hoyt Williams is listening. We shall strive and we shall grow in the glory of my greatness. Thank you and may God bless you. HW
