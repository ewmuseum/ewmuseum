Time To Relax!

26 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 26th August 2005 The time had finally arrived. The moment he had been waiting for for so long, he could barley recall. It was finally time for him to step foot in a PRIME ring for the first time in his career. Actually know he came to think about it, it was the first time in a long while that he would have to step foot inside a wrestling ring and the thought scared the living crap out of him. He had been more into street fighting now and he didn't know if he could even remember how to hit the ropes correctly. This was all starting to become a little daunting and he was set to make his debut appearance at the Tropical Turmoil PPV this Sunday in Hawaii. This wasn't no ordinary debut though, this wasn't no time filler on ReVolution, no matter how much he wanted it to be. This was a PPV match and he needed to treat it like his life depended on it. Well with the talent he has left inside a wrestling ring, his life could very well depend on it. He wasn't facing no rookie talents like himself, in fact he was the only one there that hadn't been inside a PRIME ring before and that made him feel very nauseous. He was set to face Dave Gibson, a living legend inside the squared circle and his tag team partner, Kyle Lamen, who was twice, no three times the wrestler he could ever hope of being. His head was full with all sorts of eratic thoughts about this weekend. What would happen if Tom went down seriously injured and I had to beat them both on my own ? What would the crowd make of me and would they see me as a freak ? Will I be able.......... Basically he didn't need to be thinking like this just mear days from the big day, he needed to get away and relax. But where could he go ? What could possibly keep his mind away from these crazy and foolhardy thoughts ? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tuesday 23rd August, 6:34pm. Unknown Voice: GET YOUR HEAD DOWN! What are you ? Fucking stupid ? They had been there a couple of minutes, just trying to get a clear view of their surroundings. They looked down from atop the giant hill and spotted slight movement below them. Mark didn't want to move now, for fear of being shot and he could see why Paulio was so suddenly pissed off at him for kneeling up to get a better view. Mark knew they had to move soon though to evade being spotted. That was the aim of the game after all. Blackwolf: We need to get moving, they'll spot us if we stay here. Paulio: Mark, they would probably have spot us if we stayed down there, you're 6'11'' for Christ sake, they'd have to be damn blind not to spot us. Thats why we have to make sure we're not in there field of view and this is one of the best spots around here. If we can't see them then they can't see us. Blackwolf: Who the hell taught you that one ? Children think that when there playing damn peak-a-boo and hide behind their hands. Where up on top of a damn hill, don't you think we'd be safer if we got to lower ground. Besides what if they we're behind us. Paulio: Ok smart ass, I didn't think about that. Blackwolf: Come on! Mark and Paulio begin to descend down the hill, keeping as quiet as humanly possible, which isn't very quiet when your a 400 lb man. They reached the bottom where a small hut was stationed in amongst a vast array of trees and bushes, it was the perfect place to hide while they figured out a course of action. Paulio crept in front of Mark and, keeping his back to the wall, made his way around to a small window on the west side of the hut. He crouched down and peered through the window to make sure the coast was clear. Finding no body inside he motioned to Mark, by waving his hand, letting him know that it was safe to move. Paulio placed his hands on the window ledge and leaped up, jumping through the window and crashing back down inside the small hut. Mark just shook his head as he made his way around the front and opened the door, making his way inside. Paulio was already seated on a small wooden bench at the far side of the room, with his gun resting on his lap, his head in his hands. Mark crossed the room and reached up, pulling the wooden shutter down over the window to stop people from looking in and spotting them. That would just make them look stupid and it would have been kind of idiotic to be caught out like that. He then placed his gun down against the wall and remained standing. He knew if he sat down on the bench it would probably buckle underneath him, he hasn't had much look with wooden benches in the past, or anything wooden for that matter, tables, chairs, wooden crates, but that's beyond the point. Blackwolf: What the hell are we going to do now, we can't stay here forever, somebody is bound to notice us sooner or later. Paulio: Calm down, I'm thinking. Mark couldn't remain still as he paced around the room trying to keep his mind busy. He wanted to come out here with Paulio to take his mind of Sunday and standing around in a hut so small it made him feel claustrophobic wasn't what he considered soothing. He picked his gun back up and began to load it up with all the paint balls he had left (yes there paint balling if you haven't guessed already :->). He only had five paint balls left, but loaded them into his rifle anyway. Blackwolf: Erm.....do you know where I can get some more of these things, I seem to be running a bit short and I know these won't last very long. Whats with them only giving us fifty balls anyway, like thats gonna be enough. Paulio: Fifty is by far enough. I have loads left, what the hell happened to yours ? Blackwolf: I was shooting birds. I got bored when you left me on top of that hill. I told you I needed to get my mind off Sunday and leaving me alone up there didn't help, I couldn't stop thinking about it. Paulio: So you decided to start shooting birds ? No wonder there was all that commotion. We'll have to get moving now, they'll be looking for us around here. You got any ideas ? Blackwolf: Yeah Paulio: And what's that ? Blackwolf: McDonalds. Lets screw this shit, leave our guns here and go to McDonalds, I could just about go for a Big Mac right now, what do you say ? Paulio: Why does everything you think of always come down to food ? Listen if we do this right, we won't have to use many paint balls and those you have left should do, so long as you don't go around shooting for the fun of it again. Blackwolf: Hey listen, I didn't even want to come here in the first place, this was your idea and its not helping me relax. I said I wanted to relax, not be on edge all the time. Damn my nerves are bad as it is without all this shit pilled on top as well. We can't go anywhere without thinking we're gonna be shot. You know what I don't even care anymore, I'm just gonna go outside and scream at the top of my lungs so they do shoot me, that way I could at least go back to the foyer and put my feet up....... Paulio holds his hands up in protest, trying to persuade Mark without having to use words to do it. He wasn't so good at talking people around and he knew Mark wouldn't have bought any cock and bull story he'd come up with. Mark looks at Paulio, who's face looked just about ready to swell up with tears, before turning his back and pushing the door open. Mark didn't walk off though, instead he turned around to face Paulio and motioned with his head for him to get up and follow him. Blackwolf:......Ok then fine, but can we hurry this up, I'm starting to get restless stood around here, my legs are hurting. Paulio leaps up from his seat and grabs hold of his gun with both hands, following Mark out of the door. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tuesday 23rd August, 8:15pm. Paulio: See that was ok wasn't it ? We did great, there was only two left when I got shot and if you didn't come back to me like a stupid dummy we could have won. Mark didn't answer as he bit down into a large Big Mac. Paulio had finally agreed to go to McDonalds and Mark looked a lot more relaxed and comfortable being there. He hadn't looked up at Paulio since they sat down at a small rounded table in the corner of the room, preferring instead to keep his eyes on his food. Paulio: Jesus Christ man, calm down. Its not going anywhere. You'll give yourself a heart attack shoveling it down like that. Mark wipes his mouth and looks up at Paulio who hadn't even touched his cheeseburger and fries. Blackwolf: You gonna eat that ? Paulio: Here take it, I'm not hungry. Mark reaches over the table and grips the fast food bag with his huge palm. He puts his hand inside and pulls out the cheeseburger, tossing the fries to the side. He takes a huge bite out of the greasy burger, letting the juices flow down his chin until it drips off and falls down like a lonely teardrop before crashing off the table. Paulio: So, if you don't mind me asking, why did you not want to think about the match this Sunday ? We've never been out like that since you we're a little kid. There must be something wrong. Blackwolf: There's nothing wrong, I just don't want to think about it and I thought going somewhere with you would help take my mind of things. To be honest with you it kinda makes me feel nauseous and that turns me off my food. You know how pissed off I become when I can't eat my food because it all comes back up. Paulio: Why would you feel sick ? This is gonna be the biggest day of your life if anything you should be feeling elated. Just think what it will do to your career if you beat these two next week. By the way have you spoken to Tom yet ? Mark engulfs the last of the cheeseburger, wiping his mouth with the napkin supplied. Blackwolf: No I've not. I thought it would be best if I left him alone to get ready for the match, he doesn't need me phoning him up every five minutes to ask how he's doing. Besides we're going to get together before Sunday and I thought it would be best to forget about it until then. Paulio: Whats making you feel sick ? Your not scared are you ? Mark slams his fists down into the table, rising to his feet and staring a hole through Paulio. Small particles of cheeseburger start to fly towards Paulio's face as Mark begins to go red in the face. Blackwolf: SCARED! FUCKING SCARED! DO I LOOK SCARED!?! Paulio goes white in the face crunching his eyes like he was ready to take a full blown smack to the side of the head, but none was coming. Mark just stood over him trembling with anger. Paulio holds his hands up in front of Mark, trying to calm him down. Paulio: Look I'm sorry, please calm down, you'll get us kicked out. I didn't mean to say that, I don't know why it even came out of my mouth. Blackwolf: I'm sorry I reacted like that. Look I'm gonna get going, its getting late and I need to be up early tommorow. The carpet people are coming, I'm finally going to be getting a carpet laid out downstairs. Its been great, thanks for this I needed it. Paulio: Its ok Mark, you know I'm here for you anytime you need me, and I'll take any excuse to go paint balling. Mark grabs his coke and pushes his chair underneath the table. He checks his watch one last time before reaching out a hand for Paulio to shake. The pair shake hands and Mark turns to face the door. Blackwolf: Shit sorry, you need a ride ? Paulio: No man I'm cool, I'm gonna go see Sara soon anyway, she said she wanted to see me tonight for some reason. I hope I haven't done anything wrong, god knows what it is with her. Blackwolf: Sara ? Didn't you break up with her like 6 months ago ? Paulio: Yeah I did, but she wants to see me says its important, thanks anyway. I'll give you a ring sometime next week to see how you got on. Blackwolf: You're not going to be watching it ? Paulio: No man I can't, sorry, I'm gonna be out, but I'll catch the repeats if the news is good. Blackwolf: Ok man its cool. I'll see you later Pauly. Mark makes his way across the room, pushing the glass door open and making his way out into the open. He lifts his hand up holding his coke, waving a goodbye to Paulio, who nods in the direction of Mark, but it was too late, Mark had already left, leaving Paulio alone. Paulio: He needs to calm down, whatever the result this Sunday, he should be ok when his debut match is over. Jesus, I've never seen him like that ever and I don't like it.
