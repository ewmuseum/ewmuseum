The Frog Prince

3 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 3, 2008 The black ’67 Mustang rumbles down a long dirt road, engulfed in a perpetuate cloud of dust as it winds through a long stretch of Texan landscape. It rounds a sharp curve, popping over a rise and finally coming within view of the large and impressive ranch house which is its destination. A growl roars out from the Mustang’s engine, a warning of the sudden inclination in speed. As the car draws ever closer to the house, the wicked vibrations of Rob Zombie can be heard, and felt, as they thump from the speakers. With a final roar, the Mustang pulls to a stop next to a red F-250. Tyler Rayne steps out of the car, squinting in the morning sun. Even in the rising heat of an unusually warm spring day, he conceals himself within that ever present leather jacket. Boots fall hard across the ground, kicking dust up around the frayed ends of his jeans. He hits the porch at a half-jog, hopping over the stairs entirely on his way to the door. Tyler lands lightly, however, and immediately begins to slow himself. His steps. His breathing. A failed attempt to slow the sudden pounding in his chest. His right hand balls into a fist, knuckles rising to rap upon the door. But it swings open just before he has the chance to knock. There’s a certain resemblance that makes him smile. Something in the way her jaw is set. The structure of her cheekbones. And though he knows the woman with the short blonde hair isn’t who he’s come to see, it’s hard to not recognize the disapproving glare in those cold blue eyes as something decidedly Lindsay Troy. Must be a family expression. "Alaina." The striking blue eyes trail down from his awkward grin to absorb the whole package. Tyler can feel her judging him as the gaze travels across his body, and he does his best not to fidget uncomfortably. She finishes her perusal and brings those swimming blue orbs back to meet his dusty browns. The look is a little softer, though not much, her expression now turned to more question than accusation. "You’re the bachelorette auction guy." He notes the lack of actual question in her query. She seems to be affirming the fact to herself more than asking him to do so for her. He nods anyway. "Right. Name’s Tyler. Tyler Rayne. I work at PRIME with Uni." "Huh?" He laughs in spite of the situation. "Lindsay. We’re, um…friends. I was kind of hoping to see her." That disapproving look doesn’t take long to reassert itself. "How did you know where I live?" He smiles, though she seems none too reassured by the expression. "I find people. It's what I do. Part of the job." "As a wrestler?" "No. I have...other jobs. It's not that hard, actually. Public records will give you pretty much everything you need to know about a person. Just need to have a name and city to start with. Um...look, about Lindsay..." "Well, I’m sorry, but she’s not—" "MOMMY! MOMMY! LOOK AT ME!!" The pitter patter of tiny little feet follows the cry, and a very small girl with a very large smile comes racing out from a hallway toward her mother. Tyler notes the horrified look that flashes across Alaina’s face, though the mother does well to cover it quickly. After all, how would you feel if your two-year-old daughter was about to meet Tyler Mother Fuckin’ Rayne? "I A PINCESS." The girl hops the last few inches to her mommy, landing quite authoritatively upon the floor, the frilled bottom of her blue Cinderella dress floating about her knees. She spins in so many circles it makes Tyler dizzy, but the munchkin seems none the worse for wear when she stops to flash a hopeful smile up at her mother. "SEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE??!" "Yes, honey, you look very beautiful. Now go back in the—" The little girl senses her mother’s nervousness, and for the first time, turns her shining blue eyes to the door to take in Tyler Rayne. The girl seems rather uninterested in his wardrobe, almost turning to dismiss him entirely before she catches sight of that wild mess of hair and captivating grin. The girl smiles back, and with that small show of approval, Tyler slowly lowers himself to a crouch before the little girl. "Hello there, little princess. My name’s Tyler. What’s your name?" He holds his hand out for a shake, but the girl suddenly becomes a little bundle of embarrassment. Her hands clasped tightly behind her back, she twirls one bare foot about the floor while the rest of her body slowly rocks in place. Though her head hangs low, those blazing blue eyes flash between glimpses of the ground and glimpses of his face, peering through tresses of falling blonde hair. She looks a lot like her mother. "CeCe." "Well that’s a very pretty name, Princess CeCe. Goes very well with that pretty dress, too, if I do say so myself." "Cecelia, come on, it’s time to go back—" As if her mother’s words were some sort of alarm, the girl suddenly bursts from her cocoon of shyness to return to that bouncing bundle of blue she’d been just a moment before. "Are you here to see Aunt Linny?" "Yes I am. Is she here?" The little girl nods vigorously. But her enthusiasm quickly fades, the nod stopping altogether as a shadow falls over the girls face. The tone with which she utters her next two words is damn near heartbreaking. "Linny sad." "Well, that’s why I’m here, little princess. I’m going to cheer her up." Her smile returns, though lacking some of the confidence it had before. "Are you a pince? Like Uncy Joey?" Tyler damn near has to bite his tongue to keep from lashing out at the mention of Joey Melton. His own dealings with the man have been something less than pleasant. With a concentrated effort, Tyler returns the smile to his face, shaking his head slowly but surely. "No. I’m not like Uncle Joey. Don’t think I’d make for a very good prince, either." "Why not?" Kids and their questions. He pauses, unsure of how to answer. The retort wasn’t entirely unexpected, though all the same he seems to be ill prepared for it. Why not, indeed. "Well...I think I’m more like a frog than a prince." Little Cecelia’s face contorts to disgust, as if she just bit into something sour. Her tongue sticks out the side of her mouth and she shakes her head in displeasure. "Frogs yucky." He laughs. "Well, yes. Sometimes. But other times they can be princes in disguise. And if a princess can see through the yuckiness, to the prince within, and she gives the frog a kiss…BAM! The frog turns into a prince." "Weely?" "Yep. That’s how all the best princesses get their princes. By kissing frogs." The girl is beyond giddy at this new tidbit of information. She begins hopping up and down on her feet, turning in mid hop to tug on her mother’s pant leg. "Mommy! Mommy! Can I go kiss some frogs now? PLLLEEEEAAASSSSEEEE!!!" Tyler cringes at the request. Not exactly what he’d had in mind. He pretends not to notice the menacing glare Alaina casts down in his direction. "How ‘bout we talk about that later, honey? When Daddy gets home. Maybe he can take you out and help you find a prince." "YAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYYYY!!!!!!!!!!!!" And as quick as she came, Cecilia pitter patters her way back down the hall once more, disappearing around that corner and out of sight. Tyler slowly stands, rubbing the back of his head nervously and attempting not to look Alaina in the eyes. "Uh…sorry." "Mm hmm. You just better be able to do the same for my sister." He doesn’t even attempt to hide the smile. "I’ll do my best." She spites him with a very stern, motherly look. His expression changes immediately, from satisfied relief to a confident resolve. "I mean…I will. Absolutely." Alaina nods and jerks a thumb to the stairs. "Guest room. Last door on the left." He returns the nod, raising a hand to give her an appreciative pat on the arm. However, halfway through the motion, he thinks better of it, his arm hanging awkwardly in the air for a moment before he steps inside and marches toward the stairs. He passes through a foyer on his way to the stairs, eyes pausing for just a moment at the tussled brown hair and frazzled expression of a man who’s been working quite diligently through the night. Tyler’s had enough of those nights himself to recognize that weary but determined glean in the man’s green eyes. He gives a slight nod the man, who looks up from his laptop to take one of those sweeping glances of Rayne. Alaina smiles at the man as she pulls a chair from the table and sits down beside him. "Alaina, I didn't realize modern science was able to raise Steve McQueen from the dusty grave depths." She lets loose with a little laugh, and even Tyler himself has a chuckle at the comment. "Certainly been called worse in my time. You are?" "Clean." Another snooty regard of Tyler’s person. "But if you’re looking for a name, it’s Clay Darcy. Ms. Troy’s—" The man raises a finger to pause the conversation, then immediately turns his attention to the tiny voice emanating from the Bluetooth at his ear. Tyler lingers for a second longer before turning his own attention back to previous endeavors. He bounds up the stairs three at a time, not bothering to look back at what was surely another disapproving look from Lindsay’s sister. Tyler follows Alaina’s directions to the guest room, careful not to traipse over a few dolls CeCe must have left in the hallway. He carefully turns the knob on the door, opening it quietly so as not to disturb the woman inside. The Queen of the Ring stands with her back to the door, arms crossed over her chest as she gazes out an open window into the wide expanse of her sister’s property. A slight change in posture is enough indication that she realizes someone is at the door, though she does not immediately turn to acknowledge the presence. He leans against the jamb, satisfied with nothing more than the chance to observe the finer details of Lindsay Troy. The same way her long hair always seems to flow off her shoulders and down her back, falling in a similar fashion regardless of situation. The way she unconsciously tends to put all of her weight toward the left, cocking her body and stretching an already incredibly long leg impossibly longer. He drinks her in, as he always does when he thinks she’s not looking. Lindsay finally turns from the window, stuttering only slightly in her step when she realizes just who it is at the door, though she barely gives him a glance as she limps toward the bed to take a seat. "Oh, I know I must be on meds if you're standing in the doorway." "Didn't figure you to be the self-medicating type." "Yeah, well..." her voice trails off, fighting for comprehension through the fog of science's little wonders. "Clay said he wouldn't get me out of the electric chair next week if I didn't take them." He chuckles; damn her ability to be humorous despite still being pissed at him. "What are you doing in my sister's house, Tyler." It's purposely not phrased as a question. "And how did you even know I was here." He flashes her that infamous smile, pushing up from the door jamb and stepping into the room. "I have my ways." The less than amused expression on her face is all too reminiscent of her sister. He throws up both hands in surrender. "It wasn’t that hard. I knew your sister lived out here somewhere. That whole auction thing. Just a matter of tracking her down. Which is…kind of what I do." "A regular Dick Tracy you are." "I prefer Bruce Wayne, but whoever your detective of choice may be. I, um…I’m sorry. Apparently I suck at this whole hero thing." She cocks a questioning eyebrow, but makes no other attempts to respond. "The, uh…auction thing. Since I’m here and all. Didn’t really go as planned. Nor did the Halo. And, well…I should’ve been there last week. Should’ve done…something. So, um…I’m—" "Ty." He pauses. They turn to look at each other. He almost chokes as, for the first time, he can see the sheen of defeat in her eyes. Worn. Tired. Beaten. She offers a half-hearted smile to conceal her moment of weakness. "Stop rambling." He smiles. A moment passes in silence before he fumbles within the pocket of his jacket and pulls out a ziplock bag. "Got you a present." "You brought me weed?" He looks down at the bag and frowns. "Well…no. But with the whole Nova thing, maybe I should—" A sharp glare from the Queen brings that sentence to an abrupt end. "—Just shut up and tell you what this really is." He clears a spot on the nightstand to dump out the contents of the bag. A handful of leaves, all different shades of green. A mortar and pestle. And his lighter. Without bothering to explain, he begins tearing a few of the leaves into tiny pieces and dropping them into the mortar. "There’s this African witch doctor I know. Taught me how to do this back in…ninety-five? Ninety-six? Anyway, the leaves obviously have some medicinal qualities. Each one alone doesn’t do much more than leave a bitter taste in your mouth…" He finishes tearing the leaves, and now begins grinding them to mush with the pestle. "…but together they make for a pretty potent balm. Course, my maladies were a little more…severe at the time, but over the years I’ve found a few good uses for this stuff. Rope burn, for one." With the leaves effectively ground to putty, Tyler ignites his lighter and touches the flame to the mush. A puff of yellow smoke rises from the concoction. He sticks two fingers into the mortar, scooping up a helping of the brownish goo onto his fingertips. With a sly smile he turns to her, closing the few steps between them and kneeling in front of her. She defiantly shakes her head. "No." "Come on. Trust me." "Why should I?" Those hazel eyes turn to stone, a hardened reminder that their discussion two weeks ago is still very fresh in her mind, and his actions aren't forgotten. "Because I just spent the last fourteen hours driving across three states to find this place on the half-assed notion you might be here. Because, despite my best intentions, I suck at being there for you. And if I can’t be there when it happens, you could at least let me be here after. You're pissed at me because of Danny. You're pissed at me because of Wade. And you have -- I don't blame you for feeling that way. But at least let me try to redeem myself." She sighs and he gently makes the move to grab her hand. Their fingertips touch and something passes between them that all the callous demeanors and smart ass insults could never hide. His cheeks flush, if only for the briefest second. He doesn’t look into her eyes, but rather busies himself with gently rubbing that goo across the rope burn on her wrist. Almost instantly, the burning and itching beneath her irritated skin begins to fade. As he very carefully works the concoction into her wounds, a cool, almost minty feeling begins to seep up her arm. It’s quite…unnatural. But soothing. Certainly a relief. And now, without the irritating pain there to distract her, without the incessant witty retorts and well-intentioned insults being hurled…there is just the two of them. Lindsay Troy, a woman more hurt than any could care or think to imagine. A woman who seemingly has it all, yet feels she has nothing. There she is, sitting on a bed in her sister’s guest room, trying not to stare too hard at the man kneeling before her. Tyler Rayne. A ridiculous, brazen rogue with seemingly no redeeming qualities. A man who, by all rights, should be celebrating at the top of the world. Instead he is here. Alone. With her. His glorious moment in the sun shadowed by her own hurt. But he doesn’t blame her. He doesn’t even seem to care. His only thought since becoming the most sought after talent in professional wrestling…has been of her. He’s done nothing but try to find her. Now he has. Now he’s here. Now there’s nothing between them but the silence of feelings far too hard and complicated to express. "Your hands are shaking." "No they’re not." The denial comes spilling from her lips before she can think to stop the subtle trembling of her fingers. She feels the rough touch of his fingertips as they glide ever so gently over her smooth, olive skin. Eyes fall to watch as he wraps his left hand around her fingers, squeezing softly to stop the shakes. She swallows hard, attempting to quell the rising tide within her stomach. The sudden inclination of beating within her chest. His other hand rises as well, careful not to disturb the raw and blooded skin around her wrist. His hands encompass hers, sending through them a wave of comfort, a calming and exhilarating wave that even this suave and charming rogue could have never rightly put to words. His blazing brown eyes rise to meet her own confused gaze and an indescribable spark shoots between the two. A magnet force drawing them ever closer… "Just once I’d like to attract a nice man." "I’m nice men." "No you’re not. You’re—" His lips touch hers and the world becomes a still frame. A moment on hold, like the breath in their lungs. A moment that’s been building since the very first step he ever took in PRIME. Since the first night her name ever parted his lips. The ones touching hers. Right now. At this very moment. Held for what could be forever before she presses back, a quiet submission to the urges they’ve both been suppressing for far too long. The kiss, like his grip upon her hand, is gentle and caring and full of a warmth they’ve both been missing as of late. It is everything she needs, and nothing anyone would have expected from the likes of him. Everything she hoped he wasn’t, and yet, somehow knew he was. Nothing lasts forever. Even if he wants it to. This kiss. This moment. It all has to end. And when it does, when he pulls himself together enough to pull away from her…it is absolutely impossible to pull his eyes away. His right hand reaches to her face, though the left still lingers there with her twice trembling fingers. His pinky rounds lightly around the line of her jaw, fingers falling into place around her face. His thumb strokes ever slightly across her cheek. When he looks at her with eyes unflinching, he does not stare into the determined eyes of the Queen of the Ring. He’s not here to cast gaze upon a Universal Champion. He stares into the face of a magnificent woman. He absorbs her. Soaks in every detail. The beauty and majesty and wonder that is the woman, Lindsay Troy. "Don’t tell Wade. Don’t tell anybody." He smiles. Partially because he’s been waiting for this moment for months. Partially because she can’t keep from being herself for more than a handful of seconds. She can’t keep her mind from wandering to her friends. Their feelings. How best to please everyone else. Even here, in this most personal of moments, she finds a way to be selfless. It’s the way she’s wired. To think about others first. It’s part of the reason he can’t stop thinking about her. "Cross my heart and hope to die." She rolls her eyes as he rises from his knees, still refusing to let go of her hand. He sits next to her on the bed, wrapping his free arm around her shoulder. Pulling him in closer. It’s such a stupid thing to have said. Such a childish, ridiculous response to a very serious concern. How very like him. For once, she doesn’t fight it. She lets herself fall into his arm. Lets her head fall onto his chest. Lets herself fall into his embrace. Such a stupid, stupid thing to say. But possibly more than anyone else, the dumb bastard actually means it. He’d take it to the grave if she asked. For her. He’d do it for her. The thought is almost as comforting as he is. Tyler Rayne. With a smile, Lindsay Troy closes her eyes.
