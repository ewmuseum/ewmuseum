Date: ReVolution 173 Tyler Johnson is not your average man. On a good day, he's an asshole, and on any other day, well, let's just say you wouldn't want your eyes to meet his. The bald-headed man sits in a chair, a magazine open with a number of hot girls staring back at him without any clothing on. His dark blue eyes scan the pages as his brow furrows and he loses himself into another world. His legs, covered by a pair of black pants, are crossed over one another in this awkward position inside of a, for now empty room. His right hand rests comfortably on his hip, grabbing at something unseen. Behind him, a door opens and closes with a loud click. The sound of heels clicking against the concrete echoes throughout the room and then it's followed by the sound of fingers dancing across a keyboard furiously. "Fuckin' shit," the woman mutters underneath her breath before looking over at Tyler and rolling her eyes. As usual, she thinks to herself, he couldn't give a damn in the world. Her eyes return to the screen as she scans through the words shouting back at her. As the seconds go on the expression on her face becomes worse until she reaches the end of the article and slams her hand on the table. She begins to walk over to Tyler and takes another look at him. "Think you might want to do something other then read a magazine? Maybe have a care or thought in the world?" she asks him frankly, but he just ignores her. She waits there for a moment, her blonde hair tied up into a ponytail while her hands rest on her long blue skirt and her breasts eagerly try to jump out of her low cut top. As he just reads through the magazine she shakes her head and begins to walk away from Tyler. "Typical, Tyler, just typical. We've got people dying out there and your only interest is in that damn titty magazine," she shouts at him as she walks towards another chair in the nearly empty and void room. "Maybe if you stood there without any clothes on I could get interested in something Samantha," he responds to her in this gravely, low tone without his eyes ever moving from the magazine. She turns around and shoots him a look before walking back across the room and snatching the magazine out of his hands before throwing it on the ground and stepping on it. In the blink of an eye, he explodes out of his chair and towers over her with confidence and power. "What the fuck is your problem?!" he yells at her, his voice filling up the entire room. She doesn't back down an inch though as she cocks her arm back and slaps him across the face! His head recoils from the shot, but as he gets his senses back to him he cocks his own arm back. "ENOUGH!" another voice enters the room and stops Tyler from striking back at Samantha. As Tyler looks to his left he sees a man walking from the darkness with this menacing look to this posture and face. With dark brown hair that's cut rather short and dark brown eyes that pierce from a moment's look, Derek Johnson is not a man to be taken lightly, especially with him being leader of this group. As he walks into the room, two others walk into the room behind him, a man and a woman who seem to be in a relationship of some sorts. Tyler's eyes then return to Samantha and the two look at each other for a moment as Derek walks up to the two of them. "We've got more to worry about then turning on each other," he says in this rich baritone voice. "Whatever issues you two have, drop them. Don't have time for this bullshit or I'll put a bullet into both of your skulls without a moment's notice or any ounce of remorse. Got it?" he asks them not expecting an answer in the least bit. Samantha looks over at Derek before rolling her eyes at him and walking back to the chair she had her eyes set on before. "Not like you would know what to do if I was standing there naked anyways," she says under her breath as she takes her seat and looks around at the room. The five of them had come so far and yet had seen so much pain in the world that it had affected them, more so as of late. Derek walks around the room with his arms crossed while the other two people in the room just kind of stand there holding each other's hand with this dreamy look in their eyes. Then out of nowhere, Derek slams his fist into the wall and yells out in fury. Everyone turns and looks at him, of course, and just wonder what's wrong with their fearless leader. "Some days, I don't think you guys get it," he screams at them as he turns around and starts walking back towards them. "People are dead. Our own are dying. What part of that don't you get? We're a dying breed. People like us are dying. People not like us are dying as well. This world is three inches away from exploding on itself and we're the only ones that can do anything about it. So, how about we stop trying to rip out each other's throats and start focusing on taking out the enemy?!" he continues yelling at them, his tone and intensity increasing with each syllable. The other four just stand there kind of frozen, but as he finishes, they nod their head in agreement. "What about--?" the other female in the room begins to ask before being cut off by Derek rather rudely. "Eliza, no!" he yells at her, obviously having been down this road before and not being a big fan of the idea she has in mind. Eliza looks back at him in shock and pain, upset about the way she's being talked to. As Samantha takes a look at Eliza's face, she stands up and turns her attention to Derek. "She's right, Derek. We were told if anything happened to her that we should contact him," she tells him calmly, but Derek is already shaking his head no. "She's dead, Samantha." Those words send a chill into the room as Derek and Samantha's eyes are locked for what seems like an eternity. The tension in the room turns up another notch as the other three in the room just watch uncomfortably as the two seem to be having their own war right in front of each other. "You don't have to remind me of that," Samantha responds tersely as she walks up to Derek. "What makes you think she knew what to do then?" he asks her in response, but Samantha just shrugs her shoulders. "He left us if you remember correctly." "He might have, Derek," Samantha begins to respond. "But, you should do enough to respect her wishes at least. He could help us fill that void and give us the leadership that we need around here." It's those words that set him off the most as his eyes get large and the veins in his neck being to start popping. "I am the leader around here, Samantha! Don't you for--" "You're only the leader around here because she died!" she yells back at him while sticking her finger in his face, refusing to back down on this. As she starts to fight back, refusing to be ignored or belittled because of this, the man holding Eliza's breaks their grip and rushes over to separate Samantha and Derek. "Come on, Samantha," he begins to tell her, but Samantha pushes him away before turning her frustration onto him. "Don't, James, just don't. You have no say in this matter!" she yells at him while James re-positions himself to try and keep the peace in this room. Samantha then points her finger back at James and starts jabbing it at him. "If you don't do this, James, I'll personally knock you down to the ground and step through your eye with my heels right here and now!" Behind her, Tyler sits up as Eliza looks on worried at the scene before her. Tyler's eyes get really large as he's finally interested in what's going on here. "Alright! This sounds like a good show, finally," he voices his opinion, but it's not an opinion that Samantha cares to hear as she turns around and begins to walk over towards Tyler as if she's about to kill him. "You son of a bitch," she growls at him, but before she can take more then three steps James has wrapped her arms around him and is pulling her away from the fray. She begins hitting him, but it's no use for James's strength as Derek’s just shakes his head as Eliza looks dead at him. "Derek...," she pleads with him softly. Derek just sighs as James puts Samantha down and blocks her from the rest of the room. "Fine, fine!" he says softly at first, but then yells out at everyone. The tension is obvious in his face as he has finally broken down on the stance he's kept for so long. He then begins to walk out of the room, not even interested in looking at any of them anymore. "Bring him in!" As Derek walks out of the room, Tyler follows right behind him, probably to go hit up a strip club. James looks around at the room and can't believe what he's just witnessed. He then looks back at Samantha and just has a million questions pooling inside of his head. "I don't know who this guy is, but I hope he's worth it," he says to her and she gives him this look to just shut the fuck up. She brushes past him and begins to walk towards the exit, but offers a few parting words. "Trust me, he is. He's worth it and then some." --- His red eyes seemingly gloss over the white board that's been sitting inside of this hidden room in his house for weeks now. Craig, sitting in a chair, just seems to be frustrated with himself as he finds himself trapped in a corner and not sure what to do now. Weeks ago, he had a plan that seemed to be working perfectly, but now he wasn't so certain. Something had thrown him off and he wasn't sure if it was the fact that revenge just wouldn't work any longer. For weeks now he had been gathering information, jogging his memory, and asking the right questions to people who didn’t exactly want to answer them. He had forgotten how persuasive physical punishment could be. As he looks over the whiteboard, certain names start popping out at him. Alexander Lewis. Alexandra Pierce. Malik. One name kept popping up at him and that would be Kristen. Someone who had been sent to protect him, but he wasn’t sure why. He had tried to examine that line, but had come up empty. With a pencil in one hand he can feel the anxiety building up inside of him before he snaps the pencil in half and throws the two pieces at the whiteboard, feeling no closer to figuring everything out then before. As he gets up and starts pacing around, the door to the room opens up and in walks the ever lovable, Mary-Lynn Mayweather. Craig is taken aback at first as she steps in and then is handed a cup of coffee as she smiles at him. “How the hell did you know I was in here?” he asks her, thinking no one knew about this room. It had taken him three weeks before he even knew it existed. “Saw you walking in there about a week ago. Decided you needed your peace and quiet for whatever, but finally figured out that you had to have something big going on in here for you walk in here two or three times a day,” she responds before leaning up against the wall and crossing her arms. Her eyes begin to look over the board, trying to make sense of what Craig was doing in here, but just by looking at the names she knew that this was a bigger mess then she was capable of handling. Her tongue rolls around in her mouth for a minute before noticing the broken pencils resting on the ground. “I see that the old “killing people” idea hasn’t worked out as well as you would’ve liked? What happened, discovered dead bodies don’t make great people to talk to?” she asks him sarcastically as Craig just rolls his eyes before sitting back down in the chair, his brain racing as if time was starting to wind down on him. “Look, I know you frown on those kinds of ideas, but it’s what I know. Okay? I’m not expecting you to represent me in court though, so you don’t have to worry about that,” he responds to her, his eyes fixated upon the whiteboard. She just shakes her head as she hits him in the back of the head. “I understand why you’re doing it. I’m not quite sure what I would do if the people closest to me were murdered. On top of that, my children? No, people have what’s coming to them if you ask me. Not a big fan of killing, but I can’t say you’re completely in the wrong here. Well, maybe if we’re talking about the law, and even then--,” she begins to respond to him, but Craig holds up his hand to stop her as she starts to ramble like a ranting woman. As he does so, he stands up and starts walking to his chair before drawing a big circle around Kristen’s name. “There,” he says. Mary-Lynn just looks at Craig with confusion, but interest all rolled into one. “There… what?” she asks him as he turns around and looks at her. “I’ve gotta find out more about her. I’ve known that, but if I hope to figure out those other strings… I need to figure out this one. I’ve been so focused on Alexander Lewis and Cozen that I haven’t thought about the piece that just doesn’t fit. Why was someone trying to protect me? That evening, they weren’t there to kill me, but to kill her. She died for me and I don’t know why,” he tells her while his arms starting moving along with his words. “So, where do you start?” she asks him and that’s when Craig just kind of deflates as he rests up against the wall next to her. “Honestly, I don’t have the slightest clue. I didn’t know her. I had never seen her be--,” he begins to respond to her question before Mary-Lynn’s eyes light up as if someone had put a flashlight inside of her skull. “Shut up…,” she tells him as she begins mouthing things to herself. Craig can only look on as he sticks his hands inside of his basketball shorts while he waits for Mary-Lynn to finish her train of thought. Then, she snaps her fingers and points at Craig. “I met her that one time, right?” she asks him and Craig can only nod his head, vaguely recalling an evening when Mary-Lynn and Craig had visited the bar to have a few drinks. Mary-Lynn then grabs the doorknob and opens the door as fast as she can before bolting down the hallway. Craig follows out behind her as fast as he can before turning down another hallway only to find Mary-Lynn opening up a closet and pulling a box out of it. “I know it’s in here somewhere,” she mutters to herself before pulling a picture out of the box. She then shoves it under Craig’s nose as he tries to adjust his head fast enough to make out the picture. “That’s her, right? Between you and this other random guy?” It takes a minute before Craig is nodding his head, wondering when the hell this picture had been taken. “Mary-Lynn, how the hell did you find this?” he asks her and she just smiles sheepishly. “I’ve been doing some cleaning around the house. Figured that I should make myself useful if you’re going to just let me sleep here without paying any kind of rent,” she responds before putting her arms behind her back, embarrassed by that revelation and pleased at the same time for being able to help out Craig. “I just can’t figure out when this picture was taken. I don’t remember it. Or this guy next to her. I’m just drawing a blank,” he tells her and the smile on Mary-Lynn’s face disappears as it looks like that Craig is back to step one. “Sorry, I thought it would help…,” she says and as she does, Craig looks up and wraps his arms around her before kissing her on the forehead. “Mary-Lynn, you did great. I’ve got a guy who might be able to give me some information. Trust me, you did something real good here,” Craig tells her before breaking off the embrace. He then takes another look at the picture while shaking his head. Something about the other guy in the picture just threw him off and he couldn’t quite put his finger on it. As he begins walking away from Mary-Lynn, he thinks to himself about his past and how that past would affect his future. He just shakes his head as he puts the picture in his hand and just feels like things would only get worse before they got better. For now though, this lead would take him to the next step. And that’s all he would need.
