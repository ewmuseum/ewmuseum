Date: Sometime in the last week There comes a time when every person has to deal with an awkward situation in their relationships. Sometimes these lessons result in the implosion of said relationship and sometimes it can lead to a stronger bond between that person and their significant other. We've all been through the trials and tribulations of the common status relationship, but certainly there are the rare exceptions that make the situation truly unique and impossible to determine an outcome for. This is one of those times. |~o~| “Ok, do I really have to do this?” Killean was in the bedroom, sitting on the unmade bed while putting on a pair of socks and shoes. “Are you gonna make me handle this situation on my own and not help?” Jenny replied, her gaze stuck to a mirror on the wall as she brushed her red locks of hair. “I'm always here for you Jenny, you know that. But I need to come next door and meet the person you had a tryst with that you can't remember for the life of you? Just a tad bit awkward if you ask me.” “I didn't ask actually.” She paused to turn and look directly at Killean, who had just finished trying the lace on his shoes. “All I remember is that something happened. Why I can't remember the rest is beyond me but it might be because I wasn't meant to remember. It was an error and should have never happened. End of story. She's our neighbor and we need to put this all behind us.” “Am I the only one who thinks this chick is nuts?” “She was drinking too. I'm sure it was just all a big mistake on both our parts.” “And if you're wrong?” “I'm the woman, I'm never wrong.” “Yeah, and you naked on the bed with a pink sex toy on the floor would really have been great for Dizzy to see had she have been there.” Killean said, kind of smirking at the comment but hiding it from the prying eyes of his significant other. Jenny really didn't have a response as she headed for the door. Killean went to Desiree's bedroom where she was playing with her toys. “Time to get up sweetie. We gotta meet the neighbor.” “Ok Daddy.” Desiree put her arms in the air, the universal signal that she wanted her Father to carry her. She happened to be an outgoing kid, but meeting some new people made her uneasy and shy and she felt safer in her Dads arms. |~o~| Over in her apartment, Jordan was questioning the sudden meeting that Jenny had called. Why now of all times was it seemingly required that she meet Killean face to face for the first real time? “Please tell me I'm being silly in worrying about this.” Jordan looked over from her sink to the two animals sitting in the corner. The big dark cat known as Fergie was washing his paw while the female chihuahua sat and looked inquizzitively at her owner. “Don't look at me like that Trixy. She couldn't possibly remember what happened that night. Heck, she doesn't even know my name isn't Chasey...” Trixy suddenly barked and startled Fergie, but the sudden noise inturrupting her thought almost seemed to turn on a light inside that constant twisting mind of hers. “That's right! She doesn't know that does she? So when they come and she tries to call me Chasey, I correct her and tell them I'm Jordan and that Chasey was in the video. It's perfect! Killean would then doubt anything Jenny might have told him about that night. You're so smart!” Yeah, you heard it right. She was calling her dog smart. Do any of you let your animals control your thoughts on how to get you out of a pickle? Thought not. A minute or so later, there was a knock on the door. Trixy scrambled to bark at the door a little bit and Fergie marched over to the back of the couch and sat looking handsome as usual. Soon, Trixy ceased her barking and joined Fergie on the back of the couch. Jordan took a deep breath and approached the door. She heard voices on the other side. “You know if it was a guy in that room with you, he'd be in a coma right now and I'd be in jail again right?” “Killean... why should it be any different if it was a man or a woman? Don't be so shallow. Let's just move on to putting this behind us ok?” Jordan heard a grumble in response from the Supreme Machine and smiled. She fixed her hair just a little and opened the door to her guests. She smiled and received a seemingly forced one in return from Jenny. Killean stood with Desiree in his arms, not changing his facial expression one bit. “Hi, sorry for us suddenly dropping in. Hope we didn't disturb you from something.” “Not at all Jenny. Was just doing some tidying up.” Being the apparent oddity she was, Jordan had removed all the Killean related memorabilia from the walls and tables of her loft and stuck it all in the back of her closet. She was thinking ahead. “Well, this is my fiance Killean and his... or our daughter Desiree. Killean, this is Chasey.” “Nice to meet you.” Killean's voice was rather flat, showing that he really didn't want to be in the situation he was facing. Desiree was resting her head on Sirrajin's shoulder, not yet looking at their neighbor. “Ummmm, who's Chasey? I'm Jordan... remember?” Jordan forced a laugh, trying to lighten the mood early and show she was forgiving of Jenny's supposed mistake with her name. “I... uhhhh...” Jenny didn't know what to say. She could have sworn that Chasey was her neighbors name. How could she have gotten that wrong? “Chasey was the female on the video you were watching. Could that be what you're thinking?” Jordan questioned. Killean looked a bit disturbed at Jenny not remembering something as simple as the womans name. It was exactly the look Jordan wanted to see. She smiled on the inside at how well things fell into place. “It could be... I seem to remember the name Jordan for some reason... I'm sorry. I just can't remember.” “It's ok. Hi Desiree. How are you?” Jordan addressed the youngest Sirrajin. Desiree slowly turned her head to look at Jordan and immediately upon setting her cute little eyes on the woman standing before them, she teared up and began to cry uncontrollably. The child wasn't even sure why she began to cry, so Killean and Jenny hadn't the foggiest idea either. Normally, Desiree was really good with other people. Killean cradled her in his arms, trying to calm her down. “Oh my... she's usually not like that.” Jenny said, showing concern for Dizzy. “It's ok really. She's just scared or tired or something.” “Yeah, she hasn't had her nap this afternoon yet.” Killean remained silent. His only words so far were 'Nice to meet you' and even those words were somewhat of a lie. His questioning of this whole set up seemed to be founded as he usually put a lot of faith into Desiree on determining how some people were really perceived. “Perhaps we should take Dizzy back home Jenny.” Killean said, breaking his silence in a stern enough tone that Jenny got the point without trying to sound too rude. “Oh yeah, sure Kill. She should really have her nap.” Jenny said, pausing long enough to make Killean think he had found a way out of this awkward event. “Would you like to join us at our place instead Jordan?” Killean hid his face behind Dizzy's and winced at the invite. He was almost free and now, she was coming back to their place... the place where... well that's not important. But at least he'd be in his own environment. He'd be more comfortable there. However, given a few minutes and the opportunity to put Dizzy to sleep, he realized a couple things. Women weren't always right. Neither were men. He wasn't right because as soon as he left Desiree's room, he felt like a stranger in his own loft. Jenny wasn't right because she made a silly judgement call in inviting Jordan back to their place. He just wished the whole thing would simply disappear into obscurity. He took the free time to look over at Jordan and study her, like he normally did to people he didn't know. He got a bad vibe from her right off the bat. She seemed to act too innocent to take part in the acts she did just a couple weeks before. With Killean home now for an extra week, they finally had to face the music. There was just something about this woman that made Killean shiver and for the life of him, he couldn't figure it out. He was mixing a drink for himself and grabbing some iced tea for Jenny and Jordan. He continued to study them both, trying to pick out any sort of bad sign he could find. He knew something was off. But he finally had to admit that Jordan seemed like a nice person for the most part. This whole time she couldn't stop apologizing to Killean and Jenny about what happened and she assured them both the alcohol factored into the whole mishap. It didn't make Killean any more comfortable, but it didn't make things worse for him either. But he couldn't fully trust Jordan. It wasn't just a thing with her though. Killean never fully trusted a soul because anyone could be deceptive no matter how innocent they looked or acted. The names Stacey and Charity never came up again in the Sirrajin home for that exact reason. Even Matt Ward had been a deceiving individual at times. Hell, Killean even knew he wasn't a saint. Sometimes he didn't even trust himself. So he was constantly questioning why he didn't just end this awkward situation now and let it all go like he wanted originally. He really didn't know what to do. “Killean, Jenny and I were just talking about PRIME. I've seen you there on TV before. Very entertaining if I do say so myself.” Jordan said, accepting the offering and giving an almost unrealistic smile to follow up her compliment. “Thanks.” So the silence with Killean was broken for approximately half a second. Would the entire afternoon be this way? Fast forward to a couple drinks later and Killean was not loosening up. In fact, with the alcohol, he was even more detatched from the discussion than before. An odd opposite. He still had a strange feeling in his stomach about this Jordan woman and he intended to figure out exactly why. He suddenly pushed his way into the up until now two way conversation Jenny and Jordan were having. He also took note that Jenny seemed to be a little too forgiving of the sexually explicit rendevous they shared. “So Jordan, where are you from? Family or anything around?” Both women were surprised at the rather sudden question, but Jordan answered anyways. “I'm originally from North Dakota actually. I have a few family members in Billings, Montana right now as well. My actual home town is Bismarck. Both my parents lived there, but have since passed on. I have an adopted sister in Fargo right now. She got married only a few short months ago and hyphenated her name. I never understood that and I think it's stupid personally.” She laughed a little. She seemed to have a little more info than Killean was expecting, but he threw out a curveball to see if she would bite. He was trying to avoid being forward by asking her her full name. “Perhaps she liked her original last name and wanted to keep it.” “Yeah, even I'm not fond of Hawthorne. Sometimes I just wish I had the guts to change my last name to my middle name so I could just simply be Jordan Ariel. But my parents meant the world to me and just couldn't bear to change it. Maybe Chloe thought the same thing since my parents adopted her. Who knows.” “Chloe is your sister? That's a beautiful name. So is Ariel.” Jenny said as she broke into a rather large smile. “Yeah, sometimes I wonder if she makes any good decisions, but she's my sister and I trust her...” Jordans voice was almost muted now in his head. He was doing his best to remember the names given to him without being too obvious about it. He had gotten towns as well, more than he hoped. He was intent on finding out all the information he could about Jordan, even if there was nothing odd about her after it was all said and done. He just couldn't satisfy the growing feeling that something was very wrong with this woman. |~o~| “Frankie will be here in a few man. What's this all about anyways? You made no sense on the phone.” “I made no sense cause I was making it seem like you were asking me to come down here to help you with a few things. I didn't want Jenny or that chick to know what I was doing.” Killean said, sitting in his desk beside his friend and putting his feet up to try and clear his head. “That chick? You mean the neighbor with the dildo? Ha, dude I don't know where, but somehwhere along the line you lost that male mentality. If my woman decided to ever go out and sleep with another broad I'd be a happy camper.” Vegas replied, getting a drink ready for himself and Killean. He handed Killean the glass, who liftd it to his lips but never took a sip. His face looked puzzled. “You think that's all this is? Cause I sure don't. Jenny can't remember anything about that night and I really don't see that as coincidence. When I drink, I remember at least parts of what happened the night before. She has nothing. Plus you didn't see Desiree when she saw this woman...” “Whoa, you never told me my little angel didn't like this woman. What happened?” “As soon as she set eyes on this Jordan chick, she turned away and began to cry uncontrollably. You know how good she usually is with people. I've never seen her do this before. That alone tells me something.” Vegas looked away from Killean and down at his half full glass. He shook the ice cubes around in it, making that familiar clinking sound. “Tell you what... aside from my better judgement, I'm gonna help you with this. Frankie can do some searches cause he has connections and I have some ways of finding out things. But this one is for little Dizzy.” Killean smiled a bit, satisfied with the response even though Vegas was doing it for Desiree. That little girl was like the niece he never had. Killean and Vegas were like brothers anyways. Killean tilted the glass back and up to his lips, downing the fluid in one shot. As soon as the last of the alcohol was gone, the door from the club opened and in walked Frankie Taman, the lawyer of both Killean and Dave Vegas. “This better be important guys cause I pulled myself away from an important client.” Frankie said, sitting across from Killean. The Supreme Machine took his feet off the desk and put his glass down a little more hard than he actually meant to. “The fuck you mean important client? Last I heard we were the highest on the fuckin' totem poll of your client list.” Vegas said, cracking a smirk to show that he wasn't angry at the much smaller man. Killean leaned forward as he pushed the sunglasses away from his eyes to rest them on the top of his head. He folded his hands together on the desk in front of him. “We gotta talk Frankie... bout a personal favour to me.”
