Where's Ron Howard when you need him?

undated

Date: 05/23-05 John 'Little John' Spencer was a veteran. He had served with the 25th Infantry Division of the US Army in Vietnam. As a Private First Class he had hauled around an M-60 through the hot steaming hell that was the jungles in Vietnam. He had been awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor for taking out a sniper about 10 klicks north of Cu Chi. The same sniper had taken out his Lieutenant and three of his buddies. He took a bullet to the knee in doing so. His leg had been amputated about two thirds down his thigh. You would think that such a heroic soldier would've been looked after by his dear old Uncle Sam after serving his country so bravely. Little John was homeless. He came home with missing a leg and a morphine addiction that put a monkey on his back the size of a 500 pound gorilla. He drove his wife away with his self loathing. His brother refused to speak to him. His single mother died in a senseless accident. She had slipped in the bathtub and drowned. He had heard her fall and cry for help, but was too baked on heroin to care. Instead he watched the Beverly Hillbillies. He thought the Clampetts calling their swimming pool a cement pond wsa just the funniest damn thing he had ever heard. Now Little John was in his sixties. His teeth rotted in his mouth, he stank of piss and shit and sweat and Thunderbird. Not very many opportunities to bathe or brush your teeth on the street. He lived in a cardboard box across the street from an old abandoned apartment building. He used to live in the rundown structure, but had been driven out by some crank dealing punks who used it to manufacture their wares. About 7am Chicago's finest busted the place. Round up all the punks and cleaned the place out. Little John was going to be living the good life again. He'd have a roof over his head. And he knew the drug dealing scum had usually brought food with them. Maybe there was something left? It'd be nice to go to sleep with a full stomach again. That night, he grabbed the crutch he'd had for twenty years and hobbled his way over. Carefully scanning for onlookers, he tried the front door. The cops had left it unlocked. Chuckling at his good fortune, he stepped inside and shut the door behind him. He pulled a blue Bic lighter out of his pocket and lit it, using the small flickering flame to see the door behind him well enough to lock it. He grinned as he spotted a couple of Coleman lanterns the meth dealers had used. He lit one and went into the building in search of food... ____________________________________________________ Little John looked at the room and sighed in satisfaction. He had dragged an old piss stained mattress into the corner where beams of light from the streetlight outside came in through the cracks in the boarded up window. It was light enough for him to be able to read the stack of ancient porno mags he had found in one of the apartment's closets. They smelled of mildew and other nastiness, but you could still make out the girls touching themselves, and that's all that he needed. He had found half a pepperoni pizza the dealers had brought the day before. He had driven the rats away with a few swipes of his crutch and a lot of cursing. After brushing off the cockroaches, it had made a fine repast. He had scrounged enough change to buy himself a bottle of Thunderbird to keep him company that night. He was king of all that he surveyed. After barring the door to the bedroom with an old steel curtain rod he settled down on his mattress to a nice long night of drinking and reading... ____________________________________________________ Trash, I thought you were through with this kind of thing. I know you're pissed we lost the match against Hoyt and Token but... 'Shut the fuck up, Smiley' interrupted the Trashcan Man. 'I don't want to hear one word from you.' If there had been any onlookers, they would've certainly been calling for the authorities after seeing a man muttering to himself. They would've ran when they noticed the gasoline can he was carrying with him. 'It's not like I'm going to hurt anyone. Cops cleared out the place this morning. I NEED this. I need it bad. I've got to... do something. Anything. Everything just keeps going wrong.' But this isn't the answer... You're backpedaling faster than Hilary Rodham Clinton from a prenup. You're better than this Trash. Trash stops at the steps of the abandoned apartment building in front of him. 'If you won't shut up... then I won't listen.' With that he closed his eyes for a moment. Trash, you don't have to... damn... he walled me out. Indeed the Trashcan Man had taken full dominance of their body, driving Mr Smiley Face out of sub dominant into the passive stage. All Smiley could do now is watch with Trashy's eyes and hear with his ears. And he didn't like what he was seeing as Trashy leaned back and kicked the door open with one well placed boot. We were doing so good... why did he have to get back into wrestling... Trashy's face was cold and expressionless as he started splashing gasoline over the walls and floor of the entrance hallway. Dammit Trash, listen to me, let me back in, it's not too late... Apparently it was as the Trashcan Man kicked open a door to one of the apartments. He yanked some old moldy curtains off the window and ripped one to shreds. Soaking it with the last of the gas in his can, he flicked open his trusty Zippo and lit one end. Stepping back out into the hallway, he flung it onto the ground and watched as flames leaped out and surrounded him. Yeah, very pretty... can we please leave before the cops get here? Trashy, walled off from the Smilin' Dude's entreaties stood enraptured as the flames rose higher. He smiled broadly and threw his arms out, welcoming the oncoming inferno. Oh fuck... we're going to go up like the Stay Puff Marshmallow Man... Dammit, I hate having to do this... With that Mr Smiley Face ascended, wrapping his psyche around the unsuspecting mind of the Trashcan Man and brutally shoving it back down as he took control of the body. The smile on his face grew broader and more crazed as Smiley took over. NOOOOO! I must be purified! I'm unclean, unfit! LET ME BACK OUT! Ignoring Trashy's ravings in his mind, Mr Smiley Face reached into the waistband in his jeans and pulled out the square yellow cardboard smiley face mask he wore to hide his crazed grin from the world. Cackling loudly as he always did, he analyzed the situation. 'Damn, front door's blocked off, gonna have to go out a window.' With that he headed back into the apartment the Trashcan Man had broken into. He started pulling at the boards blocking the window, coughing at the smoke that was trickling into the room as the door began smouldering. He got one yanked off and dropped it to the floor with a clatter, sucking at the cooler fresh air that the opening led to. He reached up with his orange gloved to grab another board as he heard yelling behind him. 'What'sh goin' on out there?!' He heard a loud clatter and then a thud followed by a scream of pain. Smiley spun around at the sound. 'Trash... there's someone in here with us.' Oh no... Smiley, you've got to get him out! 'Great, I'm Mr Clean again. You owe me for this one Trash, you owe me big.' With that he crossed the living room and started banging on doors. 'Where are you?!' 'I broke my fuckin' arm!' Using the drunken crying as a guide, Smiley found the bedroom door and tried it, finding it barred. 'Hey, you've gotta unlock the door!' Nothing but crying answered him and Smiley sighed. 'I hope he's nowhere near the door...' With that Smiley leaned back and gave his famous big boot... the Kick of CHEER~! sans smiley face drawn on the sole of his boot. This didn't quite have the effect he desired. Instead of kicking the door down, he had put his foot THROUGH the door, and now stood on one leg, hopping to keep his balance as he tried to extract his foot from the hole he had created. 'Oh God, don't kill me!' screamed the drunken voice. 'God is dead, call me Nietzsche!' cackled Mr Smiley Face as he hopped backwards, falling on his ass as he pulled his foot out. Flames WHOOSHED up behind him as the living room caught fire. He sprang to his feet and reached in, pulling the curtain rod out and dropping it to the floor. Opening the door, he rushed in and looked down at the homeless one legged drunk lying on the floor, crutch at his side as he cradled his broken wrist in his hand. 'What's your name, friend?' Choking off a frightened sob, he answered 'Little John.' Cackling his high pitched laugh, Smiley crossed the room and started pulling out boards in the window. 'I guess that makes me Maid Marian, although I don't quite have the bosom for those dresses.' 'We're going to die!' screamed Little John in terror. Smiley grunted and he yanked a board out. 'We're not quite popped yet, so don't be calling Orville Redenbacher!' He yanked another out as flames started licking at the doorway behind him. Eyes tearing up from the smoke filling the room, he grabbed the last board and pulled it out before spinning around. 'Now I'm going Backdraft on you, but I swear I'm not Kurt Russell.' He picked Little John up in his arms and turned to face the window. 'Why are you wearing that mask?' asked Little John, still crying as the broken ends of the bones in his wrist ground together when he was picked up. 'I'm celebrating Halloween a bit early. You know, avoid the crowds. You're the biggest Snickers bar I've ever gotten, all the other kids are going to be jealous!' With that he leaped out the window as flames erupted behind him... ____________________________________________________ Mr Smiley Face looked down at the flaming building from the top of another apartment building a couple of blocks away. 'That was close, Trash. TOO close, you almost killed someone tonight.' Inside their mind, the Trashcan Man sighed. I know, Smiley. I'm just... tired of losing. I lost it for a bit there, but I'll be ok. 'Alright, you stay there and rest tonight. You can have the body back tomorrow, you know how much I hate going through airports. Just keep and eye on Him, ok?' Thanks Smiley. 'And dammit, you better win Wednesday. I'm not doing this again. And for punishment, I'm buying a SHITLOAD of Archie comics.' Smiley hears an internal chuckle. What is it with you and Archie comics? 'Hey, Jughead is a comic GENIUS! You know he's boning Betty and Veronica behind Archie and Reggie's back, right? Cackling as he always does, Mr Smiley Face heads off into the city in search of the ever elusive 24 hour comic book shop as we fade to black.
