Control

29 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29th May 2007 There are times when things don’t work out exactly as you had planned, this was one of those times. Well at least it was for Kurt Panzer who at this present moment in time was stood, quite silent staring at his own reflection in a bathroom mirror, the faucet below erupting with water which splashed all over his jeans and bare torso. The further up the body you pan though, the more noticeable the damage that had been sustained. His ribs were heavily bruised, his muscle bound body was black and blue all over. Most noticeably though was the broken nose, from which blood was free falling from into the facet, the water that remained turning an unpleasant pink colour. Panzer was not a small man by any means, standing tall at six foot nine and weighing in at close to three hundred pounds, he stood head and shoulders above almost everyone else in the tag division. There was only one other man who really had the height and strength to challenge him. His partner Klaus Steinheil. The only thing was that partners were surely meant to get along, they would look out for each other to ensure the progression of the team, unfortunately, this team didn’t work like that. “You hove let me down Kurt” The words came from Kurt’s partner in PRIME, Klaus Steinheil a cold callous man who was clearly the dominant of the two. Since the beginning Kurt had always known that he was second best, it had been drummed into his skull that despite his age and obvious physical attributes, he was always number two. Klaus was Kurt, that was the annoying thing, genetically they were identical even down to their fingerprints, but Kurt had been deemed the failure because he had simply followed orders, he did everything that was asked of him. It was apparently his lack of initiative that had deemed him a failure. “Kurt, you will not ignore me.” There was slightly more urgency this time, the slightest hint of frustration that he had not been answered straight away, something that he had grown accustomed to in his life. Klaus however had been different, in his years of upbringing, Klaus had always been the sort of child to question everything and as a result he had shown this ingenuity that they were looking for. When Kurt was asked to jump he asked “How high?” with Klaus was asked to jump he asked “Why the hell should I do that, I’m more than you could ever hope to be” then proceed to knock him out. “I’m sorry” These words weren’t said so much with genuine emotion as a force of habit, that this had happened numerous times before, that the beatings both mentally and psychologically had worn him down, now there was nothing left but a man, who was more of a machine who complied. So the circle continued Klaus would deem himself the primary, the alpha male pure Arian despite the fact that he pulled himself away from the truth that was his life. Having dyed his hair black to wearing coloured contact lenses, just to pull himself away from the guise of ‘Nazi’, this irked Kurt, simply because he was still obeying orders, he was trying to be everything that was required of him and he was cast aside, yet Klaus with his black hair and fake brown eyes was continually embraced and paraded around like the cock of the walk. “And what are you sorry for Kurt? Klaus was pacing up and down behind Kurt in a manner which determined he was interrogating him. His arms were folded tightly across his chest his eyes never leaving Kurt’s from the reflection on the mirror. It got to Kurt that even though he knew everything in his head was true there was nothing that he could do to counteract it, not at the moment anyway, it was strange. Kurt wasn’t fully sure what had happened to him, but every time that he tried to assert himself, every time that he was close to taking that single step forwards, which would allow him to take control of his life, of his actions and rebel against Klaus against those who stood against him and finally show his creators he was the true alpha male, that he was the true Arian male. Something happened. His brain froze, there was something about Klaus that did it to him, the tone of his words and the way in which he said them. There was never any anger in Steinheil's words. That was what was most disturbing about the whole thing. He never seemed to get angry, he simply said what he needed to say and then let the violence take over, and Klaus was extremely artistic with his fists. “For not answering you.” “Wrong answer Kurt.” It was around that time that Kurt let out a brief cry of pain, Klaus’ fist buried deep within his kidney, Kurt didn’t even turn round to face his partner, choosing instead to simply stare at his reflection in the mirror, the faucet still gushing water the only noise in the room aside from the two men’s deep breathing. Klaus’ face was stone cold expressionless his fist still imbedded deep in Kurt’s kidney. He silently leans in, so that his very lips are touching Kurt’s ear. Silently Klaus removes his fist as Kurt winces slightly, and then gently places his hand on Kurt’s shoulder. “My brother, I sense your anger and your jealousy towards me, that is justified, I can only imagine the pain that you must go through, every second of your existence seeing me alive and the success that I have become where as you are just a failure, but that isn’t what angers you most is it Kurt. You hate the fact that I come from you, that every time you look at me, you see what you could have become, that you could have been a success, that you could have been loved.” It was the first time that Kurt had reacted in the entire speech, but he quickly turns around and shoots a glance of hatred towards Klaus, but little more, he knew even if he wanted to retaliate that it would be a futile pursuit. The look alone was an act of resistance for Kurt, slowly day by day his resistance grew, he wouldn’t be controlled forever, but for now he simply had to play the waiting game. “I was loved Klaus, you know that. Our father loved us.” A sense of desperation came from Kurt, he had been hurt like this before, he was a big guy and could deal with almost anything that was thrown at him physically, but it was the emotional hurt which got to him. That was the thing with being a man mountain, no-one saw the hurt child inside, the child who had been forgotten by his own father, who was clinging onto the last memories he had, memories which he hoped were real. “No Kurt, father loved me, you were just simply there. Don’t you remember our youth, how you would spend hours in your room alone, studying doing whatever was asked of you, for the greater good. Father would spend time with me, playing laughing, extra training, extra lessons, private lessons at that.” Klaus knew what he was doing mostly because he was Kurt, except that he was the golden child of the two, Kurt was the ugly duckling. He knew every single insecurity that Kurt had about himself and exploited them regularly. Klaus wasn’t a bad man, he didn’t enjoy tormenting Kurt, but he also knew in his heart that Kurt had lost the way, that he still had a long way to go before approaching anywhere near perfection and until that time arrived, he needed careful persuasion in anyway necessary, whether it was attacking him or berating him. In Klaus’ head everything he was doing, was in the best interest of Kurt. “That’s not true.” “Think about it Kurt…haven’t you ever wondered why you don’t even share fathers name. Panzer!” “…” There was no comeback to a lime as devastating as that, it was bad enough for an adopted child who questioned his upbringing, but for you to not have your own fathers name cast major doubts in your mind. Doubts that had been in Kurt’s mind for a long time. “Father never loved you, as soon as you were deemed to be a failure he lost all interest in you. I fell into your role and claimed it as my own.” ~~ ~~ ~~ Berlin 1981 “Otto, we need to talk, it’s about Kurt” A greying Otto Steinheil sighed and reluctantly opened his eyes and surveyed the immediate area. In the corner of the room sat a significantly younger Klaus Steinheil, no more than six or seven years old, sat silently reading a book on advanced warfare tactics. In front of Otto stood Johann, his lab assistant but more recently, his key figure of annoyance. Otto was a man, who was growing old and tired, spending as much time as possible with Klaus, who he had taken a strong attraction to. At first he simply wanted to be his mentor, but Klaus was such an overwhelming success that he couldn’t help but fall in love with the little guy. “What about Kurt?” The tone of Otto’s voice was not so much venomous as tired and bored, he had heard problems with Kurt for as long as he could remember, Kurt was showing issues with development, and Kurt wasn’t developing as quickly as they had hoped. Every single problem in Otto’s life was at the moment. Kurt. “It’s, well it’s a delicate matter. Kurt has begun to ask about his mother. “Have you simply not stuck with the plan?” Otto slighted a glance towards Klaus who was still nose deep in the book currently reading about Blitzkrieg attacks. In Otto’s mind he could help but wish that things had turned out differently, he had wanted to love Kurt as his own, he genuinely had originally, but as his obvious failings became more and more apparent, Otto’s love faded “Yeah, he is simply not accepting this as a viable course of action. He’s eleven years old Otto, he just wants to know why he doesn’t have a mother like everyone else.” “Everyone else? Johann, where would he receive a foolish idea like that, we live in an insular society. We control everything he sees.” It was a viable question, everything in the boys development was controlled, there was no way that anything should be able to reach their ears or eyes unless they so wished it., someone had messed up big style somewhere. “It would appear that there has been a breach of security somewhere and Kurt has got a hold of a magazine with material deemed unsuitable, a magazine which doesn’t conform to our needs.” Otto scratched his head. He had reached the end of his tether with Kurt and the failings that his existence reflected upon himself. Kurt was to have been the supreme soldier but he had done nothing that he was expected of, other than being a perfect human specimen. Now there were only two options left and neither of them were a viable option but he had, had enough. Otto wanted to spend his ending years with Klaus, finalising his final piece of work. “Is there anything that can be done to rectify this Johann?” Already Otto’s thought processes were working, maybe there was a way that Kurt could be the powerhouse they had always dreamed of. The only problem would be Johann, there was no way he would agree to it. Otto also knew that he was losing his grip over Johann, it was a mixture of age and experience but slowly, little by little Otis was losing his hold over Johann. “At the moment it would seem not.” “Go through with the electroshock therapy.” “Otto you can’t be serious.” “You said yourself nothing else can be done, Kurt cannot know the truth behind his existence, the very fact he is asking questions, places decades of work in jeopardy. “Surely there must be another way.” “Of course there is Johann, death.” A slight giggle came from the corner of the room as Klaus had turned his attention to the two grown up’s who were talking at a raised level. The two men stared for a moment at the child who continued to giggle at the two men before returning to his book. In those few laughs, there was a level of deep disturbance that ran through both men, there was something unnatural about it. It was Klaus’ eyes that were most un-nerving though, they seemed to peer into your very soul. “I’ll proceed with the electroshock Otto, but you must know that I am not happy with this in the slightest.” “It’s not your job to be happy Johann, your job is to do what I say!” ~~ ~~ ~~ Present Day. Klaus was now towering over Kurt who had almost folded in half over the faucet, the tap was still pouring water freely. Despite their almost identical frames, Kurt was the smaller man here he had been beaten down and now resembled the small child that was lost inside him. “The nightmares that you have Kurt, the screams as the pain courses through your body, the lost memories that are deep within you. Father was responsible for that, I sat there as he gave the order, I sometimes think that he should have just ordered you killed, at least it would have ended your misery and mine.” “You lie!” Kurt’s head shot up out of the faucet spraying water all over the room, turning immediately to face Klaus. Who in return had not budged even the slightest inch. He was still standing tall with a smile on his face, which represented a man in total control. “Kurt, why would I lie about something like that, if you look deep in your heart and get past the pain, you know it to be true, now clean yourself up, we’ve got a flight to catch.” Klaus turned on his heels and left the bathroom leaving Kurt standing alone, the only noises his heavy breathing and the sound of water splashing against porcelain. The quasi-silence lasted for around thirty seconds before it was interrupted by a cry of anguish, quickly followed by the sound shattered glass. The mirror in front of Kurt was gone as blood began to trickle from his forehead down his face, before dripping off his chin and falling into the basin.
