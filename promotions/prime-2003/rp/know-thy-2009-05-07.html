Know Thy

7 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/7/09 A bottle of hairspray. A bong. A pair of leather pants with a snakeskin belt. Whoever said “Know thy enemy” didn’t have to go to war with these wack-jobs. I was sitting there looking at this collection of junk spread out on the edge of my sink, when it occurred to me I could just try ‘em all. Dress up like some metrosexual cowboy and get stoned outta my mind. It sounded fucking stupid. But I was thinking about doing it, cuz it beat the alternative. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ The Day Before “You had no right.” Ellie wouldn’t let me see the tab… so I was kinda pissed. We went out for dinner and drinks with her friends, since I didn’t have any of my own. I was drinking Blue Moon cuz it’s damn good beer. She would have been drinking Guinness (ya know… if she weren’t pregnant) cuz she wishes she was Irish. At least she didn’t drink Labatt Blue. Anyways, we were having a good time… telling stories, cracking jokes, laughing at other people’s misfortunes… and then the end of the night rolled around and the waitress brought the check. Ellie reached over and grabbed it outta my hands. We get involved in this stupid game of tug-of-war right there across the table and she started giving me these evil looks and telling me to let go. Fuck it. I let go. “ ‘Scuse me?” “Don’t ever hold another check from me. I gotta right to know what kinda cash we’re throwin’ around.” Had I done it to her before? Sure. Did that make me an asshole or a hypocrite? Probably both. “What? I just wanted to make sure I paid tonight.” “Bullshit. If that was all it was about, you wouldn’t have had to hide it from me after it’d already been put on your card. You didn’t want me to see how much the bill was.” “Who cares, Kaiser? We’re not strapped for cash.” Come to think of it, I wasn’t sure why I was even all pissed off. They say that people make mountains outta mole hills. This seemed like it was probably more along the lines of making a fuckin’ continent outta… …outta something really small. Didn’t stop me from being angry though. “It’s not about money being tight. I gotta right to know what we’re spending. I don’t care if its two bucks or two hundred.” “Ya know something,” We were halfway across the parking lot when Ellied turned and pointed at me with her car keys. “You can be a real hypocritical asshole.” Yup. Both. “What’s that suppose to mean?” “Kaiser, you don’t think you’ve ever hidden the check from me? Refused to let me see how much it was?” “If I ever did (I did,) then I’m sorry.” She started towards the car again, and I jogged a couple steps to keep up. “I just wanted to go out and have a nice evening with my friends. You’re constantly pinching pennies and worrying about money. And ever since I told you I was pregnant… things have been kinda tense. I just wanted a night out with some friends without you worrying about money and raining on the parade.” “How does hiding the bill and guarding it with your life ease my concerns, exactly?” “Fine. It was a bad plan.” “Yeah.” We crossed the last couple empty parking spots and parted to opposite sides of her car. “That’s what I’m saying. It didn’t help anything to keep the check from me.” “What’s the deal, Kaiser? I admit… it was a bad idea on my part, but why are you so pissed? What about this whole ridiculous thing actually bothers you so much that we’re standing here arguing in the middle of a parking lot?” I had no immediate response. For a few seconds, I stood there mute, probably looking stupid, staring at her across the top of the car. “I dunno.” “You dunno?” “I just think you shouldn’t be hiding stuff from me. That’s not who we are.” “Kaiser, I’m not sure you’re aware of who you are. Figure that out and worry about us and our money later.” She hit the ‘unlock’ button on her keypad, but I was already lifting the door handle. They canceled each other out and we played that game for the next twenty seconds. ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ So why the hell not? Give ‘em all a go. I was gonna just do it. Two deep breaths and I picked up the bong and did whatever stoners do. I gripped it and ripped it… or however that ridiculous match concept went at CIII. Then I waited, and waited, not sure exactly what I was waiting for. Meanwhile, I decided to give the hairspray a shot, aiming the bottle and firing away. It never really crossed my mind that someone should probably have a full head of hair to actually use hairspray, I just went ahead and emptied half the bottle… using entirely too much, as I assumed he did. I felt cocky, as I smirked and posed in the mirror, but wasn’t sure who’s arrogance it was that I was feeling. Distinguishing between specific egos was more difficult than one might think. Suddenly, the drugs started to kick in. My head began to feel kinda weird, and I rubbed my hands together in anticipation, craving the opportunity to understand one of my opponents. Looking in the mirror, there was a definite difference. It had worked. Or had it? There were no new ideas streaming through my brain. No insight to the thought process of a Dual Halo winner. No clues to the function of the mysterious mind. Sure, I looked like Jesus and smelled like Larry the Cable Guy… but that was it. “Shit.” With two down and only one to go, cursing seemed appropriate as odds of success grew narrower. The snakeskin and leather pants were all that were left, and with two failed attempts already to my credit, doubt was beginning to kick in. Should I try on the pants without the snakeskin belt? Just the belt and not the leather pants? Maybe I had done this in the entirely wrong order. Perhaps I should have used the hairspray first, then the bong. Or maybe the pants should have come first. Doesn’t everyone start off their day by putting on their pants? The doubt was turning to frustration as I bit my bottom lip, gnawing on my own flesh. Reaching down, I picked up the leather pants and snakeskin belt… and I held them in front of me, focusing as hard as I could. There was no choice. I had to do this. I had to make this work, no matter the consequences. Cuz I’d rather be a half-baked, smelly diva with a crusty head than have to know thy self.
