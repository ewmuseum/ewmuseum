Weekend with a wild man

undated

Date: Today THURSDAY Jackson Darilek is crazy. It’s been said many times before. Sometimes it’s said with a chuckle: He crazy! Sometimes it’s said in admiration: Mmm, mmm, dude is cray-zy. Then other times it is said in a sort of grave whisper accompanied with bugged eyes and a half hand-covered mouth: I think he might be crazy. And now, after following him for yet another week, I too must admit it as well. Jackson Darilek is crazy. How else can it be explained? Just look at him. With much of his time spent shirtless and barefoot, Jackson has a look of cultivated dishevelment that many attempt but few achieve. Listen to his ideas. He believes that cursing during events should result in disqualification because children are watching, but thinks chair shots should be legal because it’s what the fans want to see... and it’s fun. Watch him fight. Wild eyed and full of fervor one moment, then flashing his gentle, disarming smile the next. Like I said, Jackson Darilek is crazy. How else can it be explained? “It” being the spectacle about to take place. “A real wild man.” a friend of his begins to describe Darilek before cutting himself short and bursting into a barrage of cheers. “It’s kinda scary watching him sometimes.” his girlfriend informs me after a short-lived standing ovation. Welcome to the show. Just get here? Don’t worry. The good stuff hasn’t started yet. A simple trip back home to Florida, allegedly made to “recharge” has turned into anything but. Jackson’s trip home just so happened to coincide with a show being put on with his old indy promotion, which coincidentally, was running a man short, which consequently brings me here. A dimly lit high school gymnasium transformed for one night only into a makeshift wrestling arena. Even with bleachers fully extended and several rows of chairs, it’s still a tight fit and most people find themselves unintentionally rubbing elbows with one another as marvel at the star of tonight’s show; Jackson Darilek. He puts on quite a show and is actually given a run for his money by his much larger opponent, but in the end the ever impressive “Gravity Be Damned” seals the deal. This warrants a standing ovation from the screaming crowd, one which he soaks up every decibel of while standing in the ring hoisting the PRIMEtime title high overhead. He looks battered and broken, beads of sweat race down his body, and his lip is pretty well busted, but despite all that he’s smiling. Some “recharge.” Backstage it’s like a homecoming for the local boy who made good. His friends swirl around the locker room picking up autographs and snapping pictures as they go, his girlfriend hangs close by with one of Jackson’s long toned arms around her, half talking on her cell phone, half being introduced to some of the guys. With several of the promotion’s stars it’s all high fives and hugs as they congratulate him on picking up the PRIMEtime title. With others though it’s all hatred and malice, many of them feel he stole their spotlight and should not have taken the main event. Some don’t appreciate him holding up a PRIME title in their ring, meanwhile others hate because they’re just jealous. No matter though, the negativity of those jealous few can’t reach him. Jackson’s elation burns so bright inside the locker room you can almost see his aura. Old stories and inside jokes fill the air, often followed by infectious laughter that hits even those that didn’t hear the joke. A real contrast from the twenty year old champion that spends much of his non-ring time keeping to himself either listening to his headphones or playing PS2, Jackson Darilek is the life of an impromptu locker room party. When asked about the difference as we traversed the parking lot he glanced up into the sky for a moment before shrugging and offering “I don’t know... I mean, I like PRIME. I LOVE the big crowds, but it’s not exactly the easiest place to make friends backstage. A lot of guys that come in already have a name and rep to throw around. People just flock to familiar faces I guess.” He shrugs and continues through the parking lot, Dalia under his arm. “Why kick it with a rookie when you’ve got an entourage?” FRIDAY Machine gun camera flashes click like strobe lights as a single photographer frantically rushes back and forth across a small manufactured landscape in order to capture different angles. The target? Jackson Darilek, who stands shirtless in the center of a makeshift wrestling drenched from head to high top. Clad only in a pair of frayed and faded camouflage shorts that place the top portion of a pair of Scooby Doo boxers in plain view, stark white tennis shoes, and a pair of swimming goggles worn up on his forehead and cocked to the side, he goes through pose after pose as the camera is continually fired. The shoot is for PRIME promotion material, and as a champion, Jackson being featured is a mandatory. The theme for his shoot? Wet behind the ears... Jackson is normally all smiles, with each one giving light to a childlike sparkle, but after thirty minutes of soaking wet posturing his frustration begins to show little by little. Then, for split second it comes to light. His head back and tilted slightly to the side and holding the PRIMEtime title lengthwise at waist level as though it weighs a ton combined with a defiant closed mouth facial expression that just screams “Come get some.” and a well timed camera flash spells the end of the shoot. “Excellent work Jackson! That’s the type of edge I was looking for!” the photographer exclaims as he holsters his camera. Catching a towel tossed to him by one of the photographer’s assistants, Jackson once again turns into one big ear to ear grin before removing one of his tennis shoes and pouring the water inside onto the mat. Just as he goes to remove the other, he hears his girlfriend attempt to eek something out but he’s too late to react and finds himself driven to the ground by an unseen tackler. Blows rain down on the young champion as he struggles to break free, but to no avail as his escape his hampered largely by hysterical laughter. Jackson’s attacker is a friend of his, who cackles maniacally as he pummels the PRIMEtime champion while two others quickly join in the fray. The impromptu romp causes quite a ruckus in the studio but Jackson and company are far too busy laughing to car. Following an escape from the three on one beat down that proves just why it’s been said keeping him down is as hard as nailing jello to a wall, Jackson grabs his girlfriend’s hand and dashes out of the studio with his friends giving chase. Halfway down the hall Jackson’s wet sneakers send him sliding across the floor. When the ride is over, there’s a six person pile up and a busted camera sprawled across the floor in pieces. Looking at the carnage, then up at several people in the vicinity, none of which seem to be impressed, Jackson reaches into his pockets only to find them empty and decides offers up the only thing he has. “Sorry?” SATURDAY Usually on the weekend if you find Jackson you’ll find his friends as well, but instead, today he’s with family. He’s also drawing a vivid contrast between himself and the man challenging him for his title; Peter Vetra. Facing off for the PRIMEtime title on the next edition of ReVolution we’ve got angry, brooding, legally troubled Peter Vetra going head up against champion Jackson Darilek... who’s turning cartwheels and back flips at a family barbecue. While Vetra is probably lifting weights in some dank gym with a weathered picture of Jackson hoisting his title ripped from a magazine, Darilek is outside racing his younger cousins to a slip and slide. As he continues to play the part of the fool much to everyone’s delight I realize why it is the fans embrace him the way the do. It’s not because he’s the biggest or the strongest, or even the most technically sound wrestler(especially since he’s even admitted that at times he’ll have a mental lapse and not know exactly what to do) since much of his style is trial and error, but because he has fun. The fact that he enjoys what he does makes him that much more enjoyable to watch. Every springboard, moonsault, and catapult begins with the same thing, a smile. The same smile he intends to be wearing as he walks out of ReVolution with, victorious. Scoops McKannon
