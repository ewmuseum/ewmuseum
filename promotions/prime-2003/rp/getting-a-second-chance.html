Getting a Second Chance...

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Various Walczak sits on his couch, holding a phone receiver in his right hand. His left hand rests on his forehead, and the look on his face is one of obvious distress. Getting up from the couch he makes his way over to the kitchen, and opens the liquor cabinet, taking out a bottle of Krolewska Vodka. He takes a cup and almost fills it to the brim. Walczak is not new to drinking, but this isn’t a drink to celebration. He has just spoken with Dmitry, and even though he has waited for this phone call for weeks, it still comes as a shock. I did not even know who my parents were earlier this year, but now I know more than I ever imagined. I have a choice, I can either stay here, and forget my parents existed, or I can go to Russia kill the motherfucker who betrayed my parents, and avenge their death. If I go to Russia my life ends. I would not be able to wrestle, I would not be able to live in New York, and I would have to go into an almost reclusive state. However, I know what I have to do. Walczak takes a large gulp of his drink, and makes his way into his bedroom. He starts to pack his bag, and it seems that Walczak has made up his mind. He has worked his whole life to build his career, but he feels a strong connection to people he has only know of for little over a month. Walczak is going to Russia, and his life may never be the same when he comes back. __________________________________________________________________________________________ A flood of people make their way out of the airport terminal. Walczak is easily seen among them, his head and shoulders towering over the rest of the people. His bag is slung over his shoulder and he looks around searching for Dmitry. “Tom…” Walczak sees Dmitry waving his hands in the distance and makes his way over. The two of them embrace, a much different greeting than their initial meeting. Dmitry slaps Walczak on the shoulder and leads Walczak outside towards the car. Walczak sits in the passenger’s side, and tosses his bag in the back. “How have you been Tom?” “Not to good, nothing else has been on my mind.” “You don’t have to go through with this. I told you that I could take care of everything.” “I have to do this. Trust me I thought long and hard about it, but there is no other way. This guy has hurt way too many people, and since it started with me I have to end it.” “Once you start to lead this life it doesn’t end.” “I know.” For the first time in his life Walczak is feeling compassion. He can’t stop thinking about all the other families that Janek has hurt. How could he not end this man’s life? __________________________________________________________________________________________ Walczak now sits in his hotel room watching a soccer game on the television, and trying to relax. Dmitry had offered him a room at his house, but Walczak needs to be alone. His whole adult life has been built around hurting people, and Walczak has not hid the fact that he enjoys this, but this has taken it to another level. In Walczak’s mind there are two types of murderers. Those who kill others by accident, or due to self defense, and there are those who kill out of malice, who know what they are doing but still do it anyway. He never imagined that he would be one of those people. “Ring…Ring…Ring…” The phone rings, startling Walczak, and getting him to rise from his relaxed state. He slowly makes his way towards the phone, and somewhat reluctantly picks up the phone. “Hello.” “Tom, Janek is at his restaurant, and we have a meeting set up with him in an hour. Get ready, and I will be there to pick you up.” “Okay.” Walczak hangs up the telephone, and wipes his eyes with the palms of his hands. The look on his face is no longer one of thought, or worry. His face is now simply one of relaxation, because he knows what’s going to happen next. __________________________________________________________________________________________ Dmitry’s Black Lincoln Continental pulls up in front of Janek’s restaurant. Walczak walks out of the car, and follows Dmitry into the restaurant. They are met at the front by Michal, who is a little apprehensive after their last meeting. “How are you gentlemen?” “Where is Janek?” Dmitry wastes no time getting directly to the point of why they are there. “He has not come back from his vacation yet. I told you that I will call you when he comes back Dmitry, do you not trust me?” “Stop fucking around Michal. I know that he is upstairs. I wasn’t going to leave it to you to call me, and I have been staking out this place for the past couple of weeks. Now, you are going to let Janek know that we are here, and take us upstairs, or I am going to call Nickolaj, and you know that he won’t be happy to hear this.” A large lump appears in the throat of Michal, and he wipes some of the sweat building up on his brow. He looks at his watch trying to buy some time, but Dmitry does not seem to be willing to give him an out. “Come on Michal, take us upstairs.” “Alright, I’ll call Janek.” Michal walks over to the bar on the other side of the room, and picks up a phone. He takes a little longer than Walczak would have hoped, but Michal finally makes his way back towards them. “Follow me upstairs, Janek is waiting.” Michal leads the way, and Walczak and Dmitry follow. The secretary keeps her head down as Walczak and Dmitry make their way into the office. Michal knocks on the door, but gets no response. He looks around at Walczak and Dmitry giving them an awkward smile. He wraps his knuckles on the door, another couple of times, hoping to get a reply. “Come in Michal.” Michal opens the door ushering in Walczak and his Dmitry. The office of Janek is adorned with paintings, and antiques, leaving almost no room for his desk. The antiques seem to serve almost no purpose other than showing off Janek’s wealth. They do not go with the décor, and the fact that he prides himself on his wealth only further angers Walczak, because he knows that this wealth was built on the death of his parents. “How good it is to see you Dmitry.” Janek swivels around in his large almost throne like, leather chair. A smug smile covers his face, and long gold chains adorn his neck. His head is completely shaved, and he wears red tinted sunglasses. Walczak’s clenches his fists as he looks at his parent’s murderer. “Who is you large friend here. Michal told me that he caused quite a ruckus in my restaurant while I was away.” Walczak looks over at Michal, who is doing all in his power to avoid eye contact with the giant. “I don’t know who you are, but I assure you that I do not take lightly to these types of disruptions in my restaurant.” “Janek, stop trying to skirt around the issue. You know that I was sent here by Nickolaj, and you know the amount of money that you owe him. I am here to collect, and my friend here is only around to make sure that you keep to your payment schedule.” “Why is Nickolaj worrying so much? I have dealt with him for years now; he knows that I am good for it. You can go and tell him that I will get him all the money next month.” “That’s not going to work.” Janek slams his fist down on his desk. He grits his teeth before beginning to yell at Dmitry. “LISTEN YOU LITTLE BITCH. YOU KNOW HOW I OPERATE, AND I AM NOT AFRAID OF YOUR BOSS. NOW, GET THE FUCK OUT OF HERE BEFORE I CALL THE POLICE.” “I had a feeling that you were going to respond like that, and that is why Nickolaj has given you another option.” “What is that?” BANG A gunshot makes its way through the head of Michal, and Janek immediately jumps under the desk. A scream comes from outside the office, and the secretary runs down the stairs. The shot has even managed to get the attention of Walczak, who was not at all prepared for what had just happened. Walczak looks down at the lifeless body of Michal, blood slowly pouring out the hole in the back of his head. His eyes are still open, as the color slowly starts to drain from his face. “WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU DOING DMITRY!!!!” “DO YOU REMEMBER BURNING DOWN MY RESTAURANT? RUINING MY FAMILY’S LIFE?” After Dmitry’s tirade, there is silence in the room. Walczak looks around to see if Janek is trying to find a way out, but he goes no where. “I asked you a question Janek?” “It was a business decision Dmitry, you should know all about business by now, and if you kill me Nickolaj will not like it, because it is bad for business.” “That is where you are wrong Janek. He is sick of your bullshit, and to tell you the truth he was hoping you didn’t have the money.” “Fuck you.” Dmitry walks around the right side of the desk, motioning for Walczak to do the same on the other side. Walczak follows his orders taking out his pistol, and waiting for what happens next. “It is over Janek. You have ruined a lot of lives on you rise to the top, but that ends now.” “I called the cops Dmitry. No matter what you do, it is over for you as well.” “I knew it would be, but I just want you to meet someone, so come out here.” Janek gets up from under the desk, dusting off his black suit. He smiles at Dmitry, figuring that he has won this battle. “Who is this that you want me to meet?” “Do you remember how you got into Russia in the first place?” “What are you talking about? I made my way into this country on my own. All that I have I earned.” “Fair enough. I would like you to meet my associate, Tom.” Dmitry points to Walczak who is now standing almost directly behind his parent’s murderer. Janek looks up at the behemoth, but does nothing more than smile. “Great, you have a new big ass friend. I never met a person immune to bullets, and that is what he will be filled with once the cops come.” “I didn’t bring him here to scare you. Tell him your name.” “Tom….Walczak.” The smile on the face of Janek immediately disappears, and his face goes ghost white. His hands start to tremble, and now it is Walczak who is filled with joy. Walczak takes the barrel of his gun and presses it to the back of Janek’s head. “Tom…Leave.” Dmitry’s comment brings a puzzled look to Walczak. “What do you mean?” “I can’t let you do this. He ended the lives of your parents, but he should not be able to end yours, and by killing him that is exactly what he would be doing.” “I have to do this Dmitry.” “GO!!” Walczak pulls the gun away from Janek’s head, and makes his way out of the room. He acts more out of confusion than anything else. He looks back at Dmitry, and realizes that although he has known him for only a couple of weeks he truly cares, and that is something that Walczak will never forget. As he makes his way back down the stairs he hears the gun go off, and knows what has happened. He follows the restaurant patrons out onto the street, most of them running wildly, but Walczak just slowly making his way out, his hands casually in his pocket. Once outside, Walczak hears sirens coming closer to the restaurant. Dmitry has pretty much given his life, so that Walczak could live his, and he would not disappoint him. __________________________________________________________________________________________ People make their way across a crowded street in Brighton Beach, New York. Some go in to stores, and others are engaged in conversation. However, one just stands there, his back leanings on the wall of a local bakery. Walczak looks around scanning the faces of the people who pass him by. “Come in here, Tomek.” Nickolaj Ruslin, comes out of a door near the bakery. Walczak peels himself off of the wall, and follows Ruslin up the stairs. They come to his office, and it is still as damp and musky as it was over a month ago. Nickolaj takes some papers off of a chair, making it available for Walczak. “Sit down.” “I would rather stand.” “That is fine.” “Why did you call me here? I thought that our business is done with. Janek is dead, and I am free to go back to what I do. I thought that was the deal.” “It is. I just wanted to let you know that Dmitry was killed in prison by one of Janek’s men.” Walczak’s is not that shocked at the death of Dmitry, knowing that he would more than likely be taken care of. “Is that all?” “That’s it, you will not hear from me again. I just felt that you needed to know.” “Thank you.” Walczak turns to walk out, hoping that this really is the last time that he has to see the face of Nickolaj. “Tomek.” “What?” “I know Dmitry would have wanted to wish you luck on your upcoming match.” “Yeah.” Walczak continues to make his way out of Ruslin’s office. His parents gave him strength and size, and Dmitry gave his life, so that he could use them. Chainz and Titan St. James were going to be the first to see the new Walczak, and they wouldn’t like it…
