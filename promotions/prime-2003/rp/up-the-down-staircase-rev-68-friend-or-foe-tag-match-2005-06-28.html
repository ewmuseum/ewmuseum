Up the Down Staircase (ReV 68, Friend or Foe Tag Match)

Date: 6/28/05 Nova reaches around under the bed covers, his hand groping for the TV remote. He finds it, and switches on the JVC boob-tube situated a few feet from the end of his bed. “In other news, three more teenagers were murdered last night in Richmond, Virginia, bringing the death toll in the recent crime wave to a staggering…” Click. “Man, and white people be all like taking a shower like this…ha, ha…but black people, man, black people be all like taking a shower like THIS, ya know what I’m…” Click. “And how did that make you feel?” “Jerry, I felt…I felt betrayed, and…and…*sniff*…I’m sorry. I just…” Click. “Do you ever feel lost…confused…bewildered?” Nova sits upright in bed, leaning a little closer to the TV. “Yes…” “Have there been serious problems in your marriage recently?” “Yes!” “Do you lie awake at night wondering what the missing link in your life is? What the x-factor is that will be the secret to your success, both personal and professional?” “Yes! Yes, that’s me!” “Are you having a difficult time in the workplace…feelings of inadequacy or friction with other employees? Maybe both?” “Holy crap! This fuckin’ guy is talkin’ to me!” “I have the solution, if you’re ready to answer the question.” “Fuck right I am! Lay it on me!” “Have you ever considered a sex-change operation?” … “Umm…” “Trans-gender operations have made a whole of difference for thousands of people who couldn’t…” Click. Nova switches off the TV, tossing the remote disgustedly onto the floor. Fuck that guy. Sometimes I think my dick is all I have left. ------*~*~*------ Anaheim Area Ramada Plaza, Anaheim, CA…1:15 AM. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. Nova lies awake in bed, eyes wide open as he stares at the ceiling. “Oh, fuck me, daddy! Fuck me!” Apparently the couple next door are honey-mooning…or something. Jesus. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. The tragic irony of his situation is killing him. Nova misses his wife. It hadn’t really sunk in yet. He had been able to throw himself head-long into PRIME, into what he loved. It was easy to forget that there had ever been a wife or home to lose in the first place… …until the mailman reminded him. KNOCK, KNOCK. “Who is it?” “Delivery for Mr. Vega. Mr. Vega in 306, right?” “Yeah, that’s me.” Nova undoes the latch (never can be too careful) and pulls open the door. A kid in a USPS uniform is holding a manila envelope. “What…what is it?” Two joints ago this would have made a lot more sense. “I don’t know. Papers or something. Sign here.” “Papers or something,” indeed, the little schmuck… …child support papers. Child support papers now sitting on the tidy little bureau next to the television that had suggested that a Nova sans penis might be a lot happier. Nova saw through that lie, of course. Aimz is about as dickless as they come, and she’s got more problems than the damn SAT. The damn papers, though. Nova looks over to the bureau, then over to the painting of a flower vase, something out of a Van Gogh if Van Gogh blind-folded himself, painted a flower vase, and then pissed on the wet paint… …then back to the papers. Yep, still there. A constant, tangible reminder that there’s no home in Detroit to catch a late flight back to, no warm body under the covers to roll over and be happy that you’re home much earlier than you originally said because you changed flights as a nice surprise. No baby to look in on and practically tear up like a sappy bitch watching her little belly poof out and in as she breathes. Well, that’s not entirely fair. There’s still the house, and the baby… …but the wife is gone, and a lot is different. The house isn’t home, anymore. It’s a place to visit. And the baby is still yours, of course, but it’s more hers now, because she’ll be the one who’s there every day. Shit, man. The papers are really driving all that home right now, the painful shit that drives Nova to attend shows that he isn’t booked for and make appearances and catch extra flights, all to immerse himself as deeply as possible into this profession, into this culture… …this culture that, ironically, drove his wife away in the first place. HOLY SHIT: Epiphany. It’s all fucking circular. My wife leaves me because of the business, and I go deeper into the business to cope with my wife leaving me. Dustin Hoffman and Lily Tomlin were right. It is all circular. Damn. That’s not fair, either. It wasn’t the business that drove her away. It was me. SHIT! I hate these papers. They’re like a damn therapist, forcing me to examine what exactly happened between Ariel and I every time I look over at them, which is only all the fucking time, since this hotel room is about as sparsely decorated as the interior of a Save-U-Money bargain bin coffin. Freakin’ mail-man. I was alright before…I was focused on Youngblood, on the 5-Star Title, not dealing with all this shit, and now I can’t think about anything else, all because he rap-tap-tapped on my door and oh so jovially dropped a stack of papers on me telling me that from here on out, I’m gonna need to send monthly checks to the address where I used to live. Fuck him! Well, that’s not entirely fair to the poor bastard. He was only doing his… …oh, Jesus! Why can’t I even bitch to myself? Shouldn’t I be allowed to do that? Shouldn’t I be allowed to wallow in self-pity and pointless blame games without seeing through the hoops I’m deliberately trying to make myself jump through? I wish I could. Really I do. Then I could take all the pressure off myself to change or correct my situation. I could skew the way I view the world into a categorization of absolutes, blacks and whites, with no attention given to the examination of the gray area. Yes, I could wallow and delight in the elaborate contradictions I lace about my character…it would be so easy! I could block out any painful truth about myself or my life, my choices, simply by placing it into an inflexible box and daring not, nigh, refusing to view it from any outside angle, any deviation from well-defined parameters that I myself set to protect me…from myself. And then I could shave my head, and change my fuckin’ name to Brandon Youngblood. Nova sighs and looks over again at the child-support papers that still haven’t moved from atop the bureau next to the TV that tried to tell him that a pair of piss-flaps and a C-cup would be the answer. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. “Oh, God! Harder! HARDER!” That fucking bedpost beating against the wall is killing me. And in the darkness, Nova wants his ex-wife. ------*~*~*------ Anaheim Area Ramada Plaza, Anaheim, CA…2:28 AM. “Oh, God…please! Please, yes!” THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. They’re still at it…and Nova is still awake, lying in bed staring at the slow-moving ceiling fan that doesn’t exist. He wishes it existed because his room feels like the fucking fourteenth Canto of Dante’s Hell structure. Nova sits upright in his bed, the sheets clinging to him, and stares down at his chest and stomach, where a single beam of light seems almost to cut him in half like a swath. The light stems from a shiny object gleaming in the moonlight over by the window… …the PTC Extreme Title. Something is different tonight, though. Usually, and with some small amount of self-satisfaction, Nova could look over at his titles gleaming in the dark of his hotel room and grin with pride. But that’s the whole problem, isn’t it? Let’s repeat that last statement and look for the flaw: Usually, and with some small amount of self-satisfaction, Nova could look over at his titles gleaming in the dark of his hotel room and grin with pride. Uh-huh. There’s only one title “gleaming in the dark of his hotel room” right now, and it’s driving Nova absolutely bat-shit. He can remember a fleeting moment from the end of ReVolution 67. As The Deville stood over him, his body still racked from the extended Gridlock, eyes glaring up the entrance ramp, Nova managed for a single moment to raise his head as well, and follow the Crimson Angel’s gaze until it landed upon the man Nova had first come to very much dislike and eventually hate… …“The Only Diamond in the Sport” Brandon Youngblood…and it got worse. He was holding something, and no, it wasn’t Nova’s pride, although the Rising Star felt as though that were absent as well. No, “the Greatest Wrestler Alive” was holding the 5-Star Title, and for a moment, as the image whirred and clicked in Nova’s brain in front of thousands of fans, he was overcome with emotion. It was one of the strangest feelings. It was like a flashback to when he was a kid, on the public bus riding to school in the morning. Being big into superheroes (his early love of heroes and hatred of villains helped propel him toward professional wrestling), Nova would always bring a few comics onto the bus with him to ease the painfully boring ride. All his comics were dog-eared…he knew them by heart. One day, some cock-suckers saw him reading some X-Men books (the industry insiders called them “books”) and deftly moved past him in the aisle, ripping the comics haphazardly from his hands before making their way to the back of the bus. The swiftness and careless nature of their act had astounded Nova. His eyes misted up with tears at the loss of something he owned, something he loved, but more so, the indignation he felt at the position he had been put into and this unexpected early encounter with senseless cruelty basically broke his heart…and he had to wipe off his face before reaching school or risk the humiliation of recounting this incident to his friends. They were just comic books. This was just a gold strap. The tears were gone. The hurt of that age was gone. Even the humiliation, in the sense that he felt it then, was gone. But goddammit, the indignation was still there. How dare he! How dare Youngblood even put his hands on the 5-Star Title! He hadn’t earned it. He hadn’t done shit for it. And his mantra about the purity of the business, about his being the best, hell, in his eyes the only real wrestler in PRIME… …it boiled Nova’s blood in the moment that he saw Brandon back-pedaling up the ramp with his belt…his belt! Nova had turned that strap around. No one gave two shits about the 5-Star Title when Joshua Taro Freedom wore the belt. The legacy of Ian English and Joey Troy was forgotten, and the challengers were weak. Nova had changed that, had grabbed hold of the belt (literally, during a ladder match) and had shown it off like he was goddamn proud to wear it, and made it something to be sought after, opting out of the mid-card for his title defense and choosing Hoyt Williams. Hoyt Williams, who, after beating Nova, had put the belt in the back seat, disfavored below the “God’s Title”…such bullshit. But Nova had re-liberated the belt, taking it back in the Main Event at ReVolution 61 and returning it, in his eyes, to the glory he had always wanted for it. All Nova had really wanted was to be a champion people respected, so that when he lost the belt, they might look back and say, “Yeah, it meant something when Nova was 5-Star Champion.” Seeing it in Youngblood’s hands flew in the face of everything Nova had tried to make for the 5-Star Title…and that wasn’t the worst of it. Now, Nova sits upright in bed in his room that feels like the boiler in the bottom of the Hilton Hotel located at the center of the fucking Earth, and he feels odd pangs of loss. He has an investment in the 5-Star Title, an emotional investment. It’s childish, and Nova will admit that much to himself. But the 5-Star title (and the Extreme Title, too, but to a much lesser extent) has become a crutch. Losing Ariel was a crippling blow, almost too much to take, but Nova had his belts, his accomplishments, symbolic of the very reason why he chose to take the dangerous bookings week after week and stay on the road… …success. Appreciation. And now that his only real reservoir of comfort is gone, and Nova feels strangely weak. Vulnerable. Exposed, even. He’s the 5-Star Champion without the belt, like the needy girlfriend who has to hear “I love you” in order for it to be true in her mind. … Is it now that difficult, in all honesty, to see why Nova is having a difficult time trying to sleep? THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. “Oh, oh, God, baby…oh, God! You make me feel so good!” And just like that, it all boils over. Ariel, the child custody papers, the 5-Star Title, Brandon Youngblood, the hot-as-shit hotel room… …and the fucking neighbors. Pun intended. BOOM! Clad only in a pair of Joe Boxer briefs, Nova kicks in the door to the next room over, ignoring the topless woman frozen upright over her man and making a beeline to the bathroom. CLANK! Nova emerges a moment later, a towel rack gripped in his hands like a baseball bat. He stares wild-eyed at the couple for a moment, both of them now cowering under the covers, before leaping up onto the bed. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. THUMP. Nova drives the towel rack into the wall above their heads again and again as the woman screams in terror. He looks down at them. “Do you have any fucking idea what that sounds like in my room?! DO YOU?!” THUMP. THUMP. “It sounds fucking awful, that’s what it sounds like! It sounds fucking awful!” THUMP. THUMP. “It’s almost three in the morning!” With this admittedly shabby approximation of the time, Nova stares down at the couple, now clinging to each other for their lives as bits of dry wall crumble down onto their heads. Nova takes several deep breaths and steps back off the bed. He drops the towel rack, and the color in his face slowly drains to an almost deathly white. “Uh…um…oh, God…” I need to leave. Now. With haste. “I’m…uh…I’m…” Without completing his apology, Nova turns and sprints out of the door. Several hundred yards from the entrance to the Ramada Plaza, duffel bag slung over his arm, Nova watches two police cars pull up, greeted by the night manager. Times like this Nova appreciates signing in for his room as “J. Edgar Hoover.” ------*~*~*------ Surf N Turf Diner, Anaheim, CA…3:40 AM. Nova exhales smoke into the air as he stares across the table at the one person he can count on to be up at all hours of the night… …Nick Brocken. “Yeah, I’ll have the…well, is the Surf N Turf Special any good?” Brocken asks the waitress hovering above their table. “The Surf N Turf is only available after 10:30,” the waitress regretfully informs him. “Shit,” Brocken mutters, “then give me two eggs, sunny side up.” “Alright,” the waitress replies before turning to Nova, “what about you, hon?” “Just more coffee,” Nova says grimly before taking a drag from his cigarette and mushing out the end in the aluminum ash tray. “You look fuckin’ rough,” Brocken says, a slight grin on his face in spite of himself. “I feel fuckin’ rough,” Nova agrees, scratching his beard, “thanks for meeting me here, Oz.” “I didn’t have anything better to do before dawn,” Brocken replies with an amiable shrug before adding, “and for the last time, the name ain’t…” “Can it,” Nova interrupts, “I’m not calling you ‘Nick’ or ‘Brocken’…I remember a goofy bastard with clown makeup, so if it’s all the same to you, sir, I’ll stick with ‘Oz.’” “Alright, alright,” Brocken concedes, “Jesus…” Nova pauses to light another cigarette and the waitress swings back by, pouring his cup full. “Thank you.” He turns back to Nick. “Ya know, I can’t for the life of me figure out Youngblood and Killean all buddied-up like they were at the show.” Brocken snorts a little too loudly, and a trucker turns his head over toward the commotion. “I can’t believe he kissed Aimz with the same mouth he used to tongue Youngblood’s asshole.” “Well, phony hard-ass exterior and cliché ‘seen it all, done it all’ attitude aside,” Nova says, exhaling, “Killean’s inherently a decent guy. I just don’t understand what the fuck he sees in a snake like Brandon Youngblood.” “They just both want someone to play ass-pat with,” Brocken says dismissively with a wave of his hand. “I wonder if it’s just that,” Nova says, his eyes narrowing, “or if there’s something else to it. You, uh…you think Youngblood would try to use Killean against me? If it came down to it? Or am I just being paranoid?” Brocken shrugs. “I don’t know.” “Maybe I’ll just ask him myself,” Nova says decisively, “at the show. Always seems like a good way to do things.” Brocken nods. Nova stares out at the water in the darkness from his window. “The Pacific Ocean is fuckin’ freezing, man. I don’t see why people swim in that shit.” “It’s fun,” Nick retorts, “people will brave the cold, even get used to it.” “I dunno, man,” Nova replies, still looking out at the sea, “that bitch is COLD.” Nick stares at his friend. “Maybe it’s worth it to some people, the cold…if it means they have the chance to ski, or surf. Maybe if they can do those things, the cold doesn’t seem as bad.” Nova looks back to Brocken, and for a moment, there is complete silence. Then… “Everything has to be a fucking wrestling metaphor with you, doesn’t it? Check, please!”
