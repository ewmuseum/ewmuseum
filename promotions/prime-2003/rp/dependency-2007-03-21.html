Dependency

21 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 21st, 2007 Though he had packed up and left Tokyo a month ago, Coral Avalon had agreed to do one more show in the Japanese promotion he had been working in, the Wrestling Battle Grounds. Japanese puroresu was rarely about "sports entertainment". It was a battle of wills between two men. It was about two men, standing eye-to-eye in the middle of the ring, exchanging blow after blow, and dropping each other on their heads, battling for the sole purpose of proving who was the better man. Coral Avalon had the fortune of winning such a battle of wills on this night. Still wearing his ring gear, Coral walked back to the crowded locker room with his head held high. He was feeling pretty good for a guy who took a released German suplex on the floor on this night. He thought to himself that he would miss the time he had in Japan. He had some good moments here, and while nearly two years of his career had been spent here, he would value the experience he wrought from waging war against the wicked wrestlers that wanted to waste him. Coral noticed that, outside of the locker room, Annabelle Summers was leaning against a wall. It should be noted that Annabelle was bilingual, but unlike most American people who were bilingual, her second language was Japanese (rather than, say, Spanish). This made her an excellent person to be with on these long tours of Japan, because she spoke the language. This was often why Annabelle was with him at most times in Japan, because Coral only has a cursory knowledge of the language. Annabelle often wore her long hair in equally long pigtails. She once told Coral that she got that idea from a manga she read, so we'll take her word for it, especially since it's now a very common look for her. She wore a sparkly purple tanktop, a black skirt down to her shins, and a pair of simple black dress shoes. Annabelle was busy conversing to a young Japanese woman named Inoue Doi. This woman, despite her appearance of wearing a school girl uniform, was a savage beast in the ring who could hold her own against even the larger men of the squared circle. Coral had the unfortunate distinction of being the first person to defeat her in the ring, thus meaning that Inoue would always look at him in that very scary "I will kill you" look on her face. It's very scary given that this woman, quite literally a phenom, was not only an accomplished wrestler, but also excellent at gymnastics, karate, and some bits of judo as well. Also, she's only twenty, and she's been wrestling since she was seventeen. Inoue was known in the ring as "the School Girl", and wrestled in a kind of a school girl uniform, hence the name. She had gotten her start in Harvard Avalon's "Squared Circle" promotion, which turned her into a big star. However, when the promotion folded upon Harvard's death from cancer, Inoue returned to Japan to ply her trade in her homeland. She was very surprised to find that Coral followed her to Japan, albeit unintentionally, but was very happy to smash him to pieces in many matches that resulted between them since the two came to Japan. Over time, however, there came to be an understanding between Coral and Inoue. In spite of the ever constant flow of violence that occured whenever the two met in the ring, the two gradually began to create a mutual respect and understanding for one another, even if she wanted to murder Coral in the ring any time they were on opposite sides. Yet, in spite of this antagonistic relationship, there existed one pleasant thing about her. She always had respect for anyone who spoke her language. Although Inoue understood English, she never spoke it herself and often used her brother, Ryu Doi, to do her speaking for her. This often resulted in communication problems, though often, her tone was enough to understand the gist of her attitude towards you. With Annabelle, it was different. Annabelle, who was fluent in Japanese, had always had a very civil relationship with Inoue. The two had met not long after the fairly infamous incident where Sonny Silver made her start bawling, and then he took pictures of her crying. They formed a surprisingly fast friendship given the huge number of enemies that the School Girl had made in the Squared Circle thanks to her violent attitude, and have remained so ever since. Inoue saw Coral before Annabelle did, and casually remarked, "::If it were me, it would have lasted thirty minutes shorter.::" (If you're wondering, those colons are there to indicate that another language was being spoken. If you think I'm going to use my own cursory knowledge of Japanese in an attempt to write a damn roleplay, you are out of your mind.) Coral looked at Inoue, before he sighed, "Thanks. I appreciate your criticism. Even though I don't know what you just said." "She, um... said that the match would've been shorter if she were in your place." Annabelle translated. "::Your boyfriend is a slow learner if he has been here this long and still does not understand our language.::" Inoue told Annabelle, crossing her arms. Coral scratched his sweat-covered head, "What'd she say this time?" "She says that you're dumb." Annabelle said with a small smile on her face. Annabelle's much shorted translation of her word caused Inoue to giggle and eventually burst out laughing. Annabelle had this knack of sometimes changing the meaning of things just for the sake of doing so (or teasing Coral), and not because she lacked understanding for the language. Coral sighed and crossed his arms, having walked over next to Annabelle and leaned close by, "Why am I not surprised that she thinks this?" Annabelle glanced behind her, before she remarked to Coral, "Shouldn't you shower?" Coral thought about it, before he snapped his fingers, "Knew I forgot about something." And thus, he started to walk away. However, he turned to look at the School Girl on his way out, "Inoue, don't steal my girlfriend." For some reason, the thought of Inoue Doi wearing a bandit mask and carrying a tied-up Annabelle while Coral Avalon chased them on a white horse crossed Annabelle's mind, which made her smile. Inoue, meanwhile, shouted at Coral, "::Idiot!::" "I heard that!" Coral said back, as "baka" was one word he *did* know. He soon entered the shower area, leaving Annabelle and Inoue alone with each other. Inoue relaxed her usual demeanor, and leaned against the wall next to Annabelle, "::So, you two really are leaving?::" "::I'm afraid so, Inoue-san.::" Annabelle said, leaning against the wall with just her left shoulder, "::Coral wants to return to the States.::" "::I knew it,::" the School Girl said, a confident and sage nod coming from her, "::I knew that that idiot would grow tired of being beaten by me repeatedly.::" Annabelle smiled, "::You like having him as a friend, don't you?::" "::Oh, shut up.::" the School Girl said in response. She didn't really like being teased too much, "::The only reason why I tolerate him is because he's your boyfriend. If it doesn't work out between you two, I will be the first to kill him.::" "::Oh, don't say things like that. I'm very sensitive towards my relationship with Coral.::" Annabelle said, before she paused and added, "::You know that.::" Inoue nodded. When she first met Annabelle, the violin player had been tearfully upset over Sonny Silver suggesting that Coral didn't love Annabelle. She had cheered Annabelle up, even though she was set to face Coral Avalon later on in the night. It caused Inoue no end of embarassment to learn the actual reason why Annabelle was so upset that night, but the two had remained friends anyway. "::Sorry.::" Inoue apologized sincerely, and looked away awkwardly. There was an awkward silence. Finally, Annabelle spoke up, "::What will you do now, Inoue-san?::" "::Me?::" she asked, pointing a finger at herself, "Ehhh... ::I will stay here. I happen to like being cheered for hitting people as hard as humanly possible.::" She gave Annabelle a mischevious smile, as if it was wrong, somehow, to hit people until they die from it. Actually, it was, but let's not tell Inoue that, because I think that would make her very sad. Annabelle smiled, though much less mischeviously, back at Inoue, "::So, you are not interested in coming back to America?::" "::Not in the least. I'm far more comfortable here.::" the woman known as "the School Girl" responded. Annabelle turned towards the direction that Coral walked off towards, "::I should probably get going.::" Annabelle turned to leave, but Inoue grabbed her by her shoulder, making Annabelle turn around and say, "::W-what is it?::" "::Regarding him.::" Inoue said, as she crossed her arms and leaned against the wall, "::He seems to be the sort of guy who prefers someone like you.::" "Eh?" went Annabelle. "::I knew him before he met you. Granted, I hated him and wanted to kick his brains out, but I kept an eye on him. I found that the last two girls he had, that being that woman who's with the internet geek and that blonde referee bitch, were very demanding and self-reliant sorts of people.::" Inoue said, looking up at the ceiling as if the ceiling was telling her what she was going to say. She looked back at Annabelle, "::You're neither demanding nor self-reliant.::" Annabelle pouted, "::You make me sound dependent on him.::" Inoue smiled, however, "::If you are, then you are. Just keep in mind, he's dependent on you as well. When that man's personal life is in order, he is difficult to keep down. Which is totally annoying, I might add.::" "::Do you think so?::" Annabelle asked, surprised as the validity of Inoue's words. "::He's not the sort to admit it himself, but it's easy for even me, someone who knows him only through the violence we inflict on each other in the ring, to see.::" Inoue said, flashing her adorably evil smile at Annabelle, "::If he wasn't in a relationship with you, he'd be the same idiot he was in the Squared Circle, and I wouldn't even think of him as a rival. Every blow he inflicted upon me was filled with discord and dissatisfaction with his life. Then you came along. Now, every time he hits me, he's doing so with inner harmony. Or some other weird Buddhist thing like that. Regardless, you've probably changed him for the better, so he's just as dependent on you as you are on him.::" Inoue Doi closed her eyes, nodded her head sagely, and smiled, "::It's why your relationship with him works so well.::" Annabelle was, for the lack of a better term, flabbergasted as Inoue's interpretation of the relationship between Coral and herself. Inoue smiled and giggled, however, "::Anyway, that's my take on it. I have a match soon, so I need to go find my useless brother.::" As Inoue walked away, she stopped and turned back to Annabelle, "::Oh, and tell him that if he shows his face here again, I'll be the first to break him.::" Annabelle let herself laugh at that one, but clammed up a bit afterwards and responded with a smile, "::I'll let him know.::" Annabelle crossed her arms and looked up at the ceiling, before saying one last thing to Inoue, "Thank you." "Dooitashimashite." said the School Girl, in response, on her way out. Annabelle Summers thought to herself that she was lucky to have the friends and relationships that she had. Regardless of whether she'd admit it or not, it was the fact that she was in a very deep relationship with Coral Avalon that she had the friends she had. Under normal circumstances, she wouldn't have met such a person as Inoue Doi, but it was through Avalon that she made a friend like her. It was through Coral's help that Annabelle became a stronger person. Sure, she was dependent on him. She'd admit it if someone asked her if it was true. The thought that Coral needed her just as much as she needed him, however, made her feel like she truly mattered. With her heart feeling lighter than air, Annabelle walked off in the direction that Coral had previously left.
