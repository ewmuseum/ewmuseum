Nightmares and Daydreams: A New Beginning

17 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: April 17, 2009 Friday Jenny It's been five months now and nothing. A couple phone calls came back but none were for decent jobs. The ones that did call back would pay me less than my employment insurance. But even that was bound to dry up soon and then what? What could I possibly do then? “That's what I'm here for.” I was startled into a sitting position on the bed. I saw a middle aged lady standing in the doorway of the bedroom. She looked familiar, but I was so tired lately I just couldn't place it. “You look surprised.” She said as she took a seat in the chair by my night stand. I looked into the hall and could faintly hear Killean, Dave and Desiree in the main room. “I just wasn't expecting anyone that's all.” “Well, we made arrangements to talk this week remember?” That's right, she was my psych. I do remember making an appointment for sometime this week. I laid back on the bed, my head being propped up by only a couple of pillows. I could hear her flipping pages in her little notebook. “I've tried everything and I'm going crazy being home all the time! I'm so not the housewife type.” “We talked about this last time Jenny. I want to hear about your medications.” “You made the housecall doc.” “I don't make housecalls my dear.” |~o~| “Daddy, look what I made for Uncle.” Desiree held up a freshly colored picture for Killean and Uncle Dave to see. Sure the coloring wasn't in the lines and the color combination made no sense, but it was his daughters creation. “That's a beautiful picture hun.” “Yeah, why don't you just put that by the door so I can take it home with me and put it on my fridge.” Vegas said, knocking back the last of his drink. “Ok Uncle Dave.” Desiree walked over to the small table and put the picture there. “So things are finally getting better around work. The bills are paid and money is coming back into the savings.” Vegas was glowing, a large toothy grin beaming out as he put his glass back on the table. “That and Jenny seems to be feeling a bit better. I guess the meds really are doing the trick.” “The club is bumpin' and those four live concerts last week didn't hurt the bottom line at all. Looks like we're coming out of this funk. We should celebrate.” “I'm gonna color a picture for you now Daddy.” Desiree said, sitting on the floor in front of the coffee table. Killean certainly was happy that things were going better. But unfortunately they weren't out of the woods yet. One misstep could mean certain disaster. The months of effort could all be undone in a matter of minutes. Ironic that such a situation is what got them into this problem in the first place. However, Dave was right. And what better way to celebrate with a child than a wonderful frozen treat. “Dizzy, you don't have to make me the picture now. I think we should go for ice cream instead. Whaddaya say?!” “YAAAAAAAAAAAAYYYYYYYY!” She was so cute. Hopping up and down like a mini jumping bean, she rushed to the door to put on her slip on shoes. Dave laughed and quickly followed her, tickling her and winning a giggle before reaching for his own shoes. Killean walked towards the bedroom and peaked his head inside. Jenny was laying on her back, her head turned to one side, steadily breathing. Killean knew she was sleeping, but he talked to her anyways. “Hun, taking Dizzy for ice cream...” His whispers were just loud enough that she would have heard them, but a muscle didn't move. He smiled and backed his way out of the room, deciding it best to let her sleep without her having to worry about Desiree. She and Vegas had already slipped into the hallway, with Vegas holding her up to the windows in the hall to get a good look at downtown Winnipeg. Killean quickly tied his shoe laces and closed the door behind him. The click of the deadbolt was the final thing heard before footsteps carried off into the distance with one little girls laugh echoing. |~o~| Jenny “OK Kill.” I replied as he disappeared down the hall. It wasn't like him to not respond to say goodbye, but I felt better just brushing it off. I rose from the bed and asked the doctor to move from the chair politely and proceeded to light a couple small candles. It was my preferred way of lighting up the nightstand to put on my makeup. The doctor sat on the bed instead, continuing to give suggestions. “So we're in agreement. You're gonna get all dolled up and take Killean out for a great night with dinner, a show and a possible romp back here.” The Doc was scribbling some quick notes as she talked, not really paying attention to me as I quickly applied some eye shadow. I could see her in my mirror, just going through the common motions. “Yep, this is gonna be great. I'll surprise Kill and we'll have a great night. That should get me out of this drought I'm in. We haven't had sex for over a month now, mostly my fault as I'm just never in the mood anymore.” I dropped the applicator and ran some lipstick over my lips, pressing them together and then pursing to ensure proper coverage. “A hot night should change that.” The pair of track pants and the tank top would do for the moment, as I would change when I came back home after purchasing a small gift for him. I started to pull my hair back into a pony tail and then tied it up a couple times. “It's not that I don't like doing it. I've just felt more in the mood to melt into the couch with some junk food and watch TV. I mean, I've put on ten pounds in the last while and Killean hasn't really bothered me about the lack of intimacy. Maybe he doesn't like me with the extra weight.” I wasn't a small girl in the least. My curves were my strong suit, something Killean wasn't used to with his history of dating rakes and two by fours with breasts so to speak. But he embraced the change and proved Vegas wrong when he doubted he'd last a few months with me. “Funny story, he was being hit on by a waitress at the club and she was nice looking. Big chest, thin and slender... something that he'd go for all the time. But he turned her down and came home and told me all about it. It almost sounded like he was bragging he was faithful but I took it as a HUGE compliment. We had a wild night that time. But since I've been down, it's maybe been an average of once a month. Could he be cheating?!” I turned around from finishing my hair and glared wild and wide eyed at the Psychiatrist. I suddenly felt a slight hint of anger coursing through my body, but deep down I just knew he wouldn't do that. “None of the signs are there Jenny. He doesn't stay out late at the club. He doesn't disappear without telling you where he's going and almost all the time he answers his cell phone. We've been over this a hundred times before. Killean is not cheating on you.” “I know, I know.” I said, slipping on a pair of sunglasses and checking myself out in the mirror once more. Damn I was looking good already. The slenderness of my black dress would make me look even better tonight. “Hot night here I come!” I marched for the door, paying no mind to anything else including the occupation of my bedroom. |~o~| Many children giggled and some cried that the ice cream was a lineup away. After waiting for a good ten minutes, Killean finally reached out for the rainbow ice cream cone and handed it down to Desiree, who hopped over to a park bench to sit with her Uncle. Killean received his caramel sundae shortly after that and sat opposite them beside the large “Bridge Drive Inn” sign and looked briefly at the already rainbow colored face of his daughter. He smiled. “You know man, I had some more calls this week for bands to play at the club. Steeple Chaser wants to come back and you know everyone here in their hometown just loves their shows. Mac was suggesting an intimate and interactive night with them too. Apparently, one of the waitresses used to do that for an online music site or something. It'd be so awesome!” Vegas waited for a response from his best friend for a good ten seconds or more. Killean's eyes had drifted to the water flowing under the bridge at a swift rate. “Yeah.” He finally responded before getting up off his seat and walking towards the pedestrian bridge at one of Winnipeg's most cherished attractions. He stopped about thirty yards across and leaned on the rail as Vegas quickly followed after lifting Dizzy onto his shoulders. He shoved a small spoonful of sundae in his mouth as the water mesmerized him. The already high water on the Red River was threatening some riverside homes in the city, but was creating much more devastation in the surrounding rural communities. Killean remembered reading about it and volunteering to sandbag a few properties. His wouldn't let his appearance become a huge attraction, instead opting to sign a few t-shirts and promptly returning to the work at hand. Looking at the water flowing swiftly and lapping up against the shoreline that had already been deteriorated, he just couldn't stop watching. Vegas caught up with Desiree a moment later. “Look at it Dave. Waters higher than the flood of '79. Sure '97 was much worse, but look at it. It could all come crumbling down for some people with houses water side in a minute.” “Why are you going on about this now for?” Vegas asked, quickly taking a large chunk of chocolate ice cream of the top of his sugar cone. “You volunteered and so did I. We made a weekend out of it. We can't help everyone.” “Yeah but look at the help we got at the club. News got out of the Madoff scheme and local bands started to play two for one nights at the club. That saved us some coin and brought in double the audience as they were exclusive visits. Some of the girls gave a portion of their tips to the charities we tried to pay the first time. You just seem so helpless looking down at it, the destruction it could cause.” He began to trail off, the breakneck speed of the river water was a reminder of another misstep. His return to PRIME was a success before the match with Hessian started. But in the matter of a moment, his world came crumbling down around him as well. It was only a few seconds of action to the people in the arena, but it felt like a lifetime to the him. As his body bent awkwardly around the broken ring post swung into his ribs and stomach, the pain was almost unbearable. He flopped to the canvas like a sack of potatoes and stayed there, until Hessian saw fit to deliver one more vicious shot. The punishment was carried out with a sickening reverberation of the canvas as Killean was flipped from the Canadian Backbreaker Rack landing awkward on his neck with the Ganso Bomb. In three seconds, the bell rang and music was playing that surely wasn't his own. The pain from the post shot hurt the most and it felt blinding, turning the audience into a transparent sea of white before his eyes as another boot stuck him quickly before being turned onto his stomach. His eyes closed and he just wished the night would end. He held his taped up ribs as he put the ice cream down on the rail. Only half finished, he really didn't want it anymore. Instead, he was thinking too much about PRIME all of the sudden. His mind tended to do that to him. PRIME was the furthest thing from his mind while he was home, but it was always brought to the surface when things just didn't go his way. Now he had another test... Jason Snow. Snow had been a thorn in The Supreme Machine's side before. Snow took out an insurance policy in Chainz at one point, but Killean outsmarted The Original Villain by breaking into his dressing room while Chainz was in the ring. A couple baseball bat shots later and Snow was left laying for Chainz to find moments later. But Snow had the last laugh, pinning the multi-champion after a vicious Snake Eyes kick. History repeated itself again at Overkill where Team PRIME tried to fend off Fuck You in Killean's last match at the time. Snake Eyes put him down for what was supposed to be the final time he stepped into a PRIME ring. We all know how “retirements” go. Killean thought hard ever since he found out he'd be standing across the ring from the current Universal Champion on Wednesday. Would Killean find out that the third time could be a charm and beat Snow? Or would he fall once more with Jason Snow cementing his superiority over The PRIME Choice? Killean ran each scenario over in his mind many times, each rotation depressing him more. He couldn't let Jason Snow get him again, not this time. He had to prove that his return to the company was worth while and that he could still hang with the new breed of talent working it's way into the business. He might not have been that old in reality, but he felt old sometimes watching the early twenty-somethings light up the arena like he used to do every night. It wasn't about getting more money to help Dave stay on his feet... not this time. This was about Jason Snow and finally pinning the GTT 6 winner to the mat. “... bands coming in. Dude? Are you even listening to what I'm saying?” The deep voice of Vegas came back into focus, as the loud flow of the water faded into the background again. Killean opened his eyes wide and sighed, holding his head in his hands briefly. “Sorry Dave.” “That would be a no. You always get like this around a big match and I'm not used to it. You haven't been like this since that Roulette deal months ago. Bring me to the show and I'll stomp a hole in that blonde whore monger for you.” “Hey come on, child present.” Killean lifted his daughter from the big mans shoulders and held her in his arms. The youngest Sirrajin was now chewing slightly on the ice cream cone she was more than half way finished. “We'd better get you some napkins Diz.” “Got some right here.” Vegas reached into his pocket and produced a handful of them. Killean was about to grab one as his cell phone rang, instead opting to hand Desiree back off to her Uncle before fishing the phone out of the belt clip holster. The call display read, “Vivian”. “Hey Viv, at the house yet?” Once he stopped talking, he could hear nothing but a ruckus in the background. Many people were screaming and some other strained voices were heard before Vivian came on in a panic. “Killean oh my! Is bad over here! Get home now sugah! My lordie!” Some sirens were heard in the background before the line went dead for a moment. Then he could hear some more voices, cries and sirens before the call was lost completely. He didn't realize it at first however and kept trying to talk. “Vivian? VIVIAN?! What the hell?!” He quickly returned the phone to his holster. “What was that? Something wrong?” Killean didn't respond to his friend though, instead leaving his half finished sundae on the rail and heading back across the bridge. Vegas looked at Desiree who was still chewing on her ice cream cone and shook his head, quickly following after Sirrajin towards his car. |~o~| The water dripped from the rafters, some pouring onto the floor in constant streams. It was a much different sound than the river he had heard only twenty minutes before. The rather tall men in fireman's uniforms were strewn throughout the halls, talking to the tenants of the building. Ground zero was his floor. As he approached his loft, the scorching of the walls was becoming more intense. The bricks were discolored where the flames danced around them. All the windows in the hallway were busted out and long ladders from fire trucks were leaning against the outside concrete. Many hoses were thrown across the ground. He closed his eyes tight and opened them again as he came to where his door used to be. Instead the door was on the floor, the metal obviously dented and the metal door jamb torn apart. He stepped onto the hardwood floor carefully, the carpet mat that used to occupy the space was reduced to charcol dust under the soles of his shoes. Water droplets repeatedly dripped onto his head from the ceiling that was also singed in some way. People he didn't know were in his loft, from police officers to fire men all decked out in their usual gear. They didn't stop him from entering however, knowing full well who's home this was. He slowly walked across the floor, dragging his feet and breathing in the little bit of lingering smoke in the room. The windows were all punched out and the walls were bare except for a few assorted picture frames, most destroyed beyond recognition. The obvious burn marks on the concrete walls showed how high the flames must've danced around. Many pictures of friends and family sat ruined on the floor, some due to water damage and some burned completely into waste. The glass from the windows and the pictures was spread out all across the room getting crushed with every step he took. His awards case he had in the corner was reduced to a burned and wet wooden mess. Leather straps from replica title belts were mostly burned up and the gold face plates were permanently tarnished. He bent down to retrieve a mostly disintegrated photo, removed from a good portion of it's frame. The rest of it was hanging from the picture as he held it in his hands. It was a picture of himself a few years earlier with Taun Pham, Hin See and Mr. Bojangles... the old guard in PRIME. It was a photo he treasured ever since he realized that PRIME was his true professional wrestling home. But the photo was mostly missing. Parts of Taun Pham remained holding Mr. Bojangles in his arms, and all that was left of Hin See was his trademark ten gallon hat and a portion of his face. Bending down he picked up another picture in his right hand, a baby photo of Desiree that he took as soon as she was found outside the very same loft he was now standing in. It was coupled in a dual frame along side a picture of himself, Jenny and Desiree during much happier times not so long before. The photos had survived most of the fire, but were now badly damaged due to the gallons of water that undoubtedly flowed inside from the streets below. A tear fell from his eyes as he placed both damaged memories on the badly burned shelves inside the case. He fell to his knees once more, dampening his jeans in the water on the floor to retrieve something else from the wreck. He picked up a leather title belt, the true replica given to him after his first Universal Title win over Karina Wolfenden. The leather backing smelled of putrid burning material while the face plates were tarnished and cracked, damaged no doubt by the numerous people who fought to save anyone who might be trapped inside. He looked at the title for a long while, trying to hide the pure and raw emotion in his face. His name was barely visible anymore, the front plate of the title hardly legible at all. “I thought I put out the candles...” The voice bounced off the walls of the now hollow room and reverberated into his ear drums like bass at a rock concert. As soon as he heard the words and the voice that spoke them, he closed his eyes tight again, knowing exactly what went down without having to ask many questions. He cringed, knowing just who and what caused the disaster in front of him. He opened his eyes to see Jenny standing in the door way to the loft. Her makeup was a complete mess and he immediately figured it was put on that way. She held a small bag in her hands but he didn't know that it was a small gift for him inside, nor did he really care. “Killean... I just wanted to have a romantic evening with you!” He heard her but didn't respond. The hurt expression on his face was enough as he dropped the replica of his first Universal Title to the ground. The hardware hit the floor with a loud thud as he turned his head from Jenny and walked to another part of the loft. “No Killean please!” She held out the bag in her hand, pleading with him but he paid her no mind. He barely heard the words coming from the police man's mouth as he approached her. “Ma'am, you said something about candles?” He bent down where the remains of his couch were and picked up a barely recognizable teddy bear that was practically incinerated except for a small part of the body and head. He could tell right away that it was Desiree's favorite companion, irreplaceable in her mind for almost two years now. It was rare that she went anywhere without it. All the appropriate cards fell into place on the day though, so why would that be any different? He could just imagine the pained expression on the face of his daughter. It was a good thing she stayed downstairs with Vegas and Vivian. Suddenly... the flooding river and the world of PRIME weren't really that important anymore.
