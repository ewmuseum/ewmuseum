Life As We Know It (Match RP - v Aimz)

11 May 2005

Date: 11/05/05 Life As We Know It.Written: May, 2005. 'Oh, for the sake of momentum,I've allowed my fears to get larger than life.And it's brought me to my current agendum,Whereupon I deny fulfilment has yet to arrive.' - Aimee Mann. I - Happy Families. Despite the open fire places that were incorporated into the restaurant's decor, Karina couldn't help but feel a chill shuffle up her bare arms. Doing her best to find compromise between the establishment’s dress-code and hers, the K-Wolf had settled on a crimson, silky sleeveless top, paired with her one smart pair of black trousers. With her hair reverting to a deep shade of brown, she was a sight far removed them the lilac and black clad athlete who'd defeated Dave Gibson the week before. She looked... normal. A far cry from her usual self. 'I see you've finally stopped dying your hair,' Lawrence awkwardly remarked, not quite sure what angle to break the ice from. Karina and her estranged father - Lawrence - had exchanged more dialogue with the waiter than with one another... strained relationship finally giving way when he left her mother. 'My natural hair colour is blonder than this,' she shrugged his attempt at communication off with. 'Half marks for almost sounding like you care.' 'Well forgive me for actually DARING to try and talk to you,' his frustrated words grumbled. 'Fine, we'll make this short,' Karina broke the silence with, just wanting to get the meal done and dusted. 'I'm alive, am still tempted to land one on you, seeing a guy named Angelo, am still tempted to land one on you, was offered my first movie cameo last week, am still tempted to land one on you, doing a promotional shoot for K-2, am still tempted to land one on you... and, well, I'm still tempted to land one on you.' Lawrence seemed more concerned with the couple at the next table overhearing than the sharp-edged words of his daughter. Ever the stiff upper lip and starched collar to Karina's bright clothes and ever-changing hair. 'I'm glad to see you've grown out of the aggression thing,' her father offered as a sarcastic retort. 'And, yes, I'd heard about the Angelo Deville situation...' Keeping her eyes lowered, not wanting to tempt her inner-anger by looking at him quite yet, the K-Wolf continued to play with her food, letting it slide off the fork rather than raise it to her lips. 'Yeah?' Kari hit back, not liking the contempt carried in his voice's inflection. 'I've been brushing up on the you walking out on my mother situation. Want to swap notes?' 'I don't approve,' Lawrence wasted no time in sharing. 'Well, duh,' the K-Wolf scoffed with a roll of the eyes. 'He has a penis, so no surprise there.' She could almost feel Lawrence tense-up from across the table, eyes scanning to make sure they weren't overheard. 'Oh, sorry,' she rivalled his harsh sarcasm with. 'Don't the snobs who only eat here because the priciest dish is higher than the one next door like reminding that we have genetalia?' 'I know you like to think that you're a lot more grown up than you are,' his words scolded. 'But Angelo Deville is not someone whose world you want to be a part of. I got used to all the stoners and slackers you wasted your time with, but Angelo makes them look like an upstanding collection of the nation's future.' 'I really don't think you're one to comment on character,' Kari dismissed with a mix of apathy and spite. 'Or did he have an affair for NINE years, and walk out on TWO marriages. Hell, that'd make him worse than you.' Karina was angry at him, there was no doubt about that. But rather than cool her brimming emotions, the K-Wolf seemed to be propagating their energies, ready to channel at the next person who was set to step into her path. Amy Campbell. 'Look,' Lawrence tried to chip through her defences with. 'I don't know what he's told you, but Angelo Deville is an empire as much as he is a person. Don't you ever wonder who he's talking to whenever his cell phone goes off? Don't you ever wonder who it is knocking at the door, then being taken into a side room for a quiet word? Don't you ever wonder what or who is in all the rooms you haven't set foot in?' It was something she'd heard before... repeated like a mantra by those around her to the point where Angelo seemed more myth than man. 'Hey, maybe it's his secretary,' she hurled his concern back in his face with. 'He could be DOING her on the side... do you know anything about that?' 'Fine,' he eventually surrendered, realising that his daughter’s ears were closed to him. 'I tried.' After straightening his tie, Lawrence returned to his lunch, eyes wandering over to Karina's untouched plate. 'You do still eat, don't you?' he enquired in his typically blunt manner as the rim of the wine glass left his thin lips. 'Or is there some new-age starvation fad going around?' 'I grabbed a sandwich on the way here,' a flash of mischief aided her in remarking. 'I just liked the idea of ordering one of the most expensive things on the menu, then sitting right in front of you, not eating it.' 'I see you haven't exactly travelled far down the 'not being so childish' path,' Lawrence sighed, knowing all too well the persona Karina reverted back to when stubbornness was prominent in her mind. 'And I see you haven't travelled too far down the 'not being a tool' path,' narrowed eyes punctuated. 'Look, I know that I ha-' he went to utter, before switching to a sterner gear. 'I am your father Karina, whether you li-' 'I think you'll find that you're nothing more than a lonely man, stuck in a mid-life crisis, who just happens to have the same surname as me,' Kari fiercely told him, any forgiveness for his acts a fair way off. 'And that's ignoring the fact that you're a womaniser who seems to think money can buy him happiness.' Kari reached across the table, towards her father, to grab the iced water jug. 'I'd ask how that's working out for you,' she then added in further spite, 'but I don't care.' 'Well, I was hoping that we'd be able to keep this civil,' his defeatist words lamented. 'But it appears I was wrong.' 'Oh, don't you dare try and take the moral high-ground here,' the K-Wolf lectured, their already rocky relationship having received a tectonic jolt due to recent events. 'You cheated on my mother, you left her, and you tried to buy me by cutting me off if I didn't drop at your feet and worship you as the best Daddy ever.' Every movement they made was to add emphasis to their words. 'And you know what?' she then pushed. 'It might be the last thing I ever thank you for in my life. I can support myself, without you paying my rent, without you paying my bills... without you, period.' 'And don't YOU dare insinuate that I was a bad father,' Lawrence hissed back, not wanting any scene to develop. 'You and Max had everything you asked for.' 'What, so you were stockpiling good deeds so the karma hit of breaking my mother's heart wasn't quite as heavy?' Kari asked back, wide-eyed. Lawrence was used to getting his own way. Whether it be in business, or in one of his arguments with the wayward Karina. This time, however, the K-Wolf was his equal, passion behind each jabbing word. 'You leave message after message asking to meet up,' the K-Wolf pounced on the guilty silence with. 'You send a car just to make sure I don't no-show, you ask me about my boyfriend, YOU try to take shots at HIS character, and now you're trying to act like I'm being harsh on you. What was this for, Dad? You thought that after a couple of months I'd have forgiven what you did?' Fingernails turned white as Lawrence gripped his cutlery tighter. 'Do you want access every second weekend or something?' her malicious side spat at him with harsh jest. 'Your mother and I are getting back together,' Lawrence then stated to silence his daughter, the ideal set-up he'd planned ruined by her rants. 'That's what this was about.' Karina's expression blanked, her demeanour reset by the words. 'If this is a joke,' her hushed words retreated in shock, 'I will punch you out in front of everyone here... I promise.' 'Last weekend we decided to fly down to the summer house and try to fix the mess I'd caused,' Lawrence tried to explain, eyes darting around the frosty glare his daughter sent forth. 'I was expecting us to be sorting out what she took from me in the divorce... but, well, things happened an-' 'I'm tasting vomit right now,' she dryly cut him off with. 'I just thought I'd share that with you.' 'So, I'm assuming that your blessing isn't to be found here, Karina?' he sighed, placing his cutlery nearly back on the empty plate in front of him. 'Does letting your little rebellious side get some witty insults out at me really matter more to you than your mother's happiness?' 'If that was even an attempt at making me out as the selfish one here,' fuming words almost spoke in smoke, bellowing from nostrils, 'you might want to back that chair a little further away.' 'Look,' he responded by pulling his chair even closer to the table as Karina’s eyebrows moved south in anger. 'I know that you might never trust me again, and even Max has his reservations, but the fact is I'm moving back in with your mother tomorrow... I wanted to give you the courtesy of knowing beforehand.' 'I can't deal with this right now,' Karina spoke distantly as she slid her jacket over the from the back of the chair, and onto her shoulders. 'Karina,' Lawrence ordered under a hush. 'Can you just sit back do-' 'Eight years,' she spat back at him, caring little for his desire to keep things quiet and save a scene. 'Eight years you told me, admitted to me. For eight years you were with someone else before you crawled home and slept in the same bed as my mother. And now, suddenly, you've seen the light?' Karina's thighs jolted the table as she stormed, up, attracting more than a few eyes. 'Or did you see how much she'd be taking from you in the courts?' the darkest, most hurtful aspect of Karina emerged with, every word dripping with contempt. 'That is NOT fair,' Lawrence rose with, his domineering side crashing through the calm. 'No, what's not fair is you ever expecting her to forgive you,' fragile words followed into the storm's wake. 'And the same goes for me.' At a time when they should have been coming back together... the Wolfenden family was more apart than ever. II - Taking Stock. 'Oh my God! Karina!' came an exclamation from behind the K-Wolf, the surprise leaving a few drops of coffee to dribble down her bottom lip. 'I just saw through the window, and I hardly even recognised you!' After storming out of the restaurant, Karina didn't wander for... more like just across the street to a suitably contrasting establishment. Somewhere she could just get a coffee and some peace. Despite usually shying away from window seats, Kari needed the glorious warmth of the sun to recharge her... a choice she instantly regretted. 'Er,' Kari tried to buy herself a little memory recall time with. 'Hi?' Subconsciously, the K-Wolf pulled her jacket tighter around her, feeling constrained within the seat. 'You look so different to last time I saw you!' the perky, shrill voice continued to shred through Kari's ears with. 'Remember, in Paris? I was there on my honeymoon, you were at some... er, weird skate thing and whatever. Anyway, your hair looks way better now. What was it then, like, black with a blue fringe? The Ashley Simpson look really wasn't you.' 'Oh, yeah, Mindy,' the memory finally slapped her across the face. Kari could never quite understand how as soon as the final bell of school sounded, everyone was suddenly friends when they met again. Supposedly spending years trapped in the same stuffy, un-inspirational classrooms was an experience that left all students bonded for life. 'You had just married... um...' the K-Wolf made a rare stab at politeness with, trying to remember the trivial, irrelevant detail. 'Karl Fletcher,' Mindy beamed, displaying the ring to enforce the words. 'Three years in July.' 'Ah, yeah, Karl...' triggered further memories for Kari as her eyes narrowed in reflection. 'Didn't I b-' 'Break his nose?' Mindy interrupted with, even managing to say that in a chipper tone. 'Yeah, you were a little weird in high school. Had that riot-girl rage thing happening... anyway, at least you seem normal now!' The way Mindy spoke was as if the words came out a few seconds ahead of the thought behind them. As Kari arched eye-brows in an act of 'excuse me?', Mindy slid into the seat opposite. Unloading her bags, Mindy looked the K-Wolf up and down, even leaning to get a view around the table, something the K-Wolf seemed less than comfortable with. 'You're looking great!' went the saccharine compliment. 'How do you stay so skinny? I'd, like, so kill for a figure like that! Yoga? Palates? Nicotine lunches?' '2 hours of high-impact aerobics a day,' Kari revealed, frank honesty delivering the words in deadpan fashion. '8 small meals a day, an hour of free-running 5 times a week, 3 full-contact kick boxing sessions a week, running up 500 steps every morning, a couple of 2 hour Yoga sessions every weekend, at least a week snowboarding every month, 14 hours sleep a day... and Disco. Disco really helps.' 'Wow,' Mindy exclaimed. 'You are, like, wow. Athletes do always tend to be pretty flat-chested, so I should have guessed!' Adorable, isn't she? 'That is a great outfit, by the way,' the high-pitched verbal onslaught continued. 'What are you doing nowadays anyway? I heard a couple of rumours that you were a lesbian or a wrestler or had joined the Army or something...' 'I'm...' Karina went to reply, before deciding to think of an answer that would lead to the least yapping back in return. 'I'm working for my father.' 'Oh, yeah, I remember him,' was Mindy's predictable, overly-excited reply. 'Didn't he used to, like, have a driver to take you to school? Oh, yeah, I remember now! There was a rumour going around that he was caught kissing Heidi Hemming's Mom...er... so, seeing anyone now?' 'Um,' she had to pause to recover from the torrent of peppy words with. 'Yeah, his name's Angelo.' 'Ooooooh, an Italian Stallion?' Mindy excitedly enquired, resting elbows on the table while a cheeky smiled tried to pry more out of Kari. 'So, what does he do? Nabbed yourself a rich one?' 'You could say so,' Kari answered, having to play catch-up as the conversation kept shooting back to her. 'He's 7 figures, at least, I try not to look too deep.' 'He's not Mafia is he?' Mindy then joked, giggling afterwards while Karina's lips formed a pondering smile. 'He's an...' the K-Wolf pondered. 'He's an entrepreneur.' 'Oh, he one of those Internet guys?' the questions continued to pour forth, Kari not even having a chance to sip her coffee between answers. 'You hear about Jeremy Lincoln? Y’know, he was one of the IT geeks? Used to do all my Physics homework? Anyway, turns out he was, like, a mega videogame junkie, and made some game where a guy kills everyone... made him a millionaire by the age of 20!' 'Wow,' Kari feigned interest with, before kicking herself in the shin as punishment for her voice beginning to mimic Mindy's. 'Angelo is more into mergers, acquisitions, and insurance. Has his nose in information extraction too. He enjoys his work.' 'So, how long have you known him?' Mindy continued to prise every last detail out of Karina with. 'Hmmm,' Karina stalled, long enough to finish her coffee in a couple of gulping sips. 'Has to be going on 2 years now, been to-' 'Wow,' she was cut off with. 'You must be pretty serious by now then! Moved in? Engaged? Any kids?' 'Er, no,' was the K-Wolf's sharp retort. 'We've only been properly together for a few weeks. It's, well, complicated.' 'Ahhh,' Mindy exhaled, as if everything was falling into place. 'Yeah, you always did seem a little awkward about boys. I don't even remember you hooking up with anyone until you were, like 17 or something! So, is he the one? Feeling broody? Me and Karl are trying for our second. I used to be all, like, wait and have them once I’ve lived a bit, but I’d rather have them while I'm young enough to not be all ancient-Mom on them!' 'That's,' Kari hunted for words with, 'good?' 'Yeah,' Mindy's eagerness immediately filled the brief silence with. 'We're having to sell our house for somewhere bigger now though. Might as well get a 5 bedroom place for the family to grow into, huh? And the housing market is looking pretty good about now.' 'So I've heard,' Kari feigned interest again, not really tuned in for topics like marriage, kids, and property. 'You still renting?' 'Yeah,' was the instant reply. 'Living alone?' followed. 'Yeah,' Kari's organic answer phone cut in with again. There seemed to be a pattern. 'See, I could never do that,' Mindy continued to ramble. 'I'd get, like, so bored of it after a while. I need company, if ya know what I mean? I can't really do the alone thing. Anyway, it's SO refreshing to find one of us who's still a free spirit! Everyone else is settling down, getting on the property ladder, planning weddings, getting ready for kids, and you're... not!' Ouch. As subtle as one of Aimz's fists in her face. As much as Karina Wolfenden hated it, Mindy's chirp words were starting to make her think. A very, very unexpected occurrence. 'It's like you never grew up!' she added with an oblivious, beaming grin. 'I think I'm where I want to be because I drew up a life plan. I was all 'married by 21, kids right after' and all that stuff. I can't go through life drifting, I'd never get anything done!' Despite the best efforts of so many around her, Karina had never really sat back and taken stock of her life. She was to be 24 by the year's end, comfortably into mid-20's... and was still happy to cling to the bottom rung of life's ladder. She liked having an empty side of the bed to roll over to, didn't need a ceremony to enforce any of her desires, and was happy handing over rent at the end of the month. 'You must have way more other stuff than me to keep busy!' Missy kept filling the silence with. Awkwardness overcoming her, Karina reached down into her trouser pocket, prising her phone open just enough to select the next ring tone. 'Oh, excuse me,' she feigned shock with, retrieving the phone and opening it as if to take the call. 'Angelo, hey. Yeah, sure I can. See you soon. Bye!' Slapping the phone closed, Karina did her best apologetic face. 'Ack, sorry, gotta go!' 'Of course,' Mindy kept the broad smile open with as Karina slithered herself up and left some notes on her saucer. 'Busy life of a young executive and all!' 'Yeah,' Kari muttered, the slight, unfamiliar hint of guilt creeping in through her caffeine buzz. 'That's the one.' 'Well, take care!' Mindy offered up with an over-exaggerated wave. 'Who knows, next time I see you, it might be on your honeymoon, or with your kids!' 'Bye,' was the K-Wolf's less than enthusiastic reply as she left the table, wondering why what should have been a throwaway, fake-polite conversation was suddenly bringing new thoughts to the surface. Maybe Aimz was right. Maybe she was emotionally remedial... maybe she was a child who'd refused to grow up, left behind by classmates. Maybe she needed the rope to tighten, to support her rather than let her hang free. Maybe she needed a drink. Maybe she needed a few drinks. Or maybe she could do the most untypical Karina thing there was... to talk about it maturely, devoid of cynicism. III - No Laughing Matter. 'A-AHA-AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!' Every morning the group gathered in the park, stood in a circle beside the fountain. A mix of suits, jogging gear, and mechanic's overalls broadcast that there was no entry requirements... just a collection of workers who wanted to begin the day fresh and renewed. No one person conducted the orchestra of laughs, it just flowed naturally. 'A-AHA-AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!' A long-forgotten textbook in the library overlooking the park probably had a scientific term for it, but those asked by bemused passers-by simply referred to it as laughter therapy. What better way to start the day, they'd then ask rhetorically in return. 'It's time we sorted some things out,' Karina awkwardly stated. 'We're... somewhere. I just need to know where.' Sat cross-legged on the bench across the path from the bizarre group, Karina shuffled herself around to face Angelo. Already encased in a pristinely pressed suit, Deville showed a little concern as to why the K-Wolf seemed uneasy with the words she was forming. 'And we couldn't do this not surrounded by laughing lunatics, why?' Angelo asked back, not taking well to the howling background noise polluting his hearing. 'This park is somewhere I always go to think...' Kari revealed, then saw the look on Angelo's face, pre-empting his words. 'Shut up.' Seeing that Karina, dressed in old jeans and a black vest opposed to his business attire, was descending into one of her serious moods, Deville adopted a willing pose. 'Look, something made me think yesterday,' she continued, leaving a warning glare resting upon Deville to ward off any wise-crack in return. 'Everyone I know seems to be moving on, getting married, starting families, buying houses...' He may live a life beyond most of their means, but Angelo Deville still couldn’t avoid the subconscious male reaction. 'You can pull the eyelids back together, Angelo,' the K-Wolf reassured him. With his heart returning to its natural beat, he sunk back into the bench with a relieved sigh. 'They're all taking the fast track into life, indo adulthood... and what am I doing?' she pondered, voice lacking its usual vibrancy. 'Hell, you should know better than anyone else how remedial I can be about these things. What did it take? 6 weeks for me to even convince myself I didn't loathe everything about you.' 'Yeah,' Deville concurred. 'Whirlwind Romance is something you're far more suited to in the ring.' 'That's what's got me all emo...' Kari continued to spill, not entirely sure herself where the words were going. 'Please say this has nothing to do with Amy Campbell,' Deville urged. 'Because, really, let's face it... pain in the ass can be applied to her in more ways than one.' 'No, it's not,' Kari assured him, looking out the tops of her eyes to tell him the crudeness wasn't needed. 'It's just, despite how cold I was to you, despite the putdowns, the insults, the snubs, the cold shoulders, the sl-' 'Oh, yes,' Angelo recalled. 'How COULD I resist you...' 'Will you quit it!' she just about kept a straight face in exclaiming, landing a playful punch on his arm. 'I'm trying to be serio-' 'Not to mention the physical abuse,' Deville quipped slyly, rubbing his arm where the tame blow landed. 'Fine, if you're going to be like that, I'll go,' Kari feigned standing with. 'And you'll be left a single guy sitting in a park next to a bunch of... whatever they are.' Holding both palms up in open surrender, Angelo then moved one to his lips to simulate a zipping motion. 'Despite all that, we still got together,' she rejoined the path with. 'Now, don't let this swell your ego any further... but it's been... it's felt right. I never know what I'm going to get with you, in a good way. You're not... you're someone who I can just talk to about any old crap, who doesn't play life by the rulebook.' 'Well, I guess that's one way of putting it...' he agreed with a casual, almost embarrassed shrug. 'I don't tend to let my guard down around many people,' she continued. 'But there was just something there. Whenever we used to just jaw back and forth at one another, it was with a smile behind the smirk. You even seem to know when I'm joking, which most fail at. And you can keep up with me too... that doesn't really happen that often.' 'I don't have to repeat that 'why we love you' bit, do I?' Angelo enquired with a probing tilt of the head. 'That felt stupid enough the last time.' 'No, you don't have to...' Kari sighed, likening his reservedness to hers. 'I may have flipped you off right after you said it, but I remember every word, and I thank you for it. Every now and again I need something to shove me back in the right direction and yank my head up.' 'Okay, here's where I see things,' Deville unfolded his arms with, opening up his body language. 'I'm not going to sit here and pretend that I've lived some clean and pure life. I've indulged, I've gotten my hands dirty, and I've made myself a success at the expense of others. But... that got boring, fast. It just never seemed to go everywhere, every day was a replay of the previous.' With anyone else, Angelo Deville came off as a ruthless man... a persona so sharp that he could slice through anyone he came into contact with. But, just like Karina, he seemed to be able to let his guard down with just one other. 'Everyone was yes, yes, yes, just bowing in to me,' came his admission. 'They were there because I'd either paid them, or they were holding out, hoping I would. Just as I was about to pass out from boredom, you breathed life back in to me. You infuriate you as much as you leave me smiling in your wake, you see me as I am, not how I want myself to be seen.' It didn't seem like they'd been put on the earth for any other reason than to oppose one another, clashing personalities that wouldn’t give up a step to the other. But rather than the forces pushing them apart, it embraced them, the harmony or frequency such that they overlapped. 'I want to see where this takes us, not force it and push us somewhere,' Deville let out. 'So don't worry, no proposals, no craving for a family, and I think we can agree any permanent cohabitation would be dangerous for our health.' Once the connection hit, both had to resolve to make it work. Never before did they get the same feeling, the same sense that it was just... right. It wasn't something they'd wager finding again through another random coming together. 'Yeah,' went the agreement. 'Probably safer I keep using the 'your place is closer to where I need to be' tomorrow line... which basically means you have someone who makes breakfast at yours, and I have some out-of-date cereal at mine.' 'A-AHA-AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!!' 'Would you REALLY care if I pummelled just ONE of them?' Angelo then asked as the laughter grew even louder, the group attempting harmonies with it. 'I'll make sure it's one bigger than me.' 'They're just relieving stress,' Kari lectured to him, having had her own verves and fears soothed. 'So would I be,' was his mischievous appeal. With her concern appeased by Angelo, embarrassment began to creep through Karina, adding rose to her lightly freckled cheeks. The brief silence amplified, the K-Wolf did what she did best, and excused herself. 'Well, I have a photo shoot to go to,' Karina revealed, the weight lifted from her shoulders along the K-Wolf to rise. 'So if you do, at least wait until I'm far enough not to hear them scream. Oh, and thanks... I try not to get all blah too often. I guess this was just so, well, unexpected, in a way. I'm not going to say it's too good to be true, I'm allergic to icky clichés, but you know what I mean...' Both so skilled in all number of other pursuits, but equally inept in managing to word their feelings clearly and concisely. 'Hey, we're at a good place,' Deville concurred. 'Hell, I might not even punch one of those weirdoes. That's the effect you have on me... Okay, I probably will still hit one, but only once, and just in the ribs.' 'Shhh,' Kari then teased, holding her index finger up to pursed lips. 'Someone might hear and tell Baltasar that you've gone soft.' 'If the papers tomorrow read Massacre in Park, it will all be your fault,' Deville waved her off with. 'Have fun.' 'You too,' Kari called over her shoulder, their respective defences and cool exteriors preventing any verbal messages of love, but the lingering gaze letting those watching know that a special bond was holding it there. Amy Campbell could make all the snide comments she liked, but Karina's ears would be closed. No insult, insinuation, or lewd remark from the Red Raver would put Kari off her pair of tasks at hard. The first, to make what had at once seemed the impossible task of making her relationship with Angelo Deville work. The second to finally silence her spiteful rival. As she strolled out through the park's gates, the laughter seemed to lose a voice. 'You were right, Mindy,' she mumbled under her breath with a calming smile. 'I don't have any of what you have... I've got better.'
