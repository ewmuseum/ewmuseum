Rolo's new bio

24 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: March 24, 2008 My name is Tony Rolo. I am a professional wrestler in a promotion named PRIME. I returned after a three year absence with intentions of winning the Dual Halo, the biggest match PRIME holds every year and quite possibly the biggest event in all of wrestling. I failed. ********** Monterrey, Mexico A few hours after the Dual Halo. The arena is now dark. The Halo structure, for the most part, has already been disassembled. The only noise that can be heard is the janitorial staff cleaning up and down the aisles. Inside the ring, Tony Rolo stands, now dressed in his street clothes, slowly running his hand along the ropes and gently patting one of the turnbuckles in the corner. It’s almost as if he’s reacquainting himself with the feel of a wrestling ring. A familiar voice interrupts his thoughts. “They told me I’d find you out here.” The PRIME Hall of Famer turns around to see the face of Nick Stuart, the infamous play-by-play announcer that has called every match in PRIME history. Tony pauses for a second, looking around the arena before locking eyes again with Stuart. “Yeah, I’m not really sure what I’m doing out here. I guess the Halo just went by so fast, I couldn’t enjoy it.” Nick Stuart, standing outside of the ring looking up at the former champ with a bit of a grin, offers up, “Two hours and thirteen minutes went by fast?” The statement catches Rolo off guard and he looks at Stuart inquisitively. “Is that how long I was in there?” Still grinning and now nodding his head, Nick says, “Unofficially, yeah… you were in there over two hours. It was pretty impressive.” “Thanks, Nick…,” Rolo goes on almost expressionless, likely because of exhaustion from the Halo, “I missed PRIME. I missed fans cheering my name. I missed the adrenaline rush this sport always gave me.” “Is that why you came back?” Nick asks curiously. At this point, Tony Rolo steps between the ropes and hops down off the ring apron next to Stuart before responding. “You know, I thought I had retired for good.” “So did I. With everything that happened…,” Nick cuts himself off before rehashing any of the drama that occurred between Tony, Toshiaki Motoki, Hin See, Taun Pham, and his now ex-wife Allison. “You thought I couldn’t handle anymore, huh?” The reply makes Nick Stuart noticeably awkward. “That’s not what I meant, Tony.” “It’s okay, Nick… who would have been able to take all that, you know? It’s over and done now, though. I can’t change the past. I worried about things I couldn’t control for too long. It was time for me to start worrying about the present. It was time for me to come home.” “Well, we’re glad to have you back.” “Thanks. I’m glad to be back. Hey, what are you doing now? Wanna go grab a beer?” Rolo begins walking up the aisle back towards the locker room area. Nick follows closely behind with his hands in his pockets, “Yeah, I could do that.” “Cool.” “How’s Joey been?” Nick expects an interesting answer to this question. “He’s still fuckin’ nuts, that guy.” As they walk off, Tony didn’t get the opportunity to finish his reflections on night two of Culture Shock. He didn’t accomplish the goal he had set for himself of coming back and winning the Halo. He thought that was the only way to prove to the new generation of wrestlers in PRIME that he can still hang. Once he sits down and thinks about it, he’ll realize he did a damn good job finishing in seventh place, whether it be after a three year layoff or not. If there’s anywhere in this wrestling world Tony Rolo belongs, it is in PRIME. He need not prove that to anybody. ********** Now, going back to what Tony said about Joey still being nuts… March 24, 2008 Joey Troy is staying at Tony’s house while he’s on the road since Tony has more television stations, a bigger TV, and to be honest, won’t look at the cable bill when it comes next month, allowing Joey to order all the pay-per-view his little heart, or maybe a different body part, desires. The doorbell rings. By the time Joey gets to the door, he sees a FedEx truck driving away. On the ground by the door is a FedEx envelope. He picks it up and flips it over to read the shipping label. “Huh, it’s from PRIME.” Obviously the recipient is Tony, not Joey, but being a nosey bastard, Joey decides to open the envelope to see what it is. He pulls out a letter and begins reading it to himself. “Dear Mr. Rolo, This letter is in regards to your bio page on PRIME’s official website. Due to increased wrestler interest in being able to control the information being communicated on PRIME’s website, each individual on the PRIME roster now has the ability to update their own bio page. Login instructions are below. http://prime.e-wrestling.org/backstage.php Username: [detail withheld] Password: [detail withheld] Upon logging in for the first time, you will be prompted to change your password. Best regards, Lisa Tyler Vice President of Talent Relations” Joey stands there on the front stoop of Tony’s home, still looking at the letter. And suddenly his eyes light up. If this were a cartoon, a drawing of a light bulb would have lit up above Joey’s head. He proclaims, “Tony, you want to back out of that Dual Halo bet we made… fine by me. But you’re gonna pay.” ********** Forty-five minutes later Talking to himself, Joey blurts out, “Wow, Tony, I’ve known you all of these years and I never knew that Lance Bass was your hero. There’s so much I can learn from this PRIME bio of yours.” ********** Link to Tony Rolo’s bio - http://prime.e-wrestling.org/bio.php?username=[detail withheld]
