A day late

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: A day late for the RP deadline Skylar is on the phone to his Christian friend, Thomas, who is a moron. Thomas is essentially a disciple of SkyMont's cult, but he also attends church several times a week because he is a really big geek with no mates. If this was a film there'd be a split-screen. But this isn't a film, it's Skylar's Narrative, and so the receiver is pressed to his mouth. Skylar: There are things in this world that are to insects like what insects are to us. Thomas: Uh, yeah. Yeah. Cool, so how did you get weed then, if you're not with Roger? Skylar is smoking a massive spliff. He takes a big puff. Skylar: ...Because... First of all, it's called "skunk" and Roger's not the only dealer I know, OBVZiously. Thomas: Heh, oh yeah. But how did you get the money, man? You aint got a job and your PRIME Wrestling paycheck doesn't come through till July. Skylar: I sold this kid a fake a gram of Methadrone. Thomas: HAHAHA! What? H - Skylar: It was crushed up Ritalin pills. Thomas: HHAHA Hahaha. That's funny. Skylar: Heh, yeah. Thomas: How much did you sell it to him for? Skylar: Twenny pounds. Got five left. Thomas: Haheh... Skylar: Methadrone's fucking up London, man. Thomas: It was fake. What could happen to him if he did that gram of ...Ritalin pills? Skylar: I really don't know. Thomas: Ohoh... Skylar: Only God can judge me anyway. I feel no guilt, only God can judge me. Skylar looks at himself in the mirror infront of him. Thomas: No. I can judge you and I say you're wrong, Skylar. Skylar: Fuck you. Thomas: What you did was wrong, morally Skylar: Fuck you you're just a judgemental-hypocrite-loser has-to-go-to-church-to-make-friends guy. Thomas: What? Who's that Skylar: You're such an idiot. You actually judge people yet you go to church three times a week. You don't even believe in him. Thomas: Now just ho - Skylar: Fuck you, Thomas. And you're just like Timothy. Thomas: Wh - Skylar: SHIPLEY! All you soulless leeches trying to JUDGE me! He drops the phone receiver and it wails to be picked back up in Thomas' voice. Thomas: STOP SAYING THAT SORT OF STUFF. YOU'RE TAKING IT TOO FAR SKYLAR. STOP NOW. YOU'RE NOT MAKING ANY SENSE. A branch of green foliage grows into the right corner of Skylar's box of vision. A rodent scuttles along in the shadows of the box's left corner. Then there are the lights and the foliage starts growing at an extremely fast rate. Echo: JUDGE me. JUDGE me. JUDGE me. JUDGE me. * Dailtone * Skylar: Only God can judge me He looks down at the camera (despite this not being a film). Flicks his orange hair. The latest in his line of T-Shirts sports the slogan: "YOU ARE GOD"
