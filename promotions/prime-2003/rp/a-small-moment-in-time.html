A Small Moment in Time

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: ReVolution 172 For years, he had been called many things. A murderer. A killer. Ruthless. An asshole. A gangster. A pawn. A hitman. God. The Devil. His life had been unlike any other and at the end of the long days ahead; he wouldn't have it any different. He had been a performer that shined on the giant stage whether it is in front of millions of adoring fans or in front of a few eyes that looked towards him for guidance. His knuckles crack in the background, a sign that the man who once existed had long left the building. Instead, it had been replaced by the man many thought they'd never see again. Fear had replaced their comfort; prayers replaced their words. People around the world had forgotten of the hell he could wrought with his eyes. Now, they would be reminded with a sword as sharp as a kiss. A kiss that could kill. "Deliver me from my enemies, oh God." CRACK! Knuckles, meet jaw. Jaw, well, I'm going to guess you're not quite in the mood to be introduced to anyone. Craig stands there, this odd smile on his face, as he looks at his strung up victim who happens to be playing a very convincing part as a punching bag right now. He could probably win an Oscar for his performance as Craig walks around his body before he cracks his knuckles again and unloads with another shot to the man's jaw. "Defend me from all those that rise up against me." CRACK! Blood slowly trickles from his swollen lips and lands on the stained concrete below him. His right eye is black and blue from the random shots to his face as it tries it best to remain open; it's a battle it's quickly losing. The staleness from the empty warehouse sits uncomfortably in his nose as he can feel it starting to suffocate him. Craig can only watch in earnest while each wave of anger hits him square in the face and he lets out it in such a primitive way. For so long though he had tried to be a person he wasn't; sophisticated and humane. No longer though would he run away from the person he was. The body count was slowly starting to rise. Silvia and Warren. There were also two others with names he couldn't even be bothered with learning. "Deliver me from the work of inequities." CRACK! As his fist slams into the man's jaw again it's not loss upon him that one day he will be asked as to why he kept increasing that body count. He would respond to them with a smile before a calm fuck you left his lips with not a single ounce of remorse trickling out. At this point in the game, in this little war, the amounts of deaths that stained his hands were inconsequential. It was all about sending a message to the man who started all of this so he remembered how far he would go. Then, when things went quiet and the anxiety built up to a level that would nearly kill the man who had fired the first shot, he would strike in an effort tenfold of anything imaginable. The games had just been started to play with no end in sight. "The Savior from the blood of me." BANG! The final shot though doesn't come from the stiff fist, but from the end of a nuzzle attached to a gun. As the bullet rips through his tendons and he takes his final breath, he can only think of one thing. Craig looks at the lifeless body before turning on his heel not pausing a moment to mourn this man's death. Instead, he walks out into a side room of the warehouse filled with a variety of shelves and cabinets long forgotten. In the middle of the room are two children, a boy and a girl, surrounded by their mother who can only look on in fear. She looks at the man that has just killed her husband with tears streaming down her cheeks wondering as to why a man would do such a thing to her husband. Craig just continues to walk out of the room with his hands in his pockets. She would move but the chains holding her and her children down make it kind of difficult for her to that. Hours ago she stopped trying to fight against the restraints and turned her focus into hoping the mysterious man who had ripped them from their house would just let her go. As she watches him walking away, she gets this sickening feeling that nothing like that intends to happen. Instead, she can only sit there paralyzed as Craig picks up a large gas canister from a metal table and begins pouring the liquid all over the room. Horror fills her eyes as the gasoline hits their body and immediately fear begins to settle in between that uncomfortable spot of hope and loss. “What have we done to you?!” she yells at him as the tears rip from her eyes with ferocity. Craig just shakes his head as he walks up to the scared mother and kneels before her. “It’s not what you have done. It’s what your husband has done. It’s what his bosses have done and what their bosses did as well. You, you’re just a necessary casualty. So, you have done nothing. Just like the people I loved and cared about did nothing to them,” he tells them as he stands up and begins walking around the empty warehouse. His mind is filled with thoughts as he tries to convince himself that he’s right in what he’s doing. “Any other time and you would’ve been left alone, not bothered. Your body, it sends a message to them all that this is just the beginning. Tomorrow will be a different day and so forth. For the Christians that remain among them, they’ll know this as the end days. For me? I just call this one fucked up time. That’s why--,” his voice trails off as he looks over at the mother. The sense of pain is evident in his eyes as he looks at them and wrestles with the decision over whether or not he should do this. “I’m sorry.” He then begins to walk away from her as he continues to think about what it is that he’s going to do. Craig can’t bring himself to even look at the kids and hear their pleas. This wasn’t their fault. It was their father’s. As the images of Nicole and Katie fill his head though those warm thoughts leave his mind. They hadn’t done anything wrong. They hadn’t deserved their fate. Then, he thinks about his unborn children laid to waste because of one man’s rage. Their lives snuffed out in a mere moment. The times had changed. "Please... please, what can I do?" she asks him with pounds upon pounds of sincerity and remorse in her voice. She has nothing to be sorry for as Craig pulls a lighter out of his hands and looks at the helpless mother. He then begins to walk away from her before reaching the threshold to the building. Craig flashes the former wife and the fleeting mother with a small smile on his face. "Pray." With that, flames explode from the lighter as it hits the ground. Investigators would later announce that four people died in that fire. No one would suspect that somebody was behind all of this, instead calling the crime a suicide-homicide. For a few others though, they would remember this signal. The four who had become before were just warning shots. This was the shot between the eyes.
