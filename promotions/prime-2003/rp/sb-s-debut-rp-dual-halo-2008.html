SB's Debut RP - Dual Halo 2008

7 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 3/7/08 January 16th, 1999. A younger (and even handsomer? No, impossible.) Simply Beautiful stands, breathing heavily is in his locker room. He’s soaked in sweat from his pre-match workout. SB: Let’s fuckin’ do this. He pounds his taped-up fists together and walks through the door. The camera follows him as he walks, amped up as all hell, down the corridor. It’s the backstage area of a fairly large arena. Actually, it’s the Fleet Center. He’d worked big venues, yeah…but never a place that had packed in 19,000 screaming fans. It’s the first step of upstart CPW’s grand scheme to get their foot in the business’ door. It’s a major three day event, with wrestling, music, and stage performers, all for charity. Don’t ask me why the guy running the place thought it would work. But hey, he had a passion for the business. He was insistent on not bringing in any big stars. He wanted to make his own by taking the top indie talent he could find and building from the ground up. With a better business model, he could have succeeded. But that ain’t the point of this story, jack. SB was known around the circuit, but not too well. His biggest fans were smarts – after all, those guys can’t get enough of gimmick wrestlers that can work. And baby, can SB work. He was a machine out there, tearing through most any opponent his promoter’s could throw at him. And that’s where CPW’s bookers came in, lead by head booker Jake Graham. He thought he could build the promotion around SB. To give SB credit, he was young, and the company had no idea how to market him. But at this point in time, he’s walking to his match. His “world” title match, the first ever for CPW. His opponent is Chris Cannon. SB wants this bad. He knows the title has no value, but he’s convinced that he can make the title into the top title in the business. (CPW PBP man Jimmy Harris calls the action as the camera cuts to the action in the ring) JH: SB ducks under the lariat – SEXYKICK! NO! Cannon ducked under it! Roll the Dice! Spinning Reverse DDT! The cover! ONE! TWO! THREEEEE!!! Fade out at Cannon celebrates, SB lying motionless. ==================================================== Fade in on the Italian Icon himself, Simply Beautiful: AKA Andrew Rossi. His blonde hair is perfect. His shades are pulled down on the brim of his nose, resting comfortably on his face. The Italian Flag bandanna his fans are so used to seeing him wear is tied around his bicep, and his patented SB-brand navy blue t-shirt reads “Jackson Heights Jackhammer” in brazen red letters. Track pants and SB Reeboks complete the look. SB: A lot of people have been asking me why I’m here. My friends in the business are telling me that when you got to PRIME, you better be ready to devote yourself 100% to it. Well, I am in both A1E and EPW. You can say that I’ve stretched myself a bit thin, and maybe my timing on joining the PRIME roster was a bit poor. But I would have no choice but to whole-heartedly disagree with you, sunshine. You see, I’m in exactly the place I want to be right now. Look at all the major events going down in professional wrestling today. Do you notice a trend? SB’s name is on every single card, and in a prominent position, I might add. Why limit myself when I’m on top of my game, and wrestling at my best level since…since ever? The Icon knows when to hold ‘em and when to fold ‘em. And when it comes down to a crack at the Golden Ticket in PRIME by winning the Dual Halo, I’m all in. Handsomest damn poker player you ever saw, sweetheart. It’s time for SB to add “World Champion” to his resume. And you chumps might not know this, but these multi-man matches – the clusterfucks, if you will – these are my specialty. I won the NAPW Sole Survivor Match after 50 grueling minutes in the ring with 29 of the best wrestlers on earth. I came thisclose (makes hand gesture signaling just how close he means) to winning the Pier Six Brawl over in A1E. So the Dual Halo feels less like the world’s biggest cage, and more like…home. (smiles) I ain’t nervous about this baby, even if I am the new kid on the block. There’s fifteen schmucks who’ll be out there who don’t even freakin’ work here. Those are the jackoffs that oughta be worried, not me! I’m debutin’ baby! All eyes are on me, I’m in the motherfuckin’ spotlight kid! You think I’m gonna clam up and disappoint because the odds are against me? (laughs) The odds can’t be against ya if they ain’t for anybody else, isn’t that right? The rest of the poor sons ‘a bitches locked in there with me don’t have an advantage either. So when you get right down to it, this is anyone’s match. It’s gonna get crazy. It’s gonna get wild. But I’m the best wrestler on the planet, baby. And everyone knows I can get wilder and crazier than the wildest and the craziest. Just ask your mom, sunshine! fade out..
