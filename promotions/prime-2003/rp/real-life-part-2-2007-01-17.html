Real Life - Part 2

17 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 17/01/2007 It had all started on a dark and stormy night prior to a brief outing in the King Of Kings Tournament. Cain was mentally disturbed and he had located the reason for it too. When I say located, I actually mean hunted down. When I say hunted down, I probably mean executed an attempted assassination. It had been apparent since Cain’s return that he had been distracted and that wrestling was merely an outlet for his frustration. However on this particular night in history, Cain had been busy taking his frustrations out on the man that was vying him for his wife. Blood had been spilt that night in this man’s own bedroom and Cain was enjoying every single moment of it. ********************************************************************************************** Cain put his cigarette back to his lips and then stood by the window, with his back to the wall. The victim could feel the blood trickling down his face and despite the blow, he could still clearly smell the sickening smoke swirling around the room. He lay on his back looking up at the ceiling hoping for some kind of salvation yet all he saw was the Devil that he had himself summoned the moment he started his pursuit. “I’ve had two years of you looking down on me with your contemptuous attitude and your blind obsession with my family…Two fucking years of you constantly trying to come between me and my wife…Making me sick with jealousy and rage, because someone is threatening to take away the one thing I thought I’d never find…The love of a good woman…Do you know how that feels like, to worry continually that someone is continually trying to chip away at your entire world? You say you hate me, you ain’t got a mother fucking clue about hatred…Because I hate what you did to me, what you made me and how far you pushed me…And now…When you go to bed at night…I don’t want you to dream about my wife…I don’t want you to lust after her…I want you to remember this night…Remember who I am…And remember…what hatred can do…” He swiftly bends down at grabs his rival by the throat, hauling him up with one hand and slamming him against the wall. The dark obscures the victims’ vision but he distinctly hears the swish of a switchblade knife and then feels its sharp coldness against his neck. His fearful sweat mixes with the blood that has stained his skin and now the consequences of his contempt are coming to light. For just like Tony Gamble before him, this man is now faced with an out of control Paul Cain, holding a knife. Only this time, there’s no security and on this occasion, it’s not about business, it’s about the most personal thing in a man’s life: family. “You spent all your time looking down on me thinking you were better than me, thinking you could take my family away and make a better job of taking care of them…I had to spend days and nights dealing with you, knowing you were there, knowing there wasn’t a damn thing I could to do to stop you…Thinking that I was slowly going insane…Do you know how that feels? DO YOU?!” Cain’s voice is shaky and unstable, which matches the lack of steadiness in his hand. The blade trails down from the opponents’ neck in a line down his chest and pausing at the groin area. He pushes the tip of the knife against his rival, causing him to whimper like a puppy that’s had his tail stepped on. Paul’s eyes are wild and full of emotion whilst this mysterious man is full of fear, in fact, he’s terrified. With a violent rip, Cain tears off the gag and throws it to the floor as he snarls at the victim. “It’s not nice being helpless, is it? Got any last words?” His bottom lip is quivering as he looks down at the blade in Cain’s hand. The tears are welling in his eyes and the emotion chokes him, stopping the words from coming forward. This was not how he had expected this to happen, he hadn’t even seen it on the horizon. He had always dreamed that one day Cain would be gone and would become a non entity. There was a slender hope, no matter how small that he would slip up and she would leave him. He had clung onto this anorexic possibility for years now and he would not let it go. There was no chance Cain would come for him, he would be too fearful of the consequences and stood to lose too much. So the game could just keep being played over and over until one day Cain would lose and he would win. THAT is how he believed it should happen, this was not part of the equation. “I…I’m sorry…” He hated having to say those words because he had no apology to give this man. The only times he had even attempted some kind of peace talk was to solidify his position as a decent human being in her eyes. In actuality he truly despised everything that Cain stood for, all the things he did and all the things he was. This individual did not deserve the wife and child that he had gained, Paul Cain was scum who had no idea how to treat the woman that he loved. Now, the apology that he made was no more sincere than all the others, he just wasn’t ready to die. He had to live so he could tell her what Paul had done and to put the nail into their coffin. Cain deserved nothing and that is what he would end up with. “You are no more sorry for what you have done, than I am for what I’m doing now.” With that, Cain drew his knee up hard into the individuals’ stomach, sending him crumpling back down to the floor. That same boot which connected with bone just moments ago, takes another scalp as it cracks into the rib area. Only this time, Paul doesn’t stop, over and over again as a therapeutic release for all the time he spent looking over his shoulder. His enemy is now into a tight protective ball but that barely reduces the impact of Cain’s rage. Then a different tact as Paul takes hold of his victim by the hair and pulls his head back. “Something else to remember me by…” The seering pain shoots through his adversary as Cain’s blade cuts a near perfect horizontal line across the left cheek. He screams out in agony as he feels it burn while Cain simply drops him back down to the floor, emotionless and cold. The whimpering resumes again as Cain touches the man’s face with his boot, checking to see that the damage has been done. Satisfied that his work is done, he stands at his full height looking down with that same disgust in his eyes that he always had for this pitiful being. Now the tables have been turned and Paul is the one in control with the hunted having nowhere to run and nobody to hide behind. He would smile with contentment if he did not feel the amount of hatred that was running through his veins at the mere sight of his enemy. It was time to leave because if he stayed, he would risk throwing his whole life away. “For all the time you looked on me with contempt and disgust, believe me I’ve felt the exact same way about you…You’re a pathetic miserable individual who has nothing in his life and doesn’t have the courage to go and get a life for himself…You’d rather fester like a virus and spread your poison into something good and pure…I hate you…In fact, I don’t just hate you, I pity you…Look at you, face down in your own blood…In Mummy and Daddy’s house…I pity you for thinking you could ever be me…For wasting years of life trying to fuck me up…Although…You have achieved one thing…You brought me back…You taught me how to hurt people again…So when you hear about what I’ve done…Just remember any blood spilt by me…Is the same blood that’s now on your hands too…” Cain turns his back slowly to the fallen rival and pauses for a moment. “That cut will be a nice little scar before too long…Let it be a warning…You come for what’s mine again…I’ll kill you.” He then walks slowly towards the doorway and leaves this room where so much has passed. As he departs from the scene of his own crime, he whispers under his breath. “It’s a bad time to know me.” ********************************************************************************************** That was when the trouble began, two months ago and a crime of passion later. No matter what your motives might be, to storm into another man’s home and terrorize him for a night does not look wonderful in the eyes of the law. Howver, Paul had thought he had managed to get away with the perfect crime. Exact your revenge and scare your victim half to death, that will make sure you don’t hear from him again, right? Wrong. What Cain hadn’t bargained for was the fact that despite the terror that his rival had felt, running crying to the police was a beautiful way to drive a wedge between Cain and his wife. He had calculated the risks and the rewards, given it the greatest amount of careful consideration before making his move and then he struck. That was when the trouble really began. ********************************************************************************************** The water from the tap cascaded over the head of Paul Cain as he reached blindly to turn it off. He tossed his head back and looked into the bathroom mirror with a self satisfied smile. Two nights removed from the break in and he still couldn’t remove the triumphant feeling of success that he had running through his heart and mind. It was a good time to be Paul Cain, a time that he hadn’t been able to remember for some while now. He spotted his wife standing behind him in the doorway and he smiled a smile of appreciation towards her. She returned the gesture but without as much effort as him. They stood side by side in front of the mirror as she ran her hand over his freshly shaved head. “Why did you do it?” She had hated the fact that his trademark long hair had been sacrificed in favour of a new image. He shook his head in amazement that she was still complaining about that even now, it was only a head of hair after all. “I told you baby, the right image is everything, you know that. Don’t you worry though darlin’, I’ll grow it all back one day, I promise.” He stroked her chin with a smile yet this time he received a stony cold glare back. She took hold of his wrist and pulled it away from her face. Upon seeing her a moment ago, he considered how attractive she looked with her robe wrapped tightly around her voluptuous figure, reddy-brown hair falling down her back and eyes that could mesmerize even the toughest of men. Now though, his heart froze as he saw the look fury that he had only witnessed a few times before. The words ran through his mind, “she knows, she fucking knows”. “Why the hell did you do it?! He’s just some guy who has feelings for me, it’s not his fault, it’s not my fault, it’s nobodies fault but you had to storm in there didn’t you?! The same old Paul Cain, you can change your hair, change your image…hell you could even go to a Tibetan monestry and try to reinvent yourself, but it won’t work will it?! You’re still the same paranoid and jealous guy that I’ve always known, only NOW, NOW you think it’s okay to beat the shit out of someone for something he can’t help?!” Cain raised his eyebrows in disbelief and turned his back to her. “Don’t fucking turn your back on me!” One of the things that Cain had first noticed about his wife when he met her, was her strong personality and her ability to stand up for herself. Having worked alongside airheads casted to play his love interest for the majority of his career, Cain had been relieved to find a real woman on the ‘outside’. Not only was she stunningly beautiful but she was razor sharp too, she knew how to take care of herself and she knew how to put others in their place. Cain had never had a mother in his life, he’d learnt not to respect women as a result however this woman, she commanded respect wherever she went. She had taught Paul a lot, namely that when she says don’t do something, you stop doing it. He slowly turned around to face her and stretched his arms out wide almost challenging her. “What? What now? What do you want me to say huh?! Little bitch deserved what he got!” Then came the slap right across the face, the crack could be heard reverberating around the bathroom. He stared down at the floor for a moment taking in the fact that she had had the courage to take a shot but then that was one of the things he loved about her. There were no tears, no emotions except anger, frustration and confusion as she stared intensely at her husband. He touched his hand to his lip as he looked to her from the corner of his eyes. “I had NO choice.” She leans back exasperated and runs her fingers through her hair, holding it tightly before screaming at him. “You had no choice?! Are you out of your mind?! Of course you had a choice! You could have talked to me about it, not go around there and beat the hell out of him! He can’t help how he feels, he loves me, what can he do about that, could YOU change how you feel about me at the drop of a hat?! Of course you fucking couldn’t, I can’t BELIEVE you would do this!” Cain methodically reaches for a towel and dabs at the scratch that has formed on his top lip. He tries to remain calm and avoid the confrontation for he had his reasons and they were his alone to know. However she would not let up and continued yelling, pushing at him, demanding the answers that he wasn’t prepared to give. Even as he walked out of the bathroom, he felt her hands grip around his arm, pulling at him to try and bring him back to face the music. With as little force as possible, he dragged himself away from her. Wheeling around, he put one finger to his lips to silence her and in a hushed tone, growled at her. “You’ll wake the baby!” She glanced across to the crib where their baby girl slept peacefully, oblivious to the storm that was brewing around her. He stormed over to her and leaned into her ear, whispering intensely his reasoning that he had not wanted to expose to her. “I work my ass off to make sure this family has whatever it needs, I’m the one who worries about you and our daughter and whether you’re both okay, I’m the one who lives in the real world doing everything he can to ensure that there is a roof over our heads, food on the table and that I become someone you can both be proud of…And believe me, I’m sick and tired of that little bitch trying to take my family away from me while I’m out experiencing what real life is all about, rather than some little imaginary playground that he lives in! He got what he deserved…a reality check!” She shakes her head at him and throws her arms up in the air in despair. “I told you that nothing was going on between us, he just needs me, he’s a lost soul and you know ALL about but you just don’t want to see it in someone else! What the hell got into you?! I just…I can’t believe you would go to those lengths, hurt somebody that badly just because you got all spooked by some guy I don’t have feelings for, telling me that they love me! Look at you, you’ve had girls all over you before but do you see me lose the plot like that?! Hell no! I need you stop this and I need you to get a grip on yourself and fast!” He had stopped listening already as he slipped on a T-shirt over his jeans. He knew he was justified in what he had done and that was all that mattered to him. Having to stand and listen to the prosecution cite psychological reasons behind why the victim really was a victim, didn’t interest him in the slightest. He never expected her to understand so none of this surprised him and he fought against it, pushing her words away. “Someone else loves me…Big fucking deal! I love YOU, not HIM! I’m with YOU, not HIM!” Cain stopped in his tracks as he heard these words just as there was a loud banging at the door from downstairs. He looks at her with first sign of apology in his eyes but then remembers the slimy words written out by his rival and it all comes flooding back to him. Whirling away from her, he throws the bedroom door open and jogs down the stairs, followed by her screaming at him to stop. “Don’t walk away from this, we need to talk! Paul, please!” He reaches the door and opens it, with a look of resignation on his face. He knew who it was and what they wanted but he wasn’t going to hide from them. He would never hide from anyone, no matter who they were or what power they had. Maybe he had done wrong, perhaps passion was not a good enough reason to commit a crime but he would stand by it nonetheless. There they were as he predicted, two men in uniform waiting for their own explanations. “Paul…Paul Cain?” The officer to the left was a tall and imposing older man with a graying goatee beard whilst to his right a younger African-American short and stocky male. Cain turned look over his shoulder to see his wife now show signs of sadness as tears begun to well at her eyes. He sighed and turned back to the officer, nodding his head. “I’ve a friend who’s been through all this shit…I know how it works.” The officer explained on the doorstep about how charges had been pressed against Cain for grievous bodily harm and forced entry. Standard procedures were fired at Paul who simply stared behind the officers and into space. His wife’s screams could be heard but he couldn’t make out the finer details as everything around went into an inaudible blur. All he could think of was how he had done the right thing and he would hold onto that no matter what. The handcuffs clasped around his wrists but he could barely feel them as he was lost in his own little world. Whatever would happen now, this man had learnt the cold hard truth, that you cannot take from someone else without paying the piper somewhere along the line and that for Cain, made all this worthwhile. Her screaming could still be heard as his jacket was put around his shoulders by one of the officers, as the other slowly escorted him onto the front step. “I’M PREGNANT!” Now THAT he heard as the policemen dragged him away towards the car. It was at that point for a moment that he doubted whether he had done the right thing. Now he began to wonder if he should never have opened Pandora’s Box but it was too late to waiver, in fact now more than ever perhaps he was right to protect his expanding family. Whatever happened now, new child or not, the deed had been done and there was no going back. It was time to stay strong and true to his convictions. Score settled, debt paid. ********************************************************************************************** You don’t have to be black to experience police harassment, in fact being white and somewhat of a celebrity can make you an even easier target. Paul Cain could attest to that as he once again sat in a police interview room for the umpteenth time in the last month and a half. He had grown more and more impatient with the so called ‘experts’ of law enforcement, to the point that now he barely acknowledged them when they came calling. Today was no different as he sat on the extremely uncomfortable steel chair, gazing around the depressingly bare room waiting for someone, anyone, to come back and check to see if he was even alive as it felt that long. Whilst his relationship with his wife was confused at best, his relationship with the police was at crisis point. His love rival had been relentless in his pursuit of getting Paul sent down for assault and battery but that wasn’t all. The investigating officer, who Cain mentally referred to as Barney Fife for his incompetence, was now attempting to pin the beating of two goons hired by Tony Gamble. Now just because these two guys HAPPENED to be the ones that administered a beating to Cain himself surely didn’t necessarily HAVE to mean that Cain could be involved in what looked like retaliation. The police were having a field day playing with a celebrity and PRIME were getting irritated. With his wife distant at best and his employers asking themselves just why they re-hired Paul Cain again, extra police investigation was unwanted and proving difficult. There was movement outside the investigation room and the door was slowly open. In walked ‘Barney Fife’ with his hands behind his back. He looked irritated and spotting that, Cain smiled at him slightly. “Something wrong…officer?” He could see ‘Barney’ biting his bottom lip in frustration with his breathing heavy. Eventually his words came out and it satisfied Cain to see them being spat out. “You…are free to go.” Cain raised his eyebrows and grinned from ear to ear in satisfaction. “I’m sorry officer…I didn’t quite catch that…give that to me again? I’m…?” Another deep breath as ‘Barney’ attempted to retain his cool. “Free…to…GO.” Pausing, Cain scratches his head mockingly in a confused manner. “Well now, this is a pleasant surprise…So come on, what happened then?” The face of ‘Barney’ remained contorted into a scowl and he fought desperately to keep his calm. “Your initial charge of Assault and Battery has been dropped, your victim no longer wanted to pursue the case…” Stroking his chin thoughtfully, Cain pushed the officer further. “Interesting…What about that stunt you pulled with the two goons, realized that wouldn’t stick hey?” ‘Barney’ rolled his eyes and looked back down at Cain once more. “We pulled in the guys that attacked the victims concerned.” Paul slowly rose to his feet and walked around the table with the self satisfied grin on his face. “I don’t suppose…I might get an apology, hey? I mean, this kind of police harassment can be very stressful on a man and his family. You know, even my employers have been getting a bit antsy, that’s not good for business. Not only that but my wife, she’s pregnant you know and stress isn’t good for a woman at her stage of pregnancy…Not that I give a shit but still…I really think I’m owed here.” The officer wouldn’t even look at Cain as he walked by. “Don’t push your luck, just get out before we do any more investigating!” As Cain moved towards the doorway, he laughed to himself. “I wouldn’t waste your time, you’ve done enough of that already.” With that, Paul closed the door behind him as the officer pulled out his file on Cain from behind his back and slammed it onto the table. There’s nothing worse than having that fish on the line and then losing it. He didn’t know how but Paul Cain had gotten away and there was going to be no catching him, not this time. ********************************************************************************************** Sitting on the front step of the police station, Cain took in the mixture of cigarette smoke and fresh air. He had spent a lot of time being the play thing of the local cop shop and so now he relished every moment of knowing they couldn’t touch him again. Yet something didn’t sit right with him, why would his equally insanely jealous rival back down? The two thugs mattered little to Cain but this twist in the tale was something else, why after all this blood shed that Cain had legitimately caused, would the victim suddenly go running? In truth, Paul should have been pleased but there was just a feeling that things weren’t right, this wasn’t the actions of a jealous man. Any regular guy who wanted to steal someone’s wife would remove the obstacle for as long as possible, Cain’s rival had the ideal chance to but passed it up, why? The relaxing effect of the cigarette was doing little to clear Paul’s mind as he wondered if there was more to come. As he took another long drag, a voice behind him spoke almost serenely. “Well? Aren’t you going to thank God?” Paul turned his head to the right where the voice came and saw the tall individual, all 6 foot 8 of him, standing with his arms folded across his chest. The man’s jet black hair tumbled down to his shoulders as he smiled warmly to Cain. Whether that warm was genuine or not remained to be seen as he took a step towards Paul whose mouth slowly opened in some surprise. “Malachi.” ********************************************************************************************** Fast forward to the present day and swap the police station for a hospital. Paul Cain sat in the far corner of Malachi’s room which was plunged in darkness. A sliver of moonlight poked through the curtains and shone onto the prone body of the man calling himself God. The night staff could be heard moving around outside, they knew nothing of Paul’s presence and he was happy to keep it that way. He preferred to be alone with his thoughts as he watched Malachi protectively. The images of the brutal attack carried out by Chainz on Malachi were still burnt into the mind of Cain. This man had gone out of his way to choose Paul, to save him from a life spiraling out of control and now his mortality was clear for all to see. The vicious and sadistic behaviour of Chainz would not go unpunished, Cain had become sure of this as he watched his mentor lying asleep in his bed. Paul rose from his seat and moved slowly through the darkness to Malachi’s bed side. He rested one hand on ‘God’s’ shoulder and bowed his head. “My Saviour, I will not allow mere mortals to distract me from what must be done. I shall not ignore the sacrifices you have made for me, my Father. I will not be distracted from the path of righteousness by comic book opposition, nor by a partner I know nothing of nor trust. When the time presents itself and the Evil stands before me, I shall strike it down with a fury and vengeance that you shall be proud of. I promise, Majesty, that I will not fail you and I shall repay you for your sacrifices with my eternal service. Rest now my weary Father and I shall do your bidding, I promise you this with my life.” Cain moves slowly away from the bed and heads towards the door of the bedroom. If Dusk was looking for loyalty, he would not find it here. If Garbage Bag Johnny and Face-Eater were looking for a fair fight, then they would not find it here. God has been wounded and his Disciple Of Apocalypse is on the warpath. One, two Chainz, the Son of God is coming for you. Scene fades out to black.
