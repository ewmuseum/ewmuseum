One life of tribulations... another life of unknowns

17 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/17/2006 Thursday, February 16, 2006 11:50 AM The steam was rising from the fresh cup of coffee that was placed in front of Killean. After accepting it with a thank you, he shifted his position to a more comfortable one allowing him to look at Mary while he spoke to her. He had only arrived a few minutes earlier and to his surprise, she really didn’t show all that much animosity towards him. However she was not being her friendly usual self either… but not all was lost. He was about to make amends with a friend, the wife of possibly his biggest rival in the sport of professional wrestling. Killean took a small sip of coffee while Mary started the conversation. “So if I may get straight to the point, what exactly are you doing here Killean?” Whoa, no shit! She really was getting to the point, no small talk, something that Killean was used to with her. “Honestly, I was just down in New Orleans and I figured I had to catch a plane up north so I would stop by, see how everything is going. How are you? How’s Georgie?” His nervousness showed as he took the mug to his mouth again, his hand shaking a little when he returned it to the table. “Georgie is good, she is napping right now. Me? I’m ok I guess. But that’s not why you’re here Killean, admit it.” Her tone was not demanding yet it was hard. Since Killean had decimated Matt at the ReVolution previous to The Great American Nightmare, Mary seemed a little cold towards him. Who could blame her really? Matt was her husband after all. Killean didn’t even know what kind of relationship they still had, if any. He only knew that Mary was staying with her parents. Other details he could do without. “I’ll admit that I didn’t come here to just check up on you. Really, the biggest reason for me stopping by was to give you a legitimate apology for what you saw back at ReVolution in Kentucky.” Even his voice was shaky. Most of the time, Killean could be considered a confident and reliable man. But now, he had to clear his throat of the proverbial frog and he was shaking from nervousness. Even a bit of sweat happened to pool just above his eyebrow. “Why are you apologizing to me?” She made herself sound confused at the apology, but even Killean could read that she was putting on a show now. “Matt is the one who returned to wrestling on his own accord. I always told him I didn’t like it, but he wouldn’t listen.” Confidence started to return to the Supreme Machine at that moment. Not only did she make it obvious that she was making up some old wives tale, but she also found is necessary to excuse herself from the table to offer Killean a muffin. He shook his head no as he took another quick sip from the mug, this time catching some eye to eye contact with the wife of his former best friend. “Don’t bullshit a bullshitter Mary. I’ve played that game and had it played on me. I don’t like it. Simply put, I came here as a good gesture. I wanted to make things right between us because I feel I owe that to you and to myself.” Mary pursed her lips as she thought of a reply. Truthfully the battle at hand was not between Killean and Mary. It was Killean and Matt. Yet Mary still felt somewhat involved even though she didn’t want to be. However, she didn’t blame Killean for how she felt. “I know what you mean Killean. The truth is I have never seen you as a bad person. I know how frustrating Matt can be. Being with him for as long as I have, it kinda gives me a sense of how much a pain in the ass he can be. Honestly, I don’t know what to say about the situation anymore.” Mary was being pulled in two separate directions by her emotions. Her husband could be a pain in the ass and she knew it. After all, there was a reason that she was currently living with their daughter at her parent’s home. On the flip side of the coin, Matt really didn’t deserve a serious beating that he received on ReVolution 84. Mary had witnessed the attack first hand. She was enraged that someone could really be that violent for any reason. Sitting in the kitchen with Killean just made her more confused. What could she think? The silence in the room needed to be broken, but it wouldn’t be broken by Mary or Killean. The back door to the home opened, letting in a cool breeze and coming in with it is Mary’s sister Anne. Anne was carrying a bag of groceries and she couldn’t see Killean as she walked into the kitchen area. Mary quickly jumped to her feet, fearing the worst from Anne. “Hey Mary, I just picked up a couple of things and…” She dropped the paper bag of groceries on the floor as she locked straight into the ocean blue eyes of the Supreme Machine. She stood for a second, not saying anything. No one knew what was going through her mind at that moment. Truthfully, she didn’t even know what to think. Here was Mary, her dear sister, standing in the kitchen and sharing a cup of coffee with the man who brutally beat down the husband and father of the Ward family. It didn’t even cross her mind that Killean might be there to mend bridges. It didn’t cross her mind that perhaps, Mary was not going to bend so easily. But her anger kicked in and her heart took over, replacing her thoughts and what could have perhaps been the proper move. “What the hell are you doing here you bastard?” Blunt and to the point, Killean noticed the rage in Anne’s eyes and he raised his hands in innocence. But the gesture meant just about as much to Anne as a big juicy steak meant to a vegetarian. “Anne please, this is not what it looks like. Killean came here to have a civil discussion.” Mary jumped into the situation but was quickly bounced right back out by her sister. “Don’t fall for that crap. You can’t possibly believe that he is here to make amends. Look what he did to Matthew!” “Hey whoa! Enough. I am here to make amends. None of this should have concerned Mary.” Killean said but just like Mary, he was shut down just as fast by Anne. “Yeah sure… you should have thought about that before you beat the hell outta her husband!” With that she moved forward and planted the palm of her hand across the cheek of Killean, causing it to go read and causing Killean to recoil backward. Unfortunately the chair went with him and he tumbled to the floor, landing on his side in front of the stove. He is rubbing his cheek, looking on as Mary tried to make Anne back off. But she wasn’t having much luck. “Don’t you ever show your face around here again you stupid son of a…” Mary cut of her sister, trying to stop her from saying the final word of the sentence. “ENOUGH ANNE!” Mary shouted, trying to regain some sort of order in her parent’s kitchen. Killean quickly jumped to his feet, startling Mary and causing Anne to hold fists up, thinking Killean was going to charge. But Killean wiped his hands in symbolism. “You know what? Screw this; I never should have come here.” Killean made his way for the door, putting his sunglasses on. Mary however had a small look of disappointment on her face. Part of her was saying good, let him go. She didn’t want him there anyways. But another part of her wanted this to work. Killean never did anything wrong the whole visit. He was being civil. “Killean please, don’t listen to her.” Mary pleaded but it fell on deaf ears. Suddenly, from another room, Georgie began to cry. Her cries began to echo through the hallway and were clear as day in the kitchen area. Mary was beginning to break down. There was no doubt that Georgie woke up because of the commotion caused by Anne coming after Killean. The Supreme Machine scrunched up his face in disgust, knowing what it’s like to be a parent and a single one at that. “Mary, go care for your precious baby. As for you Anne…” Killean opened the door as he whispered the words, clear enough for Anne to hear but certainly too quiet for Georgie to hear in the other room. “Kiss my ass bitch!” And with that, the door slammed shut. Mary returned to the room with Georgie pouting into her shoulder. She gave Anne an ugly look, as Anne stood and caught her breath from the extremely stressful situation. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Saturday, April 15, 2006 1:30 PM Charity came into the bedroom as Desiree was just waking up from her nap. Charity had been staying at Killean’s loft every now and again to help care for Desiree. Killean had noticed that Charity had taken a liking to Desiree and the opposite was also correct. It seemed that Desiree certainly came to love the time she had with Charity. It was a rare occurrence for Dizzy to cry in the company of Charity. Charity brought Dizzy out to the dining room table and sat her in her high chair. The child was bright eyed and happy because it was time for lunch. She came to know this time well as it was a daily thing at 1:30. No matter what time she went down for her nap, at this time she was always up, looking for her lunch. Charity walked to the fridge and was beginning to get the lunch for the little girl. But the smile disappeared from the face of Charity as a shadow was cast over her. The faint clapping of shoes was heard on the floor but it was not enough to get the attention of Charity from getting the baby’s lunch. Hands wrapped around the child and lifted her from her baby seat. She began to whimper. “Don’t worry sweetie, lunch is coming.” Charity thought that Desiree was just upset about lunch, but that was not the case. Dizzy began to cry louder now and it was finally enough to pull Charity away from the task at hand. She turned to see a blonde woman with rather tattered hair walking away from the dining room and making her way for the door with the girl in her arms. Charity went running after her, screaming Desiree’s name. But both of them came to a stop well before the door to the loft. The Supreme Machine himself walked through the door, gym bag in hand. The look on his face was inexplicable. Really, what could be said for him in this situation? He recognized the woman immediately and his rage built. “How DARE you show your face in MY HOME and lay your grubby little hands on MY DAUGHTER!” Killean was ready to blow his top. Stacey however, really was not listening… she wasn’t understanding much. She appeared to be intoxicated. “Stacey? This bitch is Stacey?!” Charity practically growled as she now came face to face with the woman with the black heart, the woman who left a baby unprotected on Killean’s doorstep only a few short months ago. Stacey didn’t say a word. Who even knew if she could form a sentence at this point in time? “Yeah, this bitch is Stacey.” Killean confirmed. “And she better quickly hand over my daughter before she wishes she was never born.” Killean approached Stacey carefully, not knowing what Stacey might do in her drunken state. She was after all, holding Killean’s pride and joy. But Stacey was not exactly ready to hand over the child. “Ts-sheeeee is mah baby toooo….” She slurred, trying to move away from Killean, her head hanging down as she stared at the floor. “What did you say?!” Killean yelled as Desiree continued to be upset. Charity looked on, trying to figure out a way she could help the situation. “No, I don’t want to hear it again. You have no child! You gave up everything when you left her on my door step. There is no way you will be allowed around Dizzy again. You are not fit to be a parent you dirty alcoholic bitch!” Killean growled, knowing that Dizzy was not looking in her direction. In fact the child was reaching out for Charity with tears in her eyes. The sight of the child like that broke Charity’s heart. But it also signaled her opening. Charity quickly ran forward and took hold of Desiree, pulling her from Stacey’s arms, leaving the blonde estranged mother a little confused with what just happened. Quickly, and out of pure anger, Charity used her free hand to pull at the hair of Stacey and then raised her foot to her hip and proceeded to push Stacey to the floor, tearing out quite a few strands of hair in the process. Stacey rose to her knees, where Charity then kicked her, making her fall to the floor again. Killean on the other hand, after checking to make sure his daughter was alright, went to open the door. He then grabbed hold of the back of the shirt of Stacey, dragging her drunken body across the floor. “I swear Stacey, if you ever dare show your face around MY daughter again… I will not hesitate to hurt you! You are bringing fear to MY daughter!” Killean said as he quickly shuffled her onto her face outside of the loft. When he was going to close the door, he stopped for a second. He dropped to a knee and held Stacey up by her chin. She was looking into his blood shot eyes. She recoiled like she just saw the depths of hell in the retina of The Supreme Machine. He wasn’t done. “If you ever do anything like this again… if you ever even look at Dizzy again… so help me I will make you disappear off the face of this planet. Don’t doubt me, I have people who can make it happen and no one will ever know…” He whispered this whole speech to her, but it seemed that through her intoxicated state, she understood what he was saying. Killean let go of her chin as her face fell right onto the floor. Killean stood up and spit down on Stacey, closing the door hard. He walked over to quickly check on Charity and Desiree. Desiree had stopped crying and Killean hugged both his daughter and Charity tightly, swearing that something like that would never happen again. “I should’ve kicked her ass.” Charity said as she handed Desiree over to Killean. He smiled, yet he was serious. “She had Dizzy; there was nothing you could do without risking her.” Charity nodded and went back to getting the lunch for the child. Killean held Desiree close. Charity knew too, perhaps more than most people that Killean would ensure that it would not happen again. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Saturday, April 15, 2006 9:05 PM Fresh in Killean’s mind was the earlier visit from Stacey, the pathetic mother of his beloved Desiree. She attempted to take Dizzy away from Killean but was unsuccessful. The same thought kept running through his mind, he had to have someone watch the building tonight incase that psychopath came back. He certainly had the resources to do that. As he walked down the street, hands in his pockets, his mind came to rest of ReVolution this coming week. Killean would find himself in the ring with an original PRIME star. He was one of the top wrestlers in PRIME when Killean came into the fold two years ago. Adam had made a name for himself. But quite a bit of the time, it was at the expense of other up and comers. Toshiaki Motoki ran the Asian Invasion and made Adam do as he pleased. That all changed, but all too late in Killean’s mind. Killean was set for the match this week. After pulling out a very tough victory against Titan St. James, he was on top of his game. Either way anyone looked at it, Killean took down the powerhouse and pinned him. The victory was his. It didn’t matter that Charity or Tyler Nelson had a hand in it, nor did it matter that Titan chased him and Charity from the building in the most shocking fashions. All that mattered was another ‘W’ in the win column and another bit of momentum swinging his way leading to his ultimate goal, the Universal Title. But Adam was standing in his path. He knew he had to work hard to attempt to get passed the PRIME alumnus. He was ready though, extra days spent in the gym, extra motivation from Nelson and extra energy from the fans, regardless if they booed him. They didn’t realize that it fueled Killean to succeed more. But what they didn’t know wouldn’t hurt them. Adam would become another notch in the belt of “The PRIME Choice”. He was sure of it. He wasn’t called that for nothing. Nelson had believed that Killean would carry PRIME above the glass ceiling of Sports Entertainment. Why wouldn’t that give Killean all the confidence in the world? Yes, he would defeat another PRIME alumnus and would further cement his status as the top man in the business. He would further solidify his Hall of Fame standing and most of all… he would move further on his path to a second Universal Title. The sun was just setting on the west horizon of Winnipeg as Killean stood in front of the back door to one of the local clubs in downtown Winnipeg. It had turned out to be one of the most popular clubs in the city, but that wasn’t why Killean was there. He held his breath as he opened the door to his second life and stepped inside, the life that no one in the wrestling business knew existed for The Supreme Machine.
