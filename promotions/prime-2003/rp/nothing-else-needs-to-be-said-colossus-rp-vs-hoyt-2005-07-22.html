Nothing Else Needs to be Said (Colossus RP vs. Hoyt)

22 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 07/22/05 All of the insults, all of the personal shots and all of the games have had quite an effect on me. Hoyt Williams has said before in front of thousands and possibly millions of people that he respects me. How could he have meant that given all of the shots he has taken at me? He is trying to get me off my game. But this time, unlike in the past, it won’t work. Hoyt Williams can play all the cards in his hand, he can even try to play the hidden spade he has been withholding, but I have the trump card. He has beaten me and I have beaten him. My first loss in PRIME was at the hands of Hoyt Williams. I made the mistake of taking him lightly, of not respecting his abilities. I won’t make that mistake any longer. Hoyt is a dangerous and powerful man. But I can handle him, I always have. Training will not get me ready for Colossus, nor will studying tapes or throwing personal shots of my own do it. I have to go into the ring with a sound mind and focus on one thing. No matter how bloody and violent that match may be, I have to respect the ability of Hoyt. Only after realizing his pure power and talent will I truly be able to beat him on July 24th. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Thursday, July 21, 2005 in Los Angeles, California Killean Sirrajin was sitting quietly in the chair beside his bed ridden friend in the hospital. The memories ran through his head like he was flipping through a picture book. He remembered the sights, the sounds and the emotions. When Tchu and Sephiroth used to be at war in the Underground, Killean was brought in to even the score in place of an injured Sephiroth. Sephiroth was managed at the time by Jin Daikusa who was in turn the trainer and mentor of Killean Sirrajin. When Killean first struck, he made the impact at Tchu’s title ceremony. Technically, the champion never even got a punch in at Killean. Every time Sirrajin attacked, Tchu wound up motionless on the canvas feeling the pain of a vicious spear or even Supreme Justice. The stage was set for the two titans to meet at the next pay per view that tU was going to offer, but the fed closed down before that event could take place. Killean has Tchu backed into a corner, but he was never able to complete the job. When tU closed, Killean kept close contact with Sephiroth and stayed in San Diego. That’s when he met Kira. Killean was disturbed from his trance like state when Tchu and Mary walked into the private room that Sephiroth had. Killean nodded to Tchu who happened to sit across from him on the other side of the bed. “Almost useless to ask isn’t it?” Tchu said, sounding very down about his long time friend still being in this vegetative state. “Pretty much, the doctor dropped by a few minutes ago but there is no word about when or even if he is coming out of this.” Killean explained, not like he was telling Tchu anything new anyways. Mary didn’t really say anything; she was still quite upset about what was happening. Even though they had their ups and downs in the past, she still looked at Sephiroth as a friend and a brother to her and Tchu. She didn’t really know what to make of all this, but she knew that she was hoping for the best, just like everyone was. “How’s PRIME?” Tchu was trying to create some discussion to make light of everything. Killean took the bait and was more than happy to clear his mind. “Things are good. I have three days till Colossus and Hoyt. This time I know I have got him right where I want him. Even in light of the recent shots and insults, I know I can beat him.” Tchu was looking at Killean, flashing a smile as he spoke. “You know, you have come a long way since you took on Hoyt back when you were Intense Champion. If he would have said one thing about Kira or you, you would have flown off the handle and lost your cool. Now…” Tchu tried to finish but he was cut off when Killean began t flash a paper at him. “You mean do what I did at ReVolution this past week? I flew off the handle and tried to even the score with Hoyt for all the personal shots at me. Instead, he had an ace up his sleeve and played it, almost blinding me. Even now I am having some sight problems and these lights are bothering me. Luckily I got this clearance from my optometrist for the match this Sunday. I am really not in the best shape to be in the ring but I know I have to be there. I still made the same mistakes, this time it almost cost me my eye sight.” Tchu nodded, but he was almost trying to reassure himself that he was only trying to help Killean. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Wednesday, July 13, 2005 Approximately 11:30 PM EST I was sure the screaming and yelling could have been heard for miles. The emergency technicians quickly wheeled me through the emergency department. I heard the beeping of many machines as I was sure I finally stopped in the trauma center of the hospital. The good thing about hospitals in major cities like New York, they have the right tools for the job. I heard Nick, the play-by-play guy talking to me, telling me that everything would be alright. He actually came for the ride to the hospital. Joey Troy had stayed at the arena, seeing no reason to crowd the situation. As we stopped, I had calmed myself as much as I could but I could not desist from yelling some more. I had never entirely lost my eye sight before. I knew my eyes were in pretty bad shape but I never thought that one day it would come to this. I was worrying all the way to the hospital, wondering if this was for good. I had to wonder if I would get my eye sight back at all, or to what capacity. Would I be able to set foot in a PRIME ring again or had Hoyt Williams finally done the impossible and ended my career? All these thoughts were running through my mind as I heard a couple doctors in the room. Finally, besides Nick I heard a familiar voice, even though the tone was full of anguish and disappointment. Amy had decided to follow and see what was happening. She was currently battling with a nurse to gain access to my room. I felt I would make it that much easier for her. “Let her into the fuckin’ room!” My voice was full of pain. I barely manage to squeeze out the last words of that short sentence before I fell back onto the bed and tried to cover my eyes. It seemed like my profanity had done some good as there was no longer any fighting and Amy was standing beside me, or so I hoped when I felt my hand grasped tightly. Amy was concerned; I didn’t even have to see it in her face. The way she held my hand was telltale enough, but I could also sense it. Somehow, Amy and I had formed a bond that neither of us was really aware of. I could sense what she felt and she could do the same. It was just one of those unexplainable things in a relationship. “Killean, are you alright? Is he going to be alright?” She was all over the place, talking to me and also to the doctors at the same time. I squeezed her hand, trying to give a non verbal method of communication a try. When it didn’t work, I had to look past the pain and tell her. “Amy, it hurts like a bitch. But I am sure I’ll be ok. It’s only like frostbite from frostybox herself.” I had to joke with her and I heard her laugh a little as she closed her hand around mine tightly. I only hoped that I was telling her the truth. I had no clue as to what the extent of the damage was but I knew one thing. I couldn’t fuckin’ see. I heard the doctors talking about my optometrist and then I felt some unfamiliar hands lift my head. It then seemed like someone turned off the lights in the room because even though I had my eyes closed, it felt like light was being taken away from me. The familiar feeling of goggles around my head was then present. “These are just like your other goggles. However they are twice as strong, meaning more protection and less sight overall.” The doctor explained the goggles but I was confused. Here I was going into Colossus and one of the biggest matches of my career, my eye sight damaged and I was now being given a pair of goggles that would make it even harder to see. I had a hard enough time adjusting to the first pair of goggles, never mind a pair with more protection. Although, I still did not know what they felt like because I had no eye sight at the moment. Amy held my hand tightly in hers and I kind of began to doze off as the room finally hit some type of silence. Everyone had left except for a silent doctor who was working on some papers and Amy. Boy was I glad to have her there with me. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tuesday, July 19, 2005 Approximately 12 Noon EST I had not been home in a couple weeks. So much for trying to take some time and get comfortable in my new home in Winnipeg. Actually, I hadn’t left this hospital in about 6 days. The bed was rather uncomfortable and the food was less than desirable. I had actually managed to talk the nurse into sneaking a burger and fries in a couple of times from the take out joint across the road. I hadn’t seen Amy in a few days. I knew she was off getting ready for Colossus. As soon as I regained enough eye sight to move around on my own I had decided to put myself to the test and dial a phone. Funny how a normal, easy task for many is very difficult when you don’t have adequate eye sight. We talked for a few minutes, about how I was feeling, about how she was doing. Funny though that we didn’t talk at all about Colossus. I knew she was facing Ellis Easton but I knew little else. I had originally planned to train with her in Winnipeg and get comfortable in the new home but I guess Hoyt put those plans to rest. The rest of the afternoon I sat in my bed, waiting for clearance to leave the hospital and return to a relatively normal life. I had tested the new goggles that were prescribed to me and they weren’t kidding when they said the sight level was diminished. These things would take some getting used to and unfortunately I didn’t even have a tune up match before Colossus. Hoyt sure knew what he was doing, taking my eye sight away just before Colossus. I could swear that boy worked for Lucifer himself and not God like he claimed. Either way, full eye sight or not, I had to be ready for Hoyt and I could not get pinned by him again like I did at ReVolution 70. My nurse finally walked in the room. She was carrying a small piece of paper with her and she came to stand right at my bedside. “You have no idea how hard it was for me to get this. The doctor wouldn’t clear you so I made a call to your own doctor. After they talked for a few minutes, your doctor convinced ours here to release you and grant your clearance for Sunday night to wrestle. You owe me for this one.” She winked at me, giving me a knowing smile. Now had I been a single guy, I would have taken her small suggestions seriously. But Amy was important to me. As far as I was concerned, this was just another hint of attraction coming from some random woman. “Now now, you know my situation. Surely you listened when I was talking to my girlfriend earlier.” I smirked as I told her justly. “I am only human Killean.” She replied handing me the paper and still smiling genuinely. It was the release from my optometrist. Now I had medical clearance to wrestle at Colossus, but would my body give me clearance? Would my eye sight betray me after returning to almost the same capacity as it was at before? Only the next few days would tell the tale. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Saturday, July 23, 2005 Approximately 1 PM EST The Pontiac Silverdome had been eventless for a few weeks now. Cars adorned its parking lot for the first time in those few weeks. People were getting ready for the grandest stage of them all, Colossus. PRIME’s yearly event was coming ‘round the mountain again and this time, all hell was breaking loose. Last year Killean Sirrajin walked out of Colossus the Intense Champion after defeating Kincade, Poet and Shadow in the Syndicate Structure match. This year was different. He was walking in the champion and he had everything to lose. But this time around the result would be different. The champion would retain his belt after the night was finished. Killean Sirrajin parked in the VIP wrestler parking specially set for the event. Few cars were there meaning few wrestlers were in the building, just the way he liked it. It was the day before the event and Killean half expected there to be an abundance of talent to be there, getting geared up for Sunday night. Some of the wrestlers in PRIME had never bared witness to an event of this caliber, never mind taken part in it. This was a once in a lifetime opportunity for many wrestlers. Killean was just thankful that he would be around for a second Colossus, to watch PRIME once again set the standard for sports entertainment. He opened the big double doors to the talent entrance and began to quietly walk the halls. He past some random road crew that were talking and they acknowledged the champions presence with line such as… “Hey champ; glad to see you’re okay!” And… “Killean, you got Hoyt, you got him man!” Even these road workers made Killean smile. It was great to walk into a building and be recognized as soon as you showed your face. These guys had the utmost respect for the talent in PRIME. Wrestlers put their careers and lives on the line every time they set foot in the ring and they respected their determination and guts for doing so. Sirrajin walked by a few doors and came to the office of Hin See. A couple light taps on the door confirmed that he was not in the building. Since he did not return any of the phone calls that PRIME officials gave him, he decided to deliver the note in person. Seeing this as ‘in person’ as it was going to get, he slipped the note clearing him for active competition in the mail slot. He was then met by a member of the camera crew. “Hey, Killean is here! I’m sure he’s got something to say.” Just as the cameraman stopped talking, Lisa Tyler emerged from around the corner, casting a knowing glace to Killean. Nodding ever so slightly, he followed her into one of the production rooms. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Given only a few minutes, the crew had everything set up for my one on one interview with Lisa Tyler. When the lights came on and the cameramen gave the signal for action, the moment had come. My last official words before the big showdown. “I am here with PRIME’s Universal Champion, Killean Sirrajin. Killean, many of us thought that you would not be in attendance this Sunday after what happened with Hoyt and the Imperium at ReVolution 70.” Her words were devoid of any sarcasm, they only contained emotion. It felt imperative to only return the same tone of voice to her. “Lisa, the only thing to keep me from Colossus would have been death, something that Hoyt would have never accomplished no matter what. You should know that by now.” She nodded, knowing what I was saying. “I am just happy to be here in Detroit for the biggest event of all time. This is Colossus, the make it or break it point of many a wrestlers career. The younger talent on the roster has the chance to make a permanent name for themselves in the record books. My name is already etched into those scripts as I have been there before. But this is the most personal battle I have ever encountered. Come Sunday it will all come to ahead and I am just happy to say that I will be at Colossus tomorrow night in the sold out Silverdome.” “Well we are all glad to hear that Killean. The bets are going on all around the country, asking who will win the big main event at Colossus II. The words are flying and the money is mounting. Would you care to put some betters beliefs to rest?” Odd question but one that I have heard before. I knew gambling would be a part of Colossus but I never knew it caught the attention of PRIME itself. “Odds makers can set the main event up as they like. One thing that everyone can bet on is that it will be a hell of a fight and both Hoyt and I will probably have difficulty walking in the morning. We will probably have no energy and we probably won’t be the same when the night comes to a close. Believe it or not, Hoyt is a dangerous man who can beat anyone in this business on any day. I have to be at the top of my game to one up him.” My reply was quick and serious, but Lisa was still looking for more. It was then that she hit the button I was hoping she would not find. But that’s reporters for you I guess. “After everything that Hoyt has said and done regarding your family, your friends and you, is this really going to be a wrestling match or is it going to turn into a fight for revenge? We all heard the words he has been saying for weeks now and I am sure they still bother you, are you ready to extract revenge?” I had to think about what I was going to say. I wanted to make it good, something everyone will remember when they listen to it and something they will remember when they are watching the main event Sunday. “Revenge is such a weak word to use in this situation Lisa. This has been something personal since day one. But after what Hoyt has said and done, he has surely made this match a battle for the ages. Everyone will remember Killean vs. Hoyt at Colossus II in the future. This is so far past personal that I can’t even think of a word to describe it. Hoyt has badmouthed my family, my friends and everything that means anything to me. He has seen it fit to blind me and almost destroy my career. How could I not be looking for some sort of retribution? Hoyt Williams claims to be the son of God. This is not about false prophecies anymore and this is not about beliefs in evil or good anymore. This is a battle between two mortal people that will stretch the limits of the word mortality. This is the be all end all between two of the best in the history of this sport. Hoyt will be going in there looking to pin me and win this title. Normally I would be going in there to beat him myself, but this time will be different. This time I will be going in there to end this whole feud. I’ll be going in there to end Hoyt Williams. Being the son of God is one thing, but even that will not be a saving grace for Hoyt Williams as we both will be going to hell and back. Even the devil himself will not be able to kill me because that is the only way I will be losing come Colossus. Talking time is over, now is the time to fight like men at the big one and only one will walk out the winner and you're looking at him.” A long winded rebuttal closed the interview and closed the time for talking. As if anything else needed to be said anyways….
