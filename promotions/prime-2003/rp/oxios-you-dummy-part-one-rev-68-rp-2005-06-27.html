Oxios, You Dummy! (Part One: ReV 68 RP)

27 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/27/05 I wasn’t at my best last week. It happens. Get used to it because if you don’t have tons of pyro, a powerful recording of people chanting your last name over and over again, and the two most overhyped moves ever executed, you can’t be undefeated. What should have been our victory turned into a stinging defeat at the hands of Dave Gibson and his overfed pet. And just when rock bottom couldn’t be any worse, relics from the past come forward into MY match and try to make themselves known at my expense. I’m nobody’s stepping stone, yet Angelus and that accursed Covenant think they can simply walk all over me because they think I’m short? Fuck ‘em. The verisimilitude of it all is this: I was in a place I was left at in RPW as that…THING. Invincible Gabe. The gigantic heat sinks that run their mouths, throw a veil about their heritage and follow that blight of humanity that is Hoyt Williams spiked me into the mat and left me in a position where some Cthulhu-praising hacks could pick me off like I was a scrap to feed their bellies. My wrestling legacy isn’t that of Bob Holly. I am nobody’s eternal launch pad. They can’t get away with this. They just won’t. My blade will swing down and my wrath will be inescapable. Then to increase the pressure of the hell I have to endure. THEY re-enter my life. Pranks With Attitude. Well, boys, never thought I’d see your festering asses back. I had hoped all of the Nerezza actors died in a fiery car crash with the debris landing squarely on HIM. You know who you are, Gareth Green. Think I’ve forgotten about the piece of Los Diablos match I had to endure while working with you? Putting on different personas because you and pastyface put my head through a wall? Believe me when I say that I hope you had a good laugh, Gaz. YOU WON’T WHEN I GET MY HANDS ON YOUR USELESS GOLDUST-IMPERSONATING CARCASS. June 22rd, 2005Outside Seattle, WA, I-5 Highway. Well, another show had come and gone. Myself and Vangellus figured that as we team together and neither of us really had a good base of operations to call our own, we decided to spring for some monstrous manor in downtown Seattle. It was quite nice when Starbucks wasn’t attempting to ram coffee down your throat. I trained with Sonny Silver at one point at his school for a couple months. For such a bad reputation, the man had a true mind for the business we participate in. Nevertheless, Vangellus actually hadn’t SHOWN me the place until now, which was the whole purpose of this excursion. That, and we needed some hardcore planning. Usually, you aren’t in the ring with fourteen other people unless the words cluster and “the f-bomb” were involved, but we still needed to strategize. We were driving in one of the many cars that this simpleton somehow managed to acquire; a nice Cornische, to be exact. Vangellus was driving and I was doing my best to keep my head awake during the drive down from his home in Canada. It was long, but hell. We were almost there. At least he was smart enough – despite what I normally say about him – to spring for Sirius Satellite Radio. I need my Howard Stern fix come next year. He’s perverted, but man, is he funny. Cruising southbound on I-5 and just passing the 169 exit with just a few more till Seattle’s vast city limits, I scratched my head. “So, Van, why is it that you haven’t chosen to show me the actual condo yet? Let me guess…you actually listened to my joke about renting out the First Starbucks. I sorely wish to yell at those bastards for what they started. Coffee. BLAH.” 'Not the FIRST Starbucks. No, I bought BOTH of them' “Both?” 'Yeah, you know, where Lewis Black said that there was the end of the universe and there was one Starbucks right across the street from the other. I bought them both, merged them into one, and now the abomination is slowly devouring Texas as we speak. So yeah, it's a flat up North.' I said nothing after that. What was I supposed to say; I was going to strangle him? Why I’m this guy’s friend, I’ll never know…well, past that…Vangellus-y exterior the world sees, he’s quite a good person overall. But if he asks, it’s because he’s richer than I and it’s fun to use his stuff. Shit. Was dozing off again. Even the sounds of The Darkness’ “I Believe In A Thing Called Love” can’t keep me from heading to the land of nod. Speaking of, before I end this witty tangent, somebody call those idiots and tell them Hair Bands are only found on, of all places, Sirius’ Hair Nation Station. Morons. And then sleep found me. Seven other teams. Seven. Not one. Not two. Not even five. SEVEN. And all of them struggle for the same thing…except those Los Diablos idiots. All they want is for somebody to finally tell them that if your complexion is nearly white, you are NOT a Mexican. Especially when just being 6'3' for a Mexican is like being Shaq's height here. I don’t care if there’s the possibility of being overwhelmed. I have to win this match. We dropped the ball against two of my own teammates last week. If I want the crack as Los Diablos then failure cannot be an option. Yet…part of me still wants to enter this match simply for the purpose of getting at Gaz Green for the torment he put me through last year. Another part wants me to go after Angelus for spiking me into the canvas last week as if I were an infinitesimal bug. I also cannot forget that I want nothing more than to be rid of The Covenant. Horace, you made the biggest mistake of your life since swearing fealty to products of Lovecraft’s imagination when you struck me down as well. Nevertheless, my team will overcome. Angelus and Baltasar, tonight, you two pay. Gaz Green, if you get in my way, you will be struck down and any worthless figment of Nerezza’s imagination won’t save you. Covenant, I’m going to single-handedly break you both in two and send you back to your master, Violence Jack, in a pine box. Tonight, we rise and bring down all those that stand before me. Whether you be relic of the past, hot commodity or simply the worst thought out idea of all time, know this. We are Teh Pwn… God, that name just killed where I was going to go with this.
