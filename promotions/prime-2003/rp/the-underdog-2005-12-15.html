The underdog

15 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 12/15/05 The underdog… Is best explained by the American College Dictionary as:1. One that is expected to lose a contest or struggle, as in sports or politics.2. One that is at a disadvantage. A term I have become very familiar with during my wrestling tenure here at PRIME. I mean I don’t think there has been one match where I have gone into that my opponent hasn’t been bigger than me, more developed then me, hell they are all considered to be better then me. But the truth of the matter is, I have held my own. I have been an active wrestler in PRIME, I have been a contributing factor the success of PRIME for a over a year now. But the truth is I’m sick of being considered the underdog. Others might be in the same position as me, always looked at as the man who has the “uphill” battle but yet they enjoy it. Don’t get me wrong sometimes I do enjoy being considered the underdog. Just think about it for a minute, when the bell rings, they expect Peter Vetra to have a horrible match no way he can compete with some of these guys. We have the likes of Emilio Rage, Dani Furher, Tchu, Killean Sirrajin, Angelo Deville, amongst many others. All of which I have been considered the under dog, or would be considered the under dog. But the fact of the matter is when the bell rang a second time against people like Emilio Rage, Brandon Youngblood, just to name a few my name was the one announced over the loud speaker, not theirs. I went into each of those matches as the underdog but left the victor, something no one expected, thus making the victory that much better and sweeter. This week is no different; I mean I placed on the Great American Nightmare card not the part that is going to be shown to the world via PPV but the part that only those in attendance can see. Should that bother me? Should I be ashamed that I’m not on the actual card? I probably should be, but the truth is I’m not. I’m actually proud to be on the card at all, in recent weeks I have done shit, I have failed miserably in my last couple of matches, I have yet to find my groove and this year, the scary part is the year is almost over and I have not found my groove, I guess there is always next year. But why should I wait till next year? Maybe Great American Nightmare I will start my turn for the upside, I mean I might take home a key win and give me some momentum going into 2006 which will be my second full year with PRIME. But after taking a once over the card, I see that the momentum I’m looking to gain is going to be something I will need to fight hard for. Chris Collins, the man I lost to less then a month ago in this same situation, only it was on ReV and it was titled. A GTT5 Warm Up match and we all saw how good I did in the warm up match and in GTT5. I got myself a first round exit. Once again I am the underdog… Maybe this week is the week I show Mr. Collins and the rest of the PRIME world that I truly am not an underdog, but each week they consider me one, it just fuels my fire. Chris Collins will be the first in the new reign of Peter Vetra. Chris, I’m sorry but it has to be someone, and you got the shit draw.
