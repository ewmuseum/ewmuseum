Date: August 27 and 28 August 27, 2008 10:57 p.m. Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Instead of a big dark blur I see a big light blur. It was like he’d just come up for air after swimming too long under water. The senses were there. They were all just… fuzzy. His hearing was distorted. Muffled. As if listening to the television from another room. Fragments of a puzzle he could piece together, but nothing that clearly made a lot of sense. His vision was blurred. A film reel out of focus. It felt like a tiny little goblin was pounding the base of his skull with a sledgehammer. The metaphor is not so far removed from reality. Synapses begin to fire at an exponential rate. It starts to come back. The picture is beginning to focus. The sound becomes clearer. Tony Gamble. That goram briefcase. "Mother fucker." The hand on his shoulder squeezes tightly. He knows it’s Lindsay Troy. He’d know those hands anywhere. He can feel her concern. Her apprehension. His senses sharpen instinctively. A reaction to the sudden foreboding she exudes. Something’s not right here. He tries to stand. Equilibrium is fucked. Matt Mills spends a night in prison with Chainz fucked. He falls back flat on his ass. Hand to his head as the world starts spinning. Stupid fucking hobbits. "You’re sure there are no tricks?" The voice is terribly familiar. But a bad case of vertigo isn’t helping him to clear his thoughts. His palm presses harder against his head. Somehow that’s supposed to help. The grip on his shoulder tightens. She’s tense. Something’s up. Something’s… "Miss Pierce, if I could…" Brooks. The realization hits him like a thunderous blow from Mjolnir. The tension in the air. The apprehension. A strong concern he knew hadn’t been for him. Angelica Brooks. The Dead Man’s Hand. Desade. His hands wrapped tightly around the ring ropes. Up on a knee. "Well hi there." That one he doesn’t know. The voice. The woman. Dead Man’s Hand. Another one of Desade’s lackeys. His grip tightens. Lindsay Troy lets go of hers. Up on both feet. Leaning hard against those ropes. But he’s up. He’s vertical. Focus, Rayne. Get your ass up. Get it in gear. Now. "Remember. Thank Lindsay Troy and that dickwad Rayne for this." No. No. No no no no no. Not her. No. Please. He opens his eyes. Forces them to focus. Forces his sense to start working again. He didn’t have time to wait. There. The PRIME*View. Quite possibly the biggest man he’d ever seen. Or maybe it just looked that way because he had tiny little Angelica Brooks pinched between his legs. Relative proportions. No. Adrenaline threw him from the ring. Distortion threatened to bring him down. He as much fell through the ropes as he dove through them. But his feet found some purchase. He stumbled through the first couple steps. Damn near ate it. But he couldn’t. The adrenaline pumped harder. His senses cleared. Sharpened. Focused. Through the curtain. He’d seen the car. His car. He knew where it was. Knew he’d never make it in time. No. Run. You can make it. You have to make it. Go. Go. Every muscle in his body burned. He’d just been through a grueling match with Tony Gamble. Been knocked unconscious. Possible concussion. Now he was sprinting through the halls of the Mellon Arena. PRIME staff cluttered the halls. Make-up ladies. Technicians. Catering crew. They were all milling about. The beginning stages of breakdown after the show. Clean it up. Pack it away. Move on to the next city. He ran faster than he could ever remember running. He threw one of the sound technicians to the side. Heard the crash of plastic containers as the unfortunate employee tumbled into a stack of make-up supplies. The more he ran, the more people got in his way. He couldn’t win. Couldn’t get there. Not fast enough. Not fast enough. Move. Move. "MOVE!!" One of the catering crew took a forearm just under the jaw. He heard the cries of the witnesses as they clustered about the injured worker. He didn’t have time to care. He didn’t have time. Out of time. I’m out of time. C’mon, you stupid fuck! MOVE! "For Christs’s sake people, get out of  the fucking way!" This was the first he’d realized Lindsay Troy was following him. The only second he’d thought of anything else than getting to that parking lot. Getting to Brooks. The door was right there. Shoulder down. Barrel through. An explosion of searing pain shot through his shoulder. Down his arm. No time. Just around the corner. She’ll be there. She’ll be fine. Almost there. Almost there. There she was. Angelica Brooks. One of his closest friends. One of the only people to embrace him when he’d first come to PRIME. For a time, all he’d had were Brooks and Pierce Lavelle. Tony Gamble had done his part in making sure Lavelle left PRIME for good. Now Brooks was laid up on the hood of his own Mustang. Chest heaving in slow, shallow breaths. Everything crumbled. Everything fell. Nothing mattered. He wanted to give up. He wanted to fall to his knees and surrender. Not her. Of all the people. "We need some help out here! Get a goddamn ambulance out here now!" From the top of his eye he saw the Hummer speeding away. No. No. No. You do not get away. You do not get away. He was sprinting again. Faster than before. He had to get them. Had to make them pay. For her sake. For his sake. For the guilt. The knot in the pit of his stomach. A dark, churning storm that screamed at him like a banshee. It’s all your fault. Tyler Rayne is many things. He is not, however, as fast as a car. There’s no way he’ll catch them. No way he can be fast enough. He scoops a rock from the ground. More like a chunk of concrete someone had knocked loose. Maybe even the Hummer as it sped away. Thing was the size of his fist. He hurled it with all the strength he had left in him. His aim was true. The rock hit solid against the back glass of the Hummer. Cracks spider-webbed from the point of impact. The Hummer turned a corner and was gone. It’s all your fault. "Don’t just fucking stand there, go get Dam!" He looks up. Watches the cameraman set his equipment down on the hood of another car and race for the arena doors. Stunned. Quiet. Utterly broken, Tyler Rayne walks solemnly toward his car. Toward the shattered figure of Angelica Brooks. He’d chase them to the end of the world if he could. If he knew where they went. If he knew who they were. If she wasn’t sprawled out across the hood of his car. Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas bursts through the door with an army of EMTs behind him. Tyler feels them descending upon him. A sudden swarm of activity and chatter. Someone grabs him by the shoulder. Pulls him away. His fingers loosen their grip. Slide along the smooth skin. Silky smooth fingers. That stupid lotion she was always rubbing on. He hadn’t even realized he’d been holding her hand. "Please, Rayne. Step back. Let us work." He does as requested. This is crucial. The next few minutes. The immediate diagnosis. The expediency with which it can be completed. The faster they can get her to a hospital. Lindsay Troy approaches him and he turns away. He can’t stand to look at her. Can’t stand to speak to her. It’s all your fault. "The fuck are you doing?" He turns away from Lindsay Troy because he can’t bear to meet her eyes. Turning his head brings his attention the new cameraman filming this tragedy. He’s not the same one as before. Some new vulture preying on the scene. Tyler begins stalking forward. The rage and guilt and… everything… bubbling beneath the skin. Molten lava in his veins. The new cameraman looks over. His eyes widen at the sight of the 5-Star Champion marching in his direction. The mask of pure and unadulterated kill splattered across Rayne’s face. The cameraman tries to move. Not fast enough. Rayne backhands the camera. It spirals out of hand. Spins to the ground. Smashes against the concrete. Shattered. Like Angelica Brooks. He slams the cameraman down on the hood of a car. Like Angelica Brooks. Tyler Rayne could tear this kid apart. Destroy him. End him. Like Angelica Brooks. "You think this is funny? Huh? DO YOU THINK THIS IS FUNNY?" "N-n-no. No. No. P-p-ple-please." "Worthless. Fucking. Vulture. VULTURE! How ‘bout I leave you here, huh? Break you over the hood of a car and film that. Huh? HOW ABOUT THAT? WOULD YOU LIKE THAT? Send the tape to your parents. To your friends. You wife and kids. Let everyone see you helpless. Victimized. You want that, you sick little fuck? DO YOU WANT THAT?" "Ease up, boss. He ain’t tha chump ya want." "The fuck off me." Tyler Rayne throws his arm back. Dam’s giant hand is tossed from his shoulder. Still… Rayne does as instructed. He backs away. Storms off in another direction. Dam takes a step to go after him, but the slightest touch of Lindsay Troy brings the Head of Security to pause. "I’ll handle him." There he is. The original cameraman. The one that had filmed the whole gods damned thing. Watched as it unfolded. Stood by and watched through the lens of a fucking camera. Skulking around in the background. Watching as it all went down. As the EMTs loaded Angelica up on the stretcher. Tyler marched right up to him and put him to the concrete with a punch that immediately broke the cameraman’s nose. "Why didn’t you do something?" Why didn’t you do something? The cameraman’s nose burst like a piñata. Blood splattered like a burst water balloon. Across Tyler’s chest. Sprinkling his knuckles. Tyler dropped a knee down across the cameraman’s chest. All his weight pressed down. Held him firm. The fist cocked back again. Another devastating blow. "Why didn’t you do something?" Why didn’t you do something? Tyler felt the bones crush beneath his knuckles. Heard the man crying for mercy. Felt the tug of Lindsay Troy as she tried to pull him off. "Why didn’t you do something?" Why didn’t you do something? "WHY DIDN’T YOU DO SOMETHING?" Why didn’t you do something? "Tyler! Stop it, you’re gonna kill him!" So. Lindsay Troy is, for all intents and purposes, a dominant woman in a man’s world. She is Princess Diana. She is Carol Danvers. Sometimes Bruce Wayne loses control. Sometimes Tony Stark loses his grip. She is there to pull him back from the brink. She is strong. Tested in the fires of war. She pulls Tyler Rayne off the cameraman. Physically drags him away. Throws him against the tire well of another car. "What the fuck do you think you’re doing?" He doesn’t look up to meet her eyes. He doesn’t answer her question. "Tyler-" "I DON’T KNOW!!" His fist crashes into the hood of yet another car. The bones crunch against the blue metal. His skin splits and peels from the hand. The fist pounds down again. His bones give way. Bolts of pain shoot like lightning up his arm. His fist pounds down again. Again. Again. Why didn’t you do something? "Why did it have to be her?" And he collapses. It’s all too much. Finally, the guilt washes over him. He falls against the hood of the car. She’s there. Right behind him. A hand on each shoulder. "Ty. They’re loading her up. We should probably-" He rises again. Sucks it up for the half dozenth time tonight. Pushes back the flood. The rage. The despair. He turns. There is an ambulance he doesn’t remember arriving. The EMTs are carefully loading her into the back. Once again he leaves Lindsay Troy behind. He can’t help it. His only concern is her now. His only thoughts on her. He stalks toward the ambulance. They’re getting ready to roll away. On to the hospital. They’re going to help her. She’s going to be fine. It’s all your fault. "I’m sorry, sir. There’s no room. You’ll have to-" Tyler snatches the EMT by his shirt and throws him to the side. Hard. Enough to send the med technician sprawling to the ground. He doesn’t care. Fuck them all. Tyler Rayne climbs into the back of the ambulance with Angelica Brooks and slams the door shut. ---- August 28, 2008 1:42 a.m. Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania One more reason for her to hate you now. Lindsay Troy grunts and tries to shift her body into a more comfortable position in a rather uncomfortable chair. A rather fruitless venture, given a stone slab offers more cushion than this. The ICU of Jennersville Regional Hospital is quiet this time of night, which does not aid in Troy's attempt to shake her mind free of thoughts and scoldings. They brought Brooks there in record time and for the longest time there was the agony of waiting. Too many times Troy's been in this position. Too many times she's been on the other side of it. When the doctors explained the diagnosis and tried to tell them to let her rest for the night, Tyler had none of it. Caused a scene. It was up to her to convince the staff otherwise. He's not leaving her bedside. And where are you? On the outside of it all. The hallway outside Brooks' room is a bleach-white, the sheer brightness of it could serve as a disinfectant. Lindsay's eyes remain trained on the floor; nowhere else to look but the lowest point possible. Outside where you belong. "Excuse me, miss?" "Yeah?" Pillows and blankets are lowered into her peripheral vision. The nurse's nails are pink and perky. "I brought these for you and your friend. I'd bring them to him myself but, well..." She trails off them. Lindsay notices the uncomfortable shift of her shoes. "He scares the shit out of you," Troy finishes for her. She didn't need to look up to sense the nurse's nod. "There's a lounge down the hall, if you wanted to rest there. I know these chairs aren't the most comfortable." "I'll be fine, thank you." Lindsay looks up, musters a smile for appearance's sake. "I'll bring these in to him." She stands, leaves a pillow and blanket on the seat of her chair for herself and quietly slips into the darkened room. Tyler Rayne sits on the edge of a cot, hands clasped beneath his chin. He doesn't move to acknowledge her entry. He just stares solemnly at the hospital bed in which Angelica Brooks sleeps. Without a word, Lindsay places the things next to him and steps back, folding her arms across her stomach. The silence is thick and suffocating. "You know Katt used to be married?" Troy's eyes travel away from Brooks and back to Rayne. "I don't know-." "You know. Katt. My bestest best friend. She was married once. Guy named Jakob." "Oh..." Silence again. "They'd been together and divorced before her and I even met. But Jakob... me and him never did get along too well. Tried to kill each other a few times. That's no exaggeration. Shot each other. Stabbed each other. Burned each other. Started riots. Wars. Everything we could think to do. A brutal, bloody, ridiculous fucking war. Jakob. Jakob... fucking... Mayhem." His fists clench, rage boiling to the surface again. Lindsay walks back to the cot and sits on the other side of Tyler. "Not exactly an original name, is it?" she starts. "Asshole grabs a thesaurus and looks up a catchy word for 'Chaos' and thinks to himself, 'Gee, this might work.'" Unlike the name, the tension-easer hardly works. The situation, the conversation, is uncomfortable and awkward. And your fault. "I'm sorry, Tyler." "What do you have to be sorry for? They didn't come after Brooks because she's friends with you." "They came here because of me, Tyler. Because of something I did months ago. She's in a medically induced coma because I told Desade, 'No', because I should have just dealt with this all myself. I shouldn't have even let you go with me to Cleveland. So many 'should's and I can take none of them back. That's more than enough to be sorry for." "Nice try, Uni, but you and I both know why they came after B. When Desade wanted you... she went after your family. Your niece. She invaded your home. Your life. Nestled right in and warmed up. It's not exactly a secret you and Angie don't get along. Anyone with half a brain could figure out why. That's why it was her. That's why it was my car he broke her over. She's here because of me. They wanted to come after you again... they wouldn't have used her. They needed someone close to me. I don't have family. I don't have a niece. Hell, I've barely got any friends to speak of. She's been there... since the day I walked in the door. Before you. Before Tsonda. Hell, even before Pierce. She was always there. And now... now she's here. Because of me." He stands. Moments pass in the darkness; in the silence. "I tried to leave this behind fifteen years ago. Lin... my fiancée... she died because of me. Yakuza. Come to the club. Figure they'll teach me a lesson. Show me who's boss. I can handle myself. I can... take care of myself. Shit. Isn't anything in the world I wouldn't give to have traded places with her. But I'm here. And she's not. Now I'm standing in a hospital room. Wondering if someone else I love will even be able to stand again. Wondering if she'll walk. People get close to me... this is what happens. All my life I've been watching people die and suffer because of something stupid I did. Do you think she'll hate me. When she wakes up? You think she'll blame me? Maybe... maybe she should. Maybe she should hate me. For what I've done. For what I am." "You can't shoulder this your-" "Forget it. I'm... I'm gonna go outside. Get some air. You think you could... just watch ov-" "Of course." He nods but doesn't quite move. A hand raises to his eye and rubs. Wiping away... She doesn't want to think about it. Feels ashamed to even be watching. As if she'd stumbled into some sort of private moment. The moment passes. He passes, quietly through the door and leaving Lindsay Troy alone in the hospital room with nothing more than darkness and the sounds of machinery to keep her company. ---- August 28, 2008 4:34 a.m. Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania A few hours of much needed sleep can do a lot to change a man’s demeanor. Tyler Rayne stirred from his exhausted slumber. Cold and disoriented, it took a moment for his eyes to adjust to the darkness. For his brain to process the rhythmic mechanical beeping. The quiet slosh of fluids. The compression of forced air. He pushed himself from the cot. Dull throbbing pain pulsed through his back. The cot wasn’t exactly built for comfort. Then again, neither was he. He crept silently across the hospital room. Not because he feared waking her. As a matter of fact, he would have been quite pleased to have woken her. At least then she would be awake. No. Simply out of habit. Out of an instinctive stealth programmed through years of sneaking around in darkness. He sidles up to her bedside. It’s all he can do to keep himself from breaking down. From losing it. The guilt. The anguish. It descends upon him in an avalanche. Buries him. Suffocates him. But somewhere deep within is a cold, seething rage. A quiet vengeance bubbling beneath the surface. Waiting to be freed. Waiting to rain blood and destruction. So fiery hot is his desire for revenge that it melts the avalanche. It absolves him of guilt. Of responsibility. But it does not warm him. It leaves him as cold and uncomfortable as the guilt. The burning vengeance is a cold, uncaring blaze that turns his skin to frost. His heart to ice. The steady beep of the heart monitor brings him back to the room. Back to Angelica. To the guilt. The avalanche. The circle will continue forever. Now caught in its grip… he will not escape. His torment. His curse. "Should’ve been me, B. Guessin’ you probably already thought that, but… yeah. I never thought that they’d… shit, kid. I never thought. Too many years. Too many gutless assholes. You know how many threats I’ve heard in my time? Far too many. So Desade rolls in and starts talkin’… I just hear empty promises. More bullshit from the mouths of would-be idols. I didn’t take her seriously. I didn’t… I’m sorry. I should have thought it out. I should have been more cautious. Five fucking seconds to think of someone but myself and I would have seen this coming. I didn’t. I’m… it’s such bullshit to even apologize. It’s worthless. You couldn’t know how sorry I am right now. But it doesn’t matter. Sorry doesn’t get you out of that hospital bed. Sorry doesn’t stop that bastard from doing this to you. Sorry doesn’t do you much of anything right now, B. And the fuck of it all? I want to apologize for that, too. I’m sorry sorry isn’t good enough. Fucked, right? You got caught in the storm that is Tyler Rayne. Collateral damage. It’s.. the only thing I’m good at. The only thing I know how to do. Pain. Destruction. Ruination. It’s the only thing I’ve ever known. The only thing that ever felt like home. My whole life… it’s one big fucking tornado. Sometimes I get hit. Sometimes my friends do. If I’m lucky, I’ll take a few enemies with me. But one way or another… someone always gets devastated. Lives are ruined. Changed. Altered forever. I can’t do anything for where you’re at now, B. Hoyt knows I wish I could. But I promise you… I fucking promise… I will bury this Mayhem mother fucker. I will put him in the ground for what he did to you, B. It’s all I know how to do. It’s all I can do. I can’t blame you if you wake up and you hate me. I can’t blame you if you never want to speak to me again. If you never want another thing to do with me. But I swear to fucking Christ… regardless of what happens when you wake up… and you will wake up… I swear to fucking Christ I will end Mayhem. It’s all I know, B. It’s all I have. It’s not enough. It wasn’t enough tonight. It won’t make up for what’s been done. For what I’ve done. This is my fault, B. I can’t take it back. God damn I wish I could. But I can’t. I can’t…" He lets her go. He walks away. Back to the cot; to a sleepless night. To thoughts of a man named Mayhem. Blood. Destruction. The circle he cannot escape. His gift. His torment. His curse.
