The second Date

14 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5/14/05 There I sat alone; my only friend was the glass of alcohol in my right hand. Jack and coke was my choice of poison, it goes down rough but it makes all the pain go away. I picked my groggy head up from looking at the bar and took a look at the clock. The clock just stuck eight; she was supposed to be here any minute. I took another sip of my cool refreshing drink; just as I place it back onto the bar I heard my name. “Peter.” It came from the voice of my beautiful blonde girlfriend. All I could do was turn around to face her and watch it as she just smiled. “Hello, Melissa.” “Hello, have you been waiting long?” “Not at all a couple of minutes. Just long enough to start my first drink, speaking of which. What would you like?” “I will take a Malibu and Rum.” A simple yet fruity drink much like I expected from the lady. The barkeep went and got her drink along with a second one of mine. I then escorted her over to a table that I had reserved. We sat slowly sipping our drinks not saying a word. Finally I had to break the silence. “Sorry… I am sorry.” A look of puzzlement overcame her face, “For what?” “Last week.” “What about last week?” “I lost that match, I looked like a fool on national television and I did not impress you at all.” “What are you talking about? Of course you impressed me I was amazed by the things you can do in the ring.” “So you didn’t mind that I lost” “You probably wouldn’t have if that Darilek guy wasn’t on your team.” “That is true, but that is for another time and place.” “So this week do you happen to have another ticket? And maybe the airfare also?” I sat there took another drink from my glass and thought for a second, she really must like me if she is still interested after seeing what happened last week. “I wasn’t really expecting you to want to go with me again. I am sure I can arrange it if you really want to come.” “Of course I want to come this week is a big week for you. I mean you get a chance to go after that Darilek guy that hurt you last week but you also get to go after one of your opponents. That right there sounds like retaliation.” She spoke so fluently, every word made perfect sense to me. “Well then I will arrange it, you are about to see me become the next Alias Champion, and you my dear will be front and center.” Now I had a right to be cocky. That title should have been mine when I faced Emilio Rage but no the Dark Age had to interfere. Now I am finally getting my chance at gold and this time I have someone on my side, the first time ever in my life I think. Has the Depression Fully gone away? I am not sure if me sitting here talking to myself is another mental problem I have but all the while it calms me down and allows me to think a little clearer. But that is the least of all of my worries right now. I think my depression has gone away and I am finally a happy man. But is it possible for a single person to turn it all around for me? Or am I just “caught up” in the initial phase of dating? Either way the depression has seem to gone away my luck is starting to turn around. Last week I was unlucky in my match but I think I can blame the fully on my so called partner Jackson Darilek. But the truth of the matter is this week is my time. This week I get to show him that I was the only reason that our team lasted as long as it did. We both know that if it was just him the match would have been over in a heart beat but no, I saved his sorry ass. But once again he is not the only man of concern this week. I need to worry about a man known as Tyler Lopez, another man I don’t see as a threat. Last week I showed him along with Nova what I am capable of doing and they both know that the pain I am going to bring is unlike what they are used to. The time has come, the reason for the win has also come. My time is now, I just feel bad for these two. My depression may or may not be healed, but this week I am going to get a final dosage of happiness as these two are about to get a dosage of reality. The gold will become mine, something I should have had a month or so ago. She has brought me to the promise land, now it is time for me to take it home.
