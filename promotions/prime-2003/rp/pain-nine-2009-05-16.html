Date: 5/16/09 I’m tired. I’m sore. I depressed, and if I wasn’t so God damn lazy I’d probably kill myself. Depression comes and goes and often haunts the humorous since we are the ones who paint over the pain. We’re funny because almost every one of us we grew up in pain, and it’s all we know. It’s an escape, it’s a way to change the story, spin a frown to a smile and everybody leaves you alone. If you’re ever backstage at a play or on the set of a movie the ego’s are amazingly disturbed. Actors think they are the best and they never let go of that demanding respect in the process. Backstage at a comedy club is much different. It’s loaded with addicts and ex-addicts trying to gain approval. Even the best comedians always come off stage bitch about how their last set was the worse yet and how unfunny they are. It’s like an orgy of clowns with knifes to their wrists and gin on their breaths. It’s as beautiful as life gets, but seldom get to see it. But I just don’t feel like smiling, nor do I feel very funny anymore. Like a dancer who’s lost a step, a singer whose voice is gone, or a bird with a broken wing –I’m just not funny anymore. There is more to life then your skill but when you lose it the big picture is hard to see and that is where I am. I just lay in bed even though the night before I slept a good 12 hours. Once the bed has me in its hold I can’t escape it. Any and all energy escapes me and I become paralyzed to reality escaping in my own warped dreams. None of which I can remember by the way –I hope they are filled with love, happiness, and sloppy sex but I doubt it. They must be decent for my body to just shut down to escape to them. My mother killed herself in 2000 by standing in front of the Midnight train on a cool September night. She had nowhere to go, she was alone in a fucked up bitter world that didn’t care, and it was the right move for her. Unfortunately suicide is selfish anyway you look at it. I’m selfish for wanting her to be here for me now even though I know her life would still be painful and scary to her. It was selfish of her to leave me alone in this world when for all those years all we ever had was each other. Poncho and Lefty be damned. To get this down takes a perfect storm and I assume mine actually started on mother’s day. I take that back the day before is when it started. My Grandmother called me out of the blue for the first time ever. I’m terrible with communicating with family and truth be told she’s the only family I have left. She told me her boyfriend of the last twenty years just died. She was now alone. A strong women who now has lived out lived two strong term marriages an example to all in an age of martial decadence. She’s out lived her only daughter a pain I can’t even imagine, her own parents, and countless friends. Every few years the God damn cancer creeps up on her, and they tell me she only has a year left. Yet every year she soldiers on and defies the odds and this cycle has continued for about twenty years. Dominick was a good man who cared deeply for her. Dominick was an old school Italian who grew up on the Rat Pack in a neighborhood where the Outfit (Chicago’s mafia) was prevalent --yet he always remained clean. His first wife died of cancer and he never stopped loving her. My grandmother was a widow for about ten years when they met. They hit it off and soon moved to the desserts of Las Vegas to live out their golden years most of which they spent in hospitals but happy. He always told me that he was taking care of my grandma and that always made me feel good and not so guilty about not being the greatest communicator. Now she has nothing. The day was also filled with friends inviting me to hang out on Sunday. It didn’t occur to me that they knew I was going to be alone on mother’s day and trying to help. It did hit me later that day otherwise I may have completely forgotten. At that point I think I knew my Mothers day would be rough. Sunday I finished a meeting and immediately started smoking dope. Dope lead to booze and booze lead to pills and pills lead to a loss of most motor skills. I rambled and laughed with my friends in the surroundings of a safe environment. Smoking loon wine brought the wino out of me and I was in a good place. Monday I woke up feeling good with no hang over and a renewed feeling of goodness. The pain was gone. I went home and spent the day relaxing gearing up for a game of Golf on Tuesday. I had just enough dope left to smoke through 18 holes, get a nice tan, and just relax in the woods. My game sucked and it tore my buddy up pretty good. Now I was physically hurting and all out of dope. I like running out of pot every now and then so that I can get things in order. If you spend to long in the cloud of smoke you get confused and lost. Once away from it you see the sun, the blue skies, the beauty of the clouds and you breathe in the fresh air. The perfect storm was brewing and I had no idea it was coming. Wednesday was for the most part a very good day. I ran out of money but I had everything covered until the next pay day. Life seemed to be smiling at me –kind of the way a dog smiles where your not sure if he’s threatening you or just happy. I got home after hitting up the bar after a show I was flat in. I fired up my less then a year old laptop and soon heard the hard drive die. It’s not a real pressure of the end of the world but the thing is I had spent the last four months writing my biography and now all those words were gone. My stories and my adventures for the first time documented wiped up by the last major investment I made. With no money to fix it my last solemn escape had betrayed me. My mind swirled and my pain increased. Now here I am writing on scraps of different computers that have failed me in the past. I’ve basically got a word processor and an internet connection although everything is slow. I’m left with my own terrible thoughts right now and I can’t escape them. I went to my mothers grave today for the first time in a few years. It took a while to find it but there it was. I was hoping for something out of the trip but all I found was a cold stone. Now here I am just a few hours from having to get into the ring and beat the hell out of a guy with a dog collar attached to both our necks. I’m not sure I can win. I don’t feel up to it and I’m sorry I have to cheat the fans as they will not have my all. I hope regardless it’s the spark I need to get out of this slump but only time will tell which sucks when all you have is time left. I’m sorry I couldn’t make you laugh today, it’s just I needed a cry. This one was for me –I’m sorry. I still got PRIME, I still have the fans, and I still have tomorrow until then I need to get Whiskey Bent and Hell Bound. =w=
