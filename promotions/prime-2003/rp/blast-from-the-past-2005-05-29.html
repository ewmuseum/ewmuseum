Blast From The Past

29 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 29-05-05 Instinct fuelled the spinebuster that sent Vampir to hell. I don’t recall making the executive decision to wait until the Soul Stalker ran at me and to counter him by throwing him down the lift shaft, that cycle of motion was instinctive. He ran, I turned…my body moved ahead of my brain. One moment I was nursing a welt from that damned camera shot, the next I was on all fours staring down at a screaming abyss. I barely caught sight of Vampir as he was shrouded in darkness, consumed by it and made to suffer its wrath. I hadn’t received word on the state of Vampir; PRIME required a little help in dispatching of some PTC scum. Never before had my duties as a mercenary been required in such capacity. I imagined Vampir’s demise much differently to how it had actually transpired. It was over a flash, no intimidating threat or gloating…just the end. It was different from the previous encounter, a commanding performance of treachery and murder played out before millions in the most extravagant of ways. This time Vampir Nosferatu was rid of at the beginning of a random show, with no more than a few spectators around and the world beyond the lens of a camera. No words, no taunting or torturing…just death. Such a prolific beast as Vampir deserved a magnificent death, one for the ages, surely? No. Something as pathetic as that deserved a peasant’s death. A slaying without the trimmings, and that’s just what he got. Of course there was the possibility he survived the fall and-…no impossible. He was buried at the bottom of a lift shaft in Madison Square Garden, perhaps that was his befitting send-off, to be buried in the world’s most famous arena. There was a lot of Dark Age burials happening lately…now with room to breathe I could concentrate on establishing myself in the company, make a proper name for myself other than “That guy that really hates Vampir”. Then again there was nothing wrong with being billed as the Killer of Immortals. With the Dark Age out of the way I could move on to the motley crew known as Imperium. I had run riot on them before, and my next match would only serve to see them suffer yet again. As far as I was concerned with Vampir however I had done away with him, if he came crawling back for revenge I’d do it again, and again, and again until Vampir realized there was no way to overcome me and stayed buried forever. Who could have ever imagined he would leave this earth a toothless, petty loser, someone who prided himself on being the Conqueror of Humanity. Suffice to say…humanity struck back and beat the Soul Stalker. ---------- He didn’t know how to handle the phone call. It had been months since he had last made contact with Minion and it was not under the best circumstances. Left for dead on the streets of Detroit was a pretty big factor into never coming into contact with his manager again, or so Hessian thought. Perhaps the little Imp had understood the error of his ways and wanted to make amends, after all he had become a sloppy manager in the later days of Hessian’s RPW tenure, leaving the Goliath to manage himself through the most high profile moment of his career to date, his Global Title reign. He had no reason to contact Hessian and vice versa, yet Von Kelsig found himself pondering the gesture where normally he would have deleted the message and moved on with life as normal. Having noted the number left on the message, Hessian picked up the receiver, hovering his hand over the keypad. Pointing a finger at the numbers, the Goliath stopped himself dialling, sighing to himself before placing the receiver back down and taking a seat at his desk. Burying his head in his hands, Hessian glanced down at a small stack of paperwork before him and a familiar name printed on the front of a CV mailed to him the previous day. Gazing ignorantly at a small CCTV monitor set up on the desk next to him Hessian looked up then quickly glanced back to see a man standing at the front door to the club, raising a hand to the buzzer. BUZZZZZZZZZ! “Hello?” Hessian asked, pressing down the telecom button on the phone. “Yeah, it’s Mr. Williams I’m here about the doorman job?” Came the husky reply. “One moment please…” Hessian said, quickly getting up and heading downstairs with the CV in hand. Jogging down the stairs from his top floor office he passed through the dance hall and bar, down the marble stairway to the first floor. Taking a moment to catch his breath, Hessian made his way through the lounge to the front door, unbolting it and opening it up to see the interviewee standing there before him. “Welcome to the Slaughterhouse, please come in. Take a seat anywhere you like.” Hessian welcomed the man warmly, inviting him in. Wandering past the tables to the one closest to the bar, Mr. Williams took a seat on one of the white leather sofas, letting out a surprised whoop as he slid. He propped himself up to a comfortable position as Hessian closed the door and joined him at the table, placing his CV on the table. “I received your curriculum vitae yesterday Mr. Williams and I must say I’m impressed. You certainly seem qualified for the position. I only have a couple of questions regarding-…wait excuse me, I never even introduced myself…” Hessian began, only to be stopped in mid-sentence by the interviewee. “Oh I know who you be…Hessian.” Mr. Williams interjected. “You’ve seen me on television I gather?” Hessian asked. “Certainly so, but I did indeed also work at the same promotion as you a few year back, only difference being I was there before you…” Mr. Williams said, a hint of pride flowing into his Louisianan drawl. “I did notice you listed one promotion for previous employment, hobbies and interests and qualities. How long were you in Primetime Championship Wrestling for?” Hessian asked, impressed that a wrestler had applied for the job yet unaware of just who he was. “Long enough to gain myself a reputation! Shit, people know who I am…I wouldn’t have figured a big name like you would know me though, even if I has won a better title than you done in PCW…heck…I don’t recall you ever winning a title in PCW…I was with the company for a few years, even after they closed and re-opened. I was loyal.” Mr. Williams’ took Hessian aback, snapping at his prospective employer and suddenly returning to a quiet polite tone. Thinking for a moment, the proverbial light bulb went off over his head as Hessian realized who was sitting in front of him. The last ever PCW Continental Champion… “Skitzo!” Hessian proclaimed, slapping his forehead and grinning as a smile grew on Skitzo’s face. “Sure, I remember you! You had a Spider’s Net match last year…that was some hardcore stuff.” “Well thank ya kindly. Coming from you that means…I’M BETTER THAN YOU SUCKER…a lot. So the job is your average bouncer gig?” Skitzo replied, his left eye twitching. Hessian opened his mouth to speak but stopped himself quickly. Staring at him from across the table, Skitzo maintained his innocent smile, the twitch in his eye fading. Hessian thought to himself for a moment, looking at the Mental One before him. Sure he had the look of a doorman, at his last weigh-in in PCW he stood 6’3” and weighed 241lbs. Just over a year had passed and now he was looking a trim 220lbs. His clear blue eyes sparkled with a madcap charm as ever they did, while his traditional goatee look had become a thick but neat beard. He had maintained a ponytail to control his greasy black hair, hanging just past his shoulder blades. He was the same old Skitzo but he looked as fresh-faced as the day he walked into the Primetime Wrestling Academy. Yet no matter how he looked on the outside, it was the inner workings that Hessian was concerned about. “Thing is William…” “Call me Bill!” Skitzo interrupted. “Bill…well thing is Bill I understand your schizophrenia helped you ascend the mountain in the wrestling industry, but this is the real world so to speak…do you think you could handle the work without flipping out at anyone?” Hessian was more curious than careful as to how he phrased his words. The prospect of a club run by one high profile wrestler and guarded by another would surely draw in the punters…but would that same wrestler be a danger to that which he was supposedly protecting? “What a thing to say! I’m harmless; I’ve never had a bad word said against me by any staff I’ve worked with in the past! Anyway it’s not like I’m…wanking into a jar and THROWING IT ON THE LITTLE CHILDREN, they will pay for their insolence Hessian-…going crazy against some curtain jerker, it’s just a case of look imposing and calmly handle any trouble from the premises. My reputation should be worth something too surely?” Bill queried, furling his brow and pausing, his mouth ajar and faintly mouthing Hessian’s response in anticipation. “Well…” Hessian began. “I supposed you’re right. But there’ll be a waiver in your contract anyway just in case you decide to get a little punch drunk and out of order…although I assume there won’t be any such incidents?” He cocked an eyebrow at the Mental One, who smiled brightly. “Certainly boss. I look forward to working with you, and hey if you ever think of stocking Gatorade…I’m your man!!” He replied extending an open hand to be met by Hessian’s as they shook in agreement. It was with this handshake that Von Kelsig and William Williams would come to work together. Through his mutual business partner Bruce Shanahan, Hessian acquired a number of other employees who would work at the Slaughterhouse, moving away from Shanahan’s own “organization” to assist the Goliath with his. The benefits would be well worth the effort to both Shanahan and Hessian, and based mainly in the depths of the cavernous hell hidden beneath the floor of the club. With Vampir disposed of it would be easier to concentrate on getting the business up and running, and after months of planning, building and hiring the time was fast approaching for the grand opening of an establishment fuelled by sin and three well known wrestlers. Working with Violence Jack as a business partner worked thanks to a mutuality born out of bloodlust…and there seemed to be some mystical and cosmic reason that made the inclusion of Skitzo into the fold seem right. Success would make Hessian an entrepreneur and provide many more opportunities away from the squared circle…not that he had any plans of quitting any time soon. At least this was something to fall back on. Loose ends felt like they were being tied up, at least all but one. Thinking back to the phone message Hessian found himself despairing. Albeit as active as Boda’s imagination Hessian still had a conscience, and it was forcing him to emote. That chapter in his life would have to be re-opened like old wounds, as he confronted his past. He had no reason to yet felt compelled to do something. Having escorted Skitzo out of the building and returned to his office Hessian collapsed into his chair and closed his eyes, letting his thoughts do the work in their own time. ---------- “This is gonna be a fucking laugh riot man! Ssh, here she comes now!” The squeaky voice emanated from a ventilation shaft adjacent to the door to the women’s toilet. Leaning against the wall below it, Hessian raised a leg, posing and scratching his chin staring into space as a sultry figure approached him. Humming her latest favourite song to herself, Aimz skipped the last couple of steps towards Hessian, sidestepping him and stopping with a hand placed against the toilet door ready to enter. “Well Hess, I didn’t know you liked to pick up chicks outside the bathroom? That’s some deep kinky shit you need to work out on your own big guy!” She laughed jokingly and punched Hessian on the arm playfully, heading into the toilet whistling away as the Mercenary of Satan managed a half smile, still staring ahead into the hallway. “You’re right about the picking up chicks part…only it isn’t me that’s doing the picking up…” He said to himself, his diaphragm tensing up as he tried to stop himself cachinnating. He listened carefully as the whistling carried on, followed by the creaking of a stall door closing…and then silence. What he couldn’t see was the vent cover slowly sliding open to reveal big yellow teeth grinning out of a little impish face. Creeping out of the vent and sliding down a pipe onto the floor, the little creature approached the stall containing the Red Raver. Holding back giggles, he slowly untied his robe at the front, parting it to reveal full frontal nudity in all its grotesque form. A few moments passed before the lock clicked into vacancy, and as the door opened Aimz was met with a shrill and heart-stopping sight. “AIIIIEEEEEEEE!!! SUCK MY ROOT BABAYYYY!!! COME TO BIG POPPA SMURF!!!” “AAAAAGGH! OH MY GOD YOU DISGUSTING LITTLE BASTARD!” Was the reply as Aimz leapt over the streaking creature and out of the bathroom past Hessian, who burst into laughter as she disappeared down the corridor shivering in disgust. Glancing at the door as it opened, Hessian slapped his hands together and began applauding his comrade. “Excellent work Minion, excellent! I wish I’d seen the look on her face!” Hessian exclaimed as Minion secured his robe and cockily strutted into the middle of the hallway. “It doesn’t make sense man, half the OSW has tried to fire into her and she’s scared of one piece of meat? Must be some Freudian shit I don’t understand. I suppose we better go get ready, you got a match against Ivan later tonight…” Minion trailed off as he made his way along the corridor to the dressing room. Shaking his head, Hessian chuckled again before setting off after Minion. Keeping an eye on his manager, the Mercenary of Satan felt an irritation in his eye not unlike an eyelash behind the eyelid. Rubbing his eyes, Hessian opened them again and gasped. “STOP IT! PUT ME DOWN HESS! I’M SORRY! I NEVER MEANT IT! HESSIAN PLEASE! GN-..UGH! PLEeease…uhh…” Jumping back, Hessian found himself staring at a crumpled heap lying on the sidewalk outside of the Gethsemane cemetery in Detroit. The robe lay torn and ruffled over the body as a little leg twitched faintly from beneath, a trickle of blood snaking its way along the concrete from a bruised and battered face, partially hidden by the hood of the robe. He wanted to reach down and check on the limp body, as disbelief and fright graced his face…yet he walked away. Backing away and turning on his best friend, Hessian left the body on the sidewalk, yet found himself glancing back in horror, desperate to help his fallen companion yet unable to physically do anything about it. No matter how hard he thought about it or tried or shouted, Hessian walked away and left Minion for dead. ---------- Guilt. Gasping, Hessian sat bolt upright in his chair, rubbing his eyes and looking over at the clock perched on the wall. An hour and fifteen minutes had passed, yet what he saw felt like it lasted ten at the most. Yawning, Hessian scratched his head and gazed at the telephone in front of him before reaching for a post-it note with a number scrawled on it. Taking a deep breath, Von Kelsig began dialling and held the phone to his ear. After a seemingly eternal ringing, a weary voice answered at the other end. “Minion? It’s Hessian…we need to talk.”
