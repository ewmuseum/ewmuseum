Date: Time keeps on slippin', slippin', slippin'...INTO THE FUTURE!!! “By GAWD woman, can't ya even get that thurr mik'raphone attached to mah shirt without gettin' in tha way of the tee-vee?" Crammed onto the tiny leather couch, Joey Melton, all 400 pounds of him, sat trying to watch the end of "Green Acres" on TVLand. With stringy blonde hair that barely covered his now balding head, the veritable couch potato sat backstage on the set of the Jerry Springer show waiting for his time in the spotlight. Hey, the Springer Show is Bizzaro-World enough...it translates just fine here too. "Done in a just a minute, Mister Melton." The svelte redheaded production assistant was struggling to work around Melton's hippopotamus-sized stomach and get the wiring secured. Not only was she the n00b of the crew, but she also drew the short straw at the morning's pre-taping meeting. Her confidence leveled fizzled when she saw Melton; once lean and sexy with eyes as blue as the ocean and teeth as white as the pearls he used to be able to afford his wife, the man had now degenerated into a blob of flesh and broken dreams with copious amounts of ill-will and repressed anger to go around. Even though there were plenty of doubters to go around, there were some who thought that the marriage of Melton and Lindsay Troy would have lasted given everything they had been through prior to their tying of the knot. But ten years and one messy divorce later had left Joey broke, scarily overweight and with a horrendous Southern accent. The welfare checks he now received were barely enough to cover the amount of bon-bons and other junk food he craved to make him forget about Troy, let alone pay expenses on the double-wide he now called home. Joey's cursed the existence of God since that time with Mike Randalls in the Mojave desert, but he can't deny that He's got a sense of humor ... a very sick one. "Finally, you're all set," said the unnamed production assistant....who we'll now call BobbySue like in that Steve Miller Band song. You know the one. "Good, now c'mere so I can get ta know ya better." "Huh?" "I'm sure ya heard me, sugah." "Just who do you think I am anyway?" "Kinda cute an' availabuhl..." said Joey, who flashed her a yellow-toothed grin. He patted his lap, the motion garnering a dubious look from BobbySue. "Like hell I'm getting any closer to you," she said before storming out of the room in a huff. "Meh." Melton pouted, then turned his attention back to the TV where Wally Cleaver was trying to make a soluble coagulate into a solid in chemistry class. ************* "Now that we've heard Joey's side of the story," Jerry Springer said with his arms folded in front of him, standing on the steps in-between two sections of audience members while the words 'My Wife Left Me For a Midget' appeared across his knees, "it's time to bring out the woman who scorned him. Please welcome Lindsay Troy!" Outrageous and zealous boos filled the sound stage as Troy appeared through a door that was cut-out of the set. Wearing indecently high boots that lifted her up to about 5'6" if she were lucky and her blonde hair piled high on her head in a bow-tied ponytail, she pranced across the stage to flaunt her petite body to the crowd. In fact, if no one knew any better, she might as well have renamed herself Duchess. Proudly sitting down in a chair positioned far away from where Melton was sitting on a different couch, Troy flipped her hair and chewed her gum obnoxiously before glancing over at Melton. The man hadn't even bothered to clean up, looking like someone had dumped a plateful of schnitzel onto his shirt as well as a scoopful of refried beans and a splattering of salsa. "Quite, like, the get-up you've got there Joey." Righteously, the audience booed while she waved them off with her perfectly manicured nails. Melton flipped her off with one chubby middle finger. "So then," Springer started while the crowd became even more raucous. "Joey's told us that your lives were pretty stable..." The camera focused on Troy before quick-panning over to Melton. "That's true, Jerry." "Until recently, correct?" Verifying the statement, she replied, "Yes, we just finalized our divorce." "Why was this?" "Xtramarital affairs." Yea....BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!! Zero people in the room were siding with Troy. Putting on a full-blown puppy-dog face, she panned the audience in hopes that one person was siding with her. And, of course, there wasn't. Batting her eyes, she continued. "I tried to, like, be faithful. But it was hard. After awhile, Joey just didn't want to go out and do anything anymore. I was still young, vivacious, even though I was doing most of the work raising our child..." Cutting in, Melton screamed, "I went places with you! You dragged me shopping with you and never once did I complain." "Damnit, Joey, trying to pull me into JC Penny because you wanted to flirt with the women in the Separates department doesn't count as shopping!" "Enough!" Jerry commanded, and the two fell silent while the audience remained rowdy. "There was marital discord for awhile then. When did the infidelity start? "Fourteen months ago. Joey didn't find out until eight months ago." Gesturing his head up and down, Springer continued. "And did he know the person you were cheating on him with?" "Hell yeah I did, she cheated on me with mah best friend! That sumbitch's been walkin' the walk eva since she left me for him!" "It ain't my fault that he's got a bigger d(BLEEEEEEP!)k than you!" Jerry shook his head. If there hasn't been a defining moment in which his show was going to Hell in a handbasket, this might be it. Kindergarteners behave better than this. "Let's bring him out then!" was the reply, to wild audience approval. Meet Adrian Evans, Joey's (now former) best friend. The two had traveled the world together, selling out Carnival cruises and saving cancer patients from Jacko's master bedroom's shower. Now, the midget bounded onto the stage and jumped into Troy's waiting arms, obscenely making out with her in front of Joey, who couldn't even move his weight off the couch to break up the display. For years, Adrian had mooched off of Melton after saving him from near-death in Japan. Now, karma The audience responded with a "JERRY! JERRY! JERRY!" chant while Melton screamed at Adrian from his seat. "No good, two-timin', elephant d(BLEEEEEEEEEEEEP!) mother(BLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP!)" "Oh yeah? Why don'tcha get off that couch and come on over here and do somethin' about it? Go on, I dare ya! I double-dog dare ya, old man!" Powerful security guards stormed the stage to keep the situation from escalating, only for the "JERRY! JERRY! JERRY! " chants to get louder and the screaming between the guilty parties to get more obscene. Thankfully, everyone's spared the cliched, "it was all a nightmare ending" and can rest easy knowing this.
