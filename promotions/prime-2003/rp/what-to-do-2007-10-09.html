What to do...

9 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 10/9/07 What to do, what to do... Titan sat in his recliner and gazed around his home. Finally, being back home. No crazy people trying to kill him, no having to run across state lines, no desire to murder the next person to come through the door. And not really much else to do. He cast around, looking from spot to spot. The gardener he'd hired had kept the house looking fine outside in his absence, and he kept the house clean out of habit. Nothing to do there. He looked at his pistol, sitting on the endtable next to him. He'd cleaned all of his weapons this morning. He pursed his lips a moment, then cast his eyes around again. The television in the corner stayed off. He didn't particularly care for using it for entertainment, and honestly, the news irritated him as often as not. United Nations condemning Israel over and over again and people all too eager to jump on the bandwagon. The television stayed off. What to do... He slowly stood up and stretched, bumping his elbows on the ceiling. His parents and Stephanie were still at Quantico, loading up. Since he'd just flat taken off, he didn't have anything to bring back, and it would be tomorrow before he could help them with anything. He had a match coming up, but what more could he do? He'd done enough tape study, working out more wasn't really going to help... With that, the big man stepped outside, carefully pulling the door closed behind him. He sighed. Everything was nice out... He shook his head. Maybe... maybe next week would be better. And maybe... maybe he'd win next week. Sorry to Chainz, long day at work and I have to go apply for a new apartment tonight. Next time I'll give you a real matchup.
