Date: 10/02/05 Prelude Everyday life has its up’s and downs, sometimes you begin to get so jaded that you wanna sleep yourself to the weekend, or sometimes permanently. As for Shane Wethers, he knows that life as the front man of a multi-platinum selling Punk-Rock band is sometimes a little hard to grasp. One minute your living the luxuries that fame can bring, but the next you are feeling as cheap as chips. A life of wrestling can help get rid of the anger, but how can it clear away the depression that eats away at you for a life time? Here’s the answer, we don’t look at the bad times, and we look at the good! Shane is finally out of rehabilitation and looking to get his career back on track, but something seems very strange in Shane’s recent attitude. The enormous ego that has been part of Shane for so many years now has began to grow. In todays epic we take a journey into the fun loving life of an Eternal Punk, here’s a hefty portion of the Eternal Punk! Monday mornings, there’s something about that very phrase that makes a weekend seem invaluable. You spend Friday night in a gratified manner whilst drinking so much your brain cells begin to tap dance in your skull. Saturdays are pretty much the same, unless you’ve got a killer hangover and you feel like a character out of Michael Jackson’s ‘Thriller’. Oh, then there’s the ever so precious Sundays where you spend you afternoons in bed, hiding under the covers, hoping the weekend will suddenly be extended whilst jacking off to the page three girls in the local paper. However you spend your weekend, that despondent scent of Monday morning always lurks around the corner. That’s unless you are the lead singer of an infamous punk rock band with hits that make your skin break dance, this is the world of an Eternal Punk. In all his expense, Shane lays back on his comforting king size bed in his mansion based in Central London. The whole room of Shane’s is decked out like a dream room, with a large marble fireplace, a mini-bar in the far left corner, a wall full of golden records and a large four post bed in the middle with purple bedcovers. Giggling and talking comes from under the bed-covers as the faint sound of a car pulling into the pebble driveway of his house can be heard. The sounds stop for a moment as the giggling continues. “Knock-knock” All of a sudden Shane’s face pops out of the covers with a surprised expression, his hair is all ruffled like a member of Kiss without the make up. He turns to look at his bed-side clock 9.15pm “What the…” Another face pops out of the covers next to him, the face of a petite blonde girl with a smile worth a million-dollars. She sits up, leaning across to Shane who has his back to her, looking at the clock. He slides out of bed as she puts her arm across his chest to try restrain him. “baby..come back to bed” she pleads in her sweet-talking tone. Shane slides on his blue t-shirt with the words ‘Eternal Madness’ in jazzy aluminous green letters. “Let me answer the door and I’ll be right back” Shane replies with a heavy sigh at the end. He picks up his dark blue baggy jeans with a silver chain hanging from the pocket, he pulls them up whilst walking into the landing of his house. He begins to lose balance, swaying forwards a little and then backwards before his legs give way causing him to fall down the steps on his front. “aaaaaaarghhh!” he yells whilst putting his hands infront of him to stop his head hitting the floor.Tumble-tumble-bang! Shane finally reaches the bottom of the stairs with a dazed expression on his face. He gets slowly to his feet and pulls up his pants, scratching his balls and shaking his head as he makes his way to the door. Knock-knock-knock knock-knock “Wait a damn minute for crying out loud. If it’s the Avon Lady again I’m gonna shove those cosmetics up your bitch ass” he mumbles as he walks towards the door, gripping the small iron handle, turning it clockwise and opening the door. At the door stands Mikey ‘The Mallet’ Fishmore. Mikey Fishmore gets the nickname ‘the mallet’ from and old business he used to run with his wife, selling many different kinds of wooden mallets to suit your needs. Needless to say, the business wasn’t exactly the Microsoft of the business world, so things went quickly from bad to worse. He caught his wife in bed with one of the workers, he weren’t best pleased to say the very least, but Mikey being Mikey; he picked up a steel mallet and beat the kid to death with it. It wasn’t a pretty sight neither, with a swift crack to the skull his cranium shattered like a world record. His wife threatened to take him to caught and sue him for everything he had. So he beat her to death too, not exactly a smart thing to do on behalf of his wife. What is Shane doing with him you ask? Well when you are a multi-millionaire lead singer of an infamous Rock band and a legendary pro-wrestler, sometimes its wise to have someone to watch your back or maybe, to keep your friends close…but your enemies closer. Anyway, Mikey contacted a few friends and did a few dodgy dealings in the car industry, before long he was earning half a million a week. The guy knows absolutely everything there is to know about the crime in London. If there’s a guy you need to do sort out your local tax man or even the guy who’s been screwing your wife behind your back, then Mr Fishmore can sort it for a good price. Mikey stood a medium sized guy with large sunglasses on with a brown lens. The guy is pretty old and is taking drags from a large Cuban cigar; he wears a large musty beige overcoat. He kind of reminds you of Jimmy Saville with an attitude, apart from the ridiculous white hair and distasteful tracksuit. “Where’ve you been Guv?” says Mikey in a concerned voice, “I’ve been pranking your mobi all morning and I got no answer”. Mikeys eyes search Shane in a disturbed manner. “…and why your pants un-done? Tell me you haven’t been whacking off” Shane looks at Mikey with equally disgraced expression “of course not you muppet, it’s 9 o’clock, what the hell you doing ‘ere?” Shane asks as he begins to walk away from the door rubbing his weary eyes. “Are you insane? It’s Monday morning… time to sort out your security for your Tour of the U., otherwise we’ve got a bunch of monkeys at Chester Zoo who will be happy to watch your backs whilst raiding the food cart.” Mikey protests, whilst inviting himself into Shane’s home. He brushes passed Shane and makes his way into the narrow hallway, slowly approaching the steep staircase. The marble floor leaves a creaking sound under the soles of his shoes as he continues to make his way up the staircase as Shane follows in a concerned manner. “Man, what you doing?” he detests in an impatient way. Mikey doesn’t reply, he just continues up the stairs with a determined look upon his long face, he walks straight into Shane’s bedroom. “Mikey… Stop!” Shane yells, but realises that Mike is seriously onto him. “What the hell?”…a yelling comes from Mikey inside the bedroom. Shane walks up the remaning set of stairs and proceeds into his bedroom where he sees Mikey stood looking wide eyed at his girl in bed, stark naked with the velvet covers around her. “Who are you?” the girl asks with fear in her eye, Mikey sees her jeans and blouse on the floor, he picks them up and throws them at her. “I’m your time-keeper, your times up, now fuck off” Mikey declares in an angry and even more impatient tone whilst Shane stands rubbing his eyes trying to make sense of it all. Without hesitation, Shane walks into the bathroom with a smirk on his face as the row continues. Shane puts the small black plug into the plug hole of the wide white sink and fills it with hot water, whilst looking into the mirror at the lines under his bright blue eyes. Who’d have thought that such demonic eyes could look so innocent? He yawns and stretches, whilst sniffing his arm pits to check if he still wreaks of sweat from the night after. Shane screws up his face in disgust of the stench and lets go a gut-wrenching belch. He wafts his hand in front of his nose and scowls. “Dang…beer breath, shame I haven’t got a hang over to go with it” Shane mutters to himself with zest. Shane leans forward and scoops water onto his face once and then twice before looking up in the mirror at his face in the hope that the lines would somehow miraculously disappear. Suddenly the bathroom door opens and as Shane turns around to see Mikey stood there trying to figure out just what the hell Shane is upto. “We’ve a busy day at hand with plenty of work to do and your too busy knobbin’ some lifeless whore!” Mikey complains whilst running his hand across the paintwork on the doorframe. “Aren’t I allowed a little fun now? After all we had a successful gig in Leeds last night. I returned home and put on the show of my life. Doesn’t the saying go like ‘work hard play hard’ ?” Shane quizzes. Mikey chuckles to himself and passes Shane a face towel. “too much play and not enough work me’ lad” he mutters under his breathe. Shane rubs his face down with the towel before handing it back to Mikey. “So what have you done with her?” Shane askes with a smug grin on his face. “Through her out with the rest of the trash” Mikey replies with not an ounce of hestitation in his voice. Shane brushes passed him, walking over to the landing and straight into his bedroom. “You’re two heavy handed with the girls… I was going to get rid of her when the time is right.” Shane says as he slides on a pair of clean Pringles socks. “Anyway, what have you got for me?” he asks, walking back into the hallway. “Ritchie ‘the screamer’ Burrows, he’s excellent with a gun…” Mikey informs Shane, “he used to go pigeon shooting with his father. Rumour has it that his father was very hard on him, anyway one day he snapped and fired the gun at his fathers Mustang, the shot rebound and hit his father square in the forehead.” Shane sighs heavily “Listen, you get who ever you feel is right” Shane informs him, “for now my mind is on beating Jackson Darlek at Culture Shock and going on to win the Tournament”“Don’t you mean ‘Darilek’” Mikey askes,“That’s right,” He informs Mikey “I’d rather we could smoke a few fatties but this isn’t High-volution”“Fatties?” Mikey askes“Yeah…y’know?” Shane responds with a sinister grin, “Like your moma”“What?!?!” Mikey shouts in anger “Damnit Wethers! You better stop talking about my moma already! I’m getting an inferiority complex!” Shane laughs and shakes his head“Don’t worry fellow Wethermaniac, It’s Culture Shock after I beat that Darlek…”Shane is cut off as Mikey interrupts “That’s DARILEK”“Yeah what-ever-they-call-him” Shane replies “ He so much more like a Darlek, because he’s that same old boring nobody, like that dumb Darlek robot from Dr Who” Mikey Smirks and Shane walks down the stairs. “Dual Halo Tournament…” Shane begins “29 wannabes and the Eternal Legend. No one is gonna stand in my way this Sunday.Shane walks over to a brown desk and takes out two classes from the glass cabinet in front of him. He grabs a bottle of Jack Daniels and begins to poor a large measure into each “I’ve always been a legend in Prime, but what respect do I get for it?” Shane informs before pausing. He stares into space with a dark expression “Then there’s that…. Pickledick who people look up to as Prime’s hall of famer” Shane continues “He will pay and all the Wethermaniacs will be happy in the knowledge that I am the ONLY TRUE Hall of Famer… questions will be answered this Sunday” Shane takes a large drink from his class and slams it down on the table as the scene ends
