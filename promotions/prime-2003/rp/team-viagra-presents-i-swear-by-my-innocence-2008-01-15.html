Team VIAGRA Presents : I Swear By My Innocence

15 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Jan 15th, 2008 EXT. OPEN ROAD HIGHWAY -- DUSK High Flyer and Tony Davis speed quite quickly. Perhaps too quickly, if there were a hint of police cruiser around that day. Feeling safe and secure, Team VIAGRA drive down this empty stretch of highway at speeds clearing a hundred miles an hour. Davis fumbles with the cd player, taking something out and putting a new burned cd in. Flyer slams his hands into the steering wheel. HIGH FLYER : Fucking Tsonda. I swear. If that little jumping bean went through as much arthroscopic knee surgery as I have; I'd've already kicked his teeth straight through the back of his skull, I swear. TONY DAVIS: I wanted to talk to you about all this swearin' too. Just then, Jack Black rips on through the stereo speakers, shouting "Fuck yeah!" HIGH FLYER: These can't be words coming from your mouth. I mean, you called my grandmother Cuntmelly Zombiestein. TONY DAVIS: Dude, I thought that was her name. HIGH FLYER: If you want to talk to me about my swearing, I want to talk to you about saying dude all the time. TONY DAVIS: It's not all the time(slight hesitation). I didn't say it there. HIGH FLYER: No, but you wanted to. 1989 called, Michelangelo wants his catch phrase back. TONY DAVIS: (Confused) I don't say cowabunga. Flyer sighs, turning the radio louder to blast some Tenacious D. It doesn't stay loud for long, as Tony spins the volume knob down to one. TONY DAVIS: Kate asked me. For your kids. She gave me a written letter because she wanted to make sure she yelled at you the correct way through proxy, but I lost the letter when I thought it was a sandwich wrapper. HIGH FLYER: Now you see why I keep the copies of our contracts. TONY DAVIS: Just, c'mon man! I mean, you hardly ever sweared in the past. I mean, you were the anti-swear. You had a whole campaign. It might as well have been governmentally funded. HIGH FLYER: You're thinking about our anti-drug propaganda we did where Temple Students paid us in roman noodles for a bunch of rants about how you shouldn't smoke because Matthew Perry is a douche. TONY DAVIS: Matthew Perry is AWESOME! What was that movie with Bruce Campbell? HIGH FLYER: McHale's Navy? TONY DAVIS: It was back when Elizabeth Hurley, well, she didn't have a career anymore, but she was trying to have a career again. HIGH FLYER: Bedazzled? TONY DAVIS: ... Are you just shouting random gibberish words at me? Tony crosses his arms across his chest, and remorses out of the side window. Flyer sighs, rolling his eyes. HIGH FLYER: Fine. I won't swear anymore. TONY DAVIS: Good. HIGH FLYER: But that's a promise I renege whenever I see Sonny Silver. TONY DAVIS: That's fine. I renege my promise not to swear whenever I see Lindsay Troy. HIGH FLYER: I don't know what your fascination is with her. Is it like a monkey at the zoo or what? TONY DAVIS: Dude, red hair. Does it to me all the time. HIGH FLYER: Forgetting that you're married to my sister, doesn't a girl just need to HAVE hair? TONY DAVIS: Sometimes not even. That girl who played with the clown guy, the girl with the breakdown and the schoolgirl outfits, and the girl who hates the Pope in 1992. HIGH FLYER: You mean Natalie Portman, Britney, and Sinead O'Connor? You think they're hot? TONY DAVIS: Oh, I forgot you didn't go to my high school. I was talking about this set of triplets who shaved their head graduating year. Cindy's in the circus, Mindy's takin' a vacation at Betty Ford, and Dandy was killed by a mob of students at graduation. HIGH FLYER: I didn't know you went to a catholic school. TONY DAVIS: Neither did I until they made me clean up the body. HIGH FLYER: Let's never talk about that again. TONY DAVIS: Amen. The blood is still on my hands. HIGH FLYER: That's new blood because you cut yourself today and you haven't washed it off. TONY DAVIS: But it keeps coming back every time I use scissors! HIGH FLYER: You could try to be more careful. TONY DAVIS: But then I get less done in a day! HIGH FLYER: You spend the majority of the day lying on the couch. TONY DAVIS: And if I took extra time to cut out my coupons I would have less time for my bloody hands to recover while lying like a sloth on the couch. HIGH FLYER: You ever think you're dyslexic, because you're thinking is all backwards. Tony doesn't respond, and lets the comment linger in the air. After a moment, he turns, looking directly into the camera. TONY DAVIS: Hey, why is there a cameraman back here. Did we take a hostage in Los Angeles? HIGH FLYER: No. No. I've got a match with some guy named Tom. I think he's a stock broker or something. Y'know, gotta do the ol' interview. TONY DAVIS: So it's 2000 again. We're gonna just have camera's follow us around and tape our useless and pointless conversations? HIGH FLYER: I'm sure things of meaning will happen. TONY DAVIS: Good. Because this is perfect for my attempts to get on the Real World. HIGH FLYER: You're, like, a decade outside of their casting range. TONY DAVIS: I can play youth. SEE! Tony's eyes burst wide with the innocence of a child. TONY DAVIS: I LOVE CAMELS AND PENGUINS! And Santa Clause is real! The Tooth Fairy gives me candy for my teeth! And Money DOES grow on trees! Tony snaps out of it. TONY DAVIS: See, perfect adolescence. I'm a shoo-in this year. I got Poser editing my piece! INT. EDITING STUDIO -- NIGHT Eddie Scott Poser, the self-proclaimed rightful heir to the throne of Poland, is busily working the controls. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Let's see... star wipe. Poser does a few clicks. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Hm, star wipe. Poser clicks some more. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: Let's try a cross fade. Poser does a few clicks, and then begins to shake his head disapprovingly. EDDIE SCOTT POSER: It just doesn't have that... star power feel. OOO! I know what does! Star wipe! EXT. OPEN ROAD -- CONTINUOUS Flyer sighs. Tony fumbles with the radio buttons for no specific reason. HIGH FLYER: I hope you know what you're doing. TONY DAVIS: I hope you know what you're doing against that Tom dude. Could be trouble. I mean, he doesn't even HAVE to have a gimmick name. he's just TOM! That's a scary mother fucker, not to even need an apostrophe'd nickname, that's for sure. HIGH FLYER: I guess we'll see. TONY DAVIS: Certainly. We'll see. Suddenly, a vulture swoops down and begins to gnaw at Tony's eyes. TONY DAVIS: OH GOD! I CAN'T SEE! I CAN'T SEE! The car goes swerving a bit, as Tony is flailing his arms wildly in a distracting manner. We hear VIAGRA's screams as we see a stock shot from Dukes of Hazard. Their car flies off of a jump, and then, freezes. NARRATOR: How WILL the boys of Viagra jam themselves out of this pickle? Why am I reading a script when there's a Writer's Strike. Ah, fuck this. I'm out. (FADE OUT. FADE IN.) NARRATOR: Next week, on TEAM VIAGRA! (The freeze framed car unfreezes, and lands safely on the ground.) NARRATOR: High Flyer faces Tom Wal... Walk... Can you get me this Tom guy on the phone so I can figure out the pronunciation?! This is due in the editor's studio by 4! I swear. Incompetents. (FADE OUT.)
