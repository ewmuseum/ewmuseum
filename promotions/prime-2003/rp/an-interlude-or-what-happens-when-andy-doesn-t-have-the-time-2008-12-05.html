An interlude (or what happens when Andy doesn't have the time to do a proper RP)

5 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 5th December This business is bullshit. I mean, seriously. How can this fuckin’ shit be classed as a sport? Ya got a two hour show, and just how many ‘a those 120 minutes are made up ‘a guys fightin’ dukin’ it out? Thirty? Maybe forty at a push? Rest of it’s nothin’ more than a bunch of jumped-up little fuckstains prancin’ around backstage like ballerinas. You got Bryan Dawkin’s whinin’ and cryin’ about some fuckin’ dead girl to any sympathetic fuckin’ retard who’ll listen, and then ya got Chandler Tsonda getting’ all hissy and pissy ‘cause he got his shit wrecked by some over-the-hill dick in a mask. So ya get maybe a third of the show dedicated to combat, an’ even then it’s hardly an’ exictin’ affair. Since when did fightin’ involve some 190lb pipsqueak flyin’ through the sky with some stupid fuckin 960 degree flip bullshit? Since when did fightin’ involve steel cages, ladders… Dual Halos? What a load ‘a bullshit. A guy like Jimmy Bonafide can show up on TV, do a couple ‘a stupid spinning flip things that make the kiddies cheer then say somethin’ in that stupid lil’ accent of his an’ call himself a grappler? Am I missin’ somethin’ here? Utter, utter bullshit. Half you pricks couldn’t fight yer way outta a damp paper bag. I mean, look at the “top dog,” Chandler Tsonda. Dude used to be a model. A fucking MODEL Tells you all ya need to know about wrestlin’ when a guy like that prissy lil’ poof can waltz in and nab the top strap. These men have no credibility, none whatsoever. Alright, couple of ‘em might’ve come from a fightin’ background, I get that. It’s the posers, the pretenders. Those are the cunts I can’t stand. You can take anyone at all an’ turn him into a star in this business, don’t matter if he can swing a punch or not. Look at “Darth” Von Doom? Fuck’s that anyway, some kinda joke? Or shit, Brock Shepherd. S’like a bunch of guys were sittin’ in the boardroom one day and one of them is all “hey, wouldn’t it be sooooo crazy and whacky to hire a cripple?” And BOOM! They pluck this one-armed fuck straight outta the neartest leper colony and, next thing you know, he’s a big wrestlin’ star. Total fuckin’ nonsense, the lot of it. Way I see it, this ain’t no combat sport. S’more like a soap opera. There’s more drama on a ReVolution broadcast then there is on the latest episode of The OC, an’ it drives me round the fuckin’ bend. Seriously. This is supposed to be a fuckin’ wrestlin’ show! I don’t care about High Flyer’s shitty jokes an’ I don’t give a flying fuck about boring cunts like Rhett Locke and Jay Phoenix. I tune into a “combat sport,” I expect to see dudes knockin’ ten shades ‘a shit out of one another, not some snivellin’ little spastic cryin’ ‘bout hw he’s all depressed ‘cause he’s “held back” or whatever. And shit, if ya thought these wankers were nauseating on television, ya should try coexistin’ with them backstage. Fuckin’ nightmare. All sorts of egos an’ attitudes flyin’ around the place. S’like a fuckin’ circus. An’ if the posing fuckstains ain’t bad enough, ya got a legion of PRIME slave monkeys to worry about, all runnin’ round the place trippin’ out ‘cause they accidentally bought the wrong kinda cavier for Tyler Rayne’s rice biscuits. This ain’t fightin’, this is a fuckin’ circus. An’ then ya got mongs like Troy Douglas runnin’ round the place talkin’ ‘bout how much they LOVE this business, how they’d give anything for it, all that bullshit. What a bucket of shit. If you are seriously tryin’ to tell me that the only reason yer in this business AIN’T the six figure salary, yer a fuckin’ liar and a cunt. It’s a shit business. It’s a soap opera masquerading as a sport, populated by a bunch of jumped-up little fucks who wouldn’t last five seconds in a straight-up fist fight and are more concerned ‘bout their next “big storyline” or how good their hair looks. Every time I step into that locker-room I’m like the fuckin’ black sheep. ‘Cause I don’t make a song an’ dance about myself, ‘cause I don’t prance and preen and kiss the asses of the fans every night, I’m the odd one out. And you know what? I fuckin’ love it. I am Jason Natas. I am the “Anti-Superstar” for a fuckin’ reason. And Fusenshoff? You ain’t shit, son.
