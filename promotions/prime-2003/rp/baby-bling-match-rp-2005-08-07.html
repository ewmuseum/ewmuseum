Baby Bling (match RP)

7 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 8-7-05 “Your Personal Savior” Hoyt Williams and his fiancée the Mother Mary are sitting in the sales office of Dusty Robes the king of in ring apparel. Dusty’s been making wrestling attire for over forty years and selling the legendary garments at his shop “The Robe Warriors Department Store” in Phoenix, Arizona. Despite being a tad eccentric and clearly homosexual Hoyt knows a good product and buys his attire only from Dusty. But today Hoyt isn’t looking for new ring attire for himself but rather the little Savior in Mary’s oven. Dusty dressed in a purple and olive green striped ring jacket strolls into his office and takes a seat at his desk kicking his diamonded studded wrestling boots up on his perfectly organized desk. He looks at Hoyt and then at Hoyt’s pregnant wife and smiles. Dusty: You stud you! Congratulations. Mary: Thank you. Dusty: I guess it’s too late to convert you to our side Hoyt? The large fashion designer winks at the Universal Champion and smiles a seductive yet grotesque smile. Hoyt: No I’m happy as a wrestler but if I do decide to give it up to work in fashion I’ll be sure the give you call. With a smile and a wink back its obvious Dusty’s come on went over Hoyt’s head. Dusty doesn’t follow up and gets right to business. A business that nets him a starting price of ten thousand dollars per robe. Dusty: We just sent you a whole wardrobe of GOD inspired Universal Championship robes. Back already? Hoyt: We are not here for me today. We are here for the baby. No way is my kid going to look like the other diaper messing, spit drooling, non-savior babies. He is the second generation of the second coming. He is the baby Jesus. We are going to name his after my decease older brother Jesus. He will be Jesus Hoyt Williams. JHW! Can we get that on his first baby boots a big JHW down the side? The oversized rhinestone junky takes his feet off the desk and leans forward to talk frankly with Hoyt. Dusty: When is the baby due? Mary: Five months. Hoyt honey, we aren’t even sure it’s a boy yet. Hoyt: Are you kidding me? Who do I look like Boda? I produce men and saviors. My sperm is biblically blessed. Taking a load from me is like taking communion. Dusty: AMEN!! Hoyt: Huh? Mary: Can you help us? Dusty: Well to be honest I produce in ring gear not baby cloths. Besides it will be at least 6 months before you really start dressing him. Hoyt: Well when my baby is in the room with the other chub rubs and their baby fat I want my son to stand out. That reminds me we are going to need a jogging suit for him to work off the lazy rolls he’s going to have laying around in his mother all day. Do you think there is a way to get him to do kick exercises while he’s in there? Mary: I don’t think I want the baby kicking me all the time Hoyt. Hoyt: What’s worse a little discomfort or a fat baby? Anyways those kids are dressed in something when you look at them through the glass I know I’m a father now. We need some Little jump suits for when he does some spit ups for Uncle Jesus. I want my kids to robes to read “Savior Two” or “Boy King”!! Dusty: The babies are wrapped in blankets and that’s it they don’t dress them. Hoyt lies back in his chair and thinks this over for a moment. Hoyt: Fine then, I want a crib and a blanket that will sparkle and blind all the other fathers. I want everybody to look at my baby and say WOW. Can you wow me Dusty Robes? Dusty: Oh honey I can WOW you anytime you want. Hoyt: Then lets wow the world when the boy king is here. You have five months to make the perfect crib and dress for the Prince of wrestling and the next generation of Savior. Dusty: Done deal! Mary: Thanks Dusty. Everybody starts picking up and gathering their accessories preparing for an exit. Dusty: So you worried about Deville? Hoyt: No he can’t produce a savior no matter who he nails. He doesn’t have the biblically blessed semen. The next generation of wrestling will be controlled by GOD at the hands of the Williams family. Hell I may spread my seed and create a whole stable of Hoyt son’s! Dusty: Call me if you need help with that. The off color remark again passes right by the Son of Sons and the King of Kings. Dusty: No honey, I was speaking of your four way match this week. With Olsig, Deville, and Lamen are you ready to take them all out? Hoyt: Oh, that match. I’m Mr. Pay-Per-View I’m not big on performing on free television. That’s for amateurs and non-champions. You can’t knock Deville’s record the man is a winner. A pathetic, disgusting, filthy, corrupt, scumbag, sinner; but a winner. However to tell you truth I’m the son of God and the Universal Champion and he isn’t. If he was so damn good he would have the title and not me. Proving once again how great I am and how pathetic he is. You see his mind is distracted by life and his environment. He can’t handle life outside of the ring and concentrate on getting ahead inside of PRIME. Hoyt stands up and starts to speak with his hands and body. Hoyt: I mean look at me, I run a multi-billion dollar tax free religious organization. I’m fathering the third coming of Christ. I head up the greatest stable in wrestling history and I do all this with out swearing or losing my temper. Some guys can’t face their demons that God and I make them confront. Hoyt reaches up to his chest and holds up the wedding ring the belonged to the dead wife of Killean and smiles. Dusty: You do dig deep baby that’s for sure. Hoyt: I know all and see all that is what makes me great. I know Olsig used to be called Furher and sucked. I know he’s nothing more then a Columbian bean farmer trying his dandiest to be great like me. “PRIME presents HOYT WILLIAMS: Savior, Saint, Champion” went on sale last Tuesday on both DVD and VHS format. It’s PRIMES top selling DVD already and from what I heard Olsig was first in line at his neighborhood Best Buy to catch a glimpse at my biblically historical success. He can take all the notes and emulate me all he wants but he will never be me. That is where so many fault, there is only one savior and his name is Hoyt Williams. Dusty: You think Ellis Easton as Ref is going to play a factor? Hoyt: Let me tell you something about Ellis Easton he is a great American and understands that important people should be placed on a pedestal while the common folk like Furher and Deville should be ignored. We the people of power and celebrity shouldn’t have to wait in lines and what not. You think Jesus had to wait for a seat at the Noah’s Ark seafood restaurant? Nope he had priority seating. He was Jesus the Savior and I am Hoyt the Savior and UNIVERSAL CHAMPION and ONE HELL OF A GUY! So no I think Ellis being the ref is a great thing. I’m going to give him one of those celebrity gift bags they give to presenters at the Academy Awards. He deserves it. He may be a lustful sinner but I dig the guy. Dusty: I DIG the guy too. Tell him if he needs a robe to call Dusty Robes! Hoyt: I’ll be sure to do that. Mary: Hey guys, you are forgetting about Kyle Lamen. Hoyt and Dusty shoot each other confused glimpses before returning their enigma filled faces back towards the Mother Mary. Hoyt: Who? Dusty: Lame man? Hoyt: I think she said Lamont. Mary: No, Kyle Lamen. He was in PCW for a short time. Hoyt: Who in PRIME wasn’t in PCW besides me? That means dick! The last two PRIME champions were NEVER in PCW. I the current UNIVERSAL CHAMPION was NEVER in that pathetic federation. What’s this kid’s story? Mary: He is the Prince of Punk Rock. Both Dusty and Hoyt break out in a hardy chuckle like Richard Pryor is telling Santa Claus dirty jokes on Christmas day after a few eggnogs. Hoyt: That’s like being Mayor of Cleveland. Who cares! I mean really, would I brag if I was heir to a junk yard. Well he may be the “Prince of Punk Rock” while I’m the “King of Kings”. I am his savior and no matter how bad the music he enjoys is, he needs to understand that simple fact. I’m the son of his creator; I am his UNIVERSAL CHAMPION, his Savior, and his taker. Mary: Oh and he’s from New Jersey. Hoyt: Oh GOD how repugnant can you get? The city spawned by Satan himself. This punk is going to learn a thing or two about respect and looking a SAVIOR in the eye. By the time I’m doing saving and converting his ass he’s be listening to good music like the Eagles and Credence. Dusty: And Cheer. Hoyt: Ummm maybe not. Mary: Also the RISP research labs tell me this guy is also a mama’s boy. Hoyt: That goes with out saying he listens to punk music. I need you to send him a memo letting him know before he gets in the ring with me he’s going to have to shower and put on a clean t-shirt. I know how these kids are and they all don’t bathe. I hope he gets permission from his mommy to bring his guitar to the ring with him because I will snag that sin tool and urinate on it with the blessed yellow showers of heaven. I will laugh as it melts as God takes home another instrument of SATAN. Dusty: When will these guys learn that to be great you need to dress great? Hoyt: These kids know nothing about being great of looking great. They are obsessed with false prophets and what the MTV tells them to do. Slaves of Satan’s corporations is what they are. Sinners will be cleansed this week as Hoyt Williams CHAMPION SAVIOR AND SAINT crucifies and saves their sorry souls. They can thank me later. Mary: Now that is the gospel truth. Dusty walks with the Williams family out of the office before shaking hands with Mary and Hoyt. Dusty: So are you going to answer Duke’s call and be on his little TV segment? Hoyt: I don’t need to face him, he is nothing more then a poop stirring washed up wrestling reporter. I think he just wants to see a world title up close as it’s been so long. Hell TONY ROLO beat him for the ICWA world title back when Punky Bruster was hip. He’s got nothing, I may just stroll out there and give him a moment of time with greatness just to make his day. Besides it’s about time for me to reveal my secret master plan. Dusty: What is the secret master plan? Hoyt: Ahhh Dusty Robes it wouldn’t be a secret master plan if I told you now would it? Dusty: I guess not. Hoyt: The day is coming spread the word of Hoyt. Mary: Thanks Dusty and call me if you have any questions on the baby stuff. Dusty: Anytime. Hoyt steps into his brand new 1966 Cadillac Coup Deville covered in mini Universal titles diamonds and all. Dusty: To hell with all them rappers this guy has style and bling~! =w=
