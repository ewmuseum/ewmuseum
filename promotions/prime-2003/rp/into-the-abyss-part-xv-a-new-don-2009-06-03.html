Into The Abyss: Part XV - A New Don

3 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 03-06-09 Monday, June 1st 2009 A fine dust lingered in the air around her hand as she filed her nails down. A soft blow from her lips disrupted the gentle ebbing of the powder and it came to settle on the blue foam mouse mat on her desk. Admiring her handiwork, Gloria turned her attention to the residue on the mouse mat and taking it in one hand blew the remnants into a bin by her side. "...Then went downtown to Senses and J.T. was there and she's like-..." "Wait, J.T. with the squint or J.T. from uptown?" "Duh, uptown J.T., as if some slack cock-eyed cow is gonna get into Senses. So anyway J.T. tells me that, get this, Josh and Cameron were there." "Ooh, they're back in town??" The squawking continued as the two girls waltzed through the double glass doors of Hi Gorgeous Inc. and swanned past Gloria, nodding politely and flashing their pearly white smiles while she half-heartedly nodded and returned to typing on her desktop. It was as they manoeuvred around her desk past the partition of frosted glass into the salon itself that she first noticed that smile. Both were smiling in conversation, the blonde and the redhead, and while the blonde girl laughed heartily and ran her fingers through her long locks something about the redhead's smile struck her as odd. Donna Lynn Jamieson was her name, and in her circle she was the sexiest girl, the dominant one – the one with the biggest mouth. Her vibrant, swirling locks of fire were as a beacon to all the party people on the scene. When the troop of cackling girls in their billowy dresses and curve-tight denim miniskirts stepped onto the street the flowing head of fiery hair let the regular talent know who was on their way to crash the party. Gloria listened intently, then as she heard Donna and her colleague greet their boss she began typing quietly on her keyboard, continuing her work but pressing the buttons with so little pressure as to be able to get work done and eavesdrop at the same time. Employer and employees exchanged pleasantries and a few moments later Donna was at her work station going over style options with her first client of the day. Sighing, Gloria returned to her work typing up appointment schedules and checking holiday requests for her fellow employees. Occasionally between clients and during cigarette breaks her boss would saunter through to reception and check on her, as he did once Donna and Bonnie, the blonde girl, went to work on their customers. "So, how are you liking work here Gloria?" He asked, casually leaning against the partition and watching her as she finished up a flyer for special discount colouring. "Hm?" She turned to find him standing over her with a comforting smile upon his face, betraying the overall cock-sure appearance of his rugged shirt, stonewashed denim and gelled spiky hair. "Sorry, didn't mean to startle you sweetheart. I just-..." "Oh, yeah...I like working here, yeah. I'm good at the job, there's a friendly environment...great wage!" She broke off, smirking at the wage comment. "Why do you ask, Larry?" Larry DeLancy was brash and bourgeoisie in the company of his boys, but if he so much as sensed the presence of a woman he slapped on the charm and sophistication like a cheap, pungent cologne and shot the "winning" smile at his potential conquests. Gloria noticed it almost immediately, simply from the greasy shine of his tan. "No reason. You've been with us for a good few months now so I was just wondering. Employee relations and all that, y'know?" Larry cocked his head, fluttering his eyebrows and leaning closer to Gloria. "Oh, right. Yeah I'm happy thanks. You run a fine business Larry." Gloria assured him, her emerald eyes glimmering as she tilted her head into the rich sunlight beaming in through the salon windows. "Well actually there is a reason I ask..." Larry began, ignoring the intended cut-off Gloria implied in her tone. "...how'd you like to cut and style like the girls?" Gloria swivelled on her chair full-body to Larry, eyes wide in disbelief, mouth ajar. "Seriously? You mean learn to cut and dry and dye and style and...and...everything?" She nearly squealed with excitement. Larry nodded and kicked at the partition, pushing himself forward and walking to the front door. Looking left to right down the street he clasped his hands behind his back and turned back to Gloria, the coy smile still upon his face. "Yeah, everything. You've got this aura about you that tells me you'd make a great stylist. So, if you want, we can start training you up. There's a college just down the road, Ebonii's, you can take up coursework and learn the trade professionally." "Wow, yeah! That'd be great. Do I have to pay? I mean, I can pay I just wonder if I need to is-..." "Stop." Larry raised a hand, his smile growing wider. "Yeah obviously you'll need to pay to learn but if you're up for it I'd like for you to work side by side with the girls and I. You're more than just a receptionist Gloria, I can tell you didn't just apply here to be a typist for a salon." "Larry! Larry you up front?? LARRY!" Came the cry from one of the hairdressers beyond the partition. Gloria glanced at the floor, her excited smile dissipating suddenly as Larry was summoned. Seeing the look on her face, Larry's own smile flopped into a frown and he watched her for a moment before checking behind the wall to see Donna waving frantically over at him. "Alright, I'll talk to you more about this at lunch, come to my office and we'll discuss it some more. For now though you're definitely interested?" Larry asked, rubbing her shoulder. "Yeah, yeah definitely. OK, I'll come see you at lunchtime!" Gloria said, looking up at Larry, a disheartened look in her eyes. Or was it still the excitement washing over her like the pure sunlight? Larry couldn't tell, but with a wink he made his way back onto the salon floor and, hands on hips, listened with little interest to what Donna had to say. "You know Josh and Cameron, don't you?" She heard coming from the floor, and glancing back saw Donna holding a pair of straighteners up in one hand and fingering her bold locks with the other. Gloria didn't need to eavesdrop on the conversation, even if compulsion drove her to anyway, she had worked at the salon long enough to know what Donna was talking about when staff, and occasionally clients, got sucked into the big black hole that was her life. Pulling, pulling them in and thrusting every word, notion, thought and feeling onto them. She held that smile as her big green eyes studied Larry's face, Gloria never noticed before but only her and Donna shared the same eye colour, everyone else was common blue. Larry's were brown. Donna pranced from one foot to the other and looked up and away from Larry, holding her head at an angle that highlighted the line of her neck and jaw and made her eyes appear much larger than normal, all while gibbering on about her weekend. Gloria returned to her desk and tucked herself in, typing away and thinking about Larry's offer, while Donna's voice resonated through the salon over the hustle and bustle of hair-dryers and clients talking to the other girls, as it did most days. The salon was full of characters, and Donna's was the loudest, just as her fiery red head was the brightest amongst the blondes and brunettes. She spent most of the morning chatting to anyone that would listen, regaling them with the tale of her weekend. Her colleagues all giggled off her boasts and let her have her moment as the centre of attention before some other piece of gossip became the word around town. While the girls like Bonnie and Claire all lavished jealousy and awe and praise on Donna for her exploits, Gloria could only shudder when she learned fully what the redhead had been up to at the weekend with Josh and Cameron. They had shared her. Bless her heart, all she probably wanted was some love and attention. Gloria knew as much but just as obvious was the complexity of her mind where all the disjointed and incoherent pieces of her personality came together like a bag full of coat hangers that could never be separated, all to form the character that was now flirting with another client. He was robust and middle-aged, judging by the flashes of grey hair streaking his sideburns and over his ears. Donna flirted, either unaware or unashamed of the gold band on his left hand. Gloria worked away tirelessly, waiting for lunch time and began thinking about Angelus. Every so often she sneakily logged onto the net and surfed the fan forums of PRIME, reading up on his Hessian character and smiling in awe of the legacy he had left in his wake. Every so often she checked the clock on her desktop, willing the hands to twelve o' clock. ---- A rolling bass line roused Gloria from a daze. She recognized it immediately as the intro to "Chelsea Dagger" by The Fratellis. Her ringtone. Scrambling through her bag for the phone she honed in on the vibrations and grabbed it from beneath her purse and checked the face. Angelus The corners of her mouth turned up into the beginnings of a smile. She glanced at the time on the computer. 11:58am. Quickly flipping her cell open she pressed it to her ear and let the gentle, caring greeting escape her soft lips. At the other end of the line every syllable caressed his ear and Von Kelsig's scarred face tightened as he grinned from ear to ear. "Heya darling. How are you?" He purred. "I'm great baby, how's Chicago?" Gloria replied, turning in her chair to find Larry seeing off another client before turning to her and gesturing at the watch he wasn't wearing on his wrist. Two minutes, she gestured, holding up a hand before peeling away a sliver of hair from her forehead and listening again to Von Kelsig. "Chicago's...windy, I guess. Did you see? I'm in the Main Event this week." He proclaimed, filling with pride. "Oh, you're not wrestling this week are you?" Gloria forced the worry from her as soon as possible, keeping Larry in her line of sight while doing so. "Baby you were all torn up after your last match you can't be booked again already, surely?" The worry was genuine, but a desperate little voice in the back of her head nagged at her to go see Larry and do something for herself. "Glory I'm the only draw they've got left after that pay-per-view! I got off lightly compared to some of the guys. Only other man worth mentioning on the card is Rezz. Oh baby wait 'til you meet him, I've known him for years. Actually headed his class back in the Primetime Wrestling Academy, you'll love him trust me..." Larry signalled that he was headed for his office, and Gloria gestured to the cell and rolled her eyes. Larry chuckled and waved then headed to his office for lunch while Gloria let a sigh filter down through her nostrils. "What's the matter Gloria?" Von Kelsig asked, aware that the great gust of wind which just blew down the line was a sigh and not adverse weather interference. "Oh nothing. My boss asked me if I wanted to learn how to be a hairdresser. I'm supposed to be meeting him just now..." She trailed off and Von Kelsig knew he was taking up valuable promotion time on idle chit chat. "Really? Great, I'm proud of you sweetheart! Don't let me hold you back, I can call back later, go talk to your boss." "Yeah? Aw you're sweet. OK, I'll phone you later, say around three?" "Any time is fine, I'm training all day today so I'm not busy persay." Von Kelsig muttered, slightly upset that someone else warranted Gloria's time over him. "Alright, I'll talk to you later." Gloria concluded. "Oh, Angelus?" "Hm?" Von Kelsig suddenly perked up. "Don't call me Percy." She blew a kiss down the phone and giggled, hanging up and leaving Von Kelsig speechless on the other end of the line. A faint breath resembling a chuckle escaped his throat and he flipped his cell shut, popping it back in his pocket and getting up from what should have been a weight bench. She believed he was training, but he was nowhere near the gym. He wasn't even in Chicago. Von Kelsig was in Las Vegas. In front of him stood a lanky figure with a menacing face, covered head to toe in scales. In his hand a steel dagger, in his eye a look of madness. ---- "What do you think?" Asked a raspy voice from the shadowy corner of his eye. The scaly man drew back his dagger and thrust it into his abdomen, stabbing himself straight through the navel. Von Kelsig winced as the man let out a horrific shriek, eyes wide in terror and mouth gaping exposing several unsightly blunt yellow teeth. His body of scales rippled around the stab wound, and yet no blood poured from the umbilicus. "Man where the hell did you dig this guy up?" Von Kelsig asked, in awe at the strange creature's talent. "Oh I never dug him up. I poached him from the deepest, darkest recesses where men fear to tread." Replied his accomplice. "The Strip?" "Yeah...pretty much. It was him or this Vietnamese pensioner who could striptease and wrap her milk duds around her neck." "You made the right choice Rezz." Von Kelsig cut in as soon as Nerezza quietened. The scaly monster man pulled the dagger from his belly and licked the blade, eyebrows fluttering cockily. "So what's the trick? Retractable blade?" He continued, gazing at both the navel and the blade. "Hardly. Go on, show him." Rezz replied, ordering the freak. The scaly freak obliged and placed his thumb and index finger inside his belly button, prising the skin apart to reveal a hole an inch in diameter and a few more deep. The skin surrounding the innards of the hole was scarred solid, and very thick. Von Kelsig made out ridges of nicked flesh courtesy of the dagger probing around inside. "So what's the trick already?" Von Kelsig demanded to know, though his monotonous tone suggested he was dumbfounded by the disgusting wound, rather than anxious to know its secret. "It's right there! It's scar tissue from an old injury. You think that's something, you should see the rest of his act. He's a definite for the bill." Rezz said, swelling with pride. "...And all the acts are like this? One great bizarre bazaar?" "Yeah. Vaudeville with a twist. Gaz and I talked about something like this for a while, but now there's an actual chance to make it a reality." Rezz stared at the floor around the scaly man's gnarled feet, like clubs with tendons and skin stretched over them. "Can't disagree there. You know I'd jump on this in a heartbeat, it's just..." "Just......what? Come on I was there for you when you needed investment for the Slaughterhouse. Or Underworld. Whatever you decided to call that place. Christ, you even squeezed Violence Jack for a few pennies! What could possibly be stopping you from getting in on this?" Rezz asked, puzzlement forming in the taut wrinkles stretching across his forehead and brow. Von Kelsig thought about the Slaughterhouse, and the subsequent episode with Blanco. No harm came to his investors though, and in that he sought solace. "Well...ah hell I'm in. Whatever you need man, name it. On one condition." "Name it." Rezz spat eagerly, his top lip hitching as he grinned. "I don't want my name involved with this in any way, shape or form. I'll contribute where I can, do whatever is needed behind the scenes but on the face of it it's all you. I don't even want my name to go any further than the ink on the cheque. No questions asked. On that condition you got yourself a partner, partner." "Done." They shook hands and Rezz, to his credit, said and thought nothing more of Von Kelsig's condition. Together with the masochistic lizard man they left the empty stage, walking across the dusty desolate floor once filled with tables and chairs which seated the vultures hungry for food and culture. They reached the front doors of the old abandoned theatre and walked out onto the streets of Vegas, breathing in the stuffy air of noon. "How's the wife?" Rezz drawled, breaking the short silence after they bid farewell to the lizard man. "Ha, wife. That's a good one." Von Kelsig replied, his barrel chest heaving delightfully. "Yeah, she's great. She's on the verge of a promotion at work." "Nice. How you finding life with a ball and chain anyway, Hessian?" Rezz enquired further, adding a little dig to the ribs for good measure. "It's...different. Good different. You know you spend so much time in a business like ours playing out fantasy like it's real life that you forget what a real life actually feels like." Von Kelsig replied, scuffing his heels against the macadam. "That good, huh?" "Oh no. It's one of the toughest things I've ever been through, and I've been through some tough shit. Hell, look at my face. I defy anybody that says what I do for a living is tougher than trying to lead a normal life. If I had a choice I'd take the bone crunching, skin splitting spots any day." Von Kelsig grinned and scratched an itch emanating from the middle of a long fresh scar running down his nose. "What makes you think you don't have a choice?" Rezz asked. "The heart wants what the heart wants man." Rezz stopped dead in the road. Von Kelsig walked on a few paces ahead and slowed to a halt, chuckling under his breath and turning to read the look of disgust on his partner's face. "...What the hell did you just say? Where is Hessian and what have you done with him?!" "No wisecracks. I might be in love but that doesn't mean I won't still wreck you." "In the words of R. Lee Ermey, ya been PUSSIFIED son!" Rezz picked up his pace again and together they walked off down the street, Von Kelsig suffering a barrage of insults at the hands of Nerezza and laughing off every jibe. ---- It was 12:40pm when Gloria left Larry's office, beaming ear to ear at the prospect of being a professional stylist. For the rest of the day she put her head down and fired through her workload, managing to knock out a couple of documents due Tuesday. At 3 o' clock she popped outside the building for a cigarette break, taking with her her cell phone and calling Von Kelsig. "Hello?" Von Kelsig answered. In the background Rezz pranced on his tip-toes, holding the bottom of his coat out like a skirt and making tittering noises. "Hey Angie!" Gloria yelled into the phone. "Hi baby, how'd it go?" Von Kelsig asked, confident of a positive outlook. "Oh it went great! I'm starting next week! Just basics, mostly theory but I get to watch the hairdressers at work and get first hand experience!" She replied, her cigarette shaking in her excited grasp. "That's great Glory, I'm real proud of you. How are you going to fit that in to your desk work?" "Oh, my boss said he'd take care of everything, he thinks I've got potential! No one's ever told me I had potential! Angie I'm so happy..." Gloria broke off to take a much needed breath, before taking a long draw of her cigarette. "Aw, I wish I was there to celebrate with you. I'll come home Thursday and we can do something nice, how's that sound?" "That'd be great baby." Gloria sighed gleefully. "It's a date. Hey, I gotta go baby I'm on spot duty at the barbells. I'll talk to you later, 'kay?" "OK, talk to you later Angie." They bid each other goodbye and Gloria hung up, taking a final drag of her cigarette before flicking the butt onto the ground and stamping it out. As she turned around to head back into the salon she came face to face with a dazzling redhead, and jumped. "Well hello dolly!" Donna squealed, tapping Gloria on the shoulder and showing off her pearly whites. "Hi Donna." Gloria replied half-heartedly. "Hey well done on the promotion, Larry made a good decision bumping you up to the floor." "Oh, thanks. That's kind of you to say." Gloria smiled politely. A momentary silence passed before Donna spoke up. "So was that your man on the phone?" Unashamedly, Donna pressed on. "Um, yeah." "Ooh, you'll have to introduce me sometime." "Well...you might know him. He's a wrestler, works for a company called PRIME. Wrestles on television under the name Hessian." Gloria proudly exclaimed to Donna. "Ohhhh, you're datin' a T.V. Star?? You go girrrl!" Donna replied with another tap on the shoulder, this time patting a little harder and flashing a brighter smile. "Yeah, he's lovely. I'll introduce everyone soon, we've still got some work to do on our home but we'll be having a big house-warming soon so you're more than welcome to come along!" "That would be swell, honey. Count me in, I love a party!" Donna chortled, her laugh verging on obnoxious. Gloria shrugged her shoulders and gnashed her teeth, grinning heartily. Seeing Larry looming around the partition she quickly walked back into the salon. "Back to the grind!" She chuckled, waving to Donna as she trotted back to her desk. Donna stood on the front step of the salon, sucking on a cigarette and moaning under her breath as she watched Gloria work away, while Larry approached her and they chatted once more. Her lips were still frozen in a smile that no one noticed, and her bright eyes blinked rapidly as thoughts ran through her mind. Yes, I do love a party...
