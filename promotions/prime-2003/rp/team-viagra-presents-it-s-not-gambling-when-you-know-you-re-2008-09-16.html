Team VIAGRA Presents : 'It's not gambling when you know you're going to win.' - Barney(HIMYM)

16 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Sept 16th, 2008 Team VIAGRA has left quite a legacy over the years, wrestling not only across the United States, but the entire World at large. From Istanbul to Australia to Egypt, there’s nary a place that the proprietors of Snow and Nads have not visited. Wrestling in tandem alone for almost a decade now, there are very few achievements the tandem of VIAGRA feels they have left to accomplish. This is the primary reason they spend the majority of their days in a lacksidasical haze. Jack Harmen, better known to the professional wrestling world as High Flyer, sprawls himself on the nearest couch, headphones on, blasting Jimmy Hendrix directly into his cranium. He wears a too loosely fitted Brian Dawkins jersey and jogging pants. Tony Davis sits, dressed oddly enough in an undone business suit, Indian style, playing his Nintendo DS. Until a woman's heel comes crashing down, shattering his DS on the ground below it. Tony groans. Tony Davis: Y'know, when we get our contract extension in November, I want a clause giving me a new DS every show. Cause this is just getting ridiculous. Tony sighs, and reaches behind him. Seemingly out of nowhere (perhaps hammerspace?) he pulls out a fresh and clean Nintendo DS. The familiar "Buh-duh!" ding sound rings out, and Tony's smile returns. Only for the girl in heels to smash it once more. Tony Davis: Hey! I'm starting think that was no accident! It was no accident, as Mary-Lynn Mayweather crosses her arms in an angry glower. She's dressed nicer than Davis is at this moment, but not nicer than usual. She wears a deep maroon red pants suit and holds a clipboard in her arms. She bites the tip of her pen. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Guys, I'd like to talk to you about Cataclysm. Tony Davis: You want to talk to us about Joey Malone's old finisher? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: ... Who's Joey Malone? Listen, we're representing PRIME, but you guys are also representing me and my still throbbing skull. Is... he listening? Mary-Lynn stops mid-sentence to look over to a dazedly dreaming Lunatic, who seems to strangely have a smile adorned across his visage. In a huff, Mary-Lynn walks over and tears Flyer's ear buds out. Flyer doesn't move, but opens his eyes to glare at his protégé. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: I wanted to talk strategy, for the Strength in Numbers champions. High Flyer: Yeah, well, I wanted to rock out. We can't always get what we want. Hmm... kinda wanna listen to the Stones now. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Tony Davis doesn't even remember what he had for breakfast this morning! How he could ever think to remember I was struck in my cranium defending this place, I have no idea. But you Jack. I thought you were full of fiery vengeance! I'd thought you would be the one ready and prepared for the inevitable payback check of violence that needs to be deposited on SCCW's front door step. So, what I'm asking here is, What I'm SCREAMING HERE IS, WHAT ARE YOUR PLANS TO MAKE THOSE FUCKS HEADS GO BOOM!? Flyer remains motionless. After a moment, he yawns. High Flyer: Remain still. You called Pete, yes? You gave him the four hundred grand I gave you, yes? Mary-Lynn stops, jaw agape for a moment. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: *quickly, flustered* There was four hundred grand in that envelope? High Flyer: Then we're set. Flyer grabs his buds and places them in his ear. He leans back, lying down on his leather couch, as he quietly closes his eyes. Mary-Lynn stares completely dumbfounded. She turns to Davis, who is looking at his reflection in his tag team championship strap, and back to Flyer, who lazily lies, blasting "Time" by Pink Floyd so loud that she could hear it softly play beneath the tension. Mary-Lynn quickly storms over and rips off Flyer's ear buds again. The Lunatic growls as he sits up, rubbing the darkness out of his eyes. He reaches down to his side and presses a remote control, whereby the music leaves the ear buds and softly plays in the background of his docile home. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: What do you MEAN we're set. Phillip Kennedy. Lovecraft. And that Katsidy girl. You know she only flirted with you in that bit to draw you off guard. You're never gonna snatch her snatch. I mean, do you know ANYTHING about ANY of them?! High Flyer: Don't worry! Jeez. Stop freakin' out. Flyer sighs and pulls out a smoke. Quickly, he strikes a match and shakes it clean. Inhale. Exhale. High Flyer: Listen. We're not planning on winning this match Mary. That left the table eons ago. Mary-Lynn's eyes narrow. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: WHAT?! YOU FUCKS! She smacks Flyer in his arm, and then smacks Tony in his forehead. Tony winces in pain. Tony Davis: What I do?! Mary-Lynn Mayweather: What do you mean you aren't gonna win the match?! AFTER EVERYTHING I'VE DONE FOR YOU!!! Mary smacks Flyer repeatedly in his arm. Three, four times, easy, as Flyer tries to restrain her. High Flyer: Mary! Calm down. MARY! Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Are you gonna throw the match, IS THAT IT?! Mary-Lynn Mayweather rushes over to the corner. She smashes a vase. High Flyer: JEEZ, THAT WAS CRAIG'S! Mary, stop it. Don't- Mary-Lynn has grabbed Flyer's PRIME tag team championship and throws it through the living room window of Craig Maloof's house. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: I figured I'd throw the tag team titles right where your honor went. Flyer stands and towers over Mary-Lynn. He narrows his eyes at his apprentice. Tony Davis stares ahead, as usual, dumbfoundedly. Tony Davis: Why did you throw his belt out the window? Mary-Lynn, in a huff, reaches over and grabs Tony's belt. They wrestle for a moment or two, but Davis, sitting, is no match for a fiery and passionate red head. She rips the belt out of his hand, and chucks it out the same now broken window. Tony Davis: Awh, dude. The sprinklers are on! Tony rushes out, leaping through the broken window and smacking his head on the window's pane. He lies in a heap, cradling his back over the windows ledge, unconscious. Back to Mary and Flyer, who completely disregard Tony's current condition. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: I'm done. High Flyer: What? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: After Cataclysm. You throw this match, and I'll go back to Brand. I'll wrestle for the Texas faithful and I'll get over my own way. I can't be a part of this. High Flyer: We've done worse in the past! Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Yeah, well, I believe in this conflict. Those pricks practically gave me a concussion. High Flyer: Listen, this'll be the best for everyone. My assets have been frozen due to this overextending divorce proceeding. I can't live with Craig forever. He throws towels everywhere. He walks around naked. I just can't deal. Y'know? And I know you're med school debts are rising. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Law school. High Flyer: Same thing. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: No it's not. One can prevent you from being charged with murder! The other can stop you from dying from cancer. Flyer snickers, and butts his cigarette into an ash tray. High Flyer: We're the favorites, you hear? Kennedy and Lovecraft haven't ever really TEAMED together. They're paper champions! They're peons! Right now, the odds are 5 to 1! FIVE TO ONE?! Do you know how many Nintendo DS's we can get with five to one odds? Mary-Lynn Mayweather deeply inhales. High Flyer: I can help pay off your debts. We can each have our own place for privacy sake. Perhaps get off of each other's nerves so much. This is a good long term decision Mary. It's going to help us all out greatly. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: How did you get 400,000 if your assets are frozen? High Flyer: Well... it's everything I've made this year, just about. Through nbW, PRIME, NFW... my contract with CSWA where I get paid even if shit doesn't happen... my team events. Some merchandising. I mean, if we blow this and win? I'm going to be living on the salary of a transcriber for the Dr. Phil show, y'hear? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: You're gonna leverage your whole future on a dishonest and illegal tactic within the confines of professional competition? Between two competing federations, fighting for honor? Beyond all that bullshit, you're gonna make my pain and suffering meaningless?! High Flyer: Mary. You suffered because you needed to learn, that you can NEVER, trust ANYONE. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: What about you? High Flyer: Ha. Especially me. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Well, I can trust Troy. Flyer laughs. Mary-Lynn shoots him a look. High Flyer: Oh, I'm sorry. You were serious. Okay. Final lesson if you're planning on leaving. Lindsay Troy used you. You don't even know it. You were bait young padawan. Sent out there to gauge the strength of our combatants. I'm surprised you didn't get crucified with how much of a Jesus sacrifice you had made out there. You weren't ready! Troy knew that. But she talked to you into it. She knew you had the fire, the passion, but she knew you weren't ready. We all knew it. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: You've COMPLETELY doubted me ever since I've become your student. Well Jack, I can't be your student forever you know. You yourself say you've taught me all I can learn from you and yet you keep giving me final lessons. You constantly bring me DOWN in this life Jack. You're barely a human being anymore, you know that? Flyer is visibly shook at this. Mayweather continues on unhindered. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: You're this hair shy of an out and out criminal. A violent, sociopathic, money grubbing, psychopath! The only reason you haven't fallen so far is because you think life has value. That honor and valor exist throughout the age?! You've said this to me! YOU! I mean, What happens when you lose your last shred of humanity, huh Jack? You've given every other shred away to get ahead in this business, isn't that what you've told me?! What happens when you give up your honor!? High Flyer: This whole feud is BOGUS Mary. You hear me, BOGUS. It's glitz and glamour, and it's an excuse for Lindsay to bring her problems to EVERYONE, so they're easier for HER to solve. If you can't see how we're all being used in that scenario, then you need to go back to med school. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Law sch- High Flyer: I KNOW WHERE YOU WENT TO SCHOOL MARY. Flyer breathes heavily. Mary-Lynn lowers her head. High Flyer: Just be careful Ms. Gullible Mayfly. The world out there is much more dangerous than you're currently seeing it. Mary-Lynn walks to the front door softly. She swings it open, and turns back to Jack, who has his hands on his hips. Mary-Lynn Mayweather: Jack? High Flyer: Yeah? Mary-Lynn Mayweather: It doesn't matter. If you throw this match. I will no longer represent you. Just... know that. And without another word, Mary-Lynn Mayweather exits Craig Maloof's home. High Flyer remains behind, and lowers his head in frustration. He sighed, realizing Tony Davis still remains propped in a precarious position, hung over the window sill. Walking over, he begins to drag Davis back into Craig's home. When Dusk appears behind him, dripping wet, and completely naked. Of course, we only see him from the waist up. He turns sternly to Flyer. Dusk: Tony owes me a new window. And with a yank, Tony is drug back inside, and lands in a flop on the living room floor.
