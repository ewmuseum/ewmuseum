Slice of Life #1

23 May 2005

Date: 5.23.05 Slice of Life...As Told by Needles Scharf It began 12 years ago. The young naive soul of Vangelus Dante' Olsig took a nasty turn for the better. For that was the day in which I was born. I, Needles Scharf...the alter ego of one Vangelus Olsig. The demon in which the man whom you all label 'Infamous' Dani Furher has long since feared. I am his deceptive side, and on this May 23rd day my legacy arose. I remember it much like a Mother remembers the birth of her first son. It was an evening filled with all of the elements in which make up this devious world in which we call home. Money, Sex, Lies, Death, Deceit. All of it was there plus more. I guess you can say they are the keys that made this evening so interesting so to speak. Young Olsig was but a teenager when these occurences were brought forth. Born in Bogota, Columbia, he was the son of Czar Olsig...a man in which I'll go to the grave despising with my every limb. I guess you can say, though, that he played a mighty important role in my existence. Czar Olsig was leader of the land named Tahj at the time. He looked to pass on his dynasty unto his son amongst proper age. I was sent to make sure that the son Czar sought to pass his worn and battered dynasty unto was not his son at all. My mission was to claim Sir Olsig as my own, a mission you can say I went far and beyond accomplshing...but I'll let you be the judge of that. It was nothing more than a normal day. The birthday of Olsig had just passed the previous wednesday and the family were still celebrating. Olsig was an only child, so he was highly favored on gift bearing days such as. This was a day, though, that Vangelus could only image would turn out for the worst. Family Portraits day. But that was not the chilling fact that disturbed Vangelus. Painting the portrait of he and his family was a member of the Myrant Revolution...a group of Nazi's gathered inside of Bogota who despised the very mention of Czar Olsig. There Vangelus sat, on the stool, sporting that look a child gives you when he is beyond petrified. Sure the arguement could be made that Vangelus was but a child...but still inside of that tiny mind he understood the Myrant's purpose. He understood it...well. Czar wiped the sweat from the head of his son telling him not to worry and that he knew what he was doing. Ha! Yea right. Czar didn't realize that by placing his oh so adored family in this perdicument, he was practically opening up the gatweways for my arrival. Thank you Czar Olsig...Thank you for being a fool. I hope you can hear me in Hell! Please excuse my unprofessionalism. As I was previously stating, Czar was putting on an act for his son...because deep in his forest green eyes I could see the fear even he installed. The Myrant photographer positioned their bodies for the pose. He placed a hand on the shoulder of Vangelus attempting to place the stubborn child in proper positioning. 'Don't Touch...' Czar stated as the Myrant gave him a devious look backing off of the child. For some awkward reason, the three were placed back-to-back...a rather unsual pose. Obviously this was not going to be intended for good. The Myrant went to the curtain in the room and quickly snatched them back revealing a trio of Myrant soldiers all holding rifles and hunting knives. As expected, the pussy Czar and his family scurried around the room in utter fear, hoping to somehow save their worthless lives with this act of ignorance. The gunshots rang throughout the room accompanied by the sound of shattering glass, constant thumps, and scream of mercy, turmoil, and most of all...fright. Czar covered the body of his only son with his body only to have it invaded with various bullets from the Myrants. Thats what happens when a fool risks his life for another fool. Czar's attempts at saving his family sadly fell in vain. Moment of sarcasm there for the uneducated of mankind. Poor young Olsig had no other option than to watch his dear and loving father crumble to the ground in a bloody heap, much like the hearts of Tahj upon hearing that their faithful leader had been snatched away from them by the enemy. If you ask me, plans of celebration would have been more fitting. Olsig's heart crashed with tears as he knelt down in a pool of blood over his dead father. Olsig always said he wanted a pool...he got that in honorable fashion on this day. Vangelus's wish was fulfilled for the bettering of mankind...in my humble opinion of course. Olsig held his father's blood soaked head in his arms as he cried mercifully. Oh, the screams of terror...they were like music to my barren ears. Vangelus didn't have much time to sob and weep, though, as he suddenly felt one of the silver bullets pierce him in the back of the neck. Vangelus immediately fell over his father's deceased body with a deceased body of his own...or so I wished...er I mean thought. Vangelus was not dead, just passed out. That left his mother who died a quick and horrid death. She ran from the Myrants but fell right into a trap when she reached the end of the hall. All that was left was a closet. She ran into the closet and held the knob hoping that her strength could match that of the Myrant's. Common Sense ought to tell you who won the battle here. The Myrants yanked the door open...nearly off of its hinges. They held a gun up to her but one...but one had decided on an even better idea. He stepped into the closet and closed the door. Lets just say things got a little graphic from here. 'Make sure the runt is dead...' screamed one of the Myrants as they reached down to pick up Vangelus. The touch awoke him as he instantly realized where he was. He sent a knee into the crown jewels of the Myrant and began to run out of the front door. He was a young cat so outrunning these older veterans seemed like nothing. He ran and ran unto his fatigure began to set in. Thats where I stepped in. He ran right into me in my human form. Vangelus screamed that wonderful cry again. 'Come with me...Lord Olsig' I said with the latter acting as my sense of humor, whatever that might account for. I wrapped my two massive hands around the scrawny neck of Vangelus and began to choke the ever loving life out of him. My motivation, you ask? I just pictured Vangelus as his father. Simple enough from there. Vangelus was out of it once again. He awoke inside of the fiery pits of what some describe as hell. Actaully this was my home. Young Vangelus pushed his way to his feet holding his head. He looked all around him to see the acts of slaying and murder occuring all around him. 'Where am I?' He asked...but there was no one to answer him. The men at war surely weren't about to halt to answer this kids petty questions. 'Dad!? Mom!?' He screamed, thinking that his parents would somehow answer him. Yep...he was sure enough Vangelus's son...I could tell by the ignorance. 'Dad!?!' The young Vangelus screamed out once again...but this time he was met with a reply. 'You will not find you Father here.' I stated as he shifted around to gaze into my fiery eyes. I was decked out in my black robe with the red designs...my favorite. This was not a matter of wearing my favorite Butchering gear, though, this was a matter of exacting the redemption in which I so rightfully deserved. 'Who are you...and where is my father.' spoke young Olsig as his eyes grew bigger the closer I came to him. I smiled revealing my blood stained fangs. 'Your father will not be found here young Olsig. As a matter of fact...your father will not be found here or anywhere else. Your father is dead.' The tears immediately began to build up in his eyes. I wanted to burst in laughter, but I held back for the time being knowing that the result of this would bring forth a bounty of laughter. 'W-Who are you...and H-How do you know my name?' Olsig stated as he lifted his head momentarily from his weeping state. 'Vangelus...worry not over these things...for I am your salvation to the madness in which has invaded your life.' 'Where am I?' 'You are in...Heaven.' I said as I smiled once again revealing my humorous side. 'This isn't heaven...My mother always told me that heaven was painted with gold. This is nothing more than fire and bloodshed.' 'Your mother was an ignorant woman, Vangelus. You are here for a purpose.' 'And that would be?' 'Sharp tongue to be so young. Vangelus...Your father, Czar Olsig, was an evil man.' 'No he was not...he wa-' 'Hush hush Vangelus. Your father did a terrible thing to me. He killed my family, much like I did yours, all because he saw us as some kind of 'Threat'.' 'What does this have to do with me?' 'You see Vangelus...I vowed to MY father to avenge his death...and now I have the chance to do so. I killed your father...but that is not enough. I plan on avenging my father in the most horrific manner possible.' 'How is that?' '...Through you' I smiled revealing my blood stained fangs once again and placed my hand over the head of Vangelus entering the first step into what would be my mighty return to the motherland to avenge the death of my long loss father. I would do it...though Czar's only son, and I would succeed. The rest of the story you ask? Well its simple and will be explained in detail at a later date. For now just note that the Dani Furher that you all so graciously worship inside of the ring...is not Vangelus Dante Olsig at all. It is his alter ego...it is me. And this coming Wednesday night... I plan on unleashing the wrath within. ...Oh yea. The End.
