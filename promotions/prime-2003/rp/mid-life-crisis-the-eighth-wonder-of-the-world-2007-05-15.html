Mid Life Crisis - 'The Eighth Wonder of the World'

15 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 15/05/07 ‘Is this really necessary?’ We’re in PRIME headquarters and Price has told me the first stage of the plan. It’s not exactly what I had in mind at all largely because of the ridiculous looming backdrop. Picture the minarets and tops of buildings that would only have been fit to work inside the realms of an Arabian nights style. The sun looks like it’s beating down on the parched sand which is lettered liberally across the concrete. In actuality it’s just several high powered lights mashed together but it gives off the effect of the red hot sun of the east. Several people were walking by around me, dressed in turbans and styles of dress that would commonly be found back a couple of hundred years ago. At least, that’s what they were telling me. Off to one side was Dave and Barney, happily enjoying the refreshing amount of women that were populating the studio right now. Despite the fact that the style of dress was supposedly conservative for the time period, it didn’t apply whatsoever to the women. Strapped bikinis and negligee tops were dominating. Not that it was a problem for Barney or Dave. They were loving it. For Barney it was like heaven: spare ribs and women, nothing could be greater. Dave appreciated them too. I guess he wasn’t as gay as I had thought. No time for me to enjoy the entire parade of hot women in the background though. Nope, I was busy in costume, sorting out what I was going to wear for this piece. ‘No, seriously, is this really necessary?’ I repeated the phrase to the stylist who had been charged for this particular assignment. Dante, my personal image consultant had already been abruptly dropped from his job, Dave citing “stylistic” differences between himself and Dante. Barney whispered to me at the time that Dante had tried it on with Dave but none of that could really be proven. Judging by this kind of business, it was probably true. This stylist however seemed to suit Dave an awful lot better. Cynthia Dalton was a little bit more reserved, not nearly as flamboyant as Dante had been and was semi-attractive to the three of us who were typically in contact with her now. I say semi-attractive because it really depended on how sluttily she dressed. Barney thinks the less she wears the hotter she is, slave to the big breasts that he is, but myself and Dave both prefer the classy, subtly sexy look. I’ll leave that to the Freud’s of the world to figure out how that attaches to my psychological profile. She had puffy brown hair that hung around her shoulders, highlighted by her pretty green eyes. She was maybe five foot five or so and had a slim build. Her breasts were somewhat distorting the frame. Honestly I hadn’t a clue if they were real or not (I never was good at guessing), but she was passable as a head turner. Age again was a bit mystical to me but I reckoned she was mid to late twenties. ‘Yes, apparently Mr. Price was adamant that you have this…this piece of crap on you.’ She said it with spite and malice. She never once called Dave by his first name, owing to the way in which he dealt with her. It was like 60’s Ireland where the wife would constantly be downtrodden and speak only when spoken to. Dave liked power too much for his own taste. It’d eventually catch him up. ‘On the bright side, your package looks pretty impressive.’ She shrugged, smirking at the comment. ‘Thanks I guess.’ ‘No problem.’ ‘Let me talk to him about it then. Because I don’t want to wear it either.’ She smiled slightly and looked amidst the rest of the clothes, looking for something a little more appropriate. I’ll bet you’re wondering what the hell I’m wearing now then yeah? A loincloth. That’s right, a loincloth. Because in the time period we’re dealing with, the real men wore nothing until they burnt themselves to death with cancer. Taking careful steps to make my way over to Barney and Dave (didn’t want anything slipping out) I knocked into a couple of girls who all giggled and looked at my groin with a little bit too much of a delight. Passing quickly I reached my two handler as it were, Barney scoffing his face full of rib while trying to keep eye contact with as many breasts and legs that he could muster. Dave meanwhile was talking to the director. ‘Jack, what’s up?’ ‘You’ve got lipstick on you Barney. But not the kind you want.’ His eyes had looked hopeful for a moment but then realised what I was on about. He grabbed a towel to attempt to clean the mess of barbeque sauce that covered his face. ‘Ah well, you know me Jack…can’t get enough of the stuff.’ ‘Your heart probably can though.’ ‘Touché.’ ‘Anyway, Barney, I don’t want to wear this. It’s looks silly, ridiculous and I’m pretty sure I’m liable to fall out of it if you know what I mean. I don’t think the majority of my audience appeal is going to be based on my package somehow so I need something a bit more…classy.’ Barney looked at me, chewing the fat of his rib over and over in his mouth like it was the precious nectar. He tilted his head a little bit more and raised an eyebrow. And then he said something I wasn’t expecting really. ‘You know…it does make your package look huge.’ ‘What?’ ‘What I said. Makes you look like a real man.’ I raised an eyebrow at this one. ‘And I’m not already huh Barney? I’m just a boy who happens to be six foot five and almost two hundred and ninety pounds? I’m just a freak of who still has to grow up is that it?’ I’d moved right up against him and he looked a bit annoyed for what he said. He was admonishing himself at that, even going so far as to taking the rib out of his mouth. H quickly made a retort. ‘Of course I didn’t mean it like that Jack. You know me, I’m just trying to figure out what’s best for you alright? You know me.’ ‘Well, strangely enough you’re not the only one who’s said my package was impressive.’ ‘What’s going on?’ Of course this was the perfect time for Dave to butt in. He sidled in between the two of us, being careful not to get any of my sweat or Barney’s barbeque on his designer suit. He cracked his neck and politely put his hand on my chest. ‘What’s the problem Jack?’ ‘You tell me. You really expect me to wear just this?’ Dave looked down and his eyes bulged slightly. Gulping a bit too loudly for my taste he turned to Barney instead of answering me directly. ‘Who told him to wear this?’ ‘You did Dave, it’s in the dossier.’ ‘No it’s not! I did not describe this to be a piece of loincloth that may as well just be some floss.’ ‘Well it wasn’t me Dave.’ ‘Cynthia! Why did you put him in this?’ Cynthia walked over daintily, avoiding the bikinis and looked at Dave with irritancy. ‘What Mr. Price?’ ‘Why did you put this…thing on him?’ He demandingly asked this of Cynthia and loomed over her with a look of pure disgust. Cynthia for her part tried to ignore him, instead deciding to just answer him straight and without any hint of malice. It was almost sickly sweet in tone. ‘I put it on him because it was in the dossier Mr. Price.’ Just to make sure it was over the top, she batted her eyes like a schoolgirl. In the background, Barney coughed horribly, shifting his weight from one side to the other. Meanwhile, Dave wasn’t buying her act. ‘Well, I’m pretty sure that I didn’t ask for this in the dossier. It doesn’t have any redeeming qualities…other than it makes his package look huge.’ Seriously what the fuck? ‘Seriously, what the fuck? Have you guys been drinking or something?’ They all looked at me funny, then in one motion all tilted their heads to the side. I just sighed and turned away. ‘I’m going to pick something else and you’re just going to have to deal with it.’ As I walked away back to the costumes, I could hear their responses. ‘Nice ass.’ ‘It is isn’t it Mr. Price?’ ‘Very…’ ‘Taut.’ ‘Excellent choice Barney, excellent.’ Shuddering is the only response I could give. *** About an hour or so later, with my dignity somehow still there, Cynthia had decided on something that made me look a bit more respectable. There was enough cloth to cover the important area while still being able to show off my physique for effect. I met with the director (think sleazy, oversized porn director; trying to make it in the real business) who was pretty sure on what he wanted for the whole thing. I didn’t have a clue what he was saying (waffle about shutter speeds and all that crap) but of course Dave knew exactly what he was talking about. ‘So, when exactly is this going to air?’ ‘This week on ReVolution.’ ‘Really?’ ‘Yeah, we’ve no time to waste Jack. We’ll have blowing up all over the place in no time.’ ‘Blowing up?’ ‘Get with the times Jack. Now, there’s no time to waste. Get on the camel.’ Oh boy, this was going to be weird.
