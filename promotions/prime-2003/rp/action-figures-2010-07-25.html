Action Figures.

25 July 2010

Date: July 25, 2010 Bright lights. Everywhere. Vangelus Olsig rebounds off of the ring ropes, aiming for a clothesline, but it’s ducked. His opponent catches him in return with a powerful clothesline of his own. Crowd. Chanting….something. The opponent begins to bury his boot deep into the abdomen of Olsig repeatedly. Each hurts worse than the other. Blood…it begins to spill from the Universal Champion’s mouth. Eyes dazed over. Head throbbing. Bright lights. Everywhere. But it’s all fading… Bright lights, but all he can see is darkness. Fading… Fade. . . . . . . . . . . . . . Crowd. Chanting…something. The chants awaken something inside of him. They remind him of 2004 when he began this journey. They remind him of 2005 when he let it slip through his hands. They remind him of 2008 when he said he was done for good. They remind him of 2010 when he conquered the Dual Halo. They remind him that he’s come too far to give up now. He springs back to life, met with a barrage of heavy fists courtesy his opponent. But this newfound determination acts as a shield for him. The blows aren’t as painful. The lights are bright again. He takes another punch, but ducks the next. He charges towards the rope, bouncing off and catching his opponent with a momentum-shifting flying clothesline. They both go down. Crowd. Chanting. Making little haste, Olsig mounts his opponent and begins to tear into him with closed fists of his own. It’s therapy. Finally, Olsig lets up, yanking his opponent back up to a vertical base. He places his opponents head in-between his legs and sets up for the maneuver which has won him so many battles. Fueled by a passion to succeed, Olsig lifts up his opponent and delivers a Spike Piledriver that shakes the arena at it’s very foundation. Spiked! Crowd. Chanting. The end is near. Olsig makes the cover. ONE! Bright lights. Not everywhere…just pinpointed dead in the center of the entrance ramp. Like a spotlight. A figure is being illuminated by the spotlight. The figure catches Olsig’s attention. TWO! Olsig’s mouth hangs open at the sight of a little boy standing there with a toy in his hand. Pale look. Glossy, little beady eyes. THR-… Olsig pulls himself off of his opponent, breaking the count. He walks towards the ring ropes, gazing intensely at the boy who matches him with an intense stare of his own. Crowd. Chanting. "What do you want?"" Olsig shouts. But the boy says nothing. Just gazes. Crowd. Chanting. "WHAT DO YOU WANT?" Olsig shouts, louder, strained voice buried underneath their chanting. Finally, Olsig darts out of the ring and takes off after the boy. As soon as he realizes this, the boy takes off running in a full sprint. Olsig continues to chase him, right through the entrance curtain. Cars. Honking. Olsig appears slightly bewildered as he finds himself not in the backstage area of the arena, but on a sidewalk with cars passing by. Olsig looks around and spots the boy standing on the other side of the street. Olsig takes off after him, dodging all of the cars and taxis alike. Once he sees Olsig coming, the boy takes off running again. Olsig continues after him but something seems to be weighing him down. Something…heavy. It’s slowing him down. He’s not running as fast. The boy is getting away. Suddenly, running is painful. He crashes to the ground. But he’s determined to get to the boy. And as he looks up, he sees the boy go inside of a large house that sits square in the middle of the street. The cars weave around the house, as if it’s placement there is perfectly normal. It’s raining over the house, but only over the house. The sun shines brightly everywhere else. Crawling, Olsig claws towards the house. His hands are bleeding, but he doesn’t know why. Whatever is on him is still heavy, and seems to be growing heavier. Finally, Olsig reaches the door of the house and pushes it open with all of the strength he can muster. He crawls inside. The first sight he sees is that of the boy playing with a host of wrestling action figures, his back turned to Olsig. A Karina Wolfeden. A Tchu. A Killean Sirrajin. A Brandon Younglood. …A Vangelus Olsig. All of them appear very beaten down, except the Olsig figure which looks to be brand new. The Wolfenden figure is missing an arm, the Tchu is missing a leg, the Sirrajin has crayon markings everywhere, and the Youngblood is bent all out of shape. But the boy seems to pay special attention to the Olsig one. Olsig begins trying to crawl over to the boy, but this weight on him has grown so the heavy to the point where he can’t even move. All he can do is shout. "Hey!" The boy continues to play as if he heard nothing. "LOOK AT ME!" The boy continues to play. "LOOK AT MEEEEEEEE!" Olsig screams, so loudly that the pitch of his voice shifts. Finally, the boy stands and slowly turns around. Olsig nearly chokes at the sight of a hole in the boy’s chest, exposing his heart which is being consumed by a barrage of worms. Olsig’s breaths grow shorter…and shorter…like he’s about to die. Fading again… "Vangelus…" the boy says in a voice far deeper than a child’s should be. "Vangelus…" "VANGELUS!" --- Olsig’s eyes pop open as he glances over at his agent, Chives. They are in the back of a limo. "You okay?" "Yeah, man…I’m fine." "Another bad dream?" "Where are we?" "Birmingham. Alabama. Autograph signing today…remember?" "Of course." "Vangelus, what’s on your mind? You’re having dreams, you’re acting all fidgety. Tell me something, man." "It’s nothing, Chives. Just nervous, that’s all." "About what?" "It’s been six years, Chives. Six years, I’ve fought to hold this belt. I honestly don’t know if I have what it takes…if PRIME even thinks I have what it takes…to hold on to it. I feel it slipping. My match with Castor, I made a lot of mistakes. My workouts have been off-balance. I think I’m getting too old for this and I’m headed for embarrassment." "No, Vangelus, it sounds more like you’re trying to find an excuse just in case you lose the belt to Chainz." "I don’t need an excuse, Chives. I’ve already proved my worth in this company. Hall of Fame, Dual Halo, Universal Champion. I have nothing left to prove. I’ve toppled many of the best this place has seen." "Then defeating Chainz should be no problem." "It doesn’t work like that, Chives. Back then I was younger. I was fresher. I had the drive that it takes to thrive. I’ve only had two matches since I’ve been back: The Halo and the Universal Title Match. I poured everything into both of those matches." "And now you feel you don’t have anything else left." "That’s what I’m afraid of. What if I do lose to Chainz? Do you know how big of a joke that’ll make me? I’ll be seen as a paper champion in it’s purest form." "You worry too much, Vangelus." "Not very helpful, Chives." "No, it is. You worried about the Halo, you worried about the Title match. And now what? Looking back on it, you realize that all of that worrying was in vain. It was useless. Vangelus, they didn’t hand you the Halo nor the Universal Belt. You WON them. You FOUGHT for them. All you have to do is go out there and do what you’ve been doing for years, because that belt you have around your waist testifies to the fact that NOBODY does it better. Helpful?" "We’ll see." Chives sighs. "This is the last time I represent one you emo people…" --- "Thanks. Nice to meet you." Olsig sits at an autograph table, shades on. He shakes the hand of a blonde lady who walks off. Up next comes a man, alongside a woman who appears to be his slightly annoyed wife. He is holding a replica Universal Title Belt. "Man, Vangelus Olsig! I’ve been a fan since your Dani Furher days!" "Those were some interesting days, huh?" "Definitely! You’re one of my absolute favorites, no lie!" "I appreciate that, man. What’s your name?" "Tony. Tony Kiser." "Alright, Tony. Here ya go. Pleasure meeting you." Olsig hands him the signed belt. "The pleasure is all mine." They walk off. Another pair of fans walk up. Two 20-something college guys. "How are you fellas?" Olsig asks. "Ah ma-…we’re gr-…great." One of them responds, obviously a bit nervous. "That’s great. What are your names?" "Oh, my-..my name is Rick. This is my fr-…my friend Pete." "It’s Bret, you idiot!" "Oh, right. Bret. Sorry." Rick lets out a nervous chuckle. "Alright, Rick and Bret. Got anything for me to sign?" "What about our shirts?" Olsig leans over and signs each plain white shirt individually. "There ya go. Pleasure to meet you." "Wow…." They both walk off. Next up comes a mother and a small child. Olsig’s eyes remain hidden by the shades, but his facial expression doesn’t fall under the disguise. He’s taken back by the appearance of the two, so much so that he takes off his shades and stares intensively at the mother…who stares intensively back. "Vangelus Olsig!" The little boy shouts. Olsig continues to stare at the mother, expressionless. She, the same. "Hey, do you remember that time you fought Nova and the Face-Eater guy at the same time? They were like glued together! That was so cool!" the boy raves. The staredown continues. "Hey, you think you can sign my Vangelus Olsig action figure?" Finally, Olsig snaps out of it and looks down at the boy. His eyes grow a foggy shade. "Ummm, sure little man. What’s yo-…your name?" The mother rolls her eyes as Olsig looks down, as if it pains him to look at the kid. "My name is Christian!" "Christian…right." Olsig signs the action figure and slides it to the boy, still trying to avoid eye contact. "SO COOL! Mommy look!" Olsig and the mother re-ignite their staredown. "That’s nice, dear" she responds, eyes yet glued to Olsig with disdain ever present in them. "Let’s go dear." Sharply, she and the boy turn to leave. "Buy, Mr. Olsig!" Olsig doesn’t respond, just smile faintly. With them out of sight, he pushes himself from his chair and walks over to Chives. "I’m done." "Done? Excuse me? We still have a whole line full of pe-…" "I’m DONE, Chives." And with that, Olsig walks off, leaving Chives bewildered, panicked, and agitated. --- It’s raining. Olsig stands outside of a home. He takes a deep breath. A very deep breath. Two of them, exactly. Mustering up the strength, he lifts up his hand and knocks on the door. Something feels heavy on him. A familiar weight. The door cracks open. It’s the mother. A look of shock is on her face as she gazes upon Olsig. "Vang-…what the he-…what are you doing here?" "Is he home?" "Yes…yes he is. Why is that of any concern to you?" "I-…I want to talk to him." "Talk to h-…are you serious?" "No-…I mean, yes. I don't know." "Well come back when you know." She says, motioning to close the door. "Wait! I-…I'm sure. I have to." Suddenly, a smile creeps unto her face. She looks at him, beaming. "I can't believe it. Hell must've frozen over. We are seeing a change in the almighty Vangelus Olsig. I'm blown. No really, I am." "I can do without your wise cracks, Lauren." "I'm sorry. But really, Vangelus. I am proud of you." "Where is he?" "Second room to the right." Olsig steps into the home and walks towards the second room to the right. From just outside, he can hear the click-clack of plastic toys. Action figures. The door is already cracked open. Olsig peers inside to see the little boy, Christian, playing with action figures. A Karina Wolfenden. Missing Arm. Tchu. Missing Leg. Sirrajin. Mark ups. Youngblood. Bent out of shape. And an Olsig…which looks brand new. Cherished. A would-be tear begins to form in the eye of Olsig as he looks at the boy. He motions to take a step forward, but draws back. He turns around and walks away from the door, back into the main area of the house. "What are you doing?" the woman asks, knowing the talk couldn’t have gone that fast. "I-…I can't." "You-…can't? Are you serious, Vangelus?" "You shouldn't have even brought him there, Lauren!" "Kidding me. You've GOT to be kidding me." "Lauren, I have to go." "Oh, so that's it? You're just going to leave? Not even going to talk to him?" "Lauren, I'm sorry. It just-…it just doesn't feel right." "Doesn't feel right? How's it supposed to feel Vangelus?" "I-..I don't know." "And that's just it! You never KNOW! What DO you know, Vangelus?" "Lauren, ple-…" "This is not a GAME, Vangelus. These are people's lives you are messing with." "I'm not trying to a-…" "You know what…every time I see you, you remind me more and more of your father." "Shut up, Lauren." SHIFT Screams. Images…of his father. Beating his mother. A young Olsig, 8 years old, watches from the top of the staircase. Tear stained eyes. SHIFT "The shadiness, the cruelty…you've become a mirror image of the old man!" "Lauren, shut…UP." SHIFT The creaking of a bed. Sex is being had in a room. The man is his father…the woman is not his mother. Young Olsig, 9 years old, listens at the door. Tear stained eyes. SHIFT "Why, Vangelus? Can't take it? Because you know it's true! You promised everyone around you that you would never become like him, but in reality, I'm seeing a man far WORSE." "I SAID SHUT UP!" SHIFT Czar Olsig shoots up. Hot buzz…another drug. He turns into a monster when he does that. Young Olsig, 10 years old, watches in fear, knowing what’s coming next. The senseless, substance-driven harassments. Tear stained eyes. SHIFT "The son has succeeded the father. You've exceeded him in everything from your pathetic treatment of women to the drug habit that almost got you k-…" *SLAP!* "SHUT UP, LAUREN!" In only a moment, Olsig realizes what he’s done. He has just struck a woman. "How coul-…" she begins to say, tears streaming. "I'm sorry. I'm-…sorry, Lauren. I didn'-…" "Vangelus…what has happened to you?" "I really…don'-…s-…" Olsig takes off. On the way out, he grabs a picture lying on a table near the door. Young Christian, age 8, emerges from his room just in time to see the back of Olsig’s head exiting the door and his mother on the floor, crying. Tear stained eyes. --- Back of a Limo. Olsig pulls out a picture. Christian. He gazes somberly. "Who’s the kid?" Chives asks. Olsig takes a deep breath. Very deep. Two, exactly. "…My son."
