Because of the facial hair he'd amassed over years of unkempt grooming as well as his slender body frame, Johnny had taken on a somewhat holy appearance to those eager to fill their lives with anything that could help define their existence. These are the same people that accept life at face-value who may or may not be tired of the party and glamorous scene that has plagued them since. He'd been leading me in a circle around the building throughout the pursuit with no visible intention of breaking the chase elsewhere in hopes to make a getaway.
