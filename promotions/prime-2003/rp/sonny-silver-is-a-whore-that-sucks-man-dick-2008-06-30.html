Sonny Silver is a whore that sucks man dick

30 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 6/30/08 The scene opens up at PRIME Headquarters in the famed Merchandise Mart in Chicago’s Loop. Former champion and fan favorite, “The Specialist” Tony Rolo stands in front of a backdrop with PRIME’s official logo. Rolo is sporting street clothes, more specifically a pair of jeans and a white Aqua VI t-shirt. Rolo: Well, Mr. Silver, Sports Entertainment Lesbian of PRIME, you get the fucking match you asked for this Wednesday at ReVolution 165. I still don’t get what you’re so bent out of shape over, though… so what if I didn’t put that freak in a mask in a better spot in the show? And I wanna know, what does Captain Justice dress as for Halloween if he wears a mask the other 364 days a year? Sorry, I didn’t mean to bring up Halloween. I know that must bring back horrible memories from your childhood since you were so ugly you had to trick or treat over the phone. Seriously, how’d you get a face like that? You must have went bungee jumping off a 90 foot bridge with a 100 foot cord. If we had to choose an actor to play you in a movie about your life, we’d have to get Corky from Life Goes On. You know, the retard. He sort of chuckles at himself. Rolo: But worse than your fuckin’ face is your breath. I think Sonny Silver uses the toilet bowl brush on his teeth. He probably figures you use it to clean crap off the toilet, why not use it to clean his tongue from all the rimjobs. That does explain, though, why every time you open your mouth, all you’re doing is talking shit. And I think I know why Silver’s teeth are yellow… he uses urinal pucks instead of Altoids. You’re such a filthy piece of shit, Silver, that when you call phone sex hot lines you give the operators an ear infection. Oh, and on that note, Red Lobster called, bitch… they want their crabs back. The Hall-of-Famer just keeps smiling at the camera, obviously still amusing himself. Rolo: Aw, Silver, this is too much fun. You’re too easy of a target to pick on. It’s great that you have a huge complex from having such a tiny pecker. If you had a Big Dick Contest with all those Asians from the Crazy Eight-Eights in Kill Bill, you’d come in 89th. You make Verne Troyer look like Dirk Digler. When a turtle gets cold, he says “I got Sonny Silver dick.” Rolo: All of this must make it pretty hard to get laid. If I asked you about the most exotic bitch you ever nailed, you’d probably reply by telling me about some cocker spaniel and terrier mix. Tony pauses for a second and takes a bit of a deep breath before continuing. Rolo: Anyway, all of that shit is besides the point. Those are all just reasons why you’re a fucking dickweed. The main point here is how I’m gonna kick your ass so bad at PRIME’s Inaugural Stars, Stripes, Armbars, and Anal Rapings that you won’t be able to sit on a chair for weeks… and considering how sore you’re going to be, I’m guessing that you won’t be in the mood and your buddy, Ceeps, is gonna have to find a different chocolate factory to dip his pretzel stick in. So I apologize to everyone in PRIME for the wrath of Cantrell over the next couple weeks. He shrugs his shoulders. Rolo: Well, we could probably get Kannon to bite the bullet and step in for a while. He’s a team player… as long as the game you’re playing consists of hiding testicles inside your mouth. The scene slowly fades away.
