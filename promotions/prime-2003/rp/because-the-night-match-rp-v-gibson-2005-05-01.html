Because The Night (Match RP - v Gibson)

1 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 1/05/05 Because The Night.Written: 1st May, 2005. 'Nobody knows it, but you've got a secret smile,And you use it only for me.' - Semisonic. I - Peer Pressure. 'This is going to get ugly,' Eleanor Kannon-Hall had prophesied at Ultra Violence. 'Very, very ugly.' Just like her managerial strategies, she wasn't far off the mark. There was something about Karina Wolfenden that seemed to cause trouble wherever she went. No attributes were present in halves, no, Karina gave the full hit of everything she possessed. Chronic apathy led to near-weekly letters home to her parents from teachers or principals, while teeth-grinding stubbornness only served to enflame the discussions their receipt triggered. A ground-scuffing low boredom threshold saw her flunk out of college, attendance percentages even lower than her grades as the world yanked her off campus for most of her first, and only, semester. A selfish need to indulge her demanding senses left her never working a day for nearly 2 years, trust fund and monthly 'subsidies' from the Wolfenden's reserves funding her exploits. A reckless style on the slopes led to many a harsh word being exchanged, every time leaving Karina to finish with, 'this is he grown-up's slope.' And then she landed in the wrestling business... 'Oh, I wouldn't just never book her again,' one promoter ranted after she bailed on his show, after having punched his main eventer flat on the nose. 'I'd book her, just to un-book her a second later.' In all fairness, the guy had let out the worst verbal volley of obscenities that the human language can muster, right in the face of a kid whose only crime was asking for an autograph, but her own conduct hardly earns her gold stars. 'Good riddance,' seemed to be the general consensus when a deal fell through with the K-Wolf. PRIME was no different. Her presence on the roster seemed to have turned Hin See's hair a lighter shade of grey, led to Motoki nearly ripping up her contract, and forced Taun Pham to go hard-line sadist on her as far as talent-control goes. So, naturally, they were less than pleased when rumours of a relationship between herself and Angelo Deville began to be spoken with growing conviction every week. Two of wrestling's biggest egos, a duo of uncompromising personalities, were likely to multiply their troublesome traits, rather than simply add them together. Others found it distasteful for reasons far removed from business. 'It's like Lolita meets Boys Don't Cry,' were the words Amy Campbell spat at her with a scathing mix of contempt, disgust, and jealousy... the proportions of which were indistinguishable. While Karina dismissed the first one as border-line respectable, art-house spank-bank fodder, she kinda liked the second. Hilary Swank's Oscar speech was a welcome alternative to the Gwyneth, and Chloe Sevigny rarely picks a bad script to work with. But regardless of the movies she name-dropped as examples, the message was clear... the relationship between Angelo Deville and Karina turned her stomach. 'I love you, essa?' Baltasar had taunted Angelo with when feelings were yanked towards the surface, the realisation of losing her forcing Deville to smash through the stony barriers. 'Are you fucking KIDDING me, holmes?' Karina was used, exploited as a weakness by Los Diablos, but Angelo didn't flinch, offering not one hint of regret for his outpouring. 'You're nothing more than a love-sick jackass!' The Mexican's rant had continued, but Deville took it on the stubbled, well-defined chin. He'd been mocked, ridiculed, taunted, embarrassed, beaten, broken, and dismissed... but never did he falter in his resolve to win over the K-Wolf. If it had been nothing more than a passing attraction, he'd have laughed it off, rather than instead wiping the Mexican's blood from his fists. 'She likes hatred, emotion,' Justin Williamson had analysed, trying to sum up the complex and chameleon-like character that was Karina. 'She uses it as fuel.' Her hatred for Deville had only served to pull them closer. Harsh words yelled down corridors became intimate talks and resolutions, while clenched fists hand become entwined fingers, narrowed stares now all-telling looks into the other's engrossing soul. 'Dating Deville just ISN'T a good idea,' was Eleanor's blunt warning. From a woman who'd lived through the ups and downs of a wrestling relationship, her advice carried experience, but every parting of overly-glossy lips added an accent of envy. To her, Karina was an unwelcome distraction... a roadblock in his career. But just as Angelo has ignored all warnings to the contrary, Karina too wasn't going to let those with vested interests erode the bond they'd formed. 'That's why I interfered in your match,' Deville had spilled out before her, every word as honest as she'd ever heard from his mouth, 'because I can't stand the thought of the world losing you to your stubborn pride.' Two weeks ago, she'd walked away, finger raised, and left him barely 30 minutes into ReVolution, stood, head bowed, in the parking lot. 'I know you don't like it, but frankly, I don't care,' were the words that remained with her from that exchange, replaying over and over in her head. They could have been spoken from her own lips, with trademark Wolfenden defiance at what others thought. 'I'd do it again, with less hesitation, and still feel good about it.' Had he apologised, tucked his tail between his legs, and pined for her forgiveness, Karina wouldn't have bothered... but he stood before her, justified, and with words chipping away at her stoic defences. It was that frank admission that made Karina resolve to make things work, to switch off whatever defence mechanisms kept screaming that it was a bad idea. That was why, at the close of ReVolution 62, she waited for Deville outside the Target Centre. No more storming away, no more dismissive comments or distancing verbal jabs. No more caring about what others said, no making their concerns and interests into her own. No more being 'Karina' about it all. II - Into The Sunset. 'Get the fuck out of my way,' Angelo Deville hissed at the unfortunate runner who'd offered to carry his bags. 'Or you'll be carrying your eyeballs around for luck.' Needless to say, The Deville was not a happy man. 'You know what?' Ellie had pressed as he'd stumbled back through the curtain, little care shown for her battered and broken charge. 'I'm going home... to my husband. If you still actually CARE about the advice you pay me for, the advice that made Christian a treble-Champion, then e-mail me or something. Consider this my contractually assured holiday, so I expect to see my money in the bank on Friday.' Every click of her heels against the tiled floor drilled into his throbbing head, while the taste of blood in his mouth, the metallic sweetness, led to his probing tongue finding a loosened tooth. 'What advice would that be,' his argumentative, pig-headed side called after her. 'Hop onto some crutches and cash in on the Insurance?' In this state, Deville was unbearable to be around. 'You know what?' the fuming Eleanor remarked while turning sharply. 'Fuck off. Fuck right off until you're out of Fucks, and aren't exactly overwhelmed with offs.' Even in her coldest of blue eyes, anger raged. Angelo bought her, she was under no illusions when it came to that. She left wrestling when her husband limped away from it, but couldn't resist the money Deville brought to the table. It was enough of a blow to her dignity, without having to deal with The Deville's baggage. 'You hired me to make you PRIME Universal Champion,' she reminded him, intricate pink and silver patterns on her nails doing little to take away from the ferocity of her jabbing finger. 'That's why I have to live on your money, that's why I spend 3 days a week away from my husband. I told you Karina would erode your focus, and I'm standing by that no matter HOW many times you try and convince people you're capable of love.' When Eleanor put all her passion into her words, the unassuming, 5'4 frame became one that would domineer the most built of men... especially those still bruised, bloody, and feeling sorry for themselves. 'I'm going home to the man I love,' she dismissed his solemn sulk with. 'Have fun with Karina, if she'll let you have your balls back to play with.' With his manager heading towards her flight back to Barbados, a tag team partner he was sure didn't trust him, and Karina most likely having left an hour or so ago, Deville was left alone as he limped towards the exit. Not even the crisp, perfectly tailored suit hanging from his broad shoulders could change that fact. 'Fuck her,' Deville grumbled as a much delayed retort to Eleanor. 'What the hell do I pay her for anyway...' His thoughts should have been on Baltasar, or on phoning his manager to set things straight. But one woman had dominated his thoughts for the last 2 months... and it certainly wasn't the uppity blonde he had flown to the States every week. He'd failed to win the match, he'd failed to watch his own back, and he’d failed in taking out Amy Campbell. She'd said it herself - at Overkill, she was going to leave Karina's knee hanging by a thread. Now, she'd still be aiming for that, but brimming with confidence from 2 huge wins in a row. 'You're fucking kidding me...' his breathless words then expelled as he saw the scribbled notes for next week's show by the wrestler's exit. Karina Wolfenden vs. Dave Gibson 'Why did I let Eleanor talk me out of enforcing an 'arrangement' upon those two Japs?' he wondered to himself. That was his usual tactic. Get hired on wrestling skill, and let the world that went on around him gain The Deville a foothold in the management structure. But in PRIME, he was merely a wrestler. Another reason he began to doubt his expensive manager. Gibson was a friend of Amy's... and at his best, carried around so few morals in his conscience that it almost rivalled the Crimson Angel himself. 'I need to talk to Kari,' he resolved with a shake of the head, smashing the doors from his path with rigid, angry palms. 'And for once, I need her to listen...' Kari's ego was enough to convince herself that Gibson was no more of a threat than Boda, or Williamson, or any other superstar she'd triumphed over. But not even a body tuned to perfection would withstand the holds Dave Gibson held in his repertoire. She'd be looking to Aimz in two weeks time, oblivious of the fact she might not even be able to walk by then. 'Ugh, why do I bother?' Deville questioned in a grumble as he stepped out into the bitter night. 'She'll just screen my call and conveniently ignore the message anyway...' But just as Angelo began to dwell on the one-sidedness of what could barely be called a relationship by the most generous of observers, a sleek, jet-black Mercedes pulled up outside the wrestlers' entrance. Lowering his bags and adopting the alert, defensive posture that had kept him healthy throughout his years, Deville tried to focus through the tinted windows, like a magic eye puzzle. With a faint, mechanic whirr, the back window opened, to show the rather out-of-place Karina slumped in the seat. 'Since this is on your credit card,' the K-Wolf's teasing lips revealed, 'you might as well get in.' III - Intoxication. 'There IS a reason that they don't just call clubs 'bars', y'know?' Karina beamed, water bottle in hand, as she danced through the dynamic crowd to where Deville was sat with his drink. 'I'm sure you have a little Travolta just bursting to get out of you!' With sweat plastering lilac hair across her face, the K-Wolf didn’t seem to care that both peach bra straps were hanging loose from her shoulders, far from the sanctuary her white vest offered them. Scraping her mane back, Karina poured the chilled water straight onto her face, leaving her bottom lip hanging just enough to catch a mouthful. 'I once had a Torratelli nearly escape from my trunk,' Angelo muttered back under his breathe, only to be destroyed by waves of deafening bass before the words reached Karina's ears. 'Huh?' she enquired, leaning across the table and brushing the hair from over her ear. 'We need to talk about next week,' The Deville then suggested, raising his voice to a near yell to be heard. 'You've got Dave Gibs-' 'No shop talk!' Kari then dismissed, recoiling herself from over the table and backing away, steps in perfect time to the music. 'Let your hair... er, forward, for once!' 'Look,' Deville tried to forcefully state, but Karina was already entwined in the bass line, its tight beat tugging her back to the dance floor as she mimicked a yawn towards the stuffy Deville. After sliding straps back over moist shoulders, Karina gave one last inviting gesture to Angelo, before disappearing back into the heaving crowd. It was her carefree attitude - in such stark contrast to his all-business demeanour - that helped Angelo become so engrossed in the enigma that was Karina Wolfenden. She didn't just roll with the punches, she no-sold their very existence. The littlest things got to Deville, right down to how the bartender dumped the ice into his scotch, rather than pouring the honey-coloured liquor over them. But for Karina... the Universe worked how it worked. Nothing personal. The music was doing little for his head... and the overly exaggerated base of Norman Cook's remix of Wildchild's 'Renegade Master' seemed to carry a frequency created especially to make his skull throb. Underneath the grey suit, that maintained the perfect cut even when seated, marbled bruises were forming. While the frosty facial expression never cracked, Deville was in pain... more than the alcohol could blot out. 'Thank fuck,' he grumpily mumbled as the disagreeable song gave way into something a little more soothing, the ambient masterpiece that is Chicane's 'Saltwater', to be exact. He took another sip, but the liquor's warmth clouded its taste, combining with the humidity of the club to thoroughly ruin Angelo's drinking experience. But as he slid his jacket off, to show the shirt below damp around the armpits, Angelo caught a sight of why he was enduring the choking, stifling heat. Every beat, every note, was seemingly a reaction to Karina's body. Every jolt of the hips, every snap of the neck, every whip of the hair, was relayed through the club's speakers. She wasn't dancing TO the music, she was living IN it. A delicate flower blowing in the euphoric wind... far tougher than its admirer would give it credit for. The bodies around Karina seemed to compress, personal space at a minimum as a collective conscious creep through them. Strangers mimicked the other's motions as their sodden backs pressed together, while backed-up bodies held onto one another, channelling movement through the other. Jealousy couldn't help but creep up on Deville, only to step on a twig and immediately alert him of its lingering presence. Just seeing so many writing bodies close to Karina had his fists clenching, and toes curling inside his shoes. Perspiration was expelled from the males that had manoeuvred themselves around her, mixing with her own. Under cover of music, then snuck in right beside the K-Wolf, but everything about her posture suggested, 'not interested.' With hands to heaven, Kari threw her hair back, lilac engulfing the face of the amorous young man behind her, who had began to extend his hands towards her hips. As he sneezed and spluttered, Karina's body repeated a wave from hands to feet, a near perfect echo of the music's peaks and troughs. Plantings hands down on the table, Deville went to pull his aching body up. Letting her arms drop, Karina used them to swat those either side of her away, letting them know that there was no interest whatsoever. Drawn deeper into the euphoric trance, Karina appeared to have signed up to the invertebrate club, her movements devoid of the rigidity her skeleton restrictively enforced. But while her body swung to all angles, her gaze was transfixed on one man. The most subtle, yet knowingly personal of smiles shepherded Deville back into his seat, letting the overly-protective Crimson Angel know that he had less than nothing to worry about. The show was for him, to sit back and enjoy, resting his ailments while Karina's dance soothed him. A smile told Angelo all he needed to know, mainly, that he was the only man on the planet who'd receive it. Gibson could wait, Aimz wasn't even a consideration, Ellie would get over it, Baltasar's time would come, and Williamson would deal with it. For once, Angelo Deville was living for the moment, and as deep, artic blue eyes watched the every graceful gyration of the K-Wolf's glistening body, he decided it was quite the moment indeed to begin living for. IV - Morning Has Broken. As Angelo Deville's ocean-deep blue eyes crept open, the glorious sun that threatened to scorch his retina was suddenly eclipsed. A man used to checking over each shoulder with nearly every step he took, Deville instantly sensed the surroundings were unfamiliar... more open, light, breezy than his stuffy and secure 'castle'. All white walls and pale, wooden floors, the interior managed to amplify the fierce morning sun, leaving eyes squinting awkwardly. A foot swung from under the sheets, nestling in a discarded piece of clothing. A far cry from home for a man who liked everything hung up or folded, and who had a different cupboard for each part of his suits. And he certainly wouldn't listen to whatever new-age, supposedly calming rubbish was polluting his ears. Taking the unfamiliar sight in, Angelo's attention drifted from cluttered shelves, to floors strewn with clothes and papers, to a messy kitchen table where the remains of the 1st of the apartment’s inhabitant’s 8 small meals a day began to cake to the bowl. After running a hand through dry, tufting hair, Deville rubbed his eyes, then rested his gaze on the most welcome sight of the apartment's owner. A light breeze skipped in through the balcony doors, doing their best to combat the stifling heat of the new day outside. But while the paper-thin curtains either side of the doors rippled, not the slightest hint of a cool lick was left to mop the beads of perspiration on The Deville's forehead. Stood on the balcony was Karina, the figure-hugging clothing she had donned leaving her silhouette appearing naked, devoid of the floaty tops and baggy trousers she preferred. Both hands ran through her lilac hair, before ensnaring it in a pony tail. Sliding back across the mattress so that he could rest against the wall, Angelo just watched her, every movement precise, serene, measured. Taking a step forward, Deville's heart almost yearned as she moved mere inches further away from him. Her hands touched down on the smooth, pale stone of the balcony's wall, anchoring her lithe body to the building. Then, as if hoisted up by a winch from heaven, Karina hopped onto the ledge, then extended herself into a handstand. His long-dormant protective side having resurfaced in recent weeks, Angelo went to lunge forward off the bed, but then rationality regained its control over him. The woman he was watching was Karina Wolfenden...everything for a reason, no action without purpose, and a body so fine-tuned that evolution's eventual outcome would blush upon seeing it. As stray strands of lilac hair fell over her face, Deville saw that her eyes were closed, lost in their focus, and nervous system not needing the benefit of sight for the exercise. In a snappy, yet smooth motion, Karina pivoted 180 degrees so that she faced the cityscape, and more importantly, the sun creeping through it's high-rise off-shoots. Leaning forward and little, narrowing his eyes to focus tighter, Angelo couldn't see the tiniest bit of movement. Karina's body, slender and toned, had locked into position, firm against the breeze, calm amidst the early-morning rowdiness of the world below. Resting his chin on the on the backs of his hands, Angelo watched on, enchanted. Mimicking the building opposite finally submitting to the sun, letting it wash over them, Karina's legs slowly parted. With no staggered motions, no awkwardness, just perfect, omnipotent control over her body's muscles. Not one finger had shuffled to the side to adjust for a shift in weight, not one flinch of her arm's muscles. The sun finally triumphed over its obstacles, having scaled the city for the last hour, finally reaching its summit. On the ledge, Karina's eyes opened, taking in the unique view of the city that she never has to share with anyone, an angle not captured in any photo. From her vantage point, then sun was under her, it's warmth dissipating down her body... bare toes baking in the unrestrained heat, while her lightly freckled nose merely tingled. So serene was her rigid posture, that a bird fluttered down from the ledge above, resting on her foot for a brief second, before flying off. Needless to say, Angelo had woken up to few sights so mesmerising. Showing the same supreme balance that ripped wrestling fans up from their seats, Karina stretched her legs out at 90 degrees to her body, extending them out over the 4 story drop. Angelo reacted just like any member of the PRIME crowd, shuffling forward off the bed, and discarding the mis-matched sheets, pillows, and bedding. He was wary about distracting her, but Deville hadn't even placed a foot down onto the messy floor when Karina's body disappeared over the balcony. 'Nooo!' went the primal roar of The Deville, disbelief wavering the tone. Feet slapped against floorboards as his toned legs thumped them down like pistons. A vest, a pile of bank statements, and a blue-tinted wine glass were sent flying as Angelo's feet ploughed through all the obstacles in his way, fingers tearing through the curtains that had been disturbed by the sudden movement of the K-Wolf. As panicked eyes prepared themselves for the sight of a broken Karina 4 stories below, they neglected to see the feet entwined around the detail of the ledge. Rising up before him, just like the sun a moment earlier, was a bemused Karina, wondering what the wail was about. With powerful legs keeping her anchored in the stone work, her flat, tight stomach muscles hoisted her body back up, then showed off just the peak of fitness Karina had reached as she remained in the position. 'What the FUCK was that?!?!' The Deville exclaimed, his heart having lost track of its natural beat. 'Er, my morning exercises?' Kari replied, typically ignorant to the fact that she tended to do things as little different to other people. Deville's hands propped him up against the balcony, his breaths slow in being caught. 'You scared the living shit out of me...' 'Yeah, because that was on my to-do-list this morning,' she jested in reply, voice showing none of the strain that her physically flawless body was taking, 'leaping to my death before the morning papers come.' The look Deville gave her didn’t need any deciphering to relay that he wasn't amused. 'You worry wayyy to much,' Kari then appeased his concern with. 'And anyway, don't you always wake up before you hit the ground?' After flashing Angelo a wink, Karina relaxed her knees and ankles, unwinding her legs from their supports. The abdominals that threatened to burst through the exposed skin beneath her cropped Adidas top retreated, leaving gravity to have it's wicked way with the limp K-Wolf. V - Nice Dream. Karina shot up straight from her slumber, covers already on the floor, and pillows having gravitated away from the flurry of lilac. Beside her the bed was empty, as it was every night... not even Psylocke and Betsy daring to venture into the domain of the light, dynamic sleeper. Her mouth was arid, tongue having to peel away from the sides. Gulping sips demolished the tepid water left at her beside, bubbles having formed on the glass where the sun's rays licked at it. A little over a month ago, Angelo would have been an unwelcome intrusion in her dream world... and would have probably copped a verbal volley the following Wednesday for his dreamt counterpart's indiscretion. But now? Her life... her pursuits... were all about overcoming obstacles. Knowing that when she touched down the other side, glory would pulse through her veins. This was no different. Kari collapsed back against the mattress, its spring rebounding her thrice before her sleek body finally settled into it. Yanking the covers back over her, Karina hugged a pillow to her toned frame, and went to sneak back into her dream world... a faint - yet telling - smile crossing her pale lips. But with Dave Gibson waiting at ReVolution, was contentment any substitute for preparation?
