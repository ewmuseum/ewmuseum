It Begins (Again)!

18 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: 04/18/2005 'And so it begins.' It's just Tyler Lopez, and he's just sitting. Sitting in his living room, sipping on a margarita. Lopez: And the question I keep hearing, over and over again, is why did I do it. Some say it was 'disappointing,' and that I was trying to be like some. Some say it was 'boring,' and that I was trying to be like Tony Rolo. Some even say will be destroyed. If I believed this, I would think I put out the worst piece of wrestling stunt ever committed to film. A stinking cesspool of bad humor and stupid jokes that nobody was entertained by in the slightest degree. But you sure are all talking about it, aren't you? Lopez obviously loves being the center of attention. Hell, he thrives on it. Lopez: So why? Why did I paint the target on my head? I could have just devoted myself to the guys that think I should have retired months ago, in a retirement match, and the likely opponents would be disappointed. I could have done it that way. I could have just played it safe, and tried to weasel my way into the main event before any of you dull little slack monkeys even noticed. I could have done that. But I'm the man in the white suit, and I've just got to stand on my white box and wave my hands around. If only I was dead like Nosferatu, THEN we'd be getting somewhere. He's mangling the metaphors worse than a traffic accident, but he doesn't mind. Lopez: And again, the question is why? And the answer is right in front of you, in the words of all those who in the back, all of them. Because I want them, and all of the rest of my opponents, lined like shooting gallery targets that I can pick off one at a time. I didn't want anyone forgetting that I am not the favorite to win this, even with my hands tied behind my back. I am the center of attention. Nobody has predicted me to walk out of this match on my own. That's irrelevant, and the sooner all of you realize that, the quicker you can start understanding that no matter who you think your main target is, they are ALL secondary to ME! I'm not afraid to 'take on the entire planet,' as some dufus once said. When I step in the ring, I don't want to be overconfident because I play it smart. I don't concentrate on the two guys who I face this week. They have it easy this week. If only they knew that easiness doesn't guarantee anything. Lopez lets out a single, loud bark of a laugh. Lopez: And there's another method to my madness, too. Lopez smirks. Lopez: Let's get this straight: there will be no more humor this week. Except the humor I get from laughing at the pitiful excuses of opponents I have. Starting with Nosferatu. Who wasn't worth my words then, and isn't much worth my words now. All I can say is that a guy doing 'Dead' parodies and crucifying in his spare time is not the sort of person I like to hear lectures from. See, I do have a life, Vampir. And that life is called 'professional wrestling.' And in professional wrestling, we do a little something called venting. If you want to give me shit because I have more time to do that than you, then do me the smallest little favor and kindly eat a fucking dick. Something is changing in Tyler. This is a side of him that has come out before. Lopez: Instinct would have told me that something like that was stupid, a waste of breath, and the kind of thing people with nothing better to talk about make into an issue. But apparently, I only thought that because I'm not well versed in the 'tricks of the trade.' I only hope that one day, in some small way, I can have mastered the 'tricks of the trade' as well as you have, Mr. Vampir. These magical 'tricks of the trade' that say, 'Sacrifice those that you are threatened by.' If they dare to respond to that, then BAM! You've got them right where you want them!' Can you teach me, Vampir? Can you? I'm desperate to learn. Lopez shakes his head in disgust. Lopez: While I study at your wise altar of 'the tricks of the trade,' maybe, in your most powerful wisdom, you can explain to me how you can discuss your mighty efforts to destroy PRIME. You 'pride yourself on all the bullshit that you've done beforehand.' Teach me, wise Master Vampir, wherein lies the difference in my recollection of past glories, and yours. Teach me, for I am eager to learn your 'tricks of the trade.' Lopez sneers. Somewhere inside him, he's having fun with this. But outside, he shows nothing but pure contempt for his opponents. Tyler: And next, as a public service to everyone watching this at home, I'm going to do you all a favor. I'm going to save you a lot of time and boredom, and make sure you NEVER have to watch an Boda again. Maybe I'm oversimplifying a little, here. I'm sorry about that, Boda. To apologize, I have something here. Reaching into his pocket, Lopez pulls out... money? Lopez: This is five thousand dollars, Boda. It's my gift to you. And if you want more, I can get it for you. I'm a fairly rich man; years in the business gets you a LOT of merchandise revenues, let me tell you. Lopez shakes his head. Lopez: But I already hear you saying it. 'Tyler, how DARE you think that money can solve all of the misery and anguish in my life? How do you think it can remove the deep emotional scars life has left on me?' Maybe it won't remove the emotional ones, but I'm sure it will remove the physical ones. You see, I'm not GIVING you this money. I'm willing to SPEND it to give you all the finest things money can buy. You can go back to being your old self again, thanks to my generous philanthropy. Why would I do this? What do I want in return? Boda, the gift of hearing you not whine about all of your fucking pain all the live long day would be enough reward for me. You have to admit: it's a pretty good start, isn't it? Get yourself a new set of friends. Get your act together, sport. I'm willing to do that, because that's just the sort of asshole I am. Lopez beams. Lopez: But you won't take this offer, Boda. You won't because you LIKE the misery. You LIKE having something to whine about week after week. When it comes down to it, you don't WANT to move on. You just want to wallow in your misery and make us watch you. You like being so pathetic, because people don't pay as much attention to the guy in the tailored suit walking down the street as they do the bum passed out in his own puke and piss on the sidewalk. With all of your sturm und drang, you're somebody. You're something special. Take that away, and you're just another loser. You're just another Jackson Darilek. Lopez looks shocked. Lopez: Oh, shit, did I just say that out loud? Damn! How could I even think that Darilek is just another waste of my time, like every single opponent I ever faced? How DARE I think that? Because, and people, I want you to sit down, because this is really big, what I'm about to tell you. I've just learned something that, well, throws everything I thought before about him into perspective. You see, what I just found out... is that Jackson Darilek... Lopez takes a long, dramatic pause. Lopez: ... has HEART! Lopez leans back quickly, as if forced back by the shockwaves of his own words. Lopez: I am so screwed, now! I am just sooooo fucked! I mean, I've faced probably hundreds of other guys who are less impressive than stuff I've scraped off the bottom of my shoe. Punks who get a few wins under their belt and suddenly think they're 'evolving' into something much better. But none of them... not a SINGLE ONE of them, has EVER said that they had HEART! He can't keep this up much longer. He laughs. Lopez: What's next? Are you going to tell us you have the 'drive' and the 'determination' necessary to 'beat the odds' and 'prove all the naysayers wrong?' Honestly, man, do you honestly believe I've NEVER heard anything like this shit before? You're nobody special. In fact you are just like Vampir and Boda. There is no special 'stuff' you have that will deter me in any way from doing to you what I've done to every other schmuck who thought they had the 'will to succeed' that would surely be my downfall. And after I pin one of these jokers at Revolution, I'm going to stalk you, and after I show everyone how much of an Apparatus I am, I will say to you the word that might as well be put next to my name in the brackets of All PRIME tournaments from here on out. He stops a moment. Lopez: Bye. A wave, just to demonstrate. Lopez: And Boda. If I have to watch this go on much longer, I'll take a sledgehammer to your knee and knock you out of the goddamn business myself, just to spare you the embarrassment. The only good thing about the Dark Age is the music. Lopez laughs to himself. He's on a roll, now. Lopez: Boys somehow, even though you've made a career, if we believe what you're saying, of destroying and running off all possible competition. From making a former World Title Contender 'your bitch' to getting carved. The fact that you're name-dropping pretty much paints the full picture for me right there. Lopez takes a deep breath. You get a feeling he's saved his biggest bit for last. Shaking his head, Lopez glares into the camera. Lopez: For all your 'disclaimers,' for all your attempts to make what you had to say into something so totally incendiary that it would completely blow everyone's mind... let me sum up. Here, are groundbreaking arguments that were so shocking that they required a disclaimer: 'Tyler Lopez is nothing special.' 'Tyler Lopez repeats himself.' 'Tyler Lopez is a hypocrite.' Nothing here, is shocking. Nothing at all. Hell, nothing I haven't heard a thousand times before isn't been said. I won't hear a thousand times more in my future position as PRIME's new found leader. You can toss in as many gratuitous 'fucks' and vocal flourishes as you want, but nothing you said surprised me. In fact, more than anything, you turned out to be a tremendous letdown. He shakes his head. It's almost too easy. Lopez: There seems to be some ambiguity on this subject, so let me make my position on this perfectly clear, so we'll have no more claims of 'hypocrisy'. I trundled around the globe pandering to loser after loser in a brave attempt to pretend they were actually on my level. Lopez slaps a hand to his face. Lopez: 'But, Tyler!' you exclaim. 'What ARE you saying? I thought you took pride in your past. Now you say that doesn't mean anything?' Shut up and listen for once. Maybe it'll actually sink in this time. Clearing his throat, Lopez leans forward. Lopez: I take pride in my past because it represented my personalized wad of phlegm, hocked up and spit into the faces of the assholes who thought that I wasn't the best. Nobody could take that away from me. For all the people who claim that Tyler Lopez is a nobody, I say they're absolutely wrong. *I* made TYLER LOPEZ into something important! Lopez arches his eyebrows. Lopez: But let's face it: compared to me, just about EVERYONE is a loser. Guys like Vampir and Boda just happened to be the kind of losers who main even on occasions. Crossing his arms, Irish laughs into the camera. Lopez: I want to tell you all a little secret. Something I've never told anyone before. I was brought into PRIME for a reason, and that was to carry the World Title, and break all records ever set by World Title holders. Lopez smiles into the camera. It's a smile that makes you wonder... could he be lying? You can't really tell. Lopez: Silly, you say? Doesn't make much sense? I say it makes about as much sense as the Dark Age claiming that they are an unstoppable force in PRIME. Lopez shakes his head. Lopez: Honestly, boys stop riding the jock of the PRIME. You... He stops, thinking to himself. Lopez: Let me just ask you one simple, yes or no question, Dark Age. Have you stopped fucking sheep yet? He stops, almost seeming to wait for an answer. Lopez: See, it doesn't really matter what you say. Go ahead and say yes. It'll just mean you were fucking sheep like crazy, but have decided to quit, because those crabs around your dick can get awful nasty after awhile. Or say no. Because it just confirms what we already know: that The Dark Age just absolutely LOVES to fuck sheep, and just can't give it up! You just have to hear his name to know that! Lopez sneers. Lopez: This is the sort of argument that gets thrown at me. This is the kind of thing you think is going to humble me, and get me thinking that maybe you're the better men in this contest. The same kind of no-win situation: 'I say you're gay. And no matter how you answer, we know the truth.' You know when I first heard that 'Have you stopped fucking sheep yet?' question. The fourth fucking grade. He's very tired of this. We can tell. Lopez: And if that's all you've got for me, boys then don't bother. Don't give me another long helping of your wonderful jokes and expect me to care. Don't waste my time again. Your attempt to fire the first shot in this battle has completely fizzled out. I've taken all your vitriol, and diluted it into fucking tap water. So you had better hope you can bring a new set of arguments to the table the next time you open your mouth. A cocky smirk. Lopez: Because if you don't... it doesn't matter if you DO wear that frying pan in your tights. You're going to feel what it's like to have Tyler Lopez fuck you up the ass. And you'd better fucking like it, bitch. Lopez stands up and walks off screen.
