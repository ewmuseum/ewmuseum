To lose somebody you love or nearly lose somebody can be heart wrenching, she’d almost lost the man she loved once and knew the pain and confusion Hailey could be feeling. The sound of the door opening startled her; she heard footsteps and finished packing the rest of the duffle bag. She turned slowly to the door. “Pierce. I’m in here. One second!” she said softly. She emerged from the bedroom and stood frozen. “Not who you were expecting Sis?” The bag dropped from her hands as her brother, Sonny Carlucci, approached her with a blade.
