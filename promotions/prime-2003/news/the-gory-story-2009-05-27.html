The Gory Story

27 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It started big. Big like four Intense competitors duking it out for a Number One Contendership in an Anything Goes Match. Meat, Skillet, Chainz, and David Noble did their damndest to try and put on a show (and, of course, beat each others' heads in). Then, to settle a serious grudge, Duke Williams and Nine channelled their inner animals. That is to say that a Dog Collar Match was necessary for these two to get all that angst out. By the time this one was done, someone was left gasping for air. In the night's next matchup, Jason Natas and Troy Douglas faced off for the second time at a PPV. The sequel to their tango at last year Great American Nightmare played out much the same, in the sense that both men focused solely on hurting their opponent. Mixing in the Falls Count Anywhere gimmick meant that both competitors got to move freely through the arena as they pummelled one another. And just like that, it was title time. Newly re-crowned champions Delta Upsilon Iota had to face two familiar foes. Colby Korver and Hank Cobb are well acquainted with V.I.A.G.R.A., but Ruby's boys made it a crowd. Would Wicked Ways sneak in between the two division stalwarts to make an impact or could one of the two past champions walk out with heads (and belts) held high? Taking the night's award for most ambitious stipulation was Wade Elliott and Killean Sirrajin, who opted for Tables, Ladders, and Chairs. The Blue Collar Brawler had to face a trial for his recent betrayal of Sirrajin. The judge, jury, and executioner? Killer himself. The two big men made full use of both T, L, and C as they tried to settle their differences, and after a horrifying spot ended the match, one man had the right to call himself a winner. Lindsay Troy. PPV wins. The two go together like alcohol and regrettable sexual encounters. So it was a cakewalk for the Queen against Christian Daniels? Right? Well, Troy is many things, but a powerhouse she ain't. And against the wall of a man, Daniels, the woman affectionately known as Uni had her hands full. That was followed by a matchup that'd been stewing for almost two months. Jon Rhine and Hessian never liked one another, that's for damn certain. But in the road leading up to UltraViolence, they'd done everything but try and kill one another's girlfr...WHAT? You mean they did do that? Wow. Well then, it goes without saying that the two absolutely tore the house down in an effort to determine which man was more Intense. One title was followed by another, as new 5-Star Champion defend his belt against High Flyer, the man he beat for the strap just a month before. One fall wasn't enough, and two's one of those irritating even numbers, so these two gamers had to resort to three falls to settle things. And, yes, we'll spoil something for you, dear reader: the third fall was most definitely needed to determine the 5-Star Champion. In the penultimate showdown of the night, a war came to an end. After destroying each other, tens of thousands of dollars in damages, and their respective reputations as sane individuals, Tyler Rayne and Devin Shakur finally shut up and put up. And boy, did they put up! In a brutal Bullrope Match, the Man in Black and the Underground Pimp did battle one final time. And when we say final, it's because one of the two men in this match didn't walk out of his own accord. After all that blood, sweat, and tears (but mostly blood), it was time for the Big Kahuna. The triple threat main event, for the Universal Title, defied precedent, pitting the three top finishers in the 2009 Dual Halo against one another for all the marbles. It was one fall for the fancy golden belt, and oh, was it mentioned that there was an unannounced Hell in a Cell stipulation? Folks will be talking about this one long after Garbage Bag Johnny, Jason Snow, and Chandler Tsonda are gone from PRIME. Simply put, the match was Ultra Violent. Sprinkle in the debut of a new tag team and Tyler Nelson (not so) happily fulfilling his duties as an interviewer, and that's a recipe for a helluva show. To see why every roster member is currently icing a dozen body parts, check out the violence for yourself!
