Colossus Spotlight: The First

3 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Castor sent me to record thoughts of The First regarding his match at Colossus, and the event itself. He felt it was a sign of good faith that his own personal biographer would be chosen for such a task, and that The First would be humbled and pledge his loyalty to Strife. I got into Salem and found it to be a weird mishmash where a tourist trap was also a historic landmark. It was like someone with a kiosk selling toy pyramids outside the real pyramids of Egypt, which is probably being done at this very moment. I’d met The First before, but I’d been at Castor’s side. Now, I was on First's turf and alone. I felt like he had the advantage, but why I thought this was a contest of wills, I can’t say. "So, where did they hang the witches?" I asked, trying to be historically correct. Most people think of witches being burned at the stake, but I knew the Salem Witch Trials ended in mostly hangings. "Oh, that all happened in Danvers really," First replied. I could tell by his easy demeanor it was a joke told many times before. I got a quick lesson in Salem history: how it used to be much bigger but because of more cities popping up, Historic Salem is now in other cities and towns. We walked down a road and ended up sitting near a Bewitched statue, a tribute to the silliness of Salem, and also an ode to witches through a TV show most people have only heard about. I asked him why he even attempted to get into an industry that routinely features 300 pound monsters, and men who stand nearly 7 feet tall. "I watched wrestling a bit when I was younger, like, from age 8 to 13 or so, and I thought it was cool, but it was only after I found out I was The First that I looked at it from a different point of view…It was the same stuff, but what I saw now was the crowd, and how they hung on every move, how there was over ten thousand people cheering or booing with every turn of the match. I wanted to be a part of that. I’ve always wanted to be a performer. I can’t sing, not much for dancing, and really the only sport I was any good at was baseball, which is a boring as hell sport to watch. I want to be entertaining, so really wrestling was the only option for me." I brought up PRIME and why he signed up with the company, hoping he’d bring up Castor. "PRIME is unique…They let you tell a story just about any way you want, and I want to talk about my life, from when I had my revelation, and from that point on…Just in a way put my past out there so people know what my situation, what my history is. You get handed a mic and told to cut a promo on somebody. You can’t talk about your first girlfriend, any personal stuff, or the days training at wrestling school…I like having that freedom here." I was kind of annoyed at no mention of Castor, since he greased rails for this kid to get a chance at Colossus to compete for a championship. I was also feeling a need for some Oxy. First has been known for being a pretty straight laced kind of guy, beyond crazy hair colors and face paint. Muse was a party girl. Although she wasn’t around, I steered the conversation subject to her, in hopes she would arrive and have some drugs on her. "What about Muse, we’ve heard a lot about Kendra in your history, is she Muse?" I asked. "No, she’s not Muse…Muse didn’t come into my life till after Kendra left, crappy situation that all was, but that’s how fate works." "Where is Muse now?" I asked, hoping he’d called her to be a part of our interview now. "Oh, I think she had a gig last night, which means she’s sleeping it off somewhere." He checked his cell phone, "Oh it’s only 2…She won’t be up till 4 at least." The words were like a punch in the gut, two hours before I even had a chance at getting some pills. I pulled my mind away from the drugs for a moment, "So what about Colossus, how does it feel to be a part of such an epic event?" I asked. "It will be surreal fighting in front of such a massive crowd on such a large stage…It’s a life experience really, the kind of stuff you remember forever. Matches like this are why you get into this business." I heard what First was saying, but I couldn’t think straight. The idea I’d be without a fix for two hours was driving me nuts. I debated asking First for pills, but then I held it together. I didn’t want him to think I was weak, and an addict on Castor’s payroll. I’m here to represent Castor and make him look good. I have a job to do. I’m going to do it, but man do I want some Oxy. At that moment, I beheld a vision: Muse wearing a red fedora and red trench coat, doing an impersonation of Carmen San Diego and walking towards us. "Muse!" I said, most likely way to loudly and cheerfully. I half ran towards her, giving her a hug. I did this mostly so I could be away from earshot of First as I asked her, "Do you have any Oxy?" I knew she was mostly into party drugs, ecstasy and adderall, but I had to try. Besides, being with Castor, she was always playing gigs with local bands in the Boston area. Somebody had to know something. "You hurting baby?" she whispered in my ear, and I released my hug. My eyes gave her a pleading look. She smiled at me, then shot a quick look at First before saying, "Spades needs some drugs, I’ll be right back." And with that she took off. I felt hurt that she was so open about what I wanted. I hated that she exposed my addiction to him and wanted to resent her, but she was leaving to get me some pills. For that she was way ahead in my book. Quickly catching myself, and not wanting to get into a conversation about drugs, I pressed on with my interview. "So what do you think about Boda? He seems like a pretty imposing opponent, and he destroyed one of the elite in the industry in Garbage Bag Johnny to win the Five Star Title." First looked at me for a moment, appeared to be lost in thought for a second, and then spoke. "Boda’s interesting, we’ve seen him and his problems with drinking, but he also has a problem with identity…So many people in this business think up a character or have a gimmick given to them by a promoter…He sees himself as Boda some times and as Chris other times…I find that interesting, the idea of being two separate people…I think we all end up seeing each other as we see ourselves, so I think all wrestlers believe there’s a public version of their opponent and a private version…Maybe that’s even true about me, maybe we’re all different when the cameras go on, but I’ve never had two different names." I was going to ask him another question, but Muse came back with a bottle of water in one hand and her other hand in a fist. How she returned so quickly I have no idea, and really don’t care. All I know is that a few moments later I got my fix. "You guys hungry? I just woke up, let’s hit Beer Works. I want some shark tips." Muse said, giving First a quick glace she’d likely used a few million times on him before. "I’m up for food, come on Spades, let’s take a walk." First replied, and with that we were off to get something to eat. As we walked, it seemed like the idea of fighting in a packed Qualcomm Stadium against a man who had him outweighed by over one hundred pounds was the furthest thing from First’s mind. Then he looked at me and said, "Colossus man…It’s gonna be amazing." He gave me a nod. His stare was far away. He was thinking about how he knew he’d be on the opposite side of things from when he was a teenager. Now he would be in the eye of the storm. And that’s where he’s always wanted to be. William K. Spades
