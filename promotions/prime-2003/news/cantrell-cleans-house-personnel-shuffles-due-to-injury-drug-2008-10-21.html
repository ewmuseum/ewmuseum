Cantrell Cleans House; Personnel Shuffles Due To Injury, Drug Policy

21 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Among the personnel moves cited in the statement: - Devin Shakur suffered a severe ankle fracture and ligament damage during the Roulette match and is expected to be out a minimum of 12 weeks. He will be moved to the inactive list alongside Lindsay Troy, who has been officially diagnosed with a sprained right knee and a high-ankle sprain, or syndesmotic injury. Troy had a "syndesmotic screw" inserted between her tibia and fibia bones following the Cataclysm show, and will be out indefinitely. - Christian Novak, Eli VanNess and Union Jack were all directly implicated as violating PRIME's Substance Abuse Policy. The Policy was not detailed in the statement. However, it was revealed that Novak tested positive for testosterone, VanNess tested positive for Nandrolone, and Union Jack tested positive for a routine diuretic. As a result of the positive tests, the superstars, as well as their respective tag team partners (Kale Ryder, Felix Young and Jeeves) were released. - The Princes of New England, Simon Knox and Connor O'Reily, were also released from their contracts immediately after Great American Nightmare. Their release was not directly tied to the Substance Abuse Policy; however, the statement was very clear in stating that neither of the former tag team champions were injured in their match with Team V.I.A.G.R.A. - The statement confirmed that The King of Wrestling, Xavier Kannon, had in fact been fired by Tyler Rayne immediately following his elimination from The Roulette. - Cantrell also confirmed the release of Cozen, who had initially been fired following Cataclysm, but was rehired for one match only at the request of Dusk. A quote from Cantrell in the statement referred to the dual releases of Kannon and Cozen as "exorcising the demons" from the Cataclysm "accident." - The Executive Producer also confirmed the hiring of Brock Shepherd, the Just Wrestling champion who appeared for an interview at Great American Nightmare. In addition, he announced the return of Jason Snow, the former 5-Star Champion whose name is still very well known in the wrestling industry.
