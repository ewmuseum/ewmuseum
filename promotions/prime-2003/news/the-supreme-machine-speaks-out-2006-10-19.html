The Supreme Machine Speaks Out

19 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After confronting both individuals, Killean told Charity and Winters that he was "THE MOTHER F*CKIN' MESSIAH" of PRIME, Killean officially informed Winters that he would become collateral damage in his dealings with Charity. "I really didn't mind Winters when I first saw him in PRIME. In fact, when he started out here I thought the guy had a lot of potential. But now, aligning with my girlfriend? You're damn straight I won't have any of that. Just because I was stuck in a situation that I couldn't fix, she just thinks that she can go off and manage someone else? Well I'll be more then happy to sign off her managerial contract to Winters, but I'm gonna take the payment out of his hide eventually." Killean, who was recently granted a work exemption by authorities in Canada was not scheduled to appear at ReVolution. His outfit was very basic with long pant legs. We kind of figured out why the pants looked odd on him. Turns out that the pant legs were custom measured to conceal the electronic bracelet that he has been wearing. But when he moved around the stage, a camera caught a glimpse of it. "Was I purposely trying to hide the bracelet? Perhaps... but it wasn't because I was arrested. Who doesn't know that by now? Fact is, I am going through all this because of something I just didn't do and covering the bracelet on my ankle is just my way of scrubbing the memory from my mind. I feel a lot better when I don't have to constantly be reminded of it." Killean is scheduled to appear at ReVolution 109 in a non-wrestling role on October 21. His in-ring return will actually take place the previous night when he enters the 6th annual PrimeTime Central GTT Tournament facing off against Yori Yakamo Jr. "I have been training for this tournament since last year when I took an early 3rd round exit at the hands of Digital Mortality. I pity the poor soul they put in my way this time around because I will not be the one making an early exit." And finally, we advised Killean that he was in the same bracket again as Digital Mortality and that he was facing another PRIME Hall of Fame Wrestler, Karina Wolfenden. "I've wrestled Karina and have beaten her. But in doing so, realized that she could beat me or anyone at any given time. I'd feel sorry for Digi, but the fact is he took a horrible draw in Round One and Karina is gonna go Black Window on his ass. And should he manage to somehow get by Karina... well, let's just say that he may not want to. And Digi, if you've got a problem with that, perhaps you should confront me face to face... on my turf and in my ring. A PRIME ring."
