PWA Promotions Immenent

23 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"PRIME viewers can expect to see Sun Tzu appear in the upcoming weeks," Williamson proudly revealed. "The aggressive young woman from the People's Republic of China has been tipped by many to fit effortlessly into the shoes that Karina Wolfenden's retirement vacated, and although it may sound like a burden of expectation, Sun will likely see it as merely the first step in her United States career." One other potential superstar set to make the jump to PRIME's main roster is a man who has been to such heights before, Paul Cain. "Paul has been here for the past few weeks getting his reflexes sharpened," revealed Williamson. "People seem to have forgotten that not too long ago the man was being ranked highly in PTC, as well as coming within milliseconds of getting huge victories over some of PRIME's best. But now he's back, and probably in even better shape than before, so he's being tipped by some who've followed his return closely to start picking up the consistent big wins that eluded him in his previous run." Wary of exposing the talent that PWA is nurturing to too bright a spotlight, Williamson was reluctant to comment on any students who aren't quite ready to make the jump up to the PRIME roster. He was, however, happy to announce that a young talent with a very familiar name is in talks to join the Academy. "Alexander Hall, wrestling as Alex Kannon, nephew of Eleanor Kannon-Hall, flew over last week to have a look around the facilities," Williamson revealed. "He's only at the beginning of his wrestling journey, but both myself and Jason have worked with his uncle, and moulding the successor of the Kannon name is something we're both looking forward to."
