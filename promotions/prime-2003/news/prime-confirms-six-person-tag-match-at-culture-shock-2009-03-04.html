PRIME Confirms Six-Person Tag Match at Culture Shock

4 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Or was suspended. Colby Korver, one half of the original DUI team had been serving a suspension first imposed by C.P. Cantrell dating back to 2008. This week, Korver’s suspension was lifted, making him immediately available for competition. "This means that Culture Shock will indeed feature a six-person tag match with the tag team titles on the line, as the complete DUI [Williams, Cobb, and Korver] defend against Team VIAGRA [Davis, Flyer, and MLM]" stated Lisa Tyler. The suspension was not lifted without a great deal of thought, and some concessions on the part of Korver. "We believed a six-man tag match for the titles would be a great draw for PRIME, harkening back to a time when some territories actually had six-man tag team championships." Ms. Tyler added, "So, from a business perspective, it made sense to lift the suspension of Colby Korver." Korver, however, first had to agree to the Prometheus board’s demands. With his return, Korver will have to remain clean and sober at all times, and will be subject to random testing at the board’s discretion. Hank Cobb and Duke Williams already face similar restrictions following some behavioral missteps. The two DUI members must not be under the influence of any sort of alcohol or drug when representing PRIME. "With Colby, we took it a step further." Lisa Tyler clarified. "If he wanted to return to the roster, he had to agree to remain sober at all times, no matter where he’s at, or when he’ll next be appearing at a show or autograph signing. So long as Korver is under contract with PRIME and wishes to remain an active member of the roster, he’ll have to remain clean. And he’ll be tested randomly to prove it." Whether Korver will be able to successfully hold up his end of the bargain remains to be seen. But, for the time being, Colby Korver is once again a part of PRIME, and has his sights set on defending that tag titles in a huge six-person tag match against Team VIAGRA. Yesterday Korver was an outcast, serving an indefinite suspension, sitting home on the couch watching the shows. Now, he’s back to business, traveling the world, ready to step back into the ring and be watched by millions. Quite the culture shock. And waiting to welcome him back? Former champions, Team VIAGRA, hell-bent on getting back those tag belts.
