High Rollin' in Atlantic City

23 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The night opened up with Jason Snow, the 5-Star Champion, defending his title against "Big Bear" Munson Monsoon. Monsoon won the right to face off against Snow after winning the 5-Star Challenge at last month's Colossus IV. Snow, who would also compete for the Universal Title later on in the evening, had another thing lurking around the corner for the past few weeks. Chainz. Would Chainz make his presence known? Would Snow, who also is the PTC Unification Champion, be successful in his defense? Or would the Bear roar his way to his first title? Then, we moved on to see Devin Shakur defend his Intense Champion against Wade Elliot. Since Elliot's debut he has been a rousing success in his short time here with a victory over Nitz Donnelly at Colossus IV and now a shot at the Intense Title. Yet, Shakur, even with his mind firmly upon the Universal Title and the Roulette match later on the evening, would not be one to just turn his focus and attention elsewhere. The two have been butting heads for week and it would end here tonight. Would Shakur be able to retain his gold or would Wade Elliot steal the title from Shakur's grasp? Two tag matches would fill out the middle of the card as we saw the UPstarts face off against a few newcomers to PRIME, Aaron Andrews and Tyler Chance. These two newcomers were viewed as just a couple of guys getting a big shot here tonight, but would it end up being more? Would the UPStarts teach these newcomers what it's like to wrestle in PRIME or would Andrews and Chance shock the world with a victory that no one could see coming? The other tag match featured the UnFuckables and Sound and the Fury, a match weeks in the making. It was only a matter of time that these four men would square off in the ring after the weeks of fighting and burning that lead up to this match happening. The Illustrious Face-Eater, fresh off of his CWMC win, has a habit of bringing insanity out of people. Would tonight be any different? Would the A-List have their way in this match, helping out Face-Eater and Wade? Or would Sound and the Fury get their revenge and defeat the Unfuckables? The sub-main event featured another member of the A-List, Chandler Tsonda, facing off against Tyler Rayne, and Hoyt Williams in his first PPV match since returning to PRIME. The past few weeks have seen the two legends, Tsonda and Williams, getting the best of each other while Rayne has been in the middle of them as the new rookie in town. Would the superstar Chandler Tsonda come out and pick up a huge victory on his way to greatness? Would God's son reclaim his throne? Or would Rayne throw the wrench in both of their plans and continue his ascent to the top? Finally, the final match of the evening was the main event, otherwise known as the Roulette. Pierce Lavelle. Jonathan Winters. Dusk. Devin Shakur. Titan St. James. Jason Snow. Sonny Silver. Tony Gamble. Lindsay Troy. The Universal Title. So many questions were asked leading into this match. Would this be Silver's final chance? Would he finally win the title? Could Gamble finally achieve the title he's been desperately chasing after all year long? Would Jason Snow claim another victory? Would Lindsay Troy add another notch in her belt and become arguably the greatest PRIME Universal Champion? So let's add a few more questions to the match. Which move had fans screaming "Holy Shit" even as they walked out of the building? Which superstar would surprise everyone and change the course of the match and the outcome? And who would stand in the middle of the ring as the champion when it was all said and done? Tune into PRIME's Dice, Dollars and Tragedy to find out the answers to these questions and so many more!
