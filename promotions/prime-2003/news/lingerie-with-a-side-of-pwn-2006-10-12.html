Lingerie With a Side of PWN

12 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Please. Playboy, Maxim and Stuff have been trying to get me to pose for them for years," Lindsay Troy said during a phone interview with PRIME's Media Department earlier in the week. "Every time some attention whore in this industry decides to strip down to get themselves over, I'll get a phone call. Someone from Playboy called me on Monday wanting to know if I'm free for an issue the beginning of next year. You'd think after four years of adamant 'NO's', someone would take the hint. But they're sneaky. They'll force a new intern to make the phone call and have them read prepared lines like 'Oh, we'll make it tasteful. Whatever you want!' What I want is to not be lumped in with women like Tomas and Tigera who debauch themselves for a little exposure." Troy, one-half of PRIME's tag team champions and potential contender for the company's 5-Star Title, has been a long-time denouncer of industry women shedding their clothes for media and print exposure. If you couldn't tell already. "For all the good things that women like myself and Ivy [McGinnis] have done over the years, all it takes is one slutty outfit, one lewd act and one naked centerfold to redo all the damage that we've tried to fix. I may get flack from the boys for my stance on things of this nature, but I have too much respect for myself and for this business to ever bare-all to boost ratings or to boost my ego." When asked for comments regarding Tomas's contract situation in Atlantic Wrestling Club, Troy had the following to say: "If the reports on Tomas being forced to do the cover are true, then she's a f(explicative deleted)g idiot for not reading her contract before signing it. She won't have to worry though; I'll make sure NOW is off her back after I rip up Johnny Noble good and proper on ReVolution 108. It's not a successful week for me if Kim Gandy isn't on my case." A harsh critique of current events from the Queen of the Ring who, as usual, pulled no punches. One thing's for certain, though: as long as there are women willing to pose naked, there'll be a magazine willing to put them on its pages.
