Quarantined

8 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the middle of the 132 program, the group was involved in two separate backstage fights that led to Lisa Tyler kicking Winters, Shakur, and Lavelle out on sight, with Dusk ordered to leave the building after his match with Danny Ferguson. Refusing to conform, Pierce Lavelle charged through the crowd and attacked Dusk once his match was over, further damaging his injured right leg. Elsewhere in the crowd, Jonathan Winters and Devin Shakur proceeded to trade fisticuffs. With security tied up in other affairs, there was nobody around to prevent the fans from getting involved in this fight. Nor was there anybody around to protect their well being. Devin Shakur threw a fan in front of him to avoid an Indifference Maker from Jonathan Winters. Pierce Lavelle’s jump from the apron into the crowd onto Shakur and Winters knocked a couple of fans down. Dusk leaped from the top turnbuckle down onto all three competitors, his momentum carrying over and knocking another fan down. Then it got really horrific. Dusk attempted to nail his ‘Lights Out’ finisher on Jonathan Winters, but at the last minute Winters moved out of the way and a fan was struck under the chin. Immediately the fan went down in a heap. Two fans held Devin Shakur’s arms behind his back while Pierce Lavelle pummeled him. Once Shakur got Lavelle away, he kicked one of the fans in the groin, turned around, and forearmed the other one in the side of the head. Jonathan Winters took a chair from a fan, allowing him to fall down, and ran it against Dusk’s head. Not to mention that fans of the wrestlers got into sporadic fights around the arena. Thankfully, police were able to calm the situation relatively quickly. The four wrestlers were subsequently arrested and ordered to spend the next couple of nights in jail by boss Lisa Tyler. We have learned that Dusk though was bailed literally minutes after he was taken to his cell by an unknown person. No other details have been released in that concern. Plus, much to her chagrin, Devin Shakur used his vast amount of money to be released and make his way back to the arena, yet again, to find Sun Tzu. Lisa Tyler caught Shakur back in the building, and it took a quick escape by the communist duo to avoid her venom. None of the wrestlers are out of the woods by far on this one. All four have been fined a heavy amount of money, which is reported to be in the high five to low six figure range. Another, more interesting, form of punishment has been instituted by the boss. What she calls a ‘quarantine’ will take effect on Revolution 133. The rules of this quarantine are that the wrestlers are to stay in their locker rooms throughout the duration of the show unless they are booked to compete. We caught up with PRIME’s figurehead for her thoughts. "I would really love to suspend all four of them, but the fact is that we are so close to Colossus and they are one of the main draws for the show. After the Colossus Pay-Per-View concludes, I will see to it that they are properly punished for their actions. What I am hoping to gain with this quarantine is to ensure PRIME one show without the four of them running roughshod. Should any of them decide to break this quarantine, the punishment will only be that much more brutal." We also managed to contact Dusk by phone and get some of his thoughts. "Look, the fact is that we've gone through this song and dance for months now. Tensions are at an all time high and we're just about ready to kill each other. It's like being brothers and sisters, except the reality is that we actually do hate each other. No one should be surprised, much less Lisa Tyler who has continually pitted us against each other. You can lock us up in jail, confine us to our locker rooms, and send us to the corners of the world, but at the end of the day this is going to be resolved. There will be only one winner." Devin Shakur is reported to of called Lisa Tyler directly and inquired about bringing in "acquaintances" to the locker room prior to the show starting. After the boss agreed to the terms, Shakur stated that he would gladly concede to the rules, and quote "be there early". Jonathan Winters and Pierce Lavelle were unable to be reached for comment. How will this play out with Pierce Lavelle and Dusk scheduled to compete in a rematch from Overkill for the Intense Championship? Tune into 133 to find out.
