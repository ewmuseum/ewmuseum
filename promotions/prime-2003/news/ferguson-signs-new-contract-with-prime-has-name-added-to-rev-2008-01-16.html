FERGUSON SIGNS NEW CONTRACT WITH PRIME; HAS NAME ADDED TO REV 150 BALLOT

16 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We're thrilled to have Danny back in the fold," said a beaming Blaine Blair during an abbreviated news conference late Sunday. "I was always very confident that we would be able to work something out with him." While many attribute Ferguson's new deal to his impromptu Universal Title shot - and close loss - against Sonny Silver on ReVolution 148. After being brought out for a 'goodbye' interview segment in front of his hometown Los Angeles crowd, Ferguson was interrupted and challenged by the champion, who offered one final match on his way out the door. That match, which tore the house down on PRIME's first show of 2008, activated a little-discussed "out" clause in Ferguson's contract, voiding it and allowing him to walk away - as he had declared he would do - without legal reaction from the company. However, despite his ties being cut, reports say that it was Ferguson who approached PRIME officials shortly after the end of Rev 148 to begin negotiations on a new deal. Calls to Ferguson were not returned and he declined to appear at Blair's news conference to discuss the new deal. Many have questioned why he was so quick to re-sign after seeming to be so vehemently opposed to returning to the company. It's possible that, after having a taste of his "last" match last week against Silver, the actor didn't feel like it was his time. Also announced at Blair's Sunday news conference, in what he termed as a "signing bonus", was the addition of Ferguson to the list of potential contenders for the triple threat Universal Title match at ReVolution 150. Ferguson joins Hoyt Williams, Lindsay Troy and Devin Shakur on the ballot, replacing the "superstar to be named." Blair denied that the "STBN" was a direct hint at Ferguson's return, admitting openly that the re-signing was a surprise to him. He declined to identify the original name planned for that slot.
