King of Kings Highlights and Notes

28 December 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The evening started off with Brandon Youngblood battling against Intense Champion Chainz in the first of two Jewel in the Crown semifinal matches. Both competitors were recently heavily involved in one another’s business over the past ReVolutions so the intensity level kicked up a few notches when the bell rang for this bout. Ultimately, Chainz could not keep his record breaking victory streak alive and was eliminated from Jewel in the Crown by The Pariah. Brandon Youngblood moved onto the Crown finals where he will face either Tyler Rayne or Tchu. The first monumental backstage moment of the evening came when Blaine Blair made a rare appearance on camera to join Vice President of Talent Relations Lisa Tyler in her office for an up close and personal viewing of King of Kings. He also informed Ms. Tyler that the Board of Directors fully supported her in the fight against Tyler Nelson and she would not be released anytime soon from her job. As grateful as BLT was to hear the news, she still seemed a little anxious while Blaine pulled up a chair and got some popcorn. Contest Numero Dos for the Pay-Per-View featured Bryan Dawkins squaring off against Johnny Raindance. The Flyin’ Hawaiian and Raindance were slated to compete at the Pay-Per-View after Lisa Tyler gave their first round Jewel in the Crown contest rave reviews and demanded to see a rematch. Unfortunately, the two competitors didn’t live up to expectations from their previous bout and ended up in the same scenario as their ReVolution 213 encounter, a no contest. The two combatants brawled outside the ring past the referees ten count and had to be separated. Hoyt Williams decorated PRIME with his own version of a Christmas tune. We would inform you about how great it was, but dude you have to see it. Order the replay. Go ahead we’ll wait. Or find it on YouTube… Did you see it? Did you see it? So good right? Rock on, Hoyt…Rock on. The second Jewel in the Crown semifinal contest went down as one of the classic matches in the history of PRIME. A dream match that most fans never thought they would get the privilege to witness. Hall of Famer Tchu took on The Underground Pimp Tyler Rayne in a back and forth melee that saw Tchu become the more aggressive individual, taking risks and targeting the knees of The Golden Boy for over twenty five minutes. Much to the chagrin of the Inhuman Being, he wasn’t able to add the possibility of a second JITC or a victory over the 2008 Dual Halo winner to his belt. Tyler Rayne moved onto the Jewel in the Crown finals where he would take on Brandon Youngblood. 5 Star Champion Tony Gamble found himself in quite a quagmire over the past few weeks when Lisa Tyler informed him that he would be defending the strap at King of Kings, and that the opponent wished to remain anonymous. The Permascar Superstar attempted various times to deduce the identity of his opponent but came up empty. When the big moment finally arrived, it was indeed a big moment, as not one but four individuals came down the ramp…The Tyler Nelson Army. Chainz, Hessian and Kazys Jankauaskas beat The Grin to a pulp and it appeared that Tyler Nelson would claim the 5 Star Championship, but the music of The Havana Harlot interrupted the scene and she decided to cash in her Guaranteed Championship shot against Gamble. However, never let it be said that The Grin goes anywhere without a contingency plan, or more specifically two very small ones. Pink and Burgundy, two of the most loyal members of The Midget Contingent, intervened several times on behalf of their benefactor, thus causing Iron Wolf Kazys Jankauskas to strike back on behalf of the Wolves of Slaughter. The interference got so chaotic that Ham Patrick called for the bell and declared the bout a no contest, much to the chagrin of fans in attendance. However, they received a treat when Blaine Blair emerged from the backstage area and declared that a rematch would take place on ReVolution 215 between the two for the Championship with no ringside interference. Jay Phoenix was recruited by Tyler Nelson at ReVolution 213 at the expense of Troy Douglas when The Eternal Flame was forced to lay down for The Greediest Player in the Game during a tag team contest. Douglas searched for answers and didn’t receive a straight one, so he elected to get his answers the old fashioned way, in the squared circle. The reluctant member of Nelson’s Army put up an ample fight but was no match for the veteran Megatron, who put Phoenix away with an End of the Road and booked another victory against Tyler Nelson and his Army. The Tag Team Titles have been on the downside for quite a significant amount of time, a subordinate Championship of sorts in the land of PRIME. For the last portion of the year, three teams made the straps inarguably the most talked about belts around the locker room. King of Kings would be the culmination of an arduous journey by three teams: The Wolves of Slaughter, Change in Spades and champions The Redeemed. The three teams battled back and forth, each team looking to seize control of opportunities and sneak attacks. In the end, Change in Spades managed to triumph over The Wolves by nabbing a pinfall and securing their first piece of Tag Team Gold. Devin Shakur bolstered the claim he could defeat Hessian if they were given a Great American Nightmare rematch. For weeks, The Murder Show refused, saying there was nothing between them. The Black Plague attracted The Construction of Destruction’s attention by placing his most prized possession on the line, The Golden Ticket. In exchange, Hessian placed his Hardcore Reputation at stake, meaning he could never again pick up a weapon of his own volition during a match if he lost. The stipulations for the contest made it all the more impossible for Shak Daddy to win. He needed to keep Hessian down not just for three seconds, but for ten. In a bloody brawl which went all throughout the arena and ended up tearing a gigantic hole in the PRIME*View, Devin Shakur set out and backed up his words by thwarting Hessian with a little help from an unexpected ally…Chainz. The Monster from Hell’s Kitchen got the jump on Hessian just as The Murder Show was set to Hellevator Shakur into sheer defeat. The Monster delivered a Chain Reaction that stunned those in attendance and allowed The Black Plague to pick up the victory, toasting to his running buddy over the broken body of The Murder Show. After those in attendance realized the Jewel in the Crown finale would feature two of the most intense superstars in the company, the decibel level rose off the charts during the entrances, the stoic and expressionless Brandon Youngblood going against the boisterous and bold Tyler Rayne. Both men held a vast degree of fighting prowess in different fields and appeared to play a long psychological game of chess throughout the evening. Neither man wanted to concede defeat. Tyler Rayne was in the 2008 JITC finals and lost to Jason Snow. He sure did not want to go down for the second year in a row. Brandon Youngblood tried so hard to acquire the Universal Championship during his previous stints but failed to do so. What happened during the forty five minutes in the ring will be discussed for years to come as it has already been deemed one of the greatest matches in the history of PRIME. For Brandon Youngblood, he finally realized his dream. Nobody left to stand in his way of going after the Universal Championship. He will headline Culture Shock Night 1 against the Universal Champion in a one on one contest. The final match of the evening had multiple players in the building on edge. The most notable being Lisa Tyler who invested a lot in The Original Villain to defeat Tyler Nelson and leave The Greediest Player in the Game with absolutely no leverage in his attempted takeover of PRIME. However, Tyler Nelson wouldn’t go down without a fight, and boy did he ever fight. The stipulations also gave Nelson an advantage…Or at least he presumed it was an advantage. Lumberjack rules. Even though, at one point, Hessian emerged from the backstage area and clobbered Jason Snow, the theme of the match revolved around one detail…Tyler Nelson had a lot of enemies. A lot of them. Brandon Youngblood. Tchu. Bryan Dawkins. Tyler Rayne. All of them came down to make their presence felt, which left Nelson in a precarious position. Then…Things went from bad to worse. Emerging from the backstage area was none other than Chandler Tsonda, the man Tyler Nelson tried to cripple at Great American Nightmare. The Model Citizen assisted those in defending against Nelson’s Army and gave chase to Nelson, long enough for him to scamper back inside the squared circle and receive the biggest Snake Eyes of his career. Jason Snow retained the Universal Championship and declared a victory for PRIME. But the evening wasn’t over. It was far from over. For a brief moment, PRIME saw the future standing in the middle of the ring, eyeball to eyeball. Brandon Youngblood got into the face of Jason Snow and pointed to his waist, indicating that he would be the one to take the Universal Championship off The Original Villain. Both men ended up stepping aside for the greater festivities of Tyler Nelson’s Army consoling their leader only to be interrupted by Lisa Tyler. She came into the squared circle and got into the face of Tyler Nelson, claiming she had some valuable information which the people of Alabama and those in PRIME might wish to know about. The source of said information was a private investigator, which Tyler Nelson arrogantly dared to step forward. Well, that person didn’t have a problem stepping forward…Or rather, those people didn’t have a problem stepping forward since they were The Man in Black Devin Shakur and The Biker Christian Daniels. The look on Tyler Nelson’s face is one that will be freeze framed on DVRs until extra space is needed and the program is deleted. Lisa proceeded to read from the report Shakur and Daniels gathered, exposing The Greediest Player in the Game as a fraud. He wasn’t Tyler Nelson, but rather his name was actually James Allen, a two bit con artist. Allen looked to convince his army that Lisa was trying to pull the wool over their eyes. Chainz and Elise Ares were confident enough to step forward to the executives at Allen’s orders and strike Lisa Tyler. It was time for Devin Shakur to make his move, and he nudged Lisa Tyler to the side, daring either Elise or Chainz to strike him…to strike their brand new boss. Silence fell over the building while Shakur held everybody in suspense. He was cashing in the Golden Ticket and acquiring a spot on the PRIME Board of Directors…Blaine Blair’s spot. From the looks of things, Blaine himself had no idea of the switch and was the most shocked person in the building. Lisa did the best she could to hide her knowledge and all three stepped out of the ring while James Allen realized that he had just been conned by an even greater scam artist, The Black Plague. Michael Sloan put a reassuring hand on Allen’s shoulder, trying to ensure Allen everything would be alright. Two seconds later, Allen felt a boot in his midsection and was doubled over. The raw power of Michael Sloan jolted him into the air and back down to the canvas not once, but twice. The Chain Reaction. Everybody else in The Army of Allen left the ring in disgust while Shakur, Daniels, Tyler and a confounded Blaine Blair watched from the top of the ramp in approval. The Man in Black allowed Lisa Tyler one final touch to the evening, her own early Christmas present. James Allen was hereby released from PRIME to the loudest pop of the evening, his downfall officially complete. With the closing of the PRIME year, a few more individuals elected to part ways with the company. For those wondering, Tyler Nelson was actually released. Jay Phoenix was so elated that Nelson could no longer hold the contract over his head that he requested walking papers from the company, citing the long and strenuous personal differences over the months due to Nelson and previous enemies. He was granted the release. Roque de la Red also asked for a release and was granted his request. On the plus side, Chandler Tsonda was given a new contract, although at the time we are unaware of the figures and dates involved. The worst of the injuries coming from the Pay-Per-View belong to Hessian, who could have a concussion after Michael Sloan’s vicious assault. He is resting comfortably in his home and everybody in PRIME is prepared for the long international Culture Shock road. PRIME would like to wish everybody a Happy Holidays.
