Colossus Spotlight: Boda

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Boda had been plagued by drugs and alcohol for a good stint of his early PRIME career and needed rehabilitation to cure him of his addictions. When he returned in 2010, many were shocked he was able to hold the same impact and resonance as what got him into PRIME lore over four years ago. He showed no signs of ring rust and was able to have a top 10 finish in PRIME’s 2010 Dual Halo. Boda agreed to discuss his career in a recent phone interview and said what got him into wrestling, "I never wanted to be in this business. It just happened. You see, I was a natural born athlete, who excelled in all sports as a child. As I grew older, I grew taller and taller. When I stopped growing, I was almost 7 feet tall. I had scholarships to play basketball at many schools. I went onto play for North Carolina, winning a National Championship. Drafted by the Orlando Magic, I played for them until the end of the 1999-2000 season. I was expelled from the league for an on court scuffle with Shaquille O'Neal. Well, if you call choke slamming the man a scuffle that is. After that, turning to pro-wrestling was only natural. I'd grown up watching Saturday morning wrestling and just knew I could do what those guys were doing. So I ended up in PCW and the rest became History." A history which includes historic feuds with Vampir Nosferatu and Kasper Spy, Boda won a 6 person Golden Envelope match at PRIME’s 50th program and set his sights on the Universal Championship. He came out on top against both Black Angel and Killean Sirrajin at Culture Shock 2005. He would lose the Universal strap to Karina Wolfenden, and extracted a rematch from Killean Sirrajin. He didn’t win and was literally buried alive a few months later. ReVolution 66 saw Boda get into a brawl with Jon Kano. He was scheduled to be out for 2 to 3 months. He wasn’t seen again until ReVolution 217. He admitted being a flawed man, but decided to align himself with Jason Snow and Brandon Youngblood. For three months in 2010, they ran roughshod over PRIME, with enough talent and tenacity to match any force which came their way. Boda told us what PRIME, as a company, has meant to him, "PRIME to me was like making into the NBA. It's the BIG SHOW. I remember when I first walked into PRIME back in 2003/2004. It was awesome. I'd been in a few companies at that time, but PRIME was way above everything else. PRIME let me showcase what I could do. Even when I abruptly left PRIME, I was welcomed back with open arms. PRIME made all the dreams come true." Once the SOB dissolved with Snow and Youngblood’s retirement, Boda was left to fend for himself and got caught in a program with Lindsay Troy sending him mysterious vignettes and causing him to lose several matches. Boda was able to gain a measure of revenge thanks to a fast count from Elvis Nixon thanks to boss Devin Shakur. Boda and Shakur would hook up later when Boda was slated to be the number one contender for the 5*Star Championship at Colossus. Instead, he ended up being that top challenger on ReVolution 227. In a segment revealed on 228, Boda and Shakur plotted to get rid of GBJ. Boda ended up blindsiding GBJ and, in the minds of most, stole GBJ’s championship. Don’t expect Boda to give the Championship back as he is taking it with him to Colossus. On the topic of Colossus, Boda spoke of its history, "It's our Super Bowl, our NBA Finals, our Daytona 500… You get the point? This PPV is where every superstar puts out 200 percent. The fans foam at the mouth for the anticipation of seeing something special at Colossus each and every year. It's been 5 years since I was last on a Colossus card. I can't wait for this one." His opponent The First came about through rather awkward circumstances. No one figured The First would get a championship opportunity for months, considering he was quite low on the totem pole. However, when all other slated talent dropped from PRIME in a rapid amount of time, First was elevated and has shown he is worthy of a shot. Boda has gotten the better of him over two shows since their match was made official, but Boda applauded First’s initiative in a brief comment about him, "The First.... a brave kid, getting his first big break in PRIME against me. I wish him well. He'll need it against the 5 Star Champion." Boda Vs The First will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
