Sirrajin No-Shows ReVolution? Arrested?

1 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Many old faces were seen and some new faces showed up as well. Even some returning superstars got their time on the show as well. 105 was full of non-stop action, heart pounding matches and a main event that could please even the most cynical fan. But the promotion was forced to endure a set back and the accompanying flame war that came with it. Advertised to appear at the show for the first time since PRIME aired Colossus III on Pay Per View and after a week off at ReVolution 104, the former two time Universal Champion Killean Sirrajin did not fulfill that obligation. Originally, it looked as if Sirrajin decided to go AWOL on PRIME much like last year around PTC’s annual GTT Tournament. Upon further investigation, newspapers and magazines all over the world hit the shelves with a very disheartening article. "Killean Sirrajin, PRIME Hall of Famer, Held for Drug Pushing and Attempted Murder." No, none of the above is a typo. It seems as if Sirrajin, 28, was arrested and questioned at the Winnipeg International Airport on Sunday while he was attempting to board a plane bound for Miami and ReVolution 105. As of this moment, the charges that Killean is being held for are alleged. The professional wrestler has not been formally charged however he is being held because the allegations are serious enough. One thing that was confirmed is that Killean is not allowed to leave the country. As of press time, rumors were circulating that Sirrajin would be forced to wear a bracelet to prevent possible flight and will be under house arrest. We can neither confirm nor deny those claims. Also, it has been rumored that all of Killean Sirrajin’s personal assets have been frozen. Either way it stands, wrestling fans should not expect to see Killean Sirrajin on a PRIME wrestling show anytime in the near future unless something of a miracle happens. But it looks like time may have run out on the wrestling superstar.
