Kannon A Doubt For ReVolution 174

30 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At Cataclysm, even after eliminating Lindsay Troy from the main event after injuring her knee, it appeared Kannon had weaselled his way out of any comeuppance for his treacherous actions when C. P. Cantrell took it upon himself to bargain a spot in The Great American Nightmare’s Universal championship Roulette for the Gold Patron Meritorious’ loyalty… … but then Xavier had to go and ask for an apology. What he got instead was a brutal beating at the hands on the Universal Champion, who took it upon himself as the promotion’s figurehead to exact revenge upon it’s most treacherous of roster members. Anyone who witnessed the attack would have assumed that Kannon would be missing ReV 174 on medical grounds, but it appears that even the PRIME medical staff are in no mood to give Kannon an easy ride, declaring him fit to appear. But, this is Xavier Kannon we’re talking about. A statement issued by Kannon’s agent, Rush Mason, reveals that the PRIME fans may not get to delight in the sight of the battered and bruised Scientologist tomorrow. "After the unjustifiable actions of PRIME’s champion last week, PRIME has proven that it is not a safe working environment for my client and that he is the target of a campaign of discrimination that is not only endorsed, but is encouraged by upper-management. Even as he lay bleeding and injured in the ring, EMT’s were given clear and audible instructions to tend to Tsonda first. At this time, there has been no fine levelled upon Chandler Tsonda for his reprehensible actions, and not so much as a verbal warning has been issued to any staff who were complicit in the attack. It is due to this that I have advised my client to only attend future PRIME shows if the following are in place:- i) An independent security team free of PRIME influence to protect my client at events ii) A guarantee is provided from the highest level of PRIME management that my client will not come to any harm outside of sanctioned matches, and that a level of punishment is put in place that will act as an effective deterrent to anyone wishing harm upon him Until documentation assuring either is received by my office, then Xavier Kannon will not be at ReVolution this week, or any future weeks." As of midday, no response has been issued by PRIME management.
