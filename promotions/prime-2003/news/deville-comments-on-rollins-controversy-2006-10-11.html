Deville Comments On Rollins Controversy

11 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rollins debuted this past ReVolution, spending most of the show shivering in a parka on account of heat-devoid monstrosities like Snow parading around all night. At one point there were fears hypothermia was taking hold (right around when Worth interrupted Snow), but miraculously, as The Renegade's life revolted against this world, Angelo Deville, shining like a million suns and with a billion times the heat, sauntered out to the rescue. Finally there was enough of a counterbalance to Snow and Worth that Rollins could shirk his Eskimo disguise and proclaim to the world his creative genius and superiority (accomplished using symbols t3h d3v1ll3 made famous). "Obviously, I knew Rollins was coming," Wrestling's Cadillac went on smugly. "I'm the guy's Messiah. He is literally and spiritually obligated to follow me, resplendent in my shadow, salivating over my castaways and hand-me-downs. Word around town," he paused here, pleased with himself for mocking the opening once more, "is that in his attempt to leave no stone unturned, he even threw a shot into Karina." PRIME Hall of Famer Karina Wolfenden was unavailable for comment. Ever helpful, Deville volunteered that she was likely out attacking babies with her skateboard. We’re not so sure. "Rich Rollins is my bitch. Sorta like Snow, except nobody's sure to whom Snow belongs. If we're to believe the almighty buzz on the street, it's some fat ex-con named Tank." Actually, according to Wikipedia, the most convenient and comprehensive encyclopaedia around, Snow is the bitch of Spring [and circling dogs. –ed]. (The editor’s note is in dispute. See the talk page.) [fuck him. –ed] "But that's not even the point. The point is that Steven Caldera is a gossiping, whiny pussy, and that shit shouldn’t play here. What's his problem anyway? It's not like he’s replacing Rollins -- Rollins was already replacing Almasy. He can't find another substitute for Seymour Almasy? Shit. It's barely two months til' Christmas. Head down to the mall and give one of Santa's Helpers $50 and a cardboard sword. Problem solved." Vainly attempting to redirect Deville back to something relevant, we asked his opinions on Tony Gamble's apparent attempt to find in him a mentor, and Tchu's latest statements from ReVolution. "You'll forgive me if I don't know how to take Tony Gamble seriously. Who wants to develop a relationship with me akin to the one I had with The HIT? I fuckin' ruined that guy. As for Tchu . . . Am I the only one amused that he still hasn't returned to his little spot in the ring? And . . . ah, Tchu? For the love of sense, if you're going to address me, make at least a reasonable attempt at coherence. You can begin by explaining how it is that I'm the one obsessed with my past when you're the one who tried to act tough -- as PRIME Universal Champion -- by calling me out after I’d been gone nearly a year. Idiot."
