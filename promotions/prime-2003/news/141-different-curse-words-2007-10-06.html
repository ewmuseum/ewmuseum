141 Different Curse Words

6 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Has Nicholas Donnelly’s suspension been lifted? Not at all." Lisa Tyler said from the comfort of PRIME’s head office this past Friday. "He said he’ll be at ReVolution this week? Good, let him come. Cause just as quick he’ll be thrown out of the building. Suspended people are suspended for a reason, they aren’t going to do whatever they want to do." Earlier in the day, "The Ego of New England" gave a call to yours truly from his cell phone, confirming that he will be at ReVolution 141. When asked about possible repercussions due to his suspension, Mr. Donnelly was quite frank. "Suspension or not, I’ll be at that god damned arena. I have some thing’s to get off my f**kin’ chest and Lisa Tyler will listen one way or the other. I’ll be ready for whatever security she decides to throw my way. She’ll be lucky if she still has a job after I’m through with her." Earlier in the week, it was reported on the GCW website that Donnelly had met with GCW officials including Steven Caldera about a possible run in the company. Nitz’s contract with PRIME does not contain a no compete clause, therefore Donnelly is free to work with another organization. "Of course I met with GCW... what kinda f**kin’ question is that? It was reported that I was there, and I was. Nothing to hide and no sh*tty games to be played. All I’m gonna say is bring your damn umbrellas to the arena, cause sh*ts gonna hit the fan with Lisa." I would like to remind fans that parts of this interview were changed to remove possible derogatory remarks. In the actual discussion, Nitz did not refer to Lisa Tyler by her actual name. You can come to your own conclusions about what was said. Is Nitz ready to leave PRIME for GCW? Will he be removed from the arena before he has the chance to speak? Is Lisa Tyler going to be called out of her comfy office chair? It’s an already jam packed show for ReVolution 141, but one has to think Nitz will not just slip under the radar for this one. "I’ll be all over the f**kin’ map this week, bank on it." Replied The Ego. Place your bets.
