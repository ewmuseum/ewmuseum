King of Kings 2010 Injury Report

8 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME's final Pay-Per-View was a landmark, and gave the company another successful year if early buyrates indicate anything. However, with great reward also comes great risk, and a lot of the wrestlers on the roster dealt with injuries during the holiday break. Nitz Donnelly received a Grade 1 concussion and complained of a strained neck. He is expected to be fully healed when PRIME returns on February 1st. The Sentinel was heavily bruised and battered after his semifinal match with Desade. Nothing noteworthy, although no one can say for sure if he will appear on the February 1st show. Big Bear complained of left shoulder problems after a sneak attack from Devin Shakur, although follow up reports said he was fine after about a week and a half. He is expected to be ready and able to compete again on ReVolution 238. High Flyer suffered a Grade 3 concussion after a violent kick to the back of his head from Devin Shakur. He was unconscious in the ring and had to be stretchered out and brought to a nearby Phoenix hospital. He is doing better according to a recent follow up report, although no one knows when or if he will compete in a PRIME ring again. Alexandra Pierce suffered a Grade 2 concussion at an unknown point during the show. She also reaggravated a previous head injury, and also was seen with a hand on her neck. All reports indicate she's fine, but in all likelihood she probably wouldn't divulge information about an injury publicly. Violence Jack suffered a broken nose and lost a tooth during his match against Nitz Donnelly. During his match with Desade, a few cuts opened over his forehead and he received 18 stitches. He is expected to be at the February 1st show.
