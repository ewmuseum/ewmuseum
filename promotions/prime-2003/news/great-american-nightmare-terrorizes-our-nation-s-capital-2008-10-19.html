Great American Nightmare Terrorizes Our Nation's Capital

19 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Delta Upsilon Iota set out to regain momentum as they faced The Awakening and Union Jack & Jeeves. Their opponents hadn't been much of a factor in recent shows, but alternate plans by C.P. Cantrell (and DUI themselves) kept things lively. Troy Douglas and Jason Natas, two men who'd earned praise as potential breakout stars, met in the ring to detewrmine who was further along in their progress. Tag Team Champions the Princes of New England defended their newly-swindled belts against former champs (and their hated foes) Team V.I.A.G.R.A. Intense Champion Kaiser Vashaun, one of the hottest young names in the company, defended against a man who was equally impressive in his short PRIME stint, Rhett Locke. Cozen and Dusk finally settled their differences after a long, sprawling personal feud that saw the Lost Soul petition to have Cozen rehired just so he could tear her apart. In the main event, Universal Champion Chandler Tsonda defended against six challengers - Devin Shakur, Tony Gamble, Killean Sirrajin, Xavier Kannon, Bryan Dawkins and Tyler Rayne - who run the gamut of personal relationships. In only his second defense since winning the title, Tsonda had to face down some of PRIME's most talented and heralded superstars. It was all live from Washington, as PRIME finishes yet another contentious chapter in its long history. It's the Great American Nightmare, and at the end of the night, many PRIMEates wished it was only a bad dream.
