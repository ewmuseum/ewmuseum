Enemies At The Gates

27 July 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kicking off the night was another test, for Redeemed member, Tyrell Dawkins. With The Redeemed scheduled to compete for the Tag Titles at Colossus, Dawkins looked at a warmup against the ertswhile Meat. And while it wasn't the prettiest match, neither man wanted to lose pride points. Next was a potent triple threat, featuring three men who had fought valiantly at ReV 200. Troy Douglas, who nearly captured a win for his team during the elimination rounds, was set to do battle with David Noble and Jay Phoenix, each of whom competed in the night's final act, with Phoenix gaining an Intense Title shot to be redeemed in the future. With so much star power in the ring, and the night so young, this one was a barnburner, with major Colossus implications for one of the competitors. The first part of the night's sizzling co-main event had Lisa Tyler's fingerprints all over it. Garbage Bag Johnny was forced into competition against his ally Chainz, in a First Blood Match, no less. Would this shaky alliance break down or would these two miscreants stick one to Lisa Tyler? Well...no reason to spoil the surprise. And in the second part of the main event, Hessian faced an interesting test in Chandler Tsonda. The Murder Show has run roughshod over opponents, knocking down big names and small ones alike. In Tsonda, though, he was facing one of PRIME's true veterans, and a man with his own impressive set of big-match wins. In this special PRIME/GCW Flag-On-A-Pole Match, the two men did battle, each looking impressive. But the decision relied as much on a surprise appearance as it did on in-ring skill.
