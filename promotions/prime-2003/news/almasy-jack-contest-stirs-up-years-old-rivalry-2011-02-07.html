Almasy/Jack Contest Stirs Up Years Old Rivalry

7 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
While many fans eagerly await the official PRIME debut of Alexandra Pierce as a member of the full time roster on ReVolution 239, equally many wrestling fans are looking forward to seeing a contest between two men who have waged war off and on for nearly five years: Bruce "Violence Jack" Shanahan and Seymour Almasy. When asked about the contest following ReV 238, PRIME fan Dan Bennett said "It’s a ‘holy shit’ type of match. I can’t believe they’re giving it away for free, but I’m sure as Hell not going to complain." Sources within PRIME tell us that the Almasy/Jack and Pierce/Walczak matches anchoring the card may well serve as an effort by the company to boost ReVolution ratings coming off of a weaker match performance on last week’s show. Whatever the case, the contest pits two men against one another who have battled without ever seeing a decisive winner. Violence Jack targetted Seymour Almasy in the middle of 2005, following him to All-Star Championship Wrestling and assaulting him alongside Ethan Knight and Horace Tully, former members of the Pariah Saint's Sect of Black Wisdom. The two men met several times over the course of that year, with their only singles meeting being a twenty minute time limit draw. A month later, Jack redoubled his efforts, sending the mammoth Hound, championed by fellow Sect member Lady Hemlocke, after Almasy. Seymour managed to turn back the challenge of Hound, and Jack soon disappeared from ACW. Following the company's rebirth in 2009, Jack and Almasy once again renewed hostilities over the ACW World Heavyweight Championship, only for both to disappear once more before a resolution could be had. "For a lot of wrestling fans," PRIME commentator Nick Stuart said, "this is a dream match. For two guys of these stature to only have wrestled each other once, and never on national television…it’s pretty amazing to be able to bring this contest to our fans." Neither Jack nor Almasy could be reached for comment, but one can only assume that both men are preparing for this contest. A win for Jack would continue a stupefying run of success over some of PRIME’s top talent, while an Almasy victory would cement him as worthy of being in PRIME’s upper echelon. On paper, both men are evenly matched, with similar resumes and offenses chock-full of powerful bombs. Jack, of course, favors his impressive arsenal of neck-weakening moves to set up the vertebrae-shattering R’yleh Anthem, while Almasy debuted an impressive moonsaulting double kneedrop, dubbed the Paradigm Shift, last week against Tom Walczak. More news on this blockbuster contest should any arise.
