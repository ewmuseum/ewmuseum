Ferg's Folly Fans Flames For Faction Fray

8 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
During the promo in question, Ferguson insinuated that by not defending the belts regularly, he and Lindsay Troy were protecting them from "the peanut gallery", referring to the rest of the tag team division. Troy was noticeably annoyed by his comment during the promo. "I don't have a lot of experience running a wrestling company, but I have plenty of experience working for one, and something I know to be fact is that you can't get a division off the ground when your champion wants to belittle his competition," said Blair. He went on to discuss the recent additions of The Blue Rogues, Pranks With Attitude, the Spanglish United Xchange and now Das Korps and The Detonators as evidence of a strong commitment to the revitalized team division. With that, the intern-turned-BMOC declared that Ferguson and Troy would be making their third title defense just one week after their second, as he signed an eight-team, 16-man...uh, person battle royal for ReVolution 127. "If Danny believes he is so far above the competition in his division, then we'll see how he fares against them all at once," Blair stated. "With all due respect to Ms. Troy, while I mean no slight on her, she's well aware of the company she keeps." With the ongoing feud with Fuck You occupying most of Ferguson and Troy's time, their defenses are few and far between. Even after the announcement of the match, more questions loom ahead, as UltraViolence closed with the champions, alongside Tchu and Killean Sirrajin, laid out by the hands of FU. Will the champions even be in a state to defend at ReVolution? Participants in the 8-team Tag Team Championship Battle Royal on ReV 127: Danny Ferguson & Lindsay Troy (c) The Blue Rogues Captain Suleimon & Jack Murphy Pranks With Attitude Spanglish United Xchange The Detonators Das Korps The Fife Posse Over-the-top battle royal rules apply. When a man or woman is eliminated, his teammate must also leave the ring. The last team remaining will be declared tag team champions.
