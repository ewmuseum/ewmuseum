Chet Worth Tired of Antics of New PRIMEates?

18 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The problem lies in the way they do things. They understand that there is a limited amount of time to be on the air. They tell me every week 'Oh, sure Chet. We'll only be a few minutes.' And a half hour later, we've got sponsors pissed off because we haven't shown their commercials. "Another problem is... well, you saw it. A flamethrower? I don't even want to tell you how much it took for me to keep the show going on due to the fire safety issue of a damn flamethrower being brought to ringside, and being used. "Nova knows how these things work. He's one of my guys. Well, he used to be. Point is, there's one way, to do things, and that's My Way." Worth's problems with the group may be a direct result of the bad blood between Jason O'Neil and Angelo Deville. "Forget all that. I could care less what Jason's done to Deville and likewise. My problem is the lack of respect that each member of this new group has for us in the offices. They sure as hell don't have a problem taking our checks, but, when it comes to listening to what they need to do to get those checks, they're coming up short." What about the name of the group? "I don't care. I certainly won't call them that. PRIME Marketing won't call them that. And F/X had been willing to overlook the use of the name, since it usually happens after 11PM. But, not anymore. If they choose to censor the name... what can I do about it?" So, what's Worth going to do to solve the problem? "Simple. They want to come out and talk, they can do it to the live audience. I'll just put commercials in the middle of their segments. They don't want to be professionals? Well, neither will I." A response from "That Stable" will be coming as soon as Jeff reads this, I'm sure. Because he's a bastard.
