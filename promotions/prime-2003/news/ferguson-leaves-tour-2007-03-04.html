FERGUSON LEAVES TOUR?

4 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All wrestlers were expected to report to Guangzhou one week before the event to facillitate all promotional media appearances. After contacting the Narita Airport in Yokohama, from which the jet departed, reporters learned that Team Ferguson's flight plan listed Los Angeles as its destination, though no airport was specified. It is assumed that Ferguson's departure is directly related to Warner Brothers' recent greenlight of The Green Lantern, based on the comic book character of the same name, a project to which Ferguson has been linked since its inception. The decision to make the movie came abruptly last week, with no insiders having a clue as to its sudden revival after months of sitting on a shelf. Following a confrontation from Chet Worth at ReVolution 121 (preceding Worth's removal from power in PRIME), Ferguson immediately left Japan. Repeated attempts by the new regime to reach Ferguson were unsuccessful. Officials are concerned that, once setting foot on US soil, Ferguson will be held accountable for his current status as a federal fugitive. He has been listed as such since leaving America to join the world tour despite not clearing legal release forms. In December of last year, Ferguson was jailed under suspicion of spontaneously combusting fellow wrestler The Illustrious Face-Eater, who later proved to be alive. Reporters are working around the clock to locate Danny Ferguson or uncover any news as to his intention of abandoning PRIME on the eve of 2007's first big show, Culture Shock.
