Everything Comes Up Double In Twin Cities

1 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
They saw two tag matches, with tag title contenders Team VIAGRA challenging Delta Upsilon Iota and then the power team of Chandler Tsonda and Tyler Rayne taking on the odd couple team of Simply Beautiful and Logic. They saw Union Jack vs. Jimmy Bonafide II, as the first match was marred with controversy. They saw Crucifix go for his second straight victory after a jaw-dropping upset of Xavier Kannon last week. Finally, in the night's double main event, they saw two of the Universal Title match participants at UltraViolence take on major challengers in matches that featured guest commentary AND guest officiating from their opponents. It sounds like a bad Price Is Right game, but we assure you that it's much, much more violent than that...ok, at least slightly more violent. It's ReVolution on FX!
