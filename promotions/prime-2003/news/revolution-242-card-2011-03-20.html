ReVolution 242 Card

20 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inarguably, the biggest card of the PRIME year will take place on Thursday, March 24th from Abu Dhabi in the Sheikh Zayed Stadium. 55,000 fans will jam pack the building to witness three title matches and possibly an event hardcore fans of PRIME never figured they would see again. Opening Contest Lance Marshall Vs Bryan Dawkins 'The Lion' got off to a strong start in his PRIME career by defeating Brian Postal in a hard fought match at ReVolution 241. The Bruh on the other hand ran Christian Daniels out of the ring at 240, and found out that Shakur has sent Postal as a gun for hire to get him. One can assume both Shakur and Daniels will be watching as a longtime hated rival and new enemy will square off in the ring. Singles Match Alexandra Pierce Vs Brian Postal Brian Postal's concussion did him in against Marshall, but this week he gets the runner up in the Jewel in the Crown 2010, GTT7 winner, and possibly the best wrestler in the world in Alexandra Pierce. The newcomer was able to hang with Universal Champion Hessian in a strong outing, but has not been able to pick up a win. Can he pull off a jaw dropping victory and shock the world in Abu Dhabi, or will Pierce continue to roll over PRIME? Intense Championship Match Lindsay Troy (C) Vs Seymour Almasy It's no secret Lindsay Troy is not on top of Shakur's Christmas card list, and this was made evident when he put his newest "investment" Seymour Almasy as the man to try and capture the Intense Championship from her. The Queen has been rather quiet as of late, but it doesn't mean she's not a force to be reckoned with. Almasy is going to be looking for PRIME gold, but also has to be weary of Jacob McKail, a man who would love nothing better than to ruin his night and chances of capturing gold. 5*Star Championship Match Ladder Match Rules Nitz Donnelly (C) Vs Big Bear Another champion who has drawn the ire of the boss, but a match that was not made by him. Lisa Tyler booked a down in the dumps Big Bear into this contest, hoping to spark some life into the former wrecking machine. On 241, Shakur gave him a pep talk and while the results of it have yet to fully be seen, it should be very apparent when the bell rings and the belt is raised high above the ring. Main Event Old School Cell Match Universal Title Match Matt Ward Vs Hessian (C) Ward has made his intentions very clear ever since winning the Elite Championship and being its last official holder. He defeated Hessian in Round 1 of JITC to earn this bounty, and is finally going to get his chance at The Murder Show. Hessian has not been in the best frame of mind lately, so Ward could have an advantage even if the cell favors the big man. There will be no way to win other than via pinfall or submission as there is a top on the cage and no door to escape from. Plus, will there be a rumored I Quit stipulation thrown into the equation? Can Ward make history as the only three time PRIME Universal Champion? Also... -Amy Campbell, yes you heard that right, Aimz, The Red Raver, has been offered a contract by Devin Shakur to appear at ReVolution 242 on his dime, full display, and get camera time. The last appearance she was advertised for was ReVolution 80. The authenticity on the contract is real, but will Campbell sign and make her long awaited return for the first time in over 5 years?
