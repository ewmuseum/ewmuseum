ReVolution Hits San Antonio

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The evening started off with Devin Shakur emerging from the backstage area carrying over his right shoulder a belt which clearly didn't belong to him, the Universal Championship. The Boss in Black announced to the San Antonio crowd that Jason Snow had officially given his release and retired from PRIME, thereby leaving the Championship vacant. Shakur vowed to address the situation later in the evening after delving in his own personal matters. The opening contest featured a star studded lineup of PTC legends: Jared Sykes, Violence Jack, Back Alley Brawler and Brandon Youngblood all vied for the Elite Championship. The match will be memorable not for the back and forth false finishes or amazing counter moves four top notch wrestlers could provide, but rather as the resurgence of The Pariah. He might have been knocked down at Culture Shock, but Brandon Youngblood doesn't stay down for long. He dominated all three wrestlers handily and finished Violence Jack with the Half Nelson suplex to retain his Elite Championship. Next, a PRIME fan favorite made his return to the ring after a long suspension implemented by The Boss in Black. Bryan Dawkins sought to get back in the good graces of PRIME by taking on a man who came up just shy of gaining his second Championship in PRIME, Troy Douglas. The two battled back and forth with Dawkins displaying his high flying abilities and Douglas showing his improved striking and technical prowess. The end came when Dawkins missed the Hawaiian Hangover, leaving himself victim to The End of the Road and a Douglas victory. The sub Main Event of the evening would come as quite a shock to those only reading a recap of the program. The Wolves of Slaughter, PRIME's Tag Team of the Year in 2009, had an encounter earlier in the evening in which Elise Ares fired Kazys Jankauskas as her Tag Team partner. However, just because Ares fired The Iron Wolf did not mean he was fired from PRIME as a whole, as Lisa Tyler highlighted. She booked the two in a match, a "Loser Leaves PRIME" match. Everybody assumed they would be seeing the last of The Havana Harlot, but the fans waiting on that will have to hold their breath just a little longer. With a clutch distraction from Chainz, Ares was able to roll her former tag partner up and get the victory, thereby disposing of him from PRIME. An Intense Main Event was slated as Chainz had to defend his Intense Championship against the man who pinned him in the Dual Halo, Tchu. The Monster from Hell's Kitchen was nursing a dislocated jaw thanks to rival Hessian's post match assault, but the injury didn't seem to slow Chainz thirst for violence. He battled Tchu for a good eighteen minutes before the veteran was able to squeak out the win with a Downfall and become the new Intense Champion. To see all of these moments and figure out how Garbage Bag Johnny showed up in two places at once, check out ReVolution 221.
