Colossus Spotlight: Hessian

24 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A 7'2" Detroit native, Von Kelsig has two stints with PRIME under his belt. His first run in late 2006 yielded little in the way of positive results and failed to show any upside. When he returned at King of Kings 2008 and ruined Merry Melee for Fusenshoff, PRIME had no clue what they were in for. Hessian seized all of his massive potential and tore through any and all competition, ranging from victories over the likes of Tyler Rayne, Lindsay Troy, Killean Sirrajin, Garbage Bag Johnny, and Kaiser Vashaun. Jonathan Rhine was only capable of taking Hessian down after two months of excruciating back and forth at UltraViolence 2009, and even then The Murder Show wasn't fully down for the count. He bounced back to capture the Elite Championship at Night 1 of Colossus VI and defeat two Hall of Famers in Nova and Sonny Silver on Night 2. Since his dominance at Colossus VI, Kelsig has bounced around doing some mercenary work and aligned himself with Tyler Nelson before the intentions of Michael Sloan eliminated him from Nelson's Army. Colossus VII will showcase the blood feud with Sloan over the past year. When two monsters collide, the result is never pretty and nobody is expecting either man to smell like a rose after leaving San Diego. Von Kelsig explained what got him involved with the wrestling business in a recent phone interview, "There was no hereditary intuition geared for success, no aspirations of being a champion of the world." Kelsig paused to reflect before continuing, "No intention to become a legend. I was simply built for violence. PCW legend Vampir Nosferatu plucked me out of obscurity and planted me in the Primetime Wrestling Academy to begin a career in hurting people. I fulfilled his and the expectations of the world when they saw a giant with a club in his hand. Now they all know my name. And they will forever." Kelsig said he has found in home with PRIME and elaborated on his feelings regarding the company, "I have been loyal to each and every company which has been fit to house me. I only worked for the best, and I only got the best. After the demise of the WO era the only stand-out company, the only place with the environment, the atmosphere and the talent to lure me in was PRIME. PRIME is the spiritual successor to PCW, the only name that can generate a buzz like the old days, and it'll be my home until I retire... or..." Kelsig didn't finish the statement although implications can certainly be made about what would have been said. Kelsig also spoke confidently of his trumphs at Colossus, noting the victory over Desade and Wyatt Connors at Colossus VI, "Last year, I defeated two of the best in the world to bring the Elite Title home to PRIME... Colossus is a night of epic proportions and very few companies can match it in terms of its prestige. Things have a tendency to happen at Colossus that you'll be talking about many years later." While spotlights of Colossus won't be shining as brightly on Von Kelsig this year, his match against Chainz will be for the services of Tracy Sloan. Von Kelsig didn't comment about his unique stipulation, but was more than willing to break his opposition down. "When I arrived in 2009 and laid waste to everyone, there was little old Mikey Sloan stewing in his britches because finally he saw what a monster looks like. While he was clawing for attention from everyone in PRIME, I only had to walk through the door unannounced to be given the main event. I've spent the full decade of my professional career re-defining violence and extreme. I've said it before and I'll say it again; there's no more Ivan Stanislav, no more Xavier Kannon, no Jeremy Howard or Tyler Nelson. I am the face of the past, the present and the future. As long as I'm around people will have something to come and see." Kelsig continued, "Then you've got Chainz. He likes to think he's a monster, with his maniacal brain and his sick ways, but there's a difference between being a monster and a deviant. A 7'2", 355lbs difference. Chainz might be able to get into my head mentally...but I'm going to physically burst his skull. Like a balloon. That's the difference between us. He wants rid of me so the spotlight can be on the diluted version of Hessian, the softcore alternative to the Murder Show. Do people want the Beard of Doom or the little bald-headed monkey with the barbie doll? It doesn't matter what the people want. What I want...is for Hessian to be the name on everyone's lips, and Chainz to be nothing more than scrawled letters on a headstone." Hessian vs Chainz will take place live from Qualcomm Stadium in San Diego, California on August 29th.
