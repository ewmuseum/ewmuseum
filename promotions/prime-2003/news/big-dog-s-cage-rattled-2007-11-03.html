Big Dog's Cage Rattled

3 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The music industry and the wrestling industry are butting heads. Toby Keith, famous country singer world-wide, has issued a complaint with PRIME. More specifically, PRIME's Intense Champion, Wade Elliott. "For one thing, I have, and always will be, "The Big Dog," said Keith in a recent interview. "For another, The Ford F-150 is my endorsement. I ain't got much against Wade in general, but when it comes down to business, I've gotta cut ties with 'im." Which means the Drifter is in need of a new entrance theme, as Keith has plans to cancel PRIME's rights to his song, "Hit It." "Toby Keith can go f**k himself," said Elliott at a local bar. "I weren't the one callin' myself 'The Big Dog.' Call me 'The Bad Dog!' See if I give a s**t!. An' if his truck means so much to him, hell, I'll give my ride to some ass**** off the street an' go buy myself a Silverado." The Southern Sparkplug proceeded to down a tumbler of bourbon, surprise surprise. I asked him if he had a band in mind for a new entrance theme, to which he offered a big grin. "The band I shoulda been doin' business with in the first place," he replied. "The one an' only Lynyrd Skynyrd." Will PRIMEates now be witnessed to "The Bad Dog" Wade Elliott strutting to a different tune? The only place to find out is on FX, where The Great American Nightmare will air this weekend. Thomas C. Robinson, signing off.
