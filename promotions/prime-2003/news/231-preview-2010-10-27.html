231 Preview

27 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whenever two forces such as Tyler Rayne and Hessian are set to headline a card, a riot is the expected norm. For those in Manchester, New Hampshire, they better prepare for the worst after the happenings on ReVolution 230. There is a new boss in town, and an even crazier one out on the prowl. Devin Shakur went over the edge at ReVolution 230, attacking an intern, and giving 49 percent of his power away to former VP of Talent Relations, Lisa Tyler. He’s also seemed to lose his mind and is now on the verge of a mental breakdown. He also ate the fall in the crucial 8 person tag match against Tyler Rayne and has vowed revenge against all people in the ring. One rumor coming out is that Shakur has made it his mission to find out who sabotaged his chances at Colossus with the cage. He intends to interrogate members of the PRIME roster until he gets an answer. The Boss in Black is also slated to compete against The First, who is coming off a loss to Vangelus Olsig and won’t be thrilled if it becomes two in a row. Other talent scheduled to be at the show are: Hessian, Lindsay Troy, Tyler Rayne, Matt Ward, Tony Gamble, Vangelus Olsig, and Patterson Loch. ReVolution 231 comes live from the Verizon Center in Manchester, New Hampshire on October 13th at 9:00 P.M on HBO.
