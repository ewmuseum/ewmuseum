Last Taste of Home Cookin'

3 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To kick things off, Wicked Ways and Wicked Huge Prick Hunter Sabuani teamed up to take on Intense division newcomer Fusenshoff and The Union. In fairness, PHS was wearing a pretty hilarious t-shirt, but that still didn't excuse his in-ring actions nor his general demeanor for the night. In the night's second match, which surely could've main evented most other ReVolution broadcast, it was the final leg of the Intense Gauntlet. Kaiser Vashaun strolled to the ring just one win away from a resounding slap in the face of Lisa Tyler and Tyler Rayne, whose sole agreement in the history of ever was to put Vashaun and his Intense Title to the test. Of course, there was the (not at all) small matter of Hessian standing in his path. With a notable win against Tyler Rayne already under his belt, would the Murder Show continue his giant-killing ways? Speaking of the Murder Show, he seemed to simply have a masterful way with numbers in the preable to his match, making an impressive statement. This week's main event was literally months in the making. Whether it was Tyler Rayne and Jason Snow in the finals of the Jewel in the Crown, Rayne shattering Devin Shakur's ankle at Great American Nightmare, or Shakur trying to turn Bryan Dawkins against Chandler Tsonda prior to Colossus, there was a boatload of mutual dislike between the teams of Dawkins & Rayne and Shakur & Snow. And to compound things, some pre-match...ahem..."antics" shifted the complexion of the match. As to the question of who stood tall heading into Culture Shock, well...you'll want to see it for yourself. Elsewhere on the card, Jay Phoenix made his case as PRIME's Good Samaritan, Wicked Ways lived up to their nickname, Wade Elliott's mood stayed sour, and Duke Williams addressed his constituents. For all this, plus several big Culture Shock announcements, you'll have to lay your eyeballs on the official transcript.
