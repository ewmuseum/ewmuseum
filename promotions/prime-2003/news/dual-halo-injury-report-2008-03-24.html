Dual Halo Injury Report

24 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Among the reported cases after Night Two: - Nitz Donnelly was forced to withdraw from the Halo after suffering a ruptured knee tendon in his 5-Star Title match against Tyler Rayne at Night One. He is expected to be out indefinitely pending the results of surgery. - Chandler Tsonda suffered an undisclosed injury to his back over the weekend, although it could not be determined if the injury occurred in the Halo or during his Universal Title match with Lindsay Troy on Night One. Although Dr. Fihlguud advised that Tsonda take time off, Tsonda obtained a second opinion upon returning to the U.S., and will return to competition immediately. - Universal Champion and new PTC Unified Champion Lindsay Troy checked out with no physical damage, but was extremely fatigued given the marathon nature of her weekend. She was advised to take at least one show off to recuperate. - Former Intense Champion Tony "The Grin" Gamble suffered a shoulder injury of undisclosed severity. It's not yet known if he is expected to miss time. - Danny Ferguson was diagnosed with a Grade III concussion after falling from the second level of a Halo. He did not regain consciousness for nearly 20 minutes after being taken to the back, and was detained overnight in the hospital. He has yet to be cleared for travel and is expected to be out indefinitely. - The Freak, Himself was also diagnosed with a concussion following a Tony Rolo chairshot. The Grade I is expected to sideline him for an indefinite period of time. - Returning legends Tony Rolo, Killean Sirrajin and Xavier Kannon all reported no notable maladies, no small feat considering all three were making a return to the ring after lengthy absences. Other than some minor joint pain and residual soreness from the first match back (and from the moves that ended their Halo chances - the Hallmark, the Rebel Yell and the Terrible Lie, respectively), they were released to travel shortly afterward. - Several superstars refused medical attention, including Dual Halo winner Tyler Rayne and new Intense Champion Dusk. Doctors reported that Dual Halo runner-up Cozen did not report for treatment. In fact, she was unable to be found after the event.
