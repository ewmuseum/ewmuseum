Tchu Comments About Arrest of Killean Sirrajin

3 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"It’s hard to imagine." Ward said when reached for comment on the issue. A former friend, and more recently, arch nemesis of Sirrajin, it’s safe to say few individuals know ‘The Supreme Machine’ as well as Ward. One month ago at Colossus III, a match four years in the making finally occurred between the two rivals. Ward was able to capture the Universal title in an epic encounter that will go down in pro wrestling history. That was the last any PRIMEate has seen of the former champion. ReVolution 104 occurred under the absence of Sirrajin, and after a three week hiatus revolving around contract difficulties between PRIME and their network home, FX, Killean was arrested in the airport while attempting to board his flight to make ReVolution 105. When news broke, it caught everyone by surprise, even those who knew the Canadian star best. "The guy can be a real sonofabitch." Ward added, "And anyone who follows wrestling knows that we’ve had more than a few problems. But our differences aside, I can’t for one second believe he’s out there pushing drugs to kids and trying to kill people." Indeed, despite the serious nature of the drug charges, it is the rumors of attempted murder that are most disturbing to fans around the world "He has a job right here in PRIME where he can unleash all the anger and aggression his heart desires." Ward defended his rival. "The squared circle offers him the opportunity to release his rage in a perfectly legal, condoned manner. What sense does it make for him to go out there and get thrown in jail over any sort of violent offense? I’ve known this guy for years; he’s a lot of things… stupid isn’t one of them." Whatever the case, it appears that the tale of Killean Sirrajin has taken a very dark turn, and for at least the immediate future, his career seems to be on hold. "You hate to hear this kind of thing. You hate even more to think it’s true." Ward finished his thoughts on the subject. "Hopefully, this will all be cleared up soon. I may not be friends with the guy, but love ‘em or hate ‘em… he has pure talent. He belongs in the ring."
