Shakur Issues Stern Warning to Wrestling World: Don't Fuck With Me

6 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Early rating indicators for ReVolution 225 show PRIME still has life and plenty of it, but Devin Shakur, Christian Daniels, and Hessian standing over the broken body of Tony Gamble garnered the most buzz. Blogs and wrestling forums have gone into a frenzy attempting to deduce what exactly brought about the alliance. No one can seem to put all the pieces of the puzzle together and figure out why. Attempts to contact Hessian were unsuccessful and nobody knows how to contact Christian Daniels (bikers don't usually list their cell numbers), so reporters and wrestling enthusiasts wondered if the one person of the group who isn't afraid to speak his mind would bother commenting. Hearing the ruckus, Shakur called from his home office in Raleigh, North Carolina and left a voice message, one which will surely be the central topic on wrestling radio shows for the next few days. "Watch the first ten minutes of ReVolution 226. All three of us will be there. Maybe we'll explain the motivations and maybe we'll leave everybody guessing. All I will give you is this... The idiot fans of PRIME and moral hypocrites in the dressing room who figured I would crack under the pressure of more enemies being added to my list have been sorely mistaken. Now, it is each and every one of you who has been put on notice: Tyler Rayne, Lindsay Troy, Matt Ward, Garbage Bag Johnny, Elise Ares, Michael Sloan, Emilio Rage, and Tony Gamble. No, scratch that, anybody in the wrestling world is invited to walk through my door and try to get over at my expense. I am never a hard man to find and with Hess and CD by my sides, I will be even more accessible. Come on in. Snoop around. Talk shit. Try to beat me up. Bring your friends, families, hell, bring anybody off the street you can find to try and stop me now. What PRIME figured to be a nightmare was nothing more than a blissful dream. Now, the real nightmare begins. The real torture is just around the corner. I've got the best two man army in the world, and last I checked I could still kick a fucker's head off if need be. If you need a bottom line, I've got one for you: Don't. Fuck. With. Me."
