New Signings, Returning Faces Reshape Roster Post-Halo

23 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- After making perhaps the most talked about PRIME debut in recent times, the Dual Halo runner-up Cozen has put pen to paper on a fulltime deal. As a reward for her record-breaking performance for a PRIME debutant in the Dual Halo structure, Cozen will face the "Queen of the Ring" Lindsay Troy for the PRIME Universal Title at an upcoming episode of ReVolution. - After almost equalling Cozen’s massive accomplishment in his PRIME debut, David Cross, better known as Crucifix, has also been signed up to a fulltime contract. Having eliminated a giant haul of 11 participants in the Halo, Crucifix has been rewarded with a shot at the Halo winner and 5-Star champion Tyler Rayne in the near future. - Xavier Kannon’s appearance was not a one-off, and PRIME has confirmed that he has signed a fulltime contract, including a full house-show schedule. Kannon was last seen on the receiving end of a brutal beating from Jason Snow, Angelo Deville and Rich "The Renegade" Rollins that united the Fuck You faction over a year ago, but returned looking back to his best to secure 3rd place in the Halo. - After his current employer, the Boston-based Ringside Wrestling agreed to release him from his contract, "The Posterboy" Jimmy Bonafide has accepted a PRIME deal. The terms of his release are expected to leave the well-travelled, self-professed Drama King free to compete at this week’s ReVolution. - After being announced as "The Future Of Sports Entertainment" by Son-(Editor, under threat of violence: MR SILVER, CHAIRMAN OF PRIME)-ny Silver, Captain Justice has been confirmed as a regular on the PRIME roster, with Silver acting as his representative. - After catching a few eyes in the Halo, "The Flyin’ Hawaiian" Bryan Dawkins was offered a PRIME contract, and duly accepted. Dawkins is expected to show more of his risk-taking, high-flying style starting this Wednesday at ReV 154. - Also offered a PRIME deal was the Halo’s 8th place finisher, Troy Douglas. Being a servant of many feds that PRIME’s Universal Champion Lindsay Troy has frequented, Douglas will look to show PRIME that he has more than a name in common with one of its most successful stars of modern times. - And, saving the biggest for last, PRIME is proud to welcome back a pair of Hall of Fame superstars who are amongst the most decorated in the federation’s history - Tony Rolo and Killean Sirrajin. Between them, the pair have amassed 4 Universal, 2 Intense, 1 5-Star, 1 Alias and 1 Tag title reign, completing a comprehensive grand slam between themselves. And with both superstars surviving to the final 10 of the Halo, it appears both are returning at top form. PRIME management have not revealed if any further contracts were offered to other various guests that participated in the Halo, but commented that the new roster members did not mean that they are now closed to new talent.
