Colossus Spotlight: Matthew Ward

3 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ward was asked, via phone interview, on what brought him into such a backbreaking business, "Wrestling has been a beautiful release and a horrific crutch. Occasionally at the same time. There have been moments where it’s consumed me, moments where it’s been lost on me. And it’s been that way for ten years. Ten highly successful, brutally ugly years." Ward has seen the highest of highs, holding PRIME’s Universal Championship on two separate occasions, and being a former Tag Champion with Clint Poteet. He has also won a Jewel in the Crown, Dual Halo, and multiple awards. Not to mention he’s been both PTC Infinite Gauntlet Champion and PTC’s Unified Champion. Ward recently saw some of the lowest of lows. He couldn’t catch a break, having to endure the wrath of his boss both in and out of the ring. It wasn’t until Ward got caught in a war of words with Dan Ryan that a sense of freedom and Ward’s true colors emerged. Ward commented on what PRIME, as a company, meant to him, "PRIME has been the pinnacle of my career… home to my greatest achievements and my greatest failures. It was where I truly made my name and earned so much of the fame and riches that I’ve garnered in my career. Being named to the Hall of Fame was the greatest moment in my ten years as a part of this business. PRIME gave me the platform to go from superstar to legend, and through ups and downs, even a stint as the self-proclaimed anti-PRIME, I like to think that I’ve become a foundation to this federation the way it has been a foundation to my career. When I retire, I want people to hear "Tchu" and think "PRIME", and I want people to hear "PRIME" and think "Tchu"" Ward has been involved in monumental moments at Colossus: III saw him capture his second Universal Championship against Killean Sirrajin in a Co-Match of the Year for 2006. IV saw Tchu tear down Safeco Field with Titan St. James in a match many believed to be his last, but Ward couldn’t keep himself away from PRIME and its spotlight. While he hasn’t competed on PRIME’s grandest stage since, he has been prominently featured in promotion for Colossus VII and has achieved a second to last match on the card. Ward gave Colossus as a stage and spectacle its due respect when asked, "There is no doubt about it, Colossus is something different, something bigger, something far more grandiose in scope. On the Colossus stage, careers can be made in a single moment. Wrestling in front of nearly a hundred thousand fans… it fills you with this adrenaline that is unmatched. Under that spectacular spotlight, I’ve held a Universal title high above my head, clutched with bloodied hands, and felt the rush of immortality. And under that same light, I’ve been driven through the canvas, broken in half, and felt the gut-churning pain of failure. And those moments, good and bad, stand out above all others because Colossus is bigger than anything you can imagine. It is the pinnacle of any road a wrestler travels. I’ve been there twice already… main evented in front of 75,000 screaming fans. I could go a thousand times and it would still be special, still be bigger than everything else. It’s been three years since I’ve participated at Colossus as anything more than a spectator. With knots in my stomach, I’m craving that opportunity to stand in that spotlight again." His match against Lindsay Troy is more personal than any other match. It could be more personal than any other match in Ward’s career. Ward and Troy have been through thick and thin in their PRIME careers but ever since Troy’s return at UltraViolence 2010, a fork was wedged in their once iron friendship. After initial confusion, both sides made their thoughts well known and are ready for battle. The fact Ward has to defend his Intense Championship hasn’t been brought up much, as defeating one of PRIME’s most iconic figures is solely on his mind. Ward wouldn’t provide in depth commentary about Troy, deciding to focus mostly on their past, "Lindsay Troy… I dunno what to say. A month ago, I would have told anyone who would listen that she was my best friend in the business, jesus… one of my best friends in life. Period. There’s a million things we have in common. We fought together in PRIME, teamed up to lead the charge against FU. We hit the bars together for a couple beers after shows. Away from PRIME, we cooked-out and shared stories, talked and laughed for hours. She knows my wife and children, has bounced little Georgie up and down on her knee. It’s so god damn funny how fast things can change in this business." Matthew Ward Vs Lindsay Troy will take place at Colossus VII for the Intense Championship in Qualcomm Stadium.
