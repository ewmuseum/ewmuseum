Another Night for the Books

5 May 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opening the evening was a Tag Team contest featuring one half of the UltraViolence Four Corner contest in Meat and Skillet against Nine and Rhett Locke. The former are no strangers to one another, locking up at 192 with Meat coming out victorious. Nine and Rhett Locke are enigmatic individuals in their own right, and as a tag team they were even more mysterious. Could the rivals turned temporary allies be able to overcome PRIME's Pythagorean and Albino or would eventual confrontation distort their chances of victory? In the first singles contest of the evening, rising star David Noble took on 'Megatron' Troy Douglas. Teaming up to defeat Chainz and Kaiser Vashaun on 192, these two were well versed in each others style and tendencies. Did any of their wide array of adversaries elect to involve themselves in the match? Would the rookie be able to take down the former Intense Champion and pad his stock, or would Megatron be too much for the cool headed superstar to overcome? Epitomizing an awkward contest, PRIME's resident monster and five tooled sicko, Chainz, squared off against King Blueberry. The Prince of Produces was coming off a rather confounding loss against Bryan Dawkins in a Fruit on a Pole match at 191 and Chainz ate the pinfall against Troy Douglas on ReVolution 192. Which individual would emerge victorious and place themselves back on the winning track? In the sub-main event of the evening, number one contender to the Intense Championship Jonathan Rhine found himself against The Anti-Superstar, and consummate prick, Jason Natas. One man had a psychologically suave night and the other was plagued with problems from the opening. Would the martial artist beat the brawler, or would Natas pummel his way into the win column? The Main Event took PRIME fans back to the tail end of 2007, more specifically the Jewel in the Crown finals. A true classic if there ever was one. The tables weren't exactly in the same places with Wade Elliott now being the resident villain, and Chandler Tsonda being in a world of back pain. What would Tsonda be forced to wear in order to compete? How would he be able to handle the Bad Dog after enduring a mysterious beatdown? Did Elliott rectify his Jewel in the Crown loss or did The Model Citizen defy the odds one more time and reign supreme? In-ring action doesn't always define PRIME and this show didn't stray from the herd. Lisa Tyler seized control of her show. Tyler Nelson attempted to squirm his way into an elite club. A mysterious attacker stalked Jonathan Rhine and the Center for Disease Control made a surprising appearance. The only way to find out the answers to all of the aforementioned inquiries and tidbits is to check out ReVolution 193 .
