Development Not Really Developing

14 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A call to Devin Shakur's home didn't provide much new insight, "I'm stuck in an FTOPs event and I'm trying to teach Sunny she doesn't need to sit on the porch with a shotgun and shoot all the mosquitoes herself." A call to Lisa Tyler's office provided a slight bit of comfort for the curious, "All of our talent, excluding our esteemed boss, has arrived in Nebraska and everyone should make at least one appearance on camera." Tyler dodged questions relating to matches, along with the statuses of Skylar Montgomery and Nitz Donnelly. Both competitors are very injured and with most talent balking at ruining their Colossus paydays one show before the big dance by wrestling, 229 is likely to run less than two hours. Montgomery is becoming one of the more injury plagued stars, having been launched from the PRIME*View by Tyler Rayne on 227 and suffering an undisclosed neck injury courtesy of Castor Strife's Director's Cut finishing maneuver at 228. A source close to the medical staff said he would be surprised if Montgomery was able to wrestle at Colossus and questions him appearing on 229 at all. Donnelly was beaten at the hands of Ares and former tag partner Vance Raymes at the tailend of 228. Shakur ordered his one time associate to wrestle on 229 whether he received clearance or not. Donnelly said he would honor the agreement and nothing has been released from his camp indicating otherwise. The storyline expected to carry 229 is Matthew Ward's (better known as Tchu) full fledged heel turn. No one knows the entire motivations and Ward left a shocked audience at 228. He is scheduled to explain his actions at the top of the show. All this and more as ReVolution 229 goes live Sunday, August 15th on HBO from the Qwest Center in Omaha, Nebraska.
