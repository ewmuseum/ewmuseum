PRIME Finalizes Colossus V Card

5 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The total, non-stop action begins on Saturday night as several bitter feuds come to a head. Arguably the more emotional or intense night of the weekend, Night One threatens to leave Charlotte in shambles before Night Two even gets underway: - Jason Natas' ongoing issues with Delta Upsilon Iota will be resolved in a six-man match, as Jimmy Bonafide joins the frat boys and Natas is flanked by the unlikely posse of Beef and El Janito, the inimitable Mega Job. - Intense Champion Troy Douglas defends his belt and his honor against Kaiser Vashaun, the heralded rookie who considers himself the man to beat for the title. - Hall-of-Famer Tony Rolo meets Tony "The Grin" Gamble in a grudge match that bubbled over from a personal dispute that had been simmering. - Killean Sirrajin's mission to clear his name from false charges of HGH use faces its final hurdle: beating the man who helped create those false charges, Captain Justice (with Mr. Silver, Sports Entertainment Liason to PRIME, waching intently from the corner). - Former Universal Champion and Hall-of-Fame inductee Nova returns to a PRIME ring for the first time in over a year to meet tabloid news personality Torres Wilson after Wilson sought to drag the Risen Star's name through the mud. - Tyler Rayne defends the 5-Star Championship against Dusk as the two men also resolve personal differences that have been burning for months. - In the main event, the self-professed "King Of Wrestling," Xavier Kannon will be forced to answer for his sins against former Sin City Championship Wrestling Universal Champion Jonathan Rhine as the two bitter enemies meet in a Last Man Standing match to settle their issues once and for all. Assuming that BoA Stadium is still standing after Night One, Night Two promises to swoop in an decimate Charlotte a second time over with a collection of dream matches that have had fans buzzing for weeks. Like Night One, the second show features the resolution of months and months of disputes and personal issues, but Night Two also features a celebration of the past as the 2008 class of the PRIME Hall of Fame is inducted. In between, however, it promises to have an undeniable impact on PRIME's future: - After going at it for weeks, Union Jack and "The Flyin' Hawaiian" Bryan Dawkins will finally meet to put their problems to rest. - In a special attraction match, former OSW World Heavyweight Champion Rhett Locke takes on former LoC superstar Malik Roland. - The much-hyped tandem of Simon Knox and Connor O'Reily, the Princes of New England, make the most of their whirlwind arrival on the PRIME tag-team scene with a title shot against High Flyer and Tony Davis, the incomparable Team V.I.A.G.R.A. - The finals of the prestigious Elite Championship Tournament will determine the first-ever title-holder. New Frontier Wrestling Superstar Steve Knox, brother of PRIME superstars Simon Knox and Captain Justice (aka Soren Knox), will meet former PCW Rising Star Champion, former AWC Frontier Champion and current Global Championship Wrestling superstar Andy Murray. - "The Queen of the Ring" Lindsay Troy will finally meet Cozen, her antagonist of the past five months, in a second singles match, a rematch of the infamous Universal Title contest that triggered the fiery feud between both women. - In the main event, Universal Champion Devin Shakur makes his second defense as he meets Jewel In The Crown tournament winner and #1 contender Chandler Tsonda. Colossus V promises to me a memorable weekend for PRIME and wrestling fans. With all this action planned, all eyes are sure to be on Charlotte to see how one of the biggest shows in federation history plays out. You can catch all the action on prime.e-wrestling.org starting Saturday night! Lindsay Troy vs. Cozen Elite Championship Match - Andy Murray vs. Steve Knox Main Event: Universal Title Match - Devin Shakur (c) vs. Chandler Tsonda Note: A previously scheduled Crucifix/Logic match has been cancelled due to an injury suffered by David Walter Smith (aka Logic) during training.
