New York State of Mind

3 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To start the night off with a bang, Lisa Tyler put her foot down, forcing two of the company's biggest superstars to sign a nifty little contract. And just like that, we already had a headlining match for Great American Nightmare. Minutes later, Tyler had to watch one of PRIME's premier talents take his show on the road (at least for now). And this was only the first ten minutes of the show! To no one's suprise, Tony Gamble and Devin Shakur were up to their usual antics. But Shakur put everyone on the roster on notice in a VERY unique way. At the same time, another one of PRIME's rising talents was nowhere to be seen. And speaking of PRIME young bloods, there was a Brandon sightning. Where and why? Well, just ask Jonathon Winters. Jason Snow's mindstate after DDT? Iffy. He competed with the aforementioned Winters, but then had a strange request for Lisa Tyler. You REALLY have to see this one to believe it. Fellow Roulette competitor Titan St. James also showed up, in one of the last places you'd ever look for him. And oh right, it might mean something that a big freakin' name from the past showed up. A big freakin' name. All this was even BEFORE Wade Elliott tested his mettle against Sonny Silver in the main event. To see what happened, WHY things at the top of the card are a wee bit testy, and everything else that PRIME could conjure up, check out ReVolution 140!
