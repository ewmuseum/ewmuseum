What The...?

7 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CEO Chet Worth - despite all the craziness surrounding his professional and personal life in lieu of the F-U stable - is said to be very pleased with the new additions. He's been a big fan of their work ranging from the work ethic of the likes of Pierce Lavelle and Jack Murphy, the charisma of Captain Suleimon and the ratings that a controversial figure like Chainz brings in. However, there is one man on the roster that sees these inclusions as nothing more than, quote, "a few pimples on the bare ass that is Planet Earth." That being the man ostentatious individual claiming to be Chairman of PRIME, Sonny Silver. "I told you all it was going to be a matter of time before that stupid neon orange blight on the Internet known as ACW or whatever it was called bit the dust," Silver barked. "Then I swooped in, had my way with its corpse, then crumbled that (censored)hole of a federation beneath my boot before scattering it into the four winds. …Oh, I took their only good talent, too. Suleimon's a good talker and he'll kill himself doing one of those twirly-whirly BS moves on PRIME TV, so that's a plus. Jack Murphy and Pierce Lavelle are technical wrestlers from what I understand, so our b(censored)y internet fans will get a chub off them. And that other guy… uh, Chainz… I thought I fired that guy with the rest of the DOA like WAY back in '98 or something?" After correcting Silver and telling him that Chainz was none other than the highly iniquitous Michael "Chainz" Sloan, he yelled at me to get out of the room, pack up my (censored) and look for a job over at that Wuh-We place. …I'm not talking to him anymore.
