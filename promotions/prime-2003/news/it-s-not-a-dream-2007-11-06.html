It's Not A Dream...

6 November 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The joint project of PRIME's "The Great American Nightmare" and GCW's "Devil's Night" has hit the air. This behemoth of a show refused to disappoint, as warriors clashed within a four posted ring at T.D. Banknorth Arena, Boston. Those in support of Global Championship Wrestling had their fun, witness to monumental show-downs in their brand. Banned & Exiled and the Saints of Perdition, Choosers of the Slain and Oblivion, Jay Terror and Jordan White and the newly formed Establishment of Lestatt Knight and Rich 'The Renegade' Rollins. This was not an invasion, but grudges were settled between these monstrous federations. Five Star Champion Jonathan Winters stood face to face with Shaman, Nitz Donnelly and Dorian Ryan had nasty words to settle in the ring, Hoyt Williams had plans to shed light on Peter Vetra, The Illustrious Face-Eater and Kimbusa had more than a bone to pick, and be sure not to forget the show's first performance, where Tony 'The Grin' Gamble took to blows with Vivica J. Valentine. But PRIME had its own demons to face. Tag Team Champions Vangelus Olsig and Machaivelli St. Romani stepped out of the shadows to put their belts on the line against the gold thirsty Sound and the Fury. Chandler Tsonda braved the mammoth Titan St. James in an earth-shaking presentation. Jonathan Winters took a second stroll in the squared circle, his new Five Star Title up for grabs against the bull-headed Brandon Youngblood. And Jason Snow stared down the monster known as Chainz in a blood bath to settle their ever intensifying score. And of course, the three against three. The terrifying triplets of Tony 'The Grin' Gamble, Devin Shakur, and Sonny Silver charged the stalwart trio of Intense Champion Wade Elliott, Danny Ferguson, and Universal Champion Lindsay Troy. Gamble and Elliott found themselves tied together in a Dog Collar Match for The Bad Dog's belt, as Wade looked to shut PRIME's funny man up for good. Devin Shakur and Danny Ferguson looked to bury their fists into one-another's heads in singles competition to settle their own score. And to close out this frightening night, a war for the Universal Championship. One last time. Lindsay Troy. Queen of the Ring. Universal Champion. Sonny Silver. PRIME's Loose Cannon. Would be King. Matchups of epic proportions. Warriors laying it all on the line. Could the righteous few of PRIME stand tall, thwart their evil foes, and live the champion's dream? ...or have we dozed away into a nightmare? It's here.
