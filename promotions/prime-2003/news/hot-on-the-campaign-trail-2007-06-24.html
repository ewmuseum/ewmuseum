Hot on the Campaign Trail

24 June 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After arriving at 5098 W 12th Street via shared cab with the new Lobo’s head coach Steve Alford - who was later inexplicably found in his office, stapled to the ceiling, and cocooned in plastic wrap with Sharpie all over his face – one of PRIME’s newest talents, ‘Big Bear’ Munson Monsoon, engaged in consuming alcohol, playing drinking games, and ultimately; riling up his new friends into such a frenzy that they massive group of students wanted to let the people of Albuquerque know just who was going to win the 5-Star Challenge on the road to Colossus IV. "I’ve got the opportunity of a lifetime. After joining PRIME about a month ago, I haven’t seen a win yet. I’ve found myself tossed and thrown all over that ring, and it was starting to get me down. But not anymore, not with Colossus IV just six weeks away; not with the 5-Star Title on the line," said Big Bear, marching at the head of the student body, numbering well over 250 young Lobos. "So we’re gonna start this thing here; from Albuquerque to Seattle, balls out; foot on the floor the whole way. I’ve got my buddy, Cabby here, to do the drivin’," said Big Bear, who was flanked by a young woman and a cab driver with a blonde fro, known as Cabby. Big Bear is still fresh in PRIME, though suffering three tough-to-take losses. The mob of students finally began to settle down after making their way to The Pit, the University’s basketball arena, and also the same place where this Wednesday’s ReVolution 131 will take place. After Police showed up in riot gear, they barricaded off the students and proceeded to use the breathalyzer on them and hand out 174 underage drinking tickets. Afterward, the APD decided to spray them all down with a fire hose, to which the students’ response was a different chant: F[censored]K! THOSE! CHUMPS! The PRIME superstar, however, managed to escape charges: "The loyalty of these PRIMEates shows that this campaign has started. Not some campaign where I’m asking you to believe in me, oh no. The kind where the actions are the words, the results are the proof, the pictures are the propaganda; no tin pins or bumper stickers, no slogans, not even the sacrifice of a baby goat. Only a promise, from the beginning to the end; to win this thing… by a landslide."
