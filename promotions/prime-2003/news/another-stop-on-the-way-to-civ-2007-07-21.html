Another Stop on the Way to CIV

21 July 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Captain Suleimon out, Tracy Stanton in as host of her very own talk show on Revolution, though what stipulations did Lisa Tyler place on the show that made Miss Stanton so upset? People continued pushing towards CIV with Tyler Rayne, Nitz Donnelly, Tom Walczak, among others all making appearances throughout the night. A title match was announced for CIV that will bring two hated individuals together to end their rivalry. Who are the men involved and why is this match predicted to be a show stealer? Fresh from their debut the week prior, the Upstarts once again found themselves against stiff competition, did they prevail? The madness that is Pierce Lavelle, Jonathan Winters, Devin Shakur, Dusk, and Tony Gamble continued. With these men around nothing is out of the question. To what lengths did they go this night. Three promising newcomers squared off in the center of the ring as Tyler Rayne, Tom Walczak, and Nitz Donnelly met in a triple threat match. What’s brewing between Lindsay Troy, Danny Ferguson, and Nova? Also, what was the Universal Champion doing helping Wade Elliott out and who is the man going to face at Colossus? Did an assault by Chainz and Jason Snow succeed in crippling Easton Hall or just pissing him off? A Main Event with all the right ingredients; a former Universal champ, a pothead, and hatred to spare. Who won the main event between Nova and Facey versus Danny Ferguson and Chandler Tsonda. An ending you won’t believe and have to see for yourself. All this and much more on our path towards Colossus as Revolution 134 airs. You KNOW you wanna check, so it by clicking HERE.
