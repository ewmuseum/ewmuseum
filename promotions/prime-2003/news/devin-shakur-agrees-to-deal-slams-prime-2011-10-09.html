Devin Shakur Agrees to Deal, Slams PRIME

9 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
UFC 136 wrapped up with a shocking knockout by Frankie Edgar over longtime rival Grey Maynard. Chael Sonnen won by submission over Brian Stann, but neither of those stories are what is sweeping YouTube at the moment. Two of the people attending were Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels, not seen in public since July 31st. They seemed enthusiastic about the fights and each pulled for their own dogs in certain bouts. No one can say for certain, but it appears they both came out ahead on the evening, specifically with the main event. Yours truly was much higher in the building but managed to work his way down and locate Shakur and Daniels while they were looking to make a hasty getaway. The intimidating presence of Daniels, despite his celebrity, had many weary and most opted not to approach the giant or his younger brother. It was a rare instance to catch them out together and I felt it required persistence. I followed them deep into the parking lot. A lot deeper than I should have gone given the lighting structure and Daniels’ known tendency in adhering to NCIS star Leroy Jethro Gibbs’ Rule #9: Always carry a knife. My first few attempts were thwarted. I was swatted away like a fly by Daniels and knocked to the ground the third time I approached. It didn’t look as if a word was going to be spoken by either man other than the muttered obscenities escaping their mouths each time after Daniels palmed my head. On my fourth attempt, I maneuvered around Daniels and got to see Shakur. With the notable exception of bags under his eyes, he looked the same. When I asked him about PRIME and its dealings, he marched forward in silence. It took bringing up the name Lisa Tyler to stop and receive his well-known death stare. Daniels encouraged him to let it go and get in the car, but Shakur gave himself a few feet of separation and flipped the keys to his older brother. What he said, I immediately posted online and it has already received 400,000 hits on YouTube. I’m thankful my camera phone was able to capture the rage in his eyes and the visceral tone of his voice. The following is a word for word transcript of Shakur’s remarks. Please note the language is highly offensive and this should not be read by children. It also does not reflect the views of PRIME or its employees. "You want a comment about PRIME? Fuck that place. I spent five years. FIVE YEARS. A sixth of my life in that shithole and you wanna know the bottom line? There’s no character to me. There’s no enhanced version of me that people see. I’m not ramped up to the nines when a red button is on and toned down once production stops. I’m not a fucking windup toy full of energy for however long they need me. I’m an asshole. I’m a prick. I fucking hate everybody in that locker room." "I had to play by the establishment’s rules to get to the top. I had to sit back and fucking job to Dusk just because he was on a team with Nova and they had to make Gamble look good by fucking having me take the goddamn fall. I was the weak link on our team even though we should have fucking won. They wanted Nova strong and that piece of shit Dusk tagged along. I held my tongue but I was absolutely disgusted." "Sonny Silver, you want to talk about a guy who couldn’t run with the goddamn ball. He fumbled it worse than Ronnie Brown last week. He over thought everything and could only get a 3 star match with Lindsay Troy! I had to play second fiddle to that faggot for months before he decided to tuck his tail between his legs and run." "And Lindsay Troy, you want to talk about hypocrites? She’s got to be the most robotic wrestling personality ever. Watching her is like watching the Kardashians: It’s got a nice exterior appearance to some but there’s next to nothing going on inside. You know how she came up with all those stupid quotes you see on the website? People made them up for her. Yeah, fun fact, she can’t make up her own jokes. In promos, she was guided by us. We set her up to make her look unstoppable and funny when in fact she’s more boring than watching paint dry… or Danny Ferguson wrestle." "Speaking of him, my God could you find a bigger dumb shit than him. There’s a reason the field lapped you and it wasn’t because Tyler Rayne fucked you up. It’s because you couldn’t take care of yourself on the fall. It was your own arrogance and stupidity that put you out of the business, not Tyler Rayne’s "aggression." Playing that angle was agonizing to watch because Rayne and Troy have the chemistry of a straight to DVD couple." "And Chandler Tsonda? Jesus Christ, I about fucking took my ball and went home when they asked me to come into Colossus and job to him. You want to talk about having your tongue on management’s dicks? Look no further than that guy. There’s no way I should have done the job when I was big enough to get Colossus to draw in my fucking state. How many people have that kind of power? I can count them on one hand. It was my night and I was made to look like shit after being built up as probably the weakest champion in history. Some little girl with a fetish complex out of a Kill Bill movie almost beat me twice. Seriously? I would knock Cozen the fuck out in five seconds if we met on the street." "But you want to know what was really painful about that shithole? The ungodly contract Rayne set up for himself. The only reason, Tyler, and I really hope you see this because we all know you don’t have the balls to come back and face me one more time, you are half, no, A QUARTER OF THE STAR YOU ARE BECAUSE MY BROTHER MADE YOU. Not this one, not the one about to knock this fucker’s head off for running us down, but Chris. He made you. Remember when you tried to pull off that new guy bullshit in GHW? I do. That’s how creative you are when you come up with your own ideas and people like US can’t run the show how we’re supposed to. The fact is Tyler, without the mask, you are nothing more than a porn star wannabe. Yeah, you can give women an orgasm by looking at them in that special way, but you can’t wrestle a goddamn lick in the ring and forced everybody in the back to play you up as a star when you got called to win the Halo. I hope to God you die a slow painful death like a virus is slowly eating away at your body because for all the people you’ve fucked in your life, you deserve nothing less. " "Know that I took pleasure in breaking your legs. It wasn’t called for. It wasn’t planned. I saw the "what the fuck is this shit" going on in your eyes when I did it. And I loved it. I l cherished watching you try to fight out of it. I caught so much shit for it backstage. Wanna know another fun fact? They fired me that night. Yeah, because I put one of the second tier stars down, they had me on the chopping block. I had more offers than I knew what to do with, but they reconsidered a few days later, told me that I could up my own contract and start writing my own rules. Creative control. Told me to keep it on the hush. Nobody else offered that so I jumped on it." "You know what I did next. I broke Bryan Dawkins neck. That wasn’t called for either. He was supposed to go over in that match, but let’s face it: We both know that little shit deserved nothing more than mid-card. HE WAS A FUCKING MID CARDER WHO LAGGED ONTO RAYNE AND TSONDA. Do you know what the main event should have been at that show? Snow vs. Shakur. That was the match people should have seen. Dawkins vs. Shakur? When I’m creatively trying to make him look like a threat in the back? Coaching him. My God, you are fucking kidding if you think Dawkins could hold a candle correctly for longer than ten seconds without fucking up." "Then finally when nobody else could, I stepped in. I finally took the controls. I fucked over whoever I wanted. Vangelus Olsig winning the Halo? How’d that feel Youngblood? Snow? You guys enjoy jobbing to him? I sure as hell loved watching it. You guys both didn’t come back and we STILL survived. I was ready to put this company down then, but I wanted to keep going. It was an obsession to keep going, to keep sticking the knife across everybody’s throats." "But suddenly, strange shit started to happen. People wanted to come and work for me. Yeah, I paid them more, but they knew what was gonna happen. I wasn’t going to protect them in the ring. If I hated them, I’d try to end them. And finally after so long, I said "you know Dev, I like to call myself that when nobody else is around, why are you still doing this? Just fucking end this stupid charade and go home." "So that’s exactly what I did, and now you people want it back. Lisa is trying to negotiate a deal?" I was hesitant to respond. God knows what he’d say next, "She says it needs your final approval." "Good God they really want PRIME back? For the five shows and Colossus? Is that what you people want? You want to see more train wreck wrestling on a network who’s biggest claim to fame was Sex and the City? I’m a Charlotte by the way. Oh, fuck Wade Elliott too. Big lumbering asshole claims to have three victories over me when I made him. If anybody really believes he can hold shit to me in the ring, on the microphone, or in the back they are DEE-LUSION-AL. He rides a truck like a surfboard, shows how great of a mind he’s got, huh?" "OK so you people want your PRIME back? You want me back on top of you like Sloan in a whorehouse? Fine. You got it back. We’ll be back. We’ll give you what you want and I’ll do what nobody in the history has ever done, kill PRIME on its biggest stage." He looked at me with a tad of relief showing through on his weary face, although he’d never admit it. "You’ve got enough quotes to last a millennium now get the fuck out of my face. Let’s see how long this shit takes to blow up YouTube." With that, he walked away. Daniels smacked him hard in the back of the head and the two got into Shakur’s Ferrari and drove off. Even though I got this exclusive, I feel it is my obligation to apologize if anybody was offended by the language Devin Shakur used. As stated earlier, PRIME does not condone this language or bashing of its employees. Shakur acted of his own accord.
