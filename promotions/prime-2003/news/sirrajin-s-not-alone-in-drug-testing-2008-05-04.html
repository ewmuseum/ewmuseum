Sirrajin's Not Alone in Drug Testing

4 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"As per PRIME's testing policy," the Pensive Punisher told us, "I was selected to be randomly tested, free of suspicion for performance-enhancing drugs. I have no issue with such testing, and in fact welcome the opportunity to prove that my nearly three-hundred pounds of muscle is the result of hard work and good genetics, unlike certain members of this roster." When pressed further, Logic readily admitted his disgust at Sirrajin's positive test. "He (Killean) has ruined it for all of us. He has become the Rafael Palmeiro, the Floyd Landis of this company. No longer can we go out and perform without the taint of illicit activity clinging to us. As one of the largest men on this roster, that suspicion will naturally fall on me above others; hence how glad I am to have been chosen for this test." Vowing a negative result, Logic has taken his comments one step further, and reportedly asked C.P. Cantrell for a match against the former PRIME Universal Champion. "Performance-enhancing drugs or not," Logic stated, "I am the Thinking Man's Grappler. I do not need such substances, nor will I lose to an athlete who has cheated himself, the other stars of PRIME, and our fans all over the world. I will defeat Killean Sirrajin in the middle of the ring, and there will be no excuses." Meanwhile, we caught up with Killean after the show was over, who was still feeling the effects of the Good Times, Painful Memories that guest official Devin Shakur clocked him with. With an icepack on the side of his head, The PRIME Choice had this to say. "I just got kicked in the head by a man who was supposed to be a ref so needless to say, Logic is not the first thing on my mind. I beat Shakur fair and square in the center of the ring a few weeks back and he just couldn’t accept that. So he fuckin’ counts me out. ‘Sa bunch of bullshit!" Killean threw the icepack across the room, smashing it against the wall. Favoring his neck possibly due to the Piano Wire, he addressed the more pressing issue. "Logic had to take a drug test; it’s just the way it is in PRIME. But if I’m correct, his test results will be compiled alongside my own. This is just another supposed ‘action’ taken by Cantrell and the Board to show that nothing will screw up. I still hold true that I have never taken anything. Logic decides to open his mouth, so I’ll shut it for him. You don’t waltz into PRIME and challenge Killean Sirrajin like you own the joint. The nearly three-hundred pounds of muscle is offset by the ten cent brain occupying his thick skull. I’m not about to respond to his comments regarding my alleged tainting of the good standing record PRIME has. Heck, we had no policy to suspend such drug users because no one has tested positive in PRIME. As far as Logic is concerned, I’ll never duck a match. So come 160, I’ll be standing in that ring victorious, one way or the other and that’s not my ego talking. That’s just plain logic." Official word about the status of the challenge should be made this weekend. With Killean accepting, we should be expecting a barn burner this coming week on ReVolution. Furthermore, word from PRIME’s Board of Directors came down that shortly after Ultra Violence, PRIME’s drug testing policy will implement a new change that suspends any wrestlers found testing positive for any substances outlawed. It was revealed also that Killean’s positive test was for HGH(Human Growth Hormones). Being that Killean had never tested positive in the past and that he is no bigger than he was back then, certain people have come forward with concerns about the legitimacy of the drug tests being administered. This is why PRIME allowed a second drug test to take place for Killean, one of the company’s top stars. Results should be known within the next week or two.
