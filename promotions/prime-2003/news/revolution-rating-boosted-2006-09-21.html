ReVolution Rating Boosted

21 September 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Obviously, this a great boost for PRIME," commented VP of media operations Blaine Blaire. "The PRIME name has a lot to live up to, so to gain such a market share of the viewership upon our immediate return gives us a great foothold to move up from. Despite some differences in opinion, FX have supported PRIME with the perfect slot for our programming, and we look forward to building upon this relationship to help break through the 7.0 barrier. But, while the network will be looking for PRIME to build on their early showing, PRIME CEO Chet Worth was quick to remind people just what he took over the running of the company to accomplish. "Just because I've moved myself back behind the curtain to deal with the day-to-day operations on PRIME doesn't mean I'm making the wrestling less of a priority," Worth remarked. "While I'm sure my backstage colleagues would have looked at the latest ReVolution and seen a high rating, what I saw was a well-deserved title win for Adam after maybe his best match of 2005, an amazing debut promo from Sonny Silver, and what may be the 1st win in a record streak of Universal Title defences by Tchu... as well as, of course, the return of a certain Angelo Deville. All a rise in ReVolution's ratings means to me is that more wrestling fans got to see a great show. No point in boasting that an extra million viewers saw your product if it wasn't something you're proud of giving them." "We showed at Colossus III that no matter what was going on in the boardroom or the network executive's office, we can still put on the best wrestling PPV's around," PRIME's CEO continued. "And, with a string of problems that plagued our weekly television product having been resolved, we're happy to be back airing the best wrestling product on TV into your living rooms." And with the return of Angelo Deville coming as fans expected to see the arrival of Jason Snow to PRIME, fans still have another debut to look forward to as PRIME rolls on to ReVolution 106.
