PRIME News Round-Up

8 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- A trio of PRIME stars are expected to be in PTC action soon as the Global and Extreme Titles are combined. Current Extreme champion Dusk will look to walk out of the match with gold still around his waist, while GTT6 winner Jason Snow and finalist Jay Phoenix will also be gunning for the unified belt. - PRIME have confirmed that Easton Hall’s mystery opponent at ReVolution 124 will not be a current member of the PRIME roster, but rather a new signing to the company. "While it’s not common for new PRIMEates to get a title shot in their first match," Blaine commented, "when looking at this person’s achievements against some of the current roster, we felt we’d throw him right in and see what he could do on our turf." - Mr Silver, Chairman of PRIME was overheard yelling into his cell-phone after ReVolution, apparently reading a man by the name of ‘Meltzer’ the riot act for "not just inventing a damn 6th star!" in response to the Smokin’ Hot Bitches division match earlier that night. - In reaction to the accusation by AWC’s Truth Waters that PRIME ‘poached’ its talent, Blaine Blair released the following statement - "Yeah, and I suppose social services taking babies from crack-head, dead-beat parents is kidnapping now too, is it? A quick business tip I’ve learned along the way - if you want to keep your workers, try paying them and, well, actually stay in business. But, anyway, thanks for advertising the fact that we’ve got your stars and you have, well, a web page… until you can’t afford the hosting on that anymore." - Following the words from the ironically named ‘Truth’, lingering tensions between those who’ve been in PRIME long-term and those who arrived from AWC were said to have risen, almost exploding several times before ReVolution went on the air. Flashpoints tend to come where various PRIME members fall back into the habit of belittling AWC, as they often did with savagery during the promotion-war of 2006. A lot of PRIME members still believe that ‘to the victors should go the spoils’ and they should be seen as above the former Clubbers.
