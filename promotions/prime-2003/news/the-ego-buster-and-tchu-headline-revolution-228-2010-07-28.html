The Ego Buster and Tchu Headline ReVolution 228

28 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Skylar Montgomery Vs Castor V. Strife The Imaginary Man continued his crusade against PRIME, and more specifically Tyler Rayne, on 227 by producing another rant which culminated in Sky Mont and Rayne atop the PRIME*View and Montgomery being thrown off. No word has trickled down on his injuries as of yet, but management still expects him to compete at 228. Castor Strife will prove to be a rather difficult test, as he is now locked in the Main Event of Colossus VII against Vangelus Olsig. He's also coming off back to back wins over Lindsay Troy and Tyler Rayne. Can Montgomery somehow bounce back from the overwhelming odds or will Strife add another name to the growing list of wrestlers he's defeated? The First Vs Tony Gamble The First came out on the losing end of a tag battle at 227 but showed he is more than capable of standing in the ring with some of the best PRIME has to offer. He's also been given a line of sight toward the 5 Star Championship at Colossus, but will have to go through Steve Starr and new champion Boda to do so. Gamble is also looking to pocket a W, as his most recent outing against Vivica Valentine knocked him out of the Elite Championship tournament. Tyler Rayne Vs Elise Ares The last time PRIME's Swaggeriffic Champion squared off against The Underground Pimp at ReVolution 218, times were a lot different. Ares had the fall out of Nelson's army working in her favor and Cyrus Sutherland to provide assistance in giving her the victory over Rayne. Now, Ares' only ally is a reluctant Vance Raymes. With the odds looking to be a bit more even, which superstar will come out on top with a much needed victory heading into Colossus? Main Event: Grudge Match Dan Ryan Vs Tchu It has been a longstanding fantasy for PRIME to net Dan Ryan for a contract, or even acquire his services for a match. Now, thanks to Lindsay Troy and a grudge against Tchu, PRIME can promote The Ego Buster as being a headliner on ReVolution. Last week, the animosity between Ryan, serving as Troy's manager, and Tchu came to a head when the two slugged it out in the middle of the ring and had to be separated by security. Ryan is regarded in most circles as one of the fiercest and most intimidating wrestlers to ever step foot in the ring, and those in the PRIME world are well aware of Tchu's accomplishments. Also... -With most of the Colossus card set in stone, how will the rest of the card shape with only two shows left until the biggest Pay-Per-View of the calendar year. -Boda acquired the 5 Star Championship and left Garbage Bag Johnny in a heap at 227. The champion is scheduled to appear on 228 and share his thoughts on the entire ordeal. -Vangelus Olsig retained the Universal Championship against Chainz in a hard fought contest. Castor Strife is now set to be his opponent. Both men will be in the building. Will they end up squaring off? -All this and much more as ReVolution 228 is live from the Mississippi Coast Coliseum, Biloxi, Mississippi
