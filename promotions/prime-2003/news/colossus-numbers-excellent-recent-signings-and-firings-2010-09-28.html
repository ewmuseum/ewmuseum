Colossus Numbers Excellent, Recent Signings and Firings

28 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The biggest Pay-Per-View of the calendar year drew 1,200,000 buys. Executives were expecting a number somewhere in the 900,000s but were pleasantly surprised with an inflated number. The company, despite having an incredibly small roster, is making revenue hand over fist in 2010. A real challenge will come over the remainder of 2010 to see if Colossus momentum can be kept. A few superstars were granted or given their releases after Colossus. Elise Ares demanded a release and was granted one, leaving with the SWAGGER Championship. She is one of the few people in recent memory, outside of Jason Snow, who can say they retired as a champion. PRIME wishes Ares the best in her future tanning endeavors and prays for those who take up her sunlight. Skylar Montgomery was let go by PRIME due to poor performance. Word through the grapevine is that PRIME has signed two newcomers, although calls to Devin Shakur, Tyler Rayne, and Lisa Tyler went unreturned. One is expected to make his debut on ReVolution 230 so fans ought to get a perspective on whoever the newcomer is when the show airs. More details about 230 and the King of Kings arc will be released tomorrow.
