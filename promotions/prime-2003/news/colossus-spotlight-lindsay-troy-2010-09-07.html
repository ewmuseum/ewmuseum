Colossus Spotlight: Lindsay Troy

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Troy made her first monumental mark in PRIME when taking on Fuck You, a stable consisting of Angelo Deville, Rich Rollins, Jason Snow, and Nova. She was a stalwart, sticking through all their incarnations, and beating each of them at every turn. Her first Universal Championship came in the midst of their white hot feud, as she upset Nova at ReVolution 129. She would go onto crack Colossus’ curse of having a champion never leave with their Championship by defeating Sonny Silver at Colossus IV. It took a persistent Sonny Silver for her to lose the Championship at Great American Nightmare 2007, and even then it didn’t stay off her waist for long. She won the Championship back at ReVolution 150, defeating Silver and tag team partner Danny Ferguson in a triple threat match. Troy would then receive an upset of her own when she lost to Cozen at ReVolution 156. Cozen went around parading herself as a clone of Troy, mimicking her, and going so far as to commit what many would consider identity theft. With the Universal Championship out of their minds after UltraViolence 2008, both women got to settle their score at Colossus V when Troy came out victorious. Next, Troy found herself in the middle of an SCCW invasion, which saw good friend Tyler Rayne at its forefront, but she was right by his side. Her allegiance with Rayne would carry through until UltraViolence 2009 when she came across Devin Shakur’s elder brother, Christian Daniels, in a match that saw her receive a vicious chokeslam from the ring onto the announcers table. The defeat to Daniels was the last time Troy was seen in action until ReVolution 225 against Castor V. Strife. But nobody was concerned with what happened in the ring as much as the rising conflict between Matt Ward and herself. An explosion between the former friends revealed Troy was pregnant during her long stint away from wrestling and wanted Ward’s support. Ward didn’t exactly comply and Troy held a grudge ever since. Both have gone back and forth, only being involved in physical altercation at ReVolution 228. Their match has been set and is garnering just as much hype as Olsig/Strife. Instead of going with a traditional phone interview, I flew down to speak with Lindsay Troy personally about her thoughts on why she got into wrestling, what PRIME means to her, Colossus, and Matt Ward. When I arrived, Troy was sitting on a balcony in Tower23 Hotel in San Diego, California. I asked why she got into wrestling and sat back while she gave a detailed answer, "I've been doing this a long time. Sometimes I wonder if I've been doing it too long. I'm into my fourteenth year now. I've wrestled for companies based all over the world, fought in front of countless fans, and have enough gold to my name to fill a vault. I began training in martial arts when I was ten. My uncle's a former member of the world Muay Thai council and he oversaw my progress. I'd been doing tournaments and such from a young age but it wasn't until I was eighteen that I considered getting into the wrestling business. I had graduated high school early and was already taking classes at USF [University of South Florida]. My best friend was training at a place in Tampa and I tagged along with him one afternoon. I went back with him the next day. By the end of the week I decided to give it a whirl. I'd be there for a couple years, working the Florida independents, then I went over to Japan. From Japan, I came back to the States. My sister and I...we inherited my father's business. For a long time, while we were growing up, other people ran it. I'm the oldest but I knew that after I graduated college, I didn't want to just jump head-first into the corporate world. You get an itch, y'know? You want to travel, see the world, experience cultures and people and I was lucky enough to find a calling that allowed me to do all that and excel at my craft." When the topic of PRIME came up, she gave another answer, reflecting first on her early years, "My time in PRIME's been funny. I came here with my ex-husband [Joey Melton] as a tag team, but his involvement wouldn't last longer than a few months. I was left here by myself and I wondered if staying would really be worth my while, since I also held the Empire Pro World Title and the TEAM Champions belt. My uncle had a stroke in October 2006 and I took time off. I considered not coming back. Ironically enough, it was Matt Ward and Killean Sirrajin who convinced me to return. It was one of the better decisions I've ever made. Over the years, PRIME's become a home to me, same as Empire Pro has been. I've said that about a few former places I've worked in, but none have had the staying power for me that PRIME's had. Even when management's been a pain in the ass, even when there've been uprisings and potential coups and less than savory people to deal with, I've stuck around. I've had to go away for a bit a couple times but I've always come back. PRIME's needed me just as much as I've needed it. You can always go back home." Colossus came next, and on her lips was a feat she felt very proud of, "I'd be remiss if I didn't remind everyone that I broke the Colossus Curse. Back when I fought Sonny Silver at Colossus IV, no prior Universal Champ had been able to successfully defend the title at the company's biggest PPV. That's something I'm immensely proud of, moreso because it came at the Fuckhead's expense. Colossus is not only a spectacle, but it's timeless. There are few events I can think of that I'd put on the same scale. It's more than just grand, over-the-top entrances and blockbuster matches. The atmosphere for this exceeds electric. Everyone gets up for this. Doesn't matter where you are on the card or what your job is backstage. Every year, PRIME sends summer out with a bang. I've had some great matches at this show. I'd put the one with Sonny in my top ten of all time. The best part is, I haven't lost at Colossus yet. I'm not planning on starting now." And finally Matt Ward, "Yeah, I read the interview with Matt. He's right, it's funny how fast things can change. It's real unfortunate what's happened with his father and, all this with Matt aside, I hope he gets better. But Matt doesn't get a pass for everything else that's happened. I'm not going to sit here and pretend that I haven't done and said some bad shit in my time, but when the people I care about need me the most I'm there. I didn't judge Matt when he told me at one point he sorta, maybe, kinda had feelings for Wolfenden when he and Mary hit a rough patch, but he's going to judge me for my feelings toward Rayne. Back when his brother Scotty was in trouble with a loan shark, I didn't try to tell him how he should handle his family situation, but he's going to get his panties in a bunch about Dan [Ryan] being by my side. I may have hung out with his family and bounced his kids on my knee, but he's not going to be able to do that with mine. I'm not even going to get that chance." Troy paused a moment, staring out at the water, and then continued, "I'm damn sick and tired of having to put band-aids on everyone's scrapes. Tired of having to keep everyone in consideration when I'm nothing but an afterthought. If Matt wanted to apologize, he should have done that last year. Not wait until it was convenient for him, when I came back to PRIME. Too little, too late. If it takes every last shred of strength in me, I'll drive some common sense back into that empty head of his. I'm not so sure he'll be wanting to stroll down memory lane anymore after that." Lindsay Troy Vs Matthew Ward will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
