Hall Of Fame Class of 2008 Announced

9 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"We are honored to celebrate these pioneers of professional wrestling, and honored to say that they were a part of PRIME's history," the statement read. "All six members of this class have helped shape the compant into wat it is today." The larger-than-normal class is a result of the group induction of See, Pham, Motoki and Bojangles, the pioneering ownership group of 3-4 years ago that helped build the foundation of the company. Troy and Silver, the only wrestlers in the class, are notable for their bitter andbrutal feud that took up much of 2007. It's widely considered that the feud is what locked up inductions for both of them. Silver, Troy and Bojangles are expected to be in attendance for the ceremony on Night Two. Motoki and Pham are not confirmed, although neither has been heard from in the wrestling world since the death of Hin See in late 2005. BIOS: Lindsay Troy "The Queen of the Ring" has been one of the more popular and important figures in PRIME since her arrival in early 2006. Her pair of tag team title reigns (with Joey Melton and Danny Ferguson, respectively) gave way to a pair of Universal Title reigns in 2007 and early 2008. She played a pivotal role in the fed-shaping PRIME vs. F*ck You storyline last year, and has found herself at the center of many of PRIME's most memorable storylines since her arrival. She is the first alpha female in PRIME history, and has arguably been the star of most shows for the majority of her tenure. Sonny Silver Sonny Silver is simultaneously one of the most hated, one of the most respected and one of the most feared men in the business today. Prolific and profane, his brand of sports entertainment came to PRIME in 2006 and quickly caught on like wildfire. As "Mr. Silver, Chairman of PRIME", he pioneered the short-lived Smokin' Hot Bitches division. He helped re-establish PRIME's Intense Title after programs with Asa Fountain and Sun Tzu. Later, he became a catalyst in the second half of the PRIME vs. F*ck You feud, then dovetailed that work into a long and bitter series of matches with Lindsay Troy, his former ally and friend. Now semi-retired and serving as PRIME's Sports Entertainment Liason, Silver's impact on the business - and on PRIME - is felt on a daily basis. Hin See After purchasing PRIME from Sebastian Starr, acclaimed Asian businessman Hin See would go on to set the foundation for PRIME's most prosperous period. His innovative ideas, including the incredible Dual Halo match, helped shape PRIME's legacy and define its direction long after his hand was no longer around to guide it. Unfortunately, he passed away following an unfortunate incident outside the arena after a PRIME show in late 2005. Taun Pham Hin See's son and de facto business partner was at his side for the majority of See's position overseeing PRIME. He served as an advisor, a lieutenant, a shareholder and a variety of other roles as the position required. There are many who would say that Pham's presence helped keep Hin See's often radical ideas from straying too wildly from the acceptable social limitations of professional wrestling. Toshiaki Motoki Another business partner during the Hin See administration, Motoki is perhaps most famous for his focus on the business side of things. Though only imtermittently involved in the inner workings of the wrestling side of the company, Motoki spent the majority of his time trying to grow PRIME as a global brand, often exploring complicated and difficult mergers or business deals designed to benefit the company. It can be argued that PRIME would not be the powerful worldwide juggernaut it is today without the persistence of Toshiaki Motoki. Mr. Bojangles The final "business partner," Mr. Bojangles is shrouded in mystery. Though he always seemed to have the ear of Hin See when it came to important decisions, his internal motivations were never clear. It is known that he never particularly cared for Toshiaki Motoki and that he often sought to be the chief advisor to See, sometimes trying to circumvent Taun Pham's opinions. It is known that he was often a very stubborn negotiator and was considered difficult to work with by anyone other than Hin See. It is also known that Mr. Bojangles was Hin See's pet cat. Little else is known.
