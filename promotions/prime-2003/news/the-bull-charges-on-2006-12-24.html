The Bull Charges On

24 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not so for one individual in particular, Jack 'The Bull' Murphy. When he was pressed for comment on his whereabouts and what he was doing the answer received was puzzling but promising: 'I'll be around. My leg might be banged up a little and hell I'm feeling the effects of that damn structure but I ain't quitting yet.' He was in good spirits, the reporter said, but added that something was plaguing his mind, almost dogging him and his eyes were glazed over at times during the brief conversation that he had with Jack. The reporter tried to figure out more of what Jack was planning but simply got this answer: 'I'll be wrestling boy, that's what I've always been good at and that's what I'll always be doing till the day I die. It doesn't matter where I'm coming or going because it won't be hard to find me. One thing's for sure, there are some major problems where I'm going.'
