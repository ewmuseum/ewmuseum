BATTLE LINES DRAWN AT THE BORDER ON REVOLUTION 152

22 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just two weeks after winning the Universal and 5-Star titles, Lindsay Troy and Tyler Rayne had their short- and long-term futures laid out for him. In El Paso's Don Haskins Center, they teamed up in a three-way tag match with Chandler Tsonda and Nitz Donnelly - their anointed challengers on Night One of Culture Shock - AND Devin Shakur and Easton Hall, the expected challengers waiting in the on-deck circle. Also on the show, a few superstars got big news that impacts the rest of the Night One card. Some new faces continued to show up, and just like last week, a recognizable old one made a cameo. Plus, one of the championship scenes underwent a major - and unexpected - change. All that plus Dusk vs. Tom Walczak, High Flyer vs. Wade Elliott, a crisis of faith, a declaration of war, and much, much more. Click here to see it all.
