Colossus Spotlight: Skylar Montgomery

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The self awarded JUST Wrestling’s Cult Icon made his PRIME debut at ReVolution 222 and had everyone captivated by his controversial gimmick and even more awkward avant-garde stance on life. He didn’t act like a normal wrestler. He didn’t think like a normal wrestler. He claims to be an Imaginary Man among many other things. Montgomery also has a knack for losing. He holds JUST Wrestling’s longest losing streak and hasn’t won a single match in PRIME, further stacking odds against him going into Colossus, where he will take on one of PRIME’s biggest match wrestlers in Tyler Rayne. If he can’t defeat Shawn Hart, Steve Starr, Adam Garcia, Tony Gamble, Nitz Donnelly, or anybody else, what makes people believe he can knock off a man who is likely better than all of those wrestlers? He is a heavy underdog in Las Vegas, favored at 25/1 to win his match against Rayne. In an event featuring a lot of even money, or close to even money matches, Rayne Vs Montgomery is expected to be a blowout. Although, if Montgomery cared about winning or losing, there’s a good chance he still wouldn’t be around promoting his message. He’s gotten more and more eccentric each week, declaring war against PRIME. He kept declaring war against PRIME until someone in management gave him enough daylight, and that someone happened to be Tyler Rayne. Their match features two competitors who definitely have a legitimate gripe with one another. Montgomery was thrown from the PRIME*View on ReVolution 227, and in retaliation laid Rayne out at ReVolution 229 with a steel chair. Even though all experts are declaring Rayne the victor, none of them are going to be inside the squared circle at Colossus. Montgomery has an offense which can’t be pinned down, and some feel if he dedicates enough time to dissecting Rayne, he might stand a chance against The Underground Pimp. Montgomery is a man who picks and chooses when he talks and he did not choose to give an interview to us for this feature, claiming it wasn’t avant-garde enough for his taste. Skylar Montgomery Vs Tyler Rayne will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
