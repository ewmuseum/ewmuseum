PRIME Announces Full Card For UltraViolence

14 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Although much of the card has been rumored for quite some time, the biggest news to come from the release involves stipulations for the night's various matches. Among the highlights: - The Universal Title match will be an elimination-style Texas Deathmatch, where champion Cozen, former champion Lindsay Troy and #1 contender Devin Shakur battle until only one of them is able to answer a standing ten count. When a competitor fails to answer the count, they are eliminated from the match. In addition, Cantrell added that all weapons will be banned from ringside for this match, resulting from the controversy surrounding Cozen's defeat of Troy at ReVolution 156. - In the night's most creative stipulation, newcomer Crucifix, whose only blemish since debuting is a rollup loss to 5-Star Champion Tyler Rayne, will meet PRIME Hall-of-Famer and living legend Hoyt Williams in a Divine Rights Match. If Crucifix loses, he must unmask in Chicago. However, if Williams loses, he must allow Crucifix to cut a cross into his body in front of his hometown crowd. - PRIME's inaugural Hall-of-Famer Tony Rolo, who returned to the company at Culture Shock, meets up-and-comer Simply Beautiful in a Falls Count Anywhere match. After losing a fall, a competitor has the chance to negate the fall if he makes it back to the ring within a ten count. - The two previous Jewels In The Crown, Chandler Tsonda and Tony "The Grin" Gamble, meet in a Cage Match with potential Universal Title implications. Cantrell would not confirm that the Gamble/Tsonda match would secure a title shot for the winner, but referenced later in the release that the main event for Colossus will be determined at UltraViolence. With eleven matches, UltraViolence 2008 is sure to rock Chicago and Wrigley Field. Nearly 50,000 fans are expected to attend the event. Check your local listings for broadcast information.
