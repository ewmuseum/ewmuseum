Commotion continues following Revolution 125

21 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fans’ witnessed the incident as glass shattered around them. One fan complained his soft-top will cost him more than he earns to fix as Lavelle fell through the roof of a fans BMW soft-top convertible. "I hope PRIME will pay for the damage my car received when one of its wrestlers ruined it. It’s a brand new model. The loan on my car is huge!" Said one fan with disgust as he witnessed his brand new BMW implode. The upper storey was housing a lot of PRIME’s equipment and many wonder as to why the ex-AWC hall of famer was up there, still unknown to many. However, word has leaked that he was last seen looking for rival, Jonathan Winters. Winters was unavailable to contact but PRIME certainly will be questioning the incident. Immediately on scene, medical managed to tend to Pierce Lavelle exceptionally and he was brought to Mercy Day hospital. Doctor Pierre Horgan spoke briefly after treating Lavelle: "He was lucky to have walked away from such a dangerous fall with minor injuries. He sustained a dislocated shoulder, five bruised ribs and a minor concussion, the gash in his head required twenty stitches. It is in my opinion and my patient’s best interest that he stays away from wrestling for two weeks, recovery at this stage is the most important thing." PRIME reporter Robinson was able to speak briefly with Pierce Lavelle when he emerged from OR. Pierce Lavelle was extremely weary of his position and spoke in mumbles, but here is a small snippet of what he said. "I know what the doctor said, but I won’t be missing Ultra Violence, because there’s one person responsible for this and we’ll finish our business in the ring, in front of the many eyes of adoring fans. No more mind games and sneak attacks." "As for the fans car, I thank him for the fact he owned a soft-top, as I’ve been told if he’d had a hard-top I wouldn’t have walked away with minimal injuries." Pierce Lavelle’s and Jonathan Winters’s manager, Charity Manale, was unavailable to comment on the incident but she did arrive at the hospital with her client, Pierce Lavelle. Time will only tell what Pierce Lavelle meant by "…one person responsible" and if he’ll be allowed to have a match at Ultra Violence.
