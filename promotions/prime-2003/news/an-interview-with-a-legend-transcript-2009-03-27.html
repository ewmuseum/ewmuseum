An Interview with a Legend: Transcript

27 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Killean: Matt, I've always believed that the most important thing in anyones life is friends and family. I came back to PRIME at the 2008 Dual Halo because I had discovered that everyone around me was happy watching me do what I loved. I love to entertain the fans. It hadn't really gotten in my way in the past. But recently, an unfortunate turn of events pulled me out of the spotlight. After the Great American Nightmare where I finished second in the roulette eventually losing only to Chandler Tsonda, I thought I still had what it took to carry the Universal Championship again. However, prior to my First Round match for the JitC, I received a disturbing call from my good friend Dave Vegas. During the match, all I could think about was getting home to try and settle some personal business issues. When I finally did, I discovered that they were much worse than I thought. It's the reason why I've been away until just recently. Matt: Mind if you eleborate a little more? Killean: Sure. To keep it simple, Dave and I own a Night Club and we also donate to several charities and other organizations. Our business originally started in the United States when we both used to live in San Diego. We had put a bunch of money away in investments and until recently, everything went fine. I'm sure most everyone in the States is aware of the actions by a man named Bernie Madoff. Well it just so happens that some of our largest investments were sucked up in the now Multi-Billion dollar scheme discovered not too long ago. Our business lost a significant amount of money. Now as most may know, personally, I am wealthy enough to live a healthy long life. However, plenty of my money was tied up in the performance of certain investments. I know that plenty of this is jibberish to most, so the simple explanation is that I had to sink a good portion of money into our business to keep it afloat. Vegas also had to do the same thing and it almost broke him. Everyone has been told that most of the money invested has disappeared and most may not even make it back to us. However, certain others may get a portion back. We've been told the same thing but as you can tell, it's going to take a significant amount of time. I've drained my current resources to my own set limit. I refuse to go any lower and I made the decision to hop a plane, show up at ReVolution and talk to Lisa. As I told her, I'm in no way broke or poor, but the extra money would help Dave and his wife a lot. They took the hardest hit and almost lost their home. I managed to bail them out of that mess, but all of this forced me to be there personally. Matt: It's understandably a very tough story to discuss as many Americans are now facing that same situation and to a point worse in some cases. But the bad news compounded for you didn't it? Killean: It certainly did. The rough economy has taken it's toll on many people. My family is no exception. My fiance, Jenny, lost her job during this rough time and unfortunately has not been able to secure employment since then. So while I've been out helping our business and friends, Jenny was home, sending out resumes to numerous companies and taking care of Desiree. It's not so much the money we are missing, but Jenny is a very independant woman. She was always one to work, to take care of herself financially and so on. Without a job to keep her busy, unfortunately she fell into a personal funk. There wasn't much positive going on to take energy from and she has recently been treated for depression with medication and counceling. Needless to say it's a tough situation for everyone involved. Matt: Certainly a devastating turn of events. So now you have resurfaced in PRIME. What are your plans? Killean: The only plan I have is to entertain like I always did and to take the money home to help anyway I can. I could do it without this money, but that would involve much more hoop jumping on my part. But let's be honest, PRIME has a deep pool of talent. Guys like Kaiser Vashaun, High Flyer and Hessian are making an extraordinary name for themselves, while Chandler Tsonda, Jason Snow and Devin Shakur are performing at a level rarely seen from any superstars in this business. I'm excited to still see Lindsay here, kicking ass like she always did. But I need to be realistic. I've been away for quite some time, I'm rusty and even perhaps a notch lower in physical conditioning. But I've been training cause I knew that I wouldn't be gone for good. However, as I personally met with Lisa Tyler, Blaine Blair and the entire board of directors, I will step back if I need to and return home for the better of PRIME since the talent pool is so deep. I'll step aside to let the future of this business blossom and I'll also help if called upon. Matt: You stated that your conditioning is down slightly... Killean: Just slightly. It wouldn't take long at all to get back to a level that I'm happy with and the ring rust would wear off quite fast. I'm not worried about that at all. But I am worried about upsetting the apple cart here so to speak. In my absence... since guys like Matt Ward, Sonny Silver and Ignatius Lisieux rode off into the sunset we like to call retirement, the big names of the new age of wrestling have risen. I will not stand in the way of the future of this business that I love. Matt: So you're saying that PRIME may soon have to bid farewell to Killean Sirrajin permanently? Killean: I'm not saying anything right now that is written in stone Matt. Only time will tell. But I promise that when I step into the ring, whether my career will last only one more match or another five years, I will give it my all. I may refuse to stand in the way of the future of this business, but the one thing I can do for each and every one of them who thinks I belong back in 2006 or 2007 is make their ass famous by pinning them to the mat or making them tap out. I don't pull punches, I win matches and I entertain. Maybe I could even teach the newbies a lesson in how strong the History of PRIME really is.
