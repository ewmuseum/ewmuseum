Oxios Challenges PTC Circa 2002/2003.

27 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's a shame that just because I ate too much and got sick in the first round of JitC that PRIME has decided to be gayer than... well, me. Two weeks without a booking? Come on! People rally behind me like Joan of Arc. Israel and Lebanon stopped their war when they heard that I'd be coming back to the fed, just so they'd have enough televisions left to watch me. I equal ratings. And not the kind of ratings where concerned parents are watching it just son they can go back and complain about it to the FCC as soon as the show ends. We're talking genuine, bona fide, keep-people-talking ratings. I scratch my ass on television and the internet wrestling community is buzzing about it for days. If PRIME was a farm, I'd be in the corner, washed up nice and clean for the world to see with a web in the corner of my stall reading, "Some Pig." Best believe I'll be at ReVolution 113, doin' my thang as only I can do. But while I'm there, it'll be a good use of my time to do something constructive. Hey, I wanna wrestle, that's my job aside from getting paid to stand still for 20 seconds and shill merchandise (which for me is a lifestyle and a bill-paying thing, and not just a cheesy ripoff attempt at a gimmick... yes, I'm looking at you, Nova and Deville). So here's what I'm gonna say about that. I want to wrestle Unknown #7. I don't care who it is, a match is in order... nobody should just get a pass into the semi-finals of King of Kings, especially not the jokes that are getting trotted out for this slot in the tournament. But I don't know who Unknown #7 will be, so I'm challenging EVERYBODY who ever flaked out of WrestleOnline/PTC between the 2002 and 2003 years of operation. Drake Maxwell? He does my gardening now, only because the town is cracking down on Mexicans without official VISAs. He's put on a little weight, and can't afford good books so now he's just taking lines from Dora's Explorer and passing them off as his own insights, but step into the match if you want. You beat me, I'll give you a raise. Hiro Saotome might as well not show up. Every morning since the June 5th 2002 I've woke up and started my day by counting to ten. If Saotome hasn't shown up by the 10 count, I win. So far that's 1,636 consecutive losses against the Whole Freaking SHO. Even I'm bored of the rematches by now. Cassius Deamont will be shot should he attempt to enter the building. Everyone who knows what I'm talking about will thank me for it. Silvio Rochelli will be hunted down and shot even if he expresses no interest in this. Pete will grin, but go on about his day. Who else we got? Epitome of Evil? El Diablo? Enmity? Somebody whose name doesn't start with the letter 'E'? I don't care if you were a part of WrestlingTattler or WrestleOnline or Primetime Central or whatever... this is an open challenge to the washouts. One of you will be tapped for ReV 113 and I'm gonna waste some time by reducing the options a little. Who's gonna step up to the plate? Oh, and Boca del Inferno: you're not my type. Stop calling me.
