PRIME in hot water with the FCC.

30 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The upcoming steel cage match pitting Jason Snow and Chainz may end up costing PRIME a record amount in fines. The FCC have expressed growing concern over the growing violence and sadism exhibited by the two men that will be locked up in the cage this Sunday. "Violence is a part of wrestling and we understand that, but when the violence turns from in-ring sparring to out of the ring assaults and batteries we have to intervene," FCC Chairman Kevin J. Martin was quoted as saying. Ever since the fragile alliance hashed between these two men ended, they have had nothing but violence on their minds. Things have been especially bad since Chainz has been reinstated following his indefinite suspension for nearly strangling Jason Snow inside of a ring, "Reinstating Michael Sloan, more commonly know as Chainz, was not something I had planned on. When I made his suspension the plan was to let it ride out to the end of his contract and then part ways with him, but when the PTC unified champion and a former champion here in PRIME wants nothing but the man who almost killed him I had to budge," Blaine Blair quoted. Michael Sloan means high ratings and profits to PRIME, but it is all ruined with the headaches and fines he inevitably brings along. "He's a good talent; strong, powerful, mean, but he is a vile human being. People hate him and will tune in to watch him, but the man is a psycho and cannot be controlled. Hiring him was Blair's decision and not mine in the first place," Lisa Tyler said in a recent interview. Jason Snow has been unusually silent in the last couple of weeks. Insults to women and other wrestlers have turned into an endless quest for revenge on the man who put him in the hospital. Lisa Tyler was asked what she thought about Jason Snow's recent change, "Well, Jason has always been one of our most followed wrestlers due to his success and talent. He was another man who people loved to hate, but lately he's turning more and more into a savage beast. It's a scary thought, but he's starting to turn in Michael Sloan." Kevin J. Martin has repeatably expressed concern over what Michael Sloan may do on live television. PRIME does it's best to censor his language, but his actions are often sudden and unpredictable. Martin was quoted as saying, "We'll be keeping a close eye on the PPV and on the steel cage match in particular. The rules of the match allow for plenty of violence and anger and if it's not enough for those two gentlemen and they decide to cause mayhem and chaos outside of their match and make trouble then we will be swift with a heavy fine. Neither Sloan nor Snow were reached for comment, but Tracy had this to say outside her home in NY, "I don't hate Jason Snow and I don't wish anything bad to happen. That's why I'm begging him to stay home this Sunday. I've done all I can to calm my husband down, but he's not having any of it. Please Jason, stay home." We all know, however, Jason Snow will not stay home.
