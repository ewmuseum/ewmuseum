Post Colossus News

2 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Dear PRIME, I am writing this in case anyone is worried about my husband and I. After much deliberation we have decided to step away from the ring for the time being to focus on our marriage, and to spend some time together. The past few months have been especially tough on us both and the decision to take a rest was mutual. The last time anyone saw us we were in the back of a hijacked ambulance headed to an unknown hell. I cannot go into details on what happened that night. I’m just taking this opportunity to tell everyone that both Michael and I are doing well. He is recovering slowly from the injuries and the toll that a heavy year of wrestling takes on a body. As soon as he is physically and mentally fit to return, he will be back to claim what is his. In the meantime we will continue to improve as a couple. I don’t want anyone to worry about us. We’ve decided to cut contact for the time being just to get away from the hustle that comes with being a PRIME superstar. This will be the last communication from us until we decide it’s time to return. Thank you to all those concerned regarding my husband’s well being. Sincerely, Tracy Stanton Sloan" The letter offered few details regarding the whereabouts of Michael Sloan or his timetable for a return. In response to the letter, Lisa Tyler issued the following statement, "While Mr. Sloan and I never saw eye to eye PRIME wishes him a speedy recovery. When he feels fit to return to PRIME his spot will be waiting for him." When pressed for further comments Lisa Tyler declined. Hessian, Christian Daniels, and Devin Shakur were not available for comments either.
