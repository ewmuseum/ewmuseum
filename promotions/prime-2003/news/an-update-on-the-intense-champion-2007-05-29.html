An Update on the Intense Champion

29 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
However, we here at PRIME headquarters have learned that not only has Dusk been cleared by PRIME doctors but he was banned by Lisa Tyler and PRIME's legal department. After Dusk disobeyed Tyler's orders not to show up at the first ReVolution after UltraViolence, she went up the ladder to do whatever it took to ensure Dusk wouldn't show up at ReV 128 and not only risk the fans to another shooting ala UltraViolence but to make sure Dusk didn't risk further harm to himself for the upcoming triple threat champion vs. champion vs. champion match that will co-main event this upcoming week's ReVolution, which is being billed as the ReVolution of the year. Yet, the interesting development in all of this is that even though PRIME doctors have approved of Dusk wrestling this upcoming week, Dusk’s own personal physician feels like that he is far from ready to compete in a match of this stature. Doctor James Burroughs had this to say when I spoke to him earlier this week. "Mr. Maloof suffered serious damage to his abdomen that still hasn’t healed itself. Even if Craig seems fine in that ring, he is not. The fact that PRIME is forcing him to go through this match shows irresponsibility on their part that I cannot condone. Yet, Craig is adamant on participating in this match and will do so even against my strong recommendations that he take some time off to make sure he is healthy above all else." When I contacted Lisa Tyler to ask her opinion of Dr. Burroughs thoughts, she simply said the following, "Dr. Burroughs is a respected doctor in the field, yet I trust the doctors that we’ve employed here at PRIME to make sound decisions. The fact remains that he is healthy enough to compete and he will do exactly that as stated in his contract. Our superstars wrestle in pain all of the time, and Dusk is no different from the rest of them." Many in the back were perturbed by the fact that there was a shooting inside of a PRIME event and have anonymously voiced their opinions to several people in management, including Blaine Blair, who have made sure to beef up the security at every PRIME event to make sure something of this nature doesn’t happen ever again. However, the fact still remains that Dusk was shot and can’t be in the right frame of mind. Right? After speaking with his personal trainer, I got a very different story out of him concerning this matter. He says that Dusk has never been more focus in his entire career, having to deal with personal and professional issues that he hasn’t experienced before in his life, and will be ready for this match with the circumstances pertaining. While this match is coming fresh off the shooting, this could be a great chance for Dusk to get back into the ring as it’s been announced that he will be participating in the upcoming CWMC tournament being hosted by PTC as well as he’s expected to toss his hat into the TEAM Tournament of Champions after being offered a guaranteed spot in the tournament. It would appear that Dusk isn’t planning on taking any more time off as he is getting back into the ring with a whirlwind schedule. In other news, Dusk made his appearance at ReVolution 127 with a brand new theme song, What I’ve Done by Linkin Park. Yet, it would appear that this was merely a onetime thing as the song was rejected by several focus groups, saying that the song didn’t match the image that Dusk has amongst his fans. PRIME management has gone on record saying that Dusk will return to his previous song, "Headstrong" by Trapt, until they find a suitable replacement. Rumors inside PRIME Headquarters state that this could be as early as OverKill, but as late as Colossus IV. This is Gerald Barrett signing off.
