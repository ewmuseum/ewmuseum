Logic Out Twelve Months

17 August 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the attack suffered by Logic at the hands of Crucifix on night one of the mammoth Colossus V event, news out of the camp of the former Survivor IV competitor is not good. "I have consulted with Dr. James Andrews, famed sports surgeon," Logic told PRIME Online, "and he has diagnosed me with a complete tear of my anterior cruciate ligament, and a partial tear of my medial collateral ligament. I will go in for surgery in a week and a half, and I have been told that I will be out of action for a minimum of twelve months." "A man of David's size puts a lot of stress on his legs," Dr. Andrews said, when asked for comment. "Twelve months is a minimum time away. He'll be able to start therapy soon after surgery, but getting back to in ring shape is a herculean task." PRIME staff could not be reached for comment, though it is expected that Logic will meet with PRIME officials to renegotiate a contract. Some believe that Logic will slide into a promotional role for the company speaking out against steroids, as he did during his feud with Killean Sirrajin. More news as we get it.
