Charges Dropped!

5 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After hearing the arguments presented by Mr. Sirrajin and a short testimony by Sean "Spinks" Lamont, the man who was allegedly beaten by Killean on August 10, 2006, Judge Garreth Malone retired to his quarters to gather and look over all the evidence. The hearing was only supposed to address the charges, but Judge Garreth Malone received and added some additional evidence recently obtained. After numerous ads in newspapers around the Winnipeg area, a third-party witness came forward with evidence she was withholding in fear of her own safety. The witness will remain anonymous but her recollection of the August 10th attack by Sirrajin on "Spinks" blew the case wide open. As it turned out, Sean Lamont was dealing drugs on his own delivery route and Mr. Sirrajin and his business partner, one David Vegas showed up on the scene and caught Lamont in the act. Sirrajin ended up throwing the drugs across the driveway and hit Lamont numerous times. He is quoted as saying... "Did you really think you could do this behind our back?! Did you really think you could put yourself and OUR business at risk by trying to come up with some kinda stupid pay day?!" The witness happened to live next door to the home where the events in question took place. After validating this new evidence, Judge Malone called all parties back to court immediately and explained that in light of new evidence, the charges were dropped against the PRIME Hall of Fame Superstar. Sirrajin had his house arrest removed and he can once again travel at will. Lamont is not seeking any other criminal charges regarding the attack. Instead, any funds he has to his name will be used to defend himself in the new Drug charges he will be facing. "This is a very great day. The last few months have been nothing but a nightmare and to have this weight lifted from my shoulders is such a relief." After numerous other questions, Killean had something to say to the fans of PRIME. "I'll be at ReVolution 114 free of any restraints. Many of my backstage privledges were removed pending the charges. I have spoken to Blaine Blair and I now have full privledges again. This means that all the members of [NAME CENSORED] are on borrowed time. Tchu, Lindsay and myself can now operate as a fully functional team without police officers watching my every move. It's time for me to do what I do best... kick some ass in PRIME."
