Sidelined?

8 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Mr. Shakur broke three bones in his right hand: The second, third, and fourth metacarpals which are located on the index, middle, and ring fingers" said Dr. Cantellope "He will need to wear a spica cast that covers the entire hand for about a month or so." This was not the only injury that PRIME’s Reject would suffer from the show. During an in-ring segment with his crush, Sun Tzu, the 5 Star Champion proceeded to pull out her two guns, Mr. Snugglepants and Kookoo Bear, and give Shakur two shots to his sternum. The bullets did not penetrate through the protective vest that he was wearing, but the impact from the bullets was enough to cause blunt force trauma, and break two of Shakur’s ribs on the left side. Dr. Cantellope added "The only real thing that we can do is give Mr. Shakur some analgesics and hope that he reduces his physical activity. It is my recommendation that he does not compete in PRIME for about a month." Devin Shakur was available for comment, although only over the phone. "Do you really think that I give a fuck what a doctor tells me to do? I didn’t go out there and get my brains beaten in by a psycho just to get my plans thwarted by a "medical expert". You can expect to see me at Revolution 124 competing, and if I have my way, walking out the new Intense Champion. You saw what I could take, and that was just a contenders match. Just imagine what lengths that I will go to so that I can obtain the Championship!" When asked about his feelings toward Sun Tzu, Shakur’s tone changed. "My sweet Sunny. I know that she had a moment of passion when she put those two bullets into me, and that her head wasn’t in the right place. I hope she’ll forgive me…" The reporter could not get anything further from Shakur on that particular topic. Shakur was then asked how he would encounter the likes of Pierce Lavelle, Jonathan Winters, Dusk, or someone else on the PRIME roster, if they should try to attack him at 124. "Haha, let’s just say that I have a nice little surprise waiting for anybody who attempts to indulge in backstage shenanigans with me at 124." Shakur then abruptly hung up.
