Doubt and Uncertainty

15 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Change In Spades retained their tag team championships to become the final champions in their storied history. Ever since, the trio of Change In Spades has not been seen on live television. Nitz Donnelly and company were scheduled to be a part of the events on 222 but were held of screen for personal reasons. Apparently, reports out of the Venice Beach area state that Jose Mendoza, father of [Ana]Leticia Mendoza and the President and CEO of Mendoza Hotels and Construction has been indicted under a number of criminal offenses. Reports have not been confirmed about the exact offenses, but Mendoza has been officially held on $800,000 bail. Under rules of the indictment Mendoza's assets have been frozen and cannot be used to bail him out. However, officials are guessing that won't stop numerous business partners of the West Coast tycoon who most likely are willing to front the cash required. PRIME's [Ana]Leticia Mendoza is not named in reports and her personal assets are safe including her gym in Venice. Confusing things further is Vance Raymes' no show at ReVolution 222. Relegated to the low payment of a manager combined with Jose Mendoza's criminal problems has effectively left the Tortured Artist earning a small portion of what he used to make. A stage hand arrived at the Spades dressing room before the show started with Vance Raymes' tag team championship belt in hand and a hand written apology directed to Nitz and Leticia. According to Donnelly, who declined a formal interview, Vance Raymes has most likely left PRIME to focus on a Mixed Martial Arts career, something he is extensively trained in. No reports can confirm that information and according to numerous sources, Raymes has yet to opt out of his managerial contract with PRIME. ReVolution 223 may offer some answers as Nitz Donnelly is scheduled to compete against Chainz and Bryan Dawkins in the Born Again tournament.
