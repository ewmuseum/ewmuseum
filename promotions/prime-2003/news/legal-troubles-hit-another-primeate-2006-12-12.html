Legal Troubles Hit Another PRIMEate!

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cain, 31, was arrested on the 1st December in connection with the assault of a 20 year old Caucasian male from the Boston, MA area, who cannot be named for legal reasons. The legal action came after the 'victim' pressed charges against the PRIME wrestler for a series of events that were alleged to have taken place between the 12th and the 26th November in and around the victims place of residence. It has been suggested that the situation between the two began after a dispute involving Cain's wife and culminated in a physical alteraction on the night of the 26th. Paul was taken into custody the night after Revolution 113 and questionned on this matter as well as the mysterious assault on two Caucasian males, thought to be asscoiates of Tony "The Grin" Gamble. Cain was released on bail and allowed to compete in the Main Event against Nova on Revolution 114, although again his private life was clearly coming into conflict with his in-ring performances, which was subsequently below par. With these matters still outstanding, it has been confirmed that Cain will not be participating in King Of Kings 2006 in any way, shape or form as the investigations continue. With yet more legal issues clouding PRIME, it remains to be seen what the future holds both personally and professionally for Cain. More news will be released on this matter as it becomes available.
