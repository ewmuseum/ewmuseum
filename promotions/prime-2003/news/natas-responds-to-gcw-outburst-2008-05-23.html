Natas Responds to GCW Outburst

23 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Yeah, I heard all about Chris Bagwell’s cricket-inducin’ speech the other day. Pretty rich comin’ from a guy who seems to be the personal jobber bitch of some over-the-hill Scottish moron in the country’s SECOND biggest wrestling promotion," said Natas, who will team up with Jimmy Bonafide, Union Jack and Simply Beautiful to clash with the GCW team of Brad Jackson, Thom LeStrange, Zacharia Taylor and Chris Bagwell. "How do I see our chances? Pretty damn good if all the other GCW guys are as odious as that squealin’ little shitcake," PRIME’s ‘Anti-Superstar’ continued. "The hell does he think he is anyway? Dude’s got as much credibility as a damp slice of bread. Bottom line; if Chris Bagwell thinks this is gon’ be some kind of easy ride, he’s in for a rude awakening. I’ve made a livin’ from stompin’ the living daylights out of motormouth idiots like him, and I’m gonna personally make sure that those GCW fools leave with their tails between their legs and their two front teeth in their pockets." The match is scheduled as part of a TEAM interfed event, and is scheduled for the 31st May.
