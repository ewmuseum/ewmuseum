ReVolutionizing the Windy City

9 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opening the evening was a match where two of the tallest superstars on the roster, Meat and Nerezza, squared off. Nerezza, now at the side of Hessian and one half of PRIMEvil, looked to establish himself and the tag team as a dominant force amongst the new horde of talent. Meat, on the other hand, yearned for a win to cope with his UltraViolence loss to David Noble. One walked out victorious and the other was left to look for a better tomorrow. The next bout featured two stalwarts of the Intense Title division butting heads, Chainz and Troy Douglas. Chainz also fell victim to The Rising Star, while Douglas was coming off a disappointing loss to The Anti-Superstar, Jason Natas. The Monster from Hell's Kitchen needed a win to reassure the roster that he was still dominant and Megatron needed a boost toward another Championship match. A classic ensued but only one elevated their stock in the win column. Up next on the slate was a match where two new tag teams fresh out of the kitchen collided. The Wolves of Slaughter arrogantly strutted into PRIME at UltraViolence and claimed superiority amongst the division. The Redeemed quietly clocked in under the radar, only wanting to show fans they could wrestle. After a tense and physical encounter earlier in the evening, these two teams were begging to go toe to toe. Once the dust settled, one team had bragging rights and the other hung their heads in defeat. The Main Event saw PRIME's monster of 2009, Hessian, taking on 'The Rising Star' David Noble. Many anticipated this match wouldn't happen until Colossus, but Jonathan Rhine broke up those plans at UltraViolence by winning the Intense strap. So the battle of Former Champion Vs. Number One Contender happened in Chicago. The superstars provided a true David vs Goliath battle that saw one receive a timely assist from a friend to pick up the clutch victory.
