King of Kings Match Changed

12 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME CEO Chet Worth got involved in the post match happenings with Former Universal Champion Killean Sirrajin and GTT6 Semi-Finalist Jason Snow on ReVolution 114. Instead of letting them restart the match after a double countout, Mr. Worth scheduled the two PRIME sensations in a match at King of Kings Sunday, December 17. But a change has quickly come about. To avoid a repeat of ReVolution 114, CEO Worth sent out a bulletin altering the match to now be a No-DQ and No Countout contest. Mr. Worth was unavailable for comment as he has been very busy preparing PRIME for the final show of 2006. But I happened to get a few quick comments from Killean Sirrajin. "Really? Good, Worth continues to make good decisions. Snow and I had a great match at 114 and the bad blood will continue at King of Kings. There is a chance that bad blood will boil over as well and I am prepared to sacrifice myself to begin to rid PRIME of the [Name Censored] curse. I'll start with Snow, Lindsay will take care of Rollins and Tchu will defeat Nova again and this time he will not come back for more. That just leaves Angelo and believe me, we have plans for him should he win the JITC." I also tried to get a few words from Jason Snow but it was a rather awkward conversation. Me: Hi is this Jason Snow? Jason: Surely you recognize my G-reatness! I called catering twenty minutes ago wench! What's the hold up? Me: What? I... Jason: AH! I would like an answer, not a question. Me: I'm Angelica Brooks, I'm calling to ask you about the stipulations for your match at King of Kings. Jason: Don't play with me wench! Stop it with the worthless questions, make me a sandwich and deliver it up here pronto would you? Just don't look directly at my G-reatness! Sight of me = ORGASM~! A fact I know all to well Ja... ummm... This is Angelica Brooks reporting for PRIME.
