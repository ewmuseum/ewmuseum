UltraViolence Injury Report

23 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve Starr required two stitches in his mouth after receiving a stiff European uppercut in the opening contest from Skylar Montgomery. Starr reportedly felt fine after receiving the stitches and is signed to compete on ReVolution 225. Another, perhaps more serious injury, came from Shawn Hart. During the match, Hart received very stiff moves onto his back and neck and was slow to leave the ring after the contest. Doctors immediately brought Hart to a nearby hospital, but nobody within PRIME is releasing details on what the extent of his injuries are. Upon the surprising advice of Devin Shakur, Shawn Hart will not be competiting until further notice. The Intense Championship match provided another night of bump and grind for The Inhuman Being, Tchu. After the show, Tchu came out to sign autographs for a few lucky fans and was quite banged up. He had an ice pack wrapped around his shoulder, his ribs were covered in tape, and he commented to fans that his back and neck were quite stiff after the brutal bout with Hush. It is unknown whether or not The Inhuman Being will compete in an actual match at 225, but with Devin Shakur on the warpath and the 24/7 hanging over Tchu's head, one would expect he will be ready to compete if necessary. Boda and Lindsay Troy didn't sustain any significant injuries during their match, although The Queen's pride might be a bit bruised, considering her first match back was tainted by the fast count of referee Elvis Nixon. Both were looked at briefly by the medical staff at separate times and sent on their way. Troy has already been officially signed to compete in her first televised match of the year at 225. Even though the match was fairly short by Pay-Per-View Championship standards, Elise Ares and Garbage Bag Johnny were both wounded from their moment of battle. The Havana Harlot refused medical treatment, even though she was holding her back in a lot of pain. She has not officially been signed to compete at ReVolution 225, but the probability of her showing up and giving her opinion the Pay-Per-View is very plausible. Garbage Bag Johnny received cuts on the back of his head and lips, but didn't seem too bothered by either. He took the medical treatment, grabbed himself a sandwich from the vending machine, and walked out of the building immediately following his match. Emilio Rage suffered a Grade 1 concussion and swollen jaw after being blindsided by Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels moments before the Main Event. It is unclear how much time Rage will miss, but Shakur has not signed Rage to compete at 225 as of this printing. The Main Event turned out to be a blood bath, one which saw both Castor V. Strife and Vangelus Olsig attempt to be taken to local hospitals after their match. Vangelus suffered a broken nose and cuts on his forehead, while Strife was seen walking rather gingerly toward an ambulance with a back brace on. Both men received stitches but neither wished to be examined by doctors. Castor V. Strife has been signed to compete at ReVolution 225 whereas Olsig is unconfirmed.
