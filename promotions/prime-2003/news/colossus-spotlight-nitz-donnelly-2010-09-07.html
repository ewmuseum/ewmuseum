Colossus Spotlight: Nitz Donnelly

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Donnelly would go on and lose his Colossus IV match against Elliott, but fans were unable to forget him. He brought an ability to string together profanities like nobody before him, and was dubbed The Ego of New England and High Class Ass as a result of his foul mouth. He drew management’s ire from the get go, but didn’t seem to mind. He dared anybody to come after him and had high flying freakish athletic ability to back his trash talk up. For a brief period, because of a suspension due to violence against Lindsay Troy, Donnelly threatened to go to GCW. Donnelly ended up signing his contract but was censored by Blaine Blair, in something which took away about half his vocabulary. Ultimately, Donnelly would fall in line and be in contention for the 5*Star Championship, falling to Tyler Rayne at ReVolution 150. His last appearance during his first stint was against Rayne at Culture Shock 2008 where he came up short against The Underground Pimp. But Donnelly would be seen once again by PRIME faithful as he returned at Great American Nightmare 2009 with a partner, Vance Raymes, and a renewed dedication to claiming Tag Team gold. He got into a long battle with teams such as The Redeemed and Wolves of Slaughter, until finally claiming gold at Culture Shock 2010. PRIME’s tag team division dissolved quickly afterward, leaving Change in Spades as PRIME’s final champions. Donnelly would continue battling against Elise Ares and eventually drew her complete attention after dealing with cohorts Chainz and remnants of Tyler Nelson’s Army. The feud between Ares and Donnelly grew to a fever pitch when Donnelly’s partner, Raymes, betrayed him and became Ares’s new bodyguard. Donnelly was enraged and vented his frustration out on Ares and Raymes over the Colossus arc. Recently, because of a slew of injuries sustained, Donnelly was questioned as to whether or not he would be able to fight at Colossus. Due to his antics, boss Devin Shakur forced him to compete in a match at ReVolution 229 whether he had medical clearance or not. Donnelly fulfilled his obligation and managed to defeat Skylar Montgomery. He has been cleared to compete at Colossus by PRIME’s medical team and will look forward to taking on The Havana Harlot. It is speculated he doesn’t care much for her Championship, so it should be interesting to see what Donnelly does with the belt if he ends up winning the match. Nitz Donnelly Vs Elise Ares will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
