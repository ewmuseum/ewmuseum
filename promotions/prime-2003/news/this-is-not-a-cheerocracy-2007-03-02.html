This is Not a Cheerocracy

2 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Backstage we see the cold steel of the door to the FU locker room, silent and ominous. Then there is a loud thud and it flies open. Angelo Deville steps, dressed in his ring gear for upcoming match a shot at contendership for the 5 Star championship. As he heads down the hall, the Devil's Don hears a disturbingly perky female voice... "Go! Go! Angelo! You can beat Flash! You can beat Snow! If you don't that'll be a bummer! But if you win I'll give you a hu-" Angelo Deville: What in the name of- Cutting off his own demand, his righteously pointing hand freezing, Deville perks an eyebrow. Angelo Deville: Actually, go ahead and finish that rhyme. Now Angelo Deville has seen Sun Tzu, Artist of War and reigning 5 Star champ before. Hell, he's seen her nekkid. But seeing a trained assassin for the Chinese government in a cheerleader outfit jumping around like a Mousketeer on meth is a little surprising even for one as jaded as the Cadillac of Wrestling. Sun Tzu: I just wanted to motivate you, honey bunny! Just think… it could you and me, together in my homeland at Culture Shock! So romantic! Angelo Deville: Even ignoring for a moment the forced sterilizations and abortions running rampant in your homeland, where do you see romance? You do realize that we'd be trying to beat the hell out of each other. The eyes of Sun Tzu grow anime huge and she smiles in a way most people not on lithium don't. Sun Tzu: I know! It's like a fairy tale come true! In contrast, Deville's eyes narrow to, uh, Deniro slits. Angelo Deville: I wasn't even aware fairy tales exist in Japan. Do the bad guys win? Sun Tzu: Oh, yes! And they get the beautiful princess too! The Artist of War closes her eyes, leans forward, and puckers her lips. Angelo Deville stares at her one one eyebrow arched. Then the Devil’s Don shakes his head and walks away. As he heads towards the ring, that perky voice can be heard echoing through the halls… "Go! Go! Angelo!" PRIME’s crack team of computer experts tracked the source of the clip to a terminal used by a ReVolution production assistant. He was found watching it over and over, surrounded by issues of Barely Legal and masturbating into a Mary-Kate and Ashley lunchbox. The production assistant has since been fired. And hosed down. The clip does raise several questions. As we all know, Angelo Deville did in fact win the match and will wrestle Sun Tzu at Culture Shock for the 5 Star championship. How will her feelings towards her opponent affect Sun Tzu’s fighting skills? Will romance cost her the championship in her homeland or is being hit in the head repeatedly her idea of foreplay? If Deville emerges victorous, will he get a billion death threats that he can’t read? And say he does win the 5 Star belt, will Angelo just want another one half an hour later? All this and more will be uncovered at Culture Shock!
