NOW to Protest ReV 109, ITO

18 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As expected, Kenjiro ITO's controversial comments on women in wrestling on ReVoluton 108 have drawn fire from many groups, chief among them the 500,000 strong National Organization of Women. "His comments were absolutely reprehensible," a spokeswoman for the group stated. "How PRIME can allow a Neanderthal like him to spout his mysoginistic nonsense on national television in the year 2006 is absolutely unfathomable." The group has promised to protest the promotion's coming edition of ReVolution, in an effort to get the controversial Japanese star pulled from the show. "Kenjiro ITO has no place on television, even in a business as obviously anti-women as professional wrestling is. And we're going to make our voices heard." ITO could not be reached for comment, but it is unlikely that his response will be positive. More news on this flap as we get it.
