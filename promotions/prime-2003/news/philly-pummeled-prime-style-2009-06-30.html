Philly Pummeled PRIME Style

30 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the opening contest of the evening, one half of PRIME's newest tag team, Giancarlo Coppano took on 'The Rising Star' David Noble. The Noble One was coming off a two show losing streak and critics were in full swing, claiming Noble was nothing more than the latest 'flash in the pan'. With Noble out to prove to everybody that he's still worthy of being the Number One Contender to the Intense Championship, and Coppano looked to making a successful singles endeavor, which man was able to come out with the victory? The second contest featured the red hot Chainz coming off a three show winning streak. The Monster seems to be invigorated and growing more dangerous by the week. His task this week was a returning Colby Korver, looking to venture a victory in the DUI name and bring the former tag champion some notoriety. One man got pounded into oblivion while the other got taken to their limit, but only one took home the big fat paycheck. Contest number 3 was the only tag match on the card and it featured a polar opposite situation, partners who have known each other practically their whole lives versus two singles wrestlers teaming up for the first time. Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels, PRIME's Public Enemies #1 got into an all out melee with Meat and Troy Douglas that found its conclusion outside of the ring where a stunning development occurred. One team managed to leave the carnage with their heads attached while the other was extremely perplexed. The Main Event featured a match that could be easily classified under the David Vs. Goliath category. 'The Murder Show' Hessian took on 'The Flyin Hawaiian' Bryan Dawkins in a match that went all over the place, stunning the crowd at every turn. False finishes were aplenty and both wrestlers left it all in the ring. Yet, only one had his hand thrust into the air after the bell rang. To find out who Lisa Tyler selected for Jason Snow, whether or not Devin Shakur released The Bruh into unemployment, which two superstars returned to restore PRIME's dignity, and which fan favorite said good night, you'll have to tune into ReVolution 198
