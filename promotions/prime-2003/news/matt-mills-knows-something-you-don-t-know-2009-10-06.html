Matt Mills Knows Something You Don't Know

6 October 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TCR: Matt, after some extensive research, questioning and digging for some information, I have dug up just what it was you saw at Headquarters. Matt Mills: Really Thomas? Go ahead, tell the world if you think you know. TCR: I've discovered that the people you saw in Chicago that day were the members of the yet to debut PRIME team called, "Change In Spades". That's right fans. Reported here, only on the official PRIME Website, Matt Mills came face to face with the people involved in the new tag team. As we've seen in a few weeks, videos began to air on ReVolution implying that PRIME needed change. After some of my own detective work, I discovered that on the day of Matt's visit to Chicago, the contracts for the newest PRIME team were signed that same day. Matt Mills: That's what you know? I could have told you that if you asked me. TCR: What? Matt Mills: Yeah. Change In Spades. I met them in Chicago. That's not the secret. Do you even know who the members of the new team are? You'd be surprised if I told you. TCR: I uhhhhhh... no I don't. Matt Mills: Exactly. Good work rookie. Get back to me when you got something concrete ok? I'm not a rookie. Anyways, as it turns out I haven't figured out everything just yet. Upon further investigation, the contracts signed by the Change In Spades members were creatively designed to hide the identities of them but I have confidence that something new will turn up with just a little more work. Matt Mills: Good luck with that..
