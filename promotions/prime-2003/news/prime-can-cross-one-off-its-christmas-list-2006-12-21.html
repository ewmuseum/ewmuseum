PRIME Can Cross One Off Its Christmas List?

21 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Both Chet Worth and Blaine Blair have been in constant negotiations with a number of talents, hammering out the details of their contracts before the PRIME offices close for Christmas. One name linked with PRIME since almost the second that AWC's momentous Triangles PPV went off the air was that of Pierce Lavelle. Seen by many as the face of AWC as it made its sizable mark on the wrestling world, Pierce defeated such names as Jack Murphy and the man many credit with being the foundation of the Club in its early days (before his untimely death), Alexander Strider on his way to a record 3 Transatlantic title reigns. At only 24, the high-flying technician is already seen one of hottest commodities in wrestling, and with the main-event experience his AWC run gifted him, many are tipping Lavelle's stock to continue to rise. "I'm very confident that Santa will be bringing PRIME the signature of Pierce Lavelle for Christmas," Blaine Blair leaked to PRIME website reporter Gerald Barrett. "We're aware of past discrepancies with certain substances that saw him serve a ban in recent times, but it was clear from the outset that certain things surrounding that case were, shall we say, iffy. Lavelle's place on the AWC Roll of Legends should be enough to tell anyone that the whole ordeal has been forgotten by all." While Blaine's words give away that only the PRIME side of a contract has ink drying, fans at King of Kings who had been following internet reports throughout the week made it clear that they'd like nothing more than the Harrisburg native sealing the deal. "Hell yes!" one young man remarked when asked if he'd welcome the AWC record-breaker into PRIME. "For the past year there've been dream matches tossed back and forth on the internet. Tchu v Lavelle, Wolfenden v Nash, Nova v Garbage Bag Johnny, and now we have a chance of seeing some of those dream inter-fed matches all under the PRIME banner on ReVolution!" "Who wouldn't want Pierce Lavelle in their fed?" another fan simply fired back, wondering how it could even be questioned. So, with PRIME's fans having spoken, and Blaine Blair tipping his capture, 2007 looks set to be another eventful year in the young and already decorated career of Pierce Lavelle.
