Record Setting Day

13 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME's own Angelica Brooks brought a tape to the head offices today and played it for the board of directors and the executives. After wiping the sweat from their forehead, management did everything in their power to lure the rather unknown star into their grasp. So they dropped the richest signing bonus in PRIME history on the table. Quickly scooping it up was the man in question. His name is Nicholas Donnelly. But you can call him Nitz for short. The ink was still wet on the contract, and a bonus check was apparently awarded to Brooks for the Diamond in the Rough type of find. "I saw him in a local indie show in Boston a couple weeks back. I just had to recommend him to management. His high flying, death defying swiftness hasn't been seen in a long time. He was the rudest person ever to pick up a microphone, but even so, the fans were in awe with most of his moves. He is lightning quick, simple as that." Brooks praised the new unknown. Blaine Blair or Lisa Tyler could not be reached for comment. But in a short conference call, Donnelly showed his egotistical brashness right off the bat. "I've been watching for a while now and I must say, thank GOD PRIME finally decided to go High Class. I've never seen such bland talent grouped together like this before. But no worry, soon enough fans and PRIMEates a like shall all be converted to believers. Believers, in The mutha f*ckin' Donnelly!" And with a crackle, the line went dead. The twenty one year old flying phenom is not scheduled to appear on PRIME television until after the Overkill Pay-Per View event. With his outright ego and rude attitude, fans may not be holding their breath. But with the seemingly infinite talent he possesses, PRIME may not be able to go much longer without showcasing Nicholas Donnelly.
