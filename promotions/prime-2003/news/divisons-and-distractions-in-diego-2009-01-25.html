Divisons and Distractions in Diego

25 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ReVolution 183 had a whole lot to bring to San Diego. Things got kicked off with a tag team showdown, in which The Union and The Trust Fund were showcased. With four new faces in the ring, one tandem gave itself some breathing room in their attempt to make a splash. Singles action was also about separating oneself from the pack. Jay Phoenix was looking to do it in non-title action against High Flyer, perhaps on the road to cashing in his 5-Star Title shot. Fusenshoff had Bryan Dawkins, Number One Contender to the aforementioned belt, on his hands. Two of these fellas went home with another "W" to their names. And the newly enthroned Tyler Rayne, among his other distractions, had to take on the phenom Hessian. After making impressive work of Jay Phoenix and Fusenshoff last week, Von Kolsig took aim at the wrecking ball that is Tyler Rayne. Some of Rayne's, ahem, "adoring fans," though, weren't content to be bystanders. To go along with the usual ring wizardry, there were typical hijinks. Jason Snow and Chandler Tsonda finally had words, and they weren't congratulatory. It was the Universal Champion's homecoming, and he checked in with some familiar faces, but with unfamiliar results. And speaking of superstars, a certain former Universal Champion got ready to strap the boots back on. If you wanna know who was hiding behind a mask, what former 5-Star Champion returned, and just how grimy Initiation Week gets for PRIME's favorite frat bros, check out the ReVolution transcript and relive a wild night on the shores of the Pacific.
