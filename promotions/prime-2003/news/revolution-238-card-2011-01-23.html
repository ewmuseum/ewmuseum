ReVolution 238 Card

23 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME's first show on the Culture Shock tour will take place in Paris, France, on February 1st. While the show is light on matches, major players are expected to be in attendance. Opening Match - Exhibition Alexandra Pierce Vs Enemigo VII The Spider in the Web came within an eyelash of capturing the Jewel in the Crown 2010, but fell short against Violence Jack. However, The Spider is still not under PRIME contract, but has expressed desire to compete on the 238 card. Devin Shakur, however, said that until she signs a PRIME contract, she is not going to face anybody of note on the roster. So, Enemigo VII is going to get the biggest opportunity of his career to take on the final GTT winner and former SCCW queen. Singles Match Christian Daniels Vs ??? The Biker is not known for making many in-ring appearances, but someone went through channels and called him out. While Daniels, even though he is the right hand man of Devin Shakur, does not know who the mystery opponent is, he is more than willing to accept a one on one fight with anybody. Who is going to step up and try to mess with Daniels? Main Event Seymour Almasy Vs Tom Walczak Many figured Seymour Almasy would never compete in PRIME, but he showed up at King of Kings and is on the roster. He is going to be thrown feet first into the fire at 238 against another rising star in Tom Walczak. The Polish Powerhouse mauled rival Tyler Rayne and Katterina Wylde at King of Kings and has been impressing management ever since his return after a lengthy absence from the ring. Who will step out on top in this match? Also... -A man at King of Kings has said he will make his return to the ring at ReVolution 238. Who is the mystery man? -Lisa Tyler promised a surprise for a certain individual at ReVolution 238. Word through the grapevine is that this surprise relates to Nitz Donnelly's 5 Star bounty not being collected during the Jewel in the Crown tournament. Also scheduled to be at the show... Tyler Rayne Devin Shakur Violence Jack Hessian Big Bear Matt Ward And many others
