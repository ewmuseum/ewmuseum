PRIME Returns to Air Next Week?

7 October 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 31st was the last time PRIME appeared on any sort of media market with Culture Shock. The Pay-Per-View had its highs and lows (most of them low at least in terms of production value and overall quality). Not a peep has been heard from anybody in the higher ups, and talent has been left oblivious, not to mention looking for jobs while the company sorts out whatever problems they may have. Some rumors suggested the mob had involvement and those in the know have been dealing with the problem. Others suggested everything from insider trading to threats of murder made by certain executives if specific parties refused to stay silent with details regarding unnamed endeavors. One tabloid source even made the accusation Christian Daniels and Devin Shakur got involved in an international incident and have been keeping themselves out of sight until the heat dies down. All of this led many to believe the company would be closing shop and not even going through with the five ReVolution finale and two night Colossus VIII Pay-Per-View. But a source very close to Lisa Tyler has confirmed that the company is working tirelessly to restructure its deal with HBO so the network will air ReVolutions 248-252 and three one-hour specials before the year ends. The deal is said to be "95% done" but needs the approval of Shakur before all systems can go forward. Separate talks have also begun with the network on bolstering the Culture Shock Pay-Per-View and adding several missing pieces that were left out of the initial production. If Tyler's work pays off, the source continued, the company could air a two night relaunch package with the Pay-Per-View airing Saturday, October 15th and ReVolution 248 on Sunday, October 16th. Even though I'm not being paid to post any of this, or keep anybody abreast of behind the scenes matters, I felt it as my duty being a fan of professional wrestling to inform any and all that might be curious regarding the status of the company. More updates as they come out.
