ReVolution 241 Card

6 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME makes its debut in Istanbul, Turkey on the Road to Culture Shock with ReVolution 241 live from the Sinan Erdem Dome on Friday, March 11th. Airtime in the United States is still 10 PM EST. Opening Bout Katterina Wylde Vs Mitchell Quinlan The Dark Angel is a veteran of many federations, more so than most would experience in three or four wrestling careers. However, she’s having a tough time adjusting to the stiff competition PRIME has brought, and has been unable to capture her first victory. Mitchell Quinlan, a former SCCW star, debuted last week unsuccessfully against Brian Postal. One of these superstars is guaranteed to walk out the victor and get a win on his or her record. Lance Marshall Vs Brian Postal Brian Postal captured a surprising victory over Quinlan last week. The only reason most are considering it surprising is because Postal was deemed unfit to wrestle by a medical doctor only moments before the match, yet was able to gut out a brave performance and capture the victory, moments before collapsing in the aisleway. His status for 241 is questionable, but he does not seem to be backing down from the challenge of a debuting Lance Marshall, who last week was in a situation that could have turned nasty against Christian Daniels. With the ink apparently dry on Marshall’s contract, he is now able to step in the ring and show why he was one of SCCW’s finest attractions. Hessian Vs Jacob McKail The Murder Show is going through what many would consider a grind. On 242, he’s going to have to deal with the pressure of defending his Universal Championship against a man who many feel is capable of taking it off his hands and making history at the same time, Matt Ward. He’s also gaining weight and appears to be more sluggish as the day goes by. Jacob McKail has not been without his own war though, encountering the unique Seymour Almasy in a battle that, as we found out last week, rages all the way back to the days both were on the independent scene. With these two set to square off, who is going to have momentum heading into ReVolution 242: UltraViolence, the champion or a man who could become a part of the discussion in three seconds? Main Event Roxy Phoenix Vs Alexandra Pierce Alexandra Pierce has left her mark on PRIME in quite a short span. She got to the finals of Jewel in the Crown 2010, and has beaten everybody in front of her in Almasy, Tom Walczak, and Enemigo VII. She’s also drawn the ire of Devin Shakur, who is looking for any possible way he can to rattle The Spider in the Web’s cage. Roxy Phoenix on the other hand is going to get the biggest test of her PRIME career, squaring off against the GTT7 winner and a chance to make even more head waves than she already has. Who will come out victorious, and for the matter, with such high profile participants, will any interference be seen? Also scheduled to appear… Nitz Donnelly Lindsay Troy Vangelus Olsig Devin Shakur Seymour Almasy Tom Walczak Big Bear Bryan Dawkins And will the mystery man who was creeping around on ReVolution 240 finally be revealed? All this and more when PRIME returns to HBO on Friday, March 11th.
