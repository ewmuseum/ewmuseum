Big Bear Returns; Rayne Gets Fired in Hectic ReV 231

28 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ReVolution 231 proved noteworthy in many regards, the biggest of which featured a return from Big Bear. The show opened with Devin Shakur continuing his spiel of crazy, looking for a cheap pop from the Manchester crowd. After toying with his audience, Shakur got to business and brought up a pressing issue, the Colossus entrance of Tyler Rayne and the public controversy surrounding that event. Shakur informed the PRIME World he had been in on a few Board of Directors meetings and the most appropriate action would be to indefinitely suspend The Underground Pimp following the night's broadcast. Needless to say, the crowd was up in arms and came close to rioting. He also vowed to reveal the individual who sabotaged his Colossus match with Tony Gamble. Matt Mills became the unexpected star of a segment, or at least his reaction to Rayne's suspension was. Mills had words with The Golden Boy over the telephone and was ecstatic upon finding out Rayne had a trick up his sleeve for The Boss. That trick was on his way to the building and would be revealed by night's end. Mills felt confident, so much so that he made a quiet but snide remark about Shakur while he walked by. Next, Patterson Loch came out and gave an address to the crowd. He wasn't too pleased with the outcome of his match against Nitz Donnelly. He showed evidence proving Donnelly did indeed illegally use the ropes, but nobody in the audience seemed to mind. Their overwhelming support of Donnelly was so intense it forced Loch to leave the ring. The first interrogation took place with The Boss in Black and #1 Contender to the Universal Championship, Hessian. The Murder Show's interrogation didn't last long, as Hessian pulled Shakur out of craziness and into a brief moment of reality, speaking of a deal between the two. This remembrance made Shakur eliminate Hessian as a suspect and then tell him to vacate the room. Boda made his way out to ringside next, proudly displaying the 5*Star Championship and gloating about his accomplishments, and demeaning his future challengers, Nitz Donnelly and Vangelus Olsig. It didn't take long for Donnelly to come out and blast Boda for wearing a New York Yankees jersey in New England. The Ego ended up trying on the 5*Star belt as a sign of disrespect from Boda, but ended up getting the last laugh as he was able to escape from the area with the strap while Boda was left with the egg on his face. Newly appointed authority figure Lisa Tyler found herself going back into her old ways by getting into a confrontation with Hessian. The two spoke at length about Hessian finally being on the verge of holding the Universal strap and how he ought to behave, even before becoming the Champion. Hessian said he would still be the Murder Show but fall in line with company requests. Matt Mills once again became the star of a segment, although the situation was far from ideal. He was snatched out of a corridor and brought face to face with Devin Shakur. The Boss, angry he didn't find any information from Hessian, was determined to get Mills to talk. He did so by getting in his face and threatening him. After a few mental lapses from Shakur, he backhanded Mills so hard he knocked the interviewer out of his chair. This brought a knock on the door from a concerned bystander, former PRIME interviewer, Angelica Brooks. She hasn't been around on even a limited schedule since ReVolution 170, but is being interviewed for her job by Lisa Tyler. Shakur also briefly questioned Brooks about her involvement, to see if she knew anything about the incoming signee, or Colossus, and was unable to provide any new information. Shakur excused her, possibly because he didn't believe someone of her size could orchestrate something against such a massive individual as Hessian. However, Shakur handcuffed Mills to the chair and was ready to get answers at any cost. Before Shakur could do that though, which makes one wonder what exactly happened to Mills while Shakur was gone, The Boss had to compete against The First. The match went somewhat as expected, at least in the early going. Shakur started out with a flurry, but First survived the storm and came back with some offense of his own. He dominated The Boss for most of the match, until a misstep late brought Shakur back into the contest. The Boss was about ready to put First away when Tyler Rayne made an appearance in the crowd and distracted Shakur. The Boss was unable to resist prodding Rayne, and was distracted long enough for First to gain a pinfall victory. Afterward, Shakur flipped his lid in the ring. Making a quick transition backstage, and proving he really does have superhuman speed, Tyler Rayne walked down a corridor and had a brief encounter with his opponent later on in the evening, Hessian. The two had a brief back and forth before going their separate ways. The First had a brief segment backstage with his manager, Muse, about First and his place in PRIME. The Painted Up Freak said he wouldn't be Castor's lackey, and he would only gain success in PRIME by winning and losing. Muse didn't seem to be pleased with the scenario. In the final interrogation scene of the night, Lindsay Troy and Dan Ryan were summoned into Shakur's office. The Boss and Dan Ryan had some back and forth banter, while Troy was put under the intense spotlight of Shakur and his rapid fire questions. Troy would only divulge information if Shakur got the light out of her face. He obliged, and despite requests from Ryan for a panini press, Troy gave Shakur a few hints, saying the person responsible was three feet in front of him. In true, psychotic fashion, Shakur got a measuring tape out and counted off three feet to find Troy was in that exact spot. One kick to the package later and Shakur knew his attacker's identity. This made The Boss hellbent on finding out who Rayne was talking about as the new signing, so he made his way toward the ring. The Universal Champion, Castor Von Strife came out to address the crowd, and said they would have to enjoy him as champion whether they wanted to or not. He ran down his opponent at 234, Hessian. It took all of about two minutes for Hessian to appear once he heard his name, and he came ready with words for the Champ. Proving he didn't have any fear, at least for the time being, Castor dared Hessian to step into the ring. After another exchange, Castor slapped Hessian in the face. The crowd was stunned to say the least. The Murder Show went for retaliation, but Strife slid between the legs and got out of Dodge. It was probably the safest bet he could have made. The Boss in Black made his way down to the squared circle, hobbling a bit from the nut shot he received only a segment ago. He demanded to know who the identity of this mystery person was. It didn't take long for an answer to be manifested in the form of Big Bear, who hadn't been seen in the PRIME landscape in over a few years. The ovation he received was inarguably the biggest of the night. Big Bear also had a PRIME contract in hand and signed on the dotted line, further enduring the rage of Shakur. The Boss kicked Big Bear out of the building just seconds before the Main Event and joined Nick and Richard at the announcers table. The Main Event wasn't necessarily a Main Event, as Tyler Rayne and Hessian were unable to get off to a match before Shakur, Daniels, and company bolted into the ring and started a three on one beatdown of The Underground Pimp. Rayne fought vehemently to keep himself afloat, but in the end he was overwhelmed by the numbers game. He received a Black Hole Slam, chokeslam, two Good Times Painful Memories kicks, and a Holy Shit on Hessian's knee. The series of moves was made even more painful when, afterward, Shakur got to a microphone and fired The Pimp. At the moment, Big Bear made his way through the crowd, while Troy and Dan Ryan fought their way past Castor Strife and Boda to get out to the ring and chase off the Assholes with Attitude.
