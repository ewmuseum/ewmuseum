It Could Happen To You

12 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That attack has landed O'Neil behind bars, and the condemnation from up and down the PRIME roster and office has been overwhelming, with PRIME management going so far as to refuse him bail. But according to Angelo Deville, no one is more disgusted than he. "If he'd worn purple and been accompanied by The Hamburglar, maybe the shit would've made some sense." Naturally, Deville took offence to O'Neil's time with him in the ring, not the dastardly actions that spectacle inspired. "Those kids? Fuck those kids. It's about time The Scarecrow realized how fickle -- how expendable -- those locker-room-rats are. If they didn't want to get smacked down, they should've remembered their pussiness before the fact and acted accordingly. "The real story here is that for the first time in our lives, Jason O'Neil and Angelo Deville had a bad, a lacklustre, a fucking laughingstock of a match. And why? For what possible reason? He can't skip rope for ten minutes a day, do a biceps curl or two? Krispy Kreme is just too oh-so-delicious? I'm sure those things played a factor for that foppish oaf, but the bottom line is that he's washed up. Finished. Finito. Rovinato. And, really, there's nothing to be ashamed of there. I mean, he had a good run -- a fucking spectacular run -- but that's been over with for a while. The man spent his best years going toe to toe with yours truly – there’s no shame being left a ruin. "These guys see Snow and I doing it, coming back after years away and not missing a beat, and they think 'Hey, maybe I can do it too.' But the sad reality -- for them -- is that they can't. Their heart is there, but it's too weak to bring the body and mind along for the ride. They're too weak, period." When asked if he was referring to Xavier Kannon, still recovering in a hospital bed, and for comments on the despicable alliance that put him there, on the arguably shocking turn of Nova, his telltale smirk reached through the phone with the nonchalant smack of his uncompromising ego. "Wyatt'll reveal his mind in good time. I called you to talk about O'Neil. Don't presume to change the subject, or I’ll provide you a physical reminder of your inferiority instead of just calling you a glorified telemarketing gossip-prostitute." He paused, apparently awaiting an invitation. "But since I was planning to mention Kannon anyhow, as the topic of your post better damn well illustrate: Hey Kannon. Did you see O'Neil? That could've been you, sport. "You're welcome."
