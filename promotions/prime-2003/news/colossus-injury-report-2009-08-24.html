Colossus Injury Report

24 August 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The show was chock full of memorable matches, eccentric entrances, the departure of a legend, and every PRIME championship up for grabs. Wyatt Connors and Desade even dropped by to have an Elite Championship match with Hessian. To read all of the festivities, click here for Night 1 and here for Night 2. As is with every Pay-Per-View, the superstars put themselves through hell and back to come out victorious and give the fans a show. The Colossus medical team was put through the ringer dealing with the injuries of the night. Here are the ones that got out: Troy Douglas suffered a mild left knee sprain and dislocated his right thumb in his match against Brandon Pierce, but is said to be healthy and will compete at ReVolution 203 against The Murder Show Hessian. Wicked Ways were both feeling a little light headed, but refused medical treatment and stormed from the building after losing their Tag Team Championships. Neither man showed up for Night 2. Wade Elliott had one of his worst performances to date, getting completely dominated by Chandler Tsonda. Word around the locker room was he needed smelling salts to stay conscious and get to his truck, but also refused an ambulance ride to the hospital. Tyler Nelson had two ice packs around his neck (which were just plastic bags with ice in them, dude isn't made of money. He freaked out when they started melting.) from The Supreme Justice he received from Killean Sirrajin. All reports are he was just hamming it up to make Lisa Tyler feel bad, or get first class airfare accommodations for 203. BLT has slated The Greediest Player in the Game to compete at The Honda Center against Tony Gamble. He's also reportedly flying coach. Garbage Bag Johnny was seen passed out with his head in a janitor's bucket after his Night 2 opening contest against Nova and Sonny Silver. He woke up four hours after everybody left and decided to spend the night with the mops and brooms. On top of losing his PRIME career, Jay Phoenix also suffered a broken nose, two cracked ribs, and may have been unconscious at one point in the hallways, although that portion can't be confirmed. Bryan Dawkins was seen limping around the corridors with a neckbrace on, unable to move his neck to the left side. The tag team of Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels are to thank for this. The Flyin' Hawaiian is expected to miss ReVolution 203 and be taken off house shows for the near future. In perhaps the most tragic injury of the evening, and maybe the fabric of PRIME, Tony Gamble was set to have a grand entrance into night 2's main event, but injured himself during pre-show rehearsals when he got a blister opening a really tight Powerade bottle. An MRI has come back negative on any severe nerve or ligament damage, but Tony has decided to look for a second opinion since it hurt like the dickens. Lisa Tyler is more than likely not paying for the second opinion.
