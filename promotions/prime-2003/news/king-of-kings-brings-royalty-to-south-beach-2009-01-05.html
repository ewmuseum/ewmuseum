King of Kings Brings Royalty to South Beach

5 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Merry Melee match was one of the longest non-Dual Halo matches in PRIME history, lasting nearly the whole night. There were presents for some, coal for others, and one very large unwrapped present that brought the whole shindig crashing down. In the first of two semifinal matches, two old-but-new competitors showed their stuff. Rhett Locke, former OSW Champion, and recent owner of a hot streak in PRIME, took on Jason Snow, legendary 4-time PCW Champion and recent PRIME returnee. The result had one walking tall, all the way to the night's main event. Then, in the only tag action of the night, Team V.I.A.G.R.A. defended its long-held belts against Delta Upsilon Iota, except with some small modifications. Tony Davis and Hank Cobb showed up, but with some unexpected seconds. You won't wanna miss seeing who, exactly, showed up to duke it out for tag gold. The second of the JITC semifinal matches saw a rematch of one of the recent PRIME classics: High Flyer v. Tyler Rayne. Weeks ago, Rayne dropped the 5-Star Title, which had been his and only his for the better part of 2008, to Flyer. If you think the Underground Pimp wasn't ready to score some revenge on Flyer, who had a pretty damn 2008 himself, you probably repeated a couple grades in elementary school. PRIME fancies itself on always having the "next big thing" somewhere on its roster. Hell, it's what King of Kings is all about. But this year, we had too many talents to include all those "next big things" in the official ceremony. And a guy who keeps coming up in that conversation is Bryan Dawkins. Dusk, meanwhile, has been on the doorstep of greatness for quite some time. The winner of the showdown between these two would be offered a shot at the 5-Star Title...but who wanted it more? Rarely does the Universal Title take a backseat to, well, anything in PRIME. But King of Kings is one of those times, so Chandler Tsonda found himself in the night's penultimate act, culminating years of rivalry with Danny Ferguson. He was ready to put his title on the line to settle things with Ferguson, who had tormented him from underneath his El Spiffy! mask. But there was a twist, always a twist with the Superstar. And then...there was that hallowed ritual, the anointing of a new Jewel. Names like Tony Gamble, Tchu, and Ian English stir the memories of any true PRIMEate and one man had a chance to join that select company. To see who would hold a guaranteed Universal Title shot for the first night of Culture Shock, as well as who wore elf costumes and what SCCW face showed up, put on your reading glasses, grab some hot cocoa, and enjoy PRIME's last offering of 2008!.
