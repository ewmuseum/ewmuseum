Hartford's Hoppin' from ReV 141

13 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Surprise, surprise: Tony Gamble & Devin Shakur caused a little trouble for...well, almost everybody. And Shakur had to team up with Brandon Youngblood against the not-quite-face-but-not-quite-heel-who-the-hell-are-these-guys A-List combo of Chandler Tsonda & Danny Ferguson. And the in-ring return? We can spoil the surprise in telling you that it's an old boss, one Worth a fair amount of hype. He faced the shorter, more mocked half of the two villains in the night's main event. Dare we call it a barnburner? Not to be outplayed, Hoyt Williams had something very serious to share with not just the PRIME audience, but all Uncle Sam's peeps. And Boston's (least) favorite son was back...with vulgarity to spare. Suffice to say, he was a bit...candid about his desires. But, as per usual, this small space barely scratches the surface. There was a bevvy of action, all of it with that special PRIME flavor. We're throwing it back to the old NBC line: if you ain't seen it, it's new to you. Just call us must-see PRIME, baby.
