FERGUSON: 'I'M DONE WITH PRIME'

2 January 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The short interview mainly discusses the film and his possible recognition, but one question in particular covered Ferguson's tenure in PRIME and revealed that his current hiatus will extend indefinitely. Full text of the exchange is below: US: You've spent a lot of time this year in the ring, between filming the movie and keeping up regular appearances with current sports entertainment juggernaut PRIME until an injury in October. Should we expect more of that in the future? DF: Probably not. I had a fun couple of years in PRIME; it gave me a nice break from film work in '05 and helped me prepare for the Funk role in '06. But I finished up my commitments there and I'm ready to move on. It's time to find my next role. PRIME was very good to me, but I'm done with that chapter in my career. There's no confirmation that Ferguson's contract with PRIME has, in fact, been terminated. As previously reported, the deal runs through August of 2008, with no stipulated "out" clauses. Additionally, there doesn't seem to be any evidence that Ferguson notified PRIME officials of his intentions before the interview. When reached, representatives Blaine Blair and Lisa Tyler declined to comment on Ferguson's status with the company. Ferguson remains under medical suspension after suffering a broken nose and concussion against Devin Shakur at the Great American Nightmare Pay-Per-View show. The loss to Shakur was his first in a singles match for the duration of 2007. He was rumored to be in line for a Universal Title match before the hiatus. However, any speculation about Danny Ferguson's future in PRIME seems to have ended abruptly.
