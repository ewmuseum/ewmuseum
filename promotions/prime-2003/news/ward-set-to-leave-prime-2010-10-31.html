Ward set to Leave PRIME?

31 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors have begun to swirl that PRIME Hall of Famer Matt Ward may very shortly be leaving PRIME. In November of 2009, Ward made a surprise return to the federation as an entrant in the Jewel in the Crown Tournament, defeating Tony Gamble. As the Inhuman Being embarked on a run to the semi-finals at King of Kings, it was revealed that the former two-time Universal Champion had signed a one-year contract to return to PRIME on a full schedule. With that contract set to expire in the coming weeks, much talk has begun in regards to Ward's future, with most rumors and whispers backstage indicating that the 34 year old superstar will retire from the ring. "There's a lot going on in his personal life," one source close to Ward said, "and I get the feeling he may want to focus his energies on things other than the business of professional wrestling." Of course, much may hinge on Ward's performance in the PTC Elite Title Tournament Finals, a triple threat match against Jared Sykes (SCCW) and Vivica J. Valentine (GCW). Ward, who has had much success in PTC competition, capturing both the IG and Unified Championships, was hand-selected by the PRIME board to represent the federation in the tournament. Winning the Elite Title might be enough to persuade Ward to sign an extension. Still, it's possible he could simply retire from a full-time schedule, competing on rare occasions. The result of the Elite title match, and any implications it might have on the PRIME Hall of Famer will be known soon enough, as Ward is in St. Louis, Missouri tonight to compete on WorldWide 140, meaning the Inhuman Being won't be on ReVolution 232. That will make two straight weeks without Ward present at ReV, only further fueling rumors that he may be severing ties with PRIME in the coming weeks. We'll be sure to bring you any and all updates on this issue as soon as the news breaks.
