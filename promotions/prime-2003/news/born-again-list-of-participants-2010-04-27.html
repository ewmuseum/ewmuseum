Born Again: List of Participants

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
According to Shakur's announcement, the tournament is structured as followed: There will be two brackets of wrestlers, a 'Stalwarts' and 'New Era' bracket. One is put into the Stalwarts bracket if they have participated in PRIME previously, and the New Era bracket consists of wrestlers who have never seen a PRIME ring in their wrestling careers. A winner from each bracket will go onto fight for the Universal Championship in a Triple Threat match with Dual Halo winner Vangelus Olsig at the UltraViolence Pay-Per-View in early June. Shakur made the tournament open to free agents and wrestlers from the past. According to multiple sources, calls were limited at first but picked up in the days leading to ReVolution 221. All in all, twenty one wrestlers will compete for the chance to become the Universal Championship. Twelve fall into the PRIME Stalwarts bracket while nine settle in the New Era bracket. Details of the matchups have not been released. When we tried to reach Devin Shakur for comment about the structure, Christian Daniels replied Shakur was in Monte Carlo trying to figure out who online poker sensation isildur1 is. The PRIME Stalwarts bracket features most of the roster: 5 Star Champion Elise Ares, Intense Champion Tchu, Chainz, Boda, Nitz Donnelly, Troy Douglas, Hessian, Bryan Dawkins Tyler Rayne, and some surprise entrants. Garbage Bag Johnny was offered a spot in the tournament and accepted. Emilio Rage also accepted the invitation for the tournament as did David Noble. Those round out the eleven of the Stalwarts bracket. The New Era bracket consists of names familiar to some but obviously not to hardcore fans of PRIME: Vic Thunder, Castor V Strife, Bishop Steele, Roxie Sykes, Crazy Joe, Hush, Skylar Montgomery, Shawn Jessica Hart and Dual Halo participant Black Death. While he competed in the Halo, he is still considered New Era in that he's never competed outside of the Invitational Halo. More details are expected in the coming days as ReVolution 222 will hinder entirely upon the tournament and hail from The Palace of Auburn Hills
