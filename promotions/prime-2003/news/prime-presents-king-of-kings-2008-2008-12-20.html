PRIME Presents: King of Kings 2008

20 December 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-The first ever Merry Melee match will kick things off, and will feature former Jewel in the Crown competitors as well as current PRIME stars. Santa will be leaving some presents at the American Airlines arena, and PRIME fans will have to see just who's been naughty and who's been nice! -The first Jewel in the Crown semi-final match will play host to up-and-coming PRIME star Rhett Locke taking on former 5 Star champ Jason Snow. Snow was considered a favorite to win the Jewel in the Crown since the announcement of his participation, but Locke is looking to cement his status as a force to be reckoned with. -The tag team titles are on the line as Team VIAGRA takes on DUI. But there's a twist...Colby Korver of DUI is suspended, and Mary-Lynn Mayweather will be filling in for teammate High Flyer. Can Tony Davis help the very green Mayweather keep the titles? Or will Hank Cobb overcome the 2 on 1 handicap and bring the belts back to the Frathouse? -They met not that long ago for the 5 Star Title, and High Flyer managed to wrest the belt out of Tyler Rayne's iron (golden?) grip. Now, they meet again in the second Jewel in the Crown semi-final match. PRIME's Golden Boy is looking to put the cap on a stellar year with an advancement to the JitC finals, whereas High Flyer is looking to make sure Rayne realizes that his 5 Star win was no fluke. -They may have lost their quarterfinal matches, but no good effort goes overlooked. CP Cantrell is looking for the next hot feud to kick off 2009, and either Dusk or Bryan Dawkins will be walking away as the number one contender to the 5 Star Title. -They were once friends...kind of. If you ask them now, they'd deny they ever liked each other, even one iota. Now the returning Danny Ferguson will meet Chandler Tsonda for so much more than just the Universal Title. Pride and ego will be on the line, and only one can claim the spotlight as their own once and for all. -Snow vs. Rayne? Locke vs. Flyer? Flyer vs. Snow? Rayne vs. Locke? Four possible Jewel in the Crown matches. Four possible Culture Shock headliners. We're not sure the seat edges will be able to take the suspense of the Jewel in the Crown Finals.
