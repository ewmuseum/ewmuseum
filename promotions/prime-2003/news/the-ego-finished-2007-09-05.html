The Ego Finished?

5 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nicholas Donnelly, the man who secured himself the largest signing bonus in PRIME history just a couple short months ago may now find himself as part of the unemployment line. Lisa Tyler appeared on the big screen in Kentucky last Wednesday and told the world that The Ego of New England was suspended for his actions involving the attack of the Universal Champion. She also hinted that there were some other situations involving the Biggest Mouth in Sports Entertainment and PRIME Management that will be under investigation. Those details have not been released. Lisa confirmed that Donnelly is on thin ice regarding his contract. One would assume with a performance based contract not earning him anything on suspension, that The Ego may just ask for his release to pursue other opportunities. Lisa may not hesitate to grant it to him, but there may be a question of ethics regarding advertising the man as the next big thing and then releasing him shortly after. It is no secret that Donnelly has been a thorn in the side of Lisa Tyler from the very minute he set foot in a PRIME arena. But there were also rumors circulating that management was working on a brand new contract for The Ego... one that was not entirely performance based. Nothing has been confirmed by either party. Lisa Tyler, Blaine Blair or anyone else in Management could not be reached for comment on the seemingly delicate issue. But Nitz Donnelly has confirmed that he will address the extra "situation being investigated" the only way he knows how... by running his mouth. What will his outlet be now that he is suspended from PRIME? What will The Ego have to say? You can bet that once something breaks, we will have the scoop right here.
