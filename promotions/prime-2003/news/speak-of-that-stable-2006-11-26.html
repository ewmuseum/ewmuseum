Speak of That Stable...

26 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These statements came less than twenty-four hours after ReVolution 112, which ended with the stable-that-cannot-be-named going to town on the resourceful owner, Nova's "We're gonna take our fuckin' time" punctuating the abuse and echoing in every mind as the PRIME logo usurped the airwaves. How savage that beating ended up being is unknown at this point, but the initial medical assessment did not bode well for Chet, who had to be rushed to hospital on a gurney. The attack was precipitated by Worth's involvement in Nova's Jewel in the Crown match against Bobby Stormbringer. Nova was in full control throughout, as anyone with a mind would have expected, but at the crucial moment 'The Most Laid Back CEO Ever' intervened, holding Nova down to be pinned. A brawl erupted right then and there, but the return of Lindsay Troy, aiding Universal Champion Tchu and legend Killean Sirrajin in their quest to face down the odds, spared him his just desserts. "I warned that idiot," Deville went on. "I fucking warned him. I tried to play nice with him, tried to keep up the appearance that he was in charge so he could feel all big and bad and tough. But that wasn't enough. Oh no, not for Chet Worth. He had to go all Montreal on us, trying to screw Nova out of what he has rightfully earned a million times over. He knew when he grabbed Nova's leg what was going to happen to him, and if he didn't, he should've." When asked if he meant "all Montreal" as in queer and French or if he was citing the most infamous screwjob in the business, Deville backhanded me. "For being even more stupid than Worth," came the clarification, along with the threat of another for good measure. "Once you can hear and make sense of words again, you mark what I'm saying, Worth, and you mark it well: Nova willbe facing Tchu at King of Kings for the Universal strap, over your dead body if need be. And if you don't like it . . . well, what's my stable's name, bitch?"
