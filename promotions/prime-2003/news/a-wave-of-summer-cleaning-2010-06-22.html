A Wave of Summer Cleaning

22 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The official releases of four wrestlers were made public today. Black Death established a presence in the 2010 Dual Halo, but only made limited appearances after the Pay-Per-View. A source within PRIME claims multiple attempts from PRIME personnel were made in an attempt to reach Black Death but none were received. Bishop Steele was another individual who came in with considerable hype surrounding his name, but he ultimately didn't pan out to be the success PRIME was hoping for. He participated in the New Era tournament but never made it out of Round 1. The Board of Directors finally came to terms on his release as well. Bryan Dawkins also found himself the recipient of a pink slip. In what appeared to be a long time coming, Devin Shakur was able to get the entire Board of Directors to overturn any encouragement of Tyler Rayne to keep The Flyin' Hawaiian on the roster. The Man in Black claimed, and convinced The Board, Dawkins didn't have the proper amount of dedication necessary for him to be a valuable member of PRIME any longer. He was subsequently released, and it is likely Tyler Rayne will be none too pleased when he hears the news. Roxie Sykes was also given her release from PRIME. This came as somewhat of a surprise, considering she held a banked 5 Star Championship match for her second place finish on the New Era side of the Born Again bracket. Details into her release aren't being publicized yet. PRIME appreciates the efforts put forth by all competitors during their stints in the company and wishes them the best with future career decisions.
