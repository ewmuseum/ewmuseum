This Ain't No Horse Race!

3 September 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a ReVolution of Pay-Per-View proportions, lines were drawn, pacts were made, and titles were put on the line in what was an intense showing as we creep ever closer to PRIME's newest Pay-Per-View: Dice, Dollars & Tragedy! Tag Team competitors, Ian Nackedy and Mike Wade faced off in singles action, with the end resulting in a less than orthodox manner. Dusk looked to step off the losing streak, as The Lost Soul, Chandler Tsonda, and Tyler Rayne jumped into a triple threat match. Could Dusk get back on track? Or did the Model Citizen and Underground Pimp have other plans? The PRIME Pariah, Brandon Youngblood, returned to the ring in a monster match against Irish Danny Ferguson. Did The Only Diamond in Our Sport prove to remain a top-end competitor post neck surgery? Or did Ferguson have plans to send him back to the hospital? Tag Teamers may have tried the singles ring, but singles competitors had their take with partners as well. Hated enemies, Intense Champ Devin Shakur and Tony 'The Grin' Gamble, were booked against former Intense Champion, Pierce Lavelle and PRIME's number one Intense Title Contender, "The Big Dog," Wade Elliott. But did everything go according to plan? Or did The Drifter's hot head put him between a rock and a hard place? And finally, the Universal Strap was on the line in a re-match of Colossus IV's history-making title bout. The Queen of the Ring and Universal Champion, Lindsay Troy, stood toe-to-toe once again with Sonny Silver. Did Troy keep her death-grip on the title? Or did her knee injury at the hands of Silver himself turn Sonny into "the King of the Ring?" Also, which superstar threatened to resign? Which hated enemies put aside their differences? Who's temper resulted in a costly decision? And which wrestler dropped out of the ten man roulette match at Dice, Dollars & Tragedy? You have questions? We have answers. And they're only a click away.
