ReVolution 238 Sees Blockbuster New Signing.

5 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If PRIME's ReVolution 238 will be remembered for only one thing, it's this: the GTT7 champion, Alexandra "Desade" Pierce has been signed to a long-term deal with the federation she was once at war with. Pierce, who wrestled a 37-second "exhibition" match against Enemigo VII at the Paris event, has long been announced as residing in the French capital, and was in town for a farewell to the federation after her successful run in the 2010 Jewel in the Crown tournament. The King of Kings pay-per-view attraction saw the self-proclaimed Queen of Lies wrestle two breathtaking bouts, including the finals against eventual winner Bruce "Violence Jack" Shanahan that appeared on many Match of the Year lists for 2010. "We're very excited to announce this signing," Lisa Tyler said in a press release after the event. "Alexandra Pierce claims she is the best wrestler on the planet, and she will have an opportunity to prove that to be the case in upcoming weeks." It's a neutrally-worded announcement, which comes as little surprise given the woman's tumultuous history with several of the federation's top stars, most notably reigning Intense Champion Lindsay Troy. In the lead-up to the September 2008 PRIME/SCCW crossover event, Cataclysm, Pierce and her colleagues in the now disbanded Dead Man's Hand faction came to PRIME and committed several heinous actions, culminating in Vincent "Mayhem" Matthews driving interviewer Angelica Brooks into the hood of Tyler Rayne's car. Which made Rayne himself using the powers granted by his Golden Contract to hire the controversial former SCCW superstar all the more surprising. Rayne is said to have approached the Spider in the Web himself, offering what has only been termed a "lucrative" deal to sway Pierce into signing on the dotted line. He claimed on the program to have done so for one reason and one reason only: to bring in a new player to bring her particular kind of troubles to PRIME head man Devin Shakur. Recent weeks have seen the federation's fortunes rise, but their closest competitor in Global Championship Wrestling continues to expand, so on the face of it, the signing seems like a win. Still, one wonders how hiring one of PRIME's biggest enemies can ever turn out well. One thing is for certain as PRIME moves to Turin, Italy, site of ReVolution 239: fans should tune in to see what Pierce's first steps against Shakur will be – and how the Devil in Black might react.
