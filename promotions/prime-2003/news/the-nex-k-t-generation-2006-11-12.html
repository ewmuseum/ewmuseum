PRIME — News archive

The NeX(K)t Generation

12 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lisa: After the events of the Great American Nightmare, emotions must be running high, so what do you say to those who claim you're biting off more than you can chew this early in your career? Alexander: I know the trainers at the PWA have said publicly that I'm not ready. But if I can't be ready for a match against the Renegade after what went down at the Great American Nightmare, I'll never be ready. Angelo Deville, Rich Rollins, and Jason Snow took the name my uncle gifted onto me, spat on it, and stamped it into the ring live on PPV... now its my duty to scrape that name up, and repair the damage they did. Lisa: Many were surprised to hear your name drawn in the Jewel in the Crowd 1st round, as PWA head-trainer Justin Williamson has only recently referred to you as an 'unpolished diamond', so how did your name come to end up in the hat? Alexander: Right now PRIME is in the midst of a huge new talent initiative, as you can tell by the likes of Ember, Malachi, Delgado, and N showing up recently. They already called up Sun Tzu from the PWA and she's been having amazing success, so when they needed another new name for the Jewel in the Crown, they offered me a spot... probably knowing I'd bite their fingers off at the prospect of wrestling alongside my uncle. But after recent events, Deville, Snow, and Rollins have stolen that opportunity from me, and it's become a revenge mission. Lisa: One other wrestler on a revenge mission is multi-time PTC Global Champion Seymour Almasy, who may have done you a giant favour last week by softening up Rich Rollins in a shocking assault. While PRIME security has been beefed up this week in light of recent events, have you prepared for the Almasy factor, should it arise? Alexander: Seymour Almasy is a legend, plain and simple, and I have every respect for both him, and his accomplishments all around the wrestling world. But, this match isn't about him, it isn't about GCW, it isn't about anything but the Kannons and Fu... Eff U. If he ends up between me and the Renegade then, well, I won't be earning myself many friends amongst Final Fantasy fans on Wednesday night. Lisa: Well, PRIME fans who've been following your progress, as well as followers of the Kannon name, will be looking forward to your debut. So, in closing, do you have any parting words for the Renegade? Alexander: As the opening lyrics of my entrance theme will announce to Rollins at ReVolution... I declare WAR.
