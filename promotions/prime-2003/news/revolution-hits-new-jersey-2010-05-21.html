ReVolution Hits New Jersey

21 May 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The evening started off with New Era Born Again action. Bishop Steele faced off against Hush and Vic Thunder in a triple threat extravaganza. Steele, who moments before the match, declared he would be victorious and go onto headline multiple cards in PRIME, faced the almost silent newcomer and massive Thunder in a brawl that went down to the bitter end. Next, Tyler Rayne made his long awaited return to PRIME action alongside David Noble and Hessian. Or did he? Did this match go down as slated? You'll have to read on to find out. Was Noble able to upset Rayne? Did Hessian muscle both competitors over? Did The Underground Pimp manage to pull out a victory and advance? In the second New Era match, Castor V. Strife took on Skylar Montgomery and Shawn Hart in a wild match. Three eccentric personalities crammed into one match. Would the ring be able to contain all of them and who came out victorious? The Main Event saw a very angry Chainz take on Bryan Dawkins and Nitz Donnelly. Dawkins and Donnelly spent the majority of the time taking the beating of the enraged Chainz, but was Chainz able to pull out the victory? Did the blood lust from earlier (say wha?) finally get the better of him? To find out the results, plus the bonus match, and what strange happenings went down on ReVolution 223, you'll need to read the show.
