Colossus Spotlight: Tony Gamble

24 August 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ever since the start of 2006, Gamble has been noted as one of the sleaziest wrestlers on the roster, but he has also been one of the staples of the federation, remaining loyal to the company which gave him his first mainstream success, "I only need one word to describe what PRIME means to me... Home. This is where I made my bones, where I made my name, and while countless others have either started out somewhere else and ended up here, or started here and gone and sowed their seeds somewhere else... I have been here, and when I decide to hang up my boots it will be here." Gamble made a gigantic splash at King of Kings 2006 when he defeated Adam in the Jewel in the Crown semifinals and later in the evening also pulled off one of the biggest upsets in PRIME history by making Angelo Deville tap out. He was awarded the Jewel in the Crown, deemed an inevitable Universal Champion, and slated to challenge fellow stablemate and newly anointed Universal Champion, Nova. Gamble came incredibly close to capturing the gold in a Culture Shock 2007 classic and also missed the boat in a triple threat match at UltraViolence 2007 against Nova and fellow challenger Tchu. Ever since being near the top of the mountain, Gamble has remained in the limelight, but not as a dominant presence. He would hang around, winning an Intense Championship and involving himself in feuds with Dusk and Wade Elliott, but it would take until the end of 2009 before Gamble held a sense of dominance over his fellow roster members again when he captured the 5-Star Championship from Jason Snow at ReVolution 205. Most in the company felt Gamble hit his stride once again and would soon be able to usurp Snow from his Universal Championship throne. Unfortunately for 'The Grin', longtime comrade and new boss Devin Shakur threw a wrench into his plans and stopped him from climbing the ladder at ReVolution 215 by playing a major part in allowing Elise Ares to take hold of the 5-Star Championship. Gamble is most known for the permanent scar on his face which gives the impression he is always smiling. He credits the scar for gaining him recognition amongst fans, "If it weren't for this damn scar that I cursed when I first decided to step back into the ring I don't think I'd be the face of PRIME I am today." Over a five year period, Gamble has stepped into the ring with the best in the world, collected three singles reigns (Internet Title reign included) and been involved in countless controversial moments. Colossus, as an event, doesn't bode well for Gamble in terms of wins and losses. He was defeated by Jonathon Winters at Colossus III, failed to get past a tag match for the 5-Star Championship at Colossus IV, and won against Tony Rolo at Colossus V. Despite the 1-2 record, Gamble still has a great respect for the spectacle of Colossus, "You're probably going to hear a lot of this, but it is the grandest stage of them all. Nothing in this industry compares to the spectacle that is Colossus. I remember when I first stepped into a ring at Colossus. I was so damn nervous I think I threw up breakfast, lunch, and the diner I had yet to eat. Since then I've cut it down to just chunking up breakfast, but I'm sure you can imagine just what being in a match at Colossus is like." Gamble's match at Colossus will be one with significant history behind it. He faces longtime friend, and now bitter rival, Devin Shakur. At the very get go of Shakur's career, he and Gamble were pitted against one another. After a bit of time at each other's throats, both realized an alliance would be more beneficial in the long term. Critics and fans alike dubbed Shakur and Gamble the second coming of Joey Troy and Tony Rolo in terms of backstage antics and in-ring tactics. The long anticipated blowup became official at 215 when Shakur manipulated Gamble out of the 5-Star Championship. Both have danced around for a few months, hiding the issue while others took center stage. August 29th will see Gamble and Shakur finally have a true one on one battle inside a steel cage. Gamble gave a breakdown of his opposition and boss. "You know, I have a lot of respect for Devin and all that he has done since he got here. Hell, it has to mean something when the man that came in and started off being that guy riding my coat tails to get noticed went on and held the Universal title before I did. Hell, I haven't even been able to catch a sniff of that belt since my match against Tchu and Nova like three years ago. So yeah, I have a little bit of respect for the man in black. Thing is, as much as I respect him, I also know that he is the slimiest piece of shit to ever step through the damn doors of PRIME. So, when this all comes to a head at Colossus, when the last hand slap against the mat is heard, you better believe it will be my hand being raised when it is all said and done. Then, I'll add him to the list of other former champions I have defeated. From Jason Snow to Vangelus Olsig, I have at one time or another had my arm raised in victory over former Universal champions... and at Colossus I will be adding the name of Devin Shakur to that list, then I'll be adding the Universal Title itself." Tony Gamble vs Devin Shakur will take place live from Qualcomm Stadium in San Diego, California on August 29th.
