Killean Doubtful for 168

21 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Things got carried away after the No DQ match. I have no problem admitting when something goes wrong at a broadcast, so let me just say that I believe that something so brutal and devastating should have never been aired on live television." Stuart said in a short interview less than 24 hours after the incident. Killean Sirrajin had just pinned Mr. Silver, Sports Entertainment Liaison to PRIME in a physically draining no disqualification match. Captain Justice, who was barred from ringside during the match, heard the bell ring and "took off like a bat out of hell" according to a member of the stage staff. "He was standing there one second and as soon as that bell rang, he was gone." Justice proceeded to jump Sirrajin from behind in the ring and after taking orders from Mr. Silver, they hung the original Grand Slam Champion from the turnbuckle with a steel chain. Killean suffocated and within minutes was not moving. Lindsay Troy and Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas came down the aisle quickly and Troy cut the chain with a pair of bolt cutters and Dametreyus Fuqueiawytas supported the weight of The Supreme Machine to prevent him from crashing to the ground. "Killean Sirrajin is a very lucky individual." EMT Josh Gregory said late Wednesday night. "He wasn't breathing and suffered some severe damage to his larynx. Time was of the essence. If Lindsay Troy hadn't of acted so quickly, I'm sorry to say we wouldn't be planning a recovery schedule for Killean. We'd be planning a funeral." Speaking of a recovery time, nothing has been set yet. However it is extremely doubtful that The PRIME Choice will be in attendance this coming week at ReVolution 168, the final event before Colossus V. We have been unable to get a statement from Sirrajin due to his injuries as it is said he is in an extreme amount of pain when he tries to talk. It's certain that nothing will keep him from Colossus in just a couple short weeks. But one can bet that if Killean does appear at ReVolution this week, he'll be out for blood.
