Post-Halo News

20 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- A close eye was being kept on Universal Champion Nova after he took a Box Office Bomb from Danny Ferguson in the Halo. Tests confirmed there was no concussion, but the Risen Star will be monitored this week, and should be cleared for his much-publicised bout against Chet Worth at ReVolution 122. - Vangelus Olsig has been removed from active competition until his long-standing injuries are suitably healed. Many expected Olsig to have surgery following his record-breaking run as Intense champion came to an end last year, but so far he’s been working through a number of nagging aches and pains. - Karina Wolfenden collapsed backstage after being helped out of the Halos, and has been pulled from active competition with immediate effect. PRIME officials are said to have a mixture of concern and anger for the K-Wolf, as despite a marathon performance in the Halo that helped shrug off her ‘lazy‘ tag, they feel she isn’t taking enough care of her body outside the ring. - The Dual Halo was the last match of Diego Delgado’s PRIME deal, which isn’t being renewed. Malachi, signed at the same time, has had his contract renewed, but will not be brought into the PRIME road schedule again right away. - PRIME confirmed the signing of the Blue Rogues - Coral Avalon and the Codemaster, who made their debut at Culture Shock Night One. Joining them in the tag ranks are the team of former PTC Extreme champion Jackson Rust, and ex-RPW starlet Carys Callicker, making her return to wrestling after 2 years away from the ring. - Many wrestlers who were in the Halo for close to two hours, including the Trashcan Man, were suffering from dehydration and were kept under observation Sunday night. None of these cases will result in any missed dates. - Azala Zameer’s recent run in PRIME has been blighted by Visa issues, causing particular problems due to the world tour. PRIME officials haven’t yet commented on whether or not the Snack Daddy is cleared to work the upcoming US dates when PRIME returns home. - PRIME wishes the best of luck to recent Alias champion and Jewel in the Crown semi-finalist Adam, as he bids his farewell to the ring. The same message went out to Bastille, who’s short PRIME run has come to an end due to family issues. - Rich Rollins and Eva Van Lorne have not been listed as appearing at upcoming PRIME dates, and have recently been left out when the members of Fuck You are listed. PRIME has yet to make any official comment on their status. - After picking up injuries in the Halo, Paul Cain and N will be taken off the road until they’re ready to work the gruelling weekly schedule again. - PRIME officials also thanked the guests from rival promotions, as well as freelance workers, who participated in the Halo.
