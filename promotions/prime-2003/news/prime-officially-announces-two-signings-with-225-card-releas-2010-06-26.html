PRIME Officially Announces Two Signings with 225 Card Release

26 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opening contest features an individual calling himself The First. A young man who just turned 25, he is an unknown to the PRIME audience, but those who witnessed his tryout gave rave reviews. Devin Shakur provided a quick comment outside the Rio Casino after a winning craps session, "He's someone the people of PRIME will want to see. A high flying machine who will produce exciting television." The First has drawn Steve Starr on the 225 card. In the second match of the evening, PRIME fans will see a returning Adam Garcia square off against Skylar Montgomery. Garcia had a few appearances during the late ReVolution 200s and defeated Benjamin Johnson at Great American Nightmare 2009. He faded out of the company before the King of Kings arc, and recently expressed interest in re-signing. PRIME was all too happy to welcome him back.
