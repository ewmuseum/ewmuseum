Intern to Sue Shakur for $5,000,000?

27 October 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At the start of the fall semester, PRIME was looking for some free help and reached out to the University of Maine to find an intern willing to do some of the cheap work. If they were a fan of wrestling, they were encouraged to show up. A lifelong wrestling fan, Brian jumped at the chance, and because he had just become a junior was up for the position. At 230, Brian got the chance to come face to face with Devin Shakur moments after The Boss in Black flipped off the deep end. Shakur appeared to admire the kid’s moxie and asked to have water brought to him. Brian asked if he wanted it bottled or tap, and then received a beating which left him with undisclosed internal injuries. Brian’s parents and the university are livid with the situation, but would not compete to PRIME. Sources close to the parents say they intend to sue Shakur for $5,000,000. Shakur scoffed at a chance to comment, saying he had better things to do than deal with irrelevant people.
