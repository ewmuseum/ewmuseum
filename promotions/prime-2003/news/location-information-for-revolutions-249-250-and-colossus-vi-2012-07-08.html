Location Information for ReVolutions 249/250 and Colossus VIII Released

8 July 2012
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 8, 2012 - CHICAGO, IL After the latest round of weekend meetings, Blaine Blair and Lisa Tyler have confirmed to me where the remaining shows of PRIME's final run will be taking place. ReVolution 249 will be held at the Phillips Arena in Atlanta, Georga. ReVolution 250 will be held at the Verizon Center in Washington, DC. Colossus VIII will be held at Soldier Field in Chicago, Illinois. "It makes sense to have the final pay-per-view event in the city where our headquarters is," said Tyler. "We couldn't think of a better way to go out." Lisa also confirmed the possibility of one additional ReVolution taking place, but said it would depend on a few factors and that nothing had been finalized. "Whether the talent wants one more show before Colossus is the big one," she said. "It may have matches, or it may just be a retrospective that the producers and others behind the scenes put together. As soon as we decide, everyone will know." Tickets for all three known shows will be on sale this week.
