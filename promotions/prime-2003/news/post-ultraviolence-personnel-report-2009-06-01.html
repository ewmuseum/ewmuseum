Post-UltraViolence Personnel Report

1 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dr. Fihlguud's staff, for obvious reasons, was worked nearly to the bone in the aftermatch of UltraViolence. It's said that nearly half of the roster headed to nearby Twin Cities General for some treatment or another, but the Vice President of Talent Relations gave some specifics. In his clash with Jason Natas, Troy Douglas just barely missed out on scoring some revenge on the Anti-Superstar. For his troubles, Megatron received a broken nose and a mild neck sprain, but the erstwhile competitor is expected to be in action at ReVolution 195. Hessian, who suffered as much brutality as anyone at the PPV, was treated for facial and abdominal lacerations, after which he was seen departing the show with former famed tag wrestler, Nerezza. Devin Shakur suffered a Grade One concussion, which will keep the Man in Black out of the ring for at least for ReVolution 195. However, he's not expected to miss any extended amount of time, and was said to be recovering well from bruises to the neck and back. His comrade Christian Daniels was in surprisingly good condition, and the two were seen sharing a celebration after Shakur's match. All three main event competitors were held out of action on ReVolution 195, on Dr. Fihlguud's say-so. Garbage Bag Johnny had small fragments of the cell embedded in the left cheek of his backside, bruised ribs, and two missing teeth, but refused to be helped by PRIME medics, hollering some brand of nonsense about how he only accepts medical treatment from shamanistic healers in the forests of Kansas. Chandler Tsonda continues to suffer from some kind of bulging disc in his back that the Tsuperstar refuses to discuss publicly. And PRIME Universal Champion, Jason Snow, broke his left hand during a valiant defense of his title. However, all three men are expected to be given the green light prior to ReVolution 196. Nonchalantly slipped into the end of Dr. Fihlguud's press release was the following ambiguous sentence: "Tyler Rayne suffered substantial injuries that will require medical attention of an unpredictable nature." It's unknown exactly what the extent of Rayne's injuries is, but those close to Rayne are somberly tight-lipped. The tale of the tape, and replays from Sunday night, suggests that Rayne won't wrestle in PRIME again until 2010, at the very least. In addition to the injury report, Lisa Tyler offered vague statements about some roster additions and subtractions. King Blueberry, it seems, showed up to try and cause a bit of mayhem at UltraViolence, and, as she had threatened, Lisa Tyler voided his contract. His attempt to sneak into the show may have been thwarted by the fact that he was dressed as a giant fruit. Additionally, Tyler said that Nerezza had inked a PRIME contract. The stipulations and terms of the contract, however, were withheld, and we know only that the Prankster With Attitude is, for the moment, on the payroll. The head of on-air operations also added that Tyler Nelson was moved from part-time to full-time employment, which should offer the former CEO access to the company dental and health plans. Perhaps the most interesting note in the memo was Lisa Tyler's omission of any buyrate numbers from UltraViolence. It is known that an HD stream of the Pay-Per-View was widely broadcast on the internet, dipping substantially into the buyrate numbers. The rumor amongst those in the know is that, despite a firmly positive response from fans, bloggers, and magazines, the show barely turned a profit. Some sites, like PRIMERing.com, are even reporting that Colossus CVI will be a scaled back affair, with none of the usual pageantry that goes along with the biggest show in sports entertainment. While all officials refused to comment on these rumors, Lisa Tyler will surely face questions about Colossus starting the minute she steps into the building this Wednesday night.
