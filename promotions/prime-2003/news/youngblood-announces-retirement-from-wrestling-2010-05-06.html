Youngblood Announces Retirement From Wrestling

6 May 2010

But now, the match up is more than likely not going to take place. Youngblood is adamant that he will show and wrestle Johnson if he can't break out of the contract, but his true desire is to relinquish the Elite Championship and allow the PTC governing body decide a new title holder. Signals point toward a possible Johnson vs. Vivica J. Valentine match up to decide the Championship, but it is too early to say. SCCW star Alexandra 'Desade' Pierce might also factor into the title picture thanks to her contendership status as a result of her winning GTT7. Damaging for PRIME officials is the thought that, while Culture Shock was a solid money maker, the company is now forced to move on without the two men involved in the much talked about bout that headlined Night One. Youngblood's retirement, along with Snow's, comes at a critical time where ratings have not picked up as expected when the company made its move to HBO. Youngblood explained his heart being a major deciding factor in wanting to continue in the sport. "I've been doing this for a lot of my life, and I see guys who get to the top only to fall back down. I don't want to keep doing this if I don't have the guts for it [any]more." When asked if it was because he perceived there to be no more challenges on the horizon, he quickly responded by saying "there's always challenges in this organization. Even with people coming in or coming back, there's major challenges. But most of them aren't challenges I want to face because the match ups are big enough. And for others, like [Garbage Bag] Johnny, I don't feel like I can give him the sort of competition he deserves." One thing is for certain though; Youngblood is still shell shocked by what happened in England. "I thought I beat him," he said, "and then after the Halo I thought we'd just be getting ready to go again. I came back to wrestling to face Jason. I don't see our conflict resolved and because of that, I can't keep going on in this sport if I can't wrestle the one man I need to face to get peace of mind. If that was where it ended, though, I'm glad because it was a watershed moment in both our careers." Youngblood leaves having left a mark not only on PRIME as a Jewel in the Crown winner and the only three time 5-Star Champion in the history of the company, but also by finishing in the top five of the Dual Halo both in 2005 and 2010. It is believed by many that he is the greatest PRIME star to never win the Universal Championship.
