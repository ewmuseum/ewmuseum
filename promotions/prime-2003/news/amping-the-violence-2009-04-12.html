Amping the Violence

12 April 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Starting off the evening with a potential barn burner, Prometheus's made superstar Devin Shakur took on one third of the Three Amigos, Bryan Dawkins. The match was clouded by events that opened the broadcast and everybody was rooting for The Flyin' Hawaiian to take out The Man in Black, extracting a measure of revenge for the good guys. One man rushed from the ring and the other needed the assistance of Elvis Nixon to wake up. David Noble sought redemption from his first loss to Nine and a beating to wrap up his ReVolution 189 by battling the monster, Chainz. With Michael Sloan competing for the first time since King of Kings 2007, how would he fare against the PRIME rookie? Would Noble continue his slide after encountering the sickest man in PRIME? In a battle of awkward styles, King Blueberry took on the undefeated Nine. PRIME's numerologist looked to continue his winning streak against the Prince of Produce in what proved to be an exciting match. Did Blueberry bring Nine back to the drawing board? In the submain of the evening, Killean Sirrajin accepted the punishment handed down from Lisa Tyler on ReVolution 189 in the form of the company's hottest superstar, Intense Champion Hessian. To spice matters up, a No Holds Barred stipulation was tacked on. Could The PRIME Choice stop the momentum of The Murder Show, or would Killean be another name added to the ever growing list of people who have fallen to Hessian? Completing the wild night, Tyler Rayne and Chandler Tsonda went toe to toe with Jason Natas and Wade Elliott in a Tag Team brawl. The Underground Pimp was already swimming in controversy from earlier actions, and his mind was no where near the wrestling ring. Chandler Tsonda's back was busted from constantly competition. Would Natas and Elliott be able to put aside their testosterone long enough to achieve victory against Tsonda and Rayne?
