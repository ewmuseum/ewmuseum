The FX Network's Complaints Department To Bring In Extra Staff?

23 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One of those wrestlers is Michael Sloan, aka Chainz. A man known for his violence, perversions, and completely psychotic nature. Chet Worth stated, "I, along with my staff, are all well aware of the problems surrounding the hiring of a man with a ‘perspective’ like Michael Sloan‘s, but this is an opportunity that was too tempting to pass up. His talent is undeniable, and the matter of the fact is that he draws ratings like very few can." Still, despite the new and rather 'unique' demographic he will attract to PRIME, the hiring of the sickest and most controversial wrestler in history has angered some. PRIME chief-interviewer Lisa Tyler stated, "I don’t care what kind of ratings he draws, the man is unstable and unmanageable. I cannot comprehend why an organization as distinguished as PRIME would take a chance on a man of such wickedness. A man who plead guilty to rape, who’s a known killer, and a man of such evil that he cannot coexist with the rest of the PRIME roster." Although many of PRIME’s roster aren’t familiar with Chainz, and do not know how to react, the females of PRIME have been said to be quite unhappy, however, knowing full well the stories of the way Chainz abuses women. Although, it has been rumoured that Sun Tzu reacted to the signing of Chainz by spinning 'Fluffy' from her holster and enquiring about PRIME's policy for using lethal force in self-defence... and just using lethal force for fun, since, after all, it is Christmas. As for the man creating so much controversy? He hasn’t been reached for comment. Mainly because nobody has actually accepted the task of tracking him down. A month before AWC closed he vanished without a word to anyone, including his fiancee Tracy Stanton, who also makes the trip to PRIME with him. "Michael has had some trying times in his life, and the past month he has had to do some soul-searching. I’m sure he’ll return to show PRIME the immense talent he possesses," Stanton said. Soul-searching? Many believe the man doesn’t have a soul, and many are apprehensive about working with a man who seems to have no morals or a decent fibre in his body. Only time will tell if PRIME has made a grievous error in signing the mad man.
