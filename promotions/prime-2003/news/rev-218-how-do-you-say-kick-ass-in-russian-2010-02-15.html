ReV 218: How Do You Say Kick Ass In Russian?

15 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Of course, what better way to start the show than some choice words from Boda? Perhaps this isn’t the best way to lure our viewers into watching the recap of the show, but suffice it to say Boda took his words straight down to Change of Spades… but did they hear? Also, Tyler Rayne decided it was time for him to flex his management muscle, and scheduled himself in an impromptu match against Elise Ares! But there was a catch: a special guest referee. Also, two members of the roster were suspended, in yet another struggle for power within the federation, and the whole of the Board of Directors got involved. It proves that the pen is mightier than the chairshot. Hessian clashed with Troy Douglas. With Hessian’s lack of momentum and Douglas riding the waves of an incredible run one must wonder who ran through who? Was Hessian able to recuperate and take Douglas to task or did Douglas show Hessian that his time may very well be over? The Five Rings Pro Wrestling Tag Team Championship was on the line as the Osaka Street Cutters defended them against Change in Spades themselves. Were the Cutters able to hold onto their title or was Nitz Donnelly and Vance Raymes able to now be dual tag team champions? Finally, Chainz locked up with Brandon Youngblood. It’s safe to say Chainz has been on a roll, as has Youngblood. But one man must win and one must lose. With Standards of Bastards standing firmly behind Youngblood and Chainz cadre of cohorts chomping at the bit to lash out at anything nearby, did that play into the ultimate outcome!? We have pep talks by Tyler Rayne! We have Elise Ares wooing not just the crowd, but a certain specific champion! We have Tracy’s boobs! And we even had one hell of a Red Scare! Want to know more?
