Where is Chandler Tsonda?

2 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tsonda, who's been all but non-existant in the last four months, has been rumored to be the new, public face of MTV Vietnam, a new network run by the Viacom owned, MTV Corp. Tsonda, who's young enough to look around 18, is rumored to have signed an eight figure deal to introduce videos, and interviews on MTVV. In addition, Tsonda was revealed to be the new face of T-Mobile's globalization effort in Asia, taking on a Catherine Zeta-Jones type role in T-Mobile commercials for the Asian Cellular carrier with another multi-million dollar contract. With this newfound celebrity, will Tsonda wrestle in PRIME ever again? People are unsure, especially in the former A-List camp. Allen North, Danny Ferguson's former buisness manager states "I wish I knew. One minute the guy and I are talking about this sweet deal on a warehouse for a club outside of Hollywood, and the next, he's just gone." "Yeah. He hasn't been to see me. Then again, no one has," Danny Ferguson told this reporter on a collect call. "I'm not sure though. I know he was kind of tired of the whole scene, you know? The women, the alcohol, the life. It's rough, you know? Wait, no. You wouldn't." A call to Chandler Tsonda's cell phone left this cryptic exchange rattling around our heads. Reporter: Hello, I'm calling for Chandler Tsonda. Answerer: This is Chandler Tsonda... Reporter: Mister Tsonda, this is Gerald Barrett, from PRIME Answerer: ... Reporter: Mister Tsonda? Answerer: Uhhh... No speaka the Englisho, sorry-o. Reporter: Habla Espanol? Answerer: Uhhh...Ching, chang wong tong bong hong song long. Reporter: Was that Chinese? Answerer: Yeah. Pretty cool, huh? I mean... Bye.
