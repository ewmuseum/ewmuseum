Born Again: Matches

27 April 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lisa Tyler also caught a late minute omission, which went virtually unnoticed by everybody but her. Shakur tried to exclude 'The Flyin Hawaiian' Bryan Dawkins from the tournament. Luckily for 'The Bruh', the Vice President of Talent Relations caught the error and castigated Shakur, forcing him to put Dawkins in the Born Again field. Each first round match of the tournament will be triple threat. Seven first round matches will be held over ReVolution 222 and 223, with winners for both brackets being determined on ReVolution 224. The New Era bracket will see only one ReVolution 224 match due to the rules, while The Stalwarts winner will have to go through two singles matches in one evening to get their spot in the Main Event of UltraViolence. First round matches for the PRIME Stalwarts bracket are as followed: Boda Vs Garbage Bag Johnny Vs Tchu will take place on ReVolution 222. Elise Ares Vs Emilio Rage Vs Troy Douglas will also take place on ReVolution 222. The winners of those matches will face off in the first semifinal match on ReVolution 224. Chainz Vs Bryan Dawkins Vs Nitz Donnelly will take place on ReVolution 223. Tyler Rayne Vs Hessian Vs David Noble will take place on ReVolution 223. The winners of those matches will face off in the second semifinal match on ReVolution 224. The New Era bracket will see the following matches: Roxie Sykes Vs Crazy Joe Vs Black Death will take place on ReVolution 222. Vic Thunder Vs Hush Vs Bishop Steele will take place on ReVolution 223. Skylar Montgomery Vs Castor V Strife Vs Shawn Jessica Hart will take place on ReVolution 223. The winners of those matches will fight at ReVolution 224 to determine the winner of the New Era bracket.
