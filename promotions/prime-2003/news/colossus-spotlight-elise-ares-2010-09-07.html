Colossus Spotlight: Elise Ares

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
She is stylish. She is brash. She is arrogant. She is a shameless self-promoter and doesn’t care who knows it. She’s also found herself in a contest with who many figure to be her male equivalent in Nitz Donnelly for her SWAGGER Championship, which was sanctioned as a title up for grabs by Devin Shakur on ReVolution 229. Ares came into PRIME as one half of what many consider to be the second most dominant tag team in PRIME history behind TEAM Viagra, Wolves of Slaughter. They made an immediate impact by getting involved with fellow tag team The Redeemed at ReVolution 195 and from there, neither ever looked back. They were pegged for dominance ever since their first match later at 195 and showed just how great they could be individually when finishing 1 and 2 in a ReVolution 200 Battle Royal. Their dominance came to a culmination when Ares and Jankauskas defeated Wicked Ways and The Redeemed at Colossus VI to become PRIME’s Tag Team Champions. Most expected their dominance to continue until turmoil brought the team apart. But, neither saw their outcome that way, at least not yet. Both showed their true colors by aligning with Tyler Nelson and his army, Jankauskas selling his Universal Championship shot to Nelson. Ares, as a singles competitor during her time with Wolves did not garner much success. The Wolves dominance came to a stark halt when Ares and Jankauskas were defeated at Great American Nightmare 2009 by The Redeemed. The Wolves stayed together for a brief amount of time afterward, but Ares wanted to break out on her own and rid herself of Jankauskas. She eventually was able to do so by capturing the 5*Star Championship from Tony Gamble at ReVolution 215 after interference from Devin Shakur. She went onto hold the Championship until UltraViolence 2010 when Garbage Bag Johnny used his banked Dual Halo shot to defeat Ares and take her championship. Ares didn’t seem bothered, claiming the 5*Star Championship was beneath her and bringing out her own belt, the Swagger Championship. From her days as a tag team great, she also inherited a feud with Change in Spades member, Nitz Donnelly. Ares made her feud with Donnelly more personal by acquiring the services of Donnelly’s former tag partner from Change in Spades, Vance Raymes. After Jankauskas was released from PRIME thanks to a match against Ares, she was in need of a new bodyguard, and who better to seek out than Raymes? He had every quality she was looking for; including complying with all demands she put forth to The Tortured Artist. While these two aren’t incredibly high on the Colossus ticket, they have been steadily going back and forth and personalizing their feud in unique ways: Ares had her car totaled by an enraged Donnelly, while Donnelly was on the receiving end of many beatings from Raymes and Ares. These two have presented something new at every show and Colossus will be the conclusion of their feud. Elise Ares Vs Nitz Donnelly will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
