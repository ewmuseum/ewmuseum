ReV 218: From Russia With Carnage

5 February 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Singles Match Troy Douglas Vs Hessian Troy Douglas has got momentum behind him, first defeating the newcomer Cyrus Sutherland and then following that win with the defeat of Tony Gamble. On the contrary, "The Murder Show" Hessian has had his share of negative momentum as of late. However, one would be foolish to ever count Hessian out! Can Troy Douglas continue to roll over the opposition? Will Hessian steal Douglas’ fire and use it to his advantage? Tune in on HBO to find out! Tag Team Match Osaka Street Cutters Vs Change in Spades The Osaka Street Cutters have had their sights set on Change in Spades since they made their debut a few weeks ago. Though this is not a tag title match, it’s clear that this match will determine if the Cutters have what it takes to be true contenders! Will Nitz Donnelly and Vance Raymes successfully put them in their place? Or will the Cutters, who now have three other ornery comrades, make it known that those titles are as good as theirs? Singles Match Tyler Rayne Vs Elise Ares No more glitter and red carpet treatment. Elise Ares, the 5 Star Champion, gets to see if she has what it takes to finally shut up Tyler Rayne. With Bryan Dawkins’ future in purgatory, Rayne was able to avoid what could have been a disastrous match against Chandler Tsonda. However, he not only has to deal with Elise Ares, but also her other friends which include Cyrus Sutherland, Chainz, and the Osaka Street Cutters. Couple that with the fact that Rayne has also pissed off Devin Shakur and just about every other member of the PRIME roster, and one can only speculate as to what’s in store for The Underground Pimp! Singles Match Brandon Youngblood Vs Chainz Two of the most accomplished stars find themselves in the squared circle in the PRIME Main Event! This isn’t going to be a competition between gentlemen. One is a self proclaimed bastard. The other could definitely be considered a bastard as well. Which is which? They’re interchangeable. With Brandon Youngblood and his Standards of Bastards crew behind him, and Chainz with his cadre of allies, it’s bound to be one hell of a match. Both men have winning streaks behind them, but there can only be one winner in this incredibly volatile match up! All of this and more is on the way for ReVolution 218!
