ReVolution 226 Preview

5 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Special Attraction Steve Starr In an effort to keep critics and bosses alike silenced, Devin Shakur has thrown a new element into the PRIME mix. He calls the feature a 'Special Attraction', although hasn't divulged details on what exactly the name entails. All he has guaranteed is that Steve Starr will compete in a match on ReVolution 226. Who he will face or how many people he will face remains to be determined, although one can bet after coming off a loss against The First on 224, Starr will be looking to overcome any and all who stand before him. Singles Match Tony Gamble Vs Skylar Montgomery Skylar Montgomery predicted his own downfall at ReVolution 225 and ended up being correct, losing to Adam Garcia. The Imaginary Man also launched a campaign, declaring himself to be against the entire company of PRIME. He certainly drew the eye, and ire, of a figurehead in the company, Tyler Rayne. As a result, or maybe because the other boss, Devin Shakur, is a complete tool, Montgomery has been thrown against wily veteran Tony 'The Grin' Gamble. The Grin had a rather unflattering return to ReVolution, being defeated by Chainz, and then getting beaten to a bloody pulp by Hessian and dropped at the feet of The Boss in Black. Intense Championship Match Tchu Vs Christian Daniels Ever since making his debut at ReVolution 185, Christian Daniels competiting inside a wrestling ring has been one of the rarest sights in PRIME. He's only stepped in the squared circle four times and holds a record of 2-0-2, participating in two wild brawls, defeating Lindsay Troy at UltraViolence 2009, and helping to defeat Troy Douglas and Meat alongside brother Devin Shakur at ReVolution 198. In his fifth ever match, he will get a chance to face a thorn in Shakur's side, The Inhuman Being, Tchu. The Boss in Black has made no secret about his feelings for Tchu, implementing a 24/7 rule while the acclaimed PRIME Superstar is still Intense Champion. Daniels also has a luxury befitting the rule, being able to pick his own time during the card to fight Tchu. Contenders Match Castor V. Strife Vs Tyler Rayne Castor V. Strife has garnered a tidal wave of momentum ever since debuting in the New Era portion of the Born Again tournament. He came out victorious and was ever so close to becoming the Universal Champion. The God of Snuff showed he is not a one hit wonder by beating The Queen of the Ring at ReVolution 225 and earning himself another chance to vie for the top prize in the industry. To do so, he will have to go through one of the most resilient wrestlers on the roster in Tyler Rayne. On the current roster, he is arguably the greatest wrestler never to hold the Universal Championship, but just like Strife has only had one direct chance to get the gold. The winner is said to get a chance at the belt on the grandest stage of them all, Colossus VII. Strife is putting all of his momentum on the line against a man who, as noted last week, is extremely hard to pin for three seconds. Rayne is looking to reach the one pinnacle never before achieved in his career and will have to go through perhaps the hottest name in PRIME right now. Also... -Vangelus Olsig... Excuse me, SIR Vangelus Olsig, wanted a challenge from The Boss in Black and received one in the form of a ReVolution 227 defense against The Monster from Hell's Kitchen, Chainz. Will the champion have any reaction or send Chives the Agent to do his bidding? Will Chainz decide to send Olsig a message? -Devin Shakur found himself a new running buddy... or rather, a new guard dog, in a returning Murder Show. His first task was completed as he left the broken body of Tony Gamble at Shakur's feet. The trio is expected to open the program and explain their allegiance... or maybe they won't. How will Gamble react to being beaten to a pulp after having his number called for Colossus VII to face Shakur? -Something doesn't look right within the relationship of Lindsay Troy and Tchu. Dan Ryan blocked Tchu and Troy from getting time to chat on 225 and all parties are slated to be at 226. Will there be another heated confrontation? -Speaking of trying to figure out answers, Vance Raymes returned last week and tried to explain his actions against former partner, Nitz Donnelly. Unfortunately, The Tortured Artist was interrupted by his benefactor, Elise Ares, and the debut of hew new Championship. Will Vance be able to get time to express himself this week? -Who will Devin Shakur handpick to face Garbage Bag Johnny at ReVolution 227 for the 5 Star Championship. What will Boda's comments be to receiving a 5 Star Championship match at Colossus? -All this and more as ReVolution 226 hails live from Conseco Field House in Indianapolis, Indiana on July 13th, 2010.
