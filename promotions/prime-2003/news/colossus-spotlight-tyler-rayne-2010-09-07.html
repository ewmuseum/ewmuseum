Colossus Spotlight: Tyler Rayne

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
He is world-renowned, oozing sex appeal and driving women wild in ways no other wrestler in PRIME has ever been capable of. He’s The Underground Pimp, a man who revolutionized the 5*Star Championship, and who has been involved with some violent feuds during his PRIME career, Wade Elliott and Devin Shakur to name a few. He has always been in controversy’s center since debuting at ReVolution 130 and getting into a heated discussion with future rival, Devin Shakur. While his talents took time to develop in PRIME’s top heavy roster, once he was able to take off, nobody was capable of stopping him. Days of jobbing to Tom Walczak time after time quickly dissipated when Rayne found his groove. Rayne added a dimension to his PRIME resume when teaming with Chandler Tsonda, and future protégé, Bryan Dawkins. His first Championship was captured at ReVolution 150 when he defeated Nitz Donnelly to become PRIME’s 5*Star Champion. Several arguments have been made at many water coolers about how important Rayne made the usually second-tier Championship. Even though Cozen, Lindsay Troy, Devin Shakur, and Chandler Tsonda were grappling over the top prize, Rayne held steady with his 5*Star Championship and ran roughshod through any and all contenders: Donnelly, Crucifix, Elliott, Justice, and Dusk. In an additional feat, Rayne won PRIME’s Dual Halo in 2008 by defeating Cozen and won himself a chance at whatever his heart desired. Rayne opted for a contract giving him executive privilege and authority if he desired to overturn most decisions. He drew a ton of ire from Lisa Tyler and she sought to eliminate Rayne however possible. Rayne would hold onto 5*Star gold for 275 days until an encounter with High Flyer at ReVolution 176 left him beltless. No worries, Jewel in the Crown was just ahead and ready to close 2008. Rayne looked to take his next step on a road guaranteed to get him first ballot Hall of Fame access. He avenged his loss to High Flyer before falling to eventual champion, and future Universal Champion, Jason Snow in what many feel was the best match of 2008. The Underground Pimp encountered a more gruesome and violent challenge in a returning Devin Shakur, hell bent on taking him out of competition permanently. Over five months, both men brawled and beat each other to a pulp. UltraViolence 2009 was the definitive low point in Tyler Rayne’s career, losing to Shakur twice and being declared "dead" in his own words after a vicious beating by Shakur, Christian Daniels, and a bunch of hired cronies. Rayne ended up unable to return until Jewel in the Crown 2009, where he once again found himself in the Main Event of King of Kings, this time squaring off against Brandon Youngblood. In what most consider a top three all time singles match, Youngblood defeated Rayne and denied him another opportunity to fight at Culture Shock for the Universal Championship. 2010 has been somewhat of a complacent year for The Underground Pimp. He has been involved in some wild brawls against the likes of Cyrus Sutherland, Garbage Bag Johnny, Chainz, Hessian, and Castor V. Strife. The latter of which was a match at ReVolution 226 to see which man would headline Colossus VII for a chance at the Universal Championship. Rayne once again came up short, but found himself a willing opponent in Skylar Montgomery. Given Rayne’s extensive history and known ability for violence, many are favoring him to come out ahead against Montgomery when they do battle at Colossus. Rayne has fared evenly on Colossus’ stage, losing to Munson Monsoon in a 5*Star contenders match at Colossus IV, but defeating Dusk in a 5*Star Championship match at Colossus V. Even though Rayne hasn’t been quite the impact player he once was. He still garners crowd pops unrivaled by anybody on PRIME’s current roster. He is also one of the more dangerous talents in the game when he puts full effort. Most experts expect him to at Colossus. Tyler Rayne Vs Skylar Montgomery will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
