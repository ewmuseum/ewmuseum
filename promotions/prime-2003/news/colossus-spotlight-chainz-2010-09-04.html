Colossus Spotlight: Chainz

4 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ever since PRIME acquired Sloan in 2007, he has been a buzzsaw of controversy. In his debut, he defeated a red hot Karina Wolfenden and immediately stood out amongst a plethora of debuting talent. His impact lost a bit of luster until he became Jason Snow’s bodyguard, putting his name on all lips in the wrestling world. He couldn’t defeat Snow when they faced off at Great American Nightmare 2007, but a loss hasn’t stopped Sloan from being a stalwart in PRIME and menacing presence in the locker room. He disgusts all with rumored past accolades and isn’t afraid to swing with anybody. So what would bring a man like Michael Sloan into professional wrestling? The man himself provided that answer in a recent interview. "I'm not going to lie; I'm a decent and honorable man. Professional wrestling is now a multibillion dollar industry. It's not queer men running around in speedos in a back yard. If you're the best, and I am, it's a chance to live a life of luxury. Where else could I bleed a man silly and be rewarded with fame and fortune? My wife enjoys fine dining and living in luxury hotels when we're on the road and in return she does things for me that would make you blush. Still, you may ask why I continue doing what I do. I have all the money I need and I will admit wrestling and being on the road takes away from my other… interests. I manage my time well and enjoy seeing the suffering in the eyes of my opponents. Those idiots can boo and hate me. It doesn’t matter. I’m getting what’s rightfully mine before I tell PRIME and all the fans to fuck off." Sloan might end up in a predicament to fuck off, but he might not get a chance to express those words to PRIME and its audience. He’s involved in what everybody expects to be a bloodbath against PRIME’s biggest renegade, Hessian. Their confrontation is a match which features Tracy, Sloan’s wife, as the winner’s prize. Hardly low stakes for someone who has seen his share of bloody brawls over a four year span. Sloan doesn’t seem to hold appreciation or respect to PRIME, acting as if he could do whatever he desired in another federation. "What’s PRIME meant to me? Jack shit really. I have no sense of loyalty to this organization. It’s nothing but a means to an end. I give them a show they need, but won’t admit they want. Without me you can kiss goodbye to the PPV buy rates and the television ratings. Blood and sex, that’s what sells and I’m about both in spades. I’ve had some memorable moments, made some bitches cry and wish they were never born, and sneaked a peak at Lisa Tyler when she was dressing or in the shower. She doesn’t know and I won’t say it to her face, but that’s a mighty fine specimen of a woman. Probably second only to my Tracy. Other than that, PRIME could eat a big fat dick for all I care." Despite his venomous attitude, don’t sell Sloan short when in-ring ability is concerned. He was able to snatch a record not many believed acquirable when, over a span of 2009, he captured eleven consecutive victories in a row, besting Hall of Famers Karina Wolfenden and Rock Startling by one. He was also seen as a breakout star for 2008-2009, and recently got his first chance at cracking PRIME’s elite echelon at ReVolution 227 with a Universal Championship match against Vangelus Olsig. Even though Chainz came up a loser, many believe it will only be a matter of time before he captures PRIME’s top gold. Amidst a wealth of destruction caused during his tenure, Chainz has only competed at Colossus one time, defeating Jonathan Rhine and David Noble in a triple threat match at Colossus VI to capture his first, and only, Intense Championship. Sloan doesn’t hold much regard for Colossus as an event, breaking a pattern from all other interviewees, "I haven’t paid much attention to Colossus in the past. I can’t even remember if I’ve been in one in the past. There may be a theme forming with my answers if you’re not too stupid to realize. PRIME means nothing to me. I could easily go and make my money in GCW or SCCW. I’m here because it’s convenient and I have some unfinished business with people. You ask me about the stage, the spectacle, and the moments of the past. I don’t give a fuck about the past. All I can tell you is this Colossus you’ll definitely want to tune in to see what happens. It will be the moment everyone remembers for years to come." Unfinished business would be an understatement to Sloan’s predicament at Colossus VII. Before Sloan’s 227 Universal title match, Tracy was kidnapped by an unknown assailant. 228 and 229 gave indications Sloan would go into his match with Hessian, suspected from the get-go as Tracy’s kidnapper, as a heavy underdog. Not much has changed in most people’s minds. Sloan has backed himself into an unenviable corner due to Hessian’s recent alliance with boss Devin Shakur and brother Christian Daniels. They run PRIME and with Hessian under their umbrella, Sloan will have to go through hell and high water just to retain his wife. But don’t tell Sloan he’s an underdog. He doesn’t believe himself to be. "Hessian is like a little kid playing with fire. Sure it’s fun and pretty, but in his hands it’ll burn. I broke his face, I broke his life, I broke his wo… No, never mind about that. He thinks siding with Shakur and Daniels will save him? Those two are rats scurrying aboard a sinking ship. No matter the dark holes they hide in when this ship goes belly up they’ll be the first ones to drown and Hessian will be like a giant, lead weight dragging them down to the bottom. My wife, my beautiful, innocent wife got caught up in the middle of all this and I swear upon the graves of many stronger men than Hessian that I will find her. Hessian’s size won’t matter; he’ll just make more noise going down. His end comes at Colossus. I will finish him. Not the match or his career, but his life. You can count on it." Chainz Vs Hessian will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium
