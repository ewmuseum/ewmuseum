How Much Longer?

16 January 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Killean has been around PRIME for going on three years now and his loyalty has been unflinching. What does he have left to accomplish?" One fan stated at a fan fair this past weekend. Killean was scheduled to be there to sign autographs but that plan was altered earlier last week. It was announced all over the local media in Atlanta. "Killean has been known to do this every now and again and we always see him come back one way or another. But this time, I dunno." Chet Worth’s Executive Assistant Blaine Blair stated as he was leaving the Georgia Dome last week. "Last I heard from him was when he told me he had some problems to deal with. He didn’t seem like his old self. This time, one has to wonder if it’s now his time." Rumors continue to flow as to what the Supreme Machine has in store. Could we soon see the last of Killean in wrestling? Could his absence be extended? Could all these rumors be snuffed out when Killean hits the ring in the near future? Only time will tell.
