Dawkins, Phoenix and Rayne Advance; Locke Given New Life; Tsonda Shaken By

9 November 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In first-round action, Jay Phoenix completed the GTT6 trifecta, joining fellow finalists Horace Tully and Jason Snow, who advanced at 176, in the next round. Alongside the resuscitated Phoenix, friends Bryan Dawkins and Tyler Rayne both advanced in the tournament. Rayne, fresh off the loss of his 5-Star Title, had a slightly odder road to travel in his victory, though, as his match was "crashed" by an unexpected guest. Talk at the show turned toward the appearance of El Spiffy!, former RUSH, PWC and RPW wrestler who is most notable for being an alter ego of Danny Ferguson, the former PRIME superstar who left the company after being injured by Rayne in the 2008 Dual Halo. Although Ferguson was reported to be retired from wrestling on multiple occasions since March, the appearance of his former masked alias raised the attention of several backstage, including former Ferguson ally (and later nemesis) Chandler Tsonda.
