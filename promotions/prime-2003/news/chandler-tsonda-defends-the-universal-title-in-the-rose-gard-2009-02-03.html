Chandler Tsonda Defends the Universal Title in the Rose Garden!

3 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
However, that's not going to be the case as ReVolution on FX will be the host of the first Universal Title defense of the year as Chandler Tsonda will defend his title against the 2008 Dual Halo Champion and a man who has carved a path to greatness in PRIME, Tyler Rayne! Rayne, who has taken over PRIME over the past couple of weeks made the bombshell announcement last week in Sacramento, and this week, millions of fans will watch as the two behemtohs of PRIME go at one another over the Universal Title. Will Chandler Tsonda manage to defeat his challenger or will it be Tyler Rayne who goes to Culture Shock for a rematch against 2008 Jewel in the Crown Winner Jason Snow? In other action, Lindsay Troy will be making her return to the ring in a match that should be one for the ages. Take one part of 2-Time Universal Champion, add the 2008 Jewel in the Crown Champion, then top it off with the two hottest wrestlers in PRIME this year, and what do you get? You get Lindsay Troy, Jason Snow, Jay Phoenix, and Hessian facing off in a fatal four-way! Also, we'll see a 3-way between Hank Cobb, Scraps, and Jack Conway in a match featuring one half of the top three tag teams in PRIME. Then, to open the card we'll see Kaiser Vashaun, fresh off his heinous attack upon Lindsay troy, take on Tony Gamble and Team VIAGRA in a handicap match that'll test the Intense Champion's mettle. Plus, more on the return of the man known as Wade Elliott, and a hell of a lot more when ReVolution takes over Portland, Oregon this Wednesday night!
