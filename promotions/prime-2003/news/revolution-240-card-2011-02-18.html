ReVolution 240 Card

18 February 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mitchell Quinlan Vs Brian Postal -First impressions are everything in the wrestling industry and two individuals who made stark impressions upon the PRIME audience were Mitchell Quinlan and Brian Postal. Quinlan opted to make his appearance far quieter, reading rather than getting into an all out melee with Hessian. Postal chose the later and got obliterated by a steel chair from an irate Murder Show. The two newcomers will meet in a match. Will Postal be able to gain his first victory or will Quinlan's PRIME debut give him a one up on the man who almost captured the Universal Championship? Christian Daniels Vs ??? -A while ago, Christian Daniels was seen as the most intimidating figure in PRIME. Even when Hessian and Chainz were running roughshod, Daniels had the capability to intimidate with a look, and an unabashed desire to pound people into the canvas. Now, he's been called into duty two times in the past three weeks, and both times by mystery opponents. The first time it was Big Bear in a ruse to sign Alexandra Pierce, and this time it is the individual who said he would make his return after one year. The only difference is The Biker chose to accept this match because he's unaware of who the new signee is, and it makes him a tad angry with all the misfortune happening to his younger brother lately. Who will the mystery person be? What tragedy befell them and cut his or her career short? Will Daniels send this person back into obscurity? Tony Gamble Vs GTT Semifinalist -Tony Gamble has seldom been used on television after Colossus, when he started the downward spiral of Devin Shakur by making him submit in a cage match. However, he made a cameo on ReVolution 239 and was set in a match against a GTT Semifinalist. The man in question, and it was confirmed by Gamble's associate that it was indeed a man, is unknown, but the word is that he's dying to get into the ring with Tony Gamble. Who could this be? Not many people fit the bill so the field is limited, but nonetheless, the match ought to be significant for both individuals. The Sentinel Vs Tom Walczak -Tom Walczak came up short for the second week in a row, losing to Alexandra Pierce, albeit this time he was able to dominate the match before making a mistake. He's still working the rust off from his game, but the powers that be are not going to let him off the hook. He is going to face a JITC semifinalist in The Sentinel, making his first appearance since being defeated by Pierce at King of Kings. An interesting match of styles pits the ultra-aggressive Sentinel against the downright frightening power of Walczak. Who is going to come out ahead in this assault? Alexandra Pierce Vs Seymour Almasy -With new, high caliber talent comes the right to put them against one another. On 239, Shakur and Pierce had their first ever confrontation and it wasn't a pretty one to say the least. If looks could kill... On the contrary, Seymour Almasy and Devin Shakur had their first ever meeting and it went pleasantly well. Shakur even convinced Lisa Tyler to give Almasy a crack at the Intense Championship come 242 against Lindsay Troy, or perhaps it was a mutual agreement. Nobody can say for certain. What can be said with certainty is that Shakur is putting his newfound neutral associate against his newest bitter enemy. Violence Jack Vs Matt Ward -A match pitting the next two men to vie for Hessian's Universal Championship. Violence Jack has said he will go to any length to capture the Championship and does not care who the champion is. Ward is looking to join a company all unto himself as he wishes to become a three time Universal Champion at 242. If Ward won, Jack would be his opposition at Culture Shock. Could 240 possibly be a preview of the Culture Shock night 1 Main Event? Also... -Vangelus Olsig has vowed to return. It has been confirmed The Prince of Delusion will appear on 240, but under what circumstances? Both announcers were heavily suspicious of his actions. -Nitz Donnelly was absent from 239 because Shakur did not fly him in. The Boss in Black guaranteed Donnelly would appear and declare his allegiance to the AWA. -Is Lance Marshall working for PRIME now? The signs are positive, but not 100 percent. Will The Lion be in Morocco? -Who exactly are the two new people fighting on the card? -Why did Lisa Tyler say 240 would be a night that would change PRIME forever?
