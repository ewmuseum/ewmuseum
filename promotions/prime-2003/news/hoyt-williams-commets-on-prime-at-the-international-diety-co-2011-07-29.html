Hoyt Williams commets on PRIME at the international Diety confrence in Brussles.

29 July 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AP - (Brussels, Belgium): Last night the Second Coming of Christ and international savior Hoyt Williams received the Savior of the Year award amongst a sold out crowd of over 50,000 other spiritual advisers including the Dalila Lama and Benny Hinn. After the service TMZ caught the Pontiff of Prime exiting the stadium before getting into his Rolls Royce designed Pope Mobile nicknamed the Holy Roller. The footage aired last night on TMZ. Hoyt did have some interesting words for the cameraman. "This year I have arranged the death of Ossma Bin Laden, caused a Hurricane in Japan after not getting a refund on a Droid that I never dropped in water like they claimed, and most recently helped negotiate an end the the NFL lockout. I'm now the savior of football. Praise Hoyt!! Now chunk I think you should do some jumping jack for Jesus and drop a few pound man. Your body is a temple to the lord. This body of mine is always in biblical shap." The camera man asked Hoyt about if he would return to PRIME. "Hoyt has come, Hoyt has risen, Hoyt will come again." A young twenty year old blonde who claimed to be Hoyt's secretary quickly ushered the Moses of the Mat into the Holy Roller as the vehicle sped away. There have been rumors of PRIME and Hoyt in early negotiations but Hoyt's agent the legendary apostle Clark Lorensen could not be reached for comment. AP 2011
