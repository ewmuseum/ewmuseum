Colossus IV

7 August 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Headlined by a match nearly three years in the making, the show is the place where an epic confrontation between former friends and even tag team champions collide when Lindsay Troy puts up her Universal Championship against Sonny Silver. Can the Queen of the Ring hang on to her throne in her battle with a man as brutal and savage as Sonny Silver? Two men of opposing views will go one-on-one to settle a score nearly a year in a making. Formerly adversaries in a faction war between Fuck You and its enemies, these two men continue their war in the ring, here at Colossus. Can Danny Ferguson extract his revenge over former Universal Champion, Nova, in a brutal two of three falls match? And four men, who have waged war with each other for many months, will do battle for the Intense Championship. PRIME itself has been rocked to its foundation by the hatred that Devin Shakur, Dusk, Jonathan Winters, and PRIME Intense Champion, Pierce Lavelle, have for each other. On this night, their score will be settled once and for all. The conclusion of the 5-Star Challenge is imminent. Only one man, between Munson Monsoon, Tyler Rayne, Tony Gamble, and Tom Walczak, will be able to challenge for the 5-Star championship. Also on the show, Jason Snow challenges Easton Hall for the 5-Star Championship. Can the man whose mere sight causes orgasms take down the proud Canadian? The Blue Rogues defend their coveted tag team championships against the team of Vangelus Olsig and Machavelli St. Romani. Can one of these two unstable tag teams come out on top? Nitz Donnelly and Wade Elliot collide in a match about pride and respect. Tchu and Titan St. James wage war against one another in a titanic struggle. And Jack Murphy teams with Mike Wafe to take on the team of Chandler Tsonda and the Illustrious Face-Eater. It's Colossus. Why are you still reading the hype for it?
