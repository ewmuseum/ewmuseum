ReVolution 225 Preview

27 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Steve Starr Vs The First Steve Starr made his return to the ring at UltraViolence 2010, defeating both Shawn Hart and Skylar Montgomery in a Triple Threat match. His return was a shining moments of the evening. If he continues down the path of dominance displayed at the Pay-Per-View, perhaps he can add another championship to his resume. The First is looking to make an impact in the land of PRIME and will need all the tricks in his arsenal to defeat the third ever Intense Champion. In a battle of high flyers, will Starr shine brightly once again or will The First leave Starr as the first of many? Adam Garcia Vs Skylar Montgomery As of late, Skylar Montgomery has been surrounded by controversy. From his memorable GTT7 appearance to his promos on ReVolution, The Imaginary Man always has something to say and his comments aren't always flattering. When PRIME fans last saw Adam Garcia, they observed a man who thought himself to be quite a big deal. Garcia wasn't afraid to talk big and back it up inside the squared circle. When these two loud mouths collide, who will emerge victorious? Skylar is seeking his first PRIME victory and Garcia will no doubt want to have his hand raised and keep his undefeated record in tact. Tony Gamble Vs Chainz These two individuals have always been threatening figures, albeit in very different manners. Gamble proves the theory of big things coming in small packages, and Chainz is a straight up beast. Gamble has competed for the highest Championship in the land, held practically every other singles belt in PRIME, and knows every dirty trick in the book. During 2009, Chainz strung together eleven consecutive victories, and was the epitome of the Intense division when he held the Intense gold. Gamble and Chainz were relatively quiet during the UltraViolence arc, mostly because one wasn't around, but both men could use this match as a platform to display their case for a Universal Championship shot. Lindsay Troy Vs Castor V. Strife On several different occasions, Castor. V Strife was less than a second away from becoming the Universal Champion at UltraViolence. Nonetheless, he has made himself a significant part of the PRIME landscape and a force to be reckoned with in a short period of time. The biggest return from the UltraViolence Pay-Per-View was that of The Queen of the Ring, one of the few people to hold the Universal Championship twice in her illustrious career. She made an impact and most are saying the only reason she lost at the PPV was because of politics and her longtime rivalry with Devin Shakur. Both of these wrestlers are looking to get back on the winning track and the battle against Strife will be the Queen's first televised battle in PRIME this year. Main Event Tyler Rayne Vs Garbage Bag Johnny Take the longest reigning 5 Star Champion in the history of PRIME and match him up against the current 5 Star Champion. Take the 2008 Dual Halo winner and put him against the 2009 Dual Halo winner. Take one of the most charismatic superstars and stick him against one of the craziest men to ever enter the world of wrestling. All of this will be taking place in one match as Tyler Rayne takes on Garbage Bag Johnny. The Underground Pimp hasn't been very vocal in the past few weeks, but if Tyler Rayne decides to put his mind to something then everyone should be running for the hills. Garbage Bag Johnny, the commissioner of GCW, is now a big time player in PRIME once again after defeating Elise Ares and capturing his first championship gold. The match is no doubt going to be full of innovation and both men are very capable of taking a beating and still ticking. ReVolution 225 will be held at the Bud Walton Arena in Fayetteville, Nebraska.
