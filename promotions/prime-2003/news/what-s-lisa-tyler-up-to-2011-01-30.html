What's Lisa Tyler Up To?

30 January 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In late November, it was reported that Lisa Tyler, or a woman who looked an awful lot like her was seen leaving the Beacon Nightclub with a mysterious man. Things have been quiet since that report and it was never confirmed that the woman was indeed PRIME's current on screen chief. My phone exploded with multiple calls however over the holiday break from people who claimed to see Lisa Tyler yet again. Most of the contacts say they saw Lisa Tyler sharing numerous drinks in a dark corner of the Myst Nightclub with an unidentified man the night prior to the King of Kings event in Phoenix. The two individuals looked very cozy, hugging and holding hands at least a few times through the evening. As things were getting a little heavier between the pair, they both fled the club through the back door, possibly to avoid being seen. No one could identify the mystery man as he was wearing thick clothing, a hat and sunglasses, much like the reported sighting back in November. Curiously, I also received a few calls claiming someone fitting the description of Tyler was seen drinking, dancing and flirting with numerous young ladies at the Cash Inn Country western-themed lesbian bar two nights after King of Kings concluded. Unfortunately, details are sketchy as to whether this piece of information could be trusted since the few callers contacted us after seeing a picture of Lisa Tyler on a King of Kings promotional billboard still posted in Arizona days after the event had already taken place. One does have to wonder though, if any of these supposed sightings were of Lisa Tyler, why would she allow herself to be seen in any questionable situations at these clubs? More to come as details are confirmed.
