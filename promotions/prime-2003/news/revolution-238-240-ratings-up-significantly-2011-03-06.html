ReVolution 238-240 Ratings: Up Significantly

6 March 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ever since 2011 began, PRIME has been on somewhat of a tear when it comes to producing quality ReVolutions. Although, the company hasn’t released its numbers, an official was able to finally get his hands on them for 238-240. 238: 3.6 239: 3.63 240: 3.75 These are higher numbers than the last company numbers to be released, 230 and 231. The numbers are expected to stay the same with strong involvement from Devin Shakur, Alexandra Pierce, Hessian, and other PRIME notables. 241 will likely continue the trend or keep close to it, while 242 is likely to draw a big number given all three titles will be on the line.
