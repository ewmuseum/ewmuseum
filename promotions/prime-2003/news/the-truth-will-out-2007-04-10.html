The Truth Will Out?

10 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When asked of his recent match on ReVolution 123, Jack Murphy had nothing but contempt and bitterness aimed towards the current crop of roster members. "It's ridiculous," he quipped on his way out of the Lawlor Events Center in Reno, "you can't just give title shots away for no reason. People complain to no end that the business has no prestige or honour to it anymore and frankly I wouldn't disagree with them lately. You can't tell me that people like Brandon Youngblood have a greater right to a title simply because of past glories, hell, he doesn't even have glories to his name! I've been screwed out of a title shot by someone who was blacklisted from the promotion we work for and what does he get for mucking up his life? He gets the goods and I'm left out in the cold. It wasn't like this back in AWC. That was a place that could at least have a solid backing for its titles. It didn't matter how big you were going into the place because you had to earn your keep no matter what. Did you see them billing me simply because of my past with OSW? No, they made me work for it and earn the right to be their champion. You can't say Youngblood has earned his way to a shot, that's for sure." Following up on this, the reporter probed Murphy about supposed backstage splits to which the Bull was quite amicable in answering. "I'm not going to lie about it, there are some definite rifts. Some people, and they know exactly who they are, need to take the sticks out of their asses and respect the fact that us refugees from the AWC put in an awful lot more effort into this business than they do. Business is picking up and if they're not careful, they might just get left behind." Murphy couldn't be questioned any further as he was in a rush to head off for his next destination, but the words were definitely something to linger on.
