Snow Waives His Right to Wait for GAN

12 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In light of these developments, the 5-Star champ is set to face off against the Illustrious Face-Eater in what will be a rematch between the two. Their first singles encounter came this past summer in a bout for PTC’s Unified Championship. Snow won via count out and reports say that Face-Eater has been chomping at the bit to get a second chance at the self-proclaimed Original Villain. "If Snow wants to waive his right to sit out the next few weeks, that’s fine by us," Blaine Blair shrugged when questioned about the champ’s decision. "It only means ratings in the end, anyway. Our main concern, of course, is that he stays healthy enough to put on the big matches when they count - at the Pay Per Views. But if he wants to push his schedule a little bit, I have no problem with that." Blair couldn’t help but get a few punches in at rival federation, GCW. "See, that’s what makes our champions at PRIME legit, unlike some companies out there. Remember Nova? He defended the Unified title ten times! Lindsay Troy just faced nine challengers in one night. And now we’ve got Snow saying he’d rather fight than take a few weeks off, despite his hectic schedule. Things like that are what make us the undisputed king of the wrestling industry." Meanwhile, Snow has other problems on his hands, and they come in the form of a near-three hundred pound monster that goes by the name of Chainz. Snow requested that Chainz’s suspension be lifted last week, and there are sure to be fireworks at Rev 141. Most members of PRIME’s staff were shocked at the move by Snow, having the man who literally endangered his life return to the organization, and tension will be high in the building tonight. It’s rumored that at the behest of Lisa Tyler, Blaine Blair requested fifty percent more of the Hartford Civic Center Coliseum’s security guards to be on hand for the evening, just in case things get out of control. Be sure to check into ReVolution 141, where Snow does battle again with the Illustrious Face Eater. The card is filled with excellent matches, including a main event featuring Tony Gamble and the return of former PRIME owner, Chet Worth. Danny Ferguson and Chandler Tsonda take on Brandon Youngblood and Devin Shakur in a match that will showcase four of PRIME’s top stars. Earlier in the night, we’ll be treated to a match of giants, where Chainz will be returning from his suspension to take on seven footer, Titan St. James, and Tom Walczak.
