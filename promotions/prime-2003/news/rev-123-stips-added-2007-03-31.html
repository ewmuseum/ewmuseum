ReV 123 Stips Added

31 March 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opening triple threat match between Pierce Lavelle, Brandon Youngblood and "The Bull" Jack Murphy will now decide Sun Tzu’s next challenger as 5-Star champion. "Jack showed the world last week that he can also make an impact in PRIME as a singles star," Blaine remarked following the Irishman’s upset of the 5-Star champ at ReV 122. "But he’s not the only one who can stake a claim - Youngblood was never beaten for the belt prior to the events that saw him leave PRIME in 2005, and Pierce Lavelle, despite suffering a set-back with a tainted loss to Devin Shakur at Culture Shock, has made a strong start in PRIME, and has shown in the past to be well suited to gold." But Sun Tzu isn’t the only champion who’s been put on notice for next week, as the match between Devin Shakur and the Trashcan Man has been switched to Intense Rules, with the winner taking on the Sultan of Soul at 124. "After defeating the Trashcan Man last week, and still being owed a rematch from Culture Shock, it could be argued that Sonny Silver deserved the next shot," Blaine half-heartedly admitted. "BUT, since he can now apparently create his own PRIME sanctioned titles, I didn’t think he’d need to worry with going against Asa once again." With those changes made, the current card for ReVolution 123 looks like… Main Event Champion vs. Champion Non-Title "37th Chamber" champion Easton Hall vs. 5-Star champion Sun Tzu Jonathon Winters vs. Shawn Stewart Intense Rules Match Winner faces Asa Fountain for the Intense Title at ReV 124 Devin Shakur vs. The Trashcan Man Tag Team Match Jackson Rust and Carys vs. The Covenant of Ethan Knight and Horace Tully Opening Triple Threat Match Winner faces Sun Tzu for the 5-Star Title at ReV 124 Pierce Lavelle vs. Brandon Youngblood vs. Jack Murphy
