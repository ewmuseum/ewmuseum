Collosus is upon us

1 August 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dark Match Sam Landry Vs. Peter Maxwell A treat for the fans, this ‘pre show’ match was just added to the CIII card. Sure to be a hot opener. Opening Tag Team Championship Match Danny Ferguson © and Chet Worth vs. Business and Pleasure The A-List has been dominant in 2006, and at ReV 100, they weaseled their way to victory over B&P thanks to a handicap rule added in their favor by Nelson. This time… not happening. Ferguson will be forced to team with the new boss man, Chet Worth, to once again defend the A-List’s precious gold against the incredibly hot (as in successful… not in a ‘me hitting on Lindz way…) Singles Match Ivan Stanislav vs. Titan St. James When these two seven foot, four hundred pound monsters collide at CIII, the ring will have to be reinforced. At ReV 100, they destroyed the squared circle (literally). This time, PRIME is ready, and so are these two giants. WATCH Match (Win A Title Chance) Kingfisher (5-Star) vs. Cruz (Intense) vs. Team Baked (Tag) vs. The Covenant (Tag) vs. Fiore (Intense) vs. Adam (5-Star) vs. "The Muffin Man" Kin Hiroshi (Internet) The Rules: * Battle Royal rules apply (Over the Top Rope Elimination, Both feet touch the floor) * Tag Teams are at an advantage as BOTH members need to be eliminated. * THE BUMP SPOT - If you are eliminated, you have 1 minute at ringside. If no one else is eliminated, you are allowed back into the ring. If someone is eliminated in that minute, you're done, and they take over the Bump Spot. * 2 of the 3 tag teams will be represented in the match. Internet Championship Match Jonathan Winters © Vs. Tony Gamble. These two have been at each other’s throats for weeks. Winters captured gold early in his PRIME career, winning the Internet Championship just a few weeks ago. But Gamble was quick to get the title back… in the manner of thievery. At CIII, the two men will meet, and one will walk away the official Internet Champion. Cage Match Danny Ferguson Vs. Kyle Lamen For months, these two have been locked in an amazing feud. At Culture Shock, Ferguson defeated Lamen (with the help of his A-List buddies) to capture the 5 Star Championship. Victory wasn’t enough. Since CS, Ferguson has taken every available opportunity to rub salt in an open wound (that wound… lamen is dying from cancer). Now, it all comes to this… a steel cage match… one on one… with pride on the line. Intense Championship Match Nova © Vs. The Illustrious Face-Eater It all started over smoking a little bit of pot… it lead to Facey leaving Nova to take the blame and end up suspended. Then it led to these two guys getting themselves glued together for the duration of ReV 100. This shit is just so good you can’t make it up. At ReV 100 Nova captured the Intense title from Olsig. Now, one of PRIME’s top prizes is on the line in this match between two amazing, stoned, superstars. 5 Star Championship Match Chandler Tsonda © Vs. Johnny Noble Things have become personal between these two, with Tsonda taking constant verbal shots at Noble about his daughters ‘hook up’ with a certain French Canadian. Chandler has quickly defined himself as a winner and stepped out of the shadows of FergFace to show the A-List truly is made of equal stars. Noble is the old timer, out of his comfort zone, trying to win PRIME gold for the first time since his debut, and he’d love nothing more than to do so at the cost of Chandler Tsonda. "Hall of Fame Championship" Match Ignatius Lisieux Vs. Karina Wolfenden An intense rivalry that started at Culture Shock over the simple idea of respect. Iggy tried to show it for the K-Wolf… Kari wanted nothing of it. Now, PRIME’s past faces off against PRIME’s present to determine where the true greatness lies. In recent weeks, Wolfenden has shown an increasing nasty side to her personality, outing Iggy to his friend Noble about fooling around with Johnny’s daughter. At CIII… Lisieux will have his chance to take care of business. Universal Championship Match Killean Sirrajin (c) Vs. Tchu The Main Event. Four years in the making. Killean Sirrajing and Tchu have engaged in what has possibly been PRIME’s longest, most heated feud. Finally, the two will collide at CIII for the top prize in PRIME. Tchu, thanks to Worth, will finally get the chance to take back the title he still considers to be his. And The Supreme Machine will finally get his chance to end what he started four years ago in a little place called the Underground.
