Southern Discomfort

14 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The show's hot opener was a rubber match between Chandler Tsonda and Wade Elliott. The Model Citizen promised the previous week that he wouldn't wait until Colossus CVI to settle his 1-1 series with the Bad Dog, and that he wouldn't let Wade share the big stage with him. The 'Bama Bruiser did his best in the show's opening moments to make Tsonda eat his words. Next up was the marked-out-for showdown between youngsters David Noble and Bryan Dawkins. At one point, it was the Flyin' Hawaiian who wore the mantle of "Next Big Thing" in PRIME, but after the events of UltraViolence, David Noble had staked his claim to that moniker. This one was nothing but entertaining, and the fans loved every minute of it. Jon Rhine stopped the monster Hessian. That's the long and short of it. But on ReV 196, it was back to work for Rhine, who had the grim task of standing across the ring from the monstrous Michael Sloan, b.k.a. Chainz. Once again looking to overcome a significant size advantage, Rhine fought another uphill battle in hopes of stretching out his win streak. The sub-main event was a doozy with global implications. For the first time in months, PRIME hosted a PTC Elite Title Match, with a surprising PRIMEate representing the federation. Wicked Ways' Ruby, a manager by trade, stepped into the ring with SCCW veteran Wyatt Connors, ready and willing to do battle with the controversial religious figure. With a homefield advantage, Ruby did her damndest to try and capture PRIME's first Elite Title. And, as ReVolution main events so often do, the grand finale had some history behind it. Kaiser Vashaun and Troy Douglas faced off at Colossus V in a midcard match that had a small amount of hype and a big amount of awesome. After their instant classic Intense Title match, Vashaun had sworn off giving Douglas any kind of rematch. Now, with Vashaun wearing a brand new strap, the Next in Line stepped back into the ring with Megatron, each man dead-set on being the star of the show. In addition to frenetic in-ring action, there was the usual glitz, glamor, and gore. To find out why David Noble might have a future in NASCAR, what bone Jason Snow broke at UltraViolence, and the huge news out of the Killean Sirrajin camp, there's only one option.
