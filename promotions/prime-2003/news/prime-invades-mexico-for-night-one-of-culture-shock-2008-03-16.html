PRIME Invades Mexico For Night One Of Culture Shock

16 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2007 Jewel In The Crown cashes in his Universal Title shot against recently-crowned two-time champion Lindsay Troy. After a bitter and needling feud between the two - with 5-Star Champion Tyler Rayne caught in the middle - who would finally get the edge when the bell rang? Rayne had his own problems with Nitz Donnelly. After besting the Ego of New England at ReV 150 to win the belt, he found himself hounded by the newfound nemesis until the rematch was inevitable. The losers of those title matches wouldn't have the opportunity for a rematch, as each belt already had a next man in line. Easton Hall and Devin Shakur had already earned 5-Star and Universal title shots, respectively, and on Night One, those two locked up in a battle between Who's Next for PRIME. Which of the two future champions and megastars would earn bragging rights? Who could start the future one night early? The Dual Halo was the last thing on the minds of Wade Elliott and Hoyt Williams on Night One. The two premier brawlers in PRIME were ready to beat each other bloody after several emotional confrontations stemming from marital impropriety. Neither man lacks passion and neither needs an excuse to pound a man senseless, so what happened when they locked horns over a woman? Would either even survive to Night Two? In the night's opening match, a long-simmering war between Dusk and Tony Gamble finally reaches boiling point under Intense rules. Would The Grin have the last laugh, or will the Lost Soul find himself a second reign as Intense Champion? All that, plus some new faces backstage and a whole lot of pre-Halo tension on CULTURE SHOCK 2008: NIGHT ONE!
