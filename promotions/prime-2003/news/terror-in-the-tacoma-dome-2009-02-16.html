Terror in the Tacoma Dome

16 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Proving her point, Lisa upheld the decision to go with a hardcore themed special edition of ReVolution, in partnership with the new Friday the 13th film, entitled "OverKill". The evening was supposed to begin with a First-Blood match between PRIME newcomer Hunter Sabuani and Enemigo III… but things didn’t turn out quite as planned, when an uninvited guest and a bucket of paint caused a problem or two. The mayhem didn’t stop there, as the night featured two hardcore themed tag-team matches. First, in a "Fans Bring the Weapons" match, four athletic superstars showed their grittier sides, as the team of Jay Phoenix and Bryan Dawkins took on High Flyer and Mary-Lynn Mayweather. And the fans in the Tacoma Dome had some pretty… interesting… items lying around the house. Later, in the first match of a double main event, DUI put their tag straps on the line against Wicked Ways in a match right up the champ’s alley… a Bar Room Brawl. Wicked Ways were ready to make a name for themselves at the expense of the reigning champions, but those party animals were quite at home in the dingy atmosphere, and had no intentions on letting gold out of their grasp. And Washington saw plenty of brutal singles action as well. The Queen of the Ring, Lindsay Troy, took on the red-hot Construction of Destruction, Hessian, in a Crown of Thorns match. Later, both superstars would make an appearance in the main event, where Kaiser Vashaun defended his Intense Championship against Fusenshoff inside a steel cage, as the first leg of the Intense Title Gauntlet instituted by Tyler Rayne, and upheld by Lisa Tyler. Plenty of blood was spilled, thanks in part to the unscheduled appearances of Troy and Hessian. And behind the scenes, while fans cheered on the carnage in the ring, the Universal Champion, Chandler Tsonda, was brutally assaulted by an unknown assailant, prompting a heated face-to-face between Lisa Tyler and an outraged Tyler Rayne. If you want to know what happened when the new boss stood toe-to-toe with disgruntled employees, what a bucket of paint was doing in a first blood match, who walked out as tag champs, and what mayhem the main-event brought to Washington, check out ReVolution: OverKill and experience the brutal winter night that wreaked havoc on Tacoma.
