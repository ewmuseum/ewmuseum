New Ownership Announces Corporate Restructuring; Blair, Tyler Given Promotions

25 March 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a Monday release, the group announced a full corporate restructuring, including a move of PRIME headquarters and the establishment of a new leadership hierarchy. Highlights from Monday's release: - PRIME Headquarters, previously stationed in Albany, NY, will be moving to Chicago, IL, effective April 1. The new offices will be housed in a 20,000 square foot space in the Windy City's famed Merchandise Mart building. - A Board of Directors has been established to oversee all company operations going forward. The members of Prometheus, LLC, will form the core of the Board, but no names were provided in the release. - Blaine Blair, the former intern who served as acting CEO after the departure of Chet Worth, has been promoted to serve as Chief Operating Officer. He will serve as the primary liason to the Board of Directors and will be stationed out of the Chicago offices following the move. Due to Blair's new responsibilities and the establishment of the Board, the now-vacant CEO position will remain unfilled. - Lisa Tyler, who served in a general manager capacity during Blair's leadership, has also received a promotion. She will serve as Vice President for Talent Relations, and in that new role she will focus on all human-resources related issues (contracts, new hires, etc.) regarding the PRIME roster. Like Blaine, she will operate out of the Chicago headquarters. - Day-to-day show operations during PRIME tours will now be coordinated through a newly-hired executive producer. Reality show guru C.P. Cantrell, whose previous projects include Survival of the Fittest and Operation: Dancefloor, has been hired in that new capacity. He will begin at ReVolution 154.
