Title Shots In Titletown As PRIME Visits Kansas

12 April 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
For Crucifix, challenging Rayne for the 5-Star Title was business, a chance to cement his fast rise through the ranks and prove that the Halo success was anything but a fluke. For Cozen, all the same applied, but it was anything but business. After kidnapping Lindsay Troy before ReVolution 155 and posing as the Queen of the Ring, the Faceless Fighter had made her Universal Title match this week very, VERY personal. After being shaken and stirred by the events of last week, the champ had some help to keep Cozen at bay for 156...some of it wanted, some of it not. Also on the show, main event fixtures Chandler Tsonda and Tony Rolo both tried to rebound from recent losses, but they had the stampeding Silver scion Captain Justice standing in their way. Speaking of Silver, he flexed his new Sports Entertainment Liason To PRIME powers to get a jump on Xavier Kannon, but Ecks Kay returned the favor in a more subtle fashion. The new faces of PRIME took center stage for 156. With Cozen and Crucifix claiming title shots, newcomers Kaiser Vashaun and Simply Beautiful made their debuts, while the Jason Natas/Bryan Dawkins and Union Jack/Jimmy Bonafide feuds continued to heat up the undercard. All that and more on ReVolution 156!
