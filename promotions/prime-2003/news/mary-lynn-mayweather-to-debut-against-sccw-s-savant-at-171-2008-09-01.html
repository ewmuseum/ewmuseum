Mary-Lynn Mayweather To Debut Against SCCW's Savant At 171?!

1 September 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After the Dead Man's Hand's attacks at ReVolution 170 -- most significantly on Angelica Brooks, but also on Mary-Lynn Mayweather and Lindsay Troy -- one might wonder why it is that C.P. Cantrell will not only allow Desade's people back on PRIME programming but has, indeed, invited them to compete in a PRIME ring, as DMH member Savant will face Mayweather in her PRIME in-ring debut at ReVolution 171. We asked Cantrell that very question. "Look, I'm a reasonable businessman," Cantrell said. "But getting Mary-Lynn in the ring against one of Desade's girls will attract attention, and that's the business I'm in. Hell, I'm thinking of calling up Massengill for a sponsorship deal." The PRIME Executive Producer chuckled then, but he leaned forward. "I had a long talk with SCCW's Head Professor after the show, because I am just as angry about this as the rest of my roster. Angie Brooks was a good kid. [Dr Nigel Kensington] and I came to an agreement, given how strict the contract that Desade's people have over there in Sin City. If I have to dangle Mary-Lynn in front of them to get them to come here, then so be it. She's a decent fighter, I've been told. Besides, I'm sure the roster has some things they'd like to say to whoever Pierce sends with her." On the subject of whether Alexandra Pierce's massive Dead Man's Hand organization will be allowed in the building, Cantrell was a little more cagey. "My head of security and I have discussed the issue. Twelve of them made it hard for the Enemigos to react. Suffice it to say, we're not going to end the show the same way two weeks in a row." ReVolution 171 airs this Wednesday, September 3rd, live from the HSBC Arena in Buffalo, New York. It can be seen on the fX network following the series premiere of the new drama "Sons of Anarchy."
