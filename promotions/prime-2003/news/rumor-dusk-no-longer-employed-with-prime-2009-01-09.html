Rumor: Dusk No Longer Employed With PRIME?

9 January 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rumors have been flying under the radar for the past few days as to pertaining the future of the Lost Soul, but it would seem like his future would have to be one without PRIME. It would seem like the contract that Dusk signed two years ago with PRIME has come to an end without any sign of another contract coming down the pipeline. Apparently, Dusk has fallen out of favor with C.P. Cantrell who has a clause in his contract that basically gives him final say on personnel decisions, and when it was brought to his attention that it was time to offer Dusk an extension he vehemently refused to give him one. He went so far as saying that if it wasn't for the fact the fans were enamored with him that he wouldn't even book him on King of Kings. Unfortunately for him, Blaine Blair and Lisa Tyler managed to override his decision and forced Cantrell to book Dusk in a high profile match. Much has been said about Dusk's career in PRIME, one that has seen him on that door to greatness, but never able to break through it. The first eight months of 2008 saw him rise to the very cusp of the Universal Title door as he won the Intense Title, came in 5th place in the Dual Halo, and defeated Wade Elliott in a match that left everyone in awe as the two superstars put it all on the line. He even was part of the main event of Colossus for a second year in a row when he faced Tyler Rayne for the 5-Star Title. It was seemingly after that evening that things started to go downhill for Dusk as he began to get more and more wrapped up in the tricky web of Desade and Cozen. Of course, after his defeat against Cozen and then moving past the first round of the Jewel in the Crown tournament it seemed like Dusk might be able to pull off the impossible and win the tournament until he ran into the eventual winner, Tyler Rayne. Another factor into Cantrell's decision is perceived belief that Dusk had betrayed him by showing up on an SCCW show a few weeks prior. Some have even said that this was the straw that broke the camel's back. Since Cantrell's arrival into PRIME, he's voiced his displeasure with Dusk rather loudly, but seemed to have to eat his words as merchandise sales for Dusk were amongst the top earners month after month and his stature in the eyes of the fans continued to grow week after week. We tried to get a comment from Dusk's close and personal friend, High Flyer, but were met with a response that we're incapable of reprinting here in this news story due to the vulgarity and profane nature. Voicemails left with Dusk's agent and manager were subsequently not returned at the time we went to press on this story. When we asked Lisa Tyler about this story, she offered a short and curt, "No comment." More on this story when we get the latest details.
