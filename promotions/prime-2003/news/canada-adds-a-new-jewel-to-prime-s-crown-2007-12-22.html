Canada Adds a New Jewel to PRIME's Crown

22 December 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The card was jam packed with action from top to bottom. The Codemaster and Titan St. James squared off in what most people thought was a lopsided affair, but eyes can be deceiving. Could the Codemaster find the code for God Mode to defeat the overpowering Israeli powerhouse? Of course, Brandon Youngblood, Chandler Tsonda and Hoyt Williams were to clash in the Jewel in the Crown Semi-Finals for the right to move on to the Co-Main event of the evening to cement their names in immortality. The other side of the coin saw Wade Elliott, Ian Nackedy and last years JiTC winner Tony Gamble facing off to face the winner of the previous match. Did Tony Gamble become the first ever repeat winner of the annual event or was a new King waiting to be crowned? Also, Aaron Andrews and Tyler Chance of Risk and Reward were able to pressure the Tag Team Champions into a title defense, but it was not in a common tag team match. One half of the titles was on the line as Gildenstern put up his half against Aaron Andrews. PRIME could very well have Tag Team Champions compromising of members of two different teams right now and you wouldn't know it. Why? Cause you need to watch on Pay-Per-View... that's why! Easton Hall, Dusk and Chainz obliterated each other in a classic and finally the Universal Champion Sonny Silver attempted to do the impossible. PRIME's Wrecking Ball has rarely been defeated and coming back to PRIME is exactly what the Champion did not want to happen. Did Tchu make history by capturing the Universal Championship for an unprecidented third time or did Sonny prove that his win against Lindsay Troy was no fluke? Certainly, his disputed claims of being the best Universal Champion ever would hold some weight should he send The Inhuman Being packing back to the wrestling old folks retirement home. What are you waiting for?
