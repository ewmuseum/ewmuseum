9 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
However, all of this has taken a backseat as the two men have turned the spotlight upon themselves. Two weeks ago, Dusk made a shocking appearance on FUSE’s KillZone 33, sending waves and ripples through the wrestling world, and infuriating the FUSE roster. Several members came out against Dusk even appearing on the show, feeling as if he was stealing from their time and insulting their top champion, John Covel. This was only to be followed up with Sage showing up on PRIME’s ReVolution 122 where he proceeded to bash in Dusk’s head with a car window.
