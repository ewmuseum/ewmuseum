UltraViolence 2010 Recap and Buyrate

22 June 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The opening contest featured the return of Steve Starr taking on Skylar Montgomery and Shawn Hart. All three wrestlers appeared to be in fantastic form and gave the crowd a very hot and exciting opener. In the end, Steve Starr managed to come away with the pinfall victory when Skylar Montgomery was unable to prevent his pinfall on Shawn Hart. With the new 24/7 rule on Tchu's head, The Inhuman Being's life has been turned upside down. He never knows when he is going to compete, and the Pay-Per-View proved that to be an undeniable fact. While preparing for his match, Tchu was jumped in his locker room by number one contender, Hush. The match continued in the backstage area, making a step at the vending machine among other places, before coming out to the ringside area. Tchu was able to finish the match and retain his Intense Championship. Surprisingly, The Inhuman Being did not get blindsided again for the remainder of the evening. Speaking of unannounced attacks, Boda finally got to come face to face with the individual who had been tormenting him for over a month. Although, not in the fashion The Big Bad Boda Daddy envisioned. Rusty, a man who helped Boda during his rehabilitation stint, appeared when Boda brought him to the center of the ring. An unscheduled appearance by "The Ego Buster" Dan Ryan signaled the first of two big returns for the evening. He introduced the actual opposition for Boda... The Queen of the Ring, Lindsay Troy. Garnering perhaps the biggest reaction of the evening, Troy came out and gave a phenomenal effort in an attempt to thwart Boda and come up with a pinfall victory in her first PRIME match of the year. Unfortunately, a referee bump and fast count from new referee Elvis Nixon halted The Queen's plans for immediate supremacy. Another individual who finally had enough of being taunted and prodded without knowing the identity of his attacker decided to take matters into his own hands. At the start of the evening, Devin Shakur received a box of things outside his locker room. The next prank consisted of his iPod having only one song, Metallica's "Better Than You". The final joke saw Shakur's giant plasma television alternating clips from Home Alone 1 and 2. Figuring it would be better to get his attacker out in the open, Shakur emerged from his office and demanded the attacker show his face. Well, the attacker showed his face, but it was the face Shakur had just seen, Kevin McCallister, hands on cheeks after having felt the effects of after shave. However, in the pictures, Shakur saw subliminal images from his earlier days and then the image of the man who decided to prod him... Tony Gamble. The oldest of Shakur's friends popped out from under the ring and got the bell rung just as Shakur turned around to face him. One small package later and Devin Shakur felt the cackling laughter of 20,000 people while Tony Gamble celebrated amongst them, announcing his return. The sub-Main event of the evening turned out to be a wild brawl from the very start. Elise Ares and Garbage Bag Johnny are the polar opposites when it comes to lifestyle. One expects only the finest luxuries in the world, and the other will bless you if you give him a dollar bill. These two met with the 5 Star Championship on the line, and the melee did not disappoint. GBJ supporters in Dallas were able to leave happy when The Dirtiest Dude in PRIME captured the 5 Star Championship belt from The Havana Harlot. In the Main Event of the evening, a new Universal Champion would be crowned. That was a guarantee. However, the guarantee never stated how many people were going to be in the match. Sure, three were slated to be involved, but only two made it into the ring. Emilio Rage was jumped in his locker room by both Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels. The reasoning behind the assault was, according to Shakur, Rage being the first person Shakur's eyes came across. Nonetheless, the show went on with Vangelus Olsig taking on Castor V. Strife in what turned out to be a long and intense bash. Neither man was willing to back down for over thirty minutes, giving the crowd everything they could ask for. High flying maneuvers, submission attempts, blood, signature moves, innovative techniques, and a new champion. Vangelus Olsig managed to snatch victory from the jaws of defeat by landing his Sacrifice finishing maneuver and coming out of UltraViolence with the Universal Championship over his shoulder. The Pay-Per-View is said to have been a treat for those who attended, but not all wrestling critics agree. The buyrates were somewhere in the 200,000s, very low for a company like PRIME. Lisa Tyler sent an e-mail moments ago to Headquarters claiming, "Nobody in the front office enjoys the low numbers, but we are hoping the events of the Pay-Per-View will spark intrigue over the next few shows as we make our way to Colossus VII." PRIME's next scheduled event is to take place at the Bob Walton Arena in Fayetteville, Arkansas on Independence Day. No matches have taken place, but with the just announced release of Roxie Sykes, PRIME is looking for a new Number 1 Contender to the 5 Star Championship.
