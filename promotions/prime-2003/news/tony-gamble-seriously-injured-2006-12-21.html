Tony Gamble Seriously Injured?

21 December 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Backs are a funny thing," Dr. Ceasar Chavez, head kinesiologist from Las Vegas General Hospital said in a conference call to the PRIME offices. "Some people can be in fantastic shape, suffer a back injury and never recover. Others can literally heal in hours and days. We're going to have to wait and see before we clear him to return to the ring." "Well, obviously, it's a terrible loss for PRIME. Maybe there's something to the whole 'Jonathon Winters didn't do it first' curse that someone mentioned," PRIME CEO Chet Worth said. "Fuck guys. Nova did this. I fucking know it. Goddamn that motherfucker. I don't know what the fuck to... Pardon me." "What Chet means, ladies and gentlemen," Blaine Blair said, taking over for a flustered Chet Worth after a few tense moments. "What, we as an organization mean is that this is obviously a terrible time for Mister Gamble and his family, and that no matter the time frame, rules are made to be amended. Because of that, Mister Gamble will get his title shot at whomever is the champion whenever he returns, if he can. If he cannot, Mister Gamble is welcome to pass it off to whomever he pleases, within the span of one year, as per his contract. "As far as a replacement for the main event at Culture Shock, Angelo Deville comes to mind as a name that is most certainly deserving of the title shot, despite what Mister Worth seems to think. Adam also could very well get the shot, or even Karina Wolfenden. What we don't want to do is rush into anything. Otherwise, we could see a very convoluted title scene at Culture Shock, and Nova deserves better than that, again, despite the feelings of Mister Worth."
