PRIME Announces ReV: OverKill!

12 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The one-night special event will fill ReVolution’s usual time-slot on the FX Network, but the evening will be anything but typical. In the spirit of one of America’s great horror franchises, ReVolution: OverKill will host five incredible matches, all of them featuring a sinister, hardcore stipulation. From ‘First Blood’ to ‘Crown of Thorns’… each match will be sure to promise the sort of carnage that has come to categorize American horror cinema. In one case, fans will even be able to play a role in the mayhem, as part of a ‘Fans Bring the Weapons’ bout. The night will be capped off by back-to-back title matches, with D.U.I. defending their tag straps against Wicked Ways in a match fitting of the champ’s nature… a bar room brawl. And the main-event of the evening will see the Intense Title on the line, as Kaiser Vashaun puts his gold up for grabs against Fusenshoff, in what could be the first of a series of ‘gauntlet’ title defenses for The Next in Line. That is, of course, assuming he can survive the brutality of being locked inside a ten-foot high steel cage with a hungry challenger ready to take him down. It’s sure to be a huge night, with major implications on the road to Culture Shock. ReVolution: OverKill will terrorize the Tacoma Dome Wednesday night @ 10:00PM, only on the FX Network! ReVolution: OverKill Card FIRST BLOOD MATCH "Peerless" Hunter Sabuani (w/ Miss Madison) vs. Enemigo III FANS BRING THE WEAPONS Jay Phoenix and Bryan Dawkins vs. High Flyer and Mary-Lynn Mayweather "CROWN OF THORNS" MATCH Hessian vs. Lindsay Troy BAR ROOM BRAWL For the PRIME Tag Team Championship Wicked Ways vs. D.U.I.(c) CAGE MATCH Leg One of the Intense Title Gauntlet Fusenshoff vs. Kaiser Vashaun(c)
