PRIME Announces Field For 2008 Jewel In The Crown Tournament

25 October 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The full slate of participants: Tyler Rayne Kaiser Vashaun Hank Cobb Jason Natas Troy Douglas Rhett Locke Killean Sirrajin Dusk Bryan Dawkins Jay Phoenix Jimmy Bonafide High Flyer Dani Fuhrer Jason Snow Brock Shepherd Vox (formerly Vangellus Oxios) (Free Agent) Jesse Jamester (Free Agent) Horace Tully (Free Agent) Fusenshoff (EPW) Christian Darke (Free Agent) El Truco (Free Agent) Clayton Byrd (SCCW) Elise Ares (BACW) Rudy Simpson (Free Agent) Jared Sykes (formerly King Blueberry) (SCCW) Darth von Doom (SNM, HOW, HSW) Dame Dillion (Free Agent) The first round will consist of nine Triple Threat matches, one fall to a finish. The quarterfinals consist of three Triple Threat matches. A unique aspect of this year's JITC will be the addition of a "Wrestleback" round. During wrestlebacks, the eliminated competitor who did not take the fall in each first-round match will be given a second chance to advance in the tournament. The wrestleback round consists of three Triple Threat matches, with the winners then facing off in a fourth Triple Threat match. The winner of the "Second Chance" wrestlebacks will join the three other semifinalists to complete a conventional four-man bracket for King of Kings. The semifinals and finals will be held at King of Kings, all in a standard one-on-one format. Given the recent personnel shake-up in PRIME and the Jewel In The Crown's propensity for making new stars, the tournament is expected to be hotly contested in the coming weeks. Jewel In The Crown matches begin at ReVolution 176, which takes place in Richmond, VA on October 31.
