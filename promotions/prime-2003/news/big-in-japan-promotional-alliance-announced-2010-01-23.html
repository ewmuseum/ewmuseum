Big In Japan: Promotional Alliance Announced

23 January 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I'm extremely happy with the terms of this agreement. No matter how you look at things, it's a win-win arrangement. We get more exposure in Japan, Five Rings gets more exposure in the western markets. Some of our younger, developmental talent - guys like Diego Foster for example - have the opportunity to get some valuable experience and exposure to the puroresu style. And in one of the more immediate consequences of this exchange, we get to bolster our tag division with one of the hottest teams in Japanese wrestling right now. It's a very exciting time to be a wrestling fan." The team she is referring to is none other than the current FRPW Tag Team Champions, Mach Hawke and Mamoru Azuma, a team that over the course of the last year and a half has run like a buzzsaw through any and all challengers to their straps. Taking on the name the Osaka Street Cutters for their overseas campaign, they are looking to extend their run of dominance to the tag division of PRIME, setting the stage for a number interesting potential match-ups against teams like the Forsaken, the Redeemed, and even the current PRIME Tag Team Champions, Change in Spades. The Osaka Street Cutters are set to make their PRIME debut at ReVolution 216 in the Saitama Super Arena in Saitama, Japan teaming with Jason Snow against The Three Amigos.
