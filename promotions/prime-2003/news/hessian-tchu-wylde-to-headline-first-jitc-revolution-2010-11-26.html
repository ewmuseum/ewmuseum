Hessian, Tchu, Wylde to headline first JITC ReVolution

26 November 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PRIME unveiled its Jewel in the Crown field earlier in the week, and tomorrow night, the Casper Event Center will play host to ReVolution 235. All matches will determine who advances into Round 2, to be held on ReVolution 237. Opening Contest The Sentinel Vs Boda Vs Drake Daniels The Sentinel made it to a bracket final in GTT6 and recently turned heads with retirement talks. So why would he be in the first round of Jewel in the Crown? Did he get an itch to come back? Only time will tell. He, and another newcomer to PRIME, Drake Daniels out of Ocean County, New Jersey, are going to receive a rude welcome to PRIME in the form of an angry Boda. The Bastard is coming off the bad end of what some are calling an upset victory at ReVolution 234, when he lost the 5*Star Championship to Nitz Donnelly. Will The Sentinel's natural aggression prevail, will Daniels wide range of styles allow him to get through, or can Boda bounce back from a tough loss and get himself one step closer to the Universal Championship? Second Contest The First Vs Jacob McKail Vs Dr. Clay 'Chainsaw' Krueger The First managed to score a victory over resident punching bag, Patterson Loch, at ReVolution 234. Jacob McKail also squeaked out a victory against Big Bear, putting them both on the winning track heading into JITC. This doesn't bode well for another newcomer, Dr. Clay 'Chainsaw' Krueger. Not much is known about him other than he's a 5'11, 225 resident of Scottsdale, Arizona. First has shown flashes of greatness, and McKail is world renowned for his abilities, so Krueger is going to have a tough test in front of him. Sub-Main Event Castor V. Strife Vs Nitz Donnelly Vs Big Bear The only match in the entire tournament consisting of wrestlers already on the PRIME roster, the sub-main event is likely to steal the show on 234. Big Bear is looking to get a foothole back to the top, and a win on 235 would get him one step closer, not to mention a future 5*Star Championship match against Donnelly. The Ego of New England can't rest on his laurels after defeating Boda, as he has to worry about another well-motivated man nipping at his heels in Castor Strife. The God of Snuff lost the Universal Championship in a battle against 'The Murder Show' Hessian and can't be too pleased with having to start from square one again. Each man will be going into this match with something to prove and should provide a ton of fireworks for the Wyoming crowd. Main Event Katterina Wylde Vs Hessian Vs Tchu The Dark Angel knew coming to PRIME would throw her into the fire at some point or later. Katterina probably wasn't expecting to be launched into an inferno her very first week on the job, going against the top dog in PRIME, and the most accomplished wrestler in PRIME's history. Katterina is by no means a newcomer to the sport, but she'll be a newcomer to this ilk of competition. Hessian finally reached the apex, winning a World Championship, and now he's got to trudge forward and look to fend off all comers. He's got a giant bullseye on his back. Tchu expressed discontent with the way he's been treated and either the draw was very coincidental, or management elected to listen to The Elite Champion's gripe and give him a chance to put up or shut up. If either Tchu or Katterina win this match, even if Hessian is not involved in the pinfall, they receive a shot at the Universal Championship in the first few months of 2011. Also... With a plethora of newcomers on their way to PRIME for 236, will any of them make an appearance? Desade provided a hype piece on 234, and a few outlets are speculating she's going to be at 235. Will she indeed prove these rumors correct, and if not will she at least provide more hype? Word through the grapevine is that Devin Shakur will come out and address those in the Casper Event Center after his grueling defeat at the hands of Lindsay Troy. What The Boss in Black will say when given a live microphone is anybody's guess. ReVolution 235 comes to you live on November 27th from the Casper Event Center in Wyoming. Tickets are sold out. Airtime on HBO is 9:00 PM Eastern Standard Time.
