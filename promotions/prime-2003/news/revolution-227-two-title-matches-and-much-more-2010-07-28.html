ReVolution 227: Two Title Matches and Much More

28 July 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An opening tag team contest featured longtime allies Tyler Rayne and Lindsay Troy squaring off against The First and Skylar Montgomery. The team with experience and chemistry started out of the gate quickly and gained an advantage. Rayne and Troy both were able to land some shots on Montgomery, but The First brought momentum back to his team and threatened to seal the match. The Queen of the Ring fought valiantly and managed to tag out to Rayne, while First tagged out to Montgomery. Rayne came in and cleaned house before Montgomery got him onto the floor. Once the match broke down, it was only a matter of minutes before Troy and Rayne seized the advantage and Troy was able to pin Montgomery for the fall at 9:45. One of the developing storylines of the evening was the disappearance of Tracy Sloan, wife of number one contender to the Universal Championship, Chainz. Chainz went into his locker room and found a note which wished him good luck in finding his wife. The Monster from Hell's Kitchen sought vengeance on the one suspecting party, Hessian. The Murder Show claimed innocence and was supported by fellow AWA members Devin Shakur and Christian Daniels. The kidnapper would make a few more appearances throughout the night but never reveal his identity. The second match featured a longtime grudge coming to a close as Intense Champion Tchu squared off against Devin Shakur in an Intense rules match. The 24/7 Rule placed over Tchu's head by Shakur was lifted for the night, and a few seconds into the match it appeared The Inhuman Being was going to walk all over Shakur. The angered champion took out his aggression on The Boss in Black and looked to be well on his way to securing a victory, until Dan Ryan walked down the ramp and turned the tide by taking Tchu's focus off Shakur. The Boss was able to win momentum back and found himself in the same predicament Tchu was before the distraction. Much to Shakur's chagrin, the other half of the tandem, Lindsay Troy made her presence felt and distracted Shakur. Seconds after her arrival, Shakur was thrown over the top rope while Ryan entered the ring and brawled with Tchu. Troy got in a shot as payback for being fast counted at UltraViolence before Shakur took off running. The Enemigo Security Squad rushed from the back and separated Ryan and Tchu. The match was declared a No Contest at 8:53. A surprising development occurred in the first of two championship matches on the night. Boda was set to take on Garbage Bag Johnny for the 5 Star Championship, and the match got started in an unorthodox manner with Boda jumping The Dirtiest Dude in PRIME during his entrance. After a somewhat emotional speech from Boda, he showed a ruthless side and made the most of the sneak attack by softening GBJ on the outside before tossing him in the ring. Johnny fought and looked to maybe pull a shocker out, but in the end Boda landed The Boda Drop and captured the 5 Star Championship at 4:03. The Elite Championship Tournament also had a featured match on 227 as Vivica Valentine of GCW took on Tony Gamble. In their first encounter at the PRIME/GCW crossover Pay-Per-View, Valentine defeated Gamble and the fact certainly wasn't lost on The Grin. He stuck to technical wrestling and primarily submissions to wear Valentine down, opting not to go for a high risk attack. Gamble had control over the first portion of the match, but Valentine got in some offense and in the end was able to secure a victory when Gamble was unable to lock in the Smile for Me submission finisher. Valentine defeated Gamble with a Repentance at 13:04. In the Main Event, Universal Champion Vangelus Olsig took on Chainz in what turned out to be a true donnybrook. From the gate, Chainz had a singular focus, to end the match quickly and search for his wife, who still hadn't reappeared. He landed a Chain Reaction (two consecutive powerbombs) in under 20 seconds, but Olsig managed to kick out. For the first half of the contest, Chainz dominated and brutalized Olsig. The most noticeable injury was a broken nose. However, The Prince of Delusion and a trusty steel chair worked tirelessly to bring Chainz down. The spot of the night saw Olsig double stomp Chainz with a steel chair from the ring post into the first row. After Olsig got Chainz back in the ring, it appeared he would have an easy road to victory, but Chainz was able to kick out and fight back. The second go around for Chainz with his Chain Reaction only worked halfway as Olsig pulled a pair of brass knuckles from his boot and escaped the second powerbomb. One Sacrifice later and Olsig retained his Championship at 14:44.
