Colossus Spotlight: Vangelus Olsig

7 September 2010
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Olsig made his first sustained impact in PRIME at ReVolution 92 when he defeated Kyle Lamen to capture the Intense Championship. He would go onto hold that championship for 361 days, and for a long time it was considered an unbreakable record. Nobody would ever come close to catching him. It took an unprecedented reign by Jason Snow in 2009 through 2010 to match Olsig’s reign done in 2006 – 2007. Once he finished with the Intense division, Olsig focused his attention on the Universal Championship, getting into with Nova, the man who took his Intense Championship at ReVolution 102, and Tchu. No matter how many chances he was given, Olsig couldn’t capture the richest prize in the sport. He held the Alias Championship for a stint from ReVolution 118 to Culture Shock Day One in 2007, and enjoyed a small run as Tag Team Champion with Machiavieli St. Romani, but never won Universal gold. At UltraViolence 2010, Olsig’s Universal wishes finally came true. He entered PRIME’s 2010 Dual Halo an underdog, someone who people didn’t figure would make it far, and ended up defeating Brandon Youngblood to win the event outright. As a result, he got a ticket allowing him access to anything he wanted. Olsig’s request was to try and capture what Killean Sirrajin had, PRIME’s Grand Slam. He needed two championships to do so, Universal and 5*Star gold. When PRIME’s Born Again tournament was playing out, Olsig stood back and watched from sidelines. He eventually defeated New Era winner Castor V. Strife to become PRIME’s Universal Champion. After going through a rigorous defense against Michael Sloan at ReVolution 227, Olsig knew he would be facing Strife for a second time at Colossus. Olsig told us via telephone what got him into such a business, "My father was a wrestling promoter, so wrestling has been embedded inside of me from the womb. I started out working on the indy scene under several different Alias'. My first gig was for Taj Escobar's mVw promotion under the name Shane Gamble. It was there that I realized that professional wrestling was not just something I wanted to do, but something I had to do. It was my purpose for being here. in 2004, I caught a big break after being signed to PRIME, and here I am today, the Universal Champion. It's been a long journey, but I'd do it all again." A man with Olsig’s talent and accolades probably could have found success anywhere, but he ended up choosing PRIME. Olsig considers PRIME home, "Simply put. Once I came to PRIME, I found the place where I belonged and I haven't been anywhere else since. My fondest memories are found in this place, and I wouldn't feel right performing on any other stage. I've seen this company through its darkest and its brightest days. My memories of this place are limitless. I love this place. It's an honor to not only hold a spot in the Hall of Fame, but also stand tall as the Universal Champion of such an illustrious organization. PRIME is, and forever will be, the greatest promotion on this planet which, in turn, makes me, as the Universal Champion, the greatest athlete on this planet. " When asked about his Colossus experience, and what Colossus meant to him, Olsig gave an answer we found commonplace about its greatness, "Colossus is the grandest stage of them all, everyone knows that. The last Colossus I participated in, I not only won the tag team championships with my good friend, Machaiveli St. Romani, but it was also the night that I, alongside Karina Wolfenden and Nova, was inducted into the Hall of Fame. It's like every round goes higher, because, now, I'm back on this grand stage, defending the Universal Championship in the main event. When I think about it, I become flabbergasted. If you would've told me back in 2004 that this would be happening, I would've laughed in your face. But it is happening, and that, to me, is awe-inspiring." And when Strife came up, Olsig did acknowledge him as legitimate, "Castor Strife is a worthy opponent, I won't lie. He's managed to achieve in only a few months what most guys, myself included, had to fight years obtain. This company hasn't seen such a lightening rise since the arrival of Tchu. But I've beaten Castor once just to secure this belt. History has a funny way of repeating itself. I'm a bit older now than I once was, but I honestly feel that I am on top of my game. Dual Halo, Universal Championship, defending the Universal Championship - I've found my stride. It took six years, but I've found it, and I just can't see some Johnny-come-lately crushing this momentum that I've built. I consider it a grave insult to try and pry this belt off of me - an insult that I don't take lightly. There will be a fight at Colossus, but only one man will emerge from that fight victorious. I rest at night KNOWING that I'll be that one." Vangelus Olsig Vs Castor V. Strife will take place at Colossus VII in Qualcomm Stadium.
