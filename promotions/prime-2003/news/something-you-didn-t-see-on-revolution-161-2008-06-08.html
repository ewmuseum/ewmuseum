Something You Didn't See On ReVolution 161

8 June 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The camera crew was set to film a backstage corridor immediately after Captain Justice and Mr. Silver were annoyed with The Princes of New England. In almost the last second, the feed was switched to another area of the backstage to catch Colby Korver stumbling into walls and locker rooms that he shouldn't be in. The reason the feed changed was because Killean Sirrajin, victorious at Ultra Violence over the newcomer Logic, arrived in the building. Immediately hearing of his arrival, C.P. Cantrell dispatched whatever security he could find to his location. The reason? Earlier in the day, the Board of Directors at PRIME Headquarters in Chicago finally voted to pass a new, yet still terribly out of date policy about drug use in PRIME. Previously, no action was taken on the Supreme Machine regarding his first failed drug test around Culture Shock. But Killean has since failed a second test and the board needed to do something. Killean Sirrajin was suspended indefinitely earlier in the day for an unknown amount of time. He was advised of this by Cantrell and his makeshift team of security only a couple minutes after entering Joe Louis Arena. "Killean Sirrajin has failed two drug tests in a row. PRIME never had policies in place previously because no one has ever failed a steroid/performance enhancer test before. Therefore I brought the board together and as quick as possible, a new policy was drafted. In the near future, more strict guidelines along with stricter penalties will be implemented, but the legal documents to introduce them is what's taking the time overall." Blaine Blair said in a conference call earlier today. Further to the news about the suspension, PRIME Brass had confirmed that Killean Sirrajin was fined in excess of $100,000 total for the two tests. Approximately $25,000 was the cost of the first failed test and once the results of the second test were known, the Canadian Wrestler was forced to pay a little over $75,000 in penalties. More on this situation will be released as more information becomes known.
