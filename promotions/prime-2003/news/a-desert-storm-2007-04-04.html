A Desert Storm?

4 April 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some well-placed barbs by Troy to potential GCW signee "The Violence" Vince Jones saw responses not only from Jones himself but from Caldera, Troy's tag team partner Danny Ferguson, GCW talent Vivica J. Valentine, PRIME rehire Brandon Youngblood, other stars from each company and from other PTC member feds. With ReVolution coming from Reno, Nevada this week, and GCW's supercard High Stakes taking place in Las Vegas early next week, rumors have already started to circulate that Troy may make an appearance there before her match with Caldera, which is certain to take place on the WorldWide following the GCW pay-per-view. "I told (MBE World Champion) Doc Silver that I'd have some time to do a charity Hold 'Em tournament with him in Vegas before heading to Salt Lake City," Troy commented during a brief phone call from her sister and brother-in-law's home in Houston, Texas. "I figure after that's over, I can take a piss in Shaman's casino then hop on a plane to Utah for ReV. "They (GCW) probably won't like that when they hear it but, y'know, the only person I like to coddle is my newborn niece. It took Caldera long enough to give me an answer about the match, but he'll have his chance to do something about it. He'll fail miserably on his own programming, but no one will be able to say that he didn't give it the ol' college try." When asked for comment regarding the ever-growing hostile situation, new PRIME CEO Blaine Blair said the following: "Chet agreed to let her make the challenge when he was still in control of the company. On behalf of Tyler Nelson and the Board, we also give Lindsay Troy our full support in this endeavor. She's fully capable of handling herself on a GCW show and we're confident that she won't disappoint." News from GCW headquarters is reporting that Caldera is waiting for an invitation acceptance from Vince Jones to attend High Stakes as well. "That'll be good," the Queen said. "I haven't yet exhausted all possible adjectives for 'moron' when it comes to him." Folks, this is going to be a hot one in the desert. We'll keep you posted.
