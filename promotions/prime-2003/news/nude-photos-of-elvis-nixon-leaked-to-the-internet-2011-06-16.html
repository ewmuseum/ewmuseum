Nude Photos of Elvis Nixon Leaked to the Internet?

16 June 2011
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leaked celebrity photos have been the rage these days, ranging from Vanessa Hudgens all the way up to the recent ones of Tito Ortiz. It seems PRIME has decided to join that unfortunate realm of being judged by the public as referee Elvis Nixon reportedly had his Facebook hacked. The 36-year-old referee, making his gimmick portraying his two favorite idols of the 70s, was allegedly photographed by an ex-girlfriend. The set of 8 photos has been sent over the Internet and while the referee has refused to comment, rumors at 246 were he did not want to come out and officiate his matches because he was too irate with the photos. This one is likely to have more validity than the prior two rumors posted today. When I tried to speak with Nixon, he was on his cellular phone screaming (in a regular voice, he sounds very much like Brad Pitt... not the actor, he made a distinct remark to ensure that) and didn't appear happy, so I didn't press further. More on this as it... develops. BAH... OK, I don't get paid enough to write stories like this covered with humor.
