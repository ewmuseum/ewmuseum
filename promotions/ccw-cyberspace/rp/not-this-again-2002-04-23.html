Not This Again

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short roleplay in which Ricky Coyote addresses Gabriel Nyte from his apartment.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Coyote Entitled: Not this again Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 23:19:33 Location: This is a: Match RP **Scene opens in a ver militant place. The room looks like a bomb had hit it and in the background we hear air to ground missles whistling. As the camera pans around we notice that the room is actually Ricky's apartment...no messier than it usually is. And he just so happens to be watching a wat movie. As Ricky comes into the picture we find him relaxed in his reliner.** Ricky: Yeah, Gab, just wanted to let you know there buddy that your probably taking this hypocrit thing a little to seriously. Either that or you just like playing war too much. I've seen your kind before, long before you ever dreamt of the CCW. And I want you to know that your not going to beat me because you have a better gimmcik, because you don't. Your not going to beat me because I'm scared of you, because I'm not. And you not going to beat me because your a better wrestler than me, because your not. You can say all you want about me and my houseshow championship because what you don't understand, what you don't seem to get is that the difference between you and me is that people respect me. I don't know if you know too much about CCW history, but you probably don't. But have you ever heard of the Burners? the Blades? Tim Inman? Kaboink and A-pac? El Mierda? Have you? Those were the guys I competed with. Those are the guys I proved myself to. Have you proven yourself Gabe? No, you haven't. And because of that, I find you inadequate. To me, you represent everything that sucks about the CCW. And you know what Nyte? This is MY home your just a visitor passing through on you way to nowhere. Keep talking trash and fighting the 'good' fight. Meanwhile, I'll be kicking your ass. **Fade to Black* View Biography
