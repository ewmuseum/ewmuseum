The Beginning, Welcome To My World Damien Nhyte CCW Promo #86 Date: March 14th, 2003 Location: In The Darkness ?Here I Am? ?The Forgotten One? ?A Man? ?That Can Not Be Contained? ?A Man? ?That CCW Can Not Contain? ?And A Man? ?That The CCW Will Never Forget? He is seen with his hands running through his jet black hair. His fingers, they clinch every strand of hair?for the search is over! He wears a pair of black jeans and a black dress shirt with the sleeves cut off. His pale white skin, it will never be forgotten. But is that his goal? Not at all, he doesn?t want to be remembered for his physical appearance. He wants to be remembered for what he has done in the CCW. He wants to be remembered for every drip of blood dropped on the ring mat from his opponents. He wants to be remembered for every broken bone that lies inside the skin of each person that opposes him. And he wants to be remembered for being a legend in the CCW. And to become a legend, he has to start somewhere. And it begins with The Disciples Of Death. What does the future hold for Damien Nhyte and Jason Dynasty? The answer is simple my friends?whatever the hell they want their future to become. ?For years I have been awaiting my return. I have been locked up in this hell for years?keeping my talents hidden. But all of that is about to change, for Dynasty has helped me bring my talents back to the ring. And I am here in the CCW for one reason and one reason only. And that reason is to eliminate the weak and to become something in the CCW. I want to be the man that every person in the world knows. I want to be the man who people boo for. And I want to be the man who is the cause for parents holding their hands over their child?s ears and attempt to block out what I have to say. But like I said, I have been released and now, now I am back. And no hands will block out my message and no Superstar will stop my wrath. For a new day has arrived, it is early?but as it progresses, the world will fall to their knees and The Disciples Of Death will show the world why we are the best tag team in the business today.? He looks up towards the camera, his eyes are filled with fury and anger that the CCW has never seen before. His eyes, no tint of blue, green, or brown?his eyes are filled with a silvered color. He looks at the camera and continues to speak? ?I anxiously await the day I lace up my boots and step between the ropes and wrestle once again. But for now, I will watch Jason defeat Gabrielle Nyte, which will not be a difficult task. And at Lockdown, the world will witness the power and strength that The Disciples of Death provide to the CCW. The world will be a better place since we are in the CC??
