Inferno Extinguised...

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short interview in which Carnage announces he has teamed with Guardian against Danny Bryson.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Carnage Entitled: Inferno Extinguised... Written in Sunday, May 12, 2002, 22:59:40 Location: Unknown This is a: Interview **The scene opens in a dark room. A figure lurks in the shadows. He steps into the light... It's Carnage. He has an evil grin spread across his face, like he's in a trance. Carnage speaks...** 'Most people go through life with a partner... A trusted friend, a sibling, a spouse... All my life I have been alone. Ditched by my parents, outcasted by society; you've heard the story. But now I have a partner. Someone who is on common ground with me... Someone that wishes to extinguish 'the Inferno' along side me. Finally I have reached a point where I am united for something, or rather, against someone...' **Carnage's grin turns sour. Now a deep frown spreads across his face...** 'United against Danny Bryson... This time I'm going to do what I attempted at Dead or Alive. This time I'm going to do what I couldn't... And that was to defeat Danny Bryson. I brought out the inferno in him and he claims to have brought out the inferno in me. Well my mission is to extinguish that inferno inside of him. Alongside Guardian, we will dismantle Danny Bryson, united by a common hatred. A common enemy...' **Carnage regains the evil grin he had before. Carnage steps out of the light back into the shadows. The scene fades to black...** View Biography
