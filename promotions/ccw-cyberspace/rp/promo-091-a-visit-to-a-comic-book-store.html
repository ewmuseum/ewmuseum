A visit to a comic book store

17 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A visit to a comic book store Melody Widmark CCW Promo #91 Date: 03/17/03 Location: Washingon D.C. Washington D.C. is a very busy place. This is where the world turns... Decisions are made that affect the lives of millions of people everyday. But as Melody Widmark walks the streets, heading for her intended target, she realizes that her destination is in stark contrast to the large and important buildings of legislative power. A small comic book shop. And it doesn't take her very long to find it... A bell over the door jingles as she pushes her way inside. Several geek fanboys ogle her as she steps inside, obviously the most beautiful and attractive women they've ever been close enough to touch. They stand rooted to the spot, eyes glued on her body. 'Dream away, boys,' Melody smirks to herself before making her way over to the counter. A man in sloppy, stain-ridden clothes is sitting behind the glass partition which shows a variety of older, and more expensive comics. The prices are extravagant, and Melody thinks that only fools would pay for something so worthless. The man behind the counter obviously likes his job. His fingers flip through the pages of a comic book, engrossed in it's 'captivating' storyline. Melody notes the title of the book, 'The Fantastic Four', and smirks again. Melody: Excuse me... The man reading the comic book doesn't respond at first, but soon glances up as he finishes his page. Man: May I help... **trails off** You? **recognition** Melody? Melody Widmark? Melody: **nods and smiles** Hi, Uncle Dan. Uncle Dan takes in the sight of his niece standing before him and all thoughts of comic books are lost. He drops his copy of the Fantastic Four behind the counter and leans forward to get a better glimpse of her long legs. Uncle Dan: **lecherous grin** Look... I know we're related and all, but I wouldn't mind forgetting all about that for a little while, if you know what I mean? Melody: **rolling her eyes and scoffing** Dan, you and I need to talk. I have a business proposition for you. Uncle Dan: Alright... Deal. I'll close the shop, and we can do it on the front counter here. Melody: It's not sex that I'm after. And if I was, I wouldn't be here. I would like your help. Dan: Okay, what is this all about? Melody: I want some back-up, that's all. It will require a lot of traveling, but all the expenses will be paid and it'll give you a chance to get out of this loser-hole and maybe start doing something real with your life. Dan: You lost me. What kind of job is this? Melody: I work for Cyberspace Championship Wrestling now... My first real job. I need someone watching my back while I'm there. Every single one of those girls, along with that psychotic Commissioner, are vicious! Dan: So you want me to stop you from getting hurt by putting my hands on any young lady that tries to get the better of you? Melody nods. Dan: Just one question first... If I, uh, 'accidently' grope one of them during a match, could they sue me for sexual harassment or misconduct? Melody: No. Everything that happens during an official match is legal. No one can get in trouble for anything that occurs. We all signed contracts to that effect. And trust me... I read over mine VERY carefully before I signed anything. Dan: Alrighty then! Deal! Dan reaches out and pumps Melody's hand up and down in an excited handshake. Melody extracts her hand from her uncle's sweaty palm and turns to exit. Melody: I'll meet you here at nine o'clock tonight when you close. There are some things we need to go over and arrangements to make. Dan: Okay, but... Wait! Why did you ask me of all people? Melody pauses in the doorway, eyes flicking momentarily back to the two geek fanboys who are still staring at her. Melody: Because, Uncle, you are one of the most perverted and nastiest men I know. Who better to unleash against my enemies? Melody smiles radiantly, and with the tinkling of the overhead bell, disappears back out into the world. fade out... View Melody Widmark 's Biography Back
