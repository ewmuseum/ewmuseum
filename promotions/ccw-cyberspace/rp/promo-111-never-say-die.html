Never Say Die

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Never Say Die 'The Angel of Death' Enigma CCW Promo #111 Date: Location: (We go back to LDT... Just moments after one of the most brutal Hardcore matches in recent history... Backstage we see EMT's putting Yama Tatsu into the back of an ambulance... Yama is bleeding on numerous spots on his body, and his arm is just dangleing off his shoulder... He isn't even conscious he just lays in the stretcher motionless... We see Enigma looking on from a distance... He is still all bloodied and battered, his white cast on his right arm full of blood stains... His ribs taped up...) Enigma: Its a shame... Tonight a man's career was ended... Alot of people thought that if I showed up to this match tonight, I would be the one being carried out on a stretcher... Well that wasnt the case tonight... It really goes to show you that doctors dont know jack shit... I mean they said that it would be months before I could even step into a ring again... What the fuck do they know? Not only did I step into that ring, I owned that ring... I walked into that match against all odds and I walked out on top... (The ambulance quickly takes off and rushes Yama Tatsu off to the nearest hospital... Enigma just watches on... He seems to be deep in thought...) Enigma: You know, I didnt come into this match with the intent to end a man's career... My only goal was to walk out of this match with a victory... But in my world of Hardcore Hell, things can get a little out of hand at times... It's expected... A man will do whatever he has to do to survive... Last week, Blayze attempted to end my career, but he failed... He didnt get the job done... There's an old saying... What goes around comes around... And Blayze, sooner or later things are gonna come around... In due time my old friend... (A young looking man with short brown hair and a small build, around 20 years old wearing a CCW staff t-shirt walks up to Enigma... The man has a name tag on his shirt that reads Mike... Enigma looks at Mike with fire in his eyes... Mike seems quite intimidated...) Mike: Ummmm.... Enigma... Sir... Enigma: You got a problem kid? Mike: No sir.... Its just president Sylkk has sent me looking for you... Enigma: What the hell does he want? Mike: He would.... Like to see you in his office... Enigma: Alright thanks for the message kid... (Enigma extends his hand in friendship but before Enigma even moves the kid runs off screaming like a little school girl) Enigma: Was it something I said? *shrugs* Oh well... (Enigma makes his way down the hall... Enigma walks right down the long hallway down to the very last door... On the door it reads 'President Sylkk'... Enigma opens the door and walks into the room... And there we see Sylkk behind his desk on his cell phone talking) Sylkk: I dont care what you gotta do, just get it done... (Sylkk looks up and he sees Enigma... Enigma sits down and he puts his feet up on Sylkk's desk... Enigma then lights up a cigarette...) Sylkk: Let me call you right back... (Sylkk hangs up his cell phone and he places it on his desk... He looks over at Enigma and he seems quite pissed... Enigma just sits there, not saying a word just smoking his cigarette... Than finally, the silence is broken) Enigma: I hear you wanted to see me? Sylkk: Who the hell do you think you are??? You come into my office, you dont even knock... Then you put you're feet up on my desk and light up a cigarette... This is a smoke-free building... I should fire you're ass right now... Enigma: So what are you waiting for? Fire me... Sylkk: Dont tempt me Enigma... You dont realize who you're dealing with? I can make you're life a living hell... I could squash you like a bug... You just dont realize that all you are is just a little pawn in my game... And everyone in CCW either playes this game by my rules, or they dont play at all... Is that understood? Enigma: Yeah whatever, is there a reason you called me in here? Or did you just call me in hear to make me listen to you're ever so annoying babbling... Sylkk: The reason I called you in here was to discuss the Revoultion PPV... Enigma: Yeah what about it? Sylkk: I've booked you in a Hardcore Title Match against Jack 'The Ripper' Manson... I just wanna know if you're physically able to compete... Enigma: Did you not see my match earliar? Am I able to compete you ask? I'm more than able to compete...And I'm gonna prove it to the whole world... I'm gonna walk out of Revolution a 4 time CCW Hardcore Chapion... Sylkk: Okay but I need you to sign something for me... (slides a piece of paper and a pen over to Enigma) Enigma: Whats this? Sylkk: If you get hurt during you're match due to you're injuries, CCW is not responsible for anything that happens... Enigma: So what happens if I dont sign? Sylkk: If you dont sign, well I guess that means Jack Manson wins by forfiet... (Enigma looks at the piece of paper for a second... But with little hesitation he signs it... Sylkk smiles... Enigma slides the piece of paper back over to Sylkk) Enigma: Are we done? Sylkk: Yeah, get the hell out of my office... (Enigma walks out of the office with a smirk on his face... Sylkk just sits at his desk and all of a sudden he notices his office is beginning to fill with smoke...Sylkk looks over and he notices his garbage can has caught on fire...) Sylkk: Oh shit.... (Sylkk runs over and grabs his fire extinguisher from the wall and he puts out the fire... Sylkk seems confused) Sylkk: How the hell did that fire start??? (Sylkk looks closely at the garbage can... He notices something... Sylkk reaches into the garbage can and he pulls out a cigarette butt... Sylkk's face turns red as his eyes fill with rage... And now the scene switches to just down the hallway from Sylkk's office... We see Enigma leaning against the wall, still with that smirk on face... He then lights up another cigarette) Enigma: Revolution... The first PPV since CCW has re-opened... My first in quite sometime... In fact, my last PPV was Dead Or Alive where I captured the CCW World Heavyweight Title... That was about a year ago... But now things have changed... This is the new era of CCW... With a new era of wrestlers... And this Sunday I will face Jack 'The Ripper' Manson, one of CCW's up and coming stars... Well Jack, there isnt much that I know about you... All I know is that you like hanging out with one eyed freaks... But none the less, I wont take you lightly... (pause)... Enigma: There is just one thing I have to ask you though? Whats with you're name? Where did you come up with it? 'The Ripper'? Like honestly I think you can do a little better than that... I mean, just because you name yourself after a serial killer, that doesent make you scary or intimidating if thats what you might think... You're still a nobody in my eyes... I remember Jack 'The Ripper' was famous for raping prostitutes and removing there sexual organs... He was one sick indvidiual... But hey, what you do on you're own time is you're persoanl business... (Enigma inhales one final time off his cigarette than he flicks it away) Enigma: Jack, all that matters to me is that I have to beat you to become Hardcore Champion once again... That title means alot to me, it signifys everything I stand for... And I'll go through you and anybody else thats willing to try to keep that title away from me... I will become Hardcore champion once more, because I am with out a doubt the most Hardcore son of a bitch this industry has ever seen... Doctors say I'm not fit to wrestle, well so what... I beat the hell out of Logan and Tatsu and I'm gonna do the same to you Jack... Nothing is going to stop me... Not you Jack, not nobody... I will become Hardcore Champion once more... (Fade to black) View 'The Angel of Death' Enigma 's Biography Back
