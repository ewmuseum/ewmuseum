In the Looking Glass...

What this is A first-person piece in which Cianan, in an airplane restroom, converses with an imagined child version of himself.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: In The Looking Glass... Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 03:00:32 Location: Plane... This is a: Match RP Alone, in the bathroom of an airplane. Traveling, hard as always, but nothing to bitch about, except the long plane flights between contract negotiations with PURE. Countless annoying children, who talk about Yu-Gi-Oh, Poke'mon.. and for god's sake.. Dijimon.. how many fucking types of monster's are there? Ugh, it was disgusting.. The flight was long, the children were restless, crying babies.. I needed to get away, I needed some space, or I would snap. With everything happening in air ports, and on planes lately, snapping would NOT be a good thing. I wouldn't make it to LDT, I wouldn't win CotR. Damsel, please.. I pour the water into my hand, and splash it against my face, trying to calm myself, trying to just relax. They wrere just children, they were just children.... 'You were a child once..' I blink, and look around, I was locked, alone in that small little compartment, that joke of a restroom. Obviously, no one's there, and I'm crazy. This has been an on going thing in my life, I really do understand. One of the side effects of.. 'It.' Or, so I hear. I wasn't patient enough to look at results of the test subject, before indulging myself. ---Excuse me, sir. But, you were a child once weren't you? You had dreams? You weren't always like this...--- I glance up, looking into the mirror, and seeing a wide eyed child.. Asian in looks, eyes of blue. Me. Innocent, before my family started to turn on me. Before the Italians beat me up, before my father failed me, and my mother forced me into the working male's place. 'I was a child.. for a very short time.. you'll learn.. things go wrong, horribly so..' ---But.. you're big, you're strong.. you can do anything!! I'm betting you're the best hero in the world! Aren't you? Do you hold the world title? Are you the strongest?! Do you have an awesome Valet?-- I look at the kid, as if it was really me, at that age looking at me now. It was strange, did I really look at the world with such a sense of innosence? Was I really that naive at one point? His eyes were peircing as he looked at me, his face was filled with hope and excitement. God, I hated it how he looked at me.. as silly as it seemed, I didn't want to let him down with my answer. I guess, I was silent for a while.. because he guessed it.. --Oh.. are.. you a bad guy then?-- 'I guess, you could say that...' --Are you strong?-- 'Yeah, kid.. I'm strong.' The kid errr.. me, looked up at me, with a look of confusion. He tried to figure out, how he became me. Funny, I was trying to find out how I came from him. It was something more then strange.. it was fuckin' spooky. What warped and deranged me to become the creature I was. --Do.. you hurt girls? -- 'Sometimes, you have to.' I saw the great disapointment on his face, and I had to wince. He was me.. I wondered how my own child, once he was born would see me. I winced again. --Guys aren't supposed to hit girls..-- 'Yeah, and Chivalry is dead.. survival is the main thing... survival.. and gold.' It made me think.. back to my begining days of CCW. My second match, I actually teamed with Damsel against Kevin Newcastle and Koriann. I offered to protect Damsel, and Akiko.. from anything. Funny. Now I have to rip the little girl apart. I bashed Valorie Venom with a 'King of Clubs.' and beat Akiko for the lightweight title. I have never thought of women as weaker.. they want to be treated like equals.. I won't hold back. It's been my policy all along. Angelica Lynn, Koriann, Damsel.. they all know, I'll be just as rough on them, as if they were any man. --You.. You're not very nice.. huh..?-- It was then.. I realized, I didn't really like myself very much. Not at all... View Biography
