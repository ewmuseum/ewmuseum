Jack Panzer - I can't snap, I can't snap...

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Jack Panzer’s manager Nicky White enlists her brother to misdirect the Germans pursuing Jack ahead of the Group Therapy tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: I can't snap, I can't snap... Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 21:19:17 Location: New York City This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens at New York City, where CCW's Group Therapy is happening. The scene opens to a shot of the La Guardia airport, inside the building, in front of the National Arrivals Gate. The camera shows several kinds of people walking out of the airport, like a young Texan, a team of female college basketball, a few businessmen and hot young actresses, trying their luck on the off-Broadway scene. At the end of the line, we can see Nicky White, Jack Panzer's manager, arriving at the airport. She's wearing a hot white cotton top and tight black cotton pants. She carries a blue Nike gym bag on her shoulder. Nicky's face looks concerned, because of the course of action the germans decided to take lately against Jack, pursuing him more avidly. Nicky walks to the outside, where she immediately hops on a cab. The driver takes off in the beautiful spring morning. The scene changes to inside the cab. The driver asks:** Driver: Where to? Nicky: 1983 5th avenue, please. Driver: Very well... **The driver speeds, while Nicky immediately grabs a cell phone on her gym bag. She dials a number on it and the phone rings for a few times. A male voice answers it on the other side:** Voice: Hello... Nicky: Hey, Earl, is that you? Voice: Who's this? Nicky: It's Nicole, you dufus... Voice: Nicky? Man, this has been a long time. What do you want? Nicky: I need a favor. I'm here in NY, and I have this friend, and we need to get some information on these german guys who are trying to get him... Voice: Ok, I think I can help you. Bring him and we'll see what I can do for you... Nicky: Great, Earl. Thanks... Voice: No problem. **Nicky hungs up the phone, while the cab drives around NYC. Eventually, the cab driver stops on 5th avenue, in front of a nice hotel. Nicky hops out of the cab and walks straight inside the hotel. The hotel is not very expensive but pretty clean. Nicky walks in the hotel and goes straight to the balcony. The man in charge behind the counter, a kind of old guy, on his late 50s, bald hair, fat and rude. Nicky walks straight to him and asks:** Nicky: Hi... Is Jack Panzer already in? Clerk: Excuse me, but we can't give information on other clients... Nicky: Ok, then maybe my 20 friends over here might persuade you... **Nicky pushes a 20-dollar bill on the balcony, and the clerk grabs it. He takes a look at the terminal and says:** Clerk: Oh yeah, Mr. Panzer. Room 1210. Nicky: Cool, thanks... **Nicky begins to walk away...** Clerk: Funny guy... **Nicky turns around, shocked.** Nicky: Funny??? Why is that?? Clerk: It's just that he orders the most funny stuff... Nicky: What? What did he order??? Clerk: I don't know, the computer screen is blurred... **The clerk waves his fingers in a mencion for more money. Nicky smiles:** Nicky: Nah, I can see that for myself. But thanks for the help anyway. **Nicky walks away, leaving the clerk completely stoned on the balcony, as Nicky gently waves her butt side to side, teasing the man. Nicky hops in an elevator and presses the 12th floor button. The scene changes to the door of room 1210. Nicky knocks on the door, but nobody answers. Nicky knocks on it again, a little harder, but once again, nobody answers. This time, she slams on the door, but once again, to no avail. She finally touches the doorknob and realizes it's open. Nicky slowly opens it, as if something dangerous or sinister was about to happen. After a few steps, she looks in the room and releases a high tone scream. There, lying on the bed, or better yet, sitting, we can see Jack Panzer. But not the usual Panzer we're used to know. We're granted the view of a 6'8' tall, military guy, short temper... Wearing a funny colorful costume. The costume resembles those of esoterics. Seven aromatic incenses circles him, and the slow smoke surrounds the air. The curtain is closed, and only a few rays of sun break through the lateral of the window. Jack is wearing a blue turbant and he has a headset on his ears and a walkman on his hands. Nicky yells at Jack once again and THIS time, he looks at her, slowly opening his eyes:** Jack (slow, funny, calm voice): Hi, Nicky... Nicky (irritated): Hi Nicky? HI NICKY? Is that all you have to tell me??? Jack: What? What's going on? Nicky: WHAT's GOING ON? LOOK AT YOURSELF, YOU LOOK LIKE A BOZO, A CLOWN!!! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON? Jack: Ahn, I talked to this psylogogist in the lobby today, about my rage and all, and he told me this could be the solution... Nicky: Psychologist? Come on, Jack, the guy was a salesman. You didn't pay for all this stuff, did you, Jack? Jack: Yeah... Nicky: How much? Jack: Not much... A couple grand... Nicky: TWO THOUSAND DOLLARS??? ARE YOU INSANE??? **Nicky really flips out. Jack puts the walkman and the headset on Nicky's hand and calmly says:** Jack: Come on, Nicky. You could use this. Nicky: I'M NOT LISTENING TO THIS BRAIN WASHING CRAP!!! **Nicky throws the walkman on the wall, crashing it into pieces. Nicky picks up a chair and sits next to Jack, who's finally beginning to come back to his former self:** Nicky: Ok, I can see you have a very good reason for doing this. So, tell me. What's it? Jack (back to normal, taking off the turbant and the clothes): It's Group Therapy, Nicky... It's on the corner... Nicky: I know that, and you should be practicing for that, running, sparring, and not listening to some phony Kenny G. Jack: HEY, I listen to anything, but Kenny G is torture. That guy on the tape was NOT Kenny G. Nicky: Oh, sorry. What's the difference? The point is, what does GT has to do with all this? Jack: *sigh* Ok, I'll tell you. **Jack gets up, walking to a small fridge on the corner of the room. He grabs two Buds, tossing one to Nicky and opening the other. Jack drinks it, doesn't like it but continues:** Jack: Damn american beer... Anyway, I was afraid of GT... Nicky: And why is that? You can't be afraid of SuMa... Jack: HELL no, that's not it. SuMa is great, Chang is damn strong, but our team is a unity, I have called Ricky and Damsel, and they promised me they're working hard, and we'll have a battle plan for the tournament. Nicky: Then what's it? Jack: Nicky, my fear is that my rage attacks might get in my way to the win. Nicky: But they GIVE you the win. Jack: Yeah, but lately, I have realized that, in a singles match, losing my mind doesn't get me into trouble, but we're talking about a tag-team match. 5-men tag-team matches. Nicky: So??? Jack: Don't you get it, Nicky? If I lose it, and I KNOW I'll lose it, I can get easily eliminated with a surprise small package by a fast entrant in the match... Nicky: Ah, I see, because you won't have control and won't see him coming... Jack: Exactly. If I lose it, I'll become vulnerable, more than ever. I have a loss in my resume because of a mishap like that. I lost it and because of that, I'm not the CCW tag-team champion with Lance Lethal now... Nicky: But Lance didn't want to fight hard for CCW, anyway... He left days after that loss. Jack: I know, but tag-team matches are all about covering your ass and your partner's ass. If I failed to protect him, because of my altered state of mind, then it's my fault. Those 4 guys, John, Ricky, Ebola, Damsel, I may not agree with some of them, but I respect them all. They're in my team, and I won't fail on them... I WON'T!!! I WON'T!!! NOT AGAIN!!! NOT AGAIN!!! **Jack puts his hands on his blind eye and begins to cry, remembering his unit that died because of him on Zaire, years ago.** Nicky: Jack, JACK! Look at me, LOOK AT ME!!! This is NOT a war. Jack: Of course it is... Nicky: NO, it's not. This is different. Yes, you need to lead your squad, YES, you need to take them to the top, but listen to me. If you're not able to do that, that's THEIR fault. Remember: It's a series of one-on-one matches. Elimination style. DQs CAN and WILL eliminate someone from the tournament, so nobody will be stupid to attack and risk getting their asses kicked from the tournament. Jack: I know... But there's my side too. I need to stay focused, I need to get to the top. Nicky: And why is that? It can't be your 'dream', that's a wrestler cliche. Jack: You're right. It's not a matter of being a champion or holding a title. It's a matter of survival. If I win a CCW Title, or even better, if I win the CCW World Heavyweight Title, I'll be able to stay on the spotlight, and those german jackasses won't be able to touch a finger on me without being dragged to jail. Nicky: Ah, now I understand. That's why you're tried so hard to control yourself. Jack: That and the fact that I can't stand seeing my unity fall again. I simply... can't. I must win this tournament, I MUST! Nicky: What if you won another title? Jack: What? What do you mean? Nicky: I don't know, you could challenge one of the champions for an impromptu match or something. Jack: Nah, that won't work. The PPV is already jam-packed, they have so many people set to wrestle in it that they can't have the luxury of putting an impromptu match in it. They'll sack my ass from live TV as soon as they could. Nicky: Hmmm... True... What if... Jack: What if what? Nicky: What if we could get a step ahead of them? Jack: What do you mean? Nicky: Come with me. Jack: Ok... **Nicky and Jack walk out of the room, and the scene fades to black. When it reopens, we're granted a shot of a nice-looking old building on Soho. Jack, still clueless, looks at Nicky, as she hops out of the cab, and decides to follow her, even though surprises are not something Jack likes. Jack and Nicky walk out of the crowd into the building, where she presses a small button on the intercom system to apartment 205:** Voice: Hello? Nicky: It's me, Nicole. Voice: Cool, come on up!!! **The door opens in front of them, and Nicky walks in the rich-looking building. Jack, still undecided, is dragged inside by a playful Nicky. The scene chages to inside an apartment. We can see a huge room, full with computers, a movie theater and lots of boxes of chinese food thrown around. Sitting in the computer, tapping like crazy in a computer keyboard. The guy is young, around the late teens, slim, filled with disgusting zits on his face. The doorbell rings and the guy gets up, walking to the door. He opens it up, looks at Nicky and jumps on her neck. They both hug each other dearly, while the 6'8' tall german war machine looks, not understanding a thing. Finally, after seeing the pain on Jack's face, Nicky says:** Nicky: Ah, sorry, Jack. This is Earl White, my little brother. Jack: Ah, your younger brother? Cool, hey, dude. Earl: Hey. So, Nicky, what do you want me to do? Nicky: Simple: I want you to clone the CCW.com website for me. Earl: Hmmm... That can be done. What do you have in mind? Nicky: Simple. Let's seat and I'll fill you into things. Earl: Ok... **Jack and Nicky walk into the room and they sit down in a very old but clean black couch on the corner. Earl sits on his computer chair and says:** Earl: Ok, what's the thing? Nicky: Jack, here, is a wrestler... Earl: I know, CCW... Great, dude... Jack: Thanks... Earl: So, ready for Group Therapy? Jack: Kind of... eeerr... pay attention to her... Earl: Sorry, cool. Go on, Nicky. Nicky: Well, he's a former german army commando who got framed in Zaire and arrested for 6 years... Earl: I know his story, I saw it on CCWTV. Go on... Nicky: Well, we suspect the germans are using some bridge to know where Ace will always be. We think they're using an inside person on CCW, maybe an employee or even a wrestler. Earl: I see... Nicky: But also, we think that CCW.com is the way they can know where Jack is going. Earl: I see... Go on... Nicky: So, I thought we could clone the CCW.com site, so they could be directed to a fake venue, drawing them away from GT, so Jack could have a clean night. Earl: That's easy. Do you have their IP? Nicky: No, you're the brainiac. Earl: Ok, piece of cake. I'll scan the last visitors to CCW.com and find the german IPs that logged on CCW.com lately... Jack: Check the government ones... Earl: Cool.... **Earl walks to his computer and starts tapping on it like crazy, trying to find the information to clone the website. In the meanwhile, Nicky and Jack walk to the side:** Nicky: So, talked to the guys yet? Jack: I called Ricky and Damsel. Ebola is a mystery, I've never seen or heard of him, and the John Linsey guy, well... He was around last LDT, and I talked to him before I kicked Darren's ass. I don't know if he's showing up, though. He'll probably chicken out... And that means we'll be short-handed. Nicky: Damn... But That doesn't mean you're still out, right? Jack: Not at all. I mean, their team is good, but look at them: Chang is like me without a brain. He's strong, but is easily beatable. Hell, he got beaten last LDT. Ricky Coyote is jobber in person. He doesn't have what it takes to go much further in this tournament. Never had. Nicky: What about Damsel and Lacey Skye? Jack: Lacey is better than Damsel, and Damsel will probably be eliminated, but Lacey has too many enemies. That's probably her biggest drawback. I mean, Devyl wants her ass, and I heard there are other girls wanting her ass, so she probably won't last. Nicky: The mystery partner? Jack: I'm not worried about him, because quite frankly, I think I know who's the guy. He's good, no doubt about that, but the ring rust and the time away from the ring will probably be his worst enemy. Nicky: Right... And that also could be SuMa's doom... Jack: Not at all... Nicky: What? Jack: SuMa is back, and I know he's been fighting strong. Although he suffered a little last Thursday, he's won the match, and to do that, he showed he can still be a strong competitor... Nicky: So, does this mean... Jack: It means nothing. SuMa is great, he's a legend, it's true, but everybody he's faced until this day was nothing more than other wrestlers, some good, many good, many bad too. Now he's facing a well-oiled man, trained in combat, who's been in every single tough place of this earth. I have endured much more pain than anyone could ever endure. SuMa is strong, but so is the War Machine. I'll be more than glad to welcome him back to CCW and show him who's the real machine around here... Nicky: NICE, that's the spirit... Jack: Yeah, IF I don't snap... Nicky: Come on, Jack... I'll be there, checking you out. If you lose it, I'll snap you back. Jack: Great, thanks, Nicky. I knew I could count on you... **Jack and Nicky embrace, and Earl, on the computer, says:** Earl: Hey, you two, first, get a room, and second, I got it. Jack: WHAT? How? Earl: Simple... I hacked into CCW.com and got their log file. There are several fans in Germany, but only 2 government computers accessed it, so I created a small script that redirects them to a clone page, which is exactly the same as the real CCW page, except that the venue for GT is not Madison Square Garden. Jack: Nice... What venue did you put? Earl: Some random venue in Virginia... Jack: What's happening there? Earl: Dude, I don't know, but it's booked for the weekend, so at least it'll delay them a little. Maybe enough for you to have some piece of mind on the tournament.... Jack: Great, dude. Thanks. Earl: No problem. Now get off my sister or I'll kick your ass!!! Jack: OK, ok, sorry... **Jack smiles and walks with Nicky out of the room. The scene fades to black. It reopens in a small hotel room. Nicky is sleeping in one bed. Jack is standing at the hotel's window, looking at the clear New York night. Jack thinks about the future. Sunday. The tough night he'll have. A huge first match, against legends. And yet, he's not nervous. He knows he can rely on his partners, even though they're not around to strategize. Ricky Thunder, Jack Panzer, John Linsey, the new generation on CCW. Ebola, a tough veteran, Damsel, a great female and a great fighter. Jack looks at the night, thinking. He sees a ring, the arena completely full. And in the middle of the ring, a beaten, cut, bloodied Jack, with his left arm raised, as the Ring announcer calls 'and NEW CCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION...'. Jack smiles and keeps looking into the night, waiting for the big show, the big moment, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
