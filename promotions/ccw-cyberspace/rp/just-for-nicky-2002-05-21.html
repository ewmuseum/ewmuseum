Just For Nicky

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Jack Panzer visits Nicky White in hospital, turns down an offer of eye surgery and dedicates his next match to her.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: Just for Nicky Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 22:31:31 Location: County hospital, somewhere in America This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens at a hospital. The room is pretty simple, something normal, since hospital rooms aren't supposed to be like hotel rooms, anyway. We can see a hospital bed, and Nicky White, lying there, sleeping. The room is cold and white, and the lighting is poor, just a dim light coming from the outside, some blueish glow, breaking gently through the curtains. The camera closes on Nicky's face, as she candidly wakes up, her little blue eyes opening, after a nice night of sleep. She gets her face up, shaking her eyes, taking off the sleep and reaching for the bed remote control. She grabs it and raises the bed a notch. When she turns around, she opens her eyes wide, noticing a shadow in the dark, which she recognizes immediately, calling for his name, in awe:** Nicky: Jack? Jack??? **Jack Panzer, CCW's newest star, is lying on top of a small wooden coffee table, wearing the same outfit he wore when we saw him the last time on TV, back in the Bahamas. Jack, with his arms crossed over his face, slowly opens them, at the calls of his 'manager', before finally realizing Nicky has awoken.** Jack: Nicky, is that you? Nicky: Of course, you moron. Who did you think I am, Madonna? Jack: HEY, no need for being a smartass... Nicky: I'm sorry, but when I get shot, I tend on being a smartass... Jack: Fine, whatever... **Jack, laughing to himself, gets up and sits on Nicky's bed. He looks at her and says, stroking her hair gently with his fingers:** Jack: So, will you be ok? Nicky: I don't know, you tell me... Jack: Well, I'm not talking about the leg... I'm talking about this crazy life I'm in. I shouldn't have dragged you into this, this is my life, my business... **Nicky, with a lot of caring on her move, picks Jack's hand and holds it with both her hands:** Nicky: Jack, this was nothing. Believe me. My life was going nowhere, I was getting ready to go to community college to see if I could, maybe, get a job as a secretary somewhere, where I'd probably have a creepy boss who'd want to screw me every time I used a short skirt to work. Jack: But... Nicky: Let me finish. With you, things are simply different. They're more exciting. Now I have a purpose. When that bitch Devyl came into the ring and tried to attack you against Yama, I was there to hold her back. That's my job, to make you have your matches calmly. Jack: I know... But you know how worried I get when the spies come haunting us, don't you? Nicky: Yeah, I do... But you had them good. Now he'll have to stay back a while. Now you know what you're dealing with, this could be great. Jack: Yeah... Nicky: So, ready for Sunday? Jack: Actually, I'm more worried about this Thursday? Nicky: COTR, huh? Jack: Yeah... I'm not actually worried, I'm kinda sensing it won't be easy. Nicky: Spider or Ken? Jack: I got Ken this week. Nicky: I see... Don't worry, I got your back. Ouch... **Nicky accuses the pain on her right leg, the one that got shot, as she tries to show she's better by trying to sit up. Jack holds her down:** Jack: You're not good yet. I don't want you on any unncessary harm. Just let me deal with Ken. You stay there and RELAX. I'll go catch something to eat. And don't even DARE to appear on that arena, you heard me? Nicky: Ok... Jack: Cool. I'll see you later... Nicky: Ok... **Nicky lays down and goes back to sleep in the bed, and Jack covers her up, before walking out of the room. The aisle is calm, with only an old nurse walking from room to room to check on the patients. Jack walks past her and walks to the elevator. He presses the button for the top floor, and waits, as the elevator makes its way to the top of the building. He walks out of the elevator and looks at the city lights. The hospital is not the highest of buildings, but is pretty tall, so the view is beautiful. Jack walks around, finally walking towards an edge. He looks down, at the cars running home. Jack stands there, just staring at the emptiness, when a voice comes from behind him:** Voice: So, you're Panzer, right? **Jack turns back and sees a young doctor, on his late 20s, wearing a big white smock and a pair of old jeans beneath it. He has long blond hair and looks calm. Jack, suspiciously, asks:** Jack: Who are you? Doctor: My name is Tom Partridge, I work at this hospital. You're Jack Panzer, pro wrestler, right? Jack: Right... How do you know that, are you a fan of CCW? Doctor: Not really, I'm a fan of your case. Jack: My... case? Doctor: The eye. **The doctor makes a montion with his finger, drawing a scar in the midair, near the left side of his face. He says:** Doctor: You know, I have read about you on several medical papers... Jack: I'm sorry... Medical Papers? Doctor: Yeah. Have a sit, I'll explain things to you. Jack: Ok... **Jack sits in a small concrete bench near the doctor. He stands up, looking at Jack. He says:** Doctor: You see, Jack, your incident has been a theme for several works on optics and trauma surgery. Jack: Ah, cool... I don't like being a clown, for people's admiration, you know? Doctor: I know, but there's a reason for that. We believe we can restore your view. Jack: WHAT? **Jack opens his eyes wide open, just realizing the man is not lying. He says:** Jack: Look, please don't kid with me. The man cut my eye, it split in half... Doctor: We know, but there's still a possibility. Tell me: If you close your good eye and stay with your bad eye, can you see a light spot? Jack: Like, REALLY bright? Doctor: Yes. Jack: Yes, I can... But what's the point in that? Doctor: The point is, my friend, that your optical nerve wasn't damaged, what makes it possible for us to give you a prototype of a bionic eye that, connected to the nerve, will give you the ability to see again with your bad eye. Jack: So you're saying... Doctor: You could be the first person in the world to see again, after being blind. **Jack stops for a while, thinking, and then asks him:** Jack: Why me? Doctor: You're strong, healthy, and in perfect conditions for the operation. It'd take a few hours, but you'd be completely out cold by anesthesics, and you wouldn't have to pay for it, because the government wants to test it to see if we finally hit the jackpot. So, what do you say? **Jack looks down, shaking his head. His heart and soul crossed by another terrible, cruel choice. He gets up, starts walking around, realizing his chances. He could see again, but at what costs? And what if that was a trap to get him? It could be the germans' perfect chance, to get him out cold and then they'd take him back to Germany. Could he trust the mystery man? And there was more: Would he really appreciate to see again? After all, it's been so long he got used to it. His whole life, his fighting style, was adapted to his reality. By changing himself, would he get a good or a bad deal? Jack stops and thinks for a few seconds, before finally looking at the doctor and saying:** Jack: I can't, doc... **The doctor, visibly saddened, asks:** Doctor: Why, Jack? You have the chance of a life-time. Jack: Do I? I don't know. Seeing again, like I used to see before the ambush, seems like a tempting offer, but the truth is, I'm used to who I am. This is who I am. I'm no freak, I'm a guy who likes what he does, does what he can do to do it better and overcomes his own handicap to prove he can be one of the best at what he does. I know about your good intentions, but I'm who I am, and I won't change it now. If you came to me 6 years ago, I'd have got that in no time, but now, all my life is adapted to the fact that I'm blind of my left eye. Doctor: I understand. How exactly do you work, with your bad eye? Jack: It's not hard, you see, doc? For example, in the ring, all I need is my right eye. My left side can be weak, true, but my hearing is better, and it compensates for the lack of an eye. Doctor: So, let's say you face a guy like the one you're facing in your next match. How do you know where he is, if he's to your left side? Jack: I sense him. I know he's there, and I can hear him exactly where he is. Doctor: Cool. Well, here's my card *hands Jack a business card*. If you ever change your mind, call me, day and night, and we'll work you. Jack: Cool, doc. Thanks. **The doctor puts his hands on the pockets and walks to the elevator, getting in it and going down. Jack, with the wind blowing a little now, looks up, saying:** Jack: I know most people may think I'm dumb, but the fact that I'm blind actually helps me. I can know better when I'm gonna be attacked and prevent it. It wouldn't be such an advantage to get my vision back. Now, this Thursday, I get match #2 at the COTR, and I get to face former CCW Heavyweight Champion and CCW legend, Kensuke Suzahara. Now, I'm sensing that he ain't been with his heart on the CCW, but I can understand him. He's been torn between CCW and PURE, and I understand it. But I also understand that this is my big chance at getting the CCW Heavyweight Title, a dream that I never imagined I'd be going for, but something that's been more real, each day that passes. I'll tell you all, I want that title, just as everybody else, but most of all, I want to have fun, have great battles, and I know Ken will give me a great match. So, Ken, I'll be waiting you on the arena, ready and loaded, so we can go on. Get better, get up and let's kick some ass. BUT I'll tell you this: I'll dedicate this match for Nicky. I MUST win, and I'll do whatever I can, even throw myself in a suicide dive if necessary. So, Ken, I'll see you this Thursday. **As Jack says that, he walks again to the edge of the hospital, putting his foot on it, and looking at the horizon, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
