Insert Snappy Title Here Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #76 Date: The Day Before Tomorrow Location: What's it to ya? “We worked well as a team, that’s for sure, but who knows what Thursday will bring.” These are the words spoken by Michael Stevens, as he walks through a nearly deserted park. It’s evening, and the sun is setting, making the sky a brilliant, fiery red/orange. His steps make little noise as the leather soles of his beat up canvas sneakers hit the dirt path on which he walks. As he looks out at the wonder of nature around him, he feels nothing but a sort of sad emptiness, deep in the pit of his stomach. The feeling is of course loneliness, despair. He thinks back to the previous week, when he and Scorpion defeated the tandem of Sheena, and Minx. Their victory was an easy one, both men barely broke a sweat, and yet now they must square off against each other, in a battle for the Independent title. “Lamen…” Yes, this was again surely the work of Brad Lamen, forcing the partners to go against each other. Stevens raged against the circumstance, but in the end he could not bring himself to fully hate Lamen. For he’d lived up to his part of the bargain thus far, Stevens had advanced in the Tag Tournament, and now he was facing the current Independent title holder. Brad was making him a star, but at what cost? First facing off against the woman he loved, and now facing his tag partner Scorpion, Stevens would soon burn every last one of his bridges. And he was doing it all for Brad Lamen, but more than that he was doing it for himself, to quench his insatiable hunger for power, respect, glory, gold. These are the things that Stevens craved most of all, and these were the things that seemed to be coming to him, and all do at least in some small part, to Brad Lamen. And it was because of this simple fact that Stevens would not break off his agreement with Brad. That he would continue to suffer the indignities and humiliations heaped upon him. He would continue to face off against all those that he might possibly find as friends within CCW. He’d face them all, he’d do anything to help fill the void inside him. Even though, in some part of himself, he realized he was going about filling that hole in all the wrong ways. “Scorpion, Scorpion, I don’t know what to think. You’re not my friend, but you’re my partner, and now it seems you must be my enemy, I don’t know what to do.” That was the heart talking, in his mind, he knew what he must do. He’d have to throw everything he had the Scorpion, do whatever it would take to win the Independent title, for that’s what he desired, needed. Or at least that what he assumes he needs. For when a man has been told all his life that the only things important in life are glories. The only things that truly matter when all is said and done, are the trophies we receive for victories on the fields of competition , it’s these things that he must strive for. Friendships might come and go, but it would be the title that would begin to solidify his status as a force to be reckoned with in CCW. But still his heart knows the truth. He needs real people around him, those that care for and respect him. Men like Scorpion, equals, friends, women like Sheena, to love and nurture him. Those are the things the heart wants, the heart needs, but like so many others in Michael’s position, he disregards what his heart wants. And why shouldn’t he? After all, when his father’s marriage broke up, because Daddy dearest was too busy running the company business, didn’t they make more money? Didn’t father become richer and more successful, when he dumped the worries of the heart, in favor of pursuits of the mind? Most certainly he did, and such is the lesson he taught his son. People come, and people go, but greed is forever, and greed shall set you free. This was father’s message, his one singly important less he taught to his son. Greed is good. Thus greed now drives Michael, and perhaps it always will. There’s very little question greed will drive him this Thursday as he takes on Scorpion. For when they step into the ring, one on one, all thoughts of friendship, partnership will be cast to the winds, as greed takes control of Stevens. And he wouldn’t have it any other way. ~Fin~ View Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens 's Biography Back
