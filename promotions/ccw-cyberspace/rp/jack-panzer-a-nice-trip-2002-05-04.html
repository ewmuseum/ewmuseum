Jack Panzer - A nice trip

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Nicky White takes Jack Panzer on a yacht trip and briefs him on his opponents Ebola and Shane Haze.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: A nice trip Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 11:31:42 Location: Los Angeles, CA This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens in front of a hotel in front of the Malibu Beach, California. It's a clear spring day, and the crowds and families that struggled through the cold winter this year, inside their homes, drinking their cocos and wearing their heavy jackets, are now playing on the outdoors, wearing bathing suits, cheerfully playing along the beach line. We can see from old women walking their little poodles, to kids trying to fish the tops out of uncautious women, lying down on the beach, getting a nice tan ready for the summer. The camera moves around from the beautiful and crowded beach, showing the hotel, a nice, majestic building, just recently built. The green glassed majestic wall, reaching the skies in a futile attempt to bring men closer to god, shines thanks to the hot, warm, bright spring californian sun, which reaches its golden arms to mankind, giving them some pleasure and warmth he failed to do during the early months of the year. In front of the building, we see a white stretch limo, who has just arrived, and from inside the building comes a very sexy and hoppy Nicky White. Her short straight red hair, flowing gently with the cool beach breeze, gives her small, curvy body a perfect touch. Wearing a red bikini top and a hawaiian towel covering her bikini bottom, and wearing a big pair of Gucci sunglasses, Nicky hops to the limo, singing some weird song. She walks into the limo and waits. A few moments pass, and we don't see the limo moving. Finally, Nicky hops out of the white stretcher, this time a little furious, stomping her feet to the ground. She immediately storms through the glass door and we can hear a HUGE commotion coming from the inside. Vases being broken, people screaming, cows mooing... Ok, maybe it wasn't a cow, but something mooed from there, I mean it. Suddenly the doors open again, and flying from the inside comes a huge man, wearing black trenchcoat and pants and a Yankees cap, helping enclose his face. He hesitates a little, looking at both sides, until Nicky, who comes out of the hotel again, this time walking straight, simply gives him a swift kick to the butt, shutting the man into the limo. She follows up next and the limo takes off.** **The limo is a very rich one. Color TV, phone, fax, it has it all. Nicky grabs a nearby bottle of champaigne, picking up two glasses next. She looks at the man and says:** Nicky: Jack, please, you can relax now... Jack: Really? I don't think so... Who's the guy driving? Nicky: I don't know... Jack: WHO'S THE DRIVER??? **Jack jumps out of his seat, across the limo, almost taking Nicky's head off, who ducks into the seat, knocking her head on the ice bucket, making it crash on the limo floor. Jack, half body into the driver's cockpit, looks at the man driving and comes back, sighing heavily. Nicky says:** Nicky: What's it? Jack: No problem, the man is clear... Nicky: And you know that just for looking at him? Jack: Sure... Nicky: And how did you know that? His uncanny ability to focus on the road? His unsuspicious face? His passport? Jack: He's chinese... Nicky: Oh... Ok! **The scene shows the limo driving through the Sunday morning traffic in LA. Jack, still not very comfortable, asks, while looking to the outside:** Jack: So, Nicky, where are you taking me anyway? Nicky: Ah, it's a surprise... Jack: But Nicky, you know I can't reveal myself like that. The germans are on my tail... Nicky: Yeah, but that guy can't reach you here... Jack: True... Who is that guy anyway? Nicky: Hmmm, I did a check on him. Listen to this. **Nicky pulls her beach bag she was carrying and picks up a few printed papers. She handle them to Jack, who reads them while Nicky says what's in it, since she made the research herself:** Nicky: Ebola, aka Joey Doyle, old timer in CCW. Former play-by-play analyst turned crazy. Decided to wrestle after some guy kept harrassing him around during the shows. He's good, Jack, but never had a lot of success in CCW because he was always considered a non-wrestler. He got a lot of wins but was always underbooked. **Jack finishes reading the report, which has, as we can see as the camera focus on it, match results, list of opponents and his stats and bio. He turns to Nicky and smiles.** Jack: This is a great job, Nicky. Now I can see who's him. Now, I need to know why he's doing it. Nicky: Could be only money... Jack: True. It could also be something else, but we won't know for now... **Jack stops for a while and looks at the outside, where we see a nice marina.** Jack: HELL no. You didn't do that... Nicky: I did... Jack: Sorry, hun, I won't go... Nicky: What? Why??? **With a sick face, covering his mouth, Jack struggles to say:** Jack: Hmhhp hmmmp hmmp Nicky: What? Jack: Ihmmmp ahhmmpp ohhmp the semmmpf Nicky: WHAT??? Take your stupid hands of your mouth! Jack: I'M AFRAID OF THE SEA, all right? I get sick even thinking about it. Nicky: What? Oh, no, you'll go. This is the only way you'll distract yourself. On the sea, they won't be able to find you. Besides it's a huge yatch, come on... Jack: No, sorry... Nicky: Oh, NOW you're coming... **Nicky starting tickling Jack, who laughs and releases a few curses in german, before finally giving up and walking out of the limo. Nicky follows him and they both get in the yatch, a marvelous 120-footer, white, european-style yatch. Nicky walks in it and Jack immediately asks:** Jack: Nicky, who's this boat from? Nicky: An old friend of mine. Just get in... Jack: Cool... **Nicky easily makes her way up the little wooden ramp that leads to the boat. Jack, on the other hand, struggles, shaking like a nice bowl of pudim. Nicky laughs, as she sees a 6'9' tall german struggling to walk over the waters. Finally Jack hops on the 120-footer, and the boat starts sailing. The scene fades to black. When it reopens, we have a nice view of the open ocean. The yatch, lost in the horizon, sails, gently slashing the ocean, as it makes its way through the calm blue blanket of the pacific ocean. The scene shows Nicky, standing at the front of the boat, her arms opened, pulling a Leo Dicaprio. She screams:** Nicky: Jack, this is wonderful!! Please don't leave me!!! **A sound of hurling is heard. Nicky turns around and sees nobody is holding her. She hops out of the dangerous spot she was and turns around to find Jack lying on the boat, head to the outside, hurling like nobody could imagine someone could hurl. Feeling guilty for dragging the huge german to the sea, she calls for help, and the scene fades to a room, inside the boat. It's a rich room, with red velvet curtains darking the place, nice wooden colonial furniture and a big bed, with a red velvet sheet thrown over it. The scene then shows the door slamming open and two huge sailors bringing Jack into the room, supported on their shoulders. Jack slowly finds his way in the bed and drags himself up. Nicky arrives later, with an ince bag on her hands. She puts it on Jack's forehead.** Jack: DAMN, this is cold. Take it off!!! Nicky: Shut up. This will make you feel better... Jack: The only thing that will make me feel better is if I get out of this floating bathtub... Nicky: Blah, shut up, or I'll feed you to the sharks. **Nicky slowly strolls the german short dark hair. She says:** Nicky: Come on, I know you're not that sick. Jack: I'm VERY sick... Nicky: Then I guess you'll not mind if the germans take you back... They're right outside now... Jack: WHAT??? **Jack jumps up and hits his head on the low room ceiling, crashing down again. Nicky immediately runs, desperately:** Nicky: SORRY, Jack, I didn't want to scare you. Please, I'm sorry... Jack: Duuuuuuuhhhh... Opponent... Next... Opponent... Duuuhhhh... Must stay focused... Not... snap... duuuuhhh **Jack passes out. The scene fades to black. When it reopens, we're back at a hotel room. The room is fairly nice, with a high-tech look at it. Metal colors and eletronic devices fill the place. Sitting in a laz-E-boy, with his feet up, Jack slowly wakes up. He has an ice bag on his head. He gets up and looks to his right, where Nicky is sitting, looking at a laptop. Jack struggles to ask her:** Jack: Hmmm... Damn... My head... What are you doing, Nicky? And how did I get here??? Nicky: Well, you hit your head hard and passed out. We brought you here so you could relax. Now, I have the data on your new opponent. Jack: Who's him? Ouch... My head... Nicky: Shane Haze... Former ProWC wrestler, now joining CCW. He may be a rookie in CCW, but he's well known in the art of wrestling. Jack: I see... Ouch... anything else? Nicky: Yes, he went all the way to the GT Tournament finals, finally losing there... Jack: I see... Very well... Shane must be tough, otherwise he wouldn't go that far... Nicky: Well, he was on SuMa's team... Jack: Ah, that's it. Got lucky. Very well, he's no different than the others. I shall show him that I am the toughest man in this federation, and I am the only person who can beat SuMa, with all my strenght, wit and stamina... OUCH, OUCH, my head... damn... **Jack sits down, as Nicky smiles. Jack puts his ice bag on his head and have some sleep, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
