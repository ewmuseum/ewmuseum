The Second Book of Nyte: Chapter 2 - The Prince of Excess Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #33 Date: Feb 24 2003 Location: The Black Library [Excerpt from “A look into the Hordes of Chaos: Section 3: The God Slaanesh”] Slaanesh, the Dark Prince of Chaos, is the youngest of the four greater Chaos gods. Known under a multitude of names, including Shornaal and Lanshor, the Lord of Pleasure is patron of all things beautiful and seductive. Master of excess and creative power, his realms of influence include music, art and passion. The embodiment of indulgence in all its forms, Slaanesh’s allure is highly addictive, and those who follow him are quickly overcome by the seductive vices of pride, arrogance and excess. Divinely beautiful and alluring, Slaanesh is portrayed as exuding a palpable and irresistible charm. Slender, long-limbed and elegant, the god is completely androgynous and defies the natural order of the world. He teases the souls of his enemies from their bodies as they gaze with adoration and longing into the hidden depths of his lustrous eyes. [We once again return to the Black Library, this time in another room, painted in black and…of all colours, pink. Most of the books in there are on Audio Tape, and most of the patrons have too much of something. Whether it is tattoos, piercings, dyed hair, what have you. Gabriel grabs a tape, places it into one of numerous playing devices in this wing of the library that is near-by the shelf. He puts the tape in and hits the play button. Good thing that he didn’t put the headphones on yet, because the text came across very loudly, and quite clearly.] Gabriel: Stupid punk rockers. [Gabriel turns it down and begins to listen to the contents of the tape. Suddenly, someone approaches him…someone huge, and I don’t mean in the tall sense.] Follower: Hey man, when are you going to be finished with that? Gabriel: Shortly [The follower walks away leaving Gabriel listening to something. His eyes light up upon hearing something…he hits the stop and rewind button, and walks away from the section….He then he power-walks to a private room devoted to the worship of Slaanesh, with a decorated icon. He then has a seat in the middle of the dark room. Remaining silent for a few moments…then silently, he begins to speak in a tongue not used for millennia. That speaking grows louder, and louder still, until he is bellowing the ancient tongue throughout the room. The wing-master then walks into the room.] Gabriel: LEAVE~! [The wing-master gets forced to the wall by the sound. He bounces off the all, leaving a dent there. Gabriel now, is surging with the power of the warp. He continues the incantation unabated. Finally ending with these words….] Gabriel: COME FORTH…DAEMONETTE OF SLAANESH…AND BE MY SERVANT! [A bright light shines from the Icon. Gabriel’s eyes remain open as the light; equal to a five hundred watt light bulb…out of the blue…the light goes out…leaving a smoking, steaming, horror of the warp behind. Gabriel is left in awe of the presence of the Daemonette, Who is scantily clad showing lots of skin.] Daemonette: How may I serve you? Gabriel: I’ll be damned. It worked, and to think, I used to call this Heretical. Come, my lady…we have work to do. {fade} View Gabriel Nyte 's Biography Back
