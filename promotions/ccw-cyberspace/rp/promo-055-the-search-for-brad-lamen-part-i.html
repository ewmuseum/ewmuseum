THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN; PART I

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN; PART I Koriann CCW Promo #55 Date: 03/03/03 Location: [Insert generic city name] [Scene opens up on the inside of a tour bus. Why? Because it's the only mode of transportation Koriann could get out of the airport after her guardian angel informed her of her quest... To find Brad Lamen. Actually, her true quest is to apologize to a family she insulted. But since she refuses to do so, she has officially changed it to... 'THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN.' And may the good Lord help anyone who gets in her way.] [We join the festivities as Koriann sits up in the front seat near the driver, trying to direct him where to go. The driver, however, has a tour to give and is trying his best to ignore her prattling dialogue. The woman standing near the front of the bus, using the PA system and giving the tour, also does her best to ignore the jackass. However, as we all know, ignoring Koriann is slightly harder than one would expect.] Koriann: *to the bus driver* 'I really think you should turn at that next street.' Bus Driver: 'But I'm not.' Koriann: 'But it would definitely help me get closer to where I'm trying to go...' Bus Driver: 'And I care because...?' Koriann: *hopefully* 'Because you're my friend?' Bus Driver: 'And, once again, you're wrong.' Koriann: 'Okay, well, we're coming up on that street. It's only about twenty yards away now. If you could turn on your blinker and turn the wheel slightly to the right...' Bus Driver: 'And yet, see how I'm keeping the wheel straight and driving right on past that road?' [The bus, indeed, drives on past the road.] Koriann: 'Okay, well, you see that next street coming up on the right? You know what I think you should do?' Bus Driver: 'Keep driving straight ahead?' Koriann: 'No, I think you should turn. There's a shiny new nickel in it for you if you do!' Bus Driver: 'Uh-oh... Look at that...' [And bus drives on past yet another street.] Bus Driver: 'We just missed your turn. It looks like I'm not gonna get that nickel. Damn... And I have kids to put through school.' Koriann: 'Well, fear not. Because you see that next road coming up? I think you should turn...' Tour Guide: *interrupting* 'Excuse me?' [Koriann looks up sharply at the tour guide. As we all know, Koriann's gaze has the ability to melt steel. However, the tour guide stands her ground against the unbridled glare.] Koriann: 'WHAT?' Tour Guide: 'You're interrupting the tour. I'm trying to tell all these people about the wonderful things here in [insert generic city name]. And they can't here me or enjoy my lecture when you're up here causing a commotion with the driver!' Koriann: 'Look, I really don't care about your stupid tour, or about stupid [insert generic city name]! I'm trying to find Brad Lamen!' Tour Guide: 'Well, you definitely won't find him here. This Brad Lamen of yours isn't on the tour.' Koriann: *stunned* 'Wait a minute... Did you just say that Brad Lamen isn't a part of your tour!? What kind of crappy operation are you running here??? Brad Lamen should be a part of EVERY tour! He's a household name!' Tour Guide: 'Well, I've never heard of this ‘Brad Lamen'... Why is he so important?' Koriann: *stunned look continuing* 'Lady... Don't say such sacrilegious things! Brad Lamen is God! He's the impotent one that looks over us all!' Bus Driver: 'I think you mean ‘omnipotent'.' Koriann: 'Shut. Up!' Tour Guide: *beside herself* 'God's name is ‘Brad Lamen'?' Koriann: 'Yes! An Angel told me! My Guardian Angel, as a matter of fact...' Tour Guide: *to the bus driver* 'Do you believe this!?' *to Koriann* 'Better question, do YOU actually believe this!?' Koriann: 'Yes! I'm on a quest to find The Lamen! And once I do, all my questions will be answered!' Tour Guide: 'HA! What a load of crap! I've heard of some stupid beliefs in my day, but I have NEVER...' [And before the tour guide can finish that thought, a particularly big pot hole on the road causes the bus to jolt, causing the already-frayed cable to the microphone that the tour guide is holding to connect briefly with the already frayed starter wire that is defying all logic by hanging out in the open. The two meet, there is a spark, and suddenly the tour guide is fed a massive amount of electricity... More than is generally considered healthy. Her hair stands on end and starts to smoke while her clothes catch on fire. Simultaneously, something explodes in the front of the bus and the bus driver fights to keep the bud from careening off the road. He manages to pull the bus onto the shoulder of the road as the Tour guide dances a little electricity jig before slumping down on the seat over from Koriann. The bus driver looks back at her sizzling corpse...] Bus Driver: 'Good Lord...' Koriann: *leaping to her feet* 'See!? That's what happens when you insult The Lamen! What's Hell like, bitch, ‘cause that's where you are now! The Lamen has no time or patience for sows like you!' Bus Driver: *shocked* 'S-she... She died because she insulted God?' Koriann: 'That's right! The Lamen will not be made a fool of!' [Koriann turns to the rest of the bus occupants.] Koriann: 'Repent all you sinners! I know for a fact that Brad Lamen hates you all! You better get on his good side before something like this happens to you! Praise Lamen!' [The bus occupants gasp in fear. Okay, not really. Most of them sit there, staring at the still-smouldering tour guide.] Bus Driver: *stands up* 'I would like to know more about this... Lamen.' Koriann: 'And so you shall. But first, we must be away from here. This vehicle looks unfit for travel, and we must find Lamen with all haste!' Bus Driver: 'Agreed! Let us be off!' [And the two of them, ignoring the rest of the bus occupants, leap out the door and into the evening. The quest continues...] View Koriann 's Biography Back
