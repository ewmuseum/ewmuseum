Sacrificing The Lamb (Tag Team RP)

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A tag-team roleplay in which Blayze and Darren Jordan plan a ritual sacrifice involving Neurosis.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Menace II Society Entitled: Sacrificing The Lamb(Tag Team RP) Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 17:16:04 Location: The Mutuba Temple(Location Unknown) This is a: Match RP [In the olden days, sacrificing an animal or at times a human being, meant honor and sending a gift to the god's, during a spiritual ritual. Nowadays, sacrificing an animal or human meant muder, no matter the reason you are performing the act. Only sickening, bloodlust human beings would engage in sacrificing, and only someone like Blayze would have a mindset for such indulgence. Inside a temple, a sacred infrastructure of religious prayers and soul healing, two sinners by the names of Jerome 'Blayze' Jordan and Darren Jordan have entered the temple for unknown reasons. As an ancient scriptured cup twirls in his shady palms, Blayze lifts the full cup and drenches his throat with the soothing liquid found inside. Looking over him is Darren, fiddling his fingers as he turns the pages of his holy Koran.] Darren: If only Allah could have told us about this temple before hand. [Blayze swallows the last sip of the liquid and lowers the cup from his lips] Blayze: Remember this my brother, Allah was put to rest over a year ago, hell how were we suppose to know he had undisclosed files on this place in his wrestling dojo Darren: Point Made [Blayze places the cup on the small tabarnacle in front of him and asks Darren to pass him the Koran. Once in his hands, he lays the book on the tabarnacle and opens up the pages to one of the chronicle chapters. A smile rises on his face, not one of joy but of insanity] Blayze: As state in the Al-Khafir New Testament Edition of the Holy Koran, a sacrificial ritual is the way fo pleasing a God and inheriting their soul in one's human body. Once achieved, a new sense of religious atmosphere, power and inner healing begin to be enhanced in the bodies of the ones who go forth with the sacrifice Darren: So my brother, what do you have planned? Blayze: Its simple, with Benny Blades now eliminated from the equation, a plan which I had thought up all day, can now be unveiled. You see brother, when you sacrifice a being to a God as gift, you cannot just sacrifice anything, it has to be an honourable sacrifice. With Neurosis as World Champion and now no one on his side and only enemies amongts his path, sending his soul to the God of Allah Shaheem will be more then a sacrifice but a praise from us, hoping we shall get what we expect in return Darren: Shouldnt we be scared of failure? Blayze: There is no such thing as failure in my eyes. We shall enter the building, plummeth the career of one Neurosis and not only claim the tag titles but also deliver a present to the spirit of Allah. No one will dare to walk the road we will once everything falls into place. Darren: And of course, the good ol' president will have second thoughts of trying to screw the minds of Menace II Society Blayze: Second Thoughts? [Blayze slams the Koran shut] Blayze: I swear to you brother, he will not have any thoughts PERIOD! I have created a spiritual line, if crossed by Spider, Neurosis or anyone..Trust me, they will be left unconscious for an eternity. Darren: The only thing I am questionning, is since Benny is out indefinetely, will Neurosis bring in another tag partner Blayze: Hahaha! He has no friends, no protection, no source of allies what-so-ever. He defines 'crack-pot' because with the mentality he possess, he will just proclaim 'Fuck You!' to help and try to tear down the powerful force of Menace II Society by himself. Its predictable, just watch. Oh yeah, and just incase SuMa or Thunder decide to stick their nose in our business just to get to Neuro, then I will be glad to sacrifice an additional two souls and add them to our unholy repetoire. Darren: Allah will be pleased with our efforts. Blayze: He will be more then pleased..he will be honored, so now my brother we must exit this temple and prepare for our destined sacrifice.. [Blayze takes one last sip from the holy cup and places down on the tabarnacle. As M-II-S leave the temple, the camera focuses in on the cup and what is found is nothing but a disgusting revelation, that the liquid in that cup was infact...Blood] View Biography
