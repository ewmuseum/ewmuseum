What this is A promo in which Angel and Guardian, outside a Louisville church with a blood-stained bat, address Danny Bryson and Jon Linsey.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: Blood Toucheth Blood Pt II Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 9:50:31 Location: Louisville, KY This is a: Match RP **Louisville, KY On the corner of 4th and St. Catherine Streets stands the impressive English Gothic style Methodist Church. Underneath the impressive stained glass window at the front of the building is the wonderfully sculptured archway leading in. In front of the archway are the cold stone steps. Sitting on these steps in basking in the glorious sunshine sit Angel & Guardian. Both wearing plain blue jeans and white T-shirts. Guardians long white hair is pulled back into a ponytail revealing the healing scars on his neck and forehead from Thursday’s Lockdown.** Danny Bryson you seem to have unlocked something in us Something that lay dormant Since last Thursday when blood first touched blood People have been asking us why…..why we did what we did I’ll tell you the same as we’ve told them And that at times…..to better understand just what it is we are dealing with, we need to take a leap of faith into the unknown. A walk in stranger’s shoes. But the fruit you offer us will not tempt us. For we have a stronger faith in what is right and just. Danny…..last Thursday when blood first touched blood you started a chain reaction that I’m afraid to say you WILL live to regret. **As she says this, Guardian reaches behind him and pulls out a baseball bat. The very same bat used last Thursday, dried blood plastered over nearly every inch** CCW will rue the day they crowed Guardian with the moniker Hardcore Spider would not sanction the challenge laid down by Carnage so we took it upon ourselves to take the matter out of his hands and into ours. You cannot escape fate Danny you can only delay it. And as long as Spider continues to work his own agenda, we will continue to take matters not into our hands…….but into HIS. Danny…….you still have a decision to make…………choose carefully for of the 2 paths available….only one will be your salvation. But be thankful HE has granted you with a choice, for this Monday night, right here in Louisville, Jon Linsey will be faced with only one path. This path Jon, is however not the one on the road to salvation. You see, nobody in CCW thinks it is possible for us to progress But then again nobody in CCW thought we would make it this far. Those without faith make a lot of mistakes. And mistakes are what people will pay for. Jon Linsey, on Monday night, when blood toucheth blood again, do not make a mistake. Do not get in the way of our progress because if you do……..if you do Jon………HE will not be happy. Remember WE are not your saviours, WE are not your salvation, but we are the punishment for your wrongs. **With that, both Angel and Guardian stand up, turn their backs to the camera and walk into the church** View Biography
