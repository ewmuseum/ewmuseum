Putting the fear of God into E.V.E. (Haw!) Koriann CCW Promo #18 Date: 2/17/03 Location: CCW Lockdown [The scene opens up on Koriann, who's once again standing near the backstage CCW Lockdown Interview area. Sly is there with her again, not looking very happy. He has his arms crossed as he stares at the jackass.} Sly: 'So... Another interview, eh? What are you going to talk about this time? How great you are compared to president Sylkk?' Koriann: 'That's a great topic, but no. I'm here to talk about Red and my upcoming match.' Sly: 'So why not wait until Lockdown? Why do it now?' Koriann: 'Because, dolt! What I have to say will emotionally scar my opponents! I plan on putting the fear of God into all of them!' Sly: 'Did you just call me a ‘dolt'?' Koriann: I plan on showing the entire world how much more intelligent and crafty I am than my opponents! And yes, I did call you a dolt.' Sly: 'Nobody uses that word anymore, you stupid bitch!' Koriann: *smug* 'Brad Lamen does!' Sly: 'Does not.' Koriann: 'Does too.' Sly: 'Does not!' Koriann: 'Does too!' Sly: 'DOES NOT!' Koriann: 'DOES TOO!' Sly: 'NOT!' Koriann: 'TOO!' Sly: *disgusted* 'If Lamen were here, he'd kick your ass for saying such sacrilegious things about him.' Koriann: 'But he's not here. He's too afraid that you'll sexually molest him while trying to give an interview.' Sly: 'Let's get this fuckin' interview over with! Jesus Christ! Your giving me brain cancer!' Koriann: 'Sure!' *grabs the mic out of Sly's hands* 'Are we recording?' Technician: 'For the last thirty seconds.' Koriann: 'Good. First, I want to talk about what happened last week. Devyl, you're a very crafty bitch. You turned my carefully sculpted plan against me. I worked on that there plan for two days, and you were able to elude me. So here's what's going to happen on Thursday. I'm going to follow you around, and with the first opportunity I have, I'm going to jump you and kick your ass!' Sly: 'Oh, good one! Tell her exactly what you're going to do, you stupid ninny!' *rolls eyes* 'What a fucking moron!' Koriann: 'Shut-up! I'm using reverse psychology! If I tell her I'm gonna do it, she'll be thinking that I'm just tricking her, and she won't be expecting me when I'm actually attacking her.' Sly: 'What!? That's not how reverse psychology works! You're completely...' Koriann: *interrupting* 'ANYWAY... This brings me to Red Headed Step Child. Red, I wanted to offer you an apology for knocking your brains in. Now, I was told I didn't cause you any lasting brain damage, which, when you think about it, is really quite a shame. Think about what a great gimmick that would be! In fact, think of the disability money CCW would have to pay you for something like that! But then again, you're really not intelligent enough to think of such things, so that's obviously out. Anyway, I'm sorry for hitting you like that. Then again, it wasn't really my fault. I mean, you were getting your ass kicked pretty bad out there. By a GIRL, no less! Ha-ha-ha! What a chump! Maybe if you weren't getting beaten so bad, you would have been able to dodge out of the way of my pipe shot! Of course, I'm giving you a little bit of credit here. I'm HOPING you would have been able to get out of the way. And that reminds me! You were in that Heavyweight Championship match with me and Cianan weren't you? Yeah, I think you were. My God, you sucked in that thing too! I kicked your ass so bad in that match that I remember you crying afterwards! Hah! You suck, Red!' Sly: *sighs* 'Weren't you supposed to be apologizing to him?' Koriann: 'Oh, yeah...' *rolls eyes, not serious* 'I'm sorry red. Now, onto my opponent for the evening... Steve...' Sly: 'Ahem! That's E.V.E.' Koriann: 'Are you sure?' [Sly nods] Koriann: 'Oh... Well that does make more sense. I wondered what a chick was doing with a guy's name. Anyway, E.V.E., you suck too. You lost the Indy tournament... To LACEY of all people! I don't believe I need to say anything more.' Sly: 'You should. Her losing to Lacey isn't an insult.' Koriann: 'Oh yeah? I say it is!' Sly: 'It's not.' Koriann: 'It is.' Sly: 'It's not!' Koriann: 'It is!' Sly: 'Jesus! Fine, whatever! Are you sure that's all you have to say?' Koriann: 'Let's see here... Psyched out Devyl... Apologized to Red... Put the fear of God into E.V.E.... Hah! Get it! Put the fear of God into E.V.E.! You know... God kicked Adam and Eve out of the Garden of Eden...' Sly: *can't believe what he's hearing, icily* 'I got it...' Koriann: 'Okay then. I'm done. Well, except for mentioning how small of a penis you have. I think I'll mention that to Brad later so we can both laugh at you.' Sly: 'Why you little...' [Sly lunges at Koriann who runs off laughing...] Technician: *sighs* 'Cut!' [Camera turns off...] View Koriann 's Biography Back
