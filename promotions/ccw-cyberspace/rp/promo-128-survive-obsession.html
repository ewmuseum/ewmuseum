Survive//Obsession

8 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Survive//Obsession Adam Christ CCW Promo #128 Date: April 08, 2003 Location: ... Sunday, March 30th. [ The scene opens within the PCW Arena at Atlantic City. It's hours after CCW's first Pay-Per-View since re-opening, being REVOLUTION. CCW officials and crewmen are seen cleaning up the place, taking down the REVOLUTION set, cleaning up the seats, removing the barricade and taking down the ramp. ...As the camera turns over, it stops at a figure sitting in the middle of the ring. ...The camera begins zooming onto the figure, revealing it to be none other than... Adam Christ. Sitting 'indian style', so to speak, Adam holds his CCW Tag-Team Championship in his lap. His back arches forward as he hunches over the belt, staring down at it. ...The camera continues moving forward, showing a few officials to now be removing the ropes from the ring. ...The camera stops, finally, once it has a nice frontal view of Adam in the ring. ...Adam seems to be caressing the belt with his fingertips, slowly tracing it's rough edges... embracing it tightly against his chest, as if someone was going to pry it from his hands. ...Adam appears to be rocking back and forth, cradleing the belt as if it were a child, throwing his head to the side to peer at some of the officials. The camera cuts to a side-view of Adam just as he looks back down to his belt, showing his mask to be strapped tightly to his head... and his eye to be wide open. He appears to be covered in some dried blood as well, but it doesn't look serious enough for alarm. Adam continues to sway from side to side where he sits, his lips moving as if he's talking to himself, but we're not able to hear it. ...Instead, we hear Adam's voice in the background, speaking calmly... ] ...What makes me wake up every morning? What pushes me to breathe? What encourages me to stand and walk? ...What?... [ Adam clenches his belt tightly and, using his feet, kicks at the ring to scoot himself back a few inches. His eye peers sharply at his surroundings as he continues to mumble things to himself. ...His voice is heard in the background once more... ] ...I ask myself that question every day, yet I can never find the answer. ...Maybe it's my thirst for pain...? ...The pain to myself, and the pain to others. ...Maybe it's my lust for blood...? To see it, smell it... taste it. ...Or maybe it's simply my will to keep going. ...To survive. [ The scene fades to black, leaving us in darkness as Adam's voice continues to speak... ] Survival is a strange thing. ...In 1972, a plane crashed in the Andes during a time of record snowfalls. ...When the passengers realized no rescue parties were going to be able to reach them, the survivors were forced to eat the passengers who had died. ...Why? To survive. [ The scene opens to clips of Adam within prison, getting his ass handed to him by other convicts. ...We see him being slammed hard against the wall, only to topple helplessly to his chest afterward. ...And as he lyes there, his voice is heard in the background... ] ...I've survived everything that has been placed before me. [ We then see clips of Adam's childhood, seeing his mother pry his right eye from his skull with a butter knife. ...The scene is so graphic that most of it has been edited and blurred out to appeal to the younger viewers. ...Adam's voice continues to narrate... ] Through all of the pain, humiliation, blood and agony... I still continue to breathe. I still continue to walk. [ The scene then cuts to several rapid clips of Adam within CCW, battling through all of the odds. We see brutal clips of him with a crowbar, as well as other various weapons. We see the Migrain and Crucifix done on various people. ...Adam's voice is heard, once more... ] ...However, my survival has been threatened by... [ The scene suddenly cuts to an image of Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens. ] ...Michael Stevens. ...Yet, he isn't really a threat, is he? Technically, I've already beat him twice. I've told him I'd make him bleed, and I did just that. ...I've seen Michael's soul, ripped it in two, and shoved it down his throat. Yet, after two defeats, he STILL desires to be given pain!? He's a moronic fool, plain and simple. [ The scene slowly fades from the image of Michael to complete darkness, but Adam's voice is still heard. ] ...If Michael Stevens wishes to suffer once more, so be it. If he wishes to rant endlessly about the way I give pain to people, I'll show him how RIGHT he is. ...Yeah, you heard me. Michael, you once said that Jack and I aren't wrestlers. You once said that we're brainless barbarians. ...Well, you're right about one thing. I'm not a wrestler. ...I'm not a fighter. ...In all honesty, I'm a coward. [ The scene shows clips of Adam freaking out in his earlier days of CCW, and how he ended up running away from a 3-way match because of the 'lack of praise' from the fans. ] I ran away from my fear. [ The scene shows Adam as a child, cowering in a corner after his mother had beat him. ...Or 'loved' him, as Adam thought. ...Tears run down his cheeks as he cringes, coughing out some blood and holding his kidneys with his arms. ] I ran away from my hate. [ We see Adam within prison, allowing himself to be tossed around... not thinking about fighting back. ...His acceptance of pain, and his strange love for it. ...The pain that will carry on in his heart for years. ] But most importantly... I ran away from myself. [ We see three images of Adam; as a child, as a convict, and as a CCW Superstar. Three completely different people, yet incredibly similar. ...Adam's voice grows to a scream... ] I'M A COWARD! I DON'T DESERVE TO LIVE!! I HIDE BEHIND WEAPONS!! I'M WEAK!! I'M PATHETIC!! I'M NOTHING--SHUT THE FUCK UP!! [ The scene suddenly cuts to the clip of Jack Manson, holding Adam by the throat and having him pinned against the wall. ...This clip is, of course, from an earlier episode of Lockdown. ...Jack speaks sharply at Adam... ] Jack Manson: They don't mean SHIT, ADAM! If you run away again... I'll fucking KILL YOU. Do you understand!? [ Adam slowly nods, and is dropped. ...And as Jack walks away, leaving Adam there to rub his sore neck... his voice can be heard in the background. ] ...He... He's right! ...I don't HAVE to run away! ...They don't... mean a damn thing!! HAHA... THAT'S IT!! I UNDERSTAND!! I UNDERSTAND!!!!! [ The scene suddenly cuts to black, and Adam's voice is heard once more, but he sounds quite calm. ] That very moment, I realized something. ...Michael Stevens, you're right. I'm not a wrestler. I've never been a wrestler. If I didn't have my weapons, I might not be here today. ...You were also correct in calling me a barbarian. I am just that. ...However, I'm not ashamed of it. I'm not ashamed of any title you give me. ...And do you know why, Michael Stevens!? Do you know why your words, your TRUE words, don't effect me!? [ The CCW Tag-Team Title is shown. ] Because MY method of fighting gave me THIS. IT gave me GLORY. IT gave me PRAISE!! IT gave me a PURPOSE! IT gave me a REASON to SURVIVE!! What does YOUR method of fighting give you!? The Trans-Atlantic Title!? HA! What a fucking CROCK!! Andrew Hawk and Gobstopper are both washed up!! You had NO competition! HOWEVER, while you might have beaten THEM, you have YET to beat ME!! And, let me explain this to you... IT'S NOT GOING TO FUCKING HAPPEN!! I'll beat you AGAIN, and AGAIN, and AGAIN, and AGAIN, until you can NO LONGER bare the PAIN!! I'll KILL YOU this time, Michael!! I'll fucking KILL YOU!!! I'll fucking KIL--Hey, Buddy! ...Hey! [ The scene cuts back to real-time, where one of the crewmen is tapping on Adam's shoulder. Adam blinks, seeming to have been in a trance, as he glances up at the crewman in confusion. ] Crewman: Buddy, you mind? I gots work to do. [ ...Adam slowly nods, and rolls out of the ring. ...Clenching the belt tightly to his breast, Adam walks around the area the ramp was once placed and makes his way to the backstage area. ...And then, the scene fades to black. ] ---- Some Other Day. [ The scene opens, simply, to Adam Christ. Sitting quite close up to the camera, staring into it. ...A grin crosses his face as his head tilts, causing his hair to move away from his brow. ...In a low tone, Adam begins to speak... ] Adam Christ: ...Michael Stevens, I've proven to you--to EVERYONE, that you're NOTHING but TALK!! ...Yet, you're set before me once more. Why? ...Why would you even BOTHER!? I've BEATEN you TWICE, Michael!! I've SEEN you BLEED! I'VE TASTED YOUR SOUL, MICHAEL!!! ...Yet, you want MORE?! ...Do you STILL want more, Michael!? ...DO YOU!? BECAUSE, I'LL GIVE YOU MORE, MICHAEL!!! OH, YEAH, I'LL GIVE YOU A LOT FUCKING MORE!! Because, like you said, I'm not a wrestler... I'm a pain giver. I USE objects to SATISFY my THIRST for pain!!! And, you know what...!? I COULDN'T BE FUCKING HAPPIER!!! BECAUSE IT GOT ME THIS!!! [ Adam thrusts his belt against the camera's lens. ] Adam Christ: DO YOU SEE THIS, MICHAEL!? THIS IS MINE!! THIS IS MY GLORY!!! THIS IS WHAT I SURVIVED FOR!!! AND I WON'T LET YOU TAKE THIS FROM ME!! EVER!! DO YOU HEAR ME!??! EVER!!! [ Adam pulls the belt back and clenches it against his chest. ...He stares intently at the camera, panting a bit... ] Adam Christ: ...It's MINE... and MINE ALONE. ...Michael, if you wish to be ripped in half this Thursday, so be it. ...I'll be glad to provide that future for you. ...I mean... I'm not a wrestler. ...No... I'm... [ The scene suddenly cuts to black before Adam could finish... but it shows the following poem in a bold, white text.: ] 'Gather ye rosebuds while ye may, old time is still a flying. And still the flower that lives today, tomorrow may be dying.' OOC NOTE: Yeah, I'm sorry for this not being my normal style/length/quality RP.
