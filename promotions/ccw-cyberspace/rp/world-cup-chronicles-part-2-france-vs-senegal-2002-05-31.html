World Cup Chronicles, Part 2 - France Vs. Senegal

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which Jack Panzer wakes Nicky White at 3 a.m. while cheering a World Cup match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: World Cup Chronicles, Part 2, France vs. Senegal Written in Friday, May 31, 2002, 18:52:11 Location: New York City This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens in Jack Panzer's apartment. The scene shows a dark bedroom, and we see the clock showing 3:00 sharp. The camera slowly pans down, and shows Nicky White, sleeping tight on her bed. The silence is absolute, and she sleeps like a baby.... That is, until...** Screaming Voice: GGGGGGGOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOAAAAAAAAAAAAALLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!! **Nicky jumps out of bed, as we hear allucinated screams and bumps on the floor. She puts on some night gown over her naked body, almost revealed, to all of us' delight, before running into the living room. As she opens the room door, she stops and stares at the scene, and the camera turns around and also gets shocked. There, wearing a Germany national squad T-shirt, jumping up and down like crazy, stands a very, VERY happy Jack Panzer, with a spilling beer cup on his right hand and a closet left fist pumping the air. Nicky stares at him, as he screams incohesive words!!** Nicky: What are you doing, Jack? Jack: SE... NE... GAL!!! SENEGAL!!! HAHA! WHAT A GOAL!!! SENEGAL!!! SENEGAL!!! HAHAHAHAAHAHHAHAHAHAHAHAAH! SENEGAL!!! Nicky: Jack... JACK!!! **Jack turns around, jumping up and down, hugging Nicky and swinging her in the air. Nicky, laughing, asks:** Nicky: What's it, Jack? Tell me! Jack: It's France, baby!!! Nicky: What about it? Jack: They'll job to Senegal!!!! SENEGAL!!! HAHAAHAHHAA Nicky: What? Senegal is beating France??? Jack: HELL YEAH!!! Senegal is kicking France's ass!!! TAKE THAT, you cheese eaters!!! Nicky: Geez, Jack, I didn't know you were so much of a soccer fan. Jack: I love it, always have. People in Europe love it. It's like a continental past-time. Nicky: I didn't know that... Oh, well... At least keep it down, would ya??? Jack: HA, that will probably not happen. Nicky: Well, don't jump too hard, you may get injured. You have a match this Monday, you need to take care, this Red Ninja dude is good. **Jack immediately changes his mood, from happiness to focused. He turns to Nicky and says:** Jack: I know, you don't have to tell me that. Nicky: I know, I'm just warning you because... Jack: Because what? Because I lost to Cianan? Because I lost the Indy Title after 3 days holding it? HUH? Nicky: That's not what I meant... Jack: I know, i know... It's just that... **Jack gets up, as France kicks a ball on the side post. Nicky follows him to the window, as he looks at the outside, with the cars riding the streets. He looks at the dark sky and says:** Jack: It was my fault. I should have won that match. I could have considered forcing a DQ to keep the title... Nicky: Nah, that's not you, Jack. You're decent, you would never consider that... Jack: I know... Well, you don't have to worry, Nicky. I'll beat Red Ninja's ass, and it doesn't matter how hard I have to work. That funny-ass jerk won't get past me, and that's a promise. I won't be used as a stepping stone. I have 4 more matches on COTR to go, and I need a good win to move forward with confidence on the tournament. Nicky: Yeah. Well, don't snap your ass on me, Jack, you've done quite a progress. Jack: *sigh* I won't, that's been healed. Nicky: Good... **Nicky embraces Jack, and then the TV is heard on the background, and they say France just kicked another ball on the post. Jack shoves Nicky to the floor and jumps like crazy, yelling at the TV for France's impending tying goal. Nicky sighs and gives up, before going back to bed. The scene then closes on Jack's face, contorting himself after every close call by France, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
