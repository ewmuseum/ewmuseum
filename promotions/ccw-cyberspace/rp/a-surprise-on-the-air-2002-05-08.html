A Surprise on the Air

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long comic-action scene in which Jack Panzer, seated in coach on a flight, assaults two agents pursuing him and learns that a former comrade is behind the pursuit, then discusses his tag match with his manager.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: A surprise on the air Written in Wednesday, May 8, 2002, 08:41:11 Location: Airplane over the Ocean This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens in an airplane, flying over the Atlantic Ocean. The scene shows the first class, with their passengers enjoying their lobsters and champaigne, sitting on their spacious, reclinable chairs that turn into beds, watching movies in their personal LCDs put on the back of the chair in front, tapping their laptops, sending news around the world over the plane's wireless service. We can see young faces, businessmen, with little headphones plugged to their cellphones, talking about business meetings they'll have in hours, we can see rich kids playing some games in their own laptop, rich society ladies, annoying the stewardesses for getting the kids quiet, and other pleasant people. The stewardess, a very young, sexy, tall slim girl, on her early 20s, short straight brown hair cut on her shoulders, blue eyes, wearing a VERY tight uniform, walks down the hall. From there, she crosses the curtain, and we can see the executive class. This place is infested with businessmen, as the name indicates, sleeping or using their laptops or checking papers or newspapers, wearing their nice Armani suits. The chairs don't turn into beds, but they recline in a nice 70 degrees angle, giving the passengers some comfort. The stewardess runs across the hall, passing a few families and couples wearing hawaiian shirts, and then runs past the curtain, to the coach class. Here, things are a little different. We hear a baby crying in the back, a swarm of headtops, almost like a sea of toupets, going all the way to the tail of the plane. We can see one particular head top, incredibly higher than the others, shaking nervously in his chair. The stewardess walks straight to him, and we can see the guy as being Jack Panzer, trying to find a way to put his knees in a comfortable position. The stewardess looks at him and sighs, walking towards him. She says:** Stewardess: Hi, sir. I have to ask you to stay quiet, there are children trying to sleep. **Jack, who doesn't look too happy, is sitting next to a woman, trying to hide her face with a hand. She's got a nice pair of breasts and is wearing a CCW T-shirt, tied on her chest, making it a top, and a pair of tight jeans. Jack looks at the stewardess and says:** Jack: Look, I know you have your job, but this is the company's fault. Stewardess: Excuse me? Jack: Yeah, it's your fault. I had first class tickets on this plane, but they overbooked me without warning me, and when I got to the airport, they said it was coach or nothing... Stewardess: Well, I'm sure that didn't happen, perhaps you didn't hear the booker say it was overbooked... Jack: Oh really? Look at me... **Jack points to his knees, who are at least 4 inches buried into the next seat. He says:** Jack: I'm not an ordinary guy. I'm tall, I need space. Don't you think I would have looked for another stupid airline if I knew this plane was overbooked? stewardess: I'm sure it was a honest mistake... Jack: It'd be, if overbooking wasn't a stupid, unrespectiful way to get passengers... stewardess: Well, anyway, I'll have to ask you to calm down... **Jack starts exploding. The girl besides him hides her face even more, this time grabbing a pillow.** Jack: Calm down? CALM DOWN??? LOOK, the only reason I'm on this stupid plane is because I HAVE to be on Florida in two hours. Your company booked me a first class ticket, but they came with this crap that the plane was overbooked. So don't you dare asking me to calm down, do your job and try finding me a seat that corresponds to the value I paid for, otherwise I'll SUE THIS COMPANY, DO YOU HEAR ME??? **By now, two more flight attendants, both male and tall, fairly strong, one short blond hair, the other red hair, come to the scene. One of them talk:** Flight Attendant: Sir, we'll have to ask you, for the safety of the plane, to sit down, or we'll be forced to sedate you. Jack: Sedate me? SEDATE ME? I'll show you how you can sedate me!!! **Jack completely snaps and runs over the two flight attendants, grabbing them by the neck and pushing them down the hall. The passengers start screaming, as they wake up, thinking the plane may be on the process of being taken hostage. Some old guy, military-looking, jumps out of his seat, on Panzer's left, but Jack simply throws one of the flight attendants over him, sending them crashing down over the seats. Jack keeps dragging the guy, his arm stretched ahead, his powerful hand grabbing the man's neck like a puppy's nose. Jack pushes the guy all the way to the executive class, where he looks around, trying to find a seat. He doesn't, obviously, so he keeps pushing the guy down the hall. The stewardesses are panicking, the business men are trying to hide their company's secrets, afraid they might get stolen. Jack passes the second curtain barrier, finding himself on the first class. He looks around and he doesn't find any seats available, again. But he can't stop noticing a young couple, smiling and enjoying a nice bottle of champaigne, being served by two stewardesses, smiling and laughing. Jack picks up the guy he's choking and turns him around, pointing to the scene. He asks:** Jack: That ain't normal. Who are they? Flight Attendant: I'm sorry, I can't tell you. Jack: Oh really? Well, I guess I'll have to snap your neck, then. Flight Attendant: NO, PLEASE, don't... I'll tell you, if you let me go. Jack: FIne, it's a fair trade. **Jack releases the hold, and the man crubmles to the floor, coughing and holding his throat. Since it's movie time, nobody is listening to them, as they're with their headsets. Jack lets the guy get his strenghts back, and after a few moments, the guy gets up. Jack looks at him with a threatening look. The guy says:** Flight Attendant: Ok, they're a newlywed couple, nothing else... Jack: I see... The thing is that I know this company, I was married before, and I know of a certain rule here... Flight Attendant: You... do??? Jack: Yes, I do. I know that, when there are newlyweds on board, the order is to bump them to first class, no charge, so they can become regulars. Flight Attendant: I don't know, I mean... Jack: So, did they get bumped from coach to first class because of that? Flight Attendant: I can't say... Jack: Oh, really??? **Jack threats to grab the guy's throat again. He finally says:** Flight Attendant: YEAH, YEAH, they were bumped from coach to first class... Jack: And they're on my seats, what are the odds??? Flight Attendant: I'm sorry, but you seemed like not one of our regulars, so... Jack: So you thought it wouldn't be a problem, since I'd never come back, right? Flight Attendant: Right... PLEASE DON'T BEAT ME... **Jack makes a mention, and the guy runs away, passing through Jack. Finally, he starts slowly walking towards the couple, sitting. They're laughing and enjoying their time. Jack stops a couple of steps behind them, where he starts listening to their conversation:** Guy: Pam, this was an incredible idea... Pam: I told you it'd work out, Jim. My friend married once, and she used this company for flying to her honeymoon. She said all you needed to do is tell people you're newlywed, and they wouldn't even check anything, they'd just bump them to first class. Jim: Yeah, and now, we can enjoy this flight as we should, as kings... Pam: Yeah, but soon we're gonna be taking these flights with our own money, as soon as we find Alex Bierhoff... **Jack's eyes get wide open. The mention of his old name shocks him. He hadn't heard about the germans in a while, and the fact that they were in the same flight as he was. He stays quiet, while the couple continues:** Jim: So, where is him? Pam: According to our source, their next show is on Virginia, on some small local arena. That's part of an inner-US tour. Jim: I see. TV show? Pam: House show. Jim: Good. This way security won't be as rigid, and we can nail him on the spot. Pam: True... Jim: Let me ask you something: Why is the boss so interested in him? **Jack opens his eyes once again. The mention of someone being behind him is new. He had always thought the german army was after him to cover the African ambush and the unfair 6 years in prison, but this question led to another story.** Pam: I guess it's because Alex still knows the location of the uranium mine... Jim: Uranium? Wasn't it a gold mine? Pam: That's what the german army thought, but it was actually an uranium mine. Apparently our boss tricked the german army so he could get his hands on the uranium... Jim: But what for? Pam: Simple. Uranium is more valuable then gold on the black market. There are always terrorist groups wanting to build a nuclear bomb. Our boss needed uranium to help sell the projects, so when he found out about the mine, he talked the german army into sending the squad. Jim: But didn't the army know about it? Pam: Not really. Only one army high officer knew about the operation, and he was the one responsible for the ambush and Alex's arrest. **Jack completely snaps again, but this time he kinds controls himself. He gets up and talks on the guy's ears.** Jack: Hey, I'm sorry, but you guys are on my seat... **The man looks back, revealing himself as being a man on his late 30s, wearing a pair of ray-ban shades and a black suit. He says without looking:** Jim: I'm sorry, there must be some mistake. We were bumped here, we're newlyweds. Jack: Really? Funny, because I thought scumbags weren't allowed to get married. Jim: What??? **As Jim gets up, revealing himself as being kinda short, Jack immediately grabs him by the neck and pulls him up, lifting the man from the floor. The woman on his side screams for help, before tyring to find something on her purse. The woman is asian, long straight black hair, also wearing a black business suit. Jack looks at the man and says:** Jack: Say hi to the boss for me, ok? **After saying that, Jack gives the man a MASSIVE punch to his abs, sending him out cold. He throws the man on the floor and then stares at the woman, who finally finds a can of mace, which Jack immediately grabs, preventing her from spraying his eyes. She cuddles back, trying to pass through the plane's wall, as Jack says:** Jack: Now, you'll tell me who the hell is after me, or else I'll make sure you and your partner have a nice landing, after jumping from 10,000 feet in the air! **Pam looks at the big german, completely in terror, before she finally cracks open.** Pam: Ok, ok... It's Capt. Ludovich. Jack: What??? Dan? **Jack hesitates, trying to reach his scorched memory. He remembers the days when he was playing soccer with his friends, including a kid he always screamed to play better, named Dan. The flashbacks turn to a future time, with him showing Jack to a girl, and Jack marrying her. The scene shows Jack getting on a military plane, and Dan and his wife, standing on an airport, looking at him fly. The scene changes to the present. Jack shakes his head and says:** Jack: No, this is impossible, Dan didn't have access to top secret information... Pam: He got it and sold several secrets to the Iraqis and the Afghanis. Now he's a rich man, living out of his money, in Berlin... Jack: Damn... I'll kill that bastard, or my name ain't Alex Bierhoff... Now, about you... Pam: What are you gonna do??? Jack: You'll see... Oh, stewardess... **The scene changes to Pam and Jim, all tied up and handcuffed to coach sits in the back of the plane, exactly one roll behind the screaming baby. The scene then changes to the first class, where we can see the curtain opening and out of it coming the girl with the tied T-shirt, and as she throws the curtain away, it's revealed she's Nicky White, Jack's manager. She slowly walks towards a sit that the stewardesses indicate. There, we can see a happy Jack Panzer, enjoying a nice plate of jumbo shrimps and a nice glass of Dom Perignon. She sits down and says:** Nicky: Well, I'll tell you one thing, youc ertainly have a way to handle flying overbooking... Jack: Yeah, and I'm sure you didn't like my methods. After all, you were hiding your face, ashamed. Nicky: Of course, otherwise we'd both be in jail and nobody would be able to bail you out. Jack: True, I'll accept that as an excuse. Now, let's talk CCW. What's up? Nicky: I just checked the card, and you have a tag-team match with Mark. Jack: Good, have you contacted him? Nicky: Yeah, and he said he's on his way. Jack: Good. What about my opponents? Nicky: Irate Pirates: Zane Blackbeard, Paula Hampton. Paula is a former CCW wrestler, had some success, nothing serious. Zane is a rookie. Paula is a cruiseiweight kinda girl, so you need to be careful against high flying moves, like hurricanranas and Acid Drops, very effective moves. Jack: Cool... Nicky: Lethal Injection: Devastator, Kerrupter, both rookies, haven't said a single word on TV yet. Might not show up. Jack: That's it? Nicky: That's it. Should you guys beat them, you face M2S for the tag titles next. Jack: Niiiiiiiiice. This is all I wanted. These two teams won't be a problem. My problem is with Blayze and Darren, and I wom't be denied a free shot at them, not like that. Those two ambushed me, attacked me and then thought they could get away with it. Not anymore. I'll prove to the world that I can also be a top dog in CCW, by defeating the two pussy brothers, and winning the tag-team titles with my big friend Mark. And then the CCW will see what the Panzer is all about. Now, look at these shrimps, don't you tell me it wasn't worth a little snapping... Nicky: Yeah, you're right. Sometimes, you have to snap to get what you want... hehehe... **They both enjoy their time on first class, while in the back of the plane, the two agents suffer and mum, trying to get free, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
