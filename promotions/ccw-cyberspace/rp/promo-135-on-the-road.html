On The Road Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #135 Date: Location: Jack's Bentley “….OK listen kid, just be there at 8pm sharp. I don’t want to have to go looking for you again and I certainly don’t want to be kept waiting……..Yes of course its important! I don’t do this because I like spending time with you. Remember what I said to you yesterday, this is arguably more important that winning the titles; there is no point winning the belts if you loose them straight away. That why we need to prepare kid………good I knew you’d get it eventually. I’ll see you tonight” Jack Manson replaces the phone back into its cradle and leans back on the leather limo seat. He grabs a glass of whiskey from the mini-bar next to him, takes a large sip and sits motionless for a moment before reaching above his head to the limo’s control panel. He punches a button and the glass divider between driver and passenger slides down. “John, is everything set for tonight?” Jacks driver looks up into his rear view mirror and answers; “Sure is boss” “All the tapes and files ready?” “Did them myself. I even ran over a few hours worth of material and marked it ready for you” Jack grins, but not his usual malevolent grin. This was more…..gentle. “Thanks John” “Its my pleasure boss. I like to help out where I can, you know that” “Yeah I know that. You’ve been a good employee over the years. Just how long have you worked for me now?” Again John looks into his rear view mirror and meets Jacks eyes in return. “Well sir, I’ve worked for your family for ooh…8 years now; the past 3 have been with you” “3 years already eh? I guess you’ve seen some things in that time John” John smiles “Yessir boss you could say that” “Well just keep watching, things are going to get very interesting around here. In fact…” Jack is interrupted by the phone ringing. Patiently he takes another sip of whiskey before answering it. “Yes?” Manson listens carefully to the voice on the other end before responding “”What?....since when? Well it doesn’t matter. It’s not going to change our preparation or indeed the outcome…..in fact; this may even work out in our favour. Never you mind how, does the kid know? Good, then everything is still as planned……OK I’ll call you when we get set up” Jack replaces the phone and arches his fingers in quite thought. “Everything aright boss?” “You know something John. In my time here at CCW I’ve never really understood just how the management works” “Eh?” “I mean I’m never really sure just what their intentions are. That was David on the phone, seems someone as decided to make Thursdays match a non title affair” “Isn’t that a good thing though boss?” “Good thing? It doesn’t matter either way John. It’s just their way of trying to control what the kid and I do. Seems they’ve had some complaints about the level we take things too. Thing is….I made myself very clear when I signed with CCW that I was not here to entertain, was not here for the money. I made sure they understood I was here to carry on my work. And now what? Now they don’t like it. Now their sponsor’s are getting squeamish? Well that’s just bad luck. Titles on the line or not. We have a game plan and we stick to it. After all what can they do……disqualify us? Then what? We still walk away as the champs to carry on doing whatever we want to” Jack starts to get agitated. He looks restless as he takes another swig of whiskey. “I mean what is it with these teams they throw at us? Michaels and Scorpion and now Blayze and Enigma. Even on their best night, working as a team, on the very same page, they would become just another pair of victims. But that’s just it. They are not a team, they are not on the same page as each other. Can Enigma really trust Blayze, and does Blayze even have the time of day for Enigma. Personally I wouldn’t piss on either of them even if they were on fire. I`m no stranger to either man. Hell I’ve come this this close to killing Enigma before and I have no problem doing it again. The only problem Enigma has to worry about is from which side the attack is coming. Me, face to face…or Blayze from behind?” “Boss, what about the battle royal, you decided what to do yet?” “Yes John….yes I have. But you, like everyone else will just have to wait and see what my plans are” Jack leans up and hits the button to raise the glass partition. “Let me know when we are 10 minutes from arrival John. Until then, I do not wish to be disturbed” The divider slides silently into place as Jack refills his glass from the mini bar.
