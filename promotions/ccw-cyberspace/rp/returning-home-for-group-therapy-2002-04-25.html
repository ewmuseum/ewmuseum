Returning Home (For Group Therapy)

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Scorpion returns to New York before Group Therapy and talks with a bar owner, an NYPD chief and Spider.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Scorpion Entitled: Returning Home (for Group Therapy) Written in Thursday, Apr 25, 2002, 21:06:00 Location: New York City, New York This is a: Match RP :::: Scorpion returns home, Friday morning 10:50 a.m. :::: :::: This message is brought to you by “The Revolution” :::: :::: Scorpion returns home to New York City for this Sunday’s pay per view. He hasn’t been home in NYC for a while now and he returns to visit the greatest state in the world, in the greatest country in the world, his hometown, New York City. :::: :::: Group Therapy will be the true test for Scorpion this Sunday night on Pay Per View. Scorpion will be teamed up with Candy Cummings, The Masked Mullet, Blayze and Carnage in an all out war to have an opportunity to become world champion at the end of the night. Scorpion has been waiting for his chance in the bright light to show everyone that he can run with the top dogs in the CCW and now this month’s pay per view, Group Therapy, Scorpion can prove that he is the real deal and not some all talk little basterd. Scorpion has made some history when it comes to CCW pay per view’s, Scorpion’s last pay per view, February’s Dead or Alive saw Scorpion winning the CCW Pacific and Hardcore titles in the same night defeating two worthy competitors, John Jupiter and Concrete Pete to capture the gold. It’s been two months since Scorpion’s last PPV because he was gone for three weeks missing Irish Spring, but now he is back and is ready to take the CCW by storm, all in one night. :::: :::: Scorpion is seen in his hometown of New York City walking around Times Square. Scorpion walks out of Times Square to the next block where Mikey’s Bar is. Scorpion walks in the bar to a loud reception. The bar’s owner Mikey walks up to him and gives him a handshake. :::: :::: Mikey: Holy Christ! What in the hell are you doing here? We haven’t seen you in months. How is the wrestling career going? :::: :::: Scorpion: It’s going great. I’m in town this weekend for a show. I’m with Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, the CCW. It’s great there. :::: :::: Mikey: The CCW huh? That’s wonderful. (Points to a stool) Sit down, sit down. What can I get you? :::: :::: Scorpion: I’ll just have the usual. :::: :::: Mikey: It’s been a while Scorpion what is the usual? :::: :::: Scorpion: Just a bud. :::: :::: Mikey: Ah, now I remember. By the way you can pay for you’re bar tab now that you are really doing well in wrestling. :::: :::: Scorpion: The check is in the mail. :::: :::: Mikey: The last thing you sent me in the mail was a Christmas card. :::: :::: Scorpion: How about I get you front row tickets to our pay per view event this Sunday? :::: :::: Mikey: Where is it? :::: :::: Scorpion: MSG. :::: :::: Mikey: I’m there, I love that place. :::: :::: Scorpion: Wonderful. That should cover the bar tab. :::: :::: Mikey: Yeah right, you will be paying for that thing until you die. :::: :::: Scorpion: Haha, very funny. :::: :::: Mikey: Hey how’s your buddy, T.J. whatever? :::: :::: Scorpion: T.J. Walker? :::: :::: Mikey: Yeah, yeah. Did he ever make it into that business whatever? :::: :::: Scorpion: Sports agent business? :::: :::: Mikey: Yeah that. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yeah he did. :::: :::: Mikey: Why isn’t he with you? :::: :::: Scorpion: I blew up on him when he challenged my ability, haven’t talked to him since. :::: :::: Mikey: When did this take place? :::: :::: Scorpion: Thursday. :::: :::: Mikey: Oh forgive and forget. You two are the best of friends; don’t lose your friendship now. :::: :::: Scorpion: Tell him that. :::: :::: Mikey: I wish I could. :::: :::: Scorpion (Finishing beer) Well thanks Mikey it’s been great but I got to go. I’ll stop in next time I’m here. See you Sunday. :::: :::: Mikey: Right on man, see you then. :::: :::: Scorpion walks out of the bar and walks a couple blocks until he is stopped by the sight of ground zero. :::: :::: Scorpion: Oh my god. I can’t believe it. This is terrible. :::: :::: Scorpion stands behind a police crossing looking at the cranes and tractors picking up the dirt and the firemen and police officer’s looking through the ruble. Scorpion is just in awe struck to the destruction that lies in front of him. As Scorpion stands there, a NYPD officer walks next to him looking at the destruction. :::: :::: NYPD officer: Hey aren’t you CCW wrestler Scorpion? :::: :::: Scorpion: (Looking at officer) Yeah I am. :::: :::: NYPD officer: Pretty amazing huh? I lost seventeen guys on my force. :::: :::: Scorpion: You a chief? :::: :::: NYPD officer: Yeah. :::: :::: Scorpion: (lends his hand out to the officer) Thank you. :::: :::: NYPD officer: (shakes Scorpion’s hand) No problem it was my pleasure to help this nation over come. :::: :::: Scorpion: We will get our revenge. :::: :::: NYPD officer: You dam right we will. :::: :::: Scorpion: You a big wrestling fan? :::: :::: NYPD officer: You dam right I am. Are you participating at the pay per view this Sunday? :::: :::: Scorpion: Yes I am. :::: :::: NYPD officer: Who’s on your team? :::: :::: Scorpion: Candy Cummings, The Masked Mullet, Blayze and Carnage. :::: :::: NYPD officer: That’s a great team. I tried so hard to get tickets for this weekend but it was sold out. :::: :::: Scorpion: (reaching into pocket) Here take these, there is four front row seats. It’s my thank you for what you have done. :::: :::: NYPD officer: (Is astonishment) Oh my god, thank you so much. :::: :::: Scorpion: No, thank you for all that you and your force have done for America, my home and myself. In return I will try my best to return the CCW World Championship back into the hands of a New Yorker this Sunday. :::: :::: Scorpion and the officer shake hands and Scorpion walks down a couple blocks till he reaches Madison Square Garden. Once inside he heads into Spider’s office in MSG. :::: :::: Scorpion: (knocks on the door) Spider, It’s great to be home. :::: :::: Spider: Home? What do you mean it’s great to be home? :::: :::: Scorpion: New York is my home but Madison Square Garden is my arena. I proved it in the WWCF and I will prove it this Sunday. :::: :::: Spider: That’s if you can get past me. :::: :::: Scorpion: If it comes down to you and I then you will get one hundred percent of Scorpion and no matter the outcome you just like everyone else will realize that I have the heart of a champion. :::: :::: Spider: What about your teammates? I know you dislike Blayze and you guys did have a little feud when you first came to CCW and fought the Menace II Society. :::: :::: Scorpion: Hey my teammates as well as I understand that we need to work together to make it to the top, after that it will be survival of the fittest. :::: :::: Spider: That’s if my team doesn’t stop you. :::: :::: Scorpion: I don’t think you and Neurosis get along to well. I heard you were going crazy when you heard you and him were on the same team. :::: :::: Spider: Yes I dislike him, but like your theory we need to work together to reach the top of the ladder and in the end it will be Spider on top. :::: :::: Scorpion: I just might be the “Cinderella” this Sunday. :::: :::: Spider: Yes, but just Cinderella, when the clock strikes midnight your time is up and I’m that clock that will strike. :::: :::: Scorpion: I don’t think that will happen. :::: :::: Spider: We shall see. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yes we shall. :::: :::: Spider: Scorpion, just some advice, stay out of my way and do your best. :::: :::: Scorpion: You can bet I will do my best and If I get in your way then it will be the last of your path towards the championship. :::: :::: Spider: This Sunday will be the true test. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yes it will. :::: :::: Spider: (extends hand) Good luck. :::: :::: Scorpion: (shakes Spider’s hand) Good luck to you as well. :::: :::: Scorpion walks out of the office and heads down the hallway to the exit of Madison Square Garden. Scorpion sits upon one of the steps leading up towards Madison Square Garden. Scorpion just sits there and he begins to think to himself. :::: :::: Scorpion: (thinking to himself) This is it, this your chance to prove once and for all that this Revolution is for real, that you are for real and that you are a true presence in the world of wrestling. It’s your chance, your opportunity, this is what you wrestle for, Madison Square Garden is your arena, and you have made history here. Now it’s time for a new chapter, a new era in your life, it’s time to add that CCW World Championship. This is your stepping-stone; use it to your best benefit. Win the gold not just for you, win the gold for your home, the gold for New York, win it on your home turf. No one expects you to win this Sunday, prove them wrong. It’s time. :::: View Biography
