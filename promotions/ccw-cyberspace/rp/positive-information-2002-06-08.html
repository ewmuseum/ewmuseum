Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Positive Information Written in Saturday, Jun 8, 2002, 13:21:22 Location: New York, NY This is a: Match RP **Regina is once again found in her New York office suite. After her 'miraculous' save of her father's company, she had received her old office back, her duties as her father's 'personal assistant' all forgotten. And as Regina reclined in her chair, feet up on her desk, she basked in the thought that this company would soon be hers. She was only awaiting the favorable report from the man who was now sitting in front of her.** Regina: Well? Are you going to say something, or are you just going to sit there with that stupid look on your face? Nick: Sorry, Ms. Winters. I wasn't aware that you wanted me to start. I thought you were.... Regina: **interrupts** Thinking about other things? Why, yes I was. I was thinking about the delicious beating I gave Lacey Skye Morris on Lockdown. I'm thinking about the beating I'm about to give her on Monday night. I was thinking about the man I bumped to in the hallway before my match.... Jackson Williams. I was thinking about how much I would like to control him.... Break him under my heel and keep him as my constant plaything.... Make him satisfy me whenever I desired. But all of that is irrelevant at this point in time. I have you here to give me a report. Now, I want to hear what you have to say. So out with it! Nick: Yes, ma'am. I have so.... Regina: **interrupts** If you call me 'ma'am' one more time, I will castrate you so fast, you'll wonder if you were ever a male to begin with. Call me 'Mistress'. Nick: **pause** Yes, mistress. As I was saying, I have some information on your father. As you know he hasn't been around all this past week. Instead, he's been trying to convince the board of directors that he still has what it takes to run this company. Regina: Do they believe him? Nick: Most of them don't. Many are looking for a new leader. Your father's methods almost cost this company a fortune.... If it hadn't been for you. Regina: So my father is in a very precarious situation? Nick: Yes, he is. One more mistake, or one more event that causes him disfavor with the board, he'll be out. So, mistress.... If you plan on making a move, it needs to be soon... Regina: **wicked yet sexy smile** You can believe me when I say that my Father's days in this office are numbered. Everything is coming together so very well.... I could almost say that.... Life is perfect! View Biography
