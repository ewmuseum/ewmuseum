Finding parchment Jason Dread CCW Promo #26 Date: 2/19/03 Location: Read it for pete's sake! ~This piece of parchment was found and returned immediately to the CCW Front Offices. The following is a letter which was written, the recipient unknown. There were obviously multiple pages to this letter, but the first is missing.~ (Page 2) It's as if this is a man whom I can no longer relate to or know. All the time which I spent with him before is like I was with a different person. No longer is there a smile from jokes, or laughter in his voice, period. I have done so much thinking about what has happened, but nothing seems to make any sense. Every piece of the puzzle which I can find doesn't fit everything as a whole. I'm starting to get very worried about the entire ordeal. There have been times when I've noticed all the old familiar 'friends' of mine coming back about this whole ordeal. The anger, the hate, the bloodlust...I've gotten all those feelings again. It's as if what has happened to him is starting to seep into me and my psyche. It's starting to control me as well. Moreover, there have other times that I've noticed myself coming inches from snapping. I've always managed to catch myself, however...well, I take that back. There was one time when I wasn't able to catch myself. Honestly...I didn't mind letting myself go, either. At the time it all just felt natural, as if subconsciously I told myself that it was alright to do so. Perhaps I should feel guilty after causing the pain which I have, then again, I SHOULD be doing something else with my life to make others live in a much better world...it doesn't necessarily mean I will. I guess you would understand if saw it, if you didn't already. It was all due to that bastard Brad Lamen and his ego that was fed as soon as he received his little title as 'Union Representative'. What's nothing more than a few words, some power, and an extra zero or two on the monthly paycheck turned out to be his chance at an attempt to humiliate and control me. As I guessed times before, nothing could be done for himself either, as he placed himself out of our match and into a zebra costume. I'd like to say that I expected this, but in honesty I figured that he would have had the courage to stand up man to man in the match which was originally planned. I realize that's the punishment which I receive for giving scum like him just a little too much credit. We both know I should have learned by now... (Page 3) ...but I guess some lessons come harder than others. As expected, he did everything he could to get the better of me during this 10 minute travesty of fighting. I couldn't get in three or four shots in on Blayze without him doing something to turn the tide of favor away from me. I eventually got the better of this trick which he was playing, but I'll admit he was one up on me again. They turned their backs to me, which was a stupid thing to do to anyone whom you just screwed over. It took me all of two seconds to decide on what to...perhaps I should explain to you now that I didn't and still don't know OR care if Blayze was in on the game which Lamen was trying to play with me. For all I cared it could have been in the Pope having his arm raised with Lamen and I still would have knocked the son of a bitch out like I did. Needless to say, I cleaned both the clocks of Lamen and Blayze. It proves what I thought all along - they can't hold a damn thing in a fair fight. I thought the night would be over at that point...but out came Michael, and for what intentions other than to bash my head in with the steel chair which he brought with him. He missed...he's always been a little slower than I am. I grabbed the chair...and I was ready to enact what I felt was necessary for him...but I couldn't. I froze... And for Christ's sake I still don't know why. I try to tell myself to give what he gives me, and I convince myself that he deserves it. I just can't bring my body to it. I guess a part of me still knows that there is the part of him inside of him that is the person who I knew through good and bad, and for whom I struck my friendship with. I just can't bring myself to hate him...and I know without a doubt what I must do. I know this may strike you as being one of the stupidest things you've ever heard...but I KNOW what I have to do. Just like I told to his face, I'm going to deliver Michael from all this which has taken hold of him. I'm going to purge his mind, body, and soul of everything which has taken hold and controls him. I WILL get him back... ...even if I destroy myself in the process... ~This is where what we can read ends, the last page or two also missing from this letter. With his match with Michael Stevens quickly approaching...what course of action will Jason take? We will find out VERY soon.~ View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
