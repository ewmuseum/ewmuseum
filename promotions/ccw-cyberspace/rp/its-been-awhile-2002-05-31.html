It's Been Awhile

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Jackson Williams visits the grave of the man he killed in the ring and meets the widow.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jackson Williams Entitled: It's Been Awhile. Written in Friday, May 31, 2002, 18:47:06 Location: This is a: Interview Staind - It's Been Awhile. :: Cinemascope. White letterbox. :: :: The shot pours down with the rain in the air, plummeting its vision onto a cemetary thats taking a beating from the hard rain drops. In the middle of the cemetary, a black body length rain jacket covering his body, stands Jackson Williams. He looks down on the headstones, one headstone specifically. The shot pans down to the level of that one headstone. It reads: Robert Serylstone 1972-2000 A great man, father and fighter. The shot swings around to see Jackson looking down on the headstone; the rain drips from Jacksons hair that lends itself in clumps over his blue eyes. He crouches down on one knee infront of the headstone, Jackson slowly runs his hand along the top of it, his eyes begin to well up. His words come softly.:: Jackson: I never knew you until after that fight Robert... all I wanted to do was win... to be the best... I guess the saying is still true... to be the best you must beat the best... :: A crash from the thunder bellows throughout the cloudy sky above. None of this affects Jackson, his mind only fills with more remorse, more pain, more guilt. Lightning fills the cloudy sky for a second, then disappears.:: Jackson: I know I don't come here that often... I should come more... its been hard... your family... your young daughter... your older son... your wife that was to be... I see them every now and then, passing by, they recognize me... I know they do, but they don't speak any words to me... they only look at me with the hate you once did... I have to tell you, they look like they're are doing great... you're family... they amaze me... :: Again thunder, then lightning pass' through the sky above. Jacksons head drops down, his eyes staring at the ground, searching for words... his body shows that he knows what he wants to say, but the words are just hard to find. The rain drops begin to come with greater violence, pounding on the grass, headstones and Jacksons soaked body.:: Jackson: Its been so long Robert... but I'm still afraid of doing to someone else what I did to you... I see whats happened, I see the consequences of my temper... my anger... my blind rage that I lost control of... now I'm back in a ring... and I'm keeping control... I haven't hurt anyone yet... but it's the yet part Robert... look at us... our lives ended that night... only I'm still on this planet and God willing you're in peaceful bliss, watching over your family... guiding them, teaching them... :: His right hand cross' his chest. Jackson's head tilts up towards the sky, the rain falling from the heaven's pounds down onto Jacksons face.:: Jackson: God.. Lord... Father Almighty... give me the strength... the strength to control myself... to keep others alive... and keep myself alive... to find success where I failed before... to give life instead of taking it... the stars, the men, the women of the CCW... those are the ones in danger Lord... please protect myself from them... protect them from me... I only want whats best for everyone... all I'm asking is that you guide me, give me that self control I need... please lord... PLLLLLEEEAASSSEEE LOOOOOOORRRRRRRDDDDDDDDD!!!!!! :: The shot swings around to see in the distance a medium height dirty blonde haired woman with her eyes locked on Jackson, her body dawns a black raincoat below her black umbrella thats taking hits from the hard rain. Jackson remains looking up to the sky, past the rain drops and into the heaven's above. Her soft voice rings out through the rain that pounds on the cemetary and all that it inhabits.:: Woman: Jackson Williams... what the hell kind of nerve do you have coming here... kneeling infront of my husbands grave like you know him... like you were with him while he was alive... :: She walks with power over to Jackson, he rises to his feet, towering over the smaller woman; Mrs. Serylstone to be exact. Her eyes pierce with blazing hatred towards Jackson. The umbrella shaking as the hand that grasps it trembles from her furious anger. Jacksons eyes still carry the well of unreleased tears.:: Mrs. Serylstone: How dare you come here... you want to make yourself useful, leave... go do something other than kill people then visit their graves like you're their best friend... you never knew him, you don't know me... you took his life... haven't you done enough already? How much more do you want to hurt my family... HOW MUCH MORE! :: A single tear drop runs from Jacksons right eye. The drop runs alongside the rain that runs down his face, his eyes showing his inner pain. His voice becomes low, calm, broken.:: Jackson: I...I.. I'm....so...rry..........I di...did...dddidn't mean to.... to.... :: One more tear, then another and another runs down his face, joining the rain. The pain Jackson shows too easily for Mrs. Serylstone to ignore, the wells in her eyes flooding, tears begin to run down her dry cheeks. Yet, she maintains control of her voice.:: Mrs. Serylstone: Jackson... I know... but please Jackson, leave me be with him... for now... :: Her hand grasps his right arm, their eyes meet.:: Mrs. Serylstone: And Jackson... don't let this happen to anyone else... just...................... don't. :: Mrs. Serylstone walks past Jackson and infront of Robert Serylstone's grave, she bends down slightly. The shot swings around to look up at the tears that run down her face, to see her kiss her fingers then place them upon the grave; to see Jacksons face, the rain running down it, gazing with solace upon the widow of Robert Serylstone: the man he placed in that grave.:: :: The scene slowly fades away.:: View Biography
