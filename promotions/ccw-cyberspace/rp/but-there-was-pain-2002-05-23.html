But There Was Pain

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A first-person roleplay in which Damsel, recovering from a coma, recalls events and prepares for her match with Regina Winters.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: But there was pain Written in Thursday, May 23, 2002, 23:58:29 Location: ? This is a: Match RP The COTR tournament........ The match against Cianan........ Flying through the air with graceful ease, only to come crashing down on the Spanish announcer's table. Shocking Monday Night........ Regina Winters made a challenge to me........ A submission/I quit match at Memorial Day Showdown. Lockdown........ I attacked Regina after her match with Brian Gaines........ And......... And what? It's all a blur........ But there was pain. Beyond those three specific events, I remember nothing from the time I woke up in the hospital from my coma. And where am I right now? Is it important? Should I even care? All I know is: Regina Winters. She fills my every thought, every fibre of my being. It's almost as if I won't allow myself to concentrate on anything else. Is that odd for me? Yes. I should be concerned about everything else........ I could have sworn Mike Lubis (my boyfriend?) Woke me up from my coma last week. He said I had to train........ That I couldn't let COTR pass me by much like I have every other opportunity in my career here in CCW. I remember the nurse telling me that Mike wasn't really there. If that's truly the case, then where has he been this past month? Hasn't he called? Hasn't he come to see me while I was in the hospital? I would think so, but there's been absolutely no sign of him. Maybe he hates me........ ***Just like everybody else!!!*** Now that was an interesting thought........ Why would everybody else hate me? Have I done something wrong? The fans cheer for me when I come out on stage. They don't hate me, do they? I know I haven't been a very good friend to anybody as of late. I haven't heard a word from Akiko (***That's my fault!!!***) and I haven't called home to have a chat with mom. At least........ I don't think I have. I don't remember. It's so hard for me to think right now........ As I said, I have an important match coming up. I have a lot to prove........ ***To whom?*** I need to beat Regina Winters. ***Why?*** Because........ Because I can't let myself down anymore........ I need to show my sister that I still have what it takes to win........ ***Your sister's dead!!!*** I........ I know that, I........ ***Devyl killed her!!!*** No........ I won't accept that........ She's still alive........ ***Face the truth!!!*** I........ I can't........ I'm afraid........ ***Devyl killed your father!!!*** Please........ Please stop........ Whoever you are........ I don't need this........ I need to beat Regina Winters. I can't let myself down again. I need my family to believe in me. I need the fans to like me. I want Mike Lubis to appreciate me. ***But he doesn't!!! He never will!!!*** Please stop........ It hurts too much........ Regina Winters........ Why does everything have to hurt so much? I will beat you, Regina. I will give you exactly what it is you are looking for. I will show you that I am not afraid of you. I will show you that I can stand up to you. You will not control me. You will not have the satisfaction of beating me. I will show you that I am more than ready to take you out. I will come out victorious........ I promise........ ***You always break your promises. You always have. You always will.*** I know I can beat you. I know I've beaten you before. ***But there was pain.*** ........ But there was pain........ View Biography
