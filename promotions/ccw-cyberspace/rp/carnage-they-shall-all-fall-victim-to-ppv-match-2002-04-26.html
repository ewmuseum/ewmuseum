Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Carnage Entitled: They shall all fall victim to... (PPV Match) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 19:15:34 Location: Unknown This is a: Match RP **Nothing is seen, the screen is black. A voice-over speaks...** (40 CCW Men and Women... 8 Teams of 5... 1 Ring... What Does That Add Up To... CARNAGE!!!) **The screen flickers on. We see the face of 'ExMa' Carnage. An evil grin spreads across his face. The voice-over continues...** (This Sunday, the plan will finally go into effect... The plan that singles out one unfortunate man that has wronged him... But at Group Therapy, all will be well... All shall go as planned as he eliminates the target and advances in the Group Therapy Tournament.... Danny Bryson... Guardian... And three unknown competitors... They shall all fall victim to...) **The voice-over fades off. Carnage's face begins to twist into a fit of rage as he speaks...** 'THE EXTREME MACHINE... CARNAGE!!!' **Carnage simply explodes into anger and evil laughter. The scene fades to black...** View Biography
