Welcome Home

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short street-walk promo in which Danny Bryson addresses Fanatik before their first singles match, ending in a head-butt on the camera.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Welcome Home Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 9:39:58 Location: This is a: Match RP *The shot opens long pan on a large street. It's late at night and the street lights cast an eiree, brittle, phosphoresant light down onto the asphalt. Walking slowly towards the camera, a man in blue jeans and a black shirt, a long, flowing black coat neatly brushing the floor. A voice begins to speak as the man approaches* 'We all walk our path in our own way. Some walk slowly, some make the journey quickly. And then there are some, the worst kind of traveller. The ones who walk out...... 'Fanatik, there was a time when I respected you more than anyone else. There was a time when I worshipped you. There was a time when you were one of us.' *The clip is interspersed with a quick clip of Bryson, standing on Fanatiks shoulders and leaping onto Cianan from 15 feet up.* 'Those days are gone' *THe figure is making his way closer to the camera. You can now make out that he has long, shoulder length hair and a beard.* 'YOu walked out and now your back, clammering for a shot at stardom. Thinking you can make yourself main event by sticking your nose in where your not wanted. Well, now is the time. Your first one on one since your return. Your first chance to shine. And they hand you to me. Dont think this is going to be easy. Don't even begin to think that you are stepping into the ring with the Real Deal. You see, Fanatik, you could say I'm a changed man. A reformed character. THe inferno that burns within me will consume you Fanatik. It will eat at you, taunt you, rent your mind while I break your body.' *The figure Is getting close enough now to make out who it is. It is Bryson, head stitched up from the attack by Carnage and Guardian, favouring his left leg. Perhaps his knee has blown again. He is almlost touching the camera now* 'Fanatik, I've waited along time for the chance to say this.' *He crouches down so that his face is framed in the entire camera window, the entire TYV scren at home and he whispers, so lightly that the camwera strugles to pick it up.....* 'Welcome Home...' *Bryson nuts the camera lens and the scren fades to black.* View Biography
