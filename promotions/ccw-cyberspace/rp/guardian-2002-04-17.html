Guardian

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short backstage challenge in which Ricky Coyote offers Guardian a match for the retired Independent Championship, now the Houseshow Championship.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Coyote Entitled: Guardian Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 11:30:22 Location: Backstage This is a: Challenge **Scene opens backstage afterShocking Monday Night. We find Ricky sitting alone in the corner where two rows of locker seats meet. Ricky emits a small sigh and runs his fingers through his hair, which is barely wet from his short match. For the first time he acknowledges the presence of the camera. He sits back and slouches down as he begins to speak.** Ricky: I know someone like you doesn't beleive in luck. But let me tell you Guardian, your win Monday wasn't because of anything you did. No, it was like you didn't even win the match, more like I lost it. But nevermore Guardian will you catch me unprepared. No more quick cradles, no more piss-poor half crabs, no more easy wins. With that being said, you wanna go one more time? Thats the least you own me, a chance to prove what a fluke that match really was last Monday. The tought of a rookie with such a horrible gimmick beating me makes my blood boil. It makes me think the unthinkable. **Ricky reaches up to one of the locker's cubby holes and pulls out a belt: The original Independent Championship which was retired after Irish Spring March 21, 1999 in which Everlasting Gobstopper, Mash, 'Hardcore' Jaysin Urban, and Kaboink faced off in one of the greatest matches ever. The belt is now know as the CCW Houseshow Championship.** Ricky: Here it is Guardian, the rarest belt ever to grace the CCW. It's only had two owners and is only competed for once every year. I'm giving you a shot at it. I'm giving you a shot a to become one of the elite. Such greats as Phil Burner, Tim Inman, El Mierda, and even the Supreme Machine have sought to hold this myth and none have done so. You and me at the pay per view for the Houseshow Championship. What do you say Guardian? View Biography
