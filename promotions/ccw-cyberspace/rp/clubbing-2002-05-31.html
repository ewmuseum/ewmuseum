Clubbing!

What this is A roleplay in which Nikki Christianson goes clubbing in Seattle and gives a match interview to a CCW camera crew.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Nikki Christianson Entitled: Clubbing! Written in Friday, May 31, 2002, 19:04:08 Location: Seattle, WA This is a: Match RP *Seattle, Washington. 11:24 Friday evening. We find ourselves in a disco club. The music is loud, the multicolored lights are flashing, and the scent of sexual desire is heavy in the air. And in the center of it all, in the middle of the dance floor, is Nikki Christianson. She's dressed in a rather outrageous clubbing outfit, and is dancing up close and personal with two guys at the same time.* *The music is intoxicating. Nikki lets it flow through her veins, rush through her and engulf her with its energy and passion. It didn't used to be this way for her. Before she got pro-active with her life and joined CCW, she was conservative lawyer........ Not much more exciting than being an accountant in her estimation. She never got out........ Never lived life. She was always too concerned about her next big case or her clients. And........ And the fact that she was always stuck in the same moment: The moment when Lacey Skye Morris embarrassed her in front of her friends and fellow classmates. But now, it was time to get past that. It was time to show Lacey, and the entire world for that matter, how great she truly was.* *The song changes and flows right into the next one without pause, and Nikki excuses herself from her dance partners and moves off the floor. She can feel the lustful eyes following her the entire way, running up and down her hot, sweat-soaked body. She's never felt more attractive or sure of herself. CCW was definitely the right step. And now that she had made her mark, she was ready to make an even bigger one. She had a match against Devyl. And although it wasn't going to be for the Women's title like she had hoped, she had a chance to make another BIG impact by defeating the woman whom many people around the locker room called the devil herself. And to that end, Nikki made certain CCW sent their camera crew to this disco to record her match comments.* *Passing by a small fringe table on her way to the bar, a rough hand reached and grabbed her ass, giving her a good squeeze. Turning to the offender, Nikki found herself looking into a pair of the deepest, most beautiful blue eyes she had ever seen. The young man who had grabbed her looked to be about 25, and in a word: handsome! Nikki smiles down at him.* Nikki- Uh-uh, handsome........ It's best to keep those wandering hands to yourself. When you touch fire, you get burned! *And with a perky bounce in her step, Nikki continues on her way to the bar. Once there, she barely has time to order herself a drink when the CCW camera crew arrives. They look bewildered and slightly out of place in the loud environment........ Especially the little roley-poley one that calls himself Devin Belly. Smiling, Nikki pushes her way through the crowd and approaches them.* Nikki- Hi guys! Devin Belly- Oh, uh, hi Nikki. You........ You want an interview here? Nikki- Yes! Devin- Uh, okay........ Guys, why don't you try to find some place we can set up........ *The crew nods and goes scouting. Nikki looks rather exasperated at the fact that the camera crew has to go hunting.* Nikki- Can't we just do it right here? Devin - Uh........ Nikki- *calling out to the cameramen* C'mon guys! Let's just do this! *The camera guys look at each other, shrug, and start setting up their equipment on the spot. A curious group of onlookers surrounds them as they get everything ready.* Devin- So, uh, Ms. Christianson........ Nikki- Just call me Nikki! Devin- Okay, uh, Nikki........ You've made quite the impact in CCW since your appearance about a week ago. Not only did you manage to prevent Lacey from holding onto the Women's title, you also have made a challenge to the current title-holder, Devyl. Nikki- Yes I have. But she rejected me, didn't she, Devin. She told me I couldn't have a title shot. Devin- That's correct. But you do have a shot at her on Monday night. Since this is going to be your first officially sanctioned match, what are your thoughts and feelings? Nikki- Thoughts and feelings? Well, for starters, Devyl's evil, of course! I knew that before I even joined up with CCW. But I don't think she's ever face an opponent quite like me before. Yeah, she's faced Lacey the brawler; Damsel the aerialist; and Koriann the Jackass........ But they're all nothing compared to me! Devin- Why do you say that? Nikki- I've been taught that........ To successfully compete in this business........ One must learn to brutally incapacitate one's opponents. The sooner you can take away something from them, whether it be a limb, their overall health, or their will to compete, the sooner you can achieve victory. The others obviously fight on the principle of putting on an entertaining show. For example, Lacey has the 'Puppies Elbow'? Give me a break! That just allows her to showboat and feed her own petty ego! Devin- Well........ It is the sexiest move in sports entertainment........ Nikki- Devin........ That's irrelevant, and you know it! Point is, Devyl isn't going to be dealing with an entertainer anymore. She has to face........ Me! And that's something nobody can do........ Not even the Women's Champion! Devin- I see, well, good luck to you Nikki. *Devin finishes up the interview, but he doesn't leave. Instead, he stands and stares at Nikki's hot little body as the camera crew packs up.* Nikki- Is there something else, Devin? Devin- Uh, yes well........ Will you have sex with me? You're really pretty, and........ *Nikki rolls her eyes, shakes her head, and walks away before Devin can even finish his sentence. Devin watches as she approaches the handsome guy from earlier, grab his hand and lead him out onto the dance floor. Devin sighs miserably as he and the camera crew turn to leave.* *Nikki once again wraps herself in the music and the energetic movements of the surrounding bodies. Life was definitely good!* View Biography
