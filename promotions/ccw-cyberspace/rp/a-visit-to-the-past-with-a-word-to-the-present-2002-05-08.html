A Visit to the Past with a Word to the Present...

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A two-part promo in which Venneman Pendragon visits his parents’ graves, then joins interviewer Ben Davis to issue an open challenge naming The Valiant One, Fat Ass and Gobstopper.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Venneman Pendragon Entitled: A visit to the past with a word to the present...(Also an open challenge with special mention of the Valiant One, Fat Ass and Gobstopper...)Read this one, messed up HTML in previous... Written in Wednesday, May 8, 2002, 04:56:38 Location: This is a: Interview (The scene opens with a shot of a fool moon. The camera pans down and we are met with another shot of a full moon, this one slightly distorted; a reflection upon a glassy lake. The camera then points toward shore and we can see the outline of a huge man. The camera races in and we are met with the concerned countenance of Venneman Pendragon. He is clad in black pants and a billowing white silk shirt. He stands with his head down, looking at a pair of tombstones; the tombstones of his parents...) Pendragon: Our ancestral home. After all the pain that it caused you, dear father, I cannot believe you would wish to be buried here. Mother I understand; she was never the same without you, but you...To leave your body to eternity in the very place that broke your spirit and took you from this world...I only hope in death you found the answers which eluded you in life. (Venneman kneels down and places the bouquet of roses on his mother's grave. He heaves a huge sigh and continues in his quiet tones...) Pendragon: It has been a long time since I have visited. Three years have passed since I last stood upon the shores of this great lake. Not too much has changed around here, except me. I am not the man I was three years ago. Your death taught me that I took too much notice in the problems of others, when I should have been worrying more about myself. About the problems of those who were truly close to me. Now I have nobody to care about. I am alone, and the only one who can judge me now is history. When I am gone, will I be remembered as a warrior Pendragon, as Arthur of old? Or shall I go down as a nobody...Someone who is not even noteworthy. If I have my way, I'll go down as a warrior. I'm stepping back into the ring; I feel I have too much yet to prove, too many souls yet to destroy. Once I fought for honor; now I fight for me. If everyone else sees it as right, then so be it. If I am looked at as the bad guy, I care not. I chase only glory and the King's Gold that is destined to accompany it. I shall return.... (Pendragon walks into the trees and as his white shirt disappears the scene cuts to another shot. We are met with a strongly built young man holding a microphone. He is all smiles as he stares into the camera. He is wearing a white short sleeved shirt and blue jeans. He smiles away, totally oblivious to those of us watching...) Cameraman: Hey Ben, ya dipshit! We're rollin'! Ben: Oh shit...My bad Bob.... (A little background here...Ben is 'The Big Time Bastard' Ben Davis; a fine wrestler in his own right, he is also Venneman Pendragon's personal interviewer. Bob is simply Bob the cameraman. Ben Looks at the camera and begins his spiel...) Ben: All right wrestling fans. This is the Big Time Bastard and I have got an exclusive, and boy is it a doozy. The other day my phone rang and I heard the voice I never thought I would hear again. Venneman Pendragon, everyone's favorite Sword Wielding, mudhole stomping, pick you up, break you and get Medieval on your ass bone crushing wrestler, is returning to action in a fed called the CCW. I can't believe it, though I am overjoyed cause it means I am back on Venny's payroll.... (He is interrupted as Pendragon steps into view. He is clad in black pants and a shirt made from what looks like chain mail armor. In his right hand he holds a sword, with his left, he claps Ben on the back...) Pendragon: I hate it when you call me that Ben, but since it has been so long, I won't take you apart... Ben: Good to see you to, Venneman...First off, let me be the first to say thank you for coming back to the sport. You have been missed. Second, what's up with the outfit? (Venneman looks at himself and puts the sword in a sheath on his back. He shrugs before speaking...) Pendragon: Just wanted to see if the old ring attire still fits, and you know that famed Excalibur is never far from my side. Ben: Uh, yeah...Anyhow. The CCW...How did you end up there? I mean, how could they possibly afford a draw such as yourself? Pendragon: I chose them. (Pendragon chuckles...) Pendragon: How could I not join a fed that boasts superstars with names like 'Fat Ass' and 'Gobstopper'? I came here because I wanted to rule...I wish to impose my own iron will upon the weak, and this seemed like the place to do it...With so many already on my list of 'must breaks', who shall I start with? How about 'The Phantom of the Opera'? Or I guess I should say The Valiant One...What darkness lurks in your soul, boy? Do you see yourself as a valiant Knight? Perhaps one who should be king were it not for a disfiguring injury? I merely see you as a dog whose inner character actually resembles his outer. Hide behind your mask, Valiant. There is nothing worse than a fallen hero...meet me in the ring, and you WILL fall. The Last Crusade's coming. You're in the way. Stay there and fall, cower in fear and fall...It's all the same to me, whelp. Ben: He is an established star in CCW...Do you think it is wise to call him out? And to make fun of Fat Ass and the Gobstopper...You must be insane! (With this Venneman and Ben both roar laughter. Ben knows the full power of devastation that Venneman wields, and Venneman needs no reminders. Who wants to tangle with the newest star of CCW? Only a fool would try....) View Biography
