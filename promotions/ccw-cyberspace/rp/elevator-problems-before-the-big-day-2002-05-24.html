Elevator Problems, Before The Big Day

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which Tim Sonic is stuck in a shopping-mall elevator with a tramp, a professor and a caped man.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Tim Sonic Entitled: Elevator Problems, before the big day. Written in Friday, May 24, 2002, 05:26:23 Location: This is a: Match RP We see Tim in his big 4x4 car driving into the shopping mall parking entrance. He speeds round the cornerand nips into a parking space between a truck and a mini. He stop[s the car and gets out, he has a bag which is labelled wrestling gear. He gets his ticket and walks to the entrance of the elevator. He presses the button and there is a bing, he steps inside. Tim is standing in the the elavator when a tramp walks in carrying a bottle of whiskey. Tramp: Floor 8 Please. Tim just looks at the man. tramp: Fine I will press the button! Just then on level 3 it stops again. A young man dressed smartly walks in, turning up his nose at the tramp and says hi to Tim. Man: Floor 6 please. Tramp: Sure. The Tramp goes to press the button. Man: You know what Trampy! I'll do it myself! The lift then stops on floor 4. A weird looking man jumps in in a cape. Cape Man: Never Fear citizens. To the 6th floor and beyond. He then presses the button. We can still hear the young man saying words like Tramp and Bum under his breath. The tramp then turns and grabs the young man ramming him against the elevator wall, just as he does this the elevator STOPS! Tramp: I think we've stopped. The young man again mumbles under his breath. Man: idiot, stupid Bum. The man in the cape starts to choke, he is holding his throat and coughing. Cape Man: Loosing Power......Can't Breath...Dying....Lack of Oxygen. He then collapses. Tim then sarcastically says: Oh no he's dead. The Tramp and the Young man yet again start calling eachother names. Tramp: Gay Man: Bum Tramp: Poof Man: Trampy. The camera then goes to Tim as he has his head in his hands. It then fades out. The camera comes back on and we see the Tramp and the Man on opposite corners of the elevator, the man with the cape is slowly getting back up and Tim is on his mobile phone talking to emergency. Tim: They say we'll be out soon and just to wait. The man with the cape then leaps up and says.. Cape Man: This sounds like the work of Dr. BAD!!! Tim: Oh God, Super Twats awake. Cape Man: Don't worry, I'll fix this dilemma. he tries to pull the doors apart but the don't budge. Cape Man: Ermm..They arn't budging. Tim: You idiot there stuck! You won't open it. Cape Man: Good thinking, what shall I call you!? tim: Tim. But don't talk to me anyway! Cape Man: Fine I will call you Silence Boy! Now help me through his air vent and we'll save the day. Tim: Fine! The sooner I'm out of here the better. I need to tget to my training session. Cape Man: Where that? Tim: The top floor, Jimmy Mcdougalds wrestling roof its called, I am trainig for my first event in the CCW the big PPV Memorial Day Showdown. Cape Man: But at Night you are known as SILENCE BOY!! Tim: Fine......stand on my shoulders. Cape Man: I'm Up! You can let go, Wait! No Tim: AAAGh Tim falls. The man and the Tramp run over to help. Man: Out of my way Bum, I know CPR! I'll handle this. Tramp: Shut up, I'll do it Twat! They then continue to argue as the camera again fades out and we can just hear the Cape Man quitly say 'Help' The camera comes back on and we see the MMan the Tramp and Tim listening to the Cape Man. Cape Man: And then I threw him into the sea and then I pulled off his mask and then I lifted a car and dropped it onto his head and.... The Tramp and Man, who seem to be good friends now and adding Uh-huh's Ums and 'Only at the age of 19' Tim: Oh Shut up! How is it you can lift a car and not open these doors. And you can't fly or you wouldn't be taking the lift. Cape Man: Err....err......don't worry! He's still delerious! Err....You go doctor. Man: Its professer. I teach at a university. My names Matt Schweiber. I'm 29 and I teach biology. Tim: Oh God, wheres my scionide pills. Matt: What? I am sorry but What? tim: Oh no! Big, smart professor doesn't understand. What a surprise. Matt: Heh! Shut Up! Well anyway, I teach full time and play soccer for for a pub team The scene fades again. It comes back Matt: When I was 5. Since then I just can't stop. Tramp: Great! Matt: Ok. Why don't you go? Tim: God No! Not the Tramp. I can just imagine it, when I was 17 I run away from home and by the time I was 19 I was a full time jigalow. Blah Blah Blah! Tramp: Shut up now before I give you a punch in that big mouth of yours. Well, my name is Freddie and I am 25. I have been on the streets since I was 20............. He carries on talking about his life. Tramp: And that my friends is my life. Cape Man: Wow! What a super cool story! Almost as super as me! No you Mr. Big who thinks he is better than everyone else ( he points at Tim.) Well if you won't I will! (Puts on a posh voice) Well, I was born with a handful of plums in my mouth and a silver spoon to eat them with. Tim: Quit it. Fine I will tell you my story. My name is Tim. I ain't telling you any more so you can find me so we can go to the pub. I am a horse trainer slash poet slash wrestler from CCW. (Fade) Minutes later Tim is crying and the other three are symphatically comforting him. Tim: And then when I had to say goodbye to Tim and Sonic I just couldn't handle it and...... The lift starts moving. Cape Man: Yes were moving. I did it with my mind. Ha Ha! Dr. Bad- 0! Super Dave- 1! Everyone gets out on the next stop. Matt: So! You fancy a pint? Tramp: Yeah sure, if your paying. Matt: Cork and Bull? Tramp :OK. The walk off together. Cape Man: Well, I'm Off Silence Boy. To the toilet....... and beyond. Tim then clears his throat and goes to the stairs and climbs to the gym on the roof. Jimmy: Where the hell have you been Tim, your an hour late. Tim: Its a long story, I'll tell ya later. The enter the gym and the camera goes off for the last time. View Biography
