Josef Pires - Nothing Will Change...

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which new CCW signee Josef Pires delivers a long monologue about change on a rainy balcony.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Josef Pires Entitled: Nothing Will Change... Written in Wednesday, May 1, 2002, 13:38:03 Location: New York City, NY This is a: Interview (Darkness. Ever surrounding. No matter how you try to escape it...it's always there. As the camera fade from that darkness, we're granted with another form of darkness...the night (Don’t ask why I’m so obsessed with darkness, no I'm not a goth). From a balcony, stars high above in the heavens shine down upon the earth as a light rain falls from the clouds. Through this rain...and through this fog of darkness, we can see one man. Just one single soul who seems to be at peace with all of this. As we zoom up, we can see it's none other than the latest CCW signing; Josef Pires. As he stands, his clothes can be made out. He doesn’t look his normal, active, self. For the first time in his life he is wearing a black tuxedo, a black tie, a black shirt, well he’s kinda black. Looking up to his facial features, we see a blank, soul less expression. The deep blue eyes coldly stare out into New York City below. A cigarette is sticking out from his lips, as he takes a long drag, and puffs out little white clouds of smoke. As he looks to the camera, the rain seems to worsen...the skies being ripped open and letting its embodiment wash the giant balcony clean. Knowing the crew behind him is ready, Josef begins to speak in a soft, quite tone…Which is unusual...) Josef Pires: They say in life, in order for one man to truly know what life is, to truly understand why it is we are alive, we must change. We must at one point in time or another, change the way we think, the way we act, we must even change our perception of the world in order to grasp life. For some people though, change is a foreign word to them. Change is a word that only means something to them when they give a cashier, or any place of capitalistic business too much money for an item and wish to receive the left over amount from their money...also known as... change. For now though, let us not harp on that side of change…let us deal with the dictionary form of change…the state of undergoing alteration or transformation...because it is within that definition, it is within those words that we will soon begin to become conscience to the idealism’s and to the actions of certain men that have either under gone change, or have sadly remained the same throughout life... Josef Pires: For myself, change has been a constant in my life, it has been an almost guaranteed that change will occur to me whether I should be expecting it or not. Many say that change has never happened to me says that for all the times they has known me, they have never seen me change one bit and in the end, I have remained the same man you have known for the past three years. Well, that is true, on some levels...because for the past three years, people like you, have only known me by the pseudonym of Josef Pires. For the past months, you has only seen what I have allowed myself to let out to the public and he has only seen what I should care to show. What you has never seen though, is he has never seen, nor has he ever known Josef Pires before those said five months. From birth to the present state of things, the man known as Josef Pires has undergone tremendous change within his life. Why, you can just simply look at the maturation of the simple parts of human life and argue that I have indeed changed from infant, to toddler, to adolescent to teenager and so on and so on, but that in itself would be too easy in a way to attack the allegations of your claims of myself not changing. Instead of looking at the maturation in which every person on this planet goes through, we could look at the change in mental state that I have gone through in my life. We can look at the early years of my life in which at an early age, all I knew was joy and love. For the early years, pain and hate were not something I was usually associated with and in that point of time in life, to even think things could be that way was never even thought about and was truly a nightmare like thought... (Takes a puff of his cigarette) Josef Pires: What should this all show? Simple, it proves I am human. As we have already seen that in fact, I have changed in life...I have undergone tremendous change…it is time that we look upon a man that in fact, hasn't changed much in his life... Josef Pires: I've stopped fighting myself...I've let the darkness cover my soul, my heart and my mind. All that I see in life, I see through my shroud of darkness…I look through the shadows of life in order to grasp the true meaning of things....They say those who do not learn from their past are doomed to repeat it...if you don't learn about my past...you are doomed to end up on a hospital bed... Josef Pires: In a world where nothing seems to be impossible, there are always things that test that theory, always things that seem so close to being true, to being impossible, that people seem not to even believe it possible. There are always people who give up, who lay down before even trying the possible, because they don't believe in themselves that they can accomplish it. The question quite simply then is...is it impossible because of the challenge, or impossible because of the challenger? For years since I have been involved in wrestling, many, including ridiculous misfits that believed that it was impossible to beat me, and even went so far as to claim that I said those words myself. Josef Pires: The fact is, it has been almost scientifically proven that there is nothing that is impossible if you put your mind, body and soul into the Challenge. Therefore, it reflects only on the challenger, the person who cannot fully attempt the challenge, because they do not accept the challenge themselves. The truth is, I am not impossible to beat, I am just very difficult because of the knowledge I gained, the skills I have, and the experience that learned from. So the lesson has been said, and hopefully it has been learned. People believed they could beat the Impossible, by forcing it down...what happened when they tried to cheat against the Impossible? They lost. Nothing is Impossible, you just aren't good enough.. Josef Pires: But what do we see these days? Repetition? Thievery? The Wrestling world has turned into a helpless merry-go-round, that people seem to think helps themselves. Does it aid you by ripping off originality? Does it aid you by proving you can't think of anything by yourself? Does it aid you by ripping off catch phrases? Does it aid you in stealing something that has already been stolen as a way to make someone look foolish? The answer to all of these questions, is never. I am original, I am something that was thought up because there was nothing else like me. The only thing that people need to have in this game, isn't strength. It isn’t quickness or agility or stamina. It is your intelligence, and how you use it. If you chose not to use it, then quite frankly you chose not to win. A select few will be the first to witness Josef Pires firsthand, in action. And, like everyone else who decides they are good enough to be in the same ring as me, will learn firsthand what it’s like to pick his teeth with broken fingers. There is nothing I would love to see more than to be a success in CCW, to aid the CCW and to put guys in their place. I don’t have the time to mess about with pathetic misfits. Guys who think big of themselves, thinking they’re the shit, they’re the saviour of the company, they’re the guys holding the wrestling business together. Guys like them are a dime a dozen. In CCW I get my chance to prove it. The pride of my name is at stake, and like it or not, CCW will be assosiated with Pires in no time at all. Unless, there is a change. Unless I can prove to myself I am worthy of gold. There isn’t a doubt in my mind that I can come out on top. Nothing will change. (With those words...he looks up and into the dark sky and lets out a content filled sigh. As he begins to walk away, he smiles. The scene fades to black...) View Biography
