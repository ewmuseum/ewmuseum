Taking a Break from studying Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #58 Date: March 4, 2003 Location: The Black Cafeteria [We begin, once again in the Black Library, but this time in a different portion of it. It is here where people, who get tired of staring at books, tend to go to unwind for a little bit. It is painted in rather Neutral colours...compared to the other rooms, this one is something special. It is known as 'THe Black Cafeteria'. The place where ancient recipes meet with modern day cuisine, and we see Gabriel Nyte right in the middle of it, sitting at a table drinking some tea.] Nyte: Well. There is nothing like a good tea. [Gabe takes a sip of it and returns the cup to its saucer.] Nyte: I am not one for tag matches. As a matter of fact, I hate them...and I have no intention for being inside the building for this one. If Sylkk want to have a word with me about it...he can find me at Khorne's mountain. Toxic, sorry pal...you are on your own. As for me...I'm going...skull hunting. Hopefully the Berzerkers haven't torched the body yet. Now, if you'll excuse me, I would like to be left alone. [Gabe takes another sip of tea, as we leave the Black Cafeteria for whiter pastures.] View Gabriel Nyte 's Biography Back
