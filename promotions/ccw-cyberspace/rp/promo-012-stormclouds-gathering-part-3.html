Stormclouds Gathering Part 3

Stormclouds Gathering Part 3 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #12 Date: 02/16/03 Location: Seattle, Washington {It's just past noon, inside Jonathan's antique store. The front window has been boarded up. Lacey is present, helping Jonathan with his work. She's busy photographing rare pieces which are set up on an animation stand. Jonathan is off in the other room, wearing a wielder's mask and working on a large piece of sculpture.} {Lacey takes several pictures, and then pauses. Suddenly, something doesn't feel right. Her heightened intuition flares. She leaves the camera, crosses over into the other room and taps Jonathan on the shoulder, while reaching for a lead pipe to use as a makeshift weapon. Jonathan takes off the wielding mask, a frightened look in his eyes.} Jonathan: There's someone here? {Lacey nods her head.} Lacey: Don't know who, but I'm not taking any chances.... {She takes the pipe and crosses slowly to the front door and flings it open, revealing Damsel.} Damsel: Hi Lacey. Can I come in? {Lacey lets out a sigh of relief and shows Damsel in. Jonathan comes out of the other room to see what is transpiring.} Lacey : {to Jonathan} Jonathan, you've already met Christine Oakes. Damsel: {grins} Just a friend of Lacey's from the old neighborhood. {A short while later, up in the kitchen of the apartment loft.... Christine, Lacey and Jonathan stand around the butcher block chopping up vegetables for an omelet. Christine cuts the vegetables very carefully.} Jonathan: {without tact} So.... Are you normally the type of woman who follows around strange, gun-toting lunatics in the middle of the night? {Damsel smiles, glances at Lacey.} Damsel: I.... ah.... take it.... you're both wondering what's going on, huh? Jonathan: Yes, I would like to know very much. People jumping through windows and brandishing guns about isn't a part of the normal routine around here. Lacey: As a matter of fact, I was hoping you'd be dropping by. I haven't seen you in quite some time. And the other night when you just suddenly showed up.... Jonathan: How long have you two known each other? And how come you've never mentioned her to me, Lacey? Lacey: I'm sorry, John. We used to work together. Damsel was one of the top performers in CCW. Damsel: I definitely wouldn't say 'top' performer. Lacey: But you were definitely good. You were the first one to stand up against Devyl.... Jonathan: Who's Devyl? Damsel: The very essence of evil made flesh. She terrorized me and turned my brother against me, making him her husband. An expert at mind games. Lacey: We worked with each other until CCW originally closed it's doors early last year. At that point, I lost track of her, and haven't seen her since.... Damsel: Yeah, well, I was having some problems. Relationship problems. Really bad ones too. It's kinda why I'm here right now. Jonathan: Which conveniently brings us back around to the question: What the hell is going on? {Lacey and Damsel both fall silent. Lacey looks at Damsel. Damsel silently begins to formulate how best to tell her story.} Jonathan: {misinterpreting the silence} Oh, come on! You can't keep this from me! There's some psycho out there gunning for me! If you know anything, spill it! {Damsel smiles briefly, shaking her head to ward off Jonathan's suspicion.} Lacey: {encouraging} So what's going on Christine? Talk to us. Damsel: The man who's after you... His name is Steven Quartre. I met him about a year and a half ago, pretending to be a man named Mike Lubis. He owned a private gym where I first met him, and we started training together. I knew that something was wrong with him right away. He kept far too many secrets and disappeared for days on end. And although I tried to find out as much as I could about him, a part of me always held back. I thought I loved him.... He was the only guy who would pay any attention to me at the time. I deluded myself into thinking that he was normal.... That he cared for me. Jonathan: {bemused} You fell in love with a professional killer? Oh, that's just great. You never noticed him disposing the bodies of his victims in the dumpster out back, huh? Damsel: It wasn't like that. He was very violent and had a temper, but he never brought his work home with him. He actually helped me regain my self-confidence.... Renew faith in myself after Devyl had stripped it all away. After CCW closed, I tired making it work with him... Tried until I found out what he really was: A mercenary for hire. I confronted him with my newfound knowledge, but he didn't seem to care whether I knew or not. And so, I left him. But it wasn't that simple. {sighs} It never is. He came after me. In his own warped way, he really does love me. I put up with him stalking me for about four months before I couldn't take it anymore. I eventually decided to turn the tables on him. I willingly went back with him, and then started stalking him and following him around to all of his jobs, preventing what death's I could. Jonathan: Are you crazy!? That's nuts! Why do something like that? Lacey: {knowing where Damsel's going with this} Because he won't really hurt you or kill you, will he? He really does love you. He can't raise a finger against you. Jonathan: That's ridiculous! Damsel: No, she's right. Steven won't hurt me. I almost think he gets some kind of satisfaction having me follow him around and attempt to thwart all his plans. But I'm getting tired of all this. I can't do it much longer.... Lacey: And you're thinking that you have to put a stop to him, once and for all, before this can go on much longer. Jonathan: And so somebody has hired this guy to kill me.... I say you find him and stop him fast! {Lacey, Christine and Jonathan all fall silent, letting the information sink in.} Lacey: {breaking the silence, remembering a quote} 'Those whom the gods wish to destroy, they first make mad.' Damsel: {nods} Something like that. Jonathan: {to Damsel} So how do you plan on stopping him? Are you going to kill him, like he's killed so many others? Damsel: I'm one of the good guys, Jonathan. I don't want it to come to that. I'm just afraid it will. {Damsel pauses and studies Lacey and Jonathan, sizing them up and analyzing the situation.} Damsel: {deep breath} I think I'm going to need help with this one, Lacey. I hate asking, but when I saw you here, I thought I lucked out. I want all this to end. Lacey: Then I'll help. We may not have always seen eye-to-eye, but it's the least I can do for you. Just tell me everything you know and what you have planned so far. {reassuring smile} I won't leave you alone in your fight against him. {Damsel smiles in gratitude and nods her head. Maybe things would turn out differently this time, after all....} View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
