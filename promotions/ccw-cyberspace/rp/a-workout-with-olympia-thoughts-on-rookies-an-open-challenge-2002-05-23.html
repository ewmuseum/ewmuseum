A Workout With Olympia, Thoughts On Rookies, An Open Challenge

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Venneman Pendragon trains in a Manchester gym and gives his opinions on rivals and an open challenge.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Venneman Pendragon Entitled: A workout with Olympia...Some thoughts on Rookies and an open challenge to all... Written in Thursday, May 23, 2002, 07:12:48 Location: Temple Gym, Manchester England This is a: Interview (The shot is of a dark alleyway shadowed by falling rain. The camera moves down the alley and takes a left. In front of us we see a red door with the words 'Temple Gym' written on it in gold letters. We are in Manchester, England, and we have come here to witness a workout. Not just any workout, but a full on, balls to the wall workout between two powerhouses of their given sports. Dorian Yates, six time Mr. Olympia is the proprietor of said gym, and it is his tutelage that has brought wrestling superstar Venneman Pendragon back to England. We open the door and descend down the stairs. We hear muffled grunts and the clanging of metal plates. The sounds and the smell make us think of pain, of prison or a dungeon (which is the gyms nickname, The Dungeon) even Hell. We reach the bottom and the place cannot even be 3000 square feet. It has the hardcore essentials, including dumbbells weighing up to 250 pounds. That is a lot of weight. On one bench, we see the hulking forms of Venneman and Dorian. ) Dorian: Damn Venn....Dontcha think you oughtta be in the states gettin ready for the bloody pay per view? I mean, you have exactly 4 days to go! Venneman: Were it not for a scheduling blunder, I would not be here...I wasn't slated into the event until later...That, though, is for another time. I am just looking to have a good workout here...Maybe talk a little shop...I am not going to discuss the upcoming event just now... (With four 45lb plates to a side, Venneman begins his set of bench presses. With 405lbs total, Venneman works through his reps, keeping strict form...) Dorian: Alright. No PPV talk right now...How bout you give me your opinion on some things goin on in CCW? Although bodybuilding was my life, in my retirement I have come to enjoy the brutality of Wrestling. You are especially brutal. Venneman (Between reps): That means a lot coming from a man who has six Sandows (Olympia trophy)...So, what exactly do you want to know? My opinions are free... (Venneman slides out and Dorian slides in. Between reps, he begins his interrogation...) Dorian: SO what about COTR? What do you think your chances are? Venneman: I think I will win. The only way anyone in the federation is gonna beat me is by some means of underhanded treachery. Believe me, I have seen plenty of it there already. People can't win on their own so they gotta cheat. Honorless men in a glorious sport fill the roster... Dorian: Well ya know I am pullin for ya, mate...Anyhow, in your pool, I notice you are doing well...3 points with your stomping of Lightning...I only got one question...Why in the HELL is there a bloody WOMAN in your pool? How are you gonna feel about beating on a female? Venneman: Unfortunately, the Carmichael lass chose her profession. Hopefully she will realize that woman should keep away from business that men are to be taking care of. I only hope that when she faces me she has ordered her children not to watch. It would be a shame for them to watch as mommy gets broken on a big bad man's knee. I don't pick my opponents, I merely destroy them for being there. (Dorian rolls to his feet and the two move over to the cable cross apparatus. Venneman goes first, using the whole stack of weights...) Dorian: That's freakin cold, mate...Course, I guess it is the attitude you gotta have to be successful in your business...What about Mark Estep? Could he give you trouble? Venneman: I thought he was gonna lose to the wench. No, he will give me no problems. I will put him in his place...Quickly. (Dorian's turn on the cables, and he is still full of questions...) Dorian: So when you gonna get some gold? I mean, you can't be a rookie forever... (Dorian chuckles and Venneman glares...) Venneman: I should have some gold after this tourney, and you know damn well I am no rookie. I have fought battles in the ring that would make any man in the CCW feel pain to even think about. As to the so-called champions, I have called them out repeatedly and none want a part of what I bring to the ring. I guess they want to hold onto their belts. I don't blame them. I always held onto my belts too...Course I defended them often, against the best competition...I did not have 'filler' matches as the champs there do...But who am I? I am merely the new kid in town...It is good to be feared though... Dorian: What do you think of some of the other 'rookies' in the fed? Some of them seem to like to run off at the mouth quite often... Venneman: Let's see...I'll give you a rundown of the rookies... Devastator...The lad is very unintelligent...He talks a BIG game, but I don' think he can deliver. He is always a day late and a dollar short as he challenges everyone he can think of...No challenge this way from the kid...Since he feels it is 'His Duty, To kick Some Damn Booty', he is probably better off challenging folks like Carnage. At least then he has a chance to live up to his trademark saying...With me, he would just look stupid. Hmmm...You should know what I think about the lucky no talent bastard that is Tristan Matthew...What about this Tim Sonic character...He has kept quiet...The proverbial good guy, I reckon he was never much of a jockey since he weighs in at 350lbs. I guess he raises horses for others to ride, not for himself to squash. Other than that, you'll have to hear what I say about the PPV to hear more about the rookies. Seems like the opening matches features 7 of them gettin beat by me...but that is another story.... Dorian: So why don't you challenge some of the champions of the fed? Why don't you call em out? Venneman: They do not listen...How's this...Anyone interested...Open challenge. You versus the legend that is Pendragon. Come to me to feel pain...Allow me to deliver you into hell. As far as the titleholders, if you want, you don't even have to put your precious gold on the line. It will come to me regardless. How's that Dorian? How about we get back to our workout? I am tired of talking about life at the office... (Dorian shrugs and points to the incline bench setup. Venneman smiles and claps him on the back. As they move to the bench, the scene fades to black...) View Biography
