Who Am I.. Who Do You Care?

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An internal dialogue in which Cianan argues with his own inner voice about family and a former partner before turning his anger on Mark Estep.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: Who Am I.. Who do you care? Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 02:39:45 Location: I dunno.. This is a: Match RP Who am I...? Where do I come from..? -You are nothing..- -You come from dirt, you're worthless..- That's not true.. I just.. don't know who I am.. -Do other people know you?- Not the real me.. -Why not?- I don't want them to see... -Afraid to be hurt?- I'm already hurt.. -Afraid to be scarred?- I am that as well.. -Then why don't you want them to see? Is there something wrong? Can't you be loved?- No, they can't see.. they're all stupid.. They just want to see themselves.. they are selfish and arrogant.. There's always something wrong.. with them, that's why I can't be loved.. There's something wrong with them.. -Or, is it that you just have too much arrogance to see that you are loved? Or..- No, they're wrong. -Stubborn as always..- Shut up.. I didn't ask you. Why are you torturing me like this? -Because you torture me..- What the hell are you talking about? Who the hell are you? -I am everything inside you.. I know you better then you allow yourself to know you.. the other side.- Bullshit, I don't believe that. -You hurt inside.. there are people that can help you.. Akiko..- SHUT UP!! She.. hasn't tried to call me.. she hasn't tried to talk to ME! -But have you tried to talk to her? You hang up before the phone rings every time.- She doesn't love me.. she said so herself. -Oh? And your parents?- You know damn well they don't care. -And the last time you've talked to them was?- You should know.. before I left for Japan. -Correct. It's been a damn long time.. people can change.- People don't change.. they're careless of other's feelings, they never gave a damn, they treated me like shit.. -Father was hurt, mom knew no skills..- EXCUSES!! -Are they 'Cianan'.. are they?- Fuck you.. I don't have to stand for this shit. You say you know me.. then you should know this. -I know people change.. people constantly change, from the time they are children to the time they are adults.- I never was a child.. I had to grow up so fast.. -So, people have to pay for that? Are you trying to reclaim your childhood threw these childish acts?- Maybe, what do you care? -I care, because you not only hurt yourself..- I don't care who I hurt.. They've all hurt me in some way.. they deserve to be payed back.. ten fold. -Ooh.. I see, you can't answer yourself, so you lash out.. so mature.. just like everyone else that can't come to terms with themselves.- I don't need to hear this.. -Oh? why is that? Beause it's something you don't want to hear?! Because it's somthing remotely close to the truth, that will shatter your darkened little.......- ~~-=the voice is cut off in mid sentance..=-~~ I said, I didn't need to hear that.. fuck you.. fuck me.. fuck the world.. they're the corrupt ones.. I'm just trying to live. Mark Estep.. why are you in my way? Do you think I'll let you hurt me? Fuck you. You won't touch me.. I'll kill you.. I'll bleed you, and sacrafice you. You savages never change, you just go on hurting people.. all of you freaks are the same.. all of you are just out to hurt me. I'M NOT GOING TO LET YOU! I'm not confused.. I'm not hurt.. I'm angry.. furious.. Estep.. you stand in my way. ...no one.. stands in my way, and is left unaltered.. prepare to change.. those who never change.. are left to die.. evolution.. survival of the fittest... are you fit to survive? I have already proven that I can.. I am the fittest.. I am.. ...I.. am... View Biography
