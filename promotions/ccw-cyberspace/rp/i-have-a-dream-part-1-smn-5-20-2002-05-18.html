I Have a Dream Part 1. (SMN 5/20)

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A mock political broadcast in which Scorpion launches the New Era Alliance, introduces its members and challenges John Coens.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Scorpion Entitled: I have a dream Part 1. (SMN 5/20) Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 11:01:07 Location: Unidentified Location This is a: Match RP :::: As a CCW commercial airs on CCW TV the commercial is interrupted by a screen that reads: “This is a message from the New Era Alliance.” As the screen fades to black there is a podium that reads N.E.A. in front of it. A huge banner that reads New Era Alliance is behind the podium. Then T.J. Walker the agent of Scorpion approaches the podium. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Let me introduce to you the leader of the N.E.A. Scorpion! :::: :::: Scorpion appears wearing a suit. He approaches the podium and takes a piece of paper out of his pocket. Unfolding until it no longer folded. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ladies and gentlemen of the Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, I’d like to introduce you to the beginning of a new tide, a new era. I’m fed up with watching these old wrestlers coming back to the CCW and stealing the spotlight from the younger talent. I’m fed up with wrestlers like The Supreme Machine, Mr. Gobstopper and Sylkk returning because they have no money left in there wallet and they come back and steal the thunder from the younger, more agile talent and I will not take it anymore. It is time that the younger talent comes together to form a pack, a pack to destroy and conquer. Starting now the N.E.A. will begin to form. Who will join the revolution to end the veteran’s stay in the CCW? Time will tell. But for now I’d like to introduce you to the current members of the N.E.A. these members are the non-wrestling talents. First I’d like to introduce to you the man with the money backing of the N.E.A. he is the agent of the stars; he is “The Agent” T.J. Walker. :::: :::: T.J. Walker walks up to the podium as Scorpion takes a seat behind him. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Ladies and gentlemen it is time, it is time the N.E.A. once and for all buries these “old-timers.” I personally have had enough of watching old matches of the CCW continue here in the present time of the CCW. Nobody cares about SuMa vs. Phil Burner again or Sylkk and Eric Clark’s long time battle on how they tried to kill each other. No one cares! That was 2000, this is 2002, a new breed of superstars has developed in the CCW and this new breed is disgusted with the vets of the CCW. It’s time we put these punks out to pasture and the N.E.A. will continue it’s revolution to a new era until we have destroyed every single CCW old timer left in this federation. So to all the young wrestlers in the CCW, I’m talking to you right now. Are you fed up with how you fight your way to the top only to be shot down by someone like a SuMa or Blayze? Well then if you are then this is the fight you must join, this is our time, it’s time for a new era and the N.E.A. is here to end these old pieces of shit’s careers forever. :::: :::: Scorpion walks back to the podium as T.J. Walker. :::: :::: Scorpion: To all those younger wrestlers out there it’s your time as well, you shouldn’t have to sit back and watch these wheelchair huggers anymore. You know you should be at the top and your not there because someone like a Blayze or a Spider will always be there blocking your way, well not anymore join the N.E.A. and you shall see that a new era will take place in the CCW once and for all. Now let me introduce to you the enforcer of the N.E.A., the head of security and the man that just got out of jail because he and his mafia friends took care of some punks, he is “The Enforcer” Johnny Gringo. :::: :::: Scorpion takes a seat as a Johnny Gringo steps up to the podium. Johnny Gringo is a massive man with incredibly huge arms. He steps up to podium. :::: :::: Johnny Gringo: To all those punks outta there that actually believe they can destroy the N.E.A. then they will have to go threw me. I grew up with a very tough life on the streets of Jersey and I know how to crack skulls. So if anyone of these has-beens’s want to take there shot at the N.E.A. then they better have good health insurance because I will put you in the hospital. And for all those who just think I’m some wannabe Soprano then your got a another thing commin’. I’m 100% Italian, I’m 100% mafia blood and I will hurt you. Now when my long time friend Scorpion contacted me asking me to become the enforcer of this group, the head of security, I said no problem they don’t call me the enforcer for nuttin’. I’m the Enforcer because I enforce pain into you and I’m the enforcer because I will destroy you. So if you want to test the N.E.A. then I got one thing to say to ya, “Forget about it!” :::: :::: Johnny Gringo takes a seat as Scorpion comes back up to the podium. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the N.E.A. for now and I can guarantee you that you will see other CCW superstars join the N.E.A. It’s time for the New Era superstars to finally shine. It all started with a dream and now it’s reality. Now onto this Monday on SMN, round robin match to get into the COTR sweet sixteen, I will face a veteran of the CCW, John Coens. John Coens you and I have had a fair share of battles so far. Our first meeting I defeated you and took away your Pacific Championship, and then you defeated me and took back the Pacific Championship. Were even, but not for long. You see the stakes are higher then ever before and I want that CCW World Championship more then anyone right now and I will prove it step by step as my path to the CCW Championship continues. The COTR is where people shine and It’s my time to shine, it’s time that someone other then a nursing home wrestler main event, it’s time for the N.E.A. to capture the CCW Championship and this is only the beginning John. You and everyone else will see the change in Scorpion, I’m no longer the man that asks for advice from the Owner of the CCW, Spider. I’m fed up with asking him for advice because he made me just like him, a choke artist. I choked a Group Therapy, I choked against Danny Bryson when I was a inch away from becoming the T.A. champion. I’m fed up with listening to him, the only person I will listen to be me and only me and this time I will not choke. :::: :::: T.J. Walker approaches the podium. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: John Coens we will make a example of you this Monday. You are a veteran of the CCW and when veteran’s approach the N.E.A. they will see the price they pay. :::: :::: T.J. Walker sits down as Scorpion takes a speech out of his pocket. :::: :::: Scorpion: I have a dream, I have a dream that the dinosaurs of the CCW will be extinct. I have a dream that the younger superstar will finally get there time. I have a dream that the N.E.A. will destroy these nursing home superstars. And every single one of these dreams will become reality and if anyone tries to have a counter revolutionary action towards the N.E.A.’s revolution then they will pay the most violent price ever dished to a human body. It’s time to unleash the New Era Alliance onto the CCW. :::: :::: The screen fades to black as the N.E.A. logo appears. :::: View Biography
