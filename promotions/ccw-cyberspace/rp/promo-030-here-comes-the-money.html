Here comes the money...

Here comes the money... The Gobstopper CCW Promo #30 Date: 2/24/03 Location: Manassas, VA *Scene opens to show a cannon sitting in a snow covered field. It pans out to widen the view as an old house comes into the picture. Suddenly, The Gobstopper walks onto the scene with his usual business attire and a legal pad in his hand. He leans against the cannon and looks sharply into the camera.* Gobstopper: Aah, the smell of freshly laid snow... it is delightfully cold out here and I am delightfully sore as can be. Luckily, I could me more sore but thanks to the help of nice people, I was able to avoid extra pain at the hands of Sylkk and Brad Lamen. I know many of you think this was all planned, but you are wrong. I am here to save CCW from losing money, thanks to my B.A. in Finance, and the fact that CCW is run my uneducated slackers like Sylkk. *Looks down at his paper and thinks a second.* Gobstopper: But in the meantime, it looks like I'm booked yet again for a three way match which just happens to be for the number one contendership to the TA title. *Crumbles up the paper.* Gobstopper: It seems my opponents are Adam Christ and one, Ace Hawk. Adam Christ, I know not who this guy is. Maybe he's the Brooklyn Brawler of the CCW, I don't know. And then there is Ace Hawk. Long time no see, but every time I see you, we're both 'returning' superstars. What's up with that? I have a short history with you Ace... Remember back in the day when you and I went toe to toe... back in your early days? Remember when I paraded around as The Animator and you suffered my wrath and lost in our most awesome Indy title match? Aaaah yes, those were the days. Too bad now that we're both back, and I fresh off my victory over the people's LUMP, Brad Lamen, maybe you and I can eradicate Adam Christ and then give the people what they came to see... an awesome match because after all Ace, 'ol buddy 'ol pal, I have to help CCW make big dollas! *Gobstopper shoves the balled up piece of paper into the cannon and stands to the rear of it.* Gobstopper: Fire in the hole! *Gobstopper then pulls the firing chain and lets out a loud 'BOOM'.* Gobstopper: Cya at Lockdown guys and let's make money! *Fade out.* View The Gobstopper 's Biography Back
