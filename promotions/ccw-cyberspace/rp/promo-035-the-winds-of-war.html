The Winds of War

The Winds of War Blayze CCW Promo #35 Date: 24/02/03 Location: Outside of The Congregation Church [The winds creating a softening breeze as it brushes through the dark green leaves of the everlasting trees. Behind the camouflaged scenery lies the Congregation Church of The Lord's Prayer, a fitting complex for African American believers in the name of the Lord. Covered by the greyish Rocky-esque hoody, an oppressed sight of the current World Champ Blayze is craddled on the concrete steps of the church's entry way. His head dugged into the palm of his hands and his legs slowing tapping each other by the kneecaps, the emotions he senses are just too strong for one invisible man to understand] Blayze: Father, I would have asked you to forgive me for the sins that I have committed but it will only give you more inutile work in the future. I have lied, I have cheated and I have backstabbed..but it felt so good in the process. [Removing his face from the nest of his hands, he tilts back and just stares into the sky. No emotion, no smiles, not even a glimpse of fear in his eyes] Blayze: Trust is just another 5 letter word that has no significant meaning, it should be erased from the minds of all human element on this earth seeing as it is just trashed and abused time after time after time. This comes to mind, I need no partners..I need to allies..I need NO AID! Backup is just a plain excuse for not being able to get the job done yourself. I will be honest, the sight of one Jack Manson as my partner does not sit well with me. I would have rather been like the young prey being thrown to the wolves in a 2 on 1 instead of having to 'put over' another athlete by having him tag with a future Hall of Fame Inductee. But that is not my main concern.. [He passes his left hand over his face, blinding his view for a good second or two. He knows what is troubling him, it is apparent by the look on his face.] Blayze: Everyone movement that I make will be radared by the appearance of Enigma. I know the games he is known to play, I know how he plans his attacks on the prey. My eyes wont leave the site of the perimeter once I enter that ring, besides the fact that I have to worry about 2 more rejects of society and a useless partner, the stress rises per minute. [Suddenly the church bell rings..the Midday Mass has ended and in a few minutes, a crowd of civilians will step out those wooden doors and out into the open.] Blayze: The sound of a bittersweet ending plays a beautiful symphony into the winds but once Thursday Night is over..It will be the ashes of two poor corpses that will be blown into the skies. These are not lies that are being spoken, this time it is the cold harded truth. Scorpion & Jason Dread shall be the next victims to be restin with the angels..Same goes to good Ol' Enigma if he plans to overstep the boundaries [As the wooden doors open, Blayze pushes himself off the steps and walks off into the empty streets sooned to be filled by the religious followers of the Congregation Church..] Amen View Blayze 's Biography Back
