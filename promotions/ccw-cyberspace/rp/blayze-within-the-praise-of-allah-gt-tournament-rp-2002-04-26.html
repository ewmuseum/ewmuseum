Blayze - Within The Praise of Allah(GT Tournament RP)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Blayze visits the grave of his mentor and recalls his teachings before the Group Therapy tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Blayze Entitled: Within The Praise of Allah(GT Tournament RP) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:07:11 Location: Allah Shaheem's Gravestone This is a: Match RP [The sun falls as the entry into the nightlife finally makes its distinct arrival, a day of recuperation, relaxation and congratulation all faded off in preparation for a long night of rest and an up start to a new day. The stars shine bright amongst the glorious sky above all of our heads, each start representing the spirit of a human laid to rest and risen into a world of peace. For as the darkness appears does not mean all has been lost, but a continuation of living that is just entering a different phase. Stricken by emotion and also lack of remorse for everyone else, a proud man (to some, but public enemy # 1 to others) stand by the landmark of a human being that changed the way he viewed life. That landmark being the grave of Allah Shaheem, a true spiritual guide and father-like figure to a once trouble teen by the name of Jerome Terrance Jordan, nowadays going by the moniker of “Blayze”. A clear but breezy night, the wind chill flows through the shadows of the night and brushes off the leaves in the tree, creating a harmonic diversity. A deranged man, Blayze has put all his horrific acts behind him for one night to reminisce on the death of his mentor. All that is good was meant for him, Blayze showing his brother the ropes of the ring was meant for Allah, the tag titles recently won was meant for Allah and hopefully winning the Group Therapy Tournament will be the icing on the cake. The mind of Blayze might never be on the right track, it is possible that insanity has inhabited his cerebral vortex but for this silent night, he remembers, he puts everything aside that was ever wrong and focused only on what is positive and humane. When Blayze stated he was sacrificing a man for the sole purpose to engage in an act that would revive the spirit of Allah and heal his own inner strenght, was it meant as a way just to pick up the tag titles, or was it a real sacrifice and he is here to pay homage to what he accomplished. In his pale black hands, he holds up his half of the tag titles, with a wooden chained cross lying over the center of the belt. He kisses the cross and then kneels down to place the belt on a bed of roses spread across the front of the gravestone. After the long-lasting tear sheds from his eye, he rises to his feet makes a sign of the cross..] Blayze: Tears I shed, only meant for the ones that have a place in my ever-glowing heart. To you Allah I seem like a holy righteous soul carrier but to others I am nothing but the central magnet to hatred. I bring nothing but love, respect and emotion to your faded presence but when I enter the world I call CCW, pain, agony and deceit is what travels through out my mind’s path. [Blayze begins to close his eyes, suddenly he begins to hear voices and re-visions the moments he shared with Allah] Allah: Jerome, you must understand that guns are not the medium of defense in today’s society. I shall show you that you can still protect yourself from these bullies that bring fear to your soul. Come my son, it is time to show you the ropes… [Blayze remembers all that Allah has taught him that has made him who he is today. He begins to picture how Allah kept his spirits up and alive] Allah: Don’t give up Jerome, you must keep the strive alive and boost up that confidence level. Remember, evacuate the dull sense from your system and inherit the confidence and pride into your body, you will never regret it [And Blayze never did, for Group Therapy, more then just pride and confidence will be needed, but also strength, endurance, charisma and the use of the mind. With those factors put into place, only an unstoppable force-field of mind, body and soul combination will be enhanced towards Blayze. As the reminiscing stage ends, Blayze kneels down and picks up his tag title but leaves the thick cross behind as a sign of respect. He now leaves, walking down the path of the graveyard, staring into the sky and begins thinking to himself of what the future will hold come Sunday Night.] Blayze: Unknown individuals were chosen to tag with me to combine a team, which is suppose to last to the finals. With what my knowledge tells me, I might be the only sole survivor in my squadron. Do I trust those who are paired me? No. Do I care for their skills or attitude? No. Do I particularly like any of them? Far from yes, a definite No. If I am to proclaim my fame in this cruel gauntlet type event, then only my body and soul will guide me through it. Lockdown’s sacrifice of one Neurosis, as a gift to the spirit of Allah, my soul itself should now have enough healing strength to pull me to the finals and then slowly, into the World Title Match. I cannot comment on the other competitors due to the lack of knowledge on how they will perform but I can say that their outing will not be as successful as mine [As Blayze makes his way out of the exit gates of the graveyard, he is struck with an eerie feeling, which begins to send chills down his spine. He closes his eyes and vividly, a voice chants through his ears, through the wind, into his head] Voice: Don’t be foolish my son, why ignore the facts of your opposition and act like nothing can go wrong? You must be over-confident, your main focus is trying your best. Understand this Jerome, not matter how you fair or how much your are hated, you will still be considered my most cherished student. I say focus on your goal and push away the meaningless sacrifices and.. Blayze: But Allah.. Voice: But nothing, all you must do is achieve what you want to achieve and don’t be blinded by ignorance. My son, I will be there with you in soul and spirit so do not worry, you will be protected Blayze: Thank You Father.. [Blayze makes another sign of the cross and walks out, he sees that his opponents mean nothing and that all he cares about is making Allah proud. If he can excel past the “mystery men”, Guardian and Bryson, his goal will be just another stepping stone away. Only time will tell if glory will once again reign in the hands of Jerome “Blayze” Jordan] View Biography
