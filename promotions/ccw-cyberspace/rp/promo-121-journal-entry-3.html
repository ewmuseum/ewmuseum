Journal Entry #3 Scorpion CCW Promo #121 Date: 3-25-03 Location: New York City, New York Journal Entry #3 Currently I write to you as the world is in disarray. A war is going on. A war to free all Iraqi’s from the regime of Saddam Hussien. This time in my life has been very stressful. It brings up old times growing up on the streets where survival and knowing how to fight was a necessity; it was a way of life. Still is today. I lived in a battlefield for most of my life growing up in the toughest parts of the City. People were getting shot in front of my house daily. Murder, rape, you name it and it all happened where I lived sadly. I dealt with my fair share of gangs beating me up for money to buy drugs and at one point in my life I was part of the gang beating people up for money to buy drugs. Something I’m not happy on doing and never will be. As I look upon those days I still wonder to myself, “why did do that” or “what was I thinking?” It still boggles my mind today, maybe it was just the only way I coupe with living in an area like that. There were many, many battles that took place on the streets. Most took place at night while occasionally the beef between two gangs would happen directly on the spot in broad daylight. In front of children and citizens of that area. I remember when I was younger running into my room and shutting the door. I would pull down all the curtains and hide beside my bed. All that I could hear was the gang members cursing at each other, hearing the gun go off and then the sounds of someone giving out their last gasp of air as they were shot with a bullet. It was very scary for me growing up like that. I learned so much about life when I was young and not to take it for granted because you never knew when you could go. I could walk out of my house onto my steps and get shot by a nine millimeter from a drive by shooting. Times were hard, but in a positive way it made you stronger and more thoughtful about the world. But as I grew older I fell into the gang life. The power of controlling people and innocent people made the adrenaline flow into my body at such a young age. As I look back it sickness my stomach how psychic my feelings towards the thrill of power truly got me. Even when I was in a gang I wished I could get out of it. But I knew that the moment I left the gang I would get shot by every single one of those guys because they had felt I had betrayed them, that I thought I was higher then them. But I wasn’t I just wanted to get out, but I couldn’t so I stayed with what I was doing. Which was putting fear into people that lived in my area. During the time of me being in gang led to a lot of stressful times in my family. My Mother and Father would constantly kick me out of the house for doing what I was doing in the gangs. My Mother and Father never enjoyed picking me up from he police station and if I was in their position I wouldn’t be happy either. I know what I was doing was hurting my Mother and Father; I knew they wanted me to get out; I even wanted to get out. But I couldn’t I was stuck and life wasn’t going to get any better till I found a way to get out before it was too late. I didn’t want find myself resting six feet under. The beginning of my end in the gang happened unexpectedly. It all began when I met my soon to be wife Jennifer at one of my friends party. We exchanged phone numbers at the party and ended up going out. My time with her took away time I would be spending with the gang getting bust and causing hell. The time I spent with her was great and I was happy I met her. Our relationship began getting serious and my time with the gang began to slowly minimize. The turning point came at two different times. The first turning point was when Jennifer and I got married. All my boys were there and were happy for me getting married. They sort of realized that my time doing things with them was coming to an end, but we continued to hang out but not as much. The second turning point came when my son Toby came into the world. It was the greatest joy of my life. That was when the real ending for being in a gang ended and my boys got the feeling it was over. But I told them straight up soon after my son was born that I couldn’t get shot running around with them anymore. I needed to be with my wife and son, providing for them. Being there to help them out, I couldn’t be six feet under and not be there for my family. I was surprised with my boys reaction they took it all in stride and shook my hand. They told me that whenever I needed anything, just let them know. The gang life showed a positive spin on my life as well. That my friendship and solidarity with these men was genuine. That for the rest of my life they got my back and I got theirs. It brought a good thing in a bad situation. I have dealt with my fairshare of things I never wished happened nor or proud of. But out there was a war everyday and survival for everyday living. To everyone of our men and women fighting in the armed forces over in Iraq, I hope you all come okay and as soon as possible. I support you. View Scorpion 's Biography Back
