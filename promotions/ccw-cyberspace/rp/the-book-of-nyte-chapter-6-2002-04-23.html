The Book Of Nyte, Chapter 6

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Gabriel Nyte, on a mission in Bethlehem, fights off an ambush and learns of his next match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Gabriel Nyte Entitled: The Book of Nyte: Chapter 6 Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 19:15:16 Location: This is a: Match RP Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that in due time he may exalt you. Cast all your anxieties on him, for he cares about you. -1 Peter 5:6-7 [Mr. Nyte is currently on a mission in Israel. He expresses his military group's interests within the holy land. A crusade is ready to break out, the tensions are that high. The camera fades in on a lonely street, ridden with bullet holes from the trade of fire between the Israeli Defense Force, and the Palestinian Militants. We close in on Bethlehem. The church of the nativity to be exact. Gabriel's Altar Boyz are guarding the Friars that work and live there 24/7. The boyz are heavily armed. Ar-33 Assault Rifles are the weapon du-jour. We then head into a small room where we see Gabriel and a few friars in the room. Matthew, Mark, and John are in the room as well.] Friar: Brother Nyte, thank you for coming on such short notice. You have my sympathies for the loss of your base. Nyte: My base can be re-built. However, you people are our interests here in the Holy Land. The Christian Quarter in Jerusalem is in hiding because of all the bombers around. The Patriarch thanks you for your courage. Friar: If we live, you can tell him that he is welcome. [An altar boy enters the room with a smoking AR-33] Altar Boy: Sir, we were ambushed by militants. Matthew: How many? Altar Boy: At least five. Mark: They're mine. [Mark chambers his shotgun round and heads out.] Nyte: Godspeed, Mark. [A cell phone rings...from the direction of the green-robed aide in John. John heads to a dark corner of the room with his cell phone.] Friar: Man, this is not good. Defilers are in here...Our artifacts are being seized...Man... Nyte: Look, we must remain calm, and pray to the Lord that everything will be ok. Do not feel anxiety, the Lord will deliver us from.... BOOM! [A shotgun blast and rifle fire explode out of nowhere. Mark stumbles back into the room...He gets against a wall and slumps down. Matthew grabs his rifle, side-steps to the door and fires off about 15 rounds at the attackers. Matthew then closes the door again, then tends to Mark.] Matthew (To outside): ASSHOLE! Nyte: Matthew! Matthew: My apologies. JOHN! We need you over here. John: One Second. Matthew: NOW! John: Ok, Fine. Brother Nyte, I was just on the phone with our agent in America. You've been booked in a match against Ricky Coyote, and our mutual target has been selected on the same team as you for the Pay-Per-View. Now, let's see this wound, eh? [John walks over to Mark to have a look at the wounds. Gabriel looks puzzled.] Friar: Brother Nyte? Are you alright? Nyte: Yes. Devyl in the Group Therapy tournament...on the same team no less. I can't touch her then...nor later...I don't know. Ricky Coyote...The Houseshow champion...how trivial. You claim a title that no longer exists, and yet you have no solace within your mind, nor your spirit. Being subservient to a cruel and unusual master has warped your fragile spirit. I cannot guarantee a spiritual repair...but I can guarantee a physical beating enroute to that. Ricky...You should be wary of what you do in that ring...for it will lead to your downfall...in nomine Patri, et Filii, et Spiritu Sancti, amen. Friar: Oh man... Nyte: What? Friar: I have to go to the restroom. Nyte: John, give me your rifle, I will accompany you, and make my way to the airport while I'm at it. [Nyte and the Friar get up and out of the room. It's a lot like Half-Life during this, Gabe side stepping around corridors, and he reaches the restroom, drops off the Friar and heads away. The scene fades to black.] View Biography
