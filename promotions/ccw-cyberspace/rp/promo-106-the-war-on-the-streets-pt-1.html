The War on the Streets (pt. 1)

24 March 2003

The War on the Streets (pt. 1) Scorpion CCW Promo #106 Date: 3-24-03 Location: New York City, New York :::: Scorpion goes to sit on the couch after returning home from another event, bandage up from Lockdown. Scorpion grabs a beer and turns on the television as he begins to watch the NCAA Men’s Tournament. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Ah, some time to relax and not be bothered by the world. I love March Madness! :::: Scorpion is sitting on the couch watching the NCCA Men’s Basketball tournament when his phone rings. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Jesus Christ this better not be telemarketers. (picking up the phone) Hello? Mysterious Voice: I’m free. Scorpion: Excuse me? Mysterious Voice: I said I’m free. Scorpion: Who the hell is this? Mysterious Voice: Your worst nightmare? Scorpion: Is this some joke? Mysterious Voice: You thought you could get revenge on me? You got another thing coming, I’m free and I have back-up better then before. Scorpion: Who is this? What do you mean? Mysterious Voice: Let’s just say your friend Mike isn’t doing so well right now. Scorpion: Leave Mike alone. Mysterious Voice: Oh don’t worry about him; I think you should be worried about yourself. :::: The voice on the other line hangs up. :::: Scorpion: Excuse me? Hello? Hello? God dam it I better give Mike a call, it’s probably him doing some sick joke to me. :::: Scorpion dials Mike’s cell phone. :::: Scorpion: Yo Mike, was that you playing some joke to me? :::: Laughing is heard on the other line. :::: Scorpion: Hello? Mike you there? Mysterious Voice: No it’s me again. Scorpion: Who is this? Mysterious Voice: You’ll have to wait and find out. Scorpion: Where is Mike? Mysterious Voice: Mike is in a bad way and will continue to be until you come to where I tell you to be. Scorpion: You let go of him right now. Mysterious Voice: Don’t tell me what to do, I’ll kill him right now and you won’t even know who did it. Scorpion: What do you want from me? Mysterious Voice: I want to have a meeting with you. Scorpion: A meeting? Mysterious Voice: Yes Scorpion a meeting, you and me. Talking about problems between you and I. I want to iron those problems out so you come and join me on my side. Scorpion: Your side? Who the hell is this? Mysterious Voice: Scorpion don’t be foolish I can’t tell you that because you wouldn’t come and join me. You just do as I say and your friend will not be harmed. Scorpion: If I have meeting with you Mike won’t be harmed, correct? Mysterious Voice: Correct, just you and me talking business. Scorpion: What kind of business? Mysterious Voice: You’ll see Scorpion; all your questions will be answered once you join me to this meeting. Scorpion: All right I’ll talk to you. Mysterious Voice: Good, good indeed. I’ll be looking for to it. Scorpion: Where are we going to meet? Mysterious Voice: We will meet at the dry docks at nine thirty at night. Come with nobody and don’t bring any weapons. You do Mike will get hurt, got that? Scorpion: No problem as long as you don’t touch Mike. Mysterious Voice: I think that’s the least of your worries. Until then Scorpion…chow! :::: The mysterious voice hangs up the phone. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Christ sakes I can’t sit down for two minutes and watch some good ol’ college basketball. This guy better not be some booker looking for money from Mike again because that bastard is going to pay his own way out of this one. But then again, I got a bad feeling about this. I know something is up. View Scorpion 's Biography Back
