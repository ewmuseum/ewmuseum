Life (Part Four) Dan Logan CCW Promo #102 Date: 23.03.03 Location: The Park The grass in tender under foot as he makes his way to their meeting place, the time is 10:30pm and he would soon arrive if he is to arrive at all. Looking about him he sees a reflection in the river beside them and an eerie silence encompasses the entire park. The moon beats down, shining a light that almost radiating the sun's heat from about it. Twelve hours ago Dan had been in the car once again travelling to the American border, this would be his final stop before making it on Monday night. He has work to do and finding Emma is top priority upon his list. He can still smell her ebony hair as he thinks of her, the musty perfume upon her body as he lay down beside all those times before. Why she had left still alluded him as he had done nothing wrong in his eyes, well in truthfulness he had, he had taken more interest within his job than he had ever with her and when not working he spent all his time with Chris. How could she not understand though, Chris is his child and his illness means that when he can Dan wishes to be with him as much as possible. Had she no remorse for what he was going through, the doctors had said it hadn't been her fault but still he blamed her. When drugs are involved it always becomes the parents fault and as Dan wasn't the carrier of the child it left only one person. As Dan looks around still daydreaming about things that he cannot change any longer a tall man with thick eyebrows walks upon behind him and taps him upon his shoulder. Dan spins around about to hit the man who has touched him so when he grins and laughing he speaks out... 'You turned up then' His gray face lights up within the moonlight leaving the scar upon his face shrouded in a deep blackness. The man he speaks to is different his golden hair shines brightly even in the night sky and though darker his green eyes still mirror emeralds. It was one week ago that Dan had been informed that she was in Pittsburgh, a lead that had turned out to be nothing more than just to late. She had moved by the time he had arrived and all he walked into was an empty flat still smelling of her and holding only few amounts of evidence that they had ever been there. The two men embrace like old friends laughing at one another as they realise that there isn't much that has changed about them in the past twelve years. It had been that long, twelve years since they Trevor and Sam had all been together that last time upon the brigade floor as Dan finally left the army. Yet still the days seem almost to have flown past and neither has changed at all. 'Yeah, an old buddy asked me to come down 'ere so I thought about it an' decided to come...' They hadn't always seen eye to eye; David had always had a way with women and usually ended up taking Dan's from him. Yet they knew that they could rely on one another like they could rely on nobody else. For their friendship had been tested in the heat of battle and it had past through the blase unscathed and ready to be tested once again. 'Will you be staying David? Will you stand by my side in my time of need?' 'Yes, I owe you...and let's be totally honest. You need me' 'I have gotta say yeah, I do need you sometimes' David grins at Dan and the two of them move toward the park bench seating themselves down they turn and face one another. Dan sighs and David shakes his head, he had fore told that she would run on him and now she had. What does Dan expect for him to do? He cannot turn back the clocks of time and alter what she has done nor would he if he had the chance. She had never been happy while she had been with him and this was only proof as such. She had constantly been with others while he had been away on exercise with the regiment, even David had his turn with the fiery little Emma when he had got his chance. It wasn't that Dan was disliked more that Emma could charm anyone into bed and usually wanted anyone but Dan. Her constant abuse of steroids and amphetamines had done nothing for her physical appearance yet she still holds in many eyes that uncatchable gleam and never ending desire. What ever she wanted she was given by Dan yet he could not give to her the one thing she desired the most. He could not give her more drugs, for Dan's love of her was strong. So strong that he refused to allow her to take anything while near him and this was in all truthfulness probably the reason she could never love him back. 'I need your help' At least Dan had come to someone about it but he had come to the wrong person, David has no idea of where he could find her. You see he could never find her when they were in the same camp let alone now when she has the money to walk around and go where the hell she wants. No, Dan has come to the wrong person... 'I know you don't know where she is so I won't ask you that. I do know though that you will have some idea where I can go to get someone that will know...please I'm begging you take me to him...' It wasn't direct help then that he seeks, no. You see David can help with this much then because yes it is true there will always be someone that knows something about her whereabouts she was to high profile even when low profile to keep quiet to everyone. David nods his head at Dan and taking out his phone he dials a phone number and begins to speak... 'Barney' 'Yeah its me' 'What do you mean your busy...listen to me now!' 'I need a favor, yes you will do it!' 'Why?!' 'Because I said you'll do it that is why!' It seems almost fruitless for David to be speaking to this 'Barney' upon the phone and that he will never accept that David is asking for his help, well maybe not asking but still... 'We're looking for someone' 'I want your help to find her that is all!' 'What do you mean you don't do that anymore...' 'BOLLOXS!' 'You were in England two months ago looking for me. Don't tell me that you don't do it any more' 'If you want I can just speak to Thomas...' 'I thought you might see it that way...' ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
