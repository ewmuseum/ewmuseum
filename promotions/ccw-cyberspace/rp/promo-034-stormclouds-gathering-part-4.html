Stormclouds Gathering Part 4 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #34 Date: february 24th, 2003 Location: Seattle, Washington East Dundee Cemetery 1997 {The sky and the clouds floating over a distant horizon bespeak an illusory peace. A haunting wail rises into the air, sending startled birds aloft. As they whirl and twirl in a flurry of wings, the same piercing cry repeats, a hurtful cry of grief, echoing surreally among the tombstones.} {Down in the graveyard, that same illusory peace continues. Moss and lichen cling to the old tombstones. The mausoleums and other large grave markers stand in shadowed silence. The bodies of men, women and children entombed all around forevermore. Many lie here, having given up their mortal coil for many a diverse reason. But what does it matter? The history books don't have a word to say about any of them.} {A man in a formal suit is found picking his way amongst the graves. He slowly and carefully makes his way through until he comes upon a second person, Lacey Skye Morris, who is clad in an equally formal dress. A bouquet of flowers lies by Lacey's side as she kneels in the grass in front of a tombstone. Tracks of tears streak her beautiful face. The haunted cry of grief came from her throat. Now, the only sounds in the graveyard are the wind and the soft footsteps of the man as he approaches Lacey from behind.} {The man in the formal suit is Doug Burger. He stands above his girl friend, desolated by her grief.} {The wind swirls through the cemetery. Lacey cannot find her voice for a moment as she feels Doug's presence behind her. When she speaks, she has regained some of her composure.} Lacey: She knew so much.... And was so full of life. Everything she ever did.... She always thought of others.... Never put herself first. {She tries to say more, but can only make a gesture which means.... 'lost'.} {A short while later... Near the outskirts of the cemetery.} {Silhouetted against the sky, Lacey and Doug walk into an empty field where the graveyard has not yet encroached. It is Doug who speaks first, but it is only a voice over, as memory doesn't recall the exact specifications of this moment. He speaks in a simple way, a sort of prayer.} Doug: {voice over} Don't you ever think you've lived like that? Full of life? Always putting yourself before others? This is a terrible tragedy, Lacey, but she lives on within you. You hold her memory in your heart. And that speaks to the testament of your friendship. Lacey Morris's College Dorm Room 1997 {The room is like every other typical dorm room. Posters adorn the walls, stuffed animals lounge in odd corners and places, and discarded clothes lie in multiple piles on the floor and bed.} {Lacey in lying on her stomach on the bed, her head nearly buried into her pillow. Doug sits in a chair nearby, holding her hand.} Doug: I know you two were very close. But you couldn't stop her from dying, Lacey. We all do. Man kills.... Accidents happen every day.... Nature follows it's course.... Lacey: I don't care who does the killing or how people die. I'm sick of it... Sick of constantly losing the people I care for. Sick of death. Doug: You can't stop living. Lacey: I didn't ask for your permission, Doug. {Lacey gives Doug a stubborn look.} {She then closes her eyes and squeezes them shut, trying to stop more tears from coming.} {Doug squeezes her hand and reaches out with his other one, brushing back her hair out of her face. He continues, smoothing out her hair and scratching her head, doing his best to comfort her.} Doug: I know you're already aware of this and don't need to be reminded, but she is in a better place. Lacey: {opens her eyes} You're right. I don't need to be reminded. Father Flanigan and I had a good long talk after the funeral.... Doug: Really? And? Lacey: He told me that I already knew this and didn't need to be reminded, but Cara was in a better place now. {They both smile at each other.} Doug: It's pointless to blame yourself for her death. {squeezes her hand again} There was nothing you could have done to prevent it. Lacey: I know.... {squeezes back} But I keep replaying it in my head. I keep thinking that, if only I was quicker.... If only I had reached her in time before the bus hit her.... Doug: {softly} It wasn't meant to be. Lacey: {softly, grief-stricken} I know.... Doug: And you can't give up. You can't stop living. Lacey: I know.... The Loft of the Antique Shop Seattle, Washington 2003 {Jonathan sits in the couch. He has been staring off into space, obviously in deep thought. He glances up as Lacey enters.} Lacey: You okay? {He looks up into her eyes and shakes his head.} Jonathan: {gruffly} No.... {Lacey sits on the couch next to him.} Jonathan: I'm an artist. I'm supposed to have this tremendous imagination. But I never once thought that I would be tracked and hunted by a cold-blooded killer. {Saying it out loud has cleared the air for him a bit. He manages to give her a wane smile. She gives him one in return.} Jonathan: Has Christine gone? Lacey: Yes.... Hunting. Hours ago. Now I have to go. She's found Quartre. Jonathan: How do you know? Lacey: {smiles} She sent me a text message on my cell phone. Jonathan: Lacey.... Don't go. Let 'Damsel' handle it. Lacey: You know I can't do that. I gave her my word that I would help. A Vacant Lot {Lacey pulls up in her rental car and quickly gets out. Something is terribly wrong here. She looks around. No sign of Damsel. A gnawing doubt begins. She reenters her car, turns it around, and roars away.} The Antique Store {Lacey pulls up in front of the store and exits the car quickly. She is suddenly very frightened for Jonathan.} {Lacey edges her way into the store. She looks around feverishly for Jonathan. He is not there.} {She moves towards Jonathan's studio where she hears the sound of his blow torch. Lacey enters the studio, her instincts screaming danger.} Lacey: Jonathan.... {And there in front of her with his back to her, is Jonathan, with his wielder's mask on, blow torch in hand. He stands in front of a piece of sculpture which is draped over with a sheet.} {'Jonathan' turns to her, taking off his wielding mask and Lacey finds herself face-to-face with Quartre.} Quartre: Hi hon.... Want to see what I've been working on? {He pulls the sheet off the 'sculpture' revealing a bound and gagged Jonathan. He is tied into the chair. There is a gag around his mouth, bruises and cuts all around his face. Quartre holds the blow torch in his hand, smiling.} Quartre: I think the face could use a little more work... Don't you? {Lacey takes a concerned step forward and Quartre increases the size of the of the blow torch flame.} Quartre: Uh-uh.... Not too close.... I'll kill him if you take another step. {He increases the flame ever so slightly more. Lacey looks as if she's about to comply, then swiftly kicks out and dislodges the nearby hose on the floor, shutting off the gas to the torch.} Lacey: What is your real game, Quartre....? You could have easily killed Jonathan at any time.... {Quartre reaches inside his trenchcoat and brandishes his gun.} Quartre: Obviously.... But if you haven't realized it yet.... I've found you to be a far more appetizing target! {Quartre raises his gun and fires, causing Lacey to leap out of the way. She hits the ground hard. Rolling to her feet, she regains them, only to take a hard elbow to the forehead, which sends her stumbling back into the wall. Like a cat, Quartre leaps behind Jonathan and places his gun to Jonathan's temple.} Quartre: You know what some of my 'colleagues' call me? 'Quartre the Cat'! Because I always play with my victims first. {Lacey takes another step forward.} Quartre: Ah-ah! Don't spoil the fun. Be good and I'll leave him alive.... This time. I want you to think about it now.... Think about how I can get him anytime I want. Yes, I can see you're already thinking about it. {So saying, he lunges toward Lacey, attempting to strike her with the butt of his gun. Lacey successfully blocks the initial attack, but leaves herself wide open to a left handed backhand that sends her flying backwards onto the floor.} Quartre: Ta, ta for now, kids! {Quartre disappears through the door, leaving Lacey to pick herself up off the ground and to check on Jonathan....} View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
