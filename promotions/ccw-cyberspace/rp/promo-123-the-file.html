The File Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #123 Date: 06 Apr 03 Location: Jack's Study Jack Manson sits alone in his study. To his left stands a small filing cabinet. 3 drawers, marked from A to Z. The top drawer stands open a few inches. In front of him on the deep red oak desk is a file, a dossier. Jack has just finished looking through it. He places a piece of paper neatly away and closes the file. He pushes it away from him on the desk. The camera spins around to reveal the name on the front of the file. Written in neat letters in the top right hand corner is the name…. Enigma. Jack sighs lightly as he brings his hands together and rests them on his chin in a steeple. “Enigma... you sure have the right name. It puzzles me why your first title defence would be against the man who had you beat last week. Oh I know what your thinking….you have the gold…you won the match right? Wrong…you know exactly what happened Enigma. You know you could never me beat so you got a little help” Jack rubs his hands together tightly and slowly as he speaks. “But I blame myself. I did not have the time to study you. I was……unprepared shall we say. And in one moment, you got one up on me. Well done. But I ask you; where is your friend now? Where is the one that robbed me? Is he celebrating with you? Is he there sharing in your glory? Or is he laying in bed gasping for air? Is he wishing he had never done what he did? Is he maybe contemplating his future as he struggles for air, hoping that the swelling in his neck will subside allowing him to breath again without the aid of a tube up his nose?” Jack reaches to his right and opens a drawer. “And what of you Enigma? What are you thinking? Are you sitting there smiling on the outside whilst running scared within? Are you glad that it was not you who felt their last breath leave his body as you fell limp to the mat? I think we both know.” With the drawer open, Jack peers inside, he spots what he is looking for and reaches inside. He pulls out a stamp. “But what happens now Enigma? Chapter one is over. Now we both know what each other is capable of. Where do we take it from here? We’ve had fire, thumb tacks, announce tables, weapons of every shape and size and some would say it’s just the beginning. Some would say the fun hasn’t really begun. I say I’m just getting started” Jack turns the rubber stamp over in his hand and removes the plastic cover from the underside. “”But let me ask you something Enigma. Do you know the eyes that watch you? Do you know exactly where and when? Do you know where the attack originates? Do you know how many and why? For now you have the mark Enigma. You have made yourself a target and I believe you don’t understand the consequences. I believe Enigma, that you have no idea what you have coming to you. You are unprepared, you are frightened, and you are alone. But you wont be so alone this Thursday Enigma, no you shall all the company you wish. For I will be there at The Staples Centre in Los Angeles this Thursday. I have a very busy night indeed; and if you are good boy maybe you’ll be invited to the party. Maybe, just maybe, we will issue you a very special invitation. An invitation to the start of something new. What? You ask….. Just wait and see; I know you’ll like it. Jack grins the same maniacal smile we have seen in weeks passed. He brings the stamps down hard on the front of Enigma’s dossier. The scene fades with a close-up of the file. Which now has, in large red ink on the front the word.... 'PENDING' View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
