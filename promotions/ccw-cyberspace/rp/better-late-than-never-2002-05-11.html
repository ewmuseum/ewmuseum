Better Late Than Never

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A violent scene in which The Supreme Machine beats his pet Chang the Mongolian Giant with a barbed-wire pipe, ending with Chang dressing in baby clothes.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Chang The Mongolian Giant Entitled: Better Late Than Never Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 15:20:36 Location: The Pen This is a: Match RP What the hell did you think you were doing last Thursday? A long steel pipe wrapped in barbed wire comes crashing down on the bald skull of Chang the Mongolian Giant, ripping great chunks out of the 24th wonder of the world's over sized cranium. Letting Fat Ass Phil Burner dress you as a baby?!? Again the pipe comes crashing down on Chang's head. As the blood begins to flow down his forehead, Chang just whimpers and tries to retreat further into the corner of the room. Alas, there is nowhere to run as The Supreme Machine continues his vicious attack on his retarded pet. You will NEVER embarass me like this again! DO YOU UNDERSTAND!?! This time the barbed wire bites hard into Chang's shoulders, tearing large pieces of flesh away as The Supreme Machine prepares to once again swing the deadly pipe at Chang. If you don't start performing up to your abilities, I'm going to have to get you put down, do you hear me? I'm going to have you sent to the damned glue factory or I'll get you made into pet food..I don't care! BUT I'm not having you being the tool that that redneck, Burner, uses to get to me. I won't stand for it, do you hear me you stupid animal!?! Chang tries in vain to protect himself as The Supreme Machine rains down blow after blow with the barbed wire covered pipe. The sickening attack continues unabated as the five time CCW Heavyweight Champion makes his rage felt on his hapless pet. Is this how you repay me after all I've done for you? I saved you from your stinking tribe in the Gobi Desert. They were going to leave you strung out to die, remember? I could have left you there but no, I saved your life. I brought you into CCW. I made you a household name around the world...and this is the thanks I get? No, I think not! Chang is still trying to get away from his irate master but there is nowhere to go. The relentless attack continues. I made you! Don't forget that you dumb animal! I made you and if I choose to I can destroy you just as easily. You are not going to embarass me again. Finally the beating stops. The Supreme Machine steps back and looks at the badly beaten Mongolian Giant with distain. There was a time when you could have taken this sort of a beating and gotten straight up. Not anymore. You have become a joke! This is your last chance Chang. You don't want to fail me again this week. I promise you the consequences will be far worse than this if you do. You disgust me! With that, The Supreme Machine drops the flesh covered pipe to the straw covered floor and turns to leave the dimly lit room. As he opens the heavy metal door that holds Chang in his pen he turns one last time and addresses the beaten giant. I mean it! Fail me again and you are history! He leaves the room and closes the door behind him. As the sound of the locks being closed on the other side of the door echoes through Chang's pen, The Mongolian Giant slowly crawls through the hay towards one of the corners of the room, where his self styled nest of hay lays stacked in the corner. The blood is flowing freely from the hundreds of open wounds over his massive body as Chang finally makes it to his nest and lays there in a panting, bleeding heap. After a few seconds Chang slowly starts delving into the hay as if looking for something. His breathing is laboured and he moans softly in pain as his huge hands continue searching through the nest. After what seems like an eternity he finally removes something form the hay and holds whatever it is tightly to his chest as he begins rocking back in forth on his haunches, continuing his soft moaning. As the camera begins to zoom in on the beaten giant, he takes whatever it was that he had hidden in the hay and places it over his badly bleeding head. It appears to be an oversized babies bonnet. Next he delves into the nest again and comes out with what appears to be an oversized diaper which he proceeds to put on himself. Finished he returns to sitting on his haunches. He cuts a bizarre figure sitting there in the hay, bleeding profusely and dressed in baby clothes. Then suddenly he smiles, his one huge tooth gleaming in what little light penetrates his pen. His face now fills the screen and he looks vacantly down the camera. G-G-G-G-GOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO View Biography
