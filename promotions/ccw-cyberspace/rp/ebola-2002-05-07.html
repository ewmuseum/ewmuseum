Ebola

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short monologue in which Danny Bryson addresses his upcoming title defense against Ebola, wandering a nightscape.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Ebola..... Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 11:25:07 Location: Who kn ows.... This is a: Match RP *The scene opens on a nightscape. The camera is panning alongside Bryson, who is wandering down the street. He turns to the camera, eyes gleaming with something that may be madness, and begins...* 'Ebola....you and I have yet to meet. We have danced in the same halls, we have played the same games, starred in the same shows. Yet we have yet to meet. You, Ebola, Joey, whatever you are calling yourself this week....you, I think, know more than anyone what it means to feel the fire. What it means to you to step into the ring and dominate your opponent. What it feels like to attack a man until he can defend himself no longer. Ebola, the Inferno burns in you, as it does in me. You know what I fel like, you have the same drives, the same will, the same NEED. Thas why, I hope you can understand, when I say that I NEED to beat you down Thursday night. I NEED you pin your shoulders to the floor. I NEED to keep the gold. You know that this is more than a want, more than a hunger, more, even, than an urge. I need to beat you down.....and I'm sorry' *The camera shoots off, leaving Bryson to wander and think Fade out* View Biography
