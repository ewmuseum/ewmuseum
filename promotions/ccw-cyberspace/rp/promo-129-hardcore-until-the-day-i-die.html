Hardcore Until The Day I Die.....

April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hardcore Until The Day I Die..... 'The Angel of Death' Enigma CCW Promo #129 Date: Location: (The scene begins inside whats seems to be an office... Its pretty typical... You see a nice black marble desk and big windows behind the desk going out onto a nice view of the city... In front of the desk we see Enigma sitting down... He is sitting there not saying a word just stareing to the ground... He he in his street gear consisting of black jeans, a white tank-top and a black leather sleevless trench coat... Enigma's Hardcore title is laying on top of the desk... Suddenly the door opens and in walks a man wearing a medical uniform... He seems to be a doctor... Enigma looks up at him not saying a word... The doctor extends his hand to Enigma) Doctor: Hello, my name is Dr. Walsh.... (Enigma dosent even shake the poor mans hand showing him no respect what so ever) Enigma: Lets get one thing straight... The only reason I'm here is cause the front office of CCW thinks it would be best if I got checked out by a doctor and they threatened to fire me if I didnt... So you took you're little x-rays, you did what you had to do... So whats the results... Doctor: Well umm, you're right arm seems to be healing fine... You had a little cartilege damage but nothing to serious... Enigma: Yeah my arm feels fine... I dont even think I need this stupid cast anymore... Doctor: Well you're going to have to wear the cast for another couple of weeks to let you're arm properly heal... You're ribs are slowly healing... One of you're ribs is still broken and may require surgery to repair... Enigma: I dont need surgery... My ribs are fine... Doctor: And you have a minor concussion... I suggest you take a month or two to recover from all of this.... Enigma: Take a month or two off? Have you lost you're mind... I'm the CCW Hardcore Champion... I cant go taking vacations whenever I please... I've got a job to do... And I've got some un-finished business I need to take care of... Doctor: Well I believe that you need to take some time off... You're concussion can get far worse... I can't give you medical clearence to wrestle with you're numerous injuries... Enigma: You dont seem to understand do you... I'm perfectly fine... I need to finish what I started... Doctor: I'm sorry Enigma, I just cant... (Enigma lunges out of his chair and he grabs Dr.Walsh by the throat and he pulls him over the desk... Enigma pulls Dr.Walsh to his face by his throat and he begins to speak with great intensity in his voice) Enigma: Listen to me Dr.Walsh... This is about more than injuries and what not... I have to go out there and wrestle... And I cant let anything get in my way... So you can either sign my papers and give me medical clearence to wrestle, or I'll make you sign those papers with you're own blood... The choice is yours... (Enigma lets go of Dr.Walsh... He is gasping for air...) Doctor: Alright (panting and breathing heavily).... I'll give you medical clearence to wrestle... Enigma: I'm glad we understand eachother Dr.Walsh... I'll expect the papers to be sent to CCW headquarters ASAP... And if you even think of trying to screw me over, I'll come find you... And next time I wont let go of you're neck... I'll keep squeezing until you're eyes pop right out of you're head... Is that clear... Doctor: ..... Yeah...... Enigma: Good seeing you again Dr.Walsh... (Enigma grabs his hardcore title which was placed on Dr. Walsh's desk and he walks out of the room... The camera focuses on Dr.Walsh face... He seems quite scared, his eyes are lost in thought... As he fears what may happen if he dosent do as Enigma says... Now the camera fades out and we fade back in... We are inside Enigma's 1994 black Mustang Cobra... Enigma sits in the driver's seat with the window rolled down... His hardcore title is laying on the passenger seat... Soflty playing in the backround we can hear 'Suicidal Dream' by Silverchair...) Enigma: Jack Manson... At Revolutions we had what was arguably the best macth of the night... We stole the show... But its a shame... I thought you would have taken you're loss like a man... Instead you cry and complain about how the ref made a fast count... Do you think I give two shits about some ref that you put in a hospital bed... I couldnt care less... All I've heard from you since the Revolution's PPV is excuse after excuse... And I'm sick and tired of it... If there is one thing I cant stand, its bitching and complaining... And thats all you seem to be good at Manson... So on LDT you're gonna get a second opportunity to defeat me and I get and opportunity at shuting you up... But you'll probably find some other excuse... Whats you're excuse gonna be this time? My underwear was too tight and it cut off the circulation to my brain and I couldnt think properly... *shakes his head in disgust*... Enigma: You say that you had no time to study me... You ask me where my friend is??? He wasnt my friend... He was nothing to me... You took out you're frustrations on him for you're loss... Well that was you're choice... And you say that now he is laying in some hospital bed... Well thats good for him... Who gives a fu*k??? You think you some how hurt my feeling by beating up some referee??? *fake crying* Oh no... How could you do such a thing... Beating up a referee its just so wrong! *back to straight face* Do you really think I give a shit about if he lives or dies??? Manson, you obviously dont know nothing about me... You dont know what I'm capable of... Do you think they call me a Hardcore Legend for no reason??? Its because I do whatever I gotta do to survive... I've spent my whole life just trying to survive... I've come across numerous guys like you... I've beaten guys like you... And I'm gonna probably end up beating alot more guys like you... See Jack, what you dont seem to understand is you're little mind games dont work on me... You cant mess with a man's emotions if he has none... (Enigma starts up his car... The Mustangs engine roars loudly... Enigma then lights up a cigarette... He inhales off the cigarette... Then he continues to speak) Enigma: Jack... Dont flatter yourself... Dont even think for a moment that I fear you... You say that I'm glad that it wasnt me laying in a hospital bed instead of that ref... You say that as if I've never been in a hospital bed before... I dont fear you Jack... In fact, there isnt much I fear in life... Except for seeing Rosie O'Donnel in a thong *shivers*.... (Enigma puts the car in gear) Enigma: 48 hours from now we finish what we started at Revolutions... One more time Jack you will step into my world of Hardcore Hell... The outcome will be no different... I'm gonna walk out with my hand raised... And you're gonna be laying in a pool of you're own blood... Its that simple... See you soon Jack..... (Enigma takes off quickly leaving a patch of tire behind as the camera slowly fades to black) View 'The Angel of Death' Enigma 's Biography Back
