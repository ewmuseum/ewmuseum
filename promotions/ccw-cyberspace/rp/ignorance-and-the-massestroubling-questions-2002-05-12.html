Ignorance and the Masses....Troubling Questions...

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A candle-lit promo in which Venneman Pendragon complains about being unrecognized in the federation and warns Tristan Matthew about the coming Monday.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Venneman Pendragon Entitled: Ignorance and the Masses....Troubling Questions... Written in Sunday, May 12, 2002, 06:20:56 Location: This is a: Interview (Venneman Pendragon sits motionless, his countenance shadowed by the half-light of a candle. Even in the poor light his eyes burn with intensity. He shakes his head as he begins speaking, his words tinged with what may be regret, but what is probably angered irony...) Venneman: Why do people question that which they do not know? Why must ignorance always be the guide? I have chosen to illumine my past and I am still questioned. I, who have downed countless multitudes of men. Some were common; some were greater than all the so-called warriors where I now find myself. Three years in solitude answered me many questions, but to one I still do not know the answer...Why do men question that which is obviously beyond them; that which is so much greater than they? The sum of a man is added not only by his words, but also by his actions...In my short time in this new place, I have questioned many, yet obscurity still shrouds me. Do people fear the unknown? I realize that, being new to the CCW I am merely given fodder to destroy early on. I can accept that. What, though, does it take to be recognized? If I break a far lesser man in my first CCW appearance, will I gain recognition? Or will the so-called 'legends' of the fed scoff and tell me I have not wrestled anyone who is for real yet? What will likely occur is that these so-called superstars will ignore Pendragon, and they will never se him coming. (Venneman chuckles as he goes on...) Venneman: And so, I am brought back to my question...Why are people misguided by ignorance? Surely those high in the ranks, especially those with the gold that I covet, must realize that I am coming for them. Perhaps they think I will become bogged down with wrestlers like Tristan 'I have no idea where I came from nor what I talk about' Matthew, but surely they can see that I am above that....Three things drive me... 1. The lust of gold. 2. Honor for my name, and 3. An appetite for destruction. You see, I want to punish anyone who even believes they can stand in the same ring as me. I want to twist limbs and break backs. Each time someone cries out from the pain that I inflict upon him shall be a testament to my superiority. (Venneman shakes his head...) Venneman: Some men have called me egotistical. They say I am nothing but a braggart. For each who has uttered these lies, I have destroyed five, including the original transgressor. Make no mistake. I do have an ego, but the things that I now share are truths. These are not braggings of some incipient rambler. These are the statements of one who would be king had the world not moved on. I have been told I am stuck in the past...My attitude does not fit a modern world. In a way, I agree. I wish I were in the past. In the past, there were a few great men such as I. In the world today, I am alone. Destined to rise above all, expected to be greater than the masses. Cursed to carry an attitude and a way of life that the world has no room for...No room because the bearer of the attitude, me, is too great. (Venneman glares at the camera and drops his voice to nearly a whisper...) Venneman: Be sure to watch on Monday...The winds bring change and there is a new sting to the air. The first to fall will be Matthews...Remember what you see....The Last Crusade comes for you as well... (With that, Venneman blows out the candle and we are left in darkness...) View Biography
