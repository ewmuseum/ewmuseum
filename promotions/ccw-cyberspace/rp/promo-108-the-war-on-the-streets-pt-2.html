The War on the Streets (pt. 2) Scorpion CCW Promo #108 Date: 3-24-03 Location: New York City, New York :::: Nine P.M., Scorpion is standing in front of the dry docks wearing a leather jacket keeping himself warm from the sea breeze. A black Cadillac Escalade pulls up in front of Scorpion. A huge man steps out of the Escalade and walks over to the door behind the driver’s side. Out walks a baldhead man wearing a long black leather coat. :::: Vinny: Let me introduce myself, my name is Vinny Grazino. I head many gangs on the streets of New York. It’s a pleasure to meet a street legend such as you Scorpion. Scorpion: Thanks, I wouldn’t actually call myself a street legend nor am I proud of my past but that doesn’t matter right now. What does matter is where the hell my friend is. Vinny: You wanna know where your pal Mike is? Scorpion: Yeah that’s why I’m here. Vinny: He’s being held hostage currently at an undisclosed location. Scorpion: Why is he being hostage? Vinny: Two reasons, number one I’m going to control his gang now so I can be the most powerful man on the streets. Second, I’m the cousin of the man you helped but in jail, Corky Stewart. Scorpion: Who the hell do you think you are trying to take control of the streets? Vinny: I’m the streets worst nightmare Scorpion. I’m going to do something my cousin couldn’t do and that is being the most powerful man on the streets ever. I have dismantled more gangs then I can count while putting them under my control. Scorpion: So why do you want me? Vinny: I want you to help me control the streets, I know you’re a mastermind at what you used to do and I could use your help. Scorpion: No man, I’m done with the streets, I don’t wanna be pulled back in. Vinny: Then your friend Mike will stay hostage. Scorpion: Are you threatening me? Vinny: Yeah you can call it that, but I call it more of a promise then anything else. Scorpion: Now you’re making a mistake with me. Vinny: Oh am I Scorpion? Let me remind you who I am, I’m the most powerful man on the streets, I can have anyone kill you or your friend at my command anytime I want. You want to talk about mistakes? If I were you I’d join up with me before it’s too late. For your own good and your family’s good. Scorpion: You keep my family out of this. Vinny: Corky told me that was a soft spot in your heart. You join with me your family will be safe, you don’t your family will face hell from my gangs and I. Scorpion: You will not touch my family you got that? Or you will deal with me. Remember one thing Vinny. I’m the toughest son of a bitch walking New York City still whether or not. There are people out that are scared shitless of me. Vinny: I’m not scared of street has been. I look to the future Scorpion and the future is for me to control the gangs in New York City, giving me greater control then any other man or woman in this city. Scorpion: Is this what this is about? Power? Vinny: You damn right it’s all about power and nothing else. Scorpion: I can tell your from Corky’s family, your abbessed of control everyone and everything. But just like Corky, you two will fall victim to me and find your ass in jail being the bitch of every single “Bubba” in the pen. Vinny: Oh really? Scorpion: You damn right. Vinny: Does that mean we got our own little war here on the streets? Scorpion: You damn right you got a war, I’m not going to lie down and let you control the gangs in this city. Vinny: We shall see, but in the meantime let’s see how you hold up against my own personal security. :::: Vinny walks back to the escalade as two muscular men walk up to Scorpion. :::: Vinny: Oh and Scorpion one more thing…this is a war you cannot win and I’ll see it to that. Boys finish him. :::: The two men walk over to Scorpion and Scorpion begins swinging at the end. He connects his left hand on one mans cheek and the man back peddles from the punch. Scorpion is then met with a right followed by a left from the other man, hitting him like a pound of bricks dropping onto his head. The punch lays Scorpion out cold, as he lies there unconscious as the escalade pulls away. :::: TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
