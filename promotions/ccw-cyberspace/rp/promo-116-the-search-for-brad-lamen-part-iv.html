THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART IV

25 March 2003

THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART IV Koriann CCW Promo #116 Date: 03/25/03 Location: ... We last left off with our heroic heroine, Koriann, taking on the dastardly forces of evil, Carla Miller. Well, okay, it's nothing like that, but we did leave them in the middle of a cat spat that was about to turn into a full-fledged catfight. The two 'ladies' circled each other, looking for a good opening with which to lunge forward, when... Koriann suddenly turned around and ran sprinting away from her adversary. Carla immediately gave chase. Unfortunately for her, the spike heels she was wearing really weren't all that conducive to a good chase. A fact that Koriann planned on using to her advantage. Koriann burst out onto the floor of the Tobacco shop, ran by several startled customers, and zipped out onto the street, Carla close behind. As Koriann exited the shop, she quickly stepped off to the side and stuck out her leg. Carla, as expected, charged through the door after the jackass, caught her shin sharply on Koriann's leg, and sprawled onto the sidewalk in front of the tobacco shop. The few pedestrians in the area stopped to watch the action. Koriann: **pointing and laughing** Haw-haw! What a clumsy yutz! You never were very good on your feet, were you? Why, I remember the time your fiancé wanted to take you to the boardwalk and walk the beaches... Oh wait, he can't do that anymore can he? He's crippled! Haw! In fact, I should... Carla's gaze shoots upwards and locks eyes with Koriann. Koriann: Uh-oh... And Koriann takes off sprinting again as Carla regains her footing and bolts off after her. Koriann rushes down the sidewalk and crosses the street, Carla hot on her heels. Unfortunately for Carla, she reaches the street at the wrong time and gets slammed by a car that started braking as soon as Koriann ran out onto the street. Carla goes down, sprawling out onto the street. The driver of the car, who is, coincidently, Bob, who Koriann sent off to steal a car, exits the vehicle and approaches Koriann, who is standing near the curb laughing her ass off. Bob: Uh... What just happened? Koriann: You just ran that stupid bitch down! Hah-hah-ha! Bob: That was a good thing? Koriann: Of course it was! That crazy skank was trying to kill me. Bob: **not understanding** Oh... I see... Koriann: Well? What are you waiting for? She's obviously not dead. The Lamen must have taken pity on her poor soul. Pick her up and throw her into the back of the car! Bob: **still not understanding** Why? Koriann: Because she was in Lamen's apartment, that's why! She must've kidnapped him! Bob: **officially confused** She was in God's apartment? Koriann: Yes. Bob: **really confused** And she kidnapped Him? Koriann: Yes. That's the only possible explanation. Bob: **shrugging** Alrighty then. Bob picks up Carla and struggles to get her diminutive form in the back seat of the car. Koriann: We must torture and interrogate her to find the whereabouts the Lamen. We must retrieve him immediately from her diabolical clutches, for if we fail, I believe the world is doomed! Bob: Really? Koriann: Nah. I just made that last part up. But if she doesn't tell me where he is, then I can't complete my sacred mission! And this, sadly, is where we must end this inanity once again.
