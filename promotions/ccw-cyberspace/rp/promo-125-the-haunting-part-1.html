The Haunting; Part 1

6 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Haunting; Part 1 Devyl CCW Promo #125 Date: 04/06/03 Location: Boston, Massachusetts The scene opens up in an old library/study. It has your typical musty library smell, accompanied by the faint residue of aromatic incense. David Oakes, ankle wrapped and bandaged, sits on a plush couch near the lone entryway to the room. Shadow, dressed neck to toe in black leather and covered by a cloak, peers at the bookcases along the far wall. Every once in awhile, she picks a tome off the shelf, flips through it, then replaces it. Devyl, the Queen of Drakness herself, sits/leans on the desk which is placed strategically in front of the room's one window. She's dressed much the same as Shadow, but sports a bright red streak through her hair. David: We've been waiting for twenty minutes now. What's keeping the old geezer? Shadow: Oh? You know this Mr. Pritchard? David: I've met him once before. He's an ass. Shadow: Most people who claim to know the mysteries of the universe usually are. What I don't understand is how this person can help us locate the Angel Gabriel... David doesn't reply, his eyes drifting over to his wife. Shadow follows his gaze and silently appraises her mistress. Devyl feels their combined gaze fall on her, and she lets the silence remain a beat before speaking. Devyl: I am not sure he can help us. Shadow: Then why are we here? David: *before Devyl can reply* Because he has more occult resources than anyone in the area. Besides, if something strange is going on, he's sure to know about it. Shadow gives David a questioning look as she walks over to the couch and sits down beside him. David: The first and only time I met this guy, Devyl and I were looking for an ancient tablet of sorts... This was about a year ago. He was pompous and an egotistical jerk-ass, and he told us he didn't know where it was. BUT... He told us he could find out. And he did. Shadow: How? David: I don't know. He told us to come back in two days time. And then, just as he promised, he had the location for us. It was very strange. Shadow: Okay... There's a lot I just don't get right now. Victoria... You have these powers beyond imagining! I mean, you're a demon for crying out loud! You know things and see things and can do things that men can only dream about. Why couldn't you find that tablet on your own... And for that matter, why can't you track the Angel Gabriel? Why do we have to ask for help at all? Devyl: *another short pause* What do you think I am, Shadow? God? I am what I am... and I have strength... but I am not all-knowing nor all-powerful. I can see into men's souls... I know what makes them tick... I can frighten them to death or seduce them unto pure ecstacy... But beyond that, I must rely on other sources. The Devil does not grant powers equal to his own. If he did, he would be challenged day and night by the mortals of this world... Mortals greedy enough to control the domain of hell itself. Shadow opens her mouth to speak once again, but falls silent as the door to the room creaks open, and in walks the ever-reclusive Mr. Pritchard. He's obviously an older man, his wild white hair sticking out in random directions, looking like it hasn't been combed in several decades. His face is pock-marked with wrinkles and several warts. His clothes consist of a dirty white t-shirt, a pair of blue jeans, over which he wears a disheveled and unkempt purple Warlock's robe. Pritchard ignores the two people sitting on his couch, even ignores Devyl, as he walks across the room to his liquor cabinet and pulls out a long bottle full of red fluid. David: *wincing as he gets to his feet* About time you showed up. I almost thought you died on us out in the hallway. Pritchard: *not turning around* Victoria... I know the enfeebled smart-ass over yonder is your husband... but if he doesn't start watching his mouth, I will make it a point to seal it permanently. David: You're welcome to try at any time, you old fu– Devyl: *interrupting* David... That is enough. *to Pritchard* Do you know why we have come? Pricthard: Don't know, don't really care. I'm glad you're here, though. Pritchard finishes pouring his drink into a tall champaign glass, then drains it in one smooth motion, sending the contents of the glass down into his stomach. He sighs and belches softly, then turns around and focuses on Devyl with bleary, yet focused, eyes. Pritchard: Let me get straight to the point... You obviously want something if you're here visiting me. I want something as well. What say we help each other out this time. We don't get any more freebies. Shadow: *still sitting, slightly apprehensive* What do mean, 'no more freebies'? What do you want? Pritchard: I mean exactly that. My services are no longer free. I will get to my part of the bargain in a moment. First, what are the three of you looking for? Devyl stands up and folds her arms across her chest. She walks around the desk, heels clicking on the hardwood floor, to the window and looks outside at the spring trees, just starting to bud and bloom in the springtime air. She shudders involuntarily. Devyl: *her back to Pritchard* There is an entity running lose. It calls itself the Angel Gabriel. I want it found so that it can be destroyed. Pritchard: *glaring at Devyl's back* The Angel Gabriel? Devyl: *turns and matches Pritchard's gaze* I do not believe it to be God's heavenly messenger. Instead, it is a creature of torment and darkness. However, it doesn't come from the hellish pits below. I need to know what it is, where it is, and how it can be killed. Pritchard: Where did it come from? Devyl: A ceremony I was conducting with a group of scientists from the nearby university went awry. I was attempting to open the gates of hell when that thing appeared and ruined everything. Pritchard: *mocking grin* Ah yes... Men of science ruin everything, you know? Analytical minds were never meant to explore or involve themselves in– Devyl: *interrupting* I do not need this lecture from you, Pritchard. Will you do this for me or not? There is a long uncomfortable silence as Pritchard looks at her, considering. Then... Pritchard: I will. Where did this debacle of a ceremony take place? Devyl: A deserted cabin out in Preacher's Corners. It no longer stands. Pritchard: I will find this creature for you. Now you, Victoria... I need someone of your stature to do something for me. There's a small situation that needs to be dealt with... Shadow stands up and places her arm on David's shoulder in an unconscious gesture of support as he takes a step forward. David: What you're really saying is that this situation is ridiculously dangerous, and you don't want to risk your own life doing it... Pritchard: *glances at David* That's exactly right. I am mortal. She isn't. She has been around far longer than you or I, and has abilities that benefit her greatly. Such is the joy of being a free-roaming demon. And although she is Legion, she is also the only individual I can trust with this matter. Devyl: Enough, Pritchard. What do you want? Pritchard: Before I get into the details, would you or your colleagues like a drink? David & Shadow: *immediately* No. Devyl: Get on with it. Pritchard: There is a house in central Boston that I need you to check out. Some former good friends of mine, the Macarios, moved into the house last year. One month ago, Mr. Macario had a serious accident and shortly thereafter went violently insane. He was committed. Just last week, Mrs. Macario suffered the same unfortunate fate. Both of them have babbled of a haunting form with burning eyes. They told of inexplicable events in the house. Neither would enter the upstairs bedroom. Something is going on there and it needs to be stopped. David: Hmph. Right. I'm surprised that you're not trying to harness whatever this is and use it to your own ends. Pritchard turns and glares at David. If looks could kill, David would've been nuked on the spot. Pritchard: One does not play around with nor harness the truly dark powers, boy. Not unless one has complete control or dominion over them. This thing is unknown and therefore dangerous. I trust that Victoria will discover the problem and deal with it appropriately. *turns to Devyl* So what do you say, my dear? Will you do it? Devyl: *nods subtly* You have a deal, Pritchard. Find the creature for me. I'll deal with your little problem... fade out... View Devyl 's Biography Back
