Heros.. Legends..

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A prose monologue interleaved with staging in which Cianan, newly back from Japan, walks through Ottawa and the Corel Centre, seething at TVO, Kensuke, Blayze, SuMa and Phil Burner; the text breaks off mid-sentence in a news report.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: Heros.. Legends.. Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 18:50:58 Location: Ottowa This is a: Match RP Hero.. a funny word. People who protect, people who are idols for those who have no lives. People who stand up for what's 'right' and for 'justice.' I never could figure out what was 'right,' and what 'justice' was. **Cianan is walking down the street in Ottowa, Canada. His hands are shoved in his pockets, his head lowered in thought, as his feet guide him. No real destination planned, or known. People stop, pointing, he doesn't care.. he's tired, having flown straight in from Japan, after his meeting with Alaxander Miyagawa, and PURE.** People follow these heros, worship them, want to be them. False idols.. fanfare, worship. For what? Limiting yourself, holding up a code of honor, that has been out dated for thousands of years? There are no knights, there is no really valiance, there is no chivalry, that died. It's been long burried. Sickening. This was battle, war. Honor is not holding back, doing everything you can. You never dishonor your opponent by not pulling out all the stops. When would you ever see Superman, bash someones skull in.. He's a fool, it's why he died. It's why he constantly gets bashed, because he underestimates, and only gives his all at the last second. **He continued to walk down the near empty streets, thinking. His footsteps echoed against the buildings, creating a very hollow sound. His hand twitched, clenching and unclenching, as the veins in his arms bulged out.** Dishonor. Death before dishonor. Three men.. who I have scuffled with before. Blayze, the man who crippled me, who took away my title. I hate him... There is Kensuke. I hate him, he's lucky, young, loved.. but holds himself back. He could have the world in his palm.. disgusting. TVO, the man who never changes. These three, all used to be friends... New Strangers. All are warped as hell.. they probably did it to each other. TVO, you by far are the most naive.. the man who never changes, no matter how pathetic and sad he gets. TVO, who do you blame for you losing? The world has changed.. there is no place for people like you. There is no place for a naive man, or a star struck kid. This is a world for men, who will act, and do everything they can. Not for people, who scrounge at the bottom.. there is no good in people, they are all inheriently evil. Deal with it.. **His steps grew faster, as he lashed out, driving his foot into a waste basket, denting it, and sending garbage all over. The can rolled to the side, hittting a parked care, and Cianan brushed his hair back, trying once more to calm himself.** SuMa... Phil Burner.. two men who just won't die. A red neck hick.. and over sized freak, with an ego problem. They ignore everything that CCW has done while they were gone. CCW never needed them back, CCW was doing fine on it's own. CCW.. is mine. I strived.. gave my life, my health for this company. I will NOT have everything I worked for be ignored. They just expect to walk back in here, and go back to where they leave off? I don't think so. They don't deserve it.. THEY HAVEN'T EARNED THE RIGHT BACK! **Cianan digs into his pocket, his hand finding something. He takes it into his hand, a snarl forming on his lips, as he runs his hands over the object. He walks into a massive parkling lot.. people were leaving, getting into their cars. Chattering away, as stupid people often do, about unimportant things. Fuckin' sheep.** This hero worship has gone too far.. Kensuke, TVO, and Fat Ass, are all prime examples of people who are worshiped, despite being completely unworthy. Kensuke is a bored child. TVO is just Naive.. Fat Ass.. a has been, a has been with a weak heart. Weaknesses, all of which could be exploited.. all of which will be exploited. They could count on it.. **Cianan pushed and shoved his way, into what appeared to be Corel Centre. Cianan shoved his way through the masses of people leaving. Soon growing frustriated, he just started tossing people to the side, sending some tumbling down the stairs. Security stepped in Cianan's way.** Security: Excuse me sir, but I must ask to to leave the arena.. the game is over. **Cianan gave him no answer, as he grabbed the gaurds face, and thrusted him backwards, slamming his head against the wall, with a sickening thump. The other secuirty gaurds just looked on, jaws dropped, eyes widened. Cianan glared at them, and they just stepped aside, letting him through.** Female Security Gaurd: I..isn't he from CCW? Old Secuirty Gaurd: I think.. he must be friends with one of the players.. just let him through.. those wrestler's you know how tempramental they are. **The other gaurds step to the side, letting Cianan through, a sneer on his face, as he stepped into the arena, and finally out of sight** -=The Next Night on ESPN=- Newscaster: Tonight on ESPN.. our top story is Tornto Maple Leaf's Goa View Biography
