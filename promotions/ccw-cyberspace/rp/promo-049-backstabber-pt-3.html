Backstabber (pt. 3)

Backstabber (pt. 3) Scorpion CCW Promo #49 Date: 3-3-03 Location: John F. Kennedy Airport :::: Scorpion and Mike are seen walking into the American Airlines terminal station in John F. Kennedy Airport. :::: Mike: We have to get a ticket and fast or we will lose track of him. Scorpion: C’mon let’s get past security and check the board for the Las Vegas flights before it’s too late. :::: Scorpion and Mike run down to the security area where they are checked. They pass through the area all right and check the terminal board to see when the next Las Vegas flight is. :::: Scorpion (reading off of the board): Los Angeles…Las Vegas! The next flight is at eleven o’clock tonight. Mike: ELEVEN O’CLOCK! What time is it now? Scorpion (looking at watch): Shit it’s seven thirty. Mike: Fuck that we got to get there now! Mystery voice: Scorpion is that you? :::: Scorpion turns around and sees New York Billionaire Donald Trump surrounded by security. :::: Scorpion: Donald Trump? Donald Trump: Hey man, I’m a huge fan of yours. What are you doing in these parts? Scorpion: Taking care of some business. Donald Trump: In an airport? What kind of business are you talking about? Scorpion: Um…we missed a friend who is on his way to Las Vegas and we can’t find a ride to get there. Donald Trump: Your heading to Las Vegas? Scorpion: Yeah, why? Donald Trump: Me too, this new casino owner is opening this new casino up where the theme is gang life in New York City. So I decided to join the festivities and see what this place was about. Scorpion: Would you know by any chance what the casino owner’s name is? Donald Trump: Yeah I got it on this card here. (Reading card) His name is Corky Stewart. Scorpion: Holy shit Corky? Donald Trump: Would you know the guy by any chance? Scorpion: Yeah I know the guy all right. Excuse me a second Mr. Trump. Donald Trump: Sure. :::: Scorpion and Mike huddle up quietly so they can’t be heard. :::: Scorpion: Mike I can’t believe this, Corky has made a casino in Las Vegas? Mike: Well his parents always did have money. Scorpion: I know that. But now we know where he will be, he will be at the grand opening of his casino. That would be the perfect time to get revenge. Mike: But we are still stranded here in New York. Scorpion: Hold on a second I got an idea. :::: Scorpion turns around facing Donald Trump. :::: Scorpion: Mr. Trump may I ask of you for a huge favor? Donald Trump: Surely Scorpion, I’m a huge fan of yours and I would do anything for you. Scorpion: It would be greatly appreciated if you could take my friend Mike and I to Las Vegas so we could catch up with our…friend. Donald Trump: No problem Scorpion! You can come with me in my Jet. It would be my pleasure to take you to Las Vegas. Scorpion: Thank you so much you don’t know how much this means to me. Donald Trump: Your welcome, just stay with my security and I. We are heading down to the runway as soon as possible. Scorpion: Ok Mr. Trump, will do. :::: Scorpion and Mike allow Donald Trump and his security members walk ahead of him while they map out a plan. :::: Mike: We are really fucking lucky. Who would have thought that the richest man in New York City would be such a huge fan of yours and was even willing to take you to Las Vegas in his pimped out Jet! Scorpion: Believe me Mike, I’m in awe myself right now. We are getting a huge break in getting revenge on the rat-faced bastard. Mike: We can’t let anything leak out about the real reason we are going to Las Vegas or we will be in major trouble. Scorpion: No doubt man we got to make sure that we just say we are going to help Corky open up the casino and that we along with him will be cutting the rope to open the casino. Mike: Yeah we will just play along that we are tight with Corky and shit. Besides Trump will never know he has always been a day late and a dollar short. Scorpion: That doesn’t matter; this guy is our ride to Sin City. Mike: I can’t wait to get my hands around Corky’s neck and suck the life out of him. Scorpion: Your not kidding man, he should have killed me when he had the chance, because he is going to pay for it now. :::: Donald Trump is at a very far distance now and turns around stopping to call for Scorpion and Mike. :::: Donald Trump: (yelling) C’mon guys or your never going to make it to Las Vegas! Scorpion: (yelling) All right we are coming. :::: Scorpion and Mike run down the hallway of the airport catching up with Donald Trump. They enter into some room that ends up taking them down to the runway where sitting right in front of them is Donald Trump’s jet. :::: Scorpion (under his breath): Holy shit… Donald Trump: C’mon boys, my plane is your plane. :::: Scorpion and Mike stop in front of the jet looking in complete awe. :::: Scorpion: (screaming) SIN CITY HERE WE COME! :::: Scorpion and Mike walk up the steps leading up to the plane as the scene ends. :::: TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
