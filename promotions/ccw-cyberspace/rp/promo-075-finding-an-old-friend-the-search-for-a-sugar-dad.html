Finding An Old Friend The Search for a Sugar Daddy Part 2 (don't bother looking for part 1, it never happened) Sheena Ramone CCW Promo #75 Date: Tonight, you ass Location: Does it really matter? “Come on Sugar daddy.” Tuesday morning, 2:00AM EST. Sheena Ramone, sits in the passenger seat of the Sodomy Express Hummer. Next to her is of course none other than Curiously Bruce, and sleeping soundly in the backseat is Our Resident ‘Tard Jimmie. Two weeks after being fired by Michael Stevens, this motley crew has been in search of a new home, and it appears now they’re almost at the end of their long journey. What else has happened on this journey you may ask? Well don’t cause I was too lazy to write down any of it, so lets just say it was wacky, and made you laugh, ok? Alright, now that we’ve got that settled, let’s move on. The crew is following up on a lead they discovered elsewhere relating to an old friend of Jimmie’s one Stoney Mcgee. “This has got to be the one, I know it. I’ll seduce him, make him mine, and we’ll be able to live comfortably forever.” “But um Boss, isn’t Stoney poor?” “Damnit Bruce he was, but you heard what his old drinking buddy Alex told us.” “Nah, I don’t remember that Boss, I think it never got written down (Bruce glances towards the writer of this narrative, oh shit that’s me….I’ve got a gay midget staring at me, MAKE IT STOP~!) why don’t you refresh my memory?” At this Bruce turns back towards Sheena, who’s apparently now the leader of this group, and it’s a good thing she is too, otherwise, well I’d have nothing to do with my background characters. But I digress. “You know, Alex Morgan, he told us we could find Stoney in Beverly Hills, he moved up in the world after some kind of big law suit.” “Oh yeah. But what makes you think he’s gonna want you Boss? Isn’t he already married?” “So. I’m Sheena Ramone, the widest hole this size of the grand canyon~! Every man wants me, even you.” “Uh, yeah sure whatever you say.” “And that’s what I say, now drive Bruce, we’ve got to find Stoney soon. I’m tired of living off whatever scraps the bartender throws me after last calls when I’ve sucked off every man in the place.” “I kinda like that myself…” “Well I said I’m tired of living off the scraps, I never said I didn’t enjoy the life.” “Gottcha.” From the back of the Hummer, comes the sound of Jimmie waking up. He yawns and stretches like a cat, well a three hundred pound bald retarded cat anyway. After rubbing the sleep from his eyes, he looks up towards Sheena and Bruce, and wails plaintively. “Jimmie need go poopy, NOW~!” “Oh Christ, not again, Jimmie, you just went an hour ago.” “But auntie Sheena, Jimmie gotta go again. Jimmie likes toilet paper.” “Oh hell, Bruce find a gas station.” “You got it boss.” Skipping ahead, we find the companions exiting SX, and walking towards the warmly lit confines of a small gas station. Jimmie starts to run for the bathroom, when he stops cold and stares at the man behind the counter. He appears to be in his late fifties, with a long snowy white beard, somewhat glassy brown eyes, and a snowy skullet. “UNCLE STONEY~!” And surprisingly, amazingly, the man behind the counter is indeed the man we’ve been searching for, Stoney Mcgee. Funny how that worked out, isn’t it? Almost like someone planned it that way….. “Holy shit man, is that Jimmie? Oh god man, I haven’t seen you for like years man, decades even.” Jimmie attempts to leap over the counter to hug Stoney, but alas his fat little legs can’t provide enough power and he ends up falling onto the counter in a heap, causing him to soil himself. Stoney pats the ‘Tard on the back, before helping him over the counter, the two share a warm embrace, with Stoney apparently not caring that Jimmie has just dropped a load in his pants. “Jimmie love Uncle Stoney~!” For those wondering, Stoney isn’t actually his Uncle, just a dude who happened to take Jimmie in one day when he was living on the streets. “Yeah man, I like you too and stuff. Say Jimmie what’s with your friends? Who’s the pink haired girl?” “I’m Sheena Ramone Stoney, and I’ve heard a lot about you, and if I must say you’re much more handsome than I expected.” “Haha, yeah thanks man. I like appreciate that. It’s like so cool that you’re all here man, in my store. I like bought it after I got all the money from the lawsuit.” “So you own your own business I must say that’s really sexy.” “Uh thanks little guy, I think.” “Shut up Bruce~! Sorry about that Stoney, Bruce is a little um, well, he’s a rump roaster. But he’s right, having your own gas station is sexy.” “Oh, well like thanks man. Say do like wanna get outta here or something, we can just like leave the store, someone’ll watch it.” “JIMMIE WANNA GO~!” “Yeah sure I guess, why not, let’s go Bruce. You’ll drive.” “Whatever you say boss.” And with that we close. Didn’t make much sense at all now did it? Nah I didn’t think so, but have no fear the continuity might actually start making sense if you keep reading this stuff, I swear. Really, come on trust me, my work will NOT make you retarded, sure it’s been shown to lower your IQ, but those reports were never confirmed. Seriously, come back next time…please?
