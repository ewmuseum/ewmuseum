What this is An interview-class roleplay introducing Jackson ’Destiny’ Williams through flashbacks to a fatal boxing match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jackson 'Destiny' Williams Entitled: An Intro to the past. Written in Wednesday, May 22, 2002, 17:02:44 Location: This is a: Interview :: The scene slowly faded into a run down apartment. In this apartment the wood floors creaked where there weren't holes, the paint was only a memory to the rotted wooden walls. The furniture consisted of a cot and a couch that looked worse than the floor it rested on. Sunlight streaked through the apartment from a window behind, it was from this same area that rattling of metal on light metal snuck through the air. It only took moments for the vision of a large white male, standing about 6'4' and carrying more than enough muscle on his upper body, to appear infront of us, and infront of the old couch. Loose wornout jeans and a dirty grey tank top hung closely from his hips and shoulders respectfully. However it would be the extremely large metal pale of water that hung down from his right hand, and the extremely large metal pale hanging from his left hand that held wood within it, that could easily draw anyones attention away from his face. His face, a face that carried a square jaw, vivrant blue eyes that sat within his face like pebbles, yet were barely noticeable through his long bangs and the scruffy brown hair he adorned. This man was Jackson 'Destiny' Williams. Calmly he sat down both pale's and dropped himself down on the couch.:: :: Reaching over the back of the couch he pulled out a rather large tin pot. He sat it down beside him for a moment while he pulled a matchbook out from his front jean pocket. He leaned towards the pale of wood, opened the matchbook and ripped a match from the book. When he went to strike the match he dropped it, the matchbook fell to the ground as well. In immense pain Jackson grabbed at his skull with both hands, his head and upper body fell back on to the couch. These striking shots of pain had been attacking Jackson since his last fight, which was a year ago. Jackson let out a pain filled moan of horror, the shot blurred immensley:: :: The scene refocused within the confines of a packed arena that right now housed a boxing ring in the middle of it. A panicing crowd had rushed the two fighters, within seconds the ring had been filled with promoters, doctors, cornermen, announcers, bodygaurds and even the odd fan. A large circle slowly formed in the middle of the ring, a well toned and tanned white man laid on his back, his face bloodier than unfrozen raw meat, a doctor kneeled beside him. The large, sweat covered, crew cut wearing, Jackson Williams, in his boxing shorts and his gloves still wrapped onto his fists stood over the fallen fighter. With a loud chattering buzz surrounding the whole scene, the doctor who had kneeled down beside the fighter looked up at Jackson and yelled at him.:: 'DDDDESTINY.... YOU DUMB OX... YOU KILLED HIM... YOU KILLED HIM.... YOU KILLED HIMMMMM!!!!!!!!' :: The face of Jackson Williams immediately became covered in shock and horror of realising what he had done. A man who knew nothing but fighting, and on that fateful day, boxing, had just ended his career by ending anothers life. The scene snapped to pure white. Again a pain filled moan ripped through the air, the scene of Jackson, in his apartment and on the couch, faded in through the white. Jackson's voice exploded into the air.:: 'WHY?? WHY?!?! WHY DID I HAVE TO LOSE CONTROL... I.... I.....I.... STOP.... PLEASSSSSSSE........ STOP!!!!' :: It had been a year since Jackson had even looked at a ring, he turned to squatting in this run down apartment and had resorted to boiling water from the river down the street so he could have something clean in his life. It had been a month ago that he had been blessed for the first time since that tragic night, he had found out about the CCW and after sending them his accomplishments, of his career prior to that fateful night, he had gained acceptence into the federation. Yet it was these flashbacks that haunted him still, his mind would never let him forget what could happen if he lost control of his rage for even an instant. Breathing heavily now, the pain in his head had disappeared, he sat with his head in his hands, it always took him some time to recover from these attacks. Today, and every day when these occured, he had prayed to himself.:: 'God... please give me the control I had before that night, all those times I was victorious when I had control of myself... please God... don't let me loose it again.. ' :: Jackson knew this could be his last chance at regaining a career that he had lost within a matter of seconds, when he prayed he prayed not for the safety of his opponents to come, but for Jackson's own well-being. Jack knew it to be true that a fighter could take another fighters life within the ring one time and it would be deemed an accident. He also knew that if it were to happen again, he would never be able to compete in a licensed match ever again. Another shot of pain hit Jackson's head, again the viscious cycle had returned, another pain filled moan exploded into the dead air of the rundown apartment. Slowly the vision of Jackson 'Destiny' William's hands gripping at his skull, praying for the pain to stop, faded away to black.:: View Biography
