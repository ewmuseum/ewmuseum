Menace II Society - The End of Days

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Blayze and Darren, as Menace II Society, unveil a new monster ally in a temple basement.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Menace II Society Entitled: The End of Days Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 22:43:56 Location: The Basement of The Mutuba Temple This is a: Match RP [A shadow lurks upon the walls of the basement of the most sacred temple on earth. As the shadow moves, a voice is overheard commenting on what he sees] Blayze: Right in front of my eyes, I witness a mammoth creation which has finally been unleashed. Once kept away from the grasp of CCW like a nuclear weapon gone wrong. At this time, the path we shall walk seems simple because now with this beast by our side Darren, we are untouchable [Blayze makes his appearance from the shadows and begins to crack his knuckles. He turns to Darren who sneaks in a smirk and nods in approval] Blayze: CCW wants to play mind games, then shall be it because now they have dug themselves in a ditch which is almost impossible to climb out of. Spider has just put his CCW career and business assets on the line of fire. Seeing that maybe payback may be in store, a little terrorizing of 2 simple beer drinking fools on LDT will give us the boost we need. Oh Darren, I just though of something, since we now have this secret weapon by our side, maybe we should take the night off Darren: I agree, let him crush the skulls of these morons to bits. Blayze: After our embarassing showing, in which we were screwed out of Group Therapy, CCW has decided to pit us against rookies who Im sure want to 'pay their dues'. Low card competition? I am disgusted!! Daren: Dont worry, revenge shall come with a sweet touch to it. [Blayze nods and turns to the creation of devastation in front of them. A roar erupts and he punches the basement wall in rage. He turns to face Blayze and a demonic smile rises on his face as he twitches his neck, his muscles bulge out in such a freakish way.] Darren: I just though that maybe CCW wants to try and make a mockery of us by having us, the greatest tag team and brother combination in history, face Two Pints Blayze: Do they want their lives to be put at risk? Oh well, hey, CCW's orders is our command and if they want to new additions to the roster become the new sole owners of a golden coffin 6 feet under, then CCW is in luck. The job shall get done and once the dust settles and each of them has been dealt a Crucial Conflict, we let the monster savor on the leftovers Darren: Haha, how sweet it is [The beast nods in total approval and excitement as he awaits his orders] Blayze: Now my brother, we must go forth and bless our new child with the gift of the God's. Let the baptism commence once we arrive at the tabarnacle a floor above. Come now, follow us my son [Blayze signals for his brother to come and the mammoth man follows in pride as he is ready to become apart of the Unholy Tribe] View Biography
