What this is A Match RP in which Cianan, sleepless in a hotel, hallucinates that fans chant his fear of Seraph and attacks a janitor.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: Cianan Fears Seraph Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 00:57:29 Location: Hotel This is a: Match RP I clutch my sheets, as I pull them around myself, I'm cold, tired. It's 5 in the afternoon, I feel like shit. I've felt this way, seemingly forever, since that fateful night. What should have been so glorious, what should have been so grand, came crashing down around me. I felt insecure, and strangely alone. I wasn't home. I wasn't in a familiar place. This never used to bother me. I felt alone. There were no voices here, it was a relief, but, I was alone. This place was too bright, it hurt my eyes, and it gave me a staggering headache. My brain felt like it was about to explode. The pressure was building up in my temples. My eyes squeezed shut, and I pulled the covers around me. I can't sleep, no matter how I try. 'It,' keeps me up, like a hundred cans of soda. But, almost painful. I haven't slept in a few days. But strangely, I don't feel the least fatigued. Interesting, to say the least. Over the courses of nights, and days with no sleep, countless thoughts have spun through my ravaged mind. (Some thoghts may seem insignificant, but to an unclear mind, and a sleep deprived mind, they become thrown out of proportion.) I go from being a finalist, in Group Therapy, to nothing. I thrashed countless people, I've held the the World Title.. twice.. and.. nothing after. I saw.. Cianan fears Seraph.. in the crowd. I fear no one.. I have countless great accomplishments, in my short time here, rivaled only by Malcom Next. I was the fastest up and comer.. I won title after title, progressed from power to higher power.. and now.. I start all over. Cianan.. fears.. Seraph.. How could I have seen that?! I've never even faced Seraph... why would I have to fear a PCW Cruiserweight reject. I've beaten super heavyweight Eric Clark.. Destroyed Enigma.. Killed Jesse Ramey.. I defeated Kensuke Suzahara.. I over came.. Why do people think I fear that whiney, snot nosed little bitch?! Why would I fear Seraph? I'm a champion (There was a small knock on the door, but Cianan didn't hear. Another knock, and a muffled voice behind the wood door. The door slowly opens.. and a head peaks in, stepping inside the hallway...) ::Cianan.. Fears Seraph:: #Cianan Fears Seraph!!# -Cianan Fears Seraph...- My eyes widen, I was no longer alone. I could hear them call out. Screaming at me, chanting. [Cianan fears Seraph!] \Cianan Fears Seraph!!!!/ I stand suddenly, throwing off the blanket, and roaring madly. The voices were back, torturing me. I had told them last time, I feared no one. There was a scrambling, and a crash, a slight shriek. I steped down, an animal instinct inside of me kicking in. ::Cianan fears Seraph!!:: -Cianan FEARS Seraph!- My pulse pounds in my ear, and a snarl raises from the pit of my stmomach, as I turn the corner, and spot.. Seraph. (The poor janitor, stands slowly, pressing his back against the wall. He glances too the side, and reaches for the door, which is kicked soundly shut but Cianan.) 'Seraph..' 'S..sir..?' The janitor damn dear shreiked, as Cianan slammed his hands on either side of his head. 'Seraph.' My teeth clenched, as I stared down at 'Seraph.' I was going to show him who I feared.. I was going to show him. Before even I realized it, my hands were around his neck, my fingers digging into the flesh of his throat. Instantly, brusises started to form, as 'Seraph,' struggled agaist my grip. His hand and fingers ran agaisnt my face, unkempt fingernails ripping across my forehead and cheek. Blood flowed down my forehead.. my face. I was more then enraged. My grip tightened as my fury grew, and grew. Seraph would know.. He soon fell unmoving to the floor, his hand twitching. Then.. stopped moving. I let go, wiping the mixture of blood, and sweat from my forehead. I threw my hand down, speckling the wall and 'Seraph,' with pink dots. I breathed slowly out, the voices were quiet. I had shown them. Just like I had shown them after Group Therapy. I staggered back, my aggression, and adrenaline taking a lot out of me. I fall back on to my bed, my chest rising and falling slowly.. as I feel the blood gush from my forehead. It was peaceful.. Calm. Alone. So.. alone.. The feeling of the blood rushing from my body, felt, like a relief of presure. Less blood, less pressure.. I smiled to myself. Proud of my victory, proud of myself. Relieved.. and.. Alone.
