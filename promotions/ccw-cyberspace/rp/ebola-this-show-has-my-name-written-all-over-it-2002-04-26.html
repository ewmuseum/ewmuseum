Ebola - This show has my name written all over it.

What this is A Match RP in which Ebola, a wrestler with a dual personality, waits at an airport before Group Therapy and takes a call from a pursuer.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ebola Entitled: This show has my name written all over it. Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:02:08 Location: Gate 12, Tucson International Airport This is a: Match RP Joseph Doyle looked down at his ticket one last time. Row 27, Seat A. Window seat. He wondered what it'd be like to look down from 10,000 feet on the Manhatten skyline with his own eyes. Just the thought of seeing was beginning to make his blood boil. He could feel it, deep inside, where the wild part of him resided. Hate and anger and frustation, embodied in an image of black and red. After all this time, all the 'therapy' it was still there. It was still there and the therapist had thrown up his hands after months of work. 'You'll just have to learn to live with it, because it's as much a part of you as anything else.' But now, the episodes weren't as bad as they had once been, the polar swings not as violent or lasting. Maybe this is what it would feel like to be the Incredible Hulk or something like that. But this Sunday, he'd need both sides to survive. Both sides to somehow be victorious. The Group Therapy tournament. He'd need his wits about him to make it to the finals. He'd need a strategy and a lot of luck. One in forty weren't all that great of odds. Still, with the chance of the ultimate reward, it was a risk worth taking. After all, who better to face Neurosis than someone who had been Neurosis. And not that Suzahara kid either. Kensu spent his time as Neurosis running around with a notebook, learning how to be evil and trecherous. Problem is, that's not something you learn, it's something you're born with. You can't fake evil, but in Kensuke's case you can mistake bitterness and jealously for it. 40 to 1 though. A long shot to say the least. But there are better odds. Odds like 5 to 1. Odds that have nothing to do with strategy or luck.. Odds that have everything to so with letting go. The Omnimatch. This was the match where he'd need that dark side. He'd need to to take over, to guide him once the blood started to flow and rational thought stopped. Hopefully he'd be able to have a semblence of control, be able to stop it once the match was over.. That, or he'd tear the arena apart piece by piece... Either way sounds like a lot of fun. **Buzz** With a start, Joey reaches for the cell phone clipped to his belt. The Caller ID says 'Pay Phone', but he had a sinking feeling he knew what this was about. 'Doyle here..' There was a raucous bombardment of words, angry and stern. But since they wee heavily laced with an accent, it was almost funny. Almost. 'You're where??' Now that was funny. Chased across the parking lot by a bunch of leather clad fairies... Another torrent of words came forth fromt he phone, so loud that a passing mother cover the ears of her son to protect against the profanity laced outburst. Joey's jaw set hard as the assualt by digital network continued. 'Look, you listen to me.. He'll be at Madison Square Garden on sunday. I'll show you right where his lockeroom is if you'll just quit hassling me..' More words, less profane, but just as menacing. Joey bared his teeth, but not in a smile but in a natural defensive reaction. 'Look Colonel Clink, I don't care what you've got on me, but if I was able to get my hands on East German genetic secrets, then just imagine what else I could do if you piss me off. I'm not going to play games with you. I'm going to do what you want, then just leave me alone, or you WILL be sorry. Do we have an understanding?' Words again, standoffish and faux-apologetic. The end of the conversation was abrupt. Joey could feel the surge of adrenaline, and the rising of his temper. He could feel the other half of himself groing closer to the surface, and he hoped it wouldn't until it was time. Still, out of reflex he peered into the cargo pocket of his carry-on bag, making sure the mask was there. Group Therapy for one or two, depending on how you look at it. View Biography
