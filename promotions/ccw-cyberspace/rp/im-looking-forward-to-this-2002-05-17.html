I'm Looking Forward to This....

What this is A scripted scene in which Regina Winters briefs Mike on a plan to infect her own company’s computers before she leaves to face The Valiant One.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: I'm Looking Forward to This.... Written in Friday, May 17, 2002, 19:45:45 Location: Regina's Apartment, New York City This is a: Match RP **Regina Winters is in the bedroom of her luxurious New York Apartment, making sure she has everything packed and ready to go for her trip to Kentucky. And amazingly, she's feeling good about herself.... Her life.... And everything in-between. Not only was she getting ready to wrestle again, but the company that had been denied her for so long was about to be hers. She smiles as she thinks about these things, and then turns for the door just as the knock sounds. She quickly struts over to the door, opens it, and gives her visitor a dazzling smile.** Regina: Mike!! I was just about to call for you! Mike: Yeah, imagine that. **Regina steps off to the side as Mike enters the apartment. He looks around briefly, but without much interest, as Regina closes and bolts the door behind him.** Regina: I have specific instructions for you while I'm gone.... **Regina walks across the room towards the rather large kitchen area, picking up several sheets of paper off the nearby desk.** Mike: Regina.... Regina: **cutting him off** Now, I'm leaving for Kentucky tonight. I have a match-up against the big oaf that calls himself 'The Valiant One'. Mike: Great. I'm really worried about Christine.... Regina: And I really don't care! You're 'girlfriend' is the last thing you're supposed to be thinking about while you're working here with me! Mike: I saw what happened to her on Lockdown. And after what you did to her a week before.... **Regina calmly folds the papers up and leans on the counter, looking directly into Mike's eyes.** Regina: This is the LAST time I'm going to say this.... You will not WORRY about Christine Oakes. You will not THINK about Christine Oakes. In fact, if I hear you utter her name one more time, I will have you neutered! Mike: I really think... Regina: Try me!! After spending all this time with me.... If you really don't think I won't do it.... You're much stupider than I gave you credit for! Mike: **long pause** When our 'work' together is done, I swear if you bother me one more time.... Regina: Stop your false bravado, Mike! You have NO bargaining power here! I hold ALL the cards. Now, I'm off to face The Valiant One.... Who's apparently not so valiant anymore. Mike: Yeah, sucks to be you.... Regina: **ignoring Mike** Even if he is ready to start hitting girls, I plan on having fun with him! He seems rather intent on making himself impervious to pain. I wonder what his current limits are? Do you know how delicious it will be to smash him to defeat.... To cause him such exquisite pain that he starts begging for me to stop? **smiles** I'm making myself wet just thinking about it! Mike: You make me sick.... Regina: Aw... Don't worry, Mike.... NO ONE will be able to take the place of your little Christine! She'll always be my favorite target! Mike: I hope that someday soon, someone manages to cripple you.... Maybe then you'll see how disgusting your actions really are. Regina: You call ME disgusting? Mike.... You find and sell women and children! You work for the largest slave cartel in this country! How many families and lives have been destroyed because of you? I really don't think you're one to talk! Mike: Regina.... You take sexual pleasure from destroying people! How can you even compare.... Regina: That is ENOUGH! Now, you listen here and listen good little man! These sheets that I have right here in my hands are the computer passwords for the main corporate building of Winters Inc. Mike: I know. I helped you get those. Regina: But what you don't know is that I've contracted an outside computer specialist to create a virus. Shortly after I leave, you are to go to the address listed at the top of the first page and pick-up the disk which holds the virus. Then, that same evening, you are to go to the corporate building, gain entrance using the access codes also listed on this sheet, and then make your way back to the centralized computer hub. Once there, you are to gain access to the five main computers with the passwords you found, and then download the virus. The entire operation should take you no more than two hours at the most. Once you are finished, lay low for a couple days. I'll be back shortly. And once I return, I'll assess the damage and we'll go onto the next stage. Mike: **long pause** This better be over soon. You said it would take only a month. Regina: Patience, Mike! We're just getting started with the festivities! But don't fret.... Your part in these affairs are almost over. **Mike takes the papers out of Regina's hands and turns to go.** Regina: Oh, and one other thing Mike.... If anyone catches you inside the building, kill them. If you don't, the resulting implications of being seen or caught will not favor you.... **Mike doesn't look back or reply as he walks to the door, unlocks it, and exits. Regina smiles a bit, still feeling on top of the world. Kentucky and TVO were awaiting her, and she just couldn't wait to get in the ring and cause more pain....** View Biography
