Many questions, few answers Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #63 Date: 03/04/03 Location: Between point A and point C “Scorpion? Why the hell am I teaming with Scorpion?” Michael Stevens questions aloud the strange pairing he’s now faced with. To wit he must team with the Scorpion, and man he knows little, and cares even less about, and they must face a newcomer in Minx, as well as Stevens’s former valet and girl, Sheena Ramone. Dire circumstances indeed for the redheaded icon. His mind travels back to the events of the last several weeks that have led him up to this rather unusual tag match, first his shattering loss to Jason Dread in the number one contendership match for the CCW world title, then firing Sheena and company on the advice of one Brad Lamen, and his screwy loss to Yama Tatsu that ripped him from the Indy title tournament. All these things have come together in the most unexpected way possible, and now he must battle his former lover for a chance to regain something of his shattered confidence. But alas will it be enough? Is beating two women, really something to be proud of? For a man like Stevens, it’s enough. A man who prides himself on being unbeatable, undeniably the best, he must have victory if he is continue this line of thinking. But, can he do it, can a man put aside all his feelings, the love he once held in his heart for her. Can he put aside that love long enough to hurt her, to cripple her, to make her submit? The answer remains to be seen, however, somewhere deep inside, he knows that he must. For to a man like Stevens, winning is everything, and he will sacrifice everything to be victorious. He’ll do anything it takes to get back to the top of the mountain. But that doesn’t stop it from hurting, that doesn’t stop him from questioning. Yes, even a man so completely assured of his own greatness, has questions in some moments of his life. And this will be one of those moments. “Michael Stevens, and Scorpion, against Sheena, and Minx. It’s insane, why the hell would they force to me to do this? Are they crazy, what the fuck are they thinking? Goddamnit, I’ve had enough of this bullshit. They’re just toying with me, messing with my mind. I bet Lamen had something to do with this.” Yes Lamen, Brad Lamen to be more precise. He’s shown interest in Stevens as of late. He’s offered to become a mentor of the young Stevens. Brad Lamen, who’s down virtually everything there is to do in CCW, has offered to take Stevens under his wing, make him a star, but it is worth it? Is it really worth it to join up with Brad Lamen. He’s a snake in the grass, there’s no doubt about that in Stevens or anyone else’s mind. And yet, Stevens cannot get it out of his head. If Brad can do what he says, if Brad can take Stevens to the top of CCW, doesn’t Stevens owe it to himself to join up with Lamen? Stevens needs to be told he’s the best, needs to be shown that he’s deserving of being at the top of the heap, and Brad Lamen can do that. Yet, by the same token, Brad’s offer is two sided. He demanded that Stevens drop his band of flunkies. Force Sheena, and Bruce, and Jimmie, away from him. Forced him to fire them, put them out on the streets. And now it seems likely that Brad may have had something to do with placing Sheena in a match against Stevens, why? A test, of course, a test of Michael’s loyalty to Brad. A test of Michael’s willingness to do whatever it takes to get back to the top of course. That’s what Lamen’s doing, by forcing Stevens to fight his former lover, he’s forcing him to make the ultimate choice, Brad, or his past. And really for a man like Stevens, what other choice does he have, but to join with Brad, but to destroy Sheena and continue on towards the tag team championships. For it’s the gold that Stevens craves, the gold that symbolizes greatness. It’s all he thinks about, all he desires. It’s what he needs to be a man. “I guess I have to, but god I don’t want to, not yet. If you did this to me, damn you Brad Lamen, damn you to hell. I know you’ve got me right where you want me you evil son of a bitch. I’ll wrestle Sheena, I wrestle Minx, and I’ll even do it handcuffed to a dead weight like Scorpion. Not because I want to, but because I have to.” Yes, as strange and awkward as it may seem, Stevens does indeed have to. For when one has so completely devoted themselves to being the so-called best, there’s nothing else left for them, and nothing else matters. Not friends, not family, nothing matters, save for that one beautiful moment of happiness, when your hand is raised, and you’ve once again proven yourself victorious. It’s a twisted circle for Michael Stevens, but it’s the only one he has, and it’s the only one he knows. ~FIN~ View Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens 's Biography Back
