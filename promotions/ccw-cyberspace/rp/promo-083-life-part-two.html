Life (Part Two) Dan Logan CCW Promo #83 Date: 05.03.03 Location: Getting In... {Number 2} 'Thanks' He says to Dru as she lifts his frail shell from the ground and carries him to her yellow BMW, struggling to find his feet momentarily she stumbles at the sheer weight of him. She smiles, remembering the good old days when they were together and none of this mess had begun. 'Why did you ever change Dan?' She asks as finally he finds his feet and continues to walk with her help toward the car. His eyes burn as slowly he opens them fully once more and is greeted by the warm glow of the morning sun in all its glory. 'I...I don't know. It was - easier' He mutters his excuse knowing what is to come next. Just like every time he answers he with this question he gets the same answer... 'It should never have been about what was easier. I gave up my life for you Dan and you - you went off with that dirty slut and married her...without even telling me!' It had never been her business though and if he had have told her she would only have attempted to change his mind before he had done it, so why should he have bothered? Precisely, only she will ever know the answer to that and to be honest even if she was to explain it to everyone I don't think we'd understand then. Dan just nods his head and wipes the vomit from about his eyelids, things would have to change soon and if he wasn't ready for that change god help him. She was right though, it would have turned out better if he had gone with her, she loved the same things he did, she was attractive and she loved him...but it wasn't as easy to admit that there was someone that you loved more than yourself and Dan just couldn't bring himself to do it...no way! Drink was a problem to him now and that had only come since Emma had left him, maybe he wouldn't have it if he'd gone to Dru - maybe isn't good enough though is it? Saying maybe is regretting having his little boy and there is no way on this earth that he was going to say that...he loves that kid - what's his name again, Chris. Yeah, he loves Chris more than anyone else around, that kid is the only reason Dan even attempts to work now...even if Emma isn't letting him near Dan. That isn't the point. 'I couldn't, it was just...to hard' That always worked, when he says that she just turns her back and ignores him for a while because she doesn't like to think him lazy. That is what he is though really, he hates trying because it can cause to much pain...he'd rather take the easy way out. He'd rather fail. 'Don't give me that again Dan, every time I say this to you and you tell me that same thing. Why can't you just tell me you never loved me...' 'Leave it alone woman!' As they arrive at the car she pushes Dan's arm from around her neck and stares him in the eye waiting for an answer. Moments pass while nothing is said, the sun still glaring beats down upon the mess that is Dan Logan as fresh beads of sweat mingle in with the vomit and putrid smells of the back street bars he had invaded the night before. He looks past her gazing into the distance at the faint image of the big city behind her as she stands waiting for him to speak. Still nothing, slapping him hard across his moist face she brings his attention back to her. This time she wants an answer and nobody is moving until she has got one... 'Well...' 'Well what?' Dan thinks within his head, 'I don't have to answer you if I don't want to'. Dan sighs and strokes his unshaven face; the bristles upon his face are intriguing and new. Playing with them for a while he attempts to ignore her once again. WHACK! 'Answer me now Dan...Why didn't you tell me?' 'You wouldn't understand if I told you...' 'That one will get her', he thinks once more. 'There is no way she can come to me and tell me that she can understand anything I say...and if she really wants me to tell her I'll just waffle cause there ain't no way I am telling her...' 'Because I do love you!' 'What...what the hell did I just say! Do I have no brain? Now she is gonna go all soppy on me and there ain't nothing I can do to stop her'...She advances upon Dan evidently smiling at those three words spoken to her. Does she love him also? Surely she cannot still love this man after all he put her through...well maybe she can. The expectant look upon her face changes for a moment only into one of fear as she reaches out and kisses him upon the cheek. She laughs childishly and turns away blushing like a schoolgirl. Opening the car door she walks around the car and gets in letting the electrical window down and pushing her face through. 'If your getting in you can come back to my place and we can talk - properly. If not then we don't see each other again, I'm sick of playing about now Dan...open the door or walk away...your choice' He turns his head and looks around. Does he really want to risk it all again? Yes it will be hard for him to get into this car and try something that he should have tried so long before...but does he really wish to miss out once again? Glancing once more at the city backdrop he smiles...'I know what I'm going to do, I'm sick of running...' Slowly he opens the car door and gets in making sure he doesn't allow to much of his vomit to touch the leather upholstery. She smiles and kisses him once more, this time though he turns his head and meets her kiss with one of his own... ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
