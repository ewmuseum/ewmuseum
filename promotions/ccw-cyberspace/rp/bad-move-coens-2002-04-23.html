Bad Move Coens

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A challenge-class promo in which The Supreme Machine answers John Coens after the chair attack and offers a title match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Bad Move Coens Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 11:08:23 Location: Somewhere This is a: Challenge Jealousy is a curse John Coens. Remember that. The Supreme Machine sits cross legged on the floor staring at a huge television that takes up an entire wall of an otherwise empty room. The moment of John Coens' cowardly attack is frozen on the screen. It eats away at you from the inside, like a parasite feeding on your insecurities....and believe me John Coens, you are full of insecurities. A wry smile appears across the five time champion's face. Think about it John Coens. By your own admission, your grandfather thinks I am better than you. Your OWN grandfather. I imagine that must be a bitter pill to swallow. Your OWN flesh and blood not believing in your abilities. Personally I find it sad, but who am I to comment on your obviously dysfunctional family. The Supreme Machine breaks out laughing as he rewinds the video tape and watches John Coens' chair shot again and again. No wonder your family have no faith in you John....you couldn't even pull off your sneak attack properly. I was standing there with my eyes shut and you STILL couldn't put me down for more than a few seconds. You had the best chance you are EVER going to get to prove your superiority over me and what did you do? You blew it, that's what you did. You are a loser John Coens. Your problem is that, unlike your family, you just haven't realised it yet. The saddest thing about all of this John, is that I'm going to give you the chance to prove just how much of a loser you really are. Did you seriously think I'd run from you? Has your jealousy clouded your judgment that much? The answer is obviously a resounding YES. CCW Universal Champion....BAH! The huge man leaps effortlessly to his feet and spits at the image of John Coens on the television screen. CCW Lightweight Champion? Are you for real? You are an idiot! But.....even an idiot deserves a chance so here's what I'll do. If you seriously think that you stand a chance against me, then I'll give you a match. However, if I'm going to soil my wrestling boots by coming down to your mid-card mediocrity, then there has to be something in it for me. What do I gain by beating your sorry ass all the way back to whatever toilet you crawled out of? Nothing. Nothing, that is, unless you are willing to put something on the line and the only thing you have that I would be even remotely interested in is the CCW Independent Title. He crosses his arms over his massive chest. So what's it to be little man? Do you believe enough in your own ability to put the title on the line? I'm thinking you'll run but surprise me......accept the match. Let me show you why it's ME that everyone remembers and not you. Let me show you how it feels to be laying beaten and broken at the feet of the greatest champion in CCW history. Your grandfather is a wise man John Coens.......it's a pity for you that you are too stupid to listen to him. So it's all up to you now. Accept the match and let me tear you apart, for tear you apart I will, John Coens, and do you know why? The Supreme Machine leans into the camera, his steely grey eyes fill the screen. BECAUSE I CAN!
