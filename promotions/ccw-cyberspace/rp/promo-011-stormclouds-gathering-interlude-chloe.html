Stormclouds Gathering Interlude: Chloe

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stormclouds Gathering Interlude: Chloe Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #11 Date: 02/15/03 Location: Seattle, Washington {The Seattle Public Safety Building. Lacey Skye Morris pulls into the parking garage, finds an empty spot to park, and gets out of her car. She goes inside the actual building.} {Inside the receiving area, a hustling local television reporter, Kate Kendall, huddles with a beat cop in the corner. She nods, slips him a twenty, which he quickly pockets.} Kate: Thanks, Matt. {He walks off, and she heads into the main receiving area, where she spots an old nemesis, Sergeant Powell. Powell walks purposefully through the area. To the Desk Sergeant, he snaps:} Powell: I still don't have the paperwork on the Chloe girl. {Kate approaches him straight on.} Kate: Sergeant Powell, just the man I was looking for. {Powell barely glances at her, and studies his clipboard.} Powell: Miss Kendall. Loved your piece last week on police officers who moonlight as male strippers. Really elevated the tone of television reporting. Kate: Hey, a girl's got to make a living, Powell. Powell: Yeah, the gals on the street tell me that all the time. What do you want, Kendall? Kate: I hear you've got a juvie here who witnessed an attack of some kind. Some guy in a mask with a gun? {Now Powell is annoyed. He puts down the clipboard.} Powell: If I ever find your source in here, he'll be walking the waterfront.... at night.... alone. You want the long or short answer to your question? Kate: I'll go for the long. Powell: All right then, here's your answer: No. Kate: All I want to do is talk to the girl! Powell: 'Girl' is the operative word. She is a juvenile. I cannot and will not release her name. {As Kate speaks, Lacey comes out of the elevator in the background. She does not overhear Kate's conversation as she approaches the desk.} Kate: I won't USE her name! I just want to know about the guy she claims she saw with a gun.... its not even related to the break-in she's been charged with. I mean, think about it Powell: a man.... with a gun. Some people would qualify that as a legitimate news story. Powell: That'd be a first for you. Kate: {trying to be sweet} I just want some information, Sergeant.... {Powell smiles at her as if he's falling for it and then:} Powell: Dial four-one-one, kiddo. Kate: Cops with snappy patter went out with Kojak. {She turns around and starts to walk off in a huff. But lingers when she overhears Lacey speak with to the desk sergeant.} Lacey: I was asked to come down and identify a girl who broke into the antique shop last night. {This catches Powell's attention. He is unaware that Kate has overheard.} Powell: {to Lacey} Chloe Mason? Lacey: If that's her name. Powell: Be with you in a second. {He turns to Kate, who quickly makes believe she is busy with something in her shoulder bag and hasn't heard a word as she moves toward the elevators.} Powell: {calling after her} Miss Kendall! Kate: What? {Here Powell does an uncanny Telly Savalas smile and wink.} Powell: Who loves ya, baby? Kate: Funny, Powell. {She watches discretely as Powell escorts Lacey through a door.} {Now in the adjoining hallway, Lacey walks down the corridor through the receiving area with Sgt. Powell.} Powell: Look, I don't understand.... We caught this kid.... We can make a good case against her. But not if you don't press charges. Lacey: No charges. Powell: Let me ask you something. This little bitch is trying to get off the hook by saying she didn't break in. She heard a disturbance, looked inside, and saw some masked lunatic with a gun who was threatening to kill everybody. {Lacey stops. She just stares at Powell for a long moment.} Lacey: {purposefully covering up the entire incident} Did she see anyone in a bat costume and a long cape? Powell: {smiles} Okay. Anyway, she had jewelry from the shop in her pocket when we nabbed her running from the scene. We got her dead to rights. Lacey: Sorry. Can I talk to her before you let her go? Powell: Sort of a Mother Theresa, there's no such thing as a bad girl, kind of woman, aren't you? Let me set you straight on something. You talk, she walks and she hasn't learned a thing. This girl needs to do a little time in juvie to straighten her out. Next month she turns eighteen, and her next visit here she gets processed through as an adult. She goes to the big house where the cons will pass her around for dessert. Think about it. {In one of the nearby interrogation rooms, Chloe is sitting at one of the tables her feet up, taking her confiscated stuff out of a manilla envelope.} Chloe: {happy} All right.... this girl is going to walk on outta here.... {Just then the door opens up, revealing Sergeant Powell and Lacey. Powell immediately sweeps Chloe's feet off the table.} Powell: This woman wants to talk to you. Give me one excuse to bust your ass while your still here, and you do ten on the taxpayer's money right here. Got it? Chloe: {sweet and innocent} Oh yes, Sergeant Powell, I certainly will, sir. Powell: {to Lacey} She's all yours. {He exits and Lacey stays there.} Chloe: Honestly, ma'am, I really do appreciate the second chance you're going to give me to become a useful member of society.... Lacey: If I let you out of here, I don't want anyone coming around asking about your little fantasies. That's the deal. Chloe: Fantasies? {gets it} Oh, you mean the one with you and all the psychos bursting into the antique shop.... right? I never saw a thing. Just made it all up. I know, I know, I'm fast. All my teacher's said so. Fast, but lazy. My lips are sealed.... word of honor. {Lacey gives her a stare of hard and vicious intensity. The kid wilts under the gaze. What she says next is as close to soul-bearing sincerity as Chloe ever gets.} Chloe: Besides, who'd I tell, y'know? {Lacey's glare doesn't soften, needing to impart the seriousness and gravity of the situation. This child could cause problems, and since she needs to figure out what's going on with Damsel, other problems she could do without...} View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
