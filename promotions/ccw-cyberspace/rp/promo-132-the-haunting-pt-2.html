The Haunting; Pt. 2

14 April 2003

The Haunting; Pt. 2 Devyl CCW Promo #132 Date: 04/14/02 Location: Boston Roxbury Sanitarium Victoria Devins and one of the many staff doctors, Doctor Lorenzo, walk down one of the dreary, sterile corridors. Dr. Lorenzo holds a clipboard in hand and walks with a slight limp. He is obviously enamored with the raven-haired woman, but does his best to attempt to hide it from her. Lorenzo: The Macario family is quite an interesting case, I must say. They haven't been here long, but they have been receiving the best psychiatric care possible. Yet their condition keeps deteriorating. Devyl: And you will not allow me to see the husband, Vittorio Macario? Lorenzo: No, it isn't possible. Vittorio is but a shell of the man he once was. He does little more than shriek, curse or mumble, depending on his mood. Devyl: But the wife is not as far gone? Lorenzo: No, she is still coherent. But every day she loses a little bit more of herself. It is quite distressing. Devyl: *nods* And the children? I was informed that the Macarios had two boys. Lorenzo: Indeed they do. They are being cared for by relatives in Baltimore. Psychologically they seem healthy enough, considering the circumstances. The doctor and the Goth Princess stop in front of one of the many doors aligning the hallway. Lorenzo removes a pair of keys from his belt clip and moves to unlock the door. Lorenzo: Well, this is it. Gabriela is inside. Devyl quickly looks up at the doctor, her features icy cold. Devyl: What did you just say? The doctor, slightly startled by Devyl's change in demeanor, falters with the key, hovering it right outside the lock. Lorenzo: I said that Mrs. Macario is inside. Gabriela Macario. Devyl is silent as she takes in this information. It's just too much of a coincidence. Devyl: I apologize for my outburst. Thank you for letting me see her. Lorenzo nods as he finally unlocks the door. Lorenzo: It's not a problem, young lady. He steps off to the side and holds the door open so that she can freely enter. Devyl starts forward into the dimly lit room. Lorenzo: Oh, one more thing... I don't recall, what did you say your interest in the Macarios was again? Devyl: *pauses in the doorway* I'm going to exorcise some demons. Victoria enters the room, allowing the doctor's questioning gaze to linger on her back. Her eyes adjust quickly in the gloom, picking out the features of the spartan room. No windows, whit walls, a 40 watt bulb incased in a cage on the right-hand wall providing the only illumination. A small end table upon which rests a single book... The Holy Bible... is placed against the opposite wall, right near the bed. Gabriela, late twenties, is lying the bed, but rises to a sitting position as Devyl enters the room, looking almost like a ghastly automaton. Gabriela: Who are you? Devyl: My name is Victoria Devins. I have been asked to look into your case by a friend of yours. Gabriela: Case? What case? Devyl: The house. Gabriela: *eyes glazing over* The house... Devyl: Yes. Your house. Can you tell me anything about it? Gabriela: *shakes her head* I don't want to. Devyl: You need to talk to me, Gabriela. Gabriela: *shakes her head again* I don't want to. Devyl is suddenly on the right side of Gabriela's bed, leaning over and speaking almost directly into her ear. Devyl: You WILL talk to me, Gabriela. If you do not, I will take you to the house so you can show me. Gabriela: NO! An evil presence lurks in the house! Devyl walks around the bed to look directly into the woman's eyes. Devyl: What kind of presence. I need you to start talking, NOW. Gabriela: I hate to think of it. I don't want to think of it. At night, I would sometimes wake up to find it leaning over me. When it was angry, the thing would cause dishes, pictures and vases to fly around the room! But mostly, it hated my husband. It hated Vittorio. It would torment him endlessly. It would drive him to the brink of insanity one minute, and then the next, it would... It would... Would... Devyl: It would what? Gabriela: I... I..t... Gabriela's back suddenly arches as if snapped forward. Her eyes roll back in her head, sowing nothing but the milky whites. Her voice becomes guttural and cracked as a twisted smile spreads across her face. Gabriela: *voice twisted and warped* You are wasting your time, woman. Devyl: *expecting this; bemused* And you are no true angel, Gabriel. The Lord's host do not posses His most prized possessions. Gabriela: *possessed* That is irrelevant. You are wasting time. You need to find me. Devyl: Why? Why do you need me to find you? Gabriela: *possessed* It is all part of the game. Finish up and find me. Find me before I find means to summon more of my own kind. Find me before this world no longer belongs to your master OR the God of Love. And then, Gabriela falls back into the bed as the spectral force releases her, no trace of consciousness left in her frail form. Devyl doesn't move from her spot. She doesn't like being baited, trapped or ensnared, and that's definitely what this was all about. There's no reason why the creature Gabriel would appeal to her to find him, unless it was a trap of sorts. There was also no reason for him to appear to her here, unless he somehow had something to do with this situation. Too many unknowns, too little time... View Devyl 's Biography Back
