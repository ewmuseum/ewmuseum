THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART V Koriann CCW Promo #124 Date: 04/06/03 Location: ??? The scene opens up in a warehouse... Why? Well, that's an easy one. That's where all the cool interrogation scenes take place in the movies. And since Koriann claims to be cool, she needs to interrogate her captured former colleague in a warehouse. So, we're in a warehouse. Carla Miller is sitting in a chair, hands tied behind her back, while a light bulb hangs above her chair, creating a circle of light around her while the rest of the warehouse remains in complete pitch darkness. Slowly, the diminutive woman regains consciousness and looks up. Carla: What...? What the hell? Where am I? What am I doing here? And then, for dramatic effect, Koriann walks forward and steps into the circle of light, right in front of Carla. She's dressed, quite stupidly, in over-sized military fatigues. Koriann: *deepening her voice to sound threatening* Only I get to ask the questions here, bitch! Carla: Wha...? Koriann? Is that you? What do you think you're doing!? Koriann: What did I just say! You've now asked eight questions within the span of fifteen seconds when your total question allotment is zero! That's punishment for you, missy! Bob! Tip the bucket! Bob: *from somewhere in the darkness* Yes, ma'am!!! And suddenly, black tar falls from the ceiling and douses Carla from head to toe. She sits there sputtering, trying to free her hands so that she can wipe the muck out of her eyes. Koriann: Now, answer my question... And tell me the truth! If you tell me one lie, the torture will continue. Now... What did you do with God? Carla: I... I... I didn't do anything with God... Koriann: Yes you did! He was living in that apartment where I found you! His earthly name is Brad Lamen. Now... What the hell did you do with him!? Carla: I don't know any Brad Lamen! Koriann: Bzzzzzt! Wrong answer! Bob? And suddenly, white chicken feathers drop from the ceiling and coat Carla. Koriann: HAW-HAW! This tar and feather gig never gets old! So... Last chance biotch... Where's Brad Lamen!?!? Carla: *starting to cry* I honestly have no idea what you're talking about! Koriann: Bob! There's a short silence. Bob: What? Koriann: What do you mean, 'What?' Give her the next treatment! Bob: *stepping into the light* Uh... We never discussed a third thing to do. Koriann: *short pause as she realizes this fact* DAMMIT!!!! Alright Carla, you sit there and think really hard about what you did with God. Me and Bob here are going to go shopping for more things to dump on you. You better tell me when I get back, or you're really going to learn what it's like when you screw with Koriann! Now come on, Bob! And Koriann grabs Bob by the ear, eliciting some 'ows' from the poor guy, and drags him out of the circle of light toward the door. Poor Carla remains sitting in the chair, covered with tar and feathers. Sadly, she's going to have to sit there for a while, as we're ending this completely unnecessary section of Koriann's search for Brad Lamen. View Koriann 's Biography Back
