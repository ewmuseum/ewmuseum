Darkened Days Blayze CCW Promo #52 Date: March 3rd, 2003 Location: Hilton Hotel Room #225 [The scene cuts to a quite relaxed and well kept Hilton hote room down in Alabama. A dim light from the nightstand table lamp reflects off a few photographed pictures which is lightly gripped in the fingers of Jerome Jordan. Tilted over the beige covered bed, bandana slowly slipping off the back of his head, not making any effort to replace it, his eyes clearly focused on the black and white pictures. The imagery of the photos strike Jerome with a gasp of flashbacks, as if he is living the current snapshot. The first photograph is one of Jerome giving props along side his long gone brother Darren Jordan. Quite a beautiful sight, the Jordan Brothers actually together for the first time since the last curtain call of the old CCW. Oh, how time flies..] 'Acquaintances may come and go but my memory holds the pictures with a firm grip. He was a good brother, a protective brother, no one ever fucked with me when he was around..but independence had to come at some point. I did not mean to desert him, it was for his own good. I cant always depend on people to be there for me, or vice versa. I am my own man, I walk my own paths and I overcome the obstacles on my own. Many of those obstacles have led to bloodshed but if it is the way I can get my missions accomplished, then by any means necessary is how I view life's challenges. ' [He flips the photo behind another one, which shows Blayze shaking hands with his former comrad Enigma in the ring at a CCW PPV, back in the summer of 2002. At one time, they could have made the most dominant force ever if only they could have stayed together. No one can deny that Menace II Society was one of the most profilic teams in CCW tag team history, sadly, too much controversy surrounded them.] 'It all could have gone the right way, if only I could have put my full trust into one individual who I knew had the potential to become a superstar. What is with the human world these days? Has everyone become the Devil's advocate? Darren turned out to be more overprotective than I thought and Enigma just seemed to be hungry for greed and exposure. Dammit, does anyone care about Compton's Finest anymore?' [He rips both photographs in half, tossing the pieces up in the air with anger] 'Why the fuck am I being ignored?? I am the WORLD FUCKIN CHAMPION!! I am the fuckin backbone to this company! It aint Lamen, it aint Enigma, and it aint Spider..Its Jerome 'Fuckin' Jordan, the Anti-Christ of Sacrifice. All I do is put over young guns..Can someone explain to me why in the Lord's name does Jason Dread, a no name before he ever had his face shown on national Television, get to go head to head versus the Black Jesus himself for the prestigious golden Heavyweight Symbol of CCW? THIS DOES NOT MAKE ANY FUCKIN SENSE!! *Sigh* To make things worse..that inbred trash worthy bitchmade child Enigma is still seeking bloodlust towards yourstruly. Damn, everyone wants a piece of the Icon these days, I must be a considered such a sacred artifact to be treat like a goldmind discovery. Well, the glory will just get stronger. I dont give a damn fuck about Jason Dread, for all I care its me, myself and I in the Lethal Lottery and it will be I who will win the Tag Titles. Only I will reign above the rest, Dread can go back and lick his own wounds and just leave me to set the nail in the coffin. I promise nothing but demise and only an advancement into the next round of the Lethal Lottery. All other will be restin' with the FUCKIN ANGELS!!! ' [Blayze bashes lamp off the nighttable, vaulting it half way across the room. His anger has finally erupted and it seems as if the true Blayze of old has returned to CCW..and to the Lethal Lottery. Oh may the Lord have mercy on those poor souls...] Amen.. View Blayze 's Biography Back
