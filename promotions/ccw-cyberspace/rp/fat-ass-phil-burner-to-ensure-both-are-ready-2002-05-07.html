''Fat Ass'' Phil Burner - To ensure both are ready

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which ’Fat Ass’ Phil Burner trains in his backyard ring and addresses The Supreme Machine ahead of their match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main ''Fat Ass'' Phil Burner Entitled: To ensure both are ready Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 11:26:37 Location: Burner's back yard This is a: Interview **The scene opens in the back yard of the home of 'Fat Ass' Phil Burner. We can see a tool shed, a swimming pool, and a large grill by the porch, but what stands out is the wrestling ring in the middle of it all. We enter the scene as the Fat One is bouncing off the ropes, delivering a clothesline to an adult black man. The man gets up and is lifted by Burner into the Bearhug Slam. In this part of the routine Phil usually looks into the crowd, and doing this, he notices the TV camera.** Fat Ass: Alright George, thanks fer comin' out. See you right back here same time tomorrow alright? **The man gets up, hand on the small of his back, which is arched to relieve the pain and pressure. The two shake hands and the man leaves.** Fat Ass: Don't mind him, he's jest a neighbor who offered to help me train fer my upcomin' match against th' Supreme Machine. Normally at this time in the proceedings us wrastlers like t' waste a few minutes to rant about how much better we are then the other guy. We'll find thet out soon enough. As I'm shor most of y'all are aware, I ain't much one fer talk. I like t' show my intentions by action, an' in fact, I plan on takin' action very, very soon. Now see, last Thursday on Lockdown I challenged the Supreme Machine to a match of th' highest magnitude. We both have two of the most, if not THE most, storied histories in CC-dubya. This match is necessary to determine who, once and fer all, is th' greatest. I have th' utmost respect for The Supreme Machine. I mean hell, why do y' think I'm out here right now, trainin' my fat ass off? It ain't to impress th' ladies, thet's fer shor. I'm givin' everything I've got fer this match. After havin' watched this past Shockin' Monday Night, I'm not all thet convinced thet SuMa is doin' the same. SuMa my boy, I don't think yer gonna make it t' Memorial Day Showdown. Hell, last night you almost got beat by another former great, Mr. Gobstopper. But like he has been in the past, Gobber was an idiot. So yeah, you landed yer Decapitator an' pinned him, then set out with yer shaven manslave, Ricky Thunder, t' break his body. How very impressive. Pussy. You ain' th' same as you was SuMa. Yer slipping. Hell after last night, I ain't all thet convinced thet our match is gonna be worth a hill of beans. I don't even know if I want to fight you any more. You shor as hell weren't focused on me last night. You think I came back here so thet I could hear myself talk? You sir are gravely mistaken. It's obvious thet I have not gotten yer attention. This Thursday on Lockdown, I aim t' do jest thet. I'm gonna do something SuMa thet you won't expect. An' after th' deed is done, maybe then I'll have yer attention. An' maybe then we kin do this th' way it was meant to be done. Rest easy SuMa. But keep one eye open. Now git outta here. **Scene fades to black as Phil throws a towel over the camera.** View Biography
