The Final Warning

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which Koriann is visited in her hotel room by a manager who warns her that she is about to be fired.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Koriann Entitled: The Final Warning... Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 20:14:36 Location: Nashville, TN This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up in Koriann's hotel room in Nashville, TN. She's sitting at the small room table, telephone in hand, obviously making a call to someone.] Koriann: 'Yeah, hi, my name is Koriann, and I'm a member of CCW. I'm just calling your facility to see if I can borrow two retards from you guys for Thursday night.' [Pause.] Koriann: 'You don't call them retards? Then what do you call them?' [Pause.] Koriann: 'Okay then, can I borrow two ‘special people' for Thursday night?' [Pause.] Koriann: 'No, no, no... They're not going to get hurt!' [Pause.] Koriann: Yes, CCW is wrestling, but they will be in absolutely no physical danger!' [Pause.] Koriann: 'Well, I want to dress one of them up as a ghetto thug, and the other one as holy girl, bring them down to the ring with me, and use them to ridicule my upcoming opponents. And then, we'll have them participate in an impromptu match wherein they beat on each other using their less than spectacular skills. During this match, I am going to point out to the crowd just how much Blayze and Angelica Lynn resemble the two retar... Uh, I mean, ‘special people'. After that, I'll enter the ring with a steel chair and lay them both ou... Uh, I mean, offer it to them to sit down after such an entertaining act.' [Pause.] Koriann: 'What do you mean, ‘it's so wrong on so many levels'!? You've obviously never used your ‘special people' to ridicule anyone before, have you!? I think you need to try it!' [A knock sounds at Koriann's door. She gives an annoyed sigh, stands up, unlatches the door, opens it, and sits back down. A man dressed in a business suit enters the room and closes the door behind him.] Koriann: 'So, this is what you do... Take one of your ‘special people', dress him up like your boss for a day, and ONLY take orders from him! When your real boss asks you what you're doing, you treat him as an escaped ‘special person' and lock him in one of those little rooms you guys use! And then...' Man in suit: *interrupting and very stern* 'Hang up the phone.' [Koriann glares at the man.] Koriann: 'Hold on a sec.' *covers the mouthpiece* 'Who the hell are you?' Man in suit: 'The person who's going to save your sorry career!' [The man grabs the phone out of Koriann's hands and slams it down in the cradle.] Koriann: 'Hey! I wasn't finished!' Man in suit: 'Yes. You were.' Koriann: 'You have five seconds to explain why you're here before I throw you out on your ass!' Man in suit: 'My name is Marty McSpew, and...' Koriann: 'Marty!? McSpew!?!? BWA-HA-HA-HA-HAH!' Marty: 'I advise you to stop laughing, because...' Koriann: 'BWA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HA-HAH!!!!' [SLAP!!! Koriann's head rockets back as Marty's backhand connects with her cheekbone. She sits in stunned silence, not believing what just happened.] Marty: 'I am here to inform you that you're about to be FIRED from CCW!' Koriann: *rubbing her face* 'W-what?' Marty: 'That's right! Spider is THIS CLOSE to pulling the plug on you!' Koriann: 'But... Why?' Marty: 'Because your one of the biggest jackasses to ever grace this federation! You're wrestling skills are abysmal! You constantly do and say things that embarrass not only yourself, but CCW as well! You're constant child antics always get you in trouble, and everyone HATES YOU!!!' Koriann: *slight pause* 'And that's bad because...?' Marty: *sighs* 'Koriann... You need to clean yourself up, or you're gone! Do you WANT to be fired? Do you want to go back to that dirty hall in Las Vegas and entertain the perverts with your strip shows and mud wrestling?' *long silent pause* 'I didn't think so.' [Koriann looks down at the floor and then back up at Marty.] Koriann: 'You're serious? I'm going to be fired?' Marty: 'If you don't change... Yes.' Koriann: 'Okay, then... What do I have to do?' Marty: 'Well, to start with, you need a fashion make-over.' Koriann: 'What's wrong with the way I look!?' Marty: 'Everything. I know you don't realize this, but nobody appreciates women wrestlers. The only reason ANY of you survive, is by looking drop dead gorgeous! Look at Lacey Skye Morris! Look at Candy Cummings! Look at Angelica Lynn! Christ, even Devyl manages to pull off the hot Goth chick thing! And now, look at you! This tom-boy thing you've been doing is definitely not doing you any favors!' Koriann: 'I'm being fired because of my looks!?' Marty: 'No, you're being fired because you can't wrestle. We're going to fix that, but that will take a lot of time. You're looks are pretty bad, and they can be fixed right away. If you don't look good, nobody has any interest in you...' Koriann: 'That's bullcrap! I...' Marty: 'That's the truth! The wrestling business is very unforgiving... Especially to females! Now, tomorrow morning, we're going to go down to the nearest salon and get you done up right. Following that, we're going to shop all day and create you're new image.' Koriann: 'Okay, that sounds good! Now, if you'll excuse me, I'll just get back on the phone and reserve my ‘special people' for Thursday Night...' Marty: 'No, you won't! You're not going to be pulling any shit! Not on my watch!' Koriann: 'But, I have to face Blayze, and...' Marty: 'And you're going to get your ass kicked! You don't need anyone at ringside with you for Blayze do that!' Koriann: 'Oh yeah? I'll show you, Mr. McSpew! My wrestling skills are fine just the way they are! I'll beat Blayze and show you that I don't need any help! I'm a former CCW Heavyweight Champion, you know?' Marty: *sighs and rolls his eyes* 'God have mercy on me...' [fade out...] View Biography
