The Day I Lost My Mind...

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comic dream sequence in which Mark Estep is questioned by an endless voice and confronted by his faceless inner self before waking beside his fiance.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mark Estep Entitled: The Day I lost my mind... Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 11:44:04 Location: In My Head... duh! This is a: Match RP (The scene opens with Mark and his fiance sitting in bed, and Amber has a book in her hand. He flips the page and Mark is watching televison. Mark's lamp is turned off, but Amber's is on so she can see the book she is reading. A shadow is cast apon the front of the book, so we are unable to see what book it is. Mostly for publicity purposes... But anyways, Mark looks over to her and starts kissing her neck. Amber starts shaking her head so Mark decides to stop when he's told. He isn't a felon or anything. So he sits back, and when he does, he hits his head off of the bed post. He holds the back of his head for a moment. 'Ouch!' he yelled... 'What happened?' asked Amber, his fiance. 'I just hit my head, didn't you hear the thud? Or were you to busy reading that stupid book?' he exclaimed. 'The book isn't stupid! It happens to be very good!' she yelled back, Mark quickly ensued in the argument. 'What the hell is it about!?' he asked, 'It's about a Mexican midget who gets struck by a car in the parking lot of a grocery store, why!?' (Just then, Mark slips into a transe, and he just sits there staring at Amber. Deep thinking goes through Mark's head while he is sitting there, motionless. Amber nudges him a couple times before he thinks...) 'Who am I...?' -You are the man- 'Where did I come from...?' -Your mothers *AHHH*- 'That's not true, I just don't think people know that I'm better than they are...' -Does Cianan know...?- 'Not yet, but he will.' -Does Blaze and Jordan know...?- 'They will when Me and Jack take their CCW Tag Team Championships from them.? -Why don't they already know?- 'Because I haven't really shown them.' -Why not?- 'Because I haven't had the chance other than run ins and sneak attacks...' -Why haven't you?- 'Because I haven't fought them in the ring yet...' -Why not?- 'Because nobody's booked me against them...' -Why?- 'Because they haven't.' -Why?- 'Ask them...' -Why?- 'Because they would know...' -Why?- 'Because they are the bosses...' -Who?- 'Spider...' -Who's that?- 'The boss...' -Why?- 'Ya know, you are really getting annoying...' -Why?- 'Because I said you are!' -Why?- 'Because you just keep asking me Why Why Why Why Why Why!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!' -.......- (quietly) 'I think he shut up...' -.......- '.........' -......- '........' -Why?- 'AHHHH!! You're driving me CRAZZZZY!!!' -Why?- 'Shut up!!' -Why?- 'AHHHH!!!' (Just then, Mark Estep sits up quickly from his bed and screams at the top of his lungs. He looks around and sees his fiance, Amber, still laying in bed next to him. He nudges on her and she moans and groans from being awakened at such an early hour...) 'What is it,' she asks, 'I'm trying to sleep...' 'I know you are,' Mark replies, 'I'm sorry, but I just had a bad dream...' 'About what?' she asks. 'Some guy asking me why, over and over again...' he replies.. 'Who?' she asks again, 'I don't know him, he just kept asking me, and I didn't like it...' 'Why?' she asks, 'Because it's annoying...' 'Why?'...... 'Because he said the same thing over and over...' 'Why?' 'I don't know, ask him...' 'Why?' 'Because he's the one that said it....' 'Why?' 'I don't know WHY he said it, he just did....' 'Why?' 'I don't know, maybe he felt like it?' 'Why?' 'Ugh... because..... hey.... wait a minute....' he said, as he turned to his fiance and grabbed her shoulder. She was facing away from him and out the sliding glass door leading out onto a balcony of sorts. He pulls her over and she sits up quickly and all of a sudden...... Duh Duh Duh!! SHE HAS NO FACE!! 'Why?' 'Ah!' 'Why?' 'Ahhhhh!' 'Why?' (Mark gets up from the bed an throws the blanket on her, but she quickly gets out of it and starts to follow him down the hallway. He turns the corner and runs down the steps and she follows, faceless as ever. Mark gets to the bottom of the steps and runs into the kitchen, where he slips on a cold, wet floor from a pipe under the sink leaking. He lays there for a moment, and passes out. When he comes to, he is tied to the wall with a few bungy cords.) 'What? Where am I?' Mark asks.... 'Not heaven, I haven't killed you..... yet.' said a voice... 'Who's there!? What are you doing to me!' yelled Mark.... 'Well, a normal person would have figured it out after the huge dramatical pause and then the word 'yet' being used... dumbass!' said the voice. (Just then, a person without a face walks out of the kitchen and sits down on the couch, facing Mark. The person crosses it's legs, so its pretty safe to assume that this person is a woman. She looks at Mark and Mark talks again...) 'Who are you?' asked Mark.... 'Well, thats simple really.... I am your inner self being. I do all the work... well, what you people call 'thinking'.' she replied, 'Every thought you think, comes from me.' 'So,' Mark continued, 'You are making me think this right now?' 'Oh no,' said the woman, 'This is very real. All your life you have been asking questions, and have been getting rewarded for great ideas, but do I get thanked? Hell no! And I have finally snapped. I am tired of being the voice inside your head that plans and predicts your every move, but gets nothing in return. The reason you have won so many matches in your life, all because of me. I plan out the moves you are going to use ten minutes before you even use them. I know exactly what I'm doing, and you know nothing. You never will... I am your mind, concience and everything you've EVER known! And finally, I'm done. I'm leaving you, and I'm never coming back. So from now on, if you need help thinking.................... Read a book!' (The woman gets up to leave, and she gets to the door. She opens it and starts to step out before... ) 'Wait....' says Mark, as the woman turns her head back, 'I'm sorry...' 'For what?' asks the woman... 'For never thanking, congradulating, or even appreciating you. Now that I think, well, you know what I mean, but I realise that you are everything that made me, me. Without you, 'The Malicious One' doesn't live inside me, and neither does the makings of being a CCW Tag Team champion. I was once the greatest when I listened to you inside my head, but lately I have been neglecting your credibility towards me, and I'm sorry. Will you come back? Please?' 'Mark, I'm sorry... but I've made my decision...' she replied, as she hangs her head and leaves the room, out the front door, shutting it softly behind her. Forever gone... and Forever worthy of a farewell.... (Then, as quickly as it started, it's over... With Mark snapping out of his transe to see his fiance Amber looking at him....) 'Why?' she asks.... 'Why what?' he replies... 'Why do you care what the story is about...?' she repeats. 'Um, because I, uhh, I was just curios... sounds like a good book.' he said. (And with that said... Mark turns over, switches the television off, and dozes off into a nice... gentle... thoughtless... dream.) View Biography
