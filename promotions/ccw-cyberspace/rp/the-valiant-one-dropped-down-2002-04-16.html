The Valiant One - Dropped down....

What this is A Match RP in which The Valiant One, Danny Bryson and Ricky Thunder talk on the New Strangers’ bus about Lockdown.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Valiant One Entitled: Dropped down.... Written in Tuesday, Apr 16, 2002, 07:56:38 Location: New Strangers' Bus on route to Boston This is a: Match RP *The scene fades in with The Valiant One, Danny Bryson, and Ricky Thunder sitting around on the New Strangers's bus. Each one of them wearing their respected NS jersey.* The Valiant One: I can't believe we lost most of our new recruits already. Ox Brown and The Desolation Angel are out of here. Bryson: I know, I'm not even sure what they did, but it had to be something bad to be fired like that. Thunder: You guys won't have to worry I am here to stay. I am not going to do anything to get myself fired. The Valiant One: That's good news. Bryson: Yup. The Valiant One: So guys, what about Lockdown this week. I know I am in the second match with some Guardian guy. Danny you are taking on Benny Blades right? Then Ricky you've got a tag match with Yama Tatsu, right? Bryson: Yup. Ricky: Yup. Bryson: It will be tough it is a king of extreme tournament match, so I could be in trouble with Benny Blades. The Valiant One: Don't get worried about it, just go in with confidence that you can win and don't think of any way that you could lose, because you have what it takes to win that match. Bryson: Yeah... The Valiant One: What about your match Ricky? Ricky: Well...both Yama and I have mystery partners. I wanted to go into a handicap match, but someone has shown up to be my partner. The Valiant One: Oh yeah, I saw that. He looks like a big guy. Kinda familiar looking too. Ricky: I don't know. What about your match man? The Valiant One: Oh, from what I have seen Guardian looks like a great talent. I could have my work cut out for me, but being booked in the second match of the night pisses me off and the frustration will be taken out on Guardian, so right now I just have to say sorry to Guardian for whatever may come from this match... *The scene fades out as the three continue to talk....* View Biography
