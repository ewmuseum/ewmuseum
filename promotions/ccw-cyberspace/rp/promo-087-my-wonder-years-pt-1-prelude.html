My Wonder Years (Pt. 1, Prelude)

My Wonder Years (Pt. 1, Prelude) Scorpion CCW Promo #87 Date: 3-16-03 Location: NYC High School ::::An age-old tale of how it began for me. I was sixteen years old and living in the toughest part of New York, the City. The citizens of New York City are the toughest sons of bitches you will ever meet in your life and will always be. Everyone in School had their cliques; there were the jocks, the nerds, the theatrical and the gangs. I found myself under the category of the gangs. It was never meant for me to join a gang; I just sort of crept into the habit of it. I was a tough kid, coming from a father who was a World War II veteran and a boxer at the athletic club. Also having a mother who was tough as nails as her father, my grandfather, was a World War I veteran and a military school teacher. My parents were strict no doubt about that and they always taught me how to be strong and to never take shit from anybody. That thinking and way of life I had made me fit in real well with the gangs. I still remember to this day how it all began. I was sitting at my lunch table talking about the Yankee game with my friends Drake, Mike and Ricky when a gang member who went by “Mad Dog” decided that it would be fun and games to pick on the younger kids in the high school. At this time I was still known as Jeff Steiner, the gang life would make me known as Scorpion. :::: Mad Dog: Hey guys, what kind of food do you got there? Drake (scared): BLT. Mike (scared): Turkey and Ham. Ricky (scared): Meatball Wedge. :::: Mad Dog stars at Jeff as he continues to eat his sandwich. :::: Mad Dog: Hey dumbass what kind of sandwich do you got? Jeff: Why does it matter to you? You want me to tell my mom to make you one tomorrow because your mom is a crack whore? Mad Dog: Excuse me? Jeff: You heard what I said, not my fault your brain is slower then a normal human brain. Mad Dog: You’re a real wise ass you know that? Jeff: And you’re a real jackass. Mad Dog: Why are you asking for a beating? Jeff: I’m not asking you for anything, you came over here and started bothering me. Mad Dog: You got a problem with that? Jeff: Obviously any normal being would see I don’t have the tolerance for stupidity, but then again your not a normal human being. Mad Dog: What in the hell is that supposed to mean? Jeff: It means you have very small brain cells. :::: At this point everyone in the cafeteria notices the verbal battle between the two and the cafeteria becomes quiet. :::: Mad Dog: That’s it you little punk I’m going to make you shut up. Come here boys! :::: Mad Dog waves over to his gang at the other end of the cafeteria. :::: Jeff: What you can’t take me on your own? You’re afraid that a sixteen year old is going to be up an eighteen year old who is the big scary leader of a gang? Mad Dog: I am not afraid of you. Jeff: Then fight me one-on-one. Mad Dog: You want it you got it. (Looks over at gang) Don’t worry boys I got this one. :::: This is the moment that basically started it all, I got up from the table and he threw a punch at me. I ducked and followed with a right hook that my dad taught me. It hit him square in the chin and knocked him out. Everyone was in a gasp and truthfully, so was I. His gang scurried out of the cafeteria very fast, afraid that they would have to face me. At that moment I could feel every single eye in the cafeteria looking directly at me, in amazement of my strength and my bravery. I felt the power and I loved it. I was faced with a five-day suspension and Mad Dog was faced with a concussion. I remember receiving a phone call from Drake saying how I became a legend in school due to my actions. :::: Drake: Dude you’re an Icon now, everyone in school can’t stop talking about you. Jeff: That’s awesome Drake, I can’t wait to get back to school. No one will want mess with me. Drake: Exactly man, but what about that gang? Jeff: Fuck em, we are starting are own gang. Drake: You want to start a gang? Jeff: Hell yeah, I thrive on the adrenaline I feel on having so much power. We will be able to give a face-lift to the streets. Drake: You think so? Jeff: Hell yeah, this is only the beginning. :::: I was right, it was the beginning. The beginning of a chapter in my life, the street life took me over. I wanted to be the best at it. The main function that leads me to feel this way was the adrenaline I felt with all that power. WHY? Why did I do this to myself? This was my “Wonder years.” Sure I’m not happy about it, but this was only the beginning. There are many more stories to tell. The feeling of adrenaline running through my veins when I felt all the power lead me to the streets and it was only a matter of time when a Sixteen year old boy named Jeff Steiner would become a man…named Scorpion. :::: TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
