Just Blink Brad Douglas CCW Promo #57 Date: Location: Present Day When you are alone, resting comfortably in the solitude of your own thoughts, what kind of man are you? Are you the same man you show to the denizens of your world or are you an entirely different man? Different in the regard that you're locked away with only those closest to you given the proper key or code to the security system you've somehow managed to conjure up out of thin air. Perhaps a bit of both. Care to take a gander? It is said that actions speak louder than words. As a direct result of this it is also said that one man's actions will determine who he is and what he is in the long run. But is this really true? Perhaps... perhaps not. And do actions speak the loudest? Yes and no. For those feeble minded mental midgets out there perhaps this is true. Thanks to society's politics you have been fooled, not taught as you believe, into playing the game of follow the leader without question. You are all, to coin a phrase, puppets. You are controlled by the puppet master without even realizing. What sick and pathetic puppets you are. But I digress for the sake of your own sanity. Actions do not speak the loudest in terms of who you are. They merely facilitate a person's opinion of you. And that, puppets, is why actions speak louder than words. Because the action you take will determine what the higher ups in this hierarchal society think of you. Not the empty words you speak and empty promises you guarantee with your life. But society is weak. Just like humanity, society is not perfect. I say this due to the fact that most of humanity doesn't have the balls to speak their mind. And in saying mind I speak of thoughts. It is thoughts that make you who you are. Those thoughts when you are alone with not a soul watching, caring, or simply being. Thoughts are the ticket to your mind. And inside your mind you are your true self. Nobody is unaffected by the political society of today. Nor it's weakness. Therefore, all of us, even those who say they don't... wear masks of self as if they were a security blanket promising to protect you when times get rough. They are used to protect and to shelter despite the obvious health risk. To get ahead in life and to lose yourself in our sea of insanity. The phantom that is your thoughts can only speak for you if you let them. But will you? Probably not. But it matters little as I have just set the stage for what is called... my point. And that is what ultimately matters. MY point. I, Brad Douglas, have given you a careful balance of action and speech all week long in hopes that those without knowledge of my prior existance would procure it. And I can honestly say that none of you have even grasped what I have said thus far. In defense, many of you might wager a statement along the lines of your uncaring for what my point is/was, but the reactions seen around the Project are quite contradictory to such a statement. Therefore, you would be made to look like the fools you are if you were to even step into that puddle of mud, if you will. So what is the point of all this psycho-babble you ask? To speak as much as I can without blatantly giving anything away. 'Tis my way. 'Tis the only way. 'Tis your downfall. You see... I have seen action upon action upon action this week over my return and I have yet to hear anything but prattle escape your dicksuckers or anything but wasted effort shown on your parts. Could that be because you're all taking me too lightly? Perhaps. But we'll see. Consider this... A man that let's his thoughts betray him is not really a man at all. He is a simpleton playing this game of legends like he is the man of all men. Dancing our dance like the pathetic little puppet he is, in hopes that one day he may become a player, rather than a pawn. And there are some of us, smart enough to read the thoughts of another, without letting words and actions confuse and abuse. I am your mind reader. I am your sinner. Yet I am but a man with the master plan. Walking inside the minds of other men. Knowing what you all think. Waiting for one... of you... to just...... blink. ... View Brad Douglas 's Biography Back
