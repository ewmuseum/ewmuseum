Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 18:23:37 Location: Your Mind This is a: Match RP 8 Teams 40 men and women Only one left standing The old stand side by side with the new Friends and Enemies reliant on each other for survival The good with the evil But sill……..only one man is left standing Legends against Rookies They say it cant be done………………….but what do THEY know? Angel: “The time is here, No more talk……………………Now more than ever do we pray………………..we know HE will steer us right……………….HE will guide our progress……………..HE will clear the path of sin ahead………………and Guardian will do the rest. It seems that peoples lips are tight……………..that nobody knows who the unknown one are………………the strangers who stand side by side with Guardian and Danny Bryson are just that…………….strangers. For when all is said and done it does not matter……………we know of Bryson’s pedigree……..he can look after himself and Gaurdian…………well…………..they say it cant be done………….but what do THEY know? This Sunday at Group Therapy shocking things will happen………………unexpected things do and will happen Cummings…….. Mullet………….. Scorpion………… Blayze……….. and Carnage ………..truly a formidable force to deal with……………I wont insult you all with stories of David and Goliath because as everyone knows…………those stories don’t always come true……………..but this Sunday is not a story…………I`m not saying this will be easy………nothing is easy………..but if you have faith in HIM and yourself then anything is possible Can it be done? This is yet to be seen Do we have the ability? Yes Do we have the faith? You know we do And this Sunday the rest of CCW will find out first hand They say it cant be done…………………but what do THEY know? CCW – get ready to repent – This Sunday will not be a day of rest But it will be the first day of the rest of your lives.' View Biography
