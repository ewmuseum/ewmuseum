Ricky Thunder - Loss of Innocence

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which Ricky Thunder, in a Mexican hotel room, vows revenge on Mr. Gobstopper after a disqualification.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: Loss of Innocence Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 21:37:43 Location: Puebla, Mexico This is a: Interview *Scene opens in a hotel room in Puebla, Mexico. Ricky is sitting on his bed, with his back propped up against the headboard watching tv. He flips channels quickly, barely taking the time to even notice what is on. Suddenly he turns the tv off and whips the remote across the room. As it hits the wall, the back comes off and the batteries fly out of it. Ricky gets up and paces around the room for a seconf before going over to the window that overlooks the heart of the city.* Ricky: 1 day. 1 more day and I could have been celebrating victory right alongside my mexican friends. They defeated the French, and I would have defeated Coens. But instead, you Gobber, have blemished my near perfect streak in CCW. You have added another smudge to an otherwise near perfect cycle. You see, I was costed a win, when Carnage interfered. I ignored him. I let it slide. But you....you crossed the line. *Ricky looks down and kicks the remote parts under the chair and walks over to the door. He opens it up and peeks out into the hallway looking both ways before returning to his room. He locks the door behind him and turns off the lights. He heads over to where the chair is, and sits down propping up his feet. He kicks his shoes off and a sigh of relief is heard.* Ricky: You see, I had made a promise to myself. A promise to the family doctor. A promise to the soul of my brother, the late, Thunder. You have cost me the honor of keeping that promise. You made me desecrate my brothers spirit. When you interjected yourself into my match, and got me disqualified, you fucked up. You may want the fans to think you are their hero. You may want them to respect what you are planning. However all you should be planning is your funeral. They think you came out, just to bash Coens up a bit, I know better. I know you came out to blemish my name. I know you just wanted to see if you could force me to break my promise. So you see, Gobber, what you have done is put me in an unsettling predicament. *Ricky gets back up and goes over to the bed. He stretches out across the bed as he removes his t-shirt and throws it on the floor. He pulls the covers back and slides under them* Ricky: I now have two options. A: Forget about what you did, what you cost me, and how I must make this up to my brother. or B: Bring you the same trouble you have brought me. I guess I will have to think about it for a while. I mean, after all, maybe you were just trying to help. But you know, your ignorance will cost you. You think that Coens will be a good place for you to make your mark on CCW? Well that may be. However by bringing me into the mix, you have just sealed your own fate. I will not stop until I feel you have recieved enough punishment to pay for what you have cost me. To pay for what I must now do. What must I now do you ask? Well that remains to be seen, but I assure you that it will be at your expense. The young inexperienced Ricky Thunder, the innocent rookie, will be no more. You Gobber have sheared my innocence. The feeling I was had, the compassion fo rwrestling, has transformed. The feeling I now feel is more like that of destruction. With you interjecting yourself into my business, and Phil deciding to return to CCW, just because SuMa is back. Well that proves only one thing. EVERYONE here, is dumber then once thought. I mean, Blayze, he is a complete moron, and he showed that by bringing that smiley faced jackass back, and now you two. I mean Jesus Christ has everyone in CCW, lost their minds? Ya know what? It don't even matter anymore. I now to the point, where no matter what I do, SOMEBODY wants to intervene. From here on out, I don't care if you are Blayze, Fanatik, Kensuke, Coens, Spider, SuMa or the god damned pope! Stay the hell out of my way. If you get caught in this path, you better be prepared to feel the Thunder! View Biography
