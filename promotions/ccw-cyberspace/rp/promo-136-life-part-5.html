Life (Part 5) Dan Logan CCW Promo #136 Date: 15th April 03 Location: Amazon Rainforest 'Will you take him Daniel?' We had been pals, pals of the worst kind maybe because we still expect too much from one another. Yet still I can't help thinking that I owe him something and that my refusal is in some way destroying our relationship further. I shake my head and he sighs 'Please Daniel reconsider, this could end in being the worst mistake you ever make' his voice is shallow and full of sincerity. What is it about this kid that could end in being so dangerous to me? What does he have that I do not posses? Once more I shake my head and he nods knowingly as he attempts to search my face for that little hint of regret that never comes. My face is as a brick wall to him and hiding the regret like an escaped convict attempting to hide his face in the crowd. 'If only I could my friend I would. I have much to do and bringing him would only slow my journey to find her' the excuse was in itself a poor one but it held my only reason and nodding his head and looking me into the eyes he smiles intently. 'Good luck my friend... this journey will lead only to more tears yet you have decided that you must go. Take this with you' he says as he hands me the paper within his own hands. Its back is moist and the text had begun to seep through it unto the back of it. 'Thank you my friend. May you stay well and live long...' the words are said wishfully as the full extent of his illness has dawned on Dan. The old man would not live to see the end of the month let alone until they saw one another again. It had been six years since Dan's last visit and him returning after today would lead only to upset on everyone's part. 'And also you Daniel, may you know when the devils approach you and may you lead yourself from their arms back into safety' he knows himself the time for his life to draw to a close is almost near and yet he still continues on as though nothing in the world would stop him, his frail white body dying around him as his mind still feels as young as if it were only his twenty third year. 'If I could my friend I would bring him with me in a second. He has learnt much from you and will prosper in anybody's care now' and so I left, leaving him with my final words as he drew breath to compliment me back. The wind had left the area now and the bright sun had returned with what seemed to have been a longer and more concentrated amount of heat. The cabin itself was made from oak and its varnish of the land had been so placed to act as glue between poles. Dan stepped from its gaze and smiled with pride for himself... now he knew where she was staying and he would still have to cope with no-one, just the fresh bold air and him as it should be. The trees stand large before me almost defying my very existence and yet I know deep down that these trees fear me. Deep down these trees know all and have all yet they fear what I have and they don't... I have life; I have the choice to move or to lie still. Whatever there aren't many things that can take that away from me. 'Go swiftly from here before the menace attempts to catch up with you!' I turn around and look down toward the old woman that stands looking at me frustrated with my lack of need for speed. My grin widens and I laugh, who is this frail old hag to tell me how fast I am to travel? When was she promoted above me and developed that right? Her frustration seems to heighten and I grin almost as though I were a child once more and had just come home to find a large present lay there waiting for me. 'What ever you are grinning for you must stop! You must leave now for your own safety! Boa has sent the menace for your blood. You will not survive for long if you do not leave now!' Her consideration for me leaves me almost touched and the act of kindness I feel an urge to have repaid, however this woman has come to the conclusion that it must be paid in a certain way save risking everything on one thing I must save my own life. 'I will go woman when I so choose to go. Whether he has sent the menace after me or not!' hate spurs me on, within my head the lights are now flashing. How could I not see it when it had been so clear, it had stood there staring my in my eyes. Why could I see that I was no longer his star pupil and that he had moved on in a way that I never had thought possible. He had made himself a new angel. The sun still beats down upon my back and the sweat pours down my face, brushing back my long velvet hair I smile. My scar smarts badly yet I do not wish to go back to a place where I am no longer wanted. The woman snarls at me and walks away her warning wasted on me. I shake my head at her pettiness and laughing at her I continue on toward the trees, the walk back to civilization would not be that long yet the perils that lie within those trees are better faced in the light than when the night has come once again. 'Thank you woman. You have made up my mind for me and I shall travel now before the night sets in' my muttering seems to grasp her attention for a moment but ignoring my words she continues on. How could she ever understand how it feels to e betrayed by a man that you had once thought had loved you like a son? She could no. My father had committed suicide and he had been there to help me... just as he had helped this menace after me. It had changed, no longer was I the little boy who had refused to grow up even when serving her majesty the queen. No longer was I the foolish man who had charged into the China shop before looking out for the bull. Now I was fully-grown and my message had been clear I wanted to end the arguments I had once had and begin my new life a fresh. Finding Emma and taking back Christopher would be the beginning to this and the end... who knows. All I knew was that I needed to get away from here and open the wrapped paper within my hand. I walk into the trees through the winding path that leads me there. There is a slight movement within the bushes and turning in towards it I see and hear nothing any longer. My imagination begins to play tricks on my mind and the small doubts to my safety begin to creep forward. I know I am safe for he would not have sent him yet. If this child was to be sent follow me it would not matter anyway for I would vanquish him. Why are there doubts within my mind? Why is the uncomfortable feeling within my gut lying waiting for me to panic as though there is nothing else I could do? I tighten my grip on the paper and pick up my pace a little, submerging myself into the dense undergrowth my feeling doesn't go away it only grows. The further I advance into the forest the more my feeling attempts to surpass my knowledge. There is a second sound around the bushes and turning to my left a small bird flies from out the bushes beside me and up into the air. Its wings cut through the air like fins through water and for a moment I stand gazing in amazement. Relaxing a little I continue onward into the forest watching my step as I go, the bright colors and many smells attempt to entice me in many directions but I stay on track going forward into the deepest parts of the forest SWISH! TWONK! The birds begin to shout loudly and other sounds erupt around me. I turn to my side and take a glance around, the silence that I had been worried about had vanished but looking toward the large tree in front of me my calm was all but destroyed. A large throwing knife is wedged into the bark and panicking I can no longer keep my cool and I begin to run. Onward and onward I run attempting no longer to keep to my designated path but to escape from the thing that had thrown the knife and to escape what the old woman had foretold... the coming of a man she named the menace. Whatever he was and where ever he had been I did not care. I continue on running ducking under branches and jumping over tripping veins of the trees in my front. I trip on an up rooted root and fall flat to the ground hitting my head hard. I cannot move any further and looking upward I can see why... he has caught up with me... ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
