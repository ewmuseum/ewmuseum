Who am I?

16 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Who am I? Adam Christ CCW Promo #13 Date: 02/16/2003 Location: Prison. 1995. [ A man is thrown to the ground. His legs automatically curl under him as the leg of another thrusts itself against his ribs. The man rolls to his side, embracing himself in agony. ] Life. [ A siren is heard. Footsteps gather. There is screaming. A struggle. The man lays. No pity is provided to him. He is alone. ] Reality. [ A group of officers hold back one African-American male. He looks angered. He is angered. Others around him step back. He, too, is alone. The officers drag him away from the man on the floor... from the source of his anger... from the purpose of his hate. Words escape his lips. Words pleading his truth. Innocent words mixed with a vulgar flavor. Desperate words, full of anger and fear. He is ignored. Slammed against a wall. Silenced by concrete. Silenced by force. ] Pity. [ The angry black man, hands binded by steel, is removed from the room. An officer comes to the aid of the man on the floor. By the officer, the man is lifted. Raised. Placed to his feet, standing where he once lay. For a moment, the mans legs feel rested. He is lifted off the ground. He is weightless. Freedom. ] Repeat. [ The officer erases the moment of freedom the man felt, throwing the man to the concrete wall as well. The man's forehead smacks it, first. The man falls back. He lays, again. Pain. Confusion. His face is red. His vision is blurry. More screaming. Another struggle. Vulgarity. Tired. Blackout. ] Judge. 'FUCK YOU! DON'T YOU FUCKING CRY! YOU FUCKING PUSSY! GET UP! GET THE FUCK UP, YOU FUCKING DICK! [ SHIT, THE FUCKING ALARMS!! ] YOU MOTHER FUCKER! I'LL FUCKING KILL YOU! DO YOU HEAR ME!? I'LL FUCKING KILL YOU!!' Wake. [ The man is laying on a small bed with white sheets. He's facing upward. Shadows hide the entire right side of his body. His left eye is seen open, however. Awake. He sees red. Blood still pours. Still. When will it end? When will it stop pouring? He feels tired. He feels weak. Screams echo in the background. Screams echo in his mind. ] Hope. [ As the man struggles to stay awake, to keep his eye open... his mind wonders. He thinks of his past. He thinks of his present. He thinks of his future. ...A smile slowly crosses his face. Softly, he speaks... ] Adam Christopher: Eight more years... [ His smile gives his blood a new crevice to slip into. He chokes back. Swallows. Closes his visible left eye. Sleep. ] -- Time Passes. -- January, 2003. [ The same man, Adam Christopher, is seen walking away from the prison. His clothes are dirty. His figure is taller, but he's thin. Very thin. As he walks, something becomes noticable. Adam has no right eye. In fact, the area where his eye was supposed to be has been sealed up completely by flesh. Only his left eye remains, open wide... not blinking. As he walks, his voice is heard in the background... as a narrative. ] Adam Christopher: Eight years. Eight long, quiet years. But now I'm free. Now, I can go home. To my mother. [ The scene fades to black before coming up once again at the trailer park Adam once lived in. Now, however, it isn't a trailor park... it's an empty field of loose rocks and broken pavement. A desert of grey. Adam stands in the middle of it, looking around... confused. He speaks in question... ] Adam Christopher: Mother...? [ The scene suddenly cuts to quick images in Adam's past. Black and white photos of his mother, laying on the floor, facing upward. Holes where her eyes should be, overflowing with blood. Two small, red pools. Then the scene cuts to Adam in prison, leaning against a fence as the other convicts lift weights or play basketball. Adam's only eye wonders off, staring into space. Adam's mind leaves him. ] Voice: What the fuck are you staring at, boy? [ Adam's mind is instantly returned to him. He blinks, staring forward at the black male questioning him. Adam, partly confused, struggles to answer... ] Adam Christopher: ...E-Excus-- [ The man jabs Adam harshly in the gut, causing him to fall back against the fence and topple to his side. The man then proceeds to kick the shit out of the fallen Adam, three or four others joining in as well. ] Black Male: You don't NEVER fucking stare at me, you understand?! [ Adam responds by coughing up blood. ...Adam's voice is heard in the background as a narrative once again... ] Adam Christopher: I love this feeling. God, I love this feeling. This extacy given to me... that I couldn't return. [ The scene fades to black, returning to the empty trailer park. Adam still stands there, but his confused appearance is gone. Adam speaks once more... ] Adam Christopher: ...You told me not to fight back. You told me that if I fought back, you would leave me. ...Just like dad. [ Adam's face becomes clustered by the appearance of disgust. ] Adam Christopher: BUT YOU FUCKING LEFT ME ANYWAY!! WHY DID YOU LEAVE ME, MOTHER!? WHY DID YOU LIE TO ME!? [ Even though his appearance is full of sorrow and anger, no tears form. ] Adam Christopher: You gave me love, mother... and I did EVERYTHING you told me!! I was THANKFUL! But THAT wasn't GOOD ENOUGH FOR YOU!?!? [ Adam's eye shuts tight. ] Adam Christopher: THEN FUCK YOU, MOTHER!! FUCK YOU, FUCK FATHER, FUCK EVERYONE!! IF RELEASING YOUR LOVE FOR ME THROUGH PAIN AND BLOOD DIDN'T SATISFY YOU, THEN I'LL RELEASE MY HATE FOR YOU THROUGH PAIN AND BLOOD TO OTHERS! I'LL FIGHT BACK!! I'LL FUCKING FIGHT BACK, MOTHER! AND WHEN I'M DONE... [ ...Adam's voice suddenly shuts off. ...His eye opens, and he stares forward, blankly. ...A few seconds pass before he speaks again... ] Adam Christopher: ...When... I'm done... [ ...As Adam stands there, lost in his own words... as the scene fades to black. ] February 14, 2003. [ Adam Christopher is seen leaning against a building of some sort. His head slowly tilts upward as he stares at the night sky. When doing this, the moon causes his face to light up a bit, showing that a beard of some sort is begging to form on his face. His appearance is still dirty. Adam's voice comes across in the background once again... ] Adam Christopher: What is my purpose now? ...Why am I here? Why am I alive...? [ As Adam stares upward, the scene cuts to the day before... CCW Lockdown, February 13th. The crowd is on fire. Chaotic screams flood the air as every second goes bye. The camera pans over them, as it did when the show began. BlackJack Slater and Joey Doyle introduce the fans at home to CCW Lockdown in St. Louis, Missiouri. The camera continues panning over the crowd, but the scene suddenly freezes. The camera zooms in harshly, to a single lone soul in the audience... Adam Christ, himself. Adam is sitting while everyone else is standing, staring forward. The camera then zooms out quickly, and the camera continues to pan over. Moments later, President Sylkk's music hits, and CCW Lockdown begins. However, the scene cuts back to Adam, still staring at the sky. Adam's voice is heard in the background once more... ] Adam Christopher: That will be my new home. [ The scene cuts to CCW Lockdown once more, showing various violent clips that occured throughout the night. One being Yama Tatsu kicks the shit out of some midget. Another being Koriann cracks a lead pipe across Red Headed Step Child's skull. Violence. Blood. Pain. The fans eat it up. The scene quickly cuts back to Adam, whose voice is heard once more. ] Adam Christopher: Men and women destroy one another for the praise of others. One human delivers pain to another, and is rewarded by the cheers of thousands. For every drop of blood they sacrafice, they are given love. [ A smile crosses Adam's face. ] Adam Christopher: I want to recieve this... love. I need to. But to do so... I must deliver pain. I must sacrafice my body and their bodies to earn the love and praise of thousands, just like them. No, I don't desire money... I don't desire power. I want the screams of thousands to make up for the screams I've given throughout my life. I'll use them to fill in the gaps. I'll use them to bring me happiness... to bring me love. The love I've wanted for eight years. ...The love my mother stole from me. ...And if getting that love means my death... I'll give them their fucking moneys worth. [ Adam pulls his right hand up to his face, which has something in it. ...A mask. Adam straps the mask to his head, lowering his hand to his side, and turning his smile to a grin. Adam looks back to the sky, as the camera pans back... showing that Adam's leaning against CCW Headquarters. The scene then fades to black. ] February 15, 2003. Voice: Ah, you again! [ The scene opens within CCW Headquarters before the desk of Nick Cashew, one of CCW's talent cordinators. Adam steps up to the desk. ] Nick: I see you went with my advice on getting a mask, eh? [ Adam nods. ] Nick: Good. If you want to get anywhere in CCW, you can't look like you do. [ Nick smirks. Adam isn't amused. ] Nick: Hm... anyway, what do you want? Adam Christopher: I was wondering when I'll debut, and who my opponent will be. Nick: Eh...? Tell me, Mister... [ Nick searches through a few files on his desk, before stumbling on a certain one. ] Nick: Ah, Adam Christopher. Were you not given the notice? Adam Christopher: ...Notice? Nick: President Sylkk feels that you aren't quite ready pysically to compete. Adam Christopher: What? Nick: I mean, look at you! You're tall enough, I'll give you that... but you're as skinny as a twig! I mean, Red Headed Step Child probably has more meat on his bones than you do! Adam Christopher: I AM ready. I'M READY! Nick: Whoa, calm down, now. Maybe if you gained some weight and went to the gym a little more, you could debut in about... three months? Adam Christopher: THREE MONTHS!? [ Adam slams his fists on top of Nick's desk, leaning forward. ] Adam Christopher: I'VE WAITED LONG ENOUGH. I'M READY NOW! Nick: Er... ahem. [ Nick coughs a little, tugging at his collar a bit. ] Nick: Look... how about this. I'll just change your profile a bit, alright? Just between you and me... [ Nick looks down at the files, pulling out a pen and writing things down... ] Nick: It says you weigh 150 pounds. How about we raise that to 190, how's that sound? Adam Christopher: Fine. I don't care. Nick: Good. You can pass as a cruserweight. ...I mean, what President Sylkk doesn't know won't hurt him, right? [ Adam doesn't respond. ] Nick: ...Let's see. [ Nick taps the tip of the pen on the desk a bit, thinking. ] Nick: I'm going to have to change your name, alright? The name 'Adam Christopher' just doesn't sound like the name of a CCW superstar... how about... Adam Christ? How does that sound? Adam Christ: Fine. It sounds fine. Nick: Alright, good. I have a gimmick for you as well... [ Nick scrambles through his files and pulls out a sheet of paper, handing it to Adam. ] Nick: Basically, you think you're Jesus Christ and that you've come to punish sinners. It's a warn out gimmick used in wrestling federations everywhere, so you can look around if you need any pointers. [ Adam takes the paper and shoves it in his pocket. ] Adam Christ: When will I debut? When!? Nick: Er... how does the Lockdown on the 20th sound to you? It's the next one coming up. [ Adam grins... ] Adam Christ: It sounds good. Who will I be against? Nick: Let's see, here... [ Nick turns to a computer next to him, scrolling down... and then coming to a stop. ] Nick: Ah! Andrew 'Ace' Hawk needs a quick boost. You'll face him. Adam Christ: Andrew 'Ace' Hawk? Nick: Yeah. He's our new Operations Manager. Adam Christ: He's my opponent. My first opponent. ...He's the first stepping stone... Nick: Er... wait, now. You're going to give Andrew a 'boost', remember? [ Adam chuckles to himself, turning and walking to the exit. ] Nick: Hey, wait! Do you understand what that means?! You need to LOOSE-- [ The door closes shut behind Adam as he leaves. Nick looks dumbfounded... ] Nick: ...Shit. These kids never listen! God damn egos! [ Nick picks up a phone, dialing numbers quickly. ] Nick: ...Yes, may I speak to President Sylkk, please? What do you mean, he can't speak?! ...He's busy!? Damn it... yeah, tell him I called. ...Is it urgent? Yeah, it's-- [ ...Nick pauses for a moment. ] Nick: ...No, it's not urgent. Forget it. [ Nick hangs up the phone. ] Nick: Puh... why was I worried? That boy is a shrimp compared to Andrew! Andrew will take care of him, even if he fights back! [ Nick smirks, straightening up his files. ] Nick: Heh... what a fool--uh? [ Nick blinks, rembering that he forgot to give Adam a copy of the CCW Rulebook. ] Nick: ...Oh, well. What harm could he do, anyway? [ Nick shrugs and continues to straighten up his files. The scene fades to black. ] February 16, 2003. [ The scene opens to several past scenes of CCW close-calls. A huge variety of wrestlers pinning another up to a two count before the other gets a shoulder up, or kicks out. Each of these clips happen quickly, but various famous CCW faces are seen. Those of Neurosis, Benny Blades, Kensuke Suzuhara, Jason McCallister, The Valiant One, THE Multiple Man, Mr. Gobstopper, Enigma, Fanatik, Akiko, and Blayze, just to name a few. Adam Christ's voice is heard in the background as these scenes continue rolling. ] Adam Christ: Human beings, after being put through hell, push themselves simply to succeed. To not fail. They've been through so much pain and agony, so much humiliation, that their desire to win forces their shoulder off the canvas. [ The clips continue to go bye as the camera pans back, showing them to be coming from a small television set... and Adam to be sitting before it. Staring intently, Adam continues to speak. ] Adam Christ: Willpower. Hatred. The greed of succession. These are the things these tortured souls posess that drive them to continue. ...These are the things that allow them to rise when fallen. It is these things that drive people to desperation. It is these things that force someone to do anything, ANYTHING... just to win, walk backstage, and claim his or her paycheck. Money... that's their goal. [ A smirk crosses Adam's face, his left hand rubbing his chin. ] Adam Christ: I'll use these things to achieve my goal. Not money... but praise. ...But, how? What must I do to achieve this? [ The television screen begins to flicker until, suddenly, various violent clips of past CCW events are shown. Glass table match, thumbtacks, baseball bats, chairs, tables, 'circus match'. Bloody pictures of certain superstars flicker across the screen for a moment. And when it all is through, the television screen goes black... and the sound of cheers seeks it's way out of the speakers. ...A grin crosses Adam's face. ] Adam Christ: That's IT. THAT'S what I need to do! I need to GIVE pain! I need to PROVIDE them with BLOOD! THAT IS WHAT WILL GIVE ME PRAISE! THAT IS WHAT I AM STRIVING FOR!! And Andrew 'Ace' Hawk... YOU will be the first stepping stone! Thursday, YOU will help me test my theory! [ Adam hunches over a bit, grabbing his hair and pulling it forward... ] Adam Christ: All my life, pain has been provided to me... and I've accepted it with open arms! I LOVED IT ALL! But, throughout all of the suffering, I've NEVER been praised! I've NEVER been loved for it! Meanwhile, YOU'RE praised for the pain you recieve and the pain you give to others! You claim it as 'entertainment', your JOB! You're PAID as you recieve praise from the fans! And when you recieve hatred from them... when the only cheers you recieve are when YOU are bleeding... you cover your wounds with money. Well, I'm SICK OF IT! I desire no MONEY! I DESIRE THE PRAISE I WILL RECIEVE BY DESTROYING YOUR SOUL! WITH EVERY DROP OF BLOOD THAT ESCAPES YOUR FLESH, I WILL BE GIVEN CHEERS! AND THE CHEERS FROM THESE PEOPLE WILL FEED MY WILL TO CONTINUE, TO LIFT MY SHOULDER WHEN I AM BREATHLESS! [ Adam releases his hair, jerking his head up and grinning at the camera. ] Adam Christ: I TIRE OF THE TASTE OF MY BLOOD, ANDREW! I WANT TO TASTE YOURS!!! [ The scene suddenly cuts to black, but Adam's whispers echo in the darkness. ] Desire. Pain. Humiliation. Greed. Hate. Love. Live. Repeat. ...I am... ADAM CHRIST. View Adam Christ 's Biography Back
