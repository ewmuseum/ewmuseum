The Second Book of Nyte: Chapter 4 - The Blood God Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #99 Date: March 18, 2003 Location: The Throne Room [Where we last left Gabriel, he was in front of a door adorned with numerous Khornate icons painted on the walls in very bright and hot colours. Yellows and Oranges. The man walks through the door that was painted. Thinking that we would be entering the final wing of the library...you are partially right. We enter there, and there are blood and gibs everywhere! It is a slaughterhouse of human effluence. Once again, like the Nurgle wing, it is littered with dead bodies, but this time around, along with that, there are arms, legs, torsos, heads, digits, and other body parts all still freshly bleeding. The sight and smell would be enough to make you gag. Of course, there are fights breaking out amongst the daemon packs, with them going too far in all cases. Nyte passes through most of the destruction in the library and he reaches his goal. he passes through a portal leading to his final destination...The mountain of skulls. It's not that high right now, because not many lives were taken, and since skulls are rather small...you can imagine. Nyte simply looks up to see the brass throne and to see the massive beast that is Khorne. He also looks around to see that he is on his island, onto the ocean of blood. He looks back to see the massive beast that is the Blood God. He looks like the rawest interpretation of Satan, but yet, he has massive wings that are hidden, the horns that are much larger, and he seems just overall bigger and more muscular.] Khorne: Ah, undivided one. What brings you in front of me? Nyte: I received your gift, o mighty blood God. Khorne: Ah, excellent. That electric current along with my power, spanning dimentions, and coursing through your mark, caused the rift that allowed my gift to come to you. I would have sent a Bloodthirster, however there was only enough power to send the Bloodletter. Has he made quite an impact? Nyte: Indeed he has. Tell me, is it easy for him to retain human form? Khorne: No, unfortunately, any chance he can get, he will return to his natural state... Nyte: A smaller version of you I suppose? Khorne: Right on the open wound as usual, undivided one. I would have sent some help to you earlier, but that skank Slaanesh sent one before I could. No doubt you'll encounter two more servants to the other two. Nyte: Nurgle and Tzeentch. Khorne: Of course. What they will send, will be their own discretion, Nurgle might send a Nurgling. Nyte: What a waste. Khorne: I know...In numbers they are powerful...by themselves...well...There would be much to be desired. That coward Tzeentch on the other hand, he has a multitude of creatures he could send to you. He never could beat me in a straight fight you know. Nyte: Not without using his magic. Khorne: Magic is a cheat. Nonetheless, are there any more gifts I can bestow upon you? [Gabriel for one moment thinks, and comes up with an answer.] Nyte: I have a match this coming week, I need the strength to get through it. Khorne: Consider it done. [Khorne lifts his giant axe into the air, and with a mighty roar, strikes it onto the ground. Thunder claps and lightning shoots from the crack in the island. The lightning strikes Gabriel's body, and catapults him into the ocean of blood. Gabriel surfaces from there, now soaking with blood.] Khorne: Undivided one, I have given you the gift of Daemonic Strength, it will multiply your strength by two. use this gift wisely. Nyte: Thank you my Lord Khorne. [Gabriel once again passes through the portal he came in, back into the Black Library. A bloodletter (Different from the gift Khorne gave him) charges Gabriel, and with a single swipe of his fist, he goes flying across the wing, landing on a warped desk, impaling the former servant.] Nyte: Wow. Dynasty will never know what hit him. {fade} View Gabriel Nyte 's Biography Back
