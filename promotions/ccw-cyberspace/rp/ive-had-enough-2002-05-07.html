I've Had Enough

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long monologue in which The Supreme Machine dismisses Coens, Carnage, Cianan and the current roster while preparing for a six-man tag at LDT.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: I've Had Enough Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 16:21:28 Location: Supreme Compound This is a: Match RP A huge television screen explodes in a shower of sparks and glass before a massive leather lounge chair comes flying from off camera, destroying what remains of the television set. The wreckage begins to smolder as the camera pans around a dimly lit, otherwise empty, room. Something moves in the shadows against the far wall. Think you can kick the crap out of me do you? Over rated am I? Just another stepping stone you have to cross on YOUR way to greatness? You wouldn't know greatness if I walked up and slapped you right in the face! FOOL! A massive figure slowly begins to emerge into the light. It's The Supreme Machine and he doesn't look happy. Since I returned to CCW I've heard nothing but whinging wannabe's complaining about me coming back and picking up where I left off...AT THE TOP. John Coens attacks me with a chair and talks about how he's sooo good. I challenge him to back up his actions and face me for his CCW Independent Title and what does he do? He goes on about how he doesn't want me "yet". What he really means is that he is afraid of me. He knows that I'd put him in his place and walk away from the match with his title strapped around my waist. Next, Carnage or ExMa or whoever the hell he is decides that he's man enough to challenge me, the greatest champion in CCW history, to a match. I accept but what happens? I'm still waiting for him to step up. Sure, he, like Coens, tried a sneak attack and got a couple of cheap shots in on me but does anyone remember what happened to him in the end? I do! He ended up being put through an announce table and knocked unconscious... BY ME! The Supreme Machine is now standing directly in front of the camera. His arms are crossed over his massive chest and he's glaring down the lens in distain. Who else is there? Ahhh yes. One of my partners in this six man tag match on LDT. Cianan. What was it you called me? A gold whore? Yes, I think that's what it was. Then I believe you went on to moan about how there are other CCW greats returning to the fold and stealing your spotlight and disrespecting you and everyone else who has been in CCW since we left. Well let me ask you something Cianan. What is it you think you've done to earn my respect? From what I hear you're nothing but a schizophrenic drug addict and where I come from that earns you nothing, well maybe pity, but certainly not respect. Can I name the last five CCW Heavyweight Champions? No I can't. Furthermore, I couldn't care less who they were because it doesn't matter! Compared to me they are nothing. The way I see it, any federation where Tim Inman can hold the major title four times can't have any talent in it worthy of respect. Sure, I lost the title to him but we had an arrangement! I let him win! He gave it back to me at the next card, three days later if I remember correctly. What happens after I leave? He actually beats people for the title and then holds onto it for, what was it, 120 days all up? The camera follows The Supreme Machine as he walks past the destroyed television set and out into a long corridor lined with trophy cases filled to the brim with various awards and memorabilia. Do any of you seriously think that the powers that be here in CCW would be welcoming all of these ex CCW wrestlers back with open arms if you current crop were up to scratch? It's all about ratings and face it, you guys just weren't cutting it. He continues walking along the corridor until he at last comes to closed door. He opens it to reveal his private training facility. The camera follows him in as he enters and moves towards the gym equipment that dominates one half of the large room. Now don't get me wrong, I'm not saying that there is no talent amongst the current crop of CCW wrestlers and I'm not saying that I have no respect for any of them. What I am saying is that respect is something you have to earn, it's not something that just gets handed to you on a platter like some of you seem to think. To earn respect you have to prove yourself, and that doesn't mean you sneak up on someone and hit them from behind or when their eyes are shut like some of you seem to think. I've earned my respect. I've got nothing to prove to anyone, I've already done that. So now we come to LDT. Seems that I've been teamed with Cianan the junkie and Blayze. Not exactly the partners I would have chosen, but so be it, I have to play with the cards that are dealt to me. Up against us there is that naive fool TVO, the CCW President himself in Spider and a man I do have respect for.. Kensuke Suzahara. What is there to say other than I plan on walking away from this match with yet another victory under my belt. Sure, people may be wondering how I'll work with Blayze, a man who is after the same thing I am in the CCW Heavyweight Title but that battle is for another day. He earned his shot at it last Monday and good luck to him. If it should come to pass that one day down the track we have to face off against each other for the CCW Heavyweight Title then, and only then, will his business be any concern of mine. Same goes for Kensuke. I have no quarrel with him, business is business and at LDT I'll be getting down to business. That's a promise. The Supreme Machine lays down on the bench press table and begins his workout. But I've had enough of jealous, spineless nobodies disrespecting me and everything that I have achieved. I've said it before but I'll say it again because some of you are either deaf or just plain stupid. If you want a piece of me then step up like a man and take your shot, I'm not hard to find and I guarantee each and every one of you that it will never be ME doing the backing down. But I'll tell you all one thing... He places the weights back down on their stand and sits up to face the camera as it slowly zooms in on his hardened features. You step into a ring with me and I'll do what ever it takes to beat you. I plan on regaining my CCW Heavyweight Title and if that means I have to tread on some toes to do it then I won't just tread on them, I'll damned well squash them to dust. And you want to know why I'll do all that? The Supreme Machine's steely grey eyes fill the screen and seem to burn through the lens. BECAUSE I CAN! . View Biography
