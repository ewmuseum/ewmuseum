The Supreme Machine - I'm Still Coming For You Neurosis

2 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which The Supreme Machine, on a shoreline at dawn, vows to take back his CCW Heavyweight Title from Neurosis.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: I'm Still Coming For You Neurosis Written in Thursday, May 2, 2002, 12:29:46 Location: ??? This is a: Interview The sun slowly peeps over the horizon, bathing the angry ocean in vivid yellows and pinks. Remember who you are I remember The surf pounds against the unmoving shoreline Remember all that you have sacrificed I remember A lone gull squawks hungrily as it flies along inches above the foaming sea. Remember all that you have achieved I remember The wind begins to blow in from over the ocean sending great columns of foam sailing onto the land. Remember why you are here I remember A lone figure stands silently staring out at the sea. Remember your destiny I remember Another massive wave breaks against the shoreline sending a wall of water high into the air. REMEMBER YOUR DESTINY I remember The wind begins to die down. The lone gull lands on the beach and stands squawking at the sea as the lone figure turns and begins to walk slowly away from the rising sun. The massive figure holds his thick black overcoat tightly around him a chill morning breeze continues to blow in from over the ocean. REMEMBER YOUR DESTINY The man stops and looks up into the brightening sky. His mane of black hair blows over his face as he turns, still gazing up at the cloudless morning. I REMEMBER The lone man turns and looks down the camera as it slowly zooms in. Neurosis! I don't know how you managed to make it through Group Therapy with my CCW Heavyweight Title belt still strapped around your worthless waist.. just dumb luck I guess, but I think you should remember what I said to you before the PPV. I told you that if I had to fight my way through the entire CCW roster in order to get my hands on you, I would. I told you that I'm coming for you. I still am. The Supreme Machine wipes the hair from his face. I couldn't care less if all of CCW joins forces to try and stop me getting to you. I've heard the rumors going around about how some of the wrestlers here are planning to team together to stop me. I don't care. It just proves how afraid they are. And they should be. But it doesn't matter how many of them there are. In the end it will me standing triumphant over their battered, beaten bodies. They will fall and I will continue on to you Neurosis.....and I swear I will make you suffer. The wind again picks up and The Supreme Machine's face vanishes behind his mane of black hair. I will make you suffer and I will regain my CCW Heavyweight Title. That I promise you. And if there is anyone else out there who thinks they have what it takes to try and stop me, I suggest you come out of the shadows where you're hiding and step up and face me....if you've got the guts! I'm not hard to find and I don't care how many of your asses I have to kick to get what I want. I will not be stopped....not by John Coens, not by Carnage, not by Ken Suzahara, Cianan, Yama Tatsu, no-one. If I must, I'll beat all of you one by one. It is my destiny to stand tall over all of you, and stand tall I will....... Once again The Supreme Machine clears the hair from his face. His steely grey eyes fill the screen. BECAUSE I CAN!!
