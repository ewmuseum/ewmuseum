The Valiant One - 6 man tag...

7 May 2002

What this is A Match RP in which The Valiant One, relaxing on a beach, discusses the six-man tag team match booked for Lockdown.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Valiant One Entitled: 6 man tag... Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 08:19:11 Location: unknown This is a: Match RP *The scene opens up on The Valiant One relaxing in the sun at the beach. He notices the camera coming in on him and he begins to speak through his mask.* The Valiant One: Thursday night...a 6 man tag team match was booked for Lockdown. On one side we have Blayze....The Supreme Machine...and...Cianan... *The Valiant One pauses, gets up and begins walking down the beach.* The Valiant One: On my team, I've got Kensuke Suzahara and either Phil Burner or Spider. The problem is that I have had problems with all three of these guys in the past. Kensuke and I have had our problems recently, up until he has that change of heart recently. I believe Ken can be trusted, but I am not going to go in to this match completely trusting of him. He will have to prove himself to me and prove himself to the fans. *A frisbee comes flying into the scene as some kid comes running up to The Valiant One who just picked up his frisbee.* Kid: Hey! You're The Valiant One! What are you doing here? The Valiant One: Just relaxing on the beach. Kid: Awesome! But TVO, why isn't Dirk training you right now? The Valiant One: I get the day off today. Kid: Oh... The Valiant One: You better run along man... Kid: Ok, bye TVO!!! *The kids runs off as The Valiant One waves him a goodbye and then continues to walk down the beach.* The Valiant One: Maybe I should be training today? I probably shouldn't take a whole day off. It will probably get me a little behind in my training. I do need a day to rest after that match with Fanatik last night though. *The Valiant One walks for a few moments being silent and then looks back towards the camera.* The Valiant One: Lockdown..I need to focus...Lockdown...teaming with Kensu and most likely Spider. I am not sure but I think Phil Burner might be unable to compete on a crowded schedule. Either way we will have a very strong team... but the opposing side is also very strong. Blayze I have dealt with many times before. We know eachother in and out and if we get to hook up in that ring, everyone better look out. Lockdown will also mark the first time I ever get in the same ring with The Supreme Machine. I really hope I get a chance to get a piece of SuMa and see how I fair. I can kick the crap out of him and I know it, it is just that people think too much of him, but all I think is that he is just another stepping stone I have to cross to someday be the best. And...Cianan...we have crossed paths a few times before. He once cost me the Hardcore Championship. Now this Thursday I'll be seeing him in the ring for the first time in a long time. Cianan and I have never and probably will never get along. I'll get to see him Thursday...Cianan, your fate rings within the walls of this world, and Thursday your fate is to be on the opposite side of the ring as me..I'll see you there... *The Valiant One pauses then looks into the sky.* The Valiant One: It's time to throw valiant out the window... *The scene fades to black.* View Biography
