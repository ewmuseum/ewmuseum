COTR...Why Offer Gold So Quickly? Lightning the First to Fall...

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A limousine promo in which Venneman Pendragon and Ben Davis discuss his disqualification in his debut and he threatens Lightning in the Champions of the Ring tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main 'The Crusader' Venneman Pendragon Entitled: COTR...Why Offer Gold SO Quickly? Lightning The First To Fall... Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 08:39:10 Location: Chicago, Ill. This is a: Match RP (The scene opens outside of the posh Hilton Hotel in Chicago, Ill. There is a silver stretch limousine waiting out front, and more than a few people have gathered around to see whom it is the car waits for. Some people hold cameras, while a few seem to be carrying plastic swords. There is also a cameraman there, with the initials CCW written on his camera. Obviously we are watching a promo for the CCW. Without warning, the back door of the limo opens, and someone shouts PENDRAGON!!!! It is not Venneman Pendragon who steps from the car, however. From the limo emerges 'The Big Time Bastard' Ben Davis. He raises his hands in a gesture to the crowd, delighted because he thinks this multitude of people is here to greet him...) Ben: Glad I waited to get outta the car. This way, even more of my fans have gathered here to see their Hero, Ben Davis. (Ben smiles warmly to the crowd, but they begin to boo immediately. Ben looks at them and lowers his head in a saddened gesture. Suddenly, he raises his head and points toward the front of the Hilton. From the doors emerge the man these people obviously came to see, 'The Man, The Myth and The Legend', Venneman Pendragon...'The Crusader'. Decked out in a sleek black shirt and black pants, He is all smiles as he approaches the crowd around the limo. The close around him, some with their cameras flashing, others with their notebooks thrust forward hoping for an autograph. He signs some, mainly for the smaller children before saying...) Venneman: Sorry I don't have more time to spend with you fans, but I have important business to take care of...Make sure you're watching Thursday though, cause I am gonna take care of business with Lightning. (Venneman follows Ben into the door of the limousine and motions for the cameraman to follow. He settles back into the seat as the fully stocked limousine takes off. Venneman drinks from a water bottle while it appears Ben has found an Amstel Light beer...) Venneman: Nice of you to come, Bob...Can I offer you some refreshment? Bob: No thanks...Only got two hands and it takes em both to operate this monstrosity... Venneman: Very well then... Ben: God damnit Venny...What the hell are you so happy about? You didn't even win your match last night. Didn't lose but surely didn't win. Venneman: Ahh yes...My match...Although I am not happy with the way it turned out, I am happy with the experience. It had been over three years since I stepped in the ring. I expected to be a little rusty, and I was. Unfortunately, the cobwebs didn't clear enough until we were already disqualified. Did you see how I snatched Matthew out of the air and drove him into the rail? That was power. Those were instincts, which I must say, I still have. Ben: Venny...DQ!!! I coulda taken you the other night. Venneman: I think not Ben. How long has it been since you have been in a ring? (Ben scratches his head and replies unsurely) Ben: Two years or so? And I am still fresher than you are. Venneman: Think what you will. If you truly believe that you can handle The Legend, maybe we oughtta find out. I do not take kindly to insults, though you are a friend, loudmouthed though you be. Perhaps I could be the travelling interviewer on your payroll. (Ben puts his hands towards Venneman palms out. He shakes his head...) Ben: You don't take kindly to humor either. Look. I was kidding. I've seen you break men larger than you on your knee. I believe I shall stay retired. So you are not upset at all about the match? Venneman: Only with the verdict. Were I at the top of my game, I would have crushed Matthew like the worm he is. There would have been no contest. He is damned lucky he met me on my return, and not now...Now that I have rediscovered my abilities. Rediscovered my faith in my skills in battle. Double DQ...Cannot remember the last time I was involved in that...But Matthew. You do not get off so easy. Someday...Maybe not today, nor tomorrow, we shall meet again. That time I will be on top of my game. Then you will feel the full force of Pendragon. Be it now or later, realize that the Last Crusade grows dark upon your horizon. I do not forget my debts, and I fear I owe you a trip to the hospital. (Ben chuckles and starts ribbing Venneman again...) Ben: Glad you're so proud of a double DQ...I mean come on! What were you thinking? Who is gonna give you any recognition for THAT!??? (Venneman chuckles himself at that...) Venneman: To topic number two. The Champion Of The Ring Tourney. Someone must have noticed, because I have been entered. Ben: WHAT??? You have been here maybe a week and they are already giving you a shot at the belt??? Venneman: Ahh yes...Championship Gold available to me through a tournament. How delightful. What I do not understand is why they would do such a thing... Ben: Yeah, you haven't showed me much either... (Venneman continues as if Ben said nothing. His eyes gleam with his Gold-lust....) Venneman: I cannot believe that they would give me a shot at the title when they know I will win. Eventually it would have come to me anyway, but they choose to lay it before me on a plate! If I were another competitor I would be pissed. What incentive is there? King's Gold always goes where it belongs; around my waist. The federation officials must truly desire I strong champion. It has been long since I last toiled in a tournament for a title. Of the three that I have been in, I have never lost... Ben Yeah, you were always a pain in the ass to us regular wrestlers... Venneman: And why not? When you put the work and time and dedication into this that I do, you should be the champion. Case closed. I plan on destroying everyone in this tournament and keeping the title for a very long time. Once I lay hands on gold, it takes either a stripping or a closing to wrest it from my hands. I feel sorry for the pathetic souls who do not stand a chance. It is sad. Ben So, uh, who do you have to wrestle first? (Venneman laughs, a truly mirthless sound...) Venneman: Lightning. (Venneman leans toward the camera and rests his elbows on his knees. His cold blue eyes burn with intensity...) Venneman: I know you watch, Lightning. Much like the child, frozen with fear of the monster under the bed refuses to take his eye from the light of the door, so too do you shiver, unable to look away from the power before you. Were I a pathetic excuse for a man such as you I would be scared as well. I am not pathetic, however, and I hold no fear. There are no men like myself that I need to worry about. I cannot believe that you were allowed to enter this competition. You may well be the worst wrestler in the federation. I saw your match with the other incompetent, Sylkk. Why did you even show up? I mean, if you wished to set the record for time it takes to lose a match I could see it, but my God man! Did you even land one move? Why didn't you use your head and stay home rather than absorbing Sylkk's blows with it? I have had to wrestle some poor wrestlers in my day but I believe you to be the poorest... Ben: I don't know...I remember some shitty ones. How else do you think I won so many matches...er...hmm... (Ben cuts off and the 'Oops I just fucked up what I was saying' look crosses his face. It is Venneman's turn to poke fun...) Venneman: That my have been the most right on thing you have yammered on tonight Ben. (Ben sits and says nothing. Meanwhile, Venneman has refocused on the camera...) Venneman: You, Lightning, are merely the first step in a short road to my glory. And I always hit the first step...Hard. In my entire career two things have remained constant. First, the satisfaction I take in destroying my opponent. I revel in the screams as I crush a man's spine upon my knee. I love the fans I gain because of my brutality. Second, I love the glory of defeating all who stand before me. Unfortunately for you, right now you are in my way. Prepare for your destruction my pathetic friend. The Last Crusade spares no man, least of all those too ignorant to leave the real wrestling up to their BETTERS. Prepare for pain, prepare for humiliation.... (Venneman starts laughing, softly at first but soon he is roaring maniacal laughter. Ben looks at him as if he just got off the boat. With a final close-up of his wild eyes, the scene fades to black...) View Biography
