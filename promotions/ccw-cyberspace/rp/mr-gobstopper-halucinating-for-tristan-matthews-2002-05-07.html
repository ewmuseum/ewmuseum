(For Tristan Matthews) Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 00:9:45 Location: A Hotel Room This is a: Interview *Scene opens in a hotel room, dimly lit. The camera wonders around the room until finding Mr. Gobstopper unpacking a suitcase and laying his belongings out across the bed. He turns around, noticing the camera, and calls for it to come closer.* Gobstopper: Damn, I feel like an Osbourne. Anyhow, I don't have much time as I have to get to the arena. It seems this Tristan Matthews is having flashbacks of high school wrestling. A coach named GOBSTOPPER. Here's an idea, hey Tristan, don't be placing my ass in your pornographic daydreams. I'd appreciate it. Now go away! *Gobstopper escorts the camera out of his room and slams the door. Fade out.* View Biography
