What this is A Match RP told as verse and scene descriptions recounting the life story of Darren Jordan before the Group Therapy tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Darren Jordan Entitled: Life's Path of Struggle(Group Therapy Tournament RP) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:42:45 Location: Location Unknown This is a: Match RP The Poetry Begins So Intriguingly… I witnessed what many don’t want to view The scenes have scarred my eyes for eternity Darren is shown kneeling down in front of a wounded friend, drenched in his own blood. A few days later he is standing by his other friends’ side as they mourn at his funeral. Introduced to a whole new lifestyle A beginning of an era filled with travesty He is introduced to a crypt-style beatdown, which later leads to Darren being inducted apart of the Crypts. As time passes by, he becomes the OG of one of the roughest Crypt crew in Compton Aided only the ones that meant it all to my life Made sure that safety and security was dawned upon them Shots of Darren are shown coming out of the courthouse with full custody of his younger brother Jerome. He gets Jerome through school, pass the streets and even inducts his own brother into the Crypts. Jerome was never ever harmed when around his brother Tragedy struck at times that were the most inconvenient Tears Flowed From Every Angle, None could be held back Darren & Jerome are by their mother’s side as she passes away from breast cancer. Darren receives news that his father had left town and has since returned. Allah Shaheem is murdered on 124th street with Jerome by his lonely side. Success wasn’t much of a factor nor a main focus Lacked certain skills to maintain the dreams I wanted to see become a reality Darren never finished college, he earned a scholarship in football but blew it away for the streets. He never lasted in jobs for more then 2 weeks, his shortest was 3 days and it was his last resort for some cash. Then It all came to a sudden downfall My life took a turn for the worse and it was not a joyous era Darren is accused of 2nd degree murder but manages to walk out of the court a free man. Thinking he is untouchable, he is arrested, charged and sentenced with a case of arson, he burnt the house of his former Girlfriend and broke her newest boyfriend’s left ankle. He was sentenced for 5 years in prison at the Arizona State Penitentiary Luck would have it that God was listening to my prayers A light re-opened my window of faith, thank the lord Darren received news that his brother Jerome was going to bail him out of Jail. As the days pass, the papers are signed and Darren is now a free man after only 2 years. The Future tells only could ambitions, hoping to be reborn Time to unleash the wrath inside on the unforgiving World Darren has vowed to get revenge on the world, with his brother by his side, he may accomplish a lot more. He has already won the tag titles for the 2nd time, could a Group Therapy shocking win with 4 other people by his side, catapult his career in a zone of stardom? Only the future holds the answer..
