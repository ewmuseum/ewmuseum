Stormclouds Gathering part 6 Chloey Mason CCW Promo #78 Date: 03/11/03 Location: Seattle, Washington Outside a small diner in the middle of the day, a battered white van with a local television logo stenciled on the doors pulls slowly into the diner's parking lot. The driver of this van also doubles as a cameraman, which makes him very useful indeed. Kate Kendall, the news reporter, rides shotgun. She sees Chloe Mason over by the side of the diner, talking with great animation to a group of street kids about her age. She is demonstrating what happened to her in the antique store, her encounter with Lacey Skye Morris and the serial killer that was out to kill them all. The whole incident looks pretty spectacular with the way she is pantomiming it. Kate: There she is. Wait here. Kate climbs out of the van and walks over to the group. Kate: Chloe? Chloe Mason? All talk ceases as the teenagers turn to check out Kate. Chloe is startled. Kate: Come here. I want to talk to you. Chloe does a double take, then glances over at her friends. Most of them, male, seem to be impressed. Kate is a major babe. Feeling threatened of her status as 'hot thing on the block', Chloe leaves her friends and walks over to Kate. Chloe: {smart ass} So.... You wanna join our gang, lady? The hazing is pretty rough. Kate: Stop hallucinating. Chloe: Well then.... What do you want? Kate: You were involved with some kind of incident at an antique shop, right? Want to tell me about it? I work for a television station. Now Chloe places her. Chloe: Oh yeah! I've seen you. You look pretty stupid standing out in front of burning buildings and riots and stuff. You need anyone to, like carry your gear when you do that? I'd love to be on TV every night. Kate: Sorry. Guess I got the wrong girl. Kate makes to turn away. Chloe: Hold on. Chill. You were asking about the stuff that happened the other night.... Thing is, I sort of promised this one chick not to spill, you know? This actually interests Kate. She reaches into her purse and takes out her wallet. Kate: What guy was that? She begins counting twenties until Chloe's hand snakes out and grabs them. Chloe: Her name was Lacey Skye Morris. She comes into the shop with this furious look on her face and says, I'm going to kill you, and I say.... Kate: Whoa. Slow down. What was the name of this shop? Chloe: I don't know, there's no sign out front. Just.... 'Antiques'. See, the window was smashed and the burglar alarm was going crazy, so I go in to check it out and make sure nobody is hurt, when this woman comes down, angry as all hell. Kate: {bored, disappointed} That's it? Great. You rip off an antique dealer, she hears the noise and investigates. {to herself} That'll make the news at eleven. Chloe: You want to hear about the other people or not? Two of them. And one says, 'I've come to kill you, Jonathan!' Because, you see, there was this other guy there with that Lacey chick. Then later another woman shows up.... making this spectacular entrance.... and says, 'no, I'm here to stop you'! Kate: {puzzled, still skeptical} Do you know who these others were? Chloe: Uhm? What's it worth? Kate: One fifty if it checks out. Chloe: Two. Kate: Go. Chloe: I don't know the others, but this Lacey chick and me are pretty close. I mean, she came to bail me out because she realized it was a mistake and all, and she's like this good Samaritan type. Told me she grew up on the streets, so she understood. We're real tight now. Give me the two hundred and I'll go on television and say it again. Kate: No. Just hang out with this Lacey Skye Morris. Try and find out more about her. See if she meets these other two again. If she does, call this number and the one fifty is yours. Chloe: Hey, I got expenses! Kate: So do I. Don't lose the number. Kate goes back to the van. Chloe carefully pockets her money and watches Kate get into the vehicle. Inside, the cameraman has been eating a sandwich while she talked. Kate closes the door. Cameraman: Got anything? Kate: I don't know. Either the girl is into chemicals I've never heard of, or you and I have an entire segment of 'Unsolved Mysteries' in our very near future.... View Chloey Mason 's Biography Back
