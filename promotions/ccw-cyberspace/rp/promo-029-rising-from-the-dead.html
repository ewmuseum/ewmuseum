Rising From The Dead....

24 February 2003

Rising From The Dead.... 'The Angel of Death' Enigma CCW Promo #29 Date: Feb 24 2003 Location: (The scene opens up to inside CCW Headquarters... Inside some office room we see none other than CCW's Lance Sullivan sitting behind a desk... Lance is looking over some papers when all of a sudden a knock is heard at the door) Lance: Come In.... (Lance's eyes almost pop out of his head as 'The Angel Of Death' himself steps into the office... Enigma takes off his silver sunglasses and he slowly walks over to a chair places right in front of Lance's desk... Enigma sits down and he puts his feet up on Lance's desk... Enigma looks around the office) Enigma: Nice little office you got here... Lance: Uhh.... Thanks.... Enigma: So Lance, you're little columm, Clocks And Oranges is doing quite well... Lance: Actually its called Clockwork... (interupted) Enigma: Yeah I read the columm and I must say I was impressed... Lance: Well thanks, I apreciate it.... Enigma: Yeah I especially enjoyed the little comments you made behind my back... (A look of fear comes across Lance's face as the room goes dead silent) Enigma: Yeah, if my memory serves me correctly you said I had a horse's mouth or something like that??? Lance: Listen Enigma, I didn't mean anything by what I said... (interupted) Enigma: Shut up!!! Do you take me for a fool??? ( Enigma gets out of his seat and he grabs Lance by the collar and drags him over the desk... Enigma grips his hand around Lance's throat and pulls Lance right to his face) Enigma: Understand that I am no fool... Most people that talk about me like you did don't usually live to see daylight, but none the less I'll let this slide for now... If you have something to say to me, at least have the balls to say it to my face next time... What, do you think you're better than me??? Lance: Ghhh (choking) Enigma: Do you think you're intellect exceeds mine just because you have a college degree and I don't??? You think that gives you the right to talk behind my back? See Lance, I cant stand people like you... At least if you had said what you said to my face, I would have had respect for you... Cause that would have meant you had some sort of courage... But then again if you were to talk smack to me in front of my face I probably would torn you a new asshole so maybe you did do the right thing... None the less, I'm giving you another chance... For once, I'm gonna be 'forgiving'... Consider yourself lucky, you caught me on a good day... Just a word of warning though Lance... If I gotta come looking for you again, you better run, you better hide, invest in a gun or something because I will make you understand a whole new meaning to the word pain... Have I made myself clear? Lance: Ghhh (turning purple) Enigma: I'm glad we understand eachother... (Enigma throws Lance to the ground and he leaves Lance's office and now the scene switches to what seems to be an abandoned warehouse... The warehouse seems to be under construction as various tools are seen laying around... Enigma is walking through the warehouse just looking around) Enigma: There is so many things here that I could use as a weapon... So many things that I could hurt people with... I feel like a kid in a candy store... (Enigma sees a crowbar, he picks it up... And he kinda looks at the crowbar, admiring it... Then Enigma winds up and he swings the crowbar right into the wall making a huge hole into the drywall) Enigma: Destruction... A thing of beauty.... I would trade my own my mother for a chance at doing that to Blayze... (Enigma begins ripping huge peices of drywall from the wall... Enigma finally stops and he continues walking through the warehouse searching for other items of destruction... Enigma stops walking and a smirk comes to his face... He walks over and he picks up a shovel from the ground) Enigma: There is nothing more beautiful than the sound of a metal shovel being cracked over another man's head... And on LDT, Brad Douglas might just be that man... You see Brad, on LDT you're gonna be stepping into my world... My world of Hardcore... A world of pain and suffering... I enjoy hurtin people Brad, it makes me feel something inside... I guess you can say I take pleasure in another man's suffering... Call it sick, call it demented, call it whatever you wanna call it... But thats the reason why I was the greatest Hardcore Champion CCW has ever layed its eyes upon... Because I posses that killer instict which no other man in CCW has... Enigma: See Brad, there isnt much I know about you... Actually from what I saw in you're last promo, you're pretty good at lifting weights... But lifting weights means nothing when it comes down to it... I don't care if you can bench press 1000 lbs, that means nothing to me... From what I saw in you're match last week, I wasn't really impressed... I've faced and beaten opponents of far greater calibur than you... But that dosent mean I'm gonna take you lightly... It just means my job is gonna be easiar... (Enigma takes a second, and he lights up a smoke... He then continues) Enigma: Brad, I have to give you credit though, you walked out of LDT the Hardcore Champion... Some may say you got lucky, but none the less you are the Hardcore Champion... And nobody can take that away from you... But just because you're the champion, dosent mean you're hardcore... In my eyes, your still just some lucky rookie punk... And on LDT, we're gonna see if you've really got what it takes to make it in the Dub... Brad, I want you to look deep into you're soul and I want you to ask yourself something... Are you ready to look death into the eyes? Do you have what it takes to beat a hardcore legend??? Personally I don't think you do... So try and prove me wrong kid... (Enigma inhales one last drag from his cigarette and then he flicks it away... Enigma then looks at the shovel he still holds in his hand, and he bashes the shovel off the ground... The bottom end of the shover snaps off) Enigma: Its beens almost one year since I've stepped into the ring... Alot of people think that I've lost my touch, some even said my career was over... I proved alot of people wrong... All my life, I've been fighting against all odds, and more often than not I've come out on top... On LDT I'm gonna show the whole world that 'The Angel Of Death' has risen from the dead... And I'm starting with you Douglas... And its a shame too... You just happened to be that first un-lucky bastard thats gonna get his ass handed to him from yours truly... Tough luck kid... You can look at it from alot of perspectives... You can see it as a learning experience... Or you can look at it as a public beating... Whatever works for you... Enigma: And as for you Blayze, don't think that I've forgotten about you... Oh no Blayze, I wanted you to be the first man I faced... But instead they give me some rookie... There is nothing I want more than you're blood on my knuckles... For over a year, I have been dreaming of getting you in the ring and tearing you limb from limb... And with every moment and every second that passes my hatred for you grows... And sooner or later Blayze, we will meet... And I will destroy you... I wont just beat you, I will hurt you... You will pay for your wrong doing Blayze... What goes around comes around... And in time I will get my chance... Enigma: In the mean time, on LDT I'm gonna pretend that Brad Douglas is Blayze... And its a shame that Douglas has to suffer for Blayze... Oh well... Life isn't always fair... Especially life in the Dub... Brad, you're destiny awaits you... You can't run from it... All you can do... Is fear it... View 'The Angel of Death' Enigma 's Biography Back
