Discussing The Future Take-Over

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Regina Winters blackmails Mike Lubis into helping her take over her father’s company.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Discussing the Future Take-over Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 20:32:12 Location: New York, New York This is a: Match RP **The corporate offices of Winters Enterprises in New York. Regina is sitting in her father’s office, her feet up on the desk. Today had been a good day.... One of the better ones in a long time. Her father had been out of the office all day, dealing with matters outside the company. And so, Regina had run of the office. And soon she would be initiating a deal that would put her in a position to demand authority and respect around the office. She could regain her dignity and her once-strong power base.** **A knock comes from the door to the office and Regina smiles, knowing this to be her afternoon appointment.** Regina: Come in! **The door opens and in comes Mike Lubis. Regina smirks at him as he enters, closes the door behind him, and approaches the desk. Regina remains seated with her feet up, staring at him intently.** Mike: Okay, I’m here. Let’s get down to business. Regina: Patience, Mike. Patience. How is your little woman....? Or in other words, how is my little bitch? Mike: She’s not your “bitch”. Regina: Well.... Maybe not yet. But soon. After Lockdown, she’ll definitely be considering it. Mike: Why? What do you have planned? Regina: Hah! Like I’m going to tell you! Let’s just say that Christine and Angelica are in for a world of pain! Pain the way I like it.... Mike: **shrugs** I think she’ll beat you. I think they both will. Regina: What? What did you just say? **Regina swings her impressive legs off the desk and stands up looking Mike right in the face.** Regina: Haven’t you been watching me destroy her all month long? She hasn’t put up one bit of resistance! It’s going to be so easy! Mike: If you say so. Regina: I do. The match will start. Angelica and Damsel will suffer. The match will end. I will stand victorious, just like I did at SMN. Mike: **smiles** Right. After Lacey Skye nearly took out Devyl, Mr. Raymond and yourself. Thank God Mr. Raymond saved you. You were about to get the Puppie’s.... Regina: Shut-up! We beat the hell out of Lacey Skye and Damsel, and that’s all that matters! I could’ve taken both of them by myself, just like I can take Angelica and Damsel with or without Koriann. Mike: **grins** I think.... Regina: What you think is irrelevant! You are here now for one purpose, and one purpose only! You are going to help me reclaim this company. Mike: You told me that already. What exactly do you need me for? Regina: A couple of simple tasks, actually. I need you to stay here in New York for the next month while you assist me. We have a very grueling series of planned events ahead of us. Mike: I don’t think I can do that. I have several meetings that I have to make across the Pacific. Not only that, but Christine and I have.... Regina: Mr. Lubis, if you do not cooperate, Christine learns EVERYTHING! And although she wouldn’t believe me if I told her, I have visual and recorded evidence to use against you. Know, you will stay here and help me, or you can lose that which is really important to you. Mike: **angry** Fine, I’ll stay. Let’s just get this over with. Tell me what you’re planning, and then let me go. I want to spend as much time with Christine as I can. Regina: That’s a good boy. Welcome to the wonderful world of Industrial Espionage....
