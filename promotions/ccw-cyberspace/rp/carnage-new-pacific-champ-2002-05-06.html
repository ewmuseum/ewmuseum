Carnage - New Pacific Champ...

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which Carnage celebrates winning the Pacific Title and challenges Danny Bryson for the Trans Atlantic Title.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Carnage Entitled: New Pacific Champ... Written in Monday, May 6, 2002, 22:40:50 Location: Unknown This is a: Interview **The CCW logo flashes across the screen. The video feed stays black, but an audio of Carnage speaking plays...** 'All across the coast, they watched in horror... Now they live in fear... A new CCW Pacific Champion was crowned at Lock-Down...' **A light flashes on and off like lightning. We see Carnage, holding his newly won CCW Pacific Title. The light flashes off and now everything is black...** 'A new champion to go along with a new era in CCW... Red Headed Step Child was just a stone in the road. And now Carnage reigns...' **The light flashes back to Carnage with the Pacific Title. The light flashes off...** 'Now it is time to reclaim what was once lost... Honor. Dignity. Respect. CCW Trans-Atlantic Title...' **The light flashes on and this time Carnage has a real pissed off look on his face. The light flashes off...** 'It all changed during the Hardcore Hell match at CCW Dead or Alive. And it was awakened two weeks ago. I'm coming back for what is mine Danny Bryson. Not just the TA Title... But a part of my soul...' **The light quickly flickers out and the scene fades to black...** View Biography
