Talks on Fat Ass, M2S

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview in a limousine in which The Supreme Machine, questioned by Gary Knowledge, talks about Phil Burner, Blayze and M2S, and Neurosis before ending the interview.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Talks on Fat Ass, M2S Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 20:00:22 Location: In a limousine with Gary Knowledge This is a: Interview The scene opens as a shiny black limousine pulls up outside a plush looking hotel. As the chauffeur gets out and moves to open one of the back doors, a large man with long, tied back hair and wearing a thick, black overcoat comes striding out of the hotel. He doesn't acknowledge the hotel doorman or the chauffeur as he breezes past them and gets into the limo. Just as the chauffeur goes to close the back door, a man dressed in a blue sports jacket and holding a microphone rushes towards the car. Excuse me. I'm Gary Knowledge from CCW. Just wondering if we could ask you a few questions? The chauffeur puts his arm out to stop the man getting any closer to the car as he shuts the back door. Step away from the car please sir. Come on, give me a break. The limousine's back window slowly opens and a voice comes from the darkness inside. I'm in a hurry. I've got a plane to catch. Please it won't take long. There is a long pause as Gary Knowledge waits next to the car. OK. Get in. You can talk to me on the way to the airport if you must. The chauffeur comes back around to the side of the limo and opens the door. Gary Knowledge and his CCW camera crew clamber into the back. Don't scratch anything you fools or I'll kick you straight out. Got it? The chauffeur closes the door after them and goes back around to the driver's side and gets in. The scene cuts to the interior of the limo. OK. We on? This is Gary Knowledge on location in New York City. I'm sitting here today with 5 time CCW Heavyweight Champion, The Supreme Machine. Thank you for your time. The Supreme Machine sits on the back seat looking bored. His face is bruised and he has stitches over his right eye. Yes. Just don't waste it. OK. Could I start off with asking you about the return of Fat Ass Phil Burner and the challenge he's made to you for Memorial Day Showdown. The Supreme Machine turns and gazes out at the passing traffic as his limousine speeds through the city streets. Phillip should have stayed retired. That may be true, but he hasn't, has he? Well I doubt that he'll even make it to Memorial Day Showdown. Did you see him in the ring at LDT? I'd say that if Phillip knows what's good for him he'll pack his bags and go back to wherever the hell it is he's been hiding. How can he think he's going to beat me with a heart like he's got? I always used to tell him he should lose some of that weight and he used to laugh. Well, he's not laughing so hard now is he? He seems to have his sights set on facing you to sort out, once and for all, which one of you is the best. The Supreme Machine sighs as he continues to gaze out of the car window. Phillip should just face facts. Even if he thinks he could have beaten me two years ago, he's not the same man he was then. I don't want to be known as the man who killed Phillip in the ring but if he insists on this foolish match then I'll be left with little choice. If he wants it that badly, then I say give it to him but he should think long and hard before he goes ahead with it. Now I know he's got an Achilles heel, well let's just say that I'll stop at nothing to win and if that includes sending Phillip into cardiac arrest...well, he got in the ring. I won't be held responsible for what happens to him once he's in there. Next question. OK. Seeing as you mentioned LDT, what are your thoughts on what Blayze and M2S did to, not just you, but everyone that was involved in the main event? You said earlier on the week that his business was none of your concern. Is that still the case? The Supreme Machine turns and stares angrily at Gary. His business became my business the second he and his little group of pathetic thugs took a swing at me. Those bastards made me bleed. I will return the favor. That's a promise. They don't seem to be afraid of you. I know you heard him mouthing off on CCW radio the other night. CCW radio.....yes. I was supposed to be doing an interview with them myself but what happens? The equipment mysteriously "malfunctions". Are you saying that you think M2S are responsible for that? Of course they are. What other explanation is there? Blayze gets his little gang of losers to sabotage the equipment so he has free reign to say what he wants. As for what he was saying the other night, well I've never heard so much shit come out of one man's mouth ever and I've heard Tim Inman ramble on before so that's saying something. Look, Blayze is nothing. I've seen his type come and go thousands of times before.. They talk big about what they're going to do, they surround themselves with stupid goons like Fanatik, they create a few ripples but then they piss off the wrong person and the vanish as quickly as they appeared. M2S have now pissed off the wrong person. ME! Well what are you going to do about them? Do you seriously think I'm about to tell you? You can just wait and see like everyone else. All you need to know is that on his own, Blayze is nothing. Take away Fanatik and Darren Jordan and what has he got? Nothing. His type can't fight their own battles because they just don't have what it takes. That's why he's surrounded himself with these fools. But he seems keen to face you. Talk is cheap. When is the match booked? It isn't. He won't face me in singles OR tag-team competition. He can run his mouth off as much as he wants, it doesn't matter. He can send his freaks out to beat me down, it doesn't matter. In the end it will be me standing over the wreckage of M2S. That's all that matters. Like I said before, he's pissed off the wrong person. I didn't win the CCW Heavyweight Championship five times for nothing you know. I had to beat people a damned sight better than him. Hell, I've been beating people better than him since I was a child. Blayze isn't anything special. Never was and never will be. Time will prove that. He thinks he's such a bad ass? Well, I've forgotten more than that fool will ever know about hurting people. He's just like all the others. Alright then, we'll get off Blayze and move onto your match against Neurosis for the CCW Heavyweight Title at PTC's Night of Champions. The Supreme Machine laughs as he turns back and resumes gazing out the limo's heavily tinted windows. What's so funny? You of all people should know the answer to that one. He's not going to show. Haven't you heard? Neurosis is running scared. I told him when I came back to CCW that I would be coming for my title. I warned him but he didn't listen until it was too late. He's not worth wasting any more of my breath on. But you're booked to face him. I said I didn't want to talk about him anymore! But....but.... The Supreme Machine reaches down and pushes the intercom. Driver... pull over. My guests are getting out right now. The limousine comes to a screeching halt in the middle of the peak hour traffic. This interview is over. Get the hell out of my car. The chauffeur opens the back door as a chorus of horns blasts from the traffic lining up behind the parked limo. But..but... GET THE HELL OUT! The Supreme Machine pushes the complaining CCW camera crew out of the limo and slams the door behind them, leaving them standing in the middle of the crowded street. The chauffeur gets back behind the wheel and the limo screeches off into the distance. Well, that was... ummm. interesting. This is Gary Knowledge in New York. Until next time goodbye. View Biography
