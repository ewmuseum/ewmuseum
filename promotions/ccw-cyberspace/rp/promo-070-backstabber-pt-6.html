Backstabber (pt. 6)

10 March 2003

Backstabber (pt. 6) Scorpion CCW Promo #70 Date: 3-10-03 Location: Las Vegas, Nevada :::: A smack across the face wakes Scorpion up from unconsciousness. Scorpion looks at a wooden plank that is coming off a boat and heading straight to the ocean. :::: Corky: How was the beauty rest boys? Mike: Where the hell are we? Corky: That doesn’t matter really does it? All that matters is that you’re going to call the bottom of the ocean home in a second. Scorpion: You’ve got to be kidding me. Corky: Does this look like a joke? Does this look like a clown is going to pop out in front of you singing happy birthday? Do you think before we drop you in the ocean I’m going to say “April Fools” and then drink a couple of cocktails talking about how funny the joke was? Mike: What the hell are you talking about? Corky: I’m not kidding you idiots this isn’t a joke. I’m going to actually drop you two off of this wooden plank while you are tied by rope and send you sinking all the way to the bottom. Mike: You won’t be able to get our bodies all the way down to the bottom. Corky: Pour soul, a poor soul indeed. I knew that! C’mon I’m not dumb like you two. That’s why I’m going to tie a cinder block to your feet so you sink faster and will stay at the bottom longer so eventually some shark will eat you. Scorpion: Your not going to get away with this. You will pay for what you have done to us in the long run. Corky (laughing): I’m going to pay? How can anyone stop me? No one and I mean no one will lay a finger on me let allow someone getting revenge for you two. If you two were smart you would have stayed out of New York when Drake died, getting the picture that you two would be next. But then again you are dumb as rocks. Scorpion: Corky I have disliked you ever since you joined this gang. I knew we should have never given you a chance because we wanted to help make you strong. You were always a snake in the grass wanting power more then anything. Corky: Exactly Scorpion! Didn’t anyone tell you two go by your instincts? Cause if you did I probably wouldn’t be standing here right now or maybe even alive. But you didn’t and now Drake, Mike and you will pay for your mistakes as I get rid of the original three members of this gang and make it my own! Mike: You cold-hearted son of a bitch. Corky: Michael why so kind? You guys need to realize that this is a human struggle, which always the strong survived. Just like Charles Darwin said when he stated that there is always a “Survival of the fittest.” I’m a survivor cause I kill off my enemies and now the strong will survive after I dispose the both of you! Scorpion: You know something Corky I got to hand it to you. Corky: What? Mike: Huh? Scorpion: Yeah I got to hand it to you, you backstabbed drake, and you got Donald Trump to trick us and now your going to finally kill us. But what are you going to do after this is all said and done? Your going to have the gang but really no one is there to help you control it and once the other gangs find out you killed us you will defiantly be hearing from them, always having to look over your shoulder. Corky: See that’s where you wrong Scorpion, I will never ever have to look over my shoulder cause I have a seven foot, three hundred pound monster to watch my back, my personal security guard Damien! Scorpion: Personal body guard? Corky: Damien will beat this whole gang up if they try and turn their back on me. He listens to what I say and only what I say. Damien: (Interrupting) Actually… Corky: Excuse me! Damien: Actually I don’t listen to you anymore. Corky: What the hell do you mean? Don’t you remember our deal! Damien: Yeah that I would get half of the gang and you would get the other half of the gang right? Corky: Exactly. Damien: I heard you talking to one of the gang members telling them that you were going to control the gang one hundred percent and that that you were going to get rid of me just like everyone else. Corky: Damien…come on I was only joking. Damien: Well I’m not, that’s why I made a little phone call. Corky: A phone call to whom? Damien: (pointing to the sky) The police department of Nevada. :::: A helicopter is flying above the boat and is getting lower to the boat. :::: Corky: Holy shit! I got to get out of here! Damien: Oh no you don’t… :::: Damien grabs Corky and begins to tie him up to a pole on the boat. :::: Corky: Damien, please let me out! We will split the gang fifty/fifty, just you and me. Damien: What makes you think I can trust you again you little bitch! Corky: Please I’ll do anything, just let me out. Damien: Fuck you Corky, your time is over. :::: Damien walks over un-tying Scorpion and Mike. :::: Mike: Finally now I get my shots in at Corky. Scorpion: Same here. :::: Scorpion and Mike begin punching Corky. Corky’s mouth begins pouring with blood and he begins crying as a police officer from the helicopter begins lowering himself on the boat. :::: Corky (crying): Please I never meant to do anything to anyone. Scorpion: That’s a load of shit, cause if you didn’t mean to do anything Drake would still be alive. Corky (crying): I’m sorry, I’m sorry. Mike: Too late your time is up asshole. :::: The police officer lands on the boat. :::: Police Officer: Thanks Damien for getting this guy we have been looking for him for a while. Damien: No problem, I love taking out the trash. Police Officer: Mr. Corky Stewart you have the right to remain silent anything can and will be used against you in the court of law, got that? Corky: Yes sir. Police Officer: All right un-tie him so I can put the handcuffs on. :::: Damien un-ties Corky and the police officer puts the handcuffs on Corky. :::: Police Officer: Thanks again guys. :::: The police officer gets pulled back up to the helicopter along with Corky. :::: Scorpion: Another job done, we finally got our revenge. Mike: Boy was that revenge sweet. Scorpion: Dam right it was. Mike: Hey Scorpion who is going to be the new leader of the gang now? Scorpion: Hold on a sec. :::: Scorpion gets the attention of the entire gang. :::: Scorpion: You guys are now without a leader and your probably wondering who should it be. Well being one of the original members I’m going to make that decision. There will be two leaders, one being Damien, the other being Mike. Mike: What? Holy shit man thanks a lot! Scorpion: No problem you deserve it. Damien: (shaking Scorpion’s hand) Thanks man. Scorpion: You saved my ass man you deserved it. :::: Later that night Scorpion and Mike are sitting in front of the boat drinking a beer. :::: Scorpion: Well it’s finally over we got our revenge. Mike: Dam it took a while though. Scorpion: It was well worth it though. Mike: You dam right. :::: Scorpion and Mike cling beers as the scene closes. :::: ~END~ View Scorpion 's Biography Back
