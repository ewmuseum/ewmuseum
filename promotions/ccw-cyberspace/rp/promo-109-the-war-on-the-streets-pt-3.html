The War on the Streets (pt. 3)

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The War on the Streets (pt. 3) Scorpion CCW Promo #109 Date: 3-24-03 Location: New York City, New York :::: A bright light is beaming through Scorpion’s eye lids. Scorpion’s head feels like it has been pound over the head by a sledgehammer. Scorpion slowly opens eyes and sees a Doctor standing at the edge of his head writing onto a pad of paper from a notebook. :::: Doctor: Oh my god! Your awake let me get your family they will be happy to see that you are out of your unconsciousness. Scorpion: Huh? Where the hell am I? :::: The doctor runs out of the room for a split second and the comes back into the room with Scorpion’s wife, Jennifer and One year old son Toby. :::: Jennifer: What the hell happened to you? How did the police find you at the dry docks knocked unconscious? Scorpion: The last thing I remember was these two huge gang members came over and I punched one in the face while the other one hit me in the head. It was the hardest punch I have ever felt. Doctor: That punch has knocked you out for over forty-eight hours. Scorpion: I have been unconscious for forty-eight hours? Holy shit that dude really knocked me on my ass. Doctor could my wife and I be left alone? Doctor: Surly. :::: The doctor leaves the hospital room shutting the door behind him. :::: Jennifer: Why were you at the dry docks? Scorpion: I was at the dry docks cause I needed to find out where Mike was. Jennifer: Why did you need to know that? Scorpion: Cause some powerful guy is trying to control all the gangs on the streets and Mike won’t give his gang up. Jennifer: Oh no, no more gang shit for you. You know that! You told me you were done with that stuff, but you always find yourself going back into that bullshit. Scorpion: I know I’m sorry Jen but I got save my friend. Jennifer: Do you not realize that you have a son now? You must be here for him I don’t want him to visit his father’s grave at a young age and you know that. Scorpion: This is it Jennifer I have to save my friend, I have to stop this guy and put him in jail just like Corky. Jennifer: Don’t take this shit into your own hands, how bout I give you a phone right now and you call the police so they can take care of it. Scorpion: Give me a break Jennifer; you know I’m not a pussy. When people take advantage my family or my friends they are going to hear from me. This guy isn’t going to get away with it. Jennifer: Your not in the physical shape you were when you were twenty one Jeff. Look at you, your lying in a hospital bed with a bandage wrapped around your head and you were unconscious for two days cause some huge gang members knocked you out. Scorpion: I know I’m not the same man that I was when I was fighting for survival on the streets Jen. I know that. But those guys drew first blood and I’m not going to sit around letting these assholes take advantage of my friends and I. Jennifer: So what? Just walk away from it Mike is a strong kid he can get out of it. Scorpion: I can’t. Jennifer: Why can’t you get out of it? Scorpion: (looking down) Because they said if I don’t join them then they are going to go after my family. Basically telling me they are going to go after you and Toby. Jennifer: (Upset) What? They are going to go after Toby and I if you don’t join? Scorpion: Yes but don’t worry I’m going to go after them before they go after you two, I promise. Jennifer: You better. What did we do to deserve this? Scorpion: They know protecting my family is a weakness on my part that will make me do anything so they threatened to do it because they want me to join them. Jennifer: How would this guy know? Scorpion: Corky is his cousin. Jennifer: That backstabber is this guy’s cousin? Scorpion: Yeah, I think that’s why he is going after me. Cause I know Corky is bitter that he is in jail because of Mike and I. That’s why they are holding Mike hostage and threatening me to do something I would never do nor will I do it. Jennifer: My god these guys are psycho’s. Scorpion: I know they are. (Grabs Jennifer’s hand) But they are not going to hurt you or Toby. I’m going to make sure of that. Jennifer: (crying) This is it. This is the last straw no more interference with streets anymore after this. We are going to move to a suburb and live peacefully. Scorpion: This is Jennifer no more bullshit let me this guy out and then everything is going to be ok. Jennifer: You promise? Scorpion: I promise that after this I will never go back to the street life again. Jennifer: All right go kick this guy’s ass. Scorpion: You damn right I will but first I got to make some phone calls cause I’m not going in this alone. Jennifer: Just be safe. Scorpion: I will. Hey when am I leaving this joint? Jennifer: Tomorrow. Scorpion: All right how’s the food here? Jennifer: Your in a hospital what do you think? Scorpion: Then I’m getting a five star meal tonight? Jennifer: (Laughs) Yeah right. Scorpion: All right I got to make some phone calls to some old friends. Jennifer: Ok, we will pick you up tomorrow morning. Scorpion: All right see you then. Jennifer: I love you. Scorpion: I love you too. :::: Jennifer with Toby in her arms walk out of the hospital room while Scorpion picks up the phone to begin the round of phone calls to some old friends of his… :::: TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
