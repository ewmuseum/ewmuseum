The Angry One

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Kensuke Suzahara rants by phone and to a camera crew about his upcoming match against The Valiant One.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Kensuke Suzahara Entitled: The Angry One Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 21:19:31 Location: Seattle, Washington This is a: Match RP [The scene opens up on a new apartment that seems to be in a state of pre-furnishing. Kensuke is jabbering at someone over a Nokia cellphone.] Kensuke: Yeah, with Layne Staley dead, it's like a freaking day of mourning here! It's ridiculous. He DESERVED to be killed for making such terrible music. [He turns and takes a brief glance at the camera, and scowls.] Kensuke: Oh great, the workers are here. I have to say something for my match with Joseph. I'll see you on Thursday. [He clicks the phone off, and turns ot address the camera. Since the last time he's been on CCW television, he's gained a few pounds, and an evil smirk...but it's the same Kensuke with his black hair and psuedo-emo t-shirts.] Kensuke: I was wondering when you'd get here! Look, a match against The Valiant One, right? He's a traitor, you know that! [The camera swings in a 'no' fashion, and Kensuke gets an angry look on his face.] Kensuke: Look! I've been friends with Joe for how long? 14 months? And once I do ONE FREAKING THING for me, as opposed to doing everything for the greater good, he says that he hates me, just like everyone else? Some friend THAT is! [The camera starts moving down towards the cameraman's feet, but it's jarred by a sharp blow.] Kensuke: LOOK AT ME WHEN I'M TALKING TO YOU! [The camera comes back up on Kensuke.] Kensuke: Joe, I'll see you on Lockdown...and it won't be pretty. We've basically got two options...be Valiant...or be a winner. Personally, I think I'd rather be a winner. [The cameraman nods a little, and Kensuke checks his pocket for the cellphone.] Kensuke: Get out of here! I'm done with this crap! I don't have time for this! [He plugs a few numbers into the phone and begins to talk, as the scene fades to black and the silent cameraman leaves.] View Biography
