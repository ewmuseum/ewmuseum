Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Dark psychosis Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 20:59:40 Location: New York This is a: Match RP **It's about 2:00 in the morning in New York City. And at the corporate offices of Winters Inc, not everything is as quiet as it should be. Two people are there late.... Without authorization and permission. Regina Winters and Mike Lubis are in the main computer room that houses the server for the entire building. Mike is seated and typing away while Regina looks on, a smug grin on her face.** Regina: I never knew you were this adept at computers. Mike: **shrugs** It's a natural gift. I was a programmer-server administrator before I found myself in the world of professional wrestling. It was dry and boring work, and I don't much care to go back to doing it anymore. Regina: Just as long as you can hack into the system and retrieve all the password codes, I don't care. Mike: This might take a little while. Why don't you go find some cockroach to torture? I'm sure it'll pass the time that much more quickly for you. Regina: Cockroaches don't last nearly long enough. They don't have the same tolerances to pain that humans do.... Mike: I saw what you did to Christine on SMN. You're a perverted bitch, you know that? Regina: **smiles** Causing other people pain turns me on! What can I say? Mike: How about, 'I need severe mental help'. Thank God there's not many people like you out there. Regina: We're not as rare as you think. Pain is a big turn-on for a lot of people. Why do you think S&M is so popular? Mike: **pause** All I know is that you are one sick fuck. **Mike types away at the computer a bit more. Regina puts her hands on his shoulders and bends down near him to whisper in his ear....** Regina: **breathy whisper** Those little grunts and moans that Damsel gives when she's writhing on the floor in front of me.... I bet you hear the same thing when you're in bed with her. I think she likes the punishment, Mike.... I think she likes it almost as much as I like giving it! On Thursday, I bet I won't be the only one ready to explode by the end of the match.... **Mike stops typing, pushes himself away from the console and faces Regina.** Mike: You know what I think? Regina: What? Mike: That you're not going to get your hands on these passwords if you don't stop distracting me. Regina: Then please.... Continue. **cruel smile** I'll just stand here and imagine how much I would like to whip that masculine body of yours into submission. **Mike rolls back to the console and starts typing again.** Mike: I don't understand you, Regina. You have this powerful company.... You make more money than most wrestlers do.... Even in your 'lowly' secretarial position.... And yet you still gravitate to the wrestling ring. Is your desire to hurt other people that powerful? Regina: **pause** Yes. That's why I'm a dominatrix AND a wrestler. I hurt others when it's legal to do so. I used to initiate fights all the time.... In the office, in the parking lot, at the gym, back when I was in school.... But the consequences were too great. This way, I can get my fix and no one can do anything about it! Mike: A couple more minutes and I'll have the files opened up.... **pause** You're the worst kind of monster, Regina. Regina: **smiles** I know. View Biography
