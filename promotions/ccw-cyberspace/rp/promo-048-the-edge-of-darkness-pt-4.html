The Edge of Darkness pt. 4

2 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Edge of Darkness pt. 4 Devyl CCW Promo #48 Date: 03/02/03 Location: Preacher's Corners [We find ourselves back at Preacher's Corners once again. Three nights ago, a 'scientific' experiment to open the gates to the netherworld succeeded beyond anyone's expectations. A creature, claiming to be the Angel Gabriel, was loosed upon the world. It brought with it insanity and death. And now, just 72 hours after the creature's release upon this world, a small cadre of experienced Demonologists have arrived at the cabin to assess the situation.] [It is evening, right around sunset, as two figures stand about thirty feet away from the cabin. The sun has just sunk below the tree-line, filling the sky with flaring red's, oranges and purples. The first figure, broad shouldered, obviously male, takes several steps forward before crouching down to the ground and running his fingers through some gray, unidentifiable soot that covers the ground. He touches it with his fingers, rubs them together to feel the texture, then brings them up to his nose to detect odor.] David: 'It is very similar to the ash found at most locations that claim to have received a religious visitation of some sort or another, but...' [He trails off, looking back down at the ground around him, and then lets his gaze trail off into the distance. The figure behind him shifts slightly, the cloak brushing back away from the body. It is a woman, as her slim and shapely figure attests to. Her dark raven hair accentuating her concerned expression as she removes the hood from her head.] Shadow: 'But...' David: 'But it's different all at the same time. Most occurrences of the holy ash are very subtle. You really have to look to find it. But here, the stuff's all over the place... Almost as if it rained from the sky for a good two or three minute period.' Shadow: 'That's a heavy concentration of spiritual energy... Are you positive?' David: 'Very. Not to mention the fact that holy ash also smells sweet... Almost like apricots. This is odorless. There's no discerning scent to it at all.' [Shadow only nods as David resumes an upright position. They both look over at the cabin with a sense of foreboding.] David: 'Have you tried Divining yet?' Shadow: *short pause* 'No.' David: 'You should. We need to know if there's anything here that could cause us harm.' Shadow: 'Divining takes a lot out of me, David. I don't think you realize how painful it is.' David: 'Well, Victoria is going to want to know, so you might as well do it. Besides, it hurts you more when she's around, right? You might as well go ahead and do it before she gets here.' Devyl: *from behind* 'It's too late for that, David.' [Shadow and David both turn to see Victoria approaching them from a short distance away. David smiles as Devyl walks right up to him, and they embrace in an extended kiss. Devyl shortly disengages from her husband and takes a step back.] Devyl: 'So what have either of you found?' David: 'Only that you were right to be concerned. This thing is unlike anything we've encountered before. It doesn't seem to be of the light or the dark. But it's definitely powerful. At this stage, from what you've told me of it's capabilities, I'd have to advise you against any direct confrontations with this entity.' Shadow: 'I agree. This place is saturated with something I've never felt before. I think you were lucky to have survived at all.' Devyl: 'It baited us. It was imprisoned inside the pentagram when it arrived. One of the foolish members of academia decided to break that prison, and allowed it purchase on this world. It's first act was to flee. It disappeared out the front door as soon as it was able.' [And silence descends upon the group. The purples and blues in the twilight sky strengthen and deepen as the reds and oranges begin to fade. Their focus once again becomes the lone cabin, standing only a short twenty feet from the edge of the forest. It's open doorway and blank windows nothing but darkness.] Devyl: 'Have you been inside the cabin?' [David and Shadow both shake their heads negatively.] Shadow: 'We were waiting for you.' Devyl: *non-threatening* 'Are you scared?' [David and Shadow both respond by shaking their heads once again.] Devyl: 'You should be.' [And Victoria starts forward, followed closely by David and Shadow. The murky air presses against them, almost as if warning them not to proceed any further. But proceed they do, right up to the front of the cabin. They mount the creaking stairs and walk right up to the front door which is currently hanging ajar on one rusty hinge. Devyl stops at the doorway and does not enter. She glances back at David before flicking her eyes back up toward the door's archway.] Devyl: 'Do you recognize those symbols?' [David takes a small step forward, eyes straining in the gloom to find what Victoria's referring to. He almost tells her that he can't see anything when he catches sight of the symbols in question. There is writing spanning across the bridge of the door... All done in archaic glyphs and runes.] David: 'What the hell?' Devyl: 'No, it's nothing from hell. Heaven neither. Not even the seraphim use this.' [David peers closer, trying to discern the shape and meaning of the symbols.] David: 'I... I don't know. I don't think I've ever seen anything like this. It must be ancient... I'm sure, given time and the appropriate materials, I could come up with a translation. But right now...' Devyl: 'Time is something we don't have. This, obviously, wasn't here before the creature arrived. What was previously written here was a simple invocation to ward off unwanted spirits. Whatever that creature was, it warped and altered the meaning of the message. We need a translation of this before the night is over. I trust you brought your laptop?' David: *nods* 'It's in the car. I'll do my best. But... Don't ask me for any miracles.' [David backs away from the door and heads out to the car to retrieve his computer.] Devyl: 'Miracles are what we need tonight, David.' [Shadow watches Devyl watch David walk out to the car. She studies her mentor momentarily before...] Shadow: 'I don't think I've ever seen you like this before. You seem uncertain about this... And that's so unlike you. You always have the answers, Devyl... To everything. What's going on here?' [Devyl breaks her gaze away from David and looks at Shadow. Her eyes, normally sharp, cool and focused, have softened immeasurably.] Devyl: 'That is what we need to find out. You are right, Shadow. I am uncertain. I have finally come across something which I do not understand... Something that even the devil himself is uncertain about. But what I do know is that this creature needs to be stopped. We must learn all we can here. We must find a weakness, and learn how to exploit it. We cannot allow it to take any souls. The people of this planet do not belong to it.' Shadow: 'I hope we can find something... Learn something... Because if we don't...' Devyl: 'If we don't... We are all doomed.' [fade out...] View Devyl 's Biography Back
