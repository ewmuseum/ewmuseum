Part 2 of the 'Cold Aprils' miniseries (PPV RP #2) Jason Dynasty CCW Promo #103 Date: 12/23/2003 Location: Varies “I am again plagued by the dreams. They have haunted my sleep at different times over the last three years, and they are always the exact same things. They start the same, they end the same and everything in between is the same. So what does the dream mean? Why can I not figure it out, why does it haunt me so much? Does it exist only to torture my soul or are there meanings behind it? I remember how the dream began, when it began and why.” May 28, 2000…USWF Pay-Per-View “Endless Sufferings”… “The Man of Steele” Ray Steele Vs “The Messenger of Light” Jason Dynasty for Steele’s World Heavyweight Title in Dynasty’s own match creation…the Cell. “I remember being helped out of the room the cell had been placed in and I can still see Steele lying in the cell bloody and unmoving. I was so out of it after that match that I could barely stand up. Back then I could walk out into the crowd and hear a noise that I no longer hear. It was the sound of the crowd cheering my name, after all I was Jason Dynasty, “the Messenger of Light”. A former Hardcore and US champion whom, after a year or more of fighting my heart out for these fans I had finally climbed to the top of the mountain. I was the USWF World Champion and the man in the federation. As I was helped to the locker rooms, with my golden belt around my shoulder, I heard nothing. I heard no cheering, no chants of “Dynasty”, and not even a collective gasp from the amounts of blood as it dripped from my wounds. I now know that I had been it so hard that it momentarily hindered my hearing, I would not have heard a gun go off in the twenty minutes following that match. But at that moment I thought I had lost the crowd, I thought that my people had turned on me, and amazingly enough it did not matter to me. I always ask myself now…if I had known what I would become, would I still not have cared?” July 30, 2000…final USWF Pay-Per-View “Final Reckoning”… “The Man of Steele” Ray Steele Vs “The Messenger of Light” Jason Dynasty for the right to be called the greatest in USWF history in a Sanctuary Match. “I killed him, I killed my enemy and left him lying in a pool of blood in a graveyard. Why did I do it? Even I cannot answer that, perhaps the stress had gotten to me, the stress of losing my World Title, the stress of the yearlong feud with Steel or not knowing what would become of me. What would happen to me once the fed that had been my first and only home for a year and a half closed its doors? I lost control, I know that now, the piece of sharpened steel was on the dirt and I saw Steele crawling to his feet. I seemed to flash back to that moment in May after the Cell Match when everything was silent. I guess I decided that if the fans refused to cheer me any longer than I am going to have them boo me, as long as they give me a reaction. So I picked up the steel, that cold steel stick and drove it through my most mortal enemy. The blood would stain my hands for months but somehow I survived, I always survived, but now I had evolved and was set to bring that evolution into the UWA and then into TW. I was the Iceman and I had cometh. UWA and Travesty Wrestling were nothing special, but they held my affections for two years. I continued on my killing spree, and soon, no matter how hard I fought I was the Iceman. I would reign as TW World Champion for four months before it was taken from me unjustly, was this the sign, the sign that the Iceman’s time had past?” September of 2002 to March of 2003…Global Wrestling Federation… “The Iceman goes 5-Star” “For a while I believed that if I toned things down a bit that I would return to the status I had once known. Sure, I won a Tag Team Title and an International Title, but neither belt held any real significance for me. Neither belt told me that I had done anything that I could not have done as The Iceman. Global Wrestling faltered and almost tumbled, it was no longer fun to be there. I had done more than many of the stars of GW yet they had the gall to show off their egos while I sat idly by and watched, disgusted with the whole turn of events. I found a loophole in my contract and pounced upon it, making my way over to CCW. Before I debuted I met up with a friend, Damien Nhyte and after talking with him and having the dreams begin I realized that my glory could only come as the Iceman. That brings me up to now.” March 22, 2003…1:30 PM Eastern Standard Time…Office of Dr. Rawlings. “You may sit up now Jason.” Dr. Rawlings replies as he closes his notebook and leans back in his chair. Dynasty sits up on the doctor’s plush couch, allowing his feet to recline back as he leans against the wall. “So am I sane or what Doctor?” Dynasty replies, obviously annoyed by the whole procedure of having to see a “psychotherapist” as part of his probation from a past crime. The doctor just stares at Dynasty for a moment before letting out a sigh and leaning forwards. “I really do not know Jason, I mean, I am so happy that you made it a practice to show up to these classes every week for the past few months but we are just now breaking the surface of your subconscious and this is your final session. We are so close to breaking through to what makes you have this need for blood and death and destruction, that I really think more sessions could be a good thing.” “No way Rawlings, this shit has taken up enough of my time over the years. I have a new job and I am aching to get back to it.” Dynasty replies as he stands for the door. “You mean CCW, I saw last week’s show Jason. I saw you kill that fan. Now I do not know what people you know but for whatever reason no law enforcement agency will touch you for any crime and you have been allowed to basically do as you want for the past 3 years. This probational therapy from when you killed Ray Steele is the only way anyone has of getting a better look into how you see things and why you do things as you do. I am afraid that I cannot give you a sane bill of health unless you agree to more sessions.” Rawlings states as he also stands up and watches as Dynasty turns around, a smug grin plastered on his face. “Dammit Rawlings, ok, I will agree to more sessions if you just grant me this sanity clause and send it in.” Dynasty says, almost pleading with the doctor. The doctor smiles and walks over to his desk. “Ok, there, consider it signed…sealed…and delivered.” On the word “signed” he signs a very official looking slip of paper, on the word “sealed” he folds the document up and places it in an envelope, sealing it shut. Then on the word “delivered” he places the envelope in a mail slot that sends the document directly to a mail truck, which was preparing to leave with the outgoing mail. “But now I am holding you to your word Jason to continue these sessions.” Rawlings says as he shuts the mail slot. “Doctor Rawlings after everything you have done for me, I will keep showing up. You have helped me through these past three years and I owe you for it. How about a hearty handshake before we part ways?” Dynasty says as he holds out his large hand. The doctor smiles and steps forward, taking Dynasty’s hand. “Of course Jason and do not worry, we will come to grips with these dreams.” “The dreams really do not affect me anymore Doctor, but I did have one more thing I wanted to talk to you about.” Dynasty says, closing the door to the office and leaving the camera staring at a wooden door. Minutes go by and the door opens to show Dynasty making his way out, in his hand is his trademark collapsible steel baton, which he is collapsing and returning to his trenchcoat even as he shuts the door to the doctor’s office. He opens his other hand to show a small vile, now empty but stained red due to whatever liquid had previously occupied it. He puts it in his pocket and adjusts his black gloves. There is a weird grin on his face as he walks out of the building. The camera fades out as the nameplate on the doctor’s door is shown. March 23, 2003…15:45 PM Eastern Standard Time…outside of Dynasty’s penthouse home. The scene fades in to show Jason Dynasty and Damien Nhyte walking towards Dynasty’s silver sedan. Nhyte has a newspaper and is reading the obituaries as he walks. “Hey Jason, that doctor of yours…Rawlings…. He died yesterday afternoon.” Nhyte states as Dynasty unlocks the car. “Really?” Dynasty replies nonchalantly. “What happened?” “They say it looked like a heart attack, even though he was in perfect shape, apparently his heart just gave out. His secretary found him late last night dead in his office.” Nhyte replies, reading what it says in the doctor’s obituary. “Lucky for you that he signed that sanity document before he died.” Nhyte says as he looks at Dynasty’s emotionless expression. “Yeah, must be luck.” Dynasty replies as he gets into the car. Nhyte smiles and shakes his head, getting into the car and shutting the door as Dynasty shuts his also. Nhyte turns to Dynasty as Dynasty puts the key in the ignition. “So where are we going?” “I was thinking that maybe we should go check out the Pay-Per-View, I have an idea.” Dynasty replies. Nhyte just nods and puts his head back as Dynasty pulls out of the parking space and the camera fades to black with the sight of Dynasty’s car fading into the distance. A moment of blackness passes and then Dynasty’s voice is heard, solemn yet enthusiastic at the same time. “Dr. Rawlings was an annoyance in my life for far too long. He believed that I was trapped in my own mind, that I did not want to be burdened by these dreams and that I was, down deep in my soul, a good person. I am The Iceman, good or bad, that is who I choose to be and as far as the dreams are concerned…they won’t be disturbing me anymore.” View Jason Dynasty 's Biography Back
