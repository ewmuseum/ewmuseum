This Segment Was Supposed To Occur After The Koriann/Carnage Match

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay of a broadcast segment in which Lacey Skye Morris, Nikki Christianson and the Women’s Champion Devyl confront each other.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: This segment was supposed to occur after the Koriann/Carnage match... Written in Friday, May 31, 2002, 11:59:46 Location: Richmond Coliseum in Richmond, VA This is a: Interview Chucky: Well, we're back, and Koriann is being treated in the back. Early reports say that she has a severe concussion, and she's lost a lot of blood. If we get any more information within the hour, we'll bring it to you. I just hope she'll be alright. Vinny: I agree. As much as I hate her antics, Koriann didn't deserve that. But on a lighter note, I talked to that priest guy and he told me that Hell wasn't frozen over. I take that as a positive sign that the apocalypse is not yet upon us. Chucky: Well, we have a couple more matches to get to in a couple minutes... 'All For You' hits the PA system as Lacey Skye Morris walks out of the back and heads immediately down to the ring, microphone in hand. The crowd pops big for her, many still believing and wanting her to be the champion. Vinny: Look, it's the abusive one! She should be kicked out of the building for what she did earlier! Chucky: Shut up, Vinny! From the looks of things, she has something that she very desperately wants to say. Lacey climbs into the ring and paces a bit, waiting for the fans to quiet down as they chant her name. They finally quiet down and Lacey stops in the middle of the ring. Lacey: Devyl... I know you just got here not more than five minute ago. Get your Goth bitch-ass out here... NOW!! Vinny: Devyl if you're listening, you don't have to come out here! Lacey is in a very volatile state right now! The crowd cheers as Lacey continues to pace back and forth. They really want Devyl out here as well. Lacey stops pacing again and looks back up at the set. Lacey: Either you come out here, or I'm going into the back and... Lacey is interrupted as 'No Matta What' by Toya hits the PA. She stops and glares as Nikki Christianson walks out of the back and down to the ring. The fans boo her all the way. She climbs into the ring and steps face-to-face with Lacey, grabbing the mic out of the strawberry blonde's hands. The tension between the two is very high. Chucky: Now here's trouble... Nikki Christianson is the woman who made damn sure Lacey lost her match at Memorial Day Showdown! Vinny: I like this Nikki girl... She's hot! Think she'll do me? Chucky: No. The crowd quiets down as Nikki raises her own microphone to her lips. Nikki: Lacey, I'm sure you want to take a swing at me right now. I'm sure you want to get it on really bad... **crowd pops** But that's not why I'm out here... Lacey continues to glare at Nikki, her entire body tense. The fans obviously want to see a catfight here... Nikki: No, I'm out here for one thing only... Nikki turns her back on Lacey and steps towards the front of the ring addressing the locker room. Nikki: Devyl, I'm out here to challenge you to a match for that title of yours. Chucky: WHAT!? She can't do that! Vinny: Well... Looks to me like she just did. Nikki: It's obvious to me, and everyone else around here, that you're the biggest threat this division has ever seen. And since Lacey here has such a hard time keeping the title around her waist... The crowd boos violently for this one. Nikki waits for the fans to quiet. Chucky: And that's only because of you, Nikki! Nikki: ...And since I'm obviously the reason you have that title in your possession, I believe you owe me a shot. Vinny: Hmmm... That makes perfect, logical sense to me. Chucky: **sighs** It would. Lacey approaches Nikki and snatches the microphone out of her hands. The crowd pops big. Lacey: Woah, woah, WOAH! I don't know what the hell you think you're doing here Nikki, and I don't care what reasons you believe you have for deserving a TITLE SHOT of all things! But I do know and care about one thing: Beating the hell out of you for costing me the CCW Women's title, and then beating the hell out Devyl to get that title back! The crowd pops big again as Nikki stares at Lacey with an incredulous look on her face. She approaches Lacey and grabs the mic, but the strawberry blonde doesn't let go. Nikki and Lacey are almost nose-to-nose as Nikki speaks... Nikki: I was going to wait a bit to beat your ass again, but I see that's not going to happen... The mic drops down to the canvas between them and the intense stare down continues, tension mounting by the second. Chucky: I think we're about to see a huge explosion here! Vinny: Woo-hoo! I love non-sanctioned chick fights! The crowd is popping and cheering for these two to go at it. But before it can happen... 'Fithos Lusec Wecos Vinosec' hits the PA system. The fans immediately boo as Devyl and David Oakes walk out onto the stage, Devyl proudly wearing the title belt once again around her waist. Lacey and Nikki turn away from each other and stare up at the women's champ, who has a mic in hand. Devyl: **grinning** I'm so very sorry to interrupt the festivities, girls... It looks like you both were about to beat each other senseless. And although I would have very much appreciated watching that, I had to come out here and deliver the good news... Vinny: Good news? What good news?! Chucky: I somehow doubt there's going to be anything good about what Devyl's going to say... Devyl: Lacey... I have just had a little talk with President Sylkk. For your earlier actions against Mr. Raymond, you have been suspended from the building for the remainder of the night! The fans boo this announcement. Lacey burns a hole through Devyl with her eyes. Devyl: And Nikki Christianson... Despite what you may believe, your interference in the title match was meaningless. The title was well within my grasp, Lacey was beaten. Your request for a shot at my title... Is DENIED!! The fans boo this announcement as well. Devyl: But I will take you on and put you in your place any day of the week, little girl... Your place being underneath the heel of my boot! Have a nice night, girls... Devyl's music plays again as she and David link arms around each other's waists and walk off stage. Lacey and Nikki glare at each other once again as the crowd shows how much they didn't appreciate Devyl's announcement. Chucky: Lacey has been ordered to leave the building and Nikki has been denied a title shot... Looks like everyone's back to square one... Vinny: Yup! And Devyl's large and in charge! You gotta love that girl... Chucky: No I don't. We'll be right back after this... View Biography
