Black Coffee Blues, Part 2 Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #104 Date: Next Thursday, I'mma time traveler. Location: Pushed hard through an alien land It’s another cold and sleepless night, and once again, he’s forced outside, into the blackness, into the night. The hunger forces him once again to mingle with the animals that would normally rate so far below him, as to escape his notice. However, it’s this desire for contact with his fellow humans that he can no longer deny. He can no longer secret himself in his solitary ivory tower, living above the world. He’s forced to become a part of it. He finds himself once again, in the only place that he’s comfortable. The city is different, the street, the address, the faces are different. But the people are the same, they’re so alike him, more so than he’d ever admit to himself. These are his people, the late night coffee crowd. On the table, a mug sits steaming, the liquid inside nearly boiling, a rich milky brown. The bitterness is gone, sugar taking away the tart, the cream dulling the blistering edge of blackness, turning it ever so slightly towards the light. “More coffee hun?” The waitress, so unlike the one in the last town, so very similar. Different face, different body, same insincere smile, same bored eyes, she’s just another carbon copy. You can find a thousand more like her in every town, but there’s something special about this one, a hint of real warmth in the smile, he finds intriguing. His eyes rise to meet hers, and he sees his own hunger mirrored in those eyes. She’s searching for much the same thing he is, companionship. A little warmth to chase away the bitter cold, just for one night, one hour, one moment. The amount of time, matters little, it’s the contact that’s truly important, for when the connection is made, time ceases to exist. “No, it’s the perfect color, can’t screw up the ratio.” His tone is light, almost flirty, and she smiles, a real smile, and real warmth touches her eyes, if only for a second. She winks before walking away, as he lights the next cigarette in an endless series of them. Now he recognizes what she wants, and it’s definitely the same thing he wants, he’ll wait until she gets off work, and they’ll go to his hotel room. It’s nothing but a foregone conclusion now, the rest of the night will be nothing but steps in the dance, the subtle give and take of the chase, and his mind comes alive with the possibilities. “She’s not Sheena, but she’ll do, I wonder what she’s doing tonight…” He mutters this to himself, before quickly cutting off his words. He’s revealed more of his feelings than he wanted to, even to himself. Part of him is willing to admit that he wants Sheena, needs her, and he’s sorry he let her go. However, Michael Stevens knows it was something he had to do. And he’d be forced to admit that he’d been more successful since releasing her. No matter how much it hurt him to do it, he had to admit that it was all worth it now, or it would be soon, for in just under a week everything he’s been working towards will come to him. Despite his initial qualms about working with Scorpion, they’d worked well together, made it all the way to the finals of the tag team tournament. They’d win, it wasn’t a question of if, their victory was certain. Jack Manson, and Adam Christ, weren’t wrestlers. They were brainless barbarians, to deal with them would be simple. One had simply to outthink them, and for an intellect like the one that Michael Stevens possesses, this would pose no problem whatsoever. Yes, he and Scorpion worked well together, and would continue to do so. Make no mistake about it, Stevens still does not like Scorpion, but he’s a useful commodity to have around. He’s got enough skill to make himself valuable in Michael’s eyes, he was a wrestler, and that made him a cut above men like Christ and Manson, so yes Stevens and Scorpion would win. Then he’d hold a piece of gold, a belt proving himself to be the very best at what he does. The gold will make it all worthwhile, the gold will fill the void, the gold will make everything all right, at least that’s what he keeps telling himself. “Christ, Manson, losers. Substituting trash cans and razor blades for headlocks and armbars. I’m not worried about them though, it’ll be too easy making them tap out. They’ve never faced a man like me, I’m goddamn Michael “Spitfire” Stevens, they’re just a couple hacks with delusions of adequacy.” As confident as he is about facing Christ and Manson, when it comes to The Gobstopper, and Ace Hawk, he’s afraid. Yes, even men like Stevens can admit when they’re frightened, and he certainly is now. These are two big stars, two men that have seen and done nearly everything there is to do in CCW. Not only that, they’re real wrestlers. Certainly their styles might be different from his own, but at heart, they’re the same, all three men are the same. They sacrifice their bodies, their hearts, their souls, everything to be the best it is at what they do. Desire to be the best, passion for the business, this is what drives Gobstopper and Hawk, just as it drives Michael Stevens. He’s facing legends, the brash young punk, taking on the old guard, the established stars. It’s not that their fame intimidates him, it’s not that their desire is what makes his stomach weak. It’s the simple fact that when he looks at either one of them, he seems himself, as he will be, everything he could become. And he knows that when they look at him, they see themselves as they once were. Arrogant and immature, ready to take on the world. They know him, perhaps better than any opponents he’s ever faced, they know him, just as they know themselves. “I can do it, I can beat them. I can win the Trans-Atlantic Title. A singles belt to go with my tag, and individual accomplishment, I need it, and thus I’ll have it.” And now he sits alone, all these thoughts rushing through his mind, but soon they’ll be pushed away, as he begins to dance in earnest with the young waitress. Tonight he’ll have his human contact, the only question is for how long? ~To Be Continued~ View Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens 's Biography Back
