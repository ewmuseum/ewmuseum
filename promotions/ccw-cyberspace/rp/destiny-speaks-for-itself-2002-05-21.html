Destiny Speaks For Itself

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short promo in which Blayze, with an hourglass, threatens Koriann ahead of their Thursday match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Blayze Entitled: Destiny Speaks For Itself Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 19:26:00 Location: Unknown Location This is a: Match RP Like Sands Through The Hourglass... So Are The Days of Our Lives... Blayze: Our lives are changed by the minute, one event can enter us on a new encounter of life itself. Mysterious yet fulfilling, we cannot expect these occurences as we must just accept them as they come. [The sand in an hourglass, it tumbles over each other and glides through the thin oval pipe inside the hourglass. A metaphor of our lives, that each day we are trounced by obstacles which takes time to overcome with precision and patience] Blayze: The days are up for certain individuals in our world. In the next few days, one person shall have there number taken by the lord who speaks to you now, she shall walk the path towards the end of days and rest in peace courtesy of a critical beatdown. Koriann's corpse is not a modelesque physique to twitch the hormons of any man but a sheer shield of muscle turning her into a beast instead of a woman. She had her spotlight, highlighted her career with a World Title but by Thursday, her 15 minutes of fame are about to fade. [The hourglass trickles through the black surface of Blayze's fingers. Glaring into the thich layers of Sand, no sight of the other side of the hourglass, Blayze lifts it up to his eyes then lets it drop. It falls..falls to its ends...CRASH! The glass shatters and the sand is exposed to the floor] Blayze: Understand this Koriann, the definition of over DOES mean the end. Your career, your well being and your life are all at stake once you step your foot into my domain. I speak only the facts, I do not have to sit here and sprew meaningless trash talk about your past flaws and accomplishments. I do my speaking in the ring and prove my theory by sacrificing your body and soul to higher ups. [Blayze walks over and steps on the sand, leaving a footpring in it.] Blayze: Just like my footprint, I will leave a mark in your mind. I shall make my presence felt when cutting off your oxygen supply. Death is not beautiful but in your case it is the only option you have. I hope you wrote out your will last night since the only destination you are headed for is..6..FEET..UNDER! Amen.. [Scene Fades] View Biography
