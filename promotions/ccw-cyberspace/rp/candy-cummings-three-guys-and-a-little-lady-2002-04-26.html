Candy Cummings - Three guys and a little lady

What this is A Match RP in which Candy Cummings prepares in a motel room for Group Therapy alongside her partners Carnage, Blayze and the Masked Mullet.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Candy Cummings Entitled: Three guys and a little lady Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 21:17:21 Location: NY City This is a: Match RP *Lights, camera, action!!!!! It's been a long time coming but here we are ladies and gentlemen. Welcome back our heroine. Your favorite porn-star and mine. Candy Cummings.* ~Candy, wearing nothing but a red, lace teddy, and reading a card as she paces back and forth infront of the fire place. Dick staggers about in the background with two young ladies (the blonde and the burnette from before) with a Colt 45 in his hand. The two drunken girls giggle insane and paw at Dick's chest~ Candy: Hmmmm.. well how interesting. I thik I can learn that move in time for the pay per view.. what do you think Dick? Dick: Come on girls... I'm nearly on my knees here? *girls giggle as Dick paws at their shirts* Candy: *rolls eyes* Yeah..anyway. Let's see. I hope this goes well. I need a good start, jumping back into this thing. I can't make a fool out of myself. Now if I can just make it to the end of this Therapy thing and beat Regina and Devyl's asses. That would be sweet. Dick: Oh yeah baby, let's take this to the bedroom. Blonde: Oh Dick you're such a tease. *Candy rolls her eyes then continues thinking* ~pop up bubble~ you won't think that when you see what he has to offer ya girls* Candy: Well, I'm sure I can take care of my end. Carnage..has an impressive name but I'm sure he can handle himself. Scorpion made short work of that dude last night. The Masked Mullet? That has got to be the oddest gimmick in this buisness. And Blaze.... Oooooh mmmmm yummy. But our first match has three mystery people. And I don't even know who the girl will be in this match. What if there isn't any girls? Oh wow!! I'm tingling from excitement! All men! And .. ME! Oh God, I'm going to explode. *Candy shivers then suddenly stops* Candy: Wait, that's just the first match. All the others, they may be tougher. But, once one is pinned aren't they out of the event? Oh my God! What if Regina or Devyl get beat right away? I won't get my chance at them. Damn! Why do I have to be so damn good? *Candy cockly walks about the room then looks out the window of her motel room and sighs* Candy: This sunday has got to be the turning point for me. The other women are going to be trying to make a statement for themselves. I gotta do the same. I've GOT to survive. *Candy walks around looking over the card and thinking. Finally after some time, she sits down on the couch and looks at a blank television screne* Candy: Well, seems it's up to me to be the true woman of CCW and uphold my boys. Carnage, Blayze and the Masked Mullet. Three men and one lady against the whole of CCW. We can do this. I can do this because. I am the best and only Candy in CCW. And everyone knows Candy melts in your mouth, not in your hands. Ha! This is going to be a breeze. And I am going to be the best woman in CCW. Get ready Group Therapy, I'll blow your mind. **camera fades** View Biography
