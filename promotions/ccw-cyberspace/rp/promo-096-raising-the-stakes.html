Raising the stakes

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Raising the stakes Andrew 'Ace' Hawk CCW Promo #96 Date: March 18 2003 Location: Las Vegas, Nevada **The scene today opens on the Sunset Strip, in Las Vegas, Nevada. The night is clear and the sidewalks are filled with all sorts of people: Couples running to get married, couples coming BACK from their marriages, people dressed as Elvis on every corner on every street, japanese tourists flashing their cameras at a rate of 30 pictures per second, and your usual high rollers, pimps, tramps, bouncers and your occasional CSI filming crew, doing an outside shoot. After walking a few yards, we see the majestic entrance of the MGM Grand, one of the biggest casinos in Vegas. Walking through the entrance, we see a middle-aged man, wearing a cowboy attire. Looking very rich and cocky, he messes with the cocktail waitresses parading around the lobby, going past the slot machines and the cheap tables, walking straight to the middle of the casino, where the big, high rollers are gathered. As he walks towards a door in the middle of the casino, a man steps in front of him. The man is well-dressed, around 6'4' tall and massive. He has a bouncer kind of look on his mexican-american face and looks straight up and down, talking to the cowboy:** 'I'm sorry, but this is a restricted game area...', the bouncer says. 'I know, I have a special credit here. The owner knows me.', the cowboy says. 'I don't care if the Pope knows you...', the bouncer says, before putting his arm forward, around the man's chest. 'Unless you have the clearance card for this place, you ain't allowed in it.' **The cowboy smiles and puts his hand inside his crocodile-leather jacket. The bouncer immediately puts his hand on his gun, fearing the worse, but the man pulls a blue Smart Card, showing it to the bouncer. The bouncer looks him funny before picking the card and walking towards the door. After pressing it against a visual scanner placed on the wall, a green light goes off above the scanner, what prompts the bouncer to quickly apologize and give the cowboy his card back. The bouncer opens the door and the cowboy goes in. We immediately see a poker table. 9 people are sitting on it, and the cowboy sits on the only available spot, waiting for his turn. The place looks heavy, with smoke filling the air from the anxious players. After the round ends, the cowboy gives his card to the dealer, who debits it for the money necessary to play: 100 thousand dollars. The man grabs his chips and places the first chip. He draws two aces. He places a 1000 dollars bet, and the table goes around, stopping at a mysterious man across the room. In front of him, more than 300 chips, proving his game capability. He hides behind a huge pair of sunglasses and a raincoat, covering the sides of his face. The man silently covers the bet and raises it by 1000 dollars. An old lady, who's on the verge of going bankrupt, raises the bet, throwing her last 2 chips on the table, raising the bet to 3000 dollars. The cowboy smiles and says:** 'I'm sorry, granny, but I can't allow you to go on...' **The cowboy covers and raises the bet again, setting the bar at 4000 dollars. The old lady, looking down, gets up, throws her cards on the table with a furious swing and storms out of the room. The people on the table look at the merciless cowboy sideways, and the cowboy says:** 'Hey, I'm sorry, but if she can't bank her bet, she shouldn't be in this table...' 'I agree...', says the mysterious man across the table. 'After all, life is a bet, right?' 'Right...', the cowboy answers. 'I mean, everybody is here because they gambled once in their life. Sometimes you win, sometimes you lose, and sometimes the stakes are simply too high for you.' 'Indeed...', the mysterious man says. 'In fact, I'm prepared to make this game interesting. How about you?' **The table looks at the man, not understanding his words. The cowboy, smiling, says:** 'Well, I'm prepared to make this game very interesting.', the cowboy says. **The mysterious man, releasing an evil grin that scares the hell out of the people in the room, says:** 'Very well. People, here's a compensation for your placed bets', the man says, giving chips to the people in the room, twice as many as the ones they bet. 'Now leave.', he says, prompting the 7 other players to run away from the room. **The dealer, completely scared, nervously awaits the man's bet. He says:** 'So, how much are you prepared to put down to back up your pride?', the cowboy asks 'Since you're the life gambler, let's make it this way...', the mysterious man says. 'How much are you worth?' 'I own 14 million dollars in credits, here.', the cowboy says. 'Can you back it up?' **The mysterious man smiles, laughing between his teeth, before throwing his card towards the dealer. He says:** 'Debit the cash, and let's rock!', the man says. **The dealer accepts it and draws the first three cards: 10, jack and king of diamonds. The cowboy nervously looks at the table and his hand, before saying:** 'AHA, nice chance for a good hand here. Unfortunately, the odds would be against you, right?', the cowboy says, before laughing. **The mysterious man doesn't change his stance, mentioning for the dealer to draw the fourth card: An ace of spades. The cowboy smiles, saying:** 'Hmmm, apparently I'm gonna double my fortune tonight.' **Once again, the man doesn't change his face. The cowboy, nervous, doesn't understand why. Finally, he waves to the dealer, who draws the fifth and last card: An ace of diamonds. The cowboy has 4 aces on his hand!! He says:** 'Well, this is where life gets its turns. Sorry pal, I have four aces', the cowboy says, putting his cards down and following up. 'Just credit his cash on my hand, dealer, and I'll be fine. It was a pleasure to play with you, man, but as I said, life gets its turns, so it's time to go on with mine.' **The man, releasing another evil grin, finally says:** 'Indeed, life gets its turns, but unfortunately, yours has just turned 180 degrees!!!' **As he says that, he lowers his hand: A 2 of clubs... and a QUEEN OF DIAMONDS!!! ROYAL STRAIGHT FLUSH!! The cowboy, completely shocked, gets up and yells:** 'HELL no, this can't be, this is a LIE! CHECK HIM OUT, I'M SURE HE'S CHEATING!!!!' **The man, looking sideways, grabs both his and the cowboy's cards, thanking the dealer. As he walks away, the cowboy pulls an automatic pistol, 7mm, out of his pocket. The man stops and the cowboy says:** 'Ok, now. You'll slowly pass me the cards, or else, I'll...' **Without even finishing, the man is thrown at the wall behind him. With a lightning kick, the man throws the cowboy across the room, the gun falling off his hands. The dealer grabs it, as security men invade the room and arrest the cowboy. The dealer, shaken, turns to thank the mysterious man, but he's vanishes, with both cards. The scene immediately changes to the outside, in front of a huge, beautiful font. Sitting in a chair is Debbie Carmichael, wearing a nice tight pair of denim jeans and a black CCW T-shirt. The mysterious man sits right besides her, scaring the hell out of her. The man takes off his glasses and we finally see he's Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. Debbie says:** 'Damn it, Ace, I hate when you do that.' 'Oh, well, ces't la vie.', Ace says. 'Ok, anyway, I hope you're not getting in trouble again...', Debbie says. 'Why would I be?', Ace says, grinning. 'Well, because I know how you love to pick a fight down here in Vegas. I can't count the number of times you were thrown out of a casino here.' **Ace smiles, before saying.** 'Don't worry, I didn't cause any trouble. Just stole someone of his entire life'. **Debbie doesn't understand, looking shocked at Ace. He quickly says:** 'NO, no, I didn't kill anyone. Relax.' 'Thank god...', Debbie says. 'I was getting worried your new attitude was transforming you. I mean, after seeing you frying that poor man on Lockdown, I can expect anything from you.' 'As I said, Debbie, you don't have to stand by me.', Ace says. 'But I will, no matter what', she says. 'All right. Then help me figure out how to conquer the only title I don't have...' 'The Tag titles?', Debbie immediately asks. 'Exactly. You know, Debbie, there's one reason, and one reason only that pushed me into going on with this. My career in CCW is incomplete. I have acocmplished everything a wrestler can accomplish in CCW: I won the CCW World Title twice, the CCW TA Title 4 times, the Indy title once, I've been considered the top rookie and wrestler in this place in the same year... I've done it all, but for 2 years, TWO YEARS, I've been trying to get the CCW Grand Slam Champion tag put alongside my name. For two years, I've tried to put my hands on the Tag Team title belts. I've been the #1 contender countless times, had several title matches, but somehow the titles have managed to avoid me. Since then, several men went ahead of me and stole my goals, my dreams, diminishing the importance of the title. But in order for my career to be complete, and in order for me to once again rise high and aim at the top prize, I have to put my hands on those title belts, at ALL costs.' 'I know, Ace, and you will... You will...', says Debbie. 'Yeah, you're right, Debbie. I will, and I'll not test my luck again. This time, I'll make SURE those titles get around my and Gobber's waist. After that, I'll take care of him. But for now, it's finally time for the Ace of wrestling to become a CCW Grand Slam Champion!!!' **As Ace says that, the scene fades to black** View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
