A victory which leads only to hardships

25 February 2003

A victory which leads only to hardships Jason Dread CCW Promo #47 Date: 2/25/03 Location: In a non-important diner As I sat in that crowded place, I let all of the events of the past day or so play back in my mind over and over. It's amazing how much one's mind can seemingly hold at one time without your head just ripping apart from all the memories, feelings, and emotions. The scene fades into a bustling diner in the middle of an early evening rush of business. In an almost stereotypical fashion we see all of what we'd expect in this place: Middle-aged women with overly large hair writing down orders on a pad of paper while openly chewing a piece of gum which has lasted for over six hours now, an overcast of cigarette smoke hanging in the air as it is supplied by pot-bellied men in red flannel hats, and the lone, ridiculous looking half-Italian cook in the back making everyone's food while sweating over the hot stove in front of him. We hear loud talking, jovial laughter, and the occasional 'tink' sound which glasses make as they drop from a mouth back down to the table. Despite everything which we imagined to be here, there is one figure not partaking in this sub-pandemonium. Sitting in a small booth nestled against a faded yellow back wall is Jason Dread, sitting calmly. The look on his face expresses that he is not of the conscious perception of the things happening around him. Merely he just stares out the window to his right side, a few last bit of orange rays from the sun fighting to not fade away. Now that I think back I don't remember how long I was in that cramped place altogether. I guess it could be anywhere from an hour, to damn near the whole night. When time doesn't make much difference on the impact of the events immediately around you, I guess you don't take notice like you normally would. I had as long as I wanted to reflect and plan on what was happening...although I didn't expect it at that particular time. I guess it's just a strange thing, this roller coaster ride which I've been on for a little less than the past year...except Six Flags anywhere can't compare as you take a trip through an emotional journey which I reflected upon as I did then. There were so many extreme ups and downs which I had undergone which, in the long run, wasn't a whole lot of time at all. It is said the mind bends and contorts to cope with the pressures of what life brings. Mine was bent to such a degree I'm surprised it didn't snap in two. Well...I take back that last statement. It did once before, and I'm still picking up the pieces... 'Another refresher on that cup?' I didn't even see the woman come up to me. From the tone her voice held, I could tell she was getting a little frustrated that every so often she had an obligation to check on the man who never touched the coffee in front of him, yet she still had to refill it as it got cold. I should have apologized sooner, but I felt that wouldn't have done any good. The last thing she probably wanted was some stranger trying to give her his reasons why he was too occupied to drink 'Iowa's finest brew'...and I didn't even think they grew coffee beans there. It took me several seconds before I even realized what she asked me. 'No thank you.' Was the only thing which I said to her. I doubt there was any response I could have come up with that would have satisfied her. With a very audible sigh, she walked off, the heels of the shoes which she wore connecting loudly with the floor. I should have left then, and let myself try and go over things lying in a comfortable bed...but I've had that fear...ever since I was alone with my feelings not too long ago...not when I almost... I shuddered at the remembrance and once again tried to fathom the possibilities which lied before me. In three short seconds I had become the number one contender to the CCW World Title. No more than nine months ago I had lost a title of the same importance, just with a different name. Now I had the chance to get something back which I should not have lost the first time...but in a different form. I'd always wanted what I had before back with me. Not for the fame and the money...but for the position of strength and priority which it brought. The last time I had it, I couldn't enjoy everything because of the selfish haze I had blocking my eyes and mind, inhibiting my actions and decisions. As I thought about it more and more, the more I realized that I had completely changed. I had turn everything around...and now I wanted what I had in the past...except this time I would do everything the right way. As I craved for that twenty pounds of material possession and my name in a couple books, I knew the chance for this opportunity came at a bittersweet cost. Once again I had the odds stacked against me by Brad Lamen in the form of my match with Michael. I knew whatever happened; the result would not be good in the long run. I am very glad and fortunate, however, that I did not deal the final blow to Michael before getting the victory over him. I never wanted to fight against him in the first place, but at the same time I wasn't going to be an idiot and not fight at all. I realized what he was capable of...and everything I did was in defense of my own well-being. I know he can't be happy at all with what happened...but none of this is my fault in the first place, as I'm sure he believes. God...I just wish I could make him see what was wrong. In the back of my mind, in a place far away from which I normally try to reach, I knew he wouldn't come to find out what was wrong on his own merit until it was too late. He doesn't understand it, but I'm just trying to help out...so nothing happens in the future with him as it did me. It was then I realized that I think about that time in the past entirely too much...and instead of feeling sorry for myself and what may happen, I need to go out and make changes happen. I need to convince Michael about what's wrong...but I doubt that he will listen. Still, I've GOT to try...I've just got to... I just don't know when the time should be...everything has to be right...all the pieces in place. It was then I realized that I didn't need to be sitting in that place any longer, letting time pass by as though it meant nothing. Even if it does not concern you...time DOES mean something to someone. A few seconds could make all of the difference in anything. I have to help as soon as I can...but only when the time is right. Who thought caring could be so difficult? Like a surgery, the process of which I was to do would be so delicate that one wrong move could result in a failure...and I couldn't accept failure at this point. Just about anything but success was out of the question. Filled with a purpose, I knew my time for sitting was done, and my time for action was at hand. I had a friend to get back, I had a past to correct in the future...everything played in my hands... ...the same hands which left a twenty five cent tip for the waitress on the table I was at. She'd been a bitch anyways... View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
