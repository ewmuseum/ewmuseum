Reasons for a comeback

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Reasons for a comeback Andrew 'Ace' Hawk CCW Promo #17 Date: 02/17/2003 Location: Arizona 'Sometimes coming back to a place you like after some time can be good...' **The scene shows Andrew Hawk getting out of his limo this past Thursday on Lockdown** 'Sometimes it won't...' **Scenes show the crowd booing and almost rioting as they see him.** 'The memories of my life in that place are huge...' **Ace is seen walking down the aisle in the empty arena after Lockdown has ended. A morbid silence can be heard.** 'The aisle, the painful place where everything in my life was put in perspective once...' **The scene shows some scenes of the historical battle between Andrew Hawk and Bobby Blades in 2001. We then see Jason McAllister and John Jupiter, Ace's stable partners, coming down the aisle and destroying Ace, giving Bobby the win. The scene cuts to later. A trashed Ace, head down, sitting on the entrance ramp in the empty arena, thinking about his future.** 'For so many times, my heart was shattered by the business...' **Scene shows Ace trying to get up after a loss.** 'Heartbreaking losses...' **Scene shows Ace losing balance and falling, unwillingly, off a scaffold in a scaffold match early in his career.** 'Backstage politics...' **Scene shows Ace losing to 'The Avenger' Tim Inman after an interference from Brad F'n Lamen, in a non-interference match, not reversed** 'Angry brothers...' **Scene shows Ace about ready to get his hands on the CCW World Title in a 5-tier cage match, but being thrown out by his twin brother Alex, hanging from the ceiling on a rope.** 'Rival presidents...' **Scene shows then-CCW president Paul Arovnik firing Ace and banning him from CCW for life.** 'But yet, I've always come back in shape.' **Scene shows, in order, Ace defeating his opponent in the scaffold match in a later regular match, Ace laughing on the crowd at Tim after he lost his title to John Jupiter, Ace beating his twin brother to a bloody pulp and Ace coming back 6 months later, after Paul himself got banned from CCW for life.** 'And today, the cycle once again restarts. It's time for the world to remember the Hawk!!!' _____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________ **The scene today opens at a huge, steep cliff, somewhere in the Arizona desert. From the distance, we can see a small dot of someone trying to beat it. As the camera zooms, we can see the man's long blond hair waving to the heavy wind blowing in the area. The scene zooms even more close and we see the man struggling to get a hold of the rock. He's about 20 feet away from a small ridge, right below the peak. The man tries to give one last push to grab the small ridge, but he loses his footing, as the rock he was supporting his weight gets loose. In the blink of an eye, though, he manages to grab the ridge above him, saving his life. As the man fights for his life, we hear a voice from above:** 'You know, it'd be a shame to loose such a great wrestler to a rock climbing accident...' **The man doesn't pay attention, focusing all his strenght on pulling himself up. After a few moments, one final push and a painful roar can be heard. The man finally manages to bring his face up and slowly get on the ridge. This moment his face is revealed as being the former old CCW World Champion Andrew Hawk. He seem different, though. His beard is full and his face is full of dirt, meshed with sweat and blood. Ace finally sits down on the ridge, and the voice speaks again:** 'Man, I gotta say: I never thought I'd see you like that in my life. You look like a homeless man who just ran away from the police.' **Ace once again doesn't pay attention to the voice. He sits on the ridge and looks at the horizon, quietly contemplating the sunset, letting the breeze blow through his body and quietly listening to the sound of the world... Just to be interrupted again.** 'You know, for someone who was so humiliated on their last stint as a wrestler, you should be thrilled to listen to what I have to say...' **Ace, finally breaking from his little trance, says:** 'You have nothing that could interest me, Alex!' **The scene finally shows the man behind the voice at the peak, and we see Ace's twin brother Alex. Aside from the clean face and short hair, they look exactly the same. Alex, looking at the sunset, says:** 'Well, I beg to differ. I mean, look at yourself... You're completely trashed. You can't possibly tell me you're enjoying this life...' 'Yes, I can...', answers Ace. 'And I will.' **Alex rolls his eyes at the top of the mountain, before saying:** 'Please, those are just words. Listen to yourself. You can't even accept the fact that your life is completely meaningless right now...' **Ace looks down, accepting the words. Alex continues:** 'And now, there you are, feeling sorry for yourself, having nothing to do. Please, Ace, at least listen to what I have for you.' **Ace, after pondering for a few moments, finally gets up and steps out of the ridge and into the peak, where Alex can be seen sitting on a beach chair. Another chair is set right on his side, and Ace sits down. Alex opens a small orange cooler, laying to his left, getting two beers. After giving one to Ace, he opens his and just savors the moment. Ace looks down and says:** 'If it's about the business, you know I'm not in the best shape of my life.' **Alex, smiling, says:** 'Yeah, I know you have been bugged by blown knees in the last year, but here's the thing: There's something that may make you rethink your retirement.' **Ace looks at the horizon, now purple, and says:** 'I don't know... Why should I come back? I mean, no federation will give me a chance after I blew CCW. And besides, my knees won't carry me to a World Title anywhere. So why fight?' **Alex smiles and then says:** 'What about... fighting for unity?' 'What are you talking about?', Ace asks, puzzled. 'Ace, I think you've been under a rock for the last 2 weeks, right?', Alex says, sarcastically. 'You can say so.', Ace answers, sharing a laugh with Alex. 'Well, here's the deal, Ace.', Alex finally says. 'CCW is back!!' **Ace's eyes finally get wide open, revealing the shock that ran over Ace's spine. He says:** 'You're lying. You HAVE to be lying...' 'No, I'm not...', says Alex, before continuing. 'And there's more: They're needing you there.' 'What???' **Alex pulls a letter out of his black Armani suit's inner pocket, handing it to Ace. The CCW logo on the top left corner, a small letter inside:** 'Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, The time has come for CCW to live again. And although you've been chastised for closing CCW once before, I know that you did it for personal and financial reasons. Now, CCW is struggling to get off its feet, and I need your help. With you on our side again, CCW will once again rise among the small stupid feds that call themselves great and show to the world what it missed in the last year. I'll be waiting for you on the offices. Money is not an issue. Ask and you'll be granted. I'll see you soon. Sylkk.' **Ace finishes reading the letter, thinks for a little while, and then, with one word, he gives his verdict:** 'No' **Alex, shocked, turns to him and says:** 'WHAT? Are you crazy? This is your chance, man.' 'I don't think so, Alex. This is just another trap by that bastard to get me humiliated again. I don't wanna go back and be hated by the world again.' 'What are you talking about? The fans? They never cared about you. They only cared about you when you were throwing suicide dives off cages onto people on the floor. But when you were screwed, time and time again, they didn't even care to side with you. They went for the attackers, laughing at you, beaten on the floor. They're nothing, a bunch of pariahs who are not worth the popcorn they buy in the arena.' **Ace looks down, his eyes beginning to show the built anger in his soul.** 'And now, here you have it, man. The chance to come back and shove it down their throats. The chance to be in a position of power and destroy their little world, beat their idols and crash their little dreams, showing who their idols really are: A bunch of narcisists who can't find their way in a decent wrestling match.' **Ace stays looking down, his eyes burning, his rage, built for years of pain and suffering, begin to emerge. Alex finally says:** 'Good. I can see your fighting fire is back. Talk to Sylkk, and see what he has to say. I'll talk to you later.' **Alex walks away and Ace is shown looking at the empty, his eyes now completely focused. The scene slowly morphs. His bears is gone. His hair is a little shorter. His clothes are changed from the mountaineering climbing gear to his usual leather jacket and white shirt. His expression is unchanged, his eyes zeroed on a TV screen. On the tube, a man he'll face on his comeback to CCW: Adam Christ. He begins mumbling a few words:** 'Pain, suffering, blood. I heard all that before in my career. It's nothing new. Stepping stone, also nothing new. I used a lot of CCW superstars as a steeping stone in my career. And even had a few punks wishing they could use ME as a stepping stone. But you, punk, don't know what's coming to you. I'm back, and I'm gonna show you what's to really be in the CCW. Because this, my friend, will be an experience you'll never forget. You can go to another fed, have some success there, it won't matter. In the end of the day, you'll sit down, look back and realize that once you go CCW, you can't go back. CCW is an unique experience, a life style that I'm not sure you're ready for. This Lockdown, I'll make sure to test you. Then we'll see if you're ready for the primetime!!' **As Ace says that, he keeps looking at the TV, and the scene fades to black** View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
