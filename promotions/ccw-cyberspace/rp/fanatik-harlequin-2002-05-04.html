Fanatik - 'Harlequin'

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Fanatik, bloodied and unstable, is interviewed in a room of boxes and threatens Kensuke, Spider and The Valiant One.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Fanatik Entitled: 'Harlequin' Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 9:29:02 Location: ??? This is a: Match RP Har·le·quin - 1. A fool, jester, or comedian in an entertainment (as a play), 2. A person who constantly plays the buffoon. [ The scene opens to the familiar room full of empty cardboard boxes. The room is dim, since only the back-up lights are on, leaving the room shaded gray. The camera pans across the room, stopping at a crouching shadow in the far corner. As the cameraman steps up to the figure, a voice echoes from it's direction... ] Fanatik: Stop... or you'll get wet... [ The voice seems muffled and low, sounding absoultely nothing like Fanatik whatsoever. The camera haults, focusing on the shadowey figure, trying anxiously to get a better view of it, but fails. Instead, the camera keeps a steady picture around the figure, in hope that he'll come out of the shadows eventually. ...There's a long pause. Suddenly, a puddle of black liquid seeks it's way out of the shadows, exposing itself to be red once it moves under the light. The cameraman steps back a few more steps... startled by this, and somewhat frightened. Nonetheless, he keeps the camera locked onto the figure... and awaits him to speak once more. ...And he gets his wish... ] Fanatik: ...Who are you...? [ ...There's a brief moment of silence before the cameraman responds... ] Cameraman: ...I'm a CCW camera man... I'm here to record your promo... [ ...Another moment of silence. Slowly, however, Fanatik speaks once more... ] Fanatik: ...What for...? Cameraman: ...Because you have two matches on Lockdown... one against The Valiant One, and another against Spider and Kensuke... Fanatik: ...Kensuke... [ The shadowey figure suddenly becomes taller, as the squeaking sound of wet flesh rubbing up a dry, flat surface fills the room. The cameraman takes yet another step back, just in case... ] Fanatik: ...That name... is familiar. ...Hearing it makes my ears ring... and makes my blood rush to my head. ...Why is that? [ ...Silence. ] Cameraman: ...I don't kn-- Fanatik: WHY is that!? Cameraman: Mn...! B...Because you're brothers...!? [ ...Suddenly, the room becomes completely silent once more. Only the long, deep breaths from the dark corner are heard... along with the sound of a dripping faucet. ...But, soon after... the silence is broken. ] Fanatik: ...What did you say? Cameraman: ...I... I said... because you're brothe-- [ Fanatik steps out of the shadows and under the light, revealing his face to be coaked with blood. His wears the same shirt he wore at Group Therapy, soaked with blood and sweat, unwashed. His long, dirty hair falls back, hiding his ears and revealing a shard of glass to be shoved in his forehead. Oddly, however... Fanatik grins widely. The blood running between his lips, mixing with his saliva... but he seems to be ignorant of that. He stares directly at the camera... unblinking, eyes wide open. ] Fanatik: What does THAT have to do with ANYTHING!? HUH!? [ The cameraman struggles for words, staggering back as Fanatik continues to step forward... ] Fanatik: Could I EVER hurt my brother?! My own FLESH and BLOOD!? MY blood?! [ Fanatik comes to a hault... touching his face and pulling his hand back, staring at his fingertips... coated red. He continues to grin as he speaks... staring down at his hand... ] Fanatik: Look at this... it's Kensuke's blood... in MY body!! KENSUKE is NOT a part of me!! KENSUKE is NOT a FUCKING part of me!! [ Fanatik's hand turns into a fist... as his eyes close. Slowly, he lowers his hand to his side... taking long, deep breaths... as his eyes re-open... staring at the camera. ] Fanatik: ...Death will come to us all... and, to some of us, it will come earlier than expected... if desired by God. [ Fanatik turns... walking back to the corner he began from. ] Fanatik: Leave. Cameraman: ...But, wait...! What about The Valiant One...? [ ...Fanatik stops... standing still for a few moments, before finally responding... ] Fanatik: ...What about him... Cameraman: ...You and him used to be pretty close, right? ...Do you think this will change the outcome of your match? [ ...Another short pause, until Fanatik turns to face the camera once more... ] Fanatik: On SMN, The Valiant One's blood will paint the ring red... and his pathetic rantings on 'being Valiant or die trying' aren't very impressive in the least. He can say that he'll beat me... say that he'll DESTROY me... but these are all just words. And, by the look of it... if a certain 'Dirk' can knock the boy on his ass... then I don't see a very large threat. ...Indeed, him and I used to be friends... until I found the truth. ...You see, I was going down the wrong path... I was leading down the path of humiliation...! Every day, the pains would tighten around my mind, almost driving me into MADNESS!! [ Fanatik's grin widens a bit... ] Fanatik: Of course, I'm not mad... because, as I said... I found truth. ...God had given me a second chance... and I took it. ...And, look at me now! LOOK AT ME!!!! I'm BETTER than I was! Before, I was nothing more than... than a CLOWN! A HARLEQUIN!! Nothing more than something one can sit on their asses and laugh at!! But, it was NEVER meant to be funny!! IS IT FUNNY!? VALIANT ONE, am I still FUNNY!? HAHA!!! [ Fanatik's hands rise to his head, running his fingertips through his long, dirty hair... tugging faintly... trying to force himself to calm down. The shirt presses tightly against his chest, showing his large, un-natural build... ] Fanatik: Valiant One... I'm going to give you something to laugh at. ...On SMN... you're going to bleed for me... and then... maybe you'll learn a few things. ...And, maybe you can see the truth as well... the same way I did. ...Pain... humiliation... HUMILIATION... HUMILIATION!! [ Fanatik stands rigidly, his head tilted to one side... ] Fanatik: ...Be Valiant... and Die Trying. [ Fanatik turns his head, spitting blood from his mouth for a moment... to turn his everlasting glare back to the camera. ] Fanatik: ...Back to Spider and... Kensuke... [ Fanatik pauses at the name... but manages to shake it off... ] Fanatik: ...Spider... are you threatened? ...If not... you should be. ...Blayze is better than before... and, if he pinning you will let him achieve his World Title shot... then, so be it! YOU will be the marked man, Spider! Even your own partner and you haven't always gotten along! Your bond will be weak...! And OUR bond... Blayze and I... are STRONGER than ever! YOU have no way to be helped, PRESIDENT SPIDER! You can't hide behind your enforcement slaves... your referees, your collegues! Because, Spider... when you step out of those curtains... and you become blinded by the sheep's cheers... then, you'll become nothing more than every one of them. And while you're smiling in your spotlight of glory... you'll find yourself drowning in it. ...Just like I did... [ Fanatik's head slumps forward faintly... staring downward, at the puddle of blood forming below him... ] Fanatik: ...And you'll love it. But it'll be a FALSE love!! LUST!! LUST!!! [ Fanatik's head jerks up, as he takes a few quick steps toward the camera... ] Fanatik: YOU don't love them, Spider! Why don't you admit that?! You CAN'T love them...! You just prance around, eating it up...!! Well, NO MORE! On SMN, while the fans continue to patheticly attempt to feed your ego... Blayze will be the one to reach down your throat and RIP it out!! And I... if needed... will be the one to snap your neck, much like how your partner did to you months ago... in order to give Blayze something much needed... and much deserved. [ Fanatik stares at the camera... his grin faintly becoming smaller... but, in relative sense, is still quite large. ] Fanatik: ...Kensuke... Kensuke... Kensuke... KENSUKE... KENSUKE... KENSUKE... KENSUKE! KENSUKE! KENSUKE! KENSUKE! KENSUKE!! KENSUKE!!! [ Fanatik franticly grabs boxes left and right, hurling them at the walls, throwing them in all directions. The cameraman jerks back, giving thought about turning the camera off... but Fanatik suddenly leaps toward him, grabbing hold of the camera. A faint yelp is heard from the cameraman's side, as the picture shakes around, until falling to the ground... showing a tilted view of the dark, empty room. ...Loud, sickening sounds are heard in the background... until the camera jerks upward... and turns to show Fanatik's face, in a close-up... covered in blood. The camera shakes a little... showing that it's being held up by Fanatik, now... as he slowly speaks... ] Fanatik: ...Kensuke... on SMN... there won't be any brotherly love... because I... am going to kill you. ...Do you hear me...?! KILL you...!! KILL YOU!! KILL YOU!!! KILL YOU!!!! KILL YOU!!!!! [ Fanatik throws the camera aside, causing the picture to flicker a few times... but, somehow, stays on... and continues to record. It's faces back where Fanatik just threw it... showing a door being slapped close... and the cameraman hung on the wall, on a large, red cross... his head hung downward... blood dripping from his chin. ...To his right... are many more smaller red crosses, covering the entire wall. ...And then... the camera fades to black. ] View Biography
