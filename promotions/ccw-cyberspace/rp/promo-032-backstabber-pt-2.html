Backstabber (Pt. 2)

Backstabber (Pt. 2) Scorpion CCW Promo #32 Date: 2-24-03 Location: New York City, New York :::: The scene opens up with Scorpion and Mike walking out the abandon warehouse where Corky tried to kill Scorpion. Scorpion following Mike’s lead, head out to a parking lot. :::: Scorpion: What are we doing here? Mike: Shhhhhh, look. :::: Scorpion peers out to a stretch limo that has pulled up to the warehouse where Corky and a few of his gang members head inside. :::: Scorpion: Mike where are they going? Mike: I have no idea. Hurry let’s get to my truck. :::: Scorpion and Mike run to Mike’s Black 2000 Dodge Pick-up truck. Scorpion gets inside the passenger side while Mike gets the truck ready. :::: Mike: All right let’s follow that limo. They probably expect your dead and they probably expect I’m dead as well too. Scorpion: Why what did they do to you? Mike: I knew about the Drake set-up way before you showed up in town. Corky turned the whole gang against Drake without Drake even realizing it. It was only a matter of time until they got him. Scorpion: So why didn’t you tell Drake? Mike: Well I was going to but I was too late. I was approached by Corky to be his right hand man once Drake was killed. I told him to “Go Fuck himself” and the next thing I know a crowbar comes across my face. That was the last time I would see light for the next couple of days and once I woke up he was dead. Scorpion: How did you know I was in town? Mike: I heard through the grape vine that you might have been responsible for Drake’s death. But I also knew what Corky’s plan was and you were never ever in them. Everyone expected you were done with the city anyway. Scorpion: So did I until I got dragged back into the getting justice for my friend. Mike: We will get justice all right, that little rat-faced bastard will not control what you, Drake and I helped built. Scorpion: I knew all along that Corky was a backstabbing son of a bitch. Drake told me we should help the kid get strong and become brave. So we let him in, not realizing he would never change. I always had a feeling Corky was the biggest mistake we made in the gang. Mike: I agree with you man. Everyone knew that his intentions were to be the leader of the group. When you were there he tried to kiss your ass. When you left he tried to kiss Drake’s ass. When Drake wasn’t going to let the kid be his right hand man, Corky backstabbed Drake and helped kill him. Scorpion: I can believe the gang would let Corky get away with this. Mike: The gang had no choice. Corky has a new best friend. Scorpion: What do you mean a new best friend? Mike: Corky has aligned himself with a man-beast. This guy is seven feet tall, three hundred fifty pounds and is all monster. Everyone in the gang that didn’t want to help Corky got the shit beat out of them or was killed. I was one of the few survivors. Scorpion: I knew that kid had something up his sleeve because I know all those guys would never ever turn their back on Drake. Drake was the closest thing some of those kids had to a father. Mike: Your right man and I know for sure that none of those guys want to have Corky as their leader. The guy is power hungry, he always has been. He is a wimp, that’s why he has the guy with him so he can move his weight around and over power the gang. I know you want nothing to do with this but we have to get the gang back before it’s too late. Scorpion: Believe me Mike I want something to do with it. I want to prove to the guys that don’t know Corky was behind tipping off the cops, not me. I have to clear my name before this idiot runs it on the streets I helped built. Mike: Good to have you back Scorpion. Most of us would have never thought that the street thug Scorpion would return to lay claim to what he helped built. Scorpion: I’m only back for one reason Mike and I’ll make this clear…revenge. :::: The black Dodge Pick-up truck driven by Mike follows the stretch limo into Kennedy airport. :::: Scorpion: What the hell is he doing here? Mike: His folks have money, major money. He’s probably going to his private jet. Scorpion: Do you know where it’s located? Mike: Yeah, I know the guard at the gate he will let us in easily. Scorpion: Alright let’s get this punk. :::: Mike pulls up the gate where the security guard approaches him. :::: Security Guard: Hey Mike, what’s up? Mike: I gotta get to the private plane is that cool? Security Guard: Sure let me get the gate up. :::: The security guard walks over to his booth and lifts the gate allowing the pick-up truck inside the runway to the private plane where Corky is boarding. :::: Scorpion: There he is! Mike: Get out the guns out of the back seat. :::: Scorpion reaches in the back and grabs two hand guns, handing one to Mike who has his head out the window, driving the truck with one hand, the other hand on the gun. Scorpion is out his passenger window preparing to shoot. :::: Scorpion: Floor this mother fucker! Mike: I’am! :::: Corky sees the pick-up truck with Mike and Scorpion hanging out of the window. Corky rushes onto the plane, leaving two guards waiting for Mike and Scorpion. :::: Mike: We are running out of time we gotta get those guys and head onto that plane. Scorpion: All right, brake it! :::: The pick-up truck comes to a screeching holt as Mike and Scorpion jump out of the truck where the two guards are waiting for them. As they jump out the plane begins to take off. :::: Mike: Dammit we missed it! Scorpion: Let’s take care of these guys, they will tell us where he is going. :::: Mike and Scorpion get into a fight with the security guards. Scorpion and Mike pummeled the hell out of the security guards. Scorpion has the gun to one of the guys head questioning him. :::: Scorpion: Where is that plane going? Gang member: (crying) It’s….it’s going to Las Vegas. Mike: You tell us the truth? Gang member: (crying) Yes…yes sir. :::: They throw the gang member down to the ground and Mike looks up to Scorpion. :::: Mike: You know what that means right Scorpion? Scorpion: Yeah we are going to be wheelin’ and dealin’ in SIN CITY MOTHER FUCKER!!! TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
