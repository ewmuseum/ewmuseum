Mr. Gobstopper - Deja Vu? I think not...

What this is A Match RP in which Mr. Gobstopper speaks at the Sydney Opera House about his coming match with The Supreme Machine.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mr. Gobstopper Entitled: Deja Vu? I think not... Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 01:01:49 Location: The Opera House in Sydney This is a: Match RP *Scene opens with the camera zoomed in on a seagull pecking at peanut shells on a sidewalk outside of the Opera House. One by one, another shell is tossed to the ground as the seagull is lured closer to the camera. Once the bird is close up, the camera pans out to show someone's feet, then legs, chest,and finally a face. It's Mr. Gobstopper, leaning against a small brick wall.* Gobstopper: Nice day! A wonderful little sea breeze coming off the harbor, seagulls all around, and not a cloud in the sky. Makes me feel like singing... but I won't. You see, it seems that the high ups of CCW think I should have a little match. I may not be too excited had I been scheduled to face someone like, oh I dunno, The Valiant One or Blayze, but oh no. I have been booked to meet the very man who beat me in the very first COTR tournament finals, The Supreme Machine. *He begins walking down the sidewalk as the looks around with the magnificient landmark rising gracefully in the background.* Gobstopper: It was almost three years ago when he and I met for the very first time in a CCW ring. While it wasn't the last, it was one of the most meaningful. He and I, legends. With the COTR 4 right around the corner, how fitting of a match than to have the first two COTR finalists EVER to face one another. Ah, but it's more than that. I came back to prove a point. To prove a point to all the Phil Burners, all the Tim Inmans, all the neurotic Neurosis fans, and all the Chang lovers. This is the summer of the Gobber! I have my sights set on many things, and first up, is the COTR. So SuMa, get ready. I know you've just come back, and I have just come back, but there is one thing I have over you. You wanna know what it is? Well, you'll just have to wait and find out this Monday night! Take care pal, I'll catch ya later. *Gobstopper continues walking down the sidewalk as the camera stops following and turns around to zoom in on the seagull as it ascends into the bright blue sky. Fade out.* View Biography
