Part 1 of the 'Cold Aprils' miniseries (PPV RP #1)

23 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Part 1 of the 'Cold Aprils' miniseries (PPV RP #1) Jason Dynasty CCW Promo #101 Date: 3/23/03 Location: Hotel In like a lion and out like a lamb, isn’t that what they say about April? Everything major, any storms or bad weather is supposed to occur at the beginning of the month while the end is calm and relatively simplistic in its innocence. April is changing, for the better? April is changing, for the worst? That is a question yet to be answered, but soon, soon April shall hit and with it shall hit a force never seen in CCW, a force that will impact the federation down to their very core. For with April this year comes ice and when the Iceman cometh, he cometh for your souls. The scene opens as the drip, drip, dripping of rain can be heard hitting the asphalt of one of Washington’s more secluded areas. A large building is shown, with a sign in front that reads “Charleston Estates”. A car pulls up, it is a 2002 Suzuki Esteem Sedan, painted silver and obviously recently waxed. Both the driver’s and front passenger side doors open and out step two rather large, rather ominous men. The passenger was a 6’5”, 265-pound beast. After a moment of standing with his back to the camera he turns and is easily identified as Damien Nhyte, one of the newest members of CCW. He turns and looks at the man who is getting out of the driver’s side. Nhyte (questioningly): Are you ready Jason? The driver shuts his door and turns to show that it is indeed the Iceman himself, Jason Dynasty. He adjusts his black trenchcoat and trademark wrap-around sunglasses, which he wears despite the rainfall and gloomy day. He then slips on some black leather gloves and tosses Nhyte a pair. Dynasty (nodding): Of course I am, don’t forget that you are the one who asked me to come along on this little trip. Nhyte (nodding and speaking with slight irritation): Hey, it isn’t my fault that you are better at this shit than I am. For if I am going to do this than I want to make sure that it is done right and that John Paul gets what is coming to him. So how do you want to do this? Dynasty makes no immediate reply but rather moves to the trunk of his car and pulls out a leather bag, a small bottle with a greenish liquid in it, a handkerchief, a large wood board with two circular holes in it about 4 inches in diameter, one in the middle of the board and the other near one end. He grabs a second piece of wood cut into a circle about 7 inches in diameter, numerous nails, a small amount of tubing and two hammers. He looks at Nhyte and hands him the boards of wood and hammers, before placing the nails, handkerchief and bottle in his coat and picking up the bag. He then grabs a small bowl and places it in Nhyte’s jacket pocket, signaling for him to be careful with it. Dynasty (making his way towards the building): What room is it? Nhyte (following closely behind Dynasty): Room 318, on the third floor. Dynasty (sarcastically): That is usually where rooms starting in three are put. The two men walk into the building and are immediately met with a rush of a very unfamiliar smell. It is the smell of lilacs, daffodils and various other flowers that adorn the lobby of the hotel complex. The two men make their way to the back of the lobby where they enter one of a row of elevators. The heavy glass doors close behind them and as they do Dynasty pushes the “3” button while Nhyte leans against the elevator wall where he stares down at the pieces of wood in his hand. Nhyte: What are these for? Dynasty (smiling slightly as he whistles a tune): You’ll see. Nhyte (nodding slowly): Look, Jason, just to be sure that we understand each other. I want John Paul dead, as in corpse, for if he ever again does what he did last week then I will never forgive myself. Dynasty: What did he do man? You never told me, you just said that you had a man that needed to have his place in hell filled. Nhyte (shaking his head): I’ll tell you what happened later ok. Can we just focus on the task at hand? Dynasty (slowly): Sure man, no problem. A bell is heard as the elevator stops and the doors open to show a wallpapered hallway with the words “Floor Three” written in bold gold lettering on the wall in front of the elevator. The two men step out and after seeing a sign reading “Rooms 301-320 ” they turn to the left and head down the narrow hallway. As they walk they see signs of room service and maid service at random doors, finally reaching a corner and turning, immediately coming to a door that reads “318” on it in gold letters. A Do Not Disturb sign hangs on the doorknob and a tray of leftover steak sits on the table next to the door. Dynasty hands the leather bag to Nhyte who carefully holds it at arm’s length as Dynasty gets down on his knees and pulls out two steel paperclips from his trenchcoat. He expertly pulls apart one, twisting it in such away that it hooks into two small holes at the bottom of the electronic keyhole. He then opens up the second paperclip and slips it into the area where the hotel key cards goes. He sets it in the center of the slot and swiftly drags it across the slit, pushing down in certain places. A click is heard and green light flashes on the door handle. Dynasty stands up, pulling the two clips out and placing them back in his coat before opening the door slowly and quietly. He takes the bag back from Nhyte who is simply shaking his head at the ease at which Dynasty picked the electronic lock. Dynasty (noticing Nhyte’s smirk and shrugging in response): What? I led a very secluded life in a house with a lot of doors. Nhyte laughs slightly before Dynasty smiles and signals for him to be quiet. Nhyte nods and Dynasty slowly opens the door all the way, a cool breeze drifts out as the room had its air conditioner on. The two men step into the room, closing it behind them and slowly making their way to where the bed was located. In the bed lay a man in his late twenties, dressed only in a T-shirt and boxers, asleep in bed with the sheets strewn about the area. Dynasty moves over to the side of the bed nearest the man’s head and takes out the handkerchief and bottle. He dumps the contents into the handkerchief and pushes the rag over the man’s mouth. The man awakens in fear, struggling and thrashing, trying to scream but inhaling the liquid as he does so. Dynasty holds down tightly and it is only a few moments before the man goes limp from the fumes of the potent liquid. Nhyte stares at Dynasty in amazement as Dynasty stands up and takes notice. Dynasty (pointing at the rag as he places it in his pocket): Ammonium, it’ll knock you right out. Nhyte (nodding): What now? Dynasty (gesturing towards the body): Now you can help me drag him to the bathroom. Nhyte only nods as he grabs the man by his feet, while Dynasty grabs him under the arms and the two men drag the unconscious body towards the hotel bathroom. The two men dump the body on the floor as Dynasty signals for Nhyte to hand him the hammers and boards as well as the bowl. Dynasty (as he empties his pockets of the stuff he had put there): Strip. Nhyte: Huh? Dynasty (pointing at the body): Strip off his clothes. Nhyte (looking at Dynasty): Are you nuts? Dynasty (unscrewing the bowl’s top): Depends on your definition of “nuts”, this will work better if he is not wearing clothing ok. Nhyte shrugs and soon the man is stripped nude and lying on the bathroom floor. Dynasty (looking at the man and smirking): I feel sorry for the women who had to sleep with that. Now lets dump him in the tub and for God’s sake Damien, don’t touch anything but his feet. Nhyte shoots Dynasty a glare as he grips the man’s feet and helps Dynasty dump him into the tub, face down. Dynasty then hands Nhyte the long board as well as the hammer and nails. Dynasty: Hammer this shut over the bathtub, drive the nails as far as possible. Nhyte nods and goes to work hammering, as he does so Dynasty begins to spread a pasty glue along the edge between the wood and the tub, sealing it airtight around the edge. He and Nhyte both finish and Dynasty quickly screws the paste jar back up, but not before grabbing a small amount and grabbing the tubing. He puts the tubing around the water faucet of the tub and leads it into the hole in the wood near the edge. He then pastes the tubing so that no air gets between the wood and faucet leads directly into the tub where the man lay. Dynasty grabs the leather bag and shows it to Nhyte who looks at it cluelessly. Dynasty dumps the contents into hole in the middle of the wood board, allowing one of the items to drop onto the wooden board. Nhyte looks down and sees a small red and black spider crawling across the board. Dynasty (gesturing towards the spider and then at the bag): There were fifty of those things in here, they call these spiders the Red Amazon Leveler Spiders. They are amazingly poisonous when provoked, luckily almost nothing provokes them. As if to show off this fact, Dynasty begins pushing the venomous spider towards the hole, finally allowing the spider to drop in. Dynasty smirks and points to the small 7-inch diameter piece of wood. He proceeds to hammer the wood over the hole, so that there is no way for the spiders to get out. Once complete he stands up and puts away all of the items he had brought and placing them in his trenchcoat. He looks at Nhyte with an approving nod, to which Nhyte responds. Nhyte: So now what? Dynasty: Now you tell me why we are doing this. Nhyte (sighing): He hit on my wife ok. She came home from the gym crying because some jackass had harassed her. I wanted to teach him a lesson and figured you could help. Dynasty (nodding towards the sealed tub): Looks like he will get that message. Ok, so now we can go. Nhyte: What if those spiders don’t bite him and he survives? Dynasty (his face showing mock sadness): Fine, you want to be sure he dies, well I told you that almost nothing provokes those spiders. Well, one thing does and causes them to bite like mad. That one thing would be water. Nhyte grins and walks over to the tub as Dynasty walks out of the bathroom, holding open the door to the hotel hallway while watching Nhyte. Nhyte puts his hand on the water handle and pauses before looking at Dynasty who simply smiles and shrugs. Nhyte laughs and quickly pulls on the handle, unleashing gallons of water into the sealed-shut bathtub before turning out the bathroom lights and walking out the room door. Dynasty (pausing to hear the sound of rushing water): And people say I am no fun. He smiles, satisfied with his work, and shuts the door as the camera fades to black with the sound of running water and the muffled screams of a man in the background.
