In Response to Fat Ass

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A candle-lit monologue in which The Supreme Machine answers Phil Burner ahead of their Memorial Day Showdown match, with Chang the Mongolian Giant at his side.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: In Response to Fat Ass Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 13:53:56 Location: The Ether This is a: Interview The light from hundreds of candles casts eerie shadows around a dimly lit room. As the camera pans around, the shadows dance their strange dance across the bare walls. Bare that is, except for a huge, framed photograph that hangs alone. The shadows continue to dance as the camera begins to zoom in, revealing an image frozen in time, a relic of a bygone age. The camera continues to zoom in. So you think I'm slipping do you Phillip? Slowly, as the camera zooms closer, the image becomes clearer. Four men, friends perhaps, with their arms wrapped around each other smile out from the past. You think I won't make it to Memorial Day Showdown do you Phillip? The four men are instantly recognizable to anyone that is familiar with CCW. On one side there is former CCW Heavyweight Champion Thunder, his long blond hair framing his smiling brown face as he holds a can of beer up as a salute to the camera. You think I'm not the same as I was do you Phillip? On the other side is CCW legend Apocalypse, his tanned face accentuating his beaming smile. You think I'm not focused do you Phillip? Between Thunder and Apocalypse......................Phil Burner and The Supreme Machine. They are both wearing WAI T-shirts, their arms wrapped warmly in friendship around each other's shoulders. Four friends. Four champions. Four legends. WELL YOU ARE WRONG!!!! DEAD WRONG!!!!! The camera zooms out and pans around the candle-lit room. I admit that I may have underestimated Mr. Gobstopper last Monday night but as you will know, Phillip, I never make the same mistake twice. If anything, that near loss has served as a wake-up call, if you will...a reality check for want of a better term. The Supreme Machine now appears in shot. He is sitting in a huge leather chair, the only furnishing in the room. His long black hair hangs loosely across his hard, impassive face and down past his massive shoulders. Beside the throne-like leather chair, Chang the Mongolian Giant, sits on his haunches staring off into space. I think that after almost two years of retirement I am entitled to a little ring rust...but I can now assure you that whatever rust was there has been removed , Phillip, and come Memorial Day I will be more than ready to step up and take you down.... once and for all. Chang purrs as The Supreme Machine begins patting his pet's shining bald head. Our meeting at Memorial Day Showdown will be a match to go down in the history books Phillip. Two legends in the world of sports entertainment finally facing each other one on one. After all these years ..you and me...in the same ring.. at the same time. Who will walk away victorious? I think we both already know the answer to that one, don't we Phillip? The Supreme Machine wipes the hair from his face with his right hand.. And before you go trying to pull any of your silly stunts on LDT, I think you should remember one small thing Phillip. Just as you know me, don't forget that there is no-one in CCW that knows YOU better I do! After all that we've been through together, I believe that I know you better than you know yourself. Do something that I won't be expecting? No, I don't think so Phillip. He pauses and stretches his shoulders back before leaning forward and glaring down the camera. You have my attention Phillip, you always have, but now I suggest you prepare yourself for Memorial Day and stop worrying about me. I can look after myself, I always have.. and as for me keeping one eye open.. well let's just say... YOU SHOULD KNOW ME BETTER THAN THAT! View Biography
