Group Therapy Hardcore Style (KoE RP)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short roleplay in which Danny Bryson, in an abattoir with a vial of blood, talks of a secret to feeling no pain.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Group Therapy Hardcore style (KoE RP) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 15:01:32 Location: This is a: Match RP The camera fades in, or, at least, it tries to, but since the room is so dark, you can't see much anyway. For those who are present, there is a faintly metallic smell wafting through the air. A voice patently begins to speak in front of the camera. You'd think Bryson would be sick of speaking in the dark by now, but who are we to try and guess whats going on in his head. 'That smell. It is the smell of victory. Or, perhaps, the smell of the byproduct. It symbolises all we stand for, all we can achieve. It teaches us that we are human, are finite. Most of all, it teaches us that we are alive. Some might say that it is the only thing that keeps us alive. Literally, they are right, but philosophically they miss the point. It is a symbolism, for those who know the secret it is nothing more.' The lights come up to find Bryson standing in the middle of a gigantic abbatoir. The smell was the odour of blood - he holds a vial in his hand, staring at it. 'The secret, you see, is what keeps the initiated going. Keeps us in the game, keeps us strong dispite the adversity. It is the secret that shows us we are alive. What is the secret? Is it enough to say it is what will drive me to become Kind of Extreme on Sunday night. That is exactly what it will do, but, no, it is not enough. Perhaps I could say that the secret will be what drives me when I drive each and every competitor through seven levels of Hell and back at Group Therapy. When I slam them through plate glass, rent their skin from their bones, watch as their blood runs from them and soils the ground of the ring. This will also happen, but not even that is enough. The secret is what will enable me to beat 5 other men. Beat in the literal sense, to a pulp. It is what will enable me to be crowned in barbed wire. But, more than this, it is why I will feel no pain, why I will not suffer. It is why the hurt and suffering I endure will be turned into pleasure. What is the secret???? Don't you see???? No one has to suffer. People live in pain because they've forgotten how not to. People cry and scream and beg because their neighbours and friends do. Because, when it comes down to the bottom line, that is what the world expects. Well, gentlemen, I've remembered how to stop the pain. Have you?' Bryson smiles and unscrews the lid of the jar as the camera fades to black View Biography
