Mr. Gobstopper - In respone to recent remarks...

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which Mr. Gobstopper, waiting in a Sydney airport terminal, answers The Supreme Machine’s remarks about their match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mr. Gobstopper Entitled: In respone to recent remarks... Written in Sunday, May 5, 2002, 01:10:08 Location: An airport terminal... This is a: Interview *Scene opens at Sydney's international airport where people are in line waiting to be ticketed. Mr. Gobstopper is shown waiting in line as well, near the back, with one black gym bag. An announcement comes over the terminal PA stating that the flight will be delayed by 45 minutes. Everyone in line returns to their seats. The camera catches up to Mr. Gobstopper who has a few quick statements to make about recent remarks.* Gobstopper: Damn, I really hate delayed flights. It's bad enough I have to have my crotch groped before I can pass through the security stations by a 62 year old aboriginie but now I have to sit here on my ass, in the middle of Sydney, and wait another 45 minutes for my flight back to the States. But hell, you're here, I'm here, so let me run my mouth about a few things. *Gobstopper sits down in a chair and props his foot up on another. A little lady walks by and takes a nearby seat. * Gobstopper: It seems like The Supreme Machine thinks I asked for a match with him. He thinks I agreed to face him. Did you see a challenge? Do you see my signature anywhere? I didn't think so. The higher ups decided it would make them richer to see you and me in a ring. While I don't have a gripe with that, I also don't have a gripe about kicking your ass all over the arena. You seem to think I have a death wish. You think alot. If I had a death wish, I'd stay here in Sydney. Sure the air is pure, but just about every where you go there are people who come up to me and tell me that you're going to... skin muh hide, mate. *Gobber swings his leg around and sits upright.* Gobstopper: Well, I'm not staying here any longer. I wouldn't want to have to hurt innocent people, then go to jail, face the death penalty, and therefore receiving my death wish, so well just forget it. But do let me remind you, SuMa, I HAVE beaten you before. I believe I defended my Independent Title against you at one time. Granted, you did beat me when it really counted. If history serves me correctly, you are up two matches to one. You beat me at the first COTR, and you defended your Heavyweight title against me in a cage match way back when. Of course, back then we had exploding cages and steel cutters. We also had douche insertions and missing camels. But that's another chapter of CCW history. *Gobber laughs as the little old lady looks at him with a shocked look upon her crown.* Gobstopper: So SuMa, please realize I'm not here to hurt you, but if that's what it takes to get you to shut up, so be it. *Gobber stands up and picks up his bag. He begins walking down the corridor.* Gobstopper: I'm a little hungry. I think I'll go get something to eat while I wait for my flight. Just remember SuMa, I won't go down easily. Remember that... oh, and a little message for Ricky Thunder...I don't know who you are. Why don't you bring me a cup of coffee Monday night. Thanks. *Gobstopper walks off as the camera stops in the middle of the corridor. The camera stares until fading out.* View Biography
