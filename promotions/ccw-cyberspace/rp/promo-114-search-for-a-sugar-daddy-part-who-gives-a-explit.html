Search for a Sugar Daddy part Who Gives a *Explitive Deleted* Sheena Ramone CCW Promo #114 Date: About fifteen minutes before I posted it Location: The land of Pain and Mayo “Anyone ever tell you that beard tickles their coochie?” Oh, god, not again. As you can prolly tell those words are from the mouth of the one, the only, Sheena Ramone. Known throughout the world as the biggest hole this side of the Grand Canyon, she sits with one Stoney Mcgee, recently divorced, recently wealthy, he was an easy mark for the loving arms, and open legs that are Sheena Ramone. It’d be silly, and illegal in most states to describe their debauchery over the last couple weeks, sufficed to say, they got it on, ugly and often. Stoney for his part looks to be in a complete haze, whether it be from drugs, or over stimulation is anyone’s guess, if you ask me, it’s most likely a combination of both. At this point, you’re most likely wondering why it’s been so long since you’ve last seen this happy couple. Well, the answer is quite frankly I don’t have a good stomach for this kinda garbage, and I can only show it to you every so often, besides, I’m not getting any, and I’m jealous of my creations. Feel better, now that you’ve humiliated me? Anyhoo, the two of them are like sitting there, talking. Let’s listen in, shall we? “Uh, no, I’ve like never really heard that man. But you know I don’t listen a lot.” “Oh don’t worry about it sweetie, I think it’s SEXY.” “Don’t you pretty much think everything is sexy man?” “That’s not the point.” Of course it’s the point. Actually, haven’t I driven this point home enough yet? She’s a slut, a BIG one, and she’ll hop anything that moves. But she’s also a gold digger so she prefers to go after rich dudes, or chicks, whatever’s closest. Besides Stoney was a good deal for her anyway, not only does he have money, but he’s an old friend of Jimmie and Bruce. Speaking of the Tard and Hard, they’ve yet to appear, which is strange. Usually by this point in the narrative, Jimmie would have done at least one stupid thing, and Bruce would have made at least one sexual advance on a guy. But alas, at the moment they’re no where to be found. Ok, so I lied, I know where they are, and I know what they’re doing. But it’s not important to the story, SO I’m not showing them, I prolly shouldn’t have even mentioned them. Alright, alright goddamnit, you don’t have to twist my arm, Jimmie is entertaining himself to staring out the window of that has become his room, he’s gazing at a duck pond, and drooling. Bruce is in-bed with several low-rent male hookers, why low rent you ask? Well it’s tough to find a gay dude hooker that’ll sleep with a midget, that’s why. Anyway, yet again, back to the real story at hand, Sheena and Stoney. They’re the real story, because frankly, she’s the one that’s got a match at the PPV, when Tard and Hard have a match, I’ll focus on them, ok? As you might remember from the last installment of “Search For A Sugar Daddy” Stoney was awarded a great deal of money from a lawsuit. Thus making him a rich man, however since he’s a terminally addicted pop head that still lives in the sixties it’s hard for him to you know, do anything with his wealth. In fact they found him at a local gas station that he claimed to own, however in reality he just works there. The real owners were too embarrassed to tell the old man he’d filled out an employment application, not ownership papers, but I digress. You should already know this, even though I’ve never let you in on these facts. I demand you all be psychic from now on, thus making my job much easier. “Will you please SHUT UP~!” This from Sheena, as she glares at me with hateful eyes. Perhaps I’m talking too much, as she turns back to Stoney, I once again let you hear their conversation, even though it’s not very interesting. “So you don’t like the beard man?” “NO, the beard is fine, I don’t mind. Listen Stoney, I was wondering, how much exactly ARE you worth?” “That’s kinda personal man.” “I know, but you can tell me, aren’t I your little “stick it anywhere you want monkey” GOD FUCK GOD, TOO MUCH INFORMATION~! “Yeah, you are man, but you know when dude’s watching us you prolly shouldn’t say stuff like that.” “What dude?” “You, know, THEM” He’s pointing towards me you know, and thus by extension you people. Shame, shame on you for listening in. “Oh don’t worry about them, come on honey, how much?” “Well man, I’m rich you know, I’m worth a whole fifty big ones.” “FIFTY MILLION~!” “Haha, no way man, fifty bucks. I got that and all the pot I could smoke for the rest of my life from the lawsuit.” “Fifty bucks? I wasted ALL THIS TIME FOR FIFTY BUCKS~! I LET YOU PUT IT…..ummm nevermind that. This last part she said looking over her shoulder, again at you people. Damn voyeurs. “Yeah man, ain’t it great to be rich?” “Oh, fucking Christ. JIMMIE~! BRUCE~! Come on, we’ve got to get outta here, this has been a big waste of time. He’s not rich, he prolly can’t even make rent this month~!” And now appear Bruce and Jimmie, both wiping liquid off their chins. I’ll leave it to your imaginations who’s wiping off what. Unfortunately I don’t get that luxury, and that hideous image is now burned into my retinas. God I hate you people. “Eh you sure we gotta go Boss? I was just getting started with Sancho, and Big Van Rod.” “Yes, we’ve got to go, and NOW.” “Jimmie wanna stay~! Jimmie like hearing fun noises coming outta Stoney’s bedroom at night. Jimmie like magic brownies~!” “Damnit Jimmie, those sounds aren’t gonna be around anymore, and as for the magic brownies I told you not to eat those. Now I don’t care what you want, we’re getting out of here.” Stoney looks about to protest, even though he’s completely bewildered by what’s happening. He stands only to get a shot to the nuts from Sheena, she’s such a bitch. And now she’s glaring at me, I mean, she’s a really nice person. So with that last parting low blow, Sheena herds Jimmie and Bruce out of the house, and out towards Sodomy Express, where they’ll most likely begin their search for a sugar daddy anew. God can only hope I won’t be drug along for the ride. ~To Be Continued~ View Sheena Ramone 's Biography Back
