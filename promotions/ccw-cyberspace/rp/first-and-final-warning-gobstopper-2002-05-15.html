First and Final Warning Gobstopper

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A promo in which The Supreme Machine, after an encounter with fans outside the United Center, warns Mr. Gobstopper and Paul Arovnik before the Champions of the Ring round robin.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: First and Final Warning Gobstopper Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 9:28:28 Location: United Centre.....Thursday Afternoon This is a: Match RP A shiny black limousine slides silently past the sea of people milling around the outside of The United Centre in Chicago. One by one the fans turn and stare as the limo slows to a halt out the front of the massive auditorium and a chauffeur dressed in a blue uniform jumps out of the drivers seat and races around to open the back door. The fans begin to converge on the parked limousine as a familiar figure dressed in loose blue jeans and wearing a tight black T-shirt emerges from the back. The Supreme Machine towers over the cheering fans as they rush towards him, thrusting various items out to be autographed. He stands there with the fans for several minutes signing children's autograph books, T-shirts and even a couple of huge breasts before slowly making his way towards the arena's entrance. As he pushes his way through the mob of fans they begin a familiar chant. SuMa! SuMa! SuMa! He turns as he reaches the main entrance to the arena and waves to the adoring fans. Man....YOU SUCK! The Supreme Machine turns in the direction of the voice to be confronted by two scruffy looking teenage boys dressed in dirty, ripped jeans and wearing T-shirts with "Summer of the Gobber" emblazoned across the front. Yeah man....Gobstopper is gonna kick your ass tonight! SUMMER OF THE GOBBER!!! YEAH!!!! The five time CCW Heavyweight Champion chuckles softly to himself and turns to enter the arena but stops when he is hit on the side of his neck by a stream of sputum. He turns back in the direction of the two teens. Fuckin' take that man! GOBBER! GOBBER! GOBBER! In a flash The Supreme Machine grabs the teenager that had spat at him by the throat and sends him hurtling through the air. He comes crashing down amidst the wildly cheering fans. The Supreme Machine then turns to face the other teenager but he is nowhere to be seen. SuMa! SuMa! SuMa! He gives the fans one last wave before turning and entering the arena where he sees a CCW camera crew preparing for the upcoming show. He is obviously highly agitated as he wipes the teenager's spit from his neck and approaches the camera crew. Get that damned camera working, I've got something to say. The crew hurriedly finish setting up there equipment. The camera man gives SuMa the thumbs up. Ready? The Supreme Machine finishes cleaning the spit from his neck as the cameraman gives him the thumbs up sign again. Real high quality fans you've got there Gobstopper, really top of the line. Now I hope you saw what I did to that little bastard because that's exactly what I'm going to do to you live on LDT. I'm going to swat you out of the way just like I would a bug. Champion of the Ring 2002 and how fitting that I'm facing you in the first round Gobstopper......the very same man I beat to win the very first COTR tournament four years ago. Well I hope you remember what happened when we faced off just last week. I beat you...AGAIN! And the bad news for you Gobstopper is that the result tonight is going to be exactly the same. You see, I plan on progressing past this pool and becoming COTR Champion for the second time, and sadly for you, you're standing in my way. Slowly, The Supreme Machine seems to be regaining his composure after the earlier spitting incident. I'm going to regain that CCW Heavyweight Title. I am the greatest Heavyweight Champion in the history of this company. A fact I going to prove to the world. I don't care who I have to go through to prove it. So Spider, bring in your mystery man, "PA" Paul Arovnik. Put me in the toughest pool in the whole damned tournament. I don't care. Arovnik may have been a great champion in CCW but that's only because he never had to face me to win it. Mark my words Arovnik, you'd better be on your game when you come face to face with me because if you're not, I'm going to bitch slap you in to next week. And that's a damned promise. Outside the arena, the fans are pressing up hard against the front doors trying to get a better look at The Supreme Machine as his voice begins to rise. He reaches unties his hair and shakes his mane of black hair loose before continuing. And I hope you're listening to this too, McAllister, because the same goes for you. I WILL have my title back. Neurosis knew it. I told him I was coming for him and where is he? He gave the title up and ran rather than face me like the champion he claimed to be. Well you should all take notice of that... McAllister, Arovnik, Blayze, .. all of you. Neurosis was a smart man. If any of you are smart, you'll follow his fine example and run rather than face me because I'll stop at nothing to win this tournament. I don't care who gets in my way, I'll do what ever it takes. You see that's something I've noticed since I've been back. Honor is dead here in the new CCW. Nowadays if you want something you just go out and take it at any cost, it doesn't matter who gets in your way. Well.... so be it. I can play that game too. So Gobstopper, if you think you're smart I won't be seeing you in the ring for this match. If, on the other hand, you are the idiot I think you are and you do turn up, I'm warning you now to be prepared to take the beating of your life. That little run in we had last week will be nothing compared to what happens to you if you show up tonight. Last week it was just a little bit of harmless fun, young Ricky getting square for you screwing up his title match. LDT is going to be a whole lot different. This time it's for real Gobstopper. This time there is something at stake. This time you're standing between me and my CCW Heavyweight Title. You've been warned. Show up to LDT and you're going down....HARD! I will have that title and there's nothing any of you can do about it. View Biography
