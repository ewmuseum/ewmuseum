Rules of the Road - The Heist

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comic scene in which Mark Estep and Jack Panzer, in a semi truck, are mistaken for robbers when Mark’s pistol falls out at a grocery checkout.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mark Estep Entitled: Rules of the Road-The Heist Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 10:18:58 Location: Krogers/On the Road This is a: Match RP Rules of the Road- The Heist The scene opens up with Mark Estep driving his bright red semi truck, with his tag team partner and new friend, Jack Panzer in the passanger seat. Mark is laughing about something as they continue driving down the road. Mark pulls up to a red light, and stops completely on the white line. Mark Estep-Dude, I don't care what you say... The German army sucks on Risk.... Jack Panzer:Are you sure you should be driving? Mark Estep:Yeah.. why? Jack Panzer:Because the way you're talking, I think you might be a little bit 'special'. He uses his fingers as air quotes. Mark Estep:Haha, that was a good one... like I haven't been called that before. Mark starts driving once again, after the light turns green. He cruises down the road and around a curve in the road, and gets stopped at another light. Mark Estep:Damn lights! If I was running things around here... Jack Panzer:It would be a great cesspool. Mark Estep:Yeah, that too... I saw your match on Thursday... Jack Panzer:Yeah, I was getting my ass kicked... Mark Estep:Heh, yeah you were... Jack Panzer:Shut up, smart ass.... Mark Estep:Hey, you said it first. Either way, you did pull out the win in the end. Jack Panzer:Yeah, and now im technically leading my pool. Mark Estep:Because you are the only person to have a match. Jack Panzer:Nuh Uh, Yama Tatsu had one too... Mark Estep:AGAINST YOU! The light turns green again and they continue driving. Mark pulls over into a large parking lot surrounded by nine or ten stores. Jack Panzer:What are we doing here? Mark Estep:I need some things. Jack looks at Mark as he opens his door and hops out of the large truck. Mark is wearing a large jacket to keep him from the cool temperatures of the early May morning. He walks over to the passanger's side and a car goes by, and he runs across the street after it gets passed, with Jack following closely. Mark goes into the Krogers store and grabs a buggy from a long line of them, and walks towards the isles. He steps on the black and white tiles as he goes down the first isle, and grabs a back of pasta for some spaghetti. He goes into the third isle and grabs a jar of Ragu spaghetti sauce, and puts it in the cart next to it. Jack Panzer:Having some spaghetti tonight, Mark? Mark Estep:No........ what a stupid question.... Jack Panzer:I was just making sure... Mark Estep:I actually have a date tonight... Jack Panzer:I like how you included 'actually' into that.... Mark Estep:Shut up, smart ass! Mark continues down the isles and then goes up to check out. He starts placing things on the belt thingy as it pulls the items towards the cashier. Cashier Woman:Do you have a Kroger card? Mark Estep:Yeah, hang on a minute... Mark starts to search through his pockets for his keys that he has the mini card on. He pulls things out of his pockets and sits it down on the belt. Finally, he pulls out a small hand pistol and sits it down on the belt. Cashier Woman:Don't Shoot me! Mark Estep:What!? No! Jack Panzer:Mark, what are you doing!? Cashier Woman:Take the money! Mark Estep:I'm not robbing you! The cashier opens the cash register and a bunch of bills fold out. The security officer sees this happening and he runs over to grab Mark. Mark sees him coming and he takes off through the door, and Jack follows. Mark hurries out to his truck and opens the door, hops in, and starts it up quickly. Jack's door is locked as the Security guard closes in. Mark reaches over and unlocks his door just in time for Jack to get in, shut the door, and the guard jump onto the walk rail at the bottom of the door. Mark starts up the truck as Jack locks the door, and they drive off. The security guard jumps off onto the pavement as Mark and Jack pull out of the parking lot. The security guard runs back in and calls 911. Jack Panzer:What the hell were you doing in there!?! Mark Estep: I didn't do anything! It was just a huge misunderstanding. Jack Panzer:Have you lost your mind!? Mark Estep:Yeah, didn't you read my last promo? Jack Panzer:What? Mark Estep:Nothin.. Jack Panzer:Why did you take a gun in there with you!? Mark Estep:A Trucker always carries a gun with him! I just forgot to leave it in the truck! Jack Panzer:Well, now we are convicts thanks to you... Mark Estep:Der Duh Dah I know that genious! The scene fades out.... for now!!!! View Biography
