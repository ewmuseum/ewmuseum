I Don't Even Understand Myself

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Cianan, alone in his home, vents his anger and cuts himself with a blade.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: I don't even understand myself.. Written in Saturday, Jun 8, 2002, 03:9:20 Location: Who cares.. This is a: Match RP There was a crash, as a mug hit the wall, the shattered ceramic pieces, hailing to the ground, in a blizzard of white. A primal scream, pierced the air. It doesn't matter who he beats.. it doesn't matter, who he hurts. Cianan, was unsure, and angry with himself, and with everyone else. [Why..] His hand cut through the air, and slammed into a door, driving a hole into the false wood. The fifth door in four weeks, and it certainly didn't help his mood. [I don't feel better..] A growl, rises from the pits of his stomach. [Why don't I feel better?] He slams his fists into the door, followed with a head butt, driving into the wall. And another. [No matter what I do.. I..] Slam.. [NEVER] Slam.. [FEEL BETTER!!] He thrased Alexander Miyagawa and John Jeremy, a former PCW champion. He was thrown back into the Asian Title Race in PURE. He felt, unfulfilled, he had justified a wrong, he has redeemed himself. [I still feel.. NOTHING!! Empty, like a doll.. a puppet with no one at the strings. What meaning does this life have for me? What?] He drove his foot repeatedly into the beaten wall. His face was red, with a mix of rage, and adrenaline. The veins in his neck, and temples pounded, only causing him to add pain to his list of frustrations. He could win, every match he was apart of.. He could destroy any opponent, but it didn't matter. [Why.. I have so much.. so many things... so many accomplishments.] He slowly peered over to the glinting object, reflecting from the knocked over lamp, from his earlier raging. The Independant Title, slowly Cianan's head cocked to the side, and he approached it, the storm quelled, for now. He looks over and stares at a belt. [Is that what I am? Am I just the possession's I have earned? Is this.. it? Does everything I've done in the past make up me? Does history mold what I am? WHAT AM I?!] He placed his hands on the belt, just staring, lost in the gold. His head, unbeknownst to the host, shakes, in protest, but his mind no longer realises it. The muscles in his arms twitch. [Ever since.. I haven't been the same.. I haven't felt.. right. One time, I knew what I was fighting for. I need to find something again. What will I fight for? My worthless self? No... no..] He slowly stand errect, and just starts looking around. [Who.. and what should I fight for..? If not for me? What cause, or reasons do I have, to mutilate these people?] Slowly, he turned on his heels, a sigh, breathed through his lips, as he made his way to the bathroom, and picked up his trusty blade. There was so much pressure, he was convinced he couldn't think. He couldn't make a sound judgement. [Just.. too much.. I need.. relief..] He placed his forearm over the sink, and not for the first time, nor un-doubtedly the last time, he cut the blade into his skin. The pain was sweet, and his eyes closed, as he bit his lip. The crimson pressure slowly left his veins. Slowly.. there was less of a pounding in his skull. Another sigh, as he clamped the wound closed, with his hand. Watching the crimson streams creep from behind his fingers. His burdens felt lightened, a little woozy, he walked away. And stopped, suddenly, staring at something ahead of him. [Akiko...] He stared at the picture, on the wall.. one that he had, since he first lusted for her when he came to CCW. [I.. just don't know.. who I am..] [and.. it's not fair..] His head lowers, and falls into a crosslegged sit. His eyes squeezed almost as hard as the hand on his bleeding forearm. [It's not fair... why, don't I ever know what to do..?] What mattered on SMN? Venneman? A trash talking wanna-be Knight? Jack Panzer? Cianan has REAL problems, that need REAL solutions. No more fantasy.. no more pretending. [I need my Noir....] View Biography
