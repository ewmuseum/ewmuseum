Sometimes You Have To Break The Rules

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which John Coens (formerly John Jupiter) explains his chair attack on the returned Supreme Machine.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main John Coens Entitled: Sometimes you have to break the rules ... Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 02:08:50 Location: Backstage hours after SMN This is a: Interview Interviewer: I'm backstage here just about ready to get some words from a resident legend turned monster overnight. In the span of a few short moments, that stretched out over an entire lifetime ... one man ... has changed the ENTIRE WORLD's view on him. For those of you who missed the events of the night ... let's show the clip again. - John: I'm back ... I'm Back ... I'm BACK! After a bit of reflecting on all my time spent here in CCW ... I decided to come back tonight ... and do something I never thought I'd get a chance to do in my lifetime. Something I never thought anyone would do ... but just last week ... one of my dreams from my early days in CCW has become possible. So without sugar-coating it anymore ... without making as big a deal out of it as I should, and as most people would ... I'd like to ask the one, the only Supreme Machine to come down to the ring right now ... right here ... TONIGHT ... so I can properly welcome you back to CCW with all the respect that you deserve. - Interviewer: John Jupiter calls out the returned legend of Supreme Machine so that he can give SuMa all that he deserves. Let's see what comes next. - He makes his way down and gets up onto the ring apron. He wipes his feet on the apron before stepping between the ropes and into the ring. JJ and SuMa stare at each other across the expance of the ring, before Jupiter extends his hand. SuMa takes it and they shake, causing the crowd to roar….JJ clasps SuMa’s shoulder and hugs him and the crowd gets evern louder. The two guys say something, too quiet for the mic to pick up, and separate…JJ shakes his head and wipes his eyes. John: I just... I can't believe this ... I still don't think it's real. It's ... I'm ... speechless really. Oh! Wait! I've got something for you! Vinny: Look, SuMa doesn’t know what to make of this. JJ’s got a gift for him? JJ: Wheel it out boys… - Interviewer: Without a doubt ... no one, and I do mean no one knew what was about to happen. - SuMa shuts his eyes, and JJ begins to unwrap the package. He opens up the flap on the top John: No peeking…. He reaches into the box and pulls out the present….10lbs of pure steel chair. He weilds it like a sledgehammer above SuMa’s head Vinny: No, Bro…not this… JJ: WELCOME BACK… As SuMa’s eyes open, JJ brings the chair down right between his eyes. The crowds cheers turn into the biggest boo you have ever heard. JJ begins to swagger around the ring, and the crowd gives him even more heat Chucky: Look, SuMa’s getting up. The shit’s about to hit the fan…. JJ turns and sees that the Machine is back on his feet. He then promptly slides under the bottom rope and runs as fast as he can up the ramp. He manages to yell behind him as he dives through the curtain…. John: THAT'S WHAT YOU DESERVE! GO BACK TO THE HISTORY BOOKS! - Interviewer: Now I'm going to try and get backstage to find out just what happened out there tonight, through the eyes of the only man in the world who understands what he did. - Knocking on the door, the interviewer walks in to find John Jupiter sitting in his boxers, watching replay after replay of that chairshot that he dropped on SuMa's head. If it were possible for someone to laugh louder and louder and still louder about a heinous act ... John is doing it tonight. He doesn't even notice the interviewer at the door until he stops to rewind the tape again. He glances over at the man without a glimmer of care in his eyes. - John: Beer? - The reporter waves the offer away, but shows his thanks at the idea. He asks if he can take a seat and run over a few things with John that ... just aren't adding up in anyone's books. Some things that I'm sure everyone wants to know right now. - John: Yeah ... I figured we'd have to do this anyway. So ... what I did, was take the liberty of writing down the questions I am willing to answer. All you have to do is read them out. If you've got any other questions ... we'll answer them at the end ... if there's time. Reporter: Umm ... ok. Let me have a look at this sheet first. John: Yeah ... go right ahead. - John hands the sheet to the man, and slouches down in his seat, as he cracks open another can of beer. - Reporter: These questions are ridiculous. No one wants to know what kind of car you drive, or what brand of clothing you wear! John: Oh no? I beg to differ. One girl was backstage earlier just trying to rip the clothes off my back so she could find out. And another girl wanted to see the backseat of my car. So I have no doubts that people want to know this information. Reporter: How about this then ... I ask you one of your questions ... and then I ask you one of my own. Does that sound fair? John: I'm not bound to answer any one I feel is distasteful or too personal. - The reporter agrees to that, even though John's own questions are more personal than any that he had for him. Running through the sheet to find some of the questions he will ask from John's sheet ... he picks the first one. - Reporter: Of all the men in CCW right now, who would you most like to see across from you in the ring? John: Line any of them up. Right now though ... i've half a mind to say Ricky Thunder. He's gone and picked himself an awfully poor choice for a tag team partner. And I doubt my chances of getting SuMa right now are too great. The bookers don't want to see him disappear again so quick. Reporter: Ok ... now my question. You've changed a lot over the course of 3 weeks. What do you feel is the prominent cause of this? John: I had nothing to do with the end result. CCW turned me into what you see today. No one deserves things moreso than the hard working people of CCW and the wrestling world. Who gets the limelight though? Old farts like SuMa. Ricky Thunder, who with all due respect ... should have his own spot on top of the roster ... has to cling to my grandfather's favourite wrestler ... SuMa. When even my glorious feat of becoming CCW's First Ever Universal Champion failed to even make it to television ... failed to even make an impact ... was put over less than how many hot dogs were sold that night ... I snapped. All my efforts. All my work. Unnoticed. I was just sitting on the fence. That just pushed me over the edge. Reporter: So what did attacking SuMa have to do with that? John: That's not one of my questions. Reporter: Right ... sorry. Ahhhh ... do you trim down 'there'? John: Yes. Everyday. That was my favourite question by the way. Reporter: I saw when I was at your door that you ripped off the John Jupiter label and instead, wrote over it with magic marker. Were you just pissed off? John: Playtime is over. No more fun and games. One of the things that I've come to realize ... and should have a long time ago ... is that when I fell from the netting ... John Jupiter DID die that night. I'm not that man anymore. Maybe I was just clinging ... maybe I was trying to hide ... but for now ... John Coens is my name. I don't need to pretend to be someone else anymore. Reporter: If you were to paint your room any colour ... what colour would it be? John: Right now? Probably green. I need something to keep me calm when I'm at home. Reporter: What are your immediate, and future goals for your time in CCW? John: Immediate? I'm going to have some fun. Long Term? That you'll have to wait and see. Reporter: How much can you bench now? John: About 345. Thanks for asking. Reporter: When you gave SuMa that hug, you two shared words ... what were they? John: He said 'It's been a long time.' and I replied with ... 'Yes, and everything happens for a reason.' Reporter: Are you still planning on getting back together with Savanda Carson? John: LadyCat? I haven't changed that much. She's the only thing that holds me together. If Savvy will have me ... I won't deny her. Reporter: Touching. Ok, my last question ... which ... I'm sure left a lot of us wondering ... but what did you mean when you yelled out 'Go back to the history books?' John: That's where he belongs. I've got antiques furnishing my condo that are younger than him. I've seen corpses in better shape. He is a cancer to CCW. His return is something big sure ... because everyone thought him dead ... but now that he is back ... what could he honestly do for CCW except bring it down? I'm seriously starting to think that Ricky Thunder is a hired aide. Someone to bring him his prune juice and make sure he doesn't break his hip. Reporter: That is definitely going to clear up some questions, but for now ... I will leave you to you ... solo celebration as I have more pressing matters to attend. John: Don't let the door hit your ass on the way out. - The reporter is a little startled at how quickly John switched from being somewhat outgoing and personable, to uncaring and unsympathetic. He hurries out nonetheless and soon stands before a urinal where he starts to relieve himself of the burden that he was almost ready to empty in his pants. However John talks now ... the look in his eyes ... nearly scared the piss out of the reporter. - View Biography
