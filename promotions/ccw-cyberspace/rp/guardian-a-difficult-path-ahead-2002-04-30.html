Guardian - A difficult path ahead

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Angel and Guardian reflect on Group Therapy and address Gabriel Nyte before their Lockdown match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: A difficult path ahead Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 17:40:9 Location: Unknown This is a: Match RP The Room is white Not just white But a brilliant bright white There are no windows or doors visible The room is empty with 2 exceptions A television sits in a corner It replays the events of CCW’s Group Therapy over and over The return of Shane Haze and his powerbomb on John Linsey, SuMa knocking out Ricky Thunder, Sylkk and Eric Clarke and the aftermath of their encounter, Fanatics attack on Danny Bryson, Guardians elimination at the hands of The Masked Mullet and Images of Gabriel Nyte……. Standing alone is watching these stands Angel. As the camera moves towards her she slowy turns her head to look directly into the lense. “Shall crime bring crime forever, strength aiding still the strong? Is it thy will, o Father that men shall toil for wrong? Group Therapy is over…………never before has HE seen such a display of sin. Men who choose the path of wanton destruction in their quest for riches……for stature and for greed………These are sights that will remain with me for a long time. Never before has so much harm been done in one night…………all the progress we have made here in the past few months comes crashing back to earth like the fallen angel himself………But if HE teaches us anything at all it is forgiveness…………and patience…………we must again rally around and teach the heathen ones right from wrong. But before this can start we face a difficult situation This Thursday on Lockdown the powers that claim to run the CCW have seen it necessary for 2 righteous men to face each other. Maybe they think that they can stop the inevitable? Maybe they think there is a Judas here? Whatever their reasons, they are wrong Gabriel Nyte……………we know of the work you do You have a rare soul It does not contain the darkness that fills so many others here in the CCW But in your mind………………… In your mind I see something else…….. What is it Gabriel? What troubles the mind so? Is it David Oakes? Is it Devyl? Is it the overwhelming sense of responsibility? Is it the burden of those around you? Tell us Gabriel………..tell us what ails your mind I know that you are not the greedy kind HE knows you did not ask for this But we cannot escape our duties For whatever reason our paths of destiny cross I am sure HE knows best Yet I cannot help but feel a sense of foreboding I am sure you know, that whatever happens Gabriel Guardian means no ill will There are is no Judas here in this house And I feel none from yours This Thursday will indeed be difficult for all Whatever happens we must not let who wish us harm to triumph. As long as your heart and mind remain focused those who sin shall not be victorious. And remember Gabriel whatever happens this Thursday Whoever wins, Its HIS choice not ours” View Biography
