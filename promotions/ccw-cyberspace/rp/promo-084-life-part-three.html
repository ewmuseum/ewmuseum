Life (Part Three) Dan Logan CCW Promo #84 Date: 09.03.03 Location: The Bedroom {Number 3} He turns his head and looks around. Does he really want to risk it all again? Yes it will be hard for him to get into this car and try something that he should have tried so long before...but does he really wish to miss out once again? Glancing once more at the city backdrop he smiles...'I know what I'm going to do, I'm sick of running...' Slowly he opens the car door and gets in making sure he doesn't allow to much of his vomit to touch the leather upholstery. She smiles and kisses him once more, this time though he turns his head and meets her kiss with one of his own... -:- Four Days Later -:- Dan turns his body to greet Dru into the morning only to be left looking into an empty space. The white silk bedding has been drawn back and the silken pillow is crushed between his body and the mattress. Slowly he rises the warm welcoming smell of sex still lingers about the room and Dan smiles in the knowledge of what had occurred during the past few nights, how he and Dru had got to know one another once more and forgotten the arguments of their past. Sitting upon his king-size bed gazing into the large Victorian mirror before him his grin widens into a smirk that covers his long stern scarred face. He brushes his long velvet hair back behind his ears and rises to the floor, putting on a red gown of satin material. He smiles once more and turns away from his image showing for a moment the long finger nail scratch marks from the night befores activities. Walking from the centre of his room Dan walks into the on suite bathroom and switches on the shower closing the door behind him. For the next twelve minutes he showers himself within the marble white bathroom singing to himself and laughing at his own little jokes. When finally he steps out of the shower he drapes a white towel around his waist and walks back into the bedroom and lifts the phone, dialing a phone number. 'It's me baby...' 'Yeah, where did you get off to so early this morning?' 'Nah, no worries babe. I was just wondering where you had gone you know...' 'Tell you what Dru, you gonna come back round tonight?' 'No, oh ok. I'll speak to you sometime this week then yeah. I've got stuff to get done the rest of the week' 'Yeah, I'll speak to you later then baby. Yeah, love you...bye' :::Beep::: Dan puts the phone down and sighs loudly his disappointment evident. He looks around the emerald green room and nods his head gently, looking at the clock he shakes his head. This was the beginning of something big; this is the beginning of something that should have happened a long time ago. Lifting the phone from his bed once more he dials a second number and begins to speak... 'Where are you? You said that you'd be here fifteen minutes ago' 'What do you mean you've been held up. I've got things to get done today and you not being here has ruined them already. Hurry up!' :::Beep::: Once again he drops the phone upon his bed and this time walks toward his wardrobe and picks out a suit and tie. This was to be the first time he had seen Maxx since he left the army and Dan wants to make a good impression. He lifts the black jacket from the hanger and puts it on; he combs back his hair and puts in an elastic band. Walking out of the bedroom he closes the door and locks up behind himself. Walking down the stairs of the hotel rooms, we come out at the bright reception room. He walks to the small old woman sitting at reception and smiles Dan - Have I any post for today? Woman - Let me just check... She turns her back on Dan and searches through the post Woman - Logan isn't it? Dan - Yes. Have I got any? She shakes her head indicating that he has no mail. Dan smiles at her and walks away without another word. Walking out of the hotel Dan walks straight to the large car park behind it and gets into his black Mercedes Benz and switching on his radio he closes the door and begins to drive... After a three-hour trip Dan finally pulls over outside a strip club in down town Pittsburgh. Walking toward the bouncer he whispers something into his ear and the bouncer nods his head. Walking into the bar Dan walks directly to the bar and takes a seat. A barman walks over toward him and asks for Dan's order... Dan - Nothing mate. Just let Maxx know that I'm here ok... The tall brown haired bar man nods his head and leaves Dan's side. Moments later, Maxx Vain walks up and takes a seat beside Dan and talks Maxx - It's been a long time Dan. How are you? How is Emma? Dan - Like you already knew Emma is gone and that Maxx is why the hell I'm here. I've got a skitso on my back and I'd like to know where the hell my ex wife has gone with my kid! Maxx - Ah, you know that I helped her out then. I had to Dan, she wasn't happy anymore Dan - So you let her take Chris away from me. What right do you have? Who the hell are you to have that kind of control over my life! Where the hell is she! Maxx - I said I wouldn't say anything Dan, I can't tell you now I'd be going back on my word... Dan - You either go back on your word or I'll kill you Maxx...your choice! Dan grabs hold of Maxx's neck and begins to strangle him Maxx - I...{cough...I can't...{cough}...I can't tell you Dan tightens the grip on Maxx's neck and grins maniacally Dan - Tell me now Maxx and I might just let you live. Hurry up though I change my mind quickly... Maxx - Pittsburgh...{cough}...she is living in Pittsburgh till the end of the month, then I don't know where she is going...honestly Dan - You better not be lying... With that Dan lets go of Maxx's neck and punches him twice in the stomach. As Maxx keels over Dan walks away taking out his mobile phone as he leaves the strip club... 'She's in Pittsburgh...' 'Yeah, you better find her quick yeah. I've waited around long enough as it is...' 'Right I'm out...don't phone me unless you've got him ok!' With that Dan puts the phone down and getting into his car he drives away ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
