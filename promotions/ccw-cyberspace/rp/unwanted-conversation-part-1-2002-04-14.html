Unwanted Conversation, Part 1

14 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Regina Winters confronts Mike Lubis in a Montreal club about his work.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Unwanted Conversation Part 1 Written in Sunday, Apr 14, 2002, 23:45:13 Location: Montreal This is a: Interview **In a club not too far from The Bell Centre in Montreal, Regina Winters is attempting to take advantage of the city’s nightlife. The crowd at the particular club isn’t rowdy, but they’re not boring either. There’s a lot of single and lonely people around, looking for someone to hook up with or just find someone to fool around with for the night. Regina is here for one purpose and one purpose only: finding a suitable “playmate”. Someone who will allow her to be their punishing mistress. And it’s never all that hard to find someone. Most men, she’s come to find, are kinky and perverted. And almost any one of them would willingly go with her and do her bidding for the night. She’s even had her eye on a couple of strong ones.... Men who look like they’re a bit more prideful than the rest. Oh how she loves breaking them in. She gets herself wet just thinking about it.** **After a good deal of surveillance, Regina picks her victim. He’s in his lower-to-mid twenties, good-looking, and obviously thinks of himself as a ladies man. She was really going to like breaking him in and showing him his appropriate place.... At a woman’s feet. Regina slides off her stool at the bar and slowly starts to pick and weave her way through the tables, closing in on her pick of the evening. As she slides past a small group of people sitting at a table, she stops as she notices someone familiar out of the corner of her eye. She scrutinizes him closely, and then smiles. Now she knows who this is: Mike Lubis. Christine Oakes.... Damsel’s.... wannabe boyfriend.** **Thinking quickly, Regina’s plans change. Male conquest no longer matters this evening. Getting a chance to speak to Mike Lubis, does. Smiling at her good fortune, Regina starts making her way through the tables once again, approaching Mike....** **She reaches his table and looks down at him. He has a deck of cards laid out in front of him, obviously playing Solitaire of some sort. He doesn’t even seem to notice her as she looks over his shoulder. Or if he does, he pays her no mind. Regina walks around the table to the chair opposite Mike, pulls it out and sits down. Regina smiles as Mike looks up and catches her eyes with his. And suddenly, she realizes why Christine likes him so much. He’s handsome, in a very rugged sort of way, and has the look of a very old soul. It’s the eyes, she thinks. They draw you in and don’t want you to go. But, that’s not how Regina plays things, and there’s no way in hell that she’s going to let this guy get any type of advantage over her.** Mike: I’m sorry, but.... I never invited you to sit down across from me. If you don’t mind, I’m trying to enjoy my game in peace. Regina: You don’t recognize me, do you? Mike: Of course I do. You’re Regina Winters. You work in CCW. I’m just not in the mood to be harassed by any of you wrestler-types right now. So if you would, be a dear and run along. Regina: Can’t handle being in the presence of one of the most attractive women you’ve ever laid eyes on, huh? I don’t blame you. You must be trying to stay faithful to Christine Oakes, and next to me, she looks like a pile of horseshit. **Mike sets down the pair of cards her was holding and folds his hands in front of him, giving Regina his full attention.** Mike: I do admit that you are a very beautiful woman. However, you lack qualities that would make me attracted to someone like you. Regina: **smirks** Like what? Mike: Kindness. Compassion. Heart. That’s just the beginning of the list. There’s a lot more if you’re willing to listen. Regina: **coyly** I thought you wanted me to leave, Mike. And now you want to talk to me? Which one is it, big man? Mike: What do you want, Ms. Winters? If you’re merely here to pester me, I want you gone. Regina: Actually, I wanted to give you my opinions on this whole messy relationship thing you’ve got going on with Christine. Mike: **shrugs** Whatever you got to say, say it. You know that Christine and I aren’t talking much anymore. Regina: **smiles** That’s what I find so enjoyable about the entire thing! It’s as obvious as night and day that she loves you. It’s also obvious that you have a thing for her. And her not being able to be with you is tearing her up inside. And that’s good, Mike. I really like that. Since I’m going to make little Christine my bitch by the end of the month, I’m truly enjoying your interaction.... Or lack thereof! Mike: **smiles** Or, I could just whip that smile of your face by talking to her and telling her exactly what she wants to know. Regina: You could, but you won’t. Mike: And why won’t I? You seem pretty confident of yourself, Regina. It seems to me I could make you a liar by walking over to that pay phone and giving her a call right now. Regina: You won’t because I figured out what it is that you’re not telling her. Seeing you in here clinched it. You won’t tell her because it will drive her off for good, and you’re not willing to risk that. You know that guys like yourself never find “good” women like you think Christine is. Mike: Tell me Regina, just what is it that you think I am? I’m just a normal guy, trying to find a relaxing evening out by himself, and playing some Solitaire. Regina: You’re a Point Man, Mike. As a dominatrix, and as someone who frequently inhabits this kinds of places, I see it all too clearly. You help deal in underground traffic.... Slave Trade more specifically. Much like me, you’re here tonight scouting out perspective clients. And if I’m seeing all of this right, you must work for one of the larger slavers as a top Pointer. That’s why you’re always able to travel around to the big cities. And since you’re one of the top Pointers, you can pick which city you want to be in at any given time. You just call ahead and set up your meetings with your contacts wherever you go. It’s pretty good work, I hear. You can make as much as $10,000 for yourself if you have a good week. Now, what would poor Christine say if she found out that you corral women and sell them to the top bidder? She’d probably be horrified, wouldn’t you think? **There is a long, pregnant pause as Mike Lubis and Regina Winters glare at each other across the space of the table. Mike’s hands slowly come unclasped and grab for the cards. He breaks eye contact with Regina and starts shuffling the few he now has in hand.** Mike: Let me tell you something, Ms. Winters.... It’s hard for a man to make money in this day and age. I used to be a pro wrestler, much like you are now. And then there was an accident. I lost my temper in the ring one evening and killed a man. And ever since then, I’ve always had to watch myself. I still have this temper, you see? When people fuck with me, I get really pissed off. When I see someone doing something that I don’t like, I get pissed off then as well. And when little, uppity bitches come along and start poking their noses where they don’t belong.... Well, guess what? I get pissed off then too! **Regina smirks and stands up from the table.** Regina: I think you have a lot more to be worried about than me, big man. When you see Damsel, do yourself a favor and tell her that she’s going to be my bitch from this point on. Trust me, it’s a lot safer than telling her that she’s been your bitch since the moment you two met! **Mike glares at Regina. She returns his look.** Regina: Good bye, Mike. Good luck with your life. You’re sure as hell going to need it! **And Regina smiles to herself as she walks away. That was just too perfect! Could her life get any better than it is right now? Well.... Yes. She needed two things: to steal her father’s company away from him and she needed to find a man to discipline until he worshipped her. And at least one of those could be taken care of this evening....** View Biography
