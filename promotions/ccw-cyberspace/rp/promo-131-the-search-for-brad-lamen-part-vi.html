THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART VI

THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART VI Koriann CCW Promo #131 Date: 04/14/03 Location: probably hell We last left the intrepid duo of Koriann and Bob as they were leaving the warehouse to get more supplies to dump on poor Carla, who they believe had done something to kidnap Brad Lamen, aka God. Now, we find them at a grocery store, picking out all kinds of different substances to spill on the poor girl. Bob rolls a shopping cart down the aisles as Koriann walks along beside him, tossing item after item into the rapidly filling bin. Koriann: Okay, so we got all different kinds of cooking oils, and we just finished with the dairy products... Now we need to find some fun toppings... Where do you think the ice cream section is? Bob: We passed it up two aisles ago. Koriann: No we didn't. Two aisles ago was cereal. Bob: No, that was three aisles ago. Koriann: No, three aisles ago was all that Easter junk. Bob: *sighs* That was SEVEN aisles ago. Koriann: Right! That's where it started! Easter products fill a whole four aisles! Bob: No they don't! Easter fills only two aisles! Koriann: Listen! I'm the one who knows God personally, okay? I KNOW what I'm talking about! Bob: Except for when it comes to the layout of the grocery store. Koriann: I know that too. Bob: No you don't. Koriann: Yes I do. Bob: No you don't. Koriann: Yes I do. Bob: No you- Voice: *interrupting* Excuse me... Both Koriann and Bob turn to glare at the unwelcome intruder, Koriann about to fire off a retort at whoever the jackass is who's interrupting them, but swallows back what she was about to say as she notices who it is... Her Guardian Angel which originally came to her and started this whole mess. Koriann: You... You're here!!! Thank God!!! Did you know that God's been kidnapped! Bob: *looks the angel up and down* Who the hell is this, and why are you telling him that God's been kidnapped? Angel: Look, I... Koriann: *interrupting* He's my guardian angel! He's the one that started me on this holy quest! Bob: For real? You're her guardian angel? Angel: Well, I... Koriann: *interrupting* Of course he is! What kind of stupid question is that? Bob: No more stupid than you not buying enough things to torture Carla with! Angel: Listen, I... Koriann: *interrupting* We had MORE than enough! I wasn't the idiot who had to waste all the tar and feathers at ONCE! Bob: What did you want me to do?!?! Use half the tar, then a little bit of feathers, then a little more tar and a little more feathers? Angel: Guys, I... Koriann: *interrupting* YES!!! That would've gotten our point across that much better! Bob: But actually, It wouldn't. If you keep using the same torture technique over and over again, especially a lame one like this, it loses it's effectiveness pretty quick! Koriann: Oh yeah? What the hell do you know about torture, Mr. I-drive-a-bus-to-and-from-the-airport? Bob: Apparently more than you, Ms. I'm-God's-personal-little-bitch! Koriann: That does it! You're going to Hell! As soon as I find Brad Lamen, you're... Angel: *fed up; interrupting* ENOUGH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Both Koriann and Bob fall silent as the angel stands there, fuming. Angel: I've had enough out of both of you! Now, you're both going to shut-up, follow me and do EXACTLY as I say to sort out this terrible mess you've both made. Bob: *points* It's all her fault! Everything! Send her to hell! Koriann: HEY! I was just doing what this angel-douche told me! *points* Send him to hell! Bob: You deserve it more than I do! Koriann: You're the jackass who let a woman fry to death on your bus! That's murder, buddy! You deserve it more! Bob: But you're the one who- Angel: *interrupting* YOU'RE BOTH GOING TO HELL! NOW SHUT-IT! Koriann and Bob immediately clam up. Angel: *with calm, restrained anger* All right. Now... Without saying a word.... You're both going to follow me... And we're going to set everything right. And if neither one of you lets out a single word over the next ten minutes, I'll put in a good word for you with the man upstairs. Now, with a simple nod of the head, do both of you comprehend what I just told you? Koriann and Bob both nod. Angel: Good. *sighs* Now let's fix this... cut to black... View Koriann 's Biography Back
