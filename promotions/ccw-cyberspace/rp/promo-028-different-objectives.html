Different Objectives

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Different Objectives Adam Christ CCW Promo #28 Date: 02/24/03 Location: The Hospital February 20. Shortly after Lockdown. [ The scene opens within a local hospital. Here we see Adam being wheeled through the halls on a stretcher. ] Adam Christ: HahAHaHAHHAHAAHAAAA!!! [ Adam's entire face is covered with blood. Suddenly Nick Cashew, one of CCW's talent cordinators, rushes to the side of the moving stretcher. ] Nick: Jesus, what the hell!? Adam Christ: Did you hear them!? DID YOU!?! Nick: What? Hear who? Adam Christ: The crowd! THE CROWD!! HAHAHAHAAAAHH!!!! [ The doctors immediately inject some morphine into Adam's neck. He doesn't seem to notice. ] Nick: The crowd?! Who gives a fuck about the crowd! Adam, you weren't SUPPOSED to hit Andrew with a chair! I mean, you lost the match... which is GOOD, but you weren't supposed to lose it by DQ! Adam Christ: GOD, IT FELT SO FUCKING GOOD!!! Nick: Hey, are you LISTENING to me!? [ Adam Christ grabs Nick by his sleeve. ] Adam Christ: I want more... MORE! Give me MORE!! Nick: More!? ...What the fuck!? [ Nick pulls his arm away as the doctors inject another needle into Adam, this one causing him to black out. Nick stops, watching as the stretcher enters a pair of doors into the E.R. Nick looks, somewhat, confused... ] Nick: ...What the hell is with that guy? [ The scene fades to black. ] ---- Earlier that day. During Lockdown. [ The scene shows Adam Christ and Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, battling one another in what would seem to be a one-sided contest. We don't hear any commentary but, instead, only the sound of the crowd. Somehow Adam Christ manages to get in his finishing maneuver, the Crucifix. When Adam returns to his feet, the crowd has gotten louder. Adam's head jerks up, he looking around, dumbfounded by this sound. This... noise. The sound of cheers blends with the sound of Adam's heart beating. His heart beat starts slow... but gets faster... and faster... and faster. Suddenly, Adam shoots out of the ring, grabs a chair, and slides back into the ring... only to lay out a few cracks to the fallen Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. The sound of the chair cracking against Andrew's flesh isn't heard. The sound of the bell ringing, signaling the end of the match, isn't heard. Instead, only the sound of the crowd's cheers growing louder after every swing is heard. ...A lustful look crosses Adam's face as he hits Andrew with the chair again, and again, and again. Suddenly, the sound of the cheers lower... and Adam's voice is heard in the background. ] Adam Christ: The desire for praise. This is why I am here. This is why I am home. These people gawk in admiration as one man, me, provides pain for another man, him. They give me the fuel to do so... a push. [ Suddenly, the crowd's cheers rise a bit more... as Yama Tatsu runs down the ramp, crowbar in hand. ] Adam Christ: ...What's this? [ Adam swing the chair at Yama, but misses. Yama then cracks the crowbar over Adam's head. ] Adam Christ: Pain. God, it feels so good. [ ...As Adam falls to his back, the crowd explodes. ] Adam Christ: ...Wait... what's this!? [ Yama cracks the crowbar against Adam's head more, and for every swing, the crowd gets louder. ] Adam Christ: He's just like me... he desires their praise. HE recieved THEIR praise!! [ Yama finally steps back, leaving Adam laying bloodied and beaten. Yama drops the crowbar to the mat. ] Adam Christ: ...He doesn't DESERVE their PRAISE! I DO!! HE HAS NO RIGHT TO STEAL THEM FROM ME! They were MINE! MINE! MINE! MINE! MINE! MINE!! MINE!! MINE!!! [ Where the cameras cut off during that match and went backstage, one camera stayed on. ...One camera that recored the aftermath of Adam Christ vs. Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. ...As the paramedics rush to Andrew 'Ace' Hawk and Adam Christ's aide, Adam's head tilts up. Blood rushes over his white mask, tinting it crimson. ...The blood also seeks it's way under his mask, coating his flesh with the color red. ...Adam spots the crowbar Yama dropped. ...Adam's voice is heard in the background, once more... ] Adam Christ: What's this? ...A present? [ The beaten Adam slowly reaches up, grabbing hold of the crowbar... ] Adam Christ: ...An invitation? [ Adam stares at it, before pulling it down and shoving it into the side of his jeans. The paramedics and officials quickly rush to Adam's side, speaking to him as they move him over the stretcher. Staring upward, Adam's eye is open wide. ...As he's carried out of the arena, Adam's ears fill with the echos of the crowd... cheering. ...Through all of the pain, blood, agony and humiliation... a smile crosses his face. And with that, the scene fades to black. ] ---- February 21. The Next Day. [ The scene opens above Adam Christ's hospital bed. The room is very, very bright... and the sound of Adam's heartbeat is heard faintly in the background. Adam, wearing no mask, has a bandage wrapped around his forehead. A little blood seeks through the fabric, but it looks dry. ...Adam's only eye opens. ] Wake. [ Adam blinks a little, trying to get rid of the blurry vision. A figure is seen hanging over him, looking down at him. As Adam blinks more, the figure comes more and more into shape... until it becomes clear. Nick Cashew stands over Adam, looking down at him. ...Adam begins to speak. ] Adam Christ: ...Where am I? Nick: What, you forgot? Heh... I guess getting hit in the head can do that to you. You're in the hospita--eh!? [ Adam grabs Nick by the collar, pulling him down a bit. ] Adam Christ: WHY AM I HERE?! Nick: Er--I just told you! You got injured! Your head! [ Adam releases Nick, who stands up straight and straightens his tie. ] Nick: Jesus... Adam Christ: ...Why am I still here, though? Nick: You don't listen, do you? You took a beating and got hit on the head! You blacked out like, five times! [ Adam reaches up, touching the bandage on his forehead. ] Adam Christ: It didn't hurt... Nick: Heh... I think the 24 stitches it took to seal it back up proves otherwise. Adam Christ: It doesn't hurt. Nick: Probably because you're full of medicine. You're probably numb as hell. Don't worry, when the medicine wears off, you'll feel it. Adam Christ: Good. Nick: ...Good? What, you WANT to feel it? [ Adam Christ nods. ] Nick: ...You're weird. Adam Christ: It feels good. Nick: What does...? Pain? Adam Christ: Yeah. Pain... I like pain. Nick: ...Are you some sort of masochist or something? [ Adam Christ doesn't respond with words, but with a slight shrug. ] Nick: ...Whatever. Look, you didn't listen to what I told you. I told you to let Andrew beat you, not-- Adam Christ: Andrew won. I did what you wanted. Nick: Yes he did, but by disqualification! I never told you to have him win by DQing yourself! Adam Christ: What's it matter? Didn't you hear them...? Nick: Hear who? Adam Christ: The crowd. The people. Nick: Not this, again. Look, I don't give a damn about the people! The only reason I have the slightest care about them is because whatever comes out of their wallet goes into mine. Do you understand? [ Adam nods. ] Nick: ...Good. Now get some more rest... you'll need it for next Thursday. Adam Christ: What? What's going on next Thursday? Nick: ...Well, President Sylkk booked you in a match against Andrew 'Ace' Hawk and Mr. Gobstopper for the Trans-Atlantic Title. Adam Christ: Why? Nick: I haven't the slightest clue, but this is a title no rookie has been able to achieve on his second match! President Sylkk must've saw a spark in you... or something. Adam Christ: I don't want the Trans-Atlantic Title... Nick: Well, you don't have a choice. The card's booked, so I would get your ass to the gym. [ Nick turns to walk out of the room, but stops. He turns around and steps back up to the bed... ] Nick: ...I've never asked you this, but I'm curious... what ever happened to your eye? [ Adam blinks, looking up at Nick. ] Adam Christ: ...My eye? Nick: ...Er... is that too personal of a question? Adam Christ: ... [ There's a long period of silence, but Adam finally responds... ] Adam Christ: ...I got rid of it when I was young. Nick: ...Got rid of it? ...Uh...? Adam Christ: It was causing me problems. Nick: ...Whatever. Just get some rest. [ Nick turns back around and proceeds to the door, but stops once more. ...He turns around once again... ] Nick: Hey, Adam... [ Adam looks over at him. ] Nick: Don't get yourself disqualified this time, alright? It'll get you nowhere. [ Nick smirks before exiting the room, leaving Adam sitting there. ...Adam stares forward, whispering to himself... ] Adam Christ: ...The Trans-Atlantic Title? Andrew 'Ace' Hawk and Mr. Gobstopper? ...I don't want the Trans-Atlantic title. ...I want... [ Adam notices something on a shelf next to him. It's his mask, and Yama Tatsu's crowbar. ...A smirk crosses Adam's face. ] Adam Christ: ...I want to return the favor. [ And with that, the scene fades to black. ] View Adam Christ 's Biography Back
