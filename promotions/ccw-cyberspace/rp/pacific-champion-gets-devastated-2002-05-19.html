Pacific Champion Gets Devastated

19 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Devastator meets his tag partner, drinks at a pub and cuts a ring promo ahead of his tournament match against Carnage.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devastator Entitled: Pacific Champion Gets Devastated Written in Sunday, May 19, 2002, 13:50:10 Location: C.C.W Arena This is a: Match RP C.C.W at 16:14 PM 19/5/2002 Sunday Afternoon {As the sun slowly goes down and devastator steps out of the house ooking over his shoulder, look to his right and then to his left. Making sure no one and nothing is around. The One-Devastator steps out of the house carrying his trusty black bag and walking up the road. He walks up towards the ccw arena. A few minutes later he finally arrives at his destination, the ccw arena. He is slightly moved by the sheer sight of the CCW just after Lockdown but the next event has been scheduled. Everything has happened to devastator, but he’s still standing and ready to fight again. He walks into the arena and makes his way to locker room, to find Kerrupter Devastator walks up to him shakes his hand as they talk.} Devastator: Hey there Kerrupter looks like me and you are united and ready to kick some lethal ass on the Carnage guy. This time there will be no problems now that we have a secret up our sleve. Carnage won’t know what hit him. What’s the worst that can happen? I would lose out of the tournament and get really pissed and guy on a rampage! Kerrupter: I mean we may out number him and we can show exactly what we got, but we have to try so hard and prove them why we are so good, plus we will have the fans behind us. One thing I don’t know why, no matter how hard I try… It seems like we need more members. We need a few more CCW members to make up more numbers in the Lethal Injection! Devastator: Just remember it’s just us two at the moment we may need more people, but after we absoloutly devastate that carnage guy and we show what we really have got, some guys are bound to follow us. And join with the lethal injection. Kerrupter: OK man cya later! Devastator: Laterz player {Devastator and Kerrupter give each other a high five as devatsator walks off leaving kerrupter in the locker room. Devastator makes his way over to a pub he mutters the name of the pub.} Devastator: … Lockdown Sweetcherry… {Devastator walks in and sits right at the back where he gets his order taken down, he sits there as some strangeman approaches him.} Devastator: Hey man get outa the way your blocking the sunlight, I do want to see today you know, take a short nap I’ll leave the table once I’ve finished. {Devastator looks properly at the strange figure. He smirks and laughs.} Devastator You Either get out of the way or I stand up and make an example of you, It hasn’t really been a good week. {The man moves out the way as Kerrupter turns around the corner with a huge sigh of relief, he moves up the Devastator as they begin to talk.} Kerrupter: Hey man I forgot to say, it may not have been a good week for you with your bother leaving and all, but don’t worry! You’ll make everything all right and you’ll show the CCW not to give you mix reactions but to show you why you are so good! Devastator: My brother may have gone but remember you know the humilation that happened to me at the eWa, But I’ll be back. Kerrupter: huh what happened? Devastator: O that’s right you didn’t turn up, I lost my eWa World Heavyweight Championship title belt to the nEo member C-Rock they came down and beat the living hell out of me, the fans screamed my name but it didn’t help the rest of the lethal injection couldn’t help me. But I said to the president that I am injured and I’ll have a few weeks off or actually quit the eWa. Haha when I return it’ll get them real good! So that’s why I need to beat the cock ass carnage tommorow night. I need to prove that I am much better than he thinks I am I need to prove why I am so much better than him and why I should progress in the matches to the tournament. And tommorow night I will prove to the CCW to Carnage to the fans why I am good and why I get cheered everywhere I go and that I may be a former champion but I’m still good enough to beat everyone in the CCW. I am that damn good and it’s my duty to kick some damn booty, So Carnage you want some punk come and get some. OK Kerrupter you head back to the eWa but make sure back here tommorow ready for the match, Cya Laterz. Kerrupter: Later! {As Kerrupter walks off, Devastator sips a drink as he stands up and smiles to everyone, as he prepares and gets ready for event that awaits him later on. Tommorow night he meets Carnage as just like Carnage said it’s going to be Carnage by Devastation not the other way round. A Commercial hits as soon as CCW comes back online “Points Of Authority” By Linkin Park hits as Devastator walks from behind the curtain once again the fans giving him a mixed reaction. He slides into the ring and grabs a Mic before shouting backstage.} Devastator: Now listen everyone you saw myself devastator get his ass kicked last Lockdown and half of you thought it was kinda funny I know the commentators did, but I’m going to remind you all that this time when I meet Carnage it’s going to be a different story and I hope more than half of you will cheer me when I finally win a match and kick the so called non rookie from existance and from the Champion Of The Ring Tournament tommorow night. He came out here a little while ago and shouted his damn motuh off asking for everything that I’m going to do to him. He said that I was a rookie that’s a bad mistake I’ll show him how devastating I can be and that carnage will have no chance what so ever to beat me. No One messes around with me now. Now hit my music… {Just before Devastator’s Music hits Carnage walks out with His Music blasting through the PA. He stands up with a Mic shouting and abusing Devastator infront of the fans, As the two wrestler stare each other down only 24 hours away from the event where these two superstars meet in the ring. As they stare into each other’s eyes the crowd start to finally chant their names. Dev! Dev! Carnage! Carnage! Dev! Over and Over again, as these two stare at each other as a Commercial Hits and CCW Goes of air.} View Biography
