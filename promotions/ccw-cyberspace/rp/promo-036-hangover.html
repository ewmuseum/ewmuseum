Hangover Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #36 Date: Figure it out Location: Stevens estate ' I can beat this guy, I know I can.' Monday morning, 11:57AM. Micheal Stevens reclines on an overstuffed red leather sofa. A pair of red silk pajama bottoms and a white teeshirt grace his body. Hair unwashed, and face unshaven, his eyes are suken and hallow. A burning cigarette hangs from the left corner of his mouth, and on the coffee table in front of him, sits an ashtray and a Bloody Mary. Stevens' left arm still wrapped in a cast, is resting on his forehead. 'It's just Yama Tatsu, I've beat him before, I know I can again. But what if I can't? I'd beaten Dread before to, and yet, he still pulled it out last week. I had all the help in the world, and I still couldn't put him away. What the hell is wrong with me?' The arm on his forehead moves just enough to pull the cigarette from his mouth, as he exhales a cloud of smoke, he flicks the excess ash into the ashtray. After settling the flaming butt into the notch on the tray, he lifts the Bloody Mary to his lips and takes a small sip, grimacing at the taste. 'God I hate this shit. Hangover cure my ass. FUCK! I gotta snap outta this, it's just Yama Tastu, he's nothing, he'll never be anything. Of course he did nearly break my arm, I guess that's part of it...' With these words, we hear the familiar ring of a doorbell. With a groan of pain, Stevens lifts himself off the couch, and begins the slow trudge towards the door. Before he can make it, the door flies open to reveal CCW Union Rep. Brad 'F'N' Lamen. Lamen walks into the house univinted, with a big goofy grin on his face, upon seeing Stevens standing there he extends his hand. 'Mike, good to see you, how've you been? Actually scratch that I really don't care, mind if I sit down, good I knew you wouldn't.' Lamen moves past Stevens never shaking his hand, before plopping himself down the couch and putting his feet up on the table. Stevens for his part just stands looking somewhat stunned by this odd occurance. After a moment he drains the rest of his Bloody Mary, before turning to look at Brad sitting on the couch. 'What the hell are you doing here?' 'Who? Me? I'm here on offical CCW business son. See as Union Rep, I think I've got to tell you, the front office is a little concerned with your work.' 'Concerned? But, no, I've only had one loss...' 'Well yeah son, but we, hell I think you've got bigger potential. I have to tell you man, this is a bit personal. You really let me down when you allowed Dread to pin you last week. I had high hopes, but you dropped the ball.' 'Me? I dropped the ball? You were the one that hit me with a chair!' 'Nah, I didn't do that, you must be thinking of someone else.' 'But I remember..' 'Getting hit by a chair? Getting your brains scrambled?' 'Wait I..' 'Nope, not gonna do it. Listen, I'm going to make one thing perfectly clear here. You've got potential, you CAN be a star. That is you can be a star if you listen to me, I'll teach you, mold you, make you my bitch..um er I mean protege.' 'I don't need your help.' Brad takes his feet off the couch, and stands up. His smile broadens as he looks Michael, and see's the condition he's in. 'Oh, don't you? You've got a busted arm, cause you're dumbass goofball lackey's didn't keep that pyscho Yama Tatsu away from you. You last last week to the guy with the shitty music, because you're retard slipped his leash and hit you with a chair. And you don't think you need my help? Of course you need my help Mike, cause this week you've gotta take on the guy that did that to your arm. Are you ready for something like that? I don't think so, but you could be, all you gotta do, is take my advice.' Stevens takes a moment to consider all this. he looks down at himself, at his wretched current condition, and seems to think on it. Finally after a heavy sigh he looks up at Lamen. When he speaks, his voice is weak, and small.' 'I don't need your help...' Brad doesn't skip a beat, and beings walking towards the door. He stops just infront of Stevens, and squeezes his shoulder, in a completely hetrosexual way, for those of you wondering about that kind of thing. 'Remember, this offer isn't open for ever. You say you don't need my help, fine, but I'll give you until Lockdown to think it over some more. I can make you a star.' Brad then begins to chuckle, as he pushes past Stevens, and walks out the door, closing it behind him. Stevens stands exactly where he was before, still obviously reeling from the short conversation with the Union Rep. 'I don't need any help...'
