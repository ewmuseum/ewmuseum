Revelations

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A scripted scene in which Devyl and Mr. Raymond visit an archaeologist friend to locate a stone tablet needed for a ritual tied to regaining the Women’s title.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: Revelations Written in Friday, May 10, 2002, 23:23:47 Location: Indiana This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on a nice home in a residential neighborhood. It's Saturday afternoon; a thunderstorm spearheading the Indiana sky and sending rain down upon the populace below. Inside the residence, the rain can be heard beating fitfully against the windows. The homestead's owner, Morgan Madison, busies herself in her kitchen, pouring drinks for her visitors and putting together some foodstuffs for her visitors. Once satisfied, she picks up the 'serving' tray and carries it out into the parlor.] [As she enters, she notices that the man who was introduced to her as Mr. Raymond is looking at her picture collection that she has kept up on the mantle. Victoria, her longtime friend back from the college days, is perusing the books in the bookcase. Morgan sets the tray down on the small dining table.] Morgan: 'Food's here!' [Raymond turns to her, gives her a dead smile, and quickly scoops up some crackers. Devyl gives Morgan a real smile as she turns away from the bookcase.] Devyl: 'Quite the set-up you have here. I know that you're an archeologist, Morgan, but I think that you need to submit all materials you uncover to your sponsor. From what I can see, you have several tomes here which definitely count as museum material.' [Morgan smiles as she hands Devyl a glass of wine and shrugs.] Morgan: 'According to the sponsors, those books were never found. It's not my fault that they're too trusting of me. But enough of all that. How are you doing!? I was so surprised when you called me out of the blue and said you were in town! I didn't even know you had my number.' Devyl: 'It wasn't hard to get. I called the University, and they told me were I could reach you.' Morgan: *smiles* 'I see. How's the wrestling business treating you? Last time I checked, you were the champion!' Devyl: 'I was... But I recently lost the title.' Morgan: 'Oops! Sorry to hear that. Must've been some pretty bad luck.' Devyl: 'I was cheated, Morgan. There was interference in the match that allowed my opponent to briefly gain the upper hand. And after that, an illegal referee counted me out. I never should have lost it.' Raymond: *angry* 'No, she shouldn't have.' [Raymond glares at Devyl, a small bit of tension building in the room. Morgan can sense it.] Morgan: 'Uh...' Devyl: *looking Raymond straight in the eyes* 'Don't worry, Morgan. Mr. Raymond here is just upset. He was supposed to help me retain the title, no matter the cost, and in turn I would perform a service for him that he so desperately requires. I lost, and so he must wait until I get the title back.' Morgan: 'Okaaaayyyy... I'll just pretend to understand what you're talking about.' Devyl: *looking away from Raymond and back at Morgan* 'I didn't just come here so that we could check up on each other, Morgan. I came here to discuss something else entirely.' Morgan: 'Okay. What's up?' Devyl: 'I want to talk about your most recent expedition...' Morgan: 'The one in Mexico? What about it?' Devyl: 'You found a stone tablet down there. It was incredibly ancient and written in a dialect that you couldn't decipher.' Morgan: 'Well... Yes, but... How did you know about that? We didn't tell anybody! Not the press... Or the museum!' Devyl: 'I was told that you had found it, Morgan... But that really doesn't matter right now...' Morgan: 'Yes, it does! That was supposed to be a secret! If one of my crew is leaking information...' Devyl: *interrupting* 'Nobody is leaking anything. Now, I need to know what you did with it.' Morgan: 'Why? How could this possibly be important to you?' Devyl: *short pause as Devyl looks into Morgan's eyes* 'You know me, Morgan. You know that I am well-versed in the dark arts. I have spoke with Satan himself and have looked upon the fiery furnaces of hell. And you also know that everything I do in my life has a purpose... A Grand Scheme if you will. Now, I need to know the whereabouts of this tablet because it fits into those plans.' Morgan: *confused* Wait... I know most of this, but... How does wrestling fit into your plans? I thought you were just doing it to make money. Devyl: 'If this were anyone other than you, Morgan, I would smite them for their insolence. I am following a prophecy of sorts... One that I stumbled upon last year after recovering one of my father's old notebooks. My father wrote of a ceremony which would, if performed together with all the appropriate pieces in place, would make the caster ‘like that of a God'. I what I read is true, and if I have even the slightest chance to perform this ceremony, I will. The prophecy in the notebook describes the means by which to go about performing this ritual. However, the notebook version that I have is incomplete. The true version...' Morgan: *realizing and interrupting* 'The true version must be on that tablet!' Devyl: *smiles* 'Precisely! CCW has been giving me the money necessary to continue living, true, but there is something far more important that I need there. The prophecy speaks of ‘A woman above all women who holds dominance over those beneath her'. It states that only that person can survive the rigorous trials that the ceremony dictates. The prophecy also states that one who does not hold dominance at the time of the ceremony will most certainly perish. That's why I NEED that title belt back! I held it for so long, but it was all for naught! I didn't have the means by which to properly carry out the ceremony. That's why I need to see this tablet of yours. I can decipher it, and then set everything in motion.' Morgan: *short pause* 'I'm assuming you're going to have a chance at getting your title back soon?' Devyl: 'Yes. This Monday.' Morgan: *short pause, considering* 'Since I do know you so well, Victoria, I know you as the type of person who would just as soon destroy your friends if they no longer mattered to you. And supposing you do manage to perform this ceremony, I can see you turning on everyone. So... Here's the deal... A verbal contract... I help you with this, you cut me in on whatever rewards you may find.' Devyl: *smiles* 'Deal.' Morgan: 'Okay then... This damn thing better be worth it. Two of the expedition members died trying to get that thing back to the surface.' Devyl: 'So... Where is it now?' Morgan: 'Not far from here. Come with me... I'll talk you to it.' [Morgan walks to the front door and grabs her coat. Devyl looks at Mr. Raymond, and the two of them follow her out of the house.] Devyl: *voice over* 'Everything is coming together rather nicely, now. Soon, I will have the full prophecy translated from its native form! Soon, I will have the CCW Women's title belt back in my possession! And Monday night... Angelica Lynn and Deborah Carmichael... You are going to be facing an opponent that cannot afford to lose. You will both pay for your transgressions against me... Angelica, for allowing Lacey Skye to get past you several months ago when you could've stopped her momentum with a simple victory... And Carmichael, for your unwarranted attacks against my person, and your arrogant assumption that you can beat me inside the ring. The darkness is coming, 'ladies'... And you can't stop it...' [fade out...] View Biography
