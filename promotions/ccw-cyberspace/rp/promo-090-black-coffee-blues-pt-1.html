Black Coffee Blues pt 1 Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #90 Date: 3-17-03 Location: Can't ya figure it out from the title? What is it? This desperate need the dwells inside each of us, the need to for basic human companionship. No matter how hard we try to deny it, no matter how hard we work against the system. No matter many nights we spend alone, swearing off humanity as a lost cause, it still returns to us, the need, the hunger. It’s said that no man is an island, unto himself, and this of course is largely true. For it’s nearly impossible for all but the most truly special among us to go for extended periods of time without human contact, deny it all you like, but the fact remains, you, I, and everyone else, needs to be in contact with someone, anyone. Of course, having said this, we cannot deny that there are occasions, in which it’s preferable to be alone. Just as we all sometimes need companionship, we also at times need to be left to our own devises, separated from the world, so that we may contemplate our lives, our actions, perhaps our very existence. However, this is not one of those times. No, this is moment definitely falls into the former category, at least for one Michael “Spitfire” Stevens. Having spent much of the last several weeks alone, he is now driven out, into the world. He’ll mingle with the animals, the unwashed masses of humanity that more often than not, he despises. He sees the people, as beneath him, their petty and miserable lives are nothing compared to his glory, his majesty. Stevens considers himself to be a king among men, untouchable, undeniably perfect. The greatest achievement in human evolution, mind and body in total synch, constantly working together to create greatness, at least that’s what he tells himself. But in times like these, with his voice would echo in the cavernous halls of the Stevens estate, when the whisper in the wind has become mocking laughter, he must forget his bias, and reach out to humanity. Michael Stevens is not a king among men, he’s not a god, and he’s most definitely not perfect. He’s just a man well versed in the art to lying to himself and others, his lies so carefully crafted that most of the time, even he believes them. He’s a victim of his own big lie, a lie so large, and told so often, that for him, it has become fact. But on a night like tonight, the cracks in his big lie show through, and he’s forced to face reality. The scent of strong freshly brewed coffee fills his nose, making him think back to better times. Time when he’d be a young man, in high school, spending long hours with friends in a place very similar to this one, and all night dive, catering to the drunks and night owls. Those had been good times for Michael Stevens, he was the center of the universe, surrounded by those that loved, and sometimes even feared him. Everything about him bespoke power in those days, his money, his movements, his voice, his words, everything worked together to create the perfect image of a future master of the universe. But like all good times, those came to an end, and Stevens was forced to face the real world, but that’s a story for another day. Sufficed to say that now, the scent of coffee has taken him back to a place in his mind, where he was happy, content. Lifting the steaming cup in front of him to his lips, he takes a small sip of the bitter black liquid. For a moment he flinches, the coffee obviously much too strong for his likely. However he cuts it with neither cream nor sugar. This is a night of memories, a night for black coffee and cigarettes. “Good times, good times.” ~To Be Continued~ View Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens 's Biography Back
