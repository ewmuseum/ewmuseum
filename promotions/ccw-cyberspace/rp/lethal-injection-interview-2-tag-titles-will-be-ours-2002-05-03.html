What this is An Interview-type RP in which the new tag team Devastator and Kerrupter, the Lethal Injection, make their CCW debut and give their stats.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lethal Injection Entitled: Interview 2 ... Tag Titles Will Be Ours! Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 10:50:00 Location: America This is a: Interview ~[The new pair known as Devastator and kerrupter the lethal injection hasn’t been here for that long infact 1-2 hours, But there more determined then ever they seem to be concentrating on the tag titles no single titles at this moment of time. No one seems to care that the new pair is probably capible of smashing any tag team in the business today. Devastator and kerrupter finally walk out of the locker room only to see that the place was empty. They were expecing something, but it was clear no films or photos just a plain red carpet. They made their way around checking out the anormous C.C.W. They walk around and pass several different shops and wrestles. The Current tag Team Champions walked by without a care in the world not even glancing at the new arrivals and possibly the new champions after a short while. They suddenly find themselves standing next to the entrace curtain.]~ ~[Smoke pours on to the ramp as a Remix of both RVD’s theme and One Step Closer hits as the crowds go wild. The Tag Team the lethal Injection walks out throwing their arms in the air as the crowd chant and scream their names. The Tag team runs down to the ring and slide in and then juming on to the turnbuckles throwing their arms in the air once again. The music finishes as devastator slides out of the ring grabs two Mics of the annoucers table and begins to talk to the crowd.]~ Devastator: So how’z everyone today then? … Now it may have come your attention all those loyal fans that have come with us to this new federation that we have left the cwf and ehw because we found a better federation. Not only is this federation better, bigger and more supreme then the others but it’s actually got a few competition maybe not outside the ring but defiently in it. Now we were going to go up to the Staff/Management and ask them about the next card and see if we can get a tag title match or at least a match to prove how good we really are. But it doesn’t matter were here to have a few interviews to get more fans and get some kind of good reputation around here. But that may not happen for a while but it really doesn’t matter because were not going anywhere and we hope that you fans aren’t going anywhere either. Kerrupter: Now my friend here has said everything that you need to know and most probably all you want to know. We are that damn good and we are not going to loose or get hurt in the middle of the ring because we are that damn good. Everything we do just remember we can do better. So all you wrestlers backstage treat us right and we won’t kick your ass at the next card because we are that damn GOOD!!! Devastator: And that’s because… “It’s Our Duty To Kick Some Booty You Want Some Punks Come Get Some…” Now hit out music. ~[Devastator and Kerrupter leave with One Step Closer blasting out of the PA as the two walk up the ramp leaving the ring and Mics behind. The soon to be tag Champs. Will be back and it get the reputation and respect not only they deserve but what the want and need to survive in the CCW. What will happen is unknown but we sent a Interviewer to ask them bout themselves. In their locker room about 30 minutes ago. The scene opens on the Titantron.]~ Interviewer:Could you please tell me about yourselves, you first Kerrupter. Kerrupter: I’m 6’4 I weigh 245 lbs I’m the former Extreme and hardcore champion at the eWa and 6 times tag champ with my pal Devatsator here. I have no wife, no family a massive mortgage and I can have a really bad attitude to life… Well sometimes. Devastator: Same here but I’m 6’2 and I weight 235 lbs But I’m the eWa Champion and the former United States Champion. There will be no title, which we don’t hold in this federation between us, because this is our generation! ~[The tag team walk off grabs there bags and walk out of the locker room.]~ Visit [detail withheld] to see Interview 2 in designed format. *I’m also just testing the HTML on the boards so please excuse us if it’s not too Good* View Biography
