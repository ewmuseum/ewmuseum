Road To The PPV, Part 2

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which The Supreme Machine and Ricky Thunder spar, followed by a promo aimed at Neurosis.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Road to the PPV....Pt2 Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 14:50:00 Location: Private Training Facility This is a: Match RP The scene opens in a dimly lit, long corridor. As the camera pans from side to side revealing cabinets filled with trophies and memorabilia lining the walls, the sounds of what appears to be an intense sparring session echo from somewhere in the distance. The camera moves slowly along the corridor, still panning from side to side, showing the endless cabinets full of trophies. After what seems to be an eternity, the camera finally comes upon an open door and enters, revealing the private training facility of The Supreme Machine. The walls around this large room are covered with more photographs and trophies commemorating The Supreme Machine's illustrious career. One side of the room is devoted to a myriad of weights and other training equipment while a wrestling ring dominates the remaining floor space. It is inside this ring that the camera finds The Supreme Machine in a sparring session with his tag-team partner, Ricky Thunder. As the pair trade blows, The Supreme Machine is in noticeable pain even though he is wearing a black arm brace over his injured forearm. The two partners pause as the camera zooms in. Man, are you sure you still want to go ahead with your matches this Sunday? You're favoring that arm quite a lot. If anyone gets ahold of it, you're gonna be fucked. The Supreme Machine stands in one of the four corners adjusting his new brace. Don't you worry about me Rick, I can look after myself. I'll be fine come Sunday. Ricky Thunder just shrugs and smiles. If you say so man. The gigantic five time heavyweight champion just nods and stares back at his partner. Yeah.... I say so. Let's get back to it. The Supreme Machine effortlessly springs across the ring, bringing his 6'10", 310lb frame crashing down on Ricky Thunder. The two grapple on the mat for a few seconds before Ricky breaks free and leaps to his feet. You're gonna have to do better than that at Group Therapy if you plan on winning anything. Man, come at me in the King of Extreme finals like that and I'll fucking kill you, I swear. I've told you not to go easy on me so don't. Fucking spar like you mean it. I can tell you're not trying. The Supreme Machine winces as he again adjusts the arm brace. I'm not going easy on you. I'm just getting used to wearing this stupid brace. Well you have to wear it, the doctor said. So get used to it. COME ON!! Ricky runs at The Supreme Machine. He sends the giant crashing to the canvas via a vicious swinging DDT. FUCK!! That was too fucking easy. Why aren't you trying? The Supreme Machine gets slowly to his feet. It's the arm. BULLSHIT!! Look you've just gotta get over this promise you made to my brother. You made a fucking promise to me too, don't forget. You promised you'd never lay down for me. I'm the one that's still fucking alive SuMa, I expect you to keep your word. Thunder's gone. Move on. The Supreme Machine just moves back into the corner of the ring. He says nothing as Ricky continues. Look.... FUCK THIS! I'm gonna go and find myself a REAL sparring partner. One that will actually give me at least a bit of a workout. Man, I'm warning you. Pull this shit on Sunday night and I'm gonna kill you. When we face off in the King of Extreme final it's every man for himself... The Supreme Team goes on the backburner for one night. Even if I have to face you in the first match of the night.....IT'S BUSINESS!!! I'm out there to win just like you are.. Don't fucking forget it! Ricky Thunder jumps out of the ring and grabs his sports bag. He reaches in, grabs his towel and wipes the small amount of sweat from his brow. Sparring my ass! He throws the towel back into the bag. I've hardly even broken a sweat. You better think long and hard about this PPV, SuMa. I'm not gonna be holding back. I suggest you wake up to yourself and do the same. He turns and storms out of the gym. The Supreme Machine watches silently as he goes. After Ricky's footsteps disappear into the distance, The Supreme Machine springs over the top rope and walks over to large punching bag that hangs from the ceiling. The camera follows him and zooms as he begins pounding his injured forearm time and time again into the bag. Every blow makes the huge man wince in pain but he continues regardless. Pain is the mind killer. Must....conquer....the.....PAIN! With a super-human effort he delivers a smashing blow with his injured forearm that sends the bag flying from it's mooring in the ceiling. It sails through the air and crashes against the far wall with a deep, resounding thud. He turns and looks directly at the camera. I have something to say. I want everyone in CCW to hear this. He winces slightly as he again adjusts the brace on his damaged arm. Since I've been back here it's become apparent that some of you think I'll be an easy mark for you... That you'll make a name for yourselves by taking out the CCW Hall of Famer. Well that's not going to happen. I just won't let it. He moves over to the free weights and begins doing arm curls with his injured arm. John Coens, you thought you'd be a smart ass and blindside me. Well, like I said....you blew it...I got straight up. Carnage...ExMa....It doesn't matter what you call yourself because the fact remains the same...You too tried a cheap attack and look what happened to you! The Supreme Machine smiles at the thought and leans into the camera. His eyes widen with pleasure. I fucked you up! He puts down the weights, grabs a bottle of PowerAde and takes a sip. He wipes his mouth with a massive hand and turns back to the camera. As I proved on LDT, you fools can hit me with chairs all day if you want... I don't care. Because no matter what you do to me, I'm going to get up EVERY time. That's what separates me from most of the rest of you. I can fight through the pain. Hell, I doubt most of you even know the true meaning of the word. You see, there is more than one type of pain. There's physical pain. Sure it hurts for a while but the bruises eventually heal...you recover. But then there's psychological pain. The pain that eats away at you. The pain of knowing that even though you gave it your best shot, you aren't up to my level. The pain of realizing when you're laying on the mat and watching me flying through the air towards you, that you are nothing. That's the pain that I'm going to be teaching to anyone that crosses my path this Sunday night. He resumes his arm curls. And when all is said and done, it's gonna be you Neurosis, that learns the hardest lesson at Group Therapy. It's going to be you who has to learn to live with the fact that even after I've cracked a bone in my forearm....even after I've had to fight through most of the damn CCW roster to get to you....even after I've had to fight in the King of Extreme final....I'm still going to destroy you!!!! That's right Neurosis. Sunday night I'm coming to claim what is rightfully mine. I gave you the chance of handing back MY CCW Heavyweight Title belt but you've foolishly decided to ignore my advice. I warned you that I would come after you....now I'm coming....and you're not going to stop me. The Supreme Machine begins to smile as he continues pushing his injured arm. My last but of advice for you Neurosis is to make sure you clean your grubby fingerprints off my belt. Do it yourself, get someone else to do it....I don't care....but I'm warning you... When I'm standing in the centre of the ring after defeating you at Group Therapy, I don't want to look down and see any of your goddamn dirty smudges on MY belt. I want your filth cleaned off it before I put it back around my waist....WHERE IT BELONGS!!!!! He stops his workout and turns to face the camera once more. It slowly begins to zoom in. And rest assured all of you...After the main event it will be me standing there as the SIX time CCW Heavyweight Champion. I won't be stopped by anyone, I don't care who gets in my way.... John Coens, Carnage, even you Ricky Thunder. I'll tear you all to pieces to get to that main event....and do you know why? The camera zooms totally in on The Supreme Machine's face. His steely grey eyes fill the screen. BECAUSE I CAN! View Biography
