Written in Thursday, Apr 25, 2002, 08:23:41 Location: NY This is a: Interview Inside the Cathedral Of Incarnation, Cathedral Av, Garden City, NY Despite the wall to wall stained glass windows, the place is in darkness. A strike of a match A single candle bursts into life, chasing the shadows further into the darkness. Just about encompassed by the light stand Angel & Guardian. In his hands, Guardian holds the dripping candle, oblivious to the beads of wax running over his hands. A band-aid covering a small scar on his forehead. The gold of the Hardcore Championship Belt glimmering in the light, dulled only slightly by the blood still splattered across its face. Blood that came from the very scar Guardian is concealing behind the band-aid. Angel: “Our journey brings us here to the state of New York……site of Group Therapy this Sunday and the Nassau Coliseum in Long Island…site of this weeks LDT……………..Long Island is also the home of David Berkowitz………………a man who’s evil shocked the world 26 years ago……… a man who once said 'I am the Monster -- Beelzebub -- the chubby behemoth”………………….The man known simply as the Son Of Sam…………………But like David Berkowitz…………..Ricky Coyote, sooner or later your sins come back to haunt you……………sooner or later you are made to repent your actions………….sooner or later you have to answer to HIM……….This Sunday Ricky……..at Group Therapy once again you and Guardian stand on opposite sides………the only difference this time is that you will have 4 others with you and so will Guardian…………….however that is the only difference………the outcome will be the same…………for HE will take his wrath and vengeance out on those who defy his every word………..those people………people like you Ricky who consider greed to be a normal attribute in a man……………..for you see…..we know it is not Guardian you crave…….not the competition you seek……..no…….what you and so many other greedy souls here in the CCW want………what you all crave for……….strive for at all costs…………ignoring the pain and suffering you inflict on the innocent……..what drives you on in your quest for more pain and suffering is gold……………gold like the belt carried by Guardian……….you will do anything you can to get it……to hold it………..to caress it……………but it consumes you………..your desire to have it chances you……………….it is the original sin………it is TEMPTATION…………you want what you cannot have……seek what you cannot find………have you never heard that patience is a virtue? Tonight you face another man on a mission………..a man who speaks familiar words……….Gabriel Nyte…………Ricky…….if you are lucky enough to survive what he is capable of and somehow make it to Group Therapy this Sunday………only then can you embark on the road in which you wish to tread………..but….. Be careful what you wish for Ricky………..because as the saying goes………….sometimes what you wish for comes true……….but if you really want the chance to take this belt here………….keep wishing………..and maybe………your wish will come true………..and if it does………..then may HE have mercy upon your soul Ricky………because we will NOT. Remember …………watch yourself Ricky………..HE moves in very mysterious ways………… With that the candle flame flickers slightly and extinguishes itself, once again plunging the room into darkness.
