Koriann - Whatever Happened To Devin Belly?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Koriann visits former CCW reporter Devin Belly at his home and discusses her upcoming tag match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Koriann Entitled: Whatever Happened To Devin Belly? Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 20:15:29 Location: Devin Belly's House This is a: Match RP [The scene opens up on the front porch of a well-to-do looking house. It is a bright and sunny day and everything seems nice and peaceful... That is until Koriann makes an appearance. She marches right up onto the porch, heads right for the door, and bangs on it rather loudly. She taps her foot as she impatiently waits. Getting no answer she pounds loudly once again, then turns away from the door to check out the neighborhood.] Koriann: 'Well... This doesn't look like too bad a place to live... Except for that Charles Manson look-alike in the bloody clown suit mowing the lawn across the street. But other than that, this looks like a splendid place to live! God forbid anybody disturb the peaceful refuge this neighborhood has to offer...' [And Koriann quickly turns back to the door and pounds on it again.] Koriann: 'I KNOW YOU'RE IN THERE!!! GET YOUR ASS IN GEAR AND OPEN THE DAMN DOOR!!! YOU DON'T WANT ME TO USE MY THUMP!!!' [The door bolt can be heard sliding back from the lock and the door swings open. Standing there is a very grizzled and unshaven Devin Belly who was once one of CCW's fledgling reporters...] Belly: 'Koriann!?' Koriann: 'Where the hell have you been, Belly!? Whenever I arrive at arenas across the country, there's nobody there to greet me, ask me questions, and try to grope me! Why haven't you been there?!?!' Belly: 'Uh... I was fired.' Koriann: 'Fired!?' Belly: 'Yeah... I thought you knew...' Koriann: 'Hell no! How did you get fired?' Belly: 'Well, it kinda had to do with that one deal we made... You remember... I help you win and you would have sex with me...' Koriann: 'Oh, right... That happened somewhere in Europe during our tour there, right?' Belly: 'Uh, right. But there's some kind of clause in our contracts that us announcers aren't supposed to have... relations... with the talent. And since you made our little escapade public, they fired me.' Koriann: 'Well that's not fair! C'mon, Belly... Get yourself dressed! We're going to go get you your job back!' Belly: 'Uh... Do we have to? CCW gave me a great severance package and I've really been enjoying my months off...' Koriann: 'You know what I think? I think you were fired, not because of me, but because you can't stop talking about yourself! Now, forget about getting dressed and grab yourself a microphone. You're first official duty is to interview me about my upcoming match.' Belly: 'I don't have any microphones here...' Koriann: *sighs* 'Your worthless, Belly!' [Belly disappears briefly back inside his house and comes back with a melting ice cream sandwich.] Belly: 'Here! We can pretend this is a microphone!' Koriann: *looks at Belly, looks at the sandwich, looks back up at Belly* Have you completely lost your dignity? Belly: 'Well, not completely. Only most of it. At least I haven't been seen naked by all the CCW fans...' [And right on cue, a strong gust of wind comes rushing in, blowing open Belly's robe to reveal his naked form underneath (appropriately blurred for television of course). Koriann just looks at him as he fumbles to re-close his robe.] Belly: 'I KNEW I should've put on underwear this morning!' Koriann: *rolls her eyes* 'ANYWAY... About my match... I'm teaming with some pirate chick that I've never heard of before to take on the talentless wonders.' Belly: 'CCW has a tag team called the ‘talentless wonders'?' Koriann: 'Yes! Damsel and Angelica Lynn comprise this worthless team. I beat the holy shit out of Angelica Lynn at Group Therapy, and Damsel seems to be having a young-life crisis. I couldn't ask for a more miserable team to beat on!' Belly: 'You mentioned something about a pirate chick?' Koriann: 'Yeah. I think she thinks that she's a real pirate. But the rest of us know better. In fact, the whole world knows that pirates, dinosaurs and the Terminator 1000 were only myths made up by people long ago to scare little children.' Belly: 'Um... Actually... Pirates and dinosaurs...' Koriann: 'Shut-up Belly! I'm not asking for your opinion! All you need to know is that me and the mythical pirate lady are going to run all over Damsel and Angelica. And do you know why?' Belly: 'Because you're... Good?' Koriann: 'No! It's because I RULE!!!' Belly: 'Oh... Right...' *short pause* 'Can I grope you now?' Koriann: *disgusted* 'Eww! No!' [Devin hangs his head as Koriann keeps giving him her 'just who the hell do you think you are' look.] [fade out...] View Biography
