Watch Out

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which The Supreme Machine brutalizes his giant partner Chang and a sparring partner with a barbed-wire bat.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Chang The Mongolian Giant Entitled: Watch Out Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 16:30:54 Location: Private Training Facility This is a: Match RP AAAARARRRRGGGGHHHHHHH!!!! The huge Mongolian Giant roars in anger as he is struck hard by a baseball bat wrapped in barbed wire. Small beads of blood become rivers as they begin to flow down Chang's already battle-scarred forehead. His dumb eyes seem to glow red as another blow swings down toward him. This time he grabs hold of the bat with one of his huge hands. He seems impervious to the pain of the wire digging into his palm as he effortlessly flicks the person holding the other end of the bat against a nearby wall. He now wields the lethal weapon as he moves in on the stunned opponent, his one sharpened tooth reflecting the fluorescent light from above. He raises the bat to strike. STOP CHANG! THAT'S ENOUGH! The Mongolian Giant pays no attention to his master's orders and brings the bat smashing down on the unconscious figure laying in a heap on the floor. GGGRRRRRAAAAAAWWWWWWLLLLLLLL STOP!!! The Supreme Machine leaps toward his Mongolian pet and grabs the bat from the frenzied Giant. Chang turns and looks menacingly at him as the badly wounded sparring partner lays moaning in a pool of blood on the ground. GGGGGGGGRRRRRRRRRRRR Chang is suddenly rocked by a sickening blow from his master. Don't even think about it! Next time I give you an order you will obey. The Supreme Machine again brings the barbed wire bat down on Chang's already badly bleeding head. The stunned giant slinks away from his furious master and squats in a corner, touching his forehead and looking at the blood. He begins to whimper. CUT IT OUT!!! What's happened to you Chang? Once everyone in CCW feared you. Now look at you! Beaten at LDT by two men who together weighed less than you. You disgust me! I need to toughen you up before I even consider bringing you out for the PPV this Sunday. The Supreme Machine adjusts the arm brace he has been forced to wear since his match with Bojangles the Clown at the last LDT, where he suffered a cracked bone in his arm from continual chair shots. I'm not bringing you down to fight on my team if I'm going to have to carry you! In the blink of an eye The Supreme Machine leaps towards Chang and again delivers a sickening blow with the bat, this time across the back of the giant's shoulders. The barbed wire digs in to the Mongolians flesh and tears great chunks of skin and meat away with it as it is again swung hard at him. This time Chang moves out of the way. AAAAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEEE In a flash, Chang the Mongolian Giant leaps at his master, but The Supreme Machine steps out of the way and hits him again with the bat. Ahhhhh that's better my pet, but not good enough. GGGGRRRRRRRROOOOOOWWWWWWLLLLLLLLL This time Chang gets a hold on The Supreme Machine. He takes the bat out of his hands and swings with great force at his master's head. He misses.......just Good. Good. Now you're getting the idea. EEEERRRRRRRRRKKKKKK The Supreme Machine and Chang both turn to look at the injured man now struggling to stand against the wall where Chang threw him. His head is bloody and he can hardly get up. The two wrestlers slowly walk over to him. H-hey! Y-y-you never said anything about getting hit with barbed wire! I-I only agreed to spar with him...not get splattered like this. The Supreme Machine helps the injured man to his feet. You'll be compensated more than adequately for your efforts. Don't worry. Chang looks on with what is almost a smile on his dumb face as The Supreme Machine escorts the injured man towards a door. Man, I never booked on for this shit. Just pay me and I'm out of here. That thing is crazy! The Mongolian Giant picks up the barbed wire bat and begins gnawing on it as The Supreme Machine pays the man and closes the door behind him. His complaints can be heard continuing as he walks away. Now why couldn't you have done that to your opponents on Thursday night? Well? The Supreme Machine shakes his head as he reaches over to a nearby bench and grabs Chang's collar and thick, shiny, chain leash. He continues to talk to his giant as he does the collar up around Chang's massive, bloodied neck. I need you at your devastating best at Group Therapy, Chang. Everyone in CCW will be out there gunning for us and I can't have you not performing again like you did at LDT. Another performance like that and I'll have to really punish you. Do you understand? The Mongolian Giant just slinks away a little and begins moaning softly to himself. The Supreme Machine pulls hard on his chain, causing Chang to look up at him I'm warning you! Don't let me down like you did at LDT! He pulls Chang to his feet and begins to lead him towards the closed door. Now, I've been over and over the plan with you so many times even you must understand what I expect of you. I will not tolerate any more failure from you, this is too important. AAAAAARRRRRRGGGGGGGHHHHHHH That's right. AAAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEE The Supreme Machine opens the door and looks at Chang. I'll forgive you for your loss this week. Just don't let me down again! The Mongolian Giant begins purring as he is lead out of the training room and the door slams behind him View Biography
