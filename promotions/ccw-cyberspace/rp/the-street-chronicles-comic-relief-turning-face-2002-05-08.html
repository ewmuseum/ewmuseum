The Street Chronicles (Comic Relief / Turning Face)

8 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comic street-interview promo in which Shane Haze and Juan visit a fast-food counter, a cab and a playground while taunting upcoming opponents Josef Pires, Roni Savar and Tristan Matthew.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Shane Haze (today only w/ Juan) Entitled: The Street Chronicles (Comic Relief / Turning Face) Written in Wednesday, May 8, 2002, 15:49:57 Location: The Streets This is a: Match RP The lights in the arena dim to almost nothing as small lights dance across the floor. The fans are confused as to what is going on when static starts to emerge on the CCWTron. The static slowly emerges into a picture. A picture of a camera that is slowly approaching a locker room door when the door opens tthere is a note on the desk that reads Out To Lunch - Shane A scoff is heard in the background and small whispers are heard as the people are very confused Cameraman: he said he would be here by now... Bob we better go out on the street and look for him! Bob: SHOTGUN!!! The interviewer bob and the camera crew walk out of the door and down the hallways as other CCW superstars are seen, walking around after a great PPV show. Many people were excited and dissapointed after the SMN but shit happens! The crew reaches the van that they are using and as requested, Bob hops into the front seat. The cameraman is in the back and a maintanence guy starts driving. The maintanence guy cant talk...he just listens... Anyway, the crew begins to drive into the town area. There are many interesting people walking around Cowns, Buffooons, Street Venders, Hobos and OH LOOK! SHANE HAZE AND HIS MEXICAN PAL JUAN! The van pulls up to them and the crew gets out but bob is angry with Shane Bob: DAMMIT SHANE! you said you'd be in your dressing room one hour ago, where the f##k were you? Shane: Well, Juan and I had a great idea. For todays promo we are going to do something special for the fans! The newest idea we thought up, is called....'Street Chronicles'. We will involve many real life things so all of you can relate to them. You will find the true meaning of love and the people that cherish it... Juan: Shane... did yo get that from a movie poster? Shane:...JUAN!..did I tell you to talk? THOUGHT SO!! Shane: As I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted... Clears throat and gives Juan a bad look Shane: In these chronicles you will find many different genres of life itself. Todays themes: Adventure and Humor (and pretending to be mexican) so ...LETS GO TO TACO BELL! The crew, Shane and Juan walk into the taco bell Juan and Shane approach the conter silently Juan: I like to call this one..mexican tourist (winks at camera ) Hola, Yo non hablo ingles...donde es el bagno Counter Guy: er...yo no speako spanisho? Shane: COME?! NON HABLA ESPANOL?! ES UN RISTORANT DI MEXICANO Y TU NON HABLA ESPANOL?!! Counter Guy: er... hola? Juan: BAGNO! BAGNO! BAGNO! DONDE ES EL BAGNO!!!!!! Shane: Ok..i try to speak the english... hello...my nombre is shane..I sm a tourist from mexico. he would like to know where is the bathroom. I wrestler I wrestle! Counter Guy: Ohhh...bathrooms over there! Juan: Che? CHE ES THERE!? Counter guy signals for Juan to follow him and brings him to the bathroom Juan: OOHH GRACIAS MI AMIGO Shane walks outside of the taco bell laughing at what just happened Shane: Did you guys see that stupid shit? He reminds me a bit of my opponents on Thursday... First he reminds of Josef Pires. This is because of his stpidity and he does not understand anything about others..like Pires does not understand the fact that im going to kick his ass in the ring Thursday. When Juan asked him where the bathroom was he thought he was ordering a burrito. I think Josef is stupid like that! I mean you tel him hes gonna lose and hes like oh...nevermind i thought i was gonna win ...Hes just stupid cocky and arrogant he doesent even know what hes getting himself into. Pires is a pure blooded idiot. ' YA ! IM GONNA WIN IM HARDCORE DONT MESS! ' sorry Josef..hat personality is already taken by about 345.8 other wrestlers...INSERT NEW COIN ...BITCH! Shane: That idiot in there also reminded me of Roni Savar. Roni is an ok guy I mean I have a bit of respect for him but I just want to say hes on crack. He cant even come close to winning a match against me because I am the man the myth the legend! Savar im not going easy on you just because your not a dumbass like Pires, I will punich you because you are a bigger threat tha Pires. Once you are lying in the ring KO'd when u wake up...dont e suprised if you find yourself wrapped in barbed wire! Juan walks out of the restaurant giggling Juan: I pinched his ass and then he tried to kiss me! Shane: I WAS RIGHT!! HE IS JUST LIKE MY OPPONENTS !!!!! (chuckle) Juan: Oook Shane: Our next stop is the playground! Juan: YAY! Shane and Juan then walk out onthe street and flag down an odd looking cab with a fairly large driver inside. He is wearing an unbuttoned shirt revealing his huge gut. They climb in the back. Cab Driver: HEY! Arent you Shane HaZe from CCW! Shane: YA! I didnt know I was famous yet....damn Cab Driver: My kids love you. They say you're gonna kick that gay ass pansy Pire's ass and those other two....nobody really knows who they are! Shane: Thanks man, I'll win the match for your kids Cab Driver: Here it is, tell ya what, the ride is on me. Juan: Thanks man! Juan and Shane get out and walk towards the playground, they find 2 little kids and Juan wants to interviewe them Juan: Hello, my name is Juan. I am from CCW a wrestling federation. Do you two know about CCW? Kid #1: YA! CCW kicks ass man! KICK ASS! WOOO! I LOVE Spider!!! Kid #2L Shut up Chuck(no pun intended)....Shane HaZe rules Juan: I work for Shane...hes the man! But anyway..Chuck is it how the fuck did you get to know such bad words...you're just a little kid Chuck: Er....Lucy and I are 19. we're midgets! Juan: Oh! Was there a mini haze at one of your midget clubs lately? Lucy: ya....Mini Haze is sooo sexy! Juan: Glad you think that way....anyhow can I ask you, Chuck what you think about my friends match on Thursday. Chuck: Cool, I'd be happy to. Juan: First, who do you guys think is way cooler, A Pansy, A hardcore idol gone wrong, a newbie or a semi talented waste of air? Chuck: Definetly the Idol..I love hardcore especially wrestling hardcore. They have the ability to do anything big and it hurts. They can slam chairs, tables ad sometimes even bats. I love hardcore style. Juan: Do you know who Josef Pires is..? Chuck: No...who da fuck is that?? Juan: Oh.... nobody Juan: Ok next what do you think about Roni Savar? Chuck: Roni Savar is a pansy. Hes probably not good enough to fight against anyone in CCW other than ..wait nobody. I think he is a weak little shit and HaZe will pulverise him in their match at Lockdown. Shane has already shown how great he is when he attacked Panzer, sure he didnt win but he still kicked ass. Shane is good to start out in a low card match, everyone does but after this, after Shane shows the world who he really is I dont think he will ever wrestle low card again. Juan: Just one more question, do you like me? Chuck: Errr...no comment? Shane approaches Juan as they give a cheesy smile and a thumbs up to the camera and it fades out to black. The black suddenly fades into a shit of the arena where the fans are tossing around bats in honor of our great hero Shane. Just wait until the match then, you'll see my true talent. Not only can I talk shit I can deal shit! Suddenly on the screen a red message reading 'Pre-Recorded' flashes. Shane Haze is standing in a dark room. Shane: The one person I didnt mentiion in that segment was Tristan Matthew. The reason? I didnt want to embarras him on TV. Other than myself he could be the only ounce of talent in this match. Surely I will beat him, as I will the others but it is only a matter of respect from me to him. Right now I am hoping to defeat him. So Tristan let this be a good match for both of us. Juan's head pops up. Juan: SUCKEEER!! We hear a smack (Shane's fist to Juan's head) and we fade out View Biography
