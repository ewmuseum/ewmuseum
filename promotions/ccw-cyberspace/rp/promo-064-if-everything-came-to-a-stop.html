If everything came to a stop...

4 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If everything came to a stop... Jason Dread CCW Promo #64 Date: 3/4/03 Location: An abyss Jason Dread - Want all...lose all, Blayze... ::-Scene fade from black onto a simple city street. The sun is at its noontime high, bathing everything in view in incandescent light. Cars pass by in the street, faint glimpses of the drivers able to be seen before they speed off into the distance. On the sidewalk on both ends of the street walk people from nearly every persuasion and ethnicity. Married couples, single men and women, groups of business...they all are seen walking upon their own paths; just as ants in a giant colony. Suddenly, a brilliant flash of white light encompasses everything, blinding all vision! As we are able to see once again, everything has changed. There is no more sun, only blackness. There is no movement, only stasis. No sound, only deafness. The only light seen is that of a small beam of illumination above every human or animal figure seen before the flash of light. From this void, we hear quiet footsteps approaching. From the distance, a figure moves closer to us. There is no light above him...it seems as though he is his own light. A slow pace the man moves, walking with hands in pockets, his head turning to gaze at these statues of the flesh, frozen in time. As he draws closer, about twenty five feet away, we notice the man is Jason Dread. His hands in the pockets of some old jeans he wears, a black leather jacket around the top half of his body. He begins to speak, his voice softly echoing through this worldly void.-:: Jason Dread - If time suddenly stopped, and you weren't allowed anything else in life...would anyone truly be happy? As statues, would anyone truly feel that they had completed their mindset task in life? I ask again...would ANYONE truly be happy? He talks to no one in particular, seemingly to himself. He walks in a curious path, slowing passing by each of the stopped figures, gazing as them as he passes. Jason Dread - Would YOU be happy, Blayze? Have you done everything you wish you could have? You can tell me yes in your quivering voice, with those shaking lips of yours, but when I look into your eyes, and I see the true you inside...you two are not yet completed. You aren't even close. How could a man be complete who takes on so many mindsets over a period of days you wonder just who exactly he is? You say every time you talk that you MUST have control over the fifteen pounds of gold around your shoulder, and you COMPLAIN about what happens if victory does not fall into your lap...you've strived oh-so hard just so you could obtain what you feel is most precious about you...the thing you so desperately want. You two act as though that is the one thing that drives you forwards through life. You wake up in the morning with the visions of your waist enveloped by a belt...and you act as to say you could quit now with a feeling of completeness? There is a lie that you live day to day, Blayze. The lie that you can look everyone straight faced to tell...but inside it eats you up. Your subconscious is at its limit, and it slips you up from time to time during your speech and false demeanors. I don't know how you are able to live like this...but you do, ...and YOU are the ones who must live this lie every...single...day! In front a portly man, with thin-wire glasses Dread comes to a stop. He seems to have a saddened look to his face, which is grimy with dirt. He stares into his glassy eyes... Jason Dread - Don't feel too bad though, Blayze. A lot of people live lies. Take this gentleman for instance. Two years ago to this date he raped and killed a 21 year old girl...his daughter's best friend. Why? He offered her the chance to sleep with him for one night and she refused. He tells himself over and over than she deserved it...that he was owed that opportunity with her. Life owed it to him...because life took his wife away from him. Over and over the lie is told in his mind, but deep inside he knows that what he did was despicable! That he is scum! Life owed him nothing, it was just part of a cycle...but he couldn't stand to live with that. He burned the body of the girl to a fine enough degree she was beyond identification. There is evidence against him, but nothing can be proven...so he walks the streets...but he suffers a fate worse than life in prison or the death sentence. His mind punishes him every second with visages of his crime. Soon, he won't be able to handle it any more. Soon, he ends his life at the hand of a blade...that is his punishment...torment. Your lies cannot last forever. The things you tell yourself soon become the downfall for your psyche. Will your fate be the same as his? Who knows...it just may... With a look of smug completeness, he moves forth once again, coming to the opposite side of the street from earlier. He stops, and the camera slowly zooms in on his face, as he comes up to a beautiful girl, walking alone on the street. Shoulder-length black hair flows behind her body, covered in a small t-shirt and capris khaki pants. He stands at her shoulder, simply looking at her as he continues, his voice quiet and still softly echoing. Jason Dread - You also befriend contradiction as you live your lies. It proves to be a difficult endeavor to follow your words for me when you constantly two-face yourself over, choosing only what scenario benefits you as being the most calm as you can be. The most nonchalant for the moment, then switching back to the original. You act as though there is nothing wrong...as though it's like living another day in your perfect world...well, there was a point which I had a perfect world as well. Just like most, my world collapsed... At the time it sent me into depression and loathing...but now I look back and I see that it wasn't the end of the world. Chances come again. I died in fire when everything went down, but I rose again from my ashes, born again as a new man. So your assumptions may be right at this point. I am NOT who I used to be... His face contorts suddenly to a grimace, as he growls out his next sentence. Jason Dread - ...I'm that much god damn more wicked!!! As soon as the emotion change was there, it is gone. We are left one again with a somber-faced, soft-spoken Jason Dread. His gaze is still on the girl's soft cheek. Jason Dread - I'm not important to you, you say, but you continue on and on about me in so many different shapes and forms...funny...is this another lie you live with? Is this how you go about coping with everything that confronts you to your face. There may be others who take you seriously. Maybe those in the former CCW pinnacle who can give a damn, but I don't care one bit about who you think you are, or who you try to be. I'm here to fight for myself. You are merely here to fight for your ego... Slowly, he outstretches his finger to the girl's cheek, and lets it slide down the length of it with an infinite softness and caring. Jason Dread - You two are not the only ones who can take advantage of any situation which may fall into your lap. Anyone with a human appetite can do so... He slowly tilts her head towards his, nothing else about her moves. Slowly he leans in towards her, every second passing is another to which their lips may touch...he stops mere millimeters from her ruby lips, taking his gaze off of her unseeing eyes. Jason Dread - ...but no...I won't sink to a level such as yours. Slowly, he turns her head to the position it was before he moved it, and gently kisses her cheek, before moving past her. He crosses the street, with several cars stopped in time on it. Slowly, Dread walks to the very center of this street, and stops moving. His relaxed expression still on his face, hands back into his pockets, he begins again. Jason Dread - Against anyone in the CCW you may be undefeated for as far as I care Blayze. However, you have ever been against that of me in a fair match. Sure, we have fought before, but you had a trump card in your corner which comes in the form of Brad Lamen. It's not too hard to win when you've got every opportunity possible NOT to lose...but that's alright with me. Unlike you, I don't have a selective memory, and I fully remember what happened as soon as that bell rang that ended our match. I not only took out that piece of trash Lamen, but I laid you flat as well. Where did you go? You ran, as I figured you would. But now, you have another trump card in your corner which came under the magnifying glass come the last Lockdown. As it seems, when I signed the contract for our match at Revelations, I cannot lay a hand upon you until then, Blayze. Once again, the guardian angel rests upon your shoulder and laughs it's mocking laugh. Coupled with our Lethal Lottery bid at the Tag Team titles we have...where the two of us were placed in a team together...all I have to say is you'd better not screw around with me, Blayze. Although I have no interest at these damnded tag team titles, I don't intend to lose...and like it or not you ARE my partner in all of this...which could always lead us unto victory...then at collapse later at Revelations. The possibilities seem odd, but the reality is always at hand with this situation. You get to choose now, Blayze...what do you want to happen? To think past that however, just because you have that belt that was handed to you, around your waist, doesn’t mean that you can beat me, Blayze. You may disagree, but there has not been one night I’ve fought to where I haven’t proved at least one more thing to just one more person. It just looks like now, Blayze…I get to prove it to you...that I am not just some rookie. Another brilliant flash of light! This time as we begin to see, there is nothing but blackness...save Jason Dread and in front of him a pedestal in the middle of the now empty street. It is about waist high, and on it lies a marble chessboard. Bathed in beauty, it is made of complete marble, shining from the lone light above it. The pieces are in standard order...except the white and black kings are missing from their desired squares...they lie in the direct center of the board. He walks towards the chessboard casually, the camera fixed on the board as he talks. Jason Dread - So here we stand...two Kings of our color in this game of chess known as life. Just like in chess, kings may never once come in contact with each other...they may just watch the other from their viewpoint from clear across the other side of the board...but when the kings do have the rare occasion to meet... The camera pans straight up into Dread's face, no longer serene but deadly focused. Jason Dread - ...it becomes a DAMN good spectacle to be seen. Power is thrown out the window...everything suddenly becomes a game of wits...a game I excel in. When they meet, none of the other pawns matter! He slams his fist square onto the board and all the pieces fall...except one. The white king in the center of the board remains upright, unmoved from its earlier position; while the black king lies on the board, easily toppled. Jason Dread - It becomes a game of man against man! Fighter against fighter! The board evens off, and you have two ready to tear the other apart...and there is nothing which prevents this from occurring! This isn't just a game for nothing Blayze, as you convince yourself it is. The stakes of this are very real...the consequences...very deep! You stand before me men without any cares except for maintaining a worthless montage about yourself, I stand before you a DEMON with the intention to kill! You have very little time left, Blayze...precious time you can use to give one last farewell to your kindred or mates...and every word I speak of is the TRUTH! Everything suddenly becomes black. The voice of Jason Dread echoes ghastly off of nothing... Jason Dread - Now can you handle EXACTLY what...you...dread?! ::-Everything suddenly cuts back to how it was before Dread ever showed his voice in the void. Cars once again move, the sun has returned in its full beauty. The only thing which seems out of place is a beautiful girl across the street from the camera. She suddenly stops walking, and touches her cheek gently, slowly looking around confused...the scene fades to black...-:: View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
