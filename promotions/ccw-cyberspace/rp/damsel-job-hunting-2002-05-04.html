Damsel - Job Hunting?

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Damsel (Christine Oakes) attends a job interview at a law office in Seattle.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: Job Hunting? Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 11:16:43 Location: Seattle, WA This is a: Match RP *Seattle, Washington; Friday afternoon. Christine Oakes finds herself in a small waiting room at a law office. She's attired in a business casual outfit and although it looks good on her, it's unexpected. She's flipping through a very old issue of 'People' magazine and wondering just what the heck she's doing here.......* Woman- Ms. Oakes? *Christine looks up from her magazine to see smartly-dressed middle aged woman looking down on her. Christine stands up.* Christine- Yes? Woman- Hi, I'm Marge Tannenbaum. I guess I'm what you would call the director of personnel around here. Christine- Pleased to meet you. Marge- If you would follow me into the back office, we'll get this interview started. *And Christine does precisely that. As they walk into the back, Marge continues her introduction.* Marge- I know your mother, Christine. She and I go back a long way. You should be very thankful to her for this opportunity. The job market is very difficult these days, and there are many more applicants who are a bit more qualified than you. Christine- Wait....... You're only doing this because of my mother? Marge- Oh no! Everyone has to start somewhere. You are the hard-working type of individual that we think would fit in perfectly here at Sigil Inc. *They reach Marge's office and enter. Marge closes the door behind them and offers Christine a seat. She takes it as Marge sits behind her desk and pulls out Christine's resume.* Marge- So........ How long have you been unemployed? Christine- I'm not. I work for Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. Marge- *dropping the resume* Then what in god's name are you doing here? Christine- *shifts uncomfortably under Marge's glare* I'm trying to branch out a bit and broaden my horizons. I love wrestling, and there's nothing I'd rather be doing, but I know that I can't keep doing it forever. Women, especially, are valued for only as long as they still look good and can perform. Marge- *nods* The wrestling atmosphere is very sexist. You're mother told me you were doing something that she didn't approve of. It all makes sense now. If you're really serious about working here, you'll need to put in about twenties hour a week – minimum. With your schedule, do you think you can handle that? Christine- I don't know........ Marge- Most of the work that we have for you can be done away from the office if necessary. A lot of it is merely typing up reports into a presentable format........ Secretarial duties. If we know where you are, and you have a computer handy, we can send all the information to you. You just need to send everything back before the deadlines. Christine- Well then........ That, I can probably do. Marge- Good. I'll get you the paperwork to fill out. Now, one last thing........ Normally, with someone of your position and your work with wrestling, we would have turned you down immediately. I'm giving you this as a favor to your mother. Don't let any of us down. Christine- I won't. *And as Marge goes to get the papers, Christine thoughts turn inward once again to her multitude of problems. Mike is gone off somewhere, Regina Winters is after her, and she has to face Koriann and Paula Hampton in the ring on Monday night. And what's worse........ The chinks in her armor are getting bigger. She finds that she has to constantly keep herself busy and thinking about other things so as not to let the big wave of depression crash down on her. She knows that she's going to have to deal with her feelings sooner rather than later, but right now, it's much easier if she just ignores them. Ignore them until she gets into the ring. Then, and only then, will she channel them the best way she knows how........ Anger.* View Biography
