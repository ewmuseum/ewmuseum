To All Those of Old, Present and Future...

13 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A promo in which Yama Tatsu, speaking from Devyl’s lounge, announces his Champions of the Ring entry and challenges Carnage while grumbling about returning veterans.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Yama Tatsu Entitled: To all those of old, present and future... Life doesn't just resume when you walk back through those doors Written in Monday, May 13, 2002, 05:12:45 Location: A Cheap Motel somewhere on Highway 69 This is a: Match RP The stakes have been upped… Legends have returned to the brawl, The Champions have risen up, The weak have simply dropped out… Yama Tatsu demonstrated to us his own way of cleaning the slate of weak and frail bodies, By aiding those who wish to destroy them… The Mayhem has just began, But the game IS over… ** The scene opens in the home of Devyl. He rests in the main lounge and entertaining area. He is resting back in the chair with his feet up. He awaits the camera as it pans over. A nice roaring fire blares in front of him. He sits there smugly in a new Yama Tatsu merchandise t-shirt and his Karate Gi pants. He speaks. ** Yama Tatsu: Welcome to the home of Devyl, she’s not in right now but leave your name and number and I’m sure she’ll come and burn your house down. ** A wicked smile resides on his face. ** Camera man: Well… We are actually here to see you sir. I mean, you’ve been avoiding cameras lately and we were wondering why? Yama Tatsu: Aahhhhh yes. I thought that’s why you were here. There is a method to my madness surprisingly. You see I have been constantly training and preparing myself, along with Master, by the way where is he? He is never around when I need him… But hey don’t you go distracting me from the subject. The reason for my absence is I have been training up for the COTR! That’s right! You heard me, I didn’t stut-stut-stut-stutter… I am planning on winning the Champions Of The Ring tournament. You see I saw how close I came to winning that CCW Title! I wanted it, I wanted so bad I could taste it! Maybe it was simple bloodlust but I wouldn’t let up till I had my team members blood all over my hands. This was my downfall. Never will this happen again! I should have walked away with Gold that night. ** He reaches over and grabs a Diet Coke. ** Yama Tatsu: Ahhhhh that’s good. Man, with all the power and talent of the flock of old school CCW-ites I am gonna have to train twice as hard. That’s why I am setting out a challenge to Carnage. That’s right big boy I want you this Thursday. You managed to beat… errr… ummm… walk to the ring and pin RHSC. You NEED a Challenge, you NEED a real match, you NEED me! So this Thursday you and me, not a problem… alright I have wasted enough air on you. ** Yama Tatsu finishes his Diet Coke and throws the empty can into the roaring open fire. ** Yama Tatsu: This place is so f’n cold, but it’s almost Summer! You know I have a real beef with all these returning stars and legends. Like I could give a royal fuck! I mean SuMa this, Fat Ass that, Mr Gobstopper right up my bald, asian ass!!! ** The veins in the neck and exposed arms start to stick out. His body mood changes to one of heavy anger. ** Yama Tatsu: I will show all of these frick’n wrestling gods just what a little boy for Osaka, Japan can do. They will all feel the pain I am sending out… remember you can't just return to the ring and expect the same respect, title shots or match time. It shouldn't be happening. I, we, have earned my way up and now some old timers who are still living in their glory days want to come and take that away, I don't think so! Life is a valuable thing boys… don’t make me take that away from you… ** Fade out. ** View Biography
