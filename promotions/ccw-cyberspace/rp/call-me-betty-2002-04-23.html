This is a: Match RP “Honesty may be the best policy, but insanity makes a better defense.” April 23, 2002 - 1:04 PM Journal entry. 'Agh.' Frustration; angry and agonizing to the point of complete destruction of my soul. The motions go on, and on, and on, and on; they roll forward, trapping me like a rat in a maze, yet stay the same despite time. Blocking the sun, the light, the hope. To eliminate them, forever, from my days seems impossible though to mow them down with splays of accurate gunfire is a dream unrealized. Just to shut the door and never look back, to never breathe the same air as they again would be heaven on earth, nirvana. When I pass by you, I can feel you there. I long to have you wrap me up with those arms and take me away, so far away that I would never remember these days. Nothing but a nightmarish memory of which this one escaped. Take me there, anywhere; away from the stench of the oozing, puslike creatures that drain the light of the sun. Take me away from this hellbound world. Its calm today, my head is quiet...and flowing. Like water, little ripples of thought..dancing. I like it .. the voices are quiet and my head isn't pounding with thoughts that are so angry they want to break out of my skull and bleed upon the floor ... I like peace. I like the euphoric pillows of silk that are around my thoughts, cushioned, soft. How can something so soft be so wrong? It can't be. How can something so gentle be wrong? It can't be... that's like looking upon the face of a child and saying it is dangerous. Wallowing...mellow...flowing, drowning .. giving in and recieving the caressing warmth. April 23, 2002 - 1:04 PM Augusta, Maine. 'Good and old.' The serene view was enough for any tourist to waste at least half of their film snapping pictures. The brooding sun was just bellowing below the horizon, reflecting orange and purple light off the still water. Boats rested in position at the harbor, fish every now and then hopping in and out of the water. A throaty sigh came from the other side of the camera. We quickly turn, and take in the image of a man. Disheveled brown hair almost falls into his eyes, emerald oculars taking in the humble view before them. Hands were stuffed into the navy denim pockets, and his legs rocking back and forth. This man was now recognizible, and it was suprising not to see the pestering Howie by his side. John Linsey, a new force in the CCW. John Linsey, soon to have a record of one win. John Linsey, your hero. 'I know most people look down on the rookies. I know most people think rookies have nothing valuable to offer. This rookie right here, will prove every single individual who thinks that terribly wrong.' Linsey let another sigh escape his pale lips, raising his hand to the back of his head. A single digit scratched at an everlasting itch, before lowering itself. John resumed his concentration, only then. 'My two opponents. I know nothing of them, nothing about them. I won't go off on how I'm so better than them. Chang, Mark..they are just two rusty ladder rungs on my way to success in the CCW. On my way to the top. And unfortunately, there is always a consequence when you get in the way of me. Being a verbal of physical attack, nevertheless, they both hurt.' His fingers moved around in the cloth of his pockets, and brought out a cigarette. He lighted it up with a maroon lighter, and stuck it between those lips. He took a breath of it in, and held it between his fingers, lowering it to his sides. 'Their names mean nothing but a slightest fragment of brain cell to me. More names to add to the list of victims, of prey. Mark, Chang..you both are simply obstacles in the way of my further achievements, and both meaningless imbeciles looking for a well preformed beat down. ..It's nothing personal, of course. Not like I have a vendetta against you guys. To put it in simple terms, I will do to you what I have done to almost all in the past. Conquer with complete authority.' John took another puff of the cancer stick. 'Don't you just love the feeling of dominance? Well - why in the heck do I ask you this, have you even experienced being the 'big dog'? No, you're not good enough. No one is good enough to beat me..' He just shakes his head and tosses the stick towards the ground. With a grind of his shoe, he walks off.. the sun now set, and the sky a dreary black. Faint words soon follow. 'Have fun playing with the Eagle.'
