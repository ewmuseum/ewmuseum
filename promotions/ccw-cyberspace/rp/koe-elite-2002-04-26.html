KoE Elite

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Ricky Thunder tours Gramercy Park and a restaurant in New York while previewing his King of Extreme match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: KoE Elite Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 13:00:23 Location: MSG This is a: Match RP *Scene opens up as Ricky is walking along the street in New York. The camera pans out and notices the street sign says East 22nd. He continues on until he reaches a secluded area. There are houses on three sides of him and an eight foot tall wrought iron fence. The fence is very well constructed and ivy is growing up the rails. The fence could almost be mistaken for an ivy wall. Ricky moves towards the wall, and runs his hand across the ivy, detecting each bar on the fence. He looks up at the top of the fence, and the around. He decides to scale the fence and disappears on the otherside of the ivy. He makes his way across a nicely maintained field and sees a park bench. He decides to go have a seat. As he is sitting there he is approached by another man.* Man: You new here? Ricky: 'Scuse me? Man: I have never seen you here before. Ricky: Aye, that's because I've never been here before. Man: Oh well in that case, welcome to Gramercy. Ricky: Thank you. Man: So where did you move from? Ricky: Move from? Man: Yes. Where are you from? Is that an Australian accent? Ricky: Oh, indeed it is. Man: Lovely isn't it? Ricky: I beg your pardon? I am starting... Man: NO! I'm sorry! I meant the park, not the accent. Not that there is anything wrong with the accent. *Ricky stares at the man for a minute, as an ackward silence ensues. The man decides to take this time to mosey off, and leave Ricky alone on the bench as he found him.* Ricky: The legendary Gramercy Park. The heart and soul of the local yuppies. Inpenetrable they say. Only way in without being caught and charged with trespassing, is with a key. Interesting concept. Lock out the ordinary and plain, and keep only the elite. Much like what is going on at the PPV. The King of Extreme finals. I stepped in and took the place of the man who would have won this event had he been present and that man was Enigma. The Angel of Death. The hardcore icon. So he liked to think. If he were here today I would show him extreme. I was a late entry and still I am here. I was the unexpected. I was the plain. I am now the elite. *Ricky gets up from the bench and makes his way along a perfectly edged sidewalk, and down towards the front entry of the park. He takes in all the scenery as he continues on with the weeping willows blowing in the breeze. The children running about and having fun, playing with their toys, and games.* Ricky: I remember my childhood. I spent most of the time getting my ass kicked by my dad and my brother. 'Training' me for a better life. Well it worked. I am now twice the man my father was, and I am going to prove I am better then Thunder was. I will not desecrate his spirit, by disrespecting him, he was my brother, and I had nothing but love for him, but this is SuMa's fault. Until he arrived, I had nothing to prove to anyone, I didn't have to show the world I was better than anybody. But now, all of CCW doubt me. They all cast me into my brother's shadow which is far from where I wanted to end up. I am being put on the spot and told I am not as good as he was. That I will never be as good as he was, well you know what, I am here to prove them wrong. *Ricky arrives at the front gate and realizes he is locked in. He would climb the fence however there are police officers manning the entrance. He decides to just sit back and take a breather for a bit longer* Ricky: In CCW, everyone is about mind games, everyone is about being better at decieving thir opponent. I am here to just be better period. I am good enough to be the King of Extreme, I am good enough to defeat Blayze, I am good enough to defeat even the all mighty SuMa. You see, Blayze claims to be the hardcore icon now that Enigma is gone. He thinks he is the 'baddest mo'fo in CCW'. With all praise to Allah of course. I remember watching from a rehab facility, when you won the CCW title at Heatstroke. You cried like a little bitch. Thug my ass. You and this crack-head brother of yours, think you have what it takes to hold onto those belts together, well imagine what you are going to indure by yourself when The Supreme Team collapses on you in this KoE match-up. Which brings me to my next subject, Danny Bryson. You are sad. Your alcoholic ways will do nothing but destroy you. You will not hold that T/A title for very long, even if I myself have to take it away from you. You are a disgrace to everyone who has worked to obtain that title. But now the catch here, is once again, I will be facing Ebola. The man that Carnage saved, last week while I was in the process of destroying him. Which, in due time Carnage, I will be coming for you. But back to you Ebola. You claim to be the killer without a cure, but just remember I found your cure, I found your vaccine. I found, that you aren't as infectious as you once believed. *The gate swings open as a security guard enters and nods to Ricky as he goes by. Ricky catches the gate with his foot and gets to his feet. He exits out the gate and lets it clang shut behind him. He continues down 19th street back towards Main. He arrives on main and hails a cab, which he directs to Times Square. Upon reaching Times Square, he pays the cabbie and head across 43rd back to WWF New York. The same guards are there as yesterday, and they recognize Ricky.* Guard: Welcome back sir, sorry about the inconvienance yesterday, come on in. Ricky: No prob. *Ricky enters the restaraunt and walks past the trophey case in the front containing articles such as the original WWF title belt, the Undertaker's urn, and Mankinds mask. He looks at the stuff and makes his way to a table set up in the upper level seating. As he gets seated a waitress comes by to take his order, but he just orders a coke.* Ricky: Well, this has got to be the absolute, with out a doubt, most over-rated place I have ever seen in my entire life. *The waitress brings back his coke, and a check for it. Ricky picks it up, and his eyes widen immensly* Ricky: FOUR DOLLARS! FOR A COKE! Waitress: Yes sir. Ricky: What a fuckin rip! *Ricky tosses the waitress a five dollar bill, and downs his coke.* Ricky: Ya know, I again will undoubtedly face off against my partner again. It is bound to happen. You know, much like this place, this WWF New York, Overcharge and Over-rated. SuMa is pretty much the same way. Overrated, and overpaid. *Ricky laughs to himself a bit* Ricky: I'm just clowning ya my friend. You see, what you don't realize, is no matter, how hard you try CCW, you can not drive a wedge into The Supreme Team. We will be ready, and we will be focused. Will you CCW, be ready for that? *Ricky gets up and head out the door, and as he's leaving one of the Guards stops him* Guard: You leaving that quick? Ricky: Aye. This is the worst place I have ever visited in my life! Over charge for everything, and there is nothing here worth looking at. Bah! You can keep it. *The guards look a bit shocked as Ricky turns to walk away. He makes his way back across 43rd to a smaller pub type of location. He walks in and disappears behind the bar. He opens up a door in the floor that leads down to some type of cellar. As Ricky gets to the bottom of the steps he closes the door above him , leaving him alone in a dimly lit corridor, as the scene fades to black.* (OOC for more info on where this leads, read into SuMa's RP.)
