Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A serialized parody in seven chapters in which Yama Tatsu, recruited by a wizard, travels from Tokyo to Chicago to enter the Champions of the Ring tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Yama Tatsu Entitled: Bored of the Rings (Chapter1-7) this is my real RP the other one was only supposed to be my interview Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 02:14:50 Location: Chicago This is a: Match RP Three spots to great, past Veterans, Seven for rookies looking to make their names known, Six to the mighty title holders, And one for our courageous leader upon his noble throne, In the land of CCW where the shadows lie, Nine granted to the likes of J-Mac, Panzer, Carnage and Bryson, Six for the remaining scattered, All these men must rise up through the conquest, To become COTR no matter how worn or tattered, In the land of CCW where the shadows lie… ﺵﺻﻃﻆﺝﻚ The Fellows of the Ring Chapter 1: Party Feuds It all started one night down in little Tokyo, Japan. A mighty great party ran wild through the City centre. We focus in on a tiny town square on the edge of this wonderful city. The small lantern’s lights twinkle on the “back dropping” river. Many kids run rampant around the feet of the taller adults. Our hero of this wonderful story resides at a small table outside of an empty, ancient trading shop. He sips a small cup of Suki. He notices the evil glare of the Sumo champion standing in a near by ring. He stands and leaves his table, cutting through the sea of tiny festival lovers. He approaches the small Sumo Wrestling ring. Here the mighty champion, Yakusaka Hinochawai, stands awaiting his next victim in this night of celebration. Yama Tatsu: I shall answer your call Yakusaka! The gigantic Yakusaka waited for his opponent to enter his ring and then suddenly charged at Yama Tatsu. Yama did not even flinch and, at the last possible moment, slammed a huge Sidekick to the jaw of the stampeding monster. This stops the rushing beast dead in his tracks, in fact he just lays there unconscious. Yama Tatsu strides from the ring. He simply returns to his seat as the shocked crowd slowly, but surely breaks out into a cheer. He rests here a few moments when a man in a cloaked hood appears behind Yama Tatsu. He reaches his old and withered hand out and is about to tap Yama on the shoulder, when Yama quickly turns and grabs the wrinkled paw of the cloaked man. Yama Tatsu: What do you want old man? Rudolph: I am Rudolph, Ancient wizard and seeker for a true fighter! Yama Tatsu: Well you may as well leave I am not interested… Rudolph: You do not understand! The land of PTC needs you to venture out and win a magical ring… The “Champions of the” ring… Yama Tatsu: …… I shall do it. I shall venture to the land of CCW and reclaim the “Champions of the” Ring. ﻘﻞﺳﺱﺆﮔלּ Chapter 2: The First Leg of The Journey Here we see Yama Tatsu riding forward on his beautiful Stallion that is Singapore Airways. He sits up in first class next to a rowdy little bugger. He is constantly throwing up and the smell is retched. Too bad for Yama that all the seats are filled. The entire plane in fact is filled up. Unless he wants to go down the back to “Cattle” class. Our warrior valiantly soldiers on through these hard times though as he knows his final goal is above his own personal sacrifice. אּהּﻵﻼﻒﻍﮊ Chapter 3: Wild, Wild West Styles Yama Tatsu lands in L.A. quickly fleeing from his battle with the venom spitting elf… also know as the annoying little kid who couldn’t keep his lunch down. When Yama is a safe distance from the fighting he finds a lodging for the night. No it isn’t the Brandywine Lodge or Wilderland Hall, far from it in all actuality. The place I am talking about is “Dirty David’s Romance Shack”. This place is the definition of cruddy. Here social rejects have come together to form a sex hole. There are even cameras in the bedrooms, just for old Dirty David. Rudolph said that this is where they would meet and if he wasn’t there by “Sun up” on January 2nd 2002, Yama Tatsu was to venture out on his own. As the hours passed through the night Yama Tatsu trained. Knowing the cameras were watching him he acted as though he didn’t know they were there. Yama Tatsu: Where is that dam fool of a wrinkled prune, Rudolph! I should destroy him when he shows up! The constant strain of being watched eventually got to Yama Tatsu and the pressure of not using his little secret was getting to much. He let his little secret unleash and he absolutely trashed the room, destroying the cameras in the process. Groups of ghetto thugs, known as the “Low Riders”, barged in to stop this mad man. The are all decked out in uniforms similar to that of the K.K.K… except the costumes black! The letters B.B.B (Big Black Baddasses) are printed across their chests. Low Rider 1: I’ms gunna rip yo aye nu one foooooool! Yama Tatsu: Bring it you burnt piece of toast! Imagon: Not while I’m here. Yama, run! Yama Tatsu jump through a nearby shattered window. While the Low Riders and Yama’s new found ally, Imagon, battle inside the burning, cheesifest of a Hotel. ﮊﮓﯽרּﻌﺹﻜﻠﻦ Chapter 4: The Upcoming Battleground Yama Tatsu ran from the place as fast as he could. He reached a wide river, with high jagged cliff banks, and the only way across was a small, dodgy, rope bridge. The wind picked up and the bridge started to sway as Yama Tatsu made his way out. Suddenly a rope snapped and the bridge dropped to one side. Yama Tatsu turned to see the leader of the Low Riders, with his bouncing Chariot going full bore in the background. The rest of his clan jumped from the bushes. Yama turned back and quickly powered on forward along the dangling bridge. SNAP! The other support rope was cut and the bridge went slamming down into the… ummm… errrr… water which was six inches below him. Realising this Yama Tatsu quickly sprinted across the river. The Lower Riders returned to their Impala and charged at the shallow river. The suddenly slammed on the brakes. Low Rider 1: Fuck that sheet yo! Low Riders in Unison: Word!!! The slammed there car in reverse and bolted down the road looking for another bridge. Meanwhile back at the river point, where Yama Tatsu is lying trying to regain his breath, Imagon appears from the bushes where the additional members of the Low Riders came from. He is missing his clothes except for his underwear. He tip toes through the once exaggerated river, which in reality is more like a tiny stream of water. Imagon: Man I saved your life back there… I was raped of my Manly hood… Yama Tatsu: ** Looking at the man’s boxer shorts, which are cover in purple hearts** Looks to me like you never had it! Imagon: What?… Ohhhh, you never wondered why I was hanging out there? Duh??? Yama Tatsu: Well Imagon… I’m a gone! Yama Tatsu pushed the homosexual hero into the water and ran. Imagon layed there in the water screaming like a little girl. ﻄﻀﺽﺷﺸﺲﺃטּ Chapter 5: Yama Gump Yama Tatsu seemed good at running ever since one magical day when he was being chased, by some boys with shanghais, and poor unfortunate Yama had leg braces, to treat a muscle/bone problem. That magical day he was trying to run as fast as he could but the braces restricted him. But suddenly the just broke off and he out sprinted the mean, mean kids. Ever since that day he has just been running and running. Sometimes he would run and not know where he was running too. He just ran cause it was fun. This was one of those times. He just ran and ran and ran and ran and ran and ran… till he just couldn’t run no more. By this time a small and loyal group of men had joined Yama Tatsu. They called themselves “The Fellows of the Ring”. The apparently ended up in Aurora, Illinois. This small place was the home of the great American music festival Waynestock! It is also just a short journey from Chicago. The destination of Yama Tatsu’s first COTR challenge, so after a short stay at a more respectable lodge, they headed off to the home of the Battleground. טּדּאָקּﺦﺙﺔﺄﱢﷲ Chapter 6: The Great and Almighty U-Turn It seems the mighty team over short their destination and had to make a quick U- Turn. שּׁﭻﮎﮓﺉﺅﷲﱞ Chapter 7:Rudolph and Yama reunited Our great and noble warriors have finally reached the city of Chicago. It is a great and wonderous City with equal opportunities for all… Yeah right! They decided to lay down at a campsite for the night. As they ate and drank and sang merry songs a great unease came over Yama. He felt he was being watched. Suddenly the shaky and scrawny figure of Rudolph emerged from the bushes. He collapsed at Yama’s feet. The Fellows of the Ring quickly tendered to his needs and aided back to some health. Yama Tatsu: Rudolph, who did this to you? Rudolph: It was the Low Riders and Dirty Dave… They pinned me to the ground and tattooed a message to you on me… Yama Tatsu: Well show it to me old friend Rudolph quickly stood, bent over and dropped his pants… Yama Tatsu: Oh my god that’s gastly! They say I will not leave Chicago alive! Dam! Rudolph: Never fear Yama we will protect you. They claim to have sent a great Knight named Jack Panzer out to kill you this Thursday night at the local fighting matches. Yama Tatsu: We must go and expel this evil warrior of theirs. I have heard he is great… but, he is many things I am not, for example brave, strong, heroic and oriental. I am dangerously Asian! I will rip this man apart, after all he is nothing to me. I was an Independent and Hardcore champion, what has he been. Answer = Nothing!!! Besides this match will be a walk over. I have my allies and the common fact that this story won’t go on with out me! So he can’t kill me… maybe rough me up a bit… but that is as far as it goes. The men talked long into the night about how bad they are going to destroy Jack Panzer as the sipped wine and feasted on roast.
