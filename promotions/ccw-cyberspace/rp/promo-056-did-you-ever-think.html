Did You Ever Think?

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Did You Ever Think? Yama Tatsu CCW Promo #56 Date: Location: Did You Ever Think As The Hearse Rolls By You Could Be The Next To Die? They Wrap You In A Big, White Sheet And Throw You In, 6 Feet Deep. The Worms Crawl Round And Round As They Consume Mauro And Ground Your Eyes Fall Out And Your Teeth Decay Such A Rotten End To A Lovely Day! We have travelled South of the border to reacquaint ourselves with a forgotten friend, a partner in ‘crime’ so to speak. The scene opens in the great ‘hole’ Mexicans proudly call Tijuana. A luxurious looking rental Limo pulls up outside a dirty, infested, rundown prison. Several midget-like Mexican police stand guard at the entry gate to the main holding block. The camera does a big cinematic pan around to the guard’s point of view looking across a small, stony parking area. The head of CCW’s Security Personnel opens his door on the opposite side of the outstretched car from the Guards. He makes his way around the front of the car, past the Drivers door and up to the rear of the car. He slowly and dramatic open the door and half a dozen straight faced, dead serious Lawyers dressed in flashy ‘Armoni’ suits and expensive shoes. Small storms of dust and dirt whip around their feet. The leader of this pack gives a quick glance down at his shoes followed by another look of disgust/arrogance. The Guards give a collective chuckle; this does not go down to well as there is obviously a show down of a sort going on here. Night is growing near and the Lawyers have no desire to linger so they make their way forward. Head of Security: Buonas tardes. Estoy aquí recoger a uno de sus internos... Kyle Thorne (I am here to collect one of your inmates… Kyle Thorne) Lead Guard: ¡Tonto blanco mudo, su como él acaba de leerlo de un libro! (Dumb white fool, its like he just read it from a book!) The Head of Security reaches into his back pocket and flicks through his translation book. The Guards piss themselves laughing. Suddenly the lead Lawyer reaches out and grabs the guard by his collar and throws him back against the wall, which is followed by a roll of money. Lawyer: Maybe you speak my language! He gives a swuarve smile at his witty joke. Lead Guard: Gracias, Gracias! ... (Thank You, Thank You! ...) Come right this way! The ancient, rusty hinges screech and grind as they are slowly pushed open. The light pours in and the darkness flows out, like smoke from a burning house. The 6 Lawyers, 3 of the guards and the Baboon of a Security Guard step forward inside of the cold and unforgiving walls. The stand penned in a cage surround by thousands of inmates. Drooling, sickening, twisted freaks of Southern society trying there best to tear through the cage to the visitors. Around the edge of the cage lay the main courtyard of this section of the prison. A burst of gunfire came from above and a couple of men at the exit of the cage dropped to the ground. The camera quickly turned away. A siren rang out loudly and the convicts scattered. Suddenly a loud booming Mexican voice rang out over the P.A. system. Yelling out something about returning to the cellblocks as small armies of armed guards rushed the Courtyard. Moment’s pass and the crew are held there until the courtyard is almost emptied. Lead Guard: I apologise, the inmates always get a little crazy when we have visitors… The poncy Lawyers brush off the lame attempt at chitchat. They now head quickly across the large dirt and gravel opening to a building on the far side of the prison from the exit. Suddenly a bottle crashes at the feet of one of the Lawyers. The camera focuses of the assailant as 2 guards rush him and beat him to the ground with their Batons. We are then hurried inside as the sounds of more bottles striking the ground and walls are left burning in the crew’s ears. Lead Lawyer: We would like to see Kyle Thorne… Guard: Oh! The American! He has been making a fair bit of noise. Right this way. The small group of Lawyers and their bodyguard accompanied by one of the Jail’s guards make there way down a long damp corridor and through a thick metal door. Guard: You are now in D Block… I tell you only the most dangerous S.O.B’s in Mexico are in this block! Your friend must be quite the hellion! Lead Lawyer: He sure is, but if you don’t mind we will take it from here… Guard: Oh no I can’t let you… CRACK!!! Head of Security: He really should have been careful with his Baton Lead Lawyer: Dammit you fool! I keep telling you violence is our last resort! We B-R-I-B-E them first, got it? Head of Security: Yes sir… The fool of a rent a cop CCW has employed to help out the Lawyers puts on a sad face, like a kid who just got his favourite toy taken away from him. The group continues on down the hall while the their baboon of a assistant grabs the guards keys. They walk past several monstrous looking criminals looked in little cells with concrete walls dividing them. It is a regular old school Jailhouse! The crooks lean on the bars of their cell doors swearing, striking and spitting at the Lawyers and the rest of the crew. He reach the end of the hall and turn to our right to find none other than Kyle Thorne lying on the hard concrete slab the Mexican Prison Authorities call a bed. He simply lies they’re tossing a ball into the air and catching it again with the same hand. The Lawyers stand there impatiently. When they can see it is obviously going to be up to them to make first contact the lead Lawyer speaks up but is swiftly cut off. Lead Lawyer: So are… Kyle Thorne: So the Cavalry finally showed up! Lead Lawyer: Tisk, Tisk, Tisk… CCW thought you would have learned your lesson by letting you hang for awhile… Besides we are under a new regime, your old contract expired months ago! I suppose you don’t get TV privileges in a dive like this! Kyle Thorne: Well it is prison you retard! Besides why would I want to watch TV anyway? Lead Lawyer: Because if you had seen TV, you would know the old CCW, the CCW you wanted to be apart of is back! Not some cheap remake of it, but the original! The Lawyers takes a glance behind them after they hear a strange grunt. There stands the biggest, meanest monstrosity anyone has ever seen. He is like the Abdominal Snowman, The Yeti, Sasquach what ever you want to call him, only instead of having a load of white fuzz he is Mexican (close enough, right ;) ). 2nd Lawyer: I guess its true what they say… Shit always does run South! Criminal: Argggghhhhh!!!!! The monster launches at the lawyer and is only held back by the metal bars that divide them. All them men squash hard up against Kyle’s cell door. They all quake in fear. Kyle’s unexpected words startle them. Kyle: I’d be careful what you say around my friend over there… He’s a little touchy and really quite a terrifying individual. ¡Calme abajo del amigo, ninguna necesidad de la cólera! Además de madre irán a los fuckers y me pronto... (Calm down friend, no need for anger! Besides these mother f**kers and I will be gone soon...) The monster, just like a ‘Ware-wolf’ at day break, he reverted back to a normal, kind of angry individual. Lead Lawyer: What did you say? Kyle: I said that shit may run South, but the stench always rises! Hahahaha The Lawyers give him a sheepish look. Kyle: Besides that right there is a prime example of a man so bored with life the only way he could alleviate his pain was to strike out in violence. I can sympathise… Lead Lawyer: Well I think it’s enough of the chitchat. We want to get you out of here. We promised the Mexican Government you would never step foot on Mexican soil again unless travelling with CCW. We also made a deal you would pay for the damage you had done… Kyle: Who said you could speak for me! Lead Lawyer: You want to get out of here and back into a CCW ring don’t you? Possibly get a title shot if you earn it! Kyle: Point well taken! … Hey who’s the big cheese of CCW now days! I think I owe him a favour for getting me out of here! Lead Lawyer: Yes, you sure do! But it actually wasn’t the president who wants you back! It’s a different man all together! Kyle: Who?… Lead Lawyer: You’ll know soon enough! He’s waiting for you outside! Kyle: I don’t like games or riddles! Tell me now! Lead Lawyer: Paticence is a virtue… obviously rotting in a cell hasn’t given it to you! Kyle: You wanna shut your mouth and open this cell door!? I want to get out off here! And I want to know who it is that’s setting me free! Lead Lawyer: No one if you don’t behave! One of the other Lawyers reach out with the keys about to unlock the cell door when a couple of shots ricochet off of the cell bars around Kyle. A bit of cussing is done as the sparks from the bullets fall gently to the ground like Snowflakes of fire. The camera and everyone else turns to see the injured guard laying propped up against the exit. He raises his gun again and pulls the trigger… empty! The lawyer quickly unlocks the door as the Prison guard hurriedly searches for extra bullets. The camera does a dramatic swing around to see Kyle charge down the hallway. Kyle swings back his leg and boots the guard square in the jaw just as he completes the reload of his weapon. By now the block is in quite a raucous and the sounds of gun fire has been heard all across the Prison. The old air raid type siren kicks in and the prisons guard yells can be heard all across the prison. The Lawyers have unlocked all the inmates doors and all the convicts rush past Kyle. The lawyers are last in queue along with Kyle’s ‘special’ friend. Kyle: Come on let us get out of here! Now! Kyle hands off the Guard’s revolver to the monstrous giant standing beside him and keeps a hold of the Guard’s baton himself. They all bail out of the D Block building and into the courtyard where they were met with such an outburst before. Here around 40 guys fight with just as many if not more Prison Guards. Our crew just run straight through the middle with Kyle up front clearing the path and the gigantic crook bringing up the rear. When they meet the metal cage they find the cold, steel gates locked. Convict: Arrrrggghhhhh!!! The prisoner blows open the lock with a single bullet. They burst into the cage and through the main entrance gate. Outside waits the limo already running and revving hard. Kyle and his escapee friend quickly pile into the back of the limo and as the Lawyers go to jump in Kyle shuts the door in their face. They try to open it… Failure, it’s locked. Lead Lawyer: This ain’t anytime to be joking Kyle! … Kyle, Kyle! We cut to inside the car. The drivers/privacy window rolls down and who is in the drivers seat, none other than Former and future CCW Independent Champion, Yama Tatsu! Kyle Thorne: So it was you who got me out of there! Yama Tatsu: Yeah with a little help from the Sylkk! Kyle Thorne: Let’s get out of here man! We can talk business on our long trip home! Yama Tatsu: Whats with the Freak? Kyle Thorne: We can just drop him at Taco Bell! The car pulls back and drives away leaving a cloud of dust and burning rubber behind them, while the lawyers are left yelling obscenities. The lawyers slowly turn around in unison when they are all tapped on the shoulder, there stands 8 Prison Guards all gripping their batons in the hands, while armed with grimacing smiles. Fade out. ========================================================= We fade in to the pleasant Southern Californian home as the white limo of Yama Tatsu’s and Kyle Thorne. It comes to a halt in the driveway and the two men exit the vehicle. Several neighbourhood kids who were out playing together run to the roadside to look at the Limo and the people that have been driving in it. Yama Tatsu: Come lets go inside away from these annoying children… The two of them quickly enter the humble abode that is Yama Tatsu’s suburban home. The shock that waits is completely unexpected. Yama’s house is blanketed in Bouquets of Flowers. Kyle Thorne: If I knew you were this kinda guy I think I would have rather stayed in my cell! This joke doesn’t go down well with Yama. He sees his answering machine button flashing. He hits it hard in anger. The following message is played “Hey Baby, I hope you don’t mind I redecorated our lovely home? I tried to check to see if you were ok on Lockdown, but those dam guards got in the way! Never mind, I might see you tonight, sweet dreams…” A shocked look comes across Yama’s face, but this quickly changes to anger! He picks up a nearby vase of flowers and throws it at the wall. The vase and flowers spread over the wall at impact like the shrapnel of a Grenade on a Battlefield. Yama Tatsu: That dam woman! Who is she!? Kyle Thorne: You got stalker problems don’t you? Yama Tatsu: How could you tell? Kyle Thorne: I have a nose for these things… But no seriously, if you got stalker problems I know the best way to get rid of them! Yama Tatsu: How? Kyle Thorne: The easiest way is a 12 Gauge Shot gun and an invitation for a romantic cruise on a Yacht! Take the Yacht out to the middle of the harbour and blow the bitches brains out! Yama Tatsu: I like your style! But no… I have no idea who she is, where she comes from or even what see looks like… Kyle Thorne: Well I’m stumped, but she can’t be that much of a problem really Yama Tatsu: You don’t call this a problem!?!? Kyle Thorne: Point taken… Yama Tatsu: Wanna give me a quick hand? The two mean start picking up the bouquets of flowers and carrying them out to the pool. There they just throw them into the pool without a care. This is repeated till they have clear the living room. Yama Tatsu: Now that’s over we can talk some further business! You see the reason I looked for you is because you are one of the toughest men to ever grace the Squared circle! You and me can take this Federation by storm man… but for my plan to work I need your full cooperation! Kyle Thorne: Trust me man, you got it! I’ve seen the way you fight, I don’t know if there is anyone I’d rather have as a partner than you! Yama Tatsu: Good, because I will need your help this coming Thursday! I am on double duty and my final match is a very important one! I really need to win that one! I will be up against Scorpion, a very fierce competitor! I know I can beat him but the extra security of you watching my back will come in hand. I am in a Tag Team match with Koriann against Blayze and Jason Dread. I don’t really wish to be partnered with such a pathetic competitor but I will no doubt win that match also! You see I am the finest wrestler in CCW! I will prove that this Thursday and the following Thursday and so on and so on! I am truly a force to be reckoned with and with you as my team mate we will run dominate this fed! Kyle Thorne: Your dam right man! We will own! Kyle’s stomach begins to grumble. An embarrassed look comes across his face. Yama gives him a sympathetic look. Yama Tatsu: Come on man, lets go get something to eat! I bet you haven’t eaten jack in like months! Kyle Thorne: You have no idea! The two men stand and stretch out. They walk out of the house and as the front door slams shut we fade to black… View Yama Tatsu 's Biography Back
