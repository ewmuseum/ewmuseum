KoE - Rookies Beware

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A promo roleplay in which The Supreme Machine warns Bojangles the Clown and Yama Tatsu before their King of Extreme match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: KoE.....Rookies Beware Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 13:32:39 Location: This is a: Match RP The Supreme Machine stands alone in his private training facility, his arms crossed over his massive chest. His long black hair is tied neatly back as he stares menacingly down the camera. Bojangles the Clown and Yama Tatsu. It appears that the two of you have the misfortune of facing me this week at LDT in the first round of the King of Extreme tournament. The camera slowly begins zooming in on the 5 time Heavyweight Champion. I understand that you both want to make a name for yourselves here in CCW, that's only natural. I also realise that you will both be gunning for me this week, that too, is only natural. But I feel it only fair to warn you...I plan on being in the final of this tournament....and you don't have to be a rocket scientist to figure out what that means. Yama Tatsu, you've already had the misfortune of being in the same ring as me. I shouldn't have to remind you what happened. You may have a bright future in CCW but that is in the future. For now, I want you to remember how it felt to be thrown around like a rag-doll. How it felt to be totally overpowered, unable to defend yourself against an overwelming force. Well that's nothing compared to how you'll feel after this Thursday. The last time we met was just a warm up for the real thing, and believe me, this time it's going to be for real. I didn't come back to CCW to be defeated by a couple of rookies, and I don't mean any offense by saying that, I'm just stating the facts. The camera continues the slow zoom. Bojangles, the same goes for you. Unlike Yama, you and I are yet to meet face to face, but I also want you to think about how you're going to feel come Friday morning when you wake up and your body is bruised and broken. Think long and hard rookies. Now The Supreme Machine's hardened features fill the screen. You think you two are extreme enough to beat me? You think you've got what it takes to bring me down? You think you're going to make a name for yourselves by beating the legend? Well, come Thursday night you two rookies are going to be famous alright....just not for the reasons you think. This Thursday night the two of you are going to learn a long, hard lesson. I hope you take notes because I don't want to have to show either of you again. Good luck rookies. You're going to need it.
