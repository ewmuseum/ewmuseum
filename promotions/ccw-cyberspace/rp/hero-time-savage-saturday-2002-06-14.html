Hero Time (Savage Saturday)

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which the Masked Mullet debuts as a self-styled superhero in San Francisco and wrongly attacks an undercover officer.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Masket Mullet Entitled: Hero Time (Savage Saturday) Written in Friday, Jun 14, 2002, 21:35:47 Location: San Francisco, CA This is a: Match RP Hero Time The air was cold.. and the city was dark. It was basically, a cold and dark city, the city of San Francisco. Now, was the time, that the world's newest superhero, The Masked Mullet, would be making his hero debut to the world. The Masked Mullet: I hope I aint treadin' on anyones toes.. being in their city.. lemme check the list.. Spiderman.. New York. Batman.. Gothem.. Superman.. Metroplis..Rent-A-Hero.. PCW. Hmm.. cleared all the basics. The Masked Mullet scratches his cheek, as he scopes out of his surroundings. His spot on the fire escape, gave him a good perspective on a dark ally way. The chilling wind blew through, and he wrapped his cape, made from old heavy metal t-shirts, tighter around him. The Masked Mullet: Damn.. this never happens ta Spiderman..or superman! My Mullet Senses must not be workin'.. Clem pulls his hand back, and slaps himself upside the head. The Masked Mullet: WORK DAMN YOU!! *SMACK!!* *SMACK!!* A woman and a man walk slowly into the alley, and begin to kiss, another man follows after, all of them are ignoring the crazyman dressed up as a superhero. Clem is completely oblivious to those below him, in the alley. Man #1: Hey baby.. ready to have a good time? Woman: Yes, are you ready to have the time of your life? You got the money..? Man #2: HALT! Man #1: Shit!! The Masked Mullet's head pops up, and he slowly looks down to see the second man pointing a gun at the couple. The Masked Mullet: Whoa.. a crime! S'time for me to.. bust it up!! YEEEHAW!!! With that battle cry said, Clem backs up, and attempts to jump off of the safty handle of the fire escape, his foot slips due to the dampness of the night, and he slips, falling head first towards the ground. The second man is slowly walking forward, when the would be super hero crashes down onto him, sending the gun sliding across the pavement. The un-harmed Clem immediately pops to his feet, and looks around, trying to figure out what happened... and he looks at the man at his feet. Woman: WHAT ARE YOU DOING!? The Masked Mullet: Don't worry!! I just used my super jumpin' powers ta get the jump on him! The first man, blinks.. then dives for the gun.. The Masked Mullet: Don't worry ma'am! I'll save ya! The Masked Mullet rushes forward, and drives his fist forward, and sends the man back into a trashcan. Man#1: You psycho!! What do you think you're doing?! The Masked Mullet: YEEEEEEEEHAW!! Savin' the day!! That's what I'm doin'! Woman: You're not.. The Masked Mullet, continues to thrash upon the man.. with punches, kicks.. and finishes him off with a Mullet Buster. The man lays there stunned, as Clem places his foot on the guys chest, and puts his hands on his hips, and grins. The Masked Mullet: I have saved you miss! With my Mullet powers.. I can beat anyone.. anytime.. anywhere! YEEEEEEEEEHAW!! I can save everyone in CCW now!! Vega and Angelica Lynn will be no match.. their evil powers.. will face.. THE WHITE TRASH FIST OF JUSTICE!! Woman: YOU DOLT!! You beat up my partner!! I'm an undercover cop!! Clem's eyes widen, and he slowly takes his foot off the man, and starts backing up. Straight into the man he fell upon before. Woman: Sam.. cuff the two.. one for soliciting a prositute.. the other for attacking a police officer.. disrupting an investigation.. and insanity. Sam: Right Maggie.. He reaches down and grabs Clem's arm.. The Masked Mullet: But.. I.. sheeeeeeeeeeeeee-it.. LOOK!! Sam: Do you really think I'd fall for that, sir? The Masked Mullet takes a step back, and stomps on the officer's foot, then takes off running. Sam: OWW!! HALT SIR!! YOU ARE RESISTING ARREST!! Maggie: Who the hell was that masked man?! And this is where our story ends.. for now.. [fin] View Biography
