Do You Believe In Miracles?

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Do You Believe In Miracles? 'The Angel of Death' Enigma CCW Promo #73 Date: Location: (We go back in time, to Lockdown last week... We see Enigma layed out on the canvas, blood trickling down his forhead after Blayze nailed him in the head with the camera... The shot of Enigma slowly begins to fade out and now the scene changes... We are in what seems to be a washroom... Enigma stands before a mirror, wearing nothing but a towel around his waist... He just stares into the mirror with an evil smirk on his face...) Enigma: When a shark tastes blood, its instict to kill reacts... The shark realizes that the kill is near... On LDT, I tasted blood, that of my own blood.... And it ignited something inside of me... A desire.... A thrist for blood... (Enigma just stares into the mirror... He touched his forhead, and he rubs the scars... Enigma seems as if he's in his own world) Enigma: My hatred has grown stronger... And those that have crossed my path shall suffer mercilessly... Does Blayze think I'm just gonna let him get away with what he did? If thats what he thinks, he will be sadly mistaken.... (Enigma walks out of the washroom and he lays down on top of his bed staring at the cieling... Enigma's eyes begin to slowly close... Enigma is back to his feet... He looks at the clock) Enigma: Oh fuck, I'm late.... (Enigma quickly in the matter of seconds throws some clothes on, puts on his boots, his black leather sleevless coat and he walks out the door... Enigma walks down the hallway, and it seems as if he is walking in slow motion... The elevator down the hall seems a mile away... Enigma begins to walk faster... Enigma than begins to jog... Now he is sprinting down the hallway...And as Enigma is running, he comes to a full stop... He takes a few steps back and he notices a door that reads 'Staircase'... Enigma opens the door and as he steps through his eyes light up with rage... He sees Blayze leaning up against the wall at the bottom of the staircase) Blayze: Did you miss me??? (Enigma runs down the staircase but as soon as Enigma makes his first step, Blayze has already begun to run down the stairs... The chase continues all the way to the bottom of the stairs... Blayze runs out the exit to outside the building... Enigma follows right after... He looks around and Blayze is gone) Enigma: Where the hell did he go? (Enigma seems confused... He looks around, everwhere but Blayze is nowhere to be found... Its almost as if he vanished into thin air... Enigma seems to be shocked at how quicly Blayze dis-appeared... Enigma begins to slowly walk down the street thinking to hismelf) Enigma: I dont understand, how did he just dis-appear so quickly like that... (As Enigma lights up a cigarette he stops dead in his tracks... Enigma turns around and he looks into a store... Behind a glass wall display are many Televison sets... Blayze is on every single TV... Enigma's eyes once again light up, filled with rage...) Blayze: Enigma, you dont stand a chance against me... I'm the CCW World Heavywieght Champion... I'm the Black Jesus... And on LDT, I'm gonna finish what Spider and Neurosis started... I'm gonna end you're career once and for all... (Enigma punches the glass display window, shattering it in a million peices... Enigma looks down and blood begins to gush out of his knuckles... Enigma then rises out of his bed... Sweat pours down his forhead... He's now realized that it was all just a dream... He gets up and goes back into the washroom and he washes his face as he looks into the mirror, he notices a man standing behind him... Its Blayze... Enigma quickly turns around, but yet no-one is there... Out of frustration Enigma punches the mirror shattering it) Enigma: There's another seven year of bad luck... (Enigma's right hand begins to bleed... But he does nothing about it...) Enigma: Blayze, on LDT I tasted blood... That of my own, and now its time that you get a little taste of you're own medicine... And I have the perfect opportunity to do so... Hardcore #1 contenders match... Its gonna be myself, Forme Carlos, Michael McTavish, Jack Manson, Gobber, and you Blayze... Now you all know as well as I know that I couldn't care less about andbody else in this match except you Blayze... You're the one that I want... Its you're blood that I want on my knuckles... And I'll go through anyone that dares stand before me to get you Blayze... And I dont suggest anybody try and stop me for you're own personal well being... (The blood from Enigma's hand is beginning to drip to the floor... Enigma just look at the floor and smiles) Enigma: As Lockdown comes closer and closer, I get excited... I get goosebumps just thinking about it... I mean, I usually get excited about any Hardcore match I'm in... But this time its different... I'm gonna enjoy watching you suffer Blayze... I've finally gotten my opportunity to face you... There is no more running, there is no more hiding... Now it time to serperate the boys from the men... (It seems as though Enigma's hand seems to be bleeding more profusley... But still Enigma does nothing about it) Enigma: 'The Angel Of Death' has risen once more to seek vegance upon one man... And Blayze, it is only a matter of time before I make you pay for betraying me... I see right through you... All this shit about you thinking you're a 'Black Jesus'... You are no savior... You are not the messiah... You're just a fake... You're nothing more than a paper champion... On LDT, you said my days are numbered... Well you're mistaken Blayze... It is you're days which are numbered... And there isn't a think you can do about it... Black Jesus??? (laughs)... You're gonna need a miracle to make it out of LDT alive... So start praying now, and maybe if you're lucky you're prayers will be answered... (As Enigma finishes those last words, he looks at the ground... Enigma crouches down and he seems to be doing something, but we cant seem to see what he's doing do to the the camera angle... Enigma then gets up and he walks out of the room... The camera then pans over to the ground.. As we zoom in closer to the ground, written in blood on the floor we see 'The Black Jesus Shall Be Crucified'... The camera then slowly fades to black) View 'The Angel of Death' Enigma 's Biography Back
