Stormclouds Gathering part 7 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #79 Date: 03/11/03 Location: Seattle, Washington Inside a local but private gym, two warriors face each other from across a ring. On the one side is the beautifully talented Lacey Skye Morris. On the other side is the now mysterious and slightly out of practice Christine Oakes.... Damsel. They both breath heavily as they circle each other in the middle of the ring, sweat glistening off their bodies and dampening their training clothes. They've been going at each other now for approximately two hours.... starting just before sunrise. Their attacks have been fierce, yet restrained.... Almost like two sisters who haven't seen each other in a long time, physically working out their differences and each other. With nearly lightning speed, Damsel moves in on Lacey and hip tosses her to the ground. The air explodes from Lacey's lungs, and she takes a moment to regroup before rolling back to her feet. Damsel watches all the while, smirking. Damsel: Even for someone who wrestles constantly, you have an amazing amount of stamina, Lacey. It's been two hours, and I'm nearly exhausted. Lacey: {slightly out of breath} Some people greet the sunrise with yoga. I do this. Lacey lunges forward, grabbing Damsel and arm dragging her to the canvas. Damsel rolls to her feet, leaps for Lacey, and is quickly stopped and put down by a spinebuster. Damsel looks up at Lacey from her spot in the ring, flat on her back. Damsel: That was a good move. It's no wonder you always used to beat me back in the day. Lacey: By my count, you gave me a run for my money on more than one occasion. {suddenly serious} Now, you know I have to find Steven. He's made it very clear that he wants me now, instead of Jonathan. Damsel gets up and they go another round with each other. Damsel shoots out with several high kicks at Lacey's head. Lacey ducks one and then blocks the other. She retaliates with a kick of her own at Damsel's stomach. Damsel catches her foot and drops Lacey with an Enzugiri. Damsel grabs Lacey by the hair and brings her back to her feet. Damsel: You think you can handle him? Lacey: No problem. And Damsel whips Lacey across the ring and into the ropes. Damsel sets herself and knocks Lacey flat onto her back with a clothesline. She puts her foot on the strawberry blonde's chest and looks down into her eyes. Damsel: Are you sure about that. Lacey: {wincing} Yes. I need to protect myself and Jonathan. Damsel reaches down and pulls Lacey to her feet.... Inside the antique shop, Jonathan is working in his studio. He seems almost dazed, in a trance, as he goes about his work. Lacey and Damsel soon enter, having cleaned themselves up and changed clothes since their early morning training session. Lacey immediately notices Jonathan's behavior. Lacey: Jonathan.... What's wrong? Jonathan: Steven Qaurtre called. He falls silent. Lacey senses that something more is bothering him. Lacey: What did he say to you, Jon? Jonathan: He'll be on Soldier Bridge tonight. He wants you to go to him. He said it'll be easier that way, and that you won't like it if he has to come after you. Lacey: That wasn't all, was it? Jonathan: {shakes head} When he's 'finished' with you.... He'll be back to finish the job with me. Damsel and Lacey exchange a look, knowing that the end game is quickly approaching.... View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
