Lacey Skye - Mother

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Lacey Skye telephones her mother and asks about a birthmark and the people behind her family’s troubles.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lacey Skye Entitled: Mother Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 20:43:20 Location: Morris Residence, California This is a: Match RP Lacey: **voice over** I'm almost there.... It's been a long and hard road, but I'm almost there. I survived a month full of unscrupulous matches wherein I was attacked by people who weren't involved in the first place. I survived several face-to-face confrontations with the devil, herself. And just recently, I survived a gigantic tournament involving the entire roster. And although I didn't make it to the end, I tried my best. But more importantly.... My family managed to survive. I was almost sure that they were goners once Devyl had her sights set on them. But my husband, my brother, my mom.... They all survived the rocky road that had been placed in front of them. And now, on the eve of the battle which will determine the fate of the CCW Women's division. I find myself looking back towards.... Home. **Inside a simple kitchen, Claudia Morris busies herself with putting together the evenings meal. She finishes cutting up and washing some carrots when the telephone rings. Quickly drying off her hands, she sets everything aside and answers the phone.** Claudia: Hello? Lacey: **on the phone** Hi mom. Claudia: Lacey!? Lacey: **smiles** Yeah, it's me. Claudia: Why are you....? I didn't expect to hear from you for a long while! Is everything okay? Lacey: Yeah, everything's fine on my end. Claudia: You said that you wouldn't be calling unless you fixed everything.... Did it happen? Lacey: Not yet, mom. But soon. Tomorrow night as a matter of fact. Claudia: That's great! Does that mean you'll be coming back home soon? Lacey: There's a good chance of it. If I can win the title away from Devyl tomorrow night, everything will go a long way towards being fixed. **changing subjects quickly** How's Time doing? Is he back home now? Claudia: Yes. His surgery was a big success! He's been home now for the past two weeks, and he still watches you every night CCW is on the air. Lacey: Is he feeling better? Claudia: He has some chronic aches and pains, but he's definitely through the worst of it. The doctors say that he will eventually be able to walk again, but he'll never get his full mobility back. He'll need to use crutches for a long while, and at best, will still need a cane to support himself. Lacey: Aren't you supposing quite a lot there, mom? Claudia: Yes.... I'm just trying to remain optimistic, that's all. Lacey: I know.... I know this is hard.... And I have something to tell you.... I know the identity of the person who got Tim involved in the car crash. **There's stunned silence from Claudia for several seconds.** Lacey: Mom? Claudia: Yeah, I'm here.... Did you say you know who hurt Tim? Lacey: Yes. **sighs** His name is Mabius Raymond. He's the head Talent Coordinator here in CCW. Claudia: You're telling me that one of the CCW officials tried to murder your brother? Lacey: That's exactly what I'm saying. Claudia: But.... Why!? That doesn't make any sense! Why would anyone from CCW want to hurt Tim? Lacey: He's been working with Devyl for the past several months now. It was meant as a strike against me. Claudia: You can't really believe that? Lacey: I'm sorry mom, but it's the truth. Claudia: But.... Lacey: I don't want to believe it either, but it happened. And while we're on the topic of unbelievable events, there's something else I have to ask you. This might sound really strange, but I need to ask it anyway.... **There's a long and pregnant pause as Claudia waits for her only daughter to ask the question. The pause frightens her a bit. Lacey is usually very straightforward with everything.** Claudia: Honey....? Lacey: Mom.... Am I adopted? Claudia: **another shocked pause** What makes you ask something like that!? Lacey: I'm just curious.... It would answer some questions I have.... Claudia: I don't know what's gotten into you, but you were definitely NOT! I gave birth to you, just as I did your three brothers before you! Lacey: I'm sorry, mom. I just had to ask. Claudia: Why!? What brought this on? Lacey: The birthmark that I have, mom.... It perfectly matches one that another person has. And since that's unheard of in the annals of natural science.... Claudia: Lacey.... You are my daughter. You were not adopted. What's this birthmark nonsense about, anyway? Lacey: It's.... Nothing, mom. It's just a wild theory I'm trying to follow up on. **pause** I love you. Claudia: I love you, too. Stay in touch, okay? Lacey: Will do. Bye. Claudia: Bye. **Claudia hangs up the phone and goes back to her carrots. What silly nonsense was Lacey involved in now? And what did her birthmark have anything to do with anything? Lacey was looking a bit too far into things that weren't there, that's all. She should be concentrating more on.... And then Claudia freezes at the kitchen counter, a sliver of memory creeping back into her consciousness. She remembered something from the day of Lacey's birth that bothered her at the time, and then forgot completely about it. She remembered giving birth to a beautiful baby girl, and having the doctor taking her away to get cleaned up. She could have sworn.... That before the doctor had taken her baby away.... There was NO birthmark on her lower back.... But when the doctor brought her back.... Claudia quickly clears away the thoughts with a shake of her head and goes back to her carrots. Thinking like that was crazy....** Lacey: **voice over** I need to find something.... Some concrete proof that what Joyce Devins told me is going to work. Meeting her as I did in her old house.... I'm coming to think that maybe I just imagined the whole thing. My nerves were high-strung as it was. Trouble is, I really don't believe that. I know that I saw her. And now I have to find Devyl's missing sister and figure out what this birth mark means. But first, I need to beat her. If I can take away her belt, the thing that seems to feed her ego, I can get a better edge over her and gain some power of my own. And then.... Maybe I can figure everything out. My path before me is set clearly. I know that my family is all right, and I also know that I'm in for one of the toughest battles of my life on Lockdown. Devyl.... Get ready.... View Biography
