CCW Stocks Up 16.7%

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCW Stocks Up 16.7% The Gobstopper CCW Promo #14 Date: 2/17/03 Location: Washington, D.C. *Scene opens to show the Washington Monument rising high above the nation's capital on a cold and extremely snowy day. The skies dim the city with a grayish shadow as crews work into the late afternoon trying to clear Constitution Avenue after getting blanketed with nearly two feet of snow. People are out frollicking on the Mall, building snowmen, creating forts, having snowball fights... and there is one man dressed in a leather jacket and business slacks watching from a distance while chowing down a bagel from one of the few brave street vendors who opened shop on this blistery day. The man finishes his bagel then opens his copy of the President's Day Wall Street Journal he had tucked away in his jacket.* The Gobstopper: 'Ah well, it looks like the CCW's stock rose nearly %17 after my return to CCW and my placement on the financial board. But I can't help to realize that our union rep, Brad Lamen, is fining people a mere $10,000 for laying waste to an official of CCW. Guy, come on now, if you want the lessons to be learned the hard way, you need to up the ante. Such an elite federation like CCW pays even the low card guys a nice chunk of change and the fine you levied on that poor rookie is chump change. I'm talking, break the intolerable. 30, no maybe even 40 k's would have been appropriate. CCW was on a downslide in terms of market value, and now that I'm here and on the financial team, I intend to put more money into CCW and help this federation grow to heights much greater than even in the glory days of this sport. The first problem we have is we're not getting the ratings. I mean, how can we with guys like Brad Lamen making a joke of a position where he's suppose to represent the people who are trying to make CCW a better place? He disgusts me and I feel to cut costs, he should be the first to go. Not only does he suck goat cheese, he can't wrestle worth a damn. It's good to know they want me back in the ring and how appropriate than to face me against Brad Lamen. Too bad the match won't receive very high ratings due to my opponent, but I will do what I can to better the evening for all. And in my quest to return CCW to it's once level of greatness, I have enlisted the services of a man whom many of you knew very well and the very same man who is 'rumored' to bring CCW out of the shit hole it has fallen deeply into. Together, we will route the CCW of those who cause a downfall such as Brad Lamen and Sylkk, and we will be victorious not just this Thursday, but for every day for the foreseeable future.' *Gobstopper places his paper back into his jacket and tips the vendor. He walks off through the snow to join the snowy fun on a day of work cancelled due to weather.* View The Gobstopper 's Biography Back
