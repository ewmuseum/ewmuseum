Brad Lamen is God?

24 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Brad Lamen is God? Koriann CCW Promo #31 Date: 02/24/03 Location: An airport somewhere... [The scene opens up on an airport. Which one, exactly, is pretty irrelevant. Seeing as how Koriann is here, she expects everything else to be pretty much irrelevant. However, CCW's 'Greatest Heavyweight Champion of all time' isn't having a very good day. She's currently stuck in the baggage retrieval area because... Well, if you listen in, you'll hear her explain it pretty well...] Koriann: *talking to anyone who will listen* 'What's wrong with airports these days? I mean we were delayed for ten hours at the first airport, put up with a senseless terrorist scare while we were in the air, were routed to another airport, where we picked up several more passengers for our already overcrowded flight, flew through what was most probably the worst turbulence to ever exist in the history of flight, landed here at this airport, and we're all quickly told that our luggage has been misplaced! Now, for people who get paid to do a job, these airline people couldn't tell competence from their own ass. And now, since I'm about 18 hours late, I seriously doubt Brad 'FUCKING' Lamen has decided to wait around here to meet me when I got off the plane. *to a passing family* Hey, didn't you guys hear me? I said I was waiting for Brad 'FUCKING' Lamen! Father: 'Who are you? And why are you talking so loudly?' Koriann: 'I'm Koriann, one of the greatest wrestlers to ever grace CCW! You remember me... I was all over the news... On all the major networks... When I won the CCW Heavyweight title! Remember? That was me! Me, me, me!' Mother: 'Oh, we don't watch wrestling. It's stupid. Right, kids?' Brother and Sister: 'Yes, mom.' Koriann: 'Hey, kids, I hope you realize that your ‘parents', if that's indeed who they are really are, are brainwashing you. EVERYBODY likes wrestling. And if they say they don't, they're either lying or have the intelligence of some stupid animal... Like a dolphin or something.' Brother: 'My mommy says dolphins are smart.' Koriann: 'Your mommy probably also says that you're smart.' Brother: 'She does and I am!' Koriann: 'Oh yeah? What's two hundred times twenty?' Brother: 'It's...' Koriann: *interrupting* 'See? You're such a stupid little shit! The answer is four hundred!' Brother: 'Actually, it's four THOUSAND! Stupid!' [Koriann is about to lunge forward and throttle the kid when the father quickly drags him away.] Koriann: 'Yeah, that's right! Run home and cry to momma! I could kick all of your asses! I'm a professional wrestler, dammit!' Voice: *from behind* 'Well... ‘Professional' and ‘wrestler' are two words that should never be used in the same sentence... Especially when describing yourself.' [Koriann whips around and glares at the young man who just mouthed off to here. He's dressed all in black, lounging up against one of the glass walls. He'd be considered attractive if you're into that kind of thing.] Koriann: 'Just who the hell are you, Guttermouth?' Man: 'Well... Sadly... I'm your guardian angel.' Koriann: 'You're my what!?' Man: 'Listen...' *sighs* 'Never mind. I'm just here to tell you that you've been chosen for an incredibly important, life-altering event. One that will change life on this planet as we know it... An event so powerful that generations of people will talk about it and your role in it...' Koriann: 'Really? You're an angel, and you have this quest for me?' Man: 'Yup.' Koriann: 'Well? What is it? Tell me, tell me, tell me!' Man: 'Geez... Calm down. Excited much?' Koriann: 'Just get on with it and tell me! I never get holy quests.' Man: 'Alright... You have been chosen... to...' Koriann: 'Yes? Get on with it!' Man: *deep breath* 'Say that you're sorry to that family you just insulted.' [Long pause.} Koriann: 'WHAT!?' Man: *puts up his hands in a calming gesture* 'You heard me. You need to...' Koriann: *interrupting* 'NO WAY! Koriann doesn't DO ‘apologies'!' Man: 'If you don't, the world will end.' Koriann: 'Yeah, right! Good one... You can't fool me. I'm far too smart for trickery like that.' Man: 'If you don't believe me, find Brad Lamen. All will be made clear when you see his face.' [The man turns to leave after giving his 'cryptic' statement.] Koriann: 'Wait a minute... Is Brad Lamen God?' Man: *turns back* 'Excuse me?' Koriann: 'Is Brad Lamen God? You said that I need to find him, and that all will be made clear when I see his face. I hear religious people saying that we need to find Jesus, and that we will know everything once we see God's face.' Man: 'Hell, no! I just meant that...' Koriann: 'Too late! The secret's out now, pal! I knew it! Brad Lamen really IS God! I need to find his punk ass and kick it for lying to me for so long! [Koriann, instantly forgetting her luggage, runs off.] Man: 'No! Wait! I...' *sighs and looks skyward* 'Lord, why me...?' [fade out...] View Koriann 's Biography Back
