THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN: PART II

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN: PART II Koriann CCW Promo #74 Date: 03/11/03 Location: Who knows... Probably Chicago or something. We once again, much to all of our mutual dismay, join up with Koriann in her hunt for Brad Lamen, aka God. Well, Koriann thinks he's God anyway, due to the fact that a man calling himself her Guardian Angel informed her that she had to apologize and... *sigh* Never mind. I'm not going to reprise all this. Let's just say that Koriann and her newfound compatriot... Bus Driver Bob (No relation to Homeless Bob... I think)... Are heading down one of the residential side streets, looking for Brad Lamen's current whereabouts. And that's where we will pick up this charade of a 'quest'. Bob: Wow... God lives in this neighborhood? I thought he could do better, seeing as how he is God and all. Koriann: I'd watch my tongue if I were you... You saw how that uppity tourist-guide-bitch paid for her sacrilege. Bob: Yeah, but... Come on! If you were God, wouldn't you want better digs than some of these rat-infested holes? Koriann: Well... Yes. But you're missing the point! I think God is trying to keep a low profile. He's tired of people bitching and moaning to him about all of their pathetic problems every day, so he came down here to earth and merely adopted the guise of Brad Lamen. That way, he doesn't have to deal with anybody! Bob: So... You're saying that God is in human form right now... And doesn't want to put up with anybody? That's kinda sad. Knowing this, how come you don't get mad when you pray to Him for guidance? Koriann: Pppphhhht! What are you, stupid? There are two things that Koriann doesn't do. Number one: I don't apologize. And number two: I don't pray! Praying's for pussies. Bob: I'm confused... You know God, but you don't pray to him, and you're trying to find him, because some guy told you that... Koriann: Listen, chumley... The announcer guy at the very beginning said he wasn't going to reprise anything, so I don't think you should either. All you need to know is that God and I hang out together, and he's cool with me because I'm never pathetically begging him for a favor. Bob: And what are you going to do when you find Him? Koriann: Look at His face, of course! I follow my holy quests to the letter! The two seekers soon pause in front of one of the dirty buildings. Koriann takes a piece of paper out of her pocket, unfolds it, and compares the numbers on the paper to the numbers on the building. Koriann: Well... Looks like we're here. Bob: Are you sure? God lives inside a tobacco shop? Koriann: No, stupid! He's renting out the space ABOVE the tobacco shop! Now, why don't you make yourself useful by stealing us some wheels so's we have transportation, and I'll go up and carefully look at Brad's face. Bob: But... But... But... I want to meet God, too! Koriann: No. First you have to prove yourself worthy by stealing a car. Now go. Bob: But isn't stealing a sin? It's one of the ten commandments! Koriann: *rolling her eyes* No! Those were only rules that were given to the stupid, un-evolved people back in the day when they cut grass with gardening shears because they couldn't figure out how to use their lawnmowers. Now go steal a car! I bet God would find it pretty cool if you were able to find a good one. Bob: Alrighty then! I won't let God down! Bob goes running off, looking for a nice car to steal. Koriann chuckles to herself. Koriann: What a dope! He actually bought that story? Everybody knows that they didn't have garden shears back then! They had to pull the grass up by hand! *sighs and smiles* Ah, stupid old, dead people... If only they knew that the lawnmowers needed gas. And on that less-than-intelligent note, Koriann turns and enters the building. And here's where we mercifully end this segment of Koriann's search for Brad Lamen. Thank God...
