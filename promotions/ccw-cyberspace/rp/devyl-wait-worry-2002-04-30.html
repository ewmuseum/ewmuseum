Devyl - Wait... Worry...

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Devyl and Mr. Raymond discuss her title defence against Lacey Skye and arrange the match officials.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: Wait... Worry... Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 22:05:36 Location: Aurora, IL This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on the familiar sight of Devyl's mansion in Aurora, Illinois. The mood around the place seems... Lighter. But not by much. Devyl and Mr. Raymond are up on the balcony overlooking the extensive backyard. Raymond is leaning on the balcony, head bowed and looking down while Devyl stands behind him near the doorway. A long silence stretches out between the two, nothing but the sound of the wind playing across the tree branches and the eves is heard. There is obvious tension between them, and for the reasons behind that tension...] Raymond: 'I didn't plan on this, you know? I thought for sure that you were going to get out of defending your title against Lacey.' Devyl: 'This is a problem, Raymond. You told me that I WASN'T going to have to defend my title. I was going to give your wife back to you. And now...' Raymond: 'I'll take care of it, okay? There's always something I can do. I'm the talent coordinator for Christ's sake! I can make an amendment to the match.' Devyl: 'But you can't get me out of it?' Raymond: 'No.' *turns around to look at Devyl* 'And why is this bothering you so much anyway?' Devyl: 'MY husband... He's still doing his best to shake the poison that Gabriel Nyte tried to instill within him. That is what's bothering me. In fact, it's bothering me so much that I can't rightfully concentrate on all that is going on. David spent several days in hell. Did you know that?' Raymond: 'No.' Devyl: 'And now he's back, claiming that he made a deal with Satan himself. Honestly, I don't like that Raymond. I wanted to keep him safe from all of that, but... After the intolerable interference from Gabriel Nyte... David was corrupted.' Raymond: 'At least you have him back.' Devyl: *faint smile* 'Yes. I do. And he will NEVER be taken away from me again.' Raymond: 'But he seems changed... More confident and full of himself...' Devyl: 'A trip to hell will do that to you. You either grow stronger to survive, or you get destroyed. David got stronger.' Raymond: 'And all this is seriously distracting you?' Devyl: 'Yes. I don't know the severity or complexity of what he went through. We haven't had a chance to really talk since we got back from New York.' Raymond: 'Are you bringing him with us to Philadelphia?' Devyl: 'If he wants to go. And I think he does.' Raymond: 'Well, I'm going to solve your match anxiety problem for you right now.' Devyl: 'How so?' Raymond: 'I think we need a special trouble-shooting referee for this match. You know... Make sure that everything goes fairly. I would be the perfect candidate for the job, but I can't do it because I'm already taking my place in your corner. Instead, I think that would be the perfect job for your husband!' Devyl: 'David as the trouble-shooting referee?' *smiles* 'I like that a lot! But I still have to deal with the ringer ref in the ring...' Raymond: 'No, I'm taking care of that too. I know Urban Outlaw has been reading the official's rulebook recently, and I bet he would LOVE the chance to be the special ref for the match.' Devyl: *big smile* 'Mr. Raymond... I like the way you think!' Raymond: 'And after you beat Lacey and send her to the bottom of the women's division... I get my wife back!' Devyl: 'That's correct.' [Together, both Devyl and Raymond exit the balcony and walk back inside the house. The starts to fade to black as...] Devyl: *voice over, whisper* 'Lacey... You can't stop it...' [fade out...] View Biography
