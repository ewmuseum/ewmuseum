Suspicions Begin

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Christine Oakes and Mike Lubis walk through New York talking about her upcoming tag match and his secrecy.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: Suspicions begin Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 00:40:48 Location: New York, NY This is a: Match RP *New York, New York; Thursday Morning. Christine Oakes and Mike Lubis are walking down one of the many city streets, doing nothing more exciting than window-shopping and enjoying each other’s company........ Or trying to. It is rather obvious that there is a bit of tension between them. And, as always, communication gaps are the primary cause........* Christine- So that’s pretty much what happened........ Regina beat the crap out of me and Devyl took out Lacey once again. It wasn’t a very good night........ Especially with happened to Benny Blades at the end of the show. That just wasn’t right. Mike- Nope. But he knew the risks he was going to take before going into that match. We all do. It was just shit-bad luck for him on Monday, that’s all. Christine- I still haven’t heard word on what’s happened to him, either. Of course, I really haven’t been keeping in touch from anybody in CCW these past few days either. The only people I’ve seen have been the trainers, and they’ve cleared me to wrestle on Thursday night. Mike- You have a match? Christine- Yeah. Another tag team match-up. Ever since Akiko left, I haven’t been very good at those. *weak smile* I guess that shows who was carrying the team, huh? Mike- *annoyed sigh* Enough of that self-pity crap. Who’s your partner? Christine- Angelica Lynn. Mike- And who are you facing? Christine- Koriann and Regina Winters. Mike- Hmph. And what’s your game plan? Christine- Well, despite my best efforts, Regina has been getting the one-up on me. She seems to be prepared for most of my moves. She countered one of my Arm drags on SMN........ Shocked the heck out of me! I know it’s not a very powerful move, but I can usually get them off pretty quick. Regina somehow was ready for it, though. Mike- Sounds to me like she’s been studyin’ up on ya. You need to hit her with a couple of surprise moves........ Something you’ve never tried before. Christine- Obviously. I still haven’t quite figured why she’s targeting me, yet. Mike- *shrugs* Who knows why? Sometimes we just have to let those questions lie and concentrate on the bigger picture. If you take out Regina, nice and hard, maybe she’ll leave you alone. Christine- *sounding doubtful* Maybe........ *The two of them walk along in silence for a bit, passing fancy window displays, pedestrians, beat cops, and loads upon loads of traffic.* Christine- So why are you here, Mike? I was very surprised when you knocked on my door this morning. Mike- Oh, you know........ I just wanted to surprise my lady and spend some time with her. Christine- *nods* I see. I was coming back to Seattle after Group Therapy. Why didn’t you wait until then? I know that you’re money situations kinda tight........ Mike- Don’t worry about it. You’re more important then my money situation. Christine- *smiles* Yeah, right! So, what kind of wonderful day do you have planned for us? Mike- Well, I’m going to take you out to lunch, drop you off at your hotel so you can get some training done this afternoon, and then I’ll catch up with you tonight before Lockdown. Christine- *frowns* You’re not going to train with me? Mike- Nah. I got some business to attend to. Christine- Business? Mike- Yeah, but don’t worry about it. It’s nothin’ important. Christine- *sighs* Why won’t you ever tell me anything........ Where you go, what you do........? Mike- *rolls his eyes* Ah Christ! Not this again! Christine- *stops walking* You need to start talking to me, Mike! I’m getting tired of all these evasive and elusive bullshit answers! *Mike stops walking as well. Pedestrians glare at them for stopping in the middle of the sidewalk.* Mike- Look, this isn’t a good place to talk........ Christine- No, it’s not! Why do you keep screwing with me and avoiding my questions? Mike- *sighs, uncomfortable* Look........ It’s a surprise that I have planned for you. You’ll find out all about it tonight, okay? I just have to get it ready. That’s why I can’t train with you. Christine- *softens a bit* Really? Mike- Yes, really. Christine- I’ll find out everything tonight? Mike- *long pause* Yes. Tonight. Christine- Okay. Good. Lead on, Mr. Lubis........ *Christine hooks her arm through Mike’s again and they continue off down the street. Inwardly, Mike breathes a sigh of relief. He actually was meeting with Regina Winters this afternoon to discuss some “plans”. Now, he needed to come up with a “surprise” for Christine. At least now he had time to build a cover story. Christine, on the other hand, is weary and content all at the same time. She had a tough match tonight, but with Angelica as her partner, she just might be able to pull it off. And then, there was Mike’s surprise. Now all she had to do was be happy and quell that little nagging voice inside of her that was telling her that Mike was lying. If only that was all she had to do........* View Biography
