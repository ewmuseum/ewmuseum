Backstabber (pt. 1)

18 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Backstabber (pt. 1) Scorpion CCW Promo #23 Date: 2-18-03 Location: New York City, New York (A light shining from a light bulb can be seen through the red bandana that covers the eyes of Scorpion. Scorpion is groggy after being attacked by gang members after Drake’s funeral that would end knocking him unconscious. The grip is tight around Scorpion’s wrist; he can feel rope holding him to a chair. The sound of the gang members gets louder as Scorpion regains consciousness.) Scorpion: Where am I? Gang member: Are backstabber is waking up boss. Scorpion: Who the fuck is that? :::: The red bandana covering Scorpion’s eyes is untied by a gang member. Standing in front of Scorpion is the gang leader that Scorpion recognizes. :::: Scorpion: Corky Stewart? Don’t tell me you’re the leader of the gang. Corky Stewart: Yeah I’m the boss, but I didn’t ask for it. I was back up if something happened to Drake and now that Drake is dead…I’m the leader. Scorpion: Why was I attacked and un-tie me now! Corky Stewart: I think you know why you’re here… Scorpion: What the fuck do you mean, I know why I’m here. Corky Stewart: We are looking for revenge for Drake. Scorpion: So why am I here? Corky Stewart: You set-up Drake! You held him up so the cops had enough time to fill his body with bullets! I thought you were his friend dog. But you turned your back on him and by tipping off the cops you must pay. Scorpion: Whoa, hold up here. I didn’t tip the cops about Drake. Corky he was my best friend; I would have never ever tipped the cops on him. I was trying to save his ass and get out of the low life. I was trying to save him and get him out of the life that you assholes live in. Corky Stewart: So you tipped the cops to teach him a lesson! Scorpion: Fuck no Corky! I didn’t tip the cops off to anything. I don’t talk to the cops and they don’t talk to me. You should know I would never rat out my best friend. Corky Stewart: He wasn’t your best friend anymore, you left him, you turned your back on him and all of us. Scorpion: Corky fuck you. As soon as I get un-tied I’m gonna kick your ass like I should have done a long, long time ago. You know I didn’t turn your back on anyone, I needed to get out of that life I wanted to live and I’m still breathing. As soon as I get out of here sadly you won’t be breathing. Corky Stewart: (Laughs) Boys you here that? A man strapped to a chair is going to kick my ass! I should take out my nine-millimeter right and fill you up with lead. But I’m not; I’m going to make you suffer. Boys let’s take him out to the junk yard… :::: Two gang members pick the chair up over there head and carry Scorpion out to the junkyard. Once in the junkyard they place Scorpion inside a car. A bulldozer is on preparing to smash Scorpion inside the car. :::: Scorpion: Corky, get me the fuck out of here! :::: Corky walks up to the car window. :::: Corky Stewart: Before I kill you I just wanted to tell you something. (Whispering into Scorpion’s ear.) I tipped off the cops. Scorpion: What the fuck! You bastard! I always knew you were jealous of Drake and I. Corky you will pay for this! Corky Stewart: (whispering) I kill my competition and now look where I am. I’m the leader of the gang you helped build and now I going to kill you. (laughs) Scorpion: Believe me Corky you won’t live long. Corky Stewart: (whispering) We shall Scorpion, oh and before I leave I just want to tell you something. :::: Corky grabs Scorpion’s chin squeezing it. Scorpion’s eyes are filled with rage. :::: Corky Stewart: (whispering) Fuck you and enjoy hell. Scorpion: I’m going to kick your ass Corky. Corky Stewart: Nice try, Scorpion. Your going to be six feet under and I’m going to pissing on your grave. Boys begin dropping the weight. :::: Corky walks away heading to his car to leave the scene. The only person left is the bulldozer operator dangling a one thousand pound weight over the car. :::: Scorpion: (screaming) GET ME THE HELL OUT OF HERE!!! :::: The weight continues to move closer and closer to the car. Once the weight gets near the car, a gunshot is heard. The weight is stopped and the bulldozer operator becomes limp with blood pouring from his head. :::: Scorpion: What the fuck was that? :::: A shadow appears from the dark with a gun in hand. :::: Scorpion: Holy shit, Mike what are you doing here? :::: The shadow is Scorpion’s friend Mike, a former gang member himself and good friends with Drake as well. :::: Mike: Scorpion no time to talk we gotta get out of here and get Corky. Scorpion: Did you know Corky tipped the cops about Drake? Mike: Yeah, I came here to shoot him and I saw you in the car so I shot the operator. No time to talk we got to get Corky before it’s too late. Scorpion: All right let’s go get that bastard. TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
