What this is A backstage interview-style segment in which Carnage takes a call from his mentor, trades a nod with Guardian, and Danny Bryson attacks Carnage with a bat while the announcers react.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Missing Feed Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 11:25:07 Location: Backstage during SMN This is a: Interview *The scene opens in the arena, right before the main event. Vinny and Chucky are sitting at the announce table* Vinny: Wait bro, there's something else going on backstage Chucky: What, not more backstage action, I think I'm gonna soil myself.... *The camera cuts backstage, where Carnage is on the phone* Carnage - 'I did it. Just like you told me... Just like I said. I crushed the Red Headed Step Child into oblivion and won the CCW Pacific Title. With you as my mentor I can accomplish anything.' Mentor - 'Yes Carnage... That's the attitude, and that's exactly what will happen. As soon as we put HIM out of action, it will be a clear shot to the World Title...' *Carnage flips the phone shut and smiles. As he walks away backstage, he comes face to face with Guardian, who hoists his Hardcore title higher up onto his shoulder. The two begin eyeballing each other* Chucky: Do we have a 24/7 rule, cos if we do, there's gonna be some BLOOD!!!!! Vinny: Have we ever had a 24/7 rule.... Chucky: No..... Vinny: Bro, shut up.....this confrontation could get heated.... *Both men continue eyeballing each other.....then the stare breaks and Carnage nods to Guardian...* Chucky: Waitaminute. Have these guys got some kind of understanding..... *Guardian nods back and both men turn* Vinny: I think they have. Carnage would usually have pounced.... *At that moment, Bryson blindsides Carnage with a ball bat, sending him clattering into the aisle. He brings the bat down two, three, four times onto Carnages head, before dissapearing back into the shadows...* Vinny: Whaitaminute...Bryson's just destroyed carnage. Guardians running back to check on him, but it's too late....He's checking on the Pacific champion.... Chucky: Whats going on between those two bro....and, why is ryson attacking Carnage.... Vinny: Who knows....maybe we're just gonna have to find out later..... *The next match begins* View Biography
