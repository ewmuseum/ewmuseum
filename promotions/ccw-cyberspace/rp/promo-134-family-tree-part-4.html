Family Tree; Part 4 Chloey Mason CCW Promo #134 Date: 04/15/03 Location: Seattle, Washington The search for Mr. Stubbs begins. Late in the morning, outside a post office, Chloe talks animatedly with a mailman who shakes his head. On a busy city street, Chloe intercepts another mailman on his delivery route. The quick conversation goes a bit more positively than the last. At the corner of a mini strip mall, Chloe talks to a Vietnamese couple outside their convenience store. Looks like she got lucky. They point down the street. It's about mid-afternoon now as Chloe reaches the outskirts of a park. Mr. Stubbs is a spry old gentlemen in his 70's. He is playing chess with a pal, Harry, as Chloe heads toward him across the grass. Stubbs: You're going down in three moves, Harry. Chloe: Mr. Stubbs? Stubbs: That's me, darling. Chloe: My name is Chloe Mason. Stubbs: Pleased to meet you. This is Harry. Chloe: The lady at the retirement home said I'd find you here. Stubbs: Every afternoon. Chloe: You used to run a candy store on Baltimore Street? Stubbs: For thirty years. Sold it last March. Now it's some sort of game store, with weirdos hanging around. Chloe: I remember your place. I used to come in when I was a kid. Stubbs: {looks her up and down} What are you now? Middle-aged? Chloe: No, I mean when I was real young. Do you remember Emily Mason? Stubbs: Emily? Course! That's going back some. Licorice. You used to love licorice. You were a cute little darling; always gave you a little extra, as I recall. Emily came in every Friday and I used to cash her check. Chloe: You have a great memory, Mr. Stubbs. Stubbs: The people who came into my store got service with a capital 'S' – never forgot them and they never forgot me. That's the way I ran my business. Poor Emily.... I remember that day as if it were yesterday. Collapsed right in front of me. Brain hemorrhage, and she was barely 30. Chloe: {realizing} So, she's really dead? I wasn't sure if I remembered it right. Stubbs: ‘Fraid so. Next thing I hear, they'd whisked you off to an orphanage. Chloe: Wasn't there a MISTER Mason? Stubbs: Oh, sure. But they was split up by then. I still see Jack hanging around, though. Chloe is astonished by this revelation. Chloe: He lives here.... in the city? Stubbs: Far as I know. He used to be in the Versailles apartments over on Madison. And as Chloe absorbs the fact that her father may be just around the corner.... Lacey drives up in the T-bird near them. Outside the Versailles apartments, Lacey pulls the T-bird up opposite them. Lacey and Chloe both stare at the building – appraising the place which is a Dept. of Building and Safety nightmare. Chloe: {joking} This is probably a CIA front; you know, deep cover. Lacey: {starting to open the car door} This is about as deep as it gets. Chloe: No.... Lacey.... if this is my dad, I'd like to do this alone. Lacey: You sure? Chloe: Yeah.... She puts her hand on the door handle, then pauses.... Chloe: On the other hand, we could just drive a couple blocks to the nearest mall and splurge ourselves. There's a new pair of killer shoes I've had my eye on for awhile now.... Lacey: {supportive} It's up to you. Chloe wrestles with her anxiety for a moment, then shrugs and opens the car door. Chloe: He probably doesn't even live here anymore. Chloe steps into the street and heads toward the building, watched by Lacey, who is very anxious about what Chloe might find inside.... View Chloey Mason 's Biography Back
