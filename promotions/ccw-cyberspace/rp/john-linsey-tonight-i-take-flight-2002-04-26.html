John Linsey - Tonight, I take flight.

What this is A Match RP in which John Linsey reflects alone at home on his unhappiness, framed by short poems, ahead of Group Therapy.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main John Linsey Entitled: Tonight, I take flight. Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:13:19 Location: The house. This is a: Match RP sometimes I feel like I’m drowning, and nobody can save me floating down deeper and deeper till I hit rock bottom little things build up until they become big gigantic, huge pushing me down so I can’t breath 'It's still light outside...' John drew away from his window and sighed, leaning back onto his bed. He hastily ran a hand through his hair, and winced once it ripped out the hair in knots. An irritated frown sadly graced his face as he watched himself in the mirror on his bureau, smoothing out his hair with his fiingers. 'Come on John, why so glum?' he could hear Max, his friend, cheerful voice echoing softly in his mind. John stared at himself in the mirror, taking in her own body with unusual disgust. 'Well, Max, there's no reason for me to be happy. That's why.' John's words hung in the air of his bedroom. Another sigh escaped her lips, and John stood, and he shook his head. His khaki slacks and blue muscle tanktop were wrinkled from lounging silently on the living room couch all afternoon. The man reached down with his hands, and bent down to pick up a few discarded papers, gently shuffling them and replacing them on her desk. 'When did I end up so unhappy? I'm the one with a clear plan and a guiding light in my soul. When did that light burn out and disappear?' John spoke aloud, and blinked as the bright afternoon sunset flashed in his eyes. 'I can't even enjoy sunsets anymore.' John had lived alone for quite a while, maintaining a single bedroom apartment for years after meeting Laura, who became his closest, most treasured friend ever since. Throughout those four or five years together, they had grown up with one another, learning and experiencing the world outside of their duties. Since then, John had gone from a spirited young man, to a mature and confident young adult at the age of 27. His apartment was sophisticated, but still clung to that homey feeling with its artsy, intricate decor. His own bedroom was just the same, decorated with interesting fabrics, flavorful colors, and a few momentos from the past. All the while, none of it mattered to him, whether he was living in a home or walking the streets in search of shelter. It wasn't any different either way. 'I know why I ended up like this...because being strong and full of dreams my whole life coincided. I wanted to be happy, but I had to be strong. I used to be able to balance everything...but school and all the rest of it mixed me up completely. My entire life was built on my independence and my stability. Now they've only withered away to nothing. Isn't there anything to make me happy anymore?!' John said to himself as he stepped out onto his front porch. The sunset was blazing now, its vibrant colors casting a ruby glow over the earth. John folded his arms and leaned against the closed screen door of his small, but comfortable home. 'My anger will be taken out on any and all opponents at the Group Therapy event. ..Beware.'
