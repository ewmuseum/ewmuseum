Panzer PTC #1: The Quest for the Truth Begins

13 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long serialized interview and flashback in which Jack Panzer, questioned in an interrogation room, recounts meeting an informant and reuniting with his manager in Central Park while hunting those who framed him.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: Panzer PTC #1: The quest for the truth begins Written in Saturday, Apr 13, 2002, 21:13:42 Location: Unknown This is a: Interview 'A dark room. Dim light breaking through the white silk curtain in the opposite wall. The first sunrays of another cool morning in the Southeast breaking through the skycrapers surrounding the historic building. The big screen tv, across the room, turning on in the weather channel. Its light turning darkness into shadows, shadows into shapes. A rich room, king-size bed, silk sheets. A man, sleeping in the bed, small, completely bald. A woman standing by his side, also asleep, wearing an expensive babydoll. It was clear she was a pro hooker, not a cheap one, though. I walked in the room by then, opened the door slowly. I was drunk, I needed to have some sleep, think about the next day. I was still on the run, I couldn't go back home. The hotel was the perfect hideout...', describes a grave voice, as the scene appears on the tv. The scene slowly fades to black and immediately, a shot of an interrogation room pops up. It's a very clear room, green walls, strong lighting. Across the table, with some documents and photos in front of him, we see 'The War Machine' Jack Panzer, really beaten by sleep and stress. His beard is 3 days overdue and the bag under his eyes show his state of mind and body. A voice comes from across the table, a grave voice, while tossing more and more documents in front of him. 'All right, I understand the room was in your name. My question remains: What were those two people doing in your room?' 'I don't know... As I told you, there are many people following me, trying to get me.', states Jack, looking to the sides, nervous. 'Look, don't come to me with this conspiracy theory, all right? From time to time I have to face guys like you...', berates the mysterious inquisitor, what changes Jack's look on his face. 'What? What are you talking about?' 'You know... Serial killers, who think they can justify their actions by saying the people they killed were trying to kill him and were from some secret government agency.', says the voice behind the camera. 'Look, I'm not saying anything, I'm saying what I saw, I'm saying what happened', states Jack, his eyes wide open, showing fear on his face.' 'All right, then tell us what happened to you, right from the start of that day. Tell us anything unusual that might have happened to you that day. Anything could help you.', says the inquisitor. 'Ok, fine... Let me think about it for a moment...' **The scene shifts to the same room as we've seen the couple fallen, before. A phrase in the lower right corner, in white letters, shows '2 days ago'. An alarm clock in a small oak phone table to the left side of the bed, the near side from the camera's standpoint, begins to blast, promptly waking Jack up, who was sleeping in the bed like a baby. Before being shattered in a thousand pieces, the clock showed '6:00'. Jack slowly makes his way out of the bed sheets, sliding to the side of the bed, when he wakes up. He looks at the clock and groans, before looking at the phone table. He reaches a small lamp, turning it on. Next, he reaches for a small piece of paper. He picks up the phone and dials a number on it. Finally, signing an answer on the other side of the line, Jack speaks:** 'Yeah, it's me. I understand you have some information about the men on my tail... I see.', says Jack, as he reaches for a small drawer on the table, looking for a pen, which he finds quickly and begins to write an address on it. 'Ok, I got it... No, don't worry, I can find the place, it's just gonna take me a while to get there, because I'm in New York right now... Fair enough, I'll get what you want... Ok, bye.' **Jack turns off the phone and gets up, shaking his head sideways, trying to get awake. The scene fades to black.** ##6 hours later## **The scene opens in a small italian restaurant. The place is crowded with business men and doctors, enjoying their lunch. The sound of ambulances on the back suggest the proximity to a local hospital. The place looks very clean, and the meals enjoyable. The waiters walk around carrying big steel treys, with piles of pasta dishes and different sauces and bottles of wine. The camera looks around, finding Jack, sitting in a corner table, looking around, as if trying to find someone. jack is wearing a blue Detroit Tigers' cap, hiding his short black hair, dark ray-ban shades, hiding his big round dark eyes and a camouflaged T-shirt, revealing his past as a german army commando. Jack waits anxiously, as a waiter apporaches him with a huge dish of spaghetti a Bologna. Jack looks at him, puzzled:** 'I'm sorry, I didn't order this' 'But I did...', says someone behind the waiter. The waiter puts a ravioli dish down, across Jack, and as he walks by, we're granted a view of a slim man, short, about 5'6' tall, long black wavy hair tied back, wearing a black leather jacket. He sits right across Jack. He smiles at him, before beginning to eat, what immediately draws the attention of near everyone nearby, because of the disgusting sound and look of the man devouring his ravioli. 'All right, who the hell are you?', asks Jack, visibly disgusted by the scene, not even touching his food. 'I'm your contact, my friend...', says the short man, between bites. The munchy noises and rests of food between his teeth as he speaks makes the scene absolutely absurd. 'I'm sorry, but I'm expecting a friend of mine...' 'Look, I may be short, I may not be German, but I know who framed you, all right?', says the short man, immediately catching Jack's attention. The memories of his past as a commando, the ambush in the african village, the torture, the german prison, the escape, all come to his mind, all at once. He looks at the human pig in front of him, staring, analyzing him, trying to find out how that little piece of man in front of him might know something about what happened 6 years before. 'Look, I know how you feel, you must be wondering how I found out, right? Well, the truth is that my family found something in a rampage inside a doctor's office that owned us some money. Inside, we found several patient's charts. That was nothing, until we found the doctor's safety box behind a painting. We found out the bastard had more than 100 grand in it... The bastard was trying to avoid paying us.' 'So, you're in the...' 'No, listen to me. Forget all that romanticized, Hollywoodesque image of the mafia. We're simply a family of old merchants who have been in the business for a long time.' 'But you do command the crime behind the scenes, right?' 'No, man, that's what THEY want the people to think. As a matter of fact, they know we're a threat to some corporations in this country. They know that, if we began some business ourselves, we'd run them out of business.' 'I see. Move on...' 'So, inside the box, we found not only the man's money, but also found a small file, with some tapes, pictures and medical records.' 'My records, I presume...', says Jack, staring deep into the man's eyes. 'Correct. Now, we're ready to give you that information, for the right price.' 'How much do you want?', says Jack, visibly worried about the development of the conversation. 'It's not a matter of money, it's more a matter of... providing lodge...' 'Excuse me???', asks Jack, studying the man in front of him, who's now stopped eating and is looking closely at him. 'This is the deal: We're having a meeting with our local bosses and we need a place for the meeting to happen. We were thinking that, since you're not affiliated with us, it wouldn't draw much attention to the reunion...', says the man, who's now pretty serious about the conversation. 'What kind of meeting is this?', asks Jack, puzzled, staring the man by the corner of his eye. 'You don't need to know. You provide the lodging, and we'll give you the information you want.' 'All right. Fine...', says Jack, with one foot behind. 'When will you have the meeting?' 'Tomorrow... Where can it be?' 'Well, I'm currently in a hotel room in the Waldorf Astoria...' 'Perfect, it's exactly the place the cops wouldn't find out. Where's the key?', asks the man, putting his hands under the table. 'Here... But please, don't do anything mafia-like...', says Jack, before reaching inside his pants' pocket for his wallet and getting the small magnetic card, giving it to the man later. 'When will I get the files?' 'Don't worry, I'll contact you tomorrow with the location of your files. And be cool, nothing is going to happen...' 'Yeah, I hope so...', says Jack, before the guy gets up and walks out of the table. Jack looks at him and looks back down at the huge pasta dish in front of him. Realizing how he was hungry, he picks up a fork and starts eating. The scene slowly fades to black. **It opens back to the interrogation room. The scene now shows the interrogator, a young man, on his mid-30s, somewhat familiar to loyal Panzer fans, wearing an Armani navy blue suit, white shirt and hawaiian silk tie. He's tapping with a Mount Blanc pen on the table. He finally says:** 'I don't buy it...', says him, looking closely to Jack. 'So, you were simply walking on the streets and got mugged...' 'Yeah, that's pretty much it.', says Jack, looking serious to hide his incredibly blatant lie. 'Ok, I don't believe you... Keep going. What else happened that day that caught your attention? Tell me every detail...' 'Ok, let me think...', says Jack, before the scene fades to black once again. **The scene now shows the Central Park, in downtown New York. The night is young, and there are several tourists and young couples and families walking around the bright spots of the park, the ones safe. The camera looks down one of the many inner roads in the park, watching people jogging, riding bikes, just plain having fun, when it suddenly focus Nicky White, Jack's manager and friend. She's walking around with a fur coat covering an astonishing red night gown, with a young man by her side. The man must be about 25 years old, clean shaved, wearing a brand new Armani suit. The couple walks romantically around, enjoying the cool spring evening.** 'You haven't told me what you do for a living yet, Nicky...', asks the man, looking at her visibly interested on her. 'Well, it's not a noble profession, like yours...', says Nicky, blushing a little bit. 'I don't care, not everybody can become a lawyer. Everybody has their jobs, and a job is a serious thing. Now... What do you do?', insists the man. 'Well, Dave, it's simple: I'm a wrestling agent!', states Nicky, proud of the last two words... 'Hmmm... Okay... wrestling agent. That must be... interesting!', stumbles Dave, caught by surprise by Nicky. 'Yeah, it's cool. I get to travel a lot around the country, maybe I even end up traveling around the world. You see, I represent this guy in CCW, and he's damn good at what he does. He loses his mind ocne in a while, gets a desire to kill whatever is moving in front of him, but I'm there to help him get his mind back together. I also take care of promo locations, hotel, lunches, it's a tough but cool job.' 'Hmmm? Yeah, it seems it is. It's getting late, i'll drive you home.', says Dave, visibly trying to throw away the date. 'No, not yet. The evening is so calm, so nice, so...', says Nicky, before being interrupted by a shadow, coming out of nowhere. The cloaked man stops right in front of the couple, punches Dave on the face and holds Nicky under the cloak, walking back into the woods. Dave slowly gets back up and doesn't find Nicky but simply decides to walk away. **The scene now shifts to behind a huge oak, across the street where the couple was walking. We see Debbie fighting the claoked shadow with her life, but it presses her against the tree. Nicky is scared, her eyes, wide open. Suddenly, the shadow drops the cloak and the face of Jack Panzer is revealed. Smiling at her, he lets her go, but she doesn't mention to leave. On the contrary, she jumps on Jack's neck, happy to see him after so much time.** 'Where have you been, you moron? I was thinking you got killed...', says Nicky, slapping on Jack's arm with such a strenght that he releases a small groan of pain. 'Hey, no need to kick my ass, all right? I've had a hard time these days already.', says Jack, looking at her with a nice fraternal smile. 'Yeah, sure... Where the hell have you been, idiot?' 'Ah, it's a long story...' 'Well, let's just say I have all the time in the world to kill now, because you scared my date away...', says Nicky, visibly upset with Jack. 'Oh, really, it doesn't look like. Look at him...', says Jack, pointing with his right thumb over his right shoulder, pointing to Dave, talking to a gorgeous blondie sitting in a bench. Nicky walks towards them and the scene closes on Jack's face, which contorts in a funny painful look every time a 'Ouch', 'Poft', 'Crash' or 'Ahhh' is heard on the background. The camera finally looks back and sees Dave out cold on the floor, filled with bruises, and Nicky walking away, stomping her feet on the floor in pure hatred. Jack walks towards her and they walk away from the scene. Jack looks at her by the corner of his eye, waiting for her to calm down a little. After a few yards, she breaks the awkward silence... 'So???' 'So what? Oh, sorry. The long story, right???' 'Right... And say it all, because if I find out you're lying...', threats Nicky. 'Yeah, I know the drill...', says Jack, making a painful look on his face as he remembers about the fallen Dave. 'Ok, here'sd the thing: I found out that there's a person inside CCW who has been giving tips to the german officers about me.' 'WHAT? This is impossible... How can they...' 'I don't know... It's something that puzzled me too. But the fact remains that someone in CCW have been giving tips on my whereabouts. The last time they always caught me... I had to leave, get on the shadows and investigate. In the end, I haven't found out who's the bastard, but I found something even better...', explains Jack calmly. 'What is it?' 'The files from that doctor's office. The one they set a trap on me...' 'Really? That's great news! Where's it?' 'I'm getting it tomorrow. For now, it's important that you leave New York and only come back when I call you, all right?' 'Sure, Jack. But where should I go?', asks Nicky, nervous. 'I don't know... Maybe you should go back to your place, stay with your parents for a while...' 'Ah, no... I won't go back to NC.', barks Nicky, upset about the idea. 'I understand you guys have your issues, but please, just go. When I call you, you come back. You need to go there and check what's going on in CCW. Who knows, maybe those bozos have booked me without telling me first... I can't miss any oportunities there...', says Jack, looking closely at Nicky, trying to convince her with his serious look. 'Ok, very well. But I'll NOT go back to Charlotte. I'll be better if I go to the CCW offices in Canada. I'll check if you were booked and get back to you.' 'Good, that's all I'm asking. Now go, I'll call you in a couple days.' **Jack calls for a cab coming nearby and Nicky looks at him with a painful look, before finally giving up and getting into the cab. The cab rides away, and Jack looks up. The night is still young, and he has lots of time to kill. Walking by the flowered, colorful Central Park trees, feeling the cool breeze of southern nights blaze around him, Jack observes the similarities between New York and Berlin, his home land. Watching the trees, the benches, the posts, the few old buildings surrounding the park, remembering his old neighborhood, the young, passionate couples, riding chariots in their first dates, the hope of a long-term relationship, all that Jack got ripped of after the African incident. You can see nostalgic traces in Jack's breathing and semblant, as he stops in front of the frozen pond, looking at the dozens of happy people just enjoying a free time, sliding on the ice for fun, the life he, Alex, not Jack, could never have anymore. Not until he got the people behind him. Not until he managed to frame every single man that destroyed his life, broke his family and stole his money and his home. And yet, the worst part of his life wasn't the constant chase, but the lonelyless he had to impose himself, not because of fear of commitment, but more for protection for his loved ones. The simple thought of having a relationship with someone, trying to rebuild a family, having a normal life again, was immediately followed by a vision of them being told something terrible happened, and the pain they'd feel. That's what bothered Alex's soul so much: The fact he couldn't even consider having a normal life. That's what drove all his life into one goal: Win. That's what always comes to the surface when he fights. All the anger, pain, resentment, frustration, all that gets focused into one single goal: Win. That's why he lost his mind. The pain and suffering have been so heavy that when they were focused, they become an unstoppable force, with no morals, no emotions, simply one carnal, brutal, mortal energy that drove his entire body till his goal was accomplished. And yet, there he found himself: Trying to tame that force. Trying to hold back on losing his mind, trying to forget about the thing that gave him so much success and so many victories. His mind, his spirit, shattered in two opposite, conflicting sides, couldn't take the load much longer. He had to end this terror. He had to end the chase. He had to get the men who turned a peaceful, easy-going army captain into a lunatic monster, every time he lost his mind. He had to, and he would do it, no matter how long it would take. He would get the men who toyed with his life and reclaim his conscience. Even if that was a life-long quest. Even if he had to die seconds after he achieved his goal.** **The present: The scene turns back to the interrogation room, where we see the man tapping his pen on the metal table. Jack looks at him, a little tired now, but still focused on the man's eyes. The inquisitor asks him:** 'Ok, so, as you're telling me, you decided to walk in the Central Park to breath. Is that correct?' 'That's correct.', says Jack, same look on his face. 'Did you meet someone in the meantime?', asks the suit, trying to analyze Jack's reactions to every question. 'No, I did not.', promptly says Jack, immediately pissing the suit's off. He throws his pen on the table and gets up, stomping the table with both his hands, leaning over it and facing Jack from about one feet away, face to face. 'You know what? I don't believe in one word you're talking about here. So you better start talking, or else...', says the man, in an intimidating way. 'Or else what? I could confess I killed Kennedy and you wouldn't be able to do SHIT to me' 'Excuse me? Who the hell do you think you are?', asks the suit, smiling. 'I'm a man who have not been read my constitucional rights, been allowed a phone call OR the presence of a lawyer. Therefore, nothing here is legal...' **The suit stands back, stunned at the german's allegation. He walks around, trying to find some lost document in the myriad of legal documents. Jack smiles now, knowing he's had a check-mate on the man. He says:** 'What, surprised I know that? Sorry, dude, but I watch Law and Order, Family Law, CSI AND The District. I love this kinda series. We didn't get much of those in Germany, you know?', says Jack, laughing right next at the man's face. **The man throws some papers on the table and storms out of the room, yelling at some people on his way out. Jack sits back and relaxes, knowing this will be a long day. Looking at some papers, he begins to analyze some of the documents the man left in the room, and Jack's face brights up, as he seems to realize a way to get out of there. As he keeps taking a good look on those papers, the scene fades to black** View Biography
