Burn In Hell

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Neurosis, alone in his Boston apartment, rants about his Group Therapy teammates and opponents.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Neurosis Entitled: Burn In Hell Written in Thursday, Apr 25, 2002, 23:04:59 Location: Neurosis' Boston Apartment. This is a: Match RP ~ The scene opens with Neurosis sitting alone in his apartment. Neurosis is sitting silently in the dark, writing on some notebook or something of the sort. Neurosis is sitting on his couch and the only light in the room is shining through the window from the moon. It is a wonder he can see what he is writing at all... ~ Neurosis: What a joke. ~ Neurosis crumples up a piece of paper and throws it on the floor in disgust. He glares at the camera and continues... ~ Neurosis: Oh great fucking plan Spider. Send some fucking Jesus freak to 'help me'. Yeah, a lot of good he did. And now I will bet he is back to molesting little boys with his Catholic priest friends. Scum. ~ Neurosis puts his feet up on the couch and sits with his head on his knees now... ~ Neurosis: Although I suppose it doesn't matter... not without Benny. They mean nothing without him there. Nothing really does. Except my belt... because I got it on my own, without anyone's help... still... I wish Benny were here. ~ Neurosis moves and lays down on the couch now. Putting his feet up on the arm rest, he continues... ~ Neurosis: So this Group Therapy thing is Sunday... and there are two real wrestlers on the whole team. Me and Spider. As much as I hate Spider, he is the only other good wrestler on our team. The Valiant One some would claim... yeah... anyone who loses to Kensuke Suzahara automatically gets stamped with the seal of sucky. So I am gonna have to say it is Spider and I as far as talent goes. Not to say that Spider won't either blow it because he sucks in his own way, or hire some goons to do his bidding. Either way would be his style. But I digress, enough about my shitty teammates like... that sucky girl and that rookie guy... on to my would be opponents. The first round match up looks to be a breeze. With one real wrestler in the whole group, whom both Spider and I have defeated, I see no problem there. Cianan will fall, he can not function with others. I should know, he tried to with me and that did not turn out well for him. Yama Tatsu is just a rookie, a good rookie, but still a rookie, his lack of experience will be his undoing. The Red Headed Step Child is the worst excuse for a human being I have ever seen, so he matters not. And that girl... yeah, shes a girl... I don't know why they were even booked in this thing in the first place. But I digress. The first round belongs to Team #3. ~ Neurosis toss his notebook down and sits up. His firey eyes lookdeep into the camera... ~ Neurosis: Round 2, I expect it will be down to Spider, I and Lynn... and we shall be facing Winters, The Red Ninja, perhaps Lamen, perhaps Jordan, and most definately that scumbag McAllister, a man who can't except the truth. Winters, I suspect can be taken by Lynn, it is not in my nature to hit a women, but if I have to, I won't shy away. The Ninja is tricky, for I have never faced him, perhaps I will not, I do not know. And this leads me to that whiny little asshole Jason McAllister. Oh yes, I am the one who turned their back on you. BULLSHIT. You couldn't... you wouldn't and you refused to see my way. You distanced yourself ever since you met John. Ever since then, it was no longer Neurosis and Jason... it was Neurosis, Jason and John... and then it was just John and Jason. You left me for him and don't you think for a second that it is any other way. You just replaced me because you couldn't deal with what I was about. I bet you hoped a really was dead when Paul nearly killed me. Well tough luck, because I am still here and I intend to punish you for these crimes against me. I am going to enjoy breaking you.... in the words of Benny... I will enjoy bleeding you, punk. ~ Neurosis picks up his notebook and reads from it... ~ Neurosis: HEAR NO EVIL. DON'T YOU SEE NO EVIL. DON'T YOU LAY NO EVIL DOWN ON ME. YOU'RE GONNA BURN IN HELL. YOU BURN IN HELL. ~ Neurosis throws the book down to the floor and grabs the camera with both hands, focusing it directly on his eyes... ~ Neurosis: All those who oppose me......... especially liars and frauds like Jason McAllister...... will burn in hell..... [whispering] the locust reigns... ~ Neurosis hangs his eyes down now, the camera showing only his head... and then it turns to snow... ~ View Biography
