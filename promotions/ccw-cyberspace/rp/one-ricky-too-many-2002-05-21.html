One Ricky Too Many

What this is A solo promo in which Ricky Thunder drives along a highway and addresses his upcoming match against Ricky Coyote.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: One Ricky too many Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 11:48:36 Location: Western, Australia This is a: Match RP (OOC: See my NoC RP's for the prequels here...if interested) *The scene shifts to the outside of a cemetery near Perth, as Ricky Thunder has gotten into his car. He pulls the door shut slowly as the he starts it up and punches the gas on the solid black Buick. As he is heading down the highway thoughts of his brother and his family were racing through his head. The things he and Thunder discussed. The things he advised him on. How he could beat Jack panzer at Memorial Day Showdown, but more importantly he showed him how to remain focused. The thoughts in his head suddenly shift to Ricky Coyote.* Ricky: Ah, the houseshow champion! The #1 jobber in CCW history. Perfect. This Thursday I get to make an example out of a living legend. Someone who has been in more matches, than Phil Burner, or Tim Inman. Someone who has lost more matches then both men combined. Someone, who for some odd reason, has all the fans support. No matter how many losses a man can accrue, no matter how many times they have been on the receiving end of a 3 count, they still keep going. What drives a man like Ricky Coyote to continue on? What drives a man, like that, to WANT to continue? These are answers I have Ricky, and these answers are simple. *Ricky Thunder takes a deep breath as he slowly exhales and glances into his left lane before changing over* Ricky: You have no other options. That is the answer, isn't it Coyote? I mean honestly, you have no talent aside from wrestling...or jobbing rather. You don't have what it takes to actually defeat your opponents so you job to them making them look good. Well your days are just about numbered Coyote, for you see, the time is now for Ricky Thunder to escape any shadows he may have been in, to escape any rumours that are circulating about him. Ricky is here to stay. THIS Ricky is undefeated. THIS Ricky is the Independent champion. THIS Ricky is walking away from Lockdown with 3 points, while you Ricky Coyote, remain at nil! *Ricky continues on down the highway not saying a word as the sun begins to set along the horizon, Ricky removes his sunglasses and places them on the dashboard* Ricky: Mr. Coyote, are you prepared to feel the Thunder?
