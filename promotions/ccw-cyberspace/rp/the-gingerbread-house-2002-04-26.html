The Gingerbread House

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A horror-tinged roleplay in which Lacey Skye and her husband search a haunted Illinois farmhouse and receive a message from Devyl’s mother.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lacey Skye Entitled: The Gingerbread House Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 12:15:01 Location: Illinois Farmland This is a: Match RP Lacey: **voice over** We’re quickly approaching it.... One of the biggest tournaments CCW has had in recent history.... Group Therapy. The entire CCW roster vying for a chance to gain the most prestigious title available to them. Training is of utmost importance for this event, yet I find that I can’t concentrate.... There’s something very important that I need to take care of before any physical training can be done. My husband told me last week that Devyl’s mother had a message for me, and that in all likelihood, it was in the house in Illinois where Devyl grew up as a child. Why he knows this.... Well, he just does. Doug has a good set of instincts, and when he follows up on them, he’s usually one hundred percent correct. So here we are, back in Illinois once again, trying to find that which may be a key to component to help me fight Devyl. **A rental car crunches along a long gravel driveway and pulls to a halt out in front of an old farmhouse. Lacey and Doug, the two occupants of the car, get out and carefully look around. The farmhouse, although having been abandoned now for less than a year, looks to be in a state of unkempt disrepair. The air is cool and a little chilly this morning, and Lacey shivers a bit as she slowly approaches the front porch. And again.... It’s more than just the cold that makes her shiver. There’s something about this house.... And although Lacey tries very hard not to believe in ghosts and supernatural entities, if she had to describe this house with one word, she would pick: haunted. She pauses before the front steps, looking up at the front door. The screen door in front of it blows slightly with the wind, tapping on the wooden frame. She stares at it, suddenly feeling as if she shouldn’t enter this house. It feels like there’s something inside that she doesn’t want to see, and doesn’t want to deal with.** Doug: **stepping up beside Lacey** You feel it too, don’t you? Lacey: **nods** There’s something in that house, isn’t there? **Doug nods once. Husband and wife stare up at the door, trying to fight the unnatural sense of disquiet that has suddenly come over them.** Lacey: **softly** This is the Gingerbread House. Doug: Pardon? Lacey: **louder** Hansel and Gretal.... Do you remember that fairy tale? They got lost in the woods and were taken in by a witch who lived in a house made out of gingerbread. She locked them up in cages and tried cooking them. That’s what this house feels like.... A gingerbread house.... A witch’s house. Doug: I don’t think you’re too far off, hon. **Doug walks up the steps onto the front porch, Lacey following right behind him. He looks around briefly and then points out a small overhang.** Doug: See that? Police found the body of a young infant here.... Neck in a noose and hanging right here in the front porch. They were called in by Joyce Devins when she came home from a grocery trip. Her husband was found dead inside the house. The police determined his cause of death as a simple heart attack. The infant, who had been kidnapped several days earlier from a Chicago hospital, had strangled to death out here, swinging at the end of the noose. Lacey: That happened last year, didn’t it? I remember reading about that in the paper. **Doug nods again, and he and Lacey turn towards the door. Doug reaches out for the doorknob and stops dead in his tracks as a loud groaning noise echoes from somewhere deep inside the house. The sun comes out briefly from behind a bank of clouds, and a shadow races across the wall in front of them: an infant hanging at the end of a rope. The sound of a creaking rope accompanies the shadow. Lacey and Doug both turn around quickly, hearts racing.... But there’s nothing there. Looking back at the wall, the shadow has disappeared as well. They look at each other again, and Lacey does her best to stop the frantic pounding of her heart.** Doug: **agreeing with Lacey’s previous statement** There’s something very wrong here. **Doug reaches out, grabs a hold of the doorknob, and gives it a turn. And much to their surprise, they find the door unlocked. It creaks open in front of them, giving them a nice view of the small front room. They both step inside, and the feeling is that of entering a corrupt tomb as opposed to an ordinary farmhouse. The front room is bare and empty. Some plaster hangs off the walls, and a lone corridor stands in front of them, presumably leading off into the rest off the house.** Lacey: So.... Where do we start looking? Doug: **very grim** Everywhere. Lacey: **voice over** And so that’s what we did. The first floor, the second floor, and the basement. We went slow, checking out every room, trying to determine if there was anything important, and getting more and more frustrated the longer we had to spend in the house. But despite the annoyance of not finding what we thought we were looking for, we both had a morbid curiosity. We both WANTED to see this house.... And maybe find out just what made Victoria the person she was today. What changed an innocent little girl into a force of evil? We really wanted to find out. And it was on the second floor.... In what once could have been a storage room off of one of the bedrooms.... That we found something.... **Doug and Lacey stand near the doorway to the storage room. Despite the sparseness and emptiness of the rest of the house, this room looked as if it hadn’t been cleaned out properly. There were two broken mannequins in the far right corner, staring at them with their blank eyes. Several items of furniture.... A couch, a desk and several wooden dinner chairs, were all bundled up with plastic and pushed into the wall next to the mannequins. And then there were clothes.... On racks, in boxes, hanging off the walls, etc. They dominated nearly the entire room. And on the left hand side, there were boxes of miscellaneous and assorted junk items.** Lacey: Why hasn’t this room been cleared out like all the others? Doug: I don’t know. When Mrs. Devins was transferred to the psychiatric hospital, all of the items in this house had supposedly been seized by the government. All this shouldn’t be here. Lacey: **stepping further into the room and looking around** But this is all just old clothes and junk! It’s nothing that couldn’t be moved.... And nothing that can help us either. Doug: **thoughtful** Maybe. **Doug walks further into the room, checking out the furniture and some of the assorted box items. Lacey heads to the right, brushing past the furniture and nearing the creepy mannequins. And for whatever reason, the mannequins were bugging her. They had no wigs, most of the fingers on their hands had been broken off, and one was standing a little lopsided because it had no leg. She had to restrain her urge to smash the damn things to pieces, they were bothering her that much. She was just about to turn her back on them when she noticed something on the ground, at their feet. Bending down to check it out, it looked like a filmy, oily substance. It looked as if it had been applied to the floor by a paintbrush, as evidenced by the straight jagging line that it made. She put her finger down to it and brought it back up.... It was still wet, whatever it was.** Lacey: Doug.... There’s something on the floor. Doug: **calling from his corner of the room** I know. I just found it myself. **Lacey stands up and looks at Doug who’s in the other corner of the room.** Lacey: It’s over there, too? Doug: Yeah. Whatever it is, it makes a pattern on the floor. I’m going to have to move some of these boxes to get a better idea of what it is. Lacey: It’s still wet. This must have been done recently.... Doug: **cryptically** Maybe. **Doug starts moving boxes and Lacey tries tracing the pattern, herself. She follows it to about the center of the room, where the jagged line coalesces into a circular pattern. Lacey pushes aside the two clothes racks that are sitting directly on top of the circular symbol. And not meaning to, she accidentally steps on the circular pattern. And as she does, the world just suddenly.... Stops.** **One moment everything was in color, and the next: Black and white. Lacey looks around in shock. All the clothes, boxes and furniture: black and white. The clothes have even stopped there movement in mid-swing, hanging suspended in the air as if stuck outside of time. Even Doug, who is also now black and white, holds a box in his hands while in the middle of setting it down on the ground. He, along with everything else, is on pause. Lacey looks around in shock and despair, not knowing what just happened, but having a very bad feeling about this.** Joyce Devins: Thank you for coming, Lacey Morris. **Lacey turns around to find a very young and very beautiful woman standing in front of her. And Lacey recognizes her immediately: Victoria’s mother.... But much younger than the old women she had become in the hospital.** Lacey: What.... Is going on here? Joyce: I do not have much time, Lacey. I am only here to relay a message. Lacey: .... Joyce: If my daughter, Victoria, is allowed to continue her reign of terror in CCW, then there will be dark days ahead. You must not only stop her, but cure her. You must help her. You must heal her fractured soul. You must turn her away from the darkness that binds her. Lacey: How? Joyce: Find her sister. Find Melissa. Only you or Victoria can do it. And Victoria is far too corrupt to know where to look. Lacey: Where can I find her, and how will that help? Joyce: You share a “mark” with Victoria. A birth mark. Figure out the meaning and let it show you the way. Lacey: Figure out the meaning? What do you.... **And suddenly, everything is back in color and time resumes. The hanging clothes swish to a stop and Doug finishes putting down the box. He glances over at her briefly and pauses when he notices the expression on her face.** Doug: Lacey.... Are you all right? Lacey: I.... Yes. I’m all right. I just received the message.... Doug: You just received the message....? **And Doug abandons his labor and moves over to his wife, who is standing in the middle of the room, trying to regain her composure.** Lacey: **voice over** So that’s what it all comes down to? Figuring out some mark and finding Devyl’s sister? I wish that could put my mind at ease. Group Therapy is coming, and I need to face Devyl. With the tourney rules that have just come into place, I don’t get my shot at her anymore.... I have to earn it. And that’s something that I definitely plan on doing. My team is heavily favored, but that’s because I’m fighting alongside the likes of The Supreme Machine.... A CCW legend. And I plan on doing my team proud. Devyl.... I’m taking the title away from you. And then I’m going to find your sister and help save you. We are going to be in a war at Group Therapy. Let’s see if you really have what it takes. View Biography
