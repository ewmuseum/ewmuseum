Damsel - I Need a New Life........

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Damsel (Christine) wakes in a New York hotel on the day of Group Therapy and weighs her future.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: I Need a New Life........ Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 19:12:58 Location: New York This is a: Match RP *The Bellview Hotel, New York, NY. Christine is in bed, resurfacing from a restless night’s sleep at the summons of an angry sounding alarm clock. She peeks open one eye, reaches over, and brings her fist crashing down on top of the infernal device that was interrupting her precious sleep. And now, even though the alarm was off, the damage had been done. Christine lays in bed, despairing. She really needed to get a new life.* Christine- *to herself* Group Therapy is tonight, isn’t it? *She pulls herself up out of bed, her aching muscles protesting every second of it. She really wasn’t in the mood for this tournament. She wasn’t even in the mood to get together with Mike in about an hour for breakfast. She WAS in the mood to beat the ever-loving crap out of Regina Winters. It focused her mind like nothing else........ “Regina Winters must be hurt!” was what the little voice was telling her.* Christine- *to herself* But I’m not going to have a chance to hurt Regina, am I? No, instead I’m stuck in the stupid tournament and taking on Lacey Skye, SuMa, Chang, Ricky Coyote........ Why!? Who’s stupid idea was this? *Christine manages to make it onto her feet without falling over and somehow makes it to the bathroom without falling over. Once there, she looks at herself in the mirror, and the depression threatens to sink in once more. Yes, her life was shit. Was it going to change anytime soon? Probably not.* Christine- I definitely need to get myself a new life. This just isn’t cutting it anymore. I need to get out there and start doing what I like to do, and that’s........ That’s........ *Christine pauses, because she doesn’t really know what else she could be doing at this moment. Then what would she like to do? She doesn’t know that either. But anything has to be better than going from arena to arena, waking up in hotel after hotel, and getting to the point where you’re never really feeling a hundred percent. But what else was there, really?* Christine- Maybe I should reorganize my priorities a bit........ Get myself involved in a program like Lacey was at one time. Or maybe I should just retire away from the wrestling biz, find a husband and settle down somewhere. Or........ Maybe I’ll just run away and join the circus. *sighs* Who the heck am I kidding? Not yourself, that’s for sure, Christine........ *She reaches down and turns on the faucet, splashing cold water on her face. Wrestling was her life. It’s where she belonged. She needed look no further than the fact that she wanted to beat the holy hell out of Regina Winters. But could she do it today? No. Her body wasn’t going to let her. Sure, she’d participate in the tourney, but she wouldn’t get very far. She must’ve pulled something in the ring when she tussled with Regina on Thursday. So this was the new game plan: Show up. Compete. Do the best she possibly could........ And lose. Then come next month, Regina Winters would be hers. And in the end, that’s all that really mattered right now.......* View Biography
