Dear Diary (Part 1) Dan Logan CCW Promo #105 Date: Unknown Location: Unknown Dear Diary, Its been awhile since I spoke to you properly, the last time I was still with Emma and the problems had just begun again. Well she has left now and it seems like my life has been turned upside down, Emma has taken Chris and ran away somewhere with some guy that I don't even know. I've got back together with Dru (I told her I love her) and my new job over at CCW is blooming. Truthfully I lost my last two fights in the company itself but that was because I wasn't fully concentrating on the fights. This time though I'm fighting Scorpion and I know that I'm going to have to concentrate fully otherwise I won't have a hope in hell. Remember Inferno? Well he has 'retired' now but he had been due to fight Scorpion a long time ago but the fight had to be cancelled. I lost to Inferno in his last fight but only just and I know that he would have beat Scorpion so I will win so long as I keep concentrated. I know it sounds stupid but I'm scared, I don't know where Chris is and I hate it when he is gone for so long. Emma can't cope with him alone and I know that this fool that she is with cannot be treating them well. She needs me! There is a fool around CCW called Jason Dynasty. He is killing people, yeah you heard me right he is killing them! I couldn't believe it myself when I first found out but he is and there is nothing being done about it. It's disgusting, I can't believe that Sylkk is letting this happen and if it doesn't stop soon I'm going to have to do something about it myself. Murder is wrong, this guy must be a Sociopath or something because he seems almost to get off on the fact that he is hurting people. How can anyone get off on that fact? How can anyone enjoy the fact that they are hurting someone? It makes no sense... Scorpion isn't as good as he is rated at being I will walk all over him that much is a promise. They have found that the way to take me down is to have two of them on me at the same time and hitting the back of my head...god I hate it when they hit the back of my head. It's like a sharp knife is being pushed gently into my skull for a few seconds and afterward I feel that dizzy it is unbelievable. Remember Bill? Yeah the fool from Ruthless Incorporated. He seems to think that his little brother is better than me, yeah I know what you mean its one of the most amusing things I have heard all month but seriously I've been told I have to teach him a lesson also. I beat Cyrus Skar from LOW and I lost to Shawn Fox. I've beat Fathom of 360 and I've also got Kage lined up for a fight against me very soon. It's quite strange I'm quite busy around this place, unlike when I was at the old independent federations like xWe. I spoke to Eddie last night, yeah he is thinking about attempting to get a contract around PTC. He wants to go big time now, he thinks that he is ready for it. Quite strange really, I've started a crazy but I've told him I'll have a word with Sylkk he might like Eddie...most other people do. We'll see how things go though, things are bound to pick up soon...they have too! Talk again soon, Dan View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
