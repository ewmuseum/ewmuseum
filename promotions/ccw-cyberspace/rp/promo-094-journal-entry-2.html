Journal Entry #2 Scorpion CCW Promo #94 Date: 3-17-03 Location: From the Journal of Scorpion Journal Entry #2 I was always taught to fight. Ever since the day I was born was to never ever take shit from anybody or anyone. I grew up in a family of fighters, my grandfather was a boxer in New York City and my father was a boxer in New York City. They were the toughest men I have ever met in my life. When I was fourteen years old my grandfather and father thought it was the perfect time to teach me how to defend for myself, how to fight back and not take shit from anybody that crosses my path. I’ll never ever forget the lessons I learned. They would take me down to the local athletic club, which would have a boxing ring there. When I first started out they would have me spar a little bit, show me how to throw good left’s and right’s. They were strengthening me mentally and physically. I’d have to move out of the way of their punches to test my reflex skills and in the beginning I would get knocked in the face a lot. But my progress was getting better I could feel myself getting better and better each Saturday we went to the athletic club to practice. I could tell my grandfather and father were impressed with my progress. They were the kind of people that wouldn’t straight up tell you because they did want your head to get to big, but you could tell they were impressed by their facial expression when I would land a right into the punching bag. The one thing my grandfather and father always wanted me to remember was to use my ability of being a fighter when it was necessary, when I needed to defend myself and growing up in the toughest part of the United States, New York City, I would need that a lot to survive. There was never a time when I was happy to learn my fighting skills then when I was walking home from school one day when a bunch of drug addicts out of money went up to me. They wanted my money because they were out of it because they used it all on drugs. You could tell that they were high to the wobbling and red eyes. They told me to hand over my money or they will make me give them money. Being the sarcastic son of a bitch I was I told them no. So they threw a punch at me in which I ducked and ended up knocking one of them on the ground. I held my own for enough time before I got some back up from some by standers. I taught those guys a lesson and gained the confidence I would need to survive in the everyday life of a person living in New York City. I’m glad I learned how to really fight and hold my ground because it comes in handy for what I do today. But most importantly it taught me that I don’t have to back down from anyone, that I could handle my own and take down anyone that ever wanted to test me. It gave me the self-confidence to become stronger mentally as my body physically became stronger. Learning how to fight is a life lesson I’m glad I learned. I don’t know where I would be today without knowing how to fight. Thank you Dad and Grandpa for teaching me the necessary lesson in life to fight. View Scorpion 's Biography Back
