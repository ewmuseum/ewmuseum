Sylkk - Psychotic? I think not (flashback)

1 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP flashback in which Sylkk is evaluated in a psychiatric facility and sedated.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Sylkk Entitled: Psychotic? I think not (flashback) Written in Wednesday, May 1, 2002, 9:38:08 Location: Meade County Hospital This is a: Interview *Scene opens up with several people sitting around watching a small 13' color tv. The are all wearing pajama type outfits and the like. Several men in white trench coats are walking around the room. The walls stretch about 10' up and at the top is a balcony with a guard on each corner, armed with tranquilzer rifles. Upon panning back down, the view zooms into a neutral corner where a man is sitting alone. The doctors come in and lead the man out of the room by his arm, they lead him down the hallway and into a semi-enclosed office. He sits down as the examiner sits across from him with flashcards.* Examiner: What do you see in this picture, Jimmy? Man: The name is Sylkk. Jimmy is gone. He left. Examiner: Ok....Sylkk. Tell me what you see. Sylkk: I see, a large bird...eating a smaller bird....that had just caught a fish. A nice looking rainbow trout at that. Examiner: Rainbow? These pictures are black and white Jimmy. Sylkk: Did you not ask me what I saw? And for the last time, it's Sylkk! Examiner: Riiiight. Ok, next. How about this one? Sylkk: I a man being brutally beaten. Examiner: Last one here. How about this one? Sylkk: Clark! You son of a bitch! WHY!? Why would you sho wme a picture of him? You think this is funny!? ANSWER ME! Examiner: No, it is part of your therapy. We must test you. You see, Sylkk, you have had a history of mental illness. You had been committed before. *The doctor opens up the file on his desk and flips through a couple of pages, before stopping to read briefly then closing the file and turning his attention back to Sylkk* Examiner: Just as I thought, BPD. Borderline Personality Disorder. Sylkk: Bullshit. Examiner: You have all the symptoms. Sylkk: And those are? Examiner: Mood swings, anger problems, self-destructiveness and psychotic rage under stress. *Sylkk curls his lip just a bit as the doctor reveals this to him. He sits back in his chair silently, watching the doctor write something in his file* Sylkk: That was in the past. Examiner: Well, you see. Here is the catch. You can't just 'get over it'. This is something that must be treated with medication and therapy. Sylkk: I went through that already. Eaxaminer: Wll, looking at your file, it says you just stayed and a facility, at your own discretion and the checked yourself out and left. Sylkk: So? Examiner: So it was never dealt with accordingly. This could be extremely dangerous to you or someone else, if I don't take care of this. Sylkk: Look, here's the deal. Group Therapy is coming up in 2 weeks. I have to train. I was shot. I am out of shape. The man I am after is in jail. I will bide my time. However, in the meanwhile I must, get to the gym. You are going to sign my release and I am going home. Easy enough? Examiner: Well unfortunately I am not able to do that. You see, I don't think you are fit for release. Sylkk: Why am I even here? I did nothing, I was a victim, and now you are trying to hold me hostage? Fuck that, and fuck you. I'm out here. *Sylkk gets to his feet, and slides the chair across the room crashing it into a wall, he swings the door open, slamming the handle into the wall as well. He exits out and is immediately tackled by four guards. Three of them restrain him, while the fourth gives him a shot of Prozac, and a tranquilizer. Within minutes Sylkk is about out of it. he can barely stand as they help him to his feet, the lead him to a small room. They sit him on a bed, and across from the bed is a make shift toilet and a shelf full of children's books. He falls over onto the bed and passes out as the door slams shut. The doctor appears behind the guards and peeks in through a tiny window in the door.* Doctor: I am warning everyone. He is VERY unstable. We must have him out of here within two weeks. He will be a danger to society, but he is going to be even more dangerous to us in here. Guard: Can you cure him that fast? Doctor: No. I am not going to administer anything. We will hold him for one and a half weeks, then release him. Hopefully this will at least calm him down a bit. He has had past problems with a institute, but he was not committed this time. He was merely here for an evaluation for trauma. I will simply add to the file, that he has overcome the trauma very well. This is a problem that should have been faced with his last stay. We are not responsible for evaluating his behavior right now. Guard: But... Doctor: No buts. 1 1/2 weeks. No more. I am doing paperwork on his release now. DO NOT open this door for the remainder of his stay. You slide food in when he is sleeping, along with drinks, he is too dangerous to let mingle with our other patients. Bottom line. *The doctor turns and walks off as a guard peeks in through the window and notices Sylkk is already starting to move about.* Guard #1: Look man. That tranq is already wearing off! Guard #2: DAMN! He is an animal! That is a 3 hour tranq! Guard #3: It's been what? 10 minutes? *The three guards eyes widen a bit as they turn and walk away from the door talking amongst themselves. back inside the room Sylkk has risen to a sitting position and has a flame burning in his eye. he looks at the window with the bars over them the back at the door. The scene fades out as a grimmacing look appears on his face* View Biography
