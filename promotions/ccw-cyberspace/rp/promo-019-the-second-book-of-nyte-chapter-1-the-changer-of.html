The Second Book of Nyte: Chapter 1 - The Changer of The Ways Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #19 Date: Feb 18, 2003 Location: The Black Library [Excerpt from the “A look into the Hordes of Chaos: Section 4: The God Tzeentch”] Tzeentch is the Changer of the Ways, and change is the essence of Chaos itself, as well as of the ever-mutating energy that is known to mortals as magic. Also known as Tchar among the barbarians of the north, Chen in the exotic east and Shunch in the steaming jungles of the south, his name is always a byword for change. Everywhere, though, he is the Great Schemer, a subtle manipulator with an all-encompassing knowledge. His plans are inevitably convoluted and vast, spanning across untold aeons, inexplicable and contradictory to all mortal minds. He is the Puppet Master, pulling the strings of Fate and controlling the destiny of his followers and enemies alike. Tzeentch does not have a single form, normally manifesting himself as a cloud of light that constantly changes colour. His symbol is often a representation of the writhing serpent of change, and his daemons and champions are frequently gifted with eerie bird-like beaks, claws and multi-coloured feathers. Their skin and armour is in constant flow, changing shape and colour, forming grotesque cackling faces that mock their opponents, always repeating their words with new and disturbing nuances. [We then head into the Black Library, Gabriel’s new home away from home. A place hidden from all, except for the believers of the Chaos gods. The library is full of many arcane books containing spells, incantations, exorcism rituals, possession rituals, demon summoning, you get the idea. Anything that involves Chaos is in Black Library. Many of the books are very old, spanning back hundreds of years…but some had to be reprinted due to them falling apart from age. We see Gabriel now, garbed in a leather jacket, jeans, and biker boots. His salt and pepper hair is tied back into a pony tail. His eyes, red as flame, are looking through the pages through an aged blue and gold book. He then closes the book and look up.] Gabriel: And so it begins. Lord Tzeentch had foreseen that I would lose my first match to the mountain dragon. A man cannot slay a dragon, that was proven aeons ago when the knights of Europe sent out men to slay the mystical creatures…when it simply took an army of them armed with trebuchets who launched diseased carcasses of cows into dragons caves all throughout the Alps and Urals. Yet…here I stand with three more dragons to slay…or are they sacrifices to Khorne, the blood god. [Gabriel closes the book and rises to his feet taking the book with him, and eventually clutching the book tightly under his arm. He then walks to a shelf bearing the Mark of Tzeentch (which also happens to be the tattoo on Gabriel’s forehead).] Gabriel: I enter a match with a man named after a famous murderer in the nineteenth century, someone who is the bitch of the company, and someone….who just seems normal. Starting with the murderer, you my friend are simply someone with a bad temper. One of the gods may have a use for you in that sense. That is…if you take it too far on more than one occasion. However, you must realize that death and obliteration is inevitable for all…and for some…it comes a lot quicker than others. You must realize that for you…it’ll come very quickly. [Gabriel places the first book back onto the shelf, and pulls another off.] Gabriel: For the bitch…forget it…not even worth my time. You shall be food for the Horrors. They will enjoy your screams. [Gabriel then returns to the same desk he was at before reading the other book.] Gabriel: Finally, to the normal person. You are going to see something that you have probably never even dreamed of. Some things that only appear in nightmares and children’s stories to get them to do chores that they hate to do. Nyte-fall is coming…there is nothing you can do to stop it. My victory is at hand, and I will not only offer three skulls to the throne of Khorne, I will offer the CCW Hardcore title to Tzeentch as a gift. His hard work, giving me insight into the future, informing me of what’s to come, and telling me of the plans of my enemy, and even assisting me on foiling those plans. Hell…he’ll even make the enemy foil their plans up by whispering false information into their minds. The waves are change wash up on my shores…Victory will be mine!
