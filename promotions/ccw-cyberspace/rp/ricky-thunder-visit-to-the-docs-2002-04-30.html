Ricky Thunder - visit to the doc's

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Ricky Thunder visits a doctor who knew his late brother and receives a letter and tape from him.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: visit to the doc's Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 23:40:12 Location: Syracuse NY This is a: Match RP *Scene opens outside of a small hospital in Syracuse. The camera pans the lot and only a few cars can be seen. The scene switches inside the building and to a small room where RickyThunder is lying face down on a table where a doctor is stitching up his back.* Doc: These are some nasty scratches Ricky: Tell me about it. They hurt like hell too. Doc: So you're still gettin in the ring on Thursday huh? Ricky: Yes! Doc: Nothing I can do to change your mind? Ricky: Why? This is my shot at gold. CCW gold! Doc: Well, it is only going to take the slightest bump to reopen these gashes. Ricky: Then I guess I will have to get them re-stitched. Doc: Boy, you are just as stubborn as your brother was. Ricky: You knew Thunder? Doc: I sewed him up several times. He just never knew when to say enough. Ricky: Well, after waiting my time, it has finally come. I earned this shot. I have waited a month to get this shot. I can't back out now. *Ricky sits up gingerly on the table. He winces a bit as he puts his t-shirt on. He slides off the table and makes his way out of the office before the doctor stops him.* Doc: Hey, can I show you something? Ricky: Sure. *The doctor leads Ricky down a long hallway where he enters a small office. He has a seat behind the desk, and offers Ricky a seat on the other side. Ricky sits down carefully as the cdoctor pulls out a small book. He shows it to Ricky who is staring at it in amazement.* Ricky: You mean... Doc: Yes. I sewed your brother up after he met SuMa in the ring. Ricky: How? SuMa jobbed that title to him! Doc: DO NOT BELIEVE THAT! Ricky: But... Doc: Your brother fought for everything he was worth to win that title. SuMa bled him like a stuck pig. They faught partner against partner, neither giving an inch. And you, always thought SuMa jobbed it? Ricky: Yeah..I mean... Doc: No need to explain it Ricky. It is something I just thought you may want to know. *The doctor gets up and leaves the room leaving Ricky sitting alone in the office.* Ricky: Thunder, actually beat you SuMa. You were beatable. By my own flesh and blood, you were defeated. If he could beat you, the I know I have what it takes to defeat Coens. John Coens, you have just appeared in the wrong place at the wrong time. You see, now I have a legacy to fulfill. I have to prove to CCW, I am not my brother. I am better than my brother, I can defeat you as he once defeated SuMa. You see, the time has come for me to begin moving up the ranks of CCW, and you happen to be the first stepping stone. Thursday it will com eto pass that my legacy begins to over-shadow that of my brothers. You see, you have made the mistake of comparing me to my brother, and that is where you are wrong. I think, that were my brother still alive, I could take him. One on One, I could take him. And you...well you are nowhere near the ability of my brother. Thursday your time as Indy champ comes skidding to a halt as the Thunder reigns Supreme. *Ricky gets to his feet and begins to head out the door, and back down the hallway. He makes it to the lobby, before the doctor again stops him.* Doc: Ricky! One more thing! Ricky: Whats up? Doc: Here, I'm sure he would want you to have this! *The doctor hands Ricky a small box, still gift wrapped.* Ricky: It's never been opened. How would you know if he would want me to have it? Doc: Because he told me so. He said he knew that eventually our paths would cross. Good Luck Ricky. *Ricky exits the building and heads to his car. As he opens the door of the black sedan, he gets in and pauses while looking at the box. He opens it up, and inside is a video tape, and a letter. Ricky takes the letter, unfolding it carefully and reads it* Dear Ricky, Guess things got pretty bad. Sorry I couldn't give you this myself, but I knew the time was coming. I want you to watch out for yourself, as I am no longer there to do so. I did however, ask our friend SuMa to keep an eye on you, so he may be there when you least expect it. I just wanted to say, be careful, if you saw the Doc, then you aren't being very careful. I know you have some wild tendencies, but, seriously, you need to watch yourself. In closing, I want you to watch this tape, it is a collection of matches, that I was in. Some you may have seen, some you may have not. But regardless, give it a look. By the way lil bro, I love you. Thunder *Ricky's eyes start to water up as he folds the letter back up and replaces it in the box. He closes it back, and sets the box on the passenger seat.* Ricky: I love you too bro. This one will be for you! Coens, you had better be prepared to feel the Thunder! View Biography
