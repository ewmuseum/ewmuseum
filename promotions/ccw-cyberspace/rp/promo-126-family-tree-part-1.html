Family Tree Part 1 Chloey Mason CCW Promo #126 Date: 04/07/03 Location: Seattle, Washington It's late in the evening, inside a clandestine casino. A croupier in his early fifties, Joe Scanlon, takes in the dice on the craps table. A half dozen gamblers are around the table, and the betting is fast and furious. Other tables are also busy, roulette, blackjack, etc. Smoke is thick in the air.... obviously a clandestine operation in a windowless back room. Suddenly, a smiling, disquieting man appears at Joe's side. His name is Clinch, and he is the bouncer and enforcer at this little fun parlor. He isn't large, but something tells you not to mess with him. Another croupier stands by Clinch's side. Clinch: You're relieved Joe. Joe: Yeah? It's early. Clinch: I said you're relieved. Garrison wants to see you. Joe: Sure. He takes off his apron. The other croupier takes over the table. They move to the bar area. It's not a great bar, and there's not a lot of customers. Most are at the tables. A large, even obese blonde man, Garrison, sits on a stool sipping anisette. He wears a lot of jewelry and a chain around his neck. He even has an earring, but it doesn't suit him. He doesn't know that. Garrison: Joe, have a drink. Joe: I usually don't drink when I'm working the tables, Mister Garrison. Garrison: You're through working the tables, Joe. Joe: {nervous} Sure. I'll.... I'll come in early tomorrow. Clinch: He said you're through working the tables, Joe. Finito. Joe: Oh, hey, if it's about last night, Mister Garrison, that guy was just on a roll. Garrison delicately sips on his anisette. Garrison: Fifty thousand dollars of the house's money isn't 'a roll', Joe.... It's a fucking avalanche! Tell him the rule, Clinch. Tell him rule number one. Clinch: The customer shall not go home with too much of Mister Garrison's money. Joe is sweating now. He'd like to run, but knows he won't get far. Joe: He was watching me, real close, Mister Garrison. I couldn't switch the dice. Garrison: Maybe you didn't want to, Joe. Maybe this was a friend of yours. Joe: Hey, you know I wouldn't do that. Not for nobody. I.... ah.... I ain't got friends I like that much. Garrison: That's very sad. But we believe you. Tell him why we believe him, Clinch. Clinch: This big winner skipped town last night. Skipped town without even checking out of his hotel. Joe is startled, angry, sickened. He tries to hide it. Joe: Ain't that a bitch. Garrison: So, if you were working with him, it means he skipped out on you, too. {slight pause} Tell him his responsibilities, Clinch. Clinch: You're responsible for your table.... You're responsible for the fifty big ones. Garrison: And I'm sure you want to pay me back. Joe stares. He smiles first, then loses it. Joe: Ah.... fifty thousand.... Ah, that's a lot of money. Garrison: {growing angry} I think that's my point, Joe. I think that's exactly my point. Joe: I'm.... I'm gonna need some time. Garrison: Sure, Joe. We know that. Tell him how much time, Clinch. Clinch: Can you count to five? Sure you can, Joe. Clinch takes Joe's right hand, lifts it up, then gently, takes the thumb, and begins counting on each of Joe's fingers. Clinch: Five days. One.... {bends back thumb} Two.... {bends index finger} Three.... {bends back middle finger} Four.... {bends back ring finger} Five! Clinch jerks back on the pinkie finger, breaking it! Joe screams in agony. Joe: Aaaaaahhh! Elsewhere in the city, at an orphanage office to be exact, Chloe Mason stands before a formidable woman, the orphanage's secretary. Chloe wears a messenger service cap on her cute head and carries a clipboard. Secretary: {studies file} I've got Chloe Mason's file right here, but I have no record of anyone asking for it. Chloe: {chews gum; refers to clipboard} For a.... Doctor James Jones. Cross town address. Hey, that's all I know. Chloe reads the file while the nurse has it flipped upside down. Secretary: We're not supposed to release orphanage files without Authorization. There are laws regarding confidentiality. Chloe: Guy's a doctor. Sounds sweet to me. Secretary: Nobody's asking you. Chloe: Hey, I'm just the messenger. The secretary now studies Chloe. Secretary: Just which company did you say you worked for? Chloe hesitates. Then she hands the secretary the clipboard. Chloe: Look for yourself. She takes the clipboard in one hand, holding the file in another. Secretary: This doesn't.... Chloe quickly snatches the file from her hand and bolts for the door. Secretary: Hey? You can't.... Come back here! But Chloe is history. Out in the stairwell of the orphanage, Chloe dashes around a corner and stops. She opens the file, and begins quickly perusing when, suddenly, a burly security guard grabs her. Guard: Right there! Chloe manages a cute, yet flirtatious smile, and glances down at the badge on the guard's shirt. Chloe: Hello.... {reads badge} ....Harold! Ahh.... Is there a problem? Guard: You steal that? Chloe: Before I answer that, Harold.... have you had the mandatory course in prudent and appropriate use of force? Guard: Stop talkin' shit and turn around so I can cuff your ass. Chloe: I guess that means no. Hey Harold, you look like the type of guy that would like to do something to my ass besides cuff it. Guard: Now, lightning lips! Chloe assumes the position against the wall as she keeps up her spiel as the guard cuffs her. Chloe: This is all just a test of your security here. I'm an undercover agent with the Public Works Special Investigation Unit. Ahh, the cuffs are a little tight, Harold. According to my stopwatch, your response time was within our codes. You should be very happy about this, Harold. And the guard pulls her away from the wall and leads her off down the hall.... View Chloey Mason 's Biography Back
