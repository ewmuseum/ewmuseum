Road To The PPV, Part 1

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which The Supreme Machine forces a doctor to clear him to wrestle with a cracked forearm.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Road to the PPV....Pt 1 Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 12:41:36 Location: ?? This is a: Match RP OK. Just one more to go The Supreme Machine sits patiently in the medical room as the CCW doctor leans over him and applies the last of many stitches to his scalp. Just get it over and done with.. I've wasted enough of my time sitting here. I'm going as fast as I can. If you're going to go around getting hit in the head with chairs, you have to expect to spend some time getting stitched back together. Anyway, if I were you I'd be thankful that you're not worse off than you are. You took some hellish shots tonight. That was nothing. It'll take a damn sight more than that to do me much damage. The doctor ties off the last of the stitches. He trims it up and applies a bandage. As he motions for The Supreme Machine to move to the seat in front of his desk, he continues. I'm afraid I'll have to disagree with you on that. The doctor sits down behind his desk and picks up an x-ray. Why? What have you found. He holds the x-ray up to the light so The Supreme Machine can see it better. It is of his right forearm. It would appear that you've cracked your radius. I strongly suspect it was from those chair shots you took in the beginning of you match tonight against Bojangles. So what does this mean? I'll still be able to wrestle at the PPV on Sunday night.. right? The doctor thinks a moment before answering. Well..... I don't know. WHAT DO YOU MEAN YOU DON'T KNOW! I will be wrestling at the PPV. No matter what! Look, I understand your position but you have to understand mine. If I let you go out into the ring and you damage your arm somemore, then I'm liable. The Supreme Machine wipes the hair from his face with his good left hand, his growing frustration apparent. I'm going to be at this PPV no matter what, I'm telling you. What if I wear some sort of protection on my forearm? And I'll sign a waiver or something saying that I take full responsibility if something happens to me. You won't have to worry. The doctor puts the x-ray down and looks nervously across his table at The Supreme Machine. He starts to shake his head. His patient is beginning to become aggitated. I don't think you heard me doctor. I said I am going to be at that PPV no matter what! The doctor still shakes his head I can't allow it. I just can't. The Supreme Machine is struggling to control his rage. He gets to his feet and leans over the doctor's desk. You're going to sign my clearance! I'm going to the PPV. It's the night I've lived for my entire life. This Sunday night I'm not only going to become the King of Extreme BUT I'm also going to regain MY CCW World Heavyweight Championship. You're not going to stop me. I won't let you. The doctor swallows hard as he leans back into his chair as hard as he can. The Supreme Machine continues. It will take more than a sore arm to stop me from fullfilling my destiny. Pain is nothing... My arm will heal. Fighting through the pain is what makes a champion different from everyone else. It's about having the heart to go on when lesser men would just give up. It's why that fool Carnage ended up laying unconcious amongst the broken pieces of the CCW announce table and why that pathetic clown, Bojangles, could hit me with chairs all day and I'd still get up and destroy him every time. It's why no matter how much CCW try to screw with The Supreme Team, they'll never beat us. It's why every single wrestler in CCW had better think twice before they EVER mess with me again. It's what makes me different. It's what makes me better. It's why I'm going to win on Sunday night. The Supreme Machine calms down a little and moves away from the terrified doctor. Just fit me with an arm brace and sign my clearance. Then I'll get out of your office and leave you in one piece. Alright. But I want it put on the record that it's against my better judgement. I want it known that you're taking full responsibility for any further injury that you may incur. The five times heavyweight champion explodes. He reaches over the table, grabs the doctor tightly around his throat and drags him effortlessly out of his seat, holding him right infront of his face. I TOLD YOU I'D SIGN THE WAIVER NOW YOU SIGN THE GODDAMN MEDICAL CLEARANCE!!! He drops the doctor on top of his table. NOW!!!! The shaken doctor staggers around to his side of the table and sits shakily down in his chair once again. OK. OK. Just don't hurt me. I'll sign the clearance. The Supreme Machine stands glaring at him as he scrawls the clearance on his writing pad. He throws the completed medical certificate at his patient. There! Take it. He scrawls something else on another piece of paper and throws that at The Supreme Machine as well. And that's the name and number of another doctor who will fit you with a forearm protector. I'll call ahead and warn him to expect you. The Supreme Machine grabs the pieces of paper and begins to walk out of the doctor's office. There you go. You could have avoided all this aggrevation if you'd have just signed the clearance to begin with. The shaken doctor finally losses it and begins sobbing at his desk. You're crazy, you know that!! I hope someone kills you at this stupid, goddamn PPV. I hope someone cripples you, you bastard!!! The Supreme Machine just chuckles to himself as he walks out the door, leaving it open behind him. I'm sure you're not the only one doc. I'm sure you're not the only one. View Biography
