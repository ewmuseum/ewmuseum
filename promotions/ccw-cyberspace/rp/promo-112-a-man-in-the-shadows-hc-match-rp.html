A Man In The Shadows (HC Match RP)

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Man In The Shadows (HC Match RP) Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #112 Date: Location: Dusk has fallen. Here in the deep countryside the night time creature begins to stir. A hazy reddish moon hangs low in the sky above. The stately home of Jack Manson, dark and foreboding, is seen in the distance. Each wing, each bedroom, each dining area has it's window curtains pulled closed tight. The lights are out, except for one. One single, solitary light breaks the darkness descending on the night. The light in question eminates from Jack’s study. As the camera moves in closer, the house appears larger and larger... until it appears to stand high above all of the trees surrounding it. The camera moves to the window where the light bleeds through it's curtains. The camera tilts, facing a small slit within the curtains and allowing us to see inside. ...We can see Jack, his back to us, standing aside some sort of small box on the floor. Suddenly, the sound of crashing drums and heavy guitar fill the air. The frantic sounds of a piano echoes in the night sky, causing birds to flee their from their safe haven up in the trees like a scene from a Hitchcock movie. Jack then turns around, staring at the camera with a grin of rage crossing his face, as the vocals kick in...: The sirens are screaming and the fires are howling way down in the valley tonight. He grins wider, ear-to-ear, and narrows his eyes. There's a man in the shadows with a gun in his eye and a blade shining oh so bright. Jack, keeping his grin, speaks during every pause within the music. 'For weeks the war has raged. Both the physical and mental battles have been faught.' There's evil in the air and there's thunder in the sky and a killer's on the bloodshot streets. 'Some wars I've won, others I've lost. ...But throughout it all, I've achieved my goals. I did what I set out to do.' And down in the tunnel where the deadly are rising, oh, I swear I saw a young boy down in the gutter, he was starting to foam in the heat. 'I have watched, I have waited, and I have made you take notice.' Oh Baby, you're the only thing in this whole world that’s pure and good and right. Jack's face relaxes a little as his grin lowers to a wide smirk. 'And I have made you bleed. I have spilled the very life that runs through your veins.' And where ever you are and where ever you go there's always gonna be some light. 'And, do you know what?' But I gotta get out, I gotta break it out now before the final crack of dawn. 'I liked it. ...I liked it more than I thought I would. It’s what drove me on. It's my motivation... it is my desire. So we gotta make the most of our one night together, when it's over you know we'll both be so alone. 'And soon, CCW will realize exactly what they have on their hands.' Like a bat out of hell, I’ll be gone when the morning comes. 'Revolution will go down in history as one of bloodiest events in CCW... ever.' When the night is over, like a bat out of hell, I'll be gone, gone, gone. 'Enigma, you were not the one I was watching. You are not the one I have infected. You are not the one who’s path should have crossed with mine.' Like a bat out of hell, I'll be gone when the morning comes. 'But it did.' But when the day is done and the sun goes down and the moonlight's shining through. 'And now, you and I must dance in front on thousands.' And like a sinner before the gates of heaven, I'll come crawling on back to you. 'But don’t worry, Enigma. ...For we aren't strangers to one another, as you may think. For I know all about you. I know everything that you're capable of.' I’m gonna hit the highway like a battering ram on a silver black phantom bike. When the metal is hot and the engine is hungry and we're all about to see the light. 'The question is…do you know what I'm capable of? ...Do you know anything of my disregard for the very blood that pumps through your heart?' Nothing ever grows in this rotten old hole and everything is stunted and lost. And nothing really rocks and nothing really rolls and nothing's ever worth the cost. 'They call me a soulless man, Enigma. ...They say I have no respect, no morale’s' And I know that I'm damned if I never get out, and maybe I'm damned if I do. But with every other beat I got left in my heart, you know I'd rather be damned with you.' 'I say... in this sport, you leave your morale’s at the door... and you do whatever you have to do to survive.' If I gotta be damned you know I wanna be damned dancing through the night with you. 'And that is exactly what I do, week after week. I do whatever it takes to not only survive, but to educate you pathetic fools.' If I gotta be damned you know I wanna be damned--gotta be damned you know I wanna be damned--If Gotta be damned you know I wanna be damned dancing through the night, dancing through the night, dancing through the night with you. 'Thursday, Enigma, will be the day that you learn just how little I care. You will see just how far behind I leave my morale’s.' Oh Baby, you're the only thing in this whole world that's pure and good and right. And wherever you are, and wherever you go, there's always gonna be some light. 'And when its over--when the blood has dried--when the screams have ended... only one us will walk away.' But I gotta get out. I gotta break it out now before the final crack of dawn. So, we gotta make the most of our one night together; when it's over you know we'll both be so alone. 'And I intend on walking... because when I’m done with you, I`m heading for more gold. Oh yes, you are not the only unfortunate one, Enigma. ...There are others.' Jacks head darts to the side as he spots something out of the corner of his eye. He mutters something under his breath as he turns round and turns the music off. As the music ends we can now hear a phone ringing. Jack walks over to it and we see it was a little flashing red light on the phone that caught his attention. He answers it. 'What? ...No, he's not with me. I thought he was with you? ...What do you mean, he’s escaped again? ...I mean, what the HELL am I paying you for, doctor?! ...No, I have a better idea. Why don’t YOU find him before I find the need to come and pay you a visit, eh?! ...Yeah, you do that, doctor. ...Please tell me he didn’t take any more of those pills your staff saw fit to leave carelessly lying around, again? ...What do you mean, you DON’T know?! I am beginning to lose patience. Dr. Forbes! What kind of hospital are you running up there? ...Look, I don’t care, okay!? Just find him! I need him to be focused, and preferably in one piece. ...GOODBYE Doctor! I think you have some work to do!' Jack slams the phone back on the receiver and rolls his eyes skywards before clenching his fist and lightly tapping himself on the forehead. 'GODDAMIT, KID!!!' Jack paces back and forth, putting his hands to his hips and shaking his head. 'Less than a week before CCW Revolution, and you STILL decide to fuck around! What the HELL are you thinking!?' Jack rubs his chin, grumbling cuss words to himself before picking up the phone again and punching in a 3 digit number. He puts it to his ear. '...Bring the car 'round front right away. We’re going into the city.' He hangs up and pauses for a moment to collect his thoughts. He sighs to himself. '...Why the hell do I bother, anyway? That stupid fucking kid. ...Well, if the streets don’t kill him, he'd better be damn sure he knows I will.' With that, Jack turns and marches out of the study. The camera zooms in slowly on the radio, before fading to black. TO BE CONTINUED… View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
