Shocking Experience!

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A scripted alley scene in which Sylkk, returning after being shot, accepts a rookie’s open challenge from Lightning for a hardcore match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Sylkk Entitled: Shocking Experience! Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 21:17:43 Location: Jacksonville, Fl This is a: Match RP *Scene opens up as Sylkk is seen dipping into an alleyway. He is on his cell phone as cameras catch him* Sylkk: I know! Don't think you are in charge ok! I know how to handle my business! I have been doing this since before you were even around! Yeah, well fuck your titles! *Sylkk flips his phone shut and shoves back into his pocket. He continues into the alley, he decides to stop and lean against a wall as he fires up a smoke stick. He takes a hard drag before throwing it into the trash that has complied along the walls.* Sylkk: Who the fuck does he think he is? Tell ME how to do things! Fuck him! I don't need him. Then to top things off, there is some rookie running around here running off at the lip. he thinks he is going to be the next big thing, he thinks he has what it takes to lay out an open challenge. He is going to experience the same fate as that of anyone who chooses to cross me. For you see, the time has come for me to extract on CCW what has been brought upon me. Pain Desperation and Complete humility For you see, when I was shot, when I was hospitalized, when I was taken out of the picture, I was bestowed with all of those. For you see, the pain I have endured, will be that which none of you will ever know! The desperation I felt, that of clinging to a meaningless life. A life I created and have lived. A life that again none of you will ever have any insight to. Why would there be desperation if it is so meaningless? Why you ask? WHY? Because it is mine. *Sylkk kicks a bit of trash across the alley as his phone begins to ring. he reaches into his pocket and answers it.* Sylkk: Hello? And? Well don't sweat it. Shit happens. I have more important shit on my mind right now. I am debating on this little punk who layed out the open challenge. The one who thinks he has what it takes. Yeah. Lightning. Cool. I will get back to you. *Sylkk again closes the phone and takes a few steps towards the entrance of the alley. He looks out across the street as the neon lights of the city reflect through a window.* Sylkk: You see Lightning, what you failed to realize, and what you fail to understand is you are going to be my first singles match back. I of course, carried my team through round one at Group Therapy before obliterating that 8' retard and Eric Clark. You are in for a ride you could never be prepared for. You wanted this match to be hardcore, you had better hope they didn't oblige your wishes, because you are looking at a former Hardcore champion. So you think it is your game? You think that is playing by your rules? You had better realize what you are getting yourself into. 3 careers ended, and yours just may be next. *Sylkk drops his head as his small jacket flaps open in the wind. He sticks the phone back into his pocket and heads across the stree to a gentleman's club to relax before flying out for LockDown* View Biography
