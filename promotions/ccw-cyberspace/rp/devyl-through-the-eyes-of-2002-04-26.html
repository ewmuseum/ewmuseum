Devyl - Through the Eyes of...

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP of several linked scenes involving Devyl, Agnes, Mr. Raymond and Rachael in the lead-up to Group Therapy.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: Through the Eyes of... Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 17:10:07 Location: Multiple This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on a retirement community. It is a nice, sunny day and the residents of this particular community go about their lives with their regular diligence... All but one. Agnes Knick sits in her rocker on the large back porch area behind the retirement home. She has been awfully fidgety all morning, and the repeated attempts of nurses to offer her calming medication have met with strong denial. And so Agnes sits in her rocker, agitated for no apparent reason at all. But if they only knew...] [Agnes can feel the storm coming. Not a storm with wind, rain and lightning, but a storm of blood, sweat and tears. She truly wishes she could vocalize her feelings to the kind people who run this place, but they wouldn’t believe her. No one would believe her. And she could only hope that she was safe here. But deep down, she knew she wasn’t. The devil was coming for her. And after the many sins she had committed, she would go willingly.] [The Devil would come as a young woman by the name of Victoria Devins... Agnes’s sister’s daughter. And oh how she wished that she could turn back time. When Patrick Devins came to her and told her of his plans to use little Victoria and her sister, Melissa, Agnes should have turned him away. But she didn’t. Instead, she played her part in what could possibly be considered one of mankind’s darkest moments. Little Melissa had disappeared and little Victoria had changed. Somehow, Vicky had been able to initiate a ceremony of her own before her father tried to go through with his. She had changed… Changed terribly. And when they tried to sacrifice Victoria... Everything had gone wrong.] [Agnes shudders a bit at the memory. She didn’t want to think about these things anymore. The day was far too nice and pleasant to be dwelling on those old memories. But they were there, and wouldn’t leave her alone. Victoria would be coming for her soon. Most likely looking for her sister. But just who Agnes would be dealing with... Vicky or the Devil himself... Was another matter entirely. She might be able to bargain with the information about Melissa’s whereabouts, but she wasn’t counting on that. The devil was coming for her soon, and there wasn’t going to be anything she could do about it. She wouldn’t be able to stop it.] [A chill wind suddenly spring up around Agnes, and she shivers. Soon... It would happen soon...] [The scene flips over to New York. Madison Square Garden in particular. The usual set-up is proceeding as scheduled, and Mr. Raymond is found wandering the back halls and making sure everything is going as planned. If everything were to go as planned, he would be able to get his wife back. And that fact made him a very happy man.] [If only Devyl were in a better mood. Ever since Lockdown, she was trying to cope with the fact that David had been “corrupted”. And she wasn’t taking it very well. Only problem, Raymond thought, as that she had better get her head on straight pretty quickly. She had a big challenge ahead of her this evening. She was going to have to ensure that no one would be able to challenge her right for the women’s title. But in her current mindset, he didn’t know if she could do it. Each team within the tournament had its strengths. And the important thing to remember, Devyl had told him, was that each team had their crippling weaknesses as well.] [As true as that might be, there was going to be a big fight, regardless. As a part of CCW’s staff, Raymond was very happy about this tournament. It wasn’t every month that every single CCW superstar had the chance to vie for the richest prize in the business all in the same night. The landscape of CCW could be forever altered, depending on who would come out victorious. And yes... There was speculation amongst himself and the other employees about who was going to come away with the victory. Many people thought it was going to be SuMa. Others thought Neurosis was going to hold onto the title. John Coens, Jason McAllister, Kensuke Suzahara and Cianan were also names that were being bandied about. And no one thought that any of the women were going to make it...] [And why should they? The men were obviously that much more powerful and dominant in the ring. But there were two that Raymond thought could possibly do it... Two that had a little bit more going for them. Call it heart, call it determination, call it just plain bitchiness... But Devyl and Lacey Skye were two hellcats that could definitely pull off an upset victory. And although Raymond doubted it would happen, it wasn’t outside the realm of extreme possibility. Many of his colleagues would have laughed at him if he told them about these thoughts. And not that he cared, but he definitely didn’t want to draw more attention to himself than he already had. He was about to get his wife back! And if Devyl was correct in what she told him, he would need time to rest and deal with the fact that his wife would be inhabiting his body.] [If Devyl had to defend her title, Raymond would most definitely be in her corner. And he felt sorry for anyone who would be facing her. but honestly, he would be just as happy if one of the men won and Devyl didn’t have to defend her title. That would be one extra step in the right direction...] [Sighing to himself, Raymond continues his rounds. Soon, this arena would be packed with thousands of screaming fans. It was going to be a good night. He could feel it in his bones...] [The scene flips one last time... To Devyl’s mansion in Aurora, IL. It is quiet and dark, and we find Devyl’s servant, Rachael, standing outside a pair of closed double doors. Devyl was inside, doing Lord knows what.] [Rachael was in the kitchen, tidying everything up when Devyl came home. She wasn’t in a very good mood. She had someone with her, but who it was Rachael didn’t know. She saw Devyl leading someone upstairs to this room. Rachael followed after them, wanting to check and see if everything was all right and if she could offer any assistance. She made it up the stairs and found Devyl standing outside the room, just having shoved someone inside. And Rachael called out to Devyl:] Rachael: “Welcome home, mistress. Does everything go well?” [Devyl’s eyes had lashed into her, and Rachael cringed a bit under her gaze. Something was obviously not right. Devyl had seemed to be in a bad mood much of the time recently, but this was really bad. And what Devyl had replied with made Rachael worry even more:] Devyl: “Rachael... No matter what you may hear over the course of the next hour, you will NOT enter this room.” Rachael: “But...” Devyl: “Under NO circumstance! Is that clear?” Rachael: “Yes mistress, but...” Devyl: “I will be back out shortly. Keep everyone out of here, Rachael. I will not tolerate any interruptions, no matter how grave the matter may be.” [Rachael had nodded and Devyl had entered the room. That had been half an hour ago. And within that time, there had been anguished cries of despair and tormented screams of pain. It was almost as if some weird form of inhuman torture was going on inside that room. Most of the screams sounded unearthly and vague.] [Racahel tried to think of what could possibly be going on. She had seen Lockdown, and watched the confrontation between Gabriel Nyte and Devyl. Gabriel had mentioned returning David to Devyl. Had that happened? Is that who Devyl was in the room with right now? If so, then unless Devyl somehow managed to cleanse him of Gabriel’s influence, she would most likely kill him. And she didn’t want to see that happen. She liked David... A lot. When he was around, Devyl’s stormy mood would always disappear. He had a calming effect on her, and she hadn’t known anyone that had been able to do that before.] [Rachael also wondered how this was going to affect the Group Therapy tournament. She had watched with interest as President Spider pulled the team names and put Devyl and Gabriel Nyte on the same team. Would she be able to work with him after something like this? That, she didn’t know. But she did know that Devyl needed to make it as far in that tournament as she possibly could to keep her title belt that seemed so very necessary for her. Would she really let a personal vendetta interfere in her goal of winning the tournament?] [The double doors suddenly open, and Devyl walks out. Her entire body is covered in sweat, and her damp hair clings to her face. She’s breathing heavily, and her black leather outfit definitely wasn’t helping her much. Letting the doors swing closed behind her, she looks Rachael directly in the eyes again. Rachael, despite what she sees, stands her ground. A long pause stretches between them before Devyl finally speaks.] Devyl: “Group Therapy, Rachael... At Group Therapy, there will be a reckoning. And NO ONE will be able to stop it!” [fade out...] View Biography
