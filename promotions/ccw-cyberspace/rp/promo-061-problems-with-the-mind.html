Problems with the mind

Problems with the mind Andrew 'Ace' Hawk CCW Promo #61 Date: March 4th 2003 Location: Hollywood **The scene today opens at a local shopping center, somewhere in Hollywood. Across the big hall the camera is facing, the entrance of a multiplex. By the quantity of people standing in front of it, including kids, it's clear we're in a weekend day, but we don't get the information of which one. From above the hall, through the glass ceiling, the beautiful blue sky helps give the place a nice and cozy ambience. That's exactly when we see Andrew Hawk enter the hall through a side entrance. Wearing his dark ray-ban shades and with his hair hidden behind his trenchcoat, it's clear he doesn't want to be recognized. He walks around, looking for some place. Going across a McDonalds, a couple of guys, about 13 years old and wearing CCW T-shirts, stop him. One of them, a chubby kid with huge bracers, ask:** 'Hey, ain't you Ace Hawk? I'm your biggest fan!!' 'Sorry, kid...', answers Ace. 'I'm not him. I get that all the time.' 'Nah, you're him, I know it', the other kid, a tall skinny black kid says. 'Your voice and style are unique.' 'No, sorry. Look, I'm in a hurry...', Ace says, sighing, and moving away. The kids look at him, disappointed, but decide to follow him. **Ace keeps moving forward, not noticing the kids behind him. His face shows clearly how worried he seems to be. After walking a few more yards, he looks at an elevator to the left, used only by local workers and security. Ace gets in it, leaving the kids behind. After 2 floors, the door opens and an old bald man enters the elevator. The doors close and as the elevator begins to go down, Ace presses an emergency button, locking the elevator between floors. The old man looks funny at Ace and says:** 'Hey, what's going on?' 'Cut the crap, Dr. Lewis. I'm the man Alex told you to meet.' **The man's face lightens a little, seemingly knowing about it. He says:** 'So, you're the patient?' 'Yeah...', Ace says, before taking his shades off to look at the doctor's eyes. 'Now, this is a pretty delicate situation. Nobody can know about this, all right?' 'I don't understand... Why is that?' 'Because... Now go ahead and tell me what's wrong with my head?', Ace says, visibly disturbed. 'Ok...', the doctor says, before opening a small chart he has under his arm. 'You have a concussion.' **Ace looks down, relieved. The doctor, however, doesn't look all that relieved. He says:** 'But there's a problem, Mr. Hawk.' 'What's it?', Ace answers, looking a little worried. 'You see, Mr. Hawk, you have a degenerative condition, thanks to the years of mistreated migraines and concussions.' 'Oh, just like Troy Aikman? I can handle that. Aikman was a pussy, anyway.', Ace says, laughing. 'Yeah, well, you're a couple of levels below Aikman, but if you don't take care, you can catch him up and go WAY beyond him.' 'How far?', Ace asks, now really worried. 'Mohammed Ali-far', sentences the doctor, drawing a cloud on Ace's soul. He shakenly presses the emergency button again and it goes back down. **The door opens on the hall level and the kids are there, waiting for him. That visibly shakes Ace, sicne he doesn't have his shades on, making him very recognizable. He sighs and greets the kids, as they walk in the elevator, together with a couple hundred people, dying to meet him. After a few hours, Ace finally finishes the impromptu autograph signing and walks to his car. Getting in it, he starts banging his head lightly on the steering wheel, his face showing discontent, at the sign of his career rapidly approaching a dead end. He shakes off and turns the car on. That's when his cell phone rings. He answers it, and it's Debbie Carmichael on the other end:** 'Hey, Ace. Where are you?' 'Nowhere...', Ace answers. 'I'm just going to grab a lunch on McDonald's.' 'Oh, cool, grab a Big Mac for me. Now, I have some news.' 'Shoot it...', answers Ace, still not very interested. 'CCW will have a lethal lothery tournament for the CCW Tag-Team titles. It begins this Thursday, and you're in it.' 'Oh, great, just what I needed right now... Who did they assign for me?', Ace asks, already imagining his fate. 'The Gobber...' **Ace shakes his head, not happy about it.** 'Damn it. Why can't I get a nice break on the tag titles? It's the only fricking title I need to become a Grand-Slam Champion, and they put me with one of the biggest losers this company has ever had? Damn it!!' 'Well, Ace, it's the luck of the draw... Anyway, I gotta go, bye.' **Ace turns his phone down, as he drives throught he dark streets of Hollywood by night. As he drives around and a light rain starts to fall, Ace says:** 'Damn it, I HAVE to win those titles. It's now or never... It's now or never...' **As he says that, the camera fades to black** View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
