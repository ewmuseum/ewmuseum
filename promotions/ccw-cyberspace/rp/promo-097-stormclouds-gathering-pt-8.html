Stormclouds Gathering Pt. 8 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #97 Date: 03/18/03 Location: Seattle, Washington Jonathan's antique store is quiet and subdued. It is late in the day, no customers. The rays of the setting sun shine through the windows. In Jonathan's studio, Lacey is throwing a couple of things together in preparation for her meeting with Quartre. She slips on her leather coat, grabs her small purse, and heads for the door. Christine Oakes lounges on a Louis XV love seat, one leg thrown over the side. She looks resigned. Damsel: You know, I've been thinking.... Ever since I've known you, everything has always been about YOU. You always get the guys.... Always have to be the center of attention. Lacey: Only lately. Damsel: Only lately? Lacey: You heard me. Damsel: What are you talking about? Lacey: I seem to recall a time in the not-so-distant past when you were the one who wanted someone so badly that you decided to pursue a man you knew nothing about. He was a serial killer, and yet you stayed with him. And from what I recall, he made you the center of the universe when you were with him. That doesn't seem to be the case any longer. You can't tell me that you're jealous that he's trying to kill me instead of you. Damsel: {insulted} Don't be ridiculous, Lacey. That's absurd! What drew me to him ended about a year ago! Lacey: That's what I said, 'lately'. Damsel: You know what your problem is? You're self-centered. Lacey: I don't have a problem. Damsel: Yes you do. Lacey: No I don't. Damsel: Yes you do. Lacey: No I don't. Stop arguing with me. Damsel: I'm not arguing. Lacey: Yes you are. Damsel: I'm not. It's a conversation. Lacey: It's a conversation? Well then, end of conversation. Damsel: Lacey? Lacey: What? Swiftly, Damsel punches Lacey right across the face, knocking her down and out with a sucker punch. Damsel: End of conversation. Jonathan: Lacey! Jonathan stands on the stairs, looking on in shock at what he just witnessed. Damsel rushes to put on her coat, then looks down at Lacey's unmoving body. Damsel: She's going to be okay. She won't be out long enough for what I need, but.... Jonathan: {making his way into the room to check on Lacey} You don't have to do this either. Just call the police and have them catch this guy! Damsel: The police can't catch him. {turns to go} Take care. I really hope I see you again. And with that, Damsel exits the room and leaves the antique shop. Christine crosses away from the antique store to her car, which is an old black Cadillac. She's unaware of Chloe Mason who is hiding close to the vehicle. She sees Damsel coming and ducks out of sight. Chloe: {under her breath} Dammit.... The mystery woman. Damsel gets into her vehicle, starts it up and drives slowly out of the alley. Chloe is riding seated on the broad back bumper where Damsel can't see her. At Soldier Bridge, the last rays of sunlight streak through the air. The bridge itself is a gothic wonder. A lone man in an elegant coat leans over the rail, admiring the view. Steven Quartre. He checks his Rolex watch, then takes out a woman's compact and examines his face in it. A lock of his long, reddish-brown hair is out of place. He fixes it and resumes his vigil. Back in the antique shop, Lacey is coming around as Jonathan holds a cold rag to her head. Lacey's eyes focus and she stands up. Lacey: Where is she? Jonathan is suddenly very tired. Jonathan: Where do you think? Lacey says nothing. Jonathan: {shaking his head} I know.... You're going to go after her. {wearily} There's no chance you'll change your mind and call the police? Lacey adjusts her coat and grabs her car keys off the nearby table. Lacey: Jonathan.... Damsel's right when she says the police can't stop him. I need to do this. Jonathan stares at her, finally realizing how hard-headed and serious she is about this. Jonathan: Is this really this simple.... This straight-forward for you? Lacey: I take care of my own problems. Jonathan: And if you can't take care of this one? Lacey: Then what's happened here with us will happen again someplace else. Jonathan: Then my thoughts and prayers go with you. Good luck. And without another word, Lacey races out of the store into the night.... View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
