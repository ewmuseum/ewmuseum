Sweet Victory...

What this is A short parking-lot scene after SMN in which Devyl, Mr. Raymond and her husband celebrate her becoming number one contender.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: Sweet Victory... Written in Tuesday, May 14, 2002, 01:23:58 Location: The Conseco Fieldhouse This is a: Interview [The scene opens up in the parking lot of the Conseco Fieldhouse, immediately following SMN. Devyl walks alongside Mr. Raymond and her husband, David Oakes, while Urban Outlaw brings up the rear, carrying Devyl's personal effects. They are all, needless to say, in a very good mood.] Raymond: 'Tonight was a great night! You should all be very proud of yourselves. Everything worked perfectly! Our lovely Devyl is the #1 contender...' David: **Smirks** 'Like there was any doubt of that...' Raymond: 'And Deborah Carmichael got exactly what she deserved! That plan was perfect! I'm sure she'll remember that beating for days to come.' David: 'You deserve some credit too! That was a nice intercept you pulled on Lacey Skye. That strawberry blonde bimbo doesn't stand a chance at MDS!' Devyl: 'Calm yourself, David. We have much work to do before that. Now that we have acquired the tablet and I have cemented my status as #1 contender, everything is firmly under control. Now, our objectives are to finish the translations on the tablet, and do whatever we can to make Lacey's next few weeks a living hell!' David: 'Right... I'm catching a plane back to Chicago right now. Rachael and the others should've made some progress on the tablet by now. I'll meet up with you guys at the United Center.' [David leans over and gives Devyl a kiss on the mouth. She smiles at him as he goes to help Outlaw with storing the luggage in the car.] Raymond: 'You're going to be champion again soon.' Devyl: 'Yes... I am.' Raymond: 'And then... Then you will give me my wife back?' Devyl: 'That is correct.' Raymond: 'And no more stalling this time. You promise to do it immediately...' Devyl: 'I promise, Mabius. As soon as I take the title from Lacey Skye, your wife is coming back to you.' [A slow smile begins to spread across Raymond's face.] Raymond: 'Well then... If David has the translation aspect of our objectives covered, then I'll take it upon myself to make Lacey's life... Miserable!' Devyl: **smiles** 'Let us go, Mabius. We have much to do, and much to discuss...' [fade out...] View Biography
