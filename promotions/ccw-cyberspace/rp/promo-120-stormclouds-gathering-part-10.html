Stormclouds Gathering: Part 10

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stormclouds Gathering: Part 10 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #120 Date: 03/25/03 Location: Seattle Washington Lacey Skye Morris runs as fast as she can over Soldier Bridge. She reaches the combatants and looks on in shock as Damsel get flipped off the bridge. Quartre hasn't noticed her and peers over the bridge down into the waters below. Lacey: Quartre! Quartre turns around and finally notices Lacey, who stands directly under one of the street lamps on the old, deserted bridge. Quartre raises his pistol and aims it directly at Lacey's face. There's a moment of pause and silence as the two of them face off with each other. After a bit, Quartre lowers his gun and drops it on the ground. He takes several steps forward and picks up Damsel's discarded knife instead. He grins at her as he stands up, the right side of his face a scarlet mask of blood. Quartre: {taunting} It'll be more fun this way. Lacey: {ignoring} It ends now. And the fight is on between these two. Quartre lunges forward and feints with a slashing motion. Lacey sidesteps to avoid, but Quartre is quick and grabs her by the hair, pulling her off her feet so that she lands on her back. Still holding her hair, Quartre brings the knife down to slit her throat. Lacey catches his arm, and then uses both of hers to prevent him from lowering the knife any closer. She kicks up and lays her booted foot into the back of his head, knocking him off her. They both get back on their feet, Quartre after her in a flash. Lacey rushes over to Quartre's Humvee and leaps up onto the car. Upon achieving higher ground, she turns and kicks Quartre across the face. She tries doing so again, but the killer grabs her foot and yanks her off the car. Lacey falls back to the concrete, landing painfully on her shoulder, and then rolls out of the way just in the nick of time as Quartre stabs down at her. Quartre: {mocking} You should've called the police, little girl. You both should've. This heroic act you're putting on is quite pathetic. It's only going to get you killed. Quartre haughtily picks Lacey up by the scruff of her coat, punches her in the stomach and then jabs her in the face with the blunt knife handle. He sends Lacey sprawling back onto the concrete, hurt and winded. Slan steps over her and smiles down. Quartre: Now just close your eyes. This won't hurt one bit! The serial killer raises the knife to finish the confrontation, and is shocked as all hell as Lacey raises his gun (which she had just fallen on) and shoots him through the stomach, just below the rib cage. Quartre takes a half-shuffle step backwards and falls to his knees, his right hand clutching Damsel's knife in a death grip. Quartre: {weakly} You.... bitch.... Lacey picks herself up and points the gun in Quartre's face, aiming right between the eyes. Chloe Mason stands at the end of the bridge, awe struck. Lacey: It's over, you bastard! For Damsel.... for Jonathan.... and for all the people you've ever tortured, hurt or killed.... it all ends right here, right now! Quartre: {sick grin} Do it, Lacey! Do it! Have you ever killed someone before? Have you ever experienced the rush.... the thrill.... of snuffing out another life? It is exquisite! It'll change you forever! Lacey pauses and hesitates. She has never taken a life before. She wants to, but.... Quartre: You're nothing but a weak little girl! You don't have what it takes to take control of this situation.... And quick as a cat, Quartre lunges forward to plunge the knife into Lacey's chest. Acting on pure instinct and adrenaline, Lacey squeezes the trigger. The silenced bullet strikes home, killing Quartre instantly. His body slumps forward onto the concrete. Lacey stares down in shock after what just happened. Chloe has approached the scene of combat and stands not more than fifteen fate away, mouth agape. For once, she has absolutely nothing to say. Lacey suddenly notices another presence, and she turns and looks directly at a terrified Chloe. Chloe raises her arms in a gesture of surrender and backs up a few paces, hoping to God that Lacey doesn't shoot her as well. But Lacey seems to look through and past her, over to the street where a news van comes pulling up to the bridge. She quickly assesses the situation in her mind.... and without another word after a final gaze straight into Chloe's eyes, she places a hand on the rail and vaults over the bridge. Lacey plummets to the icy waters below and breaks through them with a nearly perfect dive. Up near the bridge, the news van screeches to a halt. Kate and the cameraman spill out, grab their gear, and trot up onto the bridge, looking at Chloe up ahead who remains rooted to the spot. As they approach Chloe, they see what she sees. Steven Quartre's body is partly obscured by the Humvee. They don't have a good look at it from their current position. Kate: Chloe, what happened here? Chloe says nothing, just stares at the body. Kate: Chloe, who did this? Did you see? The cameraman has moved toward the body. Now he sees. Cameraman: My Lord! He turns to Kate. Cameraman: This d.b. ain't got no face! Kate is stunned. She moves to the body, then quickly turns away. For a moment her professional persona deserts her. Then, she gets a grip. Kate: Chloe, you saw this, didn't you? Talk to me. There's a thousand dollars in it for you if you just tell me what you saw. Chloe struggles with something; but makes a decision. Chloe: I didn't see anything. I got here too late.... like you. Chloe turns and starts to walk away. Kate: Hey, Chloe. Wait. That isn't what you said on the phone. Who were the other two women? Chloe, I'll put you on TV right now. Come back! Chloe! But Chloe breaks into a run and never looks back. Cameraman: We better call the cops. Kate: First, shoot the picture. Cameraman: Why? They'll never show it on the tube. Kate: Just shoot the picture, Hal! Cameraman: All right. The cameraman sets up and begins taping. Kate looks around her, as if she's stepped through the looking glass for a moment, then composes herself, and steps in front of the camera. Kate: {into a microphone} This is Kate Kendall with cameraman Hal Butler. We are the first people on the scene of an incredible, grisly murder here on Soldier Bridge. No witnesses saw what took place here, but it was, without a doubt an extraordinary event. Twenty minutes later.... on the shore by the bridge. Lacey drags Damsel from the river. Damsel, in shock, lets out a low moan of pain. Lacey pulls away Damsel's clothing so she can get a good look at where her friend was shot. Lacey: This might come as a surprise, but if we get you to a hospital right now.... You stand a good chance of living. Damsel manages a feeble, yet pained, smile. Damsel: I should've known, better than anyone else, that Steve wouldn't play fair. Is he....? Lacey: He's dead. Damsel: {coughs} You get all the fun. Lacey: Christine, listen to me.... I have to leave the city. I can't stick around here, not after what I just did. When you get better, check in on Jonathan for me, okay? Damsel: I understand. I've been running for a long while, too. Lacey remains silent, kneeling at the river's edge next to her wounded friend. A short distance away, Chloe watches from behind some bushes growing near the water. She can just make out what they're saying. Damsel: What about that girl that's been following you? Lacey: I know. Damsel: She could tell the cops what happened up there, if she hasn't already. Chloe frowns, not liking where this is headed. Lacey: She didn't. She won't. I think we came to an understanding of sorts up there. Chloe smiles in her hiding place. She knew there was something special about Lacey. One week later.... The morning sunshine filters into the second floor loft above the antique store. Jonathan sits staring blankly into space. He hasn't been sleeping well ever since Lacey and Damsel disappeared a week ago. He's heard nothing on the news, nor has he received a call from either of them. Then, from below, the buzzer sounds, indicating someone is at the back door. Jonathan: Lacey....? A look of hope spreads across his face as he hurries down the stairs. Jonathan moves to the back studio room to answer the doorbell. He cannot see who's on the other side of the door until he swings it open. Damsel, looking refreshed and somehow more beautiful than she ever was before, stands in the doorway. Jonathan: Christine! Damsel: {smiles} Hi, Jonathan. Can I come in? Jonathan: Sure, but.... Are you alone? Where's Lacey? Christine steps inside, allowing Jonathan to close the door behind her. Damsel: She's alive, Jonathan. And Steven Quartre was.... Quartre won't be calling you anymore. Jonathan: You mean he's dead? Damsel nods affirmatively. Jonathan: Then where is Lacey? Why isn't she here? Where did she go? Damsel: She didn't tell me. {grins} But I can guess. Jonathan: Where? Damsel: Are you sure you want to know? Associating with her could get you in plenty of trouble if this thing with Quartre works out badly in the legal system. Jonathan's look says it all. Three days later.... Lacey sits on the pier out behind her home in Stockton, California. She lets her feet dangle in the water as the sun shines brightly overhead. If any single spot on earth were to be called her favorite, this would definitely be it. So many fond memories here.... All having to do, in one way or another, with her missing husband, Doug. She loses herself in those memories.... that warmth.... trying to forget all about the events that occurred ten days ago. But they just won't go. She killed a man, and she's having a difficult time dealing with it. Jonathan: {from behind} I tried to phone ahead, but.... Lacey turns and sees Jonathan. In the background, Damsel stands at the beginning of the pier. Jonathan approaches Lacey and envelopes her in his arms with a big hug. Jonathan: Why did you run off like that? NEVER do that to me again, okay? Jonathan releases his hold on her and Lacey stands up, grabbing her sandals. Together they start walking back down the pier toward Damsel. Lacey: I'm a criminal now, Jonathan. A fugitive. I killed a man in cold blood. Damsel: We don't know that for certain, Lacey. The police haven't said or done anything, nor has anyone dropped by to talk with Jonathan. Lacey looks up at Damsel, who has decided to walk forward and meet them halfway out on the pier. Damsel: Unless you've been harassed this past week, I think you'll be okay. Damsel suddenly looks older than she is.... as if this event, and those leading up to it, have aged her far beyond her years. But there is strength there.... and determination. She's a lot stronger now than the girl Lacey first stepped in the ring with several years ago. Damsel: So, you might as well start enjoying life and stop moping around. Take this incident as a lesson learned and move on. You'll only drive yourself insane if you keep thinking about it. Lacey smiles at Damsel and nods imperceptibly. Damsel smiles back. Damsel: I've got to go. I need to get my own life back on track. Lacey: You're welcome to stay. Damsel approaches Jonathan, who hugs her. Damsel: Goodbye, Jonathan. Jonathan: {saddened} Will I....? Damsel: I hope so.... {turns to Lacey} Lacey.... Lacey: Christine.... With a simple nod, Damsel leaves. Jonathan: You didn't say goodbye. Lacey: {smiles} We never do. Linking arms, they walk back to Lacey's house together, the sun in the horizon beginning to set, casting purple's, red's and oranges down onto the water and ground below....
