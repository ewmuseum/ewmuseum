Going Home

22 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Debbie Carmichael returns to her family home in Phoenix, plays a prank on her siblings and talks about her CCW tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Deborah Carmichael Entitled: Going home Written in Wednesday, May 22, 2002, 11:08:51 Location: Phoenix, AZ This is a: Match RP **The scene opens in Phoenix, AZ. It's a pretty morning on the outside, and the camera shows the entrance to Debbie's house. For those of you not familiar, Debbie is married to a man in Phoenix and lives with 4 more kids. The scene shows the outside window, where you can see the inside. A small comotion can be heard, as the two middle brothers, John and Dave, fight over the remote control of the big screen TV that lays across them. John is a 12-year old kid who just discovered heavy metal: Dark clothes, black Metallica t-shirt, dark shades. Dave, on the other hand, is your regular 10-year old pre-teen punk, someone who just wants everything for themselves. The scene shows them fighting for the remote, and we can hear them scream:** John: GIVE ME THE FREAKING REMOTE! Dave: NO, I wanna watch Justice League... John: Justice League is gay and you can watch it every time in your room. Dave: My TV is broken... John: Because you broke it. Dave: No, YOU broke it by throwing it on the floor. John: No I didn't... Dave: Yes, you did. John: No I didn't... Dave: Yes, you did. John: No I didn't... Dave: Yes, you did. **Dave yanks the control hard and John lets it go, making Dave fly back and crash on the floor, back first. He starts to cry, prompting their older sister, Mary Jane, to come back down. The 17-year old dark lady comes down, with an extremely pissed off look on her face. She looks at them and says:** Mary: What the hell is going on down here? Can't I at least have some quiet time in this house? Dave: John beat me up... John: You deserved it. Mary: Yeah, stop being a whimp, Dave... Dave: What? Screw you, and screw you, go to hell... I wish Debbie was here, she'd put you guys into bed. **Then, from all of a sudden, a very known voice comes from behind him, making everybody get surprised:** Voice: But I AM here. **The camera looks up and shows Debbie Carmichael, with a suitcase on her hand. Dave rushes to hug her, and Mary and John slowly try to leave, but Debbie calls them:** Debbie: Hey, you two. Don't leave. We have some business to discuss. **Both of them put their heads down and walk to the couch. Deb walks towards a chair across it, with Dave right behind her. She sits down and, hugging Dave by the waist and pulls a small notepad, which she uses to start writing something, casually. She says:** Debbie: All right? WHo wants to pick the penalty first? Mary: I don't know... How about wrestling Guardian? That'd be a big punishment. John: Yeah... By the way, how's CCW? Are things ok over there? Debbie: Sure they are. I mean, there's nothing new, I'm on COTR and have my first real challenge against Lightning this weekend. John: Nice... I'm sorry about Mark Estep, though. Debbie: Ah, that's ok. THe man was too strong for me. I'm on that thing as a filler anyway. Mary: BLah, come on, Deb. You can win that thing. Debbie: You think? John: Sure. No wrestler there is as good as you are. Debbie: Geez, thanks. Mary: Cool. Anyway, the chat was ok, now we need to go... shopping, yeah... grocery shopping. John: Yeah, I'll go help her. Debbie: Sure, why not? Go ahead. John: Can we go to the mall after it? Debbie: Sure... Just be back by 10... Mary: We'll be. Don't worry. Debbie: All right. **Debbie gets up and walks both John and Mary to the door. She gives a gentle friendly slap on their backs, saying:** Debbie: Don't worry about the time, just have some fun... John & Mary: We will!! **THe kids walk out of the house and Dave comes with a sobbing face, disappointed. He says:** Dave: Why did you let them get away with it? When I do something stupid, you always punish me. **Debbie winks at him and says:** Debbie: Do you think I'd let those morons get away with it so easily? Nah, just look! **Debbie points at the kids, and the camera zooms on their backs, where you can see two similar papers stuck on their backs, saying: 'I'm a dumbass, kick me and call me a dumbass!'. Dave laughs and Debbie laughs with him. He says:** Dave: I love you, mom... **Debbie stares at him surprised, after realizing Dave called her mom for the first time. She hugs him, lifting him up in the air, and the scene fades to black** View Biography
