Guardian - The Traveller

16 April 2002

What this is A Match RP in which Angel and Guardian stand outside a Boston church and warn The Valiant One before his Thursday match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: The Traveller Written in Tuesday, Apr 16, 2002, 16:36:48 Location: King Street, Boston, Mass This is a: Match RP King Street, Boston Mass. It is early morning, the sun just creeping above the horizon. The streets are virtually empty. A stray dog sniffs at a lamppost, thinks twice about it and wanders off to find his first few scraps of the day. At the corner of the street is a church. A large keystone above the doorway dates the building at 1890. Standing on the steps leading up to the door we see Guardian and his beautiful assistant Angel. Angel: “So here we are in Boston. Home of Albert DeSalvo. The infamous Boston Strangler. A place where on March 5 1770 five men were shot to death by British soldiers in what legend calls the Boston Massacre. A place where evil permeates from the very walls and streets. A place with a black history.” **Guardian bows his head as he listens to Angel speak** Angel: “But history teaches us that those who sin here are punished. Albert DeSalvo is now paying for his crimes, as are those soldiers who callously open fired and murdered the innocent bystanders. You see……………….everyone who sins…………everyone who defiles HIS name………….everyone who lives a lie will be punished……….the only question is when. The how is taken are of. **At this Guardian raises his head, red eyes almost glowing in the early morning light** Angel: “WE are the how………………………we are the ones who offer redemption. We did not come to the CCW to preach…………..the people here are beyond mere words……………..those who sin, do so time and time again………………they have little or no remorse for their actions……………far to often delighting in the suffering of others…………………but we are not alone here…………there are others………………………….others who work for the betterment of the soul………………..but there are many many others who wish to harm……….wish to maim………...wish to sin. But…………..these people should be careful of their wishes…………..for HE has the power to turn those wishes against them. Right now there is a man travelling to this city…………a brave man…………a courageous man………a heroic man…………….a valiant man……………. He makes his way here with his brothers………….men who rally around him……………….he acts like some sort of false idol to these people….……………but this valiant man has no idea of his destiny. He is a man travelling here with the single-minded purpose of committing sin……………he is an angry man……he is a frustrated man…………But……………..Guardian **Angel turns around and look up at Guardian** Angel: “ But………………..this creation was subjected to frustration, not by its own choice, but by the will of the one who subjected it, in hope that the creation itself will be liberated from its bondage to decay and brought into the glorious freedom of HIS children…………………this valiant one will soon learn that anger and frustration will soon become his downfall………………..for he travels here and will learn like the others that have sinned in this glourious city……………he will learn that sin is not tolerated he will learn that HE is slow to anger, abounding in love and forgiving sin and rebellion. Yet he does not leave the guilty unpunished; he punishes not only the guilty but his children” **Guardian raises his head up to the sky** Angel: “This Thursday…………..The Valiant One will see first hand HIS power…………and the other Strangers? If they should show up to help their false idol………….then as his children they too shall suffer the same punishment. May HE have mercy upon your souls…………because we will not. View Biography
