Pain

14 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A fever-dream monologue and hospital scene in which the injured Christine Oakes hallucinates her boyfriend urging her to prepare for a Champions of the Ring match against Cianan.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: Pain........ Written in Tuesday, May 14, 2002, 22:54:00 Location: Somewhere........ This is a: Match RP Pain........ My whole world is pain........ I don't know where I am, or how I came to be here, but that's all I know........ Everything is pain........ The pain........ Is it physical? Is it mental? Is it emotional? Is it a combination of all the above? It feels like I'm swimming in a sea of blackness........ A sea that threatens to wash over me and consume me with........ With what? It's a very familiar feeling........ I remember it so well........ Could it be........ Guilt? Yes, I think that's exactly what it is........ Guilt. Pain and guilt........ Nearly inseparable......... Why would anyone want to live like this? Why am I still alive? What am I trying to tell myself that I don't want to remember? I obviously don't belong here........ Not physically anyway........ And yet this sea of blackness is like an old pair of comfortable slippers........ I feel like I've spent so much time here........ But these slippers are filled with maggots........ And I need to get out of here as soon as I can. But which way is out? Do I follow the pain, or run away from it? Do I allow the guilt to wash over me, or keep it at bay? And what if I follow those options, and I still don't find my way out? Am I going to be trapped in here forever? And just what is this place? Heaven? Hell? Purgatory? The end of everything........? Or the beginning? And then there's that flash of pain again! It seems to be coming from........ My head? Why? Regina Winters........ Why does that name sound so familiar........ Regina Winters bashes my head into a window........ I remember that! I can see that clearly apart from the blackness! She bashed my head into a window and she........ She what? My chest hurts too........ I can feel it........ It hurts to breath........ It hurts to move........ Regina Winters hurts........ That's what she does........ That's what she lives for........ And she hurt........ Me. Am I in this place because of her? Did she do something to me? I obviously don't belong here, so she must have........ The garage door. She dropped it on me........ Crushing my chest........ My ribs are on fire........ It hurts to breath........ It hurts........ To........ Breath!!! *The sound of beeping instruments are faintly heard as Christine Oakes opens her bleary eyes and looks up at the white sterile ceiling of the hospital room she currently inhabits. She tries to focus, doesn't have much success, and tries again.* Voice- Good. You're awake. We don't have much time. *Christine tries to turn her head to look at the owner of the voice, and her neck screams in agony. She winces and closes her eyes.* Christine- Mike? Is that you? Mike- Yeah, it's me babe. I'm here to get you out of this hospital. *Christine blinks her eyes open, trying desperately to focus.......* Christine- Get me out of the........ Mike- We don't have time for this. I need to get you out of here and get you ready for your match. Christine- Match? Mike- The COTR tournament is about to start. You have a match against Cianan. Now, you need to get up, and you need to get ready! *One of the hospital nurses walks by the door and stops in surprise. She watches as the formerly comatose patient starts to physically push herself up into a sitting position and swing her legs out of the bed.* Christine- Okay........ Okay........ I'm getting up........ Mike- You need to hurry. You can't blow this one, Christine. You can't let this one end like it did at Group Therapy. You can't let this one pass you by like the Pacific Title rumble. You need to get up, and you need to fight. *The nurse enters the room and quickly approaches the bedside.* Christine- I won't let you down, Mike........ I........ *The nurse touches Christine on the shoulder and she jumps a bit. She looks in shock at the nurse who she wasn't expecting to be there.* Nurse- Where do you think you're going, Miss? We have you listed under critical condition! Christine- *very weak* I'm going to train! I'm going with........ *And Christine turns to look at Mike, but her voice freezes in her throat. Mike's not there.* Nurse- Miss? Christine- Where did........ Where did he go? Nurse- What are you talking about? Christine- Mike........ My boyfriend........ He was just here........ Nurse- There's nobody here, Miss. Nobody has been here. Christine- But........ Nurse- You've been rambling on to yourself. Now, lay back in bed and I'll get your doctor. I'm sure he'll be very pleased to know that you're awake. *Without another word, Christine slowly sinks back into her hospital bed. The nurse leaves to get the doctor. And inexplicably, her thoughts are drawn to CCW. The COTR tournament. She's going to be facing Cianan. And she has a lot of training to do........* View Biography
