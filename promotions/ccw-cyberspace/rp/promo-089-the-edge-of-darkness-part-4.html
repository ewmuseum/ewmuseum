The Edge of Darkness; Part 4 Devyl CCW Promo #89 Date: 03/16/03 Location: Preacher's Corners David: I don't believe this... That disgruntled comment from Devyl's husband was all it took to prove that the answers she was looking for were nowhere even close to being answered. Devyl, David and Shadow are at the house in Preacher's Corner's, still looking into the origins and whereabouts of the creature that calls itself 'Gabriel'. David is sitting on the floor of the livingroom near the now-scattered pentagram, his laptop open in front of him. Shadow and Devyl react to his words, turning away from their scrutiny of the walls. Devyl: What did you find? David: Nothing helpful, obviously. This 'language' that you found written above the door... These symbols... Mean nothing. They're meaningless and random strings of symbols . Devyl: The symbols themselves mean nothing? David: No, the symbols and runes are real... They seem to be a conglomeration of many different languages from the prehistoric tribes that once walked the earth. None of the databases that I have access to, and believe me when I tell you that I can access nearly all of them, show any of these characters belonging together. It looks like we have indeed stumbled upon a new language. Devyl: And these symbols do not appear to be a hybrid of any known language? David: No. Devyl, sighing uncharacteristically in frustration, turns back to the walls. Not being able to decipher the message left behind was bad. Very bad. It was the one thing she was hoping to work off of. The first step toward enlightenment. And now... Now it was back to square one. Shadow: Devyl... The light phenomenon that you described... It doesn't seem possible. Devyl: What do you mean? Shadow: Well... You said that it was as if a bright searchlight had been shone through the cracks in the walls, filling the room with bright white light, correct? Devyl nods. Shadow: I can tell, just by opening myself up briefly, that these walls were never touched by Gabriel's spiritual energy. In fact, there's no ectoplasmic residue whatsoever. It almost sounds like the whole thing with the lights was in your mind. Devyl doesn't reply, conflicting emotions and feelings roiling about inside her... Anger, apprehension, fear, sadness and terror. She doesn't know whether to thank Shadow for that insightful observation or rip her heart out for making Devyl sound like she belongs in a psychiatric facility. Luckily, she doesn't have to reply as David interjects himself back into the conversation. David: More bad news... Looks like we've got company of sorts. Devyl looks over at David, her brow furrowing. David is standing, his lap top under his arm, looking out the window facing the forest. Shadow: It's pitch black out there. How do you know? David: The noises. I think we're about to have visitors of the not-quite-alive variety. And as soon as David finishes, the window shatters inward. David flinches back, nearly dropping his computer. A decay-ridden, worm-infested arm reaches in through the window. Small shards of glass stick out of the arm as it mindlessly reaches and gropes for anything living. Devyl: **incredulous** The living dead!? The rotted, decrepit face of the thing peers into the living room, it's mucus-white eyes slowly dribbling down onto it's cheeks. And then the scratches begin at the door as another one apparently tries to get through and inside. Shadow: We have zombies attacking us? How lame is this? Devyl: Very. **to Shadow and David** Out of the way children. It's time for mommy to play... Fade out... View Devyl 's Biography Back
