Me and Dad {Part 2} Dan Logan CCW Promo #122 Date: 06.03.03 Location: Moss Side, St. Marys Hospital Hospital, St. Marys, Moss Side Moss Side 06.03.03 'Huh!' Ok, so not much to say when you first wake up after seven days of rest but I was groggy and it was as I say my very first words in seven days. Remembering back, I stroke my head across the large bump that has formed. I had hit my head quite hard when I fell yet strangely it was more my rib cage and lungs that seemed to hurt 'Urgh...ah!' Mary is sat beside me her own tears streaming down her face almost drowning us in the salty tear waters of her eyes. She has stopped crying now and looks up at me looking into the bed in which I lie. Drying her eyes with the arm of her woolen jumper and gently sniffing to prevent from the harsh sobbing. A weak smile surrounds her face and through the pain I attempt to comfort her 'Mar....ah!' She extends her arm and takes hold of my own, though not frail I only just able to curl my fingers around her before my ribs begin once more to ache 'AH!' I shoot up the pain of my moving shooting up through my body. Mary looks towards me and I sigh inwardly. Nodding gently I fall backward and looking into her eyes I know deep down that she is about to confirm my deepest fears and tell me that I was right and that my father has finally finished something that I had only hopes that he hadn't done for much longer and that my father is dead. 'I've something that I need to talk to you about' I don't want to listen but moving my body is almost impossible and the bright white of the room about me is sending me into a strange unneeded sleep each time I look upon it. Not wishing to sleep and knowing that eventually I am to hear it I resentfully wait. 'He - Your father that is - well - well he passed away early two nights ago. You were still under when he left us. He - he needed you know - to tell you that he loved you' How the hell would you know, I had wished with all my might to shout out but only a half hearted groan of pain would leave my body. How can you, someone who has no children of your own - someone with nothing that can help me ever, tell me that he loved me? It made no sense and the emotions sending themselves around my body at the time were something that left little to like. What I couldn't see - and to be honest sometimes I still can't see is how that stupid caring cow ever could consider that they knew what my father felt. He had passed away due to the fact that he couldn't cope alone with me any longer and now I was lying there being told by some comparative stranger that my own self topping father loved me. 'Urgh' I forced myself to turn my body so that I no longer had to look upon her, dragging my hand from hers and resting upon it once more the tears came back. You can't hold them from falling down your face once the pain takes over like this. Your body holds it in for so long but then seems almost to force it out whether you like it or not at this sort of time. You see there is nothing else left for you to do...you've done everything else...so you cry. 'Don't cry...please don't cry' Why the hell should she care? I am the only person that really cares about him and I always will be the only person. Now though she seems to think she has some god given right to tell me what to do! Who is this stupid bitch! Who the hell does she think she is...my mother? You see no matter what she wishes for, no matter what she says. This bitch had no right to tell me what the hell to do and I proved it. I lay upon my back and cried... *** Back to Reality *** The daylight has only just broke and the sun is still burning the sky with its bright-fired flames of impassioned love for its country and its world. The birds sing within the bright greens of my hide away and the due on the morning grass still lays to ground having not yet been absorbed by the first licks of the suns warm haven. The wooden cabin within which I am staying is quite warm the Amazonian rainforest isn't as bad as people make out and the warm glare of the sun rising makes this place one of the most beautiful that I have been to date. I had rose from my bed only fives minutes ago after having a quick shower I returned to the window to look out for a while until I needed to see Mike once more about her... Still her ebony hair burns within my dreams. Still her emerald green eyes create about her an air of almost total unobtainability. I never loved her, yet she is the mother of my son and this has more warrant over my life than any other woman does that I have met. Finally I no longer wish to look upon such a beautiful day, I haven't long to remain here within the Amazon and there is much that I must do. I leave my wooden hutch of welcoming fragrance and timid cool rooms and walk out unto the soft ground of the opening in the woods. Two little boys run past me their black faces shining in the fresh sunlight of the daytime. I walk on my sandals sounding heavy upon the ground, coming up toward my friends home I knock upon the door and laugh at myself. 'KNOCK, KNOCK' Soon I will be sat down resting again knowing exactly where she is and what exactly she is doing... 'Come in my friend' His servants serve him well and upon opening that illusive door I see that he has been waiting for my arrival for longer than I must have been awake. The room isn't like mine. Though its obvious size advantage his home seems brighter some how, almost the epitome of all that is was and ever shall be color. He has grown old; just like Trevor now Keith is an old man nearing almost 48 and looking a lot worse for wear because of it. His waistline has grown and he has lost the trademark goatee that had kept him in the shit so many times while he was in the forces. Now he supported himself not with the easy to use legs that he had been born with but with a large wooden throne that seems to have been built to fit him exactly as he was those long years ago when he was an actual person. Now though he was different, the look in his eyes had changed from the anger that it had once carried to a more chilled out look of sheer annoyance and boredom. 'You've changed...' 'As have you' 'Not like you though, you have become fat and lazy...' He laughs a bold laugh yet one of admittance, he has left himself to go old and fat because now he commands his many people and they love him 'I am here...' 'I know why you are here and I shall tell you where she is only on one condition' 'And that condition would be?' 'That you take Kimeo with you' 'Why would I wish to do such a thing?' 'Because you hunt her and I know where she lives...if you take him with you he will serve you or fight beside you. He is well equipped for both...' 'How can you assure me that you know where she is? How can you promise me that it is where she shall stay?' 'I can promise that she was there as I saw her there myself...as for how long she stays I cannot guarantee and nor would I try. She is a woman and they carry their own mind' 'Yet you can tell me of where she is now?' 'I can' 'Then where is she?' 'Will you take him?' 'No!' 'Then I shall not tell you of her whereabouts' 'What justice will be done upon him if he is taken into my world? He would have to fight and I do not think him ready yet for a fight...' 'You have not seen him yet! He fights like you did to begin with' 'Then he shall fail...' 'Yet you have not...Show him the ways in which to learn and then YOU shall have the knowledge as to where she lays her head now' ' I leave soon...allow me time to think' 'She shall not remain there forever my friend...consider it wisely' ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
