Fighting the plague

10 March 2003

Fighting the plague Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #66 Date: 10 Mar 2003 Location: Atlanta It is dark here. Here in the hours between midnight at dawn. Down here on the streets were the moonlight fails to reach. Where the desperate and the lonely live. Where the alleyways criss-cross path known only by a few. Where the nobodies of the world live unnoticed by the outside world. A whole sub-culture of people forgotten by the light. Where the ground is permanently damp and the side of buildings stretch high into the night time air. This is gritty. This is reality, THIS is hardcore. Steam rises from an underground vent filling the alleyway briefly. Out on the main street a car speeds by, its headlights momentarily shedding light down this otherwise forgotten path; as it does so, it silhouettes a figure walking from the street into the steam. Suddenly the alleyways resident homeless burst into life. Frantic scrambling and whispering can be heard. The sounds of people fleeing. Survival instincts ingrained into their sub-conscious after years of living hand to mouth on the streets. Through the steam walks a man. Long black trench-coat open and billowing gently behind as he steps further into the alleyway. He pauses, looks around at the deprivation surrounding him, at the heels of the people fleeing at the far end of the alley; he knows this place and he is familiar with its stench and its feel. He grins a grin that only the truly wicked can. He takes a few steps further head glancing from side to side at the empty cardboard homes only seconds earlier fully occupied. He chuckles for he knows the reason they left. It was him. They know him down here. And those that don’t know him know of him. The wise ones stay as far away as possible. Survival instincts. They are used to the type of people who take pleasure in taunting them, spitting on them from afar. They are even used to the odd kid who thinks it’s funny to maybe beat up a bum in front of his mocking pals. All that they can deal with; that’s just life on the streets. But Jack…Jack is a different story. Everyone down here knows the stories. A story that has quickly spread from hobo to hobo; a warning. Stay away from the smart dressed man in the long coat after dark. Jack looks around as he walks slowly down the alley; ahead of him he spots what’s he’s looking for. A bum, asleep or passed in his cardboard palace. Left behind when he comrades fled. For down here there is no honour among thieves. Slowly, almost methodically Jack walks up to the tramp. He pauses, looking down in disgust at the filthy sight in front of him. From out of nowhere Jack brings his foot back and connects with a deep kick into the homeless man’s midsection. There is a sickening grunt as the foot makes contact and the man doubles up into a foetal position. Jack squats down on his haunches and comes face to face with a set of fearful eyes. “You disgust me do you know that” The man grunts “Look at you. No wonder society is crumbling around us. The likes of you bring every city in the world to its knees with your plague. You have no reason to be here” Jack reaches into his coat pocket. The homeless man catches this, and his face shows panic. He starts to squirm in an effort to escape despite the shooting pain in his stomach. With a swift movement Jack’s other hand swings out and connects squarely with the bridge of the man’s nose. The light gleams of the dagger’s blade as it is withdrawn from Jack’s coat. With his free hand Jack grabs a handful of the bum’s greasy, matted hair and yanks it backwards; in the same movement he brings the dagger round and rests it on the mans filthy neck. “You are scum my friend. A blotch on the very fabric of all that is good in the world. And you deserve to die. Would you like me put you out of your pathetic existence?” The homeless man looks at Jack petrified. “I can do it you know. I can end both our sufferings right here, right now. With just one swift move I can end it all for us” There is a flash of light as the blade is swung around and the homeless man’s head drops to the floor. Jack pull back his hand to reveal a thick bunch of hair, which he lets drop onto the floor. The bum grunts and groans as he raises his hands to his throat and realises that he is not dead. Jack reaches back into his coat and puts the dagger back. “No my filthy rotting friend. Not now. Why should I do you a favour and end your misery? No. I have much bigger fish to fry than you my greasy companion. Do you realise that this Thursday, right here in this pitiful excuse for a city I get to do my thing on a much bigger scale. Right there in the main event. I get to show the world what happens when a sane man is let loose with no barriers. There are no rules, no limits, and no depths to the places I can go. And no one…..no one can stop me. Not Carlos, not McTavish, not Enigma, not the mighty Gobber….and not my recent tag partner Blayze. I care nothing about the welfare of any of these men and that is what really makes a man dangerous. I’ve said it before and I’ll say it again, don’t fear the mad man that does what he does because he knows no different; fear the man that does what he does with the full knowledge of the consequences……yet still does it anyway. This Thursday, I turn it up a notch. No rules, no limits, no choice.” Jack stands up and walks back out of the alleyway onto the streets “Fear The Ripper” View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
