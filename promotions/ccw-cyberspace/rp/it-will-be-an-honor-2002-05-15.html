It Will Be an Honor...

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comic restaurant promo in which Mr. Gobstopper previews his Champions of the Ring round robin match against The Supreme Machine in Chicago.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mr. Gobstopper Entitled: It will be an honor... Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 03:04:45 Location: A sidewalk in Chicago This is a: Match RP *Today we find Mr. Gobstopper strolling down a sidewalk in the Windy City of Chicago. Wearing a thin tan sweater and a pair of jeans, he walks with an attitude. Passing by many of the local business dealers and residents, lots of fans notice him as he strolls to his destination. Where is he going? Our cameras have caught up to him.* Mr. Gobstopper: Oh, glad you could catch me here in the Windy City. I'm on my way to one of my favorite places in all the world. While I can find one just about in any town, it's a special event that brings me to Chicago. *He turns a corner as a postal worker passes by and gives him the thumbs up.* Mr. Gobstopper: As you, the fans of CCW, know, COTR Round Robin matches begin this week on Lock Down. It just so happens, I have a match this week on Lock Down, a Round Robin match, and it's going to be right here in Chicago! *He stops at a pedestrian crosswalk waiting for the light to turn green. A school group of second graders approach him with the extreme shyness as they can be heard giggling in the background. The light turns green and he continues walking.* Mr. Gobstopper: This city is very historic to me. I had my first CCW match here. I won the CCW Heavyweight Title from a guy who is from this town. And this Thursday, I plan on obtaining a victory in this Round Robin tournament for the COTR. *Gobstopper stops dead in his tracks and faces the camera.* Mr. Gobstopper: Do you know who I'm facing? *The camera waves side to side, as if to say 'no'. Gobstopper continues walking, but slowly.* Mr. Gobstopper: Well, it's The Supreme Machine. *Mr. Gobstopper stops again, this time he turns around and faces a building. He looks up and holds his hands out in display.* Mr. Gobstopper: Well, here it is! My favorite place to eat in all the world. *Gobstopper pulls open the door as he is met by a perky blonde female hostess.* Hostess: Hi sir! Welcome to The Outback Steakhouse, how are you doing today? Table for one? Right this way! *Gobstopper follows the hostess to his booth. He sits down and tells the hostess what he'd like to drink.* Hostess: Thank you sir, the waiter will bring your drinks in a few minutes. Enjoy your meal! *The hostess leaves as the cameraman has a seat across from Mr. Gobstopper.* Mr. Gobstopper: What'd I tell ya? Great services, great food, a little pricey but hey. Now, I'm here down under. The outback. How fitting eh? I'm facing the Supreme Machine this Thursday. Now, I remember what happened a week and a half back. That no good blunder from down under and his gay sidekick Ricky Thunder took me to the mat. They crippled me, they shed my blood, they hospitalized me. But you know what? It was only for a day. *The waiter quickly dashes to the table.* Waiter: Hello, my name is Steven and I'll be your waiter today. Do you need a minute to look over the menu? Mr. Gobstopper: Yes, Steven, that would be great. Waiter: Alright, I'll be back in a few! *Steven leaves.* Mr. Gobstopper: So now here I am. Here in Chicago. The place I had my first CCW match. The place where Lock Down eminates from this week. And... the place where I, Mr. Gobstopper, will ultimately defeat The Supreme Machine and claim my throne and become known to all in CCW, to all in PTC... I will become known as The Supreme Killing Machine! And when I get angry, you'll get DROPPED! *Mr. Gobstopper settles down while a curious crowd begins building around his booth section. He looks around as everyone cautiously returns to their seats. The waiter then comes up and asks Gobstopper if he's ok. 'I'll be fine.' he replied. Fade Out.* View Biography
