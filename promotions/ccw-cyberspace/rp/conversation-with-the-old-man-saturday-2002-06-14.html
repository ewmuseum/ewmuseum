Conversation With The Old Man (Saturday)

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Regina Winters confronts her father in his upstate New York mansion about control of the family company.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Conversation with the Old Man (Saturday) Written in Friday, Jun 14, 2002, 15:42:23 Location: New York This is a: Match RP **Regina and her father stare at each other across the expanse of his wooden desk in his office in their mansion in upstate New York. Neither one speaks a word as the silence stretches out in front of them, seeming to roll on into eternity. But that's not really the case. This confrontation between father and daughter was a long time coming, and Regina had prepared herself fully for whatever offense her father would direct at her.** Winters: Don't you have someplace else to be, Regina? A wrestling match or somesuch? Regina: Not at this moment, no. Why do you ask? Is it because you are afraid of me, daddy? Afraid that the board of directors might finally oust you out of your corporate chair because of the massive blunder that you made? Winters: Is that why you wanted to see me today? To make your veiled threats? I thought we've been over this before.... Regina: Yes, but that was before you SCREWED UP! You are old, daddy! Your vision for the future and the growth of this company are old. The recent computer disaster proved that! Winters: That 'recent computer disaster' as you put it was an inside job. A deliberate attempt to knock out our systems. And do you know who I believe is responsible for that, Regina? Surely you do. I suspect YOU. Regina: Of course you would. Why? Because you would do the same damn thing! You're a schemer, which is actually what once made you good at your job. But no longer. Computers are not your area of expertise. No, you take the old fashioned route of political backstabbing and manipulating the social circles.... All off that is virtually useless when your knowledge of computers is absent. We're in the technological age now.... Your cave-man tactics are irrelevent. Winters: **long pause** You really do want this job, don't you? Regina: **grins** What do you think? Winters: I think I have another proposition for you. You have a match coming up against two men. A submission match I believe. If you can beat this Red Ninja and Kensuke Suzahara.... Regina: **interrupts** No! No more deals with you, daddy! We're doing this my way! I know you don't understand that yet, but you soon will. I suggest you leave here and start buttering up thos in influential seats of power. Because by the time I'm through with you, you're going to need those people to find you a new job! Winters: Regina.... You listen to me very closely because I am only going to say this once... If you even TRY to do anything to me, or you attempt to hurt this company again, daughter or no, you will be severely dealt with! Regina: Are you finished? Good. Now you listen to me old man.... The change is coming! It's winds are blowing as we speak. I'm sure your tired old bones can feel it! This change is going to be absolute and complete. Your empire, such as it is, will no longer exist. In its place will be a new empire, built off of the same foundation that once held it! And once that occurs, I will be queen! I will rule over the empire, and if you are still around to see it, pray that you at least have a place at my side as the Court Jester because the alternatives are far worse! **Regina stands up from the table and looks down at her father with disgust.** Regina: I can spend no more time here talking with you about this... The very sight of you disgusts and repels me! **And without another word, Regina leaves the office and walks away down the hall. She knows with full certainty that she will never see this palatial mansion ever again. And that mere thought, amongst all others, makes her smile....** View Biography
