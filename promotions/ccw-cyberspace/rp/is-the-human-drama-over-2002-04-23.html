Is The Human Drama Over?

23 April 2002

What this is A roleplay in which Bojangles the Klown reflects on human drama before his King of Extreme match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Bojangles The Klown Entitled: Is the Human Drama Over? Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 18:29:48 Location: The Circus. This is a: Match RP (Argh... i did it again! No title, no RP shows, so now I have to re-write.) The circus is quiet. There is nothing, no light, no sound. We travel to the inside, where the owner, of this run down, and shoddy place is sitting, a scowel on his face, as he glares at nothing, from his seat. Slumped shoulder, and bad posture, as he reovers from his latest defeat from Brian Gaines. Valinda, the snake charmer, smirks, as she rounds his chair, and plops into his lap. 'Did the poor little clown, have a little set back with his 'human drama'?' She giggled, and ran her finger along his painted cheek. He grabbed her hand, quickly enough to cause her to jolt. 'Shut up.' 'Oooh, Don't be like that.' She pouts. With that, Bojangles stands, jumping the lusty red head onto the floor, in a swift second. She blinks, and glares up at him. 'What are you doing?' She nearly shreiks. 'I am not going to.. clown around.' He smiles, as he placed his hands behind his back, and stepped over her, 'Who am I facing on Lockdown?' She picked herself up, indignantly, dusting herself off. 'You have the King of Extreme Tournament. Supreme Machine, and Yama Tatsu.' 'Interesting.' With out another word, Bojangles grabs onto a small rope, and pulls a shoddy door open, and steps through. Leaving the red haired beauty confused, and quite frustraited. Bojangles stepped through the muck, and the grime. The boards creeked under his foot steps, and the wind lightly blew through the cracks, and the holes of the rotted wood. 'What is my ammusemtent for Human drama? Why do I fancy, watching the pain and suffering of others. In honesty, I am just like every man and woman. People go to the races, to watch the cars crash. People watch boxing to see who gets defeated, and messed up. Humans are blood thirsty creatures, animals at heart. Ready to devour the loser, for their own glory. SuMa and Yama Tatsu, two people who stand in my way. We meet at the King of Extreme, a car wreck in-and-of itself. Something all three of us, are ready to compete in. But, will you Yama Tatsu, and you.. SuMa, be willing to risk your lives? To grab that glittery sliver of gold, that is just set before us?' Bojangles stares up, at the yellow moon. His face, once more an un-readable mask. His hand wringing behind his back, as he ponders. 'I will.' A smile crawls over the clown's lips. 'I am prepared to give everything, for that little bit of fame. This has nothing to do with the past, SuMa. You were very wrong. This has to do with Human Drama. This has to do with what the people will want to watch. This has to do our lives. Benny Blades knew human drama, unfortunate for him. His death entertained millions, his fall, kept billions on the edge of their seats.' He smiles, as he gracefully glides down some steps. 'I am a man with out fear.. just, laughter. Human Drama will be amusing, so let us let the gods decide who will live, and who will die. I'll be extreme, because my fate is not in my own hands, and I go when I do. So what is a chair to the head? What is a piledriver through a burning table, compared to the will of a devine being? Nothing. I will not fear. I laugh. Ku ku ku ku ku..' He stares once more up at the stars. fin. View Biography
