Aftermath

What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Angel, backstage with a wounded Guardian, warns Ricky Coyote and Spider.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: Aftermath Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 12:35:08 Location: Locker room This is a: Interview Backstage at SMN Angel is tending to Guardians gaping head wound, dried blood is encrusted over his face and chest. The Hardcore belt hangs over his shoulder…….it to covered in encrusted blood. A knock on the dressing room door Angel: “Come in” A camera crew enters. Crew: “Mind if we get a few words for TV?” Angel: “Sure....that thing on?” she says pointing to the camera. Crew:”Yep…it is now” Angel: “Good because I want to make sure you get this……………. Ricky Coyote! Just what did you think you were doing back there tonight? What gives you the right to interfere in HIS business? I thought we had already shown you last week? Just what do you not understand? I thought we had already dealt with your sins? Was your redemption not enough? Tonight……..through blood and sweat Guardian fought Yama Tatsu in our hardest test since we arrived here in the CCW and you………..you stick your face into our mission? Just who do you think you are? Do you have even the slightest idea of who’s business you are interfering in? I don’t think you do Ricky……….if you did……….even you would have thought twice before getting involved. We did not ask………we did not need your help tonight…………..HE had things completely under control………..Yama Tatsu was moments away from his crucifixion………….but you thought you would help out? Ricky……I`m afraid to say you have raised HIS anger…………..if you wanted to be noticed…..then so be it………HE see’s what you have done. The blood shed from Guardian here will be nothing compared to the loss you will suffer………….. I can testify to that…………….Ricky…..be careful………you never know what is around the corner………..and more importantly…..you never know WHO….…………and speaking of not knowing who………………..we find out tonight that Guardian and Danny Bryson are part of a team at Group Therapy………..but who are the other 3? We are not being told…………….Spider says its going to be a surprise…………….well Spider……I hope for the sake of the CCW that the surprise you speak of is not an injection of even more hate and sin…………and I hope for your sake Spider that you do nothing to jeopardise Guardians chance at Group Therapy……………….now if you will please excuse us……….we must leave here for HE wants to speak to us alone.” View Biography
