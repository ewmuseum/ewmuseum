Norman Nail Debuts With A Bang And Challenges Chang

2 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A challenge-class roleplay staging the signing and first press conference of new wrestler Norman Nail, who challenges Chang.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Norman Nail Entitled: Norman Nail Debut's with a BANG!!! (Makes a HUGE Challenge to Chang) Written in Sunday, Jun 2, 2002, 19:33:39 Location: CCW press conference This is a: Challenge The camera pans on and a door are seen standing still right in front of the camera view. The door has an imbedded sign on it that reads in gold plated letters, CCW President. The camera pans back and a CCW interviewer is seen on the left hand side of the camera with a microphone in his hand. He is wearing a long sleeve black CCW logo shirt. Interviewer: Ladies and Gentlemen John Harvey hear and I am pleased to inform you, that in that very door right there, there is a new, yes I said it right, a NEW, CCW superstar being signed as we speak. They call him Norman Nail and if rumor serves correct, he is one of the most upcoming stars the CCW has seen in years. By what is said in the behind the scenes talk here at CCW, Norman is a former state champion in amateur wrestling, and has done nothing but shined ever since he has joined professional wrestling. The man is only 24, but already is looked upon by many as “the next big thing” in professional wrestling. Obviously the signing of him is huge, and this just shows how well CCW’s recruiting staff works. All of the sudden the door of the room suddenly lashes out and opens. Three or four men in suits surround what looks to be Norman Nail, the newest superstar in CCW. Reporters stand on the sides of the men and try to peak in to get an interview or question answered. One of the men yells out, “Norman will not be answering any of your questions now, he will answer whatever questions you have at the press conference”, all of the reporters stopped dead in there tracks as the man spoke, but the second he stopped the pesky reporters decided to keep on nagging the newly found superstar. The camera goes back to the CCW interviewer who is having to yell over the noise. Interviewer: Well there you here it, Norman Nail will be holding his first press conference, and if my sources are correct, it will be held today at 4:00 in the official CCW Conference room!!! For now, I’m John Harvey, reporting for CCW exclusive!!! ~~~ The camera fades off slowly as Norman is seen leaving the building ~~~ The cameras fade back on and many eager reporters are just beginning to take there seats at the press conference. The room is full of people. A reserved area for reporters sits in the middle and back of the room. A podium that has two steps on both sides and a long table with chairs and microphones in the middle of the podium stand at the front of the room. TV cameras are placed at the back of the room, and other people stand along the sides. The room is very crowded and noisy. Finally a small bald man dressed in a suit walks up the podium and leans over to talk in the mic. Man: Testing…Testing 1, Testing 2, Testing, Testing 3……Ladies and Gentlemen… The people do not quite down. The man speaks louder. Man: Ladies and Gentlemen! The room quiets down Man: I would like to introduce the newest signed wrestler in the CCW, former California State High School Wrestling Champion, and Nation Champion at the 189/215 pound weight classes, Norman…Nail!!!!! The people clap as Norman Nail walks through one of the side doors, walks up the podium, and takes a seat in one of the middle chairs of the table. Norman is dressed in a sleeveless CCW T-shirt and black jeans. He has on trimmed dark yellow sunglasses. His Shaggy Blonde Hair hangs from his head and drips onto his eyes as he sits down ready to answer questions. A couple men walk with him and take seats on both sides of him. One of the men reaches out and takes his mic. Man: We will now answer questions…only ask a question if called upon please so that this conference will go much smoother... The reporters begin lifting their hands and eagerly press to be called upon. Norman points out one of the reporters. Norman Nail: Yes, you there. Reporter: Norman Nail, theirs been a lot of accusations stating that you are the next big thing in professional wrestling…You have the size and from what I have seen of your video tapes of previous matches, the ability to be a huge success…what do you say to these accusations? Norman Nail: I’ve only wrestled professionally for about a year. But I’ve always told myself that whatever I do, I’ll do to my best ability. And if my best ability in professional wrestling ranks high, then don’t be surprised if high is exactly where I am by the end of my career. Norman points out another reporter and chooses them. Reporter 2: Norman, CCW is one of if not they, biggest professional wrestling federations in the nation…do you think you have what it takes to compete with some of the best in the world? You said yourself that you’ve only been professionally wrestling for one year, and do you think that that could play a factor and that maybe your jumping into things to fast? Norman Nail: You know, when CCW first came to me and wanted me to sing with them, I took awhile to think about every possible thing that could happen. And one of those possibilities is, yes, am I jumping into things to fast. And that may be just what I am doing. But then I thought. I thought about how many thousands of people would kill to have a chance like I have. And the simple fact that I even thought about turning this offer down, well its just crazy. Norman points out another reporter and chooses her Reporter 3: Do you think that your previous experience in amateur wrestling will play a big role in your success here in CCW? Norman Nail: Well of course my knowledge of amateur wresting is going to help me reach new heights here in CCW. Amateur wrestling really did give me the tools to go far in life, and now that I have those tools, I don’t see anything, or anyone, who can hold me back. Norman points out another reporter to ask a question. Reporter 4: Norman, who do you think your first opponent will be here in CCW. Do you have anyone in mind that you would like to go up against? Norman Nail: You know, I’ve been looking around at the roster and have been watching some videos of previous CCW shows, and there obviously is some great talent here in CCW. But theirs one guy…theirs one guy that would help me prove to everyone in the CCW that Norman Nail isn’t here to fool around. He’s the biggest guy I’ve ever laid my eyes on. He’s a hardkore legend. Chang The Mongolian Giant. Some of the people’s eyes widen in disbelief. Norman Nail: So Chang, if you wanta feel inside and out what pain really means, then accept my challenge…because I don’t care if you’re the biggest and baddest guy on the planet, Norman Nail is still going to inflict more pain in your body than humanly possible. Like the quote says, The bigger you are, the harder you fall. And Chang, when I do drop you, when you do fall, I want you to remember what I said just now. When I speak, I’m not just uttering out words and syllables, I mean what I say, and what I’m saying is that Chang, you may be big, you may be strong, but it wont be long before my last name is pierced into your heart. The reporters and people all are shocked to here that Norman Nail has challenged the biggest man in CCW. After a few moments of silence, one of the men takes his mic and slowly says, um, that’s all the questions we have time for now… The room is still in silence as Norman steps up and leaves the room. Outside the room stands John Harvey. He is standing there with the same look many of the other people had when Nail announced his challenge to Chang. Norman approaches him but Harvey is to stunned to introduce himself. He simply cuts to the chase and ask Norman what everyone has on their mind. John Harvey: What in the hell are you thinking? I mean, with all do respect Mr. Nail, you’ve just signed on with CCW, what could you have been thinking challenging the biggest man in CCW? Norman Nail: I think that Chang is going to be my example opponent. It’s going to show CCW that Norman Nail is all about limits, and how I have none. Change may be big, but he’s beatable. And I’m gana prove just that if he accepts my challenge. But for now I’ve got to go, I’m flying back to Fresno for the weekend to visit my family. But don’t worry, Norman Nail is making an impact in CCW, a big, impact! Norman walks off and leaves the building. John Harvey is left speechless as the camera fades. View Biography
