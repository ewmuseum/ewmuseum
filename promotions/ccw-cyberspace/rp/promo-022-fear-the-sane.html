Fear The Sane Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #22 Date: Feb 18 2003 Location: Jacks House **A vast house sits atop a hill. Way past its main gates and up its immaculate gravel driveway. Secluded. Quite. Private. Away from the rest of the world. Through its huge oak doors, down its exquisitely decorated hallways you will find the library. Beyond those doors, sitting alone at a desk and reading by a solitary candlelight sits Jack. The only other light in the room comes from a television sitting to the right of Jack, the image is a paused still from last weeks Lockdown showing Jack hanging onto the head of Scorpion who was draped over the top rope. He looks up directly into camera.** “Ah….welcome to my home, please take a seat” Jack carefully places a bookmark into his book and closes it, laying it on the table in front of him. The cover reads “Tracking The Worlds Serial Killers – By Former F.B.I chief Morris Coyle” “Have you ever wondered why one person would choose to take the life of another…and another? Have you ever stopped to think why we as a society fail to understand the social implications of these actions in a more positive light? No. I didn’t think you would have. You see as a society we are all to quick to turn a blind eye to what is really going on right in front of us. Oh we are all quick enough to judge and have an opinion on matters we feel do not directly affect us. But the very moment we are personally effected…….we fall to pieces. We cannot offer an opinion even if we have one. For as a society today….we are weak. As an example, lets take a look at this week’s Lockdown shall we. The “powers the be” who run CCW decide to hold a Hardcore Invitational Match. That’s fine we say. As fans you think “WOW I love hardcore matches, all that blood, all that violence” yes you love it don’t you. Watching men tear each other apart in the pursuit of what? Wealth? Fame? Gold? Whilst you sit back at home, or at the arena not affected. But what about the Fame, the wealth and the gold? Well, I’ll come back to that in a minute. Lets think about the consequences of this match. 4 men Gabe Nyte, The Red Headed Step Child, Brad Douglas and yours truly. 3 of these men fighting for what? Respect? No. Admiration? Maybe. What 3 of these men crave the most is Wealth, Fame and that ever so shiny gold belt. And these men like crazed animals will tear each other apart, stopping at almost nothing to get what they each feel they deserve. And you watch on. Applauding at every bone breaking move Whooping at every drop of blood spilled. Thinking “THESE MEN ARE MAD!!!” You know what? You may even be right? 3 of these men may be mad and their actions will most likely prove it. But come closer. You want to hear a secret? You want to know what is more dangerous than a mad man acting crazy? Well don’t strain those brains. I`ll tell you what. The one thing more dangerous than a mad man acting crazy in pursuit of what he feels is his right is the sane man fighting because he want to. That right people. A mad man does what he does because he knows no other way. Me? I know exactly what I`m doing and the reasons why. Why? Because I want to. And do you think I care about any of those men in that ring with me? Do you THINK I CARE ABOUT WHAT I DID TO SCORPION LAST WEEK? DO YOU?? DO YOU? NO YOU DON’T EVEN THINK DO YOU? THAT’S YOU PROBLEM ISNT IT? ISNT IT!!!!!!!! **Jack slams the palms of his hands against his forehead, his face twisting in rage. And as quickly as it came, it leaves. He removes his hands from his head and continues** “No…………thinking is not your strong point is it? But you will see soon enough. Unfortunately for some….not quite soon enough. But this Thursday in Chicago, people will start to realise exactly what is going on. The people of the windy city have always been a little slow on the uptake. Take HH Holmes for example. Who? I hear you cry. Oh people you have so much to learn and I have so little patience. HH Holmes was one America’s very first true serial killer’s. His exploits back in the early 1890’s went almost unnoticed under your very noses. He killed many people in many gruesome ways and only his greed gave him away Well rest assure greed will not be my downfall. This Thursday………the fun really begins” **Jack raises his hand and places it onto to candle extinguishing its flame. He continues to sit there, face bathed in the flickering light from the paused TV screen. He slowly glances over to it as a grin forms on his lips** Fade…… View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
