Shocking Sunday Afternoon (SMN)

15 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Regina Winters visits her New York office, records a taunting tape for Mark Estep and destroys her father’s mansion.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Shocking Sunday Afternoon (SMN) Written in Saturday, Jun 15, 2002, 13:55:49 Location: New York This is a: Match RP **Regina Winters, much to the surprise of the employees in attendance, bursts through the door of her New York office and quickly approaches her desk. It's Sunday afternoon, and needless to say, they are surprised to see her.** Female Secretary: Miss Winters? I didn't expect you back until Tuesday.... Regina: **cutting her off** Kim.... We're in the middle of a corporate takeover here. Of course I'm going to be here every second I possibly can. I'll just use the company's funds to fly myself back out to Nevada in the morning. Right now, there's something very important that I need to do. Charles, I need you to order a limo for me. Charles: Okay, but.... You just got here. Where are you going? Regina: To my Father's mansion. Charles: I thought you weren't allowed.... Regina: **interrupting** I don't pay you to think! Just do it! Charles: Yes, mistress.... Regina: Is anybody expecting me around here today, Kim? Secretary: No.... We all thought you were.... Regina: Good, let's keep it that way. And as far as the three of you are concerned, you never saw me here today, either. Got it? All Three Staff in Unison: Yes, mistress. Regina: Good. Now, Charles.... Get me that limo. **There's a time lapse, and when we come back, we find Regina in the back of a corporate limo, on her way to her Father's mansion. She looks thoughtful and expectant, all at the same time. There's a small video camera in front of her, installed in the limo some time back, that she now gladly uses. There's a message she needs to send to somebody in CCW. After making sure everything's recording okay, Regina begins....** Regina: Mark Estep.... I realize that I am facing you in a COTR elimination match this weekend. I'm just putting this little tape together to let you know what to expect after our match is over on Monday night. Expect a lengthy hospital visit. Expect several broken ribs and multiple lacerations. Expect to be embarrassed and humiliated at the hands of a superior technical wrestler. And expect your COTR journey to come to an end. This tournament is all about proving one's self.... And you, Mark? I don't think you can cut it! Most people in this federation can't. **The limo slows to a stop, and the tinted window rolls down so that the driver can speak with Regina.** Driver: Is this the place you wanted? The base of the hill? Regina: Yes. Put the car in park and wait. **Regina presses a button on the door handle to lower the power window to get a better view of the mansion. From her current vantage point, it looks like a miniature doll house. She turns her attention back to the camera.** Regina: I am going to LOVE playing with you in the ring, Mark. I'll do things to your body you never thought possible. Pleasure and pain will mix so abruptly that you won't know which one is which. I'll take you so much further than any competitor you've ever been in the ring with. **Suddenly, there's a loud explosion and the limo trembles a bit. Regina looks out the window and smiles at the fireball that's filling the air over the spot where the mansion once was. She turns back to the camera, still smiling.** Regina: And I'm in such a good mood, that I'll treat you to a little extra, Mark.... Have you ever been breast smothered? If not, you're in for a real treat! See you soon! **Regina clicks off the camera and exits the limo, joining the driver who's looking at the sight of destruction with disbelief etched all over his face.** Driver: What the hell? Regina: **small evil smile, under her breath** Goodbye, daddy... I'll see you in hell... View Biography
