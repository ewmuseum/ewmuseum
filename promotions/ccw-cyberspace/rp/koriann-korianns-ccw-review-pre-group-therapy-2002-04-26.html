What this is A Match RP in which the character Koriann hosts a review segment with her predictions for the Group Therapy tournament.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Koriann Entitled: Koriann's CCW Review: Pre-Group Therapy! Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 17:17:57 Location: Does it really matter? This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on an auditorium stage, the sight of all the old “Koriann’s CCW Reviews”. Koriann is sitting in her regular chair, with a clipboard in hand. The stage is vacant and bare, and from the looks of it, there is no studio audience. Koriann smiles at the camera a bit before launching into her diatribe.] Koriann: “Greetings CCW fans! Koriann here, and we’re getting ready for another exciting Pay Per View! That’s right, Group Therapy is here, and we have a big tournament scheduled to determine our next Champion! That’s right, Nuerosis or Devyl will be defending their title against the winner of the big tournament. So, without further ado, let’s look at some of the key players...” [A picture of SuMa appears on an invisible screen behind Koriann.] Koriann: “Now, many of you may remember this guy. I, for one, don’t. CCW wasn’t a very good program until I joined, so I really don’t have a clue as to who this guy is or why everyone’s wetting their pants over the fact that he’s back. Sure, I looked up his information in the CCW archives and found out that he was a big time champion and such, but that was back in the good old days when wrestling was a much simpler concept. Now, Grandpa SuMa may think he can walk in here and make CCW the way it was back in the day, but I’m willing to bet that he chokes when he steps up to the plate. My prediction: SuMa loses in the first round because his ancient, no-longer-athletic body will no longer be able to take the strain.” [A picture of Neurosis appears on the screen.] Koriann: “Now, Neurosis here is CCW Heavyweight champion right now. He’s not as good of a champion as I was when I held the heavyweight title, but the stupid rule book prohibits awesome talent like me from holding the belt, so we went and gave it to this chump. Now, Neurosis has been around for awhile as well, and he FINALLY, after two years, won the CCW title. If I may remind everyone yet again, **I** won the title after only being here about five or six months. Anyway, Neurosis has been bitching and moaning about this tournament for a while now. He apparently doesn’t like the fact that he has to participate. Some champion he is! Let’s just hope that whoever our next champion is isn’t a whiny little bitch. My prediction: Neurosis loses in the first round because he’s too busy complaining, or slitting open his wrist, or reading from a book of demented poetry. Either way, he’s as good as gone.” [A duel picture of Devyl and Gabriel Nyte appears up on the screen.] Koriann: “And what the hell is going on here!?!? I think these two need to get a room or something, because their constant backstage confrontations are starting to get on my nerves! If Devyl and Gabriel would relieve their aggression with each other by having a sex-filled night together, they could wake up the next morning, go their separate ways, and not bother calling each other. Devyl... I got news for ya! David’s not that great a catch! I mean, sure I like him and all, but he’s not good for you! He’s some sort of God-boy now, so just leave him alone. Gabriel... I don’t know what’s wrong with you, but raising people from the dead is NOT something one should be proud of. In fact, it’s utterly repulsive and disgusting. Do you know how diseases you can catch from dead people? Do you know how bad you make your ressurectees feel when they come back to life, only to realize that they still have dead people diseases? Besides, BOTH OF YOU need to grow up! There’s no such thing as demons and angels! You’re giving CCW a bad name! Okay, now, my prediction: Devyl and Gabriel Nyte both lose in the first round. They get into a big argument, both get hit over the head simultaneously, and both get their asses pinned.' [A picture of Angelica Lynn getting Fameassed by Koriann appears on the screen.] Koriann: 'And finally, we have mega-bitch, Angelica Lynn. Is she important to Group Therapy? No, not really. But I just wanted to show you all this cool picture that was taken earlier in the month! Look at my technique! It’s beautiful! This is just proof that I rule!!! And just so all of you fans know, I’ll be facing this uppity wench in the first round of competition. Just watch how badly I’m going to kick her ass! But then again, it’s really not a fair match-up. Angelica has no talent, while I do. It’s really kinda disappointing.' [The screen goes black behind Koriann.] Koriann: “So, that’s basically it in a nutshell. My prediction for the entire tournament: I WIN!!! The rest of these no-talent hacks can’t even hold a candle to my awesome might! So, until next time friendly viewers, just remember: I RULE!!!” [fade out...] View Biography
