The 'Shock And Awe' Campaign.

The 'Shock And Awe' Campaign. Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #107 Date: March 24, 2002 Location: The Eternal Warp Gate(PPV) [Inside the Empyrean, or the ream of chaos as it is more commonly known. We see a rather haggard looking arch, with chaos runes all over it. It seems to reflect within the red and violet sky. Right near it, we see a literal field of Khornate Bloodletters and servants of the lord of Decay, the Plaguebearers. On top of the gate, we see Gabriel Nyte, dressed in some weird looking Chaos battle armour.] Gabriel: FRIENDS! DAEMONS! WEIRD-LOOKING THINGYS! [The crowd of daemons quiets down.] Gabriel: We, very shortly, will head through this gate, that leads from our realm...to a place where no one has seen things like you. That is why, I must ask one thing. You all, as soon as you cross into the next realm...take human form. [A giant 'Argh?' is presented from the crowd.] Gabriel: There is a very simple reason why this is so. I don't think, the ticket takers at the PCW Pavillion haven't seen either things that look like Balrogs or rotting corpses. Now, as you come up, my chosen minions will give each of you your tickets. For tonight, I will need all of you to combat the forces of the negative plane of existance. Daemons, we will march to victory~! [A giant cheer is raised from the forces of Chaos.] Gabriel: ONWARD...TO VICTORY~! [The daemonic army then charges the gate, but the other daemons hold them back and give each one a ticket, and they file through the gate. Eventually, they all go through the gate leaving the three members of the cult behind in the Warp. The two members are in their natural form, the bloodletter looks like the balrog, and the Daemonette has four exposed breasts, purple hair, and very dark violet bikini bottoms.] Bloodletter: If they get turned away at the door...what do you think will happen? Gabriel: Riots, the spilling of blood, massive arrests... Daemonette: And you allowed them to go? Gabriel: Well, let's hope they destroy the building in the process. In the meantime, we have a succubus to eradicate. Let's go. [The chaotic three go through the gate and we fade from the warp to black.] View Gabriel Nyte 's Biography Back
