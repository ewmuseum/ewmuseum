The Dead Poet's Society

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Darren Jordan writes a threatening poem aimed at Danny Bryson in a temple.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Darren Jordan Entitled: The Dead Poet's Society Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 23:05:51 Location: The Mutuba Temple(Location Unknown) This is a: Match RP [As a sacrifice is now set to take place to a poor soul on LDT, M-II-S had said they would leave the temple to begin their preparations. Seeing how Blayze was wrapped up in his own mindstate, Darren had decided to stay around and possibly reflect on what the place meant. He sits, a pen twirling through his fingers with the tip touching his left cheek, in front of the tabarnacle with a piece of a scroll on the table. He ponders on what to put down on the paper, in an instant, he begins to script his words..in a poem] Darren: Lurking in the shadows, a man must step out and proclaim his presence Fear is no more a factor but a benefit used to cringe the opposition No hesitation, neither any remorse for the untaimed souls that are harmed Drenched in agony, no room to breathe nor any support to look up to Has the end of days come for the ones that fail to realize their faith? I say they fail to realize who is superior to them Pain is delivered without mercy, striking with sharp blows Enough to puncture a grown man's heart, but isnt that what we want to accomplish? Eyes are filled with nothing but eternal rage, read to set off Surrounding the mindset of the enemy and breaking his mental down Death is sentenced to those who fail to obey the rules of the higher ups On one Thursday Night, a man will invision nothing but pain..and Hell! Danny Bryson..Death Row Awaits. Signed Darren Jordan [Darren signs his name on the paper and reviews his poetry. He fold the paper, slips it into his pocket and walks away as the scene fades] View Biography
