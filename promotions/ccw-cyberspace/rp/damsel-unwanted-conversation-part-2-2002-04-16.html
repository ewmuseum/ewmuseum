Damsel - Unwanted Conversation Part 2

What this is A Match RP in which Damsel is helped by Mike in her locker room after an attack, and Mike’s private thoughts are revealed.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Damsel Entitled: Unwanted Conversation Part 2 Written in Tuesday, Apr 16, 2002, 00:54:48 Location: The Bell Centre; Post SMN This is a: Match RP *The Bell Centre; Montreal, PQ. About two hours after the conclusion of SMN........* *Christine is sitting alone in her locker room, and she’s hurting an awful lot. Her skin is still red and splotchy from the burnt coffee attack at the hands of Regina Winters. And not only that, but her head feels like she has a jackhammer constantly pounding away at it. This wasn’t a good night........ Attacked by Regina, rebuked by Lacey, and thrown into a lumberjack match with her on Lockdown. And who was Devyl even trying to kid? The Goth Princess hated them both very much. She and Lacey were in for a mass beating, she was sure of it. But Lacey did need to be brought back down to earth. And if Christine had to beat some sense into her, then by God, she would! But first, she needed to take care of herself. It hurt to move, and she was wondering if it would even be possible to make it out to her car tonight. Yeah, like she had a choice. She needed to get her butt out of here before they locked up for the night.* *Fighting the wave of dizziness that threatened to engulf her, Christine goes about the painful task of gathering all her things together. She thinks she just about has everything when her locker room door opens behind her. She turns quickly........ A terrible mistake........ And a wave of white light washes over her eyesight. She crumples to the locker room floor before she even has a chance to see who her intruder is. She pushes herself up onto her hands, trying to clear away the muddling pain, when a set of strong hands reaches down and helps her up off the floor. Christine only catches a glimpse of red hair as she’s helped to the nearby bench.* *She does finally manage to clear her head and notices that the intruder is none other than Mike Lubis........ The man with whom she shares a relationship that is currently in limbo somewhere.* Mike- You feelin’ better? That was quite a nasty attack you got earlier in the evening? Christine- *putting her hands to her head* You saw that? Mike- Yup. From the bar down the road. Christine- What are you even doing here, Mike? I thought we were calling it quits? Mike- Nah........ I prefer to think of it more like taking a break. Christine- *angry, and letting her emotions get the better of her* That’s because YOU couldn’t tell me what the hell you do with your life! You couldn’t tell me why you stood me up on what was supposed to be one of the best evenings we were going to share together! Mike- Every man has his secrets. Christine- Oh come on! You can’t tell me why you disappear for days at a time? That’s bullshit! Fucking bullshit! *Mike ignores her angered outburst and walks across the room to a sink, grabbing a towel and wetting it with cold water.* Mike- I saw your good buddy, Regina Winters, last night. Christine- Don’t change the subject, Mike! Mike- She found me playing some Solitaire at the bar. We had a nice little chat, her and I. Christine- About what? *Mike doesn’t reply right away. He finishes wetting the towel and walks it over to Christine, pressing the cool, damp cloth to her forehead.* Mike- She told me to tell you that you were going to be her bitch. Christine- *sounding dejected and defeated* Thanks, Mike. Thanks a lot. Mike- Look Christy........ These past couple weeks haven’t been good for me. I’m usually the last one to admit to something like this, but........ I missed you. A lot. I think I have feelings for you. Feelings that I haven’t felt in........ *pause* Well, for a long time now. I guess what I’m saying is that I don’t want to spend this next week by myself. I want to be with you again. Christine- *looking up at Mike with a hurt and hopeful expression on her face* Are you saying that you love me? Mike- *smiles* I guess I am. *A weak smile passes across Christine’s face and she leans into him.* Christine- *softly* Thank you. Mike- Now, let’s get you out of here. You need to get all rested up before your big match on Lockdown. Let’s go. *Mike helps Christine to her feet and grabs a hold of her two personal bags. He helps her to the door, ushers her out, and turns off the lights behind him. And as the lights go off, he smiles to himself. Manipulating women and their emotional states was so easy when you knew the right thing to say. Hefting the two bags in his one hand, and helping to support Christine with his other, he walks her down the hallway and to the exit. He was going to have to find and talk with Regina again. He wasn’t going to let that bitch ruin anything between him and Damsel. He wasn’t going to give the dominatrix the chance to blackmail him. Damsel was his, and he was going to make damn sure it stayed that way........* View Biography
