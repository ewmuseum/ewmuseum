Saying a Final Goodbye...

17 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saying a Final Goodbye... Scorpion CCW Promo #16 Date: 2-17-03 Location: New York City, New York (The Blizzard rages on in New York City at a local graveyard. A crowd of people in black attire is sitting around a Casket with the Priest blessing the casket before it’s lowered to rest six feet under the ground. Inside the casket lies Scorpion’s best friend as a child, Drake Rizzel recently shot dead by the police, prior to his death Scorpion would have his last conversation with him trying to save him, not realizing it was to late, his fate was already made.) :::: Scorpion in a black trench coat walks slowly in between grave stones to reach the funeral. :::: Priest: Drake lived his life the way he wanted and for that we respect him. He’s in god hands now and now I ask those who knew him best to speak kind words of their deceased friend and family member. :::: Scorpion raises his hand accepting the invitation to talk about Drake Rizzel. :::: Priest: Yes sir, do you have words about Drake? Scorpion: Yes sir. Priest: All right c’mon up here by the casket. :::: Scorpion walks up the aisle way and heads towards the casket. Scorpion peers out to the crowd and sees Drake’s mother crying in the front row while being comfort by Drake’s sister Cindy. :::: Scorpion: For those of you that don’t know I grew up with Drake. Drake was my best friend. Drake was a good man; he always looked out for his friends and I, always defending his friends. He always was there to stand side by side with you in a war but when it came to his problems he always wanted to stand alone, he was a warrior and I have the greatest respect for him. :::: Drake’s mother begins cry very loudly. Scorpion walks over to her giving her his handkerchief and a kiss on the cheek. :::: Scorpion: Drake might not be with us anymore but we need to remember his sprit, his smile and his leadership qualities. He was taken away from us to soon but he will live on in our hearts and sprits. He is in god’s hands now but he will always be there still to defend us and lend us the helping hand he always would give. :::: Scorpion puts his hand on the top of the casket. :::: Scorpion: Drake you were a warrior, a leader, a son, a father, and my best friend. I miss you man. Good-bye my friend. :::: Scorpion walks back down the aisle and stands in the back watching the rest of the funeral take place. :::: Priest: Well spoken about Drake. Drake was taken away from us way to soon. He had a lot yet to live for and he is going to be missed. Drake is in god’s hands now and he has a plan for Drake in a greater place, a place where we will all be in our afterlife. God bless your body and soul Drake. Ashes to ashes, dust-to-dust, gods rest your soul. :::: Drake’s mother and sister walk up to the casket crying, placing flowers atop the casket. They sit back down and the casket is lowered down into the ground. Scorpion stands there watching the casket get lowered where Drake’s mother, Mrs. Rizzel, approaches him. :::: Mrs. Rizzel: Jeff here is your handkerchief; I really appreciate you comforting me and interrupting your speech to comfort me. Scorpion: It was the least I could do Mrs. Rizzel. You can keep the handkerchief I have no need for it. Mrs. Rizzel: Jeff, Drake would have never admitted it to you but he really looked up to you. He had that Stone Cold persona and leadership quality but he truly looked up to you and wanted to be just like you. Prior to him being shot he was training in a wrestling school so he do what you do and leave the gang life alone, but it was too late. Scorpion: I’m shocked Mrs. Rizzel I’d never think he would look up to me. I just wished I could have saved him when I left the City in the first place, but I was too late as well. Mrs. Rizzel: It’s ok Jeff. You know Drake he wouldn’t listen either. (Laughs) I’m going to miss him. Well I best get going I have a long day still ahead of me. Take care Jeff; whenever you need anything just call me. Scorpion: Thank you Mrs. Rizzel I appreciate your kind offer. Whenever you need anything or have a problem please call me you have my number. I’ll always be there, just like Drake would do for me. Mrs. Rizzel: Thank you Jeff, I’ll talk to you later. Scorpion: All right Mrs. Rizzel. :::: Scorpion and Mrs. Rizzel hug. Scorpion kisses her on the cheek and she heads back to her car to the reception. Scorpion stays at the graveyard with the snow coming down heavily looking at the grave. :::: Scorpion (talking to the casket): I could have saved you Drake, but it was my own stupidly and I let you go. Drake I wish I never dragged you into the gang life, if I wasn’t so stubborn you’d still be alive and when I left I should have brought you with me. I’m going to miss you man. Save me a spot up their Drake. :::: Scorpion walks away from the casket and back to his car as he is getting in approached some gang members from Drake’s gang. :::: Scorpion: Hey guys what’s up? Gang member: If it weren’t for you Drake would be alive, you son of a bitch! Scorpion: Whoa, what are you talking about? :::: Scorpion is then attacked by at least five gang members punching him in the face and knocking him into unconsciousness. :::: TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
