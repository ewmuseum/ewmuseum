U put me 2 sleep

February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
U put me 2 sleep Brad Douglas CCW Promo #20 Date: Location: [ Sunday night. A light, slow-moving storm moved across the city of Calgary, dropping millions upon millions of shards of water from high above. As with any rainfall, the area shadowed by the shades of gray above quickly became discolored by the ever growing particles of water. ] [ As was the norm in February, the rainstorm brought with it a cold front. Not quite cold enough to freeze the falling water into sleet, hale or even snow, but that didn’t change the fact that it *was* cold out. The streets remained calm and quiet. Something that wasn’t very unusual during the time of a cold rainfall. ] [ The second hand fulfilled it’s most recent rotation, prompting the clock on the wall to tick loudly, signifying the start of the one o’clock hour. It was officially Monday morning.] [ Perhaps it was that very reason that he felt a stronger sense of excitement regarding his upcoming battle. Douglas was not the kind of guy to worry about his matches.On the flipside, he was more of a man to anticipate them. He’d usually be one of the first guys in the buildings every week. Taking the extra time before the building started to flood with people to quietly allow the atmosphere to sink in. ] [ He laid in bed tonight resting his head on his arm. He’d lost count of the sheep again. Sighing, Brad glanced at the clock to his right for what had to be the five hundredth time since first laying down. The mission was simple. Sleep. It’d deteriorated to anything but. Btad slid his arm out from behind his head, expressing a bit of soreness in the bicep area. While many would reply with dissatisfaction, Brad found himself smiling. He was proud of the way he’d pushed his body a few days ago. ] Thursday, February 13, 2003: “You sure you wanna do that?” Larry asked, struggling to lift the thirty pound weight he hovered over. “I mean, you could barely finish that last rep.” [ Lying on the bench, Brad rolled his eyes. ] “C’mon, buddy!” He exclaimed. “That was a light set, just one or two reps too many. Seriously, throw it on. I’m only looking to hit one or two more reps.” [ Larry mouthed “OK” as his response, hoisting the round, black weight off the floor and heaving it onto the bar. ] “Ok…” Larry replied, cautiously. “But you know I can’t lift that thing if it drops down on your throat. And if you kill yourself… I’m gonna kill you.” [ Brad slowly shook his head. The bar resting on the forks above him, Brad stared directly upward at it as he got his breathing in sync. As many times as Larry had tagged along to these training sessions, he should have been aware of the fact that even if there was double the current weight on the bar, he’d still be able to lift it. Brad knew his own limits and after the hours he’d already put in, he knew this was pushing it. None the less, he also knew it was far from impossible. Should he get in trouble, Larry would have to pull no more than five pounds of the current weight upward and Brad would be able to throw the rest back up onto the forks. ] “Now watch and learn.” Brad started, wrapping his hands around the bar above him. “This is how a man does it.” [ Brad chuckled a bit before throwing the bar up and locking his elbows. Taking a deep breath, he slowly allowed the bar to lower to his chest before starting to press it back upward. Exhaling slowly, Brad threw the bar up and again locked his elbows. He duplicated the process a second time, slowly lifting the bar again. It was obviously harder to do this time. Attempting a third, Brad lowered the bar and began to press it back up. It slowly fell back down to his chest. ] “Whoa, E…” Larry said with widened eyes. “You want me to get someone?” [ His face becoming red, Brad shook his head no as he continued to stare upward. ] “You sure, man?” Larry asked. “C’mon, don’t hurt yourself. Here, let me…” [ Larry reached down toward the bar to lend a hand. ] “No…” Brad grunted. “Don’t… Fucking… Touch… It.” [ Larry stepped back, holding his hands up in the air. Meanwhile, Brad paused from pushing for a moment, took another deep breath and simply threw the bar back onto it’s forks. ] “Three!” He shouted. [ Sitting up on the bench, Brad took a few breaths of air. He chuckled to himself, glancing back at Larry. ] “Sorry man…” Brad said with a smirk. “You know how it is…” [ Larry simply nodded. ] “I dig.” He replied. [ Naturally, his chest was the sorest portion of his body but in the end, he knew that by the time Tuesday rolled around, all traces of pain would be non-existent. That wasn’t the matter at hand though. The real pressing matter was this odd attack of insomnia that he was now a victim of. The last time he wasn’t able to sleep like this was back when he was at the tender age of seventeen. It was mid-term week and unfortunately, this was also the week he’d met a certain girl. Long story short, it was the eve of the first huge test he’d be taking and instead of studying, he’d devoted his time to… Other matters all week. He knew that bad grades meant less wrestling training and, well, that was never a good thing. ] [ But he wasn’t going to relive those moments right now. No, right now, he had to find a way to fall asleep. It was surprising that he’d be hit by this wave of insomnia at this time. He’d spent all day today training. Unlike the yesterday’s workout, focused on his strength and agility, today’s was focused on a different weapon. The weapon that Brad fully believed to be his most important. ] Friday, February 14, 2003: [The Saddle Dome was in one of its final stages of the revamping process that had been taking place all week. While a lot of promotional material was yet to be laid out and most of the electrical wiring wasn’t complete, most of CCW’s preparations for their upcoming event were completed. The ring itself stood intact where it should be, in the middle of thousands of empty seats. ] [ A pair of technicians stood high above the entrance on a mountain of scaffolding. Their construction of the large screen they were installing sent sparks falling to the semi-built ramp below. Walking past them, Brad made his way down to the ring and slowly climbed into it. He tested the top rope for a moment, tugging on it a few times. ] “My Elysian Fields” Brad admitted, holding his hands out to his sides. “It might be look pretty dingy, but to me, this is my second home.” [ The man standing in front of Brad nodded, jotting a quick something down on the notebook in his hand. ] “Look, man.” Brad continued. “We done? I mean, I can understand where you’re coming from and all, but this isn’t what I came here for. Couldn’t I just have caught you Tuesday before the show?” [ Shaking his head, the man raised his hand, pointing at the CCW logo. ] “I’ve got to get this stuff out.” He answered. “Not five minutes before the show, more like two to three days before.” [ Sighing, Brad rested his hands on his hips. ] “Y’know, this really does suck.” Brad complained. “Who cares if the people have their answers? Man, and people call this the *improved* CCW? I’m sorry, but I came here to get my mind straight. Not to do this thing.” [ The man shrugged his shoulders. He simply crossed his arms. ] “Don’t blame me.” He explained. “I’m just following orders. You’ve got the obligation to give one of us an interview every week, at least two days before every one of your matches. It’s in your contract…” [ Brad nodded his head quickly, as to say ‘I know, I know’. ] “Alright.” He finally gave in. “Ask your questions because I’ve got to get to work.” [ Smiling, the man opened his arms again, looking down at his notes. ] “You’ve got Gabe Nyte this week.” He began. “Your first match in CCW history. Your thoughts on him as a human being?” [ Brad shrugged, looking down toward the mat as he searched his mind for the most politically-correct explanation. ] “He’s a decent guy, I guess.” Brad admitted, scratching the back of his head. “A little bit of an asshole, but at that age, weren’t all of we? Nah, he’s not all that bad of a cat. I don’t dislike him.” [ Nodding, the interviewer jotted his answer down on his notebook. ] “A lot of words have been thrown around thus far.” The interviewer added. “But when it comes to Lockdown, what is your physical and or technical approach going to be?” [ Brad tilted his head, looking at the guy with that of a surprised look on his face. ] “C’mon, man…” Brad challenged. “You know I’m not going to tell you that. But I’ll humor you. I’ll give you a choice here. One, I can completely bullshit you and just say some crap about how I respect his abilities and I’m just going to attack with caution, or two, I can just plead the fifth. Your choice.” [ Shaking his head, the interviewer quickly began jotting into his notebook again. Not before mumbling something under his breath, of course. ] “Did you just call me an asshole?” Brad asked in surprise. “That’s not very professional…” [ The interviewer sighed loudly, turning his back on Brad to continue his writing. Brad snickered at the scene. ] “What?” Brad asked, holding his laughter back. “Dude, you are *such* a wanker, do you know that?” [ Throwing his notebook to the mat, the interviewer stormed out of the ring. After climbing down to the floor, he stopped and looked back. ] “You don’t like doing interviews?” He asked, his face getting a little red. “I’ll be sending in a *very* detailed report!” [ Not able to hold his laughter back any longer, Brad burst out in laughter as the guy stormed up the semi-constructed ramp. Shaking his head, Brad rubbed his eyes before getting ready to do a little meditation action in the ring. ] “What a fuckin dork.” Brad said under his breath as he swung his feet to the floor. [ Forget it, there was no getting to sleep. His was just way too excited for sleep to be an option. Instead, he made his way out to the small kitchen-area his hotel room offered and pulled the refrigerator door open. Forget the Nyquil. ] “Ah.” Brad exclaimed, twisting the cap off of the cold bottle of Molson Ice he held in his hand. “You’ll put me to sleep, won’t you, baby?” [ He made his way into the living room area now, seating himself on the small sofa and flipping on the television in front of him. He had hoped to catch anything new his opponent had put out but for now, he’d have to wait as it was a promo done by Gabe Nyte that currently played. ] [ Setting his bottle of Molson on the table beside him, Brad lowers his head and covers his eyes with his hand. After a few seconds, he raises his head showing his best Nyte facial impression. ] “Now I want to ask you people why everyone is so lovey-dovey about the old days of CCW.” Brad says, doing his best to duplicate his opponent’s voice. “Yes, the old days. When this promotion was a glorified Fight Club wrestling in basements while now we've arrived as a worldwide commodity.” [ Brad rubs his face with both hands. After shaking his head, he grabs the bottle again. ] “So what is it?” He asks. “Do you have this enormous amount of respect for my CCW, or are you pissing on it?' [ Taking another drink, Brad realizes that half the bottle is already empty. Wishing he grabbed two instead of one, he rests the bottle on the table again. Time to space it out a little. ] 'Ok, Gabe Nyte is just making me stupid by just watching his promo maybe i should stop' [ Brad smirks, reaching for his beer. However he stops, remembering he plans to space it out. ] 'Sarcasm is a bitch, isnt it?' [ Glancing at the television again, Brad is again disappointed as the promo he's been watching still is a shitty promo. ] 'Gabe Nyte, Your a waist of my time just like the rest of my opponents.Jack manson is so scared of he, he has not even shown his face yet. And Red headed step child...Thats exactly what he is going to be when i get my hands around his neck. So to tell you all the truth, I'm not a hardcore wrestler. Never was i, Nor ever will i be. But i am going to enjoy beating the hell out of you bitches' [ Turning again, Brad disappears into the room he came from. And thus ends our scene. Come tuesday, when Lockdown rolls into Chicago, Illinois, a certain Perfector will stand toe to toe with the monster that's been breathing down his throat all week. He'd taken the monster lightly. Very lightly. Come the moment of truth, he'd find out just what it is it takes to be a monster. ] View Brad Douglas 's Biography Back
