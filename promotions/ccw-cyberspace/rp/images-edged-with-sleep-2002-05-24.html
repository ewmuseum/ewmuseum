Images Edged With Sleep

24 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A dream-sequence roleplay in which Danny Bryson, in a motel, sees visions of an Inferno figure and the Trans-Atlantic and Pacific titles.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Images edged with sleep. Written in Friday, May 24, 2002, 06:04:13 Location: Somewhere on the Road to MDS This is a: Match RP *Somewhere, out in the Americas, in a motel at the side of the road, in the land between wakefulness and sleep, rocks the mind of Danny Bryson. The images in his mind are fuzzy, unclear around the edges. Fogging in and out of focus like a fonduu of David Lynch and Alfred Hitchcock. The image is still unclear, but a voice permeates through the fuzz. It's coarse and gravelly, it sounds like everything in the world has been set alight and is lamenting it's fate. 'You are ready. The anointed day is about to pass. The Inferno will be awoken in the others, just as it has been in you. The Day of the Fury is near.' The camera fades out and fades back in to 5 men standing side by side. Somewhere, in the background of this landscape, two glints of light reflect from something 'They know. Some among them moreso, but they all know. They know that Sunday will be the end. The Inferno that is awake inside you will bleach their bones and break their bodies. Both the titles will be united and Inferno's Fury will burn brighter.' The image fades out, and fades back in again to 5 bodies - not quite the 5 men, but, perhaps, their limboland counterparts - slumped on a backdrop of grey Canvass, bloody, battered and broken. Two gold straps lie across the bodies of these men - not quite the titles, but there again, not dissimilar. And a man stands above them. A man stands above them staring at something - the man may be Bryson. The image flits around, like Shrodingers cat, both alive and dead at the same time. It settles on the image of a man in a jet black hood, the Inferno sigil emblazened upon the forehead. The image zooms back all at once, giving the impression it had never moved and the man with the hood is staring at Bryson, hands outstretched, carrying the Trans Atlantic and Pacific titles. He carries on forward, eyes a burning mass, hand outstretched, gripping the belts....his motive is uncertain. He could be offering them, or taunting Bryson with them. He is now arms length from Bryson. He pauses, both men stare at each other knowingly. Then the masked one has a knife from nowhere. He pulls back his arm and lunges..... The Motel room ceiling is cold, hard and solid compared to the dreamlike images that have just passed. Bryson sits up in bed, smiling, his eyes bruning like two coals. He knows what will happen now, on Sunday night. He knows he has no reason to be afraid. View Biography
