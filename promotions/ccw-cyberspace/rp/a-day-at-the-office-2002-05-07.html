A Day at the Office....

7 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A short dialogue in which Mark Estep and Jack Panzer discuss their upcoming number one contenders tag match, ending with the author’s out-of-character note about writer’s block.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mark Estep Entitled: A Day at the Office.... Written in Tuesday, May 7, 2002, 22:45:26 Location: Erm.. in the office? This is a: Match RP May 8th, 2003 'Knock Knock Knock' Mark Estep:'Come in' The door to an office deap within the XCWF headquarters swings open to show Mark Estep sitting behind his desk. He has his back turned to the door and desk, and he is reading a newspaper. He folds the newspaper up and turns around to see Jack Panzer standing in front of him. Mark Estep:'Oh God! You scared me, I thought i told you not to do that to me...' Jack Panzer:'Sorry about that Mark. It will not happen again...' Mark Estep:'Let's hope not, I think I had a premature minor heart attack... So what do you need?' Jack Panzer:'What do you think I need? We have a number one contendors match tommorrow...' Mark Estep:'What!? Against who!? And why wasn't I informed!?' Jack Panzer:'You were, I left a message with your secretary...' Mark Estep:'Sit down, relax... we have a match tomorrow...' Jack Panzer:'I know we have a match tomorrow, I told you about it' Mark Estep:'Nah, I already knew...' Jack Panzer:'Why do I have to team with you!?' Mark Estep:'Just lucky I guess...' Jack Panzer:'So, are you gonna work out before tomorrow?' Mark Estep:'Nah, I wouldn't worry about the other two team sin the match....' Jack Panzer:'Yeah, me either... We may not have the most experience in the match, but we are the best physically, mentally, and emotionally.' Mark Estep:'Sheesh, you sound like a Tylenol Commercial....' Jack Panzer:'Shut up' Mark Estep:'So how did this whole thing between you and Blaze start?' Jack Panzer:'It didn't really just start. He just has a bad attitude, and I'm going to help him correct it.' Mark Estep:'I understand, but why did you ask me to team up with you?' Jack Panzer:'Well, I've seen you in the PCW, and I've seen you in the XCWF, and I figured 'The Malicious One' and 'The War Machine' would make a great tag team, and after LDT, everyone will know that we are the best tag team, and that we are the #1 contendors to the tag team titles.' Mark Estep:'Ya don't dont have to make me believe..... I already know we are better than most of the other teams.' Jack Panzer:'All the other teams... not just most.... All........' -The Scene Fades out- OOC:THIS IS A CRAAAAAAAAP RP! ILL SHOOT MYSELF BEFORE I WASTE YOUR TIME WITH ANOTHER ONE OF THESE! Just a lack of ideas gimme this stuff. I hate writers block. But, dont worry, ill have a spurt here soon.... hopefully... View Biography
