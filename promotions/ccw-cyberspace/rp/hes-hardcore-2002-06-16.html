He's Hardcore

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which the Masked Mullet boards an aircraft, tackles a passenger as a suspected villain and is questioned by police.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Masket Mullet Entitled: He's Hardcore.. Written in Sunday, Jun 16, 2002, 12:49:39 pst Location: meh.. This is a: Match RP Clem sat there, a smile on his face as he plopped down into the the seat of the 747 jet, that he was taking to SMN. Everything was calm, and happy, and Clem stretched out, placing his hands behind his head. The plane took off with out a hitch, everythign was going smoothly, and everyone was happy. Clem was smiling as he stretched out once more, at least, until his hand got smacked by a wrinkled old lady in the seat next to him. Old Lady: Stop that, ya whipper snapper!! Clem: Eh, sorry Ma'am.. I'll try not ta do it again.. Old Lady: Shut up.. and be quiet the rest of the flight you mulleted freak.. Clem: Yes'm.... With a sigh, he started rifling through the airplane magazines, in hope of a nice intellegant publication, like penthouse, or playboy, on which he could spend his time oogling the women he could never come close to in real life. Unfortunately, there was none. Clem frowned, and tried to find something to read, entertaining, with a vivid plot, and hot busty chicks.. unfortunately, there were no comic books either, rather disappointed, Clem sat back in the seat, and looked around. Clem: How much longer is this flight.. Old Lady: I thought I told you to.. SHUT UP!! Clem shrunk back, and sighed again, his fingers started to tap the arm rest of the seat. This was about the time of the day, where he killed his brain with alochol, unfortunately for Clem, this was an alcohol free flight, and his brain was slightly becoming more and more aware of his surroundings.. and well, more and more bored. A bored mind, is a dangerous thing, especially for someone with a low level of intellegance, they start imagining things.. Clem's eyes started looking around, he could swear he smelled the scent of evil, just at that second. He ignored it.. for now, as a flight attendant came up. Flight Attendant: Do you need anything sir? Clem: Yeah, can I get a beer? Flight Attendant: This is a non-alcohol serving flight, sir.. Clem: Yeah.. so, can I get a beer? Flight Attendant: There is alochol in beer... Clem: So, I'm not getting a beer? The Flight attendant groaned, and sighed, just moving onto the next row... Old Lady: I THOUGHT I TOLD YOU TO SHUT UP!! Clem: EEP!! 5 minutes later... Clem's mind was now bored, he could swear that he could sense an evil force lurking about this plane flight. He knew exactly, that there was something here, waiting to pounce upon the innocent people on the plane. He started looking around.. from person to person, slowly.. Finally, his eyes came to rest upon someone of middle-eastern orgins, and Clem's eyes slowly widened. He saw the man hunched over, talking to a flight attendant in the kitchen of the plane, in hushed tones. Clem's eyes slowly narrowed, as he picked himself up from the seat, and walked back into the bathrooms. He was glad he wore his costume under his clothes... he threw off his Pantera T-Shirt and jeans.. revealing his wrestling costume. He placed the Luchadore mask upon his head, and backed up, kicking the door open.. The Masked Mullet: HALT DOER OF EVIL!! OR FACE THE WHITE TRASH FIST OF JUSTICE!! Passanger #2: What the hell? Passanger #45: It's just some superhero nutcase.. let's ignore him.. maybe he'll shut the hell up... Passanger #69: Yeah.. just ignore him.. GO SIT DOWN BUDDY!! Clem ignored the crowd, and ran down the aisle, tackling the man of middle eastern orgins, and then.. all hell broke loose.... 5 Hour Later Police Officer Jones: Can you tell me what happened here? Passanger #45: Yeah.. it was horrible.. this guy, wearing spandex.. and with a mullet, he just busted out of the bathrooms, and then tackled the captain of the plane!! I've never seen anything like it!! FBI Officer Greggers: That's weird.. do you think it was Taliban related? Passanger #78: He said that he was fighting to protect america from the Taliban.. so I don't know.. he was a real nutcase.. he had the weirdest haircut.. The FBI Officer looks slowly at the remains of the flight, and sighs. Fortunately, everyone had gotten out alright, and no one was really even hurt, but this was just damns strange. FBI Officer: Whoever did this kind of damange.. must have been truely.. hardcore.. Police Officer Jones: Too true.. this is very scary.. The End? View Biography
