Cianan - Who Am I? Who are you?

5 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Challenge-type RP in which Cianan rants at returning past stars and vows to defend what he considers his title.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: Who Am I? Who are you? Written in Sunday, May 5, 2002, 02:29:01 Location: I dunno.. This is a: Challenge ((I guess this is more of an Interview/Challange.. but hey..)) We see Cianan, very unhappy, (when isn't he?). His face, a wreck, his eyes bloodshot. He runs a hand through the tangled mess he calls hair, and throws it over his shoulder. He walks.. no.. more like stalks, back and forth, a snarl, curled on his lips. ((::Laughs:: Sorry, not my best work.. tired.. massive ammounts of Vicodine..forgive me if I ramble.. but.. it does work with the character..)) Cianan: This.. is an unusual time for CCW, much more different then it was a few months ago. It was interesting then, and is interesting now. Changes have occured, mainly, the arival of old faces. I'll list them... The Supreme Machine, 'Fat Ass' Phil Burner, Mr. Gobstopper, Chang The Mongolian Giant, John Coens.. all of you people, past stars. Cianan stops, his head lowers, a growl forming from the pit of his stomach. Cianan: AND YOU WALK RIGHT BACK HERE EXPECTING TO BE HANDED WHAT IS MINE! Cianan brings his hand up, turning his head to the side, struggling to keep himself under control, which is visualized, by the constant clenching and unclenching of his fist.. the veins protruding from his neck, and forearms. Cianan: You arrogant bastards.. Gobbstopper, you in particular.. you go on, and mention everyone, and everything under the sun, about how great you and The Supreme Machine were. How you deserve this, and deserve that. Do you even know who I am? Cianan raises his chin, staring down at the camera. Cianan: Do you know, of everyone that has worked here? Everyone who has strived? In one fast move, you all.. SuMa, Fat Ass, Gobstopper.. have insulted everyone who has worked here, while you were away. You disrespect me. You disrepespect CCW. You spit in the face, of the blood, the battles, and everything that we have been threw since you left. Cianan keeps his head raised in defiance, anger, and pride for himself. Cianan: You gold whores.. Can you name the last five champions? Or do they not matter because you weren't here? We're as much of CCW's past as you are.. and we're more of it's future. You serve to strip us of what was rightfully ours, to be handed to you on a silver platter, what people like myself, Enigma, and that bastard Kensuke.. were here to do. You think that I'll give up....? No. I'll rip you all to shreds.. That title was mine.. it IS mine.. I never LOST IT. Cianan spits to the side, running his tounge over his canine, a sneer growing on his lips. Cianan: People say I'm going insane.. Well.. Phil, SuMa, Gobber.. You tried to steal what was mine.. I WANT IT BACK. You.. just like Blayze.. just like Kensuke.. you want to keep me from what is mine. Neurosis.. keep my title warm for me.. Cianan: You past superstars.. are just ghosts.. skeletons in the closet of CCW, just here to kick up dust, so you're not completely forgotten. Let's see if they'll forget you when you dissappear for good, never to trod on the hard earned name of CCW again. Cianan: Prepare to be Enlightened.. Prepare to realize.. that you can't stand up to me. Prepare to realize.. That I will.. do ANYTHING.. for what I want. I want.. respect. Not from you.. I could care less.. I want respect.. from the world.. they're the ones that owe me.. you're just pawns.. in me getting that respect.. so, which one of you pawns will be sacraficed first? A grim smile crosses over Cianan's face... Cianan: I'll sacrafice you all.. (in a mocking tone) Because, I CAN! View Biography
