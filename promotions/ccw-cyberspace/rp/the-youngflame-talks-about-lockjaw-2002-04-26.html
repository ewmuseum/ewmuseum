The Youngflame Talks About Lockjaw

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A debut interview-class roleplay in which seventeen-year-old Tristan Matthew mocks CCW’s Lockdown show from a campsite.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Tristan Matthew Entitled: --The Youngflame talks about Lockjaw-- Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 07:18:50 Location: Picnic Ground w/Campfire This is a: Interview ~Debut RP~ Prelude: Only 17 years old and already accepted into a wrestling company that claims to be 'THE BEST IN WRESTLING', Tristan Matthew's mind is only JUST handling everything coming his way. He was handed a video tape of a CCW show, Lockdown, and told to view it so that he can update himself on what is happening. Upon arriving home, Tristan skimmed through the tape and found it quite, quite boring. Prelude Images: Tristan is shown in only a pair of torn carpenter pants watching Lockdown, fast-forwarding through most parts and sort of looking around unimpressed. Prelude (cont): After managing to cut down the lengthy show to a 20minute re-cap, Tristan took up his tape and jammed it into a 1980 style video camera. Prelude Images: Tristan grabs video and really messes with the old video-camera before finally working out how to get it in. He picks up his keys to a car. Prelude (cont): Once he had his keys and video-tape, Tristan headed out in his car to a large picnic ground that he'd visited since child-hood [like 4 or 5 years of age]. Arriving at the picnic ground in a car he wasn't legally allowed to drive, Tristan chose his spot carefully, parked his car and proceeded to light a small campfire that could be easily drowned in an emergency and then took up his camera. Prelude Images: Tristan does the above but then finds trouble with the camera when he nearly drops it and then looks for the record button. Prelude (cont): Tristan thought the best way to impress his new CCW superiors was to tape-over the Lockdown video with his very own debut interview. Really, however, Tristan didn't know any CCW interviewers and wanted to talk crap about the CCW Wrestlers and how he'd rather call CCW LOCKDOWN--CCW LOCKJAW. Now up to date, Tristan will continue... Interview: Tristan dressed in a pair of cream carpenter pants and a green 'BURNED' shirt, sits behind his camp-fire, tape in hand. He quickly stands and positions the camera on a log so that he can talk without holding it. 'By doing this, the CCW guys can check me out without my quirky and so move-able hands getting in their way!' Tristan laughs at his intelligence and then sits back down. 'I watched Lockdown.' Saying this in the direction of the camera, Tristan's turn to serious-mode was quick and noticable [perhaps something that would happen in a match]. 'I could call that show, LOCKJAW~!' Tristan blasts the last word. 'The show is ALL about words! Can anyone actually WALK THE WALK? Since when was wrestling about how good you can use your mouth? Is CCW a wrestling federation or a college for hookers and lawyers alike!?' Tristan now calms himself a bit but cannot stop his momentum. 'Maybe Lockjaw was just a one off thing cause the Pay-Per-View is on in a few days? Maybe not... The show starts out talking about some Doctor and his sexual--I mean MEDICAL experience with big scary Razor Blades Ben! Oh dear me! Wouldn't want to be around when he gets back from hospital... WOULDN'T WANNA BE AROUND WHILE HE IS IN HOSPITAL IF THAT'S WHAT WE HAVE TO SEE THROUGH HIS RECOVERY PROCESS!!' Tristan throws his head down, shaking it in dissapointment. He continues in anger, not resenting any word he says. 'What happened next? Excuse my memory, I think I fast-forwarded through a match with some former PCW, 'I WISH I WAS BACK THERE' guy Mark Estep and some Wang, Kang, Bang guy or whatever. Then the commentators acted really excited and the 'I was I was back in PCW' guy won the match with a move called the 'ESTEPPER'!? What the hell kind of name is that!? It needs to be BURNED out!! I'd be GLAD to do it. So then, continuing with my little re-cap/what I thought of your show thing, a group of half-wits presented themselves on camera and proceeded to take up a whole 40seconds of my time with USELESS drivel about getting a night off, or night on, or oh WHATEVER-- Then I had to watch commericals! That was just the first segement? Half-wits who probably still eat Fruit Loops for breakfast talking about a Night-Off? What the...' Tristan tries to calm down and throws another piece of wood on the fire, causing the spark thing to happen. Tristan pays no attention and still continues. 'After the commercials started I figured I'd chuck a quickie to the toilet to go for a number 1 but once I started I felt the urge for a 2 and then had to sit down. I got back after a early jobber-match of some sort and then saw the same freakin half-wit gang I saw before! ...I decided this time I'd give them a chance and actually tuned in... ...for a whole five split seconds. How damn boring can you possibly get!? Something about husbands, Therapy on Sunday... The show, for me, was turning into more of an Oprah Winfrey/Jerry Springer marathon than LOCKDOWN, THE BEST IN WRESTLING!' Tristan mocks the 'BEST IN WRESTLING' line and laughs under his breath. He laughs until he manages to remember what to say next. 'Coyote! Yeah...' Tristan looks up to the sky and then back at the camera. 'Yeah...' Again he turns to the sky as if he was asking for help as to what to say. 'Some Night...' Tristan then gets an idea or something. 'NIGHT! NIGHT! A guy named 'Night' won the match!' Tristan realises he sounded excited. 'Oh Hoorah... The guess what happened? Commercials! I ran out and got a Fanta before the next segment.. I didn't hurry thinking I'd be subject to more half-wit entertainment for the Graduates of Hooker High.' Tristan looks at the fire. 'Mmm..Fanta...' He turns back to the camera. 'Speaking of Hooker High...' Tristan smiles. 'We then got to see these two girls. I'm thinkin' that they must've just started their courses cause the clothes didn't look THAT bad... certainly not street worthy. This Winter chick and then a Korean woman walked out and had a splappy pappy marathon for all the 'I can't get laid and watch wrestling instead' fans.' Tristan stops and corrects himself. 'Oh no, it was a tag-match. Four women... ...oh what the hell, it doesn't matter. They were still wearing street-cred material and they were trying to slap each other for the pappy poontang fans..' Tristan turns to the fire. 'MMmm..Poontang...' He, again, turns back to the camera. 'I'm even getting bored doing a re-cap for you guys. You know, if you didn't ask what I thought of the show, I wouldn't have bothered even finding this old camera, cause this is freakin useless.' Tristan decides the quicker he can finish this, the better. 'Then we got some cool Linkin Park theme music for a guy who looked like a bit of a candidate for the 'I am a bitch' award. Then a Blayze got knocked out, not burned... ...funnily enough, with the whole name thing going on there.' Tristan, in his boredom, begins to digress. 'Blayze? The Young Flame? Hey, there's a common thingy between us! Well I reckon that's probably about it. I'm young, he walks like an 83 year old on Metamucil; I'm freakin talented and fan-pleasing, he's over-rated and wouldn't know how to please himself with the comatosed body of Kirsten Dunst, let alone please the crowd! Blayze...' Tristan laughs. 'BLAYZE!? I'm the only Flame in this federation and I'm only young. With age things only grow... This Flame right here, will grow and will KEEP on BURNING.' Tristan throws another piece of wood on the fire. 'Like this wood, each CCW Hooker/Lawyer/Wrestler is burning out one minute, one SECOND at a time. Their talent is pathetic and they have no time to save it.' Tristan eyes the camera closely. 'Once something is BURNED it CANNOT be RESCUED.' He pauses. 'I am the Young Flame of CCW. I am Tristan Matthew. Seventeen years old. Honor Student. Outgoing. Takes every risk. No fear.' Tristan has obviously grown tired of his re-cap on his thoughts of Lockdown and is simply listing off what he has for and will do in CCW. 'Every damn punk, half-wit and hooker can talk about me, they can mock me, they can say how I'm young and I'm all talk, they can even challenge me to step into the ring after the Pay-Per-View, just to make me look like I'm the one talking all the talk and doing no walking.' Tristan laughs. 'That's damn FINE by me. I am CCW's New Flame. I'll be showing what a real star can do. I'll show what ENTERTAINMENT is all about!' He grabs the camera and stands. 'Suma, Panzer, Blayze, Bojangles, Chang, Kensuke, TVO, Gabe and his half-wit buddies, Estep or any other PCW miss-hitters... Hell EVEN BEN DAMN BLADES! I'm CCW's Young Flame and I'll will BURN each and every one of you OUT!!' Tristan sits the camera back down and lets it view the fire as he tips water over it and the steam rises into the air. The hissing of the flames going out is very much what CCW will hear in weeks to come as 'The Young Flame' Tristan Matthew, steps into the public eye and more-so, after PPV, a CCW ring. This isn't the LAST CCW is going to hear from Tristan until he is given a match. He has contacted a CCW TV Crew and plans on using it to the best of his ability to talk about every last bit of what he will do in CCW. Fade Out: Scene fades to a CCW logo with The Young Flame text appearing in the middle. The hissing and crackling of the drowned fire, fading on the audio. View Biography
