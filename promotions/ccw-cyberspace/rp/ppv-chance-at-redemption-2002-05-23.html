(PPV) Chance At Redemption

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Mr. Gobstopper hikes in the Blue Ridge Mountains while discussing Memorial Day Showdown.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mr. Gobstopper Entitled: (PPV) Chance at Redemption... Written in Thursday, May 23, 2002, 12:51:48 Location: Somewhere in the Blue Ridge Mountains This is a: Match RP *Scene opens in a dense forest where one person can be seen wearing a backpack and walking uphill with a wooden stick. As the camera pans in closer you can see that it's Mr. Gobstopper. Each step he takes he seems to be struggling. Birds chirp from the trees and very little sun can be seen from above the tree tops. A lizard shoots by and hides inside a hollow log. Mr. Gobstopper stops and takes a breather.* Mr. Gobstopper: You know, the only way to keep in shape is to do the things you love. I love to hike. Yeah, I'm sore as hell from Monday night, but like I said, keep in shape. My goal today is to make it to the top of this small mountain. Around here, these mountains are covered with trees, wildlife, and some are even snowcapped. It's not Colorado, but I love this place nonetheless. But I must keep going. *He takes another step, showing signs of pain, apparently suffering from bruises sustained on Monday. Slowly, he moves ahead.* Mr. Gobstopper: See, some people just can't give a guy a break. Some people treat others so unfairly. But I think it's time we started being nicer to guys like SuMa and Ricky Thunder. And me, I'm giving myself another chance. While SuMa may have beaten me twice since I've returned, it's not over between him and I. Much like this mountain, I will move forward with things. Argh... *He steps in a small ditch and nearly falls on his face. He regains his balance and sits on a rock.* Mr. Gobstopper: I've been at this mountain for the last two hours. I'm hot, I'm tired, but I am not done. And you know what? That stays true with this coming Sunday. Memorial Day Showdown. It's very fitting for me to be hiking these Blue Ridge Mountains. Just two hours west of the place MDS will come from, Washington, D.C., I figure this is the best place to focus my thoughts on what lies ahead. Seems I'll be facing some very tough opponents. *He gets up and begins ascending back up the mountain, but slowly.* Mr. Gobstopper: I get a shot at both the TA and Pacific title, neither of which I've held before. But there are a few obstacles in my way. I have five other guys gunning for the same belts. Carnage, TVO, McAllister... I've worked with those guys in the past. Tough competitors. These other guys, Spider and Danny Bryson... never worked with them directly. But I will guarantee this, I will top all five of them. Like this mountain, I will reach the summit of MDS and I will walk out a champion. If none of the other guys like that, well, they know where to shove it. So for me, I'm about 15 minutes from the top of this mountain, and that is my goal... for today. *Gobstopper continues on as the camera ceases to follow. Fade out.* View Biography
