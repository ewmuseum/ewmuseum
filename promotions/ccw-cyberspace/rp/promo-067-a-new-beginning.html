A New Beginning...

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A New Beginning... Adam Christ CCW Promo #67 Date: 10/03/03 Location: Some City [ A flashback of Lockdown feeds our eyes, showing scenes of the match that many might have bypassed. Adam Christ and Jack Manson against Toxic and an absent Gabriel Nyte. A slaughter to one unknown face by the unknown hands of two others. Flesh is torn. Blood is spilt. All the while, thousands of ignorant eyes turn away in disgust or lack of interest. Meaningless pain. The victors rise above their bloodied foe. Their arms are raised. Their effort is rewarded by their own grins. Useless glory. ] Everything that ends... once began. [ The flashback rewinds itself and stops at a segment before the match, showing the confrontation between Jack Manson and Adam Christ. The sound, however, is muted... forcuins us to use their lips and facial expressions to guess. Shortly in the segment, Jack has Adam by the throat and lifts him off of the ground. Jack screams something, Adam nods, and Jack lets him go. ] Everything that begins... eventually ends. [ The flashback then fast-forwards, stopping at one specific part during this certain match. This part, which has sound, shows the first situation of Adam Christ reacting to the noises from the audience. Blind from confusion, Adam pulls at his hair and paces aimlessly around the ring. Suddenly, however, his confusion is erased by the hand of the one who had him by the throat just minutes before. The man who threatened him--who WARNED him. This man is, of course, Jack Manson. Somehow, being slapped in the faces wakes Adam up. Somehow, Adam's mind expands and escapes from it's praise-desireing cage, setting him free for a moment... but quickly putting a whole new cage in it's place. Obidiently, Adam then follows every command given to him from Jack, forcing the shell of his former self to escape along with the flow of Toxic's blood. ...A child is born. ...A puppet of blood and flesh. ...Genesis. ] A new beginning... [ The scene then cuts to the streets of some city. The air is damp and moist, the stench of mildew drifting in the breeze. The skies are filled with clouds of grey, making the city almost look black and white. The sound of purring thunder echoes in the distance. People hiding under unbrellas hurry themselves into taxi's, busses and buildings. The first droplette or rain falls to the concrete, followed by millions. The sidewalks are suddenly abandoned. Completely empty... except for one person. The camera pans closer to this person, whom is sitting on the curb of the road. The man proves himself to be Adam Christ, still wearing his apparel from the last Lockdown. Bare-back and wet, Adam's head appears to be tilted downward, staring at the palms of his hands. His fingertips are gloved with redness that runs like water down his wrists and to his elbows. Adam's eye is wide open, and his hair is sticking to his mask like a magnet. Even as rain runs into and over his eyeballs, he dares not blink. ...His mouth cracks open to let out a long breath, which is visible in the cold air. Ever so slowly, Adam starts to speak... ] Adam Christ: ...This is what they came to see. ...And I gave it to them... and they didn't cheer. [ A thunderous roar of thunder explodes in the sky. Adam sits, uneffected. ...His fingertips curl themselves a bit, but otherwise he sits still. ...He continues... ] Adam Christ: ...Yet, I understand now... why they didn't cheer. Jack showed it to me... the truth. ...He made it all make perfect sense. [ Adam's hands raise and run themselves through his hair, wiping the blood from them. His hands then fall forward, his head tilting up as he grins at the sky, the water hitting his wide-open eye relentlessly as he screams. ] Adam Christ: THEY DON'T MATTER! THEY DON'T FUCKING MATTER!! THE PRIASE OF ONE SATISFIES ME JUST AS MUCH AS THE PRAISE OF THOUSANDS!! I CAN FINALLY SEE! I DON'T NEED TO RUN AWAY!! [ Adam grins widely as thunder screams back at him. His head lowers back down, his grin vanishing from his gaze. ...He stares forward, blankly. ] Adam Christ: ...What a delicious feeling... [ Adam sits there, staring at his own breath until a dog suddenly barks at him from behind. Adam, however, sits un-noticed. In fact, it takes about ten barks for Adam to realize anything. Adam glances over his shoulder, glancing at the small dog that's sniffing away at his fingertips, it's tail tucked between his legs. Adam stares blankly at it before an old woman steps out of a small shop behind the dog. The woman takes two steps forward and calls out... ] Old Woman: Jacob! ...Jacob, come 'ere, boy...! [ The small dog turns from Adam and rushes over to the old woman, moving to it's hind legs and placing it's front paws on her knees. She bends over and picks the dog up. ] Old Woman: There you are...! [ The dog begins to bark at Adam a bit more. The woman stares forward, over Adam and at the other side of the street. ] Old Woman: Who's there...? [ ...Adam stares at the woman, and glances over at the direction she's looking. ...No one's there. ...The woman speaks once more. ] Old Woman: Hello? ...Who's there...? [ ...Adam looks back at the woman, slowly standing up and turning to face her. ] Adam Christ: ...I'm here. Old Woman: What're you doing outside, boy? You'll catch your death of cold...! [ The woman appears to be staring in a completly different direction as Adam Christ. ...Adam, confused, takes a step toward her. ] Adam Christ: ...I'm not sure. Old Woman: You ain't sure? You should be home next to th' fireplace! The storm ain't gunna get any better. Adam Christ: ...I don't have a home. Old Woman: Don't have a home? You some kind of crazy? Adam Christ: ...I'm not a kind of crazy, no. [ The old woman lets out a crisp laugh. ] Old Woman: Come, come...! I'll get you a hot cocoa 'er' somethin'. [ The old woman turns and re-enters the small shop, leaving the door open as an invitation for Adam to follow. Adam hesitates, but does just that. ...He shuts the door behind him. Once inside the shop, Adam takes his seat at a circled table pressed against a corner. Adam looks at the old woman as she exits to the back of the shop, which also seems to be her house. Adam looks around the shop, discovering that she sells... wood-based accesories. Desks, hat racks, chairs, wooden toys, shelves... just to pick a few. Adam then follows his ears, turning his head to a television that's playing in the background. The see the screan clearly is impossible, but it's loud enough for Adam to hear. ...It says something like this: ] 'The next match... Dan Logan... Josh Marshall... Adam Christ... Lockdown...' [ The rest seems muffled. ...The lights flicker for a moment, making the dog bark once more. The old woman returns from the room, mug in hand. ] Old Woman: Hush, Jacob! Hush...! [ The woman walks up to the table Adam sits at and sets the mug before Adam, before taking a seat of her own on the opposing side. ...Adam stares at the mug and then looks back up at the old woman. She smiles, staring right through him with pale, glazed-over eyes. ...Adam stares at her, curious. ...She speaks. ] Old Woman: It ain't polite to stare. [ Adam looks away. ] Adam Christ: ...Sorry. Old Woman: It's alright. I'm used t' it, bein' blind all my life. Adam Christ: ...Are you a carpenter? Old Woman: A carpenter? Me? Naw... I ain't. My husband is, though. Adam Christ: He's good. Old Woman: Yeah, he is... but he passed away a year ago. [ ...The two sit there, silent. Adam stares at the mug she gave him, watching as the steam from the cocoa rises. ...Staring at it. ...The woman speaks once more. ] Old Woman: So, what's your name? Adam Christ: ...Adam. Old Woman: It's nice t' meet you, Adam. Are you cold? You want a towel? Adam Christ: No, I'm fine. Old Woman: Alright. [ ...The two sit there, a strange feeling of tension in the room. The woman then speaks once more. ] Old Woman: What's on your mind, Adam? [ Adam blinks, looking up at her. ] Adam Christ: ...I'm thinking about killing someone. Old Woman: Killing? Now, the Lord ain't fine with killin'...! Adam Christ: ... Old Woman: Who you fixin' on killin'? Adam Christ: ...Two people. ...Dan Logan and Josh Marshall. Old Woman: Now, why would you want t' kill thems people? Adam Christ: ...It's my job. Old Woman: You're job? What're you, a hitman? Adam Christ: ...Something like that. Old Woman: Well, remember... th' Lord comes before anythin' and everythin'. The Lord wants to bring peace to the world. The Lord has a plan t' save... [ The woman continues to speak, but Adam seems to ignore her... his attention turning to the dog that seems to be gnawing at the leg of his pants. Adam reaches down to the dog, allowing it to sniff at his fingertips once again. ...Suddenly, the dog opens it's mouth and bites at Adam's fingers. Adam lets him... not reacting to it at all, just staring in awe. Adam slowly moves his other hand over the dog's head, quickly grabbing it's jaw and closing it tightly over Adam's fingers. His own blood trinkles into the dog's mouth. Adam's eyes narrow as he presses harder, applying an extreme amount of force to the dog's mouth. The dog yelps loudly, pissing itself and shoving it's tail between it's leg, trying desperately to be released from Adam's grasp. Anger masks over Adam's face as a grin forms, his thoughts screaming throughout his mind. ] BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED-BLEED! [ Suddenly, the voice of the old woman splits into Adam's mind, forcing him to release his grasp on the dog. ] Old Woman: What're you doing to Jacob!? Stop that!! [ The dog yelps once more and scurries back, licking it's lips franticly. ...Adam looks at the old woman for a moment before quickly jerking to his feet and rushing out of the shop, entering the rain once more. ...The scene then fades to black, shortly coming back up to Adam Christ, sitting at a street's curb somewhere else within the city. The rain still pours as Adam stares at his hands once more, his own blood now coating his fingertips and palms. ...A grin covers his face. ] Adam Christ: I love this feeling! ...I LOVE IT! This is my lust... this is my extacy! [ Adam drags his tongue over his fingertips, licking his wounds and swallowing down the blood that's on them. He lets the rest drip off with the rain. Staring at the blood falling to the concrete below, Adam speaks... ] Adam Christ: ...I have another chance to spill blood at the next Lockdown. ...I have another chance to spill the blood of OTHERS. ...Dan Logan and Josh Marshall... [ His eye narrows a bit. ] Adam Christ: ...Dan Logan's mouth is bigger than his brain. ...He can't back up his endless rants and threats in the ring. He hides behind his words, hoping they'll protect him. Well, they WON'T. Dan Logan, you can sit there and threat a CAMERA, thinking I'm listening... but I'm NOT! I DON'T NEED TO LISTEN TO YOU! I DON'T NEED TO HEAR YOUR RANTS! I DON'T NEED TO HEAR YOUR THREATS! I've seen you, Dan... I've seen into your soul. I've SEEN what you DESIRE. Your desire GLORY. You desire THEIR praise! You're just like how I was... but, I saw the TRUTH! I WAS AWAKEN! MY MIND IS FREE FROM THEIR CHAINS! ...Yet, you suffer the fate that I once held dearly! Let ME be the key to your soul, Dan! I'll SHOW you how much your words don't mean SHIT in this business! I'll tear your heart out and hold it for ALL of them to see! And when you lye there, choking back on your blood and tears... you'll recieve NO praise. You'll recieve NO glory. The only thing you'll recieve is the paycheck when you return backstage. ...That is... if I LET you return backstage. You desire glory? You want to be known? ...I'll give you that chance. I'll make you known, Don! I'll MAKE YOU KNOWN!! [ Adam grins to the camera, thunder heard from above. ] Adam Christ: And Josh Marshall, I didn't forget you...! ...The truth is, Josh, I have NO FUCKING CLUE who you are! You could be a fucking 3-foot tall Chink, for all I know! But even though I don't know you, I do know OF you. The truth is... you're in a match with ME in it. And I'm NOT going to run away. Yet... I'm going to cut you a little slack. I'm going to give you a little pity, Josh. ...Because I don't know you, I'm not going to kill you. ...Not just yet. Instead, I'm just going to use you as an example. I'm NOT Adam Christopher. I'm NOT CCW's Brooklyn Brawler. I'm Adam 'FUCKING' Christ... as to not rip off Brad Lamen. It's not about the people, anymore! I know my purpose, now... Jack Manson's showed me the way! I CAN TAKE PAIN! I LIKE IT! IT FEEDS ME... IT'S MY OXYGEN! CAN YOU SAY THE SAME!? CAN YOU BLEED, LIKE ME!? CAN YOU!?! CAN YOU!?!?!? LET'S FIND OUT!!!!! [ Adam grabs hold of his hair, pulling his head down forcefully. He screams out: ] Adam Christ: IT BEGINS! IT BEGINS NOW!! ARE YOU READY!? ARE YOU FUCKING READY!?!??!?! [ The scene abruptly cuts to black. Adam's voice is heard in the darkness, speaking calmly... ] Adam Christ: ...Dan Logan's mouth will be shut... and Josh Marshall's eyes will be opened. Blood will spill. Pain will be spread like butter on toast. ...It begins now. ...When will it end? [ The scene shows quick, flashing images of Adam Christ standing up and walking off in the rain before it fades to black once more. ] View Adam Christ 's Biography Back
