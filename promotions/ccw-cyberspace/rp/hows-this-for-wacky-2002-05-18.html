How's This for Wacky?

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comic crossover dream sequence involving Spider-Man and video game fighters that ends with Spider waking and thinking about his match against Kensuke.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Spider Entitled: How's this for wacky? Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 10:15:00 Location: Spider's Dreamworld This is a: Match RP [We begin in the skies above New York City, the city is bustling today, the metallic veins of traffic glisten in the sunlight, helicopters and aircraft soar overhead, then suddenly on sound is heard.] THWIP! SMACK! [We then see, everyones fave wallcrawler, Spider-Man, swing over the roop-tops of the City. However...he looks different...He's wearing the robes of a Jedi and carrying a lightsaber!] Spider-Man(Thinking): Ah, another day, another member of the dark side to eliminate. Wait...Spider-Sense is tingling... [Spider-Man looks around and looks to a roof-top to see a leather-clad spiky haired individual, and someone with shorter har, but with no shirt and jeans with a yellow dragon on it. Yelling can be heard from a distance.] Spider-Man: Hello, what's that? Better check it out. THWIP! SMACK! [Spider-man then heads over to see Jacky Bryant from Virtua Fighter 4, and Jann Lee from Dead or Alive 3 going at it. Jann Lee then delivers a MASSIVE kick sending Jacky over the wrought iron fence that has been erected as a guard, a scream can be heard and grows silent....until a crash and a car horn is heard. Spider-man begins to walk around Jann Lee....slowly, and stalkingly.] Spider-Man: You know that this kind of fighting is illegal. Jann Lee(Dubbed): Yeah, but he was asking for it. Spider-Man: I am a witness and I can haul your ass in for it. Jann Lee(Dubbed): Take me if you can! [Jann Lee assumes a fighting stance, Spider-Man pulls out the lightsaber and ignites it. The saber glows a deep violet hue, and hums with power. Jann Lee and Spider-Man enter a staredown. Spider-Man tries to use his web, but Jann Lee jumps out of the way and delivers a flying kick right to Spider-Man.] GAHHH! WHAT THE FUCK KIND OF DREAM WAS THAT?! [We now see Spider awakened from his dream, in bed...in a cold sweat.] Spider: Man...I am fucked up. How many time do I have to tell myself...no more thinking about my match, while eating Taco Bell's chili cheese burritos. Yet I am fighting an on again-off again rival. Kensuke. What's gotten into his mind? What's gotten into my mind? Hell, how should I know. All that matters, is that he is my toughest opponent here. After I beat him...I am well on my way to becoming qualified. I make my own rules, and I have to live by them. It's my job....it's my gift...and my curse... [Spider leaves the scene...but shortly returns] Spider: I know it's overdone, so sue me! [He leaves again and we fade to black] View Biography
