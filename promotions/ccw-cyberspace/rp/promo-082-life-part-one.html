Life {Part One} Dan Logan CCW Promo #82 Date: 05.03.03 Location: Grass Verge {Number 1} Black nights and hidden paths lead the way into the unknown adventure of life. The sands of time eat away at the stones that we put in place to protect us from the storm outside, yet nothing seems to hide us from ourselves. We can hide behind a blanket of false truths all our lives or take that one chance to actually make something of it all. Which one we choose is our own choice and one that must always be wisely made. The harsh reality of the morning due hits upon Dan Logan's face as he groggily awakes upon the lush green grass outside the hired bed-sit he and his ex-wife had shared two nights prior. After a heavy nights drinking he had returned only to find the room taken by another couple with a failing marriage and hopes to rekindle a long lost flame of love. Dan had spent most of his night in the local bars, binge drinking until his body could no longer take the liquid he was throwing down his throat and began slowly to throw it back out at him, leaving the sickly after taste lingering within his mouth. Even now, he can still taste the mixture of sick and blood he began to spit out after being hit in the face by another drunkard accusing Dan of being the reason his son had committed suicide. Dan loathed the drink, but it is all now that keeps him going. His wife has left him and refused him access to his child...that poor kid is set to grow up thinking that a bald headed moron named Simon was his dad and there is nothing that Dan can do to stop it. Slowly, he turns, attempting to regain his composure enough to be able to rise to his feet without loosing his balance and falling back to the ground. Turning to the side his long black hair falls into his ebony eyes and covers the horrendous scar telling us of his childhood years. The nauseating feeling returns almost as soon as he turns onto all fours and quickly he begins to let the lumps of yesterdays meals eject from his body and engulf the once tidy grass with the putrid smell of human interior. Looking about the world still spins and reluctantly Dan drops face first back into the fresh vomit. Turning his face he closes his eyes as a small woman with green shoes begins to walk over to him looking quite concerned. 'Go away!' but they won't leave, the three shaded figures continue to advance upon him and crying he begs for mercy. They slowly advance as Dan screams louder and louder, until soon he cannot shout no more and all falls silent as still they advance. Only quicker now as he attempts to move away from them and into safety. He turns his head and sees an opening, running forward he attempts to reach it before they can reach him...the three shadows are gaining now and soon he shall not be able to escape them. A sudden flash He stands within a meadow beside his son and ex-wife, the three of them are laughing and yet Dan cannot seem to remember why they laugh...was this just another part of his life that he could not remember because of the drink? No, he would remember the important parts...surely 'No, I want to leave' There is something wrong and he knows it, but what it cannot be said. For the images keep on changing but not yet is he dead. A voice from far away is calling to him but he cannot seem to make it out... While he lays upon the grass his face covered in vomit there are still those who care enough to make sure that he does not die, just yet. For you see as Dan dozed off into the sleep that could very well become his last ever he saw the woman of his dreams and his only true love. Dru Cartel lives two miles away from the small bed-sit Emma and Dan had booked out and Dan had phoned her only three days ago to come collect him from said location if she had not heard from him within the allotted amount of days. Luckily for Dan today he had just gone a day over and Dru had begun to shake him back into life calling out his name in an attempt to create life where it had already left the body. Slowly Dan opens his eyes once more and looking over to Dru he smiles gratefully. One day she wouldn't be there and on that day he might be in a hell of a lot of trouble... 'Thanks Dru' Are the only words he speaks as she lifts him up onto her shoulders and carries him toward her yellow BMW Z3... ~ End ~ View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
