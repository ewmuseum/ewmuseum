Spider - Does the President of CCW get Hockey Tickets?

What this is A Match RP in which Spider, CCW’s president, is turned away from an Air Canada Centre hockey game with counterfeit tickets and rallies a crowd.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Spider Entitled: Does the President of CCW get Hockey Tickets? Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 20:56:15 Location: The Air Canada Centre in Toronto This is a: Match RP [We begin in Toronto tonight, a slightly chilly night, and the area is packed with cars as there is Game 5 of the playoffs tonight. We see Spider now in a line-up waiting to get into the ACC for the game tonight. Spider of course is decked out in Leaf paraphenalia, and with ticket in hand. He gets to the turnstile, and that's where we pick up the audio.] Spider: GO LEAFS GO BABY! Usher: Uh, sir....this ticket is counterfeit. Spider: What? You're shitting me right? Usher: Uh, no sir, this ticket has a Montreal Canadiens logo as the anti-counterfeit measure...you've been duped sir, I apologize. Spider: I can't believe I trusted John Jupiter when he sold me these things. Man: JOHN JUPITER SUCKS! Spider: DAMN RIGHT! THE BITCH SOLD ME THESE TICKETS AND THEY AREN'T EVEN REAL! Usher: Sir, I need you to move. Spider: Go fuck yourself. If John Jupiter is in there...I ain't waiting until Sunday to kick his and his team's ass. Usher: Don't make me call security. Spider: Oooh. Mr. 'I'm-Gonna-call-Security'. Dude, go suck a dick. Fan: FUCK YEAH! LET'S BARGE THROUGH THIS BASTARD! [The crowd is now Pro;Spider-In-The-Building. He leaves the line and sets up infront of the camera.] Spider: Ok. It seems I get another chance to get the World Heavyweight Title. I don't have much time to talk about it because I'm gonna push through the Usher and Security to get to my seat or box depending on what the crowd wants. So, I'm just gonna go through quickly my opponents. Crowd: CCDUB! CCDUB! CCDUB! Spider: Koriann...the Queen of Jackass. She belongs on a CKY Video. She is so damn stupid, she got hit by a cup and told the police that she got mugged! I'll doubt she even makes it past us. Cianan...Fuck, do I even need to go into details? This guy has been a pain in my ass before as a wrestler, and as head of the ring crew union. I finally now get a measure of Revenge against him. I personally hope he does get his union to strike...then I won't have to see his face on a regular basis in the offices. John Jupiter...Well....guys... Crowd: YOU SUCK DICK! YOU SUCK DICK! YOU SUCK DICK! YOU SUCK DICK! Spider: Thank you. Yama Tatsu...Hey, I ain't got anything against him...Good Talent and I can see him winning the tournament...IN A DRAGON'S EYE! I can't see you being champ as much as I can see Margaret Thatcher Strapping on a dildo! And Finally, the Red Headed Step Child...One word...Run...and for us...CHARGE! [Spider and the crowd charge the door. Bowling over the usher and with the CCDUB chant resounding throughout the ACC we fade to black.] View Biography
