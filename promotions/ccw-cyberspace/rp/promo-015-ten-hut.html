TEN HUT~! Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens CCW Promo #15 Date: 2/17/03 Location: Parade Deck. 'ALRIGHT MAGGOTS! ATTTTENTION!' Monday afternoon 1:00PM partly cloudy. Michael Stevens stands chewing on an unlit cigar, he wears army fatigues with the sleeves ripped off, and saftey pins holding the various tears together, a cast graces his left forearm. Directly in front of him, standing at something vaugely resembling attention are 'the maggots' Curiously Bruce, Sheena Ramone, and Jimmie the 'Tard. Each wears some parody of fatigues, each more bizare than the last. Bruce's hot pink, with purple lacy frills, Sheena wearing a dress uniform skirt, slit all the way up the side, with a blouse cut off just below the breast and only buttoned across her melons grenades hang from her ears, and finally Jimmie....actually forget Jimmie, it's just too disturbing. 'Listen up recruits, you're about to embark on a very dangerous mission. It's my job to whip you into shape so you can serve in my beloved Corps!' 'I thought this was the Spitfire Army..' 'RECRUIT BRUCE! SHUT THE FUCK UP!' 'SIR YES HONEY ASS!' 'GODDAMNIT! Don't ask don't tell remember? You wanna get sued?' 'Whoops, sorry boss.' 'That's better...Now, lookit this. Yama Tatsu, that faggot...' 'You never told me he was a faggot boss! YES!' With this exclamation from Bruce, Stevens rushes up to him, obstensibly to yell in his face. However, due to Bruce's incredibly small stature that doesn't work out properly. Stevens stands for a moment towering over the miniscule man-lover, before shaking his head in disgust and dropping down to his knees so he's roughly on eye level with the Midget recruit. 'BRUCE! WIPE THAT STUPID LOOKING GRIN OFF YOUR FACE! YOU HAD BEST STRAIGTEN YOUR ASS AND START SHITTING ME TIFFANY CUFF LINKS OR I WILL DEFINATELY FUCK YOU UP!' 'Promise?' 'Oh christ. Nevermind.' Stevens stands looking slightly dejected, beaten down by the incompetence of his lackey's. He trudges back to his position in front of the line, sighs deeply, before suddenly spinning around and regaining the demeanor of a third rate drill Sargent. 'Your mission is simple. You'll infiltrate the home of one Yama Tatsu. I won't lie to you men...' 'HEY! I'm not a man! I could use a few good men though.' 'Yeah! I could too.' 'SHEENA BRUCE! Both of you, drop and give me fifty!' Clearly, this is not a phrase you want to speak to a gay midget and a nympho. However, the damage has now been done, and Bruce and Sheena 'drop'. Sheena goes down to her hands and knees, and crawls over to 'sarge', whilst Bruce just plops down to his ass and pulls out a fifty dollar bill, thankfully he didn't get the same idea as Sheena. She arrives at Stevens before he has a chance to say anything and begins unzipping him. 'Sheena, what the fuck are you doing?' 'Finally some training I enjoy, I'll give you fifty licks alright' 'Um, eh, not really what I meant but...AT EASE MEN! Bruce Jimmie EYES DOWN! AND BRUCE STOP LICKING YOUR LIPS FOR GOD'S SAKES!' 'That make Jimmie happy in pants' Sometime later, after all the orally activity, we're treated to a montage. We prolly woulda seen the whole training activities, but we're running short of time, and hence we've got to see a montage, filled with the sites of training. Such as Bruce climbing the rope, again, and again . Like Sheena doing many many jumping jacks, like Jimmie hitting himself in the head with a chair. All of the montage of course set to the training song of all training songs, 'Superman' by Goldfinger. After we're treated to the montage we once again return to the parade deck, where the recruits are lined up again, looking exhausted and covered in sweat. Stevens however looks fine and refreshed, still chewing on his unlit cigar he holds a pina colada in his hand. 'You make me sick. How can you be exhausted you were only training for three hours! You're pathetic worthless wretches, but you're all I've got, so I guess you'll have to do.' 'Hey Boss, how come you didn't do any of the excersises with us?' 'Quiet Bruce.' 'Now listen, I'm going to tell you a little bit about your partners and their specialities. Jimmie is of course a master of Tard-Fu, show em Jimmie.' 'WEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!' Jimmie jumps out of line and beings running through his horrible impressions of everything he's seen in bad karate movies, before hitting himself in the head with his trusty chair, and wetting himself. 'Right, um Bruce's specialty is Gay Midget Kun Do, but really I don't think we need to see any of that.' Bruce, who at the mention of his name and specialty had gotten a huge grin on his face, but upon hearing there would be no demonstration, looks crestfallen, and almost weeps. 'And Sheena, well she's hot, and a slut, and we've already seen what she can do, so no use doing it again.' 'Always ready to serve, service or be serviced sir!' 'Yeah right. Anyway I of course won't be joining you, as that pyshco hit me with a pipe, and I don't wanna risk getting hurt more than I already am, I've got Dread on Thursday remember. Now Yama is a dangerous adversay remember that. The team comes first, and if none of you get out alive, well I'll finally be free.' 'What's that supposed to mean Mikey?' 'Eh forget it Sheena. Now, FALL OUT! OFF YOU GO!' The line breaks apart as Sheena and Bruce try to pull Jimmie to his feet, in vain. Finally they get him up, and begin stumbling off the deck, as Stevens shakes his head sadly, and takes a drink of his pina colada. And with that image we close once again the adventures of Michael Stevens, hero to the masses, and his gang of fools.
