All Or Nothing....

March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All Or Nothing.... 'The Angel of Death' Enigma CCW Promo #93 Date: Location: (The scene begins just 24 hours after Lockdown... We are in what is seemingly a hospital room... The camera pans around the room, it then focuses on Enigma... He lays there in a bed, eyes shut... He has numerous machines hooked up to him, his head is bandaged, as are his ribs... His right arm has a white cast on it... The rooms silence is then broken... A young looking blonde female nurse enters the room... She is checking on Enigma, monitoring his condition.... Enigma's eyes than slowly begin to open... He looks at the nurse, but he cant seem to get his eyes to focus on her as he may be groggy due to medication he's been given...) Enigma: Who the hell are you??? Nurse: My name is Sandra... Obviously as you can see I'm a nurse here... Enigma: How did I end up hear??? Nurse: Well you were thrown from 20 feet in the air onto concrete... Its a miracle how you're even alive... (The nurse pulls out an envelope from her pocket and gives it to Enigma) Nurse: Someone came by and dropped this off for you, they said they were from CCW personnel... Enigma: Probably one of Sylkk's personal bitches... (Enigma opens the envelope... He takes out a the piece of paper inside and he reads it) Enigma: I'm in a three-way Hardcore match next week on LDT... (The nurse looks at Enigma with a completly lost look on her face) Enigma: That means I have been booked for a match next week... Nurse: Match??? You mean you're actually considering fighting again after dropping from 20 feet in the air on-to a concrete floor??? You have a severe concussion, two broken ribs, a broken arm... You're in no condition to even be out of bed... Enigma: Please spare me you're medical advice... I've wrestled hurt almost my whole career... Every wrestler does... No pain, no gain... Nurse: But there is no way you'll be able to get medical clearence to wrestle... Enigma: I need no medical clearence... I do what I want, when I want... And there isnt a damn thing you or any doctor can do about it... I'll crawl into LDT if I have to... There is no way in hell I'm missing my match... Nurse: I'm sorry sir, I just can't let that happen... You're physically to un-stable to (cut-off in mid sentence)...... Enigma: Oh for God's sake shut up... You think I havent heard all this bullshit before... It all means nothing to me... Like I said, I'm gonna be in my match on LDT... One way or another... Nurse: Fine, I'm gonna postition two guards outside you're door... They will use physical force if they must to restrain you... Enigma: Do what you gotta do... (The nurse exits the room... As she does, Enigma gets up out of bed... He disconnets all the machinery which he was hooked up to... He goes into the washroom and shuts the door... About 10 minutes pass and the washroom door opens... Out steps Enigma, changed into his regualar street clothing... Enigma looks out the door window, and there stand two guards... One black male guard, around 6'3 and a solid build... The other guard, is a tanned white male around 6'6... He looks quite intimdating... An evil smirk comes across the face of Enigma... He opens the door to exit the room, and both guards notice...) Guard #1: Sir, I suggest you step back into you're room please... Enigma: (slight laughter) You dare stand before me... Do you realize who I am??? I'm 'The Angel Of Death'... Do you think I got that name for winning baking contests??? Obviously not... So I in fact suggest that the two of you step aside... And if you both do as I say, well than maybe I'll let you both live.... Guard #2: Listen buddy... Enigma: Buddy, I'm not you're buddy... I'm not you're friend... But as another living being on this planet, I am right now at this moment trying to save you're life... It is you're choice if you choose to take my advice... Guard #1: Listen sir, we can avoid all this if you just simply step back into you're room... We have a job to do here... Enigma: I too have a job to do... So I now understand what I must do... (With that said, Enigma kicks the first guard in right in the family jewels leaving the guard gasping for air... The other guard charges at Enigma, but Enigma nails right in the forhead with his cast sending the guard straight to the floor... Enigma runs down the hallway and he goes straight down the staircase.... Now the scene switches to outside the hospital... Enigma jumps into the first taxi he sees... Driving the taxi we see a typcial Indian cab driver with turban and all) Enigma: Get me the hell out of here... Cab Driver: (Strong Indian Accent) Where do you want to go? Enigma: As far away from here as possible... (The cab driver calmy continues driving down the busy streets... Both Enigma and the driver dont say much... Finally the cab driver speaks) Cab Driver: You look very familuar.... Have I met you before somewhere??? Enigma: No I dont think do... Cab Driver: Wait a minute... You are that wrestling man... (Enigma just stares out the window not even paying attention) Cab Driver: Yes wrestling people are very funny people... You people are very crazy... With the chairs... And the tables... Oh yes... I love the wrestling... My children love the wrestling very much... I have seven children... I would love to get you're signature Mr. Wrestling man... Enigma: First off, my name is Enigma... Not 'Mr Wrestling Man'... And second off all what in the hell is that awful smell in this car... It smells like something died in here... Cab Driver: Oh that is my lunch... I just relieved some gas... (Enigma opens the window and he sticks his head out) Enigma: Jesus Christ almighty that smell is in-toxicating... (gagging)... Just drop me off here... (The cab driver pulls to the side of the road and Enigma quickly gets out... Enigma hands the driver his money) Enigma: Here, invest in some air freshner... I didnt think it was possbible for a human being to make such an awful stench... But you proved me wrong... Cab Driver: Thank you Mr.Wrestling man... You are very good man... Very very good man... Enigma: Yeah whatever... (Enigma begins to walk off, and ever so coinsidently Enigma looks up and he notices that he is standing outside the arena where LDT was held... He looks at the building, not saying a word just thinking to himself... He then walks through the arena door... And now the scene switches to the press box... The very same press box which Enigma was thrown out of... Still nothing has been cleaned... Shattered glass is all over the floor... The window still broken... Enigma stands there, just taking this all in, re-living LDT in his mind.... He then begins to speak) Enigma: Blayze, did you really think that was gonna be enough to put me away? Did you actually believe that a 20 foot drop onto concrete was enough to rid me from you're life? Well it wasnt Blayze... I mean dont get me wrong... I'll admit it... You gave me a beating... But you're biggest mistake was not finsishing what you started... See Blayze, you can keep beating me down over and over and over again, but the fact is I'm just gonna keep coming back until I feel satisfied... I wont give up until I feel you've payed for you're wrong doings... Its gonna take more than a broken arm, a few broken ribs and a concussion to keep me down... 'The Angel Of Death' has risen from the ashes once again... And I seek vegance upon the so called 'Black Jesus'... ... Maybe today, maybe, tomorrow, maybe next week... Only time will tell when I will strike... But there is no doubt that I will make you suffer Blayze, and I will make you regret crossing paths with me... All in do time my old friend... Patience is the key... (Enigma lights up a smoke, and he walks over to the window which he was thrown from... He looks out the window, and he just stares down for a while, looking at the very same spot he fell from onto that unforgiving concrete floor... Blood marks still lay on the ground as do pieces of glass...) Enigma: For some odd reason, the powers that be have booked me in a match for LDT knowing full well that I'm injured... Blayze has the CCW front office in his pocket... I dunno what he had to do to get over so well with them, nor do I wanna know for that matter... But none the less I have been booked in a match for LDT... A Three Way Hardcore match involving myself, Yama Tatsu, and newcomer Dan Logan... And of course nobody expects me to even show up to the arena for LDT... But I'll be there... I will compete... And I will walk out of that match victorious... I dont ever back down from a fight... You think a few cuts and bruises are gonna stop me from stepping into the ring... Come on, you people should know me better by now... The only way I'll ever miss a match is if I'm in a coffin buried 6 feet under... (Enigma inhales one last time off his cigarette, and then he flicks the butt out the window as he watches it hit the floor 20 feet below... Enigma then steps away from the window and he looks into the camera) Enigma: On LDT, I'm gonna be rasing some hell... Me stepping into the ring with Logan and Tatsu is gonan be like sending lamb to the slaughter... And its shame... I just hope you boys realize this isnt personal... This is strictly buisness... I mean, I'll enjoy beating the living hell out of the both of you... But I won't enjoy it as much as I would if it were Blayze in the place of you two... Oh well, either way someone's getting there ass handed to them come LDT... We'll just have to wait and see which card fate deals... (Fade To Black) View 'The Angel of Death' Enigma 's Biography Back
