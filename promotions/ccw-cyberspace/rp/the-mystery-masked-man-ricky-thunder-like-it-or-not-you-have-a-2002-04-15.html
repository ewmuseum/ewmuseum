The Mystery Masked Man - Ricky Thunder, like it or not, you have a...

What this is A Match RP set in a hospital in which a dying wrestler asks his visiting friend to protect his younger brother Ricky Thunder.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Mystery Masked Man Entitled: Ricky Thunder, like it or not, you have a partner! Written in Monday, Apr 15, 2002, 06:16:36 Location: This is a: Match RP 23rd September 2000 The rain is falling heavily as a lone man waits by a helipad located high above a bustling city. A fierce gale drives the rain sideways, making the umbrella that he desperately clings to all but useless. He squints into the deluge as the unmistakable sound of an approaching helicopter slowly becomes audible over the roaring of the wind and driving rain. A shiny black helicopter gradually appears in the dark grey sky and moves toward the rooftop landing pad, causing the useless umbrella to blow inside out. The lone man throws it to the ground in disgust as he stoops and moves toward the now landed flying machine. As he approaches, a door opens and two men wearing identical black overcoats emerge into the rain, and walk toward him. One of these men towers over the other. It is to him that the original man speaks. Ahh ..hello sir. I'm Dr. Rogers. Welcome to..... The huge man raises a gloved hand for him to stop Just take me to him. Time is short. Of course sir, of course. Follow me The huge man gives the smallest of nods and the doctor turns and leads them away from the helicopter and towards a door. They pass silently through and down a small flight of stairs that leads to another door, that in turn leads into a busy hospital corridor. Doctors and nurses rush about their business as the trio walk silently on, seemingly oblivious to the people rushing around them. As they continue down the corridor, the doctor begins to talk to the massive, black clad figure. As you know sir, Mr Thunder's condition has deteriorated over the last few weeks. The tumors have spread from his lungs and are now growing all over his body. I know Yes, sir. I'm aware you've been informed but I have to prepare you. The huge man stops dead in the corridor. For what? The doctor stops and turns to face the other two men. He is obviously uncomfortable in their presence. Your friend is a very sick man, as you know. Unfortunately there's nothing more that we can do to treat his cancer...he..he hasn't got long left sir. The tall man just nods silently as the nervous doctor continues. Look, it's just that I have to prepare you for what you're about to see. He's a very sick man... You have no need not worry about me doctor. I understand that sir. It's just that.... well.... you may not even recognise him. The slightest shiver runs through the giant's body. Then we had best hurry The doctor turns and continues leading the two men through the maze of corridors, eventually stopping outside a closed door marked PRIVATE. This is his room sir. The doctor reaches for the door knob but the smaller of the two men stops him. The giant's gloved hand opens the door instead. I shall go in alone Without waiting for a reply he enters the room and closes the door behind him, leaving the other two standing in the corridor. He stops abruptly once he catches sight of the emaciated figure laying still in the bed before him. Tubes and wires connect the sickly figure to a mass of machinery that stands beside his bed. The steady beeping of the EKG is the only sound to be heard. Slowly the massive figure walks to the side of the bed. I have come, old friend. The skeleton laying in the bed stirs and slowly, almost painfully, opens it's eyes. Y... you made it Of course I did. I was worried you wouldn't get here before it's too late The huge visitor places a reassuring hand on his friend's shoulder. Don't worry, you're not going anywhere. A faint smile appears on the sick man's blue lips Man, you've never lied to me before so don't f**king start now OK. I know what's going on. That's why I called you here. He points weakly towards a nearby chair. Sit down. I've got a favour to ask. The visitor drags the chair to his dying friend's bedside and sits down. What is it? You know you can ask me anything I know that dude, but this is a biggy.... It's about my little brother. Ricky? Yeah, Ricky.... The crazy little bastard. What's he done? It's not what he's done, it's what he's gonna do. He wants to keep wrestling. Didn't he hurt his neck in NSWA a few weeks back? Yeah but he's talking about going on. The massive visitor shakes his head silently. Impossible. No-one would insure him, so no-one will hire him. I know but the kids persistent, doesn't know when to quit. A smile briefly appears on the visitor's face. Just like someone else I know, heh? The sick man tries to laugh but is in obvious pain. Look, we both know what the kid's like. I've always looked out for him but I ain't gonna be here much longer. Dude, I'm asking you to look after him for me. Of course I will No, I really mean it. You've gotta promise me you won't let him kill himself. PROMISE ME. The effort of talking is starting to take it's toll. OK, OK. Relax. I promise. The patient has now gone into a sickening coughing fit. His visitor tries in vain to make him more comfortable. Swear it to me. Swear it. I gotta know for sure. Very well... I swear I'll keep him from harm. The pain seems to vanish from the ill man's face. Good...that's what I've been waiting to hear. I knew that of all the people in the world, I could count on you. Suddenly the beeping stops. Flatline. The huge visitor leaps to his feet and reaches for the nurse's buzzer, pushing it furiously. NURSE!!! NURSE!!! He grabs his dying friend and desperately tries to revive him NO! NO!! Not like this.... not you too!!!! Flatline. Life extinguished. NNNNNNNNNNNNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! 11th April 2002 View Biography
