Ding, Ding......Round 2

18 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A limousine promo in which The Supreme Machine, stuck in traffic outside Louisville, issues a warning to Paul Arovnik ahead of Monday night.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Ding, Ding......Round 2 Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 11:22:22 Location: Private Limo This is a: Match RP Times have changed. Now you too must change to suit your new surroundings. I will adapt. You must never lose sight of the ultimate goal. I remain as focused as always. Those that would stop you are many. That is irrelevant They will seek to block your every move. Who are they? You already know. No! I don't! Who are they? You already know. You have to tell me! I need to know. How can I face them if I don't know who they are? They will reveal themselves soon enough. How will I know who they are? You will know them. Will I defeat them. No answer..... Will I defeat them? Still no answer...... Will I defeat them? It is your destiny. Gridlock. Horns blast and people yell as tensions fray under the burning sun. But still they go nowhere. The sea of motionless automobiles stretches in either direction for as far as the eye can see. Hundreds, perhaps thousands of people trapped in their cars, unable to escape their infuriating predicament. For some, the experience is made all the worse by the lack of air conditioning to shield them from the oppressive heat while others can at least endure the monotony of there situation in relative comfort. For one person though, the whole experience goes totally unnoticed. Yes, that's right. Louisville. Freedom hall. A shining black limousine sits parked in the massive traffic jam just like the rest of the cars. Behind the tinted windows, oblivious to the outside world, The Supreme Machine sits relaxing on one of the luxurious leather seats, dressed in black track pants and a black t-shirt. His long black hair hangs loose over his face and a pair of dark sunglasses cover his eyes. He is looking at a small television screen on the opposite wall as he talks into the bright red telephone that fits into the wall beside him. I don't know the address. Just look in the telephone book or something. He flicks channels with a remote control as he listens to the person on the other end of the line. He stops on a channel showing a promo for the PTC Night of Champions PPV. OK. So that's sorted then? Fine. OK. And someone will deliver that to my locker room on Monday night? Excellent. Thank you doctor. Yes. You too. OK. Bye. He hangs up the telephone and sits watching the television with a contented smile on his face. The PPV promo finishes so he goes back to channel surfing until he comes upon some Thai boxing live from Bangkok. He sips on a PowerAde as the Thai crowd cheer for their favorite, willing him on to victory. The incredible heat and the never ending traffic jam outside may as well be another world away as The Supreme Machine sits relaxing in comfort inside his private limousine. His attention is drawn closer to the television when the Thai boxing pauses for a commercial and it is an ad for CCW's Shocking Monday Night. He watches intently as the announcer runs through the card while brief promos for John Coens, Kensuke Suzahara and Guardian flash across the screen. When it comes time to mention the main event, the scene cuts to Spider revealing the pools for COTR at last weeks SMN. Spider reveals the name of "PA" Paul Arovnik as the mystery entrant in Pool H. The announcer on the TV goes on about the return of Arovnik in this weeks main event. The smile is replaced by a smirk as The Supreme Machine changes the channel then throws the remote angrily to the limo floor. So Spider thinks he's so clever bringing back this Paul Arovnik guy and throwing him into my pool in COTR. Well that's just fine with me. It doesn't matter to me if Spider has put this guy in as part of some little scheme he's got planned in order to take me out because it just isn't going to happen. I've heard the rumors going around backstage about how the draw might seem to favor certain wrestlers but none of it matters. There's only one wrestler in CCW that is going to win this year's COTR, and that's me. He takes another sip of PowerAde and reaches down to pick up the remote control. He flicks through a few channels before coming back to the Thai boxing. He watches for a few minutes before they again cut to a commercial for SMN. It is the same one as before. He shuts off the TV and throws the remote back to the floor. "PA" Paul Arovnik. Passive Aggressive. Back in CCW and looking to pick up where you left off. Well, that's what I hear backstage anyway. So what is it little man? Are you here as part of some grand scheme of Spider's or are you your own man? Not that it really matters either way because you're not going to be stopping me from winning this tournament no matter what your answer is. You see, Arovnik, on Monday night I'll be welcoming you back to CCW. It's time you were introduced to the CCW living legend seeing as I hear all this babble backstage about how fine a champion you were yourself. The Supreme Machine removes his sunglasses and lays them down on the seat next to him. He wipes his hair from his face as he takes another sip of PowerAde. I couldn't care less what you did last time you were in CCW Arovnik. You might have been a big shot last time you were here but I'll let you in on a little secret.... that's only because I wasn't here to kick your ass! I've heard that you're a bit of a psycho Arovnik so I doubt it's any good giving you advanced warning but I'm feeling generous seeing as it's your first match back and all so I'll throw a little bit of advise your way anyhow. Don't show up on Monday night Arovnik. I mean it. If you've got any sense in that screwed up head of yours you'll stay the hell out of my business. Step into that ring on Monday night Arovnik, and you're stepping into my business. You're stepping between me and the CCW Heavyweight Title. Your stepping into deep trouble. He glances out of the window beside him as the driver in the car next to them blows his horn in frustration. He turns back and runs his fingers through his hair as he stretches his legs. He cracks his neck from side to side before taking another drink of PowerAde. I don't care how good you think you are, I'll stop at nothing to get that title back around my waist where it belongs and I don't care who I have to walk over to get it. Did you see what happened to Gobstopper last week on LDT? That was meant as a warning to you Arovnik. You and anyone else who thinks they can stand between me and what I want. Like I've said before, I realize now that the way to get anything in the new CCW is to just go out and take it, do whatever it takes. If whatever it takes means that I have to resort to extreme measures to get the win then so be it. No more Mr. nice guy. The Supreme Machine finishes his drink and glances down at his watch. He reaches out and pushes a button on the intercom. How long until we're in Louisville? The chauffeur's voice crackles back from the driver's seat. I'm not real sure sir. We're having a bit of a delay with the traffic. OK. Just let me know when we're getting close. I've got a few more phone calls to make. Yes sir. He sits back in the leather chair and gazes blankly out the tinted windows. Don't be in that ring when I get there Arovnik. You've been warned. I don't care how psycho your are, if you get hit hard enough and often enough you go down just like the rest of them. Step into that ring Arovnik and I promise you that you will go down. I'm going to win the CCW Heavyweight title for the sixth time and to do that I have to beat you. And I will beat you. Prepare yourself PA. You may be good but I'm better and that's not just me making idle boasts. No, no, no. That my friend is a cold hard fact. A cold hard fact that will be proven on Monday night when you're laying flat on your back in the middle of the ring and I'm standing there over you, stomping and stomping away at your bloodied carcass. It's a fact that will be proven when I'm crowned CCW Heavyweight Champion while you're cowering in the corner of some padded cell in whatever lunatic asylum you came from, bound up it a straight jacket to stop you from ending your pathetic life. I'm The Supreme Machine Paul Arovnik. I know you've damned well heard of me. Monday night you get to find out, first hand, what it's like to meet a legend. Monday night you find out why Phil Burner went running straight back out of CCW after only one match. Monday night you find out why Neurosis gave away the title rather than face me in the ring. Monday night you get to feel what Mr. Gobstopper felt at LDT last week. Monday night I'm going to tear you apart Paul Arovnik. Monday night I'm taking one more step towards the gold. I'm The Supreme Machine Paul Arovnik. Welcome back to the big time. View Biography
