Backstabber (pt. 4)

3 March 2003

Backstabber (pt. 4) Scorpion CCW Promo #50 Date: 3-3-03 Location: Donald Trump's private jet :::: Scorpion and Mike walk up the stairs into the private jet owned by New York Billionaire, Donald Trump. Once inside the sight of a mini bar, television, recliners and computers open up the eyes of excitement upon Scorpion and Mike’s face. :::: Donald Trump: Gentlemen, don’t be afraid…welcome to paradise all on one airplane! My personal private jet opened up to you, my friends. Scorpion (in awe): Thank you so much Mr. Trump you have no idea how much this means to us. We never thought we would get out of New York City in time. This whole Las Vegas thing just came up and we really appreciate you helping us out like this. Donald Trump: Oh don’t worry Scorpion it’s my pleasure. Just sit, relax and enjoy a couple of martinis’ in the mini bar they are absolutely breath taking. Scorpion: Will do sir. :::: Scorpion and Mike grab a martini from the mini-bar and take a seat next to Donald Trump. The television is playing some local news when a story about Drake comes up. :::: News reporter from TV: This past week an alleged gang member, Drake Rizzel was shot dead by the police after robbing a local grocery store. A 911 caller tipped off the police where Mr. Rizzel was heading. No word yet on who made that 911 call. :::: Scorpion and Mike at each with fire burning in their eyes. :::: Donald Trump: I can’t believe the NYPD would shoot the guy dead. Usually they just handcuff them and put them on parole. Mike: What are you imply Mr. Trump? Donald Trump: I’m implying that is sounds like there could have been a dirty cop. Scorpion: A dirty cop? What is a dirty cop? Donald Trump: A dirty cop is someone who is put up by a gang to take out someone. That usually claim that the shot was a mistake or the victim had a gun. Mike: How do you know so much about this stuff? Donald Trump: I frequently donate money to certain NYPD offices to help them re-establish a good facility and they tell me about cops who are not truthful with their line of work. Mike: That is such bullshit, they are there to protect not kill. Donald Trump: Anyone will do anything for the power of a dollar. :::: The plane begins taking off as everyone gets in their seats. After five minutes the plane stabilizes out and everyone is back talking. :::: Donald Trump: Excuse me gentlemen I have to make a quick phone call. Scorpion: Ok. :::: Donald Trump gets up and walks into a private room with a bed inside. He shuts the door and locks it. He takes out his cell phone and begins to make his phone call. :::: Donald Trump: Corky it’s me. I got the two guys you were looking for we are on our way to Vegas now. Corky: Good…do they expect anything? Donald Trump: Nope they are absolutely clueless. Corky: Good…good our plan is in full force. I can’t wait to see Scorpion’s face when comes off the airplane and realizes he was had. Donald Trump: Corky they don’t see it coming. They think I’m on my way to be a guest at the opening of your casino tomorrow. Corky: (Laughs) Scorpion has always been oblivious to the happenings around him. He will soon find out he cannot mess with Corky Stewart or he shall pay just like Drake did. Donald Trump: (Laughs) So true my friend, so true. Corky: All right we shall see you in about an hour and a half. We will be waiting on the runway. Donald Trump: All right Corky see you then. :::: Back in the other room Scorpion and Mike are oblivious that Donald Trump is on Corky’s side. :::: Mike: I still can’t believe I’m riding on Donald Trump’s jet on our way to Las Vegas to break open Corky’s skull for what he did to Drake. Scorpion: I can’t believe it either. We have amazing luck. Corky will be very surprised that we made it Las Vegas. Mike: Do you think there were any dirty cops when Drake was shot? Scorpion: I’m beginning to think so Mike. I know the NYPD would never shoot a guy who was an alleged robber for no reason. Drake didn’t even have a gun in his hand that day he was shot and I tried to stop him. All he had was a knife, that’s all he had and the police would never ever shoot a guy for having a knife unless they felt threatened. Mike: Yeah and the people that witnessed the shooting said he walked out of a alleyway where the cops were waiting for him and two seconds later he was shot dead. Scorpion: Yeah something is definitely going on that I don’t like at all. The only way we can find out is if we get to Corky. :::: Donald Trump walks back into the room sitting down next to Scorpion and Mike. :::: Donald Trump: Well boys you going to do any gambling this weekend? Mike: Maybe a little bit, I still want to come back with some sort of money. Donald Trump: Yeah I feel your pain, if I play too much, I lose too much. Scorpion: (Laughs) I agree with you there. Donald Trump: You don’t mind if I go to sleep my bedroom on the plane do you? Scorpion: No we don’t mind. Donald Trump: Good cause I have been up the past two nights trying to work on some new plans for a hotel. Scorpion: That sucks. Donald Trump: Well it brings the money in and the money is always rewarding. Mike: So true, so true. Donald Trump: All right guys I’ll see you near the end of the flight. Scorpion: Ok, later. ::::: Donald Trump walks out of the room. :::: Scorpion: Mike I got a funny feeling something bad is going on? Mike: What do you mean? Scorpion: I don’t know, I just feel like something is up. Mike: Jesus, Scorpion we are on our way to Las Vegas on Donald Trump’s private plane just an hour or less away from ringing the neck of that bastard Corky. Scorpion: Yeah you’re right, I could use some sleep too. Mike: Same here bro and just think by the time we wake up we will be in Las Vegas…the gambling capital of the world and we will be kicking the shit out of Corky. Scorpion: Now I know I’m going to have a good sleep. All right I’m getting tired. Mike: Same here, just think good thoughts cause we know we will get revenge soon enough. Scorpion: You dam right.
