Michael McTavish CCW Promo #53 Date: 3-3-3 Location: A Pre-Show Interview .::The Scene Open's in the Back with Michel McTavish Sitting in his room waiting for the Coach to come into his room. Michel is just about to fall asleep and he hears a knock on his door making him jump a bit. He hops up and walks out and the coach stands there and Then he begins to talk.!.::. .::Coach::.~Well, Michel McTavish. You have come to CCW for what reason, what are you're goals or Purposes here in CCW.?.::. .::Michel McTavish::.~Whoa Coach WHat Kind of question is thaT.?. I mean why in they blue hell do you think I came to CCW.?.::. .::Coach::.~That's What Im trying to ask you. .::Michel McTavish::.~ Well, Like any normal Person I have come here to win the title's and do anything I want to with noone to stop me.!.::. .::Coach::.~ Hm, well I guess that is the goal of Most starts..Well what do you thikn about some of they other starts in CCW..I mean some of our Better..Like Gobber., Ace, Syllk, or Blaze.... .::Michel McTavish::.~ Well, Gobber is Nothing but a Jobber.. Ace is alright But I could take him. Syllk Well I dont even really know him..So I cant say anything about him. Blaze Hm, Our Champion Well in a Few Weeks to a Few Monthes. I will rise and beat him.!. .::Coach::.~Well Hm,. I think. .::Michel McTavish::.~Whoa, Whoa, Whoa, Whoa.. I dont Care wht you think Man.!. Do You have anymore Questions Or do you not.?!?. .::Coach::.~Well, I was just going to say... .::He Quickly Snaps Back at Him::. .::Michel McTavish::.~No I dont Care anymore questions Or I amGoing to Leave....Hurry the Hell up.. .::Coach::.~Ok Ok, Well It doesnt seem you hav any match for this week. .::Michel McTavish is looking in they Air and Looks at him::. .::Michel McTavish::.~Yeah Obviously... .::Coach::.~YES!. I know!. But I was going to ask if you could have any match next week on youre Debut. What Match would you want to have and Why.?. .::Michel McTavish::.~Well, I would go for the Worlds Hevyweight Title. I would Take on Blaze Beat him and I Would Be the World Champ.. Why You ask. WHy else Because i am that Damn good..Were Have I heard that Before.? Oh Well Come back when You dont wanna waste my time.. .::Then Michel Mushes the Coach back and he walks back into his Room and Lays Down on his Couch as the Fan blows on his Face as the Scene Does Black. and CCW Flashes across the Screen.!.
