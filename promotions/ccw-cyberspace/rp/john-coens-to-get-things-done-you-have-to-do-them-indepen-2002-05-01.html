John Coens - To get things done ... you have to do them ... indepen...

What this is A Match RP written as a list of the thoughts of John Coens ahead of his title defence against Ricky Thunder.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main John Coens Entitled: To get things done ... you have to do them ... independently Written in Wednesday, May 1, 2002, 02:23:14 Location: In his mind ... This is a: Match RP ***A before note on this roleplay ... you won't find any descriptive text or anything like that in this. It's a roleplay that is comprised of John's thoughts. Just a bunch of thoughts. Gotta try something new every now and then, right? It will not be cued by the preemptive 'John:' Rather ... it will be in a point form style, as a way to show how he jumps around in his mind, as we all do. *** - Goddamn I'm tired ... - I shouldn't have gone out for those beers ... - Oh! Where'd I put my wallet. - Nevermind, there it is. - I can't believe the rack on that one chick. - It's a good thing Savvy can't read my mind. If she knew about all the chicks that I check out when I go out ... who knows how she'd react. - Then again, I only check them out ... it's not like I make an effort towards them. Savvy is the one I want. - Damn my ribs are sore. I can't believe I took that beating on Sunday. - I can't believe Spider would rip me off for that belt! I had it! He fucking stole it from me. - Maybe I shouldn't have that belt just yet though ... - No way I shouldn't get that belt. I fought all the way from the beginning for that belt. Neurosis didn't even make it all the way. I got my ass beat down hard ... - Now Ricky wants my gold. - He still thinks SuMa is a force to be reckoned with ... yeah ... right. - The Machine. The Supreme Machine tapped out to me. - Fuckin kid. SuMa is a legend returned ... I am a legend who never left. They tell stories about me to rookies. The rookies think that there's no way this guy could still be wrestling ... to be held in such reverence. Obviously Ricky wasn't listening. He still wants a piece. - What kinda match is it again? - just a regular match? - for a BELT?!?! - are they stupid? - damn am I ever hungry ... what is there to eat in this damn house. - a REGULAR MATCH? - for MY BELT!?!? - it is my belt ... I should get to make some of the rules. - I made the Pay Per View what it was ... ratings up to the top of the tits ... and I made em that high. - respect ... - they need to give me more respect. - disappoints me to see someone of such caliber and prowess as myself to be punished like this. - you put your feds pay per view show, on just about every single highlight reel known to man ... and you don't get main event. - they give it to some ... no namers to squabble over a title that has less meaning than the women's title. - that's something that shouldn't happen ... women in wrestling ... Koriann and the world title? Should NEVER have happened ... the World Title needs to be cleansed with a real champion. - she needs to take off her shirt. - can't believe how dirty that Campbell chick in OSW is. Some of the stuff she wanted to do when I was there for Head on Collision ... I can't say that I blame Drew ... it's tempting. - never been licked there before ... - where's my tylenol 3's? I thought I threw them in my bag here? - I smell like shit right now ... I need to take a shower ... - it's not too late to book a flight to Philly for the show, is it? - nah ... it's open 24-7. I want to be on the first flight out of that dive though after the show. I don't want to get stuck there any longer than I have to be. - Ricky Thunder ... trying to get at my gold. Everyone wants a piece of me ... SuMa, his little toy Ricky. - I wonder if Ricky knows that SuMa is only playing him. SuMa can't possibly respect Ricky. His brother yes ... but not Ricky. - It's like ... he's living in the shadow of his brother ... - turkey is good - is there any mayo? - his brother was good ... granted ... but he just about died beating SuMa ... I just about had to stop myself from pulling back on his head even harder! - palm of my hand ... that's where SuMa was. - Ricky'll be there too. 'Less he cheats. Can't let that happen. Guess I'll have to cheat first. - I'm a damn God! That's what I am. - not a goddamn ... a Damn God. - would you just look at that sandwich? no mortal could have made something so beautiful. - what kind of a man does he think he is? I'm not just some run of the mill ... I've been around for awhile ... I'm great type of people. - Ricky Coyote's been around and back. Has he ever won on a televised show? - I'm a goddamn legend. I am the first ever Universal Champion. I've been with the best, and beaten them. No one else could take the crown off Inman. I did. - Arovnik! I beat Arovnik three or four times before he could even touch me. I had him working for me when I was Big Brother! - Thinks he can beat me ... - heh ... that was a funny line ... What, you didn't think I'd know what a eugugoly was? Ben Stiller is a comedic genius. - There was something about her when she was sandwiched between those finnish dwarves ... - mmm ... turkey sandwich. - I need another beer. - I feel sorry for Ricky though ... he wants to fight this next match for his brother ... - I wonder what it'll be like to disappoint someone as much as he will.
