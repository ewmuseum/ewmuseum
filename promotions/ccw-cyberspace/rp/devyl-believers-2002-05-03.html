Devyl - Believers

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Devyl and David Oakes meet in a church and discuss Gabriel Nyte and Lacey Skye ahead of a rematch.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Devyl Entitled: Believers Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 20:14:20 Location: A church in Pittsburgh This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on one of the many churches on Pittsburgh. This particular one is very old, the stonework dating back to the city's conception. Inside, the flickering light of strategically-placed candles offer the only illumination in the otherwise darkened church. David Oakes stands towards the front of the church, near the altar. He has a far-away look in his eyes, almost as if he's contemplating some sort of deep secret or hidden pain. Devyl comes walking towards him from the back of the church, not bothering to disguise her presence. Her heels echo around the church, playing off the excellent acoustics. David speaks as his wife nears him.] David: 'This place...' *long pause* 'Is a mockery of all that is true.' Devyl: 'Yes, it is. What are you doing here? Aren't you going to be joining Raymond, Outlaw and myself for our strategy session tonight?' David: 'I'll be there. I just...' [David trails off and Devyl looks at him curiously. David once again has the far-away look in his eyes. She recognizes that he's working over something in his head, and stays silent until he can voice whatever it might be. And before long...] David: 'I felt compelled to come here. Almost as if something was drawing me... And once I arrived...' *short pause* 'This place disgusts me!' Devyl: *softly and with real compassion* 'What happened to you, David? I know you went to hell...' David: 'Ha! I actually have to thank Gabriel Nyte for that! I have to thank him for a lot actually... He opened my eyes and showed me exactly what this entire world is about! Do you know what his 'loving' God made him do to me? He made Gabriel crucify me! And that wasn't enough for the God of Love... Oh no... Gabriel's God sent me straight down into the burning pits of hell... And then... On the third day... I was ripped out of that stinking sulphur pit and brought back to this world! And that was their one mistake... Satan and I... We made a deal... And now, just like you, my lovely Victoria... I am one with the darkness. I am here to do Satan's work, and his alone. And nothing shall stand in hell's way...' Devyl: 'Gabriel has definitely discovered that, David. Now that Yama Tatsu is with us, Gabriel's days are definitely numbered. Yama has a deep-seeded hatred for Gabriel... A hatred that benefits us all greatly.' David: 'If Yama Tatsu doesn't get him, I will! I plan on haunting Gabriel Nyte to the end of his days on this earth. And no matter how hard he tries, he won't be able to kill me again!' Devyl: 'The situation with Gabriel is well in hand. It is Lacey Skye Morris who is the concern at this point in time.' David: *sighs* 'I'm sorry about what happened on Lockdown...' Devyl: 'It wasn't your fault, David. Deborah Carmichael and Referee Terry Bogard are fully at fault for what happened. And they will both suffer for their actions in due time. However, I must regain the title first.' David: 'Well then... Let's go. I'm sure you have a lot you need to discuss with Raymond and Outlaw.' Devyl: *smiles* 'Yes... I do.' [Devyl and David both turn to leave the church. Once he gets to the back, David turns and looks back at the statue of the crucified Christ that's hanging above the altar. And as David's eyes settle on the statue, it starts to bleed out of it's eyes, nose, ears and mouth. David smiles in appreciation and follows his wife out into the night...] [fade out...] View Biography
