One Man Team!

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A verse and scripted promo in which Yama Tatsu trains under his Master and says he may enter an upcoming tag match at Lockdown without a partner.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Yama Tatsu Entitled: One man team! Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 03:34:07 Location: Your Mummas House This is a: Match RP As Armies march forward, One man stands still… As the train rushes forward, The stone stays stationary… As thousands of bullets fall from the sky like rain, One man… One man stays to get wet! Ricky Thunder says weighs up Yama Tatsu as equivalent to a stick, Well a stick can be shaped into mighty spear, A stick is not just a dead branch that has fallen from the group, But it is a single spirit that wishes to walk alone… The Thunder from Down under, You better watch out for the Beast from the East, Cause you sound like your full of hot air… Or some kind of air at least ;) Yama Tatsu is an unforgiving warrior, Who has his sights firmly set on being the best this fed has to offer! He has already marked his place well here in CCW history, But his resume can only grow, And grow it must… Grow it will… The Dragon’s flame burns bright, Just like Yama Tatsu’s CCW future… The Dragon has spoken……. ** The scene opens a little South of the border. Yama Tatsu races along a Mexican highway. His racy, new Harley burning past the surroundings as he approaches the camera. As quickly as he approached it took less than half that time to fly past. That section of film is replayed several times. Each time it winds down, getting slower and slower. In a blink of an eye the image snaps back to real time just in time to see Yama Tatsu disappear off down the lonely, runway type strip of tarseal. ** ** We return to sanity now. A wrestling ring, but not just any wrestling ring. Two men compete in a mock battle. One the formidable Yama Tatsu and the other an unknown, small-time Trainer. Yama Tatsu just charges forward and performs a vicious spear tackle on the trainer just as we fade in. The wise yet evil Master stands at ringside barking orders at his warrior like a little Fox Terrier. ** Master: Come on Tachi! ** Yama Tatsu quickly returns to his feet and lets rip a mighty roar. His pupil’s, as again seen recently, not there. They are just clouded over with white/grey haze. ** Master: Fight it Tachi! Even out the control… ** Yama Tatsu lets another wild scream out. The enraged voice floods the large room. When his eyes reopen from the shrieking cry the pupils seem to reappear… slowly but surely they are returning. But wait they aren’t hi normal light brown… no! They are a fiery Reddy Orange colour. A terrible colour, which would certain strike fear into the heart of any man. ** Master: You’ve done it Tachi! Hold your power! Remember this feeling it, is your true destiny! It is the way to your future. ** The Trainer looks scared shitless. He tries to make a run for it, but he stumbles in terror. He quickly grasps the Trainer and pulls him to his feet. He instantly hits the “Plague of the Dragon”. Yama, still with the evil red glow in his eyes, ascends to the top rope and hits a 450 Splash to the Trainers back. Yama Tatsu then immediately stands up a little shaken. ** Master: Tachi fight it! You must stand strong… ** Despite his Masters words of encouragement Yama Tatsu cannot stand this tremendous pressure. He crashes to the floor in an unconscious heap. Fade out. ** ** Fade in back at the gym where Yama Tatsu was training. Though this time we are not in the ring we are backstage in the locker rooms. Yama Tatsu is seated on a bench wearing a skin-tight Singlet/Tank top and a pair of Board shorts. He is slipping on a set of sandals made by a popular skate company. He looks up at the camera which has now pulled a bit closer. ** Yama Tatsu: Hey CCW! It looks like I have finally achieved my goal. I have reached my ultimate fighting state. The embarrassment I suffered at Irish Spring will never… evvvveeeerrrrr happen again. ** Yama Tatsu, now with his sandals on, stands and walks over to his locker. His muscles ripple and his abs clearly visible through his singlet. He digs around in the locker before remerging with a set of keys and a Jacket. He puts on the jacket and faces the camera. ** Yama Tatsu: As I understand it Ricky Thunder has found a partner for this Thursday. Yet I have no one. Just me, myself and I! I will storm into Lockdown with my Harley blazing so loud… The crowds gonna think it’s hell on wheels and when I jump back on it and gun up the ramp well people are going to think the worlds coming to an end! ** A menacing grin seems to be engraved deeply on his stone hard face. ** Yama Tatsu: But thinking about it I might need to try do a quick round up backstage and see who I can get to give me a hand. Yet if the problem arises… which I think it might, and I can’t find a partner this Dragon will be flying solo. I think the chances of me find an ally though a slim to anorexic! ** Yama Tatsu now faces right down the camera and addresses those fellow CCW Superstars at home watching. ** Yama Tatsu: So if anyone out there wishes to give me a hand in this competition Thursday night… Holla, cause I am in desperate need of some help with no allies currently residing in CCW. But hey, your looking at the CCW Hardcore champion and potential King of Extreme! So Ricky Thunder, it doesn’t matter how it goes down this Thursday because I know I will fight to the death before I submit to you! ** Yama Tatsu’s Master enters the locker room. Yama Tatsu quickly bows to his superior and then addresses him. ** Yama Tatsu: Kyoshi will you be there this Thursday? Master: I will Tachi! Yama Tatsu: Thank You Kyoshi. I really wish you to be there standing beside me in battle, as you may be my only source of a Partner. Master: I will be there through thick and thin my student. Yama Tatsu: Thank you Kyoshi ** Yama Tatsu bows to his Master once again and his Master walks away. The camera returns to a single shot of Yama Tatsu. Yama Tatsu throws his keys up into the air again and catches them with one hand. ** Yama Tatsu: I should split as well. I need my rest. That Ju Kumite (sparring practice) I did earlier really took it out of me. At least I have finally reached perfection. All that remains is to master perfection. Because as we all know wild, untamed beauty can soon turn nasty if not controlled. So Ricky Thunder and your “Mysterious Partner”… OGA SHIMASU!!! ** Yama Tatsu makes a series of fancy hand signals and then mimics a cutting of his throat. Fade out. ** View Biography
