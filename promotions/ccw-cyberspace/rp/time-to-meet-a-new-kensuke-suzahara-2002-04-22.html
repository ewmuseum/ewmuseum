Time To Meet A New Kensuke Suzahara

What this is A short promo roleplay in which The Valiant One challenges his former mentor Kensuke Suzahara.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Valiant One Entitled: Time to meet a new Kensuke Suzahara... Written in Monday, Apr 22, 2002, 22:42:17 Location: unknown This is a: Match RP *The scene opens up in a beautiful meadow. Flowers everywhere throughout the field as far as the eye can see. As the camera pans a shadow of a figure can be seen off in the distance. The figure continues to get bigger as you can begin to read 'New Strangers' on the shirt of the figure. You can see the flowing long blonde hair and finally recognize the figure to be The Valiant One's. He approaches the camera and begins to speak.* The Valiant One: CCW is changing my friends. *The tone of The Valiant One's voice is very humble.* The Valiant One: There is a dawning of a new era... *He continues to walk as he runs his hand through the tall grasses of the meadow.* The Valiant One: The Supreme Machine has returned and everything is set to change....really it already has. The entire roster is focused on his return. *He is looking off into the clouds, seeming sort of unfocused.* The Valiant One: Though last Thursday marked the return of The Supreme Machine, it also had the return of my former mentor....Kensuke Suzahara. *The Valiant One's voice marks a more serious tone now.* The Valiant One: Kensuke, you think that everyone hates you...well, you're right, we all saw what you did and there is no excuse for that. The time has come that we finally meet inside the ring. This Thursday on Lockdown it will be booked, oh yeah, The Valiant One vs Kensuke Suzahara, student vs teacher, mentor vs mentee. I'll see you Thursday, and it is no time to be valiant... *The scene fades to black as The Valiant One walks off into the sunset.* View Biography
