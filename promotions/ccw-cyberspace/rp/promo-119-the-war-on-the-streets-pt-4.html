The War on the Streets (pt. 4)

25 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The War on the Streets (pt. 4) Scorpion CCW Promo #119 Date: 3-25-03 Location: NY Hospital :::: Sitting up in the hospital bed Scorpion with the phone on his lap as he begins to call some old friends to help him out stop Vinny Grazino from taking over the streets. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Christ who should I call first? I’ll try the Albino Rhino. :::: Scorpion begins to call “The Albino Rhino” who’s really name is Dave. He is probably one of the only true blood Italians who is an Albino in the world. They call him “The Albino Rhino” that because he is an Albino and is a absoulte man beast. :::: Albino Rhino: Hello? Scorpion: Hey Dave its Scorpion? Albino Rhino: Hey man what have you been up too? Scorpion: Same old, same old. I called you because same jackass is going to take over the streets. Albino Rhino: Scorp I can’t man I’m outta that shit now. I got a decent job over in waste management in Jersey. I can’t leave that job to help you take out some punk. Scorpion: His cousin is Corky Stewart, the kid who killed Drake, the same kid that tried to kill Mike and I. Albino Rhino: That son of a bitch is this guy Vinny’s cousin? Scorpion: Yeah, this Vinny guy is holding Mike hostage until I join him on taking down the streets. Albino Rhino: They got Mike hostage? Now they are making this personal fo’ me. I’m in Scorpion. Anyone who disrespects my friends and thinks they can get away with it got another thing comin’. Scorpion: Alright man I’m in the hospital right now I’ll be out later today. You can meet me at the dry docks at nine o’ clock. From there we will go where they are. They will think I’m coming alone, but I’m going to bring some back up. Albino Rhino: Why are you in the hospital? Scorpion: To make a long story short, I went to the dry docks to meet this Vinny guy to try and get Mike free. He asked me to join, I told him to go fuck himself and he had his huge security guards beat me up. The knocked me out cold and some police officers saw me so they took me to the hospital. I was out cold for two days. Albino Rhino: These fuckin dudes are gonna get it bro. They better not think they can get away with trying to beat up my friends. They are going to hear from me when we head over there, guarantee that. Scorpion: All right man, I’ll see you later tonight. Albino Rhino: Damn right you will, later bro. Scorpion: Bye. :::: Scorpion puts the phone back in the phone cradle. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Who should I call next? I’m going to give Tony The Butcher a call. :::: Scorpion begins to call Tony The Butcher. Tony got the name cause he is a butcher in a deli and has been known to be a very deadly man in his past before he went to jail. :::: Tony The Butcher: Hello? Scorpion: Tony? Tony The Butcher: Yeah who is it? Scorpion: It’s Scorpion. Tony The Butcher: Scorp what’s up kid? Scorpion: It’s been good. I just got a problem and need your help. Tony The Butcher: Hey I’d do anything for you, you’re like family to me. Scorpion: This guy whose cousin is Corky is trying to take over the streets, he is holding Mike hostage until I join his group, which I won’t. His security guards beat the shit out of me a left me unconscious. I got the Albino Rhino to help back me up when I go there tonight to free Mike and take care of this Vinny. Tony The Butcher: That sounds all good, but I lost you halfway. It’s too long for me; the doctor told me I got Attention Deficit Disorder or something. Scorpion: Basically what we are doing is freeing Mike and taking care of this guy for what he has done, he has also threatened to go after my family. Tony The Butcher: Holy shit that isn’t cool. You got my back kid. When and where should I meet you? Scorpion: Nine o’clock tonight at the dry docks from there we will go after this Vinny guy. Tony The Butcher: No problem. Hey Scorpion do you want me to bring the butcher knife? Scorpion: No man, I don’t want us to get arrested or murder someone. Tony The Butcher: Ok have it your way. Scorpion: All right Tony, I’ll see you later tonight. Tony The Butcher: Ok man, bye. :::: Scorpion hangs up the phone and puts it back. As lays back down he notices his wife standing in the doorway. :::: Scorpion: How much of that did you hear? Jennifer: I have heard all of it, I have been here longer then you would expect. Scorpion: Shit. Jennifer: You aren’t going out tonight to fight these guys, they just put you in the hospital, and I don’t want them to put you back in the hospital. Scorpion: I got back up for this one. Don’t worry. Jennifer: If you return on a stretcher that’s it, let it go. But as long as you got back up…then kick this guy’s ass. Scorpion: Damn right I will. Jennifer: All right let me sign you out so we can get out of here. Scorpion: Ok. :::: Jennifer walks out of the room. :::: Scorpion: (talking to himself) Payback is a bitch… TO BE CONTINUED… View Scorpion 's Biography Back
