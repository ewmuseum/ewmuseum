Family Tree; Part 3 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #133 Date: 04/15/03 Location: Seattle, Washington Inside Jonathan's antique store, at approximately 8:00 in the morning, Lacey, Jonathan and Chloe all do their part to help prepare breakfast. Jonathan: Tell me. Lacey: If I started telling you everything that's happened to me.... Jonathan: Don't patronize me, Lacey. I'm sure Chloe wants to know, just as much as I do, what's got you so rattled. Lacey: {sighs} With all this talk of adoption, it's just got me thinking back to several incidents that happened to me when I was young. Chloe: Like what? Your brothers tease you about being the ugly one, and how you obviously had to be adopted when compared to their good looks, wit and charm? Lacey: {shakes her head} This really isn't important guys. My father wasn't the nicest of people. He could be downright nasty at times. My earliest memories begin when I was around five years old. I remember several times when he would make comments about how disappointed he was in me, and how they should have adopted another boy instead of a girl like me. Jonathan: Are you serious? Lacey: Yes, but like I said, it really isn't important right now. My father scared me, that's all. There were only several moments in my life when I can truly say that I was happy or comfortable around him. But my family problems are history; Chloe's are here and now.... {to Chloe} There's an old proverb – If you go hunting tigers, be sure you are truly prepared to find one. Chloe: Meaning, I might not like what I find? Lacey: Why do you want to know.... after all these years? There has to be a reason your father's never contacted you. Jonathan: Think about that, Chloe.... Chloe: I have. Jonathan and Lacey look at each other. They shrug. Jonathan: Do you remember anything before the orphanage? Chloe: Not much. There was a woman.... Lacey: Your mother? Emily Mason? Chloe: I dunno.... I guess she must've been. I can't picture her face, but.... I remember every Friday we had to go to the post office. I never figured out why, but I loved it. God, I wanted to be a mail-woman when I grew up something fierce. And then right down the street was a candy store.... it was run by this old guy called Stubbs. I used to call him Mr. Tubby. He'd always laugh because he's thin as a pencil. She'd buy me licorice and Mr. Stubbs'd let me look at look at the pictures in the comics. Lacey: Why do you remember that? Chloe: Huh? Lacey: What was so special about the candy store? Chloe: {a moment's pause and with great difficulty} One day, mom – Emily – was chatting to Mr. Stubbs.... and suddenly she put her hand to her forehead.... And said something like, 'I feel funny'.... and then she just fell to the floor.... Didn't move. I thought she was playing a game, so I'm pushing her and laughing.... and then there was an ambulance and lots of people running around.... and then they took her away. And Mr. Stubbs picked me up and said, 'Well, honey, what are they going to do with you?' {to Lacey} That's why I remember Mr. Stubbs' candy store. Lacey: What part of town was this? Chloe: I was four going on five. Who knew geography? I could barely find my way to the bathroom. Jonathan: Mr. Stubbs' Candy Store.... well, it's a start. Jonathan digs out the yellow pages from a cupboard and lets his fingers do the walking. Chloe: He's not going to be in there. The guy was pretty old even back then. He must've checked in his chips by now. Lacey: You said this candy store was close to a post office? Chloe looks at Lacey and their eyes meet. It's a place to start at least....
