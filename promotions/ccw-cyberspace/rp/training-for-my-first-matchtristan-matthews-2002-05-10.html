Training for My First Match...Tristan Matthews

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A training promo in which Venneman Pendragon demonstrates his Last Crusade finisher on tree stumps and threatens his first CCW opponent, Tristan Matthew.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Venneman Pendragon Entitled: Training For My First Match...Tristan Matthews Written in Friday, May 10, 2002, 19:22:38 Location: This is a: Match RP (The screen is filled by a shot of an overcast sky. We can occasionally see a flicker of lightning through the clouds. As the camera pans down we see that we are some distance from the ground. In the distance, we can see a man slashing his arms too and fro. With each slash we catch a glimmer of shiny steel. The camera zooms in closer and we are met with the hulking form of Venneman Pendragon. Barechested, he practices the forms of swordplay flowing gracefully from one position to another. With a look at his face, he seems almost to be a throwback to the past. His strong jaw, made stronger by a short trimmed blonde beard, is set in grim determination. His hair is wet due to a mixture of sweat and the light drizzle that falls from the heavens. His skin glistens from the sheen of the moisture and each muscle in his heavy arms and chest are clearly defined with each strike at each inner foe. Finally, the cameraman makes himself known...) Cameraman: Yo, Ven! It's rainin'. Can we get on with this? (Venneman ignores the cameraman and goes on about his business. At last he places the sword in a blanket that is lying on the ground. He kneels down and whispers some inaudible words over the weapon before he wraps it. He then faces the camera and his blue eyes burn with a ferocious intensity. This is indeed a man on a mission...) Venneman: Can we get on with this? Does the rain harm you, you weasel of a man? Perhaps I should have had someone else film this interlude... Cameraman: No, it's not that, it's just uncomfortable as hell! Venneman: I care not for comfort...Comfort makes a man soft. The elements, living rough makes a man IRON! You who have traveled here because you are told it is your job to film me. I who wish to deliver a message to those who would listen. I am the one who is important. It is your job. This is my life! (The cameraman says nothing else, he just goes on filming this rock of a man who chooses to train in the outside elements.) Venneman: Normally I train in solitude, but I should relay a small demonstration to the misfortunate in the CCW. What you just witnessed was swordplay. Excellent for the reflexes and for journeying within oneself...These days, unfortunately, it is not looked kindly upon when one hacks someone up with the noblest of weapons. Fortunately, I do not need a sword. Because I am allowed only my body in the ring, I have sharpened it, much like a sword...Observe.... (Venneman Points at a six-foot tree stump some ten paces away. It is about the same thickness as a man of the same height. Without warning, Venneman charges the tree and dives, spearing the tree[he calls this the Sword In the Stone, the setup to his finisher]. Instantly, the tree splinters and Venneman flies through, hits the ground and bounds nimbly to his feet. He then picks up a log, there is a pile of three beside him, all of the same size as the first stump. He hoists this up onto his shoulders, holds it there for a moment and then slams it onto his knee. Again, the log explodes into splinters. He picks up the other two and breaks them the same way. When he is finished, he stands in the middle of the now considerably smaller logs and the splinters. He sighs and again speaks to the camera...) Venneman: The Last Crusade...A move of such destructive force that men have been crippled by its devastation. At another time, I mourned the unfortunates who were downed by this move, and soon the multitudes weighed heavily upon my soul... (He shakes his head and pushes his hair from his eyes...) Venneman: I now realize that these men chose to stand before my onslaught. These poor souls were given the opportunity to move on, to not face me...Ahh but nobility has many enemies...One by one my enemies fell, and I am happy for eat. The punishment I dole out is deserved and well gotten...Those whose ignorance masks the fear they should have of me shall have none of my pity...Only my knee. (Venneman begins to pace, his large arms slightly swinging with the motion of his body. He shakes his head and continues...) Venneman: Now, in my new home, I have learned the name of the one who must first face the power of the Last Crusade. Tristan Matthew. Such a common name for someone who shall fall for such a glorious cause...Glorious because you shall be the first of many to experience the Last Crusade...My rebirth has shown me many things, yet many things still remain the same. I have been, and shall always be the best that the world has seen. In the past I showed mercy to those who were weak, but now....Now I crave to loose myself on the unwitting and cause wanton destruction. My goal is to rule this federation, and if I must start with the no-names and dregs of the roster then so be it. What sort of man are you, Tristan? I pride myself on knowing my opponents well, but you are somewhat of an enigma...You list no history...Where are you from? Are you a real pro or some newbie who just gave up his mother's tit? Though you are secretive about your history, I will say this...You are quite convincing in your ability to lose wrestling matches...You were in a fourway dance, given the opportunity to destroy three pathetic wrestlers, and you prove that YOU were the most pathetic. (Venneman throws back his head and roars with laughter...) Venneman: Why, the way you went over the top rope as soon as the match started so nobody could beat on you....PRICELESS!!! (Quickly, Venneman is all glaring seriousness again...) Venneman: You can try to run from me...You can try to hide. There won't be anywhere to go....Go outta the ring and I'll get ya....Stay in the ring and I'll destroy you. Many men have tried to hide from me, and who could blame them? It is hard to stand tall when you are sure that doom is coming. It is hard to stand when the end is near. (Venneman shakes his head in mock concern. His voice is filled with a hint of irony...) Venneman: Tristan, do you know why I wish you destroyed? What could have been your transgression to deserve this fate? Bad luck on your part is all. The powers that be evidently wanted you dismantled. I will enjoy it because you are a coward who hides his past....A coward who runs from the enemy. I will make an example of you. You will be the first testament to my greatness in this new federation. (Venneman picks up one more log and breaks it with his bare hands...) Venneman: The winds blow and the Last Crusade draws nigh. Prepare to be swept away... (Venneman starts to laugh. Slowly at first but soon he laughs in great bellows. The cameraman shakes his head as the scene fades....) View Biography
