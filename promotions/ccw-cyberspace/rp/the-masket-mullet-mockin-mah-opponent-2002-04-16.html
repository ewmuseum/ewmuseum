The Masket Mullet - Mockin' Mah Opponent

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which the Masked Mullet and his ring crew prepare a promo against Brian Gaines while a narrator interrupts.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Masket Mullet Entitled: Mockin' Mah Opponent Written in Tuesday, Apr 16, 2002, 17:06:23 Location: Back Lot of the Arena This is a: Match RP Narrator: The scene opens up, in the back lot of CCW, where the ring crew is all standing around, and a somber look on their faces. There is a desk, usually used for commissioner skits, or for random things to destroy in the hardcore title matches. Beside that desk, The Masked Mullet, well, actually his alter ego.. if you can really call him that, Clem. Clem stands at the relatively tall height of 6'1" and weighs a whopping 153 lbs. Well, I guess not really all that whopping. He stands there, trying to smooth the wrinkles out of what appears to be a third hand suit jacket, with a Pantera shirt underneath, and of course.. blue jeans, which have seen better days. I don't understand it either folks. Paul Pluto: Umm... and why are we doing this? Bobby Connelly: Umm, I think Clem told us too. Paul: You think? You're not sure? Bobby: Right. Paul: [sigh] Clem: Hey! Guys, how do ah' look? Paul: Umm.. like a red neck hick trying to look classy. Clem: Really? That good? I was going fer Springer Wear. Paul: Well, you certainly bested that. Clem: YEEEHAW!! Right on! Bobby: Lookin' sharp! Narrator: I guess Clem is going to cut a promo against Brian Gaines, the man he is scheduled to have a match against on LDT. I guess.. Clem is going to mock Brian, but after seeing the volatile temper of his opponent I'd guess I had better talk him out of it. Clem: Alright! Ready to start this thang!! Pablo, Ingkar, Joe, are ya' ready! Pablo: Si, Senior. Ingkar: Yes, I am very ready, my good friend Clem. Joe: Meh. Narrator: Umm, Clem. [The eyes of the three ring crew workers, slowly widen, as they look around to see who was talking. But of course, the narrator can't bee seen, that would be stupid. The look on edge, a slight twitch to Joe's right eye.] Clem: What is it now?! Narrator: I don't think this is such a good idea. Clem: And, why do ya' say that? Narrator: This guy looks really unstable. Clem: So?! This is gonna be fun-nay! [The three ring crew workers, are sufficiently freaked out, glancing around, still seeing no one, but noticing that Clem is conversing with this.. entity. Pablo just glances around.] Pablo: Muy es, freaky, Senior. Joe: [Nods his head.] Ingkar: Oh yes, this is terribly frightening, very indeed, sirs. Clem: Let's just git on with this. Narrator: I warned you.. Pablo: [Shrieks in terror and runs off hiding behind Bobby.] This CCW Promo, is brought to you by the Leader of the Ring Crew: Clem. [Clem stands there, in front of the prop desk, sitting on it, rather un-coothly.. bad camera angle, and tight jeans, are a very bad mix. Clem smoothes a hand over his mullet, and straightens his jacket.] Clem: Guy, you need ta' move that junk... and um.. hand my.. that.. thingy over there. Pablo: You mean the wrench, senior? Clem: ....yes. Pablo: [walks off to search for... the wrench.] Narrator: I'm still not sure about this Clem. Pablo: AIEEE!! I AM POSSESSED!! RUN AWAY!! [Pablo shrieks, and starts to run, followed close behind by Joe, and Ingkar. They rush off down the halls of the arena, until finally smacking straight into Angelica Lynn's manager, Father Jordan.] Pablo: A man of God! Please, rid the demons from my head!! I have become possessed by the devil, Padre! Joe: Yeah.. Ingkar: Yes, though you may not be of the Hindi faith, I must request that you rid my brain of the evils of this.. Satan as well.. He speaks in my mind, and prompts me to kill. Father Jordan: Umm.. well, my.. Sons.. Umm.. [Father Jordan just blanks, and turns, and starts to run, the three ring crew workers blink, and immediately begin to give chase. Following the running priest, who, screeches down the halls.] Clem: Look at that Mr. Invisible-Man! You ruin all of mah' Promo's! Narrator: That's not true, I'm just trying to help you become a bit more serious. Paul: Hey.. just what are you anyways? Bobby: He's probably just a higher power sent to tell your story. Narrator: [blinks] Clem: [Blinks] Paul: [Blinks] Narrator: Well.. Bobby, you're right. Clem: So.. are ya God? Cause, I woulda' dressed up more, if ya were. Narrator: [snorts] Hardly. Paul: Are you an angel.. or demi god, like that greased god, John Jupiter? Narrator: You are sick, you know that right? Paul: Well.. yeah. Clem: Aww, he's not sick, he's just a big ol' fan of John Jupiter! Narrator: Right.. Anywho.. I think you should start taking this stuff seriously.. you beat Bojangles the Klown, and you earned yourself a hardcore title shot, but no one takes you seriously Clem. You won't get that shot until they do.... They won't all be cakewalks you know. Clem: Wait.. so.. are ya' Satan then? Narrator: ..you weren't listening at all, were you? Bobby: Listening to what? Narrator: [Sighs] Fine, Don't listen to me. Get your ass beaten then.. See if I fuckin' care. Clem: Wait, before ya' go.. Narrator: What is it now?! Clem: Did.. you create me..? Narrator: Umm.. well.. kinda? Clem: Are.. you mah' Paw? Narrator: OF COURSE NOT!! Clem: Daddy!! Narrator: FINISH YOUR PROMO JACKASS! Clem: [Sighs] Fine.. Brian Gaines, Ah'm the Masked Mullet, I'm a veteran here in CC-Dub. Ah've been workin' here for three years.. though.. only wrestling for a few months. You should watch out! Cause Ah'm gonna kick your can! YEEEEHAW!! Narrator: Jesus Christ.. This is why no one takes you seriously. This is over. [End.] View Biography
