A Game of Chess (LD 3/20: RP #1)

14 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Game of Chess (LD 3/20: RP #1) Jason Dynasty CCW Promo #85 Date: 3/14/03 Location: Downtown Toronto Life is a circle, everything does what it does in reaction to something that happened before it. The reason a cat feeds on mice is the same as the reason why the mouse shall later feed upon the cat’s decomposing dead body…survival, hakuna matata, and the circle of life. Wrestling is much the same…when one wrestler leaves a new one is waiting in the wings and always steps up. The end is always a path back to the beginning. The end of my time in Global Wrestling is the beginning of my time in the Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. The beginning always leads to the end. My beginning in CCW equals the beginning of the end for its stars. Enjoy the first dance CCW, because with it begins a new era, a new destiny and a new Dynasty. Go to church, say your prayers and prepare to sell your soul to God because starting this week, your bodies and minds shall belong to me. A city is shown or rather the view of one from the outlook of an airplane. A flash of light and then the view is replaced by a second, this time from the streets of the same city, which one is unknown as they are all the same as far as this story is concerned. The importance is not in the city so much as it is in the man who has just arrived in it. Here, on this street, in this city and in this continent stands one man. The camera turns ninety degrees to the left and there he is…6 feet, 6 inches tall and 260 pounds. An African American dressed head to toe in black. He is wearing black boots, black khaki pants, and a black belt (in more ways than one, as you shall all soon find out), and a black T-shirt. Over that he wears a five-foot long black trenchcoat. His face is clean-, his jaw square, his nose is almost pointed and he has a grim look etched across his face. His eyes are covered by a pair of dark black, wrap-around sunglasses despite the gloomy morning that today entails. His black hair is cut in an Army-style crew cut and he has neither piercings nor tattoos. His head is covered by a black Fedora hat, which shadows even the most distinctive of his chiseled features. He is a behemoth among those few men and women who chose to walk the city streets at this early hour. As he walks, every passing person watches him. He ignores them and continues to walk down the street, not looking into the stores that line his right side or looking at the passing cars and people to his left. He simply continues on his path…always continuing on his path…back on the path. As he walks he begins to speak in a slow tone. It was easy to tell that he was an intelligent man as his voice is projected out like a young James Earl Jones. Man: They walk around amongst us with no sense of direction, purpose or reason for being, yet they think and truly believe that they are necessary for the function of all of us. At the word “us” he waves his hand in a flourish to signify the passing people, the passing masses that line the street. Man (shaking his head slowly as he speaks, always watching the eyes of the people as they pass): They move along under the orders of others and are simply pieces of the bigger puzzle, like ants who take orders and live to serve the queen. They believe they are the solution, they are nothing more than the problem. For one year, I have watched and waited and bid my time for this moment and now it is here. The months I spent in routine observation of them, their actions and their everyday tendencies is about to finally pay off. The man pauses as he nears a sidewalk vendor, one of those people who drag the big metal carts around all day and sell sodas, pizza, nachos and hot dogs. The seductive, enticing scent slowly wafts across the area, capturing the attention of those who pass by. The man pauses and leans against the shop window nearest to the vendor, reaching into his back pocket and removing 3 one-dollar bills from his wallet, which is brown and attached to a steel chain. He puts his wallet back in his pocket and readjusts his black trenchcoat before walking over to the vendor. The vendor is a small, fat man around forty years old, with balding brown hair and a greasy apron tied around his beer keg-shaped waist. He looks up and swallows in a modicum of surprise, fear and despair as the huge towering black man steps up to his small cart. Man (speaking to the vendor): I’ll have a hot dog with ketchup, mustard and onions, and a Pepsi. Vendor (nodding slowly as he watches the man): Uh…uh…yeah…uh-sure buddy. The vendor opens up the back of his cart and pulls out a hot dog with his plastic-gloved hand and begins to pile the condiments of choice on it as he grabs a can of Pepsi. He hands both items to the man as he is handed the three bills. Man (as he walks off): Keep the change. Vendor (still scared by the sheer mass of that man): Yeah…uh…uh…well, thanks man. The man stops by a bench on the sidewalk and opens the can of pop, taking a sip before setting it down next to him. He unwraps the hot dog and puts it to his mouth, greedily taking a bite. His face almost instantly changes to a look of disgust as he turns and spits the contents into the gutter behind him. He throws the week-old hot dog away and swigs down the rest of his soda, in an attempt to wash the sick aftertaste away. He turns his head but sees that the vendor is gone, the man just shakes his head and shrugs, finishing off the can and tossing it into the trash along with what is left of the hot dog. Man (disgusted): Hell, they cannot even make a simple hot dog correctly. A few moments pass as the aftertaste dissipates from his senses and the man stands up and begins to again walk down the street, towards something but that something is still unknown. As he walks he begins to speak, this time allowing his hand gestures to animate and express his words. Man (speaking as he continues his slow walk down the street): I have been around the block on this for quite a while, and I have come to a conclusion. They fear me, they know that once I arrive then their control shall be put to the ultimate test, they worry that I shall gain control of the bodies and minds of the masses. They are worried that the power they hold so tightly over these people shall be tested and that they shall fail. I learned long ago that a person will do anything you ask of him or her as long as you put the idea into their head correctly. I am a master of the mind, a master of the spirit and I know how to get what I want. I am not alone either, I have brought along a friend and he too, when he arrives shall accompany me in the tribulations that follow. Judgement Day is upon those who are our enemies, they do not know us, but we know them and soon enough they shall be screaming for Disciples of Death to show them a few Shade of Apocalypse to end their pain and misery. I am here for the long run and they all shall be taught that “when the Iceman cometh, he cometh for your souls”. My long walk has just begun but consider this a pit stop. I have thought this over long and hard and determined exactly what these people I am coming after truly are. I have come to one fateful conclusion about my enemies. What better place to speak of that conclusion…than here? The man stops his walk and turns to his left, in front of him is a long group of marble stairs leading up to a large 10-story building whose foundation is set into a hill in what is the easternmost part of the city. He slowly makes his way up the steps of the building, 37 in all, before coming up to a 2 large steel doors. To the left of the doors was a sign which read “Cyberspace Championship Wrestling: Toronto, Ontario Offices” in a bold, black print. He then looks at the sign for a moment before taking a step back and staring up at the highest point on the building. Man (solemnly, as if thinking his words over carefully): Yes, they are all pawns. The man reaches into his trenchcoat and pulls out an object, it is a white chess pawn, newly painted and with a slight glint as the rays of the morning sun finally show themselves and hit the side of the pawn. The man slowly gets down on one knee and places the pawn at the front steps of the CCW Headquarters before standing back up and looking at it. Man (as a smile slowly creeps across his face): But, now, they are my pawns. As abruptly as he came, the man turns and slowly tucks his hands into his trenchcoat, pulling it close as a cool breeze passes by. He turns and makes his way down the stairs, eventually turning and disappearing from sight. The camera pans back around to show the chess piece and as they zoom in, two inscriptions can be seen on the side of it. The carvings appear to be done by a well skilled hand or a machine due to the small print and yet masterful workmanship. One has been crossed out and reads “Cyberspace Championship Wrestling” while the other carving reads “Jason Dynasty”. The camera stays locked on that name as the scene slowly fades to black.
