What this is A Match RP in which Gabriel Nyte sits motionless in a state hospital cell while doctors discuss his case and his brother.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Gabriel Nyte Entitled: Nyte 8:2. Ground Zero of the Mind Written in Wednesday, May 1, 2002, 00:05:34 Location: Pescadero State Hospital: Los Angeles, CA This is a: Match RP [We begin at the white walled, Pescadero State Hospital in LA. Why are we here? To Pay a visit to Paul Arovnik? Sarah Connor? No...It is for something else. We head inside the very cold and sterile facade of the internal corridors of the building. As we head down one of these corridors we see a pile of Doctors huddled around a a single door. The camera heads to the door, and we peer into the cell marked '316'. We see Gabriel Nyte...sitting on the floor..motionless. He's wearing a white tanktop and white pants, bare foot. The doctors pull back and begin a conversation.] Doctor #1: Very odd...he hasn't eaten for days now...ever since his men dropped him off here...he has simply remained motionless in that position. Doctor #2: You sure he hasn't died? Doctor #1: Positive...he is still breathing, and he still has a pulse...I checked 15 minutes before you arrived for the consult. Doctor #2: Has he had any visitors? Doctor #1: Only a Cardinal named O'Ryan. Doctor #2: No family? friends? Doctor #1: According to his background check...the only family he has is his brother. He's currently in Israel, and, he's fighting for the Palestinian Resistance. Doctor #2: Name? Doctor #1: Peter Thomas Nyte. Doctor #2: Well...get him over here...maybe he can help him snap out of this. Also...set up an IV in case he doesn't snap out of it within 24 hours. I hope we can get this guy back to normal soon. He'll either get better...or worse. Doctor #1: Yes, Doctor. What about his booking for Lockdown? Doctor #2: He's not moving until...he's...moving. Doctor #1: Right Doctor, I'll get in touch with CCW management immediately. [Doctor #1 leaves the scene to head to the nearest office to call the people.] Doctor #2: Hmm...what is wrong with you, Brother Nyte? I wish we could get into that mind of yours to find out what the hell is going on in there. [The camera peers into the view window to see Gabriel Nyte once again....remaining motionless...still...serene...as we fade to black.] View Biography
