Backstabber (pt. 5)

Backstabber (pt. 5) Scorpion CCW Promo #59 Date: 3-4-03 Location: Las Vegas, Nevada :::: A nudge is felt on Scorpion’s shoulder, Scorpion cracks open his eyes and sees Donald Trump standing in front of him. :::: Donald Trump: Welcome to Las Vegas! Scorpion: Were here? Donald Trump: Yep, look outside. :::: Scorpion takes a look outside and sees the bright lights from the casino’s. :::: Scorpion: Thank god we made it. (Nudging Mike) Mike wake up! :::: Mike opens his eyes. :::: Scorpion: We are in Las Vegas! Mike: Holy shit! We finally made it! Donald Trump: Come let’s get out of this plane shall we? :::: Donald Trump followed by Scorpion and Mike walk down the stairs to the runway. Once Scorpion and Mike touch on the ground they feel a gun pointed to their head. :::: Gunman: Don’t move. Scorpion: Oh no you have got to be kidding me. :::: A limo pulls up to where Scorpion and Mike are. Out of the limo comes Corky. :::: Corky: Scorpion, Mike so we meet again! Mike: What the fuck is going on here! Corky: Hey now, watch your attitude with me or I’ll just one of my gunman implant a solid bullet into your skull. Scorpion: How did you know we were coming? Corky: Ah, gentlemen you have just been had by my good friend…Mr. Donald Trump. Scorpion: What the hell? Donald Trump was in on this thing the whole time? Donald Trump: You damn right I was and you two idiots didn’t know the whole time. Mike: Why are you helping Corky? Donald Trump: Don’t you guys remember what I told you inside that plane? Anyone will do anything for the price of a dollar. Corky offered me co-ownership of the casino if I brought you two to him tonight. I’d my part and now I get rewarded. Mike: You two faced bastard! I knew we couldn’t trust you the whole time. Donald Trump: But you proved to everyone that you lack common sense. Don’t you know the old saying…don’t trust anybody? You trusted me whom should have never done and I stabbed you in the back all for some big time bucks. Scorpion: When I get lose I’m going to personally kick your ass. Corky: (Laughs) When you get out? What are you crazy? I’m never letting you out; actually I’m going to kill you. Boys handcuff these boys with some handcuffs from a couple of friends of in New York City. The same friends that killed Drake! :::: Two gang members walk over handcuffing Scorpion and Mike as the two gunmen hold the pistols to each head of Scorpion and Mike. Mike: Holy shit, Donald Trump was right! Dirty cops did kill Drake! Donald Trump: I didn’t guess you idiot I knew the whole time. I helped Corky pay the dirty cops to kill Drake Rizzel. Then Corky kept on an eye on Drake telling the cops where Drake was headed. Once that happened that shot Drake dead so Corky could have full leadership over the gang. Scorpion: I guess the media is right…you really are a snake in the grass. Donald Trump: Always have and always will be a snake in the grass. Mike: I can’t believe this! This is such bullshit! Corky: Shut your mouth! I have had enough of this small talk. Personally I can’t wait much longer I want to kill the both of you now, but we can’t. We got to do it in style… Scorpion: What the hell is that supposed to mean? Corky: If you thought the car incident was bad Scorpion…wait until you see the way I’m going to dispose the both of you now. Mike: Tell us god dammit! Corky: I don’t have to tell you anything. But I’ll give you a tiny little hint, one of my favorite movies growing up, as a kid was Peter Pan. Scorpion: Peter Pan? What are we going to fly? Corky: (laughs) Not it’s going to be a lot worse, a whole lot worse. Oh and gunmen, you can knock them unconscious now. :::: With that being said each gunman takes the bottom of their pistol whacking Scorpion and Mike right across the temple, knocking them out. :::: Corky: All right guys, its time for our little friends to walk the plank!
