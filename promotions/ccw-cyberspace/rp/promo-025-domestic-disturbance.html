Domestic Disturbance Yama Tatsu CCW Promo #25 Date: Location: Southern Californian A sunny afternoon in beautiful Southern Californian neighbourhood is our scene. A bright lemon yellow Taxi slowly strolls though the streets. As we pace house after house we can see nothing but immaculate gardens and bright welcoming houses. We finally reach 185 Newberry St. The cab pulls over curbside. Out steps a woman in a black dress and a dark veil covering her face. She moves swiftly up to the front door of this house. She carries a big bundle wrapped in a dark coloured towel. She knocks at the door… No reply. She knocks again, but the answer is the same. She tries a third time, though this time the door is swiftly answered. Woman: Oh Yama, I have brought our love child Yama Tatsu: What!?!? Woman: Please let us in! We have nowhere else to stay and besides little Yama and I want to be with you! Yama Tatsu: Get out of here you crack addicted! I ain’t got time for your games! Woman: Oh please! Once you look at his face you will see is certainly your son… The woman lifts the piece of towel that covers the babies face from view. There sits Curiously Bruce, Michael Stevens Gay midget flunky. Bruce: Surprise asshole! The woman launches the little bundle of Homosexual evil at Yama. The dwarfed fudge packer clings to Yama’s front and tries to claw his eyes. Yama is defenceless and starts to stumble backwards into his house. Sheena signals back to the cab and the retard known as Jimmie comes flying across the lawn and up to the door. His run can only be described as a mix of Jim Carry and a Rhino. He sprints into the house as Sheena casually shuts the door behind her. Inside Yama is trying to pry the midget’s teeth from his nose. Yama quickly fights fire with fire and wrenches the Midgets scrotum in an unspeakable manner. The midget quickly lets go and lays on the ground in pain as the rest of the Goof Troup enter Yama’s living room. Yama Tatsu: I guess it’s a little late for my planned slamming of the door in your face! But never the less you couldn’t have come at a more opportune time! I would like to introduce my latest sparring partner… General Tso! We look towards the door of the kitchen expecting a huge, burly, muscle bound freak, but instead we a are given a kind of flabby, Asian Midget. He is in a sleeveless Military Uniform, with many battle insignia on it, and a pair of khaki dress pants. Yama Tatsu: Bruce, I believe you know my friend here. General Tso! He is a well decorated war Veteran. Not to mention holding the record for the smallest man to ever enter the Chinese Army! He also has a Black Belt in the mysterious art of ‘Sou Do Mi’… That’s all correct isn’t it General Tso? General Tso: … Yama Tatsu: He’s not one for talking! General Tso attack! Bruce: Arrrrgghhhhhhhhh!!! The two midgets charge at each other like the brave warriors they are! General Tso body splashes Bruce, sits up on his chest and begins to slap Bruce senseless. Yama grits his teeth and signals for Jimmie to approach. Jimmie points to himself in a confused manner and starts to look around behind him, he is completely unaware that Yama wants to fight him. Yama hangs his head in disappointment, picks up a Vase and throws it at him. Luckily for the retard it just misses his head and smashes all over the wall behind him. Yama walks towards the duo but has to stop for the two midgets slapping and diving on each other. Yama squeezes around them and continues to approach the two misfits. Jimmie suddenly getting his ass into gear and remembering the mission strikes his best Karate pose and runs at Yama. He starts Karate Chopping Yama’s chest. He grabs Yama’s right arm and starts hacking his way up it. Yama doesn’t even flinch at the pathetic attempt at an attack. Yama Tatsu: I think this is what you are trying to do… Yama grabs the idiot’s arm and chops his upper arm making it go num. Jimmie grabs his arm in pain. Yama Tatsu: That hurt don’t it! Yama kicks the knee of Jimmie and pins his body round. Yama wraps his arms around Jimmie’s head and neck and proceeds to choke the crap out of him, while Jimmie yells for his mummy! SMASH! Yama drops Jimmie and we turn to see General Tso’s head through the oven window. But he quickly recovers and blinds Bruce with a mixture of blood and saliva. The General eyes a teaspoon that has been knocked on the ground. He picks it up and proceeds to chase Bruce around the house trying to stab him, while Yama starts looking around for that little Minx, Sheena. She is nowhere to be found. Yama runs up stairs leaving the knocked out Jimmie laying in a pool of his own Urine. Yama starts scanning upstairs room by room. Yama Tatsu: Where are you! When I find you you bitch I am gunna throw you out a window… Sheena: Hehehehe Yama Tatsu: Oh hell no! For your own sake you better not be in my room! Yama sprints into the master bedroom at the end of the hall. There he finds Sheena in her very low cut top and panties. A scowl comes across Yama’s face, but this is the exact opposite of Sheena’s! He face obviously doesn’t hide her intentions. Sheena: Hey hunny! I didn’t hear you come home… Yama Tatsu: What! Get off my bed and out of here now! Sheena: Why baby boo, don’t you love me anymore? I know how I can change that… Sheena starts to lick her lips, but this moment of one way flirting is interrupted by Curiously Bruce running into the room and hiding in the corner. General Tso enters seconds later, spies Bruce in the corner and dives at him. Bruce quickly gets out of the way and runs back out of the room. The General follows. Yama walks to the door and looks down the hallway as Bruce’s helpless screams can be heard. He turns around to see Sheena unzipping his jeans. He swats her hand away. Yama Tatsu: Get your scummy hands away from me! And your aids infested lips while you’re at it! Sheena: Come on… I just want to kiss big my Yama! Her hands shoot back up to his zipper as she wrestles with it. Yama goes to grab her by the hair but pulls it clean off. Sheena lets out a glass shattering scream. Yama drops the fluorescent wig in horror, but then begins to crack up laughing, as Sheena runs out of the room, and the house screaming the whole way. Yama: HEY! You forgot you hair… Yama chuckles to himself once again as he approaches the staircase. However this laugh soon ends as he finds his little Midget Karate Master laid out at the foot of the steps. Yama rushes to his little friends aid and checks his pulse. Yama Tatsu: Fewwww, he’s still alive, just out. But where’s the rest of these fools out. I remember dropping the retard over there. Yama looks through the huge French doors at the back of the living room. Out by Yama’s pool sits the Jimmie tangling his feet in the pool smoking a cigarette and reading a magazine. Yama sneaks out of the house and up behind him. From here we can see he is reading the Wall Street Journal and is almost finished smoking his cigarette. Yama taps him on the shoulder. Jimmie looks up surprised. Jimmie: **In a real English voice** Sweet Mother of England I forgot the retarded act…. Errr… I mean, duh I like water it’s wet! Yama Tatsu: You people really are pathetic you know that! Jimmie **English Voice again** Christ, your not going to kill me are you! Yama pulls back and kicks him square in the face knocking the retard into the water. Yama chuckles to himself again. Before hearing the an angry female voice call out from behind him. Sheena: Your going to pay for the pain you have caused me today Yama Tatsu! Bruce Salami him… Yama Turns his head to see the midget creeping round from the side of the house carrying a hefty Salami stick! He slowly moves up to Yama, back kinda hunched and dragging the heavy Salami stick along beside him. Bruce lets out a violent roar and runs at Yama raising the Salami stick above his little head. Suddenly everything starts to happen in slow motion as General Tso burst through the open French doors. We focus in on Bruce’s face as the traditional slow motion sounds can be heard, however we then switch to a close up of General Tso and nothing can be heard, yet his lips are moving. Bruce is drawing dangerously close to his target, while Yama wants to move, but can’t, he is paralysed with fear (and quite frankly a homosexual midget about to crack my head open with a stick of Salami would scare the crap out of me too). General Tso seeing the only chance to save Yama leaps forward, taking the blow of the Salami stick and sending him spiralling into the pool. Yama looks into the pool and then back at the Midget, a look of anger and disgust on his face. Yama immediately punts the dwarfed queer a good 15 feet back. He then turns and quickly dives into the pool and fishes out his friend to some near by steps of the pool. Not far from them in about 5 feet of water Jimmie is yelling to be saved as he is almost drowning. Yama Tatsu: Are you ok my little friend? General Tso: … Yama Tatsu: But how can win without you? General Tso: … Yama Tatsu: Right! Rest easy my little friend, your words say it all! Bruce, now standing with Sheena, is taken back a little by this frightening scene. He leans over and whispers to Sheena. Bruce: That guy is crazier than we are! He thinks his tasty little friend is answering him! Sheena: Bruce this is no time to think about sex… But your right! Bruce: Shut up you slut! What were you trying to do upstairs before Yama flipped your wig! You know what I think you should be content already. I mean its unfair you get our fearless leader! Sheena: Bruce, it doesn’t matter, Mikey isn’t gay! Bruce: In my dreams he is… A sour look appears on the stunted gay man’s face. Yama picks up the Salami stick General Tso was carrying and slams in into his other hand. He stares straight at Bruce and Sheena and doesn’t even bother to blink. Yama Tatsu: Lets do this shit! I will make you pay for injuring my friend! Bruce: Big words, bring it! Sheena ready the secret weapon. Sheena: Certainly! Yama Tatsu: What have you got Chrome dome! Sheena reaches into her cleavage and pulls out a photograph. A strange photograph. She looks at it with lust for a moment and then turns it to show the camera and Yama Tatsu. What is revealed is a nude photo of the Queen and Andy Griffith, aka Matlock, at a nudist camp in Jamaica. In the background a sign reading ‘Welcome to Sunny Jamaica’ is barely visible. We focus back on Sheena now. Sheena: Every girls got her own fantasy! Sheena gives a seductive wink, as the faces of both Bruce and Yama drop with revolt at the sight of the image. Yama: That’s sickening! But never mind, it is now time for battle! Yama steps forward and swings his Salami roll at Bruce, Bruce sidesteps and lunges back with his Salami stick, it just misses Yama. The two move into each other and force their weapons against each other before pushing off each other. The Midget and the Asian continue to battle as if one was Luke Skywalker and the other Darth Vader, like Robin Hood and the Sheriff of Nottingham, perhaps even like David and Goliath in you wanted to go to an extreme. After a minute or so both men were beginning to tire and their Weapons were almost at breaking point. Yama leaned against the wall of the house and Bruce knelt on one knee. Yama started looking around trying to see where that Chrome dome she devil had gone. He looked down to see her unzipping his pants. Yama Tatsu: Dammit! Ok… If I let you do it will you leave me alone so I can fight? … The sounds of sucking have already begun. I think it would be appropriate if we do a time skip….. Ok we are now 10 minutes a head in the future. Yama Tatsu is back leaning against the wall and Bruce is standing waiting impatiently. Bruce: Ok sugar bum… I mean my archrival! Lets does this! Yama Tatsu: Sure! The two charge at each other for the last time. The two Salami sticks clash against each other. Yama avoids Bruce’s swing and strikes back with a swing of his own. Yama’s strike breaks his Salami stick clean across the top of Bruce’s head. Bruce falls to the ground stunned and hurt. Yama Tatsu: It’s over! Get your worthless team out of here Sheena! Don’t make me hurt you… You’ve been embarrassed enough! Jimmie, looking rather like a drowned rat sulks like a little kid as he picks up Bruce and they walk back to their stolen Taxi. Sheena gives Yama a sexual wink and blows him a kiss. Yama: Get out of here! Yama looks over poolside where a recovering General Tso sits. General Tso: … Yama Tatsu: Word! Fade out.
