Journal Entry #1 Scorpion CCW Promo #80 Date: 3-11-03 Location: US Air flying over the United States Journal Entry #1 I’ am writing this to you on a plane heading to my home in New York. It’s pretty close to midnight now, though I can’t tell by going through different time zones what time it is now. I’m heading home after a battle of revenge that I took place in, avenging the death of my fallen friend Drake Rizzel. There isn’t a second that goes by without me thinking of Drake. It’s been a long two weeks for me. I still continue to wish I took Drake out of the street life before it was too late. But he still had unfinished business there with people who backstabbed him. He stayed and I left. Our two paths went opposite ways now and I continue to wish I could have steered him the right way. I ask myself the same painful questions every single night I lay down in bed since Drake died. “Why did it take his death to make me think about saving him?” “I wish I could have convinced him when I left to come with me, why didn’t I persuade it?” “Why can’t it be me and not Drake?” It’s painful to still think he is gone, I just hope he is smiling upon me for getting the rightful revenge on that bastard Corky. Save me a seat up their buddy. I remember what made me get out of the street life as I write this. The biggest impact on my life to get out of the streets was when my son Toby was born. My wife wanted me to get out of the streets I wouldn’t budge; I couldn’t leave the gang or the streets. But I remember standing in the hospital, holding my son in my arms and he was looking at me with his blue eyes. That’s when I knew it would be good for my family and most importantly myself if I wanted to live, to get out before I was six feet under. I remember telling Drake the day after my son was born that I need to leave as soon as possible. At first he was stunned because I was his only family, both of his parents passed away and he was left alone. He felt at first that I was turning my back on everyone, that I was gutless. I told him that I needed to live for my Wife and son he eventually gave in and knew that I felt strongly of my decision. He knew that I loved my family more then anything in the world. I remember the last words he said to me before I left: “I wish I could do the same Scorpion, I wish I could leave. But I’m like a drug addict and everytime I move away the drug keeps calling me back. I respect your decision and I’ll miss you man. You’re like a brother to me.” I looked into his eyes and I could see the water in his eyes getting heavy. He was visibly upset and felt I was leaving him. In a way I was. After that Drake and I barely talked. He would call once in a while; I would call once in a while. We were on two different sides of the fence and there was no way one of us was leaving our posts. I’m beginning to land, going back home to my family. Returning to my normal life, coming back after I returned from the life I used to live for a short time. Only to avenge Drake’s death and I know Drake would do the same for me. I hope this is the last time I don’t have to deal with my past. I can only hope. When my time comes and my legacy has passed, I can only hope that when I get to heaven that I can sit next down to Drake. The way I wish I could’ve been along, sitting in lazy boys, drinking a bud and talking about anything that bugged us at the time. Till that day happens I’m going to live life to the fullest. Rest in peace Drake, I miss you bro. ~END~ View Scorpion 's Biography Back
