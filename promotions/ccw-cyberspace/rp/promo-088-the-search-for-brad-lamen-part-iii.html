THE SEARCH FOR BRAD LAMEN PART III Koriann CCW Promo #88 Date: 03/16/03 Location: *shrugs* To the dismay of millions of people, we now find ourselves rejoining Koriann as she embarks upon her quest to find Brad Lamen. Our 'heroine' is climbing the back stairs of the tobacco shop where she believes Brad Lamen resides. Why does she think this? Well, she has this address written down on a piece of note paper. Little does she realize that this ISN'T Brad Lamen's address. No, this is something far worse... Koriann reaches the top of the stairs and knocks... Rather impatiently. She waits about one second for the room's occupant to answer the door, and then pounds again. Scant moments later, the door opens up. And as it swings open... Koriann: BRAD! How great it is... to... * Standing in front of her is one of her 'friends' back from the days when she wrestled in the Las Vegas mud pits for the entertainment of all to see. The woman in front of her is about 5'2'... currently 5'6' in her 4' spike heels... Weighs about 95 pounds... And looks anorexic as all hell. She has short black hair that's chopped rather stylishly. Koriann and the woman lock eyes, and a instant later, the black haired woman cries out... Black-Haired Woman: BITCH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ... And leaps forward to throttle Koriann. Let's pause the action here to explain a little bit. This woman's name is Carla Miller. As mentioned, she once upon a time worked with Koriann. We all know how skilled Koriann is at making friends. She had the same effect on Carla as she has on most people. One night in Vegas, Koriann was scheduled to face Carla in that evening's 'Mud Bowl Main Event'. The match was rigged so that Carla would win the fight, and then propose and get married to her wealthy fiance who was going to be in attendance that night. As the match was scheduled to start, Koriann made a personal decision... One that changed her life forever. She decided that she wasn't going to take one more embarrassing scripted loss to these floozies. And so what happened? Carla was soundly thrashed in the mud pit. Koriann kicked the crap out of her, and then went on to propose in her place. Needless to say, Carla's fiancé wasn't amused. He brushed off Koriann and approached the edge of the mud pit to see if his honey was okay. As he bent over the pit, Koriann charged him from behind and nailed him with the Thump! He tumbled head first into the mud pit, landing back first on one of Carla's knees. There was an audible snap, and suddenly, Carla's fiancé found himself paralyzed for the rest of his natural life. Koriann quit the mud pits shortly after that. The reason she had Carla's address in her purse is because, just last week, Koriann had managed to track her down, and was planning on driving on over to her apartment and spray painting the message: 'HOW'S THE CRIPPLE? HAW-HAW!' on the side of the building. Before she got on the plane to [insert generic city name here], she had mistakenly grabbed Carla's address instead of Brad Lamen's. And being the not-too-bright person that she oftentimes is, Koriann had forgotten all about that fact... Until now. Resume Action. Carla flies through the air and tackles into Koriann, causing both of them to flip end-over-end and fall down the stairs. They both land in a heap at the bottom, arms and legs tangled together. Separating, they both get to their feet and... Oh, look! It's time for bed! *shrug* Oh well, we'll continue this some other time. Cut camera. View Koriann 's Biography Back
