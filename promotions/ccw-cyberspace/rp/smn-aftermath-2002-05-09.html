What this is A short challenge in which Mr. Gobstopper, leaving a hospital room, calls out The Supreme Machine and Ricky Thunder.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mr. Gobstopper Entitled: SMN Aftermath... Written in Thursday, May 9, 2002, 01:21:44 Location: A hospital room This is a: Challenge *Scene opens showing Mr. Gobstopper sitting on the edge of a bed in a hospital room. He's wearing street clothes but has many bruises and a taped up arm. The nurse has stepped out of the room to get the release papers. Gobstopper sits and stares into space before realizing he's left reality.* Gobstopper: Well it seems like those two little shits have it in for me. The Supreme Machine and Ricky Thunder. Two sons of bitches. Ya know they come out, put on a show, and team up on me. They hurt me. They beat me. They battered me. BUt you know what? I'm still here! SuMa failed to put me out. He failed to send me packing. He failed to realize he cannot defeat me. He may have won our match, but he will NOT win the war he and that little scab of a man, Ricky Thunder, have started. You see, I have a proposal. Since SuMa's an ass, and Thunder is a coward, I figured we could put them two in a ring. Not against each other, but against me. Against me and a partner of MY choosing. I know a lot of guys in the back don't care for my return, but you know what? Oh fucking well. I will find a partner, and if I don't, then I'll take on SuMa and Thunder all by my slap happy lonesome assed self. For you see, I'm playing the trump card. And it's all going to boil down to me, SuMa, and my foot up his ass. So how about it guys? Do you accept my challenge? I'm sure we'll be seeing more of each other soon enough, so let me know, will ya? *The nurse comes back in and hands Gobstopper his release papers. He stands up, grabs his bags and heads toward the door, then stops.* Gobstopper: Be careful guys, I'm coming. *Gobber leaves. Fade out.* View Biography
