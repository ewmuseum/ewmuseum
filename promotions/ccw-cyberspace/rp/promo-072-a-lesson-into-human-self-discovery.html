A lesson into human self-discovery Jason Dread CCW Promo #72 Date: 3/20/03 Location: Now in 3-D!!! As time grows nearer and nearer to the first CCW Spectacular since its reopening, Revolution, the same scene has never strayed from the head of Jason Dread. It's as if something controls an unstoppable movie reel which plays the entire scene over and over again. It was only a few weeks ago, but with what has been gone through, a few weeks can seem like an eternity. The scene of Jason Dread versus that off the World Champion, Blayze. JD: Dread is calling for the No Moment! BJS: The what? Don't tell me it's just another sharpshooter used by anyone who is able to step through a pair of legs and cross them over one another... JD: Not in the least, Blackjack. Dread picks up Blayze's legs, and turns his back...wait a second! Lamen is standing to his feet! God dammit he was fooling us all with being knocked out...so he wouldn't have to count the pins by Dread! As Lamen stands to his feet, Dread immediately drops Blayze and goes right for the Union Rep. Lamen is ready however, and takes a hard swing at Dread, which connects and rocks Dread's foundation. Lamen hits another one, which brings Dread down to one knee, but yet he still stand to his feet...and screams for more! With a scream, Brad Lamen reaches back with all he has and swings right for Dread's chin...but Dread ducks the punch, and Lamen's momentum carries him a full half turn around! Dread quickly locks Lamen up in the Cobra Clutch! JD: Look out! With his back turned to Blayze, Dread has absolutely no clue that the World Champion had struggled his best up to his feet, and right as Dread locked on the Cobra Clutch, Blayze grabs Dread from behind in an inverted rack...the set up for... BJS: ...THE CRITICAL BEATDOWN ON DREAD! Happy days are here again! As Blayze connects with the Inverted Death Valley Driver, Lamen has already hit the mat in a counting position for the pin. Blayze rolls Dread over to his back, and hooks his far leg while Lamen makes the count: 1.. 2.. 3!!! ~~~ That same mocking tone that came from that damnded ring bell never ceases to relieve itself of my head, no matter how hard I try. Sometimes it only seems a matter of getting quieter and quieter...but every time I think it all is gone, it comes back to me threefold stronger. I've tried with everything to figure out what it may be, but all I have are books to go by. The last friend I had is slowly letting himself decay...but for right now he's stable. He's not my main concern...for right now. Without question I've come to the conclusion of what the hell is going on with me. Since I no longer bring myself to physical self-punishment anymore my mind has taken the liberty to attack my, inflicting much worse than physical pain. I've never been one to call myself or act as a perfectionist, so I don't know why I've degraded slowly into what mental state I am now. Don't get me wrong, I'm sane on all levels...it's just that I guess...I can't handle being screwed over as much as I thought I could. It's happened so many times before in the past. Hell, it's almost inevitable for the latter to happen at least several hundred times in a decent-spanned career. The only reason I believe this time rings truer than all the others is that now, every action I take is critical. Any last resort that could be taken is exactly that...my last... Too strong are my convictions that I absolutely need this win which might come in two weeks...as much as I strive for everything physical that win would bring...I'm also frightened. Yes...I said that I am indeed frightened; but not of just the loss, per se...I am afraid... ...of myself. When your body and soul have almost a life of their own as I've noticed mine does, you begin to fear what actions you will take if either is unsatisfied. I know what I'm capable of, I've been down that road...but you always have to remember what is said: Once a road has been transversed once, one will venture deeper into the thick underbrush, exploring new territory. Would I be able to handle this new territory? Would I be able to live past what I'd put myself to...both consciously and unconsciously?! ... I know all this can't be normal for people to experience...and there are always times I question whether or not I came to this world for a purpose. Perhaps I think way too much...ignorance IS bliss in the long run...but whenever this question is posed, there is always a song that I think of. The writers, the instruments, the names, and the politics are all unimportant compared to what it says...and it's amazing how so few words can speak volumes: Better it would be for me And better it would have been Had I not been born, not grown Not been brought into the world Nor had come to this earth Not been suckled for the world My god...is this all reality? Or is this just my perception... View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
