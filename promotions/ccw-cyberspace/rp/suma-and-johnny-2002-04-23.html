SuMa And Johnny

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Ricky Thunder talks with his aunt in an Australian cafe and responds to SuMa and Jupiter.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: SuMa and Johnny Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 11:37:13 Location: Perth, Australia This is a: Interview *Scene opens up in a small backwoods cafe as Ricky is flipping through a CCW magazine. A woman comes over and sits down across from him and stares. He looks over the top of the magazine then into the eyes of the woman. Her stare never shifts and Ricky never blinks.* Woman: Did you hear the comments from Jupiter? Ricky: Yes. Woman: Did you hear the challenge from SuMa? Ricky: Yes. Woman: And? Ricky: And what? What do you want me to say? How do you want me to react? Should I be so proud as to maintain silence? I have never maintained silence when it has affected my career. I will speak on this when I am ready to. Woman: Ricky, I am only bringing this up because... Ricky: Beacuse we're family. because you care. Because you don't want to see me hurt. Becasue you don't want to see me end up like my brother? Which one is it? Woman: Ricky, you are my nephew, I am trying to look out for you. I only wanted ot talk, if you were ready to. I will let you be. However, remember that I will be here if you want to talk about it. *Ricky goes back to flipping through his magaizine. After about 15 minutes had passed, he slams the magazine down on the table, and glares out the window and begins speaking to himself.* Ricky: I listened to John talk all his shit about wanting to challenge me. Why? Not because he thinks I am a worthy competitor, because he wants to get at SuMa. I was attacked last night by Carnage. Why? Again, because he wants to get at SuMa. Maybe John was right, maybe I did do a poor job of selecting a tag team partner, maybe I did. However, that will be for me to decide when the time comes. *Ricky grabs his cup of coffee and takes a few sips from it before staring out across the empty cafe.* Ricky: SuMa. You challenge John for the Independent title? Did you not remember, I am the number one contender. Do you want the title that bad? If so, then take it! That title means piss to me anyhow. But remember what I told you SuMa! Remember what I made you promise. If ever our paths should cross in the ring....just remember. Whether it be you, John, Kensuke, Neurosis or the God damned Pope, I don't care. I am coming for that title. I don't want it, I only need it. You see, this title is my first stepping stone on my way to greatness, I will stop at nothing to achieve my goals. *Ricky gets up from the table and heads outside. There is a small Koala clinging to a branch with its mother nowhere to be found. Ricky watches for a while before finally, after a desperate attempt to hold on, the Koala falls.* Ricky: That looked familier. A lot like someone's career. Isn't that right Johnny? You got to the top, and once there realized that you had nothing to hold onto, no lifelines. YOu plummeted, back to the bottom of CCW. Holding that indy title as if it were some kind of prize. Well John you decide what to do, if you want to put the title up for grabs against SuMa, be my guest. However SuMa, if you are to win that title, I am still coming for it. I will prove that I am better than my brother, I will prove I do not need SuMa here, I will show the world why I am still undefeated. The warning has been issued, and a promise will be kept. Who will be the next to feel the Thunder? View Biography
