Lightning - All Read- The Lightning Effect

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Challenge-type RP in which Lightning makes his CCW debut and challenges anyone to a match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lightning Entitled: All Read- The Lightning Effect Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 15:37:18 Location: Lightnings Locker Room, Civic Centre This is a: Challenge (The camera pans backstage, until it sees an open door. It enters the room, and sees Lightning. He's wearing a red t-shirt, with a gold lightning bolt on it, and black trousers. He has shades on, but removes them as the camera enters. On the wall, there is a picture of him holding the EWF Cruiserweight title belt. Lightning begins to speak.) L: Tonight- my debut night in the CCW. I'm not wrestling yet... but I will be soon. That's why, anyone in this arena watching this, I'm challenging you to a match. (From the crowd, someone shouts 'You Suck'. This turns into a chant. Lightning waits till the crowd stops, before carrying on.) L: I don't care where, when, or what stipulation our match is. I just want a match. And anyone who thinks i'll be easy... you are wrong. So... step forward... for the Lightning Effect. (Lightning stands up, and pushes the camera back, until it's outside the door. He closes the door, and the camera shows that on the front, it has Lightning written in gold, with a gold lightning bolt. 'Jack a%?' chants arise from the crowd.) View Biography
