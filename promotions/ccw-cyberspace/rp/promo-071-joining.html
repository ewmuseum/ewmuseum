Joining... Melody Widmark CCW Promo #71 Date: 03/10/03 Location: CCW Corporate Headquarters Who makes us what we are? Who decides what we will look like, our likes and dislikes, who we love and who we hate? Is it nature... different levels of genes and chromosomes which make up all that we are? Is there a God or some higher power that determines all of it? And in the end, does it even matter. We are who we are. We grow up to be the kind of people we turned out to be. Did the society where one grew up and the influence of one's parents make a difference? Definitely. But how does any of that determine who or what you love? Who knows? But as CCW's newly recruited rookie, Melody Widmark, realizes... She really has no business asking these questions. She is who she is. She loves what she loves. This new stage of her life started rather recently... Wrestling for a federation, that is. And it all began in the office of CCW's Commissioner Mabius Raymond. As with nearly every potential candidate, there are two steps to claiming a job in CCW. The first step, you go through an interview with the president. The second step, if he likes you, you sign the contract. Very few people have to take an additional third step. But Melody does. That step is an interview with the commissioner. And so there she is, sitting in his office in front of his desk. Raymond, himself, is behind the desk her file in his hands. He flips through it, pausing in places, reading over the president's notes, and glancing at Melody's so-called 'resume'. The silence stretches on... Melody becoming slightly impatient. After all, it's not like there aren't other federations she could try her luck in. CCW was the first. There were probably many more who would want to take her in. But before she can formulate a plan of action to go with those thoughts, Raymond puts down the folder and looks her straight in the eyes. Raymond: Well, Ms. Widmark... You are quite the... Interesting... Individual. Do you have any idea why Sylkk sent you to see me? Melody: I might have some idea, sir. Raymond: If you might think it has to do with the fact that you are homosexual... A 'lesbian' if you will... You would be absolutely correct. Melody's prom queen-like face crinkles at those words. Melody: What does that have to do with... Raymond: *interrupting* My dear, our good president is racist. Especially when it comes to people who blatantly flaunt their homosexual ways. Melody: *taken aback* I don't flaunt... Raymond: *interrupting again* You are very forward, open and blunt about your opinions and sexuality. And I must tell you, Ms. Widmark, our president finds it quite disgusting. As do I. Here at CCW Enterprises, we like to promote a healthy image. We are a family show you understand. Some of our viewers might not take too kindly to your presence, nor your type of 'entertainment'. Melody's face falls, along with her hopes of getting into CCW. But she is a prideful girl, and as Raymond just mentioned, she isn't afraid of letting her opinions be known. Melody: What about Lacey Skye Morris? She disrobes in the ring nearly every other show. In fact, I've seen her topless so often, I could describe what she looks like from memory! Every soft curve of her beautifully molded chest. The way she breathes in and out... Her breasts swaying with every motion she makes... Raymond: That's quite enough, Ms. Widmark. You made your point. That is a situation I'm working to fix as we speak. The president believes that she brings in the ratings. And at the moment, I am inclined to agree with him. But the fans are fickle. They could turn on her at any moment. And once they do, she will lose much of her creative freedom. You, on the other hand, I don't think the masses will approve of. You see, the men want women who they can fantasize about. They like to picture themselves having a healthy fantasy with a Lacey Skye or a Devyl or a Sheena Ramone. Your anti-male shtick won't go over very well. Melody lets all this information sink in, but she never once breaks eye contact with Raymond. Melody: Well then, with all due respect Commissioner, why the hell am I sitting in this meeting if you've already dismissed me outright? Raymond: I never said that, Ms. Widmark. I was just informing you of this company's policy regarding people like yourself, and mine and the president's feelings as well. However, since CCW is still only in it's opening stages, there are a lot of kinks to be worked out. The president and I don't always see things eye-to-eye. This is one of those situations. He doesn't believe you should wrestle here. But since I am the Commissioner here in CCW, I have a final say over all applicants. And I believe we can work out an arrangement that is mutually beneficial for both of us. Melody: So what you're saying is... I go along with whatever you think is best, and then I can wrestle? I'm sorry, but I don't plan on letting anybody inhibit me in my matches. When I go out there and fight, I plan on enjoying my battle AND my opponents to my satisfaction, not yours. I'm not going to change my ways just because you think they're taboo. When I go out there and wrestle, I plan on having multiple orgasms in every single match I'm booked in. I will humiliate and degrade each and every one of my opponents. And if I want to, I'll tie them up and make them my personal little bitch-slaves. So, Mr. Raymond... Melody stands up. Meldoy: I appreciate you taking the time to see me, but you can take your restrictions and... Raymond: *interrupting* Ms. Widmark, you should let me finish. By 'arrangement' I don't plan on restricting you to any extant. In fact, all of that sounds quite wonderful... If used against the correct individuals. I would especially love to see you degrade Lacey Skye Morris like that. And in fact, that's where I think I'll have you start. If you can do that... If you can impress me, I can see you going places here. Melody: I don't plan on being your 'flunky' or your little 'side-kick', Commissioner. I'm my own person. I won't play your games, but... *smiles in anticipation* I definitely want to get inside the ring with 'CCW's Resident Hottie'. Raymond: Then I believe our business here is finished. Welcome to CCW, Ms. Widmark. I'm sure you'll enjoy your tenure here. Melody: *smiles* Thank you, Mr. Raymond. Melody moves to exit and pauses in the doorway, turning back to the Commissioner. Melody: You know... For a man... You're pretty decent. Don't do anything to change my opinion of you. Raymond: *smiles broadly* No fears there. Just take care of Lacey Skye Morris. Everything else will tend to itself. Melody nods and finally leaves the office. Raymond's smile melts into a sly grin... One that stays on his face for quite awhile after Melody leaves. Fade out...
