It Started With 5

25 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Ricky Thunder meets a teenage fan in Times Square and discusses being split from The Supreme Team.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: It started with 5 Written in Thursday, Apr 25, 2002, 10:13:27 Location: New York City This is a: Match RP The scene fades in as Ricky Thunder is standing in the middle of Times Square. He is looking around with a confused look on his face. He finally locates what he is looking for and takes a trip across 43rd street to WWF New York. He gets to the doors and is met by security guards. Guards: Can we help you sir? Ricky: I was just going to step inside and grab a bite to eat. Guards: Unfortunately that can't happen. We are closed due to a private party. Ricky: Damn! I was hoping ot get in to at least look around. Guards: Sorry sir. Maybe try back tomorrow. Ricky: Yah, maybe I will. Ricky walks away from the restaraunt and heads back to the center of Times Square. He ends up at a small sidewalk cafe and decides to grab a seat. As he is sitting there, watching the people go by, a kid about 14 years old, comes over and sits down across from him. Ricky looks at the boy then takes his sunglasses off, and the kid starts to speak. Kid: Sup man? I'm Jason. Ricky: Ricky. Jason: I know. By the way, don't feel bad about WWF New York. They are always turning people away due to private parties. Sad part is, mostly done by WWF wrestlers. Like right right now, I heard the party going on in there is Dwayne's mom's birthday. He wanted to have it there for some reason. Ricky: Dwayne? The Rock? Jason: Yah whatever. Anyhow, whats up? How's CCW treating you? Aside from the major fuck job they did to you. Ricky: Lottery my ass. First they put me against Ebola, in the KoE semi-finals and then Carnage wants to come down and try to fuck me over. Then they TEAM me with Ebola in the Group Therapy tournament. Jason: I am talking about what they did to The Supreme Team. Ricky: Man this is such bullshit! First off, I have to face SuMa AND Chang in the FIRST MATCH OF THE NIGHT! Then I may very well have to face him again, in the KoE finals. Where not to mention I will once again, face Ebola! I mean I go undefeated and now CCW does everything in its power to watch me fall. Well, it isn;t going to happen. Not here, not now, not ever. Ricky Thunder is the real deal. This is my chance to prove that! Jason: Yah, I feel ya. But what's up with The Supreme Team? How are you guys coping with it? Ricky: We haven't even really thought about it. I mean yeah, we have thought about it, who wouldn't? But we haven't talked about it. I mean SuMa, and Chang manage to wind up on the smae team, and I get put across the ring from them. Damn the bad luck. Jason: Have you talked to Ebola or Jack? I mean they're not exactly pushovers. Ricky: No. I have nothing to say to them. They are probably in on this ploy to destroy the Supreme Team. CCW is scared of us. They hate the thought of us running things. I mean look at Jack Panzer, he is pathetic. He just now reappeared on the scene, and he thinks that he is going to be ready to face Suma! Give me a break. Then I look back to last Monday night and I have watched the battle between Ebola and me, several times. The only good I have seen in that match was me riding that steel chair like a magic carpet. Jason: Speaking of which. What's up with that? I thought you were a proverbial face? Ricky: Regardless of my stance, I am going to let CCW know you can not walk all over me. I am there to fight. I will do whatever it takes to get the 'w'. Jason: Well, I will leave you alone after one more question. I saw your interview with SuMa the night of his return, do you think he is going to fight you? He says he is supposed to protect you. He is almost a foot taller then you , and about 100 pounds heavier. He is a monster. Ricky: He will fight. Jason: You sound confident in that. How can you be certain? Ricky: SuMa. The Supreme Machine. CCW hall of famer. He WILL fight me. He will not back down. He owes this to me. He owes this to my family. Everything that has happened in the past is done. Everything that has come and gone, stays gone. I don't care what he promised my brother, I don't care if he promised to NEVER lay a hand on me. The fact of the matter is he lied to him. When Thunder won the World title, he won it from SuMa. Do you remember that? Jason: Yah! That was a great match! Ricky: BULLSHIT! SuMa jobbed that title to Thunder. We both know what SuMa is capable of, and that wasn't it. It seems funny that SuMa fought his heart out and yet could not manage a win, however, when time came for the rematch SuMa destroyed my brother. He lied to my brother. That was the only lie he had ever told him. Until now. Jason: Until now? Ricky: You see, SuMa promised my brother two years ago he would look out for my better interests, and now, CCW is forcing this man to break a promise to my brother. SuMa offered to step out of the tournament, but no. I would not allow that. He will get in that ring and kick the living shit out of me in need be. He will do whatever it takes, to move on and so will I. If one of us is to make to Neurosis, I will be just as happy either way. CCW is trying to pit us against each other and it will not work. Jason: Word. That's fucked. Well I am gonna bounce yo. I'll be watching from the stands, good luck to both of you. Ricky: Later man. Ricky gets up and heads over to the counter and orders a medium black coffee. He take sit back to his table and sits down, where he tastes the coffee and spits it out. Ricky: This shit is horrible! Or maybe it is that CCW has spoiled my taste for life. You see by teaming me with my enemies to face my friends you think you hve done something wise. You think you have driven a stake into the heart and soul of The Supreme Team. However, what you failed to realize is this. We will live on, we have spent our lives together fo rthe most part. SuMa and Thunder, my brother, were very close friends, and for you forcing him to break his promise, I assure you, there will be hell to pay. But in the meantime, CCW, watch the havok you have created. For Team Supreme, will not be stopped. Prepare to feel the Thunder! View Biography
