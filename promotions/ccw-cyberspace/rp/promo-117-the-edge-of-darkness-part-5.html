The Edge of Darkness: Part 5

25 March 2003

The Edge of Darkness: Part 5 Devyl CCW Promo #117 Date: 03/25/03 Location: Preacher's Corners We find ourselves in a simple bedroom... Wooden floors, wooden walls, a single window on the right hand side of the room, a wooden door on the left. A rickety bed, an old termite infested night stand and several decaying book shelves spartanly fill the room. It is peaceful and silent for a brief moment. Until... David Oakes and Shadow burst into the room, the door flinging open in front of them. Once entered, David turns around and slams the door shut by throwing his full body weight against it. Shadow immediately moves to the window, hoping it will provide them with an escape option. Shadow: I hope your wife knows what she's doing in there. David: I wouldn't worry much, Shadow. She's taken care of herself far longer than you would think. Besides, I've witnessed the aftermath of some of her battles... Rooms of people that didn't stand a chance. Shadow: Yes, but those were humans. People with souls who know fear and could be manipulated. I think that for all of Devyl's vaunted power, this is a slightly more hazardous situation. Why else would she have told us to run? David: Because she never wants us around when she does her thing. Now, where does that window go? Shadow gazes out the window and looks about. Shadow: It looks clear from here. Come over here and give me a hand with the window pane. David leaves the door and takes a position besides Shadow. Bracing their bodies against the wall, they use their leverage to force the stuck windowpane upwards, letting in the cool night breeze. David: There. Piece of... David is interrupted mid-sentence as the door on the to the room suddenly explodes open. The door is knocked off it's hinges and clatters to the ground. The object that knocked the door off it's hinges hits the floor and rolls to a stop near David's and Shadow's feet. It's Devyl... Unconscious. They look down at Victoria's unmoving form in shocked silence, then look up through the doorway. The two Zombies stand there, a greenish misty glow permeating from their eyes. They begin shuffling forward, the corner of their mouths raised in a sickly grin. Shadow: **under her breath** We are so screwed! David drops to his knees and checks for Devyl's pulse, which she does have. David: Vicky's still alive. Get through the window, Shadow! Hurry!!! As the two once-living creatures enter the room, Shadow ungraciously throws herself through the window, hitting the ground and rolling. David picks up Devyl, her perfume and erotic pheremones causing his head to swim. He had almost forgotten how powerfully alluring she was when in close contact with her. Doing his best to Shake the unbidden sexual thoughts out of his head, he tries to concentrate on survival. Outside, Shadow has regained her footing, and David moves to the window to hand Devyl off to her. David: Take her and run! I'll be right behind you! Shadow: I don't think I can carry her, David... David: Just do what you can! Now go! Shadow gets a hold of Devyl and carries her about four yards before letting her drop to the ground. She starts dragging/pulling Devyl as David starts climbing through the window. He gives one final look back and finds, much to his shock, that the zombies are impossibly closer than they were just a few scant seconds ago. They closer one reaches out and grabs a hold of David's ankle as he tries pitching himself out the window. There's a brittle sound as that of a dry twig snapping as the creature twists David's ankle, and Devyl's husband lets out an intelligible shriek of pain. Shadow pauses in her trek across the field with Devyl and looks back in David's direction. She sees him hanging out the window waist-up, his lower body still inside. He screams and struggles, and it looks like he's reaching for one of the loose boards glinting in the moonlit night. Shadow leaves Devyl on the ground and runs back to help David. David, for his part, kicks at the zombie, hoping to get it to release it's deadly grip on his now-broken ankle. He scores with several shots to the creature's head, all ineffective. The second creature moves around the first to grab David's other ankle. Noticing this, he starts fighting, twisting and turning to avoid having his other ankle broken, sadly injuring his already broken ankle even further. And then Shadow is there. She hands David the board and then grabs a hold of his shoulders and pulls as David swings. On the second try, David manages to score a direct hit it the Zombie's decaying arm, snapping it off at the wrist. Shadow, yanking with all her might, suddenly finds no more resistance and the two of them tumble outside to the ground. Looking up, they see the zombies reaching for them. But they are out of reach. Shadow helps David to his feet, throws one of his arm over her shoulders, and starts helping him off into the field. The zombies turn away from the window and move back into the cabin, assuredly moving to the front door to continue chasing their hurt quarry. David and Shadow takes several steps forward and then come up short... Devyl is rising back to her feet, and as she does so, the sky overhead lights up nearly as bright as day as lightning arcs across the sky, followed by the boom of thunder. David, knowing something is about to happen, throws himself on top of Shadow and knocks her to the ground. He glances up briefly to see Devyl rise to her full height, flickering and morphing back and forth in the sporadic lightning between herself and a demonic red gargoyle. He lowers his eyes, and covers Shadows head, forcing her into the ground so that she can't look up. And suddenly all is quiet... No insects, no birds, no wind, no thunder... As if a vacuum of silence had suddenly engulfed everything. The lightning continued to flicker overhead, lighting the darkness brief seconds at a time. Devyl stood her ground, glaring at the cabin... A look of hatred so pure and unadulterated, it made her beautiful and pristine features cold and ugly. And into the silence she spoke one word: 'Burn.' And sound suddenly resumed in full stereo as the cabin near the edge of the forest suddenly caught blaze, immediately engulfed in a towering inferno. The fire so hot and intense that David and Shadow had to move from their place on the ground to avoid being seared. They stumble by Devyl and make their way out to the road and their car. Devyl remains in front of the cabin, silhouetted, as the fire rages in front of her. And just as suddenly as the lightning started, the rains come. David and Shadow watch as Devyl stands in front of the cabin, not moving an inch, for another five minutes. She eventually turns and rejoins the duo at the car. Shadow: **hesitating, to Devyl** Are you okay? Devyl stops and turns back to face the burning cabin which is just now starting to subside. She answers, but does not look at David nor Shadow. Devyl: No. I will find the Angel Gabriel and flay the wings from his back. I do not like being baited into traps. David: I agree. Do you think we could get to a hospital? My ankle is killing me. Devyl confirms that they will be going with a subtle nod. Shadow helps David into the back seat of the car. She then walks around and enters the driver's side. Devyl enters the passenger seat shortly afterwards, and the car pulls off and away, leaving behind the still burning slag that was once a cabin. View Devyl 's Biography Back
