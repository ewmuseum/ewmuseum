Stormclouds Gathering part 5 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #60 Date: 03/04/03 Location: Seattle, Washington {Outside Jonathan's antique store, a group of workmen are just finishing installing a new plate glass window.} {Inside, Jonathan's studio in particular, Lacey is there with Jonathan. He is buried in an invoice at his desk. She stands near him, near enough so that there is no question that Jonathan is aware of her presence but he says nothing and continues to work. Finally he stops.} Lacey: You want to talk? Jonathan: If I wanted to talk I suppose I'd be talking. Lacey: I was stupid to fall for that thing with the cell phone. I'm so angry I could.... {Jonathan slams a paperweight down on the desk.} Jonathan: It's working. Lacey: What is? Jonathan: His plan! If I understand this correctly now, he's after you. He wants you so upset that you can't think straight and go looking for him. Lacey: I suppose you're right. {Lacey looks at him. He can't meet her eyes. He speaks with solemn gravity.} Jonathan: I know I am. Lacey, I want to leave. {Lacey nods her head in full agreement, a small smile of relief slipping across her features.} Lacey: It'll be best that way.... Good for you to get out of harms way. {Jonathan looks at her, his eyes narrowing.} Jonathan: Is that what you think? {Lacey has obviously missed something.} Lacey: Is that what I think? Jonathan: That I'm running out of here without you? Lacey: Yes.... That's what I think. Jonathan: You'd think that you, a woman, would at least know how to listen. Lacey: You SAID you wanted to leave. And I think you're right. I think it's for the best, Jonathan. Jonathan: I MEANT I want to leave. AND you.... Leave this place.... The TWO of us.... THAT'S what I meant. {The two of them look at each other for a moment.} Jonathan: {looking down} I know you're tough, Lacey. But you can't play the hero here. This is something for the police to handle, not some wrestling chick. {Lacey folds her arms across her chest and turns away from Jonathan, looking out the window across the room.} Jonathan: Let's go. We could be in Paris by tomorrow. Lacey: You think he couldn't find us in Paris? {Jonathan just looks at her. Lacey turns back to him, arms still folded across her chest.} Jonathan: What? Lacey: This guy is a pro, Jon. He could track us wherever he wanted. This isn't something I can just run away from. Jonathan: Lacey.... Stop being so God damn stubborn! You know that I care about you.... I sure as hell don't want to see you hurt. You can't fight this guy. We have to get ourselves into the witness protection program! {explodes in fury} Damn this guy! Damn everything! Lacey: I hate this too. But for the last time, I'm not running. Jonathan: {sighs, resigned} No.... Instead you'll get us both killed. {Lacey and Jonathan lock eyes, and a world of understanding passes between them....} View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
