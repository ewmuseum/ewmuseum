Becoming A Mexican Superhero

3 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class first-person roleplay in which Stimpo rescues a woman from armed robbers and is nicknamed the Mexican Crimefighter.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Stimpo Entitled: Becoming a Mexican Superhero! Written in Monday, Jun 3, 2002, 21:00:31 Location: Rochester, NY This is a: Interview It was that day that changed my life entirely, the day that I will never, ever forget.... 'Put all of it in there...NOW!' I could hear the terrible voice so clearly as I walked on my daily routine past the condemned building. That building has been condemned since 1965 I'd say, but now the voices have been heard for maybe a week now, but today's was different. 'You don't want this getting uglier than it already is sister, just put the money in the damn bag!!' Another man had said. After he said this I could faintly hear the loading of a gun and a whimper from tears of a lady not a day older than 21. After hearing the whimpering, which has never been heard before, I decided to take a chance, I headed toward the window to see what was going on and I saw it as clear as day. 'I'll give you nothing!!' Said the lady on her knees with rope around her hands still crying and whimpering for her life. Three men in black coats surrounded her, all armed with nine millimeter guns. I must admit, I didn't plan on doing anything at the time, I really didn't want to mess up my new pants I had boughten yesterday, but that was the least of my problems. 'Whaddya think your doing here sissy man?' Another man in a black coat was behind me with a loaded weapon also. He towered over my crouching self and kicked me straight through the window making me land on the dirt ground in the darkness of the room. Only one light was lit, a light over the lady held captive. 'Look what I found in the trash, a mesely little rat!' The man beckoned to his companions mocking me in my ways. 'Let her go!' I had no idea why I said this, just thought it was something a 'hero' would say. I felt another kick behind me which then knocked me to the ground. That's when I saw somewhat of a salvation, a small pocket knife slightly visible from under the dirt. Being sneaky, I took the knife and hid it under my stomach. 'Time to put this critter out for some time!' The man behind me must have slammed the butt end of the gun smashing into the back of my head because from I saw, it was all stars. The blow must've knocked me uncouncious. 'Boss...he's waking and we still don't got the money!' said a burly man from waht I could see, I was just waking from my head attack. It occured to me that my hands were tied to rope next to the lady under the spotlight. I then started to remember I picked up a knife just before getting hit, i felt around, and there it was in my front pocket near my chest. 'If you swear to spare this man's life, I will give you what you need...' Said the wimpering lady. 'Now were talking! alright, we'll spare both of you for the money...all of it! now tell us where it is!' said the 'Boss' angrily getting close to the crying lady who turns her head away from his hideous face and breath for that matter. 'In the safe in the back, the combination is....46-34......12.' The lady lets out an enourmous cry at the last number now knowing that she had given up all the money she must've been saving or earned one way or another. The three men behind the boss head right over to the safe and start turning the knob to the lady's instructions. Sure enough, the safe shoots open shooting dust upon dust out from the pressure of the safe opening. With the full safe open, inside is kept thousands of gold pieces and mounds and columns of dollar bills. 'Boss...I think you were right about the jackpot in here...' The thin man who hit me says drooling over the crazy amounts of money that is here, his eyes are practically lit up by the shimmering pieces of gold. 'Let me check this out...' The Boss heads over to the safe leaving both the lady and myself alone tied in the chairs. 'What about us??? You said you'd let us go!' Said the lady. 'I said i'd spare you, i'm not gonna kill you, i'll let time do that sweety.' Said the Boss slyly. 'Any swell ideas?' said the lady towards me. 'You could say that...' I said as I shook myself to make the little knife bounce up and onto my leg...when it impact my leg, it opened revealing the still sharp blade. 'Now turn around and grab it' I instructed the lady. 'Ok' She said and turns around in her chair slowly and grabs the knife and starts to cut herself free...she does after seconds of cutting then she ducks for the men not to see her then she went behind me and cut me free. 'Thank god for swiss army knives eh?' I said nodding my head. 'Now lets get out of here!' We ran as fast as we could, climbed the wall up to the window I landed through and exited... 'Where the hell did they go?!? BOSS!!! THEY'RE GONE!!' said the burly man in a fury, the Boss came running and steam seemed to be shooting out of his ears he was so angry. 'GO...FIND....THEM......NOW!!!' The boss screamed at his fellow guys and they all snagged their guns and ran after us... 'Hurry...they should be on our tails any time now...In here!' I pointed to two trash cans while I was running holding the lady's hand. 'We should be safe in here.' We jumped into each can and stayed out of sight...the three men passed the cans angrily not seeing both myself or the lady. We climbed out of the trash cans, looked around the corner and they were out of sight, we both leaned up against wall and let out a huge sigh of relief. 'You saved my life' The lady kissed me on the cheek. 'How can I ever thank you?' 'No need ma'm' I felt my cheek. 'I think that kiss was quite enough.' The lady giggled 'Why, i'm flattered you liked it.' She put her hand out to introduce herself. 'My name is Nina, Nina Sanchez.' I shook her hand 'Hey Nina.' our hands let go. 'Do you have a name or should i just call you my superhero?' the lady said 'Superhero? Me?? Nah i'm just a new wrestler in the CCW...how could you think i'm a superhero?' I said in confusion 'Well you did save my life didn't you? i'd say your some kind of crimefighter, and you look Mexican...you can be the Mexican Crimefighter named.....what?' 'The Mexican Crimefighter...Stimpo!' 'I like it! Hope to be saved by you again, Stimpo!' 'Same here...' That was the day to change my life, the day I will never forget. The day I became...Stimpo! -Fin- View Biography
