Death smiles at us all... Yama Tatsu CCW Promo #37 Date: today Location: General Tso's Funeral Death smiles at us all… … All you can do is smile back A sobering, empty cemetery is the venue for this meeting of Yama Tatsu. A service is currently on outside. Only one man and the Service man stand around a gravesite. We close in to find it to be Yama Tatsu. The fresh Gravestone reads “General Tso, Your Actions Spoke Louder Than Words… 1949-2003”. The service has only just begun. Clergyman: We are gathered here today to farewell a great human being… A beloved person, admired soldier, courageous leader, and a father figure to all lost youngsters… He was cut down in his prime… such a tragic way to die…ummmmm… errr, how did he die again? Yama Tatsu: ** Completely unaffected by the death of his friend ** A salami stick… The Clergyman barely holds back the little giggle that is obvious at such a reply. Yama shoots him an evil glare. Clergyman: Ohhh, That’s right. Death by Salami! This is a tragedy that no one should ever have to face again… But one cannot dwell in the past. We must look forward to knowing, he will be looked after by our heavenly father Yama Tatsu: He was Buddhist, and believed in reincarnation! Clergyman: **Slightly Embarrassed** Well, then Buddha will look after him in what ever form he takes… Oh well, I think I have said enough, would you like to say a few words… Yama Tatsu: No… I can’t say that I do have anything to say right now. Well, just rest easy my little friend! I will get vengeance this Thursday and I will win the Independent title in your honour… Yama bends down, picks up a hand full of dirt and tosses it into the hole. Yama then turns and walks away. From his formal dress suit pocket he with draws his cigarette packet. He proceeds to pull one out and light it up with a shiny looking ‘Zippo’ lighter. He rests on the back of a nearby head stone as he takes a few puffs and thinks quietly to himself. “How will I beat Michael Stevens? I am up against a team of four and I lost my only ally! Mind you Ginger Pubes team isn’t very intelligent. I bet if I go in with a good game plan and a few of the tricks General Tso taught me, I can, no scratch that, I will whip this team of Cock Jockey’s like midget on a donkey. They are going to be left standing in my wake… I want my Independent title back, I will get my Title back and when I do, I will demonstrate why I am the future CCW Champion! Isn’t it weird how I am ranting away to myself smoking this shitty cigarette? Especially considering I have a camera right in front of me, and they probably want to know what I have on my mind… Wait a minute who’s this!” A small dented Volkswagen Beetle, a V Dub, that’s sign written up as a courier car comes flying up the dusty, gravel road. It hits one huge dip and gets airborne. It draws closer with pace. Yama Quickly puts out his cigarette butt on the Headstone he has seated himself on. We spin around to see whose head stone it is. It reads “Here Lies Smokey Patch, Should Have Turned Off The Gas, Before He Struck The Match” (Of course it is a largish head stone and the writing is small ;)). Suddenly the car flies up to where Yama is seated, slams the brakes and slides sideways. He barely grinds to a halt just at the feet of Yama Tatsu. Yama doesn’t bother flinching, he believes he’s invincible and with his past one would believe so. The driver of this car is your usual surfy type. Wears a Hawaiian Shirt with his company logo hand sewed onto the front, sunglasses and long bleached blond hair. He leans over to his passenger seat and remerges with a small envelope. Surfy Delivery Dude: Yo dude! Your like Yama Tatsu right? Yama Tatsu: Can I help you? Surfy Delivery Dude: Like this bodaciously, tubular chick wanted me to give ya this! Yama Tatsu: What was her name? Surfy Delivery Dude: Like she didn’t give me one! Which I thought was really really weird but she was a major hotty… So I didn’t care! So here’s ya letter… The guy hands over the letter to Yama, who snatches it clean from his hands with out a care. The Surfy Delivery dude leans over again and pops back up with a clip board. Yama Tatsu: Sorry I don’t have a signature… I have no need for one! Surfy Delivery Dude: Wow! That’s like me… except in my case its cause I can’t write… I feel your pain… But dude, I can’t give you this letter in unless someone signs for it. Speedy Shore Delivery policy man! Yama Tatsu: Clipboard this… Yama shifts the clipboard in front of the Delivery boys face… Crack! Yama Punches clean through the clipboard and hits the delivery boy in the face. Yama Then raises his leg and starts to kick the guy in the face. He crumbles over into the passengers seat still strapped. Yama Tatsu: Should have been wearing you seat belt! Surfy Delivery Dude: Urrrrrrgggghhhhh! Yama Tatsu: Keep it down bleach boy! Yama Tatsu pokes his head in the window and gives the surfer another hook for good measure. He then looks into his hand. The letter is still there all scrunched up. He quickly opens it up and begins to read. “Hey Sexy, I have been dreaming of you since we last met. You may not remember me, but I sure remember you, I will see you on Thursday night. I will watch from the crowd in anticipation of your victory. C Ya! Your Not So Secret Admirier” Yama Tatsu: Freaky! I hate stalkers… It could be worst, it could be a Homosexual Midget named Bruce! I really need to stop wasting my time here and move onward to my match this Thursday. I will be facing a previous opponent and it looks like our CCW Union Rep is helping him out… Good thing I didn’t vote for him! Never the less those two cannot survive what I have in store for them! They both want what I want… The only thing is they can’t have it. That thing is success! I will come out of Lockdown victorious, I am certain of that! But by what means I do not know… All I know is that if Brad F’n Lamen steps one foot inside that ring, they will be burying him along side the General come Friday! I have a number of plans! All revolving in me winning!……… So who could be my secret admirer!?!? Yama looks down at the letter one more time. A look of uninterest comes across his face! He removes his Zippo and lights the edge of the letter. He drops the paper and it floats gently to the ground. The toxic smelling perfume that has been used on it makes it burn rather quickly as the camera’s focus is totally on the bit of paper. As we fade out we hear the final words spoken. “Ashes To Ashes… … Dust to Dust” View Yama Tatsu 's Biography Back
