What this is A challenge-class roleplay in which John Coens answers SuMa from his condo.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main John Coens Entitled: You getting hard of hearing SuMa? Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 22:33:10 Location: Sitting at home, one hand on the remote ... the other scratching his man area. This is a: Challenge - The camera spins around John's condo, giving the 360 on John. He's sitting there ... Molson Canadian in one hand, scratching his nuts with the other hand. A little scruff is starting to grow on his face, his hair is wetted back on his head. - John: SuMa ... what's this you're trying to say? You want ... ME? You want ME in a match. No ... you don't want me. You want my belt. I don't blame you. The only thing you ever cared about was the gold. And that's fine. That's great. But that's not the only reason to come out and kick someone's ass. Is it? Sometimes you just want to beat the hell out of someone for doing whatever it was they did. Maybe they crippled your fiance. Maybe they stole your 'ashes.' Who knows what calls for violence. Lord knows I don't. However, I know when I want to kick someone's ass. But you MUST be getting hard of hearing ... because I already said that I don't want you yet. I've got a couple priorities that rank above you. I'm taking care of a couple of them right now. - Camera reminds us that John is scratching his boys. - John: Once I finish up with this, and once I finish taking care of some other business ... then I will get to you. Until then ... stay out of my business, unless you want to become mine. View Biography
