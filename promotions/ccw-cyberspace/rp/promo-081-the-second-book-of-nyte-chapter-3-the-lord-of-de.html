The Second Book of Nyte: Chapter 3 - The Lord of Decay Gabriel Nyte CCW Promo #81 Date: 0.070.003/.M3 Location: The Black Library [We head into a very, putrid and vile section of the ancient knowledge. This seems to be the section where everyone takes steals books from, and rips the pages out of them. The place simply smells awful...it smells like hot sick ass in a dead carcass. On top of that, everyone seems to have a disease of some sort. Flesh eating virii, The common cold, Influenza...hell even acne! Gabriel seems to be the odd man out, considering that he's in a Biohazard suit. He's once again sitting at a desk...in very poor condition. It's on the verge of falling apart, and the top is littered with engraved graffiti. Gabriel looks up from reading. He slams the book shut, and pages go flying out of the diseased air that was pushed around. He walks toward the camera, but the camera seems to have an aura of fear.] Gabriel: Devyl, what the hell do you think you are doing? Putting me in a electrified cage match? You do not realize the rift in normal space that you would create. You would bring the destruction of the Earth, just to settle a petty rivalry with me? If that electric current his this daemonvessel...what will happen...WHOA~! [Gabriel then runs into a diseased follower of the Lord of Decay, known as a nurgling. He has many exposed wounds, and numerous visible infections...including one massive one where his nose used to be. Most of his clothes are torn.] Nurgling: HEY! Watch where you're going, clean one! Gabriel: Fuck off~! [Gabriel then delivers a thunderous punch to the face. However, it goes through his face to the mushy innards of the skull!] Gabriel: AWW! DUDE! SICK! uh...sorry? He'll give you a new face...for...a finger maybe? [Gabriel pulls his fist out of the new cavity formed from the punch. The Nurgling simply stumbles around, groping his way around the library. Gabriel looks to his fist, and sees that the blood is not red like ours, but an array of translucent greens, and transparent oranges, as well as pieces of skull, cartilage, and even brain. He shakes his fist off on the floor.] Gabriel: Fuckin Nurgle Followers. His symbol on my crotch hurts everyday. I wish he never found me. I would have been happy following the other three Gods...but I guess Nurgle had to come for the ride. [Gabriel exits the rotted and old portion of the Library. Into a clean up room, where he gets decontaminated. It bears tiles where the marks of the Undivided are adorned in abundance. Gabriel, after five minutes heads into a hallway, where the green paint is gradually turning blood red.] Gabriel: Now onto my opponent. Ace Hawk, the pain of the Front office. You can try to defeat me, but you can't. If you dare to throw me into that cage, you won't. Steel and Electricity are great friends, much like Slaanesh and Tzeentch. You will realize the futility of fighting against the awesome power of Chaos. There will be no stopping me from reaching my goal. Becoming a Chaos Champion! [Gabriel finally stops dead in the hall way, whose paintjob and lighting have turned from dark green, to a bright blood red.] Gabriel: Andrew...have you ever had your blood boil? Ever seemed like your skull is about to be ripped from your body? [The camera turns to show a giant icon of Khorne (See the right pectoral of Gabriel). Very artistically done, and bordered with red rope lights.] Gabriel: Thursday...you will know. {Fade} View Gabriel Nyte 's Biography Back
