Brian Gaines - Don't you people know who I AM??? (For Lockdown)

15 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which the Hollywood producer character Brian Gaines berates and fires his writing staff and studio employees before a Lockdown match against The Masked Mullet.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Brian Gaines Entitled: Don't you people know who I AM??? (For Lockdown) Written in Monday, Apr 15, 2002, 12:51:07 Location: HQ of Chosen Productions...in the heart of Hollywood, CA. This is a: Match RP Today is a big day for Brian Gaines, owner of Chosen Productions, a large production company located in Hollywood, California. Today he has been booked in his first CCW Matchup. This Thursday, he'll be grappling with The Masked Mullet on Lockdown. But, who exactly IS Brian Gaines? What are his reasons for entering CCW? And, why is he the single most hated man in Hollywood? You'll find all the answers to these questions, and more, when- 'Are you kidding me? You call that a promo?! That SUCKS!' 'But I-' 'No excuses for that rubbish!' And so, the scene fades from black. Seeing as his this is the debut promo of Brian Gaines, perhaps we'll take things a bit slow for the first time around. Several tired-looking individuals sit at a round wooden table, cluttered with all sorts of debris, from discarded scripts, to magazines, to empty chinese food containers. The room we are in is a small one, with various movie posters on the walls, save for one wall which is nothing more than a large window, overlooking a busy street in Hollywood, California on what looks to be a sunny day. Every single person sitting at this table seems rather miserable as they listen to another person scream at them. This is the voice of Brian Gaines, new CCW signee. The shredded, crumpled remains of a script are thrown down on the table with contempt. 'Just WHAT do I pay you people for, anyway?!' The voice can now be attached to a body, when its owner walks over to the window, and with his back to the city of Hollywood, pulls off a sleek pair of oval-rimmed black shades and glares at the table of people before him. Brian Gaines: 'Pay attention to me! Don't you worthless slaves know who I am? I'M BRIAN GAINES, AND I OWN YOUR LIVES!' Brian Gaines stands about 6'2', with a fairly athletic build. His hair is short, neatly styled to show off quite a bit of forehead, with a color to match his current temperment: a rusty orange/red. A well groomed goatee on his chin matches. This is Hollywood, afterall, and Brian Gaines is sporting attire appropriate to his calling - a big time Producer. He is dressed stylishly in (presumably Italian; possibly Gucci) black shoes, black slacks, a wide-collared burgundy silk shirt with the top button unbuttoned, and a black jacket to complete the outfit. Proverbially 'dressed to kill', the well dressed Brian Gaines scowls as he again begins to pace around the table. Brian Gaines: 'That was perhaps the single most mind-numbingly god awful intro I've ever heard! And you people call yourselves writers!!' 'We-we have others, Mr. Gaines.' Brian Gaines stops dead in his tracks, finding himself once again with his back to the large window. He almost seems surprised at the fact that another voice made its presence felt. Brian Gaines (turning slightly red): '....Which one of you said that?' Dead silence from the table of now-petrified writers. Brian Gaines: 'Which one of you said that?!' 'It-it-it was m-me, Mr. Gaines...' A nervous wreck of a man raises a shaking hand, the other one holding a smouldering cigarette to his mouth. 'Mr. Gaines' peers at the man as if he were a completely different species. Brian Gaines: 'Which one are you, again?' 'I, uh, I...' Brian Gaines: 'Your name. Tell it to me.' 'Reyes, sir. Kyle Reyes.' Brian Gaines: 'Oh right, Kyle Reyes. You'll have to forgive me. I only tend to remember the names of people who bear some significance to my life. Please, stand up.' Kyle Reyes: 'Huh?' Brian Gaines: 'I just told you to stand up, as in, get off your ass and STAND UP.' Like a schoolboy being scolded by his teacher, Kyle looks around nervously at the others, and slowly stands up, breathing heavily while doing so. Even while standing, Kyle does his best to avoid making eye contact with Brian Gaines, who is staring intently at him. Brian Gaines: 'Now, I'd like you to repeat to me what you just said.' Kyle Reyes: 'I s-said that we have other intros you could try, Mr. Gaines.' Brian Gaines: 'Tell me, how do you feel knowing that you actually spoke back to the owner of Chosen Productions?' Kyle Reyes: 'I...I...' Brian Gaines: 'Well?' Kyle Reyes: 'I...' Brian Gaines: 'Just gimmie the scripts!' Kyle reaches down to the table with a shaking hand, picks up a few stapled together sheets of paper, and extends them to Brian Gaines. Gaines looks at Kyle's hands with disgust for a moment before yanking the script out of them. He then scans the sheets silently for a moment, quickly flipping through pages. When finished, he actually smiles, and gently hands the papers back to Kyle. Brian Gaines: 'You can sit down now, Kyle.' The jittery writer obediently does so. For that moment as he stands before the table of writers, Brian Gaines appears to be at peace. The red has left his face, his lips no longer show his teeth, and very demeanor seems calm. How quickly it all changes, when all the frightful signs of a very angry man return to the guise of Brian Gaines. Brian Gaines: 'Is this supposed to impress me...?' The screaming resumes. Brian Gaines: 'YOU'RE ALL FIRED!!!' With that, Brian Gaines storms out of the room, leaving a group of now unemployed writers behind him. How many lives he just destroyed, he does now know. Probably dosen't care, either. Things are busy at the HQ of Chosen Productions, and Brian Gaines is a busy man. Therefore the head honcho himself simply cannot get down the hall without being faced with some sort of idiocy. A rather attractive blonde secretary calls out to him as he passes by her desk. 'Mr. Gaines, you have a call on line two!' Brian Gaines: 'I'll take it in my office with my lunch, Marie. Who's calling?' Marie: 'A Mr. Tyler Nelson, sir.' Brian Gaines: 'NELSON?! You put TYLER NELSON on hold?!?!' Marie: 'You were in a meeting, I...' She trails off, not needing to continue, as Brian Gaines is no longer there for her to speak to. He dashes up a short flight of steps, flings open the glass double doors of his office, and runs inside. His office is elaborate and luxurious, obviously designed and decorated at a very high price. Gaines literally throws himself at his glossy black desk, grabs the phone off its base as he flies over it, and comes crashing down on a black leather chair, which flips over on impact. All we can see is the surface of the desk, and the arm of Brian Gaines coming up from beneath it, fumbling around until it finds the phone base, and then hits a button on it so he may recieve the call. Brian Gaines: 'This is Brian Gaines. I'm terribly sorry, Mr. Nelson.' Voice on the Phone: 'Who?' Brian Gaines: 'Brian Gaines, Mr. Nelson. I didn't expect your call! Had I known you were calling, you wouldn't have been put on hold, sir...' Voice on the Phone: 'No, I mean who is Tyler Nelson?' Brian Gaines's head pops up from under the desk. His face is once again turning a shade of red as he cradles the phone on his shoulder, allowing him to set the chair back up and sit. Brian Gaines: 'YOU are...' Voice on the Phone: 'Actually, I'm...' Brian Gaines: 'WHO IS THIS?' Voice on the Phone: 'It's Gary Colem-' The voice is quickly cut off by the sound of Brian Gaines slamming the phone back down onto the base. His lips are curled, and his face is now beet red. Just when he looks like he is about to explode, Brian's left eyebrow suddenly quirks. He looks down at a small console on his desk; a microphone attached to base with a switch on it. Gaines pulls the microphone closer and flips the switch. Brian Gaines (speaking into the microphone): 'Attention employees of Chosen Productions...' The voice of Brian Gaines echoes through the corridors of the building, being carried by the intercom system set up throughout this place. All attention is diverted to the speakers on the walls where Brian Gaines's voice eminates. Brian Gaines: 'It has come to my attention that a great deal of you...and when I say a great deal of you, I mean all of you...are so utterly incapable of doing your jobs that it makes my head spin when I wonder why I hired you people in the first place. As you all know, our current main project is the production of high quality promos for me to air on the Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Network, so that I can rise up the ladder and conquer CCW. To do so, I need this company to be nothing short of a well oiled machine. It is because of this that I now make the following executive decision.' The entire studio grows silent in preparation of the announcement. Brian Gaines: 'YOU'RE ALL FIRED!!!' The speakers screech with feedback as Gaines screams into the microphone. Brian Gaines: 'You will all be replaced by a new, more talented staff within the next 24 hours, when either new employees are hired, or if a group of wild monkeys wanders into the studio. Either way, I'm better off. Good day, and get the hell out!' With a single click, the speakers are shut off. Sitting at his desk, Brian Gaines lets out a heavy sigh, and turns to look at something off camera. Brian Gaines: 'I don't know about you, Mr. Silly Stripes, but I think we've got this thing in the bag.' The camera cuts to an old stuffed toy zebra with big eyes, sitting atop a pedestal nearby. A golden plaque mounted on the front of the pedestal reads 'Mr. Silly Stripes - Vice President of Chosen Productions'. Gaines gets out of his chair and playfully struts over to the pedestal, grinning with the joy a small child shows when playing with his favorite toy. He grins as he takes the stuffed zebra in his hands and holds it eye to eye with his face as he grins. Brian Gaines: 'The Masked Mullet dosen't stand a chance against me when I have the baddest Zebra this side of Hollywood on my side! C'mon, let's go watch all the idiots leave.' And so, Brian Gaines carries VP Mr. Silly Stripes under his right arm as he leaves his office to watch the former members of his payroll exit the HQ of Chosen Productions as the scene fades to black.
