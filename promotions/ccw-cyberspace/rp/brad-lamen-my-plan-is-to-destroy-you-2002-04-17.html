Brad Lamen, My Plan Is To Destroy You

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Scorpion and his agent learn of his match against Brad Lamen and respond to a promo by Lamen.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Scorpion Entitled: Brad Lamen, my plan is to destroy you. Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 17:26:11 Location: Boston, Mass This is a: Match RP :::: This message is brought to you by “The Revolution” of Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, Scorpion. :::: :::: After a week since Scorpion has been on CCW TV now it’s time for Scorpion to return to Lockdown and face the first ever CCW Grand slam champion, Brad Lamen. Scorpion, who in his last match won by default because Danny Bryson was to drunk to wrestle, now sees a new opening. This will be Scorpion’s second time facing Brad Lamen as in their first encounter Scorpion was successful. But now it’s a new Brad Lamen, a Brad Lamen that was recently the Trans-Atlantic Champion, an Brad Lamen that has also gone over the edge in past weeks. How will Scorpion do against a man that is asking Scorpion to give him the win? Check Lockdown for the answer to your questions. :::: :::: Wednesday afternoon 4:45 p.m. Scorpion is roaming the halls of the Fleet Center in Boston until he is stopped by his agent, T.J. Walker. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Scorpion where have you been I have been looking all over the place for you! :::: :::: Scorpion: I went to get something to eat, what’s the problem T.J.? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: There is no problem at all, I just wanted to know where you were because I need to ask you a question. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok, what’s your question? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Who are you facing this Thursday? :::: :::: Scorpion: Truthfully I have no idea. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Are you on the card? :::: :::: Scorpion: They told me to report here for a Thursday match. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Ok, but you don’t know who your facing? :::: :::: Scorpion: Bingo. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: That’s weird. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yeah. But who cares doesn’t matter who it is unless I beat them. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: True. :::: :::: Suddenly Scorpion and T.J. Walker are interrupted by Spider’s secretary. :::: :::: Spider’s secretary: Scorpion, Spider wants to talk to you in his office right this minute. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok I’m on my way. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Good luck man. :::: :::: Scorpion: Thanks. :::: :::: Scorpion makes his way from one hallway to the other and into Spider’s office. Spider is on the phone with someone when Scorpion enters the room. :::: :::: Spider: (Talking into phone) Yeah I fully understand. Yeah Mexico will be a great tour for the CCW. Oh someone important just walked in, can I call you back? Ok talk to you later, bye. (Hangs up phone, addresses Scorpion) Scorpion, nice to see you, sit down. I want to talk to you about Lockdown tomorrow. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yeah who am I facing? :::: :::: Spider: Let me finish please. :::: :::: Scorpion: Oops, sorry. :::: :::: Spider: It’s ok just wait for me to finish. Anyway this Thursday you will face the first ever CCW Grand Slam Champion, Brad Lamen. I know you have faced him before and defeated him but this is Lamen that is wrestling now is the Lamen of old. He is one of the top guys in the CCW and if you can prove you can beat him then you can beat the top of the heap. Just go out there and do your best. :::: :::: Scorpion: Brad Lamen, now there is a test. I have been waiting for you to give me someone that won’t be drunk. :::: :::: Spider: Haha, yeah sorry bout that. But we took care of Bryson believe me and he is sober. :::: :::: Scorpion: He’s also the Trans-Atlantic Champion. :::: :::: Spider: Yes he is. :::: :::: Scorpion: That’s a title I want around my waist. :::: :::: Spider: Prove to me you want it by beating Lamen this Thursday. :::: :::: Scorpion: I’m not going to stop proving myself now, I’m going to continue until I reach my goal. :::: :::: Spider: That’s what I like to hear. Good luck tomorrow. :::: :::: Scorpion: Thanks Spider, talk to you later. :::: :::: Spider: All right, bye. :::: :::: Scorpion walks out of Spider’s office and heads down to the next hallway and into his locker room where T.J. Walker is watching T.V. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: (Looking at TV) Jerry! Jerry! Jerry! :::: :::: Scorpion: What are you doing? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Oh you’re here, I’m just watching ah, television. :::: :::: Scorpion: Man you have problems. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Don’t be politically incorrect Scorpion, they are called issues. :::: :::: Scorpion: Give me a break T.J. Does it really matter. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: No not really, just thought you’d like to know. :::: :::: Scorpion: Thanks for that tidbit of knowledge T.J. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: No problem. So who are you facing Thursday? :::: :::: Scorpion: Brad Lamen. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Dude that’s awesome! He’s a tremendous wrestler. Now that is a great opportunity for you. :::: :::: Scorpion: I know, thanks. :::: :::: Suddenly there is a knock on the locker room door and in walks CCW announcer, Chucky. :::: :::: Chucky: Hey Scorpion sorry to bother you but turn on your television Brad Lamen is cutting a promo on you. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok thanks. :::: :::: Chucky leaves and Scorpion puts the T.V. on the CCW announcements channel in the arena and there on the television is Brad Lamen telling Scorpion a plan. A plan that will be Scorpion letting Brad Lamen win. Scorpion watches the promo until it is done and then has a confused/angry look on his face. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Plan? What plan is he talking about? :::: :::: Scorpion: Beats me. But letting him win is not the plan I intend to do to him. My plan is to take him out and kick his ass for saying that. Man, Lamen has lost it. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Yes he has. :::: :::: Scorpion: Oh I can’t wait to kick his ass. Everybody in the CCW looks up to him as a top CCW wrestler; the first to win the grand slam championship and now it’s my time to prove to the CCW that I’m ready for the next step. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Show the CCW, but most of all show Brad Lamen. :::: :::: Scorpion: You dam right I will because what I say are more then comments and statements, what I say are promises and I promise I will make Brad Lamen feel sorry for saying that stupid plan he thought of. :::: View Biography
