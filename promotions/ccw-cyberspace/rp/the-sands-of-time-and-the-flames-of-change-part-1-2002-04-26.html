The Sands Of Time And The Flames Of Change (Part 1)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long roleplay in which Yama Tatsu is transformed by a ritual fire after a parody talk-show scene, prefaced by a war poem.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Yama Tatsu Entitled: The Sands of time and the Flames of change (Part 1) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 02:49:30 Location: Your mommas house ;) This is a: Match RP I know I usually start off my Roleplays with some wise talk from my character but I thought this time, in commemoration of ANZAC Day, I would post a famous New Zealand/Australian war poem… Lest we forget… Lest we forget.... Poem by Laurence Binyon With proud thanksgiving, a mother for her children, England mourns for her dead across the sea. Flesh of her flesh they were, spirit of her spirit, Fallen in the cause of the free. Solemn the drums thrill: Death august and royal Sings sorrow up into immortal spheres. There is a music in the midst of desolation And a glory that shines upon our tears. They went with songs to the battle, they were young, Straight of limb, true of eye, steady and aglow. They were staunch to the end against odds uncountered: They fell with their faces to the foe. They shall grow not old, as we that are left grow old: Age shall not weary them, nor the years contemn*. At the going down of the sun and in the morning We will remember them. They mingle not with their laughing comrades again; They sit no more at familiar tables at home; They have no lot in our labour of the day-time; They sleep beyond England's foam. But where our desires are and our hopes profound, Felt as a well-spring that is hidden from sight, To the innermost heart of their own land they are known As the stars are known to the Night; As the stars that shall be bright when we are dust, Moving in marches upon the heavenly plain; As the stars are starry in the time of our darkness, To the end, to the end they remain. They shall grow not old....as we that are left grow old Age shall not weary them, nor the years contemn* At the going down of the sun, and in the morning, we will remember them (Note: All other wrestlers in this Roleplay are fictional and were made up in my mind as I typed this crazy concoction of words out. Any relation to any current or past e-wrestler/e-fed are purely coincidental. Please do not shit frizbies at me as this roleplay was coming off the top of my head as I wrote. Also, with out wanting to give too much away before you read, all real life people involved in this Roleplay are in no way associated with me or anyone I know.) ** The scene opens to a busy, trailer park trash full arena of Jerry Springer.The crowd is clapping and cheering as the show opens. We are centred in on Jerry himself, who is standing on some steps in the middle of an isle. The camera pans in as he starts to talk. ** Jerry Springer: Welcome, tonight’s topic… Pro Wrestlers who keep losing their Titles! So settle down and enjoy the show… ** The camera turns away from Jerry and pans along the current selection of wrestlers already out on stage. There are four. They are all dressed in full wrestling attire and trying to look very staunch… even the female wrestler on the end. She is looking quite butch, wearing Camouflage tank top and pants with big black boots. Her hair is shaven back to like ½ and inch long and dyed white. Her muscles look quite bulky and well tanned. Next to her, quite frankly looking like a little bitch in comparison, is a face painted, tassles and undies, old school/showman type wrestler. His face looking like a technicolour nightmare and his attire like that of Ultimate Warriors, his muscles also very bulky and very golden. To his left is a little Lucha Libre star from Mexico. His face is well hidden by a heavily detailed mask. It is mainly Green and red but has areas of Gold and Black around the face area. He is wearing a simple singlet with a symbol on it which matches his tattoo on his left shoulder. His green tights have strange “Celtic” designs on them. He finishes this off with white boots with black laces. The final man in the row is non other than former CCW Tag Team champion and now side kick of Devyl, Urban Outlaw. ** Jerry Springer: Hello everyone. Well today we have Former World champions, Women’s Champions, Hardcore Champions, Tag Team Champions and others who just couldn’t make it. So to get the so started here’s Sasha Commando, a former RRBWWF (Really, Really Butch Women’s Wrestling Federation! I couldn’t think of anything ;)) Champion who has dropped out of the main event circle because of a repeated knee injury… Sasha Commando: Bullshit Jerry! It was because of that cow Cindy Pinkbits! She dropped me 20ft from the top of a Cage on to the safety rail. I should have killed her when I had the chance! Jerry Springer: Hold up… What actually happened? Sasha Commando: Well Jerry I was unloading a big can of whoop ass! ** The slack-jawed yokel crowd cheers at the sound of “Whoop Ass”. ** Sasha Commando: Yeah! Dam straight! Wooooooooo! Jerry Springer: Sasha… Sasha… ** Jerry Springer starts to “sssssh” the crowd as he tries to get Sasha Commando to calm down. But she just carries on ignoring him. He makes a signal of cutting his throat and we cut to a commercial break. ** Chicken Flavoured Weenies- You know you want’em! ** We return after that great food snack commercial. Jerry has managed to calm down the hyped woman. We return to Jerry in his usual pose walking around the crowd holding his chin, thinking up questions. ** Jerry Springer: Welcome back, we are here today talking about Profeesional wrestlers who had it all and lost it… P Funk Chainsaw: ( The Ultimate Warrior wanna be who should have got out of the game years ago cuts Jerry off) Yo Jerry I ain’t lost nuttin. I can still beat yo ass down, his ass down (Points to the shrimpy Mexican next to him) and her… ummm I don’t really even wanna touch yo lesbo ass biatch! Sasha Commando: What you say punk! Lets run this shit! ** The two stand and start exchanging blows. Not surprisingly the female is over powering the 40+ year old wrestler. She is absolutely thrashing the crap out of him by the time the Security can rush in there and break it up. P Funk Chainsaw breaks free though and quickly grabs a chair. Using this chair to his advantage he starts to beat down on the security guards. One by one they start to collapse to the ground. The chair is getting extremely stuffed and soon the wields break apart and the chair splits in two. ** P Funk Chainsaw: Yo G! You better watch yourself nigger cause I am layin a bet down on yo ass! ** Suddenly a there is a flash and then black as the a small, grey dot/blur dissolves in the middle of the screen. Now it becomes apparent we weren’t actually there we were watching it on a T.V. screen. The camera pans back a bit to see it is an old T.V. set. Extremely old! The camera then turns around to see Yama Tatsu sitting on the end of his bed with a pissed look on his face and a Budwieser in his hand. A simple Skate belt wraps around his waist where the Hardcore strap should be, a menacing, angry look on his face. ** Yama Tatsu: You know I should be on that stupid show! Geez I can’t even get on that stupid “Redneckfest” of a program, something must be wrong with me! ** The door to the small Hotel room flies open and incomes Yama Tatsu’s Master. ** Master: Nonsense Tachi! You are as good as any warrior… Yama Tatsu: I know Kyoshi, but why can’t I match up to the competiton? My losing streak isn’t coming to an end Kyoshi! Master: Sunday and Thursday were just unfortunate. But this Sunday they will not stand a chance… I have a secret I wish to share with you Tachi… But not here. Come! We shall travel North to the home of … (Hushing his voice as if to imply it is a deadly secret) the home of Wandering Eagle. Yama Tatsu: WHO!?!? Master: Never mind, who! Tachi get up and take me there. I will give you directions… ** Yama Tatsu gets up out of his chair unwillingly. He is tired and can’t be bothered listening to the old mans ramblings. Never the less he gets up as he knows his trainer can hand his ass to him on a silver plateau if he wishes. He grabs the keys to his Harley and walks out of his Motel room with his Master following close behind. They exit in such a frantic pace they just leave the door dangling open in the wind. Scene switch. We are now driving down a main highway. We then pan pack to discover Yama Tatsu, with his master clinging tight, flying along at a tremendous pace. The come to a Billboard idle planted here in the countryside. ** CCW presents… Group Theraphy! Only 30 min drive! Come join in the mayhem Sunday 28th April 2k2 ** Next to this is a sign “New York City 75 miles”. The camera then switches back to the Harley, which is a little further down the road now and is pulling to a halt. They stop next to a small back road turn off. The old guy on the back taps Yama’s shoulder and signals for him to make a turn off the main highway. A few quick reves of the engine and away they ride. Fade out. ** ** Fade in. Yama Tatsu and his mean ride pull up at an old, 1920’s farm house. The safety mesh, which covers the door, is broken and separated from its top hinge. It looks like no one has been around in years. The bike pulls right up to the front porch steps. Here Yama Tatsu cuts the engine and removes himself from the Motorcycle. He charges up the steps and is preparing to knock on the door when he realises the crappy, mesh wire in his way. He quickly grabs this and rips it clean from its last remaining hinge. He once again is about to knock but stops dead in his tracks. The door slowly slides open revealing an old Indian man standing there. He is wearing a funky robe similar to the one Yama’s Master likes to wear. ** Wandering Eagle: Yama Tatsu, Kyoshi Kandu please come in Yama Tatsu: (Astounded that the man new his name) How… did… you know my name? Wandering Eagle: (Points upward) Cable Master: Enough of this petty chit chat! Wandering Eagle, you know why we are here don’t you? Wandering Eagle: I sure do! Come with me to the barn… ** Scene switch. We are now in the barn and it is considerably later. In the center of the barn is a huge carved, wooden tomb. It stands on a stage covered in Hay. The whole barn is covered in hay. Suddenly the Indian and the elderly Asian appear in shot. The are carrying gasoline cans and they make there way to the top of the stage and begin conversing with Yama Tatsu. Well at least Wandering Eagle is talking with Yama Tatsu who is locked inside the Tomb, his Master is just standing there silently mumbling an ancient chant. ** Wandering Eagle: Now we just have to lock you in. It is just a safety measurement! Yama Tatsu: (Sounding quite scared) Ok… Master: Okin Yikin Sho… Okin Yikin Sho Yama Tatsu: So you just plan to set me on fire and let me roast!? Wandering Eagle: Actually, We plan to set the whole barn a light! This way we make sure it works… Yama Tatsu: What works? Wandering Eagle: Your rebirth! Yama Tatsu: My Rebirth!?!? Wandering Eagle: The transition between warrior and god. You must rise forward like many who have gone before you… Yama Tatsu: So this really works then? Wandering Eagle: Yes……… and no! Yama Tatsu: What! Get me out of here! Wandering Eagle: Calm down Yama. Nothing will go wrong! It’s just that many warriors in the past have died due to them not being strong enough! But if you’re half as good as your Master says you are… Don’t worry at all! Just make sure you power up when you start feeling the pressure… So is that all… Yama Tatsu:….. Wandering Eagle: Good! He is unconscious… ** A wicked smile spreads across his wrinkled face. ** Wandering Eagle: Burn it all down! ** Suddenly dozens of cloaked men flood the barn spread gasoline and lighting huge bundles of hay and dried grass. The men just as quickly as they entered leave the burning hellhole. We now see the old, wise Indian man padlocking the barn doors shut. They then all gather and start to chant the same chant Master was chanting before. ** All: OKIN YIKIN SHO!! OKIN YIKIN SHO!! OKIN YIKIN SHO!! ** Wild, untainted screams of pain burn through the night air as the giant flames ravage the charcoaling building like water surrounding a fish. The chanting coninues as does the screams of pain for many minutes. Finally the sounds of Fire truck sirens can be heard in the distance as the barn starts crumbling to its knees… another couple of minutes pass. Finally the trucks arrive and start to unreel the hoses quickly. The Fire fighters start to battle the blaze and as the first blasts of water hit the barn we fade to a completely black screen. Here words start to scroll up the screen. These words are the thoughts of the smoldering Yama Tatsu. ** I once thought of myself as invincible, Now I know it to be true, This Sunday at Group Therapy, CCW will know this too, Supreme Machine… He certainly does live up to his name, Though he really doesn’t understand, I am no longer playing a game! The Thunder from down under, Is a man that doesn’t matter, By the end of Group Therapy, I will prove the clashing to be a mere patter, I am once again with out a title, Due to a little dog interfering, I was looking for residents for my hitlist, Thanks for Volunteering! Guardian, what can I say, You hold a title you didn’t earn, This Sunday if you can survive, I will make sure you feel the burn, Enough of the se childish rhymes and riddles! I do not like any member in my team, But I will fight Honourably beside them, I make no secrets when I say I wish to claim both RHSC’s, And the new John Coens (Looks like I ain’t the only one who’s snapped) belts, The Independent and Pacific… two honours, which will soon be bestowed upon me! So to my opponents and Team mate’s, GT Will be the decider!!!! OGA SHIMASU!!! ** The scene fades back to the smoky heap that was the cruddy, rustic looking barn. A Fire fighter is searching for remains of the certainly dead Yama Tatsu. He is walking around occasionally lifting up big bits of charcoaled wood. He is scared shitless when a hand suddenly thrusts its way through the surface of the terrain of ash. The Fire fighter quickly rushes over and pulls on the hand. More men gather around and start lifting out the body of Yama Tatsu. Though it is not the Yama Tatsu we used to know. All his hair is a totally different colour. It is now a fiery red. His clothes are burnt and tattered. His muscles have bulked up exponentially. Yama Tatsu is currently unconscious. Fade out. ** (OOC-Note this is part one in a continuing series of stories not one in a set of RP’s. Meaning yes this only one Rp.
