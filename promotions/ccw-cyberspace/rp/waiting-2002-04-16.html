Waiting

16 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A scripted scene in which Lacey Skye visits a psychiatric center for information about Devyl’s family, learns the patient has died, and plans to search an old farmhouse.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lacey Skye Entitled: Waiting Written in Tuesday, Apr 16, 2002, 22:56:25 Location: Chicago This is a: Match RP Lacey: **voice over** I wish I knew what else I could do. I feel as if I’m adrift on the ocean.... Floating and waiting for a glimpse of land. I’ve gotten my point across to devy. I beat her twice last week, and even got a title match signed with her at Group Therapy. So.... Now what? Well, I’m being told that I need to find someone who will second me in my match with Devyl at Group Therapy. But can I really trust anyone? Everytime I try, I get stabbed in the back. Damsel wants to join me at ringside, and I’ve already brushed off her offer. Damsel self-destructs when she’s around Devyl. Don’t ask me why, but the girl would be a hindrance at ringside rather than a help. So now, I’m doing the only thing I can really think of doing at this point.... Revisiting Mrs. Devins and getting the answers about her daughter that I need! **It is Thursday morning, and Lacey Skye is standing at the front check-in desk at the Chicago Psychiatric Center where Joyce Devins has been institutionalized. One of the nurses is busy is busy with a telephone call, but she soon finishes up and gives her full attention to Lacey.** Nurse: May I help you? Lacey: Yes, my name is Lacey Morris, and I’m here to see a patient of yours.... Joyce Devins. Nurse: Oh.... Haven’t you heard? Joyce Devins passed away last week. Lacey: What!? Nurse: Yes. I’m so sorry. Were you a close friend of the family? Lacey: Uh.... No, I.... **pauses** She just had some things that she wanted to tell me, that’s all. Nurse: I’m terribly sorry. **The scene flips to the parking lot area outside the hospital, and Lacey is seen walking out to her rental car, head hanging with a mixture of sadness and helplessness. Doug, her husband, is there, leaning against the car and waiting for her. He doesn’t say a word as she comes up to him and looks him right in the eyes. He can read the sadness and frustration there, and then gives her a supportive hug.** Lacey: **muffled a bit because of the embrace** She’s dead, Doug. **Doug just nods.** Lacey: This was a worthless trip. I’m sorry for dragging you along. Doug: Don’t apologize, Lacey. Devyl knew what you were trying to do, so she showed up and killed her own mother shortly after you left. Lacey: They told me she killed herself. I didn’t believe that. Doug: **a long pause, as if looking deep inside himself** I think she left something for you, hon.... A message of some sort. Lacey: What kind of message? Doug: I don’t know, but I think we should find out. Lacey: Then let’s go! **Lacey starts walking back towards the hospital. Doug remains by the car. She soon notices that he’s not following her and turns back to him.** Lacey: Aren’t you coming? Doug: Not into the hospital. Lacey: Why not? You said she left me a message.... Doug: Yes, but I don’t think it’s here. **Lacey slowly walks back to Doug, a confused look on her face.** Lacey: Then where could it be? Doug: I’m not completely certain. But if I could hazard a guess, I’d say we should check out the old farmhouse. Lacey: You mean the place where Devyl grew up? The place where she eventually murdered her father? Doug: Yes. It’s a good five-hour drive from here, though. We don’t have enough time to get there and then make it to your match with Damsel. Lacey: And I have to be there for that. Doug: **smiles** A battle of egos, huh? Lacey: No, not at all. I’m sure Damsel thinks it is, just as I’m sure that she thinks I’ve gotten to be a bit too big for myself. The fact of the matter is that I want to stop Devyl That’s all that matters to me right now. She’s the one who got Damsel and myself booked in this lumberjack match. And since the two of us are going to be surrounded on all sides by opponents, this isn’t going to be an easy match. But I’ve overcome far worse before. I just have to keep showing Devyl that I’m not one to be trifled with. As far as damsel is concerned, I know she’ll put up a tough match. She’s one of the better athletes CCW has right now. Her only problem is, and I know this sounds terrible for me to say, that she’s somehow allergic to success. When she held the title belt for that short time, I took it away from her on our second meeting. And both times she tried her absolute hardest to keep a hold of that title by using foreign objects, chairs, you name it.... She just couldn’t compete at the appropriate level. Doug: Well, she had to get the title somehow. She had to have been good enough. Lacey: Her boyfriend at the time, Jesse Ramey, gave it to her after he won it off of Koriann. It really wasn’t the most prestigious way of winning a title. Doug: After everything that’s happened to that poor girl, I can’t imagine what life must be like for her right now. Lacey: I know.... I feel bad for her too. But this is business, Doug. And she’s sticking her nose into mine, and I don’t appreciate it all that much. Doug: She just wants to help you stop Devyl, hon. You can’t really blame her for that. Lacey: **softly** No.... I don’t blame her for that. But I can’t let her help me, Doug. At least not until she learns to stop being so self-destructive. Doug: Well, let’s get you to the airport. You have a match to get ready for. **Doug walks around the car to the driver’s side. He looks up at his wife as he unlocks the door.** Lacey: We’ll come back here, Doug. We’ll come and find the message Devyl’s mother left for me. **Doug gives her a ghost of a smile and nods. Lacey gets in the passenger side after Doug unlocks the doors, and they head off for the airport.** Lacey: **voice over** I feel really bad about brushing Damsel off like I have, but it can’t be helped. I’m going to beat Devyl, stop her reign of terror, and take that championship belt away from her. I know that Damsel will be more than up for the challenge of the lumberjack match, as will I. Get ready, Damsel. Get ready.... View Biography
