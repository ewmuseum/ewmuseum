Yet Another Faux Pas

23 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay in which Koriann tricks Vinny Astone into carrying her bag and vandalizes the wrong locker-room door.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Koriann Entitled: Yet Another Faux Pas Written in Tuesday, Apr 23, 2002, 21:11:59 Location: The Nassau Coliseum This is a: Match RP [Scene opens up on the Nassau Coliseum. Koriann, small travel bag in hand, arrives at the arena and steps through the entrance. She braces herself for the onslaught of questions from bungling reporter, Devin Belly, and then sighs in relief when she finds that he’s not there. In fact, he hasn’t been around since the European tour... How odd. Shrugging, Koriann continues on her way, but is soon assaulted by another individual.] Vinny Astone: “Hey! Koriann! Wait up!” [Koriann slows to a halt and rolls her eyes. She didn’t want to deal with this right now. Sure, it was flattering that Vinny had the hots for her, but still... She wouldn’t just be seen with anybody... No, wait, that wasn’t true. She belonged to the scum of the earth, and they belonged to her. And so, with a heavy heart, Koriann turns to Vinny and smiles.] Koriann: “Vinny! What are you doing here? It’s Lockdown tonight, not SMN.” Vinny: *coming to a halt in front of Koriann* “I know that! I just wanted to... Uh... You know... Chucky and I were helping with set-up, and we’re just about to go get something to eat. Want to join us?” Koriann: “No thanks. I’ve seen you guys eat. Besides, there’s something about Chucky that rubs me the wrong way. I think he’s gay.” Vinny: “Ya know, I think so too. I mean, I always talk about how awesome our mom is in bed, and he always acts so disgusted. I think he has a thing against women.” Koriann: “That’s exactly what I was thinking! Guys who are disgusted by sex-talk involving members of the opposite sex are definitely homosexual.” Vinny: “So you won’t go and eat with us?” Koriann: “No.” [Koriann turns to leave but catches Vinny’s sad puppy dog-like look that he’s giving her. Koriann sighs and is about to continue walking when a brilliant idea catches her. She turns back to Vinny.] Koriann: Hey Vinny, if you really want to spend some time with me, help me carry my things to my room. Vinny: Really!? You’ll let me touch your stuff!? Koriann: Sure. Vinny: *reaching greedily for Koriann’s bag* “Do you have any underwear in there?” Koriann: “Er... Yes.” Vinny: “The silky kind?” Koriann: “Yes.” Vinny: “Can I see them?” Koriann: “... Maybe later.” [Koriann almost reluctantly hands the bag off to Vinny, who seems genuinely happy to actually be talking to a member of the opposite sex. Together they walk down the hallway toward Koriann’s locker room, making small talk as they go.] Koriann: “Yeah, so Angelica was all like, ‘I’m going to kick your ass!’ and I was all like, ‘No you won’t ‘cause you suck!’. And then I hit here with a flying Fameasser and she started crying like a little baby! She told me that she was going to tell Fr. Jordan on me, but I laughed at her and told her that Fr. Jordan was nothing but a big whimp. And then Fr. Jordan came into the room and tried to take me out, but I proved to him that priests suck by thrashing him pretty badly. And now Angelica and Fr. Jordan are petrified of me. They won’t come anywhere near me or even look at me the wrong way because they’re afraid that they’re going to get their asses kicked again.” Vinny: “Word.” Koriann: “Yeah, so... Wait. Give me back my bag real quick.” [Vinny hands Koriann her bag and she quickly digs through it. She smiles as she pulls out a can of spray paint. Vinny looks down at her.] Vinny: “What’s that for?” Koriann: “I have a message that I want to leave for Angelica!” [Koriann stands up, turns around, and starts spray painting on the locker room door that’s behind her.] Vinny: Uh... Koriann: Quiet, Vinny! There’s a work of art in progress here! [Koriann quickly spray paints the words: “I AM A STUPID BITCH!” in big letters across the span of the doorway. Koriann steps back and admires her handiwork.] Koriann: *smiles* “Not too shabby.” Vinny: “Uh, Koriann... Room 032 is your room.” [Koriann looks at the “032” room plaque, and then down at the locker room assignment slip she holds in her other hand.] Koriann: “D’OH!” Vinny: *smiles hopefully* “Now you’ll go out to lunch with me, right?” Koriann: “Grrr...” [fade out...] View Biography
