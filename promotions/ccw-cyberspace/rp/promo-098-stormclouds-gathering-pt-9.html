Stormclouds Gathering Pt. 9

18 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stormclouds Gathering Pt. 9 Chloey Mason CCW Promo #98 Date: 03/18/03 Location: Seattle, Washington Night time at Soldier Bridge. Steven Quartre watches as a vehicle approaches the bridge. The black Cadillac creeps to a stop. The headlights remain lit as a silhouette emerges from the driver's side. Because of the headlights, Steven cannot at first see who the figures is approaching. Then, as the figure draws near, he sees it is Christine. The former lovers stand on the bridge, a dozen yards apart. Steven is clearly disappointed. Chloe remains in hiding behind the big, black Cadillac. She peers around the bumper and sees Damsel and Steven square off. She watches as Damsel adopts some sort of fighting stance and Quartre draws his pistol. Chloe: {murmurs to herself} Oh, no.... This is going to be really bad.... Chloe stealthily creeps away from the Cadillac, and, when out of sight, runs toward an illuminated phone booth a half block away. On the bridge, Damsel cautiously circles Steven, who does the same to her in return. Damsel: Don't look so disappointed, Steve. When was the last time you had someone so eager to come and face you? Quartre: Why are you doing this, Christine? You know much I care about you. You know that, with me, you'd always be protected. So why don't you stop this foolishness, go on home, and start putting together a nice little dinner for me. I'm always starved after I kill. And Steven reaches into his other coat pocket and pulls out... The mask. He carefully places it over his face. Damsel: Still worrying about that pretty face of yours, Steve? Quartre: Just protecting my most valuable asset. Then he raises his gun, pointing it right in Christine's face. Quartre: It's been fun. Damsel, limber and ready, spins to the side right as Quartre squeezes the trigger. The bullet misses Christine's face by inches. She glares at him as Quartre looks momentarily shocked.... then he smiles. This might be fun. Down the block at the phone booth, Chloe practically screams into the phone. Chloe: {into the phone} You NEED to see this! It's happening! Forget the one fifty! We're talking about possible death, here! Just then, Lacey's black Jag roars past the booth heading for the bridge. Chloe sees her. Chloe: {to herself} Uh oh.... {into the phone} The third one just showed! Over at a repair garage, the cameraman pulls the van out of a service bay. Kate is on her cell phone with Chloe. Kate: {into the phone} Slow down. What? Where? We're close, already. Keep an eye on them. She clicks off the cell phone and jumps into the van. Kate: {to the cameraman} They're at Soldier Bridge. Back at the bridge, A ferocious Quartre, a very skilled and powerful fighter, physically attacks Damsel, who fights with no less ferocity. Chloe approaches the bridge like a moth drawn to the flame, the sight of two people caught in a life or death struggle. Quartre and Christine fight and parry over to Quartre's Humvee. Quartre tackles into Damsel, slamming her back hard against the car's hood. The screeching of tires is momentarily heard from the other end of the bridge. Lacey comes running up from the shadows where her car is parked. Seeing the fighting duo in the distance, she starts to run. Damsel and Quartre are both unaware of Lacey's arrival. Quartre pins damsel against the hood, one hand around her neck, the other still holding the gun. In desperation, Damsel slips a concealed knife out of her sleeve and slashes upwards. The knife catches Quartre at just the right angle and slices up, under his mask, and through his cheek. With a roar of pain, Quartre rips the mask off his face, revealing the hideous gash across his cheek. Damsel steps away, gulping in air, allowing Quartre to survey the damage in the Humvee side mirror. Quartre is horrified. What happens next takes Damsel completely by surprise. Quartre turns to her, lifts his gun, and shoots. The silencer muffles the sound of the shot as the bullet plows through Damsel's stomach. For a moment, Christine stares in disbelief and shock at her one-time lover. Then, before her body can register what's happened to it, she charges straight at Quartre with the ferociousness of a wounded tiger, her knife ready to plunge into Quartre's heart. Damsel: It's over, Quartre! Quartre: No, it's just begun, 'Damsel'! Quarter grabs a hold of Damsel's arm as she sweeps the knife at him. He drops to his knees, lowers his shoulder, plants it into Christine's midsection, rises up, and hurtles Damsel over the bridge rail. Christine grabs for the bridge rail barely misses, and then flails helplessly as her body plunges downward into the icy waters below.... View Chloey Mason 's Biography Back
