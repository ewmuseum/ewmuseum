Healing Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #92 Date: 17 March 2003 Location: Mt Alvernia Hospital - Room 158 The Mt Alvernia Hospital, Maine, New England In the rolling grounds at the foot of Mt Alvernia lies one of the worlds most exclusive and expense hospitals. Where its only patients are ‘A’ list stars, high ranking professionals and of course those whose families are bred into money. The hospital itself is grand. Victorian in design, with large solid wood panels adorning every main hallway; it is more a stately home than hospital and it houses the world’s most exclusive medical staff. In room 158 sitting up in bed is Jack Manson. His forehead and most of the left side of his face neatly wrapped in bandages. He is being tended to by a doctor. “All in all Mr Manson sir I think you’ve come out of this ok” Jack smiles “You know me doc, it’ll take more than a small bump to lay me out” The doctor laughs. “You know, I’ve said this before Jack and I’ll say it again; just why do you do this to yourself. “If you think this is bad…..” The doctor interrupts mid sentence, “yes yes I know, I should see the state of the other man, you’ve said that before too sir. I’ve just never understood why though. I mean a man of your background; your intellect could surely earn his living elsewhere?” Jacks face turns from light-hearted to serious in an instant. “You know Dr Forbes, the FBI once told me I was one of the greatest profilers they had seen in years, they said I was one of the best things to happen to them in a long long time. They also said I was unstable. Can you believe that doc? Me. Unstable. I mean come on doc, you’ve been treating me and my family now since I was knee high to a grasshopper right. Have you ever known me to do anything you’d consider unstable?” Dr Forbes senses the change in Jacks voice and becomes a little nervous “Well…I I I errr well, I do worry about your actions in these err…wrestling matches Jack. I mean when I…I…I was younger I used to watch the err matches in our local school gym every few months when they would come into town but I I I never ever saw anything like what you d d do to yourself. I mean don’t get me wrong Mr Manson I mean I admire what your do greatly, I I just don’t understand it” “No you wouldn’t do would you? What possible idea could a man like you have of the things I do eh?” Dr Forbes takes a small step backwards “Hey come on now Jack, we go back a long way you and me. I’ve put you back together more than once you know that. I’m just concerned for you that’s all” “Don’t be. I can take care of myself thank you. All you need to do is patch me up every now and then.” “Jack, I’ve been patching you up more and more frequently as the weeks go by. I mean do you realise how lucky you were that whatever was thrown into your face was not hotter than it was and didn’t go into your eyes? You could have been blinded or even scarred for life. Think about it, is it worth all that?” “Worth it? WORTH IT? What the HELL do you know what this is worth to me eh? Goddam it Forbes you are just like the rest of them aren’t you? This IS’NT about money or fame or all the other shit that goes along with it. This is about something all together more rewarding and I though a man of your background would at least understand that. Do you have ANY idea over run our society has become with ignorant, worthless ingrates? I’m doing people like us a favour Forbes. “Jack, Jack, Jack…..i know what your trying to do my friend, but I….well I’m just concerned that’s all. Hell you’re almost part of the family and well….” “Listen I don’t need your concern doc, I look after myself and myself looks after me got it?” “Then why did you bring that young kid in with you if you only look after yourself” “The kid…..how is he?” “He’s doing fine Jack, just a few bumps and bruises is all. I think he may need some…..some specialist help though. He….He….aint right Jack” The kid is fine. He doesn’t need anything else. That’s half his problem; he listens to the wrong people. All he needs is a little guidance in the right direction and from the right people and he’ll be fine” Dr Forbes shifts rather restlessly as he summons the courage to ask what he wants to. “And I suppose you are the one to give him this guidance?” Jack glares back at Forbes “You think of anyone better doctor? Eh?” The doctor looks nervously at his feet “Didn’t think so. Besides…..I have my reasons. Now if you’ll excuse me doc. I need to be alone right now.” Dr Forbes looks somewhat relieved by this window of opportunity to leave. “Ok Jack, I’ll have nurse come in and tend to those dressings in a few hours ok. Get some rest will ya. You really do look like shit” With a nervous smile, the doctor grabs his clipboard and exits the room leaving Jack alone with his thoughts.. “Dammit I gotta get out of this place tomorrow, concussion or not. Goddam people just don’t understand. This week is going to be one of the most important yet. A time when my work really start bearing fruit. Yes..A time when he finally realises just what he has in store. No more playing. No more games. Its time they all took notice” Jack reaches up and starts to undo his bandage “No..this week they won’t forget who I am. A ring full of men, of legends and wannabe’s. Where me and kid………we get to have a little fun.” As the layers of bandage peel away, a large patch of red appears. Blood socked in through the healing wounds. He continues to slowly peel. “Hawk, Gobber they will learn…..Stevens will be aware of what I did to Scorpion already…Carlos…..Hmmm Carlos……i`ll make you pay for what you did last week. You’ve already had a taste of what the kid can do….now your gonna find out what we both can do. And finally…….” The last layer of bandage is unwound and drops onto the bed. Jacks face is red raw and not a pretty sight. A gapping wound adorns his forehead, caked in dried blood. “Douglas…………the real reason I’m in CCW right now is you. Son…you have something that you know belongs to me…..I want it…….and as much as you think you can stop me…I’m going to take it. The sooner you resign yourself to that fact the better off you’ll be. Prepare yourself….all of you; for next Thursday, just like last…no boundaries, no regrets and no surrender. Game over, we win again” Jack reaches up and touches the wound on his head. He pulls his and back, see’s the damp blood on his fingertips and grins. Slowly….he starts to laugh. View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
