Acquiring Services

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A roleplay in which Regina Winters meets a contract-killer named Flix at a state park and hires him to target another wrestler.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Regina Winters Entitled: Acquiring Services Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 23:00:38 Location: Fall Creek Falls State Park This is a: Match RP **Regina Winters is sitting at a picnic table at the Fall Creek Falls State Park. The sound of roaring water can be heard in the distance, while the chatter of several nearby families fills the air nearby. Regina, dressed very conservatively, ignores them all. She was supposed to be meeting someone here, a man who's name was given to her last night at an underground Goth club. She went there looking for a man who would qualify to perform certain illegal activities. She talked to people who claimed to be his 'business associates' and told her that they would relay the message. She told them that she would like the meeting to take place right here at 3:15 P.M. And seeing as how it was now 4:00, it seemed very doubtful that the man known only as 'Flix' was not going to show.** **Sighing, Regina picked herself up from the picnic table, brushed off a stray bug that was crawling up her leg, and then squashed it under the two inch bulky heel of her hiking boots. And that's exactly what she planned on doing to Brian Gaines on Thursday. Her match with TVO on Monday didn't quite end like she wanted it to.... And she has much pent-up aggravation left over.... Aggravation that she wants to let out before Memorial Day Showdown. Brian Gaines would be the perfect target!** **So, what did she know about Mr. Gaines? He seemed to be a Hollywood executive type.... A man who seemed to crave control of every situation. And not only that, he's a man who's slightly insane. Sadly, Mr. Gaines hasn't been all that impressive in the ring as of late. Maybe that would change on Thursday. Maybe he would finally show some backbone in his match with her. And if he does.... Regina smiles to herself.... She would thoroughly enjoy snapping that new backbone in half!** **Regina makes it back to the parking area and moves to her rental. She truly couldn't wait to get in the ring on Thursday night, and perhaps that was making her more edgy than normal. And since 'Flix' hadn't shown up, she was a bit more perturbed than normal as well. Grabbing her keys, she unlocks her car door and is just about to get in when a nondescript car pulls up behind the rental and parks, blocking her exit. Regina glares at the car as the window rolls down. She immediately recognizes the man in the passenger seat. It was he that she came here to find....** Flix: You wanted to meet with me? Regina: Yes. Why are you so late? I had nearly given up on you! Flix: Quiet! I can't be seen out in the open, nor can anyone hear me talking to you. Regina: What kind of a paranoid freak are you? Flix: One that will perform the services you require. Who's the target? Regina: Mike Lubis. He's currently in New York. You can probably find him at this address. **Regina hands Flix a card with a bit of writing on it. He skims it over before tucking it in one of his pockets.** Regina: If he's not there, check out the Twin Aces. It's a club. He'll probably be there. Flix: **shrugs** Okay, now all I need to know is... Do you have enough money for this? Regina: **smiles** Money is of no consequence. Flix: Good. You and I will meet discretely at 7:00 this evening at the address written on this card. **Flix hands her a piece of paper** You will pay me $25,000 in cash, up front. The other $25,000 will be due upon completion of the target's demise. Regina: Of course. It's a pleasure doing business with you. **Flix does not reply. Instead, the window rolls back up and the car drives off. Regina shakes her head in wonder as she watches the car leave.** Regina: **sighs** Mercenary's are so quirky. This better be worth it....
