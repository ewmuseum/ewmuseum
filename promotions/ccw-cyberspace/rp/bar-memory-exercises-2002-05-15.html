Bar Memory Exercises

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A scripted scene in which Nicky White finds Jack Panzer retrieving a hidden document in a bar basement and tells him his Champions of the Ring pool.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: Bar memory exercises Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 01:56:24 Location: NYC This is a: Match RP **The scene opens in a small bar, somewhere in the world. The place has a few pools to the left of the door, where the camera is standing. To the right, the bar. The place is somewhat dark, and we can see it's jam-packed by the most different selection of people: You have two young hot girls sitting on the bar, waiting to be picked up by some nice guy, we also see some yuppies and friends shooting pool and plain having fun. On the back, we see several tables, with small phones over them. The camera also notices the bar has little phones too, in front of each bench, and each one of the phones is numbered by a big white plaque, making it obvious this is a singles bar. The scene shows the place around, as finally someone poens the door behind the camera. It gives an 180 turn to focus on Nicky White. With a snow white short hair, fireman red lipstick, black leather top and black tight leather skirt, she walks in the bar, drawing some guys' attention, then a few more, thenthe entire bar is staring at the dazzle who just entered. The cranky stuck women in the bar just pity her, their eyes burning with rage, knowing their night is lost. The guys in the pools and tables simply forget about their girls and stare at the gorgeous petit girl that moves gracefully around with her spike heels. Nicky walks straight to the bar, where a bartender, a bald guy, tall, on his late 20s, comes to her:** Bartender: Hey there... **Nicky doesn't pay much attention at him first, but then she remembers something, as she kinda snaps from a trance. She says:** Nicky: Hi, I want a Berlin Thunder, please... Bartender: What??? What's that drink? Nicky: You don't know? Bartender: No... Nicky: Weird... Just give me a beer, then... Bartender: A cold one for the hot lady coming... **The guy walks away to fill a glass, and the little white phone in front of Nicky starts ringing. She picks it up:** Nicky: Hi... Voice: Hello... I'm number 23... Nicky: Lucky you, number 23... Voice: No, I mean... Nicky: So, number 23, how does it feel to be number 23? Voice: What? No, you're not getting it. Nicky: Oh no, I perfectly understand it. I'm a lesbian... Voice: Oh... **The phone immediately is hung up, and Nicky finally gets her beer from the bartender, before taking a sip. She looks to her right, and there's this GORGEOUS blonde, just staring at Nicky. She turns to her and says:** Nicky: I'm stupid... My IQ is 19... **The blonde immediately looks away. Finally, another bartender arrives. This one looks different. Fat, long brown hair, candid look. He looks at Debbie and says:** Bartender #2: I heard you want a Berlin Thunder... Nicky: True. Can you give me one? Bartender #2: I can give you a Berlin Thunder and a Zaire Connection. **Nicky smiles, as if finally getting what she wanted. She looks at the man and says:** Nicky: So, where the hell is him? Bartender #2: Downstairs, in the old basement. Nicky: And what is he doing there? Bartender #2: I don't know, you beat me. Nicky: Ok... Thanks... **Nicky walks away from the bar, walking to a small door at the end of the bar, which she opens. The door leads to a small corridor, with a door to the right, probably an office, and an old set of stairs going down. Nicky starts slowly going down, and the place is horrid. The walls are decaying and green from the dense humidity, the decaying smell is inebriating and the sound of the cracking wood can be heard in the back. After a few moments, she finds herself at the bottom of the stairs, seeing a huge basement. The place is filled with old wooden tables and chairs, all broken. At the end of the room there's a big wooden set of shelves, holding several bottles of licor and wine, and looking at them is the big giant german known as Jack Panzer. Jack seems clueless, and is just staring at some of the bottles. Nicky calls him.** Nicky: Jack... **No answer** Nicky: JAAAAAAACK! **Finally, Jack turns back, realizing the girl's there, standing. Jack looks at her and says:** Jack: HEY, Nicky, good to see you here... Nicky: JACK, what the hell are you doing? You left the arena right after Estep's match... Jack: True. I needed to rush here... Nicky: Why? Jack: Something important... Nicky: Not the germans again... Jack: Nope, at least not for now. They have been on a low key lately, and frankly, that is pissing me off... Nicky: Then what is it? Jack: As soon as I find it out, I'll tell you. **Jack turns back to the bottles, trying to find something, but still not managing to find anything. Nicky looks at him, completely dumbfolded. She says:** Nicky: Hey, Jack, do you even know about this Thursday? Jack: Nope, what??? **With an evil smile, she says, as Jack holds a bottle of gin while leaning on a small wooden bench.** Nicky: You'll face Blayze for the vacant CCW World Title! Jack: WHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA **Jack slips down, falling on his back on the shelves, sending them crashing down all over Jack, breaking a couple of bottles, and mostly simply bouncing and hurting off his big head. Nicky just looks and laughs, as one cracked bottle slowly pours red wine on Jack's brand-new white CCW attitude T-shirt. He looks at Nicky, saying:** Jack: You'll pay me for this, I mean it... Nicky: Blah... Jack: So, seriously... Nicky: It's COTR Time, you dumbass, and you didn't even wait to hear the groups. Jack: Ah, true, true. So... How's Mark's group? Nicky: His group is fine... No problems, two rookies and a girl. Jack: Nice... What about mine? Nicky: Well, that's the problem... Jack: What's it? **Nicky looks down, kicking a bottle on the floor. She says:** Nicky: Yama Tatsu, Ken Suzahara and Spider! Jack: Really? Nicky: Really... **Nicky looks with a sad face at the big german, but instead of being sad and worried about it, the big german just smiles. Nicky says:** Nicky: Aren't you at least worried? Jack: Why should I be? Nicky: Well, I mean... Yama almost won the GT Tournament, and Ken and Spider have 3 World Titles among them. We're talking about the big guys here. Jack: That's true, Nicky, and yet, think about it: What would happen if this german rookie people keep overlooking, suddenly starts defeating former World Champs??? Nicky: People will start... Jack: Giving me more attention... Exactly. If I beat them, I can prove the world I can beat Blayze, and when I beat Blayze, I'll prove the world I can beat anybody. Nicky: True... Jack: And there's more... Wait, WAIT!!! Nicky: What??? Jack: I got it! Nicky: What??? **Jack gets up and looks at the shelves that are still in place. He finally finds one empty bottle of a french wine. He dusts it and looks at Nicky, to see her expression. The moment he turns the bottle clockwise, a small trap door opens behind the bottle, making Nicky jump on her feet.** Nicky: What is that, Jack? **Jack grabs a small envelope and says:** Jack: This is something I hid here in 1995, before I went to an operation in Bhutan. Nicky: What about it? Jack: This document can link the german army to a massacre of civilians that happened there. It had the same style of the one that happened with me... Nicky: I see... Why did you hide it here, so far from your country? Jack: Because one of our missions was to gather weapons in America, to look a terrorist attack. We bought the guns here, in NY, and I came to this bar and decided to hide this thing here, should anything happen to me. Nicky: So that's why you left so early... Jack: That and the fact that I had just remembered about it. Nicky: Well now you need to focus. COTR can jumpstart your career and make you a legend. Jack: I know, and I know what to do. Yama, Spider and Ken may be good, but they know that they'll need to go through me, and that ain't gonna be easy. I'll face them all and go up. I have to win this thing, I HAVE to. Nicky: Yeah, and I'll be there, Jack... Protecting you, holding your back. Jack: Thanks, Nicky. I knew I could count on you. Nicky: As always... **Jack grabs the envelope and puts it in his pocket. He looks at Nicky and smiles, knowing he can finally grab the army's attention, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
