A Date With The Union Rep Brad Lamen CCW Promo #10 Date: 2/14/02 Location: Chicago, IL *** The scene opens up inside a fancy restaurant. Couples are enjoying their Valentine's Day dinners, getting ready for a romantic evening alone. The camera sits down at a table and looks around, as if to be waiting for someone. Finally, Brad Lamen shows up and hands roses to the cameraman.*** Brad - I'm so sorry I left you waiting like that. I got caught up back at the office and had to finish up some paperwork. I know how upset it makes you to have to wait, but you know Brad Lamen always comes through for ya!!!! Hmm....has the waitress been by yet to take our order? *The camera shakes no.* Brad - Well, shit. I really want a drink. Garcon'! *A waiter hears Brad and walks over to him.* Waiter - Good evening, sir. How may I be of assistance? Brad - Bring me out a bottle of champagne, my good man. I'm celebrating my new job and want to get it started off right. Waiter - Right away, sir. *The waiter takes off and Brad focuses his attention back on the camera.* Brad - Ahhhh, Valentine's Day...a day for love..a day for romance....a day for fucking the shit out of some bitches ass just because you bought her a box of chocolate. Obviously, it's one of my favorite days of the year! How could you not like a day where you can get your cock all slobbery just by presenting roses to someone? I love it!!!!!! But there are other things I love as well...like this new job!!!!! You see, Sylkk is a great man and I knew he would do what is right for CCW. I mean, he brought in all these old guys, like Ace Hawk, to help him get CCW back on top! What did JMac do? Did he bring anyone in to help him? No. He just signed back that piece of shit Gob and left us all alone. Sylkk is our Savior. And I will appreciate him for this job till the day I die. Thank you, my dear friend, for making the right choice and being such an upstanding citizen of our wrestling society. Something else I love is making matches!!!!! I can't believe the power this job gives me. I am allowed to decided who gets to do what...and when!!!!!!! Now, there are some rookies in this federation that are getting a little too big in the pants. These guys think that they are owed the world, just because they were champions elsewhere and whatnot. Of course, I'm referring mostly to Jason Dread, but it also applies to Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens.... Dread, last night when you put your hands on a SANCTIONED CCW REFEREE in your match, you really screwed yourself. I'm afraid I'm going to have to fine you $10,000 for that. CCW does not tolerate any sorts of physical violence against its staff and you are no exception. You've gotta learn the ropes around here, pal... And what the hell made you think you deserved a title shot? You've been here two weeks or whatever...how the hell are you in Blayze's league? Here's the deal: You want a title shot? You've got to earn it. I'm sure you're asking yourself how. How can I earn a title shot, you say to yourself over and over, like the pathetic little slug you are. I'm going to give you just that opportunity, tho. Oh yes, you are going to have the opportunity to become the number one contender for Blayze's CCW Heavyweight Championship belt.... Jason Dread, you are going to show up next week, lace up your boots, pump yourself up, and step inside that ring ready to face your opponent. And that opponent will be none other than your little friend, Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens. Jason Dread vs. Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens in a number one contender match. And Dread, if you lose this match...YOU WILL NEVER GET A TITLE SHOT! I will make sure that you never get another chance from Blayze as long as I'm Union Rep. So Dread, this is your only shot. You've got ONE CHANCE to prove yourself to me. That's it. Fuck up and you might as well go back to GCW or wherever you came from. CCW isn't as forgiving as all the other places you've been. ONE CHANCE! *The waiter returns with Brad's champagne. He pops the cork and pours himself a glass, takes a long drink, and clears his throat.* Brad - OH, I almost forgot...I'll be at ringside, Dread, as a 'Special Enforcer' for that match...you know, to make sure no one interferes and unfairly costs you that match. Because that's what a nice guy YOUR NEW UNION REP is. Happy Valentine's Day, lover. *** Brad laughs a bit to himself, then finishes off his glass of champagne. He pours another glass and the scene fades to black. *** View Brad Lamen 's Biography Back
