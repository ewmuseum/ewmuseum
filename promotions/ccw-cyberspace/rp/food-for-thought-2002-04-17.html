Food for Thought

What this is A highway-diner promo in which Danny Bryson speaks to a cameraman about Benny and promises to push him to realize his violent nature by Thursday.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Food for Thought Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 11:03:34 Location: Some Diner, somewhere. This is a: Match RP *The camera fades in to a highway, somewhere in North Amrica. It's dark and the road is illuminated by the lights eminating from a small, prefabricated building in a layby somewhere. The camras leaves the Highway and wanders closer to the building. A sign above the door reads 'Andy's'. The lights that appear from the building are stark phosphoresents, bathing the corner of the layby that serves as a parking lot in a sterile, yellowy green glow. The cameraman goes to open the door, when he hears a rasping, whispering voice that stops him dead in his tracks.* 'Do you really want to join them?' *The cameraman tries to turn around but the firm hand on his shoulder prevents it* 'If you step into that building, you become one of them. Stale, sterile, aneasthitised.' *The cameraman turns around, to find that his assilent was not there. Confused, he was about to turn back around when the voice began again, once again from behind, whispering in his ear* 'The sad thing is, they don't realise. They really can't tell that they are being eaten alive by their own mediocrity. There are only a few who can know what it is to be truley alive.' *The cameraman swings round and Bryson stands in front of him, staring down into the lens, smiling.* 'There are only a few in this world who know the secret. They don't realise, but they do. You've come to talk to me about Benny. He knows. Deep down inside, he knows. He knows what it feels like. He knows the rage inside. The feeling of extacy as he rips someone open. He knows what it feels like to bleed, what it feels like to share the pain of others with his own. He knows the glory of release.' *The camera begins to pick up a faint gleaming in Brysons eyes. He is loving this. He looks like he believes every word he says* 'Benny, perhaps, is the wisest.....and also the most foolish. He refuses to aknowledge what he is. Refuses to accept the fire within. The avenue to his awakening will be through pain. He will realise, when I drive him to the floor. When I break him, when I bleed him. When I rent the flesh from his bones, he will know. He will know what it is to be alive.' 'Benny, I really am sorry for what I am about to do. I want you to understand. When I maul you and destroy you. It is something I have to do. It is the only way that I can release you....' *Bryson lets go of the cameraman, who keeps filming, intent, unable to turn away.* 'Thursday night Benny, you have until Thursday night.' *Bryson nods at the cameraman, turns around and walks away Fade out* View Biography
