The Kid (Tag Team Match)

24 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Kid (Tag Team Match) Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #113 Date: 24 March 2003 Location: Jack's Bentley PART 2 – The 'Kid' (Tag-Team Championship) A black Bentley makes its way off the freeway and onto the city streets. It slows down to around 30 MPH as it makes its way around. In the back sits Jack Manson, elbow leaning on the window and a glass of whiskey in hand. He doesn't look pleased. The black divider window between the front seats and the back lowers, and the driver begins to speak. 'We are on the outskirts, boss. Where to now?' Jack breaks away from his quiet contemplation and looks up at his driver. 'Down town. ...He`ll be down town somewhere.' The driver nods. 'Boss?' 'What is it?' 'I know it's not my business, but I can help you look if I knew just who we are looking for, here.' 'Yeah, I know. ...Tell me, John... you know what I have coming up on Thursday, don’t you?' 'Sure thing, boss. The CCW pay-per-view Revolution. It's what you`ve been working for these past few weeks, right?' 'Yeah it is, John... but see, I got a problem here.' John looks in the rear view mirror and catches Jack’s gaze back. 'What kinda problem, boss? I mean, I thought you had this thing all sewn up.' Jack takes a swig of whiskey from his glass before answering. 'Yeah, I have. I’m not even sweating Enigma. Hardcore match or not, the man is no match for me. ...It’s almost a joke, really. No... Enigma knows that he’s got his hands full, and if he has any capacity for logical thought, he would choose not to show up. Because when he does, things'll become problematic. ...For him, of course.' John steers the car around a corner before responding. 'So, what about the tag-team match?' 'That's the problem. Now, I have no problem taking on Stevens & Scorpion. It's just that... it's that damn kid!' 'Adam?' 'Yeah... Adam. I’ll have my hands full with the other two without having to concern myself with that boy. And now he's out doing God knows what, when he SHOULD still be in the hospital resting from Lockdown!' 'Boss? Can I ask you a question?' Jack glares at John’s reflection in the rear view mirror. 'You can ask, weither I answer you or not remains to be seen.' 'Why are you so concerned about the kid? ...Why keep him around? You could just get rid of him, couldn't you?' 'Yes... yes, I could. But he’s useful. He... has something. Something I like. And it needs to be nurtured and honed. This match on Thursday is just the kind of thing the boy needs to help keep him focused. ...Take a right here, John!' The Bentley turns a sharp right, causing a slight squeal of the tires. Jack looks out of the window, looking for Adam. 'This tag match was not something I asked for. It pryed me away from my plans. ...But now that it has happened, I plan on taking advantage. ...I don’t think CCW’s corporate hierarchy really understands what they’ve got on their hands, here. If they think for even a moment that this will be a run of the mill tag match, then I’m afraid they're sadly mistaken. ...You see, this tag match is going to become a benchmark in brutality.' John nods his head in agreement. 'I’m sure that, by now, even Scorpion would have realised he cannot beat me. He’s tried before, and he always came up short. ...As for Stevens, I think he underestimates us. He sits there and whines about us 'not being wrestlers'. ...Well, I care not. ...Let him think what he wants to think. It'll make no difference to the outcome. ...The man's a fool--a legend only in his own mind. But he will learn, just like Scorpion, that you underestimate me only at your own expense.' Jack jerks his head as he spots something outside. 'Pull over! John, pull over!!' The Bentley slows to a stop. 'God, I should have known. I’ll be back in a minute, John. Leave it running.' 'Aye, boss.' Jack steps out of the car and into the cool night air, his breath clearly seen hanging in front of him. ...Off-camera, a commotion can be heard. Jack walks towards it. His face getting angrier with each passing step. He reaches an alleyway and pauses, listening to the sounds coming from within. 'Yes, that’s right!! PISS YOUR FUCKING PANTS!! Maybe I won't wait until this Thursday! WHY SHOULD WE WAIT!? YOU'RE BOTH RIGHT HERE!! ...Maybe I should kill you both RIGHT NOW!! How about it, Scorpion!? What about you, Stevens?!? LET'S DO THIS RIGHT NOW! WE DON'T NEED FUCKING CAMERAS!!! WE DON'T NEED A CROWD!!' A crack is heard, echoeing throughout the alleyway. Jack looks a bit confused. 'GET THE FUCK UP! GET THE FUCK UP!!! HAHAHAHAHAH!!!' Jack steps into the alleyway. Silhouetted in the smog, we see three men; one facing the other two. Jack slowly walks up behind the lone man as he continues to scream at the two others, which appear to be hobo’s. One looks on with bewilderment, while the other appears to have had his head cracked against the wall or something, as blood stains the building behind him and he's toppled onto the ground. ...As Jack moves closer still, the concience homeless man look up, noticing him. And just like they have done many times before, along with their brethren, he recognises the face and flees down the alley. Adam Christ looks on and laughs. 'Yeah, that’s right! FLY AWAY!! FLY THE FUCK AWAY!! But when you land, I'll be RIGHT THERE to fucking SLIT YOUR THROAT!! DO YOU HEAR ME!?' Jack taps Adam on the shoulder. Adam slowly turns around.... and is met with a sharp jab to the jaw. Adam stumbles back, grasping his chin. Jack grabs the stunned Adam Christ by the collar, liting him from his feet and pinning him to the wall. 'Kid... I SWEAR, you`ll be the death of me if I ain't the death of you first! What the hell are you doing!? ...Where the HELL have you been?' Adam, struggling to keep up and trying to stem the flow of blood from his lip, mumbles something incoherent at Jack. 'Oh, nevermind! Just get in the goddam car and SHUT UP before I do even more damage!' Jack turns, shoving Adam out of the alleyway. Adam stumbles, falling to his side, still stunned and in awe of everything. Jack picks Adam back up to his feet. John, standing by the car, opens the rear door as Jack approaches. Jack man-handles Christ into the back of the car and climbs in afterwards. John closes the door behind them and gets back into the driver seat. 'Where to now, boss?' 'Mount Alvernia Hospital.' 'Aye.' 'Oh, and John... close the divider. I don’t want to be disturbed until we get there.' John turns around and the black panel slides soundlessly closed. Jack turns and glare at Adam, who is still holding his jaw. His hands are caked in blood. 'Adam, what the fuck were you thinking? CCW Revolution is just around the damned corner! Do you have any idea of how big this is!?' Adam slowly nods his head, moving his hands forward to stare at his blood. Jack sighs a bit and continues. 'You don't have a damned clue. We're going for the TAG-TEAM TITLES, Adam! We're going against Michael Stevens and Scorpion in less th--' 'I KNOW, I KNOW.' Adam interrupts. Jack looks a bit surprised from that. ...He leans forward, staring at Adam's bloodied face. ...Jack smirks a bit. 'You know, eh? Well, running around kicking hobo's asses isn't going to improve your chances of surviving against Scorpion and Stevens. Unlike them, hobo's don't tend to fight back.' Adam slowly nods, lowering his bloodied hands to his sides. Jack's eyes widen before he screams. 'Don't bleed on my fucking seat! Here!!' Jack hands Adam a towel that rests under the seat. Adam takes it and wraps it around his hands. ...Jack blinks, noticing something. '...Heh. You're high, aren't you?' Adam slowly looks at Jack. Jack shakes his head. 'Mother fucker. And YOU'RE my tag-team partner!? What a crock!' Jack leans back, chuckling to himself. ...Adam remains quiet. Jack breaks the silence by speaking once more. 'Look... when we get to the hospital, I want you to lay your ass down and heal your fat lip. You'll become sober by Lockdown, I'm sure. Just get your act together.' Adam slowly nods in response. The Bentley rolls into the hospital's parking lot. John's voice is heard out of the door's speakers. 'We're here, Boss.' 'Good.' Jack steps out of the car, followed by Adam. Jack waits for Adam to join him, as the two walk through the double doors entering the hospital. The camera cuts to an in-lobby view of the two of them, were Jack is seen motioning Adam to go to his hospital room. Adam does, keeping the towel around his hands and letting the blood drip from his lip, as he exits the camera's view. Jack shakes his head, turning and walking into the opposing direction. ...Jack enters a room, and closes the door behind him. In the room, Dr. Forbes is seen. ...Dr. Forbes steps up to Jack. 'Did you get him?' 'Yeah. I got him.' 'Good.' 'How does he look?' 'Like shit. He'll be ready by Thursday, though.' 'Good. I'll go talk with him.' 'You do that.' Dr. Forbes exits the room, leaving Jack to be in the room alone. Jack steps up to a desk and sits at it, looking through some papers. The camera moves until it's in front of him. ...Jack's eyes raise and look into the camera's lens. ...Jack speaks. 'Michael Stevens... you don't know me. And, of course, I don't know you. However, I've been watching you... and you've proven to be completely ignorant. You sit there, mumbling about us being mindless barbarians, and how the way to defeat us is OUTSMARTING us!? ...Well, while Adam might not be the sharpest knife in the drawer, I certainly am. And I won't be a pushover, believe you me. ...So while you sit there and believe you're pathetic thoughts, hoping you're right... I'll sit here, KNOWING everything. KNOWING of your defeat. KNOWING of the pain you'll recieve. KNOWING of the humilation you'll gain. ...And when you're in your defeat, you can just think about what you said. Until then, shut the fuck up.' Jack smirks, slowly tilting his head as he looks to the camera. '...And then, we have Scorpion. Scorpion, I've proved to you before that you're not a threat. I've beaten you before, and I'll beat you again. And again. And again. You're NOTHING. You'll probably hide behind Michael Stevens the entire time. Well, doing that might be a smart thing. You're nothing, Scorpion. You're purpose in this match is to run around in circles like a headless chicken. And that's probably all you're good at. ...And, you know what?' ...Jack slowly lifts up his Garrotte, a grin crossing his face. '...I might just MAKE you a headless chicken! ...On Revolution, you and Michael Stevens will FALL, and I will hold NOT ONLY the Tag-Team Championship... but the Hardcore Championship as well! Sleep well, Michael... Scorpion... Enigma. ...For it may be your last.' ...With that, the scene fades to black. View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
