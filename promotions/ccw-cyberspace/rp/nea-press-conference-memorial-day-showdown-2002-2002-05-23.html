N.E.A. Press Conference (Memorial Day Showdown 2002)

23 May 2002

What this is A roleplay staging a press conference by the New Era Alliance faction led by Scorpion.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Scorpion Entitled: N.E.A. Press conference (Memorial Day show down 2002) Written in Thursday, May 23, 2002, 15:00:49 Location: unknown This is a: Match RP :::: The N.E.A. appears on the television screen followed by a N.E.A. public service announcement. :::: :::: Public Service announcement: This message is brought to you by the New Era Alliance, the thoughts expressed are not of those of the CCW. :::: :::: The picture fades and there is a picture of the N.E.A. logo in the background and a podium in front of the logo. This time there is media at the podium. T.J. Walker approaches the podium as light bulb flashes from the cameras go off. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: (cover his eyes from the light bulbs flashing) God why can’t you guys wait till I get up to the podium? Anyway I’d like to welcome all of you to another N.E.A. seminar and if anyone has doubt the N.E.A. then they better think twice because if I can recall just this past Monday, on Shocking Monday Night, Scorpion defeated CCW Vet, John Coens and is climbing the ladder in Pool A and he will represent the N.E.A. in the COTR, as may others but without any further ado here is the leader of the N.E.A. the man that has sparked the revolution in the CCW, Scorpion! :::: :::: Scorpion approaches the podium wearing a black suit again. He takes a speech from his inside jacket pocket and begins to speak as flash bulbs go off. :::: :::: Scorpion: Friends, enemies and bastards of the media I’d like to thank you for coming to the New Era Alliance’s press conference here today. This past Monday I proved to many of the CCW stars that this faction is not only for real, but it is very deadly. As in a very hard fought match I defeated well-respected CCW superstar, John Coens in the middle of the ring to prove once and for all that not is the young superstar more agile, but that the younger superstar, the new era, will begin to prosper in the CCW from now on. Now onto this Sunday, Memorial Day Showdown, I, Scorpion along with Chang The Mongolian Giant will take on the team of Gaurdian and Yama Tatsu. Personally I can tell you right now that I do not care for my partner, nor would I ever in my mind ever want to be there partner, but for one night only I will prove that Scorpion can bring even the worst of them victories and don’t worry Chang with me on your side we will win. Now I believe “The Enforcer” Johnny Gringo has some words for the media. :::: :::: Scorpion takes a seat behind the podium as Johnny Gringo approaches the podium. :::: :::: Johnny Gringo: Yo media peeps. Wut up? Anyway I’d like to let everyone know that myself and T.J. Walker have been promoted to talent coordinators for the N.E.A. and that means that all young talent in the CCW will be watch at in the next couple of weeks as the N.E.A. begins to form at full strength to take down the wheelchair huggin’, nursery home lovin’, diaper wearin, old fogies that make the CCW what it is today. Just ask John Coens about the N.E.A. he will tell you what the N.E.A. is all about. Because he got his ass kicked by our leader, Scorpion. It’s time that the young guns come out of the bottom of the card and fight these old pieces of shit that take up the Main Event all the time and it’s time we tell them to get the hell out or we will take you out! :::: :::: T.J. Walker steps up to podium as Johnny Gringo takes a seat. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: If you people didn’t understand what Johnny meant then it’s plain and simple with just a couple of words, your either with us or against us and being against us is not where you want to be, I can guarantee that. Because the N.E.A. is getting stronger in all aspects, soon newer members will be up here at this podium joining the fight to stop these vets from taking over what is right fully ours, the top of the CCW! :::: :::: Scorpion steps up to the podium. :::: :::: Scorpion: I think we will take questions from the bastard gallery now. :::: :::: A reporter raises his hand. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Go ahead Jim. :::: :::: Jim: Ok my question is for Scorpion, Scorpion what do you think of your tag team partner Chang? :::: :::: Scorpion: No doubt that Chang is a monster and one hell of a wrestler but I would never in my right mind ever tag with that guy. I truthfully don’t know how in the hell CCW really made this match. I don’t have any beef with Guardian or Yama Tatsu and I really don’t get along with Chang. This is just another way CCW can hold the younger talent down like myself. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Hey go ahead Bill. :::: :::: Bill: What do you think about your opponents Guardian and Yama Tatsu? :::: :::: Scorpion: They are two great competitors, they are great young talent and they are being held back from climbing the ladder due to the fact that people like Danny Bryson, Carnage and Mr. Gobstopper are stuck in the main event status, they don’t belong there and they are staying there, so it’s really terrible for CCW not to flourish there younger talent. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Ok one last question. Go ahead Harry. :::: :::: Harry: Why did you form the N.E.A. and will there be anymore members? :::: :::: Scorpion: First off that was a very stupid question and I hate repeating myself. I formed the N.E.A. because I have had enough of watching the vets return and the vets that have been here take up the main event space for way to long, it’s time for a change. Maybe if the CCW changed then the ratings would go up. The N.E.A. equal ratings and yes there will be more members to the N.E.A. that are wrestlers. That’s all I can say now. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Ok we gotta go guys. :::: :::: Light bulbs flash as the N.E.A. walks off the stage. Then the N.E.A. logo appears on the screen. :::: View Biography
