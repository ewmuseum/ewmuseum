What this is A Match RP in which The Valiant One is ambushed by his trainer Dirk during training ahead of a match against Fanatik.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Valiant One Entitled: Learning from Dirk... Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 08:20:22 Location: The Factory This is a: Match RP *The scene opens up inside of The Valiant One's training facility, which is just known as The Factory. TVO is unwrapping his wrists after a long training session.* The Valiant One: Dang..after the ass kicking I got here today, fighting Fanatik won't be a problem. *In the background you can see Dirk sneaking up with a folding chair.* TVO: I really think I am going to be able to take anything Fanatik will throw at me on SMN. *Dirk gets right behind TVO and pulls the chair back over his head.* TVO: There has got to be no way, even though I am only this far along, I bet I'll destroy Fanatik on Monday. *Dirk swings.......BAM!!!!* Dirk: Dang it Joey...you senses aren't getting much better or maybe I am just too good at creeping up on people. *TVO moans on the floor in pain as he slowly gets up.* Dirk: Fanatik is going to lay waste on you if you don't always suspect the unexpected. TVO: Well I don't think he is going to creep up on me in a dark factory with a chair! Dirk: Don't talk back to me! TVO: Yes Sir. *TVO has to show his respect to Dirk as he is almost twice The Valiant One's age and his mentor.* Dirk: Now, you better wrap those wrists back up, because we aren't done here. TVO: Dang it! *TVO begins to wrap his wrists up again as the scene fades to black.* View Biography
