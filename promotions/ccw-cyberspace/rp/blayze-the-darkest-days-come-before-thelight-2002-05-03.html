Blayze - The Darkest Days Come Before TheLight

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Blayze writes his hatred for Kensuke on a sheet of paper ahead of a Shocking Monday Night match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Blayze Entitled: The Darkest Days Come Before TheLight Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 23:57:31 Location: Unknown Location This is a: Match RP [He writes the name in anger. He speaks the name with hate. He thinks of the name in rage and disgust. As he writes, the words become stronger and stronger] Die Kensuke Die.. [The pen scribbles all over the scripted sheet] Die Kensuke Die.. [It is not ink that drips from the pen..but fresh blood squeezed from the pulp of a human] Die Kensuke Die.. [Blayze strokes the pen, his eyes engulfed in madness and hate. Evil surrounds his mind, body and soul without control. He has a destiny..a destiny to destroy Kensuke & Spider] Die Kensuke..MUTHA-FUCKIN' DIE!! [He drops the pen, his fingers trickling in blood. He slumps his face into his palm and shakes his head] Blayze: What was his motif? Why did he do what he did? Why the FUCK did he get into my PERSONAL BUSINESS! Spotlight, its all about the fuckin spotlight isnt it Ken. Now with Fanatik by my side, you knew your fame and glory days were over but you just had to force the fans to become your followers once again and try to fuck my career over. I have had it..FUCKIN HAD IT! [The pen lifts from the table and suddenly ends up half away across the room] Blayze: We all know that the darkest days come before the light. During the night, any creature can strike without notice and nothing can be done about it. On Monday night, your dreams, destiny and faith, along with your new found bitch Spider, will fall into the hands of M2S where no escape is in sight. I wish I can say that you could still back out of this situation but both you and Spider made this the last straw. [Blayze licks the inches of blood of the tip of his fingers, savoring it with his tongue and cracks an evil grin] Blayze: Everyone wants a piece of the hottest commodity in CCW to this date. From Ricky Thunder to Jack Panzer to Estep To both your nimrods. Disposing of both you two will seal my faith above the CCW platform and once I TKO Spider and bring home a World Title Shot..CCW's days of 'happyness and success' will soon be a distant memory as Blayze will turn the tide and unleash all unthinkable nightmares. My weapon and creation Fanatik is near any sort of 'joyous mood' and burying you morons 6 feet under will be a dream come true [Blayze rises from his seat and flips his table over but graps the sheet of paper] Blayze: Look at it carefully..it reads DIE KENSUKE DIE! These are not thoughts that will stay kept in my mind but a soon to be lived reality. I never speak of death without making sure it happens and like my brother would say..Welcome To Death Row..your journey..ends..HERE! [Blayze rips the paper in half symbolizing the end of Kensuke and also Spider..Will Blayze succeed in his efforts on SMN..Only time will tell] View Biography
