The Edge of Darkness part 3

23 February 2003

The Edge of Darkness part 3 Devyl CCW Promo #27 Date: 2/23/03 Location: Preacher's Corners [What has gone before: A group of faculty members from Boston College have gathered together in a small, secluded cabin near the outskirts of a small town called Preacher's Corners. They were attempting to open a mythical gateway in space and time to another plane of existence where the could discover the wonders and mysteries of the universe which science has yet to explain. The group was 'assisted' by Victoria Devins, Devyl, who did her best to open such a portal. She wanted a demonic sacrifice. What she got... was something completely different.] [Inside the cabin in Preacher's Corners... The living room, to be more specific...] [The Angel Gabriel, if one chose to believe it's claims, hovered in the center of the pentagram in the middle of the room, wings unfurled and yet bound at the same time by the same black leather it wore all over it's body. Even it's face... Mouth, nose and eyes... were covered. It's pure white skin peeking through the leather in patchwork pieces. Devyl, still standing at the head of the pentagram, stared up at him in something almost akin to awe. Marion Allen, and the rest of the professors, looked on in shock. Brilliant white light continued to pour into the room between the cracks in the wall and through the ceiling above them.] [Devyl swallowed hard and tried to speak, but her mouth was dry, and no sound issued forth. She couldn't believe what had just happen. In all her years of performing such an ritual, nothing like this had ever happened. Her mind worked quickly, trying to figure out what had gone wrong, or what had occurred differently to summon this creature into their midst.] [Cecil Jones, Marion Allen and Rupert Merriweather were all motionless... Staring at the creature. A sharp strangled cry issued out of Crawford Harris's mouth and he averted his eyes. Robert Menkin took one look at the creature in front of him, and his sanity suddenly snapped like a piano wire pulled too tight. He stood up from his place on the floor and leapt forward, a hoarse cry of indignation issuing from his lips. The creature merely grabbed his oncoming body, lifted him high into the air, nearly to the ceiling, and with no effort, snapped the man's neck. The lifeless husk was tossed off to the side, landing in the lap of Harold Copley. And that's when the screaming started. Harold lost it... Braying out loud shrieks and started clawing at his own eyes as if to remove the sight of his colleague's fractured body.] [The creature... Ignoring everyone but Victoria... Seemed to turn it's attention back on her.] Devyl: 'You are no Angel. What are you really?' [Gabriel did not speak right away, Harold's shriek's of insanity the only sound filling the air. But when he did, his voice was dark and hollow, filling the air and seeming to originate from inside of Victoria's own head.] Gabriel: 'The Lord's host. I smite the unfaithful; the unbelievers. As you all are.' Devyl: 'If you truly are what you claim to be, then do as you threaten. Smite us all where we stand.' [But the 'angel' doesn't move from it's spot. It continues floating in mid-air.] Devyl: 'What are you waiting for? If the God of Love wishes us dead, then follow His command! Kill us all, Gabriel!' [And once again, the creature does nothing.] Devyl: *sly grin* You can't, can you? You are held in place by the pentagram. As with all demons, you are held and entrapped. You cannot strike at us unless we cross the plane. You are bound here until you are dismissed. [There is another pause. Harold's shrieking continues. Marion and Rupert are with him attempting to pull him away from the pentagram. Cecil and Crawford continue to stare at the being, enraptured, lost in their own little worlds...] Gabriel: 'You are mistaken. You know nothing. When the Lord wills it, you will be destroyed.' Devyl: 'Well then, the Lord should start willing. I am going to dispel you 'Gabriel'. I am sending you back from whence you came. And I firmly believe that you cannot do a single thing to stop me.' [Gabriel remains hovering in place as Victoria starts chanting once again. Her voice grows louder and gains confidence as the creature makes no move on her. Her voice, mixing with that of the insane screams from across the room, almost sounds like music. The brilliant lights shining into the room begin to fade and dim as Devyl's voice rises and falls. The light is nearly gone when Gabriel raises his hand, stretching out in front of him as if trying to reach the chanting woman. And then, swiftly, clenches his fist. Devyl immediately loses her voice as her windpipe constricts. She stumbles back, more in surprise and shock than anything else, and bumps against the wall. Her hands come up to her throat, trying to loosen the unseen pressure that's preventing her from breathing.] Gabriel: 'Your powers are worthless. Your dark lord has no dominion over me. [Crawford, who has been in a trance up until this point, suddenly comes to life. He reaches out with his right hand and smears the half-dry paint on the floor that makes up the pentagram. And suddenly, with a blinding flash of light, everything changes...] [The light from outside the cabin is gone. The only illumination is that provided by the flickering candles. The creature, however, is still present, but no longer flying. It steps out of the pentagram, it's feet making whispered scratching sounds on the floor. Gabriel pauses, 'looking' down at Crawford, who can only stare upwards at the towering figure.] Gabriel: 'The Lord thanks you. You will see no more evil.' [And the angel reaches down and plunges two of it's fingers into Crawford's eye sockets. Crawford lets out a heart-curdling scream as his eyes are harvested from his head. But he doesn't die. Instead, he falls onto his side, whimpering and crying, tears of blood flowing freely from the gaping and empty eye sockets.] [Without another thought or glance at the others, Gabriel calmly walks through the room and out the door, into the night air. As he leaves, Devyl finds that she can suddenly breath again. Gulping in huge amounts of air, she falls to the floor coughing and hacking. Harold's shrieks and screams continue. Victoria looks up at the frightened faces of Marion, Rupert and Cecil, and then over at the sightless Crawford.] [Silently cursing herself, she realizes she was right about the thing all along. It was not an Angel. Angel's are never summoned, nor do they appear to murder any of God's creatures. It was something else... Something that needed and wanted to gain a foothold into the world. And with Crawford's foolish actions, it did just that. And now, whatever it was, it was out there and on the lose. Although many people would assuredly die, it was not in the interest of herself or her master to let that happen. Satan wanted souls. And when this thing killed, who knows where those souls went. In the end, there was really only one option... Find it. Stop it. Kill it.] [fade out...] View Devyl 's Biography Back
