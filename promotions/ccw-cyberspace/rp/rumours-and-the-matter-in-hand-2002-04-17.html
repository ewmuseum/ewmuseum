Rumours And The Matter In Hand

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An interview-class roleplay in which Angel and Guardian are approached by a teenage fan in a Long Island arboretum.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: Rumours and the matter in hand Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 13:39:56 Location: The Bayard Cutting Arboretum This is a: Interview Long Island NY The Bayard Cutting Arboretum A place for peace, for rest and relaxation. The sun is high in the sky. Birds are flying (Duh – they’d be crap birds if they weren’t). The area’s flowers are in full bloom. Colours and smells collide and overpower one’s senses. – Its just a nice day OK. Angel is stood by a small shrub – she is picking a particularly vibrant yellow flower. She places this in amongst her long red hair. The effect is beautiful. Guardian as always is by her side. A teenager in the distance spots the pair and runs over. Teenager: “ Hey! Its you isn’t it? Angel and Guardian! Angel: “Yes child, it is us” Teenager: “Oh man I knew it!! I saw you guys come in here! You guys in town for the show over at Nassau Coliseum?” Angel :” That we are. Are you a CCW fan?” Teenager: “OH HELL YEAH©. Only like the biggest fan in the whole of Long Island! Man I was so psyched to hear you guys were touring here. Guardian is fighting TVO right?” Angel: “ Its true Its true ©” Teenager: “That’s gonna be awesome! He`s one tough S.O.B©” Angel: “He is nothing but a sinner child, a man who’s soul Guardian will claim” Teenager: “eh?……er……..yeah……right………then after that you got yourself your first title shot. You guys must be pumped about that right?” Angel: “ Pardon? We know nothing of this child?” Teenger: “ Aww come on guys…….i know all about it, I read it online at some news site – said that Ricky Coyote was mad about his loss last Monday night and has put up his belt at the upcoming Pay Per View©” Angel: “ Child……………..we know nothing of this………..the internet is full of lies……..full of smut…….full of false idols………do not believe all you read” Teenager: “ But..but….It even has a quote from Ricky on his own website saying this………Don’t you guys WANT a title shot?” Angel: “ We do not come here wanting what we do not have…………….if HE deems that we should have…..then HE will provide…………..Greed my child is still a sin……………..if Ricky wants another chance to cleanse himself………..if he still cannot come to terms with his wrong doing………….then………..then we shall see. Never look past the task at hand……. whatever will be will be………….Let us first deal with TVO and his followers tomorrow night………and then………..then if HE says so……….Ricky Coyote will once again feel HIS power.” Teenager:” Errrr……riiiiiiight… ….well I gotta go! Err……bye!” With that the teenager, rather taken aback, runs of back towards the direction in which he came in. Angel turns to Guardian “IF this is true………..and if HE wants this to be so……….then this time there will be no second chance for Ricky Coyote……..but first……..we must dispose of TVO…….lets just hope Ricky is watching………maybe then he’ll change his mind” View Biography
