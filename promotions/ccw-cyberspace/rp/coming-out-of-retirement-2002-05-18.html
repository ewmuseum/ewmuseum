Coming Out Of Retirement

What this is A comedic match-class roleplay in which a retired champion films a return promo from a Hawaiian beach and calls out two opponents for Memorial Day Showdown.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Paul Arovnik Entitled: Coming Out Of Retirement Written in Saturday, May 18, 2002, 11:42:35 Location: Beaches of Maui This is a: Match RP Gull: Caw! Caw! [The beaches of Maui. Cool. Pristine. Warm.] Paul: Uh, a little bit to the left, darling. [We see the blue sky, not a cloud in sight...] Paul: Wait. Less teeth...no, more teeth. There! Oh yeah... [The camera pans down, and we see CCW's Greatest Of All Time (Only Triple Champion, Hall Of Famer, blah blah.) He wears his patented surgical scrubs, only they've got a traditional Hawaiian pattern of flowers and palm trees. He sits under an umbrella, sipping on a mint julep with a little paper umbrella in it.] Paul: Stop. [The bobbing red head (Nancy Rachet-Arovnik) by his crotch stops bobbing for a brief moment. She's in a white bikini, very small, naturally. Paul looks up at the sky...] Paul: Okay, go. [The bj resumes, and Paul turns to face the camera.] Paul: Well hello, CCW. Glad to see me back? [Paul smiles winsomely.] Paul: I hate to disappoint, but I'm not the same person I was when I left. I'm tired of living that lie in order to get a seat on the CCW Board Of Directors. [His smile falls a bit.] Paul: You know how it is. [He resumes smiling.] Paul: But take it from me, the Greatest Of All Time is back, and better than ever. [Nancy's head bobs more affirmatively than oral-sex related.] Paul: So here I am...on the beaches of Hawaii. Recieving incredibly good head. Bonding with my son Charles... [The camera pans over, and there's another umbrella, with a stroller under it, and a 1 month old baby dressed EXACTLY like Paul, complete with his own tiny mint julep. They ARE refreshing.] Paul: ...what need do I have to come back to CCW? Nancy: Mrfffhrgfrrrgrm! [Paul ignores Nancy attempt to answer Paul's rhetorical question with a mouthful of dick.] Paul: Well, I hear that two people are having a match for the Best Of All Time at Memorial Day Showdown. Now...I've never lost to Phil Burner, and we've tangled PLENTY of times. [Nancy nods, and Charles Manson Arovnik carefull takes a drink from his (refreshing!) mint julep.] Paul: And this...Supreme Machine. I never was pinned by you either. And on Monday, we have our first contest. The Consumate Professional versus the Consumate Egomaniac. Sumy...I already DID the 'Supreme Machine' thing. Sunshine The Automaton was more complete, more powerful, and more 'Supreme' than you'll ever be! [Nancy, incredibly turned on, dips her hand into the bottom of her bikini, and Paul slaps it away.] Paul: Save it. [Nancy whimpers slightly around Paul's wang.] Paul: There you have it. On Monday, I will pin you, Supreme Machine. And when I do, there will be NO question as to who the greatest of all time is. Moi! You haven't studied up, you don't know me. You don't know the kind of fear that I inspired in my enemies. [He pauses for the briefest of moments...] Paul: But I know you. Inside and out, upside and down. After all, what else would you expect...from a consumate professional? [As Paul says this, he releases a loud and enormous fart. Nancy, in a feat that would make ANY good woman proud, keeps chugging Paul's dong.] Paul: Ha ha! Don't worry folks, our relationship has weathered worse! See you peons on Monday! [Charles Manson Arovnik gurgles happily, as we are reintroduced to...] 'Passive-Aggressive' Paul Aronvik View Biography
