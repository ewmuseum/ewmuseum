What this is A journal-style roleplay in which Cianan reflects on blackouts, a therapist and his upcoming Independent Title match against Mr. Gobstopper.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: Journal Entry Written in Tuesday, Jun 11, 2002, 22:29:14 Location: Home? This is a: Match RP Journal Entry: Today, I realize that I have no clue who I am. Everything just feels so jumbled, and insane, my mind is constantly swirling, trying to deal with everything that's going on. I don't know how to explain it, it's confusing, I guess, my body doesn't like the Noir, but hey.. Another thing that's confusing, Akiko. I want to talk to her so badly, I want to just go up to her, and wrap my arms around her.. but something is stopping me. I don't know what, it's something inside. We haven't talked... Lately, I've been blanking out. To be honest, I'm rather fearful of it. Hours, and sometimes days go by, without my recollection. A few times too many. I'd go to the doctor, but how they would effect to the.. foreign substances in my system, I'm not sure, so I won't go. Sylkk, has also requested that I go see a therapist.. Him of all people. Might as well by Kyle Lamen, or Brad for that matter. I will NOT see a therapist. My problems are my own, and no one else's. LDT- Cianan vs. Mr. Gobstopper -Indy Title. This.. is strange, I've never faced Gobber before. He was a former CotR champion, a former 'great.' Former, is a word used for the past. Gobstopper is history, just like Arvonik, just like SuMa. Do they really have any relevance towards the future? We learn from the past, to make sure we don't make the same mistakes in the future, yet, we let SuMa and Gobber, two past 'mistakes' back in. No more mistakes, coming back to bite CCW in the ass. I fear for Gobstopper, and, I pray that I don't kill him. I'm not sure about myself anymore.. At least, I knew when I hurt or killed someone before, I was aware of it. I fear. View Biography
