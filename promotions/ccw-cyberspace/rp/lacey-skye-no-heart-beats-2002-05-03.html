Lacey Skye - No Heart Beats....

3 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Lacey Skye and her husband Doug spend a day in a park after her title win over Devyl.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Lacey Skye Entitled: No Heart Beats.... Written in Friday, May 3, 2002, 12:28:03 Location: A park near Pittsburgh This is a: Match RP Lacey: **voice over** It finally happened.... The big match with Devyl. And I managed to come out on top, despite the odds. But this victory might be short-lived. Mr. Raymond has ordered a rematch for SMN, once again giving Devyl that which she thinks she deserves. And after what I just went through to stop Devyl.... To get this belt.... If she thinks I'm just going to lie down and hand this title back over to her, she's sadly mistaken. But that's a worry for Monday night. I know I have what it takes to beat her. And I WILL do it again, regardless of who I have to fight my way through. For now, it's time to slow down a bit and take stock of my life and what I'm doing. There's something that I need to do before anything else.... Slow down and relax a bit. I haven't done that for quite some time now.... **Lacey is lying on a blanket in a small park on the outskirts of Pittsburgh, a novel in front of her and a large jug of ice tea to one side near a picnic basket. She's flat on her stomach, reading through one of her old favorites by Stephen King.... The Stand. It was lengthy, but well worth the read. And on this nice day of sunshine, it didn't seem like anything could ruin her good feeling.** **But it's not long before a shadow crosses over her blanket, blocking out the sun. Lacey looks up at the source of the shadow, and smiles in surprise. She quickly gets to her feet and nearly leaps into the arms of her husband, who has just arrived. The embrace and hug tightly.** Lacey: Doug!! I'm so happy to see you!! Doug: **smiles** I happy to see you, too. I managed to catch your match on television Thursday night. Congratulations, Honey! I knew you could do it! Lacey: Thank you! Thank you for all your support, Doug. I wouldn't have been able to make it this far if it hadn't been for you. Doug: Sure you could've. You're a lot stronger than you care to admit. **They release their embrace and return to the blanket. They sit down side by side, and Lacey reaches for the food.** Lacey: Hungry? Doug: Yeah. I haven't eaten in a while. Lacey: Did you take care of that unpleasant business back in Rhode Island? Doug: Yes and no. It's a long story and I'm not going to bore you with it, but everything is basically at a standstill. Despite all the evidence, it's the kid's word against mine as to what really happened at the gas station. Ronald Meachem still claims that his son is innocent of all wrong doing, and apparently has half the city of Providence ready to vouch for him. In the end, this was nothing but a diversionary tactic.... Meant to throw us off-kilter and put our guard up. Lacey: **quietly** Unless the kid succeeded, in which case you would've been dead. Doug: **short pause** Yes.... There's that too. But it didn't happen, and it's not going to. I'm going to be around for a long time, Lacey. Lacey: **smiles** You better be! **Lacey hands Doug a sandwich and some grapes before getting some for herself. They eat in comfortable silence for a few moments...** Doug: We missed celebrating your birthday. Lacey: **nods** I know. Doug: I'm really sorry about that, hon. I have a present all ready and waiting for you, but it's back home. Lacey: It's all right. With all we had going on, it's not like I was really paying attention to the date. There were far more important things to deal with than my measly birthday.... Doug: But I still think we should do something about it. So, what would you like to do tonight? Pick anything or anywhere, and we'll do it.... Money's not a problem. **Lacey sits in silence for several seconds, thinking it over. And then she smiles to herself, knowing that thinking about it is rather pointless. She knows exactly what she wants to do....** Doug: So....? Expensive Dinner and a movie? Lacey: No. I just want the two of us to spend the day together. No talk of work.... No cases, no wrestling.... Let's just forget about all of it and be with each other! We haven't had a day like that since.... Since.... Doug: Since forever. I'm definitely up for it if your sure that's what you want. Lacey: **smiles** Of course! So why don't we kick it of with some fireworks.... **Lacey leans over to Doug and gives him a long and passionate kiss. Doug returns the kiss and the sparkling energy feeling that the kiss generates invigorates them both. They break the kiss briefly and smile as they stare into each others eyes.** Doug: How about we skip to the grande finale? **And they lean in, embrace and kiss once again. The camera pans away from the young lovers to the rest of the park.** Lacey: **voice over** This is long-needed and long-overdue. No heart beats like both of ours, moving in time with each other. I know that might sound like I'm turning into a hopeless romantic, but where Doug's concerned, I've always been one. This will probably be one of the last days Doug and I will be able to spend together before our careers send us our separate ways once again.... And before I have to get busy tracking down Devyl's sister. It's obvious that she's not going to rest until she has the title belt back, and will go to any lengths to get it. So Devyl.... You need to be stopped. For good. Taking the title away from you was only the first step. Now, I need to, in the words of your late mother, save you. Bring everything you've got on Monday, Devyl. You're NOT taking the belt away from me. You've held it and abused the power it gave you for far too long now... View Biography
