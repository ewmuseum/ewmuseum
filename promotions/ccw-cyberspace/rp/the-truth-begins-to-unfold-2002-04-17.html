The Truth Begins to Unfold

17 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long scene in which Jack Panzer, after a nightmare flashback, reads documents revealing that his wife conspired in his ambush, while the conspirators plot his capture in Boston.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: The truth begins to unfold Written in Wednesday, Apr 17, 2002, 02:38:13 Location: New York City This is a: Match RP **The scene opens to some weird shots. We see Jack Panzer, among several other men, wearing full UN Peace Corps uniform and carrying an AR-15 around his neck, smiling and laughing, as his friends and him chat among a friendly game of poker inside a very small and dark room. The room has only a door and a small window, about 1 inch x 3 inches. Jack is standing near the peep hole, looking to the outside, gun on his hand. Once in a while, we hear talks and laughs in a different language, which seems to be German. The scene fades to white, and we're granted a different shot. This shot is a shot of a nice house, very cozy home. A big living room, with decorated couches and chairs around a nice fireplace, lit. To the right, a small round table, set for dinner. Three plates are set over it. The camera walks a little further, and we see the stairs, leading to the second floor of the house, and a big arch, an opening on the wall, leading to the next room, a big, nice TV room. The windows, open, the bright white silk curtains gently shaping the cool breeze of the german spring nights. Toys, dolls, board games, spread on the floor, near a big TV, showing nothing but static. The scene fades to white again, and this same shot appears again. This time, the TV is on 'Bananas', the sun is rising, the windows are closed, and a young, 4-year-old girl is sitting in front of it. She watches the TV, combing the hair of a nearby Barbie. She looks back and smiles, showing she just recognized someone. 'Daddy', she says, as she opens her arms, reaching the camera. The scene fades to white again, and the scene goes back to the night shot. The empty, sad room is shown again. This time, no toys. Windows shut, furniture covered with white, dusty blankets. The windows, with Xs marking them. The scene moves around, checking the stairs. The camera goes up, finding itself in a T-junction. A door in front is the bathroom. Nothing special about it, dark, humid, cold. Empty. The scene fades to white and once again, it changes. The bathroom is the same, but it's a sunny day. The bathroom is decorated with flowers, tapestry, it's colorful, with shampoos and other cosmetics on the small table over the sink. The sound of water running from the shower can be heard. From behind a green curtain, the curves of a woman can be seen. She sings a melancolic song, as she bathes. The camera walks forward, trying to reach around the curtain, but when it finally beats the barrier, the scene fades to white again, and we see the same empty, black-on-white, cold bathroom again. The camera walks out, moving to one of the rooms, the one to the camera's left. The door opens itself, and we see a children's room, judging by the fading wallpaper, seeming like a wallpaper with painted animals and flowers. A big garden. The scene fades to white once again, and it shows a brighter, more colorful wallpaper. The camera gives a 180 turn, and we see a very decorated, rich child room. We can see a small cabinet to the left, open, with the top drawer filled with toys and dolls. TAt the end of the room, a small door to the closet. On the middle of the room a rich persian carpet, where the little girl, in her blond curly hair and deep blue eyes, play with a small jeep. A nicely fixed bed, with little Disney characters in it, and the scene fades to white again. It reopens with the same shot, showing Jack, with a small book, sitting in a small wooden chair, by the side of the bed, reading what seems to be Snow White, judging by a drawing on the cover of the book. The little girl, sleeping, face of an angel. The scene fades to white again, and we now see an empty room. The decaying, fading wallpaper on the wall is the only thing we can see. The camera walks out of this room and walks down the aisle again, passing the stairs and the bathroom door, opening the new door. We see another empty room, but one pink bra can be seen on the floor. The scene fades to white, and comes back to the shot of the same bra, only this time we can hear the characteristic sounds of people finishing making love. The scene checks the floor, looking around, and several pieces of clothing can be seen: A black short skirt, a white female shirt, a big red purse, two red, high-heel shoes, thrown around in disarray, and also camouflage pants and shirt and military boots. The scene comes up, slowly, showing a shot of a big, King-sized bed, and cuddling around, we see Jack Panzer and a young woman, with black short hair. The scene closes on the woman beautiful, sleeping face, as Jack looks at her, closely. The scene then shows her opening her eyes, slowly, and then calling for him: 'Jack... Jack... JACK...'** JAAAAACK!!! **A big flash changes the scene completely, and we're now having a close on Jack's face. His face is completely paralized, as if he has just seen a ghost or a demon. The camera changes the shot for a complete shot of the place. We're back at the hotel room where Jack and Nicky decided to hide, after Jack retrieved his personal file, assembled by the germans and given to a local shrink to try to make an ambush, from which Jack escaped. Jack is sitting on a cheap wooden square table, put next to the wall, across the two beds, one of them where Nicky is laying, laptop on her lap, wearing a tight pink cotton top and black cotton pants. She looks at Jack, nervous, who still looks at the paper. She says:** Nicky: Jack, what's up? Jack (finally snaping out of the trance): Sorry, Nicky... It's just that... This is unbelievable... Nicky: What, the fact that your wife is behind it all? Come on, it can't be that bad... Jack (looking at her, eyes beginning to get wet): Well, I wish it wasn't that bad... **Jack picks the document and slowly places it over the table. He puts both elbows over the desk and puts his hands on his face, in complete despair, looking down at more documents. Nicky gets up, gently placing the laptop on the bed and sliding out of it, walking straight to the table, where she sits and looks at Jack. She gently puts her hands on Jack's wrists, pulling them apart, so she can see his face. One of them are let go, but Jack firmly holds the scar and his blind eye. His left hand, trembling, his good eye, raging with fire of hate, despair, disappointment and revenge. Nicky looks at him and says:** Nicky (softly): Come on, Jack, you can't let yourself fall like that... Jack (sobbing): You don't understand, Nicky... (raising his voice) Everything I knew... Everything I fought for... (the fire burning his soul appears on the tone of his voice) Everything I survived for... (roaring) Everything I thought was good for me... (screaming) Everything I thought was true... is nothing but a big LIE!!! Nicky: How bad is it? Jack (completely out of control): How bad? Look for yourself! Documents, letters, sent to Sheila... Nicky: Sheila? Jack: My wife... letters, sent to her, reporting her that her plan of making me disappear had worked. Responses from HER, thanking the army for ridding her of a cancer... But this was the worst... (presents a small piece of paper to Nicky.) Nicky (looking at it but not understanding, because it's in german): What is this? Jack: A deposit receipt... Nicky: What? Jack: A bank deposit... On Sheila's bank account. Nicky: How much? Jack: 10 million marks... Which is about 2 million dollars... Nicky (surprised): You gotta be kidding me... Jack: No, I'm not. And this letter states why she got that money. **Nicky grabs the letter Jack gives to her, and starts reading it out loud:** 'Dear Sheila, We already have nailed the bastard who have been staying on our way. Your husband, as we agreed, has been ambushed on his top secret mission on Congo. Your information on his destination has made it easier for it. I hired a few local gunmen to take the unit off and kill them all, but to make the bastard stay alive and just torture him a bit. Now, we can finally claim the gold on that mine he was gonna secure for the government. If it weren't your help, our little group of friends would have never got it. As a little advance on our deal, I'm depositing a share of the gold we already extracted from the mine. It's not much, but there's more to come. I know how hard it was for you to stand his ugly face, while we planted him on the commandos, but his purpose was fulfilled, and now we can get what's rightfully ours. I'm coming back in a month. Then, we can head to the Bahamas and live the life we always wanted in our dreams. Love, DD **Nicky, completely shocked, looks at the desperate Jack and says:** Nicky: This... This is... terrible... Jack: I know... Nicky: And who is this JP guy? Jack: I don't know, and that's something I'll have to find out tomorrow... Nicky: Tomorrow, what do you mean? Jack: I'm leaving tomorrow to the Bahamas, to search for those two bitches!!! Nicky: Jack, you can't. You have your matches, your career. And the german forces are still on your tail... Jack: Don't you understand, Nicky? I can put an end on all this. If I can get the people who did this and turn them to justice, I'll finally have some peace... Nicky: That is, until they find you and throw you on that rot jail house you escaped, right? Jack: But... Nicky: NO BUTS!!! You have a career now, you have a life. You must let the past go and leave your life. Jack: Sure thing... I guess you're right. Nicky: Of course I am... Besides, have you seen the size of Bahamas? It's HUGE!!! Jack: Yeah... There's gotta be a better approach. And there's one other thing, too. Nicky: What's it? Jack: This thing about them knowing when I'm in a CCW show... Nicky: What do you mean? Jack: I already told you my theory, right? There's probably someone, a wrestler, that has been giving tips to the germans, because I called a couple guys backstage, and they said there was no germans making questions, unlike the times I was there. Nicky: Really? Jack: Yeah. They told me once that there were suited guys, talking in heavy accent, following me around, trying to know where I go, what I do, when I'm gonna wrestle. And only once they tried to attack me, and it was on that Duke game... Nicky: Yeah, I remember... Jack: So why are they scouting that kind of information, and who's the man tipping them off, telling them I'm on the arena? And what's that man getting for it? Nicky: Yeah, now that you talk, a snitch is a very possible explanation... Jack: That's right. I believe I have to get that bastard, if I want some peace of mind during my matches... So, who's the bastard this time? Nicky: Darren Jordan, Blayze's brother... Jack: Ahn, I know the man. He's the one who was involved in that whole lame murder story that put Blayze on the jail for some time. I read something about it. Well, it should be a fun match. Darren, as I heard, gave his brother a hard time, and frankly, it was just bad luck that Darren did not advance in the tournament. Nicky: Yeah, I saw the match... It was brutal... Jack: And yet, pointless... Those two fight each other, slice their fleshes, break bottles on each other's heads, bleed like nothing and for what? One little wrestling move later, it was all done. Nicky: Well, think of it this way: Their whole fight makes them into the same state of mind you go when in a match... Jack: That's true, and that's why I must not let this thing mess with my mind. I must concentrate, if I wanna beat Darren. I'm sure someone will try to beat him during our match, and quite frankly, I don't wanna lose by DQ. I want his ass, fair and square, in that ring, so I can take the rust out of my joints. It's been a while since I fought... Nicky: Yeah, good... That's the spirit, Jack... Jack: But yet... I wonder who the hell is 'DD'... The man who stole my life... Nicky: Come on, Jack, don't think about it. Have some rest, you have to get some ring rust out for tomorrow... Jack: Cool... **Jack gets up and leaves the documents on the table. Nicky and Jack walk to their beds and lay down. Jack sleeps right away, and Nicky stares at her computer, looking at Jack occasionally to see if he's ok. The scene fades to black and reopens inside a rich business office. We see a nice oak desk, with a iBook over it to the left of the table. Some documents laid over it, a few portraits to the right, two small trees, one at each side of the wall behind the table, a huge window, with a view of a metropolitan area, with its bright lights illuminating the night, and a huge, president-style chair, turned to the view. A small cordless phone rings over the table, and an arm, with a few rich diamond rings and what looks to be a nice Armani suit, picks it up. The grave voice of a man can be heard talking to the phone in a heavy german accent:** Man: Hello... Oh, it's you, my friend. So, do you have any news for me?... Oh, so he was booked again, and this time he confirmed he's going? Good... Don't worry, your payment is on the way... I know it's dangerous, but you'll have some backup, if he finds out about you... Don't worry, everything will be fine, when we get our hands on him... Good... Ok, see you later... **A click can be heard, and then the sound of a number being dialed can be overheard. A few moments later, the man behind the chair continues:** Man: Yeah, it's me... We got him... He's gonna wrestle on the Fleet Center, in Boston, Massachusetts. I suggest you be there and do NOT fail this time... Very well, your payments will be shipped today... Ok, later... **The man hangs out the phone, and begins dialing a new number. This time, his voice tone changes, from serious to calm and lovely!** Man: Hi, honey... Yeah, I'm still at the office... Don't worry, I'll be home later. I suggest you put that bottle of champaigne on the ice, because this time, we got him... Yeah, we got him... I know you wanna see his face, but now it won't be possible. When we get his ass deported back here, you can have all the fun you want, but tonight, you're mine, baby... **The scene shifts to a shot of a nice penthouse apartment's bathroom. In the tub, taking a bubble bath, we see the woman on Jack's dreams: Her wife. She says:** Sheila: Yeah, and you'll be all mine, baby... See you later... I love you... **Sheila hungs the phone up and laughs, as she enjoys her bubble bath. The scene focus on her evil smile, as she thinks her goal is near, and the scene turns back to Jack, lying on his bed. A split screen showing Sheila's joy and Jack's misery is shown, then turns to the office, focusing the chair, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
