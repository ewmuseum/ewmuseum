Roni Savar - Airport Trouble..CCW Contract Signature..

30 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is An Interview-type RP in which Roni Savar is detained at an airport and then signs his CCW contract.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Roni Savar Entitled: Airport Trouble..CCW Contract Signature.. Written in Tuesday, Apr 30, 2002, 15:44:52 Location: Unknown This is a: Interview SAVAR'S PROLOGUE: He hates everyone..Except..Well,no one..He hates everyone..PERIOD.And he hates William,his so called 'skitzo friend '..He doesn't know how long it'll go on,but it looks like..NEVER. {There we see Roni Savar in handcuffs at a disclosed airport..He is on a tiled floor,and in back of him is three security guards,to follow it up with two police officers,holding them down with all their might,and the police officers hold him down with their nightsticks..Savar try's to speak but the tiled floor blocks his mouth,enabling him to speak..One of the police officer's speak towards a downed Savar,in heavy caution.. Police Officer: 'We were told that you had announced,that you had a bomb on you..You know that that is a major felony..And you can be put in jail for that.' {Savar try's to speak but the security guards and the other police officer,purposely push him to the floor,enabling him to speak..Once again..} Police Officer: 'Nothing to say,eh?What's a matter with you?!?!Who dare you say such a thing on a plane,especially when they are heavily cautious these days.' {Savar suddenly leaps up,tossing the security guards over him,and the other police officer..They hold his knees,stomach,and try to take him down but to no avail..He finally gets to speak toward's the officer..} - - 'SaVaR' - - 'Listen..I have found out,that I contain a skitzo in my body..I have no way to stop it,his name is William and he does whatever he wants to and he decided to say that while the plane was relaxing,and as safe as possible..And announce that he/we had a bomb,even though we didn't..You can check my bags,my luggage..I won't have anything in it.Nothing but some shorts,shirts,briefs,and glasses and some basic drinks from germany.' Police Officer: 'So you are from GERMANY?We are not sure about the whole skitzo thing...We'll have to send you to the hospital first,and check you up with a specialty doctor,who works on these things.' {Savar nods even though he winks..The police officers,and security guards escort him out of the airport..The security guards go back in as the police officers take control..But as everything seems okay..The beast elbows both of them to their skull..Knocking them down,on their buttocks'..They try and grasp his legs,or some kind of limb but he gets away by running in a slow pace...He turns the corner,and at the corner a whole crowd is there..He goes in to the middle and just keeps running..The police officers are out of sight and not seen from there on...Savar disappears from the crowd and not seen a bit..} ( Two Hours Later..) {Roni Savar is seen walking up in front of the CCW talent agency..Coincidentally he finds it and goes inside..He seems confused but goes in..The place is cool from the air conditioner functioning,the roon is painted white and full of wrestler portraits..In front of Savar is a desk and to the side of the desk is a man with glasses,down to his nose and a black suit with a blue tie draping down his neck,he looks at a file with a few documents in it..Savar whistles a bit,getting the man's attention..He drops the file to the desk,puts his glasses down,and begins to speak..} Man: 'Well,hello..Are you someone applying?' - - SaVaR - - 'Applying?No..I called in a few months ago and recently got a call from you guys..You gave me an address,and of course I remembered it and came.Might you know who I am?' Man: 'Many people have called in and got a response and I do not know all of their names...I might know it but I don't like to mention the wrong names..So what is your name?' - - SaVaR - - 'My name is Roni SAVAR.' {He holds his pointer finger up as in saying 'hold on a minute'..He searches his file cabinet and finally pulls out a file from the few minutes of searching...} Man: 'Right here we have you..Roni Savar..Frankfurt,Germany..So would you want to sign your contract now?' - - SaVaR - - 'NOW!' Man: 'Sure.' {The man pulls out a document,from Savar's file..A pen from his suit pocket,points at a place on the document,and hands Savar the pen as he sloppily signs his name 'RONI SAVAR'..A bit wavy..Savar signs it a few more times and finally finishes as the man shakes his hand..} Man: 'Well..Thank you very much..Now you are a exclusive wrestler of the Cyberspace Championship Wrestling.Now I am going to give you the address of the arena..You'll need it.So keep it for future references.' {He grabs the pen once again..Takes out a small portion of a paper and begins writing down a few numbers and letters..Savar smiles even though he doesn't know what that is..The man hands it to Savar as he shakes Savar's hand..} Man: 'Well,thank you..Give us a call if you'd like for any questions,etc..I also wrote it down there.Thank you once again.'(Shakes Savar's hand) {Savar leaves in glee without further notice..He hops around,views the paper and just runs off as he scans the horizon on the process..From there nothing else can be seen but all that is heard is bird's chirping in to the day light..} END View Biography
