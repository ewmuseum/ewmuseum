What this is An Interview-type RP in which The Valiant One, on the New Strangers’ bus, resolves to stop caring about being valiant.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Valiant One Entitled: Wasting My Time.. Written in Wednesday, May 1, 2002, 08:26:29 Location: New Strangers' Bus This is a: Interview *The scene opens up on The Valiant One sitting around wearing his New Strangers jersey and jean shorts. He is just sitting around on the New Strangers' bus and looking out the window.* The Valiant One: It seems as if no one even knows I am here anymore. I mean a few months ago I was pitted to become the next World Champion very soon...but now...now that is very unlikely. With the return of The Supreme Machine, Sylkk, Fanatik, and now Mr. Gobstopper I will have to work my way through them. Time has come that The Valiant One forgets about all that is good. The time has come for me to just not care anymore. The time has come for me to just lay waste on anyone who steps in my way. I can't be worried about what the fans will think or what the little kids who look up to me will think...besides right now all they see is loser, because that is what I am lately..just a giant loser. I am about to turn that fact around, and there is nothing anyone can do about it, and my fellow New Strangers will be unable to slow me down if they get in my way as well. Time for The Valiant One to forget about valiantness....it's time The Valiant One kicks some ass... *The scene fades to black.* View Biography
