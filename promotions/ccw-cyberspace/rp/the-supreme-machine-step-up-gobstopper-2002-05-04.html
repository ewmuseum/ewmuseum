The Supreme Machine - Step Up Gobstopper

4 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which The Supreme Machine cuts a promo on Mr. Gobstopper ahead of their Monday night match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main The Supreme Machine Entitled: Step Up Gobstopper Written in Saturday, May 4, 2002, 06:00:9 Location: A Private Training Facility This is a: Match RP Mr. Gobstopper.... I can't believe that you, of all people, are stupid enough to agree to face me. The Supreme Machine stands alone in his private training facility. His long black hair is tied neatly back and his face is covered in perspiration from another of his grueling workouts. He reaches down and grabs a towel that he uses to wipe the sweat from his face. You must seriously be a glutton for punishment. Your first match back and you sign up to once again do battle with the greatest heavyweight champion in CCW history. You are truly more of a fool than I ever gave you credit for. What other explanation is there for this suicidal act? He throws the towel down and continues. Summer of the Gobber?!? YOU having something over ME?!? I think your year out of the ring has affected you mentally. Summer of the Gobber?!? Please...... The Supreme Machine chuckles softly to himself for a few moments before taking a long sip of PowerAde and continuing. I beat you all those years ago in the first COTR, I know you remember that, but do you remember why I beat you? Can you remember why you lost? I can. I beat you simply because I am better than YOU! I always have been and always will be and there's nothing you could have done in the three years since to change that. He takes another sip of PowerAde and continues as the camera begins to slowly zoom in. Now, I'm as sentimental as the next guy but be warned Gobstopper. Come Monday night and I'm looking across the ring at you, there will be no room for sentimentality. It will be business as usual and I think you can remember what that means....you've been beaten by me before......you know what it feels like. I came back to CCW for a reason Gobstopper, and beating your sorry carcass yet again isn't it. I came back here to fulfill my destiny and you, Mr. Gobstopper, are not going to get in the way of that. I won't let you. The camera continues to zoom until The Supreme Machine's steely grey eyes fill the screen. On Monday night I'm going to beat you, and I don't mean just beating you 1-2-3. No Mr. Gobstopper, I'm going to beat you like you've never been beaten before. I'm going to make sure you realize what a mistake you've made in signing for this match. I just hope that you've got yourself a sponsor for the soles of your boots because that's the only part of you that the fans are going to be seeing on Monday night, because you're going to be spending a lot of time laying flat on your back in the ring. So I'll be seeing you Monday night Gobstopper. I promise it will be a night you long remember... a night when I reacquaint you with a CCW ring. A night when I reacquaint you with pain and suffering. A night when I beat you senseless. And you know why I'm going to do all of this for you Gobstopper? His eyes seem to burn through the camera lens. BECAUSE I CAN! View Biography
