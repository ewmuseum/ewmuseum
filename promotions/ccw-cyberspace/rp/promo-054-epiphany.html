Epiphany

3 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Epiphany Adam Christ CCW Promo #54 Date: 03/03/03 Location: [ The scene opens within Nick Cashew's office. Nick, sitting behind his desk, has the phone pressed against his ear. ] Nick: Hello, Hotel Six? Yeah, is Adam Christ in...? ...He checked out? Alright, thank you. [ Nick hangs up the phone. He grumbles to himself a little... ] Nick: Where the hell did he run off to...? First he attacks Yama Tatsu after Lockdown, then he doesn't shot up for his match?! That boy's not going to go very far in this business if he keeps pulling off stunts like that... [ Nick sighs a bit, standing up and walking around his desk. He grabs his jacket from the coat hanger and slides it on his shoulders... ] Nick: I might as well look for him... [ Nick opens his door and steps exits his office. The scene then cuts to an alley somewhere, where a figure is seen in the distance. As the camera pans closer, it becomes apparent that the figure is... Adam Christ. ] Adam Christ: Why... didn't they cheer? ...Why... [ The scene cuts to a flashback of Lockdown, showing Adam cracking a crowbar across Yama Tatsu's skull. ...The fans react to this by booing. ...Adam panics, grabbing his hair and sliding out of the ring, running backstage. The scene then cuts back to the present time, where Adam is walking along side of a fence, dragging his shoulder against it as he does. ] Adam Christ: They cheered for HIM when he made ME bleed... but, why didn't they do the same for me!? Why...!? WHY!?! [ Adam turns, pressing his face harshly against the fence. His mask is missing, obviously, as the wound Yama created on his forehead weeks before re-opens itself. Adam pulls his head back, an ear-to-ear smile crossing his face as blood trinkles down his brow. ] Adam Christ: That's it... I understand! I UNDERSTAND!!! [ Adam jerks over, walking out of the alley. The camera follows as Adam stops, coming across a dumpster of sort. He opens it's lid and looks inside, reaching in and pulling out... a fork? ] Adam Christ: I understand...! ...They... they cheer... when I bleed! Not when Yama... but when I BLEED! I GET IT, NOW!! [ Adam shoves the fork into his arm, closing his eye tightly to surpass the pain. He walks around the dumpster, rushing at the concrete wall and cracking against it, skull-first. He stumbles back, holding his head as a mask of crimson covers his face. He seems to be... laughing. ] Adam Christ: HAHAHAHAHAHA!! Do you hear it!? I HEAR IT!! DO YOU!?!? [ All sounds seem to mute, other than Adam's voice... and the sound of thousands of people cheering. As Adam winds his head back, cracking it harshly against the brick wall once more... the noise seems to grow louder. As Adam rips the fork from his arm only to shove it in his gut, the noise grows even louder. Adam, somehow still standing, looks like a character from a horror film. ...He walks forward into a highway, his eye looking glazed over, as a car screaches to a hault inches before running him over. The car honks franticly as Adam hunches over it's hood, grinning at the driver... whom is none other than Nick Cashew. Nick steps out of his car and rushes up to Adam's aide. ] Nick: Jesus, what the hell happened?! Adam Christ: DO YOU HEAR IT!? DO YOU!?!? HAHAHAHHAHAHAHAHAAAHAHAAAAH!!!! Nick: Hear what?! ...Just, calm down! I need to get you to a hospital...! Adam Christ: Hospithh... [ Blood pours out of Adam's mouth, causing him to gag a bit. Nick steps back a little, but quickly rushes Adam to the passengers side of his car, getting him in. Nick runs around the car and climbs into the drivers seat, screeching away. The camera then cuts to an inside-car view from the backseat, showing the two of them... ] Nick: Jesus Christ... you're getting blood all over my car! Adam Christ: ...It doesn't hurt. [ Nick looks at Adam. ] Nick: You're in denial, Adam. Adam Christ: ...It feels good. Nick: ... [ Adam's head tilts up a little... ] Adam Christ: ...They didn't cheer, Nick... Nick: Who cares, Adam!? If you keep letting the fucking crowd run your life, you'll end up killing yourself! Adam Christ: THEY DIDN'T FUCKING CHEER!! I thought they'd cheer!? They cheered for HIM! FOR HIM!!!!! Nick: Adam, stop talking! You need to save your strength...!! Adam Christ: ...Why...? ...They like me like this... this is what they pay to see. Nick: Who? The people? ...Well, they do pay to see brutallity... but not like this! This is... inhumane! If I hadn't shown up, you'd be laying in an alley or something, bleeding to death! Adam Christ: And how would that make anything different? ...No one knows me. ...No one cares. Nick: Stop talking like that, damnit! You've just been in CCW for TWO WEEKS! You can't expect people to suddenly LOVE you! What the hell were you thinking?! [ ...Adam stays silent. ] Nick: ...Well, in any case, you have a match this coming Thursday... yet I'm not sure if you'll be ready to fight by then. Adam Christ: ...Who am I against? Nick: Gabriel Nyte and Toxic. It's a tag-team match... Adam Christ: ...Who's my partner? Nick: Jack Manson. Adam Christ: ...I've never heard of him... Nick: Well, I don't think he's heard of you, either. [ Nick pulls into the hospital parking lot. ] Nick: Alright... we're here. [ The scene, then, fades to black... only to come back up a few hours later, showing Adam to be laying in a hospital bed within the O.R. He's awake, and he's staring upward. ...His head is wrapped in bandage, as with his torso and left arm. ...Suddenly, Nick enters the scene. ] Nick: Hey, man... how are you feeling? Adam Christ: I'm fine. Nick: Good. You'd better recover by Lockdown. ...You need to start getting a few wins on your record. You're becoming dead weight. Adam Christ: ...Am I? ... Nick: ...Look, CCW doesn't want a wrestler that's just going to swoon and be depressed all the time. If you continue not showing up for matches, running away, and... mangling yourself, CCW will probably let you go. [ Adam suddenly sits up with surprising ease. ] Adam Christ: They CAN'T let me go! ...Why would they!? Nick: I just told you. It's no use having you compete in matches if you beat the shit out of yourself before hand. It's nonsense. Adam Christ: ...I'm sorry. Nick: ...It's alright. Look, just spend the rest of the week getting prepared for your match this Thursday. I'll check back in with you later. [ Nick exits the room, leaving Adam to sit there. ...All of this looks all too familiar as Adam stares blankly forward. ] Adam Christ: ...They thurst for my blood... so, I will give them my blood. [ A smile crosses Adam's face as he lays back... leaving the scene to fade to black. ] OOC NOTE: I'm sorry about the crappily-short RP. I just got done doing my PTC RP's, and I need to get started on my English homework. I'll do better next week, I promise! View Adam Christ 's Biography Back
