Attempted Suicide (GT RP)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A two-part roleplay in which Mark Estep appears to attempt suicide from a bridge and later hosts a visitor at home.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Mark Estep Entitled: Attempted Suicide... (GT RP) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 16:50:44 Location: A Bridge, and Mark Estep's House This is a: Match RP April 25th, 2002 'Hate. Such a strong word that any decent man wouldn't use towards another man. But in the world of wrestling, there is no emotion more evident than hate. Hate between the men, women, and officials of the CCW show behind the scenes, but we try to keep it professional in the ring. This Thursday, I'll be stepping into the CCW ring for the first time against Chang, The Mongolian Giant, and John Linsey. As much as I would love to verbally rape them once again, I have more important things to worried about, like the Pay Per View, Group Therapy. I have a shot at something that I haven't had a shot at since my old XCWF wrestling days, the World Heavyweight Title. All I have to do is go through about 35 other men, with 4 mystery men. I've been waiting for my chance at stardom for a very long time, and this Sunday, I'm going to show everyone why they call me 'The Malicious One'!' [The scene opens up with darkness all around. A cool breeze rolls across a paved ground, with raindrops flickering through the air when a street light turns on. The camera is looking up at the street light and it hears someone shivering down below it. The camera slowly pans down to show a man in a large overcoat sitting on his butt at the base of the lamp post. He has long brown hair dangling down in his face, and it's soaking wet from the rain, and he looks up to reveal his face as Mark Estep. He smiles in-between shivers and points at the camera. He turns his hand over and bends his finger to motion for the camera to come closer. It does and the camera zooms in on Mark's deep brown eyes. He stops shivering just enough to talk.] 'The Pay Per View,' he says, 'Is going to be a good time for the Malicious One. It will prove that I am here to make a statement, and that statement is that I am here for a reason, and I will not leave Madison Square Garden without the title that I will be trying to attain.' [He gets to his feet and begins to walk down the road a little bit until he comes to a bridge, that staggers about 300 feet above a river way down below. He leans on the guardrail that keeps cars from hitting the pillars that hold the bridge up.] 'Group Therapy,' he states, 'will go down in history as the greatest CCW Pay Per View in history, and the future. Not only because of the card lined up, but because the Malicious One will make his PPV In-ring debut with a chance of coming out as the world champion at the end of the night.' [He stands up and steps over the guardrail and hangs around one of the pillars holding up the bridge. He grabs onto a cable and leans over the side of the bridge looking down at the deep, cold water. The rain continues to pour down on his head, and he flips his hair back behind his head and picks one foot up to step off the bridge. He looks back over at the camera and smiles with a huge grin before speaking again.] 'I have a strong team at Group Therapy, so I'm not worried about getting through the first couple matches of the night,' he says, 'but two matches after the team finals, I'm going to have to go face to face with the rest of my team. I know I will not be the marked man in this match, because I'm the newest man in the federation, and because the World Champion and top contender is also on my team. But, the match I'm worried about is the individual finals, when I have to step in the ring AGAINST my partners for the night, including the same World Champion and top contender. And I'm going to go all out. I'm going to make sure that they don't think that I'm just a new guy, that's going to lay down for the veterans of the group, because I won't. I refuse to lose! I refuse to quit, that is, until the day I die....' [He leans off the side of the bridge to fall into the water until two lights shine on his back and a woman's voice is heard in the background. Mark stops and turns at the horrific screaming.] 'Mark!' yells Amber, his fiance, 'What are you thinking!? You have a huge match in a few days and you are out here trying to kill yourself? What the hell is wrong with you?! Sometimes I worry about you Mark, you have really changed.' [Amber grabs Mark's arm and pulls him back onto the bridge before he fell 300 feet to the now raging river below. The rain pours harder and harder as they hug, and Mark steps back over the guard rail and walks over to the car that Amber has parked slanted in the bridge, almost against the guard rail. Amber drives across the bridge as the scene fades out.] April 26th, 2002 'Sometimes I scare myself. Common sense touches most people, but it must have passed me on the way. The train to success also passed with common sense, and it is finally making its way back to me. I don't know when to keep my mouth shut, and my ego in the bag. I have a reputation for picking fights with people that could kill anyone else that is my size and stature, but so far I have come out unharmed. The ironic thing about that is It's not the large men that put me out, it's the men that have trouble opening the pickle jars, and wine bottles, that beat me. Last night at LDT, I showed the world that I will soon be the man to beat, after taking out Chang the Mongolian Giant, and John Linsey. CCW, Get ready... because this is a wave of maliciousness brewing.... Can you feel it? Can you handle it? Because.... You can't control it!' [The scene opens up at Mark Estep's house. He is watching CCW's latest edition of LDT from the night before. He keeps rewinding and rewatching his match so he can check his mistakes. He has a note pad on his lap, and a pencil in his right hand and he is scratching down notes. The door bell rings and Amber walks through the scene as Mark continues to write things down. Sounds of the door opening and two people talking can be heard in the background. Amber walks back into the room with Mark and gets his attention.] 'Mark,' she says, 'you have a visitor...' Mark turns his head and puts his left knee up on the couch as Duke Williams, the PCW President, walks into the room and stands in the corner. 'Hey Duke,' he says, 'come sit down.' Duke walks over and sits down next to Mark on the couch as he turns his attention back to the television, and the match that is playing. The tape shows Mark hitting Linsey with the Estepper and pinning him for the win, as Duke also takes notice. 'Ya know,' he says, 'that move looks like it hurts more and more every time I see it.' Mark chuckles and replies, 'Heh, yeah, to bad not a lot of people in PCW got to feel it...' Duke nods in agreement as Mark scribbles one last thing on his paper and then throwing it on the coffee table in front of him. 'So what do ya need?' asked Mark. 'Nothin,' replied the PCW prez, 'Just wanted to see how you were doin after getting fired from PCW, and all. I see you got another job now, huh?' Mark lets out a small sigh, 'Ah, yep. I'm doing pretty good so far. Hey, ya wanna beer?' 'Sure, hoss....' Duke replies. 'Amber,' Yells Mark, 'can you get us two beers?' 'Sure babe...' replies Amber, and Duke looks over at Mark. 'Two beers? You don't want anything for yourself?' he says, and they both engage in a nice long laugh. Amber comes back in with two beers and Duke opens his and begins drinking quickly. Mark tries to get his bottle open, but has problems, then when he finally gets his open... 'Cheers....' says Mark, looking at Duke while holding his bottle in the air. Duke pulls the bottle away from his mouth and burps, having just downed the entire bottle of beer. Mark shrugs his shoulders and drinks his beer... 45 minutes later.... 'Honey, see this man?' says a now drunken Mark Estep, 'This man here, this man,.... he's the man. He is, and I...' Mark tries to finish, before passing out on the couch. Duke stands up and gets Mark's arm off from around his shoulders and walks to the door. 'Thanks for coming, Duke. Come back soon' says Amber, 'I'll sure try, but do me a favor before I come back, please....' says Duke. 'What's that?' asks Amber. Duke laughs and replies, 'Teach that boy how to drink!' Amber laughs and shuts the door behind Duke, who walks down the steps and gets in his S 10 Pickup Truck and pulls away. Amber walks into the living room and pulls a blanket over Mark, who is still laid out on the couch. She walks around the coffee table and turns the television off and she turns the lights out, and heads to bed. The scene fades out once again. OOC:Once again, this could have been better, but I have a wedding tomorrow, and today is rehearsal, so I got my priorities.... View Biography
