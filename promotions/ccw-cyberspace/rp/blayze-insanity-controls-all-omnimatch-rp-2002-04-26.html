Blayze - Insanity Controls All(Omnimatch RP)

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP of short verse fragments in which Blayze scrawls phrases on a hotel bathroom wall in the lead-up to the Omnimatch.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Blayze Entitled: Insanity Controls All(Omnimatch RP) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:31:25 Location: Hotel Bathroom Floor This is a: Match RP What Have I Done…… What Have I Said…… Has Insanity Crossed My Mind……? [It repeats] What Have I Done…… What Have I Said…… Has Insanity Crossed My Mind……? [A shaken hands scribbles the phrases over a tiled wall] What Have I Done…… What Have I Said…… Has Insanity Crossed My Mind……? [As the hand writes, the paintbrush that fiddles through his fingers and the red liquid drips so elegantly from the firm brush tip. Although, there is no sign of elegance from the meaning of the phrases written on the wall.] Can They Survive The Suffering….? Can They Walk Out Alive…….? Can They Compete At My Level……? [It continues, only a hand is shown but from what we see, it is a black skinned hand that adds the finishing touches to wording.] Hate, Greed, Power…… Sweat, Tears, Pain……. Blood, Violence, Sin….. [It ends with the most crucial 3 words of them all, the hand drops the paintbrush and clutches its wrist. The camera pulls back, while in shock, views the horrid image of a bloody Blayze, laying on his hotel bathroom floor, trembling for no apparent reason. Blayze: Allah..Save Me.. [He scribbles a few more words on the wall] Omni-Hell…… Death Will Do Us ALL Part! [The camera fades] View Biography
