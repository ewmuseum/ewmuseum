Family Tree Part 2 Lacey Skye Morris CCW Promo #127 Date: 04/07/03 Location: Seattle, Washington The Seattle Public Saftey Building. Chloe Mason and Lacey Skye Morris walk outside through the front doors together. Chloe is talking non-stop. Lacey: Undercover Agent? That's good, Chloe. Chloe: Woa! Are we in a bad mood? Wait, you just got back from shopping, right? So maybe that's it. Shopping fever, and no good sales! Lacey: Why the orphanage, Chloe? Chloe: Or maybe you and Jonathan are arguing again. You know, men, who can figure them out anymore.... But Lacey is fixing Chloe with that stubborn glare of hers as she stands and waits for an answer. Chloe: Why the orphanage.... That was the question, right? Lacey: Right. Chloe: Ahh.... you know, return to the old alma mater. Lacey: That's the orphanage where you lived? Chloe: I spent a year or so there when I was, like, five. Then I did the whole foster home drill. 'Hi kids, this is your new foster sister, Chloe. Treat her just like one of the family.... Not!' A short pause as Lacey evaluates Chloe. Then.... Lacey: Come on. Jonathan was concerned about you. They get into Lacey's car and drive away. Lacey's car can be seen parked outside the Jonathan's antique shop. Inside Jonathan's studio.... Jonathan is sweaty, and works on some abstract piece of sculpture. His body moves, bends and flexes in all the right ways. Lacey finds herself approving as she watches from the doorway. Jonathan: She wouldn't tell you why she stole the file? Lacey: I could guess. She wants to know where she comes from. Jonathan: Why can't they just tell him? Lacey: It's against the law. Besides, maybe they don't know. He turns and looks over his shoulder at her. Jonathan: Why do you say that? Lacey: {shakes her head} It's nothing. Jonathan mops his brow with a shirt sleeve. Jonathan: Art can be hard work. She notes his look and smiles to cover up her uncomfortableness. Lacey: Jon.... I know that look.... I know you want to be more than friends, but I can't do that right now. He frowns a bit, then shrugs. He walks over to her, pulling off his gloves as he goes and tossing them onto a nearby workbench. Jonathan: Maybe we can help Chloe find out who her parents are. Lacey: I don't think that's such a good idea. Jonathan: Why not? And off of Lacey's resigned look.... Later in the day, Chloe and Lacey are out in the side alley between the antique shop and the building over. Lacey stands near the alley door of the shop as Chloe puts her street smarts to use as she works on Jonathan's T-bird. Chloe: Why didn't I ask you for help? Look, like it takes a while to develop the 'ask for help' reflex. Lacey: Did you see anything in the files that you didn't already know? Chloe: I just got a peek, you know. Saw a name: Emily Mason, deceased.... Lacey: You think she's your mother? Chloe: I guess. I already knew she was dead. I remember.... A moment. Lacey remains silent. Chloe: I was real little, but.... Again, she can't bring herself to explore the memory. Chloe: But, hey, I scored a three-pointer on my dad at the buzzer. Just before the rent-a-cop grabbed me. Lacey: You learned something about your father? Lacey is uncomfortable for some reason. Chloe: Yeah. Jack Mason. He and Emily were still married when she died. So.... you know.... I guess he IS.... my father, I mean. Lacey: Chloe.... If your.... if he was your father.... Don't you think he'd have.... ah.... looked you up by now? Chloe gets a cute little sheepish grin on her face. Chloe: When I was a kid, I made up a story. I pretended he was, like a spy or something. Doing dangerous stuff behind the iron curtain or whatever. So he couldn't just, like, put in a collect call and say: I want to talk to my daughter, Chloe Mason, the all-American girl! Chloe smiles. Then.... shrugs. Chloe: Kids stuff. Now.... who knows, maybe he has tried to find me. I mean, it's not like my face is on a milk carton. Lacey: {tosses Chloe a nearby tool; smiles} You going to finish up out here? Chloe: Sure. {short pause} Thanks for bailing me out. Lacey: It comes out of your pay. Chloe gives a 'you're not serious' look as Lacey starts inside. She pauses just inside the doorway as faint memories of her own childhood flicker through her head. She shakes them away, not welcoming the intrusion, and heads off. She finds herself reentering Jonathan's Studio. He watches as she enters the room and lights a blowtorch. Lacey looks up from her somber reverie as Jonathan speaks to her. Jonathan: You think we should help look for him, then? Lacey, still warding off old memories, looks up, confused. Lacey: Who? Jonathan: I overheard you two talking. Jack Mason. Chloe's father. Lacey: Oh. I wouldn't lay odds on finding him. Later that evening, in the loft above the antique shop.... Lacey is asleep with her head on Jonathan's lap as he reads. Lacey suddenly flinches awake, coming out of a nightmare. Jonathan strokes her head. Jonathan: Shh. You had a nightmare. Lacey: Yeah? Lacey sits up, smiles at him. He continues reading as she stares into the fire. The memories that she tried so hard at warding off come flooding into her with a vengeance... Morris Residence; 1984 A flickering, controlled fire burns in the fireplace across the living room. Lacey's mother, Claudia Morris, stands in the corner of the room, staring angrily at her young daughter. Lacey, face bruised and scratched, lies on the couch, the now-familiar stubborn look etched on her petite face. One of the doors leading to the livingroom is flung open and the fireplace sputters as the air currents change. Richard Morris, Lacey's father, stands in the doorway. His face is pulsing with barely contained anger and his breathing is ragged. He peers across the room, eyes wide with anger. It looks like he's ready to explode. Richard: {voice choked with rage} Could you possibly explain to me.... why, in the name of God, did you attack our guest's daughter this evening? Do you realize how important this client was to me? Do you realize how much money you just cost this family, because of that STUPID, IMMATURE STUNT OF YOURS!? His eyes bug out as he glares at his now-cowering daughter. Lacey sits up on the couch. Only five years old, she doesn't truly understand why her daddy is so mad at her. Lacey: She.... she hit me first. Richard: {incredulous} It doesn't matter! What did I tell you this afternoon? 'Be on your BEST behavior!' That WASN'T your BEST BEHAVIOR!! Lacey: {confused and hurt} Daddy.... Richard Morris swiftly walks to the couch, grabs Lacey by her strawberry blonde locks, and viciously yanks her into the air and onto her feet, bringing cries of pain from the little girl. Richard: We never should have ADOPTED you, you little BRAT! We would have been so much better off with another boy! Claudia: Richard! Richard Morris drops little Lacey to the floor where she cries and sobs miserably. He looks down at her for a short moment before leaving the room, staring intently at his wife on the way out. Richard: Clean her up and send her to bed. I should have never listened to your crazy adoption talk in the first place! And then Lacey is back in the present, still sitting on the couch and staring into the fire. Her father was a very unkind man. She remembered the events of that day, and how deeply they hurt her young, fragile spirit. And although she never heard her father utter those words again, not for the first time, she wonders to herself if her father had been speaking the truth that cold evening. It's a long while before she's able to once again fall into a fitful sleep.... View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
