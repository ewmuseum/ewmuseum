PTC Night Of Champions RP #1 - The Whole Past Revealed

21 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long interview-class roleplay in which Jack Panzer and Nicky White review documents about his past in an Atlantic City casino room.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Jack Panzer Entitled: PTC Night of Champions RP #1: The whole past revealed Written in Tuesday, May 21, 2002, 22:31:31 Location: New York City This is a: Interview Date: May 14th 2002 Time: 3:47 PM EST Place: Atlantic City **The scene opens in what appears to be Atlantic City. The scene shows the board walk, right outside the beach, as the sun starts to set. Many tourists, coming from several places in the board walk, admire the golden scenary. There are whole families, newlywed couples, old gamblers, bouncers, croupies, prostitutes, everything that makes the normal way of life of a casino town. The camera gives a 180 degree turn and it shows the entrance of a huge casino. The door is the head of a huge lion, engulfing people to his stomach of lust and gambling. The camera looks around for a while, looking at the people walking up and down, until it focus on a young couple, a very peculiar one, walking to the casino. The guy, tall, spiked, military-style cut blond hair, very strong, wearing a white CCW T-shirt and black jeans, with a huge pair of ray-ban shades covering a scary scar running through the left side of his face, and the girl a short young girl, short curly snow white hair, fireman red lipstick enhancing her lips a mile, and wearing a nice blue dress. For those not used to CCW programming, the so-called 'stupids', these people are unknowns. But for those who DO follow closely CCW, they'll recognize them as being CCW rookie sensation 'The War Machine' Jack Panzer and Nicole White. Jack and Nicky walk into the casino, and the scene chages to the inside, where they meet a young waiter, wearing a green uniform. Jack tells him something, and the waiter understands, leading them. The place is spetacular. First going through the slots, with the night losers and the poor old ladies, sneaking around the machines, waiting to see if one of the guys camping on the machine will walk away before cashing a jackpot. Going through the cheap crap and 21 tables, going more inside the semi-circle, they are lead around the cashes, right through the core of the casino, where is the baccarah tables for the high rollers, past the cashiers, and right into a small door, with two security guys standing at them. Jack and Nicky looks at the two heavy guards, but Jack simply smiles. The man opens the door by putting a small card on a slot and typing a 6-number password. The door opens, and they walk into a green meeting room. Inside, a big poker table. The waiter leaves the room, and both of them sit down on each side of the table. She says:** Nicky: Are you sure this is the only secure place on this town? Jack: I'm telling you, if you want to stay here and have some fun gambling, then we must discuss this here and now, because it's the only place on this town without a camera watching us. Nicky: Ok, now I see it. Jack: Cool, let's get to business... **Jack pulls a big yellow envelope from under his shirt and lays it on the poker table. The waiter comes in the room and brings a small laptop and a portable phone. Nicky thanks him, and she opens the laptop. The scene fades to black. It reopens to a shot of them, now more comfortable, with a coke on ice. Jack looks around the documents he's got from the envelope. Nicky says:** Nicky: Jack, to this day you have to explain me what's the whole story behind you? Jack: Sorry, Nicky. I can't... Nicky: Why? Jack: Because, girl, I don't want you more involved into this... I mean... **Jack passes his hand on his huge scar, looking down and speaking in a lower voice, showing the change on his mood.** Jack: I don't want you to end up like me... **Nicky opens her eyes and feels sorry for him. She reaches his other hand, which is leaning over the table, petting it. She says:** Nicky: Jack, you're a great friend, my life right now is about following you. I know you don't want to admit it, but you need me. Tell me the whole story, so I know where I'm stepping into. **Jack looks at her, petting his hand, and finally nods. Nicky picks up a glass of coke, as Jack leans on his chair, looking at her. He finally lays down the law:** Jack: It all began 6 years ago. The german commandos begun securing military strategic areas, to prevent them from going on the wrong hands. Nicky: Yeah, I remember, and I remember you went for an uranium mine that could go into terrorists' hands. Jack: True, in Congo. It was Zaire back then. The dictator was Mobutu Sese Seko, we're talking about a guy more vicious than Pol Pot, someone that made Hitler look a boyscout. This guy would kill for nothing, just so he could steal more money from his country. We heard he was trying to attack an international power plant to steal the uranium mine near it, and knowing that uranium is used to make atomic bombs, we needed to step in and prevent this tragedy. Nicky: Yeah, but that wasn't true, right? Jack: Right... The locals trapped us and killed my squad and then tortured me, making this horrid thing to me... **Jack puts his hand on his scar once again.** Jack: They knew where we were going, they had been tipped off. They thought we were from the United States, trying to invade Congo. Their natural nationalism made for the rest. Nicky: And how did you went to the prison in Germany? **Jack stops, trying to remember this terrible part of his life.** Nicky: If you don't wanna tell me, that's ok, I understand it must be hard... **Jack keeps trying to look on several directions on the table, trying to find some information.** Jack: Blank... Nicky: What, Jack? Jack: My memory... is blank. I can't remember how I got there. I only remember collapsing in pain after the ambush, and then waking up in the prison... Nicky: So, what's the real story behind this? **Jack picks up a few documents on the table. One of them can be read top secret. Jack looks at Nicky and says, with a clean look on his face.** Jack: This document was sent by Dan, my friend on the german army, to the Congoan government, telling them of a Delta Force squad from America that invaded the country. This proves that Dan set us off... Nicky: But he couldn't have access to this info, could him? Jack: Yeah, he could... But only from an outside source. Someone within the high rankings of the german army. Someone who plotted this whole ordeal. Nicky: I see... Jack: And then, there's Sheila... Nicky: Your wife? Jack: Yeah... **Jack reaches on his pocket, looking for his wallet. He pulls up an old grey wallet, throwing it over the table. Nicky seems curious. Jack opens it slowly and then starts looking at the bills part. He pulls out a couple of 5 marks' bills and finally he pulls a picture from it. Jack throws it to Nicky, who grabs it.** Nicky: Is this them? Jack: Yeah... He introduced us. But he had a clear purpose. **Jack picks up another document, this one a fax. He shows it to Nicky, pointing it with his finger to the date handle.** Jack: This fax, passed 3 days before I was ambushed, from Sheila to Dan, stated I was going to Congo. And this one... **Jack picks up another piece of paper, this one an email printed, and the handle saying 'Capt. Dan Ludovich, german army officer'. He says:** Jack: THIS is an email sent by Dan to some officer, the recipient email is deleted, and this was sent the day after the fax arrived. Dan was telling about it to the higher officer. Nicky: Ok, so he had some help. What were his intentions? Jack: Here... **Jack throws another document, this one a typed letter and it looks kinda old.** Jack: This is a 1979 document, stating that there was a huge plutonium mine in that area. The only natural plutonium mine in the world. Nicky: Wait... Ain't plutonium artificial? Jack: Yeah, because it's artificially made. This was the real stuff. The miners thought it was uranium, but it was even MORE terrifying. **Nicky opens her eyes. She's completely stunned. Jack continues, giving her another piece of document, this one with a presidential seal on it.** Jack: And THAT is a presidential order, advicing the army to prevent massacres in the area, and avoid attentions at all costs, and to secure the mine and not it get on the americans or the congonians. Nicky: So, you're saying they used YOUR SQUAD to secure it, lying to you? Jack: Exactly. We were sent there to secure the sight, but Dan and the high german officer tipped the congonians, who captured us... Nicky: But why? Why would they tip them??? **Jack hands a final document, which Nicky looks and suddenly turns completely shocked. She looks at Jack and says:** Nicky: No, no, this can't be... Jack: It can be... And it is. The german army man sent another crew, this one highly confidential. We went there and got caught. Their orders were to simmulate an explosion on the mine, burying it, but it would still have a passage, through a secret exit on the other side of the mountain that only THEY knew. They exploded the place and dragged me back to Germany after getting me from the congonians. Nicky: And why? Why did they rescue you? Jack: Because I was the only person alive who knew there was another exit to that cave. I had been in that mine before, with the rest of my squad. They also needed an escape goat. They dragged me to Germany, where I went to the Marshall Court, when I was arrested for reckless command. The high officer transfered me and kept me locked in the worst cell of that flee house. He thought all bases were covered. He had the plutonium to sell for the terrorists, through Dan and his international connections, and they sealed me to rot in that place. Thank god I left when they saw I was forgotten and dispensable. Otherwise, I would have never bben able to escape. **Nicky and Jack stop, looking down, as Jack painfully recovers. Finally the truth was told. Finally, all the search they've done, all the efforts they've done, have been compensated. Finally, Jack could rest. But the pain was still there. The anger, the rage, the burning fire that consumed his soul slowly throughout the years, planted a small evil seed that had grown into a monster, taking Jack's actions, a beast that would release itself when in danger, a fiery flurry of energy that made Jack double his power, but completely lose his mind. Jack finally says:** Jack: It doesn't matter now. It's all done. There's only one way to stop this, and it is to put the men behind this whole thing behind bars. Nicky: And how are you gonna find them? I mean, Dan and Sheila must be in some tropical paradise, enjoying the millions they made out of it. Jack: I'll have to find them... I'll have to. Nicky, search the web, check every single hotel room, travel agency, airlines, I don't care. Make a search, find them. I have plenty of time for this. I have till next week to get them. After Thursday, we leave and go after them. I ask Spider to give me a show's rest from COTR and we can find the bastards. Nicky: Ok, Jack, I'll start the search... **Nicky starts navigating, and Jack picks up the photo he threw to Nicky. An old, bent pic, showing Jack, with a good eye, and a smile he hasn't have in a while, between a shorter, skinnier man, short spiked blond hair, wearing regular german army clothes, and a gorgeous german girl, long blond hair, blue eyes, with a glass of beer, the three standing on a boat, the sky a little orange from the sun set. The scene fades the three people standing in the boat on the pic to a live shot of the boat. It's the same time of the day, the sun setting. The camera turns to its left and down, where we see the girl on the pic sitting in a beach chair, wearing a purple brazilian bikini and a big Gucci pair of square shades. Then, the other man on the pic, Dan, comes, with two glasses filled with champaigne. She gets up, he gives her the glass of champaigne, and they both cheer, before kissing each other, the sun on the back. The scene fades once again to the picture, which Jack finally throws away. He says:** Jack: Have you found them? Nicky: Yeah, but I have some good and bad news. Jack: The good ones first. Nicky: I found them, they're in an easy-access city on the caribbean. Jack: Cool, then there's no problem. Let's go... Nicky: Wait, this is important. We might not be able to have much time to get them. Jack: Why? Nicky: Because if we miss a flight on Friday evening, we'll only be able to come to the continent on Monday... Jack: No problem... Even if I'm booked for SMN, we'll be back on time. Nicky: No, we won't... Jack: What? Why? Nicky: I got an email from Spider. Apparently he'll give you a title shot. A shot at the CCW Independent Championship. Jack: Against Ricky Thunder??? GREAT! We go there, beat the crap out of them and come back Monday, bringing them to the authorities. Nicky: NO, Jack... It's for the iPPV... Jack: The... Interfed PPV? The Night of the Champions? Nicky: That's correct. Jack: But it's on Sunday!!! We won't have time to... Nicky: That's why we must leave the arena from CCW's LDT soon, grab a flight, and we'll have 12 hours to find them, capture them and catching the late Friday flight, otherwise, bye bye Indy Title... **Jack is shocked. After finally being able to rest some days in Atlantic City, finding out the whole truth about his past and finding the people responsible for it, he discovers he's got the biggest opportunity of his life, facing Ricky Thunder for the CCW Indy Title on the iPPV. Jack looks down, thinking, wondering, if he'd have to make a choice: Avenging his past or setting his future. Pierced by this doubt, he looks at Nicky, who gets up and gently places his head on her belly, as she stands next to him. As a child completely hopeless, the big german silently questions himself, as the scene fades to black** View Biography
