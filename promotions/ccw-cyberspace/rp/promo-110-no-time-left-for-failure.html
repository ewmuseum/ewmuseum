No time left for failure Jason Dread CCW Promo #110 Date: 3/24/03 Location: Nowhere...and everywhere Everything almost reaches a euphoric state of mind when I look backwards in time and think about the journey I've been on in such a short amount of time. I literally went from being the 'new guy' whom was told by so many people that I would never amount to anything as it came to the CCW...but in the course of only three seconds...three short seconds...my life changed. I finally wasn't a nobody... ...at that very moment I become a SOMEBODY...a very important somebody. You always seem to have 20/20 hindsight; it's simple to pick out your flaws in the past when you live comfortably in the present. As I look back, I see several things I obviously would have changed, and others which would have ultimately stayed the same. Last time I checked however...I didn't own a time machine. Now I'm stuck with my decisions and what I've done. The past is complete. Now is the time to ultimately make the future for the best which it can be...and it all starts very soon. All I have to do is put up what could be the hardest, longest, and most demanding fight I have ever come across in six year career. I try and rationalize with myself, which isn't the smartest thing to do...as it always seems to make my apathy worsen. I try and convince myself that this whole fight will simplistic...nearly systematic...clockwork. I've tried so hard to...but I ultimately can't. 'What if this ISN'T easy? What if everything gets put on the line and you get taken for the ride of your life?' is what I tell myself. I've always heard that war favors the informed, not the intelligent...this is one of the times when I wish I had neither information nor intelligence about this. Why can't I be just like any other asshole walking the streets who wouldn't love anything better than to revel in their own pride and self ignorance? Perhaps it's because I've been down that road before...and it doesn't work. Being too sure of yourself gives you nothing but peace of mind in the short-comings, and ultimate failure in the long-run. I'm either too damn smart to allow myself to do that again...or perhaps my mind has set up its own type of roadblock to prevent me from going that way again. I don't know which, if either applies...but for it I am glad. Unfortunately, however, all these questions to not bring forth answers...they just breed more infernal queries and scenarios. Victory and loss always seem to come to mind...what would happen if I won everything and proved I was better than the supposed best the CCW has right now? What if I were to come and destroy a man who had his title handed down to him with no justification for doing so? I could very well do all this...but as soon as I'm done, what is to happen with me? Will I ride my reign to its bare end, letting the better man of me and whomever I may fight ultimately end up with the titles around our perspective waist? Would I sink down into the hole I did before, letting everything fester inside of me...all the same emotions of jealousy, envy, pride, and hate? God I hope not... Then there is always the other end of the spectrum...what is to happen if I were to lose? As much as I'm trying to tell myself nothing bad would happen, I know there would be such a dear price to pay for ultimate failure. No specifics...I just know that I couldn't accept a loss at such a high price as this one would come. Not with how hard I've worked for everything. For nine months I did practically nothing aside from sitting in my darkened apartment and feel sorry for myself and my career which seemed to go all to hell...then I got my chance for redemption...my second shot to right the wrongs from which I had done before. Of course there are always skeptics who believe I shouldn't have now what I do...but in layman's terms...fuck all of them. I worked my ass off to get where I am now...and to get my opportunity. There were the men who stood in my way the whole time, however...which I managed to overcome...but ultimately...none of their stories are through. Brad Lamen...the first man who got on my case. He never seemed to take a liking to me because I was a 'rookie'. I imagine he's solemnly eating every single one of those words which he spoke before, as he 'credibility' as a former CCW Champion in both actions and tongue are both put into question with what he has said about me. Besides...it was the same 'Rookie' who was the last man to lay him flat on the mat...something which I know he seethes over to this very day. Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens...he was my best friend for so long; now look at him. He's let the same mistakes I've made overcome him...and there's very little use in talking sense into him. Lord knows I've tried so many times over the past month and a half I've been here in CCW. There have been so many nights that I was never able to get sleep as I thought of everything in my power to try and make him get a grip in reality. Nothing I said worked...and I tried not to strike him... ...but I guess everything is eventual. I know that couldn't have helped anything...but I was just protecting myself...yet I'm still punished mentally for such. If only he would just listen to me! Everything could be great again! Everything could be as it once was... ...and I realize that if I do come out on top at ReVelation, then I'm going to be the man whom he turns his attentions to, seeing as he still views himself as being better than I am in every facet. That may be true to some degrees...but his pride takes an ultimate factor in this. If I were religious I'd pray for any type of salvation to come his way... ...but I'm not...so I can only hope. Blayze...my opponent in just a few short days...a man whom never earned a damn thing which he'd ever received. It pains me to look at him, as I do so with envy. I never ONCE had everything put onto my plate as he has had. But envy isn't the only pain... ...ultimately comes anger. I seethe to think about the hardships and troubles he has put me through with every underhanded scheme he has pulled against me. I cringe every time I see his face plastered on some poster, billboard, or wall with the CCW Title draped around his shoulder or waist. But then I start to think rationally again... ...what use is a title without earning a reputation... ...what use is a title to a man without honor... ...without common sense or decency... ...what use is a title... ...to a man who has no credibility to back up his talk? What use is a title...to a man whom will lose it shortly...oh so shortly... ...to EXACTLY what...he...dreads! View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
