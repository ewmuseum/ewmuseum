Assurances Melody Widmark CCW Promo #115 Date: 03/25/03 Location: Washington DC The scene opens on the inside of an apartment complex... A hallway to be more precise. Melody Widmark shortly walks into view and makes her way down the hallway to door #210. She's dressed to kill, enjoying an uncharacteristic warm spell, dressed in a mini skirt, a halter top (and with the way her nipples mold the fabric, obviously no bra) and a pair of chunky platform sandals. If only she had another woman to share herself with, she despairingly thinks to herself. She pauses in front of 210, checks the address on the slip of paper in front of her, then raps on the door. Much to her surprise, it yields to her knocks and swings open a bit. Melody pauses... Melody: Hello? Anybody here? There's no reply. She pushes the door open fully. It swings aside to reveal a very distressed apartment. Furniture is upended, papers are scattered about, bookshelves tipped over, plant vases and chairs smashed on the floor. Melody: Uncle Dan? Melody steps carefully inside, closing the door behind her. She inches her way through the wreckage, making her way to the kitchen across the room. Upon reaching the entrance, she discovers much the same carnage in here as in the living room. Noting the kitchen is a dead end, Melody turns and makes her way to the hallway on her left. She just about reaches it when... Dan: YYYYYYYYYYAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!! Uncle Dan leaps out of the first doorway on the right hand side of the hallway, brandishing a baseball bat. His face is puffy and bruised, and his right eye is swollen shut. He swings the bat like a maniac, not noticing it's Melody until it's too late. Melody, for her part, arcs her body backwards to avoid the crushing blow. Lucky for her, she steps back onto a broken chair leg which causes her to lose her balance and spill onto her backside, allowing the bat to harmlessly whistle over her head. Dan stands his ground, bat in hand, breathing heavily. He looks down at Melody, a look of weariness in his eyes. Dan: Sorry about that. Uh... Would you like something to drink. Melody: No, but I wouldn't mind a helping hand. Dan nods apologetically and reaches down, hoisting Melody back up on her feet. His good eye runs and flickers over Melody's well-toned legs and curvaceous body. He smiles a bit. Dan: You here to, uh... You know... Help me 'forget' my pains and miseries? Melody: No. I actually came here to find out why you weren't at Lockdown to help me like you said you would. Dan: Well, uh, actually... I was kinda dealing with my own problems. Melody: I see that. **gestures** What happened here? Dan: Well, some guys stopped by. They said I owed them rent on the comic shop. I didn't have it on me, so they roughed me up and tore the place apart. Melody: **brow furrows** Some guys showed up and told you that you owed them rent? Dan: Just forget about it, okay? I'm sorry I wasn't there for your match. I'll do better next time. In fact, if you allow me the pleasure of your company tonight, I guarantee you my presence at your next wrestling match. Melody: **sighs** What's going on tonight? Dan: Just getting together with some of the guys. I want to show you off, make them think that I scored with a real hottie. You know how jealous those guys'll get? Melody: I'm sure. **sighs again** Alright. I'll do it, just as long as you promise not to touch me. And that goes for your scumbag friends too. Dan: Don't say that! Hey, Chad is going to be there. All the girls think he's a stud. Melody: I'm not 'all the girls'. In fact, I can almost guarantee you that I'll hate him. Dan: Okay, fine, whatever you say. Just promise to show up and behave yourself. This is really important to me. Melody: Fine. Dan: Can I at least tell them that we had sex? Melody: No. Dan sighs and drops his bat onto the couch. Dan: How about helping me clean? Is that too much to ask? Melody: Right now, yes. You want to know how frustrated I am? I lost to an eighteen year old on Thursday, Dan. Can you believe that? Dan: **heaves the couch into an upright position** It was probably one of them fluke wins... She grabbed your tights to pin you or something, right? Melody: No, she knocked me out, then posed over me as the ref counted the three. Dan: Ouch! That must've been pretty embarrassing. I thought you told me you were good. Melody: I am. That's what I don't understand. I mean, she really kicked my ass. It wasn't a fluke. Not a very good confidence builder, considering what I've gotten myself into this coming Thursday. Dan: **picking up books and papers** And what would that be? Melody: A mud wrestling match. Dan: **head perks up** What? I just thought I heard you say a 'mud wrestling match'. Melody: You heard me right. I'm going up against one of the most annoying (and unattractive) women in the sport. She's a complete jackass who doesn't like me parading around without any clothes on. Dan: What? You walk around naked!? Melody: **ignoring Dan** If she beats me in this match, I have to start dressing 'appropriately'... Or, whatever 'appropriate' means to her. I thought I had the match in the bag... Before I fought Chloe anyway. And now, I don't know if I can possibly pull off a win. Dan: You honestly think you could lose? Melody: Maybe. Koriann was a former Heavyweight champion, apparently. So... I need you as a back-up plan, just in case something goes wrong. I do NOT want to lose to this bimbo and be stuck hiding my perfect body from everyone. Dan: Nobody wants that. I'll be there to help you... Definitely. Melody: Thank you. **smiles** That means a lot to me. Dan: No problem! One final question though... Since I'm helping you, does that mean I get to follow you everywhere... Like, into your dressing room... So I can watch you change? Melody: No. Dan: **sighs** fade out...
