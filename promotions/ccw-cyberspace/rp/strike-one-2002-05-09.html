What this is A short scene in which Blayze scolds Fanatik for losing to The Valiant One.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Fanatik Entitled: Strike One Written in Thursday, May 9, 2002, 11:06:12 Location: ??? This is a: Interview [ The scene opens to Fanatik, head slumped forward, staring downward. Blayze stands beside him, shaking his head a bit... pacing a little, before comming to a jerking hault. ] Blayze: I'm dissapointed, Fanatik. You LOST to The Valiant One! LOST! [ Fanatik's eyes close... tilting is head faintly to move the dred locks from his face. ...There's no grin to be seen. Blayze puzzles a bit, before speaking once more. ] Blayze: You should be ashamed of yourself... you KNOW you could have DESTROYED him! What held you back, Fanatik? [ Fanatik stands still... silent. Blayze steps up to him, now speaking with a louder tone... ] Blayze: LOOK at me when I'm talking to you! [ Fanatik's eyes slide open, his head tilting upward to gaze at Blayze. Blayze stares back... a dissapointed look on his face. He steps up to Fanatik once more... and speaks in a low tone. ] Blayze: I'm dissapointed in you, Fanatik. ...Do you want me to be dissapointed in you? [ Fanatik slowly shakes his head. ] Blayze: ...Make up for it, boy... or you'll never be free. [ Blayze turns, walking away from Fanatik... while Fanatik's head lowers once more... his eyes closing. Fade to Black. ] View Biography
