Blood Toucheth Blood

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A verse-style promo in which Angel and Guardian outside a Chicago church warn of punishment for a misguided person.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Guardian Entitled: Blood Toucheth Blood Written in Wednesday, May 15, 2002, 16:36:59 Location: The Streets Of Chicago This is a: Interview Chicago The streets are a filmy, icy gray in the early hours of another new day. In the distant a dog barks at its unknown tormentor, a bird, a cat or maybe an early jogger Our camera pans across the street, nothing of interest is seen as we pan from left to right. Litter gently flutters around as if caught in some kind of invisible whirlpool. A street light flickers on and off. We see abandoned shops, a desolate looking church, stairways leading to homes of the not so rich and famous, beaten up mail boxes and trash cans. Suddenly the camera jerks to a stop. It pans back to the right quickly and stops on the musky looking church. Before the steps were empty. Now standing there standing out from the background in their brilliant white clothes stand Angel & Guardian. Angel looks into the lens as it draws in closer. Come closer Do not be afraid We are not here for you No, we come for another A misguided one The one who’s words and actions will soon bring him a pain he has not felt before And this time we do not come alone No This time HE comes with us This time the odds are in nobody’s favour but ours For you see this time we have another by our side And HE will see to it that what happens next will change things We have told you before what goes on will not be tolerated We gave you the chance to repent But you chose to ignore these warnings No Instead you carried on Whilst we watched you Watched you up close and in person Did you think you we would not see? Did you think we would not act? Did you think we would just ignore your actions? Come back to your senses as you ought; for it is you who is ignorant, and this I say to your shame. By lying, killing and stealing it breaks out; and blood toucheth blood. They crown him Hardcore They will come to see what this truly means Blood toucheth blood There is no turning back Blood toucheth blood And like a river it flows Soon There will be no turning back once the fury has been unleashed Soon it begins We are not your saviours We are not your salvation We are merely the punishment for your sins Soon Blood toucheth blood And it is to late to stop Off camera we hear a dog bark once again followed by the sound of a trash can being knocked over. The camera swings round to see a trash can rolling to a halt on the sidewalk, its lid, as in all the best movies, like a car or wagon wheel, is seen rolling down the street. When we are satisfied the action is over, the camera pans back to the stairs of the church………which are empty again. Fade To Black View Biography
