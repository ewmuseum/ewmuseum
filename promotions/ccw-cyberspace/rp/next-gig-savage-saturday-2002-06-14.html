Next Gig - Savage Saturday

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A comedic roleplay following Chang the Mongolian Giant as a roadie and band member before a Saturday show in Nevada.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Chang The Mongolian Giant Entitled: Next Gig.......Savage Saturday Written in Friday, Jun 14, 2002, 10:07:18 Location: This is a: Match RP Man! Did you see that fucked up looking bald guy singing in the second band? You know who he is don't you man? No, who? He's that wrestler, Chang the Mongolian Giant. Really? Yeah! He's fucking weird man. No shit! What was the band's name, do you remember? Fucked if I know. Damn. Whoa! Look out! One of the teenage boys reaches out and grabs his friend by the shoulder and pulls him back onto the sidewalk as a yellow taxi comes whizzing by. What the fuck are you doing man? That taxi would have fucking hit you! Didn't you see it coming? No. What's wrong with you man? You've been acting weird for the last hour or so. I don't know. Perhaps it was the coke I snorted in the toilets when I went in for a pee. What? You snorted Coke? Yeah, these guys offered me some when I went in so I had it. Are you nuts? What if your mom finds out? Well she won't will she? Don't worry I won't tell her. But now you're hooked! What are you talking about? I've had Coke heaps of times. I'm not hooked! But my mom said that if you have it once you're hooked. And your mom's a bitch! Now let's get out of here. The cameras follow the two pimply faced teenagers as they cross the road and head away from a well lit community hall. As the two darkly dressed boys disappear into the darkness, the CCW cameras turn to face the hall. The front doors are open and we can see two young men, both with long dark hair and dressed in black, cleaning the foyer area. One is carrying a large black garbage bag and both are picking up empty soda cans and tossing them in. The cameras pass them and enter the main part of the hall. Here we find a handful of young men, all dressed in black and with long dark hair, sweeping trash and folding up chairs while on the stage, the audio technicians are packing up the last of the equipment. The cameras pass by all of these people and heads onto the stage, dodging the piles of leads and road cases, and makes it's way to the backstage area and into a dimly lit corridor. We come to an open door and find five men sitting inside. Four of them are wearing black jeans t-shirts under black leather jackets while the other is wearing nothing more than a black leather g-string. He is also over seven feet tall and 500+lbs, so as you can imagine, he makes a very appealing sight. The other men are packing up their equipment. There is Mike, the guitarist, a twenty four year old college drop out with a heroin habit; Ian, the drummer; Mephistopheles, who is the band's bass player and advisor in the black arts and Pete who is the bands rhythm guitarist and elected driver. They finish packing their gear and sit down to have a beer. The fifth member of the band, former CCW Hardcore Champion, Chang the Mongolian Giant, has been sitting all the while rocking back and forth in a corner nursing his still painful jaw. As you will recall, he suffered the injury during a hardcore title match when the apple of his eye, Angel, gave him an exploding pacifier and detonated it while it was in his mouth. Look at the poor guy. Ian points at Chang. He's heart broken. The rest of the band all look at the forlorn Mongolian Giant rocking in the corner. Mike takes a mouthful of his beer and scratches at his arms. Well at least he isn't trashing things now that he's got that collar around his neck. The camera zooms in on the collar that was placed around Chang's neck by Scorpion on CCW's Lockdown Thursday. As we get closer, we can see the letters, "N.E.A." engraved into the leather collar. The camera zooms back out as Mike stands and pulls another bottle of beer out of the small bar fridge. Anyway, he'll get over it. Women are bitches. Chang continues his rocking as Mike returns to his seat and cracks open the beer. Mephistopheles looks at Mike as he takes a drink from his half full bottle of beer. So when's our next gig man? It's at the debut of CCW's new Saturday show in Nevada. The other three men look at Mike in surprise. Really? Yeah. I've been swamped with offers ever since Chang jumped on stage with us. So we're going to Nevada? By Saturday? Yeah don't worry I've hired a mini bus. We're leaving tonight. AAAAARRRRGGGGGHHHHHH!!! The four band members turn to look at Chang who has stopped his rocking and is standing facing them. AAARRRRGGGGGHHHHHH!!!!!! Chang starts picking up the band's packed equipment and carrying it out of the dressing room. What the hell's he doing? I guess he's keen to get going. He's got a match on Saturday too. Do you think he should be wrestling this soon after the exploding pacifier incident? I don't think there's anything we could do to stop him. After what Angel did to him in that title match, he's hell bent on getting revenge and I don't know about you guys, but there's no way that I'm going to try and stop him. Chang comes back into the dressing room and grabs another arm load of gear. AAAAARRRRRGGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!! He turns and trudges out the door again as the rest of the band sit there watching. Mike sits there scratching at his arms as Mephistopheles takes another mouthful of beer. So how long are we going to keep the Mongolian one in the band? What do you mean? Well, he is a little, strange man. Hey, ever since he joined the band we've been offered more gigs than in the whole four years before he showed up. He stays. Ian nods his head silently. He does make a good roadie. Chang comes marching back in and picks up the last of the equipment. Before he leaves the room, he motions for the others to hurry up. AAAAAEEEEEEIIIIIIIIII!!!!! Man, he is keen to get out of here. Of course he is. The match he's in this Saturday means a lot to him. He has to face off against three other big men in the first match of the night. After his defeat at the hands of Guardian and Angel the other week, he become a changed man. I haven't seen his bonnet or pacifier anywhere since he got out of the hospital, have you guys? The other three shake their heads in unison. Even look at what he's wearing! No more diapers. Ian shudders as he pictures Chang walking around in his leather g-string. I'm thinking that on Saturday night we're gonna see the return of the old Chang. The animal Chang. He's coming back this Saturday night and I reckon that there's going to be three big wrestlers who are going to be sore and sorry come Sunday morning. Chang comes waddling back into the dressing room. He motions again for the others to follow him. OK, OK. We're coming. Chang's four band mates all stand and begin moving towards the door. Mike lags behind as the other exit the room. He is scratching at his arms as he pushes open the bathroom door. I'll catch up with you guys in a few minutes. OK. Mike closes the bathroom door as the other three disappear down the dimly lit corridor, leaving Chang standing alone in the dressing room. He glances around quickly to make sure that there is no-one before pulling a screwed up photograph out of the front of his g-string. The camera zooms in and we can see that it is infact a photo of Angel. Chang begins purring as he gazes longingly at the photo. PPPPPPPPPRRRRRRRRRRR! He raises the photo to his lips to kiss it but recoils when he touches the photo to his injured mouth. His eyes become glazed with rage as he begins rocking back and forth as he glares at the photo. AAAARARRRRGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!!! Chang tears the photograph to pieces and hurls it onto the floor. As he sits on the floor rocking, he becomes aware of the NEA collar around his neck and tries to remove it but it won't budge. He stops trying and sits still on the floor for a few seconds before waddling out of the dressing room to join his band mates in the minivan. AAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRGGGGRRRRRAAAAAEEEEEIIIIIIIIII!!!!!!!
