Late Night Show Appearance, Escape From The Hospital

31 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A long match-class roleplay in which Venneman Pendragon appears on a late-night talk show, collapses, escapes from hospital and fights a group of vagrants.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main 'The Crusader' Venneman Pendragon Entitled: 'Late Night With David Letterman'...Escape From The Hospital and a Battle WIth The Bums...Skar, Estep...Read up! Written in Friday, May 31, 2002, 19:12:29 Location: New York City.... This is a: Match RP (Scene opens onto the set of 'Late Night w/ David Letterman. He has completed his monologue and is settled in behind his desk, drinking from his coffee cup. He wears a gray double-breasted suit today, and it looks to be a good show. The tape starts rolling and Dave begins talking...) Dave: Welcome back...We have a great show for you tonight... (Suddenly Dave is interrupted by Paul...) Paul: Dave...You a big fan of wrestling? Dave: Kinda...I watch when I am not involved in something worthwhile...Like clipping my toenails... (A universal 'Awwww' goes up from the crowd...) Paul: Cause I was flippin through the channels tonight, and I saw this giant of a man break this poor woman on his knee...I couldn't believe it! What kinda guy would do such a thing? I know what with women's rights and all, that it is okay, but my god! Where does it stop? (Dave grins and shakes his head...) Dave: Not quite sure who would do that Paul...Anyhow...I guess that it is time to bring out my first guest. This man is a wrestler for the CCW...He is a former champion in FWA and NCWA, and he just flew in after his latest victory in a tournament for the CCW world title. He's been on before....Let's hear it for Venneman Pendragon! (The crowd erupts in a mixture of claps and cheers and boos and heckles. Looks like you either love Pendragon or you hate him. Paul and the boys start up a very animated version of Metallica's 'The Small Hours' and Venneman comes out from behind the curtain. He is resplendent in a black silk suit with a black shirt and a red tie. The only offset to his ensemble is the white bandage that wraps around his head. He steps up to Dave's desk and shakes his hand, Dwarfing the tall Letterman. He smiles, takes a bow and waves to the audience before putting a hand to the back of his head and sitting down...) Dave: Good to have you back, Ven...Been a while... Venneman: Yeah...Three years I think... Dave: Sounds right to me. SO, how have you been? Venneman: Pretty good...Had to get back in the ring...Felt the call, thought the time was right. Dave: By the looks of you, the time was wrong...What happened to your head? Venneman: Ohh...That's right. You don't tune in. Well...There is a certain upstart in the CCW that does not like how I conduct myself in no DQ matches. Thought he would voice his discontent with a steel chair wrapped in barbed wire. I've been a little woozy since then, but them's the breaks I guess. Dave: Barbed wire huh? That must have hurt. How many stitches you need? Venneman: Not sure. Had to jump on a plane and come straight here after my match...haven't been to the hospital yet... (Paul lights up and interjects...) Paul: So you wrestled last night? I only ask cause I flipped on the show and saw this hulking brute break some little woman on his knee....I was repulsed. Had to turn it off. Who was that? (Venneman holds his head as he shakes it. He glowers before answering...) Venneman: I defeated a woman in the ring last night. (Paul's jaw drops open and there are a couple of boos heard from the audience. Venneman looks around as he continues... Venneman: Damnit. I warned her. I said that she should just not show, but she did not listen. She made her choice; I only cemented her decision. I gotta admit, though, that I had no intention of being so hard on her...I was just furious that Cyrus Skar attacked me and left me lying in a pool of my own blood, that I took it out on her. She was sick, my head was busted. Should have been even. Sorry everybody. Didn't mean to be so brutal. (Dave smiles as he starts to talk.) Dave: Ahh Venn...Don't be so hard on yourself. Those women wrestlers could kick my ass...They are tough! Venneman: Way to make a guy feel good Dave. Dave: (Smiling) How bout you read off the top ten, Venn? Make ya feel better.... (Dave holds out a card and Venneman takes it. The Top Ten video sequence plays until the top ten screen pops up, with Venneman's voice overlade...) Venneman: Top ten things you will never see at a CCW event.... Number ten: Teeth... Number nine: Hulk Hogan... Number Eight: A 'Clean' match... Number seven: Cyrus Skar holding a championship belt... Yeah...I agree with that one... Number six: A person whom Sylkk has not threatened to fire... Number five: A match you wanna see... Number four: A CCW'er being defeated by an LCW'er.. Damn right... Number three: 'Boobies'... Damnit! I guess it really is a family show. Number two: Mark Estep defeating Venneman Pendragon.. True, True... And finally, number one: Pendragon in a diaper! Yeah...That's more Chang's speed... (The crowd goes nuts, though I don't think it was the best top ten ever. Venneman looks puzzled...) Venneman: Why so many about me? Dave: Cause you are on the show tonight! Anyhow...Got a couple of questions for you... (Dave looks down at his cards as he plays with a pencil. Paul is still looking dumbfounded. Venneman settles further into his chair...) Dave: So what did it feel like to beat up a defenseless woman? (Venneman glares and Dave interjects...) Dave: Just kidding! What is your number one goal in CCW? I mean, everybody has to have goals. Venneman: I plan on winning the COTR tourney and becoming the CCW World Champion. Dave: And no easy task. Venneman: IN the prelims I am already guaranteed advancement. Dave: Says here you have one match left...Against a Mark Estep? Venneman: Yeah...Last 'Pool match' I have. I plan on winning the three points the Estep represents and gaining a high seed in the tourney. I will not be denied... Dave: All right...SO what do you think about this Estep feller? I don't think I have seen much of him... Venneman: As of late, nobody has. I guess he claims to have lost his mind or something. Guess he got tired of his own rap and needed a change. Can't say as I blame him, dullard that he comes off as being. Dave: Says here the guy is a veteran and has been in some high profile fights... Venneman: High profile? Has he faced me yet? No? Then undercard status is all he deserves. People don't deserve the big time in my book unless they prove they deserve to be there by putting up a good showing against me. That normally does not happen. Dave: So you have made it onto the uppercard? (Venneman kinda squirms at the question...) Venneman: In CCW? Not yet. I DO possess more talent than the high carders. Pretty soon it will be impossible for the fed to hold me down. The best always rises to the top, and here will be no different. (Dave chuckles as he goes on..) Dave: Nice to know that chair didn't damage your humility...Anything else? (Venneman stands up, a bit wobbly on his feet...) Venneman: I guess I should apologize for the Carmichael incident...I lost control, and a champion should never do that I apolog..... (Venneman slumps to the floor. Looks like he passed out. A nervous looking Dave asks for some help and soon paramedics arrive with a stretcher. They put Pendragon on it and wheel him away. Dave looks worried as the scene fades to black...) (Darkness. The only sound is a steady beep....beep....beep...) Voice: 'You should always see to yourself, Venneman'... Venneman (In a sleepy voice): Mom? Damnit...Just a dream...Mom has been gone for a while...Where the hell am I? (A crash and the a squeaky sound as it seems Pendragon is getting out of a bed. Suddenly the white of fluorescent lights come on and Pendragon is seen wearing a hospital gown, an IV stand following him...) Venneman: Man...Must be in a hospital. I haven't time for this. What the hell...Can't I get any peace? (The last question was directed at the camera crew who shares the room with Pendragon. No Venneman, you cannot. No peace for a talented CCW'er...) Venneman: Ok...You can stay. Just keep quiet. I am gettin outta here. (Venneman finds his clothes in the closet and heads into the bathroom. Momentarily he comes out minus the IV and his gown. He is wearing pants and tank top. Looks like he decided to forego the tie and jacket...) Venneman: I hate hospitals...People come here to die, and that is not what I plan on doing. (Venneman opens the door, looks around and takes off for the elevator. A nurse tries to stop him, but the doors already slide shut. He leans on the wall and presses 1. He holds his head as he starts to talk...) Venneman: Cowards. They are not definers of the world, but they sure can shake it up. Cyrus Skar...Pathetic whelp hits me with a chair. Man's way of fighting. Attack from behind. I am normally not one to be so crass, but paybacks on this will be fun. (The elevator stops and the doors open. Venneman speedily makes for the hospital exit and soon he is out onto the street in the New York night. He walks casually, a weight seemingly lifted from his shoulders...) Venneman: Skar...I know you are watching, so you should listen up. Pendragon is my real name...Not some gimmick. This is all me. I am born of noble blood and I am a man of noble action. I deliver the noblest of both brutality and pain. Nobody stands in the way of Pendragon for long. You are a pest. A lesser man would have been defeated after the cowardly act you delivered onto me, but I carried on. I carried on because I am a warrior. What are you? You do not believe you are in the land of warriors in CCW? You are wrong. Every match I have is a battle. Each victory I gain is a testament to my in ring prowess. What do you believe? DO you believe the CCW'ers to be mere men? Most are. I am not. I am a warrior. You will learn, boy. I think it is time I turn the tables... (Venneman walks on, steadily now. Looks like the head is not bothering him so much...) Venneman: I had hoped that everything in my life would not be a promo for the CCW, but I guess I will roll with it. I figure I have wasted enough breath on the coward, I guess I should address my next foe...Mark Estep. I've done my homework on him, but I have not seen anything of him for a while. He defeated the Carmichael wench, as did I. Of course, I destroyed her, while he merely got lucky. I thought she had him, but she is after all a woman. (Venneman passes an alley and a group of about ten vagrants watch him closely...) Venneman: Estep. People call you cocky. What have you to be cocky about? People call me arrogant, but they misunderstand. Anyone with my skills would have my attitude. Since I am in a class by myself of in ring skill, people do not understand. You will understand though. Come Monday, when you languish in pain upon my knee...You will know the torment of the Last Crusade. You say you've lost your mind? I plan on breaking your soul. (Suddenly three of the vagrants stop Pendragon...) Vagrant 1: Spare some change there big fella? Venneman: Get a job, scum. Vagrant 2: Not a very good attitude... Vagrant 3: Maybe we oughtta teach you some manners...Hey boys! (The rest of the gang saunters over and surrounds Pendragon. Pendragon takes up a defensive posture in the center of the 'bum ring'...) Venneman: Come on boys...I just got outta the hospital. Would you attack an injured man? Vagrant 3: Yep...And we'd rob him too... Venneman: You had your chance...Was never much for defense anyhow... (Suddenly, Venneman launches into an all out attack. He grabs two bums and smashes their heads together and they slump to the ground. A third is hefted and thrown against a wall. His head snaps back and he slides to the ground. Three down before the others can even react.) Venneman: Who's next? (Two vagrants rush him. He grabs the first by the throat and delivers a head butt but hangs onto the neck anyhow. The second punches Pendragon in the midsection and the audible sound of knuckles snapping is heard as the man breaks his hand on the steel midsection of the warrior...) Venneman: Scum like you should be eradicated... (Venneman lifts the vagrant whom he has been holding into the air and chokeslams him into the sidewalk. He then grabs the man who punched him by the arm. He wrenches it hard and the shoulder pops from it's socket. Two of the remaining five vagrants run away, and the other three stand their ground...) Venneman: Come on boys. My head is starting to hurt. I really gotta get to my hotel. Vagrant 1: What the hell are you? Some kinda superhero? Venneman: Just a modern day warrior boys... (Venneman spears the man, bounds to his feet and catches the fist of the second remaining bum. He squeezes until we hear a crunch and blood comes from Pendragon's hand. The bum screams and Pendragon smiles...) Venneman: Such glorious pain... (Forgotten, the third bum swings a pipe and hits Pendragon in the back with an audible thud. Pendragon goes to one knee and looks up with crazy eyes. The bloodlust is upon him. He needs to deliver pain. The man swings again and Pendragon catches the pipe, wrenches it free and swings as he rises to his feet. The blow catches number 3 on the chin and sends him flying. He hits the ground and lies still. Pendragon drops the pipe and turns to number 2...) Venneman: You were given fair warning. Your kind should not be allowed to share my air. (He picks up the trembling man and throws him face first into a wall. We hear his nose break on impact, and then he lies still. He looks at the camera and motions for it to come on. He walks quickly from the scene of carnage. He pulls a handkerchief from a pocket and wipes his hands. He then drops the handkerchief and begins talking...) Venneman: People just don't listen. Carmichael was told to stay away...She came, she paid. The bums were given warning, they failed to heed it, they paid....Estep. You are given warning. I am on a mission. My mood is soured because of the meddling Skar and I vow to take out upon you revenge for his treachery. Come to the ring Monday and it will be your last trip. The Last Crusade waits for you, my friend. Endless pain, endless agony waits for your soul... (With no warning, the scene goes black...) View Biography
