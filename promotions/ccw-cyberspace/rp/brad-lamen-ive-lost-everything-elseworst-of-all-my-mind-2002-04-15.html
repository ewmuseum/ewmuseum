Brad Lamen - I've lost everything else.....worst of all, my mind.

What this is A Match RP in which Brad Lamen brawls with concert-goers and then cuts a promo about loss and his upcoming Trans Atlantic Title match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Brad Lamen Entitled: I've lost everything else.....worst of all, my mind. Written in Monday, Apr 15, 2002, 19:47:48 Location: This is a: Match RP ***The scene opens up outside of a White Stripes concert at the Metro in Chicago. Various hipsters in tight black pants, white belts, and mod haircuts are standing in front, talking about the new Locust seven inch and smoking cloves. A few girls in tube tops and thong sandals enter the club and the hipsters all start laughing and pointing, making jokes about their stupid appearance. As the hipsters continue to laugh, a man in a leather jacket and ripped up jeans starts walking up the street. They start commenting on him and he hears them. He walks up and the camera sees it's Brad Lamen. The hipsters look frightened and shut up.*** Brad - You motherfuckers got a problem? Look at you fucking faggots living out a lifestyle that the fucking Who was doing in 1965...why don't you get your heads out of your collective asses and shut the fuck up. Hipster 1- Hey dude, why you gotta be talking shit like that to use? All we're doing is enjoying the night and waiting for the White Stripes conce..... Brad - The White Stripes? Hipster 1 - Yeah dude, you a fan? Brad - Ha! Hardly!!! Talk about a bunch of cocksucking SHIT...I haven't heard a band THAT band since the first time I heard Sonic Youth.....'Hotel Yorba'....awful. Hipster 2 - What the fuck? Look at you...telling us to start living in the FUTURE....you look like a fucking Ramone!!! Is that what you are? A fucking RAMONE!!! And it's not like Johnny Ramone ever had HALF the talent that Jack White does...that boy's got the blues in his ARMS...Johnny just POUNDED on his guitar.... Brad - Okay fucker, that did it....NO ONE disrespects the Ramones on MY watch.... *With that, Brad starts thrashing the hipsters. He grabs two of them by their mod hair and slams their faces together, leaving them crumpled on the sidewalk. Two of them walk over, trying to stop him and he kicks the first one in the gut, then follows with an uppercut. The other one backs down and runs inside. Brad gives all of them a few more kicks, then takes off.* Brad - If you couldn't tell, I'm a bit on end.....I've been losing EVERYTHING lately, and there was NO fucking way I was going to lose that argument and fight to those fucking hipster pussies. I don't know how closely all of CCW's been paying attention, but I've been missing in action lately. There's been a lot of shit going on. My good friend and escort/valet, Homeless Bob, has been slowly dying. He's suffering from a rare form of lymphoma that kills rather quickly. I've been spending the last few weeks trying to come to terms with his death...trying to come to terms with the loss of one of my closest friends. It's tougher than I thought it would be, losing someone this close to you. I always thought I was a badass...a tough guy...but this has thrown me for a loop...it's just too much...just....give me a second. *Brad stops walking and the camera picks up a tear in his eye and he takes a deep breath and starts walking again.* Brad - Hmmmm....and on top of that, I lost the Trans Atlantic title AGAIN.....I was too distracted by all this stuff with Bob to worry about that title, but it's bothering me now. I KNOW I should have beaten TVO in that match....I should have DESTROYED him and still be holding on to that title, defending it this Thursday against Scorpion. But that didn't happen. I let my depression get the best of me and take over. And that's my fault.....it's no one else's. It's MY fault I lost my Trans Atlantic title......but I'm not gonna lose again...I CAN'T lose again. I've just lost too much lately and it's starting to affect my mind. Now, people THOUGHT I had already lost it. They cited my silly costumes, my funny promos, my hairbrained comments...but that's just a CHARACTER I play in CCW. I'm not that stupid...if you will all recall, I graduated magna cum laude with a degree in psychology and a minor in sociology....could ANY retard do that? I doubt it. But all these problems lately are pushing me over the deep end and I'm afraid that I just may become my character if I don't start winning.... And that's where you come into the picture, Scorpion. You're going to be my savior...you're going to save Brad Lamen from himself. All you have to do, Scorpion, is let me beat you. It's that simple...that's ALL it takes to save me!!! Just let me win and everything will be okay. So, I'll see you Thursday, buddy. Just don't forget the plan: Let me win!!!! Thanks! ***Brad takes off towards his apartment and the scene fades to black.*** View Biography
