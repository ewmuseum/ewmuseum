Wait and Bleed

10 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A song-lyric promo in which Yama Tatsu rides into an alley, brutally punishes a gang of racist thugs attacking a boy, and hands them to a waiting crowd.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Yama Tatsu Entitled: Wait and Bleed Written in Friday, May 10, 2002, 00:43:42 Location: Where else..... Your Mummas house! This is a: Interview I felt the air rise up in me… We see Yama Tatsu letting out a wild scream of pain… Kneel down and clear the stones of leaves… Yama Tatsu annihilates Stacy Strong with a steel chair… I wander out where you can’t see… Yama Tatsu arises from the dust and charcoal of the burnt barn, now the perfect fighter… Inside my shell, I wait and bleed!!! Yama Tatsu charges forward and spears Kensuke through a glass table after losing his Independent title… **The song continues to play, but only as instrumental. We fade in to real time now, where life is a little to vivid. A group of racist, white power, shit heads beat down on a poor little Asian kid. In this dark ally the basic hardcore weapons lay scattered all around the environment. Trash Cans, Broom Sticks and Trash, Oh my! The roar of an engine breaks the sound of the Heavy Metal instrumental. A single head light shines on the four racist pigs. The camera pans around to see Yama Tatsu walking from his bike down the Ally way. He approaches the group giving them all a look of disgust and then down at the boy, who receives an even worse look. ** Yama Tatsu: Pathetic little kid… And you… ** He looks over his shoulder at a man preparing for a struggle. ** Yama Tatsu: You, if your going to break a kids jaw…… Do it like this! ** Yama pulls his steel cap boot back and slams it in under the poor, oriental kids jaw. He coughs up and spit blood and tooth fragments everywhere. The cameras immediately look away when they see this. We now see all those K.K.K fanatics standing watching the mayhem with evil grins on their fat, little faces. ** Thug 1: Looks likes we’s gunna hav ta teach this Asia man a fing or two as well booooys! Thugs 2/3/4 in unison: Uhhhhhhhaaaaaaa… Yama Tatsu: Oh no, you don’t want to do that Thug 1: An whhhhhy note? Yama Tatsu: Cause they got first dibs… ** The thugs turn to see 25 odd Asian and African American man standing blocking off the entrance to the Alley. They are all brandishing weapons of sorts. None too deadly, but all very effective punishing tools. ** Yama Tatsu: See ya… ** Yama wander on out of the alley, through a parted gap in the mob, and down off down the road. He now switch to a shot of Yama Tatsu walking away as screams of pain rip through the nights cool air. ** View Biography
