Preperation Jack 'The Ripper' Manson CCW Promo #39 Date: 19 Feb 2003 Location: Jacks House Welcome people. Welcome to the ever so slightly twisted world of one Jack Manson. Now I bet your wondering why you’re here aint ya? I mean you’ve got better things to do right? There's always something much more pressing that, you know deep down, you should be doing instead of being here. But no. Instead you decide to stop by. Take a glance. I mean after all, what harm can it do right? The answer...is nothing. So relax a little, don’t be so ashamed. Hell it’s no different than taking a look at a bad car wreck on the highway now is it. After all, we’ve all done it haven’t we? We’ve all slowed down once in a while to see the twisted burning wreck, hoping that maybe.....just maybe we will see the occupants of the vehicle. Bloodied, cut, who knows...maybe even dead. We look because we like it right? Well that’s precisely why you are here now with Jack and I. Who am I you ask? Well think of me as your conscious throughout this ride. Hehe and you thought you never had a conscious didn’t you? Well silly, we all have a conscious, its just that some....like our Jack here...keep it suppressed. Some people like Jack keep it VERY suppressed indeed...almost to the point where you wouldn’t believe he had one. Jack sits at a dark antique looking desk. In front of him, laid out is a fabric roll bag. The bag, much like that of a professional thief contains various neatly kept 'tools of the trade', however......these are no lock picks. Jack, his face still badly bruised following last weeks Lockdown, picks out a short dagger from the cloth. In is other hand he holds a soft velveteen cloth. Slowly, methodically he starts to polish the blade. “You know……there is a well known quote from H H Holmes who once said ‘I was born with the devil in me’ this, he reasoned was why he brutally murdered so many people all those years ago. It was part of him, of his genetic make up. He felt he had no more control over his actions than a runaway train does. Some people are just, different from the rest” Jack flips over the dagger and begins to polish the other side of the blade. “Yes…some people are just……..different. Others……..are just stupid. I mean THINK people THINK” Jack starts to grip the dagger tightly. His face quickly becomes enraged. “Do you not REALISE the consequences of your actions? Do you NOT UNDERSTAND that EVERY DAMN THING YOU DO HAS A CONSEQUENCE?” A trickle of blood drips down from Jack’s hand as his grip intensifies. He glances down at his hand, relaxes his grip and grins “ No….that’s just another one of the problems rife amongst today’s society. Nobody is willing to take responsibility for anything. If you are going to start something, at least the backbone to follow through on it” Jack slides the dagger neatly back into its place within the cloth roll, it gleams as it catches the light. He pulls out 2 short metal poles entwined with wire rope; his trusted garrotte. “Me? Well let’s just say that I never fail to deliver. Rest assured that If I start it……I will finish it. Take Lockdown 2 weeks ago. Scorpion………….Oh what a match up that was. But you see….I was never given the chance to finish what I had started. Bang! I hit Another Victim on the punk and for what? A disqualification? No. Not on my watch Scorpion. This time I will finish it. Tag team match. You……Enigma……..Blayze….and yours truly. Well one things for sure, it wont be a pretty night. So listen up closely” Jack, whilst talking has unravelled the garrotte. He snaps it taught as he looks up directly into camera. “Listen up real close whoever you are, you’ll be wise to stay out of my way. Scorpion…trust me……you’ll go down as yet another victim.” Jack lays the garrotte down on the table. He pauses, and a small grin appears on his face, again he looks directly into camera. “And speaking of trust. Blayze…..you are wise to talk about trust. I wouldn’t trust me either. You do your thing…..ill do mine. Don’t get in my way…..and I wont get in yours. This Thursday….Lockdown…..it’s a tag team match……..keep it that way and we both walk away. Anything else………….then only one does. And I don’t plan on leaving the ring any other way than walking” View Jack 'The Ripper' Manson 's Biography Back
