What this is A first-person Match RP in which Cianan recounts a breakdown in his trophy room and resolves to reclaim his title at Group Therapy; the text breaks off mid-word.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Cianan Entitled: I Think I need Therapy. (Group Therapy) Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 17:16:11 Location: Cianan's Home. This is a: Match RP I sit there, clutching my head. I'm in a corner, rocking, much like a baby, the corner holds me, as if it were my mother, a false feeling of comfort, a false feeling of safety, a false feeling of life. My fingers run through my hair, and I clutch at the side of my head, trying to force away the pain, trying to push it out.. drive it away, but it's always there, haunting me, chasing me.. NEVER LEAVING. I.. I am Cianan. I am a former CCW Champion, World Title, Independent Title, Light Weight Title, and Hardcore Title. I have fought in the bloodiest of battles, and the hardest wars, that I shouldn't have survived. Only.. Only for this. To be in that corner, rocking, with tears streaming down my cheek.. To be a fucking child. My fingernails dig into my scalp, I'm driving myself insane. I know it, everyone knows it. I need.. It.. to survive.. I wouldn't be able to walk without.. It. What that it is.. I won't say. But, everything runs through my head. Everything soars through my mind. Good, bad, even the things that don't normally come to mind, rise up and stew. They fill my brain, eat up my time. I think of nothing else, but my past.. My CCW. They abandoned me after I was injured.. fuck them.. Revenge will be sweet. The room I am in, is a trophy room, filled with pictures from my career, filled with my accomplishments, and my failures.. They all scream at me, mocking me. Constantly, yelling at me.. Each picture with their own voice. Especially that of: Kensuke Suzahara, Seraph, Sylkk, John Jupiter, and Jason McAllister. They think they're better then me, just because they went through PCW. Fuck them. We will find out who's better. I already know. My fingernails dig into my scalp, and I can see it, I'm on the inside, in my subconscious, looking at my form from the inside out. I look pathetic, I look sick. I am just on the verge of slipping into insanity, and … it … doesn't help. Blood.. I smell blood, blood from my fingernails.. They've finally broken the skin. Interesting.. I don't even feel it anymore. Pain.. Has cut off. I don't know if that's a good thing, or a bad thing. But, it's now something new to deal with. Wonderful. Everything is wonderful. At least, that's what I lie to myself. Wonderful, I'm about to slip deeper into my subconscious. This part was calm, I dread my inner emotions. They reveal so much more. ~The rationality slips, and I find myself.. facing CCW, facing PTC, facing Akiko.. Savanda.. Brad Lamen, Neurosis.. KENSUKE. I face all that I hate.. and the one that I love. The one with my child. I can't even look her in the eyes, I have to look away. She’s.. just.. beyond me... Too good for me. I turn my back to her, and look away. It was too hard to see her. I could stand against the world, and die, without blinking, but I just couldn't look at her. I find myself surrounded, trapped with in the masses, and masses of people. People I know, or have seen. They engulf me in their folds, clawing at me, ripping at me with their claws. I struggle, trying to pull away, trying in vein to escape what demons are in my mind. They are something I have to live with.. they are illusions, as much as I know I control them, they still.. bring up a sense of fear, and helplessness inside of me. I don't know how to explain it, and I won't even try. I pull away from their grasping hands, my voice is in my throat, and I can't speak. Their specter like claws rip through my clothes, tearing at the imaginary fabric. I know this is all in my mind. Yet, I fear. Why do I fear? A chemical imbalance in my mind tells me too. My subconscious tells me to fear these illusions. So, I do. Happiness, Fear, Love, Hate, all temporary chemical imbalances of the true state of mind. Something that is manufactured by the brain to have us cope with our surroundings, or how it thinks we should cope with our surroundings. I look into their hollow eyes, and I can't say anything. I know what they are, just copies of the real things, these are the pictures of people in my mind. They keep stalking towards me, and I push myself back. I can feel the temperature rising on the outside, my body is getting stressed. I hear them all chanting lightly, something I can't make out yet. I struggle and pull myself away, gasping and struggling from their grasping hands. “Why are you following me?” I scream, at them, as I continue to struggle with the spirits. The only response I was given, was their incessant mumbling and muttering, haunting whispers, which I somehow could perceive as something, that wasn't good. This was something horrible. My mind, and my body knew it. But this was my chance to return, this was my chance to come back. This was my future.. “it” was my future. “It” was now me. And my body hated it, and my mind rejected it. Fuck them. I started to use my control, forcing myself to listen to the raspy voices of the illusions of the people around me. I open my ears, as I close my eyes. I listen to them, hell, if I didn't like what they said, I could always ignore them, or stuff them down to the bottom of my being. But for now, I listen. Their voices shock me, as they rush to my now open ears, hundreds of them, thousands of voices, all in my mind, driving to ask me every question about everything. I was shocked, and horrified, I tried to shut my ears off, but the floodgates were released, and now I couldn't hold the voices back. They were here, and they were going to stay.. over confidence, on my part to think I could hold them back. “WHO ARE YOU?” I don't know. “WHY ARE YOU DOING THIS!?” It's the only thing I have left. “WHAT ARE YOU DOING!?” What I need to live. “WHY ARE YOU AFRAID!?” I am NOT afraid. “WHY ARE YOU AFRAID!?!” louder now. I am NOT afraid, shut up. “WHO DO YOU FEAR?!” No one. “WHO DO YOU FEAR?!” FEAR IS NOTHING!! I FEAR NO ONE!! “WHY DO YOU LIE!?” I'm not lying! I don't fear anyone, or anything! “WHY ARE YOU AFRAID!?” For the last time.. I AM NOT AFRAID!! “Then what do you fear?” The voices hushed, now calling to me in a soft whisper, the honey that gets the fly. I don't fear anything, or anyone. I am Cianan. “Are you really?” Yes, there is no one else that can claim that name, or my accomplishments. I am Cianan. “Who are you really?” I am Cianan. “That is your real name? That is where you come from? That is..” ENOUGH!! Cianan gasps, as his eyes snap open, drenched with sweat, his hair stringy, and unkempt. His face with a fine gristle on it, he has thrown up on himself. Though, that isn't the first of his concerns. His limbs ached, from three days of sitting in his home, motionless. Atrophy was a bitch. With a grunt, he stood, placing his hand on the drawer, and hefting himself up. He was in his achievement room. For once, the walls, the pictures, the figures.. were all silent. A pleasant surprise. He ran his fingers through hair, as he padded out from his past. His eyes, were red from the restless sleep, and he stunk. He did didn't care. He had missed LDT, and his chance to rip Spider a new asshole, but that didn't matter now. All that mattered, was to get to the pay per view. It was to get to Group Therapy. His second Group Therapy, and most certainly not his last, he wanted his title back. The one that was stolen from him by Blayze. He had never even touched the real title, only a mockery. He had gone through Kensuke, Blayze and Enigma for that belt. He had conned, manipulated and abused his power to get that belt. There was no way he was going to let it go. All of CCW stood in his way for Group Therapy, and he doesn't care. This was his destiny, at least in his eyes. Cianan stepped into the bathroom, and started to prepare, anyone who stood before him, would have more then hell to face. They would have to have more then heaven and hell on their sides. Cianan doesn't care anymore, he's out to reclaim what is his. He will do anything for it. Cianan glanced down, as he slid the door shut in the bathroom. He placed his hands on the counter, and he stared himself in the face, and looked into his own eyes. “I don't fear anything.
