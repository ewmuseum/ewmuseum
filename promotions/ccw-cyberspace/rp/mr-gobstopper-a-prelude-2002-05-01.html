This is a: Interview *Screen fades in from blackness. Slow-motion clips of a wrestling match are being shown as a hazy ghosting effect runs around the edges of the screen.* VOICE: It began with a dream... *Clip is shown of Mr. Gobstopper holding the Independent title in the air at Irish Spring 1999.* VOICE: It included a near miss... *Clip is shown of Mr. Gobstopper being pinned by The Supreme Machine at Shriekfest 1999 in the finals of the COTR.* VOICE: It was a time of humor... *Clip is shown of Mr. Gobstopper as The Animator after defeating Andrew 'Ace' Hawk in an Independent Title match.* VOICE: It was a period of good times... *Clip is shown of Mr. Gobstopper delivering the Gob Drop to Brad Lamen and pinning him to win the CCW Heavyweight Title.* VOICE: ... and bad times... *Clip is shown of Tim Inman with his foot over the fallen Mr. Gobstopper to win the CCW Heavyweight Title with members of Avengamaniacs at ringside.* VOICE: But this Thursday, HE'S BACK! *Mr. Gobstopper is shown against a black and blue backdrop.* Gobstopper: PREPARE TO BE DROPPED! *The Lock Down logo flies across the screen.* VOICE: Mr. Gobstopper returns to CCW this Thursday night only on CCW's LOCK DOWN! *Screen fades out.* View Biography
