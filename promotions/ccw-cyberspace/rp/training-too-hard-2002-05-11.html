Training TOO Hard

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A scripted scene in which Deborah Carmichael, watched by her husband and a friend, defeats four boxers in a gym in preparation for her match against Devyl and Angelica Lynn.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Deborah Carmichael Entitled: Training TOO hard Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 12:08:26 Location: New York This is a: Match RP **The scene today opens outside a boxing gym, in Brooklin, NY. It's a rainy day, and just a few people can be seen walking around, mostly running away from the rain, trying to find a shelter. There aren't many cars around and, in the horizon, we can see the Bridge and the entrance to Manhattan, almost as a new, safe world, separate from the mortal continent, segregated, where only the chosen ones can live and work. The scene turns around to the street, and you can see a taxi stopping in front of the gym. We can see through the wet window the passenger pay the driver and immediately walk out of the cab. After a few moments, the door opens, and the camera shows the passenger as being Mark, Debbie's husband, who was supposed to be in Phoenix. He's holding a small piece of paper on his hand and is wearing a black rain coat over an old piar of jeans, and is carrying a black umbrella. He checks the old gym to be certain that it is the place he was looking for, and finally he walks to the door. The doors are old, the decaying paint in the walls past it showing a decadent place. Walking through a long, dark hall, Mark can't feel secure. His legs are shaking, and his eyes moving from side to side, trying not to be surprised. After a few moments, he reaches the end of the corridor, which has a door to his right. A dim light breaks through the bottom of the door, showing there's someone behind it. Afraid, Mark slowly puts his hand on the knob and, as he turns it, he almost faints, as Danny also opens it, out of the blue.** Mark: AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH, damn it, Dan. Don't you ever do that again. Dan: Sorry, Mark. I can't help. Please, come with me. Thanks for coming. **Dan is not happy, and his face looks incredibly worried. Dan is wearing a white CCW T-shirt and a par of cut jeans. Mark says:** Mark: So, how bad is it? Dan: It's serious. Very serious. Mark: How long? Dan: She's been training hard for a week, now. Mark: Weird... She hasn't been a person of overworking herself. Dan: I know. But the fact is, she went nuts. Mark: I see... And how important is this match, again? Dan: If she wins those two, she gets to face the Women's Champion for the title. Mark: Hmmm, this is what she wanted, right? Dan: Right. Mark: Then she SHOULD be working out... Dan: Not like THIS!!! **Dan pushes a door, and both men enter a big boxing gym. We can see all the boxing apparata around the ring, which takes central stage. The scene shows four boxers in the ring at the same time. They're not wearing gloves, and have different sizes: Boxer #1 is a fast mexican lightweight, boxer #2 is a canadian, white middleweight, boxer #3 is an incredibly big african-american cruiserweight, and boxer #4 is a very tall, dangerous-looking thug, with a scar running through his cheek. In the other corner, we can see Debbie Carmichael, on her wrestling gear, stretching herself for what appears to be a match. Mark looks at the scene and then turns to Dan, completely shocked:** Mark: No, she wouldn't... Dan: You better talk to her... **Mark runs at the ring and calls for Deb.** Debbie: Hey, Mark. What the hell are you doing here?? Mark: Me? I'm here to stop you. What the hell do you think YOU are doing? Debbie: I'm working out, trying to get ready for Monday... Mark: Against THOSE guys??? **The four guys smile and laugh at the sight of Debbie getting ready to fight them** Debbie: Sure, why not? Mark: Huh, first of all, that guy over there, the big one, he doesn't seem to just want to fight you, he seems to want to do bad things with you. Debbie: Sure he does, and that's why I'll love kicking his ass... **Mark rolls his eyes, looking at Dan, before turning back to Deb.** Mark: Look, Debbie, I'm tired. If you try to fight them, I'm gonna have to stop you. Debbie: And if you stop me, our marriage is over! It's simple as that. **Mark is shocked. He didn't know Debbie would go that far. Completely sad, he looks at Dan, asking for help, but he moves his shoulders, showing he can't do a thing. Mark then, with a tough choice on his hands, have no alternative then step back.** Mark: Fine, you go ahead. But the moment I sense you're in trouble, I'll jump right in... **Debbie doesn't listen to him and finally finishes stretching. She points to the big man and yells 'fight'. What happens next is not seen, but the camera shows Mark and Dan's faces as they contort in pain. Mark makes a mention to invade the ring, but Dan stops him. On the background, sounds of heavy battles can be heard. After a couple of minutes, Dan and Mark are seen completely shocked. The camera turns into a 180 degree and shows Debbie kicking the bloody face of the big thug, which lies, completely out cold, above the other three boxers, also out cold. Debbie finally lets them go and walks out of the ring, going straight for a punch bag. Dan and Mark follow her:** Dan: Damn, Deb, that was incredible, you really beat those guys. **Debbie doesn't answer. She starts pounding on the punch bag, her short hair tied back in a pony tail, jumping from side to side on her back. Mark comes around and asks:** Mark: Deb, are you OK? **Debbie doesn't answer.** Mark: Debbie... **Debbie continues punching on the bag.** Mark: DEBBIE... Debbie: WHAT??? GET OFF ME!!! **Debbie shoves Mark away and keeps punching on the bag. Mark, completely shocked, lowers his head and starts walking away, tears beginning to come down his eyes. Debbie, after a few more punches, finally slowly stops, saying:** Debbie: No, please... Stay, Mark... **Mark stops and turns back, running towards Deb. They kiss and embrace. Dan smiles and the scene fades to black. When it reopens, we're in New York at night. The scene shows the top of the Empire State building, as the sun starts to set. Mark and Debbie stand there, looking at the horizon. Mark approaches her and asks:** Mark: Damn, Deb... I was scared back there... You didn't have to work that hard... Debbie: I did, Mark... Mark: Why? Debbie: It's a matter of survival. Mark: How come? Isn't it just a match? Debbie: When you're facing Devyl and Angelica Lynn and having Lacey Skye on the outside, no, it's not just a match. Mark: Then what? Debbie: Mark, you wouldn't understand... Mark: Try me... Debbie: What for??? You're a calm, gentle, suburban guy that never really got in a fight. **Mark smiles and then starts laughing. Debbie raises her eyebrow, clueless. Mark explains.** Mark: Honey, this may shock you, are you ready? Debbie: Sure... Mark: I'm a former NCAA Middleweight Wrestling Champion... Debbie: Oh, great... Now it's two boyfriends with secret lives. It's Ace all over again... Mark: Hehehehe. **Mark kisses Deb and continues:** Mark: So, being a wrestler, I know how a match can be important, I just don't understand why you risked your life out there. Debbie: Mark, you're a wrestler, then you KNOW. This match is not about getting to face Lacey Skye for the Women's Title. It's about pride, respect, it's about going out there and show the other girls that you're not the one talking the trash, they are. This is about going there and getting the respect they SAY they have but they SHOW they haven't. Mark: I see... Debbie: That's why I must win. The girls know I'm tough, but they actually don't know me. They know my legacy. Now, it's time to show them what the Prime Lady of Wrestling is all about. I must show those two that I'm the best, and that only I can be the one to hold the CCW Women's Title... Mark: It's cool. I just think you should go easy... Debbie: I know... **They both kiss, and the scene fades to black** OOC: Sorry this is late, a package came today and I got carried away. View Biography
