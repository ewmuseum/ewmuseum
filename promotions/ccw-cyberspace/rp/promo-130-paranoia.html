Paranoia

14 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paranoia Adam Christ CCW Promo #130 Date: 4/14/03 Location: ... April 10. CCW Lockdown. BJS: ...Blayze and Enigma are both standing in the ring!! JD: Wait! ...Uh-oh... Blayze and Enigma just locked eyes, and now they're staring at one another!! BJS: Do you feel the tension, Joey!?! JD: Enigma made Blayze LOSE his CCW Heavyweight Championship!! There is NOTHING but bat blood between these two!! BJS: They're cussing one another out!! They're going to go at it...!! [ Suddenly, Commmissioner Raymond steps out from behind the curtains! The fans EXPLODE in heat as he stops on the top of the ramp, microphone in hand! ] Commissioner Raymond: Well, what do we have here...? Blayze? Enigma? ...Working TOGETHER!? What are the odds!?! [ Blayze and Enigma seem to ignore Raymond, staring down one another... stepping closer to each other, the tension rising incredibly. ] Commissioner Raymond: Hey... I'm talking to you! ...Well, maybe THIS will get your attention!! [ Raymond snickers. ] Commissioner Raymond: Next week on Lockdown, Jack Manson and Adam Christ WILL defend their newly named Extreme Titles... against the team of... BLAYZE AND ENIGMA!!! [ The crowd ERUPTS in cheers as Blayze and Enigma break their stare to gaze at Raymond, both shocked and angered with this decision. ] Commissioner Raymond: Since you two worked SO well together just now, you'll work BETTER next Thursday!! [ Blayze screams out cuss words at Raymond's direction. Raymond shrugs them off. ] Commissioner Raymond: It's settled...! Good luck, you two!! I'm sure you'll work... INTERESTING together!! [ Raymond laughs a bit as he turns to walk through the curtains. Adam and Jack are seen on the outside of the ring, grinning to themselves as they move back up the ramp. ...Blayze and Enigma turn back to each other, and immediately step up to one another, nose-to-nose. ] Friday, April 11. [ ...The scene suddenly cuts to darkness, where Adam Christ's calm voice echoes in the background. ] ...It's funny, isn't it? ...How two men who completely loathe one another are forced to work together. Two men, Enigma and Blayze, having their futures decided for them by one Commissioner Raymond, a man who's quite familiar with making those kind of decisions. I find it hilarious. ...Oh, how similar they are to Scorpion and Michael Stevens. ...And how similar their fates will be. [ The scene quickly cuts to Adam Christ, sitting up in his familiar hospital bed within his familiar hospital room. From this angle, it's clear to see that Adam's back is blistered and cut up from the events that occured during Lockdown's Main Event. ...And as the camera begins to move around, we also notice that Adam's mask is off and set on a desktop next to the bed, where a dirty glass of water and some pills lay. Adam's head is tilted forward, and his arms are wrapped around his waist, as if he were clinging onto himself like a child to his mother. Adam's hair, wet with sweat, sticks to his forehead and the right half of his face, covering the flesh where that eye would be. His other eye--his ONLY eye--is seen to be only partly open, as if he were in a state of calmness and rest. His lips are shut and his nostril breathing can be faintly heard. A few minutes of silence pass before Adam finally begins to speak, talking in a much softer tone than usual. ] Adam Christ: ...It hurts... [ The scene slowly begins to move in closer, showing that Adam isn't really showing any facial signs of pain whatsoever. Instead, he just seems to be sitting there, staring forward... emotionless, in a way. ] Adam Christ: ...I've felt it before... [ Adam sits there, not moving much. The obvious pain on his back must be tremendous, but he seems to be incredibly ignorant to it. Suddenly, Adam's blinks and turns his hands over, palm facing upward. He looks at them, looking oddly confused, and then looks upward. ] Adam Christ: My belt... where... where's my belt?! [ Adam looks around franticly to find his belt, but to no avail. ] Adam Christ: Scorpion and Stevens took it, didn't they! They STOLE it from me--ah! [ Adam tried to move too quickly, hunching his back forward and stretching his wounds a bit. The sudden streak of pain immediatly makes him buckle back up and make a wincing 'sss' noise. ] Adam Christ: ...Ah... mm... [ The door of the room opens, and Adam quickly looks to see who it is. It turns out to be... Nick Cashew. ] Nick Cashew: Adam, there you are...! [ Adam Christ's eyes widen a bit at Nick's presence. ] Adam Christ: Nick...! [ Nick shuts the door behind him and walks up to Adam... ] Nick Cashew: Man where ha--ooh... Jesus, man. What happened? [ Nick looks over Adam's back. Adam just sits there. ] Adam Christ: ...I slipped. Nick Cashew: Slipped? ...You must've took one hell of a fall, then. Adam Christ: Yeah. It doesn't hurt, though. [ Nick snickers. ] Nick Cashew: Doesn't hurt? You're a stupid fool, you know that? That HAS to hurt! Adam Christ: ...I like it. Nick Cashew: You LIKE it? [ Adam nods. ...Nick just shakes his head. ] Nick Cashew: ...Whatever. Look, Adam, I scheduled a press conference for you to go to. Adam Christ: A press conference? Nick Cashew: Yeah. But I'll cancel it if you don't think you're healthy enough to go. You want me to? Adam Christ: No... no, I'm good. I'll go. Nick Cashew: You sure? Adam Christ: Yeah, I'm sure. When is it...? Nick Cashew: In about fifteen minutes. I'll be in the lobby waiting for you. Take your time in getting ready--and if you want to cancel, don't hesitate, alright? [ Adam Christ nods. Nick nods as well and turns, taking his leave. ...Adam remains on the bed, not moving or speaking for a few minutes before turning his torso slowly and setting his feet on the floor. Adam takes in a deep breath before pushing himself from the bed and standing upright. He shows a faint wince with his face, but seems to suck it up and ignore it. Adam grabs a white t-shirt from the corner of his bed and slips it on to cover his wounds and hide them from the press. He turns to the desk, grabbing his mask and slipping it over his face. ...He then grabs one of the pills and puts it in his mouth, using the water to swallow it down. ...The pills are, obviously, painkillers... but they only gave him a few instead of the whole bottle, remembering what happened last time. Adam turns back around, glancing over the room once more... ] Adam Christ: ...When I get back, I'll look for my belt. ...And if they took it... I'll fucking tear them in half. [ Adam's eye jerks across the room before he steps up to the door and opens it, stepping out and heading to the lobby. The scene then fades to black, before coming up to the press conference room. Reporters franticly talk amoungst themselves, the flashes of their cameras lighting up the podium that rests on the stage. After a few seconds, a man hurries up to the podium and speaks into a microphone that's attached to it. ] Man: Ladies and Gentlemen, one-half of CCW's Extreme Champions, Adam Christ! [ The man steps off of the podium as 'Everything I Touch' by Stabbing Westward plays cheapily from a stereo system somewhere in the back of the room. Adam Christ walks up the stage and turns to face the podium, looking out at the reporters quietly. Their cameras flash wildly as he gazes at them, his eye jerking from right to left, this being an entirely new experience for him. Adam leans forward and softly speaks into the microphone... ] Adam Christ: I'm willing to answer any questions you may have for me at this time. [ Adam looks around as a flurry of hands shoot into the air and a flood of chatter fills the room. Adam points out one of the hands out of the pile... ] Adam Christ: You. [ The reporters take their seat. One remains standing, notebook and pencil in hand. ] Reporter #1: Adam, the team of you and Jack Manson call yourselves 'MisUnderstood'. Why is that? [ Adam smirks a bit. ] Adam Christ: That title was probably made up at the top of Jack's head, but I think it suites us well. Don't you agree? [ The reporter sits down, and the flurry of hands shoot up. Adam picks another one... ] Adam Christ: You. [ Another reporter stands. ] Reporter #2: On last night's Lockdown, you changed the CCW Tag-Team Titles into the CCW Extreme Titles. Could you please elaborate for us on what exactly that means? Adam Christ: Sure. Well, before President Douglass... or whatever his name is, returned as CCW's President, I had a conversation with former CCW President Sylkk. Him and I made a behind the scenes contract signing agreeing to allow Jack and I to warp the CCW Tag-Team Titles into the CCW Extreme Titles, also giving them Hardcore-style capabilities. Reporter #2: So basically, they're like the CCW Hardcore Title... only in tag-team form? Adam Christ: Pretty much. Everything is legalized when defending them. [ The reporter takes his seat. The hands shoot up, and Adam picks another. ] Adam Christ: You. [ Yet another reporter arises. ] Reporter #3: Exactly how long does this contract last? Adam Christ: The contract states that the contract is perminant until Jack and I lose our titles, or if former President Sylkk decides to cancel it. And since Sylkk is caught up in legal troubles, and Jack and I aren't planning on losing our belts anytime soon, it'll be a long time before these belts return to their normal state again. [ The reporter takes his seat. Hands shoot up, Adam picks one. ] Adam Christ: You. [ The reporter stands. ] Reporter #4: Couldn't CCW's current President simply take the contract and eliminate it, himself? Adam Christ: I don't think he'd even attempt it. You see, this new contract was made while President Sylkk was in power. Therefore, it's still very much active as if he was STILL in power, and it's still very much unbreakable. ...It's like this; even thought we have a new President, we all still have our contracts from before--they don't change. As does this contract. It's embedded within CCW, and it can't be eliminated by the new President. ...So, to answer your question, no. [ The reporter sits. Adam picks out another one, and he stands. ] Reporter #5: While we're on the topic of the CCW Extreme Titles, are you aware that your match this Thursday against the team of Enigma and Blayze is NOT for the CCW Extreme Titles as originally planned, but instead it's a simple tag-team match? [ Adam blinks, tilting his head. ] Adam Christ: ...It's been changed? Reporter #5: It looks like it. Adam Christ: ...No, I wasn't aware of this. Reporter #5: Do you think that since this match is NOT hardcore-based, you and Jack won't be as powerful as usual? Adam Christ: I don't think that'll be the case at all. Jack and I enjoy pain, and enjoy giving pain. We'll use anything and everything; from our bodies to weapons. And since weapons might not be an option in this match, we'll just rely on our bodies. I've beaten people in CCW without the use of weapons before, I can do it again. Jack's done it plenty of times as well. I don't think it'll have any effect... Reporter #5: Are you sure? Last week on Lockdown, it seemed that you had to RELY on weapons to defeat Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens! [ Adam chucks a bit to the reporter, an ear-to-ear grin crossing his face. ] Adam Christ: Last week, Michael Stevens got what was coming to him. He agreed to step into the ring with me again--after I beat him TWICE before. He didn't know when to quit, and he paid the price. Now, whether I relied on that tyre iron or not isn't the point. ...The point is, I beat Michael THREE times. THREE. I've made him bleed THREE times. How many more times will it take for him to learn that HE can't beat ME!? ...He's blind, and ignorant. [ The reporter takes his seat. Adam picks out another. ] Reporter #6: And what of Michael's partner, Scorpion? Did you see and hear his comments he made on Lockdown? Adam Christ: ...Jack and I executed Scorpion at Revolution. We hung him over the ring with a noose--WE LYNCHED HIM! And because of that, Scorpion has to take a few weeks of hiatus due to neck injuries. ...However, is it not strange that Scorpion managed to defend the CCW Independant Championship later on that very same night? I think there's more to this little story that meets the eye. Scorpion has something planned... and, whatever it is, he won't get far. ...And yes, I did see his pathetic antics on Lockdown. I'll admit, he has some artistic skills. Copy and pasting my head on little children's bodies must be pretty tough work--pretty damn impressive. [ Adam cuts the sarcasm. ] Adam Christ: Whatever that pedophyle says means absolutely nothing to me. He's gone. He just wanted to leave a reason for me to humiliate him when he gets back from his 'neck injury'. And, believe you me... and he gets back, I'll do MUCH worse than I did at Revolution. ...Take my word on that. [ The reporter takes his seat. Adam picks out yet another. ] Reporter #7: Going back to Enigma and Blayze, how do you feel about them teaming up together? Adam Christ: I've noticed that Enigma and Blayze have some huge grudges against one another. ...It's really funny, how we're being put against two men who are SO similar to the team of Michael Stevens and Scorpion. Two men, hating one another, forced to join forces and fight. Bah, what a load of shit. I tire of these fairy-tale tag-teams. Enigma, Blayze, even though you were put into this match out of your will, you both MUST work together. You two MUST push aside all of you differences, and FIGHT. Jack and I aren't going to be patient with you. We're going to destroy you, period. With the little time you have before Lockdown, I suggest you get your act together. You can destroy one another later--but right now, WE'RE your enemies. WE'RE your threat. ...And we're looking to add the both of you to our 'victim' list. ...And even if it won't include noose's, tyre irons and garrottes... it WILL include blood. I can assure you that. [ The reporter takes his seat. ] Adam Christ: That's all the questions I'm willing to answer at this time. Thank you. [ Adam turns from the podium, walking aside. The flurry of questions fill the room once more as Adam heads to the doors and exits the press conference room. Nick Cashew quickly dashes up to his side... ] Nick Cashew: Hey! Man, I'm impressed! You acted really professional out there! Adam Christ: Yeah. Nick Cashew: I thought for sure you'd pull off your stupid 'freak out' stunt or something, and get all dramatic on me. Adam Christ: You told me I was supposed to answer the questions, and I did. ...Is that not what you wanted me to do? [ Adam looks at him intently. Nick looks back. ] Nick Cashew: No... you did fine! Adam Christ: Good. ...I'm going back to the hospital. [ Nick nods. ] Nick Cashew: Yeah, sure. I'll head up there in a bit... I need to try and get some of these reporters out of here. [ Nick turns and heads back to the conference room. Adam walks past an open door and into a parking lot. The scene then fades to black... ] Saturday, April 12. [ The scene opens within the familiar hospital bed, where Adam is seen laying on his chest. His back is un-covered and his wounds from Lockdown are clearly seen, but they don't look as bad as they did yesterday. In fact, they seem to be healing nicely. The camera moves forward and tilts in the direction Adam's head faces, which happens to be resting on a pillow. Adam's mask is off and his long hair covers half of his face. However, his left eye is seen, wide-open and staring directly at the camera. Adam lyes, motionless. ...Suddenly, Adam's voice is heard in the background. ] Today... is a time for relaxing. [ The camera cuts back to a birds eye view of Adam's back, showing us it's blisters and scratches that're healing, with the help of some medical lubrication. ...Adam's voice is heard once more. ] Tomorrow, Dr. Forbes has something planned for me. ...He told me it has to do with my match on Lockdown--some sort of 'training'. ...Whatever it is, I'm looking forward to it. I've never really been trained, before... [ The scene cuts back to Adam's face. ] ...These wounds were given to me by Michael Stevens. ...They're a gift. ...Well, I'm flattered. ...I'll just have to repay Michael for them, the best way I know how. [ A smirk suddenly crosses Adam's face, and the camera immediatly cuts to black. ] Sunday, April 13. ...Strange. ...I feel... more calm than usual. [ The scene opens within a gym, where Adam is seen in the far corner, slumping against a wall. He holds a bottle of water in his right hand to his side, his head tilted down and his gaze to the floor. ...As the camera moves in closer, we see Dr. Forbes enter the scene, stepping up to Adam. ] Dr. Forbes: Good to see you made it, Adam. [ Adam's head tilts to look at him. ] Adam Christ: Hi, Doc. Dr. Forbes: How's your back? [ Adam straightens up and turns to him. ] Adam Christ: It feels fine. It's healing well. [ Dr. Forbes blinks, looking at Adam's face. ] Dr. Forbes: What is it? ...What's wrong? [ Adam stares at him, his eye sharp and his gaze intense. Dr. Forbes steps back a foot, just in case. ] Adam Christ: ...My belt. ...I lost it. [ Dr. Forbes blinks. ] Dr. Forbes: Lost it? ...What do you mean? Adam Christ: I misplaced it. I can't find it. ...I think they took it from me when they worked on my back. Either that, or it was stolen... did YOU take it!? [ Adam quickly grabs Dr. Forbes by his shirt. Dr. Forbes prys himself free. ] Dr. Forbes: No, I didn't take it! Why would I take it!? [ Adam Christ stares at him, his eye wide. ] Adam Christ: YOU'RE with THEM, AREN'T YOU!? [ People in the weight room look over to see what all the noise is about. Dr. Forbes panics a bit. ] Dr. Forbes: N-no! Calm down! We need to train, Adam! [ Adam blinks, snapping back to normal. ] Adam Christ: ...Sorry. Dr. Forbes: ...It's alright. ...You're just... paranoid, or something. [ Adam nods slowly, pulling the bottled water to his lips and taking a long gulp. Dr. Forbes wipes some sweat from his brow. ] Dr. Forbes: Anyway, let's get on with the training. [ Adam lowers the bottle. ] Adam Christ: Where do we start? How? Dr. Forbes: Why, here, of course! [ Adam looks around. ] Adam Christ: ...Here? Dr. Forbes: Of course! Why else would I tell you to meet me in a gym? [ Adam shrugs. ] Dr. Forbes: Look, Adam... you're going against two big men of CCW; Enigma and Blayze. They're tough cookies to crack. Adam Christ: They could crack themselves without the help of Jack and I. Why should I feel threatened? Dr. Forbes: Alright, it's true that they would LOVE to take a shot at one another, but that STILL doesn't rule out the question--what if? What if they work good together, just this once?? And since weapons aren't LEGAL, will you be prepared!? [ Adam grins to him. ] Adam Christ: It won't be a problem, Doc. Jack and I will cover it... [ Adam pulls the water up to take another sip of it. Dr. Forbes swipes it from his hand. ] Dr. Forbes: I was told by JACK to get you preparing for that match, and that's EXACTLY what you're going to do! [ Adam looks at Dr. Forbes, his eyebrow narrowing a bit. ...Dr. Forbes looks back, but seems a bit frightened, cautiously stepping back once more. ...Adam gives him a smirk. ] Adam Christ: Sure. Alright, what do you want me to do? Dr. Forbes: Good. Come this way... [ Dr. Forbes turns and walks out of the scene, and Adam follows. The scene then fades to black. ...Adam's voice is, once more, heard in the background... ] ...For the next three days, I will be training for a match that should, and will be a pushover. ...I also discovered my belt, which was, indeed, taken and cleaned by the hospital officials. ...Idiots. ...Enigma, Blayze, you two aren't a threat. Blayze, you're a has-been. You lost your title, and now you've been reduced to nothing. You came out and stupidly stopped Jack from having his revenge against Enigma, and for your foolishness, YOU are teaming with the man who made you lose your glory!! Meanwhile, Jack and I have our glory...!! Blayze, you're YESTERDAYS NEWS! You're not a threat! You're not a CHALLENGE! Do you think that you can walk into the ring with Enigma and actually make an effort to SUCCEED?! BAH! You'll be watching your own back, making sure Enigma doesn't screw you a SECOND TIME! And, all the while, Jack and I will be on you like HOUNDS! WE won't give you a CHANCE to look over your shoulder!! WE won't give you the CHANCE to retrieve revenge for your stolen glory! YOU'RE DONE, Blayze! You shouldn't even bother coming out next Thursday. Just go home where you belong... and stay out of the ring. You'll be safer that way. ...And then you have Enigma. Enigma, if it weren't for Blayze, Jack would have had his REVENGE on you last Thursday! Therefore, ONCE AGAIN, Jack was CHEATED! I don't appreciate that, Enigma. And, quite frankly, I'm not going to allow him to be cheated again. When you did it the first time, I wasn't there to correct your sins. ...The second time, I was... but that's beyond the point. Enigma, I don't give a DAMN about how 'hardcore' you think you are. You want to come out dressed in barbed wire!? So fucking be it!! You want to pour kerosine all over me!? FINE, I like fire! But this Thursday, if you plan on doing ANY of that shit, you will LOSE. LOSE. LOSE. LOSE. Jack and I can win WITHOUT weapons! And, although it won't be as much FUN, it'll be just as entertaining! Enigma, YOU dragged yourself into this. You're not even involved in a tag-team match--this is a HANDICAP match! Blayze isn't going to HELP you! Blayze isn't going to do SHIT for you! You're HELPLESS, Enigma! HELPLESS!! I'm going to do what Blayze screwed Jack out of doing last week... and that's give Jack his REVENGE! His RIGHTFUL REVENGE!! So, Enigma... Blayze... I hope you enjoy pain. Because next Thursday, that's all you're going to feel. What, I repeat myself? I always talk about giving pain? ...That may be true, but do I not always keep my promises? ...Exactly. ...And then, we have the Main Event Battle Royale. ...I have my own plans for this match. ...I'll just... keep them to myself. ...Heh. We could all use a good surprise, couldn't we? ...Enigma... Blayze... Michael... Scorpion... CCW... it's time for blood to shed. [ End. ] View Adam Christ 's Biography Back
