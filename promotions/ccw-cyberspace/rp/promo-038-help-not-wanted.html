Help not wanted Andrew 'Ace' Hawk CCW Promo #38 Date: 02/25/03 Location: Hollywood, CA **The scene today opens at Andrew Hawk's old house, in the Hollywood hills. For those of you who know it already, don't worry. This is just an introduction of it. For those of you who don't, picture this: Hollywood hills, sunny day, with birds singing in the horizon. Walking through the dirt street, small marble columns on each side, going into the artificially-planted woods. A few yards deep, we see the house, a colonial house of sorts. Entering through the main door, we see a huge living room. Couches, dinner table, gambling table, all set to make the huge room into several smaller ones. The camera keeps moving, and it enters a big aisle, leading to a huge room in the back. Before it, though, we're granted a view of 2 doors to the left. In one of them, we hear several laughs. The camera decides to turn around and open the door, just to have a beer bottle be thrown at, missing the camera by inches and crashing on the wall behind it. The camera shakens his fear and enters the room, and what we see is a funky scene: Inside the room, we see Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, wearing his usual attire, Johnny Atlas, his friend and former bodyguard, Alex Hawk, Ace's twin brother, and five other people, apparently friends with Alex and Ace. One of them is a drunk japanese, two seem like normal businessman, one is a 6'9' tall tattooed bouncer and the fifth one looks like a white rapper, judging by his clothing. Ace finally says:** 'Ok, now, please, someone make me a serious, and I emphasize SERIOUS trading offer, or I'm calling this draft off now.' **The drunk japanese looks up, his eyes completely red, before saying:** 'You have Matsui, Sasaki and Ishii, right, Ace?' **Ace looks at him, almost breaking into laughs. He says:** 'Yeah, I do. What do you offer for them?' 'Oh, I don't know...', the japanese says. 'I wanna be fair with you, so... What do you say I give you Sosa, Helton and Nen for them?' **Ace's eyes shine bright, before saying:** 'Sure, you have a deal!!!' **After scracthing some papers in front of them, Alex says:** 'Now I see why you made the leap for those guys... Asshole...' **Everybody laughs, before Ace says:** 'Ok, no more offers, I'll have to call this quits. Everybody, out!!!' **Ace's friends don't like the call but leave the TV room one after one. The two businessmen carry the drunk japanese, which makes us assume he's their friend. Ace, Alex and Johnny stay in the room, having some beers. Alex turns at Ace the moment he comes back in the room, after leading their friends out and asks:** 'So, Ace, I bet your head must be hurting like hell right now.' 'Shut up...', Ace says, before putting his hand on his face, clearly in pain. She shakes it up and continues: 'This is nothing. I've received worst beatings in my life and bounced back all times. It won't be a few steel chair shots coming from a rookie dwarf that will make me loose my patience.' **Johnny looks at Ace, worried, and says:** 'I don't agree, Ace. I mean, you could have a concussion. After all these years, the beatings you took and you gave. The years of putting your body on the line for the fans... Your body must be screaming for help and some rest.' **Ace looks furious at Johnny, before saying:** 'Shut up, Johnny. You don't know anything. This is nothing more than a temporary drawback. It's nothing, really...' **Ace gets up and starts moving around freely. He gives a few flying kicks in the air, saying between each one:** 'See???' 'I'm completely good to go.' 'That bitch didn't hurt me.' **The moment he says that, Ace tries a round kick, but when he does, his face contorts in severe pain, and he falls on his back, completely out cold. Alex and Johnny run to help him, and the scene fades to black.** 2 days later **The scene reopens at a local hospital. Ace is lying on a hospital bed, his head being monitored with a few eletrodes. He slowly wakes up, and immediately sees a fresh, recognizable face in front of him:** 'Debbie...' 'Relax, Ace...', the former CCW Women's Champion Deborah Carmichael says. 'You're in a hospital.' 'What... What happened?', Ace says, disoriented. 'You had a concussion, and passed out in your home.', Debbie says, before taking some hair off Ace's face. 'Johnny and Alex called me.' 'You shouldn't have come...', Ace says. 'Things have changed...' 'I know...', Debbie answers. 'And you know what? I don't care... I'll take care of you, no matter how much of an asshole you've turned yourself into.' 'I don't need your sympathy...', Ace yells. 'I don't need your help!!! I can take care of myself!!!' **Ace gets up and starts ripping off all eletrodes off his head, but as he touches the floor, he falls down, bringing Debbie with him. Ace asks, mumbling:** 'Why did you stand in front of me?' 'I wanted to help you. And I will, no matter how much you say no...' **Ace nods, before passing out once again.** 2 days later **The scene changes once again to Andrew Hawk's mansion. We see the garage gym, where Ace usually practices for his matches. Ace is seen lifting some weights on the side, while Debbie is seen on the corner, with a glass of water and a couple of pills on a platter, sitting on a bench. Through reps, Ace says:** 'What are you still doing here? I told you I don't need your help' 'And I'm telling you, I will, no matter how many times you say no.' 'Fine...', Ace retorts. 'Then you'll stay there forever.' **Ace gets up after finishing his reps and moves to the punching bag. Slowly swinging kicks at it, fearful of what might happen, Ace doesn't look so good. Finally, he puts his hand on his face, showing he's still having headaches. Debbie walks to him and gives him the pills. Ace, contorting himself on his knees, looks away. Debbie then finally picks up a letter from a pocket on her denim jeans, showing it to Ace. He picks it up and reads it, his eyes gettin brighter. He says:** 'So, they're reinstating the Trans-Atlantic Championship?' 'Yes, Ace.', Debbie says. 'And you'll also have the chance to meet your old friend Mr. Gobstopper AND put your hands on Adam Christ again. But I'll only let you fight if you let me help you.' Ace laughs, sarcastically, before saying: 'Hah. The day I need you to let me do anything is the day I wanna be sent to a nursing home.' **The moment he says that, Debbie gives him a slap in the head, and Ace feels the pain, not much from the slap, but from the headache. Debbie says, while Ace contorts himself in pain on the floor:** 'You're not in shape to have this match, and I won't let you kill yourself in that ring, unless you let me help you NOW!' 'No....', Ace mumbles, on the floor. 'It's that or I'll have to call Johnny to help me restrain you here.' 'But I HAVE to fight. I have to, that title is MINE, I made that title what it is today. I HAVE to win it.', Ace yells. 'Then you'll have your shot', Debbie says. 'But first, you'll have to let me help you, OK???' **Ace struggles with his decision. After a few moments, he struggles to get up. Debbie moves away, but Ace looks at her, smiling, and finally takes the pills on the platter. Debbie smiles, and Ace tells her:** 'It won't be a mild migraine that will stop the Domination to continue running rampant on CCW. Adam will have his payback, the Gobber will have his match, and the world will have the Ace of Wrestling back, like it or not.' **Debbie smiles, and Ace slowly makes his way to the punching bag, where he slowly starts to work and the scene fades to black** View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
