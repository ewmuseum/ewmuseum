'A Brothers's Loss.' Update on Benny Blades

9 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A news-style hospital segment in which correspondent Joey Heyman reports on the comatose Benny Blades while his brother Bobby confronts him in the corridor.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Benny Blades Entitled: 'A brothers's loss.' UPDATE ON BENNY BLADES Written in Thursday, May 9, 2002, 19:23:48 Location: Misericordia Hospital in Edmonton This is a: Interview ***Scene opens in the observation section of the Hospital... The camera pans to a shot through glass of fallen CCW Star Benny Blades, lying in a comatose state in an observation room. The camera then pans to the left side of the room where a doctor is talking to a figure with his back to the cameras and a woman slung over his arm... From the outside of the room no voices can be heard, but the female is visibly distraught as the doctor continues to speak... The camera pans a full 180 to CCW Corrospondant Joey Heyman, who also can only shake his head before noticing the camera... Joey: Joey Heyman reporting here from the Misericordia Hospital in Edmonton, Alberta, from the room of Benjamin Blades. As of right now things are still dicey, but one thing has become clear, CCW Superstar Benny Blades his yet to regain consciousness and though his state has not worsened, doctor's believe that it has not improved. Speaking with head Neuro Surgeon Dr. Kevin Kostic, the experts seem to be stiffled as to anything more they can do for Benny. He is out of harms way in the sense that his life is no longer at risk, however as stated before it is unknown at this point the extent of the damage to his brain and his body... That combined with the fact that the doctor's are saying the likelyhood of his ever comig out of his comatose state is bleak at best. ***Footage of earlier is shown as Joey voices over... Joey: Earlier today, well decorated CCW Superstar Robert (Bobby) Blades and his wife Becky (Butterscotch) arrived at the hospital and remained here for much of the day speaking to physicians and experts. Bobby remained for much of this time at his brother's bedside and has not been availiable for comment as... ***Scene flashes back as the camera quickly spins as Bobby and his wife make their way out of the room... Bobby has a cold look of absolute disbelief and disdain as he closes the door... Becky is trying to console him with tears running down her own face... Joey quickly turns to face the multi time CCW Champion... Joey: Bobby... ***Bobby gives a cold look to Joey and looks his in the eyes... Bobby: Joey... I have known you throughout my career in CCW, I respect you and I consider you a close friend... But Joey, I am telling you right now... For your own sake back off right now before sI do something I might regret... This isn't the time... Joey: Bobby, I know it's difficult but the world is wondering... ***Suddenly Bobby gently moves Becky to the side as he suddenly grabs hold of Joey Heyman and slams him against a wall... Bobby: JOEY!... I AM TELLING YOU RIGHT NOW... THIS ISN'T THE FUCKING TIME!!! I DIDN'T COME BACK HERE TO CUT A FUCKING PROMO!!! I AM HERE BECAUSE MY BROTHER IS A FUCKING VEGETABLE AND IT WAS YOUR GODDAMN FED THAT DID IT TO HIM!!! ***Joey reels as Bobby tightens his grip on Joey... his face filled with anger, Becky quickly runs to his side and rubs his shoulder... Becky: Bobby! Bobby you have to calm down... ***Then as if suddenly being faced with a frightening realization Bobby releases his grip and places Joey down... He takes a deep breath and appears to regain his composure... Bobby:... I... I'm sorry Joey... That wasn't fair... Nothing seems fair right now... It's like... It's like... Do you know what it feels like to love something... Love it so much, and then turn around and... I don't know... Accidents happen... They happen... But not to him... Not to my big brother... ***Bobby trails off as his face returns back to a distant look of shock, Becky moves in and comforts him once again... Becky: (motioning) Could we turn this off... please... ***Joey motions and the camera is placed out of the shot and on the floor as the scene begins to fade out... Standing in the hallway, on the shoulder of his petite wife, the 255 lb. former CCW TA, Hardcore, 3 time Indy and 3 time CCW Heavyweight Champion... Weeps for a lost brother. View Biography
