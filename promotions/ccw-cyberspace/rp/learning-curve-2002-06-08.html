Learning Curve

What this is A promo roleplay in which Danny Bryson addresses CCW president Sylkk and his chosen opponents ahead of a Monday match.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Danny Bryson Entitled: Learning Curve??? Written in Saturday, Jun 8, 2002, 15:46:14 Location: This is a: Match RP *The camera fades in with Bryson, sitting in front of the camera, a knowing smile pasted upon his face. He begins to speak, slowly enough to be understood, yet forcefully enough to be believed* 'Ladies and gentlemen. You may remember a time when I was, perhaps, intransigent towards those who would wish to interview me. Stubborn, perhaps. Sometimes, even violent. I see now that, by behaving as I did, I missed a vital opportunity to connect to you, the fans of the CCW, on a level unmatched, even by our own, in ring presence. You see, before, I was addled, batterted and assualted internally. I carried the entire burden of the Inferno on my own. It knawed, teethed, burned at my skull. It rattled in my ear, whispering to me....whispering....but now, the burden is eased, since I have a brace of fine men to help me carry it. The Fury is bigger than the Inferno could ever be. But it is a burden no one man will ever have to bear alone. No, I see the chance I have missed. Firstly, lets discuss Sylkk. I feel sorry for you. No, no, really I do. You see, when Spider shot you, if, indeed, Spider shot you. You must have lost more than a little ring time. You must have been robbed of more than your federation. You lost, I think, your sanity, your ability and your self respect. The Sylkk that lay down in the ring Thursday night and allowed himself to be battered and broken was not the same Sylkk who was CCW champion. That Sylkk would never have been so weak, so.....unassuming and unchallenging. And that Sylkk would have known when he was beaten. Instead, we have Sylkk version two. What does it feel like to keep battering your head against a brick wall every week, Sylkk. What does it feel like to come out time atfer time, with a new foolproof plan to scupper up, only to end up looking like a fool. Sylkk, you can put us up against your personal club. You can put us up against each other. You can suspend us, strip us of our titles. But you will never stop us....you will learn. One way or another. Just like you couldn't stop us by putting us in the same ring together. Just like you couldn't stop us by putting us against Chang and TVO and Blayze. You won't stop us by putting us in the ring on Monday. I have to ask you something though, Sylkk, while we're on the subject. Are these guys all your chosen ones. ASre these the best you've got to put against us. The House show champion, the CCW inaugaral fossil and someone who can't even bear to show us his face???? I'm willing to bet it's you, Sylkk, but that would mean that you had the guts to step in the ring with us and, well, we all know the answer to that. You expect us to succumb to the House show champion, a man who should never, has never and, after Monday night, will never again be a main eventer. And what about Lamen. Brad 'Fucking' Lamen. Yeah, I get that, 'Fucking up his career cos he's gone on 5 years too long', now thats the truth of the matter. Need I remind you, el Presidente, I've stepped into the ring with The good Mr Lamen on 6 different occasions. Each time I have pinned his shoulders to the canvas for three second. Correct me if I'm wrong, but if I have a 6-0 record against a man, whats the likelyhood of him stopping me??? And finally, mr Mystery. Brave decision, not showing your face. It measn you'll have the element of surprise. You'll survive for....oooh, let me think....10 seconds longer than your buddies, maybe. Oh, and Sylkk, if it is you, please remember what happened Thursday night... There's a saying in some parts that those who do not learn from the mistakes of history are doomed to repeat them. Remeber that Sylkk, when you and your doughboys pick yourselves up from the mat, bloody bruised and broken Monmday night. Oh, and one more thing, just remember that No one is safe......least of all you.' *Bryson smiles the same sickly smile as the camera fades away* View Biography
