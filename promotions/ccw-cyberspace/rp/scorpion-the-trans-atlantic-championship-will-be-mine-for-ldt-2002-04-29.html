Scorpion - The Trans Atlantic championship will be mine! (For LDT)

29 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Scorpion is offered mentorship by Spider ahead of his Trans Atlantic Title match and helps a friend in crisis.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Scorpion Entitled: The Trans Atlantic championship will be mine! (For LDT) Written in Monday, Apr 29, 2002, 20:58:42 Location: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania This is a: Match RP :::: Wednesday evening 6:08 p.m. Philadelphia, Pennsylvania :::: :::: After a Group Therapy performance that saw Scorpion getting eliminated by Eric Clark it seems now Scorpion has been given the opportunity to have a shot at the CCW Trans Atlantic Championship against Danny Bryson. This will be the second time Scorpion and Danny Bryson have clashed heads in the CCW. The first time Scorpion faced Danny Bryson it was a DQ victory for Scorpion due to the fact that Danny Bryson was drunk and not able to perform against Scorpion. Now sober and a champion it seems that the two will clash heads again, this time with the CCW Trans Atlantic Championship on the line in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania on Lockdown. This is Scorpion’s opportunity will he succeed or fail? Scorpion holds his future. :::: :::: Scorpion is seen walking around the First Union Center in a CCW t-shirt and is drinking a glass of Pepsi. Scorpion walks into the cafeteria and grabs some ziti and takes a seat at an empty table. Scorpion begins eating until a CCW front office employee interrupts him. :::: :::: CCW employee: Scorpion? :::: :::: Scorpion: (taking a bite then looking up at the man) What do you want? :::: :::: CCW employee: Spider wants to speak with you. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok, I’ll be there in a couple of minutes. :::: :::: Spider: He wants to speak with you now. :::: :::: Scorpion: He wants me right now? :::: :::: CCW employee: Yes, right now. :::: :::: Scorpion: This better be good. :::: :::: Scorpion picks up his dinner and drinks and follows the man into a suite overlooking the First Union Center. Spider is sitting at his desk watching some old wrestling footage when Scorpion walks in. :::: :::: Spider: (turns off T.V.) Scorpion nice to see you, take a seat. :::: :::: Scorpion: (sits in a chair) This better be good Spider, I’m not even done eating. :::: :::: Spider: Well sorry bout that. I just wanted to talk to you about your future in CCW. :::: :::: Scorpion: My future in CCW? What about it? :::: :::: Spider: Well your future in CCW is going to be a big one. You’re a young star with a lot of talent and last night you proved it with two of our veteran’s, Eric Clark and Slykk. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yeah what about it. I still got eliminated. :::: :::: Spider: Yes you did but that stuff happens to the best of us. I just have an offer for you. :::: :::: Scorpion: And what might that offer be? :::: :::: Spider: Listen, I’d like to see you succeed you’re a good kid and have a lot of talent. For the past couple of weeks you have been coming to me for advice and I was wondering if you would mind if I become sort of a mentor to you. I would like to help you get better. :::: :::: Scorpion: You be my mentor? :::: :::: Spider: Yes. :::: :::: Scorpion: That would be a huge honor. :::: :::: Spider: Good. I have another offer for you after Lockdown but I want you to focus on your Trans Atlantic Title match first. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok. :::: :::: Spider: Bryson is a tough competitor and even though you have a win over him you have yet had a one on one match with him. Group Therapy was a taste of what Bryson can do. Now you get 100 percent Bryson and to beat Bryson you need to bring 110 percent Scorpion out. He is a hardcore wrestler, likes to make you bleed. You need to go higher then his level of intensity. You just have to be better then him. Prove to everyone you desire this title and that you want it more then anyone in the CCW. :::: :::: Scorpion: You bet I will. I want the gold more then anybody in the CCW and I have been hungry for a title since I lost the Hardcore Title. Bryson saw what I’m all about. I will beat him and prove to everyone that this Revolution is alive and running through the veins of the CCW like never before. :::: :::: Spider: That’s the right attitude. That’s a champion’s attitude. :::: :::: Scorpion: And more importantly it’s my attitude. :::: :::: Spider: Good luck Scorpion. :::: :::: Scorpion: Thank you Spider. :::: ::::: Scorpion gets up from his chair and as he does so does Spider they shake hands and Scorpion leaves the office and heads down the hallway and is stopped by his cell phone ringing. It’s T.J. Walker’s wife Jennifer. :::: :::: Scorpion: Oh hey Jen. What’s up? :::: :::: Jennifer: (crying) T.J. is missing. :::: :::: Scorpion: What do you mean T.J. is missing? :::: :::: Jennifer: I haven’t seen him since Friday morning. :::: :::: Scorpion: He told me he was going to Boston to visit Nomar. :::: :::: Jennifer: No he wasn’t he was going to Arizona to try to persuade Diamondbacks pitcher Curt Schilling to be his next client. :::: :::: Scorpion: Did he ever go there? :::: :::: Jennifer: No his flight was Friday night. :::: :::: Scorpion: Why didn’t you call me sooner? :::: :::: Jennifer: Because I didn’t know what the CCW’s schedule was. :::: :::: Scorpion: Oh man. This is just great what a I way to take my mind off the match. :::: :::: Jennifer: I’m sorry Scorpion, I’m just worried about him. :::: :::: Scorpion: Listen I’ll try to contact him ok and I’ll call you tonight. :::: :::: Jennifer: Ok Scorpion. Bye. :::: :::: Scorpion: Bye. :::: :::: Scorpion hangs up his cell phone and begins to call T.J. Walker. Someone answers his phone. :::: :::: Scorpion: Hello. T.J.? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: What the hell do you want? :::: :::: Scorpion: T.J. is this you? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Yeah it’s me. :::: :::: Scorpion: Jennifer is losing her mind why haven’t you contacted her? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: I went to Arizona. She knew that. :::: :::: Scorpion: She said you haven’t talked to her since Friday morning. It’s Wednesday night. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: So what. I have been busy. :::: :::: Scorpion: Busy doing what? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Trying to get Curt Schilling. :::: :::: Scorpion: I think your bullshitting me. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: No I’m not. :::: :::: Scorpion: T.J. you sound drunk, your slurring you words. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: I’m not drunk! :::: :::: Scorpion: You are to you just got out of rehab six months ago. You were clean and look at you now. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Oh so what. I was thirsty. :::: :::: Scorpion: T.J. come home tomorrow and put yourself in rehab again your not going to lose your life. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: I’m not going back. :::: :::: Scorpion: Yes you are. You got married two years ago and a little child is on its way four months from now don’t blow it. You don’t want this for your family right? :::: :::: T.J. Walker: (crying) Your right Scorpion. Tell Jen I’m on my way home and to call the rehab and tell them I’m coming back. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok man. You’re doing the right thing. :::: :::: T.J. Walker: Thank you. Bye. :::: :::: Scorpion: Bye. :::: :::: Scorpion hangs up the cell phone and calls Jen. :::: :::: Scorpion: Hello. Jen its Scorpion, he’s in Arizona and he’s drunk. He’s on his way home and he’s on his way back into rehab. :::: :::: Jennifer: (crying) Oh thank god he’s alive. I’ll call the rehab. Thank you Scorpion and good luck tomorrow. :::: :::: Scorpion: Ok, take care. Bye. :::: :::: Jennifer: Bye. :::: :::: Scorpion hangs up the phone and heads down to the Television production department to cut a promo. :::: :::: T.V. production employee: Ok Scorpion you ready? :::: :::: Scorpion: Yeah. :::: :::: T.V. production employee: Ok your on in 5,4,3,2,1. :::: :::: The cameras begin rolling and Scorpion begins to cut his promo for Lockdown. :::: :::: Scorpion: Trans Atlantic Championship on the line this Thursday. Danny Bryson vs. Scorpion. The main event of Lockdown. Danny Bryson this will be the second time we will face each other. The first time you drank your way out of an ass whooping, this time you can’t escape the beating you will get this Thursday. I want you to take a good look at that Trans Atlantic title and as you do you will see a reflection. No that reflection is not you, that reflection is my face and that reflection will take place after our match, after I beat you. It’s time for the Revolution to begin and it’s time that you feel the revolution. That Trans Atlantic Championship is mine, get used to it. Because what I say are more then comments and statements, what I say are promises and I promise I will be the Trans Atlantic Champion. :::: View Biography
