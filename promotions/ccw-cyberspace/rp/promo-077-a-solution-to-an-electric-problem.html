A solution to an electric problem

A solution to an electric problem Andrew 'Ace' Hawk CCW Promo #77 Date: March 11th 2003 Location: Hollywood, CA **The night is dark, and the rain falls heavy. The thunderous sound of water hitting concrete doesn't hide the deafening sound of a scream. A horrid scream of pain and suffering. The scene then immediately changes to a shot of an open field. The place? East Germany. The year? Unknown. We see several men jumping the concrete wall that separated East and West Berlin for decades. After a few yards, dodging bullets from the soldiers, the few men trying to escape hit a fence, but when they hit, screams fill the air. The fence is eletrified. Most of them give up, but just a few brave ones manage to smart their ways over the fence. One or two try to overcome the intense pain but can't, falling on the ground, completely toasted inside. When the men finally reach the other side and are about to reach the wall, a small group of soldiers arrive, all pointing guns at them. Only one voice can be heard, cutting through the rain:** 'Ah, SHIT!!!' **The voice is well known, and the camera zooms out, revealing the image comes from a high def TV. After a closer inspection, we see this is Andrew Hawk's house, and HE released the expletive. He seems rather bored, but his eyes don't leave the tube. After a few more moments, the TV is turned off. He turns around, saying:** 'Who the hell turned off the TV?' 'I did...', answers a VERY pissed off Debbie Carmichael. 'What the hell are you doing?' **Ace looks at her, rather intrigued. He says:** 'So, now you're my babysitter? I don't remember paying for you to change my diaper or something...', Ace says, sarcastically. 'Well, Ace, sometimes I feel like one, because you won't follow my advices. I TOLD YOU that TV might do more damage to your brain right now. You should not, in any way, endanger your health status.' 'My status is FINE, Debbie...', Ace says, before putting his hand slightly on his head. 'AHA, SEE? I KNEW IT!', yells Deb, thinking Ace is having a headache again. 'No, no, I'm just really annoyed, Deb. You don't have to help me EVERY SINGLE TIME', Ace says, sighing. 'Well, I won't, if you follow my instructions.' **Ace gets up and walks past her, saying:** 'Fine, all right, can I at LEAST get ready? I'm booked for this next Thursday...' **Debbie, following him past the corridor into a huge ball room at the end, asks, curious:** 'Who are you fighting, Ace?' 'Gabriel Nyte...', says Ace, giving his shoulders, showing he doesn't care much. 'I don't get it...', Deb says. 'Why would someone book you against...' **Debbie finally stops, after climbing the stairs in the ballroom and going past the corridor on the second floor to the pool area, where Ace went to, remembering what happened last Lockdown. She says:** 'Oh, no. Not only no, OH HELL NO!!!' 'What, Debbie? What's wrong?' 'ACE, you CAN'T FIGHT Nyte!!!' 'Oh, but I really can!', Ace says, smiling. 'He's just a crazy little man, with his ritual stuff. Too emotional, he won't stand a chance against the Ace of Wrestling.' 'DAMN IT, Ace, don't you remember? The stipulation!!!' **Ace stops, now at poolside, under a marvellous sunny sky, and asks:** 'What are you talking about?' 'Damn, Ace. Devyl was arguing with the commish about putting Gabriel Nyte in an eletrifying cage match, and instead of going herself into the match, she asked for the commish to put a suitable opponent she would find.' **Ace releases a small grin, saying:** 'And I guess that's me... GOOD!!!' **Debbie looks at him, completely shocked.** 'GOOD??? Are you KIDDING ME? You can get KILLED!!!' 'Not if I have a good plan...', Ace says, smiling. **Ace walks away, leaving Debbie standing, not understanding. They both go downstairs into the garage, under the pool, where Ace's private gym is. Debbie continues to not understand anything. Ace starts going around some stuff in the place, looking for something around several old, dusty boxes thrown in a corner closet, until he finally finds something. He picks up a small one, hiding it from the camera. After he opens it, he shows it to Debbie, whose face gets more relaxed. Ace blinks at her and says:** 'Don't worry. Devyl won't get back at me by throwing me in an eletricfied cage. She'll have to be more inventive to do that...' **Debbie smiles, grabbing the box and going upstairs. Ace walks to a punching bag and starts practicing some kicks, as the scene fades to black** View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
