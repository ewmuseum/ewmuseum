Gabriel Nyte - Lord give me strength

26 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A Match RP in which Gabriel Nyte prays in a New York cathedral and trains with David Oakes before Group Therapy.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Gabriel Nyte Entitled: Lord give me strength Written in Friday, Apr 26, 2002, 22:07:01 Location: This is a: Match RP [We start in a cathedral in the city of New York. It is about 5 blocks away from the Garden. The area is busy, but the church is empty. We see Gabriel kneeling at a pew, with his mind deep in prayer. Of course, he's wearing his normal monk's garbs.] Gabriel: Is this a test Lord? Just like how you test Moses, and how Lucifer tested your Son? If it is, I wish you would give me advise on what to do, and where to go. I am on a team with the Devil herself, a clown, a man full of Pride, and another man who is green with Envy. You are expectimg me to work with all of these sinners? [He adjusts himself on the pew...his knees were falling asleep.] Gabriel: You place me against a rich gentile, and four heathens that you want me to squash. Well...If you wish it, O Lord...I shall work with these people, to show all of CCW your words of wisdom. [David Oakes walks into the church...he shudders slightly, and unnoticably to the naked eye. He sits with Gabriel.] David: Brother Nyte...Is it time for training? [Gabriel gives himself the sign of the cross and sits up.] Gabriel: Not yet. It has been almost a month since I have taken you away from your unholy bride, David. With a name like that, you could be heaven-bound either way. David: Indeed...my name means 'King of the Jews' right? Gabriel: That is the symbolism behind it. You must realize that you must resist the temptation to go back, David...you must not allow yourself to be corrupted....Ever. David: Yes, Brother Nyte. Gabriel: Now...it is time to train. [Gabriel goes first out of the church first...bowing toward the Altar before leaving. David however...steps into the aisle...stares at the altar.] Gabriel: David..are you coming? David: Uh, yes Brother Nyte. [David turns and leaves the church as we fade to black.] View Biography
