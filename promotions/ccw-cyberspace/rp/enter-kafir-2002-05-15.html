Enter Kafir

15 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A descriptive atmospheric introduction in which Dante Kafir, an outcast figure, speaks from a skyscraper roof over New York City.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Dante Kafir Entitled: Enter Kafir Written in Wednesday, May 15 Location: New York City This is a: Match RP The stars twinkle overhead as the only thing that disrupts the peaceful night sky is the wind that blows a cool breeze. Amidst the twinkling stars and the chilly draft, the round, pale white circle of a full moon can be seen shining like a beacon, lighting up the area around it and making the scene a picturesque cold Spring night. As the wind continues to howl, the stars continue to twinkle and a little face can be seen in the bright round circle of the full moon, as the man on the moon looks down happily upon the sleeping city of New York. The night seems to be at it’s prime as the sound of New York traffic can be heard faintly in the distance behind the soft wailing of wind. It doesn’t seem to affect the man on the moon who continues to look down with a smile which shines across the star studded blanket of night and onto the city below. All is well in the fine city of New York…. The night sky with all its beauty continues to shine above as the lights of the city below begin chime in like a chorus along with the stars. It looks as though the view is from somewhere on top of the world…nothing can be done to harm it and nothing can be done to disturb the peace of the night. Snap~! As in every horror or suspense movie ever filmed there is a snapping sound which breaks the peace and the serenity, and as in every horror or suspense movie ever filmed there is a rather dark looking man standing somewhere in the background…this is no exception! Standing or rather squatting directly behind the scenic view of the city is a rather small looking man, dressed in what seems to be no more than a pair of dark jeans and a dark turtleneck with his hands clasped together somewhere in between his legs and his head hung low so that his long hair drapes across his face. Once again the wind softly howls as it’s eerie melody begins to sound out above the sound of traffic once more and the mysterious man’s hair begins to sway in the breeze blowing across his face but still keeping it concealed in the darkness of the night. The man doesn’t seem to care too much about his hair as he pushes up off of his knees and stands up, still looking rather small with the wind continuing to blow his long and seemingly dark hair across his face. The eerie melody of the wind continues to play through the air as the man on the moon seems to focus his attention onto the smallish man who is now walking forward towards the city below…. It’s as though he’s Jesus, walking on air rather water and seemingly floating off to the city below…but suddenly the man stops and falls back swinging his legs over something and then sitting upright looking down at the city below before looking up towards the full moon. Finally it all comes together… 'This is it…' The man speaks as his voice trails off into the wind, carrying it off far into the darkness. His hair also finally carrying off into the wind revealing the face of a rather good looking young man who would be in his early twenties. 'I have reached my goal on my own and there is nothing that anyone can do to stop me now….people had told me that I was going to fail, people had told me to give up hope before I let myself down…I did and now look where that’s taken me!' The man’s voice can be heard echoing in the night as the breeze continues to blow at him as he tilts his head and stares down. As he stares down the city shines up at him as he appears to be sitting atop a rather large building overlooking the city below, it’s almost as though he’s sitting atop New York! There is no other building around that even compares to the one that’s he’s sitting on in height, there is no other building that even comes close! It would appear that he is somewhere near the heaven’s but the only problem being that they aren’t opening their doors for him. 'Finally I am on top of everything that I used to despise! I gave up all hope and let the human race….but when I finally rejoined, I rose above them all!' The man begins to chuckle as he continues to speak off into the darkness. 'When I look towards the darkness now, I don’t see an enemy, I don’t push it away….I see a very close friend and I embrace it so that it! No longer do I feel pain, I feast on it….There is no explanation for what has happened, there may very well be no explanation for what will happen but all I know is that I am on top of the world….' The man raises his head and looks towards the full moon as it stares back at him and he continues to speak, almost trying to rub everything in it’s face. 'My time is now…very soon I will join the stars above and people will finally realize that everything they don’t want to see has come back to haunt them! Fear is not an enemy…Fear is nothing more than something to over-come….' 'Darkness shall be my friend!' The man stares at the moon with a sinister look crossing over his dark eyes as he looks towards the city which seems to be a million miles below him before leaning back and up onto his feet and laughing off into the distance as the wind picks itself up once more. Once again the man seems to glide off into the darkness from where he came not making any sound and having proved not much of a point. The Outcast seems to have slithered back off to his own domain leaving the New York city peaceful but stained…. The wind continues to howl and the city’s lights and stars begin to chime in with it overhead as the man on the moon can be seen looking down upon the city of New York, his happy smile now turned into an evil grin and his eyes looking sinister upon the city below! The wind begins to howl violently as the man on the moon can be heard silently chuckling to himself, having found an ally and another follower of the Darkness. Slowly but surely the lights of the city begin to fade out off into the distance as the darkness begins to envelope the surroundings…. View Biography
