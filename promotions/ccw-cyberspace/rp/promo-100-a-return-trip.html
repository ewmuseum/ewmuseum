A return trip Jason Dread CCW Promo #100 Date: 3/18/03 Location: Dread's locker room I sat and thought to myself, turning the loose fabric through my hands...what exactly was going on with everything? What is this sad state of affairs? Will the same melody play this week? I guess I'd been looking at the shirt for the better part of a half an hour. Once my mind starts wandering I'm never able to quite exactly get it back to where it belongs. If nobody else can tell by now, I'll be damnded shocked. It's been a quality I've held ever since I was younger...my mind taking itself on a journey, I guess that's the only way you can describe it. The only qualm I had with this time is that I was still sitting in the locker room which I took residence in for the night about twelve hours ago. Why exactly is it that I stayed in the arena after the night was over? I could offer an explanation as saying I'm reflecting, but that would be a lie despite my outward appearances in doing such. There's just something on the inside which draws me...makes me think about everything. Not even to plan or calculate, however. Very odd... The shirt I held smelled the same way it did since I took it off, like rotten sweat...battered bodies...even the faint smell of blood. I'd always thought of myself as one crazy son of a bitch at times, but several hours ago I was stuck in the middle of something I didn't know what I was getting myself into. Always known to me, I knew there was a fine line between brutality and insanity. What I did to Khan by ending his career was brutality. How I captured the GCW World Title for the first and only time was brutality. I know what brutality is...but what I officiated on the last edition of Lockdown was pure and utter insanity. There's absolutely no other word to describe it. Others may call me whatever names they feel appropriate, but I'd never fancied myself a man to be thrown through a plate glass window unless it meant something important, or was for a damn good cause. You see, there's getting your ass beat on one side of the spectrum, then on the other side you have when a man is brought inches to his death. That I can't say I'm too fond of in certain terms... ...on the upside, though, I have a damn good looking blood splatter on my shirt... I turned the referee's shirt over once again in my hands, running my fingers over the coarse fabric, then of the blood which managed to find it's way onto the shirt. I can't exactly remember who it was from...it's not like that was of much importance in the long run...but the crimson still stains, and still leaves a message. A message to everyone...that I've been through hell and back... ...I'd saw a whole hell of a lot in the past night... ...and I was damn ready to do it all over again. That's right...for one more week before Revelations was I to adorn the zebra costume and allow myself to be subject to another round of brutality...but this time is going to be much different. VERY different. I'm not going to be the only man in the ring to officiate. Blayze will be there with me every step of the way. I guess for the first time, at that moment I took notice of the colors of the shirt I held in my hands. White & black, negating the red of course...two completely opposite colors. It maintains such a perfect dichotomy, as does that of Yin and Yang, as well as light and darkness. White and black. Jason Dread and Blayze. What a fitting example for what the two of us have in common...damn well nothing. I've let myself be prone to Blayze for the last time. Now I've got the opportunity to strike back at him...to give the 'Black Jesus' a little taste of MY scriptures! To show him MY gospels! I'm going to CRICIFY that motherfucker!!! My fist made a tight ball around the shirt. My teeth slowly started to grit just thinking about what has been done...my body tensed. Then everything relaxed. Time is the only thing which separates the opportunity which I'm going to grab by the throat and CHOKE to death! Time is what separates me from my revenge. Time passes quickly...but as I sat, organizing the last of my thoughts...and interesting notion came to mind. It's amazing how time can take forever to pass as you are slowly being raped of life. How torture seems so slow...how the pain drags on forever. I laughed...everything is going to be... ...my time is drawing nearer... ...a demise is evident... ...Blayze has no idea what I have planned for his last supper... View Jason Dread 's Biography Back
