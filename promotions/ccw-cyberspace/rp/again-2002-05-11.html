Again

11 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A repetitive monologue in which Ricky Thunder prepares for another Independent Title match against John Coens.
Legend Board | Back to Roleplays Main Ricky Thunder Entitled: Again Written in Saturday, May 11, 2002, 03:39:11 Location: ?? This is a: Match RP *Scene opens up in a dark room. A candle is lit in the center of the room placed on a small table. There is the shape of legs sitting in a chair behind the candle but no face can really be made out. Suddenly the chair moves a bit, and into the light leans Ricky Thunder. His usually styled bright blond hair, now looking more ragged, and dirty. The color has faded to a weathered blond look. He leans forward placing his head in his hands as his fingers run through his hair.* Ricky: Again. The time has come, once again. *Ricky begins to rock back and forth a bit in his chair. He looks up a bit as the flame from the candle catches his eye, it appears that the flame is burning inside of him.* Ricky: Again. I get my shot at the Independent Title. *Ricky again buries his face in his hands and comes to a halt in his rocking motions. He runs his fingers back and forth through his hair, before starting to pull on it a bit. His voice changes from a whisper to a bit more.* Ricky: Again. Gobber has threatened to cost me the title. *Ricky gets to his feet and slowly begins pacing around the table. He paces slowly, methodically, his steps in tune with his heartbeat. Every step he takes around the table, he exhales with. He suddenly stops.* Ricky: Again. I will be unrelenting, in my assault on Gobber if he interferes. Again, I will show John Coens what I am all about. Again, I will step up to the plate. Again, I will walk away a winner. Again, my opponent will know the true meaning of agony and defeat. *Ricky makes his way back to the chair and sits down. He begins the rocking motion again, this time with his back pressed firmly against the seat. He has his arms crossed across his chest, and his hands clenched together. His eyes shut tightly, and a heavily furrowed brow.* Ricky: Again. I will prove to CCW, I can and will be better than my brother. Again, I must stand on my own. Again, I ask SuMa to stay away from the ring. Again, I ask John Coens to be a man. Again, I ask him to admit defeat. Again, I destroy a man with the Rolling Thunder. Again. Again. Again. *Ricky suddenly thrusts forward with a evil grin on his face glaring at the candle. His face so close to the flame the heat itself reflects off of his golden skin. He stares into the candle, before blowing it out and speaking in a whisper.* Ricky: Again, the CCW Independent Title gets a chance to end up where it belongs. Again, I have the chance to bring it home. Again, I face John Coens. Again, I WILL defeat him. Again, I will, it is my destiny! View Biography
