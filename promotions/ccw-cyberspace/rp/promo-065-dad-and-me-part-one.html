Dad and Me...{Part One} Dan Logan CCW Promo #65 Date: 05.03.03 Location: Council Flat 120, Moss Side 05.03.01 'Dad...you in here?' There is no answer from the old man as in I walked. You see it was two years ago exactly that my dad finally passed away after moving back into the country only a month prior. 'What the hell have you done you old fool!' I had panicked when I saw the pills beside his feet and for ten minutes all I could do was shake him. I didn't phone the medic because I couldn't even remember what a phone was, let alone how to use one. 'Get up! Get up now!' I screamed and cried and then when he wouldn't move still, I dropped onto the ground and openly wept. 'You said that we were fine! You told me that is didn't matter that mum had gone...you told me you loved me!' He had shattered my world and attempting to claw your way back into a working way of life is hard after that. The next door neighbor Mary, looked through the door and let herself in when she had heard me crying out. When she found me she did not panic she seemed to almost leave her body to it and switch off onto autopilot, sorting it all out for me. 'Don't worry, he'll be ok...I hope' Her attempts to console me did not work; I knew deep down that he had gone and that as of today...nothing would be the same ever again. Two days ago, Mary and my dad had been talking about life, sat upon the same green couch of battered leather that I now cried my heart out upon. Now he was gone and them conversations would never happen again...he would never talk about this or that and I would never again see my dad. Now he was gone and the salty tears engulfed not only my body but my mind as well. Phoning the ambulance we waited, we sat and talked about life and how it didn't matter that he was unwell because he was going to be fine. We sat and waited and waited and sat, for two hours nobody came and yet still dad lay there dying upon his own floor while I looked on unable to help in any way. When they came they rushed him toward the hospital with me and Mary sitting in the back as they attached wires to his frail body and began to judge what was wrong with him. I knew what was wrong...'He is dead!' I wanted to scream out, just to end the charade that seemed to be going on around me. Through traffic they rushed, attempting to reach the hospital before it was announced that they thought it was to late...it is never to late for them though. The noises around me were swallowed by my unhappiness and the silence I felt was warm and lived in 'You'll stay with me, won't you Mary?' She nodded her own frail head in answer, locked away within her own mind fighting to keep her cool from her own demons who laughed at her as she wished to cry for the only real friend she had left lay dying in front of her very eyes. My breathing had become heavier and quite quickly I found I could no longer breath...carbon seemed to fill the air and slowly I fell as my eyes rolled back into my head. Slowly the never ending fall toward the floor began and hitting my head I remember only the sever pain as it all fades into darkness... View Dan Logan 's Biography Back
