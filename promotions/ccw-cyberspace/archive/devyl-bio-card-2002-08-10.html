What this is Bio card for the CCW wrestler Devyl, with stats, moves and a background story: a heel brawler who returns to CCW after its closing.
Name: Devyl Hometown: Aurora, IL Email: [detail withheld] Weight: 145 lbs. Height: 5' 8" Age: 24 Status: Heel Manager/Valet: None Wrestling Style: Brawler Theme Music: "Fithos Lusec Wecos Vinosec" by Nobuo Eumatsu Singles Record: 0-1-0 Hardcore Record: 0-0-0 Tag Team Record: 0-0-0 Championships: None Favorite Quote: "I will tear your soul apart." Favorite Weapon: None Debut: August 14, 2002 Superstar Background: Devyl was a part of CCW's original incarnation, and was dominating the Women's division before it's closure. Once CCW closed she dropped out of sight and hasn't been seen for several months. Now, she's back, and won't speak of where she's been. Finishing Move Soul Stealer - Places foot on crotch, monkey flips opponent onto head. (Pin) Trademark Move Demonic Sacrifice - A surfboard variation in which after locking the move in, Devyl pulls on the neck instead of the arms to make it a chokehold submission. (Sub) Favorite Moves Leg sweep w/ mounted punching "Rear Naked Chokehold" Low Blow Cocky Pin (places foot on opponents chest instead of holding their shoulders down.)
