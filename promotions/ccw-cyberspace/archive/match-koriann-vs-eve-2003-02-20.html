CCW (1998–2005) Card Archive

CCW match: Koriann vs. E.V.E.

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Koriann vs. E.V.E. Date: 2/20/03 Show: Lockdown BJS: Well, that was a great bang-up of a match. We're not even halfway done yet, are we? JD: Nope. In fact, we have our women's match coming up right now. BJS: Superstar vs. Clone, right? JD: Well, technically, Koriann isn't a superstar, and EVE really isn't a clone, per se... BJS: **folding his arms** Yup! Superstar vs. Clone. Nayt Willems: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. On her way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 100 pounds, from Los Vegas, Nevada, Koriann!!! 'I'm Real' by J-Lo hits and the crowd boos horrendously. Koriann walks out of the back between the gold and silver spark shower, and down the isle to the ring. She enters the ring and raises her arms above her head, spinning around and showing herself off. Koriann: I RULE!!! JD: Well, I see she's lost none of her self confidence. BJS: You're right about that. It's people like her and Brad Lamen thatmake this place what it is! CCW wouldn't even be on the map if it weren't for her! JD: What have you been smoking? Koriann had nothing to do with CCW's greatness! If anything, she ruined it! Nayt Willems: And her opponent, weighing in at 200 pounds... E.V.E.!!! The crowd cheers as 'Re-Creation' by Orgy hits and EVE appears from backstage and makes her way down to the ring. She pauses briefly as she climbs up and in, checking out the crowd and wearily eyeing her unknown opponent. JD: Well, EVE might not have gotten the win over Lacey last week, but she's definitely here and ready to fight this week. I don't like Koriann's chances. BJS: EVE's got a great body, no doubt. But she's nowhere near Koriann's league when it comes to ring smarts. JD: The word 'smart' and 'Koriann' should never be used in the same sentence. Chucky signals for the bell, and E.V.E. immediately gets nailed with a charging axehandle bodyblock from Koriann, knocking her back into the ropes. Not giving E.V.E any time to recover, Koriann hits E.V.E. with a spinning elbow smash to the face, followed up by several hard chops to her chest. JD: Well... This is definitely a surprise! Did Koriann somehow learn to actually wrestle in all the time she had off? She actually looks competent out there... Koriann knocks E.V.E. to the canvas with a short Clothesline. BJS: Of course she looks competent! Nothing against Devyl, but Koriann epitomizes what it truly means to be a woman wrestler in this day and age. I foresee her winning the Heavyweight title again someday soon. E.V.E. is back on her feet, ducks under another attempted Chop by Koriann, and then gut-wrenches the jackass off her feet. JD: And E.V.E. turns the gut-wrench into a powerbomb! What strength! I don't think Koriann is going to be winning anything any time soon... E.V.E. maneuvers Koriann into an armlock leglock submission. Chucky moves to check for a tap out. Koriann tries to resist the painful hold... writhing back and forth, trying to escape. She eventually manages to reach a free hand up and grab a hold of E.V.E.'s hair. To protect herself, E.V.E. breaks the hold. Koriann, however, remains on the canvas, messaging her sore limbs. E.V.E. measures the jackass up, and drops a closed fist right into her midsection. BJS: Look at Koriann take that like the superb woman that she is! Any minute now, we're going to see her do something really cool, and you'll be sorry you ever doubted her. E.V.E. moves back to her feet. She leaps into the air and delivers a flying knee drop right into Koriann's gut. BJS: I SAID... Any minute now, Koriann's going to do something cool! E.V.E. goes up to the top rope and perches briefly on the turnbuckle. Then she flies off with beautiful confidence and grace that is only achieved through months of long practice, and connect with a Senton Bomb. E.V.E. makes a quick pin and Chucky starts the count. 1... 2... And Koriann kicks out. JD: Great effort by E.V.E. She almost had Koriann there. BJS: Yeah, but she didn't! E.V.E begins to pick Koriann up by the hair, and the jackass takes the opportunity to drive her knee into E.V.E.'s stomach. BJS: Alright! Now, do something cool! E.V.E. recovers well and tries to lock-up again. Wanting nothing to do with that, Koriann kicks E.V.E. in the back of the leg, tripping her to the canvas. E.V.E. tries to get back to her feet, but Koriann rakes her nails across E.V.E.'s back, leaving large red scratches. JD: Chucky looks like he's gonna warn Koriann about that, but E.V.E. reacts first, kicking out behind her! But Koriann grabs E.V.E.'s leg, preventing the retaliation. Instead, the jackass, Dragon Screws E.V.E.'s leg, twisting it painfully and sending her back to the mat. Koriann takes off running as E.V.E. attempts to get up again, bounces off the ropes, and connects with a thump, implanting her ass right into E.V.E.'s face. E.V.E. goes down and Koriann makes a quick cover. Chucky counts. 1... 2... And E.V.E. kicks out. BJS: Koriann almost won! A couple more moves, and E.V.E.'s done for! The two ladies both get to their feet. Koriann grabs E.V.E. by the hair and belts her across the face with a hard right... And then another. E.V.E. suddenly explodes into action and Spears Koriann to the canvas. E.V.E. starts unleashing lefts and rights into Koriann's face. Chucky restrains E.V.E. and pulls her off and away from Koriann, but not before she manages to land a good, hard kick to Koriann's midesection. Koriann climbs back to her feet, to find E.V.E. charging her again. E.V.E. promptly gets taken down with an armdrag takedown. As E.V.E. gets up yet again, Koriann slaps her right across the face. Slightly stunned, Koriann throws her off the ropes and hits her with a diving shoulder block. E.V.E.'s head raps off the canvas, dizzying her. Koriann grabs E.V.E. by the hair, lifts her to her feet, and then gives her an armbreaker. JD: E.V.E. looks really hurt and dazed now... She really needs a miracle of sorts to get back into this match... E.V.E. tries with an attempted eye poke as Koriann picks her up again, but it's ineffective as Koriann blocks it in classic Three Stooges tradition. Koriann picks E.V.E. up in a fireman's carry and flapjacks her onto the top rope. JD: And it looks like that miracle isn't happening any time soon... Koriann stands over E.V.E., taunting the downed girl and delivering several sharp kicks to the ribs. BJS: What an outstanding match this is! See, Doyle? Your girl is fucking up again! JD: She's not my girl! She wouldn't go out with me if I asked. Koriann delivers a spinning DDT to E.V.E.. The crowd starts booing and chanting, 'Koriann sucks! Koriann sucks!' Koriann flips them all off, and then puts E.V.E. into her very stupid-looking Anklelock submission. E.V.E. cries out in pain and quickly grabs a hold of the bottom rope. Chucky breaks the hold. Koriann scoffs, but then climbs the nearby turnbuckle and leaps off, nailing E.V.E. with an elbow drop. JD: E.V.E. is definitely hurting as she slowly climbs to her feet. Koriann lashes out and connects with a kick to the midsection, and... FAMEASSER!!! E.V.E.'s down and Koriann makes the cover! 1... 2... 3... JD: And this one's over! Koriann manages to pull off a victory! BJS: HA-HAH! YES! UP YOURS, DOYLE! Koriann: **inside the ring** I RULE!!! BJS: KORIANN RULES!!! The fans however, don't agree, and continue to let Koriann know exactly what they think of her. Koriann flips them all off again as she leaves the ring. JD: Well, that was quite unnecessary. Why the hell is Koriann even here? BJS: You heard her earlier... To kick Devyl's ass and become champion again! JD: **sigh** Whatever. At least she's on the right track...
