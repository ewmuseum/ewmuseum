Heatstroke 99 Show #48 - July 25, 1999

25 July 1999

What this is Cyberspace Championship Wrestling show script for Show #48 on July 25, 1999, from The Astrodome in Houston, Texas, with the card and the written play-by-play.
July 25, 1999 from The Astrodome in Houston, Texas - Show #48 Samson vs. Sid Powers - I Quit Closed Cage Match Apocalypse vs. Pepper The Cat - CCW Heavyweight Championship Doomsday vs. The Jolly Rancher - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Mash vs. Sam Frederick - CCW Independent Championship Supreme Machine vs. Mr. Gobstopper - COTR Finals "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Joquar - CCW Hardcore Championship "The Avenger" Tim Inman vs. "Big Ass" Bob Burner Sewer Savage vs. Ice Chamber Heatstroke `99 Results - July 25, 1999 Monday, 26-Jul-1999 02:52:16 [detail withheld] writes: CCW Theme Music begins playing as the opening of the show airs on PPV. *The CCW. For 5 months, the dominating force in sports entertainment!* Shows scenes of Samson and Sid Powers leading up to this match. ... Ladies and Gentlemen! Here we are! Heatstroke 1999! Tonight is the night in which we say goodbye to an athlete, and hello to a new era! Samson and Sid Powers will meet tonight inside of an enclosed cage and in an I Quit Match, loser leaves CCW! Also tonight, we have the finals of the Champion Of The Ring Tournament. Will it be Supreme Machine, or will it be Mr. Gobstopper? Only time will tell! The Heavyweight Title will also be on the line as Apocalypse defends against the #2 Contender, Pepper The Cat. Doomsday will once again defend his Trans-Atlantic Title against The Jolly Rancher. What will be in store for this second meeting? The Independent Title is on the line and much more tonight! So let's take you down to ringside for the start of our opening match here in Houston, Texas! Gene Cole: The following match is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, being accompanied to the ring by his pet rat, Mojo! From New York City.... Sewer Savage! *Savage stops halfway down the aisle and turns to the entranceway, mic in hand. "Ladies and Gentlemen, may I introduce to you... Mr. Orange!" A man in an orange suit and mask makes his way into the arena and joins Savage at ringside* Gene Cole: And his opponent... From Bakersfield, California... Ice Chamber! The fans threw ice cubes at Ice Chamber as he made his entrace into the arena. The bell rang as Savage and Chamber locked up. Chamber manuevered Savage into a headlock before he was pushed into the ropes and back into an elbow smash by Savage. The elbow smash made Chamber stumble back as Savage connected with a running clothesline over the top rope onto the outside. The fans jumped to their feet when an unfamiliar person ran out from the audience, steel chair in hand. He sneaked up to Ice Chamber and bashed him across the shoulder sending him down. He then tossed Chamber into the ring and returned to the crowd. Savage brought Chamber to his feet and delivered the Plung on him! The referee stopped the match due to the seriousness of Chamber's condition and awarded the TKO victory to Sewer Savage. Savage looked down at Ice Chamber and laughed as Mr. Orange entered the ring and began kicking at the body of Chamber. Officials had to be brought out to pull Orange off of Chamber as medical staff rushed the ring. Ice Chamber suffered a bruised shoulder and will miss one week's worth of action. "The Avenger" Tim Inman versus "Hollywood" Bob Burner Burner entered the arena first as he showcased his new movie on the CCWTron. The fans cheered at the sight of the Hollywood flick. Tim Inman made his way to the ring as the fans threw sodas and hot dogs at him. Burner bedazzled Inman with an array of agile moves in the early going. Burner climbed the top rope and performed an amazing moonsault off the top rope but was unsuccessfully landed as Inman moved out of the way. He brought Burner to his feet and there it was! The Great American Piledriver! 1-2-3!!! Tim Inman has defeated Bob Burner! Unbelieveable feat pulled off by Tim Inman here tonight at Heatstroke! Inman rolled out of the ring and made his way out of the arena as Burner sat in mid-ring looking disappointed. Let's take you now to our colleague Gene Cole who is standing by with "Supersexy" Shawn Storm. Cole: Shawn thanks for joining us tonight at Heatstroke 99. Shawn: Well Gene, I wouldn't miss this event for the world. This is just great wrestling action! Cole: I have heard things in the locker room. Shawn: What have you heard, Gene? Cole: Many wrestlers have been critizing you for your lack of respect for the sport of wrestling. Shawn: That's absolutely untrue, wrestling is my life. I don't know where I would be without it. I love this sport more than anything else in the world. Not only do I love the sport but I also love the great fans. From now on I will do what the fans want cause they are the ones that make this sport so great! Cole: Of course, everyone knows you have Bad Boy Brigens coming up but who do you plan on challenging after that match up? Shawn: Like I said, I'm gonna do what the fans want so I will let them choose my next opponent! Ok fans, who do all of you great people wanna see me wrestle? **Fans start screaming many names but the name most clearly heard is Thunder!** Shawn: Well Thunder, you heard the fans. Me and you Thunder, then the res of your Franchise buddies are gonna get beat! Cole: Thank you for your time, Shawn. We will all be waiting for Thunders reply. Up next, Joquar versus "Fat Ass" Phil Burner for the CCW Hardcore Championship! Joquar enters the arena first as rockets fire from the entranceway into the rafters as the fans go crazed just at the sight of the fireworks. Phil Burner enters next as the fans cheer for the big man and his Hardcore Title. Phil rolls into the ring as Joquar meets him with boots to the side. He sends Fat Ass off the ropes and meets him with a back body drop which is blocked by Fat Ass. Phil takes Joquar up and into a Pearl River Plunge for the 1-2- no! Burner sends Joquar to the ropes and follows closely with a shoulder butt sending Joquar through the ropes onto the arena floor. Phil walks over to the corner and down the steel steps. Joquar makes his way to his feet and grabs a broom from under the ring and nails Phil with it in his mid-section several times. This does not phase Burner as he clobbered Joquar with his Hardcore Title. Phil looks under the ring for something to smash Joquar with but has a hard time finding anything of power. He walks to the other side as Joquar comes to. He takes a chair and nails Phil from behind, sending the bohemoth down to the ground. Joquar gets next to Phil and begins to push him underneath the ring! He seems to have trouble budging Burner who is pushed up against the ring. Apparently he was too big to fit under the ring. Just then Bob Burner makes his way into the arena dawning a leather jacket, tattoos and long hair. Can we say... chi... chi... chi... chia! Bob was accompanied by a small petite woman. The woman distracts the referee as Bob and Joquar team together and give Burner a beat down. The two are able to hoist Phil up into the air for a devastating suplex across a table! Bob pulls out another table from behind the announcer's desk and sets Phil ontop of it as Joquar climbed the top rope and launched himself through the air onto the outside and through the table, and Phil Burner. Joquar was not hurt as he and Bob slid Phil back into the ring. Bob and the unknown woman make their way back to the locker room when Tim Inman enters the arena. Inman stopped Bob and demanded that he let the woman go. Burner whipped out a nailgun from his pocket and nailed Inman in the arm, making him bleed profusely. Inman crawled in pain to the ring and slipped Phil a staple gun. As Joquar went upstairs again, Phil stapled Joquar in the ass on his way down, sending Joquar into the mat harshly! Phil went for the pin, 1-2-NO! Joquar quickly kicked out and got to his feet, holding his ass in pain. Phil charged Joquar but he moved out of the way and Phil went running into the turnbuckle as Joquar got behind Phil and rolled him up... 1-2-3!!! Here is your winner! And NEW CCW Hardcore Champion, Joquar! Phil laid in the ring like a beached whale as Joquar climbed to the floor and met Franchise members halfway up the aisle with his brand new Hardcore Title. Supreme Machine versus Mr. Gobstopper in the COTR Final Match! Mr. Gobstopper entered the arena first as he began tossing Jolly Ranchers to the 23,579 people on hand here at Heatstroke 99 in Houston. SuMa made his return to the ring as he eyed Gobstopper the whole way down the aisle. The match began and the two stared at each other from across the ring. Gobstopper reached into his pocket and pulled out some Jolly Ranchers which he tossed at SuMa. SuMa flew through the air and nailed Gobstopper with a clothesline. Gobbber was down as SuMa dropped an elbow before placing him in an STF. He released the hold shortly thereafter and slammed him. SuMa went to the second rope and dropped a fist across the face of Gobstopper. He tossed Gobber into the ropes and back into the Decapitator! 1-2-3! The Supreme Machine defeated Mr. Gobstopper once again and claimed victory in the Champion Of The Ring Tournament! He left the arena as the fans booed him. Gill Bates entered the arena to congratulate SuMa and to welcome him back to the CCW. Gobstopper sat in the ring with his head between his knees, thinking hardly, and not believing what happened. SuMa must now choose which of the five prizes he'd rather have. Mash defends his Independent Title against Sam Frederick. The lights tinted green as Sam Frederick entered the arena. With his was the follower and Ben Frederick. Sam slid into the ring as The Follower and Ben split up and guarded each side of the ring. Mash entered next as the fans booed his presence. He walked down the aisle showing off his title to the fans. The match began as Mash pummeled away on Frederick. He tossed Sam into the turnbuckle and then placed him on the top rope. Mash went up and performed a face buster from the top rope on Frederick. Mash got up and went to signal to the fans but smacked the referee in the head by accident sending him down. Mash then took the opportunity to do what he does best, cheat! He removed the covering from the turnbuckle as Sam came to his feet. Mash took Sam and lifted him high above his head and plowed him to the mat. The vibration woke the referee who opened his eyes just in time to see Mash slingshot Sam into the exposed turnbuckle. The ref slowly got up and called for the bell as Mash did the Brain Masher on Sam. Frederick wins the match by disqualification, but Mash keeps the title. Mash was angry and began beating on Frederick as the Follower and Ben Frederick entered the ring and chased him off. Doomsday versus The Jolly Rancher for the TA title. The Jolly Rancher entered the arena as he tossed Everlasting Gobstoppers to the crowd. Doomsday came out next with his TA title and with Sid Powers, who was there showing his support. The bell rang and Doomsday dropkicked JR immediately. JR went down but soon got back up as Doomsday grabbed him by the arm and sent him into the ropes and back into a back body drop. Sid called Doomsday over and began giving tips to Doomsday as JR made it back to his feet. He clocked Doomsday with a roundhouse and sent him into the corner for a flying elbow smash. The lights go out. All that is seen is a spotlight shining on a masked man who walks out with his head looking down. He slides into the ring as the spotlight goes out. The lights slowly come back on as we see the Masked Man running back through the entranceway and The Jolly Rancher is out cold in the middle of the ring. Doomsday place his foot across JR's chest for the 1-2-3! Doomsday retained his TA title as he and Sid Powers left the arena taunting JR. JR made it back to his feet as Doomsday and Powers reached the locker room area. Then chased after them. Apocalypse versus Pepper The Cat for the Heavyweight Title. Pepper The Cat entered the arena with Lady Cat and Bunny, once again, in army clothes and water guns. Apocalypse entered in rare form, without his brother, but a nice shiny Heavyweight title drapped over his shoulder. The match started and Pepper was met with a hurricarana from the second turnbuckle by Apoc. Apoc was ready from the entrance to take on Pepper. He did a snapmare takedown on Pepper who didn't have the fight in him. Apoc immediately applied the Mandible Claw on Pepper who eventually submitted. Apocalypse, although rather quiet, grabbed his title and left the arena with no word. Pepper The Cat was helped by Bunny and Lady Cat back into the dressing room. The I Quit Closed Cage Match - Samson versus Sid Powers - Loser Leaves CCW. Special guest announcer Michael Buffer was on hand to introduce this colossal main event. The following match is an I quit match! The only way to win is to get your opponent to say I quit! The results of saying it is that he quits CCW forever! This is surely the biggest match in CCW history! Are you ready?! He would shout, and the fans would reply with a Hell yeah! For the thousands in attendance, and the millions watching around the world, ladies and gentlemen. The crowd seemed to hang on words, then erupted with the man: .Lets get ready to rumble! Ladies and gentlemen, entering the ring at this time, standing six feet ten inches tall, weighing three-hundred and twenty pounds, a man so feared and so avoided by other wrestlers, hes widely known for his trash-talking ability and his lethal attacks on others, the master of the Flatliner, Sid Powers! The crowd cheered, more or less for the match itself. Now entering, weighing three hundred and two pounds standing six feet four inches tall, The Man of men. Hes known in CCW for being the strongest man in the league, and also for his no-nonsense style of wrestling. Accompanied by his girlfriend, CCW Womens Champion Delilah, the one, the ONLY, Samson! The crowd erupted with deafening force. Sid Powers stood in his corner of the ring as Samson stepped in between the ropes. Samson looked longingly and hopeful at Delilah, then embedded a deep kiss on her lips, as if this would be the last time they ever stood in a wrestling ring together again. Then he smiled at the fans, soaking up the atmosphere, just in case this would be the last time he ever would be able to receive such adoration. He gave his girlfriend a hug, and finally, turned to face his opponent. The referee stood between them as the cage was slowly lowered to the ring. The roof was solid steel and had barbed wire running across the bottom of it. It was held up by supports hanging from the arena ceiling. The two men waited, each as nervous as the other. The referee called for the bell, and the match began. The two circled each other several times before finally hooking it up. It looked to be a standstill but Sid Powers, unbelievably, walked Samson slowly to the corner. He hit him with punches and chops to the head and chest. Samson returned the favor and fought his way back, threw Powers in the other corner, and was about to do a splash on him but Powers moved out of the way. Powers then hit Samson with a massive clothesline! But Samson got right back up, and Powers jumped from the crowd reaction as Samson just stared at him. The two traded punches for a while, and Samson got Powers in a Dragon Arm Twist, but Powers reversed it. Then the fun started. The Franchise came out, surrounding the ring. But The Tribe ran in right after them! And a huge outside-the-ring brawl ensued! Mr. Gobstopper and Niamouchou hooked it up with the Lamens, and Joquar, Supreme Machine, and Thunder took on Kaboink, Apocalypse, and Pepper the Cat. Jim Klown Bowe fought off Doomsday and Lord Gracemore. It started off like this, but the outside got really messy after a while and it was hard to tell who was fighting who. Back in the ring, Samson was taking control, he picked Powers up for a Gorilla Press Slam, but instead of dropping him to the mat, he ran him into the steel cage! Then Samson kicked his jaw as Powers lay on the mat. Next Samson picked him back up for a Tombstone Piledriver! Powers lay on the mat, gasping for air, while Samson shouted obscenities at him and the referee checked on him. But Powers stood back up, unfortunately put back down on the mat with a Standing Sidekick from Samson. Powers managed to fight and claw his way back up, but Samson applied a rope choke, and the referee had to pull Samson off to make sure he didnt make Powers pass out. On the outside, the MOD had just joined the fun. Everyone was fighting everyone, and occasionally someone would hit somebody from his own stable, and it took a lot of control from those wrestlers to keep from battling each other and turn to the enemy. Samson signaled for Samsons Vengeance, and initiated the move. For those who dont know Samsons Vengeance is a Torture Rack. He jumped up and down with the tall Powers on his shoulders. The referee checked on Powers, who replied with a Hell no! Eat s--- and die Samson! Samson was getting tired from the big man on his back and dropped him to the mat, then leaned against the ropes to catch his breath. Just then someone from the outside threw something inside the ring at Sid Powers. It was small and it hit him in the face. Samson, not having seen it, methodically approached Powers, who knocked him out cold with a punch to the face! Fully armed with a pair of brass knuckles now, Powers, continued to punch at Samson. Blood oozed out of Samsons head and face. The referee could do nothing about it, as this was a no-disqualification match. Powers then initiated some moves. In sequence they are: Fall Away Slam, Powerbomb, Gutwrench Powerbomb, DDT, Swinging Neckbreaker, and a Spinning Heel Kick. All of which Samson slowly stood up after, only to be brought down by the next. By now Samson could barely get up, and Sid helped him, only this time way up in a Vertical Suplex! But something really bad happened! Samsons feet got tangled in the barbed wire at the top of the cage! When Powers dropped him the barbed wire not only tore off from the ceiling but ripped into Sams! ons legs as well! Samson screamed and yelped in agony, and the referee pleaded with Samson to just say it, but it didnt happen. He hung there upside down, sort of. His back was touching the mat but his body was curved and his legs hung from the barbed wire. Powers got up and tore the barbed wire away from Samsons legs as blood had found a new way to exit his body. Many people from around the CCW joined in the brawl; it seemed every five minutes a few new people would show up. Of notice was The Avenger Tim Inman, who went left and right hitting people with a baseball bat. Powers got Samson into a hellacious Arm Twist. Say it! Say it or Ill break your f*cking arm! He yelled viciously. Samson just stood on his knees with that look of sheer pain on his face. What happened next was not pretty. Sid Powers actually did a massive elbow drop onto Samsons arm, breaking it at the elbow! Samson lay curled up in a fetal position, writhing from the pain! The crowd went nuts and a few women even started crying. Say it! Say it! Powers now dripped foam from his mouth. Chants of Say it! Say it! came from the crowd, who felt so badly for Samson and would rather have him quit then go through the torture. But all Samson could do was lay curled up in the middle of the ring. He tried to get up on his hands and knees, but Powers gave him powerful kicks to his midsection, and Samson began coughing up blood, which was already everywhere in the ring. The chants got louder, and Larry Heenan offered a prayer for the man as he commentated at rings! ide. Just then CCW stand-in Commissioner Jim Steinbrecher came running down the aisle, and began to talk to the referee. They were obviously discussing whether or not they should throw the match and hurry Samson to the paramedics. The referee nodded, but turned around and got knocked out cold by Sid Powers! Now there was no one in the ring to stop the man from killing Samson, and no one could get in! Commissioner Steinbrecher just stood in horror, and he yelled at the Event Staff to do something, but they had no wire cutters, so they went backstage to go find some equipment. Meanwhile the brawl had turned into half brawl as the participants now more or less were watching the carnage take place. Fellow Tribe members begged him to say the words, and even Delilah pleaded her case. The chants grew louder. Just then Fat Ass Phil Burner ran to ringside, shovel in hand, taking out all in his way. He tapped the cage where Sid was standing, and Sid was obviously too close to the cage wall as he apparently got knocked down by the shovel! Samson crawled over to Burner, blood oozing from his face, mouth, and it seemed pores. He said something to Burner, who nodded his head. Samson lay face down on the mat as Burner ran off. Meanwhile Commissioner Steinbrecher talked encouragement to Samson, who seemed unconscious. Sid Powers got to his feet, shook his head, then gave a devilishly evil grin as he grabbed Samson by the foot and pulled him to the center of the ring. He went for an elbow drop on Samson, but Samson rolled over his broken arm and out of the way! The yelp from Samson was so loud it actually somewhat quieted the arena for a second, and the chants came back in a stronger force. Powers tried another elbow drop, but again Samson painfully rolled away! Then Powers stood leering over Samson, and tried to kick at his face, but Samson slithered and dodged the kicks! Powers aimed a kick directly at Samsons chest, but Samson grabbed his leg in mid air and spun Powers around to the mat! Samson hobbled up to his feet, cradling his arm. Powers stood up and glared at Samson in disbelief, and Samson returned his stare with a glazed over look of mixed determination and pain. Just then the whole cage shook! As cameras zoomed to find the origin of the tremor, they found a man on the roof. It was Fat Ass Phil Burner! With a blowtorch in his hand! He went to each corner of the cage, cutting away at each of the supports holding up the one-on roof! Each support snapped, and after the last one, Phil held the blowtorch for all to see. But he evidently took a wrong step because the roof shifted and Burner lost his balance, and fell off of the cage! Worse yet, he fell on Commissioner Steinbrecher! The two lay unconscious on the floor below as a few wrestlers came to their aid. Sid and Samson, unaware of what was going on above, looked at the ceiling as it moved eerily. Sid stared at it with a puzzled look. He saw Phil Burner fall to the ground, and gave a hearty laugh. He turned around to be met by a knockout punch by Samson! But Sid retaliated, and he bounced Samson off of the ropes, and they leveled each other with running lariots! Sid who was close to the turnbuckle, hit his head on the bottom one and lay there like it was a pillow. Samson slowly crawled his way up. He went to each wall of the cage and shook it, causing them to buckle under the weight of the roof above. He loosened the cage posts with all of his remaining strength, using all of his might with that one arm! Comment from Larry Heenan: Man woman and child hes tearing the place apart! Hes going to commit suicide before giving in pain! Oh someone please get in there and stop him! The wrestlers at ringside backed away as the cage buckled from the stress. Inside Sid Powers had come to, and shockingly took notice of both his and Samsons impending doom. What the hell are you doing?! Youll kill us both! We shall see, was all Samson replied. To everyones surprise Samson painstakingly took Sid Powers up into Samsons Vengeance, just as the middle support from the top of the arena snapped, and the roof of the cage fell on top of them both, crushing the sides of the cage. For some reason or other the power went out and the last thing the audience saw was the roof falling full force, barbed wire and all, on top of Sid Powers, who was cushioning Samson. A few moments later the lights came back on, and the crowd was quiet. They listened for signs of life from inside the heap of rubble. It took the Event staff 15 minutes to get enough people positioned around the ring to where they could lift it up. A section of the audience had to be cleared back in order for the roof to be put somewhere. And everyone gasped at the sight. There in the middle of the ring, lay Samson, unconscious, hurting, and bloodied. A rib pertruding from his left side. And Sid Powers, who rolled off of Samson, clutching his abdomen in utter pain. Sid Powers was moving, but Samson was not. Medical Staff quickly entered into the ring as most of the CCW wrestlers filled the entranceway to see what horror was discovered. Samson was losing blood fast as the wrestlers were cleared from the aisle so that EMT's could bring in the ambulances. Samson was immediately placed into an ambulance and driven off as Sid Powers was placed onto a stretcher and also loaded into an ambulance and driven off. Tony Cloud, head CCW referee, was not the official in this match, but was watching the entire thing from backstage on a monitor. He made his way to the ringside area as the referee, Phil Burner, and Commissioner Steinbrecher were all taken away by ambulances. Cloud picked up a mic and came over the PA system and said, "Ladies and Gentlemen, it is my duty as Head Referee in CCW, to declare a winner in this horriffic match we have all just witnessed. Seeing as how after the smoke was cleared, Samson was not moving, but Powers was, I hereby declare the winner of this match, Sid Powers!" CCW President, Jonathan Stevens, soon made his way to the ringside area and asked to use the mic. Cloud handed over the mic as Stevens had this to say, "A tragic event has unfolded here tonight. Nothing is known of the injuries sustained by the two men and nothing is know as to their physical well-being. Mr. Cloud has announced that Sid Powers is the winner because he was the only one moving after the smoke cleared, that is not objectionable. In fact, I agree. However, this was an "I Quit Match" and neither individual said they quit. Samson had no choice but to go the way he did as the same for Powers." "I hereby declare Sid Powers as the official winner, but, neither individual will be forced to leave the CCW. Samson will remain here, as will Sid Powers. However, the two will be forced to remain out of action until their injuries heal. I will have details on the injuries Monday morning and predictions on how long each man will be out of action. After seeing such athletic ability, upon his return, I grant Sid Powers a Heavyweight Title match but not until Samson gets his Heavyweight Title match. Until their return to the ring, let our prayers be with them..." Everyone cleared the ringside area as the steel cage was cleaned up. Larry Heenan had this to say in closing... "Ladies and gentlemen I dont believe in all of my years of being associated with professional wrestling that Ive ever witnessed a sight like this. Samson was losing the battle fast. He was obviously suffering from broken bones and was bleeding severely on the inside. But the man was crazy. Rather than give up, he did what he knew would be the only way he could possibly win, he tore the cage down. With help of course from Phil Burner. The two are obviously still friends. And was there really a winner? Although Powers did say it, there was not a referee to declare it. The result will probably be determined by the CCW Board of Review, and will be placed on the comments board along with the status of each man injured tonight. This much is certain though, despite who the winner was. Both will be spending a lot of recovery time in the hospital. In conclusion I would just like to say that this was not a fun match to watch, but it definitely was the biggest. Goodnight ! to you all, and take care." CCW Broadcast CCW Home
