CCW news, 29 March to 11 April 2003

29 March to 11 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Coup D'etat?! Posted Friday, April 11, 2003 Lockdown has taken place and we've seen perhaps the most shocking piece of business guile has taken place. With a new leader "liberating" CCW from its oppressive reign of it's former ruler, what will this weeks Lockdown bring? Also on the program, Jack Manson and Adam Christ causing a Revolution of their own within the tag ranks. What was that particual Revolution? Who won last night? Click on the Latest Results link and find out already! Aftermath from reVolution takes place April 10th! Posted Thursday, April 3, 2003 CCW will be live in Tinseltown this Thursday as Lockdown comes to you live April 10th from the Staples Center in Los Angeles. The first match will consist of women losing there respected matches at the PPV as Lacey Skye Morris takes on Koriann. This is a cruical match in the battle to be a top contender for the Women's Championship. The next match will be a triple threat match between three men that need to prove themselves in CCW. Dan Logan who has taken the PTC world by storm has no had that same ability in CCW as he will try to earn his first win in CCW. He will take on Toxic and Damien Nhyte. Will Nhyte be able to rebound from the TLC match at the PPV? A re-match from reVolutions will take place at Lockdown as the CCW Hardcore Champion Engima will take on 1/2 of the CCW Tag Team Champions Jack Manson. The CCW Hardcore Championship will be on the line and this time Jack will go all out trying to defeat the Hardcore Legend. Will he be successful? Tune in and watch. Devyl will take on Chloe Mason in women's action. Will Devyl continue her winning ways or will Chloe derail them this Thursday? Our Main Event pits two of the participants in the TLC match as CCW Trans-Atlantic Champion Michael Stevens faces 1/2 of the CCW Tag Team Champions Adam Christ in a non-title match. Will Michael get revenge? Will Jack Manson interfere? Will Scorpion be there? This match will have much importance for both men. Also on Lockdown we expect to hear from the CCW World Hevayweight Champion Jason Dread. Will Scorpion be there after being hung at reVolutions? How will Blazye react to Jason Dread being Champion? All this and more as Lockdown airs this Thursday! reVolution is in the books! Posted Tuesday, April 1, 2003 CCW made it's return to PPV and it was once again a classic CCW PPV event! Title's changed hands, wrestlers were bloody and even hanged... To see all the happenings and excitement that surrounded reVolutions click the link below: http://ccw.wrestleonline.net/revolution.htm CCW Returns to Pay Per View! Posted Saturday, March 29, 2003 We are less then twenty four hours away from CCW making it's long awaited return to Pay Per View! CCW presents: reVolutions, it's first pay per view in over seven months! reVolutions will showcase a "Night of Champions" as every title in CCW will be up for grabs. reVolutions will be headlined by the long awaited match-up between CCW legend Blayze and rookie sensation Jason Dread with Blayze's CCW World Heavyweight Championship on the line. The Trans-Atlantic Championship will be reinstated as Michael Stevens, The Gobbestopper and Ace Hawk battle out in a three way dance to crown the new CCW Trans-Atlantic Champion. The CCW Women's Championship will be on the line as Lacey Skye Morris will face Sheena. Both women want the CCW Women's Championship really badly, that match-up will prove who wants it more. The CCW Independent Championship will be on the line in a strap match as the Independent Champion Scorpion will take on CCW newcomer Dan Logan. This matchup has been boiling over into a heated battle for sometime as Devyl will take on Gabe Nyte. This heated battle will come head to head at reVolutions! The next match has "Match of the Year" written all over it as the CCW Tag Team Championship will be up for grabs in a table, ladders and chairs match. As Adam Christ & Jack Manson will take on Scorpion & Michael Stevens. This match people will be talking about for years to come. Finally the male viewers of CCW will be treated with some much needed Women's action as Melody Widmark vs. Koriann in a MUD WRESTLING MATCH! Yes, a mud wrestling match. Thank you CCW Managagement! This match has written blood bath all over it as Engima, a legend in the Hardcore divsion in CCW will take on Jack Manson for the CCW Hardcore Championship. CCW returns to Pay Per View with reVolutions, live this Sunday! What surprises are in store this Sunday? Tune in and watch to find out!
