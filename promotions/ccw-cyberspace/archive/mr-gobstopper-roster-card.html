Mr. Gobstopper Real Name: Mike Smith Birthdate: Sept. 5, 1976 Hometown: Anaheim, CA Height: 6' 0' Weight: 234 lbs. Nicknames: Gobber Status: Neutral Manager(s): None Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Whiskey In A Jar' by Metallica Wrestling Style: Technical Signature Moves: None Set Up Move: Return of the Smack (Slingshot into corner followed by a Gob Drop as the opponent comes out of the corner dazed. ) Finisher: Gob Drop (A modified Pedigree. ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Independent Champion (4), Heavyweight Champion (1), Tag Team Champion (2) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: None Other Federation Awards: None Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Mr. Gobstopper was one of the original grapplers in the early days of CCW. Lasting for two years he won the Heavyweight Championship from Brad Lamen only to lose it to Tim Inman. He also held the Independent Championship on numerous occassions including one reign as the comical Animator. Forming a tag team with his partner, The Jolly Rancher, they won the tag team titles on two occassions with their team of Hard Candy. One other great accomplishment of Mr. Gobstopper was making it to the final round of the inaugural COTR tournament where he was honored to face The Supreme Machine.
