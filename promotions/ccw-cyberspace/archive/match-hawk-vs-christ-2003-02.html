CCW match: Andrew Hawk vs. Adam Christ

13 February 2003

Andrew Hawk vs. Adam Christ Date: 02/13/03 Show: Lockdown 'Everything I Touch' by Stabbing Westward hits the system as Adam Christ comes from the back of the arena. He has a slight limp from the earlier encounter, but this doesnt stop him from stalking to the ring. He stares intently at the ring as he rolls under the bottom rope and paces around the ring when suddenly he snaps to attention as Born to be wild rings throughout the arena as Ace is met on the ramp by a shower of boos from the crowd. He rushes the ring and slides in as the ref calls for the bell. Adam Christ and Ace circle each other, staring straight into the eyes of their opponent. Neither blink or flinch or give any hint of their intentions. Suddenly Ace lunges forward and Adam ducks to get out of the way. Ace finds him on the top rope at about the halfway point between each corner. Meanwhile Adam charges up a nearby turnbuckle and springs off at back at his opponent with a spring board like Missile dropkick, a very impressive move indeed. However Ace being a very wise High Flier himself swiftly avoids by diving to the ground in the direction of the on coming attack leaving Adam to fly by overhead. BJS: That was the biggest Newbie move I have ever seen! JD: The small and short pop that Adam got while pulling off that move would suggest otherwise! In the ring Adam is back up to his feet but is soon cut back down to size with a thunderous Clothesline. Adam however jumps straight back up not showing any signs of pain at all. BJS: ** In a very uninterested voice ** Looks like some one doesnt want to sell! JD: No way Jack, I think he really didnt even feel that attack! I think someone needs to go in there and tell him he was just decked by one of the most physically dominating men in the sport today! Ace is completely shocked his attack had zero effect on his opponent, who quite seriously looks like he just came fresh out of High School. Ace exams his hand and forearm, and wonders why his attack didnt even wind Adam. Adam hits the ropes, trying to surprise Ace, he comes back and hits a Flying Cross Body Splash. However Ace has other plans and catches CCWs New Recruit and drops him down into a Backbreaker. Ace applies some heavy pressure before releasing the hold. Ace gets up and starts taunting the crowd. A see of boos and jeers ring through out the Arena. Little does Ace know but his opponent is back on his feet and staring a hole straight through Aces back. A plastic Coke bottle comes flying through the air and hits Ace square in the forehead, making the CCW veteran turn around to rub his booboo. Adam Christ moves closer to Andrew Hawk Superkick! Ace is stunned and leans back into the turnbuckle. The crowd pops major time. BJS: Get him Ace! Dont take that crap for a minor, little cock jockey! JD: Ok Black Jack did you forget to take your medication? BJS: Shut it Doyle! A CCW Icon is having his ass handed to him by a Newbie Fool! It couldnt get any worst than this! JD: What if it was Brad Lamen getting his patooty carved up in there? BJS: **In a deadly serious tone** Dont even joke about something like that! JD: Psycho In the ring Ace is looking seriously mad as Adam Christ has back off to the opposite corner. Hawk moves towards Adam, but Adam looks a little uneasy about taking on a pissed Andrew Ace Hawk, and darts through the ropes to the ring apron and down to the arena floor. This just infuriates Ace more. But this Veteran does not give chase to the Smaller and faster Adam Christ. Oh no! He uses his head cuts him off with a baseball slide catching the unaware Adam Christ, who though Ace was in following him. Adam Christ goes flying into the Safety Barrier. This obviously hurts him but he insistently jumps back to his feet only, to be knocked back down again by from a Drop kick from the top rope. JD: That was ultra brutal! BJS: Word! But I guess it was gonna happen sooner or later so he may as well get it over with early in his career. JD: Hey, this is the Dub, I doubt thats the only time hes going to get hit like that! Ace not wanting to relinquish his attack gets to his feet as soon as he can. He pulls Adam Christ to his feet, lifts him high in the air and then drops him throat first across the Safety Barrier. Ace grabs him again and throws him head first into the Ring Steps. An evil glare in Aces eyes flickers as the crowd begins up a chant of Asshole. BJS: Shut up fools! Dont they know who this is!? This is Andrew Ace Fn Hawk!!! He is back and in charge! JD: This is Joey Doyle reporting that Black Jack Slater is in fact a sufferer of Bi Polar! BJS: No I was disappointed because I couldnt find my friend from last week giving the Puppies away to a good home But then I looked up to third row, Section H! JD: Should have known there would be an ulterior motive other than the poor start of Ace Hawk! Ace Hawk is going mental on this Rookie. He kneels beside him and just slams his fist into the face of Adam Christ over and over! He picks Adam up and rolls him into the ring, and dives in after him. Andrew Hawk drags Adam to the centre of the ring and applies a Figure Four Leg lock! They are in the centre of the ring and Adam is trying to find some way of escaping this hold. But its hopeless hes in the centre of the ring and towing or flipping Ace to stop the hold is impossible. The referee checks Adam Christ JD: Is Adam Christ insane he just pushed the referee away! Adam Christ has elevated his upper body and is staring straight into the face of Hawk. He lets out a wild scream. Adam even tries to take a swing at the face of Ace only to fall back to the mat. Andrew seeing this hold isnt affecting him much lets the hold go and quickly scuttles to his feet. Adam slowly gets to his feet trying not show his pain, but obviously slowed up from the stress of that hold. BJS: Jesus! Did this guy take one too many Paracedimol or what? Ace rushes at the unstable Adam Christ Superkick again! Adam Christ has countered this move just in time. He follows with a kick to the stomach Crucifx!!! JD: What a come back! BJS: Id say! And I usually dont like rookies! The crowd is going nuts! With chants of finish him. Adam Christ has a sadistic look on his face. He removes himself from the ring and approaches the time keeper. He pushes him off his chair and folds it up. Adam returns to the ring just as Ace has got back to his feet. Unfortunatley for Ace he wont be staying on them for long CRACK!!!! This match is over! Ace has one it by DQ. But Adam doesnt care and the crowd is popping hard. He hits Ace over and over. He comes some referees but they just bare the same fate. He returns his focus to Ace again. Suddenly Meet The Creeper By Rob Zombie hits and out storms Yama Tatsu with a crow bar. He runs down to the ring, where Adam Christ backs up and waits for him. Adam swings the chair, misses and is decked with the crow bar. Yama takes another couple of shots at him and then drops the crow bar to the floor as referees and backstage personnel flood the ring.
