Other Federation Awards: Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: All that is known of The Supreme Machine’s childhood is that he was sold by his starving parents to a mysterious man known only as The Master. The following decades were spent travelling the world and being trained by masters of many varying fighting techniques. Under The Master’s tutelage, the young Supreme Machine grew into a highly disciplined and highly dangerous fighting machine. After years of preparation, The Master decided in 1997 that it was finally time to unleash his creation, which he did with dramatic effect. Within a year, The Supreme Machine had taken the wrestling world by storm, winning numerous titles throughout the world. But that was not enough. The Supreme Machine needed tougher competition. The Master began searching the planet for an arena worthy of his charge. In late 1998 The Master found what he was looking for. The Supreme Machine debuted in CCW in September of 1998 and soon began to dominate the Heavyweight Title scene, winning the prestigious title on 5 occasions. All was going well until the tragic death of The Master from a heart attack suffered due to a cowardly attack by one of The Supreme Machine’s opponents during a match. Shattered at the loss of his mentor, The Supreme Machine continued in CCW for many months but there was something missing. Things weren’t the same. No longer able to continue in CCW, The Supreme Machine mysteriously turned his back on his career and disappeared from the public eye. Lacking a purpose in life, he wanders the globe desperately searching for some spark to re-ignite the fire that had been doused by the death of The Master.
