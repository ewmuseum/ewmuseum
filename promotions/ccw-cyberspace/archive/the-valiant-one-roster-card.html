The Valiant One Real Name: [name withheld] Birthdate: March 21, 1980 Hometown: St. Louis, MO Height: 6'8' Weight: 292 lbs. Nicknames: TVO, Joey, etc... Status: Face Manager(s): Dirk Current Teams: New Strangers(stable) Entrance Music: 'Breakout' by The Foo Fighters Wrestling Style: Technical/Powerhouse/Hardcore Signature Moves: German suplex, Hanging DDT, Piledriver, Sharpshooter, Boston Crab, Full Nelson, Powerslam, Wheelbarrow Suplex, Tilt-A-Whirl Backbreaker, Big Boot, Gorilla Press Slam, DDT, Cradle Piledriver... Set Up Move: The Valiant Drop (The Valiant One irish whips his opponent to the ropes and when the opponent gets back he throws him high into the air as the guy falls face first onto the mat. ) Finisher: The Valiant Bomb (Jacknife powerbomb ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: 2x Hardcore Champion, 3X Tag Team Champion, 1X Independent Champion, 2X Trans Atlantic Champion Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: 2001 COTR Participant Other Federations: GCW Other Federation Awards: GCW Hall of Fame,GCW World Champion, GCW International Champion, GCW European Champion, GCW Hardcore Champion Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] The Valiant One grew up in St. Louis. He never even knew of wrestling until he went to a small show that involved Andre The Giant and Harley Race. After that he really got involved in seeing the wrestling world. He eventually went to Killer Kowalski's training school. He graduated from there and was signed to a contract by Global Championship Wrestling where he wrestled as Valiant Joey. He had a good run there as he became the GCW World Champion and made it into the GCW Hall of Fame. After GCW closed down Joey was in search of a place to go. He then discovered Fedwars and noticed Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. In February 2001 CCW signed The Valiant One to a long term contract. TVO came in and became one of the top new stars. He was involved in the formation of The New Strangers, also in February of 2001. After a couple months The New Strangers were a huge impact on CCW. Soon after, The Valiant One became the Hardcore Champion and held onto the title for a nice reign, when no one thought he should have been in the hardcore scene. He proved many people wrong with that reign. Blayze and The Valiant One formed a tag team in the New Strangers. They won the tag team championship on two occasions, becoming the top tag team at the time that had huge matches with the other great teams. Later on The Valiant One became a tag team champion for the 3rd time after he and Fenix won the vacant tag team titles. After Blayze had turned his back on the New Strangers he became a heated heel, and a long time enemy of The Valiant One. The two had many big time matches, almost costing both their careers all the time. Including one of the most hardcore matches of all time. The Valiant One ending up winning a vacant Independent Championship by defeating Blayze. Soon there after The Valiant One went on quite a losing streak. He ended up taking a couple weeks off to regroup. When he returned everyone thought he could not cut it anymore. He ended up getting into it with current Trans Atlantic Champion Brad Lamen. After a series of matches, The Valiant One ended up winning the Trans Atlantic Championship, then lost it back to Lamen after awhile. The Valiant One and Brad Lamen began to get into a heated rivalry. Brad Lamen ended up scarring The Valiant One for life when he threw acid into The Valiant One's face. Brad Lamen walked away the new champ, while TVO walked away having to wear a mask for the rest of life. The Valiant One returned with a vengence and a blue mask. He got his revenge on Brad Lamen for putting him out of action during his one year anniversary. The Valiant One has acquired a new trainer/manager in a man only known as Dirk. Dirk is dark and mysterious and teaching TVO many different types of fighting.....making The Valiant One ready for anything...
