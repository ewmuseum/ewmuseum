Lacey Skye Morris vs. Brad Lamen Date: February 27th, 2003 Show: Lockdown JD: And now it says we’re scheduled for a “Lamen’s Rules” matchup here between your favorite… BJ: BRAD FUCKIN LAMEN!!!! JD: And your favorite for a completely different reason… BJ: PUPPIES!!!! JD: …(sigh) Lacey Skye Morris… ***”Star of Tears” by Joanne Hogg begins to play over the PA as the stage ignites into a wave of pink pyro… With a confident strut Lacey Skye Morris makes her way out from the smoke as the fans rise to their feet… RA: Ladies and gentlemen, the following is a “Lamen’s Rules” match and is scheduled for one fall!! Introducing first from Stockton, California… LACEY SKY MORRIS!!! ***Lacey smiles for the crowd and gives the camera one of her killer winks as she bends over and under the ropes… She moves to the center of the ring and with a teasing motion makes like she is removing her top then rolls her eyes and tightens it back on… The crowd is mesmerized… JD: And will we see “The sexyiest move in sports entertainment” tonight? BJ: I sure hope so Joey, man I’d love to be the guy to put her over… JD: ANYWAYS… What the hell is this match all about anyways… I mean it seems like this is only happening to poor Lacey because she has gotten under the skin of management, I mean I wouldn’t want to be in the ring with Lamen, he’s the dirtyiest player in the book! And now you toss this poor girl in there? BJ: Hey Joey, Lacey is a whole lot more then just a pretty face…and she’s beaten men before for championships even… She’s a very fierce competitor and she doesn’t screw around… JD: That was a very insightful comment BJ… BJ: Yeah, and look at those tits dude! JD:… ignoring you… *** “Mean Machine” by The Cramps blares over the PA as the crowd breaks into a mad chorus of uproarious booes… Brad Lamen has a devious look on his face as he makes his way out of the entrance way with is hands in the air RA: And the opponent…. From Chicago, Illinois… He is the CCW Union Representative… BRAD FUCKING LAMEN!!!! *** Lamen is busily waving and smiling to the incensed crowd… JD: Jesus, these people hate this guy and he just revels in it… BJ: What are you talking about? What’s not to love??? He’s the kinda guy who’ll thumb u in the eye, hit you with a chair then hit on your wife, piss his name in the snow outside your house, all the while taking up your grandparents handicapped parking space and making jokes about your premature infant son… My kind of guy… JD:… This is… So frustrating… BJ: What? JD: While I guess were almost ready to go now… Lamen has the stick, maybe we’ll find out what the hell “Lamen’s rules” are… Lamen: Hey? Where the hell is Matthew Broderick??? Christopher Walken??? Where the fuck am I??? I didn’t come all the way to Buttfuck, Mississippi for nothing did I?? You bunch of nobodies are probably wondering what the hell a Lamen Rules match is… Well to be honest… It’s nothing!!! It’s a simple statement… LAMEN RULES!!! WHO BETTA THEN LAMEN!!???!!! CROWD: YOU SUCK DICK! YOU SUCK DICK! YOU SUCK DICK! JD: Those colorful Biloxi fans… BJ: Idiots! All Idiots! JD: And Lamen was supposed to make up rules for this match but I guess we’re not having any of that… Jesus now he’s taunting ppl. from the crowd and daring them to enter the ring, look he just spat on that elderly man and his grandson! Lamen: THAT’S FOR PEARL HARBOR!!! JD: That makes no sense… BJ: I LOVE THIS GUY! JD: Well the ref is calling for the bell now and Lacey Skye Morris rushes Lamen as he ducks out of the way quickly… Lamen now sizing up Lacey as he is mocking her now… They lock up and Lamen tosses Lacey backwards across the ring… Lamen laughing now… BJ: Well he does have strength on his side Joey…and he’s smart enough to use it to his advantage… JD: Lock up again and one more time Lacey is tossed on her back side!!! Lamen now stalking her as he moves in Lacey quickly to her feet… Lamen gets a hold of her hair and now has her locked up in a sleeper type hold… For goddsakes Lamen is fondling her now and making lewd gestures… OH!!! Lacey counters with a quick elbow to the guts of Lamen… Now quickly Lacey to the offensive, she gives Lamen a shot to the throat! Lamen is choking now! Lacey ties him up and WoW! Big Hurricanrana! Lamen is tossed to the opposite side of the ring, quickly up Lacey with and arm drag! Lamen up and Arm drag again by Lacey! Lamen is getting tossed around the ring! And there he is, quickly and cowardly sliding to the outside to regain his footing… BJ: Smartly moving to the outside… JD: Lamen trying to get back in the ring As Lacey can’t wait to get her hands on him! Lamen now calling for the ref to force Lacey back as so he can enter the ring… Lacey yelling now… Lacey: C’mon Bradley… I promise I don’t play to rough, I’m just starting to get hot… See? JD: Lamen is slow to get into the ring and Lacey well Lacey is grinding to the delight of the crowd… Now she’s… BJ: She’s got her hand down her tights!!! That’s fuckin’ hot! JD: And Lamen seems just as mesmerized as he turns and is yelling at the ref! Lamen: You believe this crazy bit… JD: OH SHIT!!! Lacey Skye just clocked Lamen with a pair of brass knuckles!!! The ref is calling for the bell but no wait… Lacey has a mic… Lacey: Well since I am obviously the prettier one here, and everyone loves to see what kind of tricks and treats I have up my stocking… (Lacey smiles as she tosses away her brass knucks) And as it seems to suit my opponent “the dirtyiest player in the book”…I think we are gonna make this… A LACEY RULES MATCHUP… That means Mr. Lamen… If you want to play with me… Then you’re gonna have to like it rough!!! JD: Oooo! And Lacey nails Lamen in the stomach with a quick kick as he rolls to the outside… Lacey though quickly to the offensive and WHOA!!! CROWD: CC DUB! CC DUB! CC DUB!! BJ: COOTCHIE-CANRANA!!! JD: Well it was a big flying Hurricanrana from the top rope to the arena floor!! Lamen is in trouble! Lacey has a hold of him now as she tosses him over the time keepers table… BJ: I wish she’d put me over a table… JD: And now OH NO! Lamen reached over the security rail and just tossed some guys beer into Lacey’s eyes!!! Lacey is blinded!!! Brad quickly scores with a missile dropkick off the table!!! Lacey is down and now as Brad tosses her back into the ring and begins to work her over with a few repeated kicks… Brad quickly on top for a cover!! HE’S GOT HIS FEET ON THE ROPES!!! 1 !!!!! 2 !!!!! Kickout!!! I can’t believe this guy… Lamen now looking frustrated, he thought this young lady would fold as soon as he mounted an offence… Lamen quickly now to the outside of the ring?? He is looking around and he’s got a hold of a security guard??? BJ: HANDCUFFS!!! JD: Are you kidding me?? It’s not enough that he is cheating to begin with, now he’s good lord he’s handcuffing Lacey’s hands behind her! She’s struggling on the ground now as he slides out of the ring again… Lamen yelling at the timekeeper… Lamen: GET THE FUCK OUT OF THE WAY! JD: Lamen gets hold of the time keepers chair and bell now!!! Lamen slide back in and Oh! Lacey rushes him and hits him with a shoulder in the gut… Lamen though quickly retaliates by driving that bell into Lacey’s back!!! BJ: Hey, she called Lacey rules… She brought this on herself!! JD: Lacey now struggling to gain her footing as Lamen has set up that chair in the middle of the ring… Good god! He just nailed her again with that bell!!! Now that the hell is he doing??? BJ: He’s showing off her puppies!!!! JD: He’s taking off her top??? What kind of despicable individual is this??? Now he’s got the poor girl handcuffed and half naked and look at him! He’s laughing as Lacey is struggling to beat the ever loving piss out of him!!! Lamen hits a clothes line now on Lacey and knocks her down to the canvas again as he laughs… Haven’t you done enough??? Haven’t you… What the hell he’s calling for the mic again??? Lamen: If there is one thing I enjoy, it’s tit’s and beer!!! And the fact of the matter is miss Lacey, there is nothing else that women are good for…. Either showing their tits or getting you beer!?! But hey you look a little parched so I am gonna give you a drink here… JD: Lamen is sliding out of the ring now and he’s jesus hes got a beer form someone in the crowd now he’s back in and dumping the contents on Lacey Skye… You have got to be kidding me what the hell kind of individual is this??? Oh no, oh no!!! Now he’s gonna hit The Lament on that standing steel chair!!! NO! NO! Don’t DO it!!! You’ll break her neck!! Lamen: TIME TO GET IT YOU FUCKIN C**T!!! JD: NO! Lacey reversed her way out of it!!! Lacey pushes Lamen towards the ropes and OH MY GOD!!! BJ: DROP TOEHOLD ON LAMEN!!! JD: Lamen goes face first through the seat of that chair!!! It’s warped to hell!!! The man’s jaw must be broken!!! And look at the flexibility of Lacey Skye!!! She’s ducking her wrists under her feet and now has her hands back in front of her still handcuffed though… BJ: Lamen is screaming! I think he’s gonna need his jaw wired after this!!! JD: Lacey is on fire!!! She’s got the bell!!! Now she’s placed it on top of Lamen’s crotch!!! Wait she’s calling for it!!! BJ: Handcuffed, nearly topless and covered in beer!!! But hell if we ain’t gonna see it!!! JD: Off both ropes and OH MY GOD!!! BJ: Don’t say it!! JD: BRAD LAMEN JUST HAD HIS “BELL” RUNG!!! PUPPIES ELBOW ON THE RING BELL ON TOP OF HIS CROTCH!!! LACEY FLOPS OVER!!! CROWD: 1!!!! 2!!!! 3!!!!! JD: IT’S OVA!!!!! Jesus I have seen some crazy shit in my time but damned if that wasn’t one of the gutsiest performances by a lady I have ever seen… Lacey gets up with a triumphant smile as she spits on the downed carcass of Brad Lamen before raising her handcuffed hands and saluting the crowd.
