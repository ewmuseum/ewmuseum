Show #115 - March 5, 2000

5 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Card and staged results script of the Shockin’ Sunday Night show of March 5, 2000 from the Staples Center in Los Angeles, ending in the Heavyweight Championship match.
March 5, 2000 from The Staples Center in Los Angeles, California - Show #115 "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. "The Avenger" Tim Inman - CCW Heavyweight Championship Jim "Klown" Bowe vs. Sid Powers - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Don "Ceasar" Capone vs. J.C. Superstar - CCW Lightweight Championship Candi Bytch vs. Kelly Bowe vs. Jaicee Davis - Triple Threat Match The Wish Master vs. Diablo Impact vs. Ricky Coyote Il Medello vs. Adam Pimp vs. Seven Steele - Triple Threat Match Blue Thunder vs. Hardcore Gorilla Mask Shockin Sunday Nite From The Staples Center in Los Angeles, California BLUE THUNDER VS. HARDCORE GORILLA MASK Welcome to yet another edition of Shockin Sunday Nite! I'm your host for the evening, Jeremiah Rossendale, filling in for the absent Brian Hill. Tonight's show features three title matches, two triple threat matches, and a few other undercard attractions. So let's waste no more time and get right down to the ring for the opening bell. ("Beethoven's Fifth Symphony plays over the PA as Blue Thunder makes his way to the ring with his bokken draped over his shoulder. The crowd boos loudly.) (HCGM makes his way to the ring when Sugar The Superior Slut comes up from behind and delivers a low blow. She rolls him into the ring and then grabs a mic and says "Sorry HCGM, but I'm out of MOD, see ya later!") Can you believe that cracker? Sugar has made her presence felt here tonight by giving the ol low blow to HCGM and now Blue Thunder has HCGM on his feet as the bell rings. Blue Thunder seems to have this idea that he threw HCGM out of the Battle Royal at Ground Zero so he automatically is the best damn thing since sliced bread, well hold on! Blue Thunder just sent HCGM over the top rope AGAIN! (HCGM holds on to the rope as Blue Thunder turns around and taunts the crowd.) HCGM sliding back in and Blue Thunder doesn't notice! He spins him around and plants him with a Double Arm DDT! Referee in position! (Ref counts?: 1, 2, 3) Here is your winner of our opening match... Hardcore Gorilla Mask! (HCGM slides out of the ring and makes his way out of the arena, but not before flashing his personal "little red love toy".) IL MEDELLO VS. SEVEN STEELE VS. ADAM PIMP - TRIPLE THREAT MATCH (Johnny Lamen enters the ring, and he asks Spit, Brad, and Jennifer Aniston of "Friends" to join him. Here is what he says "Now, I know you two ladies both are pregnant, thanks in no small part to myself. What the entire world has been wondering is how Johnny is going to ratify and correct this situation. Well, after much thinking, I came up with this. You can both go fuck yourselves!!!!!" Brad and Johnny then kick the girl's in their bellies, and then DDT them. "All you girls are are pathetic cunts. I don't need you. take your asses to welfare." Brad and Johnny then leave, while the girls lay in the ring, holding their stomachs in extreme pain.) That was totally uncalled for! The Cretin Family has just beat the living piss out of Jennifer Aniston and Spit! Both those women are pregnant and this was a very dastardly act of humanity! (All three men enter the arena, one at a time, as the ref starts the match with the sound of the bell.) Well, that little fiassco involving the Cretins is over and our next match is underway. Medello slams Pimp as Steele comes from the top rope only to land on his back side in a missed attempt. Medello picks up Steele and tosses him out of the ring then returning to Adam Pimp and tossing him off the ropes into a powerslam! Here is the cover! ONE! TWO! THREE! Your winner, in an unbelievably short match... Il Medello! Certainly an amazing victory in such quick fashion for one member of Il Famiglia here tonight. But folks, Sundays are just not my day to do results so most of the undercard matches will be short due to time constraints and lack of internet use in the evenings. IMPACT VS. RICKY COYOTE (Ricky Coyote waits in the ring as Impact makes his entrance.) Here comes Impact running to the ring, he's got an aluminum baseball bat wrapped in barbed wire and has just taken out the referee! Now he's nailed the on-coming Coyote and is dragging him out of the ring and up the aisle! Where is he taking him!? (Cameras show Impact dumping Ricky Coyote in a dumpster outside of the parking garage, then returning to the ring.) Impact back in the ring now and is telling the ref to raise his arm in victory by count out but the referee isn't complying. Referee Terry Johnson is speaking to the Ring Announcer... RA: I have just been informed by the referee that the winner of this match, as a result of a Disqualification... RICKY COYOTE! What a turn of events! Impact has gotten himself DQ'd by that new rule that is being enforced here in the CCW saying referees aren't allowed to be purposely taken out or a DQ will result! What a wild conclusion! Stay tuned! THE WISH MASTER VS. DIABLO Wish Master is in the ring and grabs a mic... Wish Master: Stop the music..... (After "Creeper" stops playing, and MOD's theme music starts playing over the PA system, and the MOD come out) Wish Master: Diablo...You will never be in the MOD again, and just to make sure, the MOD will keep any interference out of the ring, so I can DESTROY you...Im not going to wait for the bell to end the fight, OH NO, you see....you will learn not to try me again... (He throws the mic, and waits for Diablo) (Diablo comes out to his music.) Here comes Diablo and the bell sounds! These two animals are going at it! Wish Master with a huge left hook sends Diablo to the mat but wait one second! Here comes Ghettodogg and he's got a steel chair! He's in the ring but members of the MOD pull him out of it and begin to beat him down, taking that steel chair to Ghettodogg as the Wish Master does the Last Wish on Diablo and the ref counts... ONE! TWO! THREE! Your winner by pinfall! The Wish Master!!! CANDI BYTCH VS. KELLY BOWE VS. JAICEE DAVIS - TRIPLE THREAT MATCH (Black out over most of the viewing area... "Please stand by") (SSN pops back on the screen) Kelly Bowe putting Jaicee Davis in the Texas Cloverleaf, what an interesting women's match we've just had and it looks like Jaicee is tapping out! YES! Your winner by submission... Kelly Bowe!!! DON "CEASAR" CAPONE VS. J.C. SUPERSTAR - CCW LIGHTWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP (The Superstar's black Lincoln Navigator stretch limo speeds into the garage and comes to a screeching halt. All the members of the Franchise jump out. JC spots the Don alone smoking on a cigar with his back facing him. JC leads the assault with his KJV Special Edition Bible and wooden cross. He smashes the cross over the Don's unsuspecting head and then all of the members proceed to beat the piss outta him. JC wishes him "Good luck tonite....that was only the beginning!!!") JC Superstar is making his way to the ring for this Lightweight Title match and is being accompanied by The CCW Indepedent Champion, Bobby Blades! And now here comes your CCW Lightweight Champion, The DON! There's the bell and JC going to work on the Don who is a bit groggy from that backstage attack inflicted by Superstar. The Don, on his feet now, hits JC with a spinning elbow smash and here comes the other members of IF! Il Medello and Savanah Hilton are making their way to the ring but Bobby Blades stops them in their tracks with his hockey stick! Meanwhile, in the ring, Jc being distracted at the arrival of IF and Don rolls him up from behind for a pin attempt... ONE! TWO! THREE! Your winner, and still CCW Lightweight Champion... The Don!!!!! JIM "KLOWN" BOWE VS. SID POWERS - CCW TRANS-ATLANTIC CHAMPIONSHIP (Cameras come on back stage, Rushmore comes out of a Janitors closet as Jim Bowe passes and wraps a rope around his throat. Rushmore chokes Jim down until he's unconcious, then a Mt. More Bomb through a near by table!......and then the Face Lift onto the cement floor! Rushmore takes out a pair of handcuffs and locks Jim's hands behind his back and leaves him there!) Sid Powers in the ring now as Jim Bowe makes his way into the arean. There's the bell and this one is underway. Powers is taking it to Bowe now with a series of right hands. Whips him to the ropes and back body drop sends Klown down with a thump and now he's holding his lower back in pain. Powers brings him to his feet but oh lookout! Impact out of no where just slammed Powers with a branding iron. He's burnt "the mark of Impact" and a mushroom cloud on the back side of Sid Powers! He's flipping out! Klown up now, Powers still running around like a headless chicken, stops Powers in his tracks with the Klowning! He's up and down! ONE! TWO! THREE!!! Still your CCW Trans-Atlantic Champion... Jim "Klown" BOWE!!!!!! "FAT ASS" PHIL BURNER VS. "THE AVENGER" TIM INMAN - CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP Folks, this is our last match of the night and it will pit Phil Burner up against Tim Inman in a No-Interference match for the Heavyweight Championship! ("No-Leaf Clover" by Metallica plays as Phil Burner makes his way into the arena where Tim Inman awaits.) The fans are going absolutely nuts here tonight but I smell a foul odor in Inman land! There's the bell and Inman ducks a missed clothesline by Fat Ass who turns to be nailed in the jaw by a dropkick from Inman. That old man sure has air! Both of these men have had a history against each otherand both have had their share of fame, but this is the match they have long awaited, the rematch from 1999 for the Heavyweight Title now as Inman sends Burner off the ropes and hits him with a flying body press but Fat Ass catches Inman and slams him with a fall forward slam! (A familiar face comes running out from the crowd with a crowbar in hand.) Oh my GOD! That's El Mierda! That's El Mierda! Former CCW Lightweight Champ and he's slammed Burner with that crowbar! What is he doing here!? He's banned from CCW! The referee is in shock himself and doesn't know what to do! The rules state that there is to be no interference, but that only implies to those who are officially in CCW! Mierda hasn't been on the roster or affiliated with any roster member in weeks! No this can't be! The ref is making the count as Inman slowly covers Burner! ONE!!!! NO!!!! TWO!!!! NO!!!! THREE!!!!!! OH damn this Man! El Mierda has just helped Tim Inman become a three time CCW Heavyweight Champion!!!! (Avengaliciamaniacs' theme hits as a celebration ensues in the ring between Mierda and Inman. As soon as they begin to exit the ring, the music is cut off and the "No Chance In Hell" theme is played as President Stevens steps out into the arena.) Stevens: El Mierda.... Tim Inman... It seems we have a problem. You see, El Mierda was BANNED from CCW many many weeks ago. Perhaps you don't understand English my international friend, you see, it's spelled B-A-N-N-E-D. BANNED. That means you are lawfully not allowed to be here so Mierda, you are hereby required to RESIGN from CCW affective immediately. I will be waiting for your response, but for now, since you violated US law, get him boys. (20 LAPD in full riot gear enter the arena and join together to bring El Mierda to his knees and handcuffing him, then carrying him out of the building, probably to a jail cell. Inman and Stevens remain in the arena.) Inman, you won that title and you are the champion. BUT... the way you won it is controversial and the referee didn't know what the Hell he was suppose to do. The right thing to do would be to throw the match out, but since the referee had made his decision you are the champ. HOWEVER, don't think you will have this win and not have to do it again, because next Sunday you will face "Fat Ass" Phil Burner for the CCW Heavyweight Championship inside of a CLOSED CAGE! We'll see how you do as the champ, we'll see if your old and frail body will be able to accomplish such a feat next Sunday as it accomplished tonight. So good luck buddy! ("No Chance In Hell" begins playing as the Pres leaves the arena followed by Inman as the show goes off the air.) CCW Home
