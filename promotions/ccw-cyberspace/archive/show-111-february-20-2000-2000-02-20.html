Show #111 - February 20, 2000

What this is Script of Cyberspace Championship Wrestling show #111 staged on February 20, 2000 from Kansas City, Missouri, with announcer commentary, matches and backstage angles.
February 20, 2000 from The Kemper Arena in Kansas City, Missouri - Show #111 "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Ghettodogg - CCW Heavyweight Championship The Manics vs. The Titans - CCW Tag Team Championship Kelly Bowe vs. Sugar "The Superior Slut" - CCW Women's Championship Doomsday vs. Corey James vs. Samson - Triple Threat Match Lin Xio vs. Delilah Scotty The Hotty vs. Rushmore The Camargo Kid vs. Adam Pimp Jaicee Davis vs. Stephanie Black SHOCKIN SUNDAY NITE From The Kemper Arena in Kansas City, Missouri Brian Hill: We are live from the Kemper Arena in Kansas City, Missouri, and LET THERE BE PYRO! *Pyrotechnics shoot off around the stage, then up the sides of the entryway, and finally around the top. The crowd is going wild, and one notable sign says, "Shut up, Blue Thunder!"* Brian: These fans are very excited to be here, and well they should be! We've got a great lineup for you tonight. A Triple Threat Match, A Tag Team Title match, a Women's Title match, and that's not all! Two-time Hardcore Champion Ghettodogg will be challenging CCW Champion Phil Burner, who is himself a former Hardcore Champion! Without any further ado, let's cut right to the first match of the evening. Jaicee Davis vs. Stephanie RA: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the first match of the evening, and it is scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring at this time, Jaicee Davis! And now, her opponent, Stephanie! Brian: Well fans, you know I'm looking forward to this match. The bell rings, and this match is underway. They lock up, and Stephanie whips Jaicee into the ropes. There's a drop toe hold by Stephanie, and Jaicee goes down. Stephanie drops an elbow to the back of Jaicee! She goes for another, but Jaicee rolls out of the way. Stephanie's up, and Jaicee hits a hair drag takedown. She pulls her back up by the hair, and hits another one! Now she whips Stephanie into the corner. Jaicee runs up and goes for a monkey flip, but Stephanie shoves her off! Stephanie now, with a rear headlock, and she's really pulling back on the neck of Jaicee. Now Jaicee reaches up and boxes the ears of Stephanie! Ouch...that hurts. Now a leg trip by Jaicee, and she's dropping elbows to the inside of Stephanie's leg. Jaicee lets go, and Stephanie hobbles up to her feet. Jaicee takes Stephanie down with a clothesline! Now she picks her back up and sends Stephanie to the ropes. She goes for a dropkick, but Stephanie held onto the ropes, and Jaicee has nowhere to go but down! Stephanie over, and there's a snap suplex onto Jaicee Davis. A cover by Stephanie, ref counts...one, two, kickout. Now Stephanie pulls Jaicee up to her feet, and there's a scoop slam. Stephanie drops a leg across the face of Jaicee. Jaicee is up to her feet, and there's a reverse knife edge chop by Stephanie. And another one! Jaicee is in the corner now, and Stephanie is climbing the ropes. She's begun punching Jaicee in the head, but Jaicee shoves her off! Stephanie crashes to the mat, and Jaicee quickly climbs to the top rope. She jumps off and lands squarely on Stephanie, and she's going for the pin...one, two, kickout! That was a close one. Jaicee picking Stephanie up to her feet, but Stephanie fires off a punch to the stomach of Jaicee Davis. Now another one. Stephanie is back on her feet now, and she pulls Jaicee Davis down into a piledriver! Stephanie now, applying a single-leg Boston Crab. While it may only be applied to one leg, folks, it hurts that one leg twice as much. Jaicee makes it to the ropes, and Stephanie lets go after a four-count. Now Stephanie dropping elbows on the knee of Jaicee Davis! Stephanie smells blood, and she's going in for the kill. Jaicee is trying to get to her feet now, but Stephanie kicks that leg out from under her. Stephanie grabs the leg, and applies a figure-four! Jaicee is hurting...and now she's tapping out! RA: The winner of the match, by way of submission, Stephanie! Brian: Stephanie, continuing her rise in the Women's Division. Is the rookie really that good, or has it just been beginner's luck? We'll find out as time goes on. Stephanie and Jaicee are now walking up the aisle, and - ***The lights in the building go off and suddenly a strobe light begins to flash as a familiar "giggle" is heard over the PA... "Summertime In The Void" begins to play... Brian: No way... ***Pyro shoots off as "The Frozen Treat" Becky Butterscotch wearing a very tight sleeveless black vinyl bodysuit and dark sunglasses stands in the illuminated entrance way... The crowd gives a huge pop as Becky raises her mic to her sultry lips... Becky: (to the crowd in a shy voice) Oh gee... did you boys miss me? Well, I sure missed all the fans... I know you're all wondering where and what I have been doing for the last while... well the truth is that I wrecked my car and covered my tracks to get away from CCW for awhile... whoa... ***Jaicee Davis and Stephanie both rush Becky but she quickly counters with a superkick to Jaicee's jaw, and a snap kick to Stephanie who is then helped backstage by Jeff... Brian: Did you see that? Stephanie is being help to the back but Jaicee is out cold with that superkick from Becky! Becky: (Giggle Giggle) Just a second hun... I'm not done yet... I think I realized while I was gone, what it really takes to be a champion here in CCW... I finally realized that it being in it for fun, just doesn't always cut it... If you want to win... sometimes you have to be a little ruthless... ***Becky kicks Jaicee's crumpled body in the stomach... She then pulls out a can of whipped cream and writes "GET LICKED" on her back... Becky: That was for diddling with Bobby... Brian: I have never seen Becky act this cold before... maybe we're seeing a different side of the frozen treat.... Becky: So all you ladies in CCW... Step up to the plate... Cuz Becky ain't gonna be a good little girl no more... Oh by the way Brian, (Becky turns and bends slightly while looking over her shoulder seductively at the camera) What do you think hun? Am I still in game shape? ***With that Becky throws the mic down on top of Jaicee and walks out the entrance way... Brian: Ladies and gentlemen, what we just saw was amazing...I was hit on by Becky Butterscotch! Life is good! You've still got it, Becky! Camargo Kid vs. Adam Pimp RA: The following match is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring at this time, The Camargo Kid! And his opponent, Adam Pimp! *As Adam Pimp enters the ring, The Kid pulls out his black shock stick and zaps him before the match starts. He then drags him into the ring as the ref signals this one's underway* Brian: Whoa! The Kid with an attack before the bell. Adam Pimp is hurting, and the Kid is putting the boots to him. The Kid goes up to the top, and there's a senton backsplash onto Adam Pimp. I don't know why the Kid's not just going for the pin here. I think he's trying to make a point with Adam Pimp here. He sends Pimp to the corner, runs in, and hits a monkey flip! The Kid goes to the top rope again. He goes for a moonsault, but Adam Pimp rolls out of the way! I don't know how he did that. But he can't take advantage, and the referee is counting both men down. *Referee: one, two, three, four, five.* Brian: The Kid is up again now. He's over and stomps Adam Pimp a few times. He should just pin him and finish this match. The Kid goes up top and hits a Frog Splash! And now he's covering and the ref counts...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of pinfall, The Camargo Kid! Brian: Well, the Kid gets the win, but chalk up a huge assist to that shock stick attack at the beginning of the match. This could have been much different had it not been for that. And now, I'm getting word that something is happening backstage. Let's put it on the Jumbotron. *Rushmore spots Sylkk back stage and attacks him. He stomps on him a few times, throws him against a wall, and then leaves him for dead....after a Mt. more bomb on the pavement!* Brian: Whoa! An attack on Sylkk by Rushmore. Does he have a plan, or is this more random violence from Rushmore? Maybe we'll find out later. For now, we've got a match to attend to! Scotty the Hotty vs. Rushmore RA: The next match is set for one fall. Coming to the ring first, Rushmore! And now, his opponent, Scotty the Hotty! Brian: The bell rings, and here we go. Scotty goes to lock up, but Rushmore clotheslines him nearly out of his boots! He pulls Scotty up to his feet, and there's a gutwrench suplex! He gets up and starts stomping on Scotty! Scotty quickly rolls out of the ring to catch his breath, but Rushmore is following him out. He whips Scotty into the guardrail! That one hurt. And now he's going to try and slam his head into it, but Scotty elbows him in the stomach, and hits Rushmore's head into it instead! Scotty now, running to beat the count, and Rushmore's right after him. They both slide into the ring, and Scotty tries to go to work on Rushmore with a series of punches, but Rushmore blocks, and floors Scotty with a punch right to the side of the head! Rushmore pulls Scotty up, and hits him with a fallaway slam! He's going for the pin, and the ref counts...one, two, kickout. Rushmore's really been in control so far, hitting a lot of power moves on the lightweight champion. Rushmore sends Scotty to the ropes, and he takes him down with a big foot to the face! Scotty's getting up again, and again Rushmore sends him to the ropes, but now Scotty comes back with a crossbody! Scotty with a running legdrop onto Rushmore. And there's a standing moonsault! He sends Rushmore outside, and runs back against the far ropes. Scotty comes back, and there's a tope con hilo to Rushmore! Scotty the Hotty has turned this thing around all of a sudden. Scotty rolls Rushmore back in, and now Scotty's going up to the top. Rushmore up to his feet, and he runs over and punches the ascending Scotty right in the groin! Scotty crumples into the ring, and that had to hurt. Now the referee is calling for the bell! RA: Your winner, by disqualification, Scotty the Hotty! Brian: Well, Rushmore doesn't look at all happy with that, but he shouldn't have gone for the illegal move. I guess he decided risking a DQ was better than risking a pinfall. And the Lightweight Champion scores another win. Lin Xio vs. Delilah RA: The next match is set for one fall. Making her way to the ring, Delilah! And now, being accompanied to the ring by Jaicee Davis, Lin Xio! Brian: Lin Xio coming to the ring with Jaicee Davis, probably to prevent any interference. Lin Xio hasn't been wrestling much lately, so let's see if there's any ringrust here. They lock up, and there's an armdrag by Lin. Delilah is up in a hurry, but a dropkick by Lin takes her down. Lin lands right on her face, and now Delilah with an elbow drop to the back of Lin Xio. She pulls Lin up, and there's a bulldog by Delilah! Delilah pulls Lin up and sets her on her shoulder, and there's a shoulderbreaker on Lin! That had to hurt. Delilah picks Lin up for a vertical suplex, but Lin manages to turn it into a crossbody and goes for a pin! The ref is counting...one, kickout. I think Lin really caught Delilah off-guard with that move. Now Lin sends Delilah to the ropes, and there's a back body drop by Lin Xio! Lin pulls Delilah up to her feet, and there's a jawbreaker. Lin climbs to the top rope, and goes for a splash, but Delilah pulled her knees up! Lin is rolling around in pain! Oh man, I wouldn't want to be in her shoes right now. Delilah picks Lin up and scoop slams her back down. Delilah stomps on her a couple of times, and now Lin is struggling to her feet. Delilah grabs her, and there's a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Delilah goes for the pin, the ref counts...one, two, kickout! Both women have been very impressive in this match so far, and it's really anyone's match right now. She pulls Delilah up by the hair, and she knees Delilah right in the face! And again! Lin is getting vicious here. There's a double-leg takedown by Lin, and then an elbow to the stomach. Delilah gets up, and Lin goes for a clothesline, but it's ducked! And now an atomic drop by Delilah! She reaches around Lin's waist from behind, but there's a standing switch from Lin. And now Lin with the German suplex! She bridges it for the pin, and the ref counts...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of pinfall, Lin Xio! Brian: Well, certainly no ringrust on Lin tonight. Great match by both ladies. And there's still a lot more action to come. Coming to the ring first, Doomsday! And his first opponent, Corey James. And the final competitor in this match, Samson! *Samson comes out as the other two wait for the bell to sound. But Samson stops before he starts down the ramp and speaks into a mic.* "Doomsday........Corey James........ Beat his ass Doomsday!" *With that Samson walks out the way he came back in.* Brian: Well, Samson has decided to save his energy for the Tag Team Title match later, and that's not a bad idea. And now, we've got the Women's Title Match coming up!
