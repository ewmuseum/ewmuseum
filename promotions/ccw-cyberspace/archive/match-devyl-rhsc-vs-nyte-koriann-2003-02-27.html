CCW match: Devyl & RHSC vs. Gabriel Nyte & Koriann

27 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devyl & RHSC vs. Gabriel Nyte & Koriann Date: 02/27/03 Show: Lockdown “Here to stay erupts as the fans bust into a chorus of boos as Koriann appears first and screams at the top of her lungs “I RULE!” Gabe just shakes his head and proceeds to the ring. Before Korian can get to the ring, Devyl rushes her from behind but is cut of by a returning Gabe. Devyl takes a step backwards as Gabe smiles a sinister smile and follows Koriann into the ring. “Why can’t we be friends” sings out as the fans burst into cheers and applause as RHSC appears. He makes his way to the ring waving to the fans all the way. JD: Listen to this crowd...This feud between Gabriel Nyte and Devyl has been brewing since CCW version one, and finally, we are going to see them get it on! BJS: Hell yeah, These two have a lot of unfinished business JD: Both of them circling each other, sizing each other up....They look ready to lock up...AND........ BJS: I can't wait! JD: AND....They both tag out to their respective partners Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! BJS: What a rip off! JD: Well...Looks like the old bait and switch worked, we have Koriann and The Red Headed Step Child starting this match off now...Both Charge....OOH! BJS: TRAIN WRECK! JD: More like an accident with two idling scooters. Both are down, and now crawling to the ropes...Nyte tags himself in! BJS: Oh Lord...Guys in the back, get the body bag! JD: Gabriel rushes over to RHSC...He's got him and pulls him to the middle of the ring. He sets him up..possibly a powerbomb? KABOOM! BJS: Yup. JD: MY GOD! THE DEFILER~! RHSC is DEAD! BJS: Jeebus. JD: He Covers! VA: 1...2...Hey! The Match should be over! JD: HE LET HIM UP? BJS: More punishment in mind by The Chosen. JD: Nyte now...Setting RHSC again...and this time pointing to Devyl! Gabriel: THIS IS YOU, BITCH! BOOM~! JD: OBLITERATOR~! COVER! VA: 1...2...3! DINGDINGDING NW: The Winners of the match, The team of Gabriel Nyte and Koriann! [The Daemonette enters the ring at this time, and throws Koriann out of the ring! ] JD: Koriann did nothing in the match, and the Daemonette and that strong...sexual...tight... BJS: I've never seen you get Gaa-Gaa over the ladies before. JD: She's attractive. But, she's standing inbetween Devyl and her tag team partner... BJS: No she's not, Devyl's leaving the ring! JD: Nyte picks RHSC up again...Powerbomb? BJS: Look at his orientation! JD: OH SHIT! The announcers run away as Gabe Powerbombs RHSC from the ring to the Announce Table JD: CHRIST NYTE! YOU COULD HAVE KILLED US! BJS: But maybe he killed The Red One? JD: I’m not quite sure Jack. We need some EMT’s out her to tend to him. In the mean time we have some more backstage action going on. The scene shifts back to the white limo as the driver is standing beside the back door. He opens the door as a set of black gators hit the ground, the words “It’s time” are heard before the camera fades to static. JD: What the fuck? WHO IS IT? BJS: Aww fuck if I even care anymore. JD: Well I do goddamnit! BJS: Anyhow, what’s the word on Red? JD: Red is out. He has been taken to the local hospital for further exams but it appears from a brief examination he may have suffered a ruptured ACL and a broken leg. We will get back to you fans for sure with more on this. We know for sure that the leg is broken, but as for the ACL we won’t know for sure until he is examined at the hospital. BJS: Ahh well….he’s a scrapper, he’ll be back in time I guess. JD: Well, Jack…I didn’t know you cared. BJS: I don’t. View Devyl 's Biography Back
