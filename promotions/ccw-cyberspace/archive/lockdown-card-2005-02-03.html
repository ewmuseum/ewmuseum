CCW Lockdown card, 3 February 2005

3 February 2005 (announced card)

Lockdown Card February 3 rd 2005 (RP Deadline: 1 st Angle Deadline: 2 nd ) Jerome "Blayze" Jordan last week attacked The Valiant One after his match showing us that the war is a long way from over. This week however he is faced with a very different job. Three of CCW's finest athletes (Jerome, Sylkk and J-Mac) are pitted against one another and three of the Prison's finest criminal minds in a six way swing to determine the man who will go forwards to next weeks Hardcore title match up. If a prisoner should win the bout they are to be released for event days only and will sign a full time contract to CCW. Other events on the card show two women's matches, the beast Barbzilla makes her debut against Lacey Skye a match request personally by the commissioner. The Valiant One won last week for the first time against now former employee JD "Inferno" Waise as did our White MC who beat Parker Louis in a bout which became a singles match. Radek Kasabien this week hopes to beat Neon quickly to regain his winning streak before it is blown entirely out of the water by another opponent. After last weeks entrance by Lady Cat, the return by Sylkk and Jason McAllister this week on Lockdown has much to contend with. The new Heavyweight Champion has been placed into a match against Emilio Dervante with the threat that this beast recruited from Lights Out Wrestling may end Emilio's CCW career. Things are hotting up and there is only one place to catch all the action… see you next week at Lockdown! [Matches Are As Follows] Singles Match "Neon" Parker Louis vs. Radek Kasabien Singles Match The Valiant One vs. MC "White" Mike Womens Match Barbzilla vs. Lacey Skye Singles Match Legacy vs. Emilio Dervante Womens Match Bliss vs. Lady Cat Main Event Six Way Swing Hardcore Championship Number One Contender Jerome "Blayze" Jordan vs. Sylkk vs. Jason McAllister
