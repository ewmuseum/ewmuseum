September 10, 1999 from The Pittsburgh Civic Center in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania - Show #59 The Crazy Train vs. The Manics - CCW Tag Team Championship El Mierda vs. Sam Frederick - CCW Lightweight Championship Pain Lord vs. J.C. Superstar vs. Carnage - Hardcore First Blood Triple Threat Match Ricky Coyote vs. Ice Chamber Big Hoss vs. Carlos The Mexican Midget Kelly Bowe vs. Woman "The Answer" Cade Peters vs. The British Wonder Supreme Machine vs. Chang The Mongolian Giant Friday Fury Results - September 10, 1999 Friday, 10-Sep-1999 23:06:00 [detail withheld] writes: *CCW, the most dominant force in electronic entertainment* Ladies and Gentlemen! Welcome once again to Friday Fury! Tonight we are coming to you from The Pittsburgh Arena here in Downtown Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania! Slated for action is the CCW Tag Team Championship as the Crazy Train get set to take on former Tag Team Champs, The Manics! What will be the deal tonight with The Manics? As their blood, Lady Cat, has been catnapped by El Mierda, will The Manics be focused enough to concentrate on their title match? And will El Mierda make a special guest appearance during the match? We'll find out tonight as we begin Friday Fury! This past Tuesday The Woman defeated Bathsheba in a one-on-one encounter. This week The Woman is looking to make it two in a row and clench a title shot for some Women's Gold! There's the bell and this one is underway as the women are circling each other. There's the lock up as Woman goes for the hair as Kelly grabs for hers! JC Superstar is out of this thing! And "Fat Ass" Phil Burner is the man that did it!
