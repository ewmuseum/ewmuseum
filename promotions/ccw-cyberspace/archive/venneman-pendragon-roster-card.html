Venneman Pendragon Real Name: Venneman Pendragon Birthdate: February 18, 1974 Hometown: St. Cloud, Minnesota Height: 6'5' Weight: 255 lbs. Nicknames: 'The Crusader', 'The Legend' Status: Neutral Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'The Small Hours' by Metallica Wrestling Style: Technical Powehouse Submissionist Signature Moves: 'The Southern Cross'-Reverse Sidewalk slam into the STF submission hold. 'Fall of a Legend'-Reverse Death Valley Driver Set Up Move: 'The Sword in the Stone' (A flying spear takedown to the opponents back as he faces away from Pendragon ) Finisher: The Last Crusade (Venneman firsts lifts his oppoenent into a torture rack position. From here he slams them down onto one knee in a devestating shoulder breker. Afterwards he lifts them back up and down into a backbreaker which he holds for submission upon his knee. ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: FWA, NCWA, BSN Other Federation Awards: NCWA International and World Champion(Unified Belts) FWA Kaiser Champion, BSN Superstar Champion Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Venneman was born to fairly average parents. His father was a professor of medieval history at the University of Minnesota. Things were wonderful and Venneman enjoyed a normal childhood until he was ten years old. At this time, his father was awarded professorship at prestigious Oxford in England. His father gladly accepted as it gave he and his family a chance to return to their ancestral home. It was here that Venneman was taught the meaning that the name he carried cast upon history. His father taught him virtue, nobility and respect, having deemed young Venneman old enough to handle the responsibility that the name commands. Unfortunately, his father was unable to live up to his name. Though he became a historian of much reknown, he never attained the glory to which his instincts desired. When Venneman was 15, his father died of what can only be described as a broken soul. After years, he finally realized that a teacher was all he would be, however villified. It was too much to bear, as he wasted away and faded from this world. After his father's death, Venneman was left the task of chasing glory. After he finished school, he dropped from site and for the next 5 years, little is known. When he returned, it seemed he had found the medium through which to chase his glory. he took up wrestling. He burst onto the scene with the NCWA in 1997. Once there, he quickly ascended to top contendership. Soon, in an epic battle he defeated the infamous Damian Knight for the International title. After more gold, he took the World title from Bullet soonafter and held the two prestigious titles at once. He was untouchable...Then, politocs got involved. The president stripped him of his 'King's Gold' and gave his titles to two yes men. Outraged, Venneman sought glory elsewhere. He found refuge in the BSN, where after a lengthy tourney, he again defeated Damian Knight and gained the Superstar title. The fed closed soon after, so once more, Venneman was fedless. After here, it was on to the FWA...Feudal Wrestling. He quickly gained rcognition and destroyed 'The Assassin' Adrian Jordan for yet more gold. The only thing that could take it away was the fed closing. He was undefeated at this time. Shortly thereafter the fed reopened, but during the tourney for the Gladiator title, his mother became ill...She died soon after and Venneman disappeared yet again. His dead parents and his ancestry drive him, but his lust for power, to be the best...These are the traits fuel him. AFter three years, he has surfaced here...Woe to those who oppose him...
