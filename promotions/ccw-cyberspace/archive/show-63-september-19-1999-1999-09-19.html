Show #63 - September 19, 1999

19 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Staged Shockin Sunday Nite results script for show #63 from Atlanta, ending with the Heavyweight title match, plus a Lightweight title match, a six-man tag and many interference angles.
September 19, 1999 from The Phillips Arena in Atlanta, Georgia - Show #63 Supreme Machine vs. Samson - CCW Heavyweight Championship Sam Frederick vs. Scotty The Hotty vs. Carnage - CCW Lightweight Championship J.C. Superstar vs. "Fat Ass" Phil Burner Pain Lord vs. Hardcore Gorilla Mask "Big Ass" Bob Burner vs. Jim "Klown" Bowe Brad Lamen vs. Steve Punjab Nightshade vs. Big Hoss Sewer Savage, Sid Powers & Doomsday vs. Ice Chamber, X-Port & Bad Boy Briggens - Six Man Tag Team Match Shockin Sunday Nite Results - September 19, 1999 Sunday, 19-Sep-1999 21:44:24 [detail withheld] writes: Welcome fans to a sold out SSN tonight here in the brand new Phillip's Arena in Atlanta, Georgia! Tonight the Heavyweight Championship will be on the line when the Supreme Machine goes one on one against the Number One Contender from July, Samson! The Lightweight Championship will be on the line as well when Sam Frederick will defend his title in a four way dance match with Carnage, Scotty The Hotty, and a mystery opponent. This looks like it's going to be an awesome SSN here in Downtown, Atlanta! For those of you who don't know, I've got two things to talk about before our first match. One is, if you step out of the arena tonight, and walk to your left for about 10 minutes, you will bump into the CNN Center, and below that, the WCW building. Hmmm, what to do, what to do? Any practical jokers? Second, tonight is my second, but final broadcast of SSN. Beginning on October 3, our next SSN, Larry Heenan will be taking over my spot. But fear not fans, I will still be your host for TNME! *Some unfamiliar music begins playing* Hey, what is this? I was told the 3 on 3 match was our first match of the evening, but who is this? Look, from the entranceway, that's newcomer Sylkk and he's walking to the ring with President Stevens??? What could this be about? In the ring now, they have a table set up with two chairs, and Commissioner Wright is also in the ring with Larry Heenan, Gene Cole, and Jimmy Lollar. STEVENS: Ladies and Gentlemen, here I have the official contract for this newcomer here, Sylkk. Sylkk, now all I need is your signature, if you will. SYLKK: I'll sign it, while you MEMORIZE THIS... I will be the number one contender, I will be the champion, and I will be the final destination on this ride! *Slams the pen on the table and walks off* ROSSENDALE: Well folks, looks as if we have another big ego here in CCW. Now on to business, I assume. Sid Powers, Doomsday, & Sewer Savage vs Ice Chamber, X-Port, & Bad Boy Briggens *Sid Power's music begins playing* Well here now comes the team consisting of Sid Powers, Doomsday, and Sewer Savage. Not too much can be said about this team, except that Doomsday is a former Trans-Atlantic Champ, but I think that was a fluke! *Ice Chamber's music begins* Ice Chamber is leading out his team of X-Port and Bad Boy Briggens. The Wrestler Of The Month has been looking more like the Jobber Of The Month as of late. Folks, I now know what happens to someone who let's their ego get the best of em! Here's the bell and it looks like Doomsday will start off against BBB. The lock up and Doomsday breaks free and whips BBB to the ropes. Look at this now, a spinning back breaker! A powerful move right off the bat! Doomdsay with a tag to Sid Powers and they set BBB up! Powers going up while Doomsday raises BBB onto his shoulders and YES! The Doomsday Device! The cover! XPort and Ice Chamber rush the ring but is met by Savage and Doomsday, they all four tumble through the ropes while Sid covers, 1-2-3! The winners of the match, Sid Powers, Doomsday, and Sewer Savage! *Arena goes black, the an orange light luminates the crowd* What the... That must be Carnival Freak! What business has he got out here? He was just brought into CCW earlier today and already is showing his true "light". He's in the ring and going to work on Ice Chamber! Sid Powers, Doomsday, and Savage are beating on X Port and BBB! U-N-I-T-Y! It's a 4 on 3 mele! Doomsday launching XP out of the ring, Powers with BBB over the top! Carnival Freak slamming Ice Chamber to the outside! Powers, now, with a mic... POWERS: Welcome to the Dark Carnival! ROSSENDALE: Dark Carnival? Now I've seen it all. Expect the supernatural to reign supreme here in CCW, NOT! Nightshade vs Big Hoss *A thick white fog begins to form in the ring. All the lights in the arena go to deepest black. A strobe light effect begins above the ring when suddenly a huge eruption of crimson red pyros explode fiercely from the four ring posts. Slowly a large menacing silouette appears from the mists of the fog. It is Nightshade. The servant of hell lets out a horrid earth shattering roar with his deep demonic voice whileslowly raiseing his arm above his head. Just before his roar ends he quickly brings his arms down to his sides. Almost at the same instant of his arms coming down the lights come back on to full power.* An interesting entrance from Nightshade. This past Fury he defeated three opponents in one match. Although he had some help, it was an impressing showing. *Hoss' music begins playing* The Big Hoss, sporting a 2-0 record in his young CCW career, is looking to take down his counterpart rookie opponent. There's the bell and Nightshade going for the spear! The BIG Hoss is down and Nightshade mounted on him punching away at his face. He's got Hoss up now, but Hoss delivers a low blow and the ref didn't see it! Hoss, with Nightshade now, sends him into the corner. Hoss charging now but Nightshade moved out of the way and Hoss went charging into the turnbuckle! Nightshade now with a dazed Hoss, he has him up and it's the Hell's Fury! But wait! Look who's coming! It's The Iron Barbarian! Nightshade going for the cover, and The Barbarian has him by the foot! He's pulling him to the outside and WHAM! He's just cold cocked him with a baseball bat! Nightshade is out! Hoss is out! The Barbarian heading back to the dressing rooms now. The ref making the count, and he's got to 10! Nightshade has been counted out and Big Hoss is the winner, going to 3-0! Nightshade, coming to, looks to the ceiling and riases his arms. A cable seems to be coming down, and, Nightshade is being pulled up to the roof! NIGHTSHADE: Next time... ROSSENDALE: Great, another looney freak! Now, is it just me, or do most of our matches here in CCW get cheated out of fairness by outside interference? In any aspect, Hoss has gone 3-0. Brad Lamen vs Steve Punjab *Lamen's music begins playing* Here comes The Lamens! They've been out of action for some time and Brad is looking to wear off the ring rust tonight. *Punjab's music starts* Steve Punjab, making his way to the ring now along with Jezebel. Here we go as the bell sounds, and look at that! No sooner did the match begin, but, Te Avengaliciamaniacs are making their way to the ring. Carlos has Johnny Lamen occupied while Konngo goes into the ring. The ref is distracted by the action on the outside as Konngo goes bare-handed against Brad Lamen. Holy shiznit! Tim Inman is in the ring with a pitchfork! And he's charged at Lamen! The fork has pierced Lamen's stomach!!!! How sickening! The ref hops out of the ring and tries to pull Carlos off of Johnny. Meanwhile, in the ring, Konngo is talking with Punjab while El Mierda, who has bought a ticket, hops from the crowd and climbs into the ring, holding Lamen. Tildo quickly brings in a chair and smashes him over the head, knocking Lamen out. The stable clears the ring as Punjab goes down for the easy cover. Carlos leaves with the rest of the men as the ref slides into the ring, 1-2-3! Punjab has defeated Brad Lamen with the aide of The Avengaliciamaniacs. Some match, eh? It looked like Punjab was ok with this, but things could change tomorrow morning. "Big Ass" Bob Burner vs Jim "Klown" Bowe *Sweet Home Alabama plays* The Big Ass is making his way to the ring. A return to singles action for the big man may be just what he needs to boost his career. *Manic's music begins playing* After a brief absence from action, Jim Bowe is making his way to the ring. The whereabouts of Lady Cat have filled most of our minds over the past couple days, but what affect will it have over this match? In the ring now, Klown is going to work on Bob Ass. Clubbing the big man with lefts and rights, Klown is going to town! Bob is staggering, and Klown winds up for one BIG right, and down goes Big Ass! Wait a minute, now! What the hell?! Apocalypse and Kaboink are making their way from out of the crowd! We know that A-Pac and Bob Ass haven't seen eye to eye lately, but why is Kaboink out here? There's your answer, folks! Kaboink is distracting the referee, and Apocalypse slides into the ring behind Bob Ass, and he's got a railroad tie in his hands! Klown glares at A-Pac over Bob's shoulder, and Bob turns around only to be sent crashing to the mat with a railroad tie to the face! Oh my good gracious! A-Pac tells Klown to cover Bob, and A-Pac slides out of the ring! Klown covers, and Kaboink lets the ref go, and here's the count.... 1..... 2..... 3! Klown defeats the Big Ass all thanks to A-Pac and his trusty railroad tie! What action, what interferences! We take you now, to a break. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Welcome back folks! During the break, we were paid a visit in the arena. Avengaliciamaniacs showed up in an armored van with "Avengaliciamaniacs: Join or DIE" written in bold red letters on the side. The official Avengaliciamaniac chauffer "Bobby Tom" breaks through a gate and drives straight through a garage and into the arena. After parking in the aisle ala Steve Austin Konngo opens the back. Everyone hops out and goes to the ring. The Avengaliciamania personal body guard staff, dressed in business suits with red ties, guard from the entranceway to prevent anyone from coming in. Konngo, El Mierda, Carlos, Tildo, and finally Tim Inman enter the ring. Inman is wearing some kind of body armour suit. Inman: You may be wondering what this armour is for. You see, because of a few ass holes, Avengaliciamania must have the top protection. I have taken the liberty to buy an armoured van and a staff of 5 body guards to protect against EVERYTHING. And I have this nifty little electric metal suit here to prevent anyone and everyone from touching me. And to show it works, I would like a volunteer from the croud. Konngo, Mierda, go get me somebody" **Against their will, a small 12-year old fan with a Porker's Champion t-shirt is pulled into the ring by Carlos and Mierda. They throw him against Inman and the small boy is shocked, falling to the ground in convulsions.** Inman: Now somebody get me a medical staff for this one... Now NOBODY will touch me, for I am Avengalicious! **They climb back into the aroured truck and leave the entranceway.** Pain Lord vs Hardcore Gorilla Mask *Pain Lord's music begins* Painlord entering the ring now with Omega while the arena is black and a light flashes and lights up the arena. On the big screen the words "the future is here...Are you ready to go??" appear on the screen as loud techo music begins to play. The lights come on and PainLord is not Painlord... He is wearing blue clothes and helmet. He looks as if he is from another planet or light years ahead of our time. Larry: Where is PainLord and what have you done with him Omega?? Omega:Painlord is gone, he was a pussy and have no sense of direction. You are now seeing DORIAN 5000. He is from light years away and is here to crash this world with his. He is much more of a wrestler than PainLord was... I am from the future and DORIAN'S world. I was never here for Painlord, I was to bring DORIAN here and now I have done so. Larry: So what happened to PainLord??? Omega:He has died and gone to hell, where he should be. DORIAN is here and that is all you need to worry about. PainLord is gone, now you have DORIAN 5000 in his place. *The screen shows the words "THE FUTURE HAS ARRIVED...ARE YOU READY TO GO???" again and DORIAN 5000 and Omega exit. Well, I suppose that HCGM isn't gonna get his match with Pain Lord, tonight. J.C. Superstar vs "Fat Ass" Phil Burner - Non-Title Match *JC's music begins* J.C. Superstar making his way out now, and notice, he hasn't got GOD with him. After this past Friday's action, JC fired GOD as his manager, claiming he is getting nowhere in the ring with GOD at his side. *Sweet Home Alabama plays* Here he comes! It's time to rumble ladies and gentlemen! The Porker's Champion, and now unprecedented three time CCW Hardcore Champion, "Fat Ass" Phil Burner! He comes down the aisle, wearing overalls, 6'4" 435 pounds! He climbs into the ring, unhooks the overalls and underneath he has a shirt with a big giant "W" on it! I wonder if that stands for anything? He mounts the turnbuckles and raises the roof Booker T style! The bell rings and J.C. Superstar charges at Burner! He hits him in the back of the thighs! Uh-oh not a smart move! Phil wobbles on the turnbuckle, and Superstar can't get away -- A Fat Ass Ass Flop! J.C. Superstar is out! Phil gets up and looks down at the cold form of Superstar. He shrugs, then he signals for it! The most electrifying move in breakfast cooking today! There goes the elbow pad! Off the ropes, hops over him, off the opposite side, and.......OH! The Porker's Elbow! The cover -- 1, 2, 3!!! Phil Burner has destroyed J.C. Superstar! Phil Burner is on a roll! Unbelievable match! Never before has JC Superstar been put down so quickly! Folks this is the time for fast matches, and Porker Elbows! Sam Frederick vs Carnage vs Scotty The Hotty vs Mystery Opponent Ladies and Gentlemen, the President of CCW! *CCW's theme begins playing* STEVENS: There is reason I'm out here prior to the start of this match. There seems to be some illegal competitors booked for this match and tonight, I am fixing that error. First of all, there is no mystery opponent. The challenge was made for this match but no one ever named a mystery opponent, so, therefore, that eliminates one. Now, it has been drawn to my attention that Carnage weighs over the 230 pound limit for Lightweight competition. So, Carnage has been removed from the card. It will now be a one on one between Sam Frederick and Scotty The Hotty. Thank you. ROSSENDALE: *Smooth plays over the speakers* That announcement is sure to make a major impact on this match, as it goes from 4 men to 2. Here comes Sam Frederick now, without the company of Ben. *Scotty's music begins* Here comes Scotty The Hotty, along with Carnage, who was just announced as eliminated from the match. So it will be Scotty and Ben. There's the bell and they lock up! Sam now being whipped to the ropes and is slammed with a powerslam from Scotty. Going to work now on the inside leg, Scotty has Sam in an awkward position. But look! Ben Frederick from the crowd! He has a pair of handcuffs! Behind on Carnage now, he's knocked him in the back of the head with the handcuffs, and he's now hooking him to the railings around the crowd with the handcuffs. Scotty is distracted and is yelling at Ben Frederick and OOOOHHHH! From behind is Sam Frederick with the Last Call! 1-2-3! The winner, and still the CCW Lightweight Champion, Sam Frederick!!! Teamwork pays off! But isn't this a singles match?? The Supreme Machine vs Samson - CCW Heavyweight Championship Well, it's time now for the big match of the evening. Tonight's Main Event is in the making! Four time CCW Champ, Supreme Machine, is set to go at it with Samson. This match has happened twice in the past, and it's ended up with each competitor taking home a victory. Although, the first time they hooked up, there was no title involved. *"Tunnelwalk" plays over the loudspeakers.* ANNOUNCER: "Ladies and gentlemen now entering the ring, standing 6 foot two inches tall, weighing three hundred and two pounds, known worldwide for his survival of the assault from Sid Powers in the cage match at Heatstroke, the toughest man, the strongest man in CCW, the master of the Vengeance, Saaaamsooooooooooon!!!" *The Contender begins* The CCW Heavyweight Champion is making his way to the ring. He's looking determined to defend his title here tonight! Oh look, someone coming to offer their services? Steve Punjab has made his way halfway down the aisle, looks like he's here to prevent interference. The two circle the ring, eyes fixed on each other. It has been too long since we've seen Samson against The Supreme Machine. Samson was granted the shot after he won the battle royal back in June, but SuMa beat him. The two lock it up!!! SuMa is walking Samson back! No! Now Samson is walking SuMa back! SuMa's backed into the corner! Samson, with knifedge chops to the chest of SuMa! But SuMa reverses and now Samson is in the corner! Samson, thrown into the opposite corner, and SuMa charges but Samson gets out of the way! Samson and SuMa, exchanging blows in the center in the ring! Both men are spent! They are both on the ground and the referee starts the ten count! Who will get up first? 7....8....9......they both get up at the same time, and Samson levels SuMa with a gigantic spear! The crowd erupts! SuMa, staggering to his feet, and Samson is behind him! Is it? Yes! Samson's Vengeance!!! The referee is checking does he submit? YES!!! Samson has submitted the Supreme Machine and won the heavyweight title! Samson drops the Supreme Machine from his shoulders, and stands dumbstruck! The referee approaches him and raises his hand! He hands Samson the Heavyweight belt! Samson stands in the center of the ring, and raises the belt above his head, still in shock at winning the gold! The crowds are chanting! "Samson......Samson......" Here comes his sweetheart Delilah! She runs up to him and they embrace! She has the CCW Women's belt in her hand! The two stand side by side, belts raised! The couple in CCW owns the gold! This is unprecedented! Let it be known, that on Sunday, September 19th, 1999, Samson, came back from his injuries! Samson defeated the Supreme Machine! Samson captured the gold! Oh, folks, I can't believe what I am seeing here! Just one week after recapturing the title for the fourth time, the Supreme Machine has lost it here tonight on Shockin Sunday Nite! What will go down this Tuesday Night as TNME comes to you from the beautiful state of Florida!? The Samson SuMa saga is just beginning! Oh what a night! Until Tuesday from Florida, this is Jeremiah Rossendale saying goodnight from Atlanta, Georgia, where Samson has finally captured the CCW Heavyweight Championship! [name withheld] CCW Home
