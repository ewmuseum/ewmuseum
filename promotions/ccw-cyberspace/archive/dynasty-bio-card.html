Jason Dynasty Real Name: Jason Danastie Jr. Birthdate: November 8, 1980 Hometown: Boston, MA Height: 6'6' Weight: 260 Nicknames: 'The Iceman' Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'Deadly Games' by James Johnston Information Wrestling Style: Psychological, Brawling and Powerhouse Set Up Move: Walking Sideslam (Dynasty delivers it so hard that his opponent literally bounces off the canvas and rolls onto his stomach to setup for the One-Minute Limit.) Finisher: The One-Minute Limit (The opponent is on their stomach, Dynasty stands over the opponent's upper back. Dynasty reaches under the opponent's arms and locks his hands together behind the opponent's neck to apply a full nelson. Dynasty then sits on the opponent’s lower back and proceeds to lean back and pulls the opponent’s upper body up, thereby applying pressure to the opponent's back and neck. It has never taken Dynasty longer than one-minute to gain a submission victory with this maneuver and rarely longer than mere seconds.) Title History: None Award History: None Other Federations: USWF, UWA, TW and GW Other Federation Awards: USWF World Heavyweight Title, USWF United States Title x3, USWF Hardcore Title, UWA Inter-Continental Title, UWA Hardcore Title, TW World Heavyweight Title, GW International Title and GW Tag Team Titles. USWF Lord of the Ring '99, USWF Best Gimmick 1999, USWF 'Greatest of All-Time', UWA Longest running champion (7 months as Inter-Continental Champion), Hall of Fame in USWF and UWA Quotes "Prepare to sell your soul to God because your bodies and minds shall belong to me. " "You will learn that when the Iceman cometh, he cometh for your souls!" "Because the Iceman cometh...for your souls!" History Appearance Jason Dynasty is an African-American who stands 6’6” and weighs 260 pounds. He has brown eyes and short dark brown hair, in an Army-style haircut. He has a well-chiseled body, including a six-pack, muscular arms and legs, a very strong upper body and only about 8% body fat, a look similar to a black Batista. He wears no tattoos or piercings and has no facial hair. In the ring he wears a black tanktop with the word “Iceman” styling the back in Silver lettering to make the words look lice ice, a pair of black wrestling boots and a pair of black wrestling tights. On the right leg it reads “Iceman” and on the left leg is an icicle with the end of it dripping blood, both the name and the icicle are in silver letters while the blood is in red. Dynasty will usually wear a black trenchcoat, a dark shirt, blue jeans or khakis and his trademark black wrap-around sunglasses when out of the ring. History Jason Danastie Jr. was born in Boston, Massachusetts on November 8, 1980 to a young woman named Lauren Danastie. He was immediately brought up in a world of religion, after being left at the Boston home of Lauren’s only relative, an uncle named Father Jason Danastie who was the reason behind Jason Danastie Jr. being named as he was. During his teen years, Jason acted as a bully towards many of his fellow classmates and it was not below him to actually physically assault them when they did something he was not in agreement with. Father Danastie chose to sign Jason up in a martial arts school to settle this aggressive side. It worked far to well as Jason became an expert in karate, tae kwon do, jujitsu, kung fu, judo, shootfighting and street fighting. By the time of his 18th birthday Jason had participated in 78 martial arts bouts and had an impressive record of 71-7. He chose at that point to enter a sport that would allow him to use all of his skills, professional wrestling. When Jason turned 18 on November 8, 1998 he changed his name to Jason “J.J.” Dynasty, a name he felt would be more welcomed in the wrestling world than his former name of Jason Danastie. He began looking for a fed to sign on with in December of 1998 and eventually found exactly the type of federation that a rookie like he would need to help bring him to the next level of competition, that federation was the newly created United States Wrestling Federation. He signed with his first federation two weeks after the federation debuted and officially began competing in the USWF on January 1, 1999 on their Thursday Rage show. While in the USWF, Dynasty was known as the Messenger of Light and was one of the most heralded champions to ever grace the federation. He spent a good amount of time feuding over that federation’s United States Championship, a belt he wore three times between March of 1999 and April of 2000. During his first few months he was a mainstay in opening matches before being given a chance to face Julian Cross for Cross’ United States Title. Dynasty took his opportunity and ran with it, defeating Cross via a roll-up and securing the victory, also becoming only the second U.S. Champion in USWF history. After losing it a month later he entered the Man of the Ring 8-Man Gauntlet, which held the stipulation of choosing any lower title to get a shot at with a stipulation of your choosing. He went on to win the gauntlet and claimed the USWF Hardcore Title from its champion Thunderhawk in June of 1999 during a ferocious Cell Match, a match born from Dynasty’s own warped ideas. A month later, Thunderhawk got a rematch for the belt in a Falls Count Anywhere match and regained his title after interference on the part of the second-place finisher in the gauntlet, Ray Steele distracted Dynasty. The two men decided to see who the better man was and they instigated the first ever 5-PPV Stipulation Series bout. This series saw such matches as a Hell-in-the-Cell Match, a Scaffold Match, a Pool of Glass Match, a 2 out of 3 Falls Match and finally a Street Fight in December of 1999 that saw both men out of action for between 6 and 8 weeks. This series legitimately made Dynasty’s career and also gave him the second 2 of his 3 United States Titles. It also was a main reason as to why Steele eventually became the USWF World Champion in February, as Dynasty was busy with the third of his three United States Title reigns. He held that belt for two months before the feud between the two men entered the World Title division. Dynasty claimed his first World Title victory in May of 2000 in the second-ever Cell Match in USWF history, holding it for a month before losing it back to Steele. A month later, Dynasty competed in the main event of the final USWF Pay-Per-View, “Final Reckoning” against his most hated enemy, Ray Steele. The match was a Sanctuary Match and took place in a graveyard on the outskirts of Dynasty’s hometown of Boston. It was during this match that Dynasty seemingly crossed the line between good and evil. After defeating Steele via Dynasty’s new “Road to Hell” finisher, Dynasty took his final vengeance out on a man who had been a thorn in his side for nearly a year. He stabbed Steele through the heart and pushed Steele into a grave to dramatically end their feud. A few weeks passed until Dynasty, USWF Owner Ryan Blackman and USWF members Chris Kanton, Julian Cross and Christian Rage showed up in the United Wrestling Alliance, or UWA. This would be Dynasty’s second federation but the beginning of his stay was far from fun as a trial took place for the murder of Ray Steele. Amazingly Dynasty was found innocent of the despite videotape evidence. The judge stated that Dynasty may not have been acting of sound mind at the time of this murder and after a settlement and probation for 6 months from any non-regular matches he was allowed free. After the trial Dynasty began portraying a darker image and calling himself The Iceman Jason Dynasty. Dynasty’s first major success in UWA occurred by him winning the UWA Intercontinental Title in early September of 2000 and holding it for seven months before losing it in April of 2001 during a triple threat match where he never got pinned. During that period, Dynasty brought in his “mentor” Gregory O’Donnell and O’Donnell’s friend the Everlasting Ghost and the three men became known as the Triple Threat of the Destinies. Following that Dynasty participated in the third ever Cell Match with this one being called the “Furnished Cell Match” against Joe Nickname with the Inter-Continental Title on the line. Dynasty kept his undefeated Cell streak alive when he killed Nickname in the cell, opened the cell door, placed the key in the body of the dying Nickname before throwing Nickname’s fiancée Jennifer into the Cell and locking the two inside. They terrorized the UWA from December until early April when Dynasty led the Destinies, who were a group of cultists who swore allegiance to his Iceman persona, against GOD and the Ghost. He became instantly the most hated man in the UWA and eventually lost his Inter-Continental Title because of it. The loss occurred when GOD paid 4 fans to hold Dynasty against the barrier during a triple threat match, forcing him to watch as the Ghost pinned Carter Fall for Dynasty’s title. Enraged and wanting revenge, Dynasty went from June of 2001 until October of 2001 in competition against the now 4-time UWA World Champion GOD in World Title matches. Despite his best efforts it would be a title that he never won from GOD, but in the process he became known as the most dominant competitor in UWA to never win the World Title. In November he returned to his murderous ways as he killed both Everlasting Ghost and Gregory O’Donnell during a No Holds Barred match between himself and Ghost. Following the match he left UWA for two weeks to “recuperate” from the events of that night. He returned to his reigning ways when he won the UWA Hardcore Title in December of 2001. His most prominent loss occurred on January 6 of 2002 when he competed in a Loser Leaves UWA, Barbed Wire Rope match for his Hardcore Title against Nova and suffered a Novacaine of the top rope, allowing Nova to pick up the win and the title. It also signaled the end of Dynasty’s UWA career. However, Dynasty’s stay in the unemployment line would be short as almost 2 weeks later he had signed on with his third federation, Travesty Wrestling, or TW. He aligned himself with the federation’s top stable, the Warlords. Dynasty, now with Warlord manager Simon Corenthos in his corner, shot to the top of TW as the newest member of the Warlords; whose members included the Intercontinental Champion Jerod, the Tag Team Champions Crow and Cloud, and the Television Champion Daniel DeBois. He won the TW World Heavyweight Title in a Steel Cage Match in March of 2002 from Brian Harris and held it for 4 months until he lost it in July. His loss in July occurred after a screwjob finish that involved the new champion Jerod, and Jerod’s newest allies Simon Corenthos and Dr. Damnation. The next month within TW would be hell for Dynasty as he would constantly be attacked by the New Warlords; Jerod, Corenthos, Damnation, Crow, Cloud, DeBois and Jackson Storm. He would eventually be placed in a one-sided Handicap Elimination Match against Jerod, Damnation, Crow and Storm. He was actually able to defeat the latter three before an attack from Cloud and DeBois allowed Jerod to gain a pinfall victory. Sickened by the acts that occurred the day before, Dynasty arrived at the August 26th show and handed in his resignation, stating that he would no longer associate himself with a federation that showed so much favoritism towards friends of the owner. He signed on with his fourth federation, the Global Wrestling Federation two weeks later in September of 2002 to prove to the top staffers in the PTC that he had what it takes to get to the next level of competition. Having seemingly returned to a more ring-based game, Dynasty made an immediate impact and qualified for both the Tag Team Titles and the World Heavyweight Title in his first GW Pay-Per-View event. Despite being unable to walk out as the company’s World Champion, Jason and his partner Shawn Corrick defeated the tag team New Found Fame to become the new GW Tag Team Champions on Halloween night. They held the titles until the Dec. 16 edition of Evolution when, in an 8-man tag team match, Dynasty turned on Corrick and allowed Sean Price and Jay Robson to claim the titles. This was the official reformation of TLM's faction The Mile High Club. At the January Crossfire PPV, Dynasty defeated Lawrence Stanley in a singles match that gave Dynasty the distinction of being the number one contender to the Primestar Title. After the Crossfire Pay-Per-View, Dynasty returned to a more psychological game, the type that had given him his greatest success back in USWF, UWA and TW, and began to come into his own in the ring. He and Sean Price aligned with each other and almost immediately began targeting such men as Pete Hardy and Lawrence Stanley, and even their own Mile High Club teammates Jay Robson and Ace Cash, who was fired immediately after. This led to the dismantling of the Mile High Club but not the end of the Price/Dynasty alliance. After victories or close victories against men such as Derek Frost, Brick and Sage Sabbot the upper men at the Global Wrestling offices chose to book Dynasty in a tournament for the International Title. Dynasty defeated Champion Derek Frost in the semifinals to become the new International Champion, only to lose in a close tournament finals match that same day against his best friend Sean Price who became the new champion. It was after this moment where Dynasty began seeing a change in Global Wrestling. Egos began to show their faces and people began to quit, the whole moral of the federation had slipped over the course of a week. Looking to keep himself from becoming bitter or angry towards the federation that had, in its better days, given him so much he chose to turn in his letter of resignation on March 1, 2003 and it was accepted. 6 months after entering the Global Wrestling Federation, Dynasty walked out a free man and in search for something new. That something would be found in Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, the federation that would become Dynasty’s fifth federation of his career, and a federation that had no idea what they were getting themselves into by hiring Jason Dynasty. Prior to his signing in early March of 2003 he approached another wrestler, Damien Nhyte, who was said to be one of the most calculating stars of the past. Though out of practice recently, Nhyte chose to return to the ring and team with Dynasty as the tag team, The Disciples of Death, and with that they chose to change CCW forever.
