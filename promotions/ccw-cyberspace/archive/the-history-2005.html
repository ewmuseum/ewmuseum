CCW: The History

undated (a standing page; captured 8 February 2005)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the past. Cyberspace Championship Wrestling has been with us since a daring Jonathon Steven’s placed into the ground a seed which would later flourish into one of the most pivotal federations of Primetime Centrals origins boasting such names as SuMa Jason McAllister and in its more recent past Jimmy "Sylkk" Hargreaves. Fundamentally whoever has retained the controlling pieces in the CCW puzzle its main goals have never slipped too far from the original goal, to be a success. September 8 th 1998 began a year of little excitement the company being forced to fall into debt almost as soon as it opened to the public, the general cost of maintaining a federation in the early days had been overlooked by Mr. Steven’s and just as the turning of its first month of broadcasting receivers were called in and CCW fell like many small federations do, at the first hurdle. This however seemed not to worry Jonathon Steven’s who in turn began once again this time with outside financial support from other small businesses and by February 26 th 1999 the soon to be powerhouse of wrestling CCW seemed to fly into the atmosphere causing many heads to turn within months of its opening. Irish Spring so called with pride let loose the beginning of a dynasty as it crowned the federation’s title bouts with now customary decorum and skill Spring 1999 was upon us and Cyberspace Championship Wrestling had been born. It was in this usually quite time of the year that CCW launched its first tournament, the "Champions of the Ring" this now yearly tournament gave CCW its first true hero, the legend know as SuMa (The Supreme Machine). SuMa would remain in the hearts of the CCW contingent forever and at the aptly named "SuMa’s Destruction" his final title run would begin. From September of that year (A month which had began the federations hopes and aspirations) till November SuMa reigned supreme. November saw the return of another hero within the CCW ring and at Showdown 1 Phil Burner proved too much for long time rival Tim Inman and began the federations then longest title run of 104 days. Hate mail was sent constantly too Mr. Steven’s a man who had created in CCW both his dream and the fans, showing that anyone big or small could make something great so long as they had the belief. The Millennium year took fans by the scruff of the neck introducing CCW to their own Millennium bug Neurosis and other stars of the squared circle in Andrew "Ace" Hawk, Bobby Blades, John Jupiter and of course Jason McAllister. These five men along with veteran CCW stars Brad Lamen, Scotty Showtime and Jim "Klown" Bowe would star in a rejuvenated CCW. The apparent death of Mr. Jonathon Steven’s was a huge blow for the company and though his death was only later talked about the federation went into mourning and the Dubb as it had become known by loyal fans was closed for the month of July. "Champions of the Ring" 2000 aired under Shriekfest during the millennium year Phil Burner winning in a final which saw Andrew Hawk hailed as a future legend (a title he manage eventually to gain) with the semi's and the final being held during the same show. Phil Burner’s retirement had been short lived as he came to the aid of CCW in their time of mourning taking the federation to even greater heights and beginning the federation on the path towards their ultimate rivals PCW. PCW under The Phantom Booker was to become the world’s largest organisation creating federations such as OSE and RUSH but during the time the two federations began their feud it was within FedWars a place where many claimed the end truly began for Burner. Phil would continue on at the helm of CCW for almost 1 year until it was revealed that many wrestlers were leaving the company due to possible scams in match fixing. Burner was adamant that this was not the case however in March 2001 the return of a dead man prompted Burners abdication of his position and Jonathon Steven’s once more returning to the hot seat.
