Fanatik entered Cyberspace Championship Wrestling with a grin on his face, and a mission on his mind. Pulling Don Stevens out of the CCW Cameraman business and forcing him into being his manager, Fanatik was nothing more than a walking bag of pratical jokes and uncontrollable mahem. Some of the matches that really stick out when I think of Fanatik are the likes of a 'Bottle O' Bleach on a Pole Match', 'Home Depot Deathmatch', and the imfamous 'Circus Match'. If the titles of these matches didn't show what kind of a person Fanatik was, then watching his promos will. Fanatik was a careless, happy individual... yet a tough compeditor, and a threat to people who faced against him. Fanatik joined the New Strangers in early 2001, joing with the likes of Kensuke Suzahara, Blayze, and The Valiant One. The stable was unstoppable, and had little to no problems within it. In fact, the New Strangers is still alive today... although, now it's with new members. Only a few original New Strangers still ride the bus... while the others have gone their seperate ways. Fanatik, however, couldn't grin forever... and as he's left CCW time and time again, only to return... Fanatik returned once more, to have people change their point of view toward him forever. Fanatik collided with Andrew 'Ace' Hawk, and their dispute went from fun-and-games to a personal war. Eventually, Fanatik did some actions that caused great concern within CCW HeadQarters, and Fanatik was arrested... and wasn't heard of since. ...Until about three months later. During Group Therapy 2002, a fan unexpectingly jumped the barricade and attacked many CCW Superstars during a bout to become the King of Extreme. This fan destroyed many of the superstars, but only let one of them stand along side of him - Blayze. This fan... was Fanatik. Although Blayze didn't win the match, CCW Management and the CCW Lockerroom stirred as Blayze smiled to President Spider, held up Fanatik's contract, and claimed ownership to one of CCW's more memoriable superstars. ...And Fanatik never stopped grinning. What happened to one of CCW's most loved superstars? ...How'd he change as much as he did? Why? ...Unanswered questions... empty answers.
