Show #55 - August 31, 1999

31 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Staged script of Tuesday Night’s Main Event show #55 from Portland, Maine, with a card listing, backstage interviews and several matches narrated in running commentary (the script breaks off before the main matches).
TUESDAY NIGHT'S MAIN EVENT August 31, 1999 from The Cumberland County Civic Center in Portland, Maine - Show #55 Crazy Train vs. Sid Powers & Mystery Partner - CCW Tag Team Championship Joquar vs. Hardcore Gorilla Mask - CCW Hardcore Championship Thunder vs. "Fat Ass" Phil Burner - Hardcore Match El Mierda & The Konngo vs. Mr. Gobstopper & Pepper The Cat Chang The Mongolian Giant vs. Jim "Klown" Bowe Ben Frederick vs. Niamouchu J.C. Superstar vs. The Jolly Rancher "The Avenger" Tim Inman vs. Pain Lord Ladies and Gentlemen! CCW would like to welcome you to the Cumberland County Civic Center in Portland, Maine and welcome back to CCW! Tonight's TNME will hold two title matches, one being the tag team championship when The Crazy Train meets up with Sid Powers and a mystery partner! Who this partner is is unknown, but if Sid Powers can obtain the friendship with one of our top competitors, who knows what the man who began the Franchise can accomplish! And in our other feature bout, the Hardcore Championship match! Joquar will defend his title against returning former CCW Heavyweight Champion, Hardcore Gorilla Mask! This will be HCGM's first crack at the Hardcore Title and he is looking to jump right back into the driver seat, perhaps, taking a lesson from Konngo who won the TA title in his return debut! Who knows what the future holds tonight in CCW! Now it's time for the action to begin! "The Avenger" Tim Inman vs Pain Lord We're set now for the first match of the night and wha... *Sam Frederick's music begins playing* What is this? Sam Frederick isn't scheduled to be out here just yet, but here he comes and he looks like he means business! He just grabbed a mic from our ring announcer, Gene Cole, and has entered the ring. Sorry for the interruption, but I have a bit of business to take care of before this show is going on! I'm not leaving this ring until I get MY Lightweight Title match! El Mierda, bring your punta-licking ass out here and let's go! *A few seconds go by* Seems like Mex-pac isn't coming out, well, then I'm not leaving until I get my shot! *Pain Lord's music begins playing* Well, uh-oh! Our first scheduled match looks like it will take place because here comes the first participant, Pain Lord! Wait a second, Sam is now off his ass and has a chair in hand. Is he going to take care of Pain Lord for Inman or is Pain Lord going to turn back?? Oh hell! Look who's coming! *The fans sound with a mixed array of cheers and boos* Tim Inman from behind... BAM! Pain Lord goes down from a clothesline from behind and Inman slides Pain Lord into the ring. Looks like Sam is heading out of here, ya know he and Inman are no longer on the same team. From nowhere, the Great American Piledriver! Pain Lord is down, 1-2-3! The Avenger has delivered payback to Pain Lord, thanks to a distraction from Sam Frederick! *Avengaliciamaniacs' music begins playing* You've gotta hand it to Inman, he seizes oppurtunity when it presents itself! *Inman leaves the ring as his music plays, proclaiming himself the greatest* Let's take you now to Larry Heenan who is in the backstage area with J.C. Superstar. Heenan: JC, I know you seemed to have some tough luck at Friday Fury against Fat Ass Phil Burner. What went wrong? You seemed so confident prior to the match. JC: Well Larry, I was definitely confident in myself but was ill-prepared for such a garagantuan in my debut. But this time round things are gonna be a lot different. I have been totally focused in my preparation for this evening's match against none other than the Jolly Rancher. GOD has taken me to a whole new level in my training and the Rancher's in for a surprise. Heenan: Hold on there a second JC....what kind of surprise are you talking about?? JC: Just watch the match and my wrestling will speak for itself.... **JC's theme music begins playing and he heads for the arena with GOD** Heenan: Well you heard it hear folks. Looks like we're in for a real treat tonite. No puns intended Mr. Rancher. J.C. Superstar vs The Jolly Rancher Interesting words from J.C. Superstar who is now making his entrace into the arena with his manager, GOD. What is the deal with this guy? He carries a giant cross and preaches portions of biblical prophecies, and, has a manager who calls himself simply, God! A religious fanatic no doubt, but, can he wrestle? *WWF's Brood music begins playing in MIDI format* Do you remember this music? It's the old Hard Candy tune! And here comes the Jolly Rancher now! Not much has been said from the offices of the Rancher lately, perhaps rumors are showing truth about how he has secretly been away from CCW, pimping the streets of Washington, D.C. But people, that is merely a rumor I've read on the Internet. Rancher now sliding into the ring and there's the bell as JC begins wailing away on JR. The Rancher, who seems a bit on the weak side this week, is looking sluggish! Off the ropes now is the Rancher and is caught by JC and turned around, a boot, oh my! The Holy Spirit Bomb! 1-2-3!! The Rancher has lost to The Superstar! A short, and first, victory for J.C. Superstar! The followings of GOD are appearing to be very rewarding on the religious front tonight. Perhaps a change is in the future for The Jolly Rancher, perhaps, well, perhaps some training! We go now, to, a commercial... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Ben Frederick vs Niamouchu *Niamouchu's music begins playing* Welcome back to TNME, Niamouchu is making his way to the ring now, I'm sure he's eager to begin tonight's match as he faces Ben Frederick. *Ben's music begins playing* Here comes Ben Frederick, long thought to be dead, but is back in CCW action as Sam Frederick strides at his side. There's the bell and this one is underway! Frederick nails Niamouchu several times across the forehead with closed fists before sending him off the ropes and OWWW! A devastating powerslam! I wonder, what is with the vets tonight? Unbelievable, The Gargoyle Suplex this early on! SLAM! 1-2- no! Ben relinquished the cover and... a Boston Crab! The ref checks and Niamouchu is tapping out in utter pain! The winner is Ben Frederick! Frederick is being greeted by Sam Frederick in the ring and are looking to the fans for cheers. Chang The Mongolian Giant vs Jim "Klown" Bowe Well it's time now for that Mongolian Giant to come out. I don't know why we need to see this match. This guy, if you can call him that, is just... just look at him! He's HUGE! SuMa looks to have this giant under control as Gill Bates accompanies them. *AnaManics music begins to play* Here come a group of party animals now! Awww, would ya look at that... Jim is coming out hand-in-hand with Kelly, how sweet! And, look at those silly sausages, Lady Cat is mounted on Pepper's shoulders. What is the world coming to? Don't they know their doom is near? Klown gives Kelly a quick kiss and hessitantly enters the ring wher.... Good God Almighty! Chang has been unleashed and Klown goes down hard! There's the bell, but Chang already has the upperhand! Chang lifts Klown over his head and drives him head first into the mat! DAMN! Chang has rolled Klown over and places him in the Camel Clutch! The referee is in Klown's face, and ... *Klown taps* Like I said, your winner is Chang The Mongolian Giant! SuMa and Gill Bates both climb into the ring and SuMa constraints Chang as Bates leads the pair out of the ring. ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- El Mierda & Mystery Partner vs Mr. Gobstopper and Pepper The Cat A wandering CCW cameraman is backstage when he catches Kaboink storming down the hallway. Kaboink is dressed in a Tribe cut-off T-shirt and blue jeans. He is carryiong brass knuckles, some rope, and a pair of pliers. He motions for the cameraman to follow him, which he does. The two are making they're way to the dressing rooms, and Kaboink is checking each nameplate for the right one. "Gobber, no.... Lady Cat, not yet..... Ah! Here it is!" Kaboink is pointing to the glod nameplate, and the camera zooms in. It reads: "El Mierda." "You ready to see some fireworks, boy? Knock on the door and tell him Gee Cole wants an interview." The camera is knocking, adn El Mierda calls out in a thick Mexican accent to come in. He opens the door, and out of nowhere Kaboink unleashes a flurry of punches with the brass knuckles!! Oh, no! Blood is pouring from Mex-Pac's face. The cameraman is rushing inside, not wanting to miss a minute. Kaboink goes to lock the door, and just in time. Apparently the Avengaliciamaniacs heard the ruckus and are coming to Mierda's aid! Quickly Kaboink uses the rope to hog-tie Mierda, and he slaps Mierda's face to awaken him. "I told you if you insulted Savvy again, you'd lose some teeth. Well, I warned you!" Oh, goodness!!! Kaboink has pulled the pliers out!! No.... NO! He's pulling teeth!!! Mierda is screaming in pain, as first one incisor comes out, then a bicuspid, and then all four of the bottom front teeth!! Oh my George Orville Delmonty!!! CRASH!!!!!!!!! Here's Big Ass Bob Burner, busted through the door! Kaboink drops the pliers and the two wail away at each other. Mierda howls on the ground, staring at the bloody roots of his teeth. Oh, Burner's size is too much for the Kaboinked One, and soon Kaboink fights his way through the Avengaliciamaniacs and rushes down the hallway, with the Konngo and Tim Inman in hot pursuit. Well, who knows whats going to happen now that El Mierda has been attacked! Thunder vs "Fat Ass" Phil Burner - Hardcore Match *Mark Of Cain begins playing* Thunder is about to make his entrance now in this Hardcore match. I have to ask, why does Thunder participate in so many Hardcore matches? His record shows he is NOT hardcore! I don't think, myself, that Thunder is a hardcore wrestler, but if he defeats the FORMER Hardcore Champ, perhaps his future in the Hardcore division will become greater! Thuner's in the ring now, weilding a steal chair, looks like he's come prepared! *Sweet Home Alabama begins playing* Well, here comes The Fat Ass, and again, I must ask a question. If Mr. Fat Ass is from Memphis, Tennessee, why does he come out to "Sweet Home Alabama"? I guess it doesn't matter, the fans seem to love this guy! *Crowd cheers for Fat Ass* I think this guy is being held back! If he were to step out of the Hardcore Division, perhaps he would become the Heavyweight, and I do mean HEAVYWEIGHT, Champ soon! He's weilding his trademark shovel and there's the bell! Thunder goes down with Burner and the referee into the lowered ring! The springboard underneath the ring must have given out due to the massive weight being slammed against it! Oh no!
