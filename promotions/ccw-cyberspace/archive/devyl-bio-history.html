CCW bio history: Devyl

undated (a standing page; capture window 7 April – 13 June 2003)

History Not much is known about Victoria Devins. Some small pieces of her past have been brought to light, and are recorded here for those interested in discovering what was: -Victoria Devins was born on August 14th, 1977 to Joyce and Pat Devins. She had one sibling; Meliissa, her sister who was 6 years older than her. Victoria's birth was considered a miracle, Joyce and Pat were getting on in years and told that although they could have another child, the chances of them doing it safely where slim to none. -When Victoria was five years of age, her eleven year old sister disappeared without a trace. It has been conjectured that Victoria's father, Pat Devins, was part of a cult who took Melissa and used her as a sacrifice. That fact has not been proven conclusively. -Victoria disappeared from home for several years. Her parents claimed she had run away, and had no explantion or idea of her whereabouts. Victoria would miraculously reappear one year later. Again, no explanation was given for her whereabouts or what she had been doing during her disappearance. All that anybody knew was that she had changed. No longer the bright and happy child, she was now somber and morose. And behind all that, a glimmer of something that would only grow worse in time... A chilling wind that would alter and change people's lives forever. -In the summer of 2001, shortly after Victoria joined Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, Pat Devins was murdered in an Illinois State Hospital after receiving a near-fatal heart attack at home. Joyce Devins would go on to tell the police that her daughter, Victoria, had been the one to do it. Having no evidence to back up that claim, the police left the case open, but it has never been officially resolved. Joyce Devins died shortly afterward under mysterious circumstances. -In CCW, Victoria tore through her opponents and established herself as a dominant force in sport's entertainment. She married one of her rival's brothers, David Oakes, and corrupted him. No longer the faithful boy scout, he follows Devyl around like a puppy on a leash, obeying her every command. And with his help, and others like Urban Outlaw and CCW's Talent Coordinator, Mr. Raymond, she launched into an all out campaign of pain and destruction. -When CCW closed, Victoria disappeared, reappearing briefly when CCW opened it's doors once again under the guidance of Jacob Brady. With her was a niave teenage girl who went only by the name 'Shadow'. Victoria didn't stay around very long, however, and departed once again. -Now, apparently, Devyl is back again, drawn to CCW for reasons known only to her.
