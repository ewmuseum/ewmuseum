What this is Posted results and commentary for Cyberspace Championship Wrestling’s Shockin’ Sunday Nite of January 30, 2000, from Pittsburgh, including a smother match and an invitational tournament final.
January 30, 2000 from The Pittsburgh Civic Center in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania - Show #105 Big Hoss vs. Samson - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Sid Powers vs. Sylkk - CCW Invitational Tournament Ghettodogg vs. The Wish Master Don "Ceasar" Capone vs. Scotty The Hotty Seven Steele vs. Killer Kyle Jessica Carney vs. Stephanie Black - Smother Match Pepper The Cat vs. Mr. Self Destruct Hellrazor vs. Ice Chamber SSN Results - Januray 30, 2000 Monday, 31-Jan-2000 00:50:39 [detail withheld] writes: Brian: We are live, from Pittsburgh, PA, and LET THERE BE PYRO! *Pyrotechnic madness ensues as fireworks shoot off around the stage, and go on down the aisle to the ring. Camera pans out the audience, and one notable sign says, "Smother me...PLEASE!"* Brian: Hello everyone, I'm Brian Hill. Welcome to the Pittsburgh Civic Arena for another edition of CCW's Shockin Sunday Nite! We've got an interesting show to say the least. A smother match between two women for the number one contendership to the women's title, the CCW Finals for the Invitational Tournament, and a Trans-Atlantic Title match. Also, Pepper the Cat has a match tonight, but he's not even in the arena yet! And I've been informed that neither Kelly nor Jim Bowe have any idea where he is. If he's still at the hospital with Lady Cat, then I, for one, wouldn't hold it against him. Ice Chamber vs. Hellrazor RA: Ladies and gentlemen, the first match of the evening is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring first, Hellrazor! And his opponent, no coming to the ring, Ice Chamber! Brian: Well, the bell rings, and our first match is underway. Hellrazor goes to lock up, but Chamber ducks under and kicks him in the stomach. Chamber sends him to the ropes, and back body drops him when he returns. Chamber's pulling Hellrazor to his feet, put Hellrazor just hit Chamber right in the jaw! Chamber stumbles back from the uppercut, and now Hellrazor is on the attack! Repeated chops to the chest, and now a scoop slam by Hellrazor! He tries to drop a knee to the chest of Chamber, but he rolls out of the way. Chamber now to his feet, and Hellrazor gets up a bit slower, favoring his knee. Chamber whips Hellrazor into the corner, and wraps his knee around one of the ropes! Chamber is pulling on the knee as the referee lays in the count. Chamber lets go after four. Chamber climbs the ropes, but Hellrazor shoves him off before he can throw any punches. Chamber landed hard on his back right there. He's getting up, but Hellrazor comes in with a kick to the back, and Chamber is down! And now Hellrazor's stomping on his back! I think Chamber may be hurt here. He rolls Chamber over and goes for the pin...one, two, kickout. They're both back up to their feet now, and they're trading punches. Chamber blocks one of Hellrazor's, and kicks him in the hurt knee! Hellrazor grabs a rope to keep his balance, but Chamber keeps kicking it! Finally, he sweeps his legs out from under him, and Hellrazor falls to the mat. Chamber turns Hellrazor over, and puts a single-leg Boston Crab on that hurt leg! Hellrazor is in a lot of pain now, folks! But he's crawling towards the ropes! If he can make it, Chamber will have to release the hold! He's almost there...but Chamber pulls him back to the center of the ring! And now Hellrazor is tapping out! RA: Your winner, by way of submission, Ice Chamber! Brian: Well, those two put on a great match there. They both found that one weakness that could lead to a victory, but the experience of Ice Chamber got him the win in the end. And now, Pepper the Cat's match, and if he's here, I haven't heard anything about it! Pepper the Cat vs. Mr. Self Destruct RA: The following match is set for one fall. Coming to the ring first, Mr. Self Destruct! And now his opponent, Pepper the Cat! Brian: Well, Pepper's making his way to the ring now, so that ends that mystery. The bell rings and the match is underway. Mr. Self Destruct making his way towards Pepper, and, WHOA! Pepper just charged at Destruct and hurled him into the corner! And now Pepper is just laying the punches into his head. I think it would be safe to say that Pepper is, if you'll excuse my language, pissed. And now he's stomping him down to the mat! Jeez. I get the feeling this is half being mad at Rushmore, and half being mad that he has to wrestle tonight instead of being at the hospital. I wouldn't want to be Mr. Self Destruct right now. Now Pepper takes a few steps back and goes for a baseball slide dropkick to Mr. Self Destruct, but he has the presence of mind to roll away! Pepper just hit the ringpost with his shin, and that has to hurt. Mr. Self Destruct over now, stomping him a few times while he has the chance. He picks Pepper up to his feet and hits him with a backbreaker! Maybe Pepper's rage got in his way this time, because that one mistake turned this match around in a hurry. Destruct now, picking Pepper back up. He goes for a bulldog, but Pepper shoves him off! Destruct lands on his back, and Pepper looks angry again! If I was Mr. Self Destruct, I would just run at this point. Plain and simple. But Destruct is back up and charging at Pepper with a clothesline! Pepper ducks, scoops him up, and sets him in a tree of woe! Oh, this doesn't look good for Mr. Self Destruct. Pepper kicks him in the stomach a few times, then drops an elbow right to his jaw! Oh god, that one looked painful. And now he's doing it again! Come on, Pepper, that's enough! Mr. Self Destruct falls from the tree of woe, and he looks like he's on Dream Street. Pepper picks him up and hits the Stank Bomb! If he had any chance of beating Pepper tonight, it just went out the window. Pepper covers...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of pinfall, Pepper the Cat! Brian: Well, Pepper just decimated Mr. Self Destruct here. And if Pepper did this to someone who just got in his way, imagine what he'll do to Rushmore! Still, losing this match is in no way indicative of Mr. Self Destruct's skills, I can't think of many people who could have beaten Pepper tonight. Jessica Carney vs. Stephanie - Smother Match for the Number One Contender to CCW Women's Title. RA: The next match of the evening is a Smother Match! *Crowd goes nuts!* Brian: This is gonna be a good one! Fans, you're gonna want to see this match. Jessica Carney put her number one contendership on the line, and the first woman to smother the other unconscious with a body part wins! I can't say I've ever heard of a match being contested under these rules, but hey, I'm not complaining! RA: Coming to the ring at this time, Stephanie! *As Stephanie walks toward the ring Sugar comes from behind her and smashes her upside her head with a purse. Then she takes the bricks out of the purse and leaves.* Brian: Sugar just NAILED Stephanie with a bricked purse to the head! Will Stephanie even be able to continue? RA: And now, her opponent, Jessica Carney! *Savanah confronts Jessica as she is on her way to the ring.* "Your the so called number one contender well, I don't thing so. But I see Kelly has gotten a little yellow on the belly since I last stepped in the ring with her so I must take you on first. So next Thursday me and you in the ring. Let's make it a hmmmmmmmmmmmmmm.... How about an Italian Death match. And if you don't know what that is. I am about to tell you instead of the Texas death match rules it is like this. The first to be thrown out of the ring and to be counted out loses. The only way you can win is to get your opponent to the floor and Them not be able to get into the ring. So do you thing you can handle that. HOLD your answer. Kelly if your listening and I know you are when I get done with Jessica your next.' *Throws the mic at Jessica then attacks her. The attack last for about 30-40 seconds and end with a serious of DDT's on the pavement. Before Savanah walks away.* Brian: And now JESSICA has been attacked! Is this match even gonna make it into the ring?!? Stephanie is finally starting to get up, and she tries to smother the barely conscious Jessica, but the referee has come down the aisle and says he won't start the match until they enter the ring! Stephanie begins dragging Jessica Carney to the ring, and Jessica hasn't moved yet! Now Stephanie rolls her under the bottom rope, follows her in, and the ref rings the bell. Stephanie immediately tries to jump on Jessica's face, but she rolls out of the way! Was she playing opossum? Jessica is up to her feet and Stephanie's on all fours, trying to get up. Jessica kicks Steph a few times in the stomach, and Stephanie finally falls down. Keep in mind that both of these competitors just received rather brutal attacks to the head, so smothering shouldn't take too long. Now Jessica picks Stephanie up and powerslams her. She pulls her up by the hair, and does a, um, a hair-drag takedown! That's as good a phrase as any. Stephanie up again, and ducks a clothesline, and catches Jessica with a fireman's carry takedown. And now she's laying punches into Jessica's face! I think I'm going to start calling Stephanie "Evil Sexy Steph Bear," unless someone objects. She lays down on Jessica's face to try to smother her, but she rolls out from underneath. Now they're both up to their feet, but Jessica grabs Stephanie by the hair and drags her to the turnbuckle. She begins hitting her head against the turnbuckle as the crowd chants along! *Crowd: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten!* Brian: And Steph is down! Jessica starts unbuttoning her top...I think I know what body part she's gonna use! *"Danger Zone" plays and Carnage runs down to the ring and slides in. He picks up Jessica Carney and throws her into the ref. Carnage extends his hand to Stephanie. Stephanie has a puppy love look in her eyes. She hugs Carnage and then he carries her off through the crowd.* Brian: Whoa! What was that? The ref is now calling for the bell...let's hear what the official decision is. RA: Your winner, by disqualification, Jessica Carney! Brian: A DQ? I thought this match could only end with a smothering. Still, a no contest ruling would have left us without a number one contender, so I guess I can understand. By the way, I've just been informed that Pepper the Cat has left the building without saying a word to anyone. I hope everything works out, Pepper. Seven Steele vs. Killer Kyle RA: The next match of the evening is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring first, Seven Steele! And his opponent, now coming to the ring, Killer Kyle. Brian: The bell rings and we're under way. Killer Kyle has recently been asked to tone down his interviews, but that doesn't mean he'll be toning down his matches any! Kyle starts off with a dropkick that takes Steele to the mat. Steele back up now, and Kyle puts him in a sleeper hold. I think it's too early for one of those, though. Yep, Steele breaks the hold with a jawbreaker. And now a clothesline from Steele! Kyle's on the ground, and Steele's heading towards him, but a drop toe hold by Kyle takes him down to the mat! Now Killer Kyle's up, and he drops an elbow on the back of Seven Steele. He pulls Steele up to his feet, and takes him down with a spinebuster. Kyle covers...one, two, kickout. Kyle up, and he hip tosses the rising Seven Steele. Steele's up again and Kyle goes for another one, but Steele blocks it. Now Steele over and he hip tosses Kyle to the mat! And now he grabs the arm of Killer Kyle and begins wrenching on it. Kyle's trying to get out of the hold, but Steele's got it locked on. Now he punches Steels in the face and Steele lets go. Kyle's up, and he goes for a clothesline, but Steele ducks it and hits him with an atomic drop! Now Killer Kyle's hopping around, I think that last hit really got to him. Steele bounces back against the ropes and catches Kyle with a big leg to the face! Steele grabs his legs...and there's a catapult into the corner! Kyle's really dazed now. Steele grabs Kyle and bulldogs him to the mat. He goes for the cover...one, two, thr-no, foot on the ropes. Whew, Kyle saved himself there. Now they're up, and Kyle blocks a right hand by Seven Steele! He hits one of his own, and sends Steele to the ropes. Kyle tries a clothesline, back Steele catches his arm, hops up on his back, and drags him down for a crucifix pin attempt! The ref counts...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of a pinfall, Seven Steele! Brian: Wow, great back-and-forth match there. It looked like Kyle was about to mount a comeback, but Seven Steele managed to nip it in the bud. A great effort by both competitors. The Don vs. Scotty the Hotty RA: The next match is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring first, Scotty the Hotty! And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by Il Famiglia, The Don! *As The Don comes to the ring The rest of I.F. fellow carrying tire irons. When they get to the ring they surround it.* Brian: Whoa. The I.F. mean business tonight, that's for sure. The bell rings, and this match is underway. The Don makes his way towards Scotty, but Scotty takes him down with a dragon screw leg whip. They're back up again, and now Scotty hits a drop kick. The Lightweight Champion is in fine form tonight. Scotty goes for a hip toss on The Don, but The Don lands on his feet and floors Scotty with a clothesline! The Don stomping on the stomach of Scotty, no doubt trying to keep him from getting a full breath of air. The Don picks Scotty up to his feet and hits a fallaway slam! Ouch. Scotty back up, slowly, and The Don begins laying chops into his chest. Now The Don with the whip to the corner, reversed by Scotty, and the ref gets creamed in the corner! It doesn't look like it did The Don much good, either. Scotty wanting to take advantage of this somehow...he goes to the outside for his Title Belt! Now I.F. is telling him to put it down...I'd listen if I were Scotty. Those tire irons look very painful. Now the ref coming to...he sees I.F. threatening Scotty and orders them to leave ringside! They are livid! Of course, Scotty gives the belt back to the timekeeper as soon as he sees the ref is awake. And now, I.F. is leaving. Man, that almost got really ugly. Scotty back in the ring, and he starts stomping on The Don. He pulls The Don up and snap mares him down to the canvas. Scotty goes up to the top rope, and this ought to be good! He goes for a senton backsplash, but The Don rolls out of the way! Oh, Scotty paid the price there, as it looks like he's hurting now. The Don capitalizing, with a series of left hands to the jaw of Scotty the Hotty. Scotty's reeling, and now he falls back against the ropes. The Don charges, and Scotty ducks out of the way, pulling down the top rope! The Don tumbles down to the floor! The referee begins counting, but The Don makes it back into the ring at the seven count. *"Back in Black" begins playing over the PA. Doomsday then makes his way to the ring as both wrestlers are confused. Doomsday then kicks Scotty in the gut and delivers a vicious "Death of a Nation"(also known as a diamond cutter) Scotty is laid out and then Don rushes Doomsday and tries a clothesline, Doomsday ducks under and catches him with the "Death of a Nation". Both men are laid out as Doomsday then just exits the ring to his theme music.* Brian: Oh man, Doomsday is really making a bigger impact now that he has returned. The ref has called for the bell, and let's wait for the final word. RA: Due to outside interference, the referee has ruled this match a no contest. Brian: Ouch. Well, I guess that's the only verdict to come up with, really. Still, things certainly remain unresolved between these two. Ghettodogg vs. Wishmaster RA: The following match is set for one fall. Making his way to the ring first, the Wishmaster! And his opponent, Ghettodogg! *Suddenly, a man in a hooded cloak sneaks up behind Ghettodogg and lays him out with a shovel!* Brian: Whoa, look at that! Ghettodogg is getting pummeled! He just took three or four shots with the shovel...and now this masked guy is leaving. Good riddance. I don't know who that was, but it wouldn't surprise me if Wishmaster was behind this attack. He wants the MOD back, and he wants it bad! Wishmaster now goes down the aisle and carried Ghettodogg back into the ring. He covers...one, two, thr-KICKOUT! How did Ghettodogg kick out there? I thought for sure he was unconscious. I guess a couple of shovel shots aren't enough to keep him down. Wishmaster pulls Ghettodogg up to his feet and hits a reverse fallaway slam. Ghettodogg is just dead weight at this point. He may have enough to kick out, but probably not much else. Wishmaster staying on the attack, pulling Ghettodogg up to his feet and whipping him to the ropes. Wishmaster bounces back against the other ropes and goes for a crossbody, but Ghettodogg ducks it! No, wait, he collapsed under it. Well, anyway, the Wishmaster came up with nothing but mat on that attempt, and he's hurting. Still, it's Wishmaster who's up first, as Ghettodogg is still feeling the effects of that attack earlier. Wishmaster over, but Ghettodogg fires a punch into the stomach of Wishmaster! And another one! He's trying to make a comeback! Ghettodogg with a double-leg takedown, and now he's wailing away on Wishmaster! Ghettodogg looks pissed, alright, because he is just viciously laying those fists into Wishmaster's head. The referee now, telling Ghettodogg to ease off. Ghettodogg grudgingly stops punching Wishmaster, but he's certainly feeling the effects of that assault. Ghettodogg pulls him up to his feet, and floors him with a clothesline! He picks him back up, and he hits a piledriver! Ghettodogg looks to be in firm control again after that pre-match attack...somehow. Now Wishmaster rolls out under the bottom rope to catch a breather. Ghettodogg goes out after him, and shoves his back against the apron! And again! Now Ghettodogg sends him back into the ring. Now he follows the Wishmaster in, but going in second was a mistake, as Wishmaster quickly kicks him in the stomach. And he kicks him again. And he hits the Last Wish! Forget about it! Wishmaster counts...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of pinfall, Wishmaster! Brian: Well, the Wishmaster wins, but I think it might have gone the other way if it weren't for that attack earlier. I guess we'll never know. Sid Powers vs. Sylkk - Invitational Tournament Finals RA: The following match is set for one fall, and will determine CCW's entrant in the Invitational Tournament! Coming to the ring at this time, Sylkk! And now, his opponent, accompanied to the ring by Whoop Ass, Inc., Sid Powers! *Sid Powers music comes on and the lights go off, everyone is puzzled. The lights quickly come back on and Sid is seen standing in the middle of the ring surrounded by WAI with shovels and bats.* Brian: Whoa, Sid Powers came with some heavy artillery. Wait, what's that? *Someone wearing a Sid Powers mask jumps over the guardrail with a baseball bat. He rushes towards the ring, but sees the well-armed members of WAI and thinks better of it. He jumps the guardrail again and disappears into the crowd.* Brian: Well, I bet Sid is glad he brought WAI with him! Sid charges at Sylkk and the bell has rung! Sid with a hard clothesline to Sylkk, and there's another. Sid taking advantage early here, and there's a scoop slam! Sylkk up to his feet, and Sid sends him against the ropes. Sylkk bounces off and hits a crossbody! That took Powers down to the mat. Sylkk now stomping, and now he drops an elbow that connects with the sternum of Sid. He pulls Sid up to his feet and goes for a hurricarana, but Sid powerbombs him! Sid picks him up to his feet and whips him into the corner. Sid runs in and hits a shoulder tackle to the stomach of Sylkk, and he damn near cut him in half! Sylkk is wobbling, and now a backbreaker by Sid! Sid covers, one, two, kickout! Whew, that was close. Sylkk trying to shake off the cobwebs now, as Sid sends him into the ropes. Sid ducks down for a back body drop, but Sylkk sees it coming and drop kicks him right in the top of the head! And Sid is down in a hurry! Sylkk up to the top rope, and he hits a diving splash onto Sid! He hooks the far leg and covers...one, two, kickout! Sylkk really making a comeback here. Sylkk now, in control, starts working on the leg of Sid. He's got a stepover toehold on, and Sid is hurting. Sylkk holds it on for a while, but Sid pushes him off with his other leg. Now Sid's back up to his feet, but Sylkk drop kicks the leg. And again. Sid's really limping now. Sylkk goes for another low drop kick, but Sid moves out of the way! And Sid takes the opportunity to begin stomping on Sylkk! Sylkk finally able to make it back to his feet, and Sid sends him off to the ropes again. Sid catching him with a spinning powerslam...and that one really hurt! Ref counts...one, two, thr-KICKOUT by Sylkk! Man, I was positive the match would be over there. Oh well, I've been wrong before. Sylkk now wearily back to his feet, as Sid is in control. Sid sends Sylkk into the corner again, but he jumps up out of the way when he tries a shoulder charge! Now Sylkk flips over with the sunset flip...one, two, kickout! Great move by Sylkk there, but he couldn't keep him down long enough. Both men back up now, and Sid floors him with a clothesline! Sid doesn't look too happy about almost being pinned there. Sylkk back up, and now Sid hits the Roadkill Supreme! This match is totally over...one, two, three! RA: Your winner, by way of pinfall, Sid Powers! Brian: Still, one hell of a match by Sylkk, and he almost had it won a couple of times. And now for the Main Event. Big Hoss vs. Samson - Trans-Atlantic Title Match RA: Ladies and gentlemen, I have an announcement about the Main Event of the evening. While I'm sure Samson is glad he's the new champion, he can't be glad about how he won it. Well, for CCW, this is Brian Hill saying...hey, wait a minute. *Brian is now talking into his headset.* Brian: Hey, if you guys get Jessica Carney out here, I could pick up in a match with her where Stephanie left off.
