Ricky Thunder Real Name: Ricky Thunder Birthdate: 4-07-82 Hometown: Perth, Australia Height: 5'10 Weight: 219 lbs. Nicknames: n/a Status: Neutral Manager(s): none Current Teams: The Supreme Team Entrance Music: Thunderstruck - Ac/Dc Wrestling Style: Technical, Submissionist Signature Moves: Impaler DDT, Senton Splash, German Suplex, Snap Release Suplex, Spinning Back Elbow, Figure Four, Texas Cloverleaf, Sharpshooter, Frog Splash, Tree of Woe, Spinning Neckbreaker, Brainbuster, Jack-knife Powerbomb, Russian Legsweep Set Up Move: Crashing Thunder (Shooting Star Press ) Finisher: Rolling Thunder (Tequila Sunrise ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Independent Champion (1x) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: 2002 King of Extreme Other Federations: NSWA Other Federation Awards: Cruiserweight Champ, 2 time tag champ, Heavyweight Champ when contract was cancelled Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Ricky grew up in Perth, where he was taught by his dad the ways of the ring. At the age of 16 he was booked in his first match in an actual arena. He had practiced for years in his yard with friends. During this show, he took a bump that jammed his spine pretty badly. He was out of commission until the age of 18 when he decided to try it again. After several months of rehab, and a couple of years when NOBODY would touch him due to insurance risks. A small fed decided to give him a tryout. Ricky was quite successful in this fed, until one day they decided to cut him off and cancel his contract. When the fans got wind of this, they decided to stand behind Ricky on this one. In losing their fan base the NSWA went under for good. It was only a matter of time before CCW caught wind of these rumours and decided to give Ricky a chance. Was the chance offered as a hand extended to a rewarding career for Ricky or was it a way for CCW to make more money and expand BACK! into a market that has been left untouched since the days of CCW great SuMA? Only time will tell. CCW Record Singles 7-1-1 The loss coming when Mr. Gobstopper, interfered, causing Ricky to be DQ'd. The no contest coming against Ebola, when Carnage intervened.
