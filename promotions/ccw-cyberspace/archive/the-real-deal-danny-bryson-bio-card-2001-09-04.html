What this is Bio card for the CCW wrestler "The Real Deal" Danny Bryson, with stats, moves and a background story: a face technician with a baseball-to-wrestling back-story.
Name: "The Real Deal" Danny Bryson Hometown: Phoenix, Arizona Email: [detail withheld] Weight: 300 lbs. Height: 6'10" Age: 26 Status: Face Manager/Valet: None Wrestling Style: Technical Theme Music: "Hallo Spaceboy" by David Bowie Singles Record: 0-0-0 Hardcore Record: 0-0-0 Tag Team Record: 0-0-0 Championships: None Favorite Quote: "Once the dust has settled, once the deal is done..." Favorite Weapon: None Debut: September 2001 Superstar Background: Bryson was born in a small Arizona town and moved to Pheonix when he was 5. As he grew up, Bryson had 2 big loves, one was baseball, the other wrestling. He tried out for ball team after ball team but no one would take him. Finally he saw a poster offering places in a small Pheonix federation. The organisers saw his height and strength as a positive advantage and signed him up. They soon became aware of his other skill - mic work!He seemed to be able to play any crowd and this was worked by the federation owners. After working here for a couple of years, he was signed up to a big time company at the age of 20. Bryson rightly saw this as a chance to cement his name in history, but unfortunately, this was not to be. Although he was given a shot, his gimmick, the silent gravedigger, always held him back. He could not demonstrate his skills with the mic and found it hard to rise in the rankings. He left a year later. He worked for various promotions from then on, they always signed him on the same gimmick and the gimmick always held him back. When Bryson heared he got a chance to audition for the CCW, he swore he would put that right. No gimmicks to hold him back. For once he would get the chance to be himself. Finishing Move The Deal Breaker - Bryson mounts the buckle and jumps off, hitting a ddt on his opponent as he falls.
