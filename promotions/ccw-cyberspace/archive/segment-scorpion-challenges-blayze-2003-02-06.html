CCW segment: Scorpion challenges Blayze

6 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scorpion challenges Blayze Date: 2-6-03 Show: Lockdown “Enter Sandman” blasts over the PA system as the lights in the arena fade out, flickers from flashbulbs and fire from liters are glowing the now darkened arena. JD: What the hell is going on now?! BJS: Isn’t that... SLEEP WITH ONE EYE OPEN GRIPPING YOUR PILLOW TIGHT… EXIT LIGHT… ENTER NIGHT… TAKE MY HAND… WERE OFF TO NEVER, NEVER LAND! Smoke billows out from the stage and Scorpion peers through the smoke to a loud ovation. Scorpion heads down the ramp and into the ring where the crowd continues to cheer on Scorpion as the music stops. “NEA! NEA! NEA!” JD: Scorpion is here and ready has a piece to say obviously! BJS: Who? Scorpion picks up the microphone as the crowd continues to cheer the initials of the faction Scorpion lead the last time CCW was open. Scorpion: You know something? I don’t care what wrestling promotion I was with when CCW closed…because there is nothing in the world that can top off coming down that ramp and hearing the real hardcore mother fucking wrestling fans that CCW has! BJS: Suck up. The fans explode into the “CC-DUB! CC-DUB! CC-DUB!” chants. Scorpion: I have had a lot of time off lately because I was retired from wrestling and when I retired I said I would only return if CCW did. And damn this monster has been awakened from the dead! Once again, the chants of “ CC-DUB! CC-DUB! CC-DUB!” rings out. Scorpion: Now that I got that off my chest I came out here for one reason only and that is to finish off where I left off in CCW. I was climbing and climbing and climbing the ladder of success, I was reaching the top of the mountain and then the door was slammed in my face as CCW closed. Now when CCW closed I was pretty pissed off, but I was more pissed off that my chance to be a legend in CCW was taken away from me. But now that climb to the top is back on track and I’m aiming for the top now. “ SCORPION! SCORPION! SCORPION!” chants begin to form throughout the arena Scorpion: There is an old saying in the wrestling business “to be the man, you gotta beat the man.” I’m going to live by that saying and I’m going to issue a challenge here tonight in Iowa City to one of the baddest men in CCW’s history, Blayze. “CC-DUB! CC-DUB! CC-DUB!” Scorpion: Not only am I issuing a challenge to Blayze, I’m also issuing a warning to you Blayze. You will never know when I will strike, how I will strike or where I will strike. You have faced many greats in CCW but none will compare to me. The clock is ticking Blayze, your time is running out. “Enter Sandman” blasts over the PA system as Scorpion walks out of the ring to the echo of cheers ringing throughout the arena View Scorpion 's Biography Back
