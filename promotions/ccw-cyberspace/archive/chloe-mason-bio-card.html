Chloey Mason Real Name: Chloe Mason Birthdate: February 25th, 1985 Hometown: Seattle, Washington Height: 5'6' Weight: 105 Nicknames: Status: Face Entrance Music: 'No Matter What' by Toya Information Wrestling Style: Aerial Set Up Move: Deja Vu (Alternate Finisher) (Used when an opponent picks up Chloe to slam her to the mat... Chloe counters the slam by spinning out of their grasp, grabbing her opponents head in a headlock, then bringing them down to the ground with a devastating DDT, all in one fluid motion.)) Finisher: Running Neckbreaker Title History: none Award History: none Other Federations: none Other Federation Awards: none Quotes History Chloe is an orphan who grew up on the streets of Seattle, She doesn't know her parents or anything about where she comes from. Through most of her young, teenage life, Chloe had gone the way of most rebellious kids. Taking up a mostly unsuccessful job as a teenage cat-burgler, she was heading for a lot of trouble. The cops had nailed her on several occassions, and she was quickly heading for the big house. Everything changed for her when she was caught breaking into an antique store. Lacey Skye Morris caught and stopped her from stealing anything, and then she witnessed a psychotic serial killer break into the store and attempt to kill them all. She soon fell in with Lacey, and the antique store's owner, Jonathan, in an attempt to clean herself up and go straight. Lacey got Chloe into the wrestling biz, and inbetween matches, she helps maintain the antique store. Moves Chloe is all about speed, quickness, and not letting her opponents lay a hand on her.
