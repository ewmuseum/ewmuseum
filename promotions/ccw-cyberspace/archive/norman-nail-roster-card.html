Set Up Move: Belly to Back Suplex (Belly to Back Suplex..duh ) Finisher: Nail Driver ((Its an inverted DDT...you put him under your arm like a DDT, but then lift him into the air and drop him on his head like a ddt...edges move...Wrestlmania 2000 the game has it shown if you need to see ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: nOnE Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: nOnE Other Federations: none Other Federation Awards: none Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Norman Nail was born and raised in Fresno California. He attended Buchanon High School and was an amature wrestler there. He was on the Varsity Team from his freshmen to his senior year. His Sophmore year he placed 3rd in state, his Junior year he went on to place 2nd, and his Senior year he was the state champion at the 189 pound weight class. After High School he went on to Wrestle at Fresno State and his Junior and Senior years there placed first in the nation. His plan was to go on at the age of 22 and wrestle in the olympics, but because of a devistating knee injury, he was unable to tryout for the United States Team and was forced to watch the olympics just like everyone else, from his living room tv. When he was 23, he got a call from a professional wrestling recruiter who wanted Norman to try his luck in professional wrestling. Norman was at first confused and not sure if he wanted to risk his job and life on trying to do something that so many dream of. But Norman took the chance and moved to Atlanta to wrestle in a small indie federation under the name of Bob Bad. After only 6 months there, he moved on to a better federation and decided to kill off the Bob Bad character and took his real name, Norman Nail, back. He wrestled there for about 6 more months and now is now signing a CCW contract. Many have dreamed of being in CCW, one of the greatest federations on the planet. Few have been able to be apart of the federation full of greats...Norman Nail will get that chance, and the sky's the limits according to Norman, and nothing is going to stop him from achieving his goals in the CCW.
