What this is The application page for the relaunched fed (G2X) explaining terms of joining, waiting and re-application periods, and listing the wrestler details and sample roleplay requested.
All information provided herein no becomes sole property of CCW Enterprises, and more specifically CCW: Generation 2X. By applying you are agreeing to have read and abide by all CCW rules and regulations. Please be as detailed as possible as Roster space WILL BE limited. We will hold applications for UP TO 30 days, if the roster is full. If you do not hear anything from someone on our staff then your application has been rejected and therefor, you must reapply, AFTER 15 days. DO NOT contact staff regarding your application, that will be grounds for immediate termination. Thank you for your interest in CCW: G2X A normal response time will be about 3 days, and you will know if there is a spot for you or if you will be added to the waiting list at that time. Your Name AIM E-mail address? Singles or Tag? Wrestler(s) Name Wrestler(s) Age Wrestler(s) Hometown Theme Music Detailed Description(s) Height(s) Weight(s) Alignment Face Heel Neutral Manager or Valet List your wrestler's favorite moves. List up to 1 of your wrestler's finishing moves and 2 Setup Moves (With Descriptions). Please give us a brief description of your wrestler's personality. Please give us a detailed bio on your wrestler. Please give us an example roleplay. Make this count, because as roster space is limited, this is your key in or out. You decide.
