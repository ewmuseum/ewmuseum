Josh Marshall Real Name: Joshua Scott Marshall Birthdate: July 16th, 1977 Hometown: Chicago, Il Height: 6'3' Weight: 223 Nicknames: Status: Face Entrance Music: 'Hollow Again' by Project 86 Information Wrestling Style: All Arounder Set Up Move: The Facelift (Double Arm Underhook Brainbuster) Finisher: The Snap Shot (Cradle Piledriver) Title History: Lightweight Champion Award History: none Other Federations: none Other Federation Awards: none Quotes History A child was born on July 16th, 1976 to the loving parents in Joann and Herbert Marshall, named Joshua Scott Marshall. His childhood was nothing of an ordinary one, as his mother saw potential in him for being a runway model, or something along those lines. Her constant pushing didn’t really bother Joshua so much in the beginning, seeing as how he loved doing things alongside him mother, but that would all change after several years of being told what to do all the time. Joshua went on to gain success in many ways as a youngster, starring in many clothing commercials and winning numerous child model contest and being told by several modeling agencies that if he kept up his work that he would be awarded a contract as soon as he had graduated from High School. Joshua makes his way through school and graduates from High School at the top of his class; an honor student and captain of the school football team, but his dreams weren’t set on being the runway model that his mother had so dearly deserved of him. He left home without his parents consent and joined up with a largely popular Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, working there for several months and acquiring training from the veteran in Jesse Ramey and soon after beginning to date his daughter Michelle, Josh achieved glory after winning the CCW Lightweight Title and soon after going mysterious for several months, in which he took up a modeling career until he was contacted by Jesse about a job opening in a rival promotion of his called RUSH Wrestling. Josh made his way to the headquarters of RUSH and had a very intriguing interview with the bosses to say the least and was shortly after hired under a long contract. After a short time with RUSH Wrestling, that was in the eyes of many, going to be Josh’s breakout in wrestling. Something went terribly wrong and he was released from his contract. After sitting on the shelf for nearly a month, Josh was picked up by $oldout Wrestling. $W floundered before it even opened and Josh was forced into going out into the unemployment line once again, or was he? Contacted shortly after he terminated his contract with $W, Josh found out an old promotion that he made his start in was reopening and he jumped on the bandwagon just as quickly as anyone else would. The only words that could bring Josh running, Cyberspace Championship Wrestling! Moves Missile Dropkick, Running Powerslam, Asai Moonsault, Running Lariat, Snap Suplex, T-Bone Suplex, Superkick, Tornado DDT.
