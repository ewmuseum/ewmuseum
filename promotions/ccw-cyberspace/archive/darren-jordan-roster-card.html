Darren Jordan Real Name: Darren Lamar Jordan Birthdate: February 21st, 1974 Hometown: Compton, South Central L.A. Height: 6'4 Weight: 278 lbs. Nicknames: Death's Right Handed Man Status: Heel Manager(s): Blayze Current Teams: Menace II Society Entrance Music: Crawling - Mobb Deep Wrestling Style: Powerhouse, Hardcore Brawler Signature Moves: Corporate Clutch(Cobra Clutch), Reverse Powerbomb To Faceplant, Reverse Brainbuster DDT, Double-Handed Chokeslam, Stalling Release German Suplex, 2nd Rope Piledriver, Roundhouse Kicks, Verteabreaker, Osaka Street Cutter, Gorilla Press Slam, Full Nelson Dragon Suplex, Fisherman's DDT, Diving Elbow, T-Bone Suplex Over The Rope, Downward Spiral, Elevated Neckbreaker, Gore, Running Boot To Face Set Up Move: The Death Row (Spinning Implant DDT ) Finisher: Corporal Punishment (Backdrop Driver(Nova's Old ECW Finisher) ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Tag Team Champion(2x) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: None Other Federation Awards: None Handler Information Handler Name: Rob AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] We all know the story of Jerome 'Blayze' Jordan losing his mother at a tender age, and his brother Darren taking custody of his younger brother. What we didnt know is what happened to Darren after Blayze joined CCW. Darren continued to hang with the Crips, he later moved to Phoenix, Arizona and was an OG of the Crips down there. He also worked as Assistant Manager at the GAP but was later fired, then charged with Money Laundering and Theft, stealing Items and shipping them to Crip members, also making business. In 2001, he was arrested on an attempted murder and arson case on a former drgu dealer who never payed up over 15,000 grand to Darren. Darren was sentenced to 5 years in prison at the Arizone State Penetentiary, community service but was actually offered release on bail after a year completed. Once Blayze bought out the CCW Division 2 contracts, he later sold them to numerous feds in return for money which he was able to use to bail his brother out of Jail. Currently, Darren has been given a job as Blayze's personal assistant, he is now living the high life with his brother on his side as they form the current Tag Team Champions, Menace II Society So Far, Darren has already made impact even though alot backstage are not to pleased with the sudden and non-deserving entry of Darren Jordan in CCW. In his first ever match, he teamed with former World Champion and now retired Enigma and made history by winning a title in his first ever match, that being the tag titles. Once Enigma retired from CCW, Darren was dumped into Singles Competition where he battled mostly rookies and came out with a decent singles record. Now Back into the spotlight, Darren is now a two time tag team champion holding the tag titles with his brother Blayze.
