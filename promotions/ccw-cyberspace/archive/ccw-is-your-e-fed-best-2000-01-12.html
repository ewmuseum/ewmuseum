Is your E-Fed Best? You're Invited...

12 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyberspace Championship Wrestling would like to join forces with any e-fed looking for a chance to show of their talent and match writing skills for a one time only PPV. The card would actually be a tournament. A 16 seed tournament with 8 guys from each of the two e-feds. It would be CCW on one side of the bracket and then your e-fed on the other side of the bracket and there would be no inter-fed matches until the final match where the top guy from CCW would face the top guy from your e-fed. Then the two fed heads get together and decided how the final round of the tournament be booked. This tournament will be hosted on the CCW Homepage which is located at http://www.angelfire.com/al/ccwhomepage . CCW's first round matches will be played out accordingly using our system, while your e-fed's matches would be played out however you usually play them out. The final winner would be considered Universal Champion of the Tournament and we could have this tournament every month with different e-feds participating each month. Or something. Anyone interested in this idea, reply to this posting and supply your email address and Homepage information. Or email me at [detail withheld] I think this will be a fun and enjoyable experience for both e-feds which take part and will also get exposure to your fed. Thanks! Owner of CCW, [name withheld]
