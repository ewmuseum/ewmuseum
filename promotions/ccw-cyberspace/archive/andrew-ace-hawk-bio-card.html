Andrew 'Ace' Hawk Real Name: Andrew Hawk Birthdate: May 2nd 1978 Hometown: Hollywood, California Height: 6'6' Weight: 260 Nicknames: The Ace of Wrestling Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'Born to be Wild', by Steppenwolf Information Wrestling Style: High Flying, Technical, Submissionist Set Up Move: Cannon Kick (Big Boot to the face) Finisher: Harley Grip (Sharpshooter) Title History: 2x CCW World Heavyweight Champion, 4x CCW Transatlantic Champion, 1x CCW Independent Champion, 1x ASW World Heavyweight Champion, 1x XWA World Heavyweight Champion, 1x FWF Hardcore Champion, and many others Award History: CCW Rookie and Heavyweight Wrestler of the Year (2000), Match of the Year (5-tier cage match - 2000), 2001 Most Hated Wrestler Other Federations: ASW, FWF, XWA, OSW, UWF Other Federation Awards: none Quotes History Ace is a brazilian wrestler, that came to America after years of domination in the brazilian wrestling scene. As a 2nd generation wrestler, Ace had his father as a rolemodel, but when he was 15, his father was killed in a double-cross by a crooked fed owner and his younger champion. That was when Ace decided to work hard to become a wrestler and avenge his father's death. Since he came to America, Ace has found success, but some bad luck too. Losing the CCW World Heavyweight Title to the biggest CCW jobber of all time due to injury and being fired and banned from the company by a rival wrestler-turned president, Ace decided to give up wrestling altogether on the december of 2001, after one last brief and unsuccessful CCW stint. Since then, Ace found a quiet place to live in the mountains, forgetting about his past. But a letter by his former enemy Sylkk made the call to the ring too interesting. With renewed interest, Ace came back to CCW as the new Operations Manager, to make things run smoothly. However, his body has been an issue. Throughout his career, Ace was known as a daredevil, one who would go near the brink of death to win something. Because of that, several nagging injuries made him change his fighting style. The question that won't last is: How these injuries will play on Ace's renewed career? Only time will say...
