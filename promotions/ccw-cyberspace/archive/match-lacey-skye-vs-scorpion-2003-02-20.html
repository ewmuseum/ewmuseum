CCW match: Lacey Skye vs. Scorpion

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lacey Skye vs. Scorpion Date: February 20th, 2003 Show: Lockdown JD: Well, ladies and gents, it's time to continue our Indy tournament! Lacey Skye Morris and Scorpion are meeting in just a couple moments to determine who goes on to fight for the belt. BJS: It's gonna be Scorpion, obviously. JD: Why? Because Lacey's a woman and can't handle herself against the men.' BJS: Well... Yeah... But that's not why I'm picking Scorpion. JD: Then why? BJS: Because Commissioner Raymond already stated that Lacey won't be getting a title shot while he's around. Nayt Willems: This contest is scheduled for one fall! On his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 235 pounds, from New York City, New York... Scorpion!!! 'Enter Sandman' by Metalica hits, pyros erupt from the titantron and the stage, as Scorpion walks down the isle and steps into the ring amidst huge cheers and loud pops from the audience. BJS: This guy needs to get the NEA going again. Wait, never mind that. The NEA sucks. JD: What's wrong with you? BJS: Ah, nothing. I just don't like these two very much. This Scorpion punk thinks he can beat Blayze and take his title away. Like that's ever gonna happen. The only reason I'm still here is because I might get to see Lacey's tits. Nayt Willems: And his opponent, wighing in at 140 pounds, from Stockton, California... Lacey Skye Morris!!! 'Star of Tears' by Joanne Hogg begins to play as Lacey comes out of the back to a huge ovation, herself. She smiles briefly at the crowd on her way down to the ring, climbs the turnbuckle, and raises one arm into the air. She allows the many cameras to capture her before turning and looking down at Scorpion. BJS: And what a glory hog she is! You don't see Koriann or Brad Lamen pulling anything like that, do you? Trying to hog all the attention. JD: Actually... BJS: Shut up! What do you know? Vinny's about to signal for the bell when, Fithos Lusec Wecos Vinosec' hits. Lacey turns and looks sharply up at the stage entrance as Commissioner Raymond comes walking out, dressed in a nice business suit; expensive walking cane included. She glares at him as he walks down to ringside, the intensity in her eyes like a burning flame. BJS: And Scorpion strikes first with a Jumping Neck Snap on a distracted Lacey! That was awesome! Scorpion and Raymond must be working together! This rules!! Vinny calls for the bell as Raymond reaches ringside and starts circling around outside. JD: I don't think so... I think Scorpion realizes what a dangerous opponent Lacey can be, and just took advantage of the situation. Scorpion sends Lacey into the ropes. She comes back, leaps, and connects with a big lariat on Scorpion. Scorpion quickly regains his footing, as does Lacey, and sends out several right hands, eaching connecting with the strawberry blond's face. Lacey blocks another incoming punch nad then lashes out with several right hands of her own, knocking Scorpion back a bit, and then leaps into the air with a spinning Roundhouse that takes Scorpion off his feet again. Keeping control, Lacey guides Scorpion to his feet, kicks him in the stomach, and then plants him into the canvas with a DDT. JD: Nice follow through and sequence by Lacey. She's really determined to win this one. And Commissioner Raymond, of course, is hovering around outside the ring like some kind of deranged lunatic. Lacey is back on her feet quickly and sends several stomps down into Scorpion. BJS: Commissioner Raymond is NOT a lunatic! He endorses Brad Lamen and Koriann, you know? Scorpion is on his feet again, and manages to reverse an Irish Whip as Lacey tries throwing him to the ropes. Lacey comes back and Scorpion Flapjacks her onto the top rope, Guillotining her as she falls neck first. Lacey hits the canvas and begins coughing. Scorpion kicks her in the face and knocks her through the ropes to the concrete below. Vinny starts to count as Scorpion follows her out. JD: And there goes Raymond... Looks like he wants a close-up of the action over there... Lacey gets to her feet as Scorpion nears her and lashes out with several strong punches. Scorpion takes them, and then sends a sharp kick down into Lacey's kneecap. BJS: That was another good move! Kneecap attacks are cool because you can seriously fuck-up somebody's leg for good! Lacey buckles to one knee, but immediately throws a punch right into Scorpion's stomach divesting him of some much needed air. She then steps forward and smashes the toe of her boot right into Scorpion's groin. Scorpion nearly drops, but Lacey grabs him and tosses him back into the ring before shooting a look over at the Commissioner who's not six feet away. JD: And check out this stare down! There's definitely no love lost between Lacey or our Commissioner. BJS: Well, Lacey's wasting time. Scorpion's recovering in the ring. She shouldn't let Raymond get to her. Lacey gives the Commissioner a few choice words before turning and climbing back into the ring. She approaches Scorpion to pick him up, only to be on the receiving end of a crotch punch as Scorpion returns the favor from earlier. Lacey covers up, which allows Scorpion to take her down with a T-bone Suplex. He then steps on her stomach, grabs her arm, and initiates an Armlock submission. JD: Vinny's checking for a tap out right now... But our resident hottie, stubborn as ever, is hanging in there. Scorpion releases the hold and cuffs Lacey on the back of the head with his hand as he lets her up. Lacey lashes out angrily and decks Scorpion right across the face. Scorpion fires back a punch of his own, but Lacey grabs his arm, jerking him downward. While holding on, she fires three swift kicks in brutal succession into Scorpion's face. Scorpion stumbles backwards. Lacey grabs him, launches him to the ropes, but is reversed again. Lacey comes back off the ropes and surprises the hell out of Scorpion with a Jump-Swinging DDT. Lacey is quickly up and stalking him from behind, waiting for him to get up. The fans continue to cheer for both competitors. JD: Scorpion is groggily back on his feet, turns around... And Lacey gets him! SKYE TWIST!!! It's all over! She's going for the cover! Vinny drops to make the count. 1... 2... 3... JD: What the hell!? Raymond grabs a hold of Vinny's leg right before the hand comes down for the three, and pulls him out of the ring. Vinny gives Raymond a 'What the hell are you doing?' look. Raymond merely smiles. BJS: YES! Our Commissioner rules!!! JD: What are you talking about!? Lacey had this match won! BJS: I believe our Commissioner is pointing out the fact that Vinny's count is off. We can't have our ref's doing shoddy work now, can we? JD: There's nothing wrong with Vinny's count! Lacey, meanwhile, has noticed that the count didn't go down because of the Commissioner. She immediately gets to her feet and leaps out of the ring. Raymond sees her coming and takes off running, the strawberry blond in hot pursuit. They run around the ring and towards the rampway. Lacey's almost caught up with Raymond when Scorpion suddenly leaps out of the ring and Clotheslines Lacey to the concrete, nearly beheading her. BJS: HA! Now this is great stuff, Doyle! I love it when the Commissioner's plans come to fruition! The crowd boos as Raymond stops running, points and laughs at the fallen woman. Scorpion picks Lacey up and tosses her back into the ring. Following her in, he quickly smashes his knee into Lacey's face as she tries to get up, and then grabs her for... JD: The SCORPION DDT!! Lacey's down and barely moving! This one is over! BJS: Or it would be if Scorpion was gonna pin her. It looks like he's not done yet, though... Lacey tries to pick herself, but doesn't have much success until Scorpion helps her. He gets her back on her feet, and then... JD: TAIL WHIP!!! He just put out Lacey's lights for good with that one! He's making the cover and Vinny with the count! 1... 2... 3...! BJS: YES! YOU LOSE, LACEY! HA-HAH! I told you, Doyle. Nayt Willems: Your winner... SCORPION!!! 'Enter Sandman' begins to play as Vinny raises Scorpions hand in victory. Scorpion drops out of the ring, giving the ring-entering Commissioner a look, before heading out of the ringside area, victory in hand. Raymond approaches Lacey, who is now just beginning to show signs of consciousness. JD: Now what the hell is this? Hasn't Lacey suffered enough for one night? BJS: No. Raymond grabs a mic and steps over the recovering beauty. Raymond: Well, well, well... What a shocking surprise, huh, Lacey? You lost!!! The crowd boos the hell out of Raymond. Raymond: I knew you couldn't do it. I TOLD you, you couldn't do it. But did you listen to me? Of course not. Now, let's see here... You lost in the Indy tournament... You lost the tag match the first week you were here. I guess that leaves me only one thing to do. You're demoted Lacey. Right now, you're starting over at the very bottom. Which means... **laughs** You won't even be eligible to fight for the vaunted women's title at the upcoming PPV! The fans continue to boo. Raymond: Now, I'm not the cold and heartless man most people believe. I'm offering you another chance. Work with me, Lacey. And although you will remain without a belt for a good while, you will learn respect and what it takes to be a winner. Lacey pushes herself up off the canvas, not even looking at the Commissioner. The fans start chanting her name, giving her support. Raymond: What do you say, Lacey? Do you now see the error of your ways? Raymond holds out his hand in an offering of peace. Lacey looks at it, then up at him. The fans react, wanting Lacey to kick his ass. JD: Don't do it, Lacey! BJS: Do it, Lacey! Lacey reaches out and grabs his hand, smiling a bit and shaking it. The fans explode into boos as a big smile widens over Raymond's face. BJS: YES! JD: Dammit!! Raymond pats her on the head with his free hand and then tries to pull away, but finds that he can't. Lacey has a firm grip on his hand. She pulls him back into him and smiles brightly, right before kneeing him in the groin, and then planting him in the middle of the ring with a Skye Twist. The fans explode into cheers as Lacey pops back up on her feet and trash talking the hurt Commissioner. JD: YES! BJS: Dammit!! 'Star of Tears' plays as Lacey leaves the ring and heads for the back, the crowd popping all the way. JD: Well, although the outcome of the match was quite suspect, everything turned out okay. BJS: Sez you! Lacey should've joined Raymond! Besides, Scorpion would've kicked out of that Skye Twist move anyway. He doesn't lose to Women. JD: Except on our very first show. BJS: Will you SHUT-UP! JD: **smiles** Nope. View Lacey Skye Morris 's Biography Back
