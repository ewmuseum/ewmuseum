News Column - TheCCW.com Online and the Lethal Lottery

c. 31 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A news column from the fed’s front-office writer reporting the theccw.com launch, new wrestlers, the first-round matches of the Lethal Lottery tag tournament, interfed talk and a returning star.
CCW HOME THE BOWE VALIANT THOUGHTS OOC BOARD TOP WRESTLER RELATED NEWS So, you actually doubted that we'd get "TheCCW.com" online? Humph, oh ye of little faith! Oh, wait... I didn't set it up, President [name withheld] did. Props to him, hehe. Four new wrestlers will join our ranks this week. This is a good start for a roster that has spots to fill, as we are not only receiving more applications, but also better quality applications. I will be in charge this weekend, as President [name withheld] will be out of town. Please send all your angles for SSN to [detail withheld]. The lethal lottery has made for quite some interesting matchups. The winners of this tag team tournament will get a number one contenders shot at Cyberslam. The first round matches for this Thursday are: -The Valiant One and Lacey Skye vs. Enigma and Koriann -The Super Unknown and Cianan vs. Brad Lamen and Spider Other participants include John Jupiter and Akiko, Danny Bryson and Devyl, The Vietnam Veteran and Damsel, Eric Clark and Billy Connors, Jesse Ramey and Kevin Newcastle, Ebola and Jake Savage, and Fenix and Neurosis. Correct me if I'm wrong, but hasn't The Vietnam Veteran has been on an indefinite leave? And what of Ebola and Billy Connors, who may be called on to defend the United States in real life at a day's notice? These booking problems should fix themselves though, as teams will be allowed to switch around to allow for the best competition. There's been talk of interfed play once again. The name that has turned up is PCW, in which we have a lot of stock with former CCWers. President [name withheld] and The Phantom Booker have been discussing possibilites. More details will be passed along as they are revealed. A recent post on the interview board has drawn speculation as to the return of a former CCW superstar. The identity of this person is unknown, except by me. Some say it could be someone who is currently in PCW. . . That's all the news for this week. Sorry all, been busy. Peace out.
