Show #193 - March 15, 2001

15 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Card for Lockdown of March 15, 2001 from Minneapolis, followed by the booker’s per-match angle, interference and prevent-interference notes for the participants.
March 15, 2001 from The Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota - Show #193 "Passive Aggressive" Paul Arovnik pinned Scotty Showtime - CCW Trans-Atlantic & Hardcore Championships El Mierda pinned Neurosis - CCW Independent Championship Johnny Flame pinned "The Darkside" Michael Crowe - CCW Lightweight Championship Akiko pinned Trinity - CCW Women's Championship Jack "Crash" Williams pinned Brad Lamen - Hardcore Match The Valiant One defeated Ricky Coyote - DQ "Pegleg" Paula Hampton pinned Chastity Bytch Urban submitted Big Smooth Lockdown results for March 15, 2001 From the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota Anytime backstage attack by The Valiant One: If Malcolm Next shows up anytime during the card or if you can put him in, I would like for TVO to attack him backstage or something. Urban DESTROYS Big Smooth, wins by submission **Angle by Urban: After the match Urban Shouts "You want Hardcore!!!" HE rolls out the ring sets up a table, lights it on fire gets on top with Big Smooth and delivers an Emerald Fushion to Big Smooth through it. After he puts Smooth through the table he rolls him back in the ring. He points to the rafters and lowers a huge wooden X. He places Big Smooth on it upside down as it raises above the ring. Urban strikes his cruxified pose as the lights go out and purple lights engulf the arena as his music plays. (Note)-Big Smooth is fired if he does not roleplay for Sunday. “Pegleg” Paula Hampton pins Chastity Bytch **Angle by Pegleg: It's an in-ring interference during the match. If there's a point where the referee isn't watching, then Paula will hit Chastity over the head with her Championship Belt (if she's still got it). Anytime Paula is around, she's got her belt with her. So, if Trinity does any sort of attack (or anyone else, for that matter) then Paula could naturally be stripped of said belt. Not much of an angle...but hey. The Valiant One defeats Ricky Coyote by DQ **Interference by Malcolm Next: Midway through the match, TVO gains an advantage and downs Coyote with a power move. The lights go out and four candles appear at the corners of the ring. As the lights slowly come back on, the four Apostles have any members of The Strangers tied up, and they are staring at TVO. Malcolm Next is standing behind TVO with a chair, and as TVO turns around, he is leveled by the chair. The referee call for the bell, but Next picks up TVO and gives him his variation of the Valiant Bomb on top of the chair. An Apostle tosses him a mic, and Next says, "That was to get even for last Thursday....and this (gives TVO the Free At Last on top of the chair)...is because I don't like to stay even. See you Sunday, and don't be a Stranger..." Next drops the mic and 'I Have a Dream' plays as the Apostles exit behind their leader. (Note)-Make sure that TVO’s angle happens as well as yours, although it doesn’t have to be at the same time. He could attack you backstage later on in the evening or something. Jack Williams pins Brad Lamen – Hardcore match **Prevent interference by Jack Williams: Eric Williams will accompany Jack to ringside, and will help prevent interference. Eric is wielding a baseball bat, and will use it. Akiko pins Trinity – Women’s Championship **Prevent interference by Johnny Flamer: Johnny's going to come out to ringside with Akiko to prevent interference by Crowe or anyone else. He'll be very protective of his lady love of course...and will fight very very ferociously on her behalf. **Angle by TVO: The Valiant One brings a bouquet of flowers to the ring when Akiko comes to the ring. It has a card that says "Dear Akiko, Good luck tonight, I hope you can decide to become a stranger. -The Valiant One" (Note)-This should cause some unrest, as Johnny Flame already has Akiko as a girlfriend. Do what you will between the two men, but make Akiko appear to be at least somewhat swayed. Johnny Flame pins Michael Crowe – Lightweight title **Angle by Flamer: Requested Moves- Out-speed Crowe. Hit the Ladder Move (Double Swanton with a Twist) as early as possible. Watch out for potential interferences. Keep him outside of the ring. Thanks (Note)-Have the match be close. Mention that if Crowe needs to participate more in order to get up to the higher caliber. **Backstage attack afterwards: The scene opens up backstage in the Strangers Locker Room after the Flame vs Crowe match. Blayze is inside working out, doing a bunch of push-ups when a loud knock is heard at the door Voice: Mr. Blayze, can you please open up Blayze thinks its a police officer who was guided to his locker room. Knocks continue Voice: Please open the door Blayze gets and takes a folding chair with him and stands on the side of the door Voice: Open up immediately Blayze: Come in, Its open The person opens the door and right as it steps into the room Blayze levels the person with a vicious chair shot. Blayze is then shocked as it was a CCW official he just hit. Blayze gets down and tends to the Official trying to revive him but then in a instant Blayze is whacked across the head with a lead pipe and his knocked out cold. 3 people then surround the fallen Blayze Thug: I told you Blayze, we could be anywhere and we can manipulate anyone to do as we say. This isnt the end Blayze, the end will only come when we paralyze your ass! All 3 jet the premises leaving Blayze and the official unconscious El Mierda pins Neurosis – Independent Title **Prevent interference by Neurosis: Neurosis is going to be wathcing, and be on guard for Mierda using his stun gun. Neurosis fully expects Mierda to bring it and attempt to use it, so he is going to be completely onguard, watching Mierda's every move. This should help him to prevent it slightly, especially if Mierda doesn't beat Neuro in RP context. **Interference by Tim Inman: During the Mierda match, Tim Inman will run-in with a mask on. When the ref's back is turned, the "masked" man hits Neurosis with a chair and Mierda goes for the pin. **Angle by John Jupiter: John will come out to the match halfway through, without interfering, and not preventing any either. He'll just let it all happpen, because he doesn't like either competitor, but then after the match, he'll grab a mic and challenge the champion to a match on SSN or the next available card. Paul Arovnik pins Scotty Showtime – TA and Hardcore titles **Prevent interference by Paul Arovnik: Paul will enter with Benny Blades and Neurosis, his Painkillers. They will remain at ringside to prevent any wrestler from interfering against Paul. If a wrestler wants to interfere against Scotty Showtime and ONLY Scotty Showtime, they are free to do so. Also, even though this is a Hardcore bout, Paul will be trying to keep it in the ring. **Prevent interference by Scotty Showtime: Scotty will make his entrance then grab a microphone. "To prevent any screw jobs from happening...I give you St. Paul's Finest... Their police force!" The police squad of about twenty guys all equipped with nightsticks come out to "Bad Boys" (Watcha Gonna Do!). They surround the ring and protect interference from anyone going after Scotty Showtime. By the way, these are SMART police officers, they can recognize who is really going after Scotty and who isn't. **Prevent interference by John Jupiter: Just a plain and simple prevent for Scotty, because PA has his two friends who like to interfere. John won't use a weapon though, he'll just beat the hell out of them with wrestling skill. **Angle by Scotty Showtime: During the match Scotty will go outside, grab a table and silde it into the ring. It won't get used for awhile, just sit on the ring floor. After awhile Scotty sets it up in the corner, goes for a whip on Paul, but Paul reverses it and sends Scotty towards the table. But Scotty stops just as he is about to crash into it. The match continues for awhile longer without the table being broken. Paul will take the advantage and set Scotty up for a running powerbomb through the table, but Scotty reverses it at the last second and sends Paul crashing through the table with a hurricanrana reversal. **Angle/Interference by Paul Arovnik: Since this is a hardcore match, anything goes. As he stated in his roleplay, Paul will enter with a steel chair and a caulking gun. During the match, Paul will use the chair more than anything else, but will use whatever other weapons are lying around. At some point during the match, Paul will put a tired Scotty up on the turnbuckle, and then stand at the other corner of the ring. A dart will fly out of the crowd and THUNK right into Scotty's neck. The announcer will reveal that from the crowd, Nurse Nancy Ratchet shot Scotty with a tranquilizer dart. Scotty will be knocked out, Paul will cover him, and get the 1-2-3, and the victory. Let Scotty get in three or four near-falls before this, though. After his victory, as he stated in his roleplay, Paul will fill Scotty's mouth with caulk from the caulking gun. (If you didn't know, caulk is a liquid plaster used to repair walls.) (Note)-this angle will override the prevents. On a side note, I think Arovnik’s handler must think we’re retarded. **Help from Jack Williams: Jack will be at ringside during Scotty’s match. During a critical moment, Jack will inject a serum into Scotty. As far as the announcers know, it is a betrayal. The serum actually is a stamina booster. Jack will remain at ringside to help Scotty. (Note)-The serum will counteract the dart from Nancy Ratchet **Interference by Tim Inman: Durning the PA/Showtime match, Tim Inman will walk again to the ring wearing a mask. This time, Inman will hit Showtime with a chair while the ref is conveniently knocked out (from earlier in the match). PA and Inman stare at each other in the ring, and Inman removes his mask. The crowd is shocked. Inman: You will all be Avenged... Inman then points to PA to go for the pin and pulls off his top shirt. Under it is a shirt with a brand new "IWA" logo t-shirt. No one knows what this means. (Note)-Because of the large amount of prevents for this match, I can’t let Inman’s angle go through totally clean. (Note)-To go along with Inman’s angle: Show Burner backstage eating (of course) and watching the match. When Inman shows up Burner nearly chokes on his chicken. Pissed off, he rushes out the door, muttering, “He won’t get away with this…” CCW Home
