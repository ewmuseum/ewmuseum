CCW match: Scorpion (c) vs. Michael Stevens (For CCW Independent Championship)

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scorpion (c) vs. Michael Stevens (For CCW Independent Championship) Date: 3-13-03 Show: Lockdown JD: Up next is a meeting of two tag partners, vying for the same singles title, this should be intense. BJS: God, you’re a tool. What you’re really about to see folks is Michael Stevens prove that he carried Scorpion to victory last week, and after he beats him tonight, he’s going to continue to carry that lump of dead wood, right to the World Tag Titles. JD: Have I ever mentioned I hated you Slater. BJS: All the time. NW: Introducing first, from New York, New York SCORPION~! “Enter Sandman” by Metallica beings and Scorpion begins making his way down to the ring, to a nice face pop. Scorp slides into the ring, and raises his arms to the crowd, drawing another big pop. NW: And now from Greenwhich, Connecticut, Michael “Spitfire” STEVENS~! The cheers immediately turn to boos as “Enter Sandman” is replaced by The Alkaline Trio’s “Cringe”, and Michael Stevens begins making his way down to ringside, his left arm still wrapped in a cast. He gets to the ring, and slides inside, completely ignoring the crowd. BJS: Alright, I can’t wait Stevens is going to make Scorpion his bitch. And after that happens maybe he’ll bring back Sheena. JD: Is sex all you think about? BJS: What else is there? The bell sounds and the two men tie up in the center of the ring. Stevens gets the quick advantage with a thumb to the eye, and then quickly hits a head and arm suplex. He turns only to find Scorpion already up to his feet. Stevens goes in, only to be caught with a T-Bone suplex from Scorpion. Scorpion then grabs a handful of Stevens’s hair, and pulls him to his feet, before whipping him into the corner, and following Stevens in with a big splash. Scorpion nails Stevens a couple times across the jaw, before mounting the second rope and pounding away on Stevens’s skull. JD: Scorpion is really taking it to Stevens here. BJS: Nah, Stevens is just biding his time….HAHA~! Told ya. JD: That’s SICK, Stevens just nailed Scorpion with a low blow. Indeed, just as Scorpion had finished his tenth punch Stevens went for the good ole ball shot. He then wraps his arms around Scorpion’s waist takes a step forward, and drops Scorpion neck first across the top rope. Stevens then hits the reverse chin lock, driving his knee into Scorpion’s back. Stevens lets go of the hold just long enough to drive his casted forearm across the bridge of Scorpion’s nose, once, twice, three times. BJS: HAHA, this is great. JD: Only you’d think so Slater. Stevens lets go of the chin lock to pull Scorpion up, and smashes him across the chest with a knife edge chop, and another, and again, and again. Scorpion’s chest is bright red, and starting to resemble ground hamburger. Stevens then spins Scorpion around, and hits him with a german suplex, he then holds on, and then hits another two, bridging up on the third for a pin, but Scorpion kicks out in two. Stevens takes a minute to complain to Vinny, while Scorpion is pulling himself up to his feet. BJS: Come on Mike, you tell Vinny, slow count! JD: You’re drinkin draino Slater, that was a perfectly fine count. BJS: Liar. JD: Stevens is wasting a lot of time here, not a good idea against Scorpion. Scorpion finally gets up to his feet, and looks to be going for a German of his own, but Stevens quickly spins around and nails Scorp in the gut with a boot. He then locks up a front chancery and moves immediately into a snap suplex, floating over for a pin, but again Scorpion kicks out in two. Stevens again goes to argue with Vinny, complaining about the speed of the count. Scorpion gets up to his knees just long enough to roll up Stevens for a pin attempt, but Stevens kicks out in two. Both men pop up quickly, and Scorpion nails Stevens with the Scorpion DDT, and both men are out. Vinny begins the ten count. BJS: Come on Mike GET UP! JD: COME ON SCORP! At eight Scorpion rolls over, draping one arm across Michael’s chest, but Mike kicks out in just two. Scorpion is up to his feet, first, and waits for Stevens to get up. It doesn’t take long before Stevens is on his feet as well, he turns, just in time for Scorpion to catch him in position for the Tail Whip, but no Stevens blocks it, and spins around attempting to lock in the Darwinian Dreams, but Scorpion blocks that, reverses, TAIL WHIP~! JD: YES! TAIL WHIP~! BJS: NOOOOO~! JD: 3! SCORPION WINS~! View Scorpion 's Biography Back
