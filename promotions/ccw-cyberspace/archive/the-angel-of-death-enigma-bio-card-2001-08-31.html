A man that went from being an absolute nobody, to a CCW legend... Enigma broke into CCW in April 2001 with a goal in his mind... To become CCW World Champion...Many doubted Enigma would accomplish his goal, but he did... And he did it the hard way... Fighting his way from the bottom of the mountain all the way to the top... Enigma is not only a CCW legend, he is also A Hardcore Icon... Enigma has wrestled in some on the most brutal matches in CCW history with the likes of Cianan, Neurosis, Benny Blades, TVO, and many other... Some would even go as far as saying Enigma is the most Hardcore wrestler to ever step in a CCW ring... Well wether Enigma is the most Hardcore wrestler in CCW history or not, there is no doubt that Enigma is as tough as they come... And now Enigma has returned to CCW 2K to bring back some memories, and to just remind everyone why he is "The Angel Of Death"...
