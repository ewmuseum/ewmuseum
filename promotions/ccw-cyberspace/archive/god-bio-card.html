G.O.D Real Name: Gene Otto Droidman Birthdate: 2-2-02 Hometown: Disclosed Height: 6'9' Weight: 375 Nicknames: The Genetic Oriented Drone, Eugene, 'Unstoppable' Status: Heel Entrance Music: 'Symphony of Destruction' - Megadeth Information Wrestling Style: Power Set Up Move: Monotheism (Slams back of opponent’s head into turnbuckle) Finisher: G.O.D.'s Wrath (Torture Rack position and then G.O.D. flips them with their head continuing to cradle their leg. This hyperextends their back and G.O.D. finishes in a Reverse Powerslam position.) Title History: None Award History: None Other Federations: NTICW and RUSH Other Federation Awards: RUSH's Biggest Wrestler, NTICW's Biggest Wrestler Quotes History The story of Gene Otto Droidman doesn’t even begin with him. His story begins with a doctor Herbert Jenkins, MD. HJ has three Phds. One in medics, one in genes, and one in physics. Dr. Jenkins is certainly a smart man, if not a genius. Once out of school entirely his well acquired studies granted him a job within the government. His work was mainly on medical techniques for soldiers. Many different projects to help M*A*S*Hs with treating patients quicker, easier, and without flaw. Little was improved in this area and the projects were abandoned save a few minor authorities. Dr. Jenkins was moved to more military work, however it is extremely confidential. Again however, after maybe two or three years basing himself in the government he left and was hired by a drug company. There he headed up a department on testing drugs on animals and other creatures. During his research there he noticed that deformities were not rare. That animals took them with ease. This made the doctor ponder something about birthing animals and even people. A possibility of cloning came to mind and Dr. Jenkins started a basement project to clone a rat. Within just a few months he did in fact clone a rat. This made him extremely pleased, however, he did not want to tell anyone for he wanted all the credit for his scientific breakthrough. Keeping it in hiding and doing his own research among his job research, he did little to expand the cloning, but wanted to focus on more of a gene splicing technique. Once he had heard of the famous Dolly he simply shrugged it off, saying that splicing would be the way to fame. However, to create such a task, he did in fact find out that even with his amazing financial supply, he did not have enough money to perfect his experiments and research. So, he looked toward the government for a backing. This time however, not in the U.S. government, but a western European government that is disclosed at this time. They did in fact decide that this was extremely useful and worthwhile. Dr. Jenkin’s backing and assistants were also supreme. Jenkins decided that his spliced creation would be made up of both bron and brain powers combined into one. It took six years, two months, and seven days, but in the end, it was possibly the greatest thing ever thought of. The project called, Genetic Oriented Drone, was named Greg Otto Droidman, or G.O.D. for short. G.O.D. has been learning, extremely fast as planned, over the past month. His speech is already perfected, and he reads at a level that surpasses many college graduates. He is being taught, less educational things, but more life skills. Mainly however, fighting skills. Once created, they decided that taking over the intellectual world was done with himself, but to take over the athletic world would be slightly more of a challenge. That is where Old School Wrestling comes in. G.O.D. has reviewed many different athletic competitions and organizations and has chosen wrestling, and more importantly, Wrestling Tattler to compete in, against the best of the best. So the WT will feel the wrath of G.O.D. Since entering the WT G.O.D. has been in two different organizations. Those being New True Internet Championship Wrestling and RUSH Wrestling. In NTICW G.O.D. had minor success as the Extreme Champion. This championship he won from one Markus Sinniatti. He would later lose that title to William 'Biohazard' Perry. That federation soon closed after G.O.D.'s title loss though and much of that roster was moved to the promotion RUSH. G.O.D. has been in RUSH since July 11 and has taken many people's attention. Unlike in NTICW G.O.D. is cheered rather than hated by many people. He is a monster of a man and the biggest, most powerful 'man' in RUSH. His success is that he won the RUSH Network Championship at the Pay-Per-View Crossroads from Scott Cage. The future certianly holds greater things for this up-and-comer and only time will tell what they are. Needless to say though God giveth, but G.O.D. taketh away...
