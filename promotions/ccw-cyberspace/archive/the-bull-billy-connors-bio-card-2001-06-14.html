What this is Bio card for the CCW wrestler "The Bull" Billy Connors, with stats, moves and a background story: a powerhouse with football and other-federation history.
Name: Billy "The Bull" Connors Hometown: Panama City, FL Email: [detail withheld] Weight: 295 lbs. Height: 6' 7" Age: 24 Status: Neutral Manager/Valet: None Wrestling Style: Powerhouse; Technical Theme Music: "Break Sh!t" by Limp Bizkit Singles Record: 6-4-0 Hardcore Record: 0-0-0 Tag Team Record: 1-0-0 Championships: None Favorite Quote: "You screw with the bull... expect the horns!" Favorite Weapon: Metal Plated Shoulder Pads Debut: August 2000 Contract Expires: July 29, 2001 Superstar Background: Billy Conners started his Wrestling career, like most superstars. He was a skilled scholastic wrestler. But his overpowering strength and quickness, along with his size, lead him down a life more suited for football. So he took a football scholarship with the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. His Sophmore year, his Pro-Football hopes were dashed to pieces by a career ending car-wreck where he fractured his upper arm, and lower right leg. His injuries healed over time, and soon he found himself out of school with really no direction in life. His "kill-kill-kill" attitude and physical strength the only things left over from his college days. At the age of 23, Billy joined the now bankrupt ECWF, where he learned the ropes of Professional Wrestling. After the ECWF collapsed, The Bull moved on to the GCW and became the Intercontinental Champ. Before GCW went under, Billy was invited to the Summer Madness Tourney and scored a HUGE upset over CCW's own "Fat Ass" Phil Burner, before losing to Burner in the second round of the Tourney. Soon after, Billy caught wind of the CCW from Jason "highway" McCallister, and Neurosis. After appearing on the CCW scene, Billy had mixed results within the CCW rankings. So, when the hype of the XFL reached Billy, he was personally recruited by Dick Butkus. His injuries healed, and his thirst for a "professional" football career finally triumphed over Conners' love of wrestling and he joined the XFL. Now that the XFL is a flop, Billy has finally realized that his true home lies with Wrestling. He's back in the mix within CCW. ALL OPPONENTS BEWARE. "F*CK WITH THE BULL....EXPECT TO GET THE HORNS!!!"
