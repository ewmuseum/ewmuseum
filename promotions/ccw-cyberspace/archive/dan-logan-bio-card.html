Dan Logan Real Name: Daniel Steven Logan Birthdate: 07.07.78 Hometown: Moss Side, Manchester, England Height: 6'11' Weight: 254 Nicknames: Avon Status: Neutral Entrance Music: Lithium - Nirvana Information Wrestling Style: All-Round Set Up Move: Logan Plex (Belly to Back Hammerlock suplex) Finisher: Logan Bomb (Crucifix Powerbomb) Title History: none Award History: none Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Quotes History Born July 10th 1978, Daniel Steven Logan was raised a quite catholic boy who during his childhood was never one to get into trouble. Daniel was raised in Moss Side, Manchester one of the roughest areas any child from England could ever have the misfortune of being raised within. Not only from a young age was Dan introduced to petty crime and drugs, Dan became one of the schools 'nerds' and so was treated most days to punishment from the other boys for being intelligent. At the age of eleven Dan decided to fight back, no longer did he want to have the others pick on him because he had a brain and knew how to use it, he wanted instead to be accepted into the groups and upon entering secondary school Dan never looked back at the child that he once had been. As a child, Dan had always believed in God and had so gone to church and enjoyed listening to the priest talk of Jesus and all of his wondrous works. Upon his twelfth birthday though, that changed. Dan became satanic and from that moment on spent the time he would have been within a church studying his faith and all the dogma surrounding it. His parents were highly catholic themselves and disagreed totally with what Dan was saying, especially to his little brother Samuel who at the age of eight was only a few years younger than Dan and seemed hell bent on becoming like his brother. For two months Dan was to live on the streets, Dan's father kicking him out and telling him not to return while he (Dan's father) was still living there Samuel and his father left the house at the same time as Dan and went to live full-time in America. Dan's father had been an American and missed the country and so during what ended to be the most crucial time in Dan's life his whole family was falling apart. His mother grew ill and so Dan returned home from the streets to take care of her. It wasn't working and after only a few months Dan's aunt came to look after her kicking Dan out again, this time accusing Dan of being the cause of his mother's illness. For only one year after, his home was the streets and during that one year Dan learnt more about life than most adults could ever know. Dan was involved in an illegal-fighting ring, in which he was paid money for every fight he won. Soon Logan became a fan favourite and changed his name to Avondale (or Avon as he was refereed to as) to fit with his change of heart. Avon continued to make an impact on the way the fighting within the ring was manoeuvring and after only two months of his fighting within the ring, Avon had booking rights and was making a profit within the business as he had named it. At the age of sixteen, Avon decided to change back to his original name and go out and get what he classed as a 'Second Rate' job at the time, Dan joined the British army in what could be said to be his best choice that he has ever made. Joining the army was a godsend to Logan, who quickly received promotion for his exemplary behaviour and his ability to lead the others. Logan was finally fitting in within an environment that allowed him to fight and to train hard. This was Dan's main goal in life from that moment onward, his personal life became almost none existent and Dan was only ever seen within the gym training or upon the training grounds. After a few months of his rigorous training regime Dan began to develop physically and it became apparent why he had worked so hard. During Dan's life he has only had one real love. That is the love that he shared for eight years with his soon to be ex-wife Emma Logan. Emma had moved on from the high school girl that Dan had known and yet she was still as stunning as the first day he had laid eyes upon her, if anything her beauty had increased and Dan was lucky enough to marry her on June 21st 1994. During this time Dan became a changed man, he left the army at the age of 23 and set out looking for a new job, one which would become to him like the army had been. After a while of searching Dan spoke out to his friend and best man Paul Harris. The man that had given him the scar so many years before was now the only man Dan could turn to find him a job with all the attributes the Dan was looking for. Paul said that some jobs would become available soon in some of the bigger federations in the world and that until one came up, Dan was to check out some of the worlds many independent federations so that he could learn the ropes and get used to the way people fought within this kind of ring. Dan joined the CWA, under his alias of Avon and almost managed to scoop the prize of becoming the rising star champion. Dan vacated this opportunity and the whole CWA after only two months after a dispute with the president, which would have lead ultimately to one or the other leaving. Luckily for Avon he had been approached by another federation known as the xWe, while in the xWe Dan made many enemies as he fought in the main cards to almost the heavyweight title. Two weeks before Dan received his shot at the number one title in the xWe, Paul gave Dan a phone call and told him to make an application to the LTD company who were now on the look out for brand new wrestling talent to fill the gaps within their roster. Agreeing with Paul that a move from the xWe into the LTD would improve his career, Dan handed in his resignation and within the two week period was told that he had a contract match against another LTD hopeful known as Tryn. Just as Logan had hoped Tryn didn't even make an appearance and Logan had become a LTD wrestler without even having to fight for it. Proud of his achievement Logan began to become cocky and it has taken Dan a few months to once again regain his composure. At the LTD one year anniversary show, Dan Logan received his first title shot for the Progressive title and against four men Dan Logan defied all statistics and came out the victor against three men who at their best were tipped to wipe the floor with Logan. This never happened and Logan stood tall as the new LTD Progressive champion and with a shot at tag team gold next up, Logan hoped to make the most out of his new found confidence and regained composure and become 50% of the tag team champions alongside his first real enemy in the LTD and tag partner Executioner. After a winning streak in the LTD, Logan left and was offered a contract by CCW, he has been accepted soon he will begin to hunt down new enemies and new friends only time will tell if he is able to stand-alone and continue on with his winning ways, what ever else happens Dan 'Avon' Logan will always be the number one Logan in his family. Samuel Logan is now also fighting within America is one of the smaller independent federations. Now only two months away from finalising his divorce, how badly does he want to win, Dan knows why he is getting s divorce and never again will he trust another person!
