What this is Referee bio card for Dwayne "DK" Kinsington with hometown, experience and a background paragraph: a mid-card referee raised in Miami.
Name: Dwayne "DK" Kinsington Hometown: Miami, Fl. Experience: 4 years Weight: 210 lbs. Height: 6'2 Age: 27 Status: Mid Card Referee Ref Background: DK was brought up in Miami, Fl. We all know that just south of South Beach is quite the rough neighborhood. He was raised there, fighting his was through life, until the time came he got the call from CCW. Though he knew nothing of refereeing, he could be used an enforcer. He was hired, much to the approval of the staff. Since his hire in, we have seen him make numerous calls in favor of Blayze, John Jupiter, Tim Inman, El Mierda, and so on. He isn't the fans favorite referee, but most of the guys don't test him.
