CCW match: Scorpion vs. Yama Tatsu (Finals of Indy Tourney for Indy Title)

Scorpion vs. Yama Tatsu (Finals of Indy Tourney for Indy Title) Date: 3-6-03 Show: Lockdown RA: Ladies and Gentlemen… This next matchup is THE MAIN EVENT AND IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL for THE CCW INDEPENDENT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!! ***'Meet the creeper' rocks the PA as the entranceway is ignited with pyro… From behind the smoke two figures walk out onto the entrance ramp… RA: Introducing first… Being accompanied to the ring by Kyle Thorne… From Okinawa, Japan… Weighing in at 253lbs… YAMA TATSU!!! ***Yama Tatsu and Kyle climb into the ring glaring out into the crowd as the music blares… Kyle is talking things over with Yama as suddenly both are startled by an explosion of pyro followed by the strains of 'Enter Sandman' by Metallica… The music blares over the PA as the crowd breaks into a mad chorus of random noise… Scorpion has a devious look on his face as he makes his way out of the entrance way with is hands in the air… RA: And the opponent…. From New York , New York… Weighing in at 235lbs…. SCORPION!!!! JD: And Scorpion choosing to come to the ring alone, unlike his opponent who has Kyle watching his back… This one should be an interesting matchup as we have two completely different styles, Yama with his martial arts background, and Scorpion who sticks more to a grinding/power move set… BJ: And it’s for the CCW INDY TITLE!!! The list of CCW greats who have held this belt is a long and distinguished one, kind of like myself… But in all seriousness Joey, it is one of the most respected titles in CCW and over the years the winner of this belt has gone on to great things… JD: Yes, there have been many men who have held this title… BJ: Hey! Bobby Blades even threw this belt down and pissed on it I think… JD:… Anyways… that was shameless… BJ: Well he did! JD: … And this matchup is underway as referee Ryan Ohashi calls for the bell… BJ: HE DID! JD: Both men circling each other now as neither really eager to lock up… now they are interlocking hands and test of strength?… Well Yama has the size advantage but I think Scorpion has the reach… Look at that! Yama forcing Scorpion down but scorpion maintains an insanely tight bridge!! Now Scorpion back up and he quickly grapevines the leg and gets the single leg take down… Yama spins and misses the leg sweep, But Scorpion quickly counters with an elbow drop… missed! Now Yama with a kipup and a snapmare on Scorpion, Yama with a rollup 1….2… no! Countered by Scorpion 1….2… No counter by Yama with a ¾ nelson! 1…2… kickout by Scorpion!! BJ: Catch as catch can wrestling Joey… JD: Both men now on their feet both a little dazed perhaps… Now they are head to head as they are barking at one another… Yama finally shoves Scorpion away and Scorpion shove him right back! Now both men opening up on each other with a series of rights and lefts!!! Yama finally working Scorpion back into the corner! Knife edge chop! CROWD: WOOOOOOOOOOO! JD: Yama again with another chop! CROWD: WOOOOOOOOOOOO! JD: Scorpion seems stunned but Yama goes to whip him out of the corner and no! Scorpion reverses and shortarms Yama as he drives the knee into his gut… Yama staggered and Scorp with a big irish whip tosses the larger man to the opposite corner and he bounces right off the turnbuckle and over the ropes!!! Scorpion is pumped now as the crowd acknowledges him!! Yama is struggling to his feet on the floor as Kyle runs over to help him up… BJ: Yama might be a little surprised by Scorpion’s strength as the smaller man, but those longer limbs of his help him in really being able to work over his larger opponents… LOOOK OUT! JD: Scorpion over the top rope and to the floor with a flying crossbody!!! Takes out both Yama and Kyle! CROWD: CC DUB! CC DUB! JD: Scorpion taking charge now as he tosses Yama back into the ring… He tosses Yama towards the ropes, Yama ducks! Off the other ropes now and catches Scorpion BULLDOG!… Yama now struggling as he is being checked on by the ref… And look at Kyle now with a blatant choke on Scorpion as the ref deals with Yama… BJ: Hey, he’s obviously out here for a reason Joey… JD: And now Kyle is quick to duck away as Yama is back to working on Scorpion…. Yama now stomping Scorpion as he picks him up and whips Scorpion to the corner… Scorpion counters! Tip up… Spinning heel kick?? No! Yama counters and drops down into the splits!!! Oooo Low blow on Scorpion but the ref missed it??? BJ: Look he’s busy talking to Kyle up on the canvas!! JD: Now Yama quickly on top 1… 2…. NO! Scorpion kicks out as Yama is whining to the referee… Yama now quickly picks up Scorpion and tosses him to the outside again! Now what?? Dammit Yama is arguing over something with the ref and meanwhile look at Kyle! Kyle tosses Scorpion into those steel steps!!! Now he feeds him back into Yama in the ring as Yama lays Scorpion in the middle of the ring… Yama off the ropes quickly, cartwheel into a legdrop! Right across Scorpion’s throat! Yama quickly covers! 1….2… just a two count as Scorpion kicks out!
