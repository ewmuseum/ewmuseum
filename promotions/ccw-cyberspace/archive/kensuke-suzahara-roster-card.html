What this is CCW roster card for Kensuke Suzahara, with hometown, status, moves, title history and roleplay list: a face American technician/Jeet Kune Do wrestler with a long history section and move write-ups.
Kensuke Suzahara Real Name: Kensuke Suzahara Birthdate: 1/25/83 Hometown: Seattle, Washington Height: 5'11' Weight: 221 lbs. Nicknames: 'Kense' or 'K.S.' Status: Face Manager(s): None. Current Teams: 'The Cause' with Jason Core Entrance Music: 'Stuck In America' by Sugarcult Wrestling Style: American Technical w/ some Jeet Kune Do martial arts Signature Moves: See below Set Up Move: Backlash (See below ) Finisher: The New Stranger (See below. ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Heavyweight Title (x1), Trans-Atlantic Title (x2), Independent Title (x1), Hardcore Title (x1), Lightweight Title (x1), Tag Team Title (x2, 'New Strangers' w/ Blayze/'Six-String Samurai' w/ Kyle Lamen) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: 2001 Best Face, 2001 Light Heavyweight Of The Year, 2001 Rookie Of The Year, 2001 Stable Of The Year (The New Strangers) Other Federations: PCW, PURE, LCW-LTD Other Federation Awards: PCW Tag Team Champion (x1, 'The Elements' w/ Miyagawa), LCW-LTD Tag Team Champion (x1, 'The Street Samurai' w/ Jin Akujin) Handler Information Handler Name: Bryan AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: N/A History Kensuke Suzahara made his debut in CCW, expected to be nothing more than a gimmicky Japanese-gimmicked character with some martial arts skill to put over the established talent in CCW. He was Johnny Flame, destined to be nothing more than a comedy wrestler. An enhancement talent. A kid. Life's funny like that. Kensuke pulled up his boots, and managed to become a living legend in CCW, due to his demeanor as an old-school face. He formed the New Strangers, one of the longest-running stables in sports entertainment, and had MANY memorable feuds and title reigns. CCW entered an interfed called FedWars. Immediately, Alexander Miyagawa and Johnny Flame sought each other out, as they were two of the minority of Japanese wrestlers in the United States. A brutal war of words ensued, sparking off a bitter feud that would send Kensuke over to PCW first as Miya's ally...but later, as his enemy. He was fired shortly after their match at 'Back To Old School.' Reappearing a few months later, after an injury in CCW sent him packing, Kensuke went on a winning streak unparalleled in CCW history. At Group Therapy 2001, Kensuke won the CCW Heavyweight Title to the approval of millions of fans. But that's just not enough sometimes. Since then, Kensuke has been discontent with his lot in life, as while loved, he still did not get enouugh respect from the 'boys' backstage. Yet he still played to the fans that adore him. But recently...things have gone wrong. He lost the CCW Heavyweight Title to an archnemesis, Cianan. His most hated enemy, Neurosis came back, and usurped him as the biggest babyface in CCW. His girlfriend left him. And John Jupiter prevented him from going down in CCW history as the first ever Universal Champion. Backstage at CCW's pay-per-view event Irish Spring, Kensuke brutalized his tag team partner, Kyle 'Velocity' Lamen with a chair, and gave him his finishing move, the Stranger, onto an open chair. Kyle's wrestling career is over. The most loved susperstar in CCW history was destined to become the most hated, until CCW's Group Therapy Pay-Per View. Until two things happened. #1: Jason McAllister made an impassioned plea for CCW to take personal responsibility for itself. #2: Kensuke was defeated by Yama Tatsu, John Coens, and Cianan...three of his most bitter rivals, in what was essentially a three-on-one match. Coens and Neurosis, probably his two most bitter enemies, then went on to fight for the CCW Heavyweight Title at the Group Therapy main event, which was a hideous mirror image of the previous GT main Event, which was face vs. face (Kensuke vs. J-Mac) where Kensuke won the CCW Heavyweight Title. On the May 2nd Lockdown Thursday, Kensuke's theme music was true to form. 'Today...I changed.' Kensuke laid out two of his former stablemates, Blayze and Fanatik, siding with Spider and essentially, everything that is right in the world. Kensuke has come home...to the fans of CCW. He has answered the call... And now...there's a whole Cause to fight for. Kensuke's brought two former PCW champions to CCW...one being Jason Core, the other being ---- -----. Finisher The New Stranger - Inverted Crucifix into Cutter Kensuke lifts his opponent up from underneath both of their arms, while facing away from them (easily done out of inverted facelock position, with Kensuke as the victim). He holds the opponent overhead in an inverted crucifix, then releases, jumps forward, and fits his arm around the victim's head in a 3/4 facelock bulldog, and drops to the mat. Tag Finisher The Cause Effect - Flapjack to Mat Slam Jason Core lifts the victim into the air with a waistlock, and falls backwards, and at the same time, Kensuke Suzahara delivers a sit-out mat slam (X-Factor). Can easily be done through a table. Primary Set-Up Move Backlash - Handspring Double Mule Kick Kensuke runs and jumps, and does a handspring into the ring ropes. As he bounces off, he pushes off his hands and kicks backwards with both legs to the victim hitting them with both soles of his feet. Kensuke then does a forward roll out of this so he can then deliver a Stranger. Set-Up Moves/Secondary Finishers Silver Slide - Leg Lever Takedown with Leg Drop The victim is standing but bent forward. Kensuke stands to the victim's side and hooks the victim's near arm. He puts the leg which is closest to the victim's arm over the back of the victim's neck and hooks it by bending his leg. Kensuke then pulls his leg back and pushes the victim's arm forward in the opposite direction. The victim is then spun sideways and lands flat on their back on the mat. As the victim is falling, Kensuke drops down with a leg drop onto the victim's neck. The Falling Star - Inverted Facelock Swinging Neckbreaker Kensuke applies an inverted facelock on the victim. He quickly spins to a side, wrenching the victim's neck and dropping them face first into the mat. Kensuke gets more height on his jump than most others who perform this move. Signature Moves The Dreaming - Drop Toe Hold into STF with Double Arm Lock Kensuke drops down to the mat hooking the leg of the victim with one foot in front of the victim's ankle and then trips the victim down by pushing with their other leg on the back of the victim's legs. Then, Kensuke grabs the victim's foot and bends it up. He lies on his side, his bottom leg is behind the victim's knee, and hi top leg is hooked around the victim's shin/ankle. Kense throws the victim's near arm in between his legs and scissor locks the arm and the victim's ankle. He reaches across the victim's chest with one arm and around the victim's free arm and locks his hands around the victim's head. The end result has Kensuke pulling back on the victim's neck, one of the victim's arms is trapped in a leg scissor, the other arm is trapped between Kensuke's chest and under his top arm which is applying the face lock on the victim. Mask Of The Angel - Off The Top Rope Shooting Star Mat Slam Kensuke and his victim face each other, Kensuke atop the top turnbuckle, and the opponent standing on the mat. Kensuke does a backflip off the top rope, and as he is landing, he grabs the opponent by the back of the head, and drives his face into the mat as he lands in a sitting position. Wind Element Suplex - Belly-to-Back Double Chickenwing Suplex with Bridge Kensuke applies a double chickenwing on the victim and links his hands together behind the victim's back. He then lifts the victim up and holds him there for a moment, putting pressure onto the shoulder blades. Then, Kensuke falls backwards, dropping the victim on their shoulders/neck/upperback. Then, he bridges for a pin. The Strike - 180 Springboard Spear Kensuke leaps to the second rope, and as he comes off, spins 180 degrees and dives at a standing victim. He grabs both their legs, causing the victim to fall backwards to the mat. Stasis Drop - Half Nelson Standing Mat Slam Kensuke stands behind the victim. He reaches under one of the victim's arms with one hand and places it behind the victim's neck. Then, Kensuke grabs the victim by his neck with his free hand and slams their face into the mat.
