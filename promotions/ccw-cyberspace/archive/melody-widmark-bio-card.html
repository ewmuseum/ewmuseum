Melody Widmark Real Name: Melody Widmark Birthdate: April 25th, 1981 Hometown: Miami, Florida Height: 5'9' Weight: 131 Nicknames: Status: Neutral Entrance Music: 'All the things she said' by t.A.T.u. Information Wrestling Style: Up close and... personal. Set Up Move: The Dominator (A more elaborate, and sexier, version of the Pedigree) Finisher: The Eliminator (Meldoy wraps her thighs around her opponents neck, burying her opponent's face into her crotch for a suffocation pin/submission) Title History: none Award History: none Other Federations: none Other Federation Awards: none Quotes History Melody Widmark grew up, attended school and college, and found her true calling in sunny Miami, Florida. She was part of an average family, receieved average grades in school and has led a pretty uninteresting life all around. In her high school years, she bloomed into a very attractive woman. However, she would date no one. It's not because she hated all the boys in her class, but simply because she just wasn't... interested. Melody had what she considered a dark secret: She was a lesbian. It all started back in middle school when she had a slumber party with a couple of her friends. They started a pillow fight with each other, happily playing and having a good time. It soon got a little more physical as the young girls started grappling with one another. Of course, it was all in good fun... No one was hurt... But Melody found that she was strangely aroused by the conflict with the other girls... dominating them, and having them do it to her in kind. And since she was a pre-pubescent teen, she was confused by her feelings. Keeping them a secret, she went on with her life and tried initiating more playful conflicts whenever she could. High School was difficult for her. She learned more about who she was and even allowed herself to explore her feelings. Melody didn't dare show any of the other girls how she felt, knowing how badly she'd be ostracized by them and the entire school. She was prom queen material, after all, and no one was going to take that from her. College was a different story. She opened herself up more and began to explore. She even found a lover. Although her new partner didn't share her enthusiasm for physical wrestling/conflict, Melody was happy anyway. Sadly, the relationship didn't last very long. Melody did what she could to make it last, but it was all for naught. Finally, chalking the whole thing up to experience, Meldoy decided to concentrate on the one thing that could possibly make her happy: Wrestling. She took classes and trained as hard as she could. And finally, she was ready. She then had a choice to make... Underground female wrestling/catfight videos, or the world of professional wrestling. The underground videos couldn't pay her much, but she'd have the contact she loved. On the other hand, professional wrestling could pay her tons of money, but she'd have to tone things down. That is... Until she found CCW. She was offered a contract, told that anything goes, and was invited to start immediately. And looking over the women's roster, she can't help but think how good she's lucked out... Moves Melody is a very clingy fighter, meaning once she's got a hold of you, she won't let go. She will control the arms, legs, and entire body of an opponent... Playing with them and enjoying herself... Wearing them down and out. She has incredibly strong and shapely legs, and a Leg Scissors is definitely one of her fav moves. She won't hesitate to dislocate limbs to give herself an advantage over a stronger opponent.
