Shriekfest 99 Show #37 - June 20, 1999

What this is Cyberspace Championship Wrestling show script for Show #37 on June 20, 1999, from Stadium Australia in Sydney, Australia, with the card and the written play-by-play.
June 20, 1999 from Stadium Australia in Sydney, Australia - Show #37 Apocalypse vs. Niamouchu - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Supreme Machine vs. "Big Ass" Bob Burner - CCW Independent Championship The Manics vs. The Crazy Train - CCW Tag Team Championship WAI vs. The Cretin Family Thunder vs. Joquar - Circle of Semi Hardcore Match Mr. Gobstopper vs. Mash Badd Dogg vs. "Faggot Ass" Mike Newsome Akron "The Holy Sage" vs. Ice Chamber From Stadium Australia in Sydney, Australia Akron "The Holy Sage" vs Ice Chamber Akron entered the stadium accompanied by The Paladin. Ice Chamber came out as the fans booed him for they did not know who he was. The match began as Chamber circled Akron before locking up with him and sending him into the ropes and upon his return, connected with a powerslam. Akron was brought to his feet as Chamber took him by the head and slammed him to the mat, then, picking him up and sending him into the turnbuckle. Chamber went for a flying body splash into the turnbuckle but Akron quickly moved out of the way. Akron took Chamber by the arm and slung him to the rope and attempted a back body drop but that was blocked as Akron was put into a powerbomb by Ice Chamber. The Paladin, who was on the outside, began chanting as this drew Chamber's attention to the outside. While Chamber was talking trash to The Paladin, Akron was gaining his lost energy and was able to get to his feet. Akron went up to Chamber and spun him around by the shoulder and connected with the Holocauster for the 1-2-3. The Paladin entered the ring and began awarding Akron with his praise. "The Man's Man" Mike Newsome vs Badd Dogg Newsome entered the stadium first accompanied by Itchiban as the duo made their way to the ring as Hanson was blaring over the stadium's speaker system. Badd Dogg came out to some new music as he had Pussy Cat at his side. The match began when Newsome locked up with Badd Dogg and delivered a few gut shots to Dogg followed by the breaking of the lock up and into a DDT. This put Badd Dogg out for a while as Newsome began stomping Dogg. Newsome soon lifted Dogg to his feet and sent him to the corner. Newsome began charging but Dogg moved out of the way as Newsome went crashing into the turnbuckle. Badd Dogg went to the side ropes as Pussy Cat was on the ring apron, and the two kissed as PC slipped something to Badd Dogg. Badd Dogg went over to Newsome and helped him to his feet before giving him a boot to the mid section which bent Newsome over in pain, holding his stomach. Dogg went to the rear end of Newsome and held up to the crowd what PC had given him. It was a raw carrot! Badd Dogg slipped Newsome's tights down and rammed the carrot deep into his anal cavity!! Newsome let out a girlish yelp of pleasure as Badd Dogg pushed him downo to the mat. Itchiban on the outside, seemed worried as to the health of his inserted lover. But Newsome seemed to have enjoyed the carrot up the ass. Just then, a helicopter flew over the stadium and lowered itself towards the ring. Punjab The Arabian Wonder was inside the helicopter holding a large red crate out of the helicopter! As Badd Dogg went to work on Newsome, Punjab dumped the crates contents out over the ring and to the surprise of many... It was a crate full of dildos!!!! Fake Penis's!!! Where ever did Punjab get such filth? The penis's landed mostly in the ring as one even knocked into the head of Badd Dogg. The arrival of these dildos quickly made Newsome horny as he gained a rush of adrenaline and turn the tables on Badd Dogg, lifting him in the air, and slamming him into the turnbuckle. Newsome went to Badd Dogg and began humping his left leg against the turnbuckle!! Unknowingly, Newsome was also choking Badd Dogg against the turnbuckle as he shifted his weight back and forth. The ref tried to break the hold but didn't really want to get near Newsome as he continued humping the left leg of Badd Dogg. So the ref called for the bell and DQ'd Newsome for failure to release the "choke" hold. Itchiban came into the ring and pulled Newsome off of Badd Dogg and escorted him out of the ring. Surprisingly, the fruity pair didn't seem upset at the loss as they quickly made their way to the locker room area with huge smiles on their faces. Meanwhile, Badd Dogg was being tended to by Pussy Cat in the ring. Mr. Gobstopper vs Mash Mr. Gobstopper came to the ring first to the sounds of Too Unlimited. Mash entered next accompanied by his manager, Sir Kirb. The match started as Mash charged at Gobstopper but the Gob stepped out of the way and allowed Mash to bounce off the ropes and back into a powerslam. Gobstopper signaled to the crowd for some noise but Mash was not kept down from the powerslam as Gobstopper turned around, Mash met him with the Brain Masher for the 1-2-3! Mash left the ring with a victory as Gobstopper laid in the ring wondering as to what happened. Joquar vs Thunder - Hardcore - Semi-Circled Match The crowd erupt as the twelve CCW eighteen wheelers reverse into place to form the ring for the "Classic Hardcore Match". As the last truck slowly reverses into place, Stadium Australia becomes shrouded in darkness. As the crowd roar in anticipation of the upcoming match a lone spotlight pierces the darkeness and shines underneath the main scoreboard. The fans go absolutely nuts as The Mark of Cain stride to their instruments and start playing "The Contender". Another spotlight pierces the darkness and shines brightly off the top of one of the semis. Thunder leaps onto the top of the truck and salutes the wildly screaming crowd before jumping onto the grass and surveying the contents of the trailors. The crowd erupt again as Joquar's music blares from the huge PA system and he makes his way towards the "ring". Red rockets burst from the top of the trailors as he stands saluting the crowd. Before Joquar can turn to face hin, Thunder lands a smashing forearm that sends Joquar crashing to the grass. In a flash Thunder is on him, ramming his face into the dirt. Joquar forces his way to his feet and rams Thunder's head into the side of one of the trailors, momentariliy stunning him. As Thunder is standing dazed, Joquar leaps into one of the trailors to find a weapon. He leaps out of the trailor brandishing a ringpost and takes a wild swing at Thunder, who, having regained his senses, ducks, kicks Joquar fair in the gut, grabs the ringpost, and nails Joquar in the head with it, sending a sickening crack across the crazed audience of 110,000, a CCW record. Joquar falls face first into the turf as Thunder unleashes with a flurry of kicks to the back of his head. Thunder turns and leaps into one of the trailors and emerges brandishing a piece of guard rail. He climbs to the top of the trailor, leaps, and nails Joquar across the throat with the cold steel. After a few more kicks to Joquar's head, Thunder returns to the trailor and comes out with another piece of guard rail. He climbs to the top, leaps....but Joquar moves at the last moment, causing Thunder to miss and fall onto the gurad rail himself. Taking the advantage, Joquar slowly gets to his feet, grabs the dazed Thunder and throws him into the back of another trailor. He pushes Thunder to the back and then pulls something from his boot. It appears to be a match. Joquar lights the match and then throws it into a corner of the trailor.This is the truck carrying the pyros. Thunder sees him throw it and, with all his remaining energy leaps from the trailor it explodes in a blinding ball of flame. The crowd are going absolutely nuts. Joquar walks over to the spot where Thunder is laying exhausted in the grass and drops two devestating elbows across the back of his neck. Sensing victory, Joquar picks the senseless Thunder up off the grass and sets him up for the Outback Powerbomb. Joquar pauses, however, to salute the cheering crowd, leaving Thunder enough time to deliver a crippling blow to Joquar's testilcles, which sends him doubled over to the cold ground. The fans are cheering madly as both men lay stunned, unable to move, surrounded by the big rigs. Slowly they both regain their composure but it is Joquar that recovers the quickest and he moves to grab Thunders head. Thunder has been playing "chicken" though and reaches out and grabs an unexploded firework, lights it off the still burning semi, and holds it to Joquar's head, burning his flesh and setting his hair on fire. As Joquar screams in pain, Thunder shows no remorse and begins beating him over the head with a chair that he grabbed from another truck. Thunder throws the chair to the ground and turns to find another weapon from one of the trucks. He emerges with a television monitor. He stands on the trailor, sizing up the non-moving Joquar and leaps.....but again Joquar moves at the last second and again Thunder misses. Before Thunder can turn, Joquar nails him with some devestating head shots before leaping onto the back of one of the trailors and and delivering a sickening knee drop to Thunder's back. Again Joquar goes into one of the trailors, this time emerging with some rope.He proceeds to tie Thunder's hand and feet before unleashing with a flurry of kicks and punches to the prone Thunder. Just as Joquar turns and climbs to the top of one of the trailors, Thunder manages to get free and scrambles after him. The fans are cheering madly as the two brothers go toe to toe on top of one of the semi trailors. Thunder eventually prevails, smashing Joquar down to one knee. Thunder grabs his hair and begins beating mercilessly on his already bloodied, burnt face. After Joquar falls, Thunder turns and salutes the crowd. "See the loser!!" he yells. But Joquar is not done yet. He leaps at Thunder, sending them both hurtling form the trailor and crashing to the ground below. Joquar lands on top and begins unleashing on Thunder with smashing blows to the face and head. He then grabs Thunder's long blond hair and begins to rip great chunks of it out, scalp and all, causing Thunder's head to start bleeding profusely. Crazed by the blood running into his eyes, Thunder rallies and nails Joquar in the throat, stunning him long enough to enable Thunder to get to his feet. The two brothers again go toe to toe for several minutes, before Joquar once again floors Thunder with a sickening right hand to the face. This time, Thunder doesn't move as Joquar slowly climbs to the top of one of the semi's and launches himself into the air, delivering a crushing leg drop onto Thunder's bleeding head. Hearing the roar of the crowd, Joquar again goes to the top of the truck and delivers another leg drop onto his unconcious brother. The crowd are cheering for blood. Joquar stands triumphantly over Thunder, smiling into the capacity crowd. He spies the TV monitor that had previously missed his head laying a few metres away, grabs it and goes to smash it down on Thunder's head but is stopped when The Supreme Machine leaps from one of the trucks and grabs it from his hands. The two get into a heated arguement and the CCW cameras zoom in. "But he's your brother for god's sake, your flesh and blood!" Joquar looks down at the unconcious Thunder and then back at The Supreme Machine. "It's not worth it." Joquar looks up at the fans. He turns back to SuMa and drops the TV. The Supreme Machine stands ready to attack but Joquar just shakes his head and walks away. The referee calls it a draw. WAI vs The Cretin Family The match began as Bob Burner against Brad Lamen as Burner worked continuously on Lamen before allowing him to tag in Johnny. As Johnny was tagged in, Bob turned and tagged the Fat Ass who totally dominated Johnny Lamen. As Phil took Lamen to the mat, he signaled to the fans that it was time... Phil bounced off one side of the ring ropes and hopped over Johnny and bounced off the other side before stoping at Johnny once again, and looking to the crowd, dropped the Porker's Elbow!! The Porker's Elbow!! Phil went for the cover, 1... 2... Brad entered to make the save as Big Ass took him out in time with the Big Ass Atomic Ass Bomb... 3!!! WAI have defeated The Cretin Family to end their tag team curse!! CCW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP The Manics vs The Crazy Train The match began as Pepper took on Kaboink. Pepper was weary as to hurt Kaboink knowing that Lady Cat was at ringside so Kaboink got the upperhand early but not before Pepper made the quick and sneaky tag to Klown who leveled Kaboink and sent him into his own corner allowing the tag to be made to Apoc. Klown sent Apoc off the ropes but Apoc ducked underneath the clothesline attempt and caught Klown on the return with a kick to the chin sending Klown down and allowing the Cataclysm to be applied. This did not defeat Klown but Apoc then lifted him up for a powerbomb and went for the cover... 1... 2... 3!!! The Crazy Train has become the NEW CCW Tag Team Champions!!! CCW INDEPENDENT CHAMPIONSHIP The Supreme Machine vs "Big Ass" Bob Burner Immediately, SuMa knocked Bob out of the ring and onto the floor mat outside of the ring. The pair of wrestlers began brawling up the aisle and spilling into the crowd. SuMa grabbed a fan's chair and clanked Bob upside his head, knocking him down. SuMa continued with the beating until the referees had to come out to break up the action. The announcer had been told by the referee that this match was a double count out! CCW TRANS-ATLANTIC CHAMPIONSHIP Apocalypse vs Niamouchu Niamouchu was not the same as he had been in previous encounters with Apocalypse. As Niamouchu sent himself off the ropes Apocalypse stopped him in his tracks and placed him in the Armaggeddon for the extremely quick cover... 1... 2... 3!!! Still the Trans-Atlantic Champion... Apocalypse! This was one of the most fastest matches CCW has ever seen for the TA title! That's it for Shriekfest 99! I would like to thank the few of you who showed up for coming to the show last night and sorry I had to cut it short due to my stomach ache last night! CCW Home
