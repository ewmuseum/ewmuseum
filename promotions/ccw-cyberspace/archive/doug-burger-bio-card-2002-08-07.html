What this is Bio card for the CCW wrestler Doug Burger, with stats, moves and a background story: a face technician who is a private investigator married to a former CCW wrestler.
Name: Doug Burger Hometown: Chicago, Il Email:[detail withheld] Weight:230 Height: 5'10 Age: 24 Status: Face Manager/Valet: N/A Wrestling Style: Technician Theme Music: "Here is Gone" by Goo Goo Dolls Singles Record: 2-0-0 Hardcore Record: 0-0-0 Tag Team Record: 1-1-0 Championships: None Favorite Quote: Favorite Weapon: Debut: August 7 2002 Superstar Background: Doug Burger is married to a former CCW wrestler, Lacey Skye Morris, and works as a private investigator for the privately funded "Millennium Group". After CCW's initial closing, Lacey was out of a job, and the two of them sat down and had a long conversation about where they were going and what should be done. They decided on two things: That they wanted to have children, and that Lacey should retire from the professional wrestling circuit to best facilitate her new duties as a mother. Having discovered several years back it was virtually impossible for them to have children (some of the plumbing wasn't working right for both of them, and the fact that Lacey had been pregnant with child about a year ago was simply a miracle), Doug and Lacey decided to adopt. They put in a request, and received confirmation for an five year old daughter named Jessica. Of course, there were going to be tough times with only one of them working. They also felt the need to get out of California and move to... Well, Chicago seemed a good a place as any. Of course, they were going to need extra money, and with an five year old daughter, Lacey's motherly responsibilities were going to be pretty demanding. They talked about Lacey's career, and she decided that once they arrived in Chicago, she would attempt to get a job with social services... A very emotionally demanding job, but one she was well-equipped for. Doug, having combat and wrestling training of his own, heard about the newly rejuvinated CCW. Figuring it could help with his anger management, releasing it in a positive and expressive way, he could use CCW as a bit of therapy. Heaven forbid he blow his top at his new daughter or his loving wife. He could wrestle, get paid and receive therapy all the while continuing with his regular job. It was a good plan anyway. Or so he thought... Finishing Move Culling Cutter - A high impact Belly to Back Slam. Trademark Move When opponent is down, stalking them from behind and waiting for them to get up.
