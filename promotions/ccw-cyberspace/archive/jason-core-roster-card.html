Jason Core Real Name: Jason Alan Bryant Core Birthdate: 9/10/67 Hometown: Tallahassee, Florida Height: 6'6' Weight: 289 lbs. Nicknames: 'J-Core' or 'Teach' Status: Face Manager(s): To Be Revealed Current Teams: 'The Cause' with Kensuke Suzahara Entrance Music: 'Here I Go Again' by Whitesnake Wrestling Style: Powerful/Technical Signature Moves: See below Set Up Move: The New School (See below ) Finisher: Second Chance (See below ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: None Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: PCW, EWA Other Federation Awards: PCW Extreme Champion, First ever EWA Heavyweight Champion Handler Information Handler Name: Bryan AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: N/A Description: J.C. is a large man, by normal standing. His body is very well-defined...he's huge from years of athletic training. Definition like Goldberg, only hold the 'roids. His rugged good looks (definitely a handsome man) are framed by dark brown hair pulled back into a 4-6 inch ponytail. His eyes are dark (brown? green?) and are usually resolute, depressed, or a 2:1 mixture of the two. I'd go with the mixture. His face is much the same, expcept add two cups of wry humor, and a dash of anger/cynicality. As for his in-ring appearance, J-Core comes to ringside wearing his t-shirt of the week/month, whatever that may be. The shirt's gone by the time the match starts, it's thrown to a kid in the audience. Apart from that, he wears black tights (J-Core on the rear) that only go to mid-thigh, showcasing his tree-trunk legs. His knees are encased in black braces, covered in black kneepads. He also wears black elbowpads, and black boots. His wrists get wrapped with white tape, as do his individual fingers, aside from index and thumb. Outside of the ring, Jason dressed in what could be described as 'cheap preppy casual chic.' Flannel shirts, t-shirts, baseball-style shirts, blue jeans or track pants, sneakers, Vans, leather jacket...there, now you've got a mental picture. You're welcome. :) Finisher The Second Chance - Double Leg-Cradle Northern Lights Suplex with pin [First off, this move is also called the 'Core Suplex' as it was J-Core who invented it, and added it to the wrestling lexicon. Jason rushes the opponent, shoves his head inbetween the victim's arm and torso, cradles BOTH of the victim's legs, arches back with a suplex, and bridges for the pin.] Setup Move/Secondary Finisher New School Slam - Full Nelson Urange [Jason gets behind his opponent, and locks in a basic full nelson. He lifts up the opponent as if to deliver a full nelson slam, but as he is lifting, he slips his left arm out of the full nelson, moves his right arm across the victim's chest, and delivers a high-impact urange slam.] Signature Moves The Sophomore Jinx - Twisting Fisherman's Chokeslam with pin [Jason grabs the victim by the throat with his right hand, while with the left, he hooks the near leg. He then lifts the victim up in air, jerk-twists 180 degrees [like Rhyno's spinebuster], and then slams them down to the mat on their back, while maintaining both the choke and the hooked leg. Note, this is NOT a submission, but a pin. When the pin is broken, he readily releases the choke.] The Widowmaker – Reverse Crucifix ¾ Facelock Bulldog [Jason lifts the victim up so they are laying stomach first across his shoulder. He then places their hands under the victim's arms and extends their arms, so the victim is held in the air. With a heave, he pushes the victim up in the air. As the victim is falling to the mat, J-Core applies a 3/4 face lock (basically, a stunner-grip) and drops to the mat, impacting the victim's chin into his shoulder.] Learning Curve – High-Release Tiger Superplex [Jason sits his victim on the top rope, and underhooks both of their arms. He pulls the opponent up into a suplex, releasing them at the highest point of the arc, and sending both men crashing to the mat. Flowing Cradle Suplex Piledriver [J-Core applies a front face lock on the victim and throws the victim's near arm across their shoulders. Then he grabs the victim's near leg and hooks it. Jason lifts the victim so they are upside down, then shifts the victim so they are in front of their body. Jason sits down and drops the victim straight down so they land on their head. Done VERY quickly, like a snap piledriver.] Body Slam into Dragon Sleeper [Jason scoops the opponent up for a body slam, yet as he is slamming them, he moves his bicep so that it is crossing the victim’s neck. Jason holds both the victim's legs and drops down, driving the victim face first into the mat.] Vertical Stall Suplex into Tombstone Piledriver [Jason applies a front face lock on the victim and throws the victim's near arm across their shoulders. History: -Taken from the Centre Daily Times from State College, PA Local Wrestler Begins Again Most pro wrestlers don't get their 'start' at the age of 34, but then again...most pro wrestlers aren't Jason Core. Most of you remember Core as the phenom back in his days wrestling for Penn State. As a freestyle wrestler, he took home NCAA Division IA gold in both his junior and senior years ('88-'89, '89-90) and was one of the brightest prospects in a pro-wrestling world starting to realize amateur potential. Sadly, Jason's status as a 'can't miss' prospect, well...missed. After a few months on the amateur circuit, a vicious ACL tear, combined with the popping of his patella tendon in his left knee, caused him to take an early retirement from independent pro wrestling. After a year and a half of rehab and surgery, the fickle business of pro wrestling had passed him by... Jason was devastated, but he began to start over. He moved to the small town of River Plaza, New Jersey...and worked as a paralegal for a law firm down there. There, he met, fell in love with, and married Jenna Estep (Yeah, you heard me...). Jenna Core gave Jason a new lease on life, and he continued to rehabilitate, and was able to lead a normal life, as if no injury had ever hampered him. Unfortunately, life in an office did not suit Jason, and he was prone to mild fits of depression, despite his good situation. Something was missing in his life... In the summer of 1996, Jason suffered another terrible tragedy, as a drunk driver slammed into the side of his Camry, and fatally injured Jenna, and he mangled his right leg in the accident. Insurance took care of most of the fiscal damages, but Jason was left...simply...devastated, at the loss of his beloved wife. For a while, he sat in a cycle of depression. Finally, an old friend, and his family managed to pull him out of his sadness, and he began to live life anew. In 1997, with several loans, he opened up the Core Wrestling School (CWS) in Detroit, Michigan. Finally, Jason could have the professional part of his life to be what he truly wanted, in the business he loved. Jason trained some of the best and brightest of the 'Attitude' generation of sports entertainment...with a strong emphasis on promos as well as the physical aspect of wrestling. Strongly against 'spot wrestling' Jason's school was famous for showcasing strengths, and masking/healing weaknesses. The wrestlers that emerged from the school are some of the top stars in the field today. With the 'Attitude' generation, came some true 'Attitude.' Jason refused admittance to a certain student, as the school became more elite at the end of 2000. The angry student set fire to the CWS building in downtown Detroit. Once again, all Jason's hopes and dreams became ash. Jason returned to State College for a little while, but the familiar settings of Happy Valley stifled him. He packed up and moved out to Tallahassee, Florida...and moved in with a high-school friend and physical therapist. With his assistance, Jason decided to rehab his body, and prepare to achieve his lifelong dream, a career as a professional wrestler...for the purpose of gaining enough finances to rebuild his wrestling school, as well as to achieve his dream. He rehabilitated for nine months, until he was in 'performance' condition again, then toured with a few independent federations, quickly picking up a head of steam with a combination of natural physical charisma, and excellent microphone skill. Yesterday, Jason was finally signed to a conditional PCW contract, fully entering him into the 'big time'. He has had four major knee surgeries, a fused vertebrae, and several broken bones and concussions over his amateur and professional wrestling career. Yet none of these have prevented him from making the jump into the career he's always wanted. Jason's a man filled with hope, determination, and desire...and nothing in this world can stop him now... After being fired from his PCW contract, Jason worked with the CWS for a while, before being signed to a CCW contract to counteract the losses of people such as the Supreme Machine and Ricky Thunder to PCW. He and Kensuke Suzahara have formed 'The Cause' a faction for the redemption of CCW. Because everyone needs a hero.
