CCW match: Andrew Hawk vs. The Gobstopper

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Andrew Hawk vs. The Gobstopper Date: 03/27/03 Show: Lockdown **'Born To Be Wild' by Steppenwolf hits the P.A. system as the crowd immediately erupts in a chorus of boo's.** Nayt Willems: Andrew 'Ace' Hawk! **Andrew steps out from behind the curtains onto the ramp, smirking as the heat he recieves grows more intense. He slowly makes his way down the ramp, topping once to spit at some of the fans who show him no love in return.** BJS: There he is... our Operations Manager. JD: Eh? BlackJack, sit down! BJS: No...! Rise, Joey! Applaud your General Manager! These fans are just in denial of his greatness...! JD: ...Yeah. As we saw last week, Andrew 'Ace' Hawk had the honor of facing off against Adam Christ in his debut match here in CCW. Adam almost even had the upset win, but he got himself disqualified. BJS: Almost had the upset win!? Andrew cleaned the floor with him! Adam had to use a chair just to get out of the match alive! **Andrew 'Ace' Hawk climbs into the ring, leaning against the far turnbuckle as he awaits the appearance of Adam Christ. Seconds go bye as confusion fills the air, until an official finally runs down the ramp and climbs into the ring, having a little chat with the ring announcer. Seconds later, Nayt speaks...** Nayt Willems: I've been told that Adam Christ has left the arena. Therfor, this is now a singles competition match! BJS: Ha. I knew it. JD: As we saw earlier, Adam Christ left the arena after assaulting Yama Tatsu with a crowbar. We're still left without reason for him doing so, but-- BJS: But nothing! The rookie missed out on his opportunity, and he can pay for it! Besides, he would have just slowed Gobstopper and Ace down. Nayt Willems: And his opponent... from Anaheim, California... weighing in at 250 pounds... **'Machine Head' by Bush hits the P.A. system and the crowd erupts in a gigantic pop.** BJS: Jesus! My ears!! Nayt Willems: The Gobstopper!! **The Gobstopper steps out from behind the curtains, somewhat surprised to recieve such a grand pop, but shrugs it off as he makes his way to the ring.** BJS: Argh...! Why the hell are they screaming so loud!? JD: Well, I think their hatred for Andrew makes them cheer louder for whomever he's against. In this case, Gobstopper. BJS: I guess... but DAMN! **Gobstopper climbs into the ring and the bell rings.** *Ding! Ding! Ding!* BJS: Here we go! Gobstopper steps up to Ace and... holds out his hand? JD: Gobstopper wants to shake Ace's hand...! Is this a sign of respect...? BJS: Puh... I think it's a trick! And so does Ace, as he's hesitant to even extend his fingers! JD: The crowd seems to have a mixed reaction to this, as Ace finally takes Gobstopper's hand and the two shake. Andrew, on alert, quickly jerks back his hand and steps back a few feet. BJS: I guess it wasn't a trick... but can we get this match going!? JD: I think they heard you, because the two lock up! Ace gets the upper hand, however, putting Gobstopper in a headlock. Gobber pushes Ace to the ropes, Ace returns... Gobber attempts a shoulder block but Ace, having the size and weight advantage, knocks Gobber onto his back! BJS: That'a boy! JD: Ace whips himself off the ropes. Gobber rolls under Ace, whom leaps over him to whip off of the opposing ropes... Gobber jerks to his feet and Ace shoots for a clothesline, but Gobber ducks it! Gobstopper reaches back... neckbreaker! BJS: Yow! JD: Ace holds his head as Gobber quickly returns to his feet, grabbing Ace and pulling him up as well. Gobber whips Ace to the ropes, whom returns... and Gobstopper sends him to his back with a short-arm clothesline! BJS: Gobber quickly gets back to his feet, kicking away at Ace's torso. JD: The referee won't allow that, as Gobber's keeping Ace from getting back up. The referee pulls Gobstopper off of Ace... and gives Ace enough time to get to his feet! Ace shoves the referee out of the way and lays Gobber out with a thunderous clothesline! BJS: There we go! JD: Ace quickly jerks Gobber back to his feet and throws him over the top rope! BJS: Lundon bridges falli-- JD: Shut the fuck up. BJS: ... JD: ...Gobstopper's on the outside, cradeling his right shoulder due to falling on it. Ace climbs out of the ring as well, pulling Gobber back to his feet... but Gobber springs back to life, delivering hard rights and lefts to Ace's face! BJS: The referee's starting the count...! 1... JD: Gobber grabs Ace's arm and whips him harshly to the steel steps... but Ace reverses, sending Gobber crashing into them instead! 2... 3... JD: Gobber looks hurt as Ace steps up to him... but Gobber gathers enough strength to suddenly boot Ace in the gut and--HOLY! *CRACK!* BJS: HEY! THAT'S NOT LEGAL, IS IT?! JD: Gobber just delivered a harsh DDT onto Ace... on the steel steps! Ace's down, and Gobber's pulling himself back to his feet! 4... 5... JD: Gobber climbs up on the ring apron, waiting for the fallen Ace to get back to his feet. Ace slowly does, and his left eyebrow's busted open! Ace stands wobbily, turning to face Gobber... who leaps at him! But Ace catches him and runs forward, cracking Gobstopper's spine against the ring post! BJS: Ooh... that's gotta hurt! 6... 7... JD: Ace pulls Gobber up and rolls him back into the ring, climbing into the ring, himself. Ace, now aware that he's bleeding, looks like he's lost it! BJS: Man, he's pissed! JD: Ace pulls Gobber back to his feet... and whips him to the turnbuckle! Ace charges full speed at Gobber, but Gobber gets a foot up and nails Ace in the chin! Ace stumbles back, and Gobber leaps forward with another clothesline, knocking Ace off of his feet! BJS: This match should've already been Ace's! That DDT onto the steel steps should have gotten Gobber disqualified! JD: Gobber rolls himself on top of Ace...! 1... 2... KICKOUT! JD: Gobber pulls Ace back to his feet, quickly whipping him to the ropes. Ace returns, and Gobber sends him head over heels with a hip toss! BJS: Ace's down, but not for long! JD: Gobber quickly drags Ace back to his feet, immediately booting him in the gut! Gobber shoves Ace's head between his leg... BJS: THE GOB DROP?! NO!! JD: Wait... Ace manages to get a hold of Gobber's legs and pulls them right from under him! Gobber falls back, and Ace begins situating him for the Harley Grip! BJS: YES!! JD: Ace tries to roll Gobber to his chest, but Gobber refuses to let him! Ace tries harder... but Gobber arches forward and grabs Ace's head, pulling it down and putting him in a roll-up! 1... 2... 3--NO! BJS: Ace kicked out! JD: Gobstopper slowly gets to his feet, but Ace gets up almost immediately! Ace hammers sharply down at Gobber's back, over and over...! BJS: Beat him down...! JD: Ace pulls Gobber to his feet, hooking his head... DDT! BJS: Make him BLEED! REVENGE!! BWaHAHAHAHHAHAhahAHHA! JD: ... BJS: ... JD. ...Ace hooks Gobstopper's leg! 1... 2... 3--Kickout! BJS: Beat 'em some more, Ace! JD: Ace returns to his feet, cussing at the referee a little for it not being a three count, and he pulls Gobber up as well. Ace sends Gobber back down with a quick snap suplex, returning to his feet to be greeted with a flurry of boo's from the fans! BJS: Eh, what do they know!? JD: Ace eats it up, using their hate for him to feed his anger towards Gobstopper! Ace rushes to a nearby turnbuckle, climbs it, and leaps off for a frog splash attempt... but Gobber rolls out of the way, making Ace crash chest-first onto the mat! BJS: Ah...! Get up, Ace! Get up...!! JD: Ace holds his chest in pain as Gobber gets back to his feet, shaking some of the cobwebs out. Gobber pulls Ace back to his feet and whips him to the turnbuckle. Gobber quickly pulls Ace up to the top turnbuckle, climbing up as well... and he leaps into the air, wrapping his legs around Ace's head and flipping back! Super hurricanrana! *WHAM!!* BJS: Jesus!! JD: Ace's down, and so is Gobber! However, Gobber gets back to his feet... and he signals for the end! The crowd loves it! BJS: The end?! It's not over...!! JD: Gobstopper climbs a nearby turnbuckle, leaping off... Candy Coating!! WAIT!! BJS: HA! ACE ROLLED OUT OF THE WAY! Gobber crashes to the mat HARD! JD: Both men are down, but Ace crawls to a corner... holding his head in pain. BJS: Is it because of the DDT? JD: I'd guess so, but I haven't a clue. Wait... someone's coming down the ramp...! BJS: It's Deborah Carmichael! JD: She has something in her hand... a bottle of water? BJS: Andrew has a water girl! ...Lucky bastard. JD: Ace's looking at her... he doesn't look too pleased. Debra offers the water to Andrew, but he shoves her away. Debra, however, doesn't listen and squeezes some water on Andrew's face. ...The water washes some of the blood off of his face. BJS: She's helping Andrew gain his strength back...! JD: Andrew takes the bottle, pulling himself back to his feet. Meanwhile, Gobber's slowly getting to his feet as well. Andrew takes a few big gulps of the bottled water and tosses it aside, turning to Gobber and screaming at him to return to his feet. BJS: That must've been magic water! Andrew doesn't look hurt at all, anymore! He looks completely focused! JD: Gobber's up, and he slowly make shis way to Andrew, swinging for a clothesline... but Andrew ducks it! Gobber turns around and is met with the Cannon Kick to the chin! The fans EXPLODE in heat! BJS: Who cares!? Andrew's going to finish this thing!! JD: Andrew quickly rushes to Gobber's legs, lifting them up and situating him... rolling hom over... HARLEY GRIP!! Andrew gets the Harley Grip on Gobber! Gobber screams in pain!! BJS: TAP! TAP!! JD: Gobstopper reaches for the ropes, desperately trying to get out of the ropes... but can't make it! Gobstopper taps! Gobstopper taps!! *Ding! Ding! Ding!* Nayt Willems: The winner of this contest... and #1 Contender for the Trans-Atlantic Championship... Andrew 'Ace' Hawk!! JD: Andrew's won it, but he won't release the move off of Gobber! The match is OVER! BJS: Andrew's lost it...! Awesome!! JD: For fucks sake…just release the hold! “Echo Side” hits as Sylkk appears at the top of the ramp laughing like a mad man. Ace glances up as Sylkk signals for him to break the hold. Ace rolls out of the ring and heads up the ramp to where Sylkk is standing. View Andrew 'Ace' Hawk 's Biography Back
