Clockwork Tangerine, 11 February 2003

11 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From From: Lance Sullivan To: CCW Office Subject: Clockwork Tangerine 2/11/03 Welcome back to another edition of Clockwork Tangerine. As always I’m your host who can boast the most roast, while still making a toast, Lance Sullivan. As you might have noticed there was no edition of Tangerine last week, as the blizzard of ’03 tore through and completely ruined everything for me. I’m not making excuses, I’m just saying if I can ever track the rat bastard down, I’mma kick El Nino square in the balls. But enough of that, two weeks ago I promised you my loyal Tangeriney’s an interview with Union Rep Brad Lamen, and I always keep my promises, so without further adieu. Here we go, as always in it’s raw and unedited best is the Clockwork Tangerine interview with Brad Lamen. LS: Brad Lamen, it's a pleasure to have you here, can I call you Brad? BL: Uh......who are you? Do I know you? How'd you get past security? SECURITY!!!!!! LS: Um I'm Lance Sullivan sir, I write Clockwork Tangerines for CCW In Demand, and you don't need to call security LS: Remember we WERE going to have an interview last week but you got snowed in BL: Interview? I don't think I approved THAT! LS: Um, I'm sorry but you did Brad, isn't this your signature? BL: Hmmmm....*looks at paper that says "I, Brad Lamen, approve of any interview with this fellow." BL: Yeah, I guess it is..... BL: Go ahead. LS: Well, then good. First I'd like to talk a little bit about Jason Dread, what's your thoughts on him? BL: Who? BL: Jason Dread? LS: Yes Jason Dread, remember him, rookie? Bad Haircut? Crappy Music? Number One Contender? Any of that ring a bell? BL: OH! The "what you...DREAD!" dude. Yeah, I remember that douche. My thoughts on Jason Dread is that he is a rookie with a bad haircut, crappy music, and should not be the number one contender. How's that? LS: Haha, very good. But more than that, how is it that he's been able to foil your plans thus far? I mean we all know you're one of the smartest guys in the business, how in the hell has this rookie been able to do that? BL: Basically, I've been drunk...really, really drunk. I mean, I haven't done much of anything besides kiss the bottle lately and that's how he managed to fool me, the crazy trickster! BUT, I ran out of PBR last week so the well's running dry and my mind is kicking into overgear. It's time for a BRAD LAMEN FEEDING FRENZY in CCW and Jason Dread is the first one to get bit. BL: That didn't sound gay, did it? BL: God, I hope not. BL: I'm not gay! LS: Good good, I'm glad to hear that....no not quite LS: of course you're not, but I'm sure there are plenty of guys that wish you were BL: Like that fruitball "Sly" Sammy Fox and like that weirdo Ace......oops, he's on my side...I mean...Acepen Johnstonvitz........ LS: Yes yes of course. Now moving on, recently you've been seen having words with another rookie, one Michael Stevens. What exactly is the nature of that relationship? BL: Michael Stevens is an obvious Brad Lamen wannabe. I think it's cute, in a purely heterosexual way, how he basically rips off my entire schtick and tries to make it his own. Of course, he's below mediocre and looks like a fucking retard, but he's STILL an adorable Lamen fan. I like it. BL: Did that sound gay? LS: A bit yeah.... BL: Fuck. LS: But, I'm sure you didn't mean it that way. So you want to take him under your wing, is that it? BL: Yeah, under my wing. I mean, look at the Lamen track record. Anyone I work with becomes a star: Jaysin Urban, Enigma, Sylkk, Homeless Bob...the list goes on and on. You could put a sack of shit with me and I'd get it over. Brad Lamen is a heat magnet and anyone lucky enough to get my rub...err....gay sounding...uhmm.....get my stroke...FUCK!!!....get my touch....JESUS CHRIST!!!... Let's just say anyone lucky enough to team with me will benefit from my immense talent. LS: And an immense talent it certainly is. Now moving on, what are your thoughts on the new CCW as opposed to what it was before? Better, worse? BL: CCW is better now that Sylkk is in charge. That much is obvious. How else would have the shrewd business sense to make Brad Lamen the Union Rep? Look at what Sylkk has done for this company and then TRY to tell me we aren't better off. Sylkk is a god and you all should recognize this PRONTO. LS: Hey I DO recognize this, Sylkk was the one that got ME a job for chirst sakes. Anyway it looks ilke we're quickly running out of time here, so perhaps we should wrap this up with a little word association, how does that sound? BL: Well, I WAS a psychology major in college, so I should be good to go. LS: Umm I thought Sociology was your major? BL: Whatever BL: Bring on the questions LS: Alright. Jason Dread BL: I dread having to watch two more seconds of his promos. LS: Michael Stevens BL: Needs improvement, could be great LS: Blayze BL: I liked him better the first time...WHEN HE WAS CALLED TUPAC....but he's aight. LS: Lacey Skye Morris BL: It was fun handcuffing her and abusing her, and it was almost as fun when I did it in the ring. LS: Sylkk BL: Uhh......President? LS: Ace Hawk BL: I wish he was named Foghorn Leghorn instead, and everytime he talked he went, "wellaah, I sayah, I sayah, what are you doing, kid?". That would be hilarious. In fact, I'm going to suggest this gimmick change to him later. LS: And finally The Gobstopper BL: If I was going to name myself after a candy, it would be Payday because I'm fucking MONEY! OR, how about not. Naming yourself after a candy is lame as hell. That dude should seriously hit the bricks and not stop till he's in heavy traffic and a semi is heading right for him. LS: Alright, well it looks like we're just about done here, unless you've got any final words to add? BL: Yeah, I DO! First off, I just want to say this has been the biggest waste of my time EVER. I feel DUMBER after having to sit here for however long, listening to your pompous bullshit. Way to be full of yourself, guy. Secondly, I don't like foreigners, except for Haitians. AND THIRD, skokie. That is all. LS: Skokie? BL: Skokie. LS: ummm alright LS: well, despite your thoughts about me, it's been a real pleasure to have you here Brad LS: thanks for your time BL: Beat it, pegleg. I've got work to do. Certainly interesting wasn’t it? Obviously Brad doesn’t think to highly of me, but never worry, I know you all love me. And now with that out of the way, let’s get to your Lockdown preview. Lockdown comes to you this week from Anniston AL. And will feature the opening rounds of the Lethal Lottery Tournament for CCW tag team titles. The lottery has created some interesting pairing and match ups for this week. Our first match up, teams Lacey Morris, with Enigma, to take on rookie Forme Carlos, and his partner Brad Douglas. Truly the real story of this match will almost certainly be what if anything Mr. Raymond has in story for Lacey Morris. And hell even if there’s nothing this will still be a great contest. The second match of the evening pits new comer rookie Toxic and his partner the nutcase Gabe Nyte, who has actually promised NOT to be in attendence for the show, against two more crazies in Jack Manson, and Adam Christ. Now if Gabe actually shows up, we’ve got three psychos and a guy noone knows anything about in one match, and if Gabe doesn’t show up, you’ve got a guy noone knows anything about thrown to the wolves. This one should be funny and brutal at the same time. Next up, for our third match we’ve got one of those interesting match up and combinations I was talking about when Scorpion teams up with Michael Stevens, against rookie-ette Minx, and Stevens’s former woman Sheena Ramone. Personally I’ve got no freakin idea what the hell is gonna happen here, but I can tell you I’m dying to see it. Then we’ve got, get this Jason Dread, and BLAYZE TEAMING UP! Those two will take on Korianne, and Yama Tatsu. The real question here is whether or not Blayze and Dread can co-exist with so much bad blood already between them. And to make matters more interesting they BOTH unknowing signed contracts saying that if they touched each other before the PPV they’d be fired. This would should be an edge of your seat match up, and personally I’d be glad to see Dread snap and get his bad haircut ass fired. Coming off that one, it’s Brad Lamen, and Devyl teaming up to take on Ace Hawk, that the Gobber. Well I can stand three out of the four anyway. I smell set up here, and I think the target is planted firmly on the back of the Gobber. It’ll be great to see just how brutally he gets beaten. And for the final match of the evening, it’s going to be the Independent title final, as Yama Tatsu and Scorpion square off. Scorpion is teamed up with Stevens earlier in the evening, a man who clearly doesn’t like Yama Tatsu, it’ll be interesting to see if Stevens will have gotten in Scorp’s ear about maybe taking a little revenge out on Yama about the arm injury he inflicted on Stevens two weeks ago. And beyond that it’s just two great established stars battling it out over gold, and quite frankly there ain’t nothing better than that. Well there you go folks, don’t forget to catch CCW Lockdown, check your local provider for listings. And on that note I must leave you again for another week, but have no fear I shall return, with next weeks Lockdown preview and another patented Clockwork Tangerine interview. With who you ask? Well I guess you’ll just have to read up next week and find out. So until Lamen actually agrees to spend the night in a hot tub with Sly Sammy, make mine CCW.
