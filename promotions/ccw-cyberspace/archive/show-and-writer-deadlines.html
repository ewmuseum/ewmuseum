Show and Writer Deadlines

c. 12 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A page listing the weekly deadlines for angles, roleplay grades, outcomes and writers’ shows for the two weekly shows, plus the days for bio, e-mail address and news submissions.
