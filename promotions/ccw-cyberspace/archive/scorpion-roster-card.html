Nicknames: 'The Revolution' 'The New Era' Status: Face Manager(s): None Current Teams: New Era Alliance Entrance Music: 'Enter Sandman' By: Metalica Wrestling Style: All-Rounder Signature Moves: T-Bone Suplex, Frog Splash, Double Arm DDT, Flapjack, Tornado DDT, Sleeper Drop, Jacknife Powerbomb, Twist of fate, swanton bomb, rolling thunder, chokeslam, crippler crossface, tazmission, acid drop Set Up Move: Tornado DDT (Puts oppenent in corner in a DDT move and spins off the top doing a tornado DDT ) Finisher: Tail Whip (The Rock Bottom ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: (1) CCW Hardcore Champion, (1) CCW Pacific Champion Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: Ranked #4 in PTC for the week of 5/13 Other Federations: CWF, HWF, ESW, ICW, WWCF Other Federation Awards: (1) CWF World Champion, (1) HWF U.S. Champion, (1) HWF Extreme Champion, (1) HWF Tag Team Champion, (1) ESW Intercontiental Champion Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: Scorpion started his career on July 7, 2001 in the now defunct Championship Wreslting Federation. Scorpion was one of the first to sign with the CWF and his first week as a wrestler he won the CWF World Championship and was crowned the first ever champion in the CWF. Scorpion's regin as champion there would end when the CWF closed for good on July 31st, 2001. Scorpion then signed with another upstart federation, the Hardcore Wrestling Federation. Scorpion was the first ever wrestler to sign with the HWF on August 1st, 2001. Scorpion's tenure in HWF was one of his best in a federation. Scorpion captured the HWF United States Championship, HWF Extreme Championship and was one half of the Tag Team Champions with his long time friend, The Equalizer. The HWF would soon go bankrupt in the early part of October and right away Scorpion wnt on the free agent market and right away he found his new home for a while, the Extremely Sucidal Wrestling (ESW). Scorpion joined the ESW in the late part of October and he soon began to fly in the ESW. In Scorpion's third week with the ESW he captured the ESW's second most prized title, the Intercontinental Championship. While Scorpion was there Scorpion forged a friendship with the Crazy Boyz, Jordan Crazy also known as Primetime Championship Wrestling's own Jordan X Shabazz and his brother Chris Crazy. When the ESW fired Scorpion in early December, Scorpion went to International Championship Wrestling to join his friends the Crazy Boyz and Rich Sherwin. Scorpion's time in ICW only last one match as Scorpion and the owner did not see eye to eye. Scorpion walked out on the ICW and a month later in the early part of January 2002, Scorpion was once again reunited with the ESW. Scorpion's second go around with the ESW was also short lived as the ESW closed once again. With no where to go Scorpion put himself back on the free agent market and was soon picked up by the World Wide Chaos Federation. Scorpion was one of he first to sign with the WWCF in the mid to late part of January 2002 and Scorpion began to tear up in the WWCF igniting a rivalry with Tyler Davidson. There rivalry would clash on the very last WWCF show in a Royal Rumble match to crown the first WWCF World Champion. It was a intense battle in Madison Square Garden, Tyler Davidson and Scorpion were the final two in the match and in the end Tyler Davidson got the best of Scorpion eliminating him. Scorpion was informed that night that WWCF went bankrupt and had to close. With no where to go again and out of a job, Scorpion soon posted his resume in Primetime Central. Scorpion was contacted by six federations within the PTC but in the end Scorpion chose Cyberspace Championship Wrestling to be his new home. Scorpion debuted in the CCW February 14th, 2002 as he faced arch-rival, Tyler Davidson who also signed with the CCW. They re-kindled there rivalry in the CCW and Tyler Davidson got the win that night. But the two superstars forged a respect based friendship in the CCW. Ten days later Scorpion captured his first championships in the CCW in his first pay per view with the CCW (CCW Dead or Alive), Scorpion defeated John Jupiter and Concrete Pete in two seperate matches to win the CCW Pacific and Hardcore Championship on the same night. Scorpion would lose the CCW Pacific title a week later to former champ, John Jupiter. As the reigning Hardcore Champion, Scorpion would then lose a match four days later and then was not seen in the CCW until April 1st, 2002. Scorpion then returned was a fresh and ready to defeat all odds to prove to the CCW that he can raise to the top of the CCW ladder. Then in May 2002 Scorpion established a dominant faction in the CCW, The New Era Alliance. As the end of May whined down, Scorpion began to have a feud with The Fury, Danny Bryson, Carnage and Guardian. With the Fury breathing down his neck and ready to end his career Scorpion added new members Jack Panzer, Mark Estep and Chang to the New Era Alliance to begin to take down The Fury and The Cretin Family to prove that the NEA is the greatest stable ever.
