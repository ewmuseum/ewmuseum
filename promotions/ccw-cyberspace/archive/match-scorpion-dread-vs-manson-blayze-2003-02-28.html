CCW match: Scorpion & Jason Dread vs. Jack Manson & Blayze

28 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scorpion & Jason Dread vs. Jack Manson & Blayze Date: 2-28-03 Show: Lockdown NW: And now coming down the isle, followed by his tag partner for the evening, Jason Dread, SCORPION~! 'Enter Sandman' by Metalica begins blasting over the PA system, as the crowd comes to their feet. Dread and Scorp come out to a HUGE face pop, as they make their ways down to the ring. The two slide in almost simultaniously, before poping up to their feet. JD: The fans really love Scorpion and Dread, don't they Jack? BJS: No accounting for taste. NW: And now followed by his tag partner, Jack the Ripper Manson, he is the CCW Heavyweight Champion, BLAYZE~! The fans cheers immediately turn to boos as 'War Ensemble' by Jedi Mind Tricks ques up, and Manson and Blayze begin to make their way down to the ring. The duo stares up at Scorpion and Dread in the ring, before sliding in them selves and immediately rushing the faces. Manson and Scorpion tie up, and so do Dread and Blayze. Both Scorp and Dread gain the advantage on their respective opponents, sending them both to the outside, causing another big face pop from the crowd. Dread and Scorp play the crowd, pumping them up, while on the outside Manson, and Blayze regroup. JD: Dread and Scorpion, have cleaned house here early on~! BJS: Oh yeah like that actually means anything, Blayze and Manson haven't even gotten started yet. Dread goes outside onto the apron, as Manson climbs into the ring, and Blayze gets on the apron himself. The bell sounds as Manson and Scorpion lock up. Scorpion powers Manson into a neutral corner, and then begins to pound away with rights, and lefts. Scorp then takes Manson out of the corner, with a hip toss, and then begins to lay in the boots to the fallen Manson. Scorp pulls Manson back up, and sends him into the face corner, with an Irish whip, and then follows him in with a big splash. Scorp then tags in Dread, who hops off the top rope, and hits Manson with a DDT on the way down. Dread pops back up to his feet, and walks to the center of the ring, where he begins taunting Blayze. JD: I don't know how smart this is by Dread, he's got Manson on the ropes, but he's not following it up. BJS: Of course he isn't. Dread's nothing but a stupid rookie that won't amount to anything. He's making a lot of bad enemies really quick here in CCW. Dread turns back around, only to bit hit with a low blow from Manson. Manson pops up and begins nailing Dread in the back of the neck and shoulders with some stiff forearms, before lifting Dread up into the air in verticle suplex position. Manson holds him up there causing all the blood to rush to Dread's head, before dropping him down pretty much square on the back of his neck. Manson then goes down the mat, blatantly choking Dread, for a four count, until he's forced to break the hold. Manson then pulls Dread back up, and locks on a front headlock, and just bulls Dread into the corner, before tagging in Blayze. BJS: YES! BLAYZE IS IN~! JD: He might not be right now if Manson hadn't taken the low blow as a short cut. Blayze push Dread into the corner, and begins laying in knees to Dread's midsection. Blayze then mounts the first turnbuckle, and nails a quick series of ten punches to Dread's skull, before locking in a front headlock himself, and coming off with a tornado DDT! Blayze goes for the cover, but Dread kicks out in two. Blayze lays in a couple more boots before tagging Manson back in. Manson goes straight to the attack with a leg drop across Dread's windpipe, following up with a side headlock choke. Manson keeps that locked in before dropping the hold long enough to nail several punches to the side of Dread's skull. Manson then tags Blayze back in, who comes over the top with a vaulting moonsault. Blayze then pulls Dread back up, hitting a snap suplex, and then popping back up to his feet to taunt the fans. Dread begins trying to crawl into his corner where Scorpion is anxiously awaiting the tag, but Blayze cuts him off with a kick to the back of the head. JD: Dread desperately needs a tag here. BJS: Ok, you're now OFFICALLY Captain Obvious. But really do you think Dread will EVER get to tag? Blayze is way too smart to let something like that happen, he's our World Champ for God's sakes. Blayze pulls Dread up to his knees, by the hair, but gets caught with a punch to the mid section. Dread hammers away into Blayze's gut before getting to his feet. He delivers a staggering blow the the ear of Blayze sending him reeling back against the ropes. Dread moves in and plants another before attempting and irish whip, which Blayze reverses and drills Dread with a short arm clothesline. Blayze hits his knees and shakes out the cobwebs. He gets to his feet and heads over to again tag in Manson, who rushes into the ring. Scorpion jumps in to try and help his partner but is cut off by the ref. Chucky pushes Scorpion back into his corner as Manson calls for Blayze. Manson pulls out his garrote and begins choking Dread as Blayze lays those heavy boots to the midsection. JD: Come on Chucky turn around! BJS: Get Scorpion out of there!! Manson releases the choke and tucks the garrote back into his tights before laying a few more boots to Dread. Manson then drags Dread to his feet and delivers a stunning snap suplex, to which he rolls through and hooks the leg. Chucky: 1…2… BJS: Just a two count on the former GCW champ. JD: This rookie Manson is showing impressive abilities tonight. BJS: He is proving he wants this main event spot! Manson gets back to his feet, but drops an elbow to the forehead of Dread. He heads to the top rope and come s flying of with a double foot stomp aimed directly at the head of Dread. Dread rolls out of the way just in time as Manson shows obvious signs of disgust. Dread rolls on his side then to his knees. He explodes from his knees with a massive clothesline taking both men down. Chucky starts his count as both men lie motionless. Chucky: 1..2..3.. Dread slowly starts inching towards his corner… Chucky: 4..5..6..7… Manson makes his way up to a knee breaking the count. He sees Dread heading for the corner and launches a desperation lunge. No Avail. JD: DREAD HAD TAGGED IN SCORPION! BJS: FUCK HIM UP MANSON! Scorpion starts with boots to the back of Manson immediately followed up with an arsenal of elbow drops. Scorpion sees Blayze rushing in and meets him with a hard left sending him back out of the ring and to the floor. Scorpion whips Manson off the ropes… JD: FLAPJACK! Chucky slides into position to make the count…. Chucky: 1…2…3…. BJS: NO! So close yet so far! Blayze rolls in with the ring bell, but it is grabbed from behind by Dread. Dread swings the bell at Blayze splitting him wide open and sending him to the outside of the ring. Dread rolls out and continues working over Blayze with the ring bell, splattering his blood all over the floor. Back in the ring Scorpion has dragged Manson to an upright position. JD: Here it is Slater! Scorpion DDT! BJS: REVERSED!!! MANSON JUST NAILED SCORPION WITH A VERTABREAKER!!!!!!!! Chucky: 1…2…3!!!!!!!!! BJS: MANSON GETS THE PIN!!!!!! JD: HOLY HELL!
