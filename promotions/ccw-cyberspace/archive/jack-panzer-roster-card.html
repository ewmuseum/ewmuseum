What this is CCW roster card for Jack Panzer, with hometown, status, moves, title history and roleplay list: a face powerhouse with a special-forces back-story.
Jack Panzer Real Name: Capt. Alex Bierhoff Birthdate: September 30th 1972 Hometown: Berlin, Germany Height: 6'8' Weight: 290 lbs. Nicknames: 'The War Machine' Status: Face Manager(s): Nicole White Current Teams: none Entrance Music: 'Paranoid', by Ozzie Osbourne Wrestling Style: Powerhouse, Technician, Submissionist Signature Moves: Thunder Strike: DVD Set Up Move: Cannon Kick (Big Boot to the face ) Finisher: Blitzkrieg (360-degrees Jackhammer ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: CCW Independent Champion (x1) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: none Other Federations: none Other Federation Awards: PTC #2 ranked wrestler Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: none Jack Panzer is actually Alex Bierhoff, Captain of the German army special forces, that work with the United Nations to help restore peace in dangerous areas, ending civil wars and assisting the locals rebuild their homes and government. During a top secret mission to secure the location of a gold mine in Kenya, Alex's unit was ambushed by local hitmen, at the service of another country. Alex's unit fell, one by one, on the hands of the armed locals, and one by one, they were killed and mutilated. Only Capt. Bierhoff was spared, a mystery unsolved to this day, but to leave their mark on Alex, the local tribal chief grabbed a hot knife and made a huge cut in Alex's face, cutting his left eye and leaving a scar that goes from the top of his forehead to near his nose height, crossing his eye. Neadless to say, Alex was blinded of that eye forever. Alex was handed to the German authorities, who secretly judged and condemned him for misleading his men into mortal danger, and he was thrown in a german military base, to slowly fade from the colective thought. His wife and 6-year old daugher were told he was killed in action and received full military pension, but that didn't spare them the pain of losing their beloved one. After 3 years, the German government was warned that an independent journalist was investigating the ambush in Kenya and that he knew Alex was alive. Fearing a public backlash, the government ordered that Alex should be killed immediately. But, in a cinematografic escape, shown in the RP below, Alex managed to fool the german government and flee Germany, asking for protection of the US government. Now, Alex, with a different name, and a new life, used his army skills in combat to join the wrestling scene. Unfortunately, his german 'friends' continue to follow him and torture him psychologically, making him completely paranoid about everything and everybody. His emotional and physical scars made him turn into a sadistic monster in the ring when he loses his reason and gets an access of rage, who won't stop and spare anybody before he achieves his goals.
