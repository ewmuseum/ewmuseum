Tim Sonic Real Name: Tim Hill Birthdate: 26/12/1974 Hometown: Aintree Height: 6'4 Weight: 350lbs lbs. Nicknames: SonicBoom Status: Face Manager(s): Jamie Hill Current Teams: Entrance Music: Here to Stay by Korn Wrestling Style: Powerful Signature Moves: Writers Block, Set Up Move: Finish Line (Clothesline from Hell ) Finisher: Sonic Boom (Vertical Suplex into a DDT ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: UEF, NTICW Other Federation Awards: UEF UK champ, ironcore champ Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Tim Hill was born on a farm in England, there he was parented by John and Karen Hill. Between the ages of 7 and 16 he became a great admirer of horses and writing peoms. He went about setting up a stable, he started to enter low profile races with his horses and his jockey brother Jamie. His peom writing was exceptional as well. They began to win very easily with their horses and grew bigger and bigger until their main horse Zengawar won the Grand National. A couple of months later Tim saw wrestling on the TV and that also interested him alot. He bought himself some training weights and started to build up his muscles. While he was doing this his horses were still winning including his favourite horse Tim and Sonic, he was his favourite because he was his first horse and he loved him dearly. Unfortunately Tim and Sonic died leaving Tim devastated, he decided in honor of his favourite horse his wrestling name would be Tim Sonic and from their Tim Sonic was born .
