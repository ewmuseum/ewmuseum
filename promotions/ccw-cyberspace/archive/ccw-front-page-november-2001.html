TOP STORIES: ANOTHER SEVERE INJURY STRIKES CCW MANAGEMENT SSN Proved to be quite in interesting show. however, another injury has struck a member of CCW management. Our beloved President Spider suffered a neck injury from the hands of Former Trans-Atlantic Champion Kensuke Suzahara, and his new move "The Stranger". Some early reports say that Spider suffered a broken neck, which could end his career. Others say it's as not as bad as it seems. We will keep everyone posted on the situation. In Other News E-mails will be sent out sometime this week for interviews regarding The VP position. Thanks to you, the fans of CCW, CCW Radio will go on the air, LIVE @ 10 PM EST on Saturday. Come one Come all, let's make this show interesting.
