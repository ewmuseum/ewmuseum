Truth, Justice and Objective Morality" Favorite Weapon: His Bare Hands Debut: August 14, 2002 Superstar Background: Raised on the streets of Toronto, Pete was a hard nosed kid who was known as someone you didn't f*** around with. He found work in the assorted physical trades, construction, plumbing or factory work. Not able to make a living, at the age of 18 he was introduced to street fighting. Putting his physical power and experience from the streets to good use, Pete was able to make ends meat. He was feared throughout the street fighting circuits, known as a vicious fighter who spared no mercy for his opponents. It is from here that Pete gained the name Concrete Pete, his trademark finishing move was a powerbomb onto the cement floor of the street. But still not content with his earnings Pete became frustrated and in desperation signed a contract with a small independent Canadian wrestling federation. He quickly showed the professional world that he is in fact a threat to all, gaining a number of championships. But still Pete was not content with the money he was making, and when he was offered a very lucrati Even Pete himself, will admit that he was not prepared for the SCW and life in large federations. Satisfied with his large paycheck, Pete's effort quickly drained, and he became a nobody with in a matter of weeks. Upset at this, Pete assessed his situation and the situation of professional wrestling and came up with a few observations. Firstly he realized that wrestling is a corrupt business where a kill or be killed attitude exists. Secondly Pete noticed that the corrupt nature of wrestling was due to the fact that federations were run by heels or allowed heels to do as they please. And finally Pete understood that the most important part of professional wrestling is not the money, fame or championships. but rather it is the adoration of fans that counts. So Concrete Pete made a decision in life, that he would uphold justice where ever he was. No longer would he let stables run amok and control a federation. No longer would he allow Presidents, or comissioners run the federation unfairly. It was his duty t Pete fought his way up the roster ladder, becoming the SCW universal champion. But just as things were looking good the SCW President G-Man demanded conformity from the entire roster, and declared that Concrete Pete should forfeit his title. Pete stood up against the entire SCW, and quit after defeating the entire roster in a gauntlet match. Realizing his mission was not finished Pete found himself searching for a new federation to sign with. Upon hearing about Concrete Pete's free agency, CCW management quickly offered him a contract, which Pete signed quite readily. Even though he accomplished such feats as being the number one contender fot the heavyweight title and even won the Hardcore title, breaking Enigma's long standing reign, Pete never really made the impact he sought to make in the CCW. But now he's back, amidst a new generation of wrestlers. Now he is one of the veterans. Now it is his time. It's now or never for Concrete Pete!
