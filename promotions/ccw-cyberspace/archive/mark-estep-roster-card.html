Mark Estep Real Name: Marcus James Estep Birthdate: 5.23.75 (he's 26) Hometown: Born in El Paso, Texas, resides in Chicago, Illinois Height: 6'4'' Weight: 225lbs lbs. Nicknames: 'The Malicious One' Status: Face Manager(s): His Fiance, Amber Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Cocky' by Kid Rock Wrestling Style: Brawler, Hardcore, Highflying tendencies Signature Moves: The Hammer Down (A Standing Chokeslam), The Jake Break (Superkick) Set Up Move: Manslaughter (Fishermans Suplex off the top rope ) Finisher: The Estepper (Vertical Suplex into a sit down DDT ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: None yet Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: None yet Other Federations: PCW, XCWF, NTICW, PWA Other Federation Awards: XCWF Grandslam and Triple Crown champion Handler Information Handler Name: Gary AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] On May 23, 1975 in El Paso, Texas, Frank and Anne Estep gave birth to a very healthy baby boy. Three days later, Mark Estep laid his eyes on his house for the first time. A few years go by, and Mark Estep is now 2 years old. His parents knew that he was going to be a powerfull person later on in life, when they came in to see him shatter a glass rattle on the side of his crib. Five years go past as quickly as they had come. Mark was now 7 years old, and outside his house was a couple of teenagers, breaking windows in an old, abandoned house. Mark walks across the street to talk to the kids. After some teasing and fun making, Mark attempted to fit in by casting his own stone at the old house. The window shattered, the police were called, and before Mark knew it, he was in the back of a police car. His first offence was at the age of 7. He got out of that incident safe, but later on in life, he wouldn't be so lucky. He went into Middle School, and graduated above average in his class, but nothing to be excited about. Later on in Highschool, he was introduced to Drugs and Alchohol, and he got addicted quickly. He tried quiting, and began to play football as El Paso High. Mark, with the physical ability that he has, easily made captain and All American for his high school. But, that wasn't the last of his story. At the beginning of his Junior Year at El Paso High, Mark Estep managed to fail the drug test that he had to take before he could play football. He was kicked off the team, and expelled from school after a few more small incidents that pushed his principal over the edge. A few days after getting expelled, Mark got a phone call from his uncle, Jason Core. Jason told Mark about the death of his Aunt, Jenna Estep-Core. Mark was devistated, and he didn't feel like living anymore. Mark flew to River Plaza, New Jersey and went to the funeral for his aunt. At the funeral, Mark and his mother had a slight falling out between two hurt hearts, and Mark stole his mothers credit card, and caught the first flight away from there. Away from home, away from family, away from El Paso, Texas. Mark got on the first plane, and he ended up landing in Chicago, Illinois. After a few months of barely making it in Chicago as a check out clerk at a nearby grocery store, Mark got a call from his Uncle. Jason invited him to take part in a new wrestling school, the Core School of Wrestling, in River Plaza, New Jersey. Mark flew to New Jersey, joined the school, and was one of the top students, before landing a job in the NTICW. Towards the end of 2000, and after a job change from NTICW to PCW2, Mark Estep got another call from his uncle saying that his school had been burned down. Jason was alright, but the building wasn't, and that was the end of the CSW. Mark later on went to win the Extreme title from Ken Kaze in the NTICW, become a contendor for the TV and Extreme titles in PCW, and a Grandslam Champion in XCWF.
