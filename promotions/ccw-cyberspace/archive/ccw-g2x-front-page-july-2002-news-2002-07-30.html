CCW: G2X Front Page (July 2002 News)

What this is The relaunch front page with dated announcements about the first card, the fed’s tie to a wider e-fed community, how cards, roleplays and rankings work, and second-character rules, plus deadlines.

Click the link to the right to see who your partner will be for the lethal lottery. You have until Sunday at 6pm EST to RP. You may cut interviews and promos as often as youd like. Good luck to everyone in the competition. PTC Interaction 07/29/02: I have been getting alot of questions as to whether or not CCW will be a part of PTC. The answer, yes. I think that we could benefit in our rebuilding. As for interaction, I would like to see us ripping up the trash talk boards and showing some of the new comers to PTC, exactly WHY CCW was always one of the top feds there. Cards, RP's and Rankings 07/29/02: The first card, the lethal lottery for the Tag team titles, will be posted Wednesday morning. That gives you all, 4 days to RP, and a week to cut your interviews and backstage promos for the card. Your interviews will have an effect on your rankings. They will not be the deciding factor but they will have a say so. The key factors to rankings are, Wins, RP quality, Interviews, and angle interaction. Good luck to everyone with the first card upcoming. What about characters? IF the roster isn't full come time for the second card, I will open the gates for a second character. This option is only available to those who I know can handle the workload of two. They will also be on a first come first serve basis. If we only have room for 5 then the first 5 qualified applicants will get their second character.
