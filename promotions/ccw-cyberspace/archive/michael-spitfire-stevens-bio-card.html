Michael 'Spitfire' Stevens Real Name: Michael Stevens (Shocker) Birthdate: 5/6/78 (hardy har har) Hometown: Greenwhich, CT Height: 6'3 Weight: 242 Nicknames: Spitfire (Amazing! Who'da thunk it?) Status: Heel Entrance Music: Cringe by Alkaline Trio Information Wrestling Style: Technical/submission Set Up Move: More of an alternate finisher the Inferno DDT (Double underhook DDT) Finisher: Darwinian Dreams (Million Dollar Dream Sleeper) Title History: None, I just got here, I suck (cries) Award History: (weeps) Other Federations: GCW, US Champ Tag Champ AWA US Champ EwC World Champ(X3) EwC Tag title Other Federation Awards: GCW Rampage Tournament winner Quotes History It's not easy growing up the best, but I did it, and damnit I sure made it look easy. Excelling in all my classes, the unquestioned smart jock on campus that got all the ladies, I had it all, I was perfection. Hell I still am perfection, but you know blah. Did I mention I'm rich, yeah I tend to forget I've got more money than the rest of you losers sometimes. It was pretty hard figuring out what to do after High School. I could go to college, I had scholarships to every Ivy League school in the country, and some that weren't. I'd been captain of the wrestler team, four time All-State, two time All America, not to mention my four time Scholastic All-American honors. But College just didn't appeal to me, more years of rotting away in a school where I was smarter and better than everyone else. The family business was out, like I really cared about mergers and shit like that. I coulda just lived the fat life on Daddy's dime, but that wasn't pro-active enough. Nah, I wanted something better, something different. I'd always been impressed with Pro-Wrestling, I guess that's why I joined the High School Team in the first place, but there was something different about the pro's. It was acting, theatrics, I liked that. I'd always had a flair for preforming, so I figured what the hell, and gave it a shot. I trained with some guy, don't even remember his name really, said he was one of the best there ever was, I think he was right, but it didn't matter. What really mattered is I was good time, actually I was great. I graduated from his 'school' in less than three months, faster than anyone had before, and shortly I was on my own. Out in the world, wrestling in arenas and gyms all over the country, I made a pretty big name for myself. Whether it was my flaming red hair, my dazzling green eyes, my perfectly chisled physique, or just my talent, I don't know, but I got noticed quickly. People loved me, or more accurately they loved to hate me. A guy named Adam Rushlow called me, President of Eastern Wrestling Coalition (EwC) said he had a spot that I'd be perfect for. Seems his world champ had gotten injured, and he needed someone to pick up the slack, that's where I came in, and met Shane Ransom, the aforementioned World Champ. He was a good guy, a lot like me, we became fast friends. I carried the company on my back for about half a year, before it folded. I figured it was back to the road again, but Shane introduced me to a Travis Gray, head of the American Wrestling Alliance (AWA). I had some good times there, never really clicked though, it was just too, stuffy for my tastes. So it wasn't long before I headed for the greener pastures of Global Championship Wrestling. Steven Caldera was the President, and man he loved me. He put me in his highly successful 'Establishment' stable, along with World Champ Jason Dread. The three of us, along with Jason O'Neil were pretty tight, it was some good times. It was around that time I met up with my old buddy Shane Ransom for a poker game, and I ended up winning his hired lackey Curiously Bruce The Gay Midget, a very helpful little guy, even if he does have a habit of humping my leg every once and a while. About a month later, I met Sheena Ramone, a hot little punk chick with pink hair, who wanted to get into the business, and she wasn't afraid to sleep her way to the top to do it. Safe to say I liked her immediately, even if I did and do have to deal with her cousin Jimmie The Retarded Sailor, I call him that cause well he's a tard, and he wears the same freakin sailor suit everyday, nevermind it reeks and it's prolly twenty sizes too small, oh well at least he can swing a chair, usually hits me though. But I digress. Anyway, GCW was some good times, but as they say, all good things must come to an end, and Steven closed the doors on GCW, forever. I wasn't happy to see it go, especially since it meant I was out of a job, AGAIN. I'd thought to retire, but it wasn't to be, when the Jason's came a calling about Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, a company that was just getting restarted. They said they could prolly use a guy like me around, so I figured what the hell, and signed up. So that's where I'm at today, ready to kick it old school like an old fool, and tear the roofs off every arena I come across, damn, it's good to be me.
