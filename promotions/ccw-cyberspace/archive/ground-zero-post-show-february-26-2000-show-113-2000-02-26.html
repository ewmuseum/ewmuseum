Ground Zero Post Show - February 26, 2000 (Show #113)

26 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Card and staged post-show summary of the Ground Zero pay-per-view of February 26, 2000 from Las Vegas, narrated by the CCW president.
GROUND ZERO February 26, 2000 from The MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Nevada - Show #113 "Fat Ass" Phil Burner, Mr. Gobstopper, Sylkk, GOD, Pepper The Cat, Tim Inman, Ghettodogg, Mash, Impact, Diablo, Hardcore Gorilla Mask, Blue Thunder, Corey James, Carnage, Ice Chamber - Battle Royal - CCW Heavyweight Championship Jim "Klown" Bowe vs. "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship The Titans vs. The Disciples - CCW Tag Team Championship Kelly Bowe vs. Delilah - CCW Women's Championship Hard Candy vs. The Untouchables vs. The Masked Duo - Triple Threat Tag Match Savannah Hilton, Stephanie Black, Becky Butterscotch, Jaicee Davis, Lin Xio, Marcy The Mammoth, Candi Bytch, Sugar "The Superior" Slut - Battle Royal for Women's #1 Contendership The Camargo Kid vs. Rushmore - Lumberjack Match Scotty The Hotty vs. Don "Ceasar" Capone - No DQ Falls Count Anywhere - CCW Lightweight Championship From The MGM Grand in Las Vegas, Nevada (Scene opens to President Stevens who is sitting behind a broadcast table, in front of a mini-CCWTron which displays the CCW Ground Zero logo, shown above.) Stevens: Hello again everyone and welcome to the Ground Zero post show where you will find what happened last night at the PPV just in case you missed it. This isn't your normal broadcast, but when we have a live PPV we will have a summary of the show the next day instead of your usual results. Throughout the show tonight I will have news and opinions regarding CCW Ground Zero and perhaps some special guests to discuss the events that went down last night. Now to get down to business. Last night before the PPV began to air, Savannah Hilton delivered a brutal attack on Kelly Bowe with the aide of a crowbar. This attack, combined with the entrance attack during the Women's Title match is the main contributor to the outcome of that match. These attacks on Kelly Bowe must be Savannah's way of announcing to the world that she wants another title match. How this attack was able to take place, I don't know, for sure we all would think that The Manics would have been around to keep the peace but apparently this was not story. We'll be hearing from Kelly Bowe within the next two days. Just as we began to air the PPV, CCW crews were almost finished with the wedding set up for it was then time to celebrate Johnny Lamen's love life with a wedding ceremony. That's right, Johnny Lamen was to get married to the "star of Friends" Jennifer Aniston. The wedding went off without a hitch, Johnny wearing his lime green tux and Jennifer with her hot pink leather bride's "gown". As the Rev. Marlin Hedkok delivered the vows, what could make this storybook dream turn into a nightmare but the arrival of Brad Lamen and Spit. Many of you may remember the secret diary of Johnny Lamen which was uncovered by Mr. Gobstopper during a live event a few months back. Well, Brad Lamen came out and announced to the fans and the love birds that Spit had an announcement to make. What is it she revealed? Why, Spit revealed she was pregnant with Johnny's baby as well! Johnny put a surprised and shocked look on his face only to be smacked off my Jennifer Aniston. Apparently this couple is broken up but we haven't heard from any member of the party so expect some news out of these two in the next couple of days as well. The CCW Lightweight Title was the first match on the card. Don "Ceasar" Capone made his entrance as any challenger would. The lights went off and a red laser beam was seen whizzing around the arena before finall coming to a stop on Capone. A gun shot was heard throughout the arena and as the lights came back on, The Don was discovered to have been shot by what appeared to be a tranquilizer dart. This was later found out to be a prop used to add drama to what would happen later on. The match went down but Diablo showed his face and teammed with The Don in double-teaming Scotty The Hotty before he took Scotty out with his gold cane, then The Don knocked out Diablo and pinned Scotty to win the Lightweight Championship. You can expect Scotty to demand a rematch for the title, but again we'll be hearing from both sides for sure come Lock Down. The next match on the card was a Lumberjack match between The Camargo Kid and Rushmore. Both participants were knocked out of the ring where lumberjacks such as Ghettodogg, Impact, Diablo, HCGM, Disciples, Bobby Blades and others went to work on them. There wasn't much to this match in terms of angles, but Rushmore ended up victorious after The Kid attempted to body slam the big guy but lost his grip as Rushmore came toppling down into a pinning combination. Rushmore extended his winning streak to two, his longest streak ever. So that brought us to the Women's Battle Royal where the winner would earn a Women's Title shot. Savannah Hilton had the most unusal entrance but before we get to that, let's take a look at what happened prior to this match as Stephanie Black's shower-time was interrupted by Becky Butterscotch... (CCWTron shows a quick shot of Stephanie in the shower of her dressing room. She calls for Jeff to bring her a towel as she tip-toes out of the shower area. To her surprise there is no answer, suddenly out of nowhere Becky Butterscotch jumps out with a stun gun and zaps Stephanie.) Becky: Stephanie I just don't think you're smiling enough lately girl! So I'm gonna do you a favor right now, like Mick Foley would say... let's turn that frown upside down!!! (*With that Becky duct tapes Stephanie's hands behind her back and begins to spread Peanut Butter on top of her body.) Becky: I want you to meet a friend of mine... his name's Bingo! I just know he'll cheer you up hun... (giggle giggle) (Becky leads a large German Shepard into the dressing room and he immediatly starts to lick the peanut butter off of Stephanie and she begins to giggle uncontrollably in her half conscious state. Becky steps over Jeff's unconscious body as she leaves Stephanie laughing hysterically in the dressing room....) Stevens: As you can see, Becky Butterscotch enjoys playing silly little jokes on all of her Women's Division rivals. If you think that was funny, wait until you see what Becky did to Savannah Hilton during the introduction to the Women's Battle Royal... (The CCWTron flashes to a shot of Savannah Hilton backstage just about to come out of the entrance way... suddenly out of nowhere Becky Butterscotch attacks her with that can of whipping cream she's carrying.) Announcer: And it look's like Becky isn't even gonna wait till the heartbreaker makes it to the ring! Look at her beating Savannah into submission... what's this? Becky: (giggle giggle) Well Savannah, you act like a bitch, it's time we introduced you as one! (Becky quickly reveals a studded dog collar which she proceeds to lock around a resisting Savannah's neck. She then attaches a leash and begins to drag a dazed Savannah out onto the entrance ramp on her fours....) Stevens: Once again Becky Butterscotch had pissed off yet another of her CCW counterparts. Would Becky be able to hold the fans spirits high throughout the entire evening? Well, there were no more practical jokes to play, but the previous ones were pretty damn hilarious. Onto the Battle Royal, Il Medello made a guest appearance only to smack the crap out of Marcy The Mammoth. Going through the elimination process, one by one they went. Well, two by two I should say. Lin Xio was the first to go at the hands of Candi Bytch, but then Candi and Jaicee Davis were knocked out by Stephanie who herself was then eliminated by Sugar. Becky was ready for this match as she then took Sugar out and with the help of Stephanie and Savannah, took out Marcy The Mammoth. Stephanie was the next to go followed by Savannah which meant Becky Butterscotch was crowned the current number one contender for the CCW Women's Championship. CCW will have an interview with Becky Butterscotch on Lock Down this Thursday to talk about her Battle Royal win. Following the Women's Battle Royal came the Triple Threat Tag Team Match between The Untouchables, Masked Duo, and Hard Candy. This match didn't take place and here's a clip that we have saved to show you folks at home who missed it... (Mr. Gobstopper is shown on the CCWTron as The Untouchables and the Masked Duo wait in the ring.) Gobber: (Laughs) How stupid is this? I bet you feel really ignorant now don't ya boys? Hard Candy isn't here for this match. It's too late boys, The Rancher told me yesterday that he didn't feel that this retirement match was necessary because both of you are a couple of losers. He has better places to be today and I got bigger fish to fry. So I hope you like playing with yourselves because I can see you'll be doing a lot of that tonight. Me? Oh don't worry about me, I have my sights set. See ya later fellas! (CCWTron cuts off as The Untouchables and Masked Duo stand in the ring in wonder.) Stevens: Well, that was certainly unexpected by Hard Candy. The Rancher skips town to further his education beginning Monday at Princeton University and The Gobber looking to get his hands on CCW Gold once again. After the announcement that Hard Candy would no show the match, The Masked Duo revealed themselves. Il Medello and The Don, who are now the number one contenders for the Tag Team Titles, called for their manager, Angel, to bring a I.F. jacket to the ring. They revealed one of the Masked Duo members as Il Frenesia. Big surprise. The Don then turns to the other masked guy and basically told him how great he was. He took the jacket as insurance that he is in for the ride as a member of Il Famiglia. They then had Angel remove the mask only to reveal that the newest member of I.F. is none other than former CCW Lightweight Champion, Scotty The Hotty! Why and how this young man came to join the stable of the opponent he just lost to earlier in the evening is beyond me. Hopefully IF will clear this up with all the fans sometime this week. If I.F. is looking to recruit Scotty The Hotty, other athletes can't be too far behind. The next match was for the CCW Women's Championship as The AnaManics entered the arena with Kelly Bowe. The AnaManics also were on the lookout for any interference on Delilah as well but in the end, that wasn't enough for either side. During Kelly's entrance, Savannah Hilton managed to sneak by a quick shot to the back of Kelly's head with a pair of brass knuckles. Unfortunately for Savannah, The AnaManics got in the way and forced her out of the arena before she could complete what she had come out to do. The earlier attack backstage combined with the brass knuckles to the back of the head helped Kelly Bowe in losing the Women's Title. Delilah got the pinfall and the title. Savannah was named the CCW Women's Number One Contender going into March even though Becky Butterscotch won the battle royal. So look for Delilah to face Becky Butterscotch sometime in the next week and the winner of that match to face Savannah Hilton in the week after. The Tag Team Title match took place following the Women's Title match and it pitted The Titans against The Disciples who had Bobby Blades at ringside for support and protection. In addition, there were 50 hired body guards surrounding not only the ring, but stationed throughout the arena to prevent any interference from any and all entrance ways. Also aiding the body guards were the Franchise who surrounded the ring in an effort to insure a fair match. The match went down without any outside interference but it appeared that Sid Powers really didn't want any part in the match with hesitated tags and double teams. This proved costly as The Disciples picked up the win and the Tag Team Championships. What will play out as a result of this match? I for one am not sure, but rumors circling the CCW Locker Rooms indicate that Sid Powers may be on his way out of Whoop Ass Inc. More will be revealed by Lock-Down! The TA Championship started off with a bang, let's show the video tape! ("Anthem For the Year 2000" plays as Pyro begins to shoot off everywhere as "Mr.Frosty" Bobby Blades, wearing a Las Vegas Thunder Hockey Jersey begins to make his way down to ringside with a focused stare on his face. He is followed from behind by Jaicee Davis who is carrying that bloodied Canadian flag attached to a hockey stick... He suddenly stops and speaks into a mic well removing his hockey jersey.) Bobby: (pointing at different wraps and bandages around his body) 4 cracked ribs... a separated shoulder... a recently punctured lung... and let's not forget about that herniated disc... But you know what Klown? I'm still here, standing before you... The Next Primetime Player in CCW... and now I'm gonna take what's mine! (As Bobby Blades makes his way to the ring, Jim Bowe comes out from behind and nails him across the back with the TA belt. Kelly leads Jaicee out of the way. Jim will continue to stomp on Bobby's back and yell "You want to see how much of a "Face" I'm am?" "Stick your ice cold and razor sharp up your ass Blades!". Jim then leads him to the ring and starts the match.) Stevens: As you see the TA title match did indeed start off with a bang! Jim Bowe not taking his normal "face" gimmick last night as he came in with a cheap shot from behind. Soon after Bowe got Blades in the ring, The Disciples came out to show support for Blades, and even had their KJV hardback Bibles in hand for any would-be interference. Not to mention they had their trusty wooden crosses. The AnaManics were not on hand for this match as both Pepper and Commissioner Lady Cat were in the back keeping an eye out for Kelly while she was receiving medical attention. Throughout the match Klown Bowe was having his patience tested as Bobby Blades kept escaping danger and kicking out of pin attempts. However, this proved to be mind-boggling for Bowe as he finally knocked the "Razor Sharp and Ice Cold" Blades out cold with a chair shot giving the win to Bobby Blades by DQ. Still your TA champ, Jim Klown Bowe and the number one contender for the TA title was announced as Sid Powers. So look for Jim "Klown" Bowe vs Sid Powers in the near future. That brought us to the Battle Royal for the CCW Championship. Prior to the match Blue Thunder attacked Impact as he walked down a hallway in the backstage area. He cracked his bokken across the back of Impact's head, legs, and slams it into his ribs. He then bent down and said "Nothing personal, you understand. I'm simply thinning out the competition a bit." So the Battle Royal entrances began with Phil Burner being last, as most champions come out. He came out to "No Leaf Clover" as usual with his stablemates Samson and Delilah. Il Frenesia, of all people, jumped the Fat Ass with a crowbar but it did no good as WAI member Samson came to the aide of Phil Burner, dismantling Il Frenesia and leading him away backstage. Delilah gave Phil some inspirational words as the odds stacked against him gave the fans in the arena the feel that tonight the long title reign of Fat Ass Phil Burner would come to an end. The Battle Royal began as all 15 men went to work in an attempt to become the new Heavyweight Champ, and a new champ we should have. The rules to this battle royal was not only eliminate your opponents by tossing them over the top rope, but also by either pinfall or submission. The first of the men to be eliminated, and at the hands of Mash, was Impact who went sailing over the top rope and hitting the floor hard. The new Independent champ eliminated by former Independent champ. Interesting, wouldn't you say? Ghettodogg was lifted over the top rope by Carnage and with the aide of Sylkk's bounce of the ropes, Carnage eliminated former Hardcore Champ, Ghettodogg. Next to go was the CCW Hardcore Champion, Carnage, who had been victim of a Fat Ass Sidewalk Slam by the true Fat Ass and then dumped out of the ring. The current Hardcore Champ eliminated by a former Hardcore champ. Interesting, wouldn't you say? Hehe... That brought up Mash who was sent sailing through the air by former CCW Heavyweight Champion, The Avenger Tim Inman. Mash showed a brilliant display of comebackness during his stay in this match, but sometimes the old does away with the young. Blue Thunder, who wasn't thrilled about the way the Championship was being defended, had his share of elimination when he ridded the ring of HCGM only to be eliminated himself from behind by Phil Burner. Diablo and Mr. Gobstopper battled it out against the ropes, but it looked like The Gobber would be heading back to the locker room after a few more seconds of Diablo's attempt at eliminating him. But that wasn't what happened, no, Phil Burner, after eliminating Blue Thunder, rushed over with his big self and took BOTH Diablo and Mr. Gobstopper over the top rope to the outside. Pepper The Cat and Sylkk with the occassional help of GOD, Inman, and Corey James, began wearing down the CCW Champion, Phil Burner. Their joint efforts looked to pay off as Phil was mere seconds away from being eliminated when the un-thinkable happened... "Crazy Train" by Ozzy Osbourne came across the arena's PA system as myself and the fans went completely nuts when former CCW Heavyweight Champion, Apocalypse, made his return to CCW after an extensive absence fighting boxers in the country of Guam. Apoc rushed into the ring with a railroad tie in one hand and a shovel in the other. He tossed the shovel to the Fat Ass and together they took out a couple more opponents such as Corey James, Pepper The Cat, and Ice Chamber. Apoc soon left the ring as Phil was left with Tim Inman, Sylkk, and GOD. As Apoc (who had gained at least 50 pounds while in Guam) watched from the outside, Inman tossed Sylkk over the top, but the man slid back in the ring then rolled out and hid under the ring. Everyone thought he was eliminated so the match continued as GOD was backed into the corner by Inman and Phil, but something clicked in his mind that sent him on a "roid" rage, taking Inman and Burner down with very strong power moves. Soon Apoc slid into the ring and caught the raging GOD in the Armageddon which put him out and allowed Phil Burner to get the pinfall elimination on him. With GOD out of the match it was Inman and Phil Burner toe to toe when Sylkk climbed out from under the ring and back into the ring. Sylkk, with a big grin on his face, was knocked over the top rope when Phil sent Inman to the ropes and bashing into Sylkk on the way. Inman, looking down at Sylkk from inside the ring, was caught from behind by Fat Ass and given the Bearhug Slam. Burner, Inman, the only two left and Inman lay motionless in the center of the ring. We all thought that the gimmick days of the old Phil Burner had gone but he stood over the downed Inman, cocked his nose into the air as if he were smelling bacon, and removed his elbow pad and threw it to the crowd. The Porker's Champ was back! He threw his arms side to side, bounced off the ropes, hopped over Inman, bounced off the other ropes, and hit Inman with a massive Porker's Elbow. Burner went down for the pin as the ref slid into the ring to make the count. The fans rocking the arena as the ref counted to three. The arena was roaring as the announcement was made... RA: Here is your winner... and STILL CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! The "FAT ASS" PHIL BURNER!!!!!! With that said, the fans rushed the ring and thousands of people wanted to get their hands on the sweaty, lard ridden ass of the Champion. Tens and tens of fans filled the ring and hoisted the Porker's Champ into the air as all of WAI came out to celebrate with the champ and the fans. It truly was unprecedented that Phil Burner survived a 15-Man Battle Royal to retain his Heavyweight Title and to keep the streak going. But his days as champ may be numbered because the number one contender was named and that man is Sylkk. So, until Lock-Down, this is Jonathan Stevens saying congratulations to Phil Burner and goodnight from Las Vegas and this wild and whacky "Ground Zero" in review! CCW Home
