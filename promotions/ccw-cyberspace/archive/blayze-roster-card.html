What this is CCW roster card for Blayze, with hometown, status, moves, title history and roleplay list: a heel extreme technical wrestler and former World Heavyweight champion with a long career narrative.
Blayze Real Name: Jerome Terrance Jordan Birthdate: November 12th, 1978 Hometown: Compton, South Central L.A Height: 6'0' Weight: 225 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Heel Manager(s): None Current Teams: Menace II Society Entrance Music: Until The End of Time - 2pac Wrestling Style: Extreme, Technical, Various Others Signature Moves: Drive-By(5 Star Frog Splash), Cradle Suplex, Tazzmission, T-Bone Suplex, Cradle DDT, Diamond Dust, Elevated Crab, Inverted Texas Cloverleaf, Three Handle Moss Family Credenza, Swinging Neckbreaker, Implant DDT, Springboard DDT, Top Rope Press To Outside, Asai Moonsault, Snap Brainbuster, Various Martial Art Kicks, Springboard Bulldog Set Up Move: Severe Testimony (Powerbomb Into Diamond Cutter ) Finisher: The Critical Beatdown (Reverse Death Valley Driver ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Heavyweight Champion(1x), Independent Champion(2x), Hardcore Champion(1x), Tag Team Champion(4x) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: 2001 Upset of The Year, Other Federations: WWAWF Other Federation Awards: WWAWF Tag Team Champio(1x) - Undefeated Handler Information Handler Name: Rob AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] CCW Record: 44-38-5 All Time Wrestling Record: 53-38-6 Blayze grew up on the concrete streets of Compton South Central L.A. After the untimely death of his parents, Blayze had to be raised by his step-brother Daren who taught him the ways of the streets. Blayze had a harsh life, ending up in a gang, took up weed and was even the hood's meanest Shorty. He quit the thuggish lifestyle when his love of wrestling kicked in. After brawling in numerous fight clubs across L.A., Blayze was scouted and given a developemtn deal with the WWFAW. That's when his success kicked in, he quickly became a tag team champ and Intercontinental Champ at the same time, he was the only undefeated wrestler in WWFAW, he stayed like that when the fed shut down on November 12th. Blayze had no home after the closing, he went to a bunch of indie-feds but never rose to the top, they found him to be 'too hardcore' but the fans saw him as an Hardcore Icon. That's when, after the indie fed failures, he saw the application for CCW. He trained and rained hard for his a chance to get in and alll that hard work payed off. Blayze entered CCW in early October and had a slow start, his biggest upset was against Sylkk who he beat in a closed cage match with the help of Chad Wild. He even showed his true extreme side by challenging World Champion Ghettodogg to a non-title street fight, even thought he lost he showed the world that he can take it to the limit. Thats when a friendship grew between him and Chad and then 'The Syndicate' was born. The Syndicate cosisted of Blayze, Chad Wild, Seth Rhinestone and Chad's Love Interest Kitty Lynn. They had a short lived feud with Urban thus putting him in the hospital but the elite group last all but a month. On November 30th 2000, a huge scandal rocked CCW which ended up leaving Chad Wild, Seth Rhinestone and others fired from CCW. This left Blayze with no one to follow and know had to go solo once again. He said he growed a friendship with the Syndicate members but now they were all gone but he didnt know was that Kitty Lynn was the other soul surviving member of the Syndicate. Kitty never got that close to Blayze but found him to be sweet, caring individual even if he was a street thug. Blayze and Kitty soon got together and was always on his side during his matches. A few weeks later in December, Blayze was left to be solo once again as Kitty took a leave of absence from CCW for a good amount of time to clear her mind of stuff. Blayze slump then grew losing matches to Alex Hawk, Bobby Blades and Neurosis. He said to everyone that he had to drop the ghetto slang and hardcore ways because it seems no one is paying that much attention to him and that he really does possess great skills. People laughed but he made them believers, he went out and seeked the services of his long time mentor and the self proclaimed 'Pastor of Wrestling' Allah Shaheem and dominated the last 2 weeks of December with impressive wins over Jack Williams, who he had a rugged feud with, and had an incredible Strap Match versus Jack at C2:D PPV and winning. The new year came and thats when the awards where upon CCW, Blayze was nomited for Most Shocking Victory and Rookie of The Year but left the award show empty handed ranking up not one single award. After the good long holidays, Blayze came back in top form and was ready for his first ever Title Shot against Indepedent Champion Mr. Gobstopper. After a hard fought battle, Blayze somehow shocked the viewers by reversing the Gob Drop into a Brainbuster and walking away victorious. He was very quick to set out a challenge and managed to beat Read Deal and Benny Blades in a triple threat match. A week later his biggest challenge came towards him, he had to face a former World Champion and now retired Andrew 'Ace' Hawk. his match had many interferences and the attack on Blayze by Tim Inman costed Blayze his Indy Title. Ace was unhappy with the outcome and gave Blayze a rematch in a steel cage match. Up to this point, that cage match could have been the highlight of Blayze's career as even though he lost, he took Ace Hawk to the limit. Puttin him through Tables, burning him, grading his face with steel and putting him through the cage and through the mat. From Then Blayze, titleless, moved up the ranks in CCW. He had entered the 2001 COTR but was outsted in the 2nd Round by CCW Hall of Famer, Paul Arovnik. Blayze was still labeled at times the old fashioned 'Ghetto Thug' but he proved had more then street smarts when he earned a birth in the Gold and Glory 2001 4 Way Main Event in which he uncovered the mystery Bag and was crowned not only Hardcore Champ but CCW World Champion. The reign only lasted 30 days as he lost the World Title to Spider only a month later. Since that day, Blayze has yet to regain the World title, but he has done something which had shocked CCW to its core. In early 2002, Blayze, tangled up(and still is) in a feud with CCW President Spider, pulled a huge swerve right under his nose and bought CCW's other 50% share which was once held by Sylkk. The battle raged on and at Irish Springs PPV, they battled for Full Ownership of CCW. In a turn of events, Spider pulled out the much-needed victory and saved CCW for entering a state of hell and suffering. Right Now, Blayze is still striving for that World Title and that ownership. He has brought in his brother into the fedeation and created a stable known as Menace II Society which had former World Champ Enigma apart of.
