Roster All new recruits to the CCW War Machine are placed in 1st division there score warrants relegation to the CCW Premiership, in which they will then become eligible for Titles. However, if anyone in the CCW Premiership fails to Roleplay for 3 matches consecutively, they will be relegated to CCW 1st to prove to the brass that they can change their ways. Usual turnaround time for CCW 1st Division is approximately three weeks.
