CCW match: Scorpion & Michael Stevens vs. Sheena & Minx (1st Round Tag Tourney)

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scorpion & Michael Stevens vs. Sheena & Minx (1st Round Tag Tourney) Date: 3-6-03 Show: Lockdown 'Cringe' by Alkaline Trio hits and Michael Stevens walks out of the back and down to the ring. The fans boo and give him a hard time as he goes. 'Enter Sandman' by Metallica hits shortly after, and Scorpion walks out to a good pop. Nayt Willems: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. First, weighing in at a combined weight of 477 pounds... SCORPION and MICHAEL 'SPITFIRE' STEVENS! The fans continue making noise, giving a mixed reaction for this pairing. BJS: Heh... Sounds like these stupid fans can't make up their minds. That's pretty damn funny if you ask me. JD: Which nobody did. 'Sheena Is A Punk Rocker' by The Ramones hits and Sheena walks out onto the stage to a chorus of boos. Minx appears on the stage shortly afterwards, and the two women walk down the ramp to the ring. Nayt Willems: And their opponents... Making their way to the ring at a combined weight of 276 pounds... MINX and SHEENA RAMONE! The women enter the ring and parade themselves around. The wolf whistles and cat calls immediately start. JD: Well...? BJS: Well what? JD: Aren't you going to say some stupid comment about how much you want to have sex with one of them? BJS: No. JD: No? Why not? BJS: Why bother? It's not like I'm ever going to see either of them naked. Sure, they both look hot and all, but just like every other woman out there, they'll take a shot at my balls if I say anything. And I, for one, am tired of having my balls attacked. JD: Well, it looks like it's gonna be Sheena starting out for the girls... And Scorpion for the guys. BJS: Makes sense, he has the most experience with fighting girls in the ring. Scorpion and Sheena circle each other, looking for a good opening. Scorpion lunges in and Sheena kicks out, but Scorpion grabs her leg and Dragon Screws her to the canvas. He quickly gets back on his feet, grabs her legs, and then Slingshots her across the ring. Sheena's face collides with the top turnbuckle, bouncing her back out to mid-ring. Scorpion grabs her as she comes back and lifts her into the air before bringing her back down with a Jackknife Powerbomb. JD: Ouch! Sheena's not faring very well here. BJS: Looks like Scorpion's been practicing his 'moves to use against women'. JD: Well, Scorpion's going for a pin here... Vinny with the count... 1... 2... JD: And Sheena kicks out! Looks like she's not to call it a day yet. BJS: God, I hope not. It's not like she's taken much punishment yet. Scorpion picks her back up and whips Sheena at the ropes. Sheena rebounds, come back, and ducks under Scorpion as he swings at her head. She rebounds and comes back again, only to have Scorpion take her down with a Back Body Drop. Sheena convulses a bit, holding her back, and quickly lunges to make the tag with Minx... JD: And Minx is entering the ring now... Scorpion is making a tag to his partner as well. BJS: Looks like Stevens is ready to fight now that Sheena ain't around. JD: I doubt they planned it that way. Just coincidence, that's all. Minx immediately runs at Stevens with a Dropkick aimed to take his head off. Stevens lets her come and then bats her feet aside as she leaps at him. Minx hits the ground, rolls to her feet, and then takes a huge blast to the face as Stevens pops her a good one. BJS: Wow... I normally don't see guys punch chicks in the face like that... I think that would be called abuse if it happened out of the ring... Stevens continues the abuse as he drops Minx with a Dragon Suplex. And then, as she tries getting up, he wraps his fingers around her stomach and pulls her over with a German Suplex. And another... And another... And another... And another. JD: Dammit! Five German's to that poor girl! I don't think Minx knows what happened to her! BJS: Forget that. Minx probably doesn't even know where she is! JD: Stevens is now locking in the Darwinian Dreams!!! He has Minx right in the middle of the ring and it looks like she's fading away! BJS: And here comes Sheena to break it up! Sheena bolts from her corner, but Scorpion is there to make the intercept. He Clotheslines her to the canvas, then drops his knee onto her neck to hold her while Vinny checks on Minx. He raises her hand once... twice... Three times, and then signals for the bell. And once again boos fill the arena. Nayt Willems: Your winners... SCORPION and MICHAEL 'SPITFIRE' STEVENS!!! View Scorpion 's Biography Back
