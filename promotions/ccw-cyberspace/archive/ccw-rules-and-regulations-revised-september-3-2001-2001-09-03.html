CCW Rules and Regulations (Revised September 3, 2001)

3 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The fed’s full rulebook: terms of agreement, the application process, officials’ positions, roster and title rules, television and angle rules, message-board rules, out-of-action rules and special recognitions.
CCW Rules and Regulations Revised September 3, 2001 I. Terms of Agreement Anyone who participates in Cyberspace Championship Wrestling in any type of form shall abide by all rules and regulations as set forth by CCW Officials. All users agree to hold CCW and CCW Officials in high regards and treat them with respect. CCW shall not be held responsible for any offensive or profane content supplied by a user for the purposes of character representation. CCW shall not be held accountable for any harm or damage caused by visiting the CCW site or using CCW Services. All users agree to relinquish their membership at any time at the request of authorized CCW Officials, with or without reason. All images, names, logos, designs, layout, content, etc, are properties that are owned by CCW Enterprises and/or affiliated web sites. Any images, names, logos, designs, etc, which are submitted to CCW becomes property of CCW and CCW reserves the right to continue use of such content should the supplying user cease affiliation or membership with CCW. Any known content submitted to CCW, which is owned by an entity not affiliated with CCW, will be used in good taste and only used as a means of representing our fictional characters. Should the original owners of submitted content wish CCW to cease use of their content, CCW shall cease use of such content if the original owner can bear proof of ownership of said content. CCW reserves the right to seek legal action against any user who may cause a lawsuit against CCW from another user, affiliate, or outside source. CCW is not in violation of any U.S. laws and will strive to remain as such. If CCW is found in violation of any U.S. laws, CCW will agree to cease operations until the site is no longer in violation of said laws. CCW agrees to report any and all criminal activity discovered being promoted through CCW content, email, or message boards. All users agree to be held legally and financially responsible for any actions they perform which may call for legal issues to be taken up in a court of law. II. Applying to CCW A. Disclaimer - CCW reserves the right to accept or decline any applicants for any reason. B. Application process – All users who wish to join CCW must fill out an application in full. After submitting an application, there will be a period no greater than ten days in which you will receive an acceptance email. If said email is not received, then the user has not been accepted into CCW. All acceptance emails must be replied to as confirmation of this acceptance before the wrestler is added to the roster. A wrestler may not participate with CCW until they have been accepted and added to the roster. For inquiries about your application, please contact the CCW Talent Coordinator. C. Limits – Each traceable IP number is allowed a maximum of two wrestlers, male or female. However, CCW reserves the right to decline the second wrestler on grounds that operating one wrestler is in itself time consuming. Doing this will also leave room for newer applicants. D. Wrestler limitations – All male wrestlers must be between 5’0” to 7’11” and 150 to 600 pounds. All female wrestlers must be between 4’10” to 6’6” and 90 to 400 pounds. All wrestler names, gimmicks, and moves must be original, although they may spoof a real life wrestler. E. Tag Team applications – Any persons wishing to have an official tag team must fill out a tag team application. Applicants must be wrestlers who are already members of CCW. If there are two handlers involved in controlling the wrestlers, only one member should fill out the application. III. CCW Officials A. Positions - CCW offers the users a chance to become a CCW Official by assigning them one of many jobs available. The positions are as follows: 1. The CCW President is in charge of CCW operations and Internet Relations. 2. The CCW Vice President takes the place of the President in the event he/she is absent. 3. The CCW Talent Coordinator is in charge of hiring new applicants. He may also fire wrestlers on the basis of non-participation. 4. The CCW Commissioner is in charge of CCW TV and PPV Bookings. 5. The CCW Inter-Fed Representative is in charge of promoting CCW through any inter-fed partner sites. 6. The CCW Inter-Fed Reporters are all members of CCW who provide CCW news to any inter-Fed partners. 7. The CCW Art Director provides CCW with new site designs, images, logos, and various other special content. 8. The CCW Columnists write columns pertaining to CCW on a weekly or bi-weekly basis. 9. The CCW Road Agent provides news reports from every CCW event to our inter-fed partner sites. 10. The CCW Writing Team is broken up into two teams which work together to bring CCW's weekly TV shows. B. Applying for a Position – CCW is always looking for members who want to contribute to our operations. If you wish to apply for a position not already filled, please email the President with your request. CCW on occasion may approach its members and offer a position, but this does not obligate the member to accept it. C. Executive Matters – All users are urged to report any problems to the President by email as opposed to promoting the issue to the public by way of the OOC board. The President will use good judgment in bringing all problems to a resolution. IV. Rosters A. Updates – Rosters are updated on the schedule of the CCW Talent Coordinator. If there is some information on the roster that needs to be changed that he may have overlooked, please contact him with said changes. Rosters are updated continuously, but not all at the same time. For that reason, some wrestler bios may not be as current as others. B. Division of Competition – While there are no divisions in CCW, there are a few limitations that are required for certain titles. 1. The CCW Heavyweight Championship is open to all male wrestlers. 2. The CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship is open to all male and female wrestlers. 3. The CCW Independent Championship is open to all male and female wrestlers. 4. The CCW Lightweight Championship is open to all male and female wrestlers weighing less than 225 pounds. 5. The CCW Hardcore Championship is open to all male and female wrestlers. 6. The CCW Women’s Championship is open to all female wrestlers. 7. The CCW Tag Team Championship is open to all male, female, and mixed tag teams. The tag team does not need to be officially on the roster in order to gain the title. C. Stables – There are no limitations on how many members a stable can have. There is no application process for starting a stable other than unveiling it to the public. The leading member of the stable should email the CCW Talent Coordinator with the names of all its members and all changes in its membership. V. Television and Competition A. Getting Booked - Making challenges on the Challenge Board is the easiest way to be booked for a match. However, should no one reply to your challenge, it is the CCW Commissioner’s discretion to book the match. The only other way to be booked in a match is to have a great participation level that will benefit the wrestlers who possess that level of participation when the Commissioner needs to book matches out of the blue. On occasion, a lack of participation in CCW causes for matches to be made up. B. Determining Winners – The CCW President decides all winners and losers of a match. He does so by judging the roleplays that are submitted by the competitors of said match. Roleplays are judged according to style and quality, not by length. Roleplays must be submitted by the set deadline in order to be accepted unless otherwise cleared by the President. Angles also play a large factor in determining winners. In the event of a “tie” between two roleplays, the President will make a fair decision based on the angles submitted for the match and by recent participation of the participants. All bookings made by the President are final and will not be reversed for any reason. C. Television Shows – CCW airs two weekly television shows as well as a monthly Pay Per View show: 1. Shockin’ Sunday Night – Airs Sunday nights. 2. Lockdown – Airs Thursday nights. 3. Pay Per Views – Airs in place of Shockin’ Sunday Night once a month. a. CCW 2001 - PPV airs in January. (Debut 2000) b. Ground Zero - PPV airs in February. (Debut 2000) c. Irish Spring - PPV airs in March. (Debut 1999) d. April Showers - PPV airs in April. (Debut 1999) e. Memorial Day Showdown - PPV airs in May. (Debut 1999) f. Shriekfest - PPV airs in June. Includes the finals of the COTR Tournament. (Debut 1999) g. Heatstroke - PPV airs in July. (Debut 1999) h. Gold & Glory - PPV airs in August. Includes a Four-Way Title match. (Debut 2000) i. Cyberslam - PPV airs in September. (Debut in 2001) j. Freakshow - PPV airs in October. (Debut 1999) k. Group Therapy - PPV airs in November. Includes a series of multiple-participant tournament matches. (Debut 1999) l. Countdown 2: Destruction - PPV airs in December. (Debut 1999) D. Angles 1. Requirements – A user must either be a wrestler or a staff member in CCW in order to submit an angle. Each wrestler is allowed two angles per show. Angles will not be used if they are against CCW rules. Angles may be edited to suit the booking and writing process. 2. Rules – Angles should be short and concise, with an accurate depiction of what you’d like to happen in the match. Angles written in TV format may not be used unless the angle is an interview or it has been cleared by the President. Angle that uses a manager, valet, or other outside source will count as one of the wrestler’s angles. If a user has more than one wrestler, he may use only two angles per wrestler, but may not angle one wrestler to help the other unless it is appropriate and follows storyline purposes. Angles may not involve kidnapping or cause injury to another wrestler unless it has been cleared by the CCW President or the other party involved. Angles must be submitted by the set deadline in order to be accepted unless otherwise cleared by the President. 3. Priorities – Situations arise often in which angles may clash. CCW has devised a system whose angles are to receive top priority. a. Booked wrestlers b. Unbooked Main Eventers c. Unbooked Midcarders d. High profile managers e. Unbooked Lowcarders f. Low profile managers g. Rookies (less than 5 matches wrestled) h. Security guards, dogs, and other no-name characters E. Rankings – CCW currently posts official rankings once a month. Rankings are determined by the President and reflect his views on who he thinks is doing a good job. Rankings do not entitle a wrestler to any special privileges or title matches unless a wrestler’s rank is noted as being a number one contender. VI. Message Board System A. Rules 1. Profanity is allowed on all of CCW’s boards, so long as it is in good taste or in character. 2. Situations involving gangs, murder, or blatant drug abuse are not allowed. 3. Users may not use the boards to run an angle. For instance, you may not attack another wrestler backstage. Using other characters not controlled by the user is not allowed unless otherwise cleared by the other party involved. 4. All posts on the Roleplay, Challenge, or Interview boards should be written in a roleplay format, with setting and description included. Replies to challenges or interviews do not need be in roleplay format. 5. HTML is allowed on all of CCW’s boards. However, do not use any music or a large number of pictures, as this will slow down loading time and may deter your fellow users from reading your posts. B. The Boards 1. Challenge Board – Use this to challenge other wrestlers to a match. Challenges may be declined. If the wrestler a user challenges does not reply or declines the challenge, the CCW Commissioner reserves the right to book the match anyway for storyline purposes. 2. Interview Board – Use this to post your wrestler’s thoughts on recent happenings or to further your wrestler’s character. CCW strongly urges its members to post frequently on this board if they do not have any upcoming matches, so that they can keep their characters fresh. 3. Roleplay Board – Roleplays are required if you have a match in an upcoming show. Roleplays must be submitted by the set deadline in order to be accepted. Do not post on this board if you do not have an upcoming match. Replies to roleplays are not allowed unless it is meant for encouragement by the handlers. Each wrestler is allowed only one roleplay per match. Both members of a tag team must roleplay for a match unless the same person handles both members. If a wrestler has more than one match in any given show, only one total roleplay is required. 4. Out Of Character Board – This board is used for handlers to ask questions, make comments, or raise discussions. However, any posts that may cause unrest among the members of CCW are not allowed unless the issue has been previously addressed to the President. Handlers are expected to respect each other. 5. Results Boards – CCW uses these boards for the Senior writers to post the written results for each show. 6. News Board – This is a rarely used board that is used only when a member wishes to post a news article of interest that is otherwise not appropriate for the OOC Board. VII. Out of Action A. Suspension - Suspensions can and will occur for various reasons. The President holds all suspension powers. Breaking the rules is the top reason suspensions occur, but many other reasons come about which may cause for a suspension. A user who finds himself on suspension cannot participate until the suspension is over. B. Injury - Injuries occur at the request of the user. Very rarely will a wrestler be injured when not requested. Injury time-out varies. C. Termination - The President can release a wrestler or user at any time for any reason. If a user or wrestler wishes to return, The President must be contacted. A contract would have to be negotiated. D. Quitting - If a user decides to quit they'll be required to wait 30 days before returning to think about it thoroughly. If a user quits, they must contact The President with a reason why. E. Retirement - A user can officially retire after being with CCW for at least one year. The user must contact The President to make plans for retirement. Retirees must wait 60 days before eligible for a return. F. Banishment - If a user is banned from CCW, they cannot return to CCW without a majority vote of CCW users. G. Vacation – CCW understands that sometimes a break is needed, for whatever reason. If you are going out of town and will not be able to participate, or you simply feel that you need a break, please contact the President. Not doing so may result in the assumption that you have quit, and your wrestler will be terminated. VIII. Special Recognition A. Honors - CCW has many honorary titles that come with an extraordinary career. 1. Grand Slam Champion - For having gained the Heavyweight, Trans-Atlantic, Independent, and Tag Team Championship throughout the course of a career. 2. CCW Universal Champion - For having won every single title over the course of a career. B. Champion of the Ring - CCW holds its annual Champion of the Ring tournament beginning in May. In COTR matches, interference is not allowed and roleplays the dominating factor in deciding the winner. The winner is named appropriately named "Champion of the Ring". Special awards or privileges may be awarded to the winner if the President sees fit. C. End of the Year Awards - The Year-end awards show is a way for the handlers to vote their peers the best of a category. The show takes place in January, and nominations for each category are open to all who qualify. D. Hall of Fame - The greatest wrestlers who have graced CCW are voted in each year at the Year End Awards show. IX. Miscellaneous A. The Information link on the main page displays updated touring schedules, staff and user deadlines, special events listing, and other information. B. The Archives are updated sparingly as CCW continues to ask any members who are willing to update them. C. All users are invited to submit URLs to columns that they generate to be posted on the main page. D. The Writer's area displays information and tips on writing shows, doing roleplays, HTML help, links to e-wrestling terms, wrestling moves, etc. E. CCW does not currently have an affiliation with any other federations or interfeds. In the event CCW joins an interfed, CCW will revise its rules to allow for interfed play. X. In Closing A special thanks goes to the handlers of CCW who make this a unique and close e-wrestling experience. Thanks you also the writers, without which CCW would not be able to function. A giant thanks goes to Jonathan Stevens, founder and former President of CCW. CCW will continue to strive to be one of the most dominant forces in e-wrestling. As long as we keep a good fan base, a wonderful and willing staff, and updates, CCW will be around in the years to come. [name withheld] Founding member
