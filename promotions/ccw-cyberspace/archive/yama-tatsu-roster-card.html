What this is CCW roster card for Yama Tatsu, with hometown, status, moves, title history and roleplay list: a heel technical/lucha libre martial artist with a monastery back-story.
Yama Tatsu Real Name: - Birthdate: 12/31/1981 Hometown: Osaka, Japan Height: 5'11' Weight: 235 lbs. Nicknames: The Mountian Dragon, Beast from the East Status: Heel Manager(s): - Current Teams: - Entrance Music: Wait and Bleed- Slipknot Wrestling Style: He is a great mix of styles. You would call him a Technical/Lucha Libre/Martial Artist. He has quite a few good Technical moves, but mainly sticks to his “Vampiro” Martial Art/Lucha Libre style, as this is the area-style in that he was first taught. Signature Moves: Plague of the Dragon (X Factor) Set Up Move: The Dragons Rampage (Running Canadian Back Breaker where Yama Tatsu dives into the corner as well to add power to the move ) Finisher: “Mystic Katana' (Flying Leg Drop to the back of opponent’s head. This would absolutely destroy the strongest of opponents in a heartbeat. He usually performs his trademark move, “The Plague of the Dragon”, before this. (Pin) ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: Independant (1), Hardcore (Current) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: - Other Federations: - Other Federation Awards: - Handler Information Handler Name: Josh AIM Name: - MSN Name: [detail withheld] He has no knowledge of his birth name as he was born in a quiet, little fishing village, to a Rich man’s, 16 year old daughter and a 72 year old WW2 Soldier. It was rape, and the man was killed for it. The baby was then given to the village Monastery, which was situated on the Mt. Dragon. The baby was then raised in the dark, sinister combat and magical practices which were secretly performed in the Holy Monastery. When his 16 birthday arrived he was set free into the wild. He moved to Singapore where he part took in Underground Shoot-fighting competitions. In all 3 years he fought in these tournaments he was never beat. Not once. At the age of 19 he returned to the Monastery, armed with hundreds of thousands of dollars, only to find it in flames. He rushed down to the village below to find the village people in the town square celebrating their purification of the area. The next day no one could be found in the village, it was like they all vanished. Only Yama knows what happened. He moved to Tokyo to join Ultimo Dragon’s Training School. This is where he took up the wrestling name Yama Tatsu. A year and a half later he was kicked out of the Training school and told never to return. In search of a wrestling career he has turned to the United States of America and CCW looking for opportunities.
