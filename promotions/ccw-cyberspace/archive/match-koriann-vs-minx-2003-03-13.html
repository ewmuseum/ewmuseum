CCW match: Koriann vs. Minx

13 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Koriann vs. Minx Date: 03/13/03 Show: Lockdown Nayt Willems: The following contest is scheduled for one Fall! Making her way to the ring, hailing from Kyoto, Japan and weighing in at 139 pounds... MINX! JD: Thank God! One more second of hearing Koriann's voice, and I would've shot myself! BJS: Minx is quite the hot one as well! Too bad she's actually wearing wrestling gear... Maybe she should have borrowed something from... JD: That's it! Enough about naked women! Christ! I'm trying to be professional out here! BJS: If you're trying to be professional, then why aren't you saying anything about Minx? JD: Well, we really don't know a lot about her, do we? She just started here in CCW last week, where she and Sheena Ramone lost in the first round of the Tag Team Tournament. And now tonight, much like Koriann, she has a chance to fight for the #1 contendership to the Women's title! Minx reaches the ring and rolls inside from underneath the bottom rope. She pops up on her feet rather quickly and circles around Koriann, who glares at her because she didn't get to fully finish her little tirade. JD: Terry Dwight is our referee for this match-up, and he just gave the signal to the bellkeeper! And we're on our way here... Koriann and Minx approach each other in the middle of the ring, and immediately lock hands together in a test of strength, trying to bend each other's fingers, shoving to gain position. Suddenly, Koriann kicks out with her booted foot to Minx's solar plexus. Minx doubles over and gasps out loud. Koriann takes advantage and Minx finds herself in a side headlock. Koriann twists and turns her own body, taking Minx with her and applying pressure from various angles. Koriann throws Minx over her hip and tumbles to the ground with her, the headlock still applied. JD: Both women on the floor, now. Minx is really struggling and kicking to get out of Koriann's grip... BJS: Yup, and Koriann just applies more pressure! Look at that... Minx's face is turning red! To add more insult to injury, Koriann rubs her fist right into Minx's little nose. Terry Dwight finally steps in and forces koriann to break the hold. Minx heaves in great lungfulls of air as Koriann walks to the middle of the ring. Koriann: I RULE!!! How does it feel to be beaten by someone who's definitely your superior, bitch? Koriann reaches out her hand to help Minx get up, then she puts her other hand between Minx's legs and executes a powerful Bodyslam. Minx bounces hard on the canvas. Koriann pulls her up and executes another bodyslam. Minx arches her back in pain and lets escape a small groan. Koriann: That's right! I guess we both know who's going on to the #1 contender's match, don't we? JD: Koriann with some trash talking, and now she leaps into the air and drops a leg right across Minx's throat! BJS: Minx is definitely have trouble breathing this match. Look at her squirm on the canvas. Koriann definitely can be brutal, can't she? Koriann stands up and brings her boot down hard on Minx's stomach. Minx gasps out loud, seriously trying to get her breath back. JD: Koriann stomps down again with the other boot, doing more damage to the midsection! Unless Minx can do something to get back into this match, Koriann is definitely going to the #1 contender's match... Koriann jumps into the air and lands with both of her knees onto Minx's already ravaged stomach. Minx groans again, her legs flopping up into the air with the impact. BJS: Minx is definitely on the losing edge, here. But what did you expect? Koriann is one of the greatest wrestlers here of all time. Koriann remains with her knees on Minx's belly smiling with arrogance down at the Japanese woman... And then goes back to work. Koriann puts one arm under Minx's shoulders and the other arm under her legs. Lifting Minx up in the air and cradling her like a baby, she then drops to one knee and brings Minx down on top of it. And taking anumber out of Melody's playbook earlier in the evening, Koriann clasps her hands together, raises them in the air, and brings them down hard onto Minx's body. JD: Well, this one is technically over, folks. Koriann pushes Minx off her knee and sends her belly-first onto the floor. BJS: Minx looks really hurt and breathless. Looks like she's gonna need a little Slater-pick-me-up after the match. JD: **mutters** I hope she castrates you. BJS: What was that? JD: What? Nothing! I didn't say anything. Koriann sits down on Minx's back facing her legs. She hooks each one of Minx's legs under each one of her arms and pulls back in a perfectly executed Boston Crab. Bracing herself, Koriann then shifts her behind back so that she is sitting on Minx's shoulders. Laughing sadistically, Koriann pulls all the way back on Minx's legs. The crowd boos the hell out of Koriann. Koriann: **laughing** Give in now, little Japanese-bitch? Minx: NO! JD: Koriann is really putting on the pressure here! Only Minx's pride is keeping her from tapping out, but I don't think she can last much longer... Terry Dwight is in position, waiting for Minx's submission. BJS: And hopefully she does soon. Koriann is only pulling harder. And check this out! Only Minx's breasts and her face are on the floor! Her entire body is bending back! Her spine must be killing her! Minx, on the verge of passing out from the pain, weakly taps the canvas, indicating her submission. Dwight immediately calls for the bell, and then hops up to force Koriann to break the hold. Koriann drops Minx's legs to the canvas, and raises her arms, above her head again. 'I RULE!!!' The crowd boos back at her. Nayt Willems: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner by submission... KORIANN!!! JD: Well, looks like Koriann will be going on to the #1 contender's match. And that's too bad. I was hoping to see Minx triumph here BJS: Hey, Doyle! Check this out! Looks like Koriann ain't done yet! JD: What? What the hell is she doing? Up in the ring, Koriann stands over her fallen opponent. Koriann: You need to be welcomed to CCW properly! And although this is your second match, better late than never! And Koriann yanks down Minx's tanktop, leaving her in nothing but her black bra. Koriann then ties Minx's hands behind her back with the tanktop, and removes the Japanese girl's jean shorts to reveal her underwear. The crowd pops and boos all at the same time. JD: What the hell is this!? BJS: **shrugs** I don't know, but I'm sure as hell not complaining. In fact, I need me some pictures! Slater takes out a camera and begins shooting away. JD: What!? Where did you get that? Slater: Well, after Melody's outfit earlier this evening, I swore I'd never let another 'masturbation moment' pass me by. So, I got me this camera so I can capture everything on film for my private collection. JD: **shudders** Koriann stands up in a victory pose with one arm raised in the air, and with her boot resting on Minx's back. The crowd continues to jeer and boo the hell out of her. JD: Well, I must say, Koriann's become more vicious since her return to CCW. I guess we'll have to see how this all plays out. BJS: I'll be right back! I need to get some close-up pictures! JD: **sighs and rolls eyes** View Koriann 's Biography Back
