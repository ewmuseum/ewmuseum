Name: Al Coholic Hometown: Windsor, Ontario, Canada Email: [detail withheld] Weight: 220 lbs. Height: 6' 0" Age: 20 Status: Neutral Manager/Valet: The Beer Man Wrestling Style: Technical; Aerial; Powerhouse (Post-Drink) Theme Music: 'To The Victor Go The Spoils' by Inspection 12 Singles Record: 0-0-0 Hardcore Record: 0-0-0 Tag Team Record: 0-0-0 Championships: None. Favorite Quote: "Never had sex...always had Ex!" Favorite Weapon: Broken Beer Bottle Debut: November, 2001 Contract Expires: N/A Superstar Background: Childhood: Alfred Thompson was born into a family of 6 and didn't have a very eventful childhood. He was the youngest of all the children but he was also the first of all the children to get into daddy's liquor cabinet. At age 9, Al already built up a tolerance to vodka and other hard liquors that would make most middle-aged men jealous. He was never a violent drunk though. It was more of a relative calmness that overcame his mind. Teenage Years: Since dad always had liquor in his cabinets, and never beer, Al wasn't introduced to the greatest of all alcohols until he was at his first kegger in tenth grade. He loved the taste, the feeling and didn't stop drinking it. It was a match made in heaven. It would have been a perfect match if only one thing weren't out of place. Al's temperment. Beer had a way of boosting Al's adrenaline to levels off the charts. His strength was boosted, his pain threshold virtually became a brick wall. Beer was to Al, what Spinach is to Popeye. However, it also made him very violent. He didn't think through every action, and hurt many a people. He has manages to stick away from beer in regular, every day life and only to touch it when he needed it. How he got into wrestling: Alfred had a friend named James who had been wrestling in a local promotion for about a year or so, and every so often when Al would go to his practices, one of the owners would invite him to join and offer him a good spot, but Al always refused, not really finding it worth his time. Finally, after several months, and many shows later, Al started to see that it could be a lot of fun and wanted to try his part. James got him introduced to the head owner, and Al started his training. He took off and learned many moves, as well as adapted some to his own technique. It wasn't until he had a big match that he decided to try drinking a beer before the match. The results were startling. He became a persona very similar to the Ultimate Warrior. Now Al has graduated from the indy fed that he was in, and is looking to join a real federation.
