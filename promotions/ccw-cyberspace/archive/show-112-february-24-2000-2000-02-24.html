Show #112 - February 24, 2000

24 February 2000

What this is Script of Cyberspace Championship Wrestling show #112 staged on February 24, 2000 from Salt Lake City, Utah, with announcer commentary, matches and backstage angles.
February 24, 2000 from The Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah - Show #112 Sid Powers vs. Impact - CCW Independent Championship "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades vs. Adam Pimp - Flaming Table Match Xavier vs. Rushmore - Closed Cage Inferno Match Mash vs. Ghettodogg J.C. Superstar vs. Hardcore Gorilla Mask Seven Steele & Corey James vs. The Untouchables Savannah Hilton vs. Stephanie Black - Hardcore Match Ice Chamber vs. Diablo LOCK-DOWN THURSDAY From The Delta Center in Salt Lake City, Utah Stevens: Welcome to another sell-out Lock-Down and welcome to The Delta Center here in beautiful Salt Lake City! We're just two days away from "Ground Zero" which is shaping up to be one of the best PPVs in CCW History! In just two days there will be a 15-Man Battle Royal for the CCW Heavyweight Championship! That is going to be the best match we've had in a long time but some people are wondering how some of those participants in that match were signed. Also at "Ground Zero" we'll see the CCW Tag Team Championship on the line when The Titans look to defend against The Disciples in what is to be a very high-octane meeting between the two teams. Hard to say who will be the victor in that one, but we'll find out come this Saturday only on CCW LIVE! We have something on the CCWTron... (Lady Cat and Pepper are backstage looking over some papers, laughing hard. Pepper notices the camera and nudges Lady Cat.) Lady Cat: Well, about time. I've got something to say real quick. First of all Phil Burner. If you didn't want me to say anything to you and stay out of your so called business, you should never have touched me. You are now my favorite new friend ~grins~ You'll find out soon enough what that means. If you manage to keep that belt at the PPV, you're gonna be soo.. ahem.. well, not to give away my gift to you. I'm cheering for you at the PPV man! ~wink~ Stevens: (Cameras cut back to the announcer's table.) The CCW Commissioner, Lady Cat, looks like she is has something planned for the Fat Ass come this Sunday at "Ground Zero". Your guess is as good as mine. (Screen cuts to high above the arena floor as a Nirvana song is played over the PA.) ICE CHAMBER VS. DIABLO Well we haven't heard much from Ice Chamber in recent months, but that's about to change here tonight as we get set for our one year anniversary bash on Saturday night. His opponent for tonight is Diablo who is already in the ring for our opening contest. (Bell sounds.) The two are locking up now as Diablo throws Ice Chamber into the ropes, but Chamber ducks a spinning elbow attempt and returns off the ropes with a flying forearm shot, knocking Diablo down. Chamber dragging Diablo to the corner, climbs to the second turnbuckle and delivers a knee drop across the upper-leg of Diablo. (Diablo holds his leg in pain.) (A strange little man runs out from the backstage area and begins trying to coach Ice Chamber to a victory.) Hey, I know that guy! That's Ice Chamber's former manager Vanilla Iceman! Why in the Hell is he back in CCW? Didn't he die a while back? No matter because Ice Chamber is distracted by him! Chamber is shouting obscenities towards Iceman but is grabbed by Diablo and sent off the ropes. Ducking the move again is Ice Chamber and he ran into Vanilla Iceman, who was on the apron, and knocked him into the guard rails which surround the crowd! (Chamber looks out of the ring and curses at Iceman.) Diablo with the roll-up from behind, we have a winner, 1-2-NO! Ice Chamber barely escaped! Diablo putting the pain on Chamber now, but he's using his upper body strength to spin Diablo around and pushing him into the corner. Chamber with a couple of knee shots to the kidney there and now sets him up for the piledriver! (Chamber focuses back to the outside where Iceman lies in pain.) (Impact runs to the ring with a homemade ice guitar in hand, sculpted of ice.) That's Impact! What business does he have in this match!? He's got the referee and dragging him to the outside and he's just piledriven the referee! That should be an automatic DQ! Impact has a match scheduled later tonight against Sid Powers but it seems he is more interested in Ice Chamber at this time! He's in the ring and Chamber charges but Impact ducks the clothesline and turns to smash the ice guitar over the head of Ice Chamber! He's out "cold"! (Impact yells "Maybe now you can be in a state of nirvana.") (Impact goes to wake the referee.) I don't think the ref knows, or even remembers what has happened to him! Diablo goes for the pin on Ice Chamber... ONE! TWO! NO! I can't believe Ice Chamber kicked out! And now Diablo goes to the top rope! What are we about to see here? Diablo launches a flying body press from the top but Ice Chamber still has sanity on his side and catches Diablo into a sidewalk slam! How about that!? Referee out of position... ONE..... TWO..... THREE! (Bell sounds.) What a match that was! Ice Chamber is the man! Now all he has to do is act like it and he may have a great future here in CCW! (Another Nirvana song blares over the PA as Ice Chamber leaves the arena with the fans in awe.) SAVANNAH HILTON VS. STEPHANIE BLACK - HARDCORE MATCH Well what a wild match that was folks! This next match is sure to be a dandy with Savannah Hilton and Stephanie Black going one on one in a Hardcore Match! Do women's hardcore matches really last very long? Not in my book, but we have two tough women squaring off here tonight! (Bell sounds as the women are already in the ring.) Savannah Hilton has brought a portion of a stone pillar with her to the ring, representing her Italian blood I suppose while on the other end Stephanie has got a bucket of golf balls? Golf balls? Come on, where's the traditional steel chair and trash can? Hilton charging slowly with that stone pillar, damn that thing must weight 100 pounds! Stephanie dodges and whacks Hilton across the back with the bucket of golf balls. Hilton loses hold of the pillar and falls to her knees but look out! Ooooh! Stephanie has slammed into Hilton's backside forcing her face forward into the stone pillar! What a bloody mess Hilton is in now! Stephanie bringing Hilton to her feet now and setting her up for a suplex. She's got her up, no she doesn't! Hilton wiggles her way into a reversal and lifts Stephanie up and suplexes her onto the stone pillar! My god! That could've broke her back! Hilton staggering to gain balance here as the blood trickles down her face. She's got the bucket of golf balls! What is she gonna do with that? Tee off? Hilton takes a golf ball in her hand and throws it at Stephanie (golf ball explodes) and oh my! Those are exploding golf balls and Hilton has a huge smile on her face now! She takes two more golf balls and slams them on Stephanie who was knocked unconscious from the first suplex. They're exploding all over Stephanie! Her legs, her arms, her chest, her head! She's bleeding everywhere! Someone get some medical staff out here NOW! Taking the empty bucket now is Hilton and she's nailed the fallen and bloodied Stephanie over the head with it! Now making the cover and the ref makes the count, one, two, three! Your winner of this hardcore match by pinfall, Savannah Hilton! Mark that as victory one for Il Famiglia tonight! SEVEN STEELE & MYSTERY PARTNER VS. THE UNTOUCHABLES (Il Frenesia enters the arena to the Italian National Anthem, followed by The Untouchables. Il Frenesia carries with him a crowbar in which is to be used for protection.) Stevens: Ya know this Saturday at "Ground Zero" The Untouchables will be facing off against "The Masked Duo" and Hard Candy. Rumors circling the locker room area are that The Untouchables will be facing only themselves come Saturday in Las Vegas. Aside from tag action, The Don will have a shot at the CCW Lightweight Championship at the PPV as well so it's looking to be an interesting night for Il Famiglia. (Seven Steele enters the arena along with Corey James and Carnage.) Well I don't know about you folks at home, but I'm seeing what looks to be TWO mystery partners for Seven Steele here tonight. I had a feeling Corey James would be incahoots with Seven Steele but I never dreamnt that the CCW Hardcore Champion would join forces with mere undercarders. The question remains, who will be Seven Steele's mystery partner? (Bell sounds.) The match has officially started and now Corey James climbs up onto the apron so I assume he is going to be the mystery partner for Seven Steele. Are these three men showing a possible stable formation here? And who is the Leader and is he behind this? Who knows anything but Seven Steele now getting clobbered from behind by The Don. Whipping Steele off the ropes now and delivers a dropkick! Steele goes down but not for long, he's back up and takes a kick from The Don, putting him back to one knee. Don off the ropes, makes the tag to Il Medello, and leaps over Steele as Medello comes in behind Don and knocks Steele back with a short arm clothesline right into the path of the returing Don who sends Steele down with a bulldog! What teamwork! A quick cover, one, two, NO! Steele managed to kick out as Corey James comes in the ring. He takes Il Medello out with a roundhouse to the face followed by a dropkick to The Don, sending him to the outside. Corey James is on fire here! Off the ropes now is Corey James and he sends himself over the top rope on a suicide mission to the outside and lands on top of The Don! Inside the ring Il Medello has Steele reeling and whips him into the corner. Going upstairs now is Medello as Steele staggers away from the corner, this could be big! Yes! Medello lets go with a moonsault which connects with Steele for the pinning combination! Referee out of position! ONE, TWO, THREE! Your winners, by pinfall, are Il Medello and Don "Ceasar" Capone, The Untouchables! What a great display of teamwork by these upcoming undercarders! Most teams will have luck on their side when facing a first time tag team, such as Steele and James, but it will be interesting to see how these guys go up against short-time rivals, Hard Candy this Saturday at "Ground Zero"! (Corey James leads Seven Steele and Carnage as they exit the arena. Cameras follow them into a dark room where a hooded man stands. Corey speaks "With the addition of Seven Steele and Carnage, our quest for destruction will soon be on it's way." With that said the screen cuts to a commercial featuring a PPV promo for Ground Zero.) J.C. SUPERSTAR VS. HARDCORE GORILLA MASK Stevens: Welcome back to Lock-Down... we had an altercation backstage during the break and if we can roll that footage again guys in the truck... (The GOD is seen walking around the food preperation area as HCGM comes in off-screen and attacks him. HCGM climbs onto the kitchen counter and delivers an axe handle smash across the head of GOD. He then pushes a refrigerator over on GOD's leg as he lets out a deep and painful scream. HCGM says "That should take care of you, asshole!") Well, HCGM sure has made himself some friends with The Disciples as of late. This latest attack is sure to make GOD furious come this Saturday at "Ground Zero". Let's take you now to the ring where J.C. Superstar awaits the arrival of HCGM. (HCGM's theme plays as he comes running out and sliding into the ring.) (Bell sounds.) This one is underway and HCGM is wasting no time as he surprises Superstar, sending him off-balance into the ropes and he's tangled in the ropes! This should give HCGM the advantage! HCGM is in JC's face, shouting at him letting him know he is the original CCW Champ. Oooh! A big chop across Superstar's chest from HCGM sends echos through the arena! This is ludicrious, HCGM won't relinquish the attack on the tied up Superstar! (The lights go out as the phrase "Prepare for Total Destruction" is heard over the PA. When the lights come back on GOD is on the outside of the ring as HCGM looks at him in disbelief. Superstar is no longer tangled in the ropes.) What's this? GOD has appeared at ringside and JC Superstar is standing, un-tied, behind HCGM who is looking at GOD! (JC taps HCGM on the shoulder and delivers the Fall From Grace as he turns around.) Oh The Fall From Grace! JC finished off HCGM let's see if that's enough, referee isn't paying attention! A late count here as the ref hops over the pin and makes the count... ONE! TWO! THREE! (Bell sounds.) Here is your winner! J.C. Superstar! Oh ho ho! What an interesting little occurrence here tonight! GOD has got his revenge, kind of, against HCGM for that brutal attack backstage. We'll be back! GHETTODOGG VS. MASH (During the break: Mash is spotted pulling up to the parking garage in his ForeRunner. Out of the blue a rusted old 1972 Orange Pinto smashes into the side of Mash's ForeRunner. Mash tries to open the door but it appears to be jammed so he climbs through the window. Out of the attacking car comes Hellrazor who pulls Mash out of the window and beats him down. Marcy The Mammoth pulls up in a Hummer and she also helps in the beatdown of Mash. They bring a trashcan from a nearby dump area and double powerbomb Mash on top of it. She takes a bite of Mash's ear before leaving with Hellrazor.) Welcome back once again to Lock-Down and again we have some footage to show you during the break! (Cameras roll the previously mentioned footage.) Mash is due to meet Ghettodogg here in just a few seconds but I am speculating wether or not Mash will be able to compete after being rammed by Hellrazor and powerbombed through a trash can! (Ghettodogg's music begins playing as he enters the arena with Diablo.) (Mash's theme begins playing for a minute before he enters the arena.) Mash is here! He's made his way into the arena and he is limping. He must have hit his leg against something when Hellrazor rammed his Pinto into Mash's ride. (Bell sounds as Mash slides into the ring.) Ghettodogg going straight for Mash and he takes him down easily with a spear. Ghettodogg brings Mash to his feet now and plants him with a DDT. He's showing no mercy on Mash and Mash is showing no offense! Perhaps some par ticipation on Mash's part would boost his offensive level? Mash is in trouble now for Ghettodogg is setting him up for the Poverty Plex I do believe! He's got him up, over, and DOWN into a pinning combination! We have ONE, TWO, THREE! Your winner, by pinfall, and continuing his rise to fame in CCW, Ghettodogg!!! Sure Mash had a bothersome injury sustained at the hands of Hellrazor, but something else must have been stopping him from showing any kind of offense here tonight in Salt Lake City! XAVIER VS. RUSHMORE - CLOSED CAGE INFERNO MATCH Folks, we've had some great action for you tonight. And this next match is sure to give us some undercard talent at it's finest. Personally, I am excited to see how well this Xavier character can handle his in-ring opponents as opposed to his imaginary foes. He's been talking smack but it's time for him to prove his worth in this organization as he faces one of our biggest challengers, Rushmore. (Both men are inside the closed cage as the ref calls for the cage to be ignited. Flames rise up from the base of the ring to the top of the cage. The only way to win is to catch your opponent on fire.) (Bell sounds.) This Closed Cage Inferno match is now underway. Rushmore has only one win under his belt here in CCW and with seven losses backing that up I will choose the rookie, Xavier, to come out on top in this one. Rushmore locks up with Xavier as he overpowers the new rookie. Xavier goes flying into the ropes as Rushmore forces him out of the lock up. Xavier quickly turning around to avoid the flames but Rushmore rushes in from behind and slams Xavier's face into the metal cage wiring. He's holding his face on the cage, inches from the fire! You can see the sweat starting to trickle down Xavier's face! (Xavier fights back in an attempt to stay away from the fire.) Xavier looking to break free from Rushmore's grasp now. Rushmore voluntarily lets go of the hold and Xavier runs away from Rushmore before turning quickly to smack Rushmore in the face with a open hand! It had no affect! Rushmore grabs Xavier by the throat and slams him to the mat with a massive chokeslam! The 400+ pounder is going to the top rope! NO! This man is going to the top of the cage and the flames have got to be burning him! Off the top, Rushmore delivers a flying elbow! Or in this case it may be frying but I don't see any flames! Connected! Xavier is wiggling about in pain! Rushmore, bringing Xavier to his feet, a gorilla press! Xavier is high above the mat as Rushmore walks to the cage wall and throws him into the cage wiring, catching Xavier's backside on fire! (Bell sounds.) Ladies and Gents, Rushmore has claimed his second career victory here tonight by putting the fire on Xavier. Will Rushmore be able to start a winning streak by defeating The Camargo Kid this Saturday at "Ground Zero"? Just two days away CCW LIVE will broadcast the live PPV as it goes down! We'll find out if Rushmore can make it two in a row at 7PM CST Saturday night! Now, on to our next match of the evening. "MR. FROSTY" BOBBY BLADES VS. ADAM PIMP - FLAMING TABLE MATCH ("Anthem For The Year 2000" blasts over the PA as Bobby Blades makes his arena entrance as Adam Pimp waits in the ring.) These former stablemates are looking to beat the ever-livin-piss out of each other tonight in CCW's first ever Flaming... or "Flamming" as some of you like to call it, Table Match! I'm sure Bobby Blades has more important issues on his mind tonight than Adam Pimp but will use this match to prepare himself for his Trans-Atlantic Title shot he will obtain this Saturday night at "Ground Zero". (Bell sounds.) There's the bell and this one is ready to go. Pimp circling around Blades and they lock up! Pimp with an armbar on Blades but it's quickly reversed into an armbar on Pimp. Blades has Pimp from the backside and pushing him to the ropes, trying for the roll up but Pimp holds onto the ropes. Blades on his feet but is knocked back down as Pimp delivers a superkick to the chin. Pimp dragging Blades to the center of the ring now and placing him in a Half Crab. Referee asking for Blades to submit but he refuses. (Jaicee Davis runs out to the ring with a flaming chair in hand.) Jaicee Davis rushing into the ring and she's got a flaming chair for cryin' out loud! She's in the ring and just smashed the chair across the back of Adam Pimp! Pimp falls on his ass as Davis helps Blades to his feet. Bobby seems to be ok on his own and lifts Pimp to his feet and hurls him into the air with a suplex. He's holding it as Jaicee Davis sets up that table! (Jaicee pours lighter fluid on the table and lights it ablaze.) I think Blades is losing his balance, no! I think his back is giving out! (Blades falls with Adam Pimp being dropped across the flaming table.) (Bell sounds.) Bobby Blades is hurting, but he has picked up the victory. Davis in the ring crying and screaming for some help. (Several paramedics run to the ring to escort Blades out of the arena.) How will this injury affect Blades' chance of winning the TA title this Saturday!? Wel'll return shortly! SID POWERS VS. IMPACT - CCW INDEPENDENT CHAMPIONSHIP (The Italian National Anthem plays as The Don enters the arena with two Il Famiglia bodyguards. He has a mic and begins to speak. "This is-a for any champ-a! It could be-a you Sid, maybe Mr-a. Bowe, or any title holder. You name-a the stipulations and I will-a be there-a Sunday. So let's-a just see-a if we have champs instead of-a chumps!" If you do not understand what I'm talking about, tune into Ground Zero this Saturday night and you may see some interesting angles take place for those who do well in CCW. Thank you and goodnight!"
