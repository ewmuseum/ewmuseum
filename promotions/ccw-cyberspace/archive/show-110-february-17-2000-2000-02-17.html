Show #110 - February 17, 2000

What this is Script of Cyberspace Championship Wrestling show #110 staged on February 17, 2000 from St. Louis, Missouri, with announcer commentary, matches and backstage angles.
February 17, 2000 from The Kiel Center in St. Louis, Missouri - Show #110 Sid Powers vs. Don "Ceasar" Capone - CCW Independent Championship Ghettodogg vs. Carnage - CCW Hardcore Championship "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Blue Thunder Samson vs. The Wish Master Savannah Hilton vs. Jessica Carney, Stephanie Black, Marcy The Mammoth - Gauntlet Match J.C. Superstar vs. "The Avenger" Tim Inman Sylkk vs. Hellrazor - Hardcore Match Pepper The Cat vs. Diablo LOCK-DOWN THURSDAY The Kiel Center in St. Louis, Missouri *"CCW Theme" plays over the PA as the cameras pan over the crowd. Yellow and blue pyro blasts from the entrance way to center-ceiling of the arena. The fans are screaming at a tremendously high noise level as the CCW President, Jonathan Stevens, makes his way to the ringside commentary table. As the smoke clears a sign is show in the crowd reading "My Ass is Fatter Than Yours" and "Here's Your Judgement". The Prez begins to introduce the show.* Stevens: Listen to this capacity crowd! Never before has St. Louis made so much noise at a CCW event until tonight! Folks, I'm Jonathan Stevens and we have a great show for you tonight here from the Kiel Center! Tonight Tim Inman is scheduled to do battle with the prophecies of J.C. Superstar and The CCW Hardcore Championship will be on the line when Ghettodogg sets to defend against one half of the Boneless Skeleton Freaks, Carnage. In our Main Event we will see The CCW Independent Championship decided when current champion, Sid Powers, go toe to toe with relative newcomer and rookie sensation Don "Ceasar" Capone! It was Sid Powers who offered this title shot to the rookie Capone not too long ago. Powers better be careful because The Don is undefeated thus far in his young career! *"School's Out" plays over the PA as The Jolly Rancher enters the arena ready to speak about his affairs in CCW.* Rancher: Fans, thank you for coming here tonight and allowing me the time to speak my mind regarding my status in CCW... First of all, as many of you know, Mr. Gobstopper is still missing but I have the feeling he'll be turning up in CCW sometime soon, so tonight I end my search for him. *Fans boo at this news.* Secondly, and most importantly, I have received a letter from Princeton University, inviting me to join their Level One Honors Program. This is a very lucky break for me to join this program, and my life is sure to go places once I graduate from this program. So fans, I'm here tonight to let everyone in this arena, everyone backstage, and everyone watching at home, that The Jolly Rancher is retiring from CCW. *Fans boo at this news as well.* *"Gob Drop" (Brood's Theme in MIDI format) begins to play as Mr. Gobstopper makes an entrance into the arena.* *The fans go crazy.* Gobstopper: St. Louis, Missouri! Mr. Gobstopper is BACK! Rancher: Wha? How did? You were? Huh? Gobstopper: Relax brother, let me explain. I was kidnapped by some goons and as I was taken to an unknown location, I could hear them speak... Speaking they were and you know what? They were speaking in ITALIAN ACCENTS! Rancher: So you are referring to The Untouchables? Gobstopper: You damn skippy! Those rookie-ass bastards thought they would have me join their little stable, Il Ugly Family, but I managed to escape their broken down Pinto parked two blocks down the street. I came out here to ask you to fight by my side one final time, before you retire, against the likes of The Untouchables. So what do you say brother? One final match before you head back to Princeton? Rancher: Well I'm glad to see that you are alright and were not seriously disoriented, but The Untouchables work in numbers. Where there are two, you will find two more. Gobstopper: That does not worry me. What do you say man? Rancher: One final match, next Thursday. Ok, I agree to this. Gobstopper: Great, great! Untouchables... do you accept the challenge?
