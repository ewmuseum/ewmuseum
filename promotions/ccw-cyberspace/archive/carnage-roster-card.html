Carnage Real Name: Marcus Vandalay Birthdate: 07-13-80 Hometown: Rural, North Dakota Height: 6'6' Weight: 268 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Heel Manager(s): His Mentor Current Teams: The Fury Entrance Music: 'Papercut' by Linkin Park Wrestling Style: Hardcore, Brawler, Powerhouse Signature Moves: Big Boot, Stall Suplex, Knees To the Face - Corner, Death Valley Driver, Chokeslam, Leaping Clothesline, High Angle Backdrop, DDT, Brawling Punches, German Suplex Set Up Move: 'Scarface' (Mix between 'Rings of Saturn' and 'Crippler Crossface'. Carnage locks one of the opponent's arms with his leg and has the other arm in a chicken wing, leaving Carnage's other arm free to pound on the opponent. ) Finisher: 'Path of Destruction' (POD) (High Impact Powerbomb, can be performed off the ropes, to the floor, through tables, ect. ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: (Current) Pacific Title, (1) Trans-Atlantic Title, (3) Hardcore Title Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: Best Hardcore Wrestler 2000 - Nominee Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Not much history is known about where Carnage was born or where he grew up for the early part of his life. His real name is unknown also; his parents took the birth certificate with them when they left him at age 4. After being orphaned he moved in with his grandfather who lived on a farm in rural North Dakota. His grandfather had him stay home and work on the farm rather than being sent to school. His grandfather named his young grandson Marcus Vandalay. 13 years later his grandfather died and he was left with the basic skills of hard work that his grandfather taught him. Marcus inherited the farm, which was left in his name. Marcus had no other family relations so he was forced to live on his own. After a few weeks he had no money and he had to sell off the farm to pay the bills. For the next year Marcus moved to the closest town and slept in the alleys. At the age of 19 he finally found work as a farm hand. It was the only skill he had ever known so he flourished at it. The owner of the farm brought Marcus in and treated him as one of his sons. 2 years went by before Marcus' life would be drastically changed... On a trip to the nearest town Marcus and the farm owner went to a bar. While in the bar, a drunken farmer made comments about how Marcus was “a retarded bastard“ and how his parents were better off without him. That was the breaking point for everything that had gone wrong in his life. The fight that ensued was how his name has come to be. Marcus beat the poor old farmer to the last ounce of his life, until the fight was broken up. The next day in the newspaper the fight was described simply as 'carnage'. By a stroke of luck, a friend of a CCW scout had witnessed the fight and immediately recommended 'Carnage', nicknamed by the townspeople, to CCW. Carnage later tried out and became an elite member of CCW. Carnage later went on to become a 2-time CCW Hardcore Champion. However, tragedy struck in a hardcore match when Carnage was thrown off the stage by Neurosis and landed on his neck. He was rushed to the hospital where it was announced he had broken his neck. Carnage was quickly forgotten by CCW and by everyone that once knew him. He had been in re-hab for the past two years, biding his time... waiting to come back... The return... Carnage came back to quickly regain his title as a 'Hardcore Legend'. Weeks after his return, Carnage got involved in his first primetime feud; his opponent... Danny Bryson, an established tough guy adnd hardcore brawler. They had their final encounter at CCW Dead or Alive in a 'Hardcore Hell' match, in which two men entered a dungeon trashed with weapons and tried to escape. Ironically, the name of the pay-per-view prevailed; Carnage and Bryson beat the hell out of each other until finally Danny Bryson pulled off the victory. Again, Carnage was sent to the hospital... Finally Carnage was released from the hospital after his skin healed from the brutal match. As many times before, he returned with bumps, bruises, and stitches. Only this time, something was different. Usually a man to keep his business to himself and rely only on his skills, Carnage returned with someone... The identity of the man is unknown, all that is known of the man is that he is the mentor of Carnage. Within weeks of his return, Carnage defeated TVO for the Trans-Atlantic Title. However after only a week of holding the TA Title, Carnage was defeated by... yep, you've guessed him... Danny Bryson. Recent rumors indicate that this deadly combination of Carnage and his mentor, is leading to even more deadly actions. It has been stated that the two are secretly planning their revenge...
