CCW match: Melody Widmark & Sheena Ramone vs. Lacey Skye Morris & Chloe Mason

13 March 2003

Melody Widmark & Sheena Ramone vs. Lacey Skye Morris & Chloe Mason Date: 03/13/03 Show: Lockdown 'No Matter What' by Toya hits and another newcomer, Chloe Mason, makes an appearance at the top of the stage. Dressed in black leather pants and a black leather crop top, she's all business as she makes her way down to the ring, the crowd popping for her as she goes. Nayt Willems: And their opponents... First, making her way to the ring from Seattle, Washington and weighing in at 105 pounds...CHLOE MASON! Chloe enters the ring and immediately starts trash talking Melody and Sheena who lounge against their corner. The two women smirk and just stare back at the just-turned-eighteen teenager in front of them. JD: Well, I'll say one thing. Chloe's as cute as a button and isn't afraid to speak her mind. Looks like she'll fit in well here. BJS: Not as well as Melody. Are you SURE she's a lesbian? JD: Yup. BJS: Are you POSITIVE? JD: Yup. BJS: SON OF A BITCH!!! 'Star of Tears' by Joanne Hogg hits and the fans all rise to their feet and nearly go ballistic as Lacey Skye Morris walks out onto the stage and heads right down for the ring. She wears her traditional cerulean halter top and short cheerleader skirt, complemented by her favorite black fashion boots. Nayt Willems: And her partner... Approaching the ring from Stockton, California and weighing in at 140 pounds... LACEY SKYE MORRIS! Lacey climbs up the ring apron and ascends her turnbuckle, raising one arm skyward and awarding the fans with a beautiful smile as the floodgates break open and bright white flashes of light from the thousands of cameras around ringside record this moment forever. Lacey then turn and glances briefly down at Chloe, before glaring across the ring at Melody and Sheena, and then dropping her gaze to that of Commissioner Raymond who stands smirking near the announce table. JD: This whole match has to be a set-up, Slater. Raymond wouldn't be out here otherwise. BJS: **tearing his eyes away from Melody's ass** Huh? What did you just say? JD: **sighs** Nevermind. Chucky Astone, also unable to keep his eyes off of Melody, strides out into the middle of the ring and signals for the bell. Sheena strides toward the center of the ring from her corner, ready to start the fight. In the other corner, Chloe climbs out of the ring as Lacey cautiously approaches the cocky punk-rocker. JD: And here we go! Lacey tries to lock up with her opponent, but Sheena tumbles right past her and springs to her feet, nailing Lacey with a dropkick to the jaw as she turned to find the high-flyer! BJS: You know what I think she should do? Tag in Melody! I want to see her lose that top! JD: Will you just go and masturbate or something? You're getting on my nerves! BJS: **shrugs** Fine, I will. And BJS up and leaves the announce table, entering the crowd, looking to hopefully find someplace nice and private. During Slater's little outburst, Lacey was knocked onto her back and Sheena gave her no rest, jumping high into the air and landing right across Lacey's midsection with a Splash. As the crowd booed with the impact, Lacey's eyes rolled back into her head as her shoulders and feet involuntarily lurched up off the canvas. Bjs: And now Sheena's tagging in her partner. Melody picks Lacey up by her hair and connects with a hard punch to the jaw! Lacey almost falls backwards from the force of the blow, but Melody holds her up. Chloe: Come on, Lacey! Melody merely laughs at Chloe and puts Lacey's arm behind her Melody's) head, grabs the hem of Lacey's skirt and executes a textbook suplex. The impact shakes the ring as Lacey grabs the back of her head and rocks back and forth in pain. JD: Melody's tagging in Sheena again, who has just climbed up to the top rope. Melody has grabbed Lacey Skye and picks her up, holding her in place for a drop kick from the top rope... The kick WOULD have connected. However, as Sheena launched herself like a missile from the top rope, Lacey stomped down on Melody's foot, causing her to howl in pain and relinquish the hold on Lacey. The result was a strong drop kick right to Melody's face. JD: Lacey rolls quickly to her corner as Sheena looks uneasily at Melody, who is shaking her head to clear out the cobwebs... Looks like Lacey wants a breather and tags in Chloe! Melody, noticing Sheena's hesitation, becomes irritated. Melody: GO GET HER, YOU IDIOT! As Sheena turns, Chloe bounds into the ring and nails her with a clothesline. Melody starts getting to her feet and Chloe grabs her and performs a standing dropkick, connecting with Melody's chest. Chloe turns her attention back to Sheena. A sidekick to the face puts Sheena back onto her ass. The crowd cheers and pops as the rookie takes it to her competition. JD: Mason returns to Melody and, grabbing the nearly naked woman by the back of her neck, throws her out of the ring! Chloe is absolutely on fire as she hits Sheena with a Forearm Smash and backs her into a neutral corner! As Chloe Irish Whips Sheena across the ring, Lacey confronts Melody on the outside. She sends several strikes down into the girl's face and then tosses her back-first into the retainer wall. Melody grimaces in pain, but lets a small smile escape her lips as Raymond comes right up behind Lacey and turns her lights off by smashing the ring bell down over her head... CLONK!!! Lacey slumps forward into Melody, who smirks and sets the strawberry blonde up for... JD: THE DOMINATOR!!! What dirty double-crossing teamwork! Lacey is down and out of this match! Lacey's limp body is kicked over so that she is lying on her back. Melody scrutinizes Lacey's unconscious form. The strawberry blonde was out cold, with absolutely no movement other than heaving of her chest, as her arms lay out at her sides. Meanwhile, back in the ring, Chloe charges across the ring and nails Sheena with a clothesline in the corner. In desperation, Sheena gouges at Chloe's eyes, momentarily blinding the newcomer, and stumbles to her corner to tag in Melody, who just suddenly reappeared after taking out Lacey. JD: Melody gets the tag and grabs Chloe from behind, spins her around and drives her knee into Chloe's stomach, right below her belly button! A loud 'OOOUUUUGGGH!' bursts from Chloe as she falls to her knees, clutching her stomach. Melody smirks down at the teenager before turning and blowing kisses to the crowd, causing the throng to go wild with lust and hate. After playing to the crowd, she returns to Chloe, who's still holding her stomach, red-faced and gasping for air. The nearly-naked man-hater grabs the teenager around the waist and lifts her up, spinning her into a devastating Tilt-a-whirl Backbreaker. The impact of the petite Chloe's back with the point of Melody's knee makes a sickening crack, as Chloe's eyes roll back in her head. JD: My God! Melody might have just ended Chloe's career with that move! Forget that, she just might have killed her! That was brutal! Commissioner Raymond and Sheena both clap and smile in approval. The crowd rains boos down upon the ring. With Chloe's outstretched body in front of her, Melody drives a Double Axe-Handle into her exposed tummy. As Chloe jerks her head up from the impact, Melody smashes her joined fists across her jaw, sending the small wrestler tumbling backwards off of Melody's knee. The crowd boos the brutal treatment the rookie is receiving. JD: Melody pulls Chloe into the center of the ring now and tags in Sheena, who had already climbed to the top turnbuckle! And here it comes... The SHEENASSAULT!!! Sheena flies through the air with a Moonsault Plancha, smashing the already-devastated Chloe into the mat. Sheena rolls over and leans back on Chloe's unmoving body for a lazy cover. JD: Chucky makes the count! 1... 2... 3...! JD: And this one is over, folks! Lacey Skye and Chloe Mason have just been annihilated by Raymond's team! Nayt Willems: Ladies and gentlemen... Your winners by pinfall... SHEENA RAMONE and MELODY WIDMARK!!! 'Sheena is a Punk Rocker' begins to play and the fans burst into boos. Raymond enters the ring and raises both Sheena's and Melody's hands over Chloe's inert body. BJS: I'm back! Looks like I missed an ass-kicking. JD: Well, yeah. Raymond cheated by knocking out... BJS: Look, I really don't care. Whatever our Commissioner did is just fine in my book. So, did Melody's top come off? JD: No, but it shifted a bit. If you look really close, you can see a nipple. BJS: REALLY? JD: No. BJS: I hate you. JD: Well, we'll have an update for you later this evening on the condition of Lacey and Chloe. Hopefully, they'll be okay.
