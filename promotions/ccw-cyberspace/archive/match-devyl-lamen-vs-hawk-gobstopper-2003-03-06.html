CCW match: Devyl & Brad Lamen vs. Ace Hawk & Gobstopper

6 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Devyl & Brad Lamen vs. Ace Hawk & Gobstopper Date: 03/06/03 Show: Lockdown 'Born to be Wild' rings out as the fans explode in a chorus of boos until the tey see Gobber is coming out with Ace. Their boos turn to a mixed reaction as the two stomp to the ring, silently. Ace and Gobber make their way to ringside, ready and capable for this matchup. 'Mean Machine' hits and the fans erupt into boos as Lamen appears on the ramp with a snarl. Devyl comes limping out behind him as they head down to the ring. JD: It looks like Devyl and Ace are going to start this one off! BJS: Devyl and Ace lock it up, and they start in on each other. JD: They break the hold and continue to circle. BJS: Ace runs at Devyl and connects with a big clothesline taking the woman down, but she's back up quick! JD: Ace couldn't react in time, and Devyl connects a series of punches, followed by an arm lock! BJS: Devyl inches over to her corner, with the hold still on and Lamen tags in! JD: Lamen gives a couple quick kicks to Ace's ribs! BJS: Gobber runs in to try and help Ace, but the ref stops her, and in the distraction, a beautiful double suplex on Ace! JD: Devyl gets out of the ring, and Lamen goes for a quick pin. Nothing. BJS: Ace kicks out and starts to crawl over to her corner. JD: Lamen grabs his legs and drags him back, tagging Devyl back in, and Devyl from the top with a boot to the back of the downed Ace! He's in pain! BJS: Rolling around on the floor like a school girl! JD: Devyl goes to work on Ace's legs as he drops a quick knee to the back of Ace's leg. BJS: Once again, Gobber tries to get in, unsuccesfully. JD: Devyl, elbow drop. BJS: Lamen, knee drop. JD: Devyl, fist drop. F - Lamen, elbow drop ... IT MISSES! JD: SOMEHOW ACE GOT OUT OF THE WAY OF THAT! BJS: He's scrambling to the corner to tag in Gobber! JD: Reaching ... reaching! BJS: Here comes Devyl, the legal man ... ? JD: ACE GOT IT! HE GOT THE TAG! BJS: Gobber runs in and throws his body at Devyl, knocking her off her feet. He plants a dropkick on Lamen ... he's clearing house! JD: He throws Devyl into the corner, and starts kicking her down! BJS: He tags in Ace, and wrenches the arm of Devyl, exposing her midsection to the big kick to the abdominal section! JD: Ace going to work now on Devyl, as he slaps a few chops onto the ... BJS: Onto the ample bosom of Devyl! JD: Yes ... there they are. And he's kicking her down now, heading to the top turnbuckle, for an ASAI Moonsault!. BJS: He HITS IT! JD: UNBELIEVABLE! Now he's going for a MOSS COVERED-THREE HANDLED-FAMILY CREDENZA!!!!! BJS: He doesn't have the strength! No go there! Devyl takes the advantage and rolls him up into a package! Ref - ONE ... TWO ... JD: AND A KICKOUT! BJS: Not just a kickout! A REVERSAL! JD: He rolls Devyl back up into a small package! Ref - ONE ... TWO .... THREE!!!!! JD: And Ace and Gobber win this one! They accept the crowds mixed reactions and head to the back, leaving Lamen and Devyl in the ring! BJS: Lamen helps Devyl up in good show, and shakes her hand for a job well done. JD: WHAT!?!?! He grabs her arm and drops her into the Lament!!! BJS: Lamen s laying into Devyl! JD: Lamen isn't letting go! He's crazy! He just keeps it locked on, and it's taking four referees to pull him off!
