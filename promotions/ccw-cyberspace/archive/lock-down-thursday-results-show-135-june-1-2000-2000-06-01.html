June 1, 2000 from The Compaq Center in Houston, Texas - Show #135 "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades vs. Andrew "Ace" Hawk - CCW Heavyweight Championship "Fat Ass" Phil Burner vs. Tower "Rampage" Hoard - CCW Trans-Atlantic Championship Jonathan Stevens & Lady Cat vs. The Strangers - Tag Team Tournament Kelly Bowe vs. Candi Bytch vs. Deborah Carmichael - Triple Threat Match - CCW Women's Championship "The Avenger" Tim Inman & El Mierda vs. Scotty The Hotty & Xavier - Tag Team Tournament Diablo & Don "Ceasar" Capone vs. Impact & Il Medello - Tag Team Tournament Scott "The Stud" Laughlin vs. Sylkk Dusty vs. Ice Chamber Delilah vs. Sugar "The Superior Slut" Lock-Down Thursday Results - June 1, 2000 (No Color Errors, sorry) Thursday, 01-Jun-2000 14:38:49 [detail withheld] writes: Lock-Down Thursday Results - June 1, 2000 Scene opens outside of the Compaq Center in Houston, Texas. Thousands of fans are trying to get their favorite CCW merchandise before the show starts. Camera cuts to inside of the Compaq Center where red and green pyros explode on the stage. Then all of a sudden blue fireworks explode in the center of the ring. Camera goes to the CCW-Tron where highlights from last week’s Lock-Down and the Sunday PPV are shown. Unfamiliar music plays and then a man walks through the entranceway down to the announcer’s table and sits down. Hello CCW fans, I’m Patrick McSmith stepping in as the Lock-Down announcer for tonight’s show. It promises to be a great show when we see action from the Tag-Team tournament as well as some Champion Of The Ring qualifying matches. So without further wait let’s go to the backstage area with some groundbreaking footage... Bobby Blades and the Franchise are sitting in their locker room when all of a sudden WAI bursts in! They beat everyone down and they grab Bobby Blades. They drag him into the ring and proceed to tar and feather him! And now Phil has a microphone: "I bet you really feel like a chicken now, dontcha Bobby! This is jest th' beginning. Every single day I will make a living hell fer you! Every single day, thet is, until I git my title shot. Because we are, an' the people are sick of seein’ you parade yer bitchass aroun' with th' belt thet you disgrace! See you later tonight ass." Then they leave and now the CCW staff comes into the ring to try and clean up the mess. Oh my, we have just seen the tar and feathering of Bobby Blades. I haven’t seen on those since the days of Paul Revere!!! Look at those CCW fools try and clean up the tar! Don’t they know that it is sticky!?! But now let’s go to the first match of the night as we see a COTR match. ED "GHETTO STAR" SHAHEED VS. XAVIER – COTR QUALIFYING MATCH Xavier is already in the ring and we are waiting the arrival of the debut of the new rookie, Ed Shaheed. The Ghetto Star is coming out but what is this... As Ed Shaheed makes his way to the ring Ghettodogg comes out from behind the curtain and zaps Ed with his TAZER! Then Ghettodogg rolls Ed into the ring to Xavier. Boy oh boy Ghettodogg sure likes to use that tazer of his. What is this, a new alliance between Xavier and Ghettodogg or the Dogg being offended by a new Ghetto Star?? But now Ed Shaheed is in the ring and Xavier goes to work on him with some stiff kicks to the ribs of the Ghetto Star. Xavier picks Ed up and gives him a nice vertical suplex. Shaheed gets up and swings for a clothesline but Xavier catches him and gives him a Rock Bottom. Ed gets up again but Xavier takes him down with a dropkick. The Ghetto Star falls to the floor with that dropkick from Xavier. Ed Shaheed tries to rethink his game plan on the outside but Xavier comes over the ropes with a suicide dive! But no! Ed Shaheed moved out of the way and Xavier crashed into the steel fencing. Ed brings Xavier into the ring and sets him up for a powerbomb, and connects with Xavier going down hard to the mat. Ed looks like he is signaling for his finisher. But Xavier is up and he goes for a kick but his foot is stuck on some tar from the tar & feathering of Blades! Xavier can’t get his foot free and now Ed comes over to help him! Oh my, Ed helped Xavier right into his finisher, Da Lo’ Ridah!!! Ed Shaheed goes for the pin 1...2...3!!! Ed "Ghetto Star" Shaheed has won his first match here in CCW against Xavier. Now let’s look to the CCW-Tron, I have word that something’s happening. The CCW-Tron shows Jessica Carney walking around like crazy... She seems worried about something. She has some guards protecting her. Then, from all of a sudden, the guards run away, like crazy. Then the camera shows 5 pit bulls running against them, and Jessica runs on the opposite direction. When she turns into a corner, Deborah Carmichael shows up and says: "Deb: Hi, dear. Remember ME?????????" Debbie then nails Jessica with a lead pipe, and then leaves, and the camera shows Jessica laying on the ground, out cold. Well fans we have just seen some shocking footage of Deborah Carmichael beating the living daylights out of Jessica Carney. Let’s take you back to the ring for the next match. SCOTTY THE HOTTY VS. EL MIERDA – COTR QUALIFYING MATCH The lights go out and all of a sudden "Livin' La Vida Loca" by Ricky Martin blasts into the arena. The fans start booing when Scotty the Hotty steps onto the entranceway and starts dancing. He walks down the aisle and then stops in the middle and starts dancing again. The fans are throwing beer, pop and anything else they can find at Scotty. He slides into the ring and gets a microphone. "Hey, didn't the announcer tell all of you redneck, welfare, pathetic fu-(BEEP) to not throw anything at the performers???" The fans start throwing garbage into the ring. Scotty gets ready to talk but a full glass of beer hits him in the face. "Ok that's it!!! I'm starting this damn interview so everyone shut the hell up!!!" The fans boo very loudly. "Ok so first, I am going to kick El Mierda's sorry ass and then win the COTR tournament. I am the #1 superstar in the tournament so let me see all of your #1 superstar hand signs." The fans flip off Scotty and continue to throw garbage and boo loudly. "Well anyway let's have that gay Mexican get out here for his ass-kicking. And remember fans, I am the future..." Well Scotty is in the ring and here comes El Mierda. The fans are actually cheering for him after Scotty’s heel interview. El Mierda slides into the ring and here we go. Scotty and Mierda are exchanging punches and Scotty gets the advantage. He kicks El Mierda in the gut and gives him a DDT. Mierda is right up and now he gets the advantage with some punches. Mierda whips Scotty into the ropes and he knocks down the Hotty with a back body drop. Mierda goes for the pin 1...2... No, too early to put Scotty away. El Mierda picks him up and sets him up for a suplex. El Mierda picks him up but Scotty flips over El Mierda and gives him a lowblow. If the ref didn’t see that cheapshot by Scotty he must be blind. And the ref claims he didn’t see it. Scotty picks him up and connects with a piledriver. El is down and holding his head and now Scotty gives him a leg-drop across Mierda’s face. Scotty is signaling for his Waller Legdrop and he climbs to the top rope. He jumps... But El Mierda moves out of the way. Scotty is in pain and El Mierda is going to the top. What’s El Mierda doing he isn’t a high flyer? He jumps... But Scotty pulled the ref in the way and El Mierda hit the ref. Mierda is trying to apologize to the referee and Scotty goes for a clothesline but El Mierda grabs his arm and twists it around. Scotty is in real pain from that submission move but gives Mierda an eye gouge and then a lowblow. Scotty picks up Mierda and gives him his finisher the Killer Dream! Scotty goes for a pin but there’s no ref. Scotty should’ve thought about that before he got the ref knocked out! Scotty is trying to revive the ref and El Mierda kicks him in the gut and powerbombs Scotty hard to the mat!! El Mierda rolls Scotty over and goes for a pin...1...2...3!!! El Mierda has defeated Scotty the Hotty to advance to the next round in the COTR tournament. Now we have some CCW-Tron footage of something going on in the parking lot. Take a look... Sylkk is coming to the arena in his brand new car. When he parks it, a 2000 Camaro comes and crashes it from behind. Sylkk looks at the driver's seat, searching for the man who did that, but he finds just a brick in the accelerator. Then, Andrew "Ace" Hawk comes from behind and nails him with his silver baseball bat several times, and then leaves. Sylkk is lying motionless on the ground, as some EMT’s run to the scene. After that attack by Ace I don’t know if Sylkk will ever be able to walk. And now it’s time for the next match, a triple threat women’s match! DEBORAH CARMICHAEL VS. JESSICA CARNEY VS. KITTY LYNN – TRIPLE THREAT MATCH Ring Announcer-"The next match is a Women’s Triple Threat match! And now making her way to the ring, Kitty Lynn!" Kitty walks out and gives high fives to the fans and then climbs into the ring. Ring Announcer-"The next competitor is Deborah Carmichael!" Deb walks out to cheers from the fans and then slides into the ring. Ring Announcer-"And the last woman to go to the ring, Jessica Carney!" Jessica walks out slowly to the ring, after the beating she took from Deborah backstage. Debbie and Kitty look at each other and then attack Jessica as she gets into the ring. They are stomping poor Jessica down to the mat. Hasn’t she gone through enough after that attack from Deborah? Kitty goes for a pin on Jessica 1...2...No, Deb pulls her off. Deb goes for a pin 1...2...No, Kitty pulls her off. Now back in the ring Kitty Lynn has turned on Deb and nailed her with a couple of right hands. Kitty picks Jessica up and gives her a vertical suplex. Jessica goes down and now Deb catches Kitty with a back breaker. Deb looks at her two downed opponents and then gives them a double elbow drop! Wow, Deb is really showing us something tonight! And too bad it isn’t her puppies! Deborah is telling the other two women to fight. Jessica and Kitty start exchanging purses, I mean punches, sorry. And now Jessica knocked Kitty down with a clothesline. Jess picks Kitty up and gives her a hurricanrana! Deb sees an opening and goes after Jessica with a German suplex. Kitty gets up and hits Deb in the back with a dropkick. All three women are up and they staring each other down. Uh, oh they have started trash talking each other, and you only know where this is leading... CAT FIGHT!!! All three women are rolling around the mat pulling hair, scratching and a whole lot more! SWEET!!! A three-way cat-fight!!! Look at the referee, he doesn’t want to breakup the fight, he loves this action. Now Debbie is up but Kitty and Jessica are still rolling around and fighting. Jessica some how manages to throw Kitty Lynn to the outside. Jessica turns around and gets kicked by Deborah. Debbie is calling for her finisher. Deborah has her in the Death Dash and connects! She goes for the pin 1...2...3!!! Deborah Carmichael has gotten the win over Jessica Carney and Kitty Lynn. She proves to be one of the top women contenders. But what’s this... After the match Sugar runs out and hits Deborah Carmichael over the head with her purse. How can a purse hurt. I don’t get it. Wait a minute... Sugar empties her purse and a bunch of bricks fall out. So that’s why. Thank god, some EMT’s are finally out there helping Deborah Carmichael walk out. Now let’s take you to the ring for our next match. It is a tag-team tournament match with the winner facing Scotty the Hotty & Xavier at SSN. JOQUAR & HARDCORE GORILLA MASK VS. TOWER "RAMPAGE" HOARD & "THE WARRIOR" WAYNE WYNTER – TAG TEAM TOURNAMENT MATCH Ring Announcer-"The winner of this Tag-Team match will face Scotty the Hotty & Xavier in the semifinals. The first team making their way to the ring, with a combined weight of 620 lbs., Joquar & Hardcore Gorilla Mask!" They sure don’t look very happy to be partners to me. They were just recently in a feud over the CCW Hardcore Title. I’m not sure if they can survive as a cohesive unit. Ring Announcer-"The second team, at a combined weight of 599 lbs., Tower "Rampage" Hoard & "The Warrior" Wayne Wynter." Well, that team looks funny doesn’t it. The Cretin Family sure went out of their way to make this the most embarrassing and humiliating tag-team tourney the CCW has ever seen. The two teams are in the ring staring each other down. You can tell in their eyes they don’t want to be in this mock tournament. And now the stillness breaks! Joquar is on the Warrior and Tower is on HCGM. They are brawling with flurries of kicks and punches. The ref can’t keep control of the action, I think he’s still remembering that triple threat cat-fight! He finally restored order and now Joquar and Tower Hoard will start the match off. Tower with a stiff clothesline to Joquar’s neck takes him down. Tower tags in the Warrior and they go to work on Joquar. Double whip into the ropes and Bossman Slam! That was enough for HCGM and he gets in and is beating up on Wayne. The ref gets keeps order and now it is HCGM taking out Wayne the Warrior. Hardcore picks up Wayne and gives him a German Suplex. Wayne tries to get to his feet but Hardcore gives him a Fame-asser. Hardcore tags Joquar back in and now we have a double team chokeslam! Tower wants in but the ref won’t let him. Joquar goes to work on Wayne with a gut-wrench suplex. Joquar goes for the figure 4 but Wayne pushes him off and gets the tag to Tower. Tower comes in and takes out HCGM who was on the apron. Tower goes for a clothesline on Joquar but he ducks and gives Tower a drop toe hold. Joquar with the pin 1...2...NO! Wayne managed to get in and get the save. Now all the men are back in the ring. Wayne and Joquar are fighting by the ropes and Tower dropkicks both over the ropes. HCGM grabs Tower from behind and locks on a sleeper hold. Wayne and Joquar continue to fight on the outside. Now they’re going up to the entrance way. Don’t they care about the match? Obviously not because they’ve disappeared to the back somewhere. Back in the ring Hardcore still has Tower locked in that sleeper hold. Tower refuses to gives up. Uh oh we might have trouble, and trouble by the name of bytch... Candi comes out in a short, black, leather mini skirt and a zip-up tight black leather jacket. She walks to the ring and gets on the ring apron. She teases HCGM and then she unzips her jacket and is down to a black lace bra. She just lured HCGM out of that sleeper hold he had Tower in. While HCGM is distracted, Tower comes from behind and gets the Throw off Tower on HCGM. Tower goes for the pin on HCGM 1...2...3!!! It’s over Tower Hoard and Wayne Wynter advance to the next round of the tag-team tournament. Candi Bytch and Tower leave together. Well we are done with four matches already and only three to go. But first I have word that something is going on in the back. Let’s take a look at the CCW-Tron to see what’s happening... Candi and Tower are shown in Tower's locker room on a black leather couch drinking champagne. Candi takes off her jacket again and her skirt and gets Tower to lay down. She gets on top of him and starts to kiss him. Tower then starts to fiddle with her bra strap. Scene goes black. Uh...WOW!!! Are we allowed to show that on nation TV. That was sure some uh...um...Disturbing. That’s all I can say. I guess Tower is getting lucky both on the mat and in bed. Now I am getting a message saying that there is more backstage footage. I sure hope it isn’t Tower and Candi again. Let’s take a look... "The Warrior" Wayne Wynter and The Strangers are meeting outside the parking area. They have a brief conversation that is whispered and cannot be heard. Then the Strangers get in a car and drive away! Then Wayne turns to the camera and speaks..."CCW, I got a few words to say to you all.... F*CK YOU ALL, and f*ck that gay ass fake accent that I have been using. I have begun the revolution and you all will pay, NO one will escape my wrath. Nothing will be said until I say it, but expect hell to freeze over, VERY soon and I will own all souls! My army has begun and will take over.....NOW!!!!" Then Wynter walks away and the CCW-Tron shuts off. Well the Strangers and Wayne were meeting for some obvious reason but the camera couldn’t get close enough to hear the conversation. And guess what, Wayne has been using a FAKE accent! Who would’ve thought??? Well anyway let’s take you to the ring for COTR qualifying action! CHANG THE MONGOLIAN GIANT PINNED THE WISH MASTER – COTR QUALIFYING MATCH Wish Master is already in the ring and now here comes Chang the Mongolian Giant. Chang runs down the aisle like a crazed animal. I wouldn’t want to be the Wish Master right about now. Chang steps into the ring and they are ready to lock it up but... "Blind" by Korn erupts throughout the entire arena. Rushmore walks to the ring and takes a seat at the commentary table. He puts on a head set and begins to talk. "See this man they call a giant?" Rushmore points to Chang. "That man is puny compared to me, I would crush him with one hand". Are you sure about that Rushmore? They don’t call him the Mongolian giant for nothin’! And now Chang hits a belly to belly suplex and drops the big elbow on Wish Master. But the former world champion gets up and gives Chang a Cobra Clutch slam. "And look at this other guy, Wish Master, I remember that last match I had against him, I lost cuz o' them Franchise Punks!" "Neither of these two should even be in this COTR tourney". Well they have as good as chance that you do Rushmore. And look both men have been knocked down by a double chokeslam! How did they do that? "I mean, it's a waste of brackets to have losers in the tourney, watch this as I go and kick the living s--- out of these two punks!" Rushmore rips of his headset and walks toward the ring... Rushmore climbs in the ring and grabs both Chang and Wish Master by the back of their necks and delivers his trademark move to both of them at the same time, THE FACE LIFT! They both hit the mat face first. And now Rushmore picks up Wish Master above his head and gives him a MT MORE BOMB. Rushmore then pulls Chang's body over Wish Master's and the ref has no other choice then to count the pin as Rushmore leaves! The ref is making the count...1...2...3!!! Wish Master didn’t have a chance of kicking out of the Face Lift and the Mt. More Bomb! I wonder who would’ve won this brawl. It seemed like an even match until Rushmore decided to interfere. Well anyway let’s take you backstage and try to get an interview with someone. Let’s see what’s on the CCW-Tron... A CCW camera crew is backstage running around, trying to find someone. Jason McAllister is wandering around in the background. Camera Crew-"Hey Jason, can we get some words with you and so when are you going to debut?" Jason continues to walk around. The camera crew tries to follow, but Jason McAllister kicks in a door to a maintenance room. He climbs in and disappears into the service tunnels under the arena. We just saw Jason McAllister backstage again. When is that guy gonna debut? He has got to be a great wrestler. Just look at his size! He might even be able to take out Chang! The next match is the last COTR tournament match of the night. We will see Samson take on rookie John Jupiter. Let’s go to the ring now... SAMSON VS. JOHN JUPITER – COTR QUALIFYING MATCH Ring Announcer-"Now entering the ring, weighing in at 302 lbs., Samson! Here comes CCW’s self proclaimed strongest man. The fans are really on their feet, cheering for one of their favorite CCW superstars. Ring Announcer-"And now entering the ring, weighing in at 274 lbs., CCW rookie, John Jupiter!" I don’t know if this John Jupiter is such a smart guy. He is up against one of CCW’s best. And they lock up, with Samson getting the advantage early with an arm wrench. John is fighting it and he reverses it with an arm wrench of his own. Now Samson is struggling and he gets out of the hold and whips John into the ropes. John Jupiter comes back off the ropes with a flying forearm. Samson is down and John is taking advantage of the situation with repeated stomps to the head. John picks him up and delivers a DDT. Samson goes down hard but gets right back up. John is surprised of Samson’s strength and is trying to apologize! That won’t work here Jupiter, this is CCW!!! Samson is pissed off and he attacks John with a flurry of right and left punches. Jupiter staggers back and falls out of the ring. Samson is quick to follow and he lands a piledriver on Jupiter to the ground. John looks out of right now after that piledriver. Samson rolls John Jupiter back into the ring and hits an elbow drop. Samson is getting the fans pumped up, but look out! John goes for a clothesline. Samson sees it out of the corner of his eye and ducks. Samson caught John’s arm and now he’s got John locked in a full nelson. The fans are cheering for Samson to drop him on his head. And that’s exactly what Samson does with a big Full Nelson Slam. Samson goes for the pin 1...2...But, Samson pulled Jupiter up. Why did he do that, he had Jupiter beat. Wait a minute, I think I see someone coming out to ring... Sugar runs to the ring and slides in under the bottom ropes. Then she spins Samson around and gives him a BIG wet kiss. Samson pushes her away and then she bashes him across the face with her purse full of bricks. She kisses Jupiter and then leaves. Oh my, Sugar came down again and attacked someone with her special purse. John Jupiter has the advantage now and he could pull the big upset. John goes for the pin............1............2................. THREE!!! WAIT????? NO!!!! Samson had his foot on the ropes. The ref saw Samson’s foot on the ropes. This match continues! John Jupiter is arguing with ref now and Samson is up. Samson spins John around and locks him into the Samson’s Vengeance!!! John is trying to get out but it’s just too hard and he gives up! Samson is the winner by submission. Samson advances to the next round of COTR! But a very strong showing by the rookie, John Jupiter. And now we have something happening on CCW-Tron... He picks her up and they go into his locker room and Scott slams the door. He then opens the door and puts a "DO NOT DISTURB" sign on the doorknob. You hear Candi say "Oh Scotty!" and then the scene goes black. I thought Candi was with Rushmore??? I guess Candi is really a bitch not a bytch!!! But first, joining our Lock-Down announcer, Patrick McSmith, for guest commentary Andrew "Ace" Hawk!!!" "Born to be Wild" plays in the PA system, and Andrew "Ace" Hawk come down the aisle. Ring Announcer-"And now the first competitor coming to the ring, the CCW Champion, "The Primetime Player", "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades!!! "No-Leaf Clover" plays and Phil Burner and Supreme Machine are coming down to the ring together! They get in the ring and both get right into Blade’s face. Phil then takes a microphone and says...
