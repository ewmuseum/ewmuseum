Contract Expires: Not Signed Favorite Weapon: Small, black, steel, baseball bat, and the ring bell Debut: November 2001 Superstar Background: History: Dimitri was born in Newyork. At the age of 3 his parents moved to a small town down south. At the age of 5 he started having a growing interest in the gothic life style and a belief in the supernatural. As a kid at home his father and himself would watch wrestling on TV Growing up his favorite wrestler was the Undertaker and he wanted to go into wrestling just so he could meet his favorite superstar. At the age of 14 he started his staining in a local gym that trained wrestlers and worked with his father for a number of years. At 16 his father was killed in a robbery while at a local jewelry shop. When the case finally went to trial the killer was let go on a technicality and he grew distant from all people except his mother. He continued his wrestling training and dedicated his life to doing what he and his father had wanted him to do. later on in his career he came across the man responsible for his fathers death and there was an altercation between them. During the struggle the man was killed and Dimitri was arrested for murder. That's when Domina entered the picture. Domina was there when the fight started and informed the police that Dimitri was just defending himself. After spending a few months in jail for murder he went to trial and was found innocent of the charges. After his release he grew even more dark in his passion for the gothic life style and the supernatural. He went to work doing what he knows and that was wrestling. He worked in the Indies for a number of years with Domina as his manager. He also went into business with a man becoming his silent partner. He also started playing the stock market and was successful at it, however wrestling was always what he loved and it remained a stable for him. Finally he decided to join a national promotion and is currently waiting on a reply from the AWF, WCCW, WCW, EICW, and the HCW. He is currently waiting for replies from those feds and from the CCW.
