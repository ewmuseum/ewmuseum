Enigma immediately returns the favor to Douglas by locking in an Elbow Submission, incapacitating Douglas' right arm. Vinny checks for a submission... But Douglas refuses to concede the match. Forme Carlos leaps off the ring apron and dashes over to help his partner. However, Lacey intercepts him before he gets anywhere near Enigma and takes him down with a Short-arm Clothesline, much to the delight of the fans. Lacey teases him a bit with her tantalizing body as she picks him up off the ground and tosses him back toward his corner, ringing his head off the ring post.
