Zane Blackbeard Real Name: William Zane Barrymore Birthdate: 4/20/74 Hometown: The Seven Seas (he'll tell you Daytona Beach, Florida) Height: 6'6' Weight: 325 lbs. Nicknames: 'Zane' or 'First Mate' Status: Heel Manager(s): None, but often accompanied to ringside by Paula Hampton Current Teams: 'Irate Pirates' (w/ 'Pegleg' Paula Hampton) Entrance Music: 'Captain Hampton And The Midget Pirates' by The Aquabats Wrestling Style: Powerful. Really powerful. Signature Moves: See Below Set Up Move: Burial At Sea (See Below ) Finisher: Man Overboard (See Below ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: None Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: None Other Federations: None Other Federation Awards: Hah! Handler Information Handler Name: Bryan AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: N/A History Billy Barrymore was a failure. Like the preverbial King Midas in reverse, everything he ever touched turned to crap. He barely made it into Florida A&M University in Tallahassee, but flunked out after his first year. He was a fairly successful bodybuilder...until he tested positive for steroids. He was known as, quite possibly, the worst wrestler of all time, when he entered the field of professional wrestling. Chang The Mongolian Giant looked like a technical prodigy compared to his slow, uneventful offense. But then, he met a woman who would change his life. Beautiful. Talented. Charming. And totally nuts. 'Pegleg' Paula Hampton was a valuable commodity in the wrestling world. One of the most talented female wrestlers the world had ever seen...yet certifiably loony. CCW wanted to make a second investment in the pirate queen...but they needed someone to watch over her, almost full-time. And Billy Barrymore, desperate for work, applied for the job. Paula quickly changed Billy's name to Zane, which 'sounded a wee bit more piratey!' and gave him the infamous surname of 'Blackbeard' because...uh... He's black. At any rate, Zane has now entered the CCW...albeit with a bit of a disadvantage. He is the trusty first mate to Ms. Hampton...and becoming a solid wrestler in ring. Is he ready for success? Is he able to control the volatile pirate princess? Can he NOT screw up this opportunity like every other one in his life? Time will tell. Description Zane's a large, very dark-skinned African-American male. He's ripped, being a former pro bodybuilder and all. He's got a shaved head (usually covered by a red pirate bandana) and a neatly trimmed goatee. Gold hoop arrings. Brown eyes. His ring attire consists of a purple pirate vest, and brown baggy pants, in addition to regular wrestling boots (brown or black). Finisher Man Overboard - Inverted Chokeslam Zane grabs the victim by the throat from behind. Then, Zane lifts the victim up in air and then slams them down to the mat on their face. Irate Pirates Tag Team Finisher Raiding Party - Back Drop Driver to Shooting Star Inverted Facelock DDT Zane is standing behind the victim, his head under one of the victim's arms. Zane then waistlocks the victim and lifts them off the ground and falls backwards, dropping the victim on their head/neck. As Zane is falling backwards, Paula leaps from the top rope, does a backflip, and falls backwards, driving the victim down to the mat on the top of their head. Setup Move Burial At Sea - Second Rope Knee Face Driver Zane is standing on the second rope turnbuckle behind the victim. He grabs the victim's head and puts his right knee to the back of the victim's head. Zane then jumps off the turnbuckle and drives the victim into the mat face first. Signature Moves Port to Stern - Running Super Forward Rolling Fallaway Slam Zane lifts the victim up so they are laying stomach first across the his shoulders (a standing fireman's carry). He then runs, leaps, leans forward and slams the victim down and then rolls over them with his own weight. Tag Team Moves Tidal Wave - Spike Tiger Driver '91 Zane bends the victim down and hooks both the victim's arms. He then lifts the victim up so they are upside down and then sits down, dropping the victim on the their head. As he is dropping down, Paula pushes the poor victim down, delivering extra impact. Complete CCW Match History Tag Team Match: Zane Blackbeard & Roni Savar vs. Tristan Matthew & John Linsey (Lockdown 5/2/02) Winner: Undetermined as of yet. -Zane's CCW debut. A tag match...but without his partner, Paula. Backstage sources say it was booked so he could prove himself on his own. In other words, he's screwed.
