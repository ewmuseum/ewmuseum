CCW Lockdown card, 17 April 2003

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE! From the Pond in Anaheim, California! Thursday, April 17th! Coup d'etat. The single phrase caused a massive feud hit a climax since May of last year, as Douglas Robert Ellington took control of CCW once again. As well as the World Champion looking for an opponent, at least that problem will be solved after tonight.. Women's Tag Team Match Lacey Skye Morris & Chloe Mason vs. Devyl & Koriann Two teams who have similar goals. Is mutually assured destruction going to be part of this match? Also, once again, the cloud of Chaos is still whirling around Devyl...will "the Chosen" make an appearance? Tag Team Match Enigma & Blayze vs. Adam Christ & Jack Manson Commissioner Raymond threw his weight around making this match last week on Lockdown. However, with Spider review the Commissioner's performance, and the hatred that is in-between Enigma and Blayze...the odds look great for MisUnderstood in this match. Singles Match Dan Logan vs. Andrew 'Ace' Hawk Dan Logan FINALLY got his first win in CCW last week on Lockdown, however, the last time we saw Ace, it was destroying The Gobstopper, and Michael Stevens. How will Dan Logan, the rookie sensation fare against this former OSW superstar, and former CCW Champion? MAIN EVENT-#1 Contenders Battle Royale EVERYONE ON THE ROSTER Chairman Ellington provided us with an announcement last week stating that the number one contender will be chosen out of EVERYONE on the active roster. This means ANYONE can become the next #1 contender...who will it be? PLUS!!! -We expect to hear from The New CCW Heavyweight Champion, Jason Dread, despite Chairman Ellington's interruption last week. -Where the hell is Gabriel Nyte? Hopefully we'll find out this week. -A Performance Review of Commissioner Raymond by Chairman Ellington. That's gonna be a bloodbath. ALL COMING UP ON LOCKDOWN ON THE 17TH OF APRIL, DON'TCHA DARE MISS IT~!
