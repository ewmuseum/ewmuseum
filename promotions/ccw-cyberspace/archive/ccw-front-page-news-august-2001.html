All Rights Reserved. Copyright © 1998-2001 Cyberspace Championship Wrestling. All Rights Reserved. JUPITER WINS JOB BACK, LOOKS TO AVENGE BURNER It was more than a match to save their careers on SSN. Last night, before a sold out MGM Grande, John Jupiter and Jason McAllister fought through blood and pain to come to a mutual agreement. That agreement - stop CEO/President Burner from ruining CCW forever. Jupiter reluctantly made the pin, and he will carry on the fight. Jason McAllister, although his wrestling career is sadly over, let his heart show last night. We most certainly have not heard the last from him. To read last night's main event and the revealing commentary, click here. OUTLANDERS CARRY ON THROUGH A SLEW OF RIVALS Despite several backstage attacks last night, The Outlanders managed to once again flex their muscle and hold strong. CEO Burner made his presence felt when he indefinitely suspended The Vietnam Veteran, for reasons that only the CEO himself thought were necessary. The Super Unknown and Spider managed to eek out a win against Emminent Demise, who are said to be only fueled further by lack of recognition. TSU also became the new Lightweight Champion, thanks to some good non-cheating tactics. TVO TO WRESTLE BURNER? Don't get your hopes up just yet. Last night on SSN, The Valiant One answered an open challenge to wrestle Burner. However, the President, being the heelish chicken that he is, put an obstacle in his way. TVO must wrestle The Blades Brothers this Thursday on Lockdown in order to get his shot. He is allowed to have a tag partner for this match, but it doesn't matter because the Blades are going to win anyway. WHEN WILL WE SEE A DOMAIN NAME? SOON! Change your bookmarks, kids! In a short amount of time, Cyberspace Championship Wrestling will be moving to theccw.com! There's still a few kinks to be worked out of the system, but we'll be seeing CCW skyrocket to dot.com status in no time! August 12 Results August 16 Card The Renner Report The Bowe Valiant Thoughts
