CCW Lockdown card, 10 April 2003

10 April 2003 (announced card)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LIVE! From the Staples Center in Los Angeles, California! Thursday, April 10th! We saw everything happen at Revolution. Every title changed hands. Some upsets, and many favourites won their respective matches. This show is the aftermath of the Pay-Per-View, and it will have the following matches. Women's Match Lacey Skye Morris vs. Koriann One Jackass, losing a mud wrestling match...The Number One Contender, losing to Sheena Ramone for the Women's Title. These two want their receipt for Sunday Night, and they're gonna get it on each other! And I don't mean in a sexual sense! Triple Threat Match Dan Logan vs. Toxic vs. Damien Nhyte Dan Logan is tearing up the PTC Exhibition Scene...However, at home in CCW, he has gotten a single win yet. In this match he takes on two hungry young bucks chompin' at the bit for a win themselves...and to get noticed. CCW Hardcore Title Re-Match Enigma vs. Jack Manson These two superstars went ahead and damn near killed each other last night for the CCW Hardcore Title...Now, they have to go at it again? What the hell is the commissioner thinking?! I just hope that we, the arena, and the city of Los Angeles has insurance. Also, Will Adam Christ and Blayze play a role in this match against Enigma...A possible Three on One Situation here! Women's Match Devyl vs. Chloe Mason Fresh off her very impressive victory at Revolutions over "The Chosen" Gabriel Nyte, Devyl'll have to contend with up and comer Chloe Mason on Lockdown. Question is, will Gabriel take his revenge over the loss on Devyl on Thursday? Or will he wait to deliver his receipt? MAIN EVENT-Non-Title Match Michael Stevens vs. Adam Christ After the extreme beating that Christ and Manson dished out on Scorpion and Stevens at Revolution, The Extreme Team seems to be on cloud number 666. In a "Lite" rematch this coming Lockdown, Stevens will try to get his receipt over one half of the Hardcore Duo. PLUS!!! -We expect to hear from all the champions, especially The New CCW Heavyweight Champion, Jason Dread. AND -We will have a full injury report from the Pay-Per-View. ALL COMING UP ON LOCKDOWN ON THE 10TH OF APRIL, DON'TCHA DARE MISS IT~!
