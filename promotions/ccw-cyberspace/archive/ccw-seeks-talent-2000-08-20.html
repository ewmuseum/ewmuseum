CCW seeks talent...

20 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cyberspace Championship Wrestling is once again searching for potential members of our unique little e-fed. We've been online for almost two years and while our 40-Man roster is filled only half-way, we remain as one of the dominant forces in indy e-fedding. Our site shut down July 1, 2000 for rennovations, and re-opened this past week. Half of our talent left the fed in order to begin full-time jobs or to go off to college. CCW remains running strong, with a cool, crisp look and easy navigation. Roleplay your way to the top and claim one of CCW's seven championships, including a women's division! Pre-order WWF No Mercy as well to support our merchandising plan and help us make CCW the best there is. We're already well on our way, but we need new members in the Men's Division, Women's Division, and Tag Team Division. The second annual Heatstroke is this Sunday from Birmingham, Alabama, so click our link to come check us out! http://www.angelfire.com/tv/ccw360 - CCW President [name withheld]
