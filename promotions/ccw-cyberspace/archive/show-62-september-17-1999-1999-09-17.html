Show #62 - September 17, 1999

What this is Staged Friday Fury results script for CCW show #62 from Bartlett, with Lightweight and Hardcore title matches, handicap and cage matches, and a returning character storyline.
September 17, 1999 from The Crazy House in Bartlett, Tennessee - Show #62 Sam Frederick vs. Apocalypse - CCW Lightweight Championship Chang The Mongolian Giant vs. "Fat Ass" Phil Burner - CCW Hardcore Championship Crazy Train vs. Steve Punjab - Handicap Match Niamouchu vs. Scotty The Hotty - Steel Cage Match J.C. Superstar vs. Big Hoss "Hardcore" Jaysin Urban vs. Carnage D-Stroy vs. X-Port Nightshade vs. "The Avenger" Tim Inman, Supreme Machine, Thunder & Chang The Mongolian Giant - Handicap Match Friday Fury Results - September 17, 1999 Friday, 17-Sep-1999 23:27:28 [detail withheld] writes: Welcome to the Crazy House! Good Evening everyone! This is good ol Jerry Rossendale coming at ya from the newly completed Crazy House here in Bartlett, TN! Tonight we have a highly packed house as we get set to see one of the most action packed Friday Fury's in recent history. As many of you may know, Brad and Johnny Lamen are back in CCW! Also, with the return of Samson and Steve Punjab already into affect, expect some heated rivalries continue on and some new ones to warm up! Well ladies and gentlemen we now have scheduled an interview I believe sponsored by the Porker's Champion. I know everyone is anxious to here what he's up to. **Sweet Home Alabama. Phil walks down the entranceway with a shovel, takes all the cheers from his adoring fans and goes to the ring to speak.** "Y'know, I been havin' some problems as ev'ryone knows with my former manager, Tim Inman. He's been talkin' 'bout...well kind of a touchy subject....my dad. Now if ya don't know much about him he's an honest-to-God hardworking man. Yeah, he always resented me cause I weren't as smart or as talented as Bob was growin' up. Hell, I weren't the favorite and I was the oldest, so I guess that says a lot. But I love my dad, I will kick the skinny little ass of Tim Inman nex.... **"Avengaliciamaniacs Theme." The entire stable, from the midgets to the monkeys to the great Konngo fall in a procession unmatched by anyone in the CCW. First the leader, then El Mierda and Konngo(the real talent of the group), then Carlos and Tildo. They all crowd around Phil in the ring. Phil does not move but looks intimidated and says nothing, he just stares into the eyes of Inman.** Tim Inman: I've got one question for you Phil....*laughs abruptly). How in the hell do you plan to kill me with all these people around ready to do anything I say? A Mexican that kills, a midget that wrestles, a highly intelligent primate, and the greatest Trans-Atlantic Champion of all time. That's what I have that you don't Phil, friends. And another thing you don't have is a dad. The one you did have was gay and stupid. Hell, Bob and Kenny were the only good ones to come out of that family. And now, with the snap of my fingers, my entire stable of Avengaliciapostles will beat you into a bloody pulp. **Carlos the Mexican Midget shouts something** Tim Inman: What was that?.....Yeah....sure, you can bite at his testicles... Now, when I snap my fingers, Phil, you will feel first hand my power. **Suddenly the lights turn out and the countdown to the Tubbellenium flashes on the CCW-Tron. 5, 4, 3, 2, 1... "2001 plays and flames burst. From the entranceway, an old man with a pencil-thin mustache and overalls stands at the entranceway. He stands there and Tim Inman looks with awe and fear at his former partner. Phil grabs the mic from a CCW staff member. As the camera takes a look at his rear, in red letters "BCB" is made out. He is an enormous man, even for his age.** "Two Ton" Phil Sr.: First of all get your little goons off my son. **even more cheers**. Second, NEVER insinuate that my son is a failure. Third, I'm not gay and I will kick your ass....OOH yes, that time will come. Inman: Noo...noo... You can't come back to wrestling. This is my league now Ton! You....it was you the whole time... Two Ton: That's right, Inman. And this ain't the USWA no more. Y2Ton is takin' over because he can. Fat Ass: That's right, now get yer asses right on out that door over there! **Fans cheer hugely as Inman and the rest of the Avengaliciamaniacs leave through the crowd, for fear of Father Burner...** I can't belive what just transpired right here in Bartlett! Phil Sr. has returned to the wrestling world and has actually scared off Tim Inman! Folks, The Avengaliciamaniacs extended a brutal attack backstage earlier today. When Supreme Machine was walking to the arena, out of nowwhere a Lincoln Continental slammed into the side of Supreme Machine, sending him to the ground. Then Inman took the title belt in his bag and says "this should be mine!" and smashes him on the head with it repeatedly. Just a few minutes ago, while Apoclypse and Kaboink were in their locker room the lights went out and The Avengaliciamaniacs bust through the wooden walls with chain saws and carry tire irons. After hitting them both for a while they decided to subsidize the violent aggressions. Before leaving, Inman stapled Kaboink's hand to the wall and spraypainted "BCB" on the wall in red ink. So much action already without our first match even beginning! Look for some big happenings tonight in CCW! Nightshade vs Avengalicimaniacs, SuMa, Thunder, and Chang The Mongolian Giant *Nightshade's music begins playing* *The stadium darkens and dark music begin playing. Red pyrotecnics explode from the four ring posts. They outline the form of Nightshade. The lights suddenly come to full power as he raises his hand up and suddenly jumps and when landing brings his hand to his side.* There's really nothing I can say about this newcomer except that he's made many "friends" since being brought into this great organization. *The Contender begins playing* Now for Nightshade's many opponents! Looks like SuMa, Thunder, and Chang are coming to the ring now. And well, I have just received word that The Avengaliciamaniacs are not going to be taking part in this match. Looks as if they have stood SuMa and company up, and after what happened earlier in the parking lot, I don't see why SuMa would even want them around! Here's the bell and the three former Franchisers are going to work on Nightshade! The man doesn't stand a chance, but the Commissioner of CCW took him up on a challenge and gave him "ALL", just as he asked for. Thunder with a whip to the ropes on Nightshade and SuMa & Chang deliver a double face buster off the fly! Thunder drops an elbow while Chang grabs a hold of NShades foot and twists it! NShade is screaming out in utter pain! *The lights go out, then a huge explosion at the entranceway. "Kashmir" begins playing* Here comes Steve Punjab and he's bringing some wooden company in the form of a 2x4! What is that? That, that looks like the remote control for Chang's electronic leash! SuMa must have dropped it backstage when he was rammed by Inman and friends! This could prove disasterous for Team Advantage! Punjab has activated the leash and Chang is going nuts! SuMa to the outside to stop Punjab but he's met with a 2x4 on his way through the ropes and SuMa is out cold on the floor! Chang in the ring, he's... he's going crazy! He's mauled Thunder and Nightshade! I can't believe it! The real beast has been unleashed thanks to Punjab! Wait! Punjab is entering the ring!? He better be careful or he's going down as well! Look out! Here comes Chang... OooooH! Punjab nails Chang with the 2x4 and it made the Giant stagger! Another hit takes him down to one knee!!! A third shot knocks him out cold! Punjab, going upstairs now... SLAM! The 2x4 comes down across the chest of a befallen Chang! Over to Nightshade now, Punjab is dragging his unconscious body over to Chang of all people! The referee, who cannot call for a DQ in this match, goes down for the slow count... 1..... 2..... 3! Unbelievable! Nightshade has survived the odds and defeated 3 men in one match! And pinned Chang! This is unpresedented, but you know, there is a feeling of depression when you think about how this was won. Nightshade owes Steve Punjab for saving his hide, but how about allowing Nightshade to a fair match? One match down and seven more to go! Surely you can bet that something interesting will result from tonight's action! D-Stroy vs X-Port Folks, we have some interesting footage of another parkinglot incident that happened several minutes ago. D-Stroy pulls into the parking lot in his 1982 Ford Escort Hatchback. Out of nowhere JC Superstar's Hummer cuts him off sending him into a concrete column. JC throws the dazed D-Stroy out of the car, pulls out a previously unseen baseball bat, and takes huge uppercuts ala Mark McGwire into the side of his head and into his ribs. D-Stroy begins to cough up blood, signs of a few broken ribs. JC: So you think you're "the man"? Quit crying like a little bitch. Ya see D-Stroy it's like this, I don't take kindly to f---ers like you talkin s--- to me. It makes me quite angry and you see what happens when you do that. **JC rears back with his foot and connects with a CRACK to D-Stroy's jaw. He then heads to his private dressing room to prepare for his match.** The newbies can't get a break in this business, can they? *D-Stroy's music begins playing* Here comes the unfortunate newcomer. He's looking rather beat up but it doesn't appear that any major damage has been done to his body, so that rules out any broken ribs. *X-Port's music starts* Entering the arena now is Sean Wal... I mean, X-Port! Nothing has been heard for a while from him, or his partner in crime, BBB. There's the ding and this one is underway! X-Port with the advantage and going behind D-Stroy with a hammerlock. Stroy struggles but to no avail as X-Port pushes him forward and then delivers a dropkick to his back, sending him face first into a turnbuckle... Smashing! XP now, bringing him up and DOWN with beautifully executed suplex. Stomping away is Xport. He's got him by his feet and something painful may be up next! OH! Not what I thought, but equally painful! There's the Texas Cloverleaf and this one is over as D-Stroy taps out in his debut match! X-Port relinquishes the hold as his hand is raised in victory! Too bad for D-Stroy, I seriously do think he has the makings to be big in CCW. With more heart and more effort, look for him to move up in the rankings next month. Tough break tonight, rookie. "Hardcore" Jaysin Urban vs Carnage *Urban's music begins playing* Well, lookie here! Jaysin Urban, after an unexplained absence, is back in the swing of things tonight as he gets set to wear off his ring rust. *Carnage's music begins playing* Although this guy is relatively new to CCW, he's made quite the impact on the boards as of late. He needs to put more UMPF into his career if he wants to make it into the spotlight. The bell sounds and here we go! Urban circling while Carnage backs down with caution. Urban lunges forward but Carnage side-steps and Urban falls to his knees. Carnage with a kick to the ribs of Urban. Another one sends Urban to his back. Carnage putting Urban in the arm scissors now and really wrenching that arm, look at the pain on Urban's face! The referee getting more involved now and breaking the hold as Urban reaches the ropes. The ref backs Carnage away as Urban climbs to his feet, but Carnage made no mistake on the back-off and takes Urban down with a short-arm clothesline. Sending Urban off the ropes and then stops him with a boot to the stomach, bends him over, lifts him up and POWERBOMB! 1-2-3! Carnage is victorious here tonight, downing ring rusty Jaysin Urban! A big win here tonight over the former Hardcore Champ. J.C. Superstar vs The Big Hoss *Big Hoss's music is heard over the PA...the crowd responds with taunts and boos* And here comes Big Hoss, one of the most recent additions to the CCW. If ya ask me, I don't think there's enough room... And here comes JC Superstar from behind! He takes Hoss out with a monstrous clothesline to the back of his head! It's a total debacle! And the ref calls for the bell and this match is underway. JC going to work on the big man with a flury of punches to the head and midsection. I don't know how much more the big man can take. JC is slowly leading Hoss to the ring, continuing to work on the midsection, no doubt trying to take the wind out him. Hoss is doubled over...OH NO! JC has picked up the steel steps from ringside and smashed the big man across the back. **Big Hoss is heard screaming in obvious discomfort from the viscious attack by the Superstar** And JC has thrown Hoss into the ring. He can't get to his feet. JC up on the top rope...Oh my GOD, he connects with a huge guillotine leg drop from the top rope! JC taking his time now and signaling to the crowd that it's time for the Holy Spirit Bomb, I don't know how smart a move this is, the sheer weight must be unbelievable...and JC moves Hoss into position and OH a reversal! Hoss has reversed the move into a back body drop! Hoss, down to one knee, slowly gets back up while JC is holding his lower back in slight pain as he stumbles to his feet. He's met with a Big Hoss Clothesline and taken down! Hoss now, with a leg drop and OH MY GOD! Down across the chest! Hoss back up now and off the ropes, NO NO NO! YES! A big hefty standing splash on JC! The cover now and 1-2-3! Here is the winner, Mr. Big Hoss! JC Superstar has lost to Hoss! The big man is 2-0 to start his CCW career while JC Superstar slowly plummets from the Wrestler Of The Month competition, but remains at the top of the Contenders list. Niamouchu vs Scotty The Hotty - Steel Cage Match *Niamouchu's music begins playing* Just as his counterpart, Niamouchu hasn't been around in quite some time. Expect some ring rust from this competitor as well tonight. *Scotty's music begins* Scotty The Hotty, for some reason, thinks he is deserving of a title shot. Now, with only one win in the win column, I personally don't think he's deserving. Perhaps a defeat of the former Trans-Atlantic Champ will help boost the popularity of this guy. Why does he always come out from under the ring? The two men are in the ring as the cage is being lowered. Niamouchu doing his little religious ceremonies in the corner but the bell has sounded and Scotty is going to work. Scotty with Niamouchu by the head and SLAMS him face first into the steel cage! He brings Niamouchu to his feet only to knock him off again with a implanting DDT! Scotty now, off the ropes and with an elbow drop and Niamouchu rolls out of the way! Niamouchu to his feet and delivers a karate kick to the throat area of Scotty! Niamouchu, off the ropes and a flying body press taking both men down but not out! Niamouchu back up and he hops to the ropes and flips in mid-air, landing a somersault! On his feet now, Niamouchu is attempting to escape the cage! He's almost to the top but Scotty is back to his feet and grabs Niamouchu by his feet! He fell! 20 feet fall and NIamouchu is down and out! Scotty climbing the cage now and he's over the top! Down, down, down he goes and the floor is reached and touched by toes! The winner of the cage match, Scotty The Hotty! Wait, he's going back in the cage! He isn't through with Niamouchu! Look, a microphone is being lowered from the rafters and Scotty has it! He's got it lowered to Niamouchu's body and has wrapped it and tied it around the body of Niamouchu! He's being raised and Scotty is beating the crap out of him. Now that the beating is over, he's removing the mic from Niamouchu and has placed him in the North Dakota Cloverleaf! He's not letting go! Finally, he's released the hold and is on his way back to the locker room. Crazy Train vs Steve Punjab - Handicap Match *Crazy Train begins playing* Here comes the Crazy Train! All aboard!!! Kaboink, the Independent Champion, and Apocalypse are making their way to the ring but someone is sneaking up behind them! He's wearing a mask so I can't really make out who it is! But he's carrying what appears to be a straight six engine block from a 1969 Pontiac Tempest! What the hell? He's slammed it against Kaboink, and now Apocalypse! He's taken off backstage and Steve Punjab is out now, taking both Crazy Train members into the ring. But Apocalypse is back to his senses and is going toe to toe with Punjab on the outside of the ring. Here comes Big Ass Bob Burner! He appears to be on the side of Punjab and is now in the ring going to work on Kaboink! Meanwhile, Apocalypse and Steve Punjab are slamming each other silly on the outside! But on the inside, Burner has Kaboink down and it's the Big Ass Ass Bomb! The ref counts Burner as a legal part of the match and makes the count... 1-2-NO! Apocalypse has grabbed the foot of Burner and pulled him to the outside! Punjab slides in the ring to go after Kaboink as Burner is hammered by Apocalypse and then tossed into the crowd. Punjab has Kaboink and it's the Arabian Night... No wait! Kaboink has reversed it and has got Punjab in the Kablooie! BAM! 1-2-3! Kaboink has pinned Steve Punjab! The Crazy Train is victorious here tonight! Chang The Mongolian Giant vs "Fat Ass" Phil Burner - CCW Hardcore Championship *The Contender begins playing* Chang is making his way out now. After what he did earlier in the night, I'm not sure if Fat Ass is gonna be safe! "Sweet Home Alabama" plays over the loud speakers. Here comes the Porker's Champion! Let's see if he has what it takes to bring back the Hardcore Title away from this "machine". He's the only one even close to Chang's weight, but Chang still outweighs him by 50 pounds! Phil is going to have to have some tricks up his sleeve to get this killer down! Phil enters the ring, and the bell sounds! Chang lunges at Phil, but he gets out of the way! Chang is looking around, and Phil goes downstairs with a low blow! There's the great equalizer for you! Chang is staggering but mostly just pissed rather than hurt. Chang grabs him and puts him in massive bearhug! I've never seen Phil Burner manhandled like that before in my life! Wait, what's this? It's Phil Sr! Two Ton has come out, and he's got a shovel! He dives into the ring and bashes it across the back of the head of Chang! Chang drops Phil and grabs Two Ton! He's got him up for a Gorilla Press Slam! But Fat Ass is back up, he takes the shovel and whacks him in the back of the knees, making him hobble, and he drops Two Ton, but Two Ton does a Diamond Cutter on the way down! Oh crap! Chang is out! Phil goes for the cover, 1-2-NO!!!!! The first ever pin attempt on Chang is unsuccessful! Phil Sr. takes care of SuMa and company outside of the ring, keeping them at bay with the shovel. Phil Jr. kicks at Chang, but they don't phaze him, he's getting up! Chang towering over Phil, and Phil takes off! Chang gives chase backstage! Phil tries throwing things in the way to slow him down, but they don't hurt the monster! They're outside in the parking lot! Phil's running across the busy intersection! And Chang's not stopping! Oh GOD somebody's going to get hurt! Let's check back in the ring: Phil Sr. is showing that he knows how to use that shovel! Down on the face of SuMa! Everyone else backs away as Phil Sr. keeps them from following Fat Ass and Chang! Where are they now? They are at the high school! Oh man there's people here! Phil jumps the fence to the football game, and Chang just busts through it! They can't do this, there's a football game going on! Phil rushes out onto the field, and the referees call for the whistle and try to stop him, but here comes Chang and everyone gets out of the way! Phil is on the sideline, throwing footballs at Chang, doinking him in the head and Chang is swatting them away like flies! Phil, oh gosh I can't believe it! He's in the stands, headed up to the press box! Chang parts the shrieking crowd and follows him. Phil is inside the tiny press box and is climbing the ladder up to the top! Chang can barely fit through the door but he does, and now both are on the roof of the press box! Phil's got no place left to go! He hits Chang with lefts and rights, but to no avail! Chang sneering, lifts his arms in the air and....the wooden floor is creaking! Chang looks at Phil shockingly and goes crashing down through the press box and to the bottom of the stands! That's at least 40 feet! Phil climbs down from the press box and the stands, trying to find Chang but he's disappeared! Looks like he's got an idea, and he goes off! But where's Chang? It looks like the mess is over so the game resumes. A pass play, but the defender in the way is suddenly Chang?! Chang roars and everyone runs away! Chang is running towards the cheerleaders! Heaven help them! HOLY SH*T!!!! Where'd that bus come from?! Ladies and gentlemen, Chang has just been run over by a school bus! Who's the maniac driving that thing?! The door opens, it's "Fat Ass" Phil Burner!!!! Phil motions to the crowd, it's time for the Porker's Elbow! He does it, but nobody knows what's going on! There's some fans cheering! He makes the cover, and the referee is there! 1-2-3!! Phil Burner wins! Phil Burner has just beaten Chang the Mongolian Giant! What a remarkable feat! And more importantly, Phil Burner has just regained the Hardcore Title! What a match! Phil Sr. comes out to congratulate him, and they walk back to the Crazy House. SuMa and company check on Chang, who not too surprisingly isn't hurt that badly. Let's here it for the Porker's Champion! Sam Frederick vs Apocalypse - CCW Lightweight Championship *Crazy Train begins playing* Here comes Apocalypse! He's looking to regain his Lightweight Title tonight, but some may wonder if Apoc is in good health following a tag team match a little while ago. *Sam Frederick's music begins* The Lightweight Champ is making his way out now! Apocalypse attacks Frederick as soon as he entered the ring! Apoc with lefts and rights as Sam fights back with left and rights of his own! Apoc lunging forward with a fierce attack but Frederick blocks it and it's the Last Call! 1-2-3!!! Frederick wins the match and thus retains the Lightweight Title! A surprise victory for Sam Frederick!!! Folks, it's be very entertaining bringing you Friday Fury tonight! Tune in Sunday for Shockin Sunday Nite, Good Night all! [name withheld] CCW Home
