Job Openings Interested with a job in Cyberspace Championship Wrestling as a wrestler? This form below is your ticket to qualifying for a position in the company. Be sure to fill it out completely and click send only once, please. Any sections that go unanswered could affect your chance of receiving a position. Approximate time to complete: 20 minutes Currect Status: Currently We Are Accepting Applications. Handler Information Your Name Your Age Email Address AIM Handle MSN Contact Character Information* Character Alias Gender Male Female Wrestling Style All Arounder High Flyer Brawler Powerhouse Big Man Woman Martial Arts Technician Amateur Other(Specify) Other(Specify) Special Characteristics Special Character Information: His gimmick, vital statistics(height/weight), and anything else that may help us understand the character himself. Job Qualifications E-Wrestling Experience (In Years) Previous Jobs Include where you have worked along with positions and addresses to these promotions. Recommendations Recommendations for Work from previous employer(s). Include their names and contact information. Sample of Work Sample work with character: Whether written in or linked with proof to show they are yours. Important in being considered. Why Should We Hire you? Why do you want to work in CCW and why should we give you a job?(500 words or less) * Fill out only if you're applying to potentially use your own character. Keep in mind you may or may not get this character. But it will give the staff a general idea of what kind of character you have worked with or want to work with. Before clicking on the "Submit" button below make sure you have filled in every field thoroughly enough so that there will be no questions directed towards you by the staff. Also, be sure you have read the Rules, FAQs and understand what Version Two is all about. Once you click "Submit" you have recognized that you understand all the rules and will adhere to them.
