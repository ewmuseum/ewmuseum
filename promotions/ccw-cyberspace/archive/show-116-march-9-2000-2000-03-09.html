Show #116 - March 9, 2000

What this is Card and staged script of the March 9, 2000 show from Sacramento, with the president’s contract announcement and the Independent, Hardcore and Tag Team title matches.
March 9, 2000 from The Arco Arena in Sacramento, California - Show #116 The Disciples vs. The Untouchables - CCW Tag Team Championship Carnage vs. Corey James - CCW Hardcore Championship "Fat Ass" Phil Burner, Samson, Sid Powers vs. Hardcore Gorilla Mask, The Mimic, The Wish Master Kelly Bowe vs. Savannah Hilton "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades vs. Impact vs. Diablo - Triple Threat Ladder Match - CCW Independent Championship Sugar "The Superior Slut" vs. Delilah Pepper The Cat vs. Rushmore The Camargo Kid vs. Sexy Boy Heenan: "Ladies and Gentlemen, let there be PYRO!!!!" "Sparks are flying here tonight, we have some great match-ups tonight, the Independent Title is on the line in a three way ladder match, and the Hardcore Title is also going up." " Another great match is the Tag Title match, lets see what’s up in the ring..." "Welcome to The Arco Arena in Sacramento, California" **Dirty Deeds plays, Bobby Tom leads the way down to the ring, followed by the American flag-clad Tim Inman, who holds the heavyweight title over his shoulder. The two men enter the ring. Heenan: "How's it going, Tim? Long time, no see. Inman: "Listen up Larry, I want you to stop talking to me as if you are my damn equal or something!" "Just shut up and let your new champion speak his damn mind!" Heenan: "okay, how about you speak your mind first about your rematch with Phil Burner this Sunday." "It's gonna be real hard for you to cheat your way out of a closed cage match!" Inman: "Shut up!!!" "I told you to shut your trap and let me speak my mind!" "I'm the champion for gods sake." "But, luckily for you, I was about to speak my mind on that very subject." "First of all, I had no idea that El Mierda was going to run-in like that." " I was never told of it and to my knowledge, El Mierda was gone from CCW forever." **Fans jeer???** Inman: "It's true, really it is!" **Bobby Tom shakes his head in concurrence.** Inman: "Anyway, now that we've got that out of the way, it seems as if your CCW champion has a little match coming up this spring break weekend against none other than my contemporary, the "THE PORKERS." "Weeeeeeeeeeellllllllll, you've got another thing coming if you think that for one second, that I will not have retained this belt into next week." "I beat Phil last week, and I’ll beat him again next week...or die trying!!" "But the thing is this, El Mierda will have no part in that match, because that lil' hairy Mexican is not in allegiance with your truly." Heenan: "You've got to be kidding me." Inman: "No, he's not, Larry, and SHUT up! I won this belt for my right to talk, SO LET ME!!" " I am not in allegiance with El Mierda, nor do I plan to be." "But.....but, but I am in allegiance with another deadly hell in CCW." "But you'll just have to watch Sunday to see for yourself." " After all you don't know what to expect the THREE,....count it.....THREE time CCW Heavyweight champion." "I'll see you ass holes Sunday, bye!" **Crowd jeers and Inman followed by Bobby Tom exit the arena.** Heenan: "Well, that was really.....no comment, here comes the president..." ** "No chance in Hell," plays as the president makes his way out from the entrance way and walks to the ring where he takes a mic from the ring announcer and makes his statement.** Stevens: "Ladies and Gentlemen, as many of you may know, El Mierda has decided to bring himself back to CCW by helping Inman win the CCW title this past Sunday in Las Angeles." **Fans boo.** Stevens: "I have received letters, not only from fans of Mierda's, but from Mierda and his legal team." "You may well know I had Mierda arrested for trespassing in the Staples Center this past Sunday, and the very next day I received a phone call from Mierda's lawyer, Dick O. Phibb, Attorney At Law." "It seems that my banning of Mierda was illegal in sense that Mierda was on contract with CCW and had just signed an extension to that very contract just days before he was banned by me." "You may ask well, Mr. President, can't you ban people keep breaking your rules?" "The answer to that is yes." "But in the case of El Mierda, the contract extension DID state the following....." ".....that during the length of this contractual obligation, CCW cannot release this employee based on verbal actions within the walls of an official Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Arena, office, or building without the majority vote from the entire CCW staff, including executive personnel and employees working for or within the CCW, including wrestlers and managers." "As you can see, Mierda's actions were that.....VERBAL an entire CCW vote was not determined, therefore, I am announcing the re-signing of El Mierda effective immediately." "However, this contract I am offering him does not have the FOREMENTIONED "fine print" which means Mierda will be under a two month probation period and will be released if he acts up once again and threatens any CCW executive staff member's well being." "Thank you for your time!" ** "No chance in Hell" plays as President Stevens leaves the ring, and the fans chanting, "Mierda SUCKS!"** Heenan: "Well, we just heard from President Stevens on El Mierda's status in CCW, and now....." **The CCWTron flickers to life as Bobby Blades is seen backstage carrying a large ladder around with him.....** Heenan: "What is this, what is Bobby Blades doing with.....oh my god he just nailed Diablo in the back of the head with that thing....." "I guess the Independent champ doesn't want to wait until the match...." **Bobby Blades wedges Diablo's knee in between the rungs of the ladder and walks over to a PowerAde machine that is elevated on a fork lift....** Heenan: "Good GOD, that's insane, don't do that...." **Bobby tilts the PowerAde machine off the lift and drops it onto the ladder and Diablo screams in pain under the weight of the machine!** (Bobby Blades: "Good luck trying to learn how to walk again...ha, ha, ha....") **The CCWTron flickers off as Bobby walks away!!** Heenan: "Wow, what a brutal attack, hopefully Diablo can make it to his match later tonight." "Maybe now we can get to the first match...oh wait the CCWTron is on again......" **Backstage, The Kid is getting ready for his next match against Sexy Boy....the Kid is putting something in his bag, but wait there's Sexy Boy, he's got a sledgehammer and he's sneaking up behind The Kid and....oh no he just nailed him in the back of the knee-cap with that thing! The Kid falls in pain and begins yelling at Sexy Boy, so Sexy hits him in the knee again... Sexy Boy: "You are the first victim of my new DARK side...." **Sexy Boy leaves the room laughing hysterically!!** Heenan: "Wow, another attack, well let's see if The Kid Makes it down to the ring in this scheduled match up...." Sexy Boy vs. The Kid Ring Announcer: "The following match up is scheduled for one fall, coming to the ring first......Sexy Boy....." **Sexy Boy's music plays...** Ring Announcer: "And coming down second.........Camargo Kid......" **The Kid's music blares out......** **The Kid Comes down in a wheel chair holding a mic...** The Kid: "Sexy Boy, I’m sorry to have to say this, but I have to forfeit this match due to a severe beat down by my own stable mates......." **The CCWTron comes on and there is a movie clip,......The whole franchise is beating the living crap out of The Kid.....** As Sexy boy watches in amazement The Kid is sneaking down to the ring.... **Suddenly Sylkk (on the CCWTron) turns the cam and says, "Sexy, you'll fall for any thing won't you.....** Before Sexy Has time to re-act he is curled from behind by The Kid...1-2....a kick out, the bell rung and the match is on its way!!! The two men lock up and Sexy goes for Kid's knee, but The Kid jumps and gives Sexy a hurricarana. Now he is on top of Sexy pounding him in the face. Sexy shrugs The Kid off, but as he gets up the kid DDT's him!!! The Kid is going up top....a knee drop, and...a miss, his knee is hurting now! Sexy lifts him up and a piledriver!! The Kid is in pain now.....Sexy is going for another piledriver, and he connects!!! Sexy goes for the cover...1-2-....The Kid grabs a hold of the ropes...Sexy lifts Kid up, an irish whip to the corner...but it's reversed....Sexy hits the turnbuckle and bounces off into the hands of a DDT by The Kid ....now the Kid puts on a figure-four leg lock....Sexy is trying to escape...but he can't he's tapping!!!!!! **The kids music blast out!!** Heenan: "Here's your winner.....THE KID, by submission!! ** "No Leaf Clover" blares out, here comes Fat Ass Phil Burner....** **Phil is carrying a 10 gauge shot gun, he fires a shot into the air and the crowd falls silent...he grabs a mic....** Fat Ass: "Come on down Timmy!" "This is something I should've done a looooong time ago..." ** Fat Ass waits....but NO Inman so he runs out through the entrance in rage, reloading his gun and saying..." guess I have to get it myself!!!"** Heenan: "Oh my god, I hope he doesn't use that thing, some one get security please!!" "Okay, well we will proceed to our next match....Pepper The Cat vs. Rushmore...." Pepper The Cat vs. Rushmore Ring Announcer: "The following Match is scheduled for one fall....coming first to the ring, Pepper The Cat..." ** Peppers music comes on and he walks to the ring!!** Ring Announcer: "And his challenger...Rushmore....." **Rushmore's new theme music, "Blind" by KoRn plays as he come to the ring holding a huge plate of food in one hand and a keg of beer in the other......suddenly from behind Jim Klown Bowe nails him with the TA title and drags him to the ring....** Jim bashes Rushmore's head off the steel stairs and then rolls him into the ring.... **Here comes Kelly Bowe wearing lingerie, she's tosses Jolly Ranchers to the crowd and then joins Jim at ring side!!** The bell rings and Pepper kicks Rushmore in the stomach, it doesn't effect him and he stands up. Pepper swings at Rushmore with a right hook, but it is blocked and Rushmore grabs Pepper by the throat and a huge chokeslam onto the mat!! Then Rushmore climbs out of the ring and runs at his food....he begins pigging out when Pepper drop kicks him from behind. He falls over and lands in mashed potatoes! Pepper grabs The keg of beer and tries to lift it, but it's to heavy for him and he falls over with the keg on top of him!!! the ref is busy in the ring counting...1-2-3-4-5-6-7... Rushmore is still eating and Pepper is trapped,.....8-9-10, ding-ding-ding.....double count out!! Heenan: "This match results in.....a double count out!!! Delilah vs. Sugar The Superior Slut Heenan: "Next match is gonna be good, here comes Delilah now..." **Delilah's music plays and she comes down** **Sugar's music plays as she runs to the ring** Delilah slaps Sugar. Sugar slaps Delilah. Delilah goes for another slap but it is blocked and Sugar punches her in the nose. Sugar grabs Delilah's hair and slams her face into the mat! Then Sugar smashes Delilah’s face into the turnbuckle! She attempts for another when Delilah reverses it and slams Sugars face into the buckle! Now Delilah's pissed, she grabs Sugar and a DDT. She goes to the top rope, a splash, connects, Sugar is hurting, Delilah pounces on her and slaps her!! Sugar pushes Delilah off and gets up. Sugar grabs Delilah and gives her a big kiss!!!! Delilah pushes her away and then powerbombs her on the mat!! a cover, 1-2-3........your winner Delilah!!!! **Delilah’s music plays!!!** Heenan: "The winner by pin fall...........Delilah!!!!" ** A camera comes on backstage, there is Phil Burner is with his gun. He is in front of Inman's dressing room, he blows the door handle off and bursts in. There is Inman, Phil presses the gun to Inman to his throat and pushes him against the wall.** Burner: "Inman, you sonofabitch, I kin see that the only way I’m gonna beat you is t' threaten yer life, cuz I know that thet's what you value most is you!" "Gimme back the belt!" ** Inman points to a gym bag on floor, and Phil, with the gun still pointed at Inman, opens it and grabs the belt. Then he clonks Inman in the head and he falls unconscious!!!** ** As Burner passes through a parking garage he tosses the gun into a garbage truck and it is destroyed by the compactor!!** Mr. Frosty "Bobby Blades" vs. Diablo vs. Impact.....3 way ladder match!!!!! Heenan: "Wow, I’ve never seen a 3-way ladder match, let's see what happens!!!!!" **Diablo's music plays and he comes to the ring!** **Impact's music blares and he runs to the ring, standing next to Diablo!** **The CCWTron flickers for a moment before suddenly the lights and "Whatever" by GODSMACK begins to blare over the PA as the CCW INDEPENDENT CHAMPION "Mr. Frosty" Bobby Blades makes his way down to the ring.....a bloodied Canadian flag and his belt over one shoulder and a home depot deluxe stepladder over the other.....Bobby is immediately rushed by Diablo and Impact.** Bobby Blades, jumps of the canvas and slings the ladder off the ropes like a seesaw and it catches Diablo and Impact both under their chins and they fall on their backs! Heenan: "Your winner.......................Savannah! by count out!!!!!!!!! Heenan: "The next match is a three way tag match..." Fat Ass Phil Burner, Samson, and Sid Powers vs. Wishmaster, Mimic, and HCGM!!!!!! **WAI enters, Sid is wearing his "Showtime" shirt that says "It's show time" on the front, and "Got Power?" on the back..
