Jason Dread Real Name: Seth Cantrell Birthdate: 6/23/1979 Hometown: Southern Tracks, Arizona Height: 5'11 Weight: 223 Nicknames: Status: Face Entrance Music: 'Just Got Wicked' - Cold Information Wrestling Style: Technical/High Flyer Set Up Move: Tres Flores (Dread locks the victim up in a Cobra Clutch, but then runs forwards a couple of steps and delivers a bulldog, inflicting heavy damage on the neck of the victim) Finisher: No Moment (Submission) (The victim lies on their back on the mat, with Dread standing over top of them. Dread picks up the victim’s legs as if he were getting ready for a wishbone legsplit, but he suddenly spins around so his back is facing the opponent, while holding onto the victim’s ankles. Dread then steps over so the victim is back on their stomach, and he has the victim’s feet/legs locked under his armpits, with their lower body going under his legs, but their upper body and head are still on the mat (essentially a Boston crab of Walls of Jericho, except Dread faces the opposite direction). He then reaches down to the opponent, still lying on their stomach, then grabs their arms and picks them up into the air, suspending them. While suspended, Dread swings the victim back and forth, putting extreme pressure on the back, up through the upper neck of the victim.) Title History: None Award History: None Other Federations: GCW, AWA Other Federation Awards: <b>AWA</b> - Hardcore Champion, (2x) Television Champion, Tag Team Champion, United States Champion <b>GCW</b> - Hard*Core Champion, Television Champion, World Champion Quotes "I am exactly what...you...<b>dread</b>!" History Jason Dread, born Seth Cantrell, was born in Southern Tracks, Arizona on January 23rd. As a small child, he grew up watching the great wrestlers of all time, and frequently mimicked whatever moves he possibly could with any nearby stuffed animal. This was shunned upon by his mother, who after his father died when he was 11, completely restricted Seth from watching any type of wrestling. This did not stop little Seth from going forward with his dream...and that was to become a professional wrestler! At the age of 19, Seth moved out of his home and to the outskirts of Southern Tracks, one hour away from Phoenix. It was at this time he came unto the knowledge of a former wrestling great of a decade ago who was willing to train any people interested to becoming professional wrestlers. Seth, along with only one other pupil appeared at the doorstep of ‘The Iceman’ Rick Davis, and took him up on the offer to train for free. Because there were only two men to train, everything went by fairly quickly, with both Seth and the man who grew up to become his closest friend, Eric Inez, learning what it took in under nine months’ time period. Both were found to be incredibly gifted inside the ring, and with the huge size difference between the two, put on a fantastic match for the head of an up and coming Mid-western wrestling organization, clinching them with jobs in their first wrestling company. Six months time passed in this organization, before Seth, dubbing himself 'Khristian Khaos' encountered his first real wrestling injury, as his suicidal high flying nature nearly costed him all movement below his neck from a springboard 450 degree splash that went terribly wrong. Three months of recuperation later, Khaos wasn’t cutting it with the fans, as the now slower nature of his matches took hold of the fans, sending them into a rally of boos whenever he came to wrestle. He went back to Davis, and slowly he began a new persona. He trained harder, put on more muscle...and soon, Jason Dread was born. On the first month back he became an extremely popular star with his new look and attitude, getting over and receiving a push very quickly. He became the federation’s World Champion when everything began crashing around him. His best friend, 'Bane' as he called himself, broke his neck during a ladder match with a fellow wrestler, and the company was on the verge of bankruptcy. Two weeks after Bane broke his neck...the company filed for bankruptcy. As Jason Dread returned back to Southern Tracks, he was visited by Rick Davis’ daughter...who told Jason about the malignant Cancer Davis had developed in the past two years. Quickly Dread went to visit Rick, who was literally lying on his deathbed. The last words spoken to Dread by the Iceman were 'Never give up on yourself. I may be going, but my spirit will always be with you.' Dread left the room...and his only mentor and tutor died five minutes later. With no job, one friend, albeit crippled, and slowly running out of money, Dread was being backed against the wall as for what to do with his life. When it seems as he was backed to the edge of the road at the age of 23, he received a phone call from Global Championship Wrestling...soon he had a job all over again. Starting from the bottom, Jason Dread systematically worked himself up. His first night in the company led him to capture the Hard*Core title from the greatest Hard*Core champion GCW ever had, The Toad. This merely started the wheels of the machine that led Jason Dread through a path of destruction and broken bodies, including Rick Sherwin, Luke Hanson, and Dan Rivero, whom he captured the Television Title from. The night he lost the T.V. Title, was the night Dread snapped. Always a fighter for the fans, Dread turned on them and allied himself with the World Champion, Octavian, in an alliance which shocked the fans...and skyrocketed Dread's career from a lowly T.V. Title contendor, to that of World Champion status. Two months were struck out by Dread as he came to prove himself time and time again that he was ready to become the champion. He waged war with a man known as Badger Benandanti, and in November of 2001, Jason Dread won a match to become #1 contendor to the GCW World Title in a 4 way dance at the next PPV...which unfortunately Dread did not win. He got one last chance at the current champion, Tristain Mayhem at the following Pay Per View...and Dread finally acheieved his goal and became the GCW World Champion after striving so long and hard for the gold. He didn't come in alone, however, Dread broke to the top along with the reformation of the strongest GCW stable ever in existence...the Establishment. Steven Caldera, Jason Dread, Toxic, Michael Stevens, and Khan ruled the GCW for two blissful months, as Dread defeated contender after contender for his title, once even defeating ten other men, an unheard of feat for GCW, in one match. The ride came to an end as Jason Dread lost his GCW World Title in a handicap match where the Jhub pinned Steven Caldera...and his world came crashing down. The friends that were so dear to him were suddenly gone, attacking him as soon as he lost his strap. Dread went into hiding. Not fighting any matches, not speaking to anyone...he stayed in the dark shadows of the wrestling arenas and watched as people went by. He had no one. Battling suicide, Dread thought that his life was surely over again...then came Khan. Khan, his former friend, found it entertaining to poke fun at Jason while he was in his downed state. Week after week he would insult him...which eventually lead to a slap to Jason's face...and a snap inside his head. A lead pipe to the back of the head was the last thing Khan recieved before a challenge to a No Disqualifications match at the greatest GCW Pay Per View to date...which was coincidentally in just a week. Jason Dread and Khan put together one of the best matches ever seen, with both men kicking out of the others' respective finishing maneuvers several times, before Dread finally got the win over Khan...and ended his career at the same time. He was back... ...but GCW wasn't. The company shut down a month later, leaving Dread without any outlet for any of the newfound hatred he possessed. On the side of the fans once again, he plans to go back to his roots as the damndest fighter GCW has ever seen...and after having everything bottled up inside for over half a year... ...he will show everyone once again why he is exactly what...you...<b>dread</b>! <u>Gimmick</u> Jason Dread is indeed an outlandish man in the world of professional wrestling. Outside the ring and on the camera, he is a proud man. This can often get him into trouble when around the wrong people and this quality is brought out. In the ring and fights however, Jason Dread becomes an evil and ruthless machine, capable of inflicting the most amount of punishment available in the smallest amount of time possible. He can take anyone apart technically, then spring to the top rope and deliver a completely innovative high flying maneuver. He is also very resourceful, being a near master of in-ring strategy and presence. He knows when he is trapped, and when to throw that low blow or hit that one move which will get him out of any jam. He loves to sneak out of the ring whenever the opportunity arises, grab a foreign object such as a chair, and use it to his advantage whenever he feels the need to. Stooping this low however, is pretty much only employed when he is near a losing situation. Normally though, he will stick to what he knows best and was taught...straight wrestling. <u>His 'Look'</u> Jason Dread’s jet black hair is cut short, only long enough to where he has a small amount of bangs lying over his forehead. The tips of these bangs are colored a deep crimson red...the color of blood. His face is slightly handsome, except for a small, but noticeable scar under his right eye. On his chin he has a dark brown to red goatee which covers his chin, but nothing more. He chooses not to wear a shirt in the ring, but wears tight taping around both of his arms, near his shoulders to show off his not extremely huge, but toned muscles. His pants are custom made black jeans that on the side, down each leg the word ‘Dread’ is printed in a fiery print, which is laced with printed-on barbed wire. He does not wear any elbow pads, and plain black boots adorn his feet. Whenever a heel, nary a moment passes where we don’t see him without a scowl upon his face, which is almost a trademark of his. Any other time however, his face remains nearly expressionless. The body of the victim resembles an upside-down “V”) 3- Double Underhook Piledriver 4- Release German Suplex 5- Double Springboard Hurricanrana (runs at turnbuckle, climbs up and springs off both sets of rope on both sides of the turnbuckle, then flies at the victim and delivers a hurricanrana.) 6- Russian legsweep 7- Rolling Senton (Opponent lies on the mat, Dread runs at the victim, does a forward roll, and as he gets back up from the first roll, does a flip splash onto the victim.
