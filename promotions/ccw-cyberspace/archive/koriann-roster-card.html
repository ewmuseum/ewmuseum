Koriann Real Name: Birthdate: August 22nd, 1980 Hometown: Las Vegas, Nevada Height: 5'6' Weight: 100 lbs. Nicknames: Jackass Status: Heel Manager(s): Current Teams: Entrance Music: 'I'm Real' by Jennifer Lopez Wrestling Style: VERY amatuerish (you could almsot call it: Asinine) Signature Moves: The Thump and The Spit Punch (*sigh*) Set Up Move: ( ) Finisher: Fameasser (While her opponent is bent over, Koriann leaps on top of them and drives their head into the ground with her ass ) Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Title History: CCW Heavyweight Champion; 3x CCW Women's Champion Cyberspace Championship Wrestling Award History: Other Federations: Other Federation Awards: Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: MSN Name: Koriann grew up in an orphanage, never knowing her real parents. Her life was actually pretty good at the orphanage, and she was treated well. But nobody wanted her, and she was never adopted. She grew bored very quickly. Koriann dreamed about escaping the everyday doldrom routine of her life at the orphanage. She ran away when she was 17. She hit the streets of Vegas and fell in with some bad company. To make money, she sold herself on the streets and eventually got involved with a Vegas mud wrestling show. Koriann spent a good two years there before she was found by several CCW scouts looking for raw talent, which she definitely had. She is young, niave, and really doesn't know very much about professional wrestling. But she's cocky and arrogant enough to believe she can succeed at anything in life. But she usually gets more than she can handle. Since coming to CCW she has earned her jackass reputation and has embarrassed herself, and CCW, on more than one occasion. She will not admit to any of it, however, and keeps striving forward, believing herself to the best sports entertainer in the world of wrestling. Period.
